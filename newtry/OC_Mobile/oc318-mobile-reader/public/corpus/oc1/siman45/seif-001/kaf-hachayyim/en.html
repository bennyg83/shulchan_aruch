1) [Seif 1] It is forbidden to enter a cemetery or within four amos of a corpse, etc. This implies that in a cemetery it is forbidden even beyond four amos from the grave; for if not, they should have combined them and said it is forbidden to walk within four amos of a grave or of a corpse. Taz, se'if katan 1. And so wrote Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1, that even within four amos of a cemetery it is forbidden, and he wrote that so it is in Yoreh De'ah siman 282 seif 4, and 367 seif 3, and 376 seif 4; and this was Beit Yosef's intent in siman 23, and so is the intent of the Gemara; end of his words; see there. And see Machatzit HaShekel. However, the Bach asked that in the Gemara it implies it is forbidden only within four amos of a corpse, but beyond four amos it is permitted; and so wrote Beit Yosef in siman 23; and he answered that in a cemetery as well it is forbidden only within four amos of a grave; see there. And so wrote Olat Tamid, ot 1. And the Taz there answered that the Tur holds that the primary prohibition is certainly within four amos of the grave, but as a fence one should not enter a cemetery with tefillin on his head, lest he come within four amos unawares; see there. And Maamar Mordechai, ot 1, answered: because a typical cemetery is full of graves, and presumably when he is there he is near a grave; therefore Maran stated a blanket prohibition; see there. Thus there are four disputes in the matter: according to the Bach and Olat Tamid, even in a cemetery it is forbidden only within four amos of a grave. According to the Taz, not entering a cemetery in tefillin is only a fence, but it is not forbidden by the letter of the law. According to Maamar Mordechai, it is forbidden to enter because it is full of graves. And according to Magen Avraham, even within four amos of a cemetery it is forbidden to enter. Therefore one should be stringent and not enter a cemetery with tefillin on his head even beyond four amos of a grave. And so wrote Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 1, and these are his words: A cemetery that is surrounded by a partition: one should be stringent not to enter it at all with tefillin on his head, even far from the graves, lest he come within four amos of some grave unawares; but outside the partition it is permitted even adjacent to it, although he is within four amos of graves, because the partition interrupts and he does not appear as one who mocks the poor; end of his words. And see Yoreh De'ah siman 367 seif 6. And ground that was designated for a cemetery, for example where one made a partition around it and the like, and there are graves inside it: the entire four amos of the designated ground is forbidden, even far from the graves. Levushei Serad; Pitchei Olam, ot 1. And see further in our words to siman 23 what we wrote on this, with Hashem's help.
b) There or inside the grave of a dead person, etc., it is forbidden to enter the grave of a dead person, not precisely because it is at the border of the dead, but if the dead person comes to his border, they need to be saved, but what the sages said in the present.
