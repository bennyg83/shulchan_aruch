[1] Lo'eg larash, etc. But for the arm it is permitted because it is covered. For this reason one may likewise permit above, siman 43, to enter a privy with shel yad tefillin, since it is covered — for Rashi explained in Berachos folio 23 that the reason shel rosh is forbidden is because it is exposed. There is also room to question whether the straps wrapped on the hand suffice, or whether we speak only when the hand straps too are covered. Subsequently Magen Avraham was printed and wrote explicitly that the straps too must be covered.
