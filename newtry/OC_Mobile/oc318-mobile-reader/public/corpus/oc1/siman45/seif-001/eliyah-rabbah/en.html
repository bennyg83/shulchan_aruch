(1) Mocking the poor, etc. Arm tefillin are permitted because they are covered. For the same reason, one may perhaps permit entering a lavatory while wearing arm tefillin, as discussed above in siman 43, because they are covered. Rashi explains in Berachos 23 that head tefillin are forbidden because they are exposed. There is still room to question whether the straps wrapped around the hand must also be covered, or whether the ruling assumes that the straps around the hand are covered as well. After Magen Avraham was printed, I saw that he explicitly writes that the straps too must be covered.
