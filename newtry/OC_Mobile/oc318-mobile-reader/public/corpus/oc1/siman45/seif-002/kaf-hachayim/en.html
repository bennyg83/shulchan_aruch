(2) There: "or within four amot of a corpse," etc. That which we say it is forbidden to enter within four amot of a corpse—not precisely when he is at the corpse's boundary, but even when the corpse comes into his boundary he must remove them; only that the Sages spoke in the present case. And some say that that which it is forbidden to enter within four amot of prayer—a reminder of the wretched and the not-wretched—who yields to whom? And so it appears from the implication of the poskim's words, as I found in the manuscript of Mahara Nachum (may his memory be blessed)—the words of Menachem, letter 1.
