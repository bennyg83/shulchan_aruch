And if — Eitz Yosef: shel yad — the straps around the finger must be covered, for otherwise there is lo'eg larash. And see Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1 — from this one learns that it is permitted to enter a privy with shel yad — see there; that is, what is written in siman 43, in the gloss, the view of our Rabbi Hai that entering is forbidden but shel yad, since it is covered, is permitted; and this is also when the straps around the finger are covered, for the straps too have sanctity, as there is a yod in them. But to relieve oneself it is certainly forbidden with shel yad; and likewise to urinate in a permanent privy, as is proven in siman 44 regarding his arm, that it is forbidden to sleep casually and regularly lest he pass wind in them — all the more so to relieve oneself; and as I wrote in siman 44 in Magen Avraham there.
