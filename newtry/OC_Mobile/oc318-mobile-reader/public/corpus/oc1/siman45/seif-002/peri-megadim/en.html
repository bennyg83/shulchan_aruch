And if. See Taz. The straps of the arm tefillin wound around the finger must also be covered. Otherwise one violates the prohibition of mocking the poor. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1, who derives from this that one may enter a lavatory while wearing covered arm tefillin. He refers to Rama in siman 43, seif 5, citing Rabbeinu Hai that merely entering is forbidden. Covered arm tefillin are permitted because the straps around the finger are also covered, and straps possess sanctity because they contain the yod knot. Relieving oneself, however, is certainly forbidden while wearing arm tefillin. The same applies to urinating in a permanent lavatory, as is evident from siman 44 regarding sleeping while wearing them because of the concern that one may pass wind. All the more so, relieving oneself is forbidden. I discussed this in siman 44 in connection with Magen Avraham.
