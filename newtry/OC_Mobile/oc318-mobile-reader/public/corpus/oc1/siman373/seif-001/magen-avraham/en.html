(א) Board. Shaga wrote that the board must be affixed with a nail so it cannot be removed on Shabbat:

(ב) The law is thus. And specifically when the entire length of the gezuztraot is not close — only along ten amot they are close — then it is an opening as written siman 372 seif 7; but if they are close to one another more than ten amot they make one eruv and not two; and the same law when the entire length of the gezuztraot is close, even less than ten — it is as fully breached and thus Maggid Mishneh in the name of Rashba and thus Bach:

(ג) They cannot join. For when they place the board from one to the other it comes from the corner of one to the other, and an opening in a corner people do not make; Eruvin 98b:

(ד) It is properly considered like an opening. Meaning it is as if the board lies level:

(ה) Like an opening. And specifically when they are distant from one another — such as the alley is wide but they are not opposite one another; and if you extend a string from one to the side opposite it there will be less than three handbreadths from the projection opposite it; but if they are literally adjacent to one another they estimate four as in the gloss and a board is not needed:
