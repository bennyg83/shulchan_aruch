They cannot make an eruv together — see Taz who implies from his words that the Mechaber also holds like Tosafos, and therefore he agreed that merely because of bending alone there is no partnership and the board does not help; see in Avkat Rochel who disagrees and holds there is no difference except that whenever there is bending within three handbreadths the board does not help, and he wrote that thus it appears in Shulchan Aruch that he did not distinguish in this, and the Mechaber is silent like Rashi and not like Tosafos in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, and thus it appears in Otzar HaShulchan that the Mechaber is silent like Rashi [and in truth the words of Taz are very difficult, for it appears from him that Tosafos also concede where it happened that he placed the board in the corner of the gezuztra from one to the other we do not consider it an opening, and one who examines Chiddushei HaRashba will see explicitly that for Tosafos who question from this about a ladder and brought proof that it helps even where the board lies in a corner — if so, Shulchan Aruch is not silent like Tosafos] and likewise the essence is that many rishonim hold like Rashi that the mishnah of gezuztra refers to a board and even so in this case where it is not opposite one another it does not help — behold Rabbeinu Yonah and Rashba and Ritva and Ri az explained, and what Tosafos questioned on his explanation was already answered in Chiddushei HaRashba and Ritva, see there.
