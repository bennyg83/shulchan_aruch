Gezuztraot — in siman 372 I, the poor one, have here a great turmoil and I must contemplate their holy words; and there are several textual readings here, and I will explain with God's help here and in simanim 2 and 3. Behold Rashi Eruvin 78b on the mishnah there s.v. "two gezuztraot," etc., "and if they are this next to this," etc., and s.v. "less" — that there is no width — implies two houses on two sides of reshus ha-rabbim opposite one another; Eruvin 98b; and Rashi explained "this next to this" means on one side in reshus ha-rabbim and it is like two dyot (balconies); one dyot — see beginning of chapter HaZorek Shabbat 97a; and the master said regarding one dyot that handing above nineteen one is liable — and thus one could say that in every case it is forbidden to carry from gezuztra to its fellow when there is air of reshus ha-rabbim between them three [tefachim] in any event, due to a decree lest one hand above nineteen; here it deals when he placed a board — there is no air of reshus ha-rabbim — unlike Tosafos s.v. "and likewise" as they explained in the name of Rabbeinu Tam: when there is air less than four [tefachim] then perforce it is explained as two dyot or courtyards and there is no air of reshus ha-rabbim at all between them. And its statement "less" — that the width of the board is not — there is a reading in Yerushalmi: "less" means the groove between the courtyards or the air between the gezuztraot is less than four [tefachim]. And know that gezuztra is in two dwellings as Rashi and Rambam explained on the mishnah — in such a courtyard even a window and opening above nineteen does not help as siman 372 s.v. "and what Taz wrote" letter 2 to join courtyards; nevertheless gezuztra is a roofed upper story, etc. And in the Gemara there 79a Rava said: that which you said "opposite one another" — not "not opposite one another"; likewise "one above the other" — we only said when there are three [tefachim] between them, etc. It appears this is the reading of Rashi and Rambam chapter 3 of five [chapters of] Eruvin 14; and Maggid Mishneh there that Rambam omitted "leaning" one from the other — only "one above the other"; and Rashi explained s.v. "not opposite one another" — one projects more to reshus ha-rabbim, etc. — if so his explanation is apparently that one is below its fellow; and from what is written s.v. "there is not between them distant" it implies not in height; however in Rambam the reading is authentic and Rashi's explanation does not suffice with a board of four — it implies that wider than this helps; and this is because an opening in a corner, etc., or in height one from the other you need to go on a board of four — behold it is much wider than this — understand this and see letter 3 from this; and with God's help it will be explained further in siman 23:
