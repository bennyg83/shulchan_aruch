The law of two gezuztraot in two upper stories. And in it one seif: Two
gezuztraot projecting from two upper stories opposite one another, and he' placed between them a board four [tefachim] wide — behold it is' like an opening and they join in one eruv.And if the distance between them is not four, even without a board likewise is the law (Maggid chapter 3).} And if they wished, each makes an eruv for itself; but if they were not opposite one another — one leaning to the east and one to the west — they cannot join in one eruv; and likewise if one was higher than its fellow it is not considered an opening; and this applies when they are distant from one another three [tefachim]; but if they are within three of one another, whether in distance or in height, it is properly considered like an opening:) ואם רצו כל א' מערבת לעצמה אבל אם היו זו שלא כנגד זו שאחת משוכה למזרח וא' למערב אינם יכולים לערב יחד וכן אם היתה אחת גבוה מחבירת' לא חשיב פתח והני מילי כשמרוחקו' זו מזו ג"ט אבל אם הם תוך ג' זו לזו בין בריחוק בין בגובה חשיב שפיר כפתח:
