Wax, etc. It is a mitzvah to buy them from charity funds from what drips from the synagogue lamps. Maharshal. One should not kindle some lamps from wax and some from oil, because people will say that two people kindled. Shaar Ephraim, siman 32. It implies that one may kindle one night with wax and another night with oil. Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah wrote that when one kindles with wax, it is proper to set each one higher than the next, following the rule that we ascend in holiness; until here. See paragraph 6 in siman 671. Now in the sefer Ner Mitzvah he wrote at great length that one who kindles with a wax lamp has not fulfilled, for Rosh wrote in a responsum, and a wax torch, etc., which shows that it is called a torch and not a lamp. In my eyes it is astonishing to reject the widespread custom of the world to use wax, which is mentioned in Minhagim and Rema. So too I saw in Kol Bo. In truth, Rosh there used a common expression and did not write it as law at all. Moreover, this is difficult from the law that one searches for chametz by the light of a torch; according to all, one searches with a wax candle, and only two braided candles are called a torch, as stated in siman 433, section 2. So it also implies regarding a torch for Havdalah in siman 298, section 2, and in Yoreh Deah, siman 11. Nevertheless, for the preferred mitzvah, olive oil is better.
