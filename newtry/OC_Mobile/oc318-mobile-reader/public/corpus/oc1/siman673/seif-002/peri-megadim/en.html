If. See Turei Zahav, as in siman 672, Turei Zahav paragraph 1 and Magen Avraham paragraph 4. According to those who are stringent there, as long as the shamash is burning in it, it is permitted also here, even if he placed more than the required measure. See Pri Chadash and Eliyah Rabbah. See siman 264, section 3, regarding tar; one may say that on Chanukah it is permitted. Regarding resin, one may say that it is forbidden on Shabbos Chanukah lest he take from it. Although it is muktzeh, Shabbos makes a person more separated from it, and still we are concerned lest he take from it; all the more so with the Chanukah lamp. On a weekday, one may say it is permitted, and we are not concerned that he will take from it and reduce it below the measure of half an hour, certainly if he places much more than the measure. If it went out, he is not required to tend to it, meaning when it went out unintentionally; see Beis Yosef and Bach. But if he extinguished it intentionally within the measure of half an hour, it is obvious that he is required to rekindle it, and it is obvious that he does not bless again even in such a case.
