Bound to it. If he placed it in a location where wind is common and it went out, he is bound to it and must light it again with a blessing. The same applies if it went out intentionally; Gan HaMelech, siman 43. In Kenesses HaGedolah he wrote in the name of Shiltei HaGibborim to require him to light it again, but he need not recite the blessing again. So too the Taz wrote that he need not recite the blessing again; see there.
To be stringent. The consensus of the later authorities is that it is proper to be stringent and light it again in every case, and especially on erev Shabbos before Shabbos; see there. The Maharshal wrote that the preferred mitzvah is to buy wax for the Chanukah lamp from what drips from the candles in the synagogue, since one mitzvah has already been performed with them. He should be careful not to buy wax from the candles of their houses of worship; see siman 154, se'if 11.
