(א) (Sq. B) B. to check K. and his hands are close together and the Chief Rabbi's writing 17 is a derogatory use and refer to 17:

(ב) Daphilo eat a daz with his hands close to the candle.

For otherwise he would buy Hanukkah, etc. — meaning we say there: Shabbat light and Hanukkah light — Shabbat light comes first because of shalom bayit; meaning if he has only one light he takes it for Shabbat and does not kindle Hanukkah — Rava's statement 23a. Impossible permitted to eat by Hanukkah light — he should kindle for Hanukkah and eat by it, fulfilling Hanukkah and shalom bayit. Although in Talmudic times they kindled outside at house or courtyard entrance — then no shalom bayit if kindling for Hanukkah; for us kindling inside within twenty — Magen Avraham — truly siman 678 possible shalom bayit thus; must be from Gemara words; although in their days they kindled outside, nevertheless lest Hanukkah mitzvah be nullified he should kindle inside — even when kindling inside in Talmudic times he fulfilled when impossible, for in danger they said place on table, siman 671 seif 5; although in danger he needs another light to use, siman 671 — for recognition it is for mitzvah, as Ran there — nevertheless without another light nearby it implies he still fulfilled Hanukkah; only when possible another light is required l'chatchila for recognition. When he has only one light, lest Hanukkah be nullified he should kindle on table even though forbidden to eat by it. Magen Avraham below siman 677: nowadays kindling inside, although forbidden to eat by it, nevertheless kindle for Hanukkah and eat by it when impossible — as in danger he places on table even though he uses it — specifically for us who otherwise kindle inside: kindle for Hanukkah and leave on table like danger; although in danger another light needed when possible for recognition; when impossible Magen Avraham permits even without another, since only recognition; for us in Talmudic times kindling outside — if permitted to eat by Hanukkah, Magen Avraham would permit kindling inside on table even without another; but since forbidden to eat by Hanukkah, they did not want to permit kindling inside since main mitzvah is outside, and also eating by it; although in danger permitted when another light for recognition — without another, to permit kindling inside and eating by it without another light, they were not lenient.
