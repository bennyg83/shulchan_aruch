Se'if 2, lighting. This is the wording of the Shulchan Aruch itself, and it requires investigation. See the Taz, who wrote that it means: since he lit it, he has already fulfilled the mitzvah and needs no more.
And even if it went out, etc. For if not, on Shabbos it would be forbidden to use the oils and wicks that the Sages forbade, because if it goes out one would be bound to it, and perhaps it will go out in such a case and he will be negligent, as Rashi wrote there.
And likewise if, etc. So is proven from the Gemara there, from the fact that they permit lighting with the oils of which they said, etc., and we are not concerned in such a case.
