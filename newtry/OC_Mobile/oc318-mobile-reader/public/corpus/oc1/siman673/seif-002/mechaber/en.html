Lighting performs the mitzvah. Therefore, if it went out before its time had passed, he is not bound to it. Even if it went out on erev Shabbos before accepting Shabbos, while it is still day, he is not bound to it. Likewise, if after he lit it he came to adjust it and accidentally extinguished it, he is not bound to it. Gloss: If he wants to be stringent upon himself and light it again, he should not recite a blessing over it.
