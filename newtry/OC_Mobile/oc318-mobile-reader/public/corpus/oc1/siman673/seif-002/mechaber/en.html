The lighting fulfills a mitzvah, therefore, if it is extinguished before its time has passed, he does not need it, and even if it is extinguished on the eve of Shabbat before the reception of Shabbat, which is still a day in advance, he does not need it, and also if after the lighting comes to be repaired and he accidentally extinguishes it, he does not need it: Haga, and if he wants to aggravate himself and return to the lighting, he should not bless it (Rashba C. ):
