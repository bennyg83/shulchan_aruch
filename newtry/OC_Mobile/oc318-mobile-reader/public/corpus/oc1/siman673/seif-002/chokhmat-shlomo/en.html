(Same, seif 2) Kindling accomplishes the mitzvah; therefore if it went out before its time passed he is not concerned for it, etc. NB: The wording is puzzling as Taz wondered; we wrote in our novellae in several ways; now it appears thus according to Beit Yosef's answer why in siman 672 they wrote permitted to benefit from Hanukkah lamp oil, and in siman 677 ruled opposite — Beit Yosef answers: if remainder from measure required for kindling, forbidden to use; if more than required measure remains, permitted to benefit. It appears one should add seasoning: since we hold nothing whose mitzvah was done and one violates me'ilah in it — therefore all burnt offerings, their ash is permitted; so too if kindled as measure and more remains, mitzvah was done, therefore permitted to benefit. But if remainder is required measure, mitzvah was not done, therefore forbidden. However Beit Yosef did not write this reason — since we hold kindling accomplishes mitzvah, if it went out not concerned; if so immediately upon kindling mitzvah was done — even if remainder is measure, mitzvah was done, therefore forbidden to benefit — he had to give another reason; see on this regarding a faded Torah scroll whether one may write secular on it — responsa to community in Autishan. At least accordingly one could say: generally we forbid leaving forbidden things lest stumbling; Pesachim chapter 1 dilution — see there; so too here appropriate to say if it went out within time he is concerned to relight immediately since forbidden to benefit, concern lest stumble — but one could say in rabbinic matters they were lenient. We find things they strengthened like Torah law — but this is one rabbinic matter; in two rabbinic matters certainly they did not strengthen like Torah. Therefore two matters "if it went out concerned": one the Gemara debates for Hanukkah law itself if obligated to relight as Hanukkah light — difference if must bless again; if from Hanukkah law he would bless like tefillin if knot broke; Maharsha brought Beit Yosef here: if went out unintentionally no need to bless — we hold if it went out not concerned; implies if concerned, must bless. On this the Gemara debates; this law does not depend on kindling versus placement accomplishing mitzvah; therefore not resolved from this — we hold if it went out not concerned, not obligated to relight from Hanukkah law. However from another reason he should be concerned since forbidden to benefit and needs burning — concern lest forget and benefit; from this no blessing required, only concern of stumbling. Similar siman 434 on checking after Pesach — no blessing since only stumbling concern — see there. If placement accomplishes mitzvah, mitzvah not yet done since he did not kindle measure; if d'oraisa forbidden to benefit, even Hanukkah they would strengthen to kindle immediately lest stumbling; but since kindling accomplishes mitzvah, immediately upon kindling mitzvah done — even d'oraisa permitted to benefit; again no Torah-like strengthening; all the more rabbinic prohibition on remainder beyond measure — if it went out not concerned even from stumbling concern, permitted to leave for other days — it fits well — examine. Explanation of difference if kindling versus placement: if kindling accomplishes mitzvah he does not kindle every moment only at start — necessarily enactment from "whatever is fit for mixing, mixing does not block" — since at start he did main mitzvah, afterward for remainder "fit for mixing" — at start of kindling his whole mitzvah was done since fit for mixing; but for placement, mitzvah is all time of placement — he placed constantly, did whole placement putting oil — mitzvah is all placement time; what "if it went out not concerned" — only because ones rachmana patrei, not obligated to trouble to relight; therefore no fulfillment, mitzvah not done, forbidden by law — such is correct. Another way to resolve: reason if it went out concerned or not depends on kindling versus placement l'shem mitzvah: for view kindling accomplishes mitzvah — he only kindles a moment, remainder burns by itself — intent as Bava Kamma chapter 1 regarding fire because of substance: what burns at first as if he kindled all then; therefore afterward measure burning is branch of mitzvah — in branch we say fit for mixing; for placement view, main mitzvah is what rests in place — all placement time is main mitzvah — in main mitzvah we do not say fit for mixing, as Rashbam Bava Batra chapter 1 — see there. Therefore if it went out concerned. However l'chatchila why not obligated to relight — necessarily reason is not from fit for mixing but enactment from start; no difference kindling versus placement l'shem mitzvah. One could say: in Torah Menachot if not mixed valid — if Torah valid b'dieved, rabbinic Hanukkah permitted l'chatchila; therefore depends on fit for mixing. This depends on dispute Gittin 65: R' Chisda holds whatever Rabbis enacted like Torah — one view only where Torah has parallel, not where Torah has no parallel; we hold minor even throwing pebble does not acquire for others — necessarily like R' Chisda; as Gemara answers partnership of alleys is rabbinic — where no Torah parallel, not enacted like Torah; therefore Gemara debate if kindling or placement — R' Chisda holds in all things enacted like Torah; if fit for mixing not blocking, l'chatchila still must kindle like Menachah — therefore not resolved from this — we hold if it went out not concerned from fit for mixing, since Torah valid b'dieved, rabbinic l'chatchila permitted; where no Torah parallel not enacted like Torah — this reason if it went out not concerned from fit for mixing; proven kindling accomplishes mitzvah, therefore not concerned — correct — examine. Mechaber tied them — examine well.
