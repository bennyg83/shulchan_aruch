Se'if 3, and a metal lamp. For it is not repulsive, as written in chapter 3 of Shabbos, folio 44a.
And of glass. See Tosafos at the end of chapter 3 of Berachos, folio 25b, s.v. 'chamber pot,' and the Rosh there, and above, siman 87, se'if 1. So too it is implied in the Gemara of Avodah Zarah, folio 33, regarding glazed vessels, according to Rabbeinu Tam's explanation there, s.v. 'glazed vessels.'
