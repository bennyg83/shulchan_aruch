In Taz s.k. 3, and so implied in hints he did not mention disgrace, etc.; nevertheless this does not answer except what he questioned — what disgrace in lecturing coins; but still difficult why Rosh needed to say because it is near the light — he should answer simply Rava Ashi taught even temporary use is forbidden; therefore he wrote since Tur did not write "against," meaning Tur truly does not distinguish near light or not, therefore wrote language "to use alone" so one not accustomed to infer from R' Elazar's wording as Rosh wrote from R' Elazar — therefore Tur used "its light" to say R' Elazar who wrote against Hanukkah light is mere wording, not to infer near Hanukkah light is required; if so, this is opposite Beit Yosef who questioned Tur should be like Rosh; on this Taz at start of his words: I find Rosh's words difficult, etc. — meaning on the contrary Rosh is difficult why he wrote thus, Tur truly did not write thus, and he should answer simply R' Elazar taught all temporary use forbidden.
