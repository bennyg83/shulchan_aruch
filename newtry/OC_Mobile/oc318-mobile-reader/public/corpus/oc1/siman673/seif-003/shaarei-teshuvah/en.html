Eliyah Rabbah; see Peri Chadash in name of Maharikash: walking by Hanukkah light to see lest he stumble — permitted, not obligated to close eyes — not called use; see responsa Penei Aryeh siman 47 — they forbade only use; whatever is not use, permitted to sit in house while Hanukkah lights burn — see there from Peri Chadash; truly so explained Magen Avraham below siman 678: nowadays buy for Hanukkah, in any case do not sit in darkness, etc.; from there also proof as Maharikash wrote: main shalom bayit is in walking lest stumble; see above siman 663 Magen Avraham s.k. 13-14 — see there.
