See Pri Chadash in the name of Maharikash, that walking by the light of the Chanukah lamp in order to see that one does not stumble is permitted, for one is not obligated to close his eyes, and this is not called use. See Responsa Pnei Aryeh, siman 47, that they forbade only using it, but anyone who is not using it for anything may sit in his house while the Chanukah lamps are burning. See there, where he brought these words of Pri Chadash. In truth this is also explained in Magen Avraham below, siman 678, in what he wrote that now that we kindle, he should buy for the Chanukah lamp, and at any rate he should not sit in darkness, etc. From there it is also proven as Maharikash wrote, for the main domestic peace is in walking so that he not stumble. See above, siman 263, Magen Avraham, paragraphs 13 and 14; see there.
