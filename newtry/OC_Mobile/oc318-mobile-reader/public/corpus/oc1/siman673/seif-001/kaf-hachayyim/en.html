1) [Seif 1] All oils and wicks are valid, etc. There are oils that are not drawn after the wick, and there are likewise wicks on which the flame does not take hold properly. On Shabbos it is forbidden to kindle with them, lest one tilt the lamp, as written above in siman 264. The ruling teaches us that they are valid for the Chanukah lamp. The Tur wrote that the reason is that we are not concerned that the lamp may go out and one will kindle it again, because even on a weekday, if it went out before its prescribed time had elapsed, one need not kindle it again. Nor are we concerned that one might tilt it because its light is poor, since it is forbidden to make use of its light.
