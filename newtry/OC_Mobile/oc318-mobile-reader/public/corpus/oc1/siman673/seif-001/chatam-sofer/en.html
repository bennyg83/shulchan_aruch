In Magen Avraham s.k. 10. See Tosafot Zevachim 72a d.h. u'livatalo — they wrote: one could say the Mishnah Avodah Zarah 74a that we consider prohibition in the majority — one could say also in an open barrel because it is considered for idolatry — see there; but certainly once mixed and not recognizable it is no longer considered for idolatry; therefore after the fact we follow, as Terumat HaDeshen's words; see Plati siman 101 who brings proof from Pesachim 89 — his words require study for the examiner — end; see responsa Panim Me'irot part 4 siman 98 and Shevut Yaakov siman 32 — both intended the same thing [from the Gaon, may he live].
