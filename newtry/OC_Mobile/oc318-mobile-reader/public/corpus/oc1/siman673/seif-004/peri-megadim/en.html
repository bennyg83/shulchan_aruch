And there is Taz, Bach, Levush who answered: with another present it is permitted; Taz forbids with oils and wicks that do not last — in such a case even with another present forbidden; only with valid oils when another is present permitted to read, as siman 275 — see there. However what some permit temporary sacred use — specifically no recognition in temporary use, as written in letter 3; also no bizui in mitzvah; some forbid — there is bizui from mitzvah to mitzvah; some say kindling light to light is one mitzvah — Ran there. What I wrote in letter 3 — requires study — perhaps because Shabbat 22 teaches sukkah forbidden, thus bizui even not against Hanukkah; like light to light forbidden by some because of bizui mitzvah — likewise here; therefore Taz ruled temporary forbidden even from distance if not sacred use — still requires study.
