See Responsa Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 39, and there in siman 40 the responsum of the great Rabbi David Oppenheim, of blessed memory, and what he wrote in Kuntres Moed Deyad printed in the sefer Beis Yehudah; see there. See also Responsa Shemesh Tzedakah, siman 30, two responsa on the matter, and there the laws of doubtful blessings are explained. See Birkei Yosef in the name of Mahari Molcho, that if one needs to examine a law about a doubt that arose to him concerning the lamps that he kindled, and he has no other lamp, it is permitted to examine that law by the light of the Chanukah lamp, for many great authorities permitted mitzvah use. Even according to the one who forbids, nevertheless for its own need one may permit it, similar to sleeping in the synagogue on the night of Yom Kippur for the need of the synagogue, and likewise regarding the red heifer, anything for its own need is not labor; see there. His proof can be rejected. But since it is temporary use, it seems obvious that it is permitted.
