Paragraph 3. His hands are close to the lamp, it is permitted, etc. This is difficult: if so, why do we kindle with them on Chanukah? There is a concern that he may tilt it for such use, since with a bad lamp we are concerned that he may tilt even for light use, as proven in siman 264, where only something that can be done without a lamp may be done by its light. This requires examination.
