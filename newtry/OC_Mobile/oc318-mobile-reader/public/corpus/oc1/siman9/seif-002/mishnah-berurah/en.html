(6) “Of wool, etc.” — and Bach wrote: since there are authorities who are strict in this not to make even wool tzitzis for other kinds, since it is not its kind as explained in Beis Yosef — therefore one should not bless except on a wool tallis with wool tzitzis. And the world is accustomed to make wool tzitzis for other kinds, and in Eliyah Rabbah he also wrote that one may be lenient in this, and such is implied in Ma’aseh Rav.

(7) “In our time” — meaning, in the time when there was tekheles, one had to insert four threads — two white threads and two tekheles threads (wool dyed with murex blood) — and through this the mitzvah of tzitzis was fulfilled properly. The Torah permitted us to insert the white pair whether of wool or of linen, whether on a wool garment or a linen garment; and per the view that Rama mentioned in his conclusion that all kinds of garments are obligated from the Torah — even with them it was then permitted from the Torah for wool and linen tzitzis together. But now that we have no tekheles, it turns out we do not fulfill the mitzvah in its fullness — therefore kilayim is not permitted for us.

(8) “Even with other kinds” — the reason is that some wool looks like silk and people will come to place linen tzitzis on it thinking it is silk, and it will be kilayim. And accordingly, with other kinds that are not silk one may permit; and there are other reasons — see Beis Yosef, Bach, and Taz; according to their words one should be strict even with other kinds; and in a time of need one may rely on the lenient authorities, as Eliyah Rabbah wrote. And see in Ma’aseh Rav that the Gra was also among the lenient in this.
