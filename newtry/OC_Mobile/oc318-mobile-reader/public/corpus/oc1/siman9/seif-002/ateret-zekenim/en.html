Some say not to make linen tzitzis at all; but the reverse—there is no reason to decree against wool tzitzis on a silk tallis, lest one make wool tzitzis on linen—for silk on linen does not substitute because they are not similar—or one could say: since the Semag holds that a linen tallis is exempt from tzitzis even with its own kind, there is no reason to decree that one might make wool tzitzis on it (so wrote the Gaon R' Leib Rofe in his gloss on the Tur in the name of Maharam Falk, may his merit protect us).
