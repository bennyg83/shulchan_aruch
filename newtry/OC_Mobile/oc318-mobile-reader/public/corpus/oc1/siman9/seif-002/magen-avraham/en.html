“In general.” The reason is that some wool looks like silk and people might come to tie linen tzitzis on a wool tallis thinking it is silk. Beis Yosef therefore wrote that on a cotton tallis as well one should not tie wool tzitzis, lest they also tie them on linen—but that is not compelling, for between cotton and linen people are quite careful, as stated in Yoreh Deah 302:2; most people use cotton only after inquiry, unlike wool garments from the coast—most people assume they are silk, such as “furstit” in foreign tongue and the like—and in Beis Yosef there is more to say.
