Note 3 — silk alone exempts. And see in the Bach, who is concerned for the view of Hagahos Maimuni in the name of Maharam, that only wool and linen exempt on other species — and that is specifically when tekheles exists. Also, for silk one should not make [tzitzis] of silk, for Baal HaItur holds that “the corner, a kind of corner” and “a thread of tekheles” refer to the latter verse — meaning the white should be a kind of corner and of the kind of tekheles, which is specifically wool; if so, this applies only to a wool garment, but other species have no remedy except with wool and linen together. If so, other species have no remedy at this time when there is no tekheles.
