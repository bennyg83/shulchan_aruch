<i>Tzitzis</i> of linen or of sheep’s wool exempt all kinds of garments except [pairing] linen [garment] with wool or wool with linen nowadays when there is no <i>tekheles</i>, because they are <i>kilayim</i>. Rema: Some say not to make linen <i>tzitzis</i> at all, even with other kinds — and such is the practice (Semak siman 31). ]:
