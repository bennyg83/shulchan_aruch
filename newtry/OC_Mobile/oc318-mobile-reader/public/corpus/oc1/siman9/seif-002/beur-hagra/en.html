Seif 2, "except," etc. See Tosafos, Kesubos 40a, gloss beginning "such as," etc.; and see Tosafos, Yevamos 5a, gloss beginning "even," etc., from what is written in Menachos 40a: since it is possible, etc.; and see Tosafos there, gloss beginning "since," etc.
"Nowadays," etc. See Midrash Rabbah, parashas Shelach: now we have only white, etc.
"And some say," etc. So wrote the Rosh in the name of the Baal HaIttur, that we rule like Rabbi Yehoshua ben Nuri there, 38b: if he has no techeles, he places white, meaning specifically wool dyed white. What it says there, 39b, "either wool or linen," is Rav Nachman, who holds so because the obligation is rabbinic; but according to us, who rule it is biblical, linen does not exempt at all; see there. The Semak wrote: Kilayim in tzitzis is entirely forbidden for us, since we have no techeles, and a linen tallis is exempt even with its own kind. Wool and linen exempt both their own kind and other kinds; other kinds exempt their own kind but not other kinds. There are two explanations of this, and it needs study in Menachos. In any case, it is proper not to make any tzitzis from linen, even for a silk tallis. The Mordechai, siman 950, wrote: a person should be careful not to make any tzitzis from linen, and for a silk tallis he should make silk tzitzis. We read in HaTecheles: wool and linen exempt both their own kind, etc.; there are two explanations of this. See Tosafos there. This means as written in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 3, halachah 5: my master told me that when they said wool and linen exempt both their own kind and other kinds, that is only together; therefore the custom is to make silk tzitzis for silk talleisim. But regarding dyeing, etc. Meaning, this is what is said in Menachos 41b: a tallis is exempted only by its own kind, as Tosafos wrote there, gloss beginning "ein," etc.: it is difficult, etc.; and further it appears, etc. What they wrote there, "and what was explained regarding silks is not exact," etc., is not correct according to this, for it is only together; here it is not discussing that. It is a gloss. This is what the Mordechai means by "see Tosafos there." But we say there, 39b: say either wool, etc.; and this is what the Semak wrote: it needs study in Menachos. The Rosh already wrote in the name of the Baal HaIttur that for Rav Nachman, who holds the obligation is rabbinic, it answers this way; but according to us, who rule it is biblical, as written in seif 1 in the gloss, we establish it as first stated, as above. This is what he wrote: one should not make any linen tzitzis, even, etc.; for a linen tallis one should not make them at all, and for wool it is forbidden because of kilayim. Likewise for other kinds, where one could only make them with both together, for us it is forbidden. The same applies that one should make wool only for wool. He mentioned linen because it has no place in any tallis.
