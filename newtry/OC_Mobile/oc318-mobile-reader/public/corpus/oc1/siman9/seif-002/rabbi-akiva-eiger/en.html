{Rama: There, note 4} Nevertheless, according to those who are lenient to make [tzitzis] — this refers to what he wrote at first, that the warp is wool and the weft silk, which has no remedy per the Rambam; and this is the view of the first authority in Magen Avraham, note 3, that wool and linen together is what exempts when not of its kind. On this he wrote regarding the lenient view: meaning that they are lenient to make wool tzitzis for silk, for they are not concerned for the Rambam’s view, as Magen Avraham wrote in note 3 — the world is accustomed to take wool threads and not silk, for silk does not exempt wool warp. As is simple from his words — not like Machatzis HaShekel. And all his words on this are puzzling.
