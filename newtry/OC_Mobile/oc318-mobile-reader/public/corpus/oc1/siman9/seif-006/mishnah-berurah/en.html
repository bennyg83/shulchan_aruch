(17) “Of linen” — for the Sages decreed because then it would be necessary to make linen tzitzis on it, and there is concern lest he also make tekheles on it and it become a kilayim prohibition when he covers himself with it at night, when there is no time for the mitzvah to apply. And even though in our time we have no tekheles, some hold that nevertheless the decree is not annulled, and many authorities permitted in this, for they hold the essence of the decree was only that they not place tekheles on it lest one come to kilayim — but not on the linen threads; and also in our time when there is no tekheles, this decree does not apply at all. Therefore Rama wrote in his gloss that if it is impossible otherwise, one may rely on them.
