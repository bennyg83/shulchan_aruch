And tzitzis of linen — and what he wrote, that one should make corners of leather, etc.: Eliyah Rabbah wrote it is not clear that one would come to do so with linen itself without leather; and likewise in the gloss, Baal Tosafos Shabbat wrote in the name of a great authority who was strict about this and cited them in Machzik Berachah, and wrote that one should not do so even in a time of need.
