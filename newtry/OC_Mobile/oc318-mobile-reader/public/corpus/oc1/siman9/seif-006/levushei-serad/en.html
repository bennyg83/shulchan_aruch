{Rama: There.} The word "even" was not there. Meaning: in the Semak that was before Beit Yosef it was written "however it is proper to be careful not to make linen tzitzis even on a silk tallis" — so far. By this logic it implies all the more one should not do so on a linen tallis, as Beit Yosef explains; but when Tur copied Semak's words the word "even" is not written.
