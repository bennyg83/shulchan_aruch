And flax tzitzis. And bless on them. So wrote Beit Yosef in this section with the agreement of the Rif, Rosh, and Rambam; see there. And Beer Heitev before me wrote such, and it seems to me he should not bless on them; see there and he did not look in Beit Yosef. And I say to him: behold we rule in many places that where there is doubt in law, doubt in blessings is to be lenient—this was already asked about in responsa Shevut Yaakov vol. 1 siman 1 and in Hagahos Maimoniyos vol. 2 siman 116, see there; Shevut Yaakov answered that therefore Beit Yosef ruled here that one should bless on them, because the three pillars of halachic ruling—the Rif, Rosh, and Rambam, on whom Beit Yosef relies in all his rulings—hold this way; see there. Magen Avraham wrote: and it seems to me one should make leather corners and put wool tzitzis in them; see there.
