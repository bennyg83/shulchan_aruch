The Baer Heitev before me writes that it appears he should not recite a blessing, but he did not examine Beit Yosef. One cannot object that whenever there is a halachic doubt we apply the rule that doubtful blessings are treated leniently. Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 1, and Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 116, were asked this question. Shevus Yaakov answers that Beit Yosef ruled that he should recite the blessing because the three pillars of halachic ruling upon whom Beit Yosef relies, Rif, Rosh, and Rambam, all hold this view; see there. Magen Avraham writes that he should make leather corners and place woolen tzitzis upon them; see there.
