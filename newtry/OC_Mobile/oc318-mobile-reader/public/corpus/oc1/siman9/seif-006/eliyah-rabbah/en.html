[6] That one should not make, etc. Levush explains the reason: it is a decree lest one place woolen tzitzis upon it, thereby creating kilayim. See Eliyah Zuta. Magen Avraham writes: 'It appears to me that he should make leather corners and place woolen tzitzis upon it.' This is not persuasive, because there remains a concern that he will place them into the linen itself, without leather corners.
