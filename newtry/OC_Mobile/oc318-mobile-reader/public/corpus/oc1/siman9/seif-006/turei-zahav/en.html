“That one not make a tallis,” etc. For we may not tie wool tzitzis on it because of kilayim, and tzitzis of other species exempt only in their kind — therefore one would be forced to make linen tzitzis — one might decree lest one also use wool — therefore Beis Shammai said not to make a sheet with tzitzis. One could ask on this like Beis Shammai — Rashi and Rif permitted linen tzitzis on linen — we do not need this decree — we do not lack like Beis Shammai — they further disagree on plain meaning beginning Yevamos as Beit Yosef — therefore if impossible [otherwise] one may rely on them not to annul tzitzis.
