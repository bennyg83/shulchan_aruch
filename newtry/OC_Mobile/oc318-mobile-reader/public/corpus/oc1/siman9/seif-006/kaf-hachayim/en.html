(6) [Seif 2] Linen tzitzis, etc. Gloss: and some say not to make linen tzitzis, etc. — this is Semak's view. Beit Yosef wrote the reason from Mahari Weil, of blessed memory: lest one come to place linen tzitzis on a wool tallis, because silk resembles wool. And likewise Magen Avraham, seif katan 2; Rabbi Zvi, letter 3; Ben Ish Chai there, letter 4. Beit Yosef also wrote there from the above Mahari Weil another reason: because some hold linen tzitzis have no place from the Torah — see there. If one has no choice but linen tzitzis, it is permitted to place linen tzitzis on a garment of other kinds — Rabbi Zvi there; and likewise siman Beit Yaakov, letter 2 — but it is inferior; see in Orach Tov afterward.
