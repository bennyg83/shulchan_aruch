Some say one should not make a linen tallis; even though the halacha is not so, a God-fearing person surpasses all of them and makes a tallis of sheep’s wool, which is obligated in tzitzis from the Torah without doubt. Rema: However if only a linen tallis is possible it is better to make a linen tallis with linen tzitzis than to nullify the mitzvah of tzitzis (Responsa Rosh klal 2).
