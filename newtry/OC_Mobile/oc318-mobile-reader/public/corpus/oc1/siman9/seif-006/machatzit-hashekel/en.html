(Seif katan 6) Of the color, etc. Therefore one should make it, etc., to satisfy both opinions, so that the tzitzis are white and also match the color of the tallis.
In siman 24 he wrote, etc. He gives another reason, based upon “You shall walk in His ways,” to resemble Him, as it were. The Sages expound the verse “Hashem passed before him”: Rabbi Yochanan said that were it not written, it could not be said; Hashem wrapped Himself in a tallis like a prayer leader and said that whenever Israel performs this order, He forgives them. The verse says, “His garment was white as snow.” Thus His tallis, as it were, is white. The view requiring the color of the tzitzis to match the tallis is based upon “This is my God and I will beautify Him.” See Beit Yosef and Taz.
