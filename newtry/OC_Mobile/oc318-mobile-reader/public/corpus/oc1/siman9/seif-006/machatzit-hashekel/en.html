(note 6) In color, etc. — and therefore one should act accordingly in order to satisfy both [opinions]: that the tzitzis be white and also [match] the color of the tallis.

And in siman 24 he wrote, etc. — he gave yet another reason: from [the verse] “and walk in His ways” to resemble Him, blessed be He — for we say “And the Lord passed before him”; R. Yochanan said: were it not for a written verse it would be impossible to say such a thing, teaching that the Holy One, blessed be He, wrapped Himself in a tallis like a prayer leader and said: when My children do before Me in this manner I forgive them. And it is written “His garment was as white as snow” — if so, His tallis must certainly be white. The reason of those who say we require [tzitzis] from the color of the tallis is for this [reason]: “This is my God and I will adorn Him” — see Be’er Hetev and Eliyah Rabbah and Taz.
