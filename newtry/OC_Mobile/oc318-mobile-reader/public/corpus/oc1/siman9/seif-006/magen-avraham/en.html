“Of linen.” Even according to those who forbid, because the decree mainly concerned tekheles and nowadays there is no tekheles, it is not comparable to something forbidden by vote that has no permission except through a greater court in wisdom and number—for when the reason for the prohibition is known, if the reason ceases the prohibition ceases automatically; this is not like Rabban Yochanan ben Zakkai’s egg enactment—see there—where the reason was not as clear, etc. But in our case there is no remedy except that Beis Shammai forbade as a decree, and since that decree no longer applies, it is permitted—see Yoreh Deah 300:6, and Rambam, Sanhedrin ch. 2—see siman 568.

“And tzitzis.” It appears to me that one may make corners of leather and tie wool tzitzis in them—see Yoreh Deah 64.
