Of linen. Even according to those who originally prohibited it, because the principal decree concerned techeiles, and today we have no techeiles. This is not comparable to an enactment made by formal vote, which cannot be rescinded without a greater beis din in wisdom and number. Where the reason for the prohibition is known, once the reason disappears, the prohibition falls away automatically. It is unlike Rabban Yochanan ben Zakkai's enactment concerning the egg, where the reason was not so apparent. Here there was no formal enactment, but only Beis Shammai's decree; since its concern no longer applies, it is permitted. See Yoreh Deah, beginning of siman 116, Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilchos Mamrim, and what I wrote in siman 468.
