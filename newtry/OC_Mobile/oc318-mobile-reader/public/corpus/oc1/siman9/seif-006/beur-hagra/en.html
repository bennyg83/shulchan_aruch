Seif 6, "some say," etc. Like Tosafos' explanation on 40a, gloss beginning "sadin," that therefore it says "exempt," meaning even with its own kind it is exempt; therefore it says "sheet," and it does not say "kilayim in tzitzis." In the Sifrei: "your garment" excludes a sheet. This is what the angel said to Rav Ketina: "a sheet," etc. This is what is stated in Shabbos 25b: his students were, etc. See Tosafos there. This is primary.

"Although," etc. For many decisors disagree and hold like Rashi's explanation, that this applies specifically to techeles.

"And he makes," etc. In accordance with his view in seif 1.

"Nevertheless," etc. In such a case there is no need to be concerned for the opinion introduced by "some say" in seif 2.
