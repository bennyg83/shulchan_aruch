(15) “From the color of the tallis” — for this [reason]: “This is my God and I will adorn Him”; and some explain because in this [case] we require “the corner’s kind.”

(16) “Even on dyed garments” — nevertheless it is fitting for one who is meticulous to make specifically four corners or a small tallis (ט"ק) white, so that when he makes the tzitzis white he fulfills everything, also because it is written “and His garment was as white as snow.” And what is the border of the garment like tekheles on our tallitot — we follow the essence of the garment.
