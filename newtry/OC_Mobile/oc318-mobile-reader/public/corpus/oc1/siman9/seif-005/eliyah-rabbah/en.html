[5] Colored, etc. Taz wrote: in any case it is fitting for one who is meticulous to make specifically four colored corners, or a white tallis, so that he fulfills everyone's requirement when he makes white tzitzis — this is my wording in Eliyahu Zuta. My grandfather the Gaon, of blessed memory, was accustomed to four colored corners called "blue" and white tzitzis; it seems his reason was a remembrance of tekhelet — so far my wording. From this you see there is no difference between Shabbat and weekday; indeed my grandfather did so on Shabbat as on weekday — unlike the abridgment of Shelah, who wrote in this in my name what did not occur to me.
