[5] Colored, etc. Taz writes that nevertheless it is proper for a meticulous person to use specifically a white four-cornered garment or a white tallis, so that by making white tzitzis he fulfills all opinions. This is what I wrote in Eliyah Zuta: My grandfather, the gaon of blessed memory, regularly wore a four-cornered garment of the color called blau, with white tzitzis. It appears that his reason was as a remembrance of techeiles. From this you can see that there is no distinction between Shabbos and a weekday; indeed, my grandfather wore the same on Shabbos as on weekdays. This excludes what Kitzur Shlah wrote in my name, which never entered my mind.
