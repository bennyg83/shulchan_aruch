Seif 5, "some say," etc. Menachos 41b, according to Rashi's explanation there and gloss beginning "meisivei"; so wrote the Rambam.
"And the Ashkenazim," etc. As stated on 38b: is dye relevant, etc. The Raavad answers in his glosses that the challenge is only regarding precedence. See Tosafos there, gloss beginning "midi," etc., and 41b, gloss beginning "ein," etc.
