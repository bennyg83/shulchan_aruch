(Seif katan 5) One should be in doubt about it — "the corner," etc. — for these are two expositions that Rava expounded, as above.

Corner, the same species as corner. And it is written in a negative commandment, etc.: "wool and linen together — make twisted cords for yourself" — and this teaches "from them," from wool and linen.

And like Rava. If so, one must fulfill and maintain one verse — either all are "corner, the same species as corner," or all are "wool and linen — make twisted cords for yourself."

But for Rav Nachman, etc. Why should it be worse? For in his view, from the Torah we require specifically wool and linen tzitzis; but other kinds, since their obligation is only rabbinic, they were lenient to make tzitzis even from other kinds; if so, if one makes some wool tzitzis that exempt from the Torah, it is preferable. See Rosh in the name of Baal HaEitur that I brought above in seif katan 3.

And nevertheless regarding travelers, etc. — meaning regarding other kinds: the Gemara concludes according to Ran that wool or linen exempt, since from the Torah they do not require tzitzis at all, but the Rabbis obligated them; therefore they discharge [the obligation] with anything whatsoever.

And even if it is not wool, as stated above in note 3 — and this supports Magen Avraham’s view.
