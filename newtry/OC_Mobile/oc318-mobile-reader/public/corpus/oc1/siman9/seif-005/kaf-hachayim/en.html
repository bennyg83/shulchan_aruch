But garments of other kinds, etc. — gloss: and some say all are obligated from the Torah, etc. In the Gemara, chapter HaTekhelet, folio 42b, there is a dispute whether other kinds are obligated from the Torah or rabbinically: Rav Nachman holds garments of other kinds are not obligated from the Torah; Rava holds garments of other kinds are also obligated from the Torah. Rif and Rambam ruled like Rav Nachman, and Maran, of blessed memory, ruled so, for such is his way; but Tosafot, Rosh, Semag, and Mordechai ruled like Rava — this is the view of our teacher, of blessed memory. In Rava's words there is a dispute: some say even for Rava wool or linen exempt them in other garment kinds, and even wool and linen together, which are kilayim, exempt; but Maharam — Hagahot Maimoni, Terumat HaDeshen, and Agur brought — hold that for Rava, in other kinds that are obligated from the Torah, only wool and linen together exempt, i.e., when there was tekhelet; but now wool or linen do not exempt other kinds, but only exempt in their kind — e.g., silk tzitzis on silk, hemp tzitzis on hemp, for we expound "corner, the same species as corner" — specifically the same species; see Beit Yosef, Bach, and Mateh Yehonatan, seif katan 3. Therefore every God-fearing person should be stringent for himself and not place on a hemp tallis except hemp tzitzis, and likewise for other kinds, to escape the dispute. And likewise Rabbi Zvi, letter 4; Chesed le'Alafim, letter 2; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 9, letter 12; Ben Ish Chai, nar"m, parashat Noah, letter 1; and see seif 6 and our words there.
