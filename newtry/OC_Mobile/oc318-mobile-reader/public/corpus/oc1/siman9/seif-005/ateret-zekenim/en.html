{Rama: Shulchan Aruch and Ashkenazim are not accustomed to make tzitzis except white.} {Rama: On this: and to remove himself from dispute, he should also make a wool tallit katan under his clothes from white wool so that the tzitzis will be white and match the tallis color, as above. The reason tzitzis must be white is that Chazal said: it teaches that the Holy One, blessed be He, wrapped Himself like a prayer leader—and that is in a tallis of tzitzis—and we find regarding Him, may He be blessed: "His garments are white as snow"—so the tzitzis too should be white, as above.}
