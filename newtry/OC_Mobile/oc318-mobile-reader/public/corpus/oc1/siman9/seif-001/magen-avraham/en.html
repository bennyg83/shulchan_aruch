“Sheep’s wool.” And ram’s wool—see Yoreh Deah siman 298. It appears to me that sheep’s wool and other kinds mixed together follow the majority, as in Kilayim, ch. Bava Basra—see there. A “ewe” is a daughter of a goat—its wool is invalid for tzitzis (Rambam ch. 2)—so it exempts only with its own kind (Beis Yosef).
