Which garments are obligated in tzitzis and which are exempt — it contains six clauses.
One is obligated from the Torah only [with] a linen garment or one of sheep’s wool; garments of other kinds are not obligated from the Torah except rabbinically. [Some say all are obligated from the Torah — and such is the halacha — Tosafos 39a, Rosh, Semag, Mordechai.]
