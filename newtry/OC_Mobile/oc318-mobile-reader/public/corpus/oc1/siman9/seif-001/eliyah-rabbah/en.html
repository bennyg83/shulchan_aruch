[1] Sheep's wool, etc., and rams. According to Rosh in chapter 2 of Bechoros, this includes the wool of a ewe descended through a ewe from a goat, the wool of a goat descended through a goat from a ewe, and the wool of a ewe descended through a goat from a ewe. See Yoreh Deah 298 and 315. Regarding the statement, “and this is the halachah,” see the end of this siman, where it is stated that a God-fearing person should be stringent although the custom is lenient. See Eliyah Zuta.
