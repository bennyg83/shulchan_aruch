But On the Taz. Here in Menachos 39b Rashi on “and He specified for you” in kilayim (Devarim 22) and negaim (Vayikra 13), and on Shabbos 26b on “since,” etc., in sheretz and death and Shevuos and tzitzis, and on “and He specified in one of them” in negaim — and behold it is true that kilayim does not need a gezeira shava, as concludes 27b there, where “tzitzis” is written explicitly; see Tosafos s.v. ela. Nevertheless, so that there not be two verses [teaching the same thing] for kilayim and negaim, one can say kilayim is superfluous for some derasha — otherwise Rav Papa would be inventing — thus Tosafos wrote there. And Rashi, Shabbos 26b s.v. ve'piret, wrote that he expounds klal u'piret, unlike in kilayim: this is not klal u'piret, for the klal “a garment” for lifting is written in Vayikra 19, and “you shall not wear shaatnez, wool and linen together” in Devarim 22, and we do not derive by klal u'piret; in our novellae on Shabbos we elaborated, and here is not the place — therefore he said “and He specified in one of them,” meaning negaim, not kilayim. However, what he mentioned about kilayim — I had thought because he concludes on Shabbos 27 that Rav Papa is inventing, and Rashi 26b s.v. he'eil omitted kilayim for this intent. And know that I had thought Rav Papa follows his view in Bechoros 17a, that a ewe (Rachel bat Avar — see Magen Avraham, note 1) whose wool does not defile with negaim; and likewise the Rambam, chapter 13, halachah 3, from tzitzis; and Rav Papa said, this applies where it is unfit for tekheles from linen that was not changed — siman 9; and Ra'avad, this applies where one is not lashed for kilayim, etc. And one can say Rav Papa holds that because of the extra [mention] of wool and linen that is permitted — for “garment” learns shaatnez from negaim, and it is superfluous to compare: what if linen without change? If not for the extra mention I would not say this hekesh; and now negaim learns by gezeira shava from kilayim that Rachel bat Avar is not lashed (and there would not be two verses), unlike R. Nachman, who learns it from “linen” or “flax,” etc. — Rav Papa would not need the hekesh if not for the extra mention; and now shaatnez comes by gezeira shava for wearing from lifting, so that “tzitzis” be superfluous for the hekesh and it not be an invention (see Tosafos Shabbos 27a s.v. ela and understand). Rather Rav Ashi concludes in Bechoros that one who braids a vine onto a fig tree is invalid, and it implies he learns the hekesh without an extra mention; and the standard of the order of the Gemara is Ravina and Rav Ashi, and on Shabbos the standard of the Talmud concludes Rav Papa is inventing — as we said. Nevertheless the Taz should not have brought kilayim, as we said; see Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilchos Issurei Mizbeach, halachah 10 — he ruled thus, and the time has come to rule here, for I have gone beyond my bounds. And see above in Magen Avraham, note 1.
