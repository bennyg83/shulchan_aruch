“But a linen garment.” The Tanna of the school of R. Yishmael: since “garments” unspecified appears in the Torah for negaim, tzitzis, and kilayim, and Scripture specifies in one of them “wool and linen” — referring to negaim — therefore all [other materials] specifically [from wool and linen]; but camel wool, rabbit wool, goat down, and “the rest” meaning silk — all are exempt from tzitzis.

“Some say all of them,” etc. They rule like Rava — Rava expounded: it is written “the corner [should be] a kind of corner” — meaning even if [the garment] is not of wool and linen, nevertheless the tzitzis should be of the garment’s material; and it is written “you shall not wear shaatnez, wool and linen together; gedilim you shall make for yourself” — how wool exempts in its kind, etc. Rema decides like this view — most latter authorities like Rava — expound “min kanaf,” from which it follows all kinds of garment are biblically obligated in tzitzis and [strings] are of the garment’s kind.
