Wool Rachel. Abhat et al. in the questions of Yabatz HB C. LB If we sew Imra in a garment of D. Katin's wings with linen threads, it is permissible to put a tuft of wool on it, just as it is permissible to sew the skins sewn with linen under a woolen garment, even though the hemp thread is wrapped around linen threads and attached to the woolen garment.
