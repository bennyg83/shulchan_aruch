Wool of sheep. See Be'er Heitev: he wrote in She'elos U'Teshuvos Ya'avetz, part 2, siman 32 — if lamb’s wool is sewn into a four-cornered cotton garment with linen threads, it is permitted to attach wool tzitzis to it, just as it is permitted to sew sewn hides with linen under a wool garment, even though the hemp thread is wound around linen threads and joins the wool garment — see there. And one may be somewhat strict about this, lest a linen thread remain hanging and they think linen tzitzis are valid — see at the end of siman 15 in the gloss, and below, notes 2 and 5.
