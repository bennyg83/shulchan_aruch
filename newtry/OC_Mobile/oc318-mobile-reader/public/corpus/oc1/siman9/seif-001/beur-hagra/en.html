Seif 1, "and some say," etc. Rav Chaviva, Rav Yehudah, and Rava disagree with Rav Nachman, and Rava is later. If because of the tanna of Rabbi Yishmael, Abaye already said that this tanna expounds, etc.; and the Rabbis also disagree with him, as written in Yevamos 5a. The first view holds that since in Shabbos 26-27 the Amoraim discuss per the tanna of Rabbi Yishmael, and Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak said it comes to include tzitzis, the halachah follows him.
