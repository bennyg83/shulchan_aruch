{Rama: Magen Avraham, seif katan 1} only in its kind. Since its wool is invalid for tzitzis — we learn in the Gemara from Scripture — there was room to say it is called wool and nevertheless the Torah excluded it and it would not exempt even in its kind; therefore it had to write "in its kind it exempts," for the verse informs us it is not wool at all and is like other kinds.
