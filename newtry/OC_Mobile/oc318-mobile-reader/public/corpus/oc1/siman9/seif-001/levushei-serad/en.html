Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. Only its own material. The Gemara derives from a verse that its wool is invalid for tzitzis. One might therefore have thought that it is still called wool, yet the Torah excluded it, so that it would not exempt even its own kind. Magen Avraham therefore needed to state that it does exempt its own kind, because the verse teaches that it is not wool at all and is treated like other materials.
