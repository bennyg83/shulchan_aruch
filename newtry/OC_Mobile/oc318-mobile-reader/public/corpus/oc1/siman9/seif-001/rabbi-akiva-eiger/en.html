{Rama: Magen Avraham, note 2} Like silk in appearance. And that which they make silk tzitzis for silk — and it is not forbidden, lest they say the wool is silk and attach wool tzitzis to silk; for when both are together before us there is recognition between one and the other. And similarly in Choshen Mishpat, siman 45, note 22, and see there. Nevertheless it is difficult: why did they not forbid sewing a silk garment with linen threads? For the same reason one might come to sew linen in a wool garment, thinking it is silk — and this requires study.
