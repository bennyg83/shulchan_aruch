They exempt On the Taz. Here: for the author, other species are rabbinic — they said wool exempts only [garments of] its species, and tzitzis [strings] they said exempt in all species; and for the other view, that all garments biblically learn from scripture like Rava — “the corner, a kind of corner” — how wool [exempts], etc., Menachos 39b. And what he wrote, “tekheles is wool” — I did not understand this; for this is the view that in kilayim one exempts only with linen, not with wool, so both can be fulfilled (Levush — see there); unlike for us, as he wrote shortly below, “gedilim” — the closeness of the matter. And one can say his words are taken from Rashi, Menachos 39b s.v. chutei tzemer — see there; it is difficult for the author: say “the corner, a kind of corner” — wool for wool and linen for linen; but linen, since tekheles requires it, dyed wool tekheles — likewise white wool exempts there, and automatically “the corner” is not precise, and the same conversely — linen for wool. Nevertheless the closeness of the corner one can explain for white wool in linen garments, and there is no need to elaborate; see beginning of Yevamos. See the introduction: and what he wrote, “two tekheles threads” for the Rambam — it is only one thread, part of it tekheles — see later authorities on this.
