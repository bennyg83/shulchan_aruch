They exempt. See Taz. According to the Mechaber, other materials are obligated D'Rabbanan, and the Sages ruled that their own materials exempt them, while wool and linen exempt all materials. According to Rama, all garments are obligated according to the Torah. Rava derives from “the corner” that the threads should match the corner, and from “wool and linen together; you shall make twisted cords for yourself” that wool and linen exempt all materials. Taz's reference to techeiles being wool is unclear to me if he follows the view that kilayim exempts only linen and not wool because both commandments can otherwise be fulfilled. Perhaps his words derive from Rashi, Menachos 39b: since techeiles requires dyed wool, white wool likewise exempts linen, and “the corner” is not exclusive. The juxtaposition may then teach white wool in linen. According to Rambam there is only one techeiles thread, part of a single thread; see Beit Yosef.
