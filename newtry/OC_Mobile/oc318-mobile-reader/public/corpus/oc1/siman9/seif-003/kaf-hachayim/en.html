Or of sheep's wool; and a ewe that is daughter of a goat — its wool is invalid for tzitzis, Rambam chapter 2; if so it is not considered wool at all and does not exempt with tzitzis except in its kind specifically: Beit Yosef. Orach Tov, letter 2; Magen Avraham, seif katan 1; Pri Megadim, Orach Chaim, letter 1; and likewise a ewe daughter of a ewe daughter of a goat is the same as a ewe daughter of a goat — Orach Tov there; and likewise a ewe whose father is a ram and mother a ewe — Pri Megadim there. If they exempt one another, see there in Pri Megadim that he left it in doubt.
