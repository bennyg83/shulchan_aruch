Note 5 — And see in the Rosh in the name of Baal HaItur — thus it should read; and it means: a tallis whose obligation is biblical requires attaching white-dyed wool; and for silk garments he holds there is no obligation except rabbinically. Therefore linen alone suffices — see there, for Ashiri, who explains that if one has no tekheles he attaches white — meaning white wool specifically, which is of the kind of tekheles. But white linen — not only because silk garments are rabbinic; for according to R. Nachman, in his view we say “either wool or linen,” for rabbinically one is exempt with any [material].
