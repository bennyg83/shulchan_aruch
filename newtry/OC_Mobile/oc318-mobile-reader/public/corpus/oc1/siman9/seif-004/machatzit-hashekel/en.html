(Seif katan 4) Of its kind, etc. even if the majority is wool. This is not like mixing kinds together — as in seif katan 1: only when mixed while still wool; but once threads were made from them they are not nullified.

Per Maharam of Rothenburg, etc. He holds that "wool and linen exempt all kinds" means specifically wool and linen together, not each separately, as written in the seif katan before this; therefore this tallit has no remedy: if one makes silk tzitzis, the warp or woof that is wool is not exempt; if one makes wool tzitzis, per Maharam the part of the tallis that is silk is not exempt.

And for this reason, etc. on hemp and linen — one must say he does not hold like "some say" whom Rama brought in seif 2; if so, per those who disagree with Maharam, one could make linen tzitzis and exempt also the part of the tallis that is hemp, for wool or linen exempt all kinds; but per Maharam, who requires wool and linen together to exempt all kinds, this tallit has no remedy. Nevertheless, per the lenient view to make a silk tallis, one should place silk threads, not wool, as above in my humble opinion. Meaning: per the lenient view, etc. — they did not heed Baal HaEitur brought in the seif katan before this, who requires specifically wool tallis and wool tzitzis; therefore they are lenient and did not heed Baal HaEitur; in any case they should heed Maharam and therefore make silk tzitzis, which are corner-kind, and not wool tzitzis, which do not exempt other kinds according to Maharam except wool and linen together.
