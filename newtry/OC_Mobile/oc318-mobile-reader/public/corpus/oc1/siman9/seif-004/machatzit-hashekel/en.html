(Seif katan 4) Of its own material, etc. Even if most is wool. This is unlike raw materials blended before spinning, as discussed in seif katan 1. Once spun into threads, they are not nullified.
According to Maharam of Rothenburg, etc. He maintains that wool and linen exempt all materials only when used together, but not separately. Therefore such a tallis has no remedy. Silk tzitzis do not exempt the woolen warp or woof, and wool tzitzis do not exempt the silk portion according to Maharam.
For this reason, etc. Regarding cotton and linen. This assumes the view that rejects Rama's “some say” in seif 2. According to those who disagree with Maharam, linen tzitzis could exempt the cotton portion as well, because wool or linen separately exempts every material. According to Maharam, however, only wool and linen together exempt other materials, so such a tallis has no remedy. Nevertheless, according to those who permit a silk tallis, one should use silk threads rather than wool threads. Meaning, although they disregard Baal HaIttur, they should at least take Maharam's view into account and use threads matching the corner, since according to Maharam wool alone does not exempt other materials.
