(Seif 4) If he put, etc., some tzitzis, etc. NB: It seems the same applies—some tzitzis of wool and some of flax one should not put, for what difference is this or that? We require all of one kind, for a corner of its kind also exempts; nevertheless combination of wool or flax does not help—the same applies that some wool and some flax does not help. Especially for us, since we have no techeles—if so flax and wool do not exempt except through the force of corner matching corner; therefore we require all of one kind. So I ruled for practical law, and so in my humble opinion; I have no leisure to extend on this now—perhaps God will grant me another time to explain more, and examine well.
