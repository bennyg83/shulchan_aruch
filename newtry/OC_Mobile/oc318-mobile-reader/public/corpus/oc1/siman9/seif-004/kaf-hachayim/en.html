Or of sheep's wool, etc. — sheep's wool and other kinds that were mixed together: we follow the majority — Magen Avraham there; and he wrote that such is the law in the last chapter of Kilayim; Mateh Yehonatan on the above Magen Avraham's words means: while they are still wool, before threads are spun from them, the minority is nullified in the majority, as in the Mishnah there, and so is ruled in Yoreh De'ah, siman 299, seif 1; unlike when threads were already spun from them, which are not nullified, as stated there — and likewise below, seif katan 4, so far. And likewise Rabbi Zvi, letter 6; and if it is half and half, Mateh Yehonatan wrote there we follow the stringent view, as there in Yoreh De'ah — meaning one should make tzitzis of wool, which exempt all kinds, as written in seif 2; and likewise Rabbi Zvi there. However, according to those who hold wool tzitzis do not exempt other kinds, as will be explained below, be"s"d, this tallis has no remedy — and likewise Rabbi Zvi there.

(4) And all this is according to Magen Avraham, who holds that even a kind in what is not its kind in garments we follow the majority; but Pri Megadim there wrote there is a dispute in this — some say the rule of majority applies only to its own kind — see there.
