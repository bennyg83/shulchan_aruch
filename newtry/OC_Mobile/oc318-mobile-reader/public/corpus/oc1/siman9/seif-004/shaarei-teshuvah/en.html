Only white. See Be'er Heitev: he wrote in Yeshu'os Shilo, beginning of Yevamos, that one should not make a large tallis green in honor of Yom Tov, but as they are accustomed — a white tallis; and even for a tallis katan some are particular that it be white only. One should be accustomed not to sew any stitching on the tallis except with white threads; and likewise the ornament they make at the top should be made only from something white — see above.
