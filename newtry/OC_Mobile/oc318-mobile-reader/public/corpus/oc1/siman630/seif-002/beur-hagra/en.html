Se'if 2, the walls of the sukkah, etc. Like the Rabbis and like Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, according to the latter version of Rava, for Rav Kahana acted in accordance with him.
Even though, etc. As written in the first chapter of Eruvin.
And if the handbreadth and the reed, etc. This is the correct reading. But in the Yerushalmi, first chapter of Sukkah, halachah 1, and at the end of the first chapter of Eruvin, it says that it does not permit because it was not made for this. See Magen Avraham. One can say that he holds that our Gemara disagrees with the Yerushalmi, for in the first chapter of Eruvin it says specifically regarding ruined openings, and does not answer that they were not made for this. Furthermore, there, regarding partitions, all agree, etc. See above, siman 362:3; and a form of a doorway is according to everyone because of partition.
And what they practiced, etc. Meaning, the reed on top is not needed, and all the more so a circular form is not needed, for according to Rav Sheshes a circular form does not help for a form of a doorway, although we do not rule like him.
For beauty. As it says: make a beautiful sukkah. There.
