And he should make for it a tzurat hapesach, etc. - See the Peri Megadim in the name of the Bach, that this is rabbinic. But regarding the generous tefach, the Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav holds that it is required by Torah law. Yet it is known that the Gemara says that a generous tefach is required in order to place it within less than three tefachim of the wall, so as to complete the measure of four tefachim. His words require examination from Tosafot on 7b, s.v. sichech al gabbei mavoy, etc., where it is explicit that the generous tefach is also rabbinic, and that by Torah law a tefach adjacent to the wall is sufficient. This requires examination. Afterwards I found that he himself retracted in Eshel Avraham se'if katan 7.
That he should place a reed upon it and upon the tefach, etc. - See the Ran, that with less than two complete walls plus a third wall of a tefach and a tzurat hapesach, it does not help to make all the walls by means of a tzurat hapesach. Although a tzurat hapesach is a complete partition regarding Shabbos and kilayim, nevertheless regarding sukkah it does not help even for the third wall, and certainly not for the other walls. The later authorities cited his words. However, the Rosh on page 19 holds that by Torah law a tzurat hapesach also helps. Still, rabbinically we specifically require a generous tefach as well, as the Peri Megadim writes in Eshel Avraham se'if katan 7. See below in se'if 6 what I wrote there in Biur Halacha.
