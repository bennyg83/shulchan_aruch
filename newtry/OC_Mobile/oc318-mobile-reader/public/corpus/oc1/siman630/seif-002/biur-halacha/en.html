(א) And make tzurat hapetach etc. — see Peri Megadim in Bach's name this is rabbinic; Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav holds recessed handbreadth is from Torah — known Gemara we require recessed to stand it less than three by a wall to complete four handbreadths — requires study on Tosafot 7b s.v. roofing over alley etc. proving recessed handbreadth is also rabbinic, from Torah a handbreadth near the wall suffices; later he retracted in Eshel Avraham sec. 7.

(ב) He places a reed on it and on the handbreadth etc. — see Ran: with less than two complete walls and third handbreadth and tzurat hapetach it does not help if he makes all walls by tzurat hapetach — though tzurat hapetach is full partition for Shabbat and kilayim, for sukkah it does not help even on third wall, a fortiori other walls; later authorities cited his words; in Rosh 19 his view is from Torah tzurat hapetach also helps; rabbinically we require specifically recessed handbreadth too [Peri Megadim Eshel Avraham sec. 7]; see below seif 6 in Biur Halakha.
