A conscientious person should take into account the Hagahos Ashri, that the walls as well should be made from materials that are not susceptible to tumah and that grow from the ground. Bach. All the later authorities, however, did not take this into account. Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. So too wrote Birkei Yosef, os 1, and in his work Machazik Berachah, os 1, whose words were cited by Shaarei Teshuvah. This is also implied by the Gemara cited below, seif 11; see there.
