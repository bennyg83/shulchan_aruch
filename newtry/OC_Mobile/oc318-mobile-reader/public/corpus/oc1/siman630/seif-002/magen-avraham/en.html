We say in Sukkah, end of page 16, that he makes a board of four and a bit and places it within less than three of the wall, and it permits because of a wall. Tosafos wrote that they hold that we say two walls according to their law. This is difficult: let him make two reeds, each one a half-handbreadth and a bit, place one within less than three near the wall and the second within less than three of the first. One can answer because we say at the end of the first chapter of Eruvin: any partition that is not of both vertical and horizontal elements is not a partition. These are their words. The Maharsha wrote that this is according to Rabbi Yosei bar Yehudah, but we rule that reed by reed within less than three is a partition for a sukkah, as stated in the Gemara, page 7. But a horizontal partition with gaps less than three does not help. So wrote the Rosh, chapter 2, siman 40. This is a great pressure: that horizontal does not help because it helps only for a caravan, but vertical helps even though it too helps only for a caravan. The Ran wrote regarding a board of four and a bit, etc., that it is speaking of a sukkah made like an alleyway, and specifically with a board of four, where the standing material has a fitting measure; but with a handbreadth placed in the middle, less than three to this side and less than three to that side, it is not enough, since the standing material does not have a fitting measure. These are his words. So too he wrote regarding the need for a form of a doorway, that we require a broad handbreadth within less than three and a form of a doorway of three. So the Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote, that we require seven handbreadths, the valid measure of a sukkah. If so, one must say that the Baraisa which teaches that reed by reed within less than three helps is when he makes four walls; but when he makes three walls, he needs two complete adjacent walls, and the third with a handbreadth and a form of a doorway. So the Ran wrote explicitly regarding "two according to their law"; see there. If so, we need not press the words of Tosafos to say that they are not according to the halachah, as the Rashal wrote. Rather, they too hold that since it is a partition only for a caravan, as written in siman 360, it is not a complete partition and does not help unless there are four walls of reed by reed within less than three. So too it is implied in the Tur, se'if 7, and this is the intent of the Rosh and the Tur. What Tosafos wrote, that there is no distinction, was before we knew this reasoning. Opposite the wall. This means opposite the end of the second corner, like this [a diagram appears in the source], a handbreadth, and as I wrote above in the name of the Ran and Hagahos Maimoniyos.
And the wall reaches. This requires analysis: what is the wall doing here? The reed on top rests upon the handbreadth and the reed opposite it, as I wrote above; and so it is explicit in the Mordechai: "and if the two reeds reach," etc. Nevertheless, this requires analysis, for in the Yerushalmi it states: a corner [meaning a woven vine stretched from tree to tree] shelters in a sukkah, but the end of the s'chach does not shelter. What is the difference between this and that? This was made for that, and this was not made for that. Until here. If so, how did the Mordechai and Hagahos Maimoniyos permit it even though it was not made for that? This requires analysis. Therefore one should be stringent. See the Tur, se'if 7, and siman 363:26.
