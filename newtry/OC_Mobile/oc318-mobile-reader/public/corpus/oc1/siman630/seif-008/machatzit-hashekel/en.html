(Se'if katan 8) If he inserted, etc. For when the partitions are not recognizable, since the roof projects over the partitions of the house, even if he placed the poles opposite the end of the wall and not on the roof in the place where the partition beneath it has already ended, nevertheless we do not say gud asik, etc. This is explicit in siman 345 regarding Shabbos, and from there we derive it for sukkah by a kal vachomer.
For even regarding a pillar, etc., and this is because of gud asik.
As written in siman 633, regarding a sukkah that is higher than twenty. If he built a platform in it to reduce its height, and the platform is four amot away from the walls, so that there is no principle of dofen akumah, even if the platform itself is ten tefachim high it is nevertheless invalid, even on top of the platform. We do not say: granted, he cannot combine it with the sukkah walls, since they are more than four amot away, but let us say gud asik mechitzata regarding the platform itself, so that it will not need the sukkah walls at all, as we say regarding a pillar for Shabbos. Thus we see that there is a case where, although for Shabbos we say gud asik, nevertheless in such a case regarding sukkah we do not say gud asik. All the more so where the partitions are not recognizable: since for Shabbos we do not say gud asik there, certainly for sukkah in such a case we do not say gud asik.
For we say chokekin. That means that if there is a thick square pillar, and it has enough material to carve out so that it would become like the corner-posts of wells, namely a double-post shaped like a Greek gamma, it would help. But we do not rule that way; rather, we do not say chokekin.
