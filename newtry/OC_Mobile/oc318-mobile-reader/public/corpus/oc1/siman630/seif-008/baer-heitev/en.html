Portico. The Turei Zahav wrote: one who wants to make a sukkah in his house and break open the roof above, as people do, with the walls of the house serving as the walls of the sukkah, namely he wants to make the sukkah in the corner of the east wall and the south wall, and there is one beam lying all year above opposite it along the length of the house near those corners, and the sukkah will be in that corner section until the beam. It emerges that the sukkah has only two walls of the house, and for the third it has only the beam above, regarding which we say that it descends and seals downward, so that it will have three walls, which is enough. He need only place two reeds beneath the beam, one near the wall of the house and one at the place where the third wall ends, meaning the place where the sukkah's s'chach ends. The reason for these reeds is so that there will be a form of a doorway in the third wall because of the ceiling over the two reeds, for here there is no actual third partition, only by reason of the edge of the roof descending and sealing. Therefore a form of a doorway is required. See there.
