The 16th wrote that whoever wants to make a sukkah in his house and break the roof over it, as is customary, and the walls of the house will be the walls of the sukkah, that is, who wants to make the sukkah at the angles of the East Wall and the South Wall, and there is one beam placed all year round up against it along the length of the house near those angles, and the sukkah will be in the 8th part of the angles up to the beam. Below, there are three partitions in this, only sixty two reeds, one at the wall of the house, and one at the end of the third partition, that is, at the place where the roof of the roof ends, so that there is a gap on the third side because of the ceiling on the two reeds.
