8) Ibid., Rama's gloss: From something that has a foul odor. This is forbidden lest he leave the sukkah and depart. After the fact, however, it is valid, even for s'chach, unless the odor is so foul that a person cannot tolerate it. In that case, it is possible that it is invalid according to the Torah, since we require, "You shall dwell," in the manner in which you ordinarily reside, as written above, siman 629, seif 14, and in our words there, with God's help.
