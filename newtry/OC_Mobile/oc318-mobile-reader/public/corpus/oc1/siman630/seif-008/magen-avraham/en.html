From inside it is valid. For we say that the edge of the roof descends and seals.
Others disagree. They hold that even through posts we do not say that the edge of the roof descends and seals, since the two walls are not joined like a gamma-shape, as written at the end of siman 361. It also implies that the beam must be four wide, as written there. The Bach inferred from the language of the Tur that a width of one handbreadth is enough; see there, but one should not be lenient. According to what is written in Chochmas Shlomo, it is invalid because the roof was made only for the needs of the portico and not for the needs of the sukkah. Maharsha disagrees with him, and so wrote the Bach, that specifically when one makes the sukkah adjacent to the portico it is invalid, since the roof was made inward and not outward. But if two walls are like a gamma-shape and on the third side posts project one handbreadth, it helps. However, it is implied in the Rosh that a form of a doorway is also required. The Bach wrote that this is specifically when it is visible from outside and flush from inside; but if it is visible inside and flush outside, it does not require a form of a doorway. This is not decisive; see there, for I have abbreviated.
