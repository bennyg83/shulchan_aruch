(12) [Seif 2] Sukkah walls if two etc. — we taught (Sukkah 6b) two as required and third even handbreadth needs tzurat hapetach; if two walls adjacent make recessed handbreadth and place less than three from one wall (meaning one of walls) and place reed opposite wall end (meaning end of wall) and place reed on it and on handbreadth — valid — Tur; meaning place reed at end of side with handbreadth opposite end of opposite wall and place reed on this end-reed and on handbreadth for tzurat hapetach. This needing three walls and third even handbreadth we learn in Gemara from verses: written in sukkah in sukkah in sukkot — two deficient one full with vav counts two, behold four, one for itself three for walls; halakhah to Moses at Sinai, reduced third to handbreadth — see there.
