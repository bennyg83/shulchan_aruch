One can use a friend to constitute a wall in a valid sukkah, even on the holiday, as long as he does not know that he is standing there to be a wall. On a weekday, even if he knows, it is fine.Even on the holiday, this is only forbidden for the three permitting walls, but it is permitted for the fourth wall (Raavad's notes, Maggid ch.4 of Sukkah).
