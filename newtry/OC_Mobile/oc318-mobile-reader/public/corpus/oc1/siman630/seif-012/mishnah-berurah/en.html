(54) From another person, etc. Specifically another person may be used. It is forbidden on Yom Tov to position an animal as the third wall that validates the sukkah, even if one does not tie it with a knot but only with a bow.
(55) To validate it, even on Yom Tov. For example, if a sukkah wall fell and he wishes to fulfill the mitzvah of sukkah by eating and drinking there, he may ask a person to stand there until he finishes eating. The unqualified wording of the poskim appears to imply that if the person agrees to stand there, even without knowing the reason, we need not be concerned that he may leave in the interim. One certainly cannot rely upon a non-Jew in this manner.
(56) But on Chol HaMoed, etc., it is permitted. For one may construct a partition on Chol HaMoed.
(57) But for a fourth wall it is permitted. Since the sukkah was already valid, adding the fourth wall is not considered construction, but merely adding to a temporary shelter, which is permitted. For the same reason, one may make a fourth wall from utensils on Yom Tov. A third wall is forbidden, because it validates the sukkah and is included in the prohibition against constructing a temporary shelter on Yom Tov. Gemara.
