(12) [Levush] Open alley etc. — Shulchan Aruch wrote stam wet; later authorities explain only when not open; appears this explains Rama: permission only where lechi and posts permit for Shabbat — end; he holds only when not open, but open forbidden on Shabbat forbidden in sukkah; regarding posts his intent: permitted only for pilgrims, not for us as Darkei Moshe in Ran's name — even if only for their animals they were lenient in sukkah for people; for us not permitted — so Ran, unlike Nachalat Tzvi; appears therefore Levush omitted Rama's words. One could say he explains only when lechi stands at alley and posts in field not courtyard — included in what he wrote end this seif; if so why write only posts not lechi itself. For law primary as Levush ruled both open alley and posts; Rabbeinu Yerucham agreed; for posts in courtyard he wrote leniency there — therefore lenient in pressing situation.
