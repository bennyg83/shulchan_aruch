He roofed over an alleyway that has a side-post, etc. Note: This law requires analysis, for it is not a sukkah fit for seven days, since after Shabbos it is invalid as a sukkah and is fit only for Shabbos. If so, it is not a sukkah fit for seven days. In particular according to Rashi's explanation in the Talmud, by Torah law such a case would not be a sukkah were it not for Shabbos; see there. If so, it is not fit by Torah law and according to everyone it should be invalid. Even if it were rabbinic, some poskim rule like Rabbi Yehudah, that even where it is not rabbinically fit it is invalid. See Sha'ar HaMelech, Hilchos Sukkah, who brought that Riatz Geyus ruled like Rabbi Yehudah. If so, this requires analysis: why did Rava say that he fulfilled the obligation? And if Rava holds like Rabbi Meir, how did the Rif rule like Rabbi Yehudah? This requires analysis for now. See what I wrote in my novellae in siman 637.
