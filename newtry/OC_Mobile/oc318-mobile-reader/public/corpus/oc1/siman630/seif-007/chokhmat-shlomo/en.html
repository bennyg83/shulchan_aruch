There, seif 7. "If he placed s'chach over a mavoi that has a lechi, etc." NB: This law requires study, for it is not a sukkah fit for seven days. Since after Shabbat it is invalid as a sukkah and is fit only for Shabbat, it is not a sukkah fit for seven. In particular, according to Rashi's explanation in the Gemara, such a sukkah would not be a Torah-valid sukkah were it not for Shabbat; see there. If so, it is not fit by Torah law, and according to everyone it should be invalid. Even if the invalidity were rabbinic, there are poskim who rule like Rabbi Yehudah that even where it is not fit only rabbinically, it is invalid. See Shaar HaMelech, Hilchot Sukkah, who brought that the Ritz Giat ruled like Rabbi Yehudah. If so, it requires study why Rava said he fulfilled the obligation. And if Rava holds like Rabbi Meir, how did the Rif rule like Rabbi Yehudah? This requires study for now.
