Extend. See Turei Zahav. The Levush omitted those who validate; see Eliyah Rabbah, because the primary view follows those who invalidate. This requires analysis, for if one has no sukkah except one like this at the edge of a roof, he should sit there and eat, for it is an unresolved question, and here we rule stringently. See Sukkah 4 and the Beis Yosef about this, and it should not have been omitted. See Magen Avraham, letter 8, and with Hashem's help it will be explained there.
