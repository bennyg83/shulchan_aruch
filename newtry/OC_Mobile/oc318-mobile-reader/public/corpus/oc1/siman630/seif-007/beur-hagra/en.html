Se'if 7, and one should not permit, etc. According to the Ran, this applies specifically in a place where it is permitted to carry on Shabbos with a side-post, such as a closed alleyway, as the Tosafos mentioned above wrote; and with boards around wells, specifically for those going up for the festivals, or even nowadays for a mitzvah matter where water is not common, as written in Eruvin 21a and as written in Hagahos Ashri there. But from the words of the Rif, who omitted there the laws of boards around wells and wrote it here, one must say that he holds like Tosafos there, s.v. sichech al gabei passin, etc. The Rosh is more lenient, that even a side-post helps even in an open alleyway, for this reason. From the language of the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch it implies that they hold like the Rosh. What is written here, "and one should not, etc.," means specifically when the boards are in the public domain. See Magen Avraham. They inferred this because it says "over an alleyway" and "over boards around wells," and does not say "like boards around wells" and over two partitions. See the Ran.
