He roofed over an alleyway. The Mechaber wrote plainly like the language of the Rambam, Rashi, and the Rosh, who hold that even in an open alleyway we validate it, for by Torah law a side-post is a full partition and it therefore has three partitions. Although rabbinically it is forbidden to carry with three partitions on Shabbos, regarding sukkah it is permitted because of the mitzvah of sukkah. The view of the Ran and Tosafos is that this applies specifically in a closed alleyway, as I wrote. Regarding boards around wells, although they permitted them only for those going up for the festivals, nevertheless since by Torah law they are a full partition and the Sages decreed, regarding sukkah they did not decree; so wrote Tosafos. This is difficult: if so, why did they not permit an open alleyway for this reason? One can say that there it is different, since it has no permission at all for those going up for the festivals, whereas here there is permission for those going up for the festivals.
In the place of the side-post, etc. Meaning specifically when the side-post stands by the alleyway, and the boards specifically in a field and not in a courtyard.
