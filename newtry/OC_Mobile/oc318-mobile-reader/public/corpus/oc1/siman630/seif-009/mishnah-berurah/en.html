(42) Within three tefachim, etc. We apply lavud, so the gap is treated as closed and there are ten tefachim.
(43) Even if the roof is very high. We extend the walls upward, as though they reached the sechach.
(44) Provided that it is within three tefachim. We apply lavud horizontally. If the gap is three tefachim, the sukkah is invalid, because even where the walls actually reach the height of the sechach, a horizontal gap of three tefachim between them and the sechach invalidates it. All the more so here.
(45) It is invalid. This applies even if the walls are ten tefachim high or more, because any wall beneath which goats can pass is not regarded as a wall at all.
