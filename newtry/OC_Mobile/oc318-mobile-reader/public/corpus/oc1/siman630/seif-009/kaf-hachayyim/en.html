9) Those who make a sukkah near a privy or a refuse heap because they lack sufficient space, and a foul odor reaches them, fulfill their obligation according to the Torah, but they should not recite Birkas HaMazon there, since the requirement to recite the blessing in its place is rabbinic. If one has another place, he certainly should not make it in this location. If the odor does not reach him but he can see the privy, it is forbidden. Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 4. See above, siman 79, seif 2, and in our words there, with God's help.
