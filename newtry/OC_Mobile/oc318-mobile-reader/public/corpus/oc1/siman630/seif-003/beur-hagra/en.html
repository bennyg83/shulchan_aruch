Se'if 3, if they were, etc. For we rule like Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi everywhere.
And also in this, etc. Since Rava said his statement after the case of a sukkah made like an alleyway, we learn that it applies to all of them.
And some say, etc. Since there is the measure of a wall by means of lavud, a form of a doorway is not needed, for a form of a doorway is needed only to complete the measure of a wall. The reason he interrupted it is because he wanted to conclude the matter and distinguish between two adjacent walls. The first view holds that since there are not seven except through lavud, it is not a wall without a form of a doorway, for throughout the sukkah lavud does not help for "two according to their law," etc. For if not, why did it say that the third may be even a handbreadth? It should need four handbreadths, since lavud is a complete partition. Furthermore, two reeds would suffice, as Tosafos wrote: place one within less than three and the second within less than three, and each should be half a handbreadth and a bit. Why is a form of a doorway needed? See Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman and se'if 5. Therefore a form of a doorway is needed. This is what he wrote: "but if, etc."
