And also in this, etc. There is a scribal error here in the Shulchan Aruch and the gloss, and this is how it should read: "that he place a reed from the board to the other wall" [gloss]. But if there is a wall of seven without lavud, one does not need a form of a doorway here until the end of the wall, etc. After this gloss belongs: "and some say that this form of a doorway is not needed." This is the view that the Beis Yosef brings in the name of the Rosh, that here, where there is a repair with a board of four, it suffices without a form of a doorway, and a form of a doorway is needed only in se'if 2, where the repair is with a board of one handbreadth, which is a lesser repair. This is unlike here, where there is a good repair with a board of four. The first view holds that here, since it is open through-and-through, it is worse than above and requires a good repair; the good repair here is no better than the lesser repair there, and therefore here too a form of a doorway is required.
