There A partition of vertical and horizontal elements is not a partition, etc., for it was permitted only for a caravan, as explained above in siman 360. Therefore this case, where it is made reed by reed, is only vertical, and does not help. This is surprising regarding the answer, for they brought a proof from that case on page 7, that reed by reed helps even though it is only vertical. About this Maharsha answered that the case on page 7 follows the practical halachah, but this statement on page 16 wants to satisfy also the view of Rabbi Yosei bar Rabbi Yehudah, who holds that a vertical partition is not a complete partition.
