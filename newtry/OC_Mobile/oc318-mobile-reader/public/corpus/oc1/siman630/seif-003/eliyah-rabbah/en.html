[3] [Levush] Even on Shabbos, etc. So wrote the Tur, but the Shulchan Aruch omitted it, and this requires analysis. One should not say that he holds it is obvious, since it requires a form of a doorway, for nevertheless he should have taught us that even a rabbinic prohibition is not present in moving it, as the Olas Shabbos and Beis Yosef wrote. Furthermore, if so, he should have written it in se'if 3, since some say that a form of a doorway is not needed. Therefore it appears to me that the Shulchan Aruch was concerned for the words of the Ran, whom the Beis Yosef brought, who wrote that one should be stringent in practice not to carry on Shabbos; and in Darkei Moshe he brought this without qualification.
