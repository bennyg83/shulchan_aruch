(3) [Levush] Even on Shabbat etc. — so Tur wrote; Shulchan Aruch omitted — requires study; do not say it is obvious since tzurat hapetach required — nevertheless should teach even rabbinic prohibition of tilting does not apply like removing Shabbat roof — Beit Yosef; further if obvious should write in seif 3 since some say tzurat hapetach not needed; therefore appears Shulchan Aruch feared Ran whom Beit Yosef brought who wrote to be stringent in practice not to tilt on Shabbat — so Darkei Moshe brought in stam.
