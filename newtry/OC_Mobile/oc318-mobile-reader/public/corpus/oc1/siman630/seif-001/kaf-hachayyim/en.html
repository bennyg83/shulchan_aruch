1) [Seif 1] All materials are valid for the walls. For whenever Scripture writes sukkah, it implies the s'chach, since a wall is not called a sukkah. Although we derive the walls from the words basukkos, basukkos, basukkos, in Sukkah 6b, we learn this from the superfluity of the verses and not from their plain implication. Therefore, the verse, "You shall make the festival of Sukkos for yourself when you gather in from your threshing floor," refers to the s'chach. Rashi, Sukkah 12a; Beit Yosef; Taz, s.k. 1.
