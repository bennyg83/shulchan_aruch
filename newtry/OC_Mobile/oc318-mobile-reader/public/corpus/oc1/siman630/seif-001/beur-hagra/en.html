Se'if 1, all things, etc. This is according to the Rosh's view, who wrote that permanence is the main reason. But according to the ruling of the Rashba and the Ran in the view of the Rif, all of them are invalid for walls when one relies upon them, except those that are not commonly used for s'chach, or those that are invalid only rabbinically. The Rashba explained there that "all of them" means even if he did not permit them, since they are invalid only rabbinically. So the Darkei Moshe wrote in the name of the Or Zarua, and he wrote that this is also brought there from the Yerushalmi. But in the Yerushalmi, first chapter, end of halachah 6, it is written the reverse: Rabbi Yudan in the name of Rabbi Chama bar Chanina: "And you shall screen the ark with the curtain" - from here that the wall is called s'chach; from here that one may make walls from something that receives impurity. It seems that the text in the Yerushalmi should read: from here that one may not make, etc.; and so it seems, since it is called s'chach. But there is no proof from there, for there it follows the approach of Rabbi Yoshiyah in the Gemara, 7b.
And one need not, etc. Since they need not be things that grow from the ground, what relevance is there to the direction of their growth?
Nevertheless not, etc. As above, siman 629:14, and they share one reason. But those whose leaves fall are invalid only for s'chach, because they fall onto the table. Rashba.
Or something, etc. As written there, se'if 12 in the gloss: "and everything, etc., we judge, etc."
