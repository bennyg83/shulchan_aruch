At the beginning of the siman, in Magen Avraham, page 6: He makes a strip of four tefachim and a bit and places it, etc. The text should read: for we know the reasoning that a partition of vertical elements is not a partition. This means that according to the initial assumption, that whatever is considered a partition for Shabbos in the case of a caravan helps for sukkah, there is no reason to distinguish between making four walls and making three. Therefore Tosafot answered that a partition made of vertical elements alone, which helps for Shabbos only for a caravan, does not help here. It is then reasonable to distinguish: if he makes four walls for the sukkah, which is an added strength, then partitions of vertical elements alone can help, since they help for Shabbos at least for a caravan. But when he makes only three walls, the baraita too agrees that partitions of vertical elements alone do not help, since they help only for a caravan.
