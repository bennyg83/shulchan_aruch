[6] Where there is no middle wall, etc. This appears to me to be the explanation of Tosafos in Sukkah, page 5, "he roofed, etc.," on page 4b, and this is its language: it is speaking of a closed alleyway on three sides, and he roofed toward the side of the side-post and inward, but did not roof toward the side of the houses. Until here is their language. Meaning: he roofed from the wall of the alleyway to the wall opposite it on the side of the side-post, but did not roof upon the middle wall; nevertheless a broad handbreadth suffices, since there is a middle wall opposite it. Examine carefully. This excludes Maharsha, who wondered about Tosafos that since it is made like an open alleyway, it should require a board of four. He did not see these words of the Shulchan Aruch, and they are from the Ran, that since there is a middle wall opposite it, it is like a gamma-shape.
