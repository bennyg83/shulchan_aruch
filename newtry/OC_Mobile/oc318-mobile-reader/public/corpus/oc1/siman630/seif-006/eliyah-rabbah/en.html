(6) That there is no middle wall etc. — this appears to me Tosafot Sukkah 5 roofing etc. page 4b — this is its language: it deals with closed alley on three sides, roofed toward lechi side inward toward houses side not roofed until here — explanation he roofed from alley wall to opposite wall at lechi side and did not roof on middle wall — anyway recessed handbreadth suffices since opposite middle wall exists; unlike Rashba who wondered on Tosafot since made like open alley we require four planks Shulchan Aruch did not see — they are Maran: since middle wall exists it is like gamma; end quote.
