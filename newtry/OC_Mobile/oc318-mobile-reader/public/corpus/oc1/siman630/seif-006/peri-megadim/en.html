All. See Turei Zahav and Rashi, Sukkah 7a, and so wrote the Magen Avraham, letter 6; see there. See Levush 13: if it does not have a form of a doorway - this is a scribal error, and it should read: even if it has a form of a doorway. According to the Rambam, of blessed memory, more than ten does not help through a form of a doorway when the breached area is greater than the standing material. Know that even with two walls and a board of four, this law applies. See Beis Yosef.
