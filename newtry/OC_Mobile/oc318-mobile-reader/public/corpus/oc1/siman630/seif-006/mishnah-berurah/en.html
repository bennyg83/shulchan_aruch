(29) Whether at the edge of the roof, etc., it is invalid. Although regarding Shabbos all agree that we extend the walls upward, regarding sukkah this opinion maintains that actual walls are required.
(30) Because we say that the walls extend upward, etc. Meaning, it is as though the edge of the house wall raises its walls upward, so that the sukkah has walls. This applies only when the roof does not project beyond the house. If the roof projects outward, even regarding Shabbos we do not extend the walls upward, as stated above in siman 345, seif 16.
(31) The walls extend upward. This applies only when the sukkah stands precisely at the roof’s edge. If it is set back, even by less than three tefachim, all agree that we do not combine lavud with extending the wall upward. The halachah follows the first opinion, that regarding sukkah we never extend the walls upward.
