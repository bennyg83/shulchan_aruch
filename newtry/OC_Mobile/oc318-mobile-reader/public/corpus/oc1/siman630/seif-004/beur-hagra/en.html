Se'if 4, in what case, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. sichech al gabei mavui: "And if you say, an alleyway, etc." They answer: "It is always, etc." The Gemara says: if so, let it teach, etc. Evidently in such a case a broad handbreadth helps, even though the third wall does not help at all regarding the sukkah.
