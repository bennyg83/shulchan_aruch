For beauty. See Turei Zahav and the Beis Yosef in the name of the Mordechai. In Ateres Zekeinim he wrote that one can say this specifically means a round form of a doorway, in order to exempt from mezuzah according to everyone; see there, and Yoreh De'ah 286:11 and 287:2. This requires analysis, for since the halachah is that a sukkah is temporary and exempt from mezuzah like the Sages against Rabbi Yehudah, why should one make a round form of a doorway for this reason? People may think that if there is a height of ten handbreadths before the circle it is obligated in mezuzah, and will recite a blessing in vain to affix a mezuzah, etc.
