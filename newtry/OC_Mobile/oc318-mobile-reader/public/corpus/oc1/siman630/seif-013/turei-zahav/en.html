One who supports his sukkah etc. — this is mishnah: supports sukkah on bed legs — valid; R. Yosi: if cannot stand alone invalid — what is R. Yosi's reason; R. Zeira and R. Abba bar Mamal: one says no fixedness, one says because he supports on impurity; Abaye: taught only supports, but roofing on bed valid; Tur explained all Shulchan Aruch here: apparently deals with bed whose head has surrounding sides above — called in foreign language headboard — main difference between first and second cases in Tur's words: in both he makes walls from bed sides called legs and roofs above; bed ground is sukkah ground; in first sekhakh rested on those walls themselves — if bed walls removed sekhakh falls — invalid for R. Yosi: no fixedness from bed ground to sekhakh no nine handbreadths; other says because supports on impurity; in second sekhakh also on bed walls but not rested on them — planted stakes in ground, sekhakh on stakes — valid though nine handbreadths from bed ground — sekhakh still stands, ground ground is sukkah ground — we do not care bed air reduced; Rosh: R. Yosi comes to explain Tanna Kamma, not disputing — no reason Tanna validates less than ten handbreadths — dira serucha — therefore we hold like R. Yosi. Further challenged for one who says impurity: roofing on stone wall invalid though not growing from ground — Ran answers: reason forbidding impurity support is decree lest roof with it; stone walls no decree — uncommon to roof; Rosh ruled like no fixedness — difference only whether ten handbreadths from bed to sekhakh; not concerned with impurity support; Beit Yosef proved from Rif; Tur and Shulchan Aruch — not like Ran concerned with supporter; see siman 629 seif 8 on placing ladder.
