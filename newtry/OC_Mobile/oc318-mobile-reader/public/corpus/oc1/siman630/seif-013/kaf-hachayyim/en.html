If the walls of the sukkah were two, etc. It is permitted to carry within it even on Shabbos, because just as it is considered a partition regarding sukkah, it is considered a partition regarding Shabbos. Tur. This is from the Gemara there, 7a. Rashi explains that although three partitions are required on Shabbos to create a private domain, on the Shabbos of Sukkos one may rely upon the walls of the sukkah. Thus, if one made a sukkah in the public domain adjacent to the entrance of his house, and it has only two full walls and a third of even one tefach, he may carry from it into his house and from his house into it; see there. The Levush writes that although for Shabbos purposes outside the context of sukkah one requires a four-tefach board, a crossbeam, or a sidepost at the fourth wall, in a sukkah it is permitted without any additional correction. This is because, since it is considered a partition by virtue of the received law regarding sukkah, it is likewise considered a partition regarding Shabbos. Nevertheless, the Ran writes that in practice it is proper to be stringent, as explained in the Beit Yosef; see there. So too wrote Chayei Adam, klal 146, os 3, that this leniency applies only in pressing circumstances, but otherwise it is proper to be stringent. So too wrote Mishna Berurah, os 11.
