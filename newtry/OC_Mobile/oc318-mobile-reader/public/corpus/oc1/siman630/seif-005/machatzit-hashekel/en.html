(Se'if katan 5) The breached area, etc. A significant measure means a width of four tefachim, as stated in se'if 3 regarding an alleyway.
Four walls, for then even reed by reed is permitted, provided that there is not three tefachim between one and the next. This is indeed the Magen Avraham's conclusion, following the Ran, to distinguish between three walls and four walls, as the Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of the siman.
