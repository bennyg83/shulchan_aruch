The breached area will be greater. It appears to me that in any event we require in the standing material the measure of a significant place, or that he make four walls, as written in se'if 2.
Greater than the standing material. Meaning, together with the two breached walls; but if, in those two complete walls themselves, the breached area is greater than their standing material, it is forbidden.
At the corners. Meaning, at all the corners. But if two partitions are connected, it is permitted. So the Ran wrote explicitly, as written in se'if 2.
