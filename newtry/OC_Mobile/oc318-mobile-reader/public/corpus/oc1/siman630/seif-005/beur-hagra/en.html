Se'if 5, only that, etc. For a form of a doorway does not help for the measure of a sukkah, even though it helps for Shabbos. Otherwise, what leniency is there regarding the third wall, about which the law came and reduced it, etc.? It would be a complete wall. Furthermore, why do I need a handbreadth, since it has a form of a doorway? Regarding Shabbos, thin reeds are enough, provided only that it be strong enough to hold even the smallest door, even of straw. According to this, at the corners too it does not help, for we require two adjacent walls, as written: what is different there, etc. See the Rif, and one must not have a form of a doorway at all the corners. See Magen Avraham.
