5) Maharil said that he saw his teacher, Maharam Shalom, mark the boards for the walls of the sukkah with alef, beis, gimel, in order to know the order in which they stood and not change them from year to year. Hagahos Derashos Maharil, Hilchos Sukkah; Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos Beit Yosef, os 3. So too is it stated in the Yerushalmi concerning the boards of the Mishkan: R' Ami said, "You shall erect the Mishkan according to its rule." Is there a rule for pieces of wood? Rather, a board that merited being placed in the north shall always be placed in the north, and similarly, one placed in the south shall be placed in the south. Shelah, 245a; Eliyah Rabbah, os 17. Certainly, however, this does not invalidate the sukkah and is only an act of piety. See above, siman 8, os 17.
