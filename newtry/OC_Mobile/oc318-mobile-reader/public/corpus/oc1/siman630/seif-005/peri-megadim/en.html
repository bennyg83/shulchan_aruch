Also Taz emendation — but should precede Mechaber's words; some say tzurat hapetach not needed etc. — halakhah like first view stam that even four planks need tzurat hapetach; when seven walls no tzurat hapetach needed; to me there are thoughts — I will speak and be refreshed. (1) Two walls opposite — four planks or Torah or rabbinic — apparently no dispute. (2) If handbreadth and something without tzurat hapetach — apparently at least along seven handbreadths valid; Levush possible all without tzurat hapetach even in seven measure invalid — requires study. (3) Levush: if seven handbreadths no tzurat hapetach in seif 2 at handbreadth by two Eruvin walls — Mishna Berurah seif 3 why not greater in seif 2. (4) If third wall seven and distant less than three from walls — perhaps valid, no tzurat hapetach needed. (5) Know recessed handbreadth or four planks in tzurat hapetach implies tzurat hapetach width at least four handbreadths from handbreadth to second reed else not an opening — see Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 4 letter 3 — see there; nevertheless tzurat hapetach alone without handbreadth and four planks does not help though Shabbat and kilayim tzurat hapetach helps — sukkah law requires recessed handbreadth at least, tzurat hapetach rabbinic stringent as Bach — tzurat hapetach alone in third wall place not so Ran and Beit Yosef; see Hagahot Maimoniyot; we hold as Ran wrote and more in Magen Avraham.
