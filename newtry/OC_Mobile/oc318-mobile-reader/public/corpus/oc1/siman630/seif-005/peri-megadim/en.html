See Turei Zahav, who emends that the gloss should come before the Mechaber's words: "and some say that a form of a doorway is not needed, etc." For the halachah follows the first view when stated plainly, that even a board of four needs a form of a doorway, and when there is a wall of seven, no form of a doorway is needed. To me, the poor one, there are thoughts here; I said I would speak and find relief. First: two partitions opposite one another with a board of four - is this by Torah law or rabbinic? Seemingly, there is no dispute about a law given to Moshe at Sinai. See Sukkah 6 and 7 and the Rosh, for it implies that they are balanced, hence by Torah law. Second: if there is a handbreadth and a bit without a form of a doorway, seemingly it is in any case valid along a measure of seven handbreadths, and so it appears in the Levush. But perhaps whenever there is no form of a doorway, even with a measure of seven handbreadths it is invalid; this requires analysis. Third: the Levush wrote that if there are seven handbreadths, no form of a doorway is needed in se'if 2, with a handbreadth near two adjacent walls, while the Rav wrote it in se'if 3; why not teach a greater novelty in se'if 2? Fourth: if there is a wall of seven handbreadths and it is less than three handbreadths distant from one of the walls, one can say it is valid and does not need a form of a doorway. Fifth: know that with a broad handbreadth or a board of four together with a form of a doorway, it implies that the form of a doorway must be at least four handbreadths wide, from the handbreadth or board to the second reed; otherwise it is not an opening. See Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 4, letter 3; see there. However, a form of a doorway alone, without a handbreadth or a board of four, does not help. Although for Shabbos and kilayim a form of a doorway helps, for sukkah the law requires at least a broad handbreadth, and we require a form of a doorway rabbinically as a stringency, as I wrote above in the name of the Bach. But a form of a doorway alone in place of a third wall does not help; so wrote the Ran, of blessed memory. See Beis Yosef and Hagahos Maimoniyos regarding this. We rule as the Ran wrote in this matter, and with Hashem's help in Magen Avraham it will be explained further.
