A partition of vertical or horizontal elements helps for Shabbos, and here two ropes should have been enough, except that one can say regarding sukkah we require somewhat of a dwelling. What the Shulchan Aruch wrote, "higher than the roof," means higher than its s'chach, for we say lavud upward and downward whenever the partition has four handbreadths and two small amounts. See Magen Avraham, and with Hashem's help I will explain further. At the beginning of the siman and in letter 17, regarding sukkah we require two complete adjacent walls and a third specifically of one handbreadth, not reeds within less than three; vertical elements do not help.
