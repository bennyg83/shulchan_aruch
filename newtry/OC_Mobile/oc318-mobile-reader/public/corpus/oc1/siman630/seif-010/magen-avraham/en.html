One who makes his sukkah, etc. But he did not support it upon the tree, for if so one may not ascend to it on Yom Tov, as written in siman 628:3; see what I wrote there, unlike the Bach. But if he supported the s'chach upon an animal, it is invalid because it has no permanence, as written in se'if 11. See Tosafos.
Strong. It is somewhat implied in Tosafos that if most of the wall is made from the strong material, it is valid. See se'if 5.
An ordinary wind. Even if it stands inside a house where there is no wind, it is not a partition. See what I wrote in siman 315.
Reeds within less than three. If so, although the wind moves the sheets, nevertheless the partitions remain through the reeds [Bach]. According to this, what is written in se'if 1 means that if they made only three walls, reeds without sheets do not help; see there.
