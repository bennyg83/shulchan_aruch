If one makes his sukkah between trees with the trees as its walls, if they are strong or tied and secured such that normal wind will not regularly shake them, and the open spaces are filled in with straw and hay such that the wind will not shake them, and they are tied, it is kosher. Therefore, it is not good to make the walls from sheets of flax without poles even if they are tied well because sometimes they unintentionally get detached and you have a wall that cannot withstand a normal wind.
