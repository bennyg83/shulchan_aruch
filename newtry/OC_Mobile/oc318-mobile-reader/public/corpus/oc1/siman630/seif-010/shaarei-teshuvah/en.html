Se'if 10 in Shulchan Aruch, until here. It is not proper, etc. See Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 31, that even one wall needed to validate a sukkah should not be made from sheets unless he weaves reeds into the partitions within less than three handbreadths. Perhaps even the Turei Zahav said this only regarding a fourth wall; then one need not be particular, since by law three suffice. So it appears from his language; see there.
