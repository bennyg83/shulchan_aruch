It is permitted to lodge a complaint. There is a difficulty in the Sema, siman 5: they do not fix a time in Nisan and Tishrei, etc.; see there. And he did not look in the Mordechai, from which it is clear that it deals with gentile court, where it is permitted to lodge a complaint; therefore Rama was careful and wrote "to lodge a complaint in court," etc. And also what he wrote, "And likewise it is permitted to make a rollover," etc., deals with a gentile, as Maggid Mishneh wrote explicitly; I heard this, and this is the main view.
