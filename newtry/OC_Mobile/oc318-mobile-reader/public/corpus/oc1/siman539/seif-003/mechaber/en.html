If one sold merchandise to another before the Moed, there is an authority who forbids demanding its payment during the Moed unless the purchaser is a person who is not usually found in the seller's locale and happened to be there during Chol HaMoed, so that failure to collect would constitute a potential loss. It appears to me, however, that since the seller converted the money due into a debt before the Moed, it is regarded as a loan, and he may collect it during Chol HaMoed even from a person who is ordinarily found in the seller's locale. {Rama: It is likewise permitted to bring a legal claim regarding debts that one is permitted to demand. [Mordechai, end of the chapter Mi SheHafach] One may also take the necessary steps to preserve such a claim when delay may cause a loss.}
