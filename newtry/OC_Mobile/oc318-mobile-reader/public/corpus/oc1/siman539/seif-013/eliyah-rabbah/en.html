[13] Market day, etc. And in Responsa Maharshal, siman 79, he permitted even in a shop that is closed, and when the buyer comes to purchase he opens it somewhat; and from Responsa Maharival it is implied that one should not permit this except for clothing and vessels in se'if 10. However, se'if 10 deals with opening somewhat immediately, even before the buyer comes. However, Maharshal also concluded: and all this is in order to spend for the time of the joy of Yom Tov. And they practice leniency in sitting in a shop throughout Chol HaMoed; and it is possible they reckoned it a davar ha'aved, for if they do not sit so much time in the shop, business dealing from Jews will diminish. And in any case, certainly one should not learn melacha from this, for it is not comparable to merchandise dealing, which has many sides of leniency in the poskim.
