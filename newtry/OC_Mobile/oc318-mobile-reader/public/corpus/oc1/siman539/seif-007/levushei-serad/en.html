S.k. 12: But other things are forbidden. This implies that specifically fruits, clothing, and vessels [are permitted], but other things, even for the need of the moed, are forbidden. And Peri Megadim wrote: And I do not know what "other things" are; end quote. And perhaps it deals with other things that are not for the need of the moed, but for the need of the seller who has nothing to eat, and he must do so privately, as Magen Avraham wrote at the end of siman 534; see there. And even though here in se'if 2 Rama wrote that there is publicity in buying them, and nevertheless it is permitted if he has nothing to eat, that is because the publicity happens of itself; but he is obligated to do privately whatever he can, similar to what is written in siman 538, se'if 2; so it appears to me.
