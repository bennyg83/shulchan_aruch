(25) That it be acquired by him. That is to say, after eight days.
(26) There are those who permit. For this is not merchandise dealing but a loan, and the essence of the acquisition will be after eight days (Hagahos Ashri).
(27) And there are those who forbid. For if you permit doing thus, they will permit all buying and selling in the world and use ha'arama so that the sale not take effect until after the moed, and during the moed it will be a loan. And for the law, certainly it is good to be stringent like those who forbid; nevertheless one should not protest against one who is lenient and relies on the first view, since in truth his intention is not to use ha'arama.
