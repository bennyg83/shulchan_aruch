(40) They do not buy, etc. And from a gentile there are those who say it is permitted to buy houses and fields and vineyards and slaves and an animal, because it is like rescuing from his hand; and see Biur Halacha.
(41) For the need of the moed. That is, houses to dwell in, and stones to repair his wall, as below in siman 540; and slaves to serve him.
(42) Who transports the purchased items. That is to say, to the buyer's house; namely, because the hired worker has nothing to eat, they permitted the seller and the buyer to do business dealing so that the hired worker earn wages; and the same applies to the broker. And it is implied from the later authorities that specifically if the truth is so, that they are doing the commerce for the sake of this hired worker; but not that they use ha'arama to trade and say that their intent is so that the broker and the like will profit.
(43) But other vessels, etc. Magen Avraham, and likewise other later authorities, concluded that one should not be lenient also regarding other vessels not for the need of the moed, unless he will not find them cheap after the moed.
