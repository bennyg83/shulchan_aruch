Se'if 12. And stones, etc. This is the reading of the Rif and the Rosh and Shiltei Giborim.
And specifically, etc. Since it specifies these; and even though fruits, etc., are forbidden in private except for the need of the moed, there it is because their craft is in that. Maggid Mishneh. And therefore it says "seller" there. And see siman 533, se'if 5: all their craftsmen, etc. And Tosafos and the Rosh and Shiltei Giborim disagree and hold that in all of them we require need of the moed, as written in se'if 9, "one who needs," etc., specifically in such a case; and so Magen Avraham challenged; see there; and one should not be lenient.
