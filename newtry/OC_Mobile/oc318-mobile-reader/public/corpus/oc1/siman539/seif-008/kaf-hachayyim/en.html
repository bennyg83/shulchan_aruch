8) There. In payment of his debt, etc. At first glance, this wording implies that the law applies specifically in this case, where the merchandise had not previously been acquired by him. If it was already his and he now wishes to bring it home, there is no prohibition of commerce, because he conducts no transaction during the festival. Nevertheless, this should be avoided for another reason explained in the preceding siman, se'if 3: it is forbidden to transport one's property from one city to another. The same applies within a single city, as explained there. If, however, he is concerned that leaving it there will cause a loss, it is permitted. Mishnah Berurah in Biur Halachah.
