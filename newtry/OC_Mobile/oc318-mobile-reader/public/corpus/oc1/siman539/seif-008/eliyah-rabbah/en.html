[8] To lodge a complaint in court, etc. There is a difficulty in the Sema, siman 5: they do not fix a time in Nisan and Tishrei; see there. And he did not look in the Mordechai there, that it deals with gentile court, where it is permitted to lodge a complaint; therefore Rama was careful and wrote "to lodge a complaint in court," etc. (Magen Avraham). But this is not precise; for the Sema did not challenge this at all, only the end of siman 545, which deals with lodging a complaint in the synagogue.
