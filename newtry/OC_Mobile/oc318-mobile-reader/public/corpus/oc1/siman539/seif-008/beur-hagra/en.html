Se'if 8. A non-Jew, etc. Even though with a Jew it is forbidden, as above, it is permitted because it is like rescuing, etc., as written in Avodah Zarah 6b; and Tosafos wrote there on 2a, s.v. "And to pay," that nowadays even with a document it is like rescuing from their hand; and likewise here, as written there: and he writes, etc., because he is, etc.
