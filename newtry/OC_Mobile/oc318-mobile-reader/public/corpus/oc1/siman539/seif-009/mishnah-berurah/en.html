(29) He will not find as he finds now. That is to say, he will not find so cheap as he finds now (Beis Yosef).
(30) And to repair the barrel, etc. Even though repairing the barrel is complete melacha, nevertheless since it is permitted to buy, it is also permitted to repair the vessels needed for this.
(31) But more than the need of his drinking. Meaning for the whole year; therefore he should not buy, for he will be able to buy for the coming year at the time of the vintage for the need of his drinking; and if so it is proved that he is buying in order to profit, and it is like other merchandise, which it is forbidden to buy and sell on Chol HaMoed in order to profit, as above in se'if 4.
(32) And if it is something uncommon, etc. That is to say, that buying wine will not be available at all after the moed for profit therefrom; therefore it is permitted even to buy in order to profit, for it is similar to a caravan and fairs clarified in se'if 5. But when he will find it, only that it will be more expensive and he will not be able to profit so much from it, it is forbidden. And there are later authorities who hold that where he happened upon buying wine very cheaply, and it is a great profit such that after the moed such profit is not found at all, it is permitted, for it is similar to the aforementioned caravan; and see Biur Halacha.
