And on the eve of the last Yom Tov of the festival. This is regarding decorating the market with fruits: specifically on the eve of the last Yom Tov of Sukkos they do so, for it is a festival in its own right, and not on the eve of the last Yom Tov of Pesach; but regarding selling publicly, on the eve of the last Yom Tov of Pesach as well they do so; so Beis Yosef wrote.
