14) There, in the gloss. And he should spend for the joy of Yom Tov, etc. Beis Yosef cites a dispute. Some forbid the transaction even privately, while others permit it privately even if none of the profit will be spent for the joy of Yom Tov. He writes that as a matter of halachah, if the profit is substantial, the transaction may be conducted privately, and one should spend from it for the joy of Yom Tov more than he had intended to spend. See there. Rama rules accordingly here in the gloss, as do Levush and the other later authorities.
