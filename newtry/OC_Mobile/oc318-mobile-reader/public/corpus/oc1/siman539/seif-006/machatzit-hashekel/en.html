(S.k. 6) It is permitted, etc. In a matter that is not of effort, etc. That is to say, it is not complete effort as in siman 537. But nevertheless buying and selling also involves effort; for they forbade buying and selling only because of effort, as Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of the siman.
It is permitted because all business, etc. These are like two reasons. One reason is what he wrote, that it is not complete effort. And further, that all merchandise dealings: he buys when it is cheap, etc. And that is to say: since a person's life and livelihood depend on this, therefore even profit in something uncommon, such as a caravan, is called a davar ha'aved. In a similar manner Magen Avraham wrote in the name of the Rosh, s.k. 9. And see in Beis Yosef, who brought the wording of Nimukei Yosef, which implies thus.
