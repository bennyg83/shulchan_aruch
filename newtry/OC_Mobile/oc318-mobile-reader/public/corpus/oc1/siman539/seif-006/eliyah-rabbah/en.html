[6] [Levush] And even for a debt, etc. He wrote this from his own reasoning; but Riaz in Shiltei Giborim, chapter 1 of Moed Katan, [holds] that specifically on erev Pesach it is a davar ha'aved. However, this is specifically for a loan that is a form of merchandise; but for a money loan, we hold that even with a mashkon it is permitted. And what the Levush wrote, "and it goes without saying that it is permitted," means even according to the view that forbids regarding a loan.
