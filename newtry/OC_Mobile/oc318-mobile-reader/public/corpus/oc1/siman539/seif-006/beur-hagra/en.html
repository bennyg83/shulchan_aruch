(ו) If it was, etc. The Rambam's wording and it is Rashi's second explanation, brought by the Magen Avraham, Nekudat HaKesef, etc.: Rashi explained an amah and a handbreadth, and if it was deep, etc., one may deepen it to the first depth; or even if he did not yet dig in it but only a handbreadth he deepens until six less one—this wording is in our Rashi and on the Ri'f there; and likewise Ri'ban and Ri'ban hold that chol ha-moed is rabbinic and he ruled leniently; and likewise Tosafot, Ra'ash, and their supporters that chol ha-moed is rabbinic; see siman 530; and it also fits for the Ramban's view, but all this contradicts what is written in seif 5, though one may distinguish—not apparent.
