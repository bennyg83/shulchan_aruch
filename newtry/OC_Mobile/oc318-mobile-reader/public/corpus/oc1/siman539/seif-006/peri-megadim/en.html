"But." See Taz. From the harvest season until the time of gathering, it is permitted to purchase enough for the entire year when delaying the purchase until after the festival would cause the price to rise; this is considered a matter that would otherwise be lost. Purchasing more than 1 year's needs is forbidden, unless the item is only rarely available at such a low price. In that case it is permitted, like purchasing at a fair, as Rama writes.
