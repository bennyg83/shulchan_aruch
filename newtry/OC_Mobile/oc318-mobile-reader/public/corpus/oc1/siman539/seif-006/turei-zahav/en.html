Unless they are fit, etc. And if they are not fit to eat on the moed, it is forbidden; and it is unlike a caravan above, which is permitted, for a find has no loss in it, only mere profit, which is not so with merchandise, where suspending it is a loss. If so, we permit because of profit only buying and selling, which is the way of all people and is not called melacha, and also there is no exertion; but salting fish is called melacha and involves exertion, so we do not permit because of profit; so Beis Yosef wrote in the name of the Rosh.
