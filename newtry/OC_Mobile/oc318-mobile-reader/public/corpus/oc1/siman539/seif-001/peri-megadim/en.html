He is not. See Taz; Gemara Moed Katan 10b. And nowadays all debts have the status of a possible davar ha'aved and are permitted; and see Magen Avraham, siman 537, that a possible davar ha'aved is permitted. And here there is no Torah prohibition at all, even according to the view that melacha on Chol HaMoed is forbidden; see siman 530. Here he is not doing melacha, only a commerce prohibition because of exertion, or lest he write, as Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Maggid Mishneh; and see there Prisha and Magen Avraham, letter 1, and God willing it will be clarified there. Therefore one should be lenient nowadays and may demand a loan even in beis din. However, if he has a secure mashkon, it is forbidden; and below it will be clarified further, with Hashem's help.
