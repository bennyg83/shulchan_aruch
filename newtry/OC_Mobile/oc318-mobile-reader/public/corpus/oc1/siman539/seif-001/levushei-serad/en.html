Magen Avraham, s.k. 2: so that he not intend his work. Certainly every davar ha'aved is forbidden when he intended his work, as is clarified at the end of the previous siman; but one might have thought this applies only to melacha. Yet this is not melacha, as Taz wrote in s.k. 1, and it is forbidden only because of the exertion; therefore he brings a proof from soaking his flax in the previous siman, se'if 1, and from Mahariv, that there too it is melacha, and there is only a commerce prohibition.
