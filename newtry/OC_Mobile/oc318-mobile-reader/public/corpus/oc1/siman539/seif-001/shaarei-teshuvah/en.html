(In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 1) All commerce is forbidden, etc. And see Responsa Ohel Yaakov, siman 63, regarding a communal agreement that even though those who need to earn for the need of Yom Tov should sell privately inside their houses, because of fencing the matter, lest they do business publicly even those who do not need to: if they can stand by this, it is a good thing; and if not, they should permit it for those who need it, while those who do not need remain under their ban and agreement, and one is not permitted to release for them what is forbidden by the law; see there. And see Beis Yaakov, siman 80, that the shop should be somewhat diminished and somewhat closed; and one who has no shop and happened upon clothing or vessels to sell may sell publicly; and see further there what he wrote on this regarding fairs; and see below, s.k. 7.
