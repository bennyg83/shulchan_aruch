(ג) {יז} And one is obligated to feed a convert, orphan, etc.—as it says (Devarim 16:14), “and the Levite, the convert, the orphan,” etc.; but one who locks his courtyard doors and eats and drinks alone with his sons and wife and does not feed or give drink to the poor and destitute—this is not the joy of a mitzvah but the joy of his stomach; regarding such it is said, “their sacrifices are like bread of the dead to them,” etc., “their bread is for themselves, it is unclean,” and such joy is disgraceful to them, as it says, “and I will spread dung on your faces, the dung of your festivals” [Rambam]; and also in the Zohar it is very severe regarding one who does not give the poor from his festival meal, meaning, and the Acharonim cited this; and it has become known how great is the obligation for all Israel; therefore it is not common that a poor person remains on Yom Tov without a meal; but what can be done for the miserable and broken in the city who are ashamed to extend a hand and ask? Therefore in some places they practice that before Yom Tov people of understanding go to the homes of the wealthy to collect for these destitute; fortunate are those who set their eye on this to merit themselves and merit the people of the city.
