[5] [Levush] Without interest, etc. This implies that with interest it is like a loan that is a form of merchandise, and it is forbidden to collect repayment both from a gentile when he has a mashkon and from a Jew when he has profit by a permitted path. But this is not so; for the source of this law is the Rosh, and there it refers to a loan that has interest; and even lending on interest, which is worse than collecting repayment, is permitted, only that out of stringency we say at the end of this siman that he should spend the interest from that week, etc., meaning like a loan; but regarding repayment it appears to me that it is entirely permitted. And so it is implied from Rabbeinu Yeruham, folio 36; and so it is somewhat implied in the Shulchan Aruch.
