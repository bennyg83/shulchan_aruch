Se'if 5. If it is, etc. Yerushalmi there: This caravan, it is permitted to buy from it on the moed if he did not know that the caravan would go and the merchandise would become cheap; and the same law applies to selling.
Even not, etc. For if not so, even without a caravan it would be permitted, as written in se'if 9.
And even from a city, etc. As written above in siman 535, se'if 1 in the gloss: And even, etc.
And a merchant, etc. See Magen Avraham.
