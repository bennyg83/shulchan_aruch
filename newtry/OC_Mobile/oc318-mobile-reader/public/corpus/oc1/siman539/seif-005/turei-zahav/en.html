And the same applies to fixed fairs, etc. Maharshal in a responsum, siman 79, wrote, and these are his words: Know that I received as practical law to permit on Chol HaMoed if a fair arrives, even a market day called "Krasni Targ," which is something uncommon and comes at intervals, similar to caravans and ships; and we reckon loss of profit as loss of principal, etc. But on other market days one should not permit at all sitting in a shop, unless the shop is closed and opened somewhat when someone comes to buy; and all this is in order to spend for the joy of Yom Tov; end quote.
