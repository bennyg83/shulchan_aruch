If it is something not always available after the festival — such as ships or caravans that arrived, or that seek to depart, and they sold cheaply or bought dearly — it is permitted to buy and sell, even not for the need of his [festival] use but to conduct business and profit. And the same applies to fairs held from time to time {and even from city to city, and it is permitted to travel in such a case}. But places that have a market day, one day a week — it is not permitted to sell and buy on the market day that falls within the festival, for this is not a case of [potential] loss, since if it is not sold on the market day within the festival, it can be sold on the market day after the festival. {Rama: And a merchant who buys from this one and sells to that one, and returns and buys and sells, it is permitted, for it is a case of [potential] loss (Kol Bo).}
