It is permitted to buy and sell. Even though profit is forbidden in a matter involving effort, as written at the beginning of siman 537: in a matter that does not involve effort, even profit is permitted, because all business dealings consist of buying when it is cheap and selling when it is dear (Beis Yosef; Nimukei Yosef).
He should sell on the market day. And specifically when he knows with certainty that he will not lose from the principal (Maharival).
And a merchant who buys, etc. For if he sells today, he will return and buy and sell on the second market day. And this requires further study, for all shopkeepers buy and then sell again, and nevertheless they are forbidden except for the need of the moed, as written in se'if 10 and se'if 11; and it is forced to say that there it deals with one selling the produce of his garden. And one can say that here specifically in his house it is permitted, but in a shop it is forbidden; and so Maharshal wrote in a responsum, that the shop should be closed, and when the buyer comes to purchase he opens it somewhat; and at a fair it is permitted to sell in his usual way; end quote.
