[4] And he should spend, etc. Even if he has money with ease, which is forbidden in se'if 4, it is permitted in this case; for many poskim permit in private even when he does not spend for the joy of Yom Tov. And so it appears at the end of this siman regarding a loan on interest, even publicly: that the main view among the poskim is that a loan is not comparable to merchandise dealing, only that out of stringency we say he should spend, etc.; therefore it is permitted even with his own [money]; so it is implied in Responsa Maharival, part 3, siman 61.
