Se'if 4. If there is, etc.; but, etc. Tosafos and Ra'am there from the Yerushalmi: if he knows that it will not be sold and that it diminishes from the principal, he sells; and if not, he should not sell. And the Yerushalmi phrased it in another wording; and see Rivash and Beis Yosef.
Nevertheless if, etc. Yerushalmi there, and the Rosh brought it; see there.
And it appears to me, etc. As is written: they do not buy houses, etc., or for the need of the seller who has nothing to eat. And Mordechai wrote: not specifically that he has nothing to eat in scant measure; rather even if he does not have with ease, it is permitted, from that Yerushalmi; and since the Mishnah says "he has nothing," specifically a case like this. And see siman 545, se'if 3; and see Magen Avraham, siman 542.
