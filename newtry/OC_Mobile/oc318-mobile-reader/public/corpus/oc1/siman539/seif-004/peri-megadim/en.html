Rather if not Taz — a fair is permitted for profit which is davar ha-aved since it is not common; the same applies to finding — the distinction is that there a person gives of his money in merchandise in order to profit; this is the essence of commerce, and if not a wind blows a bit of loss occurs from the principal; and further there is no melacha, unlike salting fish. And for method 1, every finding — even though there is no melacha, only great effort — is forbidden; and it would seem for such commerce likewise.
