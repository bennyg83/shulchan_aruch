A) [Section 1] A city that was a single property, etc. This is the language of Rambam 55 Din 19, but Rashi interpreted a city of a single person that would not always be entered by a multitude of people and made of many that houses were added to it or markets were established in it, etc. And the O'ash wrote O. A. Dzal DSL to the Sha'a as Rabbi Jonathan in the words of the Rabbi who interpreted as the Rambam's opinion and ruled as the Harif and the Rambam Yaosh and more.
