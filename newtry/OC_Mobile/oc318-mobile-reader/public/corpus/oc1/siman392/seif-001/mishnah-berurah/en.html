(א) Individual acquisition etc. — explanation: one built for himself and rented to many, or built to house residents and kept paths, platforms, and stairs as kings do (Maggid Mishneh):
(ב) Even if it became of many — and even if it has two openings where people enter through one and exit through the other:
(ג) One shituf — specifically since it was initially individual acquisition it is like one courtyard, requiring all courtyard residents to eruv together:
(ד) And they carry throughout the city — meaning after its alleyways were corrected as required:
(ה) One opening — for the entire reason shiyur is needed in a city of many is so they do not all join together and be able to carry throughout — as below — so the law of reshut ha-rabbim is not forgotten:
(ו) Even etc. of an individual — that they sold it to him:
(ז) One does not eruv the whole — a decree lest it return and become reshut ha-rabbim and they be lenient; the world is not accustomed to be careful even in a city of many; and it is possible he holds like Rashi:
(ח) In the shituf they make for themselves, and if the remaining were many — as corrected. And afterward we read and forbidden to carry (Taz):
(ט) Many — if the shiyur is only for one, shituf is not needed:

(כ) (יא) And if they wished to eruv — meaning to join shituf:
(ל) (יב) And they must make between them etc. — for otherwise it is breached to a forbidden place:
