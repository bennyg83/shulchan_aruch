(Magen Avraham note 4) There is a difficulty — and it is difficult per his words according to what Rosh concludes per the Yerushalmi method: if half the residents of an alley did not make an eruv and did not place a kora or lechi to divide their part of the alley, since they are forbidden to carry in the alley, is it enough for the people of the second courtyard who made an eruv with lechi or a beam and do not need recognizable lechi posts or a tzurat ha-petach — if so that courtyard in the middle of the alley would be forbidden to carry in the alley, and why would it need recognition? But truth dictates his path: Magen Avraham did not attend to Rosh's words but only what Beit Yosef brought in part, and did not see inside what he brought in the name of Tosafot; Ritva also brought in their name: on this wide alley they make an eruv and there must be between them a tzurat ha-petach or something from here and something from there so that each not open fully into the place forbidden to him — and likewise regarding a remainder of a large city — end; if so Rama's words are correct that we require recognition so one not open fully into the forbidden place.
