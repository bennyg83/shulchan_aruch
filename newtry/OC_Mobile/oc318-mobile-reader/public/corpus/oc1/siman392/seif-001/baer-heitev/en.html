(א) Individual acquisition. Explanation: one person built for himself to rent to others, or sold it afterward to many, or built to house residents and kept for himself paths, platforms, and stairs as kings do. Maggid Mishneh:

(ב) They do not [eruv the whole city]. Requires study, for I have not seen people careful about this; and it is possible he holds like Rashi that many are not called fewer than 600,000, as in Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 5 halacha 1:

(ג) That they left. See Ginat Veradim general rule 3 siman 23 and Yad Aharon:

(ד) And if. Should read: if the remaining were many and forbidden to carry, etc. Taz:

(ה) Recognition. Even if there is a gentile in this city they cannot eruv together — from Mahari Ber Levin Knesset HaGedolah; and in the name of Ran he wrote a gentile counts as shiyur; and Ginat Veradim wrote there on the place left — if Jews live there they must make recognition and a sign so as not to forbid the city residents — namely when Jews live there; but if they leave as shiyur a place where Jews do not live — no need to make recognition and distinction for the left place; and even a house in the middle of the road helps as shiyur though both sides are within the permitted area — see what Mahari Shabbetai wrote on it:
