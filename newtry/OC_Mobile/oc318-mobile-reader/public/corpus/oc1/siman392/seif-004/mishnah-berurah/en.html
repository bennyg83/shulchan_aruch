(א) (כא) That all residents joined etc. — deals with a city that was initially one acquisition, as he concludes [for a city of many it is explained above seif 1 that even one house left forbids; and if one remains he need not join between them, and the beginnings forbid one]:
(ב) (כב) Marker — many later authorities agreed when it is four tefachim high; see Or Zarua who wrote we also require four tefachim width:
(ג) (כג) He does not forbid them — for hereby he reveals he removes himself from the city:
(ד) (כד) Therefore etc. — meaning since division forbids, one must be careful not to divide the alleyway shituf in half; and from the Mechaber's closing wording it implies he holds one may not make more than the minimum shiyur:
(ה) (כה) In half — the reason: since it was initially individual acquisition and they were accustomed thereby to be together, they are like one courtyard and forbid each other:
