5) There. They all establish a shituf, etc. This discusses a city surrounded by a wall ten handbreadths high and having doors, so that it is a complete private domain. Maggid Mishneh there, cited by Beis Yosef. Beis Yosef writes, however, that Rashi's words imply that the discussion applies even when the city has no wall. This means that each of its alleyways was rectified according to the applicable law explained in siman 364. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1, and the later authorities write likewise.
