2) There. A city that was the property of an individual, etc. Rashba writes: What constitutes a city of an individual? Any city that an individual built for himself in order to rent it to the public, or that he subsequently sold to the public, or that he built to settle residents there while retaining for himself the roads, plazas, and highways, as kings ordinarily do. Maggid Mishneh there, Beis Yosef, Magen Avraham, subsection 1, Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1, and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1, cite his words.
