(א) (כט) This that we said etc. for half — see Biur Halacha where we explained this refers to one who wishes to divide the city to make shituf for each alley by itself, but not one who wishes to divide along the length of the alley:

(ב) (ל) Along its length — meaning the way of towns that their opening is along the length and reshut ha-rabbim passes from opening to opening; therefore residents of this side may not make shituf alone:

(ג) (לא) And they make correction between them — where the shitufim divide this way and that so each is not fully breached to a forbidden place; and at the end of the alley should be repaired in every manner where it has doors and a lock in the wall:

(ד) (לב) Lechi or koreh — see Magen Avraham who challenged this; at the place of shituf division we require specifically a pas of four or two lechayayim of two on both sides even if the place is not wider than ten:
