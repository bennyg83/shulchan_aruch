(ב) But with a pas etc. For behold it helps in a courtyard breached in its fullness to eruv by itself and they do not forbid the courtyard, as we wrote 12a-b, and as we said siman 363 s.k. 26, and see there 361:

(ג) Or he makes. As above seif 4:

(ד) Ten high. Erroneous — should read four, as in the gemara 61a, and Magen Avraham:

(ה) Some say this regarding a city etc. It is Maharam's view who explained the first version, as Rosh and Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot in his name, and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch follow the latter version as their way in many places to rule when there are two statements:

(ו) And they make correction. So Rosh in the name of Tosafos — and this is Tosafos' wording on that which is for width etc.:

(ז) Lechi or koreh helps. So he learned from what we said for half — not etc. alley to alley likewise — and therefore he speaks of lechi or koreh, for otherwise they would not forbid each other, as Rosh wrote; and it appears to me specifically with lechi and koreh etc., and therefore for half too it deals thus — otherwise the question would not arise — and nevertheless they said in the first version that for width they eruv; but it is astonishing, for Rosh and Shulchan Aruch wrote explicitly that courtyard correction is required, and so Tosafos on that which is for width etc., and for length it does not help even in such a case, for the gemara said generally one does not eruv in half at all, as Rashi explained the reason — but alley to alley they eruv too in all cases even with lechi and koreh, and therefore it asks what we said for half etc.; nevertheless lechi and koreh do not help. And in the latter version even for width it does not help even courtyard correction — it helps only alley to alley by itself or in the middle of an alley; and likewise for city shiyur as Rosh wrote there — but not for half of a city; and the above question that Rama learned from it — Rosh was careful about it, for he wrote the gemara's question was because the baraita deals when the alleyways are corrected only with lechi and koreh, as written he makes lechi etc. — Rosh's wording there: and that which it asks here alley to alley etc., for the baraita teaches etc.; and likewise it is difficult on Shulchan Aruch in the latter version that one never eruvs in half — if so courtyard correction never helps except when certainly closed; and from Rama's wording or he makes etc. at the opening of the alley — one can say it deals with alley to alley by itself, and therefore Shulchan Aruch's words there help as above; but the wording for half does not imply so, as the gemara says one does not eruv etc. rather etc.:
