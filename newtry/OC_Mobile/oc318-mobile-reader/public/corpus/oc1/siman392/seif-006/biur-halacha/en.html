(א) This that we said one does not eruv it in half etc. — at first glance it is very astonishing that the Mechaber cited Maharam of Rothenburg and Rosh when he himself in Beit Yosef holds not like them — for they ruled like the first version that for the width of an alley one eruvs in half, only so as not to be breached to a forbidden place they gave these advices; whence it is proven one who holds the latter version that one does not eruv even for width in this manner either — and if so the Mechaber who does not hold like them forbids in this manner too; and one could say the Mechaber retracted in Shulchan Aruch and ruled like them, and his intent is dividing the alley by width — if so this is the view Rama brought in the gloss; and it is evident from Rama that he does not explain thus in Shulchan Aruch. Later I found several already wondered — Gra, Beit Meir, Peri Megadim, Atzei Almogim — and the best of their answers [though strained]: what the Mechaber wrote in seif 6 this that we said one does not eruv in half — he cited only R' Meir's wording, but his intent is dividing the city in half alley by alley, which is also included in half; and he explained that in this all these advices help, and he cited Rosh's words who wrote thus explicitly see there; but to divide the alley itself in half is forbidden in every manner unless he makes a partition ten tefachim high:

(ב) And they eruv in half — meaning along the width of the alley, or as we explained to divide alleyways from alleyways; but to divide the alley along its length is certainly forbidden in every manner [unless he divides with a full partition] — so the gemara proves explicitly see there:

(ג) Or he makes a partition ten high at the opening of his alley — per what we explained that it refers to the end of the alley — we already wrote in the name of later authorities in seif 4 regarding a marker that is four tefachim; and see Beur HaGra who also holds thus and wrote there is an error in Rama's words — should read four tefachim; however Tosafos Shantz and Peri Megadim hold here since it is wider than ten it is stricter and specifically needs ten high:
