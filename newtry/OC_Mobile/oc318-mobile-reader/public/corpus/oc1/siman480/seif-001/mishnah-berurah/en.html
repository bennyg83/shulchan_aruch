(א) One begins Lo lanu — without a blessing; and they say Hallel seated, even though all year they say standing — on Pesach it is different because seated is the way of reclining and freedom.

(ב) The Hallel — one who had mourning on Yom Tov who did not practice mourning before Yom Tov nevertheless completes the Hallel.

(ג) Until Al naharot Bavel — and one does not say Rannenu tzaddikim.

(ד) Until Me'olam ve'ad olam — and our text in Yishtabach is from Me'atah ve'ad olam; b'dieved if he concluded Yishtabach with a berachah, he says afterward Yehalelucha without a conclusion.

(ה) And then he says Yehalelucha — and some practice as the poskim to say immediately after Hallel Yehalelucha until Ki me'olam ve'ad olam attah Kel, and then say the Great Hallel and Nishmas and Yishtabach and conclude with Baruch Atah Melekh mehullal batishbachot (some conclude Bochar besirei zimra etc. as always in Yishtabach); and per this custom, if when saying Yehalelucha he forgot and concluded Baruch Atah Melekh mehullal batishbachot, he does not conclude again in Yishtabach but says the Great Hallel and Nishmas and Yishtabach until Me'atah ve'ad olam.

(ו) And he drinks it — and the poems, meaning Az rov nissim, etc., one says after drinking the cup, which is only custom; Maharam would drink after the poems and after Ki lo na'eh so he would not be thirsty when lying down — so in siddurim; on the first night say Az rov nissim, on the second night Ometz gevurosecha; some say everything both nights equally.

(ז) And he blesses afterward, etc. — and with this berachah he also discharges the third cup, as above siman 474.

(ח) He must drink again, etc. — and the same applies to the third cup per the Mechaber; however regarding the blessing beforehand it applies specifically to the fourth cup, for he did not think to drink more at all; unlike the third cup where otherwise he thought to drink more based on this berachah — it is not called distraction of mind and he need not bless.

(ט) And see above siman 472 — meaning he decided there in Hagahah that for us, since we do not recline all year, one does not return to drink reclining.

(י) In order to remember it is a night of watching — and we are not afraid because of this; and in those countries they pour one cup more than those reclining and call it the cup of Eliyahu the prophet (hinting we believe that as Hashem redeemed us from Egypt He will redeem us again and send Eliyahu to herald us).

(יא) Even not at the place of the meal — and even if he does not drink the four cups in one place, there is no issue in this.
