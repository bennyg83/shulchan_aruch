(א) Until Al naharot Bavel — and one does not say Rannenu tzaddikim, etc. (Darkei Moshe).

(ב) Me'olam ve'ad olam — and per our nusach it is Me'atah ve'ad olam; and Maharil ruled as Tur that one says Yehalelucha until Attah Kel and then Nishmas and Yishtabach and concludes — so Bach, see there; the poems, meaning Az rov nissim, etc., one says after drinking the cup, which is only custom (Bach, Maharshal); Maharam would drink after the poems and after Ki lo na'eh so he would not be thirsty when lying down — so in siddurim; on the first night say Az rov nissim and do not say at the end Vayehi bachatzi halaylah; on the second night Ometz gevuratecha and do not say at the end Ve'amartem zevach Pesach (Mateh Moshe).
