(א) And so on — it appears one who had mourning occur on Yom Tov who did not practice mourning before Yom Tov nevertheless completes the Hallel, even though they do not say Hallel in a mourner's house. Chok Yaakov see there.

(ב) Babylon — and one does not say Rannenu tzaddikim, etc. Acharonim.

(ג) And then — we practice as Tur and Rosh to say Yehalelucha until Me'olam ve'ad olam attah Kel after Hallel, and then the twenty-six and Nishmas, concluding Yishtabach until Chai ha'olamim; and see Taz end of siman 485 regarding the poems — meaning Az rov nissim, etc., and Ometz gevurosecha — one says after drinking the cup, which is only custom of baalei batim and Maharshal; and Maharam would drink after the poems and after Ki lo na'eh so he would not be thirsty when lying down — so in siddurim and such is custom. First night say Az rov nissim and do not say at end Vayehi bachatzi halaylah; second night say Ometz gevurosecha and do not say at end Ve'amartem zevach Pesach (Mateh Moshe, Magen Avraham). Chok Yaakov wrote we do not practice thus but say everything both nights equally; in those countries they pour one more cup beyond those reclining and call it the cup of Eliyahu the prophet.
