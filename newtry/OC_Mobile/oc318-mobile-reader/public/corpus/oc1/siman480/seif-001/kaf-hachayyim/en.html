1) [Se'if 1] One begins Lo Lanu, etc. Tur writes that Hallel is completed over the fourth cup, beginning with Lo Lanu. Levush likewise writes that the fourth cup is poured and Hallel is completed over it. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1, similarly writes that after drinking the cup used for Birkas HaMazon, one must pour the fourth cup in order to complete Hallel and the Haggadah over it, beginning with Lo Lanu, and so forth.
