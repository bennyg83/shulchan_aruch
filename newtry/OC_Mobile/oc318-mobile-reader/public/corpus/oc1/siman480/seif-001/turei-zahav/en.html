Hallel HaGadol. The Tosafos wrote that "Ranenu Tzaddikim" is not part of the psalm, and one should not say it; and so it is in the Mahariv. And Maharshal wrote that what is written in the printed machzorim, that the blessing Borei Peri HaGafen on the fourth cup comes after "Az Rov Nissim" and "Ometz Gevurosecha," is not proper; rather, at the end of the conclusion of Yishtabach he should immediately recite Borei Peri HaGafen and drink and recite the after-blessing, and afterward say these things and the other piyyutim.
And then he should say "Yehalelucha." See what I wrote in the following siman.
