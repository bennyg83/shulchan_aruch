He should delay it, etc. If it is something for which buyers will not easily be found in the fifth hour, it is permitted to sell it before then. However, if he is in a place where he can sell it to non-Jews whom he knows will return it to him after Pesach, it is forbidden to sell it as an absolute sale. Magen Avraham. The first law requires examination: what difference does it make that buyers will not easily be found? Let us be concerned that perhaps its owner will come and eat it, as Rashi explained in Pesachim folio 13, and a person wants his own property. So too it implies in siman 292 se'if 18. Perhaps the intent is specifically very close to the fifth hour. See Sema there.
