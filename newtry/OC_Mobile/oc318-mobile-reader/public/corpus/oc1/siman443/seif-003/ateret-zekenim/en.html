If they acquired anything in exchange for chametz after the sixth hour it is permitted — not that it is permitted l'chatchila to do so, for nevertheless one benefits from chametz; rather it means chametz does not bind its monetary value so that the exchange-items become the body of the prohibition; the practical difference is that one may buy the exchange-items for money but not that Israelite ownership comes through chametz-exchange; or it means b'dieved it is permitted as explained below siman 450 — and these two views are in Ran at the beginning of the chapter "The man betroths."
