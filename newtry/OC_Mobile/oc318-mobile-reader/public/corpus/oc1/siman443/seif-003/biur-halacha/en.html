(א) If they acquired, etc. permitted — see Mishna Berurah strict views; for them not only after six d'oraisa — likewise after Pesach if sold chametz that passed Pesach forbidden to seller to benefit from money though chametz itself only rabbinic — Chullin 4 Rashi Tosafot; likewise if sold at start of sixth rabbinic prohibition — forbidden benefit from money; Gra.

(ב) Does not bind its value — if already received money or exchange object; if not yet received money forbidden to accept from buying non-Jew — when receiving money violates d'oraisa benefit prohibition of chametz; even if drew earlier for some poskim non-Jew does not acquire by draw alone — when receiving money acquisition completes and violates d'oraisa if on Pesach; after Pesach rabbinic; even if chametz acquired by non-Jew in full acquisition forbidden l'chatchila to accept money — benefit is at receipt — see 450; Yoreh De'ah 132, 144; Acharonim; Mishna Lamelech chapter 16 sale conclusion.
