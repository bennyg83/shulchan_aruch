Permitted. Bach wrote: specifically if his maidservant acquired without his knowledge — then the homeowner may benefit; but the man who bought may not benefit. Some Acharonim are lenient; so appears Shulchan Aruch here. Exchange of exchanges may be permitted even for him; strictness on exchanges in Pesach is for garments, fruit, and vessels that are recognizable — but its monetary value is permitted even l'chatchila. All this is exchange of chametz — but chametz itself even if exchanged and returned to Israel remains in the original prohibition. Grain that became chametz and Pesach passed — its growths are permitted as exchange of growths. Kaf HaChayyim.
