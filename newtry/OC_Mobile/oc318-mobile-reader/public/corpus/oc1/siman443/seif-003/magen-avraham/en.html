If they bought. The Bach wrote that this is specifically if his maidservant bought it without his knowledge; then the householder may benefit from it, but the person who bought it is forbidden to benefit from it, end quote. Rabbeinu Yerucham, chelek 5, wrote that the poskim dispute this, and the view of the Shulchan Aruch is to permit it, for he did not write that his maidservant bought it. So too he wrote in siman 450 se'if 4, and in the Tur there. See the Bach, who was pressed there, and see Chullin folio 4b. It is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 132 that even regarding wine of non-Jews, which seizes its proceeds, if the non-Jew took the wine into his possession and afterward gave him the money, it is permitted, since once the non-Jew acquired the wine by meshichah it is not considered proceeds of wine of non-Jews. However, for the owner of the wine they are forbidden forever, because he benefits from the proceeds of wine of non-Jews, etc.; see there at the end of siman 144. It seems to me that regarding chametz it is permitted even for him, according to all opinions, even according to the Bach.
