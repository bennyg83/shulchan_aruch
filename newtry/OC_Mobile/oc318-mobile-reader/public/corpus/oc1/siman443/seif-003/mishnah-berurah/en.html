(2) It is permissible - even for those who bought it in person, and others are allowed to enjoy the same thing. And the author's opinion is that he is one of the greatest judges who opined that Da'ag Delachrim should enjoy substitutes for chametz. By selling it or exchanging it, others have left it unknowingly, and it should be made worse per the opinion of these judges, which is not the place of a great loss. And if he has passed and bought something from the blood, it is permissible to enjoy the same thing in change of chametz. Even per the opinion of those who are strict about chametz, it is true that he took change of chametz at the time he gave it, but if he took the change of chametz after a time, it is permissible. Even if the foreigner took the chametz into his possession, the chametz was bought for him immediately by taking it, and whatever was given to him later in its price or exchange, they are as a gift in the Alma and do not belong to the chametz at all, nor to the cases of its exchange:

(ג) Does not bind value — chametz itself always prohibited wherever; forbidden take from non-Jew even if non-Jew exchanged and returned — rabbinic decree; grain leavened Pesach sown — growths as exchanges; Mekor Chayyim disputes — growths less than exchanges — all forbid; Chasam Sofer 104.
