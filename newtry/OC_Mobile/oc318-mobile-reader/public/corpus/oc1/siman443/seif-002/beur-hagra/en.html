And if he did not sell it, etc. He learned this from what is written on 5b: but you may see, etc. See Rashi there, who explained that 'of others' means of a non-Jew. And similarly in several places it is implied that regarding a Jew's chametz it is forbidden from 'it shall not be seen,' but what is not yours you may see. They establish it there where he did not accept responsibility. Likewise there, 6a: chametz of a non-Jew, one makes, etc.; and see Rashi, s.v. 'his chametz,' etc., which implies that regarding a Jew's chametz one must destroy it, unlike the Bach and Magen Avraham.
