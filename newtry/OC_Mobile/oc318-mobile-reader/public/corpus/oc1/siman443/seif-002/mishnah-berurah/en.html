(א) (i) We shall be delayed until the fifth hour, and shall not be sold before it, and shall not be sold

(ב) Sell to non-Jew — like returning lost object — then forbidden benefit. If fears no buyers fifth hour may sell earlier (Mekubalim explained Magen Avraham). Place sell to non-Jew return after Pesach like our mechirat chametz sale — forbidden absolute sale.

(ג) Must burn, etc. Magen Avraham: if could sell and lazy until must burn pays deposit — unpaid guardian negligence should sell before prohibition; Kaf HaChayyim: guardian vs paid guardian — most agree free guardian exempt not entering guardianship except guard object return intact not sell — hashavat aveidah not pay non-responder; if pledged passed fifth not sold forced burn at prohibition — loses debt at least (Magen Avraham all pledged surely won't return loan); if extant "here is yours" even worthless deficiency not recognized did not lose debt.

(ד) So that he not forget, etc. — and although for other eating and benefit prohibitions we do not require a partition, here it is different because they do not avoid it all year — we are concerned lest he come to eat it; therefore a partition is required for recognition; and covering a vessel on top of the chametz does not suffice, lest he remove the vessel for his use and the chametz be exposed and they did not permit covering a vessel [in siman 446 seif 1] except for chametz found in his house on Yom Tov when a partition cannot be made, and also only for a short time, for at night he will burn it.

(ה) Even if he does not need his responsibility, and even if he does not receive any of them, he does not receive any protection from the people of Israel, and he does not need to be offended by the people of Israel, and he does not need to be harmed by the people of Israel, because he is not the one who is in the house of Israel
