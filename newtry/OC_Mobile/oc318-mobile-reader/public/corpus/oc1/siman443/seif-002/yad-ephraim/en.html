In my humble opinion this requires examination, for according to them we calculate the beginning of the eating time from sunrise until one-third of the day. If so, there is a difference between them of three-quarters of an hour, for according to the Levush and the Lechem Chamudos one may eat three-quarters of an hour longer. This follows what is explained in siman 233, that from dawn until sunrise is an hour and a fifth, and from the end of sunset until nightfall is a quarter hour; if so, according to the Levush the measure is approximately three-quarters of an hour longer.
