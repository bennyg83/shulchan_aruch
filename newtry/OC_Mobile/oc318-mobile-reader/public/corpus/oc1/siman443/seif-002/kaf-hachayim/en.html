(b) There. Forbidden in benefit d'oraisa (Rambam law 9; Magen Avraham agrees law 8). Custom all Israel forbid benefit from sixth hour; arrogant posek lenient before non-Jew based on Baal HaMaor deserves rebuke — even permitted matters others forbid cannot permit before them — all the more here all Sages forbid — Baal HaMaor alone cannot stand before many; prohibition spread all Israel — ancestors ruled like forbidders (Beit Yosef). Levush forbidden d'oraisa; Avodat HaGolah 1; Magen Avraham s.k. 1; Peri Chadash; Chok Yosef 1; Mishna Berurah 1; Razah this seif 1 and 431 2; Chayei Adam kelal 123.
