(s.k. 2) Two hours, etc. On a cloudy day, etc. For this reason, benefit was forbidden only from the beginning of the sixth hour, because a person can err by one hour even when it is not a cloudy day. Therefore they were stringent to forbid it for one hour, even in benefit. But regarding two hours, although a person can err by two hours, nevertheless between the fifth and the seventh a person does not err, for in the fifth hour the sun is in the east and in the seventh the sun is in the west. If so, there is only a concern for a cloudy day. Therefore they were not stringent to forbid it except in eating. Bach.
