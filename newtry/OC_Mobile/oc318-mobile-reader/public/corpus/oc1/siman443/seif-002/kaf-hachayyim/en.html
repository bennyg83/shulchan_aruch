The widespread custom throughout the Jewish people is likewise to prohibit benefit from chametz from six hours onward. One who arrogantly issues rulings before unlearned people and rules leniently on the basis of Baal HaMaor deserves rebuke. Even with matters that are permitted but that others have customarily treated as prohibited, one may not permit them in their presence. This is certainly so here, where all the Sages of the Jewish people prohibit it. The view of Baal HaMaor, who is an individual authority, does not stand against the majority. Furthermore, this prohibition has become accepted throughout the Jewish people because their ancestors ruled according to the prohibiting authorities. Beis Yosef. Levush likewise rules that benefit is prohibited under Torah law.
