(1) We will sell. The above-mentioned dam is something that will not be found in a buyer's room at the time of the the Master. It is permissible for the seller to sell it before. Who is it that if he is in the place of a dealer to his seller for an acoum who knows that he will return it to him after Passover, the seller is not allowed to make a final sale. MA:

(ב) To burn it. Even though he does not personally violate, nevertheless he burns so the depositor does not violate; and even though possibly the depositor sold to a non-Jew in a way that he will not violate — nevertheless doubt does not remove certainty and perhaps he did not sell; therefore he must burn (Bach). Magen Avraham wrote: it appears the bailee must pay the deposit value to the depositor, for he should have sold before prohibition; and the depositor was negligent. However if the chametz is extant he says "here is yours before you"; if it was a pledge in his hand — see there. Kaf HaChayyim disagrees with Magen Avraham; see responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 17 and Even HaEzer disputes Magen Avraham.
