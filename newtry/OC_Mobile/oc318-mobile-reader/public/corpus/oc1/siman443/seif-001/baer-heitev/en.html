(א) Forbidden in benefit. Nevertheless he does not violate bal yera'eh until night — only the positive commandment of teshbitu; nevertheless Pesachim 3 (and in Bava Kama 29 Rashi implies he does violate).

(ב) Two hours before. Even rabbinic chametz such as hard chametz is also forbidden, and all the more so chametz through mixtures — likewise in Pri Chadash. On erev Pesach they take out the dead to the cemetery and return and eat and then bury him (Maharil), and so wrote Kaf HaChayyim — not like Magen Avraham.

(ג) Many together. It implies even kutach and sharp wine — even though it is called by its owner's name — it is permitted to sell before Pesach at the permitted hour to a non-Jew even if he knows he will not finish before Pesach; so agreed the Acharonim. But Bach is stringent that anything called by its owner's name may not be sold thirty days before; one who is stringent is praiseworthy.

(ד) Up to a third today. A way to eat until the beginning of the hour you drink and you are allowed to eat until the time is over. And the last agreed as the first opinion up to a third of the day and calculated the dawn until the stars came out.
