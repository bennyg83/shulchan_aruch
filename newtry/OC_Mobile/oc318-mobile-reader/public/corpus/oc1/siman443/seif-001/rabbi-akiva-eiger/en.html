Magen Avraham s.k. 2. Matzah ashirah. At first glance this is difficult, for the Rema wrote in siman 444 se'if 1, 'In these countries,' etc. I saw in Responsa Noda BiYehuda, siman 21, that he derived from this that the Rema forbids matzah ashirah only from midday onward, but in the fourth hour it is permitted. The intent of the Magen Avraham is that one should eat matzah ashirah until midday, or meat and fish after midday.
