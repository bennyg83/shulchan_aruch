It is permitted for benefit. The Tur wrote that if one betrothed a woman with it, she is not betrothed. The Taz wrote: but with chametz noksheh, which according to Rashi is two rabbinic levels, she is not betrothed; and according to Rabbeinu Tam, we are concerned for kiddushin. See Even HaEzer siman 28, where the view of Rabbeinu Tam and the Rosh is primary.
