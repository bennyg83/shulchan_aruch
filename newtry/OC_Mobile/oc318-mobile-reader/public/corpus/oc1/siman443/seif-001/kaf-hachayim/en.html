(a) [Seif 1] Chametz from sixth hour — end of six hours is start of seventh (Peri Chadash). All who eat from seventh hour on 14th lashes d'oraisa (Rambam chapter 1 law 8; Magen Avraham all Geonim; Rosh, Tur; not like lenient Tur — Avodat HaGolah later). Nevertheless no bal yera'eh until night — only teshbitu positive (Pesachim 3; Magen Avraham s.k. 1). Rashi Bava Kama 29 also from sixth violates bal yera'eh (Kaf HaChayyim 1). Levush 431 like Magen Avraham no bal yera'eh until night (Kaf HaChayyim 1). Acharonim. Aruch HaShulchan 1; Kaf HaChayyim 1; Mishna Berurah 1; Razah 431; Chayei Adam kelal 123; also Rashi can be explained thus.
