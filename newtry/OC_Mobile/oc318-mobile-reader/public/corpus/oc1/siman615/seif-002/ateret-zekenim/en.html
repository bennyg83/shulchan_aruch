One who experiences a seminal emission on Yom Kippur, etc. There is also reason to view the matter from the opposite perspective. Even if he survives the year, he should be all the more concerned, examine his deeds, and correct them. Perhaps his merits were already paid out through the mercy shown in preserving his life, and he now remains spiritually bare, without mitzvos, because he has already received their reward. He should therefore recite the first four psalms of Tehillim, which are effective against seminal emissions. This is from Lechem Min HaShamayim. The number of their words equals the numerical value of keri, and through their recitation he will be protected from such an emission. Since keri comes from Samael, he should have in mind the initial and final letters of the four psalms. Together with the four kollelim, their numerical value is 131. He should intend thereby to remove Samael from himself, whose numerical value is likewise 131.
