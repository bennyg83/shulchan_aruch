(Se'if katan 2) He should worry, etc. He has no desire for his use. This is the language of Maharil in responsum siman 204: Regarding what you asked about one who sees an emission on the great day or at night, it is obvious that there is no distinction between day and night, for he is hungry. Meaning, it says there at the end of Yoma about one who sees a seminal emission, 'If his year passes, he is assured that he is a member of the World to Come. Know that the whole world is hungry and he is satiated,' as the Magen Avraham wrote in the following se'if katan. Hungry means from marital relations, and the main hunger for marital relations is at night. He concluded as follows: Regarding what you asked, should he worry? He should search his deeds, return from the transgressions in his hand, and afflict himself as much as he is able. So I heard from halachic authorities to whom this happened. Sometimes it also comes from excessive exertion or from excessive eating, and the zav is examined by seven causes; this is in Zavim, chapter 2, mishnah 2. All of them lead to this. Even the Kohen Gadol, before the seven days, they withheld from him things that tend to increase seed. There must be a scribal error here, for on the contrary, all seven days they did not withhold food from him, only on Erev Yom Kippur toward dark, as stated in Yoma 18a. They also withheld from him on Erev Yom Kippur foods that increase seed, and so ruled the Rambam in chapter 1 of Hilchos Avodas Yom HaKippurim. Perhaps Maharil interpreted that things that increase seed were withheld from him all seven days, and on Erev Yom Kippur they added that even other things which do not increase seed were reduced, and they would not let him eat much, so that he should not come to sleep. Nevertheless, he should not despair of punishment or of mercy, and should return to Hashem with all his soul, for He is gracious and merciful, etc. End quote. His words imply somewhat that if it can be attributed to one of the seven causes by which a zav is examined, we attribute it to that, and it is not a sign that from Heaven they satiated him, neither for bad nor for good, because he caused the damage to himself.
