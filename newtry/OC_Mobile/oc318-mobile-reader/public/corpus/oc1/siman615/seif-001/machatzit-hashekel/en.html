(Se'if katan 1) His wife, etc. Also by day. The Taz is lenient by day. The law is like a niddah, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 195, se'if 5: he should not sit on the bed designated for her even when she is not present. They are forbidden to sit together on one bench that sways and is not attached to the wall. Sleeping is forbidden even in two beds if the beds touch each other. She is forbidden to spread sheets for him in his presence, and it is forbidden to look at the covered places of her body, and the rest of the distancing practices explained there.
