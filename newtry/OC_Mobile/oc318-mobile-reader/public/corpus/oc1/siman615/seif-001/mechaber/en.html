“It is forbidden to have sexual intercourse on Yom Kippur” - Containing two paragraphs. It is forbidden to have sexual intercourse on Yom Kippur and it is forbidden to touch one’s wife 113a The following comment is given by Magen Avraham , (see footnote 33.): 615:1 - "His
wife": It is forbidden (for him) to touch (her, his wife) also during the day (not only at night) and he should also not talk to her too much ( Shenei Luḥot ha-Berit , של״ה , see footnote 20a.). And in the Maharil , Maharil (R' Yaakov Moelin) , (see footnote 8), the meaning is that one is to treat her (one's wife) according to all the laws of niddah (sexual impurity due to menstruation). as if she were in her menstrual period, and so it is forbidden to sleep with one’s wife in (the same) bed (even without intercourse).
