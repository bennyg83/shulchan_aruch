In the Mechaber seif 3 in gloss: on Lag BaOmer. Note: see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayim siman 142 [there he brought the proclamation text announced in his community K"K P"B from the Gaon Moharar David Meshulam z"l, stating: since they practiced here to marry on Rosh Chodesh Iyar and Lag BaOmer and also marry during the three days of restriction, which is two contradictory things — therefore from now on it shall be fixed law to forbid weddings during the three restriction days; he wrote on this that it appears from these words they were strict only about the custom of marrying on these two times, which are two contradictory things in one place, as is understood in a large community where each year there will be two weddings on those times; but possibly in a small place where weddings are few, if a wedding occurs on Rosh Chodesh Iyar it may not occur during restriction days — then there is not two contradictions in one place; and possibly there is not so much strictness — see there well in all words of the responsum].
