Se'if 2: they practice. For in the Midrash it is stated that they died until the propes of Atzeres, which is fifteen days before Shavuos; thirty-four days remain; and part of the day is like the whole day; and that is what is meant by "and one may not get a haircut," etc. And he said in the morning, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 395, se'if 1, that we do not say part of the day is like the whole day until sunrise; and they brought proof from the end of chapter 2 of Megillah, and likewise one who observes a day [against zavah impurity], etc.; and what is written "they practice not to get a haircut," etc., is the language of the Tur; and see in the gloss.
Unless. As written in Moed Katan 17b: and the Sages agree, etc.
And in the lands, etc. As above in the Tur, who say that then, etc.
And they increase [joy]. Etc., as written at the end of Taanis, on which [day] the dying of those of the wilderness ceased.
And one may not get a haircut, etc. As above, because of [the rule] part of the day is like the whole day.
However, etc. As above from what is written in Moed Katan 17b.
And one who is, etc. As written in chapter 1 of Taanis 12a: because it was our festival day.
