And one does not say Tachanun on it: the custom of Eretz Yisrael is to go on Lag BaOmer to the graves of Rashbi and R. Elazar his son; and R. Eliezer HaLevi testified that he was always accustomed to say Nachem in Birkat Tishkon, and when he finished prayer R. Yitzchak Luria z"l told him from Rashbi buried there that he said: tell this man why does he say Nachem on the day of my joy — therefore he will be in nachem soon; and so it was that his eldest son died (nevertheless this was the intention of the Ari z"l) — behold one should not say supplication on this day.
