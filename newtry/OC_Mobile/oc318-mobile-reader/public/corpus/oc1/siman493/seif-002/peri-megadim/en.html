See Taz, what he wrote that on Lag BaOmer the dying ceased entirely, and that is the view of the Mechaber: it is somewhat difficult, as I wrote in letter 1, for on day 34 as well [they died]; and according to them it is forbidden to get a haircut until day 34 in the morning, as the Mechaber wrote explicitly. And if one would say according to what the Rav wrote, "and for those who get a haircut from Lag BaOmer day onward," and that is that Lag BaOmer too is included among those who get a haircut: that is according to Tosafos, that on a day when Tachanun is not said they did not die, and thirty-three remain on which they died; therefore they mourn from Pesach until Lag BaOmer, and part of the day is like the whole day; but not to say that on that day they ceased dying. And what he wrote, that our custom is not to marry a woman and not to get a haircut from Pesach until Shavuos, only on Lag BaOmer itself they are permitted, [as if] they hold that after Lag BaOmer they did not die: this requires further study, for that is the view of the Mechaber, and if so they died thirty-four full days, and why are they permitted on Lag BaOmer day, for one needs until day 34 in the morning; and this requires further study.
