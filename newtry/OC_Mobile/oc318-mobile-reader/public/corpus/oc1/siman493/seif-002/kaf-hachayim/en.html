(b) There. Not to marry a woman, etc. — it is implied from the plain sense of Shulchan Aruch's words that there is no distinction between marriage of mitzvah — such as when he has no wife and children — and marriage that is not of mitzvah. So too Ohr Sameach letter 1; so too Zarua Orach Chayyim siman 68: even where most are not careful about haircutting on these days, one may not permit marrying during the Omer for one who did not fulfill peru urvu, for marriage is more stringent for them than haircutting, since we have not seen they practiced leniency except in haircutting, see there. Bach on the Mechaber letter 1 wrote: although Maharam Lonzano and Peri Chadash permitted marrying a woman for one who did not fulfill peru urvu, and their reasons and rationales are with them — nevertheless the widespread custom is not to marry at all until Lag BaOmer, end of his words. So too Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz on Maharam's words — we do not practice thus. So too Ohr Sameach there. R' Zalman letter 3; Mishna Berurah letter 1; see Shevut Yaakov vol. 2 siman 35, who wrote that once a certain scholar was lenient regarding marriage during the days of counting against the custom and their match did not succeed well; therefore he wrote one who guards his soul should distance himself from marriage on such days even if there are many sides to be lenient, for danger is more stringent than prohibition, and not to nullify the custom publicly, end of his words; see below letter 12.
