Magen Avraham s.k. 6: if so regarding haircutting too one may say "one who sees." Simply: since those who haircut until Rosh Chodesh Iyar may not from Lag BaOmer onward, and those from Lag BaOmer onward are forbidden before Rosh Chodesh — if some until Rosh Chodesh Iyar and some from Lag BaOmer, this is "do not cut yourselves." So responsa Me'il Tzedakah siman 49; see there also siman 2 and responsa Tamim De'im siman 168–170:
