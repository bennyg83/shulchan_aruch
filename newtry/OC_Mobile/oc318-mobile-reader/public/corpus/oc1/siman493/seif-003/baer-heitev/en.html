(א) Places, etc. The reason is that according to all they practice prohibition for 33 days; if so, when they get a haircut until Rosh Chodesh Iyar and Rosh Chodesh is included, 33 days remain until Shavuos; and even though they get a haircut on the 33rd day and thus one day is lacking, nevertheless we hold part of the day is like the whole day; and since they practiced prohibition for part of the day it suffices; if so, it is forbidden to get a haircut even on Rosh Chodesh Sivan until Shavuos, as Bach wrote. Magen Avraham wrote: but in this country they practiced to marry and get a haircut on the three days of restriction; therefore it appears they should not marry or get a haircut on Rosh Chodesh Iyar and on the first of the days of restriction — we say part of the day is like the whole day — as Maharil wrote, end of his words, see there.

(ב) Partial. Explanation: some people will not practice thus and some will — it is "do not cut yourselves"; see Magen Avraham, who extended the sugya in Yevamot on the distinction of laws of "do not make factions, factions," see there.
