(ס"ק ג) And they increase etc. Chayei Adam wrote, and so in Eshel Avraham, that what Rama wrote one should not cut until Lag BaOmer itself and not from the evening — this is according to Ashkenaz custom that specifically on Lag BaOmer in the morning they do not say Tachanun, but in the evening they say Tachanun; but according to our custom that from the evening also no Tachanun, therefore one may cut from the evening, only not on the 32nd day but until the night of the 33rd — see there; however in my humble opinion according to Magen Avraham in sk 5 that on Lag BaOmer they permitted because we say miktzat hayom kekulo — and according to what Beit Yosef ruled in Yoreh Deah siman 395 like the poskim that night does not help to say miktzat hayom kekulo unless one waits until after sunrise, for we require miktzat hayom literally — therefore even for us who do not say Tachanun from the evening, nevertheless one should not cut at night; see Peri Chadash.
