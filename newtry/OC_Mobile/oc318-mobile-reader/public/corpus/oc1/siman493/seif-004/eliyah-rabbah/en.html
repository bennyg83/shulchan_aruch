[4] [Levush] To marry a woman. Likewise one may not learn from this to places that practice getting a haircut until Rosh Chodesh Iyar that marriage is permitted, for marriage is more stringent — they practice in all places; and this case is different after Lag BaOmer, when they ceased dying — not like Bach (Mahari Weil), and requires study; in Shibulei HaLeket and Tanya they wrote some places practice marrying until Rosh Chodesh Iyar.
