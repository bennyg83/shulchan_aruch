Nevertheless the blessing shehecheyanu is permitted to say on these days from Pesach until Lag BaOmer; and although it is written below siman 551 seif 17 it is good to be careful not to say shehecheyanu between the straits, etc. — that is different, destruction of the Temple, which is more stringent for us than saying amen, letter 2; and there is no distinction between fruit and garment; so too Ikvei HaDat siman 21, see there. Yafeh Lelev ch. 3 letter 3; so they teach in my yeshiva; and we are not concerned for what is written in Melach siman 6 letter 12 — good custom not to put on a new garment, etc. between Pesach and Lag BaOmer, see there.
