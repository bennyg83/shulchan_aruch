Nevertheless, one may recite the blessing of Shehecheyanu during these days from Pesach until Lag BaOmer. Although it is written below, siman 551, se'if 17, that it is proper to refrain from reciting Shehecheyanu during Bein HaMetzarim, etc., the destruction of the Beis HaMikdash is different because it is more severe for us. Maamar Mordechai, subsection 2. There is no distinction between a fruit and a garment. Ikarei HaDat, at the end of siman 21, writes likewise. See there. Yafeh LaLev, volume 3, subsection 3. This is also how the halachah is taught in the study hall, and no concern is given to the statement in Moed LeKol Chai, siman 6, subsection 12, that it is a proper custom not to begin wearing a garment, etc., between Pesach and Lag BaOmer.
