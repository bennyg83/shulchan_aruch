And it is written in Minhagim and Bach and the consensus of the later authorities that if Rosh Chodesh Iyar falls on Shabbos, it is permitted to marry, even according to our custom that forbids everything before Rosh Chodesh. And my brother-in-law the Rav wrote that he heard from his father-in-law, his grandfather the Gaon of blessed memory, that he instituted in Frankfurt to forbid marriages, and likewise not to get a haircut on Rosh Chodesh, but only specifically on Lag BaOmer and on erev Shavuos; end quote.
