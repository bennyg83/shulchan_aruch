It is customary not to get married between Pesach and Shavuot,
until Lag BaOmer (the 33rd day), because during that time, the students of Rabbi Akiva died. However, to do "erusin" and "kiddushin" (engagement and betrothal) is OK. And even for "nisuin" (marriage), if someone did so, we do not punish him.however, from Lag Ba'Omer onwards, all this is permitted (Abudraham, Beit Yosef & Minhagim}.):
