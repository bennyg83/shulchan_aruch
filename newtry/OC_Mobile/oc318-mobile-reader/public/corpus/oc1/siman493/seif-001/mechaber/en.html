The laws observed during the days of the Omer. It contains 4 seifim: The custom is not to marry between Pesach and Shavuot until Lag BaOmer, because during that period the students of R' Akiva died. Erusin and kiddushin, however, are permitted, and if someone went ahead and married, he is not punished. {Rama: From Lag BaOmer onward everything is permitted. [Abudarham, Beit Yosef, and Minhagim]}
