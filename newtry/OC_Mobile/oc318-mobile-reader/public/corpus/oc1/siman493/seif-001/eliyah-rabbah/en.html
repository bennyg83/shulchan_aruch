[א] [Levush] And the reason etc. In Shibbolei HaLeket and Tannaita they wrote the reason that the judgment of the wicked in Gehinnom is twelve months, and R. Yochanan ben Nuri said from Passover until Shavuot, as it says "from the time of Shabbat on its Shabbat"; and in Sefer Adam veChavah p. 44 he wrote that because they are days of judgment, for the omer comes from barley; accordingly it would be fit to forbid even on Lag BaOmer, and I wrote in Eliyahu Zuta that it appears from this reason of Maharil that he practices for himself not to cut until erev Shavuot, and so in Maaglei Tzedek — except they were lenient.
