Levush: And the reason, etc. Shibbolei HaLeket and Tanya explain that the judgment of the wicked in Gehinnom lasts twelve months, while Rabbi Yochanan ben Nuri says that it lasts from Pesach until Shavuos, as the verse states, “from one Shabbos to the next.” Sefer Adam VeChavah, page 44, writes that these are days of judgment because the Omer offering comes from barley. According to this reason, mourning practices should remain in force even on Lag BaOmer. I wrote in Eliyah Zuta that this supports the practice of Maharil, who personally did not cut his hair until Erev Shavuos. Maaglei Tzedek also implies this. Nevertheless, the accepted custom is to be lenient.
