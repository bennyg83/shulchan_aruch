The custom. Shibbolei HaLeket and Tannia explain that the judgment of the wicked in Gehinnom lasts twelve months, while Rabbi Yochanan ben Nuri says it lasts from Pesach until Shavuos. Sefer Adam VeChavah explains that these are days of judgment because the Omer offering comes from barley. For this reason Maharil practiced not cutting his hair until the eve of Shavuos.
