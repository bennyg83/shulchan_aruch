The custom in ShL and Tannaita — they wrote the reason that judgment of the wicked in Gehinnom is twelve months, and R. Yochanan ben Nuri says from Passover until Shavuot; and in Sefer Adam veChavah the reason is because they are days of judgment, for the omer comes from barley; and for this reason Maharil practiced not to cut until erev Shavuot.
