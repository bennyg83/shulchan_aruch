There. {Rama: However if it falls, etc.} NB: see what is written in Knesset HaGedolah on this siman; nevertheless I did not be lenient and I forbade on erev Shavuos when it fell on Sunday to shave on the erev Shabbos before it, even though there is room to distinguish; requires study. Likewise milah on Sunday — I did not permit on the sixth day before it, for it is not comparable to the above, since it is only his yom tov and not for the majority of the community. Lag BaOmer on erev Shabbos — it is good to make marriages at night from the day of erev Shabbos and to consummate on Shabbos night, as Eliyah Rabbah's intent.
