In the gloss: however if it falls, etc. Note: and see what is written in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah on this siman; and nevertheless I was not lenient, and I forbade on erev Shavuos that falls on Sunday to shave on the erev Shabbos before it, even though there is room to distinguish, and it is straightforward. And likewise milah on Sunday: I did not permit on the Friday before it, for it is not comparable to the above, since it is only his festival day and not for most of the public. And Lag BaOmer on erev Shabbos: it is better to make the marriage at night rather than by day on erev Shabbos, and to consummate on Shabbos night; so it appears to me is the intent of Eliyah Rabbah.
