It is proper. Lest another precede him; and it appears to me that it is permitted at that time to make a seudas erusin (see siman 551, se'if 2); but to make optional dancing and circling dances, they practiced to forbid; and it appears to me that even one who made a shidduch is forbidden to make dancing and circling dances.
Everything is permitted. That is, for those who practice to get a haircut from Lag BaOmer onward; but for us, who practice a haircut prohibition, it is likewise forbidden to marry (Bach, Levush, and Minhagim; and so it is implied in Beis Yosef and Darkei Moshe); however, until Rosh Chodesh Iyar they are permitted to marry, just as they are permitted to get a haircut, as is written in se'if 3; and when Rosh Chodesh falls on Shabbos, everyone is permitted to marry on that Shabbos (Bach and Minhagim).
