(43) Although, etc. Presumably they agreed that he should eat a little of their food with them so that they would thereby have a zimun. Consequently, if they are eating something upon which there is no zimun, he may not eat without permission. Eliyah Rabbah writes that perhaps the same applies if there were nine diners eating something upon which zimun is recited: a tenth may also eat without permission, since they presumably prefer this so that they may recite zimun with Hashem’s Name.
