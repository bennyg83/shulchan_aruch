(27) He should not drink, etc., and then recite Bircas HaMazon. If he drinks both cups at once, it appears that he is doing so to settle the food in his stomach so that after Bircas HaMazon he can return and eat much more. The Tur has the reading, “He should not bring two cups.” Many later authorities explain that even if he now intends to drink only one cup and leave the second for Bircas HaMazon, he should nevertheless not bring both at once, because observers may think that he intends to increase his drinking.
