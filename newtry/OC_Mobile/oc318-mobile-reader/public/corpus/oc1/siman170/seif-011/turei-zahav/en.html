He should not drink two cups. The Baraisa states this, while the Tur writes, “He should not bring two cups,” etc. It appears that the Tur explains the Baraisa as referring to bringing them for drinking. He intends to drink one cup and recite the blessing over the second. In that case he is not endangered by pairs, for we say in Pesachim 110a that a cup of blessing combines for benefit but not for harm. Nevertheless, he should not bring both at once because onlookers may think he intends to drink excessively. This appears correct.
