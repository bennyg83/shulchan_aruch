11) There: He washes both hands, etc. Tosafos in the chapter Arvei Pesachim and Rosh there write regarding the passage in Yoma that since washing is required, the blessing of HaMotzi is likewise required. The Tur's words, however, indicate that only washing is required, not HaMotzi, and the Rambam's words in chapter 7 of Hilchos Berachos imply the same. Knesses HaGedolah there, paragraph 2. Magen Avraham, paragraph 4, likewise writes that HaMotzi is unnecessary, as Rema states in siman 178, seif 2. Beis Menuchah, paragraph 11.
