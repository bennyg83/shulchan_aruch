[15] The greatest extends his hand, etc. Tzeidah LaDerech, page 59, writes: A person should never be the first to extend his hand to the bowl or the last to withdraw it. Bach asks that this rule already appears above, siman 167, seif 14, and is not because of gluttony. He answers that here it applies even when each person has his own bowl. Taz objects that if each person has his own loaf before him, he need not wait even for the one who recited HaMotzi, as stated above in seif 15. In my humble opinion, there the reason is not gluttony but avoiding an interruption between the blessing and eating. The Taz’s own explanation, that they eat from one bowl and he receives permission from the greatest person, is likewise strained and does not resolve the matter.
