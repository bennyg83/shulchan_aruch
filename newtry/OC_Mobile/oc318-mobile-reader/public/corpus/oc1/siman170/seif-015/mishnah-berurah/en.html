(36) He should not bite from a piece, etc. At first glance, this should follow all the more so from seif 10, where it is forbidden even to place it upon the table, and certainly one should not place it before another person or in a bowl. In truth, however, the proper reading here is “from a piece,” as found in tractate Derech Eretz and in Rokeach. The reference is not to the piece itself that he held in his teeth, for that may not even be placed on the table, as stated above in seif 10. Rather, it refers to the remaining piece from which he bit off a portion with his teeth. Even this remaining piece should not be placed before another person to eat or in a bowl. Eliyah Rabbah and Perishah.
