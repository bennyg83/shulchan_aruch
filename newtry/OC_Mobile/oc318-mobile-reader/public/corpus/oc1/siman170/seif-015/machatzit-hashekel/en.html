Seif katan 15: He should drink, because it appears, etc. Levush writes that for this reason the law is connected specifically with Bircas HaMazon. When he drinks the cups together immediately before Bircas HaMazon and then recites Bircas HaMazon, it appears even more gluttonous, as though he drinks them at once because he is full and wishes to soften the food greatly so that it will digest quickly, enabling him to return and eat soon after Bircas HaMazon. This is especially disgraceful. Although in truth he is not a glutton because he drinks no more than he needs, such as where he did not drink what he needed at the beginning of the meal so that he could drink all he required at once, nevertheless he appears gluttonous. This implies that during the meal it is permitted to drink two cups at once.
