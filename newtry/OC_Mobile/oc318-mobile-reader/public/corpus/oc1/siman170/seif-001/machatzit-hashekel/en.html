Seif katan 1: One may not converse, even in matters of Torah. So it is stated in Taanis 5b: Rabbi Nachman and Rabbi Yitzchak were seated at a meal. Rabbi Nachman said to Rabbi Yitzchak, “Let the master say something.” He replied, “Rabbi Yochanan said: One may not converse during a meal,” etc. After they had eaten, he said to him, “Rabbi Yochanan said: Yaakov Avinu did not die.” Thus, during the meal he did not wish to tell him even a matter of Torah, but deferred him on account of Rabbi Yochanan's ruling that one may not converse during a meal.
