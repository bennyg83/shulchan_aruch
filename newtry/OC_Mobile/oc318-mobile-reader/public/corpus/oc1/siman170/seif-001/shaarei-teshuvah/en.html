During the meal. Eliyah Rabbah writes in the name of Perishah that nowadays people are not concerned about speaking while eating, because the concern applied only when people ordinarily ate while reclining. Birkei Yosef writes that according to this, one should be careful on Pesach night, when the meal is eaten while reclining. The answer of Derishah requires study. The Gemara implies that there is no concern at all when reclining upon the left side. Their reclining was upon the left, so why should there have been any concern? One must say that even while reclining to the left there is danger when speaking, because the voice exits through the windpipe and speech opens the epiglottis. We must therefore rely upon the principle stated elsewhere that nowadays, since the public has become accustomed to this practice, it is treated differently. See Shabbos 129. According to this, there is no distinction between Pesach nights and the rest of the year regarding this matter.
