Seif 1: Even, etc. Yerushalmi, cited by the Rosh in chapter 6 of Berachos, siman 34. See also siman 174, seif 8.
And if he did not, etc. For this reason he washes only one hand. There, however, regarding the feet, etc., the Gemara says, “This teaches,” etc.
And that which we require, etc. Rashi there. See above, siman 158, seif 6. There is an apparent proof from Pesachim 115b: “He washed his hands for the dipping,” etc., which implies that before the dipping he need not wash. See Tosafos there, s.v. tzarich, etc. It may be answered, however, that for drinking he needs to wash only one hand.
