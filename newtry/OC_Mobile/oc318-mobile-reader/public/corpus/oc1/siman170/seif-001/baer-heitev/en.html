During the meal. Even regarding words of Torah.
Who rubbed. He must recite the blessing Al Netilas Yadayim. He first recites Al Netilas Yadayim and afterward Asher Yatzar. Magen Avraham. Taz writes that he need not recite Al Netilas Yadayim. See siman 164, seif katan 5, in Taz, and siman 165, seif katan 1, in Magen Avraham. However, if he does not wish to eat but only to drink, all agree that he need not recite the blessing Al Netilas Yadayim, for a blessing is not required merely because of a concern. Magen Avraham. See Yad Aharon, siman 164.
His hands. He need not recite HaMotzi, as Rama writes in siman 178. Magen Avraham. See also Sefer Bircas Avraham, page 24.
