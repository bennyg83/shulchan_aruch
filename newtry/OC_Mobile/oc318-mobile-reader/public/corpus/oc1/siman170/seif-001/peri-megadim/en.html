Which he rubbed. See Taz. In siman 3 it is explained that when urinating from a high place, rubbing is unnecessary. The Taz therefore concludes that if one urinated in a manner that produces droplets and transgressed by failing to rub them away, he must wash inside, because people may suspect him, since it is a mitzvah to rub. If, however, he urinated from a high place, he may go upstairs to urinate toward the ground, for everyone knows that rubbing is unnecessary and they will not suspect him. See Magen Avraham, siman 3. The Gemara mentions only that he washed one hand, not rubbing, and Rambam likewise omits it. Rashi and Taz, however, refer to the hand with which he rubbed. The Gemara may be understood in accordance with Levush. See Perishah. According to what I wrote, it is clear.
