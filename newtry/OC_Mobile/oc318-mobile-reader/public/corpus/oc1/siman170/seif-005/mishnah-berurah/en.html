(16) Whatever the host says, etc. This includes even something involving a degree of prominence or authority that the guest would not ordinarily do because of humility; nevertheless, he should do it. Tosafos. If the guest follows a particular stringency because of a possible prohibition, he is not obligated to obey the host and transgress it. If, however, it is merely an act of personal abstinence, it is good for a person to conceal his practices. Shaarei Teshuvah; see there.
(17) He should do. See Bigdei Yesha, who inclines to say that this applies specifically to matters other than eating and drinking. If he has no desire to eat or drink more and the host presses him to do so, he is not obligated to comply, so that the food should not harm him. His refusal does not constitute withholding honor from the host. See there.
