He should not. See Taz, Beit Yosef, Bach, and Magen Avraham, seif katan 18. With Hashem’s help, this is explained by the Gemara in Pesachim: one should not drink two cups because of pairs. Shulchan Aruch omitted this because the danger is not common nowadays. See Yoreh Deah 116:1, Taz seif katan 1, and siman 115, Taz seif katan 10. At first glance, regarding a prohibition we do not distinguish even when the reason no longer applies, whereas regarding danger people take precautions on their own, so the principle that the enactment is not divided does not apply. There, however, other explanations are given. In Peri Megadim there we wrote about this, and with Hashem’s help it will be explained further in Magen Avraham. This is not the place to elaborate.
