Whatever the host tells you, etc. The Tur cites an incident in which guests entered before Rabbi Shimon. He ordered them to eat and drink, but they vowed and falsely swore not to do so, whereupon he struck them. This is difficult: what proof is brought from this incident? Perhaps he struck them because they fabricated a vow. It may be answered that this is considered a vow of encouragement, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 232. Necessarily, then, he struck them because they initially refused, and their subsequent fabricated vow did not help them.
