5) There: He washes one hand, etc. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2, writes that he must recite Al Netilas Yadayim, as stated at the end of siman 164, contrary to Agudah; see there. We have already written above in siman 164, paragraphs 16 and 17, that since there is a dispute, he should not recite Al Netilas Yadayim again, because in cases of doubt concerning blessings we rule leniently; see there. If he intends only to drink and not to eat, however, all agree that no blessing is required, for a blessing is not required merely because of the concern that he might eat.
