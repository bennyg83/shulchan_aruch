Eliyahu Rabba who brings in the name of the the Master several moral laws at a meal and wrote in the name of an apothecary through the road a man shall not eat while standing nor drink while standing nor shall he drain the bowl etc. nor shall he stand immediately after the meal and for every kind of food shall be left after him as the lesson of his last blessing.
