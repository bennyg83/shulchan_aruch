But. See Taz. Darchei Moshe explains that when one says to another, “Come and eat with me,” and fears he may refuse, the other may say, “Why should I eat with you? I will eat with you another time.” It is apparent that he says this only so that the first person will not refuse him, not that the matter should result in repayment. This is permitted. See there. If so, the Taz’s difficulty is resolved.
