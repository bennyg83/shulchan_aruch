Guests. The language of the Rambam is: guests may not take anything from what is before them and place it in the hand of his son, etc., lest the host be embarrassed, for he has only what he brought before them, etc. This implies that if he placed abundant bread on the table so that they would not be lacking, they are permitted to give to them, as written in siman 169, seif 1. In Yam shel Shlomo, Perek Gid HaNasheh, seif 20, he wrote that this is forbidden specifically to his slave, but to an attendant who serves at the meal it is permitted.
