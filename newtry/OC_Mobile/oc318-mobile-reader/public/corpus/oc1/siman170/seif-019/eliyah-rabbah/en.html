And give it to his son, etc. Damesek Eliezer, folio 242, writes that the prohibition applies only if he gives away everything before him, but he may give part of it. The phrase 'from before them' does not imply this, and the Rambam's wording cited by Magen Avraham indicates that even part is forbidden. It seems that guests may give to one another. So writes the Beit Yosef. Maharshal, chapter Gid HaNasheh, writes that one may give to the servant serving the meal, while Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah disagrees. Where it is known that there is more than enough food, however, all these transfers are permitted.
