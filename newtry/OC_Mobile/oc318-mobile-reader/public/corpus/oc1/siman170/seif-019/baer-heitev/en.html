To his servant. It is permitted to give it to an attendant serving at the meal. Maharshal, Perek Kol HaBasar, siman 20. The Baer Heitev before me also copied in his name that the same applies after each person has been given his portion. See there. I followed him in Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer, siman 28, seif katan 32, and on this basis resolved the difficulty of Beit Yosef and Taz. See there. I have now reconsidered and searched Maharshal there, and found only what I copied here, and nothing further. It appears to me that the wording of Taz misled him. Taz writes in seif 18, seif katan 9, that one need wait only until each person has been given his proper portion. See there. The Baer Heitev before me assumed that the same law applies here as well, but he was not precise. See there.
