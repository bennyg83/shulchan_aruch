19) Kesubos 65a states: A woman whose husband is not with her should not drink wine. A woman staying as a guest may not do so even when her husband is with her. If she is accustomed to drinking wine in her husband's presence, she may drink a small amount when he is absent. Magen Avraham there; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 12. Magen Avraham writes that the same applies to other intoxicating beverages. Eliyah Rabbah there, however, writes that one might distinguish wine because it gladdens the heart of man. Nevertheless, it appears that Magen Avraham is correct, for we see that every intoxicating substance brings gladness. One must therefore be concerned about this with other beverages as well. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 42, paragraph 16, writes the same. See Even HaEzer, siman 70, seif 2.
