(23) From its top. The white garlic within is the superior portion, while the green leaves above it are inferior. If he begins eating from the top, he appears ravenous. Perishah. On Shabbos it is permitted because of affection for the Shabbos meal.
(24) From above it. Meaning, from the side of the leaves.
(25) With his other hand. Meaning, like one who wishes to tear one thing forcefully from another and holds it with both hands in order to tear it. In our version of tractate Derech Eretz, the reading is: “The foolish one among them held the stalk in one hand and bit it with his teeth.” See there. This implies that it is disgraceful specifically in this manner. See Biur HaGra.
