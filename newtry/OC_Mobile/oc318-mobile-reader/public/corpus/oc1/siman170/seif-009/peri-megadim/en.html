He should not. See Taz, who explains like Levush. Levush writes that one should wait until after the meal, but Taz writes that one need wait only until each person has received his portion; immediately afterward it may be given to the attendant. With Hashem’s help, see what I write in Magen Avraham, seif katan 23, regarding the attendant, and see siman 169, seif 1.
