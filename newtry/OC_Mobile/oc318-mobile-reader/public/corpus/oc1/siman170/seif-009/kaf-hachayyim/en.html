9) There: This applies when he returns to drink, etc. One who is known to be extremely sensitive washes outside even if he returns to drink, for everyone knows that his disposition cannot tolerate doing otherwise. So is stated in the Gemara there. Toras HaBayis, folio 59, writes the same, cited by Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2, and Mishbetzos Zahav. It appears that the same applies to us, who customarily wash in every case for cleanliness, as written in the preceding paragraph. We are comparable to one who is sensitive and wash outside, since everyone knows that washing is customary. Indeed, the present universal practice is to wash outside, because everyone knows of this practice and there is no suspicion.
