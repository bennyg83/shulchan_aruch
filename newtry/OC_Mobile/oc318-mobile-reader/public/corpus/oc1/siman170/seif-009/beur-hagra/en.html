Seif 9: He should not eat in a ravenous manner. As stated in several places, such conduct appears gluttonous, as explained above. Derech Eretz Zuta, chapter 6, teaches that a person should be neither excessively fastidious, nor excessively probing, nor gluttonous.

He should not hold it. See Derech Eretz, chapter 7, concerning the incident with Rabbi Akiva. Tur’s version reads: the wise one among them grasped the stalk with one hand and pulled it out, whereas the foolish one grasped the stalk with both hands. Nevertheless, the text may be explained according to our version, unlike the interpretation of the Rav.
