Seif katan 9: He pours, etc. Tosafos explains differently. The Baraisa in Eruvin states: One leaves a remainder in a cooked dish, but one does not leave a remainder in food prepared in a pan. Tosafos explains that cooked dishes are brought for eating and satiation, so if one leaves nothing it appears gluttonous. Food prepared in a pan is brought as dessert, so leaving nothing does not appear gluttonous, because there is always room for something sweet.
