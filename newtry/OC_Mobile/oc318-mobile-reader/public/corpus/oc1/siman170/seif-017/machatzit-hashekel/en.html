Seif katan 17: The elder, even if each person, etc. He found it difficult: even without concern for appearing gluttonous, siman 167, seif 17, rules that the one who broke bread extends his hand first to the dish. He therefore answers that the case is where each person has his own dish before him, so that strictly speaking one need not wait for the one who broke bread or for the elder. Nevertheless, so that he not appear gluttonous, he should wait. Taz, seif katan 6, objects that in such a case siman 167, seif 15, explicitly states that one need not wait for the one who broke bread. Eliyah Rabbah answers that there the case concerns the piece of HaMotzi, where it appears that he acts in order not to interrupt, and therefore he does not appear gluttonous.
