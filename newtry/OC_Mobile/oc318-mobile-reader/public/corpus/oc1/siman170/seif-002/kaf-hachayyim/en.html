2) Whoever prolongs his time at the table has his days and years prolonged. This is because a poor person might come and he will give him food. Berachos 54b and 55a; Zohar, parashas Eikev, 273b; Or Tzaddikim by R' Meir Poppers, of blessed memory, siman 23, paragraph 8. He writes there that if no poor person comes, or if you are traveling, set aside the poor person's portion. When you later give it, give it to someone who has the fragrance of Torah and knows how to recite Birkas HaMazon.
