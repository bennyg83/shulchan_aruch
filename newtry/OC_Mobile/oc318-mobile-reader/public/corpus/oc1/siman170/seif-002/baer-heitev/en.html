To drink. This does not apply specifically to drinking. The same law applies to any other matter for which he is obligated to wait. Magen Avraham. Tosafos in Bechoros 44 states that one should not drink water in the presence of many people, but should turn his face away. Outside a meal, even when drinking other beverages, he should turn away. Kesubos 65 states that a woman whose husband is not with her may not drink wine. At an inn, even if her husband is with her, she is prohibited. The same applies to other intoxicating beverages. Magen Avraham. If she is accustomed to drinking wine in her husband’s presence, she may drink a small amount when he is absent. See Even HaEzer, siman 70.
