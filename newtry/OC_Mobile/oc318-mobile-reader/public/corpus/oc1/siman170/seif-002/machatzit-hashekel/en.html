Seif katan 2: His hands, etc. He must recite the blessing Al Netilas Yadayim, as written at the end of siman 164, unlike the Agudah. Yet at the end of siman 164 Magen Avraham himself cites Maharshal, who agrees with the Agudah that one should not recite Al Netilas Yadayim when washing after touching a soiled part of the body during the meal, unless he left for an extended period. See there.
Nevertheless, etc. Because of a mere concern, no blessing is required. Meaning, although even if he does not presently intend to eat he must nevertheless wash, as explained here in the Shulchan Aruch, because we are concerned that he may eat, the Sages did not institute a blessing on account of that concern alone.
