And this applies. See Taz and Rashi, Yoma 30a. Regarding drinking alone, we rule in siman 158, seif 6, that one need wash only one hand. The Rama writes in the gloss that after Bircas HaMazon one need not wash for drinking, and cites Rabbeinu Yonah and Rashi. Rashi, however, explains the Gemara as referring to drinking during the meal. According to the Rama, the meaning is that only during the meal are we concerned that he may place a piece of bread in his mouth, but afterward we are not concerned. Yet it may imply that even outside a meal we are concerned that he may forget and take a piece of bread to sweeten the drink, though one need not be concerned for this. See Perishah. It also implies that even where the drink is primary and the bread merely sweetens it, so that no HaMotzi is required, it is nevertheless forbidden without washing the hands. This requires study, for siman 155 does not imply so. See siman 212; with Hashem’s help, this will be explained there. See Magen Avraham here, seif katan 6.
It is written. Toras HaBayis, Bayis 6, Shaar 5, states that one who is fastidious may wash outside. This is also stated in the Gemara: “Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak broke bread,” etc. The Gemara there further states that the attendant returns the pitcher to the guests, as Rashi explains. I do not know why the poskim omitted this.
