(1) Per Taz on seif 2—Rashi Yoma for one who holds alone to drink alone we rule in knaf 66 he need not pour one hand. And Rosh in hagahah that after hamotzi no need washing for drink, and R' Yitzchak and Rashi—Rashi made as if halachah in a meal; but if Rosh meant no need only within the meal we fear lest he give a slice, after not; rather implies even not in a meal lest he forget and give slice to sweeten drink—need not fear this, see Perishah. And implies even if main food and bread to sweeten without hamotzi, even so forbidden without netilat yadayim; and requires study for in knaf does not imply so, and siman 182 will be explained. Magen Avraham sign vav here.

(2) He wrote in Tikkunei Shabbat house six gate five: one who is fastidious (istenis) permitted to wash outside, and so in gemara bread arnabei etc., and in gemara there and returns the towel to guests as Rashi, and why did poskim omit.
