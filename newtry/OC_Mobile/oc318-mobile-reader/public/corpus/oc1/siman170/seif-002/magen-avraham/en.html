To drink. It seems to me that this is not specifically to drink; the same applies to another matter, that one is obligated to wait for him. So it also appears from the fact that the poskim wrote this without qualification, and from the Gemara there; not like the Levush. It is stated in Tosafot, Bechoros 44, that one does not drink water before many people, but should turn his face. When it is not at the time of a meal, even with other beverages he should turn. In Kesubos 65, a woman whose husband is not with her may not drink wine, and an inn guest, even if her husband is with her, is forbidden. If she is accustomed to drink wine before her husband, she may drink a little when not before her husband. See Even HaEzer, siman 70. It seems to me that the same applies to other intoxicating beverages.
