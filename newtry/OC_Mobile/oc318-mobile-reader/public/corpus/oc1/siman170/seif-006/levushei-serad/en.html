Taz, seif katan 5 So it is stated in the Baraisa. This wording implies, as Magen Avraham explains, that the prohibition applies only when he recites Bircas HaMazon. Since he has already eaten all he needs, if he drinks two cups it appears that he wishes to eat more. In the middle of the meal, however, he may drink two cups in order to moisten the food he will eat afterward. The Tur gives a different explanation: Bircas HaMazon is the greater novelty, for drinking two cups during the meal is certainly prohibited because of pairs. Even when he wishes to recite Bircas HaMazon over one of them, however, bringing both together is prohibited because he appears gluttonous.
