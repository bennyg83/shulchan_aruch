Taz note 5—in baraita so. This language implies as Magen Avraham explained prohibition only when blesses birkat hamazon having eaten all needs and drinks two cups appears wants eat more; but mid-meal permitted drink two cups to moisten what he will eat later. Tur explains one view in birkat hamazon many cups—for drinking two within meal certainly forbidden due to pairs; but even when wants bless on one nevertheless bringing together forbidden for appears glutton.
