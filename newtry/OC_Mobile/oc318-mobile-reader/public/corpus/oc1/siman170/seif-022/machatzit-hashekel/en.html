Seif katan 22: He should not take, etc. He should first place it down. If afterward he gives it to the attendant, it does not appear that he rejects it, but rather that he no longer needs to eat.
In Chullin 6, Rashba cites a Tosefta. So I saw the text corrected, and so too in Yoreh Deah, siman 278, seif 6.
As stated in Kiddushin. We find that Avraham's guests appeared to him as Arabs, yet he stood over them to serve them. Likewise, the Holy One, blessed is He, prepares food for all His creatures. See there.
