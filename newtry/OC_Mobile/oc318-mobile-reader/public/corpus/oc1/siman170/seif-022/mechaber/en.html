After you have drunk and wine remains in the cup for your companion to drink, wipe the place where the mouth touched because of disgust, but do not pour it out because of bal tashchit (the prohibition against waste). After drinking water, pour some out as a gesture of respect. {Rama: Rema: After all your eating, eat salt, and after all your drinking, drink water, as will be explained below in siman 179. It seems this applies specifically when there was no salt in the bread or in the foods eaten, and likewise when one did not drink a beverage containing water; but otherwise there is no concern. And thus is the custom: not to be careful about eating salt and drinking water after the meal, for the reason that has been clarified.}
