Seif katan 14: From its head, etc. And on Shabbos, etc. See siman 274. Although we rule above, siman 167, that one should not cut a piece for HaMotzi larger than an egg because he appears gluttonous, siman 274, and likewise above in siman 166, explains that on Shabbos it is a mitzvah to cut a large piece sufficient for the entire meal because of affection for the mitzvah. The same may be said here: on Shabbos it is a mitzvah to begin from the head because of affection for the mitzvah.
