(34) A slice upon, etc. This means a slice of bread. The reason is that the slice may fall into the bowl and make the food repulsive. The Gemara, Berachos 8b, states that when one cuts meat he should cut it upon the table or the like, and not upon his hand, lest he cut his hand, and also lest blood emerge and make the food repulsive.
(35) With a slice. Provided that he afterward eats the slice, for otherwise there is degradation of food, as explained below in siman 171. Later authorities.
