[14] [Levush] Therefore, it appears to me, etc. Bach and Derishah explain that the teaching means as follows: One should not bring two cups together during the meal, even if he does not intend to drink both, but rather one to drink and the second over which to recite Bircas HaMazon. Nevertheless, since he brings both together, he appears gluttonous. See further Bach and Lechem Chamudos, page 225, who discuss this at length and also explain it in relation to pairs. Since nowadays we are not particular about pairs, I did not cite that discussion.
