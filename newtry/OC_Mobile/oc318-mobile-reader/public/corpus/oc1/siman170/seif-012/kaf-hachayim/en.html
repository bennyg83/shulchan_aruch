(12) There and one who urinated takes two hands etc.—from Mechaber implies spoke with fellow and urinated can wash outside—for specifically urination disgusting to company must wash before them; but from baraita and Rambam implies in all cases must wash before them—and so is essential. Peri Chadash letter 1. However Magen Avraham note 5 wrote also per Maran in urination must before and even for eating see there. Eshel Avraham letter 5. So Levush.
