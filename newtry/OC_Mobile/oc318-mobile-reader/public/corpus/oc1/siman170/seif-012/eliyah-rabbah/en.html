(12) Small etc.—explained small means quarter log Shelah; in our beer measure is more than quarter (Magen Avraham). Levush Yom Tov wrote this is Shulchan Aruch language very small and likewise very large; so appears main—for Arizal said they did not say your cup small and your wine sweet and your stomach wide until all apply; therefore Shulchan Aruch precise very small and very large. Tur and Shulchan Aruch did not quote Arizal—appears because uncommon all three together—end quote; see Magen Avraham. Ketubot 65 woman whose husband not with her may not drink wine; guest even if husband with her forbidden intercourse—see below siman 200 seif 13. If accustomed drink wine with husband permitted drink little when not with husband; but when with husband even much permitted even if not accustomed. Magen Avraham wrote same for other intoxicating drinks—end quote; possible to distinguish wine gladdens heart of man.
