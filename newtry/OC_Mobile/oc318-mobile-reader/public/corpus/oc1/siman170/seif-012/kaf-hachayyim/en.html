12) There: If he remained away, he washes both hands, etc. The author's words imply that one who spoke with another and remained away may wash outside, since only one who urinated, which is repulsive to the group, must wash before them. The wording of the Baraisa and the Rambam in chapter 7 of Hilchos Berachos, however, implies that in every case he must wash before them, and this is the primary ruling. Pri Chadash, paragraph 1. Magen Avraham, paragraph 5, nevertheless writes that even according to Maran, of blessed memory, one who remained away must wash before them, even when returning to eat; see there. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 5. Levush writes the same; Derech HaChaim, paragraph 2; Beis Menuchah there.
