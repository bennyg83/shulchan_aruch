Seif katan 12: At once. And if he leaves over, etc. Magen Avraham found the phrase “and in three it is proper etiquette” apparently superfluous. Once the text says that drinking in one draught is gluttonous and in three is haughty, it follows that proper etiquette is two. Therefore, he explains that there is another intermediate case between gluttony and proper etiquette: if he leaves a little, although he is not a glutton, it is still not proper etiquette.
