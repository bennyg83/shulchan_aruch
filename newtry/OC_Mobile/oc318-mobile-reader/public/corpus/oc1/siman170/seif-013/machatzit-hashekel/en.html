Seif katan 13: A cup, etc. The Gemara in Pesachim 86b states: Rabbi Yishmael son of Rabbi Yose visited Rabbi Shimon son of Rabbi Yose ben Lakunya. They gave him a cup and he drank it in one draught. They said to him, “Does the master not hold that one who drinks his cup in one draught is a glutton?” He replied, “They did not say this concerning your small cup, your sweet wine, and my broad stomach.” Rama's ruling that a small cup may be drunk in one draught must be derived from this incident. Why, then, did he omit the other two grounds for leniency?
All are included in the category of smallness. Either the cup is literally small.
Or the wine is sweet, so that even a large cup is considered small; or his stomach is broad, so that the cup is considered small relative to him.
