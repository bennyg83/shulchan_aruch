To eat. If food was placed before him, he need not be expressly told to eat. The proof is from Eliezer, who said, “I will not eat,” although no one had yet told him to eat. Thus, no express invitation is required. Magen Avraham. He should cover the table modestly because of the holy spirit that rests upon it. He should not eat from a place from which a mouse or cat ate, for this causes forgetfulness. Regarding foods that cause forgetfulness, such as olives and similar items, this applies only to an unlearned person. One who eats them with the proper intention, as is known, increases his power of memory. One must, however, be very careful not to eat the heart of a domesticated animal, wild animal, or bird. The Arizal would eat wild herbs that were not sown by human beings, thereby fulfilling, “You shall eat the herb of the field.” Writings of the Arizal.
