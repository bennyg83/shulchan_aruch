13) There: He washes both hands because he diverted his attention. The work Tzeror HaChaim writes: I heard from my teacher, R' Peretz, that if one holds bread in his hand, he need not wash his hands, because his attention remains upon it and he will not divert his attention. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 2, in the name of his grandfather, R' Avraham Azulai, of blessed memory, in his handwritten glosses; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 3; Zechor LeAvraham, under samech.
