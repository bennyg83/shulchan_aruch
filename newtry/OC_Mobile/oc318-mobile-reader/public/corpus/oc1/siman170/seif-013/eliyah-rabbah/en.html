[13] From its head, etc. Perishah writes: It appears to me that the head of a garlic is its choicest portion, namely the white part, while the green leaves below it are inferior. If one eats the superior portion first, the green leaves may remain, and he will appear gluttonous. Some explain that the rule concerns the proper manner of eating, but the first explanation is primary. Lechem Chamudos writes that since it is called gluttonous, this proves that one should not eat in any gluttonous manner. Agudah writes that on Shabbos one should begin from its head.
