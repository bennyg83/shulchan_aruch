Give me. If they placed food before him to eat, they do not need to tell him to eat. A proof is from Eliezer, who said, I will not eat, etc., even though they had still not told him; rather, no verbal instruction is required. Shulchan Arba.
Because of interest. Meaning that it appears like interest; see Yoreh Deah, siman 162, seif 1, in the gloss. Regarding matters that cause a person to forget, such as olives and the like, this applies only to an am ha'aretz. But when one eats with intention, as is known, they increase his memory, for he rectifies it. However, one must be very careful not to eat the heart of a domesticated animal, wild animal, or bird. The Arizal would eat grasses of the wilderness, which are not sown by man, to fulfill, and you shall eat the grass of the field. Writings.
