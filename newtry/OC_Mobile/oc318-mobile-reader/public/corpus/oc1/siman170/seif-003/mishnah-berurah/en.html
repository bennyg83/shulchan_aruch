(14) The attendant’s food. Nevertheless, it is a pious practice to give him a small amount immediately as well, as stated in siman 169. See Mishnah Berurah there, seif katan 2.
(15) But when the attendant, etc. Meaning, when the attendant pours from the pot into the bowl, it is improper conduct for him to leave some for himself in the pot. Rather, he should pour everything out, and each diner should leave a little for him in the bowl.
