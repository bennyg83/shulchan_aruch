16) [Seif 2] Two people wait for one another, etc. This applies provided that the delay is not lengthy and that the one who pauses does not engage in ordinary conversation. Eliyah Rabbah at the end of paragraph 6, in the name of Tzedah LaDerech, folio 59; Machatzis HaShekel, paragraph 7. This appears to apply specifically when they eat from one bowl, but if each eats from his own bowl, it is obvious that he need not wait.
