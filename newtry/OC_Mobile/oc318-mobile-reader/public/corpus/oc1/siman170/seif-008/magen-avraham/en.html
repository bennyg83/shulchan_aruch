At once. If he leaves over a little, he is not considered a glutton; but when he drinks it in two times, that is derech eretz.
A small cup. This means that it is smaller than a reviis. Shelah. The Gemara says that sweet wine, or one whose stomach is broad, may drink it at once. The Rama omitted this because Rashi implies that it was specifically Rabbi Yishmael, whose stomach was very broad, and regarding sweet wine as well, you have made your words dependent on variable measures. However, it seems to me that there is no fixed measure for a cup; rather, everything depends on the sweetness of the wine and the breadth of his stomach. Therefore the Rama omitted it, for everything is included in a small cup. However, with our beer, its measure is certainly more than a reviis.
