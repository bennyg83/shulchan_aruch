Because of. See Taz. This is difficult from seif 22, where one wipes the place touched by the other’s mouth or pours water over it. Is there not danger otherwise? One may distinguish between remnants in a cup and a cup containing much liquid, in which case there is no danger. According to the view that it is forbidden only when handed directly to him, see Magen Avraham, seif katan 21, it is clear. Levush, seif 16, writes that even if he knows his fellow is not fastidious, he should nevertheless wipe the place touched by the mouth. See Eliyah Rabbah.
