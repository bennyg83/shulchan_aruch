Malbushei Yom Tov writes: The wording of Shulchan Aruch is “very small,” and regarding a large person too it says “very large.” This appears primary to me, for the Arizal said that the Sages did not state the rule unless one’s cup is small, his wine sweet, and his stomach broad, implying that all three conditions are required. Shulchan Aruch therefore carefully writes “very small” and “very large.” The Tur and Shulchan Aruch did not cite the Arizal’s words because it is uncommon for all three conditions to occur together. See Magen Avraham regarding this. Kesubos 65a states that a woman whose husband is not with her should not drink wine. A guest may not drink wine even when her husband is with her, because marital relations are forbidden to guests. See below, siman 240, seif 13. If she is accustomed to drinking wine when with her husband, she may drink a small amount when he is absent. When she is with her husband, however, she may drink even a large amount, even if she is not accustomed to doing so. Magen Avraham writes that the same applies to other intoxicating beverages. It is possible to distinguish, however, because wine gladdens the human heart.
