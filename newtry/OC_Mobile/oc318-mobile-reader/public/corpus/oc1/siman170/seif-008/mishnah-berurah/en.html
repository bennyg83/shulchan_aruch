(20) At one time. If he leaves even a small amount, he is no longer considered a glutton. Magen Avraham.
(21) Two is proper conduct. Meaning, when he drinks it in two draughts. See below, siman 210, and what we write there.
(22) A small cup. Meaning, less than a reviis. All this applies to an average person and ordinary wine. For a person with a broad stomach, or with sweet wine, the measure changes, and he may drink it at once even when it is larger than this. Accordingly, our beer, which is not strong, certainly has a measure greater than a reviis. Magen Avraham.
