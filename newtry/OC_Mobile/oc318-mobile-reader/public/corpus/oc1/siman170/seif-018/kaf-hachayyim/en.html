18) Tosafos in Bechoros 44b states: One should not drink water before many people, but should turn his face away. Outside a meal, he should turn away even when drinking other beverages. Magen Avraham there. See Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 8, for his challenge to Magen Avraham, and Birkei Yosef, paragraph 3, who resolves it; see there. Birkei Yosef concludes, however, that nowadays people drink water before many people even outside a meal, and the halachic authorities omitted this rule because they understand it according to Rabbeinu Tam's explanation cited by Tosafos there. Indeed, the common practice is to drink both water and other beverages before many people, whether during a meal or outside a meal.
