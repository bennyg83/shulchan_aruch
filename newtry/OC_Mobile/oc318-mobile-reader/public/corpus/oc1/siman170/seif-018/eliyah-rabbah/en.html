Damage to the meal, etc. This means that one of the dishes might be spoiled and the diners may then need the portion he gave away. The Bach explains instead that giving away one's portion makes it appear contemptible in one's eyes, as though he does not want it and therefore handed it to the servant. He should therefore first place it before himself, demonstrating that it is acceptable to him, and only afterward give it to the servant because he does not presently need to eat it. The Perishah explains that the host may quarrel with the servant, thinking the servant took it for himself rather than giving it to a diner. If the diner first places it before himself and the host sees that, he may afterward give it to the servant. According to this explanation, one need wait only until each person has received his proper portion.
