And we really need to look into why they didn't write one in Baba when they wrote the column, it's also difficult for me why it was necessary to write a dain to give to his friend in a bowl. From a slice and give it to his friend, and he shall not drink from the cup and give it to his friend, so far. To me, Dain means a piece that was bitten even on the table, but the meaning is about the piece that is left unmoved. And since Danket will not eat in a place where he will not eat, it means that what he bites eats, but what remains is returned, and therefore the portion of the Shulchan is arranged in section 10, from a piece that he bit himself. And so it seems to me to interpret the words of the dummy column that Dain wrote to put on the table by Meiri from a piece he bit and what he wrote or in a bowl by Meiri from a piece that remained even though he wrote in one Baba.
