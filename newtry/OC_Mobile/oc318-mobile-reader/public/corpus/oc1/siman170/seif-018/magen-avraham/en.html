He should not take his portion. For it is as though he rejects what the host gave him; therefore, he should first leave it there. Beit Yosef. In Chullin 6 it brings: a slave who eats from his master's food may cut off and give a slice to his son, to his daughter, and to the slave of his beloved, and he need not be concerned for theft from the master, for this was the custom. It seems to me that everything depends on the custom, and in a place where people are particular it is forbidden. When one makes a meal, it is derech eretz to pour drink himself for those reclining, as stated in Kiddushin 32.
