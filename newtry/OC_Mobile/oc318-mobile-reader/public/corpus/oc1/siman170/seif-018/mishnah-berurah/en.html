(39) He should not take his portion, etc. The Bach explains that otherwise it appears that his portion is contemptible in his eyes and that he does not want it, so he hands it to the attendant. He should therefore leave it before him, showing that it is acceptable to him, and only because he does not need to eat it does he later give it to the attendant. The Mechaber’s phrase “some mishap” means that this may cause a quarrel between the host and the guest. Levush explains it literally: he should not give it immediately to the attendant because something may go wrong with the meal and this portion may then be needed for distribution among the other diners. Rather, he should leave it until after the meal and then give it to him. The Taz writes that according to this explanation, he need wait only until each person has received his proper portion. Magen Avraham writes that when one makes a meal, proper conduct is for him personally to serve drinks to the diners, as stated in Kiddushin 32b.
