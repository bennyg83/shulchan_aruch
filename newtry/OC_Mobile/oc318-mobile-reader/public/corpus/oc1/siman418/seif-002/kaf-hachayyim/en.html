2) There. It is forbidden to fast on Rosh Chodesh. The members of the sacred community of Worms fast because of the calamity that occurred on Rosh Chodesh Sivan in the year 4856. Their custom is to read the Rosh Chodesh Torah reading in the morning and the passage Vayechal toward evening, and to deliver a derashah. Rokeach, siman 212; Beis Yosef, siman 579. Rama MiPano writes in a responsum that this practice does not establish a general precedent. Magen Avraham in this siman, subsection 1. See below, subsection 7.
