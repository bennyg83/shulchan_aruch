4) [Se'if 2] A fast may not be decreed, etc. If they did decree one, their decree has no validity and they may not begin fasting. Beis Yosef; Olas Shabbos, subsection 2; Peri Chadash. The later authorities write likewise.
5) But if they began, etc. For example, a beis din decreed a series of three fasts without realizing that one of them fell on a day when fasting is forbidden, and they already observed the first fast, they do not interrupt the series. Peri Chadash. The later authorities agree with this explanation, although other interpretations have also been offered.
