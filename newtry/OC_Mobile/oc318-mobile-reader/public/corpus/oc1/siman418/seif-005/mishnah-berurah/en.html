(א) Dream-fast — for one may fast for it even on Shabbat and Yom Tov, as above in siman 288; and the same if he fasted because of a vow.
(ב) Fast-for-a-fast — in order to atone for him for nullifying the joy of Rosh Chodesh, as above in siman 288 regarding Shabbat. And Magen Avraham wrote that if one fasts a dream-fast on Rosh Chodesh Nisan or Rosh Chodesh Av he need not sit fast-for-fast, for some say it is a mitzva to fast on them, as explained below in siman 580.
