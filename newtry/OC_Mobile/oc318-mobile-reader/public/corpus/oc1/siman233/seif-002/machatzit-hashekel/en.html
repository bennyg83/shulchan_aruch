(Seif kaf bet) And the essence etc. — that it is forbidden to offer after it, as it is written regarding the afternoon tamid — upon it the fats of the peace offerings; and they expounded — upon it — upon the morning tamid are all the offerings complete, and not after the afternoon tamid:

Therefore the primary time of Mincha is from 9 and a half, for that was the time of offering the tamid each day:

And see in Tosafot beginning of Avodah Zarah and likewise end of siman 286. Meaning: if he did not pray Musaf until Mincha Gedolah time arrived — some say he should pray first Mincha and afterward Musaf — and see there the reason:

Only when Pesach falls on erev Shabbat etc. — since the Pesach slaughter was after the tamid and also they needed to roast it immediately on erev Shabbat, for its roasting does not override Shabbat and the offering of Pesach; and its roasting took two hours — and therefore they needed to advance the tamid two hours, namely as our law at six and a half; and the duration of its offering was one hour and was completed at seven and a half; and afterward two hours for offering Pesach and its roasting as stated. And if so, the tamid was not offered at Mincha Gedolah time except on erev Pesach that fell on erev Shabbat:
