For prayer. And if he washed his hands for prayer, even though he interrupted afterward in his study, such as at a time when they expound, he need not wash again; nevertheless on Shabbat when they interrupt for selling matzot it appears to me that he must wash again for Musaf prayer unless he did not distract his mind. (And in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he ruled that all the time he is in the synagogue he need not wash his hands again, for presumably he did not distract his mind.) And if he washed his hands for eating and ate, nevertheless he must wash for prayer since he did not wash for prayer; unlike if he washed his hands in the morning which is washing for prayer — see siman 4.
