Standing from his study etc. And proof from what they say chapter 9 of Bava Metzia in Avot HaOlam — that they nullified his hands (this refers to the Gemara) — explanation: even though regarding the dead it is not relevant to say they touched an unclean place, nevertheless they require washing; and nevertheless it appears to me that if he washed his hands for prayer, even though he interrupted afterward in his study, such as at a time when they expound, he need not wash again — and so in Berachot daf 23 and siman 92 seif 5; and nevertheless on Shabbat when they interrupt for selling matzot it appears to me that he must wash again for Musaf prayer unless he did not distract his mind; and if he washed his hands for eating and ate, nevertheless he must wash for prayer since he did not wash for prayer; unlike if he washed his hands in the morning which is washing for prayer — siman 4 as stated:
