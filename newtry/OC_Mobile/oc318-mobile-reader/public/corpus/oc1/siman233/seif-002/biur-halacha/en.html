Even though he does not know any filth on them — from this wording it appears ostensibly that it is specifically with ordinary hands; but if he knows they are clean, such as that he washed his hands for eating and wants to pray, he need not wash; and what the Rama copied — even though he rises from his study — is not a contradiction to this, for even though truly l'chatchila certainly he should be careful not to scratch his head in the middle of study and not to touch covered places, nevertheless if it happened that he touched his hand in some covered place, behold the law is that he need not wash his hands but rather that he clean his hands, as explained above in siman 92 in Mishna Berurah — see there. [And what the Minhagim brought as proof from Eshel Avraham is merely an asmakhta in my humble opinion.] Unlike when he washed his hands for eating, for certainly his hands are clean — it would seem he need not wash his hands for prayer; however in Magen Avraham he stated the matter to the contrary from what we wrote, that even if he washed his hands beforehand for eating he must wash his hands for prayer; and Eshel Avraham and Shaarei Teshuvah copied this as halachah; and therefore the wording of the Shulchan Aruch that wrote even though he does not know etc. is not precise:
