And onward he fulfilled. It implies that before then he did not fulfill; and even though truly its time is after 6 hours as stated in Yoma daf 28 — nevertheless since we are not expert he does not fulfill:

And its primary time. The primary time of slaughter is d'oraisa from 6 hours and a half, only that they would offer it each day from 9 and a half in order to offer first individual offerings which are forbidden to offer after it; and therefore the primary time of Mincha is from 9 hours and a half (and nevertheless if one wants to eat he prays Mincha Gedolah l'chatchila as written siman 232; and all the more one who wants to go on a journey as written siman 89; and see in Tosafot beginning of Avodah Zarah and likewise siman 286) — only when Pesach falls on erev Shabbat they would offer at 6 and a half — and see siman 232:

And we calculate. In Terumat HaDeshen it implies that we calculate from dawn until tzeis hakochavim, and what is written siman 443 — see there; but in Levush and Levushei Serad they wrote that we calculate from sunrise until sunset, and likewise in Shulchan Gavoah chapter Tehilat Heaven; and it appears to me there is proof for their words from Pesachim daf 93 side b that slaughter time is only until the beginning of sunset; and likewise Tosafot in Menachot daf 20 side b; and it is like an hour and a fifth of an hour before tzeis hakochavim; however one can say they fixed it corresponding to the offering of Mincha as written siman 232; and also the Levush's intent is at the end of sunset and it is like a quarter of an hour before tzeis hakochavim — see in the second gloss:

Temporary hours. Or Zarua siman 89 and siman 261 — what he wrote unlike one who wrote that always we count the night until 12 hours and afterward the day begins — this is not so, for Mincha time is from midday and onward; and we learn from bein ha'arbayim from when the sun turns to the west — and if so it is in the middle of the day precisely; and also for the Rabbis Shacharit is until midday precisely, for again it is forbidden to offer the morning tamid; and according to R' Yehuda until 4 hours, namely a third of the day; and even though I wrote in siman 1 in name of the Zohar that regarding midnight night we count the night as 12 hours — for regarding the Holy One blessed be He night is like day it illuminates — and therefore He always counts 12 hours of night; and we need to aim at the time that is His will before Him; but the prayers corresponding to the tamid offerings they fixed, and everything is given into our hands per the place and manner of the sun's course; and likewise Shabbatot and festivals; and so too Maharil in responsum siman 163; and see siman 443:

Always as one. Explanation that one should not conduct himself sometimes this way and sometimes that way, and all the more so that he should not pray on one day Mincha after plag hamincha and Maariv before tzeis hakochavim:
