Mincha. What is called the afternoon prayer Mincha — because Mincha is language of bringing in, like leminach yoma; and it is the time of the beginning of the sun's setting; and in the responsum of the Rav Mohar"a he wrote: since we learn Maariv from Shacharit, for there the libations are stated explicitly — therefore they called it by that name Mincha, to teach that also in the afternoon tamid libations are offered with it; and the sage Mohar"i Matzliah challenged him — see there; and according to his view it is called Mincha because the sages disagree with Rabbi and it is stated explicitly regarding libations in the afternoon tamid; and see responsum of Magen Avraham from Panu siman 22; and in responsum of Beit Yosef siman 514 he explained for himself that it is language of offering, as the translator rendered Mincha — you shall offer etc.; and see in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah more reasons for the name Mincha; and what he brought in name of Sefer Chasidim:
