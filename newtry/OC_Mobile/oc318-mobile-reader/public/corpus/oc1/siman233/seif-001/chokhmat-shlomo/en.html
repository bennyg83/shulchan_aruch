Seif 1 in the gloss — and for us in these countries where the custom is to pray Maariv from plag hamincha. I was asked regarding one who came to the synagogue and did not pray Mincha and the congregation already began praying Maariv — whether he should pray Mincha in the synagogue or go outside. And I answered that it appears he should not pray in the synagogue but only outside, as Beit Yosef wrote in siman 263 regarding Shabbat. And behold, although it entered my mind to distinguish: that on Shabbat, since on Shabbat it is forbidden to pray a weekday prayer and if he prays the 18 blessings it shows he is still weekday for him while the congregation already made Shabbat — then it would be lo titgodedu making factions; but on a weekday, since the 18-blessing prayer in Maariv and Mincha is the same — then to the eyes of observers there is no change, only in his heart he intends for Mincha — then it would not appear as factions; and it would appear there is further proof from what the Taz and Magen Avraham debated in the laws of Mincha regarding one who came to the synagogue and did not pray Mincha while the congregation prays Mincha — whether if he prays with them this is considered prayer with the congregation — then it is proven that in any case it is permitted for him to pray Mincha there where they pray Maariv; however this can be rejected — one can say the intent is if he prays in the courtyard and they in the synagogue; but from the words of Eshel Avraham there seif kaf zayin in siman 236 who wrote — and this is his language there: and further one must distinguish, for specifically at the time the congregation prays it is like a different prayer [the word lo is a typo there] but if he prays actually with them it is like praying with the congregation — end of his words. And behold from this it is proven that when praying actually with them is discussed — if he is in the courtyard and they in the synagogue it would only be at the time the congregation prays; but it appears there is no proof from this; and on the contrary, according to my distinctions above it fits well: if he prays with the congregation when there is no visible difference from them and it is not factions through thought; but if he prays while the congregation says Shema and its blessings — then necessarily if he prays Maariv behold he is not permitted to advance his prayer before the congregation's prayer as written above in siman 90; and if so there is upon him suspicion that he advances his prayer before the congregation's prayer, and there is suspicion — for even regarding rabbinic matters they are concerned for suspicion as written in chapter 2 of Shabbat regarding the Chanukah lamp; and likewise one who walks behind the synagogue, as written in siman 90; and if we say he prays Mincha — then it would be factions and lo titgodedu; and therefore this is the main halachah: if the congregation is not standing for Shemoneh Esreh it is forbidden for him to pray Mincha, only he should go outside; but at the time the congregation stands for the 18 blessings he is permitted to stand with them to intend for Mincha; and on Shabbat in every case it is forbidden since they pray Shabbat and he weekday — so it appears to me correct as law; and see in my composition on Shulchan Aruch Orach Chayim year 5604 page 139 side b in Hilchot Tefillah siman 68 what he wrote on this to a certain scholar — see there and understand:
