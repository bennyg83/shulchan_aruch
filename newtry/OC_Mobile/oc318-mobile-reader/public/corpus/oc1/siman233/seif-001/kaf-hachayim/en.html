(1) [Seif 1] One who prayed Mincha prayer etc. — it implies that specifically b'dieved he fulfilled if he prayed from six hours and a half and onward; but l'chatchila one should not pray except from nine hours and a half and onward. And this is the Rambam's view in chapter 3 of Hilchot Tefillah; and he wrote there the reason: because Mincha prayer corresponds to the afternoon tamid they fixed its time; and because the tamid was offered each day at nine hours and a half they fixed its time from 9 hours and a half — and this is called Mincha Ketanah; and because on erev Pesach that fell on erev Shabbat they would slaughter the tamid at six hours and a half they said that one who prays after six hours and a half fulfills; and when this time arrived the time of its obligation arrived — and this is called Mincha Gedolah — end of his words; and Beit Yosef brings this — see there. However Beit Yosef wrote that from the words of the Tur and the Rosh it appears that Mincha time l'chatchila is from 6 hours and a half and onward — see there; and therefore the custom is to be lenient in time of need to pray l'chatchila from 6 hours and a half and onward; but without need one should not pray except specifically after nine hours and a half as the Rambam's view and Maran of blessed memory.
