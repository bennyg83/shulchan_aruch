[1] [Levush] until the time of sunset etc. But in Terumat HaDeshen siman 1 he wrote that their beginning and end is from dawn until tzeis hakochavim; however in Maadanei Melech page 7 he wrote that the Rambam's words in his commentary on the Mishnah chapter 2 of Pesachim support the Levush's words — see there; and likewise ruled Tosafot Yom Tov there; and see Yerushalmi Berachot page 1 side g; and it requires study for me.
