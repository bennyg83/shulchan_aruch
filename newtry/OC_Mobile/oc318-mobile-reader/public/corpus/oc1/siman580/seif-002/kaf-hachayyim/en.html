2) The Shelah wrote on 201a: These fasts are found in three works, the Tur, the Kol Bo, and Behag, and their textual versions differ. The practice is to follow the majority of the textual versions. End quote. I saw in Sefer Megillas Taanis several other textual versions, and it appears to me that one should follow them, for they are the source from which these matters emerged. Although in Sefer Megillas Taanis newly printed in Amsterdam the notation 'another version' appears alongside it, this was an addition made by the commentator, who took it from the words of the Shulchan Aruch. In the old editions, however, the notation 'another version' is not found. It appears that one who is not accustomed to observing these fasts, and, Heaven forbid, a calamity occurred to him and he wishes to fast on Rosh Chodesh Nisan because of his distress, is forbidden to do so, since his practice is to treat that day as one on which fasting is prohibited. Magen Avraham, ibid.; Mishna Berurah, os 1.
