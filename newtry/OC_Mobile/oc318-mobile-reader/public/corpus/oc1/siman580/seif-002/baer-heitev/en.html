On the 28th. In Megillas Taanis, on the 29th.
The land. In Megillas Taanis and in Kol Bo and in Halachos Gedolos the reading is: on the 7th of Elul the bringers-forth [of the evil report] died, etc.; and so it is in Beis Yosef.
On the 7th. In Megillas Taanis and Kol Bo and Halachos Gedolos the reading is: on the 6th of Cheshvan they blinded, etc.
On the 28th. In Megillas Taanis he wrote on the 7th of Kislev; and Halachos Gedolos wrote on the 8th; and Kol Bo wrote on the 5th; and the Shelah wrote that even though one who is stringent to fast on all of them [would fast,] he should not fast on Chanukah.
It is not known. And in our Selichos it is stated that Ezra the Sofer died.
On the 5th. In Megillas Taanis the reading is on the 8th of Shevat.
On the 7th of Adar. And if [the year] is a leap year, they fast in Adar I (Maharil, siman 31); and so wrote Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah in the name of Maharil Moelin and in the name of Mahariv Bruna, that when the year is a leap year he should fast in the first [Adar]; and see Magen Avraham and Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 173.
