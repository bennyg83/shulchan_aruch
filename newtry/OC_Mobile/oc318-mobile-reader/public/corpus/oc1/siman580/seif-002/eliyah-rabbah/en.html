[2] [Levush] And one need not complete, etc. This implies that if he wishes he may complete, and so it is implied in the Rama. And I found in Sefer Shibbolei HaLeket and Sefer Tanya, siman 62, that in the Geonic responsa they raise a difficulty on this and conclude that one should not fast on Rosh Chodesh; and Beis Yosef, who raises this difficulty and does not answer anything, [this source] escaped his notice; therefore it appears to me that it is forbidden to complete [the fast], and even on other days when Tachanun is not said it is implied there that one need not complete.
