3) An eclipse of the moon is an unfavorable sign for the enemies of Israel, and therefore one must fast. Sefer Chassidim, siman 230; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur; Magen Avraham, ibid.; Mishna Berurah, os 2. Nevertheless, Maamar Mordechai, os 5, raised the difficulty: What is the reason for this fast, since eclipses of the sun and moon necessarily occur according to the fixed order of the paths and movements of the celestial spheres, etc.? He answered that nevertheless, since it is a cause of distress for the world, it is proper to fast. See there. Likewise, the author of Iyun Yaakov, in his novellae to tractate Sukkah there, expressed wonder concerning this and wrote an answer. See there. Yafeh LaLev, part 2, os 12, cited his words; see there at length. See further what I, the lowly one, wrote in my novellae to tractate Sukkah, 29a. See there.
