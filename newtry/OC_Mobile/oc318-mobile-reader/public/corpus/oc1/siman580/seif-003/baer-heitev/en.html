Destruction. It is stated in the writings that it is fitting for every Jew to weep over the burning of the Torah, for through this the Torah was delivered to the kelipos; may the Omnipresent restore it to us speedily in our days. Sefer Tanya wrote: on Friday of Parashas Chukas, individuals have the custom to fast, for on that day twenty wagonloads full of sefarim were burned in France; and they did not fix it by the days of the month, because through a dream-inquiry it became known to them that the day of the parashah causes the decree of the Torah: "This is the statute of the Torah," which we translate, "This is the decree of the Torah." Also in the year 5408 two great communities were destroyed on that day. They also have the custom to fast on the 20th of Sivan throughout the kingdom of Poland. Once it occurred that in one month it was impossible to see the moon in its renewal and to sanctify it, because there were cloudy and foggy days, and a certain rav decreed a fast; and Responsa Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 10, wrote that he has nothing to rely upon to trouble the congregation with a fast; see there.
Chilul Hashem. One who cannot fast should at least pray concerning these matters; and some abstain from meat and wine on the night before them; and in the summer, when they eat before nightfall, one need not be stringent unless they accepted upon themselves to reckon it as night after Maariv (Magen Avraham); see there; and see what I wrote above at the beginning of siman 134, s.k. 3.
