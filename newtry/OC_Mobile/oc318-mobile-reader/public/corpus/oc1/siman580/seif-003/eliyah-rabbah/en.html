[3] On the first of Nisan, etc. All these fasts were copied from the Tur, and Beis Yosef wrote that all these fasts that the Tur wrote are written in Kol Bo, siman 63, end quote. And I saw many differences between the words of the Tur and the words of Kol Bo; also the Tur wrote them in the name of Baal Halachos Gedolos, and I saw many differences between him and Baal Halachos Gedolos; therefore I cast upon myself in Sefer Eliyahu Zuta to clarify toward whom among the holy ones I should turn. In place of the 26th of Nisan, Kol Bo wrote twenty-eight, and it appears to me that the main [reading] is like the Tur, for so it is in Megillas Taanis and in Baal Halachos Gedolos folio 39 and in Sefer Tzida LaDerech. In place of the 28th of Iyar, Megillas Taanis wrote the 29th; Sefer Tzida LaDerech [wrote] the 21st; and it appears to me that the main [reading] is like the Tur, for so wrote Baal Halachos Gedolos and Kol Bo. And in place of the 18th of Av, Megillas Taanis wrote another version, the 17th, and so it is in Baal Halachos Gedolos; however, that other version: Magen Avraham wrote that it is from the commentator, and in the old Megillas Taanis texts it is not present; therefore it appears to me that the main [reading] is the 18th, and so wrote Tzida LaDerech and Kol Bo. And in Kol Bo they also reckon the 19th of Av, when a city was captured and Jews were killed; and so wrote Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah in the name of Machzor Romania. In place of the 17th of Elul, Megillas Taanis wrote the 7th of Elul, and so wrote the Responsa of the Rosh at the end of klal 13, and Beis Yosef brought it, and so wrote Baal Halachos Gedolos and Kol Bo and Tzida LaDerech, and there is no doubt that there is a scribe's error in the Tur. Kol Bo wrote: on the 6th of Tishrei a decree was issued upon our fathers that they should die; on the 7th of it the makers of the calf died; and so wrote Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah in the name of Machzor Romania; and in Baal Halachos Gedolos he wrote on the 7th and on the 10th, and there is a scribe's error, and one must say on the 6th and on the 7th, and that is the main [reading]. Further, Kol Bo and Machzor Romania reckon that on the 17th of Tishrei the son of the sage R' Yonasan of blessed memory was killed. In place of the 7th of Cheshvan, Megillas Taanis and Baal Halachos Gedolos and Kol Bo wrote on the 6th, and that is the main [reading]. In place of the 28th of Kislev, Megillas Taanis wrote on the 7th; and Baal Halachos Gedolos and Tzida LaDerech [wrote] on the 8th; and Kol Bo on the 5th; and one who is stringent to fast, and a baal nefesh, should be stringent in all of them except the 28th of Kislev, which is Chanukah. In place of the 5th of Shevat: Megillas Taanis [has] the 8th, but in Baal Halachos Gedolos and in Kol Bo and Tzida LaDerech it is on the 5th like the Tur, and that is the main [reading].
