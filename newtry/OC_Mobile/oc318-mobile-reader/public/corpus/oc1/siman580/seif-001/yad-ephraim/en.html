In Magen Avraham s.k. 9. And so wrote Tosafos and Rashi at the end of chapter 18 of Shabbos, etc.; see Yam Shel Shlomo, Chullin, at the end of the chapter "Oso VeEs Bno"; and in Eliyah Rabbah at the beginning of siman 134. And what Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah wrote, that capital cases are not concluded at night: it appears to me in my humble opinion according to what Tosafos wrote in Megillah folio 14b, s.v. "descends," etc.; see there, where it is clear that regarding one who rebels against the king they properly conclude even at night. And see Minchas Yaakov, Hilchos Niddah, who brings the words of Yam Shel Shlomo on Chullin and writes on this that he is forced to give a reason for the custom of not eating meat after the fast: one may say that this is in place of a fast, and a fast is in place of a korban, and regarding kodashim the night follows the day.
