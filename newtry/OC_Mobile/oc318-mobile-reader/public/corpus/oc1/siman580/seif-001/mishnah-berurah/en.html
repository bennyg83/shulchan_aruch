(1) And it is fitting to fast. See Beis Yosef, that some of the Rishonim are doubtful about this, and according to their view one should take care not to fast when one of these [days] falls on Rosh Chodesh; and there are those who wrote that this matter was enacted in the days of the Sages of the Talmud, and therefore a baal nefesh should be stringent upon himself if it is possible for him. Nevertheless, one who is not accustomed to fast these fasts, and some trouble befalls him, God forbid, and he wishes to fast on Rosh Chodesh Nisan for his trouble, it is forbidden, since he always treats it as a day of fasting prohibition (Magen Avraham).
(2) On them. And in Sefer Chassidim, siman 230, he wrote that when the moon is eclipsed one should fast (Magen Avraham).
(3) And it is good not to complete [the fast] on Rosh Chodesh. Because it is written in Sefer Tanya that one should not fast on Rosh Chodesh.
