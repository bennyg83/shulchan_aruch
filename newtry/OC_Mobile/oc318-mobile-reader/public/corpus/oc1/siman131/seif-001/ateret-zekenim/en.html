And the main point is to tilt the head in Shacharit when one has tefillin on his head to the right side, etc. And there are those who wrote that even when one has tefillin on his head he falls to his left side, but he tilts his head slightly to the right side; and at Mincha he tilts his head to the left side. And also according to Kabbalah there is a secret in this (Mahari Menachem Isserles).
