(1) One should not speak, etc. That prayer does not hold one back , so wrote Beit Yosef; from the precision of his language it implies that if one transgressed and spoke there is nothing in it and one falls on one's face, only that it is not accepted as much; and likewise wrote Olas Tamid; and see above siman 51 seif 7 and se'if kaf (2). Rama wrote that in ordinary conversation we have no concern unless one is engaged in other matters. Sefer Chassidim wrote that it is permitted to pray in one place and fall in another place. Seder HaYom wrote that one should say the chapter "To You, Heaven, I lift my soul." And the Acharonim wrote in the name of the Zohar that one should say the chapter of Tehillim "To You, Heaven, I lift my soul," etc., after finishing "Rachum veChannun," and one should intend that he gives over his soul to death because of his sins; but he warned greatly that one must intend this intention with all his heart, and if not, Heaven forbid, he causes death to himself before his time, because it is written "A speaker of lies shall not be established," etc. Magen Avraham wrote: It seems to me that for this reason they do not say it in these countries , until here. And in Abudarham he wrote that there are those accustomed to say it, for David composed this chapter of Tehillim in alphabetical order and it lacks from it three letters which are vav-kuf , language of emptiness , and it teaches about the emptiness of repentance , until here. And in Eliyah Zuta I found: one who says this chapter of Tehillim every day does not see the face of Gehinnom , until here. And it appears that when not at the time of saying "Rachum veChannun" there is no concern of the Zohar. See also siman 50, siman 54.
