One should not speak. It requires study — for sometimes they interrupt in supplications such as El Rachum; and one must be careful not to interrupt with speech; however in siman 51 seif 4 it implies there is not so much strictness; and so in Beit Yosef in the name of Tur — specifically when he stops and occupies himself with other matters it is forbidden, but ordinary speech we have no concern; and so siman 124 seif 4 — specifically because of repetition of the shatz the congregation must be silent, but otherwise there is no prohibition; and one may reject; nevertheless it appears to me from what he wrote; and in Rivash siman 312 he wrote that R' Elazar wife would take him with words until he forgot to fall on her face — and if so there is no proof not to interrupt; one may pray in one place and fall in another (Shulchan Aruch; and see Megillah daf 22).

To lean. The reason for leaning as Shulchan Aruch wrote in the gloss; and nevertheless they practice interrupting with a garment on the hand, for it is not considered covering, for min b'mino does not interrupt, as written siman 98.

And the main point, etc. — there is reason for this per Kabbalah; and Levush wrote that also in the morning they fall on the left side but tilt slightly to the right side, and so is correct per Kabbalah, end of his words; and in Rekanati he wrote per Kabbalah to fall on the right; and therefore one may not deviate from customs that ancient custom wrote; Mateh Moshe wrote the shatz tilts his head toward the aron; and the same applies for one who sits on the side of the menorah before the ark.

And we do not know. Shelah wrote it is fit to say "and we do not know" while sitting "what we shall do" while standing — and both in a loud voice.
