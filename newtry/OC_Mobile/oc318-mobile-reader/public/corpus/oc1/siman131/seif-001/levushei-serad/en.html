{Rama: Magen Avraham summary 2 in the gloss.} And see in Magen Avraham summary 4 that nefilat apayim is forbidden even if his face is not stuck to the ground, and therefore one must tilt; and even if there is no stone floor, as Rama wrote at the end of the siman in the gloss — this is the reason for the tilt, and this helps even if he does not lean on his hand but leans to the side so his face is not against the ground literally; and what the custom is to fall on the hand is an additional leniency, for the hand interrupts between his face and the ground, as explained in Magen Avraham at the end of the siman that interruption helps even without tilt; therefore they do both that are better — tilt and interruption — for each of these two helps alone; therefore Magen Avraham wrote they interrupt with a garment, for the hand itself is not considered interruption, as explained in siman 91; and if so there is only tilt alone, and we want two things; and so it appears to me is the explanation of the words of Magen Avraham.
