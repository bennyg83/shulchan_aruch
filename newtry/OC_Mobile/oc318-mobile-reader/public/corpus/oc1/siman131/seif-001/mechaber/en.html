The laws of nefilat apayim. And it has 8 seifim:
One should not speak between prayer and nefilat apayim. When one. falls on his face, they practiced to lean on the left side.And some say to lean on the right side; but the essential law is to lean (Rivash siman 212, Beit Yosef in the name of Rokeach): in the morning when he has tefillin on his left arm, on the right side because of honor of the tefillin; and in the evening, or when he does not have tefillin on his left, he leans on the left (Minhagim). And after he fell on his face he raises his head and supplicates a little while sitting; each place according to its custom. The widespread custom is to say "and we do not know," etc., half-Kaddish, Ashrei, Lamenatzeach (Tur); and even on days when tachanun is not said they say Lamenatzeach, except Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, Purim, Erev Pesach, Erev Yom Kippur, and 9 Av (Minhagim; and see below siman 559).]:
