(1) [Seif 1] One should not speak between prayer and nefilat apayim — for all of it is like a long prayer. Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim and Sefer Ohel Moed; and likewise Levush. And as implied from the words of the Zohar that we brought above siman 130 note 9, that one may not interrupt between prayer and nefilat apayim, see there. And likewise in Shaar HaKavanot daf 91 side 3 in the prayer text — between repetition of the Amidah and nefilat apayim he may not interrupt at all, and the essence except when saying vidui, see there; and Bach above siman 48, see there; and likewise between the eighteen [blessings] and Hallel one may not interrupt — Eliyah Rabbah note 10.
