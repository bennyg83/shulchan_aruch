1) [Seif 1] One should not speak between the tefillah and nefilas apayim. For all of it is like one long tefillah. Beis Yosef in the name of the Orchos Chaim and the sefer Ohel Moed. And so wrote the Levush. And so is implied from the words of the holy Zohar, which we cited above in siman 130, ot 9, that one should not interrupt between the tefillah and nefilas apayim; see there. And so it is written in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 51c, in the text of the tefillah, that between the repetition of the Amidah and nefilas apayim one should not interrupt at all whatsoever, except for the saying of the viduy; see there, and it was cited above in siman 48; see there. And likewise between the Shemoneh Esreh and Hallel one should not interrupt. Eshel Avraham, ot 10.
