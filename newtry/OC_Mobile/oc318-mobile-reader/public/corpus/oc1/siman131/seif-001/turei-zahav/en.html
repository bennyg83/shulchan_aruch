One should not speak between prayer, etc. — Rashba likewise, and his proof from what they said in the chapter HaZahav on R' Elazar: every day she would not let him fall on her face — can it enter your mind that she did not move from him one hour, but that she would interrupt his focused prayer to stop for other matters; and again if he had fallen on his face his prayer would not be heard as much — apparently it is not proper to interrupt — end of his words in Beit Yosef.

On the left side — the reason in Beit Yosef in the name of Kol Bo: for so they would lay the continual offering when slaughtering it on the left side, and whoever slaughters lays the animal on its left; and some hold to the right side, for the Shechinah is opposite the person — "I have set Heaven before me always" — and when he leans on his right and the Shechinah is opposite him, he intends "my left under my head" and "His right shall embrace me" — end of his words.

And the main thing is to lean, etc. — Levush wrote, and these are his words: and I heard a compromise that even in the morning when he has tefillin on his head he falls on his left side, but tilts his head slightly to the right side; and at Mincha he tilts his head to the left side; and so I have seen them practice, and so it is fit to practice, for per Kabbalah there is a secret in it — end of his words; and since there is no clear proof for any view, we may not change from what the majority of the world practiced as the Rama compromise, and the Mishnah would be considered lack of knowledge — therefore one may not change.
