Seif 1, "There is none," etc. From what is written in Bava Metzia 59b, it is not so, etc.; and it is impossible that she never moved away from him ever, rather that she would interrupt him and his prayer would not be heard then.

Seif 1, "They are accustomed to lean," in Megillah 23a, that they pray leaning to the side, as wrote in seif 8.

Seif 1, "On," etc. Shem L'cham and R' David Abudarham on the verse "His left is under my head," and in the midrash.

Seif 1, "And some say," etc. That it is the left of the Divine Presence, for it is an acronym, as wrote in chapter 5 of Yoma.

Seif 1, "Tefillin," etc. That they are a sign, and Magen Avraham, and the view of the Shulchan Aruch is primary.

Seif 1, "And the custom," etc. That we explained in Kol Bo that a person can pray sitting, standing, and falling, as Moshe Rabbeinu did, as it is written "and I sat on the mountain and I stood on the mountain and I fell," etc., and we do not know more. Tur.
