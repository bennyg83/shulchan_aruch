On "one should not" — Taz brought from the students of Rashba from the incident in the chapter HaZahav (daf 59b), and it appears to me that one should not say nefilat apayim; even an individual at home should not interrupt between prayer and tachanun, and so Beit Yosef — the wording that one should not speak between prayer and tachanun, see there. And it is reasonable — certainly optional speech is forbidden; but Amen of blessings one answers, for they interrupt on Monday and Thursday with "and He is merciful," and Magen Avraham (1).
