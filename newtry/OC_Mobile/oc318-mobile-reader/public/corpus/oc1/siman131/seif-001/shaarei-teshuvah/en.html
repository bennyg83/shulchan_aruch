Nefilat apayim — Avodat HaTefillah. And even on Monday and Thursday they practice in Eretz Yisrael and other Sephardic lands to precede nefilat apayim to "and He is merciful"; and so they practice in some places also in these lands; and see in Shatz, for so it implies in Zohar parashat Terumah, and so in the customs of the Ari, and there on Monday and Thursday they say the selichot that here they say in parashat Shovavim; and when they say "El Melech," etc., one may say; and one who acts with piety — it is an acronym for Kerov Rachum VeChanun. And Maharash Vital wrote that he found so in Tikunim; and in Sefer Orach LaTzadik he wrote that in every vidui where one confesses one must be in His praise "Ana Heaven," etc., and the rabbis agreed with him, see there. They also wrote in the name of the Ari that one should say in vidui "we dealt falsely and deceitfully," see there. And what Shulchan Aruch wrote that one should not speak, etc. — Shatz wrote in the name of Mateh Yehudah that although Beit Yosef wrote it is like a long prayer, nevertheless if he heard while still falling Kaddish or Kedushah, which is like the law of Keriat Shema and no more, etc., for it is not greater than Elokai Netzor, see there; and the widespread custom is to answer Kaddish, Barchu, and every sacred matter after finishing the eighteen [blessings] before nefilat apayim or within nefilat apayim, since nefilat apayim is optional as written below.
