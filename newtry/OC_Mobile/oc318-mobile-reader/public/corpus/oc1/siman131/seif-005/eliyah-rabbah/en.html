(5) That there is an ark and a sefer Torah, etc. The same law applies — even an individual in his house falls if there is a sefer Torah without an ark; and likewise wrote Rokeach siman 324. Olas Tamid wrote in the name of Shiyurei Keneset HaGedolah that other books have the same law as a sefer Torah, and I have not seen practiced so; and furthermore, according to this an ordinary house that has a mezuzah — and they also relied on the verse "Before the ark of Heaven," and there was a sefer Torah; also in Keneset HaGedolah itself it implies somewhat that specifically in a beit midrash the law is so — and it requires examination.
