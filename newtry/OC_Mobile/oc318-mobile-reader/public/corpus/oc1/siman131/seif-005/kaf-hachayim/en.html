(5) There one should not speak between prayer and nefilat apayim — the wording of Tur: after the shatz finishes repetition of the prayer they fall on their faces and supplicate; and so is the wording of Rambam chapter 9 of Laws of Prayer law 5; but per the words of the Ari in Shaar HaKavanot end of derush 5, repetition of the Amidah one must say vidui immediately after repetition of the Amidah, and after it VeYaavor, and afterward nefilat apayim, as explained there the reason in secret; and so in the prayer text daf 91 side 3; and see there that he wrote that even on Monday and Thursday one does not change; and Bach above siman 48, see there; and likewise in Shatz daf 147 side 2, Knesset Yisrael siman 19 note 1, Zekan LeChaim, Shaarei Teshuvah note 1, Beit Meir note 2, Chasam Sofer note 1, Kaf HaChayim for Rachamim Partzim siman 16 note 1; and he wrote in Olas Tamid in Shaar HaKavanot, and these are his words: when you confess in vidui, strike with your right hand folded like a fist on your heart — from your left side strike after strike gently while you confess, whether in the morning or at Mincha — therefore word by word of Ashamnu Bagadnu, etc., one strike, etc.; and see — these strikes you cause to strike there and lower the kindnesses that are above in the hidden place, below in the place of the heart which is the revealed place; and since the right hand is the secret of the five kindnesses in their supernal root, therefore one must strike with his right hand on his heart and intend to shake the kindnesses that are above and lower them below to the revealed place, end of his words; and Magen Avraham in siman 607 wrote in the name of Midrash Kohelet that one strikes on the heart to say "you caused me"; and they brought Shatz daf 148 side 3; and Chasam Sofer wrote letter 4 that it is good to jump his hand when striking on his heart and the thumb should be under the fingers, which hints at the attribute of mercy, and he should intend to subdue the attribute of judgment; and Ben Or Chaim wrote on parashat Ki Tisa note 3 that the place of the heart is under the breast in four or five fingers and extends toward the face, and every person should be careful to strike there, see there.
