(summary 5) And not, etc. — he stood in his place, etc. — see siman 123; meaning why Maharil did not return to his place and fell there on his face, for it is explained there siman 123 that it is forbidden — it is like a dog returning to its vomit, and he must wait there; and since it teaches "he stood in his place" — meaning there, since he was not in the place designated for his sitting, he had no place to sit; therefore he stood and fell on his face — behold it is proven like Rivash that one may do so while standing.

In order to walk four amot — although l'chatchila he must wait the measure of when the shatz begins Kedushah, even when praying alone, as Magen Avraham wrote siman 123; nevertheless here he agrees that four amot suffice. In these countries, etc., and it appears the reason is no tachanun, etc.; and nevertheless he wrote in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Sefer Ila Shlucha that one who says the psalm "To You Heaven I lift my soul" every day does not see Gehennom; therefore he wrote in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah to say it without nefilat apayim; and the Zohar warning is specifically at the time of nefilat apayim.
