(Seif 4.) They are accustomed not to fall on their faces, not in the house of a mourner, etc. N.B. Here — regarding a deceased who was not given burial, in the matter that there is upon him no obligation of meals and no aninut — if they pray beside him, whether they say Tachanun or not, this depends on the two reasons why Tachanun is not said in a house of mourning: for the one reason, as it is written "And I will turn your festivals into mourning" — if so, in this case where the deceased lies and mourning has not yet taken effect upon him, one may say Tachanun; but for the other reason, that the measure of the Merciful One is extended — if so, all the more so after death before burial, that the measure of the Merciful One is extended with certainty — therefore one should not say Tachanun; so it appears to me in my humble opinion, correctly — examine well.
