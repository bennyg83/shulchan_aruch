There are those who say that nifilat apayim applies only in a place where there is an aron and a Torah scroll. R' Eliezer ben Hyrcanus — in Perek HaZahav of Shemonesh he was there, and presumably he did not pray in the synagogue and fell on his face (see Yoreh Deah siman 334 seif 2). One must say that he had an aron and Torah scroll in his room, or he refers to a time when the congregation was praying. Nifilat apayim while seated — so wrote Beit Yosef per the view of the sages of Kabbalah. But there are those who say one need not be strict that nifilat apayim be while seated, and if he wishes he may do so standing — the choice is his (responsum of Rivash siman 412). And so it is in Maharil — he fell from standing; if it happened that he finished his prayer at the time the congregation begins Tachanun, he stood in his place and did not immediately return to his prayer place and fell on his face from standing — nevertheless one need not be strict.
