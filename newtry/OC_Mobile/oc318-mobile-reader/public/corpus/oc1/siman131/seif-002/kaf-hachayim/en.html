(2) There — one should not speak between prayer and nefilat apayim. And although nefilat apayim is considered like a long prayer and is weighty for the books of the Zohar, nevertheless if while he is still falling on his face he hears Kaddish or Kedushah, the law is like the law of Keriat Shema and no more. Mateh Yehudah note 1, Machatzit HaShekel letter 7, Shatz daf 149 side 2, Zekan LeChaim letter 6, Chasam Sofer letter 13; and what is written in Knesset Yisrael siman 19 note 14 — I do not know what doubt there is, whether he answers Kaddish or Kedushah and the like; and afterward I saw in Beit Yosef letter 6 that he also wrote the main point of the law that he may answer like Mateh Yehudah, that it is not greater than Keriat Shema, and one need not doubt even per the secret, end of his words; and likewise Shaarei Teshuvah note 1 that the widespread custom is to answer Kaddish, Barchu, and every sacred matter after finishing the eighteen [blessings] before nefilat apayim or within nefilat apayim, since nefilat apayim is optional as written below, end of his words; however what implies from his words that also before nefilat apayim one may not answer except sacred matters — or in my view, only within nefilat apayim is the law so, but before he may answer all Amens and even of berachot, as we wrote above at the beginning of siman 122, see there. And afterward I saw that Mishnat Hasidim note 1 writes so. See below note 28.
