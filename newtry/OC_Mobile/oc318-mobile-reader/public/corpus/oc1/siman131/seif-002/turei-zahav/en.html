While seated. Beit Yosef writes that the reason is based on Kabbalah. Rivash writes that there is no strict requirement in this matter.
Where there is an aron and a Sefer Torah. The aron is not essential; a Sefer Torah alone suffices. So writes Rokeach, cited by Beit Yosef: ‘A mnemonic for the matter is the verse concerning the battle of Ai, “He fell upon his face before the aron of Hashem.”’ Beit Yosef writes: ‘If this is a received tradition, we accept it; if it is based on logical inference, it may be challenged.’ It appears to me that the challenge is that regarding the incident of the concubine at Givah it says, ‘They wept and returned before Hashem.’ Shall we likewise say that weeping and repentance are proper only where there is a Sefer Torah? Clearly, no such law is derived merely from the word ‘before,’ and the same applies here. The words of the Rosh cited by Beit Yosef also imply that he does not accept this rule. He writes that the concern applies specifically when one prays with and on behalf of the congregation, whereas one praying privately in his home may do so, because when praying with the congregation he would feel ashamed, etc. This implies that the law of Nefilas Apayim applies even when one prays privately at home. One cannot say that he refers only to a home opening into the synagogue; moreover, such a case would still be considered prayer with the congregation. Nevertheless, Rama saw fit to establish the halachah in accordance with Rokeach.
And the synagogue courtyard, etc. Meaning, he prays in the courtyard while seeing the open doorway of the synagogue. This is implied by Maharil there.
Or while the congregation, etc. Levush writes: ‘I have not seen this practiced, and perhaps it refers to one who hears the congregation praying.’ This is not implied at all. Since Beit Yosef’s fundamental legal basis for the nearby restriction can be challenged, one need not be stringent in this matter. Even an individual in his home performs Nefilas Apayim when the congregation is praying, because he is considered as though standing with them, since even an iron partition does not separate Israel from their Father in Heaven. All the more so, when a minyan prays in a house at that time without a Sefer Torah, Nefilas Apayim is performed there. So it appears to me.
