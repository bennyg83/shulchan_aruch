Magen Avraham, seif katan 10. “For the conductor, a song, a chapter of Tehillim.” Regarding this passage and Birchas Kohanim, some recite them in a house of mourning and others do not. It appears improper to recite them, since mourning and Tishah BeAv are compared, and at Shacharis of Tishah BeAv they are not recited. See Taz, siman 68. Knesses HaGedolah writes similarly in siman 128, Hagahos Beis Yosef, letter 26, and in siman 551, Tur, letter 11.
