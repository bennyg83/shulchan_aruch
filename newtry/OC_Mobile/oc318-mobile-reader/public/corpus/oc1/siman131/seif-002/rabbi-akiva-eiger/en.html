{Rama: Magen Avraham summary 10 — Lamenatzeach, a song, a psalm. And the priestly blessing — some say it in a house of mourning and some do not say it; and it is reasonable that it is not proper to say it, for mourning and Tu BeShevat are compared to each other, and on Tu BeShevat in the morning there is no tachanun with it. Tosefta siman 68. And likewise in Shakanat HaGedolah siman 128 in the hagahah of Beit Yosef letter 26, and in siman 591 in Tur letter 11.}
