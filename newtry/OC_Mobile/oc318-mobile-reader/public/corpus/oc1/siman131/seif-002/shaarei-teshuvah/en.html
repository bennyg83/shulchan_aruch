Tefillin — Avodat HaTefillah; and in Kol Bo he wrote one must lean on his left side, the way of freedom, for in nefilat apayim all his sins are forgiven for him, see there. And see in Pe'er Chadash that through nefilat apayim a person merits many things, see there; and in Sheyurei Berachah he wrote in the name of Maharach Chazkuni in Sefer Shetei Yadot that when the congregation falls, even if he did not pray with them, he must do nefilat apayim with the congregation and cover himself in a tallit as the congregation does, even though he does not say nefilat apayim — he stands in the verses of song, similarly, see there.
