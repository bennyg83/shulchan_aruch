(10) While seated , Beit Yosef wrote the reason is according to Kabbalah, and in Rivash he wrote there is no strictness. Acharonim wrote in a time of pressure we may rely on Rivash; therefore if he finished his prayer and must stand at least [the distance of] four amot, and before that measure the congregation began saying tachanun, he stands in his place and falls on his face while standing with the congregation; and likewise if another stands opposite him praying Shema , it is forbidden for one person to sit opposite him even the fill of his eyes, and if he cannot go to the sides he falls on his face while standing; and so in every such case.
(11) Ark and Torah scroll within it , "ark" is not precise; the same applies to a Torah scroll alone. The reason for nefilat apayim is because we find in Scripture a hint that prostration is before the ark of Heaven, as written in the war of Ai "and he fell on his face before the ark of Heaven." If there is no Torah scroll, only other books , the view of Eliyah Rabbah and Kaf HaChayim is not to fall on his face but to say the chapter of Tehillim without nefilat apayim, and some disagree. Where there is a Torah scroll, all agree even an individual at home falls on his face.
(12) Covering the face , this is our nefilat apayim.
(13) And a courtyard of the synagogue open, etc. , meaning when he prays in the courtyard, the synagogue door must be open and one can see from that side the place of the ark , it is as praying before the ark; unlike if he prays in the courtyard on other sides, such as the synagogue door is in the west and the ezrat nashim extends also to the south side and he prays there and there is no Torah scroll , he need not nefilat apayim; and likewise if the synagogue door were closed, even if he prays in the courtyard opposite it he need not nefilat apayim. Acharonim wrote that if he prays in the ezrat nashim he also needs nefilat apayim since he can see from the windows the place of the ark.
(14) Even an individual at home , implies that even if he is far from the synagogue he also falls on his face; and behold per what is explained in siman 55 seif 20, it speaks here where there is no filth interrupting between his house and the synagogue (Acharonim).
(15) He says tachanun, etc. , and this is when the congregation also says tachanun at that time. See references 50, 100.
