{In the gloss on seif 2} And a sefer Torah within it. N.B. It is written in Asarah Ma'amarot, chapter 27, part 1, from the essay Chikur HaDin, that one who knows how to direct all his needs through the power of his thought — they make for him a place of a sefer Torah; and not everyone who wishes to take the Name may take it — end of his words. However, in the notes of Keneset HaGedolah in this siman he wrote that it is not specifically a sefer Torah, but even a place of books; and he wrote that for this reason he was accustomed to fall on his face as he learned — see there.
{There} In Magen Avraham se'if kaf (10) at the end of one section. N.B. And in Eliyah Rabbah he raised to say it on Chanukah, and so too is the primary view.
