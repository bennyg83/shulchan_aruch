Regarding the gloss to seif 2: ‘And a Sefer Torah is within it.’ Note: It is written in Asarah Maamaros, Maamar Chikur Din, part 1, chapter 27, that one who knows how to direct his intent fully through the power of thought can render the place equivalent to a place containing a Sefer Torah. But not everyone who wishes to assume the distinction may do so. However, Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah writes in this siman that it is not specifically a Sefer Torah; even a place containing sacred books suffices. He writes that for this reason he was accustomed to perform Nefilas Apayim wherever he studied. See there.
{There} In Magen Avraham se'if kaf (10) at the end of one section. N.B. And in Eliyah Rabbah he raised to say it on Chanukah, and so too is the primary view.
