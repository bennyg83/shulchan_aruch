Seif 4. The custom is not to perform Nefilas Apayim in a mourner’s house, etc. Note: Regarding a deceased person who cannot yet be buried, in circumstances where the relatives are neither obligated in mourning practices nor subject to aninus, if people pray in the deceased’s presence, whether Tachanun is recited depends on the two reasons given for omitting Tachanun in a mourner’s house. According to the reason based on the verse, ‘I will turn your festivals into mourning,’ Tachanun should be recited here, since the deceased is still present and mourning has not yet taken effect. According to the reason that the Attribute of Judgment is extended there, however, all the more so after death and before burial the Attribute of Judgment is certainly extended. Tachanun should therefore not be recited. This appears correct in my humble opinion. Examine it carefully.
