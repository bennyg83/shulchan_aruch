They practiced not to fall on their faces, neither in a house of mourning, etc. — even when a Torah scroll is there. And there is also a reason so as not to awaken and increase the attribute of strict judgment. And therefore they say Selichot in a house of mourning but not Viduyim and supplications. And on the day of milah they do not say Tachanun specifically at Shacharit, and some say they do not say it even at Mincha (Maharshal) — namely when praying at the circumcised infant. And so is practiced in the community of Brisk in Lithuania. But at the groom they do not say it all day (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 5 Hilchot Tefillah).
