The custom is not to perform Nefilat Apayim in a house of mourning, etc., even when a Sefer Torah is present. Another reason is to avoid arousing and strengthening the attribute of judgment. Accordingly, Selichot are recited in a house of mourning, but not Vidui or supplications. On the day of a brit milah, Tachanun is omitted specifically at Shacharit. Some say it is omitted even at Minchah. [Maharshal] This means when praying in the presence of the infant upon whom the brit milah was performed, and this is the practice in the community of Brisk in Lithuania. In the presence of a groom, however, Tachanun is omitted throughout the day. [Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 5 of the Laws of Prayer]
