While seated — Taz, Magen Avraham (5); and see siman 123 in Taz letter 3, who wrote that one uproots his feet and waits four amot before tachanun; and apparently the reason between prayer and tachanun also requires four amot; and I have no source for what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Orchot Chaim that tachanun and prayer are like one long prayer; and Maharil who stood in his place and said tachanun — implies he did not wait four amot, for otherwise he would have to return to his place.
