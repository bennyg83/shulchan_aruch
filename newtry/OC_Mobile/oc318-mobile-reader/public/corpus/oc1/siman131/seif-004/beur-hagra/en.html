Seif 4, "They are accustomed," etc. For it is juxtaposed to a festival in chapter 3 of Moed Katan 15b.

Seif 4, "And not," etc. For it is also juxtaposed to a pilgrimage festival, as wrote in chapter 1 of Moed Katan and chapter 1 of Ketubot.

Seif 4, "From the day of milah." That they accepted upon themselves with joy, as wrote in the refutation in Shabbat, as it is written "joy," etc.

Seif 4, "Regarding a groom for whom it is impossible," etc. Even though the chuppah is after Mincha, as on Erev Pesach which is a Yom Tov, even though slaughtering the Pesach offerings is after midnight — from the Ra'ah.
