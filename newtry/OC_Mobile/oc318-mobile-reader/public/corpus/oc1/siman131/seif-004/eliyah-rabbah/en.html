(4) While sitting, etc. However, when one finishes the prayer of eighteen blessings and the chazzan sits immediately for Tachanun, or begins Barchu, or on Motza'ei Shabbat "Vi-yhi Noam" — he should stand in his place and cover his face, for everyone; and likewise wrote Olas Shabbat; and see above siman 123; and he wrote that the masters of Kabbalah agree to this, and not like Magen Avraham. And it appears to me that the same law applies when one says Tachanun without nefilat apayim — that one need not be strict about sitting; and it appears to me that when another prays opposite him he is not permitted to sit, as above siman 102.
