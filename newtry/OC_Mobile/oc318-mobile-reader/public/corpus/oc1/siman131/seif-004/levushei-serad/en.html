{Rama: Taz summary 5 — between himself and himself at home. From what Rosh wrote plainly "between himself and himself at home" it is fine, and he did not explain specifically if there is a Torah scroll in his house — lest one err that a posek must explain his words; but the incident of R' Elazar is properly established by Magen Avraham in summary 6, that it discusses when he had a Torah scroll, for the Talmud need not explain so much; and further there it is aggadah and not a halachic ruling; and further a great man like R' Elazar presumably had a Torah scroll in his house.}
