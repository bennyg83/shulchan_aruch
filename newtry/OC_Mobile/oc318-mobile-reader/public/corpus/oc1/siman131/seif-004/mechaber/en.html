The custom is not to perform Nefilat Apayim in a house of mourning, in a groom's house, in the synagogue on the day of a brit milah, or when a groom is present there. {Rama: This applies specifically when the brit milah or the groom is in that same synagogue. But if the brit milah is not in that synagogue, even though it takes place in another synagogue in the city, Tachanun is recited. [Piskei Mahari'a, siman 81] On the day of a brit milah, when Tachanun is omitted, this applies only at Shacharit, when the brit milah is performed. At Minchah, even if they pray in the presence of the infant after the brit milah, Tachanun is recited. This differs from a groom, in whose presence Tachanun is omitted throughout the day. [Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 5 of the Laws of Prayer] One is called a groom for this purpose only on the day he enters the chuppah.}
