At night, etc. Magen Avraham writes that if Minchah continues into the night, they do not perform Nefilat Apayim. This is reasonable, and it is difficult to say that it is still considered day for this purpose. See below at the end of siman 234. Moreover, Nefilat Apayim is optional, as the poskim write. Nevertheless, perhaps Rachum VeChanun may be said without Nefilat Apayim. Olat Tamid writes in the name of Mahari that after midnight one does perform Nefilat Apayim, and so writes Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah. Magen Avraham writes that it seems preferable to omit Avinu Malkeinu in order to perform Nefilat Apayim while it is still day. This implies that during bein hashmashot one does not perform it. The Taz, however, writes that as long as it is not certainly night, one does perform Nefilat Apayim. Perhaps during bein hashmashot one does not omit Avinu Malkeinu.
