(3) A little while sitting, etc. Shelah wrote on daf 255: I saw a proper chazzan who would say these words "And we do not know what to do" in a loud voice — "And we do not know" he said sitting, "what to do" he said standing — and he said that he had such a tradition in his hand — until here. And know that the correct text is "mitratzeh umitpayeis," but those who say "umitpatheh" — it is not according to law, for Ramban in parashat Mishpatim wrote that persuasion is about a false matter.
