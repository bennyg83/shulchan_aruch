(17) At night , therefore if Mincha prayer extended until night they do not fall on their faces; and at bein hashemashot of night they practice falling. On days when they say Avinu Malkenu at Mincha he may skip it nevertheless in order to fall while still day (Magen Avraham and Shaarei Teshuvah).
(18) That it is close, etc. , and in Eliyah Rabbah he brought to be lenient from chatzot and onward, and so Magen Avraham implies.
(19) For day , and some practice extending selichot until the day is proper and then fall on their faces; but on Erev Rosh Chodesh this custom is not possible, for one does not fall on one's face on Erev Rosh Chodesh (Magen Avraham).
