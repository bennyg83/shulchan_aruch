And in Rekanati on parashat Korach it is written to extend selichot until the day is proper and then they fall on their faces, end of his words; and this custom is not possible on Erev Rosh Chodesh — see siman 581; and it appears that if Mincha prayer extended until night, for everyone they do not fall, for the beginning of night is strengthening of judgment, unlike after chatzot of the night; and it appears to skip Avinu Malkeinu in order to fall while still day.
