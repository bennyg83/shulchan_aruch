Close to daybreak. Rekanati, Parashas Korach, states that Selichos should be prolonged until daylight is established, and then they should perform Nefilas Apayim. This practice is not possible on Erev Rosh Hashanah; see siman 581. It appears to me that if Minchah extends into the night, all agree that Nefilas Apayim is not performed, because the beginning of the night is a time when the forces of judgment intensify, unlike the period after midnight. It appears to me that Avinu Malkeinu should be omitted in order to perform Nefilas Apayim while it is still daytime.
