(8) At night, etc. Magen Avraham wrote: if one extends the Mincha prayer until night, one does not fall — until here. And so it is reasonable, though strained to say that it is still considered daytime in this; and see below at the end of siman 234; and furthermore nefilat apayim is optional, as the poskim wrote; and in any event it is possible that they say "Rachum veChannun" without nefilat apayim. Olas Tamid wrote in the name of Mahari that after midnight at night they fall; and likewise wrote Shiyurei Keneset HaGedolah. Magen Avraham wrote: It seems to me to skip Avinu Malkeinu in order to fall while still daytime — until here. It implies that during bein hashemashot one does not fall; but the Taz wrote that whenever it is not certainly night they fall, and perhaps because during bein hashemashot one does not skip.
