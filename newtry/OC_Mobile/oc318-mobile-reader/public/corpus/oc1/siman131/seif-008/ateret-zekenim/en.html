An important person is not permitted to fall on his face — and this applies only when they would actually fall on their faces; but now that the practice is only to tilt the head to the side, this does not apply (my teacher in Bach). And there are those who say on 15 Av they say Tachanun (Beit Yosef in the name of Rokeach); and now the world practices not to fall on their faces on 15 Av and not on 15 Shevat (see in Minhagim HaRav Avraham Trani). And there are those who say that if there is a milah in the city they do not say supplications in the entire city, since Eliyahu comes to the city (Minhagei Dora).
