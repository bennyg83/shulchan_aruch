An important person may not fall on his face when praying with the congregation unless he is confident he will be answered like Yehoshua ben Nun.Likewise it is forbidden for anyone to fall with hands and feet extended, even if there is no stone floor (Hagahot Ashiri end of chapter Tefilat HaShachar, Rivash siman 412). But if he leans somewhat on his side it is permitted if there is no stone floor; and likewise on Yom Kippur when they fall on their faces — or if they spread grass there to interpose between them and the ground; and so they practice (Mordechai).]:
