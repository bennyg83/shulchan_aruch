(summary 8) Even, etc. — and it appears he speaks regarding covering the face — that for saying tachanun without covering the face one need not join the congregation.

When the congregation says tachanun — and not when they stand praying in another place; and this is from what he said one may fall in tachanun with the congregation.

And see siman 55 seif 20, and the same law applies here — for he wrote there that if one hears them saying Kaddish or Kedushah and he is outside the synagogue, nevertheless he may answer with them; and he wrote — specifically when nothing interrupting separates between him and the synagogue — idolatry or filth.
