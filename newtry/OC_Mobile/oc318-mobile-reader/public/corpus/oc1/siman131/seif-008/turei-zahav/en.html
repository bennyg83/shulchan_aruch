When he prays on behalf of the congregation — meaning before the congregation, for he is embarrassed — they think about him that it is not proper and not fitting to be answered; but at home it is permitted (Rosh); Beit Yosef brings in the name of Mordechai, such as when the congregation does not fall in tachanun.

Even if there is no stone floor there — for one must decree lest stone floor, which is forbidden from the Torah, as it is written "and a stone floor you shall not place in your land to prostrate upon it."

If they spread grass there — it should read or they spread, etc. — for this helps even when there is a pavement of stone, as Mordechai wrote; as Beit Yosef, Tosafot wrote in the chapter of Taanit — it appears whoever says "Blessed is He who hears prayer" after finishing tachanun while seated is repugnant, as we say from here onward it is forbidden to speak in praise of the Omnipresent — meaning to add to these blessings — end. And he means with the Name; and so Tosafot daf 28 in Eretz Yisrael, Shem Tov; therefore one should protest those who say supplications in Maamadot that conclude with Elokeinu Shebashamayim — rather they should say Baruch Shem without the Name.
