At night — Taz, and one may distinguish, for at the watch [of the night] one begins to strengthen the attribute of day; see Levush 63 and Eliyah Rabbah letter 8; and see Tur in the name of Rav Neturai that nefilat apayim is optional (since they do not fall in a groom's house), see there; and Pri Chadash brought proof from Megillah 22 — Rav did not fall on her face because he did not wish to trouble the congregation — implies it is optional (see siman 268 seif 13, and I would apply it here); and Eliyah Rabbah wrote letter 8: without nefilat apayim there is no concern he will say the psalm Rachum veChanun, see there; and the reason is that nefilat apayim is to humble the attribute of judgment — at night one should not do so; but without nefilat apayim there is no concern to say a psalm of Tehillim, and so we explained.
