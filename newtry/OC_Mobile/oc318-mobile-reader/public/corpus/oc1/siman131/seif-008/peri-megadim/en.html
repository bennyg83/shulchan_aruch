At night. See Taz. One may distinguish the pre-dawn watch, when the attribute of day begins to prevail. See Levush, seif 3, and Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 8. Tur cites Rav Natronai that Nefilas Apayim is optional, as shown by the fact that it is omitted in a groom’s house. Pri Chadash proves this from Megillah 22, where Rav did not fall upon his face because he did not wish to burden the congregation, implying that it is optional. See siman 268, seif 13, though that case is not fully comparable. Eliyah Rabbah writes that when Nefilas Apayim is omitted, there is no concern in reciting the passage beginning “Compassionate and gracious.” Nefilas Apayim is intended to subdue the attribute of judgment, and therefore should not be practiced at night. Without Nefilas Apayim, however, there is no concern in reciting a chapter of Tehillim.
