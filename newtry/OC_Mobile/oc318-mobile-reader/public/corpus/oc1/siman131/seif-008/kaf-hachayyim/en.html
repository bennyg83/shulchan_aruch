8) And one must confess while standing, as stated in the Shulchan Aruch, siman 607, seif 3; and so it is written in Shaar HaKavanos, the fifth of the discourses on the night [of Yom Kippur], folio 55a. And the Magen Avraham wrote there, in s.k. 4, that one should not lean, as stated at the beginning of siman 585; and he means that there he wrote in the name of Darkei Moshe and the Maharil that one should not lean on something that, were it removed, one would fall; see there. And the Shalmei Tzibbur cited it, folio 148c; and so wrote the Ben Ish Chai, parashas Ki Sisa, ot 7. And see above siman 94, seif 8, and our words there, ot 30.
