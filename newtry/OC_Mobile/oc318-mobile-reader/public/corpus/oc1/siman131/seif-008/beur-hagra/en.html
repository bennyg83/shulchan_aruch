(Seif 8 — when praying, etc.) Tosafot on that passage in the name of the Yerushalmi.

(And likewise, etc., even if there is no, etc.) For Rosh explained the reason of Abbaye and Rava that they pray leaning — because of the prohibition of prostration on a stone floor; unlike Rif who wrote it is because of R' Eliezer; and he brought proof that the Gemara brings it in the end of Berachot regarding prostration with hands and feet extended; and Rivash wrote in siman 412 in the name of Rav Serira — they are concerned for a stone floor even if it was not there, lest it was there and the Temple was destroyed and rebuilt upon it when he is there; but Rambam's view is to permit with something interposing between his face and the stone floor, see there.

(But if, etc., if there is no, etc.) But on a stone floor it is forbidden even in such a case per Rosh's explanation — for therefore they pray leaning, for he holds that even without extending hands and feet it is forbidden d'rabbanan; and this is their teachers; and so Tosafot on that passage s.v. veAbbaye, etc., and the first version in the Gemara; and per Rav Serira's explanation — their teachers that even without a stone floor they would not fall as mentioned; but without extending hands and feet it is permitted, even per the first version, even per Rashi that d'rabbanan they did not decree.

(And likewise, etc.) Magen Avraham.
