(35) And the widespread custom, etc. — and in all these there is no tachanun, also at the Mincha before them. And behold all these seifs are regarding tachanun; but for Lamenatzeach they say it in all of them except Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, Purim, Erev Pesach, Erev Yom Kippur, and Tu BeShevat; and likewise they do not say it in a leap year on the fourteenth and fifteenth of Adar Rishon; also they do not say it in a house of mourning (Acharonim). They also wrote that Al aruch apayim and Lamenatzeach share the same law.

(36) Until after Shavuot — and there are places that practice not falling all six days after Shavuot, because the offerings of the festival of Shavuot had tashlumin all seven.
