7) Every viduy that one confesses should have at its beginning 'Ana Hashem, Eloheinu v'Elohei avoseinu,' etc. Responsa Orach LaTzaddik, Orach Chaim, siman 4; and there is the concurrence of the rabbis there. Machazik Berachah, ot 8, and in his sefer Kesher Gudal, siman 19, ot 3; Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 147c; Zechor L'Avraham there; Shaarei Teshuvah, ot 1; Beis Oved, ot 1; Chesed LaAlafim there; Kaf HaChaim there. And here in the holy city of Yerushalayim, may it be rebuilt and established, in the congregation of chassidim of the Midrash Beis El and Beis Tzion, they say the viduy according to the order of the alef-beis, the twenty-two letters and the five letters of manatzpach [the final forms], as follows: 'kizavnu, ka'asnu, maradnu, marinu devarecha, ni'atznu, ni'afnu, pashanu, pagamnu, tzi'arnu av va'em.' And so wrote the Chesed LaAlafim, ot 2; Kaf HaChaim there, ot 7.
