(7) Even an individual in his house, etc. The Levush explained that the individual's house is adjacent to the synagogue and he hears there when the congregation prays; and it appears to me even if the door of the synagogue is locked; and likewise Shiyurei Keneset HaGedolah explained in one explanation; and that which I wrote before about the courtyard of the synagogue — this discusses when it is not at the hour when the congregation prays. And proof for this appears to me from what Agur wrote — the reason for an individual in his house is because even a partition of iron does not separate between Israel and their Father in Heaven; if so, what do I care if the door is locked? And likewise it implies above siman 55 seif 20, and to exclude the second explanation that Shiyurei Keneset HaGedolah wrote there. And from this reason it appears to me that there must be that no filth separates between the synagogue and his house, as above, for there too they wrote the reason that even a partition, etc.; and likewise the law above siman 128 seif 24 regarding people before the Kohanim and at their sides — that even a partition, etc., as mentioned above; and likewise it implies in Magen Avraham; and see in Be'er Sheva daf 56. The view of the Taz: when the congregation prays, an individual in his house falls on his face even though he does not hear that the congregation prays; and Magen Avraham wrote — specifically at the hour when the congregation says Tachanun he says with them.
