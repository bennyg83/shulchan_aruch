(Not to fall.) Austrian Jews practice not to eat legumes on days when they do not fall, because it resembles rolling mourning (Maharil).

(On 15 Av.) It is explained in Bava Batra 121a — the Sages gave four reasons that it was a great festival in Temple times.

(On 15 Shevat.) Rosh Hashanah for trees; Ashkenazim practice to increase varieties of tree fruits (Tikkun Yissachar 62:25).

(On Lag BaOmer.) An incident: one practiced all his days to say "comfort" in "Who builds Jerusalem," and on Lag BaOmer they said it and he was punished for it because it is Yom Tov [intent of the Ari].
