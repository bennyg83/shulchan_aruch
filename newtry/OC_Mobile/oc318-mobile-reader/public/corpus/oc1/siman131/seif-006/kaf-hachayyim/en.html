6) And he wrote further there in Shaar HaKavanos, and this is his language: A person must also say all the details of the viduy, all of 'Ashamnu, bagadnu,' etc.; and even though the person himself knows that not all those things are in him, and it appears as though he speaks falsely, there is no concern in this, and on the contrary he is compelled to say so. And through this you will understand a proper reason why the viduy was instituted in the plural, for it should have said 'ashamti, bagadti,' etc. But the matter is that besides that for which a person is punished for his own sins, he is also punished for his fellow, by reason of the mutual responsibility (arvus), for all Israel are one body composed of many limbs; if so, it is fitting for a person to confess in the plural, in order that he confess also for his fellow. Therefore, even if there are transgressions in which he has not sinned, it is possible that his fellow has sinned in them, and therefore he must confess in the plural, even though he did not commit that transgression. End of his language. And the Shalmei Tzibbur cited it, folio 148b; and see there in the Shalmei Tzibbur what he wrote of an incident that occurred in the days of the Rambam; see there. Birkei Yosef, ot 6. And see further the Chesed LaAlafim, ot 3, who wrote additional reasons: because it is possible that he sinned in other gilgulim, and he wrote that this is the reason we say in the text of the viduy 'we have sinned, we and our fathers,' for the previous gilgul is called a 'father'; and he wrote another reason: because there are certain transgressions that a person tramples with his heels, yet they are severe, such as 'anyone who becomes angry is as though he worships idols,' and 'anyone who shames his fellow in public is as though he sheds blood,' and the like; see there. And further, there is one who explained that even though he has not sinned in these transgressions, it is possible that one from the root of his soul has sinned, and therefore he confesses for it in order to rectify it.
