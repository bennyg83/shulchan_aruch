(30) On the fifteenth of Av — for in the Gemara it states that it was a great Yom Tov in Temple times.
(31) On the fifteenth of Shevat — for it is Rosh Hashanah for trees, and Ashkenazim practice increasing then varieties of tree fruits.
(32) Before it — it implies from this that on the fifteenth of Av and the fifteenth of Shevat they fall at the Mincha before it, and they do not practice so (Erev Torah).
(33) On Purim — meaning on both days, and likewise on Purim Katan in a leap year; and in all these they do not say [tachanun] also at the Mincha before it; and likewise on Lag BaOmer, except on Erev Rosh Chodesh and on the eve of Yom Tov they say [tachanun] at the Mincha before them.
(34) On Yom Kippur — and on other days of Yom Tov one need not explain, for it is explained in the adjacent seif.
