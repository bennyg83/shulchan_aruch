(6) That which is open to the synagogue, etc. And according to this they are accustomed to fall in the ezrat of the synagogue; and if the door is locked they fall without covering — so wrote Olas Tamid in the name of Maharil. And one may ask: if one falls, what do I care about covering? And in Maharil himself he did not write so, but this is his language — one should say supplication without covering the eyes, for then one cannot fulfill "and fall," etc.; it implies that he wishes to say that one should say Tachanun without nefilat apayim at all, and he calls nefilat apayim "covering the eyes," since they cover in nefilat apayim; and this too is the intent of Rama and the Levush, as they wrote that they say Tachanun without covering the face; for Rokeach wrote explicitly that one does not fall in a place where there is no sefer Torah, and likewise it implies in Minhagim and in all the Acharonim; but certainly nefilat apayim is called covering the face, and this is clear — see Hagahot Minhagim.
