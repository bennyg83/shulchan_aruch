Seif 6, "They are accustomed," etc. As wrote in the last chapter of Taanit, they were not, etc.

Seif 6, "And not on the 15th of Shevat." For it is Rosh Hashanah for trees, and like all four days of Rosh Hashanah which are Yom Tov.

Seif 6, "And not on Rosh Chodesh." For they are all Yom Tov and forbidden in eulogy and fasting, and therefore it is impossible also to say Lamnatzeach on them, as wrote in seif 1.

Seif 6, "And not at Mincha," etc. For from the beginning of the prohibition of eulogy, as wrote in chapter 3 of Moed Katan "from when do they erect," etc., Pardes.

Seif 6, "On Lag BaOmer," etc. On which the students of R' Akiva ceased dying, as wrote at the end of Taanit regarding the days of speaking.

Seif 6, "On Erev Yom Kippur," etc. And on other Erev Yom Tov likewise, as wrote in seif 7, and see end of Parashat HaShavua in Chullin chapter 3, and see Tosafot there s.v. "k'divrei" and "that which is not," etc.
