1) [Se'if 1] Perhaps he does not wish to establish an eruv in that direction, etc. Perhaps he does not wish to gain two thousand amos on that side at the cost of losing two thousand amos in the opposite direction. Tur; Levush.
2) There. Except for his minor son and daughter, etc. The reason is that an eruv techumin may be established only for a mitzvah purpose, as stated in the following siman. Since it may be established only for a mitzvah purpose and the father wishes to educate them in mitzvos, he may establish it for them even against their will. Beis Yosef; Levush.
