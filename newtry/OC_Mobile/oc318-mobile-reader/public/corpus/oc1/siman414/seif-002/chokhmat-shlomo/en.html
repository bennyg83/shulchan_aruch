Seif 2: A minor six years old or younger goes out with his mother's eruv. NB: See what the Magen Avraham wrote, that from siman 640 seif 2 it implies that if he is sharp and does not need his mother, even when he is five he does not go out with his mother's eruv. This is astonishing, for that is precisely the Gemara's objection in Eruvin 82a; why did it not answer there: here it speaks of a sharp minor, and here of a minor who is not sharp? See there. The body of the Gemara is also difficult: why did it not answer that a minor of four or five is one who needs his mother, and from six and above certainly does not need his mother, but between four or five and six there are some who need their mother and some who do not; therefore in sukkah, whose root is Torah law, they were stringent, while in eruvin, whose root is rabbinic, they were lenient? It is also difficult with the poskim, for the Gemara there concludes by distinguishing whether the father is in town or not; why did the Shulchan Aruch not distinguish this way? However, I examined the Eliyah Rabbah in the name of the Beit Yosef and saw that the Beit Yosef already noticed this. Still, the first questions remain difficult and require study. Examine carefully.
