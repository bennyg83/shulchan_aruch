Along length ten — Rosh's view: this measure suffices to permit karpef though not complete wall — not worse than breach ten and fence, even not at once; partitions never nullified — collectively now more than ten for dwelling suffices; new wall here same. Ravyah and Tur copied; must be new wall not more than ten amot from old — else saying new wall like breaching old impossible — still open to world, new wall cannot seal not wall-to-wall, sides open more than ten; deals not distanced more than ten — real breach in old wall still does not forbid karpef, new wall blocks outsiders, side entry not more than ten breach — like non-forbidding openings.
