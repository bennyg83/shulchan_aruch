The Sages prohibited, etc. Under Torah law, these are complete private domains, as stated in Eruvin 67b: Regarding a karpeif larger than beis sasayim that was not enclosed for residential use, even if it measures a kor or even two kor, one who throws an object into it from a public domain is liable. The Tur and Beis Yosef cite this. This applies when the partitions are ten or more handbreadths high. Rambam, beginning of chapter 16. See above, siman 346, subsection 24.
