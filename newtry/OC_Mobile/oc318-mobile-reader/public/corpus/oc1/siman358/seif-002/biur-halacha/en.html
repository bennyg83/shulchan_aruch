(א) Or he opened a door for it — see Peri Megadim doubts window also helps; Avodat HaGershuni and Or Ne'erav from Meiri imply windows too; novel, Gemara mentions only door; say specifically door — noticeable intent to use space before door; windows mainly for light, cannot use regularly through windows — not enclosure for dwelling; Peri Megadim leaves doubt; even if window helps, only if using through that window to karpef; if only to look at karpef, certainly not:

(ב) From his house — if outer house partition fell and inner remains, does not help, made inward not outward (Magen Avraham):

(ג) And afterward enclosed — but if he enclosed then opened door from house, does not help. If initially enclosed karpef on three sides and on fourth built house and opened door on side — Peri Megadim permits: before house partition that side was breached more than ten, enclosure counted nothing; now sealing with house wall counts as enclosure for dwelling:

(ד) And if difficult to breach wall — Gra in Biur disputes this remedy of Terumat HaDeshen; source: swallowed lower, upper remain 362:2; Eruvin 93 mound five and partition five combine; not say tel less than ten is ground everyone treads — cannot combine; tel above three tefachim not "adjacent ground," not treading place — when partition added on tel, tel combines to complete ten; here pouring earth around wall less than ten does not diminish wall, not treading — like karpef ground; earth above three not convenient use, does not cancel wall but thickens wall; Terumat HaDeshen perhaps only mound not protruding four tefachim outside partition — no treading, combines; but we rule Shabbat 100 tel collecting ten from four amot is full partition — here karpef ground to wall top no four amot — full partition [unless tel naturally so, partition on tel must be within four tefachim of tel edge]; perhaps Terumat HaDeshen where earth placed intending to cancel wall. Also challenged returning earth Rama ends — useless per seif 14 new partition for dwelling fell — reverts though old remains, never enclosed for dwelling — new partition like breaching old (Rosh); suffices partition more than ten length not entire karpef (Tur, Beit Yosef); like pouring earth — when new partition taken, old not considered rebuilt for dwelling; taking earth — wall never enclosed for dwelling. Terumat HaDeshen swallowed lower: upper always for dwelling, only ineffective then — unlike here never for dwelling — Gra's view.
