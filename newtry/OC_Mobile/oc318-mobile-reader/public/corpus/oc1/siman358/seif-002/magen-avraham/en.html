(א) He built a house in it — before it was enclosed, as written seif 1; Meiri wrote Chayei Adam siman 88: if one side of the karpef is houses, the walls of the houses are considered enclosed for dwelling even though made for it afterward, like alley walls — see there and seif 1; and so from Rashi's explanation in the incident of the orchard; and specifically if he opened and in the end enclosed; but if the outer partition of the house fell and the inner partition remained, it does not help, for they were made inward not outward — siman 370; and it is implied in Tosafos daf 24 that tzurat ha-ptach helps like a partition — siman 372:

(ב) Until he completed ten — for new faces came here:

(ג) There is not ten in the height of the earth — but if there are ten in its height it is permitted in any case, as seif 66 if distanced three tefachim:

(ד) He returns and takes — even though a partition was made by itself it is permitted, as seif 8 (there); and one could say it does not help here since the essence of the remedy is intending to clear it — what of it, a purse too he will certainly take from there; and nevertheless it is nullification, as siman 372 seif 16 and in the Gemara there; and what difference if he intends in order to clear or will clear (Terumat HaDeshen):
