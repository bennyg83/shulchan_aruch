2) There. Such as gardens and orchards. They are enclosed to protect the fruit and seeds within them, not for people to reside in them. Rabbeinu Yehonasan writes that the same applies to naturally occurring partitions, such as clefts between rocks and the like, which are not considered enclosed for residential use.
