(א) (s.k. 4) Adjacent, etc. — see siman 401 seif 3; meaning there it implies somewhat the view of Rabeinu Chananel that even within techum it is not enclosed for dwelling: "and even if it was enclosed not for dwelling." If so, what is the novelty "even if not enclosed for dwelling," since within techum its law is as enclosed for dwelling? Also the three matters watchmanship, dwelling house (Magen Avraham brought all Hagahot Ashiri together); Rama in Hagahah brought only adjacent to house:

(ב) And provided, etc. — three laws: adjacent to house, watchmanship, dwelling house; watchmen's booth in Shulchan Aruch deals with day only or night only; dwelling house in Hagahot Ashiri: even if not permanent dwelling; Hagahot Ashiri: not that booth and house within karpef if built after enclosure counts — need house built before enclosure; path of reeds if orchard not enclosed adjacent to house (Hagahot Ashiri orchard); Tosafos Eruvin 23: for us more than seventy and four, one of three helps; Magen Avraham: Hagahot Ashiri not built after; Rama omitted watchmanship and dwelling house — no dispute; adjacent to city disputed siman 259; Hagahot Ashiri: wide different — partitions for houses around, enclosed from above (Beit Yosef, Ritva):

(ג) And the same if watchmanship, etc. — Hagahot Ashiri combined three laws; Rama brought only adjacent:

(ד) Rama in Hagahah — only one law: adjacent to house:

(ה) That dwells day and night — watchmen's booth in Shulchan Aruch: not dwelling for itself but guarding air before them, like gardens:

(ו) Even if not permanent dwelling — Hagahot Ashiri novelty:

(ז) It appears Hagahot Ashiri — not built after enclosure; intention from start; Hagahot Ashiri path when booth/house before enclosure; if built after orchard not enclosed, reeds path needed; Tosafos 104 tzurat ha-ptach like partition:

(ח) He shall make a path etc. Meaning if he wants to carry from the house standing outside the orchard to the house standing inside the orchard — and the orchard is not enclosed for dwelling — he shall make a path from one house to the other: a reed, a reed less than three tefachim — for we say lavud; by way of example — if one house stands in the east and the other in the west he shall make a partition of reeds as above from east to west on the south side and likewise on the north side; and between the two partitions he can carry from house to house. And meaning that there is in the orchard a residence and it is enclosed for dwelling and he need not a partition of reeds; or that the residence inside the orchard if it is not built before making the orchard — it is not called enclosed for dwelling; and if so one can say it deals there that the house inside the orchard was built only after making the orchard (and likewise the house outside the orchard adjacent to the orchard was built after the orchard) — and therefore he needed to repair with a partition of reeds; and therefore he wrote "there is there guarding" etc. Meaning in Hagahot Ashiri he wrote this language and the language "there is there" implies there was at first before the karpef the guarding or residence. And it appears to me that Tosafos too etc. Meaning Beit Yosef wrote that the proof of Hagahot Ashiri is from what we taught in Eruvin daf 23 — and further R' Yehuda ben Bava [said]: a garden and a karpef that are seventy amot and remnants upon seventy amot and remnants that are enclosed with a fence etc. — one may carry within it provided there be in it guarding or residence or that it be adjacent to the city; and we do not rule like R' Yehuda ben Bava but like R' Akiva — that where one does not hold only seventy amot and remnants etc. — he does not require enclosed for dwelling, as is written at the beginning of this siman; nevertheless from R' Yehuda ben Bava we learn for R' Akiva: since R' Yehuda ben Bava holds that also in one who holds seventy amot and remnants upon seventy amot and remnants we require enclosed for dwelling, and he holds one of these three kinds suffices — guarding or residence or adjacent to the city — and thereby it is called enclosed for dwelling — so is the law for us who rule like R' Akiva: if there is in it more than seventy amot and remnants upon seventy amot and remnants that require enclosed for dwelling — one of these three kinds suffices; for more than seventy amot and remnants upon seventy amot and remnants for R' Akiva equals seventy amot and remnants for R' Yehuda ben Bava; and on this Beit Yosef wrote that Tosafos disagree and proved that for R' Yehuda ben Bava himself one of these three kinds does not suffice to make it enclosed for dwelling except in seventy amot and remnants or in less than this; but in more than seventy amot and remnants even for R' Yehuda ben Bava one of these three kinds helps — see there in Tosafos s.v. "provided" etc.; if so likewise for R' Akiva one of these three kinds helps in more than seventy amot and remnants — so far. And on this Magen Avraham wrote that Tosafos too concede to Hagahot Ashiri and there is no dispute here; and Tosafos did not write that one of the kinds helps in more than seventy amot and remnants except for R' Yehuda ben Bava, as Tosafos proved there — and this is specifically for R' Yehuda ben Bava, for we see he is stringent in this that even in less than seventy amot and remnants or in seventy amot and remnants precisely he requires it be enclosed for dwelling — he is stringent also in this that in more than seventy amot and remnants one of these three kinds mentioned does not suffice to make it enclosed for dwelling; but R' Akiva whom we rule like — he is lenient in this and holds that for less than seventy amot and remnants or seventy amot and remnants precisely he does not require dwelling at all — likewise he is lenient also in this that even in more than seventy amot and remnants one of the kinds helps to make it enclosed for dwelling; for R' Akiva the law of more than seventy amot and remnants is like for R' Yehuda ben Bava seventy amot and remnants precisely:
