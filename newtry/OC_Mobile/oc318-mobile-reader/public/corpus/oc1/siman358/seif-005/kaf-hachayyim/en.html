5) There. And watchmen's huts. Although the watchman resides within it, it was nevertheless made not for residence but for guarding. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. If the watchman lives in it to guard by day and night, the Bach implies that it is considered a residential building. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 1, writes likewise, and this is also implied by the Magen Avraham at the beginning of siman 362. See below, subsection 26.
