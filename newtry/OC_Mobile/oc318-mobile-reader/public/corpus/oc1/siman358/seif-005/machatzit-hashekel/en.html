(א) (s.k. 5) He built in it, etc. — if one side is houses, walls count as enclosed for dwelling though made afterward, like alley walls; Rashi orchard incident; open then enclose; outer fell, inner not — partitions inward not outward; Tosafos 24 tzurat ha-ptach:

(ב) And so from Rashi orchard — Eruvin 25b vineyard by orchard wall with door to orchard; Rav Beivai thought inner wall of orchard helps; Rav Papa: inner partitions for orchard inward, not for vineyard — while outer stood, vineyard was enclosed; open then enclose; if enclosed then open, not enclosed; Tosafos 24: tzurat ha-ptach makes enclosed for dwelling like partition:

(ג) Siman 370 — Tur brought Tosafos explanation of orchard incident; per Tosafos no proof — Tosafos 24 implies tzurat ha-ptach helps; Gemara 24b wide place not enclosed for dwelling, open on one side to city and other to vineyard path; path enclosed for dwelling with residents, path ends at river wall; city permitted, path permitted, only wide forbidden; Abaye: make tzurat ha-ptach at path mouth toward river — camels drink and drop loads; Rashi: at path mouth open to wide place — tzurat ha-ptach permits path, and since path and wide open to each other, tzurat ha-ptach at path to river also permits wide — all three permitted; Tosafos: path mouth is side open to river, not toward wide — still open fully to forbidden wide — tzurat ha-ptach at path to river does not help path; since path and wide open to river, tzurat ha-ptach at path to river helps wide — wide becomes enclosed for dwelling — permitted in all three; per Tosafos tzurat ha-ptach makes enclosed for dwelling like partition:
