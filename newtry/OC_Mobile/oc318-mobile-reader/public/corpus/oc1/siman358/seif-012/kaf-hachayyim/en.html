The Sages did not take care to mention it because it is small, but it is always needed to complete the measure. Levush. This is also stated by Rambam in his Commentary on the Mishnah, chapter 2 of Eruvin. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1, writes in the name of several authorities that this is the halachah and that one may include this minute amount in the calculation. See there. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 2, writes likewise. Consequently, wherever the enclosure does not exceed seventy amos, four handbreadths, and that minute amount, it is permitted to carry throughout it.
