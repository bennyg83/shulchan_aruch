A karpef more than beit se'ah that was enclosed for dwelling and he planted most of it with trees — even if they are not planted row by row — they do not cancel the dwelling; but if most of it was sown with seeds, the seeds cancel the dwelling even if there is only beit se'ah sown; if a minority was sown — if there is only beit se'ah in it, it is permitted; more than beit se'ah — forbidden:
