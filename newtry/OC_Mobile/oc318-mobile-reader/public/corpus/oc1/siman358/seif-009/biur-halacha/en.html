(א) Karpef more than beit se'ah enclosed, etc. — if only beit se'ah, even all sown permitted for objects rested within — seeds cancel dwelling there; in beit se'ah no enclosure needed [even not enclosed at all if only beit se'ah sown — permitted objects inside]; for house objects to karpef or reverse forbidden — two domains; even less than beit se'ah same — Rosh; Shulchan Aruch seif 10; not like Rabeinu Chananel Eruvin 23b beit se'ah seeds do not cancel:

(ב) But if sown, etc. — see three things of Shmuel lenient walled city seeds do not cancel wall enclosure [houses first then wall — siman 401, Mor VeKetzia]; Chacham Tzvi copied, Yaavetz Moed Katan agreed in hardship — deals seeds not walled separately, else wall not partition for seed area — seeds like unenclosed partitions forbidden; Yaavetz may differ — Chacham Tzvi/Moed Katan as written; Shaarei Tzion many poskim no karpef/courtyard distinction — seeds cancel karpef partitions, same courtyard; cannot lenient wall; Maamar Mordechai and Sheyurei Berachah dislike leniency — at most as above:

(ג) Seeds cancel dwelling — poskim unclear if seeds cancel partitions entirely — always forbidden even after harvest must breach ten and re-enclose seif 2; or enclosure not effective while seeds present, permitted when removed [lower swallowed upper: ineffective then helps later — here seeds may cancel entirely like Meor and Peri Chadash water not drinkable — seeds displace enclosure; even winter dwelling intent or never sow again insufficient, must breach seif 2]; Rashba Avodat HaKodesh seeds cancel partitions — always forbidden like never enclosed; courtyard same — Rosh doubts, majority disagree Shaarei Tzion; courtyard less than beit se'ah after seeds removed — lenient not protested per Rosh doubt:
