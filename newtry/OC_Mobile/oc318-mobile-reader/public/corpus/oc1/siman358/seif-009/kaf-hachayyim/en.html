Ritva writes in his novellae to Eruvin 16b that just as it is stated there regarding a vertical partition without horizontal members that if there are three people, who are deemed a caravan, they are granted all the space they require, even more than six se'ah, the same applies to a karpeif that was not enclosed for residential use. The restriction applies specifically when it belongs to one person, but a caravan is granted all the space it requires, even more than beis sasayim. See there. Shulchan Shlomo cites his words.
