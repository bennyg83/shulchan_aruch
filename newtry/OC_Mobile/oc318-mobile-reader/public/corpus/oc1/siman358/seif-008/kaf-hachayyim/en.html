The Sages prohibited carrying, etc. Know that in a karpeif not enclosed for residential use that is larger than beis sasayim, the prohibition against carrying more than four amos applies in every circumstance, even if it belongs to one person. It certainly applies if two people are partners in a garden and established an eruv, for an eruv does not suffice to render it a private domain. Mateh Yehudah, siman 336, subsection 2.
