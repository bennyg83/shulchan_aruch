דין איזה מקומות נקראים מוקפים לדירה. ובו יד סעיפים:
The law of which places are called enclosed for dwelling. And in it are 14 seifim: Every enclosure that was not enclosed for dwelling — such as gardens and orchards and watchmen's booths that are made only to guard what is within — the Sages forbade moving objects in it more than four amot if it is more than beit se'ah; but if it is beit se'ah, which is the measure of seventy amot and four tefachim by seventy amot and four tefachim, it is permitted to move objects throughout it, whether it is square, round, long, or narrow, provided its length is not more than twice its width by one amah; and if it was enclosed for dwelling, even if it contains many milin, it is permitted to move objects throughout all of it:And see below siman 401 the law of ordinary cities whether they are enclosed for dwelling; and some say that ordinary our karpeifim are called enclosed for dwelling, for it is customary to open a door first and afterward to enclose (Mordechai chapter Osin Pasin); and some say further that any karpef adjacent to his house is called an enclosure for dwelling because his intention is upon it (Hagahot Oshr"i chapter 2 of Eruvin); and some) disagree (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos):'.
