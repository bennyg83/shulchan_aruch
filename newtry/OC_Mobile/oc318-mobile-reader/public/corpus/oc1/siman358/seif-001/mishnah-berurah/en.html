(א) For dwelling — human dwelling and its uses:
(ב) Such as gardens and orchards — enclosed only to guard fruit and seeds within; and the same for karpef, a large enclosure outside the city to store wood:
(ג) And watchmen's booths — sukkah of field watchmen:
(ד) That are not made, etc. — meaning even though this sukkah is made for the watchman's sitting, since their dwelling is not for itself but to guard the air before them, it is not a significant dwelling in itself — like enclosure of gardens and orchards; even if roofed it is not considered a dwelling; see Biur Halacha siman 362:
(ה) The Sages forbade, etc. — for from the Torah any place enclosed by partitions ten high, even wide as many korim, is full reshut ha-yachid and permitted throughout; nevertheless Sages forbade in a large enclosure more than beit se'ah not enclosed for dwelling, resembling somewhat reshut ha-rabbim and karmelit, lest one move more than four amot as in reshut ha-rabbim and karmelit:
(ו) Which is the measure seventy, etc. — beit se'ah is fifty by fifty amot, two thousand five hundred square amot; beit se'asayim five thousand square amot; as a square field it is about seventy amot and four tefachim by seventy amot and four tefachim:
(ז) And four tefachim — and slightly more, about a fingerbreadth; not mentioned because slight; nevertheless count it; anything more than seventy and four tefachim plus that amount — permitted throughout:
(ח) Permitted to move throughout — Sages relied on Tabernacle courtyard measure beit se'ah where they moved throughout:
(ט) Whether square — rule: anything not more than five thousand square amot we do not care whether round, square, or other shapes, as below:
(י) Or long and narrow — e.g. one hundred long and fifty wide, square of seventy and four tefachim:
(כ) Provided length not more than twice width — at least not more than one hundred amot length, for large area even in length alone differs from reshut ha-rabbim and karmelit; in one hundred amot they did not decree, as Tabernacle courtyard length one hundred width fifty:
(ל) One amah — less than one amah they did not decree; only if at least beit se'ah total; if less than beit se'ah total, even much more than one hundred amot length permitted if enclosed:
(מ) Whether enclosed — see siman 401: walled city; from default we say enclosed for dwelling — build houses first then wall, as below:
(נ) That ordinary karpeifim, etc. — house door open to them:
(ס) Customary — Rama: where one does not remember if karpeifim built before houses for dwelling use; most karpeifim built after houses for house use — not universal; see local custom:
(ע) Adjacent to his house — anything adjacent to city counts adjacent for this view:
(פ) His intention upon it — even this view: only if he built house then enclosed; even without opening, adjacent to city implies frequent use, presumably enclosed for dwelling:
(צ) Some disagree — this view: even adjacent to house, without open-then-enclose does not help; Acharonim rule thus:
