(א) And watchmen's booths — even though the watchman dwells within, nevertheless it is not made for dwelling but only to guard:

(ב) Long and narrow — such as if it is one hundred amot long and fifty wide, amounting to the square of seventy and four tefachim:

(ג) One amah — but less than one amah is permitted:

(ד) Adjacent to his house — that is, within two thousand amot he can walk (R' Yonatan); see siman 401 seif 3; and the same if there is a watchmen's booth where one dwells day and night, or a dwelling house even if one does not dwell there permanently (Hagahot Maimoniyot and Hagahot Ashiri); and see in Mahariv; and it should be said that Hagahot Ashiri deals where the house was built in it beforehand, for he himself wrote chapter 2 orchard not enclosed for dwelling adjacent to a house and he built a house in it — make a path, etc. — implying not if built afterward; therefore he wrote there is watchmanship, etc.; and it appears to me that Tosafos also did not write it does not help except for Rivam who holds less than seventy requires watchmanship, therefore in more than seventy it helps; but for us who do not require watchmanship in less than seventy, in more than seventy it helps; and it should be said that therefore Rama omitted the law of watchmanship and dwelling house, for there is no dispute; however adjacent to a city some disagree, as siman 259 wide is forbidden — see there; and Hagahot Ashiri holds wide is different, for partitions were not made for the width but he built houses around them and it is enclosed from above, as Beit Yosef this siman in the name of Ritva — as above:
