(א) Beit se'ah — for then it is called karmelit; but if it is beit se'ah it is called karpef and it is one domain with the courtyard for objects that rested within it, and not for objects that rested inside the house, as written siman 372:

(ב) And narrow — such as if it is one hundred amot long and fifty amot wide, which amounts to the square of seventy amot:

(ג) One amah — like the courtyard of the Tabernacle that was one hundred amot long and fifty wide; but less than one amah is permitted. Magen Avraham:

(ד) Adjacent to his house — that is, within two thousand amot that he can walk in it; and the same if there is a watchmen's booth where one dwells day and night, or a dwelling house even if one does not dwell there permanently (Hagahot Maimoniyot); and see Magen Avraham:
