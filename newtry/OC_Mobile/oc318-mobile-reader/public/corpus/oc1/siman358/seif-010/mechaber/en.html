If someone has a garden in his yard and it is most of his yard, even if it is only two seahs, he may not carry from the garden or from the yard to the house. If it is bigger than two seahs, he can only carry in the garden and the yard within four amot. If it is the minority of the yard, if it is more than two seahs, the whole yard is forbidden [with regards to carrying]. If it is two seahs or less, it is forbidden to remove something from [the garden] to the house.
