One who has a garden in his courtyard — if it is most of the courtyard, even if it is only beit se'ah, he may not move objects from it or from the courtyard to the house; and if it is more than beit se'ah he may not move objects in it or in the courtyard except within four amot; and if it is the minority of the courtyard, what is in it more than beit se'ah forbids the entire courtyard; and if there is beit se'ah or less in it, it is forbidden to remove from it to the house:
