Within — Taz that yetzei with kamatz the yod, and to say that if after toch kedei dibbur he blessed he did not fulfill the blessing — he is an forced one and it should have said he does not bless, rather certainly with tzerei; and since so it is reasonable that even after toch kedei dibbur he blesses; and what Mordechai wrote — one can say there the joy still exists; and Abudraham note 6 in the name of Ran end of first chapter of Pesachim implies that after toch kedei dibbur he does not bless at all; and so too in Sefer Chemed Moshe; and Abudraham wrote that the words of Taz appear somewhat [correct] — and it requires further study.
