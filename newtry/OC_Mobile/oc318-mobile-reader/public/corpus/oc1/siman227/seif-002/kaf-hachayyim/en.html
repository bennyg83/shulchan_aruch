2) Likewise, over a star with a tail and a radiant rod, which is the second explanation of the Rambam, Rav Hai Gaon, and Aruch, one must recite the blessing. The might of the Creator, may He be exalted, is even more evident in it because it is wondrous and occurs only at rare intervals.
