(Note 2) Within toch kedei dibbur, for example if he did not touch etc. And it is written in Sefer Abudraham that even if he desires to relieve himself he may restrain himself and bless, for it is similar to what is said in the name of Rashba above siman 92 seif 12: if in the middle of his prayer he was awakened to desire he should restrain himself until he finishes etc.; and Rashba wrote the reason above — what should he do since he did not need his bowels at all when he began to pray etc., and in the middle he was awakened etc. — see there in Magen Avraham; and the same applies here — what should he do; however see in Magen Avraham there note 2 that it implies he did not rely on Rashba but rather because it is forbidden to interrupt his prayer — see there.

And Taz wrote that the essence of the obligation is within toch kedei dibbur, and therefore one need not leave except if he can bless within toch kedei dibbur — for then is his primary obligation; but even after toch kedei dibbur he can bless — see there. But it is not explicit until when he can bless, and it is possible until the clouds disperse. And in Sefer Abudraham it is written and these are his words: and Maran end of first chapter of Pesachim and Birchos Avraham explain that one does not bless at all; and so is the implication of the poskim; nevertheless the words of Taz appear somewhat [correct] — until here; and these are the words of Ran in Pesachim 74: Yerushalmi brought also in Sefer Chafetz Chaim — it appears from this that blessing of praise is only within toch kedei dibbur of seeing and hearing — until here; in any case from a doubt in blessing one does not extract benefit, therefore after toch kedei dibbur one does not bless.

(In Shulchan Aruch seif 2) If they dispersed between lightning etc. And it is written in Sefer Abudraham and these are his words: Terumat HaDeshen explained the heavens cleared before him, and Lechem Chamudos (I did not find in Lechem Chamudos, perhaps thus in Sefer Me'irat Einayim); and it appears to me to exclude if they dispersed through wind — one here and one there — but still the entire sky is overcast — until here.
