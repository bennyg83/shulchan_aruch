In wrath. And if they blew calmly one says Blessed are You Who makes the work of Creation (Tosafos in the name of Yerushalmi); and it appears to me that nevertheless it is a great wind, only what does not blow in wrath is not heard throughout the world; and it requires further study why Beit Yosef omitted this. It appears to me that if one heard thunder and saw lightning simultaneously he blesses on both one blessing Blessed are You Who makes the work of Creation. We say in the Gemara that wrath-wind does not begin at night and does not blow two hours continuously unless it stopped in between.
