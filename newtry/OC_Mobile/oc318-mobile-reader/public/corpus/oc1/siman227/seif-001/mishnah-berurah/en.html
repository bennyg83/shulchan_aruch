(1) That shoots like an arrow — and some say it is a star that has a tail and staff of light, and both were brought by later authorities for practical halacha; and if that same star itself that has a staff he sees again on another night he need not return and bless as long as he is still within 30 days from the first sighting.
(2) From place to place — and one does not bless on one night except once, even if he saw another star running that night.
(3) Lightning — on lightning. And those that come without any thunder at all, only from heat on the side of the south wind — this is not the lightning mentioned in the Gemara and one does not bless on them.
(4) That blew in wrath — and if not in wrath, if it is a great wind one blesses Who makes the work of Creation and cannot bless Whose strength and power etc. after it is not great wrath; and it is good always to bless on a storm wind that is not common only the blessing Who makes the work of Creation, for with this one certainly fulfills his obligation either way, for we are not so expert in what is 'in wrath'.
(5) On each one etc. — and the world is accustomed to bless on lightning Who makes the work of Creation and on thunder Whose strength and power etc.; and so it is reasonable, for through thunder the power of the Holy One blessed be He is seen more than through lightning; however in truth each of the blessings applies to both; and therefore if one heard lightning and thunder simultaneously he blesses one blessing — namely Who makes the work of Creation on both; and if he blessed Whose strength and power fill the universe he also fulfills his obligation. And if they were not frequent one after another he blesses first on the lightning [which appears first to the human eye] Who makes the work of Creation, and afterward on the thunder Whose strength and power fill the universe; and the same if he did not see the lightning and heard the sound of thunder and blessed on it Whose strength and power fill the universe, or he blessed Who makes the work of Creation and afterward saw lightning — he blesses on it also Who makes the work of Creation. And see in Shaarei Teshuvah who wrote in the name of Birkei Yosef that if he blessed on the lightning and intended to exempt the thunder that comes after it, he fulfills his obligation b'dieved; and the explanation is that after the nature of creation that after lightning comes thunder, therefore his blessing applies to the thunder that comes afterward.
(6) And if he wishes etc. — but he does not bless two blessings as one, rather either this or that.
(7) Blessed etc. Whose strength and power etc. — meaning Blessed is He Who gives power to nature to show the power of the Creator of the beginning so that they fear before Him [Abudraham in the name of Abudraham].
