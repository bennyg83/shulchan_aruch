Lightning: on lightning one says Who makes the work of Creation, for it is not appropriate to say Whose strength and power fill the universe since they are not seen throughout the world. And on thunder at night — if he wishes he says Who makes the work of Creation for they are acts of Creation; and if he wishes he says Whose strength and power fill the universe since the sound is heard throughout the world. Therefore the Mechaber wrote 'in wrath' — meaning with great force — then one says Who makes the work of Creation or Whose strength etc.; but if they blew calmly one says specifically Who makes the work of Creation. Magen Avraham wrote: if one heard thunder and saw lightning simultaneously he blesses one blessing on both — Blessed [are You Heaven...] Who makes the work of Creation — for one does not multiply blessings; but if they come one after another the world is accustomed to bless on thunder Whose strength and power fill the universe and on lightning Who makes the work of Creation. What the Mechaber wrote 'and if he wishes he says Whose strength' — this applies specifically to thunder; but on lightning one blesses only Who makes the work of Creation.
