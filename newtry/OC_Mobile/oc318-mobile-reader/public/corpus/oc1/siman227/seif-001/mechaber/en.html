Blessing on shooting stars. It contains 3 seifim: On shooting stars,
which is like a type of star that shoots like an arrow across the length of the sky from place to place and its light extends like a staff; and on earthquake; and on lightning; and on thunder; and on winds that blow in wrath — on each of these one says Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who makes the work of Creation. And if he wishes he may say Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Whose strength and power fill the universe.
