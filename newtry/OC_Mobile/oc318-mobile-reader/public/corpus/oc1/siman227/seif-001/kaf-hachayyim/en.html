1) [Seif 1] Over meteors, which are like a star, etc. We have not seen people recite a blessing over a star that races from place to place. Since this is explained by the halachic authorities, however, one must recite the blessing, and absence of observation is not proof. R' Moshe ibn Chaviv, author of Get Pashut, handwritten responsum, siman 3; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1. Nevertheless, the universal practice is to recite the blessings over everything listed in this siman and the following siman without the Name and kingship. It is possible that this custom spread based upon what Rema, of blessed memory, writes in the gloss above to siman 223, seif 1; see there.
