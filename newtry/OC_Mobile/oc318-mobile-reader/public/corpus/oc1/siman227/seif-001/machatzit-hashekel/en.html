(Note 1) In wrath etc. It appears to me that if he heard etc. — behold from the law he can bless either on thunder or on lightning Who makes the work of Creation or Whose strength and power fill the universe; except that the world is accustomed to bless on thunder Whose strength and power fill the universe and on lightning Who makes the work of Creation. And the reason Taz wrote is that they want to bless both blessings; therefore it is more reasonable to bless Whose strength and power fill the universe on thunder than on lightning, for through thunder the power of the Holy One blessed be He is seen more than through lightning; but Who makes the work of Creation applies also to thunder as to lightning and they are equal to each other. If so, when one hears thunder and sees lightning simultaneously he blesses one blessing for both; and if so it is better to bless Who makes the work of Creation on both than to bless on both Whose strength and power fill the universe, for this blessing is not so applicable to lightning per the custom of the world.
