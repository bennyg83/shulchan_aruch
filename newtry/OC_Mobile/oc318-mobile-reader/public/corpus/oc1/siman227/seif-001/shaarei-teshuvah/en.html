Lightning — see Ba'er Heitev; and see in Rabbeinu Yonah that if he blessed on the lightning and intended to exempt the thunder that comes after it he fulfills his obligation; and see stated in the name of responsa manuscript to Maharam ibn Chaviv in Beit HaMikdash Get Pashut who wrote: we have not seen that one blesses on a shooting star from place to place; but since it is explained in poskim, 'we have not seen' is not proof and one must bless; and one does not bless on one night except once, and even if he saw another star running that night he need not bless; and also on a star that has a tail and staff of light — which is the second explanation of Rambam and Rabbeinu Hai and the Aruch — one must bless, and more recognizable in it is the power of the Creator may He be blessed, it being a wondrous thing and not found except at distant times; and if that same star itself he sees every night — if he sees it within 30 days he need not return and bless. And regarding earthquakes — if some time passed between one tremor and another such that he diverted his attention from it, he must return and bless; and if not he need not return and bless; and similarly regarding winds — thus ruled Maharam ibn Chaviv; and see in Halachot Ketanot siman 38.
