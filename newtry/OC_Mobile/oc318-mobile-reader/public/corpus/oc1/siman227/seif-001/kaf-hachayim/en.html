(1) [Seif 1] On shooting stars which is like a type of star etc. — we have not seen that one blesses on a shooting star running from place to place; but since it is explained in poskim one must bless and 'we have not seen' is not proof. Maharam ibn Chaviv author of Get Pashut in responsa manuscript siman 3, Birkei Yosef note 1; however the custom of the world is to bless on all mentioned in this siman and the following siman without investigation, and it is possible this custom spread according to what Moreinu the Rama wrote in his gloss above siman 213 seif 1 — see there.
