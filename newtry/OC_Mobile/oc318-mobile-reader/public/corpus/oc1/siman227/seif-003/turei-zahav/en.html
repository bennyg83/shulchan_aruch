Within toch kedei dibbur he should leave. With tzerei — for it is not appropriate to say here yetzei with kamatz for he is exempt; rather certainly he says what should he do; and he explained that he must leave from there and bless since it is possible for him to act properly and bless immediately he should do so; and if he cannot leave and bless so immediately he need not leave for the sake of the blessing; and it appears to me that the measure of the blessing is not within toch kedei dibbur, and after this measure he does not bless — for if so they should have said this measure on the essence of the law that the measure of blessing here is toch kedei dibbur; rather certainly after the measure of toch kedei dibbur also he must bless as long as he can, as is said regarding other blessings such as in siman 226 regarding one who sees trees; except that here he spoke regarding the obligation upon him to leave from there and hurry to bless — therefore they divided whether it is toch kedei dibbur or not, in my humble opinion. It is found in Yerushalmi chapter 10 Yuchasin: 'Let the humble hear and rejoice' — R' Bun said: in the future a person will give judgment and accounting for that he saw varieties of fruits and did not eat; R' Eliezer would restrict himself to eat from them from every new thing — until here. And Teshuvos Beit HaLevi wrote the reason is in order to increase blessings.
