(9) In the restroom — and the same if he was in a house where they slaughter meat, for there is a very foul smell, or he was walking in filthy alleyways and can hurry himself to leave from there immediately.
(10) If he can etc. — some say that even if he was awakened to relieve himself, nevertheless as long as he does not desire so much that there is in it [violation of] 'you shall not make yourselves detestable' he can leave from there before he relieves himself and bless; and some are stringent in this. And in particular where he estimates that he will hear again the sound of thunder and can bless afterward — certainly it is correct to be stringent.
(11) To leave and bless etc. — and this is where he did not relieve himself and did not touch his hands in the filthy place [and even though above in siman 4 it is explained that one leaving the restroom must wash his hands even if he did not relieve himself, nevertheless here so that he not lose the blessing he need not wash his hands beforehand unless his hands were filthy], or he can wash his hands in water or in something else that cleans near his hearing — for otherwise certainly the time will extend more than toch kedei dibbur.
(12) He should leave — meaning he should hurry to leave from there so that he not lose the blessing; but if he tarries more than toch kedei dibbur — for otherwise the time has already passed — he need not hurry again; and from all this it is heard that the measure of the blessing on thunder and lightning is specifically within toch kedei dibbur, and if more than toch kedei dibbur passed from the time he saw the lightning or heard the thunder he does not bless again on this thunder and lightning. And for this reason, where he relieved himself and washed his hands and heard the sound of thunder or saw lightning — he blesses first on the thunder and lightning and afterward says Asher Yatzar. And know further that hands in general — meaning that one does not know if they are dirty — are valid for blessing [siman 4 seif 23].
