3) Regarding meteors: over a racing star, one recites the blessing only once during the entire night. Even if he sees a different star race, he does not recite another blessing that night. Regarding a star with a tail, if he subsequently sees no different star but sees that same one each night, he need not recite the blessing again if he sees it within thirty days. Regarding earthquakes, if enough time passed between one tremor and another for him to have diverted his attention, he must recite the blessing again even within thirty days. Otherwise, he need not recite it again. The same applies to winds. R' Moshe ibn Chaviv there; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 3; Shaarei Teshuvah there; Chesed LaAlafim there; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch there.
