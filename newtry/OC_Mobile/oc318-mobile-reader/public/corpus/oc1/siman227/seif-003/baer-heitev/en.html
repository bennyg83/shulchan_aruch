He should leave (yetzei with tzerei) — for it is not appropriate to say here yetzei with kamatz for he is exempt; rather certainly he says what should he do, and he explained that he must leave from there and bless since it is possible for him to act properly and bless immediately he should do so — for example if he has not yet touched the filthy place and has not yet relieved himself, or he can wash his hands. Taz and Magen Avraham.
