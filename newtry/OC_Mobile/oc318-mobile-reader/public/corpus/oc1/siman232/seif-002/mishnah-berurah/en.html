(5) A person should not sit etc. — because this seif has many details I must extend somewhat. This entire seif regarding eating, haircutting, entering bathhouse and other matters mentioned here is mishnah first chapter of Shabbat forbidding close to Mincha; and the mishnah did not clarify which Mincha — Mincha Gedolah [from six and a half hours and up, and close to it is half an hour before, meaning right after chatzot] or Mincha Ketanah [from nine and a half hours and up, and close to this is from beginning of tenth hour]. Also the mishnah did not clarify what it forbade regarding eating — only a large meal such as wedding feast, then forbidden lest Mincha time pass; or even a small ordinary meal forbidden lest they prolong [and similarly one must be uncertain regarding other matters in the mishnah — entering bathhouse: only the whole bathhouse matter of washing head, bathing hot and cold, sweating, or even casual sweating forbidden lest he faint from bath heat; and regarding tannery: only large tanning or even casual inspection; and similarly other matters as in this seif]. And there in gemara are two answers — according to both the mishnah deals with Mincha Gedolah. First answer: mishnah's intent forbidding close to Mincha Gedolah deals only with large meal [but small meal permitted until close to Mincha Ketanah; and some further lenient in this answer that even close to Mincha Ketanah small meal may begin]. Second answer: mishnah's intent to forbid even small meal close to Mincha Gedolah. Poskim disagreed — Rif and Rambam ruled like second answer, and Mechaber ruled like them; and Rama at beginning of his words ruled like first answer — this is the essence of this seif; and now we begin explaining the seif's words.
(6) To get a haircut close to Mincha — this 'close to Mincha' means close to Mincha Gedolah as Mechaber wrote at the end of his words, and all these apply; and even though until end of Mincha time there is much time, nevertheless we are concerned the razor may break and he return for another and Mincha pass; and it is possible if he has two or three razors ready before him it is permitted. When Mincha time arrives it is proper not to take a child in his lap lest he soil clothes, and while returning for water Mincha may pass or he be late to synagogue (Chayei Adam siman 18).
(7) Close to Mincha — meaning half an hour before, which is beginning of seventh hour from half the day and up; and there is no difference between long or short day — always we calculate according to this view close to Mincha after half the day.
(8) To bathhouse — even to sweat casually, and all the more the whole bathhouse matter.
(9) To tannery — even casual inspection lest he see hides ruined and be distressed and be troubled and not pray (Rashi); and it is implied the same for any work or accounting that may prolong or cause distraction (see Biur Halacha); therefore those going to fairs busy buying and selling merchandise should not begin close to Mincha Gedolah until praying first (Chayei Adam).
(10) And not for judgment — even if they already heard claims and now sit to conclude judgment, forbidden lest they see merit and overturn judgment and the matter prolong.
(11) Small meal — meaning an ordinary person's meal (and large meal is wedding and circumcision); reason: we are concerned lest it prolong. Law of prohibition of eating or work before Maariv will be explained siman 235 seif 2. Regarding beginning study before Mincha — see above siman 89 seif 6 regarding study before Shacharit; and the same here when Mincha Ketanah time arrives.
(12) Close to Mincha Gedolah — see above note 7 in Mishnah Berurah.
(13) And if he began etc. — even if he began after Mincha Gedolah already arrived, and even afterward, as long as the half hour before Mincha Ketanah has not yet arrived [for if he began during this half hour he stops when Mincha Ketanah arrives, even if much time remains until evening, for primary Mincha time is from Mincha Ketanah and up — see below in gloss what is now practiced to be lenient].
(14) In any of these — even if he began a large meal or entered large tanning and similarly other matters as we mentioned in the introduction — certainly there is concern lest it prolong much; nevertheless he does not stop since there is time to pray after finishing meal or work.
(15) If there is no time — even if he began when permitted he stops.
(16) To stop immediately — even while day is still long, whenever he knows meal or work will continue until dark he must stop immediately lest he forget and time pass entirely.
(17) And from when is beginning etc. — refers to what is written above 'and if he began' etc.
(18) If they were engaged — meaning they already sat in other cases.
(19) From when they wrap — for so they practiced formerly when sitting in judgment because the Divine Presence rests with them (Beit Yosef).
(20) From washing hands — and one accustomed to loosen belt when eating, beginning is from loosening belt even before netilah.
(21) That a small meal — and according to them our haircut is also permitted close to Mincha Gedolah (and forbidden is only Ben El'asah haircut which is great labor as explained in gemara); bathhouse specifically whole bathhouse matter is forbidden then, but casual sweating permitted; tannery specifically large tanning; judgment specifically beginning judgment which requires much time and we are concerned time may pass — but concluding judgment permitted.
(22) Permitted — meaning close to Mincha Gedolah and then until close to Mincha Ketanah; but close to Mincha Ketanah from beginning of tenth hour and up — even according to them all these are forbidden, and even b'dieved if they began then they stop when Mincha Ketanah time arrives.
(23) And it is not forbidden etc. — meaning according to them it is not forbidden to begin close to Mincha Gedolah except large meal, for specifically there needs be many people therefore we are concerned lest it prolong much until time pass.
(24) Wedding or circumcision — and same pidyon haben where many people sit together and thereby it usually prolongs; and Shabbat and Yom Tov meal is not called large meal (Beit Yosef).
(25) And some say etc. and some say etc. — these two views are more lenient than previous views, for according to them only in one manner forbidden, and they differ: first view — only close to Mincha Ketanah forbidden even small meal, but close to Mincha Gedolah all permitted; second view holds opposite — large meal forbidden even close to Mincha Gedolah, and small meal permitted even close to Mincha Ketanah.
(26) Even close to Mincha Ketanah etc. — implies when Mincha Ketanah time arrives he admits forbidden to begin; and in Hagahot Mordechai he is lenient also in this, and his view is prohibition is only large meal close to Mincha Ketanah; and so implied in Mahariu that the world was accustomed to be lenient even after Mincha Ketanah arrived.
(27) And they were accustomed to be lenient according to both etc. — and so the Acharonim copied.
(28) Because nowadays etc. — and it deals with one whose way is also to go pray with congregation in synagogue; but if his way is to pray alone at home it does not help (Derech Chaim).
(29) We are not concerned etc. — nevertheless immediately when they call to synagogue one must stop and pray, for otherwise what is its remedy (there). And know specifically for small meal one relies on the sun's call to synagogue; but large meal one does not rely on calling to synagogue to permit beginning close to Mincha Ketanah [from nine hours and up] because drunkenness is common. And it is implied from Acharonim regarding going to bathhouse — even for whole bathhouse matter, and similarly all matters in this seif one relies on sun's call like small meal. And therefore it is permitted on erev Shabbat to go to bathhouse even when Mincha Ketanah arrived where the sun calls to synagogue. Nevertheless men of action pray before going to bathhouse; and some are careful to pray before even when going close to Mincha Gedolah after chatzot as mentioned in Maaseh Rav; nevertheless those lenient should not be protested since they have basis to rely as explained — but all this specifically if the sun also announces to bathhouse people to leave erev Shabbat some time before sunset so they have time after leaving to pray in time — otherwise we cannot rely on this at all.
(30) One should be stringent — meaning not rely on sun's call to begin then the meal, for drunkenness is common and when drunk calling does not help; and therefore when there is wedding on Shabbat and they did not leave synagogue until after chatzot they pray Mincha first and afterward eat, for wedding feast is large meal.
(31) Before then — meaning he began large meal before chatzot when still permitted time for all.
(32) When arrives etc. and hour passes — meaning since he estimates his meal will continue until night he must rise and pray immediately when Mincha Ketanah time arrives.
(33) Must rise and pray — it appears specifically for large meal because drunkenness is common and we do not rely on sun's call to synagogue as explained, therefore he stops immediately; but for small meal need not stop immediately since he can rely even if he began meal after Mincha Ketanah arrived — he stops from meal when sun calls to synagogue.
