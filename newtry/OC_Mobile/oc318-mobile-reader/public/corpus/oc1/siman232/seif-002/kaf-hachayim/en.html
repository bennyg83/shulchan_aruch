(2) And the essence of prayer is kavanah, for through this abundance and blessing flow to him, as written in Zohar parashat Pekudei 248, and these are its words: whoever prays prayer and binds unification — they look at him whether his prayer and binding are as fitting; and if that prayer and binding are as fitting, then he is blessed first from the place all blessings emerge — this is 'And behold he made it' etc., immediately 'And Moshe blessed them,' until here. And further see Zohar parashat Vayechi folio 231a who wrote: a person must intend his mind in Mincha prayer; in all prayers a person must intend his mind, but in this prayer more than all because judgment is revealed in the world; therefore Yitzchak established Mincha prayer, until here.
