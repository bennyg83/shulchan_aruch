(Note 2) The shaliach tzibur etc. there are not ten listening — meaning the shaliach tzibur if he prayed alone would say in a whisper and not a separate Modim blessing, except because it is communal prayer he says Modim in a separate blessing; and since there are not ten listening it is called individual prayer.

And Rama's and Levush's view above: if possible the congregation should wait and not begin praying until the shaliach tzibur finishes HaEl HaKadosh; and if impossible because the hour is very pressed, do as written here — they begin immediately with the shaliach tzibur and pray with him word for word until after HaEl HaKadosh; and it is good that at least one answer amen on the shaliach tzibur's blessings — see end.
