Seif 2, in the gloss. “It is possible that the reason is because nowadays.” See Be'er Sheva, at the end of the work, in Kuntres Be'er Mayim Chaim, letter 12.
Magen Avraham, seif katan 9. “They must interrupt even if sufficient time remains.” Although below, siman 652, we rule that regarding a D'Rabbanan obligation, even one who began in violation need not interrupt when sufficient time remains, one must say that this case is more stringent. The primary time is at the beginning of Mincha Ketanah, for at that hour they would usually offer the Tamid and were not accustomed to delay it. See Pnei Yehoshua.
