(3) [Levush] Before the barber etc. — even in our haircuts; and according to Rif and Rambam all this is forbidden even close to Mincha Gedolah, for we are concerned the razor may break and he return for another; Magen Avraham wrote, and it is possible if he has two or three razors it is permitted — until here; and it appears the Sages did not disagree. Rambam in his Mishnah commentary wrote: or a decree lest something happen with one of the haircutting vessels — see there; and according to them if they began they do not stop even in Ben El'asah haircut and beginning of judgment; but regarding a large meal Taz extended and wrote they stop — and this is not implied in Tosafos there; and so wrote in responsa Ra'avad that even in a large meal they do not stop.
