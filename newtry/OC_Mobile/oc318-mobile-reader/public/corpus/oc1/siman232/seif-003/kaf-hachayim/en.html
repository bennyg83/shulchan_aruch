(3) And the Arizal's custom was to give charity also at Mincha before beginning to pray; and he would give three perutot as written in Sha'ar HaKavanot folio 52a — see there. And one should give before entering synagogue so it ascend for him doubly — before entering synagogue and before prayer, as we wrote above siman 91 note 44 — see there. And if he does not find poor people or collectors at that hour he separates it and keeps it with him as written in the noted siman note 45 — and see there further what to intend when giving charity; and siman 132 end of note 6 — see there.
