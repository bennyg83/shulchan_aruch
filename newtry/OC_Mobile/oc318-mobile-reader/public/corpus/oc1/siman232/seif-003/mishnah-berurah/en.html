(34) Permitted — even Mincha Ketanah already arrived, even if eating much fruit — for this is not called a meal; and a dish of the five species is also permitted if he does not establish it as a meal (Derech Chaim).
(35) As an egg — and not more; and Magen Avraham wrote for drinking more than egg-volume also forbidden, even though for eating outside sukkah more than egg is considered casual — here the concern is lest he prolong, and this applies more to drinking if it is intoxicating beverage; but ordinary drink may drink even much.
