3) The Arizal's practice was to give tzedakah before beginning Minchah as well. He would give three small coins, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 52a; see there. One should give it before entering the synagogue so that it counts as two acts, one before entering the synagogue and another before prayer, along the lines of what we wrote above in siman 51, paragraph 44; see there. If he finds neither poor people nor a collector at that time, he should set the money aside and retain it, in accordance with what is stated in that siman, paragraph 45. See there further regarding the intention to direct while giving tzedakah, and siman 132, end of paragraph 6; see there.
