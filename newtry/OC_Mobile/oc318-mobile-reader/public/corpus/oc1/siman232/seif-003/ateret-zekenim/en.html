And some say a small meal etc. — and therefore they were accustomed to permit eating after Mincha Ketanah time arrived (Mahariu); and specifically when an individual eats — since there is a fixed hour for the attendant who calls to come to the synagogue we are not concerned he will be negligent; but many who entered a meal even close to Mincha Ketanah are forbidden — all the more so when Mincha Ketanah time arrived (Bach).
