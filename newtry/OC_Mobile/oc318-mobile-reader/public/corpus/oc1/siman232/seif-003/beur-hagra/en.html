Siman 63 — and this etc. As above according to Tosafos and Rosh.

And the same etc. For this too is called tasting, as written in Sukkah 26a 'when one tastes' etc.; and Rif and Tosafos there s.v. tartei that it means an egg-volume; and in this we do not rule like R' Yehoshua ben Levi who forbids tasting — he forbade only a fixed meal, unlike such eating which is called casual as explained above; and Tosafos wrote fruits are permitted as above, for fruits are always casual as written in Yoma 79b; and this is the meaning of 'meaning' etc.; and Tosafos in Sukkah there s.v. tartei etc. and this measure etc.
