(1) Pressed — meaning there is not enough time in the day that they pray first the congregation in a whisper and afterward also the shaliach tzibur's repetition properly.
(2) They should pray in a whisper — meaning the congregation should not rely due to pressing circumstances to fulfill only through the shaliach tzibur praying aloud; for prayer's essence is in a whisper, and only the shaliach tzibur shortens his audible prayer and says only the first three blessings to fulfill them with Kedushah.
(3) In the day — see below siman 235 seif 1 in the gloss when the day ends regarding Mincha prayer, and see Mishnah Berurah there.
(4) The shaliach tzibur with the congregation — meaning the shaliach tzibur says immediately aloud and the congregation say with him in a whisper word for word until after HaEl HaKadosh; and it is good that at least one answer amen on the shaliach tzibur's blessing (Rama above siman 124 seif 2 — see Mishnah Berurah what is written there).
