(1) [Seif 1] If the hour is pressed etc. — one must be very careful, for R' Chelbo said in the name of R' Huna: a person should always be careful with Mincha prayer, for Eliyahu was answered only in Mincha prayer, as said 'And it was at the ascent of the minchah Eliyahu approached' etc. Berachos 6b. And the reason Tur wrote: because Shacharit its time is known in the morning when rising from bed he prays immediately before being troubled in his affairs; and likewise Maariv at night its time is known when coming home free from affairs; but Mincha is in the middle of the day while still troubled in affairs — one must set it upon his heart and turn from all affairs and pray it; and if he did so his reward is very great, until here.
