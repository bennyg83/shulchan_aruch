Magen Avraham wrote: one should wonder etc. that they offered minchah after the tamid of bein ha'arbayim — until here is Tosafos' language chapter Eizehu Mekoman daf 107a; and this is his language: one can say in the first chapter of Berachos one should be careful with Mincha prayer, for even Eliyahu was answered only in Mincha prayer — perhaps at the time of offering the minchah he was answered, for then was an hour of favor, until here. And the meaning is the gemara brings proof to be careful with Mincha prayer, for so we find with Eliyahu, and it was accepted in the hands of the Sages of the gemara that Eliyahu was answered at the time of offering the minchah — therefore then is an hour of favor; if so it is fitting to pray Mincha also at that hour, therefore it is called Mincha. And in Yerushalmi etc. at the time of offering the ketores — and ketores was made between tamid and minchah — in any case it is proven l'chatchila one should not pray Mincha from six and a half, for then is time of slaughtering the tamid. And this supports Rambam's view; and so Rabbeinu Yonah ruled in siman 235 unlike Rosh and Tur there who hold the opposite — l'chatchila the mitzvah is at Mincha Gedolah time — see there. And know in our gemara they disagree with Yerushalmi and establish the verse 'Let my prayer be set as incense before You' 'as the lifting of my hands as the evening minchah' refers to Maariv — for so R' Yehoshua said Berachos 6b that also in Maariv prayer one should be careful, and he brings proof from the above verse 'Let my prayer be set as incense' etc.

(Note 1) They should pray etc. — requires study, for in siman 124 etc. he wrote that one enters synagogue and finds the congregation already prayed in a whisper — he must immediately descend before the ark, descend before the ark and pray in a loud voice, and need not return and pray.

For there it is a different matter, for he must descend immediately before the ark since he already prayed aloud due to pressing circumstances — they did not require him to return and pray in a whisper.
