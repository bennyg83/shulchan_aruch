1) [Seif 1] If time is pressing, etc. One must be extremely careful regarding Minchah, for R' Chelbo said in the name of R' Huna: A person should always be careful with the Minchah prayer, because Eliyahu was answered only during Minchah, as it is stated, 'At the time of the afternoon offering, Eliyahu approached,' etc. Berachos 6b. The Tur explains that the time of the morning prayer is known: upon rising from bed in the morning, one prays immediately before becoming occupied with his affairs. Likewise, the time of the evening prayer is known: at night, when he returns home and is free of his affairs. Minchah, however, occurs in the middle of the day while he is occupied with his affairs. He must therefore take it to heart, withdraw from all his affairs, and pray. If he does so, his reward is exceedingly great. These are the Tur's words.
