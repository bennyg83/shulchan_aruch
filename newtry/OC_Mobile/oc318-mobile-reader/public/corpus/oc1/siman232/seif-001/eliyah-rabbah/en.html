(1) He should pray etc. In responsa Menachem Azariah siman 39 he warned greatly to lay tefillin during Mincha prayer; and he wrote in Kenesset HaGedolah one should lay Rashi tefillin. And he wrote in siman 22: Mincha is a word derived from the verse 'to the spirit of the day' which we translate 'to rest the day' — the sun inclines at that hour to go to its rest to prostrate to its Creator in the west; and the body of the sun fixed in the sphere, in striking the western points, is itself a minchah offering to its Creator; and from this is the example for us to offer ourselves and our souls to heaven, until here. And in responsa Emunat Shmuel siman 20 he wrote: the morning tamid atones for night's sins, and the limbs and fats of the evening offering for the day's sins; but the slaughter and offering of bein ha'arbayim and the prayer given for both — not for a specific need since it is midday — therefore they truly said it is a gift and minchah; and further in Shelah folio 281a another reason. R' David Abudraham wrote: because at the tenth hour of the day Adam the first man sinned — and this is 'to the spirit of the day' translated 'to rest the day.' He wrote in Sefer Chasidim siman 18: in the evening close to prayer time a man should not take a child in his lap lest he soil his clothes; and if you say he will wash them in water — even so they will not be clean as at first; and lest while searching for water prayer time pass; or in any case they will say Kaddish and he cannot answer; or lest the child cry when he wishes to put him down and he have pity on the child and not on his Creator's honor and not go at all to synagogue, until here. And 'close' is like half an hour before Mincha time as in seif 2 and siman 235 seif 3. He also wrote: if it seems wondrous to you regarding those who stammer in speech and read chet as heh, shin as samech, kuf as tet, reish as dalet — how do they pray and read, and when reaching 'Our soul hopes to Heaven' they are found blaspheming and cursing — do not wonder at the Creator's desire Who examines hearts; He asks only for the heart of man to be wholehearted with Him; and after one does not know to speak well, He credits him as if he spoke well. And likewise those who read verses of song in pleasant loud voice not knowing the verses — saying by mistake is accepted as fragrant incense; and Heaven rejoices greatly and says how much he sings before Me according to his understanding — on this it is said 'And His banner over me was love.' An incident with a kohen who spread his hands and said yismecha — the sage removed him and showed him from heaven to the sage that if he does not restore him he will be punished harshly.
