The shaliach tzibur with the congregation — see Magen Avraham what he brought in the name of Maharalbach that in pressing circumstances one should not do so, since the shaliach tzibur says Modim in a separate blessing and the congregation says in their usual whisper — are there not ten listening to the shaliach tzibur? I did not copy it in Mishnah Berurah because it appears simple according to our practice today that the shaliach tzibur does not say aloud except after the third blessing in order to fulfill the eighteen with Kedushah — certainly the shaliach tzibur need not say the Modim blessing only in a whisper like other individuals, for this is not called repetition of the shaliach tzibur; and Maharalbach deals with what they practiced in his time when the shaliach tzibur said all eighteen aloud — see above siman 124 in Biur Halacha s.v. be'lachash.
