Nevertheless in pressing circumstances one should not do so l'chatchila, since the shaliach tzibur says Modim in a separate blessing and the congregation says in their usual manner — there are not ten listening to the shaliach tzibur; nevertheless if the hour is very pressed he should pray immediately in a loud voice and the shaliach tzibur says Modim and the congregation says in a whisper — R' Lanach, Magen Avraham, and see above siman 124 seif 2 and Shomer Shabbos. And the Arizal did not permit saying it in a loud voice except in a whisper and afterward in a loud voice, even after sunset, even on erev Shabbat.
