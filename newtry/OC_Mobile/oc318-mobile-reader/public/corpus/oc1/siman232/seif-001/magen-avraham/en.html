One might say: why is it called Mincha — if because they offered minchah, behold they also offered minchah in the morning? And one can answer: because the primary hour of favor is at the time of offering minchah — until here Tosafos; if so it is proven the primary Mincha is Mincha Ketanah as written siman 235, for then is time of offering the minchah after the tamid; but at six and a half was time of slaughtering the tamid — see there. And in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Berachos it states it was instituted at the time of offering ketores, as said 'Let my prayer be set as incense' etc. They should pray in a whisper — for the shaliach tzibur need not be concerned and fulfill aloud, for the mitzvah of prayer is in a whisper and one does not fulfill the eighteen blessings whether individual or congregation except in a whisper (Beit Yosef Shulchan Lechem); and requires study, for siman 124 seif 2 implies one fulfills his prayer aloud — and one must say there it is a different matter.

And the shaliach tzibur with the congregation etc. — see Perishah who wrote the shaliach tzibur is not called repetition of the shaliach tzibur except the three first blessings to fulfill them with Kedushah.
