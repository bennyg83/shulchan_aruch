And in Kol Bo chapter 6 of Laws of Prayer halachah 4 he ruled that fruits like an egg and bread less than k'zayit is called tasting; but more than an egg-volume of fruits and k'zayit of bread — even though not called a fixed meal, nevertheless also not called tasting, and casual eating is called eating and forbidden; and one must say that here Shulchan Aruch retracted and permitted casual eating whenever it is not called a fixed meal — and Rambam's language there indicates even casual eating is forbidden.
