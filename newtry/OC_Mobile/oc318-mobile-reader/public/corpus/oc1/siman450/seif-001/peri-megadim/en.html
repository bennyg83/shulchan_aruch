Taz brought what Beit Yosef wrote — one who bought priests' allotment appears no need to say at all, nevertheless Shulchan Aruch ruled stringent l'chatchila and b'dieved permitted without statement; Magen Avraham 2. But Taz holds for practical law: wherever he lent a sum to a non-Jew and stipulated for a term up to a year etc. that the non-Jew cannot remove principal during the term — then the Jew acquired at loan start all year's loaves, must say initially that for Pesach week he gives money not loaves — stipulation before Pesach does not help; like siman 245 seif 1 requires giving initially; priests' allotment likewise since cannot remove all year, must give initially. Not so if each week the non-Jew can remove principal to lender — each week's stipulation at week start is like initial stipulation, permitted; and so in Magen Avraham it will be explained there is division between that Shabbat case and here; also the Jew did not acquire all year's loaves at loan start — Jew from non-Jew requires pulling, and even Jew from Jew — since d'rabbanan requires pulling, one might say no bal yera'eh transgression; further it is ownerless. However where dina demalkhuta neither can withdraw l'chatchila, be stringent like Taz; b'dieved requires further study. What Taz wrote on partnership oven seif 3 permitted Jew stipulate before Pesach to give money first before Pesach week for Pesach week as Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 1 Choshen Mishpat sign 3 Beit Yosef brought — wording strained: since Jew can divide partnership anytime, it would be like priests' allotment and interest removable each week — obviously permitted; I again saw his words clear: he holds whoever stipulated at partnership start for a term neither can withdraw — in oven certainly even if says before Pesach he wants only money not loaves, does not help, already acquired Pesach week's bread by dina demalkhuta — condition does not help as siman 245, unless can divide partnership anytime — therefore condition before Pesach for Pesach week money after Pesach permitted, like stipulation from partnership start; but stipulating before Pesach "take yours for Pesach and I afterwards" is not a condition before Pesach — chametz substitutes, for he tells non-Jew take my share in Pesach week etc.; therefore wondered on Mechaber seif 3 permitting take your bread-share in Pesach week etc.; what is written in sign 2 — Jew already has share each week — do not say can divide each week; rather: even before Pesach no condition here, telling non-Jew take my share on Pesach etc. — they are partners on Pesach and Jew afterwards takes chametz substitutes he acquired — as said; and so sign 2 will explain further.
