On Mechaber seif 2 — A Jew who receives. Note: regarding estate-holders who give wage and deposit to innkeepers to sell — see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayim siman 118 [regarding an estate-holder Jew selling barrels of deposit to a non-Jew and Wirtshauser — their custom and liability etc., and he wrote in his view transgresses because chametz need not be literally yours — since available and he has a grasp it is called his, etc.; nullification does not help since not his to declare ownerless, only a mere grasp; but before Pesach must buy from Wirtshauser to cancel first acquisition and draw again as complete purchase].
