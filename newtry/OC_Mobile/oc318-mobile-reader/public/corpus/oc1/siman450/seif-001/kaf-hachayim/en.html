(1) [Seif 1] A Jew who borrowed etc. — there are places that practiced not to borrow a loaf from a friend before Pesach to repay after Pesach; I suspect they touched it because of interest concern — lending se'ah bese'ah or loaf for loaf with fixed time forbidden (Riaz, Bach in Sefer HaGedolah chapter Kol Sha'ah; Darkei Moshe Orach Chayim 1; Kenesset HaGedolah in gloss; Magen Avraham s.k. 1). According to what our rabbi the author wrote Yoreh De'ah siman 462 seif 1 in gloss permitting loaf for loaf etc. (see there) it would appear to be lenient; however since Riaz himself did not decide they practiced for this reason but wrote "it appears" — do not be lenient where they practiced, possibly they practiced to forbid for another reason. Also where they did not practice prohibition, appears one should be stringent l'chatchila to borrow before Pesach and repay after Pesach, since Shulchan Aruch wording "a Jew borrowed" implies specifically if he borrowed and did not repay he must repay after Pesach; but l'chatchila he should repay before Pesach if possible.
