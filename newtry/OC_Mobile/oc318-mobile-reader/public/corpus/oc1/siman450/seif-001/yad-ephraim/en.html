See in Magen Avraham — and even though if he hired him for other work and afterwards did yayin nesech with him his wage forbidden — there, if he did nothing he would not pay, and he takes yayin nesech wage; but here, since he rented oven for a week, even if non-Jew baked nothing he would pay — he does not take chametz wage, therefore permitted. What we learn permits when he does the other work and payment is not for those barrels — see Beit Yosef.
