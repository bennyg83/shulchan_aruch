He should say to him before Pesach. So wrote the Tur in the name of Avi HaEzri. The Beis Yosef wrote that in Hagahos Maimoniyos and Mordechai, regarding the law of a Jew who bought the priests' allotment, it says that after Pesach he may take what was missing on Pesach, implying that he need not stipulate this initially. The reason is that here there is no prohibition except a fine for having transgressed bal yera'eh, and here there is no place to fine him; nevertheless he ruled stringently. It seems to me that there is a difference between them in the time of the condition. In this law, where interest reaches him each week, each week is a separate debt, for if he wants he can remove him each week. It follows that when the Jew stipulates at the beginning of the week, it is as though he stipulated from the outset. But if he fixed the interest upon him at one time and cannot remove it from him until the time between them, the condition before Pesach does not help. This can be learned from what is written in siman 245 regarding partnership with a non-Jew for Shabbos, which requires that he stipulated. So too here: since the Jew already acquired the loaf of the week of Pesach, what does the condition help to remove himself from it? It is like selling before Pesach the chametz that he had on Pesach. This is unlike where he stipulated from the beginning, for then the Jew never acquired that loaf at all. Therefore, one who buys the priests' allotment at one time, what is due to him for a fixed period, must stipulate at the time of acquisition for the reason we mentioned, and not afterward before Pesach. If he did not stipulate at the beginning, his remedy is to nullify the transaction afterward and then stipulate, as written there regarding Shabbos. Regarding an oven in partnership with a non-Jew, which he writes shortly afterward, that a condition before Pesach helps, there too, since half the oven belongs to the Jew, he can say to the non-Jew whenever he wants: I do not want to be in partnership with you on Pesach except on condition that you give me money for my share. This works well, as it seems to me.
