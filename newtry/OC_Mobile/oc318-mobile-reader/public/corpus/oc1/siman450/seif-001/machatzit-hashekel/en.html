(א) (s.k. 1) He must [repay] etc. — and even though l'chatchila etc.; meaning: we hold chametz does not take its value and its substitutes are permitted, as written siman 443 — nevertheless l'chatchila it is forbidden etc.

(ב) The first is not in existence etc. — and therefore Kaf HaChayyim wrote that even if he pays him on Pesach something that is not chametz it is permitted; and so too from the words of Tosafot in Avodah Zarah, from which we learn the law here: they say in the beginning of the chapter of workers in Beit Rabbi Yannai — the poor borrow shevi'it produce in shevi'it and repay in the eighth year with eighth-year produce. Tosafot wrote there the reason: since at the time of repayment the shevi'it produce is not in existence they are not shevi'it substitutes; all the more so with chametz that does not take its value. Tosafot wrote there that likewise if they repaid them in shevi'it with other produce that is not shevi'it, if they already ate what they borrowed from the poor it is permitted; and so it appears from the words of the Rosh — all the more so with chametz. Kenesset HaGedolah wrote there are places that practiced etc., and Kaf HaChayyim wrote in his name because of interest prohibition they touched on it, like se'ah bese'ah which is forbidden — and so too Darkei Moshe; not because of chametz prohibition. Therefore according to what Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 462 that we practice leniency with loaves because people are not particular about such a small matter — the custom Kenesset HaGedolah wrote does not apply.
