(s.k. 5) One is not concerned etc. — as explained clearly above; and Beit Yosef strained on this (end of his words). Meaning: Tur brought this law in the name of Rashba, and Rashba brings proof from what we learn in Avodah Zarah at the beginning of the chapter of the hirer: he hired him to do another work with him, even though he told him pass me barrels of yayin nesech from place to place — permitted. The Magen Avraham challenged: there is no proof, for payment for passing those barrels is not on passing them but on the work he hired him to do. Therefore the Magen Avraham wrote Rashba explains that mishnah another way — the simple understood way; but Magen Avraham holds Rashba also explains the mishnah as above, and nevertheless brings proof properly: wherever even if the forbidden thing were not done he would have to give wages — even though that forbidden thing was done, the wage is permitted. If so, here too the wage is permitted: for even if the non-Jew did not bake at all he would have to give oven-rent — therefore even if he baked chametz in it the wage is permitted. Nevertheless l'chatchila he must say he bake matzah, for if not, since it is known his custom is to bake chametz in it, it is as if he rented it for baking chametz — and renting is forbidden.
