(א) One is not concerned about him. And even though if he hired him to do another work and afterwards did yayin nesech with him his wage is forbidden — there, if he did nothing with him he would not pay wages and he would take yayin nesech wage; but here, since he rented his oven for one week, even if the non-Jew did not bake at all he would have to pay — he does not take chametz wage, therefore permitted. Nevertheless l'chatchila he must say they bake matzah in it, as explained clearly; and Beit Yosef strained on this (end of his words).

(ב) To dwell in it. It implies it is forbidden to rent to him for chametz, for he profits from issurei hana'ah (and so Taz); and specifically when he rented during Pesach or erev Pesach (Agur).
