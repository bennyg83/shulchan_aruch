(5) [Seif 2] He should tell him before Pesach etc. Taz s.k. 1 divides: if he can remove each week, stipulation before Pesach helps; but if he fixed interest once and cannot remove until set time between them, stipulation before Pesach does not help, must stipulate at transaction start, like siman 245 partnership matter (see there). However Noda BiYehudah Orach Chayim 1 wrote on Taz's words it is not compelled — even if cannot remove until set time, can stipulate before Pesach, unlike siman 245 (see there). And so Chamed Moshe Orach Chayim 2 and Meshivat Nefesh Orach Chayim 1, except Meshivat Nefesh wrote where dina demalkhuta neither can withdraw l'chatchila, be stringent like Taz, b'dieved requires further study (end) — but in Be'er Heitev wrote even there no stringency b'dieved (see there). And so Kaf HaChayyim Orach Chayim 3 wrote do not be stringent in these laws since Jew does not acquire without pulling per most poskim (see there).
