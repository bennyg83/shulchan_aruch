(א) To rent his oven to a non-Jew on condition etc. — this is not profiting from issurei hana'ah forbidden l'chatchila, since even if the non-Jew baked nothing he would have to pay the Jew his wage, for he rented for a set time — the Jew does not profit from what the non-Jew bakes chametz. Nevertheless l'chatchila he must tell him to bake matzah, for otherwise since it is known his custom is to bake chametz it is as if he rented for baking chametz, which is forbidden.

(ב) A house to dwell in — meaning even though he rented unstipulated to dwell, it comes only to exclude renting explicitly for chametz, and because he profits from issurei hana'ah; and from Acharonim' words it seems we do not forbid profiting from issurei hana'ah except when its prohibition time arrived, such as on Pesach or erev Pesach; but before, not forbidden even l'chatchila. Some are stringent even within thirty days before Pesach.
