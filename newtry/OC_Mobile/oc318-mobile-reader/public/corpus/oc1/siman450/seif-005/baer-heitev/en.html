(א) Matzah. Taz challenged this law and left it as requiring further study; and Kaf HaChayyim reconciled and answered (end of his words) (for he concluded: nevertheless he should say that he bake matzah in it).

(ב) To dwell. The agreement of Rishonim and Acharonim: even though he rented it to him unstipulated to dwell in it, this comes only to exclude where he rented it explicitly for chametz — when he rented it during Pesach or on erev Pesach (from several hours and upward) (end of his words, Kaf HaChayyim).
