He tells him. Taz — and the content of his intent I explained in sign 1: even though Jew can withdraw from partnership each week, nevertheless not like initial stipulation (like interest if can remove principal each week — permitted), for no uprooting of partnership — only says for his Pesach share he will take afterwards — that is forbidden. What is written Tur in Rashi's name: what is written to take money means after Pesach; if he takes money, permitted since stipulated before Pesach he does not want partnership on Pesach unless he takes money — permitted after Pesach to take money; likewise chametz — money substitutes, as Avi HaEzri on interest likewise here since can remove partnership anytime, like initial condition; these show our master Taz had special intent in Tur (end of his words) responsum of Rashi: Jew should say before Pesach and take money from that week for future after Pesach — stipulate before Pesach to take afterwards money, then even chametz on erev Pesach in non-Jew's possession, money substitutes, permitted. Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 1 chametz sign 3 brought permitted to stipulate before Pesach and take money of that week; do not say Jew and non-Jew with field partnership etc.; but here since he takes money first, permitted, so Beit Yosef and Hagahot Maimoniyot in some versions I saw: but here when he takes money first, permitted — this is Taz's version: explanation "and takes money" after Pesach, from the start permitted to receive money — we do not forbid profiting from issurei hana'ah l'chatchila elsewhere except yayin nesech (there even b'dieved forbidden); need not take money before Pesach at all; likewise chametz can receive afterwards, money substitutes. Therefore no contradiction from Rashi's wording Tur brings implying no need to take money before Pesach, from Hagahot Maimoniyot and as said. In Magen Avraham sign 3 wrote Taz variant "takes" — lacking (typo in print there); intent Taz reads "and takes" with vav; but version "and took" without vav as Beit Yosef; in Hagahot Maimoniyot some prints I found with vav "and takes" as written. Further Taz wrote what Beit Yosef wrote Mordechai did not mention taking money before Pesach — on this Taz wrote Hagahot Maimoniyot also did not require taking money before Pesach, else why not write initially stipulate before Pesach and take money that week before Pesach; from not mentioning at start, implies no need before Pesach; and Tur in Rashi responsum wrote stipulate before Pesach and take money from that week, did not conclude take money before Pesach. (Tosafot typo should read "and further") further chametz permitted after Pesach, money substitutes — all who stipulate before Pesach to take afterwards money; and Taz wrote l'chatchila permitted afterwards to receive, we do not forbid profiting from issurei hana'ah except yayin nesech (there even b'dieved forbidden); and see sign 3 further; Magen Avraham sign 8 from this.
