A Jew who purchased a fixed clerical due under which bread is given to the clergy may receive after Pesach the amount that was missing during Pesach. Chametz that remained over Pesach is prohibited only as a penalty, and here there is no basis for penalizing him. Hagahos Maimoniyos and Mordechai.
