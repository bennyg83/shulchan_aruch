He should tell him etc. And b'dieved, even if he did not tell him and he brought him after Pesach it is permitted, for the Jew did not acquire it on Pesach (Bach). And a Jew who bought the priests' allotment who give them bread — permitted to take after Pesach what they withheld from him on Pesach, for chametz over which Passover passed is not forbidden except from a fine, and it appears we do not fine him (Hagahot Maimoniyot and Mordechai).
