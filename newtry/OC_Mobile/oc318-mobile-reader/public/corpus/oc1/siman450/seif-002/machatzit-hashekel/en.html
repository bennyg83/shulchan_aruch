(s.k. 2) He should say etc. — and a Jew who bought the priests' allotment etc. — permitted to take etc.; from this wording it implies that even l'chatchila he need not stipulate before Pesach; and so the Magen Avraham inferred from this wording. Therefore it must be said the priests' allotment is more lenient than one who receives loaves with interest — and that is not so; on the contrary the priests' allotment has reason to be more stringent, as Taz wrote s.k. 1. Rather, according to Mordechai, as the Magen Avraham understood (see chapter 8): he holds regarding the priests' allotment that one need not stipulate before Pesach — likewise regarding receiving loaves with interest he holds one need not stipulate, as the Magen Avraham wrote (end of his words); but we do not hold like him, and for us, since one must stipulate with interest-loaves, all the more so with the priests' allotment. Magen Avraham understood Mordechai's wording regarding the priests' allotment to teach that we are lenient there as with interest-loaves; and regarding a condition he relied on Magen Avraham himself, for we know it is no better than interest-loaves.
