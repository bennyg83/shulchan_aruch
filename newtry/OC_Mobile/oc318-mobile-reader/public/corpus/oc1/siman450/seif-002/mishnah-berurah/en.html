(א) He should tell him before — the reason: since he is obligated to give him a loaf every week, when the non-Jew designates the loaf on Pesach for the Jew it is as if he already acquired it and as if Passover passed on the Jew's chametz — therefore he must stipulate that for the week of Pesach he does not want loaves but their flour or equivalent money [or that he give loaves baked after Pesach]; if so, even if the non-Jew prepared loaves on Pesach we are not concerned, for they are not subjugated to the Jew at all and are entirely in the non-Jew's possession for now.

(ב) Chametz loaves — meaning even those prepared for him on Pesach; and Acharonim agreed all this is l'chatchila, but b'dieved even if he did not stipulate at all it is permitted to receive loaves after Pesach for the week of Pesach; and even though he designated them for the Jew on Pesach — all this did not come into the Jew's possession, he did not acquire them, and he has only a monetary debt of loaves, not those bodies themselves, and they still belong to the non-Jew all Pesach days.
