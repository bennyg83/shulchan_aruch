He told him before Pesach etc. The rabbi's understanding here was hidden from me — "take yours for Pesach" etc., siman 443 — he benefits from Pesach chametz in what he takes for the week after Pesach, all the wage even the non-Jew's share because the non-Jew takes his share in the week of Pesach. Granted for Shabbat in siman 245 stipulation helps initially, at partnership formation — then the Jew never had a share that Shabbat, as Rashi explained there brought in Beit Yosef; not so here where they stipulated before Pesach and the non-Jew already has a share every week and the Jew a share every week — there is value to the Jew's chametz on Pesach; and even though they wrote nearby stipulation helps even before Pesach, that is so he can tell the non-Jew he will not give his share for work on Pesach unless he receives money — not chametz; then even if chametz given afterwards it is money substitutes. But here inapplicable — he stipulates he will in any event take chametz after Pesach for his Pesach share in what he already acquired at partnership start — what does stipulation help. In Tur in the name of Rashi: question why tell the non-Jew "take yours for Pesach and I afterwards"; answer: stipulate before Pesach and take money from that week. It appears "and take money" does not mean forbidden to receive chametz afterwards only money, but: stipulate before Pesach to take money; and as written in the seif before this, afterwards he may take even chametz. In the case Shulchan Aruch wrote it is wondrous — he changed from Rashi and Hagahot Maimoniyot Beit Yosef brings in something not permitted apparently; in Hagahot Maimoniyot Beit Yosef brings: but for Pesach permitted to stipulate before Pesach and take money of that week; and do not say regarding Jew etc.; but here since he takes money first, permitted (end). Beit Yosef wrote Mordechai did not mention needing to take money that week before Pesach etc.; truly Hagahot Maimoniyot seems not to require taking money first specifically, for they did not initially mention taking money before Pesach; and so responsum of Rashi and "further" chametz may be taken after Pesach as in previous seif — Avi HaEzri's words in Tur. Rather "from the start" means l'chatchila, referring to what follows "permitted" — since he takes money, even l'chatchila permitted; not as next seif that only if already received (in my humble opinion).
