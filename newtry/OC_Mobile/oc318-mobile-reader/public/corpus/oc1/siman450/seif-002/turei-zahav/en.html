He said to him before Pesach, etc. I do not understand the Rav's words here, 'you take that of Pesach,' etc. In the end, he benefits from chametz of Pesach by taking, for the week after Pesach, all the wages, even the non-Jew's share, because the non-Jew took his share during the week of Pesach. Granted, regarding Shabbos in siman 245, the condition helps if made at the beginning, meaning at the time the partnership is formed, because then the Jew never had a share in that Shabbos, as Rashi explained there and the Beis Yosef brought. This is unlike here, where they stipulated before Pesach, and the non-Jew already has a share in every week and the Jew has a share in every week; it turns out that there is money for the Jew's chametz on Pesach. Although they wrote nearby that it helps even before Pesach, that is regarding where the non-Jew gives him money, so that the Jew can say, 'I will not give my share to do work with it on Pesach unless I receive money, not chametz'; then even if he later gives him chametz, it is an exchange for his money. But here this does not apply, for he stipulates that in any event he will take chametz after Pesach for his share on Pesach, which he already acquired at the beginning of the partnership. What does the condition help for this? In the Tur in the name of Rashi it is written as a question: what should he say to the non-Jew, 'you take that of Pesach and I will take afterward'? He answered that he should stipulate before Pesach and take money from that week. It seems that 'and take the money' does not mean that it is forbidden for him to receive chametz afterward and only money is allowed. Rather, it means that he should stipulate before Pesach that he will take money; and as I wrote in the preceding seif, afterward he may take even chametz. But in the case written by the Shulchan Aruch, it is astonishing to me that he changed from the words of Rashi and the words of Hagahos Maimoniyos brought by the Beis Yosef, in a matter that appears not to be permitted. In the Hagahos Maimoniyos brought by the Beis Yosef it is written: but regarding Pesach, it is permitted to stipulate before Pesach and take from him the money of that week; it is not comparable to a Jew, etc., but here, since he takes the money initially, it is permitted, end quote. The Beis Yosef wrote that the Mordechai did not mention that he must take the money of that week before Pesach, etc. In truth, it seems that Hagahos Maimoniyos also did not mean that he must specifically take the money beforehand, for they did not initially mention that taking the money must be before Pesach. So too it is written in the responsum of Rashi, and furthermore, that he can take even chametz after Pesach, as written in the preceding seif, and this is the words of Avi HaEzri in the Tur. Rather, it seems to me that what he wrote 'initially' means ab initio, and refers to what follows, where he said it is permitted; that is, since he takes money, even ab initio this is permitted, unlike what is written in the following seif, where it is permitted specifically only if he already received it. So it seems to me.
