(א) He tells the non-Jew before Pesach etc. — and if he did not say, forbidden to receive after Pesach for his share even money, as explained below.

(ב) And I will take afterwards — some Acharonim wrote: specifically when he says "and I will take afterwards money against the loaf you took" — that is considered saying he does not want to rent his oven for work on Pesach except for money; or it deals when he says "take for yourself this week of Pesach the oven whether there is [bread] in it or not and I will take the week before or after what happens to me" — that is permitted, for they divide time and have no claim on each other. But when he says "take the loaves that will be on Pesach and I will take this amount afterwards" — then he wants the loaves in the oven [for if nothing is baked on Pesach in his oven the non-Jew will not give him after Pesach] and he acquires the oven for the Jew, only exchanging with the non-Jew partner; if so he benefits from his chametz on Pesach under his share in those loaves — the non-Jew gives him his share of loaves in the week after Pesach and it is forbidden. Some forbid even when he says "and I will take afterwards money against the loaves you took" — nevertheless he benefits from the value of his chametz on Pesach. Acharonim agreed that in practice one should practice selling the oven to the non-Jew for the week of Pesach so it is entirely in his possession, set a price for this, and take money from him first before Pesach — with this there is no prohibition from what occurs that Shabbat and yom tov week while it is absorbed.
