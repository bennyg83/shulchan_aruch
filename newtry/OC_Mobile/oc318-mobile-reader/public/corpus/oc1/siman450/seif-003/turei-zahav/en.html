Even money forbidden etc. In Tur after responsum 200, i.e. seif 3: Rashba wrote: an incident came before me — a Jew had an oven and a non-Jew baked chametz on Pesach; the baker, the Jew's treasurer, brought loaves after Pesach from oven-rent and I forbade receiving them — not only chametz forbidden because the Jew acquired through oven praise and it is the Jew's chametz, but even money forbidden as wage, profiting from issurei hana'ah, since chametz on Pesach is forbidden in benefit, even a non-Jew's chametz forbidden to benefit on Pesach (end). Apparently Tur and Rashba imply dispute with Rashi — Rashba forbids money Rashi permitted; that is not so — Rashi permitted because of stipulation before Pesach; for Rashba stipulation does not apply, loaves baked on Pesach were actually the Jew's — forbidden l'chatchila even money for them. The main dispute is as Beit Yosef in Rivash: some say do not rent one's animal to a non-Jew to bring chametz, holding a non-Jew's chametz forbidden in benefit; some rent and a non-Jew's chametz permitted in benefit — therefore since Rashi permitted taking money though coming from non-Jew's chametz, he holds like those who permit a non-Jew's chametz in benefit; Rashba wrote explicitly like those who forbid even a non-Jew's. Great wonder on Shulchan Aruch bringing Rashi in previous seif and Rashba here — for Rashba no remedy for a Jew's oven even in partnership with a non-Jew unless they stipulated at oven purchase that the Pesach week belongs to the non-Jew, like siman 245. On what Rashba wrote the Jew acquired loaves the treasurer brought after Pesach and forbidden — why not say here too he is pleased to acquire, not because of prohibition, as siman 448 regarding a non-Jew bringing chametz on yom tov the Jew can later say so and it is permitted — different there, no proof he wants acquisition on yom tov. Certainly here he is not pleased with the prohibition — unlike here chametz from today's act baking in his oven today when he gives the oven to bake today; certainly he is pleased — simple in my humble opinion; and see end of this siman.
