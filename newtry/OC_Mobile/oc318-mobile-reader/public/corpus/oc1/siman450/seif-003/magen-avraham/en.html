He tells the non-Jew etc. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot that it is permitted to stipulate before Pesach and take money etc.; and do not say regarding a Jew and non-Jew who have a field in partnership that it is forbidden to say "take yours for Shabbat and mine for weekday" as siman 245 — there all the trouble is shared and it appears as trouble for the Jew; but here, when he takes money first, it is permitted (end of Hagahot Maimoniyot). But Mordechai wrote as here, that he need not take money. It appears they dispute the dispute of Rambam and Rosh in Yoreh De'ah siman 294 seif 13 regarding orlah (end of his words); if so, it requires further study on the Magen Avraham, since he ruled there it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew "take your share in orlah years and I afterwards" — that is orlah substitutes, and here too it should be forbidden. One may say: here the Jew never acquired chametz — he only sells the oven to the non-Jew on Pesach and the non-Jew can bake matzah in it; not so there where he acquired trees. Nevertheless one should practice selling the oven to the non-Jew on Pesach and taking money first as Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote, and so Bach; and on the four days that are Yom Tov it is in any event forbidden to say "take yours" etc. as siman 245.
