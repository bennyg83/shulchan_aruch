(א) Even. Taz — see signs 1 and 2: Tur brought Rashi responsum take money etc. — what Mechaber brought seif 3, omitted take money, relied on what Mordechai wrote first chapter Avodah Zarah siman 448 — Maharam asked regarding Jew and non-Jew with partnership oven etc., answered stipulation before Pesach permitted; see sign 2 what Taz challenged and what one may say. Afterwards Tur and R' Shimshon b. Avraham forbade in practice etc. — what Mechaber brought seif 4, as Be'er HaGolah noted; and since Tur and Rashba with vav imply disputing Rashi responsum — but unrelated: Rashi had condition before Pesach, Rashba Jew's oven etc.; also loaves they baked on Pesach — obviously Rashi agrees forbidden; money are chametz substitutes (what is l'chatchila is for Rashba who ruled b'dieved permitted, Eshel Avraham sign 5). Therefore dispute of Rashi and Rashba: Rashba forbids non-Jew's chametz in benefit during Pesach — profiting from issurei hana'ah (therefore what Rashba forbids taking money even before Pesach — see Magen Avraham sign 4). Rashi holds non-Jew's chametz during Pesach permitted in benefit — therefore no profiting from issurei hana'ah here etc. If so, at first glance Shulchan Aruch rulings contradict: seif 4 like seif 3 even partnership oven even if non-Jew can remove anytime and say he does not want to partner Pesach week and oven stands empty or they bake other things — nevertheless like partnership start as sign 1 end, still profiting from issurei hana'ah, wage of issurei hana'ah forbidden l'chatchila elsewhere; no remedy for seif 3 except at oven purchase start they stipulate Pesach week oven belongs to non-Jew, week after to Jew — then permitted not against will, as explained. Nevertheless difficult what Rashi holds non-Jew's chametz permitted in benefit during Pesach — difficult, Gemara Pesachim 29a concludes R' Avraham retracted; better explain Rashi permits profiting from other issurei hana'ah as above. For practical law: three seifim do not contradict — partnership oven is different; whoever stipulates before Pesach is as if stipulated at partnership start — proper. With this understand Kenesset HaGedolah glosses Beit Yosef sign 4 (see there) and siman 245 — condition before Shabbat only helps at partnership start, there appears as his agent, as Magen Avraham sign 3 and Taz per his view signs 1-2; not so seif 4 Jew's oven — condition does not help even before Pesach, forbidden to take money, ultimately profiting from issurei hana'ah; and hirer of donkey seif 7 — as Magen Avraham sign 11. All when partnership oven; but if rented oven from non-Jew to bake and both can remove anytime, even Taz agrees stipulation before Pesach helps non-Jew bake Pesach week, no profiting from issurei hana'ah, as Kenesset HaGedolah said.

(ב) And what challenged on Rashba — prohibition not pleased to acquire — not body of law forbidding profiting from issurei hana'ah; rather loaves forbidden because he acquired oven like his field and Jew's chametz over which Passover passed forbidden by law; prohibition not pleased to acquire — answered he is pleased since he left to bake etc. (end of his words); see Magen Avraham; and see there further.
