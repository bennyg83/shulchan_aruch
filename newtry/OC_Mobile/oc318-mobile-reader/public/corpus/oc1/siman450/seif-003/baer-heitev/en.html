Afterwards. And the Acharonim agreed to practice that he sell the oven to the non-Jew on Pesach and take the money that comes for his share (Kaf HaChayyim, end of his words). Nevertheless, on a Shabbat within Pesach it does not help through what he says "take yours" as on other Shabbats of the year, as written siman 245, unless they stipulated at the beginning of the partnership (end of his words). And Magen Avraham wrote: and on the four days that are Yom Tov, in any event it is forbidden to tell him "take yours" (end of his words); and Kaf HaChayyim disagrees with him (end of his words).
