(א) Forbidden to rent a utensil — see Mishna Berurah: Tur's reason because of wanting its continued existence; and there are opinions among Acharonim whether specifically in rental or even borrowing has prohibition of wanting continued existence; see Kaf HaChayyim and Ma'agalei Tzedek.

(ב) On Pesach — and proven from Shulchan Aruch of the Gra that for wanting continued existence there is no permission — Agur when renting before Pesach in all cases forbidden. However Bach implies whether profiting or wanting continued existence, in all cases there is room to permit however it was before thirty [days] (and he wrote so per his version in Agur, and likewise those who read before Pesach); and so Ma'agalei Tzedek wrote; and know further: even for those who hold prohibition of wanting continued existence applies to chametz — only l'chatchila; b'dieved no stringency, as above seif 4 regarding profiting from issurei hana'ah; and so Peri Chadash wrote; and see Chamed Moshe what he wrote on this.
