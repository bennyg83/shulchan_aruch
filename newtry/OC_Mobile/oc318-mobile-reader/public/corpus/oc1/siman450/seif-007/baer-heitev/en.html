(א) Utensils. The reason he wants its continued existence is that the chametz remain and be sustained inside — for if he removes the chametz from the pot while it is still on the fire the vessel may crack at the fire and then he benefits from chametz; but in an oven this does not apply. And further, since the vessel itself is forbidden in benefit because the pot absorbed chametz, it is forbidden to rent it l'chatchila; but an oven, when one bakes in it, is already fired and does not absorb chametz (Magen Avraham). Taz wrote: but three or four weeks before Pesach it is permitted in every case — see there.

(ב) Chametz. The agreement of Acharonim: here it deals with one who rented unstipulated and did not specify to bring chametz on it (end of his words). Bach wrote what is practiced in Poland: Jews rent an estate from the lord that has cauldrons and mills; they take a measure from every wage of beer cooked in cauldrons, and likewise take a measure of malt from everyone who grinds malt — if the Jew is obligated to repair the cauldrons and mills when they break, then they are in the Jew's possession and it is forbidden to receive even money as his wage, since their custom is to give chametz — it is like benefiting from chametz money and is forbidden. And if he has authority and power to prevent them from cooking at all, he is obligated to prevent them, for this is wanting its continued existence; and through sale it is permitted — that is, he sell before Pesach to a non-Jew all the measures that will come from this week and take the money, and even though the non-Jew gives him the measures afterwards it is permitted. And if the Jew need not repair the cauldrons and mills, it suffices to stipulate before Pesach as in seif 2. And if he has no one with whom to stipulate, it is like b'dieved and permitted without a condition (end of his words); and see Taz and Magen Avraham.

(ג) Benefit. Such as to place cold in it temporarily on dry [food], or something moist that is not an eating matter, such as washing — and specifically if it is temporary so we are not concerned he will use it for another use (Kaf HaChayyim). And Rashal forbids only washing the head. And see responsum Sha'agat Aryeh siman 66 regarding a Jew who was hired for a year to guard wine — whether it is permitted to guard and receive money for the wage on Pesach; see there.
