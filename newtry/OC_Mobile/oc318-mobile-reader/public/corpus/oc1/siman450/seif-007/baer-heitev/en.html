(א) Utensils. The reason he wants its continued existence is that the chametz remain and be sustained inside — for if he removes the chametz from the pot while it is still on the fire the vessel may crack at the fire and then he benefits from chametz; but in an oven this does not apply. And further, since the vessel itself is forbidden in benefit because the pot absorbed chametz, it is forbidden to rent it l'chatchila; but an oven, when one bakes in it, is already fired and does not absorb chametz (Magen Avraham). Taz wrote: but three or four weeks before Pesach it is permitted in every case — see there.

(2) chametz. The agreement of the latter was suppressed by Meiri that he hired just and did not mean to bring chametz on it. The BH wrote what they practice in the state of Poland that Jews rent an Orandi from the shararah that has shoots and rams that take from every wage that cooks meda and also take a share of force from everyone who grows force dam. Israel is obligated to repair the shoots and rams when they break down then they are under Israel's authority and are not allowed to receive even a fraction of their wages. Hamz is the beneficiary of the fees of Hamz and is prohibited and if he has permission and authority to prevent them from ripening at all, he is obligated to prevent them. And if there is no Israel, it is necessary to correct the shoots and the wombs, let the KP be changed as in section 2. If he does not have a people with whom, the HL is changed as a slave and a servant without the conditions of Akal and A. 16 and 11:

Such as to place cold in it temporarily on dry [food], or something moist that is not an eating matter, such as washing — and specifically if it is temporary so we are not concerned he will use it for another use (Kaf HaChayyim). And Rashal forbids only washing the head.
