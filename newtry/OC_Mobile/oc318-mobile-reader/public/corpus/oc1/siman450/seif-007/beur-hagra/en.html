Seif 7, forbidden. See Magen Avraham.
But he may rent. This is in the Tosefta, chapter 2. But in the Yerushalmi it is taught that it is forbidden, whether with a house, a ship, or a donkey. So ruled the Geonim, and as written regarding yayin nesech: one who rents the donkey, etc., there 62a. There is no distinction between yayin nesech and other prohibitions except after the fact, regarding whether the money becomes forbidden, as above. Even if he does not come with it, it is forbidden, as written in the Yerushalmi there. So too it is ruled in Yoreh Deah, siman 133, seif 1; see there. So too it is ruled in the gloss above, seif 5.
And some permit. That is to say, this opinion disagrees with the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, who wrote that it is forbidden to rent, etc. Although with a donkey it is permitted, as the Tur wrote, because this is wanting its continued existence through something else, as written in Avodah Zarah 32a, where we hold like the one who forbids there, nevertheless Tosafos there, s.v. 'and this,' permit in such a case, as they wrote: there, etc. This is what is written in the Tosafos cited above in seif 4: 'and we rule the halacha like the one,' etc. He does not disagree with him about what they forbade regarding renting, for even with his donkey it is forbidden, as above. Therefore he wrote, 'and some permit heating,' etc.
