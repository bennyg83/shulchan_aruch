(א) Seif 7 — Forbidden. See Magen Avraham.

(ב) But one may rent. Tosefta chapter 2; but in Yerushalmi it was taught forbidden whether in house, ship, or donkey; and so Geonim; and as written regarding innkeeper there 62a; and no difference between yayin nesech and other prohibitions except b'dieved to seize its value as above; and even if it did not come with him forbidden as Yerushalmi there and Yoreh De'ah siman 332 seif 1 (end of his words); and so in gloss above seif 5.

(ג) And some permit. Meaning he disagrees with Tur and Shulchan Aruch who wrote forbidden to rent etc. even though for donkey permitted as Tur wrote because of wanting continued existence through something else as Avodah Zarah 32a — we hold like those who forbid there; but Tosafot there s.v. vehai permit in such a case as written there etc.; and we rule like those who permit etc.; and he does not disagree where they forbade renting, for even for one's donkey forbidden as above; therefore wrote and some permit heating etc.
