(א) In order to. See Be'er Heitev s.k. 13 what he wrote to permit in oven; Magen Avraham wrote it deals with his unstipulated wage and oven presumably for baking chametz — not so for utensil and donkey unstipulated permitted; with this resolve that Shulchan Aruch words do not contradict seif 4.

(ב) Chametz. Tosafot Pesachim 22 implies: if a non-Jew's animal is laden with chametz, forbidden to unload except not in his presence. First version permitted because of animal's pain (Bach, end of his words).

(ג) The custom. See siman 442 and 447 — we hold forbidden to sell to a non-Jew a chicken in which wheat was found even if he does not benefit from chametz; see Yoreh De'ah 54. Further therefore say a dish cooked in chametz utensil may be sold to a non-Jew; Ma'agalei Tzedek wrote it deals with utensils specifically for washing and bathing only (end of his words); see Taz in Yoreh De'ah there.
