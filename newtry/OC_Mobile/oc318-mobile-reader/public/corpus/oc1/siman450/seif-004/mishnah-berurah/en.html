(א) And a non-Jew baked in it etc. — and it implies even without the owner's knowledge; and so Acharonim.
(ב) Even money — all the more loaves from those who baked on Pesach; forbidden to receive, even after Pesach.
(ג) For it is profiting etc. — forbidden l'chatchila d'rabbanan from all issurei hana'ah, even if he gives money before or after Pesach; and unlike seif 3 where the oven was partners' and when they divide at the time it was entirely the non-Jew's oven.
(ד) Permitted to benefit from them — profiting from issurei hana'ah is not forbidden b'dieved anywhere where it is not the body of chametz or its substitute, only money came through chametz as cause — they were not stringent except for yayin nesech because of idolatry stringency. All this if he received money; but if they gave him loaves baked in his oven, forbidden to benefit, for he already acquired them on Pesach as oven-wage if their custom is always to give loaves — it is chametz of a Jew over which Passover passed.
