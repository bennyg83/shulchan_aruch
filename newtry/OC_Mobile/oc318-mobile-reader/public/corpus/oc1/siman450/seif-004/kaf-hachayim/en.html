(4) There — he must repay etc. A Jew who lent chametz to a non-Jew before Pesach must repay chametz like it after Pesach, since he does not return the first chametz in existence — permitted (Riaz, Bach in Sefer HaGedolah chapter Kol Sha'ah; Kenesset HaGedolah in gloss). But Peri Chadash challenged and wrote even if he returns that chametz, permitted even for eating — since he lent to non-Jew it stood in his possession and is non-Jew's chametz over which Passover passed, permitted even for eating; and R' Akiva Eiger agreed there; R' Zalman Orach Chayim 2.
