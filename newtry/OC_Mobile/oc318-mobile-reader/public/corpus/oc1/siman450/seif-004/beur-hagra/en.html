(א) Seif 4 — A Jew etc. for it is etc. On seif 2 (1) — his wage etc. and we learned "he sold them" etc.; and see there Tosafot s.v. vehatnan. However, it is wondrous etc.

(ב) And if he received etc. — permitted. It implies even he himself, as Rambam wrote siman 443 and as the Gemara's plain meaning in Avodah Zarah there; and see Tosafot there s.v. tenan. It is wondrous etc.; but here it is reconciled; and see Ran perek 5 of Avodah Zarah who brought proof from Yerushalmi and wrote in the Yerushalmi, in the name of Ramban: what we conclude there is a fine they fined carriers and innkeepers specifically for innkeepers in shevi'it when produce takes its value and forbidden by first reasoning that produce takes its value — relies etc. (end of his words). Seif 5 — there: he rented to dwell etc.; and see Gemara there 65a — "if so" etc.; and likewise here, he takes nothing for the chametz, for even if he does not bake at all he will pay him.

(ג) And likewise permitted. We learn from the above; but to rent that he bring chametz inside is forbidden as Yerushalmi chapter 2 halacha 2: a Jew shall not rent his house to a non-Jew to place chametz inside; and even though they said in first chapter of Pesachim he designated a house etc. — here it deals during Pesach or erev Pesach when already forbidden in benefit — profiting from issurei hana'ah as above; and so Yerushalmi there during Pesach deals during Pesach — what if etc. and it was taught do not rent etc.; and Taz and Magen Avraham; and see Bach in the name of Agur. It appears to me that also that in Pesachim first chapter deals during Pesach, as there the baraita teaches a non-Jew entered etc. — therefore during Pesach; and above it says he deposited etc., designated etc. — to say etc.; rather there it deals when he designated a house not to bring chametz in, and permitted even when he brings inside as above; and on this they said there a non-Jew when it says etc. — to say etc.; and it was taught etc. — there too renting a dwelling, and nevertheless forbidden; and Geonim explain unlike Rashi — rather he designated a house: he rents him another house not in his house; and this is "a non-Jew entered etc." — since he is with the chametz deposited etc., even without liability he designated etc. without liability, since it is another house; and so in Yerushalmi there: a non-Jew's chametz during Pesach — what? R' Yirmiyah said permitted, R' Yissa forbidden; and it was taught: do not rent one's donkey to a non-Jew to bring chametz on it — explain it when it comes with him; and it was taught do not rent his house etc. — you may say when it comes with him; therefore even for one who permits, forbidden with him in the house; and this is what is written in Tosafot; and above siman 448 seif 3 outside the house.
