There is one who permits. This is the view of the Tur, who brings proof from what we learned: if he hired him to do another labor, even though he said to him, 'Transfer yayin nesech for me from place to place,' it is permitted. One can answer that there it is different, for it is not at all evident that he wanted him to transfer yayin nesech, since there are many other labors. This is unlike an oven, where the non-Jew will certainly use it only for chametz, so it is as though he rented it explicitly for chametz. Furthermore, this is very difficult to me, for it is written in siman 215, seif 3, that it does not help if he stipulates with the non-Jew that the animal rest on Shabbos, because the non-Jew is not trusted about that. All the more so here, where he will certainly bake chametz in it. This requires analysis.
