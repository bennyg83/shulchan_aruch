There are those who permit. This is Tosafot's view bringing proof from what we learn: he hired him for another work, even though he told him pass me yayin nesech barrels — permitted. One may say this case is different — there it is not proven he wants yayin nesech transfer, for many other works; not so for an oven — the non-Jew will certainly bake only chametz — as if rented explicitly for chametz. Further great difficulty, for it is written siman 256 seif 3: but it does not help if he stipulates with the non-Jew that his animal rest on Shabbat, for the non-Jew is not trusted — all the more here he will certainly bake chametz; requires further study.
