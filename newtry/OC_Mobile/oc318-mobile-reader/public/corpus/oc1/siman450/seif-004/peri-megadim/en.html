There is. See Taz. Regarding profiting from other items forbidden in benefit, there is one who is lenient even ab initio, like one who rents a donkey according to one explanation. Also, siman 246, seif 3, is not comparable; the printed text saying 215 is an error. There, there is a Torah prohibition and the non-Jew is not trusted, unlike here, where even a rabbinic prohibition is not clear. However, if there is a definite rabbinic prohibition, one should not create it ab initio as a rabbinic doubt; see Magen Avraham 467, s.k. 2.
