(א) To receive. Since the oven belongs to the Jew; and do not say for seif 3 that there it is a partnership oven (Taz s.k. 3); and see in Kenesset HaGedolah.

(ב) To benefit. For chametz does not take its value, as written siman 443; but if he received the loaves it is forbidden to benefit from them, for he acquired them on Pesach as wage for his oven, since their custom is always to give loaves — and if so it is chametz of a Jew over which Passover passed (Magen Avraham).
