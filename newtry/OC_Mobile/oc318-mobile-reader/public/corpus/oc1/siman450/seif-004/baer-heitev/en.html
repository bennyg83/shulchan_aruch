To receive. Since the oven belongs to the Jew, this is unlike seif 3, where the oven is jointly owned. See Taz, seif katan 3, and Knesses HaGedolah. To benefit. Chametz does not transfer its prohibition to the money received for it, as explained at the end of siman 443. If, however, he received the loaves themselves, he may not benefit from them, because he acquired them during Pesach as payment for use of his oven. Since it is always customary to give loaves, they are regarded as chametz belonging to a Jew that remained over Pesach. Magen Avraham.
