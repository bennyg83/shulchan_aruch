One may say to a worker on Pesach: take this dinar and buy and eat, even though he knows he will acquire chametz; but he should not tell him: go and eat and I will pay. There are those who permit even this, except if he gave the dinar beforehand or handed it over.And it is forbidden to buy chametz for a non-Jew on Pesach, even with the non-Jew's money. (Rivash siman 41)):
