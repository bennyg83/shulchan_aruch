(1) chametz. And if he is rented from him for a year, he will not eat at his house because of suspicion. association:

(ב) And eat. If he knows he will eat chametz (Magen Avraham). And the meaning of the language of the Gemara implies that even with a mere concern it is forbidden (Kaf HaChayyim).

(ג) They permit. And yes, this is the main ruling (Magen Avraham). But Bach, Kenesset HaGedolah, and Kaf HaChayyim ruled like the first opinion to be stringent.

(ד) Dinar. Specifically when the dinar is still in existence in the shopkeeper's hand at the time he eats. And if he did not give him the dinar beforehand, it is permitted to say: give the workers chametz and I will pay — and if so, one who has a child who needs to drink chametz may tell a non-Jew: give him to drink chametz and I will pay; but he should not take the chametz in his hand. And Kaf HaChayyim wrote that he should not say "and I will pay" (Bach). And for a sick person in danger, everything is permitted (Magen Avraham, end of his words); and see Magen Avraham siman 443 and responsum Nishmat Shabbat siman 14; and see responsum Bach siman 43. Nevertheless it is good to make a partition of ten [tefachim] to separate so that he not come to eat from it (Kaf HaChayyim, end of his words).

(ה) Of the non-Jew. For there is no agency for a non-Jew, and the Jew ends up buying them; and further there is concern lest he eat from it; and further it appears he wants its continued existence (Magen Avraham).
