(א) Forbidden. Taz — per his view sign 4 regarding profiting from issurei hana'ah forbidden, likewise seif 3.

(ב) What is written on estates. Magen Avraham — two difficulties: one, a person does not sell something not yet in existence, not a sale at all — see priests' allotment what to examine. Two, if non-Jew bought he only bought when it came to existence, and we all the more permit eating from seller — not like this. One may say: even though a person does not transfer something not in existence, he can remove himself from it like hefker — can declare hefker even for not in existence, as Bach responsum 124 hefker from vow reason, person forbids himself not in existence — see R' David seif 64, Magen Avraham 1; likewise here Jew can remove from them before Pesach siman 441 Taz end what is written there. What is written one with his own mills — no remedy except sell to non-Jew with absorption — I do not know — he may sell mill itself any erev Pesach and it appears should read "and rent with absorption" — but complete sale of mill body appears permitted any erev Pesach and as said.
