Seif 6. It is forbidden to buy chametz for a non-Jew on Pesach. If on Pesach he told a non-Jew to buy chametz for him, and he bought it for him, one may say that even according to the poskim who hold there is agency for a non-Jew as a stringency - see Yoreh Deah, siman 169 - nevertheless it is only rabbinic, and it is permitted after Pesach. Even on Pesach, if it is a case of significant loss, one may say that he need not destroy it. If he buys through an apostate, one must discuss whether we say with an apostate that there is no agency for transgression, and therefore the Jew did not acquire it, and the apostate also did not acquire it because he did not want to acquire for himself; see Teivas Gomeh by the Gaon Poras Yosef, Parashas Korach, and see his book Eshel Avraham above, siman 305.
