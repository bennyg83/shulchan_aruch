Seif 6 — And forbidden to buy chametz for non-Jew on Pesach. Told non-Jew on Pesach to buy chametz and he bought for him — one may say: even for poskim who hold agency for non-Jew stringently (see Yoreh De'ah siman 169) — nevertheless only d'rabbanan and permitted after Pesach; and on Pesach if b'dieved — one may say need not burn it; and regarding acquisition through apostate — debate if we say regarding apostate "inclined to idolatry" and Jew did not buy and apostate did not buy since he did not want to acquire — word of gematria to Gaon Porat Yosef parashat Korach; see his book Eshel Avraham above siman 305.
