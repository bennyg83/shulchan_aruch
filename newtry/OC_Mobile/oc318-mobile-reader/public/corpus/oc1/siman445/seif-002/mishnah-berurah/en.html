(א) To a non-Jew before, etc. — forbidden to give to his gentile male or female slave whose sustenance is on him if the chametz is abundant and cannot be eaten before prohibition time (Chok Yaakov in name of Bach; Chamed Moshe disagrees: we do not care that sustenance is on him since he does not pay at prohibition time but before Pesach; when Pesach comes he owes no more sustenance — only forbidden to say explicitly he should keep chametz all Pesach, for that is wanting chametz to exist; give in stam and say: take this chametz for your debt and you have no sustenance from me all Pesach. So Maharshak in his novellae; at minimum be careful the slave not eat chametz in his house lest they suspect paying debt with chametz, as siman 450:6 in Mishnah Berurah.)

(2) (j) It does not need to be burned - and it is correct for a person who practices not to take out any of his chametz when he gives it to the Akoum and to observe the mitzvah of Shabbat when he eats the crumbs that he allows to burn so that the crumbs are left and stand, and even if he allows them, he does not go astray, but instead puts his chametz at least as an olive in order to keep the mitzvah of his Shabbat as a correction [Beit Meir, etc. in HA and Hamed Moshe]:

(ג) To benefit from its coals, etc. — chametz was not yet forbidden in benefit; likewise permitted in the fifth hour to cook and other benefits. Nevertheless the custom is to make a separate bonfire even in the fifth hour so they not come to be lenient at the sixth; they burn in the courtyard, not the stove [Acharonim].

(ד) So that he not forget, etc. — and although for other eating and benefit prohibitions we do not require a partition, here it is different because they do not avoid it all year — we are concerned lest he come to eat it; therefore a partition is required for recognition; and covering a vessel on top of the chametz does not suffice, lest he remove the vessel for his use and the chametz be exposed and they did not permit covering a vessel [in siman 446 seif 1] except for chametz found in his house on Yom Tov when a partition cannot be made, and also only for a short time, for at night he will burn it.

(ה) In benefit — even if the bread mixed with other loaves and is unrecognizable, all are forbidden in benefit; no remedy to permit via redeeming benefit-value cast to destruction [Yoreh De'ah 110 Shach s.k. 2, 142]. Acharonim: there is remedy to sell bread to non-Jew except prohibition value — deduct benefit-value of chametz used to kindle from price paid to non-Jew, so he does not benefit from chametz kindling; crumble bread before selling [where pat akum permitted] lest he resell to Jew. Even if not mixed, this sale remedy exists; some forbid if not mixed; with great loss or pressing need, rely to be lenient.

(ו) And likewise the coals — likewise ash; therefore forbidden to warm by coals or benefit from their light, etc.; likewise forbidden to launder with ash, etc. Nevertheless permitted to cover slaughter blood, for mitzvot were not given for benefit [Acharonim].

(ז) His — meaning of chametz; and if coals of chametz mixed with coals of wood with which they burned the chametz, it is forbidden to benefit from all of them, and they are not nullified even in a thousand, for they have permission of benefit without nullification — namely to retain them until after Pesach; and a thing that has something permitting it is not nullified.
