And the custom — Taz: if he fulfilled burning in the fifth hour and later finds chametz, destroy like any chametz during Pesach; if found on Yom Tov, break vessel and bury it; burn even though he already fulfilled burning, for R' Yehudah's burning may be d'oraisa on Pesach like notar — karet and "you shall not leave"; chametz likewise. Erev Pesach until night — no karet, only "it shall not be seen"; therefore lenient also on the 14th and other stringencies since no karet until night (siman 447). If so, at six we burn from custom like R' Yehudah per Rashi; unlike when already fulfilled burning — see note 3. Tosafot Pesachim 12b s.v. eimatai — for Rabbenu Tam R' Yehudah burns from seven too; Tosafot 28a s.v. vechametz, 27b s.v. ein — requires study. Know we rule halacha like Sages who bury and ash forbidden — not a doubt R' Yehudah vs. Sages, rather both stringencies; practical difference: doubt if matzah baked on chametz coals — perhaps certainly Sages, and in Pri Megadim Yoreh De'ah we wrote doubt of dispute, mixed forbidden (see there). Beit Shmuel Even HaEzer 28:54 and Choshen Mishpat there imply doubt R' Yehudah vs. Sages. Beit Shmuel: she may sell to a gentile — requires study, for to him it is forbidden; see Even HaEzer laws of marriage — requires study whether ash is definite issur or doubt.
