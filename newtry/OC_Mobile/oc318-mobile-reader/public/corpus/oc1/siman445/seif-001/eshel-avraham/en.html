Magen Avraham challenged Rambam: why rule to burn — chametz is among buried items whose ash is forbidden? Requires study (Maharil buries coals — see there). Chok Yaakov: chametz is not in "all buried items shall not be burned"; also because of "it shall not be seen" stringency; also we destroy before prohibition time.
