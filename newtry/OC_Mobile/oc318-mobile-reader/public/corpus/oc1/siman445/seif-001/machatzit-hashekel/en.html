(s.k. 1) He burns it, etc. — "shall not be burned" — we decreed, etc.; possibly meaning: he shall not burn to treat it like burned items, etc.; we did not decree, etc. What Magen Avraham wrote at the mishnah's end "shall not be burned lest," etc. — is not the mishnah's words but Magen Avraham's reason; therefore one may say the Rambam does not explain the mishnah as a decree, rather the mishnah's intent is not to treat it like burned items.
