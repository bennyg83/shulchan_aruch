(א) Seif 1 — he burns it. Rambam and the girsa also "even crumbles," and even in a text that does not read "even" — nevertheless he explains "even crumbles" from that R' Yehudah says only burning; and furthermore it is impossible to say that all mishnayot in the first chapter and all baraitot where we learned they burn — all according to R' Yehudah: R' Meir, R' Yosei, R' Shimon, Rabban Gamliel, R' Eliezer, R' Yehoshua, R' Eliezer ish Bartuta — all like R' Yehudah; rather even for the Sages the essence is burning. There 21b: "and he shall not kindle," etc. — it fits only for R' Yehudah, etc. 40 — for the rabbis too it works; see Tosafot s.v. behadi, etc.

(ב) Or throws it into the sea, and if. He explains like R' Yosef, for he said to him R' Yehudah, and R' Yehudah said — learn from this that Rabbah's statement does not stand; and so is the sugya in many places.

(ג) The chametz is hard. Such as wheat, as stated in the Gemara; he explains one crumbles the wheat — not like Rashi.

(ד) And the custom. Meaning, even for R' Yehudah burning is not required until after six, as stated 12b, and as Rabbenu Tam explains in Tosafot s.v. eimatai — R' Yehudah's reason is he derives from notar, as stated 27b; and that is after its prohibition. So too in Yerushalmi chapter 2 end law 1: "Until its biur time has not arrived, you destroy it in 24 ways; once its biur time arrives, you destroy it by burning" — and it follows R' Yehudah. If so, even for poskim like R' Yehudah, because the mishnah in Temurah follows him, as stated in the Gemara end Temurah — see there; and even though it was rejected, nevertheless for the rabbis' view it was not rejected, as Tosafot 27b s.v. ein and s.v. shaf explain. Nevertheless, since they burn before prohibition time, from the Torah it is only from custom; and even though the Rosh wrote that even for Rashi who explains the opposite that at six they burn for R' Yehudah, nevertheless before six in the fifth and before that R' Yehudah agrees burning is not required, rather even if he sells and gives to a non-Jew it is permitted, as stated in the first chapter 21 — and as stated 2b, the Tur already challenged from what Rashi wrote 27b s.v. she'im, etc.; and even though we say, etc. — the custom is according to Rashi's view, but the essence is as Tosafot 12b s.v. eimatai explain; and he wrote so in the Yerushalmi, and all poskim agreed.

(ה) And good to burn. For R' Yehudah we derive from notar as above; in Yerushalmi Shabbat chapter 2, for the view that derives from notar — one does not burn on Yom Tov, and likewise at night; and 27b is a general stringency that we derive only after prohibition time — this is what "but," etc. means; see Rashi 6a s.v. oseh. At night, etc., and siman 446 seif 1 — one may say he wrote per the rabbis, but for R' Yehudah one burns by day.

(ו) If he wants. So they inferred from what he leaves, etc. — that what he finds he burns immediately.
