(א) He crumbles it; and if he throws it into the privy, he need not crumble. If he has collected water containing fish, he must not throw it there, for he benefits from it. Magen Avraham, Chok Yaakov. It is forbidden in all melachot when the time of burning chametz arrives until he burns, as written in siman 431. And on what Magen Avraham wrote on the Rambam — Yad Efraim challenged him and agreed with Chok Yaakov — see there.

(ב) The choice is his. Maharil wrote to burn specifically by day so he will remember to nullify. Maharshal received from his teachers to burn chametz on Hoshana Rabbah (and in such a case one may kindle the oven with the lulav) — see there.
