דין ביעור חמץ. ובו ג סעיפים:
The law of destroying chametz, containing 3 seifim: How is chametz destroyed? He burns it, or crumbles and casts to the wind, or throws it into the sea. If the chametz was hard and the sea does not break it quickly, he crumbles it and then throws it into the sea.The custom is to burn it; it is good to burn by day, similar to notar which was burned by day (his) own opinion). But if he wants to burn immediately after bedikah so a rodent not drag it, the choice is in his hand (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 3 and Kol Bo).):
