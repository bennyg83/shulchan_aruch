The laws of destroying hametz... How is hametz destroyed? He burns it or crumbles it up and throws it into the wind or casts it into the sea. And if the hametz was hard and the sea would not cause it to break up quickly, he must first crumble it up and then throw it into the sea. Hagah: And the custom is to burn it in the day similar to the leftovers of sacrifices that were burned in the day (da'at atzmo). But if he wants to burn it immediately after the searching in order that a rodent will not drag it, the option is his (Hagahot Maimoniyyot, Kol Bo)
