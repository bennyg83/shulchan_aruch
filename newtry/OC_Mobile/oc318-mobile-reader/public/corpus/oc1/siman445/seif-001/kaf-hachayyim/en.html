1) [Se'if 1] How is chametz eliminated? One burns it, etc. There are three methods of eliminating chametz, corresponding to three punishments the Egyptians inflicted upon the Jewish people in Egypt. Burning corresponds to their burning the firstborn Jews in a fiery furnace, as it is stated, 'He brought you out of the iron furnace, from Egypt,' and as taught in Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer, chapter 48. Crumbling corresponds to their strangling the children upon the birthstool, as it is stated, 'And you shall look upon the birthstool; if it is a son, you shall kill him,' and so forth. Casting it into the sea corresponds to their casting the children into the Nile, as it is stated, 'Every son who is born you shall cast into the Nile,' and so forth. The purpose is that we remember the bondage and praise the Holy One, blessed is He, for rescuing us from it. It may further be explained that these correspond to three kelipos: a storm wind, a great cloud, and a flashing fire. Burning corresponds to the flashing fire; crumbling and scattering to the wind corresponds to the storm wind; and casting into the sea corresponds to the great cloud, since a cloud rises from the sea. The elimination of chametz alludes to eliminating the kelipos. Three methods of elimination were therefore prescribed corresponding to the three aspects of the kelipos, so that one should intend to eliminate them and hasten the end of the exile, speedily in our days, amen.
2) There. How is chametz eliminated, etc. Women are also obligated in the positive commandment to eliminate chametz, including the obligation to eliminate it from the sixth hour onward. Responsa Sha'agas Aryeh, siman 82; Sha'arei Teshuvah.
3) There: One burns it. Until it becomes charcoal. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 4.
