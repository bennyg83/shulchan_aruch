Where ravens are found, etc. With this the courtyard need-not-check (Hagahot Maimoniyot) is understood; if in ownerless courtyard, what checking — even chametz, if he declared hefker before six, permitted (Tur 437, Beit Yosef 448 Yerushalmi, Darkei Moshe in name of Or Zaru'a: what difference giving to non-Jew or hefker). Rather it must not be ownerless; what Shulchan Aruch concludes "even though in ownerless place" means: since placed in his courtyard, it is hefker for whoever will take, nevertheless since in his domain he must destroy as required — burning for R' Yehudah, as Hagahot Maimoniyot. Kesef Mishneh: those who place chametz on Rosh Hashanah sides at six improperly — once six arrives must remove from the world; Hagahot Maimoniyot — see siman 433 and Yoreh De'ah 446.
