In Shulchan Aruch seif 3 "and if he finds after prohibition," etc. — responsum Pri Chadash 12: one had chametz in a gentile's house, forgot to sell; after chatzot remembered and nullified before chatzot as customary; asked what to do — advised to leave in ownerless place for dogs or pigs; if gentiles acquire it, buy cheap after Pesach or they return it — gentile in whose house chametz is will not let dogs waste it, he acquires and may return; see there he elaborated Gemara Yerushalmi in name of Rivash, source of this law; see Acharonim siman 433:6.
