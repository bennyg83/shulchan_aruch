(1) Before the time of its prohibition - After the time of its prohibition, it is not fun for them to abandon it, because the Omnipresent took the scripture out of His authority and prohibited it with pleasure, but is obliged to shut it down and burn it, and as above:
(ב) Where ravens are found — we rely they will eat it; see siman 433:6 where we cited many Acharonim: this law is only in a public ownerless street, not one's courtyard.
(ג) Rather he shall destroy it — Acharonim disagree: if he declared hefker and threw to ownerless place before prohibition, no further biur obligation (siman 433). Some explain Mechaber also holds so, but deals with another case (Magen Avraham, Chok Yaakov, Sha'arei Efraim). Even if placed in ownerless place, he must declare complete hefker in speech and heart, not intending to reclaim after Pesach — otherwise not complete hefker while not yet acquired by another.
