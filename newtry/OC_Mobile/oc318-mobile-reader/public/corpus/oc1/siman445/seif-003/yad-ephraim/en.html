(There) Magen Avraham s.k. 8 — and it is not similar to what was said: if he checked part of the house, etc. — see what Eshel Avraham wrote in Bechor Shor, that the distinction is that here certainly a mouse entered and we do not know whether it entered carrying chametz or entered empty — see there and other Acharonim; and in my humble opinion it appears that at first glance it is reasonable: where the house is in presumption of checked and again chametz is found inside, since certainly chametz was brought without the owner's knowledge and we do not know how much was brought — if so, when he left chametz in this corner and found it in another corner, since he does not know it is the same chametz that was in the other corner — chametz is found in a place we presumed checked; and although one might say this is the chametz from the other corner, we do not rely on that, for in chametz found in a room presumed checked we do not say this chametz was dragged from an unchecked room — only this chametz and no more; rather we say since certainly chametz came here from elsewhere, if so one may say much chametz was brought to this house, for this chametz too we do not know how it came; what difference whether from corner to corner or room to room; but regarding a mouse that entered, etc. — since what we find here one may say there is no presumption at all, for the reason it newly appeared in this house is known — through the mouse that entered there; therefore we rely that this is the loaf the mouse brought. And furthermore one might say Magen Avraham distinguishes: for a mouse the presumption is not born in the house itself, only the presumption comes from outside through the mouse that entered from outside — we rely on what is found; not so when he left it in corners, for the presumption is born in the body of the house itself that the chametz left there, in the house in that place disappeared from our sight — even if found elsewhere we do not rely; but therefore where chametz was in one corner and a mouse came and took and he checked again and found a loaf, he must check the entire house, since presumption was born in the house itself — and this is not in the plain sense; and it is clear as I wrote, that taking by mouse where the cause is known what changed its place is not like where the cause is unknown.
