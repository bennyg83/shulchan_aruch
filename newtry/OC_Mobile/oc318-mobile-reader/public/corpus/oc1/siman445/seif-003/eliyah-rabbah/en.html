If so, even if other wood was lying on the fire and he also placed the chametz on it, so that it is a case of this and this cause it, nevertheless it is forbidden; even through nullification it is forbidden. So wrote Magen Avraham. Nevertheless, I permitted in an actual case where they had heated an oven to bake matzos on Pesach and also placed in it one piece of wood soiled with chametz dough. The wood had nearly burned and the matzos were finished; they asked what to do, for the matzos would become chametz before they could heat another oven. I permitted them to sweep out all the coals and heat it with thin wood and small pieces of wood so that it would burn quickly, because presumably this soiled dough has no connection to an improved heat in the bread even by a mashehu, since it is immediately burned in the burning of the wood. Since according to the view of the Shulchan Aruch in Yoreh Deah siman 42 and many halachic decisors, where the coals were swept out they permit entirely, I relied on this to permit in this case even with its heat.
