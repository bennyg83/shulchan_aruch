Se'if 2: Like a five-year-old, etc. This is the Rambam, but it is a scribal error and should read that he is six, like Rav Ashi there. For if it ruled like those Amoraim, Rabbi Yannai and Reish Lakish, it should have said 'like a four- and five-year-old,' and explained whether his father is present. So too he ruled in Hilchos Eruvin like Rav Ashi, and so ruled the Rif there and all the Geonim. The law is one for eruvin and for sukkah, as written there. We also learned in the Mishnah regarding sukkah similarly, etc. So too in the Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Sukkah, end of halachah 9, Rabbi Oshaya taught: a minor who does not need his mother is obligated in sukkah and goes out with his mother's eruv.
