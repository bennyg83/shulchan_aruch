Like a five-year-old, like a six-year-old. Each according to his sharpness; see the end of siman 414.
Is obligated in sukkah. His mother is not obligated to educate him, as written in siman 343. However, some say that the beis din is obligated to educate him. Therefore, even though his father is not here, one should not be lenient to let him eat outside the sukkah; Terumas HaDeshen, siman 94. See siman 616. Nevertheless, it implies that another person cannot feed him directly, as written in siman 269.
