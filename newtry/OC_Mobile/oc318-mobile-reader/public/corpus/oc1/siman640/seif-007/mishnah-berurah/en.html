(35) Agents for a mitzvah. For example, one who goes to greet his teacher, to learn Torah, to redeem captives, and the like.
(36) Are exempt from the sukkah. Even while they are encamped, and even if a built sukkah is before them.
(37) Whether by day or by night. This means that even if they travel only by day, they are exempt also at night, because they are occupied in thought with the mitzvah and its preparation. It is all included in one who is engaged in a mitzvah, who is exempt from another mitzvah.
(38) And see above, siman 38. Meaning se'if 8 in the gloss, where it is proven that this applies specifically when he must exert himself after a sukkah. But if he need not exert himself after it, for example there is a sukkah prepared before him, he must enter the sukkah and eat and sleep there. If they cannot sleep so well in the sukkah and will be weary tomorrow and unable to fulfill the mitzvah properly, they are exempt.
