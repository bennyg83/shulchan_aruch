Agents of a mitzvah, etc. See Responsa Chasam Sofer, subparagraph 19, that this applies specifically when it is wholly for Hashem and he has no personal benefit from it. But one who travels for pay, where the main purpose of the agency is for his own benefit, is not thereby exempt from the mitzvah of sukkah; see there. This is from Pis'chei Teshuvah.
