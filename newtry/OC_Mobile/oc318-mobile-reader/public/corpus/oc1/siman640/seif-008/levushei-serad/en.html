Turei Zahav, subparagraph 2. Thus according to Rav Asi, etc. For he could have answered according to Rav Asi as well in this way: 'until' never includes the endpoint, and where his father is present, the measure is until the end of five, while Rav Asi, where his father is not present, gives the measure until the end of six. Rather, this answer is certainly forced. Since the halachah follows Rav Asi, as the Gemara brings him at the end of Perek Af Al Pi, and it did not want to answer this way, but instead answered that 'until' includes the endpoint, there is no longer any need to distinguish between whether his father is present or not.
