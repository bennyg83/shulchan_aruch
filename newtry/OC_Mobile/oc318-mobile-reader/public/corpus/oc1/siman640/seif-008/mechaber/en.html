Travelers on the road during the day are exempt from the Sukkah during the day and are obligated at night. Travelers during the night are exempted from the Sukkah and are obligated during the day: Haga, when they can find a Sukkah, but if they don't find a Sukkah, they can go on their way, even if they don't sit in it either day or night, for the rest of the year.
