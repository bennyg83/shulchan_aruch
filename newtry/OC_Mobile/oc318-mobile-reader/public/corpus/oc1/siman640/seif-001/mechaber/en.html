1. Who is exempt from dwelling in the Sukkah, and in it is 10 paragraphs. Women, slaves,
minors are exempt from the (mitzvah of) Sukkah. A tumtum (one has genitalia that are covered, unable to be recognized) or an androgynous is obligated out of doubt (perhaps they are male). And similarly one who is a half slave, half free person is obligated.
