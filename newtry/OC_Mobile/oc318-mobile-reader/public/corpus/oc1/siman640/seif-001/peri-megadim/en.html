Women, etc. See Turei Zahav. To me, the lowly one, a special intent appears here in Sukkah 28b. Sukkah is a halachah le-Moshe mi-Sinai, but if so, regarding slaves we cannot derive by an a fortiori argument from a halachah, and the same applies to all thirteen hermeneutic rules; even the gezeirah shavah of 'her, her' from a woman would not be derived. But if the halachah le-Moshe mi-Sinai reveals that the interpretation of the verse is not 'you shall dwell' in the manner of dwelling, or that we should not learn the gezeirah shavah of the fifteenth from Pesach, then the verse is interpreted, everything is compared to tefillin, and slaves are derived from a woman by gezeirah shavah. The Levush derives from 'the native-born' and from it being a time-bound positive commandment in the four days of Sukkos; see there. See Eliyah Rabbah and Tosafos, Kiddushin 33. Regarding 'the native-born,' one can say that according to Abaye it is a halachah le-Moshe mi-Sinai, for if from a verse, I might say women are obligated on the first night. But according to Rava, one can say that sukkah is in truth from the verse, as written in siman 639. The Gemara's phrase 'and now that you have said' does not fully imply this. The inclusion from the extra letter heh can also be explained: perhaps we do not derive a gezeirah shavah for slaves, because perhaps the thirteen hermeneutic rules were given only to expound what is explicit. Therefore it wrote the extra heh to exclude women, as though explaining that the comparison to time-bound positive commandments remains in place. Regarding the four cups, where women are obligated, one can say that since they too were in that miracle, they are obligated rabbinically. This is unlike sukkah, where even though they were in the miracle, the Torah explicitly excluded them, and Chazal did not obligate them rabbinically. This is unlike Chanukah candles and Megillah, whose essence is rabbinic; see Tosafos, Pesachim, Perek Arvei Pesachim, Tosafos Megillah 4, and the beginning of Arachin. In this way his garment is white like snow and his words are like clean wool.
