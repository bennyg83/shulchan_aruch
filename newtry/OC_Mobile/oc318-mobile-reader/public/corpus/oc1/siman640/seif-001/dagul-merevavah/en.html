See the book Tosefes Shabbos, who wanted to emend the text, but his words are forced. In the Rokeach too the reading is as I wrote. However, the intent of the Rokeach is that because of ohel, meaning on Shabbos and Yom Tov, it is forbidden under the sechach in every manner. But on Chol HaMoed, permission can be found under the sechach where he truly spread the sheet for beautification. On top of the sechach, however, where beautification does not apply, it is forbidden even on Chol HaMoed. Furthermore, it appears to me that the Rokeach there at the beginning of his words follows the view of Tosafos, which is the view of the 'some say' in siman 629, se'if 19. Even so, on top of the sechach it is forbidden because of maris ayin, for one who sees will not know that his intention is because of the sun. But under the sechach, even if one does not know that he did it because of the sun, nevertheless there is no maris ayin, for the observer will say that he spread a sheet to beautify it. After that the Rokeach wrote further: for we learned, if he spread over it, etc. This is Rashi's view. According to his conclusion, in truth there is no difference between on top of the sechach and under the sechach, except when it is truly for beautification. See the Rokeach himself and examine carefully.
