But. Although it is distressing for him to sit in the sukkah, because he wants to be alone so that he can be occupied with his grief, this teaches that he should settle his mind; this is the Rosh, unlike Maharik. The Olas Shabbos did not see there. It is written in Sefer Tanya that if the deceased was so beloved that he cannot remove him from his heart, he is exempt. This speaks where he is distressed by sitting in the sukkah, as we wrote above.
