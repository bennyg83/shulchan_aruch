[10] One for whom it is too narrow, etc. The Magen Avraham did not examine Terumas HaDeshen, siman 92, well in this matter; see there, and there is no need to elaborate. As for the Turei Zahav's astonishment about this, and his distinction between where one made it this way from the beginning, which is permitted, and where it later became narrow, there is no difficulty. Certainly everything is permitted. If he would be considered distressed even when he made it so from the beginning, he would not fulfill, for with any distress that applies to most people, even if for him it is not distress, he is nevertheless exempt. Terumas HaDeshen brings proof from making a sukkah seven by seven, and according to his calculation, even if one's feet are outside the sukkah, nevertheless his head and most of his body, which must be in the sukkah, are more than ten tefachim. Rather, perforce this is not considered distress.
