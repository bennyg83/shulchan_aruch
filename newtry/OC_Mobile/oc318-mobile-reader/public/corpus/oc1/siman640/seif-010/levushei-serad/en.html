There. The Rosh and the Tur, etc. They hold that Rav Asi does not disagree with Rabbi Yannai and Reish Lakish. Rather, Rav Asi speaks where his father is not present, and his measure is until the end of six, like the baraisa. Rabbi Yannai and Reish Lakish speak where his father is present, and the measure is until the end of four or the end of five, each according to the child's sharpness. Therefore the Tur wrote in siman 414 that when his father is present, he goes out with his mother's eruv until the end of six, and when his father is not present, he goes out until the end of five; see there. As to why the Tur did not write that with a sharp minor it is until the end of four, it appears to me that he holds that nowadays such sharpness is uncommon. This is somewhat similar to above, siman 199, regarding zimun, where the Gemara says that a minor who knows to Whom we recite a berachah may be counted for zimun, but the Rosh and Tur ruled that nowadays we do not count him.
