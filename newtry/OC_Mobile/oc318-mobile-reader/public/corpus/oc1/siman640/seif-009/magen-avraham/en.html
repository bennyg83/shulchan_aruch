The guards of the city, etc. They must go around the city, as appears in the Gemara; therefore it does not ask that they should make a sukkah there. In the Yerushalmi it says that this is specifically regarding guards against troops, but guards of property are like guards of gardens and orchards. Avdoma would sleep inside a store and asked Rabbi Chiyya, and he told him to sleep inside the sukkah. Those who sit in a store, although they are usually accustomed to eat there by day most times, nevertheless on Sukkos they must eat in the sukkah, similar to guards by day, who would need to make a sukkah there. So too they are obligated to make a sukkah there. Even if they live outside the city and their stores are in the city, they are obligated to make a sukkah there in the city, because there is his home. In the Yerushalmi, Rav Huna was traveling on the road and was thirsty, and he did not want to drink until he entered a sukkah.
