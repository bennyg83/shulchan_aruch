(46) The guards of the city. This means guards against troops, so they will not come upon the city. They must go around the city to guard it, and therefore they cannot make a sukkah in one place. This applies specifically to guards against troops. But guards of property, to prevent theft, whose way is to guard constantly, are like guards of gardens and orchards explained below; this is from the Magen Avraham in the name of the Yerushalmi. In truth, this matter depends on the nature of the guarding. For example, our store guards, whose way is to guard at night, are exempt only at night. Those who sit in stores, although during the rest of the year they often regularly eat in the store by day, nevertheless on Sukkos they are obligated to eat in a sukkah. Even if they live outside the city and their stores are inside the city, they must make a sukkah for themselves in order to eat when they need to, if they cannot go to their homes and eat; this is from the Acharonim.
