9) [Seif 2] Who is like a five-year-old or a six-year-old. Each child according to his sharpness. Levush; Taz, s.k. 2; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 3; Chayei Adam, klal 147, sec. 25. That is, if the child is sharp, from age five and above; if he is not sharp, from age six and above. The Bach wrote that it means a child who completed five years and entered his sixth year when his father is in the city, and a child who completed six years when his father is not in the city, both cases referring to one who is not sharp; see there. So too wrote Eliyah Rabbah, sec. 3. If he is sharp, however, even a four-year-old is not drawn after his mother. Eliyah Rabbah, ibid. If his father is not in the city throughout the year, then even if he comes home for the festival of Sukkos, since the child is accustomed to being with his mother, the measure is from six years and above. Bikkurei Yaakov; Mishna Berurah, Shaar HaTziyun, sec. 5. See above, siman 414, sec. 10.
