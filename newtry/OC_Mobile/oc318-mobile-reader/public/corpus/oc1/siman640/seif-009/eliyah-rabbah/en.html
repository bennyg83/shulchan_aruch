[9] One whose candles went out, etc. It appears to me that on the first night too, he must go to his friend's sukkah even if there is exertion, for this is no better than one who is distressed. Based on this, it appears to me to resolve what the Magen Avraham left with a need for analysis at the beginning of siman 639 regarding the Kenesses HaGedolah, who wrote that after the fact, under compulsion and pressing circumstances, one may rely on crossing by boat to go to a sukkah. The Magen Avraham wondered: is he not exempt from going to his friend's sukkah? In my humble opinion, it speaks of the first night. It also appears to me possible that there it speaks of his own sukkah, except that it is impossible to cross there except by boat. The Derishah wrote: from this I proved that one may also build a special sukkah for himself on Chol HaMoed when necessary, and he need not bring his meal to the other sukkah under the sechach, etc. Because of darkness; but on top of the sechach, even on a weekday, it is forbidden unless it is evident that he intends to protect, for then even on Yom Tov it is permitted, since there is no empty space between it and the sechach. See the end of siman 629; this is from the Magen Avraham.
