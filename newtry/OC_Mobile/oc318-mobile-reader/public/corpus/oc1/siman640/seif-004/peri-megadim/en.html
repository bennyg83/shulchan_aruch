But. See Turei Zahav, as above in siman 627, se'if 4, and siman 629, se'if 19, and see Magen Avraham here, letter 8; with Hashem's help it will be explained there. To protect from the candles so they should not go out is like decoration or protection, and within four tefachim of the sechach it is permitted according to the Turei Zahav, as above; see there. See siman 315: whenever it is not for permitting, one may make a temporary partition from the side, but for the purpose of permitting it is forbidden. Regarding an ohel, even where it is not for permitting, a temporary ohel is forbidden on Shabbos and Yom Tov.
