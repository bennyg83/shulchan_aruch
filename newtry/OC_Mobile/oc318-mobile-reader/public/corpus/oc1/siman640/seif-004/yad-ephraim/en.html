In Magen Avraham, subparagraph 6, 'in a place where there is cold' is how it was emended in Eshel Avraham. But in old books the text is 'in a place where there is no cold,' and this is primary. For the matter of one who is distressed with sleep is already explained in the Shulchan Aruch: because of the wind or flies. The difficulty is that above in siman 639 it is explained that because of cold the custom is to be lenient regarding sleep. If so, what does it matter that he made it in a place where he cannot sleep because of wind, since in any case there is no obligation to sleep and be distressed by cold? To this the Magen Avraham wrote that it speaks of a place where there is no cold and he is obligated to sleep, but he made it in a place where he will not be able to sleep because of the wind, etc.; then he does not fulfill. This is simple.
