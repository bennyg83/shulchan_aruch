(On the Mechaber, se'if 4, in the gloss) To go to his friend's sukkah. Note: this means at the time of eating. But it is obvious that even one who has no place in his courtyard to build a sukkah must find a place to build a sukkah for himself, and then that is considered his home, just as all year people commonly move from house to house and then that is their home. This is unlike those who err and say they are not obligated to build a sukkah elsewhere, where it is not their dwelling.
