The smell. In all these cases he is exempt even from eating; this is from the Beis Yosef. If there are mosquitoes, it is better to sleep in a canopy, even if it has a roof and is ten tefachim high, than to sleep outside the sukkah. Gemara, page 26.
At all. Therefore one should protest against those who make sukkos in streets where it is impossible to sleep because of fear of thieves; this is from the Levush. But if he does not fear for his life, and fears only that his utensils will be stolen, he fulfills his obligation, since he can bring them into his house at night; Responsa Rema, siman 28, Darchei Moshe. Exertion. Likewise, one whose sukkah has rain dripping into it from trees: if he can go to his friend's sukkah without exertion, he should go; if it involves exertion, he should eat in his house. Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 29.
