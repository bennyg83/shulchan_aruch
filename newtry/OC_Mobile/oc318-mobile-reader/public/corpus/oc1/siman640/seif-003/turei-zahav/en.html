One who bloodlets is obligated in sukkah. In Hagahos Ashiri he concluded there that he is not sick; on the contrary, he is happy and increases eating and drinking. Further, he should not have bloodlet on the festival but before or afterward. These are his words. It seems to me from this that it speaks only of one who bloodlets merely to preserve health and feels no illness. But if he feels ill and there is some chill, it is obvious that he is exempt from sukkah. Even regarding a cooked dish that spoils because of cold, the Levush wrote that one is exempt from sukkah, all the more so where there is bodily harm because of cold.
