[3] Levush: Each according to, etc. So too the Turei Zahav and Magen Avraham explained what the Shulchan Aruch wrote, 'at five and at six,' that each child is according to his sharpness. I am astonished, for this is the language of the Rambam in chapter 6, and the Kesef Mishneh explained: at five when his father is in the city, and at six when his father is not in the city. This implies even if he is not sharp, for if he is sharp, even at four he is not drawn after his mother, as written at the end of siman 414 and as explained in Eruvin 82. With this, everything the Turei Zahav wrote in explaining the Tur is rejected, and it also does not fit the sugya there; examine carefully, for I have been brief.
