(Subparagraph 6) Or sleeping. He explains: in a place where there is no cold. This means that the Rema's words are the words of the Mordechai, and above in siman 639, se'if 4, he brought in the name of the Mordechai that people are not careful to sleep in the sukkah. If so, why should the sukkah be invalid when it is not fit for sleeping? To this he wrote that the Mordechai's reason above in siman 639 is that in cold places one is distressed by the cold if he sleeps there. If so, it is still called fit for sleeping, meaning if he had pillows and cushions, as the Magen Avraham wrote there in subparagraph 7. According to the other reason the Rema wrote there to be lenient about sleeping, see the Magen Avraham there, subparagraph 8.
