[6] Levush: Or the cold, etc. In Russia it is common for it to be cold on Sukkos. It appears that any person who is chilled is exempt, and all the more so poor people who do not have garments to protect from the cold, who are exempt when they save themselves from the cold by leaving the sukkah; this is from the Bayis Chadash. A delicate person may leave even because of wind that drops leaves into the food; this is from the Talmud.
