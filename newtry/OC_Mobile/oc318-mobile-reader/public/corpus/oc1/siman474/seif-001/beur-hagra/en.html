(ב) And the custom, etc. — per the Rif's opinion; and he learned from what is stated there 109b, four cups, etc., and it has the law of consulting regarding pairs, and the same applies regarding blessing; and he brought proof from what is stated in Chullin chapter 6:2, from the students of Rav, etc., "now thus," etc., and the same, that it is not possible to recite and drink together; and Ra'avad rejected his proof — only regarding something that is a leave-taking do we say thus, as in covering the blood; and proof from what is stated 102a, companions who were, etc.; and Tosafos there s.v. ve'akru, and in Chullin there in Tosafos, and further that if he wished to drink he drinks, as stated between the cups, etc.; and in Milchamot and Ran they settled his words, that once one began the Haggadah or "Who redeemed us," it is forbidden to drink; and Tosafos 103b s.v. Rav, etc. And he holds that even if it is not a matter of leave-taking, it interrupts — only a fixed matter of obligation, or before beginning the matter, such as "remove, bless," and chat between prayer, etc.; but after beginning, and in a discretionary matter, it is not an interruption, as stated in Hallel and Megillah one interrupts; therefore there is no proof from companions, etc. And that one does not bless borei peri hagafen on maror, as R' Yehuda's opinion, that the blessing of haMotzi exempts it.

(ג) But blessing, etc. — for distraction of attention does not establish a blessing after it, as stated in chapter Arvei Pesachim "I shall consult," and so he explained what is stated there: since you said "here," etc., until they bless borei peri hagafen, and one need not cover the blood; and in Chullin 107b, and the sun blesses, etc., and one need not a blessing after it; and Tosafos there chapter 7:2, and in Arvei Pesachim 101b s.v. when they leave, etc.; and even for the Rif and Rambam, who explain there that one needs a retroactive blessing specifically when uprooting place, but not in one place.
