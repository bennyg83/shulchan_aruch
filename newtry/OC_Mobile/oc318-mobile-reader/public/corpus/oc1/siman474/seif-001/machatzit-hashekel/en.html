(s.k. 1, a) On every etc. Even though etc.; that is to say, this is the view of the Rif, and he gave two reasons: one, since each cup is a mitzvah on its own, therefore one must recite the blessing over each and every cup; and further, that the Haggadah constitutes an interruption. And the Rosh rejected the proof from the Haggadah based on that of siman 179, that if one stood up and prayed in the middle it does not constitute an interruption; the same applies to the reciting of the Haggadah. Therefore Magen Avraham wrote that nevertheless, because of the first reason, that each one is a mitzvah on its own, Beit Yosef ruled that one must recite [the blessing] over every cup.
Even though the Rosh also rejected the first reason; see there.
