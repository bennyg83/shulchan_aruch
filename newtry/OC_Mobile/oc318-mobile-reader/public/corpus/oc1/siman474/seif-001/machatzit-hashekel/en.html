(א) On every, etc. — even though "nevertheless" — meaning, this is the Rif's opinion, and he gave two reasons: one, since each cup is a commandment by itself, therefore one must bless on every cup and cup; and further, that the Haggadah is an interruption; and the Rosh rejected the proof from the Haggadah, from this in siman 479, that if one stood and prayed in the middle it is not an interruption — the same is true of reciting the Haggadah; therefore Magen Avraham wrote that nevertheless, because of reason one, that each is a commandment by itself, R' Yehuda ben Lakish ruled one must bless on every cup.

(ב) Even though the Rosh rejected also the first reason, see there.
