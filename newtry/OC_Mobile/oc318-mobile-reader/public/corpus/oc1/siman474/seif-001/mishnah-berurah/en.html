(א) Neither the first blessing — and even though the four cups that the Sages enacted, each one is a commandment by itself, nevertheless, since there is no distraction of attention from drinking — for behold, when he blessed on cup one he knew he would drink another cup — therefore he need not bless on every cup and cup.

(ב) And on the cup of Birkat Hamazon — and the fourth cup is discharged by the blessing of the third cup.

(ג) Al hagefen only after the fourth cup — and even though Birkat Hamazon is a conclusion and leave-taking and is considered a complete interruption regarding the first blessing of the third cup, nevertheless regarding the final blessing he does not lose anything, for distraction of attention does not obligate blessing the final blessing immediately; and he can wait until he drinks all the cups, and then discharge with the final blessing all four cups together.

(ד) On every cup — for once one began the Haggadah it is forbidden to drink, and therefore it is an interruption requiring blessing on the second cup; and even if we say this does not interrupt regarding blessing (and such is the case in siman 478 section 6), nevertheless, since each one is a commandment by itself, one should bless on every cup a blessing by itself.

(ה) But the final blessing — for distraction of attention does not establish a blessing after it, as above.
