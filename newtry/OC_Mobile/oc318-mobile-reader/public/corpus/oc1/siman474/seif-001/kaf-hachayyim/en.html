1) [Se'if 1] One drinks the second cup and does not recite a blessing over it, etc. Tur cites a dispute. According to Rif, one must recite Borei Peri HaGafen, because each of the four cups is an independent mitzvah and therefore requires its own blessing. Rav Natrunai, Rav Amram, Rambam, and Raavyah likewise require a blessing over every cup. Rosh, however, writes that Rif's ruling does not appear correct. Although every cup is an independent mitzvah, there is no diversion of attention. Therefore, Borei Peri HaGafen is recited only over the first cup, which is Kiddush, and the third cup, which accompanies Birkas HaMazon, but not over the second or fourth. Rav Kohen Tzedek writes likewise. Beis Yosef writes that the custom is to recite Borei Peri HaGafen only over the cup of Kiddush and the cup of Birkas HaMazon. These are his words, and this is his ruling here in the Shulchan Aruch. This is the Sephardic custom.
