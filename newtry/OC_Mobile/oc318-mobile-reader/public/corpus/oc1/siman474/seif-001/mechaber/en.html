Regarding the second cup one does not bless. Containing 1 section:
One drinks the second cup and does not bless on it — neither the first blessing nor the final blessing — for one does not recite borei peri hagafen except on the cup of kiddush and on the cup of Birkat Hamazon; and one does not recite al hagefen except after the fourth cup.And the custom among Ashkenazim is to recite the first blessing on every cup and cup; but the final blessing one recites only after the last one alone; and so is the opinion of most of the Geonim.:
