And the custom among Ashkenazim, etc. It is true that this is the opinion of Rav Sherira and Rav Hai in Tur, and "this requires further study" — for in truth this contradicts this, that if one blesses borei peri hagafen on every cup, we must rule that Haggadah and Hallel are an interruption, as R' Yehuda's opinion in siman 473; and from that one need not a final blessing after the first cups, we learn they are connected to the meal; and Haggadah is not an interruption, as Rashbam. And it is simple that for this reason Tur truly wrote, and Rama agreed to their words regarding final blessing, that one blesses only after the last — end of his words; it implies — but not regarding the first, for borei peri hagafen on every cup, for the reason I mentioned. And it appears to me the Geonim's reason: certainly it is not an interruption; and nevertheless one needs borei peri hagafen for each one, since the Sages enacted four cups — it is like a gift, meaning: at the time of blessing he does not intend to drink because of this blessing but this cup alone; since he needs another cup, he is obligated to bless on it, as above. But it is difficult: why did he write above not to eat at the first dipping more than less than a kezayit, and Rama wrote nothing on this — it implies thus people practice; but even if he eats a kezayit he need not a final blessing, since Haggadah is not an interruption; and one may say nevertheless it is preferable to fulfill R' Yehuda's opinion; and that we do not rule as him regarding final blessing — that is to be lenient in doubt of blessings.
