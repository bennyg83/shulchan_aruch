And the custom — Taz in Taanit 8, Taz 8, opinion of R' Yehuda and Rashbam and siman 174, Magen Avraham 9: wine before the meal is considered as entering during the meal, unlike first dipping which is doubtful. And what Taz wrote that each cup of mitzvah is independent — so too Magen Avraham and Eshel Avraham. And if so, good to intend the blessing of hagafen on kiddush not to take out a second cup; but if he intended thus in kiddush to exempt the second cup from hagafen — requires study; and one may say he should not bless hagafen on the second cup because of doubtful blessing to be lenient even the first blessing, for even though forbidden to benefit without blessing, this too is rabbinic and you shall not take one transgression. One who has no order of Haggadah but has a chumash should say the sections mentioned in it — Yom Tov, Chayei Adam, Taanit 8, and Rama 34, and Taz end of siman. One who has no Haggadah and cannot say Haggadah — nevertheless must drink four cups — Pesachim 108b: if he drank them one after another, by wine he fulfilled by four cups he did not fulfill — see Rashi there and Taanit 62b and by wine he fulfilled — one may say simchat Yom Tov, Or Sameach perek 7 halacha 9 from matzah and braita there that drank live fulfilled in one act — nevertheless one may say he should drink four cups for remembrance; and possible where compelled in Haggadah he fulfills by four cups but certainly hagafen blessing he blesses only on kiddush and on the cup of birkat hamazon alone, and Orach Chayim 473 and Avodat Yisrael. One who has only one of two first cups should not bless final blessing at all — birkat hamazon exempts — siman 174 seif 10 and 174 — and implies specifically two cups before eating; and Magen Avraham taf peh gimmel wrote two cups — one for kiddush and one for birkat hamazon and he blesses final blessing on birkat hamazon cup.

(ב) That one does not bless borei peri hagafen on the four cups and on the Haggadah — see in decisors resolutions on this; Or Zarua 473, Levi Deah; and elsewhere we wrote for R' Levi Deah: thought is like speech — it is like nullification of chametz; and Shema too one does not bless borei peri hagafen, for blessings do not apply to Shema; and Megillah — publicizing certainly not by thought alone one fulfills; and if so, it will be explained in Laws of Megillah; and Hallel was enacted among many.
