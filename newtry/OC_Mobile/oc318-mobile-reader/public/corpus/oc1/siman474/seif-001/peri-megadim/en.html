(א) And the custom — Or Zarua in 473, Taz in 473 chapter 8, opinion of R' Yehuda and Rashbam and siman 474; and Magen Avraham 9: a waiter who comes before the meal to open the food is considered as entering within the meal, which is not so for one dipping — it is doubtful. And what Taz wrote, that each cup is a commandment by itself — so too Magen Avraham and Beit Yosef. And if so, it is good to intend the borei peri hagafen of kiddush not to discharge the second cup; but if he intended thus in kiddush to discharge cup two from borei peri hagafen — "this requires further study"; and one may say he should not bless borei peri hagafen on cup two, for doubt in blessing is to be lenient, even the first blessing, for even though it is forbidden to benefit without a blessing, that too is rabbinic and not "you shall not take Heaven's name in vain," and see Chayei Adam. One who has no Haggadah order and has a Chumash should say the portions mentioned in it — see Chayei Adam, 473, and R' Levi Deah, and Or Zarua 474. And one who has no Haggadah and cannot say Haggadah — nevertheless he should drink four cups; see Pesachim 108b, if he drank them in a reclining position he has fulfilled the obligation of wine, he has not fulfilled the obligation of four cups; and see Rashi there and 472:62; and if he fulfilled wine — one may say joy of the Festival; and Arvei Pesachim chapter 7 halacha 9 from matzah and braita there, if he drank them alive he has fulfilled in a reclining position — not in one reclining posture; nevertheless one may say he should drink four cups for remembrance; and it is possible where he is compelled in Haggadah that he fulfills the obligation of four cups; but certainly the blessing of borei peri hagafen he blesses only on kiddush and on the cup of Birkat Hamazon alone; and Eliyah Rabbah in 4, and Bach: one who has only two cups — the first two — he does not bless any final blessing at all, for Birkat Hamazon discharges, siman 472 section 10 and 474; and it implies specifically two cups before eating; and Magen Avraham in Laws of Passover wrote: two cups — he takes one for kiddush and one for Birkat Hamazon, and he blesses the final blessing al hagefen on the cup of Birkat Hamazon.

(ב) That one does not bless borei peri hagafen on the four cups and on the Haggadah — see in decisors resolutions on this; Or Zarua 473, Levi Deah; and elsewhere we wrote for R' Levi Deah: thought is like speech — it is like nullification of chametz; and Shema too one does not bless borei peri hagafen, for blessings do not apply to Shema; and Megillah — publicizing certainly not by thought alone one fulfills; and if so, it will be explained in Laws of Megillah; and Hallel was enacted among many.
