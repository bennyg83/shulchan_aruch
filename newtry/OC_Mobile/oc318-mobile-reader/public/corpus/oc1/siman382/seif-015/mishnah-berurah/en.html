(50) If each has, etc.—meaning neither may use the room designated for his fellow—and comparable to what the Mechaber wrote above seif 13 that designated place is not like hired worker and gleaner and he cannot rent for the master:

(51) In this manner, etc.—meaning in separate rooms; but if they dwell in partnership in the whole house or even divided in the whole house except one room both may use—they need rent only from one as seif 13; many poskim disagree with Rama's words and per them even if they use all rooms together Israelite is not hired worker and gleaner and must rent from the gentile specifically; and similarly for two gentiles they must rent from both; the reason the Sages did not enact his hand as the master's hand except for hired worker and gleaner who follow him—but not partners who equally forgot in the house and neither is subordinate to the other:
