(45) Permitted until end of time—and the same if the hired worker or gleaner died—so Chayei Adam and Shulchan Aruch:
(46) Once they removed him it was nullified—for in unspecified rental he can retract after the first Shabbat whenever he wishes as seif 6; therefore such rental has no power only while he is hired worker and not afterward:
(47) From the king's treasurer—and even if he takes payment for himself—for presumably the king does not care [Magen Avraham]; and AhS is lenient also for hired worker and gleaner—see there; and the later ones wrote it is not specific to treasurer but any small royal servant is no worse than hired worker and gleaner—see below siman 391 s.k. 18 in Mishna Berurah what we wrote there:
(48) And was removed entirely, etc.—therefore lekhatchila be careful not to rent unspecified from the city official or his ministers only for a time—for sometimes he is removed and rental is nullified; many later ones agreed one can rent for a long time but lekhatchila good not to rent more than twenty years:
