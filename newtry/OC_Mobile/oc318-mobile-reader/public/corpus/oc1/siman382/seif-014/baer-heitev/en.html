From the treasurer. Even if the treasurer takes the payment for himself—for the king does not care about this. And every small servant of his is no worse than hired worker and gleaner—Ritva Orach Chayyim siman 54. Magen Avraham. And Chacham Tzvi wrote in teshuvah siman 6: that which we say the king does not care is specifically for a year or two—for presumably the king's way is not to care about such a time; but about fifty years the king's way is to care; and even though all the time he is his hired worker and gleaner we are not concerned for his care while he is still his hired worker—for even where he does not wish to rent, his hired worker rents as stated while he is still his hired worker; but that his rental should help after his hired worker passes and with care—not; and even if you say this is not specific when the king is alive but when the king died—even though the treasurer lives, all is nullified unless the new king appointed him too—end quote see there:
