They rented from his hired worker and gleaner for a time—even though they removed him within the time from being his hired worker and gleaner—they are permitted until the end of the time; but if they rented from him without specification, once they removed him the rental was nullified:': The same applies if they rented from the king's treasurer and he was removed entirely from the king; but if they removed him only from the treasury and he still eats the king's portion—he is still called his hired worker and gleaner and they can rent from him; therefore the first rental too still stands (Beit Yosef in the name of responsa Rashba):.
