If they rented from his hired worker or laborer for a specified time — even if he dismissed him as his hired worker or laborer within that time — they are permitted until the time expires. But if they rented from him without specification, once he dismissed him the rental is voided. {Rama: Rema: The same applies if they rented from the king's treasurer and he was completely dismissed from the king's service. But if he was only removed from the treasury position yet still receives the king's provisions, he is still considered a hired worker or laborer and one may rent from him, and therefore the first rental also remains valid.}
