(14) And need not rent from the gentile—know Beit Yosef at end of the siman brought another opinion in the name of Mordechai that it is not permitted to carry via doorway or even via window if it is below ten—and if he wishes to carry he should carry via window above ten or via the wall from above for his use is not pleasant and they did not decree on this; and Mordechai added also in this manner it is permitted only when each courtyard's doorway opens to another alley—for they do not forbid one another in the alleyway; but if their doorways open to one alleyway where they forbid one another in the alleyway until they rent his domain [and such as two courtyards of Israelites]—in this it is forbidden to carry courtyard to courtyard in all manners even via wall above—end quote [per Beit Yosef's explanation which is primary—see Mordechai well and as Beit Meir wrote]; and Shulchan Aruch here held Rosh's view that even via window below ten or even via doorway it is permitted since they have no regular foot traffic one upon the other—and we need not be concerned for Mordechai's words; and so Etzai Almogim—this law is simple and per him Mordechai also agrees here—see there at length; however in the second particular Mordechai added that where doorways open to one alleyway it is worse—Shulchan Aruch did not distinguish the opposite; and though the Mechaber did not distinguish, from Gra's words s.k. 8 that via alleyway is forbidden—see there—it is proved Shulchan Aruch deals where they forbid one another in the alleyway and nevertheless the Mechaber permitted via window or doorway; and nevertheless for law it appears one should be stringent at least to carry via window below ten and all the more via doorway since Bach [and AhS copied] and Kaf HaChayyim lean to Mordechai's view even for window above ten—see there. And know that per the Mechaber who ruled like Rosh that a gentile does not forbid via window since he has no foot traffic on Israel—even if the gentile's courtyard separates two Israelite courtyards, if Israelites wish to carry from one to another via the gentile's courtyard they may via eruv they make between them even though the gentile's courtyard divides in the middle—for since he does not forbid them his dwelling is as if it is not—Beit Yosef explained explicitly Mahariv's words at the beginning of the siman [brought in Magen Avraham siman 390] and though it is not compelled we may rely to be lenient—see Beit Meir and Etzai Almogim in this siman]:
