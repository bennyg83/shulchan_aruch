If the gentile has no regular foot traffic upon the Israelites he does not forbid—such as two courtyards open to one another and they have no regular foot traffic one upon the other; and in one courtyard a gentile dwells and in one courtyard two Israelites or more dwell—they may bring in and take out from this courtyard to that courtyard on Shabbat via the window between them, and they need not rent from the gentile:
