(40) In order to take out his vessels, etc., to the courtyard—it appears this is specifically for this—not to make eruv with him; but to carry from his house to his fellow's house it clearly helps; and in the law—for the concern is only lest Israel rely on this and dwell in a gentile's place and not that he be permitted to carry in the courtyard and be found in the courtyard—unlike if carrying were permitted only in the house he would still fear dwelling alone in a gentile's place; and so Rashba ruled explicitly in Avodat HaKodesh; and so from Rashi s.v. forbidden to make; but from Ritva it is proved in all cases they decreed and even to his fellow's house forbidden via his window; and so Ma'amar Mordechai from his own reasoning—and requires study for law. And know further in Avodat HaKodesh it is explicit that at least if both rent from the gentile and make eruv between them carrying is permitted in all manners house to house and house to courtyard—for at least they were not more stringent here than when two Israelites dwell with a gentile—they required only rental and did not be stringent that rental not help so he not dwell in a gentile's place; but from Tosafos s.v. alleyway for one answer there [from what they wrote and even though the gentile forbids them they can rent] it is proved that now they decreed not to assist an individual dwelling in a gentile's place—even in rental it is forbidden; and so Ritva there in one answer; however in Sefer Gaon Yaakov agreed from reasoning to permit in rental:
