Cannot make eruv. The reason it is forbidden to make an individual in a gentile's place lest he learn from his deeds—therefore these do not make eruv with him nor use with him in the alleyway so he be alone and fear the gentile lest he kill him and leave; and Peri Yehoshua specifically mentioned windows—for when they do not make eruv together the Israelite trembles and does not dwell with him; when they make eruv together he does not tremble—therefore forbidden to make eruv; but via doorways—even without eruv it is common they dwell and do not tremble since there is a doorway between them—it appears to him as dwelling with him in his house—it is explicitly permitted to make eruv; Rashi does not distinguish and even via doorways forbidden to make eruv—for what reason he leaves the dwelling; and in Tur there is a typo Rashi in place of R' Yitzchak and R' Yitzchak in place of Rashi—and Beit Yosef and Maggid did not notice this typo: a gentile's dwelling is like an animal's dwelling and an Israelite dwelling there is permitted to bring in and take out; and if an Israelite lodged with him the master does not forbid the guest nor the guest the master (Teshuvah Hashalem and Beit Yosef):
