(23) As long as the gentile does not retract—it deals when they rented unspecified without stating time; if rented for known time when time passes they must rent again in any case as seif 7; before time passes the gentile cannot retract in any manner:

(24) Even for a long time—since no time was stated in rental it helps as long as the gentile does not explicitly retract; but if the gentile wishes he can retract immediately after the first Shabbat and forbids him—for having rented only unspecified he can say I intended only one Shabbat:

(25) Until he returns the money—meaning what we said that after the first Shabbat the gentile can retract is specifically when he returns money; otherwise the Israelite can say I intended to rent for many Shabbatot and gave payment accordingly; the Sages were lenient in a gentile's place he cannot forbid him; many dispute this; per their words the gentile can retract after the first Shabbat even without returning money—for since he did not specify for how many Shabbatot he can say he intended only one Shabbat:
