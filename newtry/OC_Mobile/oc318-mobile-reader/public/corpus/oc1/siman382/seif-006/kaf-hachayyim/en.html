And they prohibit carrying for one another, etc. This excludes brothers who receive their sustenance from their father and sleep in their own houses, and those other cases stated in siman 370. Although they reside in separate houses, they are considered one because they do not prohibit carrying for one another. Rambam, chapter 5, halachah 16, likewise writes this. Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham, subsection 2. The later authorities write likewise.
