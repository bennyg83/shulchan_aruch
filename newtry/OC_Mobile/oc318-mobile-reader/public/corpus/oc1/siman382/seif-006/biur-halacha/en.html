(23) As long as the gentile does not retract, etc.—there is great confusion among the commentators on this matter; the clearest among them is Bach and AhS [as many later ones stand in their view] that when they rented for a fixed time he cannot retract mid-term as proved from seif 14 and so Temim De'im before the time he cannot retract even if he returns money—for he rented once and the Sages made this a complete rental and what good that he wishes to return money; and though they hung their words on Rashi who wrote if he rented from him he cannot retract since he took money—already the later ones [AhS, Beit Meir, Etzai Almogim, Chemed Moshe] wrote from this there is no proof—for Rashi's intent in writing "took money" is not because money is in his hand but because he took payment and the domain was acquired for the lessee. And you are correct—it is so: Bertinoro writes for he took money; and mostly he copies Rashi; and so Rabbeinu Yehonatan wrote in this—and see there it is reversed from what appears there that he cannot retract from rental when he received payment—and as is known he too mostly copies Rashi in his commentary; and why should we equate dispute among the great ones; and Magen Avraham's view is likewise that for fixed rental returning money does not help—and as AhS wrote Mishna Berurah challenged Taz on this and it is the opposite of Magen Avraham's plain words—and already Beit Meir, Etzai Almogim, and others protested Tosafot Shabbos; nevertheless for unspecified rental the poskim hold after the first Shabbat he can retract—for the gentile can say I did not rent you except for one Shabbat; however as long as he does not retract explicitly the rental stands in force—and this is what Shulchan Aruch deals with:
