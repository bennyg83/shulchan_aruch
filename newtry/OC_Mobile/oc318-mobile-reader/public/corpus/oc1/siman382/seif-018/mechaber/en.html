A gentile who rented his house to another gentile—if any possession remained for him in the' house that he has permission to place any vessels there—they can rent from him; and if no possession remained for' him—if the lessor can remove the lessee within his time they can rent from him, and if not it does not help unless they rent from the lessee:The same applies to one' person who has possession in the house—they can rent from him, for he is no worse than hired worker and gleaner (Beit Yosef in the name of Rivash siman 357):.
