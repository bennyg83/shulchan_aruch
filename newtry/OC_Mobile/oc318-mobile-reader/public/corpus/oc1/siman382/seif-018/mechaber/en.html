A non-Jew who rented his house to another non-Jew — if the owner retains any hold in the house such that he has the right to place any items there, they may rent from him. If he retains no hold at all: if the landlord can evict the tenant before the rental period expires, they may rent from him; if not, it is not effective unless they rent from the tenant. {Rama: Rema: Similarly, any individual who has a hold in the house may be rented from, since he is no worse than a hired worker or laborer.}
