(60) That he has permission, etc.—even if he did not place anything there; the reason he is no worse than the gentile's hired worker explained seif 11—since he has permission to use the house they rent from this lessor too:

(61) To remove the lessee within his time—such as they conditioned he can remove him whenever he wishes:

(62) Can rent from him—even if he did not yet remove him—for he still retained right in his courtyard; and it is obvious all the more they can rent from the lessee for the whole house is now his; and Beit Yosef wrote even if the lessee is present in his house if they wish to rent from the lessor they rent:

(63) And the same another person, etc.—meaning do not say specifically from the lessor this helps because he retained right in the courtyard—it teaches also another person helps since he has at least permission to use there:
