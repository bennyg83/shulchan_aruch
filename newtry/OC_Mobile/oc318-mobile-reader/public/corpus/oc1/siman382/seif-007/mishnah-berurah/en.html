(26) When time ends they must, etc.—even if the gentile does not retract—for automatically the first rental is nullified; and it is obvious even if the gentile says he agrees for the future it does not help until he rents again. Taz wrote if they rented from a gentile and he died leaving an heir what they rented from the deceased does not help—for rental power is nullified—for even in life he could retract, etc.—end quote; and this is per his view above seif 6 that even within time he can retract; but per what the later ones agreed there he cannot retract within time at all—then even if he died rental is not nullified even if the heir protests; and so AhS and many others; however for unspecified rental and death it is reasonable per Taz's words rental is nullified automatically; and even if the heir says he agrees to what the father did—it appears it does not help until he rents anew:

(27) And must return and make eruv—for since rental was nullified eruv is nullified too—we do not say when they rent from the gentile the first eruv returns and revives:
