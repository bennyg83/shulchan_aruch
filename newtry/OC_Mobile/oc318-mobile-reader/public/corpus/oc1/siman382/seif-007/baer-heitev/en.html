It appears to me that if they rent from a gentile and he dies and leaves an heir, what they rented from the deceased does not help—for then the rental is nullified and they must return and rent from the heir—Taz; and Taz wrote thus in siman 391 s.k. 3—see there and see siman 383. And so ruled in teshuvah Chacham Tzvi siman 6 like him but not for his reason. And so it appears to me is primary, and not as written in teshuvah Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 7 that one need not return and rent from the heir. And what he wrote there that so ruled Maharimat in Teshuvah Aharonot—there is no proof to say that if he died one need not return and rent from an heir.
