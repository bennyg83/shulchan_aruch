If he designated a place, etc. Above siman 380 seif 2 Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Maharik that we are not concerned for a designated corner—here we need he have permission in the whole house; therefore we need he have permission in the whole house; and even if he placed vessels there—as long as he has no permission in the whole house they do not rent from him—for the Sages did not distinguish unlike there where we need vessels actually even in a designated corner it suffices—so Maharik; and Beit Yosef wrote this designation means he explicitly prevented use of the rest of the house; but if he gave one room unspecified it is not considered designation—and Tur's language does not imply thus; and it appears Beit Yosef retracted and did not fix thus in Shulchan Aruch:
