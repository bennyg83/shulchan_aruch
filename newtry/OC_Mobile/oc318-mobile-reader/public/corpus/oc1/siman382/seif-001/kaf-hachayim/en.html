(1) [Seif 1] Dwells with gentile in courtyard does not forbid, etc.—a gentile's dwelling does not forbid; the Sages decreed lest he learn from his deeds; in one not common to dwell they feared lest he kill—for suspected of bloodshed they did not decree; but with more than one not afraid—they dwell with gentile; gentile will not wish to rent fearing witchcraft and Israel leaves.
