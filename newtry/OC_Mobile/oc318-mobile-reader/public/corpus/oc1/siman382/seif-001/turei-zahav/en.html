But two Israelites in one house are considered as one even if they do not eat at one table; and this matter of two Israelites is rabbinic—for they feared lest an Israelite dwelling with a gentile learn from his deeds; they sought to prevent Israelite dwelling with a gentile—therefore they said he forbids him so his dwelling will be burdensome and he leave; and from this reason nullifying domain from the gentile does not help until they rent—for he will not wish to rent and thereby the Israelite leaves and it is not common for one Israelite to dwell with a gentile lest he kill him—therefore they needed to forbid only when two Israelites dwell with him:
