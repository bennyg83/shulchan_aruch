Two courtyards one inside the other — a Jew and non-Jew in the inner one and a Jew in the outer one; or a Jew and non-Jew in the outer one and a Jew in the inner one; and even a non-Jew in the inner one and two Jews in the outer one — the non-Jew restricts. Some say that even a non-Jew in the inner one and one Jew in the outer one restricts.
