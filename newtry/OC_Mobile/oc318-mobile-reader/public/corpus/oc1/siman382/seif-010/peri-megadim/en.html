It is sufficient. See Taz. His words imply that when the first owner sold the city to a second owner, the Jewish residents rent from the second. Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 9, writes that according to Ramban, siman 17, and Levush, the reason is that the first owner sold the second every right he possessed. Since the Jewish residents had already rented for a fixed period, the first owner could not retract so long as he had not returned the money, and the same applies to the second owner. This would apply even to a sale; see there. The practical halachah requires study. If they rented without specifying a period, it appears that according to all opinions, even where the property was merely leased to another person, they must rent again from the second person. Tosefes Shabbos, seif katan 10, writes likewise; see there.
