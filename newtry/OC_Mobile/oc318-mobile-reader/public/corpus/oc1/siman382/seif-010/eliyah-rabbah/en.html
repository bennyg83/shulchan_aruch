[10] [Levush] As long as he did not return, etc. It implies even if they rented for a time he can retract when he returns money—and so Taz wrote; and it appears he need only return money remaining in his hand for the length of time he rented from him; but Magen Avraham wrote plainly he cannot retract at all when rented for a time; and so Bach; and so Nachalat Tzvi; and so Ramban's teshuvah siman 27; and so is primary. Taz wrote renting from Samaritans and he died leaving an heir—the rental is nullified for even in his lifetime he could retract if he wished; proof from eruv who died in siman 371—end quote. And it appears to my humble opinion the heir does not inherit the right already given another as above; and further Ra'osh wrote beginning of chapter Hader for leniency we say rental is like eruv not for stringency; and so Mordechai there and it is from the Gemara; and in any case for fixed rental and death it appears to me it is not nullified:
