(31) Against his will, etc.—meaning they cannot give his rental to his domain to leave it before him on the table [even a gentile who was accustomed until now to rent his domain to him] and say to him: here is your rental in exchange for your domain that we have domain to carry in your courtyard for a year as before; and even though regarding eruv there is an opinion at end of siman 367 that in eruv if he is accustomed to make eruv and now does not wish, they take from him against his will—here it is different, for we do not compare a gentile to an Israelite regarding coercion in a matter of mitzvah:
