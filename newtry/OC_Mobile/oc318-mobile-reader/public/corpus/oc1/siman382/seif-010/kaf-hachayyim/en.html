10) There. Gloss: A Jewish person who rented, etc. This applies even if the Jewish owner retains no right of use there. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1. If the Jewish owner retains a right of use in the non-Jew's house, some authorities maintain that rental is unnecessary in any event, as stated below in se'if 12. See there. This applies specifically when the Jewish person rented or lent the property to the non-Jew. If the property was sold to the non-Jew, the non-Jew certainly prohibits carrying.
