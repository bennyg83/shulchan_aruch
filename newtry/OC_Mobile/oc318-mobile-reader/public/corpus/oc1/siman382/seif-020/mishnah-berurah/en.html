(70) Where Israel and gentile dwell—meaning their house doorways open to the courtyard:
(71) Eruv via windows—meaning eruv was effected via windows between them; and all the more if doorways between them:
(72) To take out, etc., via windows—and the same via doorway if between them:
(73) Behold they are forbidden—lest one think since they made eruv together via windows or doorways between them they are considered one master dwelling in the gentile's courtyard and he does not forbid taking house to house even via courtyard air—it teaches:
(74) House to house via doorways—via courtyard air; but house to house via doorway between houses is permitted through eruv for all [Gra]:
(75) Have designated houses—meaning two or more Israelites each has a designated eating room; if they ate in one place even many are considered as one and do not forbid:
(76) From the gentile ship owner—and as wherever there are designated houses and shared courtyard they need eruv and rental; explained in Beit Yosef that renting from the ship owner does not help unless they rent before he divided places for each gentile—for then ship domain is still his alone and he can rent; but after dividing to other lessees renting from him does not help—they must rent from each gentile; and a gentile unless the ship owner can place his vessels anywhere renting from him alone suffices:
(77) Or to include it—meaning even if they did not rent afterward but when renting their place they told him this rental also rents his domain—it helps and ship fare rental also counts for domain rental:
