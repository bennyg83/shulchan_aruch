A courtyard in which a Jew and non-Jew reside, where windows open from one Jew's house to another Jew's house and they made an eruv through the windows — even though they are permitted to carry from house to house through the windows, they are forbidden to carry from house to house through the doors on account of the non-Jew, until they rent from him.Rema: A ship with many Jews on it who have designated quarters must make an eruv and rent domain from the non-Jewish ship owner, or have it included in the ship fare. See above Siman 366, section 2.
