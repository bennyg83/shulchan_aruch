In Shulchan Aruch seif 16 "rental of gentile," etc.—appears typo; should read "and hired worker of gentile"—first explained five hired workers in gentile's house in upper rooms—if their own houses each would need eruv as siman 381; one might think since one stands in gentile's place and gives eruv for him if one forgot others forbid and alley members—forbidden; teaches hired worker and gleaner not considered dwelling; Beit Yosef: if courtyard members rented from gentile even if hired workers and gleaners in his houses and all did not contribute to eruv they do not forbid at all—not considered dwelling only guests; so Beit Yosef and Levush; in Derech Mitzvosecha Beit Yosef name if rented from gentile need not rent from hired workers; appears to me this is unnecessary mishna—only Beit Yosef that one need not give eruv for one who did not rent—for all who did not rent must give eruv on his behalf; now that they rented from him need not give eruv at all:
