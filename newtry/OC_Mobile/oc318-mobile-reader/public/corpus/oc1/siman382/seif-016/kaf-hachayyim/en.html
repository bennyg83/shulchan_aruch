Renting the house is ineffective, etc. One should not think it sufficient for this Jewish person merely to contribute a share to the eruv, so that the situation resembles five people residing in a courtyard, where one person may rent the non-Jew's rights on behalf of everyone, as stated in se'if 9. The gloss teaches that this is not so. A separate rental is required for purposes of the eruv because the non-Jew resides there. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1. Ma'amar Mordechai, there, writes likewise.
