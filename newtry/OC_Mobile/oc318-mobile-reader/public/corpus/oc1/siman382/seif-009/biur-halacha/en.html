(30) On behalf of all—from this language it appears they made eruv together first and afterward one becomes agent for all—and so somewhat from Rashi 66a s.v. mah m'arve; but in truth this is not as Rambam, Semag, and Avodat HaKodesh explain—they rent first then make eruv; and even if one rents it helps for all—for at least domain was rented to one Israelite and therefore afterward they can join in eruv together; and on the contrary lekhatchila one should not make eruv before rental as we wrote in Biur Halacha at the beginning of the siman s.v. they must rent—see there:
