If the city contains breaches, the alleyway must first be rectified, after which the rights may be rented from the non-Jew. Nechalas Tzvi, end of siman 399; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1. Responsa Sefer Yehoshua, end of siman 8, however, writes that this was not the accepted practice. Nowadays, when the rights are purchased, it may be said that, on the contrary, subsequently installing a tzuras hapesach constitutes taking possession. See there. Responsa Rivash, siman 427, however, appears to agree with Nechalas Tzvi even where the rights are rented. See there. Orchos Chaim, subsection 4, cites both opinions.
