It appears to me that even if the Jewish lessor does not live in this courtyard but rented the courtyard's houses to other Jewish people and to a non-Jew, the non-Jew nevertheless does not prohibit carrying. Here too we say that the Jewish owner did not rent a house to the non-Jew with the intent that the non-Jew prohibit carrying for the Jewish residents. Chayei Adam, rule 75, subsection 9. Birkei Yosef, subsection 1, however, writes that Rashba's words imply somewhat that this applies specifically in a courtyard where the Jewish lessor resides. If he does not reside in the courtyard, perhaps he does not care whether the non-Jew prohibits carrying, and the matter can be debated. Chemed Moshe in siman 384 likewise writes that if the Jewish owner of the dwelling does not reside there, the non-Jew prohibits carrying. See there. The later authorities write that one should rule stringently, since even without this issue some authorities question the underlying ruling, as stated by Even HaOzer.
