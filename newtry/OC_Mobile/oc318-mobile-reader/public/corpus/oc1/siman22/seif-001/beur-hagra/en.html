Se'if 1, "one buys," etc. Tosefta, last chapter of Berachos: one who makes tzitzis for himself says "Blessed… Who has brought us to this time." There it also teaches: one who makes tefillin for himself says "Blessed… Who has brought us to this time." Likewise Rambam, chapter 11 of Hilchot Berachot: every mitzvah that comes from time to time — such as shofar, sukkah, lulav, megillah, and Chanukah; and every mitzvah that is one's acquisition — such as tzitzis, tefillin, mezuzah, and ma'akeh; and a mitzvah that is not frequent and not found at all times, for it resembles a mitzvah from time to time — such as one's son's milah and pidyon haben — one blesses Shehecheyanu at the time of performing it. In the first entry he learned from sukkah, lulav, megillah, and Chanukah, as stated in chapter 4 of Sukkah, chapter 2 of Shabbat, and chapter 3 of Megillah. In the second entry he learned from the above Tosefta. In the third entry he learned from pidyon haben at the end of Pesachim, and said it is from time to time, as Eruvin 40b; and Tosafos disagree, who wrote in Sukkah 46a, s.v. ha'oseh, that when making tzitzis and tefillin one does not bless. They proved from Menachot 42b: for making, one does not bless upon them — since R' Yochanan did not list them, likewise Shehecheyanu — therefore one does not bless, since he did not list them; and likewise milah — one does not bless, since he did not list it at the end of chapter 19 of Shabbat. They disagree with the above later authorities, who wrote they instituted only for mitzvos that have joy — see there. He holds the above Tosefta is before our Gemara. See Tosafos Menachot 42b, s.v. ve'elu, and the Tosefta, etc. — their suggestion: the Tosefta supports the Gemara that he listed only Shehecheyanu, not for making; as Sukkah there, s.v. hakodem, s.v. ha'oseh — specifically, etc., for he holds one does not bless for making, etc. And although Shehecheyanu was not mentioned in the Gemara, the Tosefta said one blesses — this is because of new vessels, already mentioned in Berachos. This is what Rav Sherira Gaon wrote — meaning, the Tosefta's reason is as above. See Tosafos Sukkah cited, where Rav Sherira Gaon wrote, etc. — only in this they contradict each other: in Menachot there they wrote he knew they asked, etc., not because he did not list it — if so, the whole structure of Tosafos Sukkah falls, who proved one does not bless Shehecheyanu upon them. According to the Tosefta, one also blesses on tefillin, as the Tosefta states explicitly — both according to Tosafos and according to the Rambam; but according to Tosafos Sukkah one does not bless upon them, as Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch here. See Yoreh De'ah siman 265 in the Tur — the Rambam wrote the father blesses Shehecheyanu at every milah; Beit Yosef wrote he should not bless; likewise Rabbeinu Yerucham — these are Tosafos' words and the above Beit Yosef. In Shulchan Aruch there he wrote the Rambam's words, and in the gloss Tosafos and Beit Yosef; but in the gloss siman 28, se'if 2, he wrote like the Rambam. The Rambam's words are primary — Tosafos' proof is not compelling, as above; and all the more so to reject what is explicit in the Tosefta. Likewise Beit Yosef, Yoreh De'ah siman 265, in the name of Rashba: by law one should bless, like pidyon haben — only Hallel is difficult, as Tosafos Sukkah there; their answer is insufficient, for Hallel has joy — one must say the reason is Hallel is not a mitzvah in itself, as Aruch HaShulchan — perhaps Israel makes their Passover and takes their lulav, etc. Tur here holds like the Rambam, who wrote the Tosefta's wording letter for letter. Beit Yosef asked: why did the Tur not write tefillin too? Accordingly Mahari Weil and Beit Yosef wrote: the Tur holds like Beit Yosef — but it is not so; Shehecheyanu one blesses only for one who makes for himself, as Rambam there; likewise Tosafos and Rashi in Sukkah there; as the Gemara there, "one who makes… for himself"; likewise the Tosefta there — for all mitzvos, for oneself; likewise Tur here. This does not apply to tefillin, for it is usual that the scribe makes them, as Chullin 9a, s.v. ve'idach, etc., as stated, etc. For tzitzis one must say as Rashi explained there.
