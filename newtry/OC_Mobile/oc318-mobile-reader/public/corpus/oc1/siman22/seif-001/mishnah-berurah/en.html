(1) One buys a tallis, etc. — One should not ask: on new vessels one must bless at acquisition — why did he write "and makes tzitzis on it"? One may answer: specifically when one buys a garment fit to wear as it is, one blesses immediately; but if one bought cloth to make garments, one does not bless even after finishing, only at wearing — for we require a special moment: either acquisition or wearing. Here it is not fit to wear immediately, for one must attach tzitzis to it; therefore one cannot bless at acquisition. And to satisfy also those who hold one must bless Shehecheyanu on performing the mitzvah the first time — therefore one blesses when making the tzitzis and does not wait until wearing.

(2) "For it is no less," etc. — But one does not bless Shehecheyanu on the mitzvah itself, since it does not come from time to time; accordingly, if one made tzitzis on a garment one already had, even if it never had tzitzis, one need not bless Shehecheyanu. The same if one made tefillin for oneself — one does not bless Shehecheyanu. See Biur Halacha.

(3) At the time of wrapping — first the blessing "to wrap oneself," and then Shehecheyanu (Pri Megadim and Derech HaChayim).
