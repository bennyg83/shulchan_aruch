And makes tzitzis on it. This is a Tosefta that Beit Yosef brought, and Beit Yosef asked: why did he not write thus regarding tefillin? The Rambam indeed wrote so regarding tefillin too. He answered in the name of Mahari Weil: the Tur holds one does not bless Shehecheyanu except on a mitzvah that comes from time to time, as implied in Hilchot Pesach siman 432. What he wrote regarding tzitzis here means: because he bought a tallis — as one who acquires new vessels; and likewise in the name of Bach. Therefore he wrote here "one buys a tallis, makes on it," etc. But the Tur's wording does not indicate this — he mentioned only tzitzis, not tallis. It seems the Tur's intent is that we know automatically regarding tefillin too — for in the Tosefta also he mentioned only tzitzis, for this reason, and it precedes the mitzvah of tefillin. Maharach Or Zarua brought in the name of Rashal, who wrote on the Tur's words that we do not practice thus; it seems because he holds Tosafos in chapter Lulav and Aravah plainly hold not to bless neither on tzitzis nor tefillin, and safek berachos lehakel. But it appears also on new vessels one should not bless, as Tosafos wrote stated in the name of Rav Sherira Gaon — we do not rely on "one who sees" who blesses on new vessels, for we infer in chapter "In all they intercalate" that it requires something that comes from time to time; but pidyon haben is difficult. Halacha was already decided below that one blesses on new vessels, because Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Terumat Hadeshen that it is like from time to time. Nevertheless, both for tzitzis and tefillin one should bless the first time one blesses upon them Shehecheyanu, as Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 28 regarding covering blood; and from this, for every mitzvah a person performs the first time, one blesses Shehecheyanu, as above.
