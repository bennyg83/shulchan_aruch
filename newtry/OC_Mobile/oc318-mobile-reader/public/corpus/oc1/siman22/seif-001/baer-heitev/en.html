New vessels. But one does not bless Shehecheyanu on the mitzvah itself, since it does not come from time to time; accordingly, if one made tzitzis on an old garment that one had already bought and blessed Shehecheyanu on it, one does not bless again. The same applies to tefillin — one does not bless Shehecheyanu; therefore he wrote "one buys a tallis and makes tzitzis on it," etc. One could ask: on new vessels one must bless at the time of acquisition — why did he write "and makes tzitzis on it"? One may answer: specifically when one buys a garment as it is fit to wear, then one blesses Shehecheyanu; but if one bought cloth to make garments from it, one blesses at the time of wearing; and here, since one makes tzitzis on it, one blesses incidentally at the time of performing the mitzvah. This is the view of Beit Yosef, Bach, Rashal, Shach, and Magen Avraham. But the Rambam holds that because of the mitzvah one renews in it, one blesses Shehecheyanu — therefore he wrote tzitzis and tefillin — see there. Olas Tamid wrote one should practice per the Rambam; and likewise Taz — both for tzitzis and tefillin one blesses the first time one blesses upon them Shehecheyanu, as Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 28 regarding covering blood and milah: for every mitzvah a person performs for the first time, one blesses Shehecheyanu — see there. See Yoreh De'ah there, se'if katan 2, at length. See Sefer Bnei Chayya and responsum Chavot Yair siman 237.

First wrapping. Meaning: Shehecheyanu first, and then "to wrap oneself" (Bach, Olas Tamid).
