Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1 And here, since he makes tzitzis on it — meaning: here, since it is not fit to wear at acquisition because tzitzis were not yet made on it, he does not bless Shehecheyanu then. Accordingly, per the Rambam there would be a distinction: if one buys a tallis by day, one does not bless until making tzitzis, since it is not fit to wear; if one buys at night, one blesses immediately, for it is fit to wear without tzitzis at night, since day garments at night are exempt.
