(1) [Se'if 1] One buys a tallis and makes tzitzis on it, he blesses Shehecheyanu, etc. Magen Avraham asked, se'if katan 1 — on new vessels one must bless at acquisition, as siman 223, se'if 4; why did he write "and makes tzitzis on it"? One must say: it speaks when he did not bless at acquisition; or specifically when one buys a garment as it is fit to wear, then one blesses Shehecheyanu; but if one bought cloth and made garments, one blesses at wearing, as siman 551, se'if 2. Here, since one makes tzitzis on it, one blesses incidentally at performing the mitzvah. Yehaveh Da'at brought olive shoots, letter 1; and likewise Raz, letter 1 (see Pri Megadim, Orach Chayim, letter 1); and Magen Avraham himself in siman 223, se'if katan 6. However Taz there, se'if katan 3, disagrees and holds we bless Shehecheyanu at acquiring the garment, even if one did not buy a garment fit to wear as is but must give it to a tailor — see there; see Shach page 32, side d. In my humble opinion, here is different: since a prohibition rests upon it — it is forbidden to wear a four-cornered garment without tzitzis — therefore they said one should not bless until making tzitzis, when the prohibition ceases and one may wear it. See below, letter 6.
