Se'if katan 1, "new vessels," etc. But it is difficult, etc. — as siman 551: on the second night of Rosh Hashanah there is dispute among poskim whether one says Shehecheyanu, since the two days of Rosh Hashanah are like one long day, and he already said Shehecheyanu on the first night; and although the halacha follows those who bless Shehecheyanu on the second night, nevertheless to remove himself from dispute, lechatchilah one wears a new garment at kiddush and blesses Shehecheyanu because of the garment, and is exempt from Shehecheyanu of kiddush. This is difficult: on a garment one should bless at acquisition! They answered: it speaks of a garment that needed repair after acquisition — in such a case one blesses at wearing — see there in Magen Avraham.

And here, since, etc. Meaning: why does he bless when fixing the tzitzis? He should wait until the time of wearing!

He blesses incidentally, etc. For otherwise there are poskim who bless Shehecheyanu on the mitzvah — therefore he advances the blessing.
