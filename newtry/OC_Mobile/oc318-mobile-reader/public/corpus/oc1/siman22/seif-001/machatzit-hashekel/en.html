Seif katan 1: From new utensils, etc. Yet there is a difficulty, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 600. On the second night of Rosh Hashanah there is a dispute among the poskim whether Shehecheyanu should be recited, since the two days of Rosh Hashanah are considered one extended day and he already recited Shehecheyanu on the first night. Although the essential halachah follows the poskim who require Shehecheyanu on the second night, one should initially wear a new garment during Kiddush and recite Shehecheyanu because of the garment, thereby also exempting the Shehecheyanu of Kiddush. This raises a difficulty: the blessing over the garment should be recited at the time of purchase. They answer that the case concerns a garment that required alteration after purchase, in which case the blessing is recited at the time of wearing, as Magen Avraham explains there.
And here, because, etc. Meaning, why does he recite the blessing when preparing the tzitzis? He should seemingly wait until the time he wears the garment.
He recites the blessing incidentally, etc. Since some poskim maintain independently that Shehecheyanu is recited over the mitzvah, he therefore advances the blessing and recites it at this time.
