New vessels. But one does not bless Shehecheyanu on the mitzvah itself, since it does not come from time to time. Nevertheless it requires investigation: in Yoreh De'ah siman 28 the Rama gloss writes one blesses Shehecheyanu on covering blood; and siman 265, se'if 7, he wrote one blesses Shehecheyanu at milah and pidyon haben — one must say those are not so common. See Chavos Yair, who wrote somewhat on this. But he asks: on new vessels one must bless at acquisition, as siman 223, se'if 4 — why did he write "and makes tzitzis on it"? One must say: it speaks when he did not bless at acquisition; or specifically when one buys a garment as it is fit to wear, then one blesses Shehecheyanu; but if one bought cloth and made garments, one blesses at wearing, as siman 551, se'if 2. Here, since one makes tzitzis on it, one blesses incidentally at the time of performing the mitzvah.
