From new utensils. He does not recite Shehecheyanu over the mitzvah itself, since it does not come only at fixed intervals. Nevertheless, this requires study. In Yoreh Deah, siman 28, Rama writes that one recites Shehecheyanu over covering the blood, and in siman 265, seif 7, he writes that one recites Shehecheyanu over circumcision and redemption of the firstborn. One must answer that these mitzvos are not so common. See Chochmas Shlomo, who writes somewhat concerning this. There is also a difficulty: the blessing over new utensils should be recited at the time of purchase, as stated in siman 223, seif 4. Why, then, does the text say, “and he made tzitzis for it”? One must answer that the case concerns one who did not recite the blessing at the time of purchase. Alternatively, when one purchases a finished garment ready to wear, he recites Shehecheyanu at the time of purchase. If he purchases fabric and has garments made from it, however, he recites it at the time of wearing, as stated in siman 600, seif 2. Here, because he is attaching tzitzis to it, he recites the blessing incidentally at the time of performing the mitzvah.
