1) [Seif 1] If the first day of Sukkos falls on Shabbos, etc. See above, siman 487, seif 1, where he wrote that if the first day of Pesach falls on Shabbos, we do not recite the blessing Me'ein Sheva. Therefore, it was necessary to teach us this regarding the festival of Sukkos, to say that one cannot apply here the verbal analogy of 'the fifteenth, the fifteenth' from the festival of matzos, because Pesach is different. Ruach Chaim. Regarding the reason that one concludes it with Shabbos alone, see tractate Shabbos 24b and our words above, siman 268, sec. 45. See further our words on siman 487, sec. 22. Regarding the other laws of prayer, see there and siman 491.
