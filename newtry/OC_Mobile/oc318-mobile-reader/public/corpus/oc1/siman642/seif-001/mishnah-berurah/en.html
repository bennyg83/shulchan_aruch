(1) When it occurs on Shabbos. On the first night the Maaravis piyyutim are not recited, because people are not accustomed to them and the Sages were concerned that one might take a lamp in order to look at the text. On the second night, the Maaravis of the first day are recited. The same applies when the seventh day of Pesach occurs on Shabbos. On Motzei Shabbos the Maaravis belonging to the seventh day are recited. Likewise, on Shemini Atzeres the Maaravis of Shemini Atzeres are recited. The exception is the first and second nights of Pesach, when on the second night the Maaravis belonging to the second night are recited because it is the first night of the Omer count, as stated by Peri Megadim. Bameh Madlikin is not recited.
(2) Only. This prayer was instituted only for Shabbos. The Tur writes the words, “with love, a sacred convocation,” while our customary text omits the words “with love.”
