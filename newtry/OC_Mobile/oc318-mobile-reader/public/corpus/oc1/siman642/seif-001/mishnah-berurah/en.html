(א) To be on Shabbat — then they do not say ma'ariv on the first night, since people are not accustomed to them they feared lest one take candles to see by them; and on the second night they say the ma'ariv of the first day; the same when the seventh day of Passover falls on Shabbat they say on motzaei Shabbat the ma'ariv belonging to the seventh day; the same on Shmini Atzeret they say the ma'ariv of Shmini Atzeret alone; for nights one and two of Passover they say on the second night the ma'ariv belonging to the second night because of the bechor for counting [Peri Megadim]. They do not say "With what do we light."

(ב) Only — that it was instituted only for Shabbat alone. Tur wrote "with love" sacred text; and in our customs it is written and one does not say "with love."
