Seif 1. If the first day falls on Shabbat etc. — it appears what they read as haftarah on Shabbat chol ha'moed from the wars of Gog and Magog is because the essence of the war of Gog and Magog will be through the sukkah as Chazal said that the Holy One will give them the mitzvah of sukkah; and Chazal said as written "He who sits in heaven laughs" — there is no laughter before the Holy One except on that day alone; and behold this verse "He who sits in heaven laughs" deals with the wars of Gog and Magog as Chazal said in Berakhot; if so the fall of Gog and Magog will be through the sukkah.
