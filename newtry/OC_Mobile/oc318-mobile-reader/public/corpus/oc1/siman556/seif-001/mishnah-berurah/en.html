(א) When he sees the candle, etc. — for this does not require a cup and there is no element of pleasure as there is in spices. And he should bless on the light before they read Eichah, for in Eichah it is written "He seated me in darkness" — therefore he blesses first on the light and also so he benefits afterward from its light when they read Eichah; and if he forgot to bless he blesses afterward at night since the obligation is still upon him:

(ב) And on the night of motza'ei, etc. — and on the night of Tisha B'Av he makes havdalah in tefillah even though he cannot then make havdalah on the cup; and if he erred then and did not make havdalah in tefillah he need not return since he will make havdalah on the cup at motza'ei Tisha B'Av. And women who do not know how to pray and did not make havdalah on motza'ei Shabbat in tefillah — every man must warn them that on the fast day they should not kindle fire to cook for the night's need until they say "Who separates between sacred and profane":

(ג) (c) distinguishes the glass, and I will tell you that the difference from yesterday, and even forgot yesterday to distinguish from the dark, is now seen to distinguish from the darkness that has already passed through time and snow in what will now distinguish the glass. I will show you that you are allowed to distinguish the wine and hear that it is permissible to drink itself and to give it to the baby
