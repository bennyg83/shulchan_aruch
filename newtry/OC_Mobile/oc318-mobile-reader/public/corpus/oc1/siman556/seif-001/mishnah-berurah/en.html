When he sees the candle, etc. For this does not require a cup, and there is no [issue of] pleasure in this as there is with spices. And he should recite the blessing over the light before they read Eichah, for in Eichah it is written, "He has set me in dark places"; and therefore he should first recite the blessing over the luminaries, and also so that he will afterwards benefit from its light when they read Eichah. And if he forgot to recite the blessing, he should recite it afterwards at night, since the obligation is still upon him.
And on the night of Motzaei, etc. And on the night of Tisha B'Av he makes Havdalah in the prayer, even though he cannot then make Havdalah over the cup. And if he erred then and did not make Havdalah in the prayer, he need not repeat, since he will make Havdalah over the cup on Motzaei Tisha B'Av. And as for women who do not know how to pray and did not make Havdalah on Motzaei Shabbos in the prayer: every man must caution them that on the day of the fast they should not make a fire to cook for the needs of the night until they say "Who distinguishes between holy and mundane."
He makes Havdalah over the cup. And he need not make Havdalah in the prayer, since he made Havdalah yesterday. And even if he forgot yesterday to make Havdalah in the prayer, it also appears that he need not now make Havdalah in the prayer, for its time has already passed, and it suffices that he now make Havdalah over the cup (Shaarei Teshuvah). It is stated in the Maharil that when it grew dark he recited Borei Pri HaGafen and Havdalah; if so, it is implied that it is permitted to make Havdalah over wine, and it is implied that it is permitted for him to drink it himself, and he need not give it to a child (Dagul Merevavah).
And he does not recite the blessing, neither over the candle. Even if he did not recite the blessing over the candle on Motzaei Shabbos: for they recite the blessing over the candle only on Motzaei Shabbos, at the time when it [fire] was created.
Nor over the spices. For [that blessing] is because of the additional soul, and it too is specifically on Motzaei Shabbos, as above in siman 299, se'if 5.
