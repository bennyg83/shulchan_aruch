The night of Tisha B'Av that falls on Sunday, etc. Mordechai at the beginning of Moed Katan writes that the Rosh would say aloud without a cup borei me'orei ha'eish; and so wrote Hagahot Maimon and Avudraham and Beit Yosef, and in our customs; and they make havdalah in tefillah even though they do not make havdalah on the cup (Mordechai in hagahot laws of Tisha B'Av).
