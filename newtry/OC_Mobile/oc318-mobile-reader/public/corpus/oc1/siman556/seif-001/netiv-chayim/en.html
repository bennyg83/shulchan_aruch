(Magen Avraham at the beginning) It implies etc. from minchah etc. — note: and so in siman 553 in his name — see there. But it is not so, for certainly the Ramban holds that even if he accepted the fast upon himself it is permitted to eat except for minchah time which is d'oraisa; and so the Tur explicitly in siman 553 in the name of the Ramban, and so it is in Milchamot in the first chapter of Taanit; and here the reason is since he separated, if he eats afterward it is a blessing in vain like at minchah time if he said the time. But without havdalah it is permitted to eat afterward and he eats the interrupting meal afterward; and what he brought proof from the language of Beit Yosef siman 593 is incidentally — he did not examine, for the Ramban in Torat haAdam did not write thus, only to explain the Rif's words and he does not hold so.
