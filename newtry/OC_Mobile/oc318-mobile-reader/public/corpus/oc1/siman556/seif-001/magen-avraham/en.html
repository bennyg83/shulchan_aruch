(א) The reason they do not make havdalah from the afternoon onward as is written in siman 553: since he made havdalah he accepted Tisha B'Av upon himself and it is forbidden to drink from the cup (Tur, Bahag, Ramban); if so it implies explicitly that from the afternoon onward the Ramban agrees — if he accepted the fast upon himself it is forbidden to eat, for it is the next day itself; and so Beit Yosef in siman 553 in his name. And do not give to a child to drink, for they decreed lest he come to tie himself up to drink even after he grows — this is called a fixed matter since per the year's arrangement it falls on motza'ei Shabbat; not so with circumcision on Tisha B'Av when they give to a child (Tur siman 559). And distinguish: a matter we do for ourselves — but a matter we do for the child they did not decree, lest he come to tie himself up; therefore they give him to eat and drink (Ran first chapter of Shabbos). See in Yoreh De'ah siman 341 seif 2 and siman 396 in Taz. On spices: even though it is permitted to smell on a fast day — for even tasting some permit as is written in siman 567 that only eating and drinking are forbidden — nevertheless since spices come on motza'ei Shabbat for pleasure to restore the extra soul, it is not proper to do so on Tisha B'Av; and so in Shach in Yoreh De'ah siman 265 unlike what is written there in Shulchan Aruch; and see what I wrote regarding Yom Kippur:

(ב) And on the night of motza'ei Tisha B'Av. And even per the view that one makes havdalah only on the first day as is written in siman 559 seif 4 — here since it is not possible to make havdalah he agrees (Rosh, Bahag). And one who fasts two days should hear havdalah on the night of the tenth from others and should not wait until the third night to make havdalah himself, for any approximation of havdalah to Shabbat is preferable (Matteh Moshe).
