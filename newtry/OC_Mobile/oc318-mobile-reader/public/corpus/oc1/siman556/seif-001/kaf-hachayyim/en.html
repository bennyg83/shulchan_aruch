1) [Seif 1] The night of Tisha B’Av that falls on Sunday, etc. One recites Havdalah in the Amidah as on every Motzei Shabbos, as stated below in siman 559, seif 1, in the gloss, see there. Women who do not know how to pray and recite Havdalah in the Amidah must be warned not to kindle a fire during the day in order to cook for the night until they hear Havdalah on Motzei Tisha B’Av, or else not to do so until they say, “Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe, Who distinguishes between sacred and mundane.” Shelah, 200a; Eliyah Rabbah, entry 1. However, what he wrote that they should say, “Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe,” meaning that they should recite this blessing with Hashem’s Name and kingship, follows the view of the Rif, Rosh, and Halachos Gedolos. But Maran and the Rama, of blessed memory, ruled above in siman 299, seif 10, in accordance with Rashi’s view, that this should be said without Hashem’s Name and kingship because of the principle of uncertainty regarding blessings, as explained in our words there, entry 56, see there. So too wrote the later authorities. Children are not blessed when Tisha B’Av falls on Motzei Shabbos. Derashos Maharil, Laws of Tisha B’Av, Order of Prayer; Magen Avraham, siman 559, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah there, entry 11; Ateres Zekeinim. See above siman 554, seif 20, and our words there, entry 92, and below siman 559, entry 18.
