He says borei me'orei ha'eish. For this does not require a cup and there is no element of pleasure as there is in spices; and Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Avudraham that one should bless on the light before they read Eichah. And it appears to me the reason: in Eichah it is written "He seated me in darkness" — therefore he blesses first on the light; and nevertheless it appears that if he forgot he may bless afterward at night since the obligation is still upon him:
