A. The night of etc., when he sees thus. As it is written in the fourth chapter of Pesachim: and once he sees [the light], he recites the blessing immediately; and in a case of this sort, everyone agrees.
And he does not etc. As it is written at the end of siman 555: and he should lessen etc.
And on the night of etc. As it is written concerning one who did not make Havdalah on Motzaei Shabbos: he makes Havdalah throughout the entire week; and even according to the one who holds [that this is only for] the entire day, in a place where it is not possible he agrees. Bahag. And such is the view of the Rif, and as written in siman 299.
And he does not etc. As it is written there: but not over the light; and likewise [not] over spices, for [that blessing is] because of the additional soul, and specifically on Motzaei Shabbos.
