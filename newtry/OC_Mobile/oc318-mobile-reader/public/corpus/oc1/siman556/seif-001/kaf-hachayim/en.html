(א) [seif 1] The night of Tisha B'Av that falls on Sunday, etc., and he makes havdalah in tefillah like other motza'ei Shabbat as stated below siman 559 seif 1 in the gloss — see there. And women who do not know how to pray to make havdalah in tefillah must be warned that by day they should not kindle fire to cook for the night's need until they hear havdalah at motza'ei Tisha B'Av, or that they should not do so until they say: Blessed are You, the Omnipresent our God, King of the universe, Who separates between holy and profane. Shlah daf 200a. Rabbi Akiva Eiger letter 1; however what he wrote that they should say Blessed are You, etc. means they should say this blessing with the Name and sovereignty — this is per Rif and Rosh and Bahag; but Maran and Moram z"l above siman 559 seif 10 ruled like Rashi to say this without Name and sovereignty because of danger, as explained in our words there letter 56 — see there. And so the later authorities. And one does not bless the children on Tisha B'Av that falls on motza'ei Shabbat. Derashot Maharil section Tisha B'Av in order of prayer. Magen Avraham siman 559 s.k. 1. Rabbi Akiva Eiger there letter 11. Taz. And see above siman 554 seif 20 and in our words there letter 92, and below siman 559 letter 18.
