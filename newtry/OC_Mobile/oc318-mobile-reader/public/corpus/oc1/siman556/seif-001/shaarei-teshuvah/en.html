Havdalah — see Ba'er Hetev. And R' Yonah wrote in the name of responsum of Knesset HaGedolah part 2: a sick person who eats on Tisha B'Av should make havdalah immediately on motza'ei Shabbat; and Beit Yosef there — his view leans somewhat that he can also take out his household members in this havdalah — see there. And see in Gan HaMelech siman 244 that his view is that one does not make havdalah on the cup on motza'ei Tisha B'Av l'chatchila except if he forgot to make havdalah in tefillah on the night of Tisha B'Av (and it appears to me that also on motza'ei Tisha B'Av he did not make havdalah in tefillah — for otherwise this would suffice for him); and in such a case it is better that he make havdalah on the cup than that he return to pray eighteen blessings — see there; and the custom is not so but as is written in Shulchan Aruch to make havdalah on motza'ei Tisha B'Av on the cup. And one does not make havdalah on motza'ei Tisha B'Av in tefillah at all since he made havdalah yesterday in tefillah; and it appears that even if he forgot yesterday to make havdalah in tefillah he need not make havdalah now in tefillah at the change of time and he should not interrupt now in tefillah — and what they make havdalah now on the cup suffices. And see in Gan HaMelech who disagrees with Knesset HaGedolah who permits smelling on Tisha B'Av — and he wrote it is forbidden because of pleasure, etc.; and from what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Levush it implies specifically on motza'ei Shabbat it is forbidden for it is pleasure to restore the extra soul, but on other days not — see there:
