The Mechaber cited in order "not only this but also that" — meaning not only in a vessel where it is obvious it is forbidden, for the observer will say it was heated today on Shabbat, since a vessel's way is not to preserve heat so much, and he will come to permit heating water on Shabbat; but even in the ground, such as bathtubs of bathhouses whose way is to preserve heat a full day and more — nevertheless the Sages forbade on account of the decree.
