5) There: Whether the water is in a utensil or in the ground. The author proceeds from the less novel to the more novel case. Water in a utensil is plainly forbidden, because an observer may say it was heated that Shabbos, since a utensil does not ordinarily retain heat for so long, and may consequently come to permit heating water on Shabbos. Even water in the ground, however, such as in a bathhouse pool that ordinarily retains its heat for an entire day or longer, was nevertheless prohibited by the Sages as a decree. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 2.
