(א) Some say one must be careful, etc. — see in Magen Avraham that according to him even to hold his hands in a place where the water cannot heat so much until yad soledes bo — also forbidden according to this view; for where yad soledes bo applies, even per the first view it is forbidden. And behold in the gemara it states: she smeared her hand with oil and warmed it facing the hearth; and the reason is that in oil the decree of washing does not apply; nevertheless it must deal that she does not bring her hand so close until the oil can heat well enough for yad soledes bo measure; and all this is fine for Magen Avraham's explanation; but for the other explanation in Beit Yosef that Rosh's reason is on account of the heat of the water itself — if so Rosh somewhat contradicts his own words in what he brought afterward, words of Rabban Gamliel; and it should have been explained at least that Rabban Gamliel does not deal in this manner:
