9) There, gloss: Or other limbs, etc. Mishnah Berurah, siman 5, writes: It appears to me that women do not customarily begin wearing white garments on Shabbos or Yom Tov. Perhaps this is because they do not know how to take proper care regarding washing, distinguishing between hot water heated before Shabbos or Yom Tov and water heated on Shabbos or Yom Tov. Where no custom exists, it may be permitted, provided she does not wash with a garment, lest she come to squeeze it. These are his words.
