M) [Seif 2] But if the place is roofed — forbidden, etc. Because its heat increases and he comes to sweat; and they also decreed on sweat in a bathhouse (as below seif 12) independently even without washing, lest he come to heat on Shabbat; and they decreated on sweat of Tiberias hot springs on account of sweat of other bathhouses. And some hold that in sweat too they decreed only on fire-heated water, but on Tiberias they did not decree; and even if the place is roofed — permitted to wash and sweat in Tiberias, for they did not decree so extensively — it would be a decree upon a decree.
