The laws of bathing on Shabbat. And in it 13
seifim: It is forbidden to bathe the entire body, even each limb separately, even in water that was heated erev Shabbat, whether in a vessel or on the ground, and even to pour water over one's body and rinse — it is forbidden. But it is permitted to wash in them one's face, hands, and feet.Or other limbs, whatever is not washing the entire body [Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh chapter Tinokot] — and this is regarding hot spring waters; but in Tiberias hot springs it is permitted to bathe even the entire body together, and needless to say in cold water. And that which it is permitted in Tiberias hot springs — specifically on the ground; but in a vessel it is forbidden, lest it be confused with hot spring waters.] והני מילי בחמי האור אבל בחמי טבריא מותר לרחוץ אפילו כל גופו יחד ואין צריך לומר בצוננין והא דשרי בחמי טבריא דוקא בקרקע אבל בכלי לא דאתי לאיחלופי בחמי האור:
