The laws of bathing on Shabbos. It contains 13 seifim: It is forbidden to wash one's entire body, even limb by limb separately, even with water that was heated before Shabbos, whether the water is in a vessel or in the ground. It is even forbidden to pour the water over one's body and wash. But one may wash his face, hands, and feet with it. {Rama: Or other limbs, provided that he does not wash his entire body. [Beit Yosef in the name of the Rosh, chapter Tinokot]} These restrictions apply to water heated by fire. With the hot springs of Tiberias, however, it is permitted to wash even one's entire body at once, and certainly in cold water. The permission regarding the hot springs of Tiberias applies specifically when the water is in the ground, but not when it is in a vessel, lest it be confused with water heated by fire.
