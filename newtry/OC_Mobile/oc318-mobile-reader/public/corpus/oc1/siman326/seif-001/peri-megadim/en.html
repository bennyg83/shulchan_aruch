Or Taz on Shabbat siman 40 — and his face, hands, and feet: the manner of speech is that a person washes every day to honor his Maker; and from what was taught "his entire body — limb by limb" — the minority of the body is permitted, and his other limbs. And at first glance to rinse by pouring — limb by limb alone is permitted, as taught according to Shmuel: one washes his face, hands, and feet, not his entire body; and washing where one inserts his limbs into a vessel — that is rinsing, which is forbidden for the entire body according to Abbaye; see there. And in Rambam chapter 22 halacha 2: but his face, hands, and feet are permitted; and he did not write "washes" in them to say that limb by limb of the entire body — even rinsing is forbidden:
