Baer Heitev; and see Shiyurei Berachah siman 12, who wrote that many women immerse — let them not say "and may their sins be on their bones"; and whoever has the ability to protest this, etc. And I saw that my fathers and rabbinic forebears the geonim protested against one who sought to permit women to immerse on Friday night in hot water; and likewise in Knesset HaGedolah; and even on chol hamoed there is concern, as written in Halakhot Gedolot; and likewise in Penei Moshe part 2 siman 62 he added reasons for prohibition erev Shabbat; and likewise in responsa Mekor Baruch, small siman 2, he forbids even that the Jew and the non-Jew in boiling water pour a little on Shabbat from the non-Jew's hot water erev Shabbat; and he also wrote that maidservants are accustomed to light the oven even when there is no cold, only for the Jew-and-non-Jew matter — prohibition erev Shabbat.
