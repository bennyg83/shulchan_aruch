(א) Forbidden to wash, etc. — a decree lest they thereby come to heat on Shabbat:

(ב) His entire body — and the same for most of his body, for the majority is like the whole:

(ג) Alone — and all the more if he washes in the manner of bathing the entire body together:

(ד) And even to pour, etc. — meaning even from water heated from erev Shabbat, and even if they are in the ground:

(ה) In them — meaning in hot water heated from erev Shabbat; but in hot water heated on Shabbat — forbidden even his hands alone:

(ו) Or other limbs — and a woman who wears white on Shabbat and Yom Tov is permitted to wash soiled places in water heated from erev Shabbat and Yom Tov, only that she should be careful to wash with her hands and not in a garment so she not come to wringing; and some women practice not wearing white on Shabbat and Yom Tov; and in a place where there is no known custom one may permit [Acharonim]:

(ז) In fire-heated water — therefore in a place where they practice heating the mikveh, one must be careful that it be only lukewarm, for otherwise forbidden to immerse in it [Chayei Adam]; and see in Korban Netanel chapter Bameh Madlikin siman 24 and in responsum of Noda B'Yehuda Mahadura Tinyana siman 24:

(ח) But in Tiberias hot springs — and the same for other hot springs; they did not decree on them the prohibition of washing; and see below in siman 328 in Magen Avraham s.k. 89 that in a place where the way is to wash in them only for healing — forbidden to wash in them on Shabbat; and see there what we wrote on this:
