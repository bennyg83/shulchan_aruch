(א) To wash his hands in natron — and the same for his face even if he has a beard, for natron is something that does not dissolve hair; permitted to rub with them; only he must be careful not to mix natron with water initially on account of kneading, but take them in wet hands from water; and even though some natron kneads a little in the drops on his hands — no concern, for kneading as an unusual manner is permitted; and this is what Rama concludes: even though one should be stringent not to knead even through change on Shabbat unless he placed the water from erev, as explained in siman 324 — nevertheless here since he does not place water directly on the natron but takes it in wet hands — permitted according to all:
(ב) To wash his hands in salt — the reason as below, that through his washing the salt melts and a new thing is born and resembles a melacha; but to wash his hands in salt water is permitted according to all, as I wrote above in siman 320 seif 12 in Mishna Berurah — see there:
(ג) And it is nolad — and resembles crushing snow and hail explained above in siman 320 for this reason; and see in Magen Avraham who wrote in name of Shiltei Gibborim that some permit in this, for they hold what the Sages forbade crushing snow and hail is on account of the decree of squeezing fruits that stand for beverages, for snow and hail too stand for their water; if so, natron and other lye that do not stand for beverages are permitted l'chatchila; and see in Sefer Tiferet Yisrael who wrote that in our natron, which is soft — for all it is forbidden on account of memachek and like what is stated regarding wax in siman 314 seif 11 — see there; and so I found in Sefer Devarim Menachem in name of Ma'aseh Rokeach chapter 22 of Hilchot Shabbat halacha 13 that he is memarei'ach and memachek and it is a primary melacha and psik reisha, etc., and it is simple; and one should warn the public greatly on this, end of his words; and aside from this, Rashi 50b implies natron dissolves hair, and so Ran, and so is all Israel's custom to be stringent in this:
