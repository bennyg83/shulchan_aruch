(א) To wash his hands in natron — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote that they take in wet hands [meaning even if they are tefach al menat lehatpiach — all this is included in kneading as an unusual manner]; so too Magen Avraham; and even though he is uncertain also in this whether to permit according to those who forbid above through change — nevertheless it appears one may be lenient at least in this manner, since in Mordekhai that Beit Yosef brought and in Levush they permitted even to pour a little water on natron on his hand, for this too is full change (and see Olat Shabbat and Peri Megadim); one should not be stringent at least to take dry natron in wet hands; and so in Sefer Kelalei Shabbat and Shulchan Aruch:

(ב) And forbidden to wash his hands in salt — see in Biur HaGra what he wrote on this; and regarding natron it is forbidden for him even on a weekday on account that smearing is like drinking — see below:

(ג) In other lye, etc. — see in Biur HaGra that his view is like many great poskim that even on a weekday it is forbidden, for smearing is like drinking; and in any case d'rabbanan it is forbidden, not like Rashi and his supporters who permitted entirely; however the world's custom is to wash in our natron made from lye, and only some are careful in this; and if one can obtain natron not made from lye — certainly proper to be concerned for the stringent view:
