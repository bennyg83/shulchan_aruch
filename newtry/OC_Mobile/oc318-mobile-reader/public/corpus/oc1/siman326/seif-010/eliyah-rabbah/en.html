[7] In an unusual manner, etc. Mordechai's wording is that kneading by means of a shinui is permitted. This implies that according to those who prohibit in siman 324, seif 3, it would likewise be forbidden here. It is possible, however, that here everyone agrees it is permitted, since he does not pour water onto them, but rather takes them with wet hands (Magen Avraham). This is contrary to Levush, who wrote that he pours water over them, etc. It is possible that because this is not done in a vessel in which the water remains, it is considered as though he is not pouring water onto them. Nevertheless, it appears to me that even aside from this, everyone agrees it is permitted, as explained in siman 321, seif 16. [8] His hands with salt, etc. Taz raised a difficulty from bran, where doing it in an unusual manner is permitted, and he strained to resolve it. There is no difficulty, because kneading performed with a shinui is not considered kneading, whereas creating a new entity is different.
