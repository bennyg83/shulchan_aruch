It is permitted to wash one's hands in morsen.That is, soap as an afterthought is permitted; and it is forbidden to wash one's hands in salt [Agudah], all the more so in borax called "zait" in a foreign language [Binyamin Ze'ev siman 278], or in other fat that melted on one's hand and is nolad [chapter "Bameh Ishah" and Semag and Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 21 and Tur siman 328].]:
