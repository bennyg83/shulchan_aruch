It is permitted to rinse one’s hands together with bran.RAMA: because this is considered as kneading [dough] in an irregular manner. It is forbidden to rinse one's hands with salt [Agudah] and certainly not with soap called זיי"ף in Arabic [Binyamin Ze'ev] or other products made from [animal] fat because these [substances] will dissolve in the process, thus bringing a new entity into existence on Shabbos.
