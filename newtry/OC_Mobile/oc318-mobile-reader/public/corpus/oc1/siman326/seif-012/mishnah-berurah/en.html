(א) Even to sweat — on account of transgressors who bathed in water heated from daytime and said "we are sweating"; therefore they forbade sweat too; and if he does not intend to sweat — permitted according to this view even if the bathhouse is small, for its heat is great and he will certainly sweat:
(ב) In a place where he can sweat — meaning the bathhouse is small, its heat is great, and he sweats — forbidden even though he does not intend; and according to this view those who ascend from the bathhouse and don garments in the bathhouse on Shabbat must be careful not to stay long lest they come to sweat; and at night when the bathhouse is very hot it is impossible to be careful; and regarding women's immersion one must say they relied at a time of need on the first view:
