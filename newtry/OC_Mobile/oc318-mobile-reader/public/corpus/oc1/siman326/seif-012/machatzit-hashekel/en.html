(s.k. 12) That he plugged, etc. — from here requires study, etc. — meaning there are two rooms one above the other and there are holes in the lower ceiling so that if they kindle the oven below and the lower room heats, heat enters through the holes also to the upper room — this must be forbidden, for for this reason the bather plugged his holes, as Rashi's second explanation wrote upon it, and so is essential; therefore permitted; but if the holes are open — forbidden, for through heat rising via the holes upward it is as if kindled on Shabbat; if so, forbidden to open the holes in that room on Shabbat, for it is as if kindling on Shabbat:
