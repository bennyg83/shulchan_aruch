Magen Avraham s.k. 8 that according to Ri one immerses a convert on Shabbat. This proof is not understood. For it is obvious that per the law of the gemara even a tamei person is permitted to immerse, for it is not like repairing since it appears as cooling; but according to us who are accustomed to forbid cold bathing — one may say it is repairing and forbidden even bathing of an apostate.
