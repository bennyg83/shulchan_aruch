7) There: Even pouring the water over his body, etc. The same prohibition applies to washing a corpse, even when there is concern that it will decay, and even to having a non-Jew wash it, unless one agreed upon payment with the non-Jew before Shabbos. Radakh, House 22, chamber 1; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur; Olas Shabbos, paragraph 4. Bigdei Yesha questions Radakh from the explicit Mishnah that one may anoint and wash a corpse provided one does not move any of its limbs. Pischei HaDevir, paragraph 1, has already resolved this: the Mishnah discusses cold water, whereas Radakh forbids hot water. On the contrary, he writes that the words of Bigdei Yesha themselves require resolution; see there.
