[5] [Levush] It appears as though he is cooling himself, etc. Nowadays, when people are careful to avoid bathing as stated above, one who immerses appears to be performing an act of repair, as stated by Terumat HaDeshen there. It appears that one may immerse for a seminal emission, since the obligation is d'rabbanan, as stated in siman 323, seif 7.
