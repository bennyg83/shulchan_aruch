(א) One who bathes in a river, etc. — poskim wrote they practice not to wash at all in a river or mikveh, lest one come to wringing hair and for several other reasons; see in Magen Avraham; and regarding immersion see below; however hands and feet are permitted to wash in a river when he dries before walking four amot [Magen Avraham]:
(ב) When he rises, etc. — meaning immediately when he rises near the river he should dry himself and not walk at all in the water upon him, lest he forget and walk in them four amot; if so, it implies from his garments that he removes before descending to the river he must leave them near the river; and in any case the towel with which he pats his body so he not walk in the water upon him; and see in Peri Megadim that the same applies in the river itself when he rises from a deep place and half his body is outside the water — he must be careful not to walk thus four amot, for the river is like a karmelit and he ends up carrying four amot in a karmelit; accordingly when he descends to immerse in a river and at first the water is low near the ground he must be careful not to walk four amot far from its bank, for on his return he will carry the water upon him four amot:
(ג) They were not concerned about it — meaning because they are few and it is not usual to have the measure of carrying water for which one is liable — they did not decree on this, which is not the normal manner of carrying; and see in Biur HaGra that according to Tosafos it implies even abundance of water absorbed in his garment is permitted to walk in them; and see Taz's reason:
