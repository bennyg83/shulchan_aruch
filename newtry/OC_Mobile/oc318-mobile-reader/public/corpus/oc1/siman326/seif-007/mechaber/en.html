A person who bathes in a river must dry his body thoroughly when he ascends from the river, so that no water will remain on his body, [and] he does not carry [this water] four cubits in a karmelis, considering that one who ascends from the rinsing has a majority of water on his body. But if he walks in the public domain and there is delightful rain on his head and his clothes, we are not strict with him on this.
