Permitted to immerse. Maharil wrote siman 150: women properly practice not immersing not at their time on Shabbat; for the reason is the widespread custom not to wash in cold water, and the reason is relevant to decree on account of wringing and on account of carrying water upon him four amot in a karmelit, and on account of anointing the body and cosmetics; therefore so too in Terumat HaDeshen siman 255 — even though Mordekhai is stringent in cold water specifically in a vessel, but in a river or mikveh permitted — nevertheless the custom is to forbid; therefore even to immerse is forbidden, for it appears as fixing, as stated in the gemara, end of his words; and so Beit Yosef wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 199 that it is forbidden to immerse on Shabbat on account of wringing hair, end of his words; nevertheless it appears permitted to immerse for keri since it is d'rabbanan, as written in siman 323 seif 7; and see siman 244 seif 11; and in Agur siman 376 it implies that in a river it is forbidden; also in Beit Yosef in name of Kol Bo he wrote to forbid on account of wringing hair — see there; and in Avodah Zarah siman 268 seif 4; and it appears to me a mumar is permitted to immerse since it is not d'oraisa, as written there at the end of the seif; and so in Yevamos daf 47b that according to R' Yehuda they immerse a convert on Shabbat:
