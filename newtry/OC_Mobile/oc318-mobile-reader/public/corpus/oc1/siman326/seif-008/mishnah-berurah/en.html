(24) A person, etc. — meaning even though a vessel is forbidden to immerse and purify it from its impurity on Shabbat and Yom Tov because the vessel appears as fixed thereby; a person is different because he appears as cooling himself in water, and even if the time is cold and the water foul so it is not the way to wash in them — nevertheless sometimes when a person comes from the road soiled with mud and excrement he is accustomed to wash even in these; therefore permitted even if he could immerse before Shabbat. And women practice not immersing not at their time on Shabbat; and poskim wrote they properly practiced, since ultimately the custom in our time is to forbid on Shabbat washing the entire body even in cold, as above — if so it is recognizable it is for immersion and appears as fixing; and Acharonim wrote nevertheless it appears permitted to immerse for keri since the enactment was voided and it is permitted in Torah and prayer even before immersion, as above in siman 88 — if so it does not appear as fixing through immersion; and see what is written in Biur Halacha that some are stringent and hold one should not distinguish between immersion for keri and other impurity; and one who practices to be lenient — one should not protest, for most Acharonim conclude to be lenient; and in any case for one who became impure on Shabbat and Yom Tov itself it appears certainly one should not be stringent; and so the custom has spread to permit; only he should be very careful not to come to wringing, which is a full prohibition:
