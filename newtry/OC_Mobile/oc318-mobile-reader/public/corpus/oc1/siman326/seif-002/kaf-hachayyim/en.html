13) [Seif 2] If the place is roofed, however, it is forbidden, etc. A roofed place contains much steam and will cause one to perspire. The Sages separately decreed against perspiring in a bathhouse, even without bathing, as stated below in seif 12, lest one heat water on Shabbos. They also prohibited perspiring in the hot springs of Tiberias because of perspiring in other bathhouses. Some hold that the decree against perspiring applies only to water heated by fire, but not to the hot springs of Tiberias. According to them, even when the place is roofed, one may bathe and perspire in the hot springs of Tiberias. The Sages did not extend the decree so far, since that would be like imposing one decree as a safeguard for another. Levush.
