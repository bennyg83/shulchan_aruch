Magen Avraham s.k. 4 that if they mix together it is permitted. It implies it is permitted even for bathing even though the water was drawn and came on Shabbat, since it is in the permitted category like that of opening water for a garden — see in Tosafos. And even though hot water heated on Shabbat in a permitted manner is also forbidden to bathe, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 6 — one must say that here it is preferable since no action was done on Shabbat at all.
