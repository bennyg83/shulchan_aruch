(In Shulchan Aruch seif 2) And some say it is permitted to perspire in Tiberias hot springs. And it is written in responsa Me'il Tzedakah in the bathhouse of Tepliz that there are hot waters like Tiberias hot springs — whoever enters and intends only to perspire is certainly forbidden, and forbidden perspiration automatically entails bathing in water in the bathhouse even if roofed it is permitted since it is built in the open air upon the ground. But to bathe in Tiberias hot springs within the pit beneath the ground that is covered — there is reason to forbid. And see in responsa Mayim Rabim siman 25 and 26; and all this when those bathhouses of hot water are for healing it is forbidden to bathe in them, and they are apt to come to d'oraisa desecration of Shabbat; and two Torah scholars in this country agreed with his words and wrote that he breaches a fence, etc., see there; and see below siman 328 s.k. 35 in the name of Magen Avraham.
