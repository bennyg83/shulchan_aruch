And even to pour the water on his body, etc. Meaning washing is to insert his body or limbs into the water and wash there; but rinsing is pouring on his body or limbs, which is not the manner of washing — nevertheless forbidden; and the reason Ran wrote is that thus is the way of bathers after they sweat — they take water in a vessel and pour on their backs, and people will suspect it was heated today. And it appears from the Mechaber's words that even rinsing limb by limb is forbidden whenever he rinses the entire body. See there 3; and so Mishnat Hasidim 1 that rinsing limb by limb of the entire body is forbidden — see there; and women must be warned in winter when they immerse Friday night, and their way when leaving the spring is to pour hot water on them — they should pour only up to half the body specifically; and the same for a father of a newborn who immerses Shabbat morning.
