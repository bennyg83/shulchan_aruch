(א) That he plugged its holes — those holes upon which the bather warms his hands, for fire rises from outside beneath him; and those winter houses heated from beneath, and on the holes there are iron bowls or stone plugging — they practice leniency to open them on Shabbat:

(ב) He washes in it immediately — for behold it was not heated on Shabbat; and if there is doubt whether its holes were plugged, see above in siman 325 seif 7:

(ג) His left side was heated on Shabbat — through putting wood in it from erev while still day:

(ד) Lest he extinguish in coals — and since the Sages forbade doing so, therefore he must wait until Motza'ei Shabbat bekdei she-ye'asu so he not do so another time that he not plug his holes and come to extinguishing [Tosafos Shabbos]:
