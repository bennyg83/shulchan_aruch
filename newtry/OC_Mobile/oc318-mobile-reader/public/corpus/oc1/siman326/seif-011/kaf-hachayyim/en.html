11) There: But in the hot springs of Tiberias it is permitted, etc. Beis Yosef writes that one may even bathe within them, as the ordinary meaning of bathing is to enter the water. Mordechai, however, forbids entering even cold water to bathe and permits only rinsing oneself by pouring water over the limbs. The ruling here does not follow Mordechai. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 3. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1, likewise rules that one may bathe within them.
