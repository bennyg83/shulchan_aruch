Forbidden Taz — the Mechaber omitted the law of majority; for according to Rif and Rambam a doubt is forbidden, and the halacha is like the first tanna there. And see what is written in siman 314 that a doubt we are not stringent there for the Mechaber; one should say in the evening we are lenient — all the more so here. And know that half and half is forbidden according to Rosh; therefore half is as certain; and for Rif and Rambam one follows leniency in a doubt and leans to stringency; and see siman 495 in Magen Avraham 23 — Rashi that it is a doubt; and see Shabbat 191a, two versions: one taught according to Rav — half is a doubt; and one taught according to Shmuel — half is certain, where he does for both — see there; and there is further relevance if he cannot wait — all the more when the caravan travels, and similar to the reason half is certain it is forbidden; and for the reason of doubt, where there is no choice — in such a case permitted; see siman 325 on this:
