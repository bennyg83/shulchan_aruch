Cup of blessing — at the end of Nazir Rashi explained that it refers to the cup of zimmun blessing, because they hurry to give reward to the one who blesses before the one who answers amen; and nevertheless, both are equal in reward; and this is the opinion of the Tur, from that he wrote it here; and this requires further study, for in Yoreh De'ah siman 28 they ruled that the one who answers amen is greater than the one who blesses; and in the Gemara they say it is a dispute among tannaim; and one must say that there it is a Hagahah and it is not the words of the Tur, as the Beit Yosef wrote there; and even though in Choshen Mishpat siman 382 the Rema ruled like his words regarding milah, one can say that nevertheless the law is true that if another preceded him he is exempt from paying, for in any case the one who answers amen receives reward like the one who blesses, even though they hurry to bless on account of this — he is not obligated to pay, for he did not cause him any loss. Afterwards I found that Shach likewise wrote so in Choshen Mishpat siman 382; and see there several laws.
