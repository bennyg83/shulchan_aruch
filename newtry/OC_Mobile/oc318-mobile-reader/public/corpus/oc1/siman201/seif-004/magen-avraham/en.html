The cup of blessing. At the end of Nazir, Rashi explains that this refers to the cup used for the zimun blessing, because reward is hastened to the one who recites the blessing before the one who answers amen. Nevertheless, both receive equal reward. This also appears to be the Tur's view, since he records the law here. This requires study, for in Yoreh De'ah, siman 28, he rules that one who answers amen is greater than the one who recites the blessing. The Gemara says that this matter is disputed by Tannaim. It must therefore be that the ruling there is a gloss and not the Tur's own words, as Beit Yosef writes there. Although Rama in Choshen Mishpat, siman 382, rules accordingly regarding circumcision, the law may nevertheless be correct: if another person preceded him in performing the mitzvah, he is exempt from payment, because one who answers amen receives reward equal to the one who recites the blessing. Even though reward is hastened to the one who blesses, the other person caused him no actual loss and is therefore not liable. I later found that Shach writes similarly at the end of Choshen Mishpat, siman 382. See there for several related laws.
