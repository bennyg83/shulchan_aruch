(4) [Levush] And the one who blesses Birkat Hamazon, etc. — and if so, these words apply specifically regarding a guest, and regarding the cup of Birkat Hamazon — Magen Avraham. And I say: nowadays the blesser blesses for all those seated, for he says "us," etc. But it is difficult in the Talmud [Berakhot] daf 55, Megillah [whence do we know] that they shorten — why specifically where it is written regarding one who curses you, "I will curse"; but regarding one who does not bless you, also "I will not accept you" and also "I will not bless you." And one can say: if so, "and I will bless those who bless you" — why do I need this? Let us learn from "I will curse" and from the rule that good measure is greater, etc.
