(14) One to whom they give permission to bless — meaning a guest to whom they give permission to bless in Birkat Hamazon and he does not bless — they shorten his days, because by law when the guest blesses he blesses the host, as above; and with this — that he refrains from blessing the host, who is from the seed of Abraham, as it is said of him, "And I will bless those who bless you" — from the positive you hear the negative: he causes a curse upon himself. And Magen Avraham wrote, and specifically when they bless over the cup — for then the obligation is upon the guest to bless the host. And it appears that he means that without this there is no causing of a curse; but certainly it is not proper for a person to push off a mitzvah that comes to his hand.
