In the hymn "Nodah Leshimcha," etc., with the permission of the priests and the Levites; and some read "the priests the Levites" — meaning that priests are called Levites. And their reason is because in our time Levites and Israelites are equal, as Rashi explained in the parshah of Re'eh; but Prisha distinguishes there: he speaks about a gift given for free, that an Israelite is not obligated to give it to him. And now the wonder of Magen Avraham is resolved — see there. And per this, when there is no priest, a Levi has precedence before an Israelite in haMotzi and in Birkat Hamazon; and so is implied in Bach and Prisha.
