3) There: The greatest recites the blessing, etc. Nowadays some are not particular about any of this, lest they come to quarrel with the host by telling him that a certain person is the greater and more fitting one. The host therefore gives the blessing to whomever he happens to wish, without concern for whether he is greater or lesser, fitting or unfit. Levush; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 1; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1; Shulchan Gavoah there. Sephardic communities, however, still observe the custom that the greatest recites the blessing, and there is objection if the host gives it to whomever he wishes. Beis Menuchah, paragraph 6. This refers to leading the Zimun, for regarding Birkas HaMazon the practice is for each person to recite it himself, as stated in siman 183, paragraph 38; see there.
