(3) There — the greatest blesses, etc. — and in these lands they are not particular about all this, not to bring insult together with the host by saying to him, "This one is great and honorable," and the host gives permission to bless to whomever he wishes in a casual case, without any stringency of great or small, honorable or not honorable — Shlah, Hagahot, note 1; Ohr Torah, note 1; Avnei Nezer there, note 1. But the Sephardim too, in these lands, practice this custom that the greatest blesses, and there is an objection if the host gives permission to bless to whomever he wishes — Beit Yosef, note 6. And this is regarding the blessing of zimmun; but in Birkat Hamazon the custom is that each one blesses for himself alone, as written siman 183, note 38, see there.
