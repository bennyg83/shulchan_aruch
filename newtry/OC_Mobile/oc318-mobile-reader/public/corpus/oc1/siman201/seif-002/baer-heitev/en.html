To a commoner priest. And we say in the Gemara that he is liable for death, for he disparages the Torah.

First. In the reading of the Torah — Rashi, Ran, and the Rosh wrote to be the head (rosh), for in every assembly one speaks and expounds first. The same applies in a yeshiva — the head speaks first. Magen Avraham wrote, and this requires further study: why are we not careful nowadays to precede the priest in all those matters, since they are d'oraisa? Perhaps we are not expert in priestly lineages. However, if the priest wishes to share honor with another, he may. Semag, and above siman 128, note 82, from Shitah Mekubetzet. And if a Levi must precede an Israelite — there is a dispute among the poskim: some say he must precede, and thus they established in the hymn "Nodah Leshimcha" with the permission of the priests and the Levites. And some say it is not necessary, and they read "the priests the Levites" — for priests are called Levites — Magen Avraham. (And in the book Eliyah Rabbah he ruled that when there is no priest, a Levi has precedence before an Israelite in haMotzi and in Birkat Hamazon, and so is implied in Bach and Prisha.)
