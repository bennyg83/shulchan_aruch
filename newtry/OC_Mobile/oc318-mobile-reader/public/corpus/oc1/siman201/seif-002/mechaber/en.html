An Israelite Torah scholar shall not precede an am ha'aretz priest to bless before him — through the law and judgment of priesthood; but for the Torah scholar to give him permission to bless, there is nothing wrong with that. But a priest who is a Torah scholar —. it is a mitzvah to precede him, as it is said, "And you shall sanctify him" — to open first and bless first. (And see above siman 167 seif 14.) (.
