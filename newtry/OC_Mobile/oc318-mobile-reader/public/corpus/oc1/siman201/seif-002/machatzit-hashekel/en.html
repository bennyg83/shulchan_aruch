(Note 2) To whomever, etc. — it is difficult, etc., that they bless for the host. He meant to say: from the language of the Rema it appears that he follows the Mechaber's view, who wrote that the host has permission to waive his blessing — meaning that he blesses himself; and the Rema wrote on this "and likewise," meaning that likewise in such a case he may waive; and about this is the difficulty, for in such a case he is not waiving, and they too need to bless for the host. And know that in Sefer Lechem Chamudot he wrote all his days wondering why we change the wording of the blessing for the host from what is stated in the Gemara.
