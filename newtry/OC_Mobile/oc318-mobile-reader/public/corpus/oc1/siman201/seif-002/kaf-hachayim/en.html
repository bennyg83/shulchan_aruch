(2) There — the greatest blesses, etc. — and from this they relied on what is practiced, that a mourner blesses, since he reclines at the head; but they greatly expanded the custom that the mourner blesses for all twelve months. And there is no place for this custom except when both are equal; but if the host is a mourner and he has a guest, or if the guest is a mourner — in every case, if the host wishes to bless, the permission is in his hand.
