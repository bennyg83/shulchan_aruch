2) There: The greatest recites the blessing, etc. This provides support for the custom that a mourner leads the blessing, since he reclines at the head. The custom was expanded considerably, however, so that a mourner leads throughout all twelve months. This custom has a basis only when both individuals are equal. If the host is a mourner and has a guest, or if the guest is a mourner, in every case the host may lead if he wishes. She'eilas Yaavetz, siman 74; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 12; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1; Beis Menuchah, paragraphs 4 and 5.
