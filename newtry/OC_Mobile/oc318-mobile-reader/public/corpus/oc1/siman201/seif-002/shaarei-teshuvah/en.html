Blesses — Ba'er Heitev, who showed a place to responsa Chatam Tzefanat regarding a guest who is a nazir, etc.; and I brought the words of that responsum; and the matter does not depend on a guest at all — only that such was the incident, that they honored the guest who was a nazir to bless; and he gave the cup to another, and he led zimmun by name and blessed; and the one who was holding the cup blessed with Birkat haPanim and Birkat haGefen; and on this he disagreed there that one should not do so; and see what he wrote there.
