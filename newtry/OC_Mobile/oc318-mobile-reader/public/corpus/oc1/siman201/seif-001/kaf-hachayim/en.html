(1) [Seif 1] The greatest blesses, etc. — and "greatest" means greatest in wisdom, as written above siman 167, note 97, see there; and so too Raz in this siman, note 1. And that the greatest blesses — for example, when all eat from their own; for if not so, he gives to a guest or to one of his household members, who is like a guest. Arizal, note 1; Avnei Nezer, note 1. And if the greatest one brings out saliva that they call "hostatin," another should bless afterward, for it is not proper that he stop many times and spit while the others wait. Hagah in Berachot of Mahari; Magen Avraham, note 1; Avnei Nezer there, note 1; Beit Yosef, note 1. And likewise one should not give permission l'chatchila to bless Birkat Hamazon to one who is missing a finger or one with a boil — as written above siman 181, note 28, see there.
