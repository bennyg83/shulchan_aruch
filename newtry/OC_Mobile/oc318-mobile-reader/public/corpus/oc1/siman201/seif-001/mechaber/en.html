Who blesses — 4 paragraphs. The greatest one blesses, even
if he came at the end. And if he wished to give permission to a minor to bless, he may. And these matters apply when there is no guest there; but if there is a guest there, he blesses, even if the host is greater than him, so that he bless the host. And what blessing does he bless him? "May it be Your will that this host not be put to shame nor confounded, neither in this world nor in the World to Come, and may he succeed in all his possessions, and may his possessions be successful and close to the city, and may Satan not rule over the work of his hands, and may no sinful thing or evil thought attach to him from now and forever." And if the host wishes to waive (meaning that he does not wish to insist upon) his blessing and bless Birkat Hamazon himself, he may. (And likewise he may give permission to bless to whomever he wishes (Beit Yosef in the name of Ohel Moed).) על ברכתו ולברך ברכת המזון בעצמו רשאי: הגה וה"ה שיכול ליתן לברך למי שירצה (ב"י בשם אוהל מועד.
