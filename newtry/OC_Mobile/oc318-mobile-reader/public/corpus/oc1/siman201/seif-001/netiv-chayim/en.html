(In Magen Avraham note 4) And it requires study, for in Gittin we explicitly say that it is d'oraisa, etc.; and to me the resolution is that that which was explained "ways of peace" is d'oraisa applies to the other amoraim from whom it is derived from a different verse; and you should know that if so, per the one who holds "and you shall sanctify him" — what is the challenge? It is needed, ways of peace, for the Levi who reads after the priest, and to precede him before an Israelite; and furthermore, what does it answer "d'oraisa" and "on account of ways of peace"? That works out well for the other one who holds that we should say the Torah said so to precede priest before Levi and Levi before Israel on account of ways of peace; but per the one who holds "and you shall sanctify him" for every matter — how can one say so, to sanctify him on account of ways of peace? And see in Yerushalmi which brings the dispute of the amoraim in this — whether it is from the Torah or not; and our Talmud holds like the one who says that it is from the Torah, and therefore it challenges well against all those other amoraim; and understand thoroughly.
