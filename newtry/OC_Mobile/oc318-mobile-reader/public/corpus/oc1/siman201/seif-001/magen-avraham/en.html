The greatest blesses. And if the greatest one brings out saliva that they call "hostatin," another should bless, for it is not proper that he stop many times and spit while the others wait (Hagah in Berachot of Maharam).

To whomever he wishes. It is difficult either way: if one gives permission to bless to one member of the household, it is obvious that they too are guests for this matter, that they bless for the host; and it appears that if one relies on his table and pays him the cost of his meals, and that one need not bless for the host, he may give him permission to bless. And further it appears that if two eat in one house and one has a guest at his table, that host may give permission to bless to whomever he wishes, for by law the guest should bless; and if that host wishes to waive his blessing, he may give permission to bless to whomever he wishes.
