(א) They should not bow too much, for they have already prayed and bowed within their prayer; therefore it suffices that they bow their head slightly. But when one prays alone he may bow in the eighteen blessings at Avot and at Hodayah at the beginning and end, and likewise in bending when taking three steps it is good until he sees his belt opposite his heart or until all the vertebrae in the spine loosen—but no more than this boundary. But when bowing with the prayer leader at Modim he should not bow except slightly, meaning he should move his head and no more.

(ב) And they say Modim D'Rabbanan—all in one bowing. And one should not say "that You are Heaven our Heaven," for one must straighten up at the Divine Name, as is written: "for His name is exalted, Heaven straightens the bent" (so wrote the Ra'avad and Rashba, Mahari Luria in Sefer Lechem min haShamayim). And some say one need not be particular about "Heaven straightens the bent" except at the end of a berachah (Maharam in writings siman 100). And it appears one fulfills the requirement of all if he says "Heaven our Heaven," and likewise he should bend at Modim and straighten up slightly at the Name until the end—and then it is the proper way (Maharshal and my teacher in Bach).
