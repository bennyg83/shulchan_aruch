And they shall not bow, etc. Rambam and Tosafos end of first chapter 12b s.v. kara, etc. uveleved, etc. And Rambam explains on the congregation at Modim D'Rabbanan that it refers to this in the Yerushalmi there. And see above siman 113 seif 5.

And concludes, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Berachos, and Rambam wrote without a conclusion as in our Gemara. And Beit Yosef was in doubt to say without mention of the Name, and Tur's view is to conclude with the Name, and he wrote that thus practiced his father the Rosh z"l, and that is the essential law. And see above siman 207.

And there is one who says, etc. Thus explained the Ra'avad what is written in chapter 5 of Berachos 34b: Rava bends at Hodayah at the beginning and end of the prayer leader and the individual praying—it is obvious one bends at beginning and end as in the baraita there, but he speaks only of Modim D'Rabbanan. And so it is in Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Berachos: taught R' Chalafta ben Shaul—all bow with the prayer leader at Hodayah. R' Zeira said: only at Modim. Ze'ira would draw close to her so he would bow with her at beginning and end. And in our version R' Zeira, etc. But the first version holds Ze'ira was stringent on himself, but the halachah follows R' Zeira who said only at Modim. Beit Yosef. But Rashba explained what is written "only at Modim"—meaning one need not bow at "haTov shimcha" with the prayer leader, but at the end of Modim D'Rabbanan one must bow; and Ze'ira intended to finish with the prayer leader in order to bow with him, as Rashba wrote there. But it appears the explanation is not thus, but rather that here R' Zeira refers to the formulas of Modim, and we say there: wives who bow and whisper—meaning they were bent and would say all of Modim while bent; and on this R' Zeira who thought to draw close to her—meaning he would say everything in one bowing as in the gloss, and would extend close to the prayer leader's extension until when the prayer leader said the end he was still in the bowing of Modim. And there is a printing error in the Yerushalmi, and it must read: that which R' Zeira—afterwards there are those who say this or that, but to bow at the end is not a question at all anywhere. And for halachah it appears that even "all of Modim" one need not say in one bowing, for we say there about it: Chanan bar Ba disputes, for Chanan bar Ba said: I saw Rav bow at Baruch and straighten up at the Name—meaning when he reached "You are Heaven" he had to straighten up; and if so, what they saw bowing and whispering was only until "You are Heaven," and we hold like Rav. And see in the commentary on the Yerushalmi.
