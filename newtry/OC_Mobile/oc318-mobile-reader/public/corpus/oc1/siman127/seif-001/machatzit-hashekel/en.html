Sk 1: And not too much, etc.—as required for other bowings. And that which it was necessary to write thus in Modim—Rabbi Yosef wrote: because some people are accustomed to bow more at Modim than in other places, for it appears to them that whoever adds bowing at Modim is praiseworthy; therefore they wrote that at Modim too one should not bow too much—end quote.

And the Bach, etc., [that one] nod[s] slightly, etc. And the reason — the Bach wrote, and these are his words: Since they are not now obligated to bow fully and do not bow except so as not to appear as deniers toward one to whom the prayer leader bows fully; therefore the Sages warned that they should not bow except a little, so that they know there is no complete obligation in this bowing, but only so as not to appear as deniers, for this reason. And if one comes to bow at the end of every blessing, they teach him not to bow, etc., so they not come to uproot the enactments of the Sages — so far.
