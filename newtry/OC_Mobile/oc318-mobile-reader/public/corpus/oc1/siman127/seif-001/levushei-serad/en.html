Magen Avraham sk 2—his intention. It was difficult for him—for also at the beginning of a berachah we hold one straightens up at the Name as explained in siman 113. And he answered: the enactment of the Sages is to straighten up at the end of the berachah, and therefore one straightens up at the Name; and therefore also at the beginning of a berachah if his intent is to straighten up immediately he straightens up at the Name. But here he does not straighten up until he finishes the entire berachah, and in its conclusion there is no Name—therefore even though there is a Name at its beginning we have no concern with it.
