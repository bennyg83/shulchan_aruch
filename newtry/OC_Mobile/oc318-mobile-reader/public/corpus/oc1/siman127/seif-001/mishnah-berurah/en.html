(1) And they should not bow — this refers to the congregation; and regarding the prayer leader himself, certainly he is not preferable to another person praying, for the Mechaber ruled above in siman 113, seif 5, that he should not bow much — see there.

(2) Too much — meaning they should bow as the law of other bowings, as written siman 113, seif 5. And the Bach explains that in Modim that one says with the prayer leader he should not bow but only nod his head slightly, and the world did not practice thus.

(3) And they say, etc. — and the prayer leader need not wait until the congregation finishes Modim D'Rabbanan, but rather prays in his way.

(4) Without mentioning the Name — and the Gra's view is to say "Blessed are You, God of acknowledgments," and the world did not practice thus.

(5) In one bowing — and the Bach wrote in the name of Rashal that he should straighten up slightly when he says the Name until its end, and then he bows — so far. And the world practices as Rama wrote [Magen Avraham]; and see in Biur Halacha.
