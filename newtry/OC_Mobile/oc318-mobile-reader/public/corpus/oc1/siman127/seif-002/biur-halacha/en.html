He says the prayer leader, etc.—see Mishna Berurah sk 7 regarding be'di'eved that one does not return, for without them the view of Western people is that even lechatchilah one should not say—see Tur; and also in Tosafos [Berachos 34a s.v. lo] they wrote that in the time of the Gemara the custom was not to say. And apparently if one remembered before saying "Baruch ata Heaven" he should return and say—this thing is not worse than remembering and "Who is like You," which also has no source in the Gemara, yet we hold if remembered before finishing atah chonen he returns. And see above siman 114 what Magen Avraham ruled sk 4 that be'di'eved one need not return even before finishing the berachah, and it requires study.

But without them, etc.—see Mishna Berurah; and if remembered in the morning when he began Shalom Rav before saying atah chonen, perhaps he may return and say Sim Shalom for the reason we wrote before regarding the prayer leader's berachah. But conversely if it happened in the evening that he said Sim Shalom, even if he remembered before atah chonen it appears he need not return, for since he added in the request he detracts.
