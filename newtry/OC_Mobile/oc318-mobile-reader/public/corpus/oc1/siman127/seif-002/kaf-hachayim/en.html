(2) There: the congregation bows with him—for we learn in Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Berachos: all bow with the prayer leader at Hodayah—end quote. Tur and Beit Yosef. And it implies from this that they bow like him and to the same measure as the other bowings of the eighteen blessings written in siman 113, as is clear in Beit Yosef, and so too Taz and Magen Avraham sk 1. Orach Teshuvah ot 2, and he wrote that thus is the view of Maran z"l. R' Zeira ot 1, Chayei Adam klal 30 ot 10. However Rambam z"l in chapter 9 of Hilchot Tefillah halachah 4 wrote: when the prayer leader reaches Modim all the people bow slightly and do not bow too much—end quote. And the reason he wrote there in Kesef Mishnah: because they already prayed and are not bowing except so they not appear as deniers; therefore it suffices that they bow their head slightly, and one who bows too much appears haughty—end quote. And it implies there in Kesef Mishnah that the prayer leader and congregation are equal—that they bow only slightly, as cited. But Bach wrote that from Rambam's words above it implies the prayer leader bends as required for bending, but all the people bow slightly; and he wrote the reason: since the congregation is not now obligated to bend and bow only so they not appear as deniers toward one the prayer leader bends to, therefore the Sages warned they should bow only slightly so they know this bowing is not a full obligation but only so they not appear as deniers—see there. And they brought thus in Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 1, Taz ot 1, Magen Avraham ot 2, and so in Sha'ar HaKavanot end of derush 4 of repetition of Amidah that in Modim D'Rabbanan there is only bending and straightening of the head alone; and so in derush 2 of Amidah 29a and derush 6 of 37b, and so in Pri Etz Chaim Sha'ar HaAmidah chapter 22 prayer leader 139b. Kaf HaChayyim siman 18 ot 22, Beit Yosef Yoreh De'ah ot 5. However this follows the plain sense of Sha'ar HaKavanot who wrote that in Modim in whisper one does bending of the body and straightening of the body alone, and in Modim D'Rabbanan bending of the head and straightening of the head alone. But according to Rashash z"l who deduced from words of the Ari z"l from other places and wrote one must do in whisper at Modim bending of body and bending of head and straightening of body and straightening of head, and likewise the prayer leader in repetition as written above siman 113 ot 21—if so nothing remains to do in Modim D'Rabbanan bending of head and straightening of head since we did everything in whisper; rather one must do as the prayer leader does—bending of body and head and straightening of body and head. And this is Maran z"l's view and his colleagues who hold one does in Modim D'Rabbanan like the silent prayer and like the prayer leader does. And so Magen Avraham sk 1 that the world did not practice like Bach—see there. And if so, since there is a dispute whether head-bending alone suffices or one needs like in whisper, and also those who hold head-bending alone did not say it is forbidden to do like in whisper; and what is written that there is haughtiness is specifically if one does too much as explained in siman 113 and as the gloss wrote below ot 4—therefore one should be stringent and do in Modim D'Rabbanan as the prayer leader does: at Modim anachnu lach—bending of body; at she'atah—bending of head; at Heaven—straightening of body and head, as written in siman 113 ot 21—see there and study. However it appears that needing to do bending of body and head, etc. is specifically if intending for the prayer leader's repetition and therefore must do like him; but if in psalms and the like, or already prayed and heard repetition but afterwards heard the prayer leader saying Modim—head-bending alone suffices.
