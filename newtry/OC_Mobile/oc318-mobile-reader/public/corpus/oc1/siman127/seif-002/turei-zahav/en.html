And the world practiced that after the first two blessings they say yehi ratzon, and after the third they say ken yehi ratzon. It seems to give a reason: for if there is no peace there is nothing, and the third blessing is peace and it concludes the main blessing; therefore they say ken yehi ratzon there to indicate that in this is the essence of the blessing. Transcription from the book Etz Chaim, which the divine Maharam Caliphras composed, disciple of the divine holy of holies Maharari Luria z"l, on matters of prayer: One: In the chapter of the daily offering he should say Pitum HaKetoret. "Wine of Cyprus" — the yod with a tzere. "A quarter of a kab" — he should not say except "a quarter," for anonymously it is thus in the Gemara. "The incense for the limbs" — he should not say "preceding." After the incense he should say "Heaven of hosts" three times and "and an offering to Heaven." And in the lamps, the daily offering of between the evenings precedes. "Upon it" — "the whole of the offerings" he should say "the whole" with a tzere under the lamed. Afterward Ana B'Koach — and the formula of Ana B'Koach he should say in this language: "Please, by the great power of Your right hand, release the bound," in one breath, i.e., Abgita"tz; and likewise the other names, i.e., Kra Satan Nagd Yekhsh Batrat Tzetag Chakab Tna'a. Yagel Pazak. Shaku Tzayit. Every verse he says he should say with the melody as one reads in the Torah. And likewise two verses — he should say "and here two verses," etc. One must look every day at the tzitzit, as written "and you shall see it," and it is great benefit for the soul and sin will not befall him through it. The tallit must cover the head tefillin. Every time he says Ashrei he should afterward say "and we shall bless Yah," etc. He would not remove tallit and tefillin until Aleinu. After Aleinu he should say "and Heaven shall be king over all the earth; on that day Heaven shall be one and His name one." And he should intend that he begins with vav and concludes with daled, which is ten letters — sefirot; therefore I saw there. The baraita of Tanna Devei R. Yishmael: "You have nothing before the morning daily offering," etc. — it is in the chapter Tamid nidcheh, page 59; and Tosafos wrote there it appears to R. Yitzchak that lamps and incense of the afternoon are not counted except something of burning, for in this the Torah was strict afterward, as written "and he shall burn upon it the fats of the peace offerings" — so far. When he says in the first verse "Heaven our Heaven, Heaven is one," he should intend on the four letters of the Tetragrammaton. When he says "Blessed be the name of the glory of His kingdom," he should intend on the four letters of Adnut. When he says "forever and ever," he should intend on the name Yahadonay. When he says "May His great name be exalted," he should intend that He, may He be blessed, rules over four worlds ABY"A, i.e., le'olam, which is lamed yud — against the world of Atzilut where there is great unity; and le'olmei — against the world of Beriah; when he says almaya he should intend against the world of Yetzirah; be'alma — against the world of Asiyah. And when he says yisbarach he should bow, for it is "bless You" — you bow. In the formula "who appeases with mercy" — in a new printing it is "who entices in supplications," and this is not correct, for Ramban wrote in parashat Mishpatim on the verse "and if he entices" — enticement is on false words.
