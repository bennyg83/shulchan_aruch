In the berachah. In the berachah at the opening. Kohanim im—there is no word kohanim attached to im, for we hold kohanim are agents of the Merciful One, but rather meaning the kohanim are called "Your holy people." When saying Yevarechecha one looks toward the side of the Sanctuary. Veyishmerecha—toward his right side. Ya'er Heaven—toward the Sanctuary. Panav eilecha—veyichunecha toward his left side to unify with the right. Zohar parashat Nasso.

Va'avarchem. And we do not say anything except until "Shalom."

Ratzon. It implies one should not answer except at the end. And there are those who practice answering between each verse "ken yehi ratzon," but those who say at the beginning only "ken yehi ratzon" err, for then there is no meaning to what ken yehi ratzon applies. Bach.

Sim Shalom. And in the siddur of the Ari z"l always Sim Shalom.
