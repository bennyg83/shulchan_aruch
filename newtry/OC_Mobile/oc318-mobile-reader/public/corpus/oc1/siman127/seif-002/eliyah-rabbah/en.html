(2) And they shall not bow too much, etc. All in one bowing, etc. Nevertheless Bach and Nachalat Tzvi explained—meaning he nods his head and no more. And so wrote Kesef Mishnah end of chapter 9 of Hilchot Tefillah, and so ruled in Piskei Tosafos. And it is fitting to rely on them and not like the later authorities. However, specifically the congregation who do so only so they not appear as deniers; but the prayer leader bends as required for bending.
