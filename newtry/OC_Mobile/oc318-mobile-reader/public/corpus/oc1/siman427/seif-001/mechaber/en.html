How documents are dated when Rosh Chodesh is 2 days. It contains 1 seif: When Rosh Chodesh is 2 days, on the first day one writes in documents, "on Rosh Chodesh," for it is the 30th day of the preceding month. The new month is counted from the second day, and on that day one writes, "on the first of the month." [Tur] {Rama: In a leap year, in the first Adar one writes simply "Adar," and in the second one writes "Adar Sheni." [Tur]}
