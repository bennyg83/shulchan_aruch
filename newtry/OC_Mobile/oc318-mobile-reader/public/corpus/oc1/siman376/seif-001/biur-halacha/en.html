(א) A hole between two courtyards, and so on, is not even from its deep days

(ב) And it is said that Heaven’s Word is not a blessing, and that Heaven’s Word is not Heaven’s Word and Heaven’s Word, and Heaven’s Word, and Heaven’s Word, and Heaven’s Word, and Heaven’s Word, and Heaven’s Word:

And likewise if they made at the mouth of the pit a beam four handbreadths wide — see Beit Yosef who brought in the name of Rashba that likewise if the partition was four handbreadths thick in width its law is like a roof beam; if so, when they lowered it into the pit and measured ten handbreadths from the lip of the pit downward. Note that the Shulchan Aruch's wording "if they made at the mouth of the pit" is copied from Rambam; in Avodat HaKodesh he wrote if the beam is inside the pit's rim, and so too Rabbenu Yerucham Netiv 12 part 17 — requires study for practice.
