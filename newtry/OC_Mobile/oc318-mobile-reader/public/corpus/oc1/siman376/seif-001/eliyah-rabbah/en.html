[א] Recognizable, etc. And the bucket shall not go to his fellow's domain; and that which we are not concerned to bring in a gutter pipe at the beginning of siman 358 — it is different there since he stands above and pours through the hole straight and we are not concerned it will go outside (Magen Avraham); and the Bach answered that there it is over the majority of water deeper than ten and it is a exempt place; and in truth it appears to me from Shulchan Aruch and Levush that that which requires a tefach in water is in order that there be a recognizable distinction and we are not concerned the bucket will go; and if so in a gutter pipe there is recognition as Tosafos wrote page 87 s.v. between, etc., since the hole is four by four and he makes a partition around the hole; also it appears to me that since there is recognition in this, presumably he will be careful that the bucket not pass outside:
