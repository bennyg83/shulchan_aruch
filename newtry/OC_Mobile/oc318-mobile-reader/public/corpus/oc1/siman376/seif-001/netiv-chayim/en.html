(Taz s.k. 1) And it appears to me that he is mistaken — his words are wondrous, and it appears Tur holds like the view of Tosafos who disagree between Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel in this, for Beit Shammai was particular about the partition and Beit Hillel was particular about the depression; therefore one must make above, and we rule like Beit Hillel.
