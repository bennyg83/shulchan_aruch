And between them three ruins, etc. — Rashi holds specifically when the ruins have no other owners but the courtyard owners are partners in them; if they had other owners, although we hold a hayloft is not a dwelling-house and does not forbid others, nevertheless within it carrying is forbidden and throwing into them would be forbidden. So too Baal HaMaor and Rashba cite Raavad. Tosafos cite R"I disagreeing with Rashi, holding even with other owners it is permitted since it is not a dwelling-house — so too Rosh and Rashba; Tur ruled accordingly; Or Zaru'a in R"I seems to side thus. It is surprising Shulchan Aruch did not state his view; perhaps since he was terse without distinguishing, it implies all cases are permitted. Levush ruled explicitly permitted even when they have owners, and so Gra"z.
