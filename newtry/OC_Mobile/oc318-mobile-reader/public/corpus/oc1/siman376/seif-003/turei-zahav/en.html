And the middle one is permitted to both — since both can use the middle only by throwing, no opening to the ruin; with two domains of use as below, the middle is separated from each by the outer ruin — they do not forbid through air.

And the third ruin adjacent to both courtyards is forbidden to both — although use is by throwing, it is near them with only one domain of use, therefore they forbid through air. Rashi: difficulty from well above where two houses face alley air — answered: ruin has much use near and far; well only drawing by splitting four of air; Rashi's intent clarified.
