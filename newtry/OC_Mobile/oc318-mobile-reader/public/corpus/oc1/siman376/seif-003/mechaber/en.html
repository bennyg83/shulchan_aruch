Two courtyards with three ruins between them: each resident may use the ruin adjacent to his courtyard — through windows, by throwing — and the middle ruin may be used by both. If all three ruins were adjacent to houses such that the middle ruin faced the other two in the arrangement of three points of a tripod, each resident may use the ruin on his own side, but the third ruin — which is close to both courtyards — is forbidden to both.
