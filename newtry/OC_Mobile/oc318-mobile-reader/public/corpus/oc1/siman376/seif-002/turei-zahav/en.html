That one person does not restrict his fellow through airspace — Rambam. One may ask why Rambam in siman 375 forbids a mound ten high for courtyard residents against balcony though use is only by throwing through air. Answer: this applies only to water where use is drawing, and to a ruin without fixed use; a courtyard mound has fixed use.

And some say if they are not separated, etc. — Rashi's view: "one does not forbid through air" only with two domains of use, i.e. use only by throwing or lowering like a well, and requires four handbreadths separation from one's domain; then permitted through air. Difficulty with Rambam on mound in prior siman — Mechaber Chayim answers here specifically pit and ruin; Rashi's view explained in note 3.
