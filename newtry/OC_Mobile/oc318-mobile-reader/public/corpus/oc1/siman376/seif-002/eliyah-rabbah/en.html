[ב] [Levush] It is difficult for me, etc., not specifically, etc., but strained, etc. And in my humble opinion it is not difficult at all, for it is different in a partition, for we do not say it descends and seals as is written in Avodat HaKodesh page 20; therefore it requires in any case a tefach in water; but the mouth of a beam — certainly we say it descends and seals entirely; and so is implied in Shelah Giborim in the name of Riaz and Rabbeinu Yerucham page 95; also in any case it is not difficult at all, for Rashba and R' Yonah wrote the main reason permission in a beam four wide is because the bucket does not go more than four tefachim; therefore one does not draw outside the beam — the same law in a partition four tefachim wide does not require a tefach sunk in water, only in a partition not four wide one must sink or a tefach projecting so the bucket not pass; however to Levush it appears he does not accept this reason as we said:
