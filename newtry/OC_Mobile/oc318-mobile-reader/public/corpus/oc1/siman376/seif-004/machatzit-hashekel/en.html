(s.k. 4) Even though it is, and all the more so when less than four handbreadths — these are words of Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 3 of laws of Eruvin on Rambam's words matching Shulchan Aruch. It may be said: since Rambam holds that even less than four handbreadths is called use through airspace, there is no difference for Rambam between distant four handbreadths or five or six; when not separated more than four handbreadths it is called the courtyard's domain and his fellow cannot forbid through air. Even when separated four handbreadths it is not exclusively the courtyard's and his fellow cannot forbid through air — unlike the view cited in Rama who requires specifically four handbreadths separation. When courtyards open to the path, permitted through windows per Hagahot Maimoniyot — unlike Tur when open to the path even through windows forbidden since weekday use is through openings not air. Per this view Rambam permits even when open to the path. Magen Avraham's "when" may be reconciled with Rama as referring only when not separated four handbreadths; see Turei Zahav and later authorities at length.
