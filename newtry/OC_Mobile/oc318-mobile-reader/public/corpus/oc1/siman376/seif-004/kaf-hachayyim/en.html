4) There. Unless they made a partition, etc. This is so that it is recognizable that the water is divided, with half belonging to these residents and half to those residents. Levush. With this partition we apply the principle that it is viewed as descending to the bottom of the pit, as stated in siman 355. Since the partition provides a recognizable reminder, they will not come to carry utensils that began Shabbos in a house from one courtyard to another elsewhere, in a place unrelated to drawing water. Only with respect to water did the Sages rule leniently that a partition is viewed as descending, for the reason explained there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1.
