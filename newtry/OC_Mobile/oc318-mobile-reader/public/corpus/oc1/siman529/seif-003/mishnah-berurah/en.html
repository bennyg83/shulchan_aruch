{20} He eats and drinks, etc. And even if he mortifies himself for the sake of teshuvah all the days of the year not to eat meat and not to drink wine, on Shabbosos, Yom Tov, Chanukah, and Purim he is obligated to eat and drink (Beis Yaakov, siman 73; and see the Meiri what he wrote on this matter at the beginning of chapter 2 of Beitzah).
{21} In which there is service of the Creator. And the practice of the chassidim, before whom Hashem is always present, and in all their ways they know Him: at the time of their joy they all the more praise the Holy One, blessed be He, Who has made them rejoice (Tur; see there).
