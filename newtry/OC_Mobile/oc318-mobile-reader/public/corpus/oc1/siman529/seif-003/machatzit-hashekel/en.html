(s.k. 3) Upon the wine, etc. See there in Tosafos for the reason. And the reason for Pesach is that it is a day of redemption and freedom. And for Atzeres: because even according to Rabbi Elazar in Pesachim 68b, who disagrees with Rabbi Yehoshua and holds that it depends on a person's will: if he wishes, the whole day may be for Hashem, namely to learn or to pray the whole day, or entirely "for you," namely to eat the whole day, they conclude there that regarding Atzeres everyone agrees that we also require "for you," namely that one must eat and rejoice. And on Rosh Hashana, because it is the beginning of the year, they do so for a good omen. And Shemini Atzeres, because it is important and it is a festival unto itself. But the first day of Yom Tov of Sukkos is not [included], because the people are busy with the mitzvos of sukkah and the four species and do not have leisure to prepare much.
