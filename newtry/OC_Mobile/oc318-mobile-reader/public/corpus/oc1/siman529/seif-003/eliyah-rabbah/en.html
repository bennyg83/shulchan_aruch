[3] And they did not customarily, etc. However, when Yom Tov falls on Shabbos they do customarily make [a third meal]. Magen Avraham wrote: and one is obligated to eat bread on Yom Tov at night and in the morning; and if he did not eat on the night of Yom Tov, he should eat twice during the day; and the same applies that he says the nighttime kiddush; see siman 491, se'if 1.
