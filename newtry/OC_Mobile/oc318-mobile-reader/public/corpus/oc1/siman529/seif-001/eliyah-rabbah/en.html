[1] And it is forbidden, etc. The wording of the Rambam, chapter 6, and the Kol Bo: and it is proper not to make a meal. And Magen Avraham wrote: and the same applies on the first day of Yom Tov, because it is erev of the second day of Yom Tov, end quote. The Kol Bo on folio 58 wrote that the prohibition of doing melacha on erev Yom Tov is like on erev Shabbos; see siman 249. Nevertheless they do not give him makkos mardus, and it goes without saying that they do not place him in niddui, except for erev Pesach; see siman 468.
