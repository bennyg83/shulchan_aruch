The laws of rejoicing on Yom Tov. It contains 4 seifim: The mitzvah of Yom Tov is to divide it, half for the beit midrash and half for eating and drinking. One should not be sparing with Yom Tov expenses, and one must honor and enjoy it as on Shabbos. {Rama: It is forbidden to eat from Minchah onward on the eve of Yom Tov, as on the eve of Shabbos, because this is part of honoring the day. [Rambam, chapter 6] However, if the eve of Yom Tov is Shabbos, he may fulfill seudah shlishit, but should eat only a little bread in honor of Yom Tov. It is a mitzvah to knead bread on the eve of Yom Tov in honor of Yom Tov, as on the eve of Shabbos, as explained above in siman 242. [Maharil, Laws of Yom Tov]} One is obligated to break bread over 2 loaves and to establish every meal over wine. Yom Tov clothing should be finer than Shabbos clothing. The custom is not to eat seudah shlishit on Yom Tov. On Yom Tov they come later to the synagogue and leave earlier because of the rejoicing of Yom Tov. [Gemara, chapter HaKoreh Omed]
