(seif 1) The mitzvah of Yom Tov to divide it, etc. NB: Behold, by law whether one is obligated to immerse oneself on the regel — on this he was lengthy in responsum 1 that it is not practiced in our time. In my humble opinion it is an explicit gemara in Berachot chapter 3 daf 22b that Rav Chama would immerse every erev Pesach day and the halachah is not like him; and it is difficult — for the halachah follows Rabbah, and nevertheless without a vow one requires immersion near the regel from the power of the festival; and see Chak Yosef in what he wrote that a person is obligated to purify himself on the regel, meaning near the regel; and if so it is difficult — for behold without a vow one requires immersion near the regel from the power of the festival; and even per the Tzelach that the question is from the blessing — behold for festival immersion one is also obligated in a blessing, and necessarily it is proven that in our time this obligation does not apply. And behold if we say not like the Redak — that even for a d'rabbanan mitzvah one also requires intent — it fits well that we can say there is a difference regarding intent: for one who lacks intent does not fulfill and it is a blessing in vain; and it requires study.
