{1} The mitzvah of Yom Tov is to divide it, etc. - For in one verse it is written, "an assembly shall be for you," and in another verse, "an assembly for Hashem your God." Therefore Chazal said one must divide it: half for Hashem, namely Torah and prayer, and half for you, namely eating and drinking for the enjoyment of Yom Tov. Therefore one should rebuke chazzanim who extend more than half the day. The poskim wrote that this is the proper way: in the morning they go early to the synagogue and batei midrash, pray, read in the Torah about the matter of the day, pray Mussaf, return home and eat, then go to the batei midrash and study until midday. Afterward, when the time for Minchah arrives, they pray Minchah and delight in the joy of Yom Tov for the rest of the day until night, in order to fulfill half for you. See below in seif 3.
{2} And he should not be exacting, etc. - For a person's sustenance and expenses are fixed for him from Rosh Hashanah, except for expenses of Shabbatot and Yamim Tovim: if he reduces, they reduce for him, and if he adds, they add for him, as the Gemara says.
{3} And one must honor it - Meaning washing in hot water on the eve of Yom Tov as on the eve of Shabbat; see above siman 260. Also he should wear clean clothing and not wear weekday garments, as regarding Shabbat; see above siman 262 seif 1 and 2. The same applies to Yom Tov. See siman 250, that every person must exert himself personally to prepare something for Shabbat even if he has servants; the same applies to Yom Tov.
{4} As on Shabbat - For it is written, "You shall call Shabbat a delight, the holy of Hashem honored," and regarding Yamim Tovim it says "a holy convocation." Also in Torat Kohanim it says: "a holy convocation" - sanctify it; and with what do you sanctify it? With food, drink, and clean clothing.
{5} And it is forbidden to eat, etc. - This wording is not precise, for even on the eve of Shabbat it is not a prohibition, but a mitzvah to refrain from establishing a meal from nine hours onward, as above in siman 249; see there.
{6} From Minchah onward - This means from the time of Minchah ketanah, as written earlier.
{7} For this is part of honor - So that he will eat on Yom Tov night with appetite.
{8} Can fulfill - Meaning, even if he forgot or transgressed and did not fulfill seudah shelishit before the time of Minchah ketanah, he can fulfill it afterward. But then, initially, it is a mitzvah to be careful to eat only a little bread so that he will eat at night with appetite. The same applies when the first day of Yom Tov falls on Shabbat; all these matters apply.
{9} And he is obligated to break bread - At the two meals that he eats on Yom Tov, as below.
{10} Over two loaves - For on Yom Tov too the manna did not fall, as on Shabbat, and on the eve of Yom Tov a double portion fell.
{11} Over wine - This means also during the meal, besides what he initially recited Kiddush over wine, as regarding Shabbat and as above at the end of siman 250. It is also a mitzvah to eat meat, all if he can afford it; see above siman 242. It is stated in Avodah Zarah 5 that they enacted increasing the meal on the first Yom Tov of Pesach, on Shavuot, on Rosh Hashanah, and on the last Yom Tov of the Festival.
{12} And Yom Tov garments, etc. - Because one is obligated in joy, and this too is included in joy. It is obvious that all this too is when he can afford it.
{13} And they were not accustomed, etc., to a third - Only two meals, evening and morning, and one is obligated to eat bread at them. If he did not eat on Yom Tov night, he should eat twice during the day and recite in the morning the Kiddush of night, as regarding Shabbat in siman 271 seif 8 and siman 291 seif 1. Some Acharonim wrote that although they are not accustomed to make a third meal with bread, nevertheless it is good to eat fruit. Some wrote that from this spread the custom to eat one additional cooked dish on Yom Tov morning, so it counts in place of a meal, although on Shabbat this does not help regarding seudah shelishit.
{14} They come later, etc. - In order to trouble themselves with the Yom Tov meal before going to the synagogue. Nowadays, when they prolong with piyyutim and melodies between Yotzer Or and Keriat Shema, and it is almost certain that the time for Keriat Shema will pass, one must rise early on every Yom Tov in order to recite Keriat Shema at the proper time.
