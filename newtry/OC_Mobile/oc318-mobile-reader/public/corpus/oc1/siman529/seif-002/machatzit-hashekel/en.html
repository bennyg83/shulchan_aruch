(s.k. 2) However, etc. He goes according to his own view, etc.; that is to say: according to his view there is no option other than to permit eating after its forbidden time, namely after the time of Mincha; therefore he was permitted only a little bread, namely a little more than an egg's volume, which is the measure of the third meal, as is written at the beginning of siman 291.
But we, etc. Until Mincha Ketanah, as is written in siman 249. And even though regarding erev Sukkos he wrote in siman 239, se'if 3, in the gloss, that one should not eat from midday onward (see there for the reason), nevertheless here he is certainly not speaking of erev Sukkos, for the Shulchan Aruch is dealing with erev of the first day of Yom Tov.
And it is impossible for Shabbos to fall on erev Sukkos, for if so the first day of Sukkos would be on Sunday. And we hold "lo ADU Rosh." And also regarding erev Pesach (and see Chok Yaakov, siman 471), it likewise cannot be under discussion, for then it is impossible to fulfill the third meal with bread, as is written in siman 444. Necessarily, then, he is speaking specifically of erev Shavuos. And upon this the Rama wrote: and it is forbidden to eat, etc., as on erev Shabbos. Therefore Magen Avraham asked: regarding erev Shabbos it is explained in siman 249 that it is permitted until the time of Mincha Ketanah. And if so, one can eat the third meal at a permitted time, namely after the time of Mincha Gedolah and before the time of Mincha Ketanah, and then one can eat more than a little bread.
See siman 291. And there Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 8 that if Yom Tov falls on Motza'ei Shabbos, nevertheless if he knows that he will be able to eat before the tenth hour, he should not split the morning meal into two in order to fulfill thereby the third meal, as is written there. And so it appears from siman 188, which wrote that if he forgot Yaaleh VeYavo in Birkas HaMazon: if it is Rosh Chodesh he need not go back and recite Birkas HaMazon, for if he wished he need not have eaten bread; but if he forgot Yaaleh VeYavo in Birkas HaMazon on Yom Tov, he must go back and recite the blessing, for on Yom Tov one is obligated to eat bread. And he stated it without distinguishing between the night meal and the day meal; hence both by day and by night one needs bread.
He should eat twice during the day, as is written in siman 291. And there Magen Avraham wrote that in such a case he should recite by day the nighttime kiddush, for he did not recite kiddush at night; and even if he did recite kiddush at night, he did not fulfill his obligation, for there is no kiddush except in the place of the meal. And so he wrote here in the Sefer Eliyah Rabbah.
