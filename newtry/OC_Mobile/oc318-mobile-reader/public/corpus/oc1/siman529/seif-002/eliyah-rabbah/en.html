[2] However, if erev Yom Tov falls, etc. Magen Avraham wrote: this is from the words of the Maharil, and he goes according to his own view, for he holds that it is forbidden to eat on erev Shabbos and erev Yom Tov from Mincha Gedolah onward; but we, etc., end quote. And it is astonishing that he did not look in the Maharil himself, Hilchos Yom Tov, where it is explicit that he is referring to Mincha Ketanah: namely, if he forgot and did not make [the meal] beforehand, he should make it after Mincha Ketanah with a little bread, a kezayis; but before Mincha Ketanah he may make [a meal] and eat as he wishes. And so he wrote in Responsa Maharil, siman 99. It also seems obvious to me that this is the intent of the Rama and the Levush here: for [he says] from Mincha, etc., as on Shabbos, and in siman 249 they wrote that there is no prohibition except from the ninth hour. Yet the Maharil's words contradict each other regarding what he wrote that on erev Yom Tov they are forbidden from Mincha Gedolah. One may say that regarding the mitzvah of three meals he is lenient with the majority of the poskim. One may further say that this [ruling] of erev Yom Tov is in Hilchos Sukkah and is not marked as from the Maharil; if so, it appears that it is from another posek. And more will be explained in siman 649, s.k.
