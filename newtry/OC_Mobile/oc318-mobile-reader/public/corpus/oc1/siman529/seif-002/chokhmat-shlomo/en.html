There in that seif: and he must feed the convert, orphan, and widow with the other poor. Note: here he hinted to what Scripture wrote in Kohelet: on a good day be among the good, and on a bad day see — for God made one opposite the other. It appears the intent is: God commanded in Torah "you shall rejoice on your festival," etc., "and your poor who are in your land."
