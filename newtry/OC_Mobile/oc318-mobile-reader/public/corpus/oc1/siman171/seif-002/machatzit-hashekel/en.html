Seif katan 2: A person should not sit, etc. Even if it contains books, namely when they are covered and he sits upon the covering. In Yoreh Deah, etc., it is permitted; see there in Nekudos HaKesef.
See what I wrote in siman 240, that if the vessel is large enough to hold forty seah of liquid, it loses the status of a vessel regarding impurity. The same applies here: it is not regarded as a vessel, and one may sit upon it even if it contains books, unlike Responsa Rama, which expressed uncertainty about this.
