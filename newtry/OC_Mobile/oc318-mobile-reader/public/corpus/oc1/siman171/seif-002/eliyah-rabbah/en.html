[3] A basket, etc. Bach writes that this applies specifically to one made of leather, goatskin, or the like, where the basket bends. With a wooden basket that does not bend, it is permitted to sit upon it even if it contains sacred books. In seif 7 he prohibits sitting upon a chest containing books unless it is attached to the wall with nails. In Nekudos HaKesef, siman 282, he permits it. Nevertheless, Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah writes that the practice is to be careful about this.
