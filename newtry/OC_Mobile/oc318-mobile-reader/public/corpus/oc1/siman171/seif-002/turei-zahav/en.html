A basket full of figs. It appears to me that this applies even if the basket is wooden, as is the ordinary meaning of basket in the Talmud. Nevertheless, the figs are crushed when he sits upon the full, uncovered basket. If it is covered, however, it appears to be permitted. If it contains books, it is prohibited even when covered, as I wrote in Yoreh Deah, siman 283. My father-in-law, of blessed memory, writes that if it is wooden one may sit upon it even when it contains books, and he limits the case here to a leather basket. This does not appear correct to me.
