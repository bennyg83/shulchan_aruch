Box—specifically box of Christians that bends and foods are repugnant. But box of wood that does not bend, even if sefarim inside, permitted to sit on them, and certainly food Bach. In Rama teshuvah wrote forbidden to sit on chest with sefarim inside; and Yoreh Deah siman 282 Taz wrote if fixed in wall permitted, and so practiced in beit haknesset; see teshuvat Ohel Yaakov siman 18.
