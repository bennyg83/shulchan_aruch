Basket. Specifically a basket of reeds, which bends and the foods become repulsive. But a wooden basket that does not bend - even if there were sefarim inside it - it is permitted to sit on them, and all the more so on foods; Bach. And in a responsa of the Rama he wrote that it is forbidden to sit on the chest that has sefarim inside it; and in Yoreh Deah siman 282 the Taz wrote that if it is fixed in the wall it is permitted, and so is the practice in the beit haknesset; and see responsa Ohel Yaakov siman 18.
