Seif 2. A person should not sit upon a basket full of figs. Note: See Magen Avraham, seif katan 2, who writes that this applies specifically to a basket that bends, etc. See a proof for this in tractate Taharos, chapter 5, mishnah 2: Rabbi Yose says that if one handed him a basket full of garments, if it creates a barrier it is impure through direct pressure, and if it does not create a barrier it is impure through indirect contact. See there and examine carefully, for this is a clear proof for Magen Avraham’s words.
