A person should not sit. This applies specifically to a wicker basket that bends, causing the food to become crushed and repulsive. With a wooden basket that does not bend, one may sit upon it even if it contains books, and certainly if it contains food. Bach. Responsa Rama states that one may not sit upon a chest containing books. In Yoreh Deah, siman 282, Taz writes that if it is attached to the wall, it is permitted, and this is the synagogue practice.
