A basket. See Baer Heitev and Nekudos HaKesef in Yoreh Deah there. See also my grandfather in Bechor Shor, Eruvin 30, who challenges Taz from Rashi in Gittin 5b and Tosafos in Chagigah 25a. See there. He writes that one who relies upon Bach’s view has lost nothing, even when sitting in a wagon or upon a small chest, provided that it is not designated exclusively for the books. All the more so when the chest also contains other objects, for then it is not subordinate to the books, similar to what Rashi writes in Berachos 23a, s.v. ella. With a Sefer Torah, however, one should be stringent. These are his words. See what I cited above in Shaarei Teshuvah, siman 40, from Responsa Ohel Yaakov.
