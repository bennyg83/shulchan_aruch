(15) In place of a spoon: Because a person may use bread for his needs where it does not become repulsive, as stated above. Here it does not become repulsive to the person himself who ate the porridge with it.
(16) Provided that he eats it, etc.: For another person it would be repulsive, and this would constitute wasting food.
(17) And those who are meticulous, etc.: They are concerned that using food as a utensil is degrading. They therefore eat a little of the bread each time, so that it appears as though they are using the bread to accompany the food.
(18) They afterward eat it: For to others it would be repulsive, as stated above.
