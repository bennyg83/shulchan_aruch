A basket See Taz regarding a wooden basket that is covered. If it contains books, Bach permits sitting upon it, while Taz in Yoreh Deah 282, seif katan 4, prohibits it unless it is attached to the wall with nails. His view there is that even a large chest holding forty seah is prohibited. See Magen Avraham, siman 240, seif katan 19, and what I wrote in siman 40 regarding tefillin inside a double covering. See there. Eliyah Rabbah here, in the name of Nekudos HaKesef, permits sitting upon a wooden chest that is covered. I do not presently have Nekudos HaKesef before me. If it contains a Sefer Torah, it appears that it is prohibited, and with Hashem’s help this will be explained. I later saw that Kenesses HaGedolah, Eruvin 30, concludes that a small chest is permitted if it is not designated for the books, or if it also contains other objects. With a Sefer Torah it is prohibited. Sleeping upon a chest containing books is prohibited. If it holds forty seah, it may be permitted. With Hashem’s help this will be explained in siman 240.
