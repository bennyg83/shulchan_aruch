Even washing that is not, etc. My father-in-law, of blessed memory, asks why this differs from the permitted practice of anointing the hands in order to remove filth. He answers that there it is an enactment of Chazal for the sanctity of Bircas HaMazon, which they associated with the verse, “I am Hashem,” referring to fragrant oil. For the same reason, mayim acharonim may be performed with wine and other beverages, because they remove filth, as stated in siman 181.
And one may not throw bread. Beit Yosef explains that this is prohibited even if the bread does not become repulsive, unlike other foods, for which the prohibition applies specifically when they become repulsive.
