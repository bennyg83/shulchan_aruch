1) [Seif 1] A person may make use of bread, provided that he does not render it repulsive, etc. If he does so for medicinal purposes, even something that renders it repulsive is permitted. Shabbos 108b; Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman. Magen Avraham writes further there that anything needed by a person, even if it is not medicinal, does not constitute a degradation of food. For this reason, one may wash the hands with beverages when water is unavailable, as stated in siman 160, seif 12; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, writes the same. See below, paragraph 4.
