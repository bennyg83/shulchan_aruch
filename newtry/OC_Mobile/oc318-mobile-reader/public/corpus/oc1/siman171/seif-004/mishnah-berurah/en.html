(19) They draw wine through pipes: This is done as a good sign that their tranquility and prosperity should continue. The case concerns a wooden pipe, through which the wine does not become repulsive.
(20) Provided that he collects it: Otherwise, it constitutes wasting food.
(21) Because they become repulsive: Although nuts have shells and the food within does not become repulsive, during the rainy season there is mud and mire, and they become dirty there, making them repulsive to pick up and causing a loss of food. In some places the present custom is to throw raisins before the groom on the Shabbos before his wedding, when he is called for maftir. This is improper, for raisins are soft and become repulsive thereby, as explained above.
(22) Not cakes: As explained above in seif 1.
