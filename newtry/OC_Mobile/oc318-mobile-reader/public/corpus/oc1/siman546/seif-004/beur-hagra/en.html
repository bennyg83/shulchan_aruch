Se'if 4. It is permitted, etc. Needless to say regarding milah, which is not called joy, as is written in the first chapter of Kesubos; but even pidyon haben: for nothing is called joy except a meal of a woman [wedding meal]; and so the Gemara explains from what Rav Ashi said "and not with your wife"; and it is implied that they do not disagree in the [practical] law.
