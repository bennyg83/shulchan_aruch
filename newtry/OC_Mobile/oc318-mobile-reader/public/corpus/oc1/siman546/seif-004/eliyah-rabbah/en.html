[4] [Levush] To make the meal, etc., at night, etc. So wrote the Beis Yosef, and so it appears from the Rivash; and this is to exclude the Taz, who wrote that a first meal is forbidden on the festival, and that so it appears from the Rivash in the Beis Yosef, end quote; for it is astonishing that he reverses the words of the Beis Yosef. And behold, Magen Avraham also permitted making the first meal on the festival, only forbidding on the first night, which is drawn after the day; and therefore in our lands they are not accustomed to marry on erev Yom Tov at all. However, as to Magen Avraham's wonder at the Beis Yosef, who wrote that these are rabbinic matters, whereas Tosafos in Moed Katan folio 9 proved that it is a genuine derashah, and so wrote Tosafos in Kesubos folio 47, end quote: and to my humble opinion there is no difficulty at all; nissuin and a meal together are forbidden according to the Torah, as I wrote above, but a meal alone or nissuin alone is only rabbinic according to the one who forbids; therefore it appears that in a time of pressing need one may rely to permit. And further, in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah he wrote that they are accustomed to marry on erev Yom Tov close to nightfall; and see what the Taz wrote in the name of Responsa Mas'as Binyamin in Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 399.
