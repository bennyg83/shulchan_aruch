d) Name. And it is permitted to betroth, etc., lest we be preceded by another in mercy. Gemara there 18b. And per this reason all the more it is permitted to write conditions for matchmaking abroad as is customary among us. However requires study regarding the meal they are accustomed to make after writing the conditions — whether this is in place of the meal of betrothal and forbidden as written shortly, or we say this meal is for betrothal meal after he already betrothed; then specifically there applies one may not mix joy with joy. It appears to me forbidden. Taz s.k. 2 — see there. Beur Halacha 11 in Hagahot Taz. However HaRav Our Teacher Gimmel wrote that it implies from Rivash's words siman 200 that nowadays when they do not betroth before the chuppah it is permitted to eat the first meal after the conditions; and he wrote on Taz's words that it is not clear — see there.
