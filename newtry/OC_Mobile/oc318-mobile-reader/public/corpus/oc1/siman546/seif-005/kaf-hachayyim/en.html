5) There. At the time of betrothal. This means that the betrothal should not be performed together with a meal. If a meal is held after the betrothal, it is permitted, even on the same day. Magen Avraham, subsection 2; Levushei Serad. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 3, likewise writes that the prohibition applies only 'at the time of betrothal,' meaning betrothal together with a meal. Taz, subsection 2, however, holds that even if the match was arranged and the conditions were written before the festival, the first meal after writing the conditions may not be held in the bride's house during the festival. See there. Mishnah Berurah in Shaar HaTziyun, subsection 5, however, writes that even a full meal is forbidden specifically when held at the time the conditions are written. If it is not held at that time, there is no reason to be stringent, unlike Taz. Regarding betrothal, Mishnah Berurah, subsection 4, writes that when the betrothal is performed on one day and the meal on another, it is certainly permitted. This is allowed even regarding marriage, as stated below, se'if 3. See there. See below, subsection 14.
