5. A woman can apply makeup on the holiday. She may paint her eyelids, rouge (Meaning she parts her hair according to Rashi), apply paint to her face, apply lime on the body, and similar things. These are permitted providing she will be able to remove it on the holiday. She may remove her armpit and pubic hair, both by hand and with a tool.
