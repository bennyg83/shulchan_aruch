(Magen Avraham s.k. 1) And it appears to me that there is no difficulty here, for..., etc. Note: and it is difficult against his words: firstly, according to him it is established for Tosafos as halacha like the one who says we do not mix [joy with joy], and this is against Tosafos in Moed Katan folio 9, s.v. "because," etc. And further, that Tosafos are uncertain whether one does a pidyon haben on the moed; and according to how he explains "and you shall rejoice" as [referring to] a meal that you make for the need of the festival with meat and wine, and not with your wife: in that manner one should also exclude a meal of pidyon haben. And it appears to resolve the difficulty of Maharshal: that this opinion of "on your festival and not with your wife" also holds we do not mix joy with joy, and one does not need the derashah of "on your festival" except to forbid nissuin without a meal. And so it is implied in Peri Chadash, Orach Chaim, siman 688, that according to R' Elazar as well he holds we do not mix; and according to this, Maharshal's difficulty is resolved: for there [the Gemara] asks against the one who says we do not mix joy with joy, for we do not find that anyone disagrees with Rav there. And the beraisa below on folio 19 comes according to the one who says "on your festival and not with your wife," and according to him nissuin without a meal is forbidden. And one should not pose a difficulty from this beraisa against the one who says we do not mix, for that opinion holds like this beraisa in the first chapter of Chagigah folio 8, which expounded "on your festival" to exclude birds and menachos.
