(Magen Avraham note 1) And it appears to me for what reason this etc. — NB and his words are difficult: one, that according to him Tosafot hold the halacha is like the one who says one does not mix, and this is against Tosafot in Moed Katan daf 9 s.v. mippenei etc. And furthermore that Tosafot are uncertain whether they perform pidyon haben on the festival. And according to what he explains "and you shall rejoice" in the feast that you make for festival needs — meat and wine and not in your wife — according to this one could also minimize the feast of pidyon haben. And it appears to resolve Maharshal's question: this one who says "in your festival and not in your wife" also holds one does not mix joy with joy, and it is not necessary to expound "in your festival" except to forbid marriage without a feast, and so in chapter 8 Orach Chaim siman 488 that R' Eliezer also holds one does not mix. And accordingly Maharshal's question is resolved: there he challenged the one who says one does not mix joy with joy that we do not find one disputes another against Rav there, and the baraita below daf 19 follows the one who says "in your festival and not in your wife," and according to him marriage without a feast is forbidden, and one should not challenge from this baraita to the one who says one does not mix — for this one holds like this baraita in the first chapter of Chagigah daf 8 that expounds "in your festival" to exclude birds and meal offerings.
