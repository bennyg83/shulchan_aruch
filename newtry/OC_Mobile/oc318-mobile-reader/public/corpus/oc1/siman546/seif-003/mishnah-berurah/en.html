(2) (j) And to have a feast on foot - that the main joy is not the beginning of the marriage, and so on, who rejoices at the marriage feast for all seven sheris, and it is said that if it is night, a sherri should make a feast on that other day, and refer to the MA, his thought is that this night is followed by the day, and it is forbidden to make the first feast at night, and for this reason it was written that they used not to carry it on the eve of the Hajj.
