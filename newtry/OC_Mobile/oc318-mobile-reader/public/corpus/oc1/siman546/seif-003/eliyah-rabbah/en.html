[3] Not at the time of erusin, etc. For it is forbidden only at the time of erusin, meaning erusin with a meal (Rivash); and it is implied there that nowadays, when they do not effect kiddushin before the chuppah, it is permitted to eat a first meal after the tena'im; and the Taz forbids, and it is not clear. And as to what he compares to milah, that were it not that its time is fixed it would be forbidden: and to my humble opinion there is no difficulty at all; it is not comparable at all, for regarding milah there is joy of a mitzvah, and here there is no joy of a mitzvah until erusin. Also regarding milah and pidyon haben there are reasons that one does not recite "shehasimcha bime'ono," as he wrote in the minhagim mentioned above; and at a meal after the tena'im no one would come to mind to recite [that blessing], and those reasons do not apply; and this requires further study regarding a meal of erusin.
