They are exempt from prayer. Prayer specifically requires standing, and they cannot delay that long, as stated by Beit Yosef and Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah. It is difficult, for they could pray while walking, just as one traveling prays. It may be answered that while walking they are occupied with a mitzvah and are exempt from prayer. Nevertheless, they are obligated in Krias Shema because it can be recited easily. See siman 38.
