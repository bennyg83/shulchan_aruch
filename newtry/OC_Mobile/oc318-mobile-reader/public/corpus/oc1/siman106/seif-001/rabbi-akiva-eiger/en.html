Magen Avraham, note 2 — they did not obligate them more. The Ramban in responsa Shamayim Rosh, siman 89, that women are exempt from musaf prayer, since it comes only to remember the obligation of sacrifices. A woman did not weigh the half-shekel, and she has no portion in communal sacrifices. Rather, they have already accustomed themselves to pray everything and obligated themselves in all the mitzvot — end quote. Also in Chiddushei Tzelach on Berachos 20, that women are exempt from musaf prayer because it is time-limited; see there.
