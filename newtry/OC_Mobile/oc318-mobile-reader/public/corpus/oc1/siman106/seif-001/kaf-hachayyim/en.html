1) [Seif 1] All those who are exempt from Krias Shema, such as those engaged in a mitzvah or preoccupied with a mitzvah. Tur and Levush, as explained above in simanim 70, 71, and 72; see there.
2) There: They are exempt from prayer, for since they are exempt from Krias Shema, which is D'Oraisa, they are certainly exempt from prayer, which is rabbinic. Beis Yosef; Levush; Prishah, paragraph 1; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. See below, paragraph 7.
3) There: Except for those carrying the deceased, etc. This is the opinion of Rambam in chapter 6 of Hilchos Tefillah. The Tur, however, implies that only those whose services are needed for the bier are exempt, whereas the other bearers of the deceased are obligated even in prayer, as explained in Beis Yosef. So also wrote Ateres Zekenim, paragraph 1. The Levush, however, follows the words of the Mechaber, of blessed memory, as does the Bach; so also wrote the later authorities: Olas Tamid, paragraph 2; Soles Belulah; Mekor Chaim, paragraph 1; Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1; Machatzis HaShekel, paragraph 1; and Beis Oved, paragraph 45.
