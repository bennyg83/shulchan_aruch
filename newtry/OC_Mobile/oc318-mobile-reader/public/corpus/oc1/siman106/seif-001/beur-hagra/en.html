Seif 1: All who are exempt, etc. This is certainly so regarding Krias Shema, which is D’Oraisa. See Beit Yosef, and see Rashi there, 17b, s.v. “and these,” etc.
Except, etc. This is the Mishnah there, according to Rambam’s explanation that the statement “when the bier has no need,” etc., refers to those accompanying it, as explained above in siman 72, seif 1.
