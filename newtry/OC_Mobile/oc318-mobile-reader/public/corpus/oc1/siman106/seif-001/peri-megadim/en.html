Exempt. The citation is apt afterward regarding escorts, etc., as Rashi explains at the beginning of the mishnah; and he omitted the reason of Rashba, that exempt implies even if the time for prayer passes, as Rashi explains there — and he prays while walking, as Magen Avraham wrote in note 1, and see siman 94 in Taz note 5. If the time for prayer passed, we come to the dispute of the Taz and Prisha 14, Magen Avraham, and Magen Avraham siman 93 note 5, from this.
