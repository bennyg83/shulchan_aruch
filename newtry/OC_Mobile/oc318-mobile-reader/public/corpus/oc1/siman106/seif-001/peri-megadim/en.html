They are exempt. See Taz. The citation should properly follow the words “those accompanying,” etc., according to Rashi’s explanation at the beginning of the chapter “One whose dead lies before him.” The reason given by Rabbeinu Yonah was omitted. The word “exempt” implies that they are exempt even if the time for prayer will pass, according to Rashi there, rather than praying while walking, as Magen Avraham writes in section 1. See Taz, siman 94, section 5. If the time for prayer passed, this enters the dispute between Taz and Perishah in Yoreh Deah 341. See Magen Avraham, siman 93, section 5, regarding this.
