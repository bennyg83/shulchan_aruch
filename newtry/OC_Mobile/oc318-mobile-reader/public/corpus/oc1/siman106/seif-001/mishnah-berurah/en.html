(1) From Kriat Shema — because they are engaged in a mitzvah, or they are preoccupied with the preoccupation of a mitzvah, as explained in siman 70, note 3.

(2) Needed for them — meaning they are not at all ready to help carry the bier; for otherwise they are exempt also from Kriat Shema, as above in siman 72, note 1; see there.

(3) From prayer — since it is rabbinic, and furthermore it requires standing and they cannot remain so long. But Kriat Shema — its main intention, and its standing, are only for the first verse; and one can easily stand and have intention. If the time for prayer passed because of this, one need not complete the adjacent prayer, since at the time of his obligation he was exempt by law, as below at the end of siman 93; see there in Mishna Berurah.
