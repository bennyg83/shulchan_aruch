(1) From Krias Shema: Because they are occupied with a mitzvah or preoccupied with the concerns of a mitzvah, as explained in siman 70, seif 3.
(2) There is no need for them: Meaning, they are not prepared at all to assist in carrying the bier. Otherwise they are exempt even from Krias Shema, as explained above in siman 72, seif 1. See there.
(3) From prayer: Since it is Rabbinic, and additionally requires standing, they cannot delay that long. Krias Shema, however, requires concentration and standing primarily for the first verse, and they can easily stand and concentrate for it. If the time for prayer passes because of this, they need not make it up at the following prayer, since at the time of their obligation they were legally exempt, as explained above at the end of siman 93. See the Mishnah Berurah there.
