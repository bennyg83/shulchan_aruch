Shulchan Aruch seif 3 in Rama — and if the hour does not pass. This applies also to one who studies alone; and it is from the Ran who wrote that what R' Yochanan said, "such as we stop" between Keriat Shema and between tefillah — applies when there is no leisure; for if there is leisure, why stop? And is it forbidden to study before tefillah? End quote — and thereby understand the words of Magen Avraham.
