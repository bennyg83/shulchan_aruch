(Note 2) The commandment, etc. — "to serve Him with all your heart." And Chazal expounded: what service is in the heart? This is tefillah, whose essence is in the heart (and see above in Magen Avraham siman 101). And Rambam holds that this exposition is complete; and Ramban holds, etc., that he holds this exposition is merely an asmakhta.

And see in Tosafos Berachos 20b on what we taught there that women are obligated in tefillah. The Talmud challenges: it is obvious — is it not a positive commandment whose time does not cause? And it answers: since it is written "evening and morning and noon I speak and moan," it is like a time-caused positive commandment. It teaches us that they are obligated because they are supplications. And Rashi erased that version and reads instead: women are obligated in tefillah because they are supplications, and nothing more. And Tosafos wrote the reason he erased it — for tefillah is rabbinic, and what positive commandment applies to it? And Tosafos wrote: nevertheless it can be settled, for Hallel is rabbinic and women are exempt from a time-caused positive commandment — end quote; thus it appears from the words of Rashi and Tosafos that tefillah is rabbinic.
