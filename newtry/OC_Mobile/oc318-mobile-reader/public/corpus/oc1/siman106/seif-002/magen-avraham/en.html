A positive commandment. Rambam writes this because he maintains that prayer is a positive Torah commandment, as it is written, “To serve Him with all your heart,” etc. On a Torah level, however, once a day is sufficient, in any text one wishes. Therefore, most women have customarily not prayed the Amidah regularly, because immediately in the morning, close to washing their hands, they recite some request, and on a Torah level this suffices. Perhaps the Sages also did not obligate them further. Ramban maintains that prayer is Rabbinic, and this is the view of most authorities. See Tosafos, Berachos 20b, at the beginning of the page. Semak writes that there is a mitzvah to pray in a time of distress.
One must educate them. Nevertheless, one may give them food before prayer, as written in siman 269. See also siman 343.
