Positive mitzvah. So writes the Rambam, who holds that prayer is a positive mitzvah by Torah law, as it is written, "and to serve Him with all your hearts," etc. But by Torah law once a day suffices, in any wording one wishes. Therefore most women who do not pray regularly, since they say immediately in the morning near netilat yadayim some request — by Torah law this suffices. It is possible that the sages also did not obligate them more. The Ramban holds prayer is rabbinic, and so is the view of most poskim. See Tosafos Berachos 20, beginning of 2b, and Semak, who writes that one should pray in a time of trouble.

Obligated to educate them. Nevertheless one may give them to eat before prayer, as written in siman 269 and siman 343.
