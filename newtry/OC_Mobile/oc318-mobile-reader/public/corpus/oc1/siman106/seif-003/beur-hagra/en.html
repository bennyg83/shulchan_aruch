Siman 63 — and if, etc. — and nevertheless, etc. — Berachos 13b.

And if there is no, etc. — Tosafos on Shabbos there s.v. kegon, etc.; and so too Ra'avad — certainly he is not lesser than a meal and other matters there; and so is implied in Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Berachos; and there is no need for R' Shimon who learns in order to do and not who learns in order not to do — that one who learns in order not to do, etc. — this is his wording, etc. — see there.
