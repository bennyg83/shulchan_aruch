And if he teaches others, he does not stop; he does not even stop for Keriat Shema — and specifically if their study would be nullified when he stops; but if it would not be nullified, he must stop (Beit Yosef siman 70). Its proper place is in siman 61, only I did not wish to leave out part of the page — I wrote what I found in the Beit Yosef in Orach Chayyim siman 61 cited above, which is found written in Sefer HaPeli'ah: "I am Heaven your Heaven, emes" — there was in Egypt one who practiced according to his words and all the great ones of the generation rebuked him, etc. In truth, Maharshal in a responsum wrote that when he prayed year-round he would repeat "I am Heaven your Heaven" — not the word emes that is not from Keriat Shema — to complete the words of Keriat Shema. And Mateh Moshe agreed with Maharshal's view, for although the Beit Yosef brought from three places in the Zohar to say "Heaven your Heaven emes," that is the view of the chevrah of Rashbi; but Rashbi's own view in the Zohar is to say "I am Heaven your Heaven emes," and thus: "To correspond to them are 72 words that are My court for Israel to complete them in these 87 and 22 words of the parshah of tekhelet, and one returns three words: ani — to receive Abraham; Heaven — to receive Yitzchak; Elokeichem — to receive Yaakov; and the chevrah said 'Heaven your Heaven emes' — Heaven to receive Abraham, Elokeichem to receive Yitzchak, emes for Yaakov," end quote. However, I found the author David in R' Yosef Tzimlisch z"l of Krakow in Abudraham, Tzror HaMor, Tzioni, Sefer HaMusar, Tolat Yaakov, and Ta'amei Mitsvos Rekanti — in all of them it is written "Heaven your Heaven emes"; and what completes emes even though it is not from Keriat Shema — Maharam Alashkar and Maharal Chaviv answered that nevertheless, since one may not interrupt between the word Elokeichem and the word emes, end quote. Mateh Moshe — all this I found from David cited above.
