He interrupts for Kriat Shema and must bless before and after it [Rambam chapter 2]. That which we say, one who dresses and prays, refers to Kriat Shema (Tosafos, page 18 in tractate Shabbat). We learn that he holds that even though they interrupted for Kriat Shema, they return to their study and are forbidden to pray, since otherwise they do not interrupt their study even for a moment. They do not recite the blessing on prayer, because it has no fixed time (Radbaz).

He does not interrupt. Nevertheless he should study a halachah that contains the exodus from Egypt, in order to mention it at its time; and after study he should complete the entire Kriat Shema (so in the gemara Berachos 13). See siman 70, note 64 — the words of Eldad HaTikunini, note 205, are wondrous.

He does not interrupt at all. Although in siman 89 he ruled that it is forbidden to study once the time for prayer has arrived — unlike the Ran — nevertheless, if he already began, he does not interrupt, as above.
