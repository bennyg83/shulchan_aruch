He interrupts for Krias Shema. He must recite the blessings before and after it, as stated by Rambam in chapter 2. The Gemara’s statement that he dressed and prayed refers to Krias Shema, as Tosafos explain on Shabbos 11. This shows that after interrupting for Krias Shema they return to their learning. They may not pray, since in any event they do not interrupt their learning even for a moment. No blessing was instituted over prayer because prayer has no fixed measure, as stated by Abudraham.
He does not interrupt. Nevertheless, he should study a halachah that mentions the Exodus from Egypt, in order to mention it at its proper time, and after his learning he should complete all of Krias Shema, as stated in Berachos 13. See siman 70, seif 4. The words of Olas Tamid, seif katan 5, are astonishing.
He does not interrupt at all. Although siman 89 rules, contrary to Ran, that one may not study once the time for prayer has arrived, nevertheless, if he began, he does not interrupt, as explained above.
