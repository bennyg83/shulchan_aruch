(3) There, except for those escorting the deceased, etc. — this is the Rambam's opinion in chapter 6 of Hilchot Tefillah; but in the Tur it implies only those whom the bier needs — whereas the other escorts of the deceased are obligated also in tefillah, as explained in Beit Yosef; and so too Taz note 1.
