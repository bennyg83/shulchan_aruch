Seif katan 3: One must educate them, and nevertheless one may, etc. As written in siman 269. Meaning, although according to the view that a minor eats prohibited food beis din is not commanded to separate him, it is nevertheless forbidden to feed him prohibited food directly, as written in siman 343. Even so, one may feed a child before Kiddush, because the food itself is permitted and only the time prohibits eating it. The Sages did not impose this stringency upon a child. The same applies here.
