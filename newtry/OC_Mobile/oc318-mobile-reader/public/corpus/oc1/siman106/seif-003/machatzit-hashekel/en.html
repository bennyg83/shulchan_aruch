(Note 3) Obligated to educate them, and nevertheless permitted, etc. — as written siman 269 — meaning: even though even according to one who says a minor may eat neveilah, the court is not available to separate him, nevertheless it is forbidden to feed him a prohibition by hand; and as written siman 343. Nevertheless it is permitted to feed a minor before Kiddush, since the food is permitted and only the time forbids it — in such a case they were not stringent regarding a child; and the same applies here.
