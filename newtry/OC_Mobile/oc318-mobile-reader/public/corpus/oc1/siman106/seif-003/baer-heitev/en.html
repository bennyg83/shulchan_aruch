For Keriat Shema. One must bless before and after it — Rambam chapter 2. And even though they stopped for Keriat Shema, they return to their study; and it is forbidden for them to pray, since in any case they do not nullify their study even for a moment, as implied in Tosafos Shabbos 11a s.v. kegon Rashbi, etc., for otherwise he would not challenge him at all — perhaps he wished to be stringent upon himself — and it is simple.

He does not stop. Nevertheless he should teach halacha that contains the exodus from Egypt in order to mention it at its time; and after his study they complete all of Keriat Shema — Talmud Berachos 13a.

He stops entirely. And even if he studies alone — Levush; and see. Even though in siman 89 he ruled that it is forbidden to study once the time of tefillah arrives, nevertheless if he began he does not stop — Magen Avraham. And in Eliyah Rabbah he wrote that if his study would not be nullified when he stops, he must stop.
