The wording "facing it" implies that another person reads facing it—but it is not specifically so, for one who examines Beit Yosef will see it is explained that he himself may also read. However, for practical halacha one should not do so, for the Terumat HaDeshen that Beit Yosef brought makes clear it is in truth permitted, but from the words of Rashba in Beit Yosef it is explained that it is not permitted unless he is covered—see there.
