There. Opposite it. The expression opposite it implies that another person reads opposite it, but one must say this is not precise, for one who examines the Beit Yosef will see that it is explicit that he himself may also read. However, practically, one should not do this, for from Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah, brought by the Beit Yosef, it is indeed explicit that it is permitted, but from the words of the Rashba in the Beit Yosef it is explicit that it is permitted only if it is covered; see there.
