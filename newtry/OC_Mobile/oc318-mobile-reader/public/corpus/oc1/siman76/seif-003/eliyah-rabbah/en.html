If they moved it, etc. The language of the Gemara is: passing excrement. Rashi explained: a person before whom they are carrying a chamber pot of excrement. The Rambam and Kol Bo wrote: passing excrement, for example, when it was floating on the surface of the water. Shiltei Giborim wrote: excrement in water is not nullified unless the water is murky so that the excrement is not visible in it. End quote. See later, at the beginning of siman 87, in the Beit Yosef.
