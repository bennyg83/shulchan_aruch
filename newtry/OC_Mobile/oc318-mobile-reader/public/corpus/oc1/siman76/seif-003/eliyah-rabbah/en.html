[3] They carried it past, etc. — the Gemara's term "passing feces," and Rashi explained: a person who has a chamber pot carried before him; and Rambam and Kolbo wrote "passing feces" such as when it was floating on the surface of the water. Shiltai Gibborim wrote: feces in water is not nullified unless the water is murky so that the feces are not visible in it — up to here. And see below at the beginning of siman 87 in Beit Yosef.
