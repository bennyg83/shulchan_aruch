(In seif katan 8.) The correct reading is: 'and no corrective measure is required.' See there. Meaning, if it has a foul odor or contains mucus, its law is like that of feces. Washing the affected place does not help in this case, since it had dripped onto his clothing and knees. If it has no foul odor and the mucus emerges through pressure, it comes from the intestine, and it is impossible for some of it to emerge without a trace of feces. In that case, washing away the filth is sufficient. Although it had already dripped onto his clothing, since it has no foul odor and the only concern is the slight trace of feces in it, this does not prevent recitation. He need only wash the place of the filth, meaning the anus, so that feces should not remain in its place. By contrast, if it drips continuously, it is deemed to come from the anus and is comparable to blood from a wound or an injury. Since it has no foul odor, no corrective measure at all is required. See the Radbaz, Fürth edition, siman 346.
