(In note 8) And he does not need a remedy, as should be, and see there. And its meaning is: if there is a bad odor or moisture in it, its law is like feces, and in this case what he washes in place does not help, since they were dripping onto his garments and knees. And if there is no bad odor and the moisture comes out through the ring — then it is from the stomach, and it is impossible that a little should come out without some smearing of feces. And likewise if he washes from the filth — it is fine; and even though it already went under his garments — nevertheless, since there is no bad odor, only because there is a little smearing of feces — this does not prevent; he only washes at the place of the filth — that is, at the mouth of the ring, so there should not be feces in its place. Not so if he drips frequently — then it is from the mouth of the ring and is like blood from his wound and blood from his boil; since there is no bad odor, he does not need any remedy at all. See Radbaz, Venice edition, siman 86.
