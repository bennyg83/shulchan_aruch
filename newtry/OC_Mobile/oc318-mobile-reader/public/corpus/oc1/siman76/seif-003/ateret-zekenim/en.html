If they pass feces before him, it is forbidden to recite opposite it, etc. — even as far as the eye can see, and behind him four amot from where the smell ends (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rambam). And some say that even before him it is permitted, since they pass and go on their way at a distance of four amot (R' Yitzchak Aboab and my teacher in the Bach). Nevertheless, if the feces passes only directly opposite him, for all opinions four amot suffice, and there is no difference between Kerias Shema and other blessings (above, siman 99).
