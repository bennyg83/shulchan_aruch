Peri Chadash writes in siman 108, seif 7, that if one recited Krias Shema in a place where there was reason to suspect excrement, and by the time he completed his Amidah the time for prayer had passed, he does not compensate for it with another Amidah, because he was negligent, and compensatory prayer applies only to one who was prevented by circumstances beyond his control. See there. This is obvious. See siman 58, seif 7, where one opinion states that Krias Shema has compensation. There too, the law is like that of prayer: only one who was prevented by circumstances beyond his control may compensate, but not one who was negligent.
