There is — the Taz [writes]: and if he read, he returns and reads without blessings, for in such a case one could say a doubt in blessings is lenient in a doubt of dispute, as written in sign 1. Also, most of them permit, like Rav Huna, the teacher of Rav Chisda; and so too the Perishah.

The Perishah wrote in siman 108, seif 7: if he read Shema in a place where there is a doubt regarding feces — if when he finished his prayer the time for prayer has passed, he does not make it up in another prayer, for he was negligent and makeup applies only to one compelled — see there; and it is obvious. And the case in Niddah 67 regarding Shema 11 that it has makeup — see there; likewise the law as prayer, specifically for one compelled, not for one negligent — see there. And see in Magen Avraham there in seif 8, in signs 12 and 13, from this.
