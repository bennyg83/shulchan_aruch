(4) [Seif 2] Feces in a pit — one places his sandal on it and recites, etc.; and we do not say that a sandal is nullified relative to his body and is not considered covering. Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh. Turei Zahav letter 2; and Maamar Mordechai letter 1 wrote that it appears to him from the words of Rosh cited that if he were barefoot it would not help if he places his foot on the pit to cover, for his body is not considered covering. And so it is reasonable that we require covering from something else, as explained above clearly — so is the ruling. And similarly Mahari Ayash in Sefer Beit Yehuda Orach Chaim siman 38 wrote there, and consequently the same applies to covering of other limbs — for covering of the body is not considered covering — see there. Rav Beit Knesset brought this. And similarly the Rav z"l in his book Lechem Yehuda page 11b brought it; and Yad Ephraim in Mishna Berurah Hagahat Tashbetz letter 1, Yad Ephraim letter 2; and it is reasonable to me that specifically covering of his own foot does not help, but covering of another's foot certainly helps. Lev Chayim there. Yad Ephraim there letter 2.
