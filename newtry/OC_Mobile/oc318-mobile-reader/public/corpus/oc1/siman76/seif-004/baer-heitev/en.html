On his garments. And if it is on his hand and has no substance but only ordinary filth, and its smell has gone away while it is still on his hand — similar to wiped sweat stains — it is permitted to recite opposite it. Beit Yosef.

A hole — meaning: there is a partition between him and the privy, and he extended his hand inside the partition; for if not, it is forbidden to recite opposite the privy. Rashi.

They permit — for it is written, "All the breath shall praise Yah" — that is, the mouth and nose are included; but not the rest of the limbs — even if they are not clean, it is permitted. And there are those who forbid; their reason is that it is written, "All my bones shall say, the Omnipresent, who is like You?" — we require that all be clean. The Magen Avraham wrote that it implies that if it is on his garments and covered, it is permitted according to all — see there and Pri Chadash.

Like feces. It implies that if it is on his garments and covered, it is permitted; however, in tractate Berachos, siman 299, it states that even on his garments it is forbidden. Ohel Moshe.
