Magen Avraham, s"k 7: his garments and he is covered—some emend [the text] regarding on his flesh, for if on his garments it is explained in the previous s"k that all permit. But Pri Megadim raised a difficulty: if so, what proof does Magen Avraham bring from Shabbos, which deals with his garments? And he resolves: the version before us that Magen Avraham wrote accords with the first view—meaning to exclude the view that permission exists only in a place where it is covered by itself without a garment. If so, one may say that garment cover, even on his garments, does not help—see there. And also according to this explanation it is difficult, for in siman 78 it is explained that excrement on his garments and covered is permitted in the view of all, as Magen Avraham brought in the previous s"k this proof—and it implies that the stringent view also concedes this, since the author did not bring the dispute in siman 78; and it requires some thought.
