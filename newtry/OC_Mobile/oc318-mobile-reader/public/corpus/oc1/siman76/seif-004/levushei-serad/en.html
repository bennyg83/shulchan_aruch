Magen Avraham, seif katan 7. His garments and covered. Some emend the text to read: on his flesh, for if it is on his garments, it is explicit in the previous seif katan that it is permitted according to all opinions. However, the Peri Megadim asked: if so, why does the Magen Avraham bring proof from Shabbos, which deals with something on his garments? He answers according to our text, that what the Magen Avraham wrote, according to the first view, means to exclude the some say view, which allows only where it was covered by itself without a garment. If so, one can say that covering by a garment, even over clothing, does not help; see there. This explanation is also difficult, for in siman 78 it is explicit that when it is on his garments and covered, it is permitted according to all opinions, as the Magen Avraham brought this proof in the previous seif katan, and it implies that the some say view also agrees to this, since the Mechaber did not bring any dispute in siman 78. This requires some study.
