If there was excrement on his flesh and it is covered in his garments, or if he inserted his hands into a lavatory through a hole and does not smell a foul odor—some permit reciting and some forbid. And some say that the one who permits did not permit excrement on his flesh except in a place that covers itself without a garment, such as under his arms. And it is proper to follow the stringent view.And seminal emission on his flesh—its law is like excrement (Maharil, laws of Yom Kippur).
