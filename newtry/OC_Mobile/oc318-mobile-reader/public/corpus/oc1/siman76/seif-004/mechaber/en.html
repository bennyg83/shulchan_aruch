If there was excrement on one's skin and it is covered with their garment or if one inserted their hands into a bathroom via a hole and they can't smell a foul odor, there are those who permit to recite and there are those who forbid. And there are those who say that the one who permits did not permit regarding excrement on the skin except in a place that covers itself without clothing, for example the armpit. And it is proper to do according to the words of the strict one.Rem"a: And semen on one's skin: its rule is like excrement (Maharil, Hilkhot Yom Kippur).
