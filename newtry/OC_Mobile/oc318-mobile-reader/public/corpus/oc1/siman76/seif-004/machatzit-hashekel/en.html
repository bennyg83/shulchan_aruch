(Siman kaf-dalet) Through a hole, etc. — siman 83, what he wrote. That is, since Rashi wrote “a partition between him and the bathroom,” and did not write “there are partitions to the bathroom,” nevertheless he holds that the partitions of the bathroom have the law of feces. Therefore Rashi wrote “a partition between him and the bathroom” — meaning there are partitions that separate between him and between the partitions of the bathroom. And on this the Me'or wrote that otherwise it is forbidden to recite opposite the bathroom — meaning opposite the partitions of the bathroom.
