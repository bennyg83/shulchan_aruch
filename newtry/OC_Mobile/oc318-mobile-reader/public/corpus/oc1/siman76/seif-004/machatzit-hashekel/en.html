Seif katan 4, Through a hole, etc. See what is written in siman 83. Rashi writes, "A partition between him and the privy," rather than saying that the privy has walls. This necessarily indicates that he maintains that the walls of a privy have the status of excrement. Rashi therefore writes that there is a partition between him and the privy, meaning a separate partition intervenes between him and the walls of the privy. Regarding this, Baal HaMaor writes that otherwise it would be forbidden to recite opposite the privy, meaning opposite its walls.
