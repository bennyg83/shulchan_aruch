Seif 64, "and some say," etc. — we go stringently; and some say, etc., from what is challenged in Yoma there on page 4: if it is visible, it is obvious; and if so, what is obvious? Yet R' Hillel permits, and still it did not enter his mind in its place, etc., as is challenged there; and what he wrote from the drashah of Rabbah, etc.

"And this too," etc. — see above, siman 40, seif 7, in the hagahah, and siman 613, seif 1; but from what is written in Sukkah 26b, we would say that R' Yehoshua holds, etc., 40 — that even at the time he sees keri it is permitted — Ohel Moshe.
