Seif 4, And there are those who say, etc. For we rule stringently. The second opinion is derived from the Gemara's challenge there in Yoma: What are the circumstances? If it is visible, the ruling is obvious. Were this not so, why would it be obvious, since Rav Huna permits it? At that point the Gemara had not yet considered the distinction that it is in its place, etc., as it later asks there: In what way is this different from Rav Huna's case, etc.?
And seminal discharge, etc. See above, siman 40, seif 7 in the gloss, and siman 613, seif 11. However, from what is stated in Sukkah 26b, "Shall we say that Rabbi Yose maintains, etc.?" it appears that even while experiencing a seminal emission it is permitted. See Magen Avraham.
