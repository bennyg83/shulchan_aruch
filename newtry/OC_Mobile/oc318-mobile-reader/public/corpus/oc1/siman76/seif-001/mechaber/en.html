To be careful regarding excrement at the time of Keriat Shema. It contains 8
seifim. Excrement in a glass lamp—it is permitted to recite Keriat Shema facing it even though one sees it through its walls, because the Merciful One conditioned it on being covered, as it is written "And you shall cover your excrement" (Deuteronomy 23:14), and here it is covered.
