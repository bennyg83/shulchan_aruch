(1) In a glass lamp—it is of glass or another substance that illuminates.

(2) To recite—know that in all these laws until siman 88, and likewise regarding the prohibition of ervah mentioned—wherever the law is mentioned regarding Keriat Shema, whether regarding permission or prohibition, it is not specifically Keriat Shema only; the same applies to Torah, prayer, and all matters of holiness, whether said in holy tongue or secular language.

(3) Facing it—and if a foul odor reaches him it is forbidden. And even per the view of one who holds in siman 79, seif 2, that a partition helps for urine only when the partition is a domain unto itself, but overturning a vessel does not help—and nevertheless, here one does not need four amot from where the odor ceased, but rather as long as it does not reach him it suffices, so wrote Peri Megadim in this siman. But in siman 79 in Mishbetzot the matter remains in doubt; see siman 79 in Biur Halacha.
