2) There. Excrement in a glass enclosure, etc. One need not distance oneself from vomit even though it is repulsive. Nezirut Shimshon; Rabbi Akiva Eiger; Pithei Olam, ot 1. In Tov Ayin, siman 8, he writes that a great authority was uncertain whether a person in prison may fulfill a mitzvah in a filthy place through action alone (for example, by taking a lulav and etrog without a blessing) or whether even the act of a mitzvah is prohibited there. Ba'er Heitev brings proof that it is permitted; see there. Pithei Olam there.
