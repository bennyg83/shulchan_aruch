(28) In a place where one should be concerned—meaning a place where small children are prevalent and the like.

(29) And found it afterwards — within his four amot; and if it was found outside four amot but within the measure of as much as one can see — see below siman 81, seif 2, and in Mishna Berurah there.

(30) One must return — for the sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination, which means one must check the place. And it is obvious that the same applies if after prayer he found that his clothes or shoes were soiled and he knows they were soiled because before prayer he was in a filthy place such as a latrine and the like in a place of garbage heaps, and he did not take care to be clean when leaving there — he must return, for he was negligent. But if he sat at night in a place where there was cat feces and the like, and it stuck to his garment, and he prayed in the synagogue, and upon returning home he found the filth stuck to the garment — he need not return to recite and pray, for it is as if the place was not fit to give rise to concern [see above].

(31) And to recite — with the blessings; and the same applies regarding prayer. And if he recited blessings in Birkas HaMazon and other blessings — see below at the end of siman 185, and what is written in Mishna Berurah there: the conclusion of the later authorities on this matter — whether when he finished his prayer and found feces, the time for prayer has passed — he does not make it up in another prayer, for he was negligent, and makeup applies only to one who was compelled.

(32) He need not return — for he is compelled, for what could he have done?

(33) He need not return — for even lechatchilah it was permitted to recite, as he did not know, as above in seif 7; but if it became known to him from this that there is urine here, and he proceeded and recited or prayed — he must return to recite and pray, for he transgressed a certainty of rabbinic law, as the Peri Megadim writes. See Chayei Adam, klal 3, din 33, and see below in siman 185 in Biur Halacha.
