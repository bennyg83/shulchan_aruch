[8] In the house it is permitted. And the same law applies to certain feces, except there is a doubt whether it is human or dog feces — in the house it is permitted, in a garbage heap forbidden; so it appears from acharonim in the name of Shiltai Gibborim. And Magen Avraham wrote we follow what is commonly found, as Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote path 7 siman 3 — up to here. And to me it appears that in a house it is always permitted, for human feces are not left there; and the fundamental law of this requires study. Rosh wrote that drippings are not considered "opposite one standing" and are permitted by Torah law — Magen Avraham; and see below siman 78.
