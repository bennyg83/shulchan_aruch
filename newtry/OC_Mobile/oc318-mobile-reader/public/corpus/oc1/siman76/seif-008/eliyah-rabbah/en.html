In a house, it is permitted. The same applies where there is certainly excrement, but there is a doubt whether it is human excrement or dog excrement: in a house it is permitted, but in a refuse heap it is forbidden. So it implies in the later authorities in the name of Shiltei Giborim. The Magen Avraham wrote that we follow what is common, as Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote, Nesiv 7, siman 3. End quote. It seems to me that in a house it is always permitted, for people do not leave human excrement there. The main law requires study. The Rosh wrote that a dribble is not called opposite the stream and is permitted according to the Torah, Magen Avraham. See later, siman 78.
