Seif 68, "in a place," etc. — from what he wrote, "since he sinned," etc.; and if in a place where it is not fitting to be concerned, he ought not to have written "since he sinned," etc.; and furthermore, from what he wrote, "and he saw feces opposite him," etc. — this is sufficient, and there is no difficulty that he must return and pray; rather, there — in a place where it is not fitting to be concerned — Tosefta Berachos and the Rosh; and see above, siman 81, seif 2.

"And from Rabbeinu," etc. — Tosafos, Eruvin 64a. Ohel Moshe.
