Seif 8, In a place, etc. This follows from the statement, "Since he sinned, etc." Had it been a place where there was no reason to suspect excrement, the Gemara should not have said, "Since he sinned, etc." Moreover, this follows from what is stated there: "And he saw excrement opposite him, etc." This is sufficient. The Gemara's ruling that he must repeat the Amidah does not present a difficulty, because there it concerns a place where there was no reason to suspect excrement. Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah and Rosh.
