A small amount of excrement. Even the slightest amount, because the prohibition of excrement has no minimum measure. Rabbi Akiva Eiger writes in the name of Nezirus Shimshon that one need not distance himself from a person's vomit, even though it is repulsive.
The saliva is thick. It is then considered a covering, provided that no foul odor reaches him. Pouring water over excrement is ineffective unless the water is murky enough that the excrement cannot be seen through it. This is unlike excrement in a glass lantern, because there the covering is complete, whereas here it is not. Therefore, one must be careful not to recite opposite a chamber pot full of water into which children's garments soiled with excrement were thrown.
