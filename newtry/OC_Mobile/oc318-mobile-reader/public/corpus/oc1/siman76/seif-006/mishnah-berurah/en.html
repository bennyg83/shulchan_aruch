(20) A little excrement—even any amount, for there is no minimum measure for prohibition of excrement. Written in Chiddushei Raavan in name of Nazirut Shimshon that one need not distance from what a person vomits even though it is repugnant.

(21) Thick saliva—then it is considered cover, specifically when no foul odor reaches him. If he puts water on the excrement it does not help unless the water is murky such that excrement is not visible through them; and it is not comparable to excrement in a glass lamp for there it is proper cover, not so here. Therefore one should beware to recite facing a tub full of water into which they threw small children's garments that have excrement.
