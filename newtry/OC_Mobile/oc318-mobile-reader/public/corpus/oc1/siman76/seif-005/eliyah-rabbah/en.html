[5] And there are those who forbid, etc. — it implies that if it was on his garments and covered, it is permitted according to everyone; and above in seif 2 regarding touching with a sandal, one may say as written in Sefer Tamim De'im siman 200, that there it is completely revealed like dangling feet — up to here. One may also say that a sandal is nullified relative to the body, which is not so with other garments; and see below siman 87, which implies that a sandal is like a chamber pot.
