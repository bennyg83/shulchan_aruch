s"k 9: hard water—it is obvious that certainly clear water is not preferable to saliva that is not thick. And one may say this is [the rule] for saliva because it is small in quantity; but if he pours much water on it, it is permitted—he teaches this—and he brings proof from siman 87, for there it is explained that a pottery chamber pot of excrement is forbidden to recite Keriat Shema even if one put water in it, but of metal it is permitted if washed well. It implies that if there is not good washing and there is some excrement, it is forbidden even if one put water in it—and it requires understanding.
