Seif katan 9. Water. This is difficult, for it is obvious that clear water is certainly no better than saliva that is not thick. One may answer that one might have thought that saliva is because it is small in quantity, but if he pours much water upon it, it is permitted; he therefore teaches us otherwise. He brings proof from siman 87, where it is explicit that an earthenware chamber pot of excrement is forbidden for Krias Shema even if he put water into it, but one of metal is permitted if it is washed well. This implies that if it is not washed well and there is even a trace of excrement, it is forbidden even if he put water into it. This is clear.
