The ring. One who has hemorrhoid disease and blood constantly flows from him, and because of the blood rotten, foul pus comes out and it has a bad smell — he is forbidden in everything sacred as long as rotten pus drips from him. And if there is no bad smell: if the blood or pus exits through the rectum in undergarments, it is from the belly and he has a remedy by washing the filthy place; but if it drips and flows down frequently on its own, then it is from the mouth of the ring and he has no remedy. Magen Avraham in the name of the Radbaz, Knesset HaGedolah, and Eliyahu Rabbah.
