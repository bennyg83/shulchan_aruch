The anal opening. One who suffers from hemorrhoids and constantly discharges blood, and together with the blood there emerges foul, decayed mucus that has a bad odor, is forbidden to engage in any sacred matter for as long as the foul mucus continues to flow from him. If there is no bad odor, then if the blood or mucus is expelled intermittently through pressure, it comes from the intestine and can be remedied by washing the soiled area. If it flows and descends continuously on its own, it comes from the anal opening and has no remedy. Magen Avraham in the name of Radbaz, Knesses HaGedolah, and Olas Tamid.
