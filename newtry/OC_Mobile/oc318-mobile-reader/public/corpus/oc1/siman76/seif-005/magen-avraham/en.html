Feces at the anal opening. One who has hemorrhoid illness and always has blood discharge from him, and because of the blood a putrid, moldy moisture comes out of him and has a bad odor — he is forbidden in everything holy all the time the putrid moisture drips from him. And if there is no bad odor: if the blood or moisture comes out through pressure in the intestines, it is from the bowels and has a remedy by washing the filthy place; and if it drips and descends frequently on its own, then it is from the anal opening and has no remedy (Radbaz volume 2 siman 30, thus).
