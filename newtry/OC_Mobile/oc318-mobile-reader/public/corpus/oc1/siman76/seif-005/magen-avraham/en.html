Excrement at the anal opening. One who has hemorrhoids and blood constantly flows from him, and along with the blood there emerges foul, putrid mucus that has a bad odor, is forbidden in every matter of sanctity the entire time that foul mucus is flowing from him. If there is no bad odor, then if the blood or mucus comes out intermittently by force, it is considered from the intestines and has a remedy by washing the place of filth; but if it constantly drips down on its own, then it is considered from the anal opening and has no remedy, Radbaz, volume 2, siman 30, so it seems.
