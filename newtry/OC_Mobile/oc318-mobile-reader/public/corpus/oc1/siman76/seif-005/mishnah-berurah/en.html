At the anus. One who suffers from hemorrhoids and constantly discharges blood, together with which comes putrid, foul mucus that has a bad odor, may not engage in any sacred matter as long as the putrid mucus continues to flow from him. If there is no bad odor, and the blood or mucus emerges intermittently through pressure, it comes from the intestines and can be remedied by washing the soiled area. If it constantly drips and descends on its own, it comes from the anus and requires no remedy.
