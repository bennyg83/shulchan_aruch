(16) At the anus—one who has hemorrhoid illness and blood constantly flows from him, and on account of the blood a fetid putrid moisture emerges with foul odor—forbidden in all holy matters as long as the fetid moisture drips from him. If there is no foul odor, if the blood or moisture comes out through pressure intermittently it is from the bowels and there is remedy by washing the filthy place. If it drips and runs down constantly of itself, then it is from the anus and needs no remedy.

(17) Forbidden to recite—and post factum if he recited Keriat Shema he goes back and recites; but regarding prayer there is doubt whether he should return and pray—so Chayei Adam; but many Acharonim wrote he must return and pray.

(18) In the view of all—meaning even according to those who permit in siman 45 regarding excrement on flesh and covered—here is different because at its place impurity is greater; therefore a person must stir himself and always see that the anus be clean and wash with water or saliva so nothing of excrement remain there [Shelah and Chayei Adam].

(19) When sitting—but if not visible even when sitting, it is permitted, for the Torah was not given to ministering angels.
