Doubt. And the same if there was doubt whether it is human feces or dog feces — in a house it is permitted, in a garbage dump it is forbidden (She'erit Yosef, R' Yitzchak Abohav). And to me it seems we follow what is prevalent, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 57 seif 13, that we rely on prevalence whether to be lenient or strict; so too here, if infants are more prevalent than dogs, even in a house it is forbidden, and the same conversely — if in a garbage dump dogs are more prevalent, we attribute it to dogs.
