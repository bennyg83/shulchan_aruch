And these words, etc.; but. Ran. For they contradict one another.
But, etc. As written there, 34b, and below at the beginning of siman 595: one who is not, etc.; and in the Gemara there: it is obvious, etc. See Tosafos and Rosh, unlike Rashi, from the Mishnah there: one who recited the blessings, etc.
And likewise their order. From the Mishnah there: one who recited the blessings, etc. This is unlike the order of the blessings themselves, which does prevent fulfillment, and likewise the blasts. So the Ran explained according to one explanation. Regarding the blasts this is explicit in the Yerushalmi, as written in siman 588, seif 2.
