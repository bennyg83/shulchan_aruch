(א) (c) Do not know which one knows that the truth is:

(ב) And if you don’t know how to bless those blessings, you will be able to make arrangements. And so that if you can’t get stuck, you’ll be blessed

(ג) And likewise their order does not hinder — it does not mean in the order of prayer that one preceded Zichronot to Malchuyot or Shofarot to Zichronot, for in that case one did not fulfill and must return and pray; and likewise in the order of tekiyot if one preceded teruah to the first tekiah one did not fulfill. Rather it means the order is to pray musaf and blow within the prayer, and if he changed the order — meaning he prayed before blowing — he fulfilled in both. And this is what Rama concluded with "and if he prayed" etc., and the vav is superfluous.
