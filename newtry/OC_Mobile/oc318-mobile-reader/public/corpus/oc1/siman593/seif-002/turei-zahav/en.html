One of them or two, etc. This is difficult to me: how can it be found that he knows two and not the third? If he knows tashrat, he knows all of them; and if he knows tashat and tarat, he also knows all of them. According to the one who holds in siman 590 that the shevarim-teruah of tashrat must be done in one breath, one can say that he knows only tashat and tarat, but does not know how to make the shevarim-teruah of tashrat in one breath.
