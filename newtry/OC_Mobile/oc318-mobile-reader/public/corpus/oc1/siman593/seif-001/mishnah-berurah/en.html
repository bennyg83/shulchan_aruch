The blessings of Rosh Hashanah, etc. This means those of Musaf, which include kingships, remembrances, and shofaros, and similarly those of the Yom Kippur of Yovel, in which they also say kingships, remembrances, and shofaros.
Prevent one another. This implies that on the rest of the days of the year, if one knows one blessing he should say it and they do not prevent one another; Magen Avraham. See Biur Halacha, where we wrote that even according to the Magen Avraham it is only a general plea for mercy, but he does not fulfill the obligation at all with this. For even if he skipped only one blessing from the eighteen, we rule above in siman 119, seif 3, that he must go back and say all of them in the order they were instituted, and if not, he does not fulfill the obligation of prayer with this; all the more so here. He should also try to attach it to some blessing, such as Asher Yatzar or the like, so that it be adjacent to another blessing.
