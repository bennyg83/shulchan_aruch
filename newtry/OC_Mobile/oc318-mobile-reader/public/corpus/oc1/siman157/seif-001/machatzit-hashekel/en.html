(א) (section 1) "Hour four means," etc., as the Mechaber wrote in siman 483 — meaning that from there are two reasons that we count these hours from alos hashachar, and also that seasonal hours are required; for they said there that by Torah law chametz on erev Pesach is permitted in eating until midday, and Rabbi Yehudah said: by rabbinic law one does not eat chametz except until the end of four hours; and every fifth hour it is forbidden in eating and permitted in benefit; and at the beginning of the sixth hour one must burn it. They said: what is the reason? Lest they err between before midday and after midday — but this is recognizable from the sun's course; and they answered Rava's second answer, that they are concerned for an overcast day when there is no recognition by the sun, and thereby they might err to eat also after midday; and the Gemara challenged: also in the fourth hour let us be concerned they will err — and it should have been proper to forbid also in the fourth hour; and they answered: the fourth hour is a time of a meal for everyone every day — if so, since the habit to eat at that hour, one feels in himself that hour and will not come to err; behold the permission to eat chametz in the entire fourth hour is since then it is mealtime; and if so, the time of eating chametz and the eating of every person are therefore equal; and since we hold for the matter of the morning meal in siman 483 that we count these four hours from alos hashachar and also count seasonal hours — therefore it is for mealtime, therefore we count thus.**** END BLOCK ****
