[1] Until the sixth hour, etc. Meaning, from the time he rose from bed. Magen Avraham. Beit Yosef in Choshen Mishpat, siman 5, holds that this means specifically the beginning of the sixth hour. Sema and Shach there rule, however, that even at the end of the sixth hour it is not considered like throwing a stone into a flask. I found support for their view at the end of the chapter Seder Taanis, where it says that if people were fasting and rain fell before midday, they need not complete the fast, but if after midday, they complete it. Rashi explains that the fast has already taken effect because they did not eat at the normal mealtime. Magen Avraham writes in the name of the Zohar, Parashas Beshalach, that a person should not cook food from one day for the next, nor retain it from one day to the next. It is proper to have bran prepared in a utensil so that if one's hand touches milk, he can wash it with bran. Sefer Chassidim, siman 58.
