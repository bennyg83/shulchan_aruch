1) [Seif 1] When the fourth hour arrives, etc. As we say in Pesachim 12b: the fourth hour is the mealtime of all people; the fifth is the mealtime of laborers; the sixth is the mealtime of Torah scholars. One who eats from then onward is like one who throws a stone into a flask. But if he tasted something in the morning, there is no concern; see there.
