One who apportions portions to the members of his household on Shabbat may cast lots to say: whoever's lot comes out shall have such-and-such a portion; and this is when the portions are equal, and he does so only in order to appease them so as not to cast jealousy among them; but with others it is forbidden, since they are strict with one another they will come to measuring and weighing; but to give a large portion against a small one and cast lots upon them — even with the members of his household, and on a weekday — forbidden on account of gambling (kubya); and some say that with his sons and the members of his household it is permitted to cast lots even for a large portion against a small one, since they are not strict:And it is forbidden to cast lots on Shabbat even through a non-Jew [Maharil]:]:
