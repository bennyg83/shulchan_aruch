(א) Since they are strict — see Mishna Berurah what is written, and it is from Magen Avraham; and see there that he wrote further: if we know in members of a fellowship that they are strict with one another — even to divide without lots forbidden, for we are concerned they will come to mention measuring and weighing and counting; and this prohibition is as written siman 323; and also sharing with such people is not proper initially, since they are accustomed to ask one another frequently they will certainly come to ask also on Shabbat and Yom Tov; and through their strictness with one another they will come to measuring and weighing, and by necessity an unspecified request on Yom Tov from one's fellow — even in the permitted manner among other people — among these fellowship members who are strict it is certainly not proper lest they come to measuring and weighing; and this specifically in members of a fellowship; but unspecified neighbors who are not companions — even though presumably they too are strict — nevertheless permitted to ask one from another on Shabbat and Yom Tov, since they are not accustomed to ask one another frequently the Sages did not decree lest they come to prohibition; and this is the content of Magen Avraham's words that he brought from Tosafos; and it appears that even to divide without lots they did not forbid among neighbors, since this matter is not common among them they did not concern themselves lest it come to prohibition — so it appears from Magen Avraham's conclusion, see there:

(ב) With one another — in the Tosefta it is stated that a person casts lots with his guests, and likewise the guests cast lots with one another for who takes his portion first; but not that he take his portion and his fellow's portion; and the reason: since there is no monetary significance except who takes first — it is like one who casts lots for who says Kaddish, which Magen Avraham was lenient about; but difficult: why did the Tosefta need to inform us a greater matter that even to equalize portions they too are forbidden to cast lots among guests?
