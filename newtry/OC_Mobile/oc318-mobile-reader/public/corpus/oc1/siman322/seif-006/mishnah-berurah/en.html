(א) The portions are equal — for in this there is no concern of grinding dice (kubya), since one does not profit through the lot:

(ב) Since they are strict — and to divide without lots is permitted, for we say by default they are not strict unless they wish to cast lots, for then we see they are strict; and even if they are neighbors and not one fellowship — the manner of unspecified neighbors is to be strict with one another; nevertheless the Sages did not forbid dividing without lots:

(ג) With one another — meaning that they do not forgive one another even for a small matter:

(ד) And to cast lots, etc. — meaning that whoever wins the lot takes the large one and whoever loses takes the small one — this is included in grinding dice literally. [Kubya is what they play with sticks;] and kubya is dust of theft in their words, for his mind is not to transfer it with a complete acquisition — therefore forbidden even on a weekday; and even though with the master of the house and the members of his household theft does not apply at all since everything is his — nevertheless forbidden, lest he come to be drawn after dice with others; and with others certainly forbidden on a weekday, and all the more on Shabbat, for dice resembles commerce and trade:

(ה) That they are not strict — for behold he gives them everything from his own and the concern of measuring and weighing does not apply; and also there is no dice here. And if his sons divide from their own — forbidden, a large portion against a small portion [Peri Megadim]:

(ו) To cast lots — meaning if it is to divide some matter; and forbidden even where it comes to appeasement; but to cast lots for who says Kaddish or who goes up to the Torah — permitted, for they cast lots on Shabbat in the Temple for who slaughters, who throws [Magen Avraham]; and see in Nezer HaKodesh that he brought in the name of Shevut Yaakov part 3 siman 24 that it seems permitted only to cast lots from the book as is customary, but to cast lots through notes written from erev Shabbat there alone and cast in a lottery and take out a note for who goes up to the Torah — forbidden; and so too in Sefer HaChaim:
