Lots. See Baer Heitev seif 6; and see Tosafot Yom Tov chapter 5 siman 16, for a question was asked there whether it is permitted to sell a Torah scroll by lot — namely that they write the names of the men in fixed amounts among them and stipulate that whoever's name comes up with the object shall have it, and each one transfers his share to his fellow, and the ruling was to permit, and there is no concern of gambling at all since each one transfers his share to his fellow; and see Magen Avraham that in a place of a mitzvah there is no prohibition of shesh (dice/lots); and see in Ginas Veradim part 1 general rule 2 siman 31. And for greater honor he wrote that one should leave the Torah scroll without stitching and it is found that it has no sanctity of a Torah scroll at all; and in responsa of Rivash they brought that any scroll that has an error in it has no sanctity of a Torah scroll and one may sell it:
