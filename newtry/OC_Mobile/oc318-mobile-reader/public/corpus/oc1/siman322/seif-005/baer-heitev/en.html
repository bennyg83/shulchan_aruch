And he rubs it. It must read: and he cuts it off and rubs it to smell, etc. In Shakhne'ahag he inclined to be stringent: specifically cutting off a little so it smell — permitted; but if he breaks off a piece from it in order to give to another — forbidden, see there. And Yam Shel Shlomo wrote chapter 4 that this cutting off is permitted only by hand but not with a knife; and it was not permitted with a knife except for animal food, see there.
