(א) Seif 5 "permitted." And three distinctions: animal food — permitted to move and to cut off even to pick teeth with; fragrant woods — permitted to move and to cut off to smell; but to pick teeth with them — exempt but forbidden; and likewise a splinter — exempt there too, only that there even moving is forbidden. And Rambam wrote regarding a splinter that if he cut it to pick his teeth with it — he is liable; and as is stated in the Tosefta that the Rosh brought there, and he explains "and they do not cut it," etc., it refers to a stalk; and see in the Rosh there, and so in the Tosefta; but according to what the Rosh wrote there and above in seif 4 — not like the Tosefta and Rambam; and requires study on what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Rambam that he is liable, for we do not rule like him, as stated:
