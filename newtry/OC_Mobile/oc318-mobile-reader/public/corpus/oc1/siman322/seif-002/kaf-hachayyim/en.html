5) [Seif 2] A Shabbos following or preceding Yom Tov, etc. If Yom Tov falls on Sunday and Monday and the egg was laid on the preceding Shabbos, it is permitted on the second day of Yom Tov in the Diaspora, as stated below in siman 513, seif 5, in the gloss.
6) There: If it was laid on one, it is forbidden on the other. The reason is that laying the egg is considered preparation. This preparation is only rabbinically prohibited. This appears to be Magen Avraham's view in siman 513, paragraph 8, although he cites a dispute; see there. Tosefes Shabbos here, paragraph 4, writes the same. The matter will be explained further there, with the help of Heaven.
