Born on one forbidden on the other — meaning on Sunday adjacent to Shabbat; but on Monday — permitted; and likewise if Yom Tov is on Thursday and Friday and it was born on Thursday — permitted on Shabbat, and it is forbidden only when it was born on the day adjacent to Shabbat alone; if they are two days of Rosh Hashana, then both are considered one long day.
