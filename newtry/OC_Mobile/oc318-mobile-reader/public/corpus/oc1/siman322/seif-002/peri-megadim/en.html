"decree" that there are two prohibitions — lest he climb, and muktzeh — and both are needed; Magen Avraham 3; and in Levush even if his mind was upon them, see there; and if there is a doubt whether they fell today or yesterday — forbidden, for davar sheyesh lo matirin — one does not follow the lenient side of a doubt; see siman 325; and behold if he waits after Shabbat the fruits will rot and not be fit for anything; or if he vowed not to eat fruits on a weekday except on Shabbat and if he waits the whole week they will be lost — certainly one may say it is not davar sheyesh lo matirin; and nevertheless one may say regarding a doubt whether they fell today or yesterday they are forbidden, for we establish it on its presumption that now they fell and not earlier; see Yoreh De'ah siman 397 (16) (2); and one may say a person is presumed to die unlike fruits — if they are not yet finished it is their manner to be on the tree and not fall — we say now it fell; and see 513 in Taz letter 8, and it will be explained there; and in Kiddushin 79 "her father betrothed her" and in the beginning of that chapter; and behold if he vowed not to eat fruits on a weekday as we wrote that according to him it is not davar sheyesh lo matirin — if he waits the whole week they will be lost — one may say it depends on what Magen Avraham wrote siman 318 (2) similar to terumah, since for others it is davar sheyesh lo matirin — further, also for him it does not become nullified, see there; and what is written "since no melacha was done," etc. — so too Beit Yosef; behold if melacha was done through a non-Jew — all the more; and one should not say that here all the more he will climb and detach to eat immediately — from what is taught to us that it is not so, etc.; and see siman 318 in Magen Avraham (2):
