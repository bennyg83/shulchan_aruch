1) [Seif 1] An egg laid on Shabbos, etc. The reason is a decree because of a Shabbos following Yom Tov. An egg laid that day is forbidden because of preparation, since any egg laid today was completed the preceding day. Yom Tov therefore prepared it for Shabbos, and we maintain that Yom Tov may not prepare for Shabbos. The Sages consequently prohibited an ordinary Shabbos because of a Shabbos following Yom Tov. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1. This is based on the Gemara, Beitzah 2b.
2) There: It is forbidden even to move it. Because of muktzeh. Levush.
3) There: Even if it became mixed with a thousand, etc. The reason is that it is an item that will later become permitted, since it will be fit the next day. We maintain that an item that will later become permitted is not nullified even in a thousand. Even if it is uncertain whether the egg was laid today and it became mixed with other eggs, they are all forbidden, as stated at the beginning of Yoreh De'ah, siman 102; see there. Rather than eat it now while forbidden, it is preferable to wait until tomorrow and eat all of them permissibly. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2. Regarding a compounded doubt involving an item that will later become permitted, see Yoreh De'ah, siman 110, seif 8, in the gloss and the later authorities there.
4) There: Provided that the utensil does not touch it. This refers to the utensil, as stated below in siman 513, seif 4. The reason touching is forbidden was already explained above in siman 310, paragraph 31; take it from there.
