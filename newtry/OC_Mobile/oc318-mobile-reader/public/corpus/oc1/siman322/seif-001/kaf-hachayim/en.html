(1) [Seif 1] An egg that was born on Shabbat, etc. The reason is on account of the decree of Shabbat after Yom Tov, for the egg born on that day is forbidden on account of preparation, for every egg born nowadays finished yesterday, and behold Yom Tov prepares for Shabbat; and it is simple that Yom Tov does not prepare for Shabbat — therefore they decreed on every Shabbat and Yom Tov when it was born; and it is from the gemara Beitzah folio 2b:
