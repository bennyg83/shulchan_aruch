An egg. At the end of chapter 39; a decree on account of Yom Tov after Shabbat or Shabbat after Yom Tov that is forbidden — from what? Magen Avraham letter 1. And he shall not touch it — see siman 310 letter 5 and in 513 (1) and (5) and Magen Avraham there (2) two reasons: (1) lest he sway it; (2) touching for the need of muktzeh is forbidden; and it will be explained further in 513. A treif egg that became mixed in a thousand — see Yoreh De'ah in Pri Megadim 102 regarding davar sheyesh lo matirin and Tzemach Tzedek in this; and one may say a treifah — Shabbat does not prepare for Yom Tov, and there is no reason to decree; and one may say on account of two reasons, or it was born on Shabbat — forbidden on account of nolad even if it stands to be slaughtered, for if he wants he slaughters and eats it on Shabbat and it is not food separated (mufrat), and if so there are two prohibitions and it is davar sheyesh lo matirin regarding Shabbat:
