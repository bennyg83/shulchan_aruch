And even if it became mixed in a thousand, etc. And the reason: it is a matter that has a permitted time; and it is simple that davar sheyesh lo matirin even in a thousand does not become nullified; and even if there is a doubt whether it was born today and it became mixed with others — all are forbidden, as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 102, see there; and the reason: until now he will eat it in prohibition — better he wait until tomorrow and eat all in permission.
