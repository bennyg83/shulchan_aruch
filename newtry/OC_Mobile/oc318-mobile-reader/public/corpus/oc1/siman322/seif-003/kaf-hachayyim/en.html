3) There: Even if it became mixed with a thousand, etc. The reason is that it is an item that will later become permitted, since it will be fit the next day. We maintain that an item that will later become permitted is not nullified even in a thousand. Even if it is uncertain whether the egg was laid today and it became mixed with other eggs, they are all forbidden, as stated at the beginning of Yoreh De'ah, siman 102; see there. Rather than eat it now while forbidden, it is preferable to wait until tomorrow and eat all of them permissibly. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2. Regarding a compounded doubt involving an item that will later become permitted, see Yoreh De'ah, siman 110, seif 8, in the gloss and the later authorities there.
