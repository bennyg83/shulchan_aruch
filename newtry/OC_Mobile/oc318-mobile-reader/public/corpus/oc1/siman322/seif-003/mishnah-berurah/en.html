(א) Fruits that fell, etc. — and even if there is a doubt lest they fell on Shabbat — likewise forbidden. And see in Pesachim 56 that even if his intent was for a mitzvah to declare them ownerless for the poor to eat in years of famine — likewise forbidden, for even though it was permitted for them to feed the poor, nevertheless it was not permitted for them to transgress a rabbinic prohibition of Shabbat and Yom Tov:
(ב) From the tree on Shabbat — and the same applies to seeds and vegetables found in gardens — forbidden to eat them and even to move them lest they fell today. And know that even if he knows they fell on erev Shabbat he is not permitted except to gather one by one and eat it, but to collect many fruits together — there is a full prohibition, as below in siman 340 seif 9 that anything gathered in its place of growth resembles gathering (me'amer); and see there in Biur Halacha that it is close that there is liability for a chatas:
(ג) Forbidden — on account of lest he climb and detach; and also on account of muktzeh, since it became muktzeh in his mind at twilight when it was then attached it became muktzeh for the whole day:
