(s.k. 4) His teeth, etc. And it is implied in the gemara, etc., that it is stated there: Rabbah bar Shila asked Rav Chisda: what is the law regarding bringing up stones to the roof to clean — if he went up to the roof to relieve himself? He said: permitted on account of human dignity. Ravina objected: but we learned in a mishnah — R' Eliezer says a person may take a splinter from before him to pick his teeth with, and the Sages forbid; and the Sages did not permit on account of human dignity. And they answer: granted, for eating a person fixes a place for his eating and he should have prepared from erev Shabbat a splinter for that place — therefore they did not permit; but a person does not fix a place for relieving himself and it was not possible for him to prepare from erev Shabbat; if so, when his fellow invites him on Shabbat one may say the law of relieving oneself applies, for it was impossible to prepare from erev Shabbat since they did not invite him until Shabbat:
