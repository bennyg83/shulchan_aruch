Seif 1. "It is permitted to insulate hot food from Erev Yom Kippur for Motzaei Yom Kippur." The stringent view is truly difficult, as Tur already questioned it and Beit Yosef agreed. I find it even more surprising, because the Gemara in the first chapter of Shabbos is explicit, and so we rule in Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chayyim, siman 252, seif 1, that one may place bundles of flax into an oven so that their processing continues throughout Shabbos. One may likewise put wool into a kettle; were it not for the concern that he might stoke the fire, it would be permitted even while the kettle remains on the fire. One may also place incense beneath utensils so that they absorb fragrance throughout Shabbos, as stated there in seif 5. Why, then, should insulating before Yom Kippur for use afterward be forbidden? Shabbos is more severe than Yom Kippur, since intentional violation of Shabbos carries stoning, while Yom Kippur carries kareit. It therefore appears that the principal reason for the stringency is a decree lest he eat. On an ordinary Shabbos, insulation is normally done so that the food can be eaten that same day. If one insulates food on Yom Kippur, he may mistakenly come to eat it. Just as the Sages prohibited touching food in certain circumstances because one might eat, they similarly prohibited insulation here because the ordinary Shabbos pattern could cause confusion. With this, the view of the Gaon is also understandable. He holds that after Yom Kippur one may not eat the insulated food until enough time has elapsed for it to have been prepared after the fast, and Tur questioned him. According to what I have written, the law parallels prohibited cooking on Shabbos. When a person cooks in violation of Shabbos, he must wait afterward long enough for the act to have been done permissibly, so that he does not benefit from prohibited melacha. Here too, since the Sages prohibited insulation on Yom Kippur because of the concern that he may eat, if one violated their enactment and derived benefit from the prohibited act, they penalized him by requiring him to wait the time needed to prepare it. This appears correct to me, with the help of Heaven.
