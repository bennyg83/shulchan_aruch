1) [Seif 1] It is permitted to insulate hot food, etc. For he is preparing during the weekday for the weekday. Although it remains on the stove throughout Yom Kippur and he insulates it to preserve its heat, there is no concern. On the contrary, it appears preferable that he insulate it, so that he will find his meal prepared on Motzei Yom Kippur. Tur.
