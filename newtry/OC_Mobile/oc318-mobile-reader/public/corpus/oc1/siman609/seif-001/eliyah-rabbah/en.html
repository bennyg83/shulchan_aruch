[1] [Levush] Rather, the main reason, etc. With this he resolves why in siman 254, se'if 5, he permitted baking for the need of Motzaei Shabbos, and it is easy to understand. The Magen Avraham in siman 254, subparagraph 2, distinguishes between insulating, which is forbidden, and baking, which is permitted. So too, in Responsa Radbaz, siman 31, he forbids insulating for Motzaei Shabbos.
