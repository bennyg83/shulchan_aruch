(Subparagraph 1) That one should not insulate. Because he prepares from Yom Kippur to weekday. Although he insulates on the eve of Yom Kippur, and it is preparing from weekday to weekday, nevertheless the fact that it remains on Yom Kippur itself for the sake of weekday is considered preparing for weekday, even though the preparation happens automatically. For this reason it is permitted to place incense and sulfur under vessels before Shabbos, and the vessels continue to absorb the fragrance throughout Shabbos, as written in siman 252, se'if 5, although it is for the need of weekday. So too in siman 254, se'if 5, in the final gloss, he permits placing bread in the oven on Friday and leaving it in the oven the entire Shabbos so that it bakes for Motzaei Shabbos. The Magen Avraham in siman 254, subparagraph 2, wrote that in truth the stringency of insulation is a novelty. See the Bach here, who wrote a reason. The Levush wrote that the main reason is that it appears like gluttony, that he prepares for himself so his food will be ready after the fast. If so, there is no difficulty from baking or incense. So too wrote the Eliyah Rabbah.
