Se'if 1 It is permitted to insulate. It appears to my humble opinion that this is only with something that does not add heat, but with something that adds heat it is forbidden, according to the Mechaber's view above in siman 257 that even raw food is forbidden. In this respect insulation is more severe than leaving on the fire, because a small stoking suffices and we are concerned that he will stoke for the need of tomorrow; see Magen Avraham there, subparagraph 15. But according to the view of the lenient authorities brought by the Rema there in se'if 7, that raw food is permitted, here it is permitted with anything, for he diverts his mind from it until Motzaei Yom Kippur, and it is like that case of the Rema in siman 254, se'if 5; see Magen Avraham there, subparagraph 20.
