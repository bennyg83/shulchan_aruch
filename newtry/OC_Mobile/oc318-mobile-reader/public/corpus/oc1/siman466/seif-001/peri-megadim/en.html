See Taz: even if he sees on the leftover that there is no saliva, an ordinary cow has saliva, and it is possible it dried and was wiped and will cause chimutz; and animal saliva causes chimutz like human saliva, and likewise mouse saliva; and in siman 453 in Taz 2 and Magen Avraham 5: hard wheat, a little saliva does not cause chimutz; see there. And see Magen Avraham 3: it implies even regarding a horse it is proper to give a little before it so that nothing remain; and see Chok Yaakov 5. And letter 3 there: he spat blood: it is impossible without flecks of saliva as in chalitzah, and that is when sucking; see there. And according to this, when he spat blood it is worse, for fruit juice with water hastens chimutz; see siman 462.
