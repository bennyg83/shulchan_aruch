He should chew. See Baer Heitev; and see in the new responsa of the Bach, siman 6, that regarding giving a sick person to eat and drink even rabbinic chametz, one should be stringent when there is no danger; and if the remedy is drinking barley beer or other remedies from chametz, he may eat and drink only when there is danger, and also the remedy is known, and also it is impossible to do it in a permitted way as with a prohibited way (namely, that it is possible to do it without delay and without postponement), as the Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 151; and see above siman 465.
