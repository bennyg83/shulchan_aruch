[1] He should not chew, etc. It implies that it is permitted to chew them and swallow them, for they do not cause chimutz immediately (Magen Avraham). And this is the wording of Lechem Mishneh, chapter Kol Sha'ah: so long as he chews and occupies himself with chewing, it does not come to chimutz; and from here it appears to me that it is permitted to eat dough that did not become chametz without baking; until here his wording. And in the responsa of the Ramban, siman 103, and the Rashba, siman 488: one who bites a grain with his teeth does not thereby come to chimutz ever; until here. And that is specifically that chewing afterward causes chimutz, but not a mere bite; and see siman 453 se'if 3.
