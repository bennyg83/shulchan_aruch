1) [Se'if 1] A person may not chew wheat, etc. This implies that one may chew and swallow it, because it does not become chametz immediately. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Chok Yaakov, subsection 2; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Mekor Chaim, subsection 2; Chemed Moshe, subsection 1. As long as one is actively chewing and working the grain, it does not become chametz. Eliyah Rabbah, there, in the name of Lechem Mishneh, chapter Kol Sha'ah; Eshel Avraham, subsection 1. Nevertheless, one must afterward clean one's teeth so that no small amount remains and later becomes chametz.
