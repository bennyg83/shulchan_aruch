Sift flour. Moist place like dough remains above; if need not eat flour on Yom Tov forbidden sift on Yom Tov (507)—sift through gentile to destroy chametz; though sifting Torah melacha here no waste—not father melacha; if wishes give gentile gift before mil measure (467, 458:2):

For it crumbles and mixes. Although flour in flour nullified in sixty—visible crumbs reawaken as 467:4—forbidden eat, permitted retain (and see EH 104, Beit Yosef 115). If dried on Passover like chametz mixed on Passover—for while moist did not mix, mixed on Passover—forbidden retain as Rav's son-in-law reason. Bach: mice ate flour—if dried before Passover sift and keep after Passover; found on Passover all forbidden benefit; sack unmoved—take bite place, tie, sift all sack, may retain, forbidden eat Passover—all when mouse ate flour; bit only sack doubt ate nothing—even Passover may retain, forbidden eat, even before Passover lest chametz dried crumbles—end. I explained all this in responsum. Maharam Lublin 119 on mouse-bitten sacks sifted—pressure: take other guarded flour first two nights, bake matzot from bitten flour before Passover; sifted flour from that sieve—no stringency, all permitted—end. Taz: even water fell and dried, if recognizes moist place take and sift rest; certainly mouse bites around bite, sift rest, eat Passover—end.
