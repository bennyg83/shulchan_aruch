And if impossible, etc., sift flour, etc.—this is Rokeach's view; although Rosh wrote on this view it is great leniency, Mechaber relied since Mordechai agrees—only when dried Mordechai also stringent like Rosh; Mechaber ruled stringently not like Rokeach. See Netziv and Magen Alef who appear not to have decided this:

And if dried on Passover, etc.—see Mishna Berurah. Shulchan Aruch implies even if sifting was on Passover may retain; Magen Alef doubts since mixing occurs through sieve holes—why permit retain? Source in Beit Yosef also requires sifting before Passover [if not sifted before Passover throw all—but no difference dried before or on Passover, only before Passover advice to sift]; Pri Megadim also strained; Magen Alef agreed Mechaber means dried before Passover has remedy by sifting, on Passover no remedy, forbidden to retain; only manner of speech that before Passover they are careful to sift—end:
