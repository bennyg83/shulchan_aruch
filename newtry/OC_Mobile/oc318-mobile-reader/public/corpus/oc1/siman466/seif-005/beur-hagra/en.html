465—dew chametzs. Yerushalmi chapter 3 end halacha 1: one said on herbs permitted, on gleanings forbidden; another switches—R' Chanania son of R' Hillel: even per one who permits on herbs, on gleanings from six hours onward dew damages them; although at four hours dew melts, moisture does not pass until heat of day. They also said Yerushalmi end Terumot halacha 2: R' Yochanan in name of Rashbi—if one says seven liquids, tell him dew and water are one kind and they counted two—see there and in verse 'and he wrung full bowl of water':

And likewise urine, etc. As chapter 6 of Machshirin mishnah 5—they are offspring of water:

But blood and milk. They are seven liquids not included in water like urine and the rest; Yerushalmi spoke only of dew as above; in milk they said chapter 21 Yoma 1, etc.:
