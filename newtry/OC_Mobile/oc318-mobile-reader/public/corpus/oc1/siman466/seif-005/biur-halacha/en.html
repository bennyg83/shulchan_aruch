And likewise urine—see Mishna Berurah. Mishnah Machshirin chapter 6: urine of adults and minors; Rosh and Raavad: adults—from urethra opening. Apparent contradiction: chicken droppings and animal dung do not leaven—Tur rules sack chickens sat on, moist from droppings, flour there not chametz [see Bach and Taz]. I found in Acharonim: thick excrement—not that truly thick how flour in sack became dough from droppings above; rather not fully soft—for if fully soft counted as urethra discharge [see Pri Megadim; it appears to me also his intent when much moisture]. In Chayei Adam two views on soft excrement: some like fruit juice, some never leavens—second is poskim who did not distinguish thick and soft; first author unknown who distinguishes—only Acharonim, leaning like urine that leavens]. Nevertheless even fully soft like water I greatly doubt: Tosefta challenged Rosh on Tiberias water—see there; without Rosh/Raavad I would say intent adult or minor urine, unlike eight-month infant in next mishnah—end. Later Rambam chapter 10 Laws Food Impurity copies mishnah—appears plain like our words [see Chut HaShani siman 30 on Rambam; Acharonim on Seder Taharot attribute to Gra explain plainly]. Many lean from Rosh due to Tosefta; therefore even soft like water same as excrement here. Chut HaShani sides even per Rosh mishnah not on soft excrement as urine for making fit—Tiberias proves—but his words leave no view on soft urine for fit and leavening. However all written per poskim who equate leavening and making fit—what counts as water offspring for fit also leavens; what not counted there not water for leavening. Some Acharonim hold not necessary—therefore urine of animal, dog, mouse leavens though Tosafeta says animal urine does not make fit [see Acharonim]; with another doubt certainly lenient as I wrote—end:

And human sweat—see Mishna Berurah. On chicken droppings, Maamar Mordechai 104 ruled one who bought wheat sack found dry cat droppings—permitted for Passover; though one should not learn from chicken droppings in Rokeach—birds differ for no urine concern as non-nurslings lack urine place (Shaarei Shamayim maamar 4); cats one may fear urination like dung placed there and leavened—nevertheless do not establish prohibition; perhaps no urine or far from wheat or perhaps not leavened; even if slightly leavened only minority of minority; urine law disputed in Beit Yosef; Pri Chadash and Chafetz Chayim: only human urine leavens—without visible spoilage in wheat do not be stringent else no grain without this doubt—primary—end:
