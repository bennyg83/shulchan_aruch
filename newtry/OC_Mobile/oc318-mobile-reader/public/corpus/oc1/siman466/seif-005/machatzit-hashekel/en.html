(S.k. 5) And some say: this requires further study, for on the contrary, etc. That is to say, it is known that the way of Rav Yosef Karo [Beis Yosef] is that the first view is primary in his eyes; and here, on the contrary, the view of "some forbid" is fitting to be primary.
And also the Rosh did not dispute. That is to say, the Rokeach wrote that wall sweat, even though it is not called a liquid regarding hechsher for tumah, nevertheless regarding chimutz it is called a liquid, similar to human saliva, which is not machshir and even so causes chimutz. And the Rosh challenged: why does he compare sweat to saliva? On the contrary, he should have compared wall sweat to human sweat; and just as human sweat does not cause chimutz, so too wall sweat does not cause chimutz. And even though the Rosh challenged his proof, nevertheless we do not find that he disputed him for practical law, as written in siman 462 se'if 6. See there in Magen Avraham s.k. 6 who brought the Tur's words on this: that it received [moisture] while still attached. Even though it is explained above in siman 462 that even though all grapes had rain fall upon them while attached, and likewise regarding singeing two ears of grain at the beginning of siman 463, even though rain fell upon them while attached, even so they changed when absorbed into the grapes or into the wheat and are called fruit juice; and one must say that wood is different.
