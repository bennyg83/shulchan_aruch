(22) Dew, etc.: all these that cause chimutz means if it stood without being worked for the measure of chimutz, for they are no worse than water, which through being worked does not come to chimutz.
(23) And likewise urine: that is, of a human, whether adult or child; but of an animal and other living creatures there are disputes among the poskim whether it causes chimutz.
(24) And water of the mouth and nose, etc.: whether of a human or of other living creatures; and the reason in all this is that all these are a toldah of water and are like water.
(25) But blood: and likewise bile-water also does not cause chimutz, for they are like blood and putrid mucus; and if so it is permitted to make dough from it for medicine.
(26) And milk: with a kamatz; and likewise milk with a chirik [chelev/fat] [and likewise fat], even boiling and clear, and all the more so when congealed; and the reason in all this is that it is only like fruit juice and does not cause chimutz. And even though milk and blood are otherwise forbidden, nevertheless they are nullified in sixty; which is not so for chametz on Pesach, which is in any amount.
(27) And human sweat: and animal sweat, see above in siman 453 se'if 7 and in the Mishnah Berurah there. And feces, whether of a human or of other living creatures, do not cause chimutz; therefore if a sack of flour became moistened with feces, he sifts the flour and the dough found there he throws outside, and the rest is permitted. And this is specifically regarding feces; but regarding water that comes from the orifice of the rectum, and likewise feces that are very soft, some say their law is like urine above. The poskim wrote that wheat found in animal dung, even if softened, we do not say they became chametz through the moisture found there, for dung does not cause chimutz, as above.
(28) It does not cause chimutz: behold they are all equal in this, that it does not cause chimutz when alone; but nevertheless there is a distinction among them: milk [and likewise fat and suet and blood] has the law of fruit juice according to all, and with water it hastens chimutz, and its law is as above in siman 462 se'if 2; see there. But human sweat, and likewise feces of all living creatures: several later authorities hold that it does not cause chimutz at all, and there are who are stringent in this as well.
