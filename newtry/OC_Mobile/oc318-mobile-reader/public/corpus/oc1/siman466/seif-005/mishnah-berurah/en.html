(22) Dew, etc.—all these leaven if stood without occupation for leavening time—not worse than water—for through occupation does not leaven:

(23) And likewise urine—human adult or minor; but animal and other creatures—dispute whether leavens:

(24) And water of mouth, nose, etc.—human or other creatures—for all are offspring of water and are like water:

(25) But blood—and likewise bile does not leaven—like blood and putrid moisture—permitted make dough for healing:

(26) And milk—in Kamma; likewise milk in brine [and fat] even boiling clear or congealed—for only like fruit juice does not leaven; though milk and blood otherwise forbidden—nullified in sixty unlike chametz on Passover in smallest amount:

(27) And human sweat—and animal sweat see above 453:7 and Mishna Berurah there. Excrement human or other creatures does not leaven—if sack moistened from excrement sift flour, discard dough found, rest permitted. Specifically excrement; but urethra discharge and very soft excrement—some say like urine above. Poskim: wheat in animal dung even soft—we do not say leavened from moisture there—for dung does not leaven as above:

(28) Does not leaven—all equal when alone; nevertheless difference: milk [and milk, fat, blood] like fruit juice for all—with water hastens leaven as 462:2 above; human sweat and excrement of creatures—many Acharonim hold never leavens, some stringent also:
