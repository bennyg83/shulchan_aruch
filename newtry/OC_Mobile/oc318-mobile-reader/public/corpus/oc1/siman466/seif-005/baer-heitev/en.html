And milk. The same applies to milk with a chirik [chelev/fat], even boiling and clear, and all the more so when congealed; Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah. And see above siman 462 regarding the law of fat; and Magen Avraham wrote that likewise bile-water also does not cause chimutz (for it is only mucus and blood); and if so it is permitted to make dough from it for medicine; and Peri Chadash wrote that the same applies to milk-water, that it does not cause chimutz. And in Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 211, he wrote that koum does cause chimutz; see there. And see Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah. Chicken dung does not cause chimutz; Tur. And the same applies to wheat in animal dung; Rokeach. And regarding human feces Chok Yaakov is stringent; see there. And it is obvious that all these that the Tur and the Mechaber wrote here that cause chimutz means if it stood without being worked for the measure of chimutz, for they are no worse than water, which through being worked does not come to chimutz.
