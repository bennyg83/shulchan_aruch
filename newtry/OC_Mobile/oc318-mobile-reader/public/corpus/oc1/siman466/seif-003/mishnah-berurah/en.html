(6) The wall—and likewise wooden wall that sweats:

(7) Permitted—no difference whether sweat from little air as cellars/caves stones sweat, or from water in wall as new brick walls sometimes sweat before drying, or moist wood sweating—all sweat does not leaven as impurity law sweat not water for making fit. Nevertheless not better than fruit juice—if fruit juice with water hastens leaven as 462—do not knead l'chatchila; if moist still sift and retain moist until after Passover; remainder knead and bake for Passover. Poskim: all this wall sweat; if sweat from heat and steam of hot blood in it as hot water tub and sack moistened thereby—for all it is water offspring and leavens:

(8) And some forbid—do not learn chametz from impurity making fit—for leavening any water moisture can leaven; some give reason wall sweats from water within; even wood wall like water for often rain absorption on walls, sometimes after cut from ground—per this reason stone/metal/glass sweat also not leaven like fruit juice.
