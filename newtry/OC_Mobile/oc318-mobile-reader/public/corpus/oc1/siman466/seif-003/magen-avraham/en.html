From the sweat of the wall. So it should read; and see siman 453 se'if 7.
And some forbid. This requires further study, for on the contrary most forbid: the Rokeach; and also the Rosh did not dispute him for practice; and also in the Remazim it is written without qualification that it causes chimutz; and so wrote Rabbeinu Yeruham; and so it is implied from the Tur's wording; only in Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 5 he wrote to permit. And it appears to me that the reason of those who forbid is because the wall sweats from the water that is in it, as written in siman 462 se'if 6 regarding dry meat; and according to this, regarding a wooden wall that sweats, according to all it is permitted; and it is possible that it too has moisture of water that it received while still attached.
