Seif 3: a bag full of flour that became moist from sweat of the wall is permitted, etc. Marginal note: see Tur what he wrote on dry meat that sweats that it leavens; and see Magen Avraham above siman 462 who wrote the reason from the fact that when it sweats it expels water and it is visible — see there; the intent is: since it was rinsed with water, the water was absorbed in it, and even though it later dried, nevertheless afterward when it sweats it returns and expels. I was uncertain: if the first rinsing was with water and the last rinsing was with fruit juice, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 69 that the last rinsing with fruit juice helps, and afterward the meat dried and again sweated — whether it leavens or not. Shall we say it sweats only from the last, from the fruit juice, and does not chametz? Or shall we say it sweats only from the first alone and it is water? Or does it sweat from both, and it is fruit juice with water and it is stiff chametz in any case? It appears this law is an explicit Mishnah in chapter 2 of Orlah 13, and thus: vessels that one anointed with impure oil and returned and anointed with pure oil, or anointed with pure oil and returned and anointed with impure oil — R' Eliezer says: after the first I come; and the Sages say: after the last; and see Rambam, Rosh, and Ra'avad who explained that when both dried, then when it sweats afterward and expels — for R' Eliezer we say it expels the first, and for the Sages we say it expels the last; and we rule like the Sages; if so, it is proven that if one absorbs one after the other, only the last is not expelled; if so, here too, since it was rinsed, with fruit juice it does not expel except the fruit juice, not the water of the first rinsing — therefore it does not chametz, as it appears to me correct and clear. As for the matter itself of dry meat that the Tur inserted in the middle of the words of R' Eliezer from Germiza — uncertain: the Rosh brought these words in the name of R' Eliezer, and these words about dry meat he did not bring at all; and these words of the Tur are copied from the words of the Rosh in chapter 2 of Pesachim — why did he insert in the middle these things the Rosh did not write? And if they are explained in Rokeach himself, why did the Rosh omit them? And if he does not hold thus, why did the Tur rule them? — requires a little study.
