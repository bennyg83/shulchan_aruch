Se'if 3. A sack full of flour that became moist from the sweat of the wall is permitted, etc. Marginal note: see in the Tur what he wrote regarding dry meat that sweats, that it causes chimutz; and see Magen Avraham above in siman 462 who wrote the reason: because when it sweats it releases the water and it is visible; see there. And the intent is that since it was rinsed with water, the water was absorbed in it, and even though it dried, nevertheless afterward when it sweats it returns and releases. And behold I was in doubt: if it was rinsed with a first rinsing in water and the last rinsing was rinsed with fruit juice, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 69 that a last rinsing with fruit juice helps, and afterward the meat dried and again sweated: does it cause chimutz or not? Do we say that it sweats only from the last, from the fruit juice, and it does not cause chimutz; or do we say that it sweats from the first alone and it is water; or that it sweats from this and from that, and it is fruit juice with water, and it is in any case hard chametz [chametz nuksheh]? And behold it appears that this law is an explicit mishnah in Orlah chapter 2 mishnah 13, and this is its wording: vessels that one smeared with tamei oil and again smeared with tahor oil, or that one smeared with tahor oil and again smeared with tamei oil: R' Eliezer says, I follow the first; and the Sages say, [I follow] the last. And see the Rambam and the Rash and Rabbeinu Ovadiah of Bartenura who explained that that is when both dried, then when it afterward sweats and releases: according to R' Eliezer we say it releases the first, and according to the Sages we say it releases the last; and we hold like the Sages. If so it is proven that if it absorbs one after the other, only the last is released. If so here too, since it was rinsed finally with fruit juice, it releases only the fruit juice, not the water of the first rinsing; and therefore it does not cause chimutz, as appears correct and clear to my humble opinion. And the core of this matter of dry meat that the Tur inserted in the middle of the words of Rabbeinu Eliezer of Worms requires further study: for the Rosh brought these words in the name of Rabbeinu Eliezer, and these words of dry meat he did not bring at all; and these words of the Tur are copied from the words of the Rosh in Pesachim chapter 2; and why did he insert in the middle these words that the Rosh did not write? And if they are explained in the Rokeach itself, why did the Rosh omit them? And if he does not hold thus, why did the Tur rule them? And this requires some further study; and analyze carefully.
