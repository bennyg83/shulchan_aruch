[3] For saliva, etc. My brother-in-law wrote in Chok Yaakov: it appears to me even if she spat blood, for it is impossible without flecks of saliva, as stated in Yevamos folio 105; and that is when sucking; until here. And this requires further study in the Levush, Even HaEzer siman 169 se'if 40: according to the first reasoning, even without sucking it is impossible without saliva; and in truth at first glance that is not the reason, but rather that he holds that even if it is entirely blood the chalitzah is valid; and this requires further study. One who spat putrid mucus: it is not machshir, like the poskim of Machshirin.
