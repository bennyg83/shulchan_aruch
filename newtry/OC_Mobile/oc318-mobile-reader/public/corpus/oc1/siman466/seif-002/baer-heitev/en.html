Nullified. This does not refer to nullification before Pesach, for this was not included in the bitul since it became chametz afterward, as written in siman 446; rather, since he placed it before his animal, he nullified it and abandoned it of his own mind; and nevertheless it is good to give them little by little so that they leave none; Chok Yaakov; see there.
Slime. The Bach wrote that the Mordechai as well forbids only when slime is found; and so [he wrote] in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, and he challenged the Rav here since the Rokeach explained his words explicitly as specifically when slime is found. And Chok Yaakov wrote: and nevertheless it appears to me that this is specifically regarding a horse and the like; but regarding a cow, which ordinarily has slime, certainly one must destroy as a matter of course; therefore he should give before it little by little as written.
