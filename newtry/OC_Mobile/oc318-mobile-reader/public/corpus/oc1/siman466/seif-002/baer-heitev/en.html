Nullified. This does not refer to nullification before Passover, for this was not in the category of nullification once it leavened afterward as siman 446; rather since he gave before his animal it was nullified and he renounced it from his mind; nevertheless it is good to give them little by little so they leave none. Saliva. Bach wrote that Mordechai also forbids only when saliva is found; so in Shach; and he challenged the Rav here since Rokeach explained explicitly only when saliva is found (and Mahari Weil) that in unspecified one must burn all remainder—this is certainly extra stringency—end.
