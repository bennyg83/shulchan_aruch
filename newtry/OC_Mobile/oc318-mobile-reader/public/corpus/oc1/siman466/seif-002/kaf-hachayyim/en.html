Although one does not violate the prohibition against chametz being seen with less than an olive-sized quantity, one may nevertheless be obligated under Torah law to eliminate it, just as half the prohibited measure is forbidden for consumption under Torah law; the same may apply to elimination. Peri Chadash. See, however, Erech HaShulchan, subsection 1, who discusses this at length and writes that half the measure in such a case is prohibited only rabbinically. He writes that one may leave this poultice when made from nullified chametz if it contains less than an olive-sized quantity. See the full discussion there. See above, siman 442, subsection 77.
