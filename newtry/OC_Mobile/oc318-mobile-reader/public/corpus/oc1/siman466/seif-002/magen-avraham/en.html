And if so it is a rabbinic doubt; and this requires further study, for he did not say on erev Pesach anything but "all chametz that exists," and this that became chametz now was not included in the bitul, as written in siman 446. And even if you say that before he gives to his animal he says that all leftovers that remain should be nullified, nevertheless he must destroy them lest his animal return and eat them, as written at the end of siman 448; therefore one should practice as Mahariv and Maharil wrote: that he give them little by little so that they leave none.
