Permitted. See Taz: it implies he reads "sweat of the wall" or "donkey"; see Magen Avraham 5 to distinguish between a stone wall and wood; and see Peri Chadash here. And see siman 453 in Magen Avraham s.k. 10 regarding animal sweat: after the fact one can say it does not cause chimutz; and see Chok Yaakov there.
And behold. It is proper to mention here that one who studies the Bach implies there are three species: water, and fruit juice, and a toldah of its own, like salt in siman 462; and chicken dung is a toldah of its own. And if water, they do not hasten chimutz except for the measure of a mil; and see the Levush that it is similar to chametz and forbidden by rabbinic [law], which implies fruit juice with water is hard chametz; see there; and see Or Zarua.
And behold. What is written [that] a toldah of water is like water: and above in siman 160 se'if 10, a toldah of water if one crushed a fish eye for netilas yadayim; and in Yoreh De'ah beginning of siman 93; see there. And this requires further study regarding fish fat on Pesach, whether it is fruit juice like goose fat: for at first glance it is not an absolute general rule to say that a toldah of water causes chimutz, and you have only what Chazal wrote explicitly. And behold among the seven liquids some are machshir and receive tumah and do not cause chimutz; and see Tum'as Ochalin chapter 10 law 2: animal urine is not a toldah of water, and human urine is a toldah of water, and that is a Tosefta; so Kesef Mishnah there. And in Machshirin chapter 6 mishnah 5: human urine whether of adults or of minors is a toldah of water; and the Rash explained there: liquids that come from the orifice of the rectum: behold human feces do not cause chimutz; see there mishnah 7, feces, etc.; and in the Rambam, Tum'as Ochalin chapter 10; and see Chok Yaakov. See in Machshirin there mishnah 6: and his flux and spit convey tumah and are machshir; and in mishnah 7, the hot springs of Tiberias neither convey tumah nor are machshir; and if so this requires much further study, for one may not learn from those that are machshir and convey tumah that they cause chimutz, nor from what is not machshir that it does not cause chimutz; and the hot springs of Tiberias imply certainly that they cause chimutz; and see siman 425. And see Tiferes Yisrael there on mishnah 5 regarding the hot springs of Tiberias by way of the urethra; see there. Sour apples: see Magen Avraham 462 and Peri Chadash here regarding red vinegar; see there. Woman's milk: Eliyah Rabbah left in letter 12 as requiring further study; milk with a chirik is fruit juice. And blood and milk with a chirik that are fruit juice for stringency and are not nullified in sixty; or fish blood and human blood where there is no positive commandment. And animal urine: see Peri Chadash and Chok Yaakov; and God willing in letter 4 it will be explained further.
