And one may not. See Taz; Rashi, Beitzah 32b and 34a: for it is fashioning a utensil, regarding "one may not split a reed," meaning to place [it] upon a grate; the same applies to paper and pottery, and [regarding] pieces of paper one can say for this reason they are forbidden; see Hilchos Shabbos; and in the responsa of Ralbach, regarding a siddur that they tore because of the controversy, and tearing it is not comparable to [tearing] in order to sew; perhaps the same applies to anything that is not destructive. And what the Shulchan Aruch wrote, "one may split a reed for roasting," is the explanation of the Rambam, of blessed memory.
