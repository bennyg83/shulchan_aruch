The stones. Such is the reading in the Rambam before us; but in the Maggid Mishneh and in Beis Yosef it is implied that the Rambam's text is "the tiles," and so it is in the Gemara and in all the Poskim; but as for stones, it is permitted to perform libun on them, for chissum does not apply to them.
Because one hardens them. And even old ones are forbidden, for at every moment he strengthens them further; alternatively, this is what it means: because one must perform libun so thoroughly until they become hardened (Maggid Mishneh). See siman 509, seif 5; and in the Gemara: one may not split a reed to place its membrane under the fish on the grate, for anything that one prepares for use is considered fashioning a utensil (Rashi). See siman 507, seif 3.
