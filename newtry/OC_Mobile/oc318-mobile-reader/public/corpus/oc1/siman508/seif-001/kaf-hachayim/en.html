(1) [Seif 1] One does not whiten the stones, etc. — so is the girsa in Rambam before us; but in Maggid Mishneh and Beit Yosef it implies Rambam's text is "tiles," and so in the Gemara and all poskim; but stones are permitted to whiten for blocking does not apply — Magen Avraham note 1; Shulchan Gavoah 2 and in Rambam's Mishnah commentary — see there; and so girsa in Ahavat Chesed for Rashba Bava Metzia sha'ar 2 ch. 6: tiles; and so R' Chananel and Meiri and Rokeach siman 299; and so Raz siman 1. However in Kol Bo siman 58 wrote both: one does not whiten tiles nor new stones, etc.; and so Orach Chayim 88:3 in Kol Bo's language; and so Levush; and so in Sha'arei Teshuvah; see further in Orach Chayim Achronim.
