Seif 1: the stones. See Magen Avraham.
Because one hardens them. In the Gemara there, folio 34a; and the Rambam explains that according to the latter version one need not [require that they be] new; and what was said there, "here we are dealing with a new pot," is specifically according to the first version; and therefore in chapter 9 of Hilchos Shabbos he likewise did not write "a new pot." But the Raavad there, and the Maggid Mishneh in Hilchos Yom Tov in the name of the Rashba, disagree with him; and see what I wrote in siman 498, seif 8.
