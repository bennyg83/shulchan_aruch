With a long knife. The stringency apparently applies only to ordinary people, who may not eat in a study hall and bring the knife for no permitted use. A scholar permitted to eat and drink there may bring the knife for that use, just as during any meal the knife need be covered only after use, when he begins Birkas HaMazon.
Or with an uncovered head. This is forbidden even where people customarily appear that way before officials. Before the Omnipresent it is levity, as though he lacks awe of the Divine Presence; a hat inspires awe and solemnity before Him [Levush].
