With an uncovered head, etc. The Beit Yosef writes this concerning Orchot Chaim's report that Maharam objected to entering a synagogue with a long knife or a purse. He says that the Rif glossed that the only concern is an uncovered head. This is surprising because Orchot Chaim says nothing about an uncovered head. I examined Kol Bo, folio 10, which quotes the passage as saying, 'However, there is concern only when it is uncovered.' Tashbetz, siman 202, has the same wording. It is obvious to me that this refers to the knife and purse: they are problematic only when uncovered, but permitted when covered. The passage does not discuss an uncovered head at all. This is surprising regarding the authors of the Shulchan Aruch, Levush, and later authorities.
