6) A person should not kiss his children in a synagogue, to demonstrate that there is no love like the love of the Holy One, blessed is He. Binyamin Ze'ev, siman 163, in the name of Sefer HaAgudah. So too writes Sefer Chassidim, siman 255; Knesset HaGedolah there. The Rama also writes this above, in the gloss to siman 98, at the end of seif 1. This implies, however, that one may kiss the hands of a Torah scholar, for this honors Hashem, as it is written, 'You shall fear Hashem your God,' which includes Torah scholars. Likewise, in a place where the custom is to kiss the hand of one's father or rav when he descends from an aliyah to the Sefer Torah, it is permitted, for this too is a mitzvah. So too writes Ben Ish Chai, parashas Vayikra, paragraph 11.
