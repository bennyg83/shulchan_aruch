Responsa of R' Moshe of Trani, volume 3, siman 84; Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot HaTur; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1; Magen Avraham there; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1. The Eshel Avraham writes there: 'I am uncertain regarding the courtyard before the synagogue. Perhaps it is permitted when the slaughter is needed for the kapparos of Erev Yom Kippur. This matter deserves great attention. It is forbidden in a women's section where people pray. Regarding the antechamber, even though people pray there, it was explicitly built conditionally; this requires further study.' Petach HaDevir, paragraph 1, cites him. See there, where he discusses this at length and writes that one may slaughter in a synagogue courtyard where people do not pray. If people pray there when space is limited, however, one may not slaughter there. He writes that the same applies to the open courtyard of a Talmud Torah where no one studies, either regularly or occasionally, because all study takes place in its rooms and the courtyard is used only for entering and leaving. It is permitted to slaughter there; see there. See below, paragraph 10, and the beginning of siman 154.
