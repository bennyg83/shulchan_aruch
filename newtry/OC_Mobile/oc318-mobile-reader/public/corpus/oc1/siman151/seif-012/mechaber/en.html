One should be careful not to use the floor above the synagogue for a regular use that is disrespectful. For instance, sleeping there. It is unclear if other uses are permissible or not. {Rama: Rem"a: All of this is specifically regarding a permanent synagogue that was built originally like that, but a house that was designated to be a synagogue after it was built, it is permitted to sleep above it (Piskei Mahar"i).}
