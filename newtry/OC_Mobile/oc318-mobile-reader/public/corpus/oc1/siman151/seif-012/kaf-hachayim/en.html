(12) There — Torah scholars and their students are permitted, etc. — Taz wrote in note 1 that guests may eat there, for it is stated in siman 269 that guests eat in synagogues and it is like kiddush in place of a meal; and so Beit Yosef brought in this siman in the name of Ramban — end of his words; and so Birkei Yosef note 3 in the name of R' Yeshaya the First in teshuva Beit Yosef siman 88 that even in synagogue of Eretz Yisrael guests may eat and drink there even though they are not Torah scholars — they forbade only to city residents — end of his words. And they brought Erech HaShulchan note 1 and wrote that so is the view of Semag positive commandment 29 and Orach Chayim 63 that it is like a mitzvah meal which is permitted — see there; however in Aruch HaShulchan note 2 he wondered at Taz's words, for Tosafot Megillah 28 wrote that there it speaks of a room adjacent to synagogue but in synagogue itself forbidden — so too Tosafot and Rosh at the start of Bava Batra; and also what he said that so Beit Yosef in name of Ramban is not proof, for it speaks of synagogue in Babylon made on condition that this is permitted as permitted by stipulation on ark and holy uses in siman 153 seif 8 — end of his words; and so Magen Avraham in siman 269 note 2 that there it speaks of house adjacent to synagogue but in synagogue forbidden to eat — see there; and so Birkei Yosef in his words in this siman note 3 on Taz's words that it does not appear so from words of poskim — see there; and so is view of Ran at the start of parashat Eikev; and so Maggid Mishneh in chapter 29 of Shabbat; and so Meiri end of Megillah; and so Tosafot and Mordechai there that even in synagogue of Babylon condition does not help for this matter except after they are destroyed — see there. And see in Rashbash siman 274 who elaborated on this and wrote that for Torah scholars no condition is needed, but for guests who are not Torah scholars one must stipulate thus explicitly — see there; and Beit Din Erech HaShulchan there; and see further in Sefer HaGan on Rif chapter Bnei HaIr note 3 who wrote in name of Riaz that what Gemara says synagogues of Babylon were made on condition — was said only regarding guests, for in Babylon guests passing from place to place are common — see there; it is found this matter is disputed: one says even in synagogue of Babylon condition does not help for this except after destruction; and one says it helps even at time of building; and one says even in synagogue of Eretz Yisrael where condition does not help, guests may eat there. And therefore since there is dispute, ideally guests should be careful in all possible ways not to eat in synagogue even of chutz la'aretz; and in pressing time one may rely on those who permit; and see below seif 11.
