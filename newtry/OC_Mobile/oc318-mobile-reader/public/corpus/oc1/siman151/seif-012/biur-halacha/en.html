There is uncertainty. This concerns using the upper floor, a separate room whose use is not publicly visible. Using the synagogue roof itself (for example, spreading clothes to dry or fruit where roofs are flat) is plainly forbidden because it is visible to all. An explicit Mishnah, Megillah 28, forbids spreading fruit on its roof even after ruin, and the Yerushalmi expressly forbids spreading clothes.
One may lie upon it. There is no distinction between synagogues in Eretz Yisrael and the Diaspora [Eliyah Rabbah, unlike Magen Avraham; the Gra also implies this].
