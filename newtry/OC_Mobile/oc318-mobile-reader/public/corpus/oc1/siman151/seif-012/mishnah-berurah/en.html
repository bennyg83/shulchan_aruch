(39) One may not use the roof. A fixed degrading use is forbidden. Occasional lying there may perhaps be permitted.
(40) There is uncertainty. The upper levels of the Temple courtyard were not sanctified, whereas the upper levels above the Sanctuary were sacred. Shaarei Teshuvah, citing Paar HaDor, is lenient regarding a room above a synagogue, except directly above the Aron Kodesh.
(41) For a regular use. If a separate residence was built above the synagogue, its use is permitted, but filth is always forbidden there. Taz relates a punishment that resulted from such conduct. Where rooms are only temporarily rented above a synagogue, the common leniency may have a basis.
(42) One should be careful. A conscientious person should avoid lying there, especially directly above the Aron Kodesh.
