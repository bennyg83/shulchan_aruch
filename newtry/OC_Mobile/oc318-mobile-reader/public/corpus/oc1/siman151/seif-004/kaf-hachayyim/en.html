4) One person sits and stretches out his leg; another sits while leaning on his hand; another stands and paces back and forth; another chats with his fellow about idle matters. Another diverts his attention and looks this way and that. These and other practices are neither seemly nor proper in the House of God. Sefer HaZichronos, folio 64; Knesset HaGedolah there; Olas Tamid there.
