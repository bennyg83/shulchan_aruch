(17) A guardian. Meaning a person guarding the synagogue.
(18) To sleep there. Magen Avraham says this is without bringing in a bed; Eliyah Rabbah and Rabbi Akiva Eiger disagree.
(19) For the sake of the synagogue. Such as sleeping there to guard its lamps.
(20) A mitzvah meal. The meal for intercalating the year consisted only of bread and legumes and involved no intoxication. Regarding a siyum or similar mitzvah meal, if there is no large place available elsewhere, one should not protest.
