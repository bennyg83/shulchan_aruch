Paragraph 4: And to sleep, etc. Tamid at the beginning of chapter 1 teaches that the Chamber of the Hearth was built half within the Temple and half outside it. Its non-sacred half was surrounded by stone ledges upon which the elders of the priestly watch guarding the Temple slept. The Gemara asks why they did not bring beds in (meaning, why sleep on stones) and Abaye answers that bringing beds into the Temple is improper. Magen Avraham holds that a synagogue's sanctity is greater than that of the Chamber of the Hearth, or at least no less: the half built outside had only the sanctity of the Temple Mount, while Mordechai and Maharik write that a synagogue's sanctity may resemble that of the Sanctuary. For this reason they forbid use of the roof above a synagogue, as explained at this siman's end.
