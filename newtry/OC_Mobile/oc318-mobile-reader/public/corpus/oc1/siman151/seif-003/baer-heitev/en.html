In the synagogue. One is obligated to rebuke those who sleep in the synagogue, and all the more so those who sleep during the sermon, for then one transgression leads to another and the person turns his ear away from hearing Torah. If someone sneezes in the synagogue, one does not say to him, "To your health." Maimoniyos. See Taz. Eliyah Rabbah permits this where interruption is permitted, and adduces proof for his view. See Sefer Chasidim, siman 1011. If one finds an object in the synagogue courtyard, he acquires it; we do not say that the courtyard acquires it for sacred property. Agudah, beginning of Me'ilah. Children's chatter in a synagogue is a grave prohibition. Shelah.
