To use it as a path. Unless he reads or remains there briefly, as stated above.
To shorten his route. Rambam says one may enter only for a mitzvah purpose. Pri Megadim suggests that walking to perform a mitzvah may permit the shortcut. This is unpersuasive: Rambam concerns one whose entry into the synagogue itself was for a mitzvah, after which he may also shorten his route. Here he deliberately enters only to shorten his route; the intended mitzvah can be performed without turning the synagogue into a shortcut, so it is reasonable to forbid it.
