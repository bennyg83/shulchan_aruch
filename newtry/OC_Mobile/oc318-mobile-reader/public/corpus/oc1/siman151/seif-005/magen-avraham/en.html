As a shortcut. Rambam permits entering only for a mitzvah. See se'if 1.
It is permitted for one who entered. Rambam omits the intermediate case. The Tur states that if one entered in order to pray, he may make it a shortcut, as the verse says that one who entered by the northern gate would leave by the southern gate. Beis Yosef writes that both cases are permitted, though the additional phrase concerning ascending is difficult. The explanation is that if he entered to pray, even if he also intended to use it as a shortcut, it is permitted because his principal purpose is a mitzvah. This is the Tur's intent and follows the verse and Rif. The wording in Shulchan Aruch appears to contain a scribal error and should read, "It is a mitzvah for one who entered."
