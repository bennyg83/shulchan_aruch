5) If idolatry was worshiped in it, even on a regular basis, there is no prohibition against praying there. Responsa of R' Eliyahu Mizrachi, volume 1, siman 79. There he strongly protested against one who wished to say that it was forbidden to pray in a synagogue in which the attendant was discovered committing an immoral act with a youth.
