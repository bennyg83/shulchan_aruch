Frivolity. Because of this sin, synagogues are transformed into houses of idolatry. Semak. It is forbidden to slaughter an animal in a synagogue. Mabit; Magen Avraham. Re'em writes in volume 1, siman 79, that even if a transgression was committed in a synagogue (as where the attendant was found behaving wantonly with a young woman) it remains permissible to pray there, and all the synagogue's sanctity remains upon it; see there. She'eris Yosef, siman 28, writes that neighbors of a synagogue from whose property a foul odor reaches the synagogue must distance the source; see there.
