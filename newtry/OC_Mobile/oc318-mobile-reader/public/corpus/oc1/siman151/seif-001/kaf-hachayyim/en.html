1) [Seif 1] Synagogues and batei midrash may not be treated with levity, etc. This is because they are called a minor Sanctuary, and it is written, 'You shall revere My Sanctuary.' Levush. See Megillah 29a. Through this sin, a synagogue is transformed into a house of idolatry, God forbid. Magen Avraham there, paragraph 1.
