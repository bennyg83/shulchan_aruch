(28) After they were destroyed - The synagogues and batei midrash; the reason is that it is written, "I will desolate your sanctuaries," and it is not written, "I will make your sanctuaries desolate," to teach us that they are holy even when desolate. There is no distinction in this between synagogues in the Land of Israel and outside the Land, unless they stipulated otherwise, as below in seif 11.
(29) One may pluck them, etc. - That is, it is permitted to pluck them, provided that he leaves them in their place and does not take them away from there.
(30) So that it be seen, etc. - According to this, if they demolished the synagogue from this place and built it elsewhere, and they do not intend to build again in the first place, this law does not apply; rather they should make a fence around it so people will not come to treat the place lightly. If it is in a place where they can sell it, as mentioned in siman 153, they should sell it, as Taz writes.
