(28) Even after they are ruined. They retain their sanctity, as derived from the verse. This applies both in Eretz Yisrael and in the Diaspora, unless a valid stipulation was made.
(29) One may pluck the grass. But some should be left in place.
(30) So that people will see, etc. Accordingly, if they demolished the synagogue at this site and built it elsewhere, with no intention to rebuild at the first site, this rule does not apply. Instead, they should fence it so it will not be treated disrespectfully. If it is in a place where sale is permitted, as stated in siman 153, they should sell it [Taz].
