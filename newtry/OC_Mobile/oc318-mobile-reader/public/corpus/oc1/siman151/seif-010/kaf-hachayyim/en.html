10) There: One may not eat or drink in them, etc. The same applies to smoking a tobacco pipe there, for this too is included in levity, as R' Chaim Palagi writes in Sefer Chaim, siman 45, paragraph 5; see there. Yafeh LaLev cites him in Kuntres Yosher Levav, paragraph 5. See below, paragraph 13. Likewise, one may not perform work there. Rashi on Megillah 28a, s.v. mafshilin chavalim; Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 3. It is also forbidden to teach there writing and reading in other languages. Jewish script that is half Ashuri, which is called Rashi script, is permitted, and all the more so actual Ashuri script. Ben Ish Chai, parashas Vayikra, paragraph 4.
