(31) If a stipulation was made. The stipulation is effective only when made at the time of construction.
(32) When it was built. An explicit stipulation is required. Magen Avraham disagrees with Masas Binyamin, while Eliyah Rabbah is lenient.
(33) To use it. The stipulation is effective only after the synagogue has fallen into ruin, but not while the building remains standing.
(34) While it is standing. A stipulation does not permit eating there or entering for shelter while it stands, but it does permit other uses. See Magen Avraham and Biur Halachah.
(35) But not for a degrading use. Such as sowing the site, and all other shameful uses stated in siman 153:9.
(36) To make public calculations. Such calculations are conspicuous. Pri Megadim says that for an individual's calculations, a stipulation helps after the building is ruined. Rabbeinu Yerucham may be introducing a novelty specifically for public calculations; all the more so would private calculations be permitted.
(37) Synagogues in the Diaspora. Their sanctity is not intended to remain forever after the redemption.
(38) It is effective. Magen Avraham says that in Eretz Yisrael a stipulation helps for uses that do not involve levity.
