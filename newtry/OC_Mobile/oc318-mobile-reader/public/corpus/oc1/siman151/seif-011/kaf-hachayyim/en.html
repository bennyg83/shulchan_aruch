11) There: One may not enter them because of the heat, etc. One who flees in fear from an enemy pursuing him furiously with a drawn sword may hide inside a synagogue, for nothing stands in the way of saving a life. Nevertheless, once his mind has settled, he can rectify the matter by reading a little in the synagogue, as written in this seif: if one needs to enter them for his own needs, etc., he should enter and read a little, etc. He thereby avoids any concern of prohibition. Petach HaDevir, paragraph 3.
