They stipulated concerning it. This applies only if they made an explicit stipulation; an unstated assumption is insufficient. See Magen Avraham.
When it is in ruins. This refers specifically to a synagogue that they do not intend to rebuild. Agudah; Magen Avraham.
A stipulation. This permits forms of ordinary use, such as eating and drinking. Sowing there, however, constitutes excessive frivolity and causes anguish. Magen Avraham.
That are outside Eretz Yisrael. See Magen Avraham. See also Tosafos in Megillah 28, beginning with the words "the synagogues of Babylonia," where they write that the discussion concerns synagogues in ruins, whereas when they are standing, the conduct is forbidden. Yet in the passage beginning "Nevertheless, one may not behave frivolously," they write that the discussion concerns them while standing, for when they are in ruins all these activities are permitted. See there. This requires study. See Maharsha and the work Gufei Halachos.
