And one does not eat and drink in them, etc. — forbidden to drink water in synagogue and bet midrash for one who is not a Torah scholar — Maharash ben Habatz siman 274; and the custom now that people drink water in synagogue — Birkei Yosef note 2; and this is if they sit there to learn — permitted, for even though they are not Torah scholars it is permitted so they will not neglect study.
