7) If one found an object in a synagogue courtyard, he acquires it, and we do not say that the courtyard acquires it for the sacred property. Agudah, beginning of Me'ilah, section 49; Magen Avraham, siman 154, paragraph 23; Eliyah Rabbah, end of paragraph 1. The Eshel Avraham writes in siman 154, paragraph 23, that the same law applies if one found it inside the synagogue itself; see there. This refers to an object that has no identifying mark, when he also did not see from whom it fell. Otherwise, he must announce it or return it, as explained in Choshen Mishpat, siman 262, seifim 3 and 4; see there.
