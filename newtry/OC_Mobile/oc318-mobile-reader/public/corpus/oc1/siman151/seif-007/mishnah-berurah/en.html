(24) One may spit there. Provided it is not during the Amidah, as above in siman 97:2. He should also take care not to spit before another person, who may be repulsed by it [Chagigah 5]. The Arizal was careful to avoid spitting.
(25) He should rub it with his foot. On Shabbos he should cover the spittle with his shoe until it is crushed.
(26) Or cover it. With a reed, straw, or sand.
