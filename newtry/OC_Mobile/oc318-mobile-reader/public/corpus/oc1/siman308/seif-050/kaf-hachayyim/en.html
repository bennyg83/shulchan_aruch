50) There, gloss: And a shofar may not be moved, etc. The reason is explained in Hagahos Ashri: a shofar is a utensil whose designated use is forbidden. Or Zarua writes that specifically a shofar, which is a utensil, may be moved when needed for its own use or its place. Trumpets, however, are not utensils and may not be moved even when needed for their own use or their place. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 13. It appears to me that this follows Rabbi Yehudah, but according to our ruling like Rabbi Shimon it is permitted. Magen Avraham, paragraph 12; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 11; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 22; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 12; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 20. The same applies to other musical instruments: they may be moved only when needed for their own use or their place, like any utensil whose designated use is forbidden, since blowing or playing a musical instrument on Shabbos is forbidden, as stated in simanim 588 and 339. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. See also the end of siman 588 and our words there, with the help of Heaven.
