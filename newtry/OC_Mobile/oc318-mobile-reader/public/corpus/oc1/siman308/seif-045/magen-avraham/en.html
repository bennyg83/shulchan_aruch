With a ball. And forbidden to move the ball, for in his thought he does not make it a vessel (Shulchan Aruch), as below.
And some permit. Shulchan Aruch HaRav wrote: Tosafot did not write to permit except on Yom Tov, since there is some need we say "from something permitted came something permitted," etc.; but not on Shabbos, investigate; and it does not appear so to me, for we do not say "from something permitted" except for the prohibition of carrying out; but for the prohibition of moving, Shabbos and Yom Tov are the same; on the contrary, some say muktzeh is forbidden on Yom Tov; if so, if permitted to move a ball on Yom Tov, a fortiori on Shabbos; rather on Yom Tov permitted even in reshut ha-rabbim, but on Shabbos forbidden in reshut ha-rabbim; and in Yeshuot Yaakov he wrote that even Tosafot hold that by law it is forbidden to play, only people practice to be lenient; and impossible to permit because it is fit to plug the mouth of a flask, one may say it is repulsive with mud and earth and not fit to cover (Beit Yosef).
