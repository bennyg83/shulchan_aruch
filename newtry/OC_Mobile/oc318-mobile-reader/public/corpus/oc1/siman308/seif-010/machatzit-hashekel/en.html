(s.k. 10) And sacred writings — books; unlike erev Shabbat; Gitin 60: scroll of haftarah — if not given to read forbidden to write complete scroll and read; impossible for everyone; therefore forbidden to move except "time to act"; Beit Yosef siman 284 permitted writing and reading and moving; here only scriptures read in public permitted; Sefer Or Zarua on Shir HaShirot: doubt on Megillat Esther; Or Zarua permitted; Hilchot Purim in Peri Chadash forbids since when Purim on Shabbat no reading — like haftarah scroll before permission.

In my opinion one may question this proof: forbidden to read publicly in congregation, nevertheless each person may learn from it, as R' Avin in Megillah 4: Purim on Shabbat on erev; if Purim on Sunday, not bring Megillah to synagogue Shabbat night for reading unless he learned something from it erev Shabbat — see Hilchot Megillah.

Because of chisaron kis — meaning: do not say muktzeh because standing for commerce, for siman 310 we rule R' Shimon fruit for commerce not muktzeh; rather muktzeh because of chisaron kis lest it soil; even R' Shimon concedes when particular not to soil even for plugging a flask; see Magen Avraham siman 307 s.k. 20 in the name of the Rosh.
