They are not taken — see Mishna Berurah who wrote per Magen Avraham the intent is even in mere moving; so from Shabbos 125 Ravina: how do we move nowadays? — implies pit and cistern covers forbidden even in moving; so Rambam, Ran, Levushei Serad; wondered at Peri Megadim M.Z. wrote opposite [Eshel Avraham doubts]; same Darkei Chayim. Truth: they strained for Peri Megadim why forbid moving after vessel status — in my humble opinion plain: after pit and cistern cover are land-category, equated to house doors; known what Rambam wrote on house doors: although vessels, not prepared to move — forbidden whether detached Shabbat or erev Shabbat, unlike vessel doors. Maggid on Rambam: Rashi ch. Kol HaKelim says unlike house doors for not prepared; implies if vessel status on doors they would be permitted to move — Rambam proved from the passage itself: since ground-covers forbidden without handle though vessel status, on something land-related — door or cover — vessel status does not remove muktzeh; so Ran explicitly: without handle forbidden though vessel status:

And that he prepared them, etc. — see Biur HaGra: this applies to those without a handle; meaning if they had a handle that itself is preparation to be a cover; suffices for Rashi that vessel status means fit for use; but per Shulchan Aruch like Tosafot, preparation for covering alone removes muktzeh; handle alone suffices to cover.
