[23] Then evident, etc. Not building/demolition since evident for weighing and returning; end ch. Kol HaKelim implies even with handle permission only if prepared, requires study; so Rambam; but Shulchan Aruch and Levush imply only covers without handle need preparation, requires study
[24] [Levush] Covers, etc. Meaning only attached below with clay
And he prepared them for this, etc. This means, to cover a vessel. See Shiltei Giborim. This requires study, for thought should suffice, as with palm branches in seif 20. If one distinguishes between them, it is difficult according to what the Ran inferred at the end of the chapter Kol HaKeilim, that use before Shabbos helps since it helps for palm branches. There is also difficulty why the Gemara in Shabbos 126 needed to say that Rabbi Yochanan disagrees with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who permits palm branches. See there and examine carefully.
