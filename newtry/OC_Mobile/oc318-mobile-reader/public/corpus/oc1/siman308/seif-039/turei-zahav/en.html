Forbidden to move an animal, beast, and bird. Beit Yosef brought this from Talmud Yerushalmi; Rashal in responsum 10 wrote: chickens in one's house are permitted on Shabbat to chase and take them; he brings there "one who traps is exempt" — I wondered what he relied on to permit muktzeh moving though trapping is permitted in his view — meaning to where they are guarded they may chase there, but moving in hands of muktzeh object there is no permission; unless it is a scribal error of what he wrote "and take them" — I could not settle it; therefore do not rely on permission to move; regarding trapping I dealt siman 316:
