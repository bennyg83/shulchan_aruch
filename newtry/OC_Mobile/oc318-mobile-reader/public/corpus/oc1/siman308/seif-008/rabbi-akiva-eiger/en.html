Magen Avraham s.k. 17: If he threw it on Shabbos, it is permitted. And if this finder found it in the ash heap, I do not know whether it was thrown on erev Shabbos or on Shabbos. One can say: establish the utensil according to its presumption of being whole, and today it broke; nevertheless, requires study whether presumption helps for the ash heap, for those who hold that majority does not help. And if he found it in a place where he is not permitted to move, in a karmlis or in reshus harabim, and most of the city are Israelites, one can say that presumably it broke on erev Shabbos. For practical law, requires study.
