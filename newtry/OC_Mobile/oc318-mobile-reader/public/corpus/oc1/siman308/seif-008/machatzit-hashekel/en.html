(s.k. 8) But from sun to shade, etc. — Yerushalmi; Shabbat 124b. Rabbah: even permitted vessel forbidden sun to shade; Rava: permitted. Rav Mari bar Rachel's mats before Rava — the exchange about others and guests proves trickery for sun to shade; our Talmud like Yerushalmi; for us vessel whose function is for prohibition like permitted vessel for Rabbah regarding trickery. Taz s.k. 2 on carrying knives to synagogue — permitted if may need today; after minchah perhaps forbidden as siman 323 seif 6; if carried lest stolen — permitted; see Turei Even.
