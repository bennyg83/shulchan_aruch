Their doors. Rambam wrote ch. 25: doors of a house, even though they are vessels, were not prepared for moving; therefore if they were detached, even on Shabbat, one does not move them — end of his words; requires study what "even" means — on the contrary, if detached on a weekday they were prepared for another function from while still day, as seif 6 and in the Gemara; one must say: since at bein hashemashot it was moved when opening and closing, it is permitted now too — it teaches us; and from this one learns the same for muktzeh vessels that broke on Shabbat: even though now they are not muktzeh, they are forbidden — see siman 311 and siman 310 seif 3.
