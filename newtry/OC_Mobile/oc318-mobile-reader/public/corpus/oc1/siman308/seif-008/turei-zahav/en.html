Whether detached on a weekday. Meaning, and needless to say detached on Shabbat — permitted since prepared through their parent when Shabbat entered; rather even on a weekday permitted. And do not say this is like fragments of vessels shortly that are more permitted when broken erev Shabbat than on Shabbat, for here it is still a vessel fit to be attached with the vessel, unlike vessel fragments that are not vessels except because fit for other work — then worse when broken on Shabbat, for it is nolad — so Tosafot ch.
