16) There, gloss: And he is particular about them. This applies even to dining utensils, such as bowls, glassware, and knives. It appears to me that this applies specifically to a merchant who does not ordinarily lend utensils. One who ordinarily lends new utensils does not render them muktzeh even after placing them in storage, because he is not particular about them. Chayei Adam, klal 65, paragraph 3, section beginning 'lest.'
