Pebbles — Bach; and see Magen Avraham, who wrote that they err regarding a whole candle — that it is permitted only when he needs a permitted thing, even if he thought about it from yesterday, and even via a child one should be stringent; see there. In Mora Uktzia he wrote that Magen Avraham wrote according to their mistake; but for the law, since it is not fit for food and use, it is like stones and pebbles, and even for its body and place it is forbidden; see there.
