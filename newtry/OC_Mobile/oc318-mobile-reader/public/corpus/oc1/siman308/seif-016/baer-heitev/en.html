From beforehand. The reason lest he drive in. Therefore specifically when slipped is there this concern; but when broken, lest he drive does not apply, only need another leg, therefore no concern; and Rama's "broken" is not precise but means slipped. And further it appears: if the leg already slipped and that leg is gone and the bench stands without it, no prohibition to lean on another bench, for lest he drive does not apply, as appears to me in this rabbinic stringency especially where Shabbat need.
