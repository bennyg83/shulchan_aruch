A garment that they washed and stuck a reed into to hang it to dry — he may remove it from the reed, but to take the reed from inside it is forbidden, because it is not a utensil; if he stuck a utensil into it, it is permitted to take it from inside, even if its melacha is forbidden, such as a stove from which one of' its supports was removed — it is forbidden to move it.And likewise a long bench from which one leg was removed — all the more so two — it is forbidden to move it and place it on another bench and sit on it, even if it broke during the day [Terumat HaDeshen siman] 71], unless he sat on it so once before Shabbat [Beit Yosef s.s. 313];] also forbidden to put a leg into it because of building [Beit Yosef s.s. 313]:]:
