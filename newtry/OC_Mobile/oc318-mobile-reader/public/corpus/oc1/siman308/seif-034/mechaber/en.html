Every filthy thing, such as excrement, vomit, and feces — whether of a person or of chickens and the like — if they were in a courtyard where people sit, it is permitted to take them out to the ash heap or to reshus harabim, even without a utensil; and if they were in a courtyard where he does not dwell, it is forbidden to take them out; and if he fears because of a child lest he get dirty from it, it is permitted to cover it with a utensil.
