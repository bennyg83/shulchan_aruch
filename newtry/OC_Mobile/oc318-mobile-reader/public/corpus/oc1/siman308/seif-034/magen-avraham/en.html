That one dwells in it. Meaning: that one lives there as in former days when courtyards were before houses and there was foot traffic; but if placed in the courtyard ash heap — forbidden to clear [as in the Gemara]; the same in a courtyard behind houses if one does not dwell there — forbidden; and it appears to me: if placed in an alley in a place of foot traffic — permitted to remove it to the sides.

Forbidden to remove them. It appears to me: if it is full and impossible to clear because of it — permitted to remove and return it because of human dignity, as siman 312; also according to our version permitted to move human excrement for dogs, since it is fit for dogs; however the Rif and Rosh have another version — in pressing circumstances one may rely on our version.
