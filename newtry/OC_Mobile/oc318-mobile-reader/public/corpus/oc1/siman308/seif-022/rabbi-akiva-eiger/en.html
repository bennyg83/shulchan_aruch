Seif 11: One takes the point. The view of the Rashba in Chullin is that specifically if it was taken intentionally. But on Shabbos one should permit, since they make something similar to its melacha, and they were prepared incidentally via their "fathers"; he compares it to mat remnants in the seif that follows. In my opinion, since he ruled in mat remnants that if he threw them to the ash heap intentionally, it is implied that if he threw them to the ash heap on Shabbos, it is permitted, behold, it is better than a needle. (See the Rashba, who truly holds that throwing them to the ash heap on Shabbos is forbidden, truly like a needle.) We distinguish: a needle is worse, for it does not stand at all for its melacha; thus one cannot learn one from the other at all; requires study.
Seif 22. But with anything whose way is so. One who designates blank paper to wipe with it, if it is good paper that stands for writing, he needs to designate it for that use permanently, and this helps according to all opinions since it is not subject to an act. But with coarse paper, designation for one Shabbos suffices. Magen Avraham below at the beginning of siman 312, s.k. 2, and Tosefes Shabbos there, etc.
