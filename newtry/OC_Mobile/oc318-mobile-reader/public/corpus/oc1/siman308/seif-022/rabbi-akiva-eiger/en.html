Seif 22: But in anything whose manner is such. One who designates a part of paper to wipe with — if it is good paper that stands for writing, he must designate it for that forever, and it helps for all, since it is not a matter of action; but with coarse paper, designation for one Shabbat suffices — Magen Avraham below, siman 312 s.k. 2, and Tosafot Shabbat there, etc.
