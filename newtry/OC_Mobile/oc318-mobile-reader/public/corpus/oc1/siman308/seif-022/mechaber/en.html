It is forbidden to cover the mouth of a barrel with a stone or a wood-chip, or to shut a door with them, or to strike with them upon the spigot {i.e., the reed that is inserted into barrels to draw out the wine}. Even if he thought about it while it was still day, it is forbidden, unless he designated it for this permanently; but [if] he designated it for this Shabbos alone, no. And this applies to something that is not customarily designated for this, such as those we mentioned; but with anything whose usual manner is thus — such as to crack nuts with it — designation for one Shabbos suffices. And there is one who says there is no difference. Some say that he must perform some act of preparation with it while it is still day {and see above, siman 259, that designation suffices}.
