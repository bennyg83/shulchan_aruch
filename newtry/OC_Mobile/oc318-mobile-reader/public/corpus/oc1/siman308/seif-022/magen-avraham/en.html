Designated for Shabbos, etc. Unless he did an act with it, as siman 321.
Designation for one Shabbos suffices. And some explain siman 321: there is different, for the stone is not made for sitting, unlike here where it is customary, and so siman 303 seif 22 with a round stone customary, therefore designation helps.
And see Ran: designation suffices. And those stones hidden in them do not require repair, and repair does not apply, like a basket of earth that is permitted by designation (and see siman 338), since it is not subject to an act; likewise here (Mordechai ch. 17); and so the Rosh in responsa: leftover bricks from building and the like, not subject to an act to give them the status of a vessel, therefore permitted, as siman 317, end of his words; and so in the Gemara; nevertheless even in what is not subject to an act we require permanent designation forever for something not customarily designated; and in what is customary they disagree, as written, the Rashba brought proof from a basket of earth that is permitted, and there it is not subject to an act in the Gemara; and Mordechai ch. 17 implies: to cover a barrel mouth or close a door or strike with a bolt is subject to an act, one can do an act proving it stands for that; in that all agree permanent designation is required forever, for a stone is not customarily designated to cover a barrel mouth, requires study.
