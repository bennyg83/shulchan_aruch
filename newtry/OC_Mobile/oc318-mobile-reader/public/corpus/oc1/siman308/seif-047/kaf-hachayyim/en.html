47) Mishbetzos Zahav writes in paragraph 3 that according to siman 301, seif 42, when tefillin are in an unprotected place one brings them in pair by pair. It follows that there is at least no Torah prohibition in wearing tefillin on Shabbos, and the Sages permitted it because of the disgrace to the tefillin. The same applies here: the possibility that they will be stolen is like a disgrace, so rabbinic moving is permitted. This is the substance of his words. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there likewise implies that when non-Jewish thieves might take them, creating a disgrace, it is permitted to put them away so they will not be stolen.
