That it is fit for a stew. It is written in Sefer Yereim daf 75: it deals with a soft type fit for a cup; and so the Me'or ch. 21: if not fit for a stew — forbidden to move, for anything of food that is not fit to eat until what is written for dogs also does not apply and one does not move it, for it is fit for nothing — end of his words; and so the Rosh beginning ch. 1 of Chullin, Rabbeinu Yeruham ch. 8, Shulchan Aruch, Ran, Rambam; and so Yeshuot Yaakov Chullin ch. 1 seif 40; if so, meat not fit to eat raw — forbidden to move; the same other things like congealed fat unlike the Bach; Shulchan Aruch wrote: gentile meat slaughtered today — permitted to move like an animal that died on Shabbat; all the more for gentile matters there is no muktzeh, for a gentile does not designate anything intentionally — end of his words; see siman 310 seif 2 and so end of ch. 24 regarding treifah.
