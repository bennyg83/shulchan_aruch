Even in reshut ha-rabbim. For even if she raises them he is exempt, for a living being carries itself; and if one carries with his hands or a sick person, liable; and one who carries a dead body is melacha not needed for itself, see Eliyah Rabbah.
Lest she drag it. Because she carries it [Ran]; it implies he holds it is like one who carries it; if so, forbidden even in karmelit, see siman 348, see there; and all agree: a child who needs dragging, if carried, liable for a sin-offering, see there; and so Tosafot daf 94 and the Me'or beginning ch. 21: even with a child who takes one step and puts down one, if carried, liable; nevertheless because of the child's distress they did not decree lest she raise them, see there; one who takes out a child with a pouch on his neck, liable; but one who takes out an adult dressed in his garments, exempt (Rambam), see siman 309.
