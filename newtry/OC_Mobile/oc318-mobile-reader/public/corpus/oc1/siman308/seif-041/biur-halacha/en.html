That she not drag it — see Mishna Berurah in Biur Halachah: because she carries him so much — Rashi; Magen Avraham from Ran; not necessary Ran holds so [often not]; Ran s.v. but if dragging — he himself may hold dragging only d'rabbanan, else why say rabbis disagree with R' Nasan on small dragging; or dragging forbidden lest he carry on shoulder — d'oraita to carry, rabbis stringent on dragging [Ateret Zekeinim two explanations in Ran; Maamar Mordechai ruled dragging d'rabbanan for Ran]; therefore I wrote "so Rashi"; what I wrote some are lenient — from Ran: prohibition on infant who cannot walk at all; Beit Yosef who permits goading in karmelit — no stringency on dragging in karmelit [certainly not for one who can goad]; carrying on shoulder in karmelit d'oraita for most poskim [Peri Megadim]; even Tosafot 120a small eight-day infant only d'rabbanan — many Rishonim dispute: Ramban ch. R' Eliezer milah, Rashba, Ran, Meor, Meiri — carrying non-walking child is d'oraita; therefore stringent in karmelit even if can goad; for one who cannot goad, many hold carrying d'rabbanan — still no leniency in karmelit since rabbinic animal carrying like other things [unlike siman 310 seif 1 standing in public domain head out to drink — there mere decree lest bring to him; karmelit no such decree; here when forbade carrying in karmelit no distinction from public domain]; so Magen Avraham beginning s.k. 71: Mechaber "she not drag" — one who can goad, still carrying forbidden in karmelit; Chayei Adam, Gra; especially Meor, Meiri: even goading child fully d'oraita in public domain while cannot walk — stringent karmelit:
