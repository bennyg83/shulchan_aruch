(s.k. 41) Darkei Moshe Mordechai — Magen Avraham brought Darkei Moshe, disputes gloss, questions Mordechai.
Stones dispute amoraim, palm branches tannaim.
Rashi 125, arrange stones not to touch tomorrow, forbidden to move, not sitting.
Comment. Rashi implies sitting permitted on muktzeh, Rabbeinu Yerucham no act helps sitting; Magen Avraham from Rashi sitting permitted.
Palm branches move when sit, stones don't; Mordechai large stones; Rambam small.
Rambam small stones, act required; Mordechai may concede small to Rambam.
Comment. Siman 311 straw move body, lie permitted; Rosh: move for permitted bed permitted; side move for muktzeh forbidden; Ran: move for lying is for permitted; Magen Avraham follows Ran; Eruvin.
Comment. Therefore Magen Avraham against Mordechai correct; questions Mordechai; not like Mordechai.
