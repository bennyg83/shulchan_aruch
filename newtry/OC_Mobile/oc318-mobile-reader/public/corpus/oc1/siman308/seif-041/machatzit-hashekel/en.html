(s.k. 41) Darkei Moshe Mordechai — Magen Avraham brought Darkei Moshe, disputes gloss, questions Mordechai.

Stones dispute amoraim — palm branches tannaim.

Rashi 125 — arrange stones not to touch tomorrow — forbidden to move, not sitting.

Rashi implies sitting permitted on muktzeh — Rabbeinu Yerucham no act helps sitting; Magen Avraham from Rashi sitting permitted.

Palm branches move when sit — stones don't; Mordechai large stones; Rambam small.

Rambam small stones — act required; Mordechai may concede small to Rambam.

Siman 311 straw shake body — lie permitted; Rosh: shake for permitted bed permitted; side move for muktzeh forbidden; Ran: shake for lying is for permitted; Magen Avraham follows Ran; Eruvin.

Therefore Magen Avraham against Mordechai correct; questions Mordechai; not like Mordechai.
