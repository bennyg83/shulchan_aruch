(35) Whether detached on a weekday — meaning: not to say only if detached on Shabbat permitted because prepared on their father's account when Shabbat entered; rather even on weekday permitted because still a vessel fit to reconnect with the vessel; therefore permitted to move even from sun to shade even if not fit for any other function; this is with vessel doors considered like vessel covers; but house doors or windows, though fit for some use and like vessels, since not prepared for that — forbidden to move whether detached weekday or Shabbat. Chicken coop doors are like house doors also for moving prohibition. Magen Avraham wrote: muktzeh vessels that broke on Shabbat — though now not muktzeh, forbidden — meaning: were muktzeh because of chisaron kis; when broken he is no longer particular for other use — now not muktzeh; nevertheless forbidden because of miggo that it was muktzeh at bein hashemashot it is muktzeh all day.
