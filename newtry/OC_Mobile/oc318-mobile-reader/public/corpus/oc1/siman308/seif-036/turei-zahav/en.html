One does not make a refuse receptacle, etc. Meaning like that in ch. 2 Yom Tov forbidden to hire a gentile on Shabbat because of wine lees in cups forbidden in benefit and he cannot move them from the table; and although permitted to remove a repulsive thing, nevertheless one should not make from the outset something that will certainly afterward be repulsive; "in order to remove them" — meaning he relies on this that eventually he will remove them:
