A person may bring in before Shabbat a basket full of earth and designate a corner for it, and do all his needs with it on Shabbat — such as to take from it to cover feces or spittle and the like; but if he did not designate a corner for it, it is batel against the earth of the house and it is forbidden to move it.And therefore it is permitted to take fruit buried on a weekday, for that earth is not muktzeh [Beit Yosef s.s. 311 in the name of Shelah]:]:
