"For the wealthy." Taz writes that the general rule is as follows: something fit for poor people, when it is in the house of a poor person, is muktzeh for wealthy people because it is not fit for them. Nevertheless, a wealthy person who is present in the poor person's house follows the status of that house. But something that is muktzeh for a wealthy person and is in the wealthy person's house is forbidden even to all poor people, as though the wealthy person had already thrown it into the refuse.
