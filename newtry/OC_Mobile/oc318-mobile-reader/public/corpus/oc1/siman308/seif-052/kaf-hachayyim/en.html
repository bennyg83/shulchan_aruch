52) [Seif 5] Some permit, etc. The Shulchan Aruch writes 'some permit' because some forbid it, as stated in Beis Yosef; see there. Taz, paragraph 4, therefore writes that it appears one should not be lenient. Gra writes the same. Nevertheless, in a case of significant loss one may rely on those who permit. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 22. All this applies only when one places in the utensil another item that is not specifically associated with it. If an item specifically associated with it lies there, such as garlic in the mortar used to crush it or some cooked food in a pot, everyone agrees that the utensil may be moved together with the food, even from the sun to the shade. This is implied by Beis Yosef.
