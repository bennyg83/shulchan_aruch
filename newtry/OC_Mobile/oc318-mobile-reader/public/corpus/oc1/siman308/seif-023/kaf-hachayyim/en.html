23) There, gloss: If it was used for a forbidden purpose during twilight, etc. This means that a forbidden item rested upon it, making it a base for a forbidden item. If one used a hammer to perform forbidden labor during twilight, it is obvious that the hammer may nevertheless be moved on Shabbos when needed for a permitted use or its place. Magen Avraham, paragraph 6; Eshel Avraham.
