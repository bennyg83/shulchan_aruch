(42) Are not taken — even if he fixed them and made an act proving standing to cover or constantly uses for that — muktzeh name removed, as below seif 20; nevertheless forbidden here, for as ground covers it is like building if he covers or like demolition if he removes the cover; unlike when there is a handle to hold — proven a vessel for weighing and returning, not building/demolition; see Magen Avraham who explained "are not taken" means forbidden even mere moving, even if already detached from erev Shabbat from the pit — all the more forbidden to take from the pit; the reason: since they stand ready to cover ground they are not of what is prepared for moving even though status of vessel applies.
