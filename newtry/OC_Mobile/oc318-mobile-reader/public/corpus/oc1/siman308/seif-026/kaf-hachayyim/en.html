26) There: And place it wherever, etc. Since it is already in his hand, he may move it farther. It appears to me that even if he forgot and picked it up, he may continue moving it. Magen Avraham, paragraph 7. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 11, however, writes that Magen Avraham's ruling is not compelling; see there. See Even HaOzer, siman 266, who writes that this applies specifically to a utensil whose designated use is forbidden. Since it retains the status of a utensil and may at least be moved when needed for its place, once it comes into his hand he may move it wherever he wishes. Complete muktzeh, however, must be put down immediately when Shabbos arrives, even if it came into his hand before Shabbos and he forgot; see there. Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 6, cites this. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 13, similarly writes that some forbid this regarding complete muktzeh and that one should account for their view except where loss would result. These are his words.
