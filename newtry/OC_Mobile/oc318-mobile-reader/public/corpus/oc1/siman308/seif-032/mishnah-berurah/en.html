(31) An earthenware shard, etc. — Shulchan Aruch added here to explain two matters related to the previous seif: one, what was mentioned there that if the shard is fit to cover with, it suffices even where no vessels are found to cover with, such as in karmelit or reshut ha-rabbim when taking to wipe mud from a shoe — the reason: since the status of vessel still applies to the shard, in a courtyard even on Rosh HaShanah there is no muktzeh on it; second, if he threw it to the ash heap while still day, again muktzeh applies and forbidden to move.
