One moves it. It implies that to go out with it to karmelit is forbidden, for otherwise why need to say one moves; and so from Rashi's words: and not with an amulet that is not expert — with an expert one permitted, for it is an ornament for the sick; it implies that not expert is not an ornament, only he is not liable for a sin-offering lest it heal; Rashal in responsa wrote that even not expert is an ornament and permitted for karmelit (see seif 4); requires study, for then it should be permitted even for reshut ha-rabbim; and one may ask: then an animal should be permitted — see siman 305 seif 67; rather as he wrote is the main point; and so the Tosefta: even where there is no reshut ha-rabbim, forbidden to carry rings with letters for healing without a seal unless they were well tested — and so in Sefer HaTerumah and siman 308 seif 104.
