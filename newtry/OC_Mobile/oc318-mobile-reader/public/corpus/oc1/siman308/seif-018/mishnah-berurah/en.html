(17) But mere touch, etc. — see siman 310 seif 6 and Magen Avraham there: where touch is for the need of the muktzeh object, such as pressing a vessel on muktzeh to guard it — must be careful not to touch; Gra there in his explanation ruled like Rav Maggid: touch in every manner is permitted if it will not cause shaking; and so in Biur Halacha.
