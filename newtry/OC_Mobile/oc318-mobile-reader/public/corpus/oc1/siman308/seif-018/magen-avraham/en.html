A thorn placed. Rivash wrote siman 401: specifically a thorn not visible; and likewise metal coal, the danger is not evident, for even after the flame goes out there is power to burn; but wood coal, as long as it has power to burn it will be red and recognizable, so too Behag; the same applies to a large visible stone, forbidden to move, end of his words; requires study, for we say a wooden spit is permitted to move lest they be harmed, as siman 318 seif 3 and so in siman 334; nevertheless requires study whether to permit a stone, for it appears they permitted only what was not placed there from erev Shabbos so he should have removed it yesterday, see siman 322 seif 64.
