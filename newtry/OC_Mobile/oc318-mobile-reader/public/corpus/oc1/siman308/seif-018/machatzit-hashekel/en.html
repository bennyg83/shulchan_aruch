(s.k. 18) Beit Yosef, siman 20 palm branches: thought for sitting permits moving; here candle not fit Shabbos use, how does thought help move muktzeh?
There fit for Shabbos use, palm branches; here not.
Not fit Shabbos, thought cannot permit muktzeh.
Comment. Reason of places, siman 309: child with coin, father may lift coin, dispute; candle in child's hand like coin; siman 343: educate children; father must separate; bringing candle to synagogue is mitzvah to permit feeding by hand.
See siman 343, another reason to be strict.
