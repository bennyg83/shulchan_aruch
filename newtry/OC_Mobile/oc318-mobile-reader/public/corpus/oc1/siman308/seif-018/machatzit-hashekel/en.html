(s.k. 18) Beit Yosef — siman 20 palm branches: thought for sitting permits moving; here candle not fit Shabbat use — how does thought help move muktzeh?

There fit for Shabbat use — palm branches; here not.

Not fit Shabbat — thought cannot permit muktzeh.
