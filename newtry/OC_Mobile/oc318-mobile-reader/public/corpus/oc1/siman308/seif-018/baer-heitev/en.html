Thorn. Specifically a thorn not visible; and metal coal — heat not evident, for even after redness it has power to burn. Rivash siman 401; see siman 318 seif 3; see Magen Avraham (wondering at Rivash's responsum and what he brought to law; but in Eliyah Rabbah he rejected his words — see there; also what Magen Avraham brought that he permitted only what was not placed from erev Shabbat is not clear, for thorn implies placed erev Shabbat):
