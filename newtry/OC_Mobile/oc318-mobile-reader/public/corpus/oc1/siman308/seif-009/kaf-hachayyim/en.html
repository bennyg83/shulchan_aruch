Darkei Moshe, paragraph 1. Nezirus Shimshon writes that it should at least be permitted when needed for its place; see there. Orach Chaim, paragraph 1, cites this. We nevertheless maintain the view of Maran and Rema that it is forbidden both when needed for a permitted use and when needed for its place. It appears to me, however, that if during the week one demonstrated that he is not particular about the utensil, such as occasionally using it for another purpose when necessary, for example cutting fruit and the like, it may be moved on Shabbos when needed for a permitted use or its place. I afterward saw that Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 4, writes the same. See below, paragraph 14.
