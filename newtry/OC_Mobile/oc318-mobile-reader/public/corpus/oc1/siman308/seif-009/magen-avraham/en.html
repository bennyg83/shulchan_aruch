To take it. For there is no building and demolition in vessels — see siman 314.

Lest he drive in a peg. Even in a vessel that is complete building — unlike erev Shabbat.

For since it is attached to the ground, etc. And he is liable for building (Rambam ch. 10 and Maggid Mishneh ch. 22); if so, windows too are forbidden to take and return, for they are attached.
