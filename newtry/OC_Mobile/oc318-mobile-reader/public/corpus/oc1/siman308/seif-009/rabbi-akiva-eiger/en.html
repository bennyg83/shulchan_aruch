Magen Avraham s.k. 19: And from this one learns. In my opinion, there is no proof here, since the opening is still there to return it to the house for its original use, and there is no utensil on it; see Magen Avraham nearby s.k. 38. But a utensil that is muktzeh because of monetary loss that broke on Shabbat — its thought still stands for another melacha — it is not muktzeh, except according to R' Yehuda, from the reason that it was designated during bein hashemashos; but according to R' Shimon it is permitted, as explained at the beginning of Beitzah. And this is the law below, siman 301.
