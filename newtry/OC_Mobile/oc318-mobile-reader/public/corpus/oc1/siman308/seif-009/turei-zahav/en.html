A chest door, etc. — in the Gemara: granted for a chicken coop since attached to the ground there is building concern; but for a chest, box, and cupboard — if there is building in vessels, there is also demolition, so why may they take them? Rava answered: actually there is no building or demolition in vessels; returning is forbidden lest he drive in firmly — Rashi: with force, completing melacha, liable for striking the final blow. It implies for a coop he need not fear lest he drive in; but Rif and Rambam mentioned also coop lest he drive in — wondered; Beit Yosef strained to settle: since they are vessels, the matter is not settled to say that since attached there is building and demolition, for they are attached to the ground. And in my humble opinion it is astonishing: in the Gemara ch. HaMotzi tefillin there is a baraita on a hanging door on hinges: for chest and box do not return lest he drive in; for pit and cistern covers — liable a chatat, and cistern cover is building on the ground like a coop, forbidden even without driving from building since attached.
