(S.k. 56) In your manner. Note: this is not correct; for if so, why does the Shulchan Aruch divide between meat and fish? It should have divided regarding meat itself: unsalted meat is permitted to move in your manner, but meat of cattle is forbidden to move. Rather, learn from this that the Shulchan Aruch holds that even meat of cattle is fit for raw eating; and so he ruled in seif 14, siman 67, per the view of the Rosh regarding a broken neck; see there. And tractate Yereim is no proof that he holds per the explanation of Rashi.
