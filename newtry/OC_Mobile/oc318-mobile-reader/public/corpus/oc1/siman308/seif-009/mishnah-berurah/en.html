(8) And very heavy — and the same for a large stone and large beam if it has the status of a vessel — meaning designated for use — permitted to move; and with a vessel permitted even if he is not accustomed to move it on weekdays — nevertheless we do not say that because of its weight it fixes a place and designates from intent not to move; and it appears: if on weekdays he is careful not to move it from its place lest it be lost and spoiled — automatically it is in muktzeh because of chisaron kis and forbidden to move.
