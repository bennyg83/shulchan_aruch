All utensils that broke, even on Shabbat, may have their fragments moved, provided they are fit for some melacha — such as fragments of a kneading trough to cover a barrel with, and fragments of glass to cover the mouth of a jug with; but if they are not fit for any melacha — not.And if they broke in a place where they can cause harm, such as glass vessels that broke on a table or in a place where people walk, it is permitted to move the fragments in order to clear them, so they' will not be harmed by them (Kol Bo, and Hagahot Mordechai, and Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chayim):.
