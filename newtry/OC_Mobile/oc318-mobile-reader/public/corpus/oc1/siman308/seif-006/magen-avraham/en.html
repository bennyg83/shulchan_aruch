All vessels. And see siman 495 seif 11: nolad is forbidden on Shabbat; therefore I write wherever there is nolad.

To cover with it. And per the view that nolad is forbidden — it requires that it be fit for its original function, such as shards of a tub to cover a barrel, and of glass to pour oil into it; the same if a gentile makes a vessel on Shabbat — forbidden to move according to this view.

Lest they be damaged. But if it is of earthenware it is forbidden to clear them, even though he tramples on them with his feet and breaks them — nevertheless it is davar she'eino mitkaven; see siman 336 seif 3. Also there is a prohibition in breaking them — see siman 314 and siman 318 seif 2. However in siman 336 seif 62 it implies forbidden — perhaps there is different, for there he breaks a vessel with his hands, but here it is indirect.
