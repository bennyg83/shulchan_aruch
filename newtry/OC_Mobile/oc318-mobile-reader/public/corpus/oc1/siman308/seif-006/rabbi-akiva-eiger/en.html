Magen Avraham s.k. 7: While it is in his hand. It is difficult to me from the Yerushalmi brought in the Rashba, chapter 7 of Demai 2, where it asks there: and it was not found that contaminated terumah was moved on Shabbat? But there it is already in his hand, and it is permitted to move it to place it on the table; even according to Eben Ha'Ezer above, siman 266, this permission is only for a utensil whose melacha is forbidden — since in any case he has permission for its body and place; but for complete muktzeh, not. Nevertheless, his view there — if he forgot money in a purse, since it is not a base, and it is permitted to move it together with the money for its place — it is also permitted when it is already in his hand. The same applies here with the cup: it was not a base during bein hashemashos; the same if it was a base also for a permitted thing — strong wine was in it; and it also seems that they mixed for him on Shabbat.
