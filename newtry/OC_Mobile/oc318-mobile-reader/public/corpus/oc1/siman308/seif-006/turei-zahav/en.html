Even on Shabbat. In the Gemara it is so stated in the Mishnah: all vessels that may be moved on Shabbat — their fragments may be moved with them, provided they do the kind of work [even if not the original kind] — R' Yosi; and provided they do the kind of their original work — Shmuel said: a dispute when they broke before Shabbat, but on Shabbat — permitted since they are prepared through their parent; Rav Zutra objected: we kindle with vessels but do not kindle with fragments of vessels that broke — when? If you say on erev Yom Tov they are merely wood standing from yesterday for kindling; rather on Yom Tov, and it teaches we do not kindle with fragments of vessels, and we do not say prepared through their parent; rather thus was stated: dispute when they broke on Shabbat — some say prepared (since fit for some work), some say nolad (for from yesterday it was not for this work, therefore we require the kind of their work), and Rif, Rosh, and Tur ruled like the first tanna. And it is difficult: it implies the dispute is on Shabbat — then that we do not kindle with fragments of vessels would be like R' Yosi, and for the first tanna permitted with fragments of vessels; Tur explicitly siman 301 forbids with R' Yosi an ember that broke on Yom Tov — if so, difficult: Rava explicitly said in ch. Notel (Shabbos 143) we do not kindle with fragments of vessels, and necessarily here too we require the kind of their work like R' Yosi, for one depends on the other — how did we rule here like the first tanna against Rava? And the same difficulty on Rambam who ruled like the first tanna but with fragments of vessels ruled like R' Yosi to forbid. One must say: these poskim hold they are not interdependent; specifically according to what was first said — since prepared through their parent, and this applies also to fragments of vessels for kindling — then he properly challenged from kindling fragments of vessels that it is not like anyone; but according to what was established — broken on Shabbat, and the reason was not through their parent but that they themselves are prepared, being fit for work even if not original work and not called nolad; but R' Yehuda holds since now fit for other work and not the original — it is nolad and forbidden; then that kindling with fragments of vessels is nolad for all, for now he wants to kindle with it and the break is not a vessel at all — only in the Mishnah where the fragment stands for other work and for that it is a vessel is it permitted to move. Therefore Rambam properly ruled that we do not kindle with fragments of vessels; and Rava said in ch. HaMevi wine: Rava expounded a woman may not enter a wood storage to take an ember, and an ember that broke is forbidden to kindle on Yom Tov because we do not kindle with fragments of vessels — shall we say Rava holds like R' Yosi that he has muktzeh, etc. Rambam explains he inferred from the first part not to make an ember from them, for it is muktzeh since the wood was given only for kindling, as Rashi explains there — now it is settled what the questioner asked: muktzeh in the first part, nolad in the second part. And further we will write from this, with God's help, siman 301; and Tosafot cited kindling with fragments of vessels forbidden for R' Yosi — meaning at least forbidden for R' Yosi, and for the first tanna not necessary; but possible they also hold so, as appears to me.
