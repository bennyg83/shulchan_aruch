(5) To move it — meaning: that scribes' knife is forbidden to move, for one might say that since it is stuck with other knives he was not particular — it teaches us forbidden; but the case with the knives is permitted to move even though it is also inside, for it became a base for prohibition and permission and permitted to shake this knife out of it too; and if this knife is more important than the permitted knives — forbidden then to move the case at all; see end of siman 311.
