He wants — see in Even HaEzer siman 266: his view is that specifically a utensil whose melacha is forbidden has the status of a utensil upon it, and he is in any case permitted to move it for its place; therefore, when it comes to his hand he can move it to whatever place he wants. But regarding muktzeh, it appears that its law is like sacred writings; see below siman 688, where Pri Chadash wrote that it is forbidden to move it, and Bach brought it there.
