6) There: Or for bris milah. This discusses when it is not needed for the bris. When moved for the bris, it is not muktzeh, and while it remains in his hand he may wash it. Nevertheless, doing so is forbidden because it is not needed for Shabbos. Rabbi Moshe of Trani, volume 3, simanim 84 and 89; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham, siman 331, paragraph 5.
