All vessels that broke, etc. — see Peri Megadim M.Z. s.k. 14 who leans this law applies also to vessels whose work is forbidden — permitted to move for bodily and place need when fit for some work:

For some work, etc. — Magen Avraham wrote: according to siman 495 that nolad is forbidden on Shabbat, when broken on Shabbat we require fit for original work only, such as kneading-trough fragments to pour gravy, glass to pour oil; but many Acharonim [Bach, Taz, Eliyah Rabbah, Tosafot Shabbos] wrote since still fit for some work it is not nolad and is in prepared category; but if a gentile makes a vessel on Shabbat this is certainly nolad and forbidden to move per this view:

Such as kneading-trough fragments, etc. — then even from heat to shade permitted like a permitted-work vessel; in what is not fit for any work, even for bodily and place need forbidden [Peri Megadim leans so in several proofs — see there]:
