17) [Seif 2] Every utensil, even if it is large, etc. The same applies to a large stone or beam. Although ten people are needed to move it, if it has the status of a utensil, meaning that it was designated for use, it may be moved. Rambam, chapter 25, halachah 6; Magen Avraham, paragraph 4. See Beis Yosef, who cites the Gemara's account that ten people would lift it and place it at the doorway; see there.
