(13) And to place it in any place — for since it is in his hand that he took it for its use or place he may move it further; Magen Avraham wrote the same if he forgot and took it in his hand he may move it further even if the taking was not for its use or place; see Acharonim who ruled this leniency applies only to a vessel whose function is for prohibition, not muktzeh because of its essence such as coins and stones; and in Biur HaGra on siman 266 seif 12 he disputes Magen Avraham and holds they permitted only when he began moving permissibly; and so Ran beginning Beitzah — see there; see below siman 331 seif 65 regarding a circumcision knife. It is stated in siman 311 seif 8 that moving muktzeh with one's body even for the need of the muktzeh object is permitted, for it is not called moving at all; therefore if something muktzeh lies on the ground, permitted to push with feet lest it come to loss.
