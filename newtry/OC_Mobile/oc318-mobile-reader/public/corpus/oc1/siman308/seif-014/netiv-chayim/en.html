(S.k. 66) And if a beast was upon it — forbidden. Even if they are no longer upon it — as it should be. Note: their proof from below, siman 310 s.k. 7 — there we do not rule like R' Shimon, who says that from having been designated for a beast it is designated for the whole day; see Beit Yosef. Apparently there is no proof here; it is different, since it is in his hand to release the chickens, as Tosafot on the stove, daf 73, s.v. be'odan. One can say that this reasoning is only that it is not nullified from its place; but nevertheless there is a muktzeh prohibition while they are on it during bein hashemashos. If not, to where will we throw the braita that teaches: while they are on it during bein hashemashos, it is forbidden to move it? Even for R' Yehuda one can say that it is not muktzeh, like R' Shimon, since it is in his hand to release them.
