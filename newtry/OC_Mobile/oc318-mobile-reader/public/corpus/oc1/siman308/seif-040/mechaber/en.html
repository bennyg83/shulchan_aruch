Every beast, animal, and bird may be led in a courtyard — meaning, one holds its neck and its sides and leads it if the living creatures need this — provided he does not lift it in a manner that its feet leave the ground, for they are muktzeh and it is forbidden to move them; except a chicken, which is not led, because it lifts itself from the ground and one is found moving it; rather, one pushes it from behind with his hands so that it enters.
