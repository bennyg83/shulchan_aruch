It is forbidden to sit, etc. Therefore thought of sitting means that he thinks about sitting on them, even if his thought was only to sit on them during the week and not on Shabbos, as written in Shulchan Aruch. Likewise, actually sitting on them during the week helps remove them from the thought of burning.
But a row of stones, etc. Therefore an act is required to show it, and this is the correct text.
And seif 22. For a piece of wood and stones have the same law.
What the Olas Shabbos wrote from siman 315. There it implies that thought helps even for stones, for it says there that a stone which one placed by hand on a barrel makes the barrel a base for a prohibited object, and it is forbidden to move the barrel, even to shake the stone off. But if before Shabbos he intended not to leave the stone on the barrel for the entire Shabbos, it has the law of one who forgot, and it is permitted to shake the stone off the barrel. Thus his thought helps even for a stone.
