In the name of Rashi and Ran, however, he writes that it apparently applies only within the same room; see there. Shabbos, chapter Mefanin, states that if a person has muktzeh grain, he may make a path through it with his feet when entering and leaving, because this is unusual movement for a permitted need, namely his passage. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 11, writes that according to Rosh, who maintains that moving an item with one's body is more lenient than other indirect movement and is permitted even for the muktzeh item's benefit, the Gemara mentions entering and leaving only as the ordinary case, because a person normally does so for passage. The same act is permitted for the muktzeh item's benefit; see there. See below, siman 311, seif 8. Accordingly, if a muktzeh article lies on the ground, one may push it with his feet to prevent a loss. Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 13.
