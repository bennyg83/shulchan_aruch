Shulchan Aruch seif 1 movable on Shabbat. Know from here until siman 312 four muktzeh parts explained. Part one: muktzeh because of loss — particular not to damage. Part two: not vessel, not human/animal food — stones, reeds, coins, wood, beams, dust, sand, corpse, animals, drying figs/raisins — not fit, muktzeh because of itself. Part three: forbidden-work vessel seif 3. Part four: permitted-work vessel with muktzeh on it; even removed Shabbat, twilight designated all day (and two more: twilight attached/lacking preparation; muktzeh because of mitzvah — sukkah wood/decorations explained in Yom Tov and Sukkah laws). Bach explained these simanim — examiner understands each law and which muktzeh part; each has leniencies unlike muktzeh because of loss.
