Seif 1: One use — forbidden to move it. If on Shabbat his intention is no longer to keep the knife for circumcision but to keep it like other knives for every matter, one can say that in this his designation was removed, and it is permitted according to what we rule on Shabbat like R' Shimon — we do not say "from the fact that it was designated." Except that if so, it should be permitted for its place even while he still keeps it for circumcision, since he has a permitted place to nullify his designation; see what he wrote above, siman 259, in Magen Avraham s.k. 12 in the margin, and nearby in Magen Avraham s.k. 19 in the margin. However, from the words of Tosafot at the beginning of Beitzah, s.v. ein mevak'in, etc., that it is muktzeh because of monetary loss — and behold, now he wants to split it for wood, he nullifies it from designation for building; and nevertheless, even according to R' Meir it is forbidden; and requires study.
