And his words are wondrous: why was it necessary to derive Shabbat from an a fortiori argument from a weekday? Behold, the Shulchan Aruch above, siman 31, ruled explicitly that it is forbidden to leave tefillin on Shabbat because it is a disgrace to the sign of Shabbat; and so Magen Avraham wrote afterward, "otherwise, according to him," etc. — lacking understanding.
