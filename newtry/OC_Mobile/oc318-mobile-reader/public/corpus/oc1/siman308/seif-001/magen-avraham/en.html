It is forbidden to move it. This means that the scribe’s knife itself is forbidden to move. One might have thought that since it is inserted together with other knives, he is not particular about it; therefore it teaches that it is forbidden. But the sheath together with the knives may be moved, for it became a base for both something prohibited and something permitted. See the end of siman 310.
About which they are particular. But in a place where they are not particular about it, it is permitted, as written in seif 3.
And he is particular about them. But if he is not particular about it, it is permitted. See siman 310, seif 2, in the Beis Yosef, and see siman 259, for regarding anything whose value is high it is forbidden, while for something whose value is not high, if he placed it in storage it is forbidden.
