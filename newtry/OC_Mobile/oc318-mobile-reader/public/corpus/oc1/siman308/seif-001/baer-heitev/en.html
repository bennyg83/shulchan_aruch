To move. Meaning, that scribe’s knife is forbidden to move. One might have thought that since it is inserted with the other knives, he is not particular about it; therefore it teaches that it is forbidden. But the sheath together with the knives may be moved, for it became a base for both something prohibited and something permitted. See the end of siman 310.
And he is particular. But if he is not particular about it, it is permitted. See siman 310, seif 2.
