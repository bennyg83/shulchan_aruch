In Taz s.k. 2: And he said that it is not derech hotzaah — meaning that this is only like moving from the side, not in its usual manner; and on this he wrote that in seif 1 he forbids, and in the case of a sheath, etc.; and even though its melacha is forbidden, nevertheless even for its body and place that they permit, it is forbidden because of muktzeh of monetary loss; if so, also here, even though its melacha is permitted and there is no muktzeh of monetary loss, nevertheless it is not for any need at all, and it will not help that its moving is not in the usual manner. From this it is explained that in seif 1 he forbids the sheath — not as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 1; and it must be said that he holds that since there is monetary loss in it and he was careful about it, the sheath became especially forbidden to move the sheath itself; requires study.
