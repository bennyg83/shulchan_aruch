Things permitted and forbidden to move on Shabbat. And in it 52 seifim: All
utensils may be moved on Shabbat except muktzeh because of monetary loss concern, such as a slaughter knife or a circumcision knife and a scribe's knife with which they fix quills — since they are careful not to use them for another purpose, it is forbidden to move them on Shabbat, even for its place or for its body.And even sheathed in a sheath with other knives, it is forbidden to move it (Maharil); and the same applies to a spice mortar that they are careful not to soil. {Rama: And the same applies to utensils designated for commerce that one is careful about. [Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari, chapter 5, and the Maggid Mishneh chapter 25]:) וה"ה לקורנס של בשמים שמקפידים עליו שלא ילכלך: הגה וה"ה כלים המיוחדים לסחורה ומקפיד עליהם.
