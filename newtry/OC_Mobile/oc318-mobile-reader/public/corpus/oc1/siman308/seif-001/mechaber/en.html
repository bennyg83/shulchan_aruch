Things that are permitted and forbidden to move on Shabbos. It contains 52 seifim: All utensils may be moved on Shabbos except muktzeh machmat chisaron kis, such as a slaughtering knife, a circumcision knife, barbers' scissors, and a scribes' knife used to sharpen quills. Because one is particular not to use these for another purpose, it is forbidden to move them on Shabbos even for the use of their place or for a permitted use of the utensil itself. The same law applies to a pestle used for spices when one is particular not to dirty it. {Rama: Even if such a knife is inserted into a sheath together with other knives, it remains forbidden to move. [Maharil]} {Rama: The same law applies to merchandise utensils when one is particular not to use them. [Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam, chapter 5, and Maggid Mishneh, chapter 27]}
