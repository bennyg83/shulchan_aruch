1) [Seif 1] All utensils may be moved on Shabbos, etc. This siman and those following it through the end of siman 312 explain the laws of muktzeh. Rambam writes at the end of chapter 24 that moving muktzeh is forbidden so that moving objects on Shabbos will not resemble moving them during the week. Raavad writes there that moving muktzeh is forbidden because of carrying. These are their words in brief. See Beis Yosef, who cites them at length. Bach writes that it appears even Rambam agrees that the prohibition of muktzeh is because of carrying. Rambam wished only to explain why the law forbidding muktzeh immediately follows the law governing one's manner of walking. Just as the Navi warned that one's manner of walking on Shabbos should not resemble that of the week, so too moving objects on Shabbos should not resemble moving them during the week; see there.
