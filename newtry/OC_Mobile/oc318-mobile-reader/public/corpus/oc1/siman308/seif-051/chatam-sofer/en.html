(There.) In the gloss, seif 51: "and the custom has already spread to prohibit." NB: The custom has already spread to permit; see Responsa Panim Meirot, vol. 2, and Sheilat Yaavetz, vol. 1, p. 72b and vol. 2, siman 63, and the responsa at the end of Mishnat DeRabbi Akiva. However, weekday sand clocks are forbidden even to touch, because it is impossible that the sand will not descend from the upper part to the lower part.
