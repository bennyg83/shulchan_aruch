Another type. Such as viewing in shadow.

There is room to question. It resembles measuring time or measuring shadow, and it is a vessel whose function is for prohibition — see Shulchan Aruch; and if you ask: here it is measurement of a mitzvah, learning by it — permitted, as siman 306 seif 6; this case is different, for the measurement is not a mitzvah; and if because one learns by it — then one would suspend his learning in prohibition and it would be permitted — God forbid (Maharil in responsum siman 228).
