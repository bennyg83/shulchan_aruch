51) There, gloss: Only when needed for its own use, etc. The same should apply to a lulav, allowing it to be moved when needed for its own use or its place. Taz, siman 658, paragraph 3; Eliyah Rabbah there, paragraph 1. Tosefes Shabbos here, paragraph 22, however, questions Taz: How could he write this simply without considering Rema there, who writes that it is like a stone, based on Ran and the Maggid Mishneh? This implies that it may not be moved even when needed for its own use, like a stone. The reason appears simple: it is like muktzeh because of potential financial loss, since people are presumably particular not to use it for anything lest that use disqualify it. It therefore cannot be compared to a shofar. These are his words. Chemed Moshe there, paragraph 2, likewise writes that Taz was imprecise, and Machatzis HaShekel there, paragraph 2, agrees; see there. Mishbetzos Zahav there, paragraph 3; Erekh HaShulchan there, paragraph 2; and Chayei Adam, klal 66, paragraph 13, likewise challenge him. The consensus of the later authorities is that a lulav may not be moved on Shabbos even when needed for its own use or its place, because it has no status of a utensil at all. Regarding an esrog, see siman 658, seif 2, in the gloss, and our words there, with the help of Heaven.
