Permitted to remove them. Meaning: per the view that nolad is permitted — see siman 495.

One shakes the board. One might ask: behold it is a base for the forbidden; one may answer: he did not intend to leave it there all day (see siman 309 seif 4); or since food is there it is a base for prohibition and permission; also this is not placing with intent, for he does not care where they fall — end of Tosafot beginning Beitzah; and so Tosafot Shabbat daf 44b: even if he places on Shabbat, forbidden to shake; but according to what is written siman 266 seif 9 it is not a base unless they were on it at bein hashemashot erev Shabbat — and so siman 311; nevertheless even Tosafot concede: if he removed the muktzeh from there, permitted to move it, for it is not a base since they were not on it at bein hashemashot; and so explicitly in the Gemara; Rambam wrote: if he wishes he throws them with his tongue behind him.

That they are nullified. Meaning: even though wherever there is a base for prohibition and permission, if he can shake he must shake, as siman 309 seif 3 — they said loaf or child only for a corpse, as siman 311 seif 65; these are different because they are nullified relative to bread; but anything not nullified relative to bread and without the status of a vessel — one does not move it with the bread [Rosh in responsa, and so Rabbeinu Yeruham ch. 6]; and it is implied from this that permitted to place with them a loaf or child similar to a corpse; but in his rulings ch. 2 of Yom Tov he wrote: specifically to throw into his mouth when eating into the basket with bread; but to place bread on them in order to move — forbidden, for they said loaf or child only for a corpse; and Ramban in Milchamot and Ran wrote: what is forbidden in other matters with a loaf is when he moves the prohibition itself; but when he moves the permitted and the prohibition rests on it — he moves it with it. And if you ask: let the basket and board be a base for both — one may answer: since he himself is not doing so except to throw them, it is not a base — end of his words; Rabbeinu Yerucham's words in the Rosh's responsa require study.

Permitted to lift. For it is not a base for both, as explained in the name of Tosafot and the Me'or.
