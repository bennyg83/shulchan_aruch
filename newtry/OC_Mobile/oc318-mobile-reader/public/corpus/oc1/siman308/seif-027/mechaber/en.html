Bones fit for dogs, and peels fit for animal food, and crumbs in which there is not an olive-sized piece, may be removed from the table; but if the peels are not fit for animal food, it is forbidden to move them — rather, he shakes the table and they fall; and if there is bread on' the table, it is permitted to lift the table and move it together with peels that are not animal food, for they are batel against the bread; and if he needed the place of the table, even if there is on it only things that are not fit for animal food, it is permitted to lift it and move it.
