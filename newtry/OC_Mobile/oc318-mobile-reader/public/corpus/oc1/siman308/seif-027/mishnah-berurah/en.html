(26) By means of a loaf or child — not specifically a loaf; the same if he placed another permitted object inside. Know: this view is the Rosh's, but many Rishonim dispute and hold they permitted loaf or child only for a corpse to move it by them, as below siman 311 seif 1; not for other muktzeh even if a vessel; many Acharonim ruled thus; and specifically when he places inside something not designated for it; but if something designated for it is there, such as garlic in a mortar or some of the dish in a pot — for all permitted to move the vessel with the food.
