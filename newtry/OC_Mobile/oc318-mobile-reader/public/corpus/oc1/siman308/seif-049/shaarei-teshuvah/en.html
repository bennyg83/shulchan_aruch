Another species — Bach; and regarding moving our ziggelach on Shabbat, see responsa Panim Me'orot vol. 2 siman 122, who permits; and he wrote that the Gaon Chacham Tzvi testified before him regarding an amber light, and so testified regarding the Gaon R' Naftali Katz, and the Gaon Maharash R' Chaim Yeshayah wrote a responsum to permit; therefore one should permit, if not in a place where they practice prohibition — he is not authorized to permit before them. And Beer Yosef wrote that in the cities of Italy they practice prohibition; see there. And Shevut Yaakov vol. 3 siman 125, and similarly Shmuel, and responsa Mayim Rabim siman 31 wrote to forbid, even if the key is not dependent on it; see there; and it spread greatly and greatly, and became practice. In Mishna Berurah he wrote in the name of She'elat Yaavetz siman 63 that large clocks standing in boxes on the ground — one should be stringent about moving them if not for their place; see there. And see Shevut Yaakov regarding moving what they call chandelier light — surely forbidden; and the same in Mora Uktzia, where he wrote there regarding whether it is permitted to press a repeater light to know by the knock at which hour it stands — one can lean that producing sound means a song or a loud voice, etc.; my heart was not filled to permit it, etc., end of his words. And see there that he permits moving a measuring reed of an amah with which they measure, such as in place of a staff and support, or to push some thing and the like; and likewise for its place it is permitted; see there. It appears that a artisan's amah measure, such as of tailors and builders that they are careful about, is forbidden in moving.
