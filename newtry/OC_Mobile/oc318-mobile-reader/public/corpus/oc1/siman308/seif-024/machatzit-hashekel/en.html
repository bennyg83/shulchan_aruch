(s.k. 24) Or its hole, for tumah not vessel without designation; same Shabbos; broken vessels designation helps tumah unlike needle; Zevachim wet hide; needle point removed forbidden even if prepared for stretching tamei; broken vessels regular second use permitted; needle thrown in scrap; mishnah: prepared for stretching tamei without repair, Rambam.
Unlike needle, no sitting designation for wet hide.
Tosafot intent, needle and wet hide same: not customary to designate.
Comment. Main point mishnah plain, prepared for stretching tamei without act; Zevachim: designation alone not vessel for Shabbos until designated for vessel.
