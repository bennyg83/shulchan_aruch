And some say designation does not suffice. Requires study, for Ran in the name of R' Yitzchak forbade only going out with them because it is not evident he designated them for striking, and it looks like taking out on Shabbos; but to move them, for all, designation suffices like palm branches in seif 20; and Rabbeinu Yerucham opened with moving and wrote "some say," etc., his words require study.
