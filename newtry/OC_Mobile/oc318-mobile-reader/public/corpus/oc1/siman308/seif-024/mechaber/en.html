Combed flax and carded wool that they place on a wound — if he thought before Shabbat to place them on the wound, or sat on them for one hour before Shabbat, or anointed them with fish oil, he revealed his intention that they stand for the wound; or if he wrapped them in ointment to place on the wound on Shabbat — there is no longer any concern of muktzeh, and there is also no concern of healing, for it is only like a garment, lest the clothing tear [on the] wound; and some say thought alone does not suffice.
