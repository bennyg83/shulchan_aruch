In Magen Avraham s.k. 11: And also Tosafot, Shabbat daf 60, imply that he holds, etc. In Tosafot there, tefillin are not mentioned at all; only his intent is that Tosafot at the end of chapter 1 of Beitzah wrote this rule: everything from which one does not adorn himself — and on a weekday one multiplies tefillin; but not a nailed sandal, for with tefillin, even if he leaves them, there is no prohibition, unlike a nailed sandal. According to these words of Tosafot, one does not send utensils whose melacha is forbidden on Yom Tov; but Tosafot, Shabbat daf 80, wrote that even if its melacha is forbidden, they send it; and a nailed sandal is different, for harm comes through it — there is no joy of Yom Tov, unlike another utensil whose melacha is forbidden, since when he sends it, it is like for the need of its body. Since they did not divide plainly in Beitzah, it is proven that he holds it is forbidden to leave tefillin; and so from what Tosafot wrote there, that they permit sending utensils whose melacha is forbidden, etc. And in truth, we do not find in Beitzah that they permitted utensils whose melacha is forbidden; necessarily, their intent is from what they permit sending tefillin there. Thus it is explained that Tosafot hold that tefillin are considered melacha forbidden; and it is possible that emendation is needed. Also in siman 316 it is implied that he holds it is forbidden, etc. — permitted to send; and as Tosafot wrote in Shabbat daf 60. Meaning that in siman 316 he wrote that they are suitable to leave on a weekday — it is implied that on Shabbat and Yom Tov it is not suitable to leave them at all. And what he wrote, "otherwise, according to him," is wondrous; and in our opinion, why do we not leave them, etc.? Meaning that it is certainly clear in the gemara, Shabbat, that it is forbidden to leave tefillin; only one can say the reason is lest he go out to reshus harabim; see Shabbat 61, and above siman 125 in the name of Beit Yosef. Therefore, for us, who have no Rosh, there should not be a reason to forbid leaving tefillin; necessarily, the reason is the prohibition of leaving tefillin on Shabbat.
