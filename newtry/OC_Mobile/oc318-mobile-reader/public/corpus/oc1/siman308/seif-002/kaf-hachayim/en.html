(2) Behold the general muktzeh objects forbidden to move divide into six parts. First: muktzeh because of monetary loss — vessel one is particular not to damage, such as slaughter, circumcision, scribes' knives. Second: vessel whose work is forbidden. Third: not vessel, not human food, not animal food — stones, coins, reeds, wood, beams, dust, sand, corpse, animals, fig cakes and raisins drying, and similar not fit — muktzeh because of itself. Fourth: permitted-work vessel with muktzeh because of itself on it — barrel with stone, mat with coins; even removed after, since twilight designated for twilight, designated all day.
