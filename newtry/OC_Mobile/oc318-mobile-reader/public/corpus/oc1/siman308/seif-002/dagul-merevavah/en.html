Meaning that they detached on Shabbat onto the table; and therefore, in order to clear from the table, they permit, even if he has no dogs, if in any case they are fit for dogs. But if they are not on the table — and certainly if they detached on Shabbat — it is worse than if they detached on erev Shabbat; for if they detached on erev Shabbat it was only muktzeh, and if they detached on Shabbat it was nolad; see Beit Yosef, and see Darkei Moshe.
