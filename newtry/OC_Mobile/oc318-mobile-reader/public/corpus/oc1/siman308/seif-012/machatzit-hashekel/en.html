(s.k. 12) Forbidden — we rule R' Shimon; Gemara two baraitot on shofar and trumpets; R' Yehuda forbids trumpets, R' Shimon permits; for us trumpets permitted; not muktzeh chisaron kis — R' Shimon concedes chisaron kis.

Not subject to chisaron kis — we rule R' Shimon on muktzeh; nevertheless chisaron kis forbidden.

Therefore trumpets — not chisaron kis; why baraita according to R' Shimon mentions trumpets?
