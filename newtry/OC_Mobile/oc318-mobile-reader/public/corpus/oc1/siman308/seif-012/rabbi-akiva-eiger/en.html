Magen Avraham s.k. 28: And the Maharam Meiri challenged what he wrote from all fragments. In the Rashba in Chullin he resolved: here a person does not nullify its melacha by this — to designate it to cover ten vessels and to lean a bed leg on it; rather, his thought is to fix it and return it to its designated melacha. And why do we distinguish between a courtyard and a karmlis? For in a karmlis, which is not guarded, he does not designate it in his thought except that he thinks to move it via fresh reed; but in a guarded courtyard he must designate it in his thought until he fixes it — and the Ran in the name of the Ra'avad likewise.
