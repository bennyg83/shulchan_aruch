(Magen Avraham s.k. 61) Did they not say "loaf"? Note: and to me it appears that this is the reason: when there is no water in it, it is forbidden to move it, because it is repulsive and not a utensil — even to cover with it, a vessel is not fit; and Bach likewise. Unlike when there is water in it — it is dealing with fresh water that is fit for an animal to drink, as the Rashba wrote; since there is fresh water in it, it is a utensil to give water to his animal; examine.
