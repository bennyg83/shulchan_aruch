Forbidden to return it. For it is so repulsive it is not fit even to cover a vessel with, see siman 259 [Bach]; see Shulchan Aruch: while still in his hand permitted to return it.
Water into them. Specifically when they are still fit for animal drinking while in the trough; otherwise even the water is a trough [Beit Yosef, Rashba]; and from the fact the Mechaber is silent it implies he holds even if he does not need to clear because of it, permitted to return, as the Rosh; see Mahari Weil who challenged: did they not say loaf or child only for a corpse, as siman 311, see there how he strained himself; and it does not appear so to me, for practical law a vessel is permitted as seif 5, and in Yeshuot Yaakov ch. 5 of Beitzah seif 5 he ruled like Rabbeinu Yeruham: specifically when he needs to clear because of it, or needs to remove excrement with it.
