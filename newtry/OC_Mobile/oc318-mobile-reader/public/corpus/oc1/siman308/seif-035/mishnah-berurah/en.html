(34) That has no relevance of vessel, etc. — such as an iron lamp and board; likewise a wooden chip, for it is not a vessel — forbidden even for its use, such as to drive it in a door bolt or support a board to sit on, unless he made some repair act before Shabbat proving it stands for use, or designated it from erev Shabbat forever, as below seif 22; many err in this [Acharonim]. A wick is called a vessel; even if partly lit on Shabbat that passed [if lit at bein hashemashot this Shabbat it is muktzeh all Shabbat, as above siman 279] or on a weekday — its law is like a vessel whose function is for prohibition, permitted for its use or place; but wick fragments no longer fit to light in a lamp are like pebbles and stones forbidden even for their use or place; likewise a whole candle of wax or tallow, even if already lit on weekday or past Shabbat, as long as still fit to light — called a vessel whose function is for prohibition; hence people are accustomed to move a whole wax or tallow candle lest the candle be lost or to give to a gentile to light for himself; they err, for one may not move a vessel whose function is for prohibition except for the vessel's place or when the Israelite needs the vessel itself for a permitted use such as a crowbar to crack nuts, as above seif 3; Mor VeKetzia is strict: even a whole candle for its place forbidden, for it has no vessel name at all — not fit for food or use.
