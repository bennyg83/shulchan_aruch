But not for need at all, etc. — requires study: accordingly how do they carry knives in their belt pouch hanging on the belt on Shabbat and go with them to the synagogue where there is no need for them? And one might say it is not the manner of carrying out, for in seif 1 he forbids scribes' knives even when stuck in the belt pouch with other knives, and the same here — not for need. And it appears to me they did not forbid this except when not for need the entire day; but if he will use it that day and carries it with him so it will be ready at its time — permitted. And see what is written siman 318:

And tefillin may not be moved except for need. Beit Yosef brought the words of Maharil: precious is this, for he permits moving tefillin, unlike one book that forbids moving them because of muktzeh since placing tefillin is forbidden — Tosafot wrote there is no prohibition in placing them on Shabbat. And I wonder: since permission to move depends on there being no prohibition in placing them, but Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch already ruled siman 31 that there is prohibition in placing them on Shabbat and Yom Tov as Zohar's words — if so, moving is properly forbidden because of muktzeh; why did Beit Yosef and Rama agree here to permit moving? Requires study.
