There, seif 41: A woman leads her son, etc. Note: I was asked whether it is permitted to carry a small child who cannot walk on his feet to say Kaddish on Shabbat in a place where there is no eruv. It appears in my humble opinion, like the view of the questioner, that it is permitted; but not for his reason — since carrying a child is d'oraisa melacha, what difference is there that it is a karmlis? It is only one shevut, and it is forbidden even in a place of mitzvah; it was permitted only during bein hashemashos, and not on Shabbat itself. Rather, another reason appears to me to permit: since this carrying is only to say Kaddish, it is melacha she'einah tzricha legufa, and according to the majority of poskim it is only d'rabbanan; see Magen Avraham siman 278; thus, again it is shevut in a karmlis, shevut di-shevut, and this is well permitted in a place of mitzvah; and this is clear and simple; examine well.
