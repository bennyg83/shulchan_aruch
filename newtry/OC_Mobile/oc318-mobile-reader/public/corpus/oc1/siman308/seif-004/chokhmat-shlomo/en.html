There, seif 4, in the gloss: And tefillin may not be moved except for need, etc. Note: behold, the Beit Yosef brought in the name of Sefer HaMikdash a proof for this from what we say in tractate Beitzah that tefillin are permitted to be sent on Yom Tov; from this it is proven that even if not for its body and its place it is also permitted. I was asked by a certain man: from where is this known? One can say that since we rule that muktzeh, while it is in his hand, may be moved, if so, perhaps it is dealing there that he took them for their place, and then it is permitted to move them to send them to another. And I answered that there is room for distinction: since the essence of the novelty is that they may be sent on Yom Tov, for there is no trouble that he need not move them if not for need, since they are not adorned on Yom Tov — thus it could be thought forbidden, since it is moving not for the need of that day; therefore it teaches that it is permitted, and this is a novelty. But if we say that it is dealing that they were already in his hand, if so, since in any case he could carry them to whatever place he wants, if his intention is that he wants to leave them in such-and-such a house, there it is certainly permitted, since he is permitted to carry them to any place he wants; and so it is explicit in siman 267 that he can go with them to whatever room he wants; and in the market it is forbidden from another reason — carrying four cubits in reshus harabim or in a place where there is no eruv; but otherwise, even in the market, it would be permitted to carry them to whatever house he wants, as is implied from the question of Magen Avraham from a shofar, that it should be permitted to carry it to whatever place he wants. What difference does it make that his intention is also that it be for a fellow? Since in any case he had permission to leave them there — behold, if tefillin are truly forbidden in moving, a fellow cannot receive them in his hands from him; only the one who carries them places them there; thus it is plainly permitted, and necessarily it is dealing that they were not in his hands, only that he took them in order to carry them there; this is properly a novelty of not moving and trouble not for need; and difficult; examine.
