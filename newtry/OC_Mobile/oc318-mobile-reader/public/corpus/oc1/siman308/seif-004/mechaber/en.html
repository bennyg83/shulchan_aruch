A utensil whose melacha is permitted may be moved even if it is only for the need of the utensil itself, lest it break or be stolen; but if not for any need at all, it is forbidden to move it. And sacred writings and food may be moved even if not for any need at all.And tefillin may not be moved except for need (Terumat HaDeshen; and see Beit Yosef); and a) shofar is forbidden to move except for its body or its place (Hagahot Ashiri at the end of the chapter BeMah Madlikin):.
