Magen Avraham s.k. 49: It appears to me permitted to remove it and return it, etc. — meaning, if they are in the courtyard and in a utensil, and he has no water to put into it, nevertheless it is permitted to remove it and return it; and more it appears to mark on the seif that follows, that it is forbidden to return them; on this he says that in Hagahot Gemara it is permitted; and what he caught — "to remove it" — means even if it is in the courtyard; and s.k. 60 leans on the word "to return it."
