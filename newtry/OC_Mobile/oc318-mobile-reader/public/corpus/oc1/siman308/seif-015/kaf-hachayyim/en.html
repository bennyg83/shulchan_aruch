15) There, gloss: And he is particular about them. If he is not particular about them, they may be moved although they were set aside for merchandise. Beis Yosef; Levush; Magen Avraham, paragraph 3. Any expensive item is forbidden even if it was not placed in storage. An inexpensive item is forbidden only if it was placed in storage and one is particular about it. Magen Avraham there; Machatzis HaShekel; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 6.
