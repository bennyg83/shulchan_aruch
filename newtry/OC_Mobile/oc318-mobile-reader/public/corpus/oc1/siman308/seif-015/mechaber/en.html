A sandal whose inner strap broke still has the status of a utensil upon it, and it is permitted to move it; if the outer one broke, it is nullified from the status of a utensil and is forbidden to move; and if it is in a karmlis, it is permitted to wrap fresh reed, which is animal food, around it to fix it so it will not fall from his foot; but in a guarded courtyard, it is forbidden.
