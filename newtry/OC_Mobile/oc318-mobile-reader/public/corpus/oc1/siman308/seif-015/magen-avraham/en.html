It loses the status of a vessel. For it is degrading to repair it since it is visible from outside; the Maggid Mishneh challenged: what of all broken vessels that are permitted, as seif 6? One may answer: not fit to cover vessels because it is repulsive (Beit Yosef); requires study, for we say a nailed sandal is permitted to move to cover a vessel and to support bed legs; also we require specifically what is fit to cover a vessel — otherwise even fit for another use is permitted; see seif 11 in the name of Tosafot. And it appears to me regarding our shoes: even if the strap broke they are still fit to wear and retain the status of a vessel; specifically their shoes that were fully open on top and the sides of wood or hard leather, and the strap tied over the foot as Rashi wrote — if the strap broke he could not fasten it; unlike ours.

Fresh reed. And it appears to me with a string or smearing it is forbidden, lest he nullify it and it becomes a permanent knot, as Rambam's words there; see Kesef Mishnah in the name of the Rambach — requires study to reconcile them in the Gemara.

It is guarded there — forbidden. And it appears to me: if many are there and it is degrading for him to go without a shoe — permitted because of human dignity, as siman 313 seif 13.
