Magen Avraham s.k. 56: That it is not fit to eat raw — forbidden; and the same law. In my humble opinion, nevertheless it is permitted to throw it to dogs; for from now he wants to give it to dogs, it becomes prepared for dogs, and it is permitted according to what we rule like R' Shimon — we do not say "from the fact that it was designated during bein hashemashos"; and only moving is forbidden, for all the time he does not designate it for dogs, presumably he will not give it to dogs, and it is not fit for dogs. In my humble opinion, this is the intent of the Ra'avad, chapter 24 of Shabbat, s.v. machatkim gourds; and Tosafot Tosefet that he brought there; examine.
