A vessel whose work is forbidden — even if its prohibition is only d'rabbanan [Peri Megadim] — see there further regarding a perforated vessel for nuts broken by rolling. He lengthy treated this and finally leaned lenient:

A plane to cut, etc. — the Mishnah counts also a plane for scraping cheese and more; Rashi explained there it is a knife full of notches — it appears no distinction between large and small plane; all he is particular not to damage and designates a place; it is in "large plane" in the Mishnah. But from Rambam's Mishnah commentary it appears a small plane is only in vessels whose work is forbidden — requires study of the reason. Know Peri Megadim brought in Mishna Berurah that a vessel mostly for forbidden work is also in "whose work is forbidden," with proof from Rashba regarding a pot; see there.
