Whose function is for prohibition. Meaning: designated for something forbidden to do on Shabbat.

And if he used it for prohibition. Meaning: something forbidden was resting on it, so it became a base for the forbidden; but if he did melacha with a crowbar — plainly permitted to move it on Shabbat for the need of its use or its place.

And to place it in any place. For since it is in his hand he may move it further; and it appears to me that even if he forgot and took it in his hand he may move it further; and see siman 266 seif 12: it is stated in Shabbat daf 127 that one may move muktzeh with his feet in the manner of walking, and so in Milchamot there — see siman 305 seif 11.

But from sun to shade. It is stated in Yerushalmi ch. 5 of Beitzah: when he intends it for use it is permitted to move it even if his main intent is from sun to shade; see Shabbat daf 124b.
