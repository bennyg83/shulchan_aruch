(s.k. 3) And he is particular, etc. — see siman 310 seif 2: fruit for commerce is not muktzeh and permitted to eat on Shabbat; what difference fruit for commerce and vessels for commerce? Therefore here because he is particular; if he truly is not particular — here too permitted.

Matters whose value is high — forbidden even if not placed in storage; matters whose value is not high require specifically placement in storage and being particular, as Magen Avraham siman 259.
