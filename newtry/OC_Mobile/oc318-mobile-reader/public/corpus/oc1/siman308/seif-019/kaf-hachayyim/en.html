19) [Seif 3] A utensil whose designated use is forbidden. This means that it is designated for an activity forbidden on Shabbos. Magen Avraham, paragraph 5. Even if the activity has not yet been performed with it, but it is new and was prepared and stands ready for that purpose, such as a new mortar, it is nevertheless classified by its sole intended forbidden use, although mere designation ordinarily has no effect. A use forbidden even rabbinically suffices, as demonstrated by a shofar. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 5. See there further regarding a nutcracker called knak-holtz, a receptacle with a wooden piece attached by a screw into which nuts are placed for cracking. He discusses it at length and ultimately inclines toward leniency; see there.
