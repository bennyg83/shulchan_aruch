Seif 5: A utensil whose melacha is forbidden. And the same — a utensil that is muktzeh because of monetary loss — it appears that it is permitted, even though it has no permission for itself, for its body and place; for the source of this law came from the mortar with garlic. And in the first chapter of All Utensils, he wanted to prove from the braita that a utensil whose melacha is forbidden is forbidden for its body and place — behold, it has no permission at all; nevertheless, he moves it via the garlic. If so, the same applies to a candle that was lit in it on Shabbat — it is permitted via a loaf and a child; and the Me'or brought in the name of a certain sage; see above siman 279 seif 3, and Taz siman 311 s.k. 5, and Tosafot Beitzah daf 21 s.v. kanona; examine.
