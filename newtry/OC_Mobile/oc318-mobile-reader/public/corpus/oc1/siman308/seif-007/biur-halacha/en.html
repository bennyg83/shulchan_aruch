And if he threw it, etc. — even when still fit for original work; proof from below seif 12 regarding mat fragments fit for original work in the Gemara, yet forbidden when thrown [we do not say it is like a whole vessel not becoming muktzeh by throwing because intent is nullified as in s.v. mat — see there]:

To the ash heap — appears not precise; the same if thrown from his doorway to the street; customary language cited throwing to street, for one does not usually throw to a walking place; possibly if left behind the door it is also in ash-heap category for moving as for impurity per R' Yosi and R' Akiva 29a [Rambam ruled like R' Eliezer contrary to his Mishnah commentary like R' Yosi]; but per Rashi s.v. mat leniencies one may reject:
