Pebbles. It is obvious that the same applies to a piece of wood that is not a vessel; it is forbidden to move it even for the need of its body, such as to insert it in place of a bolt for a door, unless he made some practical adjustment to it before Shabbos. Many stumble in this. Through a non-Jew, however, it is permitted regarding every prohibition of muktzeh, as written in siman 276, seif 3, regarding moving a lamp. Taz. A whole wick is called a vessel, while pieces of a wick are called pebbles; if so, it is permitted to move a whole tallow or wax candle for the need of its body or place, as written in seif 3. See Magen Avraham.
