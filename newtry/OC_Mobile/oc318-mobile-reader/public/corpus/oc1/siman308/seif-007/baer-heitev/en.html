On a weekday. The same on Shabbat as Magen Avraham and Taz wrote seif 6; see Magen Avraham and Taz:

Pebbles. Plainly the same for a piece of wood that is not a vessel — forbidden to move even for bodily need, such as to wedge in a door bolt place, unless he did some repair before Shabbat; many err in this; but via gentile permitted for every muktzeh moving prohibition as siman 276 seif 3 regarding moving a lamp. Taz. And a whole wick is called a vessel; wick fragments are called pebbles; if so permitted to move a whole lamp of fat or wax for bodily and place need as seif 3; see Magen Avraham:
