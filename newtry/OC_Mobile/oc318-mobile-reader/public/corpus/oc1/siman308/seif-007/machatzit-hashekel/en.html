(s.k. 7) And to place it, etc. — even if he forgot: not that "for its place" requires permissible beginning of moving; rather even if he began by prohibition through forgetting, permitted to place wherever he wishes; see siman 266; see Sefer Turei Even.

It is stated in Shabbat, etc. — Milchamot; baraita: one does not begin with a storehouse first (R' Yehuda forbids muktzeh); but one makes a path to enter and exit; they thought with hands, therefore the Gemara asks and answers: with feet on entry and exit. Rif copied it; Razah challenged: baraita is R' Yehuda, we rule R' Shimon; Ramban: because of the end "makes a path" — for us even muktzeh forbidden to move, nevertheless permitted to make a path with feet.
