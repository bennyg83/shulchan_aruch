That broke on a weekday. So Tur wrote, from Tosafot who wrote: if on Shabbat, we already said in ch. Notel Rava has muktzeh, and all the more nolad — end quote. Difficult: the first tanna permits even on Shabbat as written shortly — one must say Tosafot hold we do not rule like the first tanna but like R' Yosi from that statement of Rava as in the question shortly; and it appears therefore Rosh did not bring what Tosafot explained, for he ruled like the first tanna permitted even when broken on Shabbat; but on Tur it is difficult: he ruled like the first tanna permitted even broken on Shabbat, and here ruled only on a weekday. Beit Yosef answered: there with vessels that broke, here with a vessel from which a piece broke, and that piece was not fit to serve on its own before it broke, and now when broken it is fit to use on its own — it is nolad — end quote. Difficult: why did Tur strain to explain thus and not as plainly the first tanna? And it appears to me Tur also follows Tosafot and holds they did not permit in the Mishnah except when moving for work need for what it is fit for even if not the original kind; and necessarily to say so: if moving without need it would be forbidden even for permitted work as below; but here with this pottery that is a vessel in the courtyard to cover vessels, and now in public domain wiping shoes in it as in the Gemara regarding Rava — Tur's words: even where vessels are not common to cover with it, he does something else with it not called a vessel for that purpose — with this you must say permitted because today it became a vessel for something else in the courtyard; and necessarily it is nolad there, and Rava has nolad forbidden — necessarily when broken on a weekday, as appears to me.

Pebbles or stones — plainly the same for a piece of wood that is not a vessel, forbidden to move even for bodily need, such as to wedge it in a door bolt place as siman 313; unless he did some repair work before Shabbat as Mordechai ch. Kol HaKelim and so siman 308 s.k. 2; and the same if he wants to support a page on it.
