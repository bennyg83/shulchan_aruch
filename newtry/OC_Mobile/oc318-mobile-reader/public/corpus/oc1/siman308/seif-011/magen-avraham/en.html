Or its hole is forbidden. From that for tumah it is not a vessel, for Shabbat too it is not a vessel; and if you ask: what of broken vessels as seif 7? One may answer: there designation for tumah helps, unlike a needle where designation does not help unless he makes some change and repair for stretching (Tosafot daf 49); and in Zevachim daf 94 they wrote: wet hide — even though it receives tumah by thought, since he did not designate it for sitting while still wet it is forbidden to move; and the same should apply to a needle whose point or eye was removed — forbidden to move even though if he prepared it for stretching it is tamei — end of his words; and his intent: specifically with broken vessels that people regularly use for another purpose — permitted to move; unlike a needle that people regularly throw among scrap when broken, as in the Gemara; and so it appears to me as the main point — the plain sense of the mishnah implies that if he prepared it for stretching it is tamei without any repair, and so Rambam ch. 11 of Kelim.
