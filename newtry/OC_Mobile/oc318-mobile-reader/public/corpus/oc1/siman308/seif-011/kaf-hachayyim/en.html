11) There, gloss: It is forbidden to move it. This means that the bookbinder's knife itself may not be moved. One might have thought that because it is inserted among other knives, he is not particular about it; the ruling teaches that it is forbidden. The sheath together with the knives, however, may be moved, because it was made as a base for both forbidden and permitted items, as stated at the end of siman 310; see there.
