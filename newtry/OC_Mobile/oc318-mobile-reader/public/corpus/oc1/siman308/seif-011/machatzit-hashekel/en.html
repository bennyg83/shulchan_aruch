(s.k. 11) And shofar — from three categories: sacred writings, tefillin, shofar — different laws: scriptures permitted even not for need; tefillin only for need, even sun to shade; shofar only for use or place.

No sign needed — Shabbat and Yom Tov themselves are a sign; no need for tefillin sign, as Tosafot ch. 1 Yom Tov: mishnah sending garments on Yom Tov; Rav Sheshet permitted sending tefillin; Abbaye challenged: tefillin not ornamental on Yom Tov; answer: mishnah means ornamental on weekday; Tosafot: only tefillin, not nailed sandal; sending tefillin no prohibition of wearing; see Shitah Mekubetzet: mishnah "ornamental on weekday" — Chol HaMoed liable death if shows need for tefillin sign.

And Tosafot Shabbat — besides Zohar, Gemara proves Tosafot Yom Tov unlike Tosafot Shabbat forbidding leaving tefillin.

Rather they hold, etc. — Tosafot there s.v. not nailed sandal: R' Yitzchak even like Rabbis of R' Eliezer who permit moving nailed sandal forbids sending on Yom Tov though sending is like use since tragedy with sandal — no Yom Tov joy; therefore Tosafot infer permitted to send vessel whose function is for prohibition on Yom Tov; tefillin are such; forbidden to leave on Shabbat and Yom Tov.

Otherwise why not leave, etc. — proof for above: no obligation on Shabbat, nevertheless one who leaves gets reward; like women in mitzvot they may bless though not obligated; though forbidden to leave on Shabbat.

And what Bach inferred from "may not go out in tefillin" — mishnah and Gemara 61: both according to Shabbat time for tefillin and not; l'chatchila not go out lest need bathroom and remove and carry; sippa according to Shabbat not time — not a load; Bach: in house permitted. Tosafot Menachot 36: Rabbah — Rosh HaShanah night is time, Shabbat forbidden to leave, not time; Gemara excludes Shabbat and Yom Tov from "from days to days"; Tosafot: perhaps Shabbat Torah time, rabbinically forbidden as mishnah; R' Anani bar Sasson: ornaments forbidden in reshut ha-rabbim but permitted in courtyard — here courtyard; for Rav forbidden in courtyard except spit and foreign forelock; Rabbah not challenged; perhaps Rabbah agrees tefillin permitted in courtyard; no need bathroom concern every moment; Tosafot: if not time, no need "except tefillin"; Sanhedrin 68 — R' Eliezer ill Friday, Vespasian's son donned tefillin, rebuked — how leave stoning prohibition and engage in shevut; Rashi: Shabbat not time, forbidden lest go to reshut ha-rabbim; Tosafot Sanhedrin: though R' Eliezer did not move, called shevut; Tosafot: all ornaments forbidden in courtyard too; like Rav; Rashi Sanhedrin: Shabbat not time, not obligated to handle constantly — Rav fears forget and go to reshut ha-rabbim.

All ornaments forbidden in courtyard — like Rav, not R' Anani.

However tefillin Rav concedes — must handle constantly, no fear forget; Rashi: Shabbat not time, not obligated constantly — Rav forbids courtyard lest forget reshut ha-rabbim; sitting in courtyard also forbidden.

Nevertheless like vessel whose function is for prohibition — Zohar: death for leaving on Shabbat; only use or place like shofar; even Gemara: Shabbat not time — like vessel whose function for prohibition though no intrinsic prohibition of leaving, only decree lest go out; Magen Avraham: not called for permission.

And Tosafot Shabbat 46 — foul oil lamp permitted as fit to cover vessel; challenge: all pebbles? Answer: only vessel with status; Tosafot: Ulla proves even courtyard forbidden ornaments lest forget reshut ha-rabbim — therefore status of vessel needed; Tosafot hold those ornaments are vessels whose function for prohibition; same tefillin.

And see Shulchan Aruch ch. 18 — for poskim like R' Anani tefillin permitted in courtyard, therefore permitted to move as permitted vessel.
