The thorn — Mishnah beginning ch. Kol HaKelim; Mechaber omitted Mishnah end and sacks to open a door with them, for this rule is in seif beginning; here teaches we are not concerned lest he wound, for it is destructive and melacha not needed for itself; where there is distress rabbis did not decree as Sanhedrin ch. 5 — see Gemara. Magen Avraham wrote siman 328 s.k. 32: provided he beware not to draw blood, making a wound as Sanhedrin ch. 5 — meaning not psik reisha for blood; Ateret Zekeinim challenged; Baal Chesed disagreed: where distress, no decree even this way; so Sefer HaChassidut Chesed Moshe:
