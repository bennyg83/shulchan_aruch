(Magen Avraham s.k. 59) To remove it and return it. Note: requires study — it appears even without water; and in s.k. 5 he wrote in the name of Maharshal and Mahariv that it is dealing when he put water in it and needs the utensil to clear on it; thus his words contradict one another. Also in Yesh Seder and Mahariv this was not mentioned — only that he needs the utensil to remove excrement or urine; see there.
