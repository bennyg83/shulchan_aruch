And I have a question: from the words of the Shach below, siman 310 seif 8, it is clear that moving with one's body, even for a forbidden thing, is permitted, and the Rama there did not divide; if so, here too we should say that it is not moving at all, and even for a forbidden thing it should be permitted. And the source is in Maharil, responsum 222, where he compared it to straw on a bed that one sways with his body.
