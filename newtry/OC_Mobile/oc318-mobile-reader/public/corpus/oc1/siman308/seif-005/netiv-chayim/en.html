(There) But it is permitted to sit on them. Note: and from now on there is no distinction between heavy or small stones; thus the words of the Mordechai require study; but nevertheless the words of the Rama are justified — some say the law of stones is like the law of branches, for the Ran and the Rosh who permit on stones to sit on them; and now there is no way to establish sitting that does not move; and difficult.
