[2] Tefillin, etc. Rabbeinu Yonah wrote that he mentioned these not as specifically required; and the same applies—that one should not take anything in his hand during prayer except a lulav alone. And Nachalas Tzvi wrote that so it appears from the words of the Rambam, who wrote generally “and not a vessel”—so far his wording. Perforce there is a scribal error in his words, for in the Rambam this wording does not exist; rather one must say “and not vessels,” and so it is in the Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilchos Tefillah—this is his wording: “and not vessels and money,” so far. And this is what appears to me from his mentioning vessels generally—that one should not take anything; but it is not proof, for one can say that he mentioned vessels similar to money, because one is preoccupied with them; and so wrote the Lechem Mishneh, and so it appears in the book Tzeda LaDerech. However, in the Semak, siman 11, I saw as the words of Rabbeinu Yonah, and the Taz likewise agreed.
