He is not distracted. If a sefer fell to the floor and he cannot concentrate because of it, he may lift it after completing the blessing in which he is presently engaged. Sefer Chasidim; Magen Avraham.
To pray from it. I wrote above in siman 93, seif katan 2, that the Arizal would pray from a siddur in order to concentrate intensely. Even during the chazzan's repetition of the Amidah, the siddur should remain open before him so that his ears remain attentive to what the chazzan says. Shelah.
