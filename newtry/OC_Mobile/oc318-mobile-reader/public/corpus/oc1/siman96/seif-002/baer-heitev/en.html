He is not distracted. A book fell to the ground and he cannot concentrate because of it—it is permitted to pick it up when he finishes the blessing (Eliyah Rabbah; Magen Avraham).

To pray from it. I wrote above in siman 93, small seif 2, that the Ari, may his memory be blessed, would pray from a book in order to concentrate very well; and even during the repetition of the shaliach tzibbur of the Shemoneh Esrei the siddur should be open before him so that his ears are attentive to what the shaliach tzibbur says (Shelah).
