(8) He should not go back for it—because it distracts. And the Taz wrote that likewise one who prays from the siddur should be careful to mark in advance all the places he must pray in that prayer, and not search for them while praying, for thereby he will interrupt and not concentrate well. And likewise Birkei Yosef wrote that many men of action are accustomed thus when they wish to say a renewed prayer such as “Our needs” or “And inform us” when Yom Tov falls on Motzei Shabbos.

(9) To pray from it—and it is found among the later authorities that even during the repetition of the shaliach tzibbur it is proper that the siddur be open before him so that his ears are attentive to what the shaliach tzibbur says.
