(2) There: he should not hold tefillin in his hand, etc. The expounders of abbreviations said: “My prayer” written fully twice—the acronym: Sefer, tefillin, money—one who holds them in his hand during prayer, his prayer is not heard (Sefer Kol Bochim, in the commentary of Mahar"a Galanti, end of page 59; Machatzit HaShekel, Be'ur, note 1).
