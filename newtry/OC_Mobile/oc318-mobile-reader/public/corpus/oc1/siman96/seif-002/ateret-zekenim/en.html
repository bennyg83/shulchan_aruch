Prayerbooks, etc. It is good to pray from a written text, for the written text awakens concentration (Tanya). And even during the repetition of the shaliach tzibbur of the Shemoneh Esrei, the siddur should be open before him so that his ears are attentive to what the shaliach tzibbur says, and he should not turn his thoughts here and there (Toledot Chaim).
