A bowl—see Be'er Heitev. And above, siman 93, the Magen Avraham wrote that the Ari, may his memory be blessed, would pray from a book, etc.; and he wrote that in the book of intentions he wrote that the verses of praise and “Who forms light” he would say from the siddur, but the Shemoneh Esrei he would say with eyes closed. And Machatzit HaShekel wrote that each person should estimate for himself whether he can concentrate more from the book or by heart, and so he should act according to his traits; as the Magen Avraham explains, and Be'er Heitev brought it. And what the Taz wrote that one should mark in advance—see what is written below, siman 119, in the name of Birkei Yosef.
