Eshel Avraham: Berachos 23b—the Sages taught: one should not hold a Sefer Torah and tefillin and pray. Shmuel said: a knife, money, a bowl, a loaf, and the like. And for Rashi, these specifically; and for Rabbeinu Yonah, everything is forbidden. If so, what did the baraita mean when Shmuel said (a Sefer Torah and tefillin—how so? Even though there is a mitzvah to guard them, it is forbidden)? One can say: according to Talmudic law one returns and repeats if he did not concentrate. One can say three distinctions: these—one returns; other things—forbidden at the outset; and a lulav—permitted. And for us, in any case one does not return—and so it appears. And the Maggid Mishnah, chapter 5, explains that eight things do not invalidate—see there—even according to Talmudic law. And it is difficult: why did he list these? And one who prays—we too, Kerias Shema, even the verses of praise—for us. And see siman 89, note 3.
