Seif 1: Nor a sefer, etc. As stated in the final chapter of Eruvin, all sacred writings are disgraced if they fall. The same applies to every sefer, as stated in chapter 16 of Shabbos, that even those writings which may not be read require burial, including amulets and blessings. Likewise, the Gemara states there in chapter 6, 61b: Come and hear, if it was written, etc. This is certainly true of our present-day sefarim, which may be read and rescued from a fire, as the Rosh writes in chapter 16 of Shabbos. His mind will therefore be preoccupied with them.
And a lulav, etc. End of the third chapter of Sukkah.
