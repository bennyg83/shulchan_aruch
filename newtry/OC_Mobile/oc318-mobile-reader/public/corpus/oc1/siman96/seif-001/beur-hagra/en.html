A book of Scripture, and not [just] a book, etc. As is written in the last chapter of Eruvin, that all holy writings—if they fall there is disgrace in it; and likewise for every book, as is written in chapter 157 of Shabbos, that even those in which one does not read require burial, and even amulets and blessings; and they said there in chapter 86, seif 2: “If a Torah scroll was written,” etc.; and all the more so books nowadays that one reads in them and saves them from fire, as the Rosh wrote in chapter 157 of Shabbos—if so, his heart is preoccupied with them.

And a lulav, etc. End of chapter 43 of Sukkah.
