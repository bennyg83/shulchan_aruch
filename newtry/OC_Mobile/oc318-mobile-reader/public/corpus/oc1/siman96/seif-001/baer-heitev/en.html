Tefillin. After the fact, he need not pray again. Taz and Peri Chadash, unlike the Bach. Regarding the practice that on Shabbos the chazzan holds the Sefer Torah while reciting Yekum Purkan, etc., it appears to me that since his intention at that time is to pray for those who study Torah, he therefore holds the Sefer Torah, and it is like holding the lulav at its appointed time. Taz.
A bowl. Specifically these objects are forbidden, because if they fall there will be a loss or they may injure him. Other objects may be held. Rashi and Ran. Rabbeinu Yonah, however, writes in the name of some commentators that these examples are not exclusive, but reflect the usual circumstances, and that one should not hold anything during prayer except a lulav. See there. Taz writes: For this reason, it appears to me that one who prays from a siddur should first mark all the places required for that prayer and should not search for them while praying, because this will interrupt him and impair his concentration.
