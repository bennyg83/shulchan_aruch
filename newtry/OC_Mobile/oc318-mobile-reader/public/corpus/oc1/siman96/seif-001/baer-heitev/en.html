Tefillin. And after the fact one need not return and pray again (Taz; Pri Chadash), unlike the Bach. And what people are accustomed to—that the shaliach tzibbur holds a Sefer Torah in his hand on Shabbat when he says Yehi ratzon and the like—it seems to me that since his intention then is to pray for Torah scholars, therefore he holds the Sefer Torah in his hand, and it is like a lulav in its time. Taz.

A bowl. Specifically these items, for if they fall there is loss in them or they may injure him; but other things are permitted to hold (Rashi; Ran). And Rabbeinu Yonah wrote in the name of the Yereim that these are not specifically required but he mentioned them as the usual manner of speech, and likewise one should not take anything in his hand during prayer except a lulav alone—see there. And the Taz wrote; and for this reason it seems to me that one who prays from the siddur should also be careful to mark in advance all the places he must pray in that prayer, and not search for them while praying, for thereby he will interrupt and not concentrate well—so he wrote.
