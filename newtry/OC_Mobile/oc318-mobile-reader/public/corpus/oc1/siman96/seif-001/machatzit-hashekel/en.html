(Note 1) And not a bowl, etc.—specifically these, etc. And the Taz ruled as the poskim, that every thing is forbidden except a lulav and the like. He also wrote to permit the prayer leader to hold a Sefer Torah when he says Yehi ratzon, and likewise when they bless the new month and the like—even though there the Taz’s reason that he wrote regarding Yehi ratzon does not apply, for even in the Shemoneh Esrei it would seem to the Taz to permit, only he did not wish to be lenient since the permission is not found explicit, at least in other places besides the Shemoneh Esrei, if his intention in holding the Sefer Torah is to awaken concentration—so he wrote.

He also wrote that before he begins to pray the Shemoneh Esrei he should mark in the prayer siddur all the places he will need during the Shemoneh Esrei, so that he need not search during his prayer.
