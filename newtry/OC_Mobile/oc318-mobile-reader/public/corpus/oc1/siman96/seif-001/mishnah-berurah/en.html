(1) When he is praying—in the Peri Megadim he wrote that the same applies during Kerias Shema and the verses of praise.

(2) He should not hold, etc.—and after the fact one need not return and pray again unless he knows that thereby he did not concentrate in the first blessing. And the shaliach tzibbur is permitted to hold the Sefer Torah in his hand on Shabbat when he says Yehi ratzon, for since his intention then is to pray for Torah scholars, therefore he holds the Sefer Torah in his hand—not for the sake of guarding it—and it is like a lulav in its time. And likewise it is permitted to hold it when they bless the new month.

(3) And not a book, etc.—and the same applies to all our books.

(4) A full bowl—his heart is on it lest something spill from it; and with a knife lest it fall to the ground and stab his foot; and with money lest they scatter; and even if they are bundled he is preoccupied lest they be lost—unless he holds them in a manner where there is no concern lest they be lost. And Birkei Yosef wrote that likewise it is forbidden to seat a child before him during prayer.

(5) And money and a loaf, etc.—specifically these, for if they fall there is loss in them or they may injure him; but other things are permitted to hold. And some say that these are not specifically required but he mentioned them as the usual manner of speech; and likewise one should not take anything in his hand during prayer—and the Taz agreed.

(6) Because his heart is on them, etc.—it requires investigation whether it is permitted to take tefillin and a Sefer Torah in his hand where he fears thieves, for perhaps they permitted him [Peri Megadim].

(7) Permitted to hold, etc.—a book fell to the ground and he cannot concentrate—it is permitted to pick it up when he finishes the blessing in which he is standing; and if not so, he should not interrupt; and all the more so one should not gesture with a finger and the like during prayer. If he began to pray the Shemoneh Esrei and became confused, it is permitted to go to a place known to him to take a siddur from there [Chayei Adam].
