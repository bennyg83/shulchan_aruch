And tomorrow on Friday they fast etc. It does not seem appropriate to rely on this in practice, since Beit Yosef brought in name of Maharam that the fast is moved to Thursday because one cannot postpone until Sunday which is Purim, and likewise one does not fast on erev Shabbat because of honor of Shabbat; and although halacha is established that on erev Shabbat one fasts and completes — that is only when its appointed time comes on erev Shabbat, but when postponed from its time it is not established on erev Shabbat. And as in responsum of Geonim: a communal fast is not fasted — when it is postponed; and Meiri on singular in chapter Kol Marbin: sons of Rabbi Yehuda ben Bava in Tannaita in Tash — what is Ashlomi — for this they said there is torture and complements, and they were used because of Dadoka a name whose time is fixed. What is written in PB Megillat Taanit applies to Fast of Damat and completes — we were in fast of Zabor; nevertheless Rama already wrote in siman 249 a singular Megillat Taanit will not complete, and here it must complete — it will not be fasted in Beit Din, and we will see if Fast of Esther in Beit Din is fulfilled he must complete until stars come out because it is his judgment; so it comes to mind that at his appointed time per sages for Thursday he will not be tormented for milah feast, and on Shabbat he will be tormented until night and Shabbat already entered — what if he is tormented part of Shabbat or all of it? Rather he will eat the milah feast at night as in gemara and siman 12; and here they forbade establishing Fast of Esther on Shabbat because of honor of Shabbat; above siman 249 Rama brought column through people of maaseh to fast on erev Shabbat Shabbat pleasure — their way to eat greedily when fasting; after all most of the world are weak after the fast — therefore the fast should not be established thus.
