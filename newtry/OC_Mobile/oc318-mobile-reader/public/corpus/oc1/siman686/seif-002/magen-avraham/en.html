They fast on the thirteenth. Some fast night and day, Keneses HaGedolah.
To fast on Thursday. But on Friday one does not initially establish a fast, because of the honor of Shabbos, Maggid Mishneh, Bach, Hagahos Maimoniyos. See what I wrote at the end of siman 249. Maharil would not teach the halachah on the Fast of Esther because they were occupied with buying Purim needs, unless Purim fell on Sunday. In Hagahos Maimoniyos he proved from the Gemara that even on Purim they were accustomed to study before their teacher.
They should not separate. Even one traveling on the road for whom the fast is difficult, Beis Yosef, Shibolei HaLeket.
