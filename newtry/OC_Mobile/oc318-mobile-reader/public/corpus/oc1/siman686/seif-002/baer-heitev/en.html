On the thirteenth. Some fast night and day, Keneses HaGedolah. Maharil would not teach the halachah on the Fast of Esther because they were occupied with buying Purim needs, unless Purim fell on Sunday. In Hagahos Maimoniyos he proved from the Gemara that even on Purim they were accustomed to study before their teacher.
In distress. All the more so a woman within thirty days after childbirth.
They should not separate. Even one who is traveling on the road and the fast is difficult for him, Beis Yosef, Shibolei HaLeket.
To eat. It implies even more than ten. The Taz wrote that one should not rely on this gloss; rather, he should eat the circumcision meal at night, as on Tishah BeAv; see there. When Adar enters, we increase joy, and one who has a court case with a gentile should have it judged in Adar. Maharam Alashkar, siman 41, wrote that the residents of a city may enact, with agreement and a ban upon themselves and upon those who come after them, to make a Purim because a miracle happened for them. Even if they go to another city to settle there, it applies to them. One to whom a miracle happened in Adar and who vowed to make a Purim for it: some say that in a leap year he should make it in the second Adar, and some say he should make it in the first Adar, unless the miracle happened in a leap year in the second Adar, Rav Shmuel HaLevi, siman 16. See what I wrote in siman 568, section 7. In Magen Avraham he disagrees with him, and in the book Eliyah Rabbah he rejected his words in siman 685; see there. Keneses HaGedolah wrote: one who fasts on the day a miracle happened to him, and it occurs on Shabbos, should fast on Friday.
