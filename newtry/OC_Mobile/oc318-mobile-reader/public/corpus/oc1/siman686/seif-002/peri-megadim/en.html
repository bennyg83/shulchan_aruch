The explanation of his words is that one who examines Beis Yosef in the name of the Rosh, of blessed memory, in the first chapter of Megillah, in the name of a responsum of the Geonim, sees that they increase selichos and will not be able to trouble themselves for the honor of Shabbos. It is explicit that individuals may borrow and repay on any day, for they already said selichos on Thursday. The Taz connected his words to the words of the Maggid Mishneh, which he brought, that this is the reason on Thursday, that we do not establish a fast on Friday; and that which one fasts and completes when it occurs on Friday, etc., is not because of selichos, but because since it was deferred, it is deferred to Thursday because of the honor of Shabbos. However, the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 5 of Taanis concluded with the responsum of the Geonim, which implies selichos, and the case of Rabbi Avin fasting on Friday is settled. See Magen Avraham, os 3. What the Tur wrote in siman 249, the Mechaber also wrote. According to what the Rosh wrote, it works well, because the reason is selichos; when Erev Rosh Chodesh falls on Shabbos, it is deferred to Thursday because they increase selichos on Yom Kippur Katan. Therefore one who is lenient like the ruling of Rama has not lost. One should not challenge the Taz from what Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Shibolei HaLeket, that a woman asked because of the burden of travel whether she may fast, but she will not complete the fast. One can say that Shibolei HaLeket holds that even an individual completes a fast on Friday. However, what he wrote, how can one enter Shabbos afflicted, is difficult from Shibolei HaLeket. Also what he wrote from people of deed, one can say that a public fast is different.
