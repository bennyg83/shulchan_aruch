2) A pregnant woman, etc. In my humble opinion, it implies from Hagahos Maimoniyos that they should fast; see there and analyze carefully. So too it is explained in Beis Yosef at the beginning of siman 687 in the name of Orchos Chaim, and so it implies in Maharil in the name of Rabbeinu Shimshon, even regarding a woman after childbirth. Therefore it appears to me to be stringent unless they are very weak. Regarding a woman within thirty days after childbirth, one can possibly be lenient even according to our custom in siman 554, section 5; analyze carefully.
