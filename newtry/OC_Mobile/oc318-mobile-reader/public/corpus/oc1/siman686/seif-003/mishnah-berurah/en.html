(8) 3 days - 5th in the second half of the year after Purim. When Adar enters, the rabbi rejoices, and he who has a dispute with the people will judge on Adar [Gamara] the townspeople can amend by agreement and forbid them and those who come after them to perform Purim on the day on which a miracle is performed, and whoever has a miracle performed on him in Adar and vows to perform Purim when the year of the Ebor comes, he must do Purim on the second Adar if the miracle is not performed for him in the year of the Ebor on the first Adar:
