Three days. Meaning Monday, Thursday, and Monday after Purim, Beis Yosef, siman 429; see there. When Adar enters, we increase joy, and one who has a court case with a gentile should have it judged in Adar. Residents of a city may enact, with agreement and a ban upon themselves and upon those who come after them, to make a Purim on a day when a miracle happened, Maharam Alashkar, 49. See the book Masa Melech, part 7, siman 15. One to whom a miracle happened in Adar and wants to make a Purim: some say that in a leap year he should make it in the second Adar, and some say he should make it in the first Adar, unless the miracle happened in a leap year in the second Adar, Rav Shmuel HaLevi, 16. See what I wrote in siman 568, section 7, and what I wrote there, that it is proven that one should make it in the second Adar.
