3) Levush: Purim money, etc. This is the language of the book Amarkal, written on parchment: there are places where they have the custom to ask for Purim money from Rosh Chodesh, and at the very least they advance it before the reading of the megillah, to advance the shekalim before Haman's shekalim. Alternatively, since they fast, the reward of a fast is charity, and everyone gives equally, as it says, "the rich shall not give more, etc." Although at that time they were all wealthy, one can say that the verse speaks about the future. See siman 694, section 3.
