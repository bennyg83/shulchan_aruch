In Magen Avraham, seif katan 3. See Magen Avraham above, siman 417, seif katan 3, in the name of Tanchuma, and so he also wrote in the name of the Geonim, that the reason is because they were occupied with selichos and would not be able to trouble themselves for the honor of Shabbos. If so, what the Rav wrote in the gloss is well understood, that one may eat at a bris, and on Friday those who ate fast, since the selichos and supplications were on Thursday, so they can properly fast on Friday. The Taz challenged Rama, but in my humble opinion one can say as I wrote, to rely on one time of the Ran. So wrote the Rosh and the Tur in the name of the Geonim.
