Seif 1 on Chanukah and Purim etc. — meaning: even though we say in the first chapter of Rosh Hashanah that the days of Chanukah and Purim did not annul Megillat Taanit, that refers to the days themselves, therefore we fast on the 13th of Adar. See there. We need not say this for Purim since it is divrei kabbalah etc. as written there 19a; and what Megillah 5 says "was not needed" etc. — meaning for eulogy and fasting they are Purim days for everyone, and one might think even for melacha they are for everyone, but because of before and after them not — therefore he asked because of after them; even before, if something occurred and Nicanor's day was annulled as in Taanit 18a, and Mordechai in the name of Rabbati who wrote that from what we include them in Yerushalmi — they said on all we fast except Shabbat, Yom Tov, Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah and Purim — all are equal; so in Razah and Ran Taanit ch. 2; even though Yerushalmi says also before Purim to forbid because of the day before it — Yerushalmi argues with gemara and holds divrei kabbalah are not like Torah law; see Tosafot there 18a s.v. Rav etc. Rif disagrees in the first chapter of Megillah and forbids before and after; nevertheless before Purim he wrote forbidden only for eulogy alone, reason being Fast of Esther; he wrote there: even though R' Yochanan ruled like R' Meir not to eulogize, we do not rule like them since Rav Ashi says there "all that is after it" etc. — he is like R' Yosi, and therefore here in Shulchan Aruch before and after. But Ran in Taanit ch. 2 rules like R' Yochanan. Rif's words in Megillah there require study — he wrote forbidden before and after in eulogy, which is not like Rav Ashi; Yerushalmi challenges on Nicanor's day that it is not itself because it is the day before the 14th and answers to inform you it is forbidden in eulogy; Ran brought it in Taanit there — it appears these are not Rif's words; Ran likewise there.
