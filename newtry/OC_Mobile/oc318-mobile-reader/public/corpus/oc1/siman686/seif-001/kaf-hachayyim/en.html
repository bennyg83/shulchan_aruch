1) [Seif 1] On Chanukah and Purim it is permitted to fast, etc. One might have thought that it is prohibited, because Megillas Taanis states that regarding days on which one may not fast, the Sages rendered the days preceding and following them as Yom Tov as well, and fasting was prohibited then. Although Megillas Taanis was annulled with respect to the other festivals mentioned there, it was not annulled with respect to Chanukah and Purim, and it remains prohibited to fast on those days. Therefore, the ruling teaches that it is nevertheless permitted to fast on the days preceding and following them, because Megillas Taanis was annulled with respect to this matter even for Chanukah and Purim. Turei Zahav, s.k. 1.
2) There: It is permitted to fast on the days preceding them, etc. The same applies to delivering a eulogy on the days preceding and following them; it is permitted. Rambam, chapter 11 of Hilchos Avel; Beis Yosef. See below in siman 596, seif 3, and our comments there, with the help of Heaven.
3) There: It is permitted to fast on the days preceding them, etc. The Bach wrote that fasting on the day preceding Chanukah is prohibited, though a eulogy is permitted; on the day following it, both are permitted. Knesses HaGedolah cites this in Hagahos Beis Yosef, paragraph 1, in this siman, and in Hagahos HaTur at the end of siman 670. This is also the view of the Peri Chadash in this siman and of Shaarei Ephraim, siman 43. Knesses HaGedolah wrote in Hagahos HaTur, siman 670, that even the day following Chanukah is prohibited, and Magen Avraham cites this in this siman, s.k. 1. Eliyah Rabbah, siman 670, paragraph 2, wrote that Knesses HaGedolah retracted that ruling in the latter work. The attempt of Machatzis HaShekel to reconcile the cited words of Knesses HaGedolah was already rejected by Eliyah Rabbah there; see there. Therefore, one should not be stringent regarding the day following Chanukah, especially since this is the view of the Shulchan Aruch and several poskim, who permit fasting even on the day preceding it.
