1) [Seif 1] On Chanukah and Purim it is permitted to fast, etc. One might have thought that it is prohibited, because Megillas Taanis states that regarding days on which one may not fast, the Sages rendered the days preceding and following them as Yom Tov as well, and fasting was prohibited then. Although Megillas Taanis was annulled with respect to the other festivals mentioned there, it was not annulled with respect to Chanukah and Purim, and it remains prohibited to fast on those days. Therefore, the ruling teaches that it is nevertheless permitted to fast on the days preceding and following them, because Megillas Taanis was annulled with respect to this matter even for Chanukah and Purim. Turei Zahav, s.k. 1.
