On Chanukah, etc. The Bach wrote that on Chanukah it is forbidden to fast on the day before it. In truth, there are those who have the custom to fast before Chanukah in place of Erev Rosh Chodesh, and one should not change their custom; but initially one should not practice this way. In any case, it is forbidden to decree a public fast on it, Rav Avraham ben Chaim, siman 5. Even on the day after it, it is forbidden, Keneses HaGedolah.
