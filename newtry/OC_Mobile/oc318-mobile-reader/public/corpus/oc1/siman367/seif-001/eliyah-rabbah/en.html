[1] She does not make an eruv unless, etc. This is astonishing because the Shulchan Aruch's wording is the reverse: a wife may make an eruv only without her husband's knowledge. Perhaps the Levush contains a scribal error and should read, 'she makes an eruv only without his knowledge,' because we assume he is ordinarily pleased. This appears correct and is supported by the Levush's later statement that in the other case she cannot make an eruv.
