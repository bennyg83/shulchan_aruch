(1) [Seif 1] A man's wife may make an eruv for him without his knowledge, etc. — and the general rule of this siman is explained at length in Taz; and see there at length, and in Mishna Berurah, and in Biur Halacha at length; and for halacha, when he is accustomed to forbid, courtyard residents take by force even without the wife's knowledge; and when not accustomed to forbid but accustomed to participate, one requires the wife's knowledge; and when neither forbids nor is accustomed to participate, one requires his knowledge; and when he protests explicitly, one may not make an eruv for him at all, neither his wife nor courtyard residents nor members of the household.
