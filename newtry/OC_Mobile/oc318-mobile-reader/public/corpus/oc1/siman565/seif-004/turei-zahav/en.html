And he is found to be a liar in his prayer. And even though regarding a shaliach tzibbur the Tur wrote in siman 566 that even if he is not fasting he should say Aneinu, since others are fasting and he is not a liar: a shaliach tzibbur is different, for he is the emissary of the entire congregation, and he properly prays on their behalf regarding what they are fasting; which is not so for an individual, who should say only what he shares with them, as it appears to me.
