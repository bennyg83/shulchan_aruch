He says Aneinu, etc. And the practice is to say "Aneinu on the day of my fasting," as is written in the Agudah (Bach). And this requires study, for the Rama wrote "Aneinu on the day of the fast of our fasting," and so he wrote in Darkei Moshe: he may say "our fasting," that is to say, when they will fast, just as he may say "Aneinu," that is, when they will pray, as mentioned above; and so was the practice, end quote. And it appears to me that one should practice like the Bach; and so it is implied in Hagahos Minhagim.
