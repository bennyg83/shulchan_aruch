Whether an individual praying with the congregation, etc. The Bach wrote that an individual who is not fasting on a communal fast and prays with the congregation may recite Aneinu in Shomea Tefillah, saying, "on this communal fast day"; see there. Ateres Zekeinim cited his words. So too wrote Chayei Adam there. However, Maamar Mordechai, os 1, wrote that although he does not say "on my fast day," it nevertheless appears false, since the prayer he is reciting is on his own behalf. He expressed astonishment at the aforementioned words of the Bach; see there.
