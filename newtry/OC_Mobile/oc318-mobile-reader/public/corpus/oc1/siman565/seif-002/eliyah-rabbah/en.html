[Levush:] When he reaches "Yehiyu," etc. See siman 114, s.k. 9: even if he remembered before he began Retzeh, he should not say it there, but rather when he reaches "Yehiyu," etc. And if he forgot and uprooted his feet, it is good that he intend to follow the shaliach tzibbur word for word and thus fulfill his obligation (Maharil).
