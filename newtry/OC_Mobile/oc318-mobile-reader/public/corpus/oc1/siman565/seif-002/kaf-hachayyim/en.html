Meaning, he does not establish it as an independent blessing between Goel Yisrael and Refa'einu, as does the shaliach tzibbur, but includes it within Shomea Tefillah, as stated below in the Rama's gloss. If he erred and established it as an independent blessing, reciting it between Goel Yisrael and Refa'einu, he certainly should not repeat it in Shomea Tefillah, since he has already fulfilled his obligation.
