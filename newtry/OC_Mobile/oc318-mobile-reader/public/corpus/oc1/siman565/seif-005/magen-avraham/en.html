In the manner of reading. With melody and trop (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 18); and see siman 59, se'if 3. When he says "Vayikra beshem Hashem," he should pause a little between "shem" and Hashem (Darkei Moshe; Abudraham); see what I wrote in siman 61, se'if 9. One should not say "HaAderes veHaEmunah" in a congregation except on Yom Kippur [Darkei Moshe; Maharil]. One should never say any Selichos, nor the Thirteen Attributes, before midnight in any way, except on Yom Kippur (Kavanos, folio 5).
To say Selichos, etc. And so it is in the Hagahos Ashri at the end of Taanis. The Bach wondered about this: why should an individual not say Selichos; see there. And it appears to me that he should not say them in the middle of the eighteen blessings; see what I wrote at the end of siman 68.
