Between "shem" and Hashem. And this is the language of Abudraham: one must pause between "shem" and Hashem, for in the verse it has a tipcha (that is to say, under the word "beshem" a tipcha is written, and it is a pause in the phrase); and most of the masses err in this, end quote. However, Onkelos translated "and he called in the Name of Hashem," and Rashi also brought this. And according to this, its meaning is that Moshe called in the Name of Hashem, and if so one should join the word "beshem" to Hashem. But according to Abudraham, the meaning of the verse is "and he called by Name"; and who was the one who called? Hashem. And see there in the Re'em.
