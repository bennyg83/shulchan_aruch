Whether an individual praying with the congregation, etc. One who fasted a private fast and served as shaliach tzibbur should not recite Aneinu in Shomea Tefillah during the repetition, because when the congregation is not fasting, how can he recite Aneinu? If, however, he came to the synagogue and found that the congregation had already prayed silently, and he must immediately go before the lectern, he descends before the lectern and prays aloud for the congregation. He then recites Aneinu, since he is not first praying silently and then repeating the prayer. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos Beit Yosef, os 2. Shalmei Tzibbur, 175a. Yafeh LaLev, volume 2, os 1. Yafeh LaLev wrote there that if he does not wish to publicize his fast, see seif 6, he should recite it privately after completing his prayer, in Elokai Netzor, without a concluding blessing, in accordance with the law of one who forgot, written in seif 2, and the law of one who fasts a dream fast on Shabbos, written above in siman 288, seif 6; see there. His father, the author of Ruach Chaim, os 1, cited his words; see there. However, Chayei Adam, klal 132, os 33, wrote that he should recite Aneinu only after completing his prayer; see there.
