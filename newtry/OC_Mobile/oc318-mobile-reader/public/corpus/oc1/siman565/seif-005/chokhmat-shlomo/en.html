Se'if 5. An individual may not say the Thirteen Attributes, etc. Marginal note: See in the Rosh, first chapter of Rosh Hashanah, what he explained regarding the statement of Chazal "I am Hashem," in the second answer, that it refers to the thought of idolatry. Now, regarding how his words contradict the words of the Gemara in Kiddushin in three places, see in my work on Orach Chaim of the year 1852, folio 80b, in my responsum to the holy community of Botoshan; its place was acquired in the laws of Rosh Hashanah, siman 650; there I resolved the words of the Rosh properly, with the help of Hashem; see there incidentally, and study carefully.
