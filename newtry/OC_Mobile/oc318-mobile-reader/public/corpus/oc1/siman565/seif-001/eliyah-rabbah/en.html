[1] Aneinu, etc. I found in Kol Bo, page 86, that the prayer Aneinu contains sixty-three words, corresponding to the sixty-three letters in the verse, “Yaakov was greatly afraid.” Agudah writes that this corresponds to the sixty-three letters in the names of the three Patriarchs and the twelve tribes. I believe this also explains what I found in my compilation on Tehillim 53, regarding the verse “They have all turned aside together.” In the first chapter it says “turned aside,” whereas here it uses a word whose numerical value is sixty-three, corresponding to the sixty-three nations that came with Titus against Jerusalem. It appears to me that this is also alluded to in the sixty-three letters of “Yaakov was greatly afraid,” for Titus was a descendant of the wicked Esav. Thus the names of the tribes contain sixty-three letters, and Aneinu contains sixty-three words, to protect us from these nations.
