1) [Seif 1] An individual recites Aneinu, etc. I found in Kol Bo that Aneinu contains 63 words, corresponding to the 63 letters in the verse, "Yaakov was greatly afraid, etc." In Agudah it is written that they correspond to the 63 letters in the names of the three Patriarchs and the 12 tribes. Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. One can further say that they correspond to the Divine Name of numerical value 63, which relates to the aspect of Binah, and Binah is associated with Gevurah, as it is written, "I am Binah, Gevurah is mine." Therefore, 63 words corresponding to it are recited in the prayer of Aneinu on a fast day, in order to sweeten the severities that are within the aspect of Binah. However, the text in the Sephardic siddur does not contain 63 words. Nevertheless, one who wishes to add two words relevant to the blessing so that there will be 63 may do so, as explained in the responsa of the Rashba, siman 25.
