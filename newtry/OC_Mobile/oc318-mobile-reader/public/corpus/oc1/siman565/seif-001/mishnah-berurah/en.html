(1) He says Aneinu in Shomea Tefillah. That is to say, he does not establish a blessing for himself between Go'el and Rofei like the shaliach tzibbur; rather he includes it in Shomea Tefillah, as below in the gloss.
(2) Whether an individual who prays with the congregation. For only for the shaliach tzibbur did they establish a blessing for himself between Go'el and Rofei, and not for individuals.
(3) And he should not close, etc. And even a shaliach tzibbur, if he forgot to say Aneinu between Go'el and Rofei, so that he says it in Shomea Tefillah, likewise should not close there with a blessing on its own; rather he concludes "for You hear" like an individual.
(4) He should say Aneinu, etc. For a person should always associate himself with the congregation.
(5) On the fast of our fasting. And it does not appear as a falsehood that he says it in the plural, for it is impossible that there not be someone at the end of the world who is fasting today [Magen Avraham]. And there are later authorities who lean toward saying "on the day of the fast of my fasting" or "on the day of the fast"; and the world has practiced like the Rama, and so Derech HaChaim copied.
