At Minchah. The Magen Avraham wrote, and it appears to me that even if he prays Minchah Gedolah he should say Aneinu, for even if bulmus seizes him he will not have been a liar in his prayer, since in any case he fasted until midday; see there. And the Taz wrote: and it appears that if he wishes to pray Aneinu at Shacharis and skip "on the day of the fast of our fasting," he has permission to do so, for then there is no concern that he will be found a liar; and so it is fitting to do; see there.
Shacharis. See siman 557; I wrote in the name of the Taz that one should not say it even regarding the shaliach tzibbur at Shacharis on Tishah B'Av, for the reason that it is called a moed; see there.
