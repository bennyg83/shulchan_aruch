Se'if 3: Some say, etc. Rashi there.
But a shaliach tzibbur, etc. Tosafos there; and they wrote that it is impossible that one of the congregation not fast. And even though in siman 567, se'if 3, a shaliach tzibbur does not, etc., this case is different, for it is only a mere concern, and also it is only that it appears as a falsehood; for at the time of the prayer their intent is to fast, as Tosafos in Shabbos raised a difficulty from what is said, "Let the master lend and repay." And in the Semak they raised a difficulty from what is said, if they descended before midday they need not complete the fast, even though they prayed the prayer of a fast. Know this, for there in se'if 5 it is written regarding a communal fast that a shaliach tzibbur, etc., and all the more so one should not say Aneinu on behalf of the congregation.
In all your prayers. Shabbos there: Arvis and Shacharis and Minchah, etc. And the Tosefta that Rashi brought there: at times, etc. And the Yerushalmi, chapter 2, halachah 2: even an individual who decreed a fast upon himself must mention the matter of the occasion; and where does he say it, etc., like the night of Shabbos and its day. And what Rav Huna said, even if he ate and drank all night, the next day he prays the prayer of a fast: it teaches us something novel, and all the more so at night when he has not yet eaten. And the Ran wrote: and therefore we require acceptance while it is still day, and it does not suffice at night before the start of the fast, for the fast begins from the evening; and therefore whenever he finished his meal or slept, it is forbidden to eat. And this is what is meant by "and there is no mention of them in Birkas Hamazon," and therefore it is said regarding the night. And see in the Ran.
