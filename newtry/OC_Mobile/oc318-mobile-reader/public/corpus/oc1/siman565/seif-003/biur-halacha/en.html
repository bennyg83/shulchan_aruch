(א) When he prays loud, but in the fall of his whisper, he will not tell us that he will not speak to us when he hears a single one:

(ב) It is not possible that none of the congregation will fast somewhat — see below siman 566 seif 3, that specifically ten must fast; but here it deals with when there are ten whose intention is to fast, and only out of concern that perhaps they will not later be able to fast we say that since in any case a few will remain, even in the end it does not appear as if he is lying when he leads prayer; but if he knows that at the time of prayer there are not ten whose intention is to fast, then he is not the emissary of the congregation for the matter of the fast prayer (Mordechai).
