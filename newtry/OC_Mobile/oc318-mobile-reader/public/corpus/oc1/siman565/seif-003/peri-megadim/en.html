Lest. See the Taz; and he concludes: if he skips the two words "on the fast of our fasting," an individual may say it even at Shacharis and is not a liar, since he skips them. And see siman 562 regarding Aneinu on a private fast when he did not accept it or when the sun had not set: he likewise skips those two words, and he may do so; for if he forgot to say Aneinu, there is a version that he says in Elokai Netzor with those two words skipped. However, we say "on the fast of our fasting" if he remembers in Elokai Netzor; and God willing, more will be explained in Magen Avraham.
