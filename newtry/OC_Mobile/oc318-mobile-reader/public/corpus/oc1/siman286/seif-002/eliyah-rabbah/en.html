(ב) The word 'seder,' etc. And in Tanya the word 'birur' as in 'and the content of hearts.' Until here one may say 'and the Musaf,' etc., and likewise in every Musaf (Rashal, s.v.). That which they say in Shacharit 'and You did not give' and 'and You did not bequeath' is masculine language because it refers to what he concluded 'and on the seventh day'; and 'day' is masculine.
