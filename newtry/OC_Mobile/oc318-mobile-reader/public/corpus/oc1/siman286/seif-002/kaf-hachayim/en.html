(2) He wrote in Shelah: 'Tikanta' is language of seder as in 'and the content of hearts.' 'You desired its libations' — meaning in all the offerings it is written 'and a goat for atonement,' but in the Shabbat offering it is not written 'goat' — therefore they arranged to say 'You desired its libations,' which are the offerings and do not require atonement. And some explain 'You desired its libations' — not 'You desired its offering' unless Shabbat passed over it, as it is written 'and from the eighth day onward it will be accepted,' and there is no seven without Shabbat.
