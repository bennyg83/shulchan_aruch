(4) One whose heart is weak and wishes to taste something on Shabbat morning in order to eat with a good and pleasant heart — that he pray in his house with a minyan of ten, Shacharit and Musaf one after another, and sanctify and eat his meal, and afterward go to the synagogue to hear the Torah and haftarah reading; and there is no concern that he prays Musaf before the Torah, as the Gaon wrote in Chakkei Lev part 1 siman 5, see there — for whatever can be combined, Shacharit and Musaf one after another, it is fine, as explained from here that one may pray Musaf immediately after Shacharit.
