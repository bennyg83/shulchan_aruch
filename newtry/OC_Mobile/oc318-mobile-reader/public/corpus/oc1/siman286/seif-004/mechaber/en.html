If two prayer services are before him, one Minchah and one Mussaf, for example he delayed Mussaf until 6 and a half seasonal hours, which is the time for Minchah, he must pray Minchah first and afterward Mussaf. {Rama: If, however, he prayed Mussaf first, he has fulfilled his obligation. [Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba]} And some say that this applies specifically when he presently needs to pray both, for example because he wishes to eat and may not eat until he has prayed Minchah; but if he does not presently need to pray Minchah, he may pray Mussaf first. {Rama: When the time of Minchah Ketanah has arrived, however, he should pray Minchah first. [Rosh in the name of the Yerushalmi]} And there is an authority who ruled that in a congregation one should not pray Minchah before Mussaf, lest people err.
