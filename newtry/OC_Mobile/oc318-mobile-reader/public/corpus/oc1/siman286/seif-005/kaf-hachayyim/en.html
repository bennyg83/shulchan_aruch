5) If a person is about to pray Mussaf with the congregation and is called to perform a bris milah, but having the congregation wait for him would burden them, he should not postpone the communal prayer for the bris unless there is concern that the bris will otherwise be delayed beyond its proper time. Mahari Ayash in Beis Yehudah, Orach Chaim, siman 48; Rav Yaakov Castro in Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 3; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 8; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 5. Shaarei Teshuvah writes there that when the bris is performed in the synagogue and many people are waiting until afterward, the mohel should not pray Mussaf with the congregation and make them wait for him to finish, because of the burden upon the congregation. It is not respectful to have them sit and wait for him until he completes the communal prayer.
