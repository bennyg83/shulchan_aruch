Eating fruit - That is, after kiddush. See Mishnah Berurah, that if he has nothing from the five species to eat after kiddush, he should at least see to drink the entire cup of wine. Even for a kohen who must lift his hands in Mussaf, it is also permitted to drink a revi'it of our wine, for it certainly also contains water. See above in siman 128 seif 38 what we wrote there in Mishnah Berurah regarding spirits.
