In the writings of the Arizal, etc. ‘Hashem is One; He is our God.’ This means that in Naaritzcha, where the text in the siddurim reads, ‘Shema Yisrael, etc., Hashem is One; One is our God,’ they wrote that one should not say the word ‘One’ twice. The reason is that stated above in siman 61, seif 9: it is prohibited to recite Shema twice, whether one repeats the words, etc., because it appears as though there were, God forbid, two authorities.
Nevertheless, our custom is to say ‘One’ twice. This is not comparable to the case in siman 61, for the first ‘One’ refers to what precedes it, whereas the final ‘One’ refers to what follows it.
And the Mussaf offering, etc. With the conjunction ‘and.’ Maharshal concludes: ‘For it refers back to what precedes it.’ This concludes his words.
