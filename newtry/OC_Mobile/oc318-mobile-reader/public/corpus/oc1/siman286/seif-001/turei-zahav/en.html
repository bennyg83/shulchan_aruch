In the Tur it is written that in Spain they customarily say the Mishnah of the first chapter, and in siman 294 he wrote that in Spain they say Pirkei Avot on Shabbat morning. If so, why did he not mention this matter here? I heard some emend here, instead of "chapter 1," to "Pirkei Avot," meaning it was written as the abbreviation פ"א, which meant Pirkei Avot, and the copyist erred and wrote "chapter 1."
(1) Before the morning prayer he has fulfilled. Because regarding Mussaf it says "in the morning," but in tractate Yoma it is derived from an extra verse that the libations precede the Mussaf offerings, as the Tur wrote.
