(*) In Shulchan Aruch seif 2: time. The time of Musaf prayer. See Magen Avraham siman 233: if one interrupts with selling mitzvot, he must wash his hands again for Musaf, unless he knows he did not divert his attention. See Sha'arei Ephraim, gate 10, seif 47.
(*) In the gloss, seif 2: the shaliach tzibbur repeats. Those accustomed to say Kedushat Keter on Shabbat should say "with Your people Israel"; on Rosh Chodesh they should say "and Your people Israel." So writes Gan HaMelekh siman 134 in the name of the pious Rabbi David Gershon. See what Birkei Yosef and Machazik Berakhah wrote on this. The Shaliach Tzibbur writes that one must say "temidim ke-sidran," with the word temidim ending in mem; what is printed with a final nun is a mistake.
(1) To taste. See Ba'er Heitev. See Eliyah Rabbah: if one has no wine or bread, only fruit, and he feels weak, he may taste before Kiddush and before Musaf. But if he is not weak, one should not be lenient.
