(1) (s.k. 15) That every, etc. — and likewise, etc., when it is a night of immersion, etc. — meaning on Shabbat night it is a night of immersion and she must wash her whole body before immersion so nothing interposes; and washing must be from while it is still day, especially on Erev Shabbat when she cannot wash at night which is Shabbat; and the custom is to wash in the mikveh close to immersion as Shach wrote Yoreh Deah siman 199 s.k. 7; and immersion must be at night literally — and how does she manage with candle lighting: if she kindles before washing, in any case she must kindle after plag hamincha, which is an hour and a quarter before tzeit hakochavim; and if she blesses immediately, behold she accepted Shabbat and cannot wash again; and even if she stipulates that she does not accept Shabbat via kindling — where there is need one may rely on the stipulation as Rama wrote — nevertheless the matter is difficult, for she must narrow the time, for kindling must be after plag hamincha and washing takes time and must be while still day before Shabbat enters; and if she waits to kindle until after immersion, behold it is already Shabbat and she may not kindle; and to go home between washing and immersion while still day and kindle — she fears she will soil herself and there will be an interposing substance on her at immersion:

(2) What he wrote — kindling on Yom Kippur and further that they kindle in the house, etc. — so it should read. And meaning two proofs that even though she does not eat by the candles she is permitted to bless: one, for they bless on Yom Kippur even though eating is forbidden; and further, that they kindle in the house and eat in the courtyard — nevertheless they bless even though they do not eat by the candles; and even though in synagogue it is worse, for without candles the woman — candles burn in synagogue — therefore Magen Avraham began and wrote "and likewise what women practice," etc. — meaning as when two or three eat in one place where the custom is that only one blesses, for all additional light brings extra joy — likewise one can say in synagogue:

(3) And you asked there — one uses, etc. — meaning with this to reject proof 2 from that they kindle in the house and eat in the courtyard, etc. Perhaps he considers it a meal need. Meaning in such a case perhaps one is permitted to bless also on candles designated for synagogue even though they were kindled for honor — nevertheless they were also kindled for this, that the sun be seen how wine is drawn from the barrels.
