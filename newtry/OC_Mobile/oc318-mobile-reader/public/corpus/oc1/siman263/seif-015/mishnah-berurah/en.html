They accepted, etc. Meaning in saying Barechu or by reciting Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbos, everywhere according to local custom; and as above in siman 261 se’if 4; see there.
He should not pray, etc. Meaning even while it is still broad daylight; and because after the congregation sanctified the day they should not treat it afterward as weekday time among them.
But if he answered. Meaning he answered Barechu and personally sanctified the day through answering Barechu; and how can he treat it afterward as weekday time afterward; and therefore he has no corrective remedy.
Two. Meaning the first prayer for Shabbos and the second prayer for compensation for Minchah; and if he reversed, possibly he has fulfilled his obligation, since it is still day and time of minchah; so wrote Peri Megadim in this siman; and see above in Peri Megadim in Mishbetzos Zahav, siman 108 se’if katan 8.
