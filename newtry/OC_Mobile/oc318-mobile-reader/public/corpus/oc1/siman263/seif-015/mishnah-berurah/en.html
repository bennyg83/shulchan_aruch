(58) They accepted, etc. — meaning in saying Barechu or in mizmor shir leyom hashabbat, everywhere per his custom; and as above in siman 261 seif 4 — see there:

(59) He should not pray, etc. — meaning even while the day is still great; and because after the congregation sanctified the day they should not make it profane among them:

(60) But if he answered — meaning he answered Barechu and made it holy himself through answering Barechu; and how can he make it profane afterward; and therefore he has no remedy:

(61) Two — meaning initially for Shabbat and afterward a second for makeup of minchah; and if he reversed, possibly he fulfills, since it is still day and time of minchah; so wrote Peri Megadim in this siman; and see above in Peri Megadim in Magen Avraham siman 108 s.k. 8:
