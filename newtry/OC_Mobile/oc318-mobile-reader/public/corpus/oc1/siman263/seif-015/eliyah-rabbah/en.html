(15) In rooms, etc. Two wicks (Dinei Mahariu) because of shalom bayit so he not stumble on a tree or stone or anything (Mordekhai and Kolbo); it implies that one does not eat there; and likewise it implies in responsa Maharil siman 255; and from this Mateh Moshe learned in the name of Rashal: one who has several rooms and goes from the house to a room — he needs one candle in every place where there is concern lest he stumble there — see there; and likewise it implies from what Levush and Shulchan Aruch wrote at the end "one need not kindle in rooms and bless," etc. — and this is from Mordekhai's language; it implies specifically to bless he need not, since he is with his wife; but he must kindle; however I saw in Kolbo and Tashbetz they wrote only "one need not kindle" — it implies he need not even kindle, since he is with his wife who kindles in the house where he eats; and what Mordekhai wrote "and bless" — he meant to say even if he wishes to be stringent and kindle, he should not bless:
