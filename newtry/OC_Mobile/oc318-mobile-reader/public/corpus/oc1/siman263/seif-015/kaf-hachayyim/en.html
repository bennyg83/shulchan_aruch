15) [Seif 2] This applies equally to men and women, etc. Every person who eats the evening meal is obligated to eat it by lamplight, because this is included in the delight of Shabbos. The principal mitzvah of kindling Shabbos lamps is the lamp by whose light one eats. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1. See below, seif 10 in the gloss.
