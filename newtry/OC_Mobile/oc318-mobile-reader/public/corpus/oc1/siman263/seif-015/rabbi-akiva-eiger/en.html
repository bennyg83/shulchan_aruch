Seif 10. They throw them to the ground. This implies that it is forbidden for him to go with the lamp to another place in order to set it down. See below, siman 267, Magen Avraham, seif katan 19, and Even HaOzer there. Afterward I found that the Gaon Rabbi Eliyahu of Vilna addressed this in siman 266, seif katan 16.
Seif 15. Rather, he should pray Maariv twice. It requires study whether in such a case he prays Mincha first, since it is still day. Mishbetzos Zahav, siman 108, seif katan 8. Here in the book Eshel Avraham, seif katan 28, he wrote that it implies he prays Maariv first, and if he reversed the order, perhaps he fulfilled his obligation, since it is still day.
