And when she gives birth on the first Shabbat, the husband kindles and blesses. And during her days of niddah the woman blesses herself [Acharonim]:
(12) Because they are at home — and another reason: because extinguishing his candle caused death to Adam harishon. Nevertheless it is good for the man to prepare the candles [Kesavim]:
(13) Shabbat candle precedes — and even though Kiddush is d'oraisa, nevertheless on wine is d'rabbanan, for from the Torah one fulfills via prayer, as written in siman 271; and also that he can make Kiddush on bread:
(14) Because of shalom bayit — and if he has one candle it suffices and he spends the remainder on Kiddush or a Chanukah candle. And for Chanukah too one candle suffices. And if he has one candle for Shabbat and one for Chanukah and has a little more money, it appears to me better to buy more Chanukah candles to be among the mehadrin, as I explained in Biur Halacha.
