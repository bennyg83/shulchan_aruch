(Seif 4 in Hagahah) The candle burns from beforehand — meaning it was burning for another purpose; since it was not kindled for Shabbat he must return and extinguish and return and kindle for Shabbat need; but if the kindling was for Shabbat need but he kindled while day is still great he need not extinguish it, only l'chatchila he should not kindle so early it is not recognizable they kindle for honor of Shabbat; but b'dieved permitted; and what Tosafot wrote as the braita taught "provided he does not precede" — meaning from this it implies the mitzvah is not only that he have on Shabbat a burning candle but the mitzvah of kindling itself for Shabbat need; and l'chatchila proper it be recognizable the kindling is for honor of Shabbat; and per this everything depends whether the kindling is for Shabbat need or not; and Rama's language "while day is still great" is not precise; and in Tosafot and Tur they did not mention the word "great":
