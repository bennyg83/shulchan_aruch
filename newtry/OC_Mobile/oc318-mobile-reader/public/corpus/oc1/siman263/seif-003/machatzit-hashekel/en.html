(1) (s.k. 3) Chapter 1, etc.; because of recognition, etc.; and what is the reason; it appears to me that if one was compelled, etc.; meaning that without Bach's words it would occur that the reason one needs to add a light is for atonement; if so, also when compelled one needs some atonement; or it is a mistaken view that the extra light is for compensation for what she forgot; if so, likewise when compelled; unlike Bach's reason that she be careful henceforth; when compelled she need not add, for otherwise she was careful, only the compulsion caused her not to light:
(2) And if she forgot several times, etc.; and the same applies if originally her custom was to light three lights each time; if she forgot she must add one light and light four each time, and so forever. And he wrote in Avnei Rozal that a poor woman who forgot may be lenient that she need not add another light and it suffices that she add a little oil each time; and if she is accustomed to light a wax light and the like, it suffices for her; if she was accustomed to a small light, now she lights one slightly larger:
