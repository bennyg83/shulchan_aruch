(1) Be careful, etc. — here the essence of kindling the candle is an obligation for the mitzvah of oneg Shabbat; and because of hiddur mitzvah one should make it beautiful; and see below siman 264 seif 6.
(2) Beautiful — and it is stated in the Gemara that through this one merits sons who are Torah scholars, as it is written "For a mitzvah is a candle and Torah is light" — through the mitzvah candle of Shabbat comes the light of Torah; and therefore it is fit that the woman pray after she finishes kindling and the blessing that Heaven give her sons, males, illuminating in Torah; and kindling the candle must be in all rooms where one walks on Shabbat — at least one candle, even if one does not eat there, so that one not stumble on a tree or stone; however the blessing one blesses on the candle in the place of eating.
(3) To make — and if his money is limited, it appears better to buy one beautiful one and not two inferior ones.
(4) Two wicks — this is when he burns oil in a lamp; and if his kindling is with wax candles he makes two candles. And it is permitted to kindle from candle to candle, whether kindling two or more — and this is not because of bizuy mitzvah, for all are mitzvah candles; but it is forbidden to kindle a splinter or a weekday candle from a Shabbat candle because of bizuy mitzvah. And they practice to be stringent on this even if his intent is to kindle another Shabbat candle with it; and see in Biur Halachah.
(5) One corresponding to, etc. — and there are those who intend to make a wax candle from two candles twisted together, in memory of what the Sages said that "remember" and "keep" were said in one utterance; and this is a proper custom; but those men who stick them at kindling time adjacent to one another not properly — they act improperly, for in the end they heat one another and wax drips thereby; and also they stick together and fall. It is forbidden to kindle Shabbat candles from wax that comes from the house of prayer of Samaritans — even in a manner permitted for an ordinary person, such as when the Samaritans were nullified — nevertheless for a mitzvah it is forbidden because it is repulsive [above siman 154 seif 11]; and see in Peri Megadim who wrote that if he has no other candle except this one — it is permitted; and nevertheless he should not bless on it; and see above siman 154 seif 11 and in Mishnah Berurah there.
(6) To add — and there are those accustomed to kindle seven candles corresponding to the seven days of the week; and there are ten corresponding to the Ten Commandments; and they need not all be on the table.
(7) Three candles — this speaks of when she was accustomed to two; and if she was accustomed from the outset to three — she must kindle four all her days; and if she forgot several times she must add each time another candle; and all this is because of a penalty so that she be careful regarding the honor of Shabbat; and therefore if she was compelled and did not kindle — such as when she was in prison and the like — she need not add. The Avnei Nezer wrote that for a poor woman one may be lenient when she forgets — that she add each day a little oil in the candle; and if she kindles candles she always kindles one candle slightly longer than at the beginning.
(8) A directed matter — meaning two candles directed corresponding to "remember" and "keep," as above.
