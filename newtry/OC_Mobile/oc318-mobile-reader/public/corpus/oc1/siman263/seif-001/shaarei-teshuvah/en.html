A fine candle. See Baer Heitev. Responsa Beis Yaakov writes that some are accustomed to kindle two candles on the bimah before accepting Shabbos, and they honor another person with the kindling. Afterward, the shamash moves them to the place before the amud where the congregation prays. He writes that this practice should be protested, because even within the same building one should not move the candles from one place to another, as written in the Rama’s gloss to se’if 10. This applies even to synagogue candles; see there. See also the lengthy discussion in Beit Yosef, siman 675.
