Regarding Taz — and it appears puzzling, for this is regarding those who kindled they are forbidden and the rest of the household are permitted; and Avnei Rozal letter 31 wondered about this; and I say perhaps one can reconcile his words, for Avnei Rozal letter 29 in the name of Taz questioned that prayer is stringent and a stipulation does not help as in acceptance, and in kindling a stipulation helps; and one could say Avnei Rozal regarding mistake the opposite — that an act by mistake does not revert; and nevertheless from here those who kindled are forbidden and the rest permitted even though they are the minority — it is possible they did not kindle, they are not dragged after the majority who kindled; one could say after acceptance by mistake the minority is not dragged after the majority as Magen Avraham wrote letter 26; and nevertheless Peri Megadim Eshel Avraham holds that a woman who kindles is forbidden and the rest of the household permitted — even regarding a woman who kindles she is the head of the house and they are always dragged after her opinion, nevertheless they were lenient regarding kindling; and behold in prayer siman 12 the minority is dragged after the majority and it implies the same regarding the head of the house; and if so we see prayer is more stringent than kindling and still requires study.
