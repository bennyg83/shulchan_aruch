(Magen Avraham s.k. 4) It teaches us that where he has none per his view one could say — for Ran in Shabbat questioned how we push aside Kiddush d'oraisa for ner beito and ner Chanukah; and one could say he sanctifies on bread except mitzvah min hamuvchar on wine on Erev Shabbat; and Rosh holds no chidush since he can fulfill Kiddush on bread and ner beito; but on this Rosh also like Ran that we do not push aside d'oraisa for ner beito; and therefore Rosh holds if he has no bread certainly better to buy bread, for per Rosh per his view Kiddush is only d'oraisa in place of a meal — if so when he has bread and sanctifies without wine he fulfills d'oraisa, only d'rabbanan he does not fulfill; and with this ner beito is preferable — Rosh challenged if so when they sanctify on bread he would also fulfill d'rabbanan — if so he does not fulfill d'rabbanan only with wine; and it teaches us ner beito is preferable to buying wine for Kiddush — understand well.
