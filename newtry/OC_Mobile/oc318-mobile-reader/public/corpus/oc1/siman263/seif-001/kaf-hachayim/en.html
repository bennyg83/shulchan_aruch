(1) [Seif 1] He should be careful to make a beautiful candle. For R' Huna said: one who is regular with the Shabbat candle and strives to make it beautiful — he will have sons who are Torah scholars — Shabbat 23b. Tur. And it appears to me the reason is because Shabbat is in the aspect of chochmah; and further per the verse "For a mitzvah is a lamp and Torah is light" — therefore one careful with a mitzvah candle will have Torah light; and so Rashi z"l explained.
