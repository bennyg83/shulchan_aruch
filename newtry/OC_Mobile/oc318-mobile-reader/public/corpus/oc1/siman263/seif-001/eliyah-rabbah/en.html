(1) [Levush] Sons who are Torah scholars, etc. Therefore a woman should pray at the time of kindling for sons who are masters of Torah; and it is good that she read at this time the haftarah from Rosh Hashanah of Channah until "and He will exalt the horn of His anointed" (Shelah). One does not kindle with an old earthenware lamp on one's table — responsa Rashal siman 86; and it is good for a God-fearing woman not to kindle in a kansa, only she should have a special wax candle to kindle each Shabbat on Shabbat (Panach Raza):
