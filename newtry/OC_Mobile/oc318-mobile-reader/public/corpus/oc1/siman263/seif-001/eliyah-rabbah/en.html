[Levush] Children who are Torah scholars, etc. Therefore a woman should pray at candle lighting for sons who are masters of Torah. It is good for her then to read the Haftarah of Channah from Rosh Hashanah through "and He will raise the horn of His anointed," as Shelah writes. One should not light on one's table with an old earthenware lamp, according to Responsa Maharshal, siman 86. It is good for a God-fearing woman not to light with a wood splinter, but to have a dedicated wax candle with which she lights every Shabbos.
Remember, etc. Therefore some make intertwined candles, corresponding to "remember" and "guard," which were spoken in a single utterance. Others say they correspond to man and woman, because the numerical value of ner reflects the limbs of man and woman together, the woman having four additional anatomical structures. Another reason is an allusion to the two souls bound together in the human body, as Mateh Moshe writes. See below, siman 671.
To add, etc. Some have the practice to light seven candles, and others ten. These candles need not all stand on the table where one eats, as Shelah, page 139, writes.
