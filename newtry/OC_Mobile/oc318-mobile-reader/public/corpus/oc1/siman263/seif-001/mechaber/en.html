Who lights the Shabbos lamps and the law when one erred on a cloudy day. It contains 17 seifim: One should be careful to prepare a fine lamp. Some intend to use 2 wicks, 1 corresponding to Zachor and 1 corresponding to Shamor. {Rama: One may add and kindle 3 or 4 lamps, and this is the custom. A woman who once forgot to kindle lights 3 lamps for the rest of her life. [Maharil] One may add to a number that corresponds to another concept, provided that one does not reduce it. [Ashiri and Mordechai, Rosh Hashana, beginning of chapter Yom Tov]}
