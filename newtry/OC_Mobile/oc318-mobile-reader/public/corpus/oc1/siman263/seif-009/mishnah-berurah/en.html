(39) And they eat, etc. — see below end of siman 573 in Magen Avraham s.k. 15 that it is not permitted to eat in the courtyard only specifically when greatly distressed in his house because of flies and the like; for otherwise he must eat specifically where the candle is; and see in Mishna Berurah what we wrote there:

(40) In the courtyard — it implies if he eats in the house even if they do not burn until night it suffices and it is not a blessing in vain, for though still day he has benefit and joy at the time of eating from the candles; and it is the best practice to make long candles that burn until night even if he wishes to eat while still day:

(41) It is a blessing in vain — for since he cannot make any use by them when he returns afterward to his house at night lest he stumble on a tree or stone — there is no shalom bayit; and if in the house where he kindled there was some darkness and he uses there something in the light of the candles for the need of the meal — there is no prohibition even if not burning until night, and even if he eats in the courtyard:
