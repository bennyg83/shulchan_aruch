(9) There Hagahah. And one may add, etc. And some are accustomed to kindle seven candles and some say ten. Shelah. Magen Avraham s.k. 2. And pious ones and men of deed are accustomed to kindle specifically seven, which are against the seven ascents; and also because they are against the seven days of the week; and further because Shabbat is in the aspect of Malchut which is the seventh of the sefirot of building, as is known — therefore they kindle seven as a hint to the mentioned aspect; and on Yom Kippur they are accustomed to kindle six, and on Yom Tov five, and on Rosh Chodesh four — against the number of ascents that have meaning in sod, as written above siman 335 or part 19 — see there; and likewise Petaḥ HaDevarim end of or part 1, Chasdei David or part 1:
