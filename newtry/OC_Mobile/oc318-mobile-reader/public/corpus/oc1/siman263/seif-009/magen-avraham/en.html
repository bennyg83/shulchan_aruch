And they eat in the courtyard. This implies that if one eats in the house, it is permitted even if the candles do not remain lit until night. Nevertheless, it is a mitzvah to make long candles that will remain lit until night, as stated in the Maharil (Shelah, Mateh Moshe, and Darkei Moshe).
It is a blessing in vain. It is possible that if that place was somewhat dark and one used something by the light of the candle, there is no prohibition (Maharil, siman 53).
