Magen Avraham, seif katan 26. And Maharam of Trani wrote. Meaning, according to the words of the Shulchan Aruch one must say that even though acceptance of Shabbos through prayer said in error is not acceptance, lighting is more severe, for even in error it is acceptance, as written in the Beit Yosef. To this he says that Maharam of Trani wrote that there is no distinction. Rather, those poskim hold that even with prayer, acceptance in error is also acceptance; therefore they hold the same regarding lighting.
