(s.k. 9) Because, etc.; it appears to me a blind person, etc., for behold he too benefits from the lights, for through there being a light others see and save her that she not fall and not stumble on anything; and a fortiori from Chanukah that he is obligated because in any case there is publicizing of the miracle among others; even though on Chanukah the reason that others save him does not apply; and if so the blind person too does not benefit, for the Chanukah light was not lightd to benefit from its light; on the contrary it is forbidden to use that light; all the more so a Shabbos light that was lightd for his benefit to illuminate, and the blind person too benefits; he is obligated:
For there is no joy; in siman 8 it is explained that the practice is if two or three members of a household eat in one place; nevertheless each can bless, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 15: the reason is that all additional light has in it shalom bayis and extra joy, etc.; and this does not apply to a blind woman, for she does not benefit from the light except that others save her lest she stumble; and for this one light suffices; and it is also better that the master of the house who sees bless, for there is joy with him; therefore the blind woman blesses even if the master of the house who sees does not bless:
