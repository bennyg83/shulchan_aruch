(א) (s.k. 9) Because, etc. — it appears to me a blind person, etc., for behold he too benefits from the lights, for through there being a candle others see and save her that she not fall and not stumble on anything; and a fortiori from Chanukah that he is obligated because in any case there is publicizing of the miracle among others — even though on Chanukah the reason that others save him does not apply; and if so the blind person too does not benefit, for the Chanukah candle was not kindled to benefit from its light — on the contrary it is forbidden to use that candle — all the more so a Shabbat candle that was kindled for his benefit to illuminate, and the blind person too benefits — he is obligated:

(ב) For there is no joy — in siman 8 it is explained that the practice is if two or three members of a household eat in one place — nevertheless each can bless, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 15: the reason is that all additional light has in it shalom bayit and extra joy, etc.; and this does not apply to a blind woman, for she does not benefit from the candle except that others save her lest she stumble — and for this one candle suffices; and it is also better that the master of the house who sees bless, for there is joy with him — therefore the blind woman blesses even if the master of the house who sees does not bless:
