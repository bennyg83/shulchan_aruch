(9) [Levush] All her days, etc. — to add on every mitzvah candle more than she was accustomed (Mateh Moshe, Maharil). We learn from this three things: (alias one — that even if she is accustomed to three she kindles more; two — even one mitzvah candle that is not of Shabbat; three — it implies that only addition is required, such as if she kindles a larger candle or gives a little more oil, but she need not kindle another candle for the addition — unlike the understanding of Rama and Levush; and it appears to be lenient on this for a poor woman:
