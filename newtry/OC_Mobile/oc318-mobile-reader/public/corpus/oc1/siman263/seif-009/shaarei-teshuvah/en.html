When he kindles. See Baer Heitev. Panim Meiros, part 1, siman 69, writes that those who prepare a special Shabbos candle, kindle it in the synagogue, and recite a blessing are acting in error, because the blessing is in vain. The only exception is when the shamash has not yet kindled any light, and this person first kindles with a blessing, since there is then an element of domestic peace, so that people will not stumble over one another after dark. The shamash may then kindle the remaining lights. Responsa Makom Shmuel, siman 18, likewise writes that although people may purchase the privilege of kindling synagogue lights for a substantial sum, no blessing should be recited.
