(9) He does not have, etc. — the intention is not that he has nothing at all to eat, for in that case better he go door to door to buy bread for Shabbat, for thereby he fulfills the mitzvah of Kiddush and also the main Shabbat meal; rather the intention is he has nothing to eat of his own, for he is sustained from the charity fund — nevertheless he must strive to obtain a candle for Shabbat also. And one who has limited money — the mitzvah of bread for the Shabbat meal precedes all; and a candle for Shabbat precedes buying other foods; for although other foods and meat are also included in oneg Shabbat, nevertheless a candle is preferable because of shalom bayit, as below. However, because of a candle — one candle alone suffices and the rest he spends on Shabbat foods, as we say in the Gemara that even a poor person must do something small for honor of Shabbat.
