15) [Seif 2] This applies equally to men and women, etc. Every person who eats the evening meal is obligated to eat it by lamplight, because this is included in the delight of Shabbos. The principal mitzvah of kindling Shabbos lamps is the lamp by whose light one eats. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1. See below, seif 10 in the gloss.
16) Although one eats and recites Kiddush upon a roof where moonlight shines upon his table from evening, he nevertheless kindles a Shabbos lamp with a blessing. In addition to the plain reason of domestic peace, the Shabbos lamp entails an obligation according to the esoteric teachings. Ben Ish Chai, parashas Noach, paragraph 4. For the esoteric reason, see the sacred Zohar, parashas Bereishis, folio 48b, and below, paragraph 20.
17) There: Even if he has nothing to eat, etc. This means that he must purchase food with the money in his possession. Certainly, his food takes precedence over a Shabbos lamp. If he has bread to eat, however, that is sufficient, and the lamp takes precedence over purchasing other foods. Magen Avraham, paragraph 4; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 4. This implies that he requires enough bread to satisfy himself, not merely an olive-sized portion. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 4. It also implies that this applies specifically to bread for eating, but the lamp certainly takes precedence over lechem mishneh.
