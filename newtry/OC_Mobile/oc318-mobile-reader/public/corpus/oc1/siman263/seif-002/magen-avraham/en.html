What shall he eat; meaning he must buy with the money in his hand to eat with certainty; his eating precedes the Shabbos light; and so in Rosh chapter Acharon; he asks on Rashbam who holds they sanctify on bread: it is obvious a light takes precedence over wine of Kiddush, for one can sanctify on bread; and he does not answer that we teach in a place where he has no bread a light takes precedence; rather certainly bread takes precedence; and so in the Gemara there folio 101 that Rabbah was not stringent on the light but on eating; and see the end of siman 273; however if he has bread to eat it suffices and the light precedes buying other foods:
