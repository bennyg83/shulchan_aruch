(א) What shall he eat — meaning he must buy with the money in his hand to eat with certainty — his eating precedes the Shabbat candle; and so in Rosh chapter Acharon — he asks on Rashbam who holds they sanctify on bread: it is obvious a candle takes precedence over wine of Kiddush, for one can sanctify on bread — and he does not answer that we teach in a place where he has no bread a candle takes precedence; rather certainly bread takes precedence; and so in the Gemara there daf 101 that Rabbah was not stringent on the candle but on eating; and Peri Megadim; however if he has bread to eat it suffices and the candle precedes buying other foods:
