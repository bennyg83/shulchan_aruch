There are those who say that one who accepted Shabbat before dark is permitted to tell his fellow Israelite to do melacha for him:And it is permitted to benefit from that melacha on Shabbat — and all the more so on Motza'ei Shabbat. And one who delays praying on Motza'ei Shabbat or continues his meal at night is permitted to tell his fellow Israelite who already prayed and recited havdalah to do his melacha for him — to kindle candles for him and to cook for him; and it is permitted to benefit and to eat from his melacha — so it appears to me:
