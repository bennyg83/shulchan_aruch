I therefore find difficulty in Olat Tamid's statement that at fairs, where several householders live in one room, one should bless and the others listen, intend to fulfill the obligation, answer Amen, and the one blessing should intend to discharge them. Perhaps he speaks where all the people living in that room are guests. In that case there is certainly an obligation to light in the room, and no one guest has an intrinsically greater obligation than another. See below, siman 677, because this law is learned from Chanukah, as Agudah writes.
