(7) Wax candles that are of idolatry — even though it is a matter where laymen are permitted to benefit from them, nevertheless they are forbidden for a Shabbat candle; and the same applies to all mitzvah matters because they are repulsive, as written above siman 654 seif 11 and in Yoreh De'ah 139 seif 13 — see there. And the same applies that it is forbidden to make Shabbat candles from wax dripping from candles in a house of idolaters — unlike one who permitted, as written in our words above siman 654 or part 79 — see there. And the same applies to a mouse found in oil — it is forbidden to kindle it for Shabbat candles, even if there is sixty against the mouse, as written above siman 654 seif 12 and in our words there or part 89 and or part 97 — see there; and likewise Turei Zahov in this siman end of or part 1; and in Sefer Zivchei Tzedek on Yoreh De'ah siman 104 or part 6 and or part 7; and likewise oil placed under a bed that is forbidden in eating — one places a row of stones on it as in Yoreh De'ah siman 116 seif 5; and in Birkei Yosef there or part 16 — likewise forbidden to kindle it for a Shabbat candle and for all mitzvah candles because of "bring it to your mouth" — and likewise Ben Ish Chai, Parashat Noach or part 17. But bitter oil that is not fit for eating — a patch does not apply and it is permitted to kindle it for a Shabbat candle and Chanukah candle. Ben Ish Chai there.
