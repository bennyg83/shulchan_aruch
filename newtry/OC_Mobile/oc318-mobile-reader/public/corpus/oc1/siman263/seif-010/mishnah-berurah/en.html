And the custom, etc. And if the man kindles (even when he blesses on his kindling, as above in se’if 6) this custom does not apply, and he may perform melacha; nevertheless it is good to stipulate.
She accepts Shabbos. And she should pray Minchah first, for since she already accepted Shabbos she can no longer pray a weekday prayer; and when there is no time for this it is better that she pray Maariv twice, as below in se’if 15; lest one enter, Heaven forbid, into a doubt of desecration of Shabbos; and see above siman 262 in se’if katan 11.
That she stipulate beforehand. And she may stipulate only for need, since there are those who disagree and hold that a stipulation is not effective, as above.
And the essence of kindling, etc. Meaning one blesses on them and not on the rest of the candles, for l’chatchila the primary mitzvah l’chatchila concerns the candles by whose light one eats, and the blessing should be on them; and even one candle suffices [Magen Avraham]. However if there are many women and each woman does not have a separate table; she may bless on the candles standing in the house in other places, such as on the candles burning on the lamp hanging in the middle of the house and the like; for we state a general rule that in every place where one kindles in order to make some use there; the consideration of domestic peace applies, and one should bless.
The candles in the house. Meaning the homeowner who can kindle on the table and bless on it; but a guest (if he has a special room for himself, even if he does not eat there) he kindles a candle there and blesses on it, as above in se’if 6.
And one must, etc. Meaning even if he stipulates explicitly that he does not accept Shabbos until the candles are in their place.
Not, etc. Meaning it is forbidden to kindle in a place where one does not use it and to place the candle in a place where one uses it, for we hold the act of kindling constitutes the mitzvah; and since he kindled in a place where one does not use it, which is not a place where the obligation applies; his kindling accomplishes nothing even if he places it afterward in a place of obligation; and he must extinguish and kindle again. But if he kindled in the house in a place where one uses it; it is permitted to move them afterward and place them elsewhere, for the whole house is their place. Levush is stringent even on this; and where there is need one may be lenient. Chayei Adam wrote that women who kindle in the sukkah on the festival and move the candles into the house; this is not proper practice.
