(10) There in Hagahah. A woman who forgot once, etc. — and if she forgot several times she must add each time, because the matter is due to fear that she be careful from now on in honor of Shabbat. Bach. And from the simple meaning it appears to me that if she was compelled and did not kindle — such as if she was in prison and the like — she need not add. Magen Avraham s.k. 3.
