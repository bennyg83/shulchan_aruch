Per the author of Halachot Gedolot, once one has kindled the Shabbat candle Shabbat falls upon him and melacha is forbidden; and based on this some women practice that after they bless and kindle the candles they throw to the ground the wick in their hand with which they kindled, and they do not extinguish it. And there are those who say that if she stipulates beforehand that she does not accept Shabbat until the chazzan says Barchu — it is effective. And there are those who say it is not effective. And there are those who disagree with the author of Halachot Gedolot and say that acceptance of Shabbat is not dependent on kindling the candle but on Maariv prayer, for when the chazzan says Barchu everyone refrains from melacha; and according to them, when we begin "Mizmor shir leyom haShabbat" — that is like Barchu for them:And the custom is that the woman who kindles accepts Shabbat through the kindling if she did not stipulate beforehand — and even a mental stipulation suffices (Mordechai); but the rest of the household are permitted in melacha until Barchu; and the essence of kindling depends on the candles kindled on the table, but not on the rest of the candles in the house (Or Zarua);) and one must place the candles in the place where one kindles, and not kindle in this place and place them elsewhere (Mordechai chapter Bameh Madlikin):.
