(א) And the practice is so — and if the man kindles there is no established custom, and melacha is permitted; nevertheless it is good to stipulate (Bach):

(ב) She accepts Shabbat — and she prays Minchah first (Mishna Berurah):

(ג) That she stipulated first — and nevertheless it appears to me one should not stipulate except for need, since there are disputants — for otherwise she blesses first and afterward stipulates, etc.:

(ד) But not on the other candles — see there s.k. 6 in the name of Maharil that nevertheless one blesses on them, even if he kindles in rooms where they are not used for eating need — for it is no worse than in synagogue — see there s.k. 9; however specifically when he has a special room and did not bless on another candle, as written s.k. 6; but those who kindle in a communal hall do not bless, even if he did not bless on another candle, for this is not a mitzvah incumbent upon him, and they also do not use it there for eating; but in a place where they use it for eating need, even another may bless as written s.k. 9; and it is good to be careful l'chatchila to kindle in the eating place; however it appears to me it suffices when one candle stands there:

(ה) And one must leave, etc. — Peri Chadash; and one can say here it deals via a stipulation:

(ו) And not to kindle, etc. — meaning it is not apparent that he kindles for honor of Shabbat (Levush); and therefore it appears to me that if he stood a short while in its place he may afterward place it elsewhere, for one may use its light; and likewise one may take it from its place and place it on a high hanging lamp, for it is apparent he does so for honor of Shabbat; and Mordechai explains the reason that kindling in its place we require — and this too is its place; and it is forbidden only when they kindle in a place where it is not used and afterward place it in a place where it is used, for we hold kindling fulfills the mitzvah as Tur wrote; and likewise we say regarding Chanukah candle — he kindled inside and took it outside, etc.; but outside, from place to place, is permitted, as in Yeshuot Yaakov end of chapter Bameh Madlikin that it is written if he kindled above twenty and placed below twenty cubits — lest they say for his need he holds it — it implies within twenty this reason does not apply; and in Mahariyu end of Pesach he wrote that the essence of kindling depends on the candles on the table — therefore one should not kindle in one place and place it on the table, for the viewer says for his need he holds it — until here; it implies for us who hold like Maharil that the whole house is its place; nevertheless one should be careful l'chatchila to bless on the table; and in Agudah he wrote: therefore one should not kindle a Chanukah candle on Shabbat and Yom Tov and place them in their place after kindling — rather first one places them in their place and afterward kindles them:
