10) There, gloss: A woman who forgot on one occasion, etc. If she forgot several times, she must add a lamp for each occasion, because the purpose is to provide a reminder that she be careful with the honor of Shabbos in the future. Bach. For this reason, it appears to me that if circumstances beyond her control prevented her from kindling, such as imprisonment or the like, she need not add a lamp. Magen Avraham, paragraph 3; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 8; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 3. It appears that the same applies if she did not kindle because of poverty and lacked money to purchase a lamp; she need not add one.
