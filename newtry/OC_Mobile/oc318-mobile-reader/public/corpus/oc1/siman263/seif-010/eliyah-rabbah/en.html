(10) [Levush] He must bless, etc. "Ner" or "nerot" of Shabbat (Minhagim) — meaning when kindling one candle he blesses "ner," and when kindling two he blesses "nerot"; except it implies there that even with two he may bless "ner," and see Yoreh De'ah siman 120 seif 3. Magen Avraham wrote: if she forgot to bless until it darkened, one may rely on Maharash that she bless again — up to here; and it is not clear that specifically at a permitted time for kindling it is permitted and not at a forbidden time, as Mahari Bruna wrote; and likewise a woman whose time of immersion arrived on Shabbat night should not do so, but as Olat Shabbat wrote — that she kindle and bless before going, and stipulate in her heart that she does not accept Shabbat until after immersion; however there is room to hesitate, for we say "provided he does not precede" — see there. He further wrote that the husband kindles and blesses; and I heard from the house of my ancestors the Gaon Rosh z"l that they go to their house between their hair-washing and immersion, kindle, and return for immersion; and I further heard that some practice per our elders to kindle in the immersion house — and this was far in my eyes, for behold specifically "on his table" we require; but I found support in responsa Maharil siman 53 that he permitted to kindle and bless even in the synagogue adjacent to the mikveh when the candles are not designated for the synagogue — all the more so in the immersion house where there is need for a candle near dark; and nevertheless it is correct as I wrote at the beginning:
