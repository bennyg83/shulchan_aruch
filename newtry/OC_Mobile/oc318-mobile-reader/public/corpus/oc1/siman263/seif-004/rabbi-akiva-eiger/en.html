Seif 4, in the gloss. The lamp is burning from before the day, meaning that it was burning for another purpose. Since the lighting was not for Shabbos, he must extinguish it and then light it again for the sake of Shabbos. But if the lighting was for the sake of Shabbos, only he lit it while it was still very early in the day, he need not extinguish it. Rather, initially one should not light so early that it is not recognizable that he is lighting it in honor of Shabbos, but after the fact it is proper. What Tosafot wrote, as taught in the Baraisa, provided that he not light too early, means that from there it implies that the mitzvah is not merely that he have a lit lamp on Shabbos, but that the act of lighting itself be for the sake of Shabbos. Initially it is proper that it be recognizable that the lighting is in honor of Shabbos. According to this, everything depends on whether the lighting is for the sake of Shabbos or not. The wording of the Rama, while it is still very early in the day, is not exact, and in Tosafot and the Tur the word very is not mentioned.
