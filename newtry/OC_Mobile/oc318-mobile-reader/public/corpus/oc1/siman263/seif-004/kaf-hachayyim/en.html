Some say they correspond to a man and a woman, because the numerical value of two instances of the word ner corresponds to the combined number of limbs in a man and a woman. A woman has four more limbs than the 248 of a man: two hinges and two doors. Another reason is that they allude to the two souls bound together in a person's body. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2. It further appears to me that they correspond to the two additions to the levels of nefesh, ruach, and neshamah that a person receives on Shabbos, one from the aspect of the night and one from the aspect of the day, as stated above at the end of siman 261; see there.
