One should not hurry to kindle while the day is still great, for then it is not recognizable that one kindles for the honor of Shabbat; and also one should not delay. And if one wishes to kindle a candle while the day is still great and accept Shabbat upon himself immediately — he is permitted, for since he accepts Shabbat upon himself immediately this is not considered premature, provided it is from plag haminchah and above, which is an hour and a quarter before night:And see below siman 267; and if the candle was burning from while the day was still great — he extinguishes it and kindles again for the sake of Shabbat (Tur):.
