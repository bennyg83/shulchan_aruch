(15) While the day is still great — meaning when it is much time before tosefet Shabbat; and the time of tosefet Shabbat for Shulchan Aruch is a little before the end of sunset — and see in Biur Halacha. And see in siman 261 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 23 that we conclude there: one who separates himself from melacha half an hour or at least a third of an hour before sunset — fortunate is he, for thereby he fulfills all the Rishonim:

(16) He should not delay — meaning where he kindles at the end of the permitted time; and the reason is lest in any case he delay past the time; and see above in siman 262 s.k. 11 in Mishna Berurah:

(17) And to accept upon himself, etc. — meaning he separates himself from all forbidden melachot, even from those things forbidden d'rabbanan:

(18) And above — but before plag hamincha his acceptance is not acceptance even b'dieved; and see in siman 261 s.k. 25 in Mishna Berurah:

(19) The night — meaning emergence of three stars; and the view of Levush and Gra that plag hamincha is an hour and a quarter before sunset. And per all, this hour and a quarter is a seasonal hour — meaning the day is divided into 12 parts per its hours whether long or short; for Shulchan Aruch from sunset until emergence of stars, and for Levush and Gra from sunrise until sunset:

(20) From while day is still great — even if it was after plag hamincha; and it speaks where it was lit for another purpose; but if he kindled for Shabbat need, even though he did not accept Shabbat upon himself at his kindling, nevertheless he need not extinguish it; for l'chatchila he should not have kindled so early that it is not recognizable they kindle for Shabbat need; nevertheless b'dieved it is permitted; and see in Biur Halacha:
