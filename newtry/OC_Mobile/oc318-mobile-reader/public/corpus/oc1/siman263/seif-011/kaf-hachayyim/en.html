11) The same applies if she was originally accustomed to kindle three lamps each time. If she forgot, she must add one lamp and kindle four each time thereafter, and so on indefinitely. Machatzis HaShekel. Similarly, if she ordinarily kindles two lamps and once kindled only one, she must add a lamp. If she kindles many lamps and on one occasion forgot and kindled one fewer, she must kindle an additional lamp so that she will be careful. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 3. It is obvious that she must add oil and kindle the additional lamp. She should not take the quantity of oil with which she previously kindled two lamps and distribute it among three, because this does the opposite: the lamps will be extinguished sooner, further diminishing the honor of Shabbos.
