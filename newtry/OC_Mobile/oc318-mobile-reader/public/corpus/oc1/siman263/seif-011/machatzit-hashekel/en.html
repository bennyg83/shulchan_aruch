(1) (s.k. 11) When she lights; it is fit to pray, etc., for we say chapter Bameh Madlikin: R' Huna said; whoever is careful with the light will have sons who are Torah scholars; for light is mitzvah and Torah is light; through the light of mitzvah one merits Torah light; therefore it is fit that she pray at lighting that she have sons who are Torah scholars, for at the time of fulfilling the mitzvah prayer for the special reward of that mitzvah is more desired before Him, may He be blessed:
(2) Maharash wrote, etc.; and it is astonishing, for a blessing is not applicable to something already lit, etc.; and with this he rejects the first enactment of Maharash: since she lightd of her own accord and did not accept Shabbos upon herself, then Shabbos is not called a Shabbos light and it is like something lit and standing; as Rama wrote se’if 4 that one must extinguish it and light it for the sake of a Shabbos light; and how can she bless on it at night:
(3) And further regarding amirah, etc.; with this he rejects the second enactment of Maharash that she tell a gentile, etc., for it is explained below siman 276 that it is not proper to tell a gentile to light; even though many are lenient one should not protest against them; we interpret from the reason "better they be inadvertent," etc.; and how can one enact thus l'chatchila:
(4) And see in Mahariv who permitted, etc.; meaning like the first enactment of Maharash; but to Maharam it does not appear [correct] the words of Maharash and Mahariv:
(5) And what is found in the collection, etc.; I did not know whether Maharash brought proof from that collection or Mahariv, for Mahariv has no Talmud Yerushalmi; and in any case Maharam wrote on it that one may not rely on that collection, for it is not a reliable master, etc. However b'dieved, etc., and siman 276; meaning that l'chatchila one may not rely on Maharash's enactments; nevertheless b'dieved if she forgot to bless of her own accord she blesses at night via spreading the hands; meaning enactment 1; and also b'dieved if she forgot to light until it darkened she tells a gentile to light; meaning enactment 2 of Maharash, as written siman 276 that thus they practiced; and nevertheless one light suffices, for one light is enough for him so he not eat in darkness; and likewise Magen Avraham below siman 276:
