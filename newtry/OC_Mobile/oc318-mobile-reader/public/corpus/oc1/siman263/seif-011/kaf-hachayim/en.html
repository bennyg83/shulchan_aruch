(11) And the same applies if originally her custom was to kindle three candles each time — if she forgot she must add one candle and kindle four each time, and so forever. Machatzit HaShekel. And likewise if her custom was to kindle two candles and once she kindled only one — she must add. And likewise if she kindles many and forgot once and one is missing — she kindles more so she be careful. Avnei Rozal or part 3. And it is a simple matter that she must add oil and kindle the addition; but not in oil with which she had been kindling two candles should she kindle three, for on the contrary in this they detract, for they are extinguished quickly and diminish more in honor of Shabbat:
