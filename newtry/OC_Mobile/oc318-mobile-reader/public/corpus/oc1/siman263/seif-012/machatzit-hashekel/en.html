(1) (s.k. 12) After, etc., in the name of his mother — so it should read:

(2) But there is no wisdom for a woman. Meaning all women did not know to distinguish between Shabbat and Yom Tov — and if we say on Yom Tov that she bless and afterward kindle, she would do so on Shabbat too:

(3) As written siman 618 that regarding performing the act before the blessing the Sages made no distinction. For in siman 618 seif 1 he wrote that one should not bless before performing the act — meaning before immersion — but after immersion before drying, for sometimes his hands are not clean, such as when he came from the bathroom and he cannot bless before immersion; therefore they did not distinguish, and even if his hands are clean he should not bless before immersion because of lo plug; and likewise Tosafot in Pesachim: what is the reason we do not bless on immersion before immersing so it be performing the act before the blessing — because of ger immersion, for we hold he is not a ger until he circumcises and immerses, and then the ger cannot bless before immersion, for as long as he has not immersed he is not a ger — and how can he say "Who sanctified us," etc., and commanded us, for he is not yet obligated in the mitzvah; therefore even niddah immersion she blesses after immersion because of lo plug:
