12) There, gloss: She kindles throughout her life, etc. She should add to each mitzvah lamp more than she was previously accustomed to use. Mateh Moshe and Maharil. We learn three points from this. First, even if she was accustomed to three lamps, she must add. Second, this applies even to another mitzvah lamp that is not for Shabbos. Third, the wording implies that only an increase is required, such as kindling a larger lamp or adding a little oil, but she need not kindle an additional lamp, contrary to the understanding of Rema and Levush. It appears that one may be lenient in this regard for a poor woman. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 9; Machatzis HaShekel; Eshel Avraham there.
