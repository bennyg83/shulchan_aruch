(Seif 11) In doing melacha and even if he says — in Tur wrote and Tosafot wrote that it does not depend on candle kindling but depends on Maariv prayer — one who prays Maariv Shabbat applies to him; and Beit Yosef wrote need to explain this is the beginning of Maariv which is Barechu; and afterward Beit Yosef wrote what Rabbenu wrote further that one who prays Maariv Shabbat applies to him — it appears to teach even though the congregation did not pray Maariv and the individual preceded and prayed Shabbat prayer Shabbat applies to him; however if he does not wish to accept Shabbat, etc.; and more fitting since he mentioned kedushat hayom in prayer impossible to make it profane per Darkhei Chaim — end of his words; it appears this is specifically in actual Maariv prayer that mentions kedushat hayom; but in Barechu it depends on the poskim whether stipulation helps in kindling; however the language of Shulchan Aruch is difficult — for in what he first wrote if the individual preceded and prayed acceptance of Shabbat applies to him — this speaks in Barechu alone; and above he wrote even if he said he does not wish to accept Shabbat — behold this is only in actual prayer and not in Barechu; requires study:
(Seif 16) Begins to pray Minchah; and should not wait to pray Minchah until after Barechu for answering Barechu like one who needs to do on other days; since if he answers Barechu he cannot pray Minchah — end of Maharai; and possible specifically to begin before, for still no obligation to answer Barechu; and so explicit in Toldot HeAdam there — and this is he did not accept Shabbat with them such as he was not in synagogue and did not answer Barechu — implies if he is in synagogue he is obligated to answer Barechu; but in Agudah ch. 2 Shabbat if he comes to synagogue when they say Barechu he should not answer; and per what was written above seif 1 in gloss that per what we hold in kindling he can stipulate — likewise at Barechu before prayer — if so one could say he must answer Barechu and stipulate he does not accept Shabbat and can pray Minchah outside synagogue:
