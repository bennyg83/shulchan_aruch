(Seif 16) Begins to pray Minchah; and should not wait to pray Minchah until after Barechu for answering Barechu like one who needs to do on other days; since if he answers Barechu he cannot pray Minchah — end of Maharai; and possible specifically to begin before, for still no obligation to answer Barechu; and so explicit in Toldot HeAdam there — and this is he did not accept Shabbat with them such as he was not in synagogue and did not answer Barechu — implies if he is in synagogue he is obligated to answer Barechu; but in Agudah ch. 2 Shabbat if he comes to synagogue when they say Barechu he should not answer; and per what was written above seif 1 in gloss that per what we hold in kindling he can stipulate — likewise at Barechu before prayer — if so one could say he must answer Barechu and stipulate he does not accept Shabbat and can pray Minchah outside synagogue:
