And he answered that it teaches even via stipulation. And even though Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 27 that the members of the household are forbidden to touch that candle even if they stipulated not to accept Shabbat — meaning specifically when the household stipulated, for nevertheless since the one who kindled did not stipulate it is a Shabbat candle and all are forbidden to touch it; but if the one who kindled himself stipulated it is preferable and the one who kindled himself is permitted to touch it and all the more so others.
