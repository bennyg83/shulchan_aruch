16) Although one eats and recites Kiddush upon a roof where moonlight shines upon his table from evening, he nevertheless kindles a Shabbos lamp with a blessing. In addition to the plain reason of domestic peace, the Shabbos lamp entails an obligation according to the esoteric teachings. Ben Ish Chai, parashas Noach, paragraph 4. For the esoteric reason, see the sacred Zohar, parashas Bereishis, folio 48b, and below, paragraph 20.
