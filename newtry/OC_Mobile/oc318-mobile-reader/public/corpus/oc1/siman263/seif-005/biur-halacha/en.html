(א) When he kindles he blesses — and if she was in doubt whether she blessed or not, she need not bless, for doubt regarding blessings is lenient; and furthermore a custom is mentioned and derived [Nachalat Shiva and Derech HaChaim]. And if she knew with certainty that she did not bless — it is implied from Eliyah Rabbah that one may be lenient to bless as long as it is a permitted time to kindle, as Magen Avraham in s.k. 11 who relies b'dieved on Maharash; but if it is a forbidden time he disagrees with Maharash:

(ב) And see siman 611 — see in Mishna Berurah, and it appears that the Mechaber's intent is to give a reason for why he holds one should not bless, for below siman 611 seif 1 the Mechaber wrote: in a place where they practiced to kindle they kindle, and in a place where they did not practice to kindle they do not kindle — perforce even though our custom is to kindle, nevertheless one does not bless, for by custom one does not bless; and this reason is in the Gra's explanation:

(ג) After kindling — see in Mishna Berurah; and a woman who stipulates that she does not accept Shabbat through kindling, or a man — for we hold below that he need not stipulate — it is implied in Derech HaChaim that nevertheless they kindle and spread their hands and afterward bless, because of lo plug and according to Magen Avraham; but from the words of the Gaon R' Eliezer of Metz in his chiddushim and from the words of Chayei Adam it is proven that in this they bless and afterward kindle:
