When he kindles he blesses: "Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to kindle the candle of Shabbat" — both the man and the woman. Also on Yom Tov one must bless to kindle the candle of Yom Tov; and on Yom Tov that is not Shabbat — there is one who says one does not bless; see below siman 610:There is one who says that we bless before kindling and there is one who says one blesses after kindling (Mordechai end of Bava Metzia); and so that it be "over le'asiyatan" one should not benefit from it until after the blessing — therefore one places a hand before the candle after kindling and blesses, and afterward removes the hand; and this is called "over le'asiyatan," and such is the custom (Maharil):.
