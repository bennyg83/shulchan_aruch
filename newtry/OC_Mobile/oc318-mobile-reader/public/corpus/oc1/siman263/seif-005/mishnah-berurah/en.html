(21) When she kindles — Yesh Omrim: when there is a wedding canopy on Erev Shabbat and they delay there until after sunset, and the woman does not wish to accept Shabbat before the canopy lest she need do something else forbidden on Shabbat — perforce she kindles the candle without a blessing before the canopy, and afterward in darkness she spreads her hands over the candles and blesses; or while still in bein hashemashos after the canopy she says to a non-Jew to kindle, for they did not decree shevut in bein hashemashos for mitzvah need, and similarly in siman 261, and she blesses. Acharonim wondered on this, for blessing does not apply to a standing flame; and especially now is time forbidden to kindle — how can she say vetzivanu lehadlik; and on the other enactment that she say to a non-Jew to kindle — also difficult, for there is no agency for a non-Jew, and thus she is not kindling — how can she bless; and even an Israelite whom his fellow is commanded to kindle for him — per Darkhei Chaim the commanded one does not bless, only the kindler; all the more here the woman does not bless. Acharonim conclude another advice: per what we hold she can bless and stipulate in any case for need that she not accept Shabbat at kindling — likewise in our matter it is for need: she kindles and blesses before going to the canopy and stipulates; and likewise a woman whose night of immersion falls on Erev Shabbat — she kindles and blesses before going to the mikveh and stipulates she does not accept Shabbat until after rinsing and combing:

(22) To kindle a candle — and even when kindling several candles, better he say ner, for the main obligation is one candle:

(23) Also on Yom Tov, etc. — and one need not bless shehecheyanu on the kindling; however where they are accustomed, one should not protest:

(24) A Yom Tov candle — and if Yom Tov falls on Shabbat he says for Shabbat and for Yom Tov:

(25) And see above siman 631 — where the Mechaber brings the view of the posek who requires blessing, for he holds that also on Yom Kippur, even though there is no eating and drinking and oneg does not apply, nevertheless shalom bayit applies:

(26) Before the kindling — like all mitzvot where one blesses before performing them:

(27) After the kindling — he holds that if she blesses it is as if she accepted Shabbat explicitly and again forbidden to kindle; and thus on Yom Tov where this does not apply, per all she blesses and afterward kindles.
