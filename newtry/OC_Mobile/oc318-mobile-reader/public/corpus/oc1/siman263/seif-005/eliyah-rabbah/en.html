(5) Women are warned, etc. Nevertheless the master of the house prepares the candles (Kesavim). Bach wrote: even if the master of the house wishes to kindle himself, the woman takes precedence; and some are accustomed to kindle for him a twisted candle in the synagogue so that he too fulfills the mitzvah of kindling himself — up to here; and it appears that in any case he does not bless. A woman who gave birth on her first Shabbat — the husband kindles and blesses; and likewise one who has no wife kindles himself (Mateh Moshe); and in Sheyarei Kenesset HaGedolah he wrote our custom to bring a candle to a woman who gave birth and she kindles; he further wrote Sheyarei Kenesset HaGedolah z"l, Rashal chapter 2 of Beitzah:
