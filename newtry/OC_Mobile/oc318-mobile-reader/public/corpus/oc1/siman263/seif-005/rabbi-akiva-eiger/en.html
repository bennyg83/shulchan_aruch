Seif 5. On Yom Tov too one must recite a blessing. There are places where the women have the custom to recite Shehecheyanu when lighting the Yom Tov lamp, but this has no foundation. Nevertheless, one should not protest against them forcefully. She'eilas Yaavetz, siman 107.
Magen Avraham, seif katan 11. This is difficult, for in my humble opinion it does not apply even to the other remedy, that she should tell a non-Jew to light. It is difficult, since there is no agency for a non-Jew, she is not lighting, so how can she recite the blessing? The main point, according to what we have established, is that she can make a condition when there is some need; she should light beforehand and make a condition.
