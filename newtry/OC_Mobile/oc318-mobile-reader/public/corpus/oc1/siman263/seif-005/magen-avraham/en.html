When she lights; it is fit that the woman pray at lighting that the Holy One, blessed is He, grant her sons who illuminate the Torah (Mateh Moshe in the name of Bach on Parashat Yitro). There are siddurim that wrote a blessing for lights after leaving the synagogue; one should not say it (Maharam Mintz, paragraph 7). Maharash wrote in the name of Maharam z"l: when there is a chuppah on Erev Shabbos and they delay it until after sunset and the woman does not wish to accept Shabbos before the chuppah; then she lights the light without a blessing before the chuppah, and afterward in darkness she spreads her hands over the lights and blesses; or she tells a non-Jew to light after the chuppah and she blesses; until here; and it is puzzling, for there is no blessing on an already lit light; and further amirah to a non-Jew is shevut; however Beit Hillel did not decree shevut (see siman 261); and see Mahariyu who permitted to light while the day is still great and afterward spread hands and bless, etc.; and what is found in a collection that they bless on lights on Shabbos after leaving the synagogue; he does not hold it, and Ravina did not sign on it (Maharam Hagahot Maimoniyot); however b'dieved if she forgot to bless until dark she may rely on Maharash; see siman 276 se’if 2 in Hagahah; and nevertheless one light suffices:
