(14) There in Hagahah. Three candles. And if it is permitted to kindle a Shabbat candle from a Shabbat candle via an intermediary — see below siman 674 that he wrote there are those who permit if it is a matter where there is no concern the intermediary will be extinguished before the Shabbat candle is kindled; but for plain Maran this too is forbidden unless kindling candle from candle — see there. And see in Magen Avraham there that he wrote in the name of Mordekhai that they were accustomed to be stringent not to kindle mitzvah candles one from another; and Avnei Rozal wrote there it is possible this means via an intermediary, but without an intermediary even from the side of custom there is nothing — except that he concluded it is better to kindle Shabbat candles via the sun — see there.
