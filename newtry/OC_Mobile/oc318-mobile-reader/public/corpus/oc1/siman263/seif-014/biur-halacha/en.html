(א) And if an individual etc. — meaning even if he prayed Mincha then before plag hamincha; and even though on every erev Shabbat it is permitted in this manner to pray Ma'ariv before night, as below siman 267 — for we hold (Berachos 27a) "act like Mar, act" etc. — nevertheless since he is always accustomed like the Rabbis and today in error prayed thinking that it had already darkened, therefore he must return and pray; and on account of this reason even if this matter occurred on a weekday too the law is so, as Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah and Tosafos Shabbos and Peri Megadim wrote, and not like Eshel Avraham who wrote specifically if he prayed Mincha then after plag hamincha. And likewise what he wrote at the beginning that in a congregation one need not return — it deals with all cases, meaning even if they prayed Mincha that day after plag hamincha — even though it is two contradictory matters, we do not burden the congregation on account of this, and so too in Eshel Avraham:

(ב) And there are those who say that those who kindled etc. — see the reason in Mishna Berurah — because kindling is an action, therefore it is preferable, and it is from Beit Yosef; and according to this if they accepted Shabbat upon themselves through speech — meaning through Mizmor shir leyom haShabbat — and it was in error that afterward the clouds dispersed and the sun shone, they would be permitted in melacha according to all; but according to what the Gra explained in his Beur the reason of "there are those who say," it appears that this too is forbidden according to "there are those who say," for he explained the reason of "there are those who say" — that they hold acceptance in error is acceptance; and from prayer there is no proof, for prayer in error is nothing, and even in a congregation only because we do not burden them to return and pray — therefore acceptance that comes through prayer is also nothing; not so acceptance through kindling — end of his words. If so, according to this, in Mizmor shir or in plain acceptance through speech the law is like in kindling. And know that through the Gra's reason the difficulty of Eshel Avraham brought in Eliyah Rabbah is resolved:

(ג) Forbidden etc. — see in Eliyah Rabbah who wrote specifically in a congregation, but an individual who kindled candles in error is not considered acceptance at all; and Peri Megadim and Derech HaChaim are stringent in kindling even for an individual — and all this specifically after plag hamincha:
