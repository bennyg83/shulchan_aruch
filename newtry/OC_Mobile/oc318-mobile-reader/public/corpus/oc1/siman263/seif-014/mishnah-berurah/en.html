They need not. For we do not burden the congregation, even though it was in error.
From plag hamincha and above. For before plag hamincha even the congregation must repeat the prayer, for it is not the time of Maariv prayer at all. And the time of plag hamincha; see above in siman 233.
He must, etc. Since he mistakenly prayed thinking it had already gotten dark; and likewise on a weekday the same law applies, since he is accustomed always to pray Maariv at its time in accordance with the Sages, and on this day he prayed early by mistake.
Those who kindled. Even if they did not pray; but without kindling, even if they prayed the Shabbos prayer, since it was mistakenly it is not considered acceptance. And the reason of some authorities who hold that acceptance through kindling is more effective, because it involves an action; and see in later authorities who brought in the name of several great poskim who hold that an erroneous communal acceptance, even only via prayer, is considered acceptance; except that in such a case the minority is not drawn after the majority, since the acceptance was by mistake, and one should not be lenient contrary to those poskim. And in a pressing situation one may rely on the first opinion in Shulchan Aruch.
To touch it, etc. Meaning even before he kindled and did not accept Shabbos; so wrote Eshel Avraham. And Magen Avraham wrote the reason: he follows his own view that an erroneous communal acceptance is considered acceptance; and thus the candle was designated for its mitzvah; and since it was designated for the owners it is designated for all; and also forbidden to touch, lest perhaps he use it. For the sake of a mitzvah one may be lenient [Derech HaChaim]. Peri Megadim wrote: one who kindled a Shabbos candle while it was still broad daylight and stipulated not to accept Shabbos; nevertheless the candle was designated for its mitzvah and forbidden to use it for weekday use; and even others are forbidden, because an item designated as muktzeh for its owners is forbidden to everyone.
