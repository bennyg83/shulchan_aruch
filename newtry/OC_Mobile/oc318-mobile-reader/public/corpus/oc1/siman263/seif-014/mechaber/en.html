If on a cloudy day the congregation erred and thought it had darkened and kindled candles and prayed Maariv of Shabbat, and afterward the clouds dispersed and the sun shone — they need not repeat Maariv if when they prayed it was plag haminchah and above; and if an individual erred thus — he must repeat Maariv. And regarding doing melacha — both congregation and individual are permitted, for acceptance of Shabbat was in error. And there are those who say that those who kindled candles are forbidden in melacha, and the rest of the household are permitted. And there are those who say that the candle that was kindled for Shabbat — it is forbidden to touch it and to add oil to it; even if it was extinguished it is forbidden to move it.
