(א) And bless on it — for kindling a candle is obligatory because of shalom bayit, lest one stumble on a tree or stone (Mordechai); and even per one who says on a Yom Kippur candle one need not bless — nevertheless here they use it for eating need to prepare their intestines and meal vessels — unlike in synagogue; and all the more so for us who hold they bless in synagogue, for they consider shalom bayit even without eating (Maharil in responsa siman 255):

(ב) One need not kindle in his room and bless on it — but to kindle one must that they not stumble on a tree or stone; and see Magen Avraham siman 261 and siman 267:
