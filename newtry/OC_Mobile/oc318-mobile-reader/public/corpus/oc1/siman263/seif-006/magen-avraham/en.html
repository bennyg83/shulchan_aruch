And recite a blessing over it. Kindling a light is obligatory because of shalom bayis, so that one not stumble over wood or stone, as stated by the Mordechai. Even according to the opinion that one need not recite a blessing over a Yom Kippur light, here the light is used for the needs of eating, to prepare one’s utensils and meal utensils, which is not so on Yom Kippur. This is certainly so according to our ruling that a blessing is recited on a Yom Kippur light, because shalom bayis is considered a sufficient reason even without eating, as stated in Responsa Maharil, siman 155.
He need not light in his room and recite a blessing over it. He must nevertheless light there, so that they not stumble over wood or stone. See Magen Avraham, s.k. 1, and see se’if 7.
