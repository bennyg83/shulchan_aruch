(6) And if he filled a bowl with oil and surrounded it with wicks and distanced them from each other a finger-width — it does not become like a bonfire. So too Tur in siman 671 in the name of Rosh regarding Chanukah candles; and Beit Yosef wrote there that the measure of distancing a finger-width in medium wicks, and per that value one must add distance in thick ones and reduce distance in thin ones — see there. And if so the same applies to Shabbat; therefore those who kindle on Shabbat in a lamp where they place water and oil and wicks must distance the wicks from each other a finger-width if they are medium as mentioned; and the width of the mentioned finger everywhere is the width of the thumb of an average person, as is known:
