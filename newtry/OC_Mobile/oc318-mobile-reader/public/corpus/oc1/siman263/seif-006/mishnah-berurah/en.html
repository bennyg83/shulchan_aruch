(28) Unmarried men — meaning they have wives but go outside their homes; and even though his wife kindles in his house he is not exempt by his wife's blessing, since he has a special room where he lodges there; and all the more if he is not married he is obligated to kindle:

(29) In their room — even if the room is designated only to lodge there and they eat in another house:

(30) And to bless on it — for kindling a candle is obligatory because of shalom bayit, lest one stumble on a tree or stone; and the candles must be long so they burn until they come home at night; otherwise the blessing is in vain [Darkhei Chaim]. And regarding guests who lodge many in one room on Erev Shabbat, and the host is not with them in their room — the obligation of kindling with blessing applies to them; even if their wives kindle in their homes, perforce they all participate and one kindles and blesses and intends to fulfill for all through his blessing; and they too intend to fulfill through his blessing. And if the host is also with them in their room, they need not participate with him in particulars, since their wives kindle in their homes, as below in seif 7:
