Magen Avraham, seif katan 12. That the Sages did not distinguish, as written in siman 158, etc. In my humble opinion, this is understandable according to the Baal Halachos Gedolos, that lighting constitutes acceptance of Shabbos, and according to those who hold that even a condition does not help. If so, on Shabbos, when the obligation is to bless afterward, one could say that the Sages did not distinguish. But since we rule that for a need a condition helps, perforce the essential halacha follows the poskim who hold that a condition helps, only that when there is no need we are concerned for the poskim who hold that a condition does not help. For this reason we recite the blessing after lighting, in order to be stringent out of concern for those poskim, as the Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 20. If so, it is not relevant to say that the Sages did not distinguish, for the Sages did not institute anything on Shabbos in this regard, since by law, even on Shabbos one would bless first. Examine this carefully. Regarding the main law on Shabbos, that one does not bless before lighting because it would constitute acceptance of Shabbos, I do not understand it. She lights several lamps, and we do not say that after lighting one lamp there has been acceptance of Shabbos, such that she could not light the others. Perforce, acceptance of Shabbos occurs at the completion of the lighting. If so, it is obvious that the blessing is no better than the beginning of the lighting. This requires study.
