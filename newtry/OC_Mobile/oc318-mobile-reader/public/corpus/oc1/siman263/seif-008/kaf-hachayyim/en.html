8) One should not kindle an old earthenware lamp upon the table. Responsa Maharshal, siman 86; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1. A poor person who has no other lamp may certainly use it. Mishbetzos Zahav at the end of the siman. If it was fired in a kiln, or if it is made of glass or glazed earthenware, it is permitted, as stated below in siman 673, seif 3. It is proper for a woman who fears Hashem not to kindle with a wood splinter, but to have a designated wax candle to kindle every Shabbos. Paaneach Raza; Ateres Zekeinim; Eliyah Rabbah there. See below, paragraph 14.
