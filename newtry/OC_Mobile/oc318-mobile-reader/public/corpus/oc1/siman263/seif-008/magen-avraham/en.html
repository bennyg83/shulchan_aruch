Each one blesses; for all additional light has in it shalom bayis and extra joy for enjoyment of light in all corners; and likewise what some women practice when it is a night of immersion and the mikveh is adjacent to the synagogue and they do not wish to go to their house lest they become soiled; therefore they light their lights in the synagogue; one should not protest against them; per one who lightd in synagogue; or when they light in the house and eat in the courtyard; and if you say there they use a little for eating need; here too it is possible they do something in synagogue and benefit from it at night for eating need, and it is easy to find; but lights designated otherwise for the synagogue one should not bless on them, for they are not made to illuminate but only for honor; however in a city where the women's synagogue is the attic of the men's; perhaps it is considered for meal need to draw wine at night (Maharil siman 53); and Shelah wrote that nevertheless two should not bless on one lamp that has many branches:
