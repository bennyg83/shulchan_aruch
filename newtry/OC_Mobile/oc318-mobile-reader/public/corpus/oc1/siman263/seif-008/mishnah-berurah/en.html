(35) That each one blesses — for whatever additional light there is shalom bayit and extra joy for enjoyment of light in every corner:

(36) And there are those who murmur — their reason is that without this there is greater light from the candles the first one kindled:

(37) There is no practice, etc. — rather per the first view; and Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Shelah that nevertheless two should not bless on one menorah that has many branches, and there are those lenient even in this; and Peri Megadim wrote there is room to rely on this for poor women who have only one menorah:

(38) Yes — and if one of them has a room designated for him even though he does not eat there and does not use there any eating need, per all he can bless there, and likewise below in seif 6:
