The custom is not, etc. Rather, the practice follows the first view; and Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Shelah that nevertheless two should not bless on one menorah that has many branches, and there are those lenient even in this; and Peri Megadim wrote one may rely on this for poor women who have only one menorah.
