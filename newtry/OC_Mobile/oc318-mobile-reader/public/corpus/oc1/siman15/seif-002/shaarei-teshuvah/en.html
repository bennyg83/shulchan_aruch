On another garment. Eliyah Rabbah wrote in Be'er Heitev in the name of responsa of Mahari Molcho and Maharam ben Chaviv: one whose two or three tzitzis broke on his tallit and must make others — nevertheless if one remains kosher, even one, one may not untie it from the tallit to discard it, for here too there is concern of degrading mitzvah. But if he wants to make nicer tzitzis, or the first are nice but worn somewhat and not so strong, permitted to untie them to make new — see there. He wrote in Beit Yehuda that permitted to untie tzitzis bindings even though directed in gematria to the Tetragrammaton — no concern here.
