(Magen Avraham note 4) And it appears to me kosher etc. — apparently needs study: what difference between this and that in seif 2? For there too if sewn to another piece it is as bringing two corners with tzitzis and sewing to another garment — invalid; and one must say only there, since this corner alone has no garment measure, therefore invalidates tzitzis on it; therefore when sewn later it is tolmei marei — unlike here, since each part has measure to wrap, tzitzis in it were not invalidated; therefore properly may join even another piece; and accordingly one may say Bach's view who holds when he begins to sew the corner is canceled and tzitzis invalidated — if so when he returns and sews it is as bringing two corners with invalid tzitzis and sewing to invalid garment like that in seif 2; and the proof Magen Avraham brought from torn outside three — there beginning of making was in this garment in validity, as divided in note 3; meaning even though now this corner has no garment measure, nevertheless from beginning of making they were valid for this garment itself — unlike attaching to another garment where this corner was not acquired for this garment and is as beginning of making in invalidity; if so likewise since he joins to another piece and tzitzis were already invalidated as corner canceled, when he sews the corner beginning of making is invalid regarding the other piece — like seif 2 etc. Possibly this is also Darkhei Moshe whom Taz brought, explaining Rashba whether permitted to return and join because he holds joining the tallit itself is preferable: even though invalidated by beginning of sewing canceling corner, we are not concerned since beginning of making for this garment was in validity — they return and become valid — unlike attaching to another piece, beginning of making in invalidity, as Bach; also no proof from veShoin etc. implying without tekheles it is fine to bring cubit by cubit from another garment and make tzitzis — as Taz wrote may reject: he did not speak unnecessarily that even cubit by cubit with tekheles from another garment — lest one say since all tzitzis began in validity, invalid that first were invalidated and do not return to validate for the new piece — tolmei marei; even if you say both one reason, nevertheless he stated with tekheles similar to dispute torn within three that deals with sewing with tekheles; one may also say without tekheles no invalidity here only because of corner cancellation — accordingly when he begins to sew in the middle it is properly considered, as Eliyah Rabbah; therefore they stated with tekheles — in such a case in every respect invalid; Rashba deals only with completing tzitzis on that tallit itself — and from this I bring proof well from veShoin etc. that the first were not invalidated.
