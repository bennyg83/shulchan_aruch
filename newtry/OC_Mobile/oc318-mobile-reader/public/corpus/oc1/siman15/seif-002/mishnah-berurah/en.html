(5) He cannot — see Magen Avraham in siman katan 3, and it is evident from his words that specifically [applies to] a corner that has no size for wrapping; and even though they forbade in the Gemara even if it holds an ammah by an ammah, nevertheless for him there is still no wrapping measure. But if the piece had measure to wrap in, one may attach it to another garment even with its tzitzis; but Taz in siman katan 3 disagrees with this and forbids, and so it appears in Levush and Darkei Halacha.

(6) With the tzitzis — but without the tzitzis, one may attach it if it holds at least three fingerbreadths by three, and afterward tie tzitzis on it; and from all the rest of the garment one need not untie at all. And specifically if before the corner was attached to it, it too was bar chiyuva — for example, the corner was not torn from it in a circle; but if it was torn from it in a circle and became a three-cornered [garment] and was exempt from tzitzis, one must untie all the tzitzis and place them anew after it becomes four-cornered, lest it be taaseh velo min ha'asuy.

(7) On another garment — but if he wishes to attach the corner to the garment from which it was torn, even if it tore completely and nothing remained in the attachment, one need not untie the tzitzis from it, and it is not invalidated because of taaseh velo min ha'asuy, since the beginning of its making on this garment was in a valid state. And all this if the garment did not become three-cornered at the time of tearing — and likewise in siman katan 6. And also specifically if the corner holds at least three by three, as shortly in seif 4 — this is the view of Shulchan Aruch and Magen Avraham in siman katan 3; but Taz in siman katan 3 rules there is no difference whether attaching to another garment or this one itself — everything is under taaseh velo min ha'asuy, and one should be strict, and so ruled Darkei Halacha.
