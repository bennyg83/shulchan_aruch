“It was not.” From Beis Yosef’s words it implies the main reason is because of “you shall make” and not from preexisting material—for from the moment the corner was cut the tzitzis became invalid, and even if he sewed it back it is a derivative product. But it is difficult: why do we rule that if the tallis tore outside three handbreadths he may sew, as written shortly, seif 4? Therefore he said this corner was not from this garment at the time of making—then the beginning of its making was in an invalid state; not so when he sews it into the tallis itself, which is permitted because the beginning of its making on this garment was while valid—likewise in the Semag and Nachalas Yisrael, and likewise in seif 3.
