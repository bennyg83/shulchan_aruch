Not to — Taz. Here, to permit not placing them on another garment is from Tosafos Shabbos 22a s.v. Rav Amar; they wrote thus for two resolutions there, and it is necessary from what we were not taught the novelty of Shmuel permitting from garment, and the power of permission is preferable; also from the conclusion permitting from garment to garment and he did not say permitting as with kindling — see there — we learn that not placing them is forbidden. The reason for Rashi Menachos 41b s.v. lest is degrading the tallit (degrading the tzitzis is not there, for he places on another); and for Tosafos there s.v. Rav — canceling a tallit from mitzvah like a mezuzah from a house is forbidden (and a house has no relevance to degradation) (and Rashi may be said because of demons, as Beit Yosef wrote in the second chapter of Shabbos). Practical difference between the reasons: if he wants to place other tzitzis on this tallit — for Tosafos it is permitted, for the tallit does not leave mitzvah; and Rashi may be said that in degrading tzitzis it is nevertheless permitted, for there is no degradation here since he intends to make others nicer; so too Levush for this reason "to me and adorn Him." However, since Taz wrote one who unties his tzitzis — behold to destroy them and tear them is forbidden, for he destroys a mitzvah fit for another garment; however if they are only enough that they are not fit for another garment and to tie them with a new face it is permitted (see siman 12 Taz 3). But Levush wrote if he wants to make nicer and discard them, this is permitted — implying one may disqualify them again. I saw in Eliyah Rabbah letter 1 he brought in the name of Perishah that destroying them is forbidden, and apparently from Taz.

It appears to me that what Shmuel permits from garment to garment, and we rule accordingly, is only when tzitzis are not available in the city; if tzitzis are available one may not permit tzitzis from garment to garment, since one can buy other tzitzis cheaply and easily. From this I would say that Shmuel holds a box-cloth is obligated in tzitzis; therefore as Tosafos wrote Menachos 41b s.v. Rav Amar — if he has only enough for one tallit, better to give on that which he covers with it — then we have no permission when tzitzis are available in the city to take for free from a mitzvah tallit, since some say tallit is an obligation, and even if we do not rule thus one may not cancel a tallit from mitzvah unless tzitzis are not available, as I wrote. For Shmuel one need not ask how he places a tallit without tzitzis — say as Mordechai siman 13: a tallit has no prohibition except to make tzitzis, and if he has no sand as Shabbos 13 in Magen Avraham letter 8. Incidentally regarding mezuzah — one may not take it from there, and he did not distinguish whether it was fixed in another house or not, implying forbidden in any case; and as I wrote there, Beit Yosef's second chapter of Tosafos Shabbos is because of demons, when people dwell there as Beit Yosef wrote; and as I wrote in responsa — harmful to people dwelling there; but if he seals the house and no one dwells there, one may say permitted. However if he has only one mezuzah and none is available in the city to buy, certainly permitted to take from there where he wants to dwell; but if so, what do Tosafos ask on Shabbos from mezuzah — he wants to take thus, and on tallit necessarily none available to buy, for otherwise he violates the positive command for Shmuel's box-cloth. I will further explain: here the Mechaber ruled as Shmuel permitting garment to garment, as Be'er HaGolah wrote here and in siman 474 the Mechaber also ruled as Shmuel — Taz there; but Be'er HaGolah there wrote as Rav, as we said because of degrading mitzvah — if so why rule here as Shmuel? We rule as Rav in prohibitions unless for the reason Abaye said "all matters..." see Shabbos 22a — if so likewise here; in our novellae we extended on Shabbos there and here we should not extend; and I will explain this in 474, for one may say he ruled there as Rav because of the Gemara's question "kindling performs mitzvah" etc.
