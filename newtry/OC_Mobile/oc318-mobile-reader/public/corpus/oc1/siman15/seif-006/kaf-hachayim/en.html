6) And if one thread was severed, even though enough remained in it for a knot — and if it had remained on the corner it would have been kosher to place it on another garment — Maran wrote in the Beit Yosef that it is invalid; see there. Birkei Yosef letter 3 wrote: "And although Maran here in Shulchan Arukh omitted this law and it implies somewhat that he is doubtful — the law is a true law, and Maran's explanation is true and established; and how much more so now that Rabbeinu Bezalel met with it in the name of a gloss that he is an early rabbi, and so are proven the words of Rashi and the Rosh and Rabbeinu Yerucham, who wrote that the threads must be whole; and the Rif also brought it; and so Rabbi Birkat HaZevach, that the law is true; and also the Taz, Magen Avraham, and Eliyah Rabbah wrote it" — end quote; see there. It seems that when Maran wrote that it is invalid to place it on another garment, that is at the outset, as implied from the wording of Rabbeinu Bezalel that Birkei Yosef brought, and Birkei Yosef compared Rabbi Bezalel's wording to Maran's view; and so it implies from the Taz siman 12 subsection 3, and so Magen Avraham on this siman subsection 1 explicitly, that it is not forbidden to place it on another garment except at the outset; so too Etz Yosef letter 2, and so Rabbi Zalman letter 2, Pri Megadim siman 12 in Mishbetzot Zahav letter 3, Shaarei Tzion 109b [and what Shaarei Tzion wrote there, that if enough for a knot did not remain Magen Avraham validates after the fact — see what Zachor LeYisrael part 3 letter 18 wrote on it], Beit Yaakov letter 2, Sh'tilei Zeitim siman 12 letter 7, and so from the words of Chayei Adam general rule 11 letter 20 — and not as Machatzit HaShulchan wrote.
