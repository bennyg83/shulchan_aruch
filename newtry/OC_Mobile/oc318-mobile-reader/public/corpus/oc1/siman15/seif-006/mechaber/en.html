One who sews a piece of cloth into the corners of the tallis, and similarly what people sew around the hole of the tzitzis — if the tallis is silk and sewn with white silk thread, there is concern per Rashi that there be no sewing below three [fingerbreadths from the edge] nor above a thumb-knot [from the hole]. Rema: Likewise wherever one sews with thread of the tzitzis species we worry one might take that thread for tzitzis strings (Taz 46 citing Hagahos Maimoniyos). .
