One who sews a piece of fabric onto the corners of the tallis, and likewise the customary stitching around the hole through which the tzitzis pass: if the tallis is made of silk and he sewed it with white silk thread, there is reason for concern according to Rashi that there be no stitching below three fingerbreadths and above the distance of a thumb joint. Gloss: The same applies wherever one sews with thread of the same material as the tzitzis, because we are concerned that one may take that thread and use it as a tzitzis string. Terumas HaDeshen, siman 46, in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos.
