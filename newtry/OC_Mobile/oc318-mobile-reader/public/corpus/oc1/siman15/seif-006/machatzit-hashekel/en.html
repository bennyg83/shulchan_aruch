(Sq. 6) To sew, etc. — one could say that for a silk tallis they are not so strict, etc. And according to what is written in seif 5, and if it is of other kinds where the custom is to sew with threads of that same kind — he may not sew, according to Rashi; according to Rabbeinu Yonah, tzitzis must be of the tallis's color — if he sews with that same kind and also that same color; and for us, who require white tzitzis — it must be said that he sews with that same kind, but the color of the threads [must be] white.
