[6] Rashi explains that we are concerned, etc. According to this, if the tear is outside three fingerbreadths, one may sew it, because that is not the place for tzitzis. Levush. At first glance, even within three fingerbreadths, if the tear is closer to the edge than the distance of a thumb joint, sewing should likewise be permitted, because that too is not the place for tzitzis, as stated at the end of the siman. It is somewhat difficult, however, because perhaps the garment will tear further and pull the tzitzis below the distance of a thumb joint, which is valid as explained above in siman 11, seif 10. Perhaps we are not concerned to that extent.
