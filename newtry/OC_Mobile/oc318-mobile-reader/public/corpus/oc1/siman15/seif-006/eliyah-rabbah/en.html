[6] And Rashi explained that we are concerned, etc. According to this, if it tore outside three fingerbreadths, he may sew it, for there is no concern for this, for that is not a place of tzitzit — Levush. Apparently, even within three and within the thumb-knot from the edge of the corner, it is also permitted to sew it, for that too is not a place of tzitzit, as below at the end of the siman. But it is somewhat difficult: the concern lest it tear and they draw the tzitzit below the thumb-knot, which is valid, as above siman 11 section 10; and it is possible that we are not so concerned.
