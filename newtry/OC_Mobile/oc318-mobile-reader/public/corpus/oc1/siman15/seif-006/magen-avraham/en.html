“With white.” This is from the words of the Tur HaAruch and Ri ibn Shuib, and follows his approach that wrote we do not practice making tzitzis from other colors, as siman 9, seif 5, says; but Rabbeinu Yerucham ruled there to make the tzitzis from the garment’s color—so it is difficult why here he wrote white silk, for even from other colors it should be forbidden. One could say he mentioned the usual case: people are accustomed to make white garments.
“Below three.” But when the stitching is above three handbreadths and below the thumb knot, there is no concern he will take it for tzitzis, as siman 11 says; around the hole it is forbidden to sew with white thread (Tur)—see seif 4. It appears to me the reason people sew cloth patches on the corners of the tallis, even though some forbid as end of seif 11, they do so because most garments, even new ones, have stitching within three handbreadths, and for Rabbi Yochanan it is considered like fully cut; therefore they place a three-by-three patch with no stitching in it.
