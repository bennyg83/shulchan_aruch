(22) Of silk — if of wool it was already explained there is no concern at all, and it is permitted to have sewing even within three fingerbreadths.

(23) With silk thread — the same if the tallit is of other species: only if the thread is of the same species itself; but if the thread is of another species one may be lenient, since he certainly cannot take that thread for tzitzis, for it only exempts in its own species.

(24) White — if it is dyed also there is no concern at all lest he take it for tzitzis, since we do not customarily make dyed tzitzis, as written siman 9 seif 5.

(25) Below three, etc. — for there is the place of tzitzis; but when the sewing is above three and below the thumb-knot there is no concern lest he take it for tzitzis, as written siman 11. Around the hole it is forbidden to sew with white thread; in our wool tallits there is no need to be strict at all, as above.

(26) In any place — meaning if it tore and something of it remained, as such a case explained above in note 6.
