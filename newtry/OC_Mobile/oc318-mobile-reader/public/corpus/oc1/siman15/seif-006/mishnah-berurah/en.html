(22) Of silk. If it is wool, it has already been explained that there is no concern at all, and stitching may be present even within three fingerbreadths.
(23) With silk thread. The same applies if the tallis is made of other materials: the concern exists specifically when the thread is of that same material. If the thread is of another material, one may be lenient, because it certainly cannot exempt the garment except when used with its own material.
(24) White. If it is colored, there is likewise no concern that it will be used for tzitzis, because our custom is not to make colored tzitzis, as stated in siman 9, seif 5.
(25) Below three fingerbreadths, etc. That is the place for tzitzis. If the stitching is above three fingerbreadths or below the distance of a thumb joint, there is no concern that one will use the thread for tzitzis, as stated in siman 11. Around the hole itself one may not sew with white thread. With our wool talleisim, however, there is no need for concern at all, as explained above.
(26) Everywhere. Meaning, where it tore and any amount remained connected, and similar cases explained above in seif 5.
