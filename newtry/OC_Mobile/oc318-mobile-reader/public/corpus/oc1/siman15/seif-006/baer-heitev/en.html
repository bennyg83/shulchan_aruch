On the corners of the tallit: The reason they are accustomed to sew pieces of cloth on the corners of the tallit, even though some forbid at end of siman 11, is because most garments, even new ones, have stitching within three fingerbreadths, and according to Rabbi Akiva it is as if severed; therefore they place a piece of three by three that has no stitching—Magen Avraham.

From the thumb-joint knot: For there is the place of the tzitzit.
