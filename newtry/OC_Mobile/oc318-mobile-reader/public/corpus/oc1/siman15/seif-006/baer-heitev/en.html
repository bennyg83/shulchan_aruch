At the corners of the tallis. The reason for the custom of sewing pieces of fabric onto the corners of the tallis, even though some forbid this at the end of siman 11, is that most garments, even new ones, have stitching within three fingerbreadths. According to Rav Amram, this is considered as though severed. Therefore, they place a piece measuring three by three fingerbreadths that contains no stitching. Magen Avraham.
