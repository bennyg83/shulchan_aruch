“He punctures.” And no thumb-knot measure remains; see siman 11, seif 10.

“He shall not sew.” Specifically when the tear is above the thumb knot, as written in seif 6.

“Were torn.” Meaning it tore entirely.

“There is room for doubt.” Because for Rabbi Meir it is considered like fully cut and no thumb knot remains; what Beis Yosef wrote for all the explanations is according to Nachalas Yisrael and the Rosh, but for Rashi stitching is a connection.
