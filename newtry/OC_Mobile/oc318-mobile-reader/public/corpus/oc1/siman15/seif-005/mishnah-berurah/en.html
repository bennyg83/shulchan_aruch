(18) From a hole — this deals with where they did not sew, and it teaches us that even though a full thumb-knot was not left, and as written above siman 11 seif 10 that we do not require this measure except at the time of making. And if he wants to sew the tear, then if the garment is wool he may sew it with threads of other species and need not untie the tzitzis from the corner and reattach after sewing, since the corner was not invalidated; and if the garment is of other species it is forbidden to sew it with threads of the garment's species according to Rashi, lest he leave threads for tzitzis, and even though there are tzitzis there we do not distinguish but take threads of another species; and better not to sew with white thread only with dyed thread, since we do not customarily make tzitzis other than white, therefore we need not decree lest he leave sewing thread for tzitzis.

(19) Kosher for all — for Rashi, since the garment is wool there is no concern he will leave threads of other species for tzitzis; and for R. Amram, since something remained it was not canceled from garment status, and as in the previous seif, and all the more what was written in the previous seif is kosher here since tzitzis were placed after sewing.

(20) He may not sew — meaning in that same species, but in another species one may be lenient, and better with dyed thread, as above in note 18.

(21) And if torn, etc. — meaning torn entirely from the hole to the end of the edge; therefore there is room to doubt that even if the garment is wool and sewn with threads of other species, which for Rashi is certainly permitted, nevertheless perhaps for Rav Amram it is invalid since nothing remained — as explained in the previous seif per the first opinion it is as if cut off and sewing does not help even if he wants to place tzitzis afterward; and perhaps here is preferable since the corner is whole on both sides and only cut in the middle — this is the Shulchan Aruch's doubt; see Taz who disagrees and rules no doubt here for all, if he placed tzitzis after sewing it is kosher. Nevertheless better before sewing to place under the corner a piece of cloth three by three and satisfy all; and if impossible one may be lenient as Taz, since otherwise we wrote in note 15 that the later authorities hold sewing counts as attachment and helps even if the corner was torn completely from its place.
