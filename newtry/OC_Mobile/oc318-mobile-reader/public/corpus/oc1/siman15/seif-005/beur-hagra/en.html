Seif 5, "If it tore, etc.": That is, he did not sew it, even though a thumb-joint knot did not remain, as I wrote above in siman 11, seif 10—Beit Yosef.

All hold, etc.: For per Rashi it is valid because it is wool; per Rosh, because any amount remained; and per Nimukei Yosef, because it preceded the sewing.

He should not sew: See seif 6—"that he should not, etc."

And if it tore and he sewed it, etc.: That is, no amount at all remained.

"There is room to doubt." This applies according to all the explanations in the Beit Yosef. That is to say: per the Rosh and Rashi, even though if any amount remained it is valid per the Rosh, as above, and here the entire garment is intact, nevertheless the place where the tzitzit are inserted is considered as if it were cut. Likewise according to Rashi, perhaps because of the concern mentioned above it is invalid even after the fact. This is also explicit in the words of the Beit Yosef there, where he wrote: "And if it tore and some of it remained, etc., according to Rashi, etc.," because of the concern "lest he leave, etc."; otherwise not. See Magen Avraham, subsection 12. There is a scribal error in his words, and it should read: "This is according to Rashi and the Rosh; but per the Nimukei Yosef, etc."
