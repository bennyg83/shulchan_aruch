(Sq. 5) Sewing that was not spun for its sake — see siman 14, seif 2. Truly Rashi gave the reason because of talmudah; but Tosafos Menachos 40b, s.v. shema yikare, wrote on Rashi and thus: he should have listed [the reason] because we require either spinning or hanging for its sake, but talmudah does not apply, according to what I will explain below regarding making them from thorns — end quote. Therefore truly Magen Avraham wrote the reason is because we require spinning for its sake; and even though Tosafos also wrote because hanging for its sake is required, Magen Avraham omitted the reason of requiring hanging for its sake. And on this he hinted in what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 14 seif 2, meaning that there two opinions were brought whether hanging for its sake is required; therefore it is better for him to say because spinning for its sake is required, for in spinning there is no dispute that it is required for its sake. And even though what Tosafos rejected from Rashi — that he wrote because of talmudah regarding making them from thorns on 42b — this was because of their difficulty there, for the master of the court of Rav said that making from thorns is invalid, and Rav has no [invalidation] because of talmudah; and thus Tosafos there s.v. hakotzim, etc., explained in the commentary because of talmudah, and it seems to me that what is done afterward is the making — for Rav himself said he hung them and afterward cut the heads of their threads — they are valid, for that is his making, etc. — hence the reason we require hanging for its sake — end quote; and this is specifically according to Rav. But for us, who do not hold like Rav in this, but rather hold that if he hung them and afterward cut [the threads] they are invalid, as above siman 11 seif 13. Nevertheless it is explained above, specifically when he had already wound one coil and tied one knot at least and afterward cut — invalid because of talmudah; not so here, where he has not yet wound a coil or tied [a knot], and the Tosafos who challenged from Rav is preferable challenging. And so it appears from Tosafos 40b s.v. shema yikare, etc., above, that they challenged thus lechol hadeios, as above.
