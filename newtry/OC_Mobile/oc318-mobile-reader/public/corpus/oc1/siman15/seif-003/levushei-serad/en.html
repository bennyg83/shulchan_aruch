{Rama: Subsection 4} When he begins to sew — that is, when he begins to sew less than three fingerbreadths, it is still not a connection, as explained in section 4; nevertheless this corner at the place of sewing was nullified and it becomes three corners. And although its making began valid and the invalidity is not in the tzitzit themselves, here, since it has wrap measure, it is as another garment, and "you shall make and not from what is already made" applies with the Rosh's reason combined, as in the previous letter. This contradicts Magen Avraham from "outside three he may sew"; and although we wrote that with wrap measure it is stricter than outside three, nevertheless he brought proof: from "outside three" it implies whether little or much outside three — if there is wrap measure it is nevertheless valid; so too dividing into two, joining is permitted. Another contradiction from siman 10 subsection 3: although he sewed, the corners were not nullified because he will certainly untie the stitches — we follow the end even now after the end; all the more so here, when he begins to sew he begins with intent to sew the whole garment, and it is as if he sewed it all — we follow the end. Analyze well.
