There is no — Taz. According to Rashba in a responsum the questioner asked: a tallit torn in two — is it tolmei marei? One should not say because he takes two tzitzis and only two tzitzis remained and were invalidated — we require four — this is error and confusion; if so go support making tzitzis, for one cannot make all at once. Rather the question is from the moment of tearing until less than three handbreadths remains of this corner, for the corner in the middle is more than three etc., and the second corner is still attached and is a three-by-three, and he placed on a three-by-three and completed — invalid as explained siman 10 seif 5 because of tolmei marei; and he answered this is not tolmei marei, for otherwise the baraita Menachos 41a "a tallit torn outside three shall sew" etc. and veShoin sheMeivi tekheles and hangs it, and need not untie the first three tzitzis on the three hanging corners — when part tore one corner was canceled and only three-by-three corners remain, and it would be tolmei marei (and possibly he also means the corner tore completely and is not attached at all; in any case torn in a circle that only three-by-three remains) — rather we learn such a case is not tolmei marei, since at the time of making they were in validity and afterward one corner tore; when he returns and sews the corner and makes tzitzis, the first ones become valid, for it was not an invalid act in themselves — similar to siman 11:6 he placed tzitzis on tzitzis — kosher; if he cut one of them, since it was with intent of making tzitzis it was, and he cut one — the first or second become valid; so too here. Taz holds Rashba's proof also from cubit by cubit with tekheles — behold without tekheles sew, and the first tzitzis on the tallit are not invalid even though part of sewing canceled the corner — rather we learn kosher in such a case.

Again I saw Rashba's responsum is explained plainly; Levush understood the questioner's question: since two tzitzis were cut they were invalidated, and when he makes another two it is two first ones tolmei marei; the sides are a question of cubit by cubit that has tallit measure (so the questioner holds) — nevertheless the tzitzis in it are invalid since part of them tore — tolmei marei; or there he wants to attach to another and the reason is al kenafei begadim as Rashi's plain words there, and this was not at the time of making; Rashba answered they are not invalidated; proof: if it has no tekheles, sew onto a torn tallit and three tzitzis that remained there are valid even though one tore (also torn outside three shall sew, and as Nimukei Yosef in Raavad's name — also proof); Levush concluded and did not detract from a new tallit on which they place one and two and pause a long time between and complete — see there. If this is the questioner's intent and Rashba's answer, he was asked when one tzitzis was cut and the tallit was whole; and it appears his question was: since the tallit tore and now two new corners were renewed that were not there at the time of making the first tzitzis.

And behold Darkhei Moshe in siman 14 wrote (in the Fuerth edition in Pe'er because in Darkhei Moshe by the Tur Berlin edition I did not find it): Rashba wrote in responsum siman 217 — a tallit that has four tzitzis and he divided it in two — permitted to return and join it (Taz omitted the words "to return and join it") and it is not tolmei marei — end quote; this is the Rashba responsum Beit Yosef brought here; because it was difficult for him how to disqualify since two tzitzis went from them — go support making tzitzis on tallit as stated — therefore he explained another intent in the questioner: he asked if permitted to return and join — shall we say two tzitzis of part one are rejected from the other part, for behold it has four corners by itself and likewise the reverse; proof from cubit by cubit that has tallit measure — so the questioner holds — nevertheless he will not bring cubit by cubit with tzitzis and attach to tallit because the tzitzis in it were rejected from this tallit that has four corners of its own; or perhaps there the reason is al kenafei begadim and this corner etc., unlike when he returns and sews and joins that tallit — on this Rashba answered permitted; therefore from Raavad's silence here to Mechaber in seif 3 likewise it may be said there is no tolmei marei when he returns and joins them together; on this Taz wondered why it should be kosher thus, since all two tzitzis of part one are not fit for the second part when separated — one may not join them like cubit by cubit; Taz holds cubit by cubit has tallit measure (see Magen Avraham and Tzon Kadashim Menachos 41a); also al kenafei begadim — main reason is tolmei marei: this tzitzis on cubit-by-cubit corner was not fit for this tallit that tore — so too tolmei marei here when he joins. What Taz wrote — those that were in the piece taken from the garment were canceled from there — we were in a cut cubit by cubit with tzitzis, canceled from their tallit; likewise this tallit that tore — so too certainly one should disqualify in dividing in two that he not join with one tzitzis to one as stated; Rashba's intent that each be by itself and two tzitzis in each part were not invalidated — on this he brings proof well from cubit by cubit: without tzitzis attach to tallit and three tzitzis were not invalidated. Nevertheless it is clear not as Bach held, permitted to join a piece or two parts when permitting two tzitzis from part one — and examine as I will write more in letter 4 if God wills.
