“It does not have.” The Bach wrote it is invalid when sewn onto another garment, for when he begins sewing the corner is nullified and each piece has only three corners, and when he sews the whole back it is a derivative product—end quote. This is not clear to me—it seems valid, for we explicitly say in the Gemara that if it tore outside three he may sew, as written in seif 4; what is the difference if a piece tore off and he sews it, or he splits a cloak in two? Likewise Beis Yosef in the Rashba’s name—and the reason that when he begins sewing they become one garment; moreover we say in siman 10, seif 3, that even if he sewed the corners of his tallis it is not exempt, which implies sewing does not nullify the corner—and Nachalas Yisrael; therefore it appears to me one is permitted to sew it.
