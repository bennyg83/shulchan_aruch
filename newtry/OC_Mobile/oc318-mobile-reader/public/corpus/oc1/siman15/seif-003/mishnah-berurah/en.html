(8) And in each part, etc. — but if not every part has enough to wrap, as is common in our tallit katan — when it is divided in two there is no wrapping measure in them — the mitzvah of tzitzis is immediately canceled from them; and if afterward he wishes to reunite one part with its fellow or with another piece, he must untie all the tzitzis from the beginning. And if one part has wrapping measure and one does not, one need only untie from the part that lacks it, and afterward tie anew after the parts are joined together.

(9) There is no issue, etc. — for the tzitzis that remain, there is no invalidity on them, since they have enough wrapping measure; therefore if he wishes he can attach to that half a piece from another garment and make new tzitzis on the new corners; and surely he can make from the half a complete tallis — that is, make two tzitzis on the renewed corners. And if he wishes to join the parts that were divided to one another — some say he must first untie the tzitzis from one part, then sew them, and after sewing tie tzitzis on it, for if he does not untie it falls under taaseh velo min ha'asuy; but most later authorities are lenient and hold this is not talmudah at all; nevertheless it is good to be strict ab initio.
