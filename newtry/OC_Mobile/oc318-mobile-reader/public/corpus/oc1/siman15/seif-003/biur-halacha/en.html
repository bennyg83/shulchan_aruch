A properly fringed tallis that was divided, etc. If it was originally so large that it could not be worn in that form, it is possible that the tzitzis remain invalid even after the garment was divided into two, because of “you shall make,” and not something already made, according to the authorities who exempted the original garment before it was divided. See above, siman 10, seif 6. So writes Peri Megadim. See also Artzos HaChayim.
