A tallit properly fringed that they divided, etc. — And if it was initially so large that it was impossible to wear it thus, it is possible that the tzitzit are invalidated even after they divided the garment in two, because of "you shall make and not from what is already made," according to those who exempt it from the beginning before it was divided. See above in siman 10, section 6; so too the Pri Megadim; see Biur Halacha.
