And not from what is already made: For the tzitzit that remained have no invalidity; therefore he may make two tzitziyot on renewed corners, or if he wishes he may attach to that half-piece a piece from another garment and make tzitzit on it; but he may not attach the second part that was cut from it to the first part and have it as at first, because the tzitzit that were on the second part that was cut are nullified and it becomes ta'aseh velo min ha'asui; rather he must untie the tzitzit from the second part, sew it, and afterward make tzitzit anew on the second part, for it is like another new garment—Taz. And Bach forbids attaching another garment, even new, to that half-piece; rather he should make the two tzitziyot on another side of that same half-piece, that is, in the place of the cut that was cut from it. And Taz disagrees with him; see there. And in Pri Ha'aretz it is permitted even to return and sew them as at the beginning, and there is no ta'aseh velo min ha'asui in this; see there.
