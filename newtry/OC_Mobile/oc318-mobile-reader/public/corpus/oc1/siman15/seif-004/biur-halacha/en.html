With wool threads — And a linen tallit for which he made a corner of leather or hemp, into which wool tzitzit are placed, and the corner tore within three [fingerbreadths] — it is permitted to repair it with hemp, for everyone knows that this [repair] does not exempt a linen tallit [Pri Megadim; see there further].

Kosher — See in Mishnah Berurah what I wrote: some are stringent regarding the old tzitzit; this is the opinion of the Taz, who is stringent per the view of Tosafot. Know that according to them, if the strip of the garment was severed — as long as three fingerbreadths did not remain at its end along the length of the garment — it is as if it was entirely severed, and the old tzitzit are invalidated; they must be affixed anew after the sewing.
