(10) Within three fingerbreadths — but above three fingerbreadths one may sew it lechol hadeios, for according to Rashi surely there is no tzitzis place there, as siman 11 above; and no concern he will take it for tzitzis. And according to Rav Amram Gaon it has garment status. And even if that corner tore completely from the garment with its tzitzis — nevertheless one may join the corner and the tzitzis is valid; and Taz disagrees and rules one must untie the tzitzis from this corner and place them anew after the sewing, and one should be strict — and likewise above in siman katan 7.

(11) With wool threads — except with other materials; and everyone knows other materials do not exempt [with wool threads], so he will not come to attach that thread. And likewise one may be lenient also with other materials if sewn with another species.

(12) Because it tore, etc. — likewise if ab initio at the time of making the garment one sewed a piece of cloth less than three [fingerbreadths] from the garment edge, whether in length or width, and tied tzitzis on that piece — invalid according to him, for it is like one who is fully cut off, even if he sewed it to the garment; and if he ties tzitzis above that sewn piece, the tzitzis will be distant from the garment edge more than three fingerbreadths and invalid; therefore he should then sew under the corner a three-by-three garment piece — see below in siman katan 16.

(13) And if he acted, etc. — meaning even if he made new tzitzis on that corner piece after sewing it to the tallis, nevertheless it does not exempt the tallis, for sewing is not considered a connection here, and it is still considered like fully cut off if the corner was torn completely from the garment from the beginning.

(14) Any amount is valid — meaning even the tzitzis that were on it at the time it tore; and some are strict in this and hold the old ones become invalid and must be placed anew after sewing — see Biur Halacha.

(15) And some say that according to Rav Amram, etc. — hold that sewing is considered a connection and only invalidates tzitzis that were on it at the time he sewed, because of taaseh velo min ha'asuy; and see Taz who disagrees with Shulchan Aruch and wrote that all agree that if after sewing one tied tzitzis on it, it is valid — so also Bach, and Shiray Kenesses Hagedolah and Gra, and so agreed Aruch HaShulchan; but nevertheless to satisfy the first opinion one should sew under the corner a piece of tallis that has three by three in it.

(16) He satisfied all of them — therefore it is customary to sew on each corner of the tallit a piece of whole cloth three by three, because in many garments it is found that even when they are new there is sewing within three fingerbreadths, and there is invalidity per R. Yehuda per the first opinion, and as above in note 12.

(17) Where it is possible — and where it is not possible we rely that R. Yehuda's view is primary, and also that sewing is considered attachment.
