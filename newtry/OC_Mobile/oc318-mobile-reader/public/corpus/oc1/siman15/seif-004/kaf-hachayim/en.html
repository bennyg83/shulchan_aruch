4) There, it is permitted to untie tzitzit, etc. — that is, specifically when there are no ready-made tzitzit in the city; but if there are ready-made tzitzit, one may not untie tzitzit from garment to garment, since one can buy others cheaply and easily — Pri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav letter 2, Lev Chaim siman 95, Siddur Beit Yaakov letter 5, Pitchei Olam there. And further the Pri Megadim wrote in Mishbetzot Zahav siman 19 letter 1 another reason, in order to be concerned also for the one who says a box-vessel requires tzitzit — see there; and in our words to that place, letter 2.

4) And that which they permit from garment to garment — we require that the other garment be ready to affix them on it, for we are concerned for negligence; so wrote Machatzit HaShekel to Mahara Nachum, in the words of Menachem in Hagahot Tosefet letter 1.
