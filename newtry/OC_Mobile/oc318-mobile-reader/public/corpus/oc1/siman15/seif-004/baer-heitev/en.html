With wool threads: but with other types, and everyone knows that other types do not exempt with wool; therefore he should not combine that thread. See Magen Avraham.

And if he makes tzitzit on it—that is, even new ones after he sewed it, it does not exempt the tallit, for according to those who rule [strictly] it is considered as if severed; such is the view of the Rosh. And some say he holds that only tzitzit that were on it at the time he sewed it become invalid, etc.; this is the view of Nimukei Yosef. And Taz disagrees with Beit Yosef and wrote that this is not the Rosh's view, and all agree on this that if after he sewed he placed tzitzit on it, it is valid; see there. And so too in Shiurei Knesset HaGedolah and Kelalbo.
