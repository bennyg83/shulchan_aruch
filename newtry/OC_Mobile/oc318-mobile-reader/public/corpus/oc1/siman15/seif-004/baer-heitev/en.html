With wool strings. This refers only to strings of other materials. Everyone knows that other materials do not exempt a wool garment, so one will not join that thread to the tzitzis. See Magen Avraham.
And if he made tzitzis upon it. Meaning, even if he attached new tzitzis after sewing it, they do not exempt the tallis, because it is considered as though severed. This is the view of Rosh. The alternative view, which maintains that only the tzitzis present at the time of sewing are invalid, is the view of Nimukei Yosef. Taz disputes Beit Yosef and writes that this is not Rosh's view. According to him, all agree that if he attached the tzitzis after sewing, it is valid. See there. Shiurei Knesses HaGedolah and Kol Bo write the same.
