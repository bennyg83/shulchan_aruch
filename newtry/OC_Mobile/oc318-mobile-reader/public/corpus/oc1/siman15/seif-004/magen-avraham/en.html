“The stitching.” That it was not spun for its sake; see siman 14, seif 2.

“To sew.” Everyone knows that other kinds of thread do not exempt with wool, which implies that where their custom is to sew with wool, even with other materials it is forbidden to sew—a decree on account of wool. It is difficult: in seif 6 it wrote specifically when sewing with white silk thread, which implies with other materials sewing is permitted and we do not decree on account of silk. One could say that with a silk tallis they do not prohibit so strongly since some hold one is obligated only rabbinically; still one could say with strain that even where their custom is to sew with wool, sewing with other kinds is permitted.

“Do not exempt.” Likewise the Rosh: even tzitzis made afterward are invalid, for it is considered like fully cut; see siman 10, seifim 3 and 6.

“That he sewed it.” Because of derivative manufacture from preexisting material.
