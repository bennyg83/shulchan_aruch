If the tallis tore within three fingerbreadths of the corner edge one may not sew it — Rashi explained we worry a sewing thread might remain and he would add seven strings for tzitzis on it; for this reason even any tear one does not sew. Accordingly a wool tallis torn within three [of the edge] may be sewn nowadays when people do not sew with wool thread. Rav Amram explained the reason: torn within three lacks garment status as if absent — though sewing makes it “cut off”; if one ties tzitzis on it it does not exempt the tallis. On this explanation any remnant after tearing may be sewn. Some say for Rav Amram only tzitzis tied at the time of sewing are invalid; if after sewing one tied tzitzis it is valid — a God-fearing person surpasses all where possible.
