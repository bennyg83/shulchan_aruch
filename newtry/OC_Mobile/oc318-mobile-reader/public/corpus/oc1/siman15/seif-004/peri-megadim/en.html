And Rav Amram — Taz. Here according to Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch there are four explanations. (1) Rashi — decree lest protrude etc. (veShoin for Rashi has no relevance here; he brings there a baraita the halachah follows R. Meir in his decrees, implying even against the many — earlier there mem 2: linen sheet with tzitzis forbidden rabbinically lest he tear his sheet within three and Rashi explained lest protrude from sewing thread; therefore rabbanan of R. Meir — for R. Meir one does not sew within three and nothing to decree; only rabbanan who sew within three and did not decree lest protrude — in a doubtful rabbinical prohibition they did not make a fence for a doubtful rabbinical prohibition as stated several times; unlike kilayim where they made a fence lest protrude.) (2) Tosafos mem 2 s.v. lest challenged Rashi's explanation (see Bach and Tzon Kadashim in the reading "even rabbanan" and understand): if so Rav Huna who said below 41b dalet within corner measure four distant four — how interpret baraita outside three and within two? Rather they explained not as Rashi who explained three and within three as garment width, but along garment length — length and width explained Magen Avraham siman 11 letter 16: if a strip tore from garment with tzitzis, if tear did not reach three to second garment edge still attached — three fingerbreadths considered attachment and tzitzis at tear time not invalidated; if three fingerbreadths not intact in length Rama shall not sew, as spread out; tzitzis then invalidated and even sew later tolmei marei; and Hakhamim sew and tzitzis then not invalidated since slightly attached counts as attached (and baraita should read reversed: Rama sew and Hakhamim not sew, for halachah follows R. Meir in decrees — and veShoin when he brings even cubit by cubit from elsewhere with tekheles it is tolmei marei; likewise Maharshal and veShoin if torn completely and sewed, tzitzis then invalidated). What said above mem 2 lest tear within three — apparently rabbanan who say not to dig; otherwise baraita challenges Beit Yosef; but Tosafos there s.v. lest wrote "now you have heard as R. Meir" — implies reading Rama not sew is difficult (may be resolved — no place here). (3) Rosh in Raavad as Beit Yosef understood — not garment length but width (possibly between width and length but not as Tosafos — understand); as Rashi and not discussing strip severed whether still three fingerbreadths attached, only within three and outside three of corner measure; if severed within three without corner measure, even if only minority of garment severed, even sew later and make tzitzis later does not help — as cut off. If so veShoin has no explanation — what relation to dispute cut off or not and cubit by cubit because tolmei marei (explanation: no tallit measure or as Taz letter 3). Therefore whether entire strip torn completely or minority majority attached — no difference; only depends on width whether corner is on it. Taz asked strong question: if so mem 2 decree lest tear within three and tolmei marei — tolmei marei not relevant here only as cut off, and even tzitzis made later invalid as cut off (even if strained to say make remedy — leave tallit wider on face side more than three — not cut off and tolmei marei — still somewhat difficult). (4) Nimukei Yosef in Raavad as appears he mentioned corner etc. — teaches not as Tosafos; holds only tzitzis at tear time, but sewing helps afterward to place tzitzis anew; and veShoin language cubit by cubit from elsewhere is tolmei marei. Taz challenged Rosh-Raavad as Beit Yosef understood sewing does not help — if so why said with tekheles even to place afterward forbidden as cut off; and though cubit by cubit not as cut off, nevertheless since said even cubit by cubit with tekheles — behold to place after sewing even less than cubit and less than three permitted — yet as cut off.

Finally — our master Taz was challenged by the holy words of Maran HaMashbir in two matters: (1) he was lenient when strip torn completely since wider than three fingerbreadths — may sew with tzitzis hanging on strip — and there is none according to Tosafos — certainly forbidden (and one may not distinguish at all between corner from this garment or another — Magen Avraham letter 3 here). Also Rosh may be explained plainly as Tosafos in Raavad, and Nimukei Yosef may be explained without strain as Raavad in Tosafos; word corner means if three fingerbreadths still remain attached there is still a corner on this tallit. (2) What he was strict that sewing does not help — meaning sewing in any place helps; and Rosh who wrote we do not place tzitzis at that time, and veShoin shall not bring cubit by cubit — Magen Avraham letter 34: likewise that strip torn completely shall not join, even if it has tallit measure in his view letter 50 wondered on Darkhei Moshe siman 10; and per my simple view we removed all road markers and his words illuminate as I wrote.
