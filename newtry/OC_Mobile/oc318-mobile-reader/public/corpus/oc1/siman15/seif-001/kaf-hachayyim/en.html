1) [Se'if 1] It is permitted to remove tzitziyos, etc. This is permitted even from a new tallis for another new tallis, and certainly from an old tallis for a new one. Bach; Birkei Yosef, paragraphs 1 and 2. It is likewise permitted to remove tzitziyos from a tallis gadol and place them on a tallis katan. Archos Chaim, citing Responsa Beis Yehudah, Yoreh Deah, siman 29; Pischei Olam, paragraph 1. One may not, however, remove them from an adult's garment, whose obligation is D'Oraisa, and place them on a minor's garment, whose obligation is only rabbinic. The Malbim; Amudei HaShulchan on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 9, paragraph 20. Mishnah Berurah likewise writes this in the name of Archos Chaim, and Pischei Olam cites it there. See below, paragraph 13, for what we wrote in the name of Olas Tamid.
