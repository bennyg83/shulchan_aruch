<b>Whether one may untie <i>tzitzis</i> from one garment for another, and the law of a torn <i>tallis</i> — it contains six clauses.</b><br>One may untie <i>tzitzis</i> from this <i>tallis</i>
and put them on another <i>tallis</i>, but not leave them unattached on another garment. Rema: Specifically [when undoing] from a garment still obligated; but one may untie <i>tzitzis</i> from a dead person’s <i>tallis</i> (Mordechai; Tosafos Bava Metzia 22a). .
