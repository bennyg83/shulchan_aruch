Whether one may untie tzitzis from one garment for another, and the law of a torn tallis — it contains six clauses. One may untie tzitzis from this tallis
and put them on another tallis, but not leave them unattached on another garment. Rema: Specifically [when undoing] from a garment still obligated; but one may untie tzitzis from a dead person’s tallis (Mordechai; Tosafos Bava Metzia 22a). .
