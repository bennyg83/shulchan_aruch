And for our teachers. Taz: this is Rashi's language in Menachos 41a s.v. veShoin sheMeivi tekheles Mattir miBeged Acher etc. (and it was not placed for him that we teach permitting from garment to garment, for even according to Shmuel there it is not relevant here — neither the tolmei marei mentioned there nor al kenafei begadim for Rashi there). Taz explained tolmei marei does not apply at all here, but al kenafei begadim as the Mechaber wrote in seif 2 (and that is Rashi's explanation there) applies somewhat here: he possibly made tzitzis on kenafei begadim, and since he made four corners on a corner of another garment it would disqualify making on a corner of another garment — he teaches it is kosher.

Perishah wrote a novel law: if he hung long threads between two corners and tied only one tzitzis — knot with coil — even though the second is still only a plain insertion (as such a case in siman 11 seif 13 is kosher), here it is invalid since one tzitzis was already made with knot and coil, which invalidates the insertion on the second corner; this is a novelty in my view. The later authorities did not mention it; also Beit Yosef does not hold thus, as he wrote on the Tur, and therefore they omitted it in Shulchan Aruch.
