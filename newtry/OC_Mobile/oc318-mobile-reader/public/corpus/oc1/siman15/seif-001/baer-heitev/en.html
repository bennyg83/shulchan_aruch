On another tallit: Even from a new tallit, and certainly from an old tallit, it is permitted to untie [tzitzit] and place them on a new tallit, specifically when all the threads are whole; see above, siman 12, seif katan 2. And see what was written in Yad Aharon.

In another garment, no: For then there is a prohibition, for one demeans a tallit of a mitzvah for nothing. And if he wishes to make on it nicer tzitzit, it is permitted to take them off—Levush. And likewise if they were cut, even though they are valid according to law, he may untie them and make them complete—Magen Avraham. And if he wishes to make from the tallit a garment that will not have four corners, it is permitted to take them off. And after he untied the tzitzit, it is permitted to use the tallit for any use he wishes, provided it is not a demeaning use. See above, siman 20, seif katan 4; and see there.

Regarding an obligated person: Even if he is obligated only by rabbinic law—see there, small seif 3; his words are unclear.
