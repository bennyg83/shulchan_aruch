Upon another tallis. Even upon a new tallis, and all the more so upon an old tallis, it is permitted to remove them and place them upon a new tallis. This applies specifically when all the strings are whole. See above, siman 12, seif katan 2. See also Yad Aharon.
Not upon another garment. In that case it is forbidden because he needlessly degrades a tallis used for a mitzvah. If he wishes to replace them with more beautiful tzitzis, he may remove them. Levush. The same applies if the strings were severed, even though they remain valid according to the strict law; he may remove them and replace them with whole strings. Magen Avraham. If he wishes to alter the tallis into a garment that will no longer have four corners, he may remove the tzitzis. After removing the tzitzis, he may use the tallis for any purpose he wishes, provided that the use is not degrading. See below, siman 20, seif katan 4, and Olas Tamid.
One who is obligated. Even one who is obligated only D'Rabbanan. See Olas Tamid, seif katan 3, whose words are difficult.
