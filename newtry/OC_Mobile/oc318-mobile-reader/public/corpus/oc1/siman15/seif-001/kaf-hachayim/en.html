1) [Section 1] It is permitted to untie tzitzit, etc. Even from a new tallit to a new tallit, and all the more so from an old tallit to a new tallit — Bach. Birkei Yosef, letter 1 and letter 2. So too it is permitted to untie tzitzit from a large tallit to place them on a small tallit — Aruch HaShulchan in the name of Teshuvot Beit Yehudah, Yoreh De'ah siman 29, and they brought Pitchei Olam letter 1. But one may not untie from a garment of an adult, whose obligation is from the Torah, to place them on [that] of a minor, whose obligation is only rabbinic — five Levushei Mordechai, and they brought Amudei HaShulchan on Kitzur Shulchan Arukh siman 9 letter 20; so too Mishnah Berurah in the name of Aruch HaShulchan, and they brought Pitchei Olam there. And see below letter 13 what is written in the name of Etz Yosef.
