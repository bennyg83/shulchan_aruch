"That everyone" — see Taz, and it is implied in a loud voice saying Bar'khu, and it does not appear like a denier, for there are those who say afterwards berachot and prayers; which is not so on Shabbat and Yom Tov when everyone has already prayed, and therefore there is no person who will bless; and see siman 69; and nevertheless it would be fitting to say it in a whisper if not that everyone already heard Bar'khu, as the Rama wrote, for on Shabbat they delay coming to the synagogue, and also they lengthen the Pesukei deZimra more.
