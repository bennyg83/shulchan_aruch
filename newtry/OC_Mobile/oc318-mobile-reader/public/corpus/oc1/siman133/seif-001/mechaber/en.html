The law of Bar'khu on Shabbat. And it contains
one seif: On Shabbat and Yom Tov one does not say Bar'khu after the final Kaddish.Even in a place where they customarily say it on weekdays, because perhaps there were individuals not in the synagogue when they said Bar'khu; nevertheless on Shabbat and Yom Tov one does not say it, because everyone comes to the synagogue before Bar'khu [Beit Yosef in the name of the Rivash].]:
