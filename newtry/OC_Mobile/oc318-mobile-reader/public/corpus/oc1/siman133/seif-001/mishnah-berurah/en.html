(1) Even in a place, etc. — meaning that there are places where they customarily say Kaddish and Bar'khu after the completion of the prayer when the entire congregation prayed, because sometimes there are some individuals who came after Bar'khu and did not hear Bar'khu; and see above in Mishna Berurah at the beginning of siman 69 where we explained everything for the ruling.
(2) That everyone comes, etc. — for even if it happened to one who was delayed in coming after Bar'khu, nevertheless presumably he already heard Bar'khu from those who ascend to the Torah.
