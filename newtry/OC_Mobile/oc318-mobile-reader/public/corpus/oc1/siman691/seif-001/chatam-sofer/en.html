On the Mechaber, seif 1. Note: see Sanhedrin 100a, that a Megillah does not require a cloth. However, the Mahari Yaavetz, volume 2, seif katan 3, concluded that according to what we hold, that the Megillah was said with ruach hakodesh to be read and written, it is forbidden to touch it barehanded and it requires a cloth; see there. I saw my teacher, of blessed memory, that during its reading he was careful not to touch it barehanded. This still requires analysis from Magen Avraham, siman 147, seif katan 1, and requires analysis from Radbaz, volume 2, siman 771.
