(1) Only with ink. See above in siman 32, seif 3 in the gloss, and in the Mishnah Berurah there.
(2) On gevil. This is hide that was not split, and from which only the hair was removed and prepared there; from the flesh side, nothing was removed.
(3) Or on parchment. See above in siman 32, seif 7.
(4) With gall-nut water and kankantum. See the Taz in the name of the Kesef Mishneh, that what they validated with gall-nut water means with gum, for with gall-nut water alone it is invalid. So too the Gra agreed in his commentary, that the two together are called ink. Likewise, the kankantum that we validate means kankantum water, for kankantum alone is certainly invalid. The Gra in his commentary explained that what it said, “with gall-nut water and kankantum,” means that he wrote with both of them mixed together. See above in siman 32, seif 3, in Biur Halachah, s.v. “that it should not,” and so on.
(5) And it requires ruling. This means on every line.
(6) Like the Torah itself. Therefore chok tochos is invalid in it, specifically in most of it. But in a minority of it, it is valid after the fact, for it is no worse than omission above in siman 690, seif 3; see there for the details.
(7) And some say that it requires, and so on. So ruled the Rema in siman 61.
