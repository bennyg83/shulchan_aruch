Scoring lines. It is sufficient to score the first line, because scribes know how to keep the later lines straight. Levush and Magen Avraham. Chacham Tzvi, responsum 97, disagrees and rules in practice that a Megillah requires scoring on every line. See there. Forming letters by hollowing out their surroundings is invalid in a Megillah. Kenesses HaGedolah and Magen Avraham. See also Peri Chadash.
