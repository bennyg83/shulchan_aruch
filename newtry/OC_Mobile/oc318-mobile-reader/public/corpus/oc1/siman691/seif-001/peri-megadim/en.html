In afatzim water, etc. — his intent from Gittin 12a: they wrote a get in mei milin and afatz — valid; and Tosafot there and Hagahot Ashiri: at first glance afatz is not Mishnah ink; they answered when mixed, etc.; and Taz wrote on what Rama said: one can say afatz is chibur — ground gallnuts kneaded in water writing with it — not included in ink; but mei sheriyat afatzin or kneaded is ink; and the word with beit seems should be without — Magen Avraham did not bring Kol Mefareish. And know Shulchan Aruch brought Rambam ch. 2 Megillah halacha 9 specifically on gvil and klaf — not on duchsustos; and in Laws of Torah scroll Rambam invalidates Torah scroll on duchsustos; and Yoreh Deah siman 271 seif 3 wrote on gvil and klaf and did not finish if duchsustos invalidates Torah scroll; and in Mishnah it says on paper and hide invalid — implies duchsustos since paper is not valid; nevertheless ch. 2 Megillah see our master no processing lishmah required; and what not mentioned equal lines for megillah — from where; and per this on klaf and gvil not duchsustos; and from that it should be written on the scroll — specifically gvil and klaf — from where implied; and in Hagahot letter 30 in name of Semag proof megillah written among Writings individual fulfills — see there implies written with them one processing; and so Bach in name of Semag; Bach wrote see Tur; and Rabbenu Tam wrote R' Hai does not disagree what preceded; and one can say from Mishnah stam on paper invalid implies duchsustos valid; and Rabbenu Tam wrote to say it has all Torah scroll law — if so per those who invalidate duchsustos in Torah scroll — same megillah; see Tur. Rosh validates all in Torah scroll; and it appears l'chatchila not to write megillah on duchsustos and same Torah scroll per Rambam; and so Magen Avraham will explain further.
