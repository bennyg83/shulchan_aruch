In afatzim water and kankantom. Explanation: mei kankantom — but kankantom itself invalid as in Mishnah; and so Tur kankantom invalid; and same here; and this is his language: know in kankantom invalid and in its water valid; and in ch. 1 Rambam wrote: if he wrote Torah scroll tefillin mezuzot in afatzim water and kankantom — valid; and wrote in Kol Mefareish: though in Gittin implies afatz water is not Mishnah ink when mixed with gum called in foreign language goma — called ink — end; and I wonder he did not bring Kol Mefareish.
