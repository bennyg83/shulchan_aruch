Seif 1 in the Shulchan Aruch: One may not write, and so on. See Birkei Yosef, who brought in the name of Maharam Proven'al in a manuscript responsum that regarding a case where Megillos were printed on parchment, he required them to be buried, for they are invalid because of chok tochos and perhaps people will come to err and validate them. But on paper, people will not come to err; see there. See the Pri Chadash, that if most of it was printed it is invalid, but if most of it was written it is valid, unlike Sefer HaZichronos, and so on. See Sefer Korban Nesanel, who challenged him for comparing it to a case where the scribe omitted, and so on, for here it was written improperly, which is worse, as Rashi wrote on page 9. The Birkei Yosef defended the Pri Chadash, that his view is that Tosafos disagree with Rashi on this; see there. Regarding printing, whether it is chok tochos, the view of several responsa is that it is judged as writing. See above, siman 284, and in Yoreh De'ah, the laws of sefer Torah. See Tashbetz, volume 1, siman 8, that a Megillah written in Mashait script, which is like our Mashota, is invalid. He is brought in Kenesses HaGedolah, and he wondered at the authorities who omitted this from our Mishnah, which teaches that it must be written in Ashuris; see there. I have abbreviated here.
Ruling. See Baer Heitev, and what he wrote in the name of the Chacham Tzvi. See Birkei Yosef, that Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that one who trembles at the word of Hashem should seek one that is entirely ruled. Nevertheless, if only the first line is ruled, it is valid after the fact. If it is not ruled at all, it is invalid even after the fact.
