(א) On parchment or etc. like Torah scroll. There on the book; gemara derives writing from writing etc.; follows his view in ch. 1 Laws of Tefillin that Torah scroll is not invalidated by being tied with straps — see there.

(ב) And if he wrote it [with other materials]. And likewise in a Torah scroll — one should not infer that each alone invalidates; kankantom in the Mishnah implies it is not [valid] ink, and the same for a Torah scroll as in Shabbat 103b: if he wrote not with ink, etc. And in Yerushalmi Megillah ch. 1 halacha 11 they must write with ink; and afatzim water implies in Gittin 19a it is not ink except when both are together — then it is ink — and it teaches us that even if it is not erased as in Eruvin 13a, nevertheless it is permitted; and he ruled like R' Yehudah from R' Meir who said to each one throw in, etc., as in Laws of Tefillin ch. 1 — and this is their language: how is ink made? They gather smoke of oils, etc., soak it in afatzim water and the like and write with it that if erased it would erase — and that is the ink of optimal mitzvah to write a Torah scroll with. And if he wrote all three in afatzim water and kankantom that stands and is not erased — they are valid. If so, what did the halacha transmitted to Moshe at Sinai exclude — that they be written in ink — to exclude other dye types such as red and green and the like; that if he wrote in a Torah scroll even one letter in other dye types or in gold — these are invalid. And so wrote the Rosh in small halakhot. And this is what he says here: he wrote in other [materials], etc. And this is his language in his commentary on Sotah ch. 2: I saw in Eretz Yisrael they distance Torah scroll writing with one ink type called in Arabic chibur — only real ink; and here he erred in pesak halacha from the gemara, for the last thing said in the gemara on this matter: each throws in kankantom except the sota parsha in the Temple; and the copyist there said: as copyist I saw to explain what is chibur — as the Geonim explained: chibur is afatzim ink and they throw kankantom into it, a type of blackening earth that is not erased; and so wrote R' Chananel, etc. — see there.

(ג) And it requires ruled lines. On every line — unlike Levush and Magen Avraham; they erred, for regarding tefillin that do not require ruled lines we say thus as in Tosafot Gittin 6b on the matter beginning R' Yitzchak said etc.; and if you say etc.; and Rabbenu Tam says etc.; and likewise in Agudah; but mezuzah and anything that requires ruled lines perforce we require on every line; and see there and in Rosh.

(ד) And there is no hide, etc. This follows his view that he ruled even a mezuzah does not require processing lishmah; and although regarding a Torah scroll we hold it requires processing lishmah as they said in Gittin 54b, and so he ruled in chapter 10 of laws of Torah scroll and chapter 1 of laws of tefillin — they hold thus.

(ה) And some say it requires [processing lishmah]. This is Rabbenu Tam's view as he wrote in Menachot there, in the passage beginning "until," etc. Rabbenu Tam says halacha is Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel as the sugya of damages follows him; and that in the chapter "the judgment was finished" where Abbaye and Rava disagree, etc. — Rabbenu Tam and R' Chananel explained Rava like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel who requires processing lishmah; and perforce invitation is not a thing — for if not, it would be possible with skin cutting and small repairs, etc.; and so he wrote in Sanhedrin there — see there; and so he ruled regarding mezuzah; and see siman 32 and Yoreh Deah siman 288.
