[ג] Open [parashiyot] invalid, etc. In Rama's responsum he doubts this and it seems to me to rely in time of duress (Magen Avraham), and he was not precise — it is explained there he asked this of Maharai and in Piskei Maharai siman 23 he answered invalid; nevertheless Maharai wrote there are lenient who hold not all Torah scroll law applies to megillah — requires study on Rama. And in responsum Maharam Mintz siman 61 he wrote some closed some open — what he listed there does not refer to our megillot and nuschaot.
