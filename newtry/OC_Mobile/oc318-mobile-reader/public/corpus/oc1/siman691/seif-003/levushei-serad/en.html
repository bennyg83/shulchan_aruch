Shulchan Aruch, seif 2. It requires a pole at its end, and so on. This means that one leaves blank parchment in order to roll it over a wooden pole on which the Megillah is rolled. At its beginning he leaves a large blank space in order to roll the entire circumference with it, because a Megillah does not have two poles so that it can be rolled to the middle, as is done with a sefer Torah. Rather, it has one pole and is rolled from its end to its beginning.
