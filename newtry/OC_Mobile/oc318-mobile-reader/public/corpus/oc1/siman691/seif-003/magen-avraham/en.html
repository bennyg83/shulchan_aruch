Regarding parchment surrounding each letter. The same applies to every other requirement; see the gloss. Therefore its lines must be even. Tashbetz writes likewise. See Tosafos, Menachos 31b, and responsa of Maharam, siman 351, that the width of the column should equal three times the word “lemishpechosei,” and that all laws of a Sefer Torah apply. See responsa of Maharam Mintz and Kenesses HaGedolah regarding writing the Megillah.
