And to pronounce every word. This requires study. In siman 32, seif 31, Beit Yosef writes that only one who writes by heart must pronounce each word, whereas a Megillah may not be written by heart. Tur and Semag likewise require verbalization only regarding tefillin. It is therefore difficult that Beit Yosef wrote this also regarding a Sefer Torah, which must be copied from written text and should not require pronunciation of each word. Even if another person dictates it to him by heart, this qualification should have been stated explicitly. This requires study.
