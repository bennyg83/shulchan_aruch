(18) To lengthen the vav of Vayzasa. To stretch its bent head so that it is somewhat upright, as an allusion that all of them were hanged on one upright beam.
(19) In its reading. So that he should not snatch it quickly.
(20) And “ve'es” at its end. Meaning, at the end of the line; but at the end of the page he should write the word “aseres,” as the Beis Yosef writes. After the fact, if he did not lengthen the vav of Vayzasa, and likewise if he wrote more lines after the word aseres, it is valid. Some are stringent where he wrote additional lines after the word aseres. See Shaar Ephraim, siman 55, who wrote that if there is another Megillah there written according to its law, one should not initially read from this one publicly, unless there is burden on the community to wait until they bring the one written according to its law; then they may read from this one.
