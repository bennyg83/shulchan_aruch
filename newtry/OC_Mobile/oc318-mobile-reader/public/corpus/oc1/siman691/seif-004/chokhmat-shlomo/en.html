There, seif 4. One must lengthen the vav of Vayzasa in its writing, and some say in its reading. Note: see Bava Metzia, page 87, regarding what is said there: “HaElokim” requires a vav like the pole of a ship; see Rashi’s explanation there. There the intent is certainly in its writing, not in its reading, for there is no reading there. Since the same expression is used here and there, it appears that here too the primary view follows those who say it refers to writing and not reading. See what I wrote about this in my novellae to Yoreh De'ah, laws of vows, siman 207; there I elaborated on the wording of the above Talmud in Bava Metzia. Examine this carefully.
