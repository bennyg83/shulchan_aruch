(ג) And requires, etc. Yerushalmi there: R' Yirmiyah bar Bun — must be ish at the head of the page and et at its end — swayed and descended like this beam — meaning like two beams of a pocket equal on both sides like builders' balance; and see in Mishneh Torah there. And what is written at the head of the page means at the head of the line and at the end of the line — not as some explain at the head of the column, for otherwise why would R' Yirmiyah add after R' Yirmiyah in name of Rav who said there ten sons of Haman, etc., and song of 48, etc., one column above, etc. From this is drawn what they write ten sons of Haman in a column by itself in large letters. And the confusion is that we were not given to write ten sons of Haman in large script letters; rather the main point of what is written at the head of the page is at the head of the line as above. And R' Yirmiyah himself teaches: if from R' Yirmiyah in name of Rav one would say since ten sons of Haman were separated from all songs and equated to the song of 48 — for the matter that both are written alone one column above, etc. as above and as in the gemara — if so we would equate them entirely not to write within ten sons of Haman the word before it as in the song of kings of Canaan that begins "king," etc.; and this is what R' Yirmiyah said: must, etc., ish at the head, etc., and as above at head of line and end of line like pocket eye and two beams as above and like other megillah letters.
