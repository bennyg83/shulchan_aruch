If he placed in it three sinew threads, it is valid, provided they are tripled. Because there are different explanations of this, one must satisfy all of them: he should make three stitches at its beginning, three at its end, and three in its middle, and one stitch in the fourth section from this side and one stitch in the fourth section from the other side. Gloss: If he has no more sinews, it is better to sew the remainder with linen threads than to leave it unsewn, as in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 2, and Kol Bo. But if he has sinews, he should sew all of it with sinews, and the stitching should be on the outside and not on the inside, as Rabbeinu Yerucham, Nesiv 14, part 4, and the Beis Yosef in the name of Orchos Chayim write.
