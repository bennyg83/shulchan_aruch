6) Seif 2, If it was written, etc., or if a non-Jew wrote it, etc. If it was written by a woman or a minor, or was written with the left hand, some invalidate it. Maaseh Rokeach on the Rambam, Hilchos Megillah, chapter 1, and so is the view of Meleches Shamayim, rule 24, letter 3 in Chochmah. Others validate it, Pri Chadash, letter 3; Mateh Yehudah, letter 2; Machazik Berachah, letter 2. Beis Oved, letter 6, wrote as follows: therefore, the essential practical ruling appears to be that if a woman wrote it, it is valid to read from it even initially, since she too is obligated in its reading. But if a minor wrote it, one should not initially fulfill his obligation with it; however, after the fact, if he already read from it, or if he has no other Megillah, it is valid. These are his words. But Shaarei Teshuvah wrote that even regarding a woman’s writing, if another can be found easily, he should read from the second without a blessing.
