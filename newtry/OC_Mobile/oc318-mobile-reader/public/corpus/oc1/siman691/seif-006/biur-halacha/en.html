"And because there are different interpretations." Rashi explains that one divides the length of the sheet into 4 sections: 1 stitching is placed near the top at the end of the first quarter, the second at the midpoint, and the third at the beginning of the lower quarter. Rif explains "in thirds" according to its plain meaning: 2 stitchings are placed at the 2 ends of the sheet and 1 exactly in the middle. Rambam explains "in thirds" to mean 3 stitchings at the top of the sheet, 3 at its bottom, and 3 at its middle. By following the Mechaber's arrangement, one satisfies all of these opinions.
