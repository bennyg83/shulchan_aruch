Seif katan 2. That there is a dispute. The Tur brings many authorities who wrote the opposite: the pole is at its beginning, the blank space is at its end, and one rolls it from its beginning to its end. Therefore it is better to sit and do nothing, and no pole is made at all. If you ask: if so, they should also not make a blank space, for the same reason, since there is a dispute. I already wrote at the beginning, meaning at the beginning of the siman on the words of the Mechaber, seif 2, that a blank space is made. One can say that a blank space must be left so that it will cover the circumference of the Megillah, because of the honor of sacred writings, and people rely on this view that the blank space is at its beginning. If you ask: since they rely on this view because of the necessity of the blank space, they should also make the pole at its end. One can answer that specifically when there is both a blank space and a pole did the great authorities disagree which is better to make at the beginning and which at the end. But when there is only a blank space, there is no concern at all; wherever it was made, it was made.
