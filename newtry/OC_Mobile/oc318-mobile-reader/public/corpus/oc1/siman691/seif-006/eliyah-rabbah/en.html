[6] Levush: One does not vowelize, and so on. In a place where there is no one who knows how to read with the cantillation by heart, it is permitted to write it with cantillation marks, read it publicly, and recite a blessing over it, as Be'er Sheva writes. If the cantillation marks are not in the Megillah, it is permitted to read without cantillation, as written at the end of siman 142.
