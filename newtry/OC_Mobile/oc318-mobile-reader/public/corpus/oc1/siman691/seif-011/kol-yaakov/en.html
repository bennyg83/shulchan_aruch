11) There in the gloss, And to articulate every word with his mouth, etc. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 2, who disagrees with the Magen Avraham and rules that even one who copies from a book must articulate every word with his mouth. Tefillin are different, as the Magen Avraham himself wrote in siman 32, seif katan 31. The Gra, letter 16, also disagrees with the Magen Avraham, brings proof from all the authorities, and writes that the authorities stated this as a greater novelty, that even when another reads it to him he must articulate it with his mouth, and certainly when copying from a book. See there. Meleches Shamayim, rule 24, letter 8 in Binah. See our words in Yoreh Deah, siman 274, letter 7.
