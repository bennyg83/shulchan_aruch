They also had the custom not to make a pole. The reason appears to be that there is a dispute among the great early authorities whether to make the pole at its beginning or at its end; therefore the custom was not to make a pole at all. The Rashba wrote in responsum, siman 467, that one who reads the Megillah to discharge the community, even if he omitted only one word, must go back, and so on. For it implies from the Yerushalmi that only regarding the mistaken reading of “Yehudim” there, where the language and meaning are the same, does he not go back; otherwise he goes back. But regarding the listeners, they do not need to have intent for each and every word, and he brought proofs for this matter.
