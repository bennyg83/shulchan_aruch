Also practiced not to make a pole. Reason appears dispute among early ones whether pole at head or end — therefore practiced not to make pole at all. Rashba wrote in responsum siman 567: reader of megillah to discharge congregation — even if he omitted only one word returns, etc. — implies Yerushalmi in error that Jews there language and matter one — he does not return; if not so he returns; but for listeners need not intend each word — he brought proofs.
