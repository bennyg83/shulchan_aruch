Seif 2 in the Shulchan Aruch: Or if a gentile wrote it, and so on. See the Pri Chadash, who wrote that if it was written with the left hand it is valid after the fact, although regarding tefillin it is invalid. From this the Birkei Yosef learned that if a woman wrote it, it is valid. In Shiyurei Berachah there he brought proof from the fact that the Sefer Mitzvos Gadol brings proof regarding processing the parchment for its own sake from a Megillah written among the Writings, from which one has fulfilled the obligation. If so, the same applies to a woman's writing, for she is valid to write Neviim and Kesuvim, as Tosafos wrote in Gittin, page 44. See Chacham Tzvi, siman 91, regarding what he wrote to answer the difficulty of the Re'em concerning ruling the lines, that ruling is different because one sees that it is not ruled, and so on. This does not apply to a woman's writing. In Machazik Berachah he brought that the author of Maaseh Rokeach, Hilchos Megillah, invalidates a Megillah written by a woman or a minor. Machazik Berachah rejected his proofs, and also wrote that in Sefer Mateh Yehudah he wrote to validate writing with the left hand or writing by a minor; see there. However, regarding what he wrote to reject the proofs of Sefer Maaseh Rokeach from Sefer HaTerumah, brought by the Beis Yosef in siman 32, he forced the matter. See what I wrote in Yad Ephraim on the margin of the Pri Chadash, where I also brought from Sefer HaTerumah. One who sees it will see that it cannot be rejected as Machazik Berachah wrote. It appears that even the Pri Chadash perhaps validated it only after the fact, where one already read from it, but not to read from it initially. It appears that if another can be found easily, he should read a second time without a blessing, and the same applies where it was written by a woman or a minor.
