[If] it was written on paper or on hide that was not processed or was written by an idolater or a heretic, it is disqualified. The rules are the same as a Torah scroll with respect to empty parchment surrounding [each letter], the humps on a het, the disconnected hey and kuf, all the letters' shapes, and lacking or excessive letters There must be a pole at the end and a blank area at the beginning so that [empty parchment] surrounds it. Some say that it needs to have crowns, and some say that it does not. {Rama: Rema: It also has to be written based on a written copy [Ra"n]. [The scribe must] utter every word orally before writing it, just like with a Torah scroll [Mahari"k]. All of the sections are closed. If they were done opened, it is invalid [Hagahot Maimoni, piskei Mahara"i] After the fact, we do not invalidate a megillah based on lacking or excessive letters, because it is not worse than if the scribe left out some letters, in which case it is valid [Hagahot Maimoni, Or Zarua] Rem"a: The practice is to use crowns. The practice is also not to put a pole at the end at all [Mahari"l].}
