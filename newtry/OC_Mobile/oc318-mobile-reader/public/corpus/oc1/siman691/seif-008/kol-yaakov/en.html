8) There, And its law is like a Torah scroll, etc. A Megillah in which he wrote only three or four lines in the final column, leaving the rest blank, and he also did not finish in the middle of a line but at its end, is valid to read from even initially. Zera Avraham, part 1, siman 100; Machazik Berachah, letter 4; Erech HaShulchan, letter 8; Shaarei Ephraim, gate 6, letter 56; Meleches Shamayim, rule 24, letter 10 in Binah; Emes L’Yaakov, rule 22, letter 3; Beis Oved, letter 14. What Zera Avraham wrote there at the end of the siman, and Beis Oved cited there, that if it is impossible to finish in the middle of the line at the end of the column except by stretching the letters, it is not proper to do so, this is not the practice here in Baghdad, may the Almighty protect it. Rather, the practice is to stretch the letters and finish at the end of the column and in the middle of the line. See our words in Yoreh Deah, siman 272, letters 11 and 13, and see above, letter 7.
