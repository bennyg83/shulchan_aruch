(siman 11 in Shulchan Aruch) regarding a stolen lulav — as stated; and see Peri Chadash siman 649 who wrote regarding a stolen shofar that one blesses; and in his opinion the same applies here, for the law of megillah is learned from the law of shofar in this; but in Beit Yosef he wrote in the name of Meiri that some are strict regarding megillah even though they are lenient regarding shofar.
