In the Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. Only regarding tefillin, and so on. Since people are fluent in them and one is permitted to write them without a written text, therefore he must utter them with his mouth. This is unlike Megillah, which must be written from a written text, and this is the difficulty on the Rema. He added to ask on the Shulchan Aruch in Yoreh De'ah, siman 274, which wrote regarding a sefer Torah that one must utter it with his mouth; this too is difficult, for it must be written from a written text. And if you say it is when another person reads to him, so that then he need not write from a written text, nevertheless he should have explained it that way. In Magen Avraham, siman 32, seif katan 402, he answers this difficulty; see there.
