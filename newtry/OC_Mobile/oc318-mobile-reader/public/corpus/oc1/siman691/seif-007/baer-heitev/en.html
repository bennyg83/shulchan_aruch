Column. See Sheyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, glosses to Beit Yosef, os 7. In os 9 he relates a case involving a Megillah in which the ten sons of Haman were written across half the column, with “Ish” at the beginning of a line and “Aseres” at the end, but the passage was not at the end of a column; he invalidated it. Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, klal 4, siman 12, validates it.
