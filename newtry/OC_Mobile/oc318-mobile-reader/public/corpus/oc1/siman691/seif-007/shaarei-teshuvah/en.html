Page. See Baer Heitev, and see my responsa Beis Ephraim, siman 72, at length regarding this matter, and regarding the custom to write the ten sons of Haman in large letters; see there. There it is explained that initially one should also refrain from writing an allusion to the Holy Name with initials or final letters in large letters; see there, and in Shaarei Ephraim and Pischei She'arim there. See there in the name of Machazik Berachah, who wrote in the name of Zera Emes that if in a Megillah the last column had only three or four lines written, and it did not finish at the end of the page and did not end in the middle of a line, it is valid to read from initially; see there. See Birkei Yosef in the name of Maharam, that if a Megillah was written out of order, one has not fulfilled the obligation; and Maharash HaLevi is uncertain about the matter.
