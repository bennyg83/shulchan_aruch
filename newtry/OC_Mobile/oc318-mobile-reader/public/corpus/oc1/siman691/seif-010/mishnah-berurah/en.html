(27) In a Chumash. This is specifically when the Chumash is made as a scroll, as written in siman 143, because some say that in a pressing circumstance one fulfills the obligation with this. But with our Chumash, according to everyone he has not fulfilled the obligation, as the Bach and Magen Avraham write. The Pri Megadim wrote that even with this it is proper to read without a blessing, so that the Torah of Megillah should not be forgotten.
