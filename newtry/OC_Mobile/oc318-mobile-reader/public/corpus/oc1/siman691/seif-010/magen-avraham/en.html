In a chumash. Some maintain that in pressing circumstances one fulfills the obligation from such a text, but only when it is made as a scroll, as stated in siman 143, seif 2, though it contains a defect. From an ordinary bound chumash, according to all opinions, one does not fulfill the obligation. Bach, Mordechai, and Hagahos Maimoniyos. If a chazzan does not know the cantillation by heart and no one is present to prompt him, he may write the cantillation marks in the Megillah, for this is no worse than vocalization. Bach. Nevertheless, if the marks are absent, one may read without cantillation, as stated at the end of siman 142.
