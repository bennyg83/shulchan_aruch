[י] In chumash, etc. This is Magen Avraham's language specifically when made in a roll; but in our chumashim for everyone he did not fulfill (Bach) — till here. In my humble opinion clear from Bach itself: nevertheless they read it since there is no blessing; but he should have brought Bach's words on what Rama wrote afterward; and see above siman 649, etc., if they bless, etc. — implies per those who say they bless — same law in chumash specifically when rolled; but in our chumash for everyone they do not bless; but requires study: what is different from apples and pomegranates they do not take even without etrog; and even without blessing as end of siman 649 — one can distinguish.
