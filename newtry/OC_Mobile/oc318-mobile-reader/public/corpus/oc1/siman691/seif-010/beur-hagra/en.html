Seif 10: If there is no, and so on. The Tur compares this to a dried lulav, with which one fulfills the obligation in a pressing circumstance, as written in the third chapter of Sukkah: a pressing circumstance is no proof. In the Yerushalmi, chapter 2, halachah 3, it says: whether according to the one who says the whole of it, or whether, and so on, provided it is from a complete book. If it was written as Chumashim, one does not read from it publicly. Rabbi Tanchuma said: because of the unlearned. This is what the Tur means by “Chumashim,” as is written in Gittin regarding a sefer Torah.
Without a blessing. There, like lulav, as written in siman 649, seif 6.
