Blessings and liturgical poems. This is unlike a Megillah written among the other Scriptures, because there it is not recognizable as a Megillah, whereas here it remains recognizable. Rashba, siman 150. Tashbetz, siman 223, writes that its blessings should not initially be written in it. Hagahos Maimoniyos, Mordechai, and Levush write likewise.
