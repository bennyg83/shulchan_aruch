(25) That is vowelized. If there is no one who knows how to read with the cantillation by heart, it is permitted to write the cantillation marks in the Megillah as well and to recite a blessing over it, for this is no worse than vowelization. Nevertheless, if the cantillation marks are not in the Megillah, it is permitted to read without cantillation, as written at the end of siman 142.
(26) Blessings. Initially, one should not write its blessings in it.
