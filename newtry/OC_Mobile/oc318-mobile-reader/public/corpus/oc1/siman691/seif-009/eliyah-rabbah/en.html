[ט] Must extend, etc. It appears all this is valid b'dieved (Magen Avraham), and it implies the same law for ish at head of page in seif 3, etc., since I saw scribes in megillah writing — I exerted myself and examined responsum Maharam Mintz who brought correction of R' Aviyah and Knesset HaGedolah who brought many things contradicting the correction mentioned above; what is agreed I wrote anonymously and disputed matters I wrote in our correction. In R' Aviyah's correction: Charvona with hei; in ours with alef — first Charvona with hei and second with hei; Tam'an hamalka and Vashti with vav; asher lo tavo and Vashti with vav — in ours missing vav; hagola in R' Aviyah full vav — in ours missing vav; amen et hadassah missing vav; menoteha latet lah in R' Aviyah without vav and yud — in ours full vav missing yud — some scrolls full both; yeshnah without yud; in R' Aviyah et na'aroteyah without vav — in ours full vav; behagia tar tarveihu missing vav; lishloach yad bamelech Achashverosh missing vav in second Achashverosh; bechol malchut Achashverosh full vav; vedateihem shanot missing first vav; ve'am ve'am kelashono in R' Aviyah missing vav — Knesset HaGedolah wondered if explanation requires ve'am ve'am — then all books wrote ve'am ve'am; if intent kelashono missing vav after shin alone cannot say missing in plural language — then all books full — till here. In my humble opinion his intent: written in the book in megillah ve'am ve'am kelashono; beshem hamelech Achashverosh full vav — some books missing vav; veheyot atidim in R' Aviyah missing first yud since tavo cannot be full vav; vetavo'na full vav; el rechov in R' Aviyah missing vav — in ours full; ve'al tochelu ve'al tishtu all with vav; vayitapek Haman vayavo full vav; ki im oti vav defective; yoshev full vav; ve'lo ketiv hayoshev — Sefer Hazichronot in R' Aviyah missing final vav — in ours opposite missing first vav; lechatzer beit hamelech hachitzonah in R' Aviyah missing vav — some books full; tzorer missing vav; hayehudim in two yuds; vayikreu sofrei hamelech missing vav; asher hechelot lenofol in R' Aviyah full vav — in ours lenofel missing vav; gavoah chamishim missing vav; talahu missing vav; ba'et hahi with yud; asher mehodu missing vav; velihyot hayehudim in R' Aviyah one yud — in ours two yuds; atidim first yud tag like this — so in R' Aviyah; but I examined megillot and precise chumashim: written atudim and under first yud written vav; and though margin says atidim keri — nevertheless written with yud — requires study on Knesset HaGedolah who did not note this; yatzu mevohalim missing vav; vesason first missing vav second full; vehafach hu full vav; ha'ish Mordechai holech full vav; ve'et Ardei missing yud; tzorer missing vav; umishloach manot written twice manot — both full mishloach — some missing both — some first full some second full; Parshendata small alef; Parmashta large alef — some say opposite; I saw in megillot and nuschaot no change in alef in both; also Parshendata small tav; Parmashta small shin; Vaizata large vav small zayin — requires study in R' Aviyah;
