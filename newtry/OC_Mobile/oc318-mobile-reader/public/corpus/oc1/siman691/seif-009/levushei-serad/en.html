Seif katan 5. See the end of the siman, and see siman 143. There is a dispute regarding an invalid Megillah: some say one reads from it with a blessing, and some say one reads from it without a blessing. They likewise disagree in the Sefer Mitzvos Gadol regarding an invalid sefer Torah. Therefore, although the custom follows the opinion that one reads without a blessing, nevertheless in this case, where the Magen Avraham in responsum permits it entirely, he should read with a blessing. It is like a double doubt: perhaps the law follows the Rema that it is valid, and perhaps one recites a blessing over an invalid Megillah.
