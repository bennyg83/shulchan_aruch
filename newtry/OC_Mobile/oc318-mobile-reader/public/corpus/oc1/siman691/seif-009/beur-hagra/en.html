Seif 9: A Megillah that is, and so on, and likewise, and so on. Although a sefer Torah becomes invalid through vowelization, as written in Maseches Sofrim, chapter 3, halachah 7, “a sefer that was divided and vowelized,” and so on, “one should not read from it,” and likewise if he wrote blessings, as written in Megillah 32a, so that people should not say the blessings are written there, nevertheless here it is not invalidated, for it is no worse than missing and extra letters. But initially it is forbidden, as written by the Mordechai and Hagahos Maimoniyos.
