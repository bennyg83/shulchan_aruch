Not to Recite [the Recitation of the Shema] Opposite Urine - Containing 2 Se'ifim It is forbidden to recite opposite urine until one has poured a revi-it of water into it, and then it is permitted. And it makes no difference whether it is on the surface of the ground or in a vessel - as long as it is not in a special chamber pot that's designated for it [And for the law of the chamber pot see below Siman 87]. It makes no difference whether it was in a vessel first and water was poured into it or the water is in the vessel first.
