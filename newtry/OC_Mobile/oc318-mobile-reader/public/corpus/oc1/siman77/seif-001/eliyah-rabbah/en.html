(1) Into it a revi'it, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote that for a child one does not need as much, as will be explained below siman 82 se'if 10. And I wonder, for in Beit Yosef there it is explained that the case is when the child discharged inside his wrapping garment and the urine does not flow from the urine to the mother's garment, but only a few drops — then a little suffices to nullify it; but when the child urinated in the garment itself or on the ground, certainly a revi'it is required. And it is possible that he was precise from that Rama and Levush wrote there "a child discharged in it," etc., implying that he does not agree with Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham, and the distinction between an adult and a child is not clear — for it seems from the posekim there that they mentioned a child as the usual case, and the same applies to an adult, as we will explain in the responsa siman 64. It is also possible to distinguish between urine that is visible and when it is absorbed in a garment; and further, there the small amount is discussed in s.k. 2. Lechem Chamudos wrote [explicitly] on the Mishnah of the Rambam in the chapter "One whose relative died" that one must throw into it a revi'it of water at one time — until here. And I saw that all early and later authorities did not mention this, and perhaps they were silent like their explanation. And in the Rambam himself, chapter 3 of the laws of Kerias Shema, he also did not mention this and wrote in its place "a revi'it of water for urine from one time," and Bertinoro likewise wrote; and it requires examination. Radbaz wrote in his responsum part 2 siman 30: People who have hemorrhoids and constantly discharge blood from them, and because of the blood putrid moisture comes out that is moldy and has a bad smell — they cannot recite Kerias Shema, prayer, Torah, or Birchas HaNehenin as long as the putrid moisture drips from them. There is no bad smell there if the blood or moisture comes out from the intestines — it has a remedy through washing the place of filth; but if it is from the anal opening, it has no remedy. How does one know if it is from the intestines or from the anal opening? If it comes out through straining intermittently, it is from the intestines; and if it drips and flows down frequently on its own, it is from the anal opening.
