Seif 1. And there is no distinction, etc. Tur. Meaning, even when it is in a vessel that does not absorb. This is clearly established by the statement there: The Sages taught concerning a chamber pot, etc., and by the statement concerning the urine itself, etc., which implies that this applies even when it is in a vessel. See Rashi, s.v. 'and the urine itself,' etc.
Provided that one does not, etc. From the statement, 'and the urine itself,' etc., it appears that adding water is ineffective for a chamber pot.
There is no distinction, etc. This follows Rabbi Yose and Rabbi Zeira, because an actual incident is decisive, as stated at the end of chapter 17 of Shabbos and elsewhere. See Rashi, s.v. 'Bring,' etc.
