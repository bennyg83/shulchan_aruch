(1) [Seif 1] It is forbidden to recite opposite urine until one pours into it a revi'it of water, etc. If he knew there was urine here and was in doubt whether a revi'it of water was poured into it and the like — it seems to me that since a prohibition was established, even rabbinic, we go stringently. Nevertheless, in a time of pressure that Kerias Shema and prayer would be nullified because of this, it is permitted to rely on the posekim who hold that even when established we go leniently. Chayei Adam general rule 3 letter 32, and they brought it in Pri Megadim at the end of siman 76.
