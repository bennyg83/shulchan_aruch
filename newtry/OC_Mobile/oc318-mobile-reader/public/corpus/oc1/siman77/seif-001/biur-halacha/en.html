It is forbidden to recite, etc., on the ground — I was in doubt: if the urine is in two places and between them there is a place that is only enough to wet in order to wet another place, and he poured a revi'it of water only in one place — does it not help? Perhaps it is not considered connected through wetting in order to wet another place except when there is water in that place at least like the thickness of an onion peel, as we ruled regarding connecting mikvaos in Yoreh De'ah siman 201 se'if 54 in the hagahah and in Shach there s.k. 119, see there; or perhaps in our matter it is rabbinic and therefore I am lenient, and I found in Sefer Shulchan Shlomo who wrote in siman 77, and this is his wording: He shall pour a revi'it of water and see that the water goes over all the urine that is enough to wet in order to wet another place — thus. From that he requires the water to go over them, it implies he holds that wetting in order to wet another place alone is not considered a connection; and it requires examination.
