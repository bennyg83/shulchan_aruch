It is forbidden to recite, etc., upon the ground. I was uncertain about a case in which the urine is in two places, with an area between them that is merely wet enough to moisten something else, and one poured the revi'is of water only in one place. Is that effective? Perhaps moisture sufficient to moisten something else does not create a connection unless there is at least a garlic-peel-thick layer of water in that place, as we rule regarding the connection of mikvaos in Yoreh Deah, siman 201, seif 54, in the gloss, and in the Shach there, seif katan 119. See there. Alternatively, perhaps our case is different because the prohibition is Rabbinic. I found in Shulchan Shlomo, siman 77, the following: 'He should pour a revi'is of water and ensure that the water reaches all the urine that is wet enough to moisten something else.' Since he requires that the water reach it, this implies that he maintains that moisture sufficient to moisten something else alone is not considered a connection. This requires study.
