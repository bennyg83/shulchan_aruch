(1) Opposite, etc. — and for this all the laws of distance apply, as below in siman 79 seif 1 regarding excrement.

(2) Until he pours, etc. — because by Torah law it is forbidden to recite only opposite an erect penis alone, and after it fell on the ground its prohibition is only rabbinic, as explained at the end of siman 76; therefore the Sages were lenient that one can nullify them by a revi'it of water, even if the urine was abundant; and therefore the revi'it of water helps only specifically when they are not putrid; but if it is known that they are putrid, for then it is forbidden by Torah law to recite opposite them — a revi'it does not help, but one needs to increase water upon them to nullify the putridity.

(3) revi'it of water — some say we specifically require [pouring] at one time, for otherwise the first [portion] is nullified first.

(4) on the ground — and it should appear that the water goes over all places where the urine was spilled there that are enough to wet in order to wet another place; and see Biur Halachah.

(5) designated for them — for then even if there is no urine at all in it, it is forbidden to recite opposite it, and pouring water does not help to render the vessel itself fit, as will be explained in siman 87.

(6) they were in a vessel first — and likewise if there was water like a revi'it first on the ground and he urinated afterward on top of the water, it is permitted to recite Kerias Shema, even if now because of the urine it spread more.
