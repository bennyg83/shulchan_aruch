[2] There, one occasion, etc. Even when there is much urine, a revi'is is sufficient, and when there is little urine, a revi'is of water is likewise required. This is stated by the Nachalas Tzvi. I found the same in the Kesef Mishneh, chapter 3 of Hilchos Krias Shema, and the Magen Avraham wrote likewise. The Shiltei Gibborim, however, wrote: 'If the urine was less than a revi'is, it appears to me that an equal quantity of water is required to nullify it.' This implies that when the urine is less than a revi'is, a full revi'is of water is unnecessary, and water equal to the amount of urine is sufficient. This also explains why wiping is effective in siman 302, seif 10. Even according to the Kesef Mishneh, it appears to me that a revi'is is required only for all the urine discharged in one urination, even if he urinated only a small amount. If he urinated much, however, but only a small amount fell onto the garment, a revi'is is unnecessary.
