(2) There, one time, etc. Even if much, a revi'it suffices; and likewise if it is a little, a revi'it of water is also required — thus wrote Nachalat Tzvi; and thus I found in Kesef Mishnah chapter 3 of the laws of Kerias Shema; and thus wrote Magen Avraham; but in Shelomei Gibburim he wrote: if the urine was less than a revi'it, it seems to me that water corresponding to it is needed to nullify it — until here. This implies that one does not need a revi'it if the urine is less than a revi'it, but water corresponding to the urine suffices. And now it is resolved what helps in wiping in siman 82 se'if 10; and even according to Kesef Mishnah it seems to me that specifically regarding what he urinated at one time does he require a revi'it, even though he urinated only a little; but when he urinated much but only a little fell on the garment, he does not need a revi'it.
