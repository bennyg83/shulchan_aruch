Seif 2, revi'it, etc. And it requires examination: if he urinated and stopped in the middle, he needs two revi'ot; but if he were urinating at one time he would need only one. But the essence seems like the Rashba, who wrote that even within many times a revi'it suffices — similar to "all the more so" for the first Tanna, for certainly there is no difference; and thus in the Yerushalmi: one large vessel and one small vessel; and thus in the Rif on Shabbos siman 152 seif 10 and Magen Avraham — and his words are strained.
