Seif 2. A revi'is, etc. This requires study, for if one urinated, stopped in the middle, and then continued, two revi'iyos would be required, whereas had he urinated continuously, only one would be required. The primary ruling, however, appears to follow the Rashba, who wrote that even when one urinated on many occasions, one revi'is is sufficient. This is analogous to any quantity according to the first Tanna, where there is certainly no distinction. This is also stated in the Yerushalmi: whether it is a large vessel or a small vessel. The same appears in Hilchos Shabbos, siman 302, seif 10. See the Magen Avraham, whose words are difficult.
