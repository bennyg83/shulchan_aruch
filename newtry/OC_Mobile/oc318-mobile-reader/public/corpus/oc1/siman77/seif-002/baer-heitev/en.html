One time. And there is no difference whether he urinated much or a little, as explained in Magen Avraham. And in the responsum of Chacham Tzvi siman 102 he ruled that if the urine is less than a revi'it, a majority of water suffices to nullify it, and one does not need a revi'it, for we say revi'it only to be lenient — that even if urine from one time is more than a revi'it, a revi'it suffices to nullify it. And in Shelomei Gibburim in the name of Riaz he is more lenient and permits that if the urine is less than a revi'it one need not use water except equal to the urine measure for measure, and he does not require a majority of water — and it is fit to rely on him for a Torah prohibition, all the more so for a rabbinic prohibition.
