One. There is no distinction whether he urinated much or little, as is evident from the Magen Avraham. In Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 102, he ruled that when the urine amounts to less than a revi'is, a majority of water is sufficient to nullify it, and a revi'is is unnecessary. A revi'is was prescribed only as a leniency, so that even when the urine from one urination exceeds a revi'is, one revi'is of water is sufficient to nullify it. The Shiltei Gibborim, citing the Riaz, is even more lenient and permits using, when the urine is less than a revi'is, only an equal quantity of water corresponding to the urine, without requiring a majority of water. He concludes that it is proper to rely upon him regarding a Torah prohibition, and certainly regarding a Rabbinic prohibition. See there.
