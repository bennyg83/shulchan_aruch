(7) One occasion. Even if he urinated a large amount, a revi'is is sufficient, as stated above in seif katan 2. Likewise, if he urinated only a small amount, some authorities maintain that a revi'is is still required. Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 102, however, rules that if the urine is less than a revi'is, a majority of water is sufficient to nullify it, and a revi'is is unnecessary. The Sages prescribed a revi'is only as a leniency, so that even if the urine from one urination exceeds a revi'is, a revi'is of water is sufficient to nullify it, but not as a stringency. Even if the urine is from two urinations, a majority of water is sufficient to nullify it. The later authorities concur. See also what I wrote below in siman 82, Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 4.
(8) Two revi'iyos. The Mechaber rules in accordance with Rambam and the other poskim, and not in accordance with the Rashba, who is lenient in this matter, as explained in the Beit Yosef. The Magen Gibborim suggests that there is no dispute between them. Even Rambam requires two revi'iyos for two urinations only when they occurred successively in the following manner: he urinated and added a revi'is of water, and when he urinates again, he must add water again, and so on. The revi'is added the first time does not help for the later urination. If, however, he urinated several times and now wishes to recite opposite it, adding one revi'is alone is sufficient. See there. The Biur HaGra likewise agrees as a matter of halachah that adding one revi'is alone is sufficient in this case. See there, for I have written briefly.
