(7) one time — and even if he urinated much, a revi'it suffices, as above in s.k. 2; and likewise if he urinated only a little, some say he also needs a revi'it; but in the responsum of Chacham Tzvi siman 102 he ruled that if the urine is less than a revi'it, a majority of water suffices to nullify it and one does not need a revi'it, for they said revi'it only to be lenient — that even if urine from one time is more than a revi'it, a revi'it suffices to nullify it, but not to be stringent; and even if the urine is from two times, a majority of water suffices to nullify them; and the later authorities agreed; and see further what is written below siman 82 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 4.

(8) two revi'ot — he ruled like the Rambam and the other posekim, and not like the Rashba who is lenient in this as explained in Beit Yosef and Magen Gibborim, who tends to say there is no dispute between them; and even the Rambam did not say that two revi'ot are needed for two times except when they come one after another — for example, he urinated and poured a revi'it of water into it; when he returns and urinates again he must again give water into it, and so forever, and what he poured the first time does not help for this; but if he urinated several times and wants to recite opposite them, it suffices with pouring a revi'it alone — see there; and in Biur HaGra he also agrees to the law that a revi'it alone suffices in this — see there, for I abbreviated.
