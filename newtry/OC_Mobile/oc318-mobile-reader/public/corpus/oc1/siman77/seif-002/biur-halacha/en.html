The revi'is they prescribed, etc., one occasion. See the Mishnah Berurah regarding what I wrote in the name of the Chacham Tzvi. Even according to the first opinion, one may say that only when he urinated in one place is a revi'is required to nullify the prescribed quantity. When, however, he discharged part in one place and part in another, it is sufficient that there be water corresponding to the amount in each place. This is stated by the Eliyah Rabbah and Peri Megadim.
