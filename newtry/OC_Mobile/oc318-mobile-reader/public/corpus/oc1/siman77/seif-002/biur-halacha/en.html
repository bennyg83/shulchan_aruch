The revi'it that they said, etc., one time — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote in the name of Chacham Tzvi; and even per the first view one could say that specifically when he urinated in one place then he needs a revi'it to nullify the measure; but if he discharged some in one place and some in another place, it suffices that there be only water corresponding to them [in each place] [Aruch HaShulchan and Peri Megadim].
