[ב] If a little, etc. But forbidden to calculate with him and say you took more on Shabbat and I less — divide the permitted as Raavad and Beit Yosef in the name of Geonim and Bach wrote; see seif 2 — end Magen Avraham. It appears he explains what Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 2 that later the Kuti agreed to divide equally — unspecified without mentioning the extra on Shabbat; thus Raavad may agree even when Shabbat was greater; but difficult — Beit Yosef disagrees with Raavad; he disagrees only with Raavad's plain meaning that even without mention it is forbidden; now Beit Yosef is resolved, etc.
