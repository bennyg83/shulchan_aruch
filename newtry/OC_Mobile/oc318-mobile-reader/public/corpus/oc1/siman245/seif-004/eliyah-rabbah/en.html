[4] And if they wish, etc. Taz disagrees with this, maintaining that the permission applies specifically to a mint or tax farm, which is a major enterprise and a person is anxious about it. The proof he brings from Rivash, that one may not take one weekday in exchange for Shabbos unless the payment is included with other days, may be rejected. There, the non-Jew appears to be the Jew’s agent, whereas here the Jew rents it to him for all Shabbosos. See Magen Avraham and Responsa Rabbi Yom Tov Tzahalon, siman 76.
