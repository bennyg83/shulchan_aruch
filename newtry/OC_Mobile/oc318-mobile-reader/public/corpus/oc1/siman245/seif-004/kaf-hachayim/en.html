(א) Two Jews partnered with a non-Jew in animals and grinding — if they stipulated at partnership that the non-Jew take Shabbat for himself and they another day corresponding — permitted; since they stipulated at acquisition the animals are as if acquired for the non-Jew on Shabbat without Jewish share, and on the weekday corresponding for Jews without non-Jew share; also the non-Jew will not refrain from melacha since he has a share and the Jew does not take sechar Shabbat since stipulated initially; if not stipulated initially — forbidden.
