A Jew is able to give to a non-Jew money to do business with even though the non-Jew will do business with it on Shabbat. He splits with him all the profits equally since this work isn’t considered that the Jew said to do work, that they non-Jew did it as his agent. Also the work isn’t recognizable to be his. RAMA: This is referring specifically to cases where the non-Jew did work by himself with the money but if everyone worked their day and the Jew needs to work corresponding to what the non-Jew did on Shabbat, then it’s prohibited.
