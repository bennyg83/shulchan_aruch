A Jew may give money to a non-Jew to do business with it, and even though the non-Jew buys and sells with it on Shabbos, he shares with him all the profit equally, because this melacha is not one that was incumbent upon the Jew to perform, such that the non-Jew would be acting as his agent, and likewise the business is not recognizable as belonging to whom. {Rama: And this is specifically in such a case, where the non-Jew alone buys and sells with the money; but if each one does business on his day, and the Jew must do business corresponding to what the non-Jew did on Shabbos, it is forbidden. [Beit Yosef in the name of a Gaon] And regarding a Jew who has a pledge from a non-Jew, see below, siman 325, seifim 2 and 3.}
