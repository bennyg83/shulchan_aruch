There: Unspecified, through havla’ah. Meaning, he should not say to the non-Jew at all, “You take the Shabbos earnings,” etc. Rather, the non-Jew should give the Jew his share of the entire week’s profit together. The Jew then receives the Shabbos earnings through havla’ah. Beit Yosef writes similarly in the name of Rivash regarding a Jew and a non-Jew who jointly own an animal, in a case where there is no prohibition of resting one’s animal on Shabbos. If the Jew says, “You take,” etc., it constitutes Shabbos wages without havla’ah. If he says nothing at all, it is permitted because the earnings are received through havla’ah. See there. This is cited briefly by Taz at the end of this siman.
