Money. If the non-Jew gives money to the Jew to conduct business with it, and the non-Jew conducts the business on Shabbos, it is certainly forbidden. Since this Shabbos activity was the Jew’s responsibility, the non-Jew is acting as his agent. Knesses HaGedolah. When the market day is on Shabbos, it is forbidden to give money to a non-Jew before Shabbos to purchase on his behalf on Shabbos, as stated in siman 307:3. This is unlike the merchants aboard ships who hand their flax to non-Jews upon arriving at a place so that they sell it on Shabbos. This is an absolute prohibition.
