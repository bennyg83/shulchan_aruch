And if. See Taz in siman 244, seif katan 6, who distinguishes between a substantial loss and other cases. He understands Rama to permit renting it out for Shabbos without inclusion, which is forbidden. If the payment is included with weekday payment, however, it is permitted to rent it out. Although an oven and bathhouse may not ordinarily be rented even through inclusion because of appearance, as explained in siman 243, here the non-Jew owns a share, so there is no concern of appearance. Magen Avraham, seif katan 8, explains that Rama permits only through inclusion. With Hashem’s help, this will be explained there.
