Where they did not stipulate at the outset, there is a remedy: the seller may return to them the purchase money for the property, or they may sell it to another person and then repurchase it jointly while making the stipulation at the time of purchase. If they entered a partnership in a shop without stipulating, each should take back his share and they should dissolve the partnership, after which they may enter a new partnership and stipulate from the outset. If they accepted land to work jointly, they should dissolve the partnership and waive their claims against one another, and afterward enter a new partnership and stipulate from the outset. {Rama: If the Jew wishes to lease his share to the non-Jew for Shabbos, or hire the non-Jew as a contractor, it is permitted, as explained above at the end of siman 244 regarding customs duties and minting, where it is permitted, and all the more so here.}
