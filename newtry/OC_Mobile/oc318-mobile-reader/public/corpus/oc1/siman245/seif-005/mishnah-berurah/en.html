(20) If he fixed payment, etc. The non-Jew then acts for his own benefit and is not the Jew's agent.
(21) Provided, etc. If market day is on Shabbos, even an unspecified instruction is prohibited, because it is equivalent to saying, “Sell for me on Shabbos.” See the later authorities below in siman 304.
(22) He may receive, etc. This is permitted even without absorbed payment, and no stipulation is necessary.
