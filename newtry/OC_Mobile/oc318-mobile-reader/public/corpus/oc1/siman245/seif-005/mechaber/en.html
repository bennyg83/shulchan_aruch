It is permissible for a Jew to give merchandise to a non-Jew to sell if he set his wages, as long as he didn’t say to him sell on Shabbat. An oven that was taken by a Jew as collateral from a non-Jew and the non-Jew received the profits of the oven, he gives to the Jew with interest: it is permissible to take the profits on Shabbat because it belongs to the non-Jew and the Jew has no part of it. Also, the Jew didn’t say to work on Shabbat so the non-Jew is working for the sake of himself, to fulfill the conditions.
