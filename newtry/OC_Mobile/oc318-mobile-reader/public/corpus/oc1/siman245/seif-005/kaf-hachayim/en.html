(ה) There. If a little or much, etc. — but forbidden to calculate, etc. as Raavad and Beit Yosef in Geonim and Bach. Magen Avraham s.k. 2. However Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Mahari Weil that wherever they stipulated initially it is permitted; and so Mateh Yehudah; for Mahari Weil and Shulchan Aruch seif 2 they fully permitted Raavad's invention, for they hold whenever stipulated initially there is no prohibition; for practical law we have only Shulchan Aruch who permitted in all cases of stipulation, not Magen Avraham s.k. 2; Taz s.k. 2 explains Shulchan Aruch; however Rabbi Akiva Eiger wrote Bach agrees if he explicitly said to me it is forbidden for it is evident, etc.
