[5] [Levush] In the manner explained in siman 243, etc. This is an error, for that matter is not mentioned there, but only at the end of siman 244. Indeed, Rama writes only at the end, “in the manner explained at the end of siman 244.” There is no doubt that this also refers back to the beginning of the gloss, concerning renting one’s share to the non-Jew for Shabbosos.
