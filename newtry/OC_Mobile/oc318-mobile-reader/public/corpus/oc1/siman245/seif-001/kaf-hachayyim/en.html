1) [Seif 1] A Jewish person and a non-Jew who jointly own a field or an oven, etc. Although the Jewish person's share does not improve during the week through the non-Jew's work in the oven on Shabbos as it does in a field, it is nevertheless forbidden, since he is obligated to work on Shabbos as well. When the Jewish person tells him, 'You work on Shabbos and I will work during the week,' it is as though he directly appoints a worker to perform labor. Tosafos, Avodah Zarah 22a. Accordingly, if the owners of the oven do nothing, but a non-Jew who owns dough heats the oven, bakes in it, and pays rent, although the Jewish person may not receive the rent because it is Shabbos wages, he may nevertheless divide the proceeds without specification when they are subsumed within other payment, as Beis Yosef writes at the end of this siman in the name of Rivash; see there. We are not concerned that people will say the non-Jew works for the Jewish person's benefit, since the non-Jew has a share in it, for otherwise it should be forbidden even when they stipulated initially. Ran writes the same, as stated in the gloss. Magen Avraham and Machatzis HaShekel, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 7.
