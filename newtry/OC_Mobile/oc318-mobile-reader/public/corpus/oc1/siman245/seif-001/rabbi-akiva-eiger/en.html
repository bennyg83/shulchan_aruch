Seif 1: At the time they came to divide it. In the case of a convert and a non-Jew who inherited from their non-Jewish father, an initial stipulation is effective. Since a convert’s inheritance is Rabbinic, the Sages were lenient and treated them as though they were now entering into a partnership. So writes Rosh, Demai 6:4, in the name of Tosafos. See Yoreh Deah 146:4.
