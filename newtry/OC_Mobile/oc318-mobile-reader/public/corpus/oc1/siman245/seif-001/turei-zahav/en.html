If they did not stipulate, etc. This is an unresolved question in the Gemara. The Tur writes: “My father, the Rosh, ruled that because it is a Rabbinic doubt, we follow the lenient view, and the Jew need not leave all the Shabbosos exclusively to the non-Jew. Rambam's words, however, appear stringent, for he writes: If they did not stipulate,” etc., as the Mechaber concludes here.
