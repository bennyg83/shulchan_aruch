s.k. 2: Since many find it hard to understand, I will explain. Tosafot wrote: in Rabbenu Tam's days Jews collected a debt — an oven from a non-Jew; the baker who stoked it had a share in the oven; the Jews had such-and-such days and the baker likewise; they did not stipulate initially take your share on Shabbat, etc.; and likewise R' Yehudah wanted to say it is permitted, etc.; Rabbenu Tam did not agree — even though there is no praise for melacha on Shabbat, nevertheless it is like setting a worker with one's hands; R' Elchanan appears to hold that even if they stipulated initially it does not help since the whole oven belongs to the Jew and it is like hiring his oven on Shabbat and telling the non-Jew take my oven rent on Shabbat when you stoke it on a weekday; R' Yehudah also retracted and brought proof to Rabbenu Tam there is no difference between oven and field; therefore Rabbenu Tam commanded to take the debt from the non-Jew and return and lend and stipulate initially, end of Tosafot. It appears since Tosafot's conclusion commanded Rabbenu Tam to stipulate, a condition at least helps even though it implies the oven was the Jew's alone and R' Elchanan does not hold so — he holds even the oven is forbidden — and that is Beit Yosef's view; however Magen Avraham explains differently and I will write the explanation of all Tosafot's words per his view: one may say he explains that per Tosafot the oven belonged to the Jew alone; and what Tosafot wrote 'the baker who stoked had a share in the oven' means their usual practice was that one who had an oven did not stoke and bake for himself but hired a baker who would bake in it and they would stipulate he takes such days and the baker such days — fixed custom for the baker not to change; the oven owner could not change his wages without the baker's will since the baker had this right from the start; when the Jew collected the oven in debt the baker's right remained as before; if they stipulated with the baker when collecting take such days it would be permitted for initially the Jew did not acquire baking on Shabbat and one cannot say the Jew sets a non-Jew as agent on Shabbat since when the Jew acquired the oven Shabbat work fell on the non-Jew; still one could fear when stipulating to take oven rent and obligating stoking even on weekdays the Jew takes wages alone — then it is like hiring his oven; but only when stipulating take Shabbat without including other days — then since the oven is the Jew's alone it is like his hire; but if he says at stipulation you are obligated to stoke and take such days it is sechar Shabbat with concealment and permitted as siman 246 regarding vessels used for melacha, see there; all this when stipulating at collection from the non-Jew; not when the Jew already acquired and the baker remains in his prior right such as oven owner one week and baker one week — even if now stipulating take Shabbat and I take other weekdays it does not help: though no prohibition in sechar Shabbat due to concealment, still forbidden because Rabbenu Tam said it is like setting a worker — when the Jew acquired the oven he acquired Shabbat day's wages and the non-Jew other days; exchanging wages now is like setting a worker for Shabbat belonging to Israel as before collection when the baker was the oven owner's worker on Shabbat; even exchanging, the baker remains Israel's worker on Shabbat only commanded to take payment himself and take corresponding other day — Rabbenu Tam's reason for forbidding without initial stipulation. R' Elchanan: even stipulating take Shabbat day does not help — no setting worker since initially the Jew ceased acquiring Shabbat wages; still taking other day's wages is forbidden as Shabbat wages not concealed; leniency only stipulating take such days initially, exiting concern of setting worker and hiring oven with concealment — Tosafot's intent per Magen Avraham, Ateret Zekenim, Mahari Weil who wanted to write oven law like field — permitted with condition initially take Shabbat as in field where condition helps; they wrote half the oven collected not 'baker had such days' — for if wholly Israel's alone need stipulate take such days; therefore mentioned half oven like field. Hagahot Ateret Zekenim: if baker has no share in oven even stipulation does not help — cannot acquire for Kuti on Shabbat unless he buys share in oven body forever on Shabbat — cannot buy truly on Shabbat so stipulation is only hiring and sechar Shabbat forbidden; if stipulating take such days with concealment permitted; in my humble opinion Rabbenu Tam does not hold like Ran in Rema's Hagahah that when Israel need not work it is permitted — else why need initial stipulation per Magen Avraham that Tosafot discusses wholly Israel's oven; why say non-Jew on Shabbat acts for himself when acquiring oven Israel was not obligated to stoke only baker stoked as Magen Avraham s.k. 1 on partners' oven — they do nothing only others bake and stoke and raise rent — permitted when mixed with other days' rent, see there; still need stipulate take such days lest like hiring oven as s.k. 10; not applicable to say non-Jew acts for himself since he gives Israel rent for oven — sechar Shabbat; nevertheless Rabbenu Tam requires initial stipulation lest setting worker — not like Ran who only when each works his day Israel obligated corresponding to non-Jew's Shabbat appears as agent; when Israel not obligated even at collection acquired that day not forced to work and non-Jew stokes for himself — even though stokes for him weekday, saying take such days is concealed sechar Shabbat permitted; possibly Ra'ash wrote half oven holding like Ran — half oven like setting worker in his place for Shabbat work and he works weekday in exchange, end quote; not when whole oven Israel's — Ran's setting-worker reasoning does not apply since this melacha was never imposed on Israel. One may say even Ran agrees to Rabbenu Tam per Tosafot whole oven Israel's: at collection Jew acquired Shabbat rent — exchanging wages commanding non-Jew take falling Shabbat pay and Jew other day is only exchanging money later — worse: while working he is Israel's worker as initially and weekday Israel takes in exchange for non-Jew's Shabbat work — forbidden sechar Shabbat as Bach wrote — concealment does not apply here only to hiring with concealment; this is not hiring with concealment but exchanging Shabbat wages for other money — what help mixing? Therefore Magen Avraham left matters general s.k. 6 whether we need (when Israel need not work, permitted) in oven case initial stipulation specifically and wrote only no help taking without condition and not Shabbat pay separately but concealed in other days — certainly forbidden even for Ran as hiring his oven since for this he stokes weekday — must stipulate take such days for concealment; regarding whether purchase must return to stipulation per Ran — left general: possibly not required, possibly required even for Ran at start of sale; in my humble opinion clear per Tosafot 'baker had share in oven' means actual share as Ra'ash; R' Elchanan 'whole oven Israel's' — possibly must emend 'if whole oven Israel's it would be forbidden even with stipulation' as Hagahot Ateret Zekenim; practical law follows R' Elchanan here.
