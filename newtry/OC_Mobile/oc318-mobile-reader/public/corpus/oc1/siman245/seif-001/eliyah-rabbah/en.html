[א] [Levush] As if he sets up, etc. If so, if they do nothing only the Kutim stoke the oven and bake in it and give rent — even though a Jew may not take rent because of sechar Shabbat, nevertheless one may divide unspecified with concealment and we are not concerned they will say it is for the Jew since the Kutis has a share; if not, even the oven would be forbidden until here is Magen Avraham's language. It appears to me what he wrote 'even the oven' is a scribal error and should read 'even the oven is not forbidden'; where the oven belongs only to the Jew and Kutim only stoke and bake and take so many days and the Jew agreed — Magen Avraham forbids even the oven, for it is like hiring his oven on Shabbat, etc.
