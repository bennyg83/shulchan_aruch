A Jew and a non-Jew, etc. If they stipulated from the outset, etc. See below, siman 450, seif 3, and Yoreh Deah 234:13. Where the oven itself belongs to the Jew and the non-Jewish baker who heats it receives a share of the oven’s earnings, while the Jew receives the earnings of a fixed number of days, the law is like that of an oven jointly owned by a Jew and a non-Jew. The Jew must stipulate initially that the Shabbos earnings belong exclusively to the non-Jew and that the earnings of one weekday corresponding to Shabbos belong exclusively to the Jew. If they did not stipulate initially, all Shabbos earnings belong solely to the non-Jew and the remainder is divided equally. If the amount of Shabbos earnings is unknown, the non-Jew alone takes one seventh of the total earnings and they divide the remainder equally. Tosafos Beit Yosef. See also Responsa Rabbi Eliyahu Mizrachi, part 1, siman 83, who writes that it is improper to tell the non-Jew, “You take the Shabbos earnings and I will take the weekday earnings.” See there.
