It is forbidden to cook or bake for dogs, but it is permitted to handle food and place it before them.Rema: But it is forbidden to carry it out for him from one domain to another. (Hagahot Maimoniyot and HaMagid ch. 1) Similarly it is forbidden to knead bran for fowl (Mordechai) except with a change. (Kol Bo) It is permitted to cook extra for the dogs in the same pot one is cooking in for oneself, even if' one has something else one could give the dogs if one wished.
