(א) But it is forbidden, etc. Since we hold like R' Yehuda HaGlili, no melacha was permitted on their account, and hotza'ah is a complete melacha and they permitted only carrying alone. Nevertheless. And likewise in Yerushalmi chapter 5: R' Yudan asked what is the law to take out straw from a private domain to the public domain; and it is not taught in the mishna "one who carries out the straw shall not sling the basket on his back" in the mishna as one who says "for every soul" — are animal souls included? What does he need, as one who says animal souls are not included, etc.; and likewise all the poskim that they did not permit any melacha for the sake of an animal; and this is what he means "and likewise forbidden," etc.; and as Rosh wrote what he wrote regarding these peels for animals, etc., that require preparation and are forbidden for R' Yosei HaGlili as above. And this is according to his view in siman 518; but according to Rambam's view and Shulchan Aruch there it is permitted; and nevertheless chapter 1 see there; and therefore they cited in Shulchan Aruch and in Tosafot Ketubot 7a s.v. mitokh, etc.:

(ב) But if etc. — that in such a case even on Shabbat it is permitted, as written at the end of Shabbat.

(ג) And it is permitted to increase, etc., even if he does not have, etc. Thus it should read; and likewise Magen Avraham; and he means if he has something else to give, it is permitted even in another pot as he wrote there — half a dough of a gentile, etc. And Ran explained that he forbade only in a large dough that has more than filling an oven, for in that oven it is permitted according to Rabbah Shimon ben Gamliel that there is no distinction between for the need of a Jew or profane or for the need of a gentile, as stated above; and likewise Yerushalmi as mentioned; and he challenges there, in the Gemara regarding dog dough, etc., and they answer "since it is possible"; and he challenges "does he hold," etc.; and he could have challenged that "since" is effective only for lashes and not for prohibition, for otherwise it would be permitted to bake for profane need, as he wrote — one is not lashed; rather he challenged preferable — that even for lashes "since" is not needed and they answer only a negative commandment, etc.; and one must say as Ran wrote that in such a case there is not even a prohibition; and see in Ran there and see in Mishnah Berurah chapter 1 of the laws of Yom Tov; and from the aforementioned Yerushalmi that they establish it like Rabbah Shimon ben Gamliel one must say there is no carcass and it disagrees with the Gemara's establishment; and nevertheless for practical ruling the Gemara does not follow Yerushalmi; and this is what he means "and even if he does not have," etc. — that if he has, even in another pot it is permitted like our Gemara; and specifically for dogs; but for slaves and maidservants above in seif 61 in the Hagah, we do not need Raavyah who also says it is possible to appease them; rather like Mordechai's view — specifically in that pot as written in Yerushalmi; and see Peri Megadim s.k. 1:
