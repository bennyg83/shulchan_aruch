(ג) There. One does not cook for a gentile etc., and the same for Karaites. Maharar Betzalel siman 63 in the name of Radbaz. And in responsa Maharshak vol. 3 siman 15 he wrote that per the view of Maharash Chazan Halevi z"l it is permitted to cook for Karaites on Yom Tov. Thus Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tashbetz.
