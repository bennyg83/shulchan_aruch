3) There. One may not cook for a non-Jew, etc. The same applies for the non-Jewish group discussed by the authorities. Rabbi Betzalel, siman 63, writes this in the name of Radbaz. Responsa Maharshach, volume 3, siman 15, however, writes that according to Rabbi Shmuel Chakan HaLevi, cooking for that group on Yom Tov is permitted.
