(א) Seif 2 — sons of the soldiers etc. Even though they said there and it disputes R' Yehudah ben Bava etc. — Rambam explains there is no contradiction with Rav Huna, for he did not hold like R' Hillel: if they did not bake all the bread for them they could not bake one loaf to give the child; unlike in his time the Israelite's portion would not be diminished, for he gives from Israel's portion. Rather it refers to what is written there; and per R' Yehudah Gaon who said etc. these pots etc. on Shabbat what is there to say etc. — evidently whatever is forbidden to do melacha for him on Yom Tov is forbidden to carry for him on Shabbat; and it disputes R' Yehudah ben Bava who said one invites on Shabbat and carries for him, even though one does not invite on Yom Tov etc. Maggid Mishneh there.

(ב) It is forbidden to cook and to bake, etc. As R' Yosei HaGlili, for the anonymous tanna follows him regarding dog dough, etc.; and this is what he means by "to cook" as he wrote there — but that it is fit for dogs is a tanna dispute, etc.; and regarding baking dough, etc.; and there he wrote regarding (2): if so, from what is "for you" "for you" and not, etc.; they also hold like R' Yehuda HaGlili; and Rif wrote; and we hold: anonymous mishna and dispute in the braita, etc., as written in chapter 4 of Yevamot; and Rashaz challenged from that which Abaye said to R' Yehuda: these peels, etc.; and explicitly we learn: one may move a carcass before dogs and we permit sheaves that burst open and we chop and we glean and we give water to animal fodder and we give water before, etc. — evidently all of these are not like R' Yehuda HaGlili. And one can answer Rosh that regarding carrying there is no one who disagrees, for Beit Hillel say "for you" and not for dogs, and they say one removes from the table, etc., which are animal food — rather one must say as Rashbam and Tosafot there wrote that peels are not fit except through preparation, but sometimes animals eat through duress. And he challenges R' Yosei HaGlili: since it is impossible to prepare it, etc.; and what he challenges in Shabbat, etc., he challenges even according to R' Akiva: since it is not fit for an animal except through duress it is muktzeh; and this is what he means here "but it is permitted," etc. And according to Peri Megadim who disagrees, etc., there is no need for all this, for we hold like R' Yehuda ben Levi; and even though melacha is forbidden on Yom Tov, carrying is permitted on Shabbat and Yom Tov; and therefore one must say thus, that Rambam does not explain like Rashbam's explanation, for otherwise what is "and she disagrees"?
