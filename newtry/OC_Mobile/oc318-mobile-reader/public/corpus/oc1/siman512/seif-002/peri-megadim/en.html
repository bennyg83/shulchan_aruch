An apostate to provoke who wears shaatnez and is warned regarding impure things — one may say permitted to invite him; or in another pot, since see Shalem Shabbat in the name of Ran: also your brother he is and not diminished from you; and if apostate only for non-kosher — one may say forbidden lest he increase impure things in a pot alone; and lashes per Tosafot Pesachim 47b s.v. velokeh — see there; and Magen Avraham ot 1 Karaites — see there. And there it will be explained further: convert and resident alien — even though there is a mitzvah to sustain him, diminished from you; and now otherwise not practiced when yovel is not practiced; and one may say for you and not for a gentile — nevertheless the permission nevertheless permitted also for weekday need, since prohibition otherwise for every living soul is equivalent to the majority, unlike a prohibition; and one may say: if not for you I would increase for all — even for a gentile in prohibition; this requires further study. And one may say the prohibition rests on him and nevertheless not diminished from every living soul; and similar to terumah in a kohen's hand permitted to fix on Yom Tov — and for every living soul it is called; and specifically something that is only for enjoyment; and siman 514 in Magen Avraham 2 regarding healing — and there it will be explained; or meat of a gentile and Israel cooks it.
