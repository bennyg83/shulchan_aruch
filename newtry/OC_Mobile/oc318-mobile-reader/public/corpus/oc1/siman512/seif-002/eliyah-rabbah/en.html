To send, etc. It implies that even if he initially intended to send to him, it is permitted, for by means of sending we do not suspect that he will cook another pot on his behalf; which is not so when the non-Jew eats at his [home], where we do suspect. And this is what the Rama and the Levush wrote at the end: but for other non-Jews, etc. And likewise it is possible to align the words of Yam shel Shlomo, chapter 2, in siman 23. And the Taz elaborated and wondered, and with what I have explained it is resolved.
