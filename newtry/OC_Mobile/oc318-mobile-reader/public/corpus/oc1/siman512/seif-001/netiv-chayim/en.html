(Magen Avraham sk 5) It implies in Tosafot etc. I will note: per what is written in siman 324 sk 4 we consider food equivalence through coercion without cutting — it is possible to say that this entire passage where Abaye said to Rav Yosef to R' Yosi haGlili these pots for animals goes to Rav Huna who answers according to him — and he holds food equivalence forbidden; and these pots even if eaten through coercion are food equivalence; unlike for us who rule like R' Yehuda that changing food is permitted — nevertheless they ruled anonymously permitted to carry and place before them. But otherwise it is possible to say the law is: permitted to carry provisions and place before them in something fit as animal food as it is; and this teaches to exclude the view of Baal HaMaor who forbids all carrying of food for R' Yosi; and we rule like R' Yosi haGlili.
