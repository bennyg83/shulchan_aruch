(א) For a gentile's need. And the same for Karaites (R' Betzalel siman 63) and Maharshak vol. 3 siman 15 wrote some permit (thus).

(ב) Through a gentile permitted. Even food via public domain (Darkei Moshe); and the matter is thus seif 2; and Yeshuot Shalom forbids even through a gentile; it appears to me to be lenient in our time since we do not have a complete public domain: after one prepared all meal needs near the meal, permitted to invite him (Tanya); it appears to me only when he did not have intention on him before the meal — such as the gentile came to him near the meal.

(ג) In that pot. Even if it is not possible to appease them with something else — for the view that it is possible to appease them, even in another pot it is permitted; and what Beit Yosef wrote in this siman — Yerushalmi without dispute — it appears to me we do not hold like him, for according to Yerushalmi that deals with one oven, even if it is not possible to appease them it is permitted, as Beit Yosef himself wrote on Achronim's words that this is not the poskim's view, see there; and according to our Gemara the reason is because it is possible to appease them — even in another pot and in one oven it is permitted; and Mordechai does not hold the reason is that it is possible to appease them, therefore he permitted only in that pot even if it is not possible to appease them, etc., end quote Darkhei Moshe; and if so what he wrote here "but for other gentiles in every matter it is forbidden" — that is because they decreed lest one increase on his account in another pot, as Rosh wrote explicitly and likewise Yeshuot Shalom [and the commentaries who commented in Shulchan Aruch Achronim and Tosafot erred in this]; but what Rama wrote — Beit Yosef's words contradict each other — are reconciled in Yeshuot Shalom's words who wrote that specifically in a pot it is permitted to increase for one's slave and maidservant, but in one oven it is forbidden, for each loaf and loaf requires effort separately unless he has something with which to appease them, as Beit Yosef wrote; and so is the essence, and as is evident in the Gemara that says "and disagrees with R' Yehuda ben Levi," etc., and the incident of the king's troops was in one oven — therefore it implies explicitly it is forbidden; and even according to Rambam's version it is not permitted except for the reason that if they would not bake all the bread they would not give one loaf to the infant, as written in seif 2; but without this it is forbidden to increase in one oven even for one's slave and maidservant or for an animal unless he has something with which to appease them; and in one pot it is permitted even if he does not have something with which to appease them; and in another pot or in another oven it is forbidden even if he has something with which to appease them, for it is forbidden to bake on Yom Tov not for need; and likewise Yeshuot Shalom; and Bach's words require further study; and see Beit Hillel siman 503 in the name of Mordechai HaArukh and see this siman and siman 506 seif 6:
