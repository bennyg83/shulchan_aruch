1) [Se'if 1] One may not cook for a non-Jew. The reason is that the verse states, 'Only that which may be eaten by every person, that alone may be done for you,' and the Sages expound: 'for you,' but not for a non-Jew. Beitzah 21a; Beis Yosef; Levush; Taz, subsection 1.
2) There. One may not cook for a non-Jew, etc. The same applies to every melachah that may be performed for a Jew on Yom Tov: performing even part of it for a non-Jew is forbidden. Chayei Adam, rule 21, subsection 1.
3) There. One may not cook for a non-Jew, etc. The same applies for the non-Jewish group discussed by the authorities. Rabbi Betzalel, siman 63, writes this in the name of Radbaz. Responsa Maharshach, volume 3, siman 15, however, writes that according to Rabbi Shmuel Chakan HaLevi, cooking for that group on Yom Tov is permitted. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur; Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Eshel Avraham, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2.
