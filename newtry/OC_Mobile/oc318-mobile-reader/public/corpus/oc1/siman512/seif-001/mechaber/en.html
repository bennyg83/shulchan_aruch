Not to cook for a non-Jew on Yom Tov. 3 sections: One may not cook for a non-Jew on Yom Tov. Therefore it is forbidden to invite
him, lest one cook extra on his account — and specifically inviting him But one's male or female servant, as well as a messenger who was sent to him, and similarly a non-Jew who comes on his own — it is permitted to feed them along with oneself, and we are not concerned that one will cook extra on their account.to one's home; but sending food to his home via a non-Jew is permitted. (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 1 in the name of Or Zarua) Rema: It is permitted to cook extra for one's male or female servant in the same pot one is cooking in for oneself. (Mordechai ch. 2 of Beitzah) But for other non-Jews it is forbidden in any case. (Orchot Chayyim and Tosafot) A Jew who bakes in a non-Jew's oven and must give him one loaf — he should not designate a specific loaf for the non-Jew before baking, as that would mean he is baking for the non-Jew; rather he should bake without designation and then give him one afterward.
