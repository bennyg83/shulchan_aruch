(א) Gentile. And the same applies for Karaites. R' Betzalel and Maharshak wrote that some permit. Knesset HaGedolah there — see there.

(ב) Through a gentile. It appears this case is when there is a carrying prohibition to the gentile's house; therefore the gentile's agent is required. But through a Jew it is forbidden, even on Yom Tov — eruv and carrying out apply for Israel's need but not for a gentile's need. If the gentile lives in the public domain where carrying is permitted there, it is also permitted through Israel's agent — Taz there. It is written in Sefer Tanya: after one prepared all the meal's needs near the meal, it is permitted to invite him. Magen Avraham wrote; it appears to me only when he did not have intention toward him before the meal — such as the gentile came to him near the meal. See Taz.
