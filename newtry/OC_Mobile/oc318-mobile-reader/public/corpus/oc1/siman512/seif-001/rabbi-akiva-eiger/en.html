Magen Avraham sk 6 — in one pot permitted to increase in any matter; nevertheless somewhat difficult — Rama should have explained also that he has something else he can give the dogs, for even in another pot permitted; for per what we hold that even without it permitted in one pot — automatically evident from the passage that establishes the case of dogs' dough that he has with which to appease them — meaning it must establish even in two ovens; and thus explained explicitly in Darkei Moshe and as Magen Avraham wrote sk 3. Nevertheless Beit Yosef's view: in another pot forbidden immediately; and in that pot requires that he have — meaning to add afterward; but to fill one pot in any matter — permitted.
