5) There: It is forbidden to rent them to a non-Jew on Friday. One may, however, rent even utensils used for labor to a Jewish person, even on Friday, provided the payment is subsumed within payment for other days. Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. Although the Jewish person will certainly perform no labor with it on Shabbos, subsuming the payment is required because he will nevertheless use it for an activity that is not labor. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2.
