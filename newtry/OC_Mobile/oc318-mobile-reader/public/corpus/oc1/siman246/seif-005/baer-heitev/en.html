A Jew. If the horses belong to the Jew, and the non-Jew is also his servant and transports his merchandise, a sale provides no permission, since the Jew may not allow him to perform his work. If, however, the non-Jew is hired solely to travel with the horses, it is permitted according to the Rambam, as stated in siman 244, seif 5. In any event, it is forbidden to tell him, "Go on Shabbos," as stated in siman 252. The Bach writes that those who own horses and wagons, transport loads for payment on Shabbos, and deliberately sell them to a non-Jew in order to evade the prohibition of Shabbos violate the words of the Sages and deserve to be penalized. See Tzemach Tzedek, siman 35. For a mitzvah purpose, however, such as transporting esrogim, it is permitted.
