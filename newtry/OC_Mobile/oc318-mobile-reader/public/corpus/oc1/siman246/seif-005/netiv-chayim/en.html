There: “As the Rosh wrote in the chapter Mi Shehotziuhu in the name of Rashbam.” Note: His words are astonishing. Rashbam wrote this only regarding one who sits in a wagon, following Rabbi Meir, who exempts one who sits in a wagon regarding kilayim. But where one rides and directs the animal with his feet, so that it proceeds because of him, Rashbam also agrees that it is forbidden to go beyond the techum, because a rider is considered as one who walks, as Magen Avraham explains at length in siman 266. Regarding one who rides upon kilayim, all agree that he receives lashes. Examine this carefully.
