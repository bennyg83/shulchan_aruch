Seif 5: If a Jew. As explained above in siman 245, seif 1. Here too, the property is not acquired by the Jew on Shabbos.
And if he lends, etc. This follows the second opinion in seif 4.
And some permit, etc., even. This follows the first opinion there.
And he should make them an apotiki. Even so, the Jew does not acquire them, as stated in Pesachim 31a. Rava says that a creditor acquires collateral only from the time of collection onward, since if the debtor had money, etc.; it follows that he acquires it only now. For this same reason, the rule applies even to an apotiki, as stated in Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat 106:6: even if the collateral was in his possession or it was an express apotiki, since, etc. See there and what is stated there. This is also the meaning of 'or he should take them as collateral,' as stated in Pesachim there, that a Jew does not acquire a pledge from a non-Jew.
Provided that he does not. See there, and see below, siman 441.
And some permit, etc. This too follows the first opinion in seif 4.
And he should write, etc. See Magen Avraham.
