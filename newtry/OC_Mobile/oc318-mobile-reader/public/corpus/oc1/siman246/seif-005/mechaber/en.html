If a Jew and a Non-Jew are partners in an animal, the Non-Jew may use it for work on Shabbat if it was established with the non-Jew at the beginning, when it was bought, that the non-Jew will assume control of it on Shabbat and the Jew will have control of it during the weekdays. If they did not stipulate to this at the beginning, it is forbidden, even though they may have stipulated to this afterwards....
