And what Magen Avraham wrote, and these are his words: and what the Yerushalmi wrote — we do not ride because of shvisas beheimah; behold a living being carries itself, etc. And seemingly, per what he wrote that shvisah does not apply to hotzaah at all and a person is also permitted — if so, without this that a living being carries itself, one should have asked that even if the rider is like a load it is also permitted on Yom Tov; and it is strained to say that he preferred this and cited that even on Shabbat there is no prohibition of a living being carrying itself, as the Bach's difficulty in siman 305 — this is strained; and thus I heard they ask in the name of the eminent rabbi R' Naftali son of R' Levi z"l. And further — how is this difficulty connected to rejecting the proof from what was written above? And in my humble opinion the intent of Magen Avraham is because one can say in the intent of the Bach that seemingly the Bach properly brought proof from what he wrote that one is not commanded regarding shvisas beheimah on Yom Tov, and there is no rejection that there it deals with hotzaah and hotzaah was also permitted for a person, etc. — for in truth this is what he said: for the Bach challenged the Yerushalmi (per the Bach's words, the Yerushalmi's words actually refer to Shabbat) that behold a living being carries itself, and he needed to answer that even though one is not commanded regarding riding it on an animal, nevertheless since the animal suffers in his riding upon it he violates shvisas beheimah — see in the Bach. And accordingly, if we say that on Yom Tov also the positive commandment of shvisas beheimah applies — what of it that hotzaah is permitted on Yom Tov? Nevertheless it is forbidden to ride on it and to take out loads, since it has pain in riding and taking out loads is included in shvisas beheimah; rather one must say that Shulchan Aruch holds that on Yom Tov the positive commandment of shvisas beheimah does not apply at all, and this Yerushalmi teaches regarding Shabbat; and thus Magen Avraham wrote that the Bach's difficulties on the Yerushalmi regarding a living being carrying itself are not difficult for us, and the continuation of Magen Avraham's words is thus: that Yeshuos Yaakov brings proof from the plain meaning of the Yerushalmi's words — it appears that regarding Yom Tov it teaches; and on this Magen Avraham wrote; and see Bach siman 305 — meaning that there the Bach brought the Yerushalmi's words regarding Shabbat because he proved from what was written above that there is no shvisas beheimah on Yom Tov, and therefore he established it regarding Shabbat; and therefore Magen Avraham wrote that from Shulchan Aruch's words there is no proof, for there it deals with hotzaah, and in any case for other hotzaah the positive commandment of shvisas beheimah applies on Yom Tov; and that which the Yerushalmi [forbids riding] — the reason is not because it suffers in his riding, only the reason is because of techumin.
