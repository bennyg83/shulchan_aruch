Because. See Taz and Magen Avraham. This indicates that appearance is prohibited even regarding a double rabbinic prohibition. As written in siman 244, however, where there is some need a double rabbinic prohibition is permitted, and certainly appearance is then permitted; see Beit Yosef to siman 325. If it is evident that the non-Jew will take the item beyond the Shabbat boundary, as with a plow and the like, it remains prohibited even in a walled city. See Netziv and Taz to siman 444: if one knows he will take it beyond the boundary, he may not expressly instruct him to do so, and beyond twelve mil, according to the view that this is biblical, it is not a double rabbinic prohibition. This will be explained there. See Eliyah Rabbah.
