The beast of Israel. An Israelite who has a horse and the governor comes to sell it to lend it or rent it, and he cannot resist it because of enmity, we will sell it to him for a small amount, and then the governor can do with it as he wishes, and if later he returns and gives him the horse and the rent is also a gift in Alma Yahav. Answer of Shabbat Ya'akov 72 39 A.S. And the author Yad Aharon disagrees with him and wrote to the LN that if the gentile can afford the seller even if they sold him for a small amount, it is permissible, and if he cannot afford it, the seller is prohibited.
