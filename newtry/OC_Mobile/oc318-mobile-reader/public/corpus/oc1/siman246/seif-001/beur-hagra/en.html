Even though he stipulates. Here the rationale concerning rental does not apply, namely that the Jew profits, etc. See there in the Rosh, and see Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 6, seif katan 100. A baraita in the Tosefta teaches: 'One who rents, etc., and exchanges, etc.' Its meaning is that this is not an actual rental; rather, it states one case: how may he exchange? For an actual rental on Friday is forbidden, since there the ruling is stated without qualification.
