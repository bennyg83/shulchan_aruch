1) [Seif 1] It is permitted to lend or rent out one's utensils, etc. According to Maran's unattributed opinion, there is no distinction: whether the utensils are used for labor or are not used for labor, it is permitted to lend or rent them out even on Friday. According to the opinion introduced as 'some say,' utensils used for labor may not be rented out on Friday, because the non-Jew appears to be acting as the Jewish person's agent, but utensils not used for labor are permitted. The requirement that rental payment be subsumed within payment for other days, however, applies even to utensils not used for labor, and all opinions agree to this, as Beis Yosef writes; see there.
