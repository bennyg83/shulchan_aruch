From the entrance of his house. See siman 252:1. The words of the Olat Shabbat imply that this does not refer specifically to Friday.
He instructed him to take it out. Even according to us, who do not have a fully public domain, it is forbidden, as stated in the Beit Yosef at the beginning of siman 325. Accordingly, where the city is enclosed by a wall and carrying throughout it is permitted, this would be allowed. Rambam, however, writes in chapter 6: “When it leaves the entrance of his house on Shabbat, it appears as though he lent to the non-Jew, took a pledge from him, fixed terms with him, or sold to him on Shabbat.” Accordingly, the omission of Rambam’s reason by Beit Yosef requires study. In a case of need, one may be lenient.
