Any utensil. This includes even utensils not used for labor, and it is prohibited even according to the first opinion.
Even on Friday, if he can, etc. In that situation it is prohibited even to sell it to him or give it as a gift, as explained below in siman 252:1; see the Mishnah Berurah there.
Before nightfall. Meaning before sunset.
He instructed him to take it out. Meaning into the public domain. Even according to our practice, where a full biblical public domain is generally absent, it is prohibited, because instructing a non-Jew is a rabbinic prohibition even regarding a rabbinic matter. If the city is walled and carrying throughout it is permitted, and the non-Jew also resides there, one may lend him utensils not used for labor. Selling or renting them is nevertheless prohibited, lest people say that he sold or rented them on Shabbat. If they are utensils used for labor, even lending is prohibited unless the non-Jew removes them from the entrance of the Israelite's home before Shabbat, lest people say that he performs labor on Shabbat as the Israelite's agent.
