Note: See Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 63, concerning a halachic authority who permitted tobacco sellers to have a non-Jew perform their work on Shabbos in their homes after declaring the tobacco ownerless on Friday. He writes that the ruling was improper. First, the permissibility of ownerlessness was stated only regarding the resting of one's animal, not to allow a non-Jew to perform the Jew's work. Second, one may rely upon ownerlessness only when the ownerless property is outside the Jew's domain, not when it remains in his house. Third, there is at least a concern of maris ayin. He concludes that it is forbidden to permit this.
