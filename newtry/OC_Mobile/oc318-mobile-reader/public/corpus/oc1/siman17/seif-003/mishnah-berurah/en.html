(9) To acquire tzitzis for him — meaning to acquire for him a four-cornered garment and to place tzitzis on it in order to educate him in mitzvos. The measure of his tallis is written in Peri Megadim in siman 16 and in Derech HaChaim that it suffices to wrap his head and most of himself in it; and we estimate with the minor himself who wraps in it according to his size and according to his smallness. And if it has this measure, then his father must place tzitzis on it and bless with him; and if it does not have this measure, they do not bless on it:

(10) To educate him — all this is specifically when he has not yet reached thirteen; but from thirteen onward he is obligated in tzitzis like an adult. And that which is written in the derashos of Maharil in the laws of marriage that the custom is that even older youths do not wrap in tzitzis until they marry wives, and they relied on the verse “You shall make yourself twisted cords” which is adjacent to “When a man takes a wife” — this is astonishing, that until he marries a woman he should sit idle from the mitzvah of tzitzis:
