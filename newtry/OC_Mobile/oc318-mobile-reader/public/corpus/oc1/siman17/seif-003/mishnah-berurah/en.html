(9) To acquire tzitzis for him. Meaning, to acquire a four-cornered garment for him and attach tzitzis to it in order to educate him in the mitzvah. Peri Megadim in siman 16 and Derech HaChayim write that the measure of his tallis is enough to cover his own head and most of his own body. The measurement follows the size of that particular minor. If it contains this measure, his father must attach tzitzis to it and recite the blessing with him. If it does not contain this measure, no blessing is recited over it.
(10) To educate him. This applies specifically before he reaches thirteen years old. From thirteen onward he is obligated in tzitzis like an adult. Mahari Weil's Derashos, in the laws of marriage, states that the custom is for even older unmarried youths not to wrap themselves in tzitzis until they marry, relying upon the juxtaposition of “You shall make fringes for yourself” to “When a man takes a wife.” This is astonishing. Until he marries, should he sit idle from the mitzvah of tzitzis?
