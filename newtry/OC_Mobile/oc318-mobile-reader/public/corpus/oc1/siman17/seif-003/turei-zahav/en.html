“Since it is not an obligation of the person.” With this one resolves why women bless on lulav, which is also a positive time-bound mitzvah, and one answers that it is different there because it is not an obligation of the person — for even a man has no biblical obligation to acquire a four-cornered tallis; only if he wraps himself is he obligated to make tzitzis on it, unlike lulav, for regarding a man it is an obligation of the person, being an obligation of the body.
