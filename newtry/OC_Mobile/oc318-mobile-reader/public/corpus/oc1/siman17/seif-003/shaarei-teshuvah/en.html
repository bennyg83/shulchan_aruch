“They too shall bless.” Erekh HaTalmud, and that which is written that if it is certain one should not bless so much — likewise regarding the daily burnt offering and in Beit Yosef siman 490 he wrote: what of it? If because of her gender they were not commanded, women too were not commanded and bless — then likewise a certainty, see there; and his words are correct. And see in Shaagat Aryeh siman 31, who wrote that shaatnez in tzitzis the Merciful One permitted only in a place of mitzvah; therefore tumtum and androgynos, although obligated out of doubt, nevertheless shaatnez is forbidden. Therefore even for those great ones who hold a sheet in tzitzis must make white of its kind — nevertheless for tumtum and androgynos one may not place even white wool and linen, since he is not fit for dyeing of tekhelet by Torah law, see there. And it appears that even if we say he is a distinct creature, Mishnah Berurah would not reduce the prohibition of shaatnez, for women are also obligated; and if so the reason fits well that he is not fit for dyeing of tekhelet. And further he wrote in Shaagat Aryeh there that if the garment is doubtfully subject to obligation and it is forbidden to place tekhelet on it because of doubt of shaatnez, then even if it is of linen one may not place even white, for the above reason that he is not fit for dyeing of tekhelet from tekhelet, see there:
