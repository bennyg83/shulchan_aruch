“Since.” Taz — and the reading is “since it is not an obligation of the person,” for one is not obligated to acquire a tallis. Taz 1 — and I understood in Levush that he emends “since” to “and it is not” only obligation of the person; and in Levush, “man’s garment” applies to a woman as a man’s vessel — and I do not see that Levush emends thus, but he wrote his view separately — see there. Tumtum and androgynos are obligated out of doubt, for a Torah doubt is stringent; and other types per the Mechaber — rabbinically they may go without tzitzis, for a rabbinic doubt is lenient.
