Since. See Taz. The correct reading is: “since it is not an obligation upon the person,” meaning that one is not obligated to purchase a tallis. See Taz, seif katan 1. Eliyah Rabbah understood Levush to emend the text to: “since it is only an obligation upon the person,” and to add that a man's garment is inappropriate for a woman. I do not see that Levush made such an emendation. Rather, he stated his own independent explanation; see there. A tumtum and an androgynos are obligated because of doubt, since a doubt concerning a Torah obligation is treated stringently. Regarding garments of other materials, whose obligation according to the Mechaber is D'Rabbanan, they may wear them without tzitzis because a doubt concerning a D'Rabbanan matter is treated leniently. The same applies to a garment whose obligation is doubtful, as in siman 10, seif 7: for them it becomes a double doubt. See what is written in Magen Avraham concerning this.
