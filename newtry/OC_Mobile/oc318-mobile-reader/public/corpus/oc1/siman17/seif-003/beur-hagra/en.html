Section 3, "And specifically, etc." The Jerusalem Talmud there says: "It is not only to wrap himself, but to throw two corners behind him and two corners before him properly at the time of the recitation of Shema." Hagahot Maimoniyot. The reading of the Mordechai in Sukkah there is: "before him, and he holds the tzitzit properly, etc." This is what the Shulchan Arukh means by "and knows, etc." See Tosafot in Arakhin 2b, in the comment beginning "Who knows, etc."
