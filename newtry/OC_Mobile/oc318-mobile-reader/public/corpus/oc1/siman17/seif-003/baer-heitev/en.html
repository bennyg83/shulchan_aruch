Mahari Weil's Derashos, in the laws of marriage, states that the custom is for even older unmarried youths not to wrap themselves in tzitzis until they marry, and they rely upon the juxtaposition of the verse, “You shall make fringes for yourself,” to the verse, “When a man takes a wife.” This is astonishing. Until he marries, should he sit idle from the mitzvah of tzitzis? Shiurei Knesses HaGedolah; see there. See Kiddushin 8a and 29a, and Beit Yosef, siman 8. Magen Avraham, siman 8, seif katan 3, writes that the Gemara implies that an unmarried young man did not cover his head with a tallis; see there. This is not compelling, because that passage does not concern a tallis used for the mitzvah, but rather a scholar's head covering.
