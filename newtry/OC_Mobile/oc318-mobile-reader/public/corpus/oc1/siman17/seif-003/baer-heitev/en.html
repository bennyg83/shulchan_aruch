Tzitzit to educate him. Specifically less than age thirteen; but from thirteen and onward he is obligated in tzitzit like an adult. And what is written in the derashot of Maharil in Hilchot Nisu'in: the custom is that even older youths do not wrap themselves in tzitzit until they marry women, and they relied on the verse "You shall make twisted cords for yourself," which is adjacent to "When a man takes a wife"—this is astonishing: until he marries a wife, should he sit idle from the mitzvah of tzitzit? See Knesset HaGedolah there. And see in the Gemara Kiddushin 8 and 29, and in Beit Yosef siman 8.
