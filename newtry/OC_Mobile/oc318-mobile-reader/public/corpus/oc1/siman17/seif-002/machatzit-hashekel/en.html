(Seif katan 2) And it is not. Meaning, one is not obligated, etc. Magen Avraham comes to resolve the apparent difficulty that, on the contrary, we rule in siman 19 that tzitzis is an obligation upon the person. He therefore writes that the meaning is as follows. According to the opinion that tzitzis is an obligation upon the garment, a four-cornered garment requires tzitzis even when it is not being worn but lies in a box. We, however, rule that it is an obligation upon the person, and so long as he is not wearing it, it does not require tzitzis. Nevertheless, in another respect it is not an obligation upon the person like tefillin, which one must purchase. A tallis is different. For men it is proper, good, and correct to purchase a four-cornered tallis, because in a time of Divine anger one may, Heaven forbid, be punished for failing to do so, as the angel said to Rav Ketina: a person ought to bring himself into a state of obligation. Since even for men it is not an absolute obligation, when a woman does so it appears arrogant.
