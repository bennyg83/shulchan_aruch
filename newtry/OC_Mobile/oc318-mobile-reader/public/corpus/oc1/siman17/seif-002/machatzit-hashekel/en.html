(Note 2) “And he is not.” Meaning he is not obligated, etc. This comes to resolve “but behold” — on the contrary we hold in siman 19 that it is an obligation of the person. Therefore he wrote that the meaning is according to those who say it is an obligation of the object: a four-cornered garment, even if one does not wear it but keeps it in a box, is obligated in tzitzis; but we hold it is an obligation of the person, and whoever does not wear it is not obligated in tzitzis. Nevertheless, for this matter it is not an obligation of the person like tefillin, which one must buy, unlike a tallis — rather for men it is fitting and good and proper to buy a four-cornered tallis, because in a time of wrath, God forbid, we are punished for it, as the angel said to Rav Ketina that a person should bring himself to a matter of obligation.
