Caused by time. See Taz. In the general introduction we wrote that servants are derived from women through a verbal analogy. As Levush writes, perhaps servants should instead be obligated through the juxtaposition: whoever is included in the prohibition of wearing shaatnez is included in the commandment of fringes. Regarding women, Tosafos on Yevamos 4a write that the comparison in the verse, “so that the Torah of Hashem shall be in your mouth,” by which the entire Torah is compared to tefillin, is stronger than the juxtaposition. But regarding a verbal analogy, perhaps according to the opinion in the chapter HaShole'ach that a comparison is stronger, juxtaposition and a verbal analogy are of equal force. If so, we should derive stringently from the juxtaposition that servants are obligated. A proof is that Rabbi Shimon on Menachos 43a exempts women but does not conclude by mentioning servants.
