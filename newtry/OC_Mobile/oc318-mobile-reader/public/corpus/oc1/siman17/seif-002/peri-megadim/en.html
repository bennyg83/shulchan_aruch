“That time.” Taz — in the introduction I wrote that slaves are derived from woman by verbal analogy, as Levush wrote. One could say slaves are obligated from “All that has no ‘you shall not wear’ has no twisted cords,” and woman — Tosafot Yevamot 4a wrote that the comparison “so that the Torah of God shall be in your mouth” is compared thus to tefillin, which is preferable to “All that.” But verbal analogy is possible for one who says in HaSholeach that comparison is preferable; if so, “All that” and verbal analogy are equivalent, and for stringency we derive from “All that.” And proof: Rashi in Menachot 43a exempts women and does not finish with slaves:
