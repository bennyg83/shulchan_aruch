And servants. See Yad Aharon siman 16:

Obligation of the person. That is, he is not obligated to acquire a tallit for himself, as stated in siman 24. And know that we rule regarding tzitzit: both "obligation of the person" and "not obligation of the person"—and both are lenient. "Obligation of the person" is lenient to exclude obligation of the garment: all the time he does not wear the tallit, even though it has four corners, it is exempt from tzitzit. "Not obligation of the person"—he is not obligated to buy himself a tallit in order to become obligated in tzitzit; only if he has a tallit with four corners and wears it is he then obligated in tzitzit.
