An obligation upon the person. Meaning, one is not obligated to purchase a tallis for himself, as stated in siman 24. Know that regarding tzitzis we rule both that it is an obligation upon the person and that it is not an obligation upon the person, and both rulings are leniencies. It is an obligation upon the person as a leniency, excluding an obligation upon the garment. So long as one is not wearing the tallis, even though it has four corners, it is exempt from tzitzis. It is not an obligation upon the person in the sense that one is not required to purchase a tallis in order to become obligated in tzitzis. Only if he owns a four-cornered tallis and wears it does he become obligated in tzitzis. See siman 19.
