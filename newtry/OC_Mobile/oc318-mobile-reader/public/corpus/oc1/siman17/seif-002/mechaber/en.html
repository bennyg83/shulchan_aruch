Women and slaves are exempt because it is a positive time-bound mitzvah. Rema: Nevertheless if they wish to wrap and bless, they may like other time-bound positive mitzvos (Tosafos, Rosh, Ran Rosh Hashanah ch. 2, Kiddushin ch. 1); but it looks arrogant so they should not wear tzitzis, since it is not a man’s obligation [Agur 27] — meaning he is not obligated to acquire a tallis to become obligated in tzitzis; below siman 19 he says when one has a four-cornered garment and wears it. A tumtum and androgynos are obligated from doubt and wrap without a blessing. [Tumtum: gender unknown; androgynos: has male and female traits.] Rema: According to women’s practice to bless on time-bound mitzvos, they too bless (his own words).
