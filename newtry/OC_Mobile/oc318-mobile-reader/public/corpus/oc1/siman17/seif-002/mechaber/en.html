Women and slaves are exempt because it is a positive time-bound mitzvah. Rema: Nevertheless if they wish to wrap and bless, they may like other time-bound positive mitzvos (Tosafos, Rosh, Ran Rosh Hashanah ch. 2, Kiddushin ch. 1); but it looks arrogant so they should not wear <i>tzitzis</i>, since it is not a man’s obligation [<i>Agur</i> 27] — meaning he is not obligated to acquire a <i>tallis</i> to become obligated in <i>tzitzis</i>; below siman 19 he says when one has a four-cornered garment and wears it. A <i>tumtum</i> and <i>androgynos</i> are obligated from doubt and wrap without a blessing. [<i>Tumtum</i>: gender unknown; <i>androgynos</i>: has male and female traits.] Rema: According to women’s practice to bless on time-bound mitzvos, they too bless (his own words). ] טומטום ואנדרוגינוס חייבין מספק ויתעטפו בלא ברכה: [פי' טומטום לא נודע אם הוא זכר או נקיבה ואנדרוגינוס יש לו זכרות ונקבות] [הגה ולפי מה שנהגו נשים לברך במצות עשה שהזמן גרמא גם הם יברכו] [דברי עצמו]:
