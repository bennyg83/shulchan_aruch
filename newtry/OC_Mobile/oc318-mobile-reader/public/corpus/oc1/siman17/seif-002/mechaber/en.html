Women and servants are exempt because it is a time-bound positive mitzvah. {Rama: Nevertheless, if they wish to wrap themselves in it and recite a blessing over it, they have permission to do so, as with other time-bound positive mitzvot. [Tosafos, Rosh, and Ran, chapter 2 of Rosh Hashana and chapter 1 of Kiddushin] However, it appears presumptuous; therefore they should not wear tzitzis, since it is not an obligation incumbent upon the person. [Agur, siman 27] Meaning: he is not obligated to purchase a tallis in order to become obligated in tzitzis. Below, in siman 19, he says that when one has a four-cornered garment and wears it.} A tumtum and an androgynos are obligated because of doubt and should wrap themselves without a blessing. [Meaning: a tumtum is one whose sex is unknown, and an androgynos has both male and female characteristics.] {Rama: And according to the custom that women recite blessings over time-bound positive mitzvot, they too should recite the blessing.} This gloss is Rama's own ruling.
