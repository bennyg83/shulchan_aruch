Section 1, "And the verse, etc." This follows Rabbi Shimon, for the anonymous Gemara there, 40b, follows his view, and likewise in Shabbat 25b. Since it did not mention the other reasons there and entered into the dispute, infer from this that it is the accepted law. So too in chapter 2 of Berakhot, Rabbi Yehoshua ben Korchah says, "Why did the section of Shema precede, etc.," and in the Gemara there, 14b: "In the West, etc.; how excellent, etc.; therefore we, etc." Also in Kiddushin 33b: "The Rabbis taught, etc.," and in Shabbat 24b: "That comes to include the garment of a blind person, etc." Likewise in Zevachim 18b the Gemara presses to establish all the Tannaim according to Rabbi Shimon, while the other opinion derives it from "that, etc." Tosafot there and there.
