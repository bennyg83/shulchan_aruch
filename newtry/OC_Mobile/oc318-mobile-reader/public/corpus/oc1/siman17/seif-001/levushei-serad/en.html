Magen Avraham, seif katan 3. A tallis. The wording of Shulchan Aruch, “to acquire tzitzis for him,” is understandable, meaning where he already owns a four-cornered tallis. The minor's law is then the same as that of an adult at the beginning of siman 24. Regarding the wording “to acquire a tallis for him,” Magen Avraham refers to the end of siman 24, because even for an adult it is not an absolute obligation, but only proper and correct; see there.
