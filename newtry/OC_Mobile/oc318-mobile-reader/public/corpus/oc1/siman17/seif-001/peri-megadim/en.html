A nighttime garment. See Taz. Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilchos Tzitzis, halachah 7, appears to imply that the inclusion of a blind person is not derived from the apparently superfluous word “with which,” but from logic: the garment can be seen by others. See Tosafos on Zevachim 18b, s.v. “And the other,” and Menachos 43b, s.v. “And Rabbi Shimon”; see there. It is somewhat difficult. According to Rambam, as explained below in siman 18, perhaps “with which you cover yourself” should include a daytime garment worn at night. One may answer that Rambam does not expound the word “with which,” and derives the law of a blind person from logic. According to Rosh too there is a difficulty: perhaps the verse includes a nighttime garment worn by day, and excludes only a nighttime garment worn at night.
