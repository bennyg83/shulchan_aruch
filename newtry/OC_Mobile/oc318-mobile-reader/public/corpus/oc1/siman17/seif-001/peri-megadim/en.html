Night garment. Taz — see Rambam, chapter 2 of tzitzis, note 7: it seems at first glance without the word “asher” for expansion, but from reason that it exists in visibility to others. And see Tosafot Zevachim 18b, s.v. ve’idach; Menachot 43b, s.v. veRash — see there. And one may question: say “asher she-tichaseh” to include a day garment at night, for Rambam, as below in siman 18.
