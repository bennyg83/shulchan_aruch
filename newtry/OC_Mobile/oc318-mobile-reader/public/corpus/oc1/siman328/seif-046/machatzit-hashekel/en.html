(s.k. 46) One may not, etc. According to Rashi and the Tur, for they explain that what the Mishnah taught, that one may not exercise, applies even if he does not intend to sweat but only for pleasure. Nevertheless it is forbidden to do it forcefully; only gently is permitted, because of weekday activity. They brought this above in siman 327, seif 2. Here the Shulchan Aruch brought the Rambam's explanation of this matter of exercising, because the Beit Yosef held that as a matter of halacha both explanations are true, and Rashi agrees with the Rambam.
