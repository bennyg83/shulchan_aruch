One may not make apiktoisin {the Aruch explains: "apik tefei zon," i.e., to expel the excess food}, that is, the inducement of vomiting — even on a weekday, because of the waste of food. And if one is in distress from an excess of food, on a weekday it is permitted even with a drug; and on Shabbos it is forbidden with a drug but permitted by hand.
