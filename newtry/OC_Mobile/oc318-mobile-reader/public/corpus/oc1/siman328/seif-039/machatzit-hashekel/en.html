(s.k. 39) And some say that if requires study, for on the contrary, etc. , in Kesubos daf 60 it teaches: R' Merinus says: one who chokes may suck milk from an animal on Shabbos; and we rule there halacha like him; and Rif challenged: behold they said in Yevamos daf 114: Abba Shaul says: they practice , meaning to suck from a pure animal on Yom Tov; and the Gemara challenged: in any case if there is danger , even on Shabbos permitted; and if no danger , why permitted on Yom Tov; and they answer: always when there is no danger; but since he nurses with his mouth it is mafrek kele'achar yad and there is only D'Rabbanan prohibition, and in a place of pain they did not decree on Yom Tov where there is negative commandment prohibition; but on Shabbos with stoning prohibition they did not permit; therefore Rif challenged: behold for one who chokes there is no danger and nevertheless permitted even on Shabbos; and one may answer Abba Shaul disagrees with R' Merinus and so it implies from "halacha like R' Merinus" , implying some disagree and we do not hold like Abba Shaul; therefore Rif permitted even on Shabbos , and this is the first opinion brought in Shulchan Aruch; and Tosafos wrote in name of Rabbenu Tam that they do not disagree , R' Merinus deals with one who chokes who has pain from illness , for this permitted even on Shabbos; but Abba Shaul deals with hunger pain , this was not permitted except on Yom Tov , and this is the second opinion Shulchan Aruch brought; and on this Magen Avraham wondered: from Shulchan Aruch's language it implies per the first opinion , namely Rif , even on account of hunger pain permitted even on Shabbos, for Rif did not distinguish between pains; and in truth this is not so , rather on the contrary for Rif hunger pain even on Yom Tov is forbidden; and from this reason he wrote in Milchamot he is not pleased with Rabbenu Tam's words; and if so on the contrary the second opinion is more lenient than Rif who is the first opinion:
