10) There: One who asks, etc. A halachic authority must have these laws fluent in his mouth so that he will not need to delay while consulting books. If a question arises and the authority must pause to consult books, while another learned and knowledgeable person present knows the law, the latter should not defer to the rabbi's honor but should tell the questioner the ruling. Mateh Ephraim, siman 618, paragraph 16.
