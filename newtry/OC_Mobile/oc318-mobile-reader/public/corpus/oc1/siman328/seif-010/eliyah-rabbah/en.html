[9] One physician says, etc. If the patient says, 'I need this particular medicine,' and a physician says it will harm him, we follow the physician. But if the physician merely says he does not need it, we follow the patient, as stated by the Radbaz. If the patient refuses to allow Shabbos to be desecrated for him, we compel him, because such conduct is foolish piety, as stated by the Radbaz, responsum 66.
[10] [Levush] And some say that this applies specifically, etc. This is the primary ruling. It applies specifically where the Jew says that he recognizes this particular illness. Nevertheless, he is not believed to contradict the expert physician even in order to be lenient. See siman 618.
