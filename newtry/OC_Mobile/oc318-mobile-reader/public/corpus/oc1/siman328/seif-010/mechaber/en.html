Every illness that doctors say is dangerous — even if it is on the flesh externally — they desecrate Shabbat for him; and if one doctor says he needs it and one doctor says he does not need — they desecrate; and there is one who says that an expert is not required, for all people are considered experts somewhat, and a doubt in matters of life-threatening danger is resolved leniently.And some say specifically Israelites; but an ordinary non-Jew who are not doctors — we do not establish them as experts [Issur VeHeter HaAroch].
