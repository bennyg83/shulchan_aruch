Seif 10 in the gloss "specifically Israelites." Since he is commanded regarding Shabbat and says to desecrate it — he certainly relies on his protest, Levush.

Magen Avraham s.k. 16 Rashi and likewise Tosafot: that it is like "this one lifts and this one lifts," that in melacha of Shabbat both are liable. It is wondrous to me — from where did Rashi and Tosafot derive this; for one could say specifically in melacha that both do together, such as they carried out together and the like, in which each is called a carrier; but here in squeezing and opening the eye, which is not kohal at all but only prepares so a non-Jew may kohal — one could say assisting has no substance, for he does not do part of kohal; and as Shach divides in Knesset HaGedolah Yoreh De'ah siman 181 regarding hakafah — and we gain to resolve thereby Tosafot's difficulty on Shabbat that R' Zeira and R' Elazar thought to forbid since without his action it would not be done, it is like this one lifts and this one lifts; and Ameimar holds that nevertheless here, since he does not do something from the body of the melacha but only assists in a general preparation, there is no substance; and I saw in Shitah Mekubetzet Beitzah 20 likewise deals with "without his action it would not be done" and distinguishes between this and this one lifts and this one lifts — squeezing and opening does not prove he does for that purpose, for the way in any case is to squeeze and open; see there. And in my opinion it would seem to distinguish as stated.
