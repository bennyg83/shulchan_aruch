(א) That the doctors, etc. — even a gentile since he is an expert doctor; and Radbaz vol. 4 siman 66 wrote: if the sick person says 'I need such-and-such remedy' and the doctor says it is not needed — we heed the sick person; unless the doctor says that remedy will harm him — then we heed the doctor; and see in Biur Halacha what we wrote on this:
(ב) They desecrate — for safek nefashot to be lenient:
(ג) That no expert is needed — and nevertheless specifically when he will say he recognizes that illness as stated siman 618; and also he is not believed to contradict the expert even to be lenient [Magen Avraham and Avnei Nezer]:
(ד) For every Jew, etc. — meaning even for a wound that is not of the hollow; and nevertheless if possible to do through a gentile it is better, as I explained in Biur Halacha:
(ה) Specifically Jews — for since he is commanded regarding Shabbat and says to desecrate it — he certainly relies on his protest:
(ו) But stam, etc. — and so is essential [Avnei Nezer]:
(ז) They do not desecrate, etc. — for we do not say to a person 'sin so your fellow may gain'; but if the coercion was in one of the three prohibitions — idolatry, adultery, murder — and he estimates the compelled person will surrender himself to death for this — possibly he must desecrate so he not come to this:
