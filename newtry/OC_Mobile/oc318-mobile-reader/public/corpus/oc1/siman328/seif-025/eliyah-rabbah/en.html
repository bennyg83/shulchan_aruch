[27] [Levush] But if it fell off, etc. This applies specifically if it fell off inadvertently. If he intentionally removed it, he may not put it back, as stated by Shibolei HaLeket.
[28] [Levush] For the illness, etc. According to this, the leniency applies specifically when his entire body has become ill or there is danger to a limb. See seif 17.
[29] Ash, etc. This requires study, because according to what was explained in seif 17 it should be permitted, since it is only a rabbinic prohibition. One must say that the case is where his entire body has not become ill, as stated by the Magen Avraham. There is no difficulty at all according to the Rama, who holds above that permission exists only through a shinui. According to the Levush, certainly there is no difficulty. This is easy to understand.
