(א) He should not return it. Decree lest he smear — meaning to anoint it:

(ב) On a vessel he may return it. Tur brought asplenium which he explained from the bandage — they return it under the bandage on the wound; and it is a Tosefta; and Beit Yosef challenged: behold he already ruled regarding a poultice that even if it fell on a vessel one may return it — all the more so regarding one that slipped; and if so why did the poskim need to write this? Therefore it was omitted here; and it seems to me it teaches that the Tosefta forbidding returning it under the bandage — lest one come to bandage if the bandage is ruined, and this is forbidden as tying on Shabbat, as they decreed lest he smear when it falls on the ground; and this is explained in what is written "they return it under the bandage" — that "under the bandage" is permitted, but as I explained:
