A dressing that fell from atop the wound onto the ground — he may not return it; fell onto a vessel — he may return it; and through a non-Jew permitted to place it even initially.And permitted to tell a non-Jew to make a dressing on a wound or bruise (Or Zarua) HaAroch); and forbidden to place burnt ashes on it for it heals except through a non-Jew (Mordechai chapter Shmonah Sheratzim).
