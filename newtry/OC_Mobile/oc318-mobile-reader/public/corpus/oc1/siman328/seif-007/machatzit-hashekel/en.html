(s.k. 7) That he does not need to be an expert; and in any case, etc. As written in siman 618, seif 6, if the doctor says that he does not know the sick person, etc., his words neither add nor detract; all the more so regarding one who is not an expert.
And in any case he is not believed to contradict, etc. Even if he says that he knows the sick person, nevertheless he is not believed to contradict the doctor. For in siman 618 the Tur brings one opinion that if there are two doctors and one is more expert than the other, we listen to the expert; and another opinion says that we listen to one expert even against many doctors who are not expert. Even those who disagree there would agree here, for there at least both sides are doctors and have studied medical wisdom.
