(א) For any wound — even if not in the hollow of the body, and even if not on the back of the hand and foot:

(ב) Extremely hot — this is called in foreign language typhus and the like:

(ג) With simur — from the language 'the hair of my flesh stood up'; and this is called shivering in foreign language — heat and cold come in one moment; unlike common fever that begins with cold and afterward heat — this is not in the category of a sick person who has danger [Acharonim]; and nevertheless through a gentile it is permitted to desecrate Shabbat for him:
