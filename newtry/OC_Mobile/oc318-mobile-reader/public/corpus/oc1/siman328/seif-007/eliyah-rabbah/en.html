[7] [Dress] Fever, etc. He wrote Isur VeHeter: permitted to let blood through gentiles on Shabbat for fever and all other internal illnesses — even through a Jew permitted based on an expert doctor; however a person who fell or was struck and blood congealed at the place of his fall or blow is not considered an internal sick person; and since there is no danger it is forbidden to let blood called kupfen even on Yom Tov even through gentiles — until here; on leech called niglin Magen Avraham at end of this siman is uncertain whether it is melacha on account of chaburah or shevut and permitted even without danger — one should be stringent. He wrote in Sefer Zikaronot: those who have pintone'l — forbidden to change the garment or paper that sometimes extracts blood through pressure — until here; and in Magen Avraham s.k. 33 he is uncertain if the opening closed whether permitted to put beans inside to open — also to change the garment his view appears to permit; and if he knows it extracts blood when he wipes it he should not wipe it, for it is psik reisha:
