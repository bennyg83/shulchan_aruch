However one concerned about his teeth who suffers, etc. — must read his entire body became ill as stated seif 17; and so in Darkei Moshe; and extracting a tooth is melacha d'oraisa for he is choveil for healing. Magen Avraham s.k. 3 — meaning therefore one may not permit except when his entire body became ill and through a gentile as stated seif 17 because extracting a tooth is melacha d'oraisa; however according to those who say melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah is not forbidden except d'rabbanan — permitted to extract through a gentile even if his entire body did not become ill.
