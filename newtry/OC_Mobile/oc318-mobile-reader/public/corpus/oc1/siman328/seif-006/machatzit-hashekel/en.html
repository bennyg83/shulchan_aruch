(s.k. 6) A doctor, etc. — and if the doctor says, etc. — it will harm him — they listen to the doctor — Radbaz; even though in siman 318 s.k. 3 he wrote in name of Radbaz: if the patient says "I need" — even if doctors say the food will harm him — they listen to the patient end of his words; that case is different, for the patient who says he needs it — not for healing but for food, for weakness overcame him and if he does not eat he will be endangered from weakness; even if the doctor says he will not be endangered from weakness if he does not eat — on the contrary, if he eats the food it will harm him — in this they listen to the patient, for if the doctor had not said the food would harm him but only that even if he does not eat he will not be endangered from weakness — we would feed him per the patient's words, for the heart knows the bitterness of the soul and we believe the patient more than the doctor; if so, even if the doctor says the food will harm him — nevertheless we feed him since we believe the patient's words that if he does not eat he will be endangered from weakness — what concern have we for future danger through eating per the doctor's words, and we do not concern ourselves for danger before us from weakness; unlike here where the patient says he needs such-and-such a remedy that will heal him and the doctor says it will harm him — for both the patient's and doctor's words are about the future — in this they listen to the doctor and we do not say "the heart knows the bitterness of the soul," for this depends on medical wisdom; and also their words are in back-and-forth and both are about the future:
