6) [Seif 2] It is a mitzvah to desecrate Shabbos for him. The verse states, 'which a person shall perform and live through them,' meaning he shall live through them and not die through them. Yoma 85b; Rambam, chapter 2, halachah 3. Even a possible danger to life overrides Shabbos. This is stated there in the Gemara and in the Mishnah on page 83, and Rambam rules accordingly there in halachah 1, as does Shulchan Aruch below in seif 10.
