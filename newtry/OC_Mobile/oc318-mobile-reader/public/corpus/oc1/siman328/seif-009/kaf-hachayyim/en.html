Terumas HaDeshen, siman 58, explains that he should have taught this law to the residents of his city before they needed it. Beis Yosef cites him. See Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 155, which requires the treatment to be established or prescribed by an expert; see there. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2; Chayei Adam, klal 68, paragraph 1. When the treatment is established, Shabbos is desecrated even if it is uncertain whether this patient will be cured by it. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. See Beis Yosef at the end of siman 134, who writes that ordinary fever is not dangerous. Magen Avraham there. Regarding seif 7, which states that Shabbos is desecrated for one who has a fever, Taz, paragraph 2, writes that this does not refer to the fever common among us; see there. Machatzis HaShekel.
