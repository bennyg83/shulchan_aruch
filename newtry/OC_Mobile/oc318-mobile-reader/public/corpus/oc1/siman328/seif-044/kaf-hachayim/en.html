AS) [Seif 10] Any illness where doctors say, etc. In Hagahot Mordekhai chapter Mefinin he expanded on these laws and wrote they should not say that if they do not do these things for him he will die; but all that the sick person knows it is Shabbat and says he needs because he cannot bear on account of the illness — they desecrate; and so in several places below siman 618.
