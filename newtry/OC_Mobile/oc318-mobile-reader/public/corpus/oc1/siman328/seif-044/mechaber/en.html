They bathe in Ein Gedi waters, in Hamat waters, in Tiberias waters, and in the good waters of the Great Sea even though they are salty, for thus is the way to bathe in them and it is not evident he does for healing; but not in bad waters of the Great Sea and in brine which are repulsive and the way is not to bathe in them except for healing; and specifically when he lingers in them; but if he does not linger — permitted for it appears only as rinsing.
