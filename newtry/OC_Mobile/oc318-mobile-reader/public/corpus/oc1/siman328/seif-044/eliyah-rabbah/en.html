[49] [Levush] Because it is medical treatment, etc. Although there is no direct concern here that one might grind medicinal ingredients, nevertheless, since perspiration is sometimes induced in a patient through medicines, we are concerned. This requires study, because the Rambam permits a rabbinic prohibition even when performed by the patient himself, as stated by the Taz. There is no difficulty, because perhaps the present case does not involve illness of the entire body.
