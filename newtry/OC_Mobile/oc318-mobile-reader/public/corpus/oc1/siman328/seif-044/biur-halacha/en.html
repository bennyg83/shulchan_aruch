Only for healing — see in Avnei Nezer who wrote regarding Tiberias hot springs that in places where there is no manner to bathe in them except for healing — even if he does not intend for healing it is forbidden if he stays — see there; and accordingly it appears certainly soaking in soaking-water is certainly forbidden if he stays even if he does not intend for healing; and it is difficult — for we learned "but he drinks palm wine for his thirst"; and as we explained everything above in seif 37 — behold if he does not intend for healing it is permitted to drink even things designated for healing; and the same in our matter; and so it appears in Tosefta chapter 13: "A person bathes in Tiberias water — but not in soaking-water or soaking-water; when is this — when he intends for healing; but if to ascend from impurity to purity — permitted," see there — behold we concluded in the sugya in the gemara that specifically when he stayed; and nevertheless it says only when he intends for healing; and this "but," etc., is even if he stayed at the time he descended to immerse; and perhaps Tosefta deals with a healthy person and therefore the braita requires he intend for healing — meaning to strengthen his constitution; and so it appears in Magen Avraham s.k. 143 and Avnei Nezer — it deals when he has discomfort and therefore forbidden even without intent; and he follows his view in s.k. 142 where he wrote that when he has any illness even if he eats for hunger it is forbidden; and see in seif 37 what we wrote there in Biur Halacha [and know that what is stated in Yerushalmi chapter 14 halacha 3 on this braita — R' Shmuel showed R' Berechiah "provided he does not stay" — is against the conclusion of our gemara in the Bavli 109b — see there]; and see in Rambam Hilchot Shabbat chapter 21 halacha 29 who wrote therefore one may not bathe in soaking-water, etc., "because all these are distress" and "call the Sabbath a delight" — accordingly for him in these even without intent for healing it is forbidden against this Tosefta — requires study in all this:
