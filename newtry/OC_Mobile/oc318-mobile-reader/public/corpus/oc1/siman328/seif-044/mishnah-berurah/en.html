(א) They bathe, etc. — meaning even though his intent is for healing — since healthy people bathe in it, permitted; but in a time when there is no manner to bathe in Tiberias hot springs except for healing — forbidden on Shabbat if he stays [Magen Avraham and other Acharonim]:
(ב) Salty — meaning and they somewhat help to heal; and know that even if he has pimples on his head — also permitted [consensus of poskim]:
(ג) And in soaking-water — where they soak flax there; and the same in Dead Sea water:
(ד) That they are repulsive — it states in Rambam: one may not bathe in water that purges [meaning water that cools the body and leads to diarrhea] nor in mud one immerses in, etc., because all these are distress and it is written 'call the Sabbath a delight' — end quote; and so Semag; and therefore forbidden to drink purgative drink, for there is no greater distress than this [Magen Avraham]; and this is even if he does not intend for healing but for some reason; for if he intended for healing — otherwise forbidden unless he reached the category of sick person without danger — then permitted, as below in s.k. 37:
(ה) And specifically when he stays — for the matter is evident he does for healing — for the water is murky and foul and he does not stand there for pleasure:
(ו) Like cooling — and even though they are murky we say he did not find clean water and always intends to cool; and even if he has pimples on his head — also permitted [gemara]:
