(א) He will dye it. And even though one who spoils is exempt — nevertheless forbidden (Rikanti siman 123); and it appears to me with a red garment it is obviously forbidden:

(ב) And forbidden to draw blood. Rashi explained end of Eruvin that it is chobel and is an av melacha; and so Maggid Mishneh chapter 1 — one who places something that draws blood and discharge is mafrek; and from Rashi's language it implies specifically when he presses with a compress it is an av melacha, for he does an act; but when he places on it something that draws blood it is not an av melacha, for he does nothing; and if so, all the more when he places a leech called nuglin — permitted for a patient, as written seif 17 that shvut is permitted; but in Zevachim daf 19 it implies when he intends to draw blood, even without pressing, it is melacha; and in Sanhedrin daf 78 where a snake licked him — R' Meir held him liable, for it is like inserting a knife in his belly and it is not gerama, for the destructive instrument is in his hand end of his words; and even Rabbanan who exempt — this is because the snake vomits by itself; but regarding a leech it is like doing with hands; and one who sucks blood in his mouth — forbidden, for it is chibur (Rashi chapter 19); and if so, forbidden to suck blood between the teeth; and it is possible it is an av melacha; and see siman 330 seif 1:
