(א) He will dye it — and even though he damages and is exempt — nevertheless forbidden l'chatchila; and with red garment obviously forbidden [Magen Avraham]. And in a place of pressure Acharonim agreed there is room to be lenient, for it is manner of soiling:

(ב) And forbidden to extract blood — this is a matter unto itself — forbidden to press with his hands on the wound to extract blood; or when he wraps something on the wound [even in a matter containing no dyeing concern] forbidden to tighten so blood exits, for he is choveil and it is a primary melacha; and some hold likewise if he places on the wound something that draws moisture and blood [such as zinc ointment and the like] — also melacha d'oraisa since he intended for this; and in any case certainly prohibition. Leeches [called piavkes or iglin] that suck blood — Magen Avraham leans there is liability; and therefore forbidden in any case for all to do through a Jew for a sick person without danger except through a gentile. One who suckles blood in his mouth from a chaburah is liable; and therefore forbidden to suck blood between teeth [Acharonim]:

(ג) Therefore there is, etc. — refers to beginning of seif:

(ד) And there are those who say he wraps, etc. — in Beit Yosef he challenges this because he heals; and therefore he wrote this only in name of 'there are those who say'; but in Avnei Nezer in name of Malbushei Yom Tov and so in Tosafot Shabbos he leans to be lenient:
