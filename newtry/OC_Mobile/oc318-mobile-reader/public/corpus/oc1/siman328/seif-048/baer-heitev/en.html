(א) That the blood will dye. And even though one who spoils is exempt — nevertheless forbidden. Rikanti; and it seems to me with a red garment it is obviously forbidden. Magen Avraham; and see siman 330:

(ב) Blood. And from this it implies for one who has fontanel — forbidden to change the garment or paper that draws discharge; and also sometimes it draws blood and dyes the garment, as Knesset HaGedolah in name of Sefer HaZikhronot; and Magen Avraham in s.k. 33 permitted to change to another garment, for if he does not change it will rot, and great is human dignity; and also he has pain; further wrote: if the hole closed somewhat — requires study whether permitted to put lentils inside to open it — here certainly his intent is that it remain open, or perhaps since it is already open it is permitted, as written siman 314 seif 3; and the same for a hole in a wound already opened — requires study; Knesset HaGedolah; and a poultice is permitted to place on the fontanel, as written siman 327; and from Shulchan Aruch's language it implies even if there is a hole and he comes to widen it — forbidden; and in Sefer Ra'avan it implies forbidden to place a poultice on it, and if he knows it draws blood when he wipes it — he should not wipe it, for it is psik reisha; and see Magen Avraham s.k. 33. To place a leech for a patient on Shabbat — called nuglin — it implies in Magen Avraham s.k. 53 that it is forbidden; and one who sucks blood in his mouth — forbidden, for it is chibur — Rashi; and if so, forbidden to suck blood between the teeth; and it is possible it is an av melacha.
