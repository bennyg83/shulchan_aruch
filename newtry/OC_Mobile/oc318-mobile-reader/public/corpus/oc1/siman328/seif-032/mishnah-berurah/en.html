(א) One concerned about his teeth — forbidden to tell a gentile to do for him anything for healing, even a matter that is only shevut, since it is only mere discomfort; but if he has great pain from the ache and thereby his entire body weakens — permitted to do through him even melacha d'oraisa, as above in seif 3 and see there, in the gloss:
(ב) He should not soak, etc. — for the matter is evident he does for healing; and even to wait and afterward swallow forbidden:
(ג) In them vinegar — and the same sharp wine — its law is like vinegar; but in a place of great pain possible to rely on Yeshuot Yaakov that permitted to hold it in his mouth and swallow afterward [Chayei Adam]. Also wrote the same forbidden to place on the tooth negelach or spirits and hot bran — all this evident he does for healing unless it hurts him greatly that his entire body suffers, as above in seif 17 [Chayei Adam]; and see there above that through a Jew permitted specifically through change; but Chayei Adam concludes there if impossible through change — permitted even without change:
(ד) He should not swish it — meaning he should hold the oil in his mouth; and even if he swallows at the end — the matter is evident he does for healing; and all the more if he spits it out; and some permit when he swallows at the end:
(ה) In oil — and even if he puts the oil into anigran [pickled-brine water] — forbidden:
(ו) He swallows the oil — and some forbid this; and Magen Avraham concludes everything depends on place and time — if healthy people do not swallow oil there in that place forbidden, as stated seif 37:
