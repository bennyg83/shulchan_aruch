U) And Mishnat Hasidim note 1 wrote: it is simple to me that man and woman are equal for pikuach nefesh; and even a one-day-old infant; and a gentile if his months are complete we desecrate Shabbat like circumcision for we follow the majority and the fetus is viable; and even a doubt overrides Shabbat in all matters. And a fetus of a gentile and maidservant like her; and for a gentile we do not desecrate. And there is room to question regarding a fetus whether we desecrate Shabbat for him without danger to the mother; and nevertheless one can say if she miscarries there is concern of danger for her and we desecrate Shabbat for her — see there; also for a Canaanite slave we desecrate since he is obligated in mitzvot like a woman.
