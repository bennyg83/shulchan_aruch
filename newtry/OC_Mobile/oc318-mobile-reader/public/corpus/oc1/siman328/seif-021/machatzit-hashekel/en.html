(s.k. 21) Cucumbers, etc. — and specifically a slice; and as written siman 252 — this is Rosh's view who challenged on daf 18 that the law mentioned here is the braita of daf 118: a person soaks cucumbers, etc. — if so, since permitted from erev Shabbat — permitted to leave on the eye even on Shabbat; and on daf 18 it teaches: one leaves a cucumber on the eye from erev Shabbat and is healed and it continues all Shabbat — implying forbidden on Shabbat to leave; and he distinguished: this of daf 18 deals with thick cucumber — lest he smear they were stringent that even if permitted from erev Shabbat forbidden to leave on Shabbat; but this of daf 118 deals with clear where there is no lest he smear; even though there is concern of grinding tochen spices — nevertheless they were not so stringent; and if permitted on erev Shabbat — permitted to leave even on Shabbat; and so he distinguished here in Shulchan Aruch and in siman 252; but per Tosafos' view that Magen Avraham brought — they do not distinguish between thick and clear; and that which they permitted on daf 118 to leave cucumbers that soaked from erev Shabbat on the eye even on Shabbat — deals with a healthy person who leaves it under the eye for pleasure:
