(s.k. 21) Cucumbers, etc. and specifically a slice; and as written siman 252 , this is Rosh's view who challenged on daf 18 that the law mentioned here is the braita of daf 118: a person soaks cucumbers, etc. , if so, since permitted from erev Shabbos , permitted to leave on the eye even on Shabbos; and on daf 18 it teaches: one leaves a cucumber on the eye from erev Shabbos and is healed and it continues all Shabbos , implying forbidden on Shabbos to leave; and he distinguished: this of daf 18 deals with thick cucumber , lest he smear they were stringent that even if permitted from erev Shabbos forbidden to leave on Shabbos; but this of daf 118 deals with clear where there is no lest he smear; even though there is concern of grinding tochen spices , nevertheless they were not so stringent; and if permitted on erev Shabbos , permitted to leave even on Shabbos; and so he distinguished here in Shulchan Aruch and in siman 252; but per Tosafos' view that Magen Avraham brought , they do not distinguish between thick and clear; and that which they permitted on daf 118 to leave cucumbers that soaked from erev Shabbos on the eye even on Shabbos , deals with a healthy person who leaves it under the eye for pleasure:
