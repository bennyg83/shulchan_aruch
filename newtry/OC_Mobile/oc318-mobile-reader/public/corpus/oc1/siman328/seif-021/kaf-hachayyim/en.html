21) Mishbetzos Zahav writes in paragraph 1: It is obvious to me that a man and woman are equal regarding danger to life. Shabbos is desecrated even for a one-day-old infant whose full term is uncertain, as with bris milah, because we follow the majority and presume the child viable. Any doubt overrides Shabbos in all such matters. A child born to a non-Jew or female slave follows the mother's status, and Shabbos is not desecrated for a non-Jew. It is uncertain whether Shabbos is desecrated for a fetus when the mother is not endangered. Nevertheless, one can say that a miscarriage would endanger her, so Shabbos is desecrated for her. See there. Shabbos is also desecrated for a Canaanite slave, since he is obligated in mitzvos like a woman. Pis'chei Olam, paragraph 2. See also below, paragraph 91, and siman 329, paragraph 1.
