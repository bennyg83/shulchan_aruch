K) [Seif 3] Any wound of the hollow — meaning from the teeth inward, etc. However specifically where it hurts him greatly that a wound befalls him; but mere discomfort in his teeth they do not desecrate. Bach. And nevertheless if his entire body became ill from it they permitted through a gentile; but a wound in the teeth is like a wound of the hollow and in stam they desecrate Shabbat for it through a Jew even in Torah prohibition.
