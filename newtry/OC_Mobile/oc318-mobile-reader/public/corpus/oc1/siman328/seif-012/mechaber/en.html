When desecrating Shabbat for illness with danger — they strive not to do so through a non-Jew, minors, and women, but through adult Israelites of understanding.And some say that if it is possible to do without deferral and without delay through a change — he does through a change; and if it is possible to do it through a non-Jew without any delay at all — they do through a non-Jew [Or Zarua and Shibolei Leket and Maharam in the name of] Raavan] and so is the practice; but in a place where there is concern the non-Jew will be lazy — one should not do through a non-Jew [Tosafot and Ran].]:
