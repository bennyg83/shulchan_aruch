[11] Adult Jews, etc. In Yoma 84 it states: not through non-Jews and not through Kutim, but through prominent Jews. The Beit Yosef asks that if so, why mention Kutim? Even ordinary Jews who are not prominent would be excluded. He therefore explains that 'prominent Jews' means adults, in contrast to minors. The Taz objects and explains that using non-Jews involves a prohibition, whereas among Jews themselves there is a special merit for the one who performs the rescue. The Raavan, siman 358, writes, 'rather, even through prominent Jews.' The Riaz in Shiltei Giborim is explicit like the Beit Yosef, and so too it appears from the Yerushalmi in Perek Yom HaKippurim and from Torat HaAdam.
[12] And so is the practice, etc. The Taz writes at length that this is not merely the practice of meticulous people. If one clearly sees danger, even if rescue is uncertain, the person who acts himself is praiseworthy. Even if non-Jews are present and capable of rescuing, the Jew will generally act with greater alacrity.
