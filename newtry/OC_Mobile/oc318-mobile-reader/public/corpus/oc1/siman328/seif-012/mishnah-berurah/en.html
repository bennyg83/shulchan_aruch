(א) Not to do, etc. — Rosh wrote the reason: sometimes they will forget them and also come to return after them and thereby come to danger; and this reason applies regarding minors and gentiles; and regarding women there is another reason — lest they say Shabbat was not given to override even pikuach nefesh — therefore they assign it to them and they will be lazy in the matter and thereby come to danger; or perhaps women will be lenient in the matter and come to desecrate Shabbat elsewhere [Rambam]:

(ב) Except through, etc. — and when there are sages at this gathering it is a mitzvah l'chatchila to do this desecration through them [R' Meir in commentary on mishnah chapter Mefinin, Rabbenu Yerucham, and Tashbetz vol. 1 siman 54]. And know that this entire seif deals where all stand at that gathering; but if there are no men there and there are women — certainly they should not wait, and they are alacritous and rewarded [Tashbetz and Rabbenu Yerucham]:

(ג) And without delay through change — for as much as we can do permissibly we do not leave the permitted and do in prohibition; and it appears the same if through change the matter is delayed a little — only if the sick person is not urgent they delay a little to do through change, for it is only rabbinic prohibition:

(ד) Through a gentile — and the same through minors:

(ה) And so is the custom — and Taz wrote it is not an established custom; for even though he can do through a gentile — nevertheless the Jew will act with more alacrity; therefore if there is even safek salvation and there is clear danger — all the more praiseworthy is the alacritous one:
