47) If the patient says, 'I need a particular treatment,' while the physician says that treatment will harm him, we listen to the physician. Radbaz there; Knesses HaGedolah there; Magen Avraham there; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there; Chayei Adam there. This applies specifically when the physician says that this treatment will harm him. If the patient says he needs to eat, however, we listen to the patient even when the physician says the food will harm him, as stated in siman 618, seif 1, and Magen Avraham there, paragraph 3.
