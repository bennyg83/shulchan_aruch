(145) That came out of its place, etc. — Magen Avraham disagrees on this and holds specifically a broken bone permitted to return as in the gemara; but a bone dislocated from its place forbidden to return — for even to rub much in cold water they forbade, as stated in seif 31; and all the more returning it — many Acharonim copied it; and nevertheless through a gentile it appears one should not be stringent; and in Sefer Shulchan Atzei Shatim he disagrees with Magen Avraham and wrote that whatever came completely out of its place called dislocation is considered danger to a limb like a break — if not returned immediately permitted to return even through a Jew, as in seif 17 — end quote; and that which above in seif 31 for one view it deals according to his view when it did not slip completely from its place; and it appears that even according to Magen Avraham if the doctor says it dislocated forcefully and can come to danger to a limb — permitted to pull and return it to place as he does on a weekday:
