(s.k. 43) Discomfort, etc. in Tur it is written, etc. , it implies, etc. , not like Beit Yosef who wrote not specifically for hunger and thirst but ordinary manner of speech; for this Magen Avraham wrote: Tur specifically stated and for precision: if he does for healing , forbidden; and if so there is no distinction here between healthy and patient; and it must be said here ordinary manner of speech , for ordinarily a patient with discomfort does for healing, and ordinarily a healthy person his intent is only hunger and thirst:
(s.k. 43) For one who bathes, etc. he is healthy; and even so forbidden if he soaks, since evident; and here too since it is not healthy people's food and evident he does for healing , forbidden even for a healthy person:
(s.k. 43) And furthermore for they said in the Gemara daf 109: even though he has boils it is stated there: they bathe in mei garar and Tiberias waters and mei mishra even though he has boils on his head , in what case? When he did not soak; but if he soaked , forbidden end of his words; and from "even though he has boils" it implies the main braita deals without boils and teaches a leniency: even if he has boils; and on this it said in what case when he did not soak, etc. , but if he soaked forbidden , implying in all cases even without boils , namely healthy; and nevertheless forbidden since evident; and soaking too must be something not healthy people's food, for they said he does for healing:
(s.k. 43) Forbidden to stand on diyonis ground, etc. from that it stated simply forbidden to stand , implies any person even healthy; and the reason: evident he does for healing, for Rashi wrote there it toils, heats and heals , the mud is salty; and perforce standing in mud; and likewise the language "forbidden to stand on diyonis ground," etc. , and not everyone's way to stand in mud, and evident he does for healing; and healing on Shabbos , who permitted? They said soft drink , Rav said one blesses shehakol since he does for healing; Rav Yosef challenged: but we taught "and both agree one may concern the drink on Shabbos" (and explained above siman 321 seif 14) , healing on Shabbos who permitted? Abaye said per your reasoning , behold we taught: all foods a person may eat for healing on Shabbos, etc.; if so a person intends for eating and healing comes automatically , here too a person, etc.; and why not establish with a healthy person? Therefore permitted , perforce even for a healthy person forbidden where evident; and soaking too must also be something not healthy people's food, for he said he does for healing; and so Rambam chapter 21: one who needs to crush pepper, etc. , but not in a mortar because it is tochen; therefore forbidden for a healthy person to heal on Shabbos , decree lest he grind spices; how so? He should not eat things that are not healthy people's food; and Maggid Mishneh wrote on this: mishnah "one does not eat," etc. , meaning all these things for a healthy person who does not fall to bed and is not ill end of his words; behold for something that is not healthy people's food where evident , even for a healthy person forbidden if he intends healing:
