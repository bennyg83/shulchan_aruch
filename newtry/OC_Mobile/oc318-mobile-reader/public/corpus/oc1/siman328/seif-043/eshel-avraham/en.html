Uvla. Rokeach siman 106 wrote: one whose foreskin of the throat fell — forbidden to raise on Shabbat; one whose palate and mouth are full of blisters — he should not go to a melsar tree to place in his mouth from what is attached, for he uses the tree; and Rabbeinu Yerucham page 49 wrote: permitted to choke — one whose throat fell, they hang him by his neck like one being choked and his neck stretches and he is healed; and requires study. And see Seder HaYom Shabbos; and on Shabbat daf 66 Rashi explained: one whose neck-bone illness dislocated and falls in his throat — they hang him by his head so his neck stretches, similar to choking. And in Shibbolei HaLeket wrote: some forbid raising the throat by pulling hair lest one uproot one of them and be liable for a sin-offering.
