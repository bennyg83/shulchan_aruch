(א) Gonoach — heart pain whose healing is to nurse hot milk from the animal:
(ב) For in a place of pain, etc. — meaning even though milking the animal is d'oraisa on account of mifrak which is a toldah of threshing, as we wrote at beginning of siman 321 — nevertheless here through nursing which is only indirect action it is only rabbinic prohibition and they permitted in this; and even though we rule that for every illness without danger one tells a gentile and he does — here is different, for his healing is to nurse himself [Rosh]:
(ג) And there is one who says if, etc. — this language 'there is one who says' is not precise, for it implies per the first reasoning permitted on Shabbat even on account of hunger pain; and in truth they did not mention this at all; and it is possible according to them even on Yom Tov forbidden in this — rather it is a separate law that some poskim distinguish between Shabbat and Yom Tov regarding hunger pain; and Bach and Avnei Nezer wrote the halacha is like those who say:
(ד) Forbidden to nurse — for they permitted only on account of pain of illness; but not in this, for it is in any case mifrak indirectly:
(ה) On Shabbat — but on Yom Tov permitted if he has no gentile to milk through him; and if he has food to milk into — forbidden to nurse in his mouth also on Yom Tov, but he should milk into the food, as stated siman 495:
