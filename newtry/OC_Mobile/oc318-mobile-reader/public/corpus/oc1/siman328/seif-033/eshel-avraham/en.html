Seif 33. And that which Abba Shaul said in chapter Cheresh: nursing from a pure animal on Yom Tov, and the gemara asks how is this — if there is danger, even on Shabbat as well, etc., and answers two [reasons]: there is distress and it is done in an unusual manner, and on Shabbat they decreed, etc. — and we learn that even in a place of distress on Shabbat it is forbidden — we do not hold like this, but like R' Merinus as Rav Yosef ruled; see responsum Ramban siman 206.
