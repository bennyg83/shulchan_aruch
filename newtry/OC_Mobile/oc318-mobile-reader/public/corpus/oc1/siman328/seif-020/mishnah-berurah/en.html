(א) Into the eye — for all matters of healing the Rabbis decreed on account of grinding spices:

(ב) If he opens, etc. — so it enters inside — then the matter is evident that he does for healing:

(ג) Permitted — for we say he does for bathing in general [Rashi]; and nowadays that there is no manner to bathe in wine — forbidden [Magen Avraham and other Acharonim]:

(ד) And bland saliva — any that tasted nothing from awakening — it is strong and the matter is evident he intends for healing; for if for bathing it is repulsive. And if he rinses his mouth in water and afterward passes it on his eyes — permitted even if mixed with saliva, for we say he does for bathing; and if he cannot open his eyes he can moisten them with bland saliva, for this is not healing [Acharonim]:
