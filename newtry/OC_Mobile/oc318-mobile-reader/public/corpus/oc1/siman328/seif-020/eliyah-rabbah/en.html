[20] And tasteless saliva, etc. This means saliva produced before one has tasted anything after awakening, as stated by Rashi, the Maggid Mishneh, and Malbushei Yom Tov. If one puts water into his mouth and rinses it around, even though tasteless saliva mixes with it, he may wash his eyes with it, as stated by the Bach. If he cannot open his eyes, he may moisten them with tasteless saliva, because this is not medical treatment but merely enables him to open his eyes.
