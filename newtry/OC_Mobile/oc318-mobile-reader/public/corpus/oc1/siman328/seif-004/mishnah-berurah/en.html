(א) It needs no assessment — meaning whether he is endangered for the moment or not:

(ב) All that is customary, etc. — from foods and remedies [Ramban]; and it implies even though there is no danger in withholding that thing — since the illness has danger and there is some need in the matter and they are accustomed to do for him on a weekday — they do on Shabbat too [Maggid Mishneh]; and see in Biur Halacha that we brought from several poskim who hold that even where desecration is needed, through a Jew they desecrate only in a matter where there is concern that if they do not do it for him his illness will worsen and he can be endangered; but whatever is clear that withholding that thing has no concern of danger — even though he nevertheless needs it and they are accustomed to do for him — they do not do it on Shabbat except through a gentile, as is the law for needs of a sick person who has no danger; and therefore certainly proper to be stringent in Torah prohibition; and see in Biur Halacha what we wrote at end of our words:

(ג) When they know — and all the more when the doctor or sick person says it is not needed — they do not desecrate:
