[24] And new ones, etc. The Tur implies that this applies also to a sponge, and so too it appears from the Gemara, which divides the matter into two clauses, dry absorbent material and a dry sponge, as stated by the Taz. This requires study, because to the contrary, the very fact that the Gemara separates them implies that their laws are not identical. Moreover, the Bach already writes that there is a scribal error in the Tur and concludes that with a sponge even an old one is permitted. Nachalat Tzvi rules this way, and so too it appears from Rimat.
