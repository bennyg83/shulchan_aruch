Wound of the hollow, etc. In responsum Chatam Sofer 14 siman 76 he was asked regarding one who was seized — is there danger; and he brought from Taz Yoreh De'ah siman 84 in name of Rashal from responsum R' Yehuda who wrote that it is like a wound of the hollow that has danger; and he wrote that although from Rashba's responsum general 42 one can prove it is not pikuach nefesh — nevertheless in pikuach nefesh we follow leniency and one may rely on Rashal in name of R' Yehuda to feed him prohibited matter if the remedy is verified — see there; and they brought Petach HaDevir there note 13; however see Knesset HaGedolah there in Hagahot Beit Yosef note 113 who wrote this responsum of R' Yehuda in name of several poskim; and it is written there that sometimes he is endangered to fall in fire or water — see there; and they brought in Sefer Zevach Tzedek siman 84 note 132 — see there; and therefore at first glance it appears likewise regarding desecrating Shabbat one may do verified healing for him; but according to what Shulchan Aruch wrote at end of this seif 'when they recognize in that illness that waiting and no desecration is needed is forbidden to desecrate' — likewise for illness of one seized it is forbidden to desecrate for it is known this illness waits; but the difference if it is the beginning of his onset and the doctors said if they do not do healing immediately his illness will worsen and become fixed in his body — perhaps there is room to be lenient:
