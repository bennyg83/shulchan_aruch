(s.k. 26) He should not return it, etc. But as for grinding ingredients, what concern is there, etc. This is the view of Rashi, and his proof is that the Mishnah specifically mentioned a bandage, where smearing applies; see there.
And see siman 303, etc. Regarding pepper in her mouth, where she is permitted to go out with it, nevertheless if it fell she may not return it. Although there the concern of smearing does not apply, see there what he wrote in the name of Tosafos to answer this.
