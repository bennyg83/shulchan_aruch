He reveals part of the bandage, etc. — from this language it appears he does not remove it entirely — for if he removes it he may not return it afterward; and therefore the language of the braita here comes even per the first tanna and not R' Yehuda alone; and this supports the view of those who are stringent that I brought in Mishna Berurah s.k. 82; however it is difficult — for Beit Yosef's view is to be lenient in this, and nevertheless Shulchan Aruch copied the language of the braita here; and perhaps according to him we deal here when he has no desire to place on him a bandage unless on the ground, and it would be forbidden afterward to return it — therefore he reveals part of the bandage and wipes and presses:
