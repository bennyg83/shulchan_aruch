(s.k. 19) And saliva, etc. and per Darkei Moshe what is written in name of Or Zarua, etc. , meaning he stipulated further conditions to be called thin saliva; but from Rashi's and Maggid Mishneh's language it implies this suffices with what he tasted nothing from awakening; therefore Magen Avraham wrote those conditions were not mentioned in the Gemara except regarding a stain , namely if she found a stain on her garment and is uncertain whether it is blood or dye , they pass over the stain seven spices and one of them is thin saliva; and if thereby the stain passes , this is a sign it is menstrual blood; and if it does not pass , likewise dye, as explained in Niddah; but regarding healing it suffices if he ate nothing from awakening:
