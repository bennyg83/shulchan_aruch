Beit Yosef in name of Avnei Nezer; and Rashal wrote that to extract a tooth on Shabbat through a gentile there is no proof to permit from pikuach nefesh — specifically to place a drug that is certainly healing they desecrate even in Torah melacha such as grinding spices; but extracting a tooth is also pikuach nefesh — end of his words; and they brought it in Bach.
