This implies that Shabbos is desecrated for all his needs even when withholding the particular item would not endanger him. Beis Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh; Magen Avraham, paragraph 4. In the name of Rashi, however, Beis Yosef writes that Shabbos may be desecrated for a dangerously ill person only for something whose omission could cause his death. Something whose omission would not endanger his life may not be performed through a Jew; see there. Magen Avraham, paragraph 17, cites this. See Biur Halachah, which discusses the matter at length and cites several authorities who agree with Rashi. It therefore writes that the proper course is to be stringent regarding a Torah prohibition; see there. See also below, paragraph 117.
