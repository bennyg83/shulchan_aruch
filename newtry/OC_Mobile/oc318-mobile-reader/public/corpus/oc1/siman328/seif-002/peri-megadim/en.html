Or Taz, Magen Avraham sign 1: and all that is mentioned in this seif, which is from the gemara — even if a doctor or the sick person says it is not needed we do not heed them, as Bach wrote regarding tzefidna Magen Avraham sign 2 that they speak falsely because the gemara said there is danger and we do not say times have changed; and a wound of the hollow that they said in stam we desecrate — stam there is pikuach nefesh; but when doctors and the sick person say it is not needed — no. And see Pesachim 25b: a dangerous sick person is permitted in prohibited enjoyments; and when there is no danger — permitted. And see chapter 5 Foundations of the Torah and Yoreh De'ah siman 155 in Shach sign 19 — ash of prohibited enjoyments is permitted for a sick person who has no danger, for those burned permitted their ash; requires study that chametz ash in its char is also permitted for a sick person who has no danger, for it is not a manner of enjoyment; and whatever is for the sake of the sick person — permitted, even without danger, as is explained. Also what he wrote about chametz on Pesach from six hours until night — one can say it is from rabbinic law only — see in Pri Megadim chapter 1 Orach Chayim in name of Ralbag.
