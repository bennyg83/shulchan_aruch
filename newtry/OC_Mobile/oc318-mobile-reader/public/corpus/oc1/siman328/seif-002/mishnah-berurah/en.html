(א) A mitzvah, etc. — meaning to do healing for him; and the reason is because it is written 'and live by them' and not die by them:

(ב) And the alacritous one, etc. — see below in seif 12 and in the gloss there in what is written there in Mishna Berurah; and see in Yoreh De'ah end of siman 155 that we require the remedy be known to people or based on an expert [Magen Avraham]:

(ג) And the one who asks, etc. — meaning that man who shows piety and fears to desecrate Shabbat for such a sick person except through a teacher — behold he is a shedder of blood; for while he goes to ask the sick person will weaken more and can be endangered; and behold Scripture says 'you shall not stand by,' etc.; and in Yerushalmi it states 'the one asked — behold he is repugnant' — meaning because the Torah scholar in his place should have ruled publicly so all the people would know and not need to ask him; and if the sick person himself fears they will transgress Shabbat for him — they compel him and speak to his heart that it is piety of foolishness [Radbaz]:
