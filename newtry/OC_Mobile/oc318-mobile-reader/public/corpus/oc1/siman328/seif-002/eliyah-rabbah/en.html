[2] [Dress] 'That he shall live,' etc. From Rashi's language Yoma 85a: 'that a man shall do them and live' — the commandments that he shall live by them with certainty, and not come to doubtful death — until here; and since he did not write 'and he shall live' referring to the doer but wrote 'and live' referring to another — meaning you are obligated in the commandment to act when another will live with certainty; and if another has doubtful death do not act and desecrate — so it appears to me; and Dress's words are strained (Malbushei Yom Tov):
