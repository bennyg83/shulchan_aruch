(א) To place, etc. — and all the more forbidden to place crystal even if prepared from yesterday; and in this forbidden even through change — decree on account of grinding spices unless for a sick person even without danger, as stated in seif 17 — then permitted through change; and he should be careful not to come to melacha d'oraisa; also he should not mix water and oil in the manner they do, as explained below in siman 331 seif 7 [see there in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halacha]; rather he should pour everything into the vessel; and better to do everything through a gentile if possible:
(ב) Wick — made from tallow or borax or from paper and other things; and reason for their prohibition — because since he handles there many times to insert and remove — likely he will singe hair there, like with a pebble above in siman 312 seif 7; and see in Biur Halacha:
(ג) Gently — and he should not thereby come to singe hairs. Chayei Adam wrote all this if the wick was made from erev Shabbat; but to make on Shabbat the aforementioned wick — and even if he makes it from paper that he wraps and hardens — there is Torah prohibition on account of striking with a hammer unless for a sick person who has danger; but what they do from a piece of bread is permitted since it is animal food — no concern of fixing a vessel.
