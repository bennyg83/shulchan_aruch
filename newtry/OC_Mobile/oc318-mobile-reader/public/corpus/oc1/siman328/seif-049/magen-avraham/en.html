To place a wick. It is certainly forbidden to prepare a plaster dressing, even if its materials were prepared before Shabbos, and even with an unusual method, unless the person is ill, as stated in seif 17. One must take care not to come to perform a melachah according to the Torah. Where possible, it should be done through a non-Jew, as stated in seif 12. See also siman 331, seif 6.
