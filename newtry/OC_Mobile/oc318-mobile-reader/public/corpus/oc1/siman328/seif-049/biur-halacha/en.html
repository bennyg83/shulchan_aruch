To place a wick, etc. — the essence of this law the Mechaber already wrote in siman 312 seif 8, only there he wrote to handle with a spit, etc., which is of wood; and here he wrote regarding a wick; and they have one reason on account of singeing hairs, as is evident in Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe [and it implies from this that even a wick is not included in healing that they would decree on it on account of grinding spices; and only regarding crystal it appears from Magen Avraham that it is included in healing — requires study why this is not also included in healing]. And one should examine why it is forbidden regarding a wick — for he does not intend to singe hairs; for if they said in the gemara regarding a pebble that it is forbidden because it is psik reisha for singeing hairs as Tosafos wrote [for otherwise it would be davar she'eino mitkaven] — let us say we are lenient regarding a wick too as with a pebble, for a pebble is hard unlike a wick which is soft; also the essence of this law of forbidding to handle a pebble — Rif and Rambam omitted it [and it appears their reasoning is they also hold like Rashi that Rava's reason is on account of singeing hairs and not like Rashi Chananel's explanation brought in Tosafos and Rosh — see there — and they hold Rava's law is per the conclusion there that Rava himself wanted to say davar she'eino mitkaven is forbidden, and in the conclusion there that it is clear like R' Nachman bar Oshaya this statement of Rava too is rejected]; and it appears they do not hold thus; and if so certainly we rule like most poskim that a pebble is forbidden as Shulchan Aruch concluded and as Tosafos wrote that it is at least psik reisha — we have no room to be stringent where it is not psik reisha like a wick, for even Tosafos would agree in this; and especially in Ramban's chiddushim and in Rosh's chiddushim they also wrote regarding a pebble itself that it is not psik reisha — and therefore it does not appear to them like Tosafos' explanation; and they explained like Rashi Chananel's explanation — all the more regarding a wick which is soft it appears it is not psik reisha for all; and by strain we can reconcile as I wrote in Mishna Berurah that because he inserts and removes many times it is possible they considered this psik reisha for singeing hairs — and still requires study:
