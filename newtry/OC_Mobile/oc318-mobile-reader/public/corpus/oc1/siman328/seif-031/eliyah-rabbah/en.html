[32] And Rashi explained, etc. The loose skin is that which has separated upward near the nail, wherever it may be around it, while remaining attached below on the finger beneath the nail. According to Rabbenu Tam it is the reverse: it is detached and separated from below upward, while remaining attached above at the side of the finger, as stated by the Perishah.
[33] And one must be concerned, etc. Tzeidah LaDerech writes that although Rabbenu Tam's explanation is correct, one must nevertheless also take Rashi's explanation into account.
[34] [Levush] Because he did not swallow it, etc. This means that even if he ultimately swallows it, nevertheless, since he did not swallow it immediately, it is prohibited. So it appears to me from Rashi's wording in Berakhot 36 and from Tosafot, which accords with the wording of the Levush. Malbushei Yom Tov objected to the Levush based on the Beit Yosef without justification. Yet the Beit Yosef itself requires study, because the Gemara and Shiltei Giborim at the end of Perek Shemoneh Sheratzim imply that the matter depends on whether he spits it out or swallows it, even according to Rashi.
