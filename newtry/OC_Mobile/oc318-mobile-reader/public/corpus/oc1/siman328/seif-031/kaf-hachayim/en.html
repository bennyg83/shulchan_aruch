AE) There. And likewise one who swallowed a leech. Explanation: a small worm found in water; when it falls on flesh it attaches and suckles blood; and all the while it suckles it fills from the moisture it suckles and swells until it is like a barrel; and when a person swallows it in water it suckles moisture in his intestines and grows in his intestines and his stomach becomes swollen. Tosafos Avodah Zarah 12b; and they brought Avnei Nezer note 6, R' Zalman there; and they said there, in the gemara its healing is to heat hot water for him to drink; and they brought Tur and Beit Yosef; and Beit Yosef wrote likewise if other remedies are needed it is permitted to do for him — see there; and they said there, in the gemara meanwhile (meaning while they heat water for him) give him vinegar lest he die quickly.
