31) There: Likewise, one who swallowed a leech. A leech is a small worm commonly found in water. When it falls upon flesh, it attaches and sucks blood. As it sucks, it fills with the moisture and swells until it resembles a small barrel. If a person swallows it with water, it sucks fluid in his intestines and grows there, causing his belly to swell. Tosafos, Avodah Zarah 12b; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 6; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. The Gemara there states that the treatment is to heat water for him to drink, and Tur and Beis Yosef cite this. Beis Yosef writes that if other treatment is required, it may likewise be performed; see there. The Gemara adds that in the meantime, while the water is being heated, he should swallow vinegar so that he does not die quickly. Rashi.
