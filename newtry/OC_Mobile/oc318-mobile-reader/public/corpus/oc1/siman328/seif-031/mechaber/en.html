A nail that separated and tzitzin which are like thin strips that separated from the skin of the finger around the nail — if most of them separated upward and distress him to remove them by hand — permitted, exempt but forbidden; if most did not separate — by hand exempt but forbidden, by instrument liable for a sin-offering; and Rashi explained upward toward his fingertips and Rabbeinu Tam explained toward the body, and one must be concerned for both explanations.
