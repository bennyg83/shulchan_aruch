[13] A trap, etc. If a child falls into a pit, one removes a section of the wall and lifts him out even though this creates a step. If a door becomes locked before a child, one breaks the door and removes him even though the pieces become wood fit for use, because perhaps the child will become terrified and die. We do not say that one should entertain him from outside with nuts or the like until the key can be brought. Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 9, writes this, unlike the Magen Avraham, who understood from the Hagahot Maimoniyot that there may be no basis for leniency in this case.
