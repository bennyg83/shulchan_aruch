(א) From the teeth and inward. Even if it is not tzefidanit — whenever there is a wound in it they desecrate Shabbat; so Rabbeinu Yehuda in name of Rosh; and so Ran chapter 14: "from the teeth and inward" was asked and not resolved, and we rule leniently; and what is written regarding a rebellious eye — where it hurts greatly they call him "concerned" — to learn from it that with ordinary discomfort it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew; and so Bach — all this is not like Or Sameach who was not precise:

(ב) And he suffers from it. It should read: his whole body became ill from it, as written seif 17 and so in Darkei Moshe; and extracting a tooth is d'oraisa melacha, for he wounds for healing:
