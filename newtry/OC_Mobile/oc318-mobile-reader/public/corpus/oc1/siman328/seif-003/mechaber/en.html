Every internal wound, that is from the teeth inward, and teeth themselves are included — they desecrate Shabbat for it; and specifically when one of the internal organs was damaged from the wound or a boil and the like; but mere pains are not called a wound.However one who is concerned about his tooth and is distressed by it to extract it — they tell a non-Jew to extract it (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim and Issur VeHeter HaAroch).
