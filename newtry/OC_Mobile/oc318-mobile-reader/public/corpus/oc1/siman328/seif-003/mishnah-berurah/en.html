(א) From the teeth inward, etc. — and all the more if the place of the seat of the teeth — meaning the molars — became ill; they are certainly included in a wound within the hollow of the body:
(ב) And the teeth themselves, etc. — meaning where it hurts him greatly and his entire body became ill from it even if he did not fall to bed [unlike mere concern in his teeth which is not included in this, as below in s.k. 32]; and all the more tzefidna illness that begins in the mouth and ends in the intestines — its sign: when he puts food in his mouth, blood flows from between the teeth; and they desecrate Shabbat for it; and there is a difference between them: in tzefidna even if the sick person and doctor say it is not needed to desecrate — we say they do not know, for it is accepted by Chazal that it is dangerous; and in other tooth pain that is considered like a wound of the hollow — in stam they desecrate; and when the doctor or sick person says it is not needed they do not desecrate, as below in seif 4 [Peri Megadim]:
(ג) And specifically, etc. — this refers to the first part regarding a wound within the hollow of the body:
(ד) And suffering, etc. — meaning it hurts him greatly and he suffers so much that his entire body became ill from it even if he does not fall to bed, as below in seif 17; and see in Biur Halacha; and even though at first it is explained that tooth illness is called of the hollow — nevertheless extracting a tooth through a Jew is not permitted, for extracting a tooth is not a known remedy [Avnei Nezer]; and on the contrary sometimes there is concern of danger thereby; and that which Shulchan Aruch permits earlier to desecrate Shabbat for them — this is to heal the illness through drugs so he will not need to extract them:
