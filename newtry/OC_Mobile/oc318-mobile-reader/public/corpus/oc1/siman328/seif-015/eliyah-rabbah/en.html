[15] Some permit even without, etc. Behold Taz expanded to wonder on Rosh and Tur who are uncertain in this; and on Rashba who ruled thus — behold in Beitzah 22 Amemar put kohl in his eye on Shabbat and they challenged 'but it supports explicitly' and answered that he opened and mesayei ein bo mamash — therefore through a Jew it is forbidden; and he concluded he does not know to resolve until one whose heart is complete in the matter will teach him — until here; and I wonder — for in truth Rosh chapter 8 of Sheratzim who brings this gemara as a second reasoning forbids without danger to limb; therefore it appears to me Rosh deals with illness of the entire body; but illness of limb without danger — even for limb he agrees forbidden through a Jew, only regarding telling gentiles it is like other sick people — examine. Further it appears to me in the introduction what Taz wondered greatly s.k. 11 — what Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rashba in responsa contradicts Rashba who wrote Maggid Mishneh ruled like Ramban who is of the view that without danger to limb one does through change — until here; and in my humble opinion Rashba in responsum deals with melacha done through change like Ramban; and also Rosh's uncertainty appears to me it deals with this; and if so everything is resolved with Heaven's help; also it appears Rosh too holds thus — for if not, say: behold he wrote chapter 8 of Sheratzim as Ramban wrote in Sefer Torat HaAdam and there I saw explicitly that permitted through change even without danger to limb; and so Rosh himself wrote chapter Chavit in his name — except he wrote there another answer: where impossible through gentiles permitted through a Jew; and Rosh's uncertainty — in which answer shall we go — examine.
