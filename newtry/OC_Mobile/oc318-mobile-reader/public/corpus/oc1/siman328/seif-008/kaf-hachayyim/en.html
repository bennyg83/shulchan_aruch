38) [Seif 8] One overcome by a rush of blood, etc. This applies when there is concern of danger. If there is no danger, he may cool himself in water, because it appears that he intends merely to cool off and not to receive treatment. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 8. See above, paragraph 35.
39) There: Even on the first day. This means the first day on which the rush of blood seized him. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there.
