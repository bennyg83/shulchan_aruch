42) There: And the beginning of inflammation, etc. This excludes the end of inflammation or applying eye salve merely to brighten one's sight, for which Shabbos is not desecrated. Gemara there. A treatment for eye illness uses egg white cooked with specified medicinal ingredients, whose liquid is squeezed into the eye. At the beginning of inflammation, when danger to both limb and life exists, even a Jew may perform it, including to relieve pain, and even when medicinal ingredients must be ground. Certainly this is permitted when the ingredients were ground before Shabbos. At the end of inflammation, if used as treatment, a Jew may squeeze the egg unusually, meaning into his hand or a utensil, and place it in the eye. The egg itself, however, may be placed upon the eye only through a non-Jew. If at the end of inflammation this treatment serves only to brighten the eye and not to heal it, it is forbidden even through a non-Jew. Admas Kodesh, Orach Chaim, siman 6; Yad Aharon, Mishnah Berurah, Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 4.
