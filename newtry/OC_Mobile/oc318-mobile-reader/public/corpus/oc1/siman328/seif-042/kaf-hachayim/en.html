And beginning of illness, etc. Unlike end of illness and opening the eyes (to put kohl to brighten the appearance of his eye) — no; gemara there; and for healing eye illness they make egg white boiled in known spices and squeeze its liquid into the eye if it is beginning of illness for there is danger to limb and nefesh; and even to quiet the pain permitted through a Jew even in grinding spices; and all the more where spices were ground from erev Shabbat; and at end of illness for healing permitted for a Jew to squeeze the egg through change — meaning into his hand or into a vessel and place it in the eye; but the egg itself to place on the eye not permitted except specifically through a gentile; and if at end of illness this remedy does not help for healing but only to open the eyes — not permitted even through a gentile.
