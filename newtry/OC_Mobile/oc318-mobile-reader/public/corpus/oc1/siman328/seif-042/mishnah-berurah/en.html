(א) With force — meaning they extend and fold their arms before and behind them and warm and sweat [Pirkei Chagigah]:

(ב) So that he will toil and sweat — and it is included in healing; and one should not challenge: since there is no relevance of grinding spices here we should permit as in the next seif — one can say since sometimes they bring sweat upon the sick person also through spices we are concerned he will do the other healing; unlike the next seif where there is no relevance of healing for those things through grinding spices at all — they did not decree. And know that according to what we ruled above in seif 17 that through a Jew they do not do rabbinic shevut for a sick person who has no danger except through change — here too even if he reached the category of sick person without danger they do not do this except through change; and all the more if he is only healthy with discomfort. And the rubbing they do for weary laborers to restore their strength and remove their fatigue — Shaarei Teshuvah is uncertain whether this thing resembles healing and forbidden or not; but according to what Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 143 that even a completely healthy person if he does any healing to strengthen his constitution — forbidden; also here one should not be lenient:
