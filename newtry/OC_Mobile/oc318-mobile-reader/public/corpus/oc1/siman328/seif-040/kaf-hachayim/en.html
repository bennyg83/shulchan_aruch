AO) [Seif 9] One concerned about his eyes or his one eye, etc. Meaning in both eyes or even in one eye; and even though for danger to limb they do not desecrate as stated seif 17 — the eye is different because the moisture of the eye (light of the eye — membranes and membranes held in the sockets of the heart) depends on the heart's cavity.
