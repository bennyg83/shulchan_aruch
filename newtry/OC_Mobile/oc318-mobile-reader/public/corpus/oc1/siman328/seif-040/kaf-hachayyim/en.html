40) [Seif 9] One who has pain in his eyes or eye, etc. This means in both eyes or even one eye. Although Shabbos is not desecrated for danger to a limb, as stated in seif 17, the eye differs because the vessels of the eye, meaning the light of the eye, are intertwined with and connected to the membranes of the heart. Gemara, Avodah Zarah 28b.
