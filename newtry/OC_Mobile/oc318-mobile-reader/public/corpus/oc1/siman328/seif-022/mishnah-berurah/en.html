(א) And they anoint it — for in their time healthy people were accustomed to anoint with oil and it is not evident he does for healing; and see what Rama wrote in siman 327 seif 1 in the gloss regarding our countries; and it is reasonable that for a healed wound even in our countries permitted with oil:
(ב) But not with milk — and the same if the oil was solid, for it is comparable to milk [Mordekhai in chapter Bameh Tominin]:
(ג) For there is only pain — there is no pain but mere pleasure: so must read. Meaning and even though in an animal forbidden on account of mere pleasure, as below in siman 332 seif 2 — nevertheless for a person permitted:
(ד) But one does not place, etc. — for thereby the matter is evident that it is for healing:
(ה) Mixed together — and hot water alone — it appears from Magen Avraham permitted like oil alone:
(ו) And not on cotton — and in this even hot water alone that is not discernible as for healing — also forbidden on account of sechitah; but in oil alone they did not decree, for there is no manner of laundering in oil, as above in siman 319:
(ז) To place on it — and the same if the cotton was placed beforehand on the wound — also forbidden to place on it hot water and oil or hot water alone:
(ח) But he places, etc. — for the matter is not evident as for healing [Rashi]; and accordingly even in our countries where there is no manner to anoint with oil — also permitted in this:
