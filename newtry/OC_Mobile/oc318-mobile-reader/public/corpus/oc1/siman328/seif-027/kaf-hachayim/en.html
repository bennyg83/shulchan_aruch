AA) Dangerous sick person who suddenly became endangered and many say it is worms; and to make for him a segulah called oys gissin — lead — there is chatat liability; and the man who pours the lead says he healed and slept and dreamed — behold a person is believed to say he is an expert; and it is possible even if several times he did not heal — nevertheless since several times he did heal and they are accustomed to make such a segulah for this sick person on a weekday, etc. — one should not be stringent at all.
