[30] [Levush] For they intend, etc. It is permitted to remove a thorn with a needle, provided that one takes care not to draw blood, because that would create a wound, as stated at the beginning of chapter 10 of Sanhedrin by the Magen Avraham. The Gemara there states that even if one causes damage, he is exempt, and according to the opinion that one is liable, it is a melachah not needed for its own purpose and he is exempt. The Magen Avraham writes that it appears to him that one may not scratch a boil in a way that causes blood to emerge, and that this is unlike pus, which is considered already collected. In the Semag, however, I saw that one may drain pus from an abscess even though there is blood in it, because the blood is considered already collected. Sefer HaTerumot likewise permits this regarding a burn that contains blood.
