In order to widen the mouth of the wound — at first glance for this he is liable, namely with an implement [and nevertheless there is no shearing concern for peeling skin, for it is like a nail that mostly separated — Peri Megadim; and in Tosafot Shabbos some do not hold thus; and from Rashi that Peri Megadim brought it is not necessarily so — perhaps it deals where nothing was lacking from the skin only the skin peeled upward]; but if to extract pus it is permitted; but from Magen Avraham and Shulchan Aruch s.k. 97 and here in Taz sign 23 it appears that for mere pain they do not permit shevut in the normal manner — and therefore what we permit when extracting pus is only by hand and not with an implement [Peri Megadim]:
