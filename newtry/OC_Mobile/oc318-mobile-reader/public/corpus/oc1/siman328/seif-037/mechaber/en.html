All foods and drinks that are food of healthy people — permitted to eat and drink them for healing even though they are hard for some healthy people, and it is evident he does it for healing — even so permitted; and everything that is not food and drink of healthy people — forbidden to eat and drink for healing; and specifically one who has a mere pain and strengthens and walks like healthy; but if he has no pain at all — permitted.(And likewise if he fell to bed — permitted [Beit Yosef].]:
