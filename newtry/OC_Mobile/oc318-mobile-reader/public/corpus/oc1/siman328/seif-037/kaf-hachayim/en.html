And it implies all that is mentioned in this seif from the gemara — even if a doctor or sick person says it is not needed we do not heed them that they speak falsely since the gemara said there is danger and we do not say times have changed; and a wound of the hollow that they said in stam they desecrate — stam there is pikuach nefesh; but when doctors and sick person said it is not needed — no.
