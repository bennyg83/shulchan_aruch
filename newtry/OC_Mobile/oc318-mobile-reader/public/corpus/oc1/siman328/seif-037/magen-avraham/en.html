In Tur it is written "and he drinks it for his hunger and thirst — permitted"; it implies if he does for healing — forbidden even though he is healthy; and so in Semag, for one who bathes in the sea is healthy; and so in Yerushalmi daf 14 side 3 regarding mei mishra; and furthermore for they said in the Gemara: even though he has boils on his head, etc. see there; and so in Pesachim chapter 2 of Shabbos they said forbidden to stand on diyonis ground — it toils and heals; and Bava Metzia in Berachos daf 38 challenged: healing on Shabbat — who permitted?
