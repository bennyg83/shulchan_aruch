(א) Healthy people. Some delete "healthy people" and read "things" — meaning: even though it is hard on the teeth and evident he does for healing — regarding intestines it is permitted. Magen Avraham; and from this one may permit sniffing titonia herb in the nose on Shabbat even though he sniffs it for the discharge, since most of the world sniffs it on a weekday. Leket Yosher part 1 siman 101; and see s.k. 32 what is written there in name of Taz:

(ב) For one bedridden — permitted. To eat and drink; and even another Israelite is permitted to do for him a matter that is shvut, as written seif 17. Magen Avraham; and Taz and Bach forbade in every matter for a patient without danger; see there.
