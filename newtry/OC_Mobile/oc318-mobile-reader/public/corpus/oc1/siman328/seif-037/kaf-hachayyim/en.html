37) There: Or with chills. It appears that regarding everything listed in this seif from the Gemara, we do not listen even if a physician or patient says treatment is unnecessary. Their words are treated as false, since the Gemara states that these conditions are dangerous, and we do not say that circumstances have changed. Regarding an internal wound, for which Shabbos is ordinarily desecrated because danger is presumed, if the physicians and patient say treatment is unnecessary, Shabbos is not desecrated. A dangerously ill person may use items from which benefit is otherwise prohibited. For a non-dangerous illness, using them in an unusual manner of benefit is permitted. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 2. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 155, seif 3.
