(א) For some healthy people — must read 'things'; meaning even though this thing harms somewhat other limbs in the body — such as spleen good for teeth and hard for intestines and the like — and therefore the matter is evident he does so for healing for teeth; for otherwise he would not eat since it harms intestines — nevertheless permitted, for nevertheless he eats:
(ב) And all that is not, etc. healthy people — and therefore forbidden to drink purgative drinks such as wormwood and the like [Rambam] unless he suffers and his entire body became ill from it or he fell to bed — even if illness without danger permitted, as below in the gloss:
(ג) But if, etc. — and where he has any discomfort — even if he eats and drinks for hunger and thirst forbidden [Avnei Nezer and so in Tur]; and the reason — since he is sick and this is his healing they will say he does this for healing; and see in Biur Halacha:
(ד) He has no discomfort at all — meaning he eats and drinks for his hunger and thirst [Magen Avraham in name of Tur]; but if he does for healing — meaning to strengthen his constitution — Magen Avraham wrote even for a completely healthy person forbidden:
(ה) If he fell to bed permitted — meaning to eat and drink things not customary for healthy people to eat; and from Rama's conclusion it appears he need not even have a gentile hand it to him but permitted to take himself; and even though we ruled above seif 17 per the third view that shevut containing action is forbidden through a Jew and permitted only through change — must say here is different, for there it deals with rabbinic melachot or things resembling melacha; but here there is no trace of melacha, for to a healthy person permitted; and only for one who has discomfort they decreed on account of grinding spices — for a complete sick person they did not decree [Radbaz and so in Biur HaGra in name of Toldot Adam for Ramban; and so leans in Sefer Beit Meir — see there where he expanded]:
