(39) They slaughter for him — many reasons were stated on this: some say because Shabbat was permitted regarding pikuach nefesh and therefore they slaughter for him as they slaughter on Yom Tov; and even rabbinic prohibition they do not feed him where he can slaughter — from this reason; but many Rishonim wrote Shabbat is only overridden; rather the reason is lest he have repugnance for neveilah and not eat and be endangered; and in Beit Yosef he brought another reason in name of Rosh that with neveilah one transgresses on every k'zayit from it and it is more severe than Shabbat negative commandment; and another reason see there; and accordingly certainly better to transgress and give him rabbinic prohibition than desecrate Shabbat with stoning prohibition. And behold if the sick person says there is no repugnance for neveilah and neveilah is ready before him — whether permitted to slaughter for him see in Acharonim; and in any case for a minor it certainly appears better to feed him neveilah meat and a person should not slaughter for him on Shabbat. Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote: if need to boil wine for a sick person — a Jew fills and a gentile heats; and better the wine be poured as a libation than Shabbat be desecrated; and the reason see in Darkei Moshe who wrote: since prohibition of stam wine is only d'rabbanan and its prohibition is light and the sick person has no repugnance for it — it is not comparable to neveilah; therefore better the gentile heat if ready before him so he not delay thereby; and as above in seif 12 in the gloss; and further we rule in Yoreh De'ah siman 125 seif 10 that a gentile who carries an open vessel and a Jew guards it so it not be poured as libation — permitted; therefore possible to heat through a gentile without libation; and nevertheless one who is lenient for a sick person who has danger to heat himself — one should not protest, for he has whom to rely on; see Taz in s.k. 4:
