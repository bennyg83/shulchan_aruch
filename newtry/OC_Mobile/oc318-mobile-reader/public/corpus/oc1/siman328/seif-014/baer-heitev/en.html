For in neveilah he commits a transgression with each olive-sized portion — Ran; see there; and one may challenge Ran, for they said in Yoma: one seized by bulmus — they feed him the lighter prohibition first, tevel and neveilah — they feed him neveilah, for they disagree when impossible: one holds tevel is more severe, and one holds terumah is more severe than tevel; but terumah goes to the priest; and one holds terumah is more severe but tevel can be rectified, etc. end of his words; and per Ran's view it is difficult why they said they feed him tevel — behold each olive-sized portion he is liable; therefore better to separate terumah from it and feed him ordinary food by itself and terumah by itself, for he reduces prohibition — better still; and also for the one who says they feed terumah — why was the reason needed that tevel is more severe and terumah goes to the priest? It should suffice that even if tevel and terumah are equal, better to feed terumah because he reduces prohibition compared to feeding tevel where he increases prohibition on each olive-sized portion — requires study. And now recently came Parashat Derakhim and I saw he challenged thus; see there — sharp analysis and pleasant homily on this; and see what Yad Aharon answered; and Gemara wrote: if they need to heat wine for him — an Israelite fills and a non-Jew heats, and better the wine be poured as libation than Shabbat be desecrated end of his words; and Darkei Moshe wrote since its prohibition is from their words — permitted; and also its prohibition is very light end of his words; and also in his responsum Rama permits even for a patient without danger to drink their ordinary wine; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 125 seif 10 that it is possible without libation; and see siman 318 seif 3.
