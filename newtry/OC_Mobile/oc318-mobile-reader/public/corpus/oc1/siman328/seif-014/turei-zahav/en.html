They slaughter for him, etc. For in neveilah he commits a transgression with each olive-sized portion; and furthermore lest he be disgusted by eating neveilah; and in any case Beit Yosef brought this in name of Hagahot Acharonot: Mordekhai wrote Ravyah — if the patient needed to heat wine, an Israelite fills and a non-Jew heats, and better the wine be poured as libation than Shabbat be desecrated end of his words; and Beit Yosef sided whether it deals when there is danger or not; and it seems to me it deals when there is danger, and Ravyah holds like Ra'avad that Beit Yosef brings — that regarding neveilah too the law is the same, that better he transgress the prohibition of neveilah than others transgress the prohibition of stoning; but Darkei Moshe does not hold so, as Rosh wrote in his name — rather better to slaughter for him; here too better an Israelite heat, for if poured as libation then he commits prohibition with each drop he drinks; and so my teacher z"l:
