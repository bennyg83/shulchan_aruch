For Shabbat was permitted regarding pikuach nefesh; and it implies in Rosh in Yoma that even a d'rabbanan prohibition they do not feed him; but Ran wrote the reason that in neveilah he transgresses with each olive-sized portion, and in less than an olive-sized portion there is also d'oraisa prohibition even though there are no lashes, and it becomes many prohibitions and is more severe than one Shabbat prohibition; and see in Beit Yosef more reasons; and Gemara wrote: if they need to heat wine for him — an Israelite fills and a non-Jew heats, and better the wine be poured as libation than Shabbat be desecrated end of his words; and Darkei Moshe wrote since its prohibition is from their words — permitted; and also its prohibition is very light and the patient is not disgusted by it end of his words [and also in responsum Rama permits even for a patient without danger to drink their ordinary wine]; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 125 seif 10 that it is possible without libation; and above siman 318 seif 13:
