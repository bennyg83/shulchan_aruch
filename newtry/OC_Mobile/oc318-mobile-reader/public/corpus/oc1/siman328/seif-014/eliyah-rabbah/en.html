[14] [Dress] Lest the sick person, etc. And for this reason Bach ruled that if hot wine is needed — a Jew should heat and not through gentiles; and Magen Avraham wrote in name of Darkei Moshe since its prohibition is very light the sick person has no repugnance; and also in responsum Rama permits even for a sick person who has no danger to drink stam wine; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 123 and end of siman 155 that it is possible without libation. Radbaz siman 130 wrote: if the sick person is alert, such as when there are no others and he knows himself he has no repugnance for neveilah — better he eat neveilah or tell a gentile to make it neveilah and not slaughter with his hand, for here it is a negative commandment and here stoning prohibition:
