Permitted — Or Taz: Yoreh De'ah 123 permits the Mechaber for Motza'ei Shabbat for a healthy person — there is a recognizable sign through a gentile; here Taz there forbids; and in my humble opinion specifically on Shabbat it was permitted in Shulchan Aruch; but on a weekday not like siman 154 in Yoreh De'ah that in eating it is forbidden; and so in Beit Yosef here that on Shabbat they did not want to be stringent in Shulchan Aruch since melacha of Shabbat was permitted — see there; and see Levush likewise on a weekday forbidden in Shulchan Aruch for a sick person who has no danger; and on Shabbat they permitted lest it be more severe than melacha of Shabbat; and in Yoreh De'ah 113 it is explained that even for a healthy person it was permitted on Motza'ei Shabbat because there is a recognizable sign — all the more for a sick person on Shabbat:
