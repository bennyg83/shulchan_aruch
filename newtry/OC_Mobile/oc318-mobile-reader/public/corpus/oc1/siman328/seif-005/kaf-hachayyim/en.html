28) [Seif 5] Danger to life. The correct reading is 'possible danger to life.' This is Beis Yosef's wording in the name of Ramban.
29) There, gloss: See below, siman 618. The law there permits desecration even if the patient says the illness might worsen and endanger him. This is contrary to Rabbeinu Yerucham, who writes that if there is no present danger it is forbidden even though danger might develop, and contrary to Olas Shabbos, who cites Rabbeinu Yerucham. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 7, in the name of Magen Avraham. Machatzis HaShekel, paragraph 5, writes the same. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 5, likewise writes that we maintain that Shabbos is desecrated even when there is doubt whether failure to provide this treatment could cause danger or worsen the illness; see there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 5, rules the same, as do the later authorities.
