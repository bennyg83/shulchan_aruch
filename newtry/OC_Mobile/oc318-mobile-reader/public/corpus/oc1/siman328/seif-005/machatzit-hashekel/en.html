(s.k. 5) And see siman 618. This is unlike the Olas Shabbos, who brought the words of Rabbeinu Yerucham, who wrote as follows: There is one who wrote that any matter that has no danger now, although it could come to danger, we desecrate only a D'Rabbanan shevus for it. But in siman 618 it is clear that we do not rule like him, for he wrote there in seif 1 as follows: A sick person, etc., if there is a doctor there, etc., who says that if they do not feed him it is possible that the illness will worsen and he will become endangered, and it goes without saying if he says perhaps he will die, we feed him based on his word.
