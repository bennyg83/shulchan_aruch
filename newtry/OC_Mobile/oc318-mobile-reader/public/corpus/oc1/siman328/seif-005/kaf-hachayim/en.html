E) There. A decree on account of grinding spices. One who has swelling on one of his facial features — this happens from cold; and his remedy is they dip a cloth in beer and place it on the swelling and it will warm and heal. If permitted to do so on Shabbat in a manner that he not come to sechitah — meaning they should be careful not to soak the cloth in beer but only rub the swelling with beer well by hand and afterward place a dry cloth on it to preserve its heat. Shall we say permitted since there is no drug and the decree of grinding spices does not apply; or perhaps all healing was forbidden lest he do another remedy where grinding applies.
