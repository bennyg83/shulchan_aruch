(s.k. 35) He is liable, etc. Because of shearing, etc. Although Tosafos there wrote that this Mishnah must follow Rabbi Yehudah, who holds that one is liable for a melacha that is not needed for its own purpose, and from the words of the Mechaber at the end of siman 354 it appears that his primary view is like the poskim who rule like Rabbi Shimon, that one is exempt for a melacha that is not needed for its own purpose; because of this the Shach in Nekudos HaKesef, siman 198, wondered about the Tur and Shulchan Aruch and was pressed in his answer; see there. Therefore the Magen Avraham wrote regarding what he wrote at the end of siman 303, meaning in the name of the Rivash, that with shearing, even if he does not need the wool, and similarly with taking the nails, although he does not need the nails, he is liable. For we derive the melachos of Shabbos from the Mishkan, and in the Mishkan they would shear the hides of the techashim for the sake of the hides and did not need the wool. So too he wrote to answer in the responsum of Chacham Tzvi.
