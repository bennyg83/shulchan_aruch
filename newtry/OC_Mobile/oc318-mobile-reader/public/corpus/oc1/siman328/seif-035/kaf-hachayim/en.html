And for one who has fever, etc. Orach Chayim general 59 siman 26 wrote: permitted to let blood through a gentile on Shabbat for fever and all other internal illnesses — even through a Jew permitted based on an expert doctor; however a person who fell or was struck and blood congealed at the place of his fall or blow is not considered an internal sick person; and since there is no danger it is forbidden to let blood called kupfen even on Yom Tov even through gentiles — end. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef note 11, Avnei Nezer note 7; and one who became ill with fever and they made for him an amulet of herbs to hang on him for several days and when the time completed they commanded to throw it behind him into the river and the time completed on Shabbat — he may throw it since the river is a karmelit.
