35) There: And one who has a fever, etc. Issur VeHeter, klal 59, siman 26, writes that bloodletting through a non-Jew on Shabbos is permitted for fever. For all other internal illnesses, it is permitted even through a Jew upon the instruction of an expert physician. If a person falls or is struck and blood collects at the place of the fall or blow, however, he is not considered internally ill. Since no danger exists, cupping is forbidden even on Yom Tov and even through a non-Jew. These are his words. Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, paragraph 11; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 7. If a person with fever was given an herbal amulet to wear for several days and was instructed to throw it behind him into a river when that time ended, and the time ends on Shabbos, he may throw it because the river is a karmelis. Shevus Yaakov, volume 2, siman 50.
