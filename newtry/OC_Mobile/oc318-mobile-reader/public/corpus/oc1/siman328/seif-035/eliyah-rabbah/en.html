[35] He swallows much oil. So Rambam wrote and missing from Shulchan Aruch (Malbushei Yom Tov); behold Bach challenged Shulchan Aruch that specifically through anigran which is pickled-brine water is permitted and not in oil alone — and Rambam is alone — until here; and I wonder — for Beit Yosef himself in name of Tosafos explained Rashi's explanation that in oil alone permitted through immediate swallowing; and through anigran even with waiting — also possible to explain thus Rif's words and so Hagahot Maimoniyot ruled in name of Rashba; however in Magen Avraham he wrote: and it appears to me everything depends on place and time — if healthy people do not swallow there it is forbidden as written seif 37 — until here; and all this in mere discomfort; but if he has great pain that from it he weakens in the rest of his body — permitted to do all remedies; and it appears to me from Shulchan Aruch's and Rambam's conclusion that even through anigran permission is only when he swallows immediately; and he holds in this not like Rashi's explanation; and so it appears in Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham and Shiltei HaGibborim and so it appears in Tosafos and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Rabbeinu Yonah — unlike Olat Shabbat:
