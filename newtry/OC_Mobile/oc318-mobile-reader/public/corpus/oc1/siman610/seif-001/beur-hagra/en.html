Se'if 1. And if there are, etc. As written there: a tanna taught, etc.
And if it falls, etc. In the Yerushalmi, chapter 4 of Pesachim, halachah 4, it rules the law like Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar.
And one recites a blessing, etc. As written in chapter 7 of Berachos, and this follows his view, for he ruled that one recites a blessing also on Yom Kippur. See chapter 2 of Shabbos, 24.
