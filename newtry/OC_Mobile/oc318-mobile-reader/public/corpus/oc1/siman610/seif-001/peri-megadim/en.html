Place - See Taz and Magen Avraham. Even if his wife is a niddah, for this reason one should not kindle, so that he not see her and come to thoughts, etc. If he kindles at the table and in the room where he sleeps, not with a niddah, one can say it is permitted, as I wrote. See Magen Avraham. The custom is to recite the blessing, "who commanded us to kindle the candle of Yom Kippur." Regarding the word "of," see Siddur Sha'arei Teshuvah about this. Whether she recites Shehecheyanu, see siman 263.
