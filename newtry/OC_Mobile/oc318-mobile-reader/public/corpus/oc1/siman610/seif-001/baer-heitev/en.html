A minor. Nowadays they make a candle only for one who is married. The reason for the candles is because the Torah was completed on Yom Kippur, and is called a candle; Mahari Weil. Maharil wrote that they correspond to a person's limbs, or to the positive commandments. According to these reasons one should not make a candle for a woman; see Magen Avraham. See siman 154, se'if 11, regarding which candle is fit for the synagogue. In Yalkut Shimoni on Shoftim it says to make thick wicks in order to increase their light; Magen Avraham.
