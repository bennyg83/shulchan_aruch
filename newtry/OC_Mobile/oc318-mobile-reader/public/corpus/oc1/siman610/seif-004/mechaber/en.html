It is customary in all places to increase lights in synagogues and to spread fine garments in the synagogue.It is customary that every person great or small makes a candle for himself (Mordechai and Mahariv); also a ner neshama for father and mother who died (Kol Bo), and this is proper as some rabbis wrote. If these lights go out on Yom Kippur one does not tell a non-Jew to relight them (Maharil). One whose candle went out on Yom Kippur relights it at motzaei Yom Kippur and does not extinguish it again but leaves it burning until consumed; also accepts that all his days he will not extinguish his candle at motzaei Yom Kippur — neither his nor another's (so found in old minhagim). Some say one spreads tablecloths on Yom Kippur like Shabbat (Mordechai and Maharil).):
