To increase - See Taz. Regarding what he wrote, that wax and a candle from an apostate to idolatry are not accepted, but from a non-Jew we do accept, see siman 154; the same applies throughout the year, see there, se'if 11, and Magen Avraham letter 18. And in Yoreh De'ah 294, if there is concern for enmity, and all the more so with a ruler, we accept from him; see there. This requires study.
