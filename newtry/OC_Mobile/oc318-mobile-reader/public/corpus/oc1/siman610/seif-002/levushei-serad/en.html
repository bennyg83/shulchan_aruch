Subparagraph 3 That the Torah is completed. For the second tablets were given on Yom Kippur. And what he wrote, corresponding to his limbs, means 248 limbs, and spirit and soul, making 250, the numerical value of ner. Women also have a door and key. And what he wrote, corresponding to positive commandments, means the 248 positive commandments and two prohibitions that are repaired by a positive commandment, totaling 250.
