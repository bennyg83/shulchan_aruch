(6) [Seif 3] Am ha'aretz complete, etc.—meaning that in the Gemara Berachot 47 folio 2 they say that even if he read and learned and did not serve Torah scholars to understand reasons of mishnayot—they do not invite zimun on him; except that Tosafot and Rosh there wrote that nowadays they invite zimun on am ha'aretz so each should not go and build a bamah for himself—see there; and so Tur; and such are Shulchan Aruch's words.
