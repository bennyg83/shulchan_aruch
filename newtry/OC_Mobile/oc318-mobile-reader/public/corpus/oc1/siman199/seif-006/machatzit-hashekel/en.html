(s.k. 6) The toddlers about nine or ten—I saw incidentally in Sefer Shulchan Aruch with Ba'er Heitev Chadash who wrote: the toddlers as about six or seven; and Magen Avraham wrote about nine or ten—perhaps it is a printing error—until here. And in my humble opinion it is not a printing error; certainly everywhere toddlers were mentioned its measure is six and seven years; but here since Rabbi Yosef Karo wrote in the name of Rif that Tur wrote in his name that the measure of minor is nine or ten—on this Rabbi Yosef Karo wrote—it appears not precise—for behold Rambam wrote seven and eight years and it does not imply they disagree; but certainly any child who knows to whom they bless—they invite zimun on him—and that is one who reached the season of toddlers, i.e., seven and eight years, etc.; but I found for Rashba who wrote per Rif's view ten and nine years—it appears because that is the time of his education. And therefore also seven and eight that Rambam mentioned is because of time of education; but less than this, etc.—they do not invite zimun on him.

And so Rivash in responsa that even per those who permit, etc.—and that he be nine or ten years old or at least older than six, etc.—until here Rabbi Yosef Karo. And since there are disputing views—and Rashba also wrote so is Rif's view that specifically nine and ten—Magen Avraham was stringent and requires nine and ten; and he did not write in language of dispute of poskim, since it is possible Rabbi Yosef Karo also admits we require nine and ten; and so is Rama's way where it is possible the Mechaber also holds thus even if not true—and the Mechaber disagrees; nevertheless Rama did not write in language of some say when known—all the more here it is possible Rabbi Yosef Karo also holds thus that nine and ten are required; and his stam words—for everything is per the sharpness of the child.

Whether for three or for ten—meaning even though for if one ate vegetable to combine for three it was not permitted except one ate vegetable; but to combine for ten—even three vegetable eaters combine with seven who ate bread, as written siman 197—for a minor there is no distinction.

Since for him he exempted himself—meaning his obligation is great—for him he exempted himself; therefore we require specifically an adult for combination.
