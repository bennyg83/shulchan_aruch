6) [Seif 3] A complete am haaretz, etc. Berachos 47b states that even one who studied Scripture and Mishnah but did not serve Torah scholars in order to understand the reasons of the Mishnayos is not included in Zimun. Tosafos and Rosh there, however, write that nowadays an am haaretz is included in Zimun, so that each person not go and build an altar for himself; see there. The Tur writes the same, and this is the meaning of the Shulchan Aruch's words.
