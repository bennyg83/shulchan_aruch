(11) And minors—see below in seif 10.
(12) They do not invite zimun on them—meaning if there were only two men—these do not combine to three to become obligated through them in zimun—for they are not subject to obligation in zimun, as below in seif 7; and even if they wanted to invite zimun with them—they are also not permitted; and they are worse than women by themselves or slaves who have at least permission to invite zimun, as below—because their company is not fitting that combination of three be through women; and similarly through slaves who are promiscuous in licentiousness; and even a woman with her husband and sons—it is also not proper to combine for this reason.
(13) But they invite zimun among themselves—reshut; and it refers to men and slaves, not minors—for minors are not subject to mitzvah to say they invite among themselves.
(14) Promiscuity of slaves—for they are suspected of licentiousness and homosexuality; therefore even if there are three slaves they do not invite zimun with women in one company—for there is still promiscuity.
(15) With the Name—for mention of the Name is a holy matter; and every holy matter is not with fewer than ten free males, as above in siman 55.
