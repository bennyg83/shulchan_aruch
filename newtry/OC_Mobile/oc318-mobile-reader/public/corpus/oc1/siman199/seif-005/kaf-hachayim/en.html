Cuthean nowadays, etc.—and it is obvious that one wicked publicly transgressing sins—and all the more a mumar—they do not invite zimun on him, for he is no worse than am ha'aretz in Talmudic times—Magen Avraham s.k. 2; Eliyah Rabbah ot 2; Shulchan Gavoah ot 1; Yad Ephraim in Hagahat Turei Zahav; Razah ot 3—and that is one who transgresses many sins; for from one sin specifically they excommunicated him; but if they did not excommunicate him he combines as in seif 11—Avnei Nezer ot 2; and see above siman 55 seif 11 and in our words there ot 16.
