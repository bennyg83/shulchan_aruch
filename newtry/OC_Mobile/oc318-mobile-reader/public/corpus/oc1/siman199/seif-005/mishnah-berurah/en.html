(8) Who is exempt from blessing—meaning whenever his dead lies before him, as in siman 71.

(9) They do not invite zimun—and even per those who say if he wanted to be stringent and answer and bless the permission is in his hand—nevertheless since he is exempt he does not combine; and see what we wrote above in siman 71 s.k. 4 in Mishna Berurah regarding if there is a company in the city designated for escorting the dead and they handed him over to them.

(10) On him—and even b'dieved if he blessed to discharge others it does not help—for whoever is himself exempt from the matter cannot discharge others [Peri Megadim].
