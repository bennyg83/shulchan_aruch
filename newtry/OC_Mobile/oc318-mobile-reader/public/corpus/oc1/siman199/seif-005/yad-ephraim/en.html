In Shulchan Aruch seif 5 exempt—see in Rashi chapter 5 of Demai who wrote to explain that they do not bless on him, etc.—meaning regarding discharging others or others discharge him—18 in this they said he does not bless and does not invite zimun and they do not bless on him, etc.; but he himself is obligated to bless on him the discharger and birkat hamazon; and specifically Keriat Shema, prayer, and tefillin he is exempt—see there; and I have not seen this view mentioned in poskim.
