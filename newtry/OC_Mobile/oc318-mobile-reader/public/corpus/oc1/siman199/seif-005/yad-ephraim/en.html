In Shulchan Aruch, seif 5. He is exempt. See Rash in the fifth chapter of Demai, who explains the statement that no blessing is recited over his food, etc., as referring to discharging others or being discharged by others. In that respect they said that he does not bless, does not join zimun, and others do not bless for him. He himself, however, must recite HaMotzi and Bircas HaMazon. He is exempt specifically from Krias Shema, prayer, and tefillin. See there. I have not seen this opinion cited by the poskim.
