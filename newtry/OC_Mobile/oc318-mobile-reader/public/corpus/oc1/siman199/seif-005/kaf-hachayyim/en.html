5) There: A Cuthean nowadays, etc. It is obvious that one who publicly acts wickedly and commits transgressions, and certainly an apostate, is not included in Zimun, for he is no better than an am haaretz in the Talmudic era. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Shulchan Gavoah, paragraph 1; Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3. This refers to one who commits numerous transgressions. For a single transgression, he is excluded specifically if they excommunicated him; if they did not, he combines, as stated in seif 11. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 2. See above, siman 55, seif 11, and our words there, paragraph 16.
