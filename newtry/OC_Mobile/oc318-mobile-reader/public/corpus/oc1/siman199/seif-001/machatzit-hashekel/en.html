(s.k. 1) Am ha'aretz complete—in the Gemara, etc.—he comes to settle the word complete that implies there is am ha'aretz who is not complete. And if he does not recite Keriat Shema—they do not invite zimun on him (Shach)—for Sefer HaGuf wrote the reason they invite zimun on am ha'aretz is they rely on this tanna who said what is am ha'aretz—all who does not recite Keriat Shema morning and evening; and since most am ha'aretz of ours recite Keriat Shema, therefore they invite zimun from them. And the reason he wrote: if an am ha'aretz is found who does not recite Keriat Shema they do not invite zimun—for there is no tanna to rely on to invite zimun on him. And for the reason of R' Yitzchak—meaning the reason Magen Avraham wrote R' Yitzchak said: if we did not invite zimun on them they would separate from the congregation, etc.—there is no distinction in am ha'aretz; and even if he does not recite Keriat Shema this reason applies and they invite zimun on him—this is the view of Kenesset HaGedolah.

And for an individual we do not care—even if he separates from the congregation.
