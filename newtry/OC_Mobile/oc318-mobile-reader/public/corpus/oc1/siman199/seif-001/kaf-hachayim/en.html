(1) [Seif 1] The waiter who ate, etc.—lest you say he has no fixed [eating], for he goes and comes—it teaches us Berachot 47 folio 2, Beit Yosef there s.k. 1. And even though he does not fix a place for a meal—for behold he must go here and there—nevertheless since his way is thus, this is his fixing. Levush ot 1; however Petaḥ HaDe'ah in siman 197 wrote this law does not apply to the waiter practiced among us—for the reason the waiter combines even though he did not fix is because his way of eating is thus—and that was the waiter's way: to wash hands and bless ha-Motzi and stand to serve the reclining ones; but the waiter nowadays this is not his way of eating—to eat from the table of the reclining ones but afterward reclines at his table like everyone in the land; and there is no way to give him something from the table as in Talmudic times; and similarly Shlah daf 62; and if so his law returns like any other person—as the Rosh wrote another person who eats like the waiter would not combine since he has no fixity—see there. And they brought Shadich Asad ma'arechet 7 ot 8; and therefore one should follow local custom: if they practice like Talmudic times he combines even without fixing; if they practice like Petaḥ HaDe'ah he does not combine until he fixes with them.
