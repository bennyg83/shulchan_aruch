And what the Mechaber wrote—a convert they invite zimun and he may bless birkat hamazon and say al shehinchalta to our forefathers—I have many thoughts on this and I will set them forth with God's help. See Tosafot Bava Batra daf 81 folio 1 s.v. lema'utei—for Rabbeinu Tam a convert does not say to our forefathers; and for R' Yitzchak author of Tosafot he may say like R' Yehuda father of a multitude of nations; and nevertheless R' Yitzchak holds to give to us he does not say—the convert only sons of Keni, etc., as proven in sugya of Makkot 19 folio 1—see there; and therefore in birkat hamazon for a convert one may say d'rabbanan—for it is written on the good land that He gave you—not to a convert; and see Hagahot Maimoniyot there who wrote so for Rashi Berachot 20 folio 2—that women have no share in the land—the same for converts; and Olat Tamid 186 folio 1—that priests and Levites had cities of refuge, etc.—implies converts do not (in Hagahot Maimoniyot and so rules—and Zohar should be Zohar); however Rambam chapter 4 of Bikkurim halacha 3—a convert brings and reads—holds more than R' Yitzchak author of Tosafot—even all converts—even though it is written that You swore to our fathers to give us—already well did the author of Sefer Lechem Yehudah write in the name of Ramban and Ran: You swore to our fathers to give us—at first the oath was to Abraham who is father to converts too to give to all his seed—even converts are his seed (whom he taught faith—Rambam chapter 1 halacha 4 of Bikkurim); but afterward when the matter rolled through sale of Joseph, Egyptian exile—it was divided only to those who left Egypt—not so in viduy ma'aser sheni Rambam ruled chapter 1 halacha 17 converts do not confess—for impossible to say that You gave us—for implies You already gave—and this is impossible. And if so perforce even for Rambam a convert is exempt from birkat hamazon—for impossible 'that He gave you' as in ma'aser sheni as stated. And behold for priests and Levites Rambam wrote the reason cities of refuge and not to our forefathers to give us—worthy to give us like converts (as Ramban and Ran wrote)—for were it not they erred with golden calf—in Bikkurim and tribe of Levi they were worthy of a portion, etc.—so too here; and more than him he says. And for what Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote Berachot 20 folio 2 there Zecherim 1 is a convert obligated in birkat hamazon—41; but for what Magen Avraham wrote in 186 it is difficult—see tractate Ma'aser Sheni chapter 5 and chapter 1 of Bikkurim halacha 4—see there Rambam and Rashba; and here one should not lengthen.
