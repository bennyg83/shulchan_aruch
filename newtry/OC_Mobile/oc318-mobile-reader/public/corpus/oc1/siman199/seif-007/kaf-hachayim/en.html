(7) There am ha'aretz complete, etc.—it implies even if he does not know to recite Keriat Shema morning and evening they invite zimun on him nowadays. However Sefer HaGuf part 3 she'achlu wrote in the name of Riaz that if he does not recite Keriat Shema morning and evening—even nowadays they do not invite zimun on him—see there. And they brought it like Hagahat Turei Zahav; Olat Torah ot 2; and Magen Avraham s.k. 1 wrote it is not applicable here that each build a bamah for himself since most am ha'aretz recite Keriat Shema; and for an individual we do not care—see there. Eliyah Rabbah ot 2; Razah ot 3; Beit Yosef Mechaber ot 5; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 45 ot 23.
