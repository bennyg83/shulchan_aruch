[7] Deaf and fool, etc. Meaning: deaf-mute who speaks and does not hear; but if he does not hear and does not speak he does not combine, as above siman 55 seif 8; and so wrote in Lechem Chamudot. And likewise it does not deal with the fool the Sages spoke of as explained in Yoreh Deah siman 1, but a fool who is not wise and the public hold him as a fool; and in Eliyah Rabbah to Yoreh Deah it will be explained at length.
