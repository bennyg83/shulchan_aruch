Women invite zimun among themselves—permission; and the Gra's view in his Biur is that the primary law is like the Rosh and Terumat HaDeshen that women inviting zimun among themselves is an obligation; but the world did not practice thus.

But when they eat with the men—obviously even one woman.

And they fulfill through our zimun—and it is implied from the words of the small hand that she is forbidden to recite the berachah of zimun and discharge the men, but she must hear and fulfill through the zimun the men bless.
