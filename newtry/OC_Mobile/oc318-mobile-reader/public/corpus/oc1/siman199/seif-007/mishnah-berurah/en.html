(16) Reshut—one may say the reason the Sages did not want to impose on them obligation of berachat zimun when they are by themselves is because it is not so common that they be expert in berachat zimun.

(17) They are obligated, etc.—and even though it was explained in s.k. 12 they do not invite zimun of men and women together, and even if they wanted because their company is not fitting—that is specifically there when the men are only two and obligation of zimun comes through combining women—for this their combination and joining together is a disgrace; not so here—it deals when there are three men besides them and there is no need at all to combine them for obligation of zimun; therefore even though women and slaves combine to fulfill their obligation through hearing from the blesser and answering berachat zimun after him—there is no disgrace in this.

(18) And they fulfill, etc.—for as men discharge men, so they discharge women; and see in Shulchan Aruch HaRav that his view is if women wanted to separate from the men's company and invite zimun by themselves—the permission is in their hands.

(19) Even though they do not understand—and see in siman 193 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 5 what he wrote in the name of Acharonim that they also fulfill birkat hamazon through hearing from the mouth of the blesser even if they do not understand lashon kodesh; and we wrote there that nevertheless it is more proper they say after the blesser word for word silently if they can.
