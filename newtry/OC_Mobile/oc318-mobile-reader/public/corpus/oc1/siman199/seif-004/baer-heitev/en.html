And did not immerse. And whenever he did not immerse properly he is called one who did not immerse. And it is obvious that one who is wicked publicly and transgresses sins—and all the more a mumar—we do not invite zimun on him, for he is no worse than am ha'aretz in Talmudic times — Magen Avraham. And a mumar who repented—even if he did not immerse—may combine; see Yoreh Deah end of siman 268.
