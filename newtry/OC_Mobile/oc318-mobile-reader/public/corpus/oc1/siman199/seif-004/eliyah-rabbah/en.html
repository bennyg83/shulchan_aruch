[4] [Levush] That birkat hamazon requires, etc. It appears he answers thus the wonder of Lechem Chamudot daf 56—that Rama's words here and in siman 193 contradict—for there he wrote they must understand; see there. And it appears to me Levush holds to distinguish that even in berachat zimun they need not understand; nevertheless birkat hamazon requires understanding; and so explained Nachalat Tzvi Rama's words; however one must give a reason to distinguish between them; and perhaps because we rely on poskim that it seems to him women are never obligated only reshut; and since there is no obligation he ruled like Rashi and R' Yitzchak that even if they do not understand they fulfill. One may also say it seems to Rama like Kenesset HaGedolah siman 62 and Olat Tamid siman 193 in the name of Birkat Avraham—that specifically an individual discharges an individual; but an individual who discharges many such as zimun with three and one discharges two—and all the more if they are ten in zimun with the Name; and similarly in a shaliach tzibur who discharges many in his prayer—their obligation for everyone—even if they do not understand they fulfill—until here; therefore here in zimun women fulfill; and in siman 193 it deals when there is no zimun. Only from Levush it implies even when they bless in zimun with three they do not fulfill in birkat hamazon when they do not understand—for it refers to zimun and wrote and I already wrote, etc.; I also examined Sefer Birkat Avraham itself daf 247 who wrote that women for zimun since they do not combine they are like an individual discharging an individual; and see above siman 193 s.k. 2—I wrote to practice the law; therefore I shortened.
