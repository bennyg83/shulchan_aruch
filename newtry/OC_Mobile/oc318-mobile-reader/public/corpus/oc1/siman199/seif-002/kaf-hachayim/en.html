(2) [Seif 2] Cuthean nowadays, etc.—and Cutheans are those Sennacherib settled in cities of Samaria as written the king of Assyria came from Babylon and Cutah, etc.; and because he brought them from Cutah they are called Cutheans; and they converted from fear of lions as written Heaven sent among them lions, etc.—Rashi Kiddushin 75 folio 1. And afterward he made them actual gentiles as stated in Chullin 6 folio 1; and see Beit Yosef.
