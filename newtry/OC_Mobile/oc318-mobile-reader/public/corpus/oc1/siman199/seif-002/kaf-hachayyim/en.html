2) [Seif 2] A Cuthean nowadays, etc. The Cutheans are those whom Sancheriv settled in the cities of Shomron, as it is written, 'The king of Ashur brought people from Bavel and from Cutah,' etc. Because he brought them from Cutah, they were called Cutheans. They converted from fear of the lions, as it is written, 'Hashem sent lions among them,' etc. Rashi on Kiddushin 71a. Afterward, the Sages treated them entirely as non-Jews, as stated in Chullin 6a. See Beis Yosef.
3) There: A Cuthean nowadays, etc. This applies even if he is observant, but one may make a Zimun with a Tzeduki if he is observant. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2. See, however, Sifsei Kohen, Yoreh Deah, siman 2, paragraph 24, who writes in the name of Responsa R' Betzalel, siman 3, that this applies specifically to the Tzedukim and Baitusim of earlier times, who possessed several good qualities. The Karaites of the present era, however, whose deeds have deteriorated, are like non-Jews; see there. Zevach Tzedek there writes the same in the name of several authorities; see there. Therefore, one should also be stringent regarding Zimun. See the following paragraph.
4) There: A Cuthean nowadays, etc. Karaites likewise do not complete a minyan and do not combine for Kaddish, prayer, or Zimun. Rambam, Responsa Pe'er HaDor, siman 41; Beis Menuchah, paragraph 3. We wrote the same above in siman 55, paragraph 63; see there.
5) There: A Cuthean nowadays, etc. It is obvious that one who publicly acts wickedly and commits transgressions, and certainly an apostate, is not included in Zimun, for he is no better than an am haaretz in the Talmudic era. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Shulchan Gavoah, paragraph 1; Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3. This refers to one who commits numerous transgressions. For a single transgression, he is excluded specifically if they excommunicated him; if they did not, he combines, as stated in seif 11. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 2. See above, siman 55, seif 11, and our words there, paragraph 16.
