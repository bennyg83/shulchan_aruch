Women—Taz—that R' Yehuda Kohen combined a woman for zimun; and Mishnah Berachot 45 folio 1 is difficult; and he answered that in zimun of ten he combined; and Mishnah in zimun of three—for it implies in Tur that R' Yehuda Kohen also for three combined; and Taz answered that Mishnah one does not invite zimun—when she blesses—it is berachat zimun—similar to idolater they invite zimun—as Rashi explained practical difference to discharge others (perhaps it was difficult to him that for combination even vegetable kezayit suffices—and here implies bread—therefore he explained thus); so too a woman thus; but for combination of three one may say they combine—for if it is a doubt why was it not asked on daf 20—this teaches he combines; and Rambam holds for combination certainly not, etc.; and Eshel Avraham; and in our chiddushim we lengthened—one may say woman with men do not combine—for their company is not fitting or promiscuity; and R' Yehuda Kohen deals with woman with her husband and son—for those reasons do not apply; and see Levush seif 6 they do not invite zimun on a woman with her husband—see there. Half-slave half-free—perhaps they invite zimun on him; and for himself see siman 589 and 689—Eshel Avraham 5—and it requires study—there it will be explained.
