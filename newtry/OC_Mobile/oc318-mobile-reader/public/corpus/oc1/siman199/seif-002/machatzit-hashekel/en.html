(s.k. 2) And did not immerse—as written in Yoreh Deah siman 124 seif 2—all who did not immerse properly as the law brought in Yoreh Deah siman 268 forbids wine by his touch in drinking—behold he is not yet called Israel; and the same for zimun.

For he is no worse than am ha'aretz in Talmudic times—except that now they invite zimun on am ha'aretz so he not separate from the congregation entirely, as written in s.k. 1; and these, if not for this, would separate.
