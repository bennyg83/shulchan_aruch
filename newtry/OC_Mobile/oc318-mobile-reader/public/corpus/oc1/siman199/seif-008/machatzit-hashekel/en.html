(s.k. 8) And deaf, etc.—see siman 55 seif 8—for prayer the law is so; and the same for zimun.

And in responsum of Maharil, etc.—meaning Levush was forced to settle Rama's language; and to Magen Avraham it appears from Rama's stam and also in Likutei Maharil—from where Rama's law flows—he also wrote stam deaf and fool; and it is forced that he intended specifically deaf-mute who speaks, etc.; and fool who is not a complete fool—but the disciple who wrote Likutei Maharil erred in Maharil's intent; and always the disciple's view in Likutei Maharil is even complete deaf and fool combine—for he understood what Maharil wrote in responsum to one who says a minor, etc.—so too deaf, etc.; but we do not hold thus. And it was Rama's view to the disciple that what he wrote and we do not hold thus refers specifically to minor lying in a cradle; but deaf and fool—even for us he combines—for he is better than minor of Mishna Berurah. And this is Maharil's intent: since there is an opinion that even minor of Mishna Berurah combines—even though we do not hold thus—nevertheless so too deaf and fool for us in any case combine; and similarly Maharil wrote regarding prayer: if one of ten sleeps—nevertheless he combines—for there is an opinion that even minor of Mishna Berurah combines; and even though we do not hold like him—nevertheless for sleeping who is better—even for us he combines—they brought Magen Avraham above siman 55 s.k. 8 see there; but in truth this is not Maharil's intent—that deaf and fool are not better than minor of Mishna Berurah; and as for us minor of Mishna Berurah does not combine—so too deaf and fool; and Maharil did not write deaf and fool combine except per one who says minor of Mishna Berurah combines; and what Maharil concluded and we do not hold thus refers both to minor and to deaf and fool.
