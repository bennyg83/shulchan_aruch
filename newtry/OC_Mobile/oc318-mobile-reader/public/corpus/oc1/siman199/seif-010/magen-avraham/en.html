The toddlers—about nine or ten years old; and specifically one and not two, whether for three or ten (Shach); see siman 55 seif 4—for in prayer he does not combine since he cannot exempt himself from it—not so birkat hamazon—for if he wants he need not eat (Terumat HaDeshen).

We establish him as an adult—meaning one need not check for two hairs; but if we know he did not bring two hairs they do not invite zimun on him, as written siman 55 seif 5; and if he brought two hairs before thirteen it does not help; and it appears to me specifically for combination; but to discharge others we require we know certainly he brought two hairs through examination or his beard filled in, as written Choshen Mishpat siman 35—for behold birkat hamazon is also d'oraisa and we do not rely on presumption; and in Yeshuot Yaakov chapter 7 of Bava Kamma 37 he wrote that from the Torah an adult fulfills through blessing of the minor—only the Sages forbade; therefore they rely on presumption—until here; and it requires study—for in siman 186 seif 8 it implies it is d'oraisa since they ruled the doubt stringently; and further in seif 2 it says that one a son blesses, etc.; and so in Gemara Berachot daf 20; and it is difficult to answer—for since he does not know to bless the Sages did not decree lest he be idle from birkat hamazon d'oraisa, as written siman 193; and further the main reason we hold even though he fulfills he discharges—is because all Israel are guarantors for one another as stated end of chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah; and for a minor arevut is not applicable; and from the Torah he is completely exempt—how can the adult discharge; therefore it appears what he wrote is primary; and see Beit Yosef; and afterward I found he wrote in Milchamot part 3 of Berachot explicitly it is d'oraisa—see there; however if each blesses for himself we do not care; see end of siman 186.

And deaf—specifically deaf-mute who speaks and does not hear; and fool who is not a complete fool; see siman 55 seif 8 (Levush); and in responsum Maharil siman 224 he wrote: per one who says minor lying in cradle combines for ten—so too deaf and fool who ate where the Shechinah rests; but we do not hold thus—until here; perhaps the disciple heard and erred and thought that for everyone the law is so; see siman 55 seif 6.
