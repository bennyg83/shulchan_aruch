The toddlers. About six or seven years old; and Magen Avraham wrote about nine or ten years old—see there; perhaps it is a printing error.

Two hairs. Meaning: one need not check for two hairs; but if we do not know that he produced two hairs, we do not invite zimun on him. And to discharge others we require that we know certainly he produced two hairs; however if each blesses for himself silently—we do not care—see siman 156 s.k. 8 Magen Avraham.

And deaf. Specifically a deaf-mute who speaks and does not hear; and a fool who is not a complete fool—see Levush. Magen Avraham.
