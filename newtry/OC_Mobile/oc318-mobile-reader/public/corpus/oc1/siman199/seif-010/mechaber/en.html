A minor who reached the season of toddlers and knows to whom they bless—they invite zimun on him and he combines whether for three or ten.And some say they do not combine him at all until he is thirteen years old—for then we establish him as an adult who brought two hairs (Rosh and Mordechai chapter three who ate, and Tur); and so is the practice and one should not. deviate.
