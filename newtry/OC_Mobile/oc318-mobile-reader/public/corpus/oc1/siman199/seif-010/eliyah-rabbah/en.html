[7] A deaf person and an incompetent person, etc. The deaf person referred to here is one who speaks but does not hear. One who neither hears nor speaks does not join the zimun, as stated above in siman 55, seif 8, and so writes Lechem Chamudot. Likewise, this does not refer to the halachic category of an incompetent person discussed by the Sages, as explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 1, but to one who is not wise and whom people regard as mentally incompetent. In Eliyah Rabbah on Yoreh De'ah this will be explained at length. [8] See Yoreh De'ah, siman 334, and above, siman 55, seif 12.
