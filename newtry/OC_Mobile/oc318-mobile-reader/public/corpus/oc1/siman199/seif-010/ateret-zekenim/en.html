A minor who reached [the age], etc. And some say specifically only if the minor is at least eight or seven years old, for that is the time of his education; but a minor of six—even if he is sharp and knows to whom they bless and to whom they pray—counts for nothing, for he has not reached the season of toddlers and he is like one without da'at (She'elot u-Teshuvot Rashba and Rivash). And specifically one minor is combined for three, but not two—for we require a majority of adults; but for ten he may combine, even three minors, as three vegetable eaters combine (Kol Bo).
