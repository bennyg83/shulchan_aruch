(23) Who reached, etc.—and less than this his da'at is not considered at all—even if he knows to whom they bless.
(24) The toddlers—meaning about nine or ten [Magen Avraham]; and some say the same below this measure if he is only from six years and up—if he is sharp and knows to whom they bless.
(25) And he combines, etc.—meaning one and not two, whether for three or ten.
(26) Whether for three, etc.—and that which is stated in seif 6 they do not invite zimun on minors—it deals with very young minors or one who does not know to whom they bless.
(27) For then we establish him, etc.—for we say once he reached the category of years—presumably he already brought two hairs and one need not check afterward; but if they checked him and found he has no two hairs—we do not combine him for zimun. And all these views are only for combination that he combine for three and ten; but that he be the inviter and discharge others in birkat hamazon—for everyone he does not discharge until he is thirteen years and one day and known that he brought two hairs—for birkat hamazon is from the Torah and for Torah matters we do not rely on this presumption that presumably he brought; however if the inviter says only n'varech and each in birkat hamazon blesses alone for himself as our custom [as explained in siman 183 seif 7]—we do not care—for since he is thirteen years we rely on presumption for this.
(28) And deaf—it deals with deaf-mute who speaks and does not hear—for in complete deaf we hold he is exempt from all mitzvot like a minor who lacks da'at, as above siman 55 seif 8, and he does not combine. And deaf who hears and does not speak—see in Peri Megadim.
(29) And fool—not dealing with complete fool—for this one does not combine; but one who is not so wise as other people and the public hold him as a fool.
