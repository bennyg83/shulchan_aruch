There seif 3 — A gentile they do not invite zimun on him; and even a convert who was circumcised and did not immerse they do not invite zimun on him. In my humble opinion: see in Magen Avraham what he wrote—all who did not immerse properly, as written in Yoreh Deah siman 124 seif 2. And in my humble opinion there is no proof—for granted there it is for stringency, therefore all who did not immerse properly make yayin nesech; but here since from the Torah he is obligated in zimun, even though from the Sages it is not immersion—nevertheless since from the Torah he is a convert and from the Torah they are obligated in birkat hamazon, they combine; and he is no worse in this than a Cuthean whom they invite zimun on him—and that is such as if he did not immerse before three, as explicit in Yoreh Deah siman 268—for the Rif and Rambam he is invalid; nevertheless since it is only d'rabbanan—for they agree his sons are fit as written there—and the matter is necessary for them from the body of the Talmud that says R' Yehuda: circumcised and did not immerse as if he did not circumcise; and why does it not teach us that even (not) immersed—only that he is not a convert before three; and behold if he did not immerse he is not before three—and it should teach us extra greatness that even immersed not before three he is not a convert. And perforce it is proven that even for them it is only d'rabbanan; and R' Yehuda teaches us that not immersed at all is as if he did not circumcise and is not a convert from the Torah; but if he immersed not before three he is a convert from the Torah, only from the Sages he is not a convert—therefore they combine him for zimun. And proof from what it answers in Berachot—for it asks there and the gentile is obvious—and answers a convert circumcised and did not immerse as Reish Lakish said: a convert circumcised and did not immerse is as if he did not circumcise; and it is difficult here—it should have taught us greatness and said even if he immersed only that he did not immerse properly likewise they do not invite zimun on him—why did it answer that he did not immerse at all. And perforce it is proven that if he immersed, even if not properly, they invite zimun on him—and not like Magen Avraham—and examine well.
