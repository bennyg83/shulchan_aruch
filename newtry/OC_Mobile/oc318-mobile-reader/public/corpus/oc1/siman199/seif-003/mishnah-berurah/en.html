(2) Am ha'aretz complete—for in the Gemara they say that even if he read and learned and did not serve Torah scholars—that is, to understand reasons of mishnayot to ask and answer—they do not invite zimun on him; and therefore he said that even am ha'aretz complete who has no mikra and mishnah—they invite zimun on him in this time—for if they separated from them they would also separate from the congregation entirely; and if he does not fulfill mitzvot of the Torah in a matter publicized among all Israel—such as he does not recite Keriat Shema morning and evening—some say even so they invite zimun on him; and Magen Avraham's view is that in this they do not invite zimun on him—for here since all Israel recite Keriat Shema we are not concerned for damage in what we separate ourselves from individuals who do not recite; and so concluded several Acharonim; and all the more one wicked transgressing sins publicly—they do not invite zimun on him [Magen Avraham and Shulchan Aruch HaRav].
