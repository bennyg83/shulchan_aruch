Am ha'aretz complete—for in the Gemara they say that even if he read and learned and did not serve Torah scholars to understand reasons of mishnayot to ask and answer—they do not invite zimun on him; and therefore he said that even am ha'aretz complete they invite zimun on him in this time—for if they separated from them they would also separate from the congregation entirely; and if he does not recite Keriat Shema they do not invite zimun on him (Shach); and per R' Yitzchak's reason they invite zimun on every am ha'aretz (Kenesset HaGedolah); and in my humble opinion it is not applicable that each build a bamah for himself—since most am ha'aretz recite Keriat Shema; and for an individual we do not care.
