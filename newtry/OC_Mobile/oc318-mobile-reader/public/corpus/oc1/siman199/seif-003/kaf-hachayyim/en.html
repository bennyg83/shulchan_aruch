6) [Seif 3] A complete am haaretz, etc. Berachos 47b states that even one who studied Scripture and Mishnah but did not serve Torah scholars in order to understand the reasons of the Mishnayos is not included in Zimun. Tosafos and Rosh there, however, write that nowadays an am haaretz is included in Zimun, so that each person not go and build an altar for himself; see there. The Tur writes the same, and this is the meaning of the Shulchan Aruch's words.
7) There: A complete am haaretz, etc. This implies that nowadays even one who cannot recite Krias Shema morning and evening is included in Zimun. Shiltei Gibborim in the third chapter of Berachos, however, writes in the name of Riaz that even nowadays, one who does not recite Krias Shema morning and evening is not included; see there. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, and Olas Tamid, paragraph 2, cite this. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1, writes that the concern that each person might build an altar for himself does not apply here, since most amei haaretz recite Krias Shema and we are not concerned about an isolated individual; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3; Beis Menuchah, paragraph 5; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 45, paragraph 23.
