(ג) (16) In one vessel — meaning merchandise of all three partners; but in two vessels, even if they were all partners in one type, wine or oil and the like, it does not help; and although above in siman 366 note 4 it is explained that sometimes it is permitted even in two vessels, see there — that case is different where they partnered from the outset for alleyway partnership; but here since they placed it not for partnership but for merchandise in one vessel, two vessels do not help. And know that Rama follows Tur here, but most poskim disagree and hold that wine and oil, being two species not fit to mix, even when mixed in one vessel do not rely on them for partnership — see Biur Halachah.
