All the laws of eruv for courtyards — its required amount, that a man's wife may make the eruv on his behalf, that one may make an eruv without someone's knowledge if their house opens only to one courtyard, that a minor may collect it, and that one person may acquire on behalf of all — all these laws apply equally to the partnership eruv. {Rama: (and that if they were already partners they need not make an eruv) Rema: Even if one was already partnered with his neighbors in merchandise — one in wine and one in oil — they need not make a partnership eruv, provided it is in one vessel.}
