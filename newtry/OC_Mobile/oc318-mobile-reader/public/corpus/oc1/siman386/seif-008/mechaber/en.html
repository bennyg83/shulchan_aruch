One may make a partnership eruv even with food that is not suitable for him, if it is suitable for some person — such as wine for a nazirite or terumah for a Jew. Similarly, one who vowed or swore not to eat something may still make a partnership eruv with it. Some say this applies only when he vowed or swore not to eat it, but if he vowed or swore not to benefit from it, one may not make a partnership eruv with it for him. {Rama: (And if he said konام on benefiting from it or eating it — according to all opinions one may not make a partnership eruv with it.)
