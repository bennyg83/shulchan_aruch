6) There. In the open area of the alleyway. This is invalid because it is not guarded. Tosafos on Eruvin 71b maintain that initially it is preferable to place a shitufei mevo'os in a courtyard rather than a house, and to establish it with other foods rather than bread, as a recognizable distinction so that people know it is a shituf. Rashi, however, does not agree, but maintains that a house and bread are preferable. Rosh writes likewise. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 4. Maggid Mishneh, chapter 1, likewise writes that the custom is initially to establish a shituf with bread. Darkei Moshe, subsection 1.
