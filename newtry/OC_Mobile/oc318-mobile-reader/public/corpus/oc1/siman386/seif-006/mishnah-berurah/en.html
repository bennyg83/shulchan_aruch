The amount sufficient to accompany bread, etc. This amount is sufficient; the entire meal need not consist of that food.
It is not considered an accompaniment. People do not ordinarily eat bread with it.
And one needs enough to eat from it, etc. See Biur Halachah.
Roasted meat is an accompaniment. Even where some people eat it without bread, their practice is disregarded in relation to common usage. Cooked meat has the same law.
Vinegar is an accompaniment. Its measure is enough to dip vegetables eaten without bread for two meals, which the poskim define as a reviis.
Likewise cooked wine. This wording implies that one needs enough to accompany bread for two meals. Some say that enough to serve as a finishing drink for two meals suffices, but most poskim disagree.
It is not an accompaniment. Since it is not normally used with bread, one needs the amount drunk at two meals, namely two reviiyos.
