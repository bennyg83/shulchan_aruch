For anything normally used as a relish with bread, the required amount is enough to serve as relish for bread eaten at two meals. For anything not used as a relish, the required amount is enough to eat at two meals. Raw meat is not considered a relish and requires enough to eat at two meals. Roasted meat is considered a relish and requires enough to serve as relish for' two meals. Vinegar is considered a relish, as is cooked wine, but uncooked wine is not considered a relish and its required amount is two reviit, and similarly for other liquids.
