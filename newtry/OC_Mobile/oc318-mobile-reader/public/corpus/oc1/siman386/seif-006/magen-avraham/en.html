Roasted meat is considered a relish. Even in places where people eat it without bread, their practice is disregarded in relation to the general norm. See siman 320.
Vinegar. The measure is enough to dip vegetables sufficient for two meals, if the vegetables would otherwise be eaten without bread.
Undiluted wine. Cooked wine is served as dessert rather than for dipping. Its measure is therefore the amount ordinarily brought as dessert for two meals. The same applies to comparable foods.
