And raw meat, etc., and requires quantity, etc.—see in Rashba and Ritva who wondered on this, for raw meat is not fit for eating; and they answered it deals with heavily salted meat until it is not eaten because of its saltiness, and through salting it softened and became somewhat fit for eating; and Ritva further answered the Gemara's intent is not on truly raw meat but on meat that is somewhat cooked and not fully cooked [and it is supported from the sugya in Sanhedrin regarding ben sorer u'moreh that there it calls partially cooked meat "cooked" and not raw meat (daf 71)]—and this is fit for eating and is so called:
