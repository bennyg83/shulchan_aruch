(A.C.) That's not a fool. He said, “I will not give thanks to Hashem’s glory, and I will not give thanks to Hashem’s mercy, and I will not be able to do so, but I will be able to take part in the Lord’s name, but I will show that the Lord is not the same as the Lord’s Prayer.” “And we shall be holy, and we shall not be holy, and shall not be condemned, and shall not be condemned, and shall not be condemned.”
