One may make a partnership eruv with any type of' food except for berries that have hardened to seed, spices, dry beans, onion tops that have not grown a handbreadth in length, truffles and mushrooms, unripe dates,' lentils, wheat and barley, vegetables that are half-cooked, water alone, or salt alone. But if water and salt are mixed together one may use them, and some say only when oil was added to them. Some say one may make an eruv with spices.
