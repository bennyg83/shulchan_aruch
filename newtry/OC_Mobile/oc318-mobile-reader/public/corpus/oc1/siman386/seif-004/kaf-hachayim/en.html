(4) There—they gather bread or another thing, etc., and the reason there is a distinction between eruvin of courtyards and partnerships of alleyways: this is specifically with bread and all types of bread, and this with bread and even with other types of food and beverages—this is because eruvin of courtyards is because of dwelling, to combine their dwelling to make them one, and a person's dwelling is not drawn after wine and other beverages but after bread; but partnership of an alleyway is only to partner the domain of the courtyards in the alleyway, not the domain of the houses, and a courtyard is not a dwelling house; therefore one makes eruv with anything for recognition; and for this reason they can place it in the air of the courtyard or in a house that does not have four by four—Levush, Pri Megadim siman 1, Teshuvah Shulchan siman 2, R' Zalman siman 1; and see above siman 366 siman 7:
