They collect bread or another item, etc. The reason for the distinction between eruv chatzeiros and shitufei mevo'os is that the former may be made specifically with bread, including every type of bread, whereas the latter may be made with bread or even other types of food and drink. An eruv chatzeiros serves to combine residences and make them one. A person's residence is not determined by wine or other beverages, but by bread. A shituf for an alleyway, however, serves only to combine the rights of the courtyards in the alleyway, not those of the houses, and a courtyard is not a residential house. Therefore, any food may be used to establish the shituf as a recognizable reminder. For this reason, it may be placed in the open area of a courtyard or in a house that is not four by four amos.
