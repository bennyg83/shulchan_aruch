Jews living in three separate locations whose gates are each locked at night, and a beadle who collects flour from all of them — this does not help them make a partnership, since an alleyway partnership must be placed in a courtyard within the alleyway, and since they are separate from one another and distant, their partnership is not one and' their alleyways are not one.': If they made an eruv in one place, the others who did not make an eruv do not restrict them, since they have no foot traffic over one another, as the entire city is not considered one alleyway even though they live within one surrounding wall.
