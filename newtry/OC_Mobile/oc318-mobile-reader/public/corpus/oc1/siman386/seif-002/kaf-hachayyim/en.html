7) [Se'if 2] In three separate places. This means three separate alleyways, as stated at the end of the se'if.
8) There. In three separate places. They are distant from one another, with an area in which carrying is forbidden separating between them. That area may be forbidden because it was not rectified by a lechi, korah, or tzuras hapesach, or because non-Jews live there and their rights were not rented. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2.
9) There. Establishing one shituf is ineffective for them. Rather, they must establish an eruv in each separate place. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there.
