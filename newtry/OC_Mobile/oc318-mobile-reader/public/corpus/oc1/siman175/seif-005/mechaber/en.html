If many people are reclining at a meal, each one recites HaTov veHaMeitiv for himself, and one person should not recite it on behalf of all, since we are concerned that they may have the windpipe precede the esophagus when they answer Amen. {Rama: Rema: But if they were reclining to drink without eating, one recites it on behalf of all.
