Maharil writes that some say one should not recite HaTov VeHaMeitiv on Pesach night, so as not to increase the number of cups. It appears to me that one should therefore not drink two kinds of wine. If, however, another type was brought, he must recite the blessing, since the Gemara explicitly mentions it. See siman 472, seif 7, and Tur there. Agudah calls the concern an excessive stringency. Avudraham writes similarly in the name of Maharam, and Tur, siman 473, also rules that the blessing is recited. It appears to me that if he already recited Bircas HaMazon over the first wine, he need not recite HaTov VeHaMeitiv over the second wine, because he already recited HaTov VeHaMeitiv in Bircas HaMazon. When he has no more. Knesses HaGedolah writes that Raavad and Rabbeinu Yerucham hold that the blessing is not recited, while Baal HaNesivos remains uncertain. Radbaz, siman 125, decides as follows. If some of the first wine remains, but they do not wish to drink it and bring another wine, they recite the blessing. If none of the first wine remains, the second wine was not necessarily brought because of a change in quality, and the blessing is not recited.
Which had not been. Or if he transgressed and recited the blessing first upon the inferior wine.
