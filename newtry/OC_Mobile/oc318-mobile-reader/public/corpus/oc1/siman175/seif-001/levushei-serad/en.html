Magen Avraham, seif katan 1 “So as not to add,” etc. Siman 472 explains that one may drink as much as he wishes between the cups, provided he need not recite Borei Peri HaGafen. Whenever he must recite Borei Peri HaGafen, it appears as though he adds to the prescribed cups. Magen Avraham therefore says that even when he must recite HaTov VeHaMeitiv, it likewise appears as though he adds to the cups.
