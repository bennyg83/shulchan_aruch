And specifically when they were not before him, etc. Rama wrote this to reconcile two rulings of the Shulchan Aruch. Here it states that if he initially had two wines, he recites HaTov VeHaMeitiv, whereas in seif 3 it does not rule so. Rama therefore explains that the present seif refers specifically to where both wines were not before him at the time of the blessing. If they were, it is the case of seif 3. Thus, when the Shulchan Aruch says, “not specifically if it was newly brought,” it means after he had finished drinking the first wine. Even if the second was brought while some of the first remained, but only after he had already recited the blessing over the first, the law applies. Although Rama writes in the gloss, “even though he has none of the first left,” implying that this is a greater novelty, there he discusses exemption from Borei Peri HaGafen, whereas here he discusses the obligation of HaTov VeHaMeitiv. Beit Yosef indeed treated seif 1 and seif 3 as conflicting, but here he retracted. See what I write in seif 3.
