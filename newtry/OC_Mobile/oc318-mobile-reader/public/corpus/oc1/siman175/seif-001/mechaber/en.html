Laws of the blessing HaTov veHaMeitiv over wine. 6 sections: If they brought them another wine, he does not recite borei pri hagafen
but does recite HaTov veHaMeitiv over it. And it is not specifically when they brought a new wine — the same applies if they had two wines from the beginning, they recite HaTov veHaMeitiv over the second.Rema: Even if he no longer has any of the first wine. (Beit Yosef) Rema: This applies specifically when both wines were not before him together when he recited borei pri hagafen, but if they were both there together he need only recite borei pri hagafen, as will be explained in section 3.
