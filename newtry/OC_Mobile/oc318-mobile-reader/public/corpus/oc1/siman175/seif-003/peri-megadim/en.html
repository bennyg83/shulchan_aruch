See Beit Yosef and Bach, who infer from the fact that Rabbi recited a blessing over each barrel that even when there are many varieties of wine one may recite HaTov VeHaMeitiv several times. It appears to me that this applies specifically when all were not on the table simultaneously. If all were present when he recited HaTov VeHaMeitiv, one blessing covers them all. Even Borei Peri HaGafen and HaTov VeHaMeitiv may both be recited when two wines are on the table, as in seif 1, because those are two distinct blessings. That is unlike the present case. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 7. This requires study.
