[4] One is new, etc. Nachalas Tzvi writes that this applies when he first drinks new wine and afterward old wine. But if he first drinks old wine and afterward new wine, he does not recite HaTov VeHaMeitiv unless he knows that the new wine is as good as the old, since ordinarily new wine is not as good as old wine. Tashbetz, siman 296, writes that throughout the thirty days of fermentation it is called new wine. Kol Bo writes likewise.
