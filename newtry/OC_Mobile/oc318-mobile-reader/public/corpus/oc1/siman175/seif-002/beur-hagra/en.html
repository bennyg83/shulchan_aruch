Seif 2: One recites, etc. This follows Rashbam's explanation and what Tosafos write in Pesachim there, s.v. shinui, etc.: “Rashbam explains,” etc.; “however, there are,” etc. Although many disagree, as Tosafos write there, in Berachos there, and as do the other poskim, Beit Yosef writes that common practice follows Rashbam.
And there is no distinction, etc. Meaning, although the Yerushalmi says that this applies specifically to new and old wine, Rabbi disagrees, as Tosafos write there and elsewhere. Tosafos further write in Berachos there that some explain that passage, etc., but “we have not found,” etc. This is the meaning of “there is no distinction,” namely, neither regarding Borei Peri HaGafen nor regarding HaTov VeHaMeitiv.
And even, etc. This is unlike the Agur in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel. Terumas HaDeshen. Rabbeinu Chananel's reason is that all the blessings in the chapter HaRoeh apply only after thirty days, as Tosafos write on 54a, s.v. haroeh, etc. Rama's reason is that the thirty-day interval was stated only regarding blessings over seeing. Even among those blessings, thunder and lightning require a blessing each time. See Magen Avraham, siman 218, seif katan 5, and Rosh there.
