One recites HaTov veHaMeitiv over any change of wine by default, even if he does not know that the second is superior to the first, as long as he does not know it is inferior. {Rama: Rema: There is no distinction between both being new, or one new and one old. (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot, ch. HaRo'eh, Tur, and Terumat HaDeshen §34) And this applies even if he drank from it within thirty days. Some say that if one first drank red wine and they brought him white wine — even if it is of lower quality — he recites HaTov veHaMeitiv, since white wine is healthier for the body than red. (Mordechai, ch. Arvei Pesachim)}
