(א) And he breaks — see Magen Avraham who wrote [and several Acharonim agreed: Nachalat Tzvi, Nehar Shalom, Gra] — the Mechaber's words are taken from Rif, and Rif follows his view that one need not place on the seder except two matzot; therefore even though generally when sanctifying on Shabbat and Yom Tov over bread one should not break bread until finishing kiddush, here the Torah called it lechem oni and as an impoverished person's custom is to break — therefore he breaks before kiddush; and only at motzi it is good that both remain whole to fulfill lechem mishneh [and this too is not l'akiva as clear in Tur siman 475]. But for us who practice the seder on three matzot as above siman 473 seif 4 — we break the middle matzah in two at the start of the seder and do not break the upper until after birkat achilat matzah; and so Gra's explanation implies the Mechaber's words are only per Rif.

(ב) Until he completes kiddush and blesses on eating matzah — see Maamar Mordechai, who decides that Shehecheyanu is included in kiddush; and therefore he blesses Shehecheyanu and then on eating matzah.

(ג) Until gaal yisrael — see Mishna Berurah. And regarding a sick person who cannot eat a kezayit of matzah, Chayei Adam wrote he may say Yehalelucha according to all opinions; however in the berachah Asher Ga'alanu he should not say it in order to eat matzah and maror with it — only he says Ve'higi'anu halailah hazeh, ken the Holy One, Blessed be He, will bring us, etc.
