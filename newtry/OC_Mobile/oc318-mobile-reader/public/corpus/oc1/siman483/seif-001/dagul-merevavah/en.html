And if afterward wine was brought to him after he already made kiddush on bread, it appears to me he should say the hagadah from bondage to redemption and not finish; and Halleluyah that is said before him — only he begins the berachah Asher Ga'alanu and completes it, and blesses borei peri hagafen and drinks the first cup; and afterward he says Halleluyah and completes all the first half of Hallel, and blesses borei peri hagafen and drinks the second cup. I say the reason for this is so he says some praise on each and every cup; and see Mordechai. And even though the Hagahah wrote there that telling the hagadah is only relating words, and therefore they established half Hallel together with the hagadah — when one does the seder in order and said kiddush on the first cup; but in this case, where he did not make kiddush on the cup, it is better to divide the hagadah with Hallel on two cups.
