(א) It appears to me that one who has only one cup takes it for kiddush, for some say Birkas Hamazon does not require a cup; and if he has two he takes for kiddush and Birkas Hamazon; and if he has three he takes for kiddush, and on the second cup he says the hagadah until gaal yisrael, and on the third he blesses Birkas Hamazon, and afterward he says half Hallel without a cup. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Orach Chayyim: one who does his seder on bread does not bless neither Asher Ga'alanu nor Yehalelucha, for both are cup blessings end of his words; but here in Shulchan Aruch he wrote as Rif, who says gaal yisrael, for the reason of hagadah was enacted on matzah that we answer upon it many matters; but Yehalelucha he does not say in the conclusion. And he breaks: he breaks the first matzah, and the second he does not break until he blesses on eating matzah, as was written siman 475, and so in Bach; nevertheless requires further study why he does not break one before kiddush, for nevertheless he will have lechem mishneh. Therefore it appears to me these words are taken from Rif's words, who holds only two matzos are needed; but we take three — he breaks the first before kiddush, for we are in doubt whether one must bless hamotzi on a piece, as siman 475 and so in Bach; and if it fell on what was written siman 269.

(ב) Other vegetables — and he blesses borei peri ha'adamah, for they are like things coming not from the meal's heat, since they do not dip bread in them — Levush; and so Rosh and Mordechai: specifically maror that is obligatory to eat with matzah — some say matzah exempts it, but not other vegetables; but in Tosafot it implies somewhat that vegetables too — bread exempts them (see siman 477); and nevertheless it appears one should bless on them, for those vegetables we eat, namely parsley, do not continue appetite for the food.

(ג) If it is chamar medinah. It appears to me that even in a place where all year the majority of the people do not drink honey water, nevertheless if on Pesach the majority drink from it, it is called chamar medinah; see siman 182, and so in Bach in the name of Rashal, because on bread there are differing opinions — therefore it is better to take other beverages. And per what he wrote to bless Birkas Hamazon on kvass or beer if it is the drink of the majority of the masses; and if so, possibly here too, if the majority drinks kreiznacht or apple trank, it remains permitted to take it for four cups if he has no mead and wine.
