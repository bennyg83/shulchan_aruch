Magen Avraham wrote: it appears to me that one etc., for Birkas Hamazon does not require a cup. If so, kiddush is more important than Birkas Hamazon — all the more so it is more important than the two latter cups, of which Birkas Hamazon considers one, as he wrote afterward. And the cup of Birkas Hamazon is more frequent than the other two cups of Birkas Hamazon. And the cup of hagadah precedes the cup of Hallel, for hagadah is the essence of this night. And also it precedes its recitation to the recitation of Hallel — he should not pass over the mitzvah and take it for the third cup.
