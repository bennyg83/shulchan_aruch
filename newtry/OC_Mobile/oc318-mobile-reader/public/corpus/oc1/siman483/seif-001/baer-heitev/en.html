(א) Wine. See there siman 472 s.k. 15 that if he has no wine he fulfills with raisin wine if there is moisture in them when they press them on erev Pesach. And Magen Avraham wrote it appears to me that one who has only one cup takes it for kiddush; and if he has two he takes them for kiddush and Birkas Hamazon; and if he has three he makes with them the three first cups, see there. And Kaf Hachayim wrote it appears to me that if it is the first night and he has only two cups he fulfills one cup until the second night see there. And specifically when he has only two cups; but if he has four cups alone he must take all of them on the first night as stated siman 472 s.k. Taz see there. And so too in responsa Shevut Yaakov vol. 2 siman 14 see there.

(ב) And he breaks. That is, he separates first the middle [matzah] and takes half for afikoman as above siman 473 and 475. Magen Avraham see there.

(ג) Goal. But he will not say in a piece of praise as he has been given to the glass and the people.

(ד) Chamar medinah. And Magen Avraham wrote that even in a place where all year the majority do not drink honey water, nevertheless if on Pesach the majority drink from it, it is called chamar medinah. And Kaf Hachayim wrote one should not be lenient except specifically in a place where they are accustomed to drink it all year see there. For kreiznacht or apple trank, if it is the drink of the majority of the masses, it is permitted to take it for four cups if he has no mead and wine Magen Avraham. And siman 472 s.k. 15 see what was written there.
