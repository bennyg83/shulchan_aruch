(א) One who has no wine — meaning he has none at all; but if he has wine, even only for one cup [and the same for other beverages if it is chamar medinah per what Rama concludes in the Hagahah], he sanctifies on it and does not sanctify on bread, even if his custom on other Shabbasos and Yamim Tovim is to sanctify on bread; nevertheless on this night, when the Sages enacted four cups, they enacted to sanctify on wine and not on bread. And if he has two cups, he sanctifies on the first, and afterward says the hagadah without a cup, and in Birkas Hamazon he blesses on the second cup — and with this he also fulfills the view of some in siman 182 who hold Birkas Hamazon requires a cup. And if he has three cups, he sanctifies on one, says hagadah on one, Birkas Hamazon on one, and half Hallel after Birkas Hamazon he says without a cup.
(ב) (b) and shall be performed:
(ג) (c) And his hands are laid on him, for he is commanded to be eaten in his hand, as he is exalted in the cup of his holy name:
(ד) (D) And then eat other vegetables – this is the capsule. And to eat water salt or vinegar, and first to be treated as the rest of the years AA to eat Dea should not be tasted first. And for a blessing on them, it depends on whether it is anything that continues to bless them, and if they do not bless the Creator of the Land:
(ה) And he says Mah Nishtanah, etc. — and likewise he says Ha lachma anya, etc., until gaal yisrael. See Magen Avraham, who wrote Yehalelucha is not said in the conclusion, as it was enacted on the cup specifically; and Kaf Hachayim cited his words; but Peri Chadash concludes it appears better that he say it, as Darkei Moshe wrote the cup does not prevent this, for in the synagogue they say Hallel without a cup, and several Acharonim agreed.
(ו) And he blesses on maror — and dips it in charoset. Regarding washing a second time when he wishes to finish his meal, Maamar Mordechai concludes he must wash his hands, for he interrupted in hagadah and Hallel [and similar to what we ruled above that he must wash his hands for eating, even though he washed initially as required for something whose dipping is in liquid, because he interrupted in reciting the hagadah — and here likewise]; however if he intended in the first washing to finish his meal and to guard his hands from soiling them, he concludes there that he need not wash a second time.
(ז) (b) Without blessing, I shall return to the Temple Mount to the High Court of the first blessing that blesses the invention and then ends with every order without a glass of wine:
(ח) (h) And in places that drink, and so on, see the above in the letter of the book:
(ט) As was explained above, etc. — meaning, as was agreed there, in the Hagahah that on this night one does not sanctify on other beverages.
(י) (e) We must be trusted and so on, so that he can make a order as his or her, and to sustain a few cups:
(כ) (יא) If it is chamar medinah — and even in a place where all year the majority of the people do not drink honey water, and only on Pesach as well there is to be lenient [Magen Avraham, Rabbi Akiva Eiger, and other Acharonim — not like Kaf Hachayim]. And it is further possible that if the majority drinks kreiznacht or apple trank, it is permitted to take it for four cups if he has no wine.
