(א) [Levush] For four cups, etc. — one who has only one cup takes it for kiddush; if he has two, takes them for kiddush and Birkat Hamazon; if three, uses them for the first three cups (Magen Avraham); and it appears to me that if on the first night he has two cups, he fulfills one on the second night as above siman 482 regarding matzah (Chok Yaakov). And it is difficult what he himself wrote in the name of Magen Avraham end of siman 472 that one with only four cups takes them all on the first night — strained to distinguish; and it seems to me it is not comparable to matzah, for by law one suffices, only for support and remembrance we take three, unlike four cups which are a full mitzvah requiring selling what one has to fulfill them.
