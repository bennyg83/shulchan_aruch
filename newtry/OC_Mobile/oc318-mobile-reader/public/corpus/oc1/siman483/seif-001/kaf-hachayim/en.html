(1) [Seif 1] One who has no wine, etc. And one who has only one cup takes it for kiddush, for some say Birkas Hamazon does not require a cup; and if he has two he takes for kiddush and Birkas Hamazon; and if he has three he takes for kiddush, and on the second cup he says the hagadah until gaal yisrael, and on the third he blesses Birkas Hamazon, and afterward he says half Hallel without a cup. Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman; however Chayei Adam letter 2 wrote that if it is the first night and he has only two cups, he fulfills one cup until the second night see there; but Eliyah Rabbah letter 1 wrote to attain him see there; and so too Chok Yosef letter 1, that it appears to him as Magen Avraham's view, for the second night is only due to doubt — if so, it is preferable to do the mitzvah in the best manner on the first night see there. And so too the view of Maamar Mordechai letter 1 as Magen Avraham's words. And so too R' Zalman letter 5, Chayei Adam klal 126 letter 17. Mishna Berurah letter 1. And see above siman 474 letter 8.
