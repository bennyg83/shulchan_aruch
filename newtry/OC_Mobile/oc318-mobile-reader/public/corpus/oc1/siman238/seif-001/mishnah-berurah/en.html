(1) With Torah study at night, etc. — as we say, a night was not created except for review; and since for this the night was created, therefore one must be more careful with it. And it is stated in the Gemara — anyone who engages in Torah at night, the Holy One, blessed be He, draws upon him a thread of kindness by day, as it is said "By day the the Master will command His kindness." And at night a song with me. Further they said [Menachos 110] — Torah scholars who engage in Torah at night, Scripture considers them as if engaged in the Temple service, and they bring a verse there. Further they said [Tamid 32] — anyone who engages in Torah at night, the Shechinah is opposite him, as it is said "Arise, cry out at night" — meaning to engage in Torah; and it is written after it "Pour out your heart like water opposite the face of the the Master." And Rambam wrote, and it is brought in Yoreh De'ah siman 246 — one who wishes to merit the crown of Torah should be careful with all his nights and not lose even one of them in sleep, eating, drinking, conversation, and the like, etc. And what is written "in all his nights" — that is, even in summer on short nights such as in Tammuz when the main purpose of night is set for sleep as Tosafos wrote — nevertheless one must in any event learn a little before sleep [later authorities]; and see above in siman 1 in Mishna Berurah note 9:
