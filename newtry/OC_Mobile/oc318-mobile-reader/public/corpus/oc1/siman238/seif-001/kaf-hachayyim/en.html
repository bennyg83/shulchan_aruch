1) [Seif 1] One must be careful regarding nighttime Torah study, etc. Our Sages, of blessed memory, said in Midrash Rabbah on Shir HaShirim 5, concerning the verse, 'His locks are curled,' that the song of Torah exists only at night, as it is stated, 'Arise, sing at night.' Eruvin 65a records that Reish Lakish said: The night was created only for study. Eruvin 18b records that R' Yirmeyah ben Elazar said: Any house in which words of Torah are heard at night will not be destroyed. Therefore, since the hours of night are comfortable and favorable for study, the punishment of one who wastes them is severe. Tur. The introduction to the Zohar, 11a, similarly states: Come and see how mighty is the power of Torah and how exalted it is above everything. Whoever engages in Torah fears neither those above nor those below, and does not fear the world's harmful maladies, because he is attached to the Tree of Life and learns from it every day. Torah teaches a person to walk upon the path of truth. It teaches him counsel for how to repent before his Master and nullify the decree. Even if it was decreed upon him that the decree would not be nullified, it is immediately nullified and removed from him, and does not rest upon that person in this world. Therefore, a person must engage in Torah day and night and not depart from it, as it is stated, 'You shall contemplate it day and night.' If he departs from Torah or separates from it, it is as though he separated from the Tree of Life.
