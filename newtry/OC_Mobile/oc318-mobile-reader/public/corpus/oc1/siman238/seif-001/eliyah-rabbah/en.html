[1] [Levush] Only for review etc. Shach wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 246 — that is on long nights when the main purpose of night is to learn; but on short nights the main purpose is to sleep, and nevertheless one must learn a little all night, until here; and so Perishah and Mateh Moshe and Bach wrote that they accepted mishkavi ad kumi shkavi, and mikomi ad shekavi kumi. Meaning to say: from the time of Shavuos when we read Megillas Rus, for it is written therein "lie until morning," until Tisha B'Av when we read Eichah, for it is written therein "Arise, cry out at night" — shkavi — one need not rise early to study Torah; and mikomi, which is Tisha B'Av, until Shavuos — kumi — one must rise early in the morning to study Torah, until here. Magen Avraham wrote, and in Olas Shabbat he wrote from the ninth of Av until the fifteenth of Iyar; and some say the time of sleep is eight hours. And it appears to me that everything is according to what kind of person he is; and see in Mateh Moshe sha'ar 3 that he expounded greatly on one who engages in Torah at night — that it is as if he offers incense and presents offerings to Heaven, and the Holy One, blessed be He, draws upon him a thread of kindness and the Shechinah is opposite him, as it is said "Arise, cry out at night opposite the face of the the Master." The Shelah wrote daf 123 — it is good to learn before lying down even in summertime even a small amount so that he lie down amid words of Torah; and see there further.
