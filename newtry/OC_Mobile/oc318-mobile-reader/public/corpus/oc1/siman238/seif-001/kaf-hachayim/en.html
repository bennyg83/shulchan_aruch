(1) [Seif 1] One must be careful with Torah study at night, etc. — as Chazal wrote (Midrash Shir HaShirim 5 on the verse "His locks are curled") — there is no song of Torah except at night, as it is said "Arise, cry out at night." And we learned in Eruvin 65a — R' Levi said: A night was not created except for review. And we learned there in Eruvin 18b — R' Yirmiyah ben Elazar said: Any house in which words of Torah are heard at night will not be destroyed. Therefore, since the hours of night are restful and desirable, let us learn — one who neglects them, his punishment is greater (Tur). And so too in the introduction to the Zohar 11a — Torah scholar, how mighty is the power of Torah and how elevated it is above all; for anyone who toils in Torah is not afraid of those above or below, and is not afraid of harmful spirits of the world, because he is attached to the Tree of Life and learns from it every day; for Torah leads a person to walk on the path of truth, teaches him how to return before his Master so that that decree be nullified — for even if a decree was issued upon him that he not be idle, that decree is immediately nullified and removed from him and does not rest upon a person in this world. And for this reason a person must toil in Torah day and night and not turn away from it — as it is said "And you shall meditate on it day and night." And if he turns away from Torah, if he separates from it — it is as if he separated from the Tree of Life, end of his words.
