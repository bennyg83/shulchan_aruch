(2) And when he learns — in Torah one must bring the learning out with his mouth and not learn only in his heart, as it is stated in Eruvin daf 54a — see there. And so too in the introduction to the Zohar daf 4a, and these are his words — how much does a person who toils in Torah day and night have, because the Holy One, blessed be He, listens to their voice, for they are engaged in Torah; and so in parashas Bereishis daf 47a — Torah scholar, through him the Holy One, blessed be He, made the world, and through him the world is sustained — the spirit of those who engage in Torah; and all the more so the spirit of the breath of the rabbis of the house of the Rav, end of his words. And so in parashas Kedoshim daf 35a, and these are his words — that word of Torah that one brings out from his mouth goes and flies in the world and ascends on high, and how many holy supernal ones attach themselves to that word and it ascends on a straight path and is crowned with a holy crown and bathes in the river of the world to come that flows and emerges from Eden and is received in it and becomes fragrant within it and plants the merchandise of that river — a supernal tree; and then flows and emerges supernal light and is crowned through it upon that person every day, end of his words. And so the Maggid said to Maran, of blessed memory — for if permission were given to the eye to see, you would see your eyes full on every side entirely full of hosts created from the breath of your mouth when you engage in Mishnah, as written in Maggid Meisharim parashas Bo — see there:
