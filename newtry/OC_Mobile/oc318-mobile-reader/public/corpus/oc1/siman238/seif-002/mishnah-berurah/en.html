(3) Fixed quota, etc. — see above in siman 155 seif 1 and in Mishna Berurah there:
(4) He should complete it at night — meaning even on short nights he is obligated to be awake at night in order to complete his quota, and he should not delay it until the next day, for the following day is obligated on its own, and it turns out that on this day he did not complete his quota and it is crooked and cannot be straightened [Peri Megadim]:
(5) Immediately — and it constitutes a vow, as we say [Nedarim 7] — one who says "I will learn this chapter" — it is a great vow, a vow to the God of Israel; and it is stated in Yoreh De'ah in siman 204 seif 1 that if one accustomed himself to do a good thing, if his intent was to always do so it is a vow; and it is good that he stipulate at the outset that this matter should not be upon him as a vow, lest some time occur when he cannot complete it:
