We say in the chapter "Return" — a night was not created except for sleep; and Tosafos explain such as on the nights of the Tekufat Tammuz solstice. And Bach wrote that the later authorities accepted mishkavi ad kumi — meaning from Shavuos until Tisha B'Av shkavi, and mikomi until Shavuos kumi — meaning to study Torah at night; and in Peri Shabbat he wrote from the fifteenth of Av until the fifteenth of Iyar; and some say the time of sleep is eight hours. And "I have slept, then I will rest" — and it appears to me that everything is according to what kind of person he is, and as written siman 231 see there. He should complete it — that it constitutes a vow and therefore he should stipulate at the outset that it should not be upon him as a vow; see Yoreh De'ah siman 203 and 204 seif 2:
