There is. One who prohibits; see the Taz. In the Beis HaRav, when there is a lulav they circle with both, meaning the lulav and the hoshanah; and if there is no lulav in the city, they should circle on Hoshana Rabbah with the hoshanah. But on the other days of Sukkos, if there is no lulav in the city, they should not circle at all, just as on Shabbos we do not circle; see Bach, siman 660. See the gloss, seif 7: it is better not to take the aravah at all, neither when taking the lulav nor in the circling. See Magen Avraham, letter 8, and, with Hashem's help, there it will be explained further; and see the Taz, letter 5, there.
