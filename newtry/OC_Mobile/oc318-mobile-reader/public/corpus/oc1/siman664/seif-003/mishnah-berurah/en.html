(14) That even on this day, etc. - Meaning, as on the previous days, when they do not take the aravah, for aravah was established only for this day.
(15) And the custom is not, etc. - Also with the aravah. The reason is that in the Gemara there is a dispute whether one circles with the lulav or with the aravah, and the first opinion holds like the one who says that one circles with the lulav. The common custom is to satisfy all opinions, for even the one who says with the lulav does not invalidate it if he circles with the aravah, and likewise the one who says with the aravah does not invalidate it if he circles with the lulav [Beis Yosef]. See below, seif 7 in the gloss. The Acharonim agreed that if there is no lulav in the city, they should circle on Hoshana Rabbah with the aravah.
