(21) And some say that he fulfills his obligation with it - This is where he lifted it a second time for the sake of aravah, for through this there is recognizability that it is for the sake of the mitzvah. Know that even according to the first opinion, that one does not fulfill his obligation with the aravah in the lulav, this is specifically while it is bound with the lulav and the other species; but if he untied the lulav's binding after taking it, and took the aravah by itself, it is obvious that he fulfills his obligation with it.
