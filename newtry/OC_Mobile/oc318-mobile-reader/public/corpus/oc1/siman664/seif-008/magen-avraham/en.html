Not to take it with the lulav. The Arizal warned very strongly not to join them, and this is how one should practice. The Maavar Yabok, siman 25, wrote that the choicest mitzvah is with two separate branches in his hand, and likewise whenever he would beat on the ground. One Gaon took a single stalk that had seventeen branches, corresponding to the four-letter Name in minor numerical value [25]. Also in Yemin Moshe, section 4, part 2, chapter 24, he wrote not to bind them.
