(Seif katan 8) That, etc. And the Arizal warned, etc. In the Shelah he wrote as follows: I practice as I saw my teacher practice, etc., that while the lulav was in his hand he did not take the aravah at all. When he reached 'Taaneh emunim shofechim,' etc., he set the lulav and esrog down from his hand, gave honor to the aravah, and took it in his hand, because in this hoshanah water is constantly mentioned, and aravah grows by water. He held it until 'chabit chabit.' End quote. The Taz in seif katan 5 wrote as follows: when saying the hoshanah mentioned in it, 'sichim baderech mata'atam omesim beshav'atam,' one should hold the four species; this is in the hoshanah that begins 'az ke'einei avadim,' etc. If so, one should not remove the lulav and its species until the hoshanah that begins 'ometz yish'acha,' etc., and then he should hold the aravah.
