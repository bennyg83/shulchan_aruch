[8] [Levush] For it is written explicitly in the Torah, etc. This is difficult, for in Sukkah 43 the Gemara asks that lulav too should override Shabbos, in order to publicize that it is from the Torah. Rashi explains: to publicize that it is from the Torah all seven days, for this is not stated explicitly. The Gemara answers that lulav is handed over to everyone, and there is the decree of Rabbah, etc. One cannot answer based on what Tosafos wrote there, s.v. 'lo,' etc., that these are written explicitly, etc.; for one can say that Tosafos is referring to the first day, which is written explicitly. But the Levush implies that he is referring to when the Beis HaMikdash stood, and then in truth the first day overrode Shabbos even outside the Mikdash, as explained in the Talmud, so this remains difficult. Also Tosafos are difficult: they should have answered that the reason we are not concerned regarding shofar and lulav that they not fall on Shabbos is because people know that they were pushed off because of the decree of Rabbah, etc., which is not so with aravah, for the messengers of beis din bring it. See the beginning of siman 508, where I wrote another answer to Tosafos' difficulty, and see the end of siman 588.
