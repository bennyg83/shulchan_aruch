[9] [Levush] And that which they fixed, etc. The Taz wrote that the seventh day is more sacred, for they would circle seven times. End quote. This is difficult: if so, what does the Gemara ask, that it should override on one of those days, and answer that once you remove it from the first, establish it on the seventh? Rashi explains: a specific day. Let the Gemara derive it from the fact that the seventh is more sacred.
