(16) The hoshanos beautiful - It is good ab initio that there be at least three branches, and in the name of the Arizal they wrote five branches.
(17) Like the measure of the length, etc. - Meaning three tefachim, for we have not found that they were lenient with it except in this specific matter of one leaf, etc. Some say that the aravah of Hoshana Rabbah should be taken with the branches untied; but the Ramaz disagreed and wrote that they must be tied, and so is our custom.
(18) And everything that invalidates the aravah in the lulav, etc. - Therefore one must be careful that it not be stolen and not be dry. In any event, if it was not dry but only wilted, it is certainly valid. Nevertheless, it appears to me that ab initio it is better that they be moist. The proof is from what is stated there in the Mishnah, daf 45, that they would place them in golden basins so that they would not wilt, and this was because of beautifying the mitzvah.
(19) On the ground or on the vessel - In the name of the Arizal they wrote that specifically on the ground one should beat five beatings. It is good to beat first on the ground five beatings, and afterward on vessels, to remove the leaves. One need not remove all the leaves.
