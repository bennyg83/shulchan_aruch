[7] To untie, etc. In order to fulfill both opinions: all six days according to the Sages (Rokeach 223). In the Talmud, according to the Sages, no binding is needed, at the beginning of chapter Lulav HaGazul. The Bach wrote that the shaking is to stop harmful dews, and on Hoshana Rabbah one unties the binding in order to shake more strongly to stop them, since now is the completion of the sealing concerning water.
