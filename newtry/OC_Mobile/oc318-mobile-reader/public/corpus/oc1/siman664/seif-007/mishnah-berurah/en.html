(22) And after the first shaking, etc. - The reason is that we hold that outside the time of the mitzvah, one does not violate bal tosif unless he intends to add to the mitzvah. Here, since its mitzvah has passed, it is considered outside its time, and he did not intend to add to the mitzvah of lulav, but only for taking the aravah.
(23) It is a mistake in my eyes - He follows his own approach, for above in siman 651:15 he ruled that with aravah and hadas one may add as much as he wants.
(24) To make recognizable that it is an obligation - As he wrote in seif 5, that for this reason it is taken only by itself.
(25) And at the time of circling, etc. - The reason for the custom was to satisfy all opinions, for some say that we circle with the lulav, and some say that we circle with the aravah, as above in seif 3.
(26) And it is better not to take it with the lulav - In order to fulfill the mitzvah of taking aravah completely, as above in seif 5. The Arizal also warned very strongly not to join the aravah with the lulav.
(27) That after he has circled, he should remove the lulav from his hand - Meaning, unlike those who hold the lulav together with the aravah until the time of beating. In our times the custom is as the Rema wrote, that we do not take the aravah with the lulav at all.
