Like. See the Taz and see the Tur. See the Bach, that the moon reaches the foundation of water; see there. Regarding what is written in the Tur, that the custom is to untie the binding of the lulav, see the Bach: the shaking is to remove harmful dews, and therefore one unties its binding in order to shake well. According to this one may say that one binding remains, and there is a mitzvah to bind it because of 'This is my God and I will beautify Him'; only the two additional knots are untied. See siman 651 and Taz, letter 1. See Eliyah Rabbah, letters 1, 2, and 3. As for what he wrote there, that they decreed a fast because they said Pesukei DeZimrah and put on tallis and tefillin while it was still night: Pesukei DeZimrah is somewhat difficult; and the passage of Vayedaber without Yehi Ratzon is also like reading Torah.
