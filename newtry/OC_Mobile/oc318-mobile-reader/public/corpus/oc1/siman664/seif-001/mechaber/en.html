The order of Hoshana Rabbah. It contains 11 seifim: On the seventh day, which is Hoshana Rabbah, the custom is to increase the recitation of psalms as on Yom Tov. {Rama: We do not recite Nishmat, but we do recite Mizmor LeTodah, Ein Kamocha, and Shema Yisrael as on Yom Tov. The Kaddish after Mussaf is recited in the Yom Tov melody. The custom is not to perform weekday melacha until after leaving the synagogue. When there is a circumcision on Hoshana Rabbah, the piyyut Zechor Berit is recited, before Ana Azon Chen. The early authorities wrote that there is an omen in the shadow of the moon on the night of Hoshana Rabbah regarding what may happen to a person or his relatives during that year. Some write that one should not examine this carefully, so that his fortune should not be impaired, and also because many do not understand the matter properly. It is better to be wholehearted and not investigate the future; so it appears to me.} We increase the candles somewhat, as on Yom Kippur. {Rama: Those who are meticulous customarily immerse themselves before dawn, as on the eve of Yom Kippur [Minhagim], and some customarily wear a kittel as on Yom Kippur.} This is because on the Festival judgment is rendered concerning water. The custom is to loosen the binding of the lulav. We circle seven times and recite many supplications concerning water.
