(Seif katan 1) And we take, etc. That one should not bind it with the lulav. This means that one should not wrap lulav leaves around the aravah branches in order to tie the aravah branches with them, because of interposition. Specifically the lulav with the hadas and aravah are wrapped with lulav leaves, for a species with its own species does not interpose. Each one of the three species is considered like one species, and it is not an interposition, as above in siman 651 and in Magen Avraham there, seif katan 2. But a lulav with branches of hoshanah is one species with a different species, and it interposes, for the lulav has no connection with the mitzvah of hoshanah.
For anything for its beautification, etc. This is according to their reasoning. But even without this, the Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 7 that we are not concerned for an interposition; see there.
