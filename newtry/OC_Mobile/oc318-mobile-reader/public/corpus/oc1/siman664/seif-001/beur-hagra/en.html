On the seventh day. This is because of the water judgment associated with the festival. See Taz, seif katan 1, and Tur. Rosh Hashanah teaches, “Pour water before Me,” and we follow the first Tanna that the water libation lasts seven days. Therefore Hoshana Rabbah is treated in some respects like Yom Kippur and in some respects like Yom Tov. The Mishnah in Sukkah, chapter 4, describes the exceptional rejoicing, including the children’s lulavim. All the days of judgment are also festivals, as taught regarding the four periods of judgment during the year, and as stated in the Midrash and Yerushalmi: what nation is like this nation, which knows the character of its God.

Nishmat is not recited. See Rashbam, Pesachim 102b, at the end of the page, beginning “And Shmuel said,” and the continuation there.

Mizmor LeTodah is recited. As stated in Beitzah 19b.

And they recite. As explained above.

They wrote. At that time the judgment of the person is completed together with the judgment concerning water, for water gives life to the world. The Sages said that ten things are called living, and Scripture states, “The shadows have fled,” and “Their protective shade has departed from them.”

In the moonlight. The moon is appointed over the waters, which increase and diminish with its waning and waxing.

Some maintain. As stated in the final chapter of Horayot and the first chapter of Keritot.

And more. As stated in Arvei Pesachim; see there.

Some have the custom. See Magen Avraham concerning the custom in our country, and the aforementioned Yerushalmi that they wear white garments.

They increase their supplications. As explained above.
