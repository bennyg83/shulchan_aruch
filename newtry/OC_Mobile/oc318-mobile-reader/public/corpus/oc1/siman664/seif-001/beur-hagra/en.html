"On the seventh day," etc. As explained afterward, because on the festival judgment concerns water. See Taz, seif katan 1, and so too Tur. As stated in the first chapter of Rosh Hashanah, "Pour water before Me," etc., and we rule like the first Tanna that the water libation is performed only for 7 days. Therefore the seventh day is treated in some respects like Yom Kippur, and in some respects like Yom Tov, as stated in the Mishnah, chapter 4 of Sukkah, where they greatly increased the rejoicing, including the children's lulavim. All the days of judgment are also Yom Tov, namely the 4 periods of judgment during the year. So too in the Midrash and Yerushalmi, first chapter of Rosh Hashanah: "What nation is like this nation, which knows the nature of its God," etc. "And Nishmat is not recited." See Rashbam, Perek Arvei Pesachim, 102b, end of the page, s.v. "And Shmuel said," and the continuation there. "And they recite Mizmor LeTodah." As stated in the second chapter of Beitzah, 19b. "And they say," etc. As explained above. "They wrote," etc. For then the person's judgment is completed together with the judgment concerning water, because water gives life. The Sages said that 10 things are called "living," etc., and Scripture states, "The shadows flee," and, "Their shade has departed from them." "In the moonlight." Because the moon is appointed over the waters, and the waters increase and diminish with its waning and waxing. "And some hold," etc. As stated in the last chapter of Horayot and the first chapter of Keritot. "And more," etc. As stated in Perek Arvei Pesachim; see there. "And some have the custom," etc. See Magen Avraham concerning the custom in our country, and as stated in the Yerushalmi cited above, that they wear white garments. "And they increase supplications," etc. As explained above.
