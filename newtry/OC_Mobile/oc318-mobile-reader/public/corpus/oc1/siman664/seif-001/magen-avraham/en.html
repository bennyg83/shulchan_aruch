Jews have the custom to remain awake on the night of the aravah [Shibolei HaLeket], and the order has already been printed; see what I wrote in siman 494. And we say 'Na'aritzcha kevodo,' etc. Weekday labor. Some remove even the pouch [Levush]. Now, in some places they have the custom to collect esrog money in the synagogue, but this is not proper.
