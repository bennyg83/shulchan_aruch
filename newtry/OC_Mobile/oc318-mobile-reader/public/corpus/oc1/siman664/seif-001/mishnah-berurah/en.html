(1) Rabbah - Jews have the custom to remain awake on the night of the aravah. The order has already been printed.
(2) As on Yom Tov - Because on the festival we are judged concerning water, and this is the completion of the sealing. We say Lamnatzeiach, Mizmor Hashamayim Mesaprim, and other psalms, and the Kedushah of Mussaf, Na'aritzcha, as on Yom Tov.
(3) And we do not say Nishmas, etc. - Rather, Yishtabach, for in truth it is a weekday.
(4) As on Yom Tov - Meaning, with the Yom Tov melody.
(5) Weekday - Some remove from themselves even the money pouch. Regarding the collection of esrog money, this is because most of the congregation gathers on Hoshana Rabbah, and there is benefit for charity from this [Peri Megadim].
(6) Before Ana azon chen - It is better to say it before the stanza Habet labris [Acharonim].
(7) As on Yom Kippur - Because on the festival we are judged concerning water, and all human life depends on water, and everything follows the sealing.
(8) Before dawn - It is better to immerse from the evening, in order to study the whole night in sanctity. Regarding marital relations, it is like the night of Shavuos. On the night of immersion he should have relations; see what we wrote above in siman 240, Mishnah Berurah seif katan 7, and in the Biur Halacha there.
(9) The kittel, as on Yom Kippur - For then is the completion of the sealing. In our country the custom is to wear Shabbos clothing; only the shaliach tzibbur wears the kittel.
(10) And we circle seven times - As in the Mikdash, where they would circle the altar on this day seven times.
