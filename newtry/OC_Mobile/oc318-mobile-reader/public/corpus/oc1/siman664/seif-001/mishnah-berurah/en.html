(1) Rabbah. The Jewish people customarily remain awake on the night of Hoshana Rabbah. The order of study has already been printed.
(2) As on Yom Tov. Since on Sukkos the world is judged concerning water, and this is the completion of the sealing, Lamnatzeach, the chapter of Tehillim beginning Hashamayim Mesaprim, and the other Tehillim are recited. In the Kedushah of Mussaf, Na'aritzcha is recited as on Yom Tov.
(3) Nishmas, etc., is not recited. Rather, one begins with Yishtabach, because in truth it is a weekday.
(4) As on Yom Tov. Meaning, using the melody of Yom Tov.
(5) Weekday clothing. Some even remove their money pouch. Money for esrogim is collected on Hoshana Rabbah because most of the congregation gathers then, and this produces a benefit for charity, as stated by Peri Megadim.
(6) Before Ana Azon Chen. It is even better to recite it before the stanza Habet Labris, as stated by the acharonim.
(7) As on Yom Kippur. Since on Sukkos the world is judged concerning water, all human life depends upon water, and everything follows the final sealing.
(8) Before dawn. It is preferable to immerse before evening so that one may study the entire night in holiness. Regarding marital relations, the law is the same as on the night of Shavuos. On the night when one's wife immerses, he should have marital relations with her. See what we wrote above in siman 240, Mishna Berurah, se'if katan 7, and in Biur Halacha there.
(9) The kittel, as on Yom Kippur. This is because the final sealing is then completed. In our countries the custom is to wear Shabbos clothing, while only the shaliach tzibbur wears a kittel.
(10) They circle seven times. This corresponds to the Beis HaMikdash, where they circled the altar seven times on this day. See reference 260.
