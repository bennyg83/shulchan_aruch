It is customary to increase the recitation of chapters of Tehillim, etc. The custom of the pious members of Beit El, may Hashem establish their community, who follow the teachings of the Arizal, is not to add anything whatsoever to the order of prayer beyond that of the other days of Chol HaMoed. The only change is in reciting the Hoshanos during the circuits. After completing the circuits and reciting the Hoshanos, they recite Kaddish Tiskabel, put down the lulav, and perform the beating with the aravah, as will be written below in the name of Shaar HaKavanos. Afterward, they read from the Torah and pray Mussaf as on every other day. This is likewise the custom of the holy congregation of Frankfurt: they make no change whatsoever, except that Adon Olam and the final Kaddish are recited to a melody, as Mateh Moshe wrote in siman 960; see there.
