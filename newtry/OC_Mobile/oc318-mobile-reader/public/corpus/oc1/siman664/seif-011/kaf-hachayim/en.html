They practice a lot of psalms, etc., and the custom of Beit El Yakbatz Chassidim who follow the words of the late Ari that they do not add anything in the order of prayer from the rest of the days of the Holy Week, only by saying Hoshanat in the order of the circlings, and after paying the circlings and saying the circlings, say Kaddish to be accepted, and place the Lulav, and make a beat on the Arabah about a month after that in the name of Shaar HaKov', and then recite the Torah and pray a supplement every day.
