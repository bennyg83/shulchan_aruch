[10] [Levush] And the custom is, etc. In our country I have not seen the custom that one who has no lulav circles. If it is speaking where there is no lulav at all in the community, as written in siman 660, it should not have used the language 'even one who has none,' etc., and 'one who has,' etc. Also, the expression 'the custom is' does not fit this, for it is an uncommon matter. This requires investigation. Nevertheless, as a matter of law, it certainly appears that one should circle with the aravah alone, as will be explained.
