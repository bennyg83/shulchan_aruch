It appears to me. That after, etc. See the Taz. His opinion, of blessed memory, is that one should hold the lulav and the hoshanah until Kol Mevaser; then he removes the lulav and holds only the hoshanah. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 21, and Magen Avraham, letter 8. In the siddurim it is recorded in the name of Maharash in Taaneh Emunim that one should place the lulav in his hand and take the aravah; see there.
