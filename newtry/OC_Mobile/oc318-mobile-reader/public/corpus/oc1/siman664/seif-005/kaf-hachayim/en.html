(ה) And he wrote in Pnei Aryeh that the third seal made on Hoshana Rabbah night is the outer seal, made from the illumination of the inner seal of yesod, and it is lesser. The seal of yesod is from the three Ehyeh in their three milui with yud, alef, hei — gematria chotam; but now it is not a complete seal, for their secret is from the three milui of the three Ehyeh totaling 592, which with their kollel is 595, gematria Mishna of the King, for the main seal is in yesod of malchut (see there). Therefore before reading Devarim one says a yichud that he comes to read tonight Sefer Devarim, Mishneh Torah, to draw malchut of malchut, the outer seal, etc. Vihi noam.
