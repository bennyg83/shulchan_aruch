[5] To immerse at the morning watch, etc. It is proper to immerse on the eve of Hoshana Rabbah, in order to study the whole night in purity (Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah and Shelah). It is not proper for a person to have marital relations on this night, for it is the night of the sealing (Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah); see siman 494. The Shibolei HaLeket wrote: some have the custom to remain awake all night and read from Bereishis until Vezos HaBerachah, because on the following day they must complete the Torah. End quote. The custom has already become to study the tikkun.
