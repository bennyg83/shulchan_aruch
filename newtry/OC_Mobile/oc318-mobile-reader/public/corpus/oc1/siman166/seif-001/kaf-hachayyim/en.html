1) [Seif 1] Some say that one need not take care, etc., and some say that one must take care, etc. The reason the Shulchan Aruch says that some do not require care and some do is that Berachos 42a states, 'Immediately after netilas yadayim comes the blessing.' Rashi and Tosafos there explain that this refers to mayim acharonim; see there. The Rambam in chapter 6 of Hilchos Berachos and Rabbeinu Yoel explain it this way as well, and the Tur and Beit Yosef cite them. The Yerushalmi, Berachos chapter 6, however, implies that the statement, 'Immediately after washing comes the blessing,' refers to the first washing, for it states there that one who joins the blessing directly to netilas yadayim will not be harmed throughout that meal. The Tur writes the same, as do Hagahos Maimoniyos in chapter 7 of Hilchos Berachos. The Beit Yosef cites them and writes that it is proper to take care with the first washing as well. These are his words here in the Shulchan Aruch. This is also the view of the Rosh, the Tur, R' Yeshayah the First in his handwritten rulings, and Rama MiPano in Alfasi Zuta, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin, as Birkei Yosef writes in Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 1; see there. So rule the Levush, Pri Chadash, Erech HaShulchan, and Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1. See below, paragraph 6.
