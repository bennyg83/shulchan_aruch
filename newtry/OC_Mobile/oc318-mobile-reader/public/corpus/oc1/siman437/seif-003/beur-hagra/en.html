(א) Seif 3 — And some say he must. For in chapter 2 of Bava Metzia we say: one view — a person is pleased to have a mitzva performed, etc.; therefore it must be specifically in a matter without monetary loss, as written siman 14 regarding the distinction between tefillin and books; but from what Magen Avraham wrote in Hagah, since he stipulated explicitly, this is not the matter, for here one cannot say he is pleased since he speaks explicitly and stipulates — certainly he must pay; and the above difficulty Tosafot and Mordechai and HaMagid in chapter 3 of laws of chametz already resolved — specifically with his consent he is pleased, unlike without his consent; see in the Gemara of Bekhorot in the chapter on animal tithe, see there. And see Magen Avraham that what he wrote since he stipulated is the beginning of his words, but he concludes there and Abaye's conclusion is pleasing, etc., since there is a doubt he will collect from him — if not so we do not say for this reason — it implies even when unstipulated; and he means with this the commentators' difficulty from chapter 2 of Bava Metzia that we do not say pleasing, etc., as explained; but Ran disagrees with all this and holds he is not liable to pay, see there — and this is the view of Shulchan Aruch which ruled unstipulated.

(ב) Since he stipulated, etc. His reasoning: it must discuss such a case, for otherwise there would be no reasoning for the Gemara of mistaken purchase since it is not in the body of the purchase, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 232 s.k. 5 and Magen Avraham; and thus the beginning of Magen Avraham's words — it was not asked whether he is liable, etc., see there.
