(א) If, etc. Behold in Eshel Avraham and Peri Megadim and Sha'arei De'ah we wrote that wherever easy clarification exists, even for d'rabbanan, even with majority and presumption lenient, and through effort we rely on presumption even for d'oraisa; and Peri Chadash here — and this is Ran's view, proved from what he challenged we should ask him — for if presumption of checked sufficed, why ask? And what is written s.v. they said it was not checked, etc. — how is one witness believed against presumption and majority? See Yoreh Deah 127 unresolved question; and Ran Nachman bar Yitzchak meant they are trusted — see Pri Megadim there siman 1.
