3) Nevertheless, if a person entrusted the key to the house to someone else, even according to Tosafos the custodian is not obligated to search it. Their ruling applies only when the other person wishes to take possession of and acquire the house, as explained in Tosafos there and cited by Beis Yosef and the later authorities. It appears that if one entrusted the entire house to another person, that person must search, since both the key and complete responsibility for guarding the house were transferred to that person. Chukas HaPesach, subsection 2.
