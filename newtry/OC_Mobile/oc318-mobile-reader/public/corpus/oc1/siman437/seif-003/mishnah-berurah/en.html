(א) With the presumption of checked — meaning on the 14th, for presumably it is presumed checked, as above; and the same when the landlord told him explicitly it is checked.
(ב) On the tenant to check and it is not mistaken purchase — for although certainly on the day of the 14th the obligation is on the landlord to check, nevertheless if it is not before us the obligation falls on the tenant to check, and we do not say it is mistaken purchase, for he rented it with the presumption of checked because a person is pleased to have a mitzva performed with his money.
(ג) For he is performing a mitzva — and per this view the landlord need not return the money he spent for checking, for he did not benefit the landlord at all — it is a mitzva alone imposed on him and there is no monetary obligation on him at all (Ran).
(ד) And some say he must return, etc. — meaning in a place where they check for pay and he spent money on this; and even per this view it is not mistaken purchase even when he stipulated explicitly it would be checked and it is not checked — rather they hold that even if his intent was that even if it were not checked the purchase would not be voided, nevertheless since he stipulated explicitly he must pay.
(ה) Since he stipulated, etc. — but when unstipulated, even if he rented on the 14th with presumption of checked, all agree he need not pay him. And for the halacha Acharonim ruled like the first view that in all cases he need not pay, for he can say to him: I hold like this view, and the one who extracts from his fellow bears the burden of proof.
