(א) And it is not mistaken purchase — meaning certainly the mitzva is imposed on the landlord; but if the landlord said I do not want a mitzva performed, or he went on his way, it is not mistaken purchase, for presumably a person is pleased to have a mitzva performed whether in his person or in his property; and that which the Gemara asks he should return — perhaps because of something else he does not want it.

(ב) He must return — for they hold that which is pleasing to perform a mitzva with one's property applies only to something without monetary loss, such as to bless on his tallit; but with monetary loss, no — as written siman 14; see there; and requires study, for in Yoreh Deah siman 291 regarding mezuzah Rama ruled unstipulated and did not write he must return.

(ג) Since he stipulated explicitly, etc. — and it is difficult, for in Choshen Mishpat siman 232 s.k. 5 he ruled that any defect not in the body of the purchase they deduct from the price and the purchase stands — if so why does the Gemara need the reasoning of pleasing to perform a mitzva? For in every matter the law is so; and one may say there it discusses without stipulation and here even with stipulation.
