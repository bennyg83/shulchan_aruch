The landlord rents a house to his friend with the presumption of it being inspected, and if it is found to be uninspected, it is the responsibility of the tenant to inspect, and the transaction cannot be retracted. This applies even in a place where it is customary to hire someone else to check, as it is a mitzvah to fulfill.Rema: Some say that the landlord must return the inspection fee to the tenant since it was explicitly stipulated that it would be inspected (HaMagid, Perek 2, and see above Siman 14).):
