(s.k. 1) And he acquired it, etc. — per Ran's view. To exclude Tosafot's view that even if he has not yet acquired, since his intent is to acquire the house therefore if the key is in the tenant's hand the tenant must check. Bach was stringent like Tosafot, and so Kaf HaChayyim agreed with him; see Peri Chadash and Maharsha in siman 431.
