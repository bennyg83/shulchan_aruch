(א) And he acquired it — meaning through money, document, chazaka, and kinyan sudar; but when he has not yet acquired, even if the key is in the tenant's hand, he need not check, for it is like one who deposited his key with another — the depositary need not check (as Darkei Moshe per Ran and Magen Avraham).

(ב) On the landlord to check — since the chametz is his and the house is still held back from the tenant.

(ג) On the tenant to check — and he annuls as well, even though the chametz belongs to the landlord; nevertheless presumably he declared it ownerless when it left him, as Tur wrote siman 436; and nevertheless the landlord annuls explicitly, and so Tosafot.
