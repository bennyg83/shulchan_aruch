(א) And he acquired it. But when he has not yet acquired, even if the key is in the tenant's hand he need not check, for it is like one who deposited his key with another — the depositary need not check. Magen Avraham, Kaf HaChayyim.

(ב) Before he handed him the key. {Rama: (And handing the key alone does not help at all, for it is as if he handed it to guard — Darkei Moshe in the name of Ran.)} And even if he handed the key to the tenant and returned and deposited it with the landlord, the landlord must check since the chametz is his and the key is in his hand; but when deposited with another there is no obligation of checking for all, and a house that is not locked — if the tenant already acquired it, he must eliminate. Kaf HaChayyim.

(ג) On the landlord to check. And it is possible per the view of some in seif 3 that in a place where they give pay for checking he must pay the landlord the fee for checking since the tenant acquired and took possession. Kaf HaChayyim.

(ד) On the tenant to check. He also annuls, even though the chametz is not his but the landlord's; nevertheless presumably the landlord declared it ownerless and the tenant who acquires it must annul; and nevertheless the landlord annuls as well — Magen Avraham in the name of Tosafot; and Kaf HaChayyim wrote on this he did not see any meaning in Tosafot there.
