{Rama: Magen Avraham s.k. 5} they are not trusted even for d'rabbanan — Tosafot's words there regarding a minor; but slave and woman one may say here they are trusted. And what Magen Avraham wrote afterward in Pesachim they wrote what is in their hands they are trusted even for d'oraisa, etc. — this is only women and slaves but not minors, as Tosafot.
