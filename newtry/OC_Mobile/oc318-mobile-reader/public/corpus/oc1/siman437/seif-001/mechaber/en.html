One who rents a house to his fellow — upon whom does the obligation of elimination fall. Contains 4 seifim.
When one rents a house to his fellow for the need of the 14th and from then onward, and the tenant acquired it through one of the ways land rental is acquired — if before he handed him the key, the 14th falls on the landlord to check; if from when he hands him the key, the 14th falls on the tenant to check.
