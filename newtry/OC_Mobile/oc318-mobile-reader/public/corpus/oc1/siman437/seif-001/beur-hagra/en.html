(א) Seif 1 — For the need of the 14th, etc. Ran explained that the Gemara there says one who rents a house to his fellow on the 14th — and one should not say the 14th literally, for it says if until he did not, etc., and if from when he hands, etc.; see Bach.

(ב) And he acquired it through one of the ways, etc. Ran — nevertheless handing the key does not acquire, as written in chapter 8 of Bava Kamma, except in such a case; see Magen Avraham s.k. 2; and so Ran there; therefore when he acquired through ways of kinyan it speaks — from what is explained from mezuzah, and there the tenant had already acquired.
