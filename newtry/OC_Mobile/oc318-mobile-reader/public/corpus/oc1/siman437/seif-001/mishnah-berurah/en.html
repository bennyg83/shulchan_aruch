(א) For the need of the 14th — meaning he rented it to him on the 13th for the need of the 14th.

(ב) And he acquired it — meaning through money or document or chazaka or kinyan sudar; but when he has not yet acquired it through any kinyan, even if the key is in the tenant's hand he need not check, for it is like one who deposited his key with another — the depositary need not check; rather the obligation of checking is on the landlord alone.

(ג) If until, etc., the 14th falls — meaning that at the beginning of the night of the 14th, which is the time of the obligation of checking, the house was locked and the key was in the landlord's hand, the obligation of checking falls on him since the chametz is his and the house is still held back from the tenant; even if the tenant entered to dwell there within the night.

(ד) On the landlord to check — and if the house has no lock, the tenant must check since he acquired it through one of the ways of kinyan and it is open before him to enter (Acharonim; see Peri Megadim).

(ה) And if from when he hands, etc. — meaning he handed it to him on the 13th; and if the tenant returned and deposited it with the landlord, the landlord must check since in any case at the beginning of the night of the 14th it was in his hand; and Peri Chadash wrote the tenant must check since the landlord already handed him the key and it is in his power to return and take it from the landlord whenever he wishes.

(ו) On the tenant to check — even if his intent is not to enter to dwell there until after Pesach; and Acharonim wrote he must also annul, for the landlord declared his chametz ownerless when it left the house and the tenant acquired it; nevertheless the landlord annuls explicitly as well.
