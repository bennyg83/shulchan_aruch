One who rents a house, etc. And some say it discusses even when he has not yet acquired and did not acquire through one of the ways land is acquired (Tosafot and Tur); however if a person deposits the key of his house with another, the depositary is not obligated to check — specifically when he wants to acquire his house and acquire it (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran); but if the tenant returned the key to the landlord — requires study for the law; certainly if he returned on the 13th it is clear the landlord must check since at the beginning of the night of the 14th, time of checking obligation, the key was in his hand; but if he returned on the 14th there is room to doubt; but my inclination is the landlord must check since the chametz is his and the key is in his hand (so it appears to me from responsum Mishpetei Shmuel).
