One who rents a house to his fellow. In the Gemara 4a it is brought: Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak — one who rents a house to his fellow on the 14th — upon whom to check? On the landlord to check, for his chametz is stricter; or perhaps on the tenant to check, for the prohibition applies in his domain? Learn from this: one who rents a house to his fellow — on the tenant to place a mezuzah. Just as there, although the house is the landlord's, the tenant places the mezuzah because he dwells there — so here, although the chametz is the landlord's, the tenant checks because the prohibition applies in his domain.
