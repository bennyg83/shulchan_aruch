(א) One who rents, etc. Behold at first glance there are three views: (1) Rashi implies like Ran — two are required: he acquired and the key is in the tenant's hand, then the tenant checks; therefore the baraita does not teach one who rents on the 14th but rented on the 13th and acquired, for handing the key when he told him explicitly he will acquire in it acquires. (2) Tosafot — even without acquisition, since his intent is to acquire, if the key is in the tenant's hand the tenant checks. (3) Taz and Bach — one who rents on the 14th, the Gemara's question is the kinyan, and we are not concerned when division occurred, and the question is because the essence of checking was immediately at twilight in the landlord's domain.

(ב) And now let us explain Taz's words. And as it appears this is Bach's view seif 1: one who rents on the 14th — the Gemara's question is the kinyan; and in precision the 14th is mentioned and division of money we do not care when it occurred; and it is the question because the essence of checking was immediately at twilight in the landlord's domain.

(ג) And regarding if the key was in the tenant's hand and he deposited with the landlord — see Nekudat HaKesef, Kaf HaChayyim 3; even if he deposited on the 14th, on the landlord to check; Peri Chadash (1) and it appears like his words to Ran when he acquired — one may say whenever hour 13 was in the tenant's hand the obligation is on him to return and take from the landlord immediately.
