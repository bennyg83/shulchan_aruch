(א) House, etc. — refers to above, that he rented after the day of the 14th had already entered when the obligation is on the landlord to check, only we know he did not check — such as he went on his way and is not in town — and one of the household said, such as slave or woman or minor, that they checked it; they are trusted and the tenant need not check again.

(ב) That was presumed he did not check — and even if there was certainly chametz there, those who said they checked and eliminated it are also trusted.

(ג) They are trusted — and although b'dieved if they said they checked one need not return to check, nevertheless l'chatchila it is not proper to command them to check, for checking involves great effort and there is concern they may be lazy.

(ד) Sense — meaning he reached the age of education.
