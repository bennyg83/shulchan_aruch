They are trusted — since bedikat chametz is d'rabbanan; and see siman 436 that l'chatchila one should not rely on women; and so Maharil and Tosafot in Eruvin wrote they are trusted in their own house but one who sends them to do a matter — they are not trusted even for d'rabbanan, as written siman 109 s.k. 8; and in Pesachim they wrote regarding what is in their hands they are trusted even for d'oraisa.
