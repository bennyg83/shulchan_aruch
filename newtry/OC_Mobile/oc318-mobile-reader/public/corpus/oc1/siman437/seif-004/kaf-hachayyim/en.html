4) If the tenant acquired the house on the thirteenth through one of the modes by which a rental of land is acquired, but the key is still in the landlord's possession, Bach writes that according to Tosafos the tenant is obligated to search. Chok Yosef writes likewise there. Peri Chadash, however, writes that even according to Tosafos the landlord is obligated, since the key remains in the landlord's possession. See there. Chok Yaakov writes likewise there.
