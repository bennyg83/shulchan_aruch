(א) (s.k. 4) He asks, etc. — as written in Yoreh Deah siman 1: although we hold the majority of frequent cases are with expert slaughterers, nevertheless if the slaughterer is before us and we do not know if he is expert we examine him and do not rely on the majority; and a majority is preferable to presumption — all the more so we do not rely on presumption where clarification is possible.

(ב) Except for lung lesions — all eighteen treifot.

(ג) And we rely on presumption — it should not read so; rather it means majority. For there is no presumption there, only a majority of animals are kosher; per one view within its year it is possible a treifa was born, for there is no presumption — even after twelve months presumably it was born kosher, for a treifa does not live twelve months; nevertheless per one view there is presumption since it was never presumed treifa.

(ד) As explained in the words of Rabbeinu Yonah — not like Bach. Meaning Rabbeinu Yonah's text reads: an animal is different, for from birth it is presumed kosher; but a house all year is presumed not checked — end; and Bach understood his intent that here regarding chametz the presumption is weak since all year it is presumed not checked.
