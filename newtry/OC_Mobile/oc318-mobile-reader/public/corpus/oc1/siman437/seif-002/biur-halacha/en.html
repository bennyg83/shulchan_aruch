(א) He asks if they checked — and it appears at first glance that here when he tells him they checked he need not annul again, since we hold the checker must annul and presumably he did so; and one may deduce this from the Mechaber's language that he wrote the question is if they checked and not if they annulled; but in truth this is not compelled, for l'chatchila he must ask about this lest he not fulfill properly, similar to needing to ask about checking even though presumption is checked by law; nevertheless b'dieved if they did not ask about annulment and he is no longer before us to ask, it appears he may rely and say presumably they annulled; and likewise since he checked, annulment is d'rabbanan as Ran wrote at the beginning of the chapter, and one may rely to be lenient; therefore the Mechaber did not mention the question about annulment because it is not so much of a delay, as explained.

(ב) And he annuls, etc. — for in the Gemara it is an unresolved question not resolved regarding checking; therefore Rosh wrote he annuls in his heart so he fulfills his d'oraisa obligation, for then checking is only d'rabbanan and we rely to be lenient in a doubt; see Peri Megadim who doubts regarding annulment; indeed this is also found in the novellae of Maharam Chalavah and Rabbeinu Manoach.

(ג) In his heart — behold it is known there are views whether annulment suffices in the heart or specifically with the mouth; see Biur HaGra who agreed to the halacha as the Mechaber's language that in the heart suffices.
