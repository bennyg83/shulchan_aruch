(א) The tenant rents a house from his fellow on the 14th — meaning on the day of the 14th or within the night of the 14th after the time when the landlord could have checked his house at that time has already passed.
(ב) If he is in town — and the same if his wife or household members are in town, he may ask them, for they are trusted even against the presumption as stated in seif 4.
(ג) He asks if they checked — for although the way of the scrupulous is to check at the beginning of the night of the 14th per the enactment of the Sages, and every Israelite is presumed scrupulous and presumably already checked — nevertheless he must ask, for all year the house was presumed to contain chametz, and with this presumption that presumably they checked you come to remove it from the presumption of chametz; therefore one must ask.
(ד) And he annuls in his heart — for although the chametz is not his, presumably the landlord declared it ownerless when it left him and the tenant acquired it, as written above.
