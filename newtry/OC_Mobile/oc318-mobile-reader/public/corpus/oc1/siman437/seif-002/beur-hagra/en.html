(א) Seif 2 — If he is in town, etc. Since the Gemara there says per the one who says we ask him — and the practical difference is not that one need not ask, rather that if he can ask, even though his presumption is checked he must ask; and so we say in the first chapter of Chullin: all according to Ravina do not say, etc., majority of frequent cases, etc., for that language, etc., for an ignoramus we are not concerned — and they deduced from what we say regarding experts majority of frequent cases, etc., and for an ignoramus they said we are not concerned — learn that there one must ask if they exist; and so all Geonim, Rambam, and Shulchan Aruch; and so Tosafot in Pesachim there s.v. lo. And one may wonder what is the reasoning of presumption of checked — why need their statement? And one may say since he is in town he must ask, and therefore their statement helps, etc., see there.

(ב) And if not, etc. Since he annuls it, it is d'rabbanan; and as written 4:2:10:1 and a doubt in d'rabbanan is lenient.

(ג) And he annuls in his heart — in the heart suffices, as written in the Gemara 7:1, 8:2, 11:2, 12:2, 49:1; and so Rambam and Shulchan Aruch here; and so Rashi 4:2:8:1; and learn, and in siman 438 seif 2, siman 433 s.k. 7, siman 442 s.k. 8 — and learn not like Ran in the name of Yesh Ma'aminim that in the heart is not sufficient but he must express it with his lips — and it is not so.
