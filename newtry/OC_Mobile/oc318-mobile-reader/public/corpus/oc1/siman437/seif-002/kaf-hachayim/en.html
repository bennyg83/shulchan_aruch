And he acquired through one of the ways, etc. — meaning through money or document or chazaka or kinyan sudar (meaning, or through money or document, etc., as explained in Choshen Mishpat simanim 195, 191, 192, see there); but when he has not yet acquired, even if the key is in the tenant's hand, he need not check, for it is like one who deposited his key with another.
