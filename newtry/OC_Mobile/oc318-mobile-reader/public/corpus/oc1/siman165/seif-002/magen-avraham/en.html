And not to speak. This requires study. The rule that a blessing must immediately follow washing of the hands refers, according to all opinions, to mayim acharonim. Nevertheless, one may speak between mayim acharonim and Bircas HaMazon, for it is forbidden to speak only once he has taken the cup to recite Bircas HaMazon, as stated in siman 183, seif 6. Kesef Mishneh writes likewise at the end of chapter 6. The rule that one may not speak between redemption and prayer is different, because there one stands before the King and may not interrupt with other matters. Here, however, perhaps the statement that one should not speak means only that he should not discuss some other occupation or matter, which would constitute an interruption. Mere conversation of two or three words is not an interruption. Certainly, if someone asks him a question, he may answer yes or no. See the end of siman 166 and what I wrote in siman 183, seif 6.
