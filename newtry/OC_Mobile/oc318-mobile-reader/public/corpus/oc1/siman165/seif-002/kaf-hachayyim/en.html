2) If immediately after washing and rubbing his hands he recited the blessing over netilas yadayim and forgot to recite Asher Yatzar before it, it appears to me that he should recite HaMotzi, eat an olive's volume, and then recite Asher Yatzar, so as not to interrupt between the blessing over netilas yadayim and HaMotzi. He must take care not to eat anything together with the bread until he recites Asher Yatzar, lest he become occupied with his meal and forget the blessing. See below, paragraphs 5 and 7.
