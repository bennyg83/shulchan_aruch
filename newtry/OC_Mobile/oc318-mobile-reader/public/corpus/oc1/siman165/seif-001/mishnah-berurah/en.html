(a) He who does his needs, wrote in the words of “Every man shall look upon himself to the first place and as such in the book of the day.”

(2) He shall wash twice, etc.—for if he washes once and blesses first Asher Yatzar and afterward on bread, there is an interruption between the netilah and its berachah; and if he blesses first on bread and afterward Asher Yatzar, there is an interruption between the berachah of netilah and hamotzi; and to omit blessing Asher Yatzar until after hamotzi is also not correct, since its obligation applies to him immediately when he did his needs, and not afterward; and the Acharonim wrote that one must be careful not to take on the first time a complete netilah, but only a little, on account of cleanliness—for otherwise his hands were already purified on the first time, and it is not relevant to bless again on a second netilah, as above in siman 158, seif 7. And know that there are several poskim who hold that Asher Yatzar is not an interruption between the berachah of netilah and hamotzi, since everything is for the need of purifying hands, and it is like gavila litura in siman 167; therefore, b'dieved, if he washed one netilah properly for netilah for eating, he blesses first on bread and afterward Asher Yatzar, for one may rely on these poskim; and the same applies l'chatchila if it was difficult for him to wash two netilot because of lack of water, etc.—he washes once and blesses on bread and afterward Asher Yatzar. Magen Avraham wrote that one who relieves himself in the middle of a meal—in any case washes only once and blesses on bread and afterward Asher Yatzar, since he need not bless hamotzi, as below in siman 178; if so, there is no interruption here; and see what we wrote in siman 164, seif 2, in Mishnah Berurah.

(3) (c) After pouring, etc. - Ral Daumer says a blessing that he made before it was time to bless NTI, that is, after he poured only once with his hands in less than a quarter and rubbed his hands together, the taking is still not finished, and then he pours a second time with his hands to purify the first water, then he blesses NTI and wipes.
