After—Taz, from the flow of his words here and in note (2) (in what he wrote that R' Yosef bar Yakar does not hold that one netilah counts for both)—it is clear that he holds there are three disputes in the matter: R' Yosef bar Yakar holds one netilah does not count for both, since one leaving the bathroom is obligated to wash his hands (as in siman 4), and on bread he is obligated—therefore it is not fitting that one netilah exempt both; Rambam of Rothenburg holds it counts, but because of interruption of berachot one needs two netilot; Rashi and R' Peretz hold it counts and there is also no interruption, as is clear from the Tur; and on this the Tur ruled: after he pours once, etc. This is not as Derishah wrote for R' Yosef bar Yakar—that the reason is also interruption—and the Tur's ruling need not separate them; see end of his words in note (2) from this.
