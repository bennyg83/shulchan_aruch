In Taz, s.k. 2: in the place where he dried his hands from the first water, etc.—meaning: if he dries his hands a little and returns and touches there a little of the first water, then the hands themselves return to impurity; if so, he needs the second pouring to purify the hands themselves so they not return to impurity. On this Taz wrote that when he pours a revi'it there is interruption; and even per what Taz wrote that the main view is like Rashal that even with a revi'it drying is required—see above, end of siman 158. Nevertheless, drying is not purification of the hands at all, but only on account of repulsiveness alone; unlike regarding less than a revi'it, where second water is effective also to purify the hands themselves so they not return and become impure through drying.
