[1] [Levush] And between HaMotzi, etc. Olas Tamid challenges Levush: in any event, he should first recite HaMotzi and afterward Asher Yatzar during the meal. See there. I say that Beit Yosef also implies as Levush does, and Bach and Nachalas Tzvi write likewise. It therefore appears to me that the matter can be resolved well. Certainly, Asher Yatzar should come first, because its obligation devolved upon him first, immediately when he relieved himself; only afterward did the obligation of HaMotzi for the meal devolve upon him. The reason Levush did not give this explanation regarding the blessing over washing is that Al Netilas Yadayim is not an interruption at all, since it pertains to the washing. Furthermore, many poskim hold above, siman 7, that whenever one relieves himself he also recites Al Netilas Yadayim, and this is also implied by Bach. See below. The explanation of Olas Tamid is not understood from the words of Beit Yosef. See there and examine carefully.
