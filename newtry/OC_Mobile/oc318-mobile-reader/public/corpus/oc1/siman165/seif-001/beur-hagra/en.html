(61) One who does so, etc. Rashi, who wrote in Sefer HaPardes that his teacher R' Yosef bar Yakar holds that one requires two netilot and blesses on the first Asher Yatzar and on the second on bread, and one netilah does not suffice because of the interruption of Asher Yatzar between netilah and the berachah on bread; but Rashi disagreed and wrote that one netilah counts for both, and similarly R' Peretz that it is not an interruption; and proof from Rav and R' Yirmiyah who said yekaneh—one counts for both—and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Mordechai and Gemara that Rambam agreed with Rashi, and wrote that he blesses first on bread and afterward Asher Yatzar; and even though immediately to netilah of a meal is not an interruption, since he came for purification of hands it is no worse than gavila litura, as is clear in responsa Rosh beginning kelal 4 in the questioner's words; see there. But the Tur wrote the opposite—that first Asher Yatzar and afterward on bread—and also wrote that Rambam of Rothenburg holds like R' Yaakov bar Yakar; and it is possible that although he holds like Rashi, nevertheless he practiced l'chatchila like R' Yosef bar Yakar; and the Tur wrote that so practiced Rosh, and the reason is the interruption between netilah and the berachah on bread as above; and the Tur ruled that after he pours once on his hand and rubs he blesses Asher Yatzar and completes the netilah, etc., and blesses on bread; and so in responsa Rosh there. This is what Shulchan Aruch means, one who does so, like R' Yosef bar Yakar, for such is the Tur's view. And if so, etc.
