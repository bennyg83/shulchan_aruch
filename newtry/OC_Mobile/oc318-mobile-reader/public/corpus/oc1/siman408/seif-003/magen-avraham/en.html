Nevertheless if his measure ended, etc. — so wrote D.M.: Rabbeinu Yonatan wrote if he placed eruv, etc., until but if his measure ended in half the city he may not carry except half the city within width of his four cubits — end; requires study what difference, for after filling corners he should be permitted to walk entire city area as within corner techum; one may say when city is four cubits they give him only techum width of the city — end of his intent: when ended at city end whole city is four cubits and he has from the sides two thousand in every direction; when ended in middle they also give from sides two thousand but within city forbidden except four-cubit width and rest of city width counts toward his thousands from here and here — per this his words here in Shulchan Aruch; I was astonished at the view, for Rabbeinu Yonatan wrote explicitly even when his measure ended at city end he may not exit outside city gate north or south at all, for his south and north toward where he placed eruv extends and he may not walk north and south of city at all, for north and south techum of his shevitah place do not reach north and south techum of city; and seemingly even westward he has only a thousand cubits in four-cubit width, not entire city width though he initially had permission — end; therefore we learn forbidden north and south entirely, for we do not count city for his techum measure, for he has from eruv to city a thousand length and four thousand and four cubits width, and likewise from city westward; whence shall we give his south and north — see there; and what is written when his measure ended in middle of city he may not walk more than his four cubits — meaning from his four cubits two thousand north and two thousand south, and if broader forbidden to walk; but when ended at city end, even as large as Antioch he walks all of it — even there he was in doubt.
