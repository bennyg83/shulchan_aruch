[3] [Levush] Less than a thousand, etc. — such is Shulchan Aruch and Rambam language; Gemara stated plainly and did not require less; so Tur; and Rambam's view is that one cubit must be outside the city because this measure of intermediate steps is transmitted to all and cannot be shortened; I have analogous proof from R' Yonatan end of ch. how they lead beyond (Levushei Yom Tov); in my humble opinion be lenient and say one may shorten — possibly Rambam did not mean precisely; since we find Tur permits explicitly we need not be stringent, especially as this is Gemara's implication; further I found in Rambam's Mishnah commentary this language: the measure reached the end of the town or beyond it — to here; plainly he holds like Tur. Further I saw in Raavan 70: if majority of city is swallowed in techum it is as all of it; though all Poskim imply we need all literally, nevertheless here one may rely.
