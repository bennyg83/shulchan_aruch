(27) Accomplished nothing — for without eruv he also has from the city two thousand in every direction, and the whole city is four cubits for him.

(28) We do not measure — and we do not measure, as it should read; it is one matter.

(29) That combine with the city — for the sake of the city (within seventy cubits and remainder, as siman 398); even if he placed it in the last cubit of the extension they do not give him the four cubits given others who place eruv toward the outside, as explained above: when placing within a partition or place considered four cubits he has nothing toward the outside, and he is like other city residents whose techum is measured immediately from the end of the extension onward (acharonim).
