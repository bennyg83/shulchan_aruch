(In Shulchan Aruch seif 1) And some say — even when his measure ended, even though he does not have so much width opposite the place, etc.; see Netiv Chayim, Mateh Yehudah, Ne'eman Shemuel who sought to prove from these Rama words; and there he raised that if in city width opposite part of width is a village and by measuring the village the techum is long, and in part where the village is not opposite the techum is short — we do not evaluate it so techum end is equal across entire city width; all the more if the village is not even opposite part of city width but from the side it does not affect techum measure and one measures techum two thousand in compression; see siman 399 seif 8 in Hagah regarding marking on mountains per Tosafot and Raosh, and the same here; possibly even Rashi there agrees — see there.
