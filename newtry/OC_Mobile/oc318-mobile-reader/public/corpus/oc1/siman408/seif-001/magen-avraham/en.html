Distant from the city, etc. Plainly not as Ohr Sameach who wrote he must place it within techum from his house's opening — that does not exist, for in ch. 6 Rambam wrote the reason in nitgalgel: outside techum is not eruv because he cannot reach his eruv; so Rashi ch. 3; and if within city techum he can take it even though outside his house's techum, as siman 398 as above — plain. And what Rambam requires placing within two thousand of his house: if placed beyond two thousand of his house he is forbidden to return home — he must sleep in other city houses within his eruv techum.
