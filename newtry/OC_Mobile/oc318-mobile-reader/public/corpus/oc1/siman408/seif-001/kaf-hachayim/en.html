(1) [Seif 1] Hagah — permitted to make eruv, etc. Because Shulchan Aruch language implies b'dieved, therefore Hagah added permitted to make eruv to say even l'chatchila it is permitted; and this is specifically for a mitzvah matter permitted even l'chatchila, but for discretionary matter only b'dieved, as explained below siman 415 — see there.
