1) [Se'if 1] Gloss: It is permitted to establish an eruv, etc. The wording of the Shulchan Aruch implies only after the fact. The gloss therefore adds, 'It is permitted to establish an eruv,' to teach that it is permitted even initially. This initial permission applies specifically for a mitzvah purpose. For an optional purpose it is effective only after the fact, as explained below in siman 416. See there.
