[1] [Levush] And because techumin are d'rabbanan, etc. So Tur wrote; per this, for one who says techumin are d'oraisa eruv techumin does not help — requires study in Eruvin 17: they taught camp members are exempt from eruv only eruv chatzerot, but eruv techumin they are liable, as R' Chiya taught: one is flogged for eruv techumin d'oraisa — so for one who says d'oraisa eruv techumin does not help. One may say Tur refers to eruv with bread, but with his feet all agree it helps — so Levush's implication; further one may say even R' Chiya holds techumin of two thousand cubits are d'rabbanan — only regarding twelve mil he says d'oraisa, therefore they were stringent since he has d'oraisa root. However Me'or and Milchamot RITZ explained plainly on the two thousand literally — requires study; further difficulty from Eruvin 35: R' Meir says doubtful eruv — this is more stringent than a camel because he holds techumin d'oraisa and safek d'oraisa lechumra — why say safek, for even certain eruv does not help, discussing there explicitly bread, and Tosafot Shabbat 34 wrote R' Meir holds one may make eruv with anything; one cannot say he speaks of twelve mil d'oraisa therefore forbidden in safek since he has Torah root — R' Meir is lenient in safek tumah there, and Eruvin 36 safek tumah has Torah root. Perhaps Tur's intent: even for one who says techumin from Torah, eruv helps but he also has biblical hint; and it seems to me eruv was instituted by Sages because techumin are d'rabbanan — requires study.
