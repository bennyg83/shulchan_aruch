(s.k. 1) Distant from the city, etc. Within the techum from his house's opening — he is precise thus from what is taught afterward: how so if he placed his eruv one thousand cubits from his house in the city, etc.; and the reason: if he places it distant from the city but within the techum of the city, but outside his house's techum — it is not an eruv, for then the eruv is placed outside his techum and is not valid as explained end of this siman. On this Magen Avraham wrote it does not exist — for here it began distant from the city and house was not mentioned; also per Rambam's reason in nitgalgel ch. 30 there is no reason to distinguish between within his house's techum or within his city's techum.

(s.k. 1) Forbidden to return, etc. — Rambam per his view that if his measure ended in the middle of the city he has only half the city; so Rabbeinu Yonatan (and per Rama's Hagah the whole city is four cubits — truly that is not so). In any case even per Magen Avraham he may place it within his city's techum even if far from his house's techum; even if he made eruv via agent and did not place eruv himself, it must be that he is on erev Shabbat within the eruv techum; and possibly the law agrees with Eliyahu Rabbah and I did not know what he argues.
