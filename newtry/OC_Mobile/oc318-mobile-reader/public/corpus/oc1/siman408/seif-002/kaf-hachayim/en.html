(2) There in Hagah — permitted to make eruv, etc., because techumin are d'rabbanan they were lenient and permitted one who needs to walk four thousand in one direction to place his eruv at end of two thousand and walk beyond to his eruv. Two thousand; nevertheless he loses opposite the two thousand beyond the eruv, for a person has only four thousand, and that he placed his eruv at end of two thousand from the city — we see as if he rested there and from there has two thousand in every direction. Tur. And though some say techumin d'oraisa, nevertheless within twelve mil it is d'rabbanan as written above siman 397 — see there.
