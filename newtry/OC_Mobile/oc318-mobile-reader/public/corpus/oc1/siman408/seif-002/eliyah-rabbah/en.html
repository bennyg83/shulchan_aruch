[2] [Levush] He loses opposite, etc. — implies even if he reconsidered on Shabbat and is not outside his eruv; but from Shulchan Aruch language therefore when he walks, etc., implies specifically when he walks the next day outside his eruv he loses; and it appears he says so when he has permission to walk; so explained in Raavan whom I cite in s.k. 5: as soon as he made eruv he lost — so implication of Poskim.
