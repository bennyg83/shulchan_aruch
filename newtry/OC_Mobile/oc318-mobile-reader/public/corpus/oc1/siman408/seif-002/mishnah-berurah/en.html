(23) In private domain — a place surrounded by partitions.

(24) Even a ruined city — there had been residents and they were destroyed, and the wall partitions remain.

(25) Fit for dwelling — meaning: even if there are no residents now, but it is fit for residence; if not fit to dwell there, such as breached partitions or very low, etc., it is not considered four cubits and we measure two thousand from the eruv placement.

(26) All of it — we consider it all four cubits when partitions were set up for residence; but if the surrounding width were only two se'ah, even if not enclosed for residence it is considered four cubits, as above siman 396 seif 2.
