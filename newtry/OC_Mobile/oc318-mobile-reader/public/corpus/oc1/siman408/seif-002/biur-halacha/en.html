One who places his eruv, etc. — discusses when he rests elsewhere but placed his eruv there.

And even a ruined city — Gemara has dispute: Shmuel and R' Eliezer; Rambam ruled leniently like R' Eliezer; so Raosh understood from Rif since he did not bring their dispute — implies he holds leniently — see there; therefore Mechaber ruled leniently like them. Several rishonim rule stringently: Rashba brought in Beit Yosef; so Raavad brought by Ritva; so Semag in name of Rabbeinu Yeshayah; so Hagahot Maimoniot in name of Maharam (and what Raosh wrote per Rif's view is not necessary; on the contrary one may argue the opposite — R' Eliezer extracts the mishnah from its plain sense, and if he ruled like him he should have brought their words; since he did not bring their dispute at all, only the mishnah in its form — necessarily he explains it plainly per Shmuel that only when there were residents do we say all is four cubits; requires study on Raosh here); nevertheless Rabbeinu Yonatan ruled plainly like R' Eliezer; also in Razah I found he wrote to be lenient like R' Eliezer as Mechaber ruled; therefore for practical law one may certainly rely to be lenient.
