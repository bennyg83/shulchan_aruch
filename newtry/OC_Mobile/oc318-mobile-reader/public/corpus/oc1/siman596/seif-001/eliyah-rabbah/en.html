[1] [Levush] Our custom, etc. Perhaps they sinned and did not hear properly — Taz. Rama wrote: after they fulfilled [their obligation] in the synagogue they should not blow again for free; but a minor — even if he reached education — one may tell him to blow and he may blow all day — end of his words. And Levush whom he omitted — perhaps he holds like Or Zaru'a who brought in Darkei Moshe that even an adult may [blow] all day. And Taz disagrees with Rama — his wording: on Yom Tov there is no prohibition in the world, for we conclude in gemara beginning of last chapter of Rosh Hashanah that tekiah is craft and not melacha, and there is no shevut except Rabba's decree lest he carry it four cubits in public domain — and this applies only on Shabbat but not on Yom Tov. And I again saw in Tur siman 595 that Ravyah said the blower transgresses rabbinic shevut — and it is astonishing from where legs; and it is possible he says thus: since on Shabbat there is shevut prohibition — end of his words briefly. And I say that in waiving honor he did not examine Tosafot there s.v. radyat, etc., that implies shevut applies even on Yom Tov; and what it says craft and not melacha means only not forbidden d'oraisa but rabbinically forbidden due to shevut — as explained from Rashi Shabbat daf 3: his wording, radyat bread — shevut it is, for craft and not melacha it is — end of his words. And if so the same for shofar, and Tosafot wrote explicitly there and daf 117 and Chullin daf 84. But I am troubled on Rashi's explanation Rosh Hashanah daf 33 who explained on "but women hold them back" because of "do not add" and did not explain because of shevut; and Ran likewise in truth; and Eruvin daf 96b Rashi explained "but women hold them back" even though it is not Yom Tov desecration, etc., nevertheless they appear like adding — end of his words, and requires study. And it is possible Rashi holds to be lenient on Yom Tov of Rosh Hashanah since from permitting tekiah for need it is permitted not for need; and in this vein Ravyah wrote daf 80; and although Tosafot in Chullin wrote we do not say from permitting carrying we permit shofar because there is no need that day at all — one may say only for d'oraisa carrying we say thus, but for rabbinic tekiah we are lenient. And it appears to me this is Or Zaru'a's view mentioned above who permits blowing all day; nevertheless since Tur and Ravyah and Kesef Mishneh forbade, better to be stringent, and so ruled Riaz. And what Taz challenged — if so shofar should be forbidden to move after obligation like a spit roasted on Yom Tov — no difficulty, for only to blow they forbade due to shevut and not movement, for on Rosh Hashanah obligation is on all Israel — not relevant to forbid movement; and see siman 341 like scalpel of milah. Per this the challenge of elders of Jerusalem who carry lulav in hand is also no difficulty, for there is no shevut of tekiah. Also what Taz wrote mishnah that we do not hold back children from blowing implies prohibition to blow itself — deals with Shabbat as in baraita — end of his words. And it is not so, for explicitly in Rashi Eruvin I brought it deals with Yom Tov, and so in all Tosafot I brought — see there and requires study. Also at first glance why ask Rosh Hashanah daf 33 "but women hold them back" and it teaches we do not hold back on Yom Tov — perhaps mishnah is different, deals with Shabbat. And truly it appears to me nothing is proven from mishnah — one may say permitted to blow himself since permitted for him for need, unlike children who were never permitted. Also what they wrote Tosafot daf 30 only forbade blowing for free on Shabbat — in my humble opinion they mean the same on Yom Tov, requires study; and so in Tosafot pesakim there. General rule: this matter is a dispute of early great poskim and proper to be stringent. Magen Avraham wrote: even one who will blow on the second day is forbidden to blow on the first to learn; carrying not for need — obviously forbidden as above.
