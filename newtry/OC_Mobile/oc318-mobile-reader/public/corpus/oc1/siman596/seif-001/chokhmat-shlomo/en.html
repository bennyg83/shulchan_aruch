(Same section 1 in the gloss) And there are places that practice returning to blow thirty sounds; and after they fulfilled with this they should not blow again for free, etc. It appears to me: regarding the law whether another may blow to fulfill a woman after he already fulfilled — see in Rosh dispute on this; and see in responsa Sha'agas Aryeh who elaborated; and at first glance it is a kal va-chomer now from daf 30 of Rosh Hashanah which asks there: Rava asked from baraita: tekiah of Rosh Hashanah overrides Shabbat in border towns — what of "man and his household"? If man and his wife — is a woman obligated? But it is a positive time-bound mitzva and all positive time-bound mitzvot women are exempt, etc. — and it is difficult why it asks: behold below it asks we do not hold back children — but women hold back; and it teaches we do not hold back neither women nor children; and answers: this is R' Yosi who said women perform semicha as reshut — if so proven per view that women semicha is reshut they may perform semicha even though it appears like performing avoda in kodashim, nevertheless permitted; if so here too prohibition of tekiah on Shabbat is only rabbinic — and one may also say it goes like R' Yosi that women semicha is reshut and therefore overrides Shabbat "man and his household" — for what an adult overrides Shabbat in court is not because if he transgresses on Rosh Hashanah this one would be exempt — certainly if he were exempt even outside court he would blow, and perforce if he transgresses he is liable, only in court they did not decree thus; if so one may say regarding a woman since women semicha is reshut and they permitted them even though it appears like avoda in kodashim, they permitted this too. Granted per Beit Hillel who holds another cannot blow for them — one may say they did not permit any rabbinic prohibition because they are reshut in it; and what is permitted to perform semicha is because there is no prohibition in their hands at all; and what they blow themselves — no prohibition at all; one may say on Shabbat since they forbade blowing not before court there is no reason to permit women; but per Ravyah who holds another may blow for them even though there is prohibition for him to blow for free as Rama wrote here, and also holds there is somewhat prohibition in semicha appearing like avoda in kodashim yet they permitted for women — if so why ask? say it goes like view women semicha is reshut and they further permitted them to blow before court and did not decree lest he transgress — requires study as now, requires study.
