One should not blow. Be'er Heitev and see Kaf HaChayyim — it is clear from Shabbat 114b and Chullin 84a on Shabbat there, and likewise Rosh Hashanah beginning of Yom Tov that blowing shofar for free is forbidden d'rabbanan even on Yom Tov of Rosh Hashanah that does not fall on Shabbat — not like Taz siman 596 and Peri Megadim who wrote to resolve Kaf HaChayyim's question; and he wrote that on other Yom Tov Taz agrees it is forbidden — only on Rosh Hashanah Yom Tov he permits, for on other Yom Tov there is shevut lest one fix a musical instrument, which is not so on Rosh Hashanah Yom Tov where they did not decree on this as Taz wrote siman 588 — if so all day permitted to blow if not Shabbat, for on Shabbat there is another decree lest one carry it on erev Shabbat; see in Rav Meshulam at the start of the book matters collected from Kaf HaChayyim z"l who wrote that you wrote to resolve wonder in my books see there that his words aim to reject what Peri Megadim wrote in his critique on Taz see there.
