1) [Seif 1] After the prayer they sound a teruah, etc. So wrote the Tur in the name of Rav Amram, and he wrote that the reason is to confound the Satan. The Taz, s.k. 1, explained that this is so that he should not prosecute against them after the prayer, when they go and eat, drink, and rejoice, claiming that they are not afraid of the dread of the Day of Judgment, end quote. One can further say that it is in order to repel the external forces, so that they should not draw nourishment from the spiritual bounty brought down through the prayers on this awesome day.
