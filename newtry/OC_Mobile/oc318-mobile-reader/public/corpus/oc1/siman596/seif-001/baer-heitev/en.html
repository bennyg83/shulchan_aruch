One should not blow. It is forbidden to take out the shofar when there is no need to blow, for that is carrying without need. Even one who will blow on the second day is forbidden to blow on the first day in order to practice, for we are expert in the fixing of the month, and the first day is holy while the second is weekday; Magen Avraham, see there. The Taz permits blowing on Rosh Hashanah after they have fulfilled their obligation; see there, for he concluded that on Yom Tov there is no shevus of blowing. In Teshuvas Chacham Tzvi, siman 35, he wondered about him; see there. In Teshuvas Panim Meiros, siman 102, he defended the Taz; see there. See Yad Aharon at length about this. However, it is permitted to move the shofar after they have blown with it; Sheyarei Kenesses HaGedolah in the name of Maharshal. So too the Taz wrote at the end of s.k. 2; see there. And see siman 588, seif 5, in the gloss.
