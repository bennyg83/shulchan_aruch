They sound a great teruah. In order to confuse the Satan so that he not prosecute them after the prayer, when they go, eat, drink, and rejoice, by saying that they are not afraid of the dread of judgment. But we do not practice so, only at the conclusion of the final tekiah completing the hundred sounds, as we will explain below. The caller says, Tekiah Gedolah, and the blower extends it more than the other tekiyos; Mateh Ephraim.
Thirty sounds. In the Shelah he wrote to blow one hundred sounds, namely the thirty before Musaf, the blasts of the orders of Musaf, and thirty after the prayer, and to add more so that in total there will be one hundred sounds.
One should not blow further, etc. Since it is not needed, it is like every other Yom Tov, when it is forbidden to blow because of shevus. See the Taz, whose opinion is that the Yom Tov of Rosh Hashanah is not comparable to other Yamim Tovim in this regard. But most later authorities agreed to forbid, like the words of the Rema. Regarding moving it, according to everyone it is permitted the entire day, for it is fit to blow with it to discharge others who have not yet fulfilled their obligation.
For no reason. It is also forbidden to take out the shofar when one does not need to blow, for that is carrying without need; see siman 518, seif 1, in the gloss. Even one who will blow on the second day is forbidden to blow on the first day to practice.
It is permitted to tell him to blow. This means even to make an effort to teach him the manner of the blasts. It implies that even someone other than his father is permitted in this, for he is training a minor.
All day. This refers to a minor, for regarding an adult he already wrote that one should not blow further for no reason.
