It is just like lulav in siman 658, where a prohibition of moving is mentioned only on Shabbos. From Tosafos in Chullin 87, s.v. Teki'as Shofar, and in Rosh Hashanah 29, s.v. Rediyas, and Maharsha there, and the Beis Yosef, siman 590, in the name of the Ram, the Geonim, Semag, Maggid Mishneh, and Kesef Mishneh, chapter 2 of Hilchos Shofar, and so the Rosh wrote in Rosh Hashanah, siman 31, from all these we hear that one should not blow for no reason on Yom Tov, for blowing involves a rabbinic melachah.
