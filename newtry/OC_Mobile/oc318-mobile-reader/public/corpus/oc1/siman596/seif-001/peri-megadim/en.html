A great teruah. See Turei Zahav. What he wrote, that in the Rema, of blessed memory, regarding the teruah blasts that the Rema mentioned, he does not know how to resolve, requires analysis. One must say that it refers to what he wrote, that one should not blow further for no reason. And Rav Yitzchak bar Yosef in Yavneh, Rosh Hashanah 30a, and Tosafos there, and in Yavneh on Shabbos, one may not blow for no reason in the time of beis din; see there, and see siman 588. His intent, of blessed memory, is that on the Yom Tov of Rosh Hashanah that falls on a weekday, there is no prohibition to blow the entire day even for an adult, since mitzvah blowing was permitted, for the concern that one might repair a musical instrument does not apply when uprooting a Torah matter. The same applies afterward; no decree was made. This is unlike other Yamim Tovim, Pesach, Shavuos, and Sukkos, where one can say there is shevus. With Hashem's help this will be explained in os 2.
