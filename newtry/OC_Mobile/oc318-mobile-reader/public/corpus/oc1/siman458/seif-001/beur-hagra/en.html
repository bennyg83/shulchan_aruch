(א) Some practice etc. — but not per law; for if it were law, when the 14th falls on Shabbat one would need to bake on erev Shabbat, as R' Shimon of Sens brought in Mordechai end of first chapter; and he wrote matzah is equated to Pesach as written in the chapter "if one ate matzah after midnight" per Raavad etc., derived from Pesach and so here; but they disagreed that it is equated only for eating, and proof from what is written chapter 2 regarding thanksgiving loaves and nazir wafers that he made etc.; and therefore he baked on the 14th, for one does not bring on the 14th as written in first chapter "one does not bring" etc.; and all the more so after its prohibition time. And in Tosefta: when the 14th falls on Shabbat they burn everything before Shabbat and bake matzah for him from erev Shabbat. And further taught in Tosefta: one fulfills with old matzah provided it was made for matzah. And in Yerushalmi chapter 2 halakhah 4: old matzah is a dispute of Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel — meaning as they disagree regarding sukkah; and there they disagree regarding matzah made thirty days before the festival — see Tosafot Sukkah 9a s.v. sukkah etc.; and there R' Yehudah s.v. since he did not make it for Pesach it is clear he was not meticulous; but if he intended from the beginning of the year it is valid as written in Sukkah; and all the more so per the tanna kamma that even Beit Shammai forbid only before thirty days — see Mordechai there; and so is the ikar.

(ב) That it is the time. As Magen Avraham wrote chapter 1 that everyone etc. from six etc. "you shall not slaughter" etc. — Magen Avraham.

(ג) And on the 14th etc. See Hagahot Maimoniot; and so in Tosefta chapter 3: when the 14th falls on Shabbat they burn everything before Shabbat and bake matzah for him from erev Shabbat.
