(1) [Seif 1] The practice is not to knead etc. — and it is in a responsum: an incident occurred with one who baked matzot before four hours and had already destroyed his chametz, and our rabbis forbade the matzah, for matzah is equated to Pesach; and since Pesach is not slaughtered except from six hours and above, so too matzah. But R' Eliezer the Great and R' Shmuel the priest permitted; however l'chatchila one should be careful for love of the mitzvah in its time. Avi HaEzri brought Tosefta: one fulfills with old matzah provided he made it for Pesach; and he wrote Yerushalmi disagrees with Tosefta and forbids even if he made it for Pesach; and since Yerushalmi forbids, it is fit to be stringent not to make before midday. Tur. And what he wrote that matzah is equated to Pesach as in Pesachim 120b, and Rashi, Rashbam, and Rabbenu Tam wrote there from the verse "on matzot and maror they shall eat it" — see there; and so Levush. And the view of those who permit holds it is not equated to Pesach except for eating but not for making. Bach.
