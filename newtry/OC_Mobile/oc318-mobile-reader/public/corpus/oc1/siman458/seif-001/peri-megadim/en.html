Language of Taz. What the Mechaber wrote "the practice" implies not per law; and see Olat Shabbat and the hekesh; Levush wrote: "roasted in fire and matzot they shall eat it" — only for eating, not for all matters; and Eshel Avraham and Peri Chadash and in Derishah the hekesh "and you shall slaughter the Pesach" "you shall not eat upon it chametz" "seven days you shall eat matzot" Pesachim 28b per R' Yehudah that the halakha is like him — "you shall not eat upon it" at the time of slaughtering the Pesach he speaks; so too "upon it matzot" — meaning matzah baked at the time of slaughtering the Pesach one fulfills with it; and although it is written "seven days" — because of the first night one may say it is written so; and not difficult what R' Shimon challenged there; and therefore per R' Shimon it is a complete derivation that it is possible. And what Taz wrote after six and a half — so Bach and Magen Avraham chapter 1; and what the Mechaber wrote matzah mitzvah — behold other matzot they bake beforehand; and Beit Yosef challenged: if so what Tur wrote in time of duress he bakes three matzot on erev Shabbat implies that not in time of duress he bakes other matzot also after midday; and Taz answered: here it deals with what is fit to bake with matzah mitzvah many other matzot as in chapter 5 Yom Tov: filling the oven so the bread bakes well; and in time of duress he bakes at least three matzot; but permitted to bake other matzot before the 14th many, provided he guarded from chametz; and Eshel Avraham and other poskim: one who has ability, it is a mitzvah to bake all the matzot after midday (and for what is written in the name of Derishah there is support from the hekesh) — end quote Darkei Moshe 2 in the name of Eshel Avraham: good to keep the matzot hot, for hot nullifies the poison of maror — see there. (And mar'it ha'ayin explained: they will think he eats on erev Pesach matzah, and forbidden to eat matzah on erev Pesach.) And that one need not destroy from the house because he does not abandon — one may say in rabbinic matters one need not; and it is difficult to me; and see siman 471 seif 2 in the gloss: permitted to crumble matzot on erev Pesach; and if so it will be explained there; and Eshel Avraham: what is written appears like preparing from Shabbat to Yom Tov, for from minchah and above one does not do melacha; and per his view he speaks of six and a half; and see Eliyah Rabbah in Magen Avraham section 4. And what Taz wrote not to bake on Yom Tov for several reasons; and regarding something chametz before Pesach it is nullified and does not return and awaken — see siman 464. And if they bake on Yom Tov they separate challah on Yom Tov; and if kneaded on Yom Tov and it extended until night — Magen Avraham 406.

(ב) And see Magen Avraham 406 section 8 regarding challah d'rabbanan when he forgot on erev Pesach to separate, for there is no remedy in chametz; and Taz end siman 463 sided in rabbinic matters one may say it is not fixing; and see Beitzah 9 and Eshel Avraham 406: even challah d'rabbanan if he rolled on erev Pesach one does not separate on Yom Tov; and one may say one need not, for he eats and goes and afterward separates, unlike where impossible for him on erev Pesach; and responsum Panim Me'irot part 1 siman 134 daf 37 section 4 challenged: why do we not tithe on Shabbat, and Shabbat 128a: tevel-crop d'rabbanan is muktzeh; and one may say tevel: whoever does not wish now to fix is not fit and it is muktzeh; and demai: Rambam z"l wrote peirush mishnah chapter 3 halakhah 14: one does not tithe, for it is similar to buying and selling; and further fixing; and if so in rabbinic matters one may say similar to buying and selling; and Shabbat 142a: fixing implies even in rabbinic matters fixing — meaning per R' Yehudah min b'mino; something not nullified is said even in dry; and terumah that rises in one in a hundred from the exposition "and you shall set them aside"; and Tosafot Menachot 22b challenged: it is fixing — strained. And one may say fixing is not so, rather it is like muktzeh; and so challah d'rabbanan sanctifying is similar to muktzeh, unlike challah d'rabbanan burned for its own sake. However on erev Shabbat it is permitted; and likewise tevilat kelim.
