Six hours. Avodat HaKodesh; and R' Yonatan wrote: Acharonim wrote in the name of Rokeach seven and a half; and so in Shibolei HaLeket etc.; and the matter is good to wait until seven and a half; and see Radbaz siman 477 and Eliyah Rabbah and see there who wrote: I saw meticulous people pray minchah before performing the mitzvah, although all year they are concerned for Rambam's view not to pray l'chatchila minchah gedolah on erev Pesach — they would pray before making the matzot — see there; and in our places they do not practice so even among the meticulous, because on erev Pesach when they are completely free after making the matzot there is no concern of forgetting at all; and also no concern they will be delayed in baking more and continue until night, because the bakers of matzot on erev Pesach are zealous; and also per what is explained above siman 432 that now they call to the synagogue — there is no concern lest one be negligent — see there.
