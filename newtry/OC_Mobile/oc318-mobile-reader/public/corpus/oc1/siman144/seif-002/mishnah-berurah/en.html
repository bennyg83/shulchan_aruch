(10) The custom is, etc. The reason for the custom, although it often happens that one skips from one prophet to another, is that in the days of the Gemara all the books were written on scrolls and rolling them required time. Nowadays, however, when the haftaros are written in booklets or Chumashim, one can place a marker and find the passage quickly.
(11) They recite the first verse, etc. As a reminder that the following day is Rosh Chodesh.
(12) See below, siman 425. There it is explained that nowadays the custom is not to skip from one prophet to another at all.
