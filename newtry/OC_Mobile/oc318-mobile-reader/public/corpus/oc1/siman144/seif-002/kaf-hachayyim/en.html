2) There: And one does not skip in the Torah, etc. The Knesses HaGedolah wrote in the Hagahos HaTur in the name of the Maharam Mintz, siman 85, that the same applies that one should not read on one Shabbos in two scrolls, even though there is no skipping there, such as parashas Pekudei and parashas Vayikra; see there. But the Pri Chadash, ot 1, wrote that there is no reasoning in his words to rely upon him, and therefore one who reads in two scrolls has not lost [i.e., it is valid]. End of his language. And the Yad Aharon cited him in the Hagahos HaTur. And so wrote [the author] above in siman 135, ot 5, in the name of several poskim, that if it happened that they omitted and did not read parashas Pekudei, that it is permitted to read it with parashas Vayikra; see there.
