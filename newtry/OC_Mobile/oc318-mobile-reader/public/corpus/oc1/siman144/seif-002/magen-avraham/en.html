Where there is a groom. Knesses HaGedolah, siman 281, discusses at length their custom to take out a second Sefer Torah for a groom and read, “And Avraham was old,” through “and you shall take a wife for my son.” If, however, it is a day on which two Sifrei Torah are taken out in any event, they read this passage from the second Sefer Torah and do not take out a third. Their custom is also to translate that passage, and even the chazzan may translate it. This applies specifically to one marrying a virgin, but not to one marrying a widow.
