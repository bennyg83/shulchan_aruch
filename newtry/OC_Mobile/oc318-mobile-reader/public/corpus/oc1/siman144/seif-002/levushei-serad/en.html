Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. Meaning, regarding a prophet from Trei Asar. Kesef Mishneh holds that the Gemara’s statement, “provided that he does not skip from the end,” applies only to the immediately preceding case, meaning that in Trei Asar the permission to skip from one prophet to another does not include skipping backward. Within a single prophet, however, it is permitted even backward. Magen Avraham writes, “This requires study,” meaning that the Gemara’s qualification applies to all the prophets.
