(In Magen Avraham s.k. 6) In the Temple all Israel [were present]. NB: His words are difficult — why did Rif and Rosh bring this exchange? For this is halacha for the Messiah, and the straight [understanding] is what Hagahot Mordekhai brought in the first chapter of Gittin — that it is an impossible matter; and this is the reason regarding Torah reading in the time of the Talmud, that the one called up reads himself; if so it is obvious it is an impossible matter to read from memory, for who knows who will ascend for maftir; and now that Israel is scattered and separated, a little here and a little there — who will stand for them in every village and town? Chazzanim learn so they can say the portion from memory; therefore we are witnesses that the congregation waived their honor to fulfill the festival readings at their times. Israel cleaves to mitzvot. And why did Magen Avraham [criticize] Bach s.k. 1 that he erred between ach ba'aser and ba'aser in the month — Heaven forbid to make him an errant; rather a word was missing in Bach s.k. 1, and thus it should read: nevertheless what they skip is the measure after ach ba'aser, etc.
