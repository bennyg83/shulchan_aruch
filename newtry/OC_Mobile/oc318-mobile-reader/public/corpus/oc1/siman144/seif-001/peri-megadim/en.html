Such as — in Rif and Rosh Megillah 24a, who wrote: although they are truly distant, nevertheless the addition seems unnecessary; therefore Bach answered that he taught us that ach ba'aser is close and adjacent to what precedes it; even if they are very far, as long as the one who rolls is skilled and fast — see there; and ach ba'aser is to say they are one subject. And Taz had a slight difficulty: why did he teach us this, for it is known they are distant and not in one parasha — requires study.
