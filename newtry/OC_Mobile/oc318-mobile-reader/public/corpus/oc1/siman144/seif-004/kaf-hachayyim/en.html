4) There: And [only] provided that he does not read by heart, etc. That is to say, although they permitted skipping in one matter, that is specifically to read in the Sefer Torah, but by heart it is forbidden. And that which the Kohen Gadol would read 'and on the tenth of the month,' etc., by heart -- there it is different, for it was not possible, since one does not roll a Sefer Torah before the congregation, and also one does not bring another [scroll], because one does not read for one man in two Sifrei Torah, as stated in the Gemara there in Yoma, folio 70a; and the Kesef Mishneh cited it, chapter 12 of Hilchos Tefillah, law 8. Magen Avraham, s.k. 2.
