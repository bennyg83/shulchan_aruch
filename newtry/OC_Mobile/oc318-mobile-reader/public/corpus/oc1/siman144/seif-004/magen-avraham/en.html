B'S. A. B. B. The poor

JThree men — implies the first is forbidden to read in the third Torah scroll; it is astounding, for we see the practice that even one who read in the Torah goes up for maftir in the second Torah scroll; it appears to me this is not a proper custom and one should protest — Bach, end of his words; I have not seen any proof for his words; one can say here if there is no other there who knows to maftir, the first may read maftir (and siman 482 seif 5); but the gemara deals when there are those who know and the announcer informs the count of readers, as considered there extensively in Megillah 29; and it is wondrous — per his words it should have asked above why the Tur mentioned three men, that it comes to teach us the first is forbidden to read in the third Torah scroll — see Bach; therefore it appears to me it is permitted, for there is no defect except when reading in both one after the other — "why did he not read in the first," except the portion was missing in it; but when he interrupted in the middle it is like reading anew; however in any case the seventh should not read maftir in another Torah scroll, rather they roll the Torah scroll until they arrive there, as above.
