Sifrei Torah. Even for two subjects; see Kesef Mishneh, Kenesset HaGedolah, and Peri Chadash. In Magen Avraham he wrote in the name of the Kesef Mishneh: even for one subject, but that is a scribal error, and the Baer Heitev before me did not notice this.
Books. It implies that the first person is forbidden to read from the third Sefer Torah. Bach. But Magen Avraham permits; see there, and see siman 282.
Defect. Even in one subject, Peri Chadash; see there. One who bought a mitzvah for a year and died in the middle of the year: the heirs must pay the value of the mitzvah, and whatever is lacking they complete for the community; and if they sell it for more, the profit belongs to the heirs. Kenesset HaGedolah. Olat Tamid disagrees with him and ruled that the profit belongs to the community, for one does not profit from charity; see there.
