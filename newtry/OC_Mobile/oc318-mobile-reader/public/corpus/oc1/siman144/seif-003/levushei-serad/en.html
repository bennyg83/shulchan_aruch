In any event, if the statement “provided that,” etc., applies to all the prophets, and logic permits skipping within one section, Kesef Mishneh should have distinguished between Trei Asar and the other prophets. Moreover, what distinction is there? Skipping from one prophet to another within Trei Asar is not one section. In such a case it should be prohibited in all the prophets. If, however, “provided that” refers to Trei Asar, and logic prohibits skipping between two sections in the other prophets, the second question still remains. One may answer that when Magen Avraham writes “within one section,” he means one subject. The phrase “provided that” refers to skipping from one prophet to another within Trei Asar, even when both discuss one subject. Between two subjects, however, we prohibit skipping by logic in all the prophets.
