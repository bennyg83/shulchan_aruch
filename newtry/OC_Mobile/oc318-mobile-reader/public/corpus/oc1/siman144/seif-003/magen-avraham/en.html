They do not roll. On a Shabbos when two Sifrei Torah are read, they may roll the second while the first is being read. Rabbi Yaakov Levi, siman 89.
And the honor of the congregation is set aside. This requires study. In the case of the Kohen Gadol they permitted reading by heart, but did not permit rolling the Sefer Torah in the presence of the congregation. This proves that we do not say that the congregation’s honor is set aside even where no other alternative exists. If so, here too he should read by heart, as Beit Yosef writes in the name of the Rashba. One must say that in the Beis HaMikdash all Israel were present and did not waive their honor, whereas in a synagogue only a small number are present and they presumably waive their honor in order to fulfill the reading of the Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh maftir and the Yom Tov maftir, which are enactments of the Geonim. See Maseches Sofrim, chapter 11, and what I wrote in siman 143, seif 4. See Bach, whose words require study. He confused “Ube’asor” with “Ach be’asor,” for we read “Ube’asor,” and in that passage we never skip. See there, and see Beit Yosef, siman 143, and what I wrote in siman 53, seif 6.
