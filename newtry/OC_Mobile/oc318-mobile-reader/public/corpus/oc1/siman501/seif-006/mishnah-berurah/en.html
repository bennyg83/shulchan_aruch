That broke. That is, that broke so much that they are no longer fit for a use similar to their original work; but if they are fit for a use similar to their original work (that is, fragments of a trough to pour porridge into them, and fragments of glass to pour oil into them), they are like whole utensils and one may kindle with them; and see above in siman 308, se'if 6, in the Taz and Biur Halacha there.
For they are nolad. For originally a utensil and now a broken utensil, and they are forbidden in moving; therefore it is forbidden to kindle with them; and even if one does not move them at all but burns them in their place where they are lying, it is also forbidden, for the act of kindling that he does with a utensil that is lit by his means is considered like moving.
And if one kindled, etc. This gloss should be after the word "whole" in the Mechaber's words.
After they were kindled in part. That is to say, they were kindled so much that they are no longer fit for a use similar to their original work, as above.
Broken utensils. And even without turning them in the fire, even though one benefits from the muktzeh by which his cooked food was cooked there, we have no issue, for muktzeh is permitted in benefit when the benefit comes of its own accord and he does no act by hand; and it will be explained below in siman 507 in the Mishnah Berurah.
Prepared wood. And it will be explained below in siman 507, se'if 2.
With whole utensils. For even though a utensil does not stand for kindling, nevertheless we hold that all utensils may be taken even not for their designated use; and it is dealing where he has no wood for the need of his cooked food, for otherwise he transgresses "do not destroy." And sometimes it is forbidden to kindle even with whole utensils, such as a non-Jew who took a Jew's wood and made a utensil from it on Yom Tov, for it is nolad: originally wood and now a utensil. A stick with which one stirs the fire, since it is designated for that, is a utensil; and if it broke on Yom Tov it is forbidden to kindle with it, as above.
That broke while it was still day. And if they are fit for a use similar to their original work, their law is like whole ones, and it is forbidden to turn them after they were kindled in part, as above.
