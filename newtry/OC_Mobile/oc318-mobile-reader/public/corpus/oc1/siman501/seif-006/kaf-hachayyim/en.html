Small pieces, however, may not be split, because this could have been done before Yom Tov. The chapter Klal Gadol states in Shabbos 74b that one who cuts wood into small chips is liable for the melachah of grinding. Mordechai, chapter HaMeivi. Ran writes in the name of Raavad that since cooking and baking are impossible without wood, the Sages treated splitting it like crushing salt, which they permitted when performed in an unusual manner.
