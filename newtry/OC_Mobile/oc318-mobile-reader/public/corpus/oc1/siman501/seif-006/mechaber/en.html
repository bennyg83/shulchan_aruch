Vessels that broke on Yom Tov — one may not kindle with them, because they are nolad. {And if one kindled with vessels, it is forbidden to turn them over in the fire after they were partly kindled, for then they are shards of a vessel — unless he added upon them prepared wood [Maggid, chapter 2; Ran, first chapter of Beitzah].} But one may kindle with whole vessels, or with vessels that broke while it was still day.
