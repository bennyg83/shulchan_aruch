4) There. Even if it was unstable before Yom Tov, etc. We do not say that since it was unstable, he had in mind to use it as fuel if it broke. Regarding anything that was not fit at twilight, we do not say that he had it in mind, and it is considered nolad. Levush. This is also the view of Maharshal in Yam Shel Shlomo there and of Bach. Olas Shabbos there. Magen Avraham there likewise writes that even if it was unstable, it is called nolad, because initially it was a utensil and now it is a broken utensil. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2, write likewise.
