On the Taz: the Rambam in chapter 2 of Hilchos Yom Tov, halacha 14; and the language of Mechaber implies three conditions: (1) gathered; (2) a frame (even of wood also suffices, Eliyah Rabbah); (3) within the techum. But outside the techum, even if his own servant brought [wood] to cook for the need of a non-Jew, nevertheless it is muktzeh of the Jewish owner. But the Rashba and others: one [condition] suffices: a lock alone within the techum, or within seventy amos and a bit more. And see Magen Avraham (9), and God willing it will be explained further there on this.
