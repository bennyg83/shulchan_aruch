[Levush] It would be preparations, etc. And Raavad explained that they treated it like pounding salt; and the Bach wondered, for pounding salt is truly ochel nefesh itself. And I saw in Temim De'im as follows: salt that one places in food is [ochel nefesh], and splitting wood too is a need of ochel nefesh, since there can be no ochel nefesh without kindling; and so he wrote in Shibolei HaLeket, siman 81, that splitting is a need of ochel nefesh and not mere preparations for food.
