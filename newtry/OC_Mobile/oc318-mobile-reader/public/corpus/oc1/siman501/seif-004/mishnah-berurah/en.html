Since the wind scatters, etc. That is to say: since it is the way of the wind to scatter, he does not rely on having gathered them from erev Yom Tov; and even if he finds them gathered, it is forbidden because of muktzeh.
A heavy vessel from erev Yom Tov. So that the wind will not scatter them.
Behold, these are prepared. And even if he finds them on Yom Tov scattered, they are permitted, for they were already prepared from yesterday. And all this we wrote for the one who explains this law on account of muktzeh; and for the one who explains this law on account of me'amer, the reason of the opening clause is that we are concerned that when he comes to take them the wind will scatter them and he will come to gather them together; and when a heavy vessel rests upon them there is no concern for this, and it is permitted. And according to this, if he finds them scattered it is forbidden to collect them.
