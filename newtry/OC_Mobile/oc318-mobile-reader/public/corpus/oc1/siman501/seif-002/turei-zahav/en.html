And even to break by hand etc. Tur too from Rosh language seems forbidden; Beit Yosef learned since Rosh wrote do not split them since can cook and bake without splitting — exertion not for need, and av melacha like sifting silleta liable grinding etc.; our author thinks even by hand liable grinding. Rishon L'Tzion disagrees: from Rosh giving reason av melacha implies by hand permitted — no grinding except in vessel; even pepper where grinding applies no prohibition by hand only vessel. Therefore permitted per his words chapter HaMevi; I lean Tur learned from first reason Rosh — exertion not for need forbidden even by hand; Rosh intended two reasons: even without av melacha exertion not for need forbidden; ends av melacha two hundred categories. Why write exertion not for need if also av? Rather intends two reasons: even without av melacha, and av — even not fine splitting not like sifting silleta av grinding only very fine wood to kindle fire unlike here not splitting very fine as not usual to cook on such only place on fire after kindling — no grinding connection; forbidden necessarily from exertion. Therefore forbid by hand from first reason. I reconcile what cited Rav Yehuda strips hide hide — like Tur siman 519 permitted trim branches even with knife; Shabbos 322 trim straw between teeth — there for need unlike here exertion not for need. Tur explains Rashi seems permit break by hand; Beit Yosef did not know source from Rashi; Rashi weighed and did not seem forbid from Rashi. I reconcile main prohibition Rosh exertion; Rashi plain for questioner reason exertion but conclusion we do not forbid exertion only muktzeh — no prohibition by hand on non-muktzeh broken erev Yom Tov. Tur words fit; meticulous practice forbid even chol hamoed; prepare small wood needed for fish cooking erev Yom Tov — concerned exertion can burn without splitting, do not lighten; expensive wood no exertion concern. Breaking by hand do not be stringent at all chol hamoed. Rosh challenged why permit by change — machshirin possible erev; Raavad since cannot cook without wood made like salting permitted by change; I say whatever enjoys body of thing is ochel nefesh itself unlike knife/spit only enjoy what prepared — wood enjoys itself making fire warming opposite them; since split for bonfire permitted though possible erev even cook from them permitted 'since'; splitting for vessels only permitted by change — end Rosh. Beit Yosef challenged Mahari Abuhav: warming bonfire only permitted 'since' burning permitted for ochel nefesh — where say 'since' burning permitted to split wood? Rosh himself truly no warming from 'since' — itself ochel nefesh as here; Beit Yosef brought siman 511 two things warming bonfire and hot water from 'since' — even Raavad here no need 'since'.
