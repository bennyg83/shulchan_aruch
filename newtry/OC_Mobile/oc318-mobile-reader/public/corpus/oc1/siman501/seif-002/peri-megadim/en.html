On the Taz: he brought the words of Maharshal in chapter HaMevi, siman 7, and Rashi on Beitza folio 31b; and see Magen Avraham letter 4, and in Chok Yaakov on "by hand," that there is no grinding, only exertion, and it is permitted. And what he wrote on Mahari Abuhav's difficulty in Beis Yosef against the Ran is that the Ran holds that a fire is truly ochel nefesh, not merely "like" ochel nefesh (see Magen Avraham letter 3). And what the Ran wrote regarding a fire by way of "since" (mitoch) is according to Raavad, who answered regarding splitting that it is like pounding salt, and he does not hold the Ran's answer. And see siman 511 regarding heating a winter house; and God willing it will be explained there regarding ochel nefesh itself, whether we require that it be equal for every person.
