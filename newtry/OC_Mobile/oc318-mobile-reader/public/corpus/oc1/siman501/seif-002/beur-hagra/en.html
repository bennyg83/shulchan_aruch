Se'if 2: Large wood, etc. For only into large pieces, which is not [Torah] melacha but only weekday labor, did they permit; but into small pieces, regarding which they said in chapter 7 of Shabbos, "one who shreds shreddings," etc., they did not permit, since these are preparations that one could do from erev Yom Tov; and see the Mordechai there.
And even, etc. So the Tur inferred from the language of the Rosh, who forbade because it is unnecessary exertion and it is an av melacha; and that is what is meant in chapter 4 by "logs, logs," etc.; and see in Egoz. And Beis Yosef wrote: however, in Tosefta chapter 3 of Moed it is permitted by hand, for it is taught: a person may not place a stone on a log in order to break it, but he may insert it into a hole and break it.
