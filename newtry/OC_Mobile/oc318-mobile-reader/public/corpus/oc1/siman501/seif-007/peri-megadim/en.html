And torches — roasting and cooking for today's need and remains tomorrow as well. Rosh HaShana — even for two sanctities in Beitzah egg born on one forbidden on other; even one sanctity stringently not leniently unlike diaspora Yom Tov also egg born on one permitted on second since one is chol. Peri Chadash permits Yom Tov for itself as siman 498. Magen Avraham letter 16 from this; nevertheless Peri Chadash wrote to be stringent erev. See letter 12 in Levush 22 and 514; see Yeshuos Yaakov Beitzah chapter 1 simanim 3-4 — implies stringent on leftover oil and wick even same day to relight. Therefore three views on wick: (1) vessel — for body and place permitted at least to move; (2) not vessel forbidden to move — from three also preparation; Yeshuos Yaakov; torches likewise certainly not vessel forbidden for body and place; oil if edible perhaps permitted to move for all — see Magen Avraham 16.
