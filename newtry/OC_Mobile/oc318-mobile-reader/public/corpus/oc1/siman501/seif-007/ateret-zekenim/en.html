) One may not kindle in their shells even if they are fit for animal food, etc. It appears to me this is not precise, for we hold like R' Shimon as stated in chapter Nolad (Shabbos 143) — see there, R' Nasan: we have only Beit Hillel like R' Shimon, etc. R' Shimon permits only what is fit for animal food as taught in the mishnah because it is fit for animal food. See Tosafos there and Asheri. Accordingly all poskim follow this. Therefore it appears the Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch did not bring the law of date pits because some are fit for animal food and some are not as stated in the gemara there. See what was written above siman 459 skh 8 regarding fats fit for dogs — permitted to move if slaughtered on Yom Tov; so too here. What the Shulchan Aruch wrote — that if he ate on Yom Tov one may not kindle in shells — refers to walnuts and almonds whose shells are not fit for animal food; but where shells are fit for animal food it is plainly permitted, not like the Magen Avraham author. What the Rosh wrote at the end of Beitzah is not so, for the Rosh wrote in Peulat as Beit Hillel but per R' Chananel not so; other poskim hold not like R' Chananel and only permit if fit for animal food, as I wrote siman 499 bringing the Rosh in Nolad. I already wrote to distinguish Tosafos in chapter Ma Tovim 29a — reasoning for prohibition on nuts unlike duck feathers which permit goose feathers or date pits because nut shells are not needed for the food; if he intends to kindle with them he should remove from erev Yom Tov unlike duck feathers. Also date pits needed for the food itself cannot be removed erev Yom Tov therefore permitted. (Maharar Tzvi Shpitz)
