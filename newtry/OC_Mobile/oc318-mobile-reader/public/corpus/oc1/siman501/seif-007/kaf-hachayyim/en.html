7) There. He should split it neither with an axe, etc. Why are an axe and similar implements forbidden? It is so that he not act in the manner he does on a weekday, since he could have split the wood before Yom Tov. Why, then, was splitting not forbidden entirely? He may encounter a thick piece of wood that he cannot ignite and consequently refrain from cooking. The Sages therefore permitted splitting in an unusual manner. For this same reason, regarding all similar matters they permitted what they permitted and forbade what they forbade. Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilchos Yom Tov, law 10. Beis Yosef cites this. Levush, se'if 2.
