Seif 7 in the gloss: even on the second day of Rosh Hashanah. It puzzles me why this is mentioned regarding Rosh Hashanah of Yom Tov. If we come to be stricter on Rosh Hashanah more than other Yom Tov, this is because Rosh Hashanah is one sanctity. And in any case it is permitted here as on the day itself he prepared. And an egg born that day is forbidden — see Magen Avraham siman 477 regarding second day of Rosh Hashanah or second day chol, or one sanctity — see there. And here in any case what is stringent about hides is only from one sanctity.
