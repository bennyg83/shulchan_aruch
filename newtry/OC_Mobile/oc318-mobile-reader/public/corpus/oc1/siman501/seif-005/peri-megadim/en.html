Behold. On the Taz: this is the view of the Rambam, chapter 2, halacha 14 of Yom Tov, and Rashi on Beitza folio 31a; and it appears to him that "behold they are set aside" applies even to gathered wood in fields; and even so, gathering from in front of him is permitted, as we already wrote on this in letter 3. But from Magen Avraham letters 6 and 7 it does not appear so, as we will need to say there with Hashem's help. And what he wrote as the Ran's reason is that in a field it is me'amer; therefore it is forbidden, and they decreed gathered on account of scattered; a karpef is rabbinic, and therefore there is no me'amer except in the place of its growth; see Tosafos there, s.v. "Min"; rather, they are fit for tomorrow and for another day; therefore gathered is permitted.
