Behold, they are muktzeh. This is the reason of Rashi and the Rambam; but the Ran wrote a reason in the name of others, that the reason is on account of gathering, which is like me'amer, and anyone who gathers, even in a karpef, appears as one who acts for tomorrow; and gathered wood in fields is confused with scattered, and there is no permission except for gathered wood in a karpef. And there is a practical difference between the reasons: according to the Ran, even if he prepared them from erev Yom Tov it is forbidden.
