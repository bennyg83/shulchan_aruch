In the field, from in front of him. That is, near the place of cooking his pot, for his pot proves about him that he is not gathering for tomorrow's need. And in Yam Shel Shlomo he forbids in the field, for it appears like me'amer from the place of its growth, and one is liable a chatas like one who gathers reeds for a mat; and it is unlike splitting wood, which is permitted and is not truly an av melacha; end quote. And so the Bach wrote.
