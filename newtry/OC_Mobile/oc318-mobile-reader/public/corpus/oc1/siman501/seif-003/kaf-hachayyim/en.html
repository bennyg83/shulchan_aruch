3) There. Even if it was unstable before Yom Tov, etc. This law depends upon the dispute between Rabbi Yehudah and Rabbi Shimon regarding muktzeh, as is evident from the Talmud. Maran follows his position above, siman 495, where he rules in accordance with Rif and Rambam that muktzeh is forbidden on Yom Tov. Even according to the alternative opinion mentioned there by Rama, that muktzeh is permitted even on Yom Tov, it is nevertheless forbidden here because it is nolad. Rama therefore added no gloss.
