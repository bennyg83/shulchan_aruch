[Levush] Grinding, etc. Maharshal challenged and permits immediately, because there is no prohibition of grinding by hand; and Olas Shabbos wrote in the name of Maharshal to be stringent, and that is not so. However, the Taz answered that nevertheless it is forbidden because of unnecessary exertion, and that is the essential view; even though he wrote that from Rashi on Beitza folio 31, who explained according to the conclusion that the reason is muktzeh, it is apparent that he does not hold the reason of unnecessary exertion, in my humble opinion that is no proof, for the Gemara is dealing with a large piece that one cannot kindle without splitting, so the exertion is for a need. But this requires study, for Beis Yosef and Maharshal brought proof from a Tosefta that teaches: one may not place a stone on a log in order to break it, but one may insert it into a hole and break it; so it is apparent that by hand it is permitted, until here his words. And it appears to me that the log of the Tosefta may be dealing where it is large, such that one cannot kindle without splitting; and so it appears to me regarding Tosafos on folio 31 there, that one who permits by hand is dealing with such a case; and not like the Bach who brought proof from Tosafos. Therefore it is proper to forbid by hand; and see siman 533.
