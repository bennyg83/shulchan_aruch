One may not bring wood from the field, even if it was gathered there from the eve [of Yom Tov]; but one may gather in the field from what is before him {and likewise in the courtyard he takes from what is before him [Beis Yosef; Rambam]} and kindle there; and one may bring from what is gathered [and stored] in a private domain — even if it was surrounded [by a fence] not for the sake of a dwelling — provided that it has an enclosure, and it is within the Shabbos boundary. And if one of all these is lacking, they are muktzeh.
