And gathered wood in a field is muktzeh when collected long beforehand; but if he designated them from erev Yom Tov, one may say it is permitted. And according to the Ran, that gathered is confused with scattered in a field, and me'amer, designation does not help; see letter 5. And gathering has a reason that it appears for tomorrow's need. And gathering in the field to cook his pot there, which is permitted according to the Rambam even in the field even if he did not designate at all on the score of muktzeh: why is it permitted? And if you say muktzeh for the need of ochel nefesh is permitted like all melachos, only bringing from a distant place and long carrying from the pot they forbade, this requires study in siman 509 in Magen Avraham (15); and wood that fell into the oven proves somewhat, unless we say that gathering is permitted when he designated them from erev Yom Tov, and "not from the field" refers to confusion with scattered on the score of gathering. And see Magen Avraham (7), and God willing there and in Taz (5) it will be explained further.
