And close to breaking, etc. And one must say that regarding a utensil, even if it was whole yesterday and broke on Yom Tov, it is permitted according to all, for a utensil is made to break; and so it is implied in Beitza folio 32, where it poses a contradiction between two beraisos against each other, and it does not say "here where it was sound from erev Yom Tov, here where it was weak," but rather regarding a utensil there is no distinction. Nevertheless, according to Magen Avraham and the "some say" that we hold that nolad is forbidden, it is also forbidden regarding a utensil, for it is no better than a beam that is close to breaking. And in Nachalas Tzvi he inferred from this weak beam that he holds the Rama is like the Shulchan Aruch in siman 495 that muktzeh is permitted; but he was not precise, for nolad is forbidden even according to the "some say" that the Rama cited there.
