Se'if 1: Even if. See Tosafos there, folio 2b, s.v. "Ein"; and if you say, etc., and one can say, etc.; and see there in the Gemara, folio 30b, "Is it comparable," etc.
If it is impossible, etc. As the Ran wrote: and they raise a difficulty, for these are preparations that one could do from erev Yom Tov; and Raavad answered that since one cannot cook or bake without wood, they treated it like pounding salt, which is permitted by means of a change, and the same applies here. And even though the Ran wrote a different answer, Maharshal contradicted it; and see Beis Yosef.
Large pieces. See what I wrote in se'if 2.
And some, etc. And so Tosafos wrote there, except that they wrote specifically by hand; and that is not precise, for the same applies with a knife, since one is changing [the usual manner].
