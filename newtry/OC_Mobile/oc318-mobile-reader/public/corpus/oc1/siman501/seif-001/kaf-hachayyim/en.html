1) [Se'if 1] Wood may not be split from beams designated for construction. This applies even according to Rabbi Shimon, because the beams are muktzeh due to potential monetary loss, regarding which Rabbi Shimon agrees. Tur and Beis Yosef; Levush; Magen Avraham, subsection 1. Therefore, it is forbidden to move them even for use of the object itself or its place. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1. See above, siman 308, se'if 1, and our words there, with Heaven's help.
