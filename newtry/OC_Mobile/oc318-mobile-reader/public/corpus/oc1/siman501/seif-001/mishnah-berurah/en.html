Even if it was weak, etc. For it could be that from erev Shabbos he waits for it to break and did not set it aside; nevertheless it is forbidden, because in any case at twilight it was not broken and was fixed in the building and became forbidden, and it is not again permitted. And further, it is nolad, for originally it was a beam and was like a utensil, and now it is mere wood. And for this reason the Rama did not gloss here as in siman 495 that some permit muktzeh, because he relied on what he concludes there, that even the lenient ones did not wish to be lenient regarding muktzeh of nolad.
