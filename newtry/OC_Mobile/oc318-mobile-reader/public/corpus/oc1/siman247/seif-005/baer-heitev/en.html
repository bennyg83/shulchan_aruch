Such a case. Even if no postal station is established in the city, if the non-Jew does not find the addressee at home he will not follow him, since the Jew did not send him. But to send the letter with him on Friday, when there is insufficient time during the day for it to arrive, is forbidden, because he transports it for the Jew on Shabbos and carries it four cubits in a public domain on the Jew's behalf. All the more so is it forbidden to give it to him on Shabbos, as stated in siman 246, seif 2. See Magen Avraham and Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 42.
