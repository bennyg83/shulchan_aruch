Seif katan 5 "See seif 4." Meaning, this passage proves what the Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 7, contrary to the Rama there. Nevertheless, according to the Rama's understanding of the Beit Yosef, one must say that where the Jew explicitly asked him to go without payment and the non-Jew agreed, this is preferable to a case where the Jew said nothing about payment. The Eliyah Rabbah writes likewise.
