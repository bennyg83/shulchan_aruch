If he hired him by the day. Although we permitted a stipulated payment above even on Friday, that applies where he fixes a specific sum for the entire task. The gentile then acts for his own benefit, and the labor performed on Shabbos is not recognizable as being done on the Jew's agency. When he hires him by the day, setting payment for each day, then even though he does not insist when the gentile travel and the gentile likewise performs the work for his own benefit, this arrangement is still superior to one where no fee was stipulated at all and is therefore permitted on Wednesday or Thursday even according to the Mechaber. Nevertheless, if he hires him on Friday it is forbidden, because when the gentile departs immediately for Shabbos it appears as though the Jew conditioned the arrangement in that manner and the gentile travels as his agent, since he paid him for that day.
