[ג] [Levush] Only Israel, etc. He understood the difference between fixed post office and not — in non-fixed the Jew is involved; the reason is clear in all poskim: since fixed it helps when he can reach the nearby house; but without post office — postmaster who sometimes travels for business — even if he reaches his house we fear he must follow and will not leave the letter at home; lenient per poskim who permit; Magen Avraham: other utensils permitted to give a Kuti if can be done erev Shabbat even without fixing as siman 252; here since recognizable Jewish writing, etc.
