Seif 1, in the gloss "If they are needed." This refers to sending a letter. Regarding other matters, however, where one gives his utensils to a non-Jew to work upon in the non-Jew's home, even if they are not needed for that purpose, one may give them to him on Thursday without fixing a price. Magen Avraham below, siman 252, seif katan 8.
