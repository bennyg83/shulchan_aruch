The law of non-Jews who carry letters on Shabbos. It contains 6 seifim: A person may send a letter with a non-Jew even close to nightfall on Friday, provided that he fixes a price with him and does not tell him to travel on Shabbos. If no price was fixed, then if there is a fixed postal station in the city, the Jew may send it even on Friday, provided that enough time remains in the day for the non-Jew to reach a house near the city wall before Shabbos. If there is no fixed postal station, one may not send it on Friday unless there is enough time for it to reach the house to which it is addressed before Shabbos. {Rama: Some permit sending it even where no price was fixed and there is no fixed postal station in the city, provided that it is sent on Thursday or earlier. In a case of need, one may rely upon this view.}
