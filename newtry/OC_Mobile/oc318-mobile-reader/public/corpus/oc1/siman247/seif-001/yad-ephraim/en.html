Magen Avraham s.k. 1: even erev Shabbat, etc. — I brought Maharam Alashkar siman 246 s.k. 12: no concern he will take outside techum since techum is rabbinic unless given on Shabbat itself; must be about a letter — for a letter we fear he will follow to deliver Shabbat thinking he must know what is written; not so other objects he is not particular to hand directly and leaves with the guard.
