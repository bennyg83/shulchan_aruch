(א) [Seif 1] One sends a letter, etc. — he specified letter to teach that even recognizable Jewish handwriting in a non-Jew's hand is permitted with fixing. Bach; Eshel Avraham; Magen Avraham s.k. 1: same for all melachot need fixing as siman 252; specified letter to teach without fixed post office forbidden even from Sunday; only a letter without fixing and without post office forbidden from Sunday because recognizable Jewish writing; other objects not recognizable permitted even erev Shabbat without fixing if can arrive erev Shabbat; Rabbi Akiva Eiger, etc.
