Seif katan 6: on Friday it is forbidden, and so forth. This applies even where no fee at all was fixed; all the more so here, where the arrangement somewhat resembles a fixed fee. The Tevuot Shor questions this ruling: at the beginning of the siman the Beit Yosef rules like the Rif and Rambam that where no fee was fixed and there is no established postal office, sending is forbidden even from Thursday. This follows because the Rif and Rambam did not reproduce the ruling that where no fee was fixed it is permitted on Wednesday or Thursday. They evidently maintain that the baraita permitting it on those days was rejected from the halachah, and that permission exists only where there is an established postal office or a fixed fee. The Beit Yosef also cites the Ran that hiring the messenger by the day is considered a case where no fixed fee was set. According to the Rif and Rambam, then, it should be forbidden even on Sunday. Since the Beit Yosef rules like the Rambam, why does he write only that hiring by the day is forbidden on Friday, when it should be forbidden even on Sunday? This is the substance of his question; see there. In my humble opinion, this is the Beit Yosef's own compromise. True, in a case of absolutely no fixed fee, such as an unspecified arrangement or the like, he rules like the Rambam. But where the gentile was hired by the day, which somewhat resembles fixing a fee, he rules like the Rosh that it is forbidden specifically on Friday but permitted on Thursday.
