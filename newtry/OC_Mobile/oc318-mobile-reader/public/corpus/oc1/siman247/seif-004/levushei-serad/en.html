Seif katan 6 "The Rama has already written." Meaning, the Mechaber follows his own position in seif 1, that without a fixed price it is forbidden even from Sunday. He therefore teaches here that where the non-Jew is hired by the day and the Jew is not particular which day he works, some authorities consider this equivalent to a fixed-price arrangement. We rely upon them during the week, but not on Friday, as the Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 9. This is the meaning of his concluding reference to seif 6: on Wednesday and Thursday we rely upon the permissive authorities, but not on Friday. According to the Rama's position in seif 1, however, this seif creates no practical difference at all.
