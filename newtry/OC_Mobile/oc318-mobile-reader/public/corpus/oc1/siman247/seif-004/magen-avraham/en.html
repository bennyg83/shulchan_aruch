For the gentile does so on his own, and so forth. This implies that it is permitted only when the gentile independently offers the Jew to carry the letter. If the Jew tells him to go, it is forbidden even without payment, as also appears from seif 2. Yet the Rama's gloss implies that the Beit Yosef maintains that all such cases are permitted, and this likewise appears from siman 252, seif 2. See there.
