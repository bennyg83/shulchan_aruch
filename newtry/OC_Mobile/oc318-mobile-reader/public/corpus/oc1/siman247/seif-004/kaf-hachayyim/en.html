4) During a time of plague, epidemic, or dangerous illness, when the public is in distress, I permitted writing a letter shortly before nightfall on Friday and sending it through a non-Jew even on Shabbos. This is especially so for the needs of a sick person, in order to learn how others are faring. In all such cases one may be lenient, and this is obvious. One may even have a non-Jew write on Shabbos itself, for everything depends upon the circumstances of the time. Ruach Chaim.
