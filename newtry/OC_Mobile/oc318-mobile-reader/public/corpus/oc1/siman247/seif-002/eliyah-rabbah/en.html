[2] Provided that, etc. The non-Jew must also leave the Jew's house carrying it before nightfall. This is from the Rambam, chapter 7. End quote from Malbushei Yom Tov. The Rambam's wording is, "before Shabbos," and Sefer Adam VeChavah, page 82, states that the sun has not yet set, meaning that it is still shining. The Magen Avraham writes that one may not say to him, "See that it arrives there on Sunday," or "on Monday," and the like, where it is known that he cannot arrive there unless he travels on Shabbos. End quote. See siman 307, seif katan 9.
