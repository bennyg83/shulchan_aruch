2) There: A person may send a letter, etc. Similarly, one may send other articles and utensils with a non-Jew even shortly before nightfall on Friday, provided the non-Jew leaves the entrance of the Jewish person's house while it is still daytime, if the Jewish person fixed his compensation for the journey. The non-Jew then acts for himself in order to receive the fixed compensation and does not appear to be the Jewish person's agent. The Jewish person must not tell him to travel on Shabbos, because instructing a non-Jew is rabbinically forbidden even when the instruction is given before Shabbos. Nor may he tell him, 'See that you arrive there on Sunday,' or Monday, and the like, if he knows that the non-Jew cannot arrive there unless he also travels on Shabbos, for this is equivalent to telling him, 'Go on Shabbos.'
