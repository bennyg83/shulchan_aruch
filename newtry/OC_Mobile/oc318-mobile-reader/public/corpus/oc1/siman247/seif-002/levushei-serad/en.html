Seif katan 2 "From this we learn." This is a separate point. It means that since we see that the authorities are stringent even because of a doubt that he may not find the recipient and will therefore have to travel on Shabbos, it follows all the more so that one may not say, "See that it arrives there on such-and-such a day," etc. This is forbidden even where a price was fixed, as the Magen Avraham writes in siman 307, seif katan 3.
