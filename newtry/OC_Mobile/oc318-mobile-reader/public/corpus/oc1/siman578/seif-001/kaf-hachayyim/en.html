1) [Seif 1] Just as the community fasts, etc. This is from the wording of the Rambam, chapter 1 of the Laws of Fasts. He wrote this based on reason: Just as the community fasts and prays concerning its distress, so too every individual fasts and prays concerning his distress, in order to humble his heart and examine his ways so that he will return to Hashem, as explained in the Maggid Mishneh there.
