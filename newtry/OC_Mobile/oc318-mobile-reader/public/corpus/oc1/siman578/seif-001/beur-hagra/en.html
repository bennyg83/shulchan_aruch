(1) Seif 1 — just as [the congregation], etc. As written in chapter 1, the Tosefta: one who was [in distress], etc.; and regarding an individual it is discussed as explained above in siman 569.

(2) And not so. As written — and they interrupt, etc.; and in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Taanis halachah 13, and in Nedarim chapter Konam halachah 3: R' Yaakov bar Acha commanded the scribe — if it comes, if they ask you, they say to him: on all of them one fasts except Shabbatot, festivals, Rosh Chodesh, the days between Yom Kippur and Sukkot, Chanukah, and Purim.
