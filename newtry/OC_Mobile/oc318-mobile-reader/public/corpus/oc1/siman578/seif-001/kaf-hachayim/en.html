(1) [Seif 1] Just as the congregation fasts, etc. — this is from the wording of Rambam chapter 1 of Laws of Fasts; and he wrote thus from reasoning: just as the congregation fasts and prays for its distress, so too every individual fasts and prays for his distress, in order to subdue his heart and search his ways to return to the Omnipresent, as explained in Maggid Mishneh there — see there.
