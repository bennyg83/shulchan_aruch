Se'if 1 and blesses, etc. Rambam — Tosafot there s.v. safek posed difficulties for him; and furthermore, why should he not read on account of doubt, but rather read with the blessings, and because of "you shall not take [the Name in vain]"; and since we hold like R. Eliezer as Rif wrote and as written above — only Keriat Shema, and in Birkat haMazon, etc. But nevertheless there is difficulty, for Rambam ruled in Kessef Mishneh that on a doubt one does not bless, and so ruled Shulchan Arukh in many places; and see what is written in siman 488. And what Rabbeinu Yonah wrote — that it is an error, for Rambam disagrees with R. Yehudah; and so too Rosh at the beginning of chapter Kisuy haDam; and Taz erred in this as well. And it was already ruled in Shulchan Arukh siman 17 and Yoreh De'ah siman 28 and siman 265 like the words of Rosh and Rambam; and see Rashba siman 372, and Kessef Mishneh brought it. And all the more so according to what is written above that blessings are not on Keriat Shema — and I could say that it deals with one who knows he did not bless, and it teaches that blessings are not on Keriat Shema, as written above siman 60 se'if 2; but the wording "but if," etc., is difficult.
But, etc. As written — Keriat Shema is rabbinic; and the same for us in all blessings that are rabbinic, as Rosh wrote there and as written above siman 259; and the same regarding the blessings of Keriat Shema, as written in the mishnah there; and it is not, etc. — see Rashi there and as written in the Gemara there.
