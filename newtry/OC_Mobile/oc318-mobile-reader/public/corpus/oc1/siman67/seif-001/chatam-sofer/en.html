In Magen Avraham at the end of the siman: “It appears to me that this applies all the more so.” Note: I am hesitant about this, because the exodus from Egypt is not mentioned there, only the splitting of the Sea, whereas the Torah was particular that “you shall remember the day of your departure from the land of Egypt.” Our Sages merely added the splitting of the Sea as well. Therefore, Emes VeYatziv is preferable to the passage of tzitzis, but the Song at the Sea alone does not suffice. It further appears to me that if he did not intend to fulfill his obligation through reciting the Song at the Sea, he has not fulfilled his obligation, for we rule that mitzvos require intent.
