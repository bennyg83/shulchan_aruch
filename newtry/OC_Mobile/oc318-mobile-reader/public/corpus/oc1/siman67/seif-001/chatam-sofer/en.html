Gloss: And I question this, because Shirat ha-Yam is not mentioned there — only the splitting of the Sea of Reeds; and the Torah was strict: "so that you remember the day of your departure," etc., from the land of Egypt — but our Sages added also the splitting of the Sea of Reeds; and therefore emet veyatziv is preferable to parashat tzitzit. But Shirat ha-Yam alone does not suffice.
