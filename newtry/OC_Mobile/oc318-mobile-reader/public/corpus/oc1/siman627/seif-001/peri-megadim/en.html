In the airspace See the Taz, in siman 626, Taz letter 3, who discussed this at length. The Rav there, who equated much s'chach, etc., follows his own approach here, that the rule of sukkah beneath a sukkah does not apply to invalid s'chach; see there carefully. However, in the Tur here it implies that the reason is because of sukkah beneath a sukkah, as the Bach wrote, and so the Levush wrote; see there. Sukkah beneath a sukkah is also considered like invalid s'chach; see the Milchamot. See siman 628: with sukkah beneath a sukkah we require conditions, while invalid s'chach invalidates in every case, as written above in siman 626. With God's help, more will be explained in siman 627. See what I wrote in letter 2, with God's help, about a practical difference; see there, and in letter 4.
