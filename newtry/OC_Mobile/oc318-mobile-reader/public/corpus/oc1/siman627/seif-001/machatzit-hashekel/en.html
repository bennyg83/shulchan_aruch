(Se'if katan 1) If, etc. See siman 315 and you will see that there is a scribal error in his words. Meaning, the Agudah brought a proof from siman 315, but there it is written the opposite in se'if 11, in this wording: provided it is not lowered from the bed one tefach. However, the reason is explained there: since the canopy is spread for shade, the bed is made like a roof in the middle of the canopy's partition. What is lowered below is a partition. Nevertheless, that matter is not relevant here. It works well above in siman 315, where the subject is the prohibition of making an ohel on Shabbos; in such a case it is forbidden because it is an ohel. But here, where the subject is one who sleeps there and whether he is called not sleeping in a sukkah because an ohel interrupts, what difference does it make that under the bed is called an ohel? He is sleeping on top of the bed, and there no ohel interrupts. And if he sleeps under the bed, that is forbidden in any case, as stated at the beginning of the siman.
