Seif 1 — one who sleeps under a bed in a sukkah. It appears to me: we learn in the Gemara in Sukkah chapter 2 daf... one who sleeps under a bed in a sukkah does not fulfill his obligation; R' Yehuda says the custom is that we used to sleep under the bed etc. And behold Rashi and Raavad explained the reason that a bed is a temporary tent since it is moved, and the bed's hollow is not considered sukkah, and the bed itself is not four handbreadths wide to be a separation — and one who sleeps in the bed's hollow is like one sleeping in a house that is invalid because it lacks ten handbreadths. And there is to discuss whether the bed's hollow is considered like a house that is invalid and the sukkah above is valid, and he fulfills through the sukkah above — requires study.
