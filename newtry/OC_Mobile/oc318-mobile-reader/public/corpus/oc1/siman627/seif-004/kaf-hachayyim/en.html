4) Ibid. If it is ten tefachim high, etc. Even if the sides of the bed are extremely narrow and do not have even a width of one tefach, it nevertheless has the status of a tent. This is implied by Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1, and so wrote the Mishna Berurah, sec. 3. Therefore, one must take care not to sleep beneath a table that is ten tefachim high.
