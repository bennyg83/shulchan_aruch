Se'if 4: And if, etc. For the Mishnah there, regarding falling leaves, must be speaking within four tefachim. Since it states plainly, and if it is not, etc., it follows that anything not for beautification invalidates, like the first reasoning written in siman 626:2. See the end of siman 629.
And one must be careful, etc. See the Taz, and as written there on 14a, two sheets combine; the same applies here.
