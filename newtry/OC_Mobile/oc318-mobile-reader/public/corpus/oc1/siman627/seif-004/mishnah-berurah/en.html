(9) If he spread a sheet, etc. The same applies if he decorated it with other kinds of things that may not be used as s'chach, such as if he hung beneath all the s'chach foods and attractive vessels in order to beautify it.
(10) It is valid - For they are nullified to the s'chach, and it is permitted even to sit beneath them.
(11) And if it is four tefachim distant, etc. - Since they are four tefachim distant from the s'chach, they are considered independent and are not nullified to the s'chach. It turns out that he is not sitting in the shade of the sukkah, but in the shade of the sukkah decorations. Even if those decorations have more sun than shade, it is also forbidden. Some are lenient where their sun is greater than their shade.
(12) It is invalid - This is when they have a measure of four tefachim, which is like other invalid s'chach whose measure is this, as below in siman 632. If it is in the middle, it invalidates the whole sukkah for this reason, as explained there.
(13) And if it is not for beauty, etc. - See the Biur Halacha.
(14) Even though it is within four tefachim - The same applies even if it is not one tefach distant from the s'chach; he mentions four because of the first clause.
(15) And one must be careful, etc. - Meaning, although by law one need forbid only where there is a width of four tefachim, as above, nevertheless initially one should be careful even if it is not four tefachim wide, for there is concern that he may make many decorations until they have this measure and sit beneath them. According to this, one should not hang fruits, vessels, or similar items that are invalid for s'chach, even for decoration, except within four tefachim of the s'chach. Nevertheless, regarding hanging lamps in the sukkah opposite the table, one need not be stringent that they be within four tefachim; on the contrary, it is better and proper to distance them from the s'chach as much as possible.
