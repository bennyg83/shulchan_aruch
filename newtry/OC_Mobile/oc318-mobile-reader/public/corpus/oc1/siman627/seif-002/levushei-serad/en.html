There The sheets reach, etc. The sheets of the canopy slope, and therefore they are not considered an ohel unless it has a roof. About this he said that the slope of the sheets must reach the ground. But if there is a tefach between the sheets and the ground, then the sheets down to the ground do not create a slope, and it is considered an ohel. The Magen Avraham challenged this from siman 315; see there in the Taz, se'if katan 8, in the name of the Tur, that if there is a tefach in the upright part, it is considered an ohel. That is unlike here, where it is empty; how can it be considered a partition? Therefore he said that there is some scribal error in his words.
