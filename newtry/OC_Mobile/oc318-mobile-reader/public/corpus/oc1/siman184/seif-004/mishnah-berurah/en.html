(13) He must recite, etc. This means the entire Bircas HaMazon, including the fourth blessing, although it is only Rabbinic, so that people should not come to treat it lightly. Acharonim.
(14) Because of the doubt. He may also discharge another person who ate and did not recite Bircas HaMazon. Rabbi Akiva Eiger in his glosses, and likewise in his responsa, siman 6; and Ksav Sofer, siman 30.
(15) Because it is Torah-level. This applies specifically when he ate to satisfaction. If he was not satisfied, according to most poskim the obligation is Rabbinic. Nevertheless, a God-fearing person who is uncertain whether he recited Bircas HaMazon, even if he ate only a kezayis, should wash his hands, recite HaMotzi, eat another kezayis, and then recite Bircas HaMazon. Chayei Adam.
