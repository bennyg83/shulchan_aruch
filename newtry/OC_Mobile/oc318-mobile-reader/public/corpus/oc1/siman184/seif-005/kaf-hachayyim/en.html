5) There: If he recited the blessing in the place where he remembered, he fulfilled his obligation. Gloss: This applies specifically according to the Rambam, but according to Rosh, even after the fact he did not fulfill his obligation. See Taz, paragraph 1, who discusses this at length and concludes that as a practical matter we must rule leniently in cases of doubt concerning blessings. After the fact, in every case, he need not recite the blessing again if he already recited it in the second place. If he has not yet recited it, however, he should return to his original place even if he left unintentionally, provided that this is possible and he is not prevented by the group with whom he joined; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, similarly cites several authorities who hold that after the fact he fulfilled his obligation, and concludes that anyone who acts stringently in this matter is among those whose conduct is astonishing and recites a blessing in vain; see there. Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 2, Shulchan Aruch HaRav there, Chayei Adam there, and Derech HaChaim there rule likewise.
