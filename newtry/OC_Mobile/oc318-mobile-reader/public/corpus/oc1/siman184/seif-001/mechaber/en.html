To recite the blessing at the place of the meal; until when one may recite it; and one who forgot and did not recite it. It contains six se'ifim.
One who eats in one place must recite the blessing before he moves from his place. {Rama: (And see above, siman 178.)} And if he left his place and did not recite the blessing: if it was intentional, he returns to his place and recites it; and if he recited it at the place where he remembered, he has fulfilled his obligation. {Rama: This is specifically according to the opinion of the Rambam; but according to the opinion of the Rosh, who holds that even one who acted unintentionally must return to his place lechatchilah, then if it was intentional he does not fulfill his obligation even bedieved. [Tur]} And if it was unintentional: according to the Rambam he recites it at the place where he remembered, and according to Rabbeinu Yonah and the Rosh he too returns to his place and recites it.
