One who bows. Taz elaborated. See Levush; one may say another reason: in these there is no petition for one's needs — it would be unseemly, unlike prayer where one asks one's needs in the middle (see there). Therefore in the three openings — "Zokhreinu" and "Mi kamokha" — public needs, it is permitted to bow in the middle; not in "Atah Kadosh," in my humble opinion.
