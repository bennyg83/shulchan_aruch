One who bows. See Taz, who discusses this at length. See Levush. One might offer another reason: in these passages there is no request for one's needs, and therefore bowing is disgraceful. In prayer, however, where one requests his needs in the middle, see there. According to this, in the first three blessings, at Zochreinu and Mi Chamocha, which concern communal needs, one may bow in the middle, but not in Atah Kadosh. So it appears to me.
