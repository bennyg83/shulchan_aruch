Taz, seif katan 4 ‘And when it says, all agree.’ Sometimes the Gemara says ‘all agree,’ not meaning that they give thanks to someone, but that everyone concurs. In that usage it does not denote submission but praise, in that they praise this ruling. The Taz's concluding statement, ‘therefore in Hallel,’ and so forth, is not logically compelled. In Hallel and the other cases, even though all give thanks, they are thanking Hashem, so the sense of submission could also apply. Rather, the Taz means that since the term modim is sometimes found as an expression of praise, it is more reasonable to understand it similarly in Hallel and the other cases, and therefore one should not bow in them.
