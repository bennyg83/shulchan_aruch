Taz seif kaf dalet: And when he says "the whole world gives thanks" — he explained: sometimes the Talmud says "the whole world gives thanks," and it does not mean that they give thanks to so-and-so, but rather all are equal; then the language of submission does not apply but language of praise that they praise this judgment. And what the Taz concluded from this in Hallel, etc. — it is not necessary; thus it is in Hallel and the like — even though "the whole world gives thanks" means to the Holy One, blessed be He, and thus language of submission would also apply; rather the Taz's intention from this is: since we find the language of modim sometimes is praise, therefore also in Hallel and the like it is more reasonable that it is language of praise; therefore one should not bow in them.
