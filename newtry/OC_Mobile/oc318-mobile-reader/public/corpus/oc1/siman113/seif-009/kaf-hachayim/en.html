(9) [Seif 2] Those who have the custom to bow, etc. That is, those who have the custom to bow in the prayers of Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur when they say Zokhreinu and Mi Kamokha need to straighten up when they reach the end of the blessing — thus from the words of Tosafos, Berakhot page 12b, s.v. kara, and page 31a, s.v. u-motzi'o — that in all prayer they are accustomed to bow and not only in Zokhreinu and Mi Kamokha; and so too below in Shulchan Aruch siman 582 seif 4, and Levush there; and so too from the words of the Taz here seif kaf gimmel, as his language below seif 11; and not like Magen Avraham seif 2, who was forced in this — see there; and see Orach Chayyim siman 3.
