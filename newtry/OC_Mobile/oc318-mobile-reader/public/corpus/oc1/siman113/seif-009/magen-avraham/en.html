One may not add. And that which we say "true and established," etc. — it does not refer to "the the Master your Heaven" but to this matter [Tosafos page 12]. One who says "on a bird's nest may Your mercies reach" — they silence him, for the commandments of the Omnipresent are not [motivated by] mercy but decrees [Rambam ch. 9 of Prayer; Mishna ch. 5 of Berakhot]. And it says there that if he said "on the good may Your Name be remembered" — they also silence him, for a person is obligated to bless on the bad just as he blesses on the good.
