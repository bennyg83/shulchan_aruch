To add. And that which we say in any case "King of the world, God of Avraham," etc. — for in the chapter that began "to be God of Avraham" they had not yet all accepted His kingship — Chizkuni, parashat Chayei Sarah. And what he wrote "You are mighty forever" — he meant to say without any change or interruption because there is no old age before Him. "Who gives life to the dead — You," and not via an agent. "Who gives life to the dead with abundant mercy," as written in the first chapter of Rosh Hashana — three groups on the day of judgment (Rashi and Tosafos explain: when the dead will live) — "and abundant in kindness," He inclines toward kindness, etc. "Who keeps His faith to those who sleep in the dust" — they are the forefathers who sleep and did not die; He swore to give them the land and will fulfill His word at the time of resurrection. Or He keeps His faith to revive them even though they did not die, and He kills them one hour before the resurrection — see chapter HaPo'alim. "King — He kills and gives life" — just as when He kills from above, so He gives them life from above, and there is no need to bring down dew upon them — see Midrash HaNe'elam, parashat Vayera. "And faithful are You to revive the dead" — that He establishes them in their blemish so others will not say they are others' work — see what I wrote in responsa of Yoreh De'ah from siman 515 to 519.
