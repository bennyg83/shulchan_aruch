(8) There, but in their middles, etc. But the Rav Maharash Yafeh wrote in Yafeh Mareh, first chapter of Berakhot siman 18, that he hesitated to say that even in their middles one may not — from the precision of the Yerushalmi that is there — Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tashbetz. Magen Avraham seif kaf alef; and therefore one must be careful not to bow even in their middles except at a time of need — such as when the shaliach tzibbur reached Modim and he is in the middle of a blessing and must bow so he not appear as a heretic, as written in siman 109 seif 1; and see in our words there, letter 18 and letter 22.
