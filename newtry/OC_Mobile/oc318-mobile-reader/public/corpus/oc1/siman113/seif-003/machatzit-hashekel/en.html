(Seif kaf gimmel) Improper. That one should not bow, etc. And the distinction in this between that which is here versus the middle of blessings where it is permitted — see Taz.

And it is possible that he may bow slightly, as written at the end of siman 91. And there he wrote in seif kaf chet, and these are his words: and it is possible there is no strictness except when he prostrates from standing; and it is possible that when seated it is called a slight bowing, for one cannot bow until [the vertebrae] stick out, etc., when he is seated.
