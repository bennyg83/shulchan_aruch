Seif katan 3: Disgraceful. Because one should not bow, and so forth. Regarding the distinction between this case and bowing in the middle of blessings, which is permitted, see Taz.
It is possible that he should bow slightly, as written at the end of siman 91. There, in seif katan 8, he writes: ‘Perhaps the objection applies only when one bows while standing. It may be that bowing while seated is considered only a slight bow, since while seated it is impossible to bow until the vertebrae protrude,’ and so forth.
