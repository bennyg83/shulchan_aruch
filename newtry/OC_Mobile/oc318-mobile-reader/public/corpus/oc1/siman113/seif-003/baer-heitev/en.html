Improper. For one bows only in what the Sages enacted, and therefore one does not bow at "Yerushalayim" or at "Boneh Yerushalayim," unlike Maharil (Darkei Moshe). And perhaps one may bow slightly (Magen Avraham). And all this is specifically in the prayer of eighteen [blessings]; but in one who is not [praying] the eighteen, he may bow and prostrate as he wishes — and this is specifically when praying for himself. And it fits well with what is said in Arachin, that a person places him in one corner and finds him in another corner from excessive bowings and prostrations; therefore one does not bow at "U'vechol koma lefanecha tishtachaveh," etc., for since he is not saying it about himself but rather "upon the whole world it says they shall bow before Him, may He be blessed" (Taz, see there).
