Obscene. One may not bow except what the Sages enacted; and Maharil disagrees. And perhaps one may bow a little. Magen Avraham. All this is specifically in the eighteen-blessing prayer; but in voluntary prayer one may kneel and bow as he wishes — and this is specifically when praying for himself; and it works out in Rashi that a person places him in this corner and finds him in another corner from the multitude of kneelings and prostrations; therefore one may not bow in "before You every knee bows," etc., for since he does not say it about himself but that all the world says "they shall bow before Him," etc. Taz — see there.
