[3] This is disgraceful, etc. This requires study, for in siman 281 he wrote, ‘one need not bow,’ and perhaps he relied upon the ruling here that, since one need not bow, doing so is disgraceful. Lechem Chamudos, folio 43, writes that all the more so one may not bow at the beginning and end of other blessings outside the Amidah. The Taz does not appear to agree, though his words may perhaps be reconciled. See above, end of siman 51, and below, siman 139. Darchei Moshe writes that one should not bow at ‘and to Jerusalem Your city’ or at Boneh Yerushalayim, contrary to Maharil. I found this custom, however, in Tashbetz, siman 316, and Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 81, which states that one should bow not during the blessing but only at the final word of the blessing concerning Jerusalem. Magen Avraham implies that one should not bow as deeply as during the other required bows.
