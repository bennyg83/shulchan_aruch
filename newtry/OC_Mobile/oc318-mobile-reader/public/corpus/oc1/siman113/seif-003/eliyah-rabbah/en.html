[3] Behold this is improper, etc. Requires investigation — in siman 481 he wrote one need not bow, etc., and perhaps he relied here since one need not, behold this is improper. Lechem Chamudos page 43 wrote: it seems to me that since in any case one should not bow at the beginning and end in other blessings that are not from the Shemoneh Esreh — to here. And from the Taz it does not imply thus, and one can intend accordingly; see above end of siman 51 and below siman 139. Darkei Moshe wrote one should not bow at "and to Jerusalem Your city" and "who builds Jerusalem" unlike Maharil — to here. But I found thus in Teshuvos Hatashbetz siman 316 and in responsum of Maharam Mintz siman 81, and he wrote not in the blessing but only in the last word of the blessing of Jerusalem — to here. And from Magen Avraham it appears one should not bow as much as in other blessings.
