The Laws of Bowing in the Eighteen Blessings [i.e. Amidah]. Containing 9 S'ifim These are the
blessings in which we bow: in Avot [the first blessing], [at the] beginning and end; in Hoda-a [the second-to-last blessing], [at the] beginning and end. And if one comes to bow at the end of every blessing or at its beginning, we teach [that person] that one does not bow, but in their [i.e. the blessings'] middles, one may bow.
