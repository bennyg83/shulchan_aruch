Seif 1: These are the blessings, etc., at beginning and end. NB: Behold what one must intend in this is precious; my eye saw in Tikkunei Zohar (tikkun 18) that it is written: all who bow bow at "Baruch," and all who straighten up straighten up at the Name — he bows toward Adonai in Tzadik, of whom it is said "and King Solomon is blessed," and straightens up toward Havayah, to join for us both together, sufficing for yud-heh-vav-dalet-nun-heh-yud; and his words contradict one another, for afterward in this same tikkun he said: and they straighten up four straightenings corresponding to the four letters that are mine, which are Adonai, in the four that are Havayah — and it is proven to the contrary that bowings are toward the name Havayah, may He be blessed, and straightening up is toward the name Adonai. And behold, as his second words they are the essence, since it is implied from both places that the intention is to include both names together, namely yud-heh-vav-dalet-nun-heh-yud; if so, the letters of Havayah must come first with the name Adonai included within them, so that judgment be included in mercy and not the reverse — that mercy be included in judgment. And behold, there seems to be a hint to the combination of these names in that the world was created with ten utterances and the Torah was given with ten statements; behold the beginning of building the world was in the six days of Bereishit, and the completion of the world's existence was at the giving of the Torah, as the Sages said on the verse "on the sixth day" that the whole world was hanging on the sixth of Sivan, when the world was perfected. If so, behold the Sages said that at first it arose in thought to create the world with the attribute of judgment; He saw the world could not endure and joined with it the attribute of mercy. The intention is that at first He wished to create it with the name Adonai, may He be blessed, which indicates judgment; and afterward He joined with it the attribute of mercy, namely the name Havayah, may He be blessed — and that is, Adonai is included in Havayah, may He be blessed, which is the name yud-heh-vav-dalet-nun-heh-yud, may He be blessed and exalted. And since this name has yud at its beginning and yud at its end — and this is the name in which the world was perfected and was first created with ten utterances and at the end with ten — as a hint that through this the world was perfected, that its beginning is yud and its end is yud. And behold, although my soul is not worthy to understand secrets, nevertheless I did not refrain from raising this in writing; "one who increases and one who decreases," etc. — examine well.
