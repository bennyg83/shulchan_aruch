(א) And in Modim, beginning and end — that is, at the beginning of Modim and at the end when he concludes "the Good One — Your Name."

(ב) That he not bow — so that one will not come to uproot the enactment of the Sages, lest they say each person is strict as he wishes; and also we are concerned for yohara, that he holds himself more proper than the rest of the congregation.

(ג) But in their middles, etc. — and thus many later authorities concluded, unlike Yafeh Mareh, who shows one stammered to forbid.

(ד) One may bow — for we need not be concerned that they will uproot the Sages' enactment thereby, since the Sages did not enact bowing in the middle of any blessing at all.
