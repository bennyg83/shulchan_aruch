And at the end. One must intend when bowing in Avot at the beginning for the first letter of the name Havayah and the first letter of the name Adonai, namely ya; and at the end intend heh-dalet; in Modim intend vav-nun; and at the end intend heh-yud. Before he prays he should feel his tefillin of the head, close his eyes, and place his hands one upon the other (the Ari, of blessed memory).

To bow. And there are those who stammer — this is forbidden. Magen Avraham.
