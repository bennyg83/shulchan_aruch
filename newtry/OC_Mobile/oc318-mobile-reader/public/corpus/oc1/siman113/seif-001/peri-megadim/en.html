They teach him. Taz: Tosafos Berachos 34a; and Beit Yosef explained that these are two resolutions, and so Taz, and I explained in sign 2. But Ner Zahav explained one resolution: they enacted that a commoner does not bow like the High Priest at the end of every blessing — yohara; and originally there was one resolution, that he would resemble the High Priest; but in the middle he is permitted and there is no yohara for a king. One may say: a king, since he bowed at the beginning, does not straighten up until the end; but a commoner bowing in the middle alone does not resemble a king. See Rambam chapter 5 of Hilchos Tefillah 10 in Kesef Mishneh; and for Lechem Mishneh, whence did he derive that the High Priest [bows] at beginning and end — for if not, at beginning or end why would they teach him? But at the end it is not similar to the High Priest, etc., and so Ner Zahav.
