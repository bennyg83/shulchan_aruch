Seif 9: One should not add, etc. NB: Behold I saw in responsa Gur Aryeh Yehudah, Orach Chayyim part (siman 105), that he brought in the name of the Peri Megadim in a responsum that he raised difficulty: why in "Elokei Avraham, Elokei Yitzchak" is vav not stated, but in Yaakov it says "ve-Elokei Yaakov" — see there what he wrote on this. And behold the Peri Megadim's answers are not to be examined; and in truth the verse is full in parashat Va'era: "to Avraham, to Yitzchak, and to Yaakov," and vav is stated with Yaakov and not with Yitzchak — see in Zohar there what he wrote, because of a hint that Yaakov is the essence (see there). And from this grew the language of prayer clearly to distinguish Yaakov with vav; see also what I wrote on the reason of the matter in my chiddushim already, and in my chiddushei Torah of the year 552 from parashat Vayeitzei — what more he wrote on another reason per the words of the Zohar there, and the words of the Zohar in parashat Va'era (see there); also in Zohar Vayikra on the verse "and a soul that sins" there is another reason why vav is not stated — because we need to include Yitzchak in Avraham (see there) — examine well. And now it further appears: behold the Holy One, blessed be He, called Yaakov the firstborn, as written in the midrash in parashat Bo, in parashat Kadesh Li Kol Bekhor, and in prayer "congregation of Yaakov, Your firstborn son" — the intention in this is: behold in the redemption from Egypt it is stated first Avraham, etc.; but in the future redemption it says "and I will remember My covenant with Yaakov," etc. — behold Yaakov is stated first; if so, the Holy One, blessed be He, made him firstborn to the Patriarchs, and this will not be fulfilled until the future to come; then Yaakov will become firstborn to the Patriarchs. Until now Avraham was the head. And behold it is known that vav adds to the first matter; therefore at first they begin to say "Elokei Avraham, Elokei Yitzchak" as it is now in this time, and afterward they say "ve-Elokei Yaakov," who is destined to be first before Avraham; therefore vav is stated, to interrupt and say that in him is a matter on its own, and in the future they will begin with him first in the future to come to be the firstborn to the Patriarchs — so it appears; examine well. And it further appears to say per what is written in tractate Bava Metzia p. 87, that Eliyahu said it is impossible for all of them to stand together, for they would increase mercy and the Messiah would come before his time (see there). If so, likewise in saying "Elokei Avraham" Avraham is aroused above, and likewise Yitzchak, and likewise Yaakov; and if one mentioned all of them in combination, all would be aroused together above and would bring the Messiah before his time. Therefore one mentions Avraham by himself and Yitzchak by himself; and in Yaakov — even if Yitzchak and Yaakov stand together — they will not bring the Messiah before his time, since each is one relative to the other: Yitzchak is judgment and Yaakov is mercy. But if Avraham were also there, there would be two mercies and one judgment, and they would overpower the judgment; for even though this one is on this side and that one on that side, with Yitzchak in the middle they would sweeten the judgment and bring the Messiah before his time. Therefore they mention Yitzchak without vav — and it fits well; examine well. And see in my composition Chokhmat Shlomo on the Torah, with God's help, parashat Shemot, year 554, what he wrote per the above, with God's help — examine well.
