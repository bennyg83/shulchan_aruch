(2) There, in Avot at the beginning and end, etc. One must know to intend the Name Havayah that straightens up to the Name Adonai in this manner: for in the first bowing one should intend that the yud of Havayah straightens up to the alef of Adonai; and in the second Barukh one should intend that the hei of Havayah straightens up to the dalet of Adonai; and in Modim one should intend that the vav of Havayah straightens up to the nun of Adonai; and at the end one should intend that the final hei of Havayah straightens up to the yud of Adonai. And one who does not intend in it — they said in Tikkunim, Tikkun 18, that about him it is said "The the Master has given me into the hands of those I cannot rise against" — Peri Etz Chayyim, Shaar HaAmidah ch. 6; Atzei Chayyim siman 1; Kaf HaChayyim in Laws of Prayer; Chasdei David siman 1. And if he intended on it, it is said "then you shall call and the the Master will answer" — for "then" (az) is gematria 8, like the number of the two names mentioned above — Kaf HaChayyim siman 15, siman 12. And he explained: for in the first bowing one should intend that the yud of Havayah illuminates to the alef of Adonai in order to straighten it up; and in straightening up one should intend that the alef of Adonai rises to join with the yud of Havayah — and so one should intend in the others.
