[6] From HaEl HaGadol, etc. R. David Abudraham wrote: the intention — HaGadol corresponding to the world of angels because the Name is the greatest of all; HaGibbor corresponding to the world of spheres because the Name revolves the sphere with strength; HaNora corresponding to the world of actions, that through His awesome deeds done with inhabitants of houses of clay it is recognizable that He is awesome. Rambam wrote ch. 9 of Laws of Prayer: one who said in supplications "He who showed mercy on a bird's nest," etc., or "that He slaughter it and its offspring in one day — may He show mercy on us" and the like — they silence him, because these mitzvot are decrees of Scripture and are not [motivated by] mercy — to here. One who says "on the good — may Your Name be remembered" or "may the good bless You" — they silence him — Mishna ch. 3 of Megillah; see in Eliyahu Zuta.
