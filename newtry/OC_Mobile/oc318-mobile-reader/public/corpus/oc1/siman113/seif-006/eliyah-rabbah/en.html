David Aboderam wrote the great intention against the world of angels per the fact that the name is the greatest of all, the hero against the world of the wheels per the fact that the name turns the wheel with valor, the terrible against the world of deeds which by its terribleness it does to the dwellers of material houses it is evident that it is terrible. The Rambam wrote Chapter 9 of the Laws of Prayer. He who said in supplications, "He who has mercy on a bird's nest, etc., or slaughters him and his son in one day, will have mercy on us."
