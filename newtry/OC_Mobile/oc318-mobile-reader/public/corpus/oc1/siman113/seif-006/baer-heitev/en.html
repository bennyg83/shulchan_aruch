Quickly. When he says "Baruch" he should bow at his knees, and when he says "Atah" he should bow until the vertebrae protrude (Magen Avraham in the name of the Zohar and Shelah). And in Mishnat Chasidim he wrote in the name of the Ari, of blessed memory: when saying "Baruch" he bows his body, and when saying "Atah" he bows his head (see there). And likewise in the kavanot of the Ari, of blessed memory: at "Baruch" he bows his body, and at "Atah" he bows his head (see there).
