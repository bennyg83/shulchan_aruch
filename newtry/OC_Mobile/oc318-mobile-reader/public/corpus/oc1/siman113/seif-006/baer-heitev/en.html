Quickly. When he says "Baruch" he shall bend his knees, and when he says "Atah" he shall bow until his vertebrae crack. Magen Avraham in the name of the Zohar and Shelah. And in Mishna Chassidim he wrote in the name of the Ari z"l: when he says Baruch his body shall bow, and when he says Atah his head shall bow — see there; and likewise in the intention of the Ari z"l: in Baruch his body bows, and in Atah his head bows — see there.
