(Seif kaf vav) And one who comes, etc., as written in Yoreh De'ah, etc., that Rama wrote there: if princes or priests who serve idolatry in their garments pass before one, he should stand before them or remove his hat and bow before they come, so it not appear he is bowing to idolatry but to the official. And some are lenient in the matter, since it is known that even gentiles do not remove their hat and bow to idolatry but to the official; and it is good to be stringent like one view — so far Rama. But if there is danger in the matter he wrote there before this, and these are his words: some say that whatever is not forbidden except because of marit ayin — if there is danger in the matter it is permitted, and this is not [a case of] "be killed and do not transgress" — so far.
