(7) There, we teach him that he should not bow. And Tosafos wrote: "and he bowed" — so what? And they answered: they did not come to uproot the words of the Sages, so that each one should not say he is stringent as he wishes, and there is no enactment of the Sages here. And they wrote further: the Taz — because it appears as arrogance, meaning that he holds himself more kosher than the rest of the congregation; and so too R. Yona in Beit Yosef, Orach Chayyim siman 2; and see Taz and Magen Avraham siman 1.
