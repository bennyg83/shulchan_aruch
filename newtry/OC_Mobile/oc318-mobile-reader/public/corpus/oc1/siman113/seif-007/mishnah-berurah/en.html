(א) When he bows, etc. — when he says "barukh" he bows at his knees; when he says "atah" he bows until the vertebrae protrude; when he says "modim" he bows his head and body like a reed all at once and stands thus in bowing until [the word] "leolam" and then straightens up.

(ב) Straightens at the Name — as it is written, "Heaven straightens the bent."
