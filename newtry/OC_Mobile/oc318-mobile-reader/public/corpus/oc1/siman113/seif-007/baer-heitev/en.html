At the Name. The Rema MiPano asks in the first chapter of Berachos, siman 19: On Yom Kippur, when the kohanim heard the Name, they would bow, and so forth. See there and Magen Avraham. In the Baer Heitev before me, an answer to this question was brought in the name of the Taz. See there. I say that not only is this falsely attributed, since the Taz did not raise this question, but even what he presents as the Taz's answer is no answer at all and reflects a lack of understanding. The Taz wrote those words regarding a different matter. See there, seif katan 4; I copied his words in seif katan 3.
