At the Name. The Rambam asked in ch. 1 of Berachos siman 19: behold the priests in the Temple when they heard the Name would bow, etc. (see there). And in Magen Avraham and in Baer Heitev that is before me he brought an answer on this in the name of the Taz (see there). And I say: not only that he answered falsely that the Taz did not bring this question (see there), but also that which he answered is in the name of the Taz — it is not an answer, and lacks understanding; for the Taz wrote these things on a different matter (see there sec. 4), and I copied his words in sec. 3.
