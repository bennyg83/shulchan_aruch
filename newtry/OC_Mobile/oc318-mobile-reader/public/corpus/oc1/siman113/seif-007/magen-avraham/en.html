When he bows. When saying Baruch, he should bend his knees; when saying Atah, he should bend until the vertebrae protrude. Zohar, Parashas Eikev; Shelah.
And when he straightens. The Rema MiPano asks in the first chapter of Berachos, siman 19: On Yom Kippur, when the kohanim heard the Name, they would bow, and so forth. See there. See also Zohar, Chayei Sarah, page 114, which implies that this law applies specifically at Baruch. See there, and see what I wrote in siman 126.
