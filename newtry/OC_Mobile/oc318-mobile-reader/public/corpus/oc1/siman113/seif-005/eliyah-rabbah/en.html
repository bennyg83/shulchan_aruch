[5] He bows at Barukh, etc. When he says Barukh he makes a bowing at the knees, and when he says Attah he prostrates with his head until they stick out, etc. — the rulings of Tosafos; and so wrote in the Zohar, parashat Ekev. Rashal wrote in commentary on Semag: Barukh is language of "bless you" — you bow; and it implies in Modim he bows body and head at once. See in responsum of Beit Yaakov siman 124.
