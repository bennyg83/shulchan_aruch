Quickly. Ba'er Heitev. And in responsa Beit Yaakov he wrote also that he bows his body at "barukh" and his head at "atah." And Mishna Berurah wrote that what he criticized in Imrei Tzufah from the Yerushalmi may be rejected, and per Beit Yosef the students of the Ari wrote — and it is the wording of Ra'aya Mehemna, parashat Ekev; and see there that the one who straightens up straightens at the Name — the head first and then the body; see there.
