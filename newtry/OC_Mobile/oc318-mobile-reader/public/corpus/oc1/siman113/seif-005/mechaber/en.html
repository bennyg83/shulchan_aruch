One should not bow so much that one's mouth is opposite the belt of one's trousers. If one is old or sick and cannot bow until [the vertebrae] protrude, once one inclines (i.e. lowers) one's head it is sufficient, since it is recognizable that one wished to bow but finds it difficult for oneself.
