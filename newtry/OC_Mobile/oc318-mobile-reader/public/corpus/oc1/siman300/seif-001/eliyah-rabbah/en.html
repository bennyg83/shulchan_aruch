[1] He should arrange, etc. Taz explains that this refers to preparation, such as spreading a tablecloth, while the meal itself may follow his ordinary practice. Piskei Tosafot states that one should make an effort to eat the type of food he especially desires even if it is expensive. If he desires many foods, he should seek them according to his means, but should prepare from each only approximately what he expects to eat. He should not measure too narrowly, but allow generously. The same principle applies to all three Shabbos meals, contrary to Bach. Magen Avraham writes that the Gemara implies that it is proper to cook meat or another dish specifically for Motzaei Shabbos. In our times, when the third meal is eaten so late that one cannot eat another full meal on Motzaei Shabbos, the fourth meal may be fulfilled with fruit. I heard that if the third meal continues until after nightfall, one need not eat another fourth meal on Motzaei Shabbos. Shelah writes that one who fasts on Motzaei Shabbos, such as one beginning the fast before sunset, should trouble himself to prepare this meal for others and should study the teachings and laws concerning the meal.
