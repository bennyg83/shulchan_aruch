Seif 66 — If one is ill, etc. — for this is no worse than one traveling on the road; and as is written in the Pesikta, and they brought it in the Yalkut on the parsha "Speak in your hearts" — the Holy One blessed be He said: I said that one should pray in the synagogue, and if you are unable, pray on your bed, and if you are unable, meditate in your heart, as it is said "Speak in your hearts," etc.; and this is what "if [one is ill]" means:
