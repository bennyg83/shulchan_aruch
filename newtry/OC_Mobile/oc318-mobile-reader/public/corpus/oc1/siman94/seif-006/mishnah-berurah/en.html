(20) A sick person prays — and an elderly person who cannot stand sits in his place and prays; and if he can stand at the place of bowing so that he bows from a standing position, he should stand there.

(21) He may have [unfocused] thoughts in his heart — and it is possible that one fulfills [his obligation] thereby bedi'eved, and he need not return and pray again even if he is healthy at the time of prayer, since he is now under duress; see Peri Chadash and Peri Megadim; and see above in siman 62 seif 4 in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halacha there; and if he cannot direct his mind he recites Shema alone if possible for him, for the intention of Shema is only the first verse and he can easily direct his attention.
