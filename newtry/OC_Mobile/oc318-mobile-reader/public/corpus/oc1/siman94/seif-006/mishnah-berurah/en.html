A sick person prays. An elderly person who cannot stand should sit in his place and pray. If he can stand at the places of bowing so that he bows while standing, he should stand there.
He should think in his heart. It is possible that he fulfills the obligation with this after the fact, and need not return and pray, even if he recovers during the time of prayer, since he is now under compulsion. See Peri Chadash and Peri Megadim, and see above in siman 62, seif 4, in the Mishnah Berurah and Biur Halacha there. If he cannot focus his mind, he should read Shema alone if possible, since the intent of Shema is only for the first verse, and he can easily have intent for that.
