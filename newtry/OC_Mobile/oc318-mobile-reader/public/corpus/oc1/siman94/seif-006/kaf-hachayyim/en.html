(6) [Se'if 2] If he prays facing another direction, he should angle his face, etc. Magen Avraham explains that 'angle' rather than 'turn completely' concerns a place whose custom fixes that direction, or one riding a donkey; Shulchan Aruch HaRav agrees. Maamar Mordechai explains it as one facing north to become wealthy or south to become wise, who must nevertheless angle his face toward Eretz Yisrael. Eshel Avraham objects that this would not explain why the Rema states what the Mechaber already taught. Neveh Shalom therefore explains that some circumstance prevents him from facing Eretz Yisrael fully; for example, the congregation has begun and no suitable place remains. He should at least angle toward Eretz Yisrael, which is easy. Without such cause he must face Eretz Yisrael; if he wishes to face north or south, he should only angle his face that way.
