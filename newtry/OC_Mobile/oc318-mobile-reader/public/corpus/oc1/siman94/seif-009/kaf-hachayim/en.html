(9) In the synagogue — where they positioned the Holy Ark toward the south of the world and everyone prays toward the Holy Ark which is toward the south — one may pray toward the east even though the entire congregation prays toward the south; and there is no concern here of yohara or enmity — Yad Eliyahu siman 1; and one great authority there disagreed with him and wrote that it is not proper if he prays with the congregation and turns to another side; he should only pray toward the side toward which the congregation prays, see there; and they brought this in Hagahos Beir Yitzchak, Shitah Mekubetzes there; Imrei Shalom on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 18 ot tet-zayin; Chesed LeAvraham ot dalet — and Chesed LeAvraham wrote there: "Look and see how much those err who place the Ark toward another direction among the directions because of adornment of the synagogue — and this is not adornment but disgrace, to enter this dispute; and one who wishes to be stringent in a mitzvah should make the Ark and eastern wall toward the southeastern corner of the world if he can — and that is its adornment, and that is the honor of the place, and not for the sight of man shall one judge, but he should desire before Hashem" — according to Tehillim "this is all of man."
