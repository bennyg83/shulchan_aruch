Standing. Taz writes: We have never seen great authorities who prayed while seated on the road pray again upon arriving home or at an inn; see there. It therefore appears that here too he should not pray again unless he is certain that he can concentrate during his prayer, as written at the end of siman 107. Rema MiPano, Knesses HaGedolah, and Peri Chadash rule likewise, and Responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 1, agrees. See there for his objection to the Taz.
At an inn. It is stated in Shabbos 127 that Rabbi Yehoshua did not wish to enter the house of a non-Jew while wearing tefillin; all the more so regarding prayer.
A corner. If he cannot withdraw from the road and will not find a secluded corner at the inn, praying on the road is preferable. Nevertheless, he should see whether he can pray at the inn, in a courtyard, in a field, or in a garden, for that is preferable. Magen Avraham.
