Standing. Taz wrote: we never saw from the great ones who prayed on the road while sitting that they prayed again when coming to their homes or to an inn — see there; it seems here too he should not pray again unless he is sure he can have proper kavanah in his prayer, as written in siman 107; and so wrote Rama mi-Fano and Shach and Peri Chadash; and likewise agreed in responsa Yad Eliyahu siman 1; and see there what he wondered about Taz.

In an inn — as stated in Shabbos page 127, that R' Yehuda did not want to enter with tefillin into a house of idolaters — how much more so to pray.

Corners. And if it is impossible for him to leave the road, and in the inn he will not find a corner — prayer on the road is preferable. Nevertheless he should see if he can pray in the inn, or in a courtyard, or in an open field or garden — better. Magen Avraham.
