One who was compelled to pray sitting — when able he must return and pray standing, and he need not add anything.One traveling near an inn: if able to withdraw from the road where travelers will not interrupt, he should withdraw and not pray in an idolater's inn lest household members distract him. If unable to withdraw where they will not interrupt, he prays in the inn in any corner (Terumat HaDeshen siman 6; Beit Yosef siman 90).'.
