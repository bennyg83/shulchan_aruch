Must return — and similarly in Sha'agat Aryeh; and Ma'aseh Rokeach wrote in siman 19 that specifically as a voluntary prayer did Rav Ashi pray a second time; see there. And from Rashi's wording it implies that because he could not concentrate the first time since he was at the time of the derashah, therefore he would pray again; and possibly for this reason travelers are accustomed not to pray again.

So that they not interrupt him — meaning that it is nearly finished so he can pray standing without interruption.

And one should not pray in an inn — for it is stated in Shabbos 127b that R' Yehudah did not wish to enter with tefillin into a house of idol worship; how much more so to pray.

In some corner — so that the household members will not distract him; and one need not be concerned about idols since all their prayers were in towns full of idols (thus Terumat HaDeshen). And there is doubt if he cannot withdraw from the road and must pray while walking or sitting; and also in an inn he will not find a corner where he will not be distracted — which is better: prayer standing is better, or on the way is better so he can concentrate more. And it seems that on the way is better, for Rashi explains the reason that prayer standing is better because one can concentrate more — and here we see that on the way one can concentrate more. Nevertheless he should see if he can pray in the inn in a field or courtyard or garden — that is better and he fulfills both opinions.
