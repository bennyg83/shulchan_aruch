(25) Seated — and likewise one walking on the road.

(26) When he can — namely that its time has not passed.

(27) And he does not need, etc. — meaning this is not like one praying as a voluntary offering, for we hold below in siman 107 that one must add something new in it; and for practical law the later authorities agreed that he need not return and pray again; and so is the custom that travelers who prayed on the road seated do not return and pray again; nevertheless if he wishes he can pray again as a voluntary offering if he is confident he can direct his heart in his prayer as written in se'if katan 107, and he need not add something new in it, for this praying now standing is like sufficient novelty, as in Magen Giborim; and from the words of Peri Megadim it seems it is not worthwhile to pray today as a voluntary offering.

(28) From the road — to go to its sides and pray there standing.

(29) In a gentile inn — for certainly there are idols and abominations there, and one should not bring holy matters there; for Moshe Rabbeinu said as I leave the city, etc., and he did not want to pray in Egypt because it was full of idols.

(30) That household members should not disturb him — should read at the end after the word keren zavit (corner), meaning he should be concerned only to find a corner that household members will not disturb him, but not be concerned about idols, for all our prayers are in towns full of idols. And if on the eastern wall idols are hanging there, he should not pray toward that side but toward another side even though it is not east. And if he cannot leave the road and must pray walking or seated, and also in an inn he knows he will not find a corner that will not disturb him, it is better to pray on the road seated or walking since then he can direct his attention more; nevertheless if he can pray in the inn in an open area or courtyard or garden it is better, and he fulfills both — he prays standing and with proper intention.
