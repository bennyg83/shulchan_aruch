Sitting. The same applies while traveling on the road.
When he is able. That is, provided that its time has not passed.
And he need not, etc. Meaning, this is not like praying as a voluntary prayer, for we rule below in siman 107 that one must introduce something new in it. In practice, the later authorities agreed that he need not return and pray a second time. Such is the practice, that travelers who prayed on the road while sitting do not return and pray a second time. Nevertheless, if he wishes, he may pray a second time as a voluntary prayer if he is certain that he will direct his heart in his prayer, as written at the end of siman 107; and he need not introduce something new into it, for the fact that he is now praying standing is itself considered an innovation. So wrote the sefer Magen Gibborim. From the words of the Peri Megadim it seems that nowadays it is not worthwhile to pray a voluntary prayer.
From the road. To go to the side of the road and pray there standing.
In an inn of non-Jews. For it certainly contains idols and abominations, and one should not bring a matter of sanctity there, for Moshe Rabbeinu said, "When I leave the city," etc., and did not wish to pray in Egypt because it was full of idols.
So that the members of the household will not disturb him. This should be placed at the end, after the words "a corner," meaning that he should be concerned only to find a corner where they will not disturb him, but should not be concerned for the idols, since all our prayers are in towns full of idols. If idols are hanging on the eastern wall, he should not pray toward that side, but toward another side, even though it is not east. If he cannot leave the road and must pray while walking or sitting, and he also knows that in the inn he will not find a corner where they will not disturb him, it is better to pray on the road sitting or walking, since then he can have more intent. Nevertheless, if he can pray at the inn in an open field, courtyard, or garden, that is better, and he fulfills both: he prays standing and with intent.
