[9] Nevertheless, he should contemplate, etc. See Levush, siman 101, seif 2, and above, siman 62, seif 4. Sefer Tzeidah LaDerech writes that David said, “May the words of my mouth be acceptable,” when I am healthy, “and the meditation of my heart,” when I am ill. Kesef Mishneh writes that an elderly person who cannot stand should sit in his place and pray. If he can stand at the places of bowing so that he bows while standing, he should stand.
