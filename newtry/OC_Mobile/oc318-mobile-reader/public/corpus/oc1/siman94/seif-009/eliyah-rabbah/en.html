In any case he should meditate, etc. — See Levush siman 101 se'if 2, and see above siman 62 se'if 4. Sefer Tzidah LaDerech wrote that this is what David meant by "May the words of my mouth be acceptable" — when I am healthy, and the meditation of my heart when I am ill.
