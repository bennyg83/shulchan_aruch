(22) Not to lean, etc. — the reason is that prayer requires standing as below in siman 98; and standing by leaning is not considered standing; and on this view leaning somewhat is permitted, namely that he leans partially such that if that thing were removed he would not fall; but some say the reason is that it must be with awe, and on that view in all cases it is forbidden, and in a place of pressure one may be lenient per the first reason. And see below in siman 101, paragraph 1, and what he wrote there.

(23) To stand — and therefore he should beware not to lean himself on a stand; and likewise in every place where standing is required, such as when he sees a sefer Torah before him and everything similar.

(24) At the time of prayer — and a sick person who is told that he will be able to direct his attention through standing by leaning should do so, and if not he should pray seated.
