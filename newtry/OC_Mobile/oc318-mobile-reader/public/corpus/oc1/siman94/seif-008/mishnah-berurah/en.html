Not to lean, etc. The reason is that prayer requires standing, as below in siman 98, and standing by means of leaning is not considered standing. According to this, slight leaning, meaning that he leans partially so that if that object were removed he would not fall, is permitted. Others say the reason is that it must be with awe, and according to this it is forbidden in every manner. In pressing circumstances one may be lenient according to the first reason. See below in siman 141, seif 1, and what is written there.
To stand. Therefore he should be careful not to lean on a shtender; and likewise in every place that requires standing, such as when he sees a Sefer Torah opposite him, and similar cases.
At the time of prayer. A sick person who knows clearly that he will be able to have intent while standing by means of leaning should do so; if not, he should pray sitting.
