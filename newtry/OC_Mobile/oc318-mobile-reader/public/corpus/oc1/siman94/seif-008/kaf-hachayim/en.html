(א) There, gloss: And we turn our faces to the east, etc. — Because one must pray toward the east, they accustomed to fix the heichal with the sefer Torah in it in the eastern wall; however, even if they fixed the heichal in another wall, the worshipper must return his face to the east — Rosh Chacham HaLevi of blessed memory in responsum 21; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos Taz; Avnei Tzedek ot gimmel; Magen Avraham se'if kaf-gimmel; Eliyah Rabbah ot bet, siman 92 ot alef; Razah ot bet; Shitah Mekubetzes 109b:

(ב) And if one prays in a house of idolatry and idols stand in the east — it is forbidden to pray toward that side but toward another side, even though it is not east — Chayei Adam klal 22 ot yud; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 18 end of ot yud; and see below ot lamed-vav, and above siman 90 se'if kaf-gimmel:
