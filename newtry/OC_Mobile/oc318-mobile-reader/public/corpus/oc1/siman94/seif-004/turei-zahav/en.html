And likewise if he was on a ship, etc. — meaning: in a wagon or ship he can stand, for the wagon does not move so quickly in speed, and the ship is very wide so there is no anxiety comparable to when he stands inside it; and one can say: what helps him that he stands in the wagon or ship, since the wagon or ship is moving — it is as if he is walking himself, as they said one riding is considered like one walking; and one can say that sitting in a wagon or ship differs from riding, for in the first chapter of Bava Metzia they say regarding acquisition of a moving courtyard that a ship is not considered a moving courtyard, for a ship stands still and the water moves beneath it; and Tosafos challenged from what they said one riding is like one walking, and answered that truly a ship is also considered walking for other matters, only for acquisition it is not called walking, for it is like his hand that stands still and the body is what moves it; if so, it is difficult here that the wagon and ship should be like walking — and what helps that he stands inside it? One must say that here for prayer they did not require praying standing except so there be awe of the Divine Presence upon him, and with this it suffices even standing in a wagon and ship, although he is not called standing from the movement of the wagon and ship, as above.
