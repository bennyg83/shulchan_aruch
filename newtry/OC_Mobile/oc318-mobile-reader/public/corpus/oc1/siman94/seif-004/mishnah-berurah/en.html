(16) And settled mind — if he preceded the caravan and waits for them and has someone to hold his animal, he goes down and prays; and all the more so when walking on foot, for he must stand and pray, for in this standing his heart will not be disturbed since otherwise he would need to wait for the caravan.

(17) To stand at Avot — for in the blessing of Avot the intention is to pause, as below in siman 101; and one walking cannot direct his attention as much; and likewise one riding on an animal should stop it until he finishes the blessing of Avot, but he need not dismount from it.
