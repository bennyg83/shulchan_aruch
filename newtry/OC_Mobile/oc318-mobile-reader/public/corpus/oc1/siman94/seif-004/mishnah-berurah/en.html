While traveling. Because delay on the road is difficult for him, and his mind is not settled because of it.
If he can stand, etc. Meaning, he is not sitting, but standing on his feet; nevertheless, he moves with the movements of the ship or wagon as they go in their normal way.
And if not, he sits, etc. In all this, he should turn his face toward Eretz Yisrael if possible.
And his mind will be settled. If he went ahead of the caravan and is waiting for them, and he has someone to hold his animal, he should get down and pray. Certainly, when he is traveling on foot, he must stand and pray, for by standing in this way his heart will not be distracted, since in any event he must wait for the caravan.
To stand during Avos. For in the blessing of Avos, intent is indispensable, as below in siman 101, and one who is walking cannot have such intent. Similarly, one riding on an animal should stop it until the end of the blessing of Avos, but he need not dismount from it.
