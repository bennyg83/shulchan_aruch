Likewise. See Taz, who asks what benefit standing has over sitting, since one is considered walking in either case. He answers that while seated there is no sense of awe. According to what is written in Magen Avraham, letter 14, standing while moving is preferable to sitting, analogous to a korban, as explained in siman 98, letter 15. Should one say that his question concerned standing without moving, this is incorrect, for the wording does not imply it at all, and awe of the Shechinah can exist while moving. The Mechaber in seif 9 mentions specifically one who prayed while seated, implying that one who prayed while walking need not repeat. See what I will write in letter 5, with Hashem’s help.
