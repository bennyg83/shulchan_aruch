And likewise — Taz questioned: what does standing help more than sitting, for walking counts [as standing?] and walking while seated is one [thing]? He answered: sitting lacks awe; and according to Magen Avraham 14 standing while walking is preferable to seated, similar to korban in siman 98 seif 15; and you would also say standing without walking is difficult for him — this is confusion, for the language does not imply this at all; also awe of the Divine Presence can be said while walking; and the Mechaber holds in se'if tet specifically seated, but walking need not return — Mishna Berurah wrote in comment 5 if God wills.
