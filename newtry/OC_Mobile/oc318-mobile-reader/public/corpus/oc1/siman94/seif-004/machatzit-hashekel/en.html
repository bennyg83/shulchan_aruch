(Section comment 4) Toward [the sun] — toward the southeastern corner of the world; for we sit in the northwest of Eretz Yisrael; therefore if the eastern wall of the synagogue would be toward the southeastern corner of the world, we turn our faces directly toward Eretz Yisrael; and likewise this is not the way of heretics who stand directly toward the east — and thus is Rama's intent in the gloss.
