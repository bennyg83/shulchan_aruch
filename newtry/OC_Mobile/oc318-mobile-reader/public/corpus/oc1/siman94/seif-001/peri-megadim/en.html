To the direction — Taz and Magen Avraham comment 2, and what he wrote that others are not praying — it was not as Bar Kappara [said], for a righteous person [as in] Sanhedrin 97 even within a synagogue, even if he actually turns his face away from the congregation, he is not called wicked, as Bar [Yochai] etc.; and if it occurred that he erred thus in a synagogue it is possible he need not stop; nevertheless an obvious matter and walking is not so much of an interruption, therefore he should turn toward the congregation.
