Toward a direction. See Taz and Magen Avraham, letter 2. What he writes, that others are not praying, is not entirely clear, for in siman 90, seif 7, even inside a synagogue, if one turns his face completely away from the congregation he is not called wicked; see there. Therefore, if one erred in a synagogue, it is possible that he need not interrupt. Nevertheless, the matter is conspicuous, and walking is not such a significant interruption, so he should turn toward the congregation.
