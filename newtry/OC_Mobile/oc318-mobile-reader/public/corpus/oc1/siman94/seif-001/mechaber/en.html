One must face toward Eretz Yisrael, and the law of one riding or sitting on a ship; and
it has 9 seifim. When he rises to pray: if he was standing in the Diaspora, he turns his face toward Eretz Yisrael and also directs [his heart] toward Jerusalem, the Temple, and the Holy of Holies. If he was in Eretz Yisrael, he turns toward Jerusalem and also toward the Temple and Holy of Holies. If in Jerusalem, he turns toward the Temple and Holy of Holies. If behind the kapporet, he turns his face to the kapporet.
