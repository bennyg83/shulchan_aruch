In the Diaspora, etc. Meaning, when he stands in the Diaspora to the east of Eretz Yisrael, he should direct his face westward, which is opposite Eretz Yisrael; and when he stands on the western side, as in our countries, he should turn his face eastward, and so too in all directions. This is also the explanation of what he writes below: if he was standing in Eretz Yisrael, he should turn his face, etc.; if he was standing in Jerusalem, he should turn his face, etc.
Opposite Eretz Yisrael, etc. As it is stated, "and they will pray to You by way of their land."
And he should direct, etc. Meaning, he should direct his heart even though he cannot turn his face toward them; that is, he should think in his heart and mind as though he is standing in the Mikdash in Jerusalem, in the place of the Holy of Holies.
Opposite Jerusalem. As it is stated, "and they will pray to Hashem by way of the city that You chose."
His face toward the Mikdash. As it is stated, "and they will pray toward this house."
Behind the Kapores. Meaning, on the western side of the Beis HaMikdash.
His face toward the Kapores. As it is stated, "and they will pray toward this place"; and he should see himself as though he is standing before the Kapores.
