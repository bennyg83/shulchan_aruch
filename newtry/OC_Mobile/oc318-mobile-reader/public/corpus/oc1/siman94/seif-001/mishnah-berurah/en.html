(1) In the Diaspora, etc. — meaning: when standing in the Diaspora toward the east of Eretz Yisrael, he directs his face west, which is toward Eretz Yisrael; and when standing from the west as in our countries, he turns his face east; and likewise with all directions. And likewise is the explanation of what he wrote below: if he was in Eretz Yisrael he turns his face, etc.; if in Jerusalem he turns his face, etc.

(2) Toward Eretz Yisrael, etc. — as it says: and they shall pray to You toward their land.

(3) And he shall direct [his heart], etc. — meaning he directs his heart even though he cannot turn his face toward them; meaning he thinks in his heart and mind as if he stands in the Temple in Jerusalem in the Holy of Holies.

(4) Toward Jerusalem — as it says: and they shall pray to the the Omnipresent toward the city that You chose.

(5) His face toward the Temple — as it says: and they shall pray toward this house.

(6) Behind the kapporet — meaning in the west of the Temple.

(7) His face toward the kapporet — as it says: and they shall pray toward this place; and he should envision himself as if standing before the kapporet.
