(Taz se'if kuf-alef) It does not refer precisely to those who sit. For Rama alone refers to this and not the author; and if the author also referred to this, he would have written "toward a direction among the directions except west." But since it refers generally to all countries — therefore for those who sit in the north of Eretz Yisrael he would need to write "except north," and for those in the south "except south," and he would need to extend greatly; therefore he wrote generally "toward any direction," and its meaning is: in any case where his face would incline — that is, anything that is not directly opposite the side toward which he must pray, such as west to [true] west, and so for all:
