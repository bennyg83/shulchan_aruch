(Taz, seif katan 1) It does not speak specifically of those who dwell there. The Rama alone speaks of them, not the Mechaber. Had the Mechaber also spoken specifically of them, he would have written, “toward any other direction except west.” Since he speaks generally of all countries, however, for those north of Eretz Yisrael he would have needed to write “except north,” and for those south of it, “except south,” requiring great length. He therefore wrote generally “toward any direction,” meaning any direction in which angling the face is possible, namely whenever one is not facing directly opposite the direction in which he should pray, such as west relative to westward, and similarly in every case.
