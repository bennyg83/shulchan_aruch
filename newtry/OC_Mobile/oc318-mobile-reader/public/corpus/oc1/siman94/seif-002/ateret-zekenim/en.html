And we turn our faces to the east because we dwell in the west of Eretz Yisrael, and one who sits in the west turns his face to the east, as stated in the Gemara in Berachos there. All these lands in which we are dispersed are all opposite the northwest of Eretz Yisrael and are not aligned directly with the west of Eretz Yisrael. Therefore it is good and proper when they build a synagogue that they make an eastern wall where they place the aron kodesh and pray toward it, so that it inclines somewhat toward the southeast, from Maharam of Italy. And one who wishes to fulfill the saying, He who desires to become wealthy, let him face north, etc. — that is only if he sits on the south side of the aron kodesh; then he may face north. And likewise if he sits on the north side he may face south, but not the reverse, because then it would appear as turning his back and not his face toward the aron kodesh (thus it seems to me from the words of Mateh Moshe). Rather only then does he turn his face to the east alone, and it suffices for him with bowing that he bows with a slight inclination (Lechem Chamudos).
