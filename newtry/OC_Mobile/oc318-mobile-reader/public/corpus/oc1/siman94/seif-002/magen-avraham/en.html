If praying — meaning in a place where their custom is thus, or sitting on a donkey.

And we who turn [our faces] — therefore we position the heichal to the east; and even if they positioned the heichal on another side, one prays toward the east (thus in the name of R' Shimon haKatan).

Toward the sunrise — see the Levush's diagram to position an eastern wall toward the southeastern corner of the world.

He should turn his face — and Rashi explains the opposite, that he turns his face to the north or south, but his primary standing is toward the east.
