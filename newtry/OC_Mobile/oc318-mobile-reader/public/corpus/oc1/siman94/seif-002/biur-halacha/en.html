One does not make the place of the Ark, etc., but only direct toward the middle of the day — See Mishna Berurah, and here is the language of the Levush: Know that all these countries in which we dwell are all opposite the northwestern [corner] of Eretz Yisrael, and they are not directed toward the west of Eretz Yisrael precisely; therefore it appears that it is good and correct when building a synagogue to be careful that they make the eastern wall where they place the Ark and pray opposite it so that it inclines somewhat toward the southeast; and then we will stand directed toward Eretz Yisrael and Jerusalem and the synagogue and the Holy of Holies. Also, through this we will not lend support to the hands of the heretics who say we pray toward the sun like them. In what manner should we position it in these countries? It appears we should position it such that on the day of the tekufah of Nisan or the tekufah of Tishrei, or close to them on the 6th or 7th day, when the sun rises in the morning it should enter through the window in the middle of the eastern wall of the synagogue, and the ray should strike opposite it on the western wall — the ray inclined from the middle of the western wall toward the south, far from the middle, such that about half an hour or an hour after sunrise it reaches the middle of the wall precisely opposite the eastern window; and in such a synagogue it certainly stands in these countries precisely opposite Jerusalem and the Beis HaMikdash. But if we position it such that when the sun returns on those days through the eastern window the sun's ray strikes the western wall precisely opposite the window in its middle immediately at sunrise — that is precisely toward the east, and that is their custom to do so — we also do not prostrate toward Jerusalem and the Beis HaMikdash but only toward their side; therefore we do not position a synagogue in this manner. End of his words; see there further. And Bach wrote that all his words are included in what was written in the gloss on Shulchan Aruch that we direct toward the middle of the day; and so it is implied in the Prisha and Machatzis HaShekel.
