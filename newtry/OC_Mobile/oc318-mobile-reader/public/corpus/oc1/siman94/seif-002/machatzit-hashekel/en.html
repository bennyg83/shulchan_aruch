(Section comment 2) If praying — meaning in a place where their custom is thus; it troubled him why one truly prays toward the other directions; therefore he wrote that the local custom is thus and he does not wish to change from the custom; but in truth the custom is not according to law, as R' Yehuda b'Yakar said in the name of Mahari"a that there are places that practice thus. And Taz wrote: if through error he stood to the north or south, there is this remedy to turn his face toward the east. But if he positioned himself with his face to the west, it is impossible to fix by turning his face, for still his face will not be toward the east — he need not uproot his legs for this and stand toward the east; and one may rely on this position in the chapter "One shall not dig" that the Shechinah is in every place.
