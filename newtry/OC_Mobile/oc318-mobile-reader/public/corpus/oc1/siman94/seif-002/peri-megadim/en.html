Against — Taz: they stand in the synagogue not exactly toward the east but tilted somewhat southward; therefore the rabbi's place in the synagogue is on the south side of the ark, and one need not be stringent, for in any case so it is; and one who makes [his position] exactly toward the east, it is good to make the rabbi's place to the north of the holy ark so he will face (and be wise), for in the south he appears as turning his back on the holy ark; also one who does as the Levush, he cannot face north for he appears as turning his face away from Eretz Yisrael, especially when equal; and Eliyah Rabbah and Taz — see Olat Tamid and in 668 that the place of the righteous one should be on the south of the holy ark — Eliyah Rabbah.
