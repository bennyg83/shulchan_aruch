If one prays toward one of the other directions, one turns one's face toward Eretz Yisrael if in the Diaspora; toward Jerusalem if in Eretz Yisrael; toward the Temple if in Jerusalem.And we who turn our faces eastward, because we dwell west of Eretz Yisrael and our faces are toward Eretz Yisrael (Tur and Semag). We do not make the place of the ark and direction of prayer exactly opposite sunrise because that is the way of heretics; rather we direct toward the middle of the day (Hagahot Alfasi haChadashim). One who wishes to fulfill their saying — "who wants wealth faces north, wisdom faces south" — nevertheless should turn one's face eastward.
