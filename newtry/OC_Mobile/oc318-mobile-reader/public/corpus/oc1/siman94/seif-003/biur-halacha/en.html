One who cannot determine the directions, etc. — And it appears it is good that he turn his face to one of his sides; and if so, if he is standing toward the east, automatically the turning is to the north or south. And if he is standing toward the north, if he turns to his right toward the east it is obviously good; and if to his left, which is west, he has not lost in any case. And similarly in the south, the reverse. All this I brought out from Sefer Shulchan Shlomo.
