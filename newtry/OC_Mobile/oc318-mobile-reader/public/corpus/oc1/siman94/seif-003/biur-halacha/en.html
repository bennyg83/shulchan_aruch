One who cannot determine the directions, etc. It appears preferable that he angle his face toward one of his sides. Thus, if he stands facing east, the angling will naturally be northward or southward. If he stands facing north, angling to his right, toward the east, is certainly good; if he angles to his left, toward the west, he has at least not lost. The reverse applies when he faces south. I derived all this from Shulchan Shlomo.
