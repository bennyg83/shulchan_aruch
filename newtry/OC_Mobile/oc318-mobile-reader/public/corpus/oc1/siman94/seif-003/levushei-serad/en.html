(Taz se'if heh) Prayer standing is preferable — meaning this case in siman 89: when one rises early to go on the road, to pray in his house and read Shema on the way when the time arrives — because prayer standing is preferable to adjoining redemption to prayer; thus holds Tanna Kamma. And Rabbah holds he should pray while walking, for adjoining redemption to prayer is preferable; and we say there that the father of Shmuel and Levi acted like Tanna Kamma:
