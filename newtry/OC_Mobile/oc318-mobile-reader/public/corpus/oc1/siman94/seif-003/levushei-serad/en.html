(Taz, seif katan 5) Prayer while standing is preferable. This is the same issue discussed at the end of siman 89 concerning one who rises early to travel: he should pray at home and recite Krias Shema on the road when its time arrives, because prayer while standing is preferable to juxtaposing redemption to prayer. This is the view of the first Tanna. Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar holds that he should pray while traveling, because juxtaposing redemption to prayer is preferable. The Gemara states there that the father of Shmuel and Levi acted like the first Tanna.
