And he said, “He who prays in his house against Hashem, even though he is his father’s house, that he does not care for him.” “And not in the midst of the Lord’s Prayer, but in the midst of the Lord’s Prayer, the Lord’s Prayer, is not to believe in Hashem’s Word, and to the people who are told that they will be able to be blessed with them.” He will do so, and if he does not pray, he will be blessed with him
