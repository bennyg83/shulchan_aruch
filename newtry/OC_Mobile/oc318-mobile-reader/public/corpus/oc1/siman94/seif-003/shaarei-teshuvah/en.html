Eastward. See Baer Heitev. Responsa Pe'er HaDor of Rambam rules that one who prays properly in his home facing Eretz Yisrael does not demean the synagogue even if his back faces the wall of his house that is also the synagogue wall. He is not included in the prohibition against praying behind a synagogue. Birkei Yosef cites this. Regarding Shulchan Aruch, se'if 8, 'He should not lean, etc.': A sick person who is certain he can concentrate while standing with support should do so; otherwise he should pray seated. Birkei Yosef in the name of Maharim.
