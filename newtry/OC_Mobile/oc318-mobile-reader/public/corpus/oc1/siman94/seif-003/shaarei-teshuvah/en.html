To the east — Avodas HaTammid; and see responsa Pe'er HaDor to Rambam: one who prays in his house toward Eretz Yisrael properly, even though his back is toward the wall of his house which is the wall of the synagogue, there is no disrespect and he is not in the category of one praying behind the synagogue — see there; and they brought Berkei Yosef: in Shulchan Aruch seif 8 not to lean, etc., and a sick person who is told he can direct his attention through standing by leaning should do so, and if not he prays seated — so in Berkei Yosef in the name of Maharim, see there.
