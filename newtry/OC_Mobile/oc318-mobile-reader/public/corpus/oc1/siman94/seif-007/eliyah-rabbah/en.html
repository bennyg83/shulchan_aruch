If he can position [the wagon], etc. — Meaning a wagon moving slowly and gently, which is comparable to a wide ship — he should stand; and it is not comparable to a rider going on the road, for here even when standing he is not delayed on his way, as here he is in a wagon or on a ship. And see above at the end of siman 89; and that standing in a wagon or ship helps even though he is like one traveling — resolve: since they did not require him to pray except so that there be awe of the Shechinah upon him, through this he is called standing.
