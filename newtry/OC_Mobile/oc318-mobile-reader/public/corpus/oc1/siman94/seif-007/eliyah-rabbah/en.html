[7] If he can bring it to a stop, etc. Meaning, from the movement of a wagon traveling gently and from fear of the water. In a broad ship he should stand. This is unlike one riding and traveling on the road, for here standing does not delay his journey, since he is in a wagon or ship. See above, at the end of siman 89. Although a wagon or ship is moving, standing is effective because the requirement to pray while standing is only so that awe of the Shechinah should be upon him, and in this respect he is considered standing. See above, siman 2, seif katan 4.
