Although he is seated. Meaning, although he did not stand during the rest of the prayer because of fear, one might have thought that at the places of bowing it is preferable that he remain seated. The Shulchan Aruch therefore says, “Nevertheless,” so that he may fulfill the bowings.
He should turn the animal. Alternatively, he should turn himself around upon his animal, in the manner he would conduct himself when taking leave of a king. Ibid.
