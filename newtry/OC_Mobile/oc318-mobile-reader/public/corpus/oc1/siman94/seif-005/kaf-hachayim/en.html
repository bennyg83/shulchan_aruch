(5) There: If he was standing behind the Kappores, etc. — Explained: in the west of the Beis HaMikdash; Magen Avraham se'if kaf-alef, that is, at the western wall from outside — Pischei Teshuvah ot dalet. But in the Beis HaMikdash it is forbidden to stand in this time, since we are all tamei mes and we do not have ashes of the parah adumah — Razah ot alef; and see Rambam Hilchos Beis HaBechirah chapter 3; and now that the Beis HaMikdash is destroyed and we do not know in which place was the ramp and in which place was the ezras nashim — anywhere from the Beis HaMikdash it is forbidden to enter, even if he is pure from other tumos and has upon him only tumas mes alone; and see in the Mishnah, chapter Bnei HaIr, Megillah 28a, and in Midrash Shemos Rabbah chapter 2:
