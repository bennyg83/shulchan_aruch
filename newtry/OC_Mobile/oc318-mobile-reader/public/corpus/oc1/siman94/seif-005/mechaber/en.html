If one is sitting on a boat or wagon: if able to stand at the place of bowing, one stands so as to bow from standing and take three steps.Even though sitting for the entire prayer, nevertheless if possible one stands to fulfill bowing and steps properly (Beit Yosef in the name of S"Ch). If not possible, e.g. riding on an animal, one guides the animal three steps backward and it counts as if he stepped himself (Hagahot Alfasi haChadashim, end of chapter Tefilat haShachar).
