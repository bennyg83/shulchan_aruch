Required — Taz: and according to what was written in comment 4 and Magen Avraham 14, one can say the Mechaber's ruling is that there is a distinction between walking, where he need not return and pray, and Yaakov seated who returns and prays; and Rav Ashi [holds] like the first Tanna; and for halacha Magen Avraham and Taz and Peri Chadash agreed that even seated he fulfilled bedi'eved (and the vav in the word should read as Levi); and truly Rambam chapter 4 Laws of Prayer halacha 1 implies all eight matters and milah included do not invalidate bedi'eved, see there; and likewise such is in the name of Rama of Fano brought by Peri Chadash; and for the Mechaber "when he can" means its time has not passed, or he completes it in the adjacent prayer which is like under duress.

What the rabbi wrote in Rama's gloss seif 6 — he may have thoughts in his heart — one can say bedi'eved he fulfills since he is under duress; and Peri Chadash — and there is no need to elaborate.
