4) One who opens the door of a room in which a lamp is burning, and the movement of opening the door unintentionally extinguishes the lamp, is not considered to have inadvertently desecrated Shabbos. To what is this comparable? To one who locks his house to guard it and thereby finds that a deer is also confined inside. Nevertheless, it is proper for him to contemplate repentance through fasting or charity because he unknowingly caused the extinguishing, since ab initio he was not permitted to open the door until he could see that opening it would not cause the lamp to be extinguished, in accordance with the present law. Petach HaDevir, paragraph 2. The phrase 'through fasting or charity' means that he should fast for one day. If he cannot fast, he may redeem the fast with money by donating the value of his meal to charity, as stated below in siman 568, seif 2; see there.
