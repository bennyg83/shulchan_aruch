(ד) One who opens — the door of the room in which the candle is lit inside, and through the motion of opening the candle is extinguished without intent — this is not called desecrating Shabbat in inadvertence. To what is this similar? To one who locks his house to guard it and finds a deer preserved inside — nevertheless it is good to contemplate repentance through fasting or charity for having caused the extinguishing unknowingly, since l'chatchila he was not permitted to open the door until his eyes would see that he would not cause extinguishing through his opening, as is the law below. Petaḥ HaDevarim, note 2 — and what he wrote through fasting or charity means that he should fast one day, and if he cannot fast he may redeem it with money by giving the value of his meal to charity, as written below in siman 568, seif 2, see there.
