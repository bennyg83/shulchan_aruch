(ג) There — gloss: as usual. Magen Avraham wrote in note 1 that it is a scribal error, for even though he opens gently, nevertheless the wind blows and it is forbidden. This gloss should be at the end of the seif, when it is fixed in the door itself, etc., it is forbidden to open and close it as usual, but gently is permitted, see there. This appears to be the view of the Levush and the Rashaz, who did not copy the word as usual. And so too Chayei Adam there. But Eshel Avraham, Turei Zahav, Netiv Chayim, and Mateh Yehuda wrote to explain the word as usual — per their words, if he opens gently it is permitted; nevertheless, since there is a dispute in this, one should be stringent, and only in a situation of hardship may one be lenient.
