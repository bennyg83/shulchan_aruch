3) There: And the crossings of the Arnon streams, etc. The Gemara there explains that this was a known deep passage between two mountains through which the Jewish people had to pass. The Emorim hid there and wished to kill them. A miracle occurred: the mountains joined together and killed them all. The miracle of the hailstones was that large stones were cast from heaven. Regarding the miracle of the stone that Og sought to cast, he said: How large is the camp of the Jewish people? Three parasangs. I will go uproot a mountain three parasangs wide, cast it upon them, and kill them. He went, uprooted a mountain three parasangs wide, and carried it upon his head. The Holy One, blessed is He, brought ants upon it, which bored through the mountain, and it fell around his neck. He wished to remove it, but his teeth extended to this side and that side, and he could not remove it. This is the meaning of the verse, 'You broke the teeth of the wicked,' etc. The wall of Yericho sank into its place; see there. Olas Tamid, paragraph 1.
