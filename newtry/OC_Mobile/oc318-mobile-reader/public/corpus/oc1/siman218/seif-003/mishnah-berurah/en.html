(11) From thirty to thirty — apart from day he saw and apart from day he stands on it now.

(12) And then they etc. — and before that it is forbidden and his blessing is in vain.

(13) Obligation — and do not challenge from blessing on thunder and earthquakes and blessing on perfumes that one blesses each time even same day — see above siman 217 and siman 227 — for there is different for there he blesses on new thunder and lightning; and similarly perfume blessing on new smell that constantly wafts — not so here where nothing new occurred from time he blessed; therefore not considered for blessing unless much time passed — thirty days.
