(3) There and crossings of Arnon streams etc. Explained in the Gemara that it was a known place — a depth between two mountains where Israel needed to pass and Amorites were hiding to kill them and a miracle was done that the mountains stuck together and all were killed. Miracle of hailstones thrown from heaven — large stones. Miracle of stone Og sought etc. — Israel's camp is how many — three parasangs — I will uproot a mountain three parasangs and cast it on them and kill them; he went and uprooted a mountain three parasangs and brought it on his head; and the Holy One brought grasshoppers who bored holes and it descended on his neck; he wanted to pull it off — his teeth stretched to this side and that and he could not pull it off — and this is what is written two evildoers You broke etc.; and wall of Jericho swallowed in its place — see there.
