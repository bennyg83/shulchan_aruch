[3] [Levush] For there is one who says etc. Bach and Lechem Chamudot daf 61 wondered — for in Horayot daf 5 the dispute of R' Meir and R' Yehudah and the halachah follows R' Yehudah that one tribe is called congregation; and so ruled Rambam chapter 5 Laws of Unintentional Sin offerings — see there; but truly in chapter 7 of Laws of Passover he did not rule so; and Magen Avraham answered that specifically regarding court instruction we hold like them for congregation is written explicitly in the verse — until here. Proof seems to me from Yerushalmi chapter HaRo'eh that halachah was not decided here — see there; and as written in Shiyurei Kenesset HaGedolah; see there. But this requires study — what Lechem Chamudot and Ma'adanei Melech proved from Tosafot who wrote that one does not bless on a place where Sennacherib's camp fell since the miracle is not recognizable in the place — for at sea crossings one does not bless except the person for whom the miracle was done; and Ma'adanei Melech inferred that he holds one tribe is called congregation — for if you do not say so it would follow that it is not all Israel — until here. But when we examine it seems to me that for this Tosafot inferred and wrote that Hezekiah is not less than a designated person — meaning that for a designated person even though not a majority of Israel he is fit to bless — and easy to understand. If so, on the contrary proof from Tosafot that a tribe is not called congregation.
