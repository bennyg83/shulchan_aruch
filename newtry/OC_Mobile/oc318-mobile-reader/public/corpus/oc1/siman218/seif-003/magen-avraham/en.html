From thirty. And requires study if he saw them on day 30 — such as he saw on day 1 and at end of four weeks saw on day 2 if he must bless; and from what is written siman 422 in name of Ra'avad it implies he need not bless; and it seems to me it depends on dispute between Rambam and Ra'ash in siman 100 (in Tur) — for there too it is in the Gemara chapters as here; and if so per what was ruled there in Shulchan Aruch he must bless; and possible there ruled stringently but here doubt in blessings to be lenient; and possible we require thirty days apart from day he saw and day he stands on it — such as if he saw on day one we require he see on day four; and so in Levush and Ra'avad it implies he blesses on this.

For thirty days. And you might ask on thunder and earthquakes and smell of perfume why bless each time — one may say there he does not see the same thing itself; and smell of perfume even though it is the same thing — nevertheless since his enjoyment is similar to eating he blesses each time. And Raavan wrote that smell is not the thing itself for smell increases and passes; and so Abudraham end of gate 8 — see there siman 217.
