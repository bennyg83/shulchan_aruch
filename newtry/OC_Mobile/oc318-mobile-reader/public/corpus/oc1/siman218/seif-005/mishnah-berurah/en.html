(18) All other places — this is specifically for individual; but for miracles of the multitude each one when reaching place of miracle need not mention all places where miracles were done for Israel; and also for individual itself Magen Avraham wrote specifically he himself — but his son is not obligated to mention all miracles done to his father.
(19) And include all — and must mention first the place he stands on and then other places. And know that although he included other miracles — nevertheless if afterward he encounters place where other miracles were done for him he blesses again even same day.
