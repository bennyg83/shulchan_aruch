[5] [Levush] For our forefathers etc. Lechem Chamudot wrote my mind is not at ease that even if they are many they can say only for our father — until here. It seems to me the Levush also agrees to this but deals when there are sons from many people for whom a miracle occurred — easy to understand. Further it seems to me if they are two sons of one elder they can say for our forefathers — for their father is included in the miracle of their father's father since there is partnership; and per this a son alone can say for my forefathers; and so implies Rambam and Semag and Semak (and Nissim Gaon) and Tosafot Rabbenu Yonah and Remazim — for ostensibly their words are wondrous; and per what I explained it is settled — see there for I abbreviated; and in Lev Av siman 87 I saw that a son says to my elder.
