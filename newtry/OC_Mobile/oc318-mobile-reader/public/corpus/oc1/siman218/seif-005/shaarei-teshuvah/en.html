See Baer Heitev and Avnei Nezer who decided son and grandson even if born before; and other descendants even if born after; and in Birkei Yosef decided like Magen Avraham and so implies from conclusion of poskim — there is one who says grandson blesses further; and in Machatzit HaShekel brought in name of Meiri in his rulings manuscript that girsa reads son and son's son and so in She'iltot — and specifically son and son's son because they are his generation — see there; and Avnei Nezer and Yosef and wrote in Sulam BeVilah from words of Zohar and Shelah — one for whom miracle was done — good and fit that on that day each year he seclude to thank rejoice and tell somewhat of miracle; Birkei Yosef brought; and I saw in Rosh Hashanah system for Rambam at end it states Rambam accepted to seclude on day of trouble each year and day after to rejoice — Charedim brought this; and similarly Gaon author of Turei Zahav and other great ones when they left distress for relief.
