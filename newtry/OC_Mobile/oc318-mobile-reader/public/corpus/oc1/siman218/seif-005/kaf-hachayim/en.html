(5) There and all with mention of God's name and kingship — to exclude Raavad who holds that although in other blessings we require God's name and kingship, in blessings of seeing since they are only praise and thanksgiving in general — God's name and kingship are not required. Tur and Beit Yosef; and see Birkei Yosef letter alef who brought support for Raavad's words — see there; but Tosafot and R' Yonah and Ra'ash and Rashba and Mordekhai chapter HaRo'eh all agreed with Rambam that in blessings of seeing also we require God's name and kingship; and they wrote that explicitly so in Yerushalmi; and so Ra'ash in responsum that all our teachers wrote so and also practice is to mention God's name and kingship; and one need not heed Raavad's words in these matters — end quote; and Bach brought all this — see there; and so is the opinion of Achronim to bless with God's name and kingship. And see above siman 17 letter bet and examine.
