One for whom many miracles were done etc. This matter is in Gemara beginning chapter HaRo'eh — Mar brei deRavina was going in market of crossings was thirsty for water a miracle was done a spring of water came and he drank. Another time he was going in street of Mechoza a mad camel fell on him he was saved — wall fell and he entered inside because of camel; when he reached crossings he blessed Blessed Who performed miracle for me at crossings and with camel; when he reached Mechoza he said Blessed Who performed miracle for me with camel and at crossings. Ra'ash wrote from here implies individual for whom many miracles when he reaches one of places he includes all other miracles with it. It is difficult why need to learn from implication — for explicitly so in Gemara. And further Tur wrote thus — and it is as translated here. It is difficult why attribute these words to Ra'ash when explicit in Gemara. It seems Tur was challenged by his father's words from what is written and answered on this that it teaches us — not explained in Gemara that he includes miracles on one place not other places as ostensibly Ravina said at crossings and with camel — for he did not mention place of camel i.e. Mechoza — it teaches certainly must mention also other places; and camel he said is Gemara's language meaning place of camel; and this proven from that afterward he said at crossings and with camel — and this at crossings perforce he did not say so for there he was — rather certainly place of crossings; here too place of camel; therefore Tur inferred all other places etc. — meaning Who performed miracle for me at this place and at place X; body of miracle need not mention — as above — correct.
