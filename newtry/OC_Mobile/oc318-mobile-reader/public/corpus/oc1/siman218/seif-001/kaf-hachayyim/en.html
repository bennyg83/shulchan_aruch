1) [Seif 1] One who sees a place where miracles were performed, etc. This implies that the blessing is recited specifically in cases like those listed, where the miracle is evident from the place. Otherwise, such as the area surrounding Yerushalayim where Sancheriv's camp fell, no blessing is recited, as Tosafos and Mordechai write. Over one's own miracle, however, one recites the blessing even if it is not evident from the place. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. See Padeh Es Avraham, under mem, paragraph 20, who writes in the name of Kometz Minchah that one who passes through the locations mentioned in the journeys of the Jewish people must recite, 'Blessed is He Who performed miracles for our ancestors in this place'; see there. Orach Chaim, paragraph 1.
