Such as crossings — and it is written in Sifra Kippurim and Parashat Vezot HaBerachah and zayin from crossings and so appears in Perishah that one does not bless until he sees the miracle in the place itself as one who sees the sea end of siman 61; if he did not see the place of the crossing he does not bless; and so one who sees the Jordan until he sees the place of crossing; and it is written and the Arnon crossed before them and thus we began Arnon; and in every place he sees part of them or part of the Jordan he is obligated to remember Heaven's might and His salvation upon us and Yehuda and Yissachar like kaf kaf [Rashi Shabbat in the name of Malchut] and thus one who sees his miracles a place where Israel was saved whether miracle or salvation for the tribes; and in the crossing of the sea it is explained in Sifra that Eliyahu was on the mountain and the tribes below for the need of the crossing of the tribes; and there is no doubt that even if the miracle was only for Eliyahu one blesses on it even though it was not for the tribes; and what Rambam wrote one sees a place where miracles were made — thus also from outside one may bless if he recognizes the place of the miracle distinctly as we wrote; and in the crossing of the Jordan it is written in Sifra Kippurim and Parashat Vezot HaBerachah and Rashba in the name of Rambam that one blesses on the place where miracles were made; and in crossing the sea Rashi in the name of Malchut that one blesses on the place; and from all this one may say that when one sees from afar the place of the miracle he blesses; and in crossing the Jordan it is written the priests stood etc. — perhaps only when the ark stood there was the miracle; and from Joshua's language it implies the miracle was in the crossing itself; and when Joshua circumcised only the people of war circumcised not women and children; and perhaps one may still say that in crossing the Jordan the miracle was also for women and children; and in crossing the sea it is written in Sifra that the tribes were below and Eliyahu on the mountain; and from Joshua it implies the miracle was for all Israel who crossed; and when the ark stood the water piled up — the miracle was for all who crossed; and from the verse the waters stood etc. one may learn the miracle was in the place of crossing; and requires study.

And the stone Og sought etc. — see Chayei Adam what I wrote; and per Peshat there is less wonder regarding such a man who was among the giants and his father is of those Scripture says the sons of God came to daughters of man; and as we say in Niddah 61 Sichon and Og were sons of Achiyah ben Shamchazai; and see Rashi there that he and Uzael are the angels who descended in the days of Enosh; and thus their strength was akin to angels' strength.

He blesses miracles were made — and it is explained in poskim that one need not specify what the miracle was; and since it says He made miracles at this place — see R' Yehuda in Yerushalmi and Tosefta and thus the sages; and in crossing the sea Rashi in Malchut.

At this place — and it appears one may bless only as long as he sees the place; but if he already passed and does not see it — the interruption in his blessing as in re'im lekaman siman 227 seif 3; and thus the law in one who sees a person who was saved from danger if he already passed and we do not see him we do not bless; and perhaps one who passed may still see the person or the place from afar within thirty days as all have not passed thirty days from first sight; and if so even on first sight he blesses; and even without blessing at first sight the High Priest is obligated to bless on Pri Etz Hadar; and many poskim hold that when he sees again within thirty days he blesses; and Magen Avraham siman 227 seif 8; and therefore when the place is still visible one may bless; and if the place changed greatly from what it was at the miracle — requires study.
