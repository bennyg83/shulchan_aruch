Seif 2 — on a miracle made for part of Israel — whenever it was not made for all Israel; and even if made for part of tribes one does not bless on it. It appears in Magen Avraham what he wrote on this until the end; but at the end it is not written 'majority' but 'all'; and Maharik end Sharvit Kasav for Rashi; and it appears thus from Massekhet Yoma daf 52 that the name is not from my mouth as they say in the blessing on the forefathers; and why does it not say 'majority' if we are accustomed majority; if they made peace in Tuma or not; and similarly if there is a portion as they are exempt; and therefore the circumcised can act in a baraita and the portion acts; and in part if he is in his father's house the week they are exempt in a baraita; and the Kohanim themselves if Acrei Kahal made in Tuma or not; and if not we circumcise for years; and why does it not say 'majority' — the Rambam because only in the blessing on forefathers; and from this he was convinced the Rambam that only in crossing the sea one blesses; and not on miracle of tribes; and Rambam wrote only majority Israel; and if Acrei Kahal blessed on miracle of tribes if Acrei Kahal blesses; and if not one does not bless; and from this the Rambam was convinced that only in crossing the sea one blesses and not on miracle of tribes; requires study.
