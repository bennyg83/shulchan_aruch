Blessings on miracles. It contains 9 seifim. One who sees
a place where miracles were done for Israel — such as crossings of the sea, crossings of the Jordan, crossings of Arnon streams, hailstones of Beit Choron, stone Og sought to throw on Israel, stone Moshe sat on during war with Amalek, and wall of Jericho — blesses Blessed are You Heaven our Heaven King of the universe Who performed miracles for our forefathers at this place. And both this blessing and other blessings of seeing are like other blessings; all require mention of Heaven's name and kingship.
