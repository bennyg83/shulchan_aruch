(1) [Seif 1] One who sees a place where miracles were done etc. It implies specifically such as those where the miracle is recognizable from the place; but otherwise such as around Jerusalem where Sennacherib's camp fell — one does not bless as Tosafot and Mordekhai wrote. However on his personal miracle even if not recognizable from the place he blesses. Magen Avraham seif 1; and see Pedei Et Avraham section 40 letter kaf who wrote in name of Kometz Minchah that one who passes places of these journeys of Israel must bless Blessed are You Who performed miracles for our forefathers at this place — see there. Ach Pri letter alef.
