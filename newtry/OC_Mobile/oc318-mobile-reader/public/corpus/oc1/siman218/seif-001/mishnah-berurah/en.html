(1) Such as sea crossings — place where Israel crossed the sea on dry land.

(2) And crossings of the Jordan — place where Israel crossed the Jordan on dry land in Joshua's days.

(3) And crossings of Arnon streams — explained in the Talmud there were two mountains and a valley between them where Israel needed to pass; Amorites hid in caves they made in mountains at sides of the valley to fall on them suddenly when passing the valley; a miracle was done that mountains stuck together for the Ark of God's covenant upon them and all were killed there.

(4) Of Beit Choron — that were thrown from heaven on nations who fought Joshua as written in Scripture.

(5) And stone Og sought etc. — as explained in aggadah he uprooted a mountain three parasangs to cast on Israel and a miracle was done he could not cast it — see Berakhot 54b; and see Biur Halacha.

(6) And wall of Jericho — that was swallowed in its place.

(7) Blesses Who performed miracles — Magen Avraham wrote ostensibly it implies specifically those and similar where miracle is recognizable from the place — meaning when we see sea crossings we remember these waters were split; and in Jordan miracle was in the water itself as Scripture writes; and hailstones we know these are stones in which miracle was done; but otherwise such as when we come near Jerusalem where miracle occurred in Sennacherib's war that God's angel went out and killed the whole camp — one does not bless on such Who performed miracles since miracle is not recognizable from the place — meaning even if one can identify place of miracle nevertheless miracle is not recognizable for it was not in the body of the earth; however on his personal miracle even if not recognizable from the place — whenever he reaches place where miracle happened to him he blesses.

(8) And other blessings etc. — mentioned in next siman.

(9) With mention of God's name and kingship — and must also say You and our God as in other complete blessings.
