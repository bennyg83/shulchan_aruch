Who. See Taz. Berachos 54a concludes that one need not recount the miracle in detail. He need mention only the place: “Blessed is He Who performed a miracle for me in this place,” and, “in such-and-such other places,” and so forth. Kesef Mishneh, however, in chapter 10 of Hilchos Berachos, halachah 9, writes that Rif and Rambam omitted the episode of the wilderness and the camel because they understand that Rav Chanina acted beyond the letter of the law, combining all the places together, but that this is not required. Rosh holds that combining them is an obligation. Rif and Rambam hold that, since one adds no additional Divine Name and merely includes the places together, one is permitted to do so, but there is no obligation. This is unlike what Terumas HaDeshen writes, cited in siman 208, seif 18, that one should not combine into one blessing a matter that is doubtful. See there.
