[9] Of Daniel etc. Shulchan Aruch wrote and so ruled Bach and wrote it was omitted from Rambam's words of Daniel; and wondered on Rashal who ruled simply Rambam's words that on every lion pit one blesses — for if so whence for Rambam to distinguish thus — until here; and so wondered Shiyurei Kenesset HaGedolah. I wonder — for it is also explained explicitly in Rashi Berakhot daf 57 on furnace of fire — pit dug in which was lit like the furnace of Chananiah Mishael and Azariah — and he did not also explain on lion pit of Daniel — implying on every lion pit they bless; and the reason is because a lion pit is not found at all and wherever one sees it the heart is stirred on the miracle. Bach's and Shiyurei Kenesset HaGedolah's question whence to distinguish — it seems to me precisely thus — for the Talmud cites lion pit without the definite article and furnace of fire with the known definite article; and so in Ra'ash and Tur and poskim — easy to understand. And even though Rashi's explanation on Kiryat HaArba — in Hebrew the definite article is written in the second word — nevertheless since it is doubled and lion pit is also not written with the definite article it implies specifically that. I saw in Orchot Chaim who wrote in name of Seder Berakhot he received that they bless even on every furnace of fire and reads in the blessing as in this place with kaf — see there; and this per what I explained is not like those who disagree. Further in Rashba novellae and Abudraham I found like Shulchan Aruch and Levush that even on lion pits one does not bless except Daniel's; and so seems to me from the Talmud that says one who sees wicked Babylon blesses five and counts one of them lion pit and furnace of fire — implies specifically Daniel's and Chananiah Mishael and Azariah who were in Babylon. Further I can explain that also Rambam and Rashi's explanation agree and they did not need to write them due to great obviousness; and that they explained regarding furnace of fire — for one should not err that it deals with furnace of fire into which Avraham our father was thrown; and he would hold that for this the Talmud need not say they bless — for we are all sons of Avraham our father; rather it deals with furnace of Chananiah Mishael and Azariah similar to Daniel's lion pit that they bless because sanctification of God's name was done there even though not his sons — nevertheless one may bless on other lion pits without God's name and kingship.
