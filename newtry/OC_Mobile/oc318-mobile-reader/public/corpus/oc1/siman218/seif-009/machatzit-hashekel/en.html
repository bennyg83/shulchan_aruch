(Seif 9) A person etc. — Yerushalmi etc. and explained that if etc. — thus Yerushalmi per Tur's version: what will a person bless on miracle of his teacher if he was designated such as Yoav ben Tzeruyah and his colleagues, and a person through whom Divine Presence was sanctified such as Daniel and his colleagues — end quote; and Magen Avraham holds Yerushalmi's view that what was asked regarding teacher is resolved for him if teacher was designated or Divine Presence sanctified through him — certainly he blesses; and since Yerushalmi did not conclude explicitly but if teacher was not designated he does not bless — implies this remains in doubt and was not resolved for him.

Rather Rav Beit Yosef per his explanation of Yerushalmi — meaning Rav Beit Yosef challenged Tur who wrote on teacher's miracle one blesses — implying on every teacher; and brought proof from aforementioned Yerushalmi — for on the contrary in Yerushalmi it implies specifically if teacher was designated; and he answered Tur explains Yerushalmi's words that he resolved that we hold on designated person even if not his teacher one blesses — and automatically it is settled that teacher too even if not designated blesses; and per this one need not say also on designated person even if not teacher one blesses (and so for version Rav Beit Yosef wrote that was before Ra'ash — see there).

And requires study — for he is opposite all poskim; and in Sefer Akeidat Yitzchak and Chayei Adam they wrote it was hidden from them Tosafot's words (brought this reference in Magen Avraham) beginning chapter HaRo'eh daf 54b s.v. stones — thus: and the same a place where Sennacherib's camp fell — Hezekiah is not less than a designated person if he did not pay attention etc.; and again it seemed to R' Yonah since the miracle is not known from the place like these — one does not bless on it except those for whom the miracle was done — end quote; behold if Sennacherib's act were recognizable from the place he would bless even though not done for all Israel — nevertheless Hezekiah is not less than designated persons; if so perforce one must say on miracle of designated person when recognizable they bless.

In my humble opinion Magen Avraham did not understand Tosafot's intent thus — for impossible their intent in Hezekiah is not less than designated person means Yoav ben Tzeruyah — for whence for Tosafot to bless on miracle of designated person — this was not mentioned in our Talmud; and if their intent is aforementioned Yerushalmi — at least Tosafot should have brought Yerushalmi and explained; for plain meaning of Yerushalmi is not so obvious as above; or Tosafot's intent is designated person through whom Divine Presence was sanctified such as Daniel — simple and explained in our Talmud daf 57b — R' Yehoshua ben Levi taught one who sees lion pit or furnace of fire of Daniel Chananiah Mishael and Azariah blesses; and on this they wrote Hezekiah is not less than designated person — for also in that miracle Divine Presence was sanctified; and as in Seder Olam in Yalkut Melakhim 2 siman 236 — after Sennacherib's fall Hezekiah released troops in collars and they accepted yoke of kingdom of heaven; Rashi Yeshayah 45:14; but designated like Yoav we did not hear from Tosafot.
