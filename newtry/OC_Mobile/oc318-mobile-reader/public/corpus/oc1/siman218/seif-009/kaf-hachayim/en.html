(9) And the custom spread and so in Shelah that many great ones of Israel who were in trouble and were saved — each year on that day they seclude themselves in prayers and praises to God may He be blessed for the salvation they had from the miracle; and afterward to rejoice somewhat with friends and companions according to his means — and this is to publicize the miracle and it is a correct custom — for who knows if he will chance to be in that place where the miracle was done to him and bless there.
