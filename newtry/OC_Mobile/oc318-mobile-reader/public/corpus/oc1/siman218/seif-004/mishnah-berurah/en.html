(14) Miracle done for individual — already explained in seif 2 that whenever miracle was not for majority of Israel we judge him as individual.
(15) But he himself etc. — when he returns and reaches there after the miracle called him; from first time onward he does not bless when reaching unless thirty days passed from first blessing — as in seif 3.
(16) And all who go out from his loins — see Avnei Nezer who decided son and son's son must bless whether born before miracle or after — due to honor of their father; and from grandson onward they do not bless unless their fathers were born after miracle to first father — for then they are all partners in that miracle.
(17) Who performed miracle for my father — this refers to son; and from son's son onward he blesses Who performed miracle for my forefathers or for my father's father.
