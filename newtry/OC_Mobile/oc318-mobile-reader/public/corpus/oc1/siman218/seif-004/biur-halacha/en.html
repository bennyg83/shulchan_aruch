And all his descendants. It is obvious that if a miracle was performed for one’s mother or maternal grandmother, one likewise blesses, “Who performed a miracle for my mother,” or, “for my maternal grandmother.” It is also obvious that, apart from the person’s descendants, no one else may bless for another person’s miracle, not even a husband for his wife or a wife for her husband. See siman 219 regarding Bircas HaGomel. Likewise, a father may not bless for a miracle performed for his son. Abudraham writes this explicitly in the laws of blessings.
