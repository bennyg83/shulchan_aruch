And all go out to Jericho — and Peshat whether miracle was for them or for their forefathers; blesses miracles for forefathers or for their forefathers' forefathers; this Peshat is zavla dira — no one opinion to bless personal miracle; and if one alone whose name is miracle; and if they are many who went out of Jericho; see siman 219 for Gomel; and no father opinion to bless personal miracle in son; and father on father's burden; and see siman 219 for Gomel; and Abudraham to Hadiah; and thus no father opinion to bless personal miracle in son; and Abudraham etc.
