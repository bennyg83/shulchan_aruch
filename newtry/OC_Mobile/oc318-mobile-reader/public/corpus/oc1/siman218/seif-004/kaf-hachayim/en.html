(4) And one who blesses does not bless until he sees specifically the place of the miracle. As one who sees the Sea of Reeds does not bless if he did not see the place they crossed; and so one who sees the Jordan until he sees the place they crossed — and Scripture says and the people passed opposite Jericho; and so Arnon stream. However in every place he sees part of the sea and part of the Jordan and part of the stream it is fit to mention the kindness of the Holy One blessed be He and His providence upon us and thank and praise according to his ability; and so when seeing Mount Carmel place where Eliyahu may he rest in peace made the altar; and when seeing Shunem place of Elisha's ascent may he rest in peace and the like. We are accustomed to say as we see the Jordan so — by the Jordan I will bless praise thank and acknowledge Your name for Your people at the splitting of the Sea of Reeds, border of Your land its edge and end to Eliyahu You split it and in it Elisha passed exposed holy arm of Israel to save with the poor You favored Zion willingly. Kaftor VaFerach chapter 87; and it seems if he does not know the place clearly he should say Blessed are You Who performed miracles for our forefathers at this place without mentioning God's name and kingship.
