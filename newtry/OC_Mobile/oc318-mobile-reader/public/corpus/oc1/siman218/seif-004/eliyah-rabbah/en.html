[4] And all who go out from his loins etc. Even those born before then as Magen Avraham wrote above — until here Magen Avraham's words. It seems to me his explanation is from what Shulchan Aruch wrote that one blesses on his teacher's miracle like his father's miracle — implying the reason is not partnership but honor; and if so there is no distinction between born before as above. But in my humble opinion this is not correct — for Shulchan Aruch brought from Ramban that Beit Yosef brought that the Talmud reads only son and son's son because until here a father's mercy is on the son but those who go out from his loins do not bless; but for Rashba who also explained those who go out from his loins due to partnership — if so since they were already born partnership does not apply as Olat Tamid wrote; and clear proof from what Rashba wrote in his novellae — this is his language: and even though it is asked in Yerushalmi what is the law to bless on a miracle of the multitude and they did not resolve it — one may resolve from Rav Chana who blessed on Rabbi Yehuda his teacher — until here. And ostensibly it is wondrous why he did not resolve as Ramban resolved from blessing on one's father — rather perforce he goes to his view that not specifically son's son but all who go out from his loins; and perforce one must say the reason is partnership and one cannot learn teacher from it; and so it seems to me for the law. But for son and son's son one should rule that even before they were born they must bless since we rely on most poskim who explain only son and son's son and not those who go out from his loins; and if so the reason is not partnership; and further for one who says even those who go out from his loins due to partnership — nevertheless one must say for son and son's son they must also bless without the reason of partnership as we said. And now it is settled that Shulchan Aruch extended and hung the law of teacher on father and did not write simply that one blesses on teacher as I explained. R' Yom Tov Tzahalon wrote in responsum part 1 siman 87 — a miracle happened to him in the attic and he lives in a house — when he goes to the attic he blesses when going from thirty to thirty days.
