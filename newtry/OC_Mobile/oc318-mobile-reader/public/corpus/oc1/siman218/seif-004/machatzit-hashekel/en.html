(Seif 4) From thirty etc. — and requires study etc. — such as he saw on day 1 etc. he saw on day 2 — then there is not between one sighting and second sighting except twenty-eight days.

And from what is written siman 422 in name of Ra'avad etc. — he holds that a matter coming from thirty to thirty they bless Shehecheyanu; and therefore he challenged why one does not bless time on Hallel of Rosh Chodesh — certainly if the month is deficient then Rosh Chodesh on day thirty it would be fit not to bless time; but when the month is full Rosh Chodesh on day thirty and day thirty-one; and since they are expert in fixing the moon and know the last day is primary and from it they count for festivals — if so Hallel they said on day 30 is as if it did not exist and as if they said only psalms of Tehillim since it is only from custom of our forefathers; and if so Hallel they say on day 2 of Rosh Chodesh which is day 31 would have been fit to bless on it; but because we do not distinguish between full month and deficient month — behold in deficient month we are lenient that one does not bless time because between saying Hallel from past Rosh Chodesh to saying Hallel on this Rosh Chodesh is only twenty-eight days and it is not called coming from time to time to bless Shehecheyanu; and the same here.

And it seems to me it depends on dispute etc. — end of tractate Rosh Hashanah that prayer of chapters requires arranging before praying so it will be fluent in his mouth; and Ra'ash holds specifically prayer of festivals but of Rosh Chodesh is not called of chapters and need not arrange; and Rambam holds even prayer of Rosh Chodesh is called of chapters and must arrange; and Rav Beit Yosef ruled above siman 100 like Rambam; and the same law here — for Tosafot beginning chapter HaRo'eh wrote thus s.v. one who sees etc. and it seems to our Rabbi specifically for one who sees from thirty to thirty similar to one who sees the great sea for its chapters — for we say in Yerushalmi for chapters we mean thirty days — end quote; if so as he explained for chapters regarding prayer in siman 100 — the same here.

And in Ra'avad it implies he blesses on this — for he must say when Rosh Chodesh is two days — he would say time on the second day which is called from time to time even though between past Rosh Chodesh and day two of this Rosh Chodesh is only twenty-nine days.
