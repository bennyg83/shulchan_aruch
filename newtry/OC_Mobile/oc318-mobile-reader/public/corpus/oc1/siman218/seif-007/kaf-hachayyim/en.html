7) There: For some Jewish people, etc. The Yerushalmi records a dispute. According to the view that one tribe is called a congregation, one recites the blessing over a miracle performed for one tribe. According to the view that only all the tribes together are called a congregation, one recites it only over a miracle performed for all the tribes. The Tur cites this, and Beis Yosef rules that because of the doubt one should not recite the blessing; see there. Such are his words here in the Shulchan Aruch. Therefore, it appears that over a miracle performed for one tribe, one should recite the blessing without the Name and kingship. See below, paragraph 15.
