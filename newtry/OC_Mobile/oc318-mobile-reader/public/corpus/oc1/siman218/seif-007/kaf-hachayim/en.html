(7) There for part of Israel etc. In Yerushalmi there is a dispute — for one who says one tribe is called congregation they bless on a miracle done for one tribe; and for one who says only all tribes are called congregation they bless only on a miracle done for all tribes. Tur brought and Beit Yosef ruled one does not bless from doubt — see there; and such are his words here in Shulchan Aruch. And therefore it seems on a miracle done for one tribe one should bless without God's name and kingship. And see below letter 15.
