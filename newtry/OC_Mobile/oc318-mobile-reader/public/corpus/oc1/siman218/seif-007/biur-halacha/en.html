On a designated person's miracle — see Magen Avraham who wavered in this law that it is an unresolved matter in Yerushalmi and Achronim challenged him; see Eliyah Rabbah and other Achronim; nevertheless I wonder they did not challenge except from what they found in some poskim who cited this Yerushalmi statement not as an unresolved matter but casually; but nevertheless Magen Avraham's words are correct that perhaps we require specifically similar to Yoav whose nature became known throughout the world; and truly it is also implied in the plain sense of Yerushalmi itself from citing designated person such as Yoav and person through whom Divine Presence was sanctified such as Daniel and his companions; and it is implied Daniel and his companions themselves do not bless on them but because Divine Presence was sanctified through the miracle that happened to them; but if Divine Presence had not been sanctified through them — meaning some danger happened and they were saved through miracle — all Israel would not bless on them; thus even people like these are not called designated; and this precision is implied in the author himself who wrote therefore one who sees etc., implying if Divine Presence had not been sanctified through the miracle that happened to them they would not need to bless on them even though they were famous important people in Israel and also eunuchs in the palace of king of Babylon; nevertheless whoever his nature did not spread throughout all Israel like Yoav of whom it is said David executed justice and righteousness and Yoav was over the army — one does not bless on him; and if so Magen Avraham was correct who wrote we have nothing comparable now. And Eliyah Rabbah's words that nevertheless we require he be most designated do not suffice — if an important person in the world is called designated, then truly it is astonishing — behold miracle of a tribe remains in doubt and one does not bless on it even though in the tribe of Benjamin are found many great and honored people; and we require honored throughout all Israel; and Etz Chayim challenged the essence of this law of designated from that regarding tribe one does not bless and also whoever is not majority Israel one does not bless even though in many tribes are presumably many great and honored; and he wrote to resolve that perhaps Yoav is different since he went on mission of all Israel and behold it is as if it happened to all Israel, end — behold his view is like Magen Avraham and even more than him — specifically if he is emissary of all Israel [and then if miracle happened to him even though the miracle did not touch needs of all Israel but only his body, nevertheless it is as if it touched all Israel since he is the emissary] and such a person is not found in our generations since we are scattered throughout the world; and therefore correct in practice is Magen Avraham's view that today this blessing is not practiced; and in particular Ra'ah in his laws also that all these unresolved matters in Yerushalmi are unresolved matters.

Therefore one who sees a lion pit etc. — it is Tur's language; and it is implied that if it happened to a person that Divine Presence was sanctified through him afterward for some reason and he was saved through miracle, others may not bless on him afterward since there was no sanctification of Divine Presence here; and were it not for Tur's words it would appear to say Yerushalmi's intent is that after Divine Presence was initially sanctified through him [as in Daniel's act who risked himself for prayer and Heaven saved him through miracle] every person is considered like his master and must treat him with honor and thank Heaven for the miracle He prepared for him.
