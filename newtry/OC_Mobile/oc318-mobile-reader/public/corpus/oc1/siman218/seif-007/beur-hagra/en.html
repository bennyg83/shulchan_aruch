Seif 7, "over," etc. The Yerushalmi cited above asks the relevant questions and, according to one text, says concerning his companions, "he recites a blessing." Beit Yosef explains that the words "he recites a blessing" resolve all the questions: one recites the blessing concerning his father, his teacher, a distinguished person, and a person through whom sanctification occurred, etc. Tur, however, explains that the statement was said only concerning a distinguished person, etc. According to our text of the Yerushalmi, which asks concerning Daniel and his companions and concerning the miracle of the tribes, and does not contain the words "he recites a blessing," none of these questions is resolved. See Magen Avraham. The Yerushalmi should read "if there was a person," and not "and if there was," etc. "Therefore," etc. Gemara, Berachos 57b.
