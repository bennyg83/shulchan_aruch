Designated person. Thus Remazim; and so on miracle done for his teacher one blesses if he was designated like Yoav ben Tzeruyah — end quote; implies he requires both teacher and designated; for Yerushalmi asked regarding teacher and it is resolved that if teacher was designated he blesses; but teacher and not designated remains in doubt and doubt in blessings to be lenient; and so in Ra'ash; but Ramban ruled from what is visible in our Gemara that one blesses on father — the same for teacher; and so in Tur; but on designated person we do not find one who ruled to bless except Rav Beit Yosef per explanation in Yerushalmi; and requires study for he is opposite all poskim who did not write to bless on designated person; therefore it seems he does not bless — doubt in blessings to be lenient; and further possible we require specifically like Yoav whose nature spread throughout the world; and in our time we have nothing comparable.
