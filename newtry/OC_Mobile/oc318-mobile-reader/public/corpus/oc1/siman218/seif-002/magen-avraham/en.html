On a miracle. Implied in Shabbat daf 33b — when a miracle is done for a person he should fix some public needs in the city.

For part of tribes etc. Bach challenged — for in Yerushalmi it says for one who holds every tribe is called congregation he is obligated to bless on miracle of one tribe; and dispute of R' Meir with R' Yehudah and R' Shimon; and since we hold like R' Yehudah and R' Shimon and so Rambam chapter 12 Shegagot — if so why did he rule here one does not bless? And in Lev Av he answered this general principle does not exist except in practiced matters — if so one may say we hold like R' Meir — end quote. And requires study — for in Tosafot Zevachim daf 6 it implies this general principle applies everywhere; and requires study — nevertheless R' Yehudah and R' Shimon are many; and further Rambam ruled like them; and further challenged in Pesachim daf 80 we say for R' Yehudah and R' Shimon if one tribe became impure they act in impurity because it is called congregation — and Rambam ruled not like them because anonymous mishnah is not like them; and if so his words contradict each other; therefore it seems to me specifically regarding court instruction we hold like them because congregation is written explicitly in the verse; but in Passover congregation is not written explicitly and here we compare to Passover.
