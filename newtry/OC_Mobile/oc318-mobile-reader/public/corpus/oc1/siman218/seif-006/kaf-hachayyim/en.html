6) [Seif 2] Over a miracle performed for some Jewish people, etc. Shabbos 33b implies that when a miracle occurs for a person, he should provide some public need in the city. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. The reason appears to follow Sefer Chassidim: when a supernatural miracle sometimes occurs for a person, it is because it is revealed before Him, may He be exalted, that the public will obtain some merit through that person. Shulchan Gavoah, paragraph 2. Ruach Chaim writes in the name of Emes LeYaakov that the same applies to composing and printing a work needed by the public to enlighten them. See there, where he writes of the many virtues accruing both to the author and to the benefactor who sponsors its publication; see there. See further about this in our sacred work Chukei Chaim, discourse 13, concerning the acquisition of a Sefer Torah.
