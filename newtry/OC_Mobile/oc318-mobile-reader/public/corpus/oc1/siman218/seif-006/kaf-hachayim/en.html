(6) [Seif 2] On a miracle done for part of Israel etc. Implied in Shabbat daf 33b that when a miracle is done for a person he should fix some public needs in the city. Magen Avraham seif 2; and the reason seems per what is written section 8 that what is sometimes done for a person — a miracle not in the way of nature — is that it was revealed before Him may He be blessed that through him some merit would come for the multitude. Shulchan Berurah letter bet; and Rokeach wrote in name of Emet LeYaakov that the same applies to a friend and to print a book on a matter that is a need of the multitude to enlighten their eyes — and see there he wrote how many good qualities there are for the author and also for one who dedicates to publish it — see there. And further see from this in Sefer Chakkei Chaim sermon 13 on acquiring a Torah scroll.
