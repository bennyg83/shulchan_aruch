As one blesses upon the place. See Shulchan Shlomo, who writes that if one sees a person for whom a miracle was performed within thirty days after he already blessed upon the place where that person’s miracle occurred, he need not bless, since he already blessed upon the place. If he later sees that person again within thirty days of his first sighting of the person, he does not bless, even though he did not bless upon seeing him the first time and even though thirty days have already passed since he saw and blessed upon the place. The converse is likewise true.
