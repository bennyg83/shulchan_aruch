As he blesses on the place — see Shulchan Shelomo Shelah that Rambam wrote person who was saved within thirty days; if already blessed at the place of the miracle when he sees the person within thirty days he does not bless again; and if he did not bless at the place when he sees the person within thirty days he blesses; and if he sees the person again after thirty days he does not bless; and if he sees the place again after thirty days he blesses again.
