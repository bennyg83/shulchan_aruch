[6] Must mention etc. But those who go out from his loins need not mention other places — so it seems to me like a miracle of the multitude — until here Magen Avraham's words. It seems to me son and son's son mention all; and so a student for teacher; and so seems to me in Remazim. Olat Tamid wrote if he goes that day to another place where a miracle happened to him — requires study therefore he does not bless from doubt — until here. Per his words all thirty days he would not bless in all places. In my humble opinion he should bless even that day since he did not see within thirty for that place; and further in Kesef Mishneh chapter 9 Laws of Blessings he wrote that for Rif and Rambam he does not mention all places and Mar brei deRavina who acted so before the norm of law — he acted exceptionally; and from this I wonder on Taz who extended to challenge Ra'ash who wrote on the incident of Mar — from here implies an individual for whom this law applies is in Ra'ash's name — this is from the Talmud — see there — how could Kesef Mishneh's words of Rif and Rambam escape him that they did not rule this Talmud and for this Tur had to bring in Ra'ash's name that it implies the Talmud states obligation. Also what he strained to answer that Ra'ash implies one must mention place names is wondrous — for this novelty is not mentioned in Ra'ash at all; and more fitting to answer that Ra'ash implies even in many places of the Talmud only two are mentioned.
