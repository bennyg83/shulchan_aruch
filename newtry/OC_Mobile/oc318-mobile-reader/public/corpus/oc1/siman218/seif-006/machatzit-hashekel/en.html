(Seif 6) Those who go out etc. — born before then — lest you think specifically those born afterward who are partners in father's miracle and were it not that father was saved they would not have come to the world — not so those born before; it teaches that nevertheless they must bless — for this is included in honor of father; and proof from what is written in seif 16 on teacher's miracle; and therefore because this is included in honor and fear of teacher — the same for father; and even son's son born before must bless — for he is also obligated in honor of father's father; and as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 240 seif 24 in gloss.
