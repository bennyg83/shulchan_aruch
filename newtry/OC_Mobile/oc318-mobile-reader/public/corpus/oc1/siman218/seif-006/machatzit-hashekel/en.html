Seif katan 6: His descendants, etc. This includes those born before the miracle. One might otherwise have thought that only children born afterward must bless, since they share in their father’s miracle, for had their father not been saved they would never have come into the world, whereas children already born before the miracle would not. The ruling teaches that they too must bless, because this is included in honoring one’s father. Proof is brought from seif 6, which states that one must bless for a miracle performed for one’s teacher, necessarily because this is included in honoring and fearing one’s teacher. The same applies to one’s father. Even a grandson born before the miracle must bless, since he too is obligated to honor his paternal grandfather, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 240, seif 24, in the gloss.
