(26) Lot's wife — when she is a salt pillar.

(27) Blesses two — even though he does not see Lot's grave — for when he sees her he is reminded also of miracle of her husband; and what is written and on Lot — meaning for Lot's sake.

(28) Who remembers the righteous — that God remembered Avraham as written and God remembered Avraham and sent Lot from the upheaval etc.; and ostensibly on Lot's grave one does not bless at all for there remembrance of righteous is not recognizable — only when he sees his wife who became salt pillar in that place at that hour — certainly power of judgment was strong; nevertheless God remembered Avraham and saved for his sake Lot — and fit to bless on this.
