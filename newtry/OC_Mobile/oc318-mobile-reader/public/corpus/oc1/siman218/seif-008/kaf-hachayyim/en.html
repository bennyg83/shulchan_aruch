8) Know that when the Holy One, blessed is He, saves a person from distress, the salvation comes through the souls of the righteous. The Zohar, parashas Metzora, 55b, in the Tosefta, writes concerning Yaakov, 'Angels of God encountered him,' that these were Avraham and Yitzchak, who encountered Yaakov when he was saved from Lavan. This does not apply only to him. Regarding every righteous person, the souls of the righteous come before him to save him, and they are exalted holy angels; see there. Shulchan Gavoah, paragraph 2.
