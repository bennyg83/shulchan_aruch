(8) And know that salvation that the Holy One blessed be He saves a person from trouble is through souls of the righteous as written in Zohar parashat Metzora 55b in Tosefta on what is written regarding Yaakov and angels of God met him — Avraham and Isaac were aroused for Yaakov when he was saved from Lavan; and not this alone but for all righteous — souls of righteous are aroused before him to save him and they are holy supernal angels — see there.
