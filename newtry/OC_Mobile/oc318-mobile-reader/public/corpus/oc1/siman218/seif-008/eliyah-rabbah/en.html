[8] [Levush] Even if not his teacher etc. Magen Avraham extended to divide this law that we do not find one who rules to bless on a designated person — see there. I wonder on him — for Tosafot Berakhot daf 54b and Mordekhai and Rokeach siman 7 and Rashba novellae chapter Keitzad Mevarchin ruled so explicitly; and no doubt it escaped him and therefore he did not know in Machlilah the explanation of Yerushalmi clear in Rashba that on father and teacher he did not resolve at all. What Yerushalmi concludes if he is a designated person — is a new matter and does not refer above; and this proves Ramban's intent I brought above and so implies in Ra'ash; also Bach and Beit Yosef and Ma'adanei Melech strained in explaining Yerushalmi and left me room; see there for I abbreviated; nevertheless it seems we require specifically the most designated person.
