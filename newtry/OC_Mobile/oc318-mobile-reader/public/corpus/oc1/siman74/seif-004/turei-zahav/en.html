Even though they do not stop below, etc. — the reason is their nakedness is below and her heart cannot see it; and nevertheless if they stand naked there is another prohibition — namely prohibition of exposed nakedness, not from the heart but from "there shall not be seen among you any naked thing"; and as we find in Sotah that she must cover before and behind her because all of her is nakedness; and Rashi explained that her place of shame is visible from before and behind her — therefore she must sit pressed, as Rama wrote there. But what Rama concluded that then her heart does not see their nakedness — is not precise regarding her heart, for her heart in any case does not see their nakedness; rather he spoke that she not be in complete exposed nakedness; and this ruling Moreinu Chayyim wrote on it that it is not correct, and so in Yoreh De'ah; but also for a woman we require stopping between heart and nakedness even when she has clothing; and he wrote that Shulchan Aruch itself contradicts his ruling below in siman 276 that we require a woman sitting specifically, etc.; and this caused him all this because he understood according to this view that a woman has no exposed nakedness at all even standing; and since there is truly prohibition when standing, this view was nullified; and I already expanded in Yoreh De'ah siman 200 that he was not precise in this except regarding standing — also this view admits there is prohibition, but not from the heart but from exposed nakedness as written; and therefore a clear matter, fit to rely on: a woman who has clothing and covered herself before and behind does not need stopping between heart and nakedness at all, and may pray; and likewise for immersion — if standing even in clear water she may bless if her heart is outside the water, or even within the water and embraces her hand on her body under her heart; and so should a man do; and see Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 200, see there at length.
