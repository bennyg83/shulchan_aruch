Seif katan 1. A separation. He was troubled that if his heart sees the ervah, his eyes also see it, and it is prohibited even without the reason that his heart sees it. Even closing his eyes is ineffective, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 75, seif katan 9. He therefore explains that the ervah is covered, such as where two people sleep under one tallis, but there is no separation between his heart and the other person's ervah. This is clear.
