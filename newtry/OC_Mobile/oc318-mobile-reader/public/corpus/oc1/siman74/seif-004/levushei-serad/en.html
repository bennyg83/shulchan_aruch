Section 1 — interruption. It was challenged to him: if his heart sees, his eyes also see and it is forbidden without the reason of his heart sees — and even squeezing his eyes does not help, as Magen Avraham wrote siman 75 section 9; therefore he explained that the ervah is covered — e.g. when two sleep in one tallit, only there is no interruption between his heart and his companion's ervah; it is clear.
