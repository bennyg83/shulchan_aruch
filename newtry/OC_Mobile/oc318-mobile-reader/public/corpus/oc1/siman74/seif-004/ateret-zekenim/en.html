That women may bless and pray when they wear a robe, even though it does not interrupt below the chest, and they do not need thatched mats on the ground, except when they are naked so that their nakedness be covered (Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chayyim). And some say that when a woman stands, even wearing a robe, she is forbidden to pray the prayer standing, as a man is forbidden; rather she must interrupt with her hands on the robe below the heart (my teacher in the Bach).
