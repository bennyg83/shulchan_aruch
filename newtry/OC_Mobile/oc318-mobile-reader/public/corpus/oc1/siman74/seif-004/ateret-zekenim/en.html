Women may recite blessings and pray while wearing a shift, even though there is no separation below the chest. They need not press themselves against the ground unless they are naked, in which case this is required so that their nakedness be covered. This is stated by the Beit Yosef in the name of Orchos Chaim. Some maintain that when a woman is standing, even if she is wearing a shift, she may not pray the Amidah while standing, just as a man may not. Rather, she must press her hand against the shift below the heart. This is the view of my teacher in the Bach.
