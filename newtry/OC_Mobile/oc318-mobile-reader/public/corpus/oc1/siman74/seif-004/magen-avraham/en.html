Even though they do not make a separation. Their hearts do not see their ervah. The Bach disagrees with this. See the end of siman 206.
For then their hearts do not, etc. This is a scribal error. The correct text is: “For then their ervah is not visible,” as written in siman 206. I likewise found this written, contrary to the Lechem Chamudos, who strained to explain the text as follows: A woman's heart never sees her ervah, but it must be pressed against the ground so that her ervah will not be visible at all. One cannot answer that she should look elsewhere, as written at the end of siman 75. That applies specifically to another person's ervah, but when one's own ervah is exposed, it is prohibited even if one does not see it, as written in seif 1, where even if he puts his head outside the window it remains prohibited. This is not comparable to the Rama's ruling in seif 2, for there his ervah is at least covered by water. Since the water is merely clear, it is permitted when he does not see it. Rabbeinu Yonah writes this explicitly. See what I wrote at the end of siman 75.
