(א) Even though they do not interpose. For one's heart does not see their nakedness; and Bach disputes this — see siman 76.

(ב) Then one's heart, etc. — it is a textual error; and it should read: then their nakedness is not visible — as written in siman 76. And so wrote that it is not like Levush, who forced in this explanation that a woman never has her heart see the nakedness; rather, they must be pressed to the ground so that her nakedness not be visible at all. And I said: let her look elsewhere — as written in section 75 — specifically regarding a fellow's nakedness; but when her own nakedness is exposed, even if she does not see it, it is forbidden — as written in section 61, that even if one puts one's head out through a window it is forbidden. And some explain what is written in section 62 in the gloss there: in any case one's nakedness is covered in water, only that it is clear; therefore if one does not see it, it is permitted. And so Terumat Yisrael wrote explicitly — see section 75.
