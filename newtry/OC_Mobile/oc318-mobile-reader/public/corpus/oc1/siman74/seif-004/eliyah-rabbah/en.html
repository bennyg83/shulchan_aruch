(4) We consider etc. — In Lechem Chamudos he wrote the reason: because it is an act not in the usual way — for it is not people's way to embrace themselves thus, and it is called an interruption; unlike covering with his hand on his heart, which is a normal, common thing. Beit Yosef siman 206 in the name of the Rosh, chapter HaRo'eh — Yerushalmi: R' Yirmiyah asked: one sitting inside a wooden tower — what shall he do? Like a garment, stick his head outside the shirt and bless? He was uncertain: if his heart sees ervah — forbidden; and above we ruled forbidden, until here is the Rosh's language. Magen Avraham wrote plainly that the Bavli in Sukkah folio 10 was omitted from him — one sleeping in a canopy sticks his head outside the canopy and recites Kerias Shema; and if it was ten handbreadths high it is forbidden, for it is an ohel like a house, and we place the head after the majority. If so, the Yerushalmi should ask whether a tower is considered an ohel or is like a vessel as a shirt and tallit, until here. And I studied the Yerushalmi itself and saw that it also asks regarding one sitting naked in a house — what if he sticks his head outside a window? If so, perforce it does not ask whether it is considered an ohel. And indeed I greatly wonder at all the great ones who did not notice that the Yerushalmi contradicts an explicit baraita in Sukkah there — in a house he shall not stick his head out etc. And it seems to my humble opinion to distinguish between Kerias Shema and other blessings — and study is needed. And what Magen Avraham challenged — why the codifiers omitted that [sugya] in Sukkah — see in the Rosh and study is needed; also Tosafos Sukkah there needs study, and Agudah chapter HaRo'eh.
