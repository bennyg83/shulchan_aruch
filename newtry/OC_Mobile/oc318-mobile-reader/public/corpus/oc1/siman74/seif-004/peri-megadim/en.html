Even though. See Taz and Magen Avraham seif katan 8. I already explained in letter 1 that exposed ervah is forbidden even if one is covered from the waist upward and over the eyes. It is forbidden because the ervah remains exposed. The distinction between a man and a woman is that a man's heart can see his ervah, whereas this cannot occur for a woman because her ervah is positioned low. Her front must be pressed against the ground because of exposed ervah. Taz's concluding statement concerning immersion, that her heart should be outside the water, is merely an added stringency. By strict law, a woman's heart does not see her ervah. This is also written in Yoreh Deah siman 200. The reference in letter 1 and here to seif 4 is a textual error and should be to siman 200. See Eliyah Rabbah letter 6, who similarly corrects Taz's words, and Magen Avraham seif katan 8 here and siman 76 letter 8 concerning this.
