Even though Taz and Magen Avraham letter 8 — and I already explained in letter 1 that exposed nakedness is forbidden even though covered from the waist upward; squinting is forbidden because of exposed nakedness. And the difference between man and woman: in a man his heart sees his nakedness; in a woman it cannot be conceived, for her nakedness is below and we require pressing because of exposed nakedness. And what he concluded regarding immersion — her heart outside the water — is a general stringency; but by law for a woman her heart does not see her nakedness; and so in Yoreh De'ah 200 (error in letter 1 and here letter 4 — should read 200); and see Eliyah Rabbah letter 6 — he corrected thus Taz's and Magen Avraham's letter 8 words here and siman 75 seif 6 letter 8 on this.
