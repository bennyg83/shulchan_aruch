There is one who says that women may bless and pray when they are wearing a shirt, even though it does not interpose below the heart.And if they are naked, their nakedness must be pressed to the ground, or she must sit on something else — for then their heart does not see their nakedness, which is not so with a man (Beit Yosef in the name of Acharonim).
