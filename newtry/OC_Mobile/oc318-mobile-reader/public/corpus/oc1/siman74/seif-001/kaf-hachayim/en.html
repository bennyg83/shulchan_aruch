(1) [Section 1] He was sleeping naked in his tallit — he must press the tallit on his heart etc. — meaning that the tallit should cling to his heart so there is an interruption between his heart and the ervah; but in Biur Halachah it is forbidden, because although his heart is covered by the tallit on his whole body, his heart sees the ervah and faces it; therefore he must cover his heart or have the tallit interrupt beneath his heart so there is interruption between heart and ervah. From Magen Avraham, note 1.
