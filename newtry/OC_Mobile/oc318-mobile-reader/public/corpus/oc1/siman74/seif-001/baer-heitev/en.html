His heart. See Magen Avraham, who concludes that if there is excrement in the house and one places his head outside the window, he may not recite Krias Shema, because we associate his head with the majority of his body. Likewise, if he inserts his head into a room containing excrement, he may not recite, because in any event the excrement is opposite him. See there. Yad Aharon writes that this ruling is open to question.
Another person's nakedness. Meaning that there is no separation between his heart and the other person's nakedness.
