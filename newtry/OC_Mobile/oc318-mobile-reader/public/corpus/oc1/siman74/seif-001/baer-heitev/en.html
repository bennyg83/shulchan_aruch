His heart. Magen Avraham raised that if there is excrement in the house and he placed his head outside the window, it is forbidden to recite Shema, for we follow the head after the majority [of the body]. And if he brought his head into a room where there is excrement, it is forbidden to recite there too, for in any event the excrement is opposite him—see there. And Yad Aharon wrote on this that one should hesitate about this law.
