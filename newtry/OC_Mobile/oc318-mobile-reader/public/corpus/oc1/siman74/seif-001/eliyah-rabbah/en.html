[1] In his tallit, etc. Levush: do not read Keriat Shema until covering heart with tallit; Malbushei Yom Tov wondered — they already wrote even if no separation between heart and nakedness, pressing tallit on heart helps. Beit Yosef, Bach:
[2] Sees the ervah, etc. For example, two people are standing in water. One person's heart does not see his own ervah because it is covered by a garment, but it sees the other person's uncovered ervah. This is forbidden even though his eyes are above the water, because his heart nevertheless sees it. So writes the Bach.
