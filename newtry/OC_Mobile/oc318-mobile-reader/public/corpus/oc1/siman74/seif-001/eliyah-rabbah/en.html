(1) In his tallit etc. — The Levush's wording: he shall not recite Kerias Shema until he covers with a tallit over his heart etc.; and Malbushi Yom Tov wrote: one could ask, what covering is this? It was not permitted except for a woman dressed in a shirt, below in section 4 — unless we interpret his words that he meant to say another tallit, and this is not in the plain sense of his language; and also regarding the matter of his citation, if we judge one sleeping in his tallit who has nothing with which to interrupt — so wrote Malbushi Yom Tov. In my humble opinion it is plain what the Levush wrote, that he should cover with his tallit over his heart — he meant that the tallit should cling to his heart; and so it is explained in the Talmud [Berachos] folio 25, and Rashi explained the term "presses" etc. And in any case it is heard from Malbushi Yom Tov's words that if he covers his heart with a garment by itself without a tallit on him it suffices; and so wrote Lechem Chamudos folio 26 — i.e. even if he does not press it to his heart.
