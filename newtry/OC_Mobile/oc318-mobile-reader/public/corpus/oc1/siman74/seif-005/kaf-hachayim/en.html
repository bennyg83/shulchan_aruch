(5) There — because his heart sees the ervah it is forbidden. And there is no difference between study and an ordinary blessing and Shema — in all cases where they said his heart sees the ervah it is forbidden; and although we find one who holds some leniency, nevertheless they are not according to us; it is clear that his heart seeing the ervah is forbidden, and we hold there is no difference between Shema and other holy matters. From Machatzis HaShekel note 1; and he wrote that thus is implied from the codifiers and Maran's words below section 4; see there. And they cited it in Chacham Tzvi note 100; Shaarei Teshuvah there. And likewise Ben Ish Chai there. And see further below note 7.
