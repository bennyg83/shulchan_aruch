(18) "They see it," etc. — meaning we do not require that the nakedness be covered on its own by something so that the organs not be visible.

(19) Which of his organs — for we decree touching his other organs because of touching his hands, and touching his hands lest he come to improper thoughts.

(20) The member among them — but touching the pocket we are not concerned about, for there is no concern for improper thoughts; and the meticulous are meticulous even in this because there are disputants. And after the fact he has fulfilled his obligation even if the member touched the thighs [Peri Megadim according to Pri Chadash cited above in siman 73 in Mishna Berurah 4].
