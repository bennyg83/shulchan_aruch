(18) See the ervah, etc. Meaning, it is not necessary to cover the ervah separately with something so that the limbs should not see it.
(19) Any of his limbs. We decree against contact by other limbs because of contact by his hands, and contact by his hands is prohibited lest it lead to improper thoughts.
(20) The organ touches them. Contact with the scrotum is not a concern, because it does not raise concern for improper thoughts. Those who are meticulous are careful even about this, because some disagree. After the fact he has fulfilled his obligation even if the organ touched his thighs. This is Peri Megadim according to Peri Chadash cited above in siman 73, Mishnah Berurah seif katan 4.
