5) There. It is prohibited because his heart sees the ervah. There is no distinction between Torah study, an ordinary blessing, and Keriat Shema; in all of them they said that it is prohibited when his heart sees the ervah. Although we find an authority who is lenient in one of these cases, we do not rule that way. We rule that it is prohibited when his heart sees the ervah, and we maintain that there is no distinction between Keriat Shema and other sacred words. Machazik Berachah, ot 1, who writes that this is also implied by the halakhic authorities and by the words of Maran below, seif 4; see there.
