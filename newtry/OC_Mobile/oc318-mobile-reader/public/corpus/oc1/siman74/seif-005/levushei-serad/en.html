Shulchan Aruch, seif 2. From below to his heart. The concept of covering does not apply to the area from below to his heart. The term “covering” applies only when he covers his heart so that it will not see, or covers his ervah so that it will not be seen by his heart. The phrase “from below to his heart,” however, means between the heart and the ervah. In that case the relevant concept is not covering but separation, as explained in Shulchan Aruch, seif 3. For this reason, there even his arms or hands are effective, since they function as a separation. Here, however, a garment is specifically required and his hands are ineffective, since they function as a covering, for the case concerns covering his heart, as Taz, seif katan 3, and Magen Avraham, seif katan 6, distinguish. The word “below” written here teaches that his heart is also within the water, in accordance with the Rama's gloss there. Meaning, he should cover with a garment his heart, which is likewise below within the water. Magen Avraham therefore cites in seif katan 2 the words “he should cover his heart with his garment,” omitting the word “below,” in order to clarify the Shulchan Aruch's meaning. In newer editions they added the word “below” to Magen Avraham, but the addition detracts, for they did not understand Magen Avraham's intent.
