Shulchan Aruch section 2 — below his heart. "Below his heart" does not apply to covering, for the term covering applies only when covering the heart so it will not see, or covering his nakedness so it will not be visible to the heart; but "below his heart" implies between the heart and the ervah — for that, covering does not apply but interruption, as explained in Shulchan Aruch section 3; and for that reason there even his arms or hands help, since it is in the category of interruption; but here specifically a garment is required, and his hands do not help since it is in the category of covering — dealing with covering the heart — as the Taz section 3 and Magen Avraham section 6 divided. And the word "below" written here teaches that it deals with his heart also being within the water, per Rama's gloss there — meaning he should cover with a garment on his heart, which is also below within the water; and therefore Magen Avraham section 2 cited "he shall cover with his garment on his heart" and skipped the word "below" to indicate explanation of Shulchan Aruch's words. And in new editions they added in Magen Avraham the word "below" — the adder detracts and they did not grasp Magen Avraham's intent.
