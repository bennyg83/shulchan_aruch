With a garment. Beit Yosef writes: It appears to me that, for this purpose, only the organ itself is called nakedness. Touching it with his hands can bring him to improper thoughts, whereas touching the scrotum is of no concern, because even if he touches it with his hand it does not bring him to improper thoughts. Maharam Tivoli disagrees and maintains that the scrotum too is considered nakedness for this purpose, and both forms of contact are forbidden. See Yad Aharon and Ginas Veradim, Gan HaMelech, siman 121.
