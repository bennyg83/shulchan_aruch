And Beit Yosef wrote, and it seems to me that only the thigh itself is called nakedness for this matter—if one touches it with his hands it leads to arousal—but regarding touching the pocket we have no concern, for even if it touches his hand it does not lead to arousal—thus. And Maharam Tivoli disagrees and holds that the pocket too is called nakedness for this matter, and both are forbidden. See Yad Aharon and Ginat Veradim in Gan haMelekh siman 121.
