(Section 5) Above, etc. — Since his eyes are outside the water, etc. — the wording is not precise, for the heart remains in the water; and the intent is that Rama in Darkei Moshe learned this law: if one's heart is outside the water it is permitted, since Rashba wrote on that which we say that in clear water it is forbidden because one's heart sees — if so, learn that one's eyes see — if so. And he explained: since one's eyes are outside the water, etc., unlike the heart, which is in the water; and from this one learns that if the heart too is outside the water it is permitted.
