Seif katan 5, above, etc. “Since his eyes, etc.” The wording is imprecise, because the discussion concerns the heart. The intention is that the Rama in Darkei Moshe derived the law permitting the heart to be outside the water from the Rashba's question concerning the statement that clear water is forbidden because one's heart sees the ervah: why not prohibit it because his eyes see the ervah? The Rashba answers that his eyes are outside the water, unlike the heart, which is in the water. From this we learn that if the heart too is outside the water, it is permitted.
