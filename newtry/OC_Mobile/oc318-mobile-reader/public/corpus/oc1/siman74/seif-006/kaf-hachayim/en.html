(6) There — gloss. The same if his heart sees his companion's nakedness — forbidden; so too Tur and Beit Yosef. Bach wrote: e.g. when two stand in water and one's heart does not see its own nakedness because it is covered in clothing, but sees its companion's nakedness that is not covered — likewise forbidden to recite, even though his eyes are outside the water and he looks outside and they do not see the ervah, until here. And they cited Eliyah Rabbah note 2 and Magen Avraham section 1 — meaning when there is no interruption between his heart and his companion's nakedness. Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham note 1 wrote that his intent is when two sleep in one tallit, far apart and not touching, and his heart sees his companion's nakedness while his own is covered in clothing and his head is outside the shirt (i.e. outside the tallit), until here. And the reason is that they follow their method in siman 75 section 6 in Magen Avraham section 9; but for the Mechaber there, squeezing suffices without these scenarios, and the rabbi who was silent there — I concede to him. Peri Megadim there. And see our words there, with Heaven's help, that we wrote one should be stringent like the later authorities; see there.
