Seif katan 6, “We treat it, etc.” It nevertheless constitutes an interruption. Lechem Chamudos, in the chapter Mi Shemeiso, seif 113, writes regarding Beit Yosef's ruling: “This requires explanation. It therefore appears to me that it is considered an interruption because he performs an unusual act, since people do not ordinarily embrace themselves in this manner. By contrast, merely covering his heart with his hand is common and frequent, and therefore such covering is not considered a covering.” Peri Chadash disputes this ruling. See there.
