(Section 6) We consider it, etc. — as an interposition; nevertheless it is effective. And Levush wrote in the chapter What is Written, section 113, on the words of Rabbeinu Yonah, and this is his language: A reason is needed for the matter; therefore I hold that for this reason it is called an interposition — because he does an act not in the usual way, for it is not people's way to embrace themselves thus. Unlike ordinary covering with his hand on his heart, which is a normal, common thing; therefore his covering is not called covering, until here. And in Peri Chadash he disputes this law — see there.
