(21) From his waist downward. Meaning, even only until below his ervah, because that suffices by law for Krias Shema. For prayer, however, his entire body must be covered.
(22) Even though, etc. This is permitted in pressing circumstances. Otherwise it is improper initially to act this way even for an ordinary blessing, and certainly for Krias Shema.
(23) He may recite. Since his ervah is covered and the tallis also interrupts between his heart and ervah.
(24) His heart. The same applies to his entire body. The Mechaber mentions the heart because of the first clause, where for Krias Shema one need not cover the heart. Prayer is more stringent because one must regard himself as standing before the King and speaking with Him, and must therefore stand with awe. During Krias Shema, however, he is not speaking before the King.
