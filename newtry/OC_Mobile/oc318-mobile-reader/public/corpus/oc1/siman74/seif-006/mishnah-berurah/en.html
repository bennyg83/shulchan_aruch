(21) From his waist downward — meaning even only below his nakedness; with this it suffices by law for Shema, but for prayer his whole body must be covered.

(22) Even though, etc. — in a situation of pressing need; for otherwise it is not proper ab initio to do so even for any blessing, and certainly for Shema.

(23) Permitted to recite — since his nakedness is covered and the tallit also separates between his heart and his nakedness.

(24) His heart — the same applies to his whole body; he mentioned heart because at the beginning of Shema one need not cover the heart. The reason prayer is stricter is that in prayer one must see himself as standing before the King and speaking with Him, so he must stand in awe; but in Shema he does not speak before the King.
