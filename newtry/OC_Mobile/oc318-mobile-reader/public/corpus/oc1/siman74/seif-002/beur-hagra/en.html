Seif 2, and specifically... from what was taught in the Gemara; and some say on the heart forty [amot] specifically with a garment but not with hands—and this is what is meant by "and similarly..." and we learn from this—and Magen Avraham.

And if there is no... as stated there, he sat until his neck—for it does not mean literally until his neck, but because some say that in such a case specifically he muddies with his foot, and this is what is challenged there "but behold his heel..." meaning, this in any case is forbidden according to some on account of the heel; and what is challenged there on the Mishnah and they answered "cover..." and "but behold..." after he answered with leggings, it challenges—for must one also pray there? For the reason for leggings is so that he may lean, and in prayer it says there one until he covers his heart.
