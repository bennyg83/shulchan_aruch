[3] If his heart is not within, etc. Olas Tamid explains that the law is more lenient regarding the heart than regarding actual sight. Although the heart sees the ervah through clear water, since the heart is above the water it is permitted. The Bach's wording, however, seemingly requires study, for he writes: “Since his heart is above the water, his heart does not see the ervah within the water.” This requires study, because in clear water he does see the ervah within the water. Perhaps even where the water is clear and it can be seen, this is considered that the heart does not see it, because the heart is above the water.
