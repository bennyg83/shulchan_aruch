So that his heart not, etc. — meaning that water, even though clear, nevertheless helps regarding not being forbidden because of exposed nakedness; but there is still prohibition because his heart sees the nakedness, since the heart is with the nakedness in the water; therefore specifically garment covering on his heart is required; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 200 I expanded on this at length.

Not called head covering — it appears this is specifically regarding blessing or uttering other sacred matters from his mouth; but regarding not sitting or walking with exposed head casually, a little covering by hand suffices, as written in siman 8.
