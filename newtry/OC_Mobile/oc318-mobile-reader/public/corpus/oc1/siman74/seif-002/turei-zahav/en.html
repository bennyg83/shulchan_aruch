So that his heart should not, etc. Although the water is clear, it is nevertheless effective insofar as he is not prohibited because of exposed ervah. There remains, however, a prohibition because his heart sees the ervah, since his heart and ervah are both within the water. He therefore specifically requires a garment covering his heart. I discussed this at length in Yoreh Deah siman 200, with Hashem's help.
It is not considered a head covering. It appears that this applies specifically to reciting a blessing or uttering other sacred words. Merely to avoid sitting or walking briefly with an uncovered head, however, covering it with his hand suffices, as I wrote in seif katan 8.
