(7) Should cover with a garment, etc. — meaning that the water, although clear, nevertheless its covering helps so that he not be forbidden due to exposure of nakedness — on condition that he put his head outside the water and not gaze at the nakedness; since the water is clear and one's nakedness is visible in it, only that there is still prohibition because one's heart sees the nakedness, since the heart is with the nakedness in the water — therefore one must cover with a garment from below up to the heart so that there be an interposition between heart and nakedness. And the same if he interposes with a garment on his heart — it also helps, as above in section 61.

(8) When he recites — and the same applies that he should cover his head, so that he not recite with exposed head.

(9) But with hands, etc. — whether he covers on his heart or between his heart and nakedness, if he does not embrace with his arms as below; and the reason is that one body does not cover another body — specifically with his own hands. But if others cover his heart with their hands, it is permitted.

(10) Is not called, etc. — meaning, regarding being permitted to bless or utter other holy words from his mouth; and see above siman 2 and below siman 91 in Mishna Berurah.

(11) Murky — and if they are clear, he muddies them with his feet; and this applies specifically on the ground, but not in a vessel that has no dirt and mud — so wrote Magen Avraham. But Or Zarua, siman 128, wrote that even one bathing himself in a tub, this applies.

(12) Their odor is bad — see below siman 86, and in Mishna Berurah there.

(13) Only above, etc. — for water is called an interposition between his heart and nakedness. And he should also beware that his eyes be outside the water and that he not gaze at the nakedness; and as above, that it is no better than nakedness by lamplight, explained in siman 75, section 5, that it is forbidden.
