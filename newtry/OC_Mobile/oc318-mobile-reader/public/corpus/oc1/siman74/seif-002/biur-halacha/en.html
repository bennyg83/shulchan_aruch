And if the water was murky so his limbs are not visible etc. — and this is preferable to when he was dressed in a shirt without pants, for there too his limbs are not visible from above because they cling to the body and are like ground adjacent to ground, as stated in the Gemara; and thereby it is called that his heart does not see the ervah. See in siman 91, Magen Avraham section 2.
