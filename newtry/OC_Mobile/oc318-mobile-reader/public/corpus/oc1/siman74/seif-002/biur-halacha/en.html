If the water was murky, so that his limbs, etc. This is more effective than wearing a shirt without trousers, although in that case as well his limbs cannot be seen from above. The reason is that the water adheres to his body and is comparable to solid ground, as stated in the Gemara. It is therefore considered that his heart does not see the ervah. See siman 91, Magen Avraham seif katan 2.
