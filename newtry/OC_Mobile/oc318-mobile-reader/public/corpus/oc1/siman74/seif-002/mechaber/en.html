One who is bathing naked in clear water and wishes to drink should cover himself with a garment from below his heart, so that his heart will not see his ervah when he recites the blessing. This specifically requires a garment; covering with the hands is not considered a covering. {Rama: The same applies if he covers his head with his hands; this is not considered a covering.} But if his heart is beneath the water while his ervah is also beneath the water, some authorities permit him to recite the blessing, while others require a separation between his heart and his ervah. If the water is cloudy so that his ervah cannot be seen through it, it is considered a covering.
