One who bathes naked in clear water and wants to recite [Shema] should cover with a garment from below up to his heart, so that his heart not see the nakedness when he recites. And this is specifically with a garment; but with hands it is not considered covering.And the same applies if one covers his head with his hands — it is not called covering for the head; see below siman 91(Terumat HaDeshen in the name of Or Zarua).} And if the water is murky such that one's limbs are not visible in it, it is permitted to recite while he is in them, provided there is no bad odor. {Rama: And if one's heart is not in the water but only above the water, even in clear water it is permitted (Da'at Acharonim).) ואם היו המים עכורים שאין איבריו נראין בהם מותר לקרות והוא בתוכן והוא שלא יהא ריחן רע: ואם אין לבו בתוך המים רק למעלה מן המים אף בצלולים שרי (ד"ע.
