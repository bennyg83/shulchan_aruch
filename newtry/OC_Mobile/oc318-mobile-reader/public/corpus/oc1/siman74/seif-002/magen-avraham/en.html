(א) He should cover with a garment from below up to his heart. But his head, since it is above the water, is permitted, as written in the gloss.

(ב) With hands, no. And even though we say in Shabbos 120: if one placed writing on his flesh and wants to stand naked, he covers it with his hand — that case is different, for there the hand is one species and the Name another species; unlike hand and body, which are all one species, and min b'mino does not interpose (Terumat HaDeshen). And furthermore, in the Gemara we also decree lest he forget and remove his hand.

(ג) Murky. If they are clear, he muddies them with his feet (Terumat Yisrael). And this applies specifically in a spring; but not in a vessel that has no dirt and mud.

(ד) Above the water. Since his eyes are outside the water and he does not gaze at the nakedness (Beit Yosef, Rashba end of section 75).
