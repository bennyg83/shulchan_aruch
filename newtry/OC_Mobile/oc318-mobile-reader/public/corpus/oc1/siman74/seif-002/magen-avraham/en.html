He should cover himself with a garment below his heart. Since his head is above the water, however, it is permitted, as stated in the gloss.
Not with his hands. Although it is stated in Shabbos 120 that if a Divine Name is written on one's flesh and one wishes to stand naked, he covers it with his hand, that case is different: The hand is one type of object and the writing is another. Here, however, the hand and body are all one type, and an object of the same type does not constitute a separation. Terumat HaDeshen. Furthermore, in that Gemara too we decree lest he forget and remove his hand.
Murky. If the water is clear, he should make it murky with his feet. Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah. This applies specifically in a spring, but not in a vessel that contains no earth or mud.
Above the water. Since his eyes are outside the water and he does not look at the ervah. Beit Yosef; Rashba. See the end of siman 75.
