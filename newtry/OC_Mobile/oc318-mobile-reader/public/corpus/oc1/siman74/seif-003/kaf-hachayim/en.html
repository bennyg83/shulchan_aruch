(3) One standing naked in a house who stuck his head outside through a window so he would not see his nakedness — even if he covered his heart with a garment, he may not recite, since his nakedness is uncovered and visible in the house. But one sleeping naked in a canopy that is not ten handbreadths high who stuck his head outside it and also covered his heart with a garment — even though his nakedness is uncovered in the canopy, this is like being covered in a tallit, since the canopy is not ten high there is cover over it; and it is not like a house, which is a separate domain for itself; and since ervah is visible inside it is forbidden. Magen Avraham at the start of the siman and section 8. Razah note 2; Ben Ish Chai, Parshas Bo note 1; Piskei Teshuvos note 1. And if he also takes his heart outside the window — then his head and majority are outside and it is as if he is entirely outside. The same if there is feces in the house and he stuck his head outside the window — he may not recite, for we place the head after the majority; and if he brought his head into a room where there is feces, he may not recite either, for in any case the feces is opposite him. Magen Avraham there. And Birkei Yosef agreed, note 2 — unlike one authority in Hagahot Taz who wrote there is room to waver in this law; see there, Shaarei Teshuvah note 1.
