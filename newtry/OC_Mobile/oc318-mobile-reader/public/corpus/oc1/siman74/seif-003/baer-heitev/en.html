As an interruption. He explains that if one stands in clear water and his heart too is in the water, and he needs to interrupt between his heart and his nakedness—for this suffices to interrupt, even though covering with his hand is not considered covering since it is one body—nevertheless an interruption is an interruption, Beit Yosef. And Maharam Tivoli disagrees whether covering, which is preferable to interruption, does not help because the heart and hand are one body—all the more so interruption does not help for this very reason. And Pri Chadash wrote that we rule thus.
