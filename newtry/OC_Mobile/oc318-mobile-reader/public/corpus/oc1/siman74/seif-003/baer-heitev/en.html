As a separation. Meaning that if he is standing in clear water and his heart is also within the water, so that he must separate between his heart and his nakedness, it is sufficient for him to place his hand between them. Although covering with his own hand is not considered a covering, because his hand and body are one body, it is nevertheless considered a separation. Beit Yosef. Maharam Tivoli disagrees. Since even a covering, which is more effective than a separation, does not help because the heart and hand are one body, then certainly a separation does not help for that very reason. Peri Chadash writes that this is the ruling we follow.
