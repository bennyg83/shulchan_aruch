(14) As an interposition — meaning, if he stands in clear water and his heart is also in the water, and he must interpose between his heart and his nakedness — embracing suffices to interpose, even though ordinary covering with his hands is explained in section 62 that it does not help; this is preferable. And the reason: since it is not people's way to embrace themselves thus, and he does so, it is called an interposition, even though it is one body. And so the Acharonim agreed, unlike Maharam Tiktin and Peri Chadash, who are stringent in this. And when he goes in a shirt without trousers and presses with his hands on his shirt on the heart or below the heart to interpose between heart and nakedness — according to them too it is permitted.
