(14) As an interruption. Meaning, if he stands in clear water and his heart is also within the water, so that he must interrupt between his heart and ervah, embracing himself is sufficient. Although ordinary covering with his hands is ineffective, as explained in seif 2, this is better. Since people do not ordinarily embrace themselves in this manner and he specifically does so, it is considered an interruption even though it is one body. The Acharonim agree with this, contrary to Maharam Tivoli and Peri Chadash, who are stringent. If he walks in a shirt without trousers and presses the shirt with his hands at the heart or below the heart to interrupt between his heart and ervah, even those authorities permit it.
