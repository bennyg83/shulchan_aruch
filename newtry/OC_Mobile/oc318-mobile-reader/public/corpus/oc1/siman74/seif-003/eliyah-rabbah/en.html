(3) And if his heart is not inside etc. — The reason, wrote Olas Tamid, is that they were lenient regarding the heart more than sight of the eyes — for even if the heart sees ervah in clear water, since it is above the water it is permitted, until here. The Bach's wording needs study — for seemingly he wrote this language: since his heart is above the water, behold his heart does not see ervah in the water, until here. And study is needed — for in clear water one sees ervah in the water; and perhaps even in clear water, what is called "seeing" is not seeing relative to the heart, since his heart is above the water.
