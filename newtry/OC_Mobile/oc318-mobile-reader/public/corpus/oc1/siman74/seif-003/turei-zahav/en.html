We judge it as a stop. Meaning: if he stands in clear water and his heart is also within the water, and he must stop between his heart and his nakedness — with this stopping suffices; and even according to what is written in siman 91 that hand covering is not covering since it is one body — here we do not require except stopping the heart's vision to nakedness, and certainly this suffices, for he does not see; and this is similar to a woman for whom "her heart sees her nakedness" does not apply, since in any case she does not see, even though there is no stopping there outside her body — so too here.
