We treat it as an interruption. Meaning, if he is standing in clear water and his heart is also within the water, so that he must interrupt between his heart and his ervah, this interruption suffices. Although I wrote in siman 91 that covering with one's hand is not considered a covering because the hand and body form one body, this case differs, because only an interruption preventing the heart from seeing the ervah is required, and this certainly suffices, since the heart does not see it. This resembles a woman, to whom the concept of her heart seeing her ervah does not apply because it does not in fact see it, even though there is no interruption consisting of something external to her body. The same applies here.
