Siman 3, when stepping, etc. Midrash Shochar Tov.

And the measure, etc.—that one must resemble the priests in their service, as wrote above, siman 98, seif 4; and he said in Yerushalmi, beginning of Berachot: like priests, for it is written "do not go up on steps to my altar"—they would walk heel alongside thumb and thumb alongside heel. And although for standing in prayer we hold "like angels," nevertheless there is a distinction for this matter; and see Tur, siman 95, and Tur on Rosh, beginning of Ahavat Olam veAhavta.

And lekhatchilah, etc. As above; and further, as wrote: one leaving the synagogue should not step, etc.—and all the more so here.
