And some are accustomed to step the right foot toward the left, and afterward step with the left. Levush. Bach wrote that if there is pressure in the synagogue, one may reduce from the measure of thumb alongside heel and rely on Rashba, siman 581, that there is no measure for these steps; and so everyone has been accustomed to step without measure, due to pressure. But where there is no pressure, one must be precise to step thumb alongside heel, so it ends. Magen Avraham wrote: and in our view, Rashba did not write except that one need not step a medium step, which is an amah, see there; but less than heel alongside thumb is not called a step at all, see there. And the custom now is that if one finishes the prayer and another person after him is praying so that he cannot take three steps, he waits until the other finishes his prayer and afterward takes the three steps. Knesset HaGedolah.
