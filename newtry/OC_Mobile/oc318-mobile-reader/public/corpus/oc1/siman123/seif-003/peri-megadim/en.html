**And not** — Taz: See Beit Yosef and Bach, who explained that it is like a dog that returns to its vomit — literally one casts vomit and returns and eats it; so too this one distances from his master and accepts [rebuke] not to do again, only that he should return in repentance, etc., see there. And I did not understand the words of Taz, for still it is not similar to returning to one's vomit; if so it is enough with the explanation of Beit Yosef. And the matter of four cubits — behold in synagogue one who prays two prayers waits four cubits so that his mind should supplicate — if not so, it does not appear like a dog returning, for he must pray a second time (Eliyah Rabbah); and a prayer leader who returns immediately after walking four cubits because he needs to lead the congregation in Amidah does not appear like returning, etc.; but one alone in his house — why does [waiting] four cubits help? (Eliyah Rabbah, and Aruch haShulchan on this). From the words of Taz it appears that whoever waits four cubits there is no disgrace, etc.; nevertheless one must wait for the prayer leader when there is a prayer leader, and it requires study. And for Taz in synagogue certainly one alone [waiting four cubits] is enough with this — understand.
