(א) “when stepping,” etc., first — see in Mishna Berurah the reason, which is from Magen Avraham. And Magen Avraham wrote that therefore a left-footed person should uproot first his left [foot], which is the world's right. And there is another reason brought also in Taz, that therefore there is no difference between left-footed and not left-footed; and Be'er Heitev states plainly thus. However, Chayei Adam and the Gra copied Magen Avraham's first reason, and for a left-footed person the law changes, as we wrote.

(ב) “and the measure of steps,” etc. — and regarding the order of the three steps, see in Mishna Berurah seif kaf-yud-bet, and it is from Orchot Chaim. And even Rabbeinu Menachem holds that each step is moving both legs; and according to him one must do thus: step first the left leg, and then step with the right and place it level with the left, and this counts as one step; and so do three times. And Lechem Chamudos and Eliyah Rabbah held to his words, and Peri Megadim brought them. Nevertheless I did not want to conceal this, because many acharonim copied Orchot Chaim's words as halachah — are they not: the Levush holds like him that a step is moving one leg; and likewise Sha'arei Teshuvah in the name of Birkhei Yosef, and Chayei Adam copied it as halachah; and likewise the Gra in this siman under "and the measure of steps" also copied the wording brought in Orchot Chaim, that these steps must have heel beside big toe and big toe beside heel like kohanim. And it is known that this wording indicates moving one leg, as Lechem Chamudos himself wrote.
