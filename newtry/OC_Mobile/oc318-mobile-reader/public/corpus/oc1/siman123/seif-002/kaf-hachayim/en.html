(2) There, three steps, etc. And the reason for these three steps Mahari"a, may his memory be blessed, wrote in the name of Orchot Chaim that they correspond to the three mil Israel distanced themselves from Mount Sinai at the time of giving the Torah. And it is further found in the Haggadah that Moshe Rabbeinu entered in darkness of cloud and fog before Hakadosh Baruch Hu, and when he came out he came out from those three; and therefore we take three steps, until here. And Shelah wrote in the name of the Geonim a reason for three steps: because when a person stands in prayer he stands in a holy place and the Shechinah is above his head; and since he takes leave of his prayer he needs three steps backward to leave a holy place and stand in a mundane place. And proof to the matter is thus: since they return backward three steps they give peace to one another — meaning, until now we were in a holy place and we went out to a mundane place, until here. And in the name of Rabbeinu Hai Gaon he wrote that the reason for three steps is because prayers were instituted corresponding to the tamid offerings; and when the kohen ascended to the altar with the limbs of the tamid he would ascend via the right and circle and descend via the left; and between the ramp and the altar were three stone courses, and he would descend on them three steps on the heel; and we do as they would do, until here — Beit Yosef, Levush. And Magen Avraham note 1 wrote that he heard a reason: because by merit of three steps Nevuchadnezzar merited to destroy the Beit HaMikdash; and therefore we step three steps and pray that the Beit HaMikdash be rebuilt — see there. And in Sha'ar HaKavanot end of derush 6 of standing he wrote the reason: because in the standing prayer one was in the aspect of the world of Atzilut, and now he distances himself from them three steps corresponding to the three worlds Beriah, Yetzirah, Asiyah — see there. And the plain meaning of the matter is: he steps backward like a servant taking leave of his master — Magen Avraham there; and likewise Levush there. And see further above siman 95 letter zayin.
