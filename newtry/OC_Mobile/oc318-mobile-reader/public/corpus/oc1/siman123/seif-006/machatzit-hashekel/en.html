(Note 6) And not as [some say] — the reason we require six steps means: the reason we step three steps is to resemble angels, as it is written of them “and their feet were a straight foot” — their feet: two [and] one more foot, and there are three; and it is written afterward “and the soles of their feet like the sole of a calf's foot,” and there are another three; and we require six steps. And Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: these are three when stepping backward and three when returning forward (and in his note 10). And from Hamburg they are strict, etc., and he wrote there that they act not according to law: when they see someone coming to interrupt within the six steps and pass there, they rush to return to their places and do not wait the amount one must pause, as explained here; rather they push with the hand one who wants to pass, and if the passer does not listen to him, the passer bears his sin and he is clean.
