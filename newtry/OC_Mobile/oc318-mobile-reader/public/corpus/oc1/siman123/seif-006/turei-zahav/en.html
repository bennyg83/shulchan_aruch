But he does not say at the end. It appears the reason for this is that indeed "Heaven, open my lips" he must say so that Heaven help him give heart-intention to pray; but after the prayer, when he prays on behalf of others, it is not appropriate to say "and the meditation of my heart," for he did not pray for himself. Rather it should have been "and the meditation of the heart of those who hear the prayer," and that is not in the wording of the verse.
