One bows and steps three steps. The reason in Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Hai: prayers correspond to the tamid offerings, and when the kohen would ascend the altar with the limbs of the tamid he would ascend by way of the right and circle and descend by way of the left; and between the ramp and the altar were three stone courses, and he would descend on them with three heel-to-heel steps. And we do as they would do—until here.
