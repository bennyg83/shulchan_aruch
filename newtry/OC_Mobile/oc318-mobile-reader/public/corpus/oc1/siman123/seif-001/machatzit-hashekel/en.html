Seif katan 1: Three, etc. This is because of the merit of the three steps that Nevuchadnetzar took in honor of the Holy One, blessed is He. The Gemara actually states four steps in Sanhedrin, chapter Chelek, 96a. See Maharsha, Chiddushei Aggados, there.
Its plain meaning, etc. Like a servant, etc. According to this explanation, there still is no reason why specifically three steps are required.
