(1) He bows and steps, etc. — like a servant taking leave of his master; and the measure of bowing I already wrote above in siman 113 in Biur Halacha that it is so that all the vertebrae of his spine align; afterward I found in this siman that so too writes Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Tziyun L'Derech.
(2) Three steps — see in Beit Yosef several reasons; and they also wrote a reason because our Sages said that through the merit of three steps Nevuchadnezzar ran to honor Heaven and merited to destroy the Beit HaMikdash; therefore we step three steps and pray that the Beit HaMikdash be rebuilt.
(3) And after he stepped — and not like those who say oseh shalom while still stepping, for it is not proper to do so; and likewise the law regarding oseh shalom of Kaddish (Eliyah Rabbah).
(4) To the side of his left — for the one praying sees himself as though the Shechinah is before his face, and the left of a person is the right side of the Holy One blessed be He.
(5) "Shalom aleinu" — not like those who say "upon us and upon all Israel"; rather "and upon all Israel" he says when bowing before Him.
