Three steps. See in responsa Chut HaSheni, siman 54, that through the merit of three steps Nebuchadnezzar merited to destroy the Temple, and therefore we take three steps and pray that the Temple be rebuilt.

And after he stepped. And not like those who say "oseh shalom" while still stepping, for it is not proper to do so. Magen Avraham.

Alenu. Not like those who say "alenu ve'al kol Yisrael." Magen Avraham.
