When he says "oseh shalom," etc.—that is, he straightens up toward his right side and turns his face toward his left and gives peace. And from where do we learn the bowings in Kaddish? Five: in Yitgadal he bows and straightens; in Shemey rabba, at B'agleh, he bows and they answer Amen and he straightens; in Yisborach he bows; in Shemey dekudsha berich hu le'eila, he bows; in Tikabel he bows; in Kaddam avuhon he straightens; in Oseh he bows; in Shalom he straightens to the right, and afterward he gives peace on the left. And they were accustomed to bow before them like a servant taking leave of his master (Mordechai, end of chapter 1 of Avodah Zarah).
