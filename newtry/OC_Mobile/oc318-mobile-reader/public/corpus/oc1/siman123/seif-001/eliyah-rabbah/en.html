(1) In one bow, etc. R. Isaiah, may his memory be blessed, explained: in the bow there was one step backward — meaning the measure of a step of the knees; so wrote Shibolei Haleket there, and it needs examination. And in Sefer Tzidah Laderech p. 44 it implies that this bow must be until the vertebrae are bent, like the bows of Avot and Modim explained in siman 113.
