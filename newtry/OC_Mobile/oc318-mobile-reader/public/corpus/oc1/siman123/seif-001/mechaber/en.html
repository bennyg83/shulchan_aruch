The laws of bowing at the conclusion of the Amidah. It contains 6 seifim: One bows and steps backward 3 steps while remaining in 1 bow. After taking the 3 steps, while still bowed and before straightening, when saying 'Oseh shalom bimromav' he turns his face toward his left; when saying 'Hu ya'aseh shalom aleinu' he turns his face toward his right. Afterward he bows forward like a servant taking leave of his master. {Rama: The custom is then to say, 'May it be Your will that the Beit HaMikdash be rebuilt,' etc., because prayer is in place of the Temple service. We therefore pray for the Beit HaMikdash so that we may perform the actual service. [Rama's own ruling]}
