(18) In a loud voice he does not need — for he relies on the steps of the Kaddish after Uva leTzion, even though they interrupt for Torah reading and Hallel and Avinu Malkeinu — all of them come for the order of prayer and the Kaddish after them returns primarily upon the Amidah prayer [Terumat HaDeshen]; and from here the later authorities inferred that the prayer leader should not speak between prayers in conversation [not related to prayer] because the Kaddish returns upon the prayer.

(19) To return — and if he comes to step also after the Amidah prayer aloud, one should not protest him.

(20) He steps three steps — for at first he must step and take leave regarding his own supplications such as Elokai netzor, and afterward he needs [to step] after Kaddish regarding the prayer he prayed on behalf of the congregation.
