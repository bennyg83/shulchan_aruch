Taz note 5, Kedushah — Bach understood that returning to his place and turning his face are all one; and if so this ruling contradicts in two [ways] the Mechaber's words: one, that the Mechaber wrote at least until the chazzan begins, and here he says until the chazzan finishes; and further, the Mechaber says lechatchilah until Kedushah, and here even lechatchilah it is not needed. Therefore Taz answered that returning to his place is different.
