In a loud voice he does not need. For he relies on the Kaddish after and Uva leTzion, even though they interrupt for Torah reading and Hallel and Avinu Malkeinu — all of them come for the order of prayer (Terumat HaDeshen siman 13).

He does not need to return. And if he is accustomed to step, one should not protest him (Radbaz siman 153, and see in Hagahot chapter 9 of Berachos).

He steps three steps. For at first one must step and take leave regarding one's own supplications such as Elokai netzor, and afterward one needs [to step] after Kaddish regarding the prayer one prayed on behalf of the congregation (Levush).
