Forbidden. At the end of the Taz: Bach wrote that this contradicts what the Mechaber wrote—that one may not return until the prayer leader begins (and it should have said "or says"). He, may his memory be a blessing, answered that one stands in the place where he stepped; only that it is forbidden to turn one's face toward the congregation so as not to confuse them. And see Levush, seif 6; Magen Avraham brought it, sign 9. He explained it regarding a different matter—that they might suspect him of skipping or of turning his face away from the place of the Divine Presence, since the congregation stands before Him. And it implies that it does not depend on the prayer leader's finishing alone. And see siman 103 in Magen Avraham, sign 2; and see Netziv, who explained that if he finished before the prayer leader, behold he waited a long time, and there is no prohibition in waiting until the prayer leader finishes—it suffices. Do we require that he begin only when the prayer leader finishes? And if he waits four cubits' walk, then he appears like one who returns to his vomit—see there.
