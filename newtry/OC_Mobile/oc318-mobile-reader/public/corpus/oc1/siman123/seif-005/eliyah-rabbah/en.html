(5) And at least, etc. He meant to say: lechatchilah the halachah is until Kedushah; but when the place is narrow and people come to argue and it is crowded, one may be lenient and return when the chazzan begins; and so ruled Bach. And Lechem Chamudos distinguishes that when they say piyyutim he returns when the chazzan begins. And if he returns immediately to his place, the Gemara says he is like a dog returning to its vomit — see Beit Yosef for Levush's explanation. But in the responsum of Rashba siman 376 and 475, and responsum of Binyamin Ze'ev siman 567, they explained that since he returns immediately to his place it looks as though he wants to confess again, as is brought below in the laws of Yom Kippur regarding one who confessed on Yom Kippur [about sins already confessed], etc., like a dog, etc. And see Likkutei Maharil.
