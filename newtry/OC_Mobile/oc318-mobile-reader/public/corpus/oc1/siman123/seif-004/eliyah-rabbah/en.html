[4] He should stand, etc. He must align his feet as during the Amidah, Knesses HaGedolah writes in the name of Radach. This somewhat implies that each step should be completed with both feet, as he writes in the name of Lechem Chamudos. Knesses HaGedolah concludes, “See further there and in responsum Binyamin Ze’ev 167.” He means the other laws of this subject, for the requirement to align one’s feet does not appear in Binyamin Ze’ev. Olas Tamid was misled and thought that the reference applied to this law, but he was not precise.
