(4) He shall stand, etc., and is obligated to align his feet as in prayer — Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Radbaz; and it implies somewhat from this that one should make each step with two legs, as he wrote in the name of Lechem Chamudos; and Knesset HaGedolah concluded thus in his wording. And see further there and in the responsum of Binyamin Ze'ev siman 567, until here. And he meant to say from other laws in this matter; but this law that one must align his feet — there is nothing in the responsum of Binyamin Ze'ev; see there. And Olas Tamid erred and thought he spoke of this law and did not examine closely.
