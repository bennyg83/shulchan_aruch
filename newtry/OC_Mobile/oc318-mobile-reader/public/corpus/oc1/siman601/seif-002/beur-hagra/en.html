Seif 2, even in Eretz Yisrael, etc. Although we are expert in the secret of the calendar and nothing will occur that would require us to observe two days, for we establish the calendar by calculation, nevertheless we must act according to the custom of our fathers. As written in the first chapter of Yom Tov regarding the two festival days of the Diaspora: be careful with the custom, etc., lest, etc. For this reason the same applies to Rosh Hashanah in Eretz Yisrael, for otherwise an egg would also be permitted, as written there 5b: Does he not agree, etc.; and there 6a: He said to him, Let the master sit now, etc.; Rav Mordechai said to him, etc. See the Rosh there, siman 4, and Baal HaMilchamos there.
