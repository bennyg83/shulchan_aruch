[Levush] And he says Le-E-l Orech Din, etc. As for the fact that on the first day they say it at Shacharis, perhaps since there are two opinions in the Gemara whether He sits and judges during the first three hours or during the second three hours, they therefore do it once at Shacharis and once at Musaf, during the second three hours. See siman 596, s.k. 2.
