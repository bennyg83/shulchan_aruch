[Levush] And they blow, etc. The Taz wrote at length regarding a question where Rosh Hashanah fell on Thursday and Friday and they did not receive a shofar until after they had prayed the Shabbos prayer while it was still day. He permitted blowing without a blessing, and this is the main ruling. Although one may question some of his proofs, especially what he wrote that even on Shabbos there is no prohibition to blow, for it is a skill and not a melachah, as stated in the Talmud, but is only prohibited because of the decree lest one carry it in the public domain, and whenever one blows merely permissively, etc. He follows his own reasoning at the end of siman 596, and I already challenged him there.
