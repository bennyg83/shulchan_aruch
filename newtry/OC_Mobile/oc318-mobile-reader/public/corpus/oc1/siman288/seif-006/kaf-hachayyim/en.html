6) The Yerushalmi teaches: Rabbi Chaggai said, 'Shabbosos and Yamim Tovim were given only for eating and drinking.' Rabbi Berechyah said, 'They were given only for engagement in Torah.' Tanchuma explains that they do not disagree. Rabbi Berechyah's statement concerning Torah study refers to laborers who engage in their work throughout the week and come on Shabbos to engage in Torah. Rabbi Chaggai's statement concerning delight refers to Torah scholars who labor in Torah throughout the week and delight themselves on Shabbos. Regarding Rava's statement that all agree that on Shabbos we require a portion 'for you,' as stated in siman 242, one can answer that there he speaks according to Rabbi Eliezer and Rabbi Yehoshua. Alternatively, there he requires some delight, while here the discussion is whether to spend the entire day in delight. Beis Yosef. Rema cites this below in siman 290, seif 2 in the gloss. See Bach, siman 242, who discusses this at length and concludes that on Yom Tov, half the day is for Hashem and half for oneself, while on Shabbos most of the day is for Hashem and a minority for oneself; see there. He similarly writes in this siman: Above in siman 242, I concluded that even a Torah scholar needs only a minority of the day for himself, with most devoted to Torah. These are his words. See further our words above in siman 242, paragraphs 5 and 6; see there.
