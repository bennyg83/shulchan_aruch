(6) We learn in Yerushalmi: R' Chagai said: Shabbatot and Yamim Tovim were given only for eating and drinking. R' Berechiah said: they were given only to engage in them with words of Torah. And in Tanhuma it explains they do not disagree: what R' Berechiah said about Torah — those are laborers who are busy with their work all weekdays and on Shabbat come and engage in Torah; and what R' Chagai said about indulgence — those are Torah scholars who toil in Torah all weekdays and on Shabbat indulge:
