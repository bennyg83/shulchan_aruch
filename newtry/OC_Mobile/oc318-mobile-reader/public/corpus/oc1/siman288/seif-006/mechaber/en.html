One who fasts on Shabbos because of a [disturbing] dream must recite the passage Aneinu without concluding [it with a blessing] after he finishes his [Shemoneh Esreh] prayers. It is also desirable to recite the passage, “Master of the worlds, it is known and revealed before You…”.Rema: And one says after his prayer 'רבון העולמים גלוי כו' like during the week.
