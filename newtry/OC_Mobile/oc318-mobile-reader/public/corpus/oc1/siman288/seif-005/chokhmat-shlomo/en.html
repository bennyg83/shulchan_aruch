Seif 5: Some say one should not fast a dream fast on Shabbat. Note: I was asked regarding one who fasts a dream fast for his friend — whether he may fast on Shabbat. I answered he should not fast for several reasons. Are they not in my compositions on Orach Chayyim on the two quarters in Vayigash on the last page, side 2?
