It is permitted to fast the fast of a disturbing dream on Shabbat, in order that the decree of judgement may be torn up -- and they need to fast on Sunday, in order to atone for nullifying the delight of Shabbat. And if their strength is weakened and they can't fast two consecutive days, they don't have to fast on Sunday and should fast after that.Also, if Sunday is Chanukah or Purim or a festival, even the second day of a' festival in the Diaspora, one shouldn't fast until after then. Some say, one who had a nap in the middle of the day and dreamt a bad dream should fast from noon to midnight and then make havdalah, and fast on Sunday -- it's as though that person fasted the whole day of Shabbat.
