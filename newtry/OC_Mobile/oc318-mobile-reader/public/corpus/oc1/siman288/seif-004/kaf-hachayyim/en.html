4) There: It is forbidden to fast on Shabbos, etc. See Responsa Rashba, volume 4, siman 262, who writes in the name of Rabbi Yitzchak ibn Gias that the prohibition against fasting on Shabbos and Yom Tov is from the Torah. This is also stated in Tanchuma, Bereishis. See there, where it states that honoring Shabbos is superior to one thousand fasts. For this reason, it overrides the rabbinic fast of Esther.
