(ב) (כח) One dangerously ill that day — but one who is not dangerously ill, no; and when they say Mi sheberach for a sick person without danger they say 'It is Shabbat, not a time to cry out; healing will soon come.' And to bless one struggling to give birth is certainly permitted, for she is included among the dangerously ill; and likewise a woman within the first week of childbirth — it appears according to all one need not be stringent:
