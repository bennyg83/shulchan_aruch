[One who is] pursued by a bad spirit — that which they said [permitting crying out on Shabbos] is not specifically [that case]; the same law applies to any illness that poses a danger that day: they cry out and offer supplications. And likewise the custom is to say prayers [Metzla'in] on Shabbos over the dangerously ill who face a danger that day. {Rama: And likewise it is permitted to bless the dangerously ill person on that very day (Likkutei Mahari Bruna; Beis Yosef in the name of the Ran, third chapter of Ta'anis).}
