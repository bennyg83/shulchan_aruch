One afflicted by an evil spirit — as they said, this is not specific; rather the same law applies to all illness that has danger that day — they cry out and plead; and likewise the custom is to say prayers on Shabbat for dangerously ill people with danger that day:And likewise it is permitted to bless the dangerously ill person on that day (Likutei Mahari Berin; Beit Yosef in the name of Ran chapter 3 of Taanit).
