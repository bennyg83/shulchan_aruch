(10) In the gloss: to weep on Shabbat. And see this in the book Shulchan David in the collection of laws section Aninus letter 24 what he wrote on this. And see Orach Tzedek in the name of responsa Ohr Chadash siman 76 regarding an onen on Yom Tov — whether he may weep over his dead between death and burial; since he is exempt from all mitzvot, presumably he is exempt from the mitzvah of joy too, as he differs from other positive mitzvot; and the prohibition of weeping is so as not to prevent joy of Yom Tov, and the Sages have power to exempt the onen:
