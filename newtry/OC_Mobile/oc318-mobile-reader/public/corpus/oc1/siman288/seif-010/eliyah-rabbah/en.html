[10] Until half the night, etc. In derashot of Maharash he wrote only until night; and wrote one must fast Sunday because some say one must sit a fast for one who completes on Shabbat eve until night (Malbush Yom Tov); and it appears to me to rely on him on the first day of Yom Tov or on Shabbat when Yom Tov follows afterward — on Sunday or on the second day of Yom Tov that falls on the sixth day one need not fast except until night; also it appears so for one whose way is to fast even on Shabbat and he dreamed at noon — even though it is possible one who fasts already does not count; and with this it appears there is no obligation to sit a fast for his fast — for it is not nullification of Shabbat pleasure since his way is to fast. Shelah wrote daf 200: one who dreamed even in the middle of the night should not taste anything — and not enough that when he begins to fast when day breaks like other fasts; and proof from one who sleeps afternoon sleep — he begins fasting immediately; also it appears he should separate from his wife even on Shabbat which is time of conjugal relations — for if permitted to fast and nullify Shabbat pleasure, all the more this; end of his words; and likewise Magen Avraham siman 564. In my humble opinion one may distinguish between one who sleeps afternoon sleep by day and this at night; and some proof from those who fast a dream fast on Rosh Hashanah also on the second and do not fast at night; and Magen Avraham wrote in siman 596 that in a dream they showed him only by day and not at night, see there; also it appears one may distinguish between a fast that is for himself and conjugal relations obligated to his wife:
