(9) In the gloss. And likewise one who has pleasure if he weeps, etc., as stated in the Aggadah that they found the disciples of R' Akiva weeping on Shabbat and he said 'I have pleasure.' And it appears this means that from his great cleaving to Heaven his eyes pour tears, as we find with R' Akiva in Zohar Chadash that he wept greatly when saying Shir HaShirim because he knew where the matters reach.
