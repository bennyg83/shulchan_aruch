The same applies between Yom Kippur and Sukkos. Magen Avraham adds that even if the first day is Rosh Chodesh Nisan, Rosh Chodesh Av, or Erev Pesach, he fasts. Thus far his words. Perishah, however, cites Rama that on a day when Tachanun is not recited, one does not fast. It is proper to rely upon this. Chiddushei Rashba cites an opinion that for a dream fast one does not need to observe an additional fast for having fasted on Shabbos, because the Talmudic discussion concerns one who voluntarily accepted a fast upon himself on Shabbos. See there. Responsa Binyamin Ze’ev writes similarly. Although Rashba there and Rivash, siman 513, write that most poskim disagree, nevertheless, since many poskim also hold that one need not fast specifically on Sunday, and even according to Rashba there is no absolute obligation, one should not fast on a day when Tachanun is omitted. See the end of siman 420, where I challenged Magen Avraham. Nevertheless, if one fasts a dream fast on those days, even on Rosh Chodesh Nisan or Rosh Chodesh Av, he need not observe an additional fast for having fasted that day.
