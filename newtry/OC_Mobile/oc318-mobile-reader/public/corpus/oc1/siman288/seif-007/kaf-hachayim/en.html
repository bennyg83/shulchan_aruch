(7) [seif 2] Some say a person whom eating harms, etc. — meaning if even an egg or olive harms him; but if he can eat an egg or olive he must fulfill the Shabbat meal as written below rabbi siman 291, see there. A weak person who must fast by mouth in order to eat matzah at night from appetite — if it fell on Shabbat it is forbidden for him to fast on Shabbat; he should only refrain from eating his usual measure so he can eat matzah at the Seder with appetite.
