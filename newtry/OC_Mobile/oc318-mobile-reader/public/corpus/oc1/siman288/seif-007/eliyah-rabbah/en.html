[7] [Levush] In any case one must, etc. — and if on Sunday it is a public fast one is lenient in error in Shiltai Gibborim that it counts; but in responsum of Masat Binyamin siman 93 he ruled it does not count and one must fast another day; and he brought proof from Terumat HaDeshen siman 278 who wrote: one who fasts a dream fast on Rosh Hashanah need not sit a fast for his fast — for some say it is a mitzvah to fast on Rosh Hashanah; it implies if not so a fast would be required and the Fast of Gedaliah does not suffice, end of his words; and likewise ruled Olat Tamid and Magen Avraham; but Taz challenged and wrote two responsa on the matter: one — Rosh Hashanah deals if it occurred he did not fast on the Fast of Gedaliah, such as he had coercion; second — the essence of the fast is that he intend it be atonement for what he fasted and they think repentance — and on this he says one needs a fast for his fast — meaning he should intend on the Fast of Gedaliah that it be atonement, end of his words briefly. In my humble opinion Taz did not see the body of Terumat HaDeshen — for he wrote there he saw many people fasting on Rosh Hashanah and all these did not sit a fast for their fasts — and it appears because of the mitzvah to fast on Rosh Hashanah, end of his language; and if so on the first [reason] it is difficult how it is possible to say they all did not fast on the Fast of Gedaliah and coercion happened to them; and on the second it is difficult perhaps those people intended for atonement; also clear proof Taz brought from Yoreh De'ah end siman 217 — he did not see it is so below siman 568 seif 11; and in truth it is no proof at all as Magen Avraham wrote — for here atonement is required and atonement b'dieved we do not find; also I looked in Shiltai Gibborim itself and he is uncertain for this reason. And I heard a challenge on Masat Binyamin mentioned above — perhaps Terumat HaDeshen needed the reason that fasting on Rosh Hashanah is a mitzvah; for if because of the Fast of Gedaliah — if so when one fasts two days of Rosh Hashanah what can be said? For he needs to fast two days; and it appears to me if it is a public fast it counts — if so the same law that one person's fast helps when he fasted two days; and further it appears what Terumat HaDeshen wrote there requires study whether the fast of the Ten Days of Repentance helps or not since he fasts anyway, end of his words. It implies he fasted all the Ten Days and even so he says the reason of mitzvah, etc.; and from this it is heard that all the Ten Days do not count for one accustomed to fast — unlike Olat Tamid who wrote days a person accepts a fast upon himself such as the Ten Days of Repentance count, end of his language, end of his words. From Terumat HaDeshen mentioned above it does not imply so — and this is proof for what Magen Avraham wrote: one who has a custom to fast on the first day of Selichot — the Ten Days do not count for him.
