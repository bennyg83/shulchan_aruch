(1) [seif 1] It is forbidden to fast on Shabbat, etc., and for the purpose of a fast even one hour is forbidden. Maharil Laws of Fasting; and likewise Rashi in Eruvin page 41. And if so, some congregations act improperly who fast on Shabbat until midday over decrees. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Eliyahu Rabbah letter 1, Tosafos letter 1; and even a fast from meat and wine — even though there is no obligation to eat them, it is forbidden. Beit Yosef letter 2:
