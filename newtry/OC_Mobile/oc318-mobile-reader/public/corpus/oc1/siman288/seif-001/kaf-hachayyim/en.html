1) [Seif 1] It is forbidden to fast on Shabbos, etc. Even fasting for one hour with the intention of a fast is forbidden. Maharil, Hilchos Taanis. Rashi in Eruvin 41 writes the same. Therefore, some communities act improperly when they fast until midday on Shabbos because of decrees. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1. Even a fast from meat and wine is forbidden, although there is no obligation to eat them. Beis Menuchah, paragraph 2.
