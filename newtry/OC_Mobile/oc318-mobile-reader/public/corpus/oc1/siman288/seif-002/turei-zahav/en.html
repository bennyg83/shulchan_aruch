And likewise one who has pleasure, etc., as stated in the Aggadah that they found the disciples of R' Akiva weeping on Shabbat and he said 'I have pleasure.' And it appears this means from his great cleaving to Heaven his eyes pour tears, as we find with R' Akiva in Zohar Chadash when saying Shir HaShirim he wept greatly because he knew where the matters reach; and likewise among those who pray with kavanah.
