(ב) Permitted, etc. — one should not fast, meaning if Reuven saw a bad dream about Shimon. That is, on weekdays Shimon may fast as if he dreamed for himself. Nevertheless on Shabbat he should not fast. All the more Reuven who saw a dream — even though Magen Avraham wrote above siman 220 in the name of Shulchan Aruch that it is fitting Reuven should fast; but on Shabbat he should not fast:
