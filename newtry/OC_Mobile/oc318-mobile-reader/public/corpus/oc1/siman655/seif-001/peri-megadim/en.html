Even. See the Taz. See Beis Yosef and siman 324:8: for another Jew it is permitted, and for the one for whom it was brought, moving is at least permitted, while benefit is prohibited. Mitzvos were not given for benefit. We do not say that he performs a mitzvah that comes through a transgression. Even if it was brought from outside twelve mil, which some say is from the Torah, it is permitted here. But within twelve mil, one may say that it is permitted to tell a non-Jew ab initio to bring it, for a double rabbinic prohibition is permitted in the place of a mitzvah. See siman 566 in Magen Avraham, letter 24, and siman 515, letter 27; examine there well. It is prohibited to move it outside four amos in a city not enclosed by a wall; see there. See what I will write, with Hashem's help, in letter 2 about this.
