To a non-Jew. The agreement of the Acharonim is that even on the second Yom Tov, which is rabbinic, it is permitted to tell a non-Jew to bring a lulav and esrog from outside the techum. So is the custom in this country, to send after an esrog on Yom Tov. Nevertheless, on Shabbos one should not send for the sake of the Yom Tov after it; see Taz and Magen Avraham. The Rashba wrote in siman 296 that an aravah detached on the second Yom Tov is prohibited to move, even if he has no other with which to fulfill the obligation, for it is muktzeh. The Magen Avraham wrote: it appears to me that even on the first Yom Tov it is prohibited to move; but if it was detached on the first Yom Tov, it is permitted on the second according to everyone; see there. Maharshdam, Orach Chayim, siman 22, disagrees with the Rashba and permits it; see there. Maharchav in the book Etz HaChayim rejected his words and wrote to prohibit, like the Rashba.
