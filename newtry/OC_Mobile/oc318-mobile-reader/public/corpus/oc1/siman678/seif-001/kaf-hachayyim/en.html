1) [Seif 1] One who cannot afford to purchase a Chanukah light and a Shabbos light, etc. If he has one light for his home, that suffices, and he should use the remainder for a Chanukah light. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1. Although it is good and proper to have 2 lights for Shabbos, as stated above in siman 263, nevertheless, in pressing circumstances it suffices to have one light on the table at which he eats, and the others should be used for the Chanukah lights, so that he will be among those who beautify the mitzvah. Mishna Berurah, letter 1. See our words there, letter 27.
