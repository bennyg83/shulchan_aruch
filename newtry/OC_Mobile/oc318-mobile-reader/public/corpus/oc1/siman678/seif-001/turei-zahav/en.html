(א) From the peace of his home. Do not sit in the dark and regret:

He should acquire a Hanukkah light. Ran and Beit Yosef wrote: although Kiddush is d'oraisa and Hanukkah light is d'rabbanan, since one can sanctify on bread with Hanukkah oil it is preferable — end; per this if he has no bread, bread is preferable since he must sanctify on it d'oraisa. Rambam wrote here Hanukkah light and Kiddush of the day are both d'rabbanan and Hanukkah light precedes Kiddush of the day because of publicizing the miracle — if so even without bread Hanukkah light is preferable. However it appears for bread all agree bread comes first: what is written siman 271 that Kiddush precedes meal needs — that means other meal needs such as dishes; they called it in Gemara Berachot chapter 10 "honor of the day." But bread — lechem mishneh — precedes wine of Kiddush, for lechem mishneh is d'oraisa for all as R' Abba said in chapter 27 "lechem mishneh"; three meals too are d'oraisa and we require bread as siman 274 — all the more so it precedes Hanukkah light which is only d'rabbanan.
