(ד) And he cuts, etc. Magen Avraham, Rashal, Mateh Moshe, and Shibolei HaLeket practiced cutting for their wives; and in the Prisha he wrote that specifically in the morning they practiced thus, for the honor of the day takes precedence over the honor of the night; nevertheless, one who wishes to practice as is our custom to cut on one — it is fine, as implied from Rav Hai Gaon that Beit Yosef brought, and Mateh Moshe.
