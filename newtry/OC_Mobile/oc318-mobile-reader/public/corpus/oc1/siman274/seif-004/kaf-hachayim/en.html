(ד) There — whole loaves. And bread from which a bit was burned until it is unfit for eating — if it is called whole, see in our words above in siman 167, letter 8; and so too bread from which the measure of challah was removed — if it is called whole, see in our words above in siman 168, letter 10, that there is a dispute in this, and it appears that if he has no other loaf it is more correct to leave it for lechem mishna than to bless on one loaf. And if a person has two half-loaves and no whole loaf, he joins them with a stick that does not protrude, or with anything so it will not be visible — then its law is like a whole loaf, as written above in siman 168, seif 2, and in our words there at length.
