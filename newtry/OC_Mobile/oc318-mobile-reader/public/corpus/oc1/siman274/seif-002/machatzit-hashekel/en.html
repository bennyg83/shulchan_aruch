(ג) The Rosh wrote, etc. — why did Abbaye say when we were at Mar's house and heard from him Kiddush, Rabbah said to him: taste a bit of my fruit lest when I go to the inn the lamp be uprooted (it will be extinguished and they will not eat without a lamp and will not make Kiddush) — and in the Kiddush here you did not fulfill, for there is no Kiddush except in a place of a meal; therefore taste a bit and leave with the Kiddush here, for then it was Kiddush in a place of a meal. And so wrote the Rosh on why they did not eat, etc. And see siman 291 where Rama similarly wrote that if he did not eat at night he completes by day. And so too siman 271, seif 6, etc. — the Rif wrote: if he finished his meal and sanctified on it the day, he blesses Birkat Hamazon and afterward he cuts. The Rosh challenged: why bless Birkat Hamazon — for in any case after cutting he must eat again so there will be Kiddush in a place of a meal; if so, what do I gain by blessing Birkat Hamazon — he should cut from the bread, sanctify, and finish his meal; and it is strained that the Rif's view is that a meal eaten on a weekday satisfies Kiddush in place of a meal, see there. The Ran wrote: in truth this is the Rif's view, that a meal eaten on a weekday counts in place of the meal required for Kiddush, see there; nevertheless it is difficult, for at night he must complete three meals — and it is certainly not reasonable that the meal he ate on a weekday counts for the three Shabbat meals — it is called satisfying that he sanctified in a place of a meal, for the requirement of Kiddush in a place of a meal is from Scripture: and you shall call the Shabbat a delight; and they expounded: in a place of delight (meaning a meal) there shall be calling (meaning Kiddush). And delight alone was not written in Scripture regarding Shabbat but delight in general — therefore one may say that even a weekday meal counts for Kiddush in a place of meal, namely in place of delight there shall be calling; but it is not reasonable at all that a weekday meal should combine to complete the three Shabbat meals; therefore it must be that the Friday night meal he completes by day.

And even though presumably they are not equal, for on Friday evening if he did not eat at night he must complete by day, and the Motza'ei Shabbat meal — if he did not make it on Motza'ei Shabbat he need not make it up — this only means that in any case one is obligated to eat three meals on Shabbat, as derived in Shabbat 118 from what is written in the portion of the man three times today regarding Shabbat meals; nevertheless in the verse the time of the three meals is not clear, and it is possible to eat all three by day, for R' Chidka, who holds four meals are required, presumably holds these three are by day, besides the Friday night meal, and three times today means by day; and even per the Rabbis who hold three times today includes the Friday night meal — meaning that the Friday night meal and two by day suffice; nevertheless if he did not eat at night it is fine to complete by day.
