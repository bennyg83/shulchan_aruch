(א) Two loaves — a remembrance of the manna, as written: gather a double portion of bread; and also on Yom Tov one must cut on two loaves. Women are also obligated in lechem mishna, for they too were in the manna miracle. If one fulfills lechem mishna with pat haba bekisnin, see above in siman 168, seif 7, there in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halacha.
