Magen Avraham, se'if katan 3. 'It appears to me that one must also ask, Have you prepared the eruv, etc.' It appears that the Shulchan Aruch did not have the reading 'Have you prepared the eruv?' Alternatively, it may indeed have that reading, but the Shulchan Aruch refers to an eruv techumin. The Magen Avraham writes that one must also ask about an eruv chatzeros, as the Tur writes. Although an eruv chatzeros may be prepared during bein hashmashos, as stated in the following siman, nevertheless l'chatchila one should warn them, etc. See the Taz in the following siman, se'if katan 1.
