Magen Avraham s.k. 3 — and it appears one must also say eruvoteichem, etc. It seems in Shulchan Aruch we do not read eruvoteichem, or perhaps we read it correctly but it refers to eruvei techumin in Shulchan Aruch; Magen Avraham wrote one must say eruvei chatzerot as Tur wrote. Even though Aruch HaShulchan permits in HaShulchan Aruch as in siman 528, nevertheless l'chatchila one should warn them; see Taz siman 528 s.k. 1.
