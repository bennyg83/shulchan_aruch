(א) In Shulchan Aruch: it is a mitzvah to wash, etc.; and Mishna Berurah wrote in the name of the Ari z"l that his custom was to wash first his face, then his hands, then his feet in the stated order — not like those who wash hands and then the face on Erev Shabbat:

(ב) The nails — Ba'er Heitev; and in Mishna Berurah he wrote that although all the Acharonim wrote Maggid Meisharim's words not to cut the nails of hands and feet on one day, one need not be concerned, for in Seder HaKavanot of Maharash Vitale who copied from Taharat Yad HaKodesh of his father Maharachav z"l he wrote that our Rabbi the Ari z"l on the sixth day would cut the nails of his hands and feet, etc. — see there; and regarding cutting nails on Erev Shabbat see below siman 532, explained there that in responsa Nishmat Shabbat it is permitted for one accustomed on Erev Shabbat; and see Netivot Shabbat who concludes there with the combination of another mitzvah drawing a mitzvah, that there not be a chatzitzah for netilat yadayim, as Shelah wrote to warn on this, as he wrote that it is permitted even without change on Erev Shabbat in Shulchan Aruch:
