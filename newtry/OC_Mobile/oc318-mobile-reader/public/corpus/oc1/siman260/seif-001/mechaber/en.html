The laws of ushering in Shabbos. It contains two se'ifim.
It is a mitzvah to wash: {Rama: his whole body; and if that is impossible, he should wash (Tur)} his face, his hands, and his feet in warm water on Erev Shabbos. And it is a mitzvah to shampoo the head and to trim the nails on Erev Shabbos. {Rama: And if the hair of his head has grown long, it is a mitzvah to cut it (Or Zarua, chapter "Eilu Megalchin"). And when one trims his nails, he should not trim them in order; he should begin on the left hand with the fourth finger [next to the pinky], and on the right hand with the index finger. And a mnemonic for this is: on the left, [the order] daled-beis-hei-gimel-alef, and on the right, beis-daled-alef-gimel-hei (Avudraham, in Sefer HaMussar).}
