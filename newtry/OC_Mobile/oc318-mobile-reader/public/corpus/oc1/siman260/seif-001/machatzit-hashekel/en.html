(ב) And chapter 4 of Ta'anit is chapter 4, daf 26b: we learned — a Shabbat on which the 9th of Av falls within it, they are forbidden to cut hair and launder; on Thursday they are permitted because of kavod Shabbat. Rashi explained: if the 9th of Av falls on Erev Shabbat they are permitted to launder on Thursday (and this is when they sanctified by sight of the moon, and as Rosh wrote — for us the 9th of Av does not fall on Erev Shabbat) — end of his words; and from this there is no proof to reject Bach's and Rashal's words forbidding on Thursday of Shabbat, for that is not the case — on a Shabbat on which the 9th of Av falls one cannot wait until Friday, for the 9th of Av falls on Friday; therefore it is permitted on Thursday; but in general one could say it is preferable to do on Erev Shabbat. But his proof from Rashi there daf 26 — who concluded as follows: and when the 9th of Av falls on Wednesday he did not need to teach that they are permitted, as the Gemara says "it is unnecessary" — only before it, but after it is permitted — end of his words; and he wanted to establish his own words that the mishnah is when the 9th of Av falls on Erev Shabbat, and it speaks specifically when they sanctified by sight of the moon — especially it should be established when the 9th of Av falls on Wednesday, and on this he explained that this is obvious and the mishnah did not need to teach it; and from this there is properly proof for Magen Avraham that in general it is permitted on Thursday — for otherwise what is Rashi's difficulty, lest it speak when the 9th of Av falls on Wednesday — for then how was it permitted to cut hair on Thursday, for one could wait until Friday; granted if it speaks when the 9th of Av falls on Friday, for then one cannot cut hair on Friday which is the 9th of Av, they had to permit on Thursday; or that even in general it is permitted on Thursday — and also from Rambam there is proof, for Rambam did not copy that on Thursday they are permitted, and Lechem Mishneh explained his reason: he follows his view in explaining the mishnah that he wrote: before it they did not permit even for kavod Shabbat, but the mishnah speaks when the 9th of Av falls on Wednesday, and it follows Rashbag who holds that even after it is forbidden — therefore it was not permitted on Thursday, only for kavod Shabbat; and since we do not rule like Rashbag, therefore he omitted that on Thursday they are permitted — for according to us you do not find the case on Thursday except when the 9th of Av falls on Friday; when it falls on Wednesday it is obvious according to us that we do not rule like Rashbag, as Rashi wrote — and in truth when the 9th of Av falls on Friday it is forbidden on Thursday, since it is before Erev Shabbat; behold according to Rambam the mishnah speaks when the 9th of Av falls on Wednesday, for he can cut hair and launder on Friday, and nevertheless the Sages permitted on Thursday as Rashi wrote regarding the 15th — since most of the world cuts hair on Thursday, this proves not like Bach in the name of Rashal; but in my humble opinion there is no proof from chapter 4, neither according to Rashi's view nor according to Rambam's view — for one could say that which teaches "and on Thursday they are permitted" refers only to laundering and not to haircutting;
