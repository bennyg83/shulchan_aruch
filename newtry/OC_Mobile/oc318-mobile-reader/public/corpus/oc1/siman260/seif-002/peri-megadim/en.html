See the Prisha and Eliyah Rabbah, siman 5. The mnemonic for the order is: on the left hand, kmitzah; on the right hand, the finger order is bet, dalet, alef, gimel, hei, not in sequence. The word tirutz means an orderly arrangement and resolution. There is also an allusion in the right-hand order, bedagah, meaning 'with worry.' The nails symbolize excesses. The more a person pursues excesses in this world, the more he neglects Torah, and consequently loses in the World to Come. It is also impossible that he will not have some of his reward deducted in the World to Come, which is certainly a great worry. This follows the verse, 'Length of days is in her right hand; in her left are wealth and honor.' This world is called the left, as in the verse, 'My hand founded the earth, and My right hand spread out the heavens.' If one pursues excesses, he will also have worry in this world, though not as much as in the World to Come.
