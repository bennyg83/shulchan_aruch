In a gentle manner. A person may not impose excessive fear within his household, for because of fear they may desecrate Shabbos, feed him forbidden food, and come to commit many transgressions.
Light the lamps. The same applies that he should warn them to stop performing melacha. See siman 263. It appears to me that he should also ask, 'Have you prepared the eruv chatzeros?' as the Tur writes. Although it is permitted to prepare an eruv during bein hashmashos, that is only because the Sages permitted a shevus for the sake of a mitzvah. L'chatchila, therefore, he should warn them so that they do not come to rely on this.
