Shevus Yaakov, volume 2, siman 25, however, disputes this ruling of Magen Avraham and holds that he does not put on tefillin on the first day. On the second day as well, he should not put them on until after sunrise, as during the rest of the year. See there. See Yoreh Deah, siman 388, where Shach writes that some halachic authorities permit putting them on during the second day before sunrise, as on other days. Accordingly, it appears that one may permit it here. Regarding whether he may be counted in a minyan before the deceased is buried, Shevus Yaakov there writes that he may not be included. See there.
