(46) And Shemini Atzeres is seven: it is considered a festival in its own right and takes seven days for itself. Although Shemini Atzeres itself has no compensatory days for its offerings, unlike Shavuos in seif 13, nevertheless since all the festivals are compared to one another by the verse, 'These are the appointed times of Hashem,' they all have one law.
(47) And the second day of Shemini Atzeres, etc.: this means that even outside Eretz Yisrael, where two days are observed, the second day is counted regarding the thirty days like ordinary weekdays, so that afterward he needs to add only eight more days.
