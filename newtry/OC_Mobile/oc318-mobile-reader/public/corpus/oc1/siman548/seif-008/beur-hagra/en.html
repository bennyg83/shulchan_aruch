Se'if 8: And he need not, etc. That is to say, even according to the first view in se'if 10, that washing is forbidden until night: here it is permitted, as is written there, 'all agree that when the third falls,' etc., until evening; and as Rashi explained there. And from this we learn that here according to Abba Shaul it is permitted by day; and what is written 'close to evening' is according to the first view there; but according to the latter view there, one must say that here it is permitted even in the morning, and as Tosafos wrote there s.v. 'that it is forbidden'; and see what is written there.
And it goes without saying, etc. For in this even the Sages agree, as is written there.
But during the festival, etc. There, folio 17b.
And if it falls, etc. There.
