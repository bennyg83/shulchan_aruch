And it is permitted to wash close to evening - According to the view of Rama, who was stringent specifically close to evening, it is not only regarding washing; for the same applies to haircutting and laundering, as is found in Yoreh De'ah, siman 399, se'if 3 in the gloss; see there. And know that what Rama wrote, 'close to evening,' is according to the first view brought in se'if 10, who hold there [it is forbidden] until night: here it is permitted close to evening; whereas according to those who permit there, here it is permitted even before midday; so wrote the Gra, and it is obvious.
And it goes without saying if it falls, etc. - He used the wording 'and it goes without saying' because when eight days were completed for him before the festival, it is certainly obvious that the festival cancels the decree of thirty and it is permitted to cut hair on that very day; and [regarding] this case that his eighth day falls on Shabbos, so that he cannot cut hair and is forced to do this on Friday, it is considered as if today is the eighth day; so it is implied in the Gemara (Magen Avraham).
That it is permitted to cut hair on Friday - And likewise to launder and to wash; and it appears that in this he need not wait until after Minchah; and the case above is different, for there it must be recognizable that it is for the honor of the festival, which is not so here.
