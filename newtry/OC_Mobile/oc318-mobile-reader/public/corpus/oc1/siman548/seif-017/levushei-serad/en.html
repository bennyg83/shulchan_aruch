Taz, seif katan 2. And because of mourning it is forbidden. The Ramban holds that even what is permitted because of the festival is forbidden because of mourning, for otherwise the festival would not count toward the thirty days. But the Rosh holds that whatever has no prohibition because of the festival has no prohibition because of mourning, and even so it counts toward the thirty days. See the Taz in Yoreh Deah at length.
