This siman, etc. See Taz in Yoreh De'ah 399, [s.k.] 7: the reason that the second Yom Tov, which is rabbinic, counts, even though we do not mourn on it at all; and likewise according to the first view there, if he died and was buried on day one, nevertheless the second Yom Tov counts; the same applies for us, that it counts; and see Shach there, letter 8. And what the Rav wrote here [that] Rosh Hashanah [as] a second Yom Tov [counts], see Yoreh De'ah: I do not know [of support]; there is no hint of this there, only that if he died and was buried on the second Yom Tov, mourning is not observed on it; and one may force an explanation with difficulty. And see Magen Avraham here, [s.k.] 2, who is lenient on Rosh Hashanah as well even if he was buried on day one, that the second day of Rosh Hashanah counts toward the seven; and God willing, there it will be clarified further.
