And he counts thirty. And Shemini Atzeres counts in place of the seven and he completes the thirty, as written in se'if 16 (Mish'as Binyamin, siman 71; and Maharshal and Derishah; and so appears the view of the Shach in Yoreh De'ah). And the Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote that it counts only as one day; and so ruled the Bach in the name of Tosafos and the Mordechai; and so ruled the Taz; and so it appears to me, for it is similar to one who erred and did not observe mourning before the festival, for whom Shemini Atzeres is not counted as seven; and the Taz also rejected the proofs of Maharshal; see there.
