It does not take effect. See Baer Heitev. See there in Responsa Nachalas Shiva in the name of the Gaon Rabbi Shmuel Shapira, who wrote that even though the room is the place where the person died, nevertheless one should not light there, lest through this he come to eulogize. Rather, one should light in the synagogue or in other rooms of the house where people do not eat. This is brought in Eliyah Rabbah, siman 133; see there.
