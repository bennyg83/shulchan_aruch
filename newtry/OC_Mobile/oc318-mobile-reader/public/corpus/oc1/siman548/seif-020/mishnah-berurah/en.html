(53) That the festival does not count, etc.: nevertheless, if a woman after childbirth had a relative die for whom she is obligated to mourn, and the same applies to another sick person, and they cannot mourn because of weakness, since they knew of it but were prevented by compulsion from practicing mourning, if the festival occurred during the seven days, the decree of the seven days is cancelled for them, and the same applies regarding the thirty days. All the remaining details relevant to this matter are explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 399; see there.
