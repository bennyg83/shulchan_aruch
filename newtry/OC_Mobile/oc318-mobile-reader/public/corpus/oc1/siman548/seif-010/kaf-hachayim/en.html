(10) [Seif 3] One who buries his dead on the second day of Yom Tov, etc. — specifically the seven metei mitzvah explicitly in the Torah for whom a kohen becomes impure; but those they added, as stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 374 seif 4 — their mourning is only d'rabbanan and mourning does not apply to them on any Yom Tov, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 399 seif 13, see there; and see below section 13.
