Se'if 10: And if it falls, etc. There, folio 19b: all agree; and Bahag wrote that it is not specifically the third, for the same applies to other days except the seventh; and the reason it mentioned the third is because according to the first Tanna, before the third the festival does not cancel, as is written there; and see Tosafos there and the Rosh.
It is permitted, etc., but, etc. As Rashi explained there, s.v. 'that it is forbidden,' etc.; and Tosafos there asked what differs from the seventh day, where we say a portion of the day is like the whole, and [thus] it is permitted regarding the decree of thirty by day, as we say he may shave on the eve of the festival; and the Raavad answered that washing is different, for it is possible at night; and that is what is written: it is permitted to launder, but not, etc.
And some permit. According to the interpretation that it is not specifically until evening, but until after the prayer, etc.
And for us, etc. As above: for this [reason] it did not mention day one or two; and likewise for this [reason] it mentioned only washing.
