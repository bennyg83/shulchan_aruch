She'eirei Knesset HaGedolah wrote on what the Tur wrote, 'however the Semak wrote,' etc. — this is his language; and likewise he wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 375 — it implies this is his view; and one may wonder how he ruled without comment contrary to the Rosh who forbids, and likewise ruled in Shulchan Aruch; and one may wonder more about Beit Yosef who did not protest at all — up to here his language; and one may wonder at his wonder, for in Yoreh De'ah siman 375 the Tur rules like the Rosh; also I overlooked Beit Chadash siman 399 who wrote the Tur rules like the Rosh and did not write 'however the Semak wrote,' etc., but practiced leniently like him so as not to change from their custom; and with this I am satisfied with Bedek HaBayis's difficulty on Beit Yosef regarding the Tur — examine well, for I abbreviated since for practical law we follow Shulchan Aruch.
