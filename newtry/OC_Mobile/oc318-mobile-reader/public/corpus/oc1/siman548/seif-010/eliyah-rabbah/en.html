Even if it occurred, etc. If they rebuked him even on the thirtieth day, it is permitted. See siman 531. Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah wrote regarding the Tur's phrase, 'however, the Semak wrote,' that since the Tur wrote similarly in Yoreh Deah, siman 390, it implies that this is his view. He wondered how the Tur could write simply against the view of the Rosh, who forbids, and against the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch, and he wondered even more at the Beit Yosef for not objecting. But his wonder is itself surprising, for in Yoreh Deah, siman 390, the Tur rules simply like the Rosh. He also overlooked the Bach in siman 399, who wrote that the Tur rules like the Rosh, and cited the Semak only because people practiced leniently like him and one should not change their custom. This also resolves for me the Bedek HaBayis's question on the Beit Yosef regarding the Tur. I have been brief, since in practice we follow the Shulchan Aruch.
