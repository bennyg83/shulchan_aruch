10) [Se'if 3] One who buries his deceased relative on the second day of Yom Tov, etc. This applies specifically to the seven relatives explicitly stated in the Torah, for whom a kohen may become impure. Mourning for the additional relatives listed in Yoreh Deah, siman 374, se'if 4, is only rabbinic, and mourning is not observed for them on any Yom Tov, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 399, se'if 13.
