4) There. Mourning does not take effect upon him, etc. The festival does not cancel lighting a lamp in honor of the deceased. Even when the person died during the festival, the lamp is lit. Nevertheless, in the room where everyone eats, even if the person died there, the lamp should not be lit on Yom Tov, lest this lead to a eulogy. It should be lit in the synagogue or in a room of the house where people do not eat. Responsa Nechalas Shivah, siman 73; Eliyah Rabbah in siman 133, at the end of subsection 7; Shaarei Teshuvah, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 3. When it is not lit in the synagogue or in the place where people eat on Yom Tov, it should be lit by a non-Jew. Mishbetzos Zahav, at the end of siman 132.
