(15) Mourning has not taken effect upon him: he should not change his clothing. Although another mourner changes on the festival, nevertheless one who has not yet begun mourning should not do so on the festival. He also does not change his place, since he did not change it before Yom Tov. Regarding tefillin on Chol HaMoed on the first day after a relative died, see above in siman 38, Mishnah Berurah seif katan 16, and see Shaarei Teshuvah in this siman.
(16) Private matters: namely washing in hot water, marital relations, and Torah study. He practices mourning in all these and they are forbidden. Some permit Torah study. Nevertheless, regarding receiving an aliyah to the Torah on the festival to complete the number of those called, it appears that initially this is not proper.
