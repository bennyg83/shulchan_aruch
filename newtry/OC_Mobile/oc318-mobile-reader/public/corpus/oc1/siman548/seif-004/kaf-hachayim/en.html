Mourning did not apply to him, etc. — but regarding lighting the candle of honor for the deceased, the festival does not cancel; and even when one died on the festival they light the candle. However, in a room where everyone eats there — even if the deceased died there — one should not light on Yom Tov lest through this he come to eulogy; and one should light it in the synagogue or in one of the rooms of the house where they do not eat. Responsum Noda B'Yehuda siman 73. Avnei Nezer in siman 533 end section 7 — Sha'arei Teshuvah section 1, Mishna Berurah section 3; and when he does not light it in the synagogue or in a place where they eat there on Yom Tov, one should light it through a non-Jew. Maharsham siman 532; and see above siman 514 section 79 and below end section 32.
