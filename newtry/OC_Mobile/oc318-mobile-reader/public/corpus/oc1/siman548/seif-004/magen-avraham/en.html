And he should not change his clothing; even though another mourner changes [clothing] on the festival, nevertheless this one, who has not yet begun mourning, should not conduct himself so on the festival (Maharam Lublin, siman 107). And it appears to me that it is forbidden to review the [weekly] portion, for one is not obligated to review the portion of the festivals, as written at the end of siman 285; see Yoreh De'ah, siman 400, and the Taz there; and see what I wrote in siman 554, se'if 4. And it appears to me that he puts on tefillin on the first day, for the Rosh wrote in chapter 'Eilu Megalchin' that the reason a mourner is forbidden in tefillin is because it is a bitter day for him and 'a mourner sullies his glory in dust,' and this does not apply on Chol HaMoed, for mourning is not observed on it.
