Se'if 18: He heard constrained tidings, etc. For he need not observe the laws of mourning except as a mere token, and he observes [them] at night; and see the Rosh, siman 76, in the name of the Ramban; and he inferred there from what is written, 'from this [we learn that] a mourner is forbidden in wearing sandals,' that is to say that he is forbidden only in wearing sandals; see there.
