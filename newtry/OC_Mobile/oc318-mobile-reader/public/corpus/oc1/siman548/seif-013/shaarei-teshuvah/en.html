Prohibition. See Baer Heitev. See below at the end of siman 606 and in the Magen Avraham there. See Responsa Teshuas Chen, siman 31, who questioned what the Shach wrote in Yoreh Deah, siman 381, that immersing one's whole body in cold water involves no prohibition within thirty days. Although this seems somewhat implied by the plain language of the Rama, it can be rejected. Nevertheless, the practice is to be lenient with immersion in cold water, in accordance with the Shach.
