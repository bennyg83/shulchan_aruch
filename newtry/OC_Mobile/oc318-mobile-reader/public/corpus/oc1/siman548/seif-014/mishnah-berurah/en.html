(44) That he shaves on Erev Yom Kippur: for it is the seventh day and counts both ways, as above in seif 8. Certainly washing and laundering are permitted. See below, siman 606, at the end of the Magen Avraham, where he inclines regarding washing to allow it an hour or two before nightfall, but not earlier. In our case, where the seven days have been completed, one may possibly be lenient even earlier.
