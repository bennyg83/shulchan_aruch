And it becomes the sixth and seventh day for mourning. In Yoreh De'ah, siman 402, se'if 6, I wrote that if he died on Sunday and his seventh day falls on Shabbos, one should not observe the prohibition of private matters except in the morning at sunrise; and therefore it is permitted at Minchah to call him to the Torah, not like my father-in-law, of blessed memory, in this. I further wrote there that if the seventh day is on Shabbos, some householders practice sitting on the ground at night for one hour, and it is a complete error, unless he heard distant tidings on Shabbos, for then he observes mourning for one hour at night; and not like my father-in-law, of blessed memory, in this.
