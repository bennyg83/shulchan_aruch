(52) And the next day he tears: this is not precise, and means Motzaei Shabbos. If he heard a recent report on Shabbos that is Erev Yom Tov, Shabbos counts for him as one day and Yom Tov cancels the decree of the seven days, as stated by the later authorities. The Peri Megadim wrote that regarding Kaddish, one may say that the festival does not cancel it, and he has the law of seven and thirty days even if he practiced mourning for one hour before the festival.
