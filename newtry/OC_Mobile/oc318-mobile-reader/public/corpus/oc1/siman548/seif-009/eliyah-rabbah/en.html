[9] To wash and cut hair, etc. — so it is in all books of Shulchan Aruch; and what he wrote 'one need not wait until night' must be said to refer to bathing, which is permitted at night; and in this manner Beit Yosef explained the Tur's words at the beginning of daf 76 as mentioned above, and not like R' Moshe Rivkash who deletes the words 'and cut hair'; and what he wrote that it is not in Mordechai and Hagahot Ashiri — this is no difficulty, for Rama follows his reasoning in Yoreh De'ah siman 559 seif 3 that one may not cut hair close to evening; and further it appears to him they learned bathing from haircutting as Darkei Moshe wrote. Further R' Moshe wrote: what is written 'one need not wait,' etc. — meaning he may wash in cold water — up to here; and if his intent is that Rama does not allow on erev Yom Tov except in cold water, he overlooked Darkei Moshe's words in Yoreh De'ah who wrote, in the name of Terumat HaDeshen siman 287 that he allows even in hot water — we practice thus; and if his intent is on Yom Tov itself, for Rama wrote in siman 511 that we practice to forbid hot bathing — it is difficult for him what is said 'one need not wait,' for it is forbidden; therefore he explained: he may wash at night in cold water; and so Magen Avraham implies — and this is astonishing, for also even without erev Yom Tov it is permitted in cold water on the seventh day of mourning even by day, as Shach wrote siman 381; and in truth it is no difficulty from siman 511, for in such a case there it is permitted even in hot water; one may also say Rama informs us: since there is no prohibition of hot bathing on Yom Tov only out of stringency, etc. — see there at length.
