Seven. Specifically when he died before the festival, we count Shemini Atzeres as seven; but if he died on the festival or on Chol HaMoed, we count Shemini Atzeres only as one day, as the later authorities [hold], and not like Maharshal and Mish'as Binyamin. And see s.k. 2; and see Maharit Tzahalon, siman 78; and in Sefer Bnei Chayya, Yoreh De'ah, siman 399; and in the Responsa Nachalat Shivah, siman 18.
