I did not understand his intent in this, for the Rama there deals with weekdays but on Shabbat and Yom Tov onen status does not apply — so why should Torah study be forbidden? In my humble opinion it seems permitted on Shabbat and Yom Tov to study Torah; and you may know from what they said in Moed Katan daf 23b: the difference between them is marital relations — he did not say Torah study; if so, by inference all agree Torah study is permitted; and perhaps Maharam of Lublin spoke only on the night of the second day of Yom Tov.
