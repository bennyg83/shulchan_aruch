This siman is explained in our words in Yoreh Deah siman 399; here we will only copy a few new laws written there at length and here briefly.
(1) He counts the seven from Yom Tov, etc. This implies even if he was buried on the first day by non-Jews, unlike what Maharshal wrote, distinguishing that specifically if he was buried on the second day he counts from the second day; but on Rosh Hashanah, we say that specifically if he was buried on the second day he counts the seven from the second day, but if he was buried on the first day he does not count even from the second day. See what I wrote on this in Yoreh Deah, seif 13.
