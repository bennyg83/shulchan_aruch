And the days - See Taz, see Yoreh De'ah 399, letter 2, who expanded. And regarding wearing a shirt laundered in water alone on the festival, where the custom is one hour before the festival from the law of thirty: one may say that since the custom is to forbid all thirty [days of] laundering, one may say that one who deals with this, if it is public, is [still] permitted to wear [a garment] laundered in water alone; and also [one] laundered with natron requires further study; see siman 389, [s.k.] 11, that we do not have pressing [as formerly], and see siman 551, se'if 3. However, in water alone, even if there is no other [garment] to wear, one may say it is permitted in such a case. Afterwards I saw that this is a mistake, for it is clarified in se'if 10 that in such a case it is permitted to launder and he wears it at night; and see Shach, Yoreh De'ah 389, letters 2 and 3. However this [point] remains a doubt regarding our pressing, as I wrote nearby. And in Magen Avraham [s.k.] 12, God willing, it will be clarified further that nevertheless what the Rav wrote, and in Yoreh De'ah 389 where they practice laundering [prohibition] for all thirty, one may say the same applies to natron and boris, etc.
