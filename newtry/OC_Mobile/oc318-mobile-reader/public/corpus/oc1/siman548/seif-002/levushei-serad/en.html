Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. As written in seif 16. This means that just as in seif 16, where he was buried before the festival, Shemini Atzeres is counted as seven days regarding the decree of thirty days, so too when he was buried on the festival: even though the mourning begins after the festival, nevertheless Shemini Atzeres is counted as seven days regarding the thirty days. This also appears to be the view of the Shach in Yoreh Deah, siman 399, seif katan 6. But the Hagahos Maimoniyos distinguishes, holding that Shemini Atzeres is not counted as seven days unless he was buried before the festival, and so ruled the Taz in Yoreh Deah there, seif katan 4, and in this siman, seif katan 4.
