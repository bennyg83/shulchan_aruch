(6) From the last second day of Yom Tov: excluding the first second day of Yom Tov, which is no worse than Chol HaMoed and does not count in the number.
(7) Although he does not practice, etc.: this seif speaks of one who died during the festival, for if he died on the last day, the view of the Mechaber is explained below, that he practices mourning on it.
(8) It counts for him in the number: the same applies to the two days of Rosh Hashanah, whether he was buried on the first day by non-Jews or on the second day. Even though he does not practice mourning on them according to all views, nevertheless the second day counts for him in the number.
(9) See Yoreh Deah, etc.: this is a textual error, for this matter is not mentioned there.
