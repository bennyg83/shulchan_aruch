From the last second Yom Tov. Even of Rosh Hashanah (Tur, siman 399; Mish'as Binyamin; Bach; and Shach). And the Taz wrote specifically when he was buried on the second day of Rosh Hashanah it counts for him, but if he was buried on the first day the second day does not count for him; and it appears to me to be lenient; and see Mabit, part 3, siman 89.
