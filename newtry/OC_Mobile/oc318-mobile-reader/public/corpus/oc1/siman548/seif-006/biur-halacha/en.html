Within his house — see what Chacham Tzvi wrote; behold even though in Or Zarua it appears his practical conclusion is like his grandfather Maharash as we will quote below, Chacham Tzvi ruled like Ateret Zekenim because that colleague came later and mentioned only his view as we wrote in Mishna Berurah in his name; nevertheless what Chacham Tzvi was more lenient than him is not clear at all; I will quote Chacham Tzvi's words: a partner of a mourner who died on chol hamoed — permitted to deal in the shop immediately after Yom Tov [Ateret Zekenim siman 548] (what Ateret Zekenim concluded "like other mourning" means like there on the seventh day permitted to open his shop — so here if he died on the first day of chol hamoed permitted immediately after Yom Tov that is the seventh day) — it appears for us who practice prohibition only three days, likewise if he died on last Yom Tov — permitted — end quote; in truth not comparable, for there the mourner sat at least three days in mourning whereas here he has not sat at all; our leniency suffices if he died on chol hamoed on the first day per Ateret Zekenim, for at least now it is the seventh day from death — not to grasp two leniencies, three days and no mourning conduct at all [especially Pri Megadim doubts Ateret Zekenim too and tends to be stringent like his grandfather; if we are lenient like Ateret Zekenim, no more]. I quote Or Zarua: it appears to me a partner of a mourner who died on chol hamoed first day — permitted to deal in the shop immediately after Yom Tov, since we are lenient in other mourning after three days; but I heard in name of my grandfather the Gaon he ruled to forbid like other mourning — end quote; behold even Ateret Zekenim's view is only three days for a partner generally, as Maharshal; nevertheless in our case he was stringent specifically if died on chol hamoed first day so opening the shop is at least on the seventh day from death, as above; therefore the reason as we wrote above is clear in my humble opinion. Know that regarding close report it is simple: although seven days from death already passed, commerce is forbidden all mourning like other mourners, Yoreh De'ah 402 — close report is like burial day; so Pri Megadim end of siman:
