(In Shulchan Aruch seif 8) and all the more so if his eighth day falls on erev Shabbat, etc. — and see in responsum Beit Chadash HaChadashot siman 12 that it is permitted to return immediately to his place at the entry of this Shabbat; and likewise permitted to wear Shabbat garments; nevertheless forbidden to enter the wedding hall on this Shabbat, see there; and likewise the view of Beit Hillel in Yoreh De'ah siman 399. But Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 90 responded to Beit Chadash and his view is to forbid returning to his place and wearing Shabbat garments, see there; and he wrote there is no proof from the cancellation of his thirty on Shabbat that is permitted on erev Shabbat — there the Rama is lenient that one may say the 29th day is like a weekday, etc., as appears from Darkei Moshe siman 10, see there; and nevertheless it appears since Beit Chadash permits this, which is only the custom of instructors to be lenient, it is not an error in ruling, as Shevut Yaakov wrote he has what to rely on from what Darkei Moshe wrote — there is no proof from that; for perhaps even though it is impossible on Shabbat, therefore he needed to combine the matter that one may say regarding bathing that is forbidden by law — one might think to forbid, for the 29th day is like the view that, etc.; but changing place and wearing Shabbat garments, which are only custom, are permitted; and perhaps if Shevut Yaakov saw Beit Chadash's words he would retract his view and rule halakha like Beit Hillel. In any case, regarding wearing Shabbat garments it seems to be stringent if they are new and their pressing is still very evident — see Yoreh De'ah siman 389 seif 5 and below in Magen Avraham siman 551 s.k. 30; and if he has no others, perhaps there is room to be lenient even in this.
