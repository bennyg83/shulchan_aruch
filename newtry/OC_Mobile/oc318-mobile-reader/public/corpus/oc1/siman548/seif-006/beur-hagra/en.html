Se'if 6: In their homes. Rabbeinu Yeruham; and as is written there, 'if his work was in the hands of others,' etc.; but there [it is] specifically regarding contracted work that he took on before his mourning, and not like Tosafos there, folio 19b, s.v. 'and his work,' etc.
The count of days. There, folio 20a: the last three days, the many do not [work for him], etc.
