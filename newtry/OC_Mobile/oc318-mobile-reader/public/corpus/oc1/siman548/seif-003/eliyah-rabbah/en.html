Private matters. These are marital relations, washing, and Torah study, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 400. This requires study, since according to some poskim cited in the Tur there, learning is considered public conduct. Although we do not rule that way, nevertheless on the festival perhaps one may rely on the poskim who hold that even private mourning practices do not apply on the festival. The Magen Avraham wrote that it is forbidden to review the parsha, since there is no obligation to review the festival readings, as is explained at the end of siman 285. This implies that he holds simply that Torah study is forbidden, and it requires study. See the following seif katan.
