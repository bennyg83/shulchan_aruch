Second. See Baer Heitev. See the new responsa of the Bach, siman 9, who wrote at length to uphold this view, that the second day of Rosh Hashanah counts for him. Likewise, the second day of Yom Tov in the diaspora that falls on Shabbos counts, even though even in Eretz Yisrael he could not have practiced mourning that day. Nevertheless it counts for him, and he completes six days after it. See Yoreh Deah, siman 399, in the name of the Bach, and in the Baer Heitev there, who wrote that the same is stated in Ginas Veradim and in Responsa Toras Chaim.
