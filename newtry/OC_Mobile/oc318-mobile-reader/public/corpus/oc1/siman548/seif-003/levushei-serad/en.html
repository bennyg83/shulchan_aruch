There the Shulchan Aruch writes only that the festival does not cook, meaning cancel, the mourning, and that his law is like one who buried his dead during the festival. This is true according to all views, even for one who buried his dead during the festival, since Shemini Atzeres does not cancel the mourning. But whether Shemini Atzeres is counted as seven days regarding the thirty days is not clarified. It seems that according to the Mishnah Berurah and Maharshal, there too it is counted as seven days, since he wrote that its law is like one who buried his dead during the festival. This requires study.
