And there is no need to console him, etc. Similarly, one does not console the mourner on the Erev Shabbos after the festival, before Barchu as is customary, if seven days have already passed since the death, as I found in the name of Tosafot Yom Tov. The same applies if the last day of Yom Tov is on Erev Shabbos, even if seven days have not passed since the death, as stated by the Taz at the beginning of siman 526. As to what he wrote there, that the learned men of the holy community of Frankfurt ruled improperly in requiring the mourners to wrap their heads and change their place, in my humble opinion one can reconcile it. It speaks of the wrapping practiced throughout the thirty days, as the Shach writes in siman 400. The same applies to changing place: even though it came after Barchu, since it is practiced throughout the thirty days it is not considered a public matter. Although I wrote at the beginning of this siman that one who has not begun during the weekday should not change on the festival, nevertheless regarding Shabbos he holds that one should change.
