(7) [Seif 2] He counts the seven from the last second day of Yom Tov, etc. — and likewise on the second day of Yom Tov of Rosh Hashana; for example, if his deceased died on the first day of Yom Tov of Rosh Hashana, he counts the seven from the second day of Yom Tov, which is also from their words — Beit Chadash in Yoreh De'ah siman 399, Shach there s.k. 8. However, Taz there s.k. 7 distinguishes: specifically when buried on the second day of Rosh Hashana it counts for him, but buried on the first day the second day does not count for him, see there. And likewise in this siman s.k. 1; and likewise Mabit part 2 siman 89. Perach Mateh Aharon part 1 siman 98. However, Magen Avraham s.k. 2 wrote one should be lenient like Beit Chadash and his colleagues, see there. And likewise Avnei Nezer section 2; and likewise in Toras Chesed siman 4 and in Ginzei Yosef Chayei Adam general rule 5 siman 7 they agreed to the halakha like Beit Chadash, see there; and likewise agreed Beit Din siman 313. Sha'arei Teshuvah section 3, Yeshuos Yaakov section 3, Mateh Yehuda in his book Shevet Yehuda on Yoreh De'ah siman 399 wrote and so they practice. And likewise testified Maharam of Amstelveen that so was done in practice in the rabbinical court of the rabbi Perach Magen Avraham. And Beit Din in the book Torach Chaim part 3 in the book Olelos HaKerem siman 11. Section 11 in Hagahot Beit Yosef on Rosh Hashana section 1, Nahar Shalom section 2, Chochmas Adam general rule 169 section 2, Mishna Berurah section 8.
