Thirty. And likewise a groom who married on the eve of the festival and during the festival a death occurred to him: even though an ordinary groom to whom mourning occurred during the seven days of feasting does not count toward the thirty except after the days of feasting, nevertheless for this groom the seven days of feasting count for him toward the thirty (Mish'as Binyamin). But the Taz ruled that here too the thirty are not [counted] from that reckoning; and see Yoreh De'ah, siman 399.
