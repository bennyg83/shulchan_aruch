And festival days count for him toward the thirty — in Yoreh De'ah siman 399 Shulchan Aruch wrote: behold the law of thirty — namely laundering and haircutting — applies on the festival, and not only from festival law alone is it forbidden, but also from mourning law, for from festival law one may wear laundered new white garments, etc., and from mourning law it is forbidden in all of them; and this is Ramban's view. But Tur wrote, in the name of Rosh that even in mourning law it is permitted on the festival — what is permitted on the festival; and I wrote in Yoreh De'ah there one should rule like Ramban and not like Mishna Berurah who ruled like Rosh.
