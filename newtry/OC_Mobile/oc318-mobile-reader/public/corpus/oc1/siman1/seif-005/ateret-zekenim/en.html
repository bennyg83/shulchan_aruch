It is good to recite the order of the offerings specifically during the day. Only the mishnah beginning Eizehu Mekoman may be recited before daylight. One should recite the Ten Commandments aloud in order to arouse concentration. The Ten Commandments should be placed near Baruch She'amar because there are ten occurrences of “baruch” from Baruch She'amar through “Blessed are You, Hashem, merciful Father,” corresponding to the Ten Commandments. One should recite the version of “I am Hashem” in parashat Yitro in the morning, and the version in parashat Vaetchanan in the evening after the bedtime Keriat Shema. Rabbi Yitzchak Luria.
