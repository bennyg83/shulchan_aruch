"Specifically." — Taz: See Berachos 12a and Rashi — the sectarians' complaint so they would not say to amei ha'aretz, etc., "because of the sectarians we are not responsible" — see there. Yet afterward Rashi [explains] Sadducees, idolaters — for if Sadducees and Baytosim believe in the whole Written Torah, then surely we are not accountable to idolaters. See the Darishah in Maharshal's name, who ruled not to recite it in public before Baruch She'amar — Rashi explained they wanted to establish Krias Shema with its blessings — see there. We rule like Shulchan Aruch; yet individuals say it quietly in synagogue, not aloud, and it is proper to say it before Baruch She'amar, as Maharshal wrote, for forty-seven words [begin] Baruch She'amar and its opening seals forty-seven [letters of "Anochi"], expounded from the first utterance "Anochi" — see there.
