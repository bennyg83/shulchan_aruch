(13) Parshat Ha'Akida - before the Parshat of the sacrifices. and can recite the Akida parashat and Haman parashat even on Shabbat. And the saying is not enough, but let him observe what he says and know the wonders of God. And also what they said in the Gemara: "Anyone who says glory to David 3 times every day, is guaranteed to be a son of the next world", also in this way. And there is a reason for saying all this, because Parshat Akida, to remember the right of the ancestors every day, and also to subdue his desire, as Yitzchak gave his soul. And the chapter on Haman, so that he believes that all his food comes from nothing under private supervision, and as it is written: The one who prefers and the one who diminishes is not the one who lacks", instructing that the abundance of intercession is of no benefit to a nation. And with it in Yerushalmi blessings: whoever recites the Parashat Haman, he is guaranteed that his sustenance will not be diminished. And the Ten Commandments, so that he remembers every day the standing of Mount Sinai and his faith in God is strengthened. And the Parshat of the Sacrifices, Damrinen in the offerings: "This is the law of sin", everyone who engages in the law of sin is as if the sacrifice is a sin, etc.
(Yid) And Shalom - and the same is true of Parshat Toda, a model is at all Shalom. And after he ascends and makes peace and gives thanks, he will say Parashat Neskim, because there is no sacrifice without Neskim. But you have sinned and are guilty, no one should agree.

(16) In the crowd - against the unbelievers, who will say: There is no Torah but this one. And especially in our time. And that's why they don't write it as well on the special counter for the public. Some have written that it is not forbidden to say it in public, but to state it between the blessings like a Shema reading; But first Baruch said, it is allowed even in public. Vanan Nectinen forbids gathering altogether. And in the singular, precisely not within the prayer; But to determine within the blessings, even for an individual is prohibited [OT and ARAH].
