5 It is good, etc. As Rashi wrote in Yoma 28b in the name of the Jerusalem Talmud: in order to mention the merit of the patriarchs, they mention Chevron; and likewise before prayer. And in Bereishis Rabbah, parashah 56, Rabbi Yitzchak says: He said before Him, “Master of the universe,” etc.; “May it be Your will before You,” etc.

And the passage of the manna. In the last chapter of Yoma: they asked Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai, etc.; and in Sotah 48b, as it was taught: Rabbi Eliezer the Great says, etc.; and in Mechilta 34b: when Yirmiyahu said to Israel, “Why are you not engaging in Torah?” they said to him, “From what will we be sustained?” He brought out for them a flask of manna and said to them, “O generation, see the word of Hashem.” Your ancestors, who engaged in Torah, see from what they were sustained; so too, if you engage in Torah, the Holy One, blessed be He, will sustain you from this.

And the Ten Commandments. As stated in the first chapter of Berachos 12a: they sought to establish them in the recitation of Shema, but refrained only because of the claims of the heretics. And this is what is stated in the gloss: “specifically,” etc.

And the passage of the burnt-offering. For they are the general principles of all the offerings.
