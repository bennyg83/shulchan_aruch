(17) By day — for sacrifices are offered only by day. But Parashas HaKiyor and Parashas Terumas HaDeshen may be said before daytime. If he lacks time, he may also say the parshiyos of the sacrifices at night. If standing is required, see siman 48 in Mishna Berurah note 1. The Shelah wrote that on Shabbos and Yom Tov one does not say "Yehi ratzon," for a voluntary offering is not brought then; but there is no loss in saying the parshiyos, like reading Torah. Nevertheless, if he is a Torah scholar, it is better that he engage in the parashah of the day. An avel should not say the parshiyos of the sacrifices (because he is forbidden in Talmud Torah).
