By day. For the offerings are brought only by day. One should say the passage of the offerings while standing, because we hold that sitting disqualifies one for Temple service (Olat Tamid; Yad Aharon). Likewise, Mizmor LeTodah should be said standing (Yad Aharon). But the passages of the laver and the removal of the ashes may be said before day, as stated in Yoma 20.
