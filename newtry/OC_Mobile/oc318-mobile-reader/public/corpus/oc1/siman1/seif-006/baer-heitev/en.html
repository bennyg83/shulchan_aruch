By day. For offerings are brought only by day. One should say the passage of the offerings standing, for we hold that sitting disqualifies one for Temple service (Olas Tamid; Yad Aharon). Likewise, Mizmor LeTodah should be said standing (Yad Aharon). But the passages of the laver and the removal of the ashes may be said before day, as stated in Yoma 20.
