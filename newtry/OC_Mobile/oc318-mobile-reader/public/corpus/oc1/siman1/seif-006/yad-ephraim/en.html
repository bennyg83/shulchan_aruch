(Magen Avraham, section 7) Bachye wrote… Bachye did not write thus about the parashah of the Binding [of Isaac], only about what Chazal said: “Anyone who occupies himself with the parashah of the olah…” And the matter is that he means in explaining the parashah — to what subject it alludes. For through this the eyes of his intellect will be opened and he will understand wonders from the Torah of sacrifices; and from this he will strive more in Torah and mitzvos, and his sins will be forgiven him as if he offered a sacrifice. And one should not say that the intention is that he should review and recite the wording of the parashah, etc. And similarly they said: “Anyone who says Tehillah leDavid…” — the intention is that through contemplating what the verses teach and what they allude to, then he will recognize and discern wonders of profound wisdom. And his heart will be strengthened in faith in Heaven and in His service. And through this he will inherit life in the World to Come — so ends Bachye.
