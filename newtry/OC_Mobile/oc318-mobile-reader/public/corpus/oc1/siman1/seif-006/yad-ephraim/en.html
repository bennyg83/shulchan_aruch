In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 7: 'Rabbeinu Bachya writes,' etc. Rabbeinu Bachya did not write this concerning the passage of the Akedah, but concerning the Sages' statement, 'Anyone who engages in the passage of the burnt offering, etc.' He means that the person should intend to understand what the passage signifies. Through this, the eyes of his intellect will be opened and he will understand wonders from the Torah of the offerings. He will consequently exert himself more in Torah and mitzvos, and his sins will be forgiven as though he had brought an offering. It cannot mean merely that he should utter and repeat the wording of the passage. The Sages likewise said, 'Anyone who recites Tehillah LeDavid, etc.' Their intent is that by contemplating what the verses teach and signify, he will recognize and discern the wonders of the One perfect in knowledge. His heart will be strengthened in faith in Hashem and His service, and through this he will inherit life in the World to Come. End of Rabbeinu Bachya's words. It therefore appears that the citation mark should be placed upon the words 'and the passage of the burnt offering.'
