“Offering incense.” For after the tamid they would offer the ketores (Beis Yosef, Orach Chayim). Hagahos Maimoniyos writes to say ketores before tamid, because the Mishnah (Yoma ch. 3) says the morning ketores was done between [sprinkling] the blood and the limbs. Seder Hayom, however, writes to say tamid before ketores, for the main atonement is through blood; so too Rabbeinu Yerocham ch. 4 of Berachos regarding Minchah—that the essence is opposite the blood—hence from the start of the tamid slaughter period (six and a half hours into the day), while Shacharis is from alos hashachar; for Minchah all agree tamid precedes ketores as in Yoma. A printed gloss (see there) that says to recite Minchah before ketores is not precise, for Abaye arranged the pyre, etc.—see there.
