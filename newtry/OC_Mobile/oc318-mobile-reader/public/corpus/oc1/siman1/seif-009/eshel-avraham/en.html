In Eliyah Rabbah he cites this baraisa: R' Eliezer the Great says, Whoever engages in Perek Shirah every day, etc. He wrote in the name of Maharam of Trani that the Ari z"l composed Perek Shirah through ruach hakodesh. He was precise in the wording "whoever engages" and did not say "whoever recites," for engaging means knowing the matter of the things and their intent for benefit — to receive moral instruction from these songs — see there.
