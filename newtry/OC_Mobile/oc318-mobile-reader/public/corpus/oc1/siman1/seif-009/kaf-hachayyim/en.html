Beshelah and P. Mount Sinai, etc. Even the lower man is not dressed in it. Later I saw in S. Sh. S. H. 3 in Drosh A. Mishnoi the prayers O. 8. He wrote that it is necessary to get up at night Shk at midnight, a thin night like today, will be illuminated and there is no fear of the outsiders, and so on in his book Shash Chad, called Petah Einim ba darush tikkun chatzot. And MMM also on Shabbat nights and Yot, he will try to get up at midnight so that he does not cancel his habit and also on behalf of what we wrote in C. Ralakh O. 3 Yaush.
