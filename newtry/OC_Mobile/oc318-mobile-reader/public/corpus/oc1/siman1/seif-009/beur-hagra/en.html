Seif 9, there are those, etc. As stated in chapter 1 of Tamid: until they hear, etc.; he took, etc.; and in chapter 2 of Yoma. And this, etc.

And afterward the passage of the altar, etc. For the incense was offered between the blood and the limbs, as stated in chapter 3 of Yoma, in the Mishnah and the Gemara there: Abaye would arrange, etc.; and in Tamid. And all atonement is through the blood, as stated in several places.

And he says the passage of the altar. Because in it it says, “and he shall burn,” etc.

And the passage of the ingredients, etc. And its preparation. And he should say, “And you shall make.”
