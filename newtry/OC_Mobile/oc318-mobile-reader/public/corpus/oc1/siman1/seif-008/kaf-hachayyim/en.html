8) Even one who did not awaken precisely at midnight but only an hour or two later should rise at that time. He should not say that since he failed to awaken precisely at midnight he might as well remain asleep. The earlier he rises from sleep, the better, for the aforementioned reason: so that the external forces do not attach themselves to him and he does not taste the taste of death for an extended time. If he cannot do even this, he should at least endeavor to rise before dawn so that he can recite Tikkun Chatzot and join night and day through Torah study, as explained below.
