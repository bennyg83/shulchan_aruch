Seif 8, he should say with the offerings, etc. In Vayikra Rabbah, chapter 2: And regarding the ram it says, “northward before Heaven.” They said: When Avraham our father bound Yitzchak his son, the Holy One, blessed be He, prepared two lambs, one for the morning and one for the evening. Why so much? Because when Israel would offer the daily offering upon the altar and read this verse, “northward before Heaven,” the Holy One, blessed be He, would remember the binding of Yitzchak. I call heaven and earth as witnesses over Me: whether a non-Jew or Israelite, whether a man or woman, whether a male slave or maidservant, when they read this verse, “northward before Heaven,” the Holy One, blessed be He, remembers the binding of Yitzchak.
