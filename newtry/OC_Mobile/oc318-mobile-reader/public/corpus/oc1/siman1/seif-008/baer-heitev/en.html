“And he slaughtered it”—see Beis Yaakov §108: Taz writes one who says “May our lips substitute for bulls” before Parashas HaTamid and Eizehu Makoman errs—for Chatas is mentioned there and perhaps he is not liable, like bringing non-sacred meat in the courtyard; rather say “May our lips substitute for bulls” in place of the Tamid offering—see there.
