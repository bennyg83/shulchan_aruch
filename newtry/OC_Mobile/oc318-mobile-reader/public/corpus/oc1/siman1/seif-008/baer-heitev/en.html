"And he shall slaughter it." See Beit Yaakov, siman 108. Taz wrote: one who says, "May the offering of our lips be accepted," before the passage of the tamid and Eizehu Mekoman errs, for chatat is also mentioned there and perhaps he is not liable, and it would be like bringing non-sacred animals into the Temple courtyard. Rather he should say, "May the offering of our lips be accepted in place of the tamid offering," see there.
