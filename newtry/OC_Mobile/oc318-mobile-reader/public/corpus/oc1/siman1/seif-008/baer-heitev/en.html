Taz writes that saying 'We will render the bulls of our lips' before the Tamid passage and Eizehu Mekoman is erroneous, because the chatas is included and perhaps the speaker is not liable to one, making the statement resemble bringing chullin in the Temple courtyard. Rather, he should say: 'We will render the bulls of our lips in place of the Tamid offering.'
