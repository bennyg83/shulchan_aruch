Taz, seif katan 2) Rather, it is an independent matter of alacrity. For “he should strengthen himself like a lion” means that immediately upon awakening from his sleep he should rise:
And he should be the one who awakens the dawn means that he should accustom himself to light foods and the like so that he does not sleep much and will awaken before dawn, because strengthening oneself does not apply during sleep. If so, it should have said “and he should be” rather than “that he should be.”
