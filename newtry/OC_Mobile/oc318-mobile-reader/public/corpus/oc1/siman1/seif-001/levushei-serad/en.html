Taz subsection 2: Rather, it is alacrity in its own right — "he should strengthen himself like a lion" means that immediately upon awakening from sleep he should rise. "And he himself should awaken the dawn" — the intent is that he accustom himself to light foods and the like so he does not sleep much and stirs before dawn, for strengthening does not apply in sleep; therefore it should have said "and he should," not "and he himself should."
