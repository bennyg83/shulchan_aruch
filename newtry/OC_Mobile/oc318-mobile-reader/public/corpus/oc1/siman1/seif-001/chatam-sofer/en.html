(Magen Avraham subparagraph 4.) Our Gemara explains “even in summer” as equal hours; for prayer we reckon seasonal hours. See Responsa Chatam Sofer O.C. §199 regarding unspecified “hours” in the Talmud where poskim in many places rely [on fixed hours], and he elaborates on seasonal versus equal hours and that sometimes “an hour” means only a short interval.
