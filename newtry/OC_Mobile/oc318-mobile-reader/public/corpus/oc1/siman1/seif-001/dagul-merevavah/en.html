So it is explicit in tractate Kerisos 22b: Rava said, and from the words of both of them we learn that a definite asham does not require prior awareness; and the words of the Rambam, chapter 9 of Shegagos, laws of Yoreh De'ah, are astonishing; and what the Kesef Mishneh wrote — that it is a dispute between R' Tarfon and R' Akiva. He followed the words of Rashi, and this is against the words of Rava cited above; and see Tosafos in tractate Shabbos 72b s.v. man damar; and Magen Avraham comes to resolve the words of the Tur — and there is no difficulty on the Tur from the words of the Rambam, unlike what Acharonim wrote that the words of the Rambam were hidden from Magen Avraham.
