The words of Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilchos Shegagos, halachah 10, are puzzling. What Kesef Mishneh wrote, that this is a dispute between Rabbi Tarfon and Rabbi Akiva, follows Rashi's explanation, but is contrary to Rava's statement cited above. See Tosafos in Shabbos 72b, s.v. Man De'amar. Magen Avraham came to reconcile the words of the Tur, and there is no difficulty against the Tur from the words of Rambam, unlike Eliyah Rabbah, who wrote that Magen Avraham had overlooked Rambam's words.
