Let him … Yerushalmi Berachos: David weak sleeping yet “Awake my glory … awake harp”—“I awaken dawn”—I rouse dawn, dawn didn’t rouse me—inclination mocked—therefore strengthen … see Taz.

Nevertheless … Berachos 8 auspicious times; Avos morning sleep removes person; Avos Rabbi Natan ch.21 morning sleep—don’t intend sleep past Kerias Shema … Rashi Avos.
