Seif 1, “He shall strengthen himself,” etc.; “for the service,” etc. As it is stated in chapter 5 of Avos: “Be bold,” etc., and “strong as a lion to do,” etc.

Like a lion to rise. Midrash Rabbah, parashas Balak: “Behold, a people rises like a lion cub,” etc.; they rise from their sleep, stand like lions, seize the recitation of Shema, crown the Holy One, blessed be He, and their deeds are like lions, etc.

That he should be, etc. Jerusalem Talmud, first chapter of Berachos: in any case, dawn never came and found David sleeping. This is as David says, “Awake, my glory; awake, harp and lyre; I will awaken the dawn.” “Let my glory awaken before the glory of my Master; my glory is considered nothing before the honor of my Creator.” “I will awaken the dawn” means: I would awaken the dawn; the dawn would not awaken me. And his evil inclination would prosecute against him. This is why it says, “He shall strengthen himself,” etc.; see Turei Zahav.

And in any case, etc. As stated in the first chapter of Berachos, page 8: “When is a time of favor,” etc. And likewise in Avos: “morning sleep,” etc., “removes a person from the world.” And in Avos DeRabbi Nasan, chapter 21: What is “morning sleep”? It teaches that a person should not intend to sleep until the time for the recitation of Shema has passed, etc. And Rashi explains likewise there in Avos.

“I have set,” etc. This is a great principle in the Torah, as stated in Berachos 63: Bar Kappara expounded, etc.

And in the virtue of the righteous, who, etc. As it is written: “Noah was a righteous man, perfect; Noah walked with God”; and similarly regarding Chanoch; and similarly regarding the patriarchs: “before whom my fathers walked” (Bereishis 48:15). This is the entire virtue of the righteous.

For there is no, etc. He will attain it immediately, as stated in Berachos 28b: “May it be His will that the fear of Heaven be upon you,” etc.; “if only you would know,” etc.

And not his speech. In Berachos 61a: A person’s words before the Holy One, blessed be He, should always be few, etc.

All the more so when he places. As stated in chapter 2 of Avos: “Reflect upon three things,” etc., “and you will not come,” etc.; and in chapter 2 of Chagigah 16a: Rabbi Yochanan says, “This refers to one who transgresses,” etc.; and in Kiddushin 31a, see Rashi there, s.v. “and not,” etc. This is what it means by saying that He stands over him; and see Bava Kama 79b.

And immediately, etc. And his shame, etc., as stated in chapter 2 of Nedarim 20a: It was taught in a baraisa, “so that His fear shall be,” etc., “that you not sin”; others say, etc.

Always. As above: “I have set,” etc.

And he should not be ashamed. As it is stated: “Be bold as a leopard”; see Tur. And it says this with regard to the service of Heaven, may He be blessed. But otherwise they said: “The brazen-faced go to Gehinnom”; and in chapter 2 of Chagigah: “These are the brazen-faced of the generation”; and in tractate Kallah: “a brazen-faced person,” etc.; and in other places.

Also, etc. Sukkah 49b: “He has told you, man,” etc.; and are these matters not an a fortiori inference, etc.? And he means to say: even though he should not be ashamed, nevertheless whatever he does privately is preferable.

And when lying down. This means to say that in this too he should act with modesty, as stated in Shabbos 118b: “In all my days they never saw,” etc.; and at the beginning of chapter 5 of Yoma.

And immediately, when the measure, etc., he should rise. As it is stated: “and light as an eagle to do the will,” etc.
