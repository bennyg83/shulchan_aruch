Seif 1. In the Shulchan Aruch: 'Also to walk modestly.' At first glance this means that he should act privately, thereby fulfilling the statement, 'and to walk modestly, etc.' See Sukkah 49b and Rashi there. In the Shlah's kuntres, however, it states, 'when he sits in his house and when he lies down, etc.' This suggests somewhat that he interprets it to mean that even while a person is privately at home, his conduct should be with Hashem his God, as stated in siman 2: even in the innermost rooms he should not say, 'Who sees me?' Yet the expression 'to walk modestly' does not imply this.
