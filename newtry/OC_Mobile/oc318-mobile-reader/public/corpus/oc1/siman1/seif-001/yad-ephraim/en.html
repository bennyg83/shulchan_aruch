Section 1 — In Shulchan Aruch, even walk modestly: it seems to mean he should act modestly to fulfill what they said, walk modestly, etc. See Sukkah 49b and Rashi there. But in Shelah, Kuntres Aseres Maamaros, it is found also when sitting at home and when lying down, etc. It seems somewhat that he explains it means that even when at home in private he should walk with Hashem his God, and as in siman 2, even though in inner rooms he should not say who sees me, etc. But what is written walk modestly does not imply thus.
