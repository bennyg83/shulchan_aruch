“When one finishes parashat olah…” — Novel remark: See Magen Avraham [sk 10] citing Mahari that asham nazir comes for atonement. This is astonishing—how was he unaware of the Gemara Shevuot 37a, which implies from nazir tamei exempt from lashes that the korban is written so as to enable nazirut taharah to take effect; Rashi explains “the korban is written—‘he shall consecrate to Hashem the days of his nazirut and bring a yearling lamb’”—there it challenges from asham; in any case the beraita there discusses asham, for it distinguishes nazir from shevuat hapikadon where one is liable an asham; yet it answers there it is not for atonement but so nazirut taharah may take effect, as Rashi says—it enables nazirut taharah to begin counting from now and ties it to the korban but it does not come for atonement—explicitly the opposite of his words; requires analysis—see there.
