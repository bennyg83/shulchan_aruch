Subsection 7 — Parashas HaAkedah. Bachye did not write thus on Parashas HaAkedah. In the Akedah the reason is to remember before Him the merit of the Akedah and the Patriarchs.

And also that he surrender his soul for the sanctity of His Name, may He be blessed, as Yitzchak did. In Parashas HaMan the reason is that through it he trust in God and believe all things come under His providence, may He be blessed. In the Ten Commandments the reason is to remember that honored, awesome standing so that fear of Him, may He be blessed, be upon his mouth. In Parashas HaKorbanos the reason is as God promised: when there is no Temple, our speech shall be accepted and we fulfill bulls with our lips. So the Sages said: whoever engages in Torah — it is as if he offered an olah. On this he wrote: saying the verses alone does not suffice; rather he should contemplate the reasons of the korbanos and their laws according to his grasp (and he must also repent from sin in his hand, for in a korban repentance and confession were required, lest it be the sacrifice of the wicked, an abomination). Likewise in reciting Tehillah leDavid the intent is that through recitation with heartfelt intent he recognize somewhat the Creator's kindnesses and somewhat His greatness; through this he will tremble before God and guard himself from sin; and if sin is in his hand he will distance it and return in complete repentance, and thereby merit the World to Come.
