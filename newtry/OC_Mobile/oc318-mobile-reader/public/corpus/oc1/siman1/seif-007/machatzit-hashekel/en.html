Se'if katan 7: 'The passage of the Akedah; Rabbeinu Bachya writes, etc.' Rabbeinu Bachya did not write this concerning the passage of the Akedah. The reason for reciting the Akedah is to invoke before Hashem the merit of the Akedah and of the Patriarchs.
And also so that he will surrender his life for the sanctification of His blessed Name, as Yitzchak did. The reason for reciting the passage of the manna is that through it he will trust Hashem and believe that everything comes through His providence. The reason for reciting the Ten Commandments is to remember that honored and awesome assembly, so that fear of Him will remain before him. The reason for reciting the passages of the offerings is that the Holy One, blessed is He, promised that when the Beit HaMikdash does not exist, our recitation will be accepted, and 'we will substitute the bulls with our lips.' Our Sages of blessed memory likewise said: anyone who engages in the Torah of a burnt offering is considered as though he brought a burnt offering. Regarding this Rabbeinu Bachya writes that merely reciting the verses is insufficient. One must contemplate the reasons for the offerings and their laws to the extent of his understanding. He must also repent of any transgression he possesses, because an actual offering likewise required repentance and confession; otherwise it would be 'the sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination.' Similarly, when reciting Tehillah LeDavid, the intent is that by reciting it with concentration he will recognize something of the Creator's kindness and greatness. Through this he will tremble at the word of Hashem and guard himself from sin. If he has a sin in his possession, he will distance it from himself and repent fully; through this he will merit the World to Come.
