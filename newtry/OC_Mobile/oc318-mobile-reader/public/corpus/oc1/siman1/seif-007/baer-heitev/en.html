Magen Avraham and Taz challenged him—see there. Magen Avraham concludes one should practice: after Parashas Asham say Yehi Ratzom “if I became liable for Asham …,” for Asham does not require foreknowledge at the outset, but after Parashas Chatas say nothing—see there. Eshel Tzedek, Eliyahu Zuta, Sefer Bnei Chiya, and Yad Aharon all agree with Maharshal: say “if I became liable for Chatas may this be considered as if I offered Chatas, and if not I am as one reading Torah”—valid—see Sefer Zevach HaShlamim siman 1. Responsa Emunas Shmuel §53 cites Shelah: on Shabbat and Yom Tov one does not say Yehi Ratzom because voluntary sacrifice does not apply—but the portions may be read as Torah without loss; nevertheless if he is a scholar better engage Parashas Yoma; Soles Belulah wrote one should not say other sacrifices even with Torah-reading intent—and Baer Hetev copied before me—see there; I do not know why.
