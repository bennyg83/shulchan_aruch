See Be'er Heitev subsection 13. In the responsum of Shevut Yaakov siman 2 there is a doubt whether one may say Parashas Chatas and Asham on the first day after Yom Kippur. According to what Beis Yosef wrote, one may also say Yehi ratzon after Parashas Asham, because they said of Baba ben Buta that he would volunteer an asham talui every day — see there. Is not the conclusion of the mishnah "except the first day after Yom Kippur"? They told him: Wait until you enter into doubt, etc. One may also doubt whether one should say the parshiyos alone — as one may not say Mizmor leTodah on the eve of Yom Kippur; but he leaned toward a reason for the custom: because we say one is liable for chatas and asham, and one who certainly transgressed them on Yom Kippur must bring afterward — but Yehi ratzon one should not say at all — see there.
