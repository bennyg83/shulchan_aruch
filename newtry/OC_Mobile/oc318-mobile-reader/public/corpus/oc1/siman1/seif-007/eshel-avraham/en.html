Responsa Shev Yaakov §2 doubts whether one says Parashas Chatas ve’Asham on the day after Yom Kippur; according to Beis Yosef one would also say Yehi Ratzon after Parashas Asham because they said of Bavli Beis Gimmel that he volunteered Asham Taluy daily—see there—but the end of the Mishnah is except the day after Yom Kippur—wait until doubt enters… One might also doubt that even the portions alone should not be said like one cannot say Mizmor LeSodah erev Yom Kippur; still one may justify the custom because those liable for definite Chatas ve’Asham who transgressed them—after Yom Kippur they must bring—but Yehi Ratzon one does not say at all—see there.
