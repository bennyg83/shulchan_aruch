Magen Avraham subsection 12 — not like the printed gloss. It is an error, and the source of these words is in that Rashi which he brought from Beis Yosef: after the tamid they would offer the ketores; on this he wrote "not like the printed gloss" that wrote Minchah before ketores — and that is the minchah belonging to the morning tamid — likewise in Tosafos Shabbos.
