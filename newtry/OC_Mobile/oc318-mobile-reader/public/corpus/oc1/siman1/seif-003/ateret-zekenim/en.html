One should sleep with Rachel's sorrow every night for the exile of the Shekinah which is in Rachel's hands and for the destruction of God, Kamesh Kumi Roni, on the night of the year of the Lord, and after that he will engage in Torah until the dawn pillar rises, and if he is unable to do tikkun and sleep, and after that he will rise before the dawn pillar (bread from heaven). Anyone who is among the first ten in the Bhakti is a great tikkun, and he who is first in God's presence is above all a tzaddik (This is fr. Truum) And it is necessary to accept for him the mitzvah of doing and loving your neighbor as you do, because this time his prayer is included in all of Israel and in particular the love of friends And he should include himself because he is one of the friends, and if there is a friend in trouble, he should pray for him or when he is sick and in sorrow, he should share in his sorrow.
