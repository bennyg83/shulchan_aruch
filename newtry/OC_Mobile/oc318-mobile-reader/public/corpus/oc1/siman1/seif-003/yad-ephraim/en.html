"Like" — as for the holiness of Israel. "Like" is a scribal error and should read "meaning." And what he wrote "to the holiness of Israel" means the attribute of Krias Shema, which is their holiness and His Presence among them. See Peri Megadim — and it is not clear. Nevertheless, even if we emend to "to holiness" and write in its place "to Knesses" — nevertheless the word "like" is a scribal error and should read "meaning," to Knesses Yisrael; but in my humble opinion the word kedushah also fits.
