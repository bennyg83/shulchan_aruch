There: 'Like the sanctity of Israel.' The word 'like' is a scribal error and should read 'that is to say, etc.' The phrase 'the sanctity of Israel' means the quality of Knesset Yisrael, which is their sanctity, with the Divine Presence dwelling among them. See Peri Megadim, whose explanation is not clear. In any event, even if we emend the word 'sanctity' and write 'Knesset' in its place, the word 'like' remains a scribal error; it must read, 'that is to say, Knesset Yisrael.' In my humble opinion, however, the word 'sanctity' also fits well.
