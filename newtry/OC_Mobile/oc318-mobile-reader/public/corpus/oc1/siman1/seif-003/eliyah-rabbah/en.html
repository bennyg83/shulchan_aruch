[C] “If he hides … immediately awe reaches him …” I explained at the start of chapter Seder Taanis: the elder says words of “conquest” before them—Rashi: subdues hearts to repentance; Tosafos cite Rav Hai: reveals secrets of their deeds like “hidden lambs of the Merciful”—astounding—is the elder a prophet? Rather I explain: he tells them the Holy One fills the earth with His glory and sees secret deeds—great rebuke that awe should seize him.
