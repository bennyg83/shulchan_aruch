[3] If he hides, etc. — awe will immediately reach him, etc. This I explained at the beginning of chapter Seder Taanis: the elder among them speaks before them words of rebuke — Rashi explained that he subdues their hearts to repentance; Tosafos explained in the name of Rabeinu Chananel that he reveals to them the secrets of their deeds like "with the lambs of the Merciful One." This is astonishing — is the elder a prophet? Rather it seems to me his explanation is that he tells them that the Holy One, blessed be He, fills the whole earth with His glory and sees the secrets of their deeds — and this is great rebuke that awe will reach him.
