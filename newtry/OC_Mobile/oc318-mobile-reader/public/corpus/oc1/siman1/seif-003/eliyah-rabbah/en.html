[3] If he hides, etc.; awe will immediately come upon him, etc. In this manner I explained the beginning of the chapter Seder Ta'anis: “The elder among them addresses them with words of admonition.” Rashi explains that he subdues their hearts toward repentance. Tosafos explain in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel that he reveals to them the hidden matters of their conduct, comparable to the expression, “the hidden matters of the Merciful One.” This is difficult, for is the elder a prophet? Rather, it appears to me that he tells them that the Holy One, blessed is He, fills the entire world with His glory and sees their hidden deeds. This is a powerful rebuke, through which awe will come upon a person.
