(There, s.k. 11.) And the Tur wrote that after asham one says Yehi Ratzon. Attention: See Niddah 70a — because he brings kodshim to a disqualified Temple — see Rashi; this applies only when he actually brought sacrifices and they became invalid. But for mere recitation, the Sages also agree one may make a conditional stipulation on asham — otherwise let it be a nedavah. By this the Tur's words are settled, and how many great ones were perplexed by this difficulty that asham does not come voluntarily. What appears from Tosafos Kiddushin 55a s.v. udilema — that one cannot make a stipulation — no challenge from this; what Tosafos wrote that nesachim of nedavah are for rich men is only that the baraisa challenges Shmuel who holds thus in Zevachim 91a; but halachah is not like Shmuel — nedavah nesachim are also for plates — so too Rambam Hilchos Maaseh HaKorbanos 14 — see there. But Rambam Hilchos Pesulei HaMukdashim 18 — accordingly it is puzzling why he wrote that finding a two-year-old male, there is no remedy lest it is asham and its owner not yet atoned — can he not bring conditionally as Tosafos challenged? — unresolved.
