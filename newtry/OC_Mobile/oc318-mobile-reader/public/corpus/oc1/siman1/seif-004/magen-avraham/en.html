“A little.” The Gemara says: whether one increases or decreases, provided his heart is directed toward Heaven—that is, even though the one who increases also had intent, still, if the one who decreased had no doubt that what he did was right, his offering is as desirable to the Holy One as the rich man’s. So too from the proof brought from the bird olah of the poor: it too is called a pleasing aroma, like the cattle olah brought by the wealthy.
