A little. The Gemara states: “Whether one does much or little, provided that he directs his heart toward Heaven.” This means that although the one who does much also directs his heart toward Heaven, if the one who does little lacked the ability to do more, he is as pleasing to Hashem as the one who does much. This is also evident from the proof concerning the poor person's bird offering, of which the verse likewise says, “a pleasing aroma,” just as it says regarding the animal offering brought by a wealthy person.
