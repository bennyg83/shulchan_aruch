4) There, in the gloss: 'And immediately when he awakens,' etc. This is not literal: he should wait briefly and not rise suddenly immediately after sleep. Magen Avraham, se'if katan 3; Eliyah Rabbah there. Seder HaYom writes that upon rising he should say: 'Modeh ani lefanecha, Melech chai v'kayam, shehechezarta bi nishmati b'chemlah, rabbah emunatecha.' He need not wash his hands for this, even if they are soiled, because he mentions neither God's Name nor a Divine title. Shalmei Tzibbur, page 17d; Chayei Adam, rule 1, end of ot 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 5; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 1, ot 2. Although Chida writes in Kesher Gudal, siman 1, ot 1, that some say Modeh Ani requires washing the hands, since most poskim write that it does not, that is how we rule.
