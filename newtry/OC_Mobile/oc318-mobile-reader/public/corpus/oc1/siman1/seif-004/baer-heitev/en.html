With intention. This means that one who has constraint and cannot prolong his prayers and abbreviates the supplications but says them with intention is considered before the Holy One, blessed be He, like one who has leisure and prolongs his supplications with intention. Mishna Berurah, and likewise Yad Aharon. And Olas Tamid, and in the responsum of Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 44. And see Tosafos Yom Tov on tractate Avos, chapter 2, mishnah 16. Sefer Mekor Chaim wrote that the same law applies to Torah study as well, and likewise in Solet Belulah.
