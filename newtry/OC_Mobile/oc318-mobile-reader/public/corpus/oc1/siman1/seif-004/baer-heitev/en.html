With concentration. One who is prevented by circumstances from reciting lengthy supplications and therefore says fewer supplications with concentration is regarded by God like one who has ample time and lengthens his supplications with concentration. Mishnah Berurah; Yad Aharon. See Taz; Responsa Shevus Yaakov II:44; Tosafos Yom Tov on Avos 2:16. Mekor Chaim writes the same regarding Torah study; Soles Belulah agrees.
