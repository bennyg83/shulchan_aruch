Seif 2, one who rises early to supplicate. For the primary time to supplicate is at night, as it is written: “Arise, cry out at night.”
According to the hours, etc. As it is written: “at the beginning of the watches”; and at the beginning of Berachos 3a: the night consists of three watches, and at each watch, etc.; and if you wish, say that all of them, etc. And this is what it says: “that prayer,” etc.
And at the end of the night. For the donkey brays and the dogs bark until midnight, and at the end of the night a woman converses with her husband. See Zohar, volume 1, page 242b: “Come and see: at the time when night enters,” etc.; and volume 2, 46a and 173b: “There are three watches in the night; corresponding to them are three camps that are divided, as has been stated,” etc.; and in other places.
Over the destruction, etc. For the Holy One, blessed be He, remembers it then.
