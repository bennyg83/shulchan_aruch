The dawn — see Baer Heitev and Birkei Yosef, who wrote that in our printed Zohar it is not found. But in the Zohar manuscript, page 10, it is found that he wrote, and for this he was liable for death on account of Heaven; and he wrote that one must wash his hands even if he does not wish to rise. If it happened that the water was far and he has no one to bring it to him, some conscientious people would walk a cubit at a time — see there. And it is not clear in my humble opinion; it is better that he go quickly so as not to delay the attribute of mercy that rests on his hands. And in responsa Shevut Yaakov vol. 3 siman 8 he wrote that they are not careful about walking four cubits without netilas yadayim; and one may say they relied on the Rashba's view in Berachos that the whole house is like four cubits of water — see there. And it is obvious one should not rely on this except in time of need. See Rabbi Akiva Eiger, section 4; and see below siman 4, what is written on this.
