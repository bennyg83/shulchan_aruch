Responsa Shav Yaakov, siman 1, concludes that for purposes of the night watches, the period from the emergence of the stars until dawn must be divided into three watches, as implied by the Talmud in Berachos, page 3; see there. This is contrary to the view of the Zohar. However, one who awakens to rise at midnight in accordance with the Zohar should calculate exactly twelve hours after solar noon, in accordance with the Zohar's view; that is, from the time when the sun stands at the middle of the sky. See there.
