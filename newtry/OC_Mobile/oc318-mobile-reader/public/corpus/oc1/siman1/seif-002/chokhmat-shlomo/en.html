(Section 7) When he finishes the parashah of the olah, etc. NB: See Magen Avraham [note 10], what he wrote in the name of the Gaon Mahari z"l that the nazir's asham comes for atonement. I wonder at him — how was it hidden from him the arranged Talmud in Shevuos [37a], which says there: From this we learn that a tamei nazir is exempt from lashes — behold, a korban is written regarding him there, that he brings a korban so that nezirus of taharah should take effect upon him; and Rashi explained "behold, a korban is written regarding him" — "and he shall be a nazir to Heaven the days of his nezirus, and he shall bring a lamb in its first year," etc. — behold, it challenges there from asham. And in any case, the baraisa there deals with asham, for it distinguishes there between nazir and shevuas hafkadan; and for an oath one is obligated asham, and nevertheless it answers there that it is not for atonement but only so that nezirus of taharah should take effect upon him, as Rashi wrote there, namely: so that nezirus of taharah should take effect upon him, that he begins counting from now, and Scripture ties it to the korban and it does not come for atonement — see there, explicitly the opposite of his words; it requires investigation; understand.
