(MA. SKH.) I was asked why he wrote the column. P.S. And the little one Shimon Hirsch, above, according to MS. Before this, after he comes up and makes peace and offers, he says P. We agree because there is no sacrifice without consent, but you have sinned and are guilty. There is no charge. It will be said later, P. Neschim, there is no sacrifice without Neschim.
