(Magen Avraham, s.k. 8.) I was asked why the Tur wrote [olah before chatas]. Attention: To me, the young Shimon Hirsch — it appears according to what was said before, that after olah, shelamim, and minchah one says parshas nesachim, for there is no sacrifice without nesachim; but chatas and asham do not require nesachim. If one says olah after chatas, he would then say parshas nesachim — then parshas nesachim would also refer to the chatas and asham he said earlier; so too Taz in s.k. 7 regarding "venishleimu parim" — see there. And if he does not say parshas nesachim afterward, [the rule] "there is no sacrifice without nesachim" [would be violated] — therefore one says olah before chatas.
