Magen Avraham subsection 2 — "because of," and "in any case he should not quarrel with them" (Beis Yosef). And this is his language: because the trait of brazenness is very repugnant and one should not use it at all, even in divine service, for he acquires in his soul a disposition to be brazen even outside his service of Hashem, may He be blessed — end of his words.
