Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2: 'Because; and in any event he should not quarrel with them.' Beit Yosef. These are his words: 'Because the trait of brazenness is exceedingly reprehensible, it is improper to use it at all, even in the service of Hashem, for he will acquire brazenness as a settled trait in his soul and will be brazen even outside the context of His blessed service.'
