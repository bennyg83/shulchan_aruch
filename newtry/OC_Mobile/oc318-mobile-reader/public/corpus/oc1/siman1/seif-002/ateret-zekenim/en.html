One who rises early to engage in Torah should dress himself properly to prepare to call upon his God (Zohar, parashas Noach; see Magen Avraham). And in Sefer Yesh Sachar it is good to rise standing to engage in Torah so that he does not come to sleep (Zohar, parashas Lech). And although one must rise with alacrity without laziness, as written in Sefer HaYirah and in Sefer HaMusar chapter 4, nevertheless he should pause a little and not stand up suddenly (Levush Chadash, chapter 1 of Berachos). And Seder HaYom writes: upon rising he should immediately say, "I give thanks before You, living and eternal King, that You have restored my soul to me with great compassion; great is Your faithfulness." Washing the hands is not required for this even if his hands are dirty, because he does not mention the Name or a substitute. And after ani he should say the seventy-two-letter Name from the acronym Ana b'koach gedulat, etc.
