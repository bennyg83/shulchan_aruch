One who rises early to study Torah should dress properly in preparation to meet his God. Zohar, parashat Noach. Sefer Yesh Sachar writes that it is good to stand while studying Torah so that one will not fall asleep. Zohar, parashat Lech Lecha. Although one must rise energetically, without laziness, as stated in Sefer HaYirah and chapter 4 of Sefer HaMusar, he should nevertheless wait briefly and not stand up suddenly. Lechem Chamudot, chapter 1 of Berachot. Seder HaYom writes that upon rising he should immediately say: “I thank You, living and enduring King, for mercifully restoring my soul within me; great is Your faithfulness.” He need not wash his hands before this, even if they are soiled, because the statement mentions neither a divine name nor an appellation. After washing his hands, he should recite the forty-two-letter name formed by the initial letters of “Ana Becho'ach Gedulat,” etc.
