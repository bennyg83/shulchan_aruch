[B] “Watchmen for morning” must precede daylight even in summer. If near sunrise say Kinah first then begin “watchmen for morning,” skipping Kinah in between—Rav Hai Gaon; see Responsa Be’er Eshek §124. Kenesses Gedolah siman 219: people who early attend synagogue but doze in prayer—even during Kerias Shema—those are “early risers” in the wrong sense. Properly: if they can intend in prayer they should rise at the watch; but if on Shabbat they come early only to sleep during prayer, better intend when rising in the morning—end quote. See below siman 151 seif 2—sleeping in synagogue forbidden even occasional sleep.
