[2] In the morning, etc. "Watchmen for the morning" must be before daylight even in summer. If he is close to sunrise he should say the kinos first, then begin "watchmen for the morning" and skip the kinos — so wrote Rosh, and see responsa Be'er Sheva siman 124. Knesset HaGedolah wrote siman 239: those people who rise early to synagogue every day yet doze at prayer time — my eyes have seen that even during Shema reading sleep seizes them; regarding these I apply "behold, early risers of the morning." Therefore the good and upright: if they can also concentrate in prayer, let them rise at the watch; but if on Shabbat they come to sleep at prayer time after rising early, it is better that they concentrate in prayer when they rise in the morning — so far. See below siman 151 seif 2 that sleeping in synagogue is forbidden even casual sleep.
