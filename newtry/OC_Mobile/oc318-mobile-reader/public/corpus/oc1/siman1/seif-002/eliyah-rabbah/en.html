[2] In the morning, etc. Shomrim LaBoker must be recited before daylight, even in the summer. If it is already close to sunrise, he should first recite the kinnot, then begin Shomrim LaBoker, and omit the kinnot that appear within it. So wrote Rabbeinu Chananel; see Responsa Be'er Eishek, siman 124. Knesses HaGedolah writes in siman 619: Regarding those people who rise early for the synagogue every day but doze during prayer, and my own eyes have seen sleep overtake them even during Krias Shema, of them I apply the verse, “Woe to those who rise early in the morning.” Therefore, the good and proper course is that, if they can also concentrate upon their prayers, they should rise during the pre-dawn watch. But if, by rising early on Shabbos, they will come to sleep during prayer, it is better that one concentrate upon his prayer than that he rise early in the morning. See below, siman 151, seif 2, that it is forbidden to sleep in a synagogue even temporarily.
