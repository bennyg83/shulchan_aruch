One who says "Modim Modim," etc. — I am in doubt: if he said only the words "Modim Modim" alone and said the rest as required by halacha, whether they silence him in this too; for perhaps the Gemara deals with one who doubled also the words that follow, but in this alone it does not appear as two authorities after he afterwards said "that You are our Heaven, Heaven." And from Berachos 33b, where it says regarding Shema that one who said "word word" and taught it — and presumably the same applies regarding Modim. It appears from the outset from the words "word word" that not Shema alone did he say, but he said in duplication also the extra words; nevertheless there is room to reject that the language was not precisely used, and you may know from that which is said there too regarding one who said "verse verse." And according to those who hold that in this they silence him — certainly even if he did not say only the verse Shema twice they silence him. And even if you say that regarding Shema we are lenient with the word Shema Shema alone, because through this it does not appear as two authorities — it is possible that the word Modim Modim is stricter, similar to what is said there on folio 14b, "every truth truth seizes this man," and it requires further study.
