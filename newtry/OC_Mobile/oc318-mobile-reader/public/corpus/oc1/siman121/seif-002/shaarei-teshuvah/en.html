And Shabbos Teshuvah wrote that in Jerusalem the Sephardim are accustomed to bless with Birkat Kohanim in a house of mourning — then even when there are no priests they say the text, and this is straightforward. And Rama on Yisrael wrote in the name of Moreinu HaRav Baal Chessed l'Avraham that in the blessing "grant peace," one who says "and may it be good in Your eyes," etc., errs — he should only say "and may it be good in Your eyes," etc.; and the full verse is "for it was good in the eyes of Heaven to bless Israel," etc.; and in Shabbos Teshuvah he brought Maharada that "and may it be good in Your eyes" means "and may it be good in Your eyes," etc. — see there.
