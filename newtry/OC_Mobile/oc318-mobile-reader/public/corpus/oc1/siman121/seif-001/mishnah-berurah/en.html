(1) They bow — if saliva occurs to him, he should not bow until the saliva has left his mouth [Magen Avraham in the name of Sefer Chassidim]; and see above siman 97 seif 2.

(2) In Modim — it is stated in the Gemara that if one did not bow in Modim, his spine becomes a snake after seven years.

(3) Beginning — that is, at the beginning of Modim when one says "Heaven, may You raise up."

(4) And at the end — in the blessing "the Good One is Your name," that is when saying "Blessed are You," and when saying "Heaven, may You raise up," and as explained above in siman 113 seif 7 regarding the blessing of the Fathers.
