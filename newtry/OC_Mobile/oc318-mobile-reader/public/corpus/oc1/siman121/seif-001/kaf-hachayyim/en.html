1) [Seif 1] We bow at Modim, etc. And one should not bow at Modim if there is saliva in his mouth, until the saliva leaves his mouth. Sefer Chassidim, siman 18; Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos HaTur; Olas Tamid, ot 1; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1; Soles Belulah; Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 133, beginning of side 4. And see what the commentator wrote there on the Sefer Chassidim, ot 1.
