Individual. In Maharil it is written that an individual says it, and also at Mincha on a communal fast — even one who is not fasting says it; and on an individual fast the individual says it, but the prayer leader does not say it even on Erev Rosh Hashana, because it is not called a communal fast (Darkei Moshe siman 581). And in Darkei Moshe siman 127 he disagrees and writes that an individual does not say it, and likewise R' Levi ibn Habib siman 15; and in Levush there he wrote that they are accustomed to say it, and it appears there is no obligation to protest those who say it.
