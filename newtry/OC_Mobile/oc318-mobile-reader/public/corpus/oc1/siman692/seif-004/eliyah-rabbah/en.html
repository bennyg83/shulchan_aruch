[ד] Baruch Atah [the Name] Elokeinu Melech HaOlam Harav etc. — like poskim who do not say Baruch Atah [the Name] Elokeinu Melech HaOlam HaEl since already mentioned the Name and said Elokeinu; and some read HaEl; and Eliyah Rabbah ruled like Shulchan Aruch not to say; and likewise not to say in Eretz Yisrael HaEl Hanifra etc.; main other matter — see all poskim; Bach wrote to say Baruch Atah [the Name] HaEl Hanifra; even though we do not say Baruch Atah [the Name] Elokeinu Melech HaOlam HaEl — end. He was not precise: Tur and Shibbolei Leket and Agudah and Tzidah Laderech and R' David Abudraham wrote Baruch Atah [the Name] Elokeinu Melech HaOlam HaEl; even so they wrote not to say HaEl at end; and all the more per our poskim not to say at beginning; and what challenged from Knesset HaGedolah on Bach from Rambam nuschaot — undoubtedly before Bach Rambam books with Hagahot Maimoniot and Maggid printed year 3000 where wrote HaEl Hanifra. Abudraham wrote they enacted six wordings corresponding to six of Israel who fought Amalek: Harav et riveinu through Yehoshua; Vedan et dinenu through Ehud who killed Eglon king of Moav and Amon and Amalek with him; VeHoshia et amenu through Gideon Hanifra etc. through Shaul VeHishlam etc. through David; closing Hanifra etc. corresponding to Mordechai and Esther miracle; HaEl Moshi'a for future; Beit Yosef wrote another reason — see there. Minhagim: say first VeHishlam etc. then Hanifra etc. so closing is adjacent to closing; Bach challenged and Knesset HaGedolah argue all authors wrote opposite and in Minhagim — end. Abudraham gave reason why no closing like closing — see there; however in Rokeach I saw nusach like Minhagim.
