(s.k. 4) One should not talk. And the reader etc. — meaning plainly the listener may not talk — while talking he does not hear reader; but the reader etc. like Shema siman 66 — distinction between middle of chapter and between chapters; however he concludes in Shulchan Gavoah: requires study what counts between chapters in megillah since must read whole as iggeret — end. However in Malach Habayit parashat Kaf he read siman 20: since megillah has parashiyot even closed — nevertheless called chapters — current practice; see there.
