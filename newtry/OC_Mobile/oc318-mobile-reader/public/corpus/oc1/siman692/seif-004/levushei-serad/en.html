Seif katan 4. To interrupt, and so on. It implies that he explains this as referring to Megillah reading, not like the Taz, and that he explains it as referring to the listener. This is as explained in siman 690, that even if he did not hear one word, he has not fulfilled his obligation. But regarding the reader himself, where this reason does not apply and there is only the prohibition of interruption, his law is like Krias Shema, where there is a distinction between between the sections and in the middle of a section, as explained in siman 66.
