After praying Maariv; but I would not permit tasting a little and waiting until reader comes as Berachot: one should not say I will eat a little etc. then read Shema; and stronger for megillah lest sleep seize and cancel reading — much waiting and reading is stricter obligation — all mitzvot deferred before it [Terumat HaDeshen]; and see siman 687 in name of R' Moshe of Prague: since beloved one does not neglect; and why they did not forbid learning like bedikat chametz — end. If so lenient from Shema and chametz — and since Shema and chametz permit tasting as siman 235 and 472 — kal vachomer here; and primary megillah reading is by day; and Rama changed wording wrote forbidden to eat etc. — implies tasting permitted; nevertheless do not lighten except great need; Tosefta chapter 1 Shabbat wrote megillah is like lulav and shofar and Shema; implies Maharil: even sick should not eat before reading unless danger; and simple one under duress may hear daytime reading before sunrise after alos — siman 687; Beit Yosef in name of Orach Chayyim custom read somewhat mibaal yad to ease people and pregnant not to fast excessively.
