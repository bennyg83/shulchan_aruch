4) Ibid. And Shehecheyanu. When reciting Shehecheyanu, he should also intend that it apply to mishloach manos and the Purim meal, which are also mitzvos. Shelah. It appears to me that he should have this intention during the Shehecheyanu blessing of the daytime reading, because their time is during the day. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 2; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur. This should be made known to the shaliach tzibbur, because both the listener and the one enabling him to hear require intention. However, according to the Rambam and the Mechaber, who hold that Shehecheyanu is not recited during the day, he should intend during the nighttime Shehecheyanu to fulfill the requirement also for mishloach manos and gifts to the poor, even though their time is not at night. Eshel Avraham, os 1.
