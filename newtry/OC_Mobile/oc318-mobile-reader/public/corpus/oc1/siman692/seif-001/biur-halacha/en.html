(א) Veshehecheyanu — see Mishna Berurah s.k. 1 what we wrote: some say fit to bless etc. — this is from Mor Uktzia who challenged Magen Avraham; and his words: I did not understand why this is a common matter — behold without mitzvot of mishloach manot and meal it is fit to bless shehecheyanu on the day; and even though divrei kabbalah require strengthening like Torah; and more so for an individual because of the miracle's force; and since it comes time to time it is like all festivals of the Name on which we bless shehecheyanu; and shehecheyanu does not require a cup as halacha says even in market; and Yom Kippur has no eating and drinking only sitting and rest — nevertheless we bless etc.; and he concludes there it is fine l'chatchila for one without megillah to attach shehecheyanu to cup of meal — end. But truly per his view he should bless shehecheyanu on the day itself because of the miracle — then he should also bless she'asa nissim; and I found in Meiri on Shabbat regarding Chanukah [and likewise here Purim] who wrote: one without lighting who is not where he can see — some say he blesses for himself she'asa nissim and shehecheyanu on one night and she'asa nissim all nights — words seem plausible — end. Also on his wonder at Magen Avraham — behold without mishloach manot etc. fit to bless shehecheyanu — I do not know; possible Magen Avraham agrees but he discusses when he had megillah at night and blessed shehecheyanu then and thereby fulfilled on the time itself [and what Peri Megadim supports that per Mechaber shehecheyanu at night covers mishloach manot and day meal is unclear; also forced on Magen Avraham's language]; but by day he had none — possible he agrees. Then I found in Birkat Yosef: if no megillah bless shehecheyanu but not she'asa nissim; and Rabbi the Rav [R' Yosef Molkho] — but acharonim wrote also shehecheyanu do not bless — end; I do not know which acharonim found this except Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah who copied — no proof as we wrote; requires study in practice; see above siman 696 in She'arit Yosef letter 3.

(ב) Except in congregation — in Beit Yosef he wrote Orach Chayyim wrote thus in name of Yerushalmi; and see in Eliyah Rabbah who proved from several poskim one blesses even individually; and see Biur HaGra that from Yerushalmi also no proof — it refers only to Birkat HaTorah. Nevertheless it appears unfit to bless afterward since anyway this blessing even in congregation is not obligatory and depends on custom as in gemara; and who will say custom spread today to bless individually; and so Peri Megadim: safek berachot lehakel.
