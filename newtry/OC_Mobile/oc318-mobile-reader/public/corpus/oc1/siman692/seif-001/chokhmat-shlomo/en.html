Seif 1 — one who reads megillah blesses three blessings etc. NB I will copy here what I was asked in an incident in our community: reader became mute — does second reader start where he stopped or from beginning. Known what Magen Avraham wrote: mute — second must start from beginning. But Magen Avraham's words are wondrous: he himself finished within 24 that he need not bless anew since he intended from start to discharge entire congregation; if so examining laws Kiddushin siman 140 and Yerushalmi source of Torah reader who became mute must start from beginning — lest they say first reading was not blessed after and last not blessed before; Rosh and successors wrote this is another enactment each blesses. Implies: if not this reason — had they blessed before/after in reverse order he would start where stopped. If megillah need not bless again since he discharged congregation — why start anew. Cannot say Magen Avraham's intent is proof from Rambam who holds no blessing needed yet must start — reason as Beit Yosef siman 140 explains Yerushalmi per Rambam: starting mid-way looks like distraction. Even without blessing before, start from beginning. But most poskim argue Rambam — why Magen Avraham strict on rabbinic matter for Rambam alone. Where blessing itself depends — fine for Torah not intending to discharge many — must return and bless and start. But megillah here he discharges all and exempt from blessing — need not return; why strict rabbinically for Rambam. Even for Rambam perhaps distraction only in Torah reading where blesser does not discharge — unlike here all must fulfill with one blessing — second reader like he blessed first; for himself no concern if paused mid-reading. Yerushalmi proves only with both reasons together — first blessed before not after, last after not before; from last alone concern they not blessed before — only combined we worry. Fine in Torah with blessing after — first not blessed after. Megillah no blessing after — Harav et riveinu is not on megillah only miracle per my Sefer HaChayyim — so cannot say first not blessed after; no concern for first; from last alone insufficient — need not return. Especially Rambam: even returning he does not bless and does not fix last — only looks distracted; perhaps only because he mentioned first. Where no blessing after — no concern for first; even if read from start does not fix last — no need return for this. Rambam may agree; and for others certainly no return; safek motzi midei vaday. Need not leave majority poskim for Rambam safek. Also possible dispute on closing blessing on megillah — double safek. Will not expand. Magen Avraham brought from 484 maftir not 140 body — requires study. My ruling as written; saw Eliyah Rabbah and Degel Merubah sensed part of our words — their intent; they did not explain fully — see my library for difference — check well.
