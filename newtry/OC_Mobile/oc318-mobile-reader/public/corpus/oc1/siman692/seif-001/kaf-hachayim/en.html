(א) [Seif 1] One who reads megillah etc. — good that two men stand beside the shaliach tzibbur right and left when he reads megillah; and even fixed shaliach tzibbur takes permission before starting. Orah Vesimchah daf 25; Malach Habayit siman 31 letter 104; Ben Ish Chai parashat Tetzaveh letter 12. Meaning before blessing he says birshut mori verabotai; and custom of Chasidei Beit El YKB"TZ after chazan says birshut etc. says leshem yichud etc. behold we come to fulfill mitzvah of megillah reading to fix its root in supernal place; vihi noam etc.; then begins to bless.
