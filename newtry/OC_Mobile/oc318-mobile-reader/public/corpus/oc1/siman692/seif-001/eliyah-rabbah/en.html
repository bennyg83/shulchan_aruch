[1] Over the reading, and so on. Regarding why we do not bless “to hear the reading of the Megillah,” as we bless “to hear the sound of the shofar,” the Avudraham answered that with shofar he must hear it with his ears, because understanding of the heart does not depend on it. But with Megillah, even if he did not hear it with his ears he has fulfilled the obligation, for the reading is primary, since understanding of the heart depends on it. One can say further that if we blessed “to hear,” there would be room to be concerned that the listeners would understand the language of hearing as meaning understanding, as in “for Yosef understood,” and if so women and ignorant people would not fulfill the obligation; but we rule that they do fulfill it. Therefore we bless “over the reading of the Megillah.” But regarding shofar, it is impossible to bless “to blow,” because it includes teruah and shevarim; and likewise it is impossible to bless “to sound a teruah,” because there is also tekiah. Therefore one must bless “to hear.”
