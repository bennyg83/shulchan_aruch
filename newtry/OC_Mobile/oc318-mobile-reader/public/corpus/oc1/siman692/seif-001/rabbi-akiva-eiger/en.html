Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. To begin at the start. For me, this is not clear. According to most authorities above in siman 140, the second reader blesses at the beginning and the end, and for this reason he must begin from the start. If so, regarding Megillah, where he does not need to bless, he does not return to the start. See Magen Avraham, siman 585, seif katan 6 and 7, where it also implies that these are two laws, returning to the start and the blessings. But in truth it is all one matter: since each one is not exempted by the other one's blessing, therefore he returns.
