1) [Seif 1] One who reads the Megillah, etc. It is proper for two people to stand beside the shaliach tzibbur, one on his right and one on his left, when he reads the Megillah. Even a permanently appointed shaliach tzibbur should request permission before beginning. Orah V'Simchah, 25; Moed L'Chol Chai, siman 31, os 104; Ben Ish Chai, Parashas Tetzaveh, os 12. This means that before reciting the blessing he should say, 'With the permission of my teachers and masters.' The custom of the pious men of Beit El, may it be rebuilt and established speedily in our days, is that after the chazzan says, 'With permission,' etc., he says, 'For the sake of the unification,' etc.: 'Behold, we come to fulfill the mitzvah of reading the Megillah, to rectify its root in the supernal realm. May the pleasantness,' etc. He then begins the blessings.
