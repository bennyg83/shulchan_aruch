(9) One may not speak while it is being read. This means that both the listener and the reader are initially forbidden to speak, even words of Torah. Regarding after the fact, there is a distinction: if the listener spoke during the reading, he has not fulfilled his obligation, even if he missed hearing only one word, because he did not hear all of it. But the reader has fulfilled the obligation, and sometimes it is permitted even initially, such as to greet or respond between the sections because of honor, as with Krias Shema in siman 66. All of this concerns speaking in the middle of the reading. But if one spoke between the blessing and the beginning of the reading, whether the listener or the reader, he has lost the blessing. Likewise, between the reading and the final blessing it is also forbidden to interrupt.
