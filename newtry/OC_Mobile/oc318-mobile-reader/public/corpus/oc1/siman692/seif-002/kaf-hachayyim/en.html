2) Ibid. Concerning the reading of the Megillah. Why do we not recite the blessing, 'to hear the reading of the Megillah,' as we say, 'to hear the sound of the shofar'? The Abudraham answered that regarding shofar, one must hear it with his ears, since comprehension of the heart is not involved. But regarding the Megillah, even if he did not hear his own voice, he fulfilled his obligation, because reading is the essential act, since comprehension of the heart is involved. One can further say that had the blessing been formulated 'to hear,' there would be concern that listeners might understand the term 'hearing' as meaning comprehension, as in, 'For Yosef understood.' If so, women and unlearned people would not fulfill their obligation, yet we rule that they do fulfill it. Therefore, the blessing is, 'concerning the reading of the Megillah.' Regarding shofar, however, one cannot recite, 'to sound the shofar,' because its mitzvah includes teruah and shevarim; nor can one recite, 'to sound a teruah,' because it also includes tekiah. Therefore, one must recite, 'to hear.' Eliyah Rabbah, os 1; Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, os 2. See further above, siman 585, os 16.
