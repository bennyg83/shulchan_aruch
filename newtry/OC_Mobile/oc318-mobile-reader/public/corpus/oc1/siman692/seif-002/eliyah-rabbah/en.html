[2] And Shehecheyanu, and so on. In Shehecheyanu he should also have intent for mishloach manos and the Purim meal, for they too are mitzvos, as the Shelah writes. It appears to me that he should have this intent in the daytime Shehecheyanu, since their time is by day, as the Magen Avraham writes. See siman 695, seif katan 5. The Magen Avraham wrote further that one who has no Megillah should not recite Shehecheyanu over mishloach manos.
