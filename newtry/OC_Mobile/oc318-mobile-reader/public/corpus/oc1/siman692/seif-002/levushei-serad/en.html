Magen Avraham s.k. 1 Shabbat and Yom Tov. Good to emend: and further they did not enact at all; and two reasons: one — since it is common practice one does not bless shehecheyanu; and further we see they did not enact blessing at all on mishloach manot — kal vachomer not to bless shehecheyanu on this; reason of matter: many mitzvot have no blessing as explained in many places.
