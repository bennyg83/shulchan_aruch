(s.k. 2) And some say etc. primary etc. by day. And above siman 687. Reason: they derive it must be read by day and night from verse in chapter of Tehillim Eli Eli lamah azavtani that Esther said Ekra yomam velo ta'aneh velailah velo dumiah li: trouble was day and night; therefore require publicizing miracle day and night; and since yomam written first: day is primary.

4. And likewise Mordekhai wrote in name of Rabbenu Yoel: Torah reader in congregation who talked mid-reading must return and bless: since talking is distraction; and since distracted must bless. He brought proof from Yerushalmi: Torah reader became mute: standing other must bless; therefore reason is not he did not bless yet: he fulfilled with first's blessings as Tosafot megillah R' Meir blessed on megillah then read. Rather in becoming mute reason is distraction. So too when talking mid-reading. Rabbenu Efraim disagreed distraction does not harm: when talking no need bless; but mute standing other must bless because not yet blessed: not like megillah R' Meir blessed then read: megillah different: he blessed for entire congregation: so too mute in megillah standing other need not bless.
