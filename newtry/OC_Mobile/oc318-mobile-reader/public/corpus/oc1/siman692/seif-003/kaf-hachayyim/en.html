3) Ibid. Who performed miracles, and Shehecheyanu. If one has no Megillah, he should recite Shehecheyanu, but he should not recite the blessing of 'Who performed miracles' or the blessing of 'Who champions our cause.' Mahari Molcho, manuscript responsum, siman 78. However, the later authorities wrote that he should not recite Shehecheyanu either. Birkei Yosef, os 1. See below, os 5.
