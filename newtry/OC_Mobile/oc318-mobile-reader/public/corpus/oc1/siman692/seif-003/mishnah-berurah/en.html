(10) Even though he already fulfilled the obligation, he recites the blessing, and so on. All the more so he can discharge them with the reading even though he already fulfilled the obligation. Nevertheless, there are authorities who hold that regarding the blessing, if they know how to bless themselves, they should bless themselves, since he has already fulfilled the reading. The common custom is to be lenient and discharge them in every manner. See above, siman 585, Mishnah Berurah seif katan 5.
(11) To discharge another's obligation. He should read it for women after he leaves the beis haknesses, for it is a mitzvah that he himself fulfill the obligation through the public reading. However, if he intends not to fulfill the obligation with that reading, it is proper for him to fulfill it afterward through the public reading. When he blesses for women, it is good that he bless “to hear the Megillah,” because some authorities hold that a woman is not obligated in reading, but only in hearing.
