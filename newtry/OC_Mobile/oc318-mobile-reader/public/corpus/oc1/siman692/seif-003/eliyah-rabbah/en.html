[ג] [Levush] and afterward etc. Requires study from siman 484 seif 4 that l'chatchila one should not read after haftarah (Levushei Yom Tov). In my humble opinion I am there: reason of enactment to read maftir in Torah for Torah honor — if afterward reads haftarah no Torah honor; so Beit Yosef siman 482; if reader became mute mid-megillah second starts where first stopped without blessing — like shofar siman 691 seif 3; unlike Torah reader siman 140 who starts from beginning and blesses — there Levush: Birkat HaTorah enacted for itself, unlike here discharging others. One may distinguish haftarah 484 seif 5 from here. Further: there reason requires blessing before and after; closing after megillah was not enacted on megillah — unlike Magen Avraham who ruled start from beginning without blessing for all this.
