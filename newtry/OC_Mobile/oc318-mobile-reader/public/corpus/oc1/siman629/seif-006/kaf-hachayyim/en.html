6) The author of Sefer Bikkurei Yaakov, at the beginning of this siman, was uncertain whether it is permitted to use paper for s'chach, even paper made from vegetation. He wrote that paper made from woolen garments is invalid for s'chach according to the Torah, whereas paper made from vegetation or linen garments is invalid only rabbinically. There is a practical difference in a pressing circumstance when one has only paper for s'chach, see there. Ruach Chaim, sec. 2, cited his words and wrote that this requires great consideration. First, if rain falls on the paper, it will destroy it and it will fall. If it is exceedingly thick, the stars will not be visible through it at all. If one makes holes in it, it is possible that it would be permitted in a pressing circumstance, see there. We already wrote above, sec. 2, that whenever the prohibition is rabbinic, in a pressing circumstance one should not recite a blessing.
