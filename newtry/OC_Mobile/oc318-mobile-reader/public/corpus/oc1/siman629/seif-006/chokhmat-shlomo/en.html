Se'if 6 Regarding a mat of reeds and straw, etc. Note: I saw in the book Yeshuot Yaakov that he questions whether nowadays one may use this mat as s'chach, the kind now made in ceilings, since the way is to make a ceiling from them, etc. It appears to me that the decree of a ceiling applies only if one roofs with this alone. But with those ceiling structures, the main roofing is with boards, and the reeds are only attached over the boards. If so, the main roofing is not with the reeds. Also, the reeds themselves come only to hold the plaster so that it not fall, and the main roofing is the plaster and not the reeds. Therefore there is no concern or question here at all, and they are permitted by law. This is clear in my humble opinion and obvious.
