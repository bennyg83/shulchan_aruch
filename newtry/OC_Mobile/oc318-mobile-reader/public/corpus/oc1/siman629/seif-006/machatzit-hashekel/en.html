(Se'if katan 6) For s'chach. Even if he made a rim for it. Perhaps this is because the Rosh wrote that it does not depend at all on the intent of the craftsman or on the purchaser's intent, but only on the custom of the city, as the Rema wrote. That is because his personal intent is nullified relative to everyone else. However, in the Rosh the reason written is because of appearances, and the responsum of the Radbaz is not under my hand.
