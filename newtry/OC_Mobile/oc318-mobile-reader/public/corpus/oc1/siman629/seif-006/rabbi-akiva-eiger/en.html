Se'if 6 in the gloss That is, most of the people of that city. I did not merit understanding this. In the first law, regarding a small mat, where we say unless he made it for s'chach, and the Rosh wrote that this means that most of the city makes them for s'chach, the Rosh wrote the reason: not everyone knows that this person made it for s'chach. If so, that is a stringency because of appearances. But here, with a large mat, if he made it for lying, even if most of the city do not make them for lying, nevertheless since he made it for lying, it is susceptible to tumah and one may not use it as s'chach. In the Tur this is written only regarding the law of a small mat made for s'chach, and he did not write so regarding a large mat made for lying, as above. This requires great examination.
