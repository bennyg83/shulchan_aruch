Magen Avraham se'if katan 6 Even if he made a rim for it, it is valid. What is written in the Shulchan Aruch and the Gemara, that these words apply only when it has no rim, etc., refers to an unspecified case. With a large mat, which is valid when unspecified, a rim invalidates it, for without specification it is made to receive. But if he made it explicitly for s'chach, it is valid. However, the Levush wrote regarding a rim that it is invalid even if he made it for s'chach.
