(Se'if katan 2) A receptacle, etc. See se'if 2. Meaning, it teaches that even if they broke, they are invalid, because since at one time they were fit to receive tumah, the name of invalidity remains upon them. The same applies here.
And the same applies to a gutter. See Yoreh De'ah 201:36, that if one carved a hollow in a gutter to receive pebbles, it is susceptible to tumah. Regarding the required size of the hollow, there is a distinction between wood and earthenware; see there.
