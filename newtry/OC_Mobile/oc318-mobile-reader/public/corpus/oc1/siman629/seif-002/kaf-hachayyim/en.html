Thus, something that does not grow from the ground, something attached to the ground, or something fit to contract tumah according to the Torah, is invalid according to the Torah. However, something fit to contract tumah only rabbinically is invalid only rabbinically. Therefore, if one has no other material for s'chach, it appears that he should use this material but should not recite a blessing. Mishbetzos Zahav, sec. 1; Mishna Berurah in Biur Halacha.
