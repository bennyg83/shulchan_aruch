In Magen Avraham seif katan 8: the words "a ladder from side to side, like ours" are the Magen Avraham's own words, and afterward he wrote the Bach's words, "and wagon ladders," etc. It is clear to one who studies the Bach that he wrote that the Rashba forbids even if it is perforated from side to side. What is written "and see seif 8" is a scribal error, and it should read: see seif 18.
That we do not decree: one may say the Bach wrote to decree regarding all ladders because of ladders that receive impurity, and he wrote that also regarding roofing with boards one may say as the Raavad wrote, that the halakhah follows R. Meir in his decrees; see there. On this he wrote that we do not rule like the Raavad, as explained in seif 18. If so, here too we do not decree because of other ladders.
