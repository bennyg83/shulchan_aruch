1) [Seif 1] An item used for s'chach must grow from the ground, etc. For it is written, You shall make for yourself the festival of Sukkos, etc., when you gather from your threshing floor and your winepress. Therefore, one may use for s'chach only something that grows from the ground, is presently detached, and is not susceptible to tumah, similar to the waste of the threshing floor and winepress, namely straw and empty clusters, which possess all these characteristics. Levush. See above, at the beginning of siman 626. The custom is to use tree branches for s'chach, because the numerical value of the word ilan, tree, equals that of sukkah. Therefore, tree branches are the preferred manner of performing the mitzvah. Ben Ish Chai, beginning of Parashas Haazinu. Also, the word sukkah contains the letters that allude to 26 and 65. Twenty-six is the numerical value of the Name Havayah, and 65 is the numerical value of the Name Adnus. Therefore, when one uses tree branches for s'chach, he should have in mind that ilan has the same numerical value as sukkah and also as Havayah combined with Adnus. He should also have in mind that the sukkah is an aspect of surrounding light, as written above, siman 625, sec. 1. See further there, sec. 11.
