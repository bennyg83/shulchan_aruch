If the iron heads had been attached, even the male arrows are invalid, because they became susceptible to tumah through the iron, which is a utensil. So ruled the Riaz, cited by the Shiltei Gibborim in the first chapter of Sukkah; Bach; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos Beit Yosef; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2; Eliyah Rabbah, sec. 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 3; Mishna Berurah, sec. 10.
