(15) Falling leaves — specifically valid because he can eat there when wind is not blowing; it is not invalidated because it was not fit to eat there during wind — we find so this siman per Ran: at eating time forbidden to place on sekhakh a garment soaked in water; before eating permitted — see there — we do not say since it was invalid while the garment lay on it it remains invalid; so here.
