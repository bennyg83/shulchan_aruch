(40) Likewise, it is forbidden to use a bundle as sechach. Although the word 'likewise' is used, it means only that just as the Sages prohibited the case in seif 14, so too they prohibited this case. Nevertheless, the present case is more stringent, because even after the fact it is forbidden. The materials in seif 14 are invalid only because one may abandon the sukkah and leave, whereas a bundle is invalid because of the concern that one may make a sukkah from material already in place. Therefore it is invalid even after the fact. In our countries, bundles of evergreen branches sold under the name 'Yelnik,' which are cut to scatter through the house, may perhaps not have the status of a prohibited bundle, because they are not ordinarily left to dry; on the contrary, once dry they have no value. This is the ruling of Chayei Adam.
