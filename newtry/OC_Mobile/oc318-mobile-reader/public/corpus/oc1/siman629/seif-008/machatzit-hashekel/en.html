(Se'if katan 8) A ladder, etc. That is perforated, and the hole does not go from side to side.
It is called a receptacle. As in se'if 3 regarding arrows that have a receptacle: although they are made to receive the male arrows inside them, as stated there in the Gemara, nevertheless it is called a receptacle.
From side to side. Then there is no receptacle. Ladders of wagons are made to receive.
The text should read: and see se'if 18. For it teaches that boards whose width is not four tefachim, even if they are planed and resemble vessels, may nevertheless be used as s'chach. The text should not read: because of vessels. The same applies here, that we do not decree because of ladders that are vessels.
