(Seif katan 8) A ladder, etc., that is perforated. And the hole does not go through from side to side.
It is called a receptacle. Like in seif 3 regarding arrows that have a receptacle, even though they are made to receive the male parts into them, as stated there in the Gemara; nevertheless it is called a receptacle.
From side to side. Then there is no receptacle. The same applies to those of wagons, which are made to receive.
