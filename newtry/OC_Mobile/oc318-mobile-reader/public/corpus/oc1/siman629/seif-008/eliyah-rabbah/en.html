[8] [Levush] And even if he made it, etc. But the Kenesset HaGedolah, Olat Shabbat, and Magen Avraham ruled in the name of the Radbaz that it is permitted if he made it for s'chach. This requires examination, because they did not mention the Levush, who ruled the opposite. Ma'agalei Tzedek wrote that in these places an unspecified mat is for lying upon.
