In a place See the Taz, and see letter 21 and Magen Avraham letter 22. It implies that if after the fact he covered with mats in such a case, he fulfilled his obligation even if his friend's sukkah is available. All the more so with laths and shingles, so long as they are not four tefachim wide, he fulfills his obligation after the fact. During Chol HaMoed he should cover it properly with tree branches, as is customary. In a place where only these are available, one may cover even with boards four tefachim wide, as the Magen Avraham wrote in letter 22. Whether one may bless over it will be explained there, with God's help.
