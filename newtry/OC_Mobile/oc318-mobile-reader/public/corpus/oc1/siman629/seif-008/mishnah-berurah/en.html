(26) To fasten, etc., with iron nails. This is permitted according to all opinions, even according to the view that forbids supporting sechach with an object that contracts tumah, because the sechach does not rest upon the nails. The nails merely hold the beams that support the sechach. For the same reason, one may tie those beams together with worn cloth strips or flax ropes. Nevertheless, one should initially avoid tying the sechach itself to the beams with ropes, because he thereby fastens the sechach itself with something that contracts tumah. This is the ruling of the later authorities.
