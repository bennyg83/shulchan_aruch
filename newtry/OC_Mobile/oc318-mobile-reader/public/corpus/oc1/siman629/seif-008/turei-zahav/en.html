To connect the poles, etc. In the Beit Yosef in the name of the Terumat HaDeshen, he wrote that we do not rule like that answer in the Gemara which forbids using as a support something susceptible to tumah; therefore this is permitted. If so, one must examine why the Rema forbade placing a ladder in order to hold the s'chach, since it is only a support, as I wrote. It appears to me that placing a ladder is forbidden because of the observer, who might come to increase ladders as s'chach and make them the body of the s'chach. Therefore one should not place above the s'chach in the air, and certainly not in the s'chach itself, any vessel, even a broken vessel, for a broken vessel is invalid for s'chach, as the Gemara says, and not worn-out vessels. If so, there is concern that he may increase it in the s'chach.
