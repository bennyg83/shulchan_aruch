Se'if 12 Their law is like invalid s'chach and they invalidate. It appears to me that they are judged this way only to be stringent. But not to be lenient. For example, if there are two tefachim of invalid s'chach and two tefachim of these vegetables that quickly dry, then if they were judged as empty space they would not combine to invalidate, as below in siman 632:3. In that case we do not judge them leniently, for they still have the name invalid s'chach upon them, since they are susceptible to tumah, and they combine to invalidate with four tefachim. There is proof to this: regarding tumah they bring tumah and do not block it. This shows that stringently they are treated as though they exist. See Rashi's explanation that the Rabbis decreed that they do not block because when they dry, etc. That is only rabbinic, and here too regarding the invalidation of empty space it is rabbinic. If so, automatically, for stringency, its law is like invalid s'chach. So it appears to me, with God's help.
