And anything whose way is to dry, etc. - See the Levush, who wrote that if it will dry until its sun is greater than its shade, even now it is invalid, for the Sages decreed about it lest one come to neglect the mitzvah of sukkah, perhaps because he will not be able to make another within the seven days. See the Peri Megadim, who questions his words. In truth his source is from the Ran, cited in the Beit Yosef. However, one should examine this, for the Rema copied the Ran's words in his gloss but omitted from the Ran the phrase until its sun is greater than its shade; this seemingly implies that he does not rule that way as halacha. This requires examination.
