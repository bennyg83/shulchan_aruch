Magen Avraham se'if katan 2 One time. For they become susceptible to tumah through the iron, and therefore even though it was removed from them they are invalid, as in se'if 2 regarding fragments of vessels, which originally were capable of receiving tumah. Se'if katan 3: from male arrows, etc., which do not have a receptacle; one may use them as s'chach even though they are intended to be placed into female arrows, at which point they will become susceptible to tumah.
