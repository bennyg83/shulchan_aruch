It has a receptacle; it is valid. If the arrows once had iron fitted into them, they are invalid even after the iron was removed [Bach; Shiltei HaGibborim]. See se'if 2. Reeds are valid even if they have a hollow, because they were not made to receive anything; the same applies to a gutter [Beis Yosef; Darkei Moshe; Rosh; Levush; Radbaz, unlike Orchos Chaim].
