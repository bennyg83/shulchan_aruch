In truth, Responsa Rashba siman 195 already addressed this. I later saw that Birkei Yosef brought the Rashba and wrote that the Mabit in Kiryat Sefer also addressed this and wrote that it is more preferable to resolve as the Rishon LeTziyon wrote, p. 53; see there. Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Likkutei Maharil that he saw a piece of wood lying on the sukkah with iron fastened at its head, and he ordered that they remove it, for it is already a vessel and perhaps became tamei; see there. What he wrote, "became tamei," is not precise, for it does not depend on actually becoming tamei; rather, since it was fit for tumah and was disqualified from being fit for sekhakh, it no longer becomes fit again, as written in the Levush. See Peri Megadim on this.
