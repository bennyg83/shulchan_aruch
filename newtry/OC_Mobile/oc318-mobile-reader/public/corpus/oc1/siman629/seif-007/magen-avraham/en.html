(1) ladder Because he has a designated receiving house in the place where the Shalivit are to be interred, even though it may be filled with his named receiving house, as in Rashba 3:16 and in Rashba 3:55 pure scales, we were among those specified beyond our kind, and scales of carts or those which are prescribed by the Kotel to place food for the the Master are impure, therefore all scales [BH] and Esach dela Gazarinan should be prohibited:

On it — one does not support sekhakh on something that receives impurity lest he come to roof with it; even though one supports on stone walls because it is uncommon to roof with them and all know such a case is called a dwelling; some place reeds under sekhakh — pious practice (Ran); thus reconciles what siman 631 seif 8 wrote: though sekhakh is valid on spits it is valid since they permitted only if he placed valid sekhakh on them — they did not decree roofing with spits — as there seif 9; Bach wrote by law forbidden to support, not because of decree — strained; per his words the case of spits is still difficult; perhaps in siman 634 Rabbenu Yosef explicitly ruled permitted to support on impurity, else even ten high invalid — see there; also seif 8 ruled for a colleague etc. no concern — proves he holds like Tosafot Har Hashalom permitted to support on impurity, and so Levush explicitly; therefore it appears what he forbade on a ladder is because the ladder itself is invalid sekhakh [and siman 632]; so Rashba siman 195 — know Ramban's responsa is also Rashba's — they intended one matter; but to support sekhakh on impurity is permitted as Rosh, Tosafot Har Hashalom, and Maharil responsa 87 wrote; and it appears b'dieved permitted as siman 634, l'chatchila forbidden as here seif 7; seif 8 permitting to tie with bolts is as siman 634 in Ran's name — since sekhakh itself does not rest on them but only bundles rest on them it is permitted — primary unlike Levush.

(ג) He is prohibited. This is the case:

That receive impurity — Maharil wrote we fear perhaps they became impure through treading — implies if known certainly not impure, e.g. new ones, permitted; requires study, for in any case they receive impurity, and so Rama's wording forbids even new ones; Bach wondered how they place poles on a connected wall and did not mention what he himself wrote in Tosafot Har Hashalom that detached and later attached is valid for sekhakh — so Beit Yosef siman 626; so siman 638 seif 9; see siman 628. Know any wooden vessel somewhat wide and fit to place something on it receives impurity as similar to a receptacle — so Tosafot 5a; therefore do not place a rake or hoe on the sukkah even if broken.
