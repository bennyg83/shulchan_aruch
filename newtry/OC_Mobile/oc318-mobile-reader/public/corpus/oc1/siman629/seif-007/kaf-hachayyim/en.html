7) Ibid. Although it grows from the ground. Meaning, that it develops upon the ground. The word from is not precise. Mishna Berurah, sec. 3. Regarding whether one may use for s'chach the feathers of birds that grow on a tree, such as where one detached them while the birds were still attached to the tree, see Pischei HaDevir, vol. 2, siman 204, folio 39c, who wrote that it is permitted. Nevertheless, because of a decree, it appears proper to forbid it, lest people come to use for s'chach the feathers of other birds that are created from eggs, for those certainly are invalid because they do not grow from the ground, see there. See Yoreh Deah, siman 84, seif 15.
