On top of it. For one may not support the s'chach with something susceptible to tumah, lest he come to use it itself as s'chach. Although one may support the s'chach on a stone wall, that is because it is uncommon for one to use stones as s'chach. Some have the custom to place reeds beneath the s'chach as supports, and this is an act of piety. Ran. The Magen Avraham concluded that it is permitted to support s'chach with something susceptible to tumah, and that what is forbidden with a ladder is because the ladder itself is invalid s'chach. See there, and see the Taz and Ateret Zekenim.
