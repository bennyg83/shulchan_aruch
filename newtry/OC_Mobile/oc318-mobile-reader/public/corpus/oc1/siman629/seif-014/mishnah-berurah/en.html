(38) Initially. If one transgressed and used such material as sechach, the sukkah is valid and one may sit in it. This applies even where other sechach is available; he is not required to replace it. If the odor is so offensive that an ordinary person cannot tolerate it, however, it may be invalid according to the Torah, because dwelling in a sukkah must resemble ordinary residence.
(39) That they shed. This applies specifically when they continually shed. If they shed only when the wind blows, the sukkah is valid.
