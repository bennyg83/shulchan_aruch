(Se'if katan 14) Initially, etc. Valid. So the Rambam wrote, etc. And this is the wording of the Rosh: but he fulfills his obligation, etc.; and so the Rambam wrote. This is the end of the Rosh's wording, so the Rosh equated his view with the Rambam's. At first glance they disagree, for according to the Rambam, if he used them as s'chach it is permitted initially to sit in it, while the Rosh implies that specifically if he already sat in it he fulfilled his obligation, and this has relevance on the first night, but he may not sit in it initially. Therefore one must say either that the Rambam's wording is not precise or that the Rosh's wording is not precise.
