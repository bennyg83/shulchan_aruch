Initially. If he transgressed and used them as s'chach it is valid; so the Rambam wrote, implying that it is permitted to sit in it. The Rosh's wording is: but he fulfilled his obligation if he used them as s'chach, and the Rambam wrote similarly.
That shed. Specifically when they shed constantly. But if they shed only at the time of wind, it is valid, and so it implies at the end of this siman, as the Bach writes.
