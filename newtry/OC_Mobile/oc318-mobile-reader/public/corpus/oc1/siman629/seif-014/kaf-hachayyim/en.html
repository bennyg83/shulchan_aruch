14) [Seif 3] If he covered it with arrows, etc. Although they are utensils, flat wooden utensils are not susceptible to tumah. Although there are flat wooden utensils that are susceptible to tumah rabbinically, these are not susceptible to tumah even rabbinically. Beit Yosef in the name of the Ran. So too wrote Tosafos, Sukkah 12b.
