Se'if 17: But a bundle, etc. As is written there, 11a. All the more so according to the Rashba's explanation, that attached material is also excluded from this verse, make and not, etc. This is all the more true of attached material, with which no act at all was done, even for the sake of dwelling. This is what the Gemara challenges there: why specifically because of, etc.? See Tosafot there, 12b, s.v. ein mesachchin, and from this, etc. But one can say that nothing can be proven from this, for there too, 11a, the Mishnah teaches plainly: or if he cut them, they are valid, and the Gemara says that he must shake them.
