And untied it See the Taz, and see the Beit Yosef. The Bach discussed this at length. The Bach wrote that one can say that even when he raised it for s'chach, Rav Chiyya would shake it after untying it and move it from its place to another place. The same applies to anything that requires shaking, like attached material after being cut; see there, where he elaborated. See the Beit Yosef and Levush. A bundle, even after the fact, does not work rabbinically, and the Darchei Moshe considered this. See the Taz, who wrote that after the fact he fulfilled his obligation; this requires examination. In Tosafot Sukkah 12b, s.v. ein, it is explicit that even after the fact he did not fulfill his obligation, like any rabbinic invalidity. We do not decree initially, meaning that after the fact, such as the first night of Yom Tov and the like, where there is no other sukkah, in such a case we do not decree so as to nullify the mitzvah of sukkah; see Magen Avraham 22. This, as above, is the halacha.
