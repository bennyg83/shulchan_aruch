(17) [Levush] Or to shield from sun etc. — so Rabbeinu Tam in Tosafot Sukkah 10a; Taz explained only when sun entered the sukkah, but if only near the sukkah forbidden — end. Challenge: if so what did Ran challenge on Rabbeinu Tam from the case of spreading a wet shirt on the sukkah — Rav Ashi said they will say we roof with impurity — behold that case shade exceeds sun which Rabbeinu Tam permits — end; perhaps it deals when sun did not enter to dry, and so Tosafot and all poskim imply Rabbeinu Tam permits in every case. I wonder on Ran's challenge: perhaps that case also causes leaves not to fall so sun would exceed — and the permission for wet was when not at eating time as explained there; Tur and Shulchan Aruch's view appears they answered Ran's challenge: that case speaks l'chatchila because of marit ayin, for permission is only for beauty, and Rabbeinu Tam speaks it is not invalid b'dieved.
