(27) Types of food. This means food fit for human consumption, because animal fodder does not contract tumah.
(28) They contract tumah, etc. Even if they have not yet been rendered susceptible to tumah, they are invalid as sechach.
(29) And one may not use them as sechach. Any aromatic plant that is not eaten for its substance but is used only for fragrance, appearance, or the like may be used as sechach. This is the ruling of Peri Megadim. Regarding cinnamon, although Peri Megadim is lenient, Bikkurei Yaakov proves that it is considered food, since siman 202 states that the blessing borei peri ha'adamah is recited over it.
