Se'if 19: And some say that a sukkah, etc. See what I wrote in siman 626:1.
Or that it is soaked, etc. See Magen Avraham se'if katan 25, and as I wrote above in siman 626:2. But the Tur wrote the matter without qualification because he wrote this according to Rabbeinu Tam's view, as he wrote: in order to protect, or, etc. According to Rabbeinu Tam, anything that does not assist in the sukkah's validity does not invalidate it. The Rashba and Ran proved like the first view from what is said there: the same would apply to beautifying it, etc.; that is only the usual manner of the matter. But according to Rabbeinu Tam's interpretation, it is not usual that they would shed so much that its sun would become greater than its shade. Furthermore, the Gemara says there that people should not say that he is using it as s'chach, etc. If so, why would they think that the sukkah would have more sun than shade without the sheet? He likewise ruled plainly above in siman 626:1 like the first view.
