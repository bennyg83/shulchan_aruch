(19) [Seif 4] He roofed with flax not combed and not beaten etc. — in Gemara we doubt where it was soaked though not combed and beaten whether one may roof with it, and poskim ruled leniently; Ran wrote the reason is rabbinic only. Beit Yosef. However Riaz wrote to be stringent in doubt; Beit David Hagahot chapter 1 Sukkah; and so Ritva per Rashi and per those who explain combed flax is invalid because they receive impurity in negaim — even soaked and not combed we go stringent. So Orach Chaim 631 in Maharitz Gaon's name — even soaked and not combed invalid; so Baal HaItur and She'iltot. But from Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch who wrote not combed and not beaten etc. implies soaked is permitted — so R. Yonah Netiv 8 ch. 1; Ritva in chiddushim on Sukkah that the doubt is rabbinic and we go lenient. Beit David Beit HaShoeva letter 21 — for law we hold since most early and late — Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Tur, Ran, Ritva — ruled to validate soaked not combed and beaten, and so Maran in Shulchan Aruch; but in practice since Riaz and Orach Chaim in Maharitz Gaon and Ritva per Rashi — even soaked alone invalid — one should be concerned to be stringent l'chatchila at least not to roof with flax poles from the soak even not combed — see there; and so Mishna Berurah note 11.
