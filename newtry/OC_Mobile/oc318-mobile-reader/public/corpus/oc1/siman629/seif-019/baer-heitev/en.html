Soaked. Nevertheless, at the time of eating it is forbidden, as the Ran and Beit Yosef write. It appears to me that if rain is dripping from the s'chach, it is better to spread a sheet than to eat outside the sukkah. However, he should not recite the blessing to sit in the sukkah, because in cases of doubt concerning blessings we are lenient. Magen Avraham.
