(Magen Avraham se'if katan 19) And also untying. Note: I do not know any reason to distinguish between this case and the case of boards. A bundle is valid s'chach, and is forbidden only because of the decree of a storage pile; it is invalid because of make it, and not from what was already made. Since he returns to nullify that problem and shakes the bundles, he demonstrates that he is knowledgeable about the rule of make it and not from what was already made, and there is no reason to decree lest he sit beneath the storage pile, as the Rosh wrote this reasoning regarding boards in Sukkah, siman 29. For this reason he need not move it from its place, just as with a ceiling that has no plaster over it, where he does not move the boards from their place but merely removes the nails that had been fixed there; we see that he is knowledgeable about make it and not from what was already made. Therefore one should not move away from the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch, that shaking without untying is sufficient.
