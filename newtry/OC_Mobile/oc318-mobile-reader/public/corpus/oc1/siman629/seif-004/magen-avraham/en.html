It is invalid. Because its form was changed, it is regarded as though it is not grown from the ground [Rambam]. Accordingly, even cotton and hemp, which are not susceptible to tzaraas of garments, may not be used as sechach after being crushed and combed [Bach]. Ran explains that it is fit to be placed in pillows and cushions and will become susceptible to tumah. Maggid Mishneh asks how this differs from solid arrows. It appears to me that hollow arrows are independently susceptible to tumah, so the solid arrows do not cause their susceptibility; a pillow or cushion, however, becomes susceptible only when filled and sewn, as stated in chapter 16 of Keilim. Thus, specifically crushed flax may not be used as sechach.
