Se'if 16 in the Shulchan Aruch: And likewise it is forbidden, etc. The Devar Shmuel wrote that bundles of wood called fashini may be used as s'chach, because those who buy them do not usually intend, by tying them, to dry them; rather, they intend to place them that way on the fire and reduce the trouble of gathering them one by one. Since each bundle does not contain twenty-five reeds, etc., it may be permitted. Even if we have some doubt, it is permitted in a rabbinic matter. See there.
