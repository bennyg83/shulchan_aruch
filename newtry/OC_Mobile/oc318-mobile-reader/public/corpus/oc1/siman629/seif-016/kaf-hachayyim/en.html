16) Reeds, although they have a hollow receptacle, are not susceptible to tumah, because their hollow was not made for the purpose of receiving. Therefore, they may be used for s'chach. Responsa of the Rosh, rule 24, siman 12; Beit Yosef; Ateres Zahav; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos Beit Yosef; Magen Avraham, ibid.; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 4; Chayei Adam, rule 146, sec. 11; Mishna Berurah, ibid. The same applies to a gutter or pipe, which may be used for s'chach because it was not made to receive. Magen Avraham, ibid.; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ibid.; Chayei Adam, ibid.; Mishna Berurah, ibid. See Yoreh Deah, siman 201, seif 36, which states that if one carved a hollow in a gutter to catch pebbles, it is susceptible to tumah. Regarding the required size of the hollow, there is a distinction between wood and earthenware, see there. Machatzis HaShekel.
