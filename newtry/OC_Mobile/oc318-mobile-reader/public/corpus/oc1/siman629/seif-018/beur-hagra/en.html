Se'if 18: And so they forbade, etc. See Tosafot there, 2b, s.v. hafchan, etc.
And the custom is not to, etc. Perhaps he will come to use them as s'chach in such a manner that rain will not descend, as Rabbeinu Tam wrote there, provided he made it for shade. See the Rosh there. In the Hagahot Ashri he wrote the reason: since nowadays people use these to roof their houses, the decree of a ceiling also applies to them.
