Magen Avraham se'if katan 19 Untying. For otherwise it is forbidden rabbinically in se'if 15. Se'if katan 21: See what I wrote in se'if 16. This refers to se'if katan 11, concerning an item that is somewhat wide. The Magen Avraham holds that this is specifically where it was designated, but in the Taz at the end of se'if katan 20 it does not sound that way; this requires examination.
