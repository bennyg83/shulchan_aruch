(36) Attached. Meaning, originally attached to the ground, such as a vine or gourd. If it was detached and later affixed, Beit Yosef writes in siman 626 in the name of Terumas HaDeshen that it is valid.
(37) One may not use it as sechach. See siman 626, seif 2, regarding one who transgressed and used attached growth as sechach and then cut it.
