6) [Seif 2] A person may not measure barley, etc. This appears as though he is measuring it for sale. Rashi.
7) There: Rather, he estimates it mentally. The Gemara states that he may hollow out a kav or two kavim and place it before his animal without concern. Rashi explains 'he may hollow out' to mean that with the kav-measure itself he hollows into the pile and fills it, but not in the usual measuring manner of placing it into the measure by hand. Rif, however, explains that he estimates it mentally. Rambam, chapter 4 of the Laws of Yom Tov, writes likewise. Beis Yosef. This is also the ruling here in Shulchan Aruch, and Bach rules the same. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2; Chayei Adam, klal 59, paragraph 3; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 1. This implies that all opinions permit taking it with another utensil not used for measuring while estimating mentally that it contains a kav or two. See Mishbetzos Zahav at the beginning of the siman.
8) There: Rather, he estimates it mentally. Although he estimates mentally that what he places before his animal is a kav or two kavim, there is no concern that this is considered measuring, and it is permitted. Bach.
