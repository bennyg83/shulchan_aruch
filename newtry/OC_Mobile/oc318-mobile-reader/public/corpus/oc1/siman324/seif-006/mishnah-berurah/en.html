(א) Pumpkins — and specifically when they are hard and not fit to eat without cutting; but when soft it is included in exerting in foods and forbidden. And even when hard one should not cut them fine fine on account of concern of grinding, as above in siman 321 seif 2 regarding cutting vegetables fine fine; and see there further that prohibition of grinding applies only if his intent is to feed them after some time; but what his intent is to feed pumpkins and vegetables and the like before animals and birds at once — this is not called grinding:
