(א) Morsan. Ramban wrote in Milchamot for the first view that if one kneads little by little it is permitted without shinui, as stated in siman 321 seif 4, see there:

(ב) And he places before each animal and animal. But forbidden to knead more than needed; and it is difficult, for we say in the gemara even a kor and even two korim, etc. Abaye was pressed on this; and to me it is still difficult, for behold he wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 338 that a cow's food is four kavin per day — if so how does R' Yehuda say even two kavin, what is "even"? Therefore it appears that certainly according to all it is forbidden to prepare more than needed for the day; only Rav Menasya forbids preparing more than they are accustomed to at once on a weekday, and R' Yehuda permitted this.
