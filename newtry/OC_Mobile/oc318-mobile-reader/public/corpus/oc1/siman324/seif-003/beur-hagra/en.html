(ב) "And one shakes it." There, and it is not, etc.

(ג) "And permitted to mix." So explained Rambam from what is stated "what about separating," etc. Semag:

(ד) "And some forbid." As Rabbi, for the anonymous mishnah of the first chapter is according to him, as written above siman 321 seif 1; and the passage at the end of the chapter deals when he placed the water erev Shabbat; and the first version of chapter Kol HaGadol of R' Yochanan bar Yehuda, because the anonymous mishnah of the end of the chapter is according to him.

(ה) "And see above siman 321." Meaning as written in Darkei Moshe that from the Yerushalmi he is liable for kneading when placing water into garlic — the same here, for they have one reason, see there.
