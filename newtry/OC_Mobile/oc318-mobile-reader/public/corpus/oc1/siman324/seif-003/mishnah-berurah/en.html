(א) One does not knead, etc. — even though it is not subject to kneading, nevertheless the Sages decreed lest he come to knead flour that is liable for lash [Rambam]; and the view of many Rishonim is that even in morsan one is liable for lash; and see in Biur Halacha:

(ב) Morsan — and there is no distinction whether he kneads much or little by little:

(ג) But they place water in it — and if permitted to place water into flour in this manner, see Taz who wrote this depends on the poskim's views — for Rambam forbidden and for other poskim permitted. And all this is per the first view here; but per the second view brought at end of the seif that holds even into morsan forbidden to place water because placing water is its kneading — how much more into flour that is included in kneading:

(ד) Since he does not, etc. — but if he stirs or turns with a spoon it appears like lash:

(ה) Even a kor, etc. — and this measure he needs to divide before them on that Shabbat day; otherwise forbidden to exert unnecessary exertion:

(ו) Some forbid, etc. — is the view of Ba'al HaTerumot and his supporters who rule like Rabbi that holds one is liable for kneading by placing water alone in flour and morsan and ashes and every matter; and necessarily water must be placed on it from erev; and even so forbidden to knead the next day explicitly unless through hotza'ah and eruv, as explained:

(ז) And see above siman 321, etc. — meaning there it was explained they practice stringently like Ba'al HaTerumah's view; and Chayei Adam wrote in a time of need one may permit through a non-Jew that the non-Jew places the water on Shabbat and kneads through the above shinui. And if the water was boiling — forbidden even through a non-Jew to pour from a kli rishon on account of cooking; rather the non-Jew shall pour the water initially into a kli sheni and afterward pour the water on the morsan. And know that morsan mentioned in Shulchan Aruch is the husk of grain remaining in the chaff when they winnow the flour; and regarding chaff that falls at threshing it was not mentioned here in Shulchan Aruch; and see in Biur Halacha that we explained regarding chaff certainly one should be careful not to place water himself on Shabbat, for according to many great Rishonim one is liable for lash:
