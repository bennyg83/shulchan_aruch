One may not knead bran for animals or chickens, but one may put water in it and pass a ladle or stick through it crosswise — since he is not kneading with his hands and is not rotating the ladle or stick, it is permitted. One may shake it from vessel to vessel to mix it, and may mix the bran this way in one vessel and distribute it among many vessels placing it before each animal. One may mix' in one vessel even a large quantity. Some forbid placing water on bran on Shabbat and say the ruling about passing a ladle crosswise only applies when the water was already placed on it before Shabbat.
