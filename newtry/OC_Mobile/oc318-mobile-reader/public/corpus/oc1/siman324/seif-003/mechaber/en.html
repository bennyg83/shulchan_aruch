One may not knead bran for an animal or for chickens; but one puts water in it and passes a spoon or stick through it crosswise — since he does not knead with his hand and does not rotate the spoon or stick, it is permitted. And one shakes it from vessel to vessel so that it mixes; and permitted to mix the bran in this manner in one vessel and divide it among many vessels and place before each animal and animal, and mix in one vessel even a kor and even several korim. And some forbid placing water' on bran on Shabbat, and they did not say that one passes a spoon through it crosswise except when the water was placed on it from the day before.
