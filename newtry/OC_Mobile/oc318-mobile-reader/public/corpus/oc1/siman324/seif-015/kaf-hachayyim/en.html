15) Dry tobacco powder may not have water added and be kneaded by hand, because it resembles bran. Although Maran rules without qualification like those who permit, this permits only adding water; all opinions forbid kneading by hand. In Baghdad, may Heaven protect it, the custom on Shabbos is to place drops of water into the small tobacco container, close it, and strike the container against the palm several times so that the powder turns within and mixes by itself, distributing the drops throughout. This resembles the permission to pour bran from one utensil into another. Ben Ish Chai, Parashas Mishpatim, paragraph 15. This permission consequently appears limited to the unqualified view of Shulchan Aruch. According to those who forbid adding water to bran on Shabbos, it is also forbidden to add water to tobacco. We already wrote in the preceding paragraph that Maran himself accounts for the prohibiting view when possible. Here it is possible to inhale the tobacco without water, so adding water on Shabbos is forbidden even when one does not knead it by hand. Where this custom is already established, one should nevertheless not protest. Every person who fears Heaven should be stringent personally and thereby satisfy all opinions. See above, siman 321, paragraph 69.
