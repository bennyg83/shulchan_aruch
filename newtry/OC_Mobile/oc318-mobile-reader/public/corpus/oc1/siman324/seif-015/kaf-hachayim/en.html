(15) Toot powder — when dry, forbidden to put water on it and knead it by hand, for it resembles mursan; and although Maran stam like those who permit — that is only to put water in it; but to knead by hand — for all forbidden; and the custom in Baghdad — see Ben Ish Chai Parashat Mishpatim letter 15 — to leave drops of water on Shabbat in the small vessel of toot powder and close the vessel and strike the vessel on the palm several times so the powder turns inside and kneads from it and in it so the drops of water mix throughout — and this resembles what they permitted to shake mursan from vessel to vessel. Ben Ish Chai Parashat Mishpatim letter 15. And if so it implies this permission is specifically for the stam of Shulchan Aruch; but according to those who forbid putting water in mursan on Shabbat, likewise in toot powder forbidden to put water; and we already wrote in the previous letter that Maran too is concerned for the view of those who forbid where possible; and here it is possible to inhale without water — therefore forbidden to put water in it on Shabbat even though he does not knead by hand; nevertheless where they already practiced, one should not protest; but every Hashem-fearing person should be stringent for himself to satisfy all views; and see above siman 321 letter 69:
