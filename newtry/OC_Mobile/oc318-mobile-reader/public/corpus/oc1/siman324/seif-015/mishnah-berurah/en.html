(א) To rake the trough — that they rake it so dust in the trough not mix with the barley placed before it and it be disgusted by its food:
(ב) Lest he come to level mounds — meaning where it is customary he intends to level so the barley not fall in the mounds [Tosafos]; and therefore they decreed raking even in a trough in a vessel; but permitted to rake a box on Shabbat from refuse found in it, for decree does not apply:
(ג) To remove the straw, etc. — for when it is abundant he wants to remove it so the ox not trample it while grazing; and forbidden because certainly some is already repulsed by trampling of its feet and not fit for moving:
