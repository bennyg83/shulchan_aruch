While they are still moist — see in Shelah who wrote that then they are presumed set aside for animal food; otherwise they are muktzeh and forbidden to move; and likewise siman 308 s.k. 28; and according to what Rashi explained in Shabbos 155 s.v. zirin of cedars that while still moist they are fit for an animal, and most people set them aside for firewood — his answer does not fit well; and see in Beit Yosef siman 308 what he wrote about bundles of zardin that it implies there that even if fit for animal food, nevertheless we require designation specifically from erev Shabbat; and see Bach and Perishah. However I found one of the Rishonim who spoke of this — Rashba in his novellae, and this is his language: he holds we make food; etc., and it is difficult to me, for we learned in chapter Mefanin: bundles of straw and bundles of wood and bundles of zardin — if he prepared them for animal food one may move them, and if not one may not move them; behold whatever he did not prepare for animal food from erev Shabbat — even for moving it is forbidden, all the more we do not make food; and one may say there it deals when he does not need them for animal food but for the need of their place, or for the need of sitting on them themselves — end of his words:
