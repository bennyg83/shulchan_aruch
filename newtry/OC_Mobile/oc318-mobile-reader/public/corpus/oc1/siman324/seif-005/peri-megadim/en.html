There is none — Taz brought Rambam's language chapter 21 letter 8: what the cutter, etc. (printing error chapter 8); and the content of his intent is that the reason tircha in food is the reason it appears like grinding for Rambam, since it is not for eating need, for otherwise it is fit to eat. And he resolves per the textual reading in Rambam; see there; and accordingly for Rambam whatever he cuts in large pieces, even if fit to eat thus — permitted, for it is not grinding; but for us forbidden to all, for tircha in food is forbidden. And the matter is in siman 321 seif 12 like borer, and for that meal permitted and for another forbidden even on the same day.
