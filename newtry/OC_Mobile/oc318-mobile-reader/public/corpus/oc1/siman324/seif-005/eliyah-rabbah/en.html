[5] [Levush] And some permit, etc. I have not found the opinion of those who permit in Malbushei Yom Tov. So too it appears from the Shiltei Giborim and Tosafot Yom Tov in Mi Shehechshich that everyone prohibits this. See siman 314, seifim 7, 8, and 9.
[6] While they are, etc. Meaning, while they are moist, for then they are not muktzeh, as stated by the Shiltei Giborim.
