To feed silkworms — see in Beit Yosef that this means he gives them grass to eat and scatters before them mulberry leaves and the like; see there [and therefore scattering makes food for them, for otherwise scattering would be forbidden, as below; and Beit Yosef's language is very strained accordingly, and it requires study]. And Birkhei Yosef wrote to sweep beneath them the dry leaf remnants even if they are disgusting and not fit for other creatures — if the silkworms do not eat that day until they sweep the leaf remnants beneath them and then they are endangered in their beds, they sweep them in an unusual manner not in the manner of sweeping on a weekday:
