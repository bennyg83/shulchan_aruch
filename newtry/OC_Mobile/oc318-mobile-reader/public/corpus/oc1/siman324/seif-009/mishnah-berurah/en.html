(א) One does not stuff, etc. — all this on account of excessive exertion:
(ב) That it cannot return it — stuffing it for him inside the gullet:
(ג) To a place it cannot, etc. — and implied in the gemara wherever he does not fatten the animal he cannot stuff so deeply; and the place it can return is:
(ד) Chickens and geese, etc. — meaning forbidden to stuff food in their throats until they can return it. And geese that they fatten and were accustomed on weekdays to eat through fattening and do not eat otherwise — permitted to tell a non-Jew to fatten them on account of animal distress; but he shall fatten them only once a day even if accustomed to eat twice a day — so too in responsum of Rama siman 79; and implied there if there is no non-Jew, fattening is permitted even through an Israelite on account of animal distress; and good to do through a minor, end of Magen Avraham's words in siman 305 s.k. 12; but in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he was stringent not to fatten through an Israelite, like Maharshal who forbids this; and likewise in Netiv Chayim he was stringent on this.
