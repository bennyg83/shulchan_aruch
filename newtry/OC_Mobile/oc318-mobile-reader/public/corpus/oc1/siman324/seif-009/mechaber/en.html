One does not stuff the camel — meaning he feeds it by hand so much that the intestines expand like a feeding trough; and one does not force-feed — meaning he presses food in its throat to a place it cannot regurgitate. But coaxing is permitted. One does not muzzle calves but one may coax them. What is coaxing? Placing in a place it cannot return the placing. Force-feeding is placing in a place it can return it.And the law of chickens and geese is like the law of calves [Maharil].]:
