One may not overfeed a camel — meaning feeding it by hand so much that its intestines expand like a trough — nor force-feed it by pressing food down its throat to a point from which it cannot regurgitate. But one may coax it to eat. One may not force-feed calves but may coax them. What is coaxing? Placing food where it cannot be regurgitated. Force-feeding is placing food where it can be regurgitated. {Rama: Rema: The law of chickens and geese is the same as that of calves.}
