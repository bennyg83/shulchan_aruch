Seif 4 "a knot that is not permanent." In my humble opinion, from Rambam's words chapter 10 law 4 that he wrote "everything fit for animal food — permitted to tie it on Shabbat," etc., it appears at first glance that in any case permitted to tie it. And if so — automatically it would be permitted to untie it. And so I saw in HaReikash siman 317 who brought Rambam's words to be lenient. And he wrote on this: and it appears that this is even if it will be permanent artisan work when it dries. End quote.
