(ב) (s.k. 8) For Rosh wrote on the mishnah "one cuts pumpkins" — when detached from erev Shabbat (for if on Shabbat even R' Shimon concedes regarding muktzeh of something attached, for it resembles dried figs and raisins that are forbidden, for they are called prepared by hand since not gathered from yesterday) — what does it teach; and Rosh wrote and it was explained to me there is no concern of making it food according to one master nor exerting in food according to one master. And Rabbeinu Yerucham explained without cutting they are eaten through pressure; therefore for R' Yehuda who only permits making food, since they are not eaten except through pressure — the cutting is called making food; and also for R' Nachman who only permits exerting in food and not making food — for him this is called exerting in food, since without cutting they are not eaten except through pressure, end of his words. Behold when eaten through pressure — even for R' Nachman permitted; and necessarily Rav Yehuda who argues and the mishnah is for making food — and we rule accordingly — even if not eaten at all without cutting, permitted; behold not like Eliyah Rabbah:
