(א) Before bees and not before, etc. — even if they are his, nevertheless their sustenance is not upon him, for they are common in the field; and it is exertion not for need:

(ב) And not before a swine — for its sustenance is not upon him, since forbidden to raise it, as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 409. And if they fell to him in payment of his debt that permitted to retain until he finds to sell them at their value — called their sustenance is upon you, and permitted to give them sustenance:

(ג) Before a dog — and even a dog one does not raise in his house is permitted, for there is somewhat of a mitzvah also to give it sustenance, as they said the Holy One was particular about it because its sustenance is scant and it holds its food in its bowels so it will not digest for three days; and see in Eliyah Rabbah. And before a bad dog forbidden like before a swine, for forbidden to raise pigs and a bad dog [Acharonim]. Some practice to give wheat before birds on Shabbat shirah, and this is not proper, for their sustenance is not upon you:
