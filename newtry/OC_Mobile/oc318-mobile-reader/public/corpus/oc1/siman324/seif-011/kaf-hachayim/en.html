And he passes a shovel through it, etc. — Rashi explained once he passes the stick back and once forth; and Beit Yosef wrote it appears from his words he is not permitted to pass the stick more than once back and once forth; but from Rif and Rambam's words it appears even many times he may pass the shovel back and forth and he is not concerned, since he does not mix by hand nor turn it.
