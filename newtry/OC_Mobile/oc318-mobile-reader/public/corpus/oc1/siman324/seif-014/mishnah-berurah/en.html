(א) And they give before an ox — for an ox is not disgusted by food left from a donkey that does not emit saliva:

(ב) And it is not fit anymore for a donkey — only through pressure; and exerting in vain; and specifically a donkey that is not its species is disgusted by its food on account of saliva; but before another ox that is its species it is not disgusted on account of saliva and permitted according to all:

(ג) And to give before another — lest he find saliva in the food and be disgusted by it and the exertion be in vain. And Acharonim wrote one should not protest the hand of those who practice leniency, for they have on whom to rely — except in known species whose food is repulsed by saliva emerging from its mouth and the other species is disgusted by what is placed before it:
