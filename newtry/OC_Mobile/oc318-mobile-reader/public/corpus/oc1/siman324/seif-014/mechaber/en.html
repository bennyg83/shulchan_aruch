One takes food from before a donkey and places before an ox; but one does not take from before an ox to place before a donkey, because it becomes repulsive from the ox's chewed cud and is no longer fit for the donkey.And some are stringent also regarding other species of animals, to take from before one' and place before another that is not its species [Mordechai chapter Tolim and Semag and Sefer HaTerumah].]:
