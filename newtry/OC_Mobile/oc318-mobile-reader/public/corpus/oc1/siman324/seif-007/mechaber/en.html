One may cut up a carcass before dogs, even if it died that same day — whether it had been ill or healthy. This applies to a hard carcass that they cannot eat without cutting, but if it was suitable for them without cutting, one may not cut it, since we do not take the trouble to prepare something that is already suitable. {Rama: (See above Siman 321 regarding whether one may chop it finely for birds.)}
