And see above siman 321 whether it is permitted to cut fine, etc. In siman 321 Rama cited a responsum of Mahariv in Terumat HaDeshen siman 56 that one should not cut raw meat fine fine before birds, since they cannot eat it without cutting — he makes it food; and this is very astonishing, for here we rule like Rabbi that making food is permitted and tircha in food is forbidden, as is written in this seif; and if so, why does he forbid here to cut fine fine since it is impossible otherwise; and in Terumat HaDeshen he wrote, and this is his language: and further one should touch it on account of the prohibition of making food for an animal, and on account of tircha in food for an animal like that in chapter Mi sheHechshich — end of his words. It appears his intent is that the primary prohibition is on account of tircha in food; and what he mentioned on account of making food was for emphasis; and it appears to me his intent is that whenever he cut it into pieces the birds can eat, only in what he cuts fine fine is there tircha, since they can eat without it. And Rama's words that it is impossible to eat it otherwise are very difficult, for from permission he makes prohibition; and in truth one may also wonder at Terumat HaDeshen, for it is impossible for birds to eat if he does not cut fine fine, and it would be making food and permitted; and further, Rambam wrote that in a mortar one is liable if he pounds shachat and carobs, on account of grinding; but one cuts the carcass, etc., and there is no grinding, as is written below — his words prove that cutting fine fine that otherwise they could not eat has no grinding at all; and it requires great study what Terumat HaDeshen considered to forbid this, and all the more Rama who wrote here for birds in siman 321 and here it is explained to permit in making food — this is only astonishment and lack of precision; and in Levush he followed Rama and did not notice their contradiction:
