(א) Seif 67 "whether." There 45b, and as Mar son of R' Yosef — for they concluded Ri'f and Rosh "it appears to us that according to all it is permitted." Beit Yosef. And it escaped me — the words of Rosh there, and we establish it, etc., and likewise the Tur in siman 518, and likewise Semag and Mordechai beginning of chapter 19 explicitly, and he brought proof from what we say at the beginning of chapter 4 of Yom Tov — specifically in a shaky sukkah that one expects, etc., likewise specifically in an ill person that one expects; and as written in chapter 3 of Yom Tov; and likewise Rashba in responsum 72 and in Bava Kamma 46b: a healthy animal that died — whether on Shabbat or on Yom Tov — one may not move it from its place, and even to cut it for his dog, for a person does not expect when his animal will die, and this is like dried figs and raisins that he has no intent on at all. If it was dangerously ill from erev and died tomorrow — permitted to cut it for dogs, for this one was prepared from erev for dogs. If it was ill from erev and died tomorrow on Shabbat — permitted to cut it before dogs; but on Yom Tov — forbidden, for there is muktzeh on Yom Tov and there is no muktzeh on Shabbat — and all this is not per the words of Shulchan Aruch. But it requires study from what he explained that even when slaughtering an ill person who fell ill on Shabbat — permitted, for there is no muktzeh, only rejected by hand — if so, all the more so when it died on its own, for behold slaughtering did not enter his mind at all; and one should say as written in the first chapter of Yom Tov: because prepared for a person is not prepared for dogs — this is specifically according to R' Yehuda who said muktzeh, and we do not rule except in muktzeh on account of prohibition that is rejected by hand, as stated. And it requires study on Rosh and Tur, and see above siman 318 seif 2. And also possible to say that these passages that disagree regarding an ill person on Shabbat — they disagree in the dispute of the amoraim who disagree according to R' Shimon in Pesachim 46 that died, and R' Dimi of Neharde'a whom the halachah follows; and likewise the passages in the first chapter of Yom Tov as stated prove like Mar son of R' Yosef as stated.
