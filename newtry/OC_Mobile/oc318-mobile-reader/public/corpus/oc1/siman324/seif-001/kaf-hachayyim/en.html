1) [Seif 1] One may not sift, etc., because of sifting. Yerushalmi; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1.
2) There: One may not sift straw in a sieve so that the chaff falls, etc. Straw is made from stalks cut with threshing sledges, so that the entire tail of the ears becomes straw. Chaff is the upper beard of the ear and is not fit for food. The straw is sifted so that the chaff falls away. Rashi.
3) There: So that the chaff descends. This constitutes weekday conduct. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2.
4) There: Although the chaff falls from it. It falls through the holes of the sieve. Rashi.
5) There: Since he does not intend it. This follows Rabbi Shimon, who holds that an unintended result is permitted. Rashi.
