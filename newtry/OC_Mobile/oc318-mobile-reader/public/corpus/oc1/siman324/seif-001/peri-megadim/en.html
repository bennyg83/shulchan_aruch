(ב) But Taz Shabbos 125b and Beitzah — Rif and Rosh ruled like R' Yosi b'R Yehuda, that stam tanna is like him, namely the mishnah they put water in mursan; see there. And I the simpleton in siman 321 did not understand thus; and so Rosh's language appears: one authority — R' Yosi b'R Yehuda; and it implies the halacha is like him since he said "one authority" means to inform that it is halacha; and perhaps also like R' Yosi b'R Yehuda he holds, and nevertheless forbids; and one should not elaborate here. Beit Yosef's view in Rif, Rambam, and Rosh is thus, as I will say: for halacha they all ruled like R' Yosi b'R Yehuda like the later stam; and Rava too said R' Yosi b'R Yehuda is not a lone opinion (and this is what Rif wrote, since we resolve according to Beit Yosef's understanding); and also Ra'ah in chapter 21 halakhah 34 was silent — he conceded to him; and Tosafos' view also appears thus. However regarding dust that is not a matter of kneading — if there is liability for a sin-offering according to R' Yosi b'R Yehuda in this, there is no proof to rule like Rambam, for Rambam, Tosafos, and many Geonim disagree on this and hold he is liable for a sin-offering (and kitma is permitted — there are explanations and resolutions); if so it is fit to rule like the many in this, since there is practical difference — a leniency from them: if he puts water in flour for chickens on Shabbat — for Rambam forbidden, is kneading not so, and R' Yosi b'R Yehuda said thus he is not liable but there is a prohibition in water; and for Tosafos and Ra'ah — the leniency of dust: liable in kneading in any case, and water exempt; and mursan is not a matter of kneading — water permitted; and they have no reason to decree since it is the day's need; and flour without the day's need — they decreed; behold flour for the day's need — water permitted according to R' Yosi b'R Yehuda, for the halacha is like him. And what Beit Yosef wrote — Tosafos did not mention mursan, even though they mentioned it in daf 18a — that is for Abbaye; but for Rav Yosef one may say mursan in truth is not really a matter of kneading and he is liable in kneading it, and water is permitted in mursan for the day's need, and not in flour without the day's need they decreed on account of kneading; and practical difference for disqualification of testimony, lashes, excommunication, as we explained in the comprehensive introduction and in Shakh — tircha not for need is not a prohibition at all, and one is not lashed nevertheless. Or one may say: certainly there is mursan — thick peel is not a matter of kneading, and thin peel is a matter of kneading — requires study; and Peri Megadim there in Magen Avraham letter 1. And however it may be, Beit Yosef's words are clear and straight; and see there; and for what Eliyah Rabbah and our master Taz wrote — I concede to Beit Yosef for Tosafos: flour for the day's need — permitted in putting water; and for Rambam certainly forbidden, for if they decreed one does not knead mursan on account of kneading in flour — all the more they decreed water on account of kneading in flour; except according to Maggid Mishneh's explanation he disagrees with Beit Yosef: Beit Yosef understood Maggid Mishneh wrote proof for Rambam that dust not a matter of kneading has no sin-offering liability, for if there were sin-offering liability in kneading according to R' Yosi b'R Yehuda — why was putting water in mursan permitted, which is not a matter of kneading, and why did we not decree, etc.; and Tosafos should be challenged; and one must say Tosafos hold we do not decree, for this is not compelling; also what Beit Yosef wrote — R' Yosi b'R Yehuda "he is not liable" implies there is a prohibition — not compelling, for otherwise he should have clarified perhaps, etc.; rather Maggid Mishneh spoke gently: for Rambam therefore water in mursan is permitted because it is not a matter of kneading; if it were a matter of kneading — water forbidden; and it is compelled for Rambam since they decreed kneading mursan on account of kneading flour — all the more a close decree; and practical difference in flour likewise forbidden water for the day's need for Rambam; and proof for Rambam is from what is written, and Kitma testifies on this; see chapter 8 letter 16; and understand — this is what appears to me in his words; and see siman 321 and answer through shinui for one who holds Torah law — there must be complete shinui; and for one who holds rabbinic — shinui avails where possible; or the dispute of R' Yosi and Abbaye; and the matter is in siman 321.
