(א) One does not sift — on account that it is like miraked:

(ב) Straw — is what they cut the stalk in a morig and make the entire tail of the ear into straw; and chaff is from the upper beard of the ear and is not fit for animal food; and they sift it so the chaff falls; and it teaches us that even this is forbidden on Shabbat — and all the more one may not sift grain from its refuse, for this is miraked literally and liable:
