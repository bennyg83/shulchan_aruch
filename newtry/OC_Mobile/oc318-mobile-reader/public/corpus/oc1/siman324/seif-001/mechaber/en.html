דין הכנת מאכל בהמה בשבת. ובו טו סעיפים:
Laws of preparing animal food on Shabbat. Contains 15 sections One may not sift straw through a sieve to cause the chaff to fall to the ground, nor place it in an elevated position so the chaff descends. But one may take it in a sieve and place it in the feeding trough — even though the chaff falls on its own, it is permitted since he does not intend this.
