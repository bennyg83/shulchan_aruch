דין הכנת מאכל בהמה בשבת. ובו טו סעיפים:
The laws of preparing animal food on Shabbat. And in it 15 seifim: One may not sift straw in a sieve so that the chaff falls to the ground, nor place it in a high place so the chaff descends; but one may take it in a sieve and place it in the feeding trough — even though the chaff falls on its own, it is permitted since he does not intend it.
