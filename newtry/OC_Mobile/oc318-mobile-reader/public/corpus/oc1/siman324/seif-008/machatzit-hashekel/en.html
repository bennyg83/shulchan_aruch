(s.k. 15) And some are stringent, etc. — Bach; and he wrote the reason: Mordekhai cited in name of Sefer HaTerumah to forbid — truly so wrote in the short Sefer HaTerumah; but in the long Sefer HaTerumah he was uncertain; and likewise Semag is uncertain; and in Semak he wrote explicitly to permit, since the Sages did not mention prohibition regarding other animals; and since there are those who practice leniency, one may rely on them; one should not protest his hand.
