1) [Seif 1] Rama's gloss: One may not make a sukkah beneath a house, etc. For we require that it be made beneath the open sky. It is written, You shall dwell in sukkos, with the word sukkos written defectively, without a vav, and we expound: not in a sukkah beneath another sukkah, not in a sukkah beneath a tree, and not in a sukkah beneath a house. Gemara, Sukkah 9b; Tur; Levush; Taz, s.k. 1. A house itself is invalid, for the Merciful One said sukkah, and not one's house of the entire year. Rashi, Sukkah 14a. A tree is invalid because it is attached to the ground. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1, and Levushei Serad. Something attached is invalid because it is written, when you gather from your threshing floor and your winepress, and we expound: make your sukkah from that which you gather from your threshing floor and your winepress, and not from something attached to the ground. Beit Yosef; Levush. See below, at the beginning of siman 629.
