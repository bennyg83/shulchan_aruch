(S.k. 1) Beneath, etc. A house is invalid because the Merciful One said sukkah, etc. The reason he did not suffice, regarding a house as well, with the reason that it is attached, is because the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 629, s.k. 11, in the name of the Terumas HaDeshen, that something detached and later attached is called detached regarding sukkah. Therefore Rashi needed another reason.
