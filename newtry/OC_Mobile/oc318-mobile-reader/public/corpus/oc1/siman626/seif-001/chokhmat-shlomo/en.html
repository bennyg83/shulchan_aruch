Se'if 1 But if the sukkah's shade is not greater than its sun except through the tree, he must lower the branches and mix them with the s'chach, etc. Note: Regarding the reason why it is permitted to mix invalid s'chach with valid s'chach in order to nullify it, there are different views about the reason. See my work on Yoreh De'ah, sixth edition, siman 152, which is on Yoreh De'ah siman 99, where I wrote a proof that the main reason is that it is still the time of permission, and not the Levush's reason that it is a mitzvah matter; see there, where I brought a proof from Menachos. Consider this carefully. See Taz s.k. 3, where he brought views about whether the invalidity of sukkah beneath a sukkah applies to invalid s'chach. See my novellae to Orach Chaim, siman 651, in the year 5607, where I brought proof from chapter HaNechenakin that sukkah beneath a sukkah does not apply to invalid s'chach; see there and consider it. See also my responsum to the holy community of Bizhinov, in my work on Orach Chaim from the year 5616, page 67, where I wrote at length about this, with God's help, explaining why the poskim omitted Rava's statement there: four tefachim, what is the law if he made, etc. See there, for a fine analysis that also relates here. Consider this carefully. Now, regarding what is stated there in the Shulchan Aruch: one who makes his sukkah beneath a tree, some say that if the tree's shade is greater than its sun, it is invalid. See the Ran, who asked why much s'chach is needed, since a breached area equal to the standing area is permitted. See my work on Orach Chaim in the omissions to siman 651:1 and siman 629, where I wrote a resolution to the Ran's question; see there and consider it.
