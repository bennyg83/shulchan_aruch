Magen Avraham s.k. 1 Invalid, etc. His intent is not a sukkah beneath a house, for that is derived from a verse, as brought in the Taz s.k. 1. Rather, his intent is a dwelling house in which one lives that was made into a sukkah, like the case at the end of siman 631. Likewise, regarding a tree, because it is attached, that is to say the tree itself; but a sukkah beneath a tree is derived from a verse. It is a little difficult why the Magen Avraham wrote this, for even if a house and a tree were valid, nevertheless the sukkah beneath them would be invalid from the verse, just like a sukkah beneath a sukkah, where although the upper one is valid, nevertheless the lower one is invalid. One can say that he comes to explain that a tree is invalid s'chach, which is the reason for the whole siman, and incidentally he also wrote the invalidity of a house.
