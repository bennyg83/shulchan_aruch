Beneath See the Taz, Gemara 9b. A sukkah beneath a sukkah is invalid when there is a measure of ten tefachim between them, as in siman 628, together with the other conditions. But a sukkah beneath an attached tree, or beneath a house, is invalid because of make it, and not from what was already made, even if there is no space between them and the like. See Magen Avraham letter 1, and the Bach and Levush here. With God's help, it will be explained further there.
