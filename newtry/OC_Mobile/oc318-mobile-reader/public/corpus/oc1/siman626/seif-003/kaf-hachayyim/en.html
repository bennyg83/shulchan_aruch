3) A person fulfills his obligation only with a sukkah that was made solely for shade, similar to the Clouds of Glory. If, however, it was also made as a dwelling, meaning to reside in it permanently and not merely to shelter in its shade, or if it was also made for a private use, such as a storehouse or another use that must be performed in a private place, or if it was also made as protection and shelter from storm and rain, then whenever it was not made solely for shade, it is not a sukkah but a house. A house is designated for all these purposes, and the Torah said sukkah, not a house. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1.
