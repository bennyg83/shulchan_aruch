Magen Avraham s.k. 6 And so it is in the Gemara, folio 14b, regarding boards. This is puzzling, for there no intervening item exists, and they are also directly next to one another, yet it is valid. Rather, in truth the reason there is different: each board itself is valid s'chach, even if the whole sukkah were made from them. If so, there is no proof from there. Afterwards the book Machatzis HaShekel was printed, and I saw that he too raised this point.
