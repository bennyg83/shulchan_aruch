Hagah, seif 1 And an individual says it, etc. That is, regarding an individual there is no distinction between the eve of Yom Kippur and Yom Kippur, for in both he says it after completing his prayer. But regarding the shaliach tzibbur there is a distinction, for on Yom Kippur he says it within the prayer. Thus Beit Yosef explained the words of the Tur.
