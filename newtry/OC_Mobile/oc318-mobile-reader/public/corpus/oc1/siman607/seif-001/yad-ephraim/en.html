In Magen Avraham subparagraph 1 As it is written, "fortunate is he whose transgression is forgiven, etc." This is specifically where there is no loss to others, as stated in Sotah 7: Granted, Yehudah confessed so that Tamar should not be burned, but why did Reuven confess? Did Rabbi Shimon not say: one who specifies his sins is brazen, etc.? It answers: so that his brothers should not be suspected. See what my grandfather wrote in Tevuos Shor, siman 5, subparagraph 7, and in his novellae to Chullin 41; see there regarding this matter.
