(Subparagraph 1) Before, etc. Lest something occur, etc.; this is the reading of the Rif.
The Rambam wrote in chapter 2 of the Laws of Repentance, in these words: and the mitzvah of confession on Yom Kippur, etc. Before he eats, lest he choke during his meal before he confesses, end quote. The Lechem Mishneh wrote that he explains the words of the Talmud, "lest something occur, etc.," this way. See the Bach. But our reading in Yoma 87b is: lest his mind be confused during the meal. Rashi explained: because of drunkenness. So too wrote the Ran.
