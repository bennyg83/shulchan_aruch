A) [Section 1] One should confess to the offering before a meal, etc., lest something spoils the meal (drunkenness, etc.) and he cannot confess afterward. II. MA SKA. The Rabbis O'1 Mb2 O'1 and the Rambam PB2 of the 50th 67th wrote lest he should choke on a meal before he confesses. And the Bedouin B2h wrote that this was the view in Bariata and that the 3rd of the Hagat O'1 wrote a different version would have been for him. And it seems to me that the Rambam interprets Lest something spoils happen at the meal P. Lest he suffocates at the meal.
