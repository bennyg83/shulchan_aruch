Magen Avraham subparagraph 2 For certainly the amoraim in Yoma 86 after Rav Yehudah: Rav raised a contradiction, it is written "fortunate is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered," and it is written "one who covers his transgressions will not succeed." It is not difficult: here with a public sin, here with a sin that is not public. How could one think that they disagree with the tanna'im, Rabbi Yehudah ben Bava and Rabbi Akiva, who do not distinguish at all between public and not public? The Beis Yosef discusses this at length, and the Magen Avraham answers that the amoraim speak aloud, while the tanna'im speak quietly. Therefore there is no distinction between public and not public.
