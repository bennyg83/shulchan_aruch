Ibid., seif 3. “One must confess while standing,” etc. Note: See Magen Avraham, who writes that one should say, “unintentionally and intentionally,” because one must first seek forgiveness for the lighter offense. This is a dispute in the Gemara between Rabbi Meir and the Sages, and we rule like the Sages. Yet I am astonished, for Scripture states explicitly in Tehillim 32: “I made my sin known to You, and I did not conceal my iniquity; I said, I will confess my transgressions to Hashem.” It thus explicitly confesses the inadvertent sin first and afterward the intentional transgression. Rabbi Meir’s contrary order is therefore extremely difficult. This requires study. Examine carefully.
