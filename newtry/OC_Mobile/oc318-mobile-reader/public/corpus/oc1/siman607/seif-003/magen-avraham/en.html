Standing - He should bow as in Modim (Shelah), and should not lean, as written at the beginning of siman 585.
But we have sinned - Meaning that here the confession begins, until after "we are guilty" (Shelah). The plain sense of the Talmud implies that one needs no more, for one need not specify the sin, and the matters follow the heart's intention. One should not speak during the confession (Hagahos Minhagim).
