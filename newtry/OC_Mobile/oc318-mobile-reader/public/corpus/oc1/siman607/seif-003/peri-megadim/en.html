It is not - See Taz. The language is forced, for the Rav is stating his own view, as he wrote in Darkei Moshe, that the prayer formula is not considered specifying, as marked in the Shulchan Aruch. What the Taz wrote, "and like the one who says one must specify," requires study, for he ruled that aloud it is forbidden to specify. See Magen Avraham. One can say it is as if he says: even according to the one who says one must specify, he wrote by reasoning that this is not considered specifying.
