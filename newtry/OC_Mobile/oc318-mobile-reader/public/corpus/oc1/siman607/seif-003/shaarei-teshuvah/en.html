Standing. See Ba'er Hetev. In Yoreh De'ah siman 32 it is written that what the Magen Avraham wrote in the name of the Shelah, to say "forgiveness for iniquities and pardon for transgressions," is a scribal error, for in the Shelah it is explained the reverse. Although there is room to say so, nevertheless since he testified in the Shelah in the name of the Rema, Heaven forbid that I disagree with him. Also in the piyyut "El Melech yoshev" the text is "pardon for sins, etc." In the Yerushalmi at the end of Yoma the text is: atonement for transgressions, pardon for iniquities, forgiveness for sins; see there.
