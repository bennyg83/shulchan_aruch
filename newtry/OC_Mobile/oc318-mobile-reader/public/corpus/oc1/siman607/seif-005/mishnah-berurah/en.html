(1) In the offering prayer, etc. - To Epoki from the opinion of Rashi DSL Dachel that a confession close to the offering prayer can be signed by the the Master the Omnipresent the Forgiving, and the the Master does not sign the confession that follows it and he took a offering prayer because the sign Iiri in the offering prayer is a dove:
(2) He does not seal, etc. - And after my the Omnipresent, until I was not created, which is the Omnipresent as a kind of confession, he will say, My the Omnipresent, I will besiege my tongue, etc. Those who say it will always be to the will immediately after the prayer, before the supplications of the Omnipresent.
