At the Minchah prayer on Erev Yom Kippur, one does not conclude with the confession that [normally] follows it. {Rama: And the chazzan does not repeat the confession at Minchah, but rather prays the Shemoneh Esrei as on the rest of the days of the year (Tur; Mordechai); and we do not say "Avinu Malkeinu" (see siman 614), and all the more so Tachanun.}
