5) A support for the custom of saying before the confession, "Please, Hashem, our God and God of our fathers, etc.," appears, in my humble opinion, to be the confession of the Kohen Gadol on Yom Kippur, when he says, "Please, Hashem, atone now for the sins, etc." We derive this in the Gemara, Yoma, chapter 3, through a gezeirah shavah, see there.
