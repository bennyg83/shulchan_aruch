(א) Seif 5, in minchah prayer, etc. See Rashi there s.v. he does not conclude; but our text lacks it, etc., and Rosh wrote one who concludes in vidui errs; Tur wrote per first Rashi explanation one must conclude when obligated; better not conclude since dispute among rabbis; also one should not conclude as Rashi rejected first explanation; also even per first explanation not obligatory except Yom Kippur prayers; how fix blessing in minchah for itself? Shaliach tzibbur does not conclude in vidui in middle but changes from "sanctified" as Rashi there — not permitted to conclude blessing for vidui alone as Rashi explained.
