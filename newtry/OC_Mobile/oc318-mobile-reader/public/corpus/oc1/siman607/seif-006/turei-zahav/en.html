Face to the north etc. In his clothing he wrote, "And the the Master will turn his face to the south, and not seldom, but his back should not be to the east or to the west, which are his place,' which the Shekhinah resides there. And it is difficult since we kneel and bow down in the Holy of Holies towards the east, and why do we not do so in the confession and above? He said that he should turn his face to the north side, that the main sin of man is because of wealth, and writing from the north of gold, he therefore submits himself to that side to announce that that is where the sin comes to him as above.
