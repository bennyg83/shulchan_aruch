Forty lashes - Not precisely; rather, thirty-nine.
For through this, etc. Meaning that in truth lashes do not help nowadays, for we do not have ordained judges, and there is also no present obligation of lashes because there was no warning. Rather, they do this so that through it he will return, etc. (Beis Yosef). See Responsa R. Levi ibn Chaviv, who wrote at length about ordination and lashes nowadays; according to everyone, outside the Land there are no ordained judges. It is written in the writings that the Ari was not particular about lashes, but they would give him four lashes corresponding to the four letters of the Name, and afterward he immersed, to fulfill "we came through fire and through water," end quote. See what I wrote in siman 606. In some new books they arranged all the laws of lashes, but gained nothing by their arrangement.
Any amount - Meaning although by law its width is a handbreadth.
Of a calf - Since this comes to awaken a person's heart, so that he knows his Maker, this suffices. One does not need straps of donkey hide, since once he is awakened by this, it is enough for him.
His face to the north - It seems to me that the reverse also applies. See siman 3, se'if 5. See the Gemara in Yoma and Tosafos, Shevuos 13, and at the beginning of Sefer Asarah Ma'amaros regarding the atonement of Yom Kippur.
