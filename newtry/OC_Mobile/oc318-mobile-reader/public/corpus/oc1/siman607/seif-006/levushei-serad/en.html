There One must specify. His intent is not that we rule that one must specify, for in truth aloud one must not specify. Rather, his intent is that the Rema should not have written in this language, which implies that it is according to everyone; he should have written that although according to the Tur one should not say Al Chet, nevertheless it is proper for us to say it and rely in this matter on the opinion that one must specify. And even according to the opinion that one need not specify, we can say this is not called specifying. Still, the language of the Taz is difficult.
