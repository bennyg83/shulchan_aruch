Forty. The Ari, of blessed memory, was not particular about lashes; they would give him only four lashes corresponding to the four letters of the Divine Name, and afterward he would immerse, to fulfill "we came through fire and through water."
To the north. The same applies in reverse, with his face to the south. It excludes only that his back should not be to the east or west, which are places where the Shechinah rests; Levush and Magen Avraham. The Taz wrote that specifically he should turn his face toward the north, because the main sin of man is because of money, and it is written, "gold comes from the north"; therefore he subdues himself toward that side, to show that from there came his sin; see there.
