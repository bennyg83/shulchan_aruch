(13) "Nevertheless, he may," etc. Even regarding sins between one person and another, such as theft, robbery, and the like, although the other person forgave him and he returned what he stole, he may nevertheless confess them on Yom Kippur every year, because he also sinned against Hashem. But if he merely provoked his fellow with words, or interfered with a poor person seeking to acquire an available opportunity, one may say that once he asked forgiveness from his fellow and confessed on 1 Yom Kippur, he need not confess the same matter on a second Yom Kippur. [Pri Megadim] (14) "To confess again." The Gemara says that doing so is praiseworthy, based on the verse, "And my sin is always before me."
