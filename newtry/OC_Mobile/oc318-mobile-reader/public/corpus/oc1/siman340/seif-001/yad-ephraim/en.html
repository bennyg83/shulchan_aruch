In Magen Avraham beginning of the siman the cutter etc. forbidden to do as is written end of siman 336 that some say forbidden to immerse as it should read; and what he wrote but actual shevut forbidden to do etc. — meaning where the gentile cannot remove by hand only by instrument; and if she herself removes by hand it would be actual shevut; and what he wrote and to tell a gentile to do a d'oraisa prohibition etc. — meaning even though it is lighter than shevut by an Israelite since it is shevut with no act either etc.; and if so perforce one must do by scraping; and again he wrote requires study whether there is room to permit with scraping for it is difficult to scrape well — therefore better etc. because in a place of need one may rely on the view of Tur that melacha she'eina tzricha l'gufa is exempt and if so it would be shevut and preferable to do thus rather than she herself remove by hand; and even though this would be shevut deshevut according to Tur — nevertheless preferable to do through a gentile as he wrote that in such a case it is shevut deshevut with no act; and on this he wrote and see in Yoreh De'ah etc. — meaning that also for this reason better that the gentile cut even with an instrument rather than she take with her teeth; and it is possible he means that for this reason preferable to tell a gentile to cut with an instrument even where possible to remove by hand through a gentile since impossible to remove well without an instrument:
