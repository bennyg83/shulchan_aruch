(ב) It appears to me to take a black board and the like and white that is white to write on with ink; and likewise reverse if in its place room to write two letters — liable; for what difference erasing ink that was never writing in order to write two letters — liable, even though in Mishkan they erased letters — nevertheless essence is erasing in order to write, for letter written in error is as if nonexistent and like ink; and Levush; and see in Rosh — if so one who buys board or parchment not fit to write thus and improves it or reverse in order to write one letter — liable, for essence of liability is on melacha of thought; and in smudged ink in Tur one can say thus.
