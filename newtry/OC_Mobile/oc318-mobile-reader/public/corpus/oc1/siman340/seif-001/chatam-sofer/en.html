In Magen Avraham at the beginning and he wrote Taz in Yoreh De'ah. Note: see well responsum Chacham Tzvi siman 85 and requires study for practice. And see what Taz wrote above beginning of siman 328 and see in Seder Taharah siman 198 s.k. 139 that he sided like Magen Avraham to permit through a gentile, for the essence of shearing nails and hair if he does not need them is melacha she'eina tzricha l'gufa as Magen Avraham wrote below s.k. 3 and below siman 426 s.k. 11 and not like Rivash siman 394 see there whose words are correct; and see what is in the margin above end of siman 303. I returned and saw that it is so — if he does not need his hair and nail he is not liable for gozez but he is liable for mochek if he needs to beautify the place such as removing a nail or a white hair from among black, and this thing is explained in the words of Rashi on Shabbos daf 73a s.v. deguzez and s.v. hatzad etc. and s.v. hamemakhehu etc. And therefore what Tosafos wrote in Makos daf 20b s.v. bamelaket etc. likewise — it deals there when they warned him for the gozez prohibition but if they warned him for mochek not so — he is liable according to all; and according to this shearing the nail to beautify the place is liable for mochek and likewise for chatzitzah it is mochek.
