A) [Seif 1] One who removes his hair or nails by hand — whether for himself or others — exempt but forbidden; and with a utensil liable to a chatat; and liable for two hairs. Tur. And likewise Rambam chapter 9 laws 8 and 9; and with a utensil even for his fellow liable. Bach. And likewise Magen Avraham beginning of siman Tosafos Shabbos note 1; and likewise Eshel Avraham note 1 that for us who rule like Rabbanan there is no distinction between cutting for himself or fellow — with utensil always liable and by hand always exempt except he wrote in Yerushalmi chapter HaMotzi it is proven one who removes for his fellow even with utensil all exempt — see there; however for practical law we rule like our Gemara; and distinction for repair. Nevertheless see Tosafos Shabbos 94b s.v. aval who wrote this is according to R' Yehuda who obligates in melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah; but according to R' Shimon exempt; and Eshel Avraham note 1 wrote such is Shulchan Aruch's view — see there; however Rivash's view in siman 394 is that in this one is liable even though melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah, for in the Mishkan they sheared tachash skins and needed only hides — see there; and Tosafos Shabbos brought it note 1; and in note 2 wrote such is Shulchan Aruch's view like Rivash, not like Tosafos — see there; and likewise in Neshmat Adam general rule 21 note 3 Rivash's view is Tur's view, not Tosafos — see there; and likewise R' Zalman note 2 and Chayei Adam note 4; and see below note 6 and above siman 278 — examine:
