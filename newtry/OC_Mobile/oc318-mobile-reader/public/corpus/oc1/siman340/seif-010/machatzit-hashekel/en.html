(ב) It implies in Gittin if one inserts (meaning not through sewing but the garment is prepared that one can insert letters there — what they call sheroyf, etc.) — and if so perhaps forbidden. Sefer Teshuvah VeHanhagah challenged: on the contrary in Gittin it implies it is not writing — for it says there a slave does not go free through writing on a kippah and andoktedi; and Rashi explained they are ornaments — and if letters of manumission are embroidered on them the get is not writing because it is not written and fixed but placed on the garment and both its ends inserted, end of his words — behold it implies it is not writing; and he wrote to resolve: perhaps Magen Avraham deals when inserted also in the middle and therefore called writing; and Sefer Teshuvah VeHanhagah concluded on Magen Avraham's words: and if so plain where he should have explained, end of his words; and in my humble opinion also no reason to distinguish between inserted from two sides or inserted from three sides — meaning inserted also in the middle; and in my humble opinion without this there is no question — certainly no proof from there against what Magen Avraham wrote, for regarding a slave there is a different reason stated there: it says one who says to his wife "here is your get and the paper is mine" — she is not divorced; and Rashi explained since the paper is his he gave her nothing — behold the letters float in air; and the same for a manumission get — for their law is equal; and accordingly if he gave her a get and said on condition the paper is mine — even though he gave her at time of giving the get a large piece of parchment in length and width of the get — nevertheless she is not divorced, for nevertheless the letters of the get float in air; and we require "and he shall write for her a scroll of severance and give into her hand" — and this is Rashi's intent in what Rashi wrote on writing on andoktedi because it is not written and fixed, etc. — and it is as if the letters float in air — it does not help, as if he said on condition the paper is mine and gives him another paper — as above nevertheless the letters of the get float in air; and this reason does not apply to Shabbat writing — for in any case it is something enduring; and in this Magen Avraham never doubted — one should not compare get and Shabbat in this; and Magen Avraham did not need proof except that one might think it is not called writing except marking in ink and dyes and marking letters or a shape or marks — but when the letters are of silver and the letter is already made and he merely attaches the letters to something else — such a case one might think is not writing; therefore he wrote it implies from Gittin it is called writing — namely because Rashi needed to give a reason "because it is not written and fixed," etc. — teaches it is not writing at all as above; rather in any case writing is called writing — only there it is as if the letters float in air; and this does not apply to Shabbat as above; and if you say since this is not absolute necessity — perhaps Rashi stated one of two reasons — therefore Magen Avraham wrote: and if so perhaps forbidden, etc.:
