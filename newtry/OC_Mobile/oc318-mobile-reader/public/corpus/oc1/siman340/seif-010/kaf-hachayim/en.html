J) And from this reproof of life on those who grow head hair to resemble gentiles — even on weekdays forbidden; and on Shabbat additional prohibition that they comb with comb and pluck hairs on Shabbat; and also anoint with oil and the like; and even through gentile forbidden rabbinically and liable niduy except one may lighten niduy if he was unintentional not knowing forbidden; but behold nevertheless obligated to fast forty fasts as our teacher wrote in gloss above siman 334; and greater than this Magen Avraham s.k. 33 wrote even in rabbinic domains he fasts; and even unintentional obligated to fast as Zera Emes part 2 Yoreh De'ah siman 89 wrote — see there. And from this I warn others when I see them wiping ear wax and nasal mucus on Shabbat, for through this hairs pluck; and even though davar she'eino mitkaven permitted, where psik reisha one should be stringent and must beware; for sometimes also if with cloth concern for squeezing (meaning if moist); and likewise one washing face who passes on beard and corners and holds them — impossible not to pluck; and known story of Ari z"l who unintentionally placed hand on beard and did not remove until Motzei Shabbat; and because of this he was careful to say "whoever observes Shabbat according to its law" — meaning no dispute in halacha but agreed as its law without any doubt in halacha.
