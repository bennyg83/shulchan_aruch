(א) (s.k. 11) Thread when he tied, etc. — meaning over two stitches even though without tying he would not be liable for sewing, for it is something that does not endure and he is not liable for sewing except through tying — nevertheless liable also for tying; and this is Ra'avad's view in Bartenura:

(ב) For Ran wrote on what the Gemara concludes on this that we learned one who sews two stitches is liable — but it does not endure; and they answer when he tied; and Ran wrote from this Ra'am proved why the sewer should not also be liable for tying — rather he is not liable for tying except when making two knots one over the other; and in sewing two stitches one knot suffices; now. But Ra'avad in Bartenura wrote on one who sews two stitches and ties them — liable for two, for tying and for sewing; except in Mishnah chapter Klal Gadol that counted 39 melachot — and the number comes to teach that if one did them all in one lapse of awareness liable for 39 chataos — even though among them is sewing and he is liable for it for two — nevertheless it counted only one, for tying was already counted separately; and for two ties in one lapse of awareness one is not liable except one — therefore there are not more than 39 chataos; and see there in Tiferet Yisrael chapter 7 mishnah 3 s.v. avot melachot:
