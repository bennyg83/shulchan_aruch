[11] [Levush] But, etc. — missing here what Shulchan Aruch wrote: one should beware not to write in liquid on the table or in dry dust; but if he writes with finger in honey or congealed fat as done in dust forbidden (Perishah); and in Tosefta chapter 12 I found: wrote on walnut shells, pomegranate shells, in congealed blood, congealed milk, on olive leaf, carob leaf, gourd leaf, and anything permanent — liable; on lettuce leaf, leek leaf, onion leaf, vegetables leaf, and anything not permanent — this is the rule: wrote permanent on non-permanent or non-permanent on permanent exempt; not liable until he writes permanent on permanent — end; and obvious same law in erasing; and Rambam chapter 9: hide, parchment, paper, wood and the like are permanent; ink, black, sikra, komos, gallnut water and the like are permanent; and in Terumat HaDeshen siman 63 liquids and fruit juices and dry dust are not permanent; nevertheless l'chatchila forbidden since mark recognizable for a time; and even writing non-permanent on non-permanent prohibition nevertheless exists as Kenesset HaGedolah wrote. Rambam wrote: one who writes on his flesh is liable because it is hide even though warmth of flesh removes writing after time — resembles erased writing; but one who scratches form of writing exempt; and requires study, for from Rabbeinu Yerucham page 87 it appears liable one who tears on hide form of writing liable; one who marks lines and forms on wall and document and the like as painters mark — liable for writing; one who erases liable for erasing; and see s.k. 8. Minor holds quill and adult holds hand and writes — liable; adult holds quill and minor holds hand and writes — exempt; and in Tosefta there unintentional holds quill and intentional holds hand and writes — liable. One who draws on bean shells and lupine shells exempt; one who rules in order to write two letters liable; one who corrects a letter such as removing roof of chet and making two zayins — liable; and Rabbeinu Yerucham page 87 wrote further: removed roof of dalet and made resh and corrected book — liable; and likewise Shiltei HaGibborim chapter HaBoneh in name of Ri'az; and in Yerushalmi chapter Kol Gadol I found: one writes one dot and liable for writing and erasing; one erases one dot and liable for writing and erasing — how so? dalet and made resh, resh and made dalet; and likewise implied in Rokeach siman 86. Wrote with left or back of hand, foot, mouth, elbow — exempt — end; and possible same for erasing; therefore appears to me good to break aforementioned cakes with left and likewise aforementioned books.
