That resembles gathering (me'amer). But threshing (disha) — no, for threshing is only in large ground; and likewise winnowing (mirur); and see siman 316 seif 8. And what Or Sameach wrote — why did he rule like Abaye against Rava? In Rosh the text is Rabbah, and so is primary, for Rava himself says daf 75 there is no threshing except in large ground — therefore he ruled like him, for from here it comes to distinguish between threshing and winnowing, for all are derived from the Mishkan; and so Rambam chapter 8; and Maggid Mishneh wrote likewise that the text is Rabbah and the halachah is like Abaye who is later, end of his words.
