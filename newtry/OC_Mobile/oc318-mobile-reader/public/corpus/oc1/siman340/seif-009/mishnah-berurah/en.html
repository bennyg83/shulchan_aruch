(א) From salt marshes — there are places where water flows into them from the sea and the sun burns them and they become salt:
(ב) That resembles, etc. — for the melacha of me'amer is to gather stalks; and this too is similar; and specifically in the place of the marshes, for there is its place of growth like stalks in a field; but if it scattered elsewhere — permitted to gather to one place:
(ג) For me'amer — and therefore forbidden rabbinically; but gathering itself there is none, for me'amer applies only to produce of the ground:
(ד) Any thing — meaning fruits and vegetables and wood and grass and anything growing from the ground; and in this there is also liability when gathering in their place of growth, for this is the melacha of me'amer itself; and this that Mechaber used the language "forbidden" is to teach us that not in their place of growth — such as when fruits scattered in a house — even prohibition does not exist when gathering them together; and see above siman 335 seif 5.
