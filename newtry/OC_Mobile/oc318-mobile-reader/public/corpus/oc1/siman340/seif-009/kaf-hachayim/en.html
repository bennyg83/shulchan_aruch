I) Therefore one should beware not to rub beard much and not occupy there lest he pluck hair; and likewise women with much hair on head should not rub fingernails much, for certainly hairs will pluck; and inside nose hairs are common — beware to wipe dried nasal mucus gently lest uproot hairs; and likewise when wiping in relieving oneself beware to use much water and wipe gently, for if water is little and he wipes forcefully hairs will certainly pluck.
