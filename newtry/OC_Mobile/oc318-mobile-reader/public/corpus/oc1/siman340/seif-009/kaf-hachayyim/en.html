9) Therefore, one should take care not to scratch his beard extensively or occupy himself with it, lest he detach a hair. Women with much hair on their heads likewise should not scratch extensively with their nails, because they will certainly detach hairs. Hairs are common inside the nose, so one should gently remove somewhat dried nasal matter to avoid uprooting them. Similarly, when cleaning oneself after relieving himself, one should use abundant water and wipe gently. If too little water is used and he wipes forcefully, fine hairs will certainly be detached.
