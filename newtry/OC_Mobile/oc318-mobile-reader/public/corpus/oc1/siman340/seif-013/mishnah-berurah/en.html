(א) One does not break pottery and one does not, etc. — in order to place on them and the like:

(ב) On account of, etc. — and it deals when he is not particular about the measure; for otherwise there is also concern of mechatech; and if he tears paper into pieces in order to wipe himself with them or for some other use — liable for tearing, for he tears in order to fix, as below in seif 14; and Mechaber did not mention here the reason on account of tearing — see in Biur Halacha. And to tear a sealed letter — even if he is careful not to break the letters of the seal but only tears the paper around it — likewise forbidden for all; and even to tell a non-Jew one should beware unless for great need; and in Agudah it is written that he should tell the non-Jew "I cannot read it as long as it is not open"; and if the non-Jew understands by himself and opens it — we have no concern:
