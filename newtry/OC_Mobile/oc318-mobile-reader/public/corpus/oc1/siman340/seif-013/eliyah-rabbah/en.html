[13] [Levush] And also not a letter, etc. — Bach wondered that in chapter HaBoneh it states liable for marking; and if so on non-enduring exempt but forbidden like writing; therefore Bach disagreed and ruled forbidden even on parchment; and Magen Avraham brought it; and it appears to me Levush answers this: marking deals specifically like letters; but for a mere sign on an error found in a book — permitted; and likewise implied in Nachalat Tzvi; and Taz answered since he makes only one mark and also non-enduring — two leniencies and permitted; and even if he makes two marks nevertheless since each is for something specific — permitted; and Gemara deals with two marks on one matter; and Rashal wrote in name of Semak to forbid and explained specifically on paper that endures but on parchment permitted. And this is Malbushei Yom Tov's language: I found Or Zarua forbids marking on fingernail on page as reminder lest he forget, etc.; and R' Yosi whose reason accompanies him forbids mere marking; end; and accordingly it appears to me no difficulty on Tur, for possible he holds halacha is not like R' Yosi as Bartenuro wrote there and Semak's view possibly also like Or Zarua. And Yerushalmi chapter HaBoneh wrote: does not engrave — there is a tanna even engraves; Rav Chisda said: one who says does not engrave — in relief at place of writing like a dinar; one who says even engraves — like a ledger.
