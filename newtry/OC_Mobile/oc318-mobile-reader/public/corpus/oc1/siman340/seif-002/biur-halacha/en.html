A wart from his body — see what is written in Mishna Berurah; and likewise Taz in siman 336 at its end: one should beware not to pluck from his skin by hand or in other places, for it is like attached to the ground, as is implied at the beginning of Sanhedrin that man is like ground, end; and behold its law is true, as is explained in siman 328 s.k. 31 — see there; and even though there it deals with hanging skin-tags of nails according to Rashi — plainly the same in other places when they separated somewhat from the skin; and in Rambam chapter 9 it states explicitly hanging skin-tags of skin that separated; but what Taz hung the reason on account of man is like ground implies the reason is uprooting something from its growth — in Rashi Eruvin 103 s.v. im bekli it appears because of gozez; and likewise implied in Rambam chapter 9 that he counts all this in the melacha of gozez; and likewise Magen Avraham in siman 328 s.k. 35; and this is when done with a utensil, for this is the manner of shearing; by hand exempt but forbidden; and one can obligate them on account of uprooting something from its growth — end; for we already conclude in Bechoros 25 it is called back of the hand, for it is not his manner to uproot by hand but with a utensil; and when he does with a utensil, which is his manner — automatically his liability is for gozez, as stated there regarding plucking a wing from a bird liable for gozez. And know further that per the conclusion there in Bechoros 25, in a matter whose manner is to pluck by hand one is liable for plucking for gozez — if so when there are some hanging skin-tags on his lips that did not mostly separate — perhaps one who plucks them is liable to a chatat even by hand, for the manner is to pluck there by hand and not with a utensil; and accordingly even when they mostly separated — permitted above in siman 328 to pluck by hand when they pain him — here forbidden, for here since it is his manner it is like a utensil:
