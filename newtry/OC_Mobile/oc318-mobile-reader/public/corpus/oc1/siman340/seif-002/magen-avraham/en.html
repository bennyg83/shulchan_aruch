To cut a wart. One who cuts a wart with a utensil is liable if it is moist (Maggid Mishneh chapter 9 — not like Rambam). One who shears — whether from the living, whether from the dead, whether from domestic or wild animal, even from the flayed hide — liable. One who plucks a wing from a bird — liable. One who spins from the living — exempt, for this is not the manner of shearing (there). It implies one who plucks wool from an animal is exempt; and see siman 498; and if he does not need the wool or hair it is melacha she'eino tzricha legufa; see Eliyah Rabbah:
