B) And Taz wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 198: a woman who forgot to cut nails from erev Shabbat and her immersion is Shabbat night — she should not tell a gentile to cut them, for scraping is possible; and why permit telling a gentile; and furthermore she tilts her hand and assists, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 481; therefore she should scrape them well; but Shevut Yaakov there in his book Nahar HaKesef expanded and wrote permitted, for assisting has no substance as written siman 328 seif 17 in the gloss; and furthermore melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah as Tosafos wrote; nevertheless if possible to take by hand better to take by hand — end; and it appears she should tell gentile to take by hand, for it is shevut di-shevut permitted in place of mitzva as written siman 307 seif 5; and if impossible by hand permitted to cut with utensil. Magen Avraham beginning of siman — see there. Eshel Avraham note 1 — however according to what is written above in previous note that Tur and Shulchan Aruch hold like Rivash that cutting hair and nails with utensil has liability even though not needed for themselves — meaning for hair and nails — if so one should not permit through gentile woman, only cut by hand or teeth, for shevut di-shevut in place of mitzva permitted. And such is R' Zalman's view note 2 — see there. And see Ben Ish Chai parashat Ki Tisa note 16 they practiced in their city to rule like Shevut Yaakov to remove through gentile woman and only teach the gentile woman to pull the woman's hand in order to cut and the woman not bring her hand close to her — end; and if so where practiced — practiced; and if no gentile woman it appears in time of pressing need one may rely on scraping she scrape well; and for toenails even with gentile woman better through scraping.
