Erasing. See Ba'er Heitev and what he wrote in the name of Levush. And Magen Avraham regarding books — and see in responsum of Rama siman 119 that he permits explicitly like a vessel made to open and close, and likewise Maharash HaLevi siman 26 see there; and he concluded in responsum of R' Yisrael siman 67 that those who are stringent — they are from those who wonder; and see in responsum Beit Yehudah siman 205 that he sided to permit tzitzit bindings that are in gematria Hashem BaHu since there are no explicit letter forms. He wrote letters on his hand and on erev Shabbat forgot to erase them — and from now it is fit to be concerned whether permitted to wash hands on Shabbat — see in responsum part 5 chapter 2 siman 4; and after pilpul the law arose that it is permitted provided he pours a revi'it on them at once or immerses his hands in a manner that he need not wipe them:
