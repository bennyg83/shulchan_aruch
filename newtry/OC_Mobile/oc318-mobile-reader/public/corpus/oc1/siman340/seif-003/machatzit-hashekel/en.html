(א) (s.k. 3) To cut, etc. If it is moist; but if dry — clear in Eruvin daf 103 there is no d'oraisa prohibition with a utensil, for the Gemara challenges; and Maggid Mishneh even in dry there is d'rabbanan prohibition even by hand:

(ב) From the flayed hide — meaning shearing from the hide when it was flayed from the animal. And Taz wrote in siman 337: if a person plucks from human skin on himself it is like plucking from the ground; Or Sameach in siman 336 there is no manner of shearing thus and no manner of beating thus and no manner of spinning thus, as it should read; and so Rambam; and Maggid Mishneh wrote this is from what is stated in tractate Shabbos daf 74b: R' Yehuda said one who spins wool on an animal on Shabbat is liable for three chataos — for shearing, beating, and spinning; R' Kahana said there is no manner of spinning thus and no manner of beating thus and no manner of shearing thus; and he ruled like Rav Kahana:

(ג) And it implies one who plucks, etc. — from that he mentions regarding an animal shearing and did not mention also plucking as he wrote regarding a bird — it implies regarding an animal exempt for plucking; and on this see siman 498 who wrote in name of Rosh that nevertheless liable for plucking from an animal because of uprooting something from its growth.
