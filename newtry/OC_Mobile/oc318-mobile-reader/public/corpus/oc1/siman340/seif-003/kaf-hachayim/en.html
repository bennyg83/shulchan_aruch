C) One who shears wool or hair whether from domesticated or wild animals, whether from living or dead, even from shalach — liable; and likewise one who plucks a wing from a bird (whether living or dead — Chayei Adam general rule 21 note 2) — toldah of gozez; but one who spins wool from living exempt, for no manner of shearing thus. Rambam chapter 9 law 7; and implies plucking wool from animal exempt, for no manner of shearing thus. Magen Avraham s.k. 3; and likewise R' Zalman note 1; however Eshel Avraham note 4 distinguishes that plucking from living animal exempt because its manner is shearing; but plucking from dead liable; and likewise Tosafos Shabbos note 5, and it is according to what Taz wrote end of siman 336 in name of Yerushalmi — see there; however already we wrote there note 37 that Yesh Einayim proved from our Gemara and Rambam chapter 9 not like Yerushalmi and there is no chatat liability even plucking from dead, only rabbinic prohibition — see there; and likewise Shevut Shalom in this siman — see there; however some say plucking from animal liable on account of uprooting something from its growth as Beit Yosef wrote siman 398 and Magen Avraham there s.k. 22 and 23 — see there; and likewise Eshel Avraham in this siman note 3 — see there; but Chayei Adam general rule 21 note 1 wrote no manner of harvesting on hide except specifically with utensil — see there; and likewise Shevut Shalom in name of Chiddushei Rashba — see there; and all this when needing wool or hair; but if not needing wool or hair — melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah. Magen Avraham s.k. 3.
