(ב) Forbidden to break, etc. And permitted to give to a child (Mordechai); and I am accustomed to write in honey mixed with a little water, for it is fruit juice and is not erasing (Agudah); and requires study, for in any case his mark is recognizable:

(ג) Like letter forms. Requires study — for if so, even if forms are written on it it should be forbidden, for one who forms a shape is liable — and if so one who erases is also liable; requires study. And Rama wrote in responsum siman 119: a sefer on whose pages letters are written — permitted to open and close it, for since it is made to close and open there is no concern of erasing; Or Sameach elaborated; and Levush wrote it is close to chatas liability; and as above to be stringent — Rama's proofs are not compelling; and nevertheless wrote that the practice is to permit; and so R' Shalom Levi siman 57; and wrote see there that specifically when they write on cakes letters from another substance — but when the writing is from the cake itself in a mold or by hand it is permitted; and therefore they eat cakes that have designs on them, end of his words; requires study:
