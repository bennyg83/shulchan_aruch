(ב) And liable — see in Mishna Berurah what we wrote regarding measure. And behold even though regarding grinding Rambam in chapter 8 law 15 holds one who saws wood to enjoy the sawdust — even though toldah of grinding and grinding's measure is like a dried fig — nevertheless here liability is for any amount, for there is no necessity from the av to the toldah regarding measure there, for sawdust's measure is considered for any amount; and likewise scraping metal whose metal is considered for any amount — see there in Rambam and Maggid Mishnah; unlike regarding gluing hide which is like sewing — we find no distinction in sewing between hide garment and other garment; and in all we require measure for liability; and the same regarding gluing. And regarding paper it appears from Shulchan Aruch's language likewise one law for them; but this requires study regarding separating, for his liability is for tearing; and even though not in order to sew, since his intent is for some need and not to spoil — considered like in order to sew, as below; or tearing in anger [according to Rambam who obligates] — whether measure of two stitches applies or perhaps when we say in tearing this measure — specifically where liability is because intent was in order to sew; therefore in less than this measure not considered; unlike where liability is because intent is for some other fixing, such as in our matter where paper and hide must be separated, or tearing in anger to settle his mind as Rambam wrote, or any tearing in order to fix — whenever there is the fixing he intends he is liable and does not depend on this measure. And know in tearing for a dead person we say in the Gemara liable because there is mitzva fixing through the tearing — if so when there is tefach measure, for less than this not measure of tearing even for other mourners as written in Moed Katan and he did not fix at all — behold it does not depend on two stitches' measure; if so possible the same in every matter according to its fixing, whether to lenient or stringent [and our words are according to Tosafos there 105b s.v. ha r' y — see there; but according to Rambam there chapter 10 law 10 and on this mishnah his reason is his heat and pain are soothed through the tear — not clear there too if specifically tefach]; and behold what is in the mishnah spoiling in order to fix — its measure is like the fixing, meaning in a matter that has known fixing measure like erasing in order to write and tearing in order to sew and demolishing in order to build — each according to its measure, as in Rambam chapter 1 law 18 — see there; and if so likewise tearing in order to fix some fixing — measure depends on what is needed and does not depend on two stitches — requires study:

(ג) Only — all this is Rambam's language chapter 10 of Hilchot Shabbat law 10; and his view is clearly implied that what the Gemara requires tearing in order to sew is because otherwise it would be spoiling; but if he intends for some fixing benefit, even intending not in order to sew — liable; and with this it works that Rambam included opening the neck of a garment on Shabbat also in tearing laws for liability, even though certainly not in order to sew, since he fixes the garment through his tearing; and wonder at Beit Yosef who wrote in siman 317 on the obvious matter that opening the neck of a garment for this reason is not included in tearing; and he himself ruled in siman 340 like Rambam that all tearing in order to fix is liable; and in truth proven explicitly from the Gemara (Shabbos 105b) regarding tearing in anger and over a dead person lying on him [those mentioned in the parashah: his father and mother, his son, etc., of whom some may not be sewn afterward] that even not in order to sew liable whenever there is some fixing in the tearing. And afterward I found this proof brought in Gra's gloss and also all the above matters are essentially explained in Neshmat Adam general rule 29 in laws of Shabbat — see there; and we will speak on this below; and Peri Megadim hinted to this [but what Gra wrote from Tosafos 93b s.v. vetzarikh it appears we require specifically in order to sew — can be rejected that Tosafos' language is loose and their intent is when he does not need the wood there is no harvesting concern for liability, for he makes no fixing in the cut thing itself; and similar to tearing where we require in order to sew or fix — it is similar to sewing; otherwise no liability, for he spoils and it is not included in melacha; the same here; and know Rambam certainly holds we do not require specifically in order to sew, and he also holds melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah liable, and nevertheless rules in pruning that specifically when he needs the wood then liable for harvesting; and therefore what Tosafos wrote in other houses — lest he forget, since there is no opposing concern — meaning no agitation in other houses and certainly they do not permit muktzeh — can be said also according to Tosafos; and afterward they strengthened their view much from that cut branches have no harvesting at all, and likewise there is no threshing at all even to forbid l'chatchila; and also daf 94a Tosafos wrote only in spoilings such as extinguishing and demolishing it is reasonable to require in order to fix like in the Mishkan, etc. — see there and they did not mention in order to sew at all; and therefore as we wrote on account of fix is like in order to sew; and likewise below daf 73 Rashi wrote tearing not in order to sew was not in the Mishkan — also deals when there was no intent to fix, for this is like in order to sew; we do not require specifically as in the Mishkan itself; see daf 75 end of their words; and compelling proof for all our words from tearing over a dead person or in anger; and as above]. And I saw in Neshmat Adam he wrote the law of separating papers and likewise all tearing in order to fix — specifically if we hold melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah liable; but if we hold exempt — requires specifically tearing in order to sew literally; and because of this he wondered at Shulchan Aruch who brought Rambam's law anonymously, for behold in siman 316 and siman 334 he sided more with those who exempt; and his words are not clear to me at all, for needless to say according to Tosafos there regarding tearing over his dead that where there is mitzva fixing it is called melacha needed for its purpose since he needs it — the same in all tearing in order to fix; and even according to Rashi and supporters who hold even tearing over his dead exempt because melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah — meaning they hold need of mitzva is not considered fixing enough to be called melacha needed for its purpose; but in all fixing matters needed in themselves, such as in separating papers where he needs paper thin — for all melacha needed for its purpose like tearing in order to sew; and what we exempt there, in the Gemara regarding tearing in anger for one who exempts melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah — because he fixes nothing in the garment body through his tearing, even when he frightens household members, as Tosafos wrote daf 94; and also Rashi's view is proven thus, for he wrote there tearing over his dead exempt because melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah — like taking out the dead for burial [meaning willingly it did not come to him and he did not need this whole matter of tearing in anger and over his dead]; and he did not write plainly the reason because it is not in order to sew; rather certainly even not in order to sew liable even according to Rashi where the fixing itself he wants the tearing for. And likewise implied from Me'or's words in chapter R' Eliezer Omer at the end of his words who wrote what R' Shimon exempts even tearing to frighten household members — because the need is not in the melacha itself — see there implies if he needed the tearing itself, even not in order to sew liable; and likewise Rabbeinu Yerucham part 12 ruled explicitly like R' Shimon and nevertheless part 14 one who tears over a dead person whom he is obligated to mourn — liable; and like Tosafos' view behold tearing in order to fix liable; and likewise implied in Meiri chapter HaOreg explicitly tearing in order to fix even not in order to sew liable — see there. The conclusion of our words: whether according to Rambam or Rashi and Tosafos and all the aforementioned poskim — tearing in order to fix liable, like in order to sew; and even according to R' Shimon who exempts in melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah liable here; except according to Tosafos we require for R' Shimon fixing in the body of the torn thing itself — see there daf 94a; and Ritva's view brought in many places chapter 10 of Hilchot Shabbat who holds we require tearing in order to sew specifically is solitary; and also the sugya of HaOreg is against him, as Gra challenged; and also Beit Yosef, though he too wanted to say thus in siman 317 — behold Shulchan Aruch here returned and ruled anonymously tearing in order to fix liable even not in order to sew; and likewise proven in Perach Yoreh De'ah siman 118 tearing in order to fix liable for all; and in vain one challenged him in Neshmat Adam general rule 29 on this. And nothing remains except to explain Rashi's view daf 48 who wrote opening the neck of a garment on Shabbat — his liability is striking the final hammer-blow because he makes it a utensil; and why not write on account of tearing in order to fix; and it appears his view is tearing in order to fix applies only when he spoils at the time of the action but intends for some fixing similar to the Mishkan where a curtain had a tear they tore and sewed — the tearing spoils the curtain more, only thereby they can afterward sew it; and similarly what is said there Shabbos 104 regarding servant like pouch — benefit for whole garment; and when he tears something from the side of the garment intending to fix the garment that was long or defective at its edge — the torn piece is not fixed — tearing in order to fix does not apply; unlike opening the neck of a garment on Shabbat where he makes a utensil through the opening itself — tearing name does not apply at all and liable on another name, striking the final hammer-blow, which applies in every melacha completion; and likewise proven there, in the Gemara in a baraita he makes it a utensil in one category with not placing stuffing into a cushion, and as striking the final hammer-blow applies here; and also Rambam was concerned with this except he follows his view in chapter 22 law 23 — see there; this is my humble opinion on Rashi's reason and other poskim who wrote on account of striking the final hammer-blow; and Shulchan Aruch ruled anonymously in siman 317 seif 3; and there is no contradiction with this seif; and see Magen Avraham siman 317 s.k. 10 who rejected Eshel Avraham's words who wrote opening the neck of a garment — his liability is tearing; and according to what we clarified the law with Magen Avraham regarding opening the neck of a garment, but not in all tearing matters, as Mechaber ruled anonymously here that in all tearing in order to fix liable:
