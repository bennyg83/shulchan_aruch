(א) This is a toldah of sewing — and it is reasonable its measure is also like in sewing, meaning the measure of two stitches for liability; and see in Biur Halacha:

(ב) Only — except for some benefit, even if he did not intend thereby in order to adhere them more — nevertheless liable for tearing, for we do not require in tearing specifically in order to sew, but for some fixing so it not be spoiling. And if he intends only to spoil — exempt and forbidden l'chatchila like all spoilings of Shabbat:

(ג) This is a toldah of tearing — and all the more tearing a garment at the place of stitching for some benefit — liable [Tosafos Shabbos]:

(ד) And liable — and specifically in this where the adhesion was made for permanence; but where the pages adhered to one another through wax or at the time of binding — permitted to open them as is written siman 314 seif 10, since it was not made for permanence; all the more here where it happened without intent — therefore not at all comparable to sewing and there is no tearing concern [Magen Avraham]; and if they adhered in the place of the letters — forbidden to separate them; and see above in seif 3 in Biur Halacha there. And to tear new pages of books that were not cut from while still day — there is liability, and even through a non-Jew forbidden [Peri Megadim; and see in Biur Halacha]. One whose garments became entangled in thorns — he separates them privately and gently so they not tear; and if they tear — they tear; and likewise permitted to wear new garments, and if they tear — they tear, for he does not intend tearing. And one cracks nuts in a cloth [meaning he strikes on the cloth in order to break them] and is not concerned lest it tear [Rambam chapter 22]:
