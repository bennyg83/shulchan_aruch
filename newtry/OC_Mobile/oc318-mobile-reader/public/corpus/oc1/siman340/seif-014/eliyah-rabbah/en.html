[14] [Levush] That opened, etc. — Shabbos daf 75: Rav said one who stretches a sewing thread is liable; and a student, etc.; and one who knows to calculate seasons and constellations and does not calculate — forbidden to speak with him; Rashi explained sewn garment standing and he left thread and they separated and he stretches, etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote from Rambam it appears one who stretches thread he wishes to sew with so it not stuck is liable, for this is a need of sewing; end; and Olat Shabbat wondered at Shulchan Aruch who omitted — end; and in my humble opinion Beit Yosef missed Maggid Mishnah's words that it is clear there Rambam also rules [like Rashi] except they divide in reason: for Rashi reason is actual sewing and for Rambam reason is need of sewing; and therefore Shulchan Aruch omitted; and likewise appears to me for practical law one should not obligate stretching thread one wishes to sew with, for I saw Semag, Kolbo, Rabbeinu Yerucham, and all authors ruled like Rashi's law of sewn garment and not one omitted to bring Rambam's law except clear Rambam also explained like Rashi, only they divide in reason; and when I preached here Prague this aforementioned Gemara three teachings to one another at length with good taste by way of derash I said on Rambam who argues on Rashi's reason, for great difficulty in Gemara [Shabbos daf 73] on the arranger who arranged this law in tearing in order to sew and did not arrange it before in sewing law — therefore I explained Rambam this is not actual sewing but need of sewing like tearing in order to sew; and for aforementioned Rashi requires study on the difficulty mentioned above. And incidentally I mention the reason I said in the aforementioned derash: at first glance wondrous why forbidden to speak with one who does not calculate seasons — what measure-for-measure is this; rather it appears to me Avodah Zarah daf 35: Rava said do not speak with Ivo who eats gentile bread; and explained in long Tur HaBayit page 88 they should not relate tradition from him; Perishah explained: because Sages with their wisdom made not to eat gentile bread and he does not observe — therefore unfit to be considered sage to say tradition from him — end; and if so it appears to me forbidden to speak with him also to expound tradition from him, for calculating seasons' reason is to be considered wise and understanding as stated in Gemara there; and if so since he does not calculate and does not wish to be considered wise — measure for measure:
