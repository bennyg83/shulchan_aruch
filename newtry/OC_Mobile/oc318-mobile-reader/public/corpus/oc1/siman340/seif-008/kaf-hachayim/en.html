Specifically two hairs; but one hair even prohibition does not exist — requires study for practical law; see there note 1; but Magen Avraham s.k. 2 disagreed and wrote in one there is prohibition like every half-measure — not like Eliyah Rabbah; and likewise Eshel Avraham note 2, Tosafos Shabbos note 3, R' Zalman note 1, Birkei Yosef note 1; however see Eshel Avraham note 2 where he brought dispute whether half-measure forbidden d'oraisa or only in eating prohibitions and all prohibitions; and again he brought from Mahari Weil in Hagahot Eretz Rabbanan note 41 who expanded to prove from Rambam's words half-measure not forbidden d'oraisa except in eating prohibition and similar — see there; and all this cutting one hair with utensil; but if he cuts one hair by hand or teeth — certainly only rabbinic prohibition, since even many are only rabbinic prohibition.
