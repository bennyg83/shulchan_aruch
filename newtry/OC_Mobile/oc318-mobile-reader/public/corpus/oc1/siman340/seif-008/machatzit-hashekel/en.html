(s.k. 8) Permitted, etc. to watch craftsmanship on Shabbat, etc. And so explicitly in the Gemara 103a: R' Shabbtai said one who strikes with a hammer on an anvil liable; and they challenge what did he do? Rabbah and R' Yehuda both said because he trains his hand — the students of Rachbah challenged: if so, watch craftsmanship on Shabbat and learn — here too liable?
