(א) (s.k. 7) In liquids, etc. One who writes on his flesh, etc. — similar to writing that was erased — meaning what he writes on his flesh writes on something that endures from its own aspect, for he writes on skin; even though something else — namely the heat — causes it not to endure — nevertheless it is called writing on something enduring immediately, as writing on actual skin that in most days through sleeping it will be erased — and nevertheless liable:

(ב) Sap over ink exempt — for it is spoiling. Only over the writing. And behold in writing there is no spoiling and it is as its repair:

(ג) Chet and wrote two zayins — wrote two letters, etc., as it should read; and the word liable after the word two zayins is a printing error — such exempt; and so Rambam; see there. One who removes the roof of the chet and they become two zayins — should read he first wrote two zayins and wrote tagin on them as required for shin ayin tet nun zayin gimmel tzadi that require tagin; and afterward he erred and wrote on it a crown and it became a chet — if so when he removes the crowns two zayins with tagin remain as required — therefore liable. But if he first wrote two zayins without tagin as required for two zayins of a chet — even though afterward he removed the crown and two zayins remain — nevertheless exempt, for the tagin are lacking, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 10:
