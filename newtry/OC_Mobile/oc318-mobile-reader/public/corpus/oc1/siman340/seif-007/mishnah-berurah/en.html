(א) Those who tighten — their manner was to put a thread inside the sleeve on the arm, for when he wishes to tighten it he would stretch the thread and it tightens well:

(ב) Wide somewhat and adjusted — for then it is not at all comparable to sewing, for it is like one who inserts eyelets in loops since it is permanently adjusted to stretch and tighten when wearing; and likewise to repair when he wishes to remove it; and if he does not tighten it well and sometimes decides to leave it thus forever and remove it when tightened — forbidden to tighten on Shabbat:

(ג) In the stitching — around the hole; and then even if the thread is inserted in a perforated needle hanging there permanently in order to insert it by hand in the holes — permitted, and there is no concern of moving, since the needle hangs on the cord and the cord on the garment and never separates from it:
