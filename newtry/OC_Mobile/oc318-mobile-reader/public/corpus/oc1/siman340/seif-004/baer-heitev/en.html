(א) In liquids. And if he wrote — exempt; and the same for one who writes in ink on vegetable leaves and on anything that does not endure — exempt until he writes something enduring on something enduring; and likewise one who erases. One who writes on his flesh is liable. But one who scratches on his flesh the form of writing — exempt. One who tears on skin in the form of writing — liable. One who marks on skin in the form of writing — exempt. One who passes ink over sap — liable for two, for writing and for erasing. Ink over ink or sap over ink — exempt. One who marks marks and shapes on a wall and on a document and the like in the manner painters mark — this one is liable for writing; and likewise one who erases. One who rules lines — liable. One who writes in order to ruin the skin — liable, for his liability is not on the place of writing but on the writing. One who erases in order to ruin — exempt. He wrote one letter adjacent to writing — writing over writing. He intended to write a chet and wrote two zayins — (liable). He wrote two letters that are not used together — exempt. He wrote one letter in this scroll and one letter in another scroll — liable, for when they are brought together they are used together and are not lacking an act to bring them together. He wrote an acronym of one letter — exempt. One who corrects one letter — for example removes the roof of the chet and it becomes two zayins — liable. He wrote with his left or an ambidextrous person who wrote with his right — exempt. One who is skilled with both hands — liable with both. A minor holds the quill and an adult holds his hand and writes — liable. An adult holds the quill and a minor holds his hand and writes — exempt. All this Magen Avraham brought:

(ב) In ash. And the same in honey and congealed fat:

(ג) To mark in air. For his mark is not recognizable as in liquids and ash. And accordingly it is not specifically in air that is permitted — likewise if one marks letter forms on a dry page it is permitted, for also there his mark is not recognizable at all; and so implied from what is written in liquids on the table, etc. — it implies on a bare table it is permitted.
