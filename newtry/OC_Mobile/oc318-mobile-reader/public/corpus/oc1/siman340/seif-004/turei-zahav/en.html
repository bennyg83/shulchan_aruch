Permitted to mark in air, etc. The reason in Terumat HaDeshen is that his mark is not recognizable as in liquids and ash. And accordingly it is not specifically in air that is permitted — likewise if one marks letter forms on a dry page it is permitted, for also there his mark is not recognizable at all; and so Terumat HaDeshen in name of Or Zarua wrote one must be careful not to write on Shabbat in liquids on the table, etc. — it implies on a bare table it is permitted:
