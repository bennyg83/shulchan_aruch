[4] Whether with a utensil, etc. — Maggid Mishnah chapter 9 wondered that end of Eruvin implies moist and with utensil liable; and Olat Shabbat answered Rambam holds melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah exempt — end; and one can wonder if so why does he obligate earlier there; and further wonder that end of siman 328 Tur and he himself and likewise poskim Rambam obligates in melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah. And in my humble opinion to answer Maggid Mishnah's wonder Rambam deals dry and moist he relied on what he wrote afterward that forbidden to cut in the Temple a utensil — perforce liability, for there is no shevut in the Temple; and likewise implied in Rashi and Ran; and Magen Avraham did not address all this. Rambam wrote: one who shears wool or hair from domesticated or wild animals whether from living or dead, even from their shalach, liable; and measure close to two-thirds of a zeret; and explained shalach meaning flayed hides whose wool was removed and not yet worked — end. And Taz end of siman 336 wrote one must beware in a garment made from animal hide not to pluck hair from it, for there is chatat liability, as in Yerushalmi one who plucks from a dead animal is liable, for its plucking is its shearing — end; but Magen Avraham wrote plucking wool from an animal exempt — see siman 398; and if he does not need wool or hair it is melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah.
