(א) To stretch it — meaning two pieces of the garment separated from one another in part and the threads of the stitches extended and he wishes to stretch the head of the thread in order to tighten and join:

(ב) On account of sewing — and one who does so is liable to a chatat [Gemara]; and this is when he made a knot at the end, even if not permanent; but without any knot — exempt; and if three stitches separated and he stretched them — liable even without a knot [Acharonim]. And incidentally we explain some law of sewing. One who sews two stitches is liable when he tied the thread ends on both sides so it not slip out, even if not permanent; and if permanent — liable also for tying; and all this regarding liability; but regarding prohibition some say forbidden even if he made no knot at all; and accordingly those people who insert the crease with the garment in the needle in two passes — not well done [Korban Netanel]; and if he made three stitches — liable even without knot, for the sewing endures [Rambam]:
