Seif katan 3, "And they give," etc. Therefore it is sufficient when they give the wine to the infant receiving brit milah. It is forbidden to give it to other children lest they become accustomed to drinking on a fast day. "Although when a brit milah occurs on Tisha B'Av, siman 559, seif 7, states that it is given to children." Since a brit milah does not occur on Tisha B'Av every year, we are not concerned that the children will become habituated. Yom Kippur, however, is Torah-level, and Rama therefore treated it more stringently than Tisha B'Av, as Magen Avraham writes above in siman 559. "Levush wrote that since Minhagim states that they give it to children." Meaning to other children as well, not only to the infant receiving brit milah as Rama writes. Levush thus equates Yom Kippur with Tisha B'Av. "Nevertheless, one must say, besides," etc. This refers to Rama's statement that it should be given to the infant receiving brit milah.
