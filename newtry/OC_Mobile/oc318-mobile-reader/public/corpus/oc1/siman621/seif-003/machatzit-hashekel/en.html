(Se'if katan 3) And they give, etc. Therefore it is sufficient when they give it to the infant, etc. But to other children it is forbidden, lest they come to be drawn after it.
And although if a milah falls on Tishah B'Av, he wrote in siman 559, se'if 7, that they give it to children. Since it is not fixed every year that there will be a milah on Tishah B'Av, we are not concerned lest they come to be drawn after it. Nevertheless, Yom Kippur, which is according to the Torah, the Rema treated more stringently than Tishah B'Av. So wrote the Magen Avraham above, siman 559.
And the Levush wrote: since it is written in the Minhagim that they give it to children. Meaning, to other children, and not only to the infant being circumcised, as the Rema wrote. He equates Yom Kippur with Tishah B'Av.
Nevertheless, one must say, besides, etc. This refers to the words of the Rema, who wrote that it must be given to the circumcised infant.
