Se'if 3, One recites the blessing, etc. As stated in chapter Arvei Pesachim and in chapter 8 of Berachos: the one who recites a blessing must taste. We do not give it to a child, as stated at the end of chapter 3 of Eruvin. Tosafos disagree and say that regarding something not fixed, we are not concerned for this. A difficulty was raised against them from chapter Tolin, and the Ran resolved it, saying that perhaps he will sow every year. See above, siman 559, se'if 7, and Yoreh De'ah, siman 265, se'if 4. See Tosafos, Shabbos 159a, s.v. 'veliten,' etc., and Eruvin 40b, s.v. 'dilma,' etc.
And some say, etc. See the Magen Avraham: since, etc. The Ra'avan wrote there that therefore we give it to a child all year because of fast days; see there. He wrote that with such a small child there is no concern.
