We bless the circumcision without a cup (of wine). 178 A cup of wine is used in the circumcision ceremony, but because it is Yom Kippur there was some question as to whether this was permitted or not. (There are those who say that we bless (the circumcision) with a cup (of wine) and we give it to the infant who is 179 circumcized, and this is the custom. 179 Children, and therefore certainly infants, are not subject to the laws of fasting on Yom Kippur. The custom, therefore developed that a cup of wine was used in the circumcision ceremony on Yom Kippur. The infant is usually given a small piece of cloth or cotton to suck on which has been dipped in wine. This sanctifies the baby's circumcision according to the Jewish law. )
