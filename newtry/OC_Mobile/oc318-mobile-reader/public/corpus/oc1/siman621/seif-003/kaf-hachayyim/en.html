Regarding anyone who grieves over the loss of the righteous or sheds tears over them, the Holy One, blessed is He, proclaims concerning him and says, "Your iniquity has departed and your sin will be atoned." Moreover, his children will not die during his lifetime, and concerning him it is written, "He shall see offspring and prolong his days." Zohar there, 57b. The Magen Avraham cited his words at the beginning of the siman. The essential point in this is that through it he should take to heart the need to repent from the sins in his possession, and the living should take it to heart: If a flame has seized the cedars, what shall the hyssops of the wall do? Mateh Ephraim, siman 619, sec. 51; Mishna Berurah, sec. 2.
