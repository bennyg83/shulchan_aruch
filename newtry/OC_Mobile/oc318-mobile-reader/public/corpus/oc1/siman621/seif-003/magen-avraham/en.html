"And they give it to the infant receiving brit milah." The tasting of the cup is required only because it is unbecoming for people to say that this blessing was recited for no purpose. It is therefore sufficient to give some of it to such a small infant. [Raavan, chapter 3 of Eruvin] Responsa Ran, siman 52, however, writes in the name of Rashba that this remains a blessing in vain because the wine is given to such a young child. Levush writes that since Minhagim says simply that it is given to children, one should not protest against those who follow the lenient custom. Nevertheless, it appears to me that besides placing some wine in the infant's mouth when saying "Through your blood, live," one should give him a little wine to drink from the cup itself. Otherwise there still remains a lack of dignity toward the cup. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 265, end of seif 1 and end of seif 4.
