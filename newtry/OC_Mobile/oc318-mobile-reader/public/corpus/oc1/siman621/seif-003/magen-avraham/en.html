And they give it to the circumcised infant. Since tasting from the cup is only because of disgrace, so that people should not say that this blessing is unnecessary, it is sufficient when they give it to such a small child (Ra'avan, chapter 3 of Eruvin). In Responsa Ran, siman 52, he wrote in the name of the Rashba that it is a blessing in vain, since he gives it to such a small child. In the Levush it is written: Since it is written in the Minhagim, 'and they give it to children,' one should not protest against those who practice leniently. End quote. Nevertheless, it seems to me that besides what they place in the infant's mouth when saying 'Through your blood you shall live,' one must give him a little to drink from the cup; otherwise, there is still disgrace to the cup. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 265, end of se'if 1 and end of se'if 4.
