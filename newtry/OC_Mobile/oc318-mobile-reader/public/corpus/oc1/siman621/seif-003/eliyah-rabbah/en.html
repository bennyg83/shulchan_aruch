[3] [Levush:] 'Yom LaYabashah,' etc. The Hagahos Maimoniyos is uncertain about this, and the custom is not to say it. The reason appears to be that it is like Hallel, which is not said. However, this is somewhat difficult, for why do we say it on Rosh Hashanah, at the end of siman 584? Also according to the Hagahos Maimoniyos it is obvious that it is said on Rosh Hashanah. Therefore it seems that the entire service of Yom Kippur overrides Shabbos, and there is no special elevation in the milah that occurs on it. According to the reason the Magen Avraham wrote at the end of siman 584, that on Shabbos there is not so much concern for adjoining redemption to prayer, it seems that the reason is because it is not a day of distress, as above in siman 111. But Yom Kippur, since we fast, is called somewhat of a day of distress, as explained in se'if 3.
