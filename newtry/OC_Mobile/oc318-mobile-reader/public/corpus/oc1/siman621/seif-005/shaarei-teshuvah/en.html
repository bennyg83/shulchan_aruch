Avodah. The Noda BiYehudah, Mahadura Tinyana, siman 127, wrote: What they omitted in Yom Kippur Mussaf, 'besides the sin-offering of atonement,' they did well, for the inner sin-offering is not from the Mussaf offerings, and its main place is in Parashas Acharei among the offerings of the day. Also, this text has no meaning in this place, since he already said 'and two daily offerings according to their law'; how can he then say afterward, 'besides the sin-offering of atonement and the continual burnt-offering,' etc.? Are there three daily offerings? Those who do say it should say only 'besides the sin-offering of atonement' and no more. Similarly in the Mussaf of Rosh Hashanah one should say only 'besides the burnt-offering of the month' and no more; but those who conclude 'the continual burnt-offering' are not correct, because they already said 'and two daily offerings according to their law,' unlike the Rema in siman 595. Later I reconsidered, for the Torah mentioned 'besides the sin-offering of atonement' together with the Mussaf offerings, and the Rambam in chapter 10 of Hilchos Temidin u'Musafin, etc., implies that the inner sin-offering of atonement is somewhat connected to the Mussaf offering. Therefore, were I not afraid to say so, I would say that on Yom Kippur too one should say 'and two goats to atone,' and then they should not conclude at all with 'besides the sin-offering of atonement.' One who does not want to say 'and two goats' should conclude only with these three words, 'besides the sin-offering of atonement,' and no more. See Dagul MeRevavah, who also wrote that one should say 'and two goats to atone' and should not say 'besides the sin-offering of atonement.' See siman 591, where I wrote in the name of the Dagul MeRevavah, and what I wrote there. See the Taz, siman 113, that those who beat their heart when saying that they sinned and transgressed act properly, because we imagine ourselves as though we are those members of Yisrael, and we regret, etc.
