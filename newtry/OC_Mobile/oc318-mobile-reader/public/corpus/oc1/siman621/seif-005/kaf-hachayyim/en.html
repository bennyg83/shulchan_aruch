5) It is obvious that if he did not complete the parashah with the final person called up, and that person recited the concluding blessing and Kaddish was said, it is unnecessary to return and read the remainder of the parashah, as Gever the Beit Din ruled in siman 60 concerning parashas Mishchu on the first day of Pesach. However, if he remembered before Kaddish, it is proper that another person be called up and read the remainder, since our custom is to add to the required number of those called up on Shabbosos and festivals. Shalmei Tzibbur, ibid.
