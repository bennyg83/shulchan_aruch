In the Zohar: Anyone who is distressed over the death of Aharon's sons, or sheds tears over them, has his sins forgiven and his children do not die in his lifetime. So too is written in the name of the Ari. In the haftarah one does not say 'King Who pardons and forgives,' but only 'and Your word is true and enduring forever. Blessed are You, Hashem, King over all the earth,' etc. (Levush, Darkei Moshe). Milah on Yom Kippur. We say 'Remember the covenant' and 'Do not annul,' etc. If the child was born on Shabbos, we say the redemption hymn 'Yom LaYabashah' (Levush). See what I wrote at the end of siman 584.
