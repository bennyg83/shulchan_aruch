The order of the reading of the Torah and of circumcision on Yom Kippur. It contains six se'ifim.
We take out two Torah scrolls; in the first, six [men] read in Parashas Acharei Mos, until "And he did as Hashem commanded"; and if it falls on Shabbos, seven read; and the maftir reads in the second [scroll], in [Parashas] Pinchas, "And on the tenth of the month"; and the Haftarah is in Yeshayah, "And he said: Pave, pave, clear the way," until "for the mouth of Hashem has spoken."
