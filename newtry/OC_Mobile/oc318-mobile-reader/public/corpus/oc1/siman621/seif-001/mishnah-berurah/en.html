(1) Six read - Because its sanctity is greater than that of other Yamim Tovim, for it is forbidden in all melachah; therefore we add one, but we do not add more.
(2) In the section of Acharei Mos - It is stated in the Zohar: Anyone who is distressed over the death of Aharon's sons, or sheds tears over them, has his sins forgiven and his children do not die in his lifetime. The main point in this is that through it he should take to heart to repent from the transgressions in his hand: if flame fell among the cedars, what shall the hyssop of the wall do? [Mateh Ephraim].
(3) Seven read - As on other Shabbosos. See the Magen Avraham, siman 282, that initially it is not proper to add beyond seven. Perhaps in a place where those called up give much money to charity, one who is lenient has not lost [Pri Megadim].
(4) And the maftir - In the haftarah one does not say 'King Who pardons and forgives,' but only 'and Your word is true and enduring forever. Blessed are You, Hashem,' etc. [Darkei Moshe and later authorities].
