At Musaf, the chazzan says the Order of the [Temple] Service [Seder Avodah]. {Rama: And the custom is to fall on their faces when they say "And the kohanim and the people," and also in "Aleinu Leshabe'ach"; but the chazzan is forbidden to move from his place during the prayer in order to fall on his face, and one should protest against those who do so.}
