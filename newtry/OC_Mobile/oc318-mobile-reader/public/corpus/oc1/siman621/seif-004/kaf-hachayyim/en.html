4) Ibid. From parashas Acharei Mos, etc. The shaliach tzibbur should be especially meticulous not to omit a word from the service of the day. If this was said regarding the other days, all the more so on this sacred day. L'David Emes in Toras HaShelamim, siman 13, sec. 1. It is possible that since the reason for the reading depends upon the service of the day, namely because of, "Let our lips substitute for bulls," if he omitted or altered somewhat in the description of the service, he must repeat it. However, it appears that the proper course is for the shaliach tzibbur to repeat aloud what he omitted, without a blessing. Shalmei Tzibbur, 274c. It appears that this applies specifically if they already completed the reading. If they have not yet completed it, they should reread with the second person called up whatever was omitted or altered.
