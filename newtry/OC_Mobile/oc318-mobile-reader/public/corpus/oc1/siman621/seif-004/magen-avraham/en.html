The order of the Avodah. See the Beis Yosef, who notes many exact points in their order. Our piyyutim follow the halachah, but in the selichos there are things that are not according to the halachah; examine and you will find. What the Olas Shabbos wrote regarding the lots, see Tosafos Yom Tov. We say 'Ana Hashem' and 'Ana vashem' because the Kohen Gadol pronounced the Name as written. But 'before Hashem you shall be purified' is a verse that we read, so we say the Name in its substitute pronunciation; likewise, 'for Hashem as a sin-offering.' See Rivash, siman 219, unlike the Bach and Kenesses HaGedolah, who wrote to say 'for HaShem as a sin-offering.' All the more so in 'May it be Your will,' etc., where even the Kohen Gadol would not pronounce the Name as written, and furthermore we say in it several requests for ourselves that the Kohen Gadol did not say. So is the custom, unlike the Bach.
