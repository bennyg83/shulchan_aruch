[4] [Levush:] 'Remember the covenant,' etc. That is, at Shacharis we say 'Remember the covenant' and 'and I will remember the land,' but we do not say 'Do not annul' at all. The Kitzur Shelah wrote: It is forbidden to do metzitzah on Yom Kippur with wine or any liquid; rather, he should suction with the mouth without liquid.
