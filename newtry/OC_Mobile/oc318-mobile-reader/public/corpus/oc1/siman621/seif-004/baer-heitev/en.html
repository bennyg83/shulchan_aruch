Avodah. For the main service of the Kohen Gadol was in Mussaf; see the Taz and Magen Avraham.
To uproot. The custom has already spread that the shaliach tzibbur also falls on his face. Levush. But he should not uproot his feet; rather, he should stand somewhat distant from the lectern at the beginning, and so is the custom. See siman 131, that one should spread grasses on the floor.
