(13) In Mussaf, etc. - For the main service of the Kohen Gadol was in Mussaf, and therefore we also say it in order to fulfill "and let our lips substitute for bulls."
(14) To fall on their faces - See siman 131, that one should spread grasses on the floor because it is forbidden to spread one's face on the floor, or he should interpose with his tallit.
(15) And one should protest - For we learned: even if a snake is wrapped around his heel, he should not interrupt.
(16) Against those who do so - But the practice has already spread that the shaliach tzibbur also does so, and it appears that they rely on the fact that walking is not called an interruption [Taz]. Now the practice has spread that they place another shtender before him, and during the service they remove that shtender, and he does not move his feet [Peri Megadim].
