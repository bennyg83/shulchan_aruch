A circumcision on Yom Kippur — we circumcise between Shacharis and Musaf, after the reading of the Torah; and after the circumcision we say "Ashrei" {and the custom is to circumcise after "Ashrei" (Minhagim)}. And if it is in a place where one needs to leave the synagogue [for the circumcision], we do not circumcise until after the returning of the Torah scroll, and we return and say Kaddish.
