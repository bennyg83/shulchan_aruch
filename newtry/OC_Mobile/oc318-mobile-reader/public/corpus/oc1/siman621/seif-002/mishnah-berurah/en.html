(5) Milah - And they say 'Remember the covenant.' If it falls on Shabbos, they say 'Yom LaYabashah.'
(6) Between Yotzer and Mussaf, etc. - Because the Shacharis prayer comes first, since it is frequent, unlike Mussaf.
(7) After Ashrei - Some have the custom to circumcise before Ashrei [Magen Avraham]. See Eliyah Rabbah, siman 591, who also agreed that this is correct to do, for several reasons; see there.
(8) In a place where one must leave - Such as where there is no eruv and they cannot bring the infant to the synagogue.
(9) Until after returning the Torah scroll - To its place, so that it should not be a disgrace to leave the Torah scroll and go outside.
(10) And they return and say Kaddish - This is correct according to the Mechaber, that the milah is before Ashrei; if so, when they return they will say Ashrei and Kaddish, and it is known that this Kaddish refers to Ashrei. But according to the Rema, that the milah is after Ashrei, there is a large interruption between Ashrei and Kaddish through going outside. However, this speaks of a place where they say Yehi Ratzon before Mussaf, which contains several verses, and the Kaddish will refer to them. In places where they do not say the Yehi Ratzon, one should say some chapter of Tehillim before saying Kaddish.
