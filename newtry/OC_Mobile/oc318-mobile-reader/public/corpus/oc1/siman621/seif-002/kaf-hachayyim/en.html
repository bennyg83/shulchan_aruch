In the holy Zohar, parashas Acharei Mos, 56b, it is stated further: We read the passage concerning the sons of Aharon on Yom Kippur so that it should serve as atonement for the sins of Israel. The Holy One, blessed is He, said: Occupy yourselves with the deaths of these righteous men, and it will be considered for you as though you offered sacrifices on this day to atone for yourselves, etc., see there.
