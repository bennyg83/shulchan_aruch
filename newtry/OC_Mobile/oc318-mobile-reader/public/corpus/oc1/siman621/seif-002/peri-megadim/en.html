In the Tur and Beis Yosef, the first is 'HaShem' with a hei, and the second is 'vashem' with a beis, referring to the essential Name, Havayah, blessed be He and blessed be His Name, complete mercy. It appears that 'HaShem' has the hei of calling, meaning that he calls to Hashem at first; and in the second, 'vashem,' it appears that the beis is open, like 'to guide them,' referring to the known Name, the essential Name, yud-hei-vav-hei, blessed be He and blessed be His Name, complete mercy. See Tosafos Yom Tov, Yoma chapter 6, mishnah 2, 'Ana Hashem' and s.v. 'when they would'; see there. See Eliyah Rabbah. What was written regarding 'Ana Hashem' and that we do not read it as Adonai is because the Kohen Gadol would say it as written, and we are not permitted to do so. See Beis Yosef and Bach on this. But 'before Hashem you shall be purified,' although the Kohen Gadol said it with the explicit Name, since it is a verse we say it as Adonai. So too in Yotzer, 'Holy, holy, holy, Hashem Tzevakos,' we say Adonai, although it is a narration of what the angels say, and presumably they too say it as written; since it is a verse, we say Adonai. See Even HaEzer regarding the marriage blessings, that wherever there is a doubt about a blessing, one should say 'Blessed are You, HaShem'; see there.
