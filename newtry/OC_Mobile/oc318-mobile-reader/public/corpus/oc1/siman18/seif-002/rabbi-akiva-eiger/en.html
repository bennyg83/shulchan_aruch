{Rama: Magen Avraham s.k. 4.} That the same applies in the evening until the stars emerge. It is difficult: amud ha-shachar is day by law, unlike bein ha-shemashos which is doubtful night — how can one bless from doubt? And perhaps there are two doubts: doubt of day versus night, and doubt whether a day garment is obligated at night — unclear for practical law. Perhaps the intention of Magen Avraham is since Mordechai's view is that everything depends on sight and not on day; and similarly Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Maharal that until tzeit ha-kokhavim is time for tzitzis, since everything depends on sight.
