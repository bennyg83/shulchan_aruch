“Sheets,” etc. It is written in Hagahos Maimoniyos that according to the Rambam, who holds a night garment [worn] by day is obligated, therefore sheets are obligated in tzitzis—and Rav Eliyahu used to make tzitzis for them—end quote. The plain practice is not to attach tzitzis to sheets, whether of linen or wool—end Beis Yosef—and I do not know the reason wool ones should be exempt. Sefer Yereim also writes they are obligated. Mordechai writes that Sifrei [says] “a garment except a sheet.” It appears to me that nothing is obligated in tzitzis except what is normally worn as clothing; “that you may cover yourself with it” also implies a manner of wearing as a garment; even a manner of wearing that would obligate kilayim, the inclination is to exempt, for [the verse] is compared only to wearing. Pillows and cushions too Sifrei implies are excluded from “that you may cover yourself with it,” and Rabbi did not agree with him—see there in Tosafos—end quote. According to them one must say the dispute of Beis Shammai and Beis Hillel about a sheet for tzitzis is when one wraps in it as R. Yehoshua ben Ilai did. Tosafos, page 41, wrote that for tzitzis one does not distinguish between spreading out and wearing. It appears to me that with wool sheets one should make one corner round to exempt from tzitzis according to all opinions.
