(Seif katan 2) And on the night, etc. In order to resemble angels. The prosecuting angel says before Hashem: “You have a nation on earth like the ministering angels, wearing white and wrapped in white.” Thus, the prosecuting angel mentions wrapping as well.
Levush writes in siman 581 that one should not recite the Thirteen Attributes, etc. The Gemara in Rosh Hashanah states: Rabbi Yochanan said, etc., this teaches that HaKadosh Baruch Hu wrapped Himself like a prayer leader when He taught the Thirteen Attributes. This implies that a prayer leader should wrap himself when reciting the Thirteen Attributes.
It appears to me that every, etc. As the Gemara says, “This teaches, etc.” Since it says “like a prayer leader,” it implies that whenever a prayer leader goes before the amud, he must wrap himself. If the intention were only “like a prayer leader when reciting the Thirteen Attributes,” meaning in accordance with an already established custom for prayer leaders to wrap themselves, that custom did not yet exist, because the Thirteen Attributes had not yet been taught. It is strained to say that the Gemara speaks in anticipation of the later practice, meaning like a prayer leader who now recites the Thirteen Attributes. Moreover, it should then have stated explicitly, “like a prayer leader when he recites the Thirteen Attributes.” Lechem Chamudos writes similarly at the beginning of Hilchos Tzitzis.
