(Note 2) And at night, etc. — to resemble angels. Since the Torah mentions before Him: You have a nation in the land like angels wearing white and wrapped in white — therefore the Torah also mentions wrapping.

And Levush wrote siman 581, in the comment beginning "Thirteen Attributes," etc.: for we say in Rosh Hashana that Rabbi Yehoshua said from Rabbi Yochanan, etc., learning that the Holy One wrapped Himself like a shaliach tzibbur when He said the thirteen attributes. It implies a shaliach tzibbur should wrap when saying the thirteen attributes.

And in my opinion that every, etc. — as we say, "this teaches," etc. Since it says "like a shaliach tzibbur," it implies that a shaliach tzibbur, whenever he passes before the ark, requires wrapping. If it means "like a shaliach tzibbur" when saying the thirteen attributes, that implies this was already the custom of the shaliach tzibbur to wrap, yet we still would not have known the thirteen attributes, so there was not yet such a custom. And if it means by reference to the future, namely like a shaliach tzibbur who now says the thirteen attributes, that is forced; it should have explained, "like a shaliach tzibbur when he says the thirteen attributes." And Lechem Chamudot wrote at the beginning of the laws of tzitzit.
