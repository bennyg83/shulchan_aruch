Section 2, "Sheets, etc." This follows the Rosh and his view. But according to the Rambam it is obligated, as the Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote there. According to the explanation of Tosafot, a linen sheet is exempt, but we do not rule that way, as I wrote above in siman 9. However, the Mordechai wrote in the name of Sifrei: "'Your garment' excludes a sheet." It appears to me that this is because one is obligated only in the manner of wearing, as it is written, "with which you cover yourself," and as stated in Sifrei: "'Your garment' excludes a sheet; 'your garment' excludes a pillow." See Magen Avraham, that the dispute of Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel concerns the manner of wearing, like the case of Rabbi Yehudah bar Ila'i in Shabbat and Rav Ketina's cloak in winter, etc.
