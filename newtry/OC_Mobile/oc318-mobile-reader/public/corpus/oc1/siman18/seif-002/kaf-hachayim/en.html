B) There: Per Rambam, everything worn at night is exempt, etc. But nevertheless it is permitted to make tzitzit at night for a tallit designated for day, even per Rambam, who holds a tallit designated for day if worn at night is exempt. And there is no requirement for intent, and an analogy: one whose heart lacks no time by day and time comes automatically.
