And per the Rosh — Taz. Beit Yosef: the Rambam in chapter 3 of Tzitzis halacha 7 wrote that one does not put tzitzis on a linen garment — a decree lest one cover oneself with it at night; and in the Gemara they said (Menachot 40b) a decree because of a night garment — thus the Rambam holds that likewise a day garment at night is also not obligated; and the Gemara said the greater concern is a night garment, which was never permitted in it at all. And the Rosh has proof from the Gemara that says "night garment" — learn from this that everything depends on the garment. And what is the difference? Since it was permitted once, etc. — so in the Rosh, Beit Yosef; and it is difficult, for according to Rabbeinu Tam and the Rosh the reason is not "since it was permitted" but that a day garment is obligated at night; and there is a second explanation there that a day garment is obligated only by day — see there; and see Tosafos Menachot 40b s.v. mishum and s.v. tekheles, where R' Chaim contradicts the explanation above; and see Beur HaZahav there. And one can resolve somewhat: even if he has a garment designated for night, a four-cornered garment, he may walk in a day garment at night with kilayim and it is not considered possible to fulfill both — see Tosafos in the chapter Elu Na'arot from this.
