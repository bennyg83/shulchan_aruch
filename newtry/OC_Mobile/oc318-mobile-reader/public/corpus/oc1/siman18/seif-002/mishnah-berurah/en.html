8) Tzitzit in them. Since their principal use is at night, they are called a night garment. According to this reason, however, they would be exempt only according to the Rosh; according to Rambam, who maintains that a night garment worn during the day is obligated, they would be obligated. Another reason is offered for exemption: The Torah imposed the obligation only when the garment is worn in the normal manner or used to wrap oneself, not when it is merely spread over oneself. Other authorities disagree and maintain that even if one spreads a four-cornered garment beneath oneself, it is obligated in tzitzit, and all the more so when it is spread over oneself. Magen Avraham therefore concludes that one should round one corner and thereby satisfy all opinions. His words imply, however, that one need be stringent only with a woolen sheet, but not with one of linen or other materials. See the reason in Peri Megadim.
