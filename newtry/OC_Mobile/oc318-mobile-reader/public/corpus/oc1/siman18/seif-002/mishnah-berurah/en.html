And some say another reason to exempt: because the Torah obligated only a manner of wearing as clothing or a manner of wrapping, not a mere manner of wearing for warmth. And some disagree and hold that even if one spreads a four-cornered garment beneath him he is obligated in tzitzis, and all the more so a manner of wearing for warmth. Therefore the Magen Avraham ruled that one should make one corner round and thereby be exempt according to all opinions. Indeed it is implied from his words that one should be strict about this only with a wool sheet, but not with linen or other materials — see the reason in the Peri Megadim.
