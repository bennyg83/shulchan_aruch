Section 3, "From when, etc." Menachot 43b: "Once he became obligated in the mitzvah, etc." This means at that time, for then he became obligated in the recitation of Shema and in tefillin. See Rashi there, in the comment beginning "Once," and this is also the view of the Mordechai and the gloss, "And if he wore it, etc." He wrote the reason: because it is written, "and you shall see it," meaning at a time of seeing. But the words of the Rambam and Shulchan Arukh are primary, for from where do we know this regarding tefillin? Rather, it must be from the teaching mentioned above: "Once he became obligated in this mitzvah, etc.," and as stated in the Jerusalem Talmud and cited by Tosafot there, in the comment beginning "Between," that "and you shall see it" is derived from the adjacent passage. Thus the Mordechai's reasoning from "and you shall see it, etc." falls away; on the contrary, from there the opposite is proven.

"And if, etc." 36a. See Tosafot, in the comment beginning "And when he reaches, etc."
