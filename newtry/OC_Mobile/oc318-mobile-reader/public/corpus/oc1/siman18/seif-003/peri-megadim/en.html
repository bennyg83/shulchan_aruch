Designated — Taz. Beit Yosef wrote the halacha like the Rambam, and further safek berachos to be lenient; and a night garment by day is also a doubt — see Taz here. And if we say the Rambam's view is like Rabbeinu Tam, as the Bach wrote (72b), and it is a third view as the Rambam wrote here — specifically a day garment by day; and so wrote the Rambam, may he rest in Eden, "lest one cover oneself with it at night," and in the Gemara "night garment" means by day (for in this there is not so much decision whether the Rambam holds thus — Beit Yosef, and you will understand) — see there, safek berachos to be lenient. See Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari'a: our tallitot, which are only for the mitzvah of tzitzis and not for wearing, are not considered a garment designated for day (and it is not clear if tzitzis tore at night whether one may go so); and Beit Yosef rejected this, that a garment designated for day is considered, and per the Rosh tzitzis is required at night in such a case — see there; and so is the halacha. What I wrote in letter (1): if one attached tzitzis on Shabbat unwittingly, the tzitzis are valid — for we do not say regarding everything the Merciful One said "do not do," for if one did it, it does not help — see Choshen Mishpat siman 207 and R' Chaim in a purchase made in violation on Shabbat, see Sema there: since in the purchase the violation is only of time, it is not considered "do not do"; and slaughter on Shabbat proves in the unwitting case — see what he wrote in Pri Yoreh De'ah from this.
