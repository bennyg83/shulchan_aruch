C) If one made tzitzit on Shabbat: if done unwittingly, some say they are valid; but if done intentionally, on this there is room to consider — Peri Megadim there, Petach HaDibbur. There they were further uncertain in Peri Megadim: if one designated a garment for night and made tzitzit on it when it was exempt, then later reconsidered and designated it for a day garment — is intent required, since at the time he made them it was exempt, or perhaps since it was not lacking an act it is not intent-dependent? And they left it unresolved. And in responsa Ne'ot Deshe siman 62 he ruled there is no difference between made invalidly and made while exempt not for its purpose — in all cases intent applies and it is invalid; see there. And they brought Petach HaDibbur there.
