When does one bless on tzitzis in the morning? From when one can distinguish its tekheles from its white. If one wears it from alos hashachar onward some say one blesses — and such is the practice (Mordechai Megillah ch. 2). If one wore earlier, e.g. at Slichos, one does not bless on it; when day breaks one handles it and blesses (Tashbetz). ]:
