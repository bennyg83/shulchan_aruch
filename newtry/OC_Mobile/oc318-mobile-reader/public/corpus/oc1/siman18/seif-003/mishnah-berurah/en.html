(9) From when one recognizes, etc. — and this is the time when he can see his regular companion with him at a distance of four amot and recognize him, as mentioned below in siman 58 regarding Kerias Shema [Beit Yosef in siman 58 in the name of the Rosh that it is one measure — see there, and in Beur HaGra in our topic].

(10) And so is the practice — see Beur HaGra who ruled like Shulchan Aruch; in Peri Megadim he wrote that if the hour is pressed, we are lenient to put it on from alos hashachar onward and we do not rebuke him; but at any rate le-khatchilah it is certainly very proper to wait to bless until he can distinguish between tekheles and white, and so appears to be the view of Eliyah Rabbah. And be-di'eved it appears that even if he blessed before amud ha-shachar he should not go back and bless again, lest the halacha is like the Rosh that a day garment at night is obligated.

(11) He should handle them — meaning in his tzitzis.
