Maariv prayer. Ba'er Heitev. And Rama wrote that one must be careful not to be wrapped in tzitzis and tefillin after sunset, and one must remove them from one's head at sunset. See there, in the name of Sefer HaKavvanos that the Ari, may he rest in Eden, was extremely careful and feared greatly in this matter. And likewise Beit David in the name of the holy kabbalist Mahari Gikatillia, may he rest in Eden — see there; and see Eshel Avraham.
