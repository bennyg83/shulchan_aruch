3) If one made tzitzit on Shabbat, it may be said that they are valid if he did so unwittingly. If he did so deliberately, however, the matter requires analysis. Pri Megadim there; Pitchei Olam there. Pri Megadim was further uncertain about one who designated a garment for nighttime use and attached tzitzit while it was exempt, but afterward changed his mind and designated it for daytime use. Does this constitute ta'aseh velo min he'asui because the garment was exempt when the tzitzit were made, or, since no further physical act is lacking, does it not constitute ta'aseh velo min he'asui? He left the question unresolved. Responsa Neot Deshe, siman 62, concludes that there is no distinction between tzitzit made in an invalid manner and tzitzit made unnecessarily while the garment was exempt: ta'aseh velo min he'asui applies in every such case, and they are invalid. See there. Pitchei Olam cites this ruling there.
