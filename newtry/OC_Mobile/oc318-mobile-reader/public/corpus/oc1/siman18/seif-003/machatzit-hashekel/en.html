(Note 3) Sheets, etc. — and I do not know the reason, for we are strict like the Rambam and the Rosh; a garment designated for night, if worn by day, he puts tzitzit on it per the Rambam, but does not bless per the Rosh. If so, linen sheets are exempt, for we hold thus like Beit Shammai, as above siman 9; see Rabbeinu Yerucham. But a woolen sheet is fit to make tzitzit on it.

And even by way of donning that obligates in shaatnez. That is when they are for warmth as in Yoreh Deah siman 301. And tzitzit is compared to shaatnez as written in the negative commandment she'atnez — "fringes you shall make for yourself."

(3) but for wearing. That is, what is a manner of wearing as clothing, for in this verse where we compare tzitzis to kilayim only wearing was mentioned; and wearing for kilayim is derived from a different verse, as it is written: "and a garment of kilayim, sha'atnez, shall not come upon you."

And according to them one must say, etc. regarding a sheet with tzitzis — in Menachot page 41 and Shabbat page 25 they dispute whether we permit kilayim, that is, tekheles which is wool on a sheet which is linen; it teaches that one is not obligated at all in tzitzis; and if so it is certainly forbidden when one wraps oneself in it — that is, per the Rosh they are designated for wrapping.

As R' Yehoshua ben Levi did. On Shabbat page 25b, and so it states there on erev Shabbat — they would bring him a tub full of hot water and he would bathe, etc., and wrap himself and sit in sheets with tzitzis, etc.

And in Tosafos Menachot page 41, etc. between spreading out and wearing. And this is the opposite of Mordechai, who held that even in spreading out, where there is no Torah prohibition of kilayim as explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 301, nevertheless one is obligated in tzitzis.
