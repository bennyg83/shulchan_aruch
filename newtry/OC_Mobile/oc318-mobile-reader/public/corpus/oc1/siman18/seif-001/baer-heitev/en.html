Evening prayer. And on a public fast the Bach wrote that one removes the tallit when reaching Barchu. And Kaf Hachayim wrote until after maariv; see in Shiurei Kaf Hachayim and see siman 543, note 3. Mahari wrote in his writings: one who forgot or deliberately did not wrap in tzitzit all day — after they pray maariv he should wrap without a blessing.

And on the night of Yom Kippur. And everyone who passes before the ark must wrap. And Lechem Chamudot wrote that even one who says Kaddish Yatom should wrap for the honor of the congregation, and one does not bless on it.
