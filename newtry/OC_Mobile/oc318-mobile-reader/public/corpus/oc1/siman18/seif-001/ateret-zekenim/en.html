And regarding a doubt about blessings, to be lenient — Beit Yosef ruled like the Rambam that section: a doubt about blessings is to be lenient. And one could raise a difficulty: do we not sometimes find a stringency in the Rambam's leniency? That is, if one wears during the day even a night garment, he must bless on it, and likewise the reverse per the Rosh — if so, the Rosh's stringency becomes a leniency and the Rambam's leniency becomes a stringency. Accordingly, what the Rambam said as "obligated" means obligated in tzitzit but not obligated to bless — even per Rambam on a night garment worn during the day. And accordingly what Rama said, "and a doubt about blessings is to be lenient," means as Beit Yosef said, that the Rambam's words are primary; therefore it is permitted to wear it at night. See Taz.
