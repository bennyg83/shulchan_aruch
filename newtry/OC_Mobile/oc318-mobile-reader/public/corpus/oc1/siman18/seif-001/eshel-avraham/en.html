Time. Responsa Beis David strongly objects to those who customarily wrap themselves in a tallis for Maariv on Friday nights and Yom Tov evenings. No such practice is found among the poskim, and those who permit it have not walked in the light. Even without a blessing, it is completely forbidden. See also the works of Kabbalah, which state that one who wraps himself at that time causes various effects and arouses severe judgments, Heaven protect us. This is likewise the view of the Zohar, Ramban, and Raavad. The ruling in siman 581 that the prayer leader wraps himself concerns a time still close to daytime, when there is no concern, comparable to the law of Tachanun. Thus far.
