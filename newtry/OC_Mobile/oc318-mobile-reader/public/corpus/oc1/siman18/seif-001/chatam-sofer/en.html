In Magen Avraham note 1: why is it called [thus]? NB: In Sema siman 5 note 7 he wrote that at night, when like day, it is called day for judicial purposes — a fortiori for tzitzit, where "day" is not written explicitly, for the matter depends on seeing, as written "and you shall see it." And it is difficult: if so, it would not be a positive time-bound commandment.
