See in the Rosh, who asked: if so, why is it called a positive mitzvah shehazman grama, since one is obligated also at night — see there; the early authorities do not discuss it there. The Bach wrote: even if he had a tallis on him he should remove it when they begin Barchu afterward, for now they do not wear the tallis except for the mitzvah of tzitzis; if it remains on him it looks as if he holds night is time for tzitzis — likewise siman 30, seif 4.

And on Yom Kippur night he wraps. The Bach wrote the reason is to resemble angels. The Levush wrote in siman 618 / 611 that one does not recite the thirteen attributes without wrapping; it is not clear to me that everyone who passes before the ark must wrap, as we say "this teaches that the Holy One, blessed be He, wrapped Himself like a shaliach tzibbur." In Lechem Chamudot he wrote that even one who recites Mourner's Kaddish should wrap for the congregation's honor.
