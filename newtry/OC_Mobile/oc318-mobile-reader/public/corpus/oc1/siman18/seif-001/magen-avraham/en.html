And according to Rosh. See Rosh, who asks: if so, why is tzitzis considered a time-bound positive commandment, since it is obligatory even at night? See there. Olas Tamid did not examine Rosh's words. Bach writes that even if one is already wearing a tallis, he should remove it when the congregation begins Barchu. Since nowadays the tallis is worn only for the mitzvah of tzitzis, leaving it on would make it appear that he holds that nighttime is a time for tzitzis. The same appears from siman 30, seif 4.
And on the night of Yom Kippur he should wrap himself. Bach writes that the reason is to resemble angels. Levush writes in siman 581 that one should not recite the Thirteen Attributes without wrapping oneself. It appears to me that anyone who leads the prayers must wrap himself, as the Gemara says: “This teaches that HaKadosh Baruch Hu wrapped Himself like a prayer leader.” Lechem Chamudos writes that even one who recites Mourner's Kaddish should wrap himself because of the honor of the congregation.
