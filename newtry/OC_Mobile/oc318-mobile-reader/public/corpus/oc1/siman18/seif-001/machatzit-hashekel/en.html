(Seif katan 1) And according to Rosh, etc. Since it is obligated even at night. Rosh answers that because a garment designated specifically for nighttime is nevertheless exempt, the mitzvah is properly considered time-bound. Time determines the obligation with respect to designation, for the garment must be designated for daytime. Bach writes that even if one was already wearing the tallis at Minchah, such as on Erev Rosh Chodesh or a communal fast, he should remove it.
The same appears from siman 30, seif 4, where we take this concern into account regarding tefillin, and the same applies to tzitzis.
