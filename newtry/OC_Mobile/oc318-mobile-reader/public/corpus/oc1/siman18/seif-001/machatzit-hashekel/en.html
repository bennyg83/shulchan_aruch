(Note 1) And per the Rosh, etc. — since he is obligated also at night. And interpret the Rosh: since in any event a garment designated for night is exempt — if so it is properly called time-bound, for time causes the obligation, namely regarding designation that must be designated for day. Bach wrote even if the tallit was on him at minchah time, such as on Rosh Chodesh or a public fast.

And likewise siman 30 note 4 that they are concerned for this regarding tefillin, and the same regarding tzitzit.
