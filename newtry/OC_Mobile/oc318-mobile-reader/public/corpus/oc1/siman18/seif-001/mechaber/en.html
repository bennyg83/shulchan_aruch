The time [for] tzitzis — it contains three clauses.
Night is not the time for tzitzis, for we exclude “and you shall see them.” According to the Rambam, whatever one wears at night is exempt even if designated for day; what one wears by day is obligated even if designated for night. According to the Rosh, a garment designated for night is exempt even if worn by day; a garment designated for day or for day and night is obligated even if worn at night. Rema: With doubt in blessings we are lenient — therefore one does not bless except when wearing by day a garment also designated for day (Hagahos Maimoniyos). After Maariv even while still daytime one does not bless (Piskei Maharai siman 221). On Yom Kippur night wrap while still day and bless (Tashbetz).
