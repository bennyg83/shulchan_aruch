The time for tzitzis. This siman contains three seifim:
Night is not the time for tzitzis, for it is excluded by the verse, “and you shall see it.” According to Rambam, anything worn at night is exempt, even if it is designated for daytime; and anything worn during the day is obligated, even if it is designated for nighttime. According to Rosh, a garment designated for nighttime is exempt even if worn during the day, while a garment designated for daytime, or for both daytime and nighttime, is obligated even if worn at night. Gloss: Regarding a doubtful blessing, we are lenient. Therefore, one recites a blessing only when wearing it during the day and it is designated also for daytime. Hagahos Maimoniyos. After Maariv, even if it is still daytime, one does not recite a blessing over it. Piskei Mahari, siman 121. On the night of Yom Kippur, one should wrap himself while it is still daytime and recite the blessing. Tashbetz.
