[2] Maariv, etc. See what I wrote in seif katan 5. Lechem Chamudos writes that the custom is nevertheless for one who recites Mourner's Kaddish to wrap himself because of the honor of the congregation. Mahari, in Terumas HaDeshen, part 2, siman 121, writes that one who forgot or deliberately failed to wrap himself in tzitzis throughout the day should wrap himself without a blessing after Maariv, or even at night. Bach writes that on a communal fast, when the congregation wears talleisim at Minchah, they should remove them upon reaching Barchu. Shiurei Knesses HaGedolah writes: “My custom, however, is not to remove the tallis and tefillin that I am wearing until after Maariv.” We do not follow Bach in this matter.
