[1] Per the Rosh one is obligated in tzitzit, etc. Nevertheless it is properly called a time-bound commandment, because the day causes the garment's obligation — thus Tosafot wrote Menachot 40, in the comment beginning "Because," etc.; see Olat Tamid — and it seems he did not see Tosafot there. I abbreviated since it does not emerge for practical halacha. And it implies it is forbidden to wear a night garment by day without tzitzit, and likewise a day garment at night.
