Section 1: Night is not the time for tzitzit, for "you shall see it" is diminished. NB: I was asked whether it is permitted to make tzitzit at night. In the body of the matter I saw that Peri Megadim raised this, but to exclude one part — for in my opinion it appears permitted. And proof from what is stated in the first chapter of Yoma in Rashi, in the comment beginning "The time of slaughter," etc.: slaughter at night is invalid, for it is written "on the day of your sacrifice." And why do I need a verse for slaughter, since offering at night is invalid? And we learn from "on the day" — or it would suffice to write "on the day of your offering," and since we know offering at night is invalid, likewise slaughter is invalid, for it is a preparatory act of the mitzvah. See also Rashi there page 29, in the comment beginning "Rather, even melikah of a bird," etc., that he derived it from slaughter, etc. And why is all this needed — since offering at night is invalid, its preparatory acts are invalid at night automatically? Rather, certainly without the verse I would have said preparatory acts are valid at night; if so, with tzitzit, where there is no multiplicity — wearing and fulfillment do not apply at night — nevertheless the making is valid at night, for it is only preparatory, since a box-cover is exempt and the essence of the mitzvah is wearing; making tzitzit on the garment is only preparatory, like in offerings slaughter, kemitzah, and melikah. This is clear in my humble opinion; examine well.
