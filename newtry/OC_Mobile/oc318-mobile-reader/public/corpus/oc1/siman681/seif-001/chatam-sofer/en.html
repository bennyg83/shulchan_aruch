Taz s.k. 1 in d.h. all this, etc., Shabbat unlike, etc. NB: requires study in Ra'avad Mishnah 6 chapter 8 of Berachot: blessings we can do without appearing as burden, such as candle and spices — we precede to Birkat HaMazon — end; meaning: but havdalah we delay lest it appear as burden. Here we see blessing on candle without havdalah wording is not release of Shabbat — unlike Taz.
