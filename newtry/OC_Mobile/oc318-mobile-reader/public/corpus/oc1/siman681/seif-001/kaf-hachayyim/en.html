1) [Seif 1] On Motzei Shabbos one does not recite Havdalah over a Chanukah light, etc. It appears that this applies specifically if he first recited the blessing for kindling the Chanukah light, in which case he may not afterward use it for Havdalah. However, he may first recite the blessing over the light for Havdalah, extinguish it, and then rekindle it for the mitzvah of the Chanukah light. It is preferable to do so, because once one mitzvah was performed with it, another mitzvah should be performed with it. Nevertheless, since we rule that it is preferable to delay the departure of the holy day, the custom is to kindle the Chanukah light first, and it is then impossible to use it for Havdalah, even after it has burned for the required measure of time. Bach; Olas Tamid; Mishna Berurah, letter 1. However, what he wrote, that this applies even after it has burned for the required measure, follows his view. According to the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch above, siman 672, seif 2, it is permitted after it has burned for the required measure. Nevertheless, we already wrote there, letter 21, that several later authorities maintain the view of the Bach, that if he placed the oil in the light without qualification, it is forbidden to derive benefit from it even after it has burned for the required measure; see there.
