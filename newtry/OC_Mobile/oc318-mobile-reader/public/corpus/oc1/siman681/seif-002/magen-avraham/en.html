Before Havdalah. Because we delay leaving Shabbos. In Ner Mitzvah he wrote that they kindle before Veyiten Lecha. It seems to me that one who forgot Atah Chonantanu should recite Havdalah and afterward kindle, for nowadays people are not accustomed to refrain from kindling before Havdalah in prayer, as written in siman 299. If so, he is forbidden to kindle since he has not recited Havdalah. Although he can say, Blessed is the One Who separates, etc., nevertheless, since some say that one always recites Havdalah first, in such a case one should act according to their view. In a congregation, however, he should not do this; rather, he should say, Blessed is the One Who separates, kindle, and afterward recite Havdalah.
