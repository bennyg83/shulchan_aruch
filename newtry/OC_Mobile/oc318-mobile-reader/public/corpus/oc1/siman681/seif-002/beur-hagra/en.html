Section 2: We kindle. Berachos 52a: the departure of the day, etc. Although the blessing of the day is preferable according to Beis Shammai over what is frequent, and all the more so with respect to what is frequent, and all the more so according to the last two answers in Tosafos, Shabbos 23b, beginning with the word hadar, and further, etc. Turei Zahav objected that according to Beis Hillel, what is frequent is preferable even over the entry of the day, and all the more so over the departure of the day. But the words of Terumas HaDeshen and Shulchan Aruch, siman 489, section 9, escaped him: and when the last Yom Tov falls, etc. See what I wrote there. All the words of Turei Zahav here are rejected; examine them.
