Before. In Matz he wrote they kindle before and he will give you. Magen Avraham wrote: one who forgot Atah Chonantanu should make havdalah and then kindle, for now people practice not to kindle before havdalah in prayer — if so forbidden to kindle since he did not make havdalah; nevertheless in congregation do not do so — only the one who makes havdalah kindles and afterward makes havdalah — end. Tosefet Yom Tov wrote the Gaon Maharal of Prague instituted havdalah first lest he forget havdalah in prayer and commit prohibition in kindling; he concludes so one should practice; so Taz l'maaseh — havdalah in his home at least first then Hanukkah light — end. {Rama: In Eliyah Rabbah he brought to halacha that always kindle first then make havdalah and rejected Taz's words with two hands — end}:
