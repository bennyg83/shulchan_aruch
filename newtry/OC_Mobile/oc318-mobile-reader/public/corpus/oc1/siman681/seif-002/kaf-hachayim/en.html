(ב) [Seif 2] They kindle Hanukkah light in synagogue before havdalah — so Beit Yosef in the name of Terumat HaDeshen siman 60 that they kindle Hanukkah light in synagogue before havdalah to delay taking out the day; Radbaz wrote some kindle it after making havdalah on the cup and then recite Yiftach (the prayer opening after havdalah on the cup), etc. — end Beit Yosef. He ruled here in Shulchan Aruch like Terumat HaDeshen; so Darkei Moshe — Maharil, Agur, Kol Bo wrote like Terumat HaDeshen; so practiced to kindle before havdalah — end; see below letter 4.
