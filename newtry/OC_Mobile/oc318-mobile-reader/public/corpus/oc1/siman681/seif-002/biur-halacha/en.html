They kindle, etc., before havdalah — see Mishna Berurah in what we wrote in the name of Taz; truly many Acharonim uphold his method — havdalah precedes — Maharal of Prague, Tosefet Yom Tov [brought in Eliyah Rabbah], Peri Chadash, Dagul MeRevavah, and many Acharonim uphold Shulchan Aruch and Rama — namely Magen Avraham, Eliyah Rabbah, Gra, Beit Meir; in Chemdat Moshe he answered all Taz's proofs; in She'elat Yaavetz he agrees with Shulchan Aruch; indeed this dispute is heard among the living and already divided among the early ones, aside from what Beit Yosef brought that Terumat HaDeshen holds Hanukkah light precedes and Maharil, Agur, and Kol Bo hold likewise; R' Avudraham wrote some make havdalah first — in Meiri our custom is to kindle Hanukkah light before havdalah; in Eshkol laws of Hanukkah: I saw many bless before havdalah — to the writer it seems error, etc. — see there [see Machatzit HaShekel, Birkat Yosef, Zechor L'Avraham]. Therefore correct to practice as several Acharonim agreed: in synagogue do not change custom always practiced to kindle Hanukkah light first; at home — whoever follows his custom; also Peri Megadim wrote do not rebuke anyone for he has whom to rely on.
