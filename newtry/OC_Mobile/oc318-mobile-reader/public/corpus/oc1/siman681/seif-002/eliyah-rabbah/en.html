Levush: And some say that in his home, the Chanukah lamp, etc. Rema wrote that in one's home it is all the more so, and it seems that his explanation is that since he already recited Havdalah in the synagogue, there is no prohibition at all to kindle. According to this, there is no source for the aforementioned some say, even though Terumas HaDeshen mentioned the synagogue, for one may say that he mentioned the synagogue as a novelty. So too Kol Bo. In Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah he wondered even more about Levush: how did he write some say, meaning Terumas HaDeshen, when the first view is also the view of Terumas HaDeshen? For according to Rabbi David Abudarham, one always recites Havdalah first. He did not see in Minhagim, where it is written explicitly that they kindle first, both in the synagogue and in his home. I further found in Maharil that he wrote: one kindles the Chanukah lamp and afterward recites Havdalah, even at home. Until here. If so, it is clear that the first view is from Maharil, who wrote that they kindle. Now it is also understandable that he brings some say, for they learned from Terumas HaDeshen and Kol Bo, who specifically mentioned the synagogue, that they speak only of the synagogue; after all, home is a greater novelty according to Maharil. It is forced to say that the authorities disagree about which case is the greater novelty. It is also difficult: from where do we know this, since it is explicit from Terumas HaDeshen and Kol Bo only regarding Bedek Habayis? From this there is a difficulty on Rema, who wrote and all the more so, etc. Also the reasoning that I wrote, that it is because it is permitted to kindle, may be rejected, for it is already permitted by Havdalah in prayer, or when he says Blessed is the One Who separates, etc., as I explained at the beginning of this siman. See Agur, siman 1041.
