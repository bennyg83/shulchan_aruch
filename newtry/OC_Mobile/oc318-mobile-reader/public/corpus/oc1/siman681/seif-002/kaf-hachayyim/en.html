2) [Seif 2] One kindles the Chanukah light in the synagogue before Havdalah. So wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of Terumas HaDeshen, siman 60, that the Chanukah light is kindled in the synagogue before Havdalah in order to delay the departure of the holy day. Abudraham wrote that some kindle it after reciting Havdalah over the cup, and afterward he should say, "May Hashem open for you, etc.," end quote from Beit Yosef. Here, in the Shulchan Aruch, he ruled in accordance with Terumas HaDeshen. So too wrote Darkei Moshe that Maharil, Agur, and Kol Bo wrote in accordance with Terumas HaDeshen, and the custom is to kindle before Havdalah, end quote. See below, letter 4.
