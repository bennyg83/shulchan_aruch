The custom is not to put on tefillin, etc. It is preferable to place ashes from the stove upon one’s head in the place of tefillin, “ashes instead of glory.” Kol Bo in the name of the Ra’avad; Darkei Moshe, entry 1; Ateres Zekeinim. This is the custom of Beit El, may it be rebuilt speedily in our days, in our holy city Jerusalem, may it be rebuilt and established, and there are also several synagogues that act this way. Before the recitation of the kinnot, the attendant brings a vessel containing ashes and carries it to each person. Each person takes a small amount and places it on his forehead, where it remains until the kinnot are completed, after which they remove it. Nevertheless, care must be taken not to place it on the hair, lest one fail to wash it away properly or forget it, and it constitute an interposition at the place where the tefillin are placed.
