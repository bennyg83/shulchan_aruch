(2) There. The custom not to lay tefillin, etc.; and it is preferable to put ashes of a stove on one's head in place of tefillin — ashes under the glory — Kol Bo in name of Ra'avad. Darkei Moshe seif 1 on Taz. And the custom in Beit El Yitzchak in our holy city Jerusalem, and also some synagogues do so: before saying kinot the shamash brings a vessel with ashes and passes it to each one, and each takes a little and places it on his forehead and it remains until kinot end, then they remove it. Nevertheless one should be careful not to place it on hair lest he not wash well or forget and there be an interposition at the place of laying tefillin:
