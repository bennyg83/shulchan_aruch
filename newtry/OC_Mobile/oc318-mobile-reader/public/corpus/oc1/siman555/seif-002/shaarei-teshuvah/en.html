And tefillin. See Ba'er Heitev; and see in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 23 who brought the words of Maharil to remove them after Kedushah, and Levushei Serad wrote to remove before the evening prayer; and similarly in Knesset HaGedolah that so was the custom, and he also brought the words of his brother-in-law in Or Zarua and he ruled like Maharil and wrote that thus appears the agreement of Magen Avraham and Maggid Mishneh, see there. And see above siman 130 in Ba'er Heitev from this; and see in Responsa of Rama that those accustomed to lay tefillin on Tisha B'Av in the morning — even though on other days they lay two pairs — on Tisha B'Av they lay only Rashi, and at mincha both; and in Knesset HaGedolah he disagrees on this, for since the custom is to lay in the morning he may lay Rabbeinu Tam as well; and in Responsa of Rama he settles the words of Rama per the secrets of the matter; and see there what is written in Halakhot Ketanot part 2 siman 139 that the Ari did not lay them — I have not seen thus, etc.; but the kabbalist Maharash Sharabi laid Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam on days of his mourning and on Tisha B'Av in the morning; and one who stands in the secrets of the matter will see that this is primary; and see Beit David siman 337 and Dvar Moshe siman 38; and see in responsum of Knesset HaGedolah Orach Chayim part 2: if there is a circumcision and they circumcise before noon and the sandek wore a large tallit — one should not protest against him, see there; and see in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 37 on Tisha B'Av that was postponed: a sick person without danger fasted until after noon and became weak until the doctor commanded he should not fast more, and the teacher ruled he should pray mincha gedolah and afterward eat; and because he prayed privately he forgot to lay tefillin, and he answered well: the teacher's ruling was to eat after mincha gedolah since it was postponed — he need not complete even a slight illness; and so I was accustomed to rule for a woman in childbirth within thirty days or pregnant with slight pain, for even for honor of circumcision they permitted as explained in siman 559, all the more where there is concern of illness (and it must be said there was no place to rely on the doctor's statement so much that he was not a clear doctor and the like — otherwise this reason would not be needed); and regarding tefillin — certainly if he can go to the synagogue or assemble a minyan in his house at mincha time he should join them in laying tefillin and have intent with the prayer leader in repetition of the prayer with the order of Kedushah, and afterward remove them as written in responsum part 2; and if he cannot assemble a minyan, then he prays Ma'ariv voluntarily and wears tefillin and prays in them Ma'ariv Shema with its blessings, and afterward removes the tefillin before praying the eighteen blessings; even though I wrote in that responsum one should not pray Ma'ariv with tefillin — nevertheless here where he forgot due to compulsion of illness he is permitted to do so so he not be like a bare skull, etc., see there; and I did not know why Shema and its blessings — is it not enough that he wear tefillin before beginning Ma'ariv and recite Shema in them as is customary with tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam:
