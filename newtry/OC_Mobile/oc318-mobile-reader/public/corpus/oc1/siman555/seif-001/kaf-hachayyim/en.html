1) [Seif 1] The custom is not to put on tefillin, etc. This is because tefillin are called glory, and a tallis is included because it is written, “He has carried out His word,” which the Targum renders, “He tore His royal garment.” Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. This reason applies specifically to those whose custom is not to put them on. However, several great authorities hold that even on Tisha B’Av one is obligated to put on tefillin as on every day, namely the Rosh, Ramban, and Ran, as explained in the Tur and Beit Yosef, see there. Therefore, some have the custom to put them on in the morning and recite the blessing, as stated below in entries 3 and 4, see there. Nevertheless, see Shaarei Chaim, siman 12, who cites the view of several poskim who hold that one should not recite a blessing over them, see there. However, we have already written in several places that where there is an established custom, we do not apply the principle of uncertainty regarding blessings.
