(1) The custom, etc. — per the Midrash: "In sweat you shall eat bread" — in Aramaic: "in the sweat of his garment" — he cast from heaven to earth; "the glory of Israel" — this is tefillin. But by law one is obligated in tefillin even though he said "every mitzvah," etc., and even though in a positive commandment a mourner is exempt, like turning over the bed which they did not concede to him, and similarly in other matters; nevertheless in a negative commandment he is likewise not obligated except those counted there, and the commentator explains which are forbidden, etc. — specifically what is forbidden all seven days is forbidden on Tisha B'Av; and this is what he said "things practiced," etc., that it is not stricter than after three days of mourning, which is old mourning as written in Yevamos, and therefore permitted in work like "he has nothing to eat," and similarly he answers the questioner; and see above siman 38 seif 66:

(2) And at mincha, etc. — for regarding these matters they were lenient at mincha, as written in Magen Avraham beginning chapter 19: in the morning one does not say Kedushah and Kaddish, and at mincha they say Kedushah and Kaddish; and similarly many matters depend on custom. The reason is that then they lit fire in the Temple as written above siman 557, and per Tosafos Kiddushin 31b s.v. istaya, etc., on "he became ill," etc., and we say in Midrash Rabbah there "Your iniquity," etc., in which Israel took the opposite of completeness, etc.:
