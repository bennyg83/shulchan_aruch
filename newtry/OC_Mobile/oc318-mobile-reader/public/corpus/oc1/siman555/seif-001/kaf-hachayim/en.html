(1) [Seif 1] The custom not to lay tefillin, etc. — because it is called "glory," and tallit because it is written "in sweat you shall eat bread" and Onkelos renders "in the sweat of his garment." Magen Avraham s.k. 1. This is the reason specifically for those accustomed not to lay; but there are several great authorities who hold that even on Tisha B'Av one is obligated to lay tefillin like every day — they are Rosh, Ramban, and Ran as explained in Tur and Beit Yosef, see there; and therefore some are accustomed to lay in the morning and bless as written below seifim 3 and 4, see there; however see Sha'arei Chaim siman 12 who brought the view of several poskim who hold one should not bless on them, see there; nevertheless we already wrote in many places that where there is a custom we do not say "leave Israel":
