(1) The custom, etc. — per the Midrash: "in sweat you shall eat bread" — in Aramaic "in the sweat of his garment" [this is the tallit] — he cast from heaven to earth; "the glory of Israel" — this is tefillin:
(2) Without a blessing — nevertheless if he put them on at night, some say he must bless on them in the morning:
(3) And at mincha — for then they lit fire in the Temple and "Your iniquity ceased" in what the Holy One poured His wrath on wood and stones [Gra]; and some say in order to show comfort in our mourning on that very day; and all this is for everyone only regarding tefillin because it is a matter that depends on general custom; but all five afflictions are not cancelled the entire day:
(4) And tefillin — one accustomed to lay tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam should lay them now as well [Acharonim]:
(5) And they bless on them — and it appears one should be careful not to read now the parshiyot of Shema and likewise parshat Kadesh, for now is not its time but only like one who reads in the Torah; and Torah study is forbidden the entire day:
