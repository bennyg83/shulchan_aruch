Not to put on etc. And those who are accustomed to put [tefillin] on should not put on in Shacharis except those of Rashi, and at Minchah he should put on both of them (Responsa Rema of Fano, siman 107); and Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah disagrees, [holding] that even in Shacharis he should put on both of them.
