That they fast in Eretz Yisrael. He comes to exclude other places, as below in se'if 9.
Torah scholars begin, etc. That is, even on the seventeenth day itself; and so it implies in Beur HaGra in se'if 9.
And all the students, etc. That is, that the obligation is upon every student, and he cannot exempt himself to say "I am exempt"; and specifically when they ask him a matter of halacha in his study and he says it, for then he is in the category of a Torah scholar and is obligated to begin fasting; but if he has not reached that level, he is not obligated to fast, except that if he wishes he may fast and there is no [concern of] arrogance in him; but any other person may not, for one who sees that he is fasting, the matter proves that he does it because of the rains, and it is [deemed] arrogance.
Like the law of an individual's fast. That is, regarding that they interrupt them on Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, and Purim if they encounter these days; which is not so for a communal fast, as above in siman 572; and also regarding what is explained in siman 566, see there. And it appears to me that the same applies regarding that one must accept them while it is still day, like other individual fasts; to the exclusion of a communal fast, as above in siman 562, se'if 12.
