The order of the fast, etc. And see in Halachos Ketanos, siman 90, that one should conduct the order of the fast nowadays; and see in Birkei Yosef, who brought that so he heard from the elders of Jerusalem and the elders of Chevron that they once carried out such an act; and see there, where he wrote to distinguish: that in Eretz Yisrael, where it is common (and see there that even in Eretz Yisrael a drought like this, that would require the entire order, is not common, and the elders are witnesses that it is not in memory except once in many years, once in sixty or seventy years; and if so, even in Eretz Yisrael it is not common; see there), we act as agents of the earlier ones; which is not so outside the Land, for the terrain of the earth is not one, and there are lands and regions where there is no drought; they did not wish to distinguish within the diaspora itself from place to place, and they equalized their measures, that there is no communal fast outside the Land; see there. And so wrote in Responsa Noda BiYehuda, part 1, siman 31, that in these lands they are not accustomed amid drought to make thirteen fasts in the order of the Mishnah; rather they decree one or two fasts and appease with prayers and supplications; see there.
