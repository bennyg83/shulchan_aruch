The order of the fasts, etc. It is a positive commandment to cry out and sound the trumpets over every trouble that should not come upon the community; and it is known to all that because of their evil deeds harm has come upon them, as it is written "Your iniquities have turned away," etc.; and this will cause the trouble to be removed from them; but if they do not cry out, etc.; and from the words of the Scribes [there is an obligation] to fast, etc. (Rambam). And Maggid [Mishneh] wrote: either shofar or trumpets. And Magen Avraham at the beginning of siman 576 wondered why we are not accustomed to sound at a time of trouble; and even though there is no communal fast in Bavel, nevertheless according to the Torah there is a mitzvah to sound without a fast; and see what I wrote in siman 585, that this sounding is done standing.
