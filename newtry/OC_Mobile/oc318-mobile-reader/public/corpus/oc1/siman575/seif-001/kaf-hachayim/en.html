(1) [Seif 1] The order of fasts that they fast in Eretz Yisrael for rains, etc.; whether in our time we should practice the order of fasts as we learned in our Mishnah — as I heard from elders that when the year is intercalated they should not begin the fasts in the order of the Mishnah; regarding the essence I say we perform the agency of the earlier ones. Leket Yosher part 1 siman 90 note 11 in Hagahot Tosefta; Pri HaAdam part 4 p. 8a. However see Birkei Yosef or part 5 what he wrote at length; he wrote he heard from elders of the Holy Land Jerusalem and Hebron may they be built that in their days rain was withheld and they practiced the whole order as Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote; at first he wrote to reject this because we have no reliable ones nowadays, then wrote he saw in responsa of Maharam ben Chaviv manuscript siman 6 that we should practice in our time in Eretz Yisrael the order written in our Mishnah, and therefore wrote to reconcile, and concluded: although such a matter — Eretz Yisrael and chutz laAretz are equal — nevertheless in chutz laAretz it is not one land's measure, for sometimes drought is found in one country and many countries have no drought at all except a minority of a minority; since not all places are equal in measure, the Sages equated their measures and there is no communal fast in chutz laAretz, see there. So too in responsa Noda BiYehudah part 1 siman 31 that in these countries they do not practice on drought thirteen fasts in Mishnah order — only decree one or two fasts and entreat in prayers and supplications, see there. Beit Din HaGadol part 1, note 1; see responsa Nachalat Binyamin part 2 end of pamphlet Pri miPri p. 36a at the end: I heard from the Rav haKollel Maharar Yerucham Vilna may his light shine that in Tzfat they did not even do those ten stringent fasts because we have no reliable ones even in Eretz Yisrael, see there. So too in Sefer HaTakkanot uMinhagim of Jerusalem may it be built in laws of fasts note 92: in the time of the great generation the Rav R' Moshe Suzin ztz"l such occurred and his agreement was not to do as Maran and Birkei Yosef, end of his words; see below seif 9.
