See the Taz: it implies that a student, even though he is not truly a Torah scholar, is nevertheless obligated to fast; and it teaches us that you should not say that this is arrogance; it teaches us that this is not arrogance, and consequently he must fast. And Magen Avraham, [s.k.] 1, explained that specifically a true Torah scholar cannot exempt himself; and a student alone has no [concern of] arrogance, and it is optional: if he wishes he may fast; but the rest of the people, even if they wish, may not, for it is arrogance. And even in this time when there is no [true] Torah scholar, nevertheless they fast; and God willing, in Magen Avraham more will be explained.
