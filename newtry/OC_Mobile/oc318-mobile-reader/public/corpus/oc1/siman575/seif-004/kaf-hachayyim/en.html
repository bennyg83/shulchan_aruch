4) In the year 5524, the 17th of Marcheshvan occurred on Monday. We have already stated that the consensus of the Rabbis of Jerusalem is to wait until the 17th has passed, and if rain has not fallen they decree a communal fast on Monday, Thursday, and Monday. Since, had they waited until the 24th of Marcheshvan, the third day of the fast would have occurred on the day of Rosh Chodesh, and primarily because there was great pressure and distress concerning the water, for its price had risen greatly, they agreed for this reason that in that year the individuals should begin on the 17th of Marcheshvan itself, which was Monday. Mizbach Adamah on this siman, 8c; Birchos Mayim, 42c. See below, os 40.
