Seif 4: On which they sound the shofar. Although in chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah we say, “In a place where there is a shofar there are no trumpets; in a place, etc.,” this does not refer to a fast, contrary to Rashi’s explanation. For in chapter 1 of Taanis we say that the sounding is with shofaros, and similarly the Yerushalmi states: Before Rabbi Yehoshua they sounded, meaning with a shofar, on a fast. Rabbi Yose asked: Should they not sound trumpets? He had not heard the teaching that trumpets are used in the Mikdash, but trumpets are not used outside the Mikdash. The statement, “In a place where there are trumpets, etc.,” refers to war, as Ran explains in chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah. This is also the view of all the poskim, unlike Rashi there.
