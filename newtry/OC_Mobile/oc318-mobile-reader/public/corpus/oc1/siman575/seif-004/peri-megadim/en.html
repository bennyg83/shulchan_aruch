On the night. See the Taz: in the responsa of the Rashba, of blessed memory, siman 152, which Beis Yosef brought, it implies specifically in Bavel, where they do not fast from while it is still day, as is written here in se'if 10, there is no communal fast in Bavel; and therefore the fast began only from dawn. But in Eretz Yisrael, similarly on the middle and last [sets of fasts], where they interrupt [eating] while it is still day, it implies that if [rain] fell on the night of the fast, they say Hallel HaGadol; and the Mechaber does not imply so; and this requires further study for now.
