(In Shulchan Aruch, se'if 11) And they should say Hallel HaGadol. And Birkei Yosef wrote that in the holy city of Jerusalem, may it be rebuilt and established, in the year 490, rains fell for them after midday, and they completed [the fast] and said Hallel HaGadol; and see in Peri HaAretz, part 2, who wrote that his proofs are not decisive, and the main [ruling] is that if [rains] fell after midday, that the law is that they complete [the day], they do not say Hallel, etc.; and see in Ritva, Taanis, etc.; and so is the view of Mahari Avi, who carried out an act in Chevron when they were answered after midday, not to say Hallel; and so wrote Eliyah Rabbah; see there. And see above, siman 569, s.k. 1, in Eshel Avraham, who brought from Responsa Kenesses HaGedolah that an incident occurred in Jerusalem, etc.; and he transcribed the words not in their place, for what connection does saying Hallel HaGadol have there, where it has no place except here regarding a rain fast; and about this he so wrote in Peri HaAretz, as I wrote from Birkei Yosef.
