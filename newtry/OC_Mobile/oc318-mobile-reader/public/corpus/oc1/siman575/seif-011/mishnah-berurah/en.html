They should complete that day. For most of the day has already passed in holiness; and afterward one does not say "Aneinu" in the Mincha prayer nor other entreaties; and Hallel too would have been fitting to say, except that they do not say it on an empty stomach [for since it is written in it "Who gives food to all flesh," it is fitting to be said on a full stomach; Rashi]; and some say that since they were answered that day, they say Hallel HaGadol [Dagul Merevavah].
And if they began to fall before midday. It implies from this wording that even if the requisite measure of rain was not completed before midday, since the beginning was before midday and it did not stop at midday, we follow the beginning, and they do not complete [the fast].
They do not complete. For until then it is still morning.
They should eat and drink. For one does not say Hallel HaGadol except with a satisfied soul and a full stomach; and specifically in this case, which is after the prayer; but before the prayer it is permitted; and therefore we say it in Pesukei DeZimra at Shacharis on Shabbos. And in a place where drunkenness is common, they say Hallel HaGadol and afterward eat and drink; and the same law applies if [rain] fell before midday and most of the congregation wish to complete, that they complete, and as above in siman 569, se'if 1, they should say Hallel HaGadol before eating.
Hallel HaGadol. They are the twenty six [verses of] "for His kindness is forever"; and they conclude "We thank You for every drop," etc., as is written in siman 221; and afterward the individual need not bless. And one should begin before "Hodu" with "Shir HaMaalos," "Behold, bless," etc., "those who stand in the house of Hashem at night"; and he says "Who raises vapors from the ends of the earth," etc. [Magen Avraham].
