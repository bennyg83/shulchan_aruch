Shaa the Great Hallel except when the soul is satisfied and the stomach is full, and precisely at the time of prayer, but before the prayer is a prayer, and therefore I say to her in the verses of Dezmera at dawn on Shabbat (Toss) and in the place of frequent drunkenness, say the Great Hallel, and then eat and drink (II Gm) and above DHA, if the majority of the public wanted to complete, they would say the Great Hallel before eating:

(2) The Great Hallel. They are twenty-six because his grace is eternal, and we are sealed to thank you for every prayer, etc., as in Mash 3 Racha (Harif) and the Rabbinate wrote that each individual should be blessed and wrote in the verses of the Tos.
