They should eat and drink. The Great Hallel cannot be recited except by one whose soul is satisfied and whose stomach is full. This applies specifically at the time of prayer, but before prayer it is permitted; therefore we recite it in Pesukei DeZimrah on Shabbos morning. Tosafos. In a place where intoxication is common, they recite the Great Hallel first and afterward eat and drink. Beit Yosef; Gemara. It appears to me that the same applies if most of the congregation wishes to complete the fast; they should recite the Great Hallel before eating.
The Great Hallel. These are the twenty-six occurrences of “for His kindness endures forever.” They conclude with, “We thank You for every drop,” etc., as written in siman 221. Rif. Ran writes that afterward each individual need not recite a separate blessing. Piskei Tosafos writes that one should begin with, “A Song of Ascents. Behold, bless Hashem, all servants of Hashem, who stand in the House of Hashem at night,” because the song for rain is recited at night; and one says, “He raises clouds from the ends of the earth,” etc.
