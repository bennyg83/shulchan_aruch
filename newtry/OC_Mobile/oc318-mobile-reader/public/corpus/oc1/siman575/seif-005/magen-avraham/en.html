Not during the first three. Because the Divine anger is not yet so intense. Nor during the final fasts, because there are seven of them and pregnant and nursing women cannot endure them. This is Rashi’s explanation. It is difficult, for they should at least observe three fasts. One must say that since they cannot observe all of them, they are not obligated to fast at all. This is also implied by what was written above in siman 577 and siman 576, seif 14. Accordingly, if the community decreed three fasts and a weak person cannot observe all of them, he need not fast at all. This requires study.
