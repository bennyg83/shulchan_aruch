Of joy, etc. It refers above also to business and commerce: it is forbidden only that which is of joy, as he wrote in siman 551. And they reduce betrothal, etc. Meaning that they do not betroth at all then (Magen Avraham), for they are stricter than Tisha B'Av, on which they do betroth (Tosafos, Megillah folio 7); and this requires further study from what I wrote in s.k. 1 in the name of Maggid [Mishneh].
