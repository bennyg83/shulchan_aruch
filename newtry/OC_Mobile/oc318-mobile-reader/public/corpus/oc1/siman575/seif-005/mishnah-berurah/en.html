They fast. On these three middle fasts.
But not, etc. For on the first three, His anger is not so severe; and the last ones, since they are seven, pregnant and nursing women cannot withstand them; and it is difficult: let them at least fast three fasts; and one must say: since it is impossible to fast all of them, they are not obligated to fast at all.
And to fast. Because it is bad for the fetus. If the community decreed three fasts and there is a weak person who cannot fast all of them, he need not fast at all; and this requires further study [Magen Avraham].
