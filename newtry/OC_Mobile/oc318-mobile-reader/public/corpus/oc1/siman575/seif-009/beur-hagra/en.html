Seif 9: When does this apply, etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 1, halachah 1. Although at the end of chapter 1 of the Gemara we say that Rabbi disputes this, and the halachah follows Rabbi, it may be that he disputes only changing the fixed text of the blessing, as written above in siman 566, seif 2, from the Yerushalmi. See there.
Individuals, etc., and they interrupt. As above in seif 1.
And afterward they wait, etc. As written above in seif 2: “When Rosh Chodesh arrives, etc.” It is like six days after the Monday, Thursday, Monday fasts of the individuals.
Like, etc. It says “like” because in a deficient month there are only five days.
