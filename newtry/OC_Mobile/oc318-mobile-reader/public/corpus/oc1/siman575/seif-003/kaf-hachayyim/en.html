The 17th of Marcheshvan arrived and rain had not fallen, etc. When the 17th of Marcheshvan occurs on Monday, the consensus of the Rabbis of Jerusalem is not to begin fasting on that day, because the phrase 'the 17th arrived' means only after the 17th has passed. Pri HaAdamah, part 3, 21c; Mizbach Adamah, 8, citing Shulchan Melachim. Nevertheless, Birkei Yosef, os 1, wrote to prove that one should fast on that very day, and wrote that this is the ruling we adopt. However, in Sefer Birchos Mayim on this siman, 42, he decided through proofs from the manuscript of the gaon, the Rif, from the author of Orach Chaim, and from the actual conduct of his father, the author of Pri HaAdamah, at a gathering of all the Rabbis of the holy city, that they should not begin fasting on that day. He also answered the words of the aforementioned Birkei Yosef. See there. Ikarei HaDat, siman 29, os 7, cited his words.
