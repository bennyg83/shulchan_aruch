(In Shulchan Aruch seif 11.) And they say the Great Hallel; Beit Yosef wrote: in the Holy Land Jerusalem may it be built, year 490, rain descended after midnight and they completed and said the Great Hallel; see Pri Eretz part 2 that his proofs are not decisive; the essence is that if rain descended after midnight the law is they complete — they do not say Hallel, etc.; see Ritva Taanit, etc.; so is the view of Mahari and Avi who acted in Hebron when they were answered after midnight not to say Hallel; so too in Eshel Avraham, see there; see above siman 569 note 1 in Eshel Avraham who brought from responsa 25 that an incident occurred in Jerusalem, etc., and copied the matters not in their place — what is the relevance of saying Great Hallel there where it has no place, only here in rain fasts; on this he wrote so in chapter 5 as brought from Beit Yosef.
