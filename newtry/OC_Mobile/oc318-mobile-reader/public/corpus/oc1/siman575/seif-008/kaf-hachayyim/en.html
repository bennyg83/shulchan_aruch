This is not difficult at all, for there it refers to a Torah scholar who, when asked a matter of halacha concerning his studies, can answer it. However, one who has not reached that level is not obligated to fast; rather, if he wishes he may fast, and there is no concern of haughtiness. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eshel Avraham, os 1. So too ruled Mishna Berurah, os 3. Even nowadays, when there are no Torah scholars of that standard, they nevertheless fast. Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1. It appears that their law is like that of students, who are not obligated, but may fast if they wish.
