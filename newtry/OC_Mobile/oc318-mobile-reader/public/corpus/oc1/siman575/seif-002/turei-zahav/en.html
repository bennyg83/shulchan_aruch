And they supplicate in the usual manner, etc. The Tur also wrote so. It appears that included in this is the Torah reading of "Vayechal," as on other fast days, as the Tur concludes at the end of siman 566 in the name of Rav Shalom. He did not need to mention it here regarding this fast. The reason he mentioned the reading at Mincha is incidental, for when he came there to mention the order of the day, he mentioned this too. Even though in siman 579, regarding the seven fasts, he also mentioned the reading, that is because there the reading differs from other fasts, since there one must read "Im Bechukotai," etc., and not "Vayechal." Beit Yosef extended his explanation of the Tur's words unnecessarily.
