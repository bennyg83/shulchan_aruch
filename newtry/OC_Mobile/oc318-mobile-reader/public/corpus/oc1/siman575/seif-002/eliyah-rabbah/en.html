[Levush] Like on Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av, etc. In the Tur he wrote only on Yom Kippur, which implies that we require adding while it is still day; and the Levush concluded also with Tisha B'Av to teach that only twilight is forbidden and not an added [early start]; and so Maggid [Mishneh], chapter 3, agreed, that these should not be stricter than Tisha B'Av.
