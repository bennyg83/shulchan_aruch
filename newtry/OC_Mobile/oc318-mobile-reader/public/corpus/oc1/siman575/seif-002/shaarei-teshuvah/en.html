Rosh Chodesh Kislev arrived, etc. And Tashbetz, part 2, siman 11, wrote: a beis din that wished to decree a fast over the rains, and it happened that one day was the Tenth of Teves, counting for them as day one, that is, the Tenth of Teves in place of the second day; see there; and Birkei Yosef brings it.
