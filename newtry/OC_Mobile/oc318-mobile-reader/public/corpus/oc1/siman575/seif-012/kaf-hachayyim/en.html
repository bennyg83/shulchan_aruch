12) [Seif 2] Rosh Chodesh Kislev arrived and rain had not fallen, the beit din decrees, etc. Sefer Shulchan Melachim wrote that if the beit din did not decree fasts for rain, once Rosh Chodesh Kislev has passed the decree automatically takes effect and they are obligated to fast, for these fasts were already decreed by the Sages of the Mishnah. These are his words. This is incorrect, for the wording of the Mishnah and the poskim, who state that the beit din decrees them, refutes his view. Likewise, the statement of Shmuel in the Gemara, 'There is no communal fast in Babylonia,' breaks his argument. I also found in the manuscript responsa of the pious scholar R' Yosef Molcho that is in my possession, siman 21, that he wrote that if they were not decreed, they do not take effect. He brought a proof as I wrote, and he further wrote at length to prove that the beit din must decree them. I showed this responsum to the Rabbis of our time, and they rejoiced at his words. Birkei Yosef, os 3.
