[Levush] For drunkenness they say, etc. And the same law applies if most of the congregation wish to complete [the fast]: they should say Hallel HaGadol before eating (Magen Avraham); and see siman 569. And this requires further study: for if so, also when [rain] fell after midday, that they complete, they should say Hallel before eating at night; rather, they say it only when they eat by day; and so it implies in Maggid [Mishneh] at the end of chapter 1.
