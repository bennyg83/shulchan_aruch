12) [Section 2] Rabbi Kesliu arrived and it did not rain in 4 Guzerin, etc. He wrote in S that the kings did not decree rain fasts in 4. If Rabbi Kesliu passed, the decree applied and they must fast as they are already decreed by the sages of the Mishnah Akkad. And Lita from the language of the mishna and the P. D. Gozerin were quoted in 4 Guzerin. Also in the MS in the Shas, Shmuel said that there is no OT in Babylon, it was a translation. Likewise, I found in the Tsho' Rabbi the Chasid Mahari Molcho 21 that in my hand he wrote in siman 20 that they did not judge, they do not apply and he brought evidence as a MS and further went on to prove that the DAVID must judge. And I showed Tash' to the rabbis of our time and they rejoiced at his words. Rabbi O. C:
