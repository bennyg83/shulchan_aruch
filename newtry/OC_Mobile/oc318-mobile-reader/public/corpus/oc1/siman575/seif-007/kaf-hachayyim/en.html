Accordingly, when people remain in the House of Hashem at night to perform a Tikkun Kares and seek mercy for a drought, so that rain should fall, how good it is to establish their study in tractate Shevuos or in the Rambam's laws concerning the aforementioned subject, for this is a timely matter, and it will be accepted favorably so that the rains fall in their proper time.
