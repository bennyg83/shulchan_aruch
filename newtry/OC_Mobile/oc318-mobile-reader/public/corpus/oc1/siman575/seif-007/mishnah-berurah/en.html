Of joy. Not specifically; rather the same law applies to any building that is not needed, that is made only for beauty and mere comfort. And know further that "of joy" refers also to business and commerce: it is forbidden only that which is of joy, such as needs of a wedding canopy for one's son [Magen Avraham]; and see siman 551, se'if 2, and Mishnah Berurah there regarding these laws.
His wall. Of a building of joy, such as a wedding house for his son, and even if there is no danger in it.
And they reduce betrothal. Meaning that they do not betroth at all; and see Biur Halacha.
The constellation of Taurus. And that is thirty days after the tekufah of Nisan falls.
