19) Once he has already been removed, his present status is that of an initial appointment. Raanach 2:57. Rashdam, Orach Chaim 32, writes that if the entire congregation agreed to remove him and did so, and they now wish to appoint him again, even a minority of the congregation may object. Mishpat Tzedek 2:77 writes that this applies specifically when the congregation agreed to remove him and he remained out of office for many days or years. If the congregation did not agree to remove him and he did not remain out for days or years, the minority cannot object. Knesses HaGedolah, ibid.; Magen Avraham, ibid.; Eliyah Rabbah 29; Shalmei Tzibbur, ibid.; Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 7; and Chayei Adam 29:11.
