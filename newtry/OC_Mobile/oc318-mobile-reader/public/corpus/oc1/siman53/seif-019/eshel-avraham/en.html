To prevent. See Responsa Devar Moshe, siman 4, regarding the circumstances in which an individual may object. See also Knesses HaGedolah; Responsa Maharashdam, part 1, siman 35; Yam Shel Shlomo, first chapter of Chullin, siman 58, in the name of Maharanach; and Sefer Chassidim, siman 759. See also the aforementioned Responsa Devar Moshe, siman 5, concerning a chazzan who took an oath regarding a future act and violated it, and who also stole. The chazzan claims that the witnesses have a personal interest in their testimony, that the oath was taken under different circumstances, and that three people had already released him from it. What is the law? See Responsa Nechalas Shivah, siman 23.
