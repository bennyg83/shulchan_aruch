There are those who prevent a convert from being shaliach tzibbur, and we reject' their words. Even a single individual may block and say, "I do' not want so-and-so to be chazzan," if they had not already agreed on him from the outset.': And specifically when that individual has a valid reason per the tuvei ha'ir; but otherwise a single individual cannot object to a shaliach tzibbur [Mahariv siman 60]. If he is his enemy, he may object to him before they agreed on him [Maharam Padua siman 104; Maharik, root 44]. One who hates the shaliach tzibbur should not be called up to the Torah when the rebuke is read [Or Zarua].]:
