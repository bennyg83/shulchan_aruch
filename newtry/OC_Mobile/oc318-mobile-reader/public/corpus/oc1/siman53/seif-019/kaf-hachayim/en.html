(19) Whoever they have already removed—behold, he is now at the beginning of acceptance. Ranach, part 2 siman 57. And Rashdam, in part Orach Chayyim siman 32, wrote: the consent of the entire congregation arose to remove him, and they removed him; and they wish to raise him up another time—even a minority of the congregation can protest. Baal Mishpat Tzedek, part 2 siman 77, wrote that this is specifically when the congregation agreed to remove him and it stood thus many days and years; but wherever the congregation did not agree to remove him and it also did not stand thus for days and years, the minority cannot protest—as Knesset HaGedolah there. Magen Avraham there. Ateret Rachel, note 29. Shaarei Teshuvah there. Peri Megadim, Orach Chayyim, note 7; Eshel Avraham, general rule 29, note 11.
