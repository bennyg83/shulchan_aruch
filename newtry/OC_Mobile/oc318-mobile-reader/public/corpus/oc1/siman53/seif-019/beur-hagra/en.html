Section 19 — there is, etc.: As stated in chapter 1 of Bikurim, and when he is in the synagogue, etc.

And he vowed, etc.: See there Rashi; and then it teaches: they taught in the name of R' Yehuda Ger, etc.; and likewise in Tosafos Bava Batra chapter 1, page 1, s.v. lema'utei, in the name of R' Yehuda — not like Rabbeinu Tam; and so rule the poskim; and there is a practical precedent.

Even an individual, etc.: As stated in Taanit 16a; and see Aramaic Rashi there, s.v. umerutza, etc. — they agree to his prayer; and how did they fulfill [their obligation] if they do not agree to his prayer? And the same applies in place of the sacrifice, as stated at the end of chapter 4 of Berachos; and they say at the end of Taanit: and how was his sacrifice, etc.? And all the more so when there is no desire in his sacrifice; and they say in Nedarim 35–36: everyone requires awareness, etc.
