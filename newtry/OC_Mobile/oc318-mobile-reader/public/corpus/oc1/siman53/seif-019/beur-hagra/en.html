Seif 19, there is, etc. As stated in the first chapter of Bikkurim: and when he is in the synagogue, etc.
And he excommunicated him, etc. See there in the Rash. Then it says: it was taught in the name of Rabbi Yehudah, a convert, etc. So too Tosafot to Bava Basra 81a, s.v. to exclude, wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yitzchak, not like Rabbeinu Tam. And so the poskim ruled; and practical incident confirms this.
Even an individual, etc. As stated in Ta'anis 16a. See Rashi there, s.v. and accepted, etc.: they agree to his prayer. How can they fulfill their obligation if they do not agree to his prayer? The same applies in the place of a korban, as stated at the beginning of the fourth chapter of Berachos. And we say at the end of Ta'anis: how can his korban be offered, etc.; all the more so when he does not desire its offering. And we say in Nedarim 35b to 36a: all require intent, etc.
And if he is, etc. As above.
And one who is, etc. Seif 8, because of danger; see there, and see Magen Avraham. It appears as written in the Kenesses HaGedolah.
