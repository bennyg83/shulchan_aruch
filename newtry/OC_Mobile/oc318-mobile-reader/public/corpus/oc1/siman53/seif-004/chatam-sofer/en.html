In the Mechaber, seif 4: Study Yam Shel Shlomo, first chapter of Chullin, from siman 49 onward, and Mateh Moshe, siman 60. See also Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, who writes that when a man’s married daughter committed adultery, he is disqualified from serving as chazzan. Yet below, siman 128, regarding a kohen, he writes that the practice is not to disqualify a kohen because of his daughter. Perhaps a distinction may be drawn between a kohen and a chazzan. See my responsum.
There: Seif katan 20. Note: See Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 206, where several laws concerning the appointment of a rav are explained.
