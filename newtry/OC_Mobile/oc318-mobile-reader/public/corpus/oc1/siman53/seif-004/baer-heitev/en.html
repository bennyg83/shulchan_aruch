**Proper.** Maharam Mintz wrote: the garments of the shliach tzibur must be clean, without filth and sweat, and his garments must be long so that his legs are not visible; and he should enter the beis haknesses first and leave last, see there siman 81; and he should not be a fool and simpleton, but one who can speak about the congregation's affairs as is fitting. Rashal.

**In his youth.** In responsum of the Ra'avan siman 86: for an ordinary shliach tzibur who is not shliach tzibur on a fast, he need not have an attractive appearance; rather, whoever now has good deeds—even though in his youth a bad reputation came upon him, and even if he worshipped idolatry—since he returned in repentance, he is fit to be permanent shliach tzibur; and likewise Rashak part 2 siman 107 and R' Elazar Sasson siman 157. And Maharshal in his responsum siman 50 agreed as well—specifically on a fast and at ma'amadot; but on other days, even as permanent [shliach tzibur], whoever is not wicked and is pleasing to the people and his voice is pleasant and he is accustomed to read from a minyan as shliach tzibur; and likewise the author Yad Aharon concluded in halachah: for an ordinary shliach tzibur we do not require an attractive appearance, and whoever we see returned in repentance—we read with him; and likewise most of the latter; and see R' Shmuel HaLevi part 14 siman 28; and thus brought up the Orach Torah and concludes: and it seems to me that if the congregation decrees a fast, even though there is no communal fast now, nevertheless such a shliach tzibur should not pray; and kal vachomer on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, see there. Further the Orach Torah brought up that even the author did not be stringent in what he ruled—that no bad reputation should come upon him in his youth—meaning not to appoint him at the outset; but to remove him and dismiss him after he was established as shliach tzibur we have no need to be stringent; and likewise Magen Avraham. And in responsum of Ra'anach part 2 siman 41; and see seif 25: And he wrote further there that if a bad reputation came upon him now and it is an ongoing rumor, even an individual can protest to remove him; but all who already removed him and he stood thus for days and years with the consent of the congregation and they wish to appoint him—it is now like the beginning of acceptance, and even a minority can delay; and if the objectors are the leaders, it is as if the whole congregation objects, unless in a place where there are selectors and there is a cherem not to change their decree—then the administrators are not effective (Magen Avraham in the name of Mahari Katz, see there, and in such a case). And in responsum Chut HaSheni siman 75 he wrote: since presumptuous waters passed over him, he should be removed; but in a case where he was invited to convert and did not convert willingly and returned in complete repentance and they already accepted him upon them as shliach tzibur—they should not remove him, see there; and see Chavot Yair siman 176 and Sha'ar Ephraim siman 31 and Eidut B'Yehosef siman 87. And according to everyone, if he transgressed under duress and returned in repentance, he is permitted even at the outset; and likewise Magen Avraham. A shliach tzibur from whom trefah emerged many times from under his hand—they remove him from shliach tzibur (Mahari Katz part 2 siman 75). It is good to seek a shliach tzibur who is son of a tzaddik, for the prayer of a tzaddik son of a tzaddik is not comparable, etc. (Beit Chadash; Rashal). And Taz note 3 disagrees and wrote that one should draw near one who is not of distinguished lineage to bring the distant ones near to the Divine Presence—for the Merciful One requires the heart—and his prayer will be heard more than a tzaddik son of a tzaddik. One whose arms fell off is permitted to be shliach tzibur, because the way of the Holy One blessed be He is to use broken vessels, as it is written: "a broken and contrite heart," etc. (Rashal, first chapter of Chullin); and Magen Avraham note 8 disagrees with him and wrote that one should be careful regarding shliach tzibur not to be one with a blemish; and moreover "present it now to your governor" (and in Sefer Eliyahu Rabba he brought several proofs that one with a blemish is permitted to be shliach tzibur, see there):
