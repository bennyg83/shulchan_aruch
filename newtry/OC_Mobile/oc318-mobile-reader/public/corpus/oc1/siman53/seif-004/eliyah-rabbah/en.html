[7] Acceptable to the people, etc. Meaning, humble and agreeable to others, etc., for whenever people are pleased with him, Hashem is pleased with him. Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 81, writes that if there is a dispute or quarrel between two householders, the chazzan must be careful to remain passive, both in speech and in action, openly and secretly. All the more so, if a non-Jew is engaged in a dispute with a householder, he must remain passive, even if that non-Jew is related to him by marriage or is otherwise his relative. If he himself has any quarrel or enmity with individuals or with one member of the congregation, he must place the matter before the rabbi or the congregation for mediation and act in accordance with whatever they deem proper. If the deficiency or obstruction then comes from the opposing party, he is blameless, for what more can he do? See further there.
