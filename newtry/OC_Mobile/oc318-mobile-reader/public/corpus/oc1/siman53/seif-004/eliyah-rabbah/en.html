[4] He shall wait, etc.: The language of Maharil — he shall wait half an hour and more — up to here. And see in the responsum of Maharam Mintz siman 8; and it implies there that if they said "Yishtabach" immediately and afterward the quorum assembled, even so they say Kaddish if he did not interrupt — even though at the time of Yishtabach there was no quorum; and likewise in the responsum of Shaarei Ephraim siman 17. And see below siman 55, note 3. And Magen Avraham wrote: but the individual says Yishtabach immediately; and likewise on Shabbat, if he finished the verses of zimrah before the shliach tzibur, he says Yishtabach immediately.
