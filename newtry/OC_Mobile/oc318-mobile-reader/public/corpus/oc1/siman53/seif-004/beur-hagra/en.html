Section 64 — sheliach tzibur, etc. Taanit 16b: "It happened to me…"

And which one, etc. There, page 1; and it appears to the Taz that the same applies on the other days of the year, and so is implied from the words of the poskim — not as stated in Kol Bo in the name of Rav Hai Gaon.

That he should, etc. And he omitted "a caretaker who has no [dependents] and has toil in the field" — for this applies only regarding a fast. And he omitted "and to change," etc., which is not in the text of the Rosh. Also "expert in all the blessings" — the Rambam and Tur omitted [these requirements], even regarding a fast. See below, siman 579.

And that he should not leave, etc. Tur; and as explained, what is written there, "and his house empty," etc. — likewise in siman 579. He wrote, in the name of the Rambam there, who explains regarding his sons, the members of his household, and all who accompany him, etc.; likewise in Shulchan Aruch there. And he proved there in Beit Yosef per the Rambam's explanation — for per the Tur's explanation, why need [the requirement that] "his chapter is pleasant"? Infer from this.
