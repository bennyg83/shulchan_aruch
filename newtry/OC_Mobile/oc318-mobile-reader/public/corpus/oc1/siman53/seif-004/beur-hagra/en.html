Seif 4, a shaliach tzibbur, etc. This is from Ta'anis 16b, 'I had, etc.'
And who is, etc. There, 16a. The Tur and Shulchan Aruch maintain that the same applies during the rest of the year as well; and so it is implied from the words of the poskim, unlike what is written in Kol Bo in the name of Rav Hai Gaon.
That he should be, etc. He omitted 'one who has dependents and has nothing,' and 'one who has labor in the field,' for this applies specifically to a fast. He also omitted 'and to teach, etc.,' for that is not found in the version of the Rosh. Also, 'and expert in all the blessings,' was omitted by the Rambam and the Tur even with regard to a fast; see below, siman 579.
And that he should not have gone out, etc. This is from the Tur, in accordance with his explanation of what is stated there, 'and his house is empty.' So he also wrote in siman 579. There he wrote in the name of the Rambam, who explains it as referring to his sons, the members of his household, and all who are attached to him, etc.; and so it is written in the Shulchan Aruch there. In the Beit Yosef there he proves the Rambam's explanation, for according to the Tur's explanation, why would it need to say that his youth was proper? This proves it.
