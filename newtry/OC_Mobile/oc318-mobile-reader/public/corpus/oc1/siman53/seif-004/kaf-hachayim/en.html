(4) [Section 2] One should not say Yishtabach unless he has said Baruch she'amar and some verse of pesukei dezimra—for it is the concluding blessing on the verses of pesukei dezimra, as written at the beginning of siman 51. Magen Avraham, note 2. Razah, note 2. And many err when they are on the road and there is no time to extend [pesukei dezimra], and they begin from Yishtabach—and whoever does so blesses a blessing in vain at the outset. Ateret Rachel, note 2, in the name of Nitzavim. Razah there. And there are people who delay on Shabbat from coming to the beis haknesset until the congregation says Nishmat kol chai, and they permit saying Nishmat kol chai with them—they will not say Yishtabach for fear of a blessing in vain, but they will say Nishmat kol chai without Yishtabach; and this is simple.
