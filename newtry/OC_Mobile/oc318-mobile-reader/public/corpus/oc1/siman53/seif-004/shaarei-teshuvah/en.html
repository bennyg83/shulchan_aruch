The shaliach tzibbur. See Be'er Heitev; in my humble opinion there is doubt — it seems that also an individual who must hear Kaddish and answer after it — the listener is as one who answers — should not interrupt between Yishtabach and answering Kaddish. And see what is written in Yad Efraim on Taz note 1. And see below siman 54: if they interrupted before Kaddish for some mitzvah need, when they return to pray, the shaliach tzibbur should say some verses from pesukei dezimra and say Kaddish on them — see there. And he wrote in Shachen Anglii in the name of Magen Avraham that one who says Yishtabach without reciting pesukei dezimra is merely mistaken — and see below.
