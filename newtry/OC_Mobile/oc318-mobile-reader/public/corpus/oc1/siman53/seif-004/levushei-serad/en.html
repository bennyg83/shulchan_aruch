Taz, seif katan 3. “For the Merciful One desires the heart.” Meaning, Hashem will see the congregation's intention, that they do this in order to draw him, who is of that distant lineage, close. A righteous person who is the son of a righteous person will draw close on his own. Through this, Hashem will accept the former's prayer more readily than the prayer of a righteous person who is the son of a righteous person.
