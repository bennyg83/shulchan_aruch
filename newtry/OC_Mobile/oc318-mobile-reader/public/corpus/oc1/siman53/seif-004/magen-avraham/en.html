A shaliach tzibbur must, etc. See siman 579 regarding a fast, where we require additional qualities of this kind, and everything depends on the matter.
Even in his youth. This means that he should not be appointed initially, but we do not remove him because of this. When a bad reputation comes out about him now, and it is a rumor that has not ceased, even an individual may object in order to remove him, Rav Avraham ben Chayyim, part 2, siman 41. See below, seif 25. In any case where they already removed him, and he remained so for days and years with the consent of the community, and they now wish to appoint him, this is presently like an initial acceptance, and even a minority may object, Rav Avraham ben Chayyim, part 2, siman 27, Maharshdam, and Mishpetei Tzedek, part 10, siman 77. If those who object are the communal leaders, it is as though the entire community objects, unless it is a place where there are appointed arbiters and there is a ban not to alter their decree. In that case, the objection of the parnasim is ineffective, Mishpetei Tzedek there. It is proper that a shaliach tzibbur have long garments so that his legs not be visible, and that he enter the beis haknesses first and leave last, Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 8.
