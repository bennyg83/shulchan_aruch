The shaliach tzibbur requires, etc. See siman 579 regarding a fast—we require further things like these, and all as the matter requires.

Even in his youth. That is, not to appoint him initially; but they do not remove him because of this. And when a bad reputation emerges about him now, and it is an unceasing rumor—even an individual may protest to remove him (Raaviah part 2 siman 41). Regarding siman 205: whoever they have already removed and he stood thus for days and years with the congregation's consent, and they wish to appoint him—behold, it is now the beginning of acceptance, and even a minority may protest (Raaviah part 2 siman 27, and Rashdam, and Machatzit Hashekel part 1 siman 17). If the protesters are the leaders, it is as if the whole congregation protests—unless in a place where there are arbitrators and there is a cherem not to change their decree; then the protest of the officers is ineffective [Machatzit Hashekel ibid.]. It is fitting that the shaliach tzibbur have long garments so that his legs not be seen, and that he enter the beis haknesset first and leave last (Maharam Mintz siman 8).
