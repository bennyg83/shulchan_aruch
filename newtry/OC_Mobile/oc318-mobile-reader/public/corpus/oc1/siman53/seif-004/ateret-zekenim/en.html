And he should have pleasantness and his voice should be sweet; it is written in Pesikta: "Honor Hashem from your wealth"—they read it as "from your throat"—if your voice is sweet, stand up and honor Hashem with your voice; and if it is not fitting, they should leave him and let another pray, for because of the pleasantness of his voice the Holy One blessed be He does not accept his prayer, as it is said: "He gave it to me with loud voices; therefore I hated it."
