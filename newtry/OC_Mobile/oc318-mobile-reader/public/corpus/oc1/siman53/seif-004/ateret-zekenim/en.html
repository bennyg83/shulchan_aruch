And he has a pleasant manner and a sweet voice. It is written in the Pesikta: “Honor Hashem with your wealth.” Read it as, “with your throat.” Thus, if your voice is sweet, stand and honor Hashem with your voice. But if a person is unworthy, and they allow him to lead the prayers because of the pleasantness of his voice, the Holy One, blessed is He, does not accept his prayer, as it is stated: “She raised her voice against Me; therefore I hated her.”
