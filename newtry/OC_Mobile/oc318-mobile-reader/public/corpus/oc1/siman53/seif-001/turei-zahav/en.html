It means that the Shatz shall say it standing but not singular, and the late Mu'ah gave reason for saying that there is no need for standing on the part of an emir, he will praise himself except according to the fact that a standing Kaddish is followed by a standing Kaddish, and when the Shatz says he will praise a seated person, he will have to stop standing between the standing praise and the Kaddish that follows it, but it does not mean that in Hagm Shambi' 2i and the Zall, the number between the standing praise and the creation of an offense is In his hand and for this they used to say that a person standing like a creator will be praised, since it is forbidden to stop in the meantime. It means Dachl Adam Kai and already 20 Rama 2 in 19 that they used to stand when they say Ishtabh and in the Maharil it is written that the Mahari Segal would not stand either for the Kaddish or for his blessing, but every Kaddish that he caught from a standing person remained standing until he finished Amen, it would be 20. And I also saw in S. the intentions of the late Ari and the above, and the Tor and the Sha'a did not mean that one should stand for the Amir' Ishtabah, but we meant that the Shatz, who should stand in front of the ark when it begins to say his blessing, should precede himself by saying Yishtabah that he should say 36 standing so that it is attached to what is said after it without a break:
