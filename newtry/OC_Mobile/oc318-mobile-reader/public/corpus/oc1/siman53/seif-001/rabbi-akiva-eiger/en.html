Seif 2: only between Yishtabach and Yotzer. It is good that he bless on it between Yishtabach and Yotzer, since he interrupts between the songs of praise and Yishtabach — upon them [the songs] it applies; and their final blessing is it — so Levush. If so, one may say: only between the songs of praise and Yishtabach does the blessing apply to them; and it is not fitting that there be a break between them — for a final blessing should be adjacent to the mitzvah. But before that, in pesukei dezimra — at least between the chapters he puts on the tallit and blesses. Since even between the chapters in Kriat Shema, the author in siman 66 holds that he puts on and blesses; and even for Rama there, who does not bless — nevertheless putting on is permitted. If so, all the more so pesukei dezimra between the chapters. However, from Darkhei Moshe here on Kol Bo's words: whoever did not put on the tallit before Baruch She'amar should wait until after Yishtabach, put on the tallit, and bless. And it is possible that for this matter pesukei dezimra are stricter than the blessing on Kriat Shema — since there is a remedy to wait until after Yishtabach, for they are not so particular to say pesukei dezimra without a tallit. And from this one may say regarding tefillin too: even though Rama ruled in siman 66 that for tefillin, even in the middle [of pesukei dezimra] one blesses — nevertheless one may say that in pesukei dezimra, even between chapters, he does not put them on, since it is possible to wait until after Yishtabach — and it requires study for the law.
