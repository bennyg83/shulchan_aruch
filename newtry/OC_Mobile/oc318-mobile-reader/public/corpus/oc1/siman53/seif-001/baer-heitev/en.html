Standing. This implies that an individual does not need to stand. The same applies on Shabbos, when the chazzan chants the end of the blessing Yishtabach; the individual does not need to stand when he says Yishtabach, Bach. However, at the end of siman 51 it implies that one must stand, see Taz. All the fifteen praises in Yishtabach, from 'song and praise,' etc., until 'from now,' should be said in one breath, Shelah, Magen Avraham. But they are only thirteen praises, Yad Aharon. See also the sefer Mishnas Chassidim, folio 65a, and so it is in the kavanos.
