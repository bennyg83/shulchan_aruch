And likewise on Shabbat, when the chazzan draws out [the melody] in Baruch She'amar, the individual need not stand when he says Yishtabach with the congregation. But in a double doubt some say it implies that one must stand. Eliyah Rabba: all fifteen praises in Yishtabach from "a song and praise," etc., until "until now," one should say in one breath (Shelah; Magen Avraham). And they are only thirteen praises (Yad Aharon). See in Sefer Massekhet Chassidim page 65 side a, and likewise in Kavanos.
