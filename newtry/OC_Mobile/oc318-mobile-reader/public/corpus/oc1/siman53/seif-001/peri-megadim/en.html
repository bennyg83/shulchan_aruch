Says — Taz. Certainly the Gemara too is for the reason of adjacency to Kaddish (Yotzer Or does not require standing, and the kedushah in Yotzer is recited seated — Magen Avraham 59:2); but it implies the congregation's custom that they too must stand for Kaddish. On this Taz brought Maharil's words that an individual need not stand for Kaddish; and see Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah, who wrote that in siman 66 they relied on custom and law — only the shaliach tzibbur, see there. Levush wrote in the Tur's language that the shaliach tzibbur begins; and the author changed it because one must begin from Baruch She'amar — and I do not know why Levush did so. Also Levush's words that all Pesukei deZimra [are required] — Netziv achieved that partial suffices; and Eliyah Rabbah wrote that in a congregation one must say all Pesukei deZimra.
