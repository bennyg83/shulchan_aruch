(1) The shaliach tzibbur, etc. — so that Kaddish may adjoin to it immediately and he need not interrupt between them when standing before the ark; but in siman 51 seif 6 in the gloss it implies that even one who prays alone must also stand when saying Yishtabach; and see Eliyah Rabbah, who resolved that there it is from custom and here is from law; and so wrote the Gra; and so wrote the Taz that men of deed are accustomed to stand when they say Yishtabach, whether on Shabbos or a weekday and even when alone. All fifteen praises in Yishtabach from "A song of praise" until "now" — he should be very careful not to interrupt between them, and one who interrupts receives a great punishment; but he need not [say them] in one breath [Chayei Adam in the name of the Gra, and so wrote Rav Yitzchak Elchanan, and so is implied in Zohar Terumah, not like Shelah]:
