But in siman 51, seif 6, in the gloss, it implies that even one who prays alone must also stand when saying Yishtabach. See Eliyah Rabbah, who answers that there it is by custom, whereas here it is by law. So too the Gra wrote, and so the Atteres Zekeinim wrote, that men of deed customarily stand when saying Yishtabach, both on Shabbos and on weekdays, and even when praying alone. Regarding all fifteen praises in Yishtabach, from song and praise until from now, one must be very careful not to interrupt between them. One who interrupts is severely punished, but they need not be said in one breath.
