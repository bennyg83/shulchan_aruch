(SKA 1) Ma'om'am means when he prays singularly. Of course when he prays in a group, the individual should also stand like the Sha'tz A'G Dbash'Z the reason for him to rely on the Kaddish is that he sat in the Is'tbah and will have to stand to offer the Kaddish. And the pause between the Is't'bah and the Kaddish is MM. When the individual prays not in tzabur because he will praise Gofia A'tz to stand and also on Shabbat when the cantor plays and prolongs at the end he will praise by saying Baii then there is a stay for the individual who prays in tzabur to stand for an hour that the cantor plays and then the individual does not have to stand in praise. But in Ss 11 (sz7 in the 19th century) it means that it is necessary to stand as a rule in Had Baba, he will praise with God and bless David, which means that their laws are the same, and even when he praises, he should stand because his body will be praised, as God will praise and bless David:
