Seif katan 1: “Standing,” etc. This implies that the ruling concerns one who prays alone. Certainly, when praying with a congregation, an individual must stand just as the chazzan does. Although the reason for the chazzan is that Kaddish should follow immediately, since if he sat during Yishtabach and then had to stand for Kaddish there would be a slight delay and interruption between Yishtabach and Kaddish, nevertheless the individual must also stand during Kaddish, at least while reciting “Amen, yehei Shemei rabbah,” as stated below in siman 56, seif 1. By contrast, when an individual prays without a congregation, he need not stand because of Yishtabach itself. Likewise, on Shabbos, when the chazzan sings and prolongs the conclusion of Yishtabach while saying “Blessed are You, Hashem,” there is time for the individual praying with the congregation to stand while the chazzan is singing, and the individual therefore need not stand during Yishtabach. However, at the end of siman 51, seif 7 in the gloss, it appears that one must stand, since Yishtabach is included in a single clause together with Baruch She'amar and Vayevarech David. This implies that their laws are the same, and that one must stand during Yishtabach because of Yishtabach itself, just as during Baruch She'amar and Vayevarech David.
