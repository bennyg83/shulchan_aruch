Magen Avraham, note 1: for an individual he need not explain that the whole congregation must stand for Yishtabach in order to hear Kaddish immediately after it and not need to interrupt standing between Yishtabach and Kaddish; rather, when praying alone, he need not stand. And even in congregation—specifically on weekdays they must stand at the beginning of Yishtabach for the reason explained; but on Shabbat, when the shaliach tzibbur prolongs with melody, no. And he concluded that in siman 51, seif 10, it implies one must stand for Yishtabach on its own account, like Baruch she'amar and "and David blessed"—and therefore even for an individual, and even on Shabbat; and he disagrees with Taz.
