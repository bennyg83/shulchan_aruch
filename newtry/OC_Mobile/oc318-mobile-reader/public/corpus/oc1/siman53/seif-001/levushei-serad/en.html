Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. “When praying alone, one need not.” He explains that the entire congregation must also stand during Yishtabach so that they can immediately hear the Kaddish that follows it and will not need to interrupt between Yishtabach and Kaddish by standing up. But when one prays alone, he need not stand. Even with a congregation, they must stand at the beginning of Yishtabach only on a weekday, for the reason explained above, but not on Shabbos, when the chazzan prolongs it with melody. He concludes that siman 51, seif 10, implies that one must stand during Yishtabach because of Yishtabach itself, as with Baruch She'amar and Vayevarech David. Therefore, one must stand even when praying alone and even on Shabbos. He disagrees with the Taz.
