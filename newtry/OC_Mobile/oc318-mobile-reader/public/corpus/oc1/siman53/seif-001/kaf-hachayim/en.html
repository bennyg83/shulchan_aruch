(1) [Seif 1] The shliach tzibur says Yishtabach standing, so that Kaddish adjoin immediately after. Beit Yosef. Bach. Taz, note 1. Magen Avraham, note 1. But an individual need not stand when saying Yishtabach, as written in siman 51, note 42. And what Magen Avraham wrote at the end of siman 51 implies one must stand—Ateret Rachel, note 1, wrote that there it is from custom and here from law—see there. And Peri Megadim brought this in Mishbetzot Zahav, note 1; and we already wrote above that the widespread custom among us is that an individual does not stand when saying Yishtabach—see there.
