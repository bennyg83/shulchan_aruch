And in my humble opinion, therefore the author wrote on Motza'ei Shabbat—and it is certain that what he wrote is not specifically only to exclude Friday night, where there is the blessing of ma'ain sheva; and in this he fulfills the eighteen [blessings] for one who forgot and did not mention Shabbat. However, in Beit Yosef he wrote another reason—that Maariv is optional—and as in Darkei Moshe he wrote that we have no such custom among us; I abbreviated.
