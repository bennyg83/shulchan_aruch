Standing. See Be'er Heitev. And as he wrote, see Taz — it implies his view is as Beit Chadash: an individual need not stand, only the shaliach tzibbur, because of the interruption; and the Taz's words on this lack explanation and are well explained in Yad Efraim. And see in Shulchan Tziyyon that the rabbi z"l wrote one should not stand, for its secret is "from seated," as is known. And as he wrote that he should say the fifteen [psalms of] praise in one breath — and in Sha'arei Tziyyon he wrote that the Zohar, parashat Terumah, does not imply so, but only when interrupting in speech are these words said. He also wrote that he saw in Jerusalem a manuscript of Sefer HaKavanot from Mahari Chai z"l that one must say at the end of the blessing "Melekh El chai ha'olamim" and not say "uv'khen Yishtabach," etc., only on Shabbat eve.
