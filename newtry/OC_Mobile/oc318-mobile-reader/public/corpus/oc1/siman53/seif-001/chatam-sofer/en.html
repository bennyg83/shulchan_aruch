In the Mechaber's seif 4: examine well in Yeshuot Shmuel chapter 1 of Chullin from siman 49 onward, and Mateh Moshe siman 60; and see Sheyurei Knesset HaGedolah, who wrote that when his daughter committed adultery from marriage he is disqualified for shliach tzibur. And see below siman 128 regarding a kohen—he wrote that they are not accustomed to disqualify a kohen because of his daughter. And perhaps one may distinguish between kohen and shliach tzibur.
