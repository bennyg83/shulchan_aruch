All the fifteen praises in Yishtabach, from Shir and Shvachah, etc., until from now, should be said in one breath, Shelah. Standing. In order that he join Kaddish to it immediately. This implies that when one prays alone he need not stand. The same applies on Shabbos, when the chazzan sings Baruch Attah Hashem, the individual need not stand when he says Yishtabach, Bach. But at the end of siman 51 it implies that one must stand.
