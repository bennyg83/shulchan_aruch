All fifteen praises in Yishtabach—from "shir" and "shevachah," etc., until "me'atah"—he should say in one breath (Shelah): standing, so that Kaddish adjoins it immediately. This implies that when praying alone he need not stand. And similarly on Shabbat, when the chazzan sings "Baruch Hu uvaruch Shemo," the individual need not stand when saying Yishtabach (Bach). But in Shulchan Aruch, siman 51, it implies he must stand.
