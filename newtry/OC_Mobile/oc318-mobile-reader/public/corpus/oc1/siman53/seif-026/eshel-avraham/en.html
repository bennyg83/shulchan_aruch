And he wrote to Eliyah Rabbah siman 12 on the matter of a chazzan whom the entire congregation accepted, and now the majority of the congregation do not want him and they appointed another chazzan—the minority cannot hold back with their hands. And he answered: even though from Rashba's responsum it implies they can remove him, nevertheless in our case it is not so (and likewise the Ralbach z"l there)—for he concluded nevertheless that since the majority of the congregation want the latter, both should serve by way of compromise in the matter—see there.
