[26] One does not appoint a shliach tzibur, etc.: And in Levush he wrote: even one appointed by the congregation should not strive to hold the position — see there. And it seems to me that nowadays an individual cannot protest if the shliach tzibur was appointed according to a communal regulation — even if individuals object, he may hold the position according to custom, since he was appointed according to law. And even though Beit Yosef brings a case when he was already appointed — that is when there was no objection at all but they intended to distribute honor, see there; or when they object according to law per the early authorities — and this requires investigation.
