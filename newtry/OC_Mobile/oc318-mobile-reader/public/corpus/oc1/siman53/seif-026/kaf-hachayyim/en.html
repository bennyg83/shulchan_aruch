26) [Se'if 5] If they cannot find, etc., they should choose the best person in the congregation in wisdom, etc.; one who engages in Torah wisdom. Tanna Devei Eliyahu, Seder Eliyahu Rabbah, chapter 18, states that one who engages in Torah can ask for mercy in prayer before the Holy One, blessed is He. Yafeh LaLev 3:2.
