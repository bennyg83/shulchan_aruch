Section 66 — the congregation, etc., even etc. As stated in Bava Metzia, chapter 3, page 1 — Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says there was no need to state, etc.; thus the custom is as if he stipulated explicitly. And in the Gemara there, "Let us see how the custom is practiced," etc.; and in the Yerushalmi there, and the Rosh brought it — "this teaches," etc.; and at the end of Bava Kamma 116b, "And the donkey-drivers are permitted," etc., and not etc.; infer from this. And likewise there is no suspicion, as with a shofar — Gittin 62.
