(26) [Section 5] If they do not find [one fitting], etc.—they choose the best of the congregation in wisdom, etc., one engaged in the wisdom of Torah—for it is taught in Tanna deVei Eliyahu, seder 1, chapter 18: one who engages in Torah can ask for mercy and pray before the Almighty.
