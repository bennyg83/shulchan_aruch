The congregation for a time. See Responsa Chemdas Shlomo, siman 7, regarding hiring a chazzan for a period of three years, where he concludes concerning this law here that it applies specifically in a place where the custom is that after the period these people leave. But in our times, when the way is not to remove without suspicion, certainly one should not dismiss him for nothing, so as not to cast suspicion upon him; see there. See also Responsa Chasam Sofer, siman 206, who writes similarly, unless they explicitly stipulated that after the time they fixed, his term would end and both sides would be able to retract, and a new acceptance and appointment would be needed; see there.
Written for his sake. Meaning that it was written for the sake of Heaven and not for self-glorification, even if the individual's siddur is written more beautifully than the congregation's. Regarding printed books there is no distinction between one belonging to the congregation and one belonging to an individual. The Peri Megadim wrote that it is proper even for an individual to pray from a siddur, and all the more so a shaliach tzibbur, upon whom the awe of the congregation rests, that he should pray from inside the text. A shaliach tzibbur is called a chazzan because he must see how he will read; the Targum of 'he saw' is 'he gazed.' Radak.
