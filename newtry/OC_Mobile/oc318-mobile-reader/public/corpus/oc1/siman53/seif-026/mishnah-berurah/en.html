(87) Written for his name — meaning written for Heaven's sake and not for show; and even if an individual's siddur is in finer script than the congregation's. In printed books there is no distinction between congregation and individual. Pri Megadim wrote: it is proper even for an individual to pray from a siddur — all the more so a shaliach tzibbur, upon whom the congregation's awe rests that he pray from a book.
