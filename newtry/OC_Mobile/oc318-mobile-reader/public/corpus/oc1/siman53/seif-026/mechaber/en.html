A congregation accustomed to appoint people for communal needs for a term—when the term arrives, those leave and others enter in their place, whether for chazzan, charity fund, or other appointments the congregation needs, whether paid or unpaid; even if they did not fix a term for them, their silence is as their explicit stipulation, once they practiced thus.There is one who wrote that the shaliach tzibbur should pray from the book designated for the congregation—for it was certainly written for its sake [Maharil in the name of Rokeach].]:
