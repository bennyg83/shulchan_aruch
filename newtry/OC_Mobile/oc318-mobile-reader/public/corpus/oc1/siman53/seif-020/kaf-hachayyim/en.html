Eliyah Rabbah 7. A shaliach tzibbur who insulted an individual member of the congregation in the synagogue and before beis din must ask that individual for forgiveness in the presence of those before whom he sinned. Maharash Chayun in Mishpatim Yesharim 14; Yad Aharon, second edition, Hagahos HaTur 1.
