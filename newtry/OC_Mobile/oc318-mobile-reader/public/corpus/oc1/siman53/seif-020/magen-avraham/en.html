For his father. Explanation: his father died and he wishes to pray in the manner of mourners.

Whomever the congregation wishes. The reason is that specifically Kaddish he may say for his father, but he cannot be the congregation's agent to pray against their will (ibid.). And from what is the reason—I hold this from what is written in Hagahot Maimoniyot in Laws of Ten, 10 Tevet, that the mohel postpones the mourner from praying, not like Ramam; nevertheless, if the congregation wishes the mourner to pray, the mourner prevails. And I hold that his time precedes the mohel if the congregation is not strict about this—for according to Ramam, even a mourner precedes.
