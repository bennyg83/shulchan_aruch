**his father.** Meaning that his father died and he wishes to pray in the manner of mourners.

**the congregation.** For specifically Kaddish he may say for his father, but he cannot be the agent of the congregation to pray against their will. And it is a mitzvah to allow a mourner to pray, for it is comfort for the deceased and saves him from gehinom; and see Zeit before the funeral if the congregation is not strict. See Magen Avraham.
