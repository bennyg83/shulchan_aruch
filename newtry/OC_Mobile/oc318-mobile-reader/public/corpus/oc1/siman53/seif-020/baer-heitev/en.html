His father. That is to say, his father died and he wishes to pray as mourners do.
The congregation. For specifically Kaddish he may say for his father, but he cannot be the agent of the congregation to pray against their will. It is a mitzvah to allow a mourner to pray, for it is a satisfaction to the dead and saves him from the judgment of Gehinnom. A yahrzeit precedes the mohel if the congregation is not particular; see Magen Avraham.
