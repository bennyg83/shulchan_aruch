(20) There—and that he be humble and pleasing to the people. Explanation: humble and easy with creatures, etc.—for whenever creatures are at ease with him, the spirit of the Omnipresent is at ease with him. Ateret Rachel, note 7. And a shaliach tzibbur who insulted one individual of the congregation in the beis haknesset and before the court—the shaliach tzibbur must seek forgiveness from the individual in the presence of those before whom he sinned. Maharshach in Sefer Mishpatim Yesharim, siman 14. Yad Eliyahu in Maharam Benet, Hagahot Tur, note 1.
