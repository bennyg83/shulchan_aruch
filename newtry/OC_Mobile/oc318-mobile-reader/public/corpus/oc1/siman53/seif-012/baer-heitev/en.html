Ayin letters. The same applies to one who reads ches letters as hei letters. If everyone speaks this way, he may be shaliach tzibbur, Radbaz, Knesses HaGedolah, Magen Avraham, Olas Tamid; see there. Tosafot ask at the end of the chapter 'One who reads the Megillah while standing': How did Rebbe bring Rabbi Chiya down before the ark when he decreed a fast in HaPoalim? They answer there that when he intended to pronounce the ches he would pronounce it properly with effort; see there. But in HaPoalim they answered that this applies only where someone else is possible, but where someone else is not possible, it does not; see there. This latter answer appears to me to be the primary one, for if it were so that he could pronounce the ches with difficulty, how could he say to him: When you reach 'And I will wait for Hashem,' are you not found blaspheming and reviling? Perhaps when he would reach that verse and the like, he would trouble himself to read properly and would not blaspheme and revile. Rather, the primary answer is as they answered in HaPoalim, Pri Chadash.
