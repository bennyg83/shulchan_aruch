Eunuch. See Be'er Heitev. And see in responsum Pe'er HaDor to Rambam z"l siman 116, who was asked about a forty-year-old in whom no hair grew in his beard, and he answered that his law is as a eunuch from heat or because of illness he suffered — his hair did not grow, etc. — so that this man may be shaliach tzibbur, end quote. And see in Machatzit HaShekel and in responsum Orach LaTzaddik siman 5.
