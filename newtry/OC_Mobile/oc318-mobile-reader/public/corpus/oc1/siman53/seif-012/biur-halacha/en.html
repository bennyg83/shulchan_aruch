One does not appoint. At first glance, the same should apply even on an occasional basis, and he may not descend before the lectern as chazzan, since the reason is that he does not articulate the words properly. One cannot prove this from Tosafos, Megillah 24b, s.v. Keshe'atah, who ask concerning Rabbi Chiya how Rebbe allowed him to lead when he decreed a fast, because a communal fast observed on one occasion has the status of a permanent appointment, as is evident there on 24a in Tosafos, s.v. Ve'eino Over; see there. See above what we wrote in the name of Ran concerning a beard having fully grown, that even occasional service should be treated stringently when another person is available. All the more so here, one should always be stringent at least when another person is available, even for occasional service.
One who pronounces, etc. See below, siman 128, Taz, seif katan 30, who is lenient in our times, when most people do not distinguish these pronunciations.
