One does not appoint — it would appear that the same applies that one who is only occasional may not go down before the ark to be sheliach tzibur, since the reason is that he does not pronounce the words from his mouth properly; but one should not bring proof for this from Tosafos on Megillah (24b), s.v. "When you" — for they asked from Rosh Chodesh how Rabbi [Yehuda HaNasi] went down when he decreed a public fast: one can reject that a public fast is only occasional, like permanent, as is proven there, page 1, s.v. "and he does not transgress," see there. And see above what we wrote in the name of the Ran regarding one whose beard has filled in — that even occasionally one should be stringent when there is another available; and all the more so here, where one should always be stringent, at least when there is another available, even occasionally.

One who reads, etc. — see below in siman 128, in Taz, note 30, that one is lenient about this in our time, since most people do not distinguish this.
