[12] And one day, etc. Meaning, he has produced two pubic hairs whose tips can be bent back to their roots, as Beit Yosef writes. See siman 55, seif 5. Know that the phrase “and one day” does not mean that a full additional day after thirteen complete years is required. Immediately after the completion of thirteen years he is a bar mitzvah, and no complete additional day is needed. Sema writes likewise in siman 35. See Lechem Chamudos, chapter Yotzei Dofen. Responsa Bach, siman 145, states even more clearly that he becomes an adult immediately at nightfall, even though thirteen full years measured to the exact hour from his birth have not elapsed.
