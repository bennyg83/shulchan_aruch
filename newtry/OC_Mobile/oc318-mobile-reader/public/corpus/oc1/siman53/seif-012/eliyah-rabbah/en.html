[12] and one day, etc.—who brought two hairs in order to align their heads to their root (Beit Yosef). And see siman 55 seif 5; and know that 'and one day' was not said to teach that we require thirteen complete years, but immediately after thirteen he is a bar mitzvah and does not need one complete day; and so wrote Sema in siman 35. And see in Lechem Chamudot, chapter Yotzei Dofen. And in responsum of Beit Chadash siman 145 it is explained further that immediately when it grew dark he became an adult, even though there are not thirteen years from hour to hour from when he was born.
