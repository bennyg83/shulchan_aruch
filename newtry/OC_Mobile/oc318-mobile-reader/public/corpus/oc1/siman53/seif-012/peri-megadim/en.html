And one should consider Maariv prayer, which corresponds to the limbs and fats [of the daily offering]; and possibly the owners' consent is not required if they can delay him, since it is a mitzvah that one whose father and mother died should pray, as explained in Yoreh De'ah. Nevertheless, one certainly should not quarrel with the congregation over any mitzvah, for the modest withdraw their hands from dispute; and Eliyah Rabbah 25.
