Alefs as ayins. This means that the pronunciation of the ayin is stronger and deeper than the pronunciation of the alef. The same applies to one who reads ches as hei. If everyone speaks this way, he is permitted to be shaliach tzibbur, Radbaz, part 2, siman 45, Maharit, part 1, siman 16, and see Sefer Chassidim, siman 18, Knesses HaGedolah.
