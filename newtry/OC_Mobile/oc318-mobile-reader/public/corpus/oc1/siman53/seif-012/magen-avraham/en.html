For alephs and ayins. Explanation: the syllable of ayin forcefully and more deeply than the syllable of aleph; and the same applies to one who reads chet as heh. If everyone speaks thus, it is permitted for him to be shaliach tzibbur (Radbaz part 2 siman 45, and Ri'az part 1 siman 16, and see in Shulchan Aruch siman 18) (Kol Bo).
