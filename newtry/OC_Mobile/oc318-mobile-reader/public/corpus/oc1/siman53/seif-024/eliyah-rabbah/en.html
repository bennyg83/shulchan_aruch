And specifically where there is another shliach tzibur whom the congregation can hire for his fee; but if he is not available, it is not in their power to hold back the congregation on account of his hatred—even if he clarifies in his claim that this second one is superior to the first relative to adding the fee, even so they can say we have no ability to add so much to the fee—up to here are his words, abbreviated. And Sheyurei Knesset HaGedolah wrote: this is when he does not want because of his hatred; but when he gives a true reason that he is not fit to be shliach tzibur—even one who does not bear the burden can protest. And see above siman 151. Levush wrote: in our time an individual cannot protest—examine there. Sheyurei Knesset HaGedolah and Olat Tamid wrote in the name of Rashal: if the shliach tzibur spread a bad name about this protester, even though they accepted and reconciled, he can protest—up to here. And according to this, even if he was already accepted as shliach tzibur and they agreed on him, he can protest.
