24) There: He has a pleasant melody and voice. Pesikta expounds, 'Honor Hashem with your wealth'; read it as 'with your throat.' If your voice is pleasant, stand and honor Hashem with your voice. If a person is unfit and they nevertheless allow him to pray because his voice is pleasant, the Holy One, blessed is He, does not accept his prayer, as it is written, 'She raised her voice against Me; therefore I hated her.' Ateres Zekeinim 2. See below, paragraph 54.
