(71) He takes precedence — even though they thereby lack the mitzvah of prayer, since they have no one to fulfill their obligation; nevertheless, that there be a rabbi and posek who teaches them God's Torah and its laws is preferable to prayer. See Be'er Heitev Levush, where we wrote the extent of the obligation in every city for this; and he wrote in responsa Chavat Yair Orach Chaim siman 206 that all the people of the city are obligated to give for this, and no one can say "I do not need a rabbi and posek," for nevertheless he must bear the burden with the congregation, similar to all the people of the city being obligated to give for the mikveh — see there.

(72) "And if not" — meaning: since in any case he cannot instruct the people how to conduct themselves by Torah law, a sheliach tzibbur takes precedence to at least fulfill their obligation in prayer.
