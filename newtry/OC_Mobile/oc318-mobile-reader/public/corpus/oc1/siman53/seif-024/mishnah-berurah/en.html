Although through this they will lack the mitzvah of prayer because they will have no one to discharge them from their obligation, having a rav and halachic decisor who will teach them the Torah of G-d and His laws is preferable to prayer. See Biur Halacha, where we wrote about the great obligation in every city for this. Chasam Sofer wrote in a responsum, Orach Chaim, siman 206, that all the people of the city are obligated to contribute to this, and no one can say, 'I do not need a rav and halachic decisor,' for nevertheless he must bear the burden with the community, just as all the people of the city are obligated to contribute to the mikveh; see there.
