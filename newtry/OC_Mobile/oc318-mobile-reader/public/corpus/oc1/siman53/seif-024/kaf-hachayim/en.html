(24) There—and that he have pleasantness and a pleasant voice. It is written in Pesikta: "Honor the Lord with your wealth"—read "with your throat": if your voice was pleasant, stand and honor the Lord with your voice; and if it is not fitting and they let him pray because of the pleasantness of his voice, God does not accept his prayer, as it is said "she gave me her voice, therefore I hated her." Etz Chayyim, note 2. And see below, note 74.
