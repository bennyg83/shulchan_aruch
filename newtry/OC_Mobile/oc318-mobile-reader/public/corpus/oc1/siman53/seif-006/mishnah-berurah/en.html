(23) Honor of the congregation — and the congregation cannot waive their honor in this.

(24) Incidentally — all this during the rest of the year; but on fast days and Rosh HaShanah and Yom Kippur, even incidentally we do not bring down one whose beard has not filled; and even in this we do not lighten even through the congregation's waiver.

(25) When he brought two hairs — meaning after he reached thirteen years, for before that hairs do not help; see Magen Avraham, who wrote that presumably there is no need to check hairs in this, for in a rabbinic matter we follow the majority who bring two hairs at that time; see Pri Megadim, who wrote that for Rambam, who holds prayer is from the Torah, this shaliach tzibbur would be forbidden to discharge others in prayer until we know with certainty that he brought two hairs—see there.

(26) Known times — for this is regularity for those times; and this is not included if the congregation appointed him that he be ready to pray if sometimes the cantor is not in the synagogue—that this one stands to pray; there is no prohibition at all in this, for it is not called fixed since it may not happen—and they prohibited fixed only for known times when at that time he is certainly fixed [Taz]. Bach and the author disagree that three times is called fixed, and it is not called incidental only when he was not appointed at all.
