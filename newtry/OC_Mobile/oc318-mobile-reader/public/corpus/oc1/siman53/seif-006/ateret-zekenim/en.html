They do not appoint a shliach tzibur unless his beard has filled in, because of honor of the congregation, etc. And even if they wish to waive their honor it does not help; and likewise wherever Chazal enacted because of honor of the congregation, the congregation cannot waive their honor, such as rolling a Sefer Torah before the congregation, and a woman may not read before the congregation, and a minor may not lift his hands for Birkat Kohanim (my teacher in Bach). And likewise is implied in Beit Yosef, and likewise wrote the Rosh, Rashba, Ran, and Tosafos at the end of chapter Lulav HaGazul; and not like Tosafos at the beginning of chapter 5 of Chullin, where it seems from their words that it is permitted.

But incidentally, when he has brought two hairs, etc., and if he is thirteen years and one day old he can pray incidentally without checking the hairs afterward, for we rely on the majority who have hair (Maharam of Padua). And some say that he should not go down before the ark on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur or in a time of distress, God forbid, unless he is an exceptional person (Or Zarua). A man stricken by providence that his arms fell off is fit and worthy to be shliach tzibur, for the Holy One blessed be He desires to use broken vessels, as it is written: "A broken and crushed heart, O God, You will not despise" (Shelah). And the shliach tzibur is not disqualified by any physical blemish, whatever it may be, except by age (Maharshal in the name of Maharam). And they are disqualified by blemish only for priests.
