The congregation. And the congregation cannot waive its honor, Taz, Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah, and see Magen Avraham.
Two hairs. Anyone who has reached the category of thirteen years, we are not exacting about the hairs, as written in siman 55.
Known. For this is permanence for those times. It is obvious that this does not include a case where the congregation appointed him to be prepared to pray if, at times, the chazzan will not be in the beis haknesses, and this one will stand to pray. There is no prohibition at all in this, for this is not called permanent at all, since it is possible that it will never come to that. They prohibited permanence only for known times, when at that time he is certainly permanent, Taz. The Olas Tamid wrote that this applies specifically to one whose beard has not filled in; but one who is invalid to be shaliach tzibbur because of a transgression cannot be shaliach tzibbur even occasionally. So wrote Responsa Mishpetei Tzedek, part 2, siman 75. The same applies on a fast day and Rosh Hashanah as well, that one whose beard has not filled in is not brought down even occasionally, Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah and Olas Tamid.
