**The congregation.** And the congregation cannot forgive their honor. Taz, as in Knesset HaGedolah; and see Magen Avraham.

**Two hairs.** And whoever has reached the age of thirteen, they are not strict about hairs, as was written siman 55.

**Known times.** For this is fixed appointment for those times; and it is simple that this is not included if the congregation appointed him to be ready to pray in case sometimes there is no chazzan in the beis haknesses that he should stand to pray—there is no prohibition at all in this, for it is not called fixed at all, since it is possible that this will not come to pass; and they only forbade fixed [appointment] for known times when at that time he is certainly fixed (Taz). And Orach Torah wrote: and this is specifically one whose beard has not filled in; but one who is disqualified for shliach tzibur because of a sin—even occasionally—cannot be shliach tzibur; and likewise in responsum of Mahari Katz part 2 siman 75; and likewise on a fast and on Rosh Hashanah too they do not put down one whose beard has not filled in even occasionally. As in Knesset HaGedolah; Orach Torah.
