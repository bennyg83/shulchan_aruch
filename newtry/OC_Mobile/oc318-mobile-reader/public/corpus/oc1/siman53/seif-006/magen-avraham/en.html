Because of the honor of the congregation. If the congregation wished to waive their honor, they may do so, Shiltei Gibborim, and so Rabbeinu Yonah wrote according to the Rambam. But from what is written nearby, that he should not be appointed by the mouth of the congregation, it implies that even if the congregation waives its honor, it is forbidden. So too wrote the Bach; see Beis Yosef. Nevertheless, initially one should not do this. Regarding a fast, it implies in siman 579 that we require one who is actually elderly, not like what is implied in Tosafot to Megillah, that it is enough if his beard is fully grown.
From when he has brought two hairs. Anyone who has reached the category of thirteen years, we do not investigate his hairs, as written in siman 55, seif 5.
