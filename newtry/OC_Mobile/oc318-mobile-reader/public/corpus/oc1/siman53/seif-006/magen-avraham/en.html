Out of honor of the congregation. And if the congregation wishes to waive their honor, they may (Shelah; and so Ri in the view of Rambam). But from what is written immediately below—that he should not be appointed from the mouth of the congregation—it implies that even if the congregation waives, it is forbidden; and so Bach on "at the beginning." And nevertheless, ab initio one should not do so. And regarding a fast, it seems in siman 579 that an actual elder is required—not as implied in Tosafot on Megillah that when his beard is filled it suffices.

When he has brought two [pubic] hairs. And whoever has reached the category of thirteen years, we are not strict about hairs, as written siman 55, seif 5.
