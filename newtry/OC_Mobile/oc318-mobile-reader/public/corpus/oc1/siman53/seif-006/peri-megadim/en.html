For behold — Taz; and likewise Magen Avraham letter 13: thirteen years' completeness suffices, and one day is not required. And Eliyah Rabbah in letter 12 in the name of Beit Chadash's responsum 145: thirteen years' completeness does not require completeness above completeness — whoever has the appearance [of maturity] looks acceptable; but on Yom Kippur, no — for we require completeness until full [maturity]. The writer said: incidentally I saw [fit] to write a brief pilpul, and this belongs to letter 2, see there.

Behold Rambam, zt"l, chapter 8 of Laws of Prayer halakhah 11, wrote: one whose beard is not filled — because of honor of the congregation; and there it implies that even occasionally — for it does not write "poreis," etc.; and as Peri Chadash wrote. And behold he has a resolution: prayer of Torah [obligation] (matter 106) — for otherwise, why does a minor not go down, etc., as is established in siman 286 that a minor dispenses Rabbinic [obligations], and necessarily because of honor of the congregation; and as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashba's responsum in the name of Raavad — and see Levush. If so, the congregation's waiver helps (Avudraham on Maariv from this). And it is difficult: why discuss a minor? — all whose beard is not filled, waiver also helps; in a minor too it implies prayer of Torah [obligation], and Rabbinic [obligation] does not discharge Torah [obligation] (and we do not accept the reason of Kol Bo in the name of Rabbi Natan that we require "the prayer of a man" — Avudraham and Levush). And it is said: poreis with Kriat Shema; and it is said that one discharges even in Kriat Shema — unlike the author in siman 66, who disputes Rambam. If so, one may say: prayer of Rabbinic [obligation]; and Taz was correct in siman 66 letter 2. And Eshel Avraham: that they did not have plag hamincha — i.e., its time was all day and night; but for us, who fixed Maariv as an obligation — Magen Avraham 28 letter 7; and even though women do not pray Maariv (Magen Avraham), nevertheless for men who do not have plag hamincha — and it will be explained there in siman 66. What requires examination in siman 53 regarding one who reveals [his arms] will be explained there in letter 8 of this.
