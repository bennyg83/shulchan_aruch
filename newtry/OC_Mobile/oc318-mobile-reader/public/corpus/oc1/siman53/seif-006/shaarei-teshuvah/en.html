Fit. See Be'er Heitev. And Azulai wrote in Bikurei Shoshanim page 119 on what Chazal said, "She put her voice upon me" — this is a shaliach tzibbur who is not fit: for the shaliach tzibbur comes to arouse the attribute of mercy, and one who is unfit turns [prayer] to the attribute of din, which is from the feminine side; therefore they used feminine language "she gave," etc. And see there that he extended in rebuke of ethics and applied to this the verse, "A voice sings in the window, Horev at the threshold" — like one who sends to his master fine fruit offerings, etc. And many books from rishonim and acharonim extended greatly on this; and one who fears the word of God should set his heart to this.
