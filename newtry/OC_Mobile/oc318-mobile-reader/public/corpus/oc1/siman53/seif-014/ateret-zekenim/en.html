A blind person may descend before the lectern, provided that he does not read from the Torah, etc. Some say that a blind person may be called to the Sefer Torah and recite the blessings before and after the reading. So ruled Maharshal and Responsa Masas Binyamin, siman 68, and this is the primary ruling. See below, siman 139, seif 3, and siman 109, seif 10. In some places they allow mourners to lead the prayers only once or twice a week. Where there is no established custom, however, one should not prevent a mourner from leading the prayers. One who prevents a mourner is as though he prevents a person from performing a mitzvah, and as though he robs the deceased, since it brings satisfaction to the deceased and saves him from the judgment of Gehinnom (Maharam Mintz, siman 43).
