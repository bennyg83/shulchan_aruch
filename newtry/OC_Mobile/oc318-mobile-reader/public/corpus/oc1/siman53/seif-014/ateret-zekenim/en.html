A blind person may go down before the ark provided he does not read from the Torah, etc. And some say that a blind person is permitted to ascend to the Torah and recite the blessings before and after (Maharshal; in responsa Masat Binyamin siman 68), and this is the essential ruling. See below siman 139 seif 3 and in siman 109 seif 10. There are places that do not allow mourners to pray more than once or twice a week; but where there is no custom one may not object to a mourner praying, and one who objects to a mourner is as one who objects to a person doing a mitzvah and as one who robs the deceased, for it is comfort for the deceased and saves him from gehinom (R' Moshe Mintz siman 43).
