(14) [Seif 4] The shliach tzibur must be fit, etc.—as it is written "she gave me her voice, therefore I hated her." And Mar Zutra bar Toviah said: Rav said—and some say R' Chama said R' Elazar said—this is a shliach tzibur who is not fit who goes down before the ark. Gemara Taanit 16b. Tur. And that the verse speaks in feminine language—the rabbi wrote in Knesset Yisrael on tractate Taanit 16b, and this is his language: it is known that every prayer is called mercy, and the shliach tzibur must arouse mercies that are from the right side, which is masculine, as known to those who know kabbalah. A shliach tzibur who is not fit—not only does he not arouse the masculine side, but he even reverses from mercy to judgment, which is from the feminine side; therefore Scripture calls him in feminine language "she gave," etc.—end quote. And see there what Musar Tochachah wrote on a shliach tzibur who is not fit; and they brought from Machberes, note 1. Shaarei Teshuvah 271 side g. Sha'arei Teshuvah, note 6. And see further below, note 16.

(14) There—the shliach tzibur must be fit, etc. That which they are accustomed to call the shliach tzibur "chazzan"—Abudraham wrote because in Targum "vayar" is "v'chaza," that he must see how he reads. Ateret Rachel, note 5.
