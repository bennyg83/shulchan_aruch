And it seems this is where there is no other, or his father did proper repentance; but otherwise, behold the Sages said it is not similar to the prayer of a righteous son of a righteous person, etc.—up to here. And it requires investigation in Yoreh De'ah siman 281, that Shulchan Aruch ruled that a Torah scroll that a mamzer wrote is invalid; and Shach wrote in the name of Mordechai that a mamzer does not discharge the many of their obligation, and whoever does not discharge is unfit to write. And really, for the other reasons Shach wrote there, one may distinguish between a Torah scroll and tefillin; but this reason seems primary—examine there. Again I found in Magen Avraham siman 39 that he wrote in the name of Darkei Moshe that a mamzer is fit to write tefillin, and it requires investigation.
