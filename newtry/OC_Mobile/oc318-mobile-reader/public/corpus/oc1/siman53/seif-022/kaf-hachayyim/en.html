22) There: Acceptable to the people. The chazzan must take care that if a dispute or quarrel arises between two householders, he remains passive and takes no side, whether in speech or deed, openly or secretly. All the more so, if a non-Jew has a dispute with a householder, he must take no side, even if that non-Jew is his relative by marriage or other relative. If he himself has a quarrel or enmity with an individual or with one member of the congregation, he must submit the matter to the Rav or the congregation for compromise and do whatever they deem proper. If the deficiency or obstruction comes from the opposing party, he is blameless; what more can he do? Eliyah Rabbah 7 in the name of Responsa Maharam Mintz; see there.
