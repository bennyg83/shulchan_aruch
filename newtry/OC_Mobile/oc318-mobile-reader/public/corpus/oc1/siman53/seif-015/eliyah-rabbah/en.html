[15] This is disgraceful, etc. I found an even stronger statement in Sefer Chassidim. When Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel was being taken out to execution, he said to Rabbi Yishmael Kohen Gadol: My brother, why am I being taken out to execution? He answered: Perhaps you would expound publicly, your heart would rejoice, and you would derive personal pleasure from words of Torah. He replied: My brother, you have comforted me.
