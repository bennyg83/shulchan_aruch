And he should not wait — and in Biur HaGra this is contradicted from Erchin 11b, that the sheliach tzibur consults [the congregation] about prayer, see there. And from the Tur as well it is not proven except that he need not refuse, but not that he need not wait at all. Nevertheless, with difficulty one can reconcile the ruling of Shulchan Aruch: the Gemara deals with a sheliach tzibur who is not permanent regarding prayer, but is appointed for other matters; and according to what Rashi explained on Chullin 24b — there is a sheliach tzibur appointed for shofar-blowing, excommunication, and other matters, see there. And even though certainly the congregation would agree that he should be their agent for prayer as well, nevertheless he acts by consulting; unlike a sheliach tzibur whose appointment was primarily that he be the sheliach tzibur for prayer — he need not consult at all. And among the Levites there, since this is the first day they were appointed to their service, as shown in the verse there, it is possible that this is inferior to a permanent sheliach tzibur who always stands for this.
