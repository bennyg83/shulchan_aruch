[21] He should not wait, etc. Regarding the ruling below, siman 690, that one should not read the Megillah until the congregation tells him, “Read,” out of respect for the congregation, this will be explained there, with Hashem’s help. Tosafos at the end of the seventh chapter of Pesachim, 86b, s.v. ein mesarvin, write that when an act involves arrogance or authority, one should initially decline even if the person making the request is a great man.
