Seif katan 21: “Who has already,” etc. “But many may,” etc. Yam Shel Shlomo, Chullin, chapter 1, siman 20, writes that this applies specifically when they constitute a majority. Half the congregation cannot protest after having agreed. Even when the protesters are a majority and may remove him, they must nevertheless pay the wage that would have accrued to him through the end of the term for which he was hired.
