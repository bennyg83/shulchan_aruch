Convert. See Be'er Heitev, who wrote that a mamzer depends on dispute whether he is fit to write tefillin; and Rama meant that it is a dispute in Mordechai. And see above siman 39. And see in Biur Yehoshua, Yoreh De'ah siman 281, and in the words of Shulchan Tziyyon there; and in Sefer Rishon LeTziyyon (compiled by the holy Ba'al HaAri) he wrote regarding the law of disqualification that it applies only to the parchment panel — a mamzer who does not write the Divine Name intentionally — but other scrolls are kosher. And in Biur Yehoshua there in Yoreh De'ah they conclude that the entire Torah scroll is invalid; he also shall not be shaliach tzibbur. However, tefillin — he wrote — are kosher, as Moreh u'Ketziah wrote in siman 39.
