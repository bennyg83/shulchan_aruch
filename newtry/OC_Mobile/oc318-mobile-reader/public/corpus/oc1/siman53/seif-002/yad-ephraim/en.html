(In Magen Avraham note 8) He can be a shaliach tzibbur for all—see Knesset HaGedolah, for they hold the main [law] is like the Tur: we do not require these qualities only on fast days, but in every circumstance we require that he be fitting in these qualities; nevertheless they concede in this, and it must be that the reason is because Ishmaelites are not idol worshippers, as explained in Yoreh Deah siman 133 regarding libation wine; and it must also be that we are dealing with one who did not commit sins at the time of removal but returned to Jewish practice.
