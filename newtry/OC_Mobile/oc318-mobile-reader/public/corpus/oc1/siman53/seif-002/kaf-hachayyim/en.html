4) [Se'if 2] Yishtabach should not be recited unless one said Baruch She'amar and part of Pesukei DeZimrah. Yishtabach is the concluding berachah over Pesukei DeZimrah, as stated at the beginning of siman 51. Magen Avraham 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 2. Many err while traveling, when they have no time to pray at length, and begin with Yishtabach. One who does so first recites a berachah in vain. Eliyah Rabbah 2 in the name of Netz HaChaim; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ibid. Likewise, some people delay coming to the synagogue on Shabbos until the congregation reaches Nishmas Kol Chai and begin Nishmas with them. They should not say Yishtabach because of concern for a berachah in vain; rather, they should recite Nishmas Kol Chai without Yishtabach. This is obvious.
