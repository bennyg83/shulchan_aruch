Because — Taz. The author here retracted from this, for he brought the words of Rambam [regarding] honor of the congregation and concluded in the language of Rosh that one appoints him, etc. — implying that even if they waive [the requirement] it is forbidden; and as Beit Chadash and Magen Avraham wrote, letter 9; and as Pri Megadim Eshel Avraham wrote, that Beit Yosef there wrote to Mordechai and Rabbeinu Yerucham that the congregation's waiver helps — except that from Yerushalmi a different reason is implied, see there. However, as Rav Bei Rav wrote in hagahah there in Knesset HaGedolah in a time of distress, etc.; and Magen Avraham 9 [on] honor of the congregation — and here, etc. — implies that honor of the congregation helps for waiver. One may say: honor of the congregation is honor of Heaven, and waiver does not help; and when Shulchan Arukh is complete, only that there is an error and one complete chumash — there is no honor of the congregation at all, and this is simple.

And incidentally, Peri Chadash wrote that Rambam holds that even occasionally one does not pray before his beard is filled — this is from what he wrote in chapter 583 of prayer halakhah 11; but regarding poreis al Shema, thirteen [years] implies he goes down before the ark — not even occasionally. And so too there he wrote Lechem Mishneh, title BaA. And behold, he did not challenge from Megillah 24a: why discuss a minor, etc.; and the precise reading of the mishnah — a minor does not pass, but one of thirteen passes. One may say: a minor does not pass and the congregation's waiver does not help; but one of thirteen passes — the congregation's waiver helps. And we say: honor of the congregation — occasionally is not fixed; even Rambam admits waiver does not help. Nevertheless the author does not hold thus, as he wrote in letter 6 of this.
