(2) One should not say — whether the sheliach tzibbur or another person.

(3) "Unless" — for Yishtabach is the final blessing instituted to be said after Pesukei deZimra, and it begins without "Baruch" because it is adjacent to the blessing of Barukh Sheamar; therefore one may not say it without Barukh Sheamar. Nor [may one claim] as those err who, when traveling or in another time of pressure, begin from Yishtabach — for thereby they bless in vain.

(4) Beit Yosef said — therefore; but an avel or on a yahrzeit who were late coming to the synagogue until close to Birkat Kohanim and are compelled to stand at least to say Yishtabach before the ark — for it is forbidden to interrupt between Yishtabach and Kaddish — they should at least say for themselves from the beginning Barukh Sheamar and Ashrei; and likewise the cantor on Shabbat who was late until close to Birkat Kohanim should at least say for himself from the beginning Barukh Sheamar, Ashrei, and Nishmat; and likewise in siman 52 in Mishna Berurah seif kaf heh.
