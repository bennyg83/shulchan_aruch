Of song. For it is the concluding blessing over Pesukei D'Zimrah, and not like those who err and begin from Yishtabach, thereby reciting a blessing in vain. One who came to the beis haknesses at the time when the shaliach tzibbur begins Yishtabach, and if he says Baruch She'amar and Tehillah L'David he will not be able to pray with the congregation, should not say Yishtabach alone either. It is proper that he be silent, listen to Yishtabach from the shaliach tzibbur, answer amen, and then pray with the congregation, Mateh Moshe, Olas Tamid, Magen Avraham.
