**pesukei dezimra.** For it is the concluding blessing on the verses of pesukei dezimra, and not as those who err who begin from Yishtabach—for they would bless a blessing in vain. One who comes to the beis haknesses when the shliach tzibur begins Yishtabach, and if he says Baruch She'amar and Tehillah leDavid, cannot pray with the congregation; and Yishtabach alone likewise he may not say. It is good that he be silent and hear Yishtabach from the shliach tzibur and answer Amen, and then pray with the congregation. Mateh Moshe; see Magen Avraham.
