Dezmara It is a final blessing on the verses of Dezmera and not as a claimant who begins with a praise that blesses a blessing to cancel it. He who comes to Bakhch at the time when the Shatz begins should praise, and if he says Baruch He Hemer and praise be to David, he will not be able to pray with the public and will praise alone, likewise there is no saying. It is good for him to be silent and listen and praise the Shatz and answer Amen and then pray with the public.
