Seif katan 5. “The amount of time needed.” Meaning, at the beginning of siman 65, regarding Krias Shema, if one paused even for the time needed to complete the entire passage, he need not return to the beginning. If, however, the pause was caused by circumstances beyond his control and lasted long enough to complete the entire passage, some authorities require him to return to the beginning. The present case is comparable to circumstances beyond his control, since no quorum is present. Therefore, he should wait only half an hour, since ordinarily this amount of time is not enough to complete the entire passage.
