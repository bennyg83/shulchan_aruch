Note 5: the measure of "kedei." Meaning: at the beginning of siman 65 regarding Keriat Shema—if he waited even for the time to finish it all, he need not return to the beginning; if by unavoidable circumstance he waited the time to finish it, some say he must return—and this is like unavoidable circumstance, since there is no minyan, therefore he should not wait only half an hour—for this measure by default is not the time to finish it all.
