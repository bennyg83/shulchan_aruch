If he transgressed inadvertently, such as if he killed a person, etc., but if he did so intentionally—no. And only if he killed a person does repentance not help him to be fit as shliach tzibur; but if he transgressed other sins between man and God where he left the religion in his youth and returned in complete repentance, he is fit (Maharshal, Beit Shemuel siman 2). And it is not proper to remove a permanent shliach tzibur on account of another whose voice is sweet; rather both should serve as one (Maharal ben Chaviv siman 15). And if they removed him for known reasons, they do not return him except with repentance known to them.
