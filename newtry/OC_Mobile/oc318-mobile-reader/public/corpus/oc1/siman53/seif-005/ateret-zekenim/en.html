Who committed a transgression inadvertently, such as one who killed a person, etc. But if he acted intentionally, he may not serve. This applies specifically where he killed a person, in which case repentance does not enable him to become fit to serve as chazzan. If, however, in his youth he committed other transgressions between man and Hashem, even to the extent that he left the faith, and he returned in complete repentance, he is fit (Maharshal, Be'er Sheva, siman 2). It is improper to remove a permanent chazzan in favor of another merely because the latter has a sweeter voice. Rather, they should both serve together (Mahari ben Lev, siman 15). If they removed him for known reasons, they do not reinstate him unless his repentance is evident.
