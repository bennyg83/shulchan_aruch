(Note 5) And he shall be silent, etc.—according to all. And see the beginning of siman 65. Meaning: there is a dispute among poskim whether if one delayed in order to finish it entirely due to unavoidable circumstance he must return to the beginning; nevertheless, if not due to unavoidable circumstance, all agree he need not return. And one who waits for a minyan—there is no unavoidable circumstance; therefore all agree he need not return.

See there siman 483. Meaning: even in Birkat HaMazon there is doubt—see there. And in siman 582, note 70, he brought in the name of Darkei Moshe that in Hallel, which is rabbinic, even if one delayed in order to finish it entirely, he need not return to the beginning—and the same applies here.
