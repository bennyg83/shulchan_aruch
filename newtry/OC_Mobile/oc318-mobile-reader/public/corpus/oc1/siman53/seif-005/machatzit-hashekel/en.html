Seif katan 5: “And remain silent,” etc. According to all authorities. See the beginning of siman 65. Meaning, the authorities disagree whether one who paused because of circumstances beyond his control for the time needed to complete the entire passage must return to the beginning. If the pause was not caused by circumstances beyond his control, however, all agree that he need not return. Waiting for a quorum is not considered circumstances beyond his control. Therefore, according to all authorities, he need not return.
See what is written in siman 483. Meaning, even regarding Bircas HaMazon he remains uncertain; see there. In siman 502, seif katan 70, he cites Darkei Moshe that regarding Hallel, which is D'Rabbanan, even if one paused for the time needed to complete the entire Hallel, he need not return to the beginning. The same applies here.
