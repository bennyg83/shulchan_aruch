(5) [Section 3] One should not bless on wrapping tzitzit between pesukei dezimra, etc.—for even for the sake of a mitzvah it is forbidden to interrupt, as written in siman 51, seif 4. Magen Avraham, note 3. Razah, note 3. But between Yishtabach and Yotzer it is lenient—from between Baruch she'amar to Yishtabach, for there one may speak for a mitzvah, as written in siman 54, seif 3. Peri Megadim, Orach Chayyim, note 3. Razah there. And see below, note 7. And in siman 74, note 11.
