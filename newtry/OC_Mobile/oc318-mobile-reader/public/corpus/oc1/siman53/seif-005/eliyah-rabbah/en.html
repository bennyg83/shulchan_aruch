[5] Empty of transgressions, etc. Knesset HaGedolah wrote in the name of Maharit: one who violates an oath—the verse "his house is empty" applies even though witnesses have not yet testified against him; nevertheless there is reason to heed rumor and convene a beit din on such a matter. And even if he transgressed only a vow of futility for the future—which according to Rashi z"l does not disqualify—he is not fit to appoint as shliach tzibur. It is written in the Gemara in Chullin siman 69: he should not be foolish and dull but should be experienced in communal affairs as needed. Abudraham wrote: that which they are accustomed to call the shliach tzibur "chazzan"—because in Targum "vayar" (and he saw) is "v'chaza," meaning he must see how to read—up to here. And see below siman 142 further.
