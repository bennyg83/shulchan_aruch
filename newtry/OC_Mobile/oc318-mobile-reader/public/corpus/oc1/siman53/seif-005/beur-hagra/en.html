Section 65 — if there is no, etc. From what is written, "and the rabbis were given authority over him," etc.

And if there was, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. "even though," etc.; and the same applies here.

One who transgressed, etc. See Magen Avraham in the name of D.M.; and as Rabbeinu Tam wrote regarding one to whom a sin came by chance and he repented — on this they said, "in a place where those who repent stand," etc.

But, etc. Mishnah, chapter 3 of Megillah 24a; and see Tosafos there, s.v. "and he is not," etc.; and that which is taught, etc.; and likewise at the end of chapter 3 of Sukkah, and in Chullin there.
