Seif 5, if there is no, etc. From what is stated there, 'and the Rabbis cast their eyes upon him, etc.'
And if he was, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. although, etc.; and the same applies here.
One who transgressed, etc. See Magen Avraham in the name of the Darchei Moshe. This is as Rabbeinu Tam wrote, that where a transgression came to his hand incidentally and he repented from it, concerning this they said: in the place where penitents stand, etc.
But, etc. This is from the Mishnah in the third chapter of Megillah, 24a. See Tosafot there, s.v. and he does not, etc., and that which it teaches, etc.; and so it is written at the end of the third chapter of Sukkah and there in Chullin.
