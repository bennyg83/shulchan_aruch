Nevertheless, an adverse reputation has attached to him. See Mishnah Berurah regarding what I wrote in the name of Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah. I ruled without qualification in accordance with them, even though many other poskim hold that the requirement that no adverse reputation have attached to him applies even throughout the year, as is implied by Knesses HaGedolah and, even more clearly, by Biur HaGra. I nevertheless ruled in accordance with Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah because Rama's ruling, that a person who intentionally committed a transgression even once is deemed to have acquired an adverse reputation despite having repented, is itself unclear. Magen Avraham already questioned it, and Biur HaGra likewise appears to disagree in his commentary; see there. The responsum of Or Zarua itself also appears to disagree, upon examination. Rama's words are therefore his own view. It is astonishing that the printers inserted into the text, in the name of Responsa Or Zarua, the opposite of what is written there concerning an intentional transgression; see there. Alternatively, Rama may have found this view in some other posek.
