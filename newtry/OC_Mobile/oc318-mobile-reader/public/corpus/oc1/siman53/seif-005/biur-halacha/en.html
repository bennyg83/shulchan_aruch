Nevertheless, a bad reputation went out against him — see Mishna Berurah in what I wrote in the name of Magen Avraham and R' Akiva Eiger on this, and I ruled in accordance with their words, even though many other poskim hold that the law that a bad reputation should not have gone out against him applies even throughout the year, as implied in Knesset HaGedolah, and more than this in Biur HaGra. Nevertheless, I ruled like them, because this law of Rama — who holds that on the first time one intentionally commits a transgression, even though he repented it is called that a bad reputation went out against him — is likewise not clear, for Magen Avraham already challenged him, and so the Gra appears to hold in his commentary, see there; and so it appears from the responsum of R' Akiva Eiger himself for one who examines it. And Rama's words are his own opinion [and it is surprising that they printed inside in the name of the responsum of R' Akiva Eiger the opposite regarding intentional sin, see there], or perhaps he found some posek.
