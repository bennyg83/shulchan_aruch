There is, see Taz, Magen Avraham, seif katan 12, and Beit Yosef. In our chiddushim to Megillah 24a we asked that below, siman 186, Magen Avraham, seif katan 3, rules that even one obligated on two Rabbinic levels may discharge one obligated on a single Rabbinic level. Nevertheless, one younger than thirteen may not lead the service. Even if Krias Shema itself is not discharged by another person and only its blessings and the Amidah are Rabbinic, we still require the prayer of an adult man, based on the verse, “Why did I come and there was no man,” or because it is beneath the congregation's dignity for a minor to discharge their obligation. Maariv is different, for there is no comparable communal obligation there and he does not discharge them. See Beit Yosef in the name of Kol Bo citing Rabbi Nassan, and Hagahos Levush. Regarding today's responsive portions of Krias Shema, in which only Kaddish and Barechu are recited, there is room to question whether a minor mourner may lead them. This will be explained, with Hashem's help, in siman 69.
