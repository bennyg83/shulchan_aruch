There is — Taz; and Magen Avraham letter 12; and Avudraham on this. And in our novellae on tractate Megillah 24 we challenged: it is established below in siman 286, Magen Avraham letter 3, that even two [people of] Rabbinic [obligation] — one of Rabbinic [obligation] dispenses — nevertheless one does not go down before the ark less than thirteen [years]; even though it is said that Kriat Shema — one does not discharge his fellow, only from blessings and prayer of Rabbinic [obligation] — nevertheless we require "the prayer of a man," as it is said, "Why have I come and there is no man?" or because there is no honor of the congregation that a minor should discharge them. Unlike Maariv — there is no obligation there and one does not discharge. And Avudraham in the name of Kol Bo in the name of Rabbi Natan, and in Levush hagahot; and regarding poreis al Shema nowadays, when they say only Kaddish and Barechu — one should be uncertain whether a minor mourner may do this; and there, in siman 69, it will be explained.
