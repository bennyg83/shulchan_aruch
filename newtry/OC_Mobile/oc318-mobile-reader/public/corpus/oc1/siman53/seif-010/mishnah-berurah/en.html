(30) Maariv prayer — because they do not discharge the multitude from their obligation, for they only repeat the prayer when they say Barechu and Kaddish. And other reasons — see Beit Yosef; and all this is strained only to uphold the custom, but he himself holds it is not proper l'chatchila to do so:

(31) On Motzei Shabbos — the same applies to weekday Maariv, except that the custom practiced in those places was on Motzei Shabbos:

(32) In Maariv prayer — because of Barechu in it; and the same applies that he should not lead Keriat Shema for one who did not hear Barechu at Shacharit, for in truth Barechu too is a mitzvah for the entire congregation to hear morning and evening, and a minor who is not obligated does not discharge them with this:

(33) For behold he is still [a minor] — meaning: in places where they are accustomed to pray the Shabbat service on erev Shabbos while it is still day, because of tosefet Shabbos, nevertheless his thirteen years are not yet completed until Shabbat night, for tosefet Shabbos does not help regarding the years of a youth. But if he prays at night — Maariv after nightfall — it is permitted for him to go down before the ark. And what was said "in every place, thirteen years and one day" — not that he needs one day more, but rather meaning that he needs thirteen complete years from day to day, excluding a shortfall of some hours; therefore immediately at the beginning of the night his years are completed and he becomes an adult. And even if he was born on Rosh Hashanah day before bein hashmashos, he becomes bar mitzvah at the beginning of the night of Rosh Hashanah of his fourteenth year:
