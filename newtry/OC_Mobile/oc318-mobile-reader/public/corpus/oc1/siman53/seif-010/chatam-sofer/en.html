In the text [of Maharal] to spread Torah, NB; and therefore what Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah end of siman 244, 'if one held authority'—refers that his sons precede, but not rabbanut of Torah, as I explained in my responsa in Orach Chayyim siman 2. And in the responsum of R' Shaul at the end of the book of responsa of Maharam of Lublin, Amsterdam edition, which is lost—from it this distinction between spreading Torah and other appointments. And in responsa Ginat Veradim, Yoreh De'ah part, siman 7 and siman 8, he disagrees on this—examine there.
