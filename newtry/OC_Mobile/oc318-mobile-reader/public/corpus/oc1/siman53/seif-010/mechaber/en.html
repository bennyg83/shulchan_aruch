One should judge favorably places where the custom is that minors go down before the ark to pray the evening prayer on Motza'ei Shabbatot.And in places where they did not practice thus, a minor should not pass before the ark—even for the' evening prayer. And even if he reached the category of thirteen years on Shabbat day, he may not pray the evening prayer of Shabbat—for behold he still does not have thirteen years [Maharil].]:
