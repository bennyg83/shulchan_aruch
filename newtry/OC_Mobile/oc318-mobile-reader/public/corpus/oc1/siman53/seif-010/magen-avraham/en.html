Maariv on Motzaei Shabbos. For he is not discharging the public from their obligation, since we do not repeat the prayer, and he only says Kaddish and Barchu.
He does not have thirteen. This means that in our country they pray Maariv while it is still day, and he does not enter his fourteenth year until the beginning of Friday night, which is the day on which he was born. But if they pray Maariv at night, he is permitted to pass before the ark. And that which we say everywhere, thirteen years and one day, means that we require thirteen complete years. Since the day on which he was born begins, it is called one day, Bach. It seems to me that this is also the opinion of the Rama; see what I wrote in siman 55, seif 9. So too the Shach wrote at length in Choshen Mishpat, siman 35, and in the Sema.
