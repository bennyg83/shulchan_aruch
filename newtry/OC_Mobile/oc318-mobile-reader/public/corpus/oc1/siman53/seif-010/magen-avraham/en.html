Maariv on Motza'ei Shabbat. For he does not fulfill the congregation's obligation, since they do not repeat the prayer—only Kaddish and Barchu are said.

He does not have thirteen [years]. Explanation: in our country they pray Maariv from plag hamincha, and he does not enter his fourteenth year until the beginning of Shabbat night, which is the day he was born; but if they pray Maariv at night, it is permitted for him to pass before the ark. And what we say in Kol Bo, "thirteen years and one day"—means he requires thirteen complete years; and since the day on which he was born begins, we count it as day one (Beit Chadash). And I hold that Rama's view is likewise, as I wrote in siman 55, seif 9; and so wrote Shach in Choshen Mishpat siman 35 at length, and in Semag.
