10) There, in the gloss: If no minyan is present in the synagogue, the shaliach tzibbur should wait at Yishtabach, etc. An individual, however, should recite Yishtabach immediately. Likewise, on Shabbos, if he completes Pesukei DeZimrah before the shaliach tzibbur, he should say Yishtabach immediately.
