Already — Taz; and see Lekush. Now an individual cannot protest, and they accept a shaliach tzibbur through the majority of tax payers, and they are chosen (or per the seven good men of the city), and see Eshel Avraham 381. See Eliyah Rabbah letter 23: the shaliach tzibbur stands in place of a kohen who offers the daily offerings and is called karov; and R' Eliezer was called karov, and the piyyutim were called keruvot (in Beit Yaakov) — they offer Israel to their Father in Heaven — and they too are in keruv and in the middle of prayer.
