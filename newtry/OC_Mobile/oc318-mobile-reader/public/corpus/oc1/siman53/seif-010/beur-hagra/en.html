Seif 10, one may learn, etc. For the Raavad explained the above to mean that a minor may not pass before the ark, etc., even though prayer is D'Rabbanan and one who is obligated D'Rabbanan can discharge another who is obligated D'Rabbanan, as stated in the third chapter of Berachos. Rather, it is because of the honor of the congregation, and therefore whenever they waive their honor, it is permitted. But since Rashi explains it as because he is not obligated, one may say that this case is different because it is voluntary, and also because the prayer is not repeated. See the Beit Yosef, for all this is strained in order to uphold the custom. This is what is stated in the gloss, 'and in places, etc.' See above, siman 186, Magen Avraham seif katan 3.
