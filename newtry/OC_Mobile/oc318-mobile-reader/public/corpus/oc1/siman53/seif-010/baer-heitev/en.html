Year. Meaning, in our countries they pray Ma'ariv while it is still day, and he does not enter his fourteenth year until the beginning of the night of Shabbos, which is the day on which he was born. But if they pray Ma'ariv at night, he may pass before the ark. And that which we say everywhere, thirteen years and one day, means that we require thirteen complete years, and once the day on which he was born begins, it is called one day with respect to him, Bach, Magen Avraham, Taz, Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah. So wrote the Shach in Choshen Mishpat, siman 35, and the Sema there. See Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, siman 55, in the glosses to the Tur, seif 6, who brings in the name of a manuscript responsum of Mahari Bruna that even a boy of thirteen years and one day is not called an adult until that day passes, and he brought proofs for this; see there.
