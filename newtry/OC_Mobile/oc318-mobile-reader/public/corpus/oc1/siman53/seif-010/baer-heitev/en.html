**year.** Meaning that in our country they pray Maariv while it is still day, and he does not enter his fourteenth year until the beginning of the night of Shabbat on which he was born. But if they pray Maariv at night, it is permitted for him to go down before the ark. And that which we say everywhere "thirteen years and one day" means that we require thirteen full years, and since the day on which he was born begins, one day we call him an adult (Beit Chadash; Magen Avraham; Taz; Knesset HaGedolah; and likewise Shach in Choshen Mishpat siman 35 and Sema there). And see in Shiurei Knesset HaGedolah siman 55 in the gloss on the Tur, seif 6, which cites in the name of responsum 21 of Mahari Bruna that he wrote that even a boy of thirteen years and one day is not called an adult until that day passes, and he brought proofs on this, see there.
