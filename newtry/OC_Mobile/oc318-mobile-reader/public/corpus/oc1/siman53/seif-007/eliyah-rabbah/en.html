[7] Pleasing to the people, etc.—meaning humble and easy with creatures, etc.—for whoever [the spirit of] creatures is at ease with him, the spirit of the Omnipresent is at ease with him. Responsum of Maharam Mintz [siman 81]: the chazzan should beware—if there is quarrel and strife between two householders, he should be passive and not act, neither in speech nor in deed, neither openly nor in secret; and all the more so if there is a dispute with a gentile and a householder—he should be passive and not act, even if that gentile is his father-in-law or relative. If he has any quarrel or hatred with people or one of the congregation, then he must present the matter to the rabbi or the congregation for mediation and to do whatever seems right to them; and if there is deficiency and obstruction from the opposing side, then he is innocent—what can he do?
