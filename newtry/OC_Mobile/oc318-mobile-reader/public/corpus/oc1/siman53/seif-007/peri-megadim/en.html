That he should not — Taz in siman 141 letter 3: a blind person goes up to the Torah and blesses, and the shaliach tzibbur reads the Torah; but a blind person does not say by heart things that are written — "you are not permitted [to recite] by heart." And the author's language here is difficult for me, for it implies that if he learned by rote it is permitted — see siman 49; and it is established that one letter not from the script is forbidden — see siman 139, that Torah reading in public is specifically from a kosher sefer Torah, and even chumashim — all the more to read by heart. It is true that Rambam, chapter 12 of Laws of Prayer halakhah 8, wrote that it is forbidden to read one letter of Gittin, things that are written, etc.; and it requires study now — see what Eshel Avraham wrote from Taanit, see there.
