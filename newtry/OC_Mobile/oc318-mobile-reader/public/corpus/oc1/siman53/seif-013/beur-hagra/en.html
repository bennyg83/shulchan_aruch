Section 13 — "And he," etc.: Thus wrote the Rif there — he is one whose garments are torn and whose shoulders and arms are bare, such that the garment on him is like a belt under his armpits and below, as stated: "And you shall open the sack," etc.; and the Targum of Yonatan: "Go and remove the sack from your loins," and it is written "and he did so, walking naked and barefoot"; and the Targum Onkelos: "pocheach," etc. — thus he is girded at his waist and his shoulders and arms are bare. And in Yerushalmi, end of chapter 1 of Moed Katan: if a death occurred to him, he tears; if another death occurred to him, he moves three fingers away and tears his garment — from before him he begins from behind, from above he begins from below, from below he begins from above; these and those — R' Chiya bar Abba of Jaffa said: let us act as pocheach; and they say explicitly in Yoma 37a, etc. And it follows from the Rif's words that specifically his shoulders too must be bare — and such is the Tur's language.
