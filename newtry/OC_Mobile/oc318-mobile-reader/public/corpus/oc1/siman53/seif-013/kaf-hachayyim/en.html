13) A group reciting Tehillim in the synagogue that did not have a minyan before Baruch She'amar may not recite Kaddish in the middle of Pesukei DeZimrah. Responsa Pnei Yehoshua, Orach Chayyim, siman 5; Yad Aharon; Shalmei Tzibbur; Kesher Gudal; Beit Oved. Likewise, if there was no minyan before Hodu and the Kaddish ordinarily recited before Hodu was therefore omitted, once they have begun Baruch She'amar and Pesukei DeZimrah they should no longer interrupt Pesukei DeZimrah in order to recite that Kaddish. This is implied by Shaar HaKavanos, discourse concerning Kaddish, which explains that Kaddish was instituted between each spiritual realm and the next. Once its proper place has passed, there is no reason to interrupt Pesukei DeZimrah in order to say it. Pnei Yehoshua and Beit Oved write likewise, unlike Mahari Azulai; see Shaarei Teshuvah, siman 51, letter 3. Likewise, in a place where mourners customarily recite the Kaddish after Yishtabach, if 1 mourner has not yet said Yishtabach and is still in the middle of Pesukei DeZimrah, he should not interrupt in order to recite that Kaddish. According to Shaar HaKavanos, the principal Kaddish that benefits the deceased is the final Kaddish before Aleinu. Furthermore, Kaddish was instituted between spiritual realms, and this person has not yet completed the rectification of the spiritual level in which he presently stands so as to elevate it. Rav Pealim, part 2, siman 14, writes that because he recites the Kaddish for the congregation, he may do so. In my humble opinion this is not correct. Since he has not yet completed the rectification and elevation of his own prayer, how can he elevate the prayers of others? Even a shaliach tzibbur elevates the prayers of others together with his own. If his own has not yet been rectified, how can he rectify others? Rav Pealim further writes that once the mourner has begun the zemirot he already has a connection with Yetzirah and may therefore say Kaddish. This too appears incorrect. The ascent of the 7 heichalot of Yetzirah to their own Holy of Holies occurs through the 5 Hallelujah psalms, and through Halleluhu El Bekodsho they ascend to the Holy of Holies, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos, Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaZemirot, chapter 6, and the siddur of the Rashash. Since their rectification has not yet been completed so as to ascend to their Holy of Holies, how can they ascend to Beriah through Kaddish? Since the rectification of Yetzirah itself has not been completed, how can one rise from it? Therefore the principal ruling is not to interrupt.
