13) A group reciting Tehillim in the synagogue that did not have a minyan before Baruch She'amar may not recite Kaddish during Pesukei DeZimrah. Responsa Pnei Yehoshua, Orach Chaim 5; Yad Aharon, Hagahos Beis Yosef; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 67c; Kesher Gudal 7:36; and Siddur Beis Yaakov 12. Similarly, if no minyan was present before Hodu and the Kaddish preceding Hodu was not recited, but Baruch She'amar and Pesukei DeZimrah have begun, Kaddish should no longer be recited during Pesukei DeZimrah. This is implied by Shaar HaKavanos, Derush HaKaddish, which says that Kaddish was instituted between each world and the next; see there. Since its time has passed, one need not interrupt Pesukei DeZimrah to recite it. Pnei Yehoshua, ibid., writes likewise. Siddur Beis Yaakov 13 agrees, unlike Mahari Azulai; Shaarei Teshuvah 51:3. Likewise, in a place where orphans customarily recite the Kaddish after Yishtabach, if one of them has not yet recited Yishtabach and is still in Pesukei DeZimrah, he should not interrupt to recite Kaddish. The principal Kaddish that benefits the deceased is specifically the final Kaddish before Aleinu, as Shaar HaKavanos states there. He should therefore not interrupt Pesukei DeZimrah for this Kaddish. Moreover, Kaddish was instituted only between one world and the next. He has not yet completed the aspect of that world in order to elevate it, as is known. Rav Pe'alim 2:14 writes that since he recites it for the congregation, doing so is permitted. In my humble opinion, this is incorrect. Since he has not yet completed the rectification of his own prayer in order to elevate it, how can he elevate the prayers of others? Even the shaliach tzibbur elevates others' prayers only together with his own; if his has not yet been rectified, how can he rectify others? Rav Pe'alim further writes that because he began the songs, he has a connection to the world of Yetzirah and may recite Kaddish. This too appears incorrect. The ascent through the seven palaces of Yetzirah to their Holy of Holies occurs through the five Halleluyahs, and through 'Praise God in His holiness' they ascend to the Holy of Holies, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos and Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaZemiros, chapter 6, and explained in Rashash's siddur. Since their rectification to ascend to their Holy of Holies is not yet complete, how can they ascend to Beriah through Kaddish? Moreover, since the rectification of Yetzirah is not complete, how can it ascend? The principal ruling is therefore not to interrupt.
