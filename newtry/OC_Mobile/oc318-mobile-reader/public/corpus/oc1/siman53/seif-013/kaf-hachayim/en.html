(13) A group who say Tehillim in the beis haknesset, and it happened they had no minyan before Baruch She'amar—it is forbidden to say Kaddish within pesukei dezimra. Responsum Penei Yehoshua, Orach Chayyim siman 5. Yad Aharon in Hagahot Beit Yosef. Shaarei Teshuvah 273 side g. Kissei Eliyahu, siman 7, note 36. Siddur Beit Yosef, note 12. And likewise if there was no minyan before Hodu and they did not say the Kaddish before Hodu and began to say Baruch She'amar and pesukei dezimra—one does not say Kaddish again within pesukei dezimra, as implied from Sha'ar HaKavanot, derush on Kaddish, who wrote that Kaddish was enacted between each world and world—see there. If so, since its time has passed, one need not interrupt and say it within pesukei dezimra; and so wrote Penei Yehoshua there. And Beit Yosef agreed, note 13, and wrote—and not like Mahari Azulai z"l, Sha'arei Teshuvah siman 51 note 3. And likewise in a place where orphans are accustomed to say the Kaddish after Yishtabach, and it happened that one had not yet said Yishtabach but was still within pesukei dezimra—he should not interrupt to say Kaddish, because the main Kaddish beneficial for the dead is specifically the final Kaddish said before "Let us praise"—as written in Sha'ar HaKavanot there. If so, one should not interrupt to say this Kaddish within pesukei dezimra. And further, they enacted Kaddish only between world and world, and he has not yet finished that world-aspect in order to elevate it, as is known. And what Rav Pe'alim wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 14: since he says it for the congregation—one can say according to his view that since he has not yet completed correcting his prayer to elevate it, how can he elevate others' prayers? For even a shliach tzibur who elevates others' prayers does so by way of his own prayer; and if he is not yet corrected, how does he correct others? Also what he wrote there afterward: since he began in zemirot, he has connection to the world of Yetzirah and can say Kaddish—or because the ascent through the seven halls of Yetzirah to their hall of Kodesh Kodashim is in "Hallelujah" and "Hallelu El bekadsho" until Kodesh Kodashim, as written in Sha'ar HaKavanot and in Pardes, Sha'ar HaZemirot chapter 6, and explained in Siddur Rashash z"l. If so, since their correction to ascend to their hall of Kodesh Kodashim is not yet finished, how will they ascend to the world of Beriah through Kaddish? And also, since correction of the world of Yetzirah is not yet finished, how does he ascend? Therefore the main point is not to interrupt.
