The wages of the prayer-leader should be taken from the public funds of the congregation. Even though the prayer-leader is fulfilling [the prayer obligations] of the poor like the rich, nevertheless, the hand of poor doesn't pay as [much as] the rich. {Rama: Rem"a: And there are those who say that we collect [from the members] half [of the total funds] according to [each person's] wealth and half according to the [number of] people (ie. a set amount that each person gives), and so is the custom of the congregations (Maharam Padua Siman 44).}
