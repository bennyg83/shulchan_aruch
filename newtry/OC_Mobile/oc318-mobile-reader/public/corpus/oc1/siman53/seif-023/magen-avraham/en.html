The wages of the shaliach tzibbur. Anything that is a communal enactment and is carried out through money is paid according to wealth. In the Darkei Moshe: the custom of our country is that poor and rich pay equally, but he has a fixed payment from every wedding, and this is according to wealth. The Levush wrote that in a place where there is no custom, they collect according to wealth. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 163, end of seif 3, in the gloss.
