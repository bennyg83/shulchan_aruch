Shaliach tzibbur wages. And everything that is a communal ordinance and is done through money—they give according to money. In Darchei Moshe: the custom in our countries is that poor and rich give equally; but he has a quota from every marriage, and this is according to money. And Levush wrote that where there is no custom, they collect according to money—Choshen Mishpat siman 163, seif 66 in the hagahah.
