Section 23 — wages, etc.: For whatever is a communal enactment and is done through money, they collect according to wealth. Rashba.

And some say, etc.: As stated in Bava Kamma 116b — "and if they hired him," etc.; and the Rosh and Tur explain that half is according to wealth and half according to persons; and see Choshen Mishpat siman 163, seif 3. And the same applies here, for prayer — which is the tamid and musaf offerings that come from the half-shekel, poor as rich, as stated in chapter 4 of Shekalim — and likewise Kaddish and Kedushah, in which all are obligated. But in other matters for which they also hire the chazzan, such as at weddings and festivals, which are like matters that come from a donation, and likewise when they choose one whose voice is more pleasant — this is according to wealth; and therefore half, etc. And Maharil siman 66 wrote: because they needed to travel elsewhere and they would endanger lives and money; and the first version holds it is only monetary danger, as stated in Hagahot Maimoniot, chapter 1 of the laws of prayer — see there; and as stated at the beginning of Bava Kamma there; and likewise in chapter 1 of Bava Batra — see there; and in Mordechai, chapter 1 of Bava Batra. And see below siman 55, where Shulchan Aruch wrote per the gloss's view here.
