Seif 23, the salary, etc. For anything that is an enactment of the community and is carried out through money is collected according to wealth. This is from the Rashba.
And some say, etc. As stated in Bava Kamma 116b: and if they hired, etc. The Rosh and the Tur explained that half is according to wealth and half according to persons; see Choshen Mishpat, siman 163, seif 3. The same applies here, because the prayer corresponds to the daily offerings and mussaf offerings, which came from the half-shekel, where the poor person and the wealthy person were equal, as stated in the fourth chapter of Shekalim. The same applies to Kaddish and Kedushah, in which everyone is obligated. But other matters for which the chazzan is also hired, such as weddings and festivals, are like matters that come as a voluntary offering; and similarly, where they choose him because his voice is more pleasant, that is according to wealth. Therefore, half, etc. The Maharil, siman 66, wrote that this is because they would need to go to another place and would be endangering lives and money. The first opinion maintains that it is only monetary danger, as written in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 11 of Hilchos Tefillah; see there. This is as stated at the beginning of Bava Kamma there, and likewise in the first chapter of Bava Basra; see there, and in the Mordechai in the first chapter of Bava Basra.
