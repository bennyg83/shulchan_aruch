The wages of the shaliach tzibbur. In siman 55, seif 21, where he rules that half is according to wealth and half according to persons, see Eliyah Rabbah, who distinguishes that a chazzan is different; see there. In Biur HaGra it implies that the Mechaber retracted and ruled there like the gloss here.
From the communal fund. Which is collected according to wealth.
Half, etc. For there is reason to say that the poor person needs the chazzan just as the wealthy person does. Sometimes the wealthy give more money to the chazzan whose voice is more pleasant. Also, the poor person can go to another place. Therefore they made this compromise.
The communities. The Darkei Moshe wrote that the custom of our lands is that poor and wealthy give equally, and this is the half according to persons; and from every marriage he receives according to the dowry, and this is the half according to wealth. The Levush wrote that in a place with no custom, they should collect according to wealth; see Choshen Mishpat, siman 163, seif 1 in the gloss. Magen Avraham. See the Darkei Moshe, from which it implies that even according to the poskim who rule according to wealth, this is only regarding hiring the shaliach tzibbur whom they hire for the Days of Awe. There they hold it is according to wealth, because the wealthy, due to their wealth, cannot leave their houses and leave them empty, whereas the poor can go for those times to another city. But regarding a shaliach tzibbur hired for the entire year, we do not calculate according to wealth alone.
