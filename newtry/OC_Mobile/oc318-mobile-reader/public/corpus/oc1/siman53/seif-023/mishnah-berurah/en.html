(67) Wages of a sheliach tzibbur — and in siman 55 seif kaf alef he rules half according to wealth and half according to persons; see Eliyahu Rabbah, who distinguishes that for a cantor it is different — see there; and in Biur HaGra it implies that the author retracted and ruled there as the gloss here.

(68) From the congregation fund — which is collected according to wealth.

(69) "Half," etc. — for there is reason to say that the poor person needs a cantor like the rich, and sometimes the rich give more money to a cantor whose voice is sweeter; also the poor person can go elsewhere; therefore they made this compromise.

(70) The communities — in Darkei Moshe he wrote that the custom in our countries is that poor and rich give equally; and this is the half according to persons; and also from every marriage he receives according to his dowry — and that is the half according to wealth. Levush wrote that where there is no custom they collect according to wealth; see Choshen Mishpat siman 163 seif 1 in the gloss. [Magen Avraham] And see Darkei Moshe, for it implies from him that even the view of the poskim who rule according to wealth applies only to hiring a sheliach tzibbur for the Days of Awe — for in that case the reasoning of wealth applies, because the rich, on account of their wealth, cannot leave their homes and leave them empty, while the poor can go at those times to another city; not so a sheliach tzibbur hired for the whole year — they do not calculate by wealth alone.
