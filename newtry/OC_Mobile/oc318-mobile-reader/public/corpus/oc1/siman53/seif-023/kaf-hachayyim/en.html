23) The halachic authorities write that among the qualities required of a shaliach tzibbur is that he forgo his claims against others. Taanit 25b relates that R' Eliezer descended before the lectern and was not answered; R' Akiva descended, said Avinu Malkeinu, and was answered. A heavenly voice declared: It is not because this one is greater than the other, but because this one yields concerning his personal claims and the other does not. Solel Boneh, letter 2; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 71b. He must also not be a flatterer. Sotah 41b states that R' Elazar said that anyone who possesses flattery brings wrath, as it is written, "Those who flatter in heart incur wrath." Moreover, his prayer is not heard, as it says, "They do not cry out when He binds them." It is likewise written, "For what is the hope of a flatterer when he gains," and, "Will God hear his cry when distress comes upon him?" Mahari Abuhav writes that 1 category of flatterers consists of people who perform good deeds so that others will honor them. Such spiritual filth corrupts a person's entire service. There are people who pray with a pleasant voice while thinking during prayer how pleasant their voice is and how many listeners derive pleasure from hearing them. Shalmei Tzibbur; Solel Boneh. Shaar HaKedushah by Rabbi Chaim Vital, part 2, gate 4, writes that one who publicizes his good deeds before people not only receives no reward for them, but may also be punished. See there.
