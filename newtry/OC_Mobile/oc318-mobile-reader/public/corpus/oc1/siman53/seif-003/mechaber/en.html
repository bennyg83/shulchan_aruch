One should not bless on wrapping tzitzit between pesukei dezimra and Yishtabach, but between Yishtabach and Yotzer [and see above, siman 54, seif 3].[If the shaliach tzibbur did not have a tallit at first, he should wrap in tzitzit before he begins Yishtabach so that he says Kaddish immediately after Yishtabach and does not interrupt [Kol Bo siman 5].
