One should not say the bracha on the wrapping of the tzitzit [i.e. putting on a tallit] between P'sukei D'Zimra and Yishtabach [i.e. the closing blessing], rather, he should say it between Yishtabach and Yotzer [Ohr] [and see below 54:3]. {Rama: Rem"a: If the leader did not have a tallit at the beginning, he should wrap in tzitzit before he begins Yishtabach so that he will say the Kaddish immediately after Yishtabach and not interrupt [Kol Bo 5], and similarly if there is no minyan in the synagogue, the leader should wait at Yishtabach and be silent until the minyan comes and then say Yishtabach and Kaddish [Maharil].}
