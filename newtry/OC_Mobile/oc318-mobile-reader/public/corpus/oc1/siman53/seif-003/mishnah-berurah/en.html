(5) One should not bless — even if he did not have a tallit until now; and even though he is between the chapters, nevertheless since the blessing applies to the verses of Pesukei deZimra, it is not fitting that there be an interruption between them and its concluding blessing, for a blessing should be adjacent to the mitzvah. But before that, in Pesukei deZimra — at least between the chapters he puts on the tallit and blesses; and all the more so regarding tefillin, for it is permitted to bless on them between the chapters, especially if he is among many people and is embarrassed to sit without tallit and tefillin:

(6) Tzitzit — and the same applies regarding tefillin [author].

(7) Between Yishtabach and Yotzer — all this is specifically only if he did not have one beforehand, for otherwise this too is forbidden; and this is what Rama pointed out. See below, etc.

(8) However, the shaliach tzibbur, etc. — for it is unseemly for the congregation to wait for him between Yishtabach and Kaddish, and Kaddish too depends on Yishtabach; therefore he should wrap himself immediately in a tallit, and the same for tefillin, and bless. But if they did not bring him the tallit until after he finished Yishtabach, before he said Kaddish, he may put it on there and bless, and the congregation will wait, since it is impossible otherwise—and as will be explained below at the end of siman 54 in the gloss; see there.

(9) The congregation waits for the shaliach tzibbur — so that he can adjoin Kaddish to him immediately afterward; but an individual says Yishtabach immediately. And likewise on Shabbat, if he finished Pesukei deZimra before the shaliach tzibbur, he says Yishtabach immediately.

(10) And he should be silent — meaning he should not interrupt in speech and can wait half an hour with Yishtabach; and even if he delayed because of unavoidable circumstance in order to finish it all, he need not return to the beginning.

(11) Yishtabach and Kaddish — and after the fact, if he also finished the blessing of Yishtabach and then a minyan came, they say at least three verses from Pesukei deZimra, and the shaliach tzibbur can say Kaddish; for otherwise one may not say Kaddish, since they were not present at the time of saying some of Pesukei deZimra or Yishtabach at least.
