One should not recite a blessing. Even if he did not have a tallis until now. Although this is between sections, nevertheless, since the blessing refers to Pesukei D'Zimrah, it is not proper for there to be an interruption between them and their concluding blessing, for the blessing ought to be adjacent to the mitzvah. But earlier than this, during Pesukei D'Zimrah, he may at least between sections put on the tallis and recite the blessing. This is all the more so regarding tefillin, where it is permitted to recite the blessing over them between sections, especially if he is among the public and is embarrassed to sit without a tallis and tefillin.
Tzitzis. The same applies regarding tefillin. Eliyah Rabbah.
Between Yishtabach and Yotzer. All this applies specifically if he did not have it earlier, for otherwise even this is forbidden. This is why the Rama noted: see below, etc.
However, the shaliach tzibbur, etc. For it is disgraceful for the congregation to wait for him between Yishtabach and Kaddish, and the Kaddish also refers back to Yishtabach. Therefore he should immediately wrap himself in the tallis, and the same applies to tefillin, and recite the blessing. But if they did not bring him the tallis until after he completed Yishtabach, before he said Kaddish, he may put it on there and recite the blessing, and the congregation should wait, since it cannot be otherwise, as will be explained below at the end of siman 54 in the gloss; see there.
The shaliach tzibbur should wait. So that afterward he will be able to join Kaddish immediately to it. But an individual should say Yishtabach immediately. Likewise, on Shabbos, if he completed Pesukei D'Zimrah before the shaliach tzibbur, he should say Yishtabach immediately.
And remain silent. Meaning that he should not interrupt with speech. He may wait half an hour after Yishtabach, and even if he paused because of compulsion long enough to complete all of it, he need not return to the beginning.
Yishtabach and Kaddish. After the fact, if he also completed the blessing of Yishtabach and then a minyan arrived, they should at least say three verses from Pesukei D'Zimrah, and the shaliach tzibbur may say Kaddish. Otherwise Kaddish should not be said, since they were not present during the saying of at least some of Pesukei D'Zimrah or Yishtabach.
