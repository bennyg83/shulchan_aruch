**to say Yishtabach.** See in responsum Ginat Veradim, part Orach Chayyim, general rule 1, siman 51, and in Shiurei Knesset HaGedolah, and in responsum Mateh Yosef part 2 siman 11: one who needed to relieve himself within the verses of pesukei dezimra and went and did his needs and returned should wash his hands and bless, and put on tzitzit and tefillin and bless on them (Ginat Veradim, part Orach Chayyim, general rule 1, siman 51 side b, see there). And according to what was written above in siman 25, note 16, in the name of Magen Avraham, it implies that he need not bless; and see above siman 8, seif 14. A group that says Tehillim in the beis haknesses, and it happened that there was no minyan before Baruch She'amar—it is forbidden to say Kaddish within the verses of pesukei dezimra (responsum Penei Yehoshua, part Orach Chayyim siman 5; and see above siman 51, note 3, in the name of Makor Baruch).

**the shliach tzibur.** But the individual says Yishtabach immediately; and likewise on Shabbat, if he finishes the verses of pesukei dezimra before the shliach tzibur, he says Yishtabach immediately. Magen Avraham.

**and be silent.** Meaning that he should not interrupt with speech and may wait half an hour with Yishtabach (Maharam Mintz). And Magen Avraham wrote: it seems to me that even if he waited because of unavoidable circumstance in order to finish it all, he need not return to the beginning.
