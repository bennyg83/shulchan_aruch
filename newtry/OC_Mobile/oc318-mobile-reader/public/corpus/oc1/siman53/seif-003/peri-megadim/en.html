But — Taz. Tosafot in Sukkah 42a, s.v. hayodea, and Megillah 24a, s.v. ve'eino — it is not so, for a fast day is occasional like fixed, and one does not go down unless his beard is filled (and as Rash and Rashba wrote — I have not found it now, only that all year on a fixed [basis] it is forbidden, but not on a fast occasionally). And Magen Avraham 9: on a fast we require a real beard, etc. Behold, in Eshel Avraham on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur it is explained that at most thirty [years suffices]; and fasts in Israel for rain are stricter; and further, there itself only a more praiseworthy elder is [required] (an unspecified elder is sixty), but if [the beard] is filled it also suffices — and so too Rambam, chapter 4 of Taanit, see there. And as he wrote: better to draw near, etc. — Magen Avraham letter 8. And on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, if his beard is not filled — whether the congregation's waiver helps — see Eliyah Rabbah letter 10; and it is like a double doubt: perhaps a fast is not fixed, as implied from Tosafot Chullin (for Beit Yosef's understanding), and perhaps waiver helps; nevertheless one should not be lenient — see Eshel Avraham. And on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur it is good to take a righteous shaliach tzibbur, son of a righteous man; and there is no proof from despised family members, for nevertheless they are righteous. One who is disqualified due to a sin — even occasionally it is forbidden — Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Mishpat Tzedek; and this is when he did not repent.
