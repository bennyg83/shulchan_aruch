He should not recite the blessing. See Mishnah Berurah regarding what I wrote concerning between the sections. It is from the novellae of Rabbi Akiva Eiger in the name of Levush. Although he concludes that the practical ruling requires study, Chayei Adam likewise writes that one should put on the tallis without a blessing until after Yishtabach, when he should touch it and recite the blessing. See Birkei Yosef and Magen Gibborim, who concurred with this as the practical ruling, because they found an explicit responsum of Rambam on this matter. According to this, it is certainly permitted at least to recite Asher Yatzar between the sections. See Chayei Adam, who remains uncertain about this. Therefore, one who needed to relieve himself in the middle of a section of Pesukei DeZimrah, and went and attended to his needs, should wash his hands, finish that section of Pesukei DeZimrah, recite Asher Yatzar, put on his tallis without reciting a blessing, as explained in siman 8, seif 14 and Mishnah Berurah there, and put on tefillin with their blessings. Since one may not enter a privy while wearing tefillin, the original blessing was displaced, as explained above in siman 25, Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 47.
