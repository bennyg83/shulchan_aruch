One should not bless — see Mishna Berurah in what is written regarding between the sections: it is an innovation of R' Avraham in the name of the Levush; and even though at the end he wrote that it requires study for practical law, and likewise Chayei Adam wrote that one should lay the tallit without a blessing until after Yishtabach, use it, and bless — see in Birkhei Yosef and likewise in Magen Giborim, who agreed thus for practical law, because they found an explicit responsum of the Rambam on this matter. If so, according to this it is obvious that all the more so the blessing of Asher Yatzar — one may bless between the sections at least. And see in Chayei Adam, who is uncertain about this. Therefore, one who had to relieve himself in the middle of the chapter of pesukei dezimra, and went and did his needs — he washes his hands and finishes the chapter of pesukei dezimra and blesses Asher Yatzar, lays the tallit and does not bless on it, as explained in siman 8, note 14, and in Mishna Berurah there; and he lays the tefillin and blesses on them — for since he may not go to the bathroom wearing tefillin, the first blessing is postponed for him, as explained above in siman 25, Mishna Berurah, note 47.
