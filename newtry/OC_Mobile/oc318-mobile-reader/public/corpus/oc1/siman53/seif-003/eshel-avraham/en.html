A quorum. A matter after which Kaddish is recited requires ten men. See siman 234. Likewise, one who recites the introductory portion of Shema should first recite several verses. See the responsum in Beit Yehudah, siman 30, which distinguishes as follows: for a Kaddish instituted by the Sages, the verses need not be recited in the presence of a quorum. By contrast, when one wishes to recite Kaddish after his own study or the like, once ten men have assembled they must also study some baraisa or Mishnah.
