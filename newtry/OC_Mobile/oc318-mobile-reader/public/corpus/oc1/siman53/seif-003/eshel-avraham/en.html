minyan. For something over which Kaddish is said requires ten men; and see siman 234. And likewise one who leads Shema should first say which verses; and see responsum in Yoreh De'ah siman 244, that he distinguishes between Kaddish that the Sages enacted—which does not require a minyan for saying verses—versus when one says Kaddish over his study and the like: when ten have come, he must also study some baraita or mishnah.
