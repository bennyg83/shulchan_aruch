One should not bless [on tzitzit]. For behold, even for the sake of a mitzvah it is forbidden to interrupt, as written in siman 51, seif 4.

The shaliach tzibbur should wait. But the individual says Yishtabach immediately; and likewise on Shabbat, if he finished pesukei dezimra before the shaliach tzibbur, he says Yishtabach immediately.

And be silent. Explanation: that he not interrupt in speech. And Maharam Mintz wrote, siman 8, in the name of Maharil, that he may wait half an hour with Yishtabach—for this is the measure of time to finish it all, and he need not return to the beginning, according to all views and Rosh siman 65. And nevertheless I hold that even if he waited because of unavoidable circumstance in order to finish it all, he need not return to the beginning—see siman 183.
