One should not recite the blessing. For even for the need of a mitzvah it is forbidden to interrupt, as written in siman 51, seif 4.
The shaliach tzibbur should wait. But an individual should say Yishtabach immediately. Likewise on Shabbos, if he completed Pesukei D'Zimrah before the shaliach tzibbur, he should say Yishtabach immediately.
And he should remain silent. This means that he should not interrupt with speech. Rabbi Moshe Mintz wrote in siman 8 in the name of Maharil that one may wait half an hour with Yishtabach, for that is the measure of time needed to complete all of it, and therefore he need not return to the beginning according to all opinions. See the beginning of siman 65. Nevertheless, it seems to me that even if he paused because of unavoidable circumstances long enough to complete all of it, he need not return to the beginning. See what I wrote in siman 183.
