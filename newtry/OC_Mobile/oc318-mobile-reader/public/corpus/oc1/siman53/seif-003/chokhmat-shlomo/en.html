(seif 15) permanent shliach tzibur, etc., and he need not wait to be told, etc. NB: Here I saw in Shulchan Aruch of the pious Gaon of Vilna, who wrote that it is puzzling from what we say in Arakhin [page 11b] from the case of even though he consults on behalf of others; and in my humble opinion, after forgiveness from the rabbi, two separate matters: here the author's intention is thus—a different person who is not permanent should not be the one to begin the matter and ask to pray, for this is arrogance, as if he is fit to pray; therefore he must wait until others tell him first, and they are the ones who begin with him first. But a permanent shliach tzibur has no arrogance and he is permitted to begin the matter and go down before the ark; therefore he wrote that a permanent shliach tzibur goes down by himself and others need not tell him first. But nevertheless, even though he begins and goes down first, he must still consult with the congregation first—and this is what Arakhin deals with: he will be the one to begin and ask them whether he should pray. And this is clear and correct in my humble opinion, examine well.
