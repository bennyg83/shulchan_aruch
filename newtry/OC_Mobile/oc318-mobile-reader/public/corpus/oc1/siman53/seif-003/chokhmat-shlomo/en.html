(Section 15) Shatz is fixed, etc. and he will not wait to be told, etc. P.S. Here I saw in the Sha'a of the Hasidic Gaon from Vilna, who wrote that she was puzzled by what Damrinan was doing in Arakhin [Page 11 EB] from the hand of Dahua even Dammlich AS and in the Panad after the forgiveness from Kathar Dima two separate issues here, O the author's intention is this. Let others tell him that you will be sick and they will be the ones who start with him that you will be sick A fixed Shatz like Yohara Vershai is to begin with a word and go down before the ark, and for this he wrote a fixed Shatz that comes down by himself and others who tell him to pray, but Mm is also the one who starts and goes down and begins to pray.
