Seif 25, in the gloss. “Who sings foreign songs.” In the edition of Kol Bo before us, the text reads, “songs of the raven.” I likewise copied this reading in Responsa Toras Chayim, part 3, siman 1. He writes concerning it: Here we see that because of something people regard as minor, namely singing songs of the raven, we say that it is proper to remove him, etc. These are his words.
