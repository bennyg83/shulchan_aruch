(25) There—and accustomed to read Torah, etc.—that the verses of prayer be arranged before him. Rashi in chapter 2 of Ta'anit, 16 end of side a. Beit Yosef. Levush. Razah, note 4. It also seems to me that if he is not accustomed to read, he is included in "he turns his ear from hearing Torah"—and his prayer is an abomination. Ateret Rachel, note 8. And it seems further to me that in Torah is written the performance of mitzvot, and in Prophets and Writings rebukes and reproof—through this he will be humble and return in mitzvot, and his prayer will be accepted willingly. And see above, note 16.
