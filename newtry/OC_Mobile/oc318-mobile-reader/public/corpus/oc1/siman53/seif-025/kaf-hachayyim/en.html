25) There: Accustomed to reading Torah, etc. The verses of prayer should be ordered before him. Rashi, second chapter of Taanis, 16a at the end; Beis Yosef; Levush; and Shulchan Aruch HaRav 4. It also appears to me that one unaccustomed to reading falls under, 'One who turns his ear from hearing Torah; even his prayer is an abomination.' Eliyah Rabbah 8. It further appears to me that the Torah records performance of the mitzvos, while Neviim and Kesuvim contain ethical rebuke. Through them he will become humble and diligent in mitzvos, and his prayer will be favorably accepted. See above, paragraph 16.
