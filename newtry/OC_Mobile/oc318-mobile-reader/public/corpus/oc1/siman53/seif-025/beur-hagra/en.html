Section 25 — one does not remove, etc.: See Gittin at the end of chapter HaNizakin, page 60b — "they combine," etc., because of suspicion.

And one does not remove, etc.: See Gittin chapter 9, page 1, and Ketubot 36b.

But, etc.: As stated above, siman 64.

And a shliach tzibur who is, etc.: As stated in chapter 15 of Shabbat — garments that he laundered, etc.

And if he is not [permanently appointed], etc. — to exclude [the case where] one substitutes even though he does not substitute all day; even though he falls within the category of those who change [the appointee], as stated in Shabbat there; nevertheless, they do not remove him for this reason. There (ibid.).

And the sheliach tzibur, etc. As above.

Or etc. As stated, "You shall not set up a monument…," and they said it was beloved in the days of the Patriarchs, as stated in the Sifrei there; and see Magen Avraham.

And the sheliach tzibur, etc. — his son takes precedence over any other person, etc. As stated in the Torah Kohanim, parashat Tzav, "from among his sons" — this teaches that his son takes precedence over everyone. One might think… And in the Sifrei, parashat Shofetim, "he and his sons among all Israel" — this teaches that if his son dies, [another] stands in his place. From where [do we learn] that all leaders of Israel — their sons stand in their places? Scripture teaches: "among Israel" — whoever is among Israel.

If he fills [the role], etc. As stated in the above Torah Kohanim — one might think even if he does not fill his father's place; Scripture teaches "whom He will empower" — at the time when he fills his father's place. And according to Ketubot — and this is what [is meant by] "even if there is no [full replacement]" — if etc., as stated in Ketubot there.

And there is no [requirement that he alone serve], etc. As stated in the Tosefta, last chapter of Bava Metzia: One who was a supervisor for the public, a scribe for the public, a notary for the public, a table-server for the public, and there is no other [qualified person] there but him, and the festival season arrives and he wishes to go into his house — they may detain him until another stands in his place. But if he stipulated with them in court, or they granted him the road — the authority is in his hand; thus even without stipulation it is not required that he alone serve, and he is permitted to appoint another for periods.
