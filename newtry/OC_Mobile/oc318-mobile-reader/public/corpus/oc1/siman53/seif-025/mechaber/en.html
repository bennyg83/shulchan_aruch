They do not remove a chazzan from his craft unless disqualification is found in him.They do not remove him for mere rumor—for example, that a reputation went out that he was caught with a non-Jewish woman, or that he informed on a person. But if witnesses came on this or the like, they remove him. A shaliach tzibbur who is a slaughterer and examiner should not pray in filthy, malodorous garments; if he does not wish to change his garments at prayer time and pray, they remove him. A shaliach tzibbur who defiles his mouth or sings gentile songs—they warn him not to do so, and if he does not heed, they remove him [Kol Bo page 125, column 4]. A shaliach tzibbur] who has aged and wishes to appoint his son to assist him at times—even if his son's voice is not as sweet as his, if he fills his place in other matters, his son takes precedence over anyone, and the congregation cannot object [responsa of Rashba siman 9].']:
