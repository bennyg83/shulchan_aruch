18) Olas Tamid further writes that even Shulchan Aruch's stringency (that no bad reputation should have emerged in one's youth) applies only to appointing him initially. We need not be stringent so as to remove or dismiss him after he has become established as shaliach tzibbur. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, writes likewise in the name of Raanach 2:41, as do Magen Avraham 7; Eliyah Rabbah 6; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 73c; and Shulchan Aruch HaRav 6.
