[18] torn, etc. Because of honor of the congregation, Maharam Mintz wrote in his responsum siman 81: the garments of a shliach tzibur must be clean without filth and sweat, and in particular the surplice and the matronite; and his garments must be long so that his legs are not visible; and if they are not so long, he should wear stockings up to the knees; and it is good that he have special clean trousers for prayer. He also wrote: it is proper that the chazzan enter the synagogue first and leave last. Also there: when he stands to pray, he should turn his heart from all thought and distraction, and set his eyes downward so that he not look at anyone; and he should place his hands on his heart under the surplice, folding the right hand over the left, and not remove his hands from each other except as needed to turn the pages and arrange his tallit and the like; and he should not engage in anything else—for there are some chazzanim whose hands are busy with vain things and light candles during their prayer, or look at their customs or other things—and he should not do so—up to here are his words, abbreviated.
