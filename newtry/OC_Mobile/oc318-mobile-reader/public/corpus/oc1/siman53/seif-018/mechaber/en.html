If a person says "I will not lead because my clothing is colorful or because sandals are on my feet," he cannot lead at all for that prayer because that he is the way of the heretics to be particular about this, and we are afraid that heresy has fallen upon him. {Rama: Rem"a: [And even if he gives a valid excuse for his words, it is not effective] [Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbi Y'honatan].}
