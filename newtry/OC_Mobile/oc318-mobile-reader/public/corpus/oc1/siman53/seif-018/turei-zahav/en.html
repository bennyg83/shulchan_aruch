Colored garments. The Mishnah's language: even in white he shall not pass. Apparently he interprets the language that "even" is according to his intention — even though he is satisfied with white garments, nevertheless we do not leave him be. And accordingly, if he repents and says, "I am passing with colored garments" — we leave him; but from Shulchan Aruch's words it seems we do not leave him at all for that prayer. Therefore one must say that what he meant is: if he returned and said, "I am praying in colored garments" — it is obvious he does not descend, for we see he was initially particular about his heresy, only that afterward he retracts out of fear of people — for if not, it is not human nature to retract one's speech so completely. But with white garments we do not see clearly any proof of heresy; therefore one can say he spoke for another reason, such as modesty or the like — therefore he said "even in white he shall not pass."
