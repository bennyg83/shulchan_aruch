(18) And he wrote in Orach Chayyim that the author of Orach Chayyim did not be strict in what he ruled—that no bad reputation attached in his youth—meaning not to appoint him initially; but to remove him and dismiss him after he had been established as shaliach tzibbur, we have no [reason] to be strict. And so Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef, citing Ranach, part 2 siman 41. Magen Avraham, note 7. Ateret Rachel, note 6. Shaarei Teshuvah 273. Razah, note 6.
