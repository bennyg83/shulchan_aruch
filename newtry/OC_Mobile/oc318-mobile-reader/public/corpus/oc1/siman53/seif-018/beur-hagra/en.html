Section 18 — in that prayer, etc.: Rambam and Tur and several places — for he says "even," etc., and does not say he does not transgress.

And even if, etc.: Since he says "even in white garments," etc. — no question that all the more so in colored garments he does not transgress, even if he repents; and all the more so in one who stands by his word. But it deals with this case — unlike one who repents retroactively, for he had heresy in him at first.
