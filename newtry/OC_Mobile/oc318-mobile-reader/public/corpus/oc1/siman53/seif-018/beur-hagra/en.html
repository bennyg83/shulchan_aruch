Seif 18, in that prayer, etc. This is from the Rambam and Tur. So too it is implied, for it says 'even, etc.,' and does not say, 'he does not transgress.'
And even if, etc. For it says 'even in white garments, etc.,' and does not teach the greater novelty, 'even in colored garments he should not pass,' even if he retracts. This is certainly so when he maintains his statement. Rather, it is speaking in such a case; but where he retracts, it is revealed retroactively that there was heresy in him at the beginning.
