(46) He should not go down — even if he later regrets and says "I shall transgress by wearing colored [garments]," we do not leave him [in place].

(47) In that prayer — for since we did not hear from him explicitly words of heresy, it suffices to be concerned on account of this for that prayer alone.

(48) An excuse — such as when he said his speech was for the honor of the Place and to wear white; nevertheless, since the congregation requested of him to pray for them thus, he ought not have been refusing, since by law it is permitted. Peri Chadash wrote: specifically if afterwards he gave the excuse; but if immediately when he said he would not transgress by wearing colored garments he at once gave the reason that there is no way of honor to the Omnipresent except in white, he should go down—see there.
