In that prayer. Since we did not explicitly hear words of heresy from him, it is enough to be concerned because of this only for that prayer.
An explanation. Such as if he said that his words were for the honor of the Omnipresent and in order to wear white garments. Nevertheless, since the congregation asked him to pray for them in that manner, he should not have refused, since by law it is permitted. The Pri Chadash wrote that this applies specifically if he gave the explanation afterward. But if immediately when he said, 'I will not pass before the amud in colored garments,' he immediately stated the reason, that it is not honorable to G-d except in white garments, he may descend; see there.
