Seif katan 18: “An explanation,” etc. Since the congregation asks him to serve, it would not have been proper, out of regard for the congregation's honor, to conduct himself with excessive piety. We must therefore conclude that his explanation is false and that a heretical impulse entered him. Nevertheless, even if he gives no explanation, it is sufficient to remove him from this particular prayer. He remains fit for other prayers, because we presume that at that moment a temporary spirit of heresy entered him and that he certainly reconsidered afterward. So is the view of the early authorities.
