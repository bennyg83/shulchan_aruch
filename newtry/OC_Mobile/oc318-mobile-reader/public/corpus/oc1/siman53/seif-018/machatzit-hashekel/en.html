(Note 18) "An excuse," etc.—since the congregation requests, etc.—for the honor of the congregation it was not fitting to behave with excessive piety; or the excuse is false and heresy is cast upon him. However, even if he does not give an excuse, it suffices with what they remove him from this prayer; but for other prayers he is valid—for we say at that hour, as fits that hour, the foolishness of his kind is cast upon him, and certainly he has repented—so [wrote] the early authorities.
