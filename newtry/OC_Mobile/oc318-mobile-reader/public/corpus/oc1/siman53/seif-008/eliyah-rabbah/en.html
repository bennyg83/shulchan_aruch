[8] And his voice is pleasant, etc. Regarding him it is stated, “Honor Hashem with your wealth,” and Navos, whose voice was pleasant, was punished over this. “And he is accustomed,” etc., so that the verses of prayer will be arranged fluently in his mouth. It also appears to me that one who is not accustomed to read is included among those who turn their ear away from hearing Torah, concerning whom it is said that even their prayer is an abomination.
