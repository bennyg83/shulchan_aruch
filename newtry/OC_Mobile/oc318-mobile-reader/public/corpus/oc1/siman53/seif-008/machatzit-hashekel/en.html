Seif katan 8: “It is permitted,” etc. This implies that even if he deliberately committed a transgression once, it is permitted. See Yoreh Deah, siman 2, seif 6, and Shach there. Meaning, since he writes, “only if he was habitually inclined,” this implies at least twice. Although he concludes, “But one who encountered an inadvertent incident,” even though one deliberate transgression does not disqualify him from being chazzan, it may be explained that Or Zarua was answering an actual case brought before him, and that case involved an inadvertent act. Therefore he wrote, “But one who encountered an inadvertent incident.”
