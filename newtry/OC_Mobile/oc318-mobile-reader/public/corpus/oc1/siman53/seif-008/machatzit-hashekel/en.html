(Note 8) Permitted, etc.—it implies that even if he transgressed once deliberately, etc.; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 2 seif 6, and Shach there. And meaning: only specifically when it was habitual, etc.—it implies in any case twice. And what he concluded "but one to whom an accidental act came"—even if he transgressed once deliberately, he is permitted to be shliach tzibur. One can say that in that case he answered regarding an act that came before him and the act was inadvertent; therefore he wrote "but," etc., "accidental act."

Like one who became an apostate—since Ishmaelites are not idol worshippers. And see siman 128, note 54, what he wrote in the name of Rambam.

And Maharshal, etc.—these apply only to those thirty [qualities]; so it should read.
