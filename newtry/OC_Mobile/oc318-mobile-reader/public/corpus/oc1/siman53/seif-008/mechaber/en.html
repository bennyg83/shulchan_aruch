One who is not bearded: anyone recognizable as having reached the age at which a beard should properly be full—we call him one whose beard is full. Therefore a man of twenty years, even if he has no beard, we appoint.And likewise if he has a beard, even a little—we call him one whose beard is full if he is eighteen or older [Beit Yosef in the name of Tractate Soferim chapter 14; Nimukei Yosef on HaKorei Omeid].]:
