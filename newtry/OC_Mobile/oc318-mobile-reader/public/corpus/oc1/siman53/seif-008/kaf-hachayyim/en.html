8) There, in the gloss: If the shaliach tzibbur initially had no tallis, he should wrap himself in tzitzis before beginning Yishtabach, etc. This applies specifically to the chazzan, who should not descend from the lectern after beginning Yishtabach. An individual should wait until after Yishtabach, and then wrap himself in tzitzis and recite the berachah, because interrupting then is preferable, as stated above at the beginning of siman 51.
