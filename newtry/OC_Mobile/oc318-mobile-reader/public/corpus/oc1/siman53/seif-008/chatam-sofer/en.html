NB: see in responsa Chakham Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 11 and siman 204 [and in siman 204, in the middle of the answer, I already wrote elsewhere that the practice of writing a term of three years in documents of accepting a rabbi and bonded service is not a fixed term—for who has heard of such that they accept a rabbi from a distant place, etc., and then remove him from there; but the custom of Israel is Torah regarding the rabbi and the bonded—that if he wishes, after three years he may leave, the choice is in his hand, even though one worker similarly whose work is a matter of irretrievable loss may not retract in the middle of his term, and his term is forever—nevertheless he may retract after three years. And the reason: because it is forbidden to hire oneself out for more than six years, and some say for more than three years it is like a Hebrew slave. And see Shach Choshen Mishpat siman 333 s.k. 17; and examine well in that responsum and in the responsum after it]:
