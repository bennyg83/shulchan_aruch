Section 68 — one who is not, etc. And likewise a eunuch, etc. End of chapter 14 of Maaser Sheni, halachah 17: One who passes before the ark and raises his hands until he is twenty years old and has a beard; and even if he has no beard but is twenty — even though he appears as a eunuch. And some say even an actual eunuch is permitted; but if his beard has grown, even from age eighteen he is permitted to pass before the ark and raise his hands.
