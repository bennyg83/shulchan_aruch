Seif 8, one who is not, etc.; and likewise, etc., a saris, etc. In chapter 14 of Maseches Sofrim, halacha 17: One who passes before the ark and one who raises his hands for the priestly blessing must be twenty years old and have a beard. Even if he has no beard but is twenty years old, although he appears like a saris, he may do so; and some say that even a saris himself is permitted. But if his beard grew, even from age eighteen he may pass before the ark and raise his hands.
