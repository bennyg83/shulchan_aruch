Colored garments — Taz. The author changed from the language of the mishnah and Tur (and Levush copied as Tur) "even in whites," etc., to say that he does not go down at all — neither in colored nor in white garments; and that which [the mishnah also says] even barefoot, etc., in siman 53 — one who reveals his arms bare — and see Ran in Megillah 24 regarding Yom Kippur barefoot; and in our novellae we expanded there; and there, in Laws of Prayer, it will be explained. Peri Chadash wrote: if immediately when he said he does not pass in colored [garments] he states the reason that there is no way of honor to Hashem except in white — he goes down, see there; and specifically when he afterwards gave an excuse. Eliyah Rabbah letter 18 in the name of Maharam Mintz 8: the shaliach tzibbur's garments should be clean, without perspiration and grime (a slaughterer changes his clothes); long ones — and if short, he should wear stockings up to the knees (which implies he is permitted to pray barefoot; however, in a place where they do not stand before elders, this is forbidden); and he should stand in awe and not adjust the lamps to improve them — and see in customs, see there.
