(8) There—{Rama: the shaliach tzibbur, if he did not have a tallit at first, should wrap in tzitzit before he begins Yishtabach, etc.} And specifically the chazzan, who may not return and descend from the ark after he has begun Yishtabach; but an individual should wait until after Yishtabach and then wrap in tzitzit and bless on them—for then it is more preferable to interrupt, as above at the beginning of siman 51.
