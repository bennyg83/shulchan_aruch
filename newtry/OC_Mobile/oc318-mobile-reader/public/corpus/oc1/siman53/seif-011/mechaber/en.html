A shaliach tzibbur who prolongs the prayer so that they hear his voice sweetly—if it is because he rejoices in his heart at giving thanks to the Holy One, blessed be He, with sweetness, a blessing shall come upon him, when he prays with gravity and stands in awe and fear. But if he intends to make his voice heard and rejoices in his voice—this is repugnant. And in any case, whoever prolongs his prayer does not act well, because of burdening the congregation.
