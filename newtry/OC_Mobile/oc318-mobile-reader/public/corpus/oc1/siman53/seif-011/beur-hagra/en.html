Section 11 — shliach tzibur, etc., if, etc.: As stated in Taanit there — and he has a pleasant voice; and in Pesikta, parashat Re'eh, it is written "Honor Hashem from your wealth" — they read it "from your throat": that if your voice was pleasant and you were standing in the synagogue, stand and honor Hashem from your voice. Navot had a pleasant voice, etc.; and see Yalkut Mishlei siman 832; and as stated above, "and his voice was pleasant."

And he, etc.: As stated in the mishnah and in the gemara at the end of chapter 5 of Berachos — one does not stand to pray, etc.

But, etc.: As stated in chapter 2 of Taanit: "It was given to me," etc.

And nevertheless, etc.: As stated in Berachos 31a — such was the custom of R' Akiva when he prayed with the congregation, etc.; and they likewise said in several places because of trouble to the congregation.
