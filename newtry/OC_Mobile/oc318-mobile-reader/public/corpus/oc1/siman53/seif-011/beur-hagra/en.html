Seif 11, a shaliach tzibbur, etc., if, etc. As stated there in Ta'anis, 'and he has a pleasant voice.' And in Pesikta, parshas Re'eh, on the verse, 'Honor Hashem from your wealth,' read it as 'from your grace': if your voice is pleasant and you are standing in the synagogue, stand and honor Hashem with your voice. Navos had a pleasant voice, etc. See Yalkut Mishlei, siman 932, and as stated above, 'and his voice is pleasant.'
And he, etc. As stated in the Mishnah and Gemara at the beginning of the fifth chapter of Berachos: one does not stand to pray, etc.
But, etc. As stated in the second chapter of Ta'anis: 'It was placed upon me, etc.'
Nevertheless, etc. As stated in Berachos 31a: such was Rabbi Akiva's practice when he prayed with the congregation, etc.; and similarly they said in many places, because of burdening the congregation.
