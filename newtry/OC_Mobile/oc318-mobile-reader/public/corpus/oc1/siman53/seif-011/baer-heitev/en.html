**pleasantly.** For it is stated in Pesikta: "Honor Hashem from your wealth"—they read it as "from your throat"; and one who has a pleasant voice should sing to the Holy One blessed be He, and not other songs (Shulchan Aruch siman 471).

**unseemly.** For it is stated in Shulchan Aruch: when Rabbenu Shimon ben Gamliel went out to be executed, R' Yehuda said to him, "My brother, why are you going out to be killed—perhaps you lectured in public and rejoiced in your heart and enjoyed words of Torah?" He said to him, "My brother, you have comforted me," etc.

**prolonging.** If it is not with the will of the congregation, even on Shabbat and Yom Tov; and even with the will of the congregation, to extend more than necessary—this is unseemly (Rashal, first chapter of Chullin).
