Shaliach tzibbur. One who has a pleasant voice should sing to the Holy One, blessed is He, and not other songs. If he spat, he should wipe his mouth and afterward pray. Sefer Chassidim, siman 251.
Disgraceful. It is stated in Sefer Chassidim: when Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel went out to be executed, he said to Rabbi Yishmael the Kohen Gadol, 'My brother, why am I going out to be executed?' He said to him, 'Perhaps you would expound in public, and your heart rejoiced, and you derived pleasure from words of Torah.' He said to him, 'My brother, you have comforted me.'
The congregation. Yam Shel Shlomo, siman 50, wrote that it is forbidden for him to prolong the service without the congregation's consent, even on Shabbos and Yom Tov. Even with the congregation's consent, excessive length is disgraceful, for this is neither half for G-d nor half for you. Kenesses HaGedolah wrote in the name of a responsum of Ri Bruna that a shaliach tzibbur whose voice became defective, if it sounds as though it trembles and is broken, is invalid. He further wrote that if two chazzanim are equal, a kohen precedes a Levi, and a Levi precedes a Yisrael; and a Torah scholar precedes an ignoramus, even if the ignoramus is a kohen, and certainly if he is a Levi.
