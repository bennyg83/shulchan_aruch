(11) There—in the gloss: and he shall be silent until a minyan comes, etc.—meaning he should not interrupt with speech. Maharam Mintz, siman 8, wrote in the name of Maharil that he may wait half an hour with Yishtabach—for that is the measure of time to finish it entirely, and he need not return to the beginning according to all. And see the beginning of siman 65; nevertheless it seems to me that even if he delayed due to unavoidable circumstance in order to finish it entirely, he need not return to the beginning. Magen Avraham, note 5. Yad Aharon there. And the reason Peri Megadim wrote in Orach Chayyim, note 5: pesukei dezimra are rabbinic and one does not return to the beginning; and it is not like Keriat Shema, which is Torah—see there. Razah, note 3. Siddur Beit Yosef, note 10.
