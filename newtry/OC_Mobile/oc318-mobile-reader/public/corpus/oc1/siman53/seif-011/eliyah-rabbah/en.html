But when they appoint him simply that he pray in his place when the shliach tzibur is not free to come to the synagogue, or when he is somewhat weak, it is permitted to appoint him—for this is occasional; and so wrote Taz. And it requires investigation—for Beit Chadash did not mention that he forbids also in this, since he was appointed for this he is called permanent and is not called occasional except when there is no appointee at all.
