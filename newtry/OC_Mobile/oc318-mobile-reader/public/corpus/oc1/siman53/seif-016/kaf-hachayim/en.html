(16) There—and what is meant by "empty of transgressions." And this is the language of Zohar Vayikra—R' Yitzchak said: if the anointed priest sins, this is the lower priest appointed for service, and sin is found in him for the guilt of the people—woe to those who rely on him. Similarly a shliach tzibur who sins and sin is found in him, because the people are not found innocent before the Holy One—woe to those who rely on him—end quote. And in Shulchan Arukh HaRav he wrote: those shliach tziburim who are not perfected in Torah and mitzvot and go down before the ark—about them it is said "and your advocate has transgressed against Me"—end quote. Shulchan Arukh HaRav, note 1. Shaarei Teshuvah 272 side a. And this is the language of Rama in the gloss on what he wrote "show me Your countenance." Meaning: first correct your deeds and let the image of the Omnipresent be on your face as before you sinned; then let me hear Your voice in prayer and supplications, for Your voice was pleasant to me when Your countenance was comely—end quote. And the reason: if a person merits some spark of the holiness of the Shekhinah, then his prayer is accepted; and he does not merit this unless he is perfected in all 613 mitzvot, as written in Tikunim on "everything in which there is a blemish shall not approach." Therefore if some sin is found in him—woe to those who rely on him—Shulchan Arukh HaRav there. If so, the shliach tzibur must be very careful not to speak between the silent prayer and the repetition—for even regarding an individual they wrote Kissei Eliyahu siman 18 note 21, and Siddur Beit Yosef note 10 in the name of Gur Aryeh of the Ari z"l, that it is a grave sin to speak between silent prayer and repetition, and all the more so a shliach tzibur. And as Petach HaDvir siman 111 note 3 wrote, and Kaf HaChayyim of Rav Chaim Palagi siman 15 note 52—see there: how can he make himself forbidden and come to be shliach tzibur to pray the repetition before the Omnipresent on behalf of the people, and he is not afraid of "she gave me her voice, therefore I hated her"? And this will be explained further in our words on siman 124 seif 1, with the Omnipresent's help.
