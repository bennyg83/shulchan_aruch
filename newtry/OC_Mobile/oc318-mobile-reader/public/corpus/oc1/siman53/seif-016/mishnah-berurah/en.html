To refuse. A shaliach tzibbur who did not wish to continue as shaliach tzibbur and they accepted another in his place does not need permission, since it is still in his power to be shaliach tzibbur. Specifically, he does not need permission from the congregation, but from the second shaliach tzibbur he must take permission. So wrote Responsa Yad Eliyahu. According to what we explained in Biur Halacha, even such a shaliach tzibbur must first take permission.
At all. For one does not refuse a great person. Tosafot wrote at the end of the seventh chapter of Pesachim that in a matter of arrogance and authority, even if the one telling him is a great person, he should refuse.
