(44) To refuse — a shaliach tzibbur who did not wish to continue being shaliach tzibbur and they accepted another in his place does not need permission, since it is still in his power to be shaliach tzibbur; and specifically from the congregation permission is not needed, but from the second shaliach tzibbur permission must be taken—so Yad Eliyahu wrote in responsum. And according to what we explained in Beur Halakhah, such a shaliach tzibbur also needs to take permission initially.

(45) General rule — one does not refuse a great person. Tosafot at the end of chapter 7 of Pesachim wrote that in a matter of grossness and lordship, even one who says to him he is a great man may refuse.
