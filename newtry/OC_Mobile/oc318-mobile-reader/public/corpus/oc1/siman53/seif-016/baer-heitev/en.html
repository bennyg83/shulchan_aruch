**to refuse.** A shliach tzibur who did not wish to be shliach tzibur anymore and they accepted another in his place—he need not take permission, since it is still in his power to be shliach tzibur. And specifically from the congregation he need not take permission; but the second shliach tzibur must take permission (Yad Eliyahu siman 30, see there).

**principle.** That one does not refuse a great person. And Tosafos wrote at the beginning of chapter 7 of Pesachim that regarding a matter of rudeness and domination, even one who says to him that he is a great man may refuse.
