To refuse. A shaliach tzibbur who no longer wanted to be shaliach tzibbur, and they accepted another in his place, does not need permission, since it is still in his power to be shaliach tzibbur. Specifically from the congregation he need not take permission, but from the second shaliach tzibbur he must take permission, Yad Eliyahu, siman 30; see there.
At all. For one does not refuse a great person. Tosafot wrote at the end of chapter 7 of Pesachim that in a matter of arrogance and lordship, even if the one telling him is a great person, he should refuse.
