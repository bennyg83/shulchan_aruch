(9) There—in the gloss: before he begins Yishtabach, etc. But if they brought [tallit and tefillin] only after Yishtabach, even the shaliach tzibbur should wrap and bless between Yishtabach and Kaddish. Magen Avraham, siman 54, note 4. Shulchan Arukh HaRav there, note 4. Razah there, note 3. Peri Megadim, Orach Chayyim, in this siman, note 3. Yafeh Lelev there, note 3. But between Kaddish and Barechu he should not interrupt for anything—and all the more so he should not interrupt after the shaliach tzibbur said Barechu before they begin the blessing of Yotzer. Morenu Rabbeinu in gloss on siman 54. And there is no distinction between the shaliach tzibbur himself and another. Levush in gloss on this siman and on siman 54, note 3. Razah there, note 3. And see Peri Megadim in Orach Chayyim on this siman, note 3. And in siman 54, note 4, who wrote that between Kaddish and Barechu is like between the chapters, and between Barechu and Yotzer is like the middle of the chapter—for Barechu refers to "Yotzer Or"—see there. And so Ateret Rachel there, siman 54, note 5. And so is implied from the words of Levush there. If so, this follows the view of Morenu Rabbeinu in gloss on siman 66, seif 2, who holds that one does not bless on the tallit between the chapters—as cited—and it will be explained there, with God's help.
