[9] Deliberately, etc. It seems not specifically murder—for the same applies to other sins as in seif 4 above; and it is strained to say that above refers to one about whom rumor spread and not that he repented. And likewise in Kelbo page 156 it is explained that even if he repented for other sins, he is still called as having a bad reputation emerged. It further implies in Kelbo there that one disqualified for intentional sin even if he returned in repentance—this is on a fast day; but on other days of the year it is permitted even to appoint him lekhatchilah; and in this vein R' Eliyahu Mizrachi wrote in responsa siman (86) 88. Knesset HaGedolah wrote in the name of Nimukei Rama: he struck his wife with a splintering tree while she was pregnant and it did not darken until she died, and it was evident she died from that blow—he is not fit to be shliach tzibur—up to here is his language; and I need to examine his source.
