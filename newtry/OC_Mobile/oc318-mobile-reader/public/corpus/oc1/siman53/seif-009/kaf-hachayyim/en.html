9) There, in the gloss: Before beginning Yishtabach, etc. If the tallis was brought only after Yishtabach, even the shaliach tzibbur should wrap himself and recite the berachah between Yishtabach and Kaddish. Magen Avraham 54:4; Solel Boneh 4 there; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 3 there; Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 3 in this siman; and Yafeh LaLev 3. Between Kaddish and Barchu, however, one should make no interruption whatsoever. All the more so should one not interrupt after the shaliach tzibbur says Barchu and before beginning Yotzer. Rama's gloss at the end of siman 54. There is no distinction between the shaliach tzibbur and anyone else. Levush's gloss to this siman and his comments to siman 54:3; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 3 there. See Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 3 in this siman and paragraph 4 in siman 54. He writes that between Kaddish and Barchu is like between sections, whereas between Barchu and Yotzer is like the middle of a section, because Barchu relates to Yotzer Or; see there. Eliyah Rabbah 54:5 writes likewise, and this is also implied by Levush there. This follows Rama's view in his gloss to siman 66:2 that one does not recite the berachah over a tallis between sections. The matter will be explained there with God's help.
