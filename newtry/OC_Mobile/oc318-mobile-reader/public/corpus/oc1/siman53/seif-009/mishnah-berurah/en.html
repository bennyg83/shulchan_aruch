(28) A saris — that is, the signs of a saris were seen in him; for otherwise, since he did not bring two hairs, we say he is still a minor, as written in siman 55 seif 5; see Mishna Berurah there. And Maharshal in Chullin siman 45 wrote that one should not appoint a saris, for he is unseemly for the congregation:
