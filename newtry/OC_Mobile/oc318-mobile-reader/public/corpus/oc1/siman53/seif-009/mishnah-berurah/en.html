Saris. Meaning that signs of a saris appeared in him. Otherwise, since he did not produce two hairs, we say that he is still a minor, as written in siman 55, seif 5; see there in Mishnah Berurah. Maharshal in Chullin, siman 45, wrote that one should not appoint a saris, for it is disgraceful to the congregation.
Twenty years old. For before then, even if signs of a saris appeared in him, in any case he cannot be shaliach tzibbur even occasionally, since he is still a minor.
