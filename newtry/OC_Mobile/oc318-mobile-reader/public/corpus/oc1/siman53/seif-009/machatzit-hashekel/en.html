(Note 9) "Because of," etc.—forbidden. And so too Bach, that the explanation of "because of this" is that it is not the honor of the congregation that they make agents like angels to the Omnipresent; if so, the main reason is because of the honor of the Omnipresent, and therefore they cannot waive—so Bach.

In Tosafot on Megillah, page 85, they challenged on what we say there—that a minor who did not bring [two pubic] hairs does not go down before the ark, which implies that if he brought [two hairs] he goes down before the ark. And in tractate Chullin they say that specifically one whose beard is filled [is required]. And resolve one side [of the contradiction]: in Taanis specifically one whose beard is filled [is required], and with this tractate Chullin deals.
