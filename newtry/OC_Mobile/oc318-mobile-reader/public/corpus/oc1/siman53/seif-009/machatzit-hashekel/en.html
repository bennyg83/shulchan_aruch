Seif katan 9: “Because,” etc. It is forbidden. Bach likewise writes that the meaning of “because of the congregation's honor” is that it is not honorable for the congregation to appoint such people as their emissaries before Hashem. Thus, the essential reason is the honor of Hashem, and they therefore cannot waive it. So writes Bach.
Tosafos in Megillah 24b asks about the Gemara's statement there that a minor who has not produced two pubic hairs may not lead the prayer service. This implies that if he has produced two hairs, he may lead the service. Yet in Chullin it is stated that his beard must specifically have filled out. Tosafos answers, in one resolution, that for a fast day his beard must specifically have filled out, and the passage in Chullin concerns that case.
