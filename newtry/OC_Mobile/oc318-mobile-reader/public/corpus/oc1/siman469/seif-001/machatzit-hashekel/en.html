(א) (Note 1) Any, etc. — even fowl; even a chicken, so in Maharil.

(ב) And it is a stringency, etc. — that is, in a chicken and the like; but doves and pigeons — Magen Avraham also concedes that by law it is forbidden, as stated in the note afterward. And fish: the Bach wrote that even for Maharil it is permitted; and see in Kaf HaChayyim.
