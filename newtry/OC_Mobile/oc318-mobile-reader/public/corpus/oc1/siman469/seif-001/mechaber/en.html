It is prohibited to say over any cattle, whether living or slaughtered,
"this meat is for Passover" due to the fact that one appears to be consecrating his animal as his Paschal lamb, and he thereby eats consecrated items outside the permitted area. Rather, one may say "this meat is for the Festival".
