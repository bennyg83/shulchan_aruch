The law of not dedicating meat for Passover. Containing 1 section: It
is forbidden to say regarding any animal, whether living or slaughtered, "this meat is for Passover," because it appears that one consecrated it while alive as a Paschal offering and one ends up eating consecrated items outside [the permitted place]. Rather, one should say "this meat is for the Festival."And a kid mekulas (meaning: whole upon (its head and upon its legs and upon its innards) — even if one did not specify and said "this meat is for Passover" — it is forbidden to make it (Tur), as below in siman 476.} But one may say "this wheat is for Passover." אבל מותר לומר חטים אלו לפסח:
