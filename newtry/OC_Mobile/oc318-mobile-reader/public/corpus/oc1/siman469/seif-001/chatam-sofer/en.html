In the Mechaber: forbidden to say regarding any animal. It appears to me, see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 139 [regarding a certain youth who had a milk lamb in his flock and said to his uncle "this is my Paschal offering," and after they raised an outcry about this the youth apologized that he knew nothing of the matter of consecration and they only spoke in jest and mockery and the talk of youths. And in his responsum he first explains the matter in Pesachim daf 50b, and wrote that according to this, what he said — "this lamb shall be a Paschal offering" — is consecrated for everyone. And afterward he wrote that if one believes him that it was jest, it is possible it is not a claim at all as he explains there. Therefore one should ask about his vow on Passover and regret — except that it is difficult to permit the prohibition of the stringency of consecrated items through a question and opening of a sage in this time, who are lenient in permitting vows and do not wish to understand what is explained to them how they should regret; therefore it appears to me to devise another combined remedy for permitting that vow — namely, after his vow was already permitted, he should command a minor gentile who does not exchange with an adult Jew, and cause a blemish in it fit to permit it through three members of the congregation as with a firstborn; and afterward stand it before a court and they shall assess its monetary value and redeem it and cast it into the river; and a kohen is not required, as stated in Sanhedrin daf 15a. And in any case, since it is good, there is no loss that the assessor be a kohen; and see there well in the entire responsum].
