Animal — Ba'er Hetev; and see what Or Zarua wrote in Machatzit HaShekel in his novellae on Pesachim daf 13, who wrote to resolve the Bach's question; and see what he wrote that a kid mekulas has no prohibition except eating it on Passover nights, for it appears he eats it for Passover; and if he leaves it for tomorrow and another day it is permitted (and meaning: even though he roasted on Passover night while it is mekulas, there is concern only on the eating, see there). And see in Sha'arei Knesset siman 5, that even if one said at the time of slaughter for the sake of Passover, it is permitted d'oraisa, only d'rabbanan forbidden, see there; and in Teshuvot siman 12. And see in responsa Chinuch, son of the author, siman 17, regarding one who saw at the butcher a slaughtered kid and said to the butcher: how much do you give for this Paschal offering — the lenient one did not lose. And see in Knesset Yechezkel siman 18, who expanded to prove that one who says "this meat for Passover" is permitted b'dieved in eating, as the opinion of Taz and Shach, see there.
