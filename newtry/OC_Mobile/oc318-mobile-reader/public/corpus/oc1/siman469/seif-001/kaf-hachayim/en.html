(1) [Section 1] Forbidden to say, etc. And if one said "this meat for Passover," it is forbidden to eat it; and even for benefit it is forbidden and requires burial (Bach, Or Zarua, and so too Peri Chadash). And he wrote that so is frequent in the book of Damascus Eliezer in tractate Chullin daf 77a, see there. And Magen Avraham note 2: to distinguish — in a kid and lamb one should be stringent b'dieved, and in other meat one should not be stringent, see there. And so inclined Chok Yosef section 3, and so too Chayei Adam general 131 section 8. However in Orach Chayyim daf 77a section 11 he wrote in the name of R' Shimon the purchaser: one should not say "this for Passover," for it appears as consecrating for the name of Passover; and if he said, it is permitted to eat, see there. And so too Taz note 1: if one transgressed and said, b'dieved there is no prohibition of eating, see there. And so too Eliyah Rabbah section 1, and so is the opinion of the rabbi Mekor Chayyim on daf 173b, that if he said, b'dieved it is permitted, see there. And so in responsa Knesset Yechezkel siman 18 he expanded to prove as the opinion of Taz and Shach that b'dieved it is permitted in eating, see there; and so agreed Chayei Adam section 1, Birkat Yosef section 1, Ma'amar Mordechai section 2, and so is the opinion of Nahar Shalom section 1, Laws of Passover section 1, Magen HaElef section 2; and so too R' Zalman section 2, that b'dieved even if one said on a living kid and lamb "this meat for Passover," they are permitted in eating, even if he said so verbally after midnight — because of mar'it ha'ayin one does not forbid b'dieved — end of his words; except that he wrote there in a gloss that in a kid and lamb it is good to be concerned for the opinion of the stringent and forbid in eating specifically when there is no great need and not a time of distress, see there. And in any case, this is simple: unspecified vows are to be stringent and their explanation is to be lenient; and we also require mouth and heart equal, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 210 and siman 211; therefore one should ask him, and if he says his intention is only for the Festival of Passover, then one may permit.
