(א) Forbidden to say regarding any animal — even if it is not a kid or lamb. Rosh, according to Rashi, that the reason is that they will say one consecrated it for the monetary value of the Paschal offering, whether large or small. Beit Yosef from the reason stated above, and so too from the Gemara there that he only brought out wheat and did not distinguish between one animal and another animal.

(ב) Whether — as stated above, "this meat," whether slaughtered.

(ג) That it appears one consecrated it, etc. This is according to Tosafos, and specifically a kid and lamb; but according to Rashi, because it appears one consecrated it for the monetary value of the Paschal offering as stated above, and so too in Yerushalmi chapter 7 of Pesachim 5:1, that it is forbidden for a person to say to his fellow: "Here is the money, go and buy me meat for Passover"; but he says to him: "Here is this money, go and buy me meat for roasting."

(ד) Rather — Rosh there.

(ה) And a kid, etc., but, etc. — Gemara there.
