Seif 1: Regarding any animal. Even if it is not a kid or lamb. Rosh explains this according to Rashi, because observers may say that he consecrated the animal’s monetary value for a Korban Pesach, whether the animal is large or small. Beit Yosef gives the same reason. This is also implied by the Gemara, which excluded only wheat and did not distinguish among different animals.
Whether. As stated there, “this meat,” meaning whether slaughtered or not.
Because it appears that he consecrated it, etc. This follows Tosafos and applies specifically to a kid or lamb. According to Rashi, however, the concern is that it appears he consecrated its monetary value for the Korban Pesach, as explained above. This is also implied by the Jerusalem Talmud, Pesachim, chapter 7, halachah 1. It is forbidden for a person to say to his fellow, “Here is the money; go and purchase meat for Pesach for me.” He may say, “Here is this money; go and purchase meat for roasting for me.”
