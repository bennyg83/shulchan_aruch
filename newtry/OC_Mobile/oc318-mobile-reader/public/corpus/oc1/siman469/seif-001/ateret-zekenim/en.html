It is forbidden to say "this meat is for Passover," but meat of fish and fowl is permitted (my teacher in the Bach). And some forbid [even] a chicken (Maharil). And if one said "this meat is for Passover," it is forbidden to eat it and even for benefit it is forbidden, and it requires burial because it appears as though one consecrated consecrated items outside [the Temple]. Nevertheless, regarding chickens one does not forbid b'dieved, because it is only a stringency l'chatchila; so it appears to me from the words of the Talmud.
