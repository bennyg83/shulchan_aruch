It is forbidden to say, “This meat is for Pesach.” Regarding fish and poultry, however, it is permitted. This is from my teacher, Bach. Some authorities forbid saying this even regarding a chicken. This is the view of Maharil. If one said, “This meat is for Pesach,” the meat may not be eaten. It is even forbidden for benefit and requires burial, because the statement appears as though he consecrated an offering outside the Beis HaMikdash. Nevertheless, in the case of chickens, the food should not be prohibited after the fact, because the restriction is only a stringency observed initially. This appears to me from the words of the Gemara.
