On the road. The reason is that it is very burdensome to walk a long distance barefoot. But when one goes to a field near the city, it is forbidden to put on shoes, and the same applies to a cemetery, unless there is mud and mire or he is among non-Jews. It appears to me that when one sits in a wagon or rides, he must remove them even on a long road. Magen Avraham.
