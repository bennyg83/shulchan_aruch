They are permitted to wear. It appears to me that the reason is that it is very burdensome to walk a long distance barefoot. But when he goes to a field near the city, it is forbidden to put on shoes, and the same applies to a cemetery; see Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 382, unless there is mud and mire or he is among non-Jews. It appears to me that when one sits in a wagon or rides he must remove them, even on a long road.
In the Jewish street. Even though some stores are rented to non-Jews and non-Jews pass there, it is forbidden; Rashdam, siman 19. It appears to me that if many non-Jews pass there, it is permitted.
