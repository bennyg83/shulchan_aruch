(17) Ibid. There is one who forbids studying through mental contemplation. Even though mental contemplation is not equivalent to speech, in matters of mourning it is different, because the primary reason [for the prohibition] is because of joy, and mental contemplation involves joy. Responsa of the Maharil ibid. It seems to me that for this reason it is forbidden to study any discourse, or a question and answer, even on the sorrowful passages, because it brings him joy. It is also forbidden to render halachic rulings unless it is for a sick person who needs it now, as stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 384 seif 1, for a ruling is considered like mental contemplation, as stated above at the end of siman 85.
