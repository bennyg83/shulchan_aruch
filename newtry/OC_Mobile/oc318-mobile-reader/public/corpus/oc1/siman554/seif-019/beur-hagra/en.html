Seif 19: And some forbid, etc. As stated concerning a mourner on a festival in chapter 1 of Kesubos and concerning Shabbos in chapter 3 of Moed Katan. The first opinion maintains that matters practiced privately are more lenient than ordinary mourning. See Tosafos, Yevamos 43b, s.v. ‘It is different,’ etc., where Rabbeinu Tam explains, etc. The wording of the Tosefta at the end of Taanis is: When Tishah B’Av falls on Shabbos, one eats, etc., and withholds nothing from himself. It explicitly permits everything.
