He is permitted in all of them, even, etc. For the baraisa states that he sets it on his table and refrains from nothing. Those who forbid hold that this Shabbos is like one who buries his dead during a festival, where private matters of mourning apply during the festival. According to this, it is also forbidden to wash one's hands in hot water, for that too is called private.
And so is the custom. The attendant announces that private matters of mourning apply. However, if her immersion night occurs on this Shabbos night, the Magen Avraham cited the Shelah that one may rely on the first opinion, and she immerses and has relations. Some are stringent even in this. See Chayei Adam, who wrote that since the immersion night is a mitzvah of onah, one may rely on those who permit it, and the Rama's custom to forbid applies where there is no mitzvah of onah, unlike an immersion night. See also the Biur HaGra, who implies that he inclines halachically toward the Mechaber. Therefore one may rely on leniency at least on an immersion night.
