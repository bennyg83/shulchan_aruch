(40) And likewise they practice — and the shamash announces things done in private as practiced. However if the night of her immersion occurred on this Shabbos — this brought Magen Avraham in the name of Shlah that one may rely on this on the first view and she immerses and has marital relations; and some are strict also in this; and see in Chayei Adam who wrote that since on the night of immersion it is a mitzvah of marital relations, one may rely on this on the permissive view; and the Rama wrote they practice prohibition — meaning in a place where there is no mitzvah of marital relations here; not so on the night of immersion erev, in my humble opinion; and see in Biur HaGra that it is implied from him too that he sides with the halachah per the Mechaber's view; and if so one may rely to be lenient at least on the night of immersion.
