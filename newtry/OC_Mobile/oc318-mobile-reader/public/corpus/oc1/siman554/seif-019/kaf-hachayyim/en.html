(19) [Seif 4] One may recite the entire daily prayer order, etc. It is obvious that studying the order of the Ma'amados is forbidden even if one studies it every day, and not like those who mistakenly recite the Ma'amados also on Tisha B'Av. Birke Yosef os 2, Sha'arei Teshuvah os 4. The same applies to one who recites eighteen chapters of Mishnah every day, or the sacrificial passages of Parshas Tzav and other regular studies that a person has fixed for himself and recites daily, they are forbidden. Shulchan Gavoah os 7. Similarly, reciting the verses of Chok L'Yisrael is certainly forbidden. Zechor L'Avraham os 9 on this seif, Milei D'Chasidusa siman 10 os 90. Regarding Parshas Kadesh and Parshas Ki Yevi'acha, some have the practice of reciting them on Tisha B'Av and some do not, and each person should follow his custom in this. Ben Ish Chai Parshas Devarim os 25. See above siman 25 os 30.
