(א) Comment 19: With etc. — and it appears to me that by day one may be lenient; although in siman 613 on Yom Kippur Magen Avraham was stringent and forbids even by day; nevertheless he was lenient on Tisha B'Av which is d-rabbanan, and there is some reason: Taz wrote there that on Yom Kippur it is forbidden only at night because there is more concern for habit from day; on Tisha B'Av even at night it is permitted — specifically on Yom Kippur when women adorn themselves for the day's honor there is concern for yetzer hara, unlike Tisha B'Av where they go in disgraceful garments — no such yetzer hara concern. Therefore Magen Avraham holds on Yom Kippur from adornment there is concern even by day; but on Tisha B'Av where there is no adornment reason, by day we are not concerned; nevertheless at night there is more concern for habit. Also at night, whether Yom Kippur or Tisha B'Av, if they go adorned and not in disgraceful garments, on Tisha B'Av too we are concerned and stringent.
