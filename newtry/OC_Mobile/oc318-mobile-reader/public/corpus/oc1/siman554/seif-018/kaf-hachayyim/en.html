(18) It is forbidden to adjudicate monetary cases, for legal rulings constitute Torah study. However, if the litigants cannot wait until the next day because they need to travel, it is perhaps permitted since it is a case of public need. Magen Avraham ibid. Eliyah Rabbah ibid. Yad Aharon ibid. Chayei Adam ibid. Mishna Berurah ibid. However, in Siddur Amudei Shamayim by R' Yaakov Emden, he wrote that it is forbidden to sit in judgment, and even if the litigants are pressed for time, for who can compel the judge in this matter? Let the litigants wait or go on their way, see there. The Tosfos Chayyim on Chayei Adam ibid. os 3 cited his words. Pischei Teshuvah os 5.
