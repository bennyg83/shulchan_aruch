When Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos, etc. It appears that one should say Av HaRachamim. Although Tisha B'Av is called a moed, Av HaRachamim is like the kinnos recited on Tisha B'Av; see the end of siman 284. Furthermore, even regarding Tachanun and Tzidduk HaDin the Taz, siman 559, wrote that they should be recited on Tisha B'Av, although our custom is not so. In any case, since this is not mentioned in the poskim, let us not add. The Taz already wrote there, seif katan 5, that it is permitted to eulogize on Tisha B'Av, and he cited Maaglei Tzedek mentioning the souls of earlier rabbis and communities killed for the sanctification of Hashem, and Av HaRachamim. This implies even on Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos.
