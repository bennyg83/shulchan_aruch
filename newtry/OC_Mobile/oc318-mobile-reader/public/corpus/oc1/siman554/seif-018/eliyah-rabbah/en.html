[18] When Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos, etc. It appears to say Av Harachamim — even though Tisha B'Av is called a festival, nevertheless Av Harachamim is like kinot that are recited on Tisha B'Av; see the end of siman 484. And furthermore, for even in Tachanun and Tziduk HaDin the Taz wrote siman 599 that one should say it on Tisha B'Av even though they do not practice thus; nevertheless do not add, since it was not mentioned by the poskim; and the Taz already wrote there seif katan 5 that it is permitted to deliver a eulogy on Tisha B'Av, and this is his language: "Magalei Tzedek mentions the souls of the earlier rabbis and the communities killed for the sanctity of the Name and Av Harachamim," until here; and it is implied also on Tisha B'Av on Shabbos.
