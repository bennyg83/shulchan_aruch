Seif 5: Nevertheless, etc. Tosefta, chapter 2 of Taanis: Pregnant and nursing women fast on Tishah B’Av and Yom Kippur, etc.; on the other fasts they do not fast. They should not indulge themselves in delicacies, but should eat and drink only what is necessary to sustain the child. The same is stated in the Yerushalmi, chapter 1 of Taanis, halachah 5.
