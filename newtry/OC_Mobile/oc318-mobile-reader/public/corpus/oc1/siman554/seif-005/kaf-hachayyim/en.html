(5) Ibid. And wearing shoes. It is forbidden to wear shoes the entire day. Magen Avraham s.k. 1 in the name of the Geonim. The Shelah wrote that some bring their shoes in their hands and when they pray Maariv they put them on. The Eliyah Rabbah os 2 cited his words and wrote that it is possible they prayed Maariv close to nightfall. Furthermore, he wrote above in the name of the Geonim that anyone who does one of all these the entire day on Tisha B'Av should be excommunicated, see there. This implies that his view is that even if they prayed Maariv, it is forbidden by strict law. However, see the Machberes os 2 where he discussed at length and questioned the words of the Eliyah Rabbah and wrote that it is merely an enactment, and if their custom all year round was to pray Maariv while it is still day, it is perhaps permitted. However, for practical purposes he wrote that one should wait until nightfall and it should not be permitted beforehand even if they prayed Maariv, as the later authorities wrote, see there.
