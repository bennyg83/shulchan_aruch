There is no greeting, etc. In Yoreh Deah, siman 385, some say that what we call giving shalom is not called the greeting of their times. This requires study, for the Tur wrote, shalom aleichem, and the Gemara says, shalom alecha, my teacher; therefore one should not be lenient, as the Magen Avraham wrote. In my humble opinion, the Rama meant the phrase used nowadays, tzafra demarei tav, which is not considered a greeting, as written above in siman 89. It also appears to me that on Tisha B'Av everything is forbidden, for it is no better than a mourner within thirty days, where all is forbidden. The Magen Avraham wrote that it is forbidden to send a gift to one's fellow on Tisha B'Av.
