(ב) And so in Chagigah daf 5b: R' Yaakov bar Chati'a was accustomed to receive his rabbi's face every day. When he aged, his rabbi told him not to grieve in walking every day because it is hard for him due to old age; he answered: who diminishes the reward, as written "and he lived still forever he shall not see corruption," and it is written after it "for the wise die" — meaning the verse: why "and he lived still" etc. — because "for the wise die" etc.; and what the wise see in their death, so in their life all the more, end of the Gemara. In Avot deRabbi Natan he challenged: if so why did the husband of the Shunamit ask why you go to him today — not new etc.; what of it, every day there is also a mitzvah — see there. It is not a difficulty: necessarily it was not her way to go to him every day for whatever reason; and when her husband saw she changed her way he wanted to know the reason for the change; and in truth she did not go that day for receiving the rabbi's face but for her son's reason, which he did not know. But in siman 554 s.k. 7 it implies he holds like the first version that on weekdays there is not even a mitzvah — his language: to receive etc. one is obligated on the festival. But on Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh there is no obligation but there is a mitzvah etc.; and see siman 554 s.k. 12, end of his words — behold he holds on Rosh Chodesh and Shabbat there is only a mitzvah, from which on weekdays even a mitzvah there is not. One must say he explains R' Yehuda's words who said a person must receive his rabbi's face on the festival, and concluded from this that on Rosh Chodesh and Shabbat one must go — meaning there is at least a mitzvah; if so on the festival we elevate from three and there is at least obligation — one must say in siman 554 he holds like the first version he wrote here.
