In Shulchan Aruch, seif 12, one who goes to greet, etc. See Shevus Yaakov, part 1, who concludes that one is not obligated to stand before his rebbe on Tisha B'Av. Birkei Yosef cites Beit Yaakov, part 2, who challenges him and concludes that he is obligated. Machazik Berachah wrote that the simple custom in Eretz Yisrael and Chutz LaAretz is that on Tisha B'Av people stand before their rebbe and Torah scholars as on other days, though there is no proof from the custom that this is an actual obligation.
