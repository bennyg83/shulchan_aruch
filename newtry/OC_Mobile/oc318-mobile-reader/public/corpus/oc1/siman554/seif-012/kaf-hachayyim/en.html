(12) Ibid. And if there are verses of consolation among them, one must skip them. From here it is proven that the consolations written at the end of each kinah must be skipped. So too wrote the Pri Chadash in the collected writings on siman 559 seif 5 that those who say a consolation at the end of each kinah are mistaken, for if so, why did [the Mechaber] write here that if there are verses of consolation one must skip them, see there. So too wrote the Machberes on this siman os 3, Kissei Avraham os 1, Ma'amar Mordechai siman 559 os 4. However, see Milei D'Chasidusa siman 10 os 67, who distinguished between verses of consolation and the recitation of consolations at the end of the kinos. Therefore he wrote that one should not deviate from their custom, for at night they do not say the concluding passages of the kinos, but during the day they say the concluding passages of all the kinos that contain consolations, see there. However, we follow the majority view, and also because passive inaction [shev v'al ta'aseh] is preferable. But if there are those who practice this way, one should not protest against them.
