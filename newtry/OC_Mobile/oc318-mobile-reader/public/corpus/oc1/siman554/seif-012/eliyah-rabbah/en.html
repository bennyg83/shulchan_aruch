To greet, etc. Meaning that he went during the week to greet his rebbe for Shabbos. Alternatively, even if there is no obligation during the week, there is nevertheless a mitzvah, as is implied in Chagigah; Magen Avraham. This requires study, for if so, why did Elisha ask the Shunammite, why are you coming to me today, it is neither Rosh Chodesh nor Shabbos, if there is a mitzvah even during the week?
