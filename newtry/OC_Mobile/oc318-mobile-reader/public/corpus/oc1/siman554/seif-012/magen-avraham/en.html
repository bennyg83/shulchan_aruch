The face of his rebbe. It appears to me that he goes during the week to greet his rebbe for Shabbos, for during the week there is no obligation to greet one's rebbe. Alternatively, even if there is no obligation during the week, there is nevertheless a mitzvah, and so it is implied in Chagigah.
On the return it is permitted. For if it were forbidden to return, he would refrain from going for the mitzvah.
