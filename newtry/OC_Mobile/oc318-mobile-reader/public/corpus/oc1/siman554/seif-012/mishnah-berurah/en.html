The face of his rebbe, etc. Even during the week, although there is no obligation to greet his rebbe during the week, nevertheless there is a mitzvah, lest he hear some Torah from him. This is specifically a student going to his rebbe, but the rebbe going to the student is not permitted to pass through water, for if the student needs him, the student should go to his rebbe. Likewise, if both are equal, it is also forbidden.
And likewise on the return it is permitted. For if it were forbidden to return, he would refrain from going for the mitzvah.
