(23) [Seif 5] Pregnant and nursing women fast, etc. Regarding minors, whether one should train them to fast an hour or two before [bar/bas mitzvah age] as on Yom Kippur below in siman 616, see Erech HaShulchan os 2 who wrote that on a rabbinic fast one should not be strict because of the concern of danger, but regarding training in partial fasting [by hours] where there is no concern of danger, one should train them even on other fasts, unlike the Mateh Yehuda on seif 1, see there. So too wrote Beis Hillel in Yoreh De'ah siman 265. See above siman 550 os 9 and os 1.
