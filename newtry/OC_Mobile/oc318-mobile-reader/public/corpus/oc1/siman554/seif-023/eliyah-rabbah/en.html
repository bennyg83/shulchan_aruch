If possible, etc. This requires study, for at the end of siman 533 they permitted it because it is a matter of loss, and in a matter of loss the following seif permits even when it is possible through non-Jews. Perhaps here it discusses a case that is not a matter of loss. In truth, I already challenged this in siman 533, for the Terumas HaDeshen itself indicates that it is not a matter of loss and nevertheless is permitted. What the Magen Avraham here wrote in the name of Terumas HaDeshen, that according to the reason of affliction even a matter of loss is forbidden, is not found in the Terumas HaDeshen. Rather, he says there that work that requires no exertion and is an amateur act is forbidden according to the reason of affliction, and permitted according to the reason of the prohibition of work. But in a matter of loss, it seems that according to all reasons it is permitted even by oneself, for they were not stringent with a matter of loss.
