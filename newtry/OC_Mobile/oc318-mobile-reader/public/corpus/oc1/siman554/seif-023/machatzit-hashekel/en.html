As stated in the Gemara Taanis daf 12b that explains regarding that they forbade on a fast washing, anointing, marital relations, melacha — granted all these have pleasure in them, but melacha is affliction. R' Chiya, R' Yosi bar Avin said: sanctify the fast, call an assembly, gather the elders. Just as on a festival melacha is forbidden, so too on a fast melacha is forbidden. If as on a festival — also at night; so too a fast, even at night. R' Zeira, etc. — gather the elders: just as gathering elders is by day, so too a fast also by day. And Rashi explained gathering elders by day — for at night each is in his house and they are not gathered, end of his words.
