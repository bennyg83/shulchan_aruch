Seif katan 23, that, etc. As written nearby in seif katan 25; see what is written there.
Twilight of Tisha B'Av is forbidden in eating and work. Thus even twilight is forbidden in work, certainly the night.
From here it implies, etc. This refers to the baraisa that when they said work is forbidden, they said only by day, but at night it is permitted. Rashi explained that this refers to a public fast, as later in siman 575, seif 3, and from here it implies that on the night of Tisha B'Av work is permitted. Thus Rashi's words contradict one another.
And it is not comparable to a public fast, as Rashi there compared them. In Taanis 12b the Gemara explains that although bathing, anointing, relations, and work were forbidden on a fast, work is distress rather than pleasure. The verse says, sanctify a fast, call an assembly, gather elders. Just as an assembly is forbidden in work, so a fast is forbidden in work. It might follow that just as an assembly applies even at night, so the fast applies at night. Rabbi Zeira derives from gathering elders that just as the gathering of elders is by day, so too the fast. Rashi explains that elders gather by day, for at night each person is in his house and they do not gather.
Or because of affliction. This is Rashi's reason cited in seif katan 25.
