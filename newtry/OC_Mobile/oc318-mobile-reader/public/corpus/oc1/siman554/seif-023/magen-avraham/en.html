A matter of loss. If a market day or caravans arrive, it is permitted to buy from them through a non-Jew, and certainly to sell, for it is a matter of loss, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 380, seif 7, regarding a mourner; Hagahos Maimoniyos and Matteh Moshe. The Rosh wrote in tractate Taanis that regarding business, which is public, they were more stringent on Tisha B'Av than by a mourner. Nevertheless one may be lenient, since it is only a custom.
