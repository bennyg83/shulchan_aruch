Something liable to loss, etc. See what we wrote regarding a fair or caravans in Mishnah Berurah, and it is in Mateh Moshe, siman 742; and what we wrote there, through a non-Jew specifically, I somewhat settled the reason afterward. I found in Eliyah Rabbah who wrote: and this requires further study, for through Cutheans it is clarified in the previous se'if that even without something liable to loss it is permitted even by himself; and perhaps this is [more] public, and for the many he did not wish to be lenient; end quote.
