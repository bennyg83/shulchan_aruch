(א) (s.k. 25) If possible etc. — but Tisha B'Av is because of affliction as Rashi wrote. This is from what we say daf 30b: in a place where they practiced to do melacha etc., Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says: a person should always make himself like a Torah scholar so he will fast. And Tosafot haRosh wrote (but in our Rashi it is not and Rashi did not explain it) and this is his language: so he will fast — meaning affliction is for them because they were idle from melacha.

(2) But in some poskim it is implied, etc. — and likewise he proved in Terumat HaDeshen there and challenges Rashi that nullifying melacha is affliction. Behold on the contrary on daf 12b the Talmud calls doing melacha affliction, brought above seif katan 23, see there. And it must be understood how Terumat HaDeshen understood the words of Rashbag who said "so that he will fast" without Rashi's explanation. For if doing melacha is affliction, what is "so that he will fast"? Therefore it must be that he explained as I wrote in seif katan 23 — that they not distract their minds from affliction, for through doing melacha he will distract his mind from mourning and affliction and will not fast. Not so through nullifying melacha — they will be afflicted through the afflictions forbidden to him, for he will not distract his mind from them. And it must be that Terumat HaDeshen holds that for this reason it would have been fitting to permit melacha that is not laborious and the act of a layman — for such melacha is not distraction of mind. Also what he concluded that likewise the reason is because of prohibition of melacha — meaning not from the aspect that it is affliction; but nevertheless the reason that melacha is forbidden in itself like Yom Kippur (even though he equated it in responsa to Yom Kippur, nevertheless they are not equal in their reasons) — rather on Tisha B'Av the reason is lest he distract his mind, for otherwise the statement of Rashbag "so that he will fast" is difficult as above; and if so it must be what Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 23 and Terumat HaDeshen pilpul to find a reason — for also Terumat HaDeshen holds this reason.

Rather, if it is allowed by a Gentile, it is better to be religious, in order to feel, in the opinion of Rashi DSL, the taste is because of a custom. As above.
