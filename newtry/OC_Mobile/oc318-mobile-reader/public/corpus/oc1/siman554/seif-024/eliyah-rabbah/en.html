A matter of loss, etc. Matteh Moshe, siman 742, wrote that if a market day or caravans came, it is permitted even before midday to buy from them through non-Jews, and certainly to sell, for it is a matter of loss. This requires study, for through non-Jews the previous seif explains that even where there is no matter of loss it is permitted, even by oneself. Perhaps this refers to a public case and for the masses he did not wish to be lenient; this resolves what the Magen Avraham asked from the Rosh.
