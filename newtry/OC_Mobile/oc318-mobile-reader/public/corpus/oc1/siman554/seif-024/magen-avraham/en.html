Whoever does work. This implies even in a place where the custom is to do work, and so it appears from the Rif, Rosh, and Tur. In the abbreviated rulings of the Rosh, he omitted the matter of a place where the custom is to do work and wrote only that one sees no sign of blessing. If so, it requires study why people are not careful about this. Nevertheless, from the Gemara it implies that this is specifically when one sets himself to work and diverts his attention from mourning. Therefore even though it is permitted to engage in business after midday, he should not prolong his affairs, so as not to divert his attention from mourning.
