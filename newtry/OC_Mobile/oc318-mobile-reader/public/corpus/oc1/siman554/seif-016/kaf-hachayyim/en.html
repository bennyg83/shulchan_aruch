(16) [Seif 3] There is one who forbids, etc. The Beit Yosef wrote this in the name of the Agur who wrote in the name of Maharil Moelin, and it is in the aforementioned responsa of the Maharil. See Shem HaGedolim by the Chida, section on Great Ones, letter yud os 238, who wrote that the Maharil is Mahari Segal and he is Mahari Moelin, see there. The reason he wrote this in the Shulchan Aruch in the name of "there is one who forbids" even though the Beit Yosef did not cite a dispute on this, is because generally we hold that mental contemplation is not equivalent to speech, except under duress, as stated above in siman 47 and siman 62, see there. Additionally, one can say that this is the manner of the Beit Yosef, sometimes when he finds a position held by only a single authority, he writes it in the name of "there is one who says," as written by Yad Malachi in the rules of the Shulchan Aruch os 12, see there.
