Forbidden. The Taz wrote all the more so one must forbid for those who have gaiters of leather — that is, both what is on the legs of leather, and also those that are of cloth, only that the edges are of leather are forbidden, end of his words. And the Bach is stricter to go completely barefoot. And even though it is forbidden on Tisha B'Av to wear sandals, nevertheless they bless "who has provided all my needs," and the Arizal ruled not to bless.
