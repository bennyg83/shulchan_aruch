Forbidden. The Taz wrote that certainly one must forbid those who have leather leggings, meaning even where the part on the feet is leather, and even those made of cloth but with leather soles are forbidden. The Bach is stringent that one should go entirely barefoot. Magen Avraham. Although wearing shoes is forbidden on Tisha B'Av, nevertheless one recites the blessing, Who has provided me all my needs; but the Arizal ruled not to recite it.
