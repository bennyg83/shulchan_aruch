And it is forbidden to read from the Torah, etc. — one who undertook to ascend to the Torah on Tisha B'Av and it happened that he is a mourner may ascend to the Torah on the morning of Tisha B'Av, since it is bad matters, and everyone is forbidden in Torah like him. Beit Yehuda volume 2 siman 84. And it is simple that certainly he may ascend to the Torah in the morning and read the haftarah even though he is a mourner. Machatzit HaShekel seif 1. And Beit Din HaSh'at seif 1. And he wrote and it is implied that even if he did not undertake to ascend to the Torah on Tisha B'Av the law is likewise. And see below siman 599 seif 55.
