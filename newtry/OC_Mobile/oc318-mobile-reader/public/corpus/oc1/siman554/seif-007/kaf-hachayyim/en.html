It is forbidden to study Torah, etc. One who has an established practice of being called up to the Torah on Tisha B'Av and it happens that he is a mourner, he may be called up to the Torah at Shacharis on Tisha B'Av, since it consists of sorrowful matters and everyone is forbidden in Torah study just like him. Beis Yehuda vol. 2 siman 84. This is obvious, for he may certainly be called up to the Torah at Shacharis and read the Haftarah even though he is a mourner. Machberes os 1. The Sha'arei Teshuvah os 1 cited his words and wrote that this implies the same applies even if he does not have an established practice of being called up to the Torah on Tisha B'Av. See below siman 559 os 55.
