A woman after childbirth all thirty days, etc. See the Taz, who challenges Maharshal from siman 618 regarding Yom Kippur. In my humble opinion there is no difficulty, for Maharshal explicitly discusses a woman after childbirth who must fast on Yom Kippur after seven days even if she says she needs to eat, as explained in siman 617. A sick person, however, is discussed even if he does not say that he needs it, and the law says no assessment is required.
