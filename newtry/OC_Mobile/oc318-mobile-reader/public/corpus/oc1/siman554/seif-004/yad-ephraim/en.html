In the Taz, s.k. 5. And all the more so after three days, within seven, when she says 'I need,' etc. And apparently this requires further study: for in the preceding s.k. they are lenient when she does not say 'I need'; and perhaps according to what he wrote at the end, 'and perhaps for this reason they customarily were stringent, as the Rama wrote, and as Maharshal wrote, that after seven even saying 'I need' [she fasts],' if so he explains that his general words apply even when she says 'I need,' like Maharshal; and regarding this he says that it applies specifically after time [after seven].
