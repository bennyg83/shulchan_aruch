Order. The custom is to recite Tehillim and Shir HaYichud at Minchah. Although Torah study is forbidden the entire day, one must say that they hold that since these are recited as supplication, it is permitted. For this reason they are lenient regarding tefillin at Minchah. In Pozna they recite the portion for the following day and that of the previous day.
The parsha. It appears to me that this means the reader may review the parsha before reading it, or that it discusses Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos, when even an individual may review the parsha, as written at the end of siman 285. See siman 553; Magen Avraham. The Taz writes that it means that one who is accustomed to review the parsha on that day, for example when Tisha B'Av falls on Thursday, may do so, for it is part of the order of the day; see there.
