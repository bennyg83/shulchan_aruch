But he may read, etc. Nevertheless, it is forbidden to study in them a derashah, question, or answer, because this brings him joy; Matteh Moshe. The Taz writes that this is only the plain meaning, but analytical study, even in thought, to explain a difficult matter, is forbidden. This is not entirely implied by the Levush, and the matter requires study. In any event, the explanation of Iyov, which is difficult, is cited by the Beit Yosef as permitted, and we rule that it is permitted.
