It is permitted to read, etc. In Pozna the custom is not to say Tehillim and Shir HaYichud, but on the following day they say that of the previous day and that day; Levush. In this country the custom is to say them at Minchah. Although Torah study is forbidden all day, one must say that since they are said as supplication, it is permitted to say them at Minchah. For this reason they are lenient with tefillin at Minchah.
