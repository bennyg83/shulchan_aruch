(14) There. And the chapter "These may shave." And even though it contains several laws of nidui and cherem, it is permitted to study; but certainly to carry and give before a Torah scholar — since the matter is publicized greatly, it is possibly forbidden. Responsa Maharil siman 201. And Beit Din Beit Yosef. Magen Avraham seif katan 4. And likewise the Taz seif katan 2 that even in a place where study is permitted — that is, that he should study in the plain sense of matters, but not through pilpul; and even in thought to explain a difficult matter is forbidden because he will have joy after he settles himself, end of his words. And Beit Din Mishna Berurah seif 4; and see further below seif 15.
