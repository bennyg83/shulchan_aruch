Sick person. One who was mentally impaired and recovered from day to day may eat meat and drink wine the entire week and does not fast on Tisha B'Av. Maharil.
To fast. The Taz wrote that this applies specifically after seven days, but within three days, even if she says she does not need to eat, she should not fast and is forbidden to fast, since Shabbos is desecrated for her, as written in siman 330. Certainly after three days and within seven, when she says, I need to eat, she is forbidden to fast. After seven days, even if she says, I need to eat, she fasts, unless she is somewhat ill. This is also the agreement of Maharshal and Maharil; see siman 618.
