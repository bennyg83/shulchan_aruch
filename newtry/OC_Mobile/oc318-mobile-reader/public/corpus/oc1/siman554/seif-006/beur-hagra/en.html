Seif 6: A woman after childbirth, etc. See Taz. Just as instructing a non-Jew on Shabbos was permitted, and likewise wearing shoes for a woman after childbirth during the entire first thirty days, and anointing for a sick person whose life is not in danger on Yom Kippur, because these prohibitions are D’Rabbanan and the Sages did not enact their decree in such a case, the same applies here.
