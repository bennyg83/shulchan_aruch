In a place of illness, etc. — see in the book Pitchei Olam that in a place where cholera is not prevalent, Heaven forbid, he should eat less than a kotevet within the time of eating a peras, and likewise for drinking per the measures in Shulchan Aruch siman 618 (thus one should instruct a questioner on Tisha B'Av that by this the fast is not completely uprooted, and the Merciful One desires the heart); and one who wishes to fast in a place where the illness is not in wrath, Heaven forbid, should be advised and warned not to go from the threshold of his house all day, and when he goes out he should carry around his nose and mouth a piece of camphor and a bit of mint grass, end of his words.
