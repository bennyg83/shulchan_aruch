Sick person. See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef wrote that some say that if a nursing woman's infant is sick, and the doctor says that the mother's fast will alter the milk and harm the infant, she may eat. See Devar Shmuel, siman 107, who wrote that if the nursing woman's weakness is so great that she does not have enough milk to nurse, and the child refuses to eat or nurse except from her and there is danger for him, any safek nefashos overrides Shabbos and Yom Kippur, and certainly Tisha B'Av, which is D'Rabbanan; see there.
