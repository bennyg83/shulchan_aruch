The parsha, etc. The Magen Avraham explained that the reader may review the parsha before he reads it. This seems obvious, for the parsha Ki Solid Banim is relevant to the day. In Eliyah Zuta I explained that the Rama and Minhagim refer to the weekly parsha. The Taz similarly wrote that one accustomed to review the parsha on that weekday, such as when Tisha B'Av falls on Thursday, may do so. The Magen Avraham also explains that it discusses Tisha B'Av falling on Shabbos.
