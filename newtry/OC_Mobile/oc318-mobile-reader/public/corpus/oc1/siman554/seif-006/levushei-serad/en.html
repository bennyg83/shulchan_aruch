Taz seif katan 3. It appears that this is what he said. It was difficult for him — for behold the essence of the mitzvah to review the parasha of Shemot is on erev Shabbos, and Tisha B'Av does not fall on erev Shabbos; therefore he explained "one whose way is to review Shemot on Thursday" — because he is occupied on erev Shabbos with Shabbos needs. And Magen Avraham in seif katan 7 explained in another way; and see responsa Arba Turim Even siman 11.
