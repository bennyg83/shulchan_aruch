Taz, seif katan 3. It appears that this is his meaning. He was troubled because the principal mitzvah of reviewing the weekly portion twice with the verse and once with Targum is on Erev Shabbos, and Tishah B’Av never falls on Erev Shabbos. He therefore explains that this refers to one whose practice is to review it on Thursday because he is occupied on Erev Shabbos with Shabbos preparations. Magen Avraham in seif katan 7 explains it differently. See Responsa Arbaah Turei Even, siman 11.
