Magen Avraham, s.k. 22. Meaning, etc.: that is to say, in the Tur it is stated 'there is no greeting of peace to friends'; and on this the Bach wrote, meaning Torah scholars, etc.; and it is implied that to another who is not a Torah scholar it is permitted to give greeting; and on this he wrote that in the Mordechai it is stated 'to his fellow,' etc. And Magen Avraham wondered: for certainly the Tur also admits that it is forbidden to give greeting to any person; and what he wrote 'there is no greeting of peace to friends' means that friends who know the law do not give greeting to any person; and according to the one who says that 'to friends' does not serve as an object going out to another, but rather for themselves, as if he said friends do not ask after peace.
