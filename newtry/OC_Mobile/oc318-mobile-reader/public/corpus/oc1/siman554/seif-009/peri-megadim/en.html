See Taz; and see siman 553, letter 2; and immersion in its time on Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos: see Magen Avraham, letter 20, who rules in the name of Shenei Luchos HaBris that it is permitted; and see Eliyah Rabbah, letter 19, which implies that he forbids marital relations with her; and with Hashem's help in Magen Avraham it will be clarified regarding this. Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos: Eliyah Rabbah wrote that some say one says Av HaRachamim, for it is like kinot, and they mention the souls of the righteous; and in siman 559, letter 6, in the Taz, that it is permitted to deliver a eulogy on Tisha B'Av for a Torah scholar, and the same law applies to Av HaRachamim.
