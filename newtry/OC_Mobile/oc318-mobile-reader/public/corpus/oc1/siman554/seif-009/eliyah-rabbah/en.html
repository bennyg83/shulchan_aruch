Levush: rather, since, etc., they practice permissively, etc. Meaning, she washes and combs on Motzaei Tisha B'Av night, and he was not concerned that she will hurry home. But during the day, even at twilight, it is forbidden, as explained at the beginning of his words. What he later wrote, if she cannot wash on Tisha B'Av itself, means the night of Tisha B'Av itself, and this is also implied in siman 551, seif 16.
