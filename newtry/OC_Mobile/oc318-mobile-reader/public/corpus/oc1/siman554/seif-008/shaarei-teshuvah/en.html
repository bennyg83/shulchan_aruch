Immersion — Be'er Heitev: see Mahari Bruna in the name of Zera Emet — if it happened that she did not bathe and did chafifah on Tisha B'Av, b'dieved one should permit her to bathe and do chafifah at the conclusion of Tisha B'Av; and see there; and see in responsa at the end of the book Mishnat R' Eliezer on daf 91, 14; and I was amazed at what he mocked the Be'er Heitev — how did he not see in Magen Avraham that Be'er Heitev did not innovate anything, only copied it in the letter of Magen Avraham's words; and what he wrote "and see siman 591," etc. — meaning that there it is explained that specifically if it is impossible at the conclusion of Tisha B'Av, and he did not come to dispute but to explain; and the disputing rabbi did not pay attention to him that it is so in Magen Avraham, only at the end he mentions from Magen Avraham's words to learn from him that chafifah is permitted on Tisha B'Av in all respects; and in my humble opinion Magen Avraham's intent was to show a place for the Rama as written; and see there that for the law his view is that even l'chatchila she should do chafifah on Tisha B'Av and at the conclusion of Tisha B'Av do chafifah a little (and then she must be careful not to go completely barefoot lest mud and mire stick to her, as Levush wrote, if she wishes to practice like the Rama — when possible to do chafifah at the conclusion of Tisha B'Av, command the woman to stay one hour in the business of chafifah and she fulfills all views of Rashi and Tosafos; and thus it is fitting to instruct, see there).
