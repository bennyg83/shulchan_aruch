But nowadays. Meaning, since women have the custom to count seven clean days specifically for even a drop of blood, the immersion is no longer at its proper time. Why should she immerse, since the mitzvah of onah cannot be fulfilled on Tisha B'Av?
She should not immerse on it. Rather, she should wash and comb on Erev Tisha B'Av, and on Motzaei Tisha B'Av she should comb a little before immersion, for immersion must be close to combing. Regarding wearing white garments on Tisha B'Av, see above, siman 551, seif 3, in the gloss, and the Mishnah Berurah there.
