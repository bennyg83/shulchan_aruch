Even regarding the sorrowful passages in Yirmiyahu, Kinos, and Iyov, this applies specifically to children who do not understand what they are saying, but a child who has reached the level of understanding what he says is permitted to study the sorrowful passages in Yirmiyahu. Bach. Knesset HaGedolah in the glosses os 1, Ateres Zahav, Shulchan Gavoah os 1. The Taz s.k. 1 wrote that Iyov and other subjects that are permitted for adults are also permitted to study with children, and they only forbade the studies that children learn in their regular curriculum, namely Chumash and Gemaras, see there. This is also the view of the Dagul Merevavah. However, the Magen Avraham s.k. 2 wrote that it is forbidden to study anything at all with others; one may only teach them about the destruction, which is merely a narrative account and breaks the child's heart, see there. This appears to be the view of the Nishmas HaShulchan os 1, and the reason as the Ya'aros P'ri wrote is that it is forbidden to study anything at all with others, because the adult derives joy from studying with others; so too wrote the Machatzis HaShekel. Thus there are three opinions in this matter: according to the Bach, only with a child who understands is it permitted to study the sorrowful passages; according to the Taz, even with one who does not understand, the sorrowful passages are permitted; and according to the Magen Avraham, whether the child understands or not, even the sorrowful passages are forbidden, and it is only permitted to teach him about the destruction. It seems that according to all opinions, if the child studies by himself the sorrowful passages, whether he understands or not, it is permitted. Therefore, one should allow children to study the sorrowful passages on their own so that they do not go roaming the streets and occupy themselves with idle matters.
