Seif 8: Therefore, etc. Although Tosafos there asked from the end of Yoma 88a that even according to the opinion that immersion at its proper time is not a mitzvah, immersion is permitted, as it states: ‘What is the reasoning of the Sages? The Sages hold,’ etc., implying that the first Tanna of the first baraisa holds that it is not a mitzvah and nevertheless permits immersion. Tosafos in Yoma there answers that the meaning is: immersion is unnecessary for the sake of prayer, but she should immerse for the sake of immersion at its proper time. See there, s.v. ‘The Sages hold.’ Ramban answers that the baal keri there had an emission before Yom Kippur, so the immersion was not at its proper time, and it was permitted only for prayer. The halachah follows the view that immersion at its proper time is a mitzvah, like the first Tanna there and Beis Shammai and Beis Hillel, who disagree with Rabbi Yose bar Yehudah. This is the meaning of, ‘Immersion, etc.,’ unlike Rabbi Chanina ben Antigonus. It is also unlike Tosafos’ alternative answer concerning sacred foods, for the difficulty from the baal keri remains. It must therefore be explained as above.
