And the chapter Elu Megalchin. Although it contains several laws of one who is under ban or excommunication, it may be studied, for it is no worse than a mourner. Only regarding public mourning is Tisha B'Av more stringent than a mourner, but in private mourning, such as studying by oneself, Tisha B'Av is more lenient, as Tosafot wrote in HaCholetz. But analytical give-and-take in halacha is certainly forbidden; responsum of Maharil, siman 229.
