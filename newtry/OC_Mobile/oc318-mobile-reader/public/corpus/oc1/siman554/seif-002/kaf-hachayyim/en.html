(13) [Seif 2] One may study Midrash Eichah, etc. And likewise the aggados of the destruction in the chapter HaNizikin (Gittin ch. 5) and in chapter Chelek, and to read about the destruction mentioned in Yosippon is permitted. Levush. Mishna Berurah os 3.
(14) Ibid. And the chapter Eilu Megalchin [in Moed Katan]. Even though it contains several laws of one who is excommunicated or under a ban, it is permitted to study. However, to certainly engage in detailed scholarly discussion before Torah scholars, which greatly publicizes the matter, is possibly forbidden. Responsa of the Maharil siman 201. The Beit Yosef cited his words. Magen Avraham s.k. 4. So too wrote the Taz s.k. 2 that even where it is permitted to study, this means studying the plain meaning of the text, but not through pilpul [dialectical analysis]. Even to mentally analyze a difficult matter is forbidden, because he will derive joy after it becomes clear to him. The Mishna Berurah os 4 cited his words. See further below os 17.
(15) Ibid. And the commentary on Iyov. However, in Orchos Chayyim, Laws of Tisha B'Av, he wrote in the name of the Ra'f that specifically the commentary on Eichah [is permitted], but not the commentary on Iyov, because it is too deep, see there. This is also in the Beit Yosef in the name of the Kol Bo. The Yafeh L'Lev vol. 2 os 2 cited their words and wrote that this is a reasonable position as discussed.
