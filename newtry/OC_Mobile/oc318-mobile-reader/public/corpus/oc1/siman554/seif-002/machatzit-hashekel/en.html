Seif katan 2, and children, etc. Even though the child does not understand, good and bad subjects are the same for him, for he rejoices in knowing how to read and say the meaning of the words even without grasping the matter. Therefore the Magen Avraham writes that it is forbidden to study with others, as in Yoreh Deah. Accordingly, with a child, whether he understands or not, it is forbidden, even sad matters, because of the teacher's prohibition. However, since the Shach writes that where the public needs him it is permitted, and a teacher of children is considered needed by the public, one could have said this is permitted. Still, on Tisha B'Av the mourning applies also to a minor who has reached education, so it is more stringent. The Taz held that the prohibition of studying with the child is not because of the child but because of the teacher, and this applies specifically to subjects that are not sad matters.
