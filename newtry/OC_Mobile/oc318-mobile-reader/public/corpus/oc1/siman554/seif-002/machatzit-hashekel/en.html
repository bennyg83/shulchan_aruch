(Seif katan 2) And infants, etc. — even though he does not understand, etc.; if so, regarding the infant good and bad things are one; nevertheless he rejoices in what he knows to read and say — explaining the words even though he does not know the content of the matter. And therefore, etc., with others as written in Yoreh De'ah. And if so, with an infant — whether he understands or does not understand — it is forbidden; and even bad matters from the prohibition of the teacher. However according to what is written in Yoreh De'ah there — if many need him, it is permitted. And Shach wrote that a teacher of infants is considered like many, it must be so. And also the Taz who did not lighten except after three days before — from this Magen Avraham understood siman 599 seif katan 8 that Tisha B'Av is called after three days. And perhaps since here on Tisha B'Av the mourning applies also to the minor who has reached education, it is stricter; and the Taz wrote here that all study with the infant is not because of the infant, for the infant is not joyful in his learning, and the prohibition is only because of the teacher. And if so, even an infant who does not understand is forbidden. And this is specifically what is not bad matters.
