(There in Magen Avraham, s.k. 20) And see in the work Eliyah Rabbah that his opinion is to forbid; and he wrote regarding this Magen Avraham's intent whom he cited; and see there that his opinion is that in this there is no immersion in its time of mitzvah; and see in Peri Megadim who explained Magen Avraham's intent differently; and in any case the opinion of Eliyah Rabbah is to forbid. And it was said to me that so my father and master, of blessed righteous memory, was stringent [from the Gaon Ksav Sofer].
