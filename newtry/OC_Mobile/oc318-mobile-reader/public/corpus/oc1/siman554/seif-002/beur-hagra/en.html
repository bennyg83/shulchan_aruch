(1) Sab and Morar 20. R. D. L.T. actually reads without Pi. and Z.S. and also to study 2. And finish it as follows:

And chapter 26. Like a mourner — as the Rosh wrote in chapter 3 of Moed Katan: as stated in Mishnah chapter 10 of Taanit: if a relative died for him, they sit before him and converse before him in laws of mourning etc. — forty days he is forbidden, meaning to converse with them; nevertheless we learn from there from what is stated in laws of mourning forty days this is permitted, and he even permitted conversing before him; and so other matters stated in that chapter — all words of rebuke and pain, and permitted like Iyov and Amora'im.
