In Shulchan Aruch: And it is forbidden to read from the Torah, etc. And Machatzit HaShekel wrote in the name of Beit Yehuda volume 2: one who undertook to ascend to the Torah on Tisha B'Av and became a mourner — he is permitted to ascend to the Torah in the morning, since it is bad matters and everyone is forbidden in Torah like him, see there. And Machatzit HaShekel wrote that it is simple he may ascend to the Torah and read haftarah; and it is implied that even if he did not undertake to ascend to the Torah on Tisha B'Av the law is likewise.
