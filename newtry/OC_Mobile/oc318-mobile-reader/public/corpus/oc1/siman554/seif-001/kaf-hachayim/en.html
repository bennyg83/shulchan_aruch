(1) [Seif 1] Tisha B'Av is forbidden in bathing, etc. And everything the Sages forbade is forbidden until night, such as eating and drinking; except things that are only custom not mentioned in the Talmud, such as sitting on the ground — in this some practice to be lenient after noon. Shlah page 199b. And likewise the Bach siman 599. And Rosh Chodesh wrote: let one who does one of all these all day be in excommunication. And Beit Din Hatnia and She'ilat Ya'avetz. Magen Avraham seif katan 1. Eliyah Rabbah seif 2. However those who go among the gentiles and engage in commerce after noon — they are permitted to wear shoes, and from them everyone learned to be lenient, and one should protest against them. Magen Avraham there. Eliyah Rabbah there. Mishna Berurah seif 1; and see below seif 5.
