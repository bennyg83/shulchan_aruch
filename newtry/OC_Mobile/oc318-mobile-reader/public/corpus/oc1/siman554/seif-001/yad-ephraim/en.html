In Magen Avraham, s.k. 2. As written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 384, see there; that is to say, accordingly it is forbidden even in harsh subjects, for when he grows older he has joy in this, that he learns with others; only the churban is permitted, because this is only a narrative of events, and also from the child's side there is no joy here, for it breaks a child's heart.
