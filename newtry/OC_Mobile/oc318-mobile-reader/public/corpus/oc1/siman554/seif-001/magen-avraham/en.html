And wearing shoes. It is forbidden to wear shoes the entire day; Geonim. Rabbeinu Chananel wrote that whoever does one of these matters the whole day is under ban; Tanya, Shibolei HaLeket, Maharil, Bach, and Shelah. Those who walk among non-Jews and engage in commerce after midday may wear shoes, but from them everyone learned to be lenient, and one should protest against them.
And children, etc. Even sad matters are forbidden to study with a child, for the child's primary joy is that he knows how to read, even if he does not understand. But a child who understands what he says may be taught sad matters; Bach. It appears to me that it is forbidden to study with others at all, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 384; see there. It is only permitted to teach him the destruction, for that is merely narration and breaks the child's heart.
And sad matters. It appears to me that one may not read of calamities of non-Jews; see what I wrote in siman 591, seif 5.
