[1] [Levush] And even to lie down, etc. And it is possible that it is forbidden to touch it as on Yom Kippur (Darkei Moshe), and it appears to me that one may be lenient by day (Magen Avraham), and study is required — for at the end of siman 615 he forbids even by day if it is reasonable for him to distinguish between Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av in this; if so, whence does he have to distinguish to study at night from Yom Kippur? However, the Taz there permits touching on Tisha B'Av entirely, since she goes about in soiled garments and will not desire it so much; also from the reason of the Levush there because of keri it is implied that on Tisha B'Av it is permitted; nevertheless it is good to be strict.
