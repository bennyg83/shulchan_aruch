And children — the Taz wrote this to exclude the Bach's view; and from Magen Avraham seif 2 it appears a child twelve years old, somewhat grown and understanding — even the Taz concedes forbidden to study alone what is joy for him; and specifically a small one who has distress in study, etc. And see siman 591 in Magen Avraham letter 38. Eliyah Rabbah and Eliyah Zuta: words of Torah are forbidden all day until nightfall — whatever is forbidden by law; and specifically sitting on benches that is custom — permitted after noon. See Tur: one who eats and drinks receives makkat mardut; and study is required whether for other afflictions; and if he ate during twilight whether lashes also at nightfall — see responsa Razd in practice of Iza and Shibuta in Beit Yosef that one does not lash makkat mardut on doubt; and in Shoshanas HaAmakim in makkat mardut we wrote from this; difference for testimony disqualification — Rambam laws of testimony ch. 10: obligation is from their words; see Rambam Laws of chametz and matzah ch. 1 hal. 7 regarding Pesach — nevertheless implied in hal. 6 no; hal. 10 "enjoys" implies no lashes; nevertheless rule: all these if not asked, likewise no lashes; rabbinically yes; see there.
