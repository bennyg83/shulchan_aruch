They are idle in it — the Taz wrote the prohibition regarding children is not because they rejoice, for they certainly have no joy, but because of the teacher who rejoices in words of Torah when he studies with them, end of his words; and he comes to exclude the Bach's view; and from Magen Avraham it is implied he agrees with the Bach that they have joy. Know that per the Taz, when children study by themselves there is no prohibition; however in truth the Gemara's wording and Teshuvot HaRashba "are idle in it" implies completely idle; and in particular a child between twelve, somewhat grown and understanding — even the Taz concedes it is forbidden to study alone in improper matters, because it is joy for him, as Peri Megadim wrote:
