Levush: and he must recite Asher Yatzar, etc. In siman 613, seif 2, he wrote this only if one wiped or rubbed. It appears to me that regarding Yom Kippur, which is according to the Torah, he was stringent. This resolves the Taz's difficulty on the Tur in siman 613. What the Tur wrote here, between Tisha B'Av and Yom Kippur, refers back to those obligated in immersion, not to what follows. Shiltei Gibborim wrote that women who cook on Tisha B'Av may wash meat with their hands; see siman 551, seif katan 35.
