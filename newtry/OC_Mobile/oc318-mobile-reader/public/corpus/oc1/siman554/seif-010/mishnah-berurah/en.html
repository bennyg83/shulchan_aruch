A person washes his hands in the morning. Because of the ruach ra'ah that rests on the hands, which is like being soiled with mud or excrement and is permitted. So wrote the Beit Yosef in siman 613. See there in the Biur HaGra, who questions this ruling. Therefore it appears to me that one should not recite Al Netilas Yadayim in the morning after that washing. Rather, after relieving himself, he should wash his hands until the end of the finger joints and then recite Al Netilas Yadayim because of prayer. See Matteh Yehudah, who wrote that it is permitted to wash one's hands even for Minchah, because it is like receiving the Divine Presence and like an immersion for a mitzvah, which they permitted.
