Seif 10: He washes, etc. They prohibited only washing for pleasure, as is evident from the ruling concerning one who has scabs, etc., and from the statement that all those obligated to immerse, etc., and that one going to greet his teacher may cross through water, etc. All the more so is this permitted for prayer. There is no greater case of soiled hands than this, concerning which they said, ‘A hand to the eye,’ etc.; and there is no greater discomfort than being unable to touch. There is also proof from the Yerushalmi, as written in seif 11.
