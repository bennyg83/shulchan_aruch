Seif katan 10, immersion, etc. Since we require closeness, etc. Rashi and the Sheiltos disagree. According to Rashi, initially the combing should be on the day before the immersion night, lest because she is hurried and rushing home she not comb properly. According to the Sheiltos, it is preferable to comb at night before immersion, so that the combing is as close to the immersion as possible. Therefore the Beit Yosef wrote in Yoreh Deah, siman 199, that the custom is for her to begin combing while it is still day and continue until night. If her immersion falls on Motzaei Shabbos, when she cannot comb while it is still day, the Rama writes there that she combs on Friday and then repeats some combing on Motzaei Shabbos. The same applies here on Tisha B'Av. See siman 551, seif 16, in the gloss, which states that even if she immerses on the night of the tenth of Av, she may wash on Erev Tisha B'Av if she cannot wash on the night of the tenth. This implies that if she can wash on the night of the tenth, she may not wash on Erev Tisha B'Av, and we rely on the view of the Sheiltos.
