(s.k. 10) Immersion etc. — requires close etc.; Rashi and Sheiltot disagree: for Rashi l-chatchila the combing should be on the day before immersion, on the night of immersion we are concerned that because of tumult and hurrying home she will not comb well; for Sheiltot it is better she comb at night before her immersion so combing is close to immersion as much as possible; therefore the custom — Rabbi Yosef in Yoreh De'ah siman 199 wrote she should begin combing from erev and engage in combing until dark. If her immersion is motzaei Shabbat, when she cannot comb from erev, Rama wrote there she combs on erev Shabbat; and what he wrote she returns and combs etc. — if so the same here on Tisha B'Av; and see siman 551 seif 16 in gloss that wrote: even if she immerses on the night of the 10th of Av, she may wash on erev Tisha B'Av if she cannot wash on the night of the 10th — from this it implies if she can wash on the night of the 10th it was not permitted to wash on erev Tisha B'Av, and they rely on Sheiltot.
