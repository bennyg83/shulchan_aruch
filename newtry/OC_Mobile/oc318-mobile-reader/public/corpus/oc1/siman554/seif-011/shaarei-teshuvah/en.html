Forbidden. See Baer Heitev. See Peri Megadim, part 2, siman 28, that one should be stringent not to go out on Tisha B'Av and Yom Kippur with felt shoes made to the measure of our shoes, which protect the foot so that he does not feel at all that he is barefoot, since this is not included in affliction; one who is stringent will be blessed. He also writes that one must be careful not to walk on Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av with socks whose underside is covered with leather, for that is considered a shoe according to all opinions. So too the author of Leket HaKemach wrote in the name of Mishpetei Shmuel, page 34. See Kenesses Yechezkel, siman 11, and above, siman 128.
