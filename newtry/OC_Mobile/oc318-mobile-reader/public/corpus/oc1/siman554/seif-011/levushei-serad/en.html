Magen Avraham, seif katan 20. See seif 8. Meaning, although it is explained there that nowadays there is no immersion that must take place at its proper time, and therefore she should not immerse, that is because sacred foods are not practiced nowadays and, in any event, she is forbidden to her husband on Tishah B’Av. In a case such as this, however, where she is permitted to her husband on the night of immersion, she may immerse. See Arbaah Turei Even, siman 64, and Minchas Yaakov, Hilchos Niddah, beginning of siman 197.
