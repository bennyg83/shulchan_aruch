After it has been wiped dry, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Taanis halachah 6: as on the public fast days one washes his face, hands, and feet in his usual manner; on Tisha B'Av one washes his hands and passes over his face; and the Tur's textual reading is "over his eyes," and he explained it in this manner. And they said there, in Yom Kippur — one washes his hands and wipes with a cloth and passes the cloth over his face — meaning like a kind of dried vessels; and from this there is proof that washing hands is permitted on Tisha B'Av and Yom Kippur.
