Seif 11: After he dried, etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 1 of Taanis, halachah 6: On a communal fast one washes his face, hands, and feet in the usual manner. On Tishah B’Av he washes his hands and passes them over his face. The Tur’s reading is, ‘over his eyes,’ and he explains it in this manner. They also state there that on Yom Kippur one washes his hands, dries them with a cloth, and passes the cloth over his face, meaning with a cloth that is merely damp after drying. This proves that washing one’s hands is permitted on Tishah B’Av and Yom Kippur.
