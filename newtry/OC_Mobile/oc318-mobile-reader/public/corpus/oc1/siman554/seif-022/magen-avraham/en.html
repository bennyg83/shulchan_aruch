And in Terumat HaDeshen he pilpul to find a reason as written nearby; and it appears to me for this reason melacha is also forbidden at night (for at night too one is obligated to mourn); and likewise Rashi on Pesachim daf 54b — between his suns it is forbidden in eating and melacha; and study is required — for in Taanis daf 13 Rashi from here implies that on the night of Tisha B'Av they are permitted in melacha and the prohibition is only by day, and they do not publicize the matter, end of his words; and from the conclusion of all poskim it is implied that it is forbidden also at night. And in my humble opinion for the public fast days — there the reason is that they be free and gather to pray (see siman 575 seif 3); therefore they are permitted at night, for they did not gather at night, as stated in the Gemara; not so on Tisha B'Av where melacha is not forbidden and it depends on their custom — they practice prohibition; their reason is lest they distract their minds from mourning, or because of affliction; therefore also at night it is forbidden; therefore any melacha that has no delay is permitted, for he does not distract his mind thereby, as above.
