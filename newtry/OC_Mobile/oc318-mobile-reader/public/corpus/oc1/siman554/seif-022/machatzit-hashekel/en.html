Seif katan 22. The text, to his fellow, meaning a scholar, is missing. The Tur should read: there is no greeting among fellows on Tisha B'Av, and ordinary people who do not know, etc. The Bach explained that fellows means Torah scholars, called chaverim. The Magen Avraham objects, for even if the Tur's fellows means scholars, it does not exclude ordinary people. Rather, it means that if an ordinary person who does not know greets a Torah scholar, he should answer softly.
