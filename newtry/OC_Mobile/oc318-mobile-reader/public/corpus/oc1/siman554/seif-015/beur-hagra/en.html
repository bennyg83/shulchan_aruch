Seif 15: Anointing, etc. This is Rambam’s wording. Regarding Yom Kippur, however, he writes that both anointing for pleasure and anointing not for pleasure are forbidden. This is from the Yerushalmi, final chapter of Yoma, and chapter 1 of Taanis: On Yom Kippur, both anointing for pleasure and anointing not for pleasure are forbidden. On Tishah B’Av and a communal fast, anointing for pleasure is forbidden, while anointing not for pleasure is permitted.
But one who, etc. This is permitted even on Yom Kippur, as stated in the Gemara in the final chapter of Yoma.
