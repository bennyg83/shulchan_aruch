And so they practiced, etc. A street in which all the Jews live, even if it is a main road trodden by non-Jews, is forbidden; Kenesses HaGedolah and Rashdam. The Magen Avraham wrote that if many non-Jews pass there, it is permitted. From the language of Kenesses HaGedolah cited above, this is somewhat not implied, and the Rashdam himself requires study. Moreover, let us not add beyond the custom.
