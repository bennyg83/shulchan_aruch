Anointing, etc. Anointing means with something normally used for anointing, such as oil, fat, soap, and the like.
It is forbidden only, etc. It is forbidden even to anoint one limb, as below in siman 614 regarding Yom Kippur. A bride during the thirty days after her wedding may wash her face and also anoint herself so that she not become unattractive to her husband.
