Through thought. Although thought is not like speech, as written in siman 62, here it is different, for the main reason is joy, and thought also contains joy; Maharil. For this reason it appears to me that it is forbidden to study any derashah, question, or answer, even in sad matters, because it gives him joy. It is also forbidden to issue a halachic ruling unless for a sick person who needs it now, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 384, seif 4, for ruling is like thought in Torah, as written in siman 85. It is forbidden to judge monetary cases, for judgment is Torah. However, if the litigants cannot wait until tomorrow because they are pressed to travel, perhaps it is permitted, since the public needs him.
