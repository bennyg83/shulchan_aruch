(20) In Pozna [Posen] the practice is not to say Tehillim or Shir HaYichud, and only the next day they say [both] the previous night's and that day's [portions]. Hagahos HaLevush. In this country the practice is to say them at Minchah. Even though Torah study is forbidden the entire day, it must be said that they hold that since one recites them as a form of supplication it is permitted to say them at Minchah. Magen Avraham s.k. 6. Mishna Berurah os 7. However, he wrote that the practice of Pozna is the correct one, and if they missed one day's Tehillim they should say it the next day as is done all year round. Eishel Avraham os 6.
