(2) To his fellow. (In the Tur it is written "to my fellows") — he explained Torah scholars do not give peace to one another; but in Mordechai he wrote "to his fellow" — implying whoever is his fellow, even if not a Torah scholar, is forbidden (Bach); and we do not know what he is saying, for certainly it is forbidden to give peace to any person; but regarding laymen who do not know — when they give peace, they respond to them in soft language.
