Seif katan 20, and some say, etc. The Shelah ruled, see Sefer Chassidim, that although in seif 8 it was written that nowadays there is no immersion at its proper time and no mitzvah, the Beit Yosef in Yoreh Deah wrote that when her husband is in the city there is a mitzvah even nowadays, since Yehoshua was punished for neglecting one night of procreation. The Shelah holds that the mitzvah of the immersion night is more stringent than the ordinary mitzvah of onah, because conception is more likely close to immersion. Therefore on a weekday Tisha B'Av Chazal did not permit relations even on the immersion night, for the House of our G-d is worthy that one immersion be suspended for it. But when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos, since most poskim permit relations, he relied on the lenient opinion for this great mitzvah. However, Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of his grandfather, the Gaon Maharash, that it is forbidden.
