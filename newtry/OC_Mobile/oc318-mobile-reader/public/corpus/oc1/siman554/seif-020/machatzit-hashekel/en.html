(Seif katan 20) And some say, etc. — and he ruled Shlah, etc. (Essay 8) that Shlah wrote in the gate of the letters, letter kuf. Meaning that he wrote in seif 8 that in this era there is no immersion at its time and there is no mitzvah; nevertheless behold Rabbeinu Yerucham in Yoreh De'ah — when her husband is in the city, even in this era there is a mitzvah. For Yehoshua was punished and did not leave a son, since he nullified Israel one night from marital relations, as stated at the beginning of Megillah (and it should be for Shlah that they are stricter regarding the night of immersion more than other mitzvah of marital relations — for on the night of immersion there is more concern for nullifying marital relations, for a woman conceives only close to her immersion; therefore if the night of her immersion did not occur on Shabbos he did not rely to permit marital relations so much in Avot of R' Natan). If so, since Rabbeinu Yerucham considered immersion a mitzvah when her husband is in the city — therefore marital relations then is an important mitzvah; and meaning when Tisha B'Av falls on a weekday they did not permit marital relations even on the night of immersion, for it is fitting that the house of our God be nullified for it one immersion (as R' Chiya the deputy high priest said, chapter 1 of Taanis); but when it falls on Shabbos, since in any case most poskim permit marital relations for a great mitzvah, he relied on the permissive view. However in Avot of R' Natan he wrote in the name of his elder likewise Mahararash that it is forbidden on erev Shabbos.
