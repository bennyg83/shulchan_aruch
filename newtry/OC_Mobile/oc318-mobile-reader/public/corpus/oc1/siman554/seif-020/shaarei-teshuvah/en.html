Work. See Baer Heitev. See Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah in the name of Mahari Bruna, that Rabbi Yitzchak corrected his Torah scroll on Tisha B'Av, and the scribe corrected errors in it. In my humble opinion, this must be because he did so in order not to leave an uncorrected scroll, or because he needed it to read from that day. It appears that one who develops a Torah insight should not write it on Tisha B'Av. Although the Birkei Yosef wrote to permit it, Tisha B'Av is different, for he rejoices when writing Torah insights, and since it is only one day there is no concern that he will forget.
