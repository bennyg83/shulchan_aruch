4) [Seif 2] One must add from the weekday onto the sacred, etc. This applies both to the prohibition against eating and to the performance of melacha, as written above, siman 608, sec. 1, see there.
5) Ibid. They should wait a little after the emergence of the stars. Meaning, one must wait a little after it is certainly night, so that it is evident that he refrains because of the added sacred time, and not because of the uncertainty of bein hashmashos. Maamar Mordechai, sec. 2. See above, siman 608, sec. 4. One should not prolong it beyond the proper measure, because of the weak and because of pregnant and nursing women. Siddur of Rav Yaavetz; Pischei Olam, sec. 1.
