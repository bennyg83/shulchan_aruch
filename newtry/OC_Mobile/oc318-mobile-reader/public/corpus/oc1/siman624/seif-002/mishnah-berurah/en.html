(3) That they should wait a little after the emergence of the stars - During this time it is forbidden to eat, wash, anoint, etc. However, there is no fixed measure for the addition, and therefore once they pray after the emergence of the stars, all of these become permitted, even though he has not yet made havdalah over a cup. Only eating and drinking remain forbidden until after havdalah, whether Yom Kippur fell during the week or on Shabbos.
