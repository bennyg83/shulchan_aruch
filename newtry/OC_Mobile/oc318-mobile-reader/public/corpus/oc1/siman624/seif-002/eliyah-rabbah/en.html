(2) And even if etc. — Bach ruled to bless at Motzaei Shabbat and it is not a blessing in vain since he benefits; so ruled Tosafot, Taz, and Magen Avraham. It appears for himself it is permissible; for others who do not benefit from his candle it is forbidden to light for them until he makes Havdalah, as Magen Avraham wrote in sec. 7.
