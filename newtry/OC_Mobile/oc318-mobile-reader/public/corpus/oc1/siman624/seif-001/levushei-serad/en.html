Magen Avraham, at the beginning, s.v. 'There are places.' 'Regarding kiddush, according to everyone.' His intent apparently is to what is written in siman 269, that kiddush is recited in the synagogue on Shabbos nights and the cup is given to a child, for it is forbidden for an adult to drink it because kiddush is valid only in the place of the meal. But the phrase 'according to everyone' that he wrote here is somewhat difficult, for the Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of siman 269 that there is an opinion that even an adult may drink it, only that we do not rule that way.
