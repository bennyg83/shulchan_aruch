They pray the evening prayer, etc. The Tur wrote that the Ne'ilah prayer does not exempt one from it. Meaning, this is not like Rav, who says that Ne'ilah is at night and automatically exempts one from the evening prayer, which is at night. Although above, in siman 623, the Tur ruled like Rav, and this is from the words of the Rif, one may say that the Rif himself wrote that nowadays the practice is to pray Ma'ariv after Ne'ilah. This means that in this matter we do not follow Rav.
