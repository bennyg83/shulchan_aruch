1) [Seif 1] One recites the Maariv prayer. For the Ne'ilah prayer does not exempt one from it. Tur and Beit Yosef; Levush; Taz, s.k. 1. The Levush wrote that the reason Ne'ilah does not exempt Maariv is that one mentions in it matters pertaining to Shabbos and Yom Kippur. Even those who extend it into the night are not exempted from Maariv, since they mentioned Yom Kippur in it. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1.
