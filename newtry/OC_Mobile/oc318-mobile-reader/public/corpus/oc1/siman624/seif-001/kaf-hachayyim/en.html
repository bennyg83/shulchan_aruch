1) [Seif 1] One recites the Maariv prayer. For the Ne'ilah prayer does not exempt one from it. Tur and Beit Yosef; Levush; Taz, s.k. 1. The Levush wrote that the reason Ne'ilah does not exempt Maariv is that one mentions in it matters pertaining to Shabbos and Yom Kippur. Even those who extend it into the night are not exempted from Maariv, since they mentioned Yom Kippur in it. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1.
2) Ibid. And he recites Havdalah in the blessing of Chonen HaDaas. As on every other Motzei Shabbos and Yom Tov. Levush. See above, siman 294, and our words there, with G-d's help.
3) And if Yom Kippur fell on Shabbos, one recites the verses of Veyiten Lecha, but not Vihi Noam and VeAtah Kadosh, because the festival of Sukkos will occur during that week. Levush. This is based on what the Rama, of blessed memory, wrote in his gloss above, siman 295. However, we wrote there that according to the words of the Arizal, of blessed memory, one must recite Vihi Noam and VeAtah Kadosh on Motzei Shabbos even when Yom Tov occurs during that week, see there. This is also the custom of the Beit El community, may it be rebuilt speedily in our days, who follow the teachings of the Arizal, of blessed memory, to recite Vihi Noam and VeAtah Kadosh on Motzei Yom Kippur when Yom Kippur fell on Shabbos.
