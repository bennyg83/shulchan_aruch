Se'if 5, A Jew, etc. See the Magen Avraham, and as is stated in the Gemara there. This contradicts what was written in se'if 4.
Nevertheless, etc. Berachos 52b: 'What is Shabbos?' etc., and 'But was it not taught?' etc. Regarding fire that rested, Shabbos and Yom Kippur are equal. See the Be'er HaGolah.
Some say to make havdalah, etc. For it is a fire that rested. See the Taz.
And some say that one should not, etc. As it says there: the fire of the synagogue, etc.
And they eat, etc. As it says in the Midrash, that a heavenly voice goes out on Motza'ei Yom Kippur and says, 'Go, eat with joy,' etc. See Tosafos at the end of chapter 15 of Shabbos.
Some are stringent, etc. As it says in Rosh Hashanah 21a: Rava was accustomed, etc.
And this can be annulled. See the Magen Avraham.
And one should not practice, etc. As it says there: Rav Nachman was sitting, etc. And in Yerushalmi, Chalah chapter 1, halachah 1: There they were concerned for the Great Fast for two days. Rav Chisda said to them: Why do you put yourselves into possible danger? There is a presumption that the court does not delay. Bar Avuy, the son of Shmuel bar Rav Yitzchak, was concerned for himself and fasted two days; his intestines ruptured and he died, meaning that his bowels were severed. Also according to the law there is no need to be concerned, as stated at the beginning of Yom Tov, that we are expert in the fixing of the month; the practice is only because they sent from there, 'Be careful,' etc. This is not so regarding Yom Kippur, where there was never such a custom. See Rashi there, 5b, s.v. 'Mi lo modeh,' etc.
One who fasts, etc. That is to say: although on Rosh Hashanah in such a case he must fast forever, because some say that it is a mitzvah to fast, here there is no mitzvah at all, as above.
We do not say supplications, etc. Because it is a Yom Tov, like Yom Kippur in some measure, as stated in the Midrash on the verse, 'And you shall take for yourselves on the first day,' etc.: From here onward, etc. And they are the days of the dedication of the Temple. See the first chapter of Mo'ed Katan and the second chapter of Shabbos.
And the meticulous, etc. See above, the end of siman 625.
