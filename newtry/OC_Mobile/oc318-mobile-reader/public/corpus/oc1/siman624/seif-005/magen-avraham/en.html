(ד) Of the synagogue alone — meaning if impossible to make Havdalah on both, better to make Havdalah on another candle alone than the synagogue candle alone; and it implies that even if Yom Kippur falls on Shabbat one does not bless on light produced from striking stones, to teach that this day is holy from Shabbat through the light. One does not fast from Yom Kippur until Sukkot on a day one's father died (Hagahot Maimoniyyot) — those are days of joy when they inaugurated the altar in Solomon's days (Maharil). The day after Yom Kippur they rise early to synagogue (Matzah Minhagim). Those who observe two days of Yom Kippur — even if ten — pray only weekday prayer; Beit Yosef explained the reason is lo titgodedu, one congregation in one city; and it appears they should also remove tefillin for the same reason, see siman 31, or because it appears arrogant and leads to dispute. Bach explained the reason one may not pray a prayer the Sages did not institute — it is a blessing in vain; but selichot and supplications he did not protest (Rashdam Yoreh Deah siman 153). Thus Maharil in responsa siman 212: two days Yom Kippur — one does not change all prayers since an individual does not establish; even one hundred and ten do not change prayers, only piyyutim and selichot they may say as they wish and the thirteen attributes as one reading Torah — end; implies as Bach wrote; and for Raavad's reason piyyutim are permitted since it does not appear so much they do so because of Yom Kippur, permission to say selichot any day; nevertheless it appears they should not say them in the middle of prayer only after Yom Kippur, Essen 566; also not read Torah even Vayechal. When Yom Kippur falls Thursday forbidden to prepare Shabbat needs on erev Yom Kippur which is their Yom Kippur; but they eat with others who did not cook for them — so ruled Maharam, and our master in Hilchot Eruvin chapter 8 halakhah 10 — Hagahot Maimoniyyot end Yom Kippur Essen 627 s.v. Rambam: Yom Kippur falling on erev Shabbat or after Shabbat — it appears to me they are like one day and one holiness. That which permitted to eat what others cooked — written in Maharam responsa siman 76: since cooked permissibly, not applicable to forbid due to penalty (see siman 518); even if he knows him permitted, we do not decree lest he increase for him next year, for Israel does not transgress before Him a Torah stumbling block to increase for him, not so for a non-Jew (Essen 325) — if so what a non-Jew cooked on erev is forbidden if he knows him; nevertheless requires study forbidden due to preparation, not worse than something trapped or detached on Yom Tov forbidden on Shabbat; although some hold Yom Kippur and Shabbat are one holiness, to be lenient we do not say so; perhaps even to be lenient it is one holiness; and see children appear on that day; nevertheless if he cooked something impossible to eat raw such as fish, forbidden to eat on Shabbat since one holiness — see siman 595 seif 4 and siman 616 for eruv, and s.v. 693. Magen Avraham wrote: some say one who did once two days cannot retract etc.; Mahariv wrote per this view even a condition does not help — Mahariv siman 191: must practice all his days since he accepted; it appears even if he conditioned initially not to do except this once — not like one who said I will be a nazir less than thirty days, condition invalid since naziriteship not less than thirty days; since he said I will be a nazir condition invalid — here too no; and Ashkenazim permit through release — implies condition in any case invalid, like nazir who has release and condition invalid (see Beitzah 18 and Menachot end). Nevertheless requires study: for nazir, once he said I am a nazir, naziriteship of thirty days applies and condition no longer helps, not so here where this year he is concerned for doubt and observes two days — not obligated forever; why should condition not help? All the more nowadays we are expert in fixing the month and do not observe two days except from custom — if our ancestors did not practice two days on Yom Kippur why should we? I also heard a reason not to observe two days Yom Kippur: when observing a second day Yom Kippur, then from yesterday it is a festival and forbidden to fast — how can he fast? It contradicts itself; therefore not to practice; all the more our custom not to fast between Yom Kippur and Sukkot; and in Likutei Pardes it implies one does not fast at night only by day.
