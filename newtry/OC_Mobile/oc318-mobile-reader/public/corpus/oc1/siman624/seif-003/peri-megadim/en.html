Although on Motza'ei Shabbos one recites the blessing over it, there the reason is that fire was created on Motza'ei Shabbos, when Adam HaRishon struck stones against each other, as in siman 298, Taz 1 and Magen Avraham 2. On Yom Kippur it is a mitzvah to seek it out. See Eliyah Rabbah; it implies that even when Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos, one does not recite the blessing over fire produced from stones, in order to show that this day is more severe, for it was forbidden to use fire. It appears that if he has only fire produced from stones when Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos, he should recite the blessing over it; but this requires analysis, since on Shabbos one need not seek after it, whereas on Yom Kippur, etc. One can resolve this. See the Magen Avraham, and with Hashem's help it will be explained further there.
