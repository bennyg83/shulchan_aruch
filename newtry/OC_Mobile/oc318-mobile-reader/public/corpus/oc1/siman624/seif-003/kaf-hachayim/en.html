(3) And if it falls on Shabbat he says the verses Veyiten lecha but not Vihi noam and Attah kadosh, because Sukkot comes that week (Levush). This is according to what Moreinu wrote that on Shabbat during the Ten Days of Repentance we do not say Tzidkatcha because the angels of Shabbat and repentance argue — if so on Motzaei Yom Kippur that is Shabbat we say Veyiten lecha which is joy, not Tzidkatcha which is judgment — see there at length.
