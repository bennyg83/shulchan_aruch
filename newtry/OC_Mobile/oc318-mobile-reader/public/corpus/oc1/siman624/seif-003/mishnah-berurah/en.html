(4) One makes havdalah, etc. - In places where they make havdalah in the synagogue over a cup on every Motza'ei Shabbos and Yom Tov, now too the shaliach tzibbur should make havdalah and give a taste from the cup to a child, or even to an adult who intends to fulfill his obligation with this havdalah.
(5) Even if it falls on Shabbos - For the reason for the blessing is to restore the soul because of the extra soul that departed, and now, on Yom Kippur, he did not have an extra soul because of the fast. However, many of the later authorities disagree with this and hold that when Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos one should recite the blessing over the spices, and there is no concern of a blessing in vain since he derives benefit. Nevertheless, one should not instruct the congregation this way or protest against them in a place where the custom in the synagogue is not to recite the blessing over the spices; only for himself in his home may he recite the blessing [Pri Megadim]. If it falls on Shabbos, we say 'Veyiten Lecha,' but we do not say 'Vihi No'am' or 'Ve'Atah Kadosh,' because Sukkos falls in the middle of the week.
