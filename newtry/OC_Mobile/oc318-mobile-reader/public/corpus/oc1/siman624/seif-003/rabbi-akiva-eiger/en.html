There: It is written in Responsa Maharam, siman 76, that since it was done permissibly. Seemingly this is not necessary, for even one who cooked on Shabbos deliberately, others may eat it immediately on Motza'ei Shabbos. It is also difficult: even if they cooked a great deal for this person, it should be permitted, for although he has in mind that this is for Yom Kippur, nevertheless the cooking is done with the permission of beis din. Therefore, even if it is revealed before Heaven that today is Yom Kippur, since here he acted under a permitted doubt, he is at least like one who says, 'I thought it was permitted,' and in such a case it is permitted even to the cook himself and to the one for whom it was cooked, immediately on Motza'ei Shabbos. This requires great analysis.
