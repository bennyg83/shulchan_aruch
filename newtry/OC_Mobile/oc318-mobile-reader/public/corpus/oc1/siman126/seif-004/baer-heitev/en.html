The first [three berachos]. Levush wrote — specifically when he remembered before he completed his prayer, but after he completed his prayer he does not return; and Biur Halacha disagrees, and Magen Avraham agreed with Levush, see there. A kohen praying the eighteen [berachos] and it was heard that there is a dead person in a house adjacent to the synagogue — they should not tell him until he finishes his prayer, because this impurity is only rabbinic — see what I wrote in Yoreh Deah siman 373 seif kaf alef, and in the places I indicated there.
