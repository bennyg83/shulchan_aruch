Seif 4, "if he erred," etc., "rather he relies," etc. — in Kesef Mishneh on the preceding tract, that he relies on the Musaf prayer; and furthermore we say at the end of Rosh Hashana that it is only to arrange his prayer.

And "he," etc. — Kesef Mishneh wrote that he found a manuscript version in Rambam and thus is the Tur's version, that he did not err in that which is [said] aloud, etc., and there is no distinction in the first three [berachos] and the last — Magen Avraham.
