[4] And so is the practice. Rama and Levush omitted [Shabbat and Yom Tov] because the Shulchan Aruch cited Rosh Chodesh, as Beit Yosef and Lechem Chamudos wrote several times. Menachem Azariah wrote that for amplification it cited Rosh Chodesh — even though they are not obligated to enjoy it as on Shabbat and Yom Tov — nevertheless they were concerned for burden of the congregation; and all the more so Shabbat and Yom Tov — until here. And it appears that for this reason they were lenient at the beginning of siman 268 regarding Shabbat prayer more than Rosh Chodesh. And accordingly it appears to me to resolve the reason for Bach's ruling that on Shabbat Musaf they do not require him to repeat, but on Rosh Chodesh Musaf they do — examine closely. And even though he wrote before a view to the contrary — that on Rosh Chodesh they were more concerned about bother because of cancellation of melacha — that is according to Baal Halachot Gedolot, who holds that Shabbat is inferior, which is not so for Rif and his supporters who did not mention this distinction in law. If so, when we say on Rosh Chodesh Musaf they require him to repeat, it is specifically Rosh Chodesh — examine closely. And Magen Avraham wrote that Bach's words contradict and there is a scribal error there; and per my humble opinion to resolve without error; and moreover the remnants of Knesset HaGedolah wrote Bach's conclusion according to its plain sense; and moreover if we attribute a scribal error we must say that on Rosh Chodesh in Musaf they require him to repeat and that this is the majority of poskim — and this is difficult, for Rif, Rosh, Rambam, Rabbeinu Yerucham Netiv 15, and Tur ruled that they require him to repeat. And it is more difficult regarding Semag and Tur in Halachot Gedolot — before them at the end of chapter Tefilat HaShachar they ruled that they require him to repeat — and the law of a yachid, see siman 422 seif 1.
