(4) There — they remove him immediately, etc. Beit Yosef wrote it is implied from Rabbeinu Yonah's words that if he erred in blessing velamalshinim they remove him from shaliach tzibur — see sources; and so Bach. But Pri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav ot 2 wrote they remove him for the moment for a time lest Apikorsus was cast upon him, but they do not remove him entirely; and so Rambam's view in chapter 15 of Hilkhot Tefillah, and not like Rabbeinu Yonah — end of his words. And so Pri Megadim in Eshel Avraham ot 1 that they do not remove him except in that prayer specifically, since it is only a general concern and not entirely, and not like Rabbeinu Yonah — see sources. And so Magen Avraham siman kaf-bet that they do not remove him from shaliach tzibur because of once, and especially in this era — see Yoreh De'ah siman 312. And so Eliyah Rabbah ot 1, Birkei Yosef ot 1, and wrote and so is the main point Maamar Mordechai ot 2, Rabbi Zev ot 1, and see above siman 53 seif 18; and if he did so repeatedly, one can say they remove him entirely — Eshel Avraham ot 2:
