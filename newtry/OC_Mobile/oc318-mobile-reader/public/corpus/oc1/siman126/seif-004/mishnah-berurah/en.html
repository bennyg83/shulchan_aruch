(16) "He relies," etc. — and if silently at Maariv, he returns, since he has nothing on which to rely; but on Shabbos he may rely on the abbreviated seven-blessing form, as it is not less than an individual who relies on it, as in siman 268 [Peri Megadim].

(17) In the first three — specifically when he remembered before he completed his prayer, for there is no burden on the congregation in this — so too; but after he completed his prayer, he does not return — so the Levush, and many achronim agreed with him. The Tur disagrees and is lenient even in the first three in all cases, and one who relies on him did not lose [Chayei Adam in the name of achronim, and so it is implied in Biur Gra that the halacha follows the Tur].
