(siman 4) Like, etc. And what Rabbeinu Yehudah wrote — meaning reason for view alef:

As written siman 268 seif 2 — Yerushalmi: it is proper that one should pray all 18 blessings on Shabbat as on weekday, but the Sages did not burden him and seven suffice; and on Rosh Chodesh do they not pray 18 blessings? Therefore regarding burden coming through prayer they were more concerned on Shabbat than Rosh Chodesh; and here too it deals with prayer, and it is clear the Sages were concerned about burden on Rosh Chodesh — all the more on Shabbat and Yom Tov:

Only in keriah, etc. — meaning on Rosh Chodesh they read Torah with four olot and also do not add; on Shabbat seven olot and if they wish they add; and the reason is it was instituted by Moshe that they read Torah on Shabbat so all Israel engage in Torah at least on Shabbat day; and Shabbatot and Yamim Tovim were not given to Israel except so all Israel engage in Torah, since they are then free from melacha; therefore they read seven olot, not so Rosh Chodesh which has cancellation of melacha — four olot suffice; and this is specifically regarding keriat haTorah, for for this Shabbat was given, not regarding tefillah:
