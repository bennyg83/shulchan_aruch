On the author, seif 2, and see responsa of Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 21 [he wrote to resolve the difficulty of my father-in-law the Gaon Rabbi Akiva Eiger (may his righteous memory be blessed) on Beit Yosef this siman on the passage "and know that one who stands and calls up kohanim begins Sim Shalom" — and why does he not begin with avodah, since the first three and last three are considered as one. And requires study in the manner that one praying when he reaches Retzei and knows in his soul that he must stop for burial of the dead and another stands in his place and that one will need to begin Retzei — if so he is immediately removed at Retzei; and see there what he answered to strengthen the question. Further he wrote there in that responsum in the case of a prayer leader who became mute in the Selichos of Musaf of Yom Kippur and another was placed in his place and began from where the first became mute and not from the beginning of the beracha Attah Bechartanu — and there were gedolei ha'machir city scholars. And he wrote that in his opinion they acted according to law, since still before the second reaches "or kel mechoel avonoseinu" etc. in Barechu Elokeinu Mekadesh Yisrael of Yom Kippur there is no concern if he shortened in the middle and did not say Attah Bechartanu; and even in Musaf of Yom Kippur where the second does not mention avodah in the text and mefanei chata'einu — even if this is obstructive there is no matter, for in Selichos he mentions matters of avodah many times, and in the text there is no concern except that he not shorten like the beracha on fruits; and see Magen Avraham siman 114 seif kaf tes].
