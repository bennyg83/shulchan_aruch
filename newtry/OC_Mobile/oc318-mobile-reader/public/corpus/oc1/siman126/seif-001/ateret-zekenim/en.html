If the prayer leader erred and skipped one of the berachos, and even if he erred or skipped two or three berachos, we do not say that he is established in skipping (Tur in the name of Yerushalmi); and the same is true for more than three — they do not remove him. But if he skipped the blessing against heretics, they remove him entirely from being prayer leader (Rabbi Yonah, chapter 10). If the prayer leader erred, another should pass in his place; if one is found who had proper intent with the prayer leader throughout the entire prayer, he should descend and complete the prayer. And if he is not found, another should descend even if he did not have intent — nevertheless he should not begin except at the beginning of the beracha in which the prayer leader erred; thus it is implied from the words of Beit Yosef.
