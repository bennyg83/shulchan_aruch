If he skipped the blessing of the heretics: The Tur brought a Yerushalmi — a prayer leader who skipped two or three blessings, they do not remove him, except one who did not say the resurrection of the dead, lest perhaps he denies the Torah; and who subdues the wicked, lest perhaps he is an apikores; and who builds Jerusalem, lest perhaps he does not believe in the coming of the Messiah. And I say: he is an apikores. Beit Yosef wondered why he brought this Yerushalmi, since our Talmud does not hold so — that specifically in the blessing of the heretics there is suspicion. And it seems the Yerushalmi deals with one who skipped two blessings at one time, that they remove him — that is, two of these: the resurrection of the dead, the blessing of the heretics, and who builds Jerusalem; in this case we suspect that perhaps he does not believe in them. And although in the blessing of the heretics alone they also remove him, nevertheless it counts him together with the other two. And further, in this case, even if he had begun it they remove him, since there is another wickedness — that he skipped another blessing of resurrection or Jerusalem — and our Talmud does not deal with this combination; therefore we have a proper learning from this Yerushalmi.
