Seif 2: If the prayer leader erred and does not know to return to his place, etc., and begins from the beginning of the beracha in which he erred, etc. I would say (') it appears that this applies only if the second did not hear the beginning of the beracha; but if he heard the beginning of the beracha, since the second fulfills his obligation through this, he can return to the place where he stopped as with megillah in siman 693 where the Acharonim disagree with Magen Avraham; and see Shaarei Teshuvah on laws of megillah that it is sufficient for the second to return to the place where he stopped since he fulfilled through the reading of the first as with shofar — and if so here too in Shulchan Aruch it is so. However, if it is a short beracha and there is no bother to the congregation, he should return to the head of the beracha. But if it occurred on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur where there is bother to the congregation, he should begin only from the place where he stopped, as Shaarei Teshuvah wrote in laws of megillah — this distinction applies here too; thus in my humble opinion it is correct as law — requires study.
