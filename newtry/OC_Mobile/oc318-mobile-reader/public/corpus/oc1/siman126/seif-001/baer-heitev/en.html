Immediately. That is, they do not wait for him to remember, but rather remove him from the prayer stand immediately; and it is implied that they do not remove him from being prayer leader for a single occasion. Magen Avraham.

And erred. And if he skipped the beginning of the beracha and completed its concluding formula, we do not find that they remove him; and if he did not want to say velal kofrim but only velamalshinim, there is a doubt whether they remove him — Shechinat Devora in the name of Maharatz. And in siman 111 he wrote that Romanians say velamalshinim. If they erred in a place where they return them and they were not reminded until after everyone dispersed to his way, whether they are obligated to gather again or not — see Leket Yosher, part 2, siman 123.
