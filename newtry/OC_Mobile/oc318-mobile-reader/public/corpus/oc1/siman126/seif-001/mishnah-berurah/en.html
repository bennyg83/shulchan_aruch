(1) If he erred and skipped — to exclude if he did so intentionally and skipped; even in the other blessings they remove him immediately.
(2) "Of all the blessings" — meaning: even if he erred and skipped the blessing of the resurrection of the dead or who builds Jerusalem, they do not remove him, to say that perhaps he denies the resurrection of the dead or does not believe in the coming of the Messiah; rather, we attribute it that in error he omitted it. And the reason: see Beit Yosef in the name of R' Isserles, and see Taz, who holds that if he omitted both of them, we certainly need to be concerned about this, and even if he had begun they remove him — and some disagree with him. And know that it is clear to all that if we know that he denies the resurrection of the dead or does not believe in the future redemption — and all the more so if he does not believe in the Torah from Heaven or in reward and punishment — according to all he is a complete apikores, and it is forbidden to allow him to be prayer leader; and if he stood in presumption, they do not answer amen after him. See above in siman 53, seif 8.
(3) They do not remove him — rather, they wait until they remind him, and when he remembers he returns to the place where he erred, like an individual who errs. See Beer Heitev Levush.
(4) But if he skipped — specifically when he skipped the whole blessing; but if he skipped the head of the blessing and finished its closing formula, we do not find that they remove him. And likewise, if he did not want to say "to the heretics" but rather "and to the informers," they do not remove him, because there are places that say l'chatchila "and to the informers."
(5) Immediately — meaning: they do not wait for him to be reminded, but rather remove him from the amud immediately, because perhaps apikorsus came upon him now and he does not want to curse himself; therefore he skipped this blessing — and even if he had been presumed until now to be kosher and righteous, we are also concerned about this. Nevertheless, they do not remove him from being prayer leader due to "perhaps," since we can say that he erred.
(6) They do not remove him — even if a long time passed in between and he could not remember; even so, we attribute it to error, since he began it.
