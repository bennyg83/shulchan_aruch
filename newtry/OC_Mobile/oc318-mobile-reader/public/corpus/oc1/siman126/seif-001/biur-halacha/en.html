The prayer leader who erred and skipped one, etc. — the same is true even if he erred and skipped two or three berachos; the law is likewise; rather he cited the matter that occurs. Etz Chaim and Perishah.

They do not remove him — see Mishna Berurah, and see Rambam chapter 10, from which it is implied that specifically if an hour passed and he could not settle his mind to return to his place, then another stands in his place; and it is implied that up to an hour they must wait for him. However in other poskim I have not seen this measure, and likewise in Beit Yosef who copied in this siman the words of Rambam — he omitted from them the word "hour" — requires study.

The beracha, etc. — it is possible that specifically if he skipped only that one alone; but if he skipped it and its adjacent companion, it is evident that this came to his hand in inadvertence.

They remove him immediately — see Mishna Berurah; and it appears that if he remembered on his own and returned to the beracha velamalshinim, he need not be removed, for it is evident that initially it was inadvertent.
