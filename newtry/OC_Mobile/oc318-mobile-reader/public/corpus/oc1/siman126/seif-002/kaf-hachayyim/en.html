2) There: And when they remind him he knows how to return, etc. That is to say, they do not replace him immediately, but wait until they remind him; and if he recalls and knows how to return to his place, he completes the tefillah. But if he does not recall even after they remind him, another passes before the ark in his stead, for what are we to do, since they remind him and he does not recall; and this is even if he erred only in one of all the other berachos. But if he erred in the berachah of 'V'lamalshinim,' they do not wait with him to remind him, but remove him immediately, for we are concerned that perhaps he is a heretic. Bach; and so wrote the Magen Avraham, s.k. 2; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1; Maamar Mordechai, ot 2.
