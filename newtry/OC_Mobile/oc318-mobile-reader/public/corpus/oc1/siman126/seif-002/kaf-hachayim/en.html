(2) There — "and when they remind him he knows to return," etc. — meaning they do not remove him immediately but wait until they remind him; and if he remembers and knows how to return to his place he completes the prayer; but if he does not remember even after they remind him, another passes before the amud beneath him — what should we do, since they reminded him and he did not remember; and even if he did not err except in one of all the other blessings — but if he erred in the blessing concerning heretics [velamalshinim] they do not wait with him to remind him but remove him immediately, lest he be an Apikorus — Bach, and so Magen Avraham siman kaf-bet.
