(siman kaf-bet) Immediately, etc. And from what is anonymous here, etc. — meaning Rabbeinu Yehudah brought in name of Tosafot on that which we say: if he erred in all blessings they do not replace him (meaning they do not remove him); if he erred in blessings of minim they replace him. And they challenged: if he erred in all blessings they do not replace him, yet we learned: one who passes before the amud and errs — another passes beneath him. And Tosafot: and this is its language — this is temporarily; but here he said they do not remove him, meaning also if he erred in other blessings they remove him in this prayer and another passes beneath him. And what he said "they do not replace him" — meaning they do not remove him so he should never again be shaliach tzibur forever. And therefore Rabbeinu Yehudah wrote: it is implied from his words that if he erred in blessing of minim they remove him from shaliach tzibur — end of his words. And therefore Magen Avraham wrote: from what is anonymous in Shulchan Aruch and he did not write in explanation that they remove him if he erred in blessing of minim so he should never again be shaliach tzibur — therefore he does not hold like Tosafot on this. And regarding Tosafot's question it is already resolved in Shulchan Aruch's words which are Rashba's words — the distinction is: in other blessings if he skipped they remind him, and if through reminding he knows to return to his place they do not remove him even from this prayer; and if even through reminding he does not know to return to his place — in this we learned "one who passes," etc., another passes beneath him. Not so if he skipped blessing of minim — they remove him immediately from this prayer and do not remind him lest he know to return to his place:

And especially in this era — meaning minim are not so commonly found. However above siman 1 siman kaf-tet regarding Ten Commandments in congregation it implies somewhat the opposite:

See Yoreh De'ah siman 312 where he wrote there they do not slaughter into a pit; and if slaughtered, some say it is forbidden lest he be a min; and Rama wrote that in this era we do not forbid after the fact because in this era idol worship is not done this way — and similarly one can say here the law changed in this era from the time of the Sages of the Talmud, since minim are not found among us:
