(3) There — but if he skipped the blessing concerning heretics, etc. Tur wrote citing Yerushalmi: a shaliach tzibur who skipped two or three blessings — they do not remove him except one who did not say resurrection of the dead, lest he deny resurrection; "and humbles the arrogant" — lest he be an Apikorus; "Who rebuilds Jerusalem" — lest he not believe in coming of Mashiach; and I say he is Apikorus — thus however Beit Yosef wondered on Tur who brought this Yerushalmi and wrote that since it disagrees with our Talmud which states anonymously: if he erred in all the blessings they do not replace him except blessing velamalshinim — and so Rif, Rambam, Rosh, and they did not mention this Yerushalmi at all — see sources. And so Birkei Yosef ot 2 that there is no question here like the Yerushalmi; and what Taz answered I did not hear — end of his words. And so Maamar Mordechai ot 1 that Taz's words have no reason; and also on Bach's answer he wrote it does not appear — see sources. However what Derishah wrote ot 1 that Tur brought it to say not one blessing but even if he skipped two or three, in this our Talmud does not disagree — see sources. Maamar Mordechai brought there and wrote so too in Vayigash Yehudah and it is correct; but on another answer Derishah wrote he cannot stand on his intention and there is no doubt there are several errors — see sources.

(3) And know — it is clear to all that if we know he denies resurrection of the dead or does not believe in future redemption — and all the more if he does not believe in Torah from heaven or reward and punishment — according to all he is a complete Apikorus and it is forbidden to allow him to be shaliach tzibur; and if he stood in presumption, one does not answer Amen after him — may salvation come speedily — Mishna Berurah ot 2:
