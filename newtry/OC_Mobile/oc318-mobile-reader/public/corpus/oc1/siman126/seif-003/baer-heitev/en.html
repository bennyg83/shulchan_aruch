Mateh Yehuda wrote in siman 25 — were it not that I am afraid, I would have said that an individual who did not mention Rosh Chodesh in Shacharis and prayed Musaf does not need to pray Shacharis again, for after the fact for an individual is not inferior to the outset for the many, therefore. And in our opinion it is proper in practice that he should not pray, so that it should not be merely a doubt, like Knesset HaGedolah siman 422.
