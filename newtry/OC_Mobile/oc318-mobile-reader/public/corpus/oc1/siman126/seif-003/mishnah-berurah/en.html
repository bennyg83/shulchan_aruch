(12) That if the prayer leader forgot — and regarding an individual, if he forgot, see above in siman 124, seif 10.

(13) And he did not mention yaaleh v'yavo — and if he forgot "who makes the wind blow," dew, rain, and the like, they return him; specifically in yaaleh v'yavo, because one remembrance applies here and there [Peri Megadim].

(14) "That if he erred," etc. — meaning that he forgot and prayed the weekday Amidah and did not mention the matter of Shabbos and Yom Tov, and completed his prayer; but if he remembered before he completed his prayer, he returns to "Make us rejoice, Moses" or on Yom Tov to "You have chosen us" — so Peri Megadim. From the words of the Rav in the gloss below in siman 268, seif 5, it somewhat does not imply so.

(15) His law is like Rosh Chodesh — the Levush brought views on this and did not decide, but the achronim wrote like the Rav.
