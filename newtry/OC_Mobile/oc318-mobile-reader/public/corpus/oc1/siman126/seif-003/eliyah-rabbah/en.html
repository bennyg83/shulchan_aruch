[3] From the beginning of the blessing where he erred, etc. Even though he erred only at the end, the reading of that blessing is as if it did not exist; and ab initio there should be someone else who has intention for the entire prayer together with the shaliach tzibur, and who did not chat while the shaliach tzibur was praying; and if such a person is not found, they may also take someone who did not have such intention; and even so he does not begin except from the beginning of the blessing because of burden of the congregation — and so it is implied in Beit Yosef.
