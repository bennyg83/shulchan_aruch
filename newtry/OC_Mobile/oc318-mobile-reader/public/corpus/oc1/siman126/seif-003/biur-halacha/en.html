Except for Shacharis of Rosh Chodesh — and the same is true for the morning of Chol HaMoed — Peri Megadim.

And so they practiced — see Mishna Berurah in the name of Peri Megadim that this law applies to one who completed his prayer; and indeed this law is not found at all that the listeners will not remind him until he completes his prayer. And even if we say not like Peri Megadim, likewise it is not found that he would pray all the middle berachos and not mention it [for if he is still standing within the beracha, certainly according to all he must return for the beracha of Shabbat and Yom Tov — see below siman 268]. And perhaps the intent of Rama in what he wrote "and so is practiced" is regarding if Shabbat and Yom Tov coincide and the prayer leader forgot to mention Shabbat — that it helps that he will mention Shabbat in Musaf also for Shacharis.
