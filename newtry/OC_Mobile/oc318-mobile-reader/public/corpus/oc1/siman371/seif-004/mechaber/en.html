One of the courtyard residents who died' on Shabbat — if he had made an eruv, even if an outsider inherits from him, he does not restrict. If he had not made an eruv: if an outsider inherits from him, as long as the outsider does not come to live with them he does not restrict; if he comes to live on Shabbat in the house of his benefactor, he restricts. If one of the courtyard residents inherits from him — if the inheritor had made an eruv and was living together with the deceased in his house, he does not restrict even though the deceased had not made an eruv. But if he was not living with the deceased but in another house in the courtyard, he restricts even though the inheritor had made an eruv, since the deceased had not made an eruv. If the deceased died while it was still day and an outsider inherits from him, the outsider restricts — provided he comes to the deceased's house on Shabbat.':
