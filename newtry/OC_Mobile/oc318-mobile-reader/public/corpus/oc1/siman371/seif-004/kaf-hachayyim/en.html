4) There. If he put it out of his mind, etc. This is from Rambam, chapter 4. Although the discussion there states that one who goes to spend Shabbos with his daughter does not prohibit carrying, but one who goes to his son does, and the Tur writes likewise, Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam maintains this was stated only as a common example. People more commonly spend Shabbos with a daughter than with a son. In every case, if he put returning out of his mind, even if he went to his son he does not prohibit carrying; if he did not put it out of his mind, even if he went to his daughter he does prohibit carrying. See there. Beis Yosef cites his words. The halachic authorities likewise rule in accordance with the Shulchan Aruch.
