If he made an eruv, etc. — for then we say "since Shabbat was permitted it remains permitted." And without an eruv the inheritor does not restrict unless he comes on Shabbat; for we say "since it was forbidden at the entry of Shabbat it is forbidden for the entire Shabbat," for here he had no owners on the day of Shabbat all the time the inheritor did not come after his father's death — a residence without owners does not restrict. But when the inheritor came there on Shabbat, behold the residence has owners, and then it is forbidden due to what the father did not make an eruv at first; and the inheritor's eruv does not help for what he inherits on Shabbat. Such is Tur's view according to Rosh.
