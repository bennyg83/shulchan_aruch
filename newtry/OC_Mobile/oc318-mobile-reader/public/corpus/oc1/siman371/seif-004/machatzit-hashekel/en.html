(1) (s"k 4) After nightfall he restricts — and one could ask, etc., "once permitted, permitted" — this means all the time the marketplace resident was alive, for he had a residence-house in the courtyard; and meaning that he eats there, as we rule in siman 370 seif 5, and he needs to make an eruv; if so it was never permitted even in his lifetime.

(2) He is not accustomed to make an eruv with them even though he eats there, since he is not accustomed to use there; and he came to inform us, etc. — this is a different explanation: why did he write "one from the marketplace"? To inform us that we should not say, etc., he diverted his attention; and the reason in truth is as Magen Avraham wrote in the s"k after this.
