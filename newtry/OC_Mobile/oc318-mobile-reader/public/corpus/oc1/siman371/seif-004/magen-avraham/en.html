(1) If he comes to live, etc., he restricts. For regarding him himself we do not say he diverted his attention — specifically when he was here and went away do we say thus; and likewise at the end of the seif. See there siman 65.

(2) And he was living with the deceased, etc. If so, it is comparable to five groups who need not all contribute to the eruv — see siman 370 seif 3. But there is no proof from this that they say "his hired workers and harvesters give his eruv," as written in siman 382 seif 1 — for perhaps that applies only to a non-Jew whose residence is not so significant; but for a Jew, no (Tosafos and Rosh). See siman 367.
