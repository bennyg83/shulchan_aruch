(4) There — if he diverted his attention, etc. — this is from Rambam's words ch. 4. And although in the sugya there it states one who goes to spend Shabbat at his daughter's but not at his son's — and likewise Tur — Maggid Mishneh wrote there that Rambam holds they did not say thus except to speak of what is present and common, that people's way is to spend Shabbat at their daughter's more than at their son's; but always, if he diverted his heart, even if he went to his son's he does not restrict; and if he did not divert, even at his daughter's he restricts — see there, and Beit Yosef brings it. And likewise the poskim, as Shulchan Aruch's words.
