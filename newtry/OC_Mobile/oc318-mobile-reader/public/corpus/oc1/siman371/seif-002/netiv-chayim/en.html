(Taz s"k 1) But if it was evident. — Not so, for if so what is Rosh saying, and it is not comparable to a gentile? If the gentile diverted his attention, in truth according to his view he does not restrict. But truth instructs his way: certainly we do not say from estimation, whether for a gentile or a Jew, that he diverted his attention in order to be lenient and say he does not restrict — "for there is no longer a prophet." Rather it deals with one who went to spend Shabbat at his daughter's; he said explicitly that his intent is to spend Shabbat and not return on Shabbat — therefore he does not restrict, for a Jew's word will not prove false even in his mouth. Not so regarding a gentile "whose mouth," etc. — and likewise Rabbeinu Yehonasan wrote; see there.
