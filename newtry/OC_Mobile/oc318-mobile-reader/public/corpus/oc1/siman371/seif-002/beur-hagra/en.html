Seif 2 — Jew. "Once permitted, permitted" — but many disagree; their proof is from nullifying domain when one returns, and further from a gentile coming on Shabbat; and Rosh wrote it is not comparable to a gentile coming on Shabbat, whose eruv from the outset was not fit to remain valid — and this is from Tosafos there 65b s.v. "that he came." But it requires study, for if so why do we say there 72b "one, etc., after nightfall restricts"? Bach questioned similarly. See Taz s"k 3 who wrote "and he was not precise," etc. — but he was not precise, for this is comparable to this law in seif 2; and Magen Avraham s"k 4 wrote "and he did not see Rashi and Tosafos," etc. And there is no implication in Rashi and Tosafos there at all. In truth Ritva explained thus, but it is very strained that he would explain "from the marketplace" as courtyard residents specifically, but marketplace residents not — for according to all explanations marketplace residents have no difference, but marketplace residents not. Impossible.
