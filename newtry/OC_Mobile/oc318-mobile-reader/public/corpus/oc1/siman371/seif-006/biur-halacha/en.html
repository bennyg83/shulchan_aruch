One of the courtyard residents who was in a dying state, etc. — see what we wrote in Mishna Berurah in the name of Magen Avraham who wrote in the name of Tosafos annotations; and see in Sefer Atzei Almogim who is very doubtful on the essence of this law, and wrote that from Taz's ruling without comment above at the beginning of seif 4 — if he died on Shabbat, even if one of the marketplace residents inherited and did not make an eruv he does not restrict, and they did not stipulate specifically when he was not in a dying state — apparently he does not hold like this gloss; and he further brought some proof that Tosafos does not hold thus — see there where he expanded on this; and his view for halacha is to be lenient here; and in a time of need it appears one may rely on this.
