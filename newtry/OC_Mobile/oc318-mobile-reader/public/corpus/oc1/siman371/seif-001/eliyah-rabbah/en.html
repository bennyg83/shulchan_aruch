[1] Who left his house, etc., while it was still day (Tur). It appears to me he wrote thus to inform us that even if he returned he does not restrict; alternatively he wrote "while it was still day" because of Kutim. But in truth even if he went on Shabbat he does not restrict (Magen Avraham). In my humble opinion, Tur deals with an unspecified case, and then we require "while it was still day"; but when we ask him and he says he does not intend to return, even if he went on Shabbat he does not restrict — and thus Shulchan Aruch and Levush deal with this case; and in this manner Avodat HaKodesh wrote on daf 23.
