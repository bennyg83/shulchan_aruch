(1) One of the courtyard residents, etc. — and the reason is that a residence without owners is not called a residence and does not restrict in the courtyard even though it did not give a share in the eruv.
(2) Who left his house and went, etc. — it deals with one who went with all the members of his household from there while it was still day; and some are lenient even when he went on Shabbat if he did not change his mind that day.
(3) He restricts them — even if they made an eruv among themselves.
(4) If it is farther, etc. — and although regarding one far away too there is room to be concerned that he went on a weekday until near here and will come on Shabbat — nevertheless we are not concerned for this.
(5) That a non-Jew too, etc. — see Taz who wrote that if the non-Jew is accustomed to return to his house that day it is forbidden according to all; and here it deals with one who went with intent to spend Shabbat there the entire day.
(6) In another courtyard — see Biur Halacha that we explained in the name of several poskim that one should not be lenient except in another city, and not in another courtyard.
(7) He does not restrict — like a Jew who went to spend Shabbat elsewhere and diverted his heart from his residence, for we consider him as though he does not live there that Shabbat.
