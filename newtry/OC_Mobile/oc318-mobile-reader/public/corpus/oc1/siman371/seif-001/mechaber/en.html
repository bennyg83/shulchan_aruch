When One of the Courtyard Residents Leaves or Dies — 6 Sections One of the courtyard residents who
left his house and went to spend Shabbat in another courtyard — even one adjacent to his own — if he put it out of his mind and does not intend to return to his house on Shabbat, he does not restrict them. This applies to a Jew, but a non-Jew — even if he went to spend Shabbat in another city — still restricts them until they rent his place from him, if it is within one day's journey, since it is possible he may come on Shabbat. But if it is further away, he does not restrict. Some say that a non-Jew too, if he went to spend Shabbat in another courtyard, does not restrict.
