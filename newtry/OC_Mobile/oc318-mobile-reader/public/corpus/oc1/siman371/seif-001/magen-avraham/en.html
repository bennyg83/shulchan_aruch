(1) Who left his house while it was still day. (Tur) It appears to me he wrote thus to inform us that even if he returned he does not restrict; alternatively he wrote "while it was still day" because of the non-Jew, see siman 383. But in truth even if he was here and did not make an eruv and went on Shabbat he does not restrict, as written regarding one who died on Shabbat and did not make an eruv.

(2) A day's journey. And even though regarding one who is far away too there is room to be concerned that he went on a weekday until near here and will come on Shabbat — nevertheless we are not concerned for this.
