After nightfall he restricts. And one could ask: let us say "once Shabbat was permitted it remains permitted" (Bach) — but he did not see the words of Rashi and Tosafos who wrote that it deals with one who had a residence in this courtyard and needs to make an eruv. And that which he wrote "one from the marketplace" — he followed the manner of speech, writing "one from the marketplace" because one in the marketplace is not accustomed to make an eruv with them since he is not accustomed to use there; and he came to inform us that we should not say: since he did not come at the entry of Shabbat he diverted his attention.
