One — Taz; and likewise Magen Avraham (4), for Bach in seif 4 there — it appears he explains a marketplace resident had a house in the courtyard and did not live there, that he does not restrict — for us a hayloft and cattle barn do not restrict; if so after nightfall let us say "once permitted, permitted." But Rashi and Tosafos Eruvin 72b s.v. "and one," and in Tosafos "one" — one may say he followed manner of speech, writing marketplace resident because a marketplace resident does not make an eruv; nevertheless he restricts them because he had a residence-house there.
