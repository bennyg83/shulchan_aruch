(9) One from the marketplace, etc. — it deals with one who did not make an eruv on the house he has in the courtyard; and the same applies if he lived in the courtyard and did not make an eruv; but he wrote "one from the marketplace" because the usual manner of speech is thus — when one lives in the marketplace he does not make an eruv on the house he has in the courtyard.
(10) The inheritor does not restrict — whether he entered to live in it or not.
(11) That the eruv, etc. — even if he made an eruv on his house before his benefactor died, for the essence of acquiring the eruv is at twilight, and then the house was already his.
(12) He restricts — even if the inheritor did not come to live in that house; nevertheless it is called a residence with owners since he lives in that courtyard.
(13) Even though he made an eruv, etc. — on his house that he has in this courtyard.
(14) It does not help, etc. — since at twilight, which is then the time of acquiring the eruv, the house was not his, the eruv does not take effect on this house.
