3) There. Who left his house and went, etc. This means before the onset of Shabbos. Tur. It appears to me that this detail was stated to teach that even if he returns on Shabbos, he does not prohibit carrying. Alternatively, it states that he left before the onset of Shabbos because of the law concerning a non-Jew, who does prohibit carrying if he returns on Shabbos, as stated in siman 383. In truth, however, even if the resident was present and did not establish an eruv but left on Shabbos, he does not prohibit carrying, as stated in se'if 4 regarding one who died on Shabbos without having established an eruv. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. In my humble opinion, it appears that the Tur discusses an unspecified case, which requires that he leave before the onset of Shabbos. If, however, he is asked and states that he does not intend to return, then even if he leaves on Shabbos he does not prohibit carrying. The Shulchan Aruch and Levush discuss this latter case. Avodas HaKodesh, folio 23, writes similarly. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1. However, regarding the statement of Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah that even if he leaves on Shabbos he does not prohibit carrying, as in se'if 4, see Beis Meir, who distinguishes between the two cases. Gaon Yaakov on tractate Eruvin likewise rules that the Tur specifically means that he left before the onset of Shabbos, and also distinguishes between the two cases. See there. Rashi on 47a likewise writes that the discussion concerns one who left before the onset of Shabbos. Rashba and Ritva also write that it applies specifically before the onset of Shabbos. See there. One should therefore rule stringently.
