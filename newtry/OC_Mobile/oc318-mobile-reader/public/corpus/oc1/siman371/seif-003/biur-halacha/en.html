And if he died after nightfall he restricts, etc. — Bach questioned: why do we not say "since it was permitted at twilight when he had no owners then, it is permitted for the entire Shabbat," comparable to that of seif 2? See Taz who wrote on him that he was not precise; and Beur HaGra already challenged, and likewise other later authorities on Taz's answer. See what Magen Avraham answered in s"k 4 — and on this too Beur HaGra challenged, see there; and because of this difficulty Beur HaGra was compelled not like seif 2, see there. However according to what Magen Avraham wrote in s"k 5, Bach's difficulty is resolved, and we do not need at all his answer that he answered in s"k 4; and likewise Machatzit HaShekel — see there.
