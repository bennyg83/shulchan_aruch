Radbaz, part 4, responsum 241, disagrees and writes as follows: If ten people each prayed individually, the obligation of Kaddish and Kedushah has departed from them, as we say regarding Bircas HaMazon below in siman 194, seif 1. Since the obligation has departed from them, even if ten later assemble, they may not repeat the prayer with Kaddish and Kedushah. If they do repeat it, the blessings are in vain. See there. His words are cited briefly by Magen Avraham. Magen Gibborim and Chayei Adam cite his ruling as halachah. Responsa of the Rema of Fano, responsum 84, and Derech HaChaim, laws of Barechu, paragraph 3, appear to rule like the Shulchan Aruch. Chasam Sofer, Orach Chaim responsum 17, however, wrote that common practice follows the Radbaz. Even according to the Radbaz, when one of them has not yet prayed, they may be Pores al Shema and may also go before the amud. He should not first recite the prayer silently, but should immediately pray aloud. In such a case, the obligation of prayer cannot be said to have departed from them, since he recites it for his own obligation. Magen Gibborim likewise wrote this in siman 124. The same applies that one of those who already fulfilled his obligation may be Pores al Shema and go before the amud for the sake of the one who has not yet prayed, as stated shortly in the Shulchan Aruch. Since at least one person has not yet fulfilled the obligation of prayer, all Jews are responsible for one another. Radbaz there.
