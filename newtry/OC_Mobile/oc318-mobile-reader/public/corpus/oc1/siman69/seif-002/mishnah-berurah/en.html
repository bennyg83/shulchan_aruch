(1) “Each one” — and Radbaz in siman 241, chelek 4, argues on this; his words: if they prayed with a minyan but each individually, Kaddish and Kedushah “flew away” from them, as we say regarding Birkat HaMazon [below siman 194 se'if 1]; and since they flew away from them — even if ten later assembled, they cannot return and pray with Kaddish and Kedushah, and if they returned and prayed it is a blessing in vain — see there — and his words are cited briefly in Magen Avraham, and in Sefer Magen Gibborim, and Chayei Adam brought his words for practical halacha. And in responsa Rama siman 84, and in Derech Chayyim on the laws of Bar'khu, note 3, it implies he rules like Shulchan Aruch; but in responsa Chasam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 17 he wrote the common practice follows Radbaz. However, even for Radbaz, if one of them has not yet prayed — he may poreis al Shema and also descend before the ark, but he should not pray the prayer from the beginning silently; rather he prays the prayer immediately aloud, for in such a case the “flying away” of the prayer obligation does not apply to him, since he is saying for himself; and likewise Magen Gibborim siman 124; and likewise one of those who already fulfilled may poreis al Shema and descend before the ark for him — as ahead in Shulchan Aruch — since in any case one has not yet fulfilled prayer, all Israel are guarantors for one another [Radbaz there].
