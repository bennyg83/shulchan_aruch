A blind person — even though, etc. He needed to write this law here even though it is written in siman 53 that a blind person may descend before the ark, because here it is novel that he says Yotzer haMe'orot — for what we taught "a blind person spreads for Shema" means that in any event he skips Yotzer haMe'orot — teaches that not; and thereby resolves what Tur wrote here; and so Ateret Zekenim z"l that he can pray and discharge others, provided he does not read, for it is forbidden to read Torah by heart — until here.
