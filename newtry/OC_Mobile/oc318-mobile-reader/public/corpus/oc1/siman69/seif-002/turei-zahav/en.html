**A blind person, though, etc.** He had to record this ruling here even though it is similar in siman 53, that a blind person may descend before the ark — because something new appears here: that he says Yotzer haMaoros; for if so, what they taught that a blind person poreis al Shema would imply that in any case he skips Yotzer haMaoros — this teaches no; and with this one answers what Taz wrote here; and likewise Or Zarua of blessed memory, that he may pray and discharge others provided he not read, for it is forbidden to read Torah by heart, end quote — and it is hard why he brought this matter in the name of Rosh when it is explicit in the Mishnah as the Sages rule and the halacha follows them; moreover why did the Tur need only to add “provided he not read Torah from a text,” etc., when similarly in siman 53… and according to Magen Avraham’s words it is fine that from Rosh’s words too it proves one does not skip Yotzer haMaoros once he has completed it, provided he not read… and if he did not complete — provided he not say Yotzer haMaoros — but certainly he does not skip this matter; and I saw someone strain here for no reason; and see Rif siman 88 where Beit Yosef distinguishes between that case there, where a blind person does not bless on the lamp at Motzei Shabbos, and this ruling about Yotzer haMaoros.
