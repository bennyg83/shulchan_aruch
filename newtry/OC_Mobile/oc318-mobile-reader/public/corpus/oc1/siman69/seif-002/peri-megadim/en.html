And he began. See Taz. The Mechaber implies that if the chazzan has not yet begun, he should recite Barechu a second time, and Beit Yosef wrote this explicitly and raised a difficulty. Levush implies that there is no concern with repeating an arrangement of praise. It may also be said that the two recitations are not consecutive, since verses, Kaddish, and Barechu intervene, and therefore the repetition is permitted. See siman 63, Taz, seif katan 3.
