**And he began** — Taz on the Mechaber implies that if he did not begin he says Bar'khu again; Beit Yosef likewise, and he raised objections, etc. Levush implies that for Pesukei Dezimrah there is no concern for repetition; and one could add that they do not say [the sections] consecutively but only with verses, Kaddish, and Bar'khu between, and that constitutes an interruption and is permitted — see siman 53 in Taz 3.
