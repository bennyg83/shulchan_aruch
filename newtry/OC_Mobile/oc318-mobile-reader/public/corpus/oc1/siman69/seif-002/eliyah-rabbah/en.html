Because there is no Kaddish, etc. And in the responsum of Menachem Azariah there I saw another reason — it is found in Aggadah that in what is considered "in every morning the man whose soul is in his nostrils" — it does not settle with him until he answers Barchu; and in Arvit where this reason does not exist they did not practice. And that they practice to repeat when the congregation finished their prayer — Bach wrote the reason: because all those who did not hear Kaddish and Barchu gather at the entrance and it is forbidden to pass before those praying — therefore they wait until they finish their prayer.
