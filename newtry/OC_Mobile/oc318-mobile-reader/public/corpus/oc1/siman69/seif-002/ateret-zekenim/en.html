And if there are not ten — even one who did not hear, they say Barekhu likewise (Agur — that in Ashkenaz they practiced everywhere like Rashi and Rokeach, that they pray even for one individual). But Tosafot and Rosh wrote that six are required who did not hear — meaning a majority of the minyan; and so is implied from the words of Rambam chapter 8 of prayer; and so in Machzor Vitry and She'elotot — Rashi's custom to pray even for one individual; and so Mahari Abuhav. But one should be careful not to say Kaddish and Barekhu with fewer than ten:
