**And nowadays they do not practice [as written], etc.** The custom today is that after the shaliach tzibbur finishes the prayer, one stands at the doorway and they gather to him those who did not hear Bar'khu, and he poreis al Shema; and from this comes a problem that part of the congregation is still praying while these pass before them — as stated in siman 102 — therefore one must be careful about this.

**And he began with the blessing of Yotzer Or.** Beit Yosef in the name of Manhig wrote thus; but if right after Bar'khu they come immediately and the chazzan did not open Yotzer Or and they did not poreis al Shema — he returns and says Bar'khu to them, end quote — and this is very puzzling, for it resembles saying Modim Modim, where they silence him.
