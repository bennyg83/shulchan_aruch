And nowadays they did not follow this practice, etc. The present custom is that after the shaliach tzibbur completes the prayer, someone stands near the entrance and gathers those who did not hear Barechu, and he leads the responsive recitation of Shema for them. This causes a problem, because some members of the congregation are still praying and these people pass in front of them, contrary to siman 102. Care must therefore be taken regarding this.
And he began the blessing of Yotzer Or. The Beit Yosef cites Sefer HaManhig: 'If they arrive immediately after Barechu, before the chazzan has begun Yotzer, and they themselves did not conduct the responsive recitation of Shema, he should repeat Barechu for them,' etc. This is exceedingly difficult, for it resembles one who says 'Modim, Modim,' whom the Sages silence.
