Blessed is Hashem, etc. If he did not hear Barechu itself but heard the congregation responding, he answers Amen. See above, siman 57, seif katan 1.
Heard. If he heard it from the one reading from the Sefer Torah, that is sufficient, and they do not repeat it for him. See Magen Avraham. The same applies that the entire Amidah repetition may be recited for one person who has not prayed. Beit Yosef; Darchei Moshe. The Magen Avraham writes that, nevertheless, it appears to him that when ten people who have not prayed are not present, the chazzan should not first pray silently. Rather, he should begin immediately aloud, since the principal purpose is the recitation of Kedushah. The Magen Avraham writes in siman 74 [54] that if they conduct the abbreviated Krias Shema service after the final Kaddish, they should first recite several verses, because Kaddish is never recited except after verses or after the Amidah.
And he should lead. Nowadays, the practice is that mourners conduct the abbreviated Krias Shema service even though they themselves have heard it, provided one person present has not heard it. This is improper. Magen Avraham.
First. Meaning that he should complete his own prayer. It follows all the more so that one who conducts the abbreviated Krias Shema service or leads the Amidah should not interrupt to do so before first praying, even though he will consequently have to recite Krias Shema afterward and will not juxtapose redemption to prayer. The Magen Avraham writes that ideally he should not act this way. Rather, he should pray in the regular order, juxtapose redemption to prayer, and afterward conduct the abbreviated Krias Shema service.
Or. If he has not yet begun, he repeats Barechu for those who have come to the synagogue. Beit Yosef; Levush.
To interrupt. Between redemption and prayer, for in any event they interrupt there with verses. In the middle of the blessings, however, he should not interrupt. Magen Avraham.
The first. Even the first chazzan may do so. Levush. Mishpetei Shmuel writes that if the first group took out a Sefer Torah and read from it, the second group should not take out a Sefer Torah again and read from it.
