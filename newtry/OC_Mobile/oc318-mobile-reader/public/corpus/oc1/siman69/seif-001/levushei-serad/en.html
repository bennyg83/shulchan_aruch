Magen Avraham on psukei dezimra raises the question: namely, even though afterward he must say Yotzer Or not with the congregation, and therefore he will say Yotzer Or after Barechu — nevertheless he does not say Barechu, since he already heard it from the sheliach tzibbur; but if ten others happen to be present with him, even if they heard Barechu, it is permitted for him to say Barechu, since he immediately says Yotzer Or.
