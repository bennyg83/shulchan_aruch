Magen Avraham 'Pesukei DeZimrah, and he should have said.' Meaning, although he must afterward recite Yotzer without the congregation, so that in fact he will say Yotzer after Barechu, he should nevertheless not recite Barechu because he already heard it from the shaliach tzibbur. If, however, ten other people happen to join him, then even if they already heard Barechu, he may recite Barechu because he immediately proceeds to Yotzer Or.
