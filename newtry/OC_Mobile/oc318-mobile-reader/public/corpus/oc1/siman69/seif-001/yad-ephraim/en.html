**In Magen Avraham** (beginning of the siman): Rama wrote in responsa siman 85, etc. — all who are present with them at the time of the first Bar'khu of the blessing of Yotzer Or, etc.; and if there is a minyan summoned regarding him, it is permitted for him to “open” Bar'khu, as we said that in every case it is permitted for him to open Bar'khu for himself alone, provided it is with a congregation or another for his sake, etc., as needed; and as I emended the text, so it reads in Rama. His intent: there he calls the Bar'khu with which they poreis al Shema the “later Bar'khu,” and the Bar'khu the congregation said before Yotzer he calls the “first Bar'khu.” The intent of his words: if this individual has not yet said Yotzer Or — he may well open Bar'khu, he or another for him, even though he already heard and answered at the start or heard from those ascending to the Torah — and that which he wrote “only where he did not bless on the Shema,” as we said in every case, etc., means even though he already heard and answered. But an individual who did not hear Bar'khu and already said Yotzer Or — then too it is permitted to say Bar'khu for him even without adjoining Yotzer Or to it; for this applies specifically when he did not hear at all; but if he already heard Bar'khu, even if he heard only from those ascending for Torah reading — we do not repeat for him to say Bar'khu. It appears he himself should not say Bar'khu either. And see there: he wrote that if one heard Bar'khu from the mouth of a child ascending for Maftir, even though one answered after him — it seems reasonable another may say Bar'khu for his sake — so it appears.
