4) This prohibition against walking four amos without washing one's hands applies whether one rises with no intention of sleeping again or rises intending to return to sleep. Its fundamental reason is the evil spirit, and the evil spirit rests upon him throughout the night. Zechor LeAvraham.
4) The prohibition against walking four amos without washing one's hands applies specifically to one who slept past midnight. One who rises at midnight but did not remain asleep past midnight need not take this precaution. Divrei Shalom, question 48. Regarding its statement that he need not wash three alternating times, Emes LeYaakov, question 41, writes that the good and proper practice is to wash three alternating times; see there.
