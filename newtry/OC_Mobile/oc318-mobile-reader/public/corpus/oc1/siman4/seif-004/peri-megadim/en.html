In a place. See Taz, who explained that bloodletting and a vat refer specifically before morning washing. Regarding what he wrote about a vat, why would there be a curse of should be cut off if one wishes to damage his own money, the Bach also wrote this. What he wrote as Rashi's proof, that regarding those matters we find a prohibition all day, one may say that regarding the member, for a married man and below the crown it is permitted, as explained in siman 3; before morning washing it should be cut off. Also, if so, why did the Gemara interrupt between the pious woman and the vat with member, mouth, and ring, which apply all day? What he wrote about hemorrhoids I did not find; in Shabbos 81a it counts ten matters and not this; see there. Eliyah Rabbah s.k. 3 cited the abridged rulings of the Rosh in Shemonah Sheratzim, seif 14: to the eye, mouth, member, and nose before morning washing should be cut off. I wonder, for it is certainly a scribal error: it should read ear, not member. Why did he omit ear? From there there is proof like the Beis Yosef's explanation, for otherwise why did it not mention all eight matters, but only four, eye, mouth, nose, and ear, in place of member? This accords with the Beis Yosef.
