In the place of — Eshel Avraham explained that bloodletting and the privy before morning netilah are discussed specifically. And what he wrote about the privy — there is no need to cut the anus; one cuts only if he wishes to lose his money — so too Bach. And what he wrote — Rashi's proof that those openings we find prohibited all day — one can say: the nipple on a married woman with a head covering, and below is permitted, as explained in siman 3; and before morning netilah one cuts. Also, if so — why did he interrupt between the chest and the privy at the nipple and mouth, ring, and anus — all day? And what he wrote about lower garments — I did not find it; Shabbos 41a lists ten things and does not include this — see there. In Eliyah Rabbah 3 he wrote an abridgment of Rosh's rulings in eight sections, se'if 3:14 — eye, mouth, nipple, and nose before morning netilah one cuts. I am astonished — certainly it is a scribal error: "nipple" should read "ear"; why did he omit "ear"?
