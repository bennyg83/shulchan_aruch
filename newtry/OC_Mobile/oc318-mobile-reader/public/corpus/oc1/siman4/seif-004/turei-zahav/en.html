It implies even after morning netilah, all day; and likewise [regarding] the wine-strainer thereafter; and so Beit Yosef wrote explicitly, and they learned from what we say in the gemara: "hand to the eye, cut off" etc., "hand blinds" etc., and Rashi explained that all those refer to before morning netilah; and it seems to me that "hand to the mother" is not because of [morning] netilah but because of keri etc., end quote. And Beit Yosef explained that Rashi's reason is because he did not say in the gemara regarding "hand blinds" etc. the harm that comes from "hand to the mother" and the ring; and accordingly, for the wine-strainer and the barrel as well, even all day it harms the place touched; and I wonder accordingly why Rashi did not list "hand to the wine-strainer," which applies even all day, for it is mentioned in the gemara before "mother"; and it seems that certainly all of them apply before morning netilah except "mother" and ring — Rashi's proof that it applies all day, because we find regarding them in another place a prohibition all day due to keri and [contact with] undergarments — it is on Shabbos page 81; therefore Rashi listed specifically these two. But all the rest apply specifically in the morning; and the reason for wine-strainer and barrel appears also to be because of danger, and it is included in "blinds" and "deafens" that the gemara listed — that when he drinks beer afterward, it is as if it reaches from his hand to his mouth and nose. And I wondered at Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch's explanation that the barrel is because of loss of the beer — would the Sages say "cut off" for that? And that the Tur did not count wine-strainer and barrel seems that he listed only what occurs every day — are we peddlers to count every matter of danger? So it seems.
