{Rama: Magen Avraham, note 1} And in the Gemara it does not imply this — that is, that sugya of miltha 46, that one first puts on garments and afterward washes his hands; but one must say it deals with another person who washed his hands and dresses him. Therefore he brought that sugya: "Do not take your robe from the attendant's hand — rather, he himself shall dress" — and it implies that it is forbidden even if the attendant washed his hands.
