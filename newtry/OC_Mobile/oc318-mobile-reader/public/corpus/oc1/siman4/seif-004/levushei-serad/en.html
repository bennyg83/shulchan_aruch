Magen Avraham, s.k. 1 And the Gemara does not imply this. This refers to the beginning of siman 46, where first one puts on the garments and only afterward washes his hands. However, one may say it speaks where another person who had washed his hands dresses him. Therefore he brought the rule that one should not take his robe from the attendant; rather he himself should dress, implying that it is forbidden even if the attendant washed his hands.
