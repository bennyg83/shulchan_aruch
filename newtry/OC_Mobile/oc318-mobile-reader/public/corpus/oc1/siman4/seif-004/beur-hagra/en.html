Seif 4, "even," etc. Rashi there: the membrum and the anus. The Beis Yosef wrote that Rashi was compelled to this because the Gemara does not explain the injury there as it does regarding the eye, etc. From this the Beis Yosef learned regarding a barrel and tub. This is what Rashi wrote afterward: the hand blinds, etc., produces foul odor; "and it does not seem," etc. But it is difficult to say this because of loss of reward, "it should be cut off." It is satisfactory to explain that an evil spirit rests on it and it harms people, and so wrote the Bach and Taz. Rashi's words are also not compelling, for one can say it refers to a permitted case, such as with one's wife, or below the crown; and regarding the membrum it means the place of the opening, for before washing it harms every bodily opening.
