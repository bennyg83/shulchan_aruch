“A vessel.” But on a scroll used for sorcery it is forbidden—see siman 181:2. Seder Hayom writes it is good that the lower vessel be unappealing, and one need only immerse the fingers and raise them upward. Some writings say one should not pour on one hand three times in a row; rather once on the right and once on the left, and wash up to the elbow joint; one should spread the palms to receive purity like one who receives something, and raise them toward the head—see siman 613.
