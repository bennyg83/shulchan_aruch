8) There — and he blesses on netilas yadayim. Seder HaYom wrote: immediately after netilah he blesses and does not wait until drying — brought in Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos Tashbetz. Orach Teshuvah letter 2, Eliyah Rabbah sk 5; so Berachas Avraham ch. 3 that drying does not invalidate this washing; Beis David in Knesset HaGedolah Hagahos Tashbetz letter 6, Magen Avraham sk 6; so Mitzpeh Eisan p. 3 — bless before drying. However Machazikei Berachah letter 1 wrote in name of Maharam of Padua that evil spirit does not depart until after drying; he wrote that therefore it seems one should not bless until he dries his hands, unlike Knesset HaGedolah in name of Seder HaYom — see there. But Rav Ben Ish Chai (may he live) there letter 5 wrote: per view of our Rabbi the Ari z"l, after netilah as required, three times alternating, evil spirit is entirely uprooted — therefore one must bless immediately before drying, unlike later authorities who wrote it is not uprooted until after drying and therefore hold to bless after drying; so explicitly in book of our Rabbi the Rashash — bless before drying — end quote. I heard that practical people pour a fourth time so the first waters run off and there is no further concern for all views; so too it is proper to practice. See also below letter 12.

8) Further — Machazikei Berachah wrote letter 2: per what was said, one dries the hands first and afterward washes the face — so those rabbis; and per Pnei Yehoshua, even if he dried his hands, if he did not wash his face and went to synagogue and washed his hands, he must dry them and afterward wash the face, per Zohar that one spirit is in the synagogue — end quote; brought in Shaarei Tziyyon p. 18a; so Zachor LeChaim letter 50, Ben Ish Chai letter 6. In this too some practice pouring a fourth time and washing the face before drying.
