For Shema. See Taz. From what he wrote from the Zohar it implies that a vessel and pouring specifically are required. According to this, even if one washed his hands from flowing water from a high place, and certainly if he inserted his hands three times into snow or river water, there is doubt regarding evil spirit whether it helps. Nevertheless, he blesses on all these, and it implies over washing the hands, not over cleanliness of the hands. See what Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 9, and see seif 22 there.
