11) Water brought into the privy to wipe with is invalid for netilat yadayim since they are repugnant, as Bach wrote in siman 105; Be'er Heitiv brought there sk 8: water from which pig or dog drank is invalid for netilat yadayim because repugnant from drinking. Although Rav Maamarim there sk 26 disagreed with Bach — see there — nevertheless one must be concerned for doubt in blessings. So ruled Rav Ben Avraham Parashat Acharei Kedoshim letter 13: water brought into privy is invalid for netilat yadayim.

11) And regarding netilat yadayim of the privy — Zachor LeChaim ch. 3 maamar 50 nun wrote: we see most people when leaving the privy pour the water in the vessel they brought to the privy and do not wash with that water — so too Rabbenu HaGadol in Zivchei Tzedek vol. 2 siman 116 letter 48: we have special vessels always in the privy and do not wash from them — proper for every person — end quote. So practice chassidim of Beit El Yachbatz: they do not wash from water brought into privy; however in pressing need one may permit as Zachor LeChaim there, since one does not bless on netilat yadayim.

11) There — however "some say" he does not bless on it. From conclusion of this wording it implies he does not bless on them at all — neither al netilas yadayim nor for cleanliness of hands. But Levush wrote he does not bless al netilas yadayim but blesses for cleanliness of hands; so wrote Prisha. Magen Avraham sk 5 wrote: what Maran wrote that he does not bless — perhaps here addresses one who does not intend to pray now — end quote. However in Pri Tzaddik p. 74a he wrote: one cannot bless for cleanliness of hands, for this was not instituted except when he does not wash in water but wipes hands with something that cleans; but when he washes in water it does not apply. Per this what is written in seif 1 — invalid water for meal netilah is valid for morning netilah but he does not bless on it — means he does not bless at all, unlike Levush and Magen Avraham following him who say he blesses for cleanliness of hands if he wants to pray immediately; but this blessing does not apply when he washes in water — end quote. So too Maamar Mordechai sk 3 at length; so too Rav Neveh Shalom sk 2. And this is primary as we rule.
