One who slept. See Taz. The doubt is whether, like the Zohar, even by day washing is required, or like Orchos Chayim, that night causes it. What the Rav wrote here, and likewise regarding one who rises before daylight, that he should wash without a blessing, is not because of doubt; if so, certainly no blessing applies for evil spirit, as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 13, and there is no proof to the contrary from the Levush; see there.
