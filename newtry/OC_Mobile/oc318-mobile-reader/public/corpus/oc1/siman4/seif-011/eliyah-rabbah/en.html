One should not wash from someone who has not washed, etc. For it is written, the pure shall sprinkle upon the impure. Should you ask how the right hand receives from the left, since it is impure, one may answer that here he wishes to remove the impurity from himself and it is impossible otherwise. But a person who has not yet become pure and remains in his impurity has no power to remove another impurity until he is purified. So wrote Seder HaYom. He further wrote that this concern applies only to the morning washing because of the spirit of impurity, but for other washings during the day one need not be particular, and whoever is particular is among those who cause wonder. So too wrote the Shelah, folio 194, and Knesses HaGedolah. I therefore wonder at the Taz, who wrote that all the more so for washing for eating, whose main obligation is because of impurity connected to terumah. He did not address the words of the great authorities mentioned above. Abudarham and Tashbetz, siman 277, also imply that it is permitted, and so it appears below in siman 159, seif 11 and seif 12. See the Agudah at the end of Maseches Derech Eretz.
