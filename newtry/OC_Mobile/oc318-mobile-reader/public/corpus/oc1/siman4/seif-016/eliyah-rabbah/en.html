[16] Forbidden to mention, etc. It implies when he does not mention the Name he may pray or study with soiled hands — possibly not precise; proper to be stringent — so Olas Tamid wrote. But in seif 1 I wrote in the name of Seder HaYom that one may pray without Name and epithet. However for study it seems forbidden, as below siman 92 seif 4; see below in Shulchan Aruch end of siman 47 that one may study before netilah — there it deals with not having touched soiled places — easy to understand.
