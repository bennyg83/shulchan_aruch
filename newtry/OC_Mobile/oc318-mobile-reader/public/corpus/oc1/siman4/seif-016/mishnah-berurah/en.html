(35) Sixty breaths — meaning, consecutive; but he would doze many times less than sixty breaths until midnight, and from midnight onward he would strengthen himself like a lion:

(36) That specifically by day — the latter authorities challenged him, for this is not found there so in the Gemara except regarding the Amoraim; but David himself was also careful at night; and the practical difference — all this for us — that a scrupulous person should be stringent at least by day. Shabbat is not included in this. And see below in siman 231 seif 1, that if it is impossible for him to study without a midday nap it is permitted to sleep a little, but he should not prolong it — see there; and see there further that this should likewise not be his intent for his own pleasure but only for service of the Blessed One.
