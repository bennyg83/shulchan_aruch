Sixty breaths. Meaning consecutively. Rather, he dozed several times for less than sixty breaths until midnight, and from midnight onward he strengthened himself like a lion.
That specifically by day. The later authorities challenged him, for this is not found there in the Gemara except regarding the Amoraim, but David himself was careful even at night. The practical difference of all this for us is that a spiritual person should be stringent at least by day. Shabbos is not included in this. See below in siman 231, seif 1, that if it is impossible for him to learn without a midday sleep, it is permitted to sleep a little, but he should not lengthen it; see there. See there further that even this should not be for his own pleasure, but for the service of Him, may He be blessed. See Machatzis HaShekel, who wrote that the matter of sleep during the day depends on what the person is like and on what is needed for his service of Him, may He be blessed.
