David, etc.; sixty breaths. Opinions vary greatly about this measure. Some say three hours, proving it from the Arizal's practice of sleeping two or three hours on Shabbos. Others reject this: a Torah scholar has a mitzvah of Shabbos delight, and perhaps he awoke several times and never slept sixty breaths continuously. Some say a little more than half an hour; others, only slightly more than three minutes. A conscientious person should be stringent according to his ability.
