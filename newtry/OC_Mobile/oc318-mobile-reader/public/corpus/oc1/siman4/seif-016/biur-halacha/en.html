David, etc., sixty breaths — opinions multiplied on this measure. Some say it is three hours, with proof from that the Ari z"l would sleep on Shabbat two or three hours. Some reject this: a Torah scholar on Shabbat is different, for there is a mitzvah to enjoy Shabbat; moreover it is not compelled at all, for perhaps he woke several times during his sleep and did not sleep sixty breaths at once. Some say it is more than half an hour. Some say it is a measure slightly more than three minutes. Therefore a conscientious person should be stringent according to his capacity.
