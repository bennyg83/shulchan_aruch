S.k. 16, chapter, etc. Concerning the amoraim. Abaye said that the master's sleep, meaning Rabbah's, by day was like Rav's, meaning he conducted himself in daytime sleep like Rav, and Rav like Rabbi, and Rabbi like David. This means that these amoraim conducted their daytime sleep according to the measure of David's sleep. But it was not mentioned regarding David's sleep whether it was by day or even by night. One can say that David was stringent even at night, while the amoraim were stringent upon themselves only by day, like David at night, who dozed like a horse, and after midnight strengthened himself like a lion and rose and did not sleep at all.
And in the Gemara, etc. Meaning that he should not be stringent, etc. According to this, the Rama's phrase that he was careful is not precise.
