(Note 16) The chapter, etc. On the amoraim. Abbaye said: "My master's sleep during the day" — namely, Rabba's — "is like Rav's" — namely, he conducted himself regarding daytime sleep like Rav; Rav like Rabbi; Rabbi like David. Namely, these amoraim conducted daytime sleep according to David's measure of sleep; but regarding David's sleep it was not stated whether it was by day [or] even at night. One may say that David was stringent even at night, but the amoraim did not impose stringency on themselves except by day — like David at night, who dozed like a horse, and after midnight he would strengthen himself like a lion, rise, and not sleep at all.

And in the Gemara, etc. He explains that one should not be stringent, etc.; and accordingly Rama's wording "he was careful" is not precise.
