16) After rubbing do not leave fingers bent downward — immediately raise hand and fingers upward to the head, at least to the face (face is part of head), and bless al netilas yadayim immediately when raising, so the raising is not in vain — as in Zohar: forbidden to raise hands emptily; hands high and upright until finishing blessing. We bless netilat yadayim — language of exaltation like "He lifted and exalted them" — not on washing hands. When raising both hands to head, insert arm joints called cubit in Old French and aks in Arabic forward of body in secret "and all their backs inward." Spread both palms at blessing as receiving something — hinting acceptance of purity — as our Rabbi in Shaar HaKavanos and Orchos Tzadikim. Ben Avraham there letter 4.
