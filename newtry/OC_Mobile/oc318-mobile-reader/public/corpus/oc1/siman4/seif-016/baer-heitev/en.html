Was careful. See what Magen Avraham wrote in se'if kaf 16. The wording "was careful" does not imply so; rather David himself was careful. The author Yad Aharon wondered at Magen Avraham and wrote that from Rashi's words there it implies David was careful by day, for they said there: his sleep was like that of Mar, like Rav and our teacher Rav, like David, etc. Rashi wrote there that he received from his teachers that this was David's sleep — sixty breaths — "he would sleep by day," end of his words; thus explicitly from Rashi's words that David too was careful to sleep by day more than sixty breaths, end of Yad Aharon's words there. And he made a great error: "sixty breaths" {Rama: (in the printed texts)} "he would sleep by day" are two statements of Rashi. "He would sleep by day" refers forward, to Abaye "he would sleep" as he entered from Pumbedita, etc., and for this Rashi wrote "he would sleep by day"; see Sukkah folio 26 side b, and see in She'eris Knesses HaGedolah se'if 9: {Rama: (see in Eliyah Rabbah se'if kaf 11, lengthy discussion on this, reconciling the view of the Zohar with the Gemara and resolving Magen Avraham's difficulties on the Rama — see there)}:
