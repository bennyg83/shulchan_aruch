In Magen Avraham siman katan 4 "Kosher," etc. And some erase this subsection marker. And so it seems to me in my humble opinion that it is siman katan 1 with siman katan 3; and his intent is that therefore Rama corrects below siman 164, for there it speaks of the law of disqualified water, regarding which some say one does not bless upon them. But the law of a vessel and human force is not mentioned in siman 164 but only in siman 159 — the author does not deal with this here, for it does not hinder and one blesses according to all:
And see in Peri Megadim siman katan 3 which forces this [reading] — and it is not [correct]; and it is possible one need not erase at all but only add the word "etc.," and this "4" with the word "Kosher" is one word; and so it should be in siman 164 "Kosher," etc., but "vessel" as it should be; and he meant to say that regarding those waters invalid for netilat yadayim for a meal mentioned in siman 164 — regarding this he wrote here "Kosher," etc.; and his intent is that one should not bless (and one edition separated the "4" and made from it a marker by itself — and it is not [so]). For even though [the water is] kosher, nevertheless it hinders somewhat. But vessel and human force do not hinder at all be'dieved and one blesses. And I saw someone who corrected even "vessel," etc. — and it is a mistake; for it is obvious that vessel and human force are more lenient; for even one who is scrupulous about water not to bless — nevertheless regarding vessel and human force he blesses, as he indicated the place in seif 7; and it is obvious that what he wrote there was even for one who holds that with disqualified water one does not bless on [God's] Name.
