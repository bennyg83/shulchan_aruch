And he blesses al netilat yadayim. Because hands are busy, and it is impossible that they did not touch unclean flesh at night; therefore they enacted a blessing before he recites Keriat Shema and prays — so wrote the Rosh. The Rashba gave a reason: in the morning one becomes a new creature, as it is said, "They are new every morning"; therefore they enacted netilah at that time. And although netilah is from the word "anteel," meaning a vessel, and here a vessel is not really required but only for eating — nevertheless one does not change the blessing, as Beit Yosef wrote. And the matter is discussed: whether one must bless immediately after netilah or wait until he comes to the synagogue:
