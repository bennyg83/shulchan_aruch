{Rama: Magen Avraham, seif kaf bet} And now it is permitted to touch. Therefore, if he does not have water to pour except one time only — even though he recites a blessing on netilat yadayim, since it is effective for prayer — nevertheless he does not recite Asher Yatzar, for it is still forbidden to touch the orifices as long as he has not poured three times. And likewise when he rises before daylight and washes his hands — since it is still forbidden to touch the orifices out of doubt whether ruach ra'ah rests until daylight (see siman 134) — he does not recite Asher Yatzar until daylight and washes a second time. And according to Magen Avraham he recites [Asher Yatzar]. Therefore it appears proper to fulfill the words of the authors brought in Magen Avraham, seif kaf aleph — that he should act thus: he pours water once on his hands and recites Al Netilat Yadayim, for it is effective for prayer; but the obligation of the blessing Asher Yatzar has not yet applied to him.
