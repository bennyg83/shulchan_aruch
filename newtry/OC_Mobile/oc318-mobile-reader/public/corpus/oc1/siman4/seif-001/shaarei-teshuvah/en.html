Netilat yadayim. See Baer Heitev. Machazik Berachah cites Or Tzaddikim by R' Meir Poppers, who writes in Maharam Nigrin's name that the morning ruach ra'ah does not leave the hands until after they are dried. Accordingly, one should not bless until drying, unlike the practice Knesset HaGedolah cites from Seder HaYom, which Magen Avraham quotes as Baer Heitev records. One should also dry the hands first and then wash the face. According to the Zohar's statement that a distinct spirit exists in the privy, etc., if one dried his hands without washing his face, then entered the privy and washed his hands, he must dry them and afterward wash his face; see there. Our custom is to wash the face without first drying the hands. One who wishes to be stringent may nevertheless walk to obtain a towel even if it is more than four amos away, because there is no need for such stringency in this matter, as I wrote in siman 1; see there.
