Netilat yadayim. Baer Hetev in Machzik Berachah in the name of Or Tzaddikim to Maharam Papresh, who wrote in the name of Maharam Nigrin that no ruach ra'ah departs from the hands in the morning until after wiping; therefore he should not bless until he wipes — unlike what was written in the name of Seder Hayom, Kitzur Nachlat Avraham (they brought it in Magen Avraham, as Baer Hetev wrote). And one should wipe the hands first and afterward wash the face; and according to what is written in the Zohar that one spirit is in the bathroom, etc. — if he wiped his hands and did not wash his face, and went to the bathroom and washed his hands — he must wipe them again and afterward wash his face. See there.
