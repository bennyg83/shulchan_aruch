Netilat yadayim. In Tosafos on Sotah chapter 1, s.v. kerias shema and prayer, it is explained that blessings over mitzvos may be recited in any language; see Sefer Birchas Avraham volume 9 from siman 288 through siman 295. Seder Hayom writes that immediately after netilah he should bless and not wait to dry his hands; so too Birchas Avraham volume 3. See above siman 1, se'if 2 in the name of Toldos Yaakov: he wrote in Seder Hayom that one should not touch his garments until he washes, and the Gemara does not imply so — see siman 46. And it is stated in Berachos folio 51: do not take your robe in the morning immediately upon sunrise and put it on. {Rama: (It is written in Sefer Emek Berachah: one should have kavanah in the blessing of netilat yadayim, for it has thirteen words corresponding to the thirteen attributes)}:

His needs. Because a new creation comes about, the texts imply that he should bless Al Netilat Yadayim immediately and afterward relieve himself and say Asher Yatzar. Magen Avraham wrote that it is better to relieve himself first and then bless netilat yadayim and Asher Yatzar; and all the more so if he needs to clean his nostrils, for it is forbidden for him to bless — refer above:

Below. The intention of the Rama is specifically water whose appearance has changed or in which the melachos mentioned in siman 160 were performed; but a vessel and human force do not impede, and he blesses as stated in se'if 7:
