Netilat yadayim. In the Tosafot of Sotah, chapter Eilu Ne'emarim, s.v. Keriat Shema and Tefillah, it is explained that blessings of mitzvot are recited in any language, and see the book Birkat Avraham, part 9, from siman 288 until siman 295. It is written in Seder HaYom that immediately after the washing one should bless and not wait for the drying, and so wrote Birkat Avraham, part 3; and see above siman 1, seif katan 2, what is written there in the name of Tola'at Yaakov. He wrote in Seder HaYom that one should not touch one's clothes until one washes, but in the Gemara it does not seem so; see siman 46. And it is stated in Berakhot daf 51: Do not take your robe in the morning from the hand of the attendant and put it on. (It is written in the book Emek Berakhah that one should intend in the blessing of netilat yadayim that it has 13 words corresponding to the 13 Attributes):
His needs. Because one becomes a new creation; in the writings it is implied that one should bless over netilat yadayim immediately, and afterward do one's needs and say Asher Yatzar.
