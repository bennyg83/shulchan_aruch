1) [Se'if 1] He should wash his hands, etc. Tolaas Yaakov writes that Zohar states: One who walks four amos in the morning without washing his hands is liable to death. Bach; Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur 4; Halachah Berurah 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, second edition, siman 1:7; Birkei Yosef, siman 1:1; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 18a; Siddur Beis Yaakov 1. Pri Etz Chaim by Maharchu and Olas Tamid by Mahari Tzemach, page 17a, likewise state that one may not walk four amos without this morning washing, as Zohar expounds upon, 'Who placed a god in his hand.' Matsas Shimurim, page 3a, and Ben Ish Chai, Toldos 6, write likewise. Regarding Bach's words, see Birkei Yosef there. Eliyah Rabbah, siman 1:4, cites Damesek Eliezer concerning Tolaas Yaakov's statement: 'Perhaps this applied only in their times, as with uncovered liquids and pairs.' Birkei Yosef writes that this is unconvincing. Shaar HaKavanos also implies that it applies nowadays, as cited below in paragraph 12. Every person should therefore prepare the vessel beside his bed so that he need not walk four amos. Ben Ish Chai writes likewise.
