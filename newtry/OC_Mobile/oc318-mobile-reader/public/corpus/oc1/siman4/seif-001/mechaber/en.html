The laws of washing the hands — it contains twenty-three clauses.
One washes one’s hands and recites the blessing on hand-washing. Rema: Some say one also [recites] Asher Yatzar even if one did not relieve oneself (and such is the custom) (Avudraham). Water that is invalid for hand-washing before a meal — see below siman 160 — is valid for hand-washing before prayer. However, some say one does not recite a blessing over it.
