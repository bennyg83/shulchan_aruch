The laws of washing the hands. It contains 23 seifim: One should wash his hands and recite the blessing over washing the hands. {Rama: Some say that he should also recite Asher Yatzar even if he did not relieve himself, and this is the custom. [Abudarham]} Water that is invalid for washing the hands for a meal, as discussed below in siman 160, is valid for washing the hands for prayer. However, some say that one does not recite a blessing over such water.
