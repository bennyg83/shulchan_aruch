Asher Yatzar has no rule of a blessing adjacent to its fellow that one should not open it with "Baruch," for Al Netilat Yadayim one says several times a day without Asher Yatzar (so writes Abudraham and Shulchan Aruch):

That he does not bless over them means the blessing of Al Netilat Yadayim, but he blesses for cleanliness of hands. And some say he blesses Al Netilat Yadayim; if he did not wipe his hands with a bundle or a cloth, then he blesses for cleanliness of hands (my teacher Bach wrote in the name of Rosh at the end of chapter HaRo'eh in the name of other poskim). Nevertheless lechatchilah he should go back for kosher water and another vessel, as for netilat yadayim for a meal. And some say the same applies to Mincha and Maariv prayer: if he relieved himself and washed his hands, he blesses Al Netilat Yadayim; but all day, when he relieved himself, he need only bless Asher Yatzar, for they did not institute Al Netilat Yadayim except for a meal and prayer (Rosh at the end of chapter HaRo'eh):
