1) [Seif 1] He shall wash his hands, etc. Tolaas Yaakov wrote: in Zohar it states one who walks four cubits in the morning without washing hands is liable to death — Bach. Knesset HaGedolah Hagahot Tosefes, letter 4, 52, letter 1, Razah, siman 1, letter 7 in Maaseh Torah. Berach Yosef siman 1 letter 1, Shaarei Tzion page 18a, Beis Yosef letter 1; so Pe'as HaChokhmah of Maharachav and Orchos Tzadikim of Mahari Tzemach page 209a: forbidden to walk four cubits without this morning netilah, as Zohar on "who brought God in his hand"; so Machatzis HaShekel page 3a. Ben Avraham Parashat Toldos letter 6 — what Bach wrote, see Berach Yosef there. What Eliyah Rabbah siman 1 letter 4 in the name of Damascus Eliezer on Tolaas Yaakov above: "perhaps only in their time like regarding revelation and pairs" — end quote — Berach Yosef wrote his words are not plausible, as from Ari z"l in Shaar HaKavanos it applies also in this era; Beis Din below letter 12. Therefore every person should prepare the vessel near his bed so he need not walk four cubits — so Ben Avraham there.
