(Note 17) With water and not with something that cleans — this is not a general rule, for where one touches his body in an unclean place on his hand, even if he wishes to study, even l'chatchila he need not use water; it is enough to clean with earth, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 92, note 5.

And he need not three times. In Eliyah Rabbah he brought in the name of Sefer Mekor Chayim that a vessel is not required for all of them; nevertheless, for one leaving the bathroom (also in Magen Avraham in siman 7 he brought in the name of Sefer Heichal HaKodesh that one leaving the bathroom needs three times, and Magen Avraham disagreed — see there) — and one who walks among the dead and attends to his bed needs three times even though no vessel is required.
