S.k. 17, with water and not with something that cleans. This is not a general rule, for one who touched a soiled place on his body with his hand, even if he wants to learn, even lechatchilah he does not need water and cleaning with earth is enough, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 92, s.k. 5.
And it does not require three times. In Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he brought in the name of Sefer Mekor Chayim that although a vessel is not required for all of them, nevertheless one who leaves the privy, and Magen Avraham in siman 7 also brought in the name of Sefer Heichal HaKodesh that one who leaves the privy needs three times, though Magen Avraham disagreed there, and one who walks among the dead or had marital relations requires washing three times, even though a vessel is not required.
