Magen Avraham, s.k. 9 And he should bless over washing the hands. The initials mean over the cleanliness of the hands. That is, although here the invalidity is not in the water itself, and such a deficiency does not prevent the blessing, so he blesses over washing the hands as in Magen Avraham s.k. 6, nevertheless this is worse even than where the water itself is invalid. There, there are opinions, see Magen Avraham s.k. 5, and the Bach ruled to bless over washing the hands because defective water does not harm morning washing. Here, however, the reason is that Magen Avraham holds in s.k. 1 that the primary view is like the Rashba, because one becomes like a new creation; according to Magen Avraham, for that reason pouring is needed, similar to the laver. Thus where it is not by pouring it helps only for prayer, and is comparable only to a pebble, where according to everyone one blesses over cleanliness as in seif 22. However, Magen Avraham disputes even there regarding a pebble, and that is why he concludes: see what I wrote in s.k. 10.
