{Rama: Magen Avraham, note 9} And he blesses al netilat yadayim — the acronym is for cleanliness of the hands; namely, although here there is no invalidity in the water itself and the like, it does not prevent the blessing and one blesses al netilat yadayim, as Magen Avraham wrote in note 6; nevertheless this is inferior even to where there is invalidity in the water itself, for there are opinions — see Magen Avraham, note 5, that Bach ruled to bless al netilat yadayim because netilat yadayim does not harm invalid water in the water — not so here. The reason is that Magen Avraham holds in note 1 that the main view is like Rashba, that one becomes a new creature; and as stated for Magen Avraham, for this reason pouring is required, similar to the basin — not so here, where it is not by pouring, it helps only for prayer; therefore it is not similar except to a bundle, where one blesses according to all on cleanliness, as in seif 22; however Magen Avraham disagrees there too regarding a bundle — and this is what he concludes, "see note 10":
