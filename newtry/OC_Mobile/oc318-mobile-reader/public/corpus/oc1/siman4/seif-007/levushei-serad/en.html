Magen Avraham, s.k. 3 The mentioned ones, meaning those that are invalid because of the water itself. Regarding this some say he should not bless. This differs from where the water itself is fit for washing and the deficiency is from another side, such as the vessel, human force, or the like, where according to everyone he blesses, as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 4 and s.k. 6.
