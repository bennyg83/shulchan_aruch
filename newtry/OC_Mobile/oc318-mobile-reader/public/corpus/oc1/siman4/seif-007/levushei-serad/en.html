{Rama: Magen Avraham, note 3} Those mentioned — namely, what is invalid on account of the water themselves; in that case some say he does not bless. Not so if the water on their own is fit for netilat yadayim and the deficiency is from elsewhere, such as the vessel, the force of pouring, and the like — according to all he blesses, as Magen Avraham wrote in note 4 and note 6.
