[7] But not for spirit, etc. Even if he rinsed three times in this water — nevertheless dipping hands three times in water gathered on the ground also helps remove the spirit. One cannot say water gathered on ground becomes impure from the hands; however if it lacks mikveh measure there is room to doubt. In pressing need when he has no ready water, he may thrust hands in snow on the ground in three alternating places — so Olas Tamid wrote. From Beis Yosef's words it seems he doubts Zohar's words whether we require specifically pouring from vessel — if so there is no permission in all this; so Rama page 165 and Lechem Chamudos 4:4 imply. Again Magen Avraham was printed: plainly one does not need forty se'ah, not less than bundle seif 22 — unlike Olas Tamid — end quote. This is incorrect: bundle helps for cleanliness, but Olas Tamid speaks that they should not become impure from the hands.
