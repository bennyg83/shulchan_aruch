Strength of a man — Baer Hetev wrote in Shlemei Tzibbur regarding morning netilat yadayim one need not be strict about uncovered water even where snakes are common — which is forbidden only for drinking. But in Yoreh De'ah siman 116, Baer Heitev wrote there, citing the gaon Maharar Shapira, may he rest in Eden, that one should not wash except in covered water all night — even hands, all the more rinsing the mouth. And see the Hagahah there, that so is implied from Seder Hayom brought in Kitzur Nachlat Avraham; but in our countries they are not strict about uncovered [water] at all, even for rinsing the mouth, and all the more for netilat yadayim:
