Human force. See Baer Heitev. Shalmei Tzibbur writes that morning netilat yadayim does not require concern for uncovered water, even where snakes are common, because uncovered water was prohibited only for drinking. Baer Heitev on Yoreh De'ah, siman 116, however, cites the gaon R' Sheftel, of blessed memory, that one should wash only with water that remained covered throughout the night, even for the hands and certainly for rinsing the mouth. See the gloss there, which says that Seder HaYom, cited by Knesset HaGedolah, implies the same. In our countries, however, no concern is shown for uncovered water at all, even for rinsing the mouth and certainly for washing the hands.
