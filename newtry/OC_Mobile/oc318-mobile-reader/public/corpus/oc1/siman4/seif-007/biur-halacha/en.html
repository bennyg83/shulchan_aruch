It is good, etc.; that invalidate. Matters that require only initial care in netilas yadayim need not be observed here. Pri Megadim writes likewise.
And the other matters, etc. Magen Avraham 3 and Pri Megadim explain that Rema refers only to water less than a revi'is; if the water itself is invalid, Rema follows the cited view that one should not bless. Biur HaGra on this gloss appears to understand that according to Rema, after the fact no defect is indispensable at all, as Rema's plain wording indeed implies. I omitted this from Mishnah Berurah because it makes no practical difference: in paragraph 7 we already cited the later authorities who reject that stringent view.
