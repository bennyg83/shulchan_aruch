It is good, etc., the invalidating factors — it appears that matters that are not part of netilah but only lekhatchilah stringencies need not be observed here; so too Peri Megadim.

And the other matters, etc. — see Magen Avraham sk 3 and Peri Megadim, who explains his words: Rama's intent here is only regarding whether the water is less than a revi'it; but if the water itself is invalid, Rama is stringent like the view above not to bless on it. From the Gra's explanation on this gloss it appears he explains Rama holds that be'di'eved it is not invalidating at all for any matter — and so the plain meaning of Rama's wording indeed implies. However, I did not copy all this in Mishna Berurah because for actual law there is no practical difference, for we already copied in Mishna Berurah sk 7 that the later authorities do not hold like that view above.
