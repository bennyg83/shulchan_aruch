From Taz. And behold a non-Jew — one can say he does not accept impurity; and as the Zohar states, "one who slumbers" etc. — proof of this is the graves of non-Jews; according to one opinion even by contact one does not defile. And the world truly is not wary of this. But women should be very careful in preparing foods lest they defile by their hands. See Taz: also children — why are they not wary of this? It is proper to wash their hands every morning, because danger is more severe than prohibition. And in Mishna Berurah [regarding] netilat yadayim for a meal he challenged in Eliyah Rabbah note 7, and so Shlah and elsewhere — that for netilat yadayim for a meal, which is only for added sanctity, one may take from one who did not wash his hands — as in Orach Chayyim simanim 159 and 160.
