However, neither is it an impediment — neither a vessel, meaning bedieved it is not an impediment — and he blesses Al Netilat Yadayim; it is not comparable to invalid water, for which one blesses for cleanliness of hands, as Beit Yosef implies. For netilat yadayim for a meal he should pour water from the right hand onto the left, as the Zohar states in parashat Terumah. See Levush and Magen David. It is written in Seder Hayom: immediately after netilah he should bless and not wait to dry his hands.
