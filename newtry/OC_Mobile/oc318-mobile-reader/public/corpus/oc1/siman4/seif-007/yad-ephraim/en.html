Seif 7 in all these matters — meaning, even regarding a vessel and human force [on the water] — it is proper to be scrupulous about this; unlike disqualified water which is more deficient, for one does not bless upon it, as above in seif 30. And regarding this Rama wrote "but it does not hinder etc." — his intent is that even though the author wrote one should be scrupulous in all these matters, nevertheless this is only where it is possible; but if it is not possible it does not hinder — neither what is invalid because of the vessel nor because of human force.
