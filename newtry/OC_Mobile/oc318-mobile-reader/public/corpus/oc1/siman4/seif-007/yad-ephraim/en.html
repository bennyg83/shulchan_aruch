Se'if 7: “In all these matters.” This means that even concerning the vessel and human force, it is proper to be particular. Invalid water is more serious, however, because no blessing is recited over it, as stated above. Concerning the other matters the Rema writes, “Nevertheless, they do not invalidate,” etc. He means that although the author says one should be particular concerning all of them, this applies only when compliance is possible. When it is impossible, a defect involving the vessel or human force does not invalidate the washing. Regarding the invalid water discussed in siman 160, however, one should be particular even after the fact when no alternative was initially available, and should seek valid water and wash again, because some authorities maintain that no blessing is recited over the invalid water.
