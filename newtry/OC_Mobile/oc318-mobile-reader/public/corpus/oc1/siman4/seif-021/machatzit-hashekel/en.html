(Note 23) There is no, etc. Up to the chest. In my humble opinion, everything depends on the country and their manner of dress: where, as it seems, the shirt's sleeve is wide at the hand, and thus they do not wrap around the neck — then it remains until the measure Magen Avraham wrote, where most of the arm is exposed, and there is no rubbing of sweat there. But where they wrap a handkerchief around their neck and the shirt sleeve at the hand is short — then those places too are mostly covered, and there is also rubbing of sweat there.
