One must be careful during prayer or when eating (and see below siman 92 s. 7, and siman 164) not to touch the calf, thigh, or covered parts of the body, because sweat beads gather there (melimelei zeia — “beads of sweat,” chapter “Their impurity” — like small barley grains). Likewise one should not scratch the head; but places that are exposed on the head and face and exposed parts of one’s arms need not be guarded so strictly.
