One must be careful during prayer or when eating (and see below siman 92, seif 7, and siman 164) not to touch the calf, thigh, or covered parts of the body, because melimelei zeia gather there (melimelei zeia—in the chapter “Their impurity”—are like small barley grains). Likewise one should not scratch the head; but exposed places on the head and face and exposed places on his arms need not be guarded so strictly.
