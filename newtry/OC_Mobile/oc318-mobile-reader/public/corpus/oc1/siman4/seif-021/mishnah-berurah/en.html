In prayer. The same applies while he is engaged in Torah; in siman 92, seif 7, in the gloss.
Siman 92, seif 6. For it is explained there that if he is standing in prayer and remembers that he touched a filthy place, it is enough to clean with earth, or to rub his hands on the wall, for what else can he do in the middle of prayer. However, if he is still in Krias Shema or in Pesukei D'Zimrah, he must go and wash his hands.
And siman 164. For there it is explained that if he transgressed and touched during the meal, he must wash his hands a second time according to the law. However, regarding the blessing of al netilas yadayim, the agreement of most later authorities is not to repeat the blessing; see there in the Mishnah Berurah.
Pellets of sweat. See Yoreh Deah, siman 116, that one must be careful of sweat, for all sweat is a deadly poison except the sweat of the face. A sign for the matter is: 'By the sweat of your face you shall eat bread.'
