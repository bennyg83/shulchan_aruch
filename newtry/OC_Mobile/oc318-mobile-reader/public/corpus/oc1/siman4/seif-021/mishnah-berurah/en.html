(49) In prayer — The same applies when he is engaged in Torah [in siman 92, seif 7 in the gloss]:

(50) Siman 92, seif 6 — It is explained there that if he is standing in prayer and remembers that he touched an unclean place, it suffices to clean with earth, or if he rubs his hands on a wall — what should he do in the middle of prayer? However, if he is still in Keriat Shema or in Pesukei Dezimra, he must go and wash his hands:

(51) And siman 164 — There it is explained that if he passed and touched during the meal he must wash his hands again as required; but regarding the blessing of al netilat yadayim, the majority of the Acharonim agreed not to repeat the blessing — see Mishna Berurah on siman 164:

(52) Traces of sweat — See Yoreh De'ah, siman 116, that one must beware of sweat, for all sweat is a potion of death except facial sweat; and a sign for the matter: "By the sweat of your brow you shall eat bread":

(53) And on his face — And likewise on his neck up to the chest [Beit Yosef]. Machatzit HaShekel wrote that this depends on local custom, whether the way is to go uncovered there:

(54) On his arms — Up to the joint called the elbow. And likewise if one walks barefoot, it is possible that this is included among exposed places [Peri Megadim in siman 92]:
