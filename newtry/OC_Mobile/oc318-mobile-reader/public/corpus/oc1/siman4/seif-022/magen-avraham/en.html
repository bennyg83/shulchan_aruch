“Or with earth.” Or with wood (see Sefer Chasidim), whether they are dirty or whether he uses them absentmindedly (Tur siman 92).

“On cleanliness of the hands.” This requires study: in Shulchan Aruch siman 159 he ruled that one always blesses on netilas yadayim, and so too in Rashba’s responsum siman 191, and in siman 494—see there—for the essence of the commandment is to wash from a vessel; therefore we do not bless netilas yadayim for the Minchah prayer, because wiping with earth suffices. Why then did he rule here on cleanliness of the hands? See what I wrote in siman 61 and siman 128, seif 6.
