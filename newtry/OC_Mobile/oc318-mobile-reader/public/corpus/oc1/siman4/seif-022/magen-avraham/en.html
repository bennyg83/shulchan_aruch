“Or with earth.” Or with wood (see Sefer Chasidim), whether they are dirty or whether he uses them absentmindedly (Tur siman 92).

“On cleanliness of the hands.” This requires study: in siman 159 Mishnah Berurah ruled that one always blesses on netilas yadayim, and so too Rashba responsum 191, and siman 494—see there—for the main command is to wash from a vessel; therefore we do not bless netilas yadayim for Minchah because wiping with earth suffices. Why then rule here on “cleanliness of the hands”? See what I wrote in siman 61 and siman 128:6.
