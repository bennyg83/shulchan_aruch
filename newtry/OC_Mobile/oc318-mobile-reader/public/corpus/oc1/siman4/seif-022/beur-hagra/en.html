Seif 22, "and he recites the blessing," etc. So wrote the Rosh. This follows the Mordechai and Semag, who wrote that one recites "al netilas yadayim" only when washing from a vessel, as written at the end of siman 159, seif 20, in the gloss. But the Rashba and Rabbenu Yonah wrote there that one always recites "al netilas yadayim," because of the washing that we were commanded about; the larger amount includes the smaller. So wrote the Shulchan Aruch there.
