He should wipe his hands with a pebble, etc. This is the language of Olas Tamid: Sefer Birkat Avraham wrote that one must wipe three times, similar to water. This does not appear correct, for since it does not help for the evil spirit, why do I need three times? For mere cleanliness, one cleaning is sufficient. He looked only at Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah and not at Birkat Avraham itself, folio 49, where he wrote so according to the first answer, that it also helps for evil spirit; but according to the later answer, that only water helps for evil spirit, three times are not required. Lechatchilah it appears proper to be concerned for the first answer. See above, s.k. 2.
