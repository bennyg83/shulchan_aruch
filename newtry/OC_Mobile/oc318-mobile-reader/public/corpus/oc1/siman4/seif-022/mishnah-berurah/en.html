(55) If there is none, etc. — If he has water he must wash even for mincha and maariv prayer, even if he knows of no impurity on them; and the washing is without a blessing, for they enacted a blessing only in the morning, as stated in siman 7, seif 1. If he has no water, some say his law is as here — he cleans with anything that cleans, lest something repulsive or filth adhere to them; see below in siman 92 and in Rabbi Levi Ginzburg in Mishna Berurah:

(56) He wipes his hands — And even if they were certainly soiled — such as if he defecated and wiped — this too is effective; see below in siman 92 how far one must go to obtain water:

(57) With earth — Or he rubs with a dry cloth. Know that regarding cleanliness, since he stands where water is available, he must clean the whole hand — back and palm — until the joint; bedieved, until the end of the finger joints suffices; but not as some do, who clean only the fingertips:
