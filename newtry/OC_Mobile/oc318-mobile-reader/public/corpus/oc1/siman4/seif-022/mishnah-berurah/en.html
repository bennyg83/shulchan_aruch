The washing is without a blessing, for they instituted a blessing only in the morning, as stated in siman 7, seif 1. If he has no water, some say that his law is like here, that he should clean them with anything that cleans, lest some disgusting matter or filth be attached to them. He should wipe his hands. Even if they were certainly dirty, such as if he relieved himself and wiped, this also helps. See below in siman 92 regarding how far one must go after water.
With earth. Or he should rub them on a dry garment. Know that since cleaning stands in place of water, he must clean the entire hand, both the back and the inside, until the joint. After the fact, cleaning until the end of the finger joints is sufficient, but not as some people practice, cleaning only the tips of their fingers.
