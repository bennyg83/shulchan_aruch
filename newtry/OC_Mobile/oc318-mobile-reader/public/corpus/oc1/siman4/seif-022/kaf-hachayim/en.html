22) Machazikei Berachah wrote there: one should be careful to wash children's hands every morning, for danger is more severe than prohibition, yet the world is not careful in this — I do not know why — end quote. So Chida z"l in Moed BaKodesh siman 2 letter 60: one should warn to wash children's hands properly so they not become impure from all they touch and the sitra achra dwells in them, God forbid — end quote. In Shulchan Aruch of Razah there he wrote the reason people are lenient about touching young children who have not reached education: the main entry of the holy soul into a person is at thirteen years and one day for a male and twelve for a female — therefore then they become obligated in Torah mitzvos and become "children of punishments." The beginning of entry of this holy soul is through education in Torah and mitzvos the Sages obligated, including milah — therefore one is careful from touching a child from the day of milah onward; a holy person may be said of him — end quote. Ben Ish Chai wrote there letter 10 in name of Chida: this practice is a good segulah for children to grow in purity and be holy offspring.
