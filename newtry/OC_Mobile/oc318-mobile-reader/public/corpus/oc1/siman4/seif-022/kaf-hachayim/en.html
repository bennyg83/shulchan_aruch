22) Machazikei Berachah wrote there: one should be careful to wash children's hands every morning, for danger is more severe than prohibition, yet the world is not careful in this — I do not know why — end of his words. So too Chida z"l in Moed BaKodesh siman 2 letter 60, and these are his words: one should warn to wash the hands of young children properly so they not become impure from all they touch and the sitra achra dwells in them, Heaven forbid — end of his words. In Shulchan Aruch of Razah there he wrote the reason people are lenient about touching young children who have not reached education: the main entry of the holy soul into a person is at thirteen years and one day for a male and twelve for a female — therefore then they become obligated in Torah mitzvos and become children of punishments. The beginning of entry of this holy soul is through education in Torah and mitzvos the Sages obligated, including milah — therefore one is careful from touching a child from the day of milah onward; one may call him sanctified — end of his words. Ben Ish Chai wrote there letter 10 in the name of Chida: this practice is a good segulah for children to grow in purity and be holy offspring.
