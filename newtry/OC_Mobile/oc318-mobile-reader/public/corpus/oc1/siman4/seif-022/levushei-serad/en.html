Shulchan Aruch, se'if 14. 'He should wash without a blessing.' Here and in the following se'if this differs from the preceding one. There he omits the blessing because of doubt, since doubtful blessings are lenient. Here omission is the actual law, not uncertainty: washing is required solely because of the evil spirit, and no blessing is recited over removing an evil spirit, as Magen Avraham writes here.
