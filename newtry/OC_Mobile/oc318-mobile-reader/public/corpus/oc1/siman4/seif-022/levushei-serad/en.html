{Rama: Shulchan Aruch, seif 14.} And he washes them without a blessing. In this and also in the following seif the intention is not as in the previous seif, where because of doubt he washes without a blessing — for doubts in blessings are resolved leniently. But here he does not bless not because of doubt; rather, by law there is no doubt whether he should wash, but only on account of ruach raah, and for ruach raah one does not bless, as Magen Avraham wrote here.
