9) There — in the gloss "some say" also "Who formed," etc. So Abudraham — Maran brought in Beis Yosef and wondered; Moram z"l in Darkei Moshe letter 3 reconciled — so Levush, Bach, Derishah end of this siman, Magen Avraham sk 2. But Maamarim letter 1: I found Ri'u ch. 3 Netiv 1 wrote explicitly "Who formed" if he relieved himself, etc. — explaining Maran's words: whoever did not relieve himself should not bless Asher Yatzar — end quote. Also Orchos Tzadikim letter 2: in Shitah Gedolah Parashat Ro'eh ruled like Maran — appears so — end quote. So Shaar HaKavanos: all eighteen blessings from al netilas yadayim until end of blessing on Torah one must order and bless daily even if not obligated, for all are wondrous allusions to supernal lights — except blessing of al netilas yadayim and Asher Yatzar: if not obligated do not bless — end quote. So Pe'as HaChokhmah Shaar Berachos ch. 1; Rashash in Nefesh HaChaim page 87a; Shaarei Tzion pages 45–46; Zachor LeChaim ch. 1 Laws of Berachos letter 300. Berach Yosef siman 6 letter 3: for actual law we hold like Ari z"l and his school not to bless in morning if he did not relieve himself — end quote. See Rav Yaffe Lev ch. 1 letter 4 who brought many rishonim and acharonim holding so — see there.
