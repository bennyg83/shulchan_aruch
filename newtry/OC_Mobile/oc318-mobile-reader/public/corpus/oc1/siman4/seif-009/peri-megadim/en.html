If. See Taz. The place of doubt regarding removing evil spirit is whether the reason is the Zohar or Orchos Chayim. The doubt regarding prayer is whether the reason is the Rashba and the Sages did not differentiate, or the Rosh and the rule of not differentiating does not apply here, where he did not touch. See Be'er HaGolah s.k. 90, even according to the Rosh. He holds that according to the Rashba, in the Beis Yosef's view, although he wrote perhaps, it is indeed because they did not differentiate. The doubt is perhaps the halachah follows the Rosh, and he follows his own view in s.k. 1 and siman 7, s.k. 1, that even for us the reason of the Rosh applies. What he wrote that according to the Rashba they did not differentiate, not merely to save one from sin, although the Rashba too gives the reason that he touched a soiled place or ordinary hands, is because here, since he became a new creation, he needs a vessel like a kohen from the laver; therefore they instituted a blessing, unlike every day where cleaning with earth is enough. See Magen Avraham below s.k. 25, who holds that perhaps because of the reason of new creation alone they instituted a blessing. You see that according to this, even one who slept a temporary sleep, where we are not concerned that he touched a soiled place, and according to the Rashba he is a new creation, nevertheless one should not bless, lest the halachah follow the Rosh. However, if he relieved himself and slept a temporary sleep, according to everyone he blesses. Although for us in siman 7 one does not bless all day, such a case can exist, either because they certainly did not differentiate or because most times one's hands are not clean, as the Taz wrote in siman 7, s.k. 1. Likewise on Shavuos night, if he slept a temporary sleep he should relieve himself and bless over washing the hands; see siman 494. But according to Magen Avraham s.k. 1 and 2, who rejects the reason of the Rosh for us, even if one slept a temporary sleep and did not relieve himself, he certainly blesses over washing the hands. See what I wrote in Magen Avraham s.k. 12; and according to Magen Avraham s.k. 13, one new creation, not twice, and the Taz will concede this.
