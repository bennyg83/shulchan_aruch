Magen Avraham, s.k. 5 Over cleanliness. That is to say, the opinion introduced as some say agrees that at least one blesses over cleanliness, for it is no worse than a cleaning agent in seif 22. This is difficult against the Mechaber, who implies that one does not bless at all. Therefore he answered that the case of seif 22 is specifically where he wishes to pray immediately.
