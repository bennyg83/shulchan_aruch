Over them. See Baer Heitev. One who follows the Shulchan Aruch and washed with this water without a blessing, and afterward obtains valid water, should wash again without a blessing. This is a case of doubtful blessings, because perhaps he fulfilled the washing obligation with the first water. Although he did not bless then, the ruach ra'ah was already removed by the first washing, so he can no longer say regarding this washing, 'Who commanded us concerning washing.' The time for blessing over the first washing has passed. See Eliyah Rabbah, siman 128, se'if katan 12. See below, siman 158, regarding washing for a meal and whether one blesses after drying. Taz holds that one does not, and this certainly applies here.
