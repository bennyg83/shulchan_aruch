Upon them. Baer Hetev. And one who practices according to Shulchan Aruch and washed from these without a blessing — if afterward kosher water became available to him, he should return and wash from them without a blessing, for it is a doubt in blessings — perhaps the first washing fulfilled [the requirement]; and even though he did not bless then, nevertheless since ruach ra'ah already departed through the first washing, he cannot bless on this washing; and it passed, and on the first washing the time elapsed.
