5) There — he should wash his hands. Although by law the obligation is only netilah of hands, nevertheless it is a mitzvah to wash the face as well — Shaarei Tziyyon 19a, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 80 letter 3, Ben Ish Chai there letter 11; so in Shaar HaKavanot in the drush of the night, drush 1, he washes the face; so Pri Etz Chaim in shaar tikkun chatzot ch. 3. Although it is written in Shaar HaKavanot that he washes feet too, in Pri Etz Chaim he did not write washing feet; so in Shaar HaKavanot in the drush of morning blessings he did not write washing feet. HaEmet wrote well in letter 2: we lack power to reject the externals from the feet, for there their hold is strong — only priests, by merit of service in the Temple, as written: "Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet." By this understand washing for arba minim on Sukkot, for the merit of Shabbat can protect — end quote. Ben Ish Chai brought there at end of letter 1; so Salt BeLula letter 2; so Shaarei Tziyyon p. 4.
