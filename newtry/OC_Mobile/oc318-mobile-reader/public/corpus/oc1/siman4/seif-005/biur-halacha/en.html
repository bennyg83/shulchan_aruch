He should not touch a vat. Meaning after washing, because it may spoil the beer; the passage does not discuss before washing. Bach, Taz, and Biur HaGra agree that before washing one may not touch a beer vat, because he may introduce an evil spirit into the beer and harm those who drink it. According to them, even after the fact one must be extremely careful not to drink that beer. Chayei Adam also rules it forbidden. Artzos HaChaim concludes leniently after the fact; practical application requires examination. Regarding other foods touched before washing, Chayei Adam is lenient after the fact, as I copied in Mishnah Berurah.
