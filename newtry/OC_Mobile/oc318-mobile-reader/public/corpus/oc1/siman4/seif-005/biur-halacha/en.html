He shall not touch a barrel (gigis) — meaning, after netilat yadayim and because of spoiling the beer; but before netilah the passage does not address anything at all. However Bach, Taz, and the Gra's explanation agreed that before netilah it is forbidden to touch a beer barrel, because thereby one may introduce an evil spirit into the beer and harm those who drink it afterward. Per their view it is plain that even bedieved one must be very careful not to drink this beer. Chayei Adam also brought this as law to forbid it. However in Aruch HaChaim he concludes to be lenient bedieved, and it is doubtful for practical purposes. Regarding other foods, if one touched before netilah, one may be lenient bedieved — so too Chayei Adam, and thus I copied in Mishna Berurah.
