(There siman katan 28) "With great compassion" — the word "with compassion" should be said with a pausal accent, for "great is Your faithfulness" — one should not interrupt between them; and it is from the verse "they are new every morning" etc., that the Holy One blessed be He fulfills His faithfulness to restore the souls entrusted [to Him] morning by morning.
