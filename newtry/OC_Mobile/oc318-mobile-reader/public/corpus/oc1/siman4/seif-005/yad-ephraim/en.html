There, se'if katan 28: In the phrase “with great compassion,” one should pause after the word “compassion.” One should not separate “great is Your faithfulness,” because this derives from the verse “They are new every morning,” etc.: the Holy One, blessed is He, faithfully restores the souls entrusted to Him each morning. Those who recite the preceding words in one breath and then separate “great” from “Your faithfulness” are mistaken.
