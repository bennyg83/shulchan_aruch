Sulam Belulah sk 3: what Rashi wrote blinds and deafens — although not literally in physical sense, the matter is Torah — meaning one who touches one of these becomes that day "blind" or "deaf" to Torah, not understanding what he sees or hears, as "Who is blind but My servants" and "deaf as My angels I will send" — see Shaar HaAvodah chapter 12 page 49:3 — end quote.
