It is forbidden to mention, etc. It implies that if he does not mention the Name he may pray or learn with soiled hands; perhaps this is not precise, and it is proper to be stringent, as Olas Tamid wrote. But in siman 1 I wrote in the name of Seder HaYom that it is permitted to pray without the Name or any appellation. Nevertheless, to learn appears forbidden, as below in siman 92, seif 4. See below in Shulchan Aruch at the end of siman 47, that one may learn before washing; there it refers to a case where he did not touch soiled places, and it is easy to understand.
