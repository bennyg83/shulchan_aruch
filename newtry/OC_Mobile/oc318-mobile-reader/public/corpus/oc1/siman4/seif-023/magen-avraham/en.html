“Before washing.” For ordinary hands are not disqualified for a berachah, since we bless on fruit and do not require netilas yadayim.

“Naked.” Presumably his hands are soiled from touching the private parts, as stated in seif 21.

“That he wiped them.” With pebbles or anything that cleans (see Rabbeinu Yerucham siman 153). Still, when he rises he says Modeh ani before You, living and enduring King, who has restored my soul to me in great compassion—Your faithfulness—without containing a Divine name or epithet (Seder Hayom).
