Seif 23, "they instituted only," etc. As stated in Berachos 15a: this applies to Shema, but for prayer one must seek it, etc.

"But blessings," etc. For all of them were instituted before washing, as written there in seif 2. And we say in Chagigah 18b: one who washes his hands for fruit, etc. What is said in Berachos 43 about guests, etc., is where his hand is not clean, as Tosafos wrote there in Chagigah, and below siman 158, seif 5, in the gloss. This is what is written there: unless, etc. And that case in Berachos 62, presumably he slept naked; see the Rashba. All the more per the Rosh, who wrote that ordinary hands after sleep are unclean. One must explain the above Berachos passage as meaning that for blessings only cleanliness is needed; this is what he wrote: "until," etc.
