{Rama: Magen Avraham, note 13} Even if he slept again. It appears he follows his method in note 1 — that for us the main view is like Rashba; but for Taz, note 1, it must be said that the author here speaks of one who is not sleeping, but for one who is sleeping there is no doubt at all that he must wash, and even for prayer per the Rosh. However, in the responsum of the Rosh in Beit Yosef it seems that whenever one slept again it is considered temporary sleep, even if he slept a long time; and note 38 means that he touched an unclean place — see there.
