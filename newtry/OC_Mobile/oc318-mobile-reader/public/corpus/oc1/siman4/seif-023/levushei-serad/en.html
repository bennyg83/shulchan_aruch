Magen Avraham 13. Even if he slept again. It appears Magen Avraham follows his view in paragraph 1 that Rashba is primary for us. According to Taz 1, the Mechaber must concern one who did not sleep; if he slept, there is no doubt that washing is required even for prayer according to Rosh. Responsa Rosh cited by Beis Yosef, however, implies that any second sleep is considered temporary even if lengthy, and we do not fear he touched a dirty place; see there.
