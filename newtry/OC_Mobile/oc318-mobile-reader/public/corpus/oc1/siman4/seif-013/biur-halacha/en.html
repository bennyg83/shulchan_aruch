If he was awake, etc. — see Mishna Berurah sk 28. Also, if he slept at night with his hands covered so he certainly did not touch an unclean place — per the Rosh he need not wash his hands in the morning except because of the evil spirit and without a blessing (Peri Megadim and Aruch HaChaim).

All night — see Mishna Berurah sk 27. If he slept at the beginning of the night and rose and washed his hands to remove the evil spirit — it seems to me that for the Rashba he must again in the morning wash his hands for prayer with a blessing, for one cannot say that a new creature was already made when he rose at the beginning of the night, as it is written "new every morning," etc.; and only to satisfy the Rosh's view — for per him he need not bless again in the morning. Even so, he should relieve himself before prayer and be obligated to wash and bless according to all views.

And wash them without a blessing — see Mishna Berurah sk 30 in the name of the agreement of the later authorities. Their reason: per the Rosh, the reason one must bless in the morning is because his hands are not clean; and although for Minchah we rule in siman 7 seif 1 that one need not bless even when washing because [the hands] are not clean, nevertheless for morning the Sages instituted that one is obligated to wash hands and must bless. Therefore if he relieved himself or touched an unclean place — he must wash: for the Rosh; and also for the Rashba, who wrote the reason for morning netilah is because one becomes a new creature — one must bless even for brief waking, for they did not differentiate. And further, for renewal of the world one must bless even without enjoyment, as the Rashba wrote in responsum siman 193.
