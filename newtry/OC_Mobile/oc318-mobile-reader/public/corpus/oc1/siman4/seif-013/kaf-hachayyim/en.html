13) Some are meticulous that the right hand should never receive the vessel directly from the left. Rather, after the left hand pours upon the right, it places the vessel on the ground; the right hand then takes it from the ground and pours upon the left. The right hand, however, always gives the vessel directly to the left so that the left will pour upon it. This demonstrates the left hand's submission to the right, like a servant whom his master commands to serve him. Ben Ish Chai, ibid.
