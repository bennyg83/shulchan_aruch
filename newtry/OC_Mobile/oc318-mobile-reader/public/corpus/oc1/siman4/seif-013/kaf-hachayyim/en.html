13) There is a rule that the right hand should not take the vessel from the left hand at any time, but the left hand, after pouring on the right hand, will place the vessel in the land and then the right hand will take it from the earth and pour it on the left hand, but the right hand every time she gives the vessel to the left hand so that she can pour it, she must show submission to the left hand against the right hand, who is like a master who judges his servant for his use.
