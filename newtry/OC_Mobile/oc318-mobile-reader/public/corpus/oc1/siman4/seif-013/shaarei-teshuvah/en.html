Shaking. In Barchi Yosef he wrote that according to Rabbi Cohen when he was shaking all night there is no rabbi line and also if he took his hands before AH etc. But the remnants of a blessing he brought in the name of a book of a new donation as T'i Dish for him to take a second time, Delina rejects in the NTA as a way that rejects the sanctification of hands and feet, therefore, it seems to be the case that the Rama's should be taken without a blessing, and so on.
