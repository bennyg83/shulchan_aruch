Awakening. In Birchei Yosef he wrote that Maharach Cohen holds that when one was awake all night there is no ruach ra'ah resting; and likewise if he washed his hands before erev [prayer]. And so in the book Derech Chayim in the name of the Ari and Ramaz. But Shiyurei Berachah brought in the name of Sefer Terumat Chadashah [manuscript] that he must wash a second time — for staying overnight invalidates netilat yadayim, as it invalidates sanctification of hands and feet. See there.
