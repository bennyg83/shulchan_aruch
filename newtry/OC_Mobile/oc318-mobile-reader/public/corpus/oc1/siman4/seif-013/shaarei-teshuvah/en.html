Awake. Birkei Yosef writes that according to Maharchah Kohen, the ruach ra'ah does not rest upon one who remained awake all night; the same applies if one washed before dawn. Derech Chayyim writes likewise in the names of Arizal and Ramaz. Shiyurei Berachah, however, cites a manuscript of Terumah Chadashah that he must wash again, because remaining overnight invalidates netilat yadayim just as it invalidates the priestly sanctification of hands and feet. It therefore appears proper to follow Rama and wash without a blessing. I write likewise below in siman 494 regarding those who remain awake on Shavuos night. Magen Avraham there contains a scribal error: instead of 'and see Yoreh De'ah,' it should read, 'in siman 4, seif 13.' See there.
