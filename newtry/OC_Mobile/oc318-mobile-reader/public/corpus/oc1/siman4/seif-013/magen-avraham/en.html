“To remove.” For a minor we are not concerned that he touched an unclean place, nor has he become a new creature.
“Without a blessing.” Bach wrote that one should bless because the Sages do not make distinctions, and he proved this from a responsum of the Rosh. Still, it is not such a strong proof, for the Rosh holds that even for Minchah one needs a berachah on netilah—so here too even before prayer time, since he will end up praying, he would bless. But for us the main reason is the new creature, and that applies only to one who slept.
