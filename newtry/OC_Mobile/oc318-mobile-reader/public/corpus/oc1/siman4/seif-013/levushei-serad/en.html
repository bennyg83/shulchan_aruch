{Rama: Note 7.} See siman 181, seif 2 — namely, there he wrote that the last waters one does not pour onto the ground because ruach raah rests upon them; but onto herbs it is permitted, for ruach raah rests only on actual ground without interruption of anything else — not so these waters, for ruach raah rests upon them from the hands; therefore it is forbidden even onto herbs, lest a person walk on those herbs:
