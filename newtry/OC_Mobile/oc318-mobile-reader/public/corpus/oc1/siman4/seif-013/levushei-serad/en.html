S.k. 7 See siman 181, seif 2. That is, there he wrote that final waters are not poured on the ground because an evil spirit rests on them, while on chips of wood it is permitted, for the evil spirit rests only on the ground itself without any intervening object. Here, however, the evil spirit rests on these waters from the hands, and therefore it is forbidden even on chips of wood, because a person may walk over those chips.
