S.k. 13, spirit, etc. Rather, the doubt is because of evil spirit. That is, the doubt is whether sleep causes the evil spirit, so once he washed after rising from sleep, even though it was night at the time of washing, the evil spirit no longer rests again; or whether night itself without sleep causes the evil spirit, so he needs a second washing when day becomes light. Therefore even if he slept again, in which case the evil spirit certainly rested again, for whether night causes it or sleep causes it, both night and sleep are present, he needs washing, but he does not bless, because no blessing is required for evil spirit.
