(Note 13) Spirit, etc. Rather, the doubt is on account of ruach raah — namely, the doubt is whether sleep causes ruach raah; if so, since he already washed after rising from sleep and did not sleep again, even though it was night at the time of netilat yadayim, nevertheless ruach raah does not rest again; or whether night without sleep causes ruach raah and a second washing is needed when day breaks. Therefore, even if he slept again — certainly ruach raah rests again, for whether night causes it or sleep causes it, there are two factors — night and sleep — and washing is required; but he does not bless, for for ruach raah one need not bless.
