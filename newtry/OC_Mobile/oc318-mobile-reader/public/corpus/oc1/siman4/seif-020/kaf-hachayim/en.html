20) There — he shall not touch before netilah, etc. One must be very careful not to touch any food or drink before netilah lest defile them; warn women especially, for most food preparation is by them. Razah in Maaseh Torah letter 2, Machatzis HaShekel sk 7, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 2 letter 5, Amudei Shulchan letter 7; likewise before netilah of meal one must be careful — see above letter 8. Bedieved if he touched food or drink it is permitted — Chayei Adam klal 2 letter 2; Yiras Shamayim was stringent on himself — Amudei Shulchan there. Per Malbim: food touched before netilat yadayim — washing three times helps reject [impurity] — with him there.
