From the bathroom. Baer Hetev. And Mishnah Berurah wrote that these details he lists are not from one reason — rising from the bed and exiting the bathroom and washing and trimming nails is because of ruach ra'ah; nevertheless specifically rising from bed requires three times onto a vessel, but not the others, because ruach ra'ah does not have such dominion in those. And one who walks among the dead and touches a corpse requires three times; and likewise attending a sickbed because of impurity. But one who removes his shoes and touches his head — this is because of cleanliness; and so Maharach Cohen in Mekor Chayim — see there at length. And upon exiting the bathroom we see elderly rabbis washed three times and washed once if they removed leather shoes — see there. And I saw one who wrote it seems one who touches a bedbug need not netilat yadayim, because it does not come from human contamination; and siman 316.
