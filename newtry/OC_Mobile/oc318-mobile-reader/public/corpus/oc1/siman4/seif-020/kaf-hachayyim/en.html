20) There: He should not touch before washing his hands, etc. One must take great care not to touch any food or drink before washing, so as not to defile it. Women must be especially warned about this, since most food preparation is done by them. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, second edition, 2; Pri Megadim, Mishbetzos Zahav 7; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch 2:5; and Amudei HaShulchan there, paragraph 7. The same precaution applies before washing after using the lavatory; see above, paragraph 8. After the fact, food or drink that was touched is permitted. Chayei Adam 2:2. A God-fearing person should be stringent for himself. Amudei HaShulchan, ibid. According to Malbim, food touched before handwashing can be remedied by rinsing it three times. Amudei HaShulchan, ibid.
