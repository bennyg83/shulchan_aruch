{Rama: Note 11} That he touched an unclean place. This was needed even though he already refuted this reason in note 1 — namely, for the matter of blessing alone, as explained there; but here in the author it seems that even to wash without a blessing there is doubt whether it is required. Therefore he needed to explain that he did not touch. The essence of the doubt is whether we say lo plug because of beriah chadashah, as written in Taz, note 9. Know that what Magen Avraham marked on the word "and to remove" is a printing error; the mark is on the word "to pray," for regarding ruach raah there is a different doubt — namely, whether ruach raah is due to sleep, even during the day, or due to night, even while awake. The doubt Magen Avraham wrote here is for prayer.
