S.k. 11 That he touched a soiled place. He needed this although he already rejected this reason in s.k. 1, for there that was only regarding the blessing. Here, however, the Mechaber implies that even to wash without a blessing there is doubt whether it is necessary, so he had to explain that we are not concerned that he touched. The main doubt is whether we say the Sages did not differentiate because he became a new creation, as in Taz s.k. 9. Know that what Magen Avraham marked on the word and to remove is a scribal error; the mark belongs on the word to pray. Regarding evil spirit the doubt is different, namely whether the evil spirit comes because of sleep, even by day, or because of night, even when awake. The doubt Magen Avraham wrote here concerns prayer.
