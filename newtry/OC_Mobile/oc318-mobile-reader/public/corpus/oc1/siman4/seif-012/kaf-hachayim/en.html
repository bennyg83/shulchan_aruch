12) [Seif 2] He should be careful to pour water on them three times, etc. Our Rabbi Ari z"l wrote in Shaar HaKavanos: order of morning netilat yadayim — first take the water vessel in right hand, give to left, pour from left to right, then from right to left — once; repeat twice and thrice, washing each hand three times, not both hands together three times each hand but once on this hand and once on the other alternately, for thereby spirit of impurity called Shivta Bat Malka is scrupulous and leaps until completely removed; otherwise not removed — as in Zohar on writing of hand — end quote. So Pe'as HaChokhmah there ch. 3, Machatzis HaShekel page 3, Orchos Tzadikim page 212. Beis Yosef laws of netilah letter 4, Ben Avraham there letter 1 — unlike Seder HaYom whose words Knesset HaGedolah brought in Hagahos Tosefes and other acharonim from Zohar: pour three consecutive times — see there. However one who wishes to be stringent may first pour three consecutive times, then pour twice more on each hand alternately, as Zachor LeChaim ch. 1 letter nun, Chasam Sofer, Rav Eretz HaChaim — Rav Yaffe Lev brought (n.r.) ch. 1 letter 7; better to pour three consecutive and three alternating, as Yaavetz in his siddur Amudei Shamayim and Petach HaDevir; Maharachaf agreed in Kaf HaChaim siman 8 end letter 22; Hashem Yaffe Lev agreed there — see there. Primary is Ari z"l: since he poured three times leaping on each hand, impurity is uprooted and gone — permitted to bless before drying as above letter 8 — see there. Reason water leads impurity: impurity is from externals which are harsh gevuros as Ari explained; water is chasadim — therefore chasadim come and nullify gevuros.
