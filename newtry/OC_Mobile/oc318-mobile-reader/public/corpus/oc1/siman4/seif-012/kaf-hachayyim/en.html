12) [Se'if 2] He should take care to pour water upon them three times, etc. The Arizal writes in Shaar HaKavanos: 'The order of morning handwashing is as follows. First, he takes the vessel of water in his right hand and transfers it to his left. He then pours water from his left hand upon his right, and afterward from his right hand upon his left. This is one time. He repeats this order a second and third time, so that each hand is washed three times. He should not wash one hand three consecutive times and then the other, but once upon this hand and once upon the other, alternately. Through this, the spirit of impurity called Shivta, the king's daughter, which is fastidious, skips and leaps until it is entirely uprooted. Otherwise, it is not uprooted. This is also found in a manuscript of Zohar.' Pri Etz Chaim there, chapter 3; Matsas Shimurim, page 3; Olas Tamid, page 17; Siddur Beis Yaakov, laws of handwashing 4; and Ben Ish Chai, ibid. 1, write likewise. This is unlike Seder HaYom, cited by Knesses HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur and other later authorities, who writes in Zohar's name that one should pour three consecutive times, etc.; see there. One who wishes to be stringent should first pour three consecutive times and then pour two more times upon each hand alternately, as Zechor LeAvraham 1, entry nun, writes. Chesed LaAlafim and Artzos HaChaim agree, as cited by Yafeh LaLev 1:7. It is even better to pour three consecutive times and then three additional alternating times, as Yaavetz writes in Siddur Amudei Shamayim and as Pischei HaDevir rules. Rav Chaim Palagi agrees in Kaf HaChaim 18, end of paragraph 22, as does Yafeh LaLev there; see there. The primary practice, however, follows the Arizal: after one pours three alternating times upon each hand, the impurity is uprooted and gone. He may therefore recite the berachah before drying, as stated above in paragraph 8. Water carries away the impurity because impurity derives from the external forces, which are severe gevurot, as explained by the Arizal, whereas water represents chasadim. The chasadim therefore come and nullify the gevurot.
