“And for prayer.” And he blesses netilas yadayim, as stated in siman 22 and explained there.

“In three seas.” The same applies to a river: the first “sea” they used for themselves and these others are different bodies—see Chullin 25b and Eruvin 46b. It is obvious that forty seah are not required, no worse than a pebble in siman 22; see siman 159, not like Eliyah Rabbah, and siman 160:7.
