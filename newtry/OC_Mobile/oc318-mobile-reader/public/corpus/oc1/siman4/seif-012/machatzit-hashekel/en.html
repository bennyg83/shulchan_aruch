S.k. 12, without a blessing. The Bach wrote that he should bless because the Sages did not differentiate. That is, the Beis Yosef wrote that according to the Rashba's reason, that one becomes a new creation, if he was awake, although he did not become a new creation, perhaps because the Sages did not differentiate he requires washing and a blessing. By contrast, according to the Rosh's reason, where he was awake and did not touch, they certainly did not institute it. In my humble opinion, the reason to distinguish is this: according to the Rashba, morning washing is a new enactment because one became a new creation, so perhaps even if he did not become a new creation he needs washing and a blessing because the Sages did not differentiate. But according to the Rosh, the concern is that he may have touched a soiled place; this is not a special morning enactment, for even at Minchah, if he relieved himself, he requires washing and a blessing. If so, why should morning, even when he was awake and did not touch, require washing and a blessing because the Sages did not differentiate? Then all day, even for Minchah when he did not relieve himself, should require washing and a blessing, which is certainly not so. The Bach disagreed, for necessarily according to the Rosh too the rule that the Sages did not differentiate requires washing and a blessing, since the Rosh was asked about one who rises before dawn to learn whether he needs washing and a blessing, and he answered that he does. If so, the words of the Rosh seem contradictory, for the Rosh holds that if one relieved himself and then wishes to learn, he does not need a blessing. Necessarily, what he wrote about rising to learn is not precise, but that was the incident, and the same applies if he rose to do his work; he holds that he needs washing and a blessing because if he rose and wished to pray immediately he would need washing and a blessing for the prayer. Therefore even if he rose and does not yet wish to pray, he still needs washing and a blessing because the Sages did not differentiate. Once we say they did not differentiate, even if he was awake and certainly did not touch a soiled place, he still needs washing and a blessing. Nevertheless this is not such a proof from the Rosh, meaning that according to the Rosh one can always say there is no rule of not differentiating, and that when he ruled for one who arose to learn that he blesses, the reason is not learning, as the Bach said it was not precise, but because eventually he will pray and will then need to bless. Therefore he blesses immediately now. But if he was awake, even when he prays he does not bless, for he certainly did not touch and there is no doubt. The Beis Yosef was uncertain whether he should bless because for us the main reason is the Rashba's, as written in s.k. 1, that he became a new creation; although this applies only where he slept, nevertheless washing and a blessing are required because the Sages did not differentiate.
