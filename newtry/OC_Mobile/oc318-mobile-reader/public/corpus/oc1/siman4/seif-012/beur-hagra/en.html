Seif 12, "if he rubbed them," etc. This is the language of the Rashba there: he should move his hands inside the vessel, and that is acceptable both in the morning and for eating, since we say regarding the laver: "from it" and not "inside it"; but generally even inside it is valid. This follows the Behag's view in Tosafos, chapter 25, 107a, gloss beginning "delo"; see siman 159, seif 8. But the Rada wrote that here too human force is required, as above in seif 7 in the gloss; see there.

"But not," etc. For the first time the water became impure and like waste water, and three times are required. This is what he wrote, "and if he moved," etc., for the Gemara implies that this is enough, as it says: she is particular until he washes, etc.; but in the Zohar there it says that pouring from a vessel is required.
