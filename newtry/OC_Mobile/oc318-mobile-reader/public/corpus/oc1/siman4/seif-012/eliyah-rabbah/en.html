But not for the spirit, etc. Even if he shook his hands three times in those waters. However, one who immerses his hands three times in water gathered in the ground, this helps even to remove the spirit, and one cannot say that water gathered in the ground becomes impure because of the hands. If they do not have the measure of a mikveh, however, there is room for doubt. In pressing circumstances, where he has no water prepared before him, he should insert his hands into snow lying on the ground in three different places; so wrote Olas Tamid. From the words of the Beis Yosef it implies that he is uncertain in the words of the Zohar whether pouring specifically from a vessel is required; if so, none of this is permitted. So too it implies in Rama, folio 326, and Lechem Chamudos, folio 74. Afterward the Magen Avraham was printed and wrote: it is obvious that forty se'ah are not required, for it is no worse than a pebble in seif 22, unlike Olas Tamid. This is not correct, for a pebble helps for cleanliness, whereas Olas Tamid is speaking of a case where the waters should not become impure from the hands.
