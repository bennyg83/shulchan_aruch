[12] In water, etc. Specifically water and not mere cleaning. Seder HaYom wrote one need not pour three times except when rising from bed. Rama wrote one must be careful lekhatchilah with all stringencies customary for netilat yadayim for eating in all those matters, and not like those who wash little by little. However bedieved it does not hinder — end quote. But in Makor Chaim he wrote: one leaving the privy — even if evil spirit dwells on him — nevertheless it is not Bat Malka and does not need a vessel; likewise one walking among the dead needs three times but no vessel; likewise one who uses his bed. But one leaving the bath and trimming his nails need not wash three times. In Magen Avraham siman 7 he leans that also for the privy we do not require three times — so too in siman 165. Seder HaYom wrote: one must wash his hands with all possible haste to remove from himself the force of that impurity and depart from it. See below siman 200 on these laws.
