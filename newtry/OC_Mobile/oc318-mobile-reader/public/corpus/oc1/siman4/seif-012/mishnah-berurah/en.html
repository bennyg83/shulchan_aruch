(25) For Keri'at Shema and for prayer — and he may recite the blessing on netilat yadayim, for we say [they wash] in a basin and "they washed from it" — not in it — though in general even in it [is valid]; but to remove ruach ra'ah one specifically requires pouring three times, and rubbing in a vessel even one hundred times is not counted but as one, since immediately the water became impure and are like poured-out water. Therefore they were uncertain afterward about alternating waters — it is possible that we do not specifically require pouring to remove ruach ra'ah and even washing suffices, or that we specifically require pouring from a vessel onto the hands. And the Peri Megadim wrote that accordingly, if one dips his hand in a river three times or in snow placed on the ground in three alternating places, even so it is effective only for prayer and he may recite "Al Netilat Yadayim," but there is doubt whether it is effective to remove ruach ra'ah, for perhaps for this we require pouring from a vessel; and the Eliyah Rabbah and the Levushim Serad ruled that in a river, likewise in snow, and in mikveh water it is effective even to remove ruach ra'ah even if they do not contain forty se'ah — see there further in the Eliyah Rabbah:
