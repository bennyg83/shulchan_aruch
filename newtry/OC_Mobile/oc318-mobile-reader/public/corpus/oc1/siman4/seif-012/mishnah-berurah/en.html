For Krias Shema and prayer. He may recite the blessing of al netilas yadayim, for regarding the laver we say, 'and they shall wash from it,' and not inside it; but elsewhere, even inside it is acceptable. However, to remove ruach ra'ah, one specifically needs pouring three times, and swishing in a vessel, even one hundred times, is considered only once, for the water immediately becomes impure and is like wastewater. For this reason he later expressed doubt about changed waters, for perhaps pouring is not specifically required to remove ruach ra'ah, and even washing suffices, or perhaps one specifically needs pouring from a vessel onto his hands. The Peri Megadim wrote that according to this, if one inserted his hand into a river three times, or into snow lying on the ground in three different places, even so it helps only for prayer, and he may recite al netilas yadayim, but it is doubtful whether it helps to remove ruach ra'ah, for perhaps pouring from a vessel is required for this. The Artzos HaChaim and Levushei Serad ruled that a river, and likewise snow and mikveh water, help even to remove ruach ra'ah, even if they do not contain forty se'ah.
