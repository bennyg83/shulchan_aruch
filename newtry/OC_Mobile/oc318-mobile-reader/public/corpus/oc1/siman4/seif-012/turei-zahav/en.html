For Krias Shema and prayer. From the wording concerning the laver, "They shall wash from it," rather than "in it," we infer that immersion in other water is effective. To remove the evil spirit, however, one needs three pourings. Swishing the hands in the same water even one hundred times counts as only one washing, because the water immediately becomes impure and is considered wastewater. I later had some doubt whether separate waters are required, since perhaps pouring is not essential and washing alone suffices; however, the Zohar implies that a vessel is required.
