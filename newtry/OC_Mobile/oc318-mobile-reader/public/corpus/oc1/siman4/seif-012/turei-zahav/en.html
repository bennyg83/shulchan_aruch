For Keriat Shema and for prayer for we say in [the verse about] the basin "and they washed from it" — not in it — thus in general even in it [is valid]; but for ruach ra'ah one specifically requires pouring three times, and rubbing in a vessel even one hundred times is not counted but as one, since immediately the water became impure and are like poured-out water. Therefore they were uncertain afterward about alternating waters — it is possible that we do not specifically require pouring to remove ruach ra'ah and even washing suffices, except that from the words of the Zohar it appears that a vessel is required:
