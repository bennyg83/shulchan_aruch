And for prayer. He should bless as I wrote in se'if 22; see what I wrote there:

Evil. For an evil spirit requires pouring three times specifically; and rubbing in a vessel, even one hundred times, counts only as one, for the water immediately becomes impure. Therefore he later was uncertain about alternating waters: perhaps pouring specifically is not required to remove an evil spirit and even rinsing suffices, or perhaps we require pouring specifically from a vessel:

Alternating. The same applies in a river: the first and first have passed and these are others. In my view the same applies in a mikveh, for the reason is that the water immediately becomes impure — and this does not apply in a mikveh.
