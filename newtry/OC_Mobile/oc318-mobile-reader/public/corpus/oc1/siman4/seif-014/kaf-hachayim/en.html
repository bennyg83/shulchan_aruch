14) One must wash all fingers and palm to the wrist joint — Pe'as HaChokhmah Shaar Berachos ch. 3, Orchos Tzadikim page 18b, Ben Avraham there letter 2. What helps on Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av only to finger joints — Mahari Tzemach in Orchos Tzadikim page 17b: on Yom Kippur it does not rule; on 9 Av it is not scrupulous about such a small matter, for its whole day is in our sins — they brought Ben Avraham in that letter.
