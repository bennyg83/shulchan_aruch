14) One must wash all the fingers and the palm through the wrist. Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaBerachos, chapter 3; Olas Tamid, page 18b; Ben Ish Chai, ibid. 2. Regarding why washing through the finger joints suffices on Yom Kippur and Tishah BeAv, Mahari Tzemach writes in Olas Tamid, page 17b, that the evil spirit does not exercise dominion on Yom Kippur, while on Tishah BeAv it is not particular about so small a matter, because, due to our sins, the entire day belongs to it. Ben Ish Chai cites this in the aforementioned paragraph.
