Evil spirit. Here he did not mention prayer, as stated in se'if 13: certainly, since he sanctified his hands once, he need not sanctify them again even if he slept again, for the first time a new creation came about — and not for that reason alone; rather the reason is because of an evil spirit. Therefore even if he sleeps again he need not bless, for an evil spirit does not require a blessing. Magen Avraham.
