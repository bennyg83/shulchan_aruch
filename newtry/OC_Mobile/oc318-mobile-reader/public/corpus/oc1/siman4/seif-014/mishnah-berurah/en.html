(31) When day breaks — because some say that at dawn ruach ra'ah returns to dwell a second time:

(32) To remove, etc. — here he did not mention prayer, as written in seif 13, for certainly regarding prayer there is no need here again to wash hands; and even if he returned and slept a second time before dawn — in this case he certainly needs again to wash hands because of ruach ra'ah; nevertheless he cannot recite "Al Netilat Yadayim," even if the sleep was substantial sleep, because we are concerned for the view of the Rashba who holds that the reason for netilah is because in the morning one becomes a new creature, and "in the morning" man became a new creature only once and not twice; and for ruach ra'ah alone they did not enact a blessing:

(33) Without a blessing — here it appears that even if the first time when he washed his hands he did not recite "Al Netilat Yadayim," nevertheless he should not recite now, for how can he say "and commanded us regarding washing of the hands" — has he not already fulfilled the obligation of netilah for prayer per the Rosh, as explained in Beit Yosef; and similarly wrote above in Sha'arei Teshuva siman katan 4; nevertheless it is proper that he bring himself to obligation of blessing — that is, he should relieve himself before prayer and become obligated to recite "Al Netilat Yadayim" — and similarly in siman katan 4 in the name of Chayei Adam:
