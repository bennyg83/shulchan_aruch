When the day becomes light. For some say that at dawn the ruach ra'ah returns to rest a second time.
To remove, etc. Here he did not mention prayer, as he wrote in seif 13, because certainly for prayer he need not wash his hands again in this case. Even if he went back to sleep a second time before dawn, in which case he certainly must wash his hands again because of ruach ra'ah, nevertheless he cannot recite al netilas yadayim, even if the sleep was a fixed sleep, for we are concerned for the view of the Rashba, who holds that the reason for washing is that in the morning one becomes a new creation, and a person becomes a new creation once, not twice. For ruach ra'ah alone they did not institute a blessing.
Without a blessing. It appears that even if the first time he washed his hands he did not recite al netilas yadayim, nevertheless he should not bless now, for how can he say 'Who commanded us concerning netilas yadayim,' when he has already fulfilled his obligation of washing for prayer according to the Rosh, as explained in the Beit Yosef. The Shaarei Teshuvah wrote similarly above in seif katan 4. Nevertheless, the proper course is to bring himself into an obligation of blessing, namely by relieving himself before prayer, so that he will be obligated to recite al netilas yadayim, as above in seif katan 4 in the name of the Chayei Adam.
