And wash them without a blessing — see Mishna Berurah sk 33 in what he wrote: the proper practice is to bring himself to obligation and not trouble the inquirer — for at first glance it contradicts what we wrote in sk 32, and that is from Magen Avraham's words that one must consider the Rashba's view regarding one who slept a fixed sleep, and consequently the same applies when he relieved himself, for they share one law, as proven in Eliyah Rabbah sk 9. It is not similar to the agreement of the later authorities in sk 30, for there the case is where he had not yet washed his hands at all — not so here. However, in truth it is satisfactory: for here we address the case where he did not bless the first time; in this one can say that even per the Rashba we may bless now, as the Beis Yosef wrote in siman 7, and the blessing applies to the first washing — we already expanded on this in Biur Halacha above at the beginning of the siman — see there. Know further that what is written in the gloss here "and wash them without a blessing," and similarly in seif 15 — is not similar to what he wrote in seif 13, for there it is only because of doubt; here according to all views he need not bless [later authorities].
