18) [Seif 3] He shall not touch his hand before netilah to mouth or nose, etc. Maor U'Ketz wrote: this stringency of touching before netilah to mouth and eyes is specifically not to touch the inner part of these limbs; outside is permitted — end quote. So Prisha siman 46 letter 5; so Eliyah Rabbah there. However Chida z"l in Machazik Berachah here letter 3 wrote their words are not clear — certainly inside and outside are the same; so Zohar and Alpasi Zuta end of Berachos to Rama from Pano and Rav from Edam there and Perach siman 46 — see there; Maamarim siman 46 letter 3 agreed; so Shalmei Tzibbur 18a and Shaarei Teshuvah siman 46 letter 3 in that name.

18) And if itch comes in any of the above during sleep, rub through a garment. Also do not inhale nostril dust before netilah; if desire is very strong, rise, wash hands, then inhale. But daytime sleep or sleep after midnight — no need to be careful in all the above, although we wrote he washes three times. Ben Avraham there letter 15.
