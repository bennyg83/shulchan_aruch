These require washing with water: one who rises from bed; one who leaves a privy or a bathhouse; one who trims his nails; one who removes his shoe; one who touches his feet; one who shampoos his head; some say also one who walks among the dead; one who touches a corpse; one who adjusts his clothing; one who has marital relations; one who touches the bedframe; one who touches his body with his hand. Whoever did one of these and did not wash — if he is a Torah scholar, his learning is forgotten; if not a Torah scholar, he loses his mind.
