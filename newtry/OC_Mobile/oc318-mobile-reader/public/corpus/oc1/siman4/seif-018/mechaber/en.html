These require netilas yadayim with water: one who rises from bed; one who leaves the privy; one who leaves the bathhouse; one who trims his nails; one who removes his shoe; one who touches his feet; one who shampoos his head; and some say also one who walks among the dead; one who touches a corpse; one who searches. his garments; one who has marital relations; one who touches the bedframe; one who touches his body with his hand. Whoever did. one of these and did not wash—if he is a Torah scholar, his learning is forgotten; if he is not a Torah scholar, he loses his mind.
