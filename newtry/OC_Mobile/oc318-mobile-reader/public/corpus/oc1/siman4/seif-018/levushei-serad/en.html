S.k. 10 The first water passes away. That is, a flowing river. One might have thought that when he shakes his hands the first time, even the water above becomes impure, since it is all one connection. Therefore he says that because it flows, the upper water does not become impure, as implied in Chullin 105 regarding until the harmful waters pass, and in Avodah Zarah regarding one who bows to a river. Know that Magen Avraham speaks of a river that does not have enough to purify a person from his impurity, for otherwise the water would not become impure at all, as will be explained later, with the help of Heaven.
