{Rama: Note 10.} "The first passes and the next comes" — namely, a flowing river; and the reason is that when one rinses the first time, even what is above becomes defiled, since all is one connection; therefore he says that since it flows, what is above is not defiled — as implied in Chullin 105 regarding "until the bad waters pass," and in one version regarding one who bows in a river.
