See Yad Ephraim, where it appears that Ba'er Heitev emended the words of Magen Avraham, that seif katan 3 and seif katan 4 in Magen Avraham are one seif katan, and he aimed well, unlike those who emend in another way; see there. Shalmei Tzibbur wrote regarding water in barrels in which liquor was produced, or rosewater that flows through the pipe from the boiler, while the water inside the barrel cools the pipe, which is like the production of liquor in our country: according to what Rama wrote, that even in the morning one should not bless over water whose appearance changed or with which work was done, and work was done with this water, as explained in siman 160, therefore he should wash with it if he has no other water, but should not bless al netilat yadayim; see there. See below seif katan 5, where the Acharonim hold that with water with which work was done, he should wash and also bless. If so, in pressing circumstances, where he has no other water except this flowing water, he may wash and bless over it as well. See below seif katan 7. See there in Shiyurei Berakhah, who wrote that one may wash his hands in pressing circumstances with water left over from household utensils, but should not rinse his mouth with it because it is repulsive; see there. See siman 160: water with which unwashed vessels were rinsed is invalid for netilat yadayim. If so, according to Rama he should wash and not bless. There in siman 160 it is explained that Bach and other Acharonim are stringent regarding netilat yadayim for a meal with water from which a dog or pig drank, for it is like wastewater; one should be stringent here too not to wash with it in the morning except in pressing circumstances. Regarding the blessing, one who follows Rama should not bless in this case either. See there further several distinctions in law that can be learned from there to here.
