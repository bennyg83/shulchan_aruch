3) If he rose from bed and went to a nearby netilah vessel and did not find enough water to pour three times on each hand — to the joint — he takes a little water to the knuckles so he does not go four amot without washing hands, similar to Yom Kippur netilah: poskim agree that although on Yom Kippur one washes only to the knuckles, it is called netilah and one blesses al netilas yadayim. One may be lenient for travelers similarly when water is scarce — Shaarei Tziyyon p. 18a, Shaarei Teshuvah letter 6, Ekrei HaDat siman 1 letter 12; so Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 2 letter 3 in pressing need until knuckles. See Razah letter 7: only if water is entirely unavailable may he bless on this washing even from afar; but if water is available elsewhere and to avoid going four amot without washing he washed only to knuckles, he waits until he reaches the water and washes to the joint and blesses.
