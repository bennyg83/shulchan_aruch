[3] That use of the hand spoils, etc. So Beis Yosef explained Gemara's words: hand for bloodletting — hand for barrel is cut off. It is not clear, since it is only slight loss we do not find a leniency for loss — therefore Bach and Taz explained it deals specifically with touching before morning netilat yadayim; and because of danger when they soak afterward the beer harms him. Hand for bloodletting — reason: evil spirit enters the body through the orifice and harms him. According to this one must also explain Gemara: hand to arm is cut off. So I found in summary of Rosh's rulings, chapter Shemonah Sheratzim — this is his language: hand to eye and to arm and to mouth and to nose in the morning before he washes his hands is cut off — end quote. From this I wonder on Beis Yosef who wrote Tur explained hand to arm is cut off because of keri — therefore they omitted here in Shulchan Aruch the law of hand to arm, and he did not heed my brought words of Rosh's summary. One need not challenge: for in truth without evil spirit reason it would be cut off because of keri — one can say here it deals from the belt downward where there is no prohibition of keri, as above siman 3 seif 14; so Agudah implies there. Or here it deals even if married.
