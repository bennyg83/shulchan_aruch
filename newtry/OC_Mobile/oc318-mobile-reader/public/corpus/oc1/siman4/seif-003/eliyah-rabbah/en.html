That touching with the hand causes loss, etc. So the Beis Yosef explained the words of the Gemara: a hand to bloodletting, a hand to a vat, should be cut off. This is not clear, for where the matter is only monetary loss we do not find such a curse over loss. Therefore the Bach and Taz explained that it speaks specifically where he touches before the morning washing of the hands, and it is a danger because when they later soak the beer it harms him. Regarding bloodletting, the reason is that the evil spirit enters the body through the opening and harms him. On this basis one can also explain the Gemara's statement, a hand to the member should be cut off. So too I found in the abridged rulings of the Rosh, chapter Shemonah Sheratzim: a hand to the eye, to the member, to the mouth, and to the nose in the morning before he washes his hands should be cut off. This is a wonder against the Beis Yosef, who wrote that the Tur explained a hand to the member should be cut off because of emission, and therefore omitted here in Shulchan Aruch the law of a hand to the member, without noting the abridged rulings of the Rosh that I brought. One should not ask that even without the reason of evil spirit it should be cut off because of emission, for one may say that here it refers from the crown downward, where there is no prohibition because of emission as above in siman 3, seif 14, and so it implies in the Agudah there. Alternatively, here it refers even to a married man.
