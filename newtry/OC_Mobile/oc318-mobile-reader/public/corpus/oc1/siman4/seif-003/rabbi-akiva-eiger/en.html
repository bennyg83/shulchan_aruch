{Rama: Magen Avraham, seif kaf yud bet} But for us — the main reason [for netilah is ruach ra'ah]. This is not proven. [See Taz in our siman 7.] And one can also say that we are in doubt whether like the Rashba or like the Rosh — therefore one does not bless when he relieves himself and prays Minchah — lest it is like the Rashba and one does not bless out of doubt. And if so, here regarding one awake all night — Beit Yosef is in doubt whether we say "we do not distinguish" — one can say: if he relieved himself, he blesses, for it is a safeik safeika (double doubt): perhaps like the Rosh; and perhaps even for the Rashba he blesses because of lefi peshuto.
