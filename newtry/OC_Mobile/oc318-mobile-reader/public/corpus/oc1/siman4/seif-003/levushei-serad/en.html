{Rama: There} And although netilat yadayim, etc. — and in truth it is not needed here, etc. This refers to the opening of his words, namely the view of the Rosh; but according to Rashba, in truth a vessel is also needed here, similar to a kohen from the basin, as explained in Beit Yosef; but Taz holds the main view is like the Rosh, therefore he wrote these words.
