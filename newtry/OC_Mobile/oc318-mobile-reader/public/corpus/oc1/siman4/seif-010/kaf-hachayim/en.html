10) There. Water invalid for netilat yadayim for a meal (gloss below siman 105) is valid for netilat yadayim for prayer. However "some say," etc. Magen Avraham sk 3 wrote: Rama's intent is specifically water whose appearance changed or work was done with it mentioned in siman 105; but vessel and human force do not hinder and one blesses as in seif 7 — end quote (so per Hagahos Be'er Heitiv sk 3, Yad Ephraim, Levush). But Yeshuos Yaakov sk 2 wondered on Magen Avraham — he did not know whence he derived that one blesses; again he found in Derishah that one should not bless — see there; also Maamarim sk 2 rejected Magen Avraham: whoever is stringent on vessel and human force is stringent on invalid water; whoever is not stringent on one is not on the other — meaning of poskim brought in Beis Yosef; so explicitly from Moram z"l in Darkei Moshe, etc.; so Levush implies — end quote. This is primary, for we hold one may bless even against Maran and even against majority, as Zachor LeChaim wrote in Laws of Berachos letter 60 and Chayei Adam letter 60.
