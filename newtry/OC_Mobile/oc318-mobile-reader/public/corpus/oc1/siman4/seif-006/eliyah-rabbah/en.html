[6] He shall not take from one who did not take, etc. As written: "the pure shall sprinkle on the impure." If you say: how does the right receive from the left when it is impure? One can say: here he wishes to transfer impurity from himself and cannot otherwise. But a person not yet purified who desires his impurity lacks power to transfer another impurity until he purifies — so Seder HaYom wrote. He also wrote: no stringency here except specifically morning netilat yadayim because of spirit of impurity; but rest of day in other washings one need not be stringent — whoever is stringent is only among the astonished — end quote; so Shelah page 194 and Knesset HaGedolah wrote. Therefore I wonder on Taz who wrote this language and it seems to him all the more for eating netilah the main obligation is impurity of terumah touch — end quote. He did not speak of the great ones mentioned above. So in Abudraham and Tashbetz siman 177 it implies permitted; so below siman 159 seifim 11–12. See Agudah end of tractate Derech Eretz.
