6) There — and he blesses al netilas yadayim. Because hands are busy, it is impossible that at night they did not touch unclean flesh — therefore they instituted blessing before Shema and prayer; so Rosh. But Rashba gave a reason: in the morning one becomes like a new creature, as it says "new every morning," and one must thank God and need purity for this service like a priest from the laver — therefore they instituted netilah at that time — Beis Yosef, Prisha letter 1, Taz sk 1. But for Chayei Adam's reason, morning netilah is because of Shivta and Bat Melech to remove evil spirit of song at night on the hands — from Gemara Shabbos 109a. For Zohar's reason: one who sleeps tastes taste of death in sixty breaths, and when he awakens the sitra achra sticks to the hands — one should not bless al netilas yadayim because of danger, and one does not bless only on mitzvos — Peri Megadim, Orach Chaim letter 1. From Rosh's reasons one need not require a vessel by law, and invalid water for meal netilah is valid here; for Minchah too if hands were soiled one blesses al netilas yadayim. For Rashba one specifically needs vessel and human force and all matters required for meal netilah; for Minchah prayer one need not bless al netilas yadayim. Brief waking at night follows Chayei Adam's reason, not Zohar; daytime sleep of sixty breaths follows Zohar alone — Orach Chaim there. See Mishbetzos Zahav letter 1; further nuance: if he slept with hands bound and knows certainly he did not touch an unclean place — for Rosh he does not bless, for Rashba he blesses — Orach Chaim there, Mishbetzos Zahav there.
