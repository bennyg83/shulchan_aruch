In order. See Taz. The Levush wrote that the attribute of judgment is subdued, as at the Binding of Yitzchak. Another reason is that the right is important and the left is like a servant serving the master. According to this reason, even for washing hands for a meal it is proper to give water from the left onto the right first; with the help of Heaven this will also be explained in the laws of washing hands for a meal. See siman 2 regarding shoes without tying.
