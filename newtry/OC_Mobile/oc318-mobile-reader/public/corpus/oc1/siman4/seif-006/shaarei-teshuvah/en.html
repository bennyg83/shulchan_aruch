Three times. See Baer Heitev, who writes that one must wash as far as the wrist. Shalmei Tzibbur writes that if only a small amount of water was prepared, insufficient to wash each hand three times to the wrist, he may wash only to the finger joints, as on Yom Kippur, and recite Al Netilat Yadayim. Travelers may likewise be lenient when water is scarce. See Eliyah Rabbah, siman 4, se'if katan 5, and siman 128, se'if katan 8.
