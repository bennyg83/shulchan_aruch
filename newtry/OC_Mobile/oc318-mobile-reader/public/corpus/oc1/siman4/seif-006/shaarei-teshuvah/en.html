Three times. Baer Hetev — that one must wash until the joint of the arm. And Shlemei Tzibbur wrote if they prepared for him only a little that is not the full measure to wash until the joint three times — each hand can wash only until the knuckles of his fingers, like Yom Kippur, and bless Al Netilat Yadayim; and likewise one may be lenient for travelers when water is scarce.
