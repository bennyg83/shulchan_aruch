{Rama: There} In order to escape the doubt. It is puzzling what harm this doubt suffers if he blesses al netilat yadayim before his needs. One may say that per the writers one blesses al netilat yadayim, and with the blessing of Asher Yatzar he is permitted to wait until he attends to his needs; therefore he wrote that he should not wait, but is obligated to bless immediately in order to escape the doubt, and therefore it is fitting to attend to his needs immediately. Alternatively, Magen Avraham would bless al netilat yadayim first — the writers did not say to bless it first except so that afterward he is permitted to wait. Alternatively, Magen Avraham holds that the blessing of al netilat yadayim is an interruption, and he does not follow those who obligate blessing before his needs — the first [explanation] is primary.
