Ibid. In order to escape the doubt. It is surprising: how does it harm this doubt if he blesses over washing the hands before relieving himself? One may say that according to the writings he blesses over washing the hands, while with Asher Yatzar he may wait until he relieves himself. Therefore he wrote that he should not wait, but is obligated to recite Asher Yatzar immediately to escape the doubt, and therefore it is proper to relieve himself immediately. If so, according to Magen Avraham he should bless over washing the hands beforehand, for the writings said to bless over washing the hands first only so that he may then wait. Alternatively, Magen Avraham holds that the blessing over washing the hands is an interruption and that he does not satisfy the view requiring Asher Yatzar without relieving himself. The first answer is primary.
