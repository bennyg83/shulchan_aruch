15) After netilah three times alternately one must rub hands together, explicit in Ari z"l in Pe'as HaChokhmah there, Orchos Tzadikim page 19a. Although his words' closure seems once suffices, we find Rashash z"l requires rubbing three times with kavanos each time — therefore be careful to rub three times. Ben Avraham there letter 3; see Nefesh HaChaim page 80a. Ben Avraham wrote: during rubbing be careful fingers are upright upward, not bent. Intention of netilah and rubbing — see below siman 162 letter 2.
