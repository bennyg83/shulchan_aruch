From sweat friction — Eshel Avraham: and specifically the covered places on a person. The lay author writing said: because the ways of wisdom have been hidden from me, I said I would briefly investigate several inquiries belonging to this siman and other simanim. Six netilot are for added sanctity: meal, shacharit, mincha, Keriat Shema, words of Torah, dipping food in liquid. Netilat yadayim for a meal is explained in siman 159 and 165 — things that prevent and things fitting lechatchila — see there. Morning netilah lechatchila one should beware all hindrances to netilat yadayim for a meal, as the Mechaber wrote here se'if 7; and what he wrote about hindrances — one can say that what there is lechatchila (as in 159:8 and 165:13) is not required for prayer, unlike rubbing in a vessel which Rashba requires lechatchila here and there se'if 14; and intention — one can say here intention is not required, as we need to say below, im yirtzeh Hashem. Know that if we say rabbinic mitzvot also require intention, then slaughter of chullin which needs no intention before rabbinical court, and netilat yadayim for chullin in 159:13 needs no intention, and a niddah to her husband needs no intention even though they are deoraita — necessarily one must say every mitzvah from which one cannot be exempt requires the doer's intention; unlike slaughter, immersion, etc., where one does not slaughter in order to eat, immerse in order to use, etc. — for these no intention; thus morning netilat yadayim, which cannot be without netilah and prayer — one can say requires intention. However Magen Avraham siman 60:3 brought several views that rabbinic mitzvot need no intention; thus morning netilah for all opinions needs no intention, but we require water for two reasons — one for evil spirit, second for prayer we require water specifically if possible. Mincha is explained in Relev Tov — see there — if he has no water he cleans with a bundle; see se'if 7 there letter 2, and 162 letter 1 holds the Mechaber's rulings contradict each other — see there. Keriat Shema implies for all no need to search for water four mil — only for prayer must one return; see Beit Yosef 92 and 98 there — from Gemara Berachos 15a — this is Keriat Shema... the reason and difference: Keriat Shema is deoraita but prayer derabanan — see Mishneh Torah ch. 3 Keriat Shema 1 and ch. 4 Laws of Prayer 2; but according to Rambam prayer is Torah — at least prayer once — how divide Keriat Shema from prayer and not prayer itself between once and twice? See ch. 1 Laws of Prayer; and Shulchan Aruch siman 106 as Magen Avraham there — prayer deoraita (and by this one can say RLG: mincha derabanan, shacharit deoraita, maariv reshut — therefore shacharit stricter). Another reason to divide Keriat Shema: its time is brief — from here onward only like Torah reading, unlike prayer deoraita from here onward prayer reward exists — see Rashi Berachos 15a — we wrote in our novellae; therefore Keriat Shema of maariv, whose time is all night, is Torah — if past chatzot, mitzvat Keriat Shema is preferred over prayer where reward of prayer not in time — obvious must search for water. And upon this ch. 1 Keriat Shema in what Magen Avraham wrote — all day its time for Rambam — see ch. 1 Keriat Shema 13. All these — Keriat Shema, prayer, meal — even stam yadayim disqualify, as explained 93 — Keriat Shema likewise. But words of Torah: stam yadayim are valid for words of Torah, blessing, and mentioning God's name, as Magen Avraham 92:4. Also difference: words of Torah and blessings — for all, earth-cleaning suffices, no water needed, etc. Dipping in liquid explained 158:4 requires netilah without blessing; Taz there 6 implies only cleanliness, not because of terumah residue — therefore its law as other blessings with soiling: lechatchila earth-cleaning suffices, and likewise here with stam yadayim; but one who examines Beit Yosef and Levush there — many reasons because of terumah residue or liquids liable to impurity — if so, certainly earth-cleaning insufficient even for stam yadayim without water specifically, and do not bless. Two types of soiling: (1) filth in unclean places and those listed here se'if 18 — for this specifically water needed for evil spirit; though for Keriat Shema and prayer if lacking, earth suffices; and for words of Torah and blessings lechatchila also earth; (2) soiling with mud and mire — thing not disgusting, no filth — because of evil spirit, earth suffices for words of Torah and blessings lechatchila, and anything that cleans; Keriat Shema and prayer lechatchila need water, bedieved cleanliness suffices; if standing in prayer and touched — explained 92:6 — lechatchila earth-cleaning suffices. Difference if mud/soiling for words of Torah and blessings — need only that place alone, unlike those listed se'if 18 here because of evil spirit — certainly lechatchila need whole forearm to at least knuckles — see Terumat HaDeshen and Magen Avraham here letter 7 — I wrote there. It appears to me stam yadayim are invalid for prayer and one must clean the back of the hand too, not only the palm used for touching — all clean if he has water; if not, clean with earth to the knuckles. If distinction between stam yadayim and soiled for returning for water — see there 92. Know every revi'it of water on the ground, if not drawn, is valid for vessels and mikveh law applies — a fortiori a spring purifies more — see 159:14, Taz 18, Magen Avraham 26 — they accept no impurity; see Pesachim 17b — "not drawn on ground," etc. Therefore no objection to Magen Avraham (10)'s challenge on Eshel Tzafon — we wrote there — now seen the law is with him; explanation that a bundle suffices for prayer and for receiving impurity they do not receive at all and we are not concerned for evil spirit, as 159:14; also showed 165:12 snow has mikveh law — see Rambam; some hold even if not melted, some stringent; for evil spirit, if it has mikveh law no evil spirit rests — but there is concern for evil spirit even if snow is not fit to receive impurity — see Rambam 5:21 — do not extend; see Magen Avraham more, im yirtzeh Hashem:
