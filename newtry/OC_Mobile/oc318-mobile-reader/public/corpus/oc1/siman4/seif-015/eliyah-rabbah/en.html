[15] He shall dry his hands with a bundle, etc. This is Olas Tamid's language. Berachas Avraham wrote one must dry three times like water. This does not seem [right], since it does not help for evil spirit — why three times? mere cleaning suffices — end quote. He did not look except in Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah and not in Berachas Avraham itself page 49, who wrote so for the first answer that it also helps for evil spirit; but for the second answer that it does not help for evil spirit but water — we do not require three times. Lekhatchilah it seems proper to be concerned for the first answer. See above sk 2.
