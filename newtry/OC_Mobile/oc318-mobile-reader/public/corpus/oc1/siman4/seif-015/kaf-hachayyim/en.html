15) After washing three alternating times, one must rub the hands together, as stated explicitly by the Arizal in Pri Etz Chaim there. Olas Tamid, page 19a. Although the plain meaning of his words indicates that rubbing once suffices, Rabbi Rashash requires rubbing three times and gives an order of kavanos for each of the three. One should therefore take care to rub three times. Ben Ish Chai, ibid. 3. See Nehar Shalom, page 80a. Ben Ish Chai writes there that during the rubbing one should keep his fingers pointing upward and not bent. Regarding the kavanos for washing and rubbing, see below, siman 162:2.
