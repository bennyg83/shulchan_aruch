(8) He should be meticulous — because apart from the reasons mentioned in siman katan 1 that one must wash one's hands for prayer, one must also wash because of ruach ra'ah that rests on the hands; but where netilah is only because of ruach ra'ah alone, they would not enact a blessing therefor; therefore we need [to satisfy] also the reasons stated above:

(9) To pour upon them — He should wash lechatchila until the wrist; and if he does not have enough water, until the knuckle joints of his fingers. He must extend his palms as one who wishes to receive something, and raise them opposite the head. It is also good to rinse his mouth; on a public fast day he should not rinse:

(10) Three times — Even pouring much at one time is ineffective, for the spirit departs only through three times — namely, once on the right hand and then on the left, until three times alternately. In Maaseh Rav it is written: four times three times to remove ruach ra'ah, and the fourth [set] to remove the water that became impure. Women must be warned to be careful with netilat yadayim three times alternately like men; also because they prepare foods and should not defile them with their hands. Children too should be warned to wash their hands in the morning because they touch food.
