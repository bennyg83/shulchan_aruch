GP. If you pour a lot at one time, it is not helpful because the wind only goes three times, that is, once on the right hand and then on the left hand up to three times alternately. When he gives water to his right hand, each time he will say the big hand and in the left hand the strong hand. When he washes his face, he will say his head is a spot of gold, etc. And when he washes his eyes, he will say his eyes are like doves, etc. When he washes his face, he will say his life is a bed of perfume, etc. When he washes his lips, he will say his lips are roses, etc. And when he wipes his face, he will say And their face is the face of a man and the face of a lion to the right of the four, and in this he will find favor in the eyes of all who see him. Women should be warned to be careful about holding hands 3 times alternately.
