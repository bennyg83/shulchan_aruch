Three times. Even if he pours a great deal at once it does not help, for the spirit only departs in three times — that is, once on the right hand and then on the left, up to three times intermittently. When he pours water on his right hand he should say each time "the great hand"; on the left, "the strong hand." When he rubs his hands together he should say three times "the exalted hand." When he fills his hands to wash his face he should say "His hands are rods of gold," etc. When he washes his face he should say "His head is finest gold," etc. When he washes his eyes he should say "His eyes are like doves," etc. When he washes his cheeks he should say "His cheeks are beds of spices," etc. When he washes his lips he should say "His lips are roses," etc. When he dries his face he should say "their appearance… the face of a man and the face of a lion on the right to the four," and thereby he will find favor in the eyes of all who see him. Women must be warned to be careful with netilat yadayim three times intermittently, so they do not defile foods; they must wash up to the wrist.
