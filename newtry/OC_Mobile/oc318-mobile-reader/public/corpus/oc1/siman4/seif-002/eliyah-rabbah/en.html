That he does not bless, etc. The Bach and later authorities ruled to bless over washing the hands, since in any event he is washing with water. However, where he cleans with earth they agree that he blesses over the cleanliness of the hands, as below in seif 22 and as the Rosh and Tur wrote explicitly. Although Responsa Rashba, siman 191, implies that one always blesses over washing the hands, that is because he holds that a vessel is indispensable even in the morning; we hold that it is not indispensable, as the Beis Yosef wrote, for we did not find among the poskim anyone who requires a vessel except the Rashba. However, for washing hands for eating, it is possible that one indeed always blesses over washing the hands, for there everyone requires a vessel. See below at the end of siman 159. Afterward the Magen Avraham was printed and wondered at the Shulchan Aruch. In my humble opinion the matter is clear as I explained.
