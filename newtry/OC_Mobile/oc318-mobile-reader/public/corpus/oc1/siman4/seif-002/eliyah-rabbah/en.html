[2] That he does not bless, etc. Bach and later authorities ruled to bless on netilat yadayim since in any case he washes with water. However where he cleans with earth they agree he blesses on cleanliness of hands, as below seif 22 and as Rosh and Tur wrote explicitly. Although in Rashba's responsum siman 191 it implies one always blesses on netilat yadayim — that is because he holds a vessel hinders even for morning, and we hold it does not hinder, as Beis Yosef wrote: we do not find among poskim one who says a vessel is required except Rashba. However for netilat yadayim for eating it is possible one properly always blesses on netilat yadayim, for there a vessel is required according to all. See below end of siman 159. Again Magen Avraham was printed and wondered on Shulchan Aruch — it appears to me it is clear as I explained.
