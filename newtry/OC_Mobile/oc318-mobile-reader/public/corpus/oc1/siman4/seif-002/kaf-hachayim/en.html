2) If he rose from bed and did not find the netilah vessel near him and cannot tell another to bring it — Berach Yosef there: he heard from holy rabbis who practice walking less than four cubits at a time as they said regarding Shabbat — so Morah Ba'etzba on line of thumb-knot siman 1 letter 4, Shaarei Tzion page 18:3, Zachor LeChaim ch. 3 in that name, Rav Ohr Chaim letter 1, Petach HaDevir ch. 1 letter 1, in Kitzur Chaim Shenayim Yishalem letter 2; so Ben Avraham letter 7 — unlike Beis Yosef letter 3 and Shaarei Tzion siman 1 letter 2 who say conversely mitzvah to run to water to quickly remove impurity force — see Rav Ohr Chaim there who rejected them; Rav Petach HaDevir rejected them — see there. Rav Ohr Chaim concluded: walk less than four cubits at a time and sit somewhat — sitting measure equals walking measure — see there. However from above poskim who mentioned only standing it implies one need not sit.
