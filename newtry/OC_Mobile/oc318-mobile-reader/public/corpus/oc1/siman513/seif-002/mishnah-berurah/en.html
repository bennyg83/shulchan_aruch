{ה} Forbidden — and if it became mixed with others there are views among poskim whether here too we say it is not nullified because it is a davar sheyeish lo matirim; Magen Avraham sides to be stringent, and some later authorities disagree and hold that since it is a rabbinic matter it is reasonable to rely on the lenient to permit, and even if this doubt became mixed one in two it is nullified. And in a place of loss one may rely on them.
