(s.k. 2) That is, etc. For the prohibition of touching is from the Yerushalmi on that which is taught on folio 4 of Beitzah, etc.: but one may invert a vessel over it so that it not break. The Yerushalmi concludes: only that he not touch it. Beis Yosef brought it in siman 310, and Maggid Mishneh, chapter 25 of the laws of Shabbos, wrote, and this is his language: I do not know the reason, etc. And perhaps the egg will sway through the vessel (that is to say, because the egg is round it sways through the touching); end quote. And in Terumat HaDeshen he wrote that this reason is strained. And perhaps Terumat HaDeshen holds that one should not forbid touching for this reason, for it is something not intended, and also it is not a psik reisha. And even if it is a psik reisha, one can say that Terumat HaDeshen follows his view that regarding a rabbinic matter a psik reisha is permitted (as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 314, s.k. 5). And Terumat HaDeshen wrote, and this is his language: rather it appears to distinguish: there, since placing the vessel on the egg is for the need of the egg, which is a muktzeh item, therefore it is forbidden to touch it; but when the placing and use are for the need of something that is not muktzeh, when one touches the muktzeh through the placing it is permitted; end quote of Beis Yosef above in siman 314.
And likewise siman 277, seif 3: for there Rama wrote, nevertheless it is permitted to touch a board, etc. And the same law applies that it is permitted to touch a lamp, etc., only that he not cause it to sway, etc.; and he stated it without stipulating specifically when the touching is for the need of a permitted matter; therefore even for the need of muktzeh it is nevertheless permitted, not like Terumat HaDeshen. And likewise in siman 308, seif 3 in the gloss he wrote: all muktzeh, etc., but mere touching that does not sway it is permitted; and he likewise stated it without writing specifically when he touches for the need of a permitted matter; rather, perforce he holds that when it does not sway, in every case it is permitted even for the need of the muktzeh item, not like Terumat HaDeshen.
However it is difficult, etc. even though it sways from his touch. And to resolve the Yerushalmi that wrote "and only that he not touch," which implies that touching is forbidden: necessarily one must say as Terumat HaDeshen wrote, to distinguish whether the touching is for the need of a permitted matter or not; and if so Rama's words contradict one another. And in Darkei Moshe he wrote in the name of Or Zarua, etc.: that is to say, according to the words of Darkei Moshe Rama's words are resolved.
It is implied that it discusses, etc. That is to say, Or Zarua discusses when a permitted item rests on the lamp; therefore it is permitted to take a permitted item from the lamp even though thereby the lamp is swayed; nevertheless since he does not touch the lamp itself, only through a permitted item resting on it, it is only moving from the side, and therefore permitted. And with this Rama's words in siman 265 are well: certainly also in siman 265 he holds not like Terumat HaDeshen, and there is no distinction between touching for the need of a permitted matter or for the need of muktzeh. And when it sways, in every case it is forbidden. And that he wrote "and it is forbidden to touch a lit lamp," etc., "lest it sway," etc.: he stated a settled matter, for when the lamp is lit it is forbidden even if one touches it through a permitted item resting on it, for it is not merely moving from the side; but when it is not lit, the prohibition is not a settled matter, for sometimes it is permitted, namely when he does not touch except through a permitted item, that is, moving from the side. And even though in the Yerushalmi it wrote regarding "and he inverts a vessel over it" and "only that he not touch it with the vessel," as Beis Yosef wrote in siman 310 the wording of the Yerushalmi, and as is written here in seif 4: the reason is that Rama holds that swaying too is called moving muktzeh; nevertheless we hold in siman 311 that moving from the side is permitted, and that is specifically when the moving is for the need of a permitted matter; therefore Magen Avraham wrote that a permitted item rests [on it]. But in the Yerushalmi, which discusses inverting a vessel, which is for the need of the muktzeh egg: in such a case even moving from the side is forbidden.
For the Mechaber wrote without distinction, etc., that it is forbidden to move it, etc. but it is permitted to invert a vessel over it, and only that he not touch it; end quote of Beis Yosef. And he stated it without writing that it discusses a round item that sways; perforce even if it does not sway it is forbidden, since there the touching is for the need of the muktzeh item, as Magen Avraham wrote there: that is, according to the view of Terumat HaDeshen. And if so, Rama, who does not hold like Terumat HaDeshen, should have distinguished there: specifically when it sways it is forbidden.
