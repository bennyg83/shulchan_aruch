(2) There. Forbidden to touch. And even though a muktzeh item is not forbidden to touch as long as one does not tilt it, as written above siman 308 seif 3 in the gloss — an egg is different because since it is something that rolls we are concerned lest one cause movement when touching it. Beit Yosef. Bach. And this is the reason our teacher z"l wrote in the gloss "that is, to move it" — meaning the reason for the prohibition of touching is because the egg rolls and it is tilting, as Magen Avraham seif katan 2 wrote; but muktzeh that does not sway is permitted to touch, as written above siman 308 seif 3 in the gloss — see there. And if so, likewise regarding an egg if it rests in a place where it does not sway — if one touches it, touching is permitted. But per the view of Shulchan Aruch there is no permission to touch muktzeh even if it does not sway, except for the need of a permitted matter, as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Terumat HaDeshen — see there. And likewise Magen Avraham there. And likewise above in siman 308 section 30 and siman 510 section 31 — see there.
