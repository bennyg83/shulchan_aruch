Doubt, etc. In Tur it is written the opposite: doubt, etc., forbidden, and if it became mixed in a thousand all are forbidden. From the fact that Beit Yosef changed the wording it is implied that he holds that when there is doubt that it became mixed, all are permitted, for each one is a double doubt / safek sefeika, as Maggid Mishneh wrote. And it requires study, for in Yoreh De'ah siman 102 he ruled to forbid; and perhaps there it discusses when it falls on Sunday which is d'oraisa and a davar sheyeish lo matirim, but here which is rabbinic — nevertheless it appears to me to be stringent, for even a double doubt where both are in the item some forbid in a davar sheyeish lo matirim, as is written in siman 496 seif 4. And a fortiori when one doubt is in the item and one through mixture it is worse, as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 110, and likewise in Darkei Moshe and Terumat HaDeshen per the view of Tur.
