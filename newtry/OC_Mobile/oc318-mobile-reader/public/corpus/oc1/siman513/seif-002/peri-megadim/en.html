And fish trapped on Yom Tov and pieces became mixed in a way they cannot be recognized — afterward perhaps we follow "perhaps" and it requires study; one can say the piece of the whole is one fish, and regarding taste see 107 in Shach and Rashi Chullin 98a on the passage "all fish" — impure fish in a pot: Beit Yosef implies in pure fish one taste, and it cannot be established also "perhaps" in danger even nullified in sixty — I saw in Chemdas Moshe Maharam son of R' Nero to the Gaon av beit din of community Przemysl questioned Rashi Pesachim for the view of Rava: min bemino in any amount — from where to him? In Bechoros 4b: from where do we learn in its kind in any amount? And behold there it says regarding offerings and per R' Yehuda for Rava, and one can say in its kind it is not nullified and from there there is no proof whether the halacha follows Abaye or Rava — in any case it helps.
