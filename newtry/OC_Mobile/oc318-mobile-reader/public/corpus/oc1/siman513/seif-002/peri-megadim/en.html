Even see Taz: a davar sheyeish lo matirim in its kind is forbidden in a thousand, and one cannot say a rabbinic doubt for leniency; Beis Yosef. Said the layman who writes: I will write a bit briefly. Egg white and yolk depend on the dispute, as is written in the laws of Shabbos, whether they are two kinds or one kind; and see siman 322. And fish trapped on Yom Tov whose pieces became mixed so that one cannot recognize them: we follow the name, and this requires further study, for one can say that the piece of the whole has one name, "fish"; and regarding taste see Yoreh Deah 107 in Shach, and Rashi Chullin 98a s.v. "kalkis": an impure fish in a pan; Beis Yosef implies regarding pure fish one taste, and one cannot establish [otherwise]; also it implies that even in danger it is nullified in sixty. I saw in Sefer Chemdat [Maharam], Maharam son of R' Nero, to the Gaon Av Beis Din of the community of Przemysl, who raised a difficulty on Rashi in Pesachim for the view of Rava that same kind [forbids] in any amount: but he holds we do not mix for the corners (Temurah 4b); from where does he have same kind in any amount? And behold, there it says a name written regarding the firstborn for olim, and per R' Yehuda for Rava; and one can say that its kind is not nullified, and from there there is no proof whether the halacha follows Abaye or Rava regarding whether what is done is effective; for one can say there that in its being a firstborn and maaser as a gift they add one in a hundred, etc.; Yehoshua added on the reason of Raavad, or that he has only a verse; and understand.
Further, siman 102, Taz [note] 5: it is implied that the main reason depends on taste; and according to the view of "olim," one can say taste and name; and see siman 85 in Shach 16, and in Peri [Megadim] there.
