Perhaps — see Rashi Beitzah 7b on the passage "R' Yehuda is not common" — as is apparent there that the majority emerged and the minority returned; minority that remained overnight — the common minority, and it is not balanced so it is like majority for prohibition, and even a double doubt does not help when it became mixed — per the Mechaber Magen Avraham 4. And the wording of R' Yehuda "is not common" — so it is implied.

And incidentally I will mention what my master my father the rabbi z"l wrote in his novellae on Beitzah on this: what is the practical difference? And he did not say the practical difference for buying and selling — that if the seller accepted payment and said it was from memory and the buyer brings witnesses it was born at night — mistaken transaction; one can say the majority emerged and returned and we do not follow money after the majority. And behold if we say minority of a minority we follow — do not rely minority on presumption; see Tosafos beginning of chapter HaMeniyah and as is written in siman 36 and 305. If so mistaken transaction; and one can say also "remained" — majority by day and common minority at night, and in balance minority against minority of a minority is majority alone, and we do not follow the majority. And with God's help when I merit to print his book I will explain this. And in Magen Avraham it will be explained further with God's help, and in our novellae we elaborated.
