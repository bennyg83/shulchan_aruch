(א) (l) The eggs are complete – we were even in their cortex:

(ב) It is a time when it is not born that it is a body:

{לה} After Shabbat — and we do not say: perhaps if it had not been slaughtered they would have been born today, and it turns out they already finished yesterday on Shabbat and it is preparation from Shabbat to Yom Tov; for even if it finished on Shabbat it is not considered preparation unless it was born through this finishing. But all this when it was not born — this finishing is not called preparation, for its taste is better when born.
