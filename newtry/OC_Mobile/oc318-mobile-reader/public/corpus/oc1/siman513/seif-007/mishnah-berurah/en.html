{33} Finished eggs: that is, even in their shells.
{34} They are permitted: for so long as it has not been laid, we consider it as her body.
{35} That after Shabbos: and we do not say, perhaps if he had not slaughtered it they would have been laid today, and it turns out they were already finished yesterday on Shabbos, and it would be preparation from Shabbos to Yom Tov; for even if it was finished on Shabbos, that finishing is not considered preparation unless it was laid through that finishing; but so long as it was not laid, this finishing is not called preparation, for its taste is better when it has been laid.
