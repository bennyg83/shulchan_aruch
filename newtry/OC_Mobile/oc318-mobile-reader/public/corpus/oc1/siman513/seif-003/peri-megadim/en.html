That was cooked see Taz; see Magen Avraham on this; and see Beis Yosef, what he wrote, that the Ran's words are the words of Rabbeinu Tam; a minor difficulty, for there are two matters: a different kind is nullified in sixty, and in its own kind as well when dissolved it is nullified; and Levush brought them; and see Yoreh Deah 102 in Shach for the practical ruling: it is implied that whatever dissolved, in its kind [forbids] in any amount, and emission alone; see there and in Peri [Megadim] there. And if the food will spoil by tomorrow, it is not a davar sheyeish lo matirim.
And regarding what there is of a double doubt of an egg, whether it was laid on Yom Tov and was cooked intentionally with a different kind, and there are sixty: even in a Torah doubt such a case is forbidden; and this is unlike butter in Yoreh Deah; see there in Peri [Megadim]. Nevertheless, in a rabbinic matter, what is the law also when there are not sixty and it spoils by tomorrow: do we say in such a case through rolling [of doubts] for leniency? And if it spilled and one does not know whether there were sixty in a different kind, and it spoils: if born after Shabbos it is a Torah doubt, and on ordinary Yom Tov it is rabbinic.
