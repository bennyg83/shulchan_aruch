(There) But to touch it directly is forbidden; that is to say, because through the touching it sways, and it becomes actual moving, not from the side; forbidden. And if so, this [law] in siman 265, which is from the words of Or Zarua, likewise discusses a case like this, where there is a permitted item; and therefore when it is not lit it is permitted, even though it sways thereby, because in such a case, when one takes the permitted item and the lamp sways of itself, it is moving from the side. And therefore he raised a difficulty on Rama, who stated the rule without distinction in siman 310, and did not distinguish that specifically regarding a round item that sways through touching it is forbidden.
