Permitted. For a different kind is nullified in a davar sheyeish lo matirim, even if it became mixed and was beaten into the cooked food.
The egg. It is implied that the egg is still whole, and nevertheless [it is permitted] specifically when it was cooked with meat; but with other eggs, all the eggs are forbidden, because it is the same kind. But Rama's view in Yoreh Deah siman 102 is that its taste is nullified even in a davar sheyeish lo matirim; see there.
For appearance. It is implied that if it is not recognizable in appearance, but only in taste, it is nullified even though it came to improve the pot, such as water and salt in dough, which are nullified. Magen Avraham. If the egg is not peeled and was cooked with others, even sixty are not needed, for it is merely like egg-water. Kol Bo and Toras Chatas. Magen Avraham; see there.
