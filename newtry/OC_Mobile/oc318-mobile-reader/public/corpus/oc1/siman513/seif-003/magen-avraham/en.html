All is permitted. For a different kind is nullified in a davar sheyeish lo matirim, even if it became mixed and was beaten into the cooked food.
Except for the egg. It is implied that the egg is still whole, and nevertheless [it is permitted] specifically when it was cooked with meat; but with other eggs all the eggs are forbidden, because it is the same kind. But Rama's view in Yoreh Deah siman 102 is that its taste is nullified even in a davar sheyeish lo matirim; see there.
That is done for appearance and taste. It is implied that if it is not recognizable in appearance, but only in taste, it is nullified even though it came to improve the pot, such as water and salt in dough, which are nullified, as Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Terumat HaDeshen, who inferred thus from Ravyah; see there; and likewise in Yoreh Deah siman 102 in Shulchan Aruch, that specifically when they filled it with a hen, where it is recognizable in appearance, then it is not nullified; and likewise in Toras Chatas and in the simanim; and even though he brought in Toras Chatas the words of Maharai, he did not bring them as practical law; and see there in Shach and in Toras Chatas. If it is not peeled and was cooked with others, even sixty are not needed, for it is merely like egg-water (Kol Bo); and so Toras Chatas ruled; and see Beis Yosef here.
