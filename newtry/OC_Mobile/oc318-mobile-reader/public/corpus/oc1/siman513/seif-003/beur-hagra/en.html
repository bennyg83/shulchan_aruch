Seif 3: that was cooked unwittingly. Specifically as is written at the end of chapter 5 of Terumot: a seah of terumah that fell into less than a hundred, and afterward chulin fell there: if unwittingly, etc.
If there are sixty, etc. And even though a davar sheyeish lo matirim is not nullified even in a thousand, as is written in chapter 1 and chapter 5: that is specifically same kind in same kind. And as is written in Nedarim 58a [and] 24: it has permitting factors, such as tevel, etc.; and we say in chapter 3 of Challah that tevel forbids all the more so in its kind, and not in a different kind [only] by giving taste; and in Nedarim there he said to them, I too, etc.; and see the Ran s.v. "he said to them," etc.; and sheviis also in its kind, as is written there and in chapter 7 of Sheviis, and there [Nedarim] 52a, that one who vows from a thing, etc. And even though it is a davar sheyeish lo matirim, as is written there 59a, R' Elazar said: konamos are different, etc.; and in Yerushalmi chapter 6 of Nedarim, halacha 8: for R' Assi said in the name of R' Yehoshua regarding a davar sheyeish lo matirim, such as tevel, maaser sheni, hekdesh, and chadash, the Sages did not give them a measure except same kind in same kind, all the more so, and a different kind by giving taste; and all that has no permitting factors, such as, etc. These vows: what do you do with them, as a davar sheyeish lo matirim or as a davar she'ein lo matirim? It is reasonable that we treat them as a davar sheyeish lo matirim, etc. We learned there: one who vows and it became mixed with another, if it gives taste it is forbidden. Resolve it as different kind regarding a davar sheyeish lo matirim. And see Tosafos later 38a s.v. "and to be nullified," etc., and one can say that this applies only, etc. And all this discusses a peeled egg; but when it is not peeled it does not forbid at all, as is written in Chullin 97 and 98: are we saying it gives taste, but they said, etc.; and see Tosafos there s.v. "in an egg of," etc. And this is what he wrote, "with meat and cooked food," which is not so with eggs, which are same kind in same kind. But in the Tur he wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Tam: specifically when it is present in substance; but its taste is nullified in sixty. And so he wrote in the gloss in Yoreh Deah siman 102, seif 4.
