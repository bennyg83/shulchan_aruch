Seif 3 — that it was cooked unwittingly. Specifically as is written at the end of chapter 8 of Terumot: a seah of terumah that fell into less than a hundred and afterward secular produce fell there — if unwitting, etc.

If there are sixty, etc. And even though it is a davar sheyeish lo matirim, even in a thousand it is not nullified, as is written in chapter 1 and chapter 8 — specifically min bemino. And as is written in Nedarim 58a, 24 — it has permitting factors such as untithed produce, etc., and we say in chapter 3 of Challah that untithed produce forbids, all the more so in its kind, not in a different kind on Yom Tov. And in Nedarim there he said to them: I too, etc., and Or Zarua on the passage "he said to them," etc., and shevi'it also in its kind as is written there and chapter 7 of shevi'it and there 52a, 1 — one who vows from the thing, etc. And even though it is a davar sheyeish lo matirim as is written there Nedarim 59a, 1 — R' Eliezer said: konam vows are different, etc., and in Yerushalmi chapter 6 of Nedarim halacha 8 — R' Assi in the name of R' Yehoshua regarding a davar sheyeish lo matirim such as untithed produce, second tithe, consecrated property, and new grain — the Sages did not give them a measure except min bemino, all the more so not in a different kind on Yom Tov. And all that lacks permitting factors such as, etc. — these vows, what do you do with them — as a davar sheyeish lo matirim or as a davar she'ein lo matirim? It is reasonable that we act as a davar sheyeish lo matirim, etc. We learned there: one who vows and it became mixed with another — if there is in it one part in sixty it is forbidden. Resolve: min in what is not its kind in a davar sheyeish lo matirim. And see in Tosafos later 38a, on the passage "and to nullify," etc., and one can say that this applies only, etc. And all this discusses a peeled egg, but when it is not peeled it does not forbid as is written in Chullin 97 and 98 — to say it gives taste, but they said, etc., and see Tosafos there on the passage "in the egg of," etc. And this is what he wrote "with meat and stew" — not so with eggs which are min bemino. But in Tur he wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Tam: specifically when it is in substance, but its taste is nullified in sixty. And likewise in the gloss in Yoreh De'ah siman 102 seif 4.
