A fortiori when one doubt is in the item itself, etc. — meaning it is worse, etc. — meaning the Mechaber wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 114 seif 9: an egg of doubtful terefah that became mixed with others — all are forbidden, etc. Since the first doubt was in the item itself one may not permit it through a double doubt. And on this Rama wrote: and some say the reason it is forbidden is since the doubt is a Torah prohibition and we cannot say another doubt that there is no prohibition, only that it became mixed with others — this is not called a double doubt. And Shach wrote: there is a difference between the two reasons — if the first doubt was rabbinic, per Rama it is permitted and per Beit Yosef forbidden; and in the end Shach sided that one can say even the Mechaber agrees where the first doubt is rabbinic it is permitted through a double doubt — that is, when it became mixed even though the first sixty is in the item itself and the second through mixture — see there. And if so here, which discusses ordinary Yom Tov where the egg's prohibition is rabbinic — if so, even one doubt in the item and one through mixture is a double doubt and permitted. Nevertheless such a case where one is in the item and one through mixture is worse than other double doubts, for in such a case in Torah law it is not a double doubt, and in a davar sheyeish lo matirim some poskim forbid even a double doubt where both are in the item. In any event one should be stringent in a davar sheyeish lo matirim with one doubt in the item and one through mixture, for it is not a double doubt, and three mixtures in a davar sheyeish lo matirim — see above siman 496 what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Shach.
