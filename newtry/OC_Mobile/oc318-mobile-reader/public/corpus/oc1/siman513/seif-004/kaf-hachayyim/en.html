4) There. If it became mixed even with one thousand, etc. This is because it is an item that will become permitted. If it is left until after Yom Tov, it will become permitted by itself. The Sages ruled stringently that an item which will become permitted by itself is not nullified even in one thousand. It is better to wait to eat it until the time of its permissibility arrives than to eat it while forbidden by relying upon nullification. Levush, se'if 4; Taz, subsection 2; Magen Avraham, subsection 3; Chemed Moshe, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 4; Chayei Adam, rule 101, subsection 4; Mikraei Kodesh, siman 7, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 4.
