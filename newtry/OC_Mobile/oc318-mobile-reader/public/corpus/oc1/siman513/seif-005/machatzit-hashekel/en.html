(Seif katan 5) The rule, etc. — even if it became mixed and was terefah, etc. — the intent is not that Shulchan Aruch discusses even when it was terefah. For this is as Magen Avraham wrote later in seif katan 8 afterward — Shulchan Aruch discusses when it is whole; rather Magen Avraham wrote that the true law applies even when it was terefah.
