And likewise on Shabbos and Yom Tov. And if you ask: when it was laid on Shabbos, behold it was finished on Friday, which is a weekday, so it should be permitted; one can say: since it is forbidden on Shabbos by rabbinic law, if you permit it on the Yom Tov after it, it turns out that Shabbos prepared for Yom Tov and not for itself; and this prohibition is only rabbinic, for according to the Torah even on the first day it is permitted, and if so it prepared for itself; end quote of the Ran. And in Yam Shel Shlomo he wrote that according to what Tosafos wrote in the first answer, that the laying is considered preparation, if so it is forbidden according to the Torah on the day after it; end quote. And it seems he did not see the words of Ramban in Milchamot, who wrote that the laying is considered preparation, and nevertheless it is permitted according to the Torah on the second day, for the first day prepared for itself; end quote. And so he wrote in Sefer HaChinuch, that you do not find preparation according to the Torah except regarding an egg laid on Shabbos after Yom Tov, or on Yom Tov after Shabbos; and see what he wrote at the end of seif 6, and siman 507, seif 2.
