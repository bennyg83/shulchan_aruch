If it was laid on the first day of Yom Tov it is permitted on the second day of Yom Tov in the two days of Diaspora holidays. But for the two days of Rosh Hashanah, and similarly for Shabbat and Yom Tov that are adjacent to each other — if it was laid on one it is forbidden on the other. {Rama: Rema: If Yom Tov falls on Sunday and Monday and the egg was laid on the preceding Shabbat, it is permitted on the second day of the Diaspora holiday. (Maharil)}
