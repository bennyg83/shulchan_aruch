(There, seif 5 in the gloss) And if Yom Tov falls on Sunday and Monday, etc. Note: It is implied explicitly that on the first day it is forbidden, for Shabbat does not prepare for Yom Tov — it appears that likewise the reverse: if Yom Tov is on Friday it is forbidden on the Shabbat after it, for Yom Tov does not prepare for Shabbat, and eruv tavshilin does not help in this as is written above regarding milk. And I brought proof from the words of the Rosh section 96 in the name of Avodat HaKodesh, and likewise here — see there and understand. And behold in the words of the Ran cited above that in a davar sheyeish lo matirim we do not follow the majority — I elaborated greatly in my novellae, and now it is very doubtful what the Ran would answer to the explicit Mishnah in Machshirin chapter 2, 11: fruits of the second year that exceeded those of the third, and of the third over the fourth, and of the fourth over the fifth, etc. — we follow the majority — see there in Ra'avad and in the explicit sources, and it is difficult: behold untithed produce is a davar sheyeish lo matirim — why do we follow the majority in this? Perforce it is proven that even in a davar sheyeish lo matirim we follow the majority, and there perforce we come on it from the reason of majority, for if from the reason of nullification it is obvious there is a difficulty that untithed produce is not nullified in a thousand — perforce that we do not come on it from nullification but only that we say this is from the majority and whatever separates from the majority separates. If so it is proven that we follow the majority in a davar sheyeish lo matirim. And behold from my words here it would appear to prove from that Mishnah that majority is preferable to the latter — behold it is difficult why we follow the majority; let us follow the latter as we establish in chapter 1 of Pesachim. And one cannot say it discusses when they used together community by community as they answer in chapter 1 of Pesachim — for if so why does the Mishnah state explicitly "fruits of the second exceeded the third" which is the opposite of the way it should be, that the new should exceed the old, for there is an advantage in the latter also; therefore it teaches the opposite — when the second exceeded the third, even though the latter is the minority, we follow the majority. If so it is proven that it discusses when they did not use together, only one after another, and it is proven that majority is preferable to the latter. And it requires study in all this, and it is not the time to gather here — perhaps God will grant me another time to explain more on this. However per what the Rosh wrote there that the intent of "we follow the majority" is to be stringent — our difficulty is resolved well, and also our proof that not like the Ran is rejected in this — see there and understand.
