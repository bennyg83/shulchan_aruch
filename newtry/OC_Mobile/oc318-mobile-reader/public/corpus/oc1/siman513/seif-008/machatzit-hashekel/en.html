(s.k. 8) And likewise on Shabbos and Yom Tov; and if you ask, etc. For necessarily the reason is not that Shabbos and Yom Tov are one sanctity and are like one long day; for we hold in siman 416, and so it is in the Gemara in Beitzah, that they are two sanctities; rather, perforce the reason is because of preparation.
And on this he raised a difficulty: but it was finished on Friday.
One can say: since it is forbidden on Shabbos by rabbinic law; so it should read; for we decree on account of one laid on Shabbos after Yom Tov.
For according to what Tosafos wrote. Beitzah folio 4, who also raised the difficulties of the Ran mentioned above, and likewise answered as the Ran answered. And in the first answer they wrote to resolve the Ran's difficulty, that the laying is considered preparation; that is to say, through the laying it was prepared to raise a chick, and it is also better for eating, as he wrote in s.k. 14.
