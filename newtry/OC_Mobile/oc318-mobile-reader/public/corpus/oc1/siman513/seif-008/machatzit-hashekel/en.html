(Seif katan 8) And likewise Shabbat and Yom Tov — and you might ask, etc. — perforce the reason is not because Shabbat and Yom Tov are one sanctity and it is like one long day, for we establish in siman 496 and likewise in the Gemara Beitzah that they are two sanctities; rather perforce the reason is because of preparation.
