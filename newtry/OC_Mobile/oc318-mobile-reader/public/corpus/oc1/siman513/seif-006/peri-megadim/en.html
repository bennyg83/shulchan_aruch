On Yom Tov see Taz 19: and regarding Shabbos there is no either-way argument; and the same law applies to Yom Tov on Thursday and Friday, and if it was laid on it, it is forbidden on the Shabbos after it; and see Rambam at the end of chapter 1 of the laws of Yom Tov. And nevertheless it is difficult, for this is not sufficient, since they are two sanctities; only according to what Tosafos wrote in Beitzah 4a s.v. "rather," and in Rashi there it is implied that laying is preparation according to the Torah, or by rabbinic law that Shabbos prepared for Yom Tov; see Magen Avraham 8, and see the Ran; and with Hashem's help in Magen Avraham it will be explained further; and see Yam Shel Shlomo, s.k. of seif 3.
