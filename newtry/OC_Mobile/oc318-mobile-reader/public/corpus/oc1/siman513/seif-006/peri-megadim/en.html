On Yom Tov — Taz 19, and Shabbat there is no either way, and likewise Yom Tov Thursday-Friday and it was born on it — forbidden on the Shabbat after it. And Or Sameach end of chapter 1 of Yom Tov. And nevertheless it is difficult, for this is not sufficient — they are two sanctities only at Motzei Shabbos, per Tosafos Beitzah 4a on the passage "rather," and in Rashi there it is implied birth is preparation d'oraisa or rabbinically that Shabbat prepared for Yom Tov — Magen Avraham 8, and Or Zarua, and with God's help in Magen Avraham it will be explained further, and see Yeshuot Shalom seif katan 3.
