That it is done for appearance, see Baer Heitev and what he wrote above in siman 507; and see in Mor Uketziah: if they smeared the face of the cake with it to whiten it, and scraped it off with a spiked board, and made from it a cake made with fat, it is permitted, for its taste and appearance have gone; and even though it was done to improve a pot it is permitted, as Magen Avraham wrote; and all the more so here, for this smearing is not to improve a pot; see there. And see in Responsa Pri Tevuah, part 2, siman 11, regarding an egg laid on Yom Tov that became mixed with others, and they took from the mixture an egg and whitened the food and it did not suffice, and they took another egg from the mixture and put it into the cooked food, and it was a large pot with sixty [times], and also if they had whitened only with the muktzeh egg alone the whitening would not have been noticeable at all, only the appearance comes from all the egg put into the food; and he brought the words of Tosafos, chapter 25, siman 88, who wrote that they all became the body of the prohibition, and also that there is concern that the fourth egg was the prohibition; and see above in Taz, siman 318, that when it was completed through the prohibition it is forbidden, and Eliyah Rabbah challenged him on this; and also that there is a double doubt: perhaps it remained in the mixture, and perhaps in the end it was not [the forbidden one]; and see in Yoreh Deah siman 114, that like a double doubt in a davar sheyeish lo matirim, a double doubt helps for a need, and all the more so when there is doubt whether there is a prohibition here at all; and he concluded to permit eating the cooked food on Yom Tov; see there.
