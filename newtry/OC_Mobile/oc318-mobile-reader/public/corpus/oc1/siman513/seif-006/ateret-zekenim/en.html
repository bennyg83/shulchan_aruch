Hashem’s Word tells us that Hashem’s people are born in Hashem’s Word, and Hashem’s Word is Hashem’s Word, and Hashem’s Word is not Hashem’s word, but if Hashem knows that Hashem’s Word is not Hashem’s will, He will not be able to believe it

It is said that Hashem was born on the Shabbat, and that Hashem was born in Hashem’s Word, and that it was written on the Shabbat
