(א) And a non-Jew who brings eggs on the first day of Yom Tov and speaks loosely that they were born yesterday — it is permitted on the second day of the diaspora. And likewise if born on the first day of Yom Tov it is permitted on the second day, even per the view of those who are stringent in siman 506 seif 1 — see there. And specifically regarding a non-Jew who is not expert in the benefit of a Jew; but if the non-Jew knows that it was born on Yom Tov it is forbidden — one may not rely on him, for he intends to improve his merchandise in order to permit it. He says thus (Beit Yosef in the gloss):

(ב) "Perhaps it was born on Shabbat" — in twilight it is implied that there was concern that perhaps it was born on Yom Tov, meaning on Sunday, for then it would be preparation of Rabba which is d'oraisa. And see in Bach on this.
