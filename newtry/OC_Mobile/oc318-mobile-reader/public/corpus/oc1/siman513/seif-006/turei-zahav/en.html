(א) You can drink at night. And the PLO whose majority will not be born at night here is the same as a minority who was tested yesterday and today in the dawn and found and did not include a majority depletion from yesterday and back (today) it is not common to doubt it:

But if he did not check, etc. Explanation: he arose at dawn and found, but he did not check at dawn, rather during the day he found — forbidden out of doubt lest it emerged today; doubt in a davar sheyeish lo matirim is forbidden in equal doubt.

And forbidden d'oraisa. This is the view of Terumat HaDeshen, and the reason is because of preparation — an egg born today finished yesterday, and Shabbat does not prepare for Yom Tov, and it is a d'oraisa prohibition as is written "and you shall prepare that which you bring," and in doubt d'oraisa we are stringent. Mahariv ruled that they are permitted even on Yom Tov after Shabbat, since he does not improve his merchandise and diminishes it, for eggs born today are preferable to yesterday's. Maharshal wrote that this is the main view, for even though we do not rely on a non-Jew speaking loosely in Torah matters — here is different, for most eggs are already born from several days and they are the majority of the world; and although we do not permit because of majority in such a case since we are stringent in doubt, nevertheless that in a thousand all are forbidden is rabbinic — nevertheless we rely on speaking loosely, per Mordechai's words — until here Maharshal's words.
