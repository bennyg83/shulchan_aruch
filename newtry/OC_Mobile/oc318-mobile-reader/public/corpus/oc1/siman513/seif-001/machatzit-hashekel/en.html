(א) (C.A.) is not allowed to be named an epic in the chicken. Dessert is assigned to the egg in the egg of the chicken and eats everything inside the egg is forbidden from preparation:

And that it is forbidden in tilting, etc. Yeshuot Shalom — per his view Magen Avraham is brought in siman 515: every thing that is not muktzeh on its own account — even though it is forbidden in eating and one does not intend to throw it to dogs — nevertheless tilting is permitted; if so, for one designated for eating it would be forbidden in eating because of preparation, nevertheless in tilting it would be permitted — thus he wrote because we do not distinguish, meaning for one designated to raise eggs the egg is muktzeh and forbidden in tilting, and since they also forbade eating for one designated for eating because of preparation they did not want to distinguish between them regarding tilting and forbade tilting. But Magen Avraham later siman 515 disagrees with Maharshal and holds that every thing forbidden in eating is muktzeh and forbidden in tilting. And if so, one cannot say "because we do not distinguish" here that tilting is forbidden even for one designated for eating.
