(1) [Seif 1] An egg laid on Yom Tov — forbidden to touch. Even from a hen designated for eating, for on Yom Tov that falls on Sunday it is forbidden d'oraisa, for every egg born today finished yesterday in its womb, and Shabbat does not prepare for Yom Tov, and we decree on ordinary Yom Tov because of Yom Tov that falls on Sunday. And that it is forbidden in tilting is because we do not distinguish. Yeshuot Shalom chapter 1 of Beitzah seif 5, Magen Avraham seif katan 1. And it is from the Gemara Beitzah 2b. And what he wrote "because we do not distinguish" — meaning there is no distinction between an egg born from a hen designated to raise eggs and one born from a hen designated for eating; since both are forbidden in eating they are also forbidden in tilting, as Yeshuot Shalom wrote there and Beit David in Orach Chayim section 1, and likewise Rambam chapter 1 law 20: as it is forbidden to eat it so it is forbidden to tilt it. And likewise Rashba in Avodat HaKodesh section 5 section 4, and likewise Orach Chayim in the name of several early commentators who forbid tilting an egg born from a hen designated for eating — not like Tosafos Shabbos 45b — see there. And see further from this above siman 322 and our words there in seif 4.
