In Taz seif katan 1: when it is hanging on it — at first glance it appears Taz explains that even though there the reason is lest it sway and tilt — meaning it discusses when a permitted item rests and there is no concern of tilting, for it is from the side, as Magen Avraham seif katan 1 wrote; but touching directly is forbidden because through touching it sways — therefore it is difficult for him: on an egg too we should say thus, and he did not want to explain what Rama wrote here "that is, to move it" — that he means through touching one comes to tilting, as Magen Avraham seif katan 1 wrote; rather he holds Rama meant actual tilting specifically. And it appears more that before Taz in the gloss text of siman 265 the word "and tilt" was not in the text, and Taz held to explain Rama's words in siman 265 that even though it is not tilted and not muktzeh for touching alone — nevertheless one must be concerned lest it sway and it is forbidden because of muktzeh. And so it appears from Rama's words in siman 308 seif 3: if it certainly will not sway it is permitted. And there it discusses touching a lamp in the synagogue — meaning because they are heavier than usual or fixed in place and will not sway; but where there is concern that through touching one comes to swaying it is forbidden. However in Darkei Moshe in the name of Or Zarua it is explained as Magen Avraham wrote — because of tilting he comes on it. And so it appears that since in the gloss he wrote "forbidden to touch a lit lamp" that is burning now — if because of tilting muktzeh, even after it went out it should be forbidden.
