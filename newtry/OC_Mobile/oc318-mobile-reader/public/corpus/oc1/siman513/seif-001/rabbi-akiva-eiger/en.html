Magen Avraham seif katan 13: because it is a davar sheyeish lo matirim — see in the Ran chapter 1 Ein Tzedadin who is uncertain in this foundation whether we say regarding a davar sheyeish lo matirim "from the majority it separates." And see in Yoreh De'ah siman 15 that in Ulla they said "from the majority it separates" even though it is a davar sheyeish lo matirim.
