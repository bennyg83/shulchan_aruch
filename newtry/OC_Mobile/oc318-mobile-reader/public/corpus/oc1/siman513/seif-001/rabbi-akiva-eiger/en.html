Magen Avraham s.k. 13: because it is a davar sheyeish lo matirim. See in the Ran, chapter Ein Tzadin, who is in doubt about this principle, whether we say regarding a davar sheyeish lo matirim that whatever separates follows the majority. And see in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 16, that regarding an eiver min hachai we say whatever separates follows the majority even though it is a davar sheyeish lo matirim.
