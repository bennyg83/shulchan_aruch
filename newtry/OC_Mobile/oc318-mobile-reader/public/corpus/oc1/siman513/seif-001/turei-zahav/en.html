And this is from what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Mordechai in siman 308 that there is no prohibition on muktzeh by touching alone without tilting; however Beit Yosef concludes here: an egg is different since it is something that rolls — we are concerned lest one cause movement through touching. And Rama ruled this reasoning also in siman 265 seif 3 that it is forbidden to touch a lit lamp when it is hanging. And therefore it is wondrous to me why he did not write here also the prohibition even without tilting, and why he strained to be lenient and distinguish between an egg and a hanging lamp.
