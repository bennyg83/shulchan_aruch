Even so Taz Rosh Hashanah 34b and Rashi and Beit Yosef and Perishah — doubt if he will find blowers; nevertheless safek Torah is preferable to certainty of d'rabbanan. Requires study on the second day — whether they are expert in establishing the month. Also if he blew one order at home and no more — whether he should go to doubt that they blow. For Rambam z"l every safek Torah l'kula and d'rabbanan l'chumra — one could say here it is different since he is obligated to do what is upon him; all the more a safek of the law of Eretz Yisrael how they blow does not enter this category. And in Yerushalmi end of Rosh Hashanah Beit Yosef brought briefly: on the 19th of Rosh Hashanah that falls on Shabbat one goes to the one who blesses and not to the blower — for one who lacks time to blow; and you said go to the one who blesses — this teaches they disagree. See in the Gaon who explains there — apparently per the view that blowing is preferable even on the second Yom Tov when Rosh Hashanah day one was erev eruv for both as siman 15 see there; even so blowing is preferable to blessing — requires study still on such a doubt.
