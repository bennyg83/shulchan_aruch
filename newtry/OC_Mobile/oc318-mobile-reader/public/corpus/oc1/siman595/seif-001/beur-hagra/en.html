(ב) However. Yerushalmi there: in one place they blow and in one place they bless. It teaches: one version — he goes to the blower; another — he goes to the one who blesses. He who says go to the blower — when there is not enough time in the day to blow; he who says go to the one who blesses — when there is time in the day to blow. And even though it concludes there that some disagree even when there is no time in the day that one goes to the one who blesses — we do not rule thus, for our gemara does not hold so, as written in Shulchan Aruch.
