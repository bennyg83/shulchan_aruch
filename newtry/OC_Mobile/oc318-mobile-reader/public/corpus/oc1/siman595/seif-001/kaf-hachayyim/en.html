1) [Seif 1] He should go to a place where they are proficient in sounding the shofar, etc. The reason is that sounding the shofar is a Torah commandment, whereas the blessings are by rabbinic enactment. Beit Yosef; Levush; Taz, s.k. 1; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. The same applies if, in one place, they know only one series of blasts; he should go to them, for this too is included in the doubt. Magen Avraham, ibid.; Mateh Ephraim, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 2.
