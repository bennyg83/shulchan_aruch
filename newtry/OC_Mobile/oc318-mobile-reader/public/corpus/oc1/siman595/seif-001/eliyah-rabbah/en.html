He should go to the place where they are expert, etc. If it is possible to obtain a shofar through non-Jews from outside the techum, see the end of siman 586. Maharil wrote that what we say in the Gemara, Rosh Hashanah, folio 16, that any year in which they do not blow at the beginning, they sound the alarm for it at the end, applies specifically when they acted intentionally; but if it was because of compulsion, no harm will befall them. End quote. This requires analysis, for in Abudarham, folio 99, this is his language: It is not speaking of Rosh Hashanah that fell on Shabbos, for Chazal would not decree and enact something that entails danger. Rather, it means if an unavoidable circumstance happened to them, such as that they had no shofar or no blower, etc. End quote. So too wrote the Baal Halachos Gedolos, folio 38, Tosafos on folio 16, Ran, and Agudah there. Later I saw in the Taz, siman 585, that he explained the Baal Halachos Gedolos to mean that the Holy One, blessed is He, brings about the circumstance and shows them that matters will go badly for them at the end of the year so that they will repent and be pained over this; through this they will be atoned and saved. End quote. But in the Abudarham that I brought, it is forced to explain this way.
