To the place where they are expert in the blasts. For the shofar-blasts are D'Oraisa and the blessings are rabbinic, even on the second day. All the more so if before him there is a place where he can hear the shofar-blasts, although there too there is no minyan and he will need to pray alone, and he can go to a place that has a minyan but they have no shofar, it is better to go hear the shofar-blasts even though he will pray alone; Chayei Adam.
And regarding the blasts, there is doubt. If there are blowers there, a Torah doubt is preferable to a definite rabbinic obligation. The same applies if they know only one order; he should go to them. If there is one shofar in the city and he wants to go to the place where they pray and send a non-Jew to bring him the shofar, and it is outside the techum, so that he can fulfill both, it is permitted, for techumin are rabbinic, and this is a shevus of a shevus.
Afterward the blasts. He should not first go to the place where they blow and afterward to the place where they pray, for the Musaf prayer should not be delayed beyond seven hours, as written in siman 286, whereas the time for the shofar-blasts is the entire day. If in one place there is a proper blower but the people praying there are not proper, and in another place proper people pray but the blower is not proper, he should go to the place where the blower is expert and fit, for the blower discharges him of his obligation, whereas in prayer he does not discharge him of his obligation.
