(א) Doubt. Because of Dawray's religion and the blessing of Darin, and "If B does not know only one order, we will go to them with a doubt," he said

(ב) That all day [one may blow]. Even though the time of musaf too is the whole day, nevertheless l'chatchila not after seven hours — see siman 286. And it appears to me that if in one city there is a shofar and he wants to go to the place where they pray and send a gentile to bring him the shofar there while he is outside the techum and could fulfill both — it is forbidden, for he can go from his place to a city that has a shofar; and even though he cannot pray there, prayer is d'rabbanan and we do not push aside one d'rabbanan before another d'rabbanan as written siman 455. However one may say that since techum is d'rabbanan, it is a shevut of a shevut and permitted.
