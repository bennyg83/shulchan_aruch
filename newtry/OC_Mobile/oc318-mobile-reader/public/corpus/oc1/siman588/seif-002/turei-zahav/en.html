And the Levush wrote that if he interrupted with one sound twice, it is no worse than interruption by waiting, and it is permitted; and even according to the Ramban in siman 590, se'if 8 (regarding a blower who blew and sounded a teruah and again blew and sounded a teruah, who has not fulfilled) that case is different, for there the blower himself blew another sound in error, and this is an interruption for the blower who performs the act of blowing; but the hearer, who performs no act but only heard a sound that he should not have heard, has fulfilled if it is not out of the order that he is to hear; and accordingly, also in the case of siman 590, if the blower had already fulfilled and now blows to discharge the congregation, they would fulfill even according to the Ramban, so it appears to me, end quote. And I recognize no distinction between one error and another error, for in the end it is an error and has no connection to [the mitzvah], and it is an interruption; and likewise between hearer and blower there is no distinction, for the essence of the mitzvah is the hearing and not the blowing.
