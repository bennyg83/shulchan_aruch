(5) Nine tekios etc. (He used the language of the Gemara and according to the essential law, that we require only nine sounds) namely tekiah, teruah, tekiah three times; and for us, if he heard the three series over nine hours, as below in siman 590, se'if 2.
(6) He has fulfilled. After the fact, if he intended to fulfill, and even if he was distracted and interrupted with speech in between; and Magen Avraham wrote that all this is where he waited between them not because of coercion; but where he waited under coercion, if he waited even only long enough to complete the entire series, he returns to the beginning of that series, according to the Rama's ruling in siman 65 (see there); and regarding whether he must also return and recite the blessing in such a case, see above in siman 65 in the Biur Halacha, that there is a dispute among the later authorities on this; and see the Biur Halacha here what is written on this.
(7) Specifically when he did not interrupt etc. He holds that this is worse than distraction and speech, for we require that there be a teruah in the middle and before it and after it one tekiah each; and when he mixes in another sound the order is spoiled. And even though he himself did not blow, nevertheless since he intended to fulfill, he is like one who blows himself; and therefore if he heard a shofar sound that is not appropriate in that series, all three tekios are void [however, the six sounds from before are not void], and he returns and blows; nevertheless he does not recite the blessing again.
(8) That is not appropriate in that series, For example, he is standing in Tashat and heard a teruah, or he is standing in Tarat and heard shevarim; but the later authorities agreed that according to the Mechaber's ruling below in siman 590, se'if 8 (that even if he doubled the shevarim or the teruah twice it is an interruption) the same law applies here when he heard two shevarim or two teruos one after the other and intended to fulfill: he likewise loses all three sounds and must blow anew.
