If, for some reason, one was delayed and did not hear the shofar blasts until bein hashmashos, he should then sound the shofar without a blessing, because the time of bein hashmashos is uncertain whether it is part of the day or part of the night, as stated in tractate Shabbos 34b, see there, and in a case of doubt concerning blessings we rule leniently. So ruled Chayei Adam, klal 141, os 1. See above, siman 260, os 1, regarding the time at which bein hashmashos begins, see there. Also see below, siman 652, regarding taking the lulav if one was delayed until bein hashmashos, see there.
