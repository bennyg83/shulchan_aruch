(There seif 3) He heard nine tekios from nine people who all blew as one — he did not fulfill etc. NB: here there are variant readings in the Gemara — some read fulfilled and some read did not fulfill; and in my opinion the main reading is like this reading did not fulfill, as Shulchan Aruch wrote, and it is compelled in the Gemara from what we read there in tractate Rosh Hashanah daf 34: R' Yochanan said: one who heard nine tekios from nine people in nine hours of the day fulfilled etc.; and who said R' Yochanan thus, for R' Yochanan said in Hallel and Megillah: once he waited time to finish all of it he returns to the beginning etc.; and it is very difficult for me why he challenges those who hold [Shulchan Aruch Laws of Kriat Shema siman 64] that waiting time to finish all of it returning to beginning is only when he waited due to coercion, but without coercion he does not return — if so why does he challenge, perhaps R' Yochanan deals with interruption not due to coercion, therefore he fulfilled; and this view is greatly astonishing and I have not seen one who noticed this; therefore it appears to answer thus: the reason of the view that distinguishes between waiting due to coercion or not must be the reason that it is like what we establish: whatever is fit for kneading, kneading does not prevent it; and what is not fit for kneading, kneading prevents it — so too here if not due to coercion and fit to be together, therefore even though not together it helps; but where due to coercion and not fit for kneading, kneading prevents it, therefore it fits well there; but in tekios, since he heard nine tekios from nine people as one, he did not fulfill because there is no plain tekiah before it and plain tekiah after it; and why should we not say behold it is fit for kneading and fit for listeners one after another — this proves that regarding plain before and plain after, fit for kneading does not help, only we require literally before him and after him; and if so here too if there is interruption of another matter this is not at all before and after, only per those who say waiting time to finish all does not return, even when due to coercion it is not fit for kneading per force for him waiting is not considered interruption by itself, therefore it is also considered before and after; but per those who say waiting time to finish all returns to beginning, per force this is considered interruption, therefore where not due to coercion it is not interruption, i.e. from another reason it is fit for kneading; but if so in tekios since he heard from nine people as one he did not fulfill, for we require before and after and behold it is fit for kneading; and per force that regarding before and after fit for kneading does not help and we require before and after literally; therefore where there is interruption it is not before and after literally and fit for kneading does not help here; and he challenges well; therefore the matters compel that we read here did not fulfill — for if the reading were fulfilled, our difficulty stands in its place, why does he challenge; therefore in any case for those who hold there specifically due to coercion he did not fulfill, this reading is also compelled; even for those who hold in any case he did not fulfill, nevertheless our difficulty stands, since the Gemara wishes to prove from this that in any case waiting does not return — and if so whence do we know this, perhaps in truth there is a distinction between where fit for kneading or not as we establish in several places, and we can say what R' Yochanan said that he returns is where not entirely possible, but where possible he does not return — and why does he challenge;
