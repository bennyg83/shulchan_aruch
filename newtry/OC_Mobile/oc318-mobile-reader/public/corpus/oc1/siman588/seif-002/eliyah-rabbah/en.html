[2] [Levush] "And that is provided he heard them etc." It appears that he aimed well in ruling thus unqualifiedly, for in siman 590, se'if 8, he also ruled thus unqualifiedly in the Shulchan Aruch; and it is difficult on the Shulchan Aruch that they wrote here in the language of "and some say" and one cannot say that this case is different because he is not blowing himself, for it is clear in the Beis Yosef here and there that there is no distinction; and even regarding hearing one sound twice, which the Levush innovated from his own mind to distinguish; it is not clear, for it is proven in the Ran that it is always invalid, and so it is proven in Lechem Mishneh, chapter 3 of Hilchos Shofar, and so ruled the Taz.
