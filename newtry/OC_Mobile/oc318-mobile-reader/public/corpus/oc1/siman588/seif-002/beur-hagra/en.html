Se'if 2, "And some say specifically." Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Berachos: R' Yose in the name of R' Yochanan, even if he heard them all day, he has fulfilled, provided that he heard them in order. And in chapter 2 of Megillah, halacha 2; and the Rosh explained in chapter 4 of Rosh Hashanah, se'if 10, in the name of the Ravyah, that since he changed the order he has not fulfilled at all, even the first tekiah, because he interrupted; and this is the reason of R' Abbahu, who needed to enact Tashrat, Tasht, and Tarat because of interruption by another sound, and not because of interruption by waiting, for waiting-interruption does not invalidate, for we hold like R' Yochanan, as above; and so wrote the Ran in the name of the Ramban; and as written above in siman 590, se'if 8.
