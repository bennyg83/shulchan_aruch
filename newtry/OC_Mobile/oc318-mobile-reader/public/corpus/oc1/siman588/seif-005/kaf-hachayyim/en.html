Its mitzvah begins from sunrise, etc. Regarding the practice of many to sound on Rosh Hashanah the series of blasts sounded while seated before the prayer, at sunrise, as the most preferred fulfillment of the mitzvah, see Mateh Yehudah, who wrote to reject this view on the basis of the Mishnah, Gemara, and commentaries, see there. So too wrote the Kisei Eliyahu, os 1, that it is not proper to sound the shofar in the morning at all, but only after the prayer, the repetition, and the Torah reading. This was also the practice in Alexandria, and he abolished the custom of certain individuals who had been accustomed to sound the shofar in the morning, see there. So too wrote the Birkei Yosef, os 1, that according to the mystical approach as well, it is not proper to sound the shofar in the morning at all. The blasts must be sounded in the place established by the early great authorities, after the Shacharis prayer, its repetition, and the Torah reading. This is the primary ruling. It is a great principle that passive inaction is preferable, and what place is there for alacrity where objections arise according to both the plain meaning and the mystical teaching? See there. So too he wrote in his Machazik Berachah, os 1, and in his Chaim Sha'al, vol. 2, siman 10, os 3, attributing it to the eminent kabbalist, the Rashash, of blessed memory, who discussed it with him orally, see there. The Shaarei Teshuvah cited his words. So too wrote the Ruach Chaim, os 1, proving it from several Rishonim and Acharonim, see there. This is also evident from what we wrote above, siman 585, os 28, that the shofar blasts complete the drawing down of the mochin that were drawn down during the silent prayer and the repetition, see there.
