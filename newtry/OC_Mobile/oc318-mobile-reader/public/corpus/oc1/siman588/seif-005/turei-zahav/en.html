One does not blow the shofar. Because lest he take it in his hand to go to an expert to learn, and carry it four cubits in the public domain. And the Mizrachi raised the difficulty: on Yom Tov too let us decree lest he repair a musical instrument; and he dwelt on this at length and in the end answered nothing. And it appears to my humble opinion that the sound of the shofar was not given for song but for obligatory blowings; therefore they did not so decree. And further it appears to me to answer that they should not decree and uproot entirely words of Torah that commanded to blow on this day; granted on Shabbos it is not entirely uprooted, since when it is not Shabbos they blow on Yom Tov; and it appears that this is included in what they made a fence for the Torah and not to transgress words of Torah, for this would be to transgress words of Torah by uprooting them entirely. And for this reason what Beis Yosef wrote in the name of the Ran also fits: what is different about milah, which overrides Shabbos, and we do not decree lest he carry the infant four cubits in the public domain, and he answered by distinguishing between this and that with difficulty; and according to what I wrote it fits, for the Torah explicitly expanded "and on the eighth day" even on Shabbos; they did not wish to uproot explicit words of Torah because of a decree; and similarly I wrote in Yoreh De'ah, siman 117.
