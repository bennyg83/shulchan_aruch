Know that the Rambam and Rashi of blessed memory disagree on the meaning of "Mikdash" and "Medinah": for the Rambam of blessed memory holds that wherever "Mikdash" is mentioned, Jerusalem is included, and "Medinah" means the other districts, but Jerusalem is not included in "Medinah"; whereas Rashi of blessed memory holds that "Mikdash" means specifically the Temple, and "Medinah" means Jerusalem and the other districts. And I have heard difficulties raised in the name of the sages of Eretz Yisrael from that which we say in Yoma on the Mishnah that ten miracles were wrought for our ancestors in the Beis HaMikdash, and the Gemara challenges: it began with the Mikdash and concluded with Jerusalem, and according to the Rambam's view that Jerusalem is included in Mikdash, if so what is the challenge, for Jerusalem is included in Mikdash? And see Yad Aharon, who raised many further difficulties.
