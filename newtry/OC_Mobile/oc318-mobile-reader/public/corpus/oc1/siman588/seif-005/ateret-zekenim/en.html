Rosh Hashanah falling on Shabbat, etc. — even though Shabbat is not a time for tekiah, it is permitted to tell a minor who has not reached chinuch to blow on Shabbat in order to learn to blow. And some in our time forbid blowing for every minor on Shabbat altogether, even Shabbat of Rosh Hashanah — these words were said only when tekiah of shofar pushed aside Shabbat (Ran in name of Radba and Razah).
