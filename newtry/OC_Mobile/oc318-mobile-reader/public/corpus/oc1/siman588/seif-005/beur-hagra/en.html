Se'if 5, "Yom Tov etc." For from what is written "beis din," even though it was occasional; nevertheless we require a beis din of semuchim in Eretz Yisrael. Rambam and the rest of the poskim. And Rashi, the Rosh, and the Tur wrote that we require a beis din of twenty-three; and all of them disputed the Rif, who explains [it as] any outstanding beis din (and see the Rosh).
And it is forbidden etc. See Tosafos of Shabbos 36, s.v. "ha R' Yehuda etc." and s.v. "ha R' Shimon etc." And the practical difference is that this law is according to everyone; both according to the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch in siman 495, se'if 4, and according to the ruling of the Rav [Rama] there.
