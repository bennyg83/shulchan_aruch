See the Taz: apparently his answer is one with what the Levush wrote, namely, wherever the Torah explicitly expanded that one must do this thing, the Sages have no power to uproot it even by passive abstinence, though they do have power [in general]. "and you shall keep My charge"; see Yevamos 90b, where the opposite was not stated explicitly in the verse. And in a derush we wrote what is said in Rosh Hashanah 29b and Sukkah 42 and Megillah 4. "lest he carry it"; and on Yom Tov carrying is not relevant, for it is permitted; however, according to Beis Shammai, who do not hold of "mitoch" (Beitzah 12a), carrying four cubits in the public domain on Yom Tov is forbidden like on Shabbos, so how can one decree only for Shabbos when the decree applies on Yom Tov as well? And it is forced to say [the difference is] Shabbos [involves] kareis etc. and it is forced to say that Beis Shammai dispute an anonymous Mishnah that on Shabbos they blow, and that [the Gemara] would not omit saying so; and to say that carrying four cubits is a law given to Moshe at Sinai on Yom Tov; perhaps it was not said, and it is not included in "you shall not do work": first, the Ran on Shabbos, chapter 23, [regarding] the fathers [of melachah] (forty less one) explained that carrying is included in hotza'ah; and further, according to Rashi who explained that likewise the decree of hotza'ah is also difficult; and according to Tosafos it fits, for hotza'ah has a distinctive mark, or as the Kesef Mishneh; they are careful about hotza'ah explicitly. And one must say that Beis Shammai in a place of Beis Hillel is not a Mishnah, and [the Tanna] did not trouble to say that according to Beis Shammai they blow; but in the Yerushalmi Beitzah, that se'or is [forbidden] in the size of an olive and likewise chametz for eating, for if not so, let him teach thirty-six [cases of] kareis according to Beis Shammai; and see the Gaon who explained there why he needed to say regarding Rosh Hashanah that some say.. come and hear: regarding Shabbos it is not written "one says"; and see Penei Yehoshua, and this is not its place here.
