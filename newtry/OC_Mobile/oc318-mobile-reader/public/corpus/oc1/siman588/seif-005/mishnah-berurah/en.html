(13) One does not blow the shofar. A decree lest he take it in his hand to go to an expert to learn, and carry it four cubits in the public domain.
(14) And it is forbidden to carry it, For it is a utensil whose [primary] use is for a forbidden purpose.
(15) For the need of its body. That he wants to draw water with it and the like; and specifically on Shabbos it is permitted, for since they do not blow, it was not set aside for its mitzvah, which is not so on Yom Tov: even though there is no prohibition of carrying a shofar even all day [and as below in siman 596 in the Taz, s.k. 2], nevertheless to use it is forbidden, for it was set aside for its mitzvah, as regarding an esrog (see siman 665).
