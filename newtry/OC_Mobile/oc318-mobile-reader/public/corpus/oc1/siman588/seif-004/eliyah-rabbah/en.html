[4] [Levush] "And milah when it falls on Shabbos etc." And one cannot say that milah is different because there is kareis, for there is kareis only when one does not circumcise himself, but for the father there is only a positive commandment; furthermore one may say: the sprinkling on Erev Pesach that falls on Shabbos proves [the point], for there is kareis, and even so we say in the chapter "These are the things" that it does not override [Shabbos]; and see siman 659 what I found difficult on this.
