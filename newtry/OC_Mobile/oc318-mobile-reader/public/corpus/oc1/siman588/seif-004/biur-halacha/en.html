One tekiah discharges both of them. (See the Mishnah Berurah what is written: specifically the entire tekiah etc.) that is from the words of the Taz, namely where he prolonged for each one the measure of a tekiah; and it is implied from what is not written that the words of the Taz apply to the first view there in siman 587; but according to the second view, that we do not require the entire tekiah, they fulfill with this. And in my humble opinion, from the language of the Taz it is implied that his view is that needing to split the tekiah for two is worse; and as is stated there in the sugya regarding one person; that even if we say that the beginning of the tekiah alone or its end alone suffices, nevertheless where one needs to split it for two it is worse; so too in our matter with two people, where one needs to split it for two it does not help. However, in the Sefer Nahar Shalom he disputes the Taz, and his view is that even according to the first view there one fulfills with this, for they disagree at the end of the preceding siman only where its beginning was not under obligation; but if all of it is under obligation, according to everyone he has fulfilled; and this is not related to "we do not split tekios," which was said only for one person, that it does not count for him as two tekios since he became obligated in interrupted sounds (see there); and this requires further study.
