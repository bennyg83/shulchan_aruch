(12) Both of them (Specifically, we can use the entire tekiah for this one and for that one; but to interrupt a single tekiah) half of it for this one and half for that one, for example he blew a long tekiah that contains the measure of two tekios, and one intended to fulfill from its beginning until its half and the second from its half until its end; that does not help at all, for we do not split it into halves; and see above in siman 587 in the Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 14.
