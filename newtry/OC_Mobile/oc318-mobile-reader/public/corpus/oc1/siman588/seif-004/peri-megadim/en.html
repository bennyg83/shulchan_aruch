Tekiah. See the Taz: that is, even if he heard half a tekiah and it contains the measure of a tekiah; and see siman 587; there its beginning was with disqualification, and it teaches us that even regarding the entire tekiah, its beginning and end are with validity; and if one heard a portion and it has the measure, and likewise the second heard a portion and it has the measure; and likewise if one intended through half and the second intended from its half until the end and there is the measure; we do not split a tekiah [into halves]; each one must intend from beginning to end, even if it is very long.
