(s.k. 4) And if so, let us say it is forbidden because of all of this; and in my humble opinion, it is not necessary [only] according to the view of the poskim in siman 338 that nowadays, when we are not expert in musical instruments, if so one may well say there is a practical difference in the reason of "lest he carry it" where they are not expert; rather, even according to the other poskim, they wrote there that in a place of mitzvah (for example to dance on Simchas Torah) it is permitted; if so, all the more so here one should permit, and we would not set aside a Torah mitzvah were it not for the reason of "lest he carry it." And it is possible to say that specifically regarding clapping and dancing those poskim hold to permit nowadays, or according to others in a place of mitzvah; which is not so here, where there is concern lest one repair the shofar itself; certainly one should decree; and for this reason it is forbidden to play a musical instrument even in a place of mitzvah, since there is concern lest they repair it; and see Magen Avraham at the beginning of siman 338.
