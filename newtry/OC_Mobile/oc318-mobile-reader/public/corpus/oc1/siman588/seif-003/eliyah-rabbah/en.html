[3] And it is forbidden to carry it etc. I wonder: do we not hold like R' Shimon that muktzeh is permitted? Responsa of the Maharshal, siman 40. And it is astonishing at his wonder: for Tosafos wrote on Shabbos, folios 35-36, that R' Shimon agrees that it is forbidden [to move] from sun to shade, and he permitted only for the need of its body or its place; and so wrote Sefer HaTerumos, siman 254, and Hagahos Asheri at the end of the chapter "With what may one kindle," and Semak, siman 281. Magen Avraham wrote: and on Yom Tov it is forbidden to use it at all, as is written in siman 665, for it was set aside for its mitzvah; and see siman 21.
