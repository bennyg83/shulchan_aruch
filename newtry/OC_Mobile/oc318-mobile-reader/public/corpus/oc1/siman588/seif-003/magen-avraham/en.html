And some say specifically. And if he heard a sound that is appropriate for that series twice (for example tekiah and teruah, and teruah and tekiah) he has fulfilled, for it is no worse than interruption by waiting; and specifically regarding one who blows himself we rule below in siman 590, se'if 8, that he has not fulfilled, since he performs an act (which is not so regarding the hearer, who performs no act at all but only heard, and he has fulfilled) end quote of the Levush. And so it appears to me, for Rabbeinu Tam and the Rashba validate even a blower who blew another sound for the sake of the mitzvah, as the Ran wrote; and if so, it is enough for us to be stringent regarding one who hears another sound, but regarding that [same] sound he has fulfilled. Nevertheless, in the Ran it is implied that he holds there is no distinction between hearer and blower, for if not so he raises no challenge at all (see there).
