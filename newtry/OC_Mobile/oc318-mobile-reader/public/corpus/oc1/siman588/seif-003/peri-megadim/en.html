He sets his mind. See the Taz: because it is dear to him; and see in Hilchos Rosh Chodesh regarding Hallel similarly; and see Beis Yosef. And from one person (shofar and trumpets) anything that is not dear to him, it is obvious that [two sounds] are not distinguished; and when it is dear, one may say that from one person they are not distinguished, but from two people they are distinguished.
