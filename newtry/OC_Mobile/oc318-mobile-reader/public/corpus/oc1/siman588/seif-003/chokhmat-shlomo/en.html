(There seif 5) Yom Tov of Rosh Hashanah falling on Shabbat — do not blow shofar. Note: I questioned if Rosh Hashanah fell on Shabbat and passed, or one forgot and blew one or two tekiot without finishing the third — should he finish the third? Perhaps Chazal established their decree only where the mitzvah still applies, i.e. on Yom Tov; then keeping their enactment is proper and uprooting Torah words is avoided. But here he already violated their positive command by standing and acting, already blew — if he blows more he still violates their words while performing Torah tekiot; if he stops he failed the mitzvah entirely and only violated rabbinic law without gain, since each tekiah needs plain before and after. Perhaps Chazal did not establish their decree here since rabbinic enactment was not fulfilled anyway. I wanted to resolve from Beitzah 21 regarding Festival lambs slaughtered not for their purpose or before their time — if Shabbat do not cast blood, if cast b'dieved may burn fats at night; if he does not cast he creates Torah violation retroactively yet they upheld not casting on Shabbat; if he casts he fulfills offering. Still can distinguish: there even if offering kosher he violated Torah by offering initially forbidden and desecrated Shabbat; there not obligation of the day. Here if he blows properly he fulfills Torah shofar without Torah violation, only overriding rabbinic decree — perhaps Chazal did not establish decree here; requires study for practical ruling. Reason for not blowing on Shabbat per aggadah I wrote in Ki Tavo in my Torah composition 1797; see Taz on explicit permission; Tosafot if not — for his words must say why no decree lest repair instrument since cannot repair completely — if permit Yom Tov not Shabbat inconsistent.
