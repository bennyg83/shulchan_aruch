In Machazik Beracha he further wrote in his responsa Chaim Shaul, volume 2, siman 10, letter 3, regarding the custom at Rosh Hashanah tekios to blow at sunrise: I wrote in the Sefer Machazik Beracha and in the pamphlet Moreh BaEtzba to cancel this custom, even though they wrote that the Maharbach and his teacher the Ramaz practiced thus; and in the writings of the Ramaz he wrote that there is room for this according to the sod; nevertheless I am not concerned with secrets that the Arizal did not reveal; and also the outstanding kabbalist Moreinu HaRav Rabbi Shalom Sharabi. I heard from his holy mouth that it should be cancelled; and so he acted and cancelled it in the great beis midrash of those who follow the practice according to the Arizal.
