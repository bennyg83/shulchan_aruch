And if one blew after the column of dawn rose he fulfilled — see above siman 58 in Biur Halacha that per Gra and several Acharonim the column of dawn is called when the face of the east brightens and not before; and proof for their view from what is stated in Taanis daf 12: until when may one eat and drink — until the column of dawn rises, the words of Rabbi; and in Tosefta chapter 1 and Yerushalmi halachah 4 it is stated in the name of Rabbi until the east brightens — thus they are one.
