Taz, s.k. 1. A doubtful day. Even though one can imagine that he heard part before sunrise (which is a doubt of night) and part after sunrise, and did not fulfill out of doubt; nevertheless [the Taz] wanted to teach us that he certainly did not fulfill and must blow again with a blessing; and this is impossible unless he blew from before dawn until after dawn, according to what he wrote at first that he fulfills after the fact. And what the Taz wrote, that it is impossible that one would do this etc. he wrote that for extra clarity of the matter; for even if it were possible to make such a long tekiah, nevertheless you would not find a case needing to blow afterward with a blessing, according to the second view in the preceding siman: that if there is the measure of a tekiah within the obligation, he has fulfilled, and this is easy.
