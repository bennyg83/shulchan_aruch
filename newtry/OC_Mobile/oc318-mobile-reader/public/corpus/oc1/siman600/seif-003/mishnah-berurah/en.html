(ב) And some say to say it, etc. — meaning even without a new garment and new fruit, because this day they do not fulfill with yesterday's tekiyah and we do not say it is one long day; the same applies to the blessing on tekiyah. Nevertheless l'chatchila it is good that the blower wear a new garment at the shofar [Magen Avraham and Eshel Avraham]. See in Beit Yosef that in their place they practiced not to bless Shehecheyanu on the shofar on day two; but we practice like Rama. A community that sent to another city to bring them a shofar and the messenger was delayed until the end of day two — and Rosh Hashanah was on Thursday and Friday and Shabbat — when he came with the shofar they had already prayed Shabbat prayer; but it was still a "great day" — if there is no expert blower there who did not yet accept Shabbat, then one who accepted Shabbat may still blow without a blessing and blow only tashrat tashat terat and no more; specifically if the day is still great; but if already at twilight one may not blow at all, for on the second day there is no obligation to blow except d'rabbanan and it is a safek d'rabbanan; but on day one the obligation is d'oraisa — one must blow, and without a blessing. Regarding telling a gentile — even at twilight of day one it is permitted.
