If the 1st day (of R'H) falls on Shabbos, we say Sheychianu on the Shofar during the 2nd day (as the first day it is forbidden to blow). {Rama: Rema: Yet, there are those that say to say (the Sheychianu blessing on the 2nd day) even if the 1st day is on a weekday. So is the custom in our countries [Haagos Maimoni, Laws of the Shofar].}
