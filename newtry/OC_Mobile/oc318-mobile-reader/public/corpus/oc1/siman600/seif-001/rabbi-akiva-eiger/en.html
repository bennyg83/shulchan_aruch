Taz s.k. 2 — and we hold no oath takes effect to transgress mitzvot. This is not so, for we hold above siman 485 that an oath takes effect to nullify a mitzva in a general sense; likewise accepting Shabbat is general for all melacha. Also here on the second day of Rosh Hashanah tekiyah is only d'rabbanan and an oath takes effect on it as stated in Yoreh Deah siman 239 seif 6.
