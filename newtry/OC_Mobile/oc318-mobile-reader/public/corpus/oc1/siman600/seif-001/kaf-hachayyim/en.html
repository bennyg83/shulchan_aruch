1) [Seif 1] An egg laid on the first day of Yom Tov, etc. It is forbidden on the second day. Although on other Yamim Tovim it is permitted on the second day, here it is forbidden because both days are regarded as one sanctity. Tur. Levush. Taz, s.k. 1. The fact that they are regarded as one sanctity applies specifically as a stringency and not as a leniency, as written above, siman 503, os 10, see there.
