A new garment. This requires analysis, for in siman 223, seif 4, he ruled that one should recite the blessing at the time of acquisition. One can say that there it speaks of one who bought a finished garment; then the joy is when he buys it. See siman 122, seif 6. If he has new grape juice, he should recite Kiddush over old wine, and when he reaches Shehecheyanu he should take the grape juice in his hand; Darkei Moshe, Minhagim. Although one may recite Kiddush over grape juice, as written in siman 272, seif 2, nevertheless old wine is the preferred mitzvah. See siman 225, seif 5. Mateh Moshe wrote that he need not take it in his hand, but should set his eyes on it.
