In Kiddush, etc. In order to remove himself from dispute, because some say that Shehecheyanu is not said, since both days are one sanctity and he already recited Shehecheyanu on the first day.
A new garment. Although in siman 223, seif 4, he ruled that one should recite the blessing at the time of acquisition, there it speaks of one who bought a finished garment; then the joy is when he buys it. Here it speaks of one who bought a piece of cloth and gave it to a craftsman to prepare it; then the joy is when he uses it.
A new fruit. In countries where grapes are not common and are brought from other places, and generally they are unripe, one should not take them, for there is a doubt about the blessing, since he does not know whether they reached the stage of the white bean, when the blessing over them is Borei Pri HaAdamah. Also, they are sour, and one should not eat sour things on Rosh Hashanah; and fruit that has not ripened is also not a good sign; Mateh Ephraim. In Maaseh Rav he wrote that one should not eat grapes on Rosh Hashanah, and the reason is according to sod. If he has new grape juice, he should recite Kiddush over old wine, for that is the preferred mitzvah, and when he reaches Shehecheyanu he should take the grape juice in his hand or set his eyes upon it; later authorities. They further wrote that the same applies to a woman when reciting the blessing over candle lighting on the second day: she should wear a new garment or place a new fruit, and then she can recite Shehecheyanu.
Nevertheless he should say Shehecheyanu. For although it is one sanctity, nevertheless they are two days. Some err and take a new fruit even on other Yamim Tovim, and that is an error.
