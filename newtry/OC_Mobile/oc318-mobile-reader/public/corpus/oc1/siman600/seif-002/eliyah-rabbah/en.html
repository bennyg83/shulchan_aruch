An egg that was laid, etc. If there is doubt whether it was laid on the first day, it is permitted on the second; Kenesses HaGedolah, siman 513. This is not implied by all the poskim in seif 6 there, who wrote that it is forbidden to buy from non-Jews on the second night of Rosh Hashanah. It is forced to say that they speak only where it is known to be from that day, for not one of the poskim would have failed to explain this. However, non-Jews who speak incidentally and say that it was born on a weekday are believed even on the first day of Rosh Hashanah; and in siman 513, seif 6, I wrote at length.
