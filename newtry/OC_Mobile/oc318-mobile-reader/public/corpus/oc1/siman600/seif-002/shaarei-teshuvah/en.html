New. See Baer Heitev. Shevus Yaakov, part 3, wrote that on the second Yom Tov of Rosh Hashanah, when one has no new fruit, one may take new wine in order to recite Shehecheyanu over it, even though he already recited a blessing over grapes that he ate before Rosh Hashanah. Although during the rest of the year one should not recite Shehecheyanu over new wine if he ate grapes, because there are doubts about the matter, as written in Magen Avraham, siman 225, nevertheless on the second night of Rosh Hashanah, when the view of all the poskim is that one recites Shehecheyanu, except that initially we practice to have a new fruit, in this case he properly fulfills it with new wine, for it is like a full double doubt. So it is also implied from the Magen Avraham, who himself copies in siman 600 the words of the Darkei Moshe to recite the blessing over new grape juice, implying even if he already ate grapes; see there. See Eliyah Rabbah, that the same applies to women in the blessing for candle lighting: either to wear a new garment and intend to fulfill the blessing of Shehecheyanu, or to place the fruit that he will later set down at Kiddush; see there. The same applies, all the more so, to another new fruit.
