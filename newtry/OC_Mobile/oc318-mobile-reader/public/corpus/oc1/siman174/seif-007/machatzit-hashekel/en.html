Seif katan 7: And they hand him. Meaning, after he recited the blessing, and so forth. Magen Avraham writes in siman 179, seif katan 6, that even if he has not yet drunk the entire first cup, and so forth; see there. If, however, several cups were handed to him before he recited the blessing, all are certainly exempted by one blessing. Taz writes that nowadays, when the custom is for many people to hand him cups, he need not recite another blessing over a later cup, because he certainly had all of them in mind.
