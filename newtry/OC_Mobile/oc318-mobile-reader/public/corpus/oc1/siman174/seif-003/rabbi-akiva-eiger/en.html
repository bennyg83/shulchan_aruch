Seif 7. And he should recite the blessing with that intention. If, after he recited the blessing over the cup, the cup spilled, he cannot simply bring another cup, because what should he do regarding the blessing? Perhaps bread exempts beverages drunk during the meal, and the first blessing never took effect at all, so he must recite a blessing over the second cup. Or perhaps bread does not exempt them, the blessing did take effect on those beverages, and he need not recite another blessing over the second cup. This is written in Tosafos Shabbos at the end of siman 271. See Machatzis HaShekel there, who disagrees and rules that he should drink the second cup without a blessing.
