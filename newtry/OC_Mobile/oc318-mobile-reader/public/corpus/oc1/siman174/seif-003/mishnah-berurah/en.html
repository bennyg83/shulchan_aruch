(5) He sets it aside, etc. Even according to the view that Bircas HaMazon does not require a cup of wine, it is nevertheless the preferred manner to recite it over a cup when possible, as explained below in siman 182, seif 1. Nevertheless, if he is thirsty and has no other beverage, it is better that he drink beforehand, thereby also satisfying the view of Mordechai, cited by Rama below in siman 197, seif 4, who holds that when one is thirsty he is not obligated by Torah law to recite Bircas HaMazon until he drinks.
