He leaves it after meal—in teshuvat Rashba siman 342: per Beit Hillel hamotzi does not require cup, permitted to drink before birkat hamazon; Beit Shammai hold obligated to drink before, for v'she'asa zeh shetiyah etc.; Yerushalmi explains another reason; Mechaber wrote here since poskim dispute as siman 182, if he has cup better not drink, Rav Yosef Karo per his view wrote siman 197 no concern for drinking; but per Rama there not obligated d'oraisa to bless if thirsty and did not drink—here too better to drink, especially since nowadays practice per those who hold no cup required; nevertheless if not so thirsty obviously obligated to leave for birkat hamazon.
