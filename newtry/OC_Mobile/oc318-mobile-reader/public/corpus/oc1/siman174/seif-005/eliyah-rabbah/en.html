[5] Levush: And he should not recite a blessing, etc. It appears to me that, because of the doubt, he likewise should not recite an after-blessing before the meal, unless we say that the blessing Me’ein Shalosh over wine is D’Oraisa. This requires study. Bach appears to rule leniently, and the same is implied by Taz and Magen Avraham in siman 299. If, however, he specifically intends that the blessing not exempt the later wine, he certainly must recite an after-blessing before the meal. Taz explains below that “one who recites Havdalah at the table” excludes a case where he recites Havdalah in another room.
