Of kiddush per Taz—for kiddush only where meal, connected to meal and exempts during meal; havdalah although not for meal exempts; Beit Yosef and poskim teach only wine before meal considered comes to open bowels and drink exempts during and after meal; but kiddush wine not for drinking one might say does not teach—on contrary mitzvah more important, Taz intent; regarding final blessing siman 299 some hold before netilat yadayim does not exempt during meal—bless final blessing; but for us doubt in blessings to be lenient, no final blessing on havdalah, exempts wine during meal.
