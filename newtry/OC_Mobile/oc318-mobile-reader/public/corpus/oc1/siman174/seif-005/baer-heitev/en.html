Other. Taz writes that it appears obvious that the same applies to eating. For example, if one bought a single loaf for himself intending to eat it all, recited the blessing with that intention, and afterward desired to eat more and sent someone to buy another loaf, he must recite HaMotzi again. But if he has bread at home, cuts himself a piece to eat, and afterward wishes to cut another piece, this is not considered a change of mind, for that is the ordinary manner of people. Taz. See siman 206, seif 5.
Changed his mind. Nowadays, when the custom is that if someone joins people who are already drinking they give him many cups, it is presumed that when he recited the blessing over the first cup he intended to cover everything else they would give him. Taz; see there.
