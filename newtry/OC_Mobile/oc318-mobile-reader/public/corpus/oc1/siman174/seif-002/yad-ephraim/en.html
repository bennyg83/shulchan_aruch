What he writes, “and so is implied by siman 474,” refers to what is written there concerning the first cups, in his comments on the Rif. I saw that Dagul Merevavah asks that Havdalah is comparable to the second cup on Pesach; see there. In truth, the matter is well explained according to what Semag writes, as cited by Bach here, that on Pesach night, when one is obligated to establish himself to drink four cups, this constitutes a full establishment. Therefore, the second cup, over which he recites a blessing before washing, exempts the wine drunk during the meal. See there.
