Even. See Taz. Darchei Moshe, os 4, inclines this way, based on what the Tur writes in siman 213, that nowadays there is likewise a fixed gathering for drinking. It is therefore comparable to a meal. Consequently, even if the beverages were not before him when he recited the blessing over the wine, they require neither an initial nor an after-blessing, even outside a meal. See Magen Avraham, siman 208, seif katan 24; with Hashem's help, this will be explained there.
