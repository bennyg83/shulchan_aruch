Seif 2. Wine exempts. See Batei Kehunah, part 2, Beis Din, siman 2, at length. He concludes that this applies specifically when one drinks wine as an ordinary beverage because he wishes to drink wine. If, however, he drinks it only as the cup accompanying a mitzvah blessing, such as the cup of Bircas HaMazon and the like, it does not exempt other wine. See Magen Avraham at the end of seif katan 11.
Seif 4. Not to exempt. See Magen Avraham, siman 639, seif katan 16, and Tevuos Shor, siman 19, seif katan 13.
