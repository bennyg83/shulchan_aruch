And it does not. See Taz. Tosafos, Berachos 41b, s.v. If so, discusses Kiddush and the blessing of betrothal, and some say Havdalah as well. Even wine that is invalid for Kiddush, as in siman 272, seif 1, is fit for the blessing Borei Peri HaGafen, since Chazal did not distinguish. See Tosafos there for additional reasons. Some say that because wine changed for the better, one recites Borei Peri HaGafen over it. This is difficult, for porridge likewise changes from Borei Peri HaAdamah to Borei Minei Mezonos, so why does bread exempt it? We discussed this in our novellae.
