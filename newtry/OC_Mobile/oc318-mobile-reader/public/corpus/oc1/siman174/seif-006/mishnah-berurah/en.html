(23) One should not recite an after-blessing, etc., because Bircas HaMazon exempts it. It has the status of foods that come because of the meal and are exempted by Bircas HaMazon, as explained in siman 177. Wine is considered to come because of the meal, since it settles the food. Although one recites a first blessing over wine, as explained above in seif 1, no separate after-blessing is required.
(24) Before the meal. Since such wine comes to open the digestive organs and stimulate a person’s appetite, it too is considered something that comes because of the meal. Obviously, this applies only when he drinks it at least close to the meal, so that it is considered the beginning of the meal. Otherwise, it does not.
(25) And even if he did not, etc. This is the correct reading. Meaning, although in that case it cannot be said that this wine forms one continuous act of drinking with the wine during the meal, it is nevertheless exempt because it itself is included in the meal, as explained above. Regarding water and other beverages drunk before the meal when he does not drink them during the meal, many later authorities hold that he must recite an after-blessing over them. Bircas HaMazon does not exempt them, since having been drunk before Hamotzi they are not connected to the meal at all. Liquor is an exception and is comparable to wine because it stimulates the appetite. See Biur Halachah.
(26) See also the end of siman 272. Seif 7, concerning Kiddush, where it is explained that Bircas HaMazon exempts that wine as well. See Biur Halachah for our discussion of this matter.
(27) Regarding Havdalah wine. Meaning, Kiddush and Havdalah wine do not come for the needs of the meal. See there, where we wrote that if he did not recite the after-blessing before the meal, he should no longer recite it, but should allow Bircas HaMazon to exempt it. Regarding whether Bircas HaMazon exempts wine drunk after the meal ended, the poskim disagree. Some say that Bircas HaMazon does not ordinarily exempt it, and he must recite Al HaGefen unless he explicitly intended in Bircas HaMazon to exempt the wine, in which case he is exempt after the fact, as explained in siman 208, seif 17. Others say that no after-blessing is required, and Bircas HaMazon ordinarily exempts it even initially. This issue is uncommon among us, because, as explained below in siman 177, we do not withdraw from bread until Bircas HaMazon. Everything is therefore considered part of the meal, and Bircas HaMazon exempts it. Magen Avraham. Nevertheless, ideally it is preferable to have this intention when reciting Bircas HaMazon.
