Before the meal. Since it comes to open the intestines and draw a person toward an appetite for the food, it is considered to come because of the meal. Beit Yosef; Rosh. Moreover, wine provides sustenance. See above, siman 471. Bread also exempts wine after the meal from a concluding blessing, since wine provides sustenance. The Gemara asks why one should not recite the three blessings of Bircas HaMazon over wine, and answers that people do not ordinarily establish their meals upon it. This shows that were it ordinarily used as the basis of a meal, one would recite the three blessings over it. Accordingly, when one drinks wine within a fixed meal, since the meal is fixed with respect to eating, it is also fixed with respect to drinking. Beit Yosef; Rashba; Rabbeinu Yonah. It follows that one must recite a concluding blessing over other beverages consumed after the meal, for they are no better than foods that come because of the meal but are eaten after the meal, as explained in siman 177. This is certainly so according to those who hold that even beverages during the meal require a blessing, as stated in seif 7. Hagahos Maimoniyos at the end of chapter 4 explicitly includes them with other foods. The Rosh likewise implies that bread exempts only beverages that come during the meal. Tosafos in Pesachim 113a also imply that only wine does not require a concluding blessing, and their intent is as explained above in the name of Rashba. This law, however, is not common among us, as explained in siman 177. Shiltei HaGibborim writes that anise confections eaten after the meal to sweeten the drink are like radish and olives, and the blessing over the wine exempts them. It appears to me that with other beverages drunk during the meal, the blessing over bread exempts them, because the beverage is subordinate to the bread, and the anise confection is subordinate to the beverage. This is similar to Tosafos’ statement that when one recited a blessing over the fruits of Ginosar, it exempted the salted food and the bread, because the bread was subordinate to the salted food and the salted food was subordinate to the fruit.
