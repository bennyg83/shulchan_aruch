6) There: Wine covers, etc. See Responsa Chasam Sofer, siman 47, who inclines to say that even something subordinate to a beverage requires no blessing. For example, if after drinking wine one drinks coffee and wishes to eat a little sugar to sweeten it, he need not recite a blessing even over the sugar; see there. This does not appear correct to me. The principal law is itself disputed, for according to Mordechai, nowadays wine does not cover other beverages unless they were before him when he recited the blessing, as stated in the preceding paragraph. Although we follow the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, it is sufficient that wine covers other beverages, and not also what is subordinate to those beverages. The best course is that when eating the sugar, he should intend to eat something sweet and not merely to sweeten the coffee, and then recite a blessing over it. Alternatively, he should place it into the coffee until it dissolves and is no longer discernible, and not recite a blessing.
