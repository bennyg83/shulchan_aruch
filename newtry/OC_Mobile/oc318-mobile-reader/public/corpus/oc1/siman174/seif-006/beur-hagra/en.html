Seif 6: There is no, etc. The reason is that these are items that come because of the meal, as stated there, 41b, only regarding the first blessing, since the wine causes an appetite, etc., and as Tosafos write in Pesachim. This implies, however, that Bircas HaMazon does not exempt it. Although siman 208, seif 17, states, “but,” etc., that applies specifically if he recited a blessing over the wine itself or intended to exempt it, as Rashi writes, 41a, s.v. aval, etc., and as Rabbi Shimon states in the Yerushalmi, cited by the Rosh there, siman 28, regarding Rabbi Huna, who ate, etc. This resolves Rashba’s question. See Beit Yosef at the end of siman 208. This is the meaning of, “and regarding wine,” etc. See also Tosafos in Pesachim cited above and Tosafos there, s.v. ana, etc.
And likewise, etc. For otherwise, if he had to recite a blessing beforehand, it would not exempt the wine drunk during the meal. See Tosafos there, at the end of s.v. yedei yayin.
