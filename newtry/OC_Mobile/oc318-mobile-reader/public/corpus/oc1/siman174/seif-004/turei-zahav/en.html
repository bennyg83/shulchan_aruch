Before the meal, etc. It exempts even wine consumed after the meal but before Bircas HaMazon, because that wine is consumed as a drink, like wine before the meal. Wine consumed during the meal, by contrast, is only to soak the food and is not regarded as drinking, and therefore does not exempt wine consumed after the meal. The phrase “if he established” does not mean that without establishing himself to drink it would not exempt. Rather, it excludes a case where he did not initially intend to drink later, in which case he has changed his mind.
Of Kiddush. We should not say that Kiddush is an independent mitzvah. Havdalah presents an even greater novelty, since it does not pertain to the meal at all as Kiddush does, and nevertheless it exempts wine consumed during the meal. The same applies to wine consumed after the meal: Kiddush and Havdalah exempt it because they have the same status as other wine consumed before the meal. Regarding the after-blessing before washing, see siman 299, seif 7.
