Before. See Taz. Wine before the meal is consumed to open the intestines and is considered drinking, like wine after the meal. Wine during the meal, however, is consumed merely to soak the food. See Tosafos and Rosh, Berachos 42b. The phrase “if he established” is not exact, for even on a weekday wine is consumed during the meal. It is only regarding wine after the meal that there is a distinction between Shabbos or Yom Tov and a weekday; see Tur. This differs from wine during the meal, even though the Rosh derives that law only by an a fortiori argument. Nevertheless, this applies specifically when he drinks in one place. If he changes location, he must recite a new blessing even during the meal. See Berachos 43a, Tosafos s.v. ba, and, with Hashem's help, what I will write on Magen Avraham, seif katan 6. See also siman 178 and Tosafos, Berachos 42a, s.v. Rav Sheshes.
