1) [Seif 1] A minor eats neveilos, etc. The same applies when he commits other prohibitions, as stated in Beis Yosef, and this is the consensus of the later authorities. The same applies to Shabbos prohibitions when he acts on his own initiative. If he acts for an adult, however, one protests, as stated above in siman 334, seif 25; see there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1, and Chayei Adam, part 2, klal 66, paragraph 4, write the same.
