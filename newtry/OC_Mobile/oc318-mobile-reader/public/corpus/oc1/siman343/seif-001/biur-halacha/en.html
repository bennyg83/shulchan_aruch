From rabbinic law and likewise, etc. — Rashba and Ran's view: if the child needs it, it is permitted even to place in his hands something forbidden rabbinically; but the Mechaber ruled anonymously like those who disagree and forbid in every case; and in responsum of Rabbi Akiva Eiger regarding carrying books to synagogue without eruv via a minor — whether to rely on Rashba that rabbinic prohibition one may place in his hands — he answered this is not so, for Ran wrote explicitly in chapter 20 that even for Rashba one feeds by hand only for his need and not for our need; and on the contrary one educates by hand — there is a remedy to give the minor a chumash and siddur to carry to synagogue for his own need to pray and hear Torah reading, and automatically the adult will join him to pray together:
