(1) In any language—for it is written "and you shall bless," in any language that you bless; and specifically that he understands that language [Biur Halacha above siman 62 see there]. Bach wrote in siman 193 that all this is from the side of law, and for the mitzvah from the preferred we require specifically Hebrew. Sefer Chinuch wrote: all who are careful in birkat hamazon—his sustenance is available to him all his days in honor. The meticulous should be careful to bless l'chatchila from the book and not by heart. Sefer Chasidim wrote a story of one who died and was revealed in a dream to his relative, and said to him: every day they judge me for not being careful to bless all blessings with heart intent, etc.—see there.
