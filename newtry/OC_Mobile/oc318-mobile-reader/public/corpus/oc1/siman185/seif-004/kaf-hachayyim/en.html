4) [Seif 2] If he did not make it audible to his ears, he fulfilled his obligation. The Shulchan Aruch below, siman 206, seif 3, rules likewise, as do Levush and the later authorities. Sefer Chareidim, however, writes that he did not fulfill his obligation. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 2, Beis Menuchah, paragraph 4, and Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1, cite this. As a practical ruling, however, one must follow the Shulchan Aruch and the later authorities that after the fact he fulfilled his obligation.
