3) There: Birkas HaMazon may be recited, etc. Why do we not recite over it, 'Who has sanctified us with His mitzvos and commanded us to recite Birkas HaMazon,' as with other mitzvos? If one had to recite a blessing over the blessing, saying, 'Who has sanctified us to recite a blessing,' he would likewise require a third blessing over the second, saying, 'Who has sanctified us with His mitzvos and commanded us to recite a blessing over the blessing,' and similarly a fourth and fifth without end. Therefore, no blessing was instituted over any blessing in the world. Beis David, siman 83. In my humble opinion, the reason appears to be that a blessing gives praise to Hashem, may He be exalted, for having privileged us with this mitzvah. This applies only to a mitzvah that is not itself a blessing. Regarding a mitzvah that is itself a blessing, this is inapplicable. Therefore, no blessing was instituted over it.
