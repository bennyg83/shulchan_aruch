(2) With his lips—but if he thought in his heart he did not fulfill. And if due to illness or other ones he blessed birkat hamazon in his heart he fulfilled [Magen Avraham]; and see above siman 62 in Mishna Berurah s.k.7 that we clarified that this "fulfilled" is not completely—for we hold thought is not like speech; rather meaning that at least when he cannot bless he should think in his heart and HQBH will establish reward for him; but essentially he does not fulfill; therefore when the ones passes, if the food is not yet digested he should bless birkat hamazon; and see what we wrote there in Mishna Berurah and Bach in the entire seif, for all applies here.
