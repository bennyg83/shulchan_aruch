If Taz. And in Magen Avraham letter 3 he settles this, and the Taz himself settled it with difficulty. Know that in siman 76, Magen Avraham 13—we were uncertain regarding urine certainly whether he returns in tefillah and shema; now I saw Mahari, may his light shine, in Sefer Lechem Yehudah in Laws Shema s.63 yod-dalet in the name of the Early Authorities see there, and he wrote that urine in tefillah certainly he returns, and in other blessings they are uncertain—see there. See there: if he blessed and cannot stand on himself the measure of walking a parsa—there is room to doubt whether it is like shema and tefillah or not; siman 62 implies that for shema, when he knew l'chatchila he returns—see there. And behold, if we say urine—even if certain, since rabbinically they were not stringent in birkat hamazon, therefore the same applies here to one who walks: he fulfills even though he cannot stand on himself the measure of a parsa, only rabbinically—and requires study. And one who is drunk like Lot—in other blessings he did not fulfill if he cannot speak before the king; he fulfilled; in birkat hamazon, words of Torah—he returns.

If due to ones he recited shema and tefillah and birkat hamazon in his heart—he actually fulfilled, as Magen Avraham 1 wrote; if he was in a place of feces he did not fulfill, for it is forbidden to think words of Torah in a place of feces; nevertheless "and your camp shall be holy"—siman 85; and opposite ervah thought is permitted, not opposite feces; and above I explained it, with God's help.
