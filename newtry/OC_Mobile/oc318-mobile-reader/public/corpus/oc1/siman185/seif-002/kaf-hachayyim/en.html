2) Bach writes that the ordinary letter peh does not appear in Birkas HaMazon because whoever recites Birkas HaMazon with proper intent is not ruled by wrath, fury, or anger, whose Hebrew words contain that letter. Sefer HaChinuch, parashas Eikev, writes that some say whoever is careful about Birkas HaMazon will have his sustenance available to him honorably throughout his life. This is also found in a work called Gan. Sefer Chassidim, siman 46, relates that a man died several years before his appointed time. After twelve months, he appeared to a relative in a dream. The relative asked how he was treated in the world where he now was. He replied: Every day I am judged for not having been meticulous to recite Birkas HaMazon and the blessings over fruit with heartfelt intent. They say to me, 'You directed your intent toward your enjoyment.' The relative asked: Is the judgment of the wicked not limited to twelve months, while more than twelve months have already passed for you? He replied: They do not judge him with punishments as severe as during the first twelve months. Bach; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 1; Ateres Zekeinim, paragraph 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1.
