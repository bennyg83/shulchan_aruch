9) A patient who recovered during mourning should recite HaGomel before ten but should not receive an aliyah to the Torah. Responsa R' Avraham Pala'gi, volume 3, siman 479; Kerem Shlomo; Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 5; Sedei Chemed, Asifas Dinim, section Mourning, paragraph 52. It appears that if fewer than three days of mourning remain, he should wait until completing his mourning and then recite the blessing. See below, seif 6. The blessing, 'Who performed a miracle for me,' however, should not be recited during mourning. Sedei Chemed there in the name of Chaim BeYad, siman 122; see there.
