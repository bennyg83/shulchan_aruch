(9) A sick person who recovered during his mourning has [permission] to bless ha-gomel before ten and does not go up to the Torah. Responsum Maharaf part 3 siman 479. Knesset HaGedolah on Tur Yoreh Deah letter 5, Shach Asifas Dinim Laws of Mourning letter 52 — and it appears if less than three days remain of his mourning he should wait until he completes his mourning days and then bless. And see below seif 6. However one who performed a miracle for me — he does not bless during his mourning. Shach, in the name of Sefer Chayim Beyad siman 122; see there.
