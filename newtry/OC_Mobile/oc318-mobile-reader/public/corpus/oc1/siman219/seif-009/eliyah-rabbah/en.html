(9) And he answers amen, etc. And this is Olat Tamid's language; and it seems we also require that he intend to fulfill with it and hear it from beginning to end, and the blesser should also intend to release the listener, as above siman 213 — end quote. It would seem one can distinguish: above it speaks of blessings that are not face-to-face, but here he says "Who bestowed good upon you"; and nevertheless it seems the listener must intend to fulfill with it, as I will explain.
