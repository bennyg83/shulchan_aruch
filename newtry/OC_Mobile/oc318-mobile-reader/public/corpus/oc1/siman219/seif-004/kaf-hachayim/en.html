(4) There — sea travelers when they ascend from it — that is, even if a miracle occurred to him in one place that was a place of danger and he was saved — at first he need not bless immediately but waits until he ascends from the sea; and likewise if he travels by ship and sometimes they come to port and delay there a day or days — he need not bless since he is still traveling and his intent is to return immediately to the sea. However if he delays a long time, requires study whether he blesses. Likewise Olat HaTodah letter 1, and they brought Eshel Avraham letter 1 and wrote he found written that one should not bless until he has completely left the danger, see there. And likewise Etz Yosef letter 2 — and therefore it appears even if he delays much he should not bless until he reaches his desired destination. And likewise Birkei Yosef letter 3, and in Machazik Berachah letter 5 in the name of responsum Kol Yaakov siman 3 that one who goes from place to place and on his way passes through large towns and rests there a day or days — he does not bless ha-gomel there until he goes to his desired destination, see there. And they brought in his book Lechem David siman 23 letter 5 Sha'ar HaTevah letter 1, and likewise Ma'amar Mordechai letter 1, Lev Chaim part 3 siman 54.
