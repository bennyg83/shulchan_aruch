King. In Birkei Yosef wrote Blessed God our King etc.

That rejoices in it. Implies specifically he rejoices in heart that this one was healed; but if not so happy only says so for peace — do not say so with God's name and kingship; Beit Yosef wrote some practice when wives give birth husbands stand and bless HaGomel; but Rashbatz wrote specifically for teacher applies not for other friend; and concludes should not bless; and for father and teacher all permit to bless on his healing for Rashbatz also agrees; and it seems ostensibly then he should not say Who bestows good upon the undeserving — for thereby harms honor of father or teacher — for although himself confessing says so — nevertheless another should not say so on him; see Taz.
