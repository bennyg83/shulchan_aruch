(א) Blessed is the Merciful One King etc. In Rabbenu Yonah it is written Blessed is Heaven King etc.; and he wrote further there, and it seems they can say it even with fewer than ten — end quote.

(ב) On his fellow's good whom he rejoices in. It implies specifically that he rejoices in his heart that this one was healed or saved; but if he is not so joyful in his heart but says so only for peace's sake, he should not bless with God's name and kingship for his blessing is in vain; rather he should say Blessed is the Merciful One Who has bestowed good upon us; and that which R' Yehuda exempted them from giving thanks — for they knew Rav Chana of Gadda was his faithful friend; and further from that he blessed with God's name and kingship, and Beit Yosef wrote that some practice when their wives give birth the husbands bless HaGomel, but Rashba wrote this applies specifically to one's teacher and not to another friend, and he concludes one should not bless and they rebuke the blesser. On one's father and teacher all permit blessing on his healing, for on this Rashba also agrees. And it seems ostensibly that then he should not say HaGomel good to the undeserving, for in this he impairs the honor of his father or teacher; rather one should say undeserving refers to himself confessing he is undeserving and merited that his father or teacher was healed or saved as above.
