(s.k. 4) "Another" implies he holds, etc. — not like Beit Yosef wrote; and it appears to me in Shulchan Aruch he retracted from what he wrote in his work Beit Yosef — that specifically a disciple over his teacher may bless as we find in the gemara, but not another; and even a husband on his wife who gave birth — if he blessed they rebuke him; but in Shulchan Aruch unstated — and if he wrote "if another blessed" it implies even if he is not his disciple.

And the other, etc. — before fewer than ten, so Terumat Yisrael — there is dispute, meaning it is not better than those if the sick person blessed for himself without ten, whom they dispute in seif 3.
