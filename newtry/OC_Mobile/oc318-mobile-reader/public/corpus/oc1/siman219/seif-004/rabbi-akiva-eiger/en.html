(4) And he answered amen — he fulfilled. See above siman 213 seif 2; and one must say here the law of listener like responder does not apply, for he must say in a different formula, namely Who has bestowed good upon me; but through amen which is also praise and thanks he fulfills. And Tur in name of Ra'ash wrote the reason here is different: specifically when the blesser is also obligated in the blessing, then the listener fulfills without answering amen; but here where the blesser is not obligated in this blessing, specifically answering amen is required — requires study.
