Seif 4. And he answered amen, he has fulfilled his obligation. See above, siman 213, seif 2. It must be that the principle that hearing is equivalent to responding does not apply here, because he must use a different formula, namely, “Who has bestowed good upon me.” Rather, by answering amen, which itself expresses praise and thanksgiving, he fulfills his obligation. Tur, in the name of Rosh, explains differently. Only when the person reciting the blessing is himself obligated can the listener fulfill his obligation without answering amen. Here, since the one reciting the blessing is not obligated in this blessing, the listener must specifically answer amen. This requires study.
