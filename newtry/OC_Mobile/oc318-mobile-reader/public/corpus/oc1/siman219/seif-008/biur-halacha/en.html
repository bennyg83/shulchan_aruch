Such as a wound in the hollow of the body — See what Mishna Berurah wrote regarding illness of fever; and behold the main law is brought in Eliyah Rabbah in name of Maharil, that for illness of fever one must bless — but in general on which fever? And it appears to depend on this: if he holds like the Mechaber, even for passing fever (which they call fever) he also blesses; and if like Rama's view, he does not bless except for burning fever (which they call shvayderin), as Taz wrote in siman 328 — and this is simple. And know that Taz wrote on Rama's view that the same applies if he is laid in bed more than three days, and the Achronim copied this; and it is true that this measure is mentioned in Yoreh De'ah laws of visiting the sick — see there in siman 335 regarding visiting those far away, that they do not visit except after three days, and see the Gra's explanation there that then he is called a deathly ill person. But all this is not the matter here, for per Rama's words it is specifically for a wound in the hollow of the body and the like; if so, just as for the matter of desecrating Shabbat for him in siman 328, this measure of three days of illness is not mentioned there at all, but the matter depends only on whether it is a wound in the hollow of the body or burning fever and the like, where doctors say he is dangerous — so too here. And if there is room for his reasoning, it is only per the Mechaber's method, that whoever became ill, even though there is no danger in him, nevertheless is comparable to one raised to the gallows for this — one can say all this is specifically for an ill person of three days, for then he needs to request mercy and not before this. But in truth from the Mechaber it implies that in every case it is required, since he took to bed in any event. How it will be — per the gloss's words, certainly there is no place for Taz's measure; and perhaps he wrote thus as a deciding factor — and it requires study.
