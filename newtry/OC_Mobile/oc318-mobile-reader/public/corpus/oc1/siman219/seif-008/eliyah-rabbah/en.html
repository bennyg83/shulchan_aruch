(8) And it is good to return, etc. And Bach ruled not to bless at all, because the language "need" is specifically l'chatchila; and so ruled Sefer Be'er Sheva daf 100. And in my humble opinion his proof is from the beginning of the second chapter of Gittin, "they destroy," for Bach's proof from Berakhot daf 15 and from Eruvin — in Gittin it entered his mind that it is l'chatchila, and on this the siman — and in truth it seems to me that one must interpret the two explanations in the language "need"; therefore it seems to me the main thing is as Shulchan Aruch and Levush ruled; and further I saw that Rabbeinu Yeruham ruled he has not fulfilled, and so it implies in Mordekhai and poskim.
