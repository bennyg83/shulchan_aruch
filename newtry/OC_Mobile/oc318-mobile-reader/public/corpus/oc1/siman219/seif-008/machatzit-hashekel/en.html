(s.k. 8) And so they practice, and Bach, etc. — and so ruled above siman 60 from the words "and so ruled" etc. — it was written in error and refers to above seif 5 that wrote in Shulchan Aruch "if another blessed" etc. and intended to include etc. — implies he holds mitzvot require kavanah; and on this Magen Avraham wrote so ruled the Rav above siman 60 s.k. 4 that wrote some say mitzvot do not require kavanah and some say they require kavanah, etc. and so is the law, end of his words — behold he decided like those who say mitzvot require kavanah, not like Etz Yosef who questioned that here it is plain that kavanah is required and above siman 60 he brought two views — for in truth also in siman 60 he decided kavanah is required; however Magen Avraham above siman 60 s.k. 3 wrote specifically in a d'oraisa mitzvah, but rabbinic mitzvot do not require kavanah — Radbaz, etc.; therefore Magen Avraham's intent is not to explain thus the Rav's words in Shulchan Aruch — for here it is rabbinic and nevertheless he ruled kavanah is required.
