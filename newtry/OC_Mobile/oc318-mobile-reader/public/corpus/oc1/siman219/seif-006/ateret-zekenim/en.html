And correct not etc. — for until three days he blesses — however they did not write from when he blesses; and it seems one should not bless until he fully left danger; and so sick person until fully returned to health; as Mechilta on walk outside; and so Rashi — behold all time he does not walk on his health still in danger doubt; and if so for Birkat HaGomel good is not complete all this time not finished; and perhaps in what they enacted to say Who bestowed all good — meaning good is whole not majority; and so sages said why not said it is good on second day because not finished etc. — behold language good indicates complete good finished in good and fullness (Etz Chaim in name of Maharash in Maharal).
