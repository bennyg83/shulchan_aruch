(19) If he delayed — meaning he did not bless at the time he ascended from the sea or at the time he returned to health from his illness.
(20) Three days — for until this time he is called one who has come from the road; and the Achronim wrote that even if he must because of this bless without a Torah scroll per the custom, nevertheless he should not delay more than three days — such as if he left on Monday after they already read [Torah], he blesses with ten without Torah scroll and does not wait until Thursday; and see what is written above in sk 8.
