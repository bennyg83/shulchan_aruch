(31) Only these four — because they are most common they enacted HaGomel blessing on them; unlike other cases that are not common at all — one does not bless HaGomel on them; rather for those for whom a miracle was done he blesses Who made a miracle for me when he reaches that place. And one who walked on the road even not in a place of danger and bandits came upon him to kill him and he was saved — according to all he blesses Birkat HaGomel.

(32) Without mention of shem u'malchut — and the Achronim wrote the custom is like the first view and so is reasonable.
