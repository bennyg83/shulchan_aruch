10) If a bone lodged in one's throat and he was saved, whether he must recite HaGomel was asked in Responsa Divrei Nechemyah, which concludes that the practical ruling requires consideration; see there. Sedei Chemed, Asifas Dinim, section Berachos, siman 2, paragraph 14, cites this. Therefore, he should recite it without the Name and kingship. Likewise, if one fell from a ladder and suffered pain in his limbs but was not bedridden and did not become ill, he should recite it without the Name and kingship. Rivash, siman 336; Radbaz, volume 3, siman 572; Ikarei HaDinim, siman 10, paragraph 52.
