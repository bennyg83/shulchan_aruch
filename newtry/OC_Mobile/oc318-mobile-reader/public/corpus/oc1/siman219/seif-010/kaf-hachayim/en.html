(10) If a bone was stuck in his throat and he was saved — if he must bless ha-gomel was asked on this in responsum Divrei Nechemyah and he concluded that le-ma'aseh requires study, see there. And they brought Shach in Asifas Dinim Laws of Blessings siman 2 letter 14. And therefore one should bless without shem u'malchut. And likewise one who fell from a ladder and was concerned on account of pain of limbs and did not take to bed and did not fall ill and blessed without mentioning shem u'malchut — Rivash siman 336 and Radbaz part 3 siman 472, Ikkarim HaDin Torim siman 10 letter 52.
