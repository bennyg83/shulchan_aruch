(s.k. 10) And some say, etc. — and it appears to me one who traveled on the way etc. and bandits came upon him and he was saved — he blesses; see seif 7 — meaning in combination with the view of those in seif 7 that even going from city to city one blesses, with the view of those in this seif that even if bandits came upon him in his house he blesses — if so, in such a case where bandits came upon him on the way even though he did not go in the desert, he blesses.

And now they practice, etc. — and Turei Zahav small seif 5 wrote that for illness and lying in bed three days, according to all, one must bless, for he needs request for mercy from another, as we find in Rava in Nedarim chapter Ein Bein HaModar daf 39.
