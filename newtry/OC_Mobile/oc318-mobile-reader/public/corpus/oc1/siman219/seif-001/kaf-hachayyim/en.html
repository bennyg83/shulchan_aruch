1) [Seif 1] Four must give thanks, etc. One who became obligated for all four circumstances recites only one blessing for all of them. Pri HaAretz, Orach Chaim section, siman 7; Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur. Likewise, one who was imprisoned two or three times requires only one blessing. Kol Ben Levi, siman 1; Ginas Veradim, rule 8, siman 48, writes the same.
