And if Taz — although in Gemara Berakhot 54b regarding four who need to give thanks Abaye said and one needs ten, it should read: one needs — some say b'dieved; but and one needs, ostensibly it is superfluous, for he should say Abaye said and before ten; and in the Gemara they said and two rabbis, and Tosafot wrote that even if there are no rabbis he blesses, and so Mordekhai, and the Mechaber brought; and ostensibly from that it did not say and one needs two rabbis, that two rabbis are needed b'dieved is implied — this is impossible. Abudraham wrote Tosafot's view stringently that we require twelve; and Rif and Ra'ash hold so, and Rambam and Semag hold we take the mention of rabbis leniently, therefore Shulchan Aruch omitted; and I would say the rule applies that three pillars of instruction rule like them, and words of Kabbalah are like Scripture in their doubt — see Magen Avraham from this. And it requires study.
