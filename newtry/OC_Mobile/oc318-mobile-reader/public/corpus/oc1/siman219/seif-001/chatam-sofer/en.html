(there) In the Mechaber, four need to give thanks. NB: see my responsum ch. 6 Orach Chayim letter 51; there I wrote and justified the rabbi who asked, who blessed Birkat HaGomel at night when he ascended from the sea in peace. And one wise man arose against him that one does not make this blessing at night, because it was enacted in place of a thanksgiving offering, as Ra'ash wrote in the chapter HaRo'eh, and a thanksgiving offering is not offered at night. And in his reply he distinguishes between those who descend the sea and the other [categories] who need to give thanks, and wrote that so too is proven from what David said regarding the other three who need to give thanks: "and they offered thanksgiving offerings" etc., and not regarding those who descend the sea etc. — see there. And after he expands there to clarify the entire matter, he wrote the conclusion from this, that HaGomel is not in place of a sacrifice to be specifically standing and by day; or even if it is in place of a sacrifice in any case, that is the other three; but for those who descend the sea, sacrifice is not written — see there well.
