And recovered. One who was ill with fever that is not recurrent — if a day or two he rises with interruption without illness, is he obligated to bless each time the illness ceases from him? And likewise sea travelers: if his intent is to travel some distance by ship in the heart of the sea far away, and sometimes upon reaching port he delays the ship there for days — are they obligated to bless each time they come among Jews? See Teshuvot Rashba part 1 siman 82, and in responsum Aleph Yud he raised that even if a miracle occurred to him on his journey he is not obligated to bless until he has ascended from the sea — and all the more so in the ruling mentioned — and he brought proof from the verse of sea travelers etc.: "let them rejoice when it is stilled" implies that even if a storm is stilled to calm, nevertheless they still have not given thanks to Heaven for His kindness until they arrive at their desired destination; and likewise the ruling of the sick person mentioned until it completely ceases from him. And see Sanhedrin 78, and see further in that responsum that if one's small son was ill, the father and son are exempt; and likewise in responsum Maharam Mintz siman 14 that the father is exempt — perhaps he was not elevated on account of his sin but judged on account of his end or for atonement from another gilgul; and he should bless the plain "Who bestows good to the undeserving" without mentioning "le-chayavim tovot" — has he not already written that such is the text of R' Yosef and Rosh, and it is like a mishnah from the coin the sages minted; and a small son is plainly not one obligated.

In the prison house. One who was imprisoned two or three times — does he need to bless each time? Since he was already obligated for every salvation — some say: even though they are matters of obligation, nevertheless since he pushed off once unintentionally and once intentionally until he was caught a second and third time — now one blessing suffices. See responsum Kol Ben Levi question 1, who raised that one blessing suffices, and brought support for his words from Terumat HaDeshen siman 254, and a second time for him the words of Rif in Pesachim from Ran's agreement that one only blesses one final blessing on the entire cup; and likewise it appears from Baal Ginat Veradim general 8 siman 48, see there, who brought many proofs for his words — all beloved and clear.
