To give thanks. Baer Heitev and in Lechem Todah the gaon Mahari of Essen wrote a minor blesses; and Birkei Yosef wrote that so they practiced in their Galilee — see there; and in our country they practice like Maharam Mintz and Magen Avraham, and Ben Avraham brought in general, and so in Sha'arei Ephraim section 4; and in Machatzit HaShekel wrote in name of responsum Ohel Yosef that a father on his minor son should not bless except without God's name and kingship — see there. In Shulchan Aruch and desert travelers — and Birkei Yosef wrote in name of Mahari that he asked in Sefer Kol Yaakov: one who goes from place to place and on his way passes through great towns — he should not bless until he reaches his desired district.
