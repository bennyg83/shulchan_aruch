(1) When they ascended from it — and specifically when they ascended from it completely; and this does not include one who stands with the ship when it came to port and the people on it go down to dry land for a day or two or until the time the ship goes onward — for in this one does not bless, for he has not yet been saved from danger completely; and likewise for desert travelers — on their way if they pass through some city they also do not bless.

(2) And recovered — and he already walks on his feet.

(3) And one who was imprisoned — Magen Avraham's view is that it deals specifically with imprisonment regarding capital matters, and many disagree with him; and see in Biur Halacha what we explained on this. The Achronim wrote that one who became obligated in all four does not bless except one blessing for all of them. They also wrote that a minor is not obligated to give thanks [and even from the mitzvah of chinuch]; and also women — the custom of the world is that they do not bless this blessing; and the Achronim wrote the reason: because the order of this blessing is before ten as below, and it is not a woman's honor. And some wrote that nevertheless it is proper that she bless before ten at minimum, before women and one man.
