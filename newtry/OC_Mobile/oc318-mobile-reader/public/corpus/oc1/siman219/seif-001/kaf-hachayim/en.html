(1) [Seif 1] Four must give thanks, etc. — One who became obligated in all four does not bless except one blessing for all of them. Pri Ha'aretz Orach Chayim siman 7. Yesh Omrim in Hagahot Tashbetz. And likewise one who was imprisoned in the prison house two or three times — one blessing suffices. Kol Ben Levi siman 1, and likewise Ginat Veradim general 8 siman 48.
