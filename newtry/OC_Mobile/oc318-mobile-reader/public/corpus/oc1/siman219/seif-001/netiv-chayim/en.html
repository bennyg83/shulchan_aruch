(in Magen Avraham sk 1) On capital matters. It appears to me (question): Once someone went to a town and the village the Master accused him until he was seized in iron chains; when he was released , is he obligated to bless Birkat HaGomel or not? One could say that what the Sages obligated when one leaves the prison house is specifically when seized on capital matters; and in our case the main accusation was over money (answer): he is obligated to bless. If you wish, say: regarding desert travelers the verse says "their soul wraps in them"; and for a sick person who recovered "they reach the gates of death"; and for those who descend the sea "their soul melts in evil"; but for one imprisoned in the prison house it does not mention concern for life but "sitters in darkness and shadow of death, prisoners of poverty and iron" , the plain meaning is either that they reached that they are sitters of darkness and shadow of death, or prisoners of poverty and iron; when he leaves those two evils he gives thanks to Heaven for His kindness , whether they were imprisoned for money or for life. And if you wish, say reason: for Rosh wrote in chapter 1 of Bava Batra siman 22: everything the nations innovate decrees and afflictions on Israel , even if they afflict them in suffering and prevention of food and drink , they collect everything according to money, for their main intent is on the money, end of his words; it would follow now not to bless at all ever, even when seized on capital matters, for their main intent is on the money , and this did not escape him from what the Rav Magen Avraham wrote in siman 219 specifically when seized on capital matters. * Since I saw a horned man standing against him. The first , Ran in chapter HaRo'eh who wrote, z"l: Rav Hai Gaon was asked why he did not say in the order written in the chapter of Tehillim; and he answered that he wanted to count first those more endangered , sea travelers and desert travelers are more endangered than illness, for most sick recover, and imprisoned is often imprisoned on account of money and his danger is not so great, end of his words; behold he explicitly holds one is obligated to bless the blessing of thanks even when imprisoned for money; and in the Talmud and early and late poskim they did not distinguish thus except the Rav Magen Avraham remained alone; and had he not seen the words of Ran he would not have filled his heart to dispute; therefore it appears to me to rule the law that one is obligated to bless the blessing of thanks even when seized on account of monetary accusations; and so the people say , so it appeared to me, I wrote.
