(s.k. 1) Minor, etc. In this way he wondered etc.: behold b'dieved it suffices without ten; and the meaning is: since women cannot bless with ten on account of immodesty, it is like b'dieved and she blesses without ten. But in truth for us it is no difficulty, for the Rav in seif 3 did not decide whether he fulfilled if he blessed not before ten: and therefore ruled he should repeat and bless before ten without shem u'malchut; and if so, even if you say women are like b'dieved, how can we tell them to bless without ten: perhaps we hold he has not fulfilled and their blessing is in vain? Perforce one must say the Rav decided like those who say he fulfills b'dieved if he blessed without ten; and Magen Avraham who brought them did not bring them except to write on it; and it is possible etc. that these blessings are optional: since in these lands one must say the poskim hold that he fulfills if he blessed without ten, nevertheless women do not practice blessing: from this it appears plausible to him these blessings are optional; and if so, for us there is no need to say so; nevertheless we do not innovate dispute, and we also say for us it may be said they are optional; and it is possible also for us it is compelled they are optional; and proof from women: for we hold in seif 4 that if another blessed, he too fulfills; and Magen Avraham wrote that that other need not bless with ten: if so, still a difficulty regarding women: her husband should bless who does not need ten and nevertheless she fulfills: perforce one holds these blessings are optional.

(s.k. 1) Imprisoned. On matters of his life: and wrote in Eshel Avraham, and such are the words of Terumat Yisrael: the word implies even on account of money, holiday garments, not like Magen Avraham, end of his words; and likewise in Netiv Chayim at length, and such are the words: Terumat Yisrael asked Rav Hai Gaon why we did not say in order as written in the chapter of Tehillim, and he answered he wanted to count first those more endangered: sea travelers and desert travelers are more endangered than illness, for most sick recover, and imprisoned is often imprisoned on account of money and his danger is not so great, end of his words; and wrote in Netiv Chayim mentioned that had Magen Avraham not seen the words of Terumat Yisrael mentioned he would not have filled his heart to dispute, end of his words; and in my humble opinion Magen Avraham saw the words of Terumat Yisrael and does not dispute them as Rosh wrote chapter 1 of Bava Batra, they also brought in Netiv Chayim, and such is ruled in Choshen Mishpat; and likewise Rosh in responsum: everything the nations innovate decrees and afflictions on Israel, even in the matter of that suffering etc.: they collect everything according to money, for their main intent is on money, end of his words; and if so, what Rav Hai Gaon said that imprisoned is often imprisoned on account of money and his danger is not so great: behold he did not write on matters of money but on account of money; and the meaning is as Rosh said. And behold Rosh's intent regarding tax: for sometimes in truth the nation's intent is literally for souls, Heaven forbid; nevertheless since one cannot stand on the matter and most times even if they decree, Heaven forbid, on souls, their ultimate intent is to collect through this money from Israel: therefore it is good to collect according to money and not according to souls; and therefore Rav Hai Gaon also wrote imprisoned is often imprisoned on account of money: meaning even though he is imprisoned on matters of life, nevertheless ultimate intent of imprisonment in most cases was to receive money; and if so, since one can strive through money, again there is not so much danger; and in such a case even Magen Avraham agrees, since the main imprisonment was on matters of life he must bless; and what Magen Avraham wrote "on matters of life" comes to exclude if at first the imprisonment was on account of money: for they said immediately that on account of money he was seized, in such a case he does not bless; and in this Rav Hai Gaon also agrees.
