The blessing of individual thanksgiving. 9 sections: Four people must
give thanks: those who descend to the sea when they have come up from it; those who travel through deserts when they reach an inhabited area; one who was ill and recovered; and one who was imprisoned and was released. And your mnemonic is "and all the living shall thank You" — Choleh (sick), Yisurin (suffering/imprisonment), Yam (sea), Midbar (desert).
