(1) When he ascends from it. That is to say, even if a miracle occurred for him in one place, he need not bless immediately but waits until he ascends from the sea; and the same law applies if he went on a ship and sometimes they come to a port and stay there a day or two — he need not bless; however if the delay is long it requires study — so wrote Olat Tamid. And I found one who wrote that one does not bless until he has completely left the trouble, for therefore "for it is good" is not said in the second [blessing], that the work of the waters was not finished — behold the language "good" indicates a good that was completed in goodness and in fullness; and for a sick person, until he walks on his feet — end quote. And Shlah wrote on daf 202 that they should say the portion of thanksgiving in the portion of Tzav, "and this is the law of the peace offering" etc., "and if for thanksgiving" etc., "until morning he shall not leave from it" and he should say "Master" etc., as if I offered thanks in its time — and see above siman 1.
