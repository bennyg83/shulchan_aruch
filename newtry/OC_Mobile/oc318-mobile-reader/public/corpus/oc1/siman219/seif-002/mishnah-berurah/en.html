(4) Who bestows good to the undeserving — meaning even to those who are liable He nevertheless bestows good upon them; and also I, even though I am not worthy, nevertheless He bestowed all good upon me; and this formula is not an impediment, all that he said the matter of the blessing, as below in siman 202. Rambam wrote: the blesser must be standing at the time of the blessing, and b'dieved he fulfills even sitting.

(5) And those who hear say — and if they did not say it is not an impediment [latter authorities].
