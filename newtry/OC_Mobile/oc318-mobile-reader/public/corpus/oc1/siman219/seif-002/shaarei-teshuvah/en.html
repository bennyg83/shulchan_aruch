Imprisoned. See Baer Heitev and Birkei Yosef brought from Rabbenu Yonah and elaborated; and responsum R' Yehuda Migash, and so in Ben Avraham in name of Turei Zahav, that even for imprisonment over money matters he blesses (and there he brought the language of responsum R' Yehuda Migash: since he was imprisoned in prison etc. and was not master over his soul, and now he left that state to be master over his soul, especially if he was imprisoned for a fixed tax on every person or a debt because his hands did not reach, and afterward Heaven arranged for him etc.; and there afterward it seems his view is that if he was able but did not want, or there were places that would remain for him and it did not help etc., unless it is said you gave your words in measures — see there; and it seems in such a case he blesses without Heaven's name and kingship) and he concluded that so is the simple custom that every type of imprisoned person blesses on leaving — see there; and in Machatzit HaShekel brought that also in Netiv Chayim he challenged Magen Avraham on this; and see in Birkei Yosef that a father should not bless on his son who fell into a pit and was saved, unlike responsum Beit Yehudah and Maharash of Duren at end of Tashbetz.
