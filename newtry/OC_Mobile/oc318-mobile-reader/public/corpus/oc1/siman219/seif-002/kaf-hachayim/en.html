(2) A minor less than age 13 does not bless the blessing of thanksgiving, for it is not relevant to say "to the undeserving good," for he is not one subject to punishment; and if he says on his father "to the undeserving" — this is forbidden. And if he skips the word "le-chayavim" one may not change the coin the sages minted. And if we say "le-chayavim" refers to him — that suffering comes upon him from the aspect of gilgul — from where does he know perhaps it is for his father's sin? And therefore he should not bless. Maharam Mintz siman 14. Kenahag in Hagahot Tashbetz. Magen Avraham beginning of the siman. However in responsum Lechem Todah siman 5 he disputes and agreed that even a minor blesses. And they brought Birkei Yosef letter 1 and wrote that such is the custom in their provinces that a minor blesses, see there. However Sha'ar HaTevah letter 1 wrote that in their land they practice like Maharam Mintz that a minor does not bless, see there. And likewise in our provinces in all the land of the deer we have not heard and not seen that a minor blesses, and therefore anywhere there is no custom one should not bless because it is a doubt causing nullification of blessings. And it appears from all the poskim mentioned that after 13 all agree he blesses. And likewise Eshel Avraham beginning of the siman, see there; and see further below letter 29.
