5) Maamar Mordechai writes there that emissaries from Eretz Yisrael practice reciting Birkas HaGomel in every place they visit. He explains that their lengthy stays there are the reason for this practice. He further writes that he saw people traveling from one place to another recite HaGomel upon reaching their destination and recite it again when they returned home safely; see there. Sedei Chemed, Asifas Dinim, section Berachos, siman 2, paragraph 11, cites this. There he writes in the name of R' Yosef Abulafia that those who travel to prostrate themselves at the graves of the righteous practice reciting it at each place. The reason is that those setting out for the graves of the righteous desire and intend each separate location, and they depart with this intent. He writes that he himself acted accordingly and recited the blessing; see there. It appears that this applies when one intends to remain and rest there for several days. If he intends only to prostrate himself and return immediately that day or the next, he should not recite the blessing until reaching the place where he intends to rest for several days or until returning home safely. We acted accordingly in the year 5668, when we traveled from Yerushalayim to Chevron to prostrate ourselves at the graves of the Patriarchs. Since we did not intend to remain but to return on the second day, we did not recite HaGomel there, but only upon returning to Yerushalayim.
