(s.k. 5) And this is not, etc. — meaning if one comes to explain what does it teach us that this is not a blessing in vain — it is permitted even l'chatchila for another to bless; nevertheless Shulchan Aruch that wrote "if another blessed" implies b'dieved language — that he holds even l'chatchila it is permitted; nevertheless since it deals with b'dieved what difference — if his blessing is in vain, what passed passed; on this Rama came to give reason why the sick person fulfills through another's blessing — behold it would be in vain, and in such a case his fellow does not fulfill as written siman 213; therefore Rama wrote this is not a blessing in vain and the sick person properly fulfills.
