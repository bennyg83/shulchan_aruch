Seif 5, in the gloss. “The one reciting the blessing is also obligated.” This implies that if the one reciting it is not obligated, then even if he says “Who has bestowed good upon us” in order to discharge his fellow’s obligation, the latter does not fulfill it without answering amen. See what I wrote in the margin to seif 4. Accordingly, in all blessings over mitzvos, although we rule that one who has already fulfilled his obligation may discharge another, since the one reciting the blessing is not presently obligated, the listener does not fulfill it without answering amen. If the one reciting the blessing is obligated because he was ill and recovered, while the listener is obligated because he traveled through a wilderness, Sefer Urim Gedolim, in its derashos on Parashas Tzav, is uncertain whether the listener fulfills his obligation without answering amen, or whether he does not fulfill it even by answering amen. See there.
