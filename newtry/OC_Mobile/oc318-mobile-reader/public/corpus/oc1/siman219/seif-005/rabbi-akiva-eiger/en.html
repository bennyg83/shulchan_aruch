(5) In the gloss that the blesser is also obligated — it implies if the blesser is not obligated in the blessing, even though he blesses in this formula Who has bestowed good upon us to fulfill his fellow, the fellow does not fulfill without answering amen. See in the margin seif 4; and if so in all mitzvah blessings where we hold if he fulfilled he fulfilled — nevertheless since the blesser is not now obligated in this blessing, one does not fulfill without answering amen; and if the blesser is obligated and blesses the blessing because he was sick and recovered, and this listener is obligated in the blessing because he walked in the desert — it is uncertain in Orim Gedolim in his sermons on parashat Tzav whether he fulfills without answering amen or even with amen he does not fulfill — see there.
