(5) Before ten, etc. And l'chatchila one should be concerned for Tosafot and Tur, who wrote that stringently we require both, and for hiddur one seeks after two rabbis besides the ten — and that is twelve; and so ruled Rokeach. And behold in Lechem Chamudot daf 68 he wondered on Tur: behold this is a leniency, if he blesses only with twelve, for safek berachot lehakel; and so expanded Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah. And in my humble opinion there is no difficulty at all, for Tur per his reason ruled that one fulfills the blessing even with fewer than ten; and if so, the pilpul of ten and two is only l'chatchila for hiddur of the mitzvah, and Tur wrote well that it is a stringency — and this is clear. And see above at the end of siman 30: Rabbeinu Yeruham wrote on daf 104, one must give thanks with ten and two of them Torah scholars; and Rif did not bring two Torah scholars except within ten — end quote. And one can wonder: behold Rif brought this, and this is his language "and two of them are rabbis" — and one must say Rabbeinu Yonah [means] two Torah scholars besides ten — and this is strained. And what Levush wrote, that they practice to bless after reading the Torah etc. — I found in Torat Chayim, chapter Chelek, another reason: when one says "Bless Heaven," it is also like giving thanks, as it is written "give thanks to Heaven, bless His name"; and for a woman in childbirth, the husband can intend when saying "Bless" that he gives thanks on behalf of his wife, and she answers amen on his blessing — end quote; and see above sk 12.
