(5) And he wrote there Ma'amar Mordechai that emissaries of Eretz Yisrael practice that in every place they go they bless birkat ha-gomel, and he wrote the reason: since they delay there a long time, many practice thus. And he wrote further that he saw practice when going from place to place that they bless ha-gomel upon reaching their desired destination, and return to bless ha-gomel upon returning home in peace, see there. And they brought Shach in Asifas Dinim Laws of Blessings siman 2 letter 11 and recorded in the name of Mahari Abulafia that those who go to prostrate on graves of the righteous — the custom is to bless at every place, and the reason: because those who go to travel to graves of the righteous — their desire and will is to every place and place, and with knowledge they go forth; and he wrote that he too, z"l, made an act to bless, see there. And it appears this is if his intent is to delay and rest there some days; but if his intent is only to prostrate and return immediately that day or the next, he should not bless until he reaches the place he wants to rest some days, or until he returns home in peace. And we did likewise in practice in the year 5688 when we went from Jerusalem to Chevron to prostrate on graves of the Patriarchs — and our will was not to delay there but to return on the second day, and we did not bless ha-gomel there until our return to Jerusalem.
