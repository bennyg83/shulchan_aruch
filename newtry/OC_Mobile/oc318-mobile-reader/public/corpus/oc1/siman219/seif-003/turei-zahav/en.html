And if he blessed with fewer than ten etc. — so Tur, from that we say in the Gemara and one must give thanks before ten, implying specifically l'chatchila — end quote. And Beit Yosef: the precision does not seem correct to me, for on the contrary it implies there is an impediment in the matter; and so R' Yehuda wrote, and if there are not ten he must return and bless — end quote. And I too wondered on Tur, for also regarding the essence of the obligation of thanks they said the language need — for they said four need to give thanks, and there it does not mean specifically l'chatchila, for certainly he does not fulfill as long as he did not give thanks.
