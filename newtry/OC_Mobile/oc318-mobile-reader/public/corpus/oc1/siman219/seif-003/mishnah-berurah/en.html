(6) Before ten — with the one upon whom the miracle was done.

(7) And in the assembly of elders — and elder means only one who acquired wisdom; and Magen Avraham wrote we require those who teach halacha.

(8) Some say he has fulfilled — for the entire essence of ten is only for the mitzvah; and therefore if he knows ten will not become available to him he blesses without ten even l'chatchila; and see in Hilkhot Ra'ah who wrote he waits until thirty days lest ten become available to him so he can fulfill the mitzvah properly, and he should not wait more.

(9) Without mention of shem u'malchut — because safek berachot lehakel.
