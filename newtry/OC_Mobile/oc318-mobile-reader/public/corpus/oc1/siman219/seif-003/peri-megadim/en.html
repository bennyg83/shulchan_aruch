Regarding — Taz — on his father and teacher he may bless Who has bestowed every good upon you, or undeserving refers to the blesser. And regarding childbirth Ateret Zekenim wrote the husband can intend in the Torah blessing when he says Bless Heaven the blessed One to fulfill for his wife and she answers amen, as written Give thanks to Heaven, bless His name; and in ot 12 he wrote she blesses herself between herself and herself — this would be doubt of blessing in vain; but the third way that the husband says Who has bestowed good upon you and she answers amen — there is no concern. See regarding undeserving language for a woman like a minor, for a woman's main punishment is on account of her husband — see there; also this requires study, for she has her own punishments too; and to me there is some difficulty how they say on that Heaven has no mercy on your heart Who has bestowed good upon us and not upon the undeserving — behold Heaven's name is there as in siman 187 and Taz ot 11, and it is forbidden to mention the name alone; and one could say specifically when mentioning the name regarding a person; but regarding the Merciful One there is no concern; if so he should say the entire blessing Merciful One King etc.; and one could say kingship is forbidden to mention — see Orach Chayim siman 34 in Chayei Adam and Be'er Sheva ot 6, ostensibly the main concern is the name not kingship; and one could say here too from Taz that it is forbidden; and further if so why were they so anxious in doubt of blessings to be lenient because of do not take in vain — it is possible he could bless in Aramaic and fulfill, for blessings may be said in any language, and do not take in vain does not apply unless he said Merciful One alone with no concern; but when he said King of the world it indicates the meaning of Merciful One is Heaven — forbidden.
