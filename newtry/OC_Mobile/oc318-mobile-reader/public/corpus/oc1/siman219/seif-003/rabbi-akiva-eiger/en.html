(3) Before ten and two of them rabbis. Ra'avan wrote in his sermons on parashat Emor that certainly those people must be apart from those giving thanks and the one saved; and when they are on the road where there are not ten and two rabbis, even if they are many, since all are those saved, one may not bless.
(There) In the sugya it states Abaye said and one must give thanks before ten; Mar Zutra said and two of them rabbis; and I say in a house of ten and two rabbis; and Tosafot wrote we act stringently even if there are no two rabbis; and Tur wrote in name of Tosafot that since it was not resolved we act stringently and require both; and per our version in Tosafot I wondered: how is it relevant to be stringent to bless, for everywhere the rule is doubt in blessings to be lenient and we do not bless because of doubt of do not take in vain; and if their intent is that the language need to give thanks is l'chatchila, but if he does not find ten he blesses even with fewer than ten — see Tur and Beit Yosef; if so all the more it is wondered, since all the analysis is if l'chatchila one must trouble to find ten and two rabbis, it should be ruled one must trouble for ten and two rabbis. And Tur's version works well that they go stringently and require both; and in truth ostensibly what stringency is this — he does not bless until both exist, and this is called lenient language as we say everywhere in Berakhot to be lenient; but it seems Tur's intent is all the analysis is only l'chatchila, but b'dieved if he does not find ten he also blesses, as seems from Tur's view for one who examines — if so he wrote well that we go stringently to trouble after ten and two rabbis; but our version in Tosafot — requires study.
