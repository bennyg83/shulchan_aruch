(3) Women are obligated in birkat ha-gomel, and if because one must respond before ten and it is not a woman's honor to stand before men — she may stand in the women's synagogue and bless and the men hear in their synagogue; and at minimum she should bless before one man or women, Kenahag in Hagahot Tashbetz, see there. Yesh Omrim in Hagahot Tashbetz, Birkei Yosef letter 2, and wrote that the rabbi the pious Mahari Molko agreed in responsum 21 siman 141. However what Kenahag wrote that she may bless before one man or women — that is per the view of those who say if he blessed before fewer than ten he has fulfilled, as written below in seif 3; but per the view of those who say he has not fulfilled, it is a blessing in vain — and therefore for us who are concerned for doubt in blessings, a woman should bless only before ten like a man; and because a woman's honor is not to stand before men, she should bless in the women's beit knesset and ten men hear in their beit knesset, or before ten men from her relatives and acquaintances; and if not, she blesses without shem u'malchut.
