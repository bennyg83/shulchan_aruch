3) Women are obligated in Birkas HaGomel. Regarding the requirement to give thanks before ten and the impropriety of a woman standing before men, she may stand in the women's section of the synagogue and recite the blessing while the men hear in their section. At minimum, she should recite it before one man or before women. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur; see there. Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 2, who writes that the pious R' Yosef Molcho agrees in a handwritten responsum, siman 141. Knesses HaGedolah's statement that she may recite it before one man or women follows the view that one who recited it before fewer than ten fulfilled her obligation, as stated below in seif 3. According to the view that she did not fulfill her obligation, however, this would be a blessing in vain. Therefore, since we are concerned about doubtful blessings, a woman should recite it only before ten, like a man. Since it is improper for a woman to stand before men, she should recite it in the women's section while ten men hear in their section, or before ten male relatives and acquaintances. Otherwise, she should recite it without the Name and kingship.
