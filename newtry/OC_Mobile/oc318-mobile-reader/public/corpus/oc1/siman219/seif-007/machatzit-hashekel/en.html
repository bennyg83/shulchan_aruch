(s.k. 7) Ailment, etc. — nevertheless not every day, meaning even though he recovered from this illness already many times, it was not the way of nature but each time a miracle was done for him, as they say in Nedarim chapter Ein Bein HaModar: R' Alexandri said Rabbah bar bar Chanah said: greater is what is done for the sick than the miracle done for Chananyah, Mishael and Azaryah, etc.; if so, since not every day does a miracle occur and he was in presumptive danger — therefore when he stood he must give thanks.
