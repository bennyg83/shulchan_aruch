"One who urinates," etc. Even if he is clothed and urinates in front of his bed, the text speaks of one who is naked only because that is the ordinary case. [Rashi, Niddah 57a] Likewise, one who cuts his fingernails in their natural consecutive order brings himself to poverty, forgetfulness, and the burial of children. [Knesset HaGedolah] Rather, he should cut them in the alternate order recorded by the authorities, beginning with the left hand and afterward the right. [Hagahot Ashiri; Agudah; Beit David; Abudarham in the name of the sages of France and Provence]
