One who urinates water etc. — even if he is dressed and urinates before his bed, and it mentions naked — this is the ordinary way of speech (Rashi on Niddah 57). And likewise one who cuts his nails in order also brings poverty, forgetfulness, and burial of sons (Knesset HaGedolah); rather he should cut them in this order: first with the left hand as in Hagahot Maimonides, and afterward the right hand (Agudah, Beit David) (Abudraham in the name of the sages of France and Provence).
