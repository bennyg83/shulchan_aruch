(א) That the Holy One blessed be He hates etc. — because the will of the Holy One blessed be He is that a person conduct himself in a path of cleanliness and holiness, and this one conducts himself in a path of filth and impurity, and the Divine Presence cannot rest with him.

(ב) Before his bed — and all the more so if he urinates before other places that require greater cleanliness, such as before his table and the like.

(ג) Naked — not specifically naked, but the same applies if he was dressed; rather it is the ordinary way of speech: since he is naked he does not go outside to urinate.

(ד) One who urinates etc. — it is an abbreviated expression, and its meaning is additionally our sages said one who urinates etc.

(ה) Brings poverty — for we say in Arvei Pesachim 111 that the angel of poverty, Naval is his name, loves to dwell in a place of filth [and therefore he is called Naval, for he wishes to disgrace himself], and urinating before one's bed is filth.

(ו) Toward the outside etc. — for the spray goes and splashes far, and from this it follows that for a woman it is forbidden even in such a case [Magen Avraham].

(ז) On a garment etc. — nevertheless a Torah scholar must cover himself with a vessel from above, for it is impossible for a Torah scholar without thinking in Torah.

(ח) We have no concern — and when he wishes afterward to bless the blessing "who formed man," he should wash his hands and also gird himself so that his heart not see nakedness, and he should distance himself as required from the place of urination if the vessel is not covered as required.
