(א) Naked — not specifically, but it is the ordinary way of speech: since he is naked he does not go outside to urinate.

(ב) Poverty — for the angel of poverty, whose name is Naval, dwells in a place of filth.

(ג) Toward the outside we have no concern — for the spray goes and splashes (far) — Rashi; it implies that for a woman it is forbidden.

(ד) On a garment we have no concern. Nevertheless a Torah scholar must cover himself with a vessel as is written in siman 87, for it is impossible for him without thinking in Torah; and may Heaven grant me to finish the laws of Shabbat to completion, for the sake of the exile and not for my honor, amen.
