[1] [Levush] From a window of a private domain, etc. In this case the permission applies even if the window is not ten handbreadths high. In the latter clause, however, where the pit is four handbreadths away and only its rim is ten high, the window itself must also be ten high.
[2] Four handbreadths, etc. Rabbenu Yerucham, page 95b, writes three handbreadths. I am astonished, because the Gemara in Eruvin 99 and all the halachic authorities state four. See Lechem Mishneh, chapter 15.
