(א) Garbage heap in reshus harabbim, etc. — and if standing in karmelit see Peri Megadim:
(ב) That is ten high — for if not ten high karmelit is upon it and forbidden to throw to it from reshut ha-yachid:
(ג) Of the public — for the public throw their refuse and manure there:
(ד) Permitted to throw to it — and even if the garbage heap is far from the wall four tefachim and on the interruption between them there is reshus harabbim — nevertheless permitted to throw to the garbage heap from a window in the house, provided the window is ten high from the ground so the throw is from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via makom patur; and what Mechaber wrote "adjacent to it" comes to exclude if the garbage heap is far from reshut ha-yachid not to throw to the garbage heap lest it fall in reshus harabbim:
(ה) Adjacent to it — and no eruv is needed since there are no dwellers:
(ו) Repulsive things — meaning not needed and he will not come to bring afterward even if it falls:
(ז) As is their custom — that they were accustomed there before, unlike public garbage heap where they do not appoint an overseer for the garbage heap and always it should be ten tefachim high, for all day everyone throws refuse and manure there and its height will not lack:
