B) There. If it stands within four, etc. Meaning the pit itself stands within four of reshut ha-yachid. Taz s.k. 1. If the pit is four by four from reshut ha-yachid and only the rim reduces it — then if the rim height is not three tefachim that place is reshus harabbim. Machatzit HaShekel part 1; and if the rim is three high and four wide it is karmelit and it is taking out from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via karmelit.
