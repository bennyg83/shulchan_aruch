2) There. If it stands within four handbreadths, etc. This means that the pit itself stands within four handbreadths of the private domain. Taz, subsection 1. If the pit is four handbreadths away from the private domain and only the rim reduces that distance, then if the rim is less than three handbreadths high, that area is a public domain. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1. If the rim is three handbreadths high and four handbreadths wide, it is a karmelis, and one is consequently transferring from one private domain to another private domain by way of a karmelis. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 2. Regarding whether it is forbidden to convey an object by way of a karmelis, see below, subsection 6.
