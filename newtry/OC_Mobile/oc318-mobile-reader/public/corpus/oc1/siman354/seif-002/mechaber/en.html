If a refuse heap in the public domain is 10 tefachim high and 4 tefachim wide, and it belongs to the public, one may throw into it from an adjacent private domain. {Rama: We are not concerned that the object might roll from the refuse heap into the public domain and that he may come to carry it, because people ordinarily throw only repulsive things onto a refuse heap. [Maggid Mishneh, chapter 15, in the name of Rashba]} If the refuse heap belongs to an individual, it is forbidden, because an individual is apt to clear away his refuse heap, and people will then continue pouring there in their usual manner and will thereby be pouring into the public domain.
