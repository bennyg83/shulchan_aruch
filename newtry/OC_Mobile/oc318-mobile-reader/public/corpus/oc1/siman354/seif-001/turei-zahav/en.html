If it stands within four tefachim. Meaning the pit itself; and the empty space between pit and wall is makom patur; and even though we rule a tel in reshus harabbim without four is not makom patur unless three high — here is different, for the entire interruption less than four between pit and wall the public does not tread at all; perforce makom patur without four even if not three high; Beit Yosef in Ritva's name; and there is a scribal error there and must read as written:
