The laws of a pit and a garbage heap in reshus harabbim. And in
it are 2 seifim: A pit in reshus harabbim with a rim around it — if it stands within four tefachim of reshut ha-yachid, it is permitted to draw from it to reshut ha-yachid even if the rim is not ten high; and if it is four away from reshut ha-yachid, one may not draw from it unless the rim is ten high:
