Seif 1 — a pit in reshus harabbim and a rim around it if it stands within four tefachim of reshut ha-yachid, etc. Note: examining the Gemara daf 59 where the Gemara says R' Yochanan said even if you say in proximity — this teaches pit and rim combine to ten; and Rashi explained the mishna is not as we thought the rim alone is ten apart from the pit, rather it means pit and rim together total ten from the pit floor is reshut ha-yachid, etc.; and I wondered on Tur who copied this mishna and wrote even if the rim is not ten high and did not write the extra novelty that even in the pit there is not ten — they combine between them to ten; also siman 347 when Shulchan Aruch copied the law of a ten-deep pit being reshut ha-yachid did not write this extra novelty that pit and rim combine — and it is wondrous and I saw no one who raised this. And while on this I will mention what I was asked: if there is a small pit less than ten and a small mound adjacent less than ten, do they combine to ten to be like a partition regarding not needing eruv — I answered it is not judged as a partition; one: even though the Gemara implies pit and rim combine to ten, Rashi there in Eruvin in his language "and rim around it" implies specifically if encircled on all sides its interior is reshut ha-yachid; but if not encircled on all sides only on one side, even its interior is not judged reshut ha-yachid; and one should not say that regarding its interior it works since it has no partitions on other sides, but regarding being judged in the tel place as a partition it works like a five-high mound and five-high partition that combine — but this is not so, for five-high mound and five-high partition are ten high from the ground; then resting for use is not comfortable so the public do not come and nullify the partition and it is reshut ha-yachid; but here where on other sides it is less than ten, resting for use is comfortable so the public come — therefore not on reshus harabbim. I again saw in Tosafos Shabbos daf 99b and Sefer Tosefta Shabbat siman 345 brought them wrote thus — even though pit and rim combine it is only for its interior to be reshut ha-yachid, not on top — see there; and Sefer Tosefta Shabbat added in their words since it is level on other sides with reshus harabbim ground — and understand my intent in their view; and further it appeared to me support for this law — one may say the reason Tur omitted pit and rim combine to ten is prior to also explain why Tosefta Shabbat wondered why they omitted the explicit law in Tosafos Shabbos daf 99 that even though pit and rim combine to ten, nevertheless above on top is not reshut ha-yachid and we do not say for others he makes a partition for himself — all the more so — see there; and to resolve all this it appears Acharonim hold certainly according to what the Gemara concludes Shabbos there where Ulla said if for others he makes a partition for himself — all the more so; and certainly even if the rim is not ten high we say thus if for others he makes a partition for himself — all the more so; and even on top is reshut ha-yachid; and what Tosafos challenged why mention twice pit and rim combine to ten — explain thus: since the examiner there in Shabbos first asks R' Yochanan on a wall in reshus harabbim ten high and concludes Ulla if for others he makes a partition for himself — all the more so; and then concludes R' Yochanan himself said thus; if so perforce R' Yochanan retracted, for initially he did not hold this reasoning if for others he makes a partition for himself — all the more so; and afterward concludes this reasoning; if so one may say what R' Yochanan answers in Eruvin deals with proximity and teaches pit and rim combine to ten — why mention twice; for why inform us in Shabbos twice — one may say as Tosefta Shabbat wrote it teaches on top is reshut ha-yachid; but according to what Ulla concludes and R' Yochanan retracted and holds this a fortiori if for others he makes a partition for himself — all the more so — then even on top is reshut ha-yachid; therefore R' Yochanan actually retracted also from his resolution in Eruvin, only also explaining like Rav Huna dealing with four away from the wall and the rim itself ten high — therefore we do not hear from there pit and rim combine to ten; therefore needed to inform us in Shabbos; but if so why not inform us in Shabbos regarding b'dieved if he took out liable and why not inform us the permission even l'chatchila to bring from it to reshut ha-yachid such as Eruvin's case of pit adjacent to wall and the like; automatically we know all the more liable; therefore proven l'chatchila permission pit and rim do not combine to ten to make reshut ha-yachid since we require encircled on all sides with rim — we are concerned he will err thinking even not encircled he will say if encircled on one or two sides with a tel he will say it is reshut ha-yachid; therefore they decreed even fully encircled on all sides also forbidden l'chatchila to take from it to reshut ha-yachid — only regarding Torah liability it is reshut ha-yachid; therefore Tur and Shulchan Aruch omitted this law pit and rim combine to permit l'chatchila; and through this there was room to resolve Tosefta Shabbat's challenge on Gittin chapter 2 on five-high mound and five-high partition for Rashi there — one may say there it goes only for us and therefore one view holds l'chatchila permission mound five and partition five do not combine; for b'dieved liability from Torah pit and rim combine and the other holds even l'chatchila permission they combine; and this Eruvin case is Rav Huna there; and from Shabbos no proof except from Torah it is reshut ha-yachid not d'rabbanan to permit l'chatchila; therefore they argue there; however Tur and Shulchan Aruch explain in Gittin explicitly Tosafos there and in pit and rim hold d'rabbanan it is not reshut ha-yachid; therefore they omitted this; and if so all the more for eruv partition law pit and small adjacent mound do not combine as above in my humble opinion;
