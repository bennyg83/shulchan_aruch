(31) He recites Kiddush in the courtyard and eats there. But not Kiddush indoors and eating in the courtyard, as above in se'if 1.
(32) And this is reasonable. When he suffers greatly; otherwise he must eat where the lamp is. Pri Megadim inclines that it is better to recite Kiddush indoors, eat a little, then finish in the courtyard. See siman 263:9.
