S.k. 9: It appears to me that this is unnecessary. That is to say: although he has fulfilled the obligation of Kiddush, since he will eat in another place one might have thought that he must recite a blessing over a cup before eating, in the manner of Kiddush recited in the morning. The ruling teaches that this is unnecessary, as explained in siman 291, seif 4, regarding the third meal, which does not require a cup. The Tur there gives the reason that just as wine is required only once at night, it is required only once during the day.
