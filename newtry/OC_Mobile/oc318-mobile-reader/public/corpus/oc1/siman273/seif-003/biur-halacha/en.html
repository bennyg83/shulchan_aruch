And so all the great ones mentioned who copied Magen Avraham's words for practical law relied the law of leaving outside on the case of one who delayed due to constraint, and it implies they were stringent b'dieved only in this manner and not for leaving alone; and afterward I found in Petaḥ Devarim who expanded greatly on this matter and sided with the law not like Shakhna, and brought in the name of Sefer Nachalat Tzvi, student of the Bach, that his view explicitly is that when one left outside and returned home it is also within change of place for Kiddush; nevertheless it appears one should not be stringent b'dieved against Shakhna and Yad Malachi, for whom it is simple, and for them it would be a blessing in vain to sanctify again; and also all the great ones mentioned who copied Magen Avraham's words also appear they hold like Shakhna when there was no delay, as written above, and especially since the essence of Kiddush in a place of a meal is only rabbinic, and certainly in hardship one may rely on them not to return and sanctify again; but l'chatchila one should certainly be careful in this.
