3) There: Kiddush is valid only in the place of a meal. This is Shmuel's opinion. Although Rav and Rabbi Yochanan disagree with him, we maintain Shmuel's view, as explained in Tur and Beis Yosef; see there. Ginas Veradim, klal 3, siman 12, writes that since the daytime Kiddush is quite lenient, one who relies upon Rav and Rabbi Yochanan has not acted improperly. We do not mean that one should rely upon this ab initio. Only in pressing circumstances and for the honor of a large gathering may one rely upon this rationale to be lenient, and one should not protest against the public when they act this way. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1, cites this; see there.
