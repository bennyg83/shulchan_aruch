[Levush] Not to eat at all, etc. This is how the Levush explains Rama. Magen Avraham explains instead that one intended to interrupt with other matters and did so; even if he later ate at the kiddush place, the delayed eating does not fulfill kiddush in the place of a meal. If he intended to eat immediately but an unavoidable circumstance delayed him, he need not repeat kiddush. If, however, he left the place in the interim, he must make kiddush again.
