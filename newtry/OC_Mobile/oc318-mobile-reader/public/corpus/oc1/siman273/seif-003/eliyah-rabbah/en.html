[3] [Levush:] Not to eat at all, etc. Levush understands Rama in this manner. Magen Avraham, however, explains that Rama means that the person intended to interrupt with other matters. If he did interrupt, then even if he ate later in the place where Kiddush was recited, he does not fulfill his obligation. If he intended to eat immediately but was unexpectedly prevented and did not eat until later, he need not recite Kiddush again. If, however, he left his place in the interim, he must recite Kiddush again. See also siman 100.
