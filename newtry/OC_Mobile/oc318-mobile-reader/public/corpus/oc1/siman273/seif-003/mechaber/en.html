If one makes kiddush but does not eat a meal (afterwards), even regarding kiddush he has not fulfilled his obligation.Rema: One must eat in the place one makes kiddush. However anyway, even if one eats in the place that kiddush was recited, one has not fulfilled (one's obligation). (Mahar"i) If it was his intention to not eat)) there immediately, and then he went and ate, he has fulfilled his obligation. (Beis Yosef, end of the Siman).
