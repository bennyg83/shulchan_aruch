(3) And in the Gemara, page 9. Rav Hon' said there that after he had first recited Kedesh, he had extinguished the candles and entered the house of his son's wedding where the candles were still lit, and he had recited Kedesh a second time and eaten. And from this the Shas proved that it is probable that there is no Kiddush except at the place of a meal:

(ד) Specifically because he left, etc. — even though a story occurred thus, nevertheless since the Gemara brought the entire story that he left his place, it implies the matter depends on this, and for this reason he sanctified again. And so too from Rama's wording, who wrote his intent was initially to interrupt — it implies if his intent was initially not to interrupt and afterward through constraint and necessity he interrupted, he did not lose.
