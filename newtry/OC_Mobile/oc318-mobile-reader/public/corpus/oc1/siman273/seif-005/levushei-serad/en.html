S.k. 8: That he drank a reviis. His intent is to resolve the difficulty raised by Turei Zahav, s.k. 4: a meal is not constituted by less than a reviis, whereas melo lugmav is only the majority of a reviis. Although in siman 130 the authorities are uncertain whether the measure obligating an after-blessing for a beverage is a kezayis or a reviis, and melo lugmav is explained there to exceed a kezayis (seemingly posing a difficulty according to the opinion that a kezayis suffices) the answer is that the measure of a meal does not depend on the obligation to recite an after-blessing. Rather, a significant measure is required, namely a reviis. The Geonim used the obligation of an after-blessing merely as an indicator. They themselves were uncertain whether its measure is a kezayis or a reviis, and because one is lenient in cases of doubt concerning blessings, the obligation applies only to a reviis. The matter is readily understood. Chemed Moshe therefore raised an unnecessary commotion about this.
