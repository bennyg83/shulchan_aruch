Gloss, seif 3: Or if he intended, etc. The word ‘or’ indicates that the principal case does not concern one who intended to eat immediately. Nevertheless, if he did eat immediately, he fulfilled his obligation. According to this, Rema’s concluding words, ‘or if he intended,’ etc., are superfluous, since this is already necessarily evident from here. Perforce, the case there concerns one who intended to eat in another house; the word ‘there’ indicates this. Turei Zahav and Magen Avraham likewise explain it there. According to this approach, however, Rema’s use there of the word ‘immediately’ is superfluous. This requires some consideration.
