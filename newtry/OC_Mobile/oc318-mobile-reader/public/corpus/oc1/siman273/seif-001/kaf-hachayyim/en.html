1) [Seif 1] Kiddush is valid only in the place of a meal. This applies both to the night Kiddush and to the daytime Kiddush, as stated below at the beginning of siman 289. The reason is the verse, 'You shall call Shabbos a delight': where the calling occurs, there must be delight. Beis Yosef; Taz, paragraph 1. According to this reason, even one who recites Kiddush over bread must do so in the place of a meal. The person reciting Kiddush must also eat in that place, as stated in seif 4. Although he recites Kiddush for himself and others, if they eat there and he does not, they fulfill their obligation but he does not. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1. This implies that the reverse also applies: if he recites Kiddush for himself and others and he eats there but they do not, he fulfills his obligation but they do not. See below, paragraph 4.
