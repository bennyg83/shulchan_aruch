That Kiddush be recited in the place of the meal. It contains 7 seifim: Kiddush is valid only in the place of the meal. Within one house, from corner to corner is considered one place. If one recited Kiddush intending to eat in this corner and then changed his mind to eat in another corner, even if it is a large hall, he need not recite Kiddush again. {Rama: And from the house to the sukkah is considered like from corner to corner. [Mordechai, chapter Arvei Pesachim]} And some say that anyone who sees his place, even from a house to a courtyard, need not recite Kiddush again. And some say that if one recited Kiddush in one place with the intention to eat in another place, it is effective (see above, beginning of siman 178), provided that the two places are in one house, for example from room to room or from the roof to the ground floor (and this is the primary view).
