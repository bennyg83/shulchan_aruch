Magen Avraham, s.k. 4: The meaning of ‘he did not eat here’ is that he did not eat here at all. If, however, he ate a small amount here, he may finish the meal elsewhere, as explained in seif 5. This clarification was written so that one should not mistakenly interpret the phrase ‘and afterward he changed his mind’ as referring to one who changed his mind in the middle of the meal.
