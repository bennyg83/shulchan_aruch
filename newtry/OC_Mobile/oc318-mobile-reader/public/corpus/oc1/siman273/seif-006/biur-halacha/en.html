(א) And if he sanctified, etc. — see what is written in Mishna Berurah that even l'chatchila, and so too when he sanctifies on bread, etc. And it is difficult, for below in siman 296 the Shulchan Aruch ruled in seif 6 that Havdalah one says seated, and the reason the Biur HaGra wrote there is because of the reason [we require establishment when he releases others], and our sitting is like their reason; and how can he release his neighbor when he is in another house? And even Rama there, who holds standing is permitted — is not the reason explained in Beit Yosef that since they establish themselves to fulfill Havdalah they also establish for Birkat Hamazon, see there — behold in any case we require that they establish together, and not when this one is in this house and his neighbor in another house, for establishment does not apply at all; and for b'dieved it is not difficult, for already ruled above in siman 167, seif 13, that b'dieved one may release even without any establishment at all, for it is difficult for me according to Magen Avraham, who wrote that even l'chatchila he may sanctify for his neighbor [and from the sugya there in Pesachim 101, where it states to release guests from their obligation, for they ate and drank and slept in the synagogue — there is no proof, per what the Ran answered in the first answer — that guests may eat in the synagogue from the charity box]; unless we strain ourselves that since the listener intends to fulfill, it is called establishment even if he is in another house.

(ב) For example, the matter’s promise to Abraham, and so on, is that the matter’s promise is that the matter’s promise is to be revealed The judgment of the matter’s people will be fulfilled, and the matter’s judgment will be revealed in His Word In retrospect, if only we can hear that it is not intended to come out of the hands of the Sages, but according to what is written by the Holy One, it is said that it is not appropriate for them The darkness of the matter’s wrath is in the place of sin. And also the main reason for this is not to divide it and In the psalmist, the matter’s promise to Abraham is a great sin, and the matter’s promise to Abraham: “I am in the the spirit.”
