There: As Tosafos wrote. The discussion there concerns people who recited Kiddush in the synagogue. Rabbi Yochanan holds that they fulfilled their obligation of Kiddush because Kiddush need not be recited in the place of the meal. They also fulfilled their obligation regarding the wine in their homes and need not recite another blessing over the wine they drink there, because walking from the synagogue to their homes is not considered an interruption of attention. Tosafos explain that the case must be one in which they drank wine in the synagogue; otherwise, how could they have fulfilled their obligation regarding wine? See there. Shmuel disagrees and maintains that they did not fulfill even the obligation of Kiddush, because Kiddush must be recited in the place of the meal. We rule in accordance with him. Thus, although they drank melo lugmav, the synagogue is not considered the place of their meal, because a meal requires a reviis.
