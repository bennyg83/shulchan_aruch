Magen Avraham, s.k. 7: He did not consider the Gemara in Rosh Hashanah 29, cited above at the end of siman 167. Although one who is not himself benefiting cannot recite HaMotzi on behalf of other adults, he may do so for minors in order to train them in mitzvos. Kiddush differs, for one may discharge even adults. If one would answer that Shulchan Aruch discusses adults who know how to recite Kiddush themselves, the same should apply to minors who know how: he should not recite Kiddush for them, since they can do so themselves.
