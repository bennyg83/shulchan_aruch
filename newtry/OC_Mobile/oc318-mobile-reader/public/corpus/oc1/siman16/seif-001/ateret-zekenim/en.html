The rabbi, author of Ateret Zekenim, extended his analysis at length and the paper would be too short to contain it; therefore I was forced to shorten by saying: As for the measure that most of the masses are accustomed to wear tzitzis on a small garment like a man's torso—even a five-year-old child does not cover his head and most of his body in it, and also no adult of thirteen years was found who would not be ashamed to go out even casually in the market in such a custom—it is fitting to abolish it, for everyone recites a blessing in vain (see in the Taz).
