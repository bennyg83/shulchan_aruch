Measure. Taz. Gemara Menachos 41a; and that which the Tanna cites "and the adult goes out casually" (Beit Yosef in the name of Mahariya and Mahari Chaviv—I brought it in Magen Avraham)—is an amplification, meaning even if the adult goes out only casually and not regularly, still it is obligated. Still, even an important person who is not ashamed in this to go out casually—it is obligated. And in the Tur he wrote "and the adult is not ashamed," as Rashi's language in the Gemara—it appears to be a condition and not an amplification; also Mahariya's explanation is impossible, for he wrote measure as Magen Avraham wrote; and what he did not say in the conclusion this condition—that if the adult does not go out casually, all the more so. And what he wrote "and he said to him, why do I need both"—he feared repeating the words of the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch, for according to their words there is one measure—why do I need "and the adult goes out" as amplification? And it is like Magen Avraham's question; and he answered that we did not know which child the beraisa deals with; therefore he gave this measure of child and adult plainly: most people go out casually, and certainly this means a child of nine years, as Raavad wrote, of blessed memory, chapter 3 of Tzitzis: they did not speak of every child, for otherwise the adult would not go out casually. And he explained the Rambam's words in the gloss that he agrees with the Mechaber, not like Magen Avraham—for in Darchei Moshe he copied Bach's wording and agrees with him; and here the explanation is like Mahariya, that it does not deal with the child, for there is no obligation on the child. And one should see: certainly a child who has reached chinuch—in siman 17 his father is obligated to make tzitzis on his tallit. Still, certainly we gauge by the head and majority of the child as he is; and if bar shith we gauge by his head and majority, and his father is obligated rabbinically to place tzitzis on him. And if a garment covers his head and majority but it is disgraceful dress and one does not go out in it to the market casually, and plainly most people go out in it to the bathhouse—for they hold both are required, as the Tur ruled for Taz—it is possible this is not so, for it is not fitting to bless on such a tallit, for we follow his intent—and it requires study.
