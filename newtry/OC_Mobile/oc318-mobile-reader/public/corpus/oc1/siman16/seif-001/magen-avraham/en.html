In the responsum of the Rema / Maharam Mintz, siman 110, he discusses how one fulfills [the mitzvah] with four small corners below this measure, and the gist of his words is that it is better to go in them than to go the whole day without tzitzis. Darchei Moshe, siman 9, [suggests] teaching the people to bless in the morning on a large tallis and thereby exempt the small one and go in it all day. Still, if a person can make a large one of the [proper] measure, how good and pleasant that is—end quote. It is written in the name of Maharil that one should make the shoulder [straps] wide so that air from this side and that side does not enter and invalidate it. “A child.” In the Tur it is written “nine years,” and it appears to me he holds that is the measure of chinuch, as Tosafos [at the] beginning of Arachin [say], unlike what the Bach wrote that the Tur disagrees with the Semak.
“When an adult wears it.” From his wording it implies that if the adult is embarrassed to go out in it casually to the market he is exempt—for the Rema came to teach there is no obligation on the child, only on the adult who sometimes wears it casually, as Beis Yosef wrote there. This does not need explanation, for Beis Yosef here used the Rambam’s wording about tallis measure, etc., to teach that we gauge by the child, whereas in truth the obligation is on the adult—so what did the Rema innovate in the Hagahah except to say that the matter also depends on this: that the adult wears it, etc.—likewise in the Tur, except that in Darchei Moshe he copied only Beis Yosef’s words, which needs study. The Bach wrote that if he goes out in it regularly, even if the child is not covered by it head and most [of the body], he is obligated; and if the adult is embarrassed to go out in it to the market, even occasionally, even if he wears it at home it is exempt, even if the child is covered head and most—end quote. Likewise in the Gemara: if like the explanation of R. Yehudah Habib and Rabbeinu Yerucham it should have said “and the adult wears it”—why mention going out to the market? Even the Rambam, who mentioned nothing of an adult, one could say he holds that usually when a child is covered head and most the adult is not embarrassed to go out in it casually to the market—likewise in the Raavad there; therefore one should be stringent not to bless on it if he is embarrassed to go out in it casually to the market—likewise in Levush and Eliyah Rabbah; and if he goes out in a tallis that others do not go out in, his intent is nullified.
