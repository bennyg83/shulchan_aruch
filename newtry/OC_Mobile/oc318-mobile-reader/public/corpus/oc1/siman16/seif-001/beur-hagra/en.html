And specifically, etc. Tur and Gemara there; as Rashi explained, and the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch omitted it; and R. Yitzchak of Acco and R. Yechiel explained that what it says "and the adult," etc., is not for the measure but for the law: if the adult wears it casually he is obligated, for the child is not subject to obligation at all. And this is why it says in the conclusion "the child is not," etc., even though, etc., and it does not say "or if the adult is not," etc. And it is difficult to reconcile, as Magen Avraham wrote that if so it should not say "goes out" but should say "wears it"; and furthermore it is difficult for those who hold an obligation of tallit; and what is the Gemara's question "but we learned 'there is no'," etc.? And one cannot say it asks on what was said "specifically when the child covers," etc., for that does not apply to casual wear; and in any case it cannot be reconciled, for he explains explicitly in his commentary on Kilayim that there is no casual wear in donning them, and likewise in his composition, section 1, halacha 18; and if so what does the Gemara ask? And therefore another textual reading is needed for the Rambam, and from this one learns the law stated above of one who walks alone, etc.—specifically; but the Tur and the gloss explain as Rashi, not as R. Yechiel.
