Seif 1, in length and in breadth. Orach Chayim—and likewise the expression “is covered with it” implies [the same].

“Specifically,” etc. The Tur and Gemara there follow Rashi and the Rambam, and Shulchan Aruch omitted [this]; RI”a and RI”e explained that what it says “and the adult,” etc., is not for measurement but for the law: if the adult wears it casually he is obligated, for the child is not subject to obligation—and that is what the latter clause means, “the child does not [fulfill],” “even though,” etc., and it does not say “or that the adult [does not].” It is hard to reconcile with what Magen Avraham wrote, for otherwise why “fulfills” rather than “wears it”; and it is difficult for the view that [tzitzis is] the tallis’s obligation; and further, what is the Gemara’s difficulty “but we learned in the Mishnah ‘there is no [wool and linen],’” etc.? One cannot say it asks on what Magen Avraham wrote, “specifically when the child is covered,” etc., for that does not apply to intermittent wearing; in any case it is impossible to resolve that she explains in his commentary per Tosafos Kilayim that there is no intermittent wearing of them, and likewise in his composition chapter 10, halacha 18—so what is the Gemara’s difficulty? Therefore the Rambam must have had a different text, and from this one learns the law stated above of “one who walks alone,” etc.—see; but the Tur and Rema explain like Rashi, unlike RI”ch.
