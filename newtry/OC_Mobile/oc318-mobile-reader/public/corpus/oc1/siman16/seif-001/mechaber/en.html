The measure of a tallit. It contains one seif:
The measure of a tallit obligated in tzitzis is that with it one covers in length and width one's head and the majority of a small child who walks alone in the market and needs no other to watch him:Then it is obligated in tzitzis, specifically when an adult sometimes wears it casually and goes out with it to the market (Beit Yosef in the name of Ben Chabiv and Mahariya).]:
