The measure of a tallis — it contains one clause.
The measure of a tallis obligated in tzitzis is that with it one covers in length and width one’s head and the majority of a small child who walks alone in the market and needs no other to watch him. Rema: Then it is obligated in tzitzis, specifically when an adult sometimes wears it casually and goes out with it to the market (Beit Yosef citing Ben Chabib and Mahariya).
