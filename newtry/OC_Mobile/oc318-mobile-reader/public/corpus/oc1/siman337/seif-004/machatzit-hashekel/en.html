(s.k. 4) Via a gentile permitted, because for amirah we are not particular even if it is psik reisha, as Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 253 s.k. 41. And in Yesh Nochalin he forbids even via a gentile, but he did not explain there the reason for the prohibition.
