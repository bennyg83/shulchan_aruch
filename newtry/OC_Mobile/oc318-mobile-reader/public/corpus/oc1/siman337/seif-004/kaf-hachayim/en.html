(d) There whether large or small, etc. But very large ones are forbidden to drag, for it is psik reisha of making a groove, and forbidden even on marble ground, Beit Yosef in the name of Rokeach; and even though when digging it is a rabbinic melacha (for by Torah law one is not liable until he digs with a hoe and mattock and the like, as is written in the prior note) — nevertheless in psik reisha it is forbidden. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Chokhmat Shlomo note 1, R' Zalman note 1, Chayei Adam general rule 40 note 6; and in a heavy barrel too, even in a marble attic — forbidden. Eshel Avraham note 1; and a child's wagon — even very large — permitted to drag on the ground, for a wagon is different, for it presses down and does not make a groove.
