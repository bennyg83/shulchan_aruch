3) There: Therefore, a person may drag a bed, etc. Even when one intends to make a furrow, dragging a chair or bench does not involve a Torah prohibition. When the chapter Klal Gadol, Shabbos 73b, states that digging is a derivative of threshing, it appears this is a scribal error and should read that it is a derivative of plowing, as stated there in the Gemara and Rashi on page 46b, and that one is liable, this applies when using a hoe or similar implement, but not a bed, chair, or bench. Beis Yosef.
