(c) There therefore drags a bed, etc. And dragging a chair and bench — even if he intends to make a groove there is no d'oraisa prohibition, and when we say in the chapter Kol Gadol (Shabbos 73b) that digging is a derivative of plowing (it appears to me a printing error and it should read of reaping as is in the Gemara and Rashi daf 46b) — that is with a hoe and mattock and the like; but with a bed, chair, and bench — no.
