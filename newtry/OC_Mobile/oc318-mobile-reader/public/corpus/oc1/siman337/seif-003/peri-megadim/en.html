(א) That they break — Taz; and Magen Avraham letter 5; and all detached things have no breaking prohibition; nevertheless it is a vessel and destructive work is exempt but forbidden; and psik reisha — even in rabbinic law one should forbid; and Bach wrote it is like weekday activity; and it also appears when one treats Shabbat prohibition lightly — therefore one should not sweep with long palm-fiber brooms; but via a goose wing permitted to sweep; and if not clear in Magen Avraham, it will be explained further:

(ב) What the Mechaber wrote "one should not set a barrel upright" — meaning that it sits well on the ground, for from its weight when setting upright it levels the ground smoothly, and it is psik reisha; and Or Zarua and Tosafos wrote on this; and Magen Avraham letter 6; and on paved — if they decreed on account of unpaved — Magen Avraham 1; and for one reason one can say permitted, and for Taz's reason one can say forbidden, as we said; see Perishah; and R' Ovadiah letter 4 — muktzeh via blowing permitted in siman 308 s.k. 63; and thus we are concerned for leveling bumps, since there is not so much need as in sweeping — Magen Avraham letter 6:
