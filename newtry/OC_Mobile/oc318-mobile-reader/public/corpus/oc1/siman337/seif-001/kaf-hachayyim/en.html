1) [Seif 1] An unintended result is permitted. The law follows Rabbi Shimon, who states this principle. Beis Yosef. The Gemara concludes at the end of the chapter Kirah that Rabbi Shimon permits an unintended act even when performing it intentionally would violate a Torah prohibition. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1; Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1. This is contrary to Levush.
