דין כיבוד הבית ודבר שאינו מתכוין. ובו ד סעיפים:
The law of honoring the house and davar she'eino mitkaven. And in it are 4 seifim: Davar she'eino mitkaven is permitted, and that is when it is not psik reisha; therefore a person drags a bed, chair, and bench, whether large or small, provided he does not intend to make a groove; permitted to sprinkle the house since he does not intend to level bumps but only so dust should not rise.
