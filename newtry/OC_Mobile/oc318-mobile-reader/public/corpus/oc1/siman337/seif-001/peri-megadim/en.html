the difference between melacha she'einah tzrichah legufah and psik reisha requires study there; and know that davar she'eino mitkaven is permitted for R' Shimon l'chatchila even for a Torah prohibition, as is concluded in Shabbos 46b from those who sell garments and tailors sew kilayim chapter 9 from 46, and halacha is widespread in Yoreh De'ah siman 301 seif 66; nevertheless one can say the modest who are stringent on themselves, etc., as there; but in a rabbinic matter perhaps even the modest are not stringent; and Beit Yosef brought this there and they all wondered — see there; and R' Ovadiah and Tosafos ruled that in Torah law all that is not mitkaven is permitted even l'chatchila; and see Levush who brought this from Beit Yosef and brought siman 276, 277, and 336; and behold regarding grasses the main thing is kil'achar yad, and extinguishing melacha she'einah tzrichah legufah, and the like; and from that of those who sell garments it is clear thus; and I say perhaps in grasses it is mainly kil'achar yad; and perhaps because the Mechaber ruled in Yoreh De'ah 301 that to evade the tax it is forbidden to wear, and those who sell garments are permitted — two mishnayot there in Kilayim 24 per Rambam — see there in Taz and Shach;
