Forbidden to sweep the house unless the ground is paved, and some permit even when it is not paved:And some are stringent even on paved (Tur in the name of R"i, Mordechai at the beginning of the chapter Kol HaKelim, Rabbeinu Yerucham part 13, Semag, and Sefer HaTerumah), and such is the practice and one should not deviate; however, via a non-Jew it is permitted (Rabbeinu Yerucham part 13), and likewise via clothing or a cloth or a soft goose wing, and he does not level bumps (Agur); and forbidden to sweep garments with brooms made of palm fibers lest their fibers break (Hagahot Alfasi chapter Hazorek, and see Hagahot Maimoniyot daf 124).
