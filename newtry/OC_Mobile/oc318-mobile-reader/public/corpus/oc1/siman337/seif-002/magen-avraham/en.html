(א) Forbidden to sweep. For he moves dust and levels bumps; and some permit, for it is davar she'eino mitkaven:

(ב) Paved with stones. And all the more with boards (Beit Zahav, Rosh Hashana):

(ג) Via a non-Jew permitted. And in Yesh Nochalin he forbids even via a non-Jew, only that they practiced to be lenient; and also via a goose wing he forbids — see there chapter 2 of Beitza 133; Beit Yosef wrote that even if there are walnut shells in the house that are muktzeh — permitted to sweep them, for for him it is like a refuse pit; see siman 308 s.k. 27:

(ד) That they break. And certainly even if they break there is no d'oraisa prohibition as is written siman 318 seif 12; nevertheless forbidden as psik reisha, and therefore forbidden to sweep even on paved unless with date-palm and not those made from tree branches — see s.k. 61:
