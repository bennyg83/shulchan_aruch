The ground — Taz: paved with stones, and all the more boards — there is no leveling bumps; and some permit on unpaved, for it is not psik reisha, and like R' Shimon; and some say even on paved — they hold on unpaved (as it should read) it is psik reisha, and they decreed paved on account of unpaved; and even though it is melacha she'einah tzrichah legufah, nevertheless they decreed on it; and therefore when the whole city is paved — one can say like Abaye who permits zilyakha via a non-Jew; but there is another view that even on paved there is leveling bumps between the pavers — see there. And Beit Yosef: if it was not honored before Shabbat — there is reason to forbid, for it levels bumps — see there; nevertheless via a goose wing and the like it is possible to permit this too; and even if there are muktzeh things in the house like walnut shells and the like — moving from the side via something else for Shabbat need; and Yom Tov — there is reason to permit via a non-Jew; see siman 276:
