(a) [Seif 1] Women and slaves are exempt from Keriat Shema, etc. It appears to me that women are also exempt from the blessing of Keriat Shema, for they too have a fixed time for it, as stated at the end of Siman 58. Yet it appears to me that nevertheless they are obligated to say Emes veYatziv, for remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt is from the Torah, as stated in Siman 66, and therefore they must link redemption to prayer (for in prayer they are obligated, as stated in Siman 106 seif 2) — Magen Avraham subnote 1; Mishnah Berurah subnote 1; Eliyah Rabbah subnote 1; Seder Beit Yosef on the laws of Keriat Shema of Maariv subnotes 1–2, and he wrote that likewise at Maariv Emes veEmunah — women are obligated. However, the Peri Megadim in Orach Chayyim subnote 1 wrote that women are not obligated in remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt except rabbinically, see there. And likewise Shaagas Aryeh Siman 12, that women are not obligated in remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt; for this reason see there, and he brought it from Machatzit HaShekel subnote 1. And see Eliyah Rabbah subnote 1. And what Beit Yosef writes there, that the main opinion follows Magen Avraham, is not compelling, for what connection is there between this and that — see there, and see well. And nevertheless, even according to those who say they are exempt — if they fulfill the mitzvah they have reward, only it is not like one who is commanded and performs, as our Sages of blessed memory said in Kiddushin 31a, see there. And see what is written on this in Chakkei Chayim in derush 1 on acquiring a Sefer Torah, see there. And if they wish to bless also the other blessings of Keriat Shema and also Pesukei deZimra and its blessings, the permission is in their hands. Eliyah Rabbah there. Therefore women who know how to learn practice praying the entire order of prayer like men, not less than Parshas HaAkaida until the end of Aleinu:
