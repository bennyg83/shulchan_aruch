Keriat Shema. They are likewise exempt from the blessing of Keriat Shema, for they too have a fixed time; but they are obligated to recite emes ve-yatziv, for mention of the Exodus from Egypt is by Torah law; therefore they must connect redemption to prayer, for they are obligated in prayer, as below siman 106 seif 2.
