Women, etc. It appears to me that women are also exempt from the blessing of Keriat Shema, for they too have a fixed time for it, as stated at the end of Siman 58; and likewise from what Terumat HaDeshen wrote as follows: and even though prayer has a fixed time for it, nevertheless since they said “if only a person would pray the entire day,” it is like a mitzvah that is not time-dependent for it — or alternatively because it is [a matter of] mercy, end quote; and so too in the Gemara it implies they are exempt from these blessings — also from Birkat HaLevanah they are exempt, as stated in Siman 426. Yet it appears to me that nevertheless they are obligated to say Emes veYatziv, for remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt is from the Torah, as stated in Siman 66, and therefore they must link redemption to prayer.
