Women, etc. — it seems to me women are also exempt from the blessing of Keriat Shema for they also have a fixed time as written siman 58; and so from what Terumat HaDeshen wrote, etc.: and although prayer has a fixed time, nevertheless since they said 'would that a person could pray all day' like a commandment that time does not limit it; or because it is supplication — end quote; and so in the Gemara it is implied they are exempt from these blessings; also from the blessing of the moon they are exempt as written siman 426. However it seems to me they are nevertheless obligated to say Emet veYatziv for remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt is from the Torah as written siman 66; and if so they need to adjoin redemption to prayer:
