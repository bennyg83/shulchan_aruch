Seif 1—women and slaves. In the Yerushalmi he derives it, for it is written: Hear, O Israel, the Omnipresent our the Omnipresent, the Omnipresent is One—you, who have no master except the Holy One, blessed be He. This excludes one who has another master; therefore a half-slave and half-free man is also exempt. However, per the final mishnah, they compel his master to free him—he is obligated; see chapter 1 of Chagigah, page 4a. Nevertheless, there is a practical difference where the Omnipresent is a minor, for it is impossible to compel him; or if he is a eunuch; or if he already fulfilled six [years] when he was a gentile before he became a slave—for those, since they cannot be compelled, he is exempt from Kriat Shema for the reason of the Yerushalmi.
