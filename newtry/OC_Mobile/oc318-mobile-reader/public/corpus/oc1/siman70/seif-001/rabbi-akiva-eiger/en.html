Seif 1 — women and slaves. In the Jerusalem Talmud he derives it from what is written 'Hashem our God, Hashem is One' — excluding one who has a master other than the Holy One blessed be He; per this half-slave and half-free is also exempt; however per the final mishnah that they compel his master to free — obligated. See chapter 1 of Chagigah 4a.
