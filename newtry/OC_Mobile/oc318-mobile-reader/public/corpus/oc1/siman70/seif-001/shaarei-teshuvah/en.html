Kriat Shema—Ba'er Heitev wrote in the name of the Magen Avraham that in Aleinu women are obligated; but in Sha'agat Aryeh, siman 13, he wrote as follows: One should wonder about our women, for many women do not recite Kriat Shema and its blessings because they are exempt and are not careful to mention the Exodus from Egypt; but I say that regarding similar matters they said, Leave Israel alone, etc.—for certainly women are exempt, for it is a positive time-bound commandment; even though it applies by day and by night, nevertheless the daytime remembrance is an independent mitzvah, and if he did not mention it by day he need not mention this remembrance at night; and what he mentions at night is an independent mitzvah, etc.—see there. And what he wrote on the words of the Magen Avraham, siman 58, in what he challenged against the Kesef Mishnah—see there.
