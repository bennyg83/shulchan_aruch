Keriat Shema — Baer Hetev wrote in the name of Magen Avraham that in Emet veYatziv women are obligated; but in Sha'agat Aryeh siman 13 he wrote: it is fitting to wonder on our women that many women who do not read Keriat Shema and its blessings because they are exempt and are not careful to mention the Exodus from Egypt; but I say that on similar matters they said 'leave Israel alone,' etc. — certainly women are exempt for it is a positive time-bound commandment; although it applies by day and night, nevertheless the daytime remembrance is a mitzvah in itself and if he did not mention it by day he need not mention this remembrance at night; and what he mentions at night is a mitzvah in itself, etc. — see there.
