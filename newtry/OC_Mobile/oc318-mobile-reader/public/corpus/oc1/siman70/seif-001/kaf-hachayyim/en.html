(א) [Seif 1] Women and slaves are exempt from Keriat Shema, etc. — it seems to me women are also exempt from the blessing of Keriat Shema for they also have a fixed time as written siman 58. However it seems to me they are nevertheless obligated to say Emet veYatziv for remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt is from the Torah as written siman 66; and if so they need to adjoin redemption to prayer (for in prayer they are obligated as written siman 106 seif 2). Magen Avraham note 1. Mishnah Berurah in Biur Halacha note 1. Razah note 1. Siddur Bei Yankev in laws of evening Keriat Shema note 12 and wrote that likewise Emet veEmunah in the evening — women are also obligated. However Pri Megadim in Eshel Avraham note 1 wrote women are not obligated in remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt except rabbinically — see there. And likewise Sha'agat Aryeh siman 12 that women are not obligated in remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt — see there and Machatzit HaShekel brought it note 1. See also Avnei Nezer note 1. And what Bei Yankev wrote there that the essence per Magen Avraham is not necessary — what does this have to do with that — see there and examine. And nevertheless even per those who say they are exempt, if they fulfill the mitzvah they have reward, only it is not like one commanded and fulfilling as our Sages said in Kiddushin 31a — see there. And see what is written on this in Sefer Chakkei Chaim in derush 1 of acquiring a Torah scroll — see there. And if they wish to bless also the other blessings of Keriat Shema and also pesukei dezimra and its blessings — permission is in their hands. Razah there. And therefore women who know how to learn pray the entire order of prayer like men — no less from Akedah until the end of Alenu leShabeach:
