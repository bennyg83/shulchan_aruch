Seif 1, because, etc.—Gemara there.

It is proper, etc., and they should read, etc.: this is a mere stringency, unlike another authority who wrote that women are obligated—for it does not appear so in the Gemara, for the one who says since, etc.; and in Yerushalmi there: slaves—from where [is it derived]? as it is said "Hear, O Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is one"—[this speaks of] one who has no master but the Holy One blessed be He; excluded is the slave who has another master.
