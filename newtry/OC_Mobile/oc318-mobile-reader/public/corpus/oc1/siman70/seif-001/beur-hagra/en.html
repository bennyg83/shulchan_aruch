Seif 1: Because, etc. Gemara there.
And it is proper, etc., and they should recite, etc. This is merely an additional stringency, unlike Ateres Zekeinim, who wrote that women are obligated, for the Gemara does not imply this, as it says, according to the one who says, since, etc. In the Yerushalmi there: From where do we know regarding slaves? As it is stated, 'Hear, Israel, Hashem our God, Hashem is One.' This includes one who has no master other than the Holy One, blessed be He, excluding a slave who has another master.
