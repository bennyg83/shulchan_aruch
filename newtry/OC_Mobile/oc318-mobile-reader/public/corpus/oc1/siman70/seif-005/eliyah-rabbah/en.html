[5] And per Raavad, etc. Levush wrote, and Malbushei Yom Tov wrote — see below siman 235 seif 5 that anonymously it is like Raavad — until here. And to my humble opinion here it deals where he began before the time of obligation arrived, as Beit Yosef wrote in the second resolution; and therefore Levush was clever and wrote 'and the time of Keriat Shema arrived' which implies he began before the time. But in siman 235 it deals where he began after the time arrived as he explicitly wrote 'if he began to eat after the time of Keriat Shema arrived he stops,' etc.; and if so it is clarified that on the contrary anonymously it is like Rambam. And behold Bach understood Shulchan Arukh's words that he rules like the first resolution and therefore wondered that the essence is the second resolution. And he did not examine precisely, for Shulchan Arukh copied Rambam's wording; and Beit Yosef already proved and so from Rambam's own wording that it deals where he began before the time arrived; also in Bach chapter 2 of Laws of Keriat Shema he concludes thus. And from this there is also difficulty on Magen Avraham — also what he wrote that one can wonder on Beit Yosef wonder of wonders that even Raavad agrees where he began before the time he does not stop, for there is no one who disputes this; rather Raavad did not bother to be precise in Rambam's words and thought it deals after the time arrived — until here. And I say his words are wondrous that Raavad erred in what is clear and proven in Rambam; and what he wrote that no one disputes this — it is not so, for below siman 252 he wrote that there are those proven from Tosafot Sukkah 38 s.v. mai, etc., that even if he began before the time he stops. Also I wrote in this siman that Rambam and Rabbenu Yerucham hold thus that even if he began to study before the time he stops for Keriat Shema; however in this one can say eating is stricter than words of Torah, for in words of Torah he does not stop for it is not an interruption since Keriat Shema is also words of Torah — which is not so in eating and the like he does not stop as Kesef Mishneh wrote there.
