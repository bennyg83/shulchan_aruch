Seif katan 5: He should not interrupt, etc. And also for 'Blessed is the name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever,' because that passage likewise contains acceptance of the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven, as does the first verse. See above, siman 61, seif katan 11, what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Levush. For this reason Levush also wrote here in seif 1 that it is proper to teach them to accept upon themselves the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven and to recite at least the first verse, and that one should also teach them to say 'Blessed is the name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever.' He may recite it as one reading Torah, but should not say the blessings of Krias Shema. These are the words of Migdal Oz on chapter 4 of Hilchos Krias Shema, concerning Rambam's ruling that wherever one is exempt from Krias Shema, if he wishes to be stringent he may recite it. But if the exempt person is agitated, he may not recite until his mind has settled. Raavad objected: What is wrong with this? Let him recite and be as one reading Torah, and let him not appear to cast off the yoke of Heaven, etc. Migdal Oz wrote that Rambam also agrees to this, except that he should not say the blessings of Krias Shema. The phrase 'in Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam' means that he inferred from the Tur that even nowadays, when other people also do not concentrate properly, if the exempt person is so agitated and bewildered that he cannot concentrate as other people do, he may not recite.
