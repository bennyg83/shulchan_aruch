(Subnote 5) He should not interrupt, etc. And in Shema with its surrounding [blessings], for in Shema with its surrounding there is acceptance of the Kingdom of Heaven like in the first verse. And see above Siman 61 subnote 11 what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Levush; and for this reason Levush wrote also here in seif 1 that he wrote: it is proper to teach them to accept upon themselves the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven and to read at least one verse, for even in Shema with its surrounding one teaches him so, like one reading Torah — only he should not say the blessing of Keriat Shema — and these are the words of Migdal Oz part 4 of Hilchot Keriat Shema on what Rambam wrote, like wherever one who is exempt from Keriat Shema, if he wishes to be strict, reads. But if that exempt person was panicked, he is not permitted to read until his mind settles — and Raavad challenged, and he wrote as follows: what of it — he reads and it is like reading Torah, and it will not be like removing the Kingdom of Heaven from upon him, end quote — and on this Magen Avraham wrote that to this the Rambam agrees too, only that he should not say the blessing of Keriat Shema. And Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam — meaning one learns from Tur’s words that he holds even nowadays, when also other people do not have intent — nevertheless if that exempt person is panicked and amazed and cannot settle his mind like other people, he is not permitted to read, end quote.
