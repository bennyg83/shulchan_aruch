(Subnote 5) And see Mishnah Berurah subnote 3 subnote 4, for there it is explained that there is an opinion that a mitzvah does not require intent to fulfill one’s obligation; therefore if he intends like one reading Torah he may read and it helps according to that opinion. But according to those who say mitzvot require intent — if so, since he intends like one reading Torah, what gain is there in his reading? So it appears to me was his intent. And there is still a question: for even according to those who say a mitzvah does not require intent, nevertheless since he intends like one reading Torah, it is as if he intends not to fulfill his obligation, and he does not fulfill it.
