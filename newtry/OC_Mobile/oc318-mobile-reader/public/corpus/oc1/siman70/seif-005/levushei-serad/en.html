Seif katan 5. See siman 63, seif 4, where it is explained that some authorities hold that mitzvos do not require intent to discharge one's obligation. Therefore, if he intends merely to read as one reads from the Torah, he may recite it and it will be effective according to that opinion. According to the opinion that mitzvos require intent, however, since he intends merely to read from the Torah, what benefit is there in his recitation? This appears to be Magen Avraham's intent. It nevertheless requires study, for even according to the opinion that mitzvos do not require intent, if he specifically intends to read merely from the Torah, this is like intending not to discharge his obligation, and he does not fulfill it.
