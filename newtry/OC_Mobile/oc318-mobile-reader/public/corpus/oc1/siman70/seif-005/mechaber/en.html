If one was occupied with eating, or was in the bathhouse, or occupied with a haircut, or processing hides, or was occupied with a civil matter — per Rambam: he finishes and afterward reads Keriat Shema; and if he feared lest the time for reading pass and he stopped and read — behold this is praiseworthy. And per Raavad he stops and reads even though there is time to read [see below siman 235]:
