If one was occupied with eating, or was in the bathhouse, or occupied with a haircut, or processing hides, or was occupied with a civil matter - according to the Rambam: he finishes and afterwards recites the Recitation of the Sh'ma. And if he was worried that the time to say [Sh'ma] would pass, and he stopped and read, behold, this is praiseworthy. And according to the Raavad, one stops and recites even though there would be enough time to recite (afterwards). [See below: Siman 235][]:
