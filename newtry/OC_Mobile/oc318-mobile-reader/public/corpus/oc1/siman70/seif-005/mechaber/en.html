If one was occupied with eating, or was in the bathhouse, or was occupied with a haircut, or was processing hides, or was occupied with a lawsuit — for the Rambam: one finishes and afterwards recites Keriat Shema. And if one feared that the time for recitation would pass, and one stopped and recited, this is praiseworthy. For the Raavad, one interrupts and recites even though there would remain enough time to recite afterwards.[See below Siman 235.][]:
