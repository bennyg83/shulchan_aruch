(e) There. And it is proper to teach them to accept upon themselves the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven — that is, the first verse, as Beit Yosef states in the name of Ohel Moed. And likewise the Levush, that it is proper to teach them to accept upon themselves the Kingdom of Heaven as Maran wrote, and likewise Peri Chadash subnote 1; Mishnah Berurah in introduction to Magen Avraham subnote 1; Or Tzaddik subnote 1; Yaavetz in Hagahot Beit Yosef; Maamar Mordechai subnote 1; Terumat HaDeshen subnote 1; Shaarei Teshuvah 94a; Eliyah Rabbah subnote 1; Seder Beit Yosef on the laws of Keriat Shema of Maariv subnote 1. And not like Bach — and Eliyah Rabbah brought subnote 1 that they are obligated to accept upon themselves the Kingdom of Heaven, see there — but only that it is proper and fitting to teach them, etc. And likewise it is proper and fitting to teach them Shema with its surrounding [blessings] with one verse, for included in one verse is [what was said], as I wrote Siman 66 seif 1 and in our words there subnote 13 and Siman 61 subnote 45. And likewise Levush and Mishnah Berurah and TaZ on this seif and Shaarei Teshuvah there; Knesset HaGedolah Siman 11 subnote 3.
