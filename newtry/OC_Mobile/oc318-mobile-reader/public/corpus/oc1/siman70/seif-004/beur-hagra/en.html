Seif 4, if he was, etc. Rambam, and Beit Yosef writes that it is from Yerushalmi end chapter 5: R' Yirmiyah said—one may not stand and pray except from a matter of halachah; R' Yirmiyah said—one engaged in communal needs is like one engaged in words of Torah; and Rambam explains accordingly and cites there two teachings of R' Yirmiyah together; and likewise Kesef Mishneh in chapter 6 of Hilchos Tefillah, as I wrote above siman 93; and Rosh explains that it refers higher up, to standing from within it to pray, as he wrote. But here one need not say all this, for it is explicit in Tosefta chapter 1: Rabbi Yehudah said—I was walking after R' Akiva and after R' Elazar ben Azaryah; the time for Keriat Shema arrived; it seemed to me they had given up reading, but they were busy with communal needs; I read and repeated, and afterward they began, and the sun was already visible on the mountaintops; and likewise Yerushalmi chapter 1 and Tosafos there; see the commentaries on those passages. Beit Yosef's words are puzzling, for behold one engaged in words of Torah is obligated in Keriat Shema—even Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai and his colleagues in prayer: from this one learns correctly that it means like Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai and his colleagues, as Rambam writes there; and likewise Lechem Mishneh asked, and likewise Maggid Mishneh rejected there.

And they read, etc. Meaning one need not repeat all that, as I wrote there; it seemed to me, etc.
