Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of Rambam that specifically in earlier generations, when people dealt with them for Heaven's sake—even nowadays, if they are involved even in saving their property and there is no one who will exert himself except him—he does not interrupt, for one engaged in a mitzvah [is exempt], so conclude.

Their activity. Nevertheless he should read the first verse and Shema and its blessings if possible. If one is exempt from reading yet reads anyway, he is called an ignoramus with no authority. Darkei Moshe implies that if his intent is like one reading from the Torah, it is fine—Magen Avraham, Eliyah Rabbah, and Yad Aharon. Magen Avraham writes, and it appears to me in any case, that he should say a passage that contains mention of the Exodus from Egypt even if the time for Keriat Shema has passed.
