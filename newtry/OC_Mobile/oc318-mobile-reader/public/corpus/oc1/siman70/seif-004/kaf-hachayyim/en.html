4) There: Because it is a time-bound positive mitzvah. Women are likewise exempt from the blessing over the new moon because it is a time-bound positive mitzvah. Although women perform other positive mitzvos, such as sukkah, they do not perform this mitzvah because they caused the moon's diminishment.
