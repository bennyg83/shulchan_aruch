(16) In communal needs—it deals with a case where there is no one to attend to it except him; and therefore he does not stop, for one engaged in a mitzvah [is exempt from another mitzvah], even if he is engaged only in saving their property.

(17) And when the time arrived, etc.—this implies that if he began another prohibited matter when the time arrived, he must stop; and see in the Peri Megadim, who leans that in every case he need not stop.

(18) He should not stop—and if he can stop for Kriat Shema and afterward return and complete the communal needs without bother, he should stop. The Peri Megadim wrote that even though one engaged in communal needs is exempt from Kriat Shema, nevertheless if he stopped and read, he properly fulfills his obligation; for he is not truly exempt from Kriat Shema, but rather he is then engaged in another mitzvah.

(19) He should complete their affairs—and nevertheless he should do what he can and recite at least the first verse with b'shekhivah u'mishkav in its time, for this is a short matter and possible for him even at the hour when he is engaged in communal needs.

(20) If time remains—and if time does not remain, what should he do? Behold, he is engaged in a mitzvah; nevertheless he is obligated to mention the Exodus from Egypt even after the time of Kriat Shema has passed, for this is an independent mitzvah and its time is all day; therefore he should say some passage that contains the Exodus from Egypt, if the time has already passed that he cannot say the blessing of Emet veYatziv, as explained in siman 58—and see the Mishna Berurah there; and see below in siman 93, seif 4, regarding prayer, and in the Mishna Berurah there as well. And know that if the time of his engagement in communal needs extends until after chatzot, when he is exempt according to all opinions even from prayer—nevertheless, all agree it is forbidden to eat before he fulfills the mitzvah of tefillin, whose time is all day; and also according to what the Peri Megadim wrote in siman 89, it is forbidden for him then to eat before he prays the Minchah prayer; therefore when half an hour after chatzot arrives, he should pray the Minchah prayer and afterward eat; and see in Machaneh Yisrael, chapter 7.
