Seif 3: Three days, etc. The Tur wrote four days and four nights, meaning that if he married on Wednesday before Shacharis, he is exempt through Motzaei Shabbos, inclusive. For this reason he is exempt during Shabbos because of the marital relations of the following night. Similarly, in the final chapter of Niddah it states, until Motzaei Shabbos, four nights. Although Kesubos 6b says that he is exempt even on the third day, and does not say on the fourth, this is because on the fourth day he is obligated. The Shulchan Aruch says three days because the usual practice is to marry after the morning Krias Shema. Nevertheless, it did not explain this properly, for it should have said four nights, as Rabbeinu Yonah indeed wrote.
