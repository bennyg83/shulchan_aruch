Seif 3, three days, etc. In Tur it says four days and four nights—meaning if he married on Wednesday before Shacharis until Motza'ei Shabbos inclusive; therefore he is exempt on Shabbos day because of marital relations the night after it; and likewise in the last chapter of Niddah until Motza'ei Shabbos, four nights. That which it says in Kesuvos 6b that he is exempt even on Wednesday—and there is no question on Wednesday because on Wednesday he is liable—and Shulchan Aruch says three days because the usual practice is to marry after Keriat Shema of Shacharis; nevertheless it did not explain well that it should have said four nights as well, and likewise in Tosafos Yom Tov.
