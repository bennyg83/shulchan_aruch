One who marries a virgin, etc., is exempt because he is preoccupied with a matter of a mitzvah; but if he is preoccupied with a matter that is not a mitzvah—for example, his ship sank at sea or the like—he is not exempt from Keriat Shema (Berachos 14a).

But nowadays, etc., one who marries a virgin reads [Keriat Shema]; and just as he is obligated in Keriat Shema, he is likewise obligated in prayer and in tefillin (responsa Maharam no. 132; see below siman 106 seif 1).
