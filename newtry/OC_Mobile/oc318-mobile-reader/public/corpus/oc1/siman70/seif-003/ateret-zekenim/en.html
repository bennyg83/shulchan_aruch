One who marries a virgin, etc., is exempt because he is preoccupied with a mitzvah. However, one who is preoccupied with a matter that is not a mitzvah, such as when his ship sank at sea or the like, is not exempt from Krias Shema (Berachos 14).
But nowadays, etc. One who marries a virgin recites Krias Shema; just as he is obligated in Krias Shema, he is also obligated in prayer and tefillin (Responsa of Maharam of Rothenburg, no. 132; see below, siman 106, seif 1).
