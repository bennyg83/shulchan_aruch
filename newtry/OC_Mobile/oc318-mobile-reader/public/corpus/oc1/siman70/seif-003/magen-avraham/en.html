Three days. If he entered the chuppah before reciting the morning Krias Shema, he is exempt for four days, according to the Beit Yosef. If he wishes to be stringent with himself and recite it, he is not permitted to do so because it appears arrogant. The Rosh, Rif, and Rambam write that we are not concerned for arrogance. The Beit Yosef writes that according to all opinions, the halachah in our Mishnah follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. This requires study, for in Choshen Mishpat, siman 163, they rule contrary to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. See there. The Kenesses HaGedolah likewise writes this in the name of a gloss. See also Tosafos Yom Tov to Pesachim, chapter 4, Mishnah 5; Rabbi Yosef HaLevi, responsum 46; Beit Yosef, Choshen Mishpat, siman 231; and the Rashba's responsa, siman 41.
