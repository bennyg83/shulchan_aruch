Three days. And if he entered the chuppah before he read Keriat Shema of Shacharit, he is exempt four days (Beit Yosef). And if he wished to be strict upon himself and read, he is not permitted, for it appears as yuhara — Rosh, Rif, and Rambam wrote that we are not concerned for yuhara. And Rav Bachya wrote that all agree the halacha follows Rabban Gamliel in our Mishnah; and there is a question, for in Choshen Mishpat Siman 163 they ruled not like Rabban Gamliel, see there; and likewise [sources] cite in the name of the Gra and Tosafos Yom Tov chapter 4 of Pesachim mishnah 5, and in Rav Yehuda HaLevi Siman 46, and in Beit Yosef Choshen Mishpat Siman 231, and in responsa Rashba Siman 41.

He reads and prays. (Terumat HaDeshen Siman 6.) See what is written Siman 38 subnote 7.
