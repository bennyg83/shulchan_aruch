Kriat Shema, three days—in the Tur it is written four days, and he challenged in the name of Abuhav: but we find only three days from the marriage, which is on the fourth day until Motza'ei Shabbat; and on the fourth he reads in the morning. And the answer of the Beit Yosef is that also on the fourth day before the chuppah he is exempt, for he is engaged in a mitzvah all day in preparation for the marriage; and whoever is engaged in a mitzvah is exempt from another mitzvah, as the Rama wrote in siman 38, if he needs to be engaged even at the time of Kriat Shema and prayer—and all this specifically in their time, as he concludes here in Shulchan Aruch.
