One who consummates marriage with a virgin is exempt from Keriat Shema for three days if he did not perform the act (marital relations), because he is preoccupied with the preoccupation of the mitzvah. And this applied only in the time of the early generations; but nowadays, when the rest of people also do not have proper intent, one who consummates marriage with a virgin also recites.See also Siman 99 regarding whether one who is drunk recites Keriat Shema.
