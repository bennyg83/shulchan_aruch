He who consummates a marriage with a virgin is exempt from the Recitation of the Sh'ma for three days if he did not do "the act" (marital relations) because he is anxious due to the anxiety of the commandment [of marital relations]. And this was the case only in the time of the early ones, but nowadays, when also the rest of the people are not able to have proper focus [for the Sh'ma], one who consummates a marriage with a virgin also recites [the Sh'ma].Rema: See also Siman 99 [regarding] if one who is drunk should recite the Recitation of the Sh'ma.
