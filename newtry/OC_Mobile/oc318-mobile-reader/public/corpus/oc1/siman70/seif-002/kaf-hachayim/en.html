Women and slaves are exempt, etc. A half-slave and half-freeman: as the last mishnah rules, we force his master to free him — he is obligated; and where the master is a minor, for it is impossible to force him, or where he already fulfilled Peah when he was a gentile before he became a slave — in those cases, since we do not force [the master], he is exempt from Keriat Shema per the Yerushalmi — Gra. And the Peri Megadim in Orach Chayyim subnote 1 wrote that our Gemara implies the Yerushalmi is not [accepted], for in a stam mishnah “slaves” implies completely [slaves]; here half-slave half-freeman they are obligated, as concludes Chagigah, see there. And see below Siman 589 seif 5. And a tumtum and an androgynus are obligated from doubt, as stated above Siman 17 seif 2 and below Siman 589 seif 4.
