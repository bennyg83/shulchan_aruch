(Magen Avraham subnote 2) Shacharit. Beit Yosef wrote two resolutions on what the Tur wrote regarding the four days: resolution one was brought in the TaZ, and what Magen Avraham wrote is resolution two; and even though Beit Yosef in his second resolution wrote like the custom in Egypt that they wed the virgin on Tuesday night, Tur already challenged Darkei Moshe that [the mishnah] applies specifically on the fourth day, as we learned, so that he may rise early to rabbinical court; therefore Magen Avraham wrote that he should enter the chuppah on Tuesday before Keriat Shema is read.
