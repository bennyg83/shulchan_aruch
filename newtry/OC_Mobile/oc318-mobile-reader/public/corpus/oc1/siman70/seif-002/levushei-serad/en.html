Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. Morning. The Beit Yosef gives two answers concerning the Tur's statement that the exemption lasts four days. The first answer is cited by the Taz, while the answer cited by Magen Avraham is the second. Although in his second answer the Beit Yosef writes that this accords with the custom in Egypt, where a virgin is brought under the chuppah on Wednesday night, see there, the Darchei Moshe already objects that the Mishnah specifically says Wednesday by day, so that the husband may rise early and go to beis din. Magen Avraham therefore writes that he should enter the chuppah on Wednesday before dawn.
