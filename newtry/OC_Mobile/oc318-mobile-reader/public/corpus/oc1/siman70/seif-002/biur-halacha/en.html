Minors are exempt, etc., and according to Rashi even if he reached, etc.—Behold Vilna Gaon wrote that according to Rashi they are exempt also from the blessings of Keriat Shema; and ostensibly one should examine, for perhaps specifically the time of Keriat Shema, which extends only until the third hour, is not common for him, whereas afterward it is—but from Rashi's words in Berachos that prayer is supplication, etc.; see there—that it likewise extends afterward; nevertheless he needed this reason. Nor is it reasonable to be stricter about the blessings of Keriat Shema than about Keriat Shema itself; and women with the blessing emes ve-yatziv are different for Magen Avraham.
