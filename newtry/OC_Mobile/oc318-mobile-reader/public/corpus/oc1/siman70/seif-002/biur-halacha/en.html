Minors are exempt, etc., and according to Rashi even if they have reached, etc. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes that, according to Rashi, they are also exempt from the blessings of Krias Shema. At first glance, this requires consideration, for perhaps only the time for Krias Shema, which extends merely until the end of the third hour, is not commonly available to the child, whereas the time for its blessings continues afterward. However, Rashi’s words on 20b, where he writes that prayer is a request for mercy, etc., indicate otherwise. See there, for the time for prayer also continues afterward, yet Rashi still required that reason. Furthermore, it is not reasonable to be more stringent regarding the blessings of Krias Shema than regarding Krias Shema itself. The Magen Avraham’s ruling concerning women and the blessing Emes V’Yatziv is different.
