Minors are exempt according to Rabbeinu Tam - when they have not reached [the age of] education; and according to Rashi, even when they have reached [the age of] education because he is not usually with him (i.e. the father) at the time of the Recitation of the Sh'ma in the evening and he (i.e. the child) sleeps in the morning. And it is proper to act according to Rabbeinu Tam.
