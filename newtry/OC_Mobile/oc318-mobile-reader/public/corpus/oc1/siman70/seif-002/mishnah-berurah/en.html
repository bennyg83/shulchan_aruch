(6) According to Rabbeinu Tam, etc. Once a child has reached the age of education, approximately six or seven years old, his father must train him as with all other mitzvos. He must also train him to recite Krias Shema at its proper time, together with the blessings before and after it.
(7) When he has not reached, etc. Nevertheless, once the child knows how to speak, his father has a mitzvah to teach him the first verse of Krias Shema, though not specifically at the time for Krias Shema. [Gemara.]
(8) In the morning. Meaning, because of this difficulty the Sages did not impose upon his father the obligation to train him. See the Bach, who writes that when the child is twelve years old, according to all opinions his father must train him in this.
(9) It is proper to follow this practice. Meaning, although Rashi's view appears correct according to the strict law, and the Gra likewise ruled so, it is nevertheless proper to follow Rabbeinu Tam. This also appears from the Rambam. See Lechem Mishneh and Pnei Moshe on the Yerushalmi. Regarding prayer, however, everyone agrees that a child who has reached the age of education is obligated, as stated below in siman 106, seif 2.
