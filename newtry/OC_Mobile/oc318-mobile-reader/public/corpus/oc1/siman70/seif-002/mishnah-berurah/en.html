(6) According to Rabbeinu Tam, etc.—but when he reaches the age of education, when he is already six or already seven, his father is obligated to educate him, as with all other mitzvot; and also that he read it at the time of Kriat Shema with the blessings before and after it.

(7) When he has not reached [that age], etc.—nevertheless, the first verse of Kriat Shema—it is a mitzvah upon his father to teach him from when he knows how to speak, and not specifically at the time of Kriat Shema. [Gemara].

(8) In the morning—meaning, for this reason they did not impose upon his father to educate him [in the morning Shema]; and see in the Bach, who wrote that if he is twelve years old, all agree that it was imposed upon his father to educate him in this.

(9) And it is fitting to conduct oneself [thus]—meaning, even though by law it is reasonable according to Rashi, and thus wrote the Gra, may his memory be blessed; nevertheless, it is proper to conduct oneself according to Rabbeinu Tam, and this is also implied from the words of the Rambam. See in Lechem Chamudos and in the Peri Megadim on the Yerushalmi. But for prayer, all agree he is obligated if he reached the age of education, as below in siman 106, seif 2.
