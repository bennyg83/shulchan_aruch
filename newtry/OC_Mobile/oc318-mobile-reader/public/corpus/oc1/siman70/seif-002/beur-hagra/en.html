Seif 2, minors, etc.—Mishnah there.

According to Rabbenu Tam, etc., and according to Rashi, etc.—Tosafos there s.v. ve-ha-ketanim, etc.; and Yerushalmi there is like Rashi, who says there: minors—from where [is it derived]? as it is said "So that the Torah of the Lord be in your mouth" at the time he is accustomed to it; and without that, Rabbenu Tam's explanation is forced, as Tosafos wrote. What was difficult for him from what is said at the end of chapter 3 of Sukkah—a small child who knows to speak: there it is not because of Keriat Shema and the time of Keriat Shema, but to teach him Torah—"they shall teach him these two verses"; and see above siman 37.
