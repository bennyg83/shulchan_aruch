Seif 2: Minors, etc. Mishnah there.
According to Rabbeinu Tam, etc., and according to Rashi, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. 'and minors,' etc. The Yerushalmi appears to follow Rashi, for it says there: From where do we know regarding minors? As it is stated, 'So that the Torah of Hashem shall be in your mouth,' at the time when he is regularly occupied with it. Apart from this, Rabbeinu Tam's explanation is strained, as Tosafos wrote. As for his difficulty from that which is stated at the end of the third chapter of Sukkah concerning a minor who knows how to speak, there it is not because of Krias Shema or the time of Krias Shema. Rather, in order to teach him Torah, one teaches him these two verses. See above, the end of siman 37.
