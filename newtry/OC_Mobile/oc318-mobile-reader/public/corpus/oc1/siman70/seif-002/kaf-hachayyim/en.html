2) There: Women and slaves are exempt, etc. A person who is half slave and half free is obligated according to the final Mishnah, which rules that his master is compelled to free him. Where the master is a minor and therefore cannot be compelled, or where the slave had already fulfilled the mitzvah to be fruitful and multiply while he was a non-Jew, before becoming a slave, he is not compelled to free him; according to the Yerushalmi, such a half slave is exempt from Krias Shema. Hagahos Rabbi Akiva Eiger. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1, writes that our Gemara implies that the Yerushalmi's ruling is rejected. The Mishnah states simply that "slaves" are exempt, implying only complete slaves, whereas one who is half slave and half free is obligated, as the Gemara concludes in Chagigah; see there.
