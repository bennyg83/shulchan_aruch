(25) Bulmus - He is in danger of death through this, and when his sight returns it is known that he has recovered. It is stated in the Gemara that the primary sign that his eyes have lit up is when he can distinguish between the taste of good food and the taste of bad food.
(26) Until his eyes light up - The wording implies that in this case there is no need to be exact and give him less than the measure, as above, because through waiting he may come into danger.
(27) See above, siman 328, se'if 14 - It is explained there that since he needs to eat immediately and the neveilah is prepared, it is better to feed him neveilos.
