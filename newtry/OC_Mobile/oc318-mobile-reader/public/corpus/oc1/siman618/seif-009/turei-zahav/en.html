We feed them little, etc. In Maharil in the name of Maharshal of Austria, it is said that they do not give him the full measure at once, but we do not practice this way; rather, we say that in a doubt involving life we rule leniently, place food before him, and say to him, 'Today is Yom Kippur; if he eats, permission is in his hand.' It seems to me that one must say to him: if you know that there is danger to you if you do not eat the measure, then eat it; and if not, first eat less than the measure. For if one says to him only, 'Today is Yom Kippur,' he will think that according to the law it is forbidden for him to eat, and he will bring himself into danger.
