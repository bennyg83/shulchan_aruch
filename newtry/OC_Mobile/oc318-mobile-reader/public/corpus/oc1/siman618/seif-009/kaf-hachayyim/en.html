9) There: Even if the ill person says, ‘I do not need it,’ one listens to the doctor. Perhaps the patient is confused and, because of his great weakness, does not perceive his illness; in a case of possible danger one is lenient. Levush. This implies that even if the patient himself is an expert doctor, one nevertheless does not listen to him, because his illness has confused him and he does not know his condition. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 2; Mateh Efraim, paragraph 1; Mishna Berurah, paragraph 3.
