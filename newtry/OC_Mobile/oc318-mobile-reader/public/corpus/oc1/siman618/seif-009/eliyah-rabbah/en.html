[9] [Levush:] For the sick person's 'I do not know,' etc. It appears from this that if the sick person himself understands and is a physician, his 'I do not know' is like another physician's statement (Taz), and so it implies in the Perishah. However, this requires analysis to me, for perhaps even though he is a physician he does not understand regarding himself, for a physician is not a physician for himself; this requires analysis.
