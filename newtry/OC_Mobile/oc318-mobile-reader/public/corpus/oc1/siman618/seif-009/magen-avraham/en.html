One is more severe than the other. Terumah is more severe than tevel, and tevel than neveilah, and neveilah than shevi'is. If there is only tevel, then if it is grain, even if it was grown in an unperforated pot, we tithe it on Shabbos before feeding him, because we decree because of a perforated pot. If it is a vegetable tithe, which is rabbinic, we feed him without tithing [Gemara, Yoma]. This requires analysis regarding the Rambam, chapter 14 of Ma'achalos Asuros, who wrote plainly: tevel and terumah, if it is impossible to fix the tevel, meaning such as where what remains will not be enough, we feed him tevel, for it is not holy like terumah. End quote. He did not distinguish between vegetable tithe and produce. We rule that the halachah follows Rabbi against his colleague. Perhaps the Rambam speaks there on a weekday.
