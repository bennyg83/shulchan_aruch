(Se'if katan 9) One, etc. Terumah is more severe than tevel, even though both are punishable by death, because tevel can be permitted through separating terumah, but terumah has no permit for a non-kohen.
And tevel is more severe than neveilah, because tevel is punishable by death, while neveilah is a negative prohibition.
And neveilah is more severe than shevi'is. Rashi in Yoma 83a explained that shevi'is means shevi'is produce that remained after the time of bi'ur, whose prohibition is a positive commandment. However, the Rambam in chapter 14 of Hilchos Ma'achalos Asuros wrote: shevi'is means shevi'is aftergrowths, whose prohibition is rabbinic, as will be explained in Hilchos Shemitah, beginning of chapter 4. This implies that shevi'is after the time of bi'ur is not lighter than tevel, and it requires explanation why. I saw in Tosafos Yom HaKippurim there that he awakened to this, see there, and wrote that necessarily the Rambam holds that a positive commandment is more severe than a negative commandment; therefore, where neveilah has a negative commandment and shevi'is after bi'ur is a positive commandment, we feed him neveilah. See there, where he discusses this at length.
