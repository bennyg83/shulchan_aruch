(17) We feed them little by little - This speaks where this is enough for them to quiet their hunger, as below in se'if 8.
(18) About two thirds, etc. - The same applies to a little more, provided that it is not close to an egg, for in that case there is liability.
(19) And they should wait - Meaning, after his eating, so that the acts of eating should not combine with each other.
(20) The time of eating four eggs - If it is hard for him to wait this amount, he should at least wait the time of eating three eggs, for according to several authorities this measure is also an interruption and they do not combine [Magen Avraham].
(21) They should check in the sick person himself - Meaning that regarding drinking a cheekful, one must measure in him and not according to the average person, as above in siman 612. The later authorities wrote that the sick person should check this before Yom Kippur, meaning that he should put liquid into his mouth and spit it into a vessel. Likewise he should measure before Yom Kippur and look at a clock to see how many minutes equal the time of eating four eggs; this is the time he should wait on Yom Kippur between one eating and another, and likewise between one drinking and another, as below. See Responsa Chasam Sofer, part 6, siman 16, who wrote that the interruption between one eating and another should be nine minutes, which is the measure of kedei achilas pras. Know that between eating and drinking one need not wait at all, as above in siman 612, because eating and drinking do not combine.
