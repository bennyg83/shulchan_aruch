When we feed pregnant women - This means where she smelled food and her face changed, as above in siman 617, se'if 2. The same applies to any person who smelled food and whose face changed, and likewise to a woman within three days after childbirth when she said that she does not need to eat. If she did not say, 'I do not need,' we feed her in the normal way and there is no need to divide into less than the measure. So wrote the glosses of the Gaon Rabbi B. Frankel. The GraZ is stringent in this.
