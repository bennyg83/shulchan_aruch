7) If the doctors say that if he does not eat he will certainly die, whereas if he eats he may live, he is fed. One may not derive otherwise from the incident involving Rabbi Akiva, for it is possible that he knew he would certainly die and there is no place for pious conduct in such a case. Radbaz, volume 3, siman 444; the Mechaber, paragraph 1; Ruach Chaim, paragraph 2; Mateh Efraim, paragraph 7.
