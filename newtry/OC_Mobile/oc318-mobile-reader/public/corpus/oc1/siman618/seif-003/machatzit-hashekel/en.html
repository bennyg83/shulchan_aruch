(Note 3) Requires study even if doctors say food will harm him, etc. See in his name siman 330. In 330 I found nothing; in 328 Magen Avraham note 16 in Radbaz: ill says need certain medicine, doctor says not needed — heed ill; if doctor says that medicine harms — heed doctor. Distinguish food vs medicine: food — heed ill, heart knows its bitterness; medicine if doctor says harms — heed doctor, doctor knows better than ill.
