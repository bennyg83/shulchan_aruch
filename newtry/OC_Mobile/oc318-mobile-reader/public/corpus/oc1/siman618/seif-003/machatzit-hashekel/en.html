(Se'if katan 3) The text should read: even if the physicians say that the food will harm him, etc. See what he wrote in his name in siman 330. I did not find the matter in siman 330. However, in siman 328, Magen Avraham, se'if katan 6, he wrote in the name of Radbaz: if the sick person says, 'I need this particular remedy,' and the physician says, 'he does not need it,' we listen to the sick person; but if the physician says that this remedy will harm him, we listen to the physician. End quote. One must distinguish between food and medicine. Regarding food, we listen to the sick person, for the heart knows the bitterness of its soul. But regarding medicine, if the physician says it will harm him, we listen to the physician, because there is reason to say that regarding medicine the physician knows more than the sick person; since he says it will harm him, we listen to the physician.
