(9) And if the sick person and one physician with him say that he does not need, etc. - This teaches that we combine the words of the sick person with the physician, and we do not say that he is frightened and does not sense his illness, as above in se'if katan 3, since one physician also says like him.
(10) And one physician says he needs, etc. - If that physician is more outstanding in wisdom than the others, we are concerned for his words and feed the sick person even though they are many against him. But if the outstanding one says he does not need, and two who are not outstanding say he does need, we follow the majority number and feed him.
