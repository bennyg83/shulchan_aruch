Uncertain. Meaning, he recognizes the illness but is uncertain whether the person will become endangered. Taz.
I do not know. The sick person's 'I do not know' is nothing, because most sick people do not know and are not sufficiently expert in their illness. So wrote the Beis Yosef in the name of Ramban. It appears from this that if the sick person is a physician and understands, then his 'I do not know' is automatically like another physician's statement. Taz.
