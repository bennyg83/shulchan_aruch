(Se'if katan 2) Who says, etc. That we are concerned lest he become endangered, etc. One may say that this is also the view of the Shulchan Aruch. What it wrote, 'and he will be endangered,' is not the physician's wording; rather, when the physician says that the illness may become more severe, we are concerned that it may become dangerous. The Bach wrote similarly about the Tur, who used the same language as the Shulchan Aruch; see there.
