(In Shulchan Aruch, se'if 9) And if there is no permitted food. The Noda BiYehudah, siman 36, wrote regarding what the Magen Avraham asked in siman 618, se'if katan 9, against the Rambam, and he wrote to resolve it, that the Rambam is speaking on a weekday. This is not so; rather, if it is possible to fix it, one must tithe even on Shabbos, even with vegetables; see there.
