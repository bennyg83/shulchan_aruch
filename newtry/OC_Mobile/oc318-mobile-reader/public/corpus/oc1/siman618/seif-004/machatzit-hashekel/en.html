(Se'if katan 4) So it seems to me, etc. We follow the expert, meaning even to be stringent: where one says he needs and one says he does not need, and the one who says he does not need is more expert, we follow him and do not feed the sick person, since they are equal in number. The same applies with two against two: we follow the expert even to be stringent. But ordinary expertise is not enough, since the others are also physicians. Even though those who say he does not need are more expert, we do not follow them to be stringent unless one is specifically outstanding in wisdom. From the words of the Bach it somewhat implies that when they are equal in number, he need not be outstanding; if he is more expert than the other, we follow him to be stringent; see there.
