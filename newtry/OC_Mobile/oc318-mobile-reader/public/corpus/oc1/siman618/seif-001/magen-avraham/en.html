Even if he is a non-Jew. The Rosh wrote in the name of Riva that a non-Jew or a woman is believed to contradict a Jew.
Who says. Even if he does not say that he will be endangered, but only says that it is possible that the illness will become heavier upon him, we give him food, because we are concerned that perhaps he will become endangered [Bach, Maharshal, Levush, and Maharil].
I need. Even if the physicians say that the food will harm him, we listen to the sick person [Radbaz, part 1, chapter 6; Kenesses HaGedolah]. See what I wrote in his name in siman 330.
