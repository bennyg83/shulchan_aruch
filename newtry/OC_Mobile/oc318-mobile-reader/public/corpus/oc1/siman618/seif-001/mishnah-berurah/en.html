(1) An expert physician - Meaning, one who is expert in that place. See above, siman 328, se'if 10, where the Mechaber brought the view of one who says that we do not require an expert, but every person has a presumption of some expertise regarding a doubt of danger to life, meaning at least when he says that he understands this illness. The Rema concludes there that some say this applies specifically to a Jewish physician, but a non-Jew is not believed unless he is expert. The same applies here. Know that regarding a woman after childbirth, according to everyone we believe women, for they are expert in these matters [Darkei Moshe].
(2) And he will be endangered - The same applies even if he does not say explicitly that he will be endangered, but only says that it is possible that the illness will become more severe for him; we give him food, because we are concerned that perhaps he will become endangered.
(3) We listen to the physician - Because perhaps he is frightened and, because of great weakness, does not sense his illness. This applies even if the sick person himself is an expert physician [Pri Megadim].
(4) I need - Even if the physicians say that the food will harm him, we listen to the sick person.
(5) We listen to the sick person - This is when the sick person says that he feels in himself that he needs to eat, for if he does not eat perhaps the illness will become more severe. But first they must remind him that today is Yom Kippur, for perhaps he forgot. After they inform him that today is Yom Kippur and he asks to eat, one need not examine him further, for the heart knows the bitterness of its soul, and we do not presume people to be wicked. The authorities wrote that if a sick person wants to be stringent after he needs to eat, about him it is said, 'But your blood of your souls I will demand.' They further wrote that in a place where we feed him, he does not need atonement for this, for the Torah exempted one under compulsion, and all the more so where it was only about half a measure; atonement was said only regarding one who sins inadvertently.
