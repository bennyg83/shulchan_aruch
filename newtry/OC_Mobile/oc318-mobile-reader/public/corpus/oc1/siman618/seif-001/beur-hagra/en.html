Se'if 1, Even if he is a non-Jew, etc. There, 54b: 'but they join,' etc., as the Rosh wrote in se'if 14 in the name of Riva. Riva would read, etc., 'we do not say,' etc., that they should not be believed, etc., unlike Rashi there, s.v. 'on the basis of women,' etc. See the Ran there.
Who says, etc. Meaning, even according to the version 'if I do not eat, I will die.' See Yoreh De'ah, siman 150, se'if 3 in the gloss, and all the more so according to the version in our books.
And if the sick person, etc. See Tosafos there in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel, and we rule like him.
