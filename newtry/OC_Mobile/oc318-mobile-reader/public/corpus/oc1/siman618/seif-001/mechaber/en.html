The law of a sick person on Yom Kippur. It contains ten se'ifim.
A sick person who needs to eat: if there is a skilled physician present — even a non-Jew — who says that if they do not feed him, it is possible that the illness will worsen upon him and he will be endangered, we feed him according to his word; and it need not be said [that we do so where the concern is] lest he die. Even if the sick person says he does not need [to eat], we heed the physician; and if the sick person says, "I need [to eat]," even if a hundred physicians say he does not need, we heed the sick person.
