Non-Jew. The Rosh wrote that a non-Jew or a woman is believed to contradict a Jew.
And he will be endangered. The same applies even if he does not say that he will be endangered, but only says that it is possible that the illness will become heavier upon him; we give him food, because we are concerned that perhaps he will become endangered. Later authorities.
To the sick person. Even if the physicians say that the food will harm him, we listen to the sick person. Radbaz, part 1, siman 86; Kenesses HaGedolah. First we remind him that today is Yom Kippur, for perhaps he forgot. Levush. The Issur VeHeter wrote: If the sick person wants to be stringent after he needs this, about him it is said, 'But your blood of your souls I will demand.' Sheyarei Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that we feed him against his will.
