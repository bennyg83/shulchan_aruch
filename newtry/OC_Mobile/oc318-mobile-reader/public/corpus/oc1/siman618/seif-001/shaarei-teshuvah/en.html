(Se'if 7) When we feed, etc. About two thirds of an egg, etc. The Noda BiYehudah, Mahadura Kama, Yoreh De'ah, simanim 37 and 38, wrote that one sage wrote a pilpul regarding the measures and disagreed in some matters with the great early authorities. The Gaon, of blessed memory, explained to him gently that the words of the early authorities are living and enduring, and wrote him open rebuke that he should not continue to overturn the words of the earlier authorities and disagree with them; would that we merit to understand their words, and where they disagree we are granted permission to decide. See there. See Beis Ephraim on Treifos, in the responsa pamphlet there, where I wrote regarding the Torah measure of a revi'is and the measure of the thumb, and I brought there the words of the Tzelach in Pesachim.
