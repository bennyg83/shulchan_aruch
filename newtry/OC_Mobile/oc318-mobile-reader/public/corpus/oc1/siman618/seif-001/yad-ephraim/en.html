In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 3: See what he wrote in his name in siman 328; so the text should read. This requires analysis, for why did he write there in the name of the Radbaz the opposite, that we listen to the physician? Seemingly, regarding an external remedy, one must say that the physician is more expert in this and knows that this remedy will harm him, whereas the patient does not know. But here, where he craves to eat, we say that the heart knows the bitterness of its soul; if he does not eat there will be definite danger to him, and even though the physician says the food will harm him, we listen to the patient, who senses himself better. I do not now have the responsum of the Radbaz with me to examine it.
