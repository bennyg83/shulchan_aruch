And if they assessed him, etc. See Responsa Binyan Tziyon, siman 24, that with each eating, even if he was permitted once to eat more than the measure, nevertheless regarding the second eating, if less than the measure would have sufficed for him and he eats the measure, he is liable to kares. Therefore one must assess at every eating whether less than the measure is sufficient for him. This is very difficult to assess, and requires quickness and expertise; see there [Pischei Teshuvah].
