And the sick person says, 'I do not know,' etc. For the sick person's 'I do not know' is nothing, since how would he know? Most sick people do not know and are not expert in their illness. So wrote the Beis Yosef in the name of Ramban. It appears from this that if the sick person himself understands and is a physician, his 'I do not know' is automatically like another physician's statement, as it seems to me.
