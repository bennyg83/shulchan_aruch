(Se'if katan 8) All, etc. And one should say to him, 'It is Yom Kippur,' etc. The Taz wrote: It seems to me that one must say to him, 'If you know that there is danger to you if you do not eat, eat the measure. If not, first eat less than the measure.' For if one says to him only, 'Today is Yom Kippur,' the sick person will err and think that according to the law he is forbidden to eat, and he will bring himself into danger. End quote.
