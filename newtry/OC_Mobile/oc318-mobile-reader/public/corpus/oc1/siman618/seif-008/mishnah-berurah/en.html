(22) The time of eating four eggs - At least they should wait the time of eating three eggs.
(23) The measure of drinking a revi'is - For according to several authorities this measure is an interruption, so that the drinkings do not combine with one another, as above in siman 612, se'if 9; see the Mishnah Berurah there.
(24) Or the sick person says so - The later authorities wrote that the custom is to place food before him and say to him: Today is Yom Kippur; if you are concerned that there will be danger to you if you do not eat a full measure at once, eat at once, and if not, eat little by little, less than the measure. The later authorities wrote that every halachic decisor must have these laws fluent in his mouth before Yom Kippur, because it may be that even a small delay involves danger to life. If it is a question that requires the decisor to examine the law, and there is another expert present who knows how to answer immediately, we do not give honor to the rabbi in this matter. See more of these laws in Hilchos Shabbos, siman 328 and siman 330, for the only difference between Yom Kippur and Shabbos is that deliberate violation of this is punishable by stoning and deliberate violation of that is punishable by kares.
