Residence. Even when Yom Tov falls on Friday, it is permitted to establish his Shabbos residence. See siman 416.
Kiddush. This is to publicize the matter. He must specifically remain there during twilight. Magen Avraham. Mishpetei Tzedek writes that in our times the practice has spread to embark on a ship on Shabbos. There is no basis for permitting this when the ship travels for the sake of a Jew, for according to all opinions that is forbidden. Regarding this prohibition there is no distinction between Yom Tov and Shabbos, even on the second day of Yom Tov, and it is forbidden even in private. Maharam Sasson likewise rules so; see Knesses HaGedolah, Olas Shabbos, and Magen Avraham.
