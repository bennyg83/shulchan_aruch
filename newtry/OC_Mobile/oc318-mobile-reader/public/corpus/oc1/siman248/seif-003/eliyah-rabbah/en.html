[3] [Levush] And for a mitzvah matter, etc. It appears to me that even when the ship is below ten tefachim it is permitted. Although the Rosh forbids because of techumin, nevertheless since we hold that before three days it is permitted for a voluntary matter like Maharik and not like the Rosh, the same applies for a mitzvah matter even on Friday. Furthermore, the Bach wrote that even the Rosh permits in this case, and so too in seif katan 4 there his view implies permission. Know this, for according to Tosafot and Sefer HaTerumah, who explained that the reason is because of swimming, nevertheless they permit for a mitzvah matter, and we do not say regarding this that they upheld their words in the face of a positive commandment. So too it is implied in Rashal's commentary to the Semag. Likewise from what they permit in seif 4 for a mitzvah matter on Friday even though the Jew himself will perform desecration, like the Rivash. All this is unlike Olat Tamid, who ruled plainly at the end of this siman to forbid when it will lead to desecration or going outside the techum, and that this is Rama's view. What he wrote, that Shiyarei Kenesset HaGedolah wrote so, is imprecise, for he said that it is forbidden specifically according to Radbaz; meaning, where one goes out in a caravan to ascend to Eretz Yisrael. According to us, who permit, the same applies here. Below in seif katan 12 this will be explained further, also unlike the Magen Avraham at the end of this siman.
