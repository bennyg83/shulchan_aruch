(2) And he stipulates with him, etc.: Magen Avraham writes that on Shabbos itself it is forbidden to embark even for the sake of a mitzvah, since he cannot stipulate with the non-Jew that he rest and not travel. Some authorities incline to permit embarking even on Shabbos for the sake of a mitzvah. See Shaarei Teshuvah at the end of this siman and Chayei Adam, klal 4.
