It is all considered a matter of mitzvah. This is difficult: how does this differ from the ruling in siman 444 concerning chametz in one's home, where it is considered a matter of mitzvah only when he is going to circumcise his son, and so forth, as the Gemara states explicitly? One might answer that here the prohibition involved in embarking at sea is only rabbinic. Yet there as well, if he nullifies the chametz, only a rabbinic prohibition remains. It must therefore be that the stringency of chametz is different: because a person is accustomed to it, an additional degree of distancing is required. They consequently ruled more stringently that he must return on its account unless he is engaged in a major mitzvah.
