It is permitted. Mahari ben Lev writes that since he knows with certainty that Shabbos will be desecrated, it is forbidden. Radbaz likewise rules so, as cited by Knesses HaGedolah and Magen Avraham, and this is the accepted practice.
And one who ascends to Eretz Yisrael. This refers specifically to one who intends to settle there. Some say that it applies even when he intends to return, since even walking four cubits in Eretz Yisrael is a mitzvah. Knesses HaGedolah.
Danger to life. A concern that his money, animal, or winter clothing may be stolen is considered danger to life. Responsa of Maharam Galanti, siman 110. Magen Avraham.
For commerce. This applies even if he already has food and travels for commerce in order to increase his profit. Traveling to collect money from a non-Jew who is ill is not considered a certain loss so long as the debtor is not moribund. Mishpetei Tzedek and Rabbi Yosef HaLevi. See Magen Avraham, siman 339, seif katan 13. Regarding whether one may travel in a small or large boat on Shabbos in order to complete a minyan and pray with the congregation, see Radbaz, part 1, siman 47; Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 115; Responsa Avodas HaGershuni, siman 123; and Responsa Maharam of Rothenburg, simanim 1 and 2. See also above, siman 90, seif katan 12.
A caravan. It appears to me that one should not be lenient regarding a caravan. Magen Avraham.
