And confusion. For the sake of a mitzvah it is permitted, because one who is occupied with one mitzvah is exempt from another mitzvah.
The ship. Meaning that although the water is ten handbreadths deep, if the ship is submerged in the water and he sits lower than ten handbreadths, he is found to be traveling below ten handbreadths. Even if he sits above that height, it is forbidden because the ship is four handbreadths wide, as stated in siman 404. See there and see the Magen Avraham.
Within the techum. Within the techum it is permitted, and we do not decree lest he go beyond the techum. Magen Avraham.
Which is forbidden. Travel in a wagon is forbidden lest he descend from the wagon and walk in violation of the prohibition, but that concern does not apply here. Taz. The Magen Avraham writes that wagon travel is forbidden lest he cut a branch, whereas here the animals travel far away and the concern that he might cut a branch does not apply.
