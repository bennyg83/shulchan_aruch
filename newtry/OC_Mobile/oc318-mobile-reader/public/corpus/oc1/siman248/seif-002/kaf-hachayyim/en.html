2) There: Even on Friday, etc. On Shabbos itself it is forbidden, because one cannot stipulate with the shipmaster that he rest. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. Machatzis HaShekel explains the reason: he boards on Shabbos intending to set sail immediately. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraphs 4 and 5, however, permits setting sail even on Shabbos for the purpose of a mitzvah; see there. Rav Yaakov Castro in Hagahos HaTur maintains, like Magen Avraham, that on Shabbos itself it is forbidden even for the purpose of a mitzvah. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1, and Chayei Adam, klal 4, paragraph 1, write the same. See below, seif 3 and our words there in paragraph 39, and siman 613, seif 6.
