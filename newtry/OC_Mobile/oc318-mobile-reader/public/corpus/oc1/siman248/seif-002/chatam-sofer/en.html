Regarding the Magen Avraham, seif katan 10, who writes that it is forbidden to enter: Note: In my novellae I concluded that according to several authorities, the decree against swimming applies only to a ferry that crosses the width of a river. One who travels a long distance by ship, however, was not included in the decree on account of swimming; the concern is instead techumin. See there. See also Chasam Sofer, part 6, siman 98.
