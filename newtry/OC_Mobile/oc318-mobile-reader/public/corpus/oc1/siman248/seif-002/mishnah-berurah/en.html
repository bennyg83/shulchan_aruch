(1) Even on Friday: Meaning that although he will certainly lack Shabbos enjoyment as a result, as the Mechaber writes in seif 2, it is nevertheless permitted because the journey is for a mitzvah, and we follow the rule that one occupied with one mitzvah is exempt from another. This is obvious where the ship travels in water deeper than ten handbreadths, for there is no concern of leaving the techum because we rule that the prohibition of techumin does not apply above ten handbreadths. Even where some places have shallow water less than ten handbreadths deep, however, it is permitted to embark on Friday when traveling for a mitzvah. Eliyah Rabbah.
