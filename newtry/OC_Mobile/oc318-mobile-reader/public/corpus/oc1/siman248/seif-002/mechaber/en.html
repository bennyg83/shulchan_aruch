The reason it is forbidden to commence a journey within three days of Shabbat is because of the enjoyment of Shabbat, since for the first three days travelers suffer discomfort and confusion. This only applies to sea voyages; travel in rivers do not cause any pain and it is therefore permitted to commence such a journey even on a Friday. This is only if we are not aware that the depth of the waters is less ten hand breaths; in places where we know that from the bottom of the boat to the bottom of the river the distance is less than ten hand breaths it is forbidden ''to travel outside of the Techum'' because of the prohibition of Techum. {Rama: Rema: Likewise in a ship where a Jew will have to perform Melacha on Shabbat one should not enter three days before Shabbat, even if they are flowing rivers and the ship is above ten hand breaths. (Rivash; Mahari"k) There is no prohibition, however, if animals have to drag the boat from the bank of the river and it is not the same as traveling in a wagon, which is forbidden.'}
