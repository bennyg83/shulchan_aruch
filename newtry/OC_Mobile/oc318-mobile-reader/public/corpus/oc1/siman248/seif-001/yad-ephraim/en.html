Magen Avraham s.k. 2: meaning on the fourth day, etc. — erev Shabbat wrote per Ra'ash's reason of Oneg — permitted on the fourth day before Shabbat distress from fourth until Shabbat; not per Razah's reason lest need melacha on Shabbat — therefore three days before Shabbat is before Shabbat so fourth day itself also forbidden. Erev Shabbat: Mechaber in seif 2 reason Oneg — permitted to sail on fourth; Magen Avraham holds Mechaber forbids day 4 since may come to chillul as seif 4; therefore Magen Avraham s.k. 3: before three days means day 3 as seif 4, not as Erev Shabbat who permits day 4 per Oneg, etc.
