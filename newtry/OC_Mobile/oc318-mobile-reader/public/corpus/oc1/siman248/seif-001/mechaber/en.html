The law of embarking on a ship and traveling with a caravan on Shabbos. It contains 4 seifim: It is permitted to embark on a ship even on Friday if one is traveling for the purpose of a mitzvah, and he stipulates with him that he will rest, and if afterward he does not rest, it is of no consequence. But for an optional journey one may not embark within 3 days before Shabbos. {Rama: But more than 3 days beforehand it is permitted even on a ship that is drawn by animals, even where the water is less than 10 tefachim deep. [Maharik, shoresh 45] And this is so even where the Jew will afterward need to perform a melacha on Shabbos in order to direct the ship. [Rivash, siman 152]} And if it is a short journey, such as from Tyre to Sidon, between which there is only a one-day journey, it is permitted to embark on Friday morning because it is possible to arrive there before Shabbos. And in a place where the custom is not to embark at all on Friday, even for a short journey, one does not embark.
