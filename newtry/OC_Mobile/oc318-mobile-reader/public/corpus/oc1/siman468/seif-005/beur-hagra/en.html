(א) Seif 5 — even, etc. Rambam's language; he holds the Mishnah where they practiced to do deals with it; he explains the Sages are lenient from R' Meir and do not dispute to forbid; so Ra'avad there from what is difficult; and the Sages only do three crafts, etc., as explained in the Gemara there "come hear R' Meir," etc., "and they work," etc. — meaning they finish only where they practiced to do; thus "a place where they practiced not," etc., specifically for Festival need as in the Gemara there; see Levushei Serad; and the three crafts specifically for Festival need as in Moed Katan 13:1; he ruled like the Sages and R' Meir only in the three crafts.

(ב) And there are those lenient — explaining the Mishnah where they practiced not to do; in the Gemara "and they work," etc., means even not for Festival need; thus in the Gemara "a place where they practiced not," etc., "for Festival need," etc. — two proofs that not for Festival need — per Rambam what he wrote "a place where they practiced not" cannot be explained; the question remains.

(ג) Three, etc. — specifically for Festival need; he need not have written "for Festival need" as Moed Katan 13:1; so Ra'avad in objections and Tosefta: "a place where they practiced melacha on erev Pesach," etc., "even where they said not to do, the three crafts they do — tailors," etc.

(ד) And if he began, etc. — meaning where they practiced not to do; he relied on the beginning and also "until midday" was only to exclude what Rosh wrote in the name of R' Yehuda that all day is permitted.
