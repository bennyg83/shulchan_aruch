That is — Taz: Mechaber ruled like Rambam on erev Pesach; Be'er HaGolah: to prepare a place, etc. — permitted all day even after midday; but to set on eggs forbidden after midday; where they practiced not to do — forbidden even before midday without having begun that day; and similarly if he began — only if for Festival need. But Tur explained like Rashi: setting means to hatch eggs, and similarly a hen that fled, etc.; Levush ruled like Tur and Rashi and Magen Avraham 14; this "to prepare a place" — if mere repair permitted; but to build them a coop, etc., one may say forbidden as complete melacha.
