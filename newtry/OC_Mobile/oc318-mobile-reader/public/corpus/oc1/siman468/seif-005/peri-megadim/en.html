That is. See Taz: the Mechaber ruled like the Rambam of blessed memory; see Beis Yosef and Be'er HaGolah. To prepare a place, etc., is permitted the entire day, even after midday; but to set them on eggs is forbidden after midday; and in a place where they practiced not to do, it is forbidden even before midday if he did not begin that day; and the same law applies if he began, whenever it is not for the need of the Festival. But the Tur explained like Rashi, that "they set" means to raise eggs, and all the more so a hen that fled, etc.; and the Levush ruled like the Tur and like Rashi's explanation; and see Magen Avraham (14). And this "to prepare a place": it is not clear if mere preparation [is meant]; but to build them a coop and the like, one may say it is forbidden, for it is complete melacha.
