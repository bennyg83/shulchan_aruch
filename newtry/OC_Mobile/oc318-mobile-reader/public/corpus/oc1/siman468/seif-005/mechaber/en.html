Even in a place where they are accustomed to do [melacha], one should not begin a melacha at the beginning of the fourteenth [of Nissan], even if he could finish it before midday, except for three crafts only that they begin in a place where they are accustomed to do and they work until midday — and these are: tailors, scribes, and launderers. But other crafts — if he began them before the fourteenth, he finishes until midday.And there are those who are lenient to say that the three crafts mentioned begin and work until midday even in a place where they are accustomed not to do; and if he began during the day before [the fourteenth] in other crafts for the need of the Festival, they work until midday, and this is the custom (Tur in the name of the Rosh}.):
