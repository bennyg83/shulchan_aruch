(א) He should not begin, etc. — meaning even if for Festival need; and what was mentioned above that where they practiced to do they do — means finishing melachos begun before, or the three crafts as he concludes.
(ב) And these are the tailors, etc. — if for Yom Tov need; they were lenient only in these because the public needs them greatly.
(ג) Before, etc. — and he does them for Festival need.
(ד) Even in a place, etc. — because they are great Festival need.
(ה) And if he began, etc. — in other crafts it is forbidden to begin in every respect since it is a place that practiced not to do; but if he began before the fourteenth he may finish if for Festival need; all this where they practiced not to do; but where they practiced to do, permitted whether to begin all melachos or finish for Festival need or not.
(ו) During the day — meaning before sunrise of the fourteenth.
(ז) Other crafts — and shoemakers are included in other crafts per most Poskim; similarly making new shoes; but repairing torn ones for Yom Tov — all agree permitted to begin and repair until midday [Acharonim].
(ח) They work until midday — and once midday arrives he must stop in the middle of melacha.
