And the books. Even through a non-Jew it is forbidden to shave after midday (Mateh Moshe); and it appears to me that the reason is because he tilts his head and assists somewhat (see there). And the Bach wrote in the name of the Maharshal to permit through a non-Jew; and through a Jew, even for free, it is forbidden. And in the Levush, Yoreh De'ah siman 399, it implies that they practiced a prohibition even through a non-Jew. And it is obvious that a Jew is forbidden to cut hair for a non-Jew. And Maharil and Mahariv wrote that it is forbidden to cut the nails; see siman 532.
