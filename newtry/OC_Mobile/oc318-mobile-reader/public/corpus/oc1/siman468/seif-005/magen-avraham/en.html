And the scribes — even through an akum it is forbidden to shave after midday (Magen Tzedek); and it appears to me the reason is because he tilts his head and assists somewhat (see there); and Bach wrote in the name of Rashi to permit through akum; and through a Jew even for free is forbidden; and in the Levush Yoreh De'ah siman 399 it implies they practiced prohibition even through akum; and it is obvious a Jew is forbidden to cut hair for an akum; and Maharil and Mahariv wrote it is forbidden to cut nails — see siman 472.
