4) There. From midday onward, etc. See Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, siman 696, Hagahos Beis Yosef, subsection 5. He writes that although the custom on the eve of Pesach after midday is not to engage in commerce, that case differs because work after midday is prohibited due to offering the korban, unlike Purim. Nevertheless, we have neither seen nor heard of anyone penalizing a person who occasionally conducted business on the eve of Pesach after midday, especially in private. See there. Beis David, siman 243, however, writes that commerce is permitted on the eve of Pesach. See there. Zechor LeAvraham cites this in the present siman and writes that Beis David apparently did not see the cited words of Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, since he does not mention them. Zechor LeAvraham further writes that he instituted the closing of shops at midday in his city and heard that this is likewise the custom in Sofia. See below, subsection 30, and above, siman 251, subsection 2.
