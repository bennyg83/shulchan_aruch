(א) Because of the dispute — for melacha cannot be done discreetly like other things that are permitted when his intention is to return (Tosafot and Ran).

(ב) He does so in the wilderness — when his intention is to return (Magen Mishneh); and if he is still outside the city's boundary, even if he does not intend to return it is permitted (Ran).

(ג) That he left from there — when his intention is to return; but if he does not intend to return he practices even the leniencies of the place he went to, as in the first chapter of Chullin; and similarly the Ran; and so in siman 214 in Yoreh De'ah; and the words of the Shach there require further study.

(ד) And the stringencies of the place he went to — even though his intention is to return, because of the dispute.

(ה) And nevertheless he should not appear, etc. — meaning: when he practices the stringencies of the place he left from there, he should not appear, etc.

(ו) And provided he does not appear, etc. — it implies that this is a general rule for everything edible; and other things are permitted discreetly except melacha, which cannot be done discreetly — therefore it is permitted only in the wilderness, as stated; and so in Magen Mishneh; and it is implied in the Ran that if they came and found him eating he must cover the food before them, but before Torah scholars who know this depends on custom he need not cover it; see end of siman 496 and Yoreh De'ah siman 83 and responsum of Rashba siman 337 and responsum of R' Levi Chaviv. Maharil wrote: the youths who eat in Mainz from lung examinations where they are lenient there — there is no concern, for the Mordechai wrote we are not expert in the stringencies of the place one left from and the place one went to. And again he said it is in Tosafot first chapter of Pesachim daf 14: in Jerusalem it is reasonable to practice prohibition of melacha on erev Pesach since people gather there even from places that practiced prohibition there; and hence the practice that the patzikan of the sons of Rhenish did not [eat] in his house because youths gather there from other places; and requires further study what he wrote about examinations above — perhaps because milk prohibition is d'oraisa with karet they were stringent, end of his words. And it appears to me: regarding examination it is called discreet, for the youths know what happened in the animal's examination — only the examiner knows; but the patzikan are evident and it appears they practice prohibition in other places — therefore we are not permitted to permit them before them, similar to Jerusalem where they practiced melacha prohibition which is a public matter. And what he wrote to permit youths to eat from examinations, etc. — implies he holds youths are considered as intending to return even though their intention is to study there two or three years; and nevertheless requires further study why he wrote we are not expert, etc.; and further that if we are not expert we should be stringent; and it is possible he means one should not protest against them because we are not expert and in doubt one does not protest — great doubt here, for there are many differences between country and country regarding examinations; and therefore he should have practiced stringencies of the place he left — as in Yoreh De'ah siman 64 seif 9 and siman 214. And in Mordechai beginning of Yevamos it is written: sons of Mainz when they come to Germiza, sons of Germiza must inform them in several matters they practice leniencies, and then when they inform them if their inclination follows them it is permitted to practice those leniencies even though forbidden in their place, as they say in chapter Kirah: R' Abba in the place of R' Yehuda bar Levi would move a lamp; and Maharam wrote; and in Mordechai later he concludes: and requires further study in chapter "the place where they practiced" that they impose stringencies of the place he left; and further that R' Abba holds to permit like R' Shimon, and in places of R' Yehuda he was stringent, end of his words. And it appears to me for the Mordechai too this is the intent: if it appears to him according to law that it is permitted, it is permitted to eat — but the early ones' difficulty in their places stands; and perhaps for this Maharil wrote we are not expert.
