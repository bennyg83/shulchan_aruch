One who travels from a place where they do [melacha] to a place where they do not — he should not do [melacha] in the settlement because of the dispute. But he may do so in the wilderness. And one who travels from a place where they do not to' a place where they do — he should not do [melacha], and we impose upon him the stringencies of the place he left and the stringencies of the place he went to. And nevertheless he should not appear before them as though he is idle due to the prohibition — for a person should never change [his practice] because of dispute. And similarly one whose intention is to return to his place conducts himself like the people of his place, whether to be lenient or to be stringent, provided he does not appear before the people of the place where he is because of the dispute.':
