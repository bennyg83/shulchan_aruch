The tailors. See Taz, for we find regarding them a leniency on Chol HaMoed; and if he has nothing to eat it is permitted even after midday; see siman 542, se'if 2. And Chol HaMoed is more stringent than erev Pesach after midday; see Magen Avraham (3), and in the Rambam, Hilchos Yom Tov, chapter 8, law 18. And in Eliyah Rabbah (5), "the books" means to shave; see Magen Avraham (13); and in Eliyah Rabbah (4): those who are permitted to shave on Chol HaMoed, the same law applies on erev Pesach after midday; see siman 531. And to give garments to a non-Jewish laundress after midday: for us, since melacha through a non-Jew is permitted, the same law applies to this; see there.
