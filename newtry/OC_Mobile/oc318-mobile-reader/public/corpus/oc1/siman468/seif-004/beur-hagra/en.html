(ב) He should not do — there 52a, and he holds one who does not intend to return — unlike Tosafot there.

(ג) And one who travels, etc., and we impose, etc. — Mishnah there.

(ד) And nevertheless, etc., forever — there 51b; Rava explained: unlike appearing before them; the Mishnah means: forever one should not change, etc., meaning he should not appear before them — thus "and nevertheless," etc., forever, etc.

(ה) And similarly one — there 51a.

(ו) And provided — there: since they saw him fasting; and though Abaye objected, this is because it is discreet and they already found him eating — no dispute among scholars, for scholars know he acted lawfully — see there in Ran. "from a place," etc., and the case in settlement is forbidden, etc., with intention to return — if without intention to return, once he reached settlement even in wilderness forbidden, for everything follows the place he intends to settle; even if he left from there as explained in Sefer 496:3 sons of Israel, etc., per Tosafot as above; all this not from the Chullin case; but if: as above everything follows where he intends to settle; and all, etc., from the Chullin case as above.
