(d) There — from midday onwards, etc. — see Knesset HaGedolah siman 496 in Hagahot Beit Yosef letter 5: though the custom on erev Pesach after midday is not to engage in commerce, there it is different because after midday melacha is forbidden because of the sacrifice offering, unlike Purim; yet we have not seen or heard anyone who fined one who did business on erev Pesach after midday incidentally, and all the more discreetly — see there; however Rav Beit David siman 243 wrote business on erev Pesach is permitted — see there; Zechor LeAvraham brought this and wrote it appears he did not see Knesset HaGedolah's words since he did not mention them; and wrote perhaps he practiced in his city to close shops at midday, and so he heard the custom in Sofia — Hashem save us; see below letter 30 and above siman 251 letter 2.
