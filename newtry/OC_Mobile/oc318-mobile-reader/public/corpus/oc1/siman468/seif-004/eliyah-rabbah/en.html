[4] Through a Samaritan, etc. And so they practiced to get a haircut through a Samaritan after midday, for through a Jew it is forbidden; and it is obvious a Jew is forbidden to cut hair for Samaritans. Maharil wrote in the gloss laws of Yom Tov not to cut nails from midday onwards, and its law is like all Chol HaMoed law, end of his words; and in Eliyah Zuta I wrote to permit if he forgot to cut before midday — see there. I further wrote that all those permitted to shave on Chol HaMoed as written in siman 471 — the same law permits on erev Pesach after midday; see R' Yosef Minz siman 4. Lechem Rav wrote in the name of his brother-in-law the Gaon Maharash that women should not give garments to a laundress after midday, end of his words; at first glance it appears permitted, for we hold like Rama that they practice melacha through akum after midday — why is giving to a laundress worse on erev Pesach after midday? Difficult to distinguish.
