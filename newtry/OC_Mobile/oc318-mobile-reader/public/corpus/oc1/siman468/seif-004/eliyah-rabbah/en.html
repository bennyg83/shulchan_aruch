[4] Through a Kuthi, etc. And so they practiced to cut hair through a Kuthi after midday, for through a Jew it is forbidden; and it is obvious that it is forbidden for a Jew to cut hair for Kuthim. Maharil wrote in a gloss, Hilchos Yom Tov, not to cut nails from midday onward, and its law is like every law of Chol HaMoed; end quote. And in Eliyahu Zuta I wrote to permit if he forgot to cut before midday; see there. I further wrote that all those who are permitted to shave on Chol HaMoed, as written in siman 531, the same law applies that they are permitted on erev Pesach after midday; and see R' Yehuda Mintz, siman 4. Lechem Rav wrote in the name of his brother-in-law the Gaon Maharam Shmuel that women should not give garments to a laundress after midday; end quote. And ostensibly it appears to permit, for we hold like the Rama that they practice [to permit] melacha through a Kuthi after midday; and why should giving garments to a laundress on erev Pesach after midday be worse? And it is forced to distinguish.
