And for us: see Taz; and one who wishes not to do melacha even in a place where they practiced to do may do so; an onlooker says he has no need of melacha, and there is no arrogance and no [issue] because of controversy. And in a place where they practiced not to do, they are permitted at night until sunrise; see Magen Avraham (5), and with Hashem's help it will be explained there further.
