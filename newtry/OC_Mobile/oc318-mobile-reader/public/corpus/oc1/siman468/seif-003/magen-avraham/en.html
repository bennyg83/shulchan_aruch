A place where they practiced, etc. The Ra'em in Semag has a difficulty, from whichever way you turn: if they err regarding it, thinking it is forbidden, we hold that it is permitted to be lenient for them; and if they know it is permitted and practice a prohibition, even on other days of the year it is also forbidden, for it is like a vow, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 214. And one must say that it deals with those who know it is permitted, and the practical difference is for those who come there from other places: they must practice the stringencies of the place they went to; end of his words. And it appears to me that a custom they practiced because of a fence, such as here: their children are not permitted to change their custom; but if they practiced on other days of the year not to do melacha, their children are permitted to do [change] their custom.
