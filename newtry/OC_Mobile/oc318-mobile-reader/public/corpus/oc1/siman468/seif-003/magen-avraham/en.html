(א) A place where they practiced, etc. Ra'avan in Semag 100 — if they err and think it is forbidden, we rule that it is permitted to be lenient for them; and if they know it is permitted and practice prohibition, even on other days of the year it is forbidden, for it is like a vow, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 214. And it must be that we deal with those who know it is permitted; and the practical difference is for those who come there from other places — they must practice the stringencies of the place they went to, end of his words. And it appears to me that a custom they practiced as a fence, such as here — their children are not permitted to change their custom; but if they practiced on other days of the year not to do melacha, their children are permitted to do their custom. And see chapter 4 of Pesachim and Tosafot daf 51a.

(ב) They do not do — and at night it is permitted until sunrise (Gemara; see Tosafot daf 2).

(ג) And for us, etc. The Levush wrote it is possible that in his place they practiced thus, but in other places he passed through he saw they do complete melacha.
