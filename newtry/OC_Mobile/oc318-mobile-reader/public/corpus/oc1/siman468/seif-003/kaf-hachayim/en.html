(c) And when erev Pesach falls on Shabbat — Maharil wrote in derashot Shabbat Hagadol: one who wants to be stringent on himself not to do melacha after midday on that Shabbat eve may be stringent because of not distinguishing — see there; Beit David Kitzur Hagahot in his book Pesach Ma'uvin letter 93 wrote this is only a stringency and concluded he did not see people careful even as stringency — see there; and similarly Zera Emet was asked and concluded since most Poskim agreed on the sacrifice reason it appears plainly permitted — see there; and Rav Nachfeh in Kesef Cha'ach siman 7 concluded by law it is fully permitted and one who is stringent transgresses — see there; however Rav Adam Kadish part 1 Orach Chayyim siman 10 inclined to forbid — see there; however Birkei Yosef letter 1 on Rav Adam Kadish's proofs wrote his proofs are not decisive and therefore wrote the main law is like Nachfeh and Knesset HaGedolah — see there; see Ateret Zekenim letter 2 wrote where they practiced to do on erev Pesach until midday it appears permitted on erev Shabbat after midday even per Rashi since he does not hold the preoccupation reason — see there. Thus most Poskim agree the prohibition reason is the sacrifice; and even where they practiced not to do all day, when erev Pesach falls on Shabbat melacha is permitted that erev Shabbat; and where they practiced until midday — also per Rashi permitted; nevertheless one who is stringent where they practiced all day may be concerned for Rashi's view.
