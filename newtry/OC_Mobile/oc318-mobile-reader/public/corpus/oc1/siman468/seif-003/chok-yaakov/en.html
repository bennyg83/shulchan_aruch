Where the custom is not to perform labor, they do not perform it. Since they practice this as a fence and safeguard, as written above in s.k. 1, it takes effect upon them, upon their descendants, and upon those who come to live there, as is explicitly explained in Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 214. What the Magen Avraham wrote about this consists of simple matters that are explicitly explained there.
They do not perform labor. But at night it is permitted, as stated in the Gemara.
And for us, it is a place where the custom is not to perform labor. The Levush wrote that he did not see people following the practice to prohibit it, end quote.
