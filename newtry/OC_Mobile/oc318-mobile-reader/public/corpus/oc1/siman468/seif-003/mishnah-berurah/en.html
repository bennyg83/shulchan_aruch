(10) A place where they practiced, etc., they do: and even for wages; and in se'if 5 this se'if will be explained; see there in the Mechaber and Rama.
(11) Not to do: that is, from sunrise and onward [and the entire night is permitted]; and even their children after them until the end of all generations are not permitted to do, because of "do not forsake the teaching of your mother"; and even hatarah is of no avail in this [later authorities].
(12) And for us, etc.: see in the Levush and in the other later authorities that this is not unanimously agreed, for there are many places that practice to do until midday; and therefore each one depends on the custom of his place.
(13) And specifically complete melachos, etc.: as above in se'if 2 in the gloss regarding after midday.
