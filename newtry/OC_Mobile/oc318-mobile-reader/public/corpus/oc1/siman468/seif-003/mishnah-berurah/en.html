(א) A place where they practiced, etc., they do — even for wages; and in seif 5 it will be explained in Mechaber and Rama — see there.

(ב) Not to do — meaning from sunrise and onwards [and all night permitted]; and even their children afterward for all generations may not do because of do not forsake your mother's teaching, and even if he releases it is ineffective [Acharonim].

(ג) And for us, etc. — see Levush and other Acharonim this is not agreed, for many places practice until midday; each depends on local custom.

(ד) And specifically complete melachos, etc. — as below seif 2 in the gloss after midday.
