(א) (Seif katan 3) Complete melacha — and all permitted on Chol HaMoed, etc., as in the Gemara and Poskim; Rambam chapter 1 of laws of Yom Tov law 18: therefore the fourteenth of Nissan melacha is forbidden by rabbinic law like Chol HaMoed and it is lighter than Chol HaMoed, forbidden only from half the day, etc., but before noon, etc., end of his words — he wrote it is lighter than Chol HaMoed and so clear in the Gemara it is lighter than Chol HaMoed law written in Shulchan Aruch seif 7.

(ב) Hen, etc., and if it died they set another under it, etc.; on Chol HaMoed similarly forbidden — as below siman 476 seif 4 and these are Gemara words daf 55b and more that Chol HaMoed is stricter than erev Pesach; Chayei Adam wrote in Shulchan Aruch that if he has nothing to eat he may do even after midday — there is no greater Festival need; complete melacha implies even for Festival need forbidden per Shulchan Aruch words.

(ג) However otherwise if he has nothing to eat it is permitted as on Chol HaMoed — see siman 472; see Magen Avraham if he has bread and water it is forbidden to do — see there further.
