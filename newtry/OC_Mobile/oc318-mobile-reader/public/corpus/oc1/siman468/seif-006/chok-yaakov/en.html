That is, to repair. This implies that to seat a hen initially on the eggs is forbidden. So wrote the Taz and the Magen Avraham, unless it is in the manner mentioned in the following seif. So too wrote the Maggid Mishneh at the end of Hilchos Yom Tov according to one explanation of the Rambam's view. However, at the end he concludes that the Rambam too agrees with Rashi's view, that it is permitted initially to seat it on the eggs on the fourteenth; see there. See Tosafos Yom Tov at the end of chapter Makom Shenahagu. So too wrote the Levush, that it is permitted initially to seat it on the eggs. See the Tur and Beis Yosef, that all of these cases that are permitted are permitted the entire day, even after midday, and even in a place where the custom is not to perform labor the entire day, it is permitted to begin these labors on the fourteenth.
