See Taz; and the difficulty of Beis Yosef is that he should have mentioned on the fourteenth after midday it is forbidden unless it accumulated, etc.; and the Taz resolved this: that it is not his manner to bring anything but what they said in the Gemara, and Rava establishes it upon Chol HaMoed; see there. And what he wrote here in se'if 8, "they sweep," means he should take it outside; and so it is in the Mishnah, Pesachim 55b; one must say that it deals with when it stands in the courtyard, which is not so when it stands in the barn, where it is forbidden unless it accumulated; and see Bach.
