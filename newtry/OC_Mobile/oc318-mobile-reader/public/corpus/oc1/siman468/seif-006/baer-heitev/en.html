To prepare. It implies that to set initially on eggs is forbidden, as Taz and Magen Avraham wrote, unless in the case mentioned in the next seif; however according to Rashi it is permitted initially to set on eggs on the fourteenth — see Taz end of chapter "the place where they practiced," and Levush wrote it is permitted to set on eggs initially. See Tur and Beit Yosef that all these permitted matters are permitted all day even after midday, and similarly even where they practiced not to do — all day one may begin these melachos on the fourteenth — see siman 476.
