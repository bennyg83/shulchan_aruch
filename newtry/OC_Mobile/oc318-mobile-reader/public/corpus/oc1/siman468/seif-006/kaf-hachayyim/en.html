Rambam, Hilchos Yom Tov, chapter 8, halachah 17. Maggid Mishneh explains there that a ban is more severe than lashes for rebellious conduct, as explained in chapter 1. See there. Tosafos, Kesubos 28a, beginning with the words 'Rav Huna,' write likewise, and this also appears in Rashi, Kiddushin 70b. Chok Yaakov, subsection 2, cites these sources. See there. A Torah scholar is not placed under a ban, but is given lashes privately. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1, based upon Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De'ah, siman 334, se'if 42. See there. See below, siman 496, se'if 1, and Responsa Sha'ar Ephraim, siman 64, which discusses at length that a Torah scholar is not placed under a ban. See there. See also below, siman 476, subsection 11.
