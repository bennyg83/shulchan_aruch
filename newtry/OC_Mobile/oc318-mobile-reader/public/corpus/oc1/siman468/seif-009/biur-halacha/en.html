Dung in the courtyard one should not take out, etc. — see Biur Gra who wrote per Rambam this law applies only on Chol HaMoed, not on the fourteenth [even after midday] one takes to the garbage dump in all cases; per his words also for R' Chananel whose Gemara text reads thus. Know from Yerushalmi it is clear from a pen one certainly may take to the garbage dump; his words are brought in R' Chananel's explanation; whether Yerushalmi deals only on the fourteenth or even Chol HaMoed, on the fourteenth it is certainly permitted; it appears Bach was unaware of this Yerushalmi.
