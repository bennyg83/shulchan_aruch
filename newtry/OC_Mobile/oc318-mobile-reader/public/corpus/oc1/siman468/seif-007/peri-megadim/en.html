One does not say. Mizmor leTodah on erev Pesach and Chol HaMoed of Pesach, etc., in siman 429; and in Peri Chadash there: there are places that practice to say it, and it is proper not to say it. And according to this, if he began Mizmor leTodah he does not stop; and it is not comparable to a mandatory prayer, where if he began in the manner of obligation he stops, for there is a blessing in vain; which is not so here, where he finishes like one saying Tehillim, and they say [it]; we, in any event, do not interrupt if he began. And one may ask: why do I need the reason of the chametz in it, that it reduces the time of eating? For the matzah in it too: all the more so they do not eat [it] the entire day at all, as in siman 471 in Magen Avraham (5): matzah of a todah is not rich matzah; and see Pesachim 13b, which says because of chametz that it reduces, etc.; and it is forced to say that it said "perhaps"; and see Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilchos Chametz uMatzah, law 12. Nevertheless there are those who say that its prohibition of matzah after midday is somewhat settled; and according to the Rambam of blessed memory, the entire day is difficult. And one may say: matzah of a todah is a mitzvah; this is not like one who comes upon a betrothed woman in his father-in-law's house, which is optional before a blessing, etc.; and a todah is a positive commandment, which is not so regarding chametz that reduces the time. See Levush: one does not say El Erech Apayim, and Lamnatzeach one does not say, for erev Pesach after midday is Yom Tov of the korban Pesach, which is not so regarding other erev Yom Tov days. And therefore Yom Tov of atonement of sins: all who eat, etc.; and in my customs I wrote about this. See Yoreh De'ah 399, se'if 3; but on erev Pesach see there. And they do not make the mourner's meal of consolation; but on erev Pesach, Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Maharshal, letter 20. And see siman 548, and it implies that the entire day, even before midday, they do not make the mourner's meal of consolation on erev Pesach; and this requires further study. And see Yoreh De'ah 378: on Shabbos near the house of mourning they do not make the mourner's meal of consolation; and in the Shach letter 11, the law of erev Pesach and Shabbos; see siman 444; and in Magen Avraham, with Hashem's help, it will be explained further.
