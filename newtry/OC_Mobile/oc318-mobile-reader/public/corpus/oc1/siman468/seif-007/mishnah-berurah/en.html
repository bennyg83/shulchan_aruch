(א) Three days, etc. — for in three days they are invalidated for eating entirely and there is great loss if another is not set under them; but before that while still somewhat fit for one of good disposition and not fastidious we are not lenient to set a hen on them.
(ב) And it died — and similarly if it fled they return it to its place.
(ג) Lest the eggs be ruined — proves that to set initially a hen on eggs on erev Pesach is forbidden after midday; however several Poskim are lenient and one may be lenient somewhat when needed; per this view if it died they set another even if the first did not sit three days.
