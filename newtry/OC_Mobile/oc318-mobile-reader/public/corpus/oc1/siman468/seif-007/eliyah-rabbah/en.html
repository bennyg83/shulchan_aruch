[7] [Levush] one who writes, etc. but for others even, etc. Rama did not write this; nevertheless it is reasonable as complete melacha forbidden even for mitzvah need; also difficult from siman 251 where he ruled specifically for wages forbidden but for free permitted for others since it is a mitzvah — and Tur's language there and here are identical; what appears to me: also wonder why Rama and Levush did not write above that complete melacha is forbidden even for free — it appears they hold Beit Yosef did not write thus only on erev Pesach where we find Mordechai holds so, but on erev Shabbat where we do not find Mordechai so — no distinction between repair and complete melacha, always permitted for free when for Shabbat or mitzvah need; the reason on erev Pesach where we excommunicate is stricter.
