(Seif katan 7) Because of, etc., permitted when intending to return as seif katan 12; know Magen Avraham follows his view that Shulchan Aruch language which is Rambam's is like Tosafot — without intention to return need not practice stringencies of place he left — so Yoreh De'ah siman 214 and below siman 474; therefore here must deal with intention to return; and what Shulchan Aruch wrote end of seif 7 "and similarly one whose intention is to return," etc., implies at the beginning without intention to return — Shach Yoreh De'ah 214 strained to explain thus Shulchan Aruch's words here deal even without intention to return and therefore wondered Shulchan Aruch contradicts itself — see there; but Magen Avraham holds at the beginning too deals with intention to return; and what is taught at the end "and similarly one whose intention to return," etc., means at the beginning in melacha custom there is no distinction between discreet and public; therefore he wrote "and similarly one whose intention to return," etc., practices, etc., meaning with intention to return also in other customs not dependent on melacha — as Magen Avraham seif katan 12 the law is thus.
