Seif 7 — hen, etc. Rambam's text begins on Chol HaMoed; the question from "if it died," etc., see siman 476 seif 4 — meaning what R' Huna said "within three" refers to the beginning and on Chol HaMoed; after three days of sitting refers to the end; second view — and one who examines Rambam will see his intent is not so, for he did not mention "within three days for rebellion" at all; Maharif omitted R' Huna's words but ruled like Rav Ami and Rambam like R' Huna.
