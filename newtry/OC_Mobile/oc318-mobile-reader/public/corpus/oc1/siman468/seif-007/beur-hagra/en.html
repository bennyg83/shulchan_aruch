Se'if 7: A hen, etc. The Rambam's version there: the first clause is on Chol HaMoed, and the difficulty from "if she died," etc.; and see siman 536, se'if 4. That is to say, what Rav Huna said, "within three days of her rebelling," refers to the first clause and to Chol HaMoed, and "after three days of her sitting" refers to the latter clause and to the fourteenth. And one who looks into the Rambam will see that his intent is not so, for he did not mention at all "within three days of her rebelling," and it is implied there that also in the first clause and on Chol HaMoed it is specifically where she sat three days, for he wrote "a hen that sat," etc., as written here. And he wrote: and on the Moed one may not set [them], but if she fled they return her to her place; it is implied similarly to the first clause. Rather it appears to me that he did not have the reading in Rav Huna's words "within three days of her rebelling," and so it is implied from the Rif, who omitted Rav Huna's words and only ruled like Rav Ammi, while the Rambam ruled like Rav Huna.
