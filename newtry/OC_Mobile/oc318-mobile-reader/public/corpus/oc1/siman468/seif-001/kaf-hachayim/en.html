(a) [Seif 1] One who does melacha on erev Pesach from midday onwards, etc. — the reason is explained in the Yerushalmi: because it is the time of the Pesach offering from midday onwards; and on a day an individual brings a sacrifice melacha is forbidden; and what is not forbidden in melacha on erev Pesach except from midday onwards is because he may not offer it until midday; and even nowadays when there is no Pesach, since it was forbidden though the reason ceased the prohibition did not cease — Tosafot and Ran beginning of chapter "the place where they practiced"; and so Rosh there brought proof — see there; Beit Yosef, Beit David. However Rashi wrote the reason lest he be preoccupied with melacha and forget his chametz removal, Pesach slaughter, and matzah preparation for the night — see there; and though Rashi wrote this reason for those who practice not to do melacha all day, nevertheless there is a practical difference for erev Pesach on Shabbat: though there is no Pesach on erev Shabbat, melacha is forbidden that erev Shabbat for those who practice all day per Rashi's reason; but per Tosafot, Ran, and Rosh, since it is only because of the sacrifice and on erev Shabbat there is no sacrifice, it is permitted — as below letter 3 — see there. It appears from Beit Yosef he only brought Ran and Rosh who do not hold Rashi's reason; and so Levush only Tosafot and Ran's reason; and so Taz seif katan 2; Magen Avraham seif katan 1; and so Peri Chadash this reason is primary unlike Rashi — see there; and so Chok Yosef letter 1 this reason of Tosafot and Ran; Ma'amar Mordechai letter 1; Razah letter 1; Chayei Adam general rule 129 letter 2.
