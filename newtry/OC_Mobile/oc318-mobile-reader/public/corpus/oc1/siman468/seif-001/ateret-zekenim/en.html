And women should not give garments to launderers after midday (Lechem Rav in the name of the pious Gaon Maharash in Mahari, of blessed memory); and one does not comfort a mourner on erev Pesach — I found written in Shulchan Aruch that our teacher the Gaon, may the memory of the holy be blessed, also [wrote that] taking nails is forbidden on erev Pesach after midday (Maharil). The custom is to get a haircut after midday through an akum, but not through a Jew — even for free it is forbidden (my teacher in Bach).
