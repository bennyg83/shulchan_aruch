(א) Seif 1 — one who does, etc. See Tosafot 50a s.v. "a place," etc., and proof from what is written above 308:1 "let us say," etc., and Tosafot there s.v. "let us say," etc., and Tosafot Eruvin 5:2 s.v. "and the Mishnah."

(ב) And even — as on Chol HaMoed; see siman 472 seif 1 and as written below.

(ג) And there is one who forbids, etc. — as on Chol HaMoed; see Moed Katan 12:1 and letter 4 of Pesachim and Moed Katan 13:1: erev Pesach equals Chol HaMoed only in those matters the Sages distinguished; and though on erev Pesach prohibition is only rabbinic, nevertheless amira l'akum is forbidden in rabbinic matters too, as in the case of a non-Jew coming to extinguish, etc., and even if he did it himself it is forbidden as in the case of a non-Jew who brought flutes, etc., and they said one who comes from outside the boundary, etc.; and further Chol HaMoed some say is rabbinic.

(ד) And there is, etc. — as in Bava Metzia 90a: they asked — it appears in rabbinic prohibition it is plainly permitted, and though on Shabbat forbidden even in rabbinic matters as above, Shabbat is different since its essence is d'oraisa as Tosafot wrote in many places; Chol HaMoed is not proof as Tosafot there s.v. "but," etc. Per the first reasoning one may say the same on erev Pesach, for it too is included in moed; and further one may say in every melacha prohibition they decreed lest it lead to Shabbat.
