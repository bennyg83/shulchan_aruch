A non-Jew. See Baer Heitev; and see Shevut Yaakov, part 1, who wrote that even though Or Zarua permits, his heart did not fill him to be lenient in practice against Mahariv and Maharil, and even regarding one who is accustomed (in every erev Shabbos, see there, etc.). And see above, siman 260, regarding cutting nails on erev Shabbos during Chol HaMoed, where I wrote there the responsum of Neos Shemen, and it is also brought briefly below in Baer Heitev, siman 532; and in Shevut Yaakov on that siman he challenged him and wrote that it is not comparable to immersion, for there is not so great a mitzvah on erev Shabbos, and also regarding intervening substance for netilas yadayim there is none, for they are not so particular about a bit of filth between the nails from Shabbos to Shabbos, and in any case there are those who are lenient regarding netilas yadayim, etc.; and all the more so where it is possible to cut them through a non-Jew; and if that is impossible, nevertheless it is possible to cut them with his hands or with his teeth, for in such a case even on Chol HaMoed it appears that it is permitted; nevertheless, for a mitzvah immersion one should not do so; and see siman 532; end of his words. And it appears that regarding filth under the nails, because of particularity for netilas yadayim, he may scrape the filth from there without cutting the nails.
