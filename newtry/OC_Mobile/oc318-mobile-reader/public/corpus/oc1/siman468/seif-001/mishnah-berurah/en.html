(א) From midday onwards — the reason is because it is the time of Pesach slaughter, for each Jew is obligated in it, and a day one brings a sacrifice is like a Yom Tov; therefore melacha is forbidden by rabbinic decree; and even nowadays when there is no sacrifice the prohibition remains in place.

(ב) We excommunicate him — and also he does not see a sign of blessing from that melacha.

(ג) For free is forbidden — if it is complete melacha as below in seif 2.

(ד) Even through a Jew — as on Chol HaMoed.

(ה) And there is one who permits — even if he does it in a Jew's house, for they hold they were not stringent regarding amira l'akum as on Chol HaMoed and Yom Tov. Regarding getting a haircut through akum after midday, many Acharonim agreed to be lenient even though he tilts his head toward the barber and assists somewhat; but through a Jew it is forbidden even for free because it is complete melacha. Cutting nails — if he forgot before midday, there is room to be lenient even by himself or through another for free.
