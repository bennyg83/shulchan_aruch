(א) We excommunicate him. In the Tur he brings the Gemara: he does not see a sign of blessing forever; and Rashi and the Mizrachi explained that this means specifically in that matter; and "forever" means even if he has some profit from it, he will have a loss corresponding to it from another place.

(ב) And so is the custom. Thus Rashi: and the custom is to get a haircut after midday through an akum, but not through a Jew — and so I received, end of his words. And Maharil wrote that even to cut one's nails is forbidden on erev Pesach after midday; and the reason for the prohibition of melacha on erev Pesach is that every individual who brings a sacrifice — that day becomes for him like a Yom Tov and melacha is forbidden; and on erev Pesach after midday they are obligated in slaughtering the Pesach, and even though we have no Pesach sacrifice the prohibition remains in its place.
