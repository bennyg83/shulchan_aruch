Akum. They practiced to get a haircut through an akum after midday, but through a Jew it is forbidden — and all the more so a Jew should not cut hair for an akum. Mahariv and Maharil wrote that cutting nails is also forbidden after midday. Ateret Zekenim permits cutting nails if one forgot before midday — see Magen Avraham seif katan 13 and Eliyah Rabbah. Law of a mourner whose [mourning] fell on erev Pesach in the middle — see Yoreh De'ah siman 399. Magen Avraham wrote that everything permitted on Chol HaMoed is all the more so permitted on erev Pesach. Women should not give garments to a launderer after midday. (Taz)
