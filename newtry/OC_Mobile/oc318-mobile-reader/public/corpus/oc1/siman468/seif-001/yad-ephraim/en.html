In Magen Avraham, s.k. 12. And it does not appear to me: granted regarding examination it is called private, for from where, etc.; to my humble opinion this requires further study, for in Baal HaMeor, Likutim of Maharil, this is what is difficult for him against Maharil: that he should have permitted the youths themselves to eat pantzen, just as he permitted them to eat from lung examinations; and if so, there is no place at all to distinguish between privately and publicly. If one who comes from another place must practice prohibition because of the stringencies of the place from which he left, it is well to say that also those who dwell there may not permit before them; and that is the reason of Jerusalem: since at that time they were expert in the stringency of the place from which he left, therefore those who gathered were forbidden in melacha according to the custom of their place, and also the people of Jerusalem were not permitted to permit before them. And see below, siman 493, in Magen Avraham, s.k. 6, who wrote the reason of Tosafos that those who gather in other places have a prohibition because of "do not cut yourselves"; but since Maharil stated regarding lung examinations like the Mordechai and permitted even those coming from other places themselves to eat, for the reason that they are not expert, etc., if so also regarding pantzen he should have permitted even the youths themselves to eat for that reason; and whenever the permission is also for the youths themselves, there is no place to distinguish between privately and publicly; and therefore Maharil's collecting student needed to resolve Maharil's words so that they not contradict one another, because he was stringent with cheilev according to the Torah; so it appears to my humble opinion. And see Shach, Yoreh De'ah siman 109.
