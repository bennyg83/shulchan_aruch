From midday onwards — see Mishna Berurah; it is per the Yerushalmi the Poskim brought that the reason is because of Pesach slaughter; accordingly if Pesach falls on erev Shabbat, melacha is permitted by basic law until minchah since Pesach slaughter is the next day on Shabbat, as Maharil wrote; Rashi further explained lest he be preoccupied with melacha and forget his chametz removal, etc., and matzah preparation for the night, etc.; per this reason even when Pesach falls on erev Shabbat one should be careful not to do melacha from midday since he is preoccupied with chametz removal and matzah preparation for tomorrow's need; but most Poskim hold the Yerushalmi reason — therefore possibly there is no need to be stringent.
