(א) From midday. For it is the time of slaughtering the Pesach; and even though now there is no Pesach, nevertheless it is a matter [prohibited] by vote — unlike uncovered water siman 116 — Rosh. And that it is not mentioned in Kol Bo is because of the daily offerings, for that is a communal sacrifice; and Rashi wrote the reason lest he delay chametz removal; practical difference: erev Pesach on Shabbat — Kaf HaChayyim.

(ב) We excommunicate — all the more one strikes lashes of rebellion which is lighter than excommunication — see Kesubos 55 and Kiddushin 70 and Orach Chayyim siman 9; and also he does not see a sign of blessing from that melacha — see Rabbenu Nissim seif 2.
