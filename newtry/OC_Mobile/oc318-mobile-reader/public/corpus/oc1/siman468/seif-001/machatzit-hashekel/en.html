(א) (Seif katan 1) From midday, etc. See siman 458 — meaning though we learned they slaughtered Pesach at six and a half, nevertheless from midday is called Pesach slaughter time; and since it is Pesach slaughter time Yerushalmi wrote it becomes Yom Tov, for all year when an individual brings a sacrifice it is his Yom Tov; similarly on erev Pesach when each Jew would slaughter his Pesach; only from midday is not Yom Tov until midday because its time is from midday onwards unlike Pesach whose time is from morning — therefore not Yom Tov until midday; Chayei Adam wrote in the name of Maharil: if erev Pesach falls on Shabbat, by law melacha is permitted on erev Shabbat until minchah time as forbidden from Shabbat (as siman 251) since Pesach overrides Shabbat and they slaughtered Pesach on Shabbat; only one who wants to be stringent may be stringent because of not distinguishing.

(ב) And Chayei Adam wrote nevertheless per Rashi's reason who wrote melacha is forbidden lest he be preoccupied and forget chametz removal, Pesach slaughter, and matzah preparation from beforehand — therefore also when erev Pesach falls on Shabbat melacha is forbidden on erev Shabbat.

(ג) Unlike uncovered water — we rule in Yoreh De'ah siman 116 since snakes are not common among us, uncovered liquids are not forbidden, for though initially they forbade from fear a snake drank and injected venom — we do not say it is a matter [prohibited] by vote requiring another vote to permit; see siman 9 seif katan 7 — should read another reasoning to distinguish there.
