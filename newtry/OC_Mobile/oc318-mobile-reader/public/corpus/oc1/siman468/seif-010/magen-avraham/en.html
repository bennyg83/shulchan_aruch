Not to perform bloodletting — as stated in end of chapter 5 of Shabbos that on erev Shavuos the demon Tavuach emerges, and were it not that Israel accepted the Torah he would slaughter their flesh and blood; and the Sages decreed on all Festivals because of erev Shavuos. Kaporos — Mahariv forbids and Maharil permits except on Hoshana Rabbah because it is a day of judgment; and on the last Yom Tov of Pesach everything is permitted because it is not a Festival in its own right (Magen Tzedek); and on the night before Yom Tov it appears to me it is permitted to let blood except the night of Hoshana Rabbah.
