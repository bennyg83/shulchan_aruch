Bloodletting. As stated in end of chapter 5 of Shabbos that on erev Shavuos the demon Tavuach emerges, and were it not that Israel accepted the Torah he would slaughter their flesh and blood; and the Sages decreed on all Festivals because of erev Shavuos. Kaporos that we call Kaporos — Mahariv forbids and Maharil permits except on Hoshana Rabbah because it is a day of judgment. And on the last Yom Tov of Pesach everything is permitted because it is not a Festival in its own right (Magen Tzedek). And on the night before Yom Tov it appears permitted to let blood except the night of Hoshana Rabbah — Magen Avraham. And Kaf HaChayyim wrote it appears that in any event on erev Shavuos one should be stringent with Kaporos, unlike other Festivals which are only a general decree because of erev Shavuos — Eliyah Rabbah. One does not recite the psalm of thanksgiving on erev Pesach nor on Chol HaMoed of Pesach; and the Mishnah of thanksgiving they say is only a mere remembrance — Taz. And see Yoreh De'ah siman 91 in the gloss.
