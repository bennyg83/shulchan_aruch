The same applies to scarification, which we call cupping, as Mahari Weil and Maharil permit, except on Hoshana Rabbah because it is a day of judgment, end quote from the Magen Avraham. It appears to me that in any event on Erev Shavuos one should be stringent, for danger is more severe, as stated at the end of chapter 18 of Shabbos, unlike the other eves of Yom Tov, where it is only a general decree because of Erev Shavuos.
On any Erev Yom Tov. The Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Matteh Moshe that on the night before Yom Tov it is permitted to let blood, except on the night of Hoshana Rabbah. And on the eve of the last day of Pesach everything is permitted, for it is not a festival in its own right, end quote.
