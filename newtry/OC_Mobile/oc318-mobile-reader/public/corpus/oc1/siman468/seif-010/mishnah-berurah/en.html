(38) Not to let blood. On erev Shavuos a destructive spirit named Tavoach emerged. Had the Jewish people not accepted the Torah, it would have slaughtered their flesh and blood. The Sages therefore issued a decree concerning bloodletting on every erev Yom Tov because of erev Shavuos. Some permit cupping procedures known as bankes or kopfsetzen, except on Hoshana Rabbah, which is a day of judgment. In any event, one should be stringent on erev Shavuos. It is permitted to let blood on the night preceding erev Yom Tov, except on the night of Hoshana Rabbah. On erev the final Yom Tov of Pesach everything is permitted, since that day is not a separate festival. All this applies when one wishes to let blood for general health. When it is needed because of danger, however, many people have already acted leniently, and “Hashem protects the simple.” Even on erev Shavuos, Eliyah Rabbah permitted it when physicians ordered it for a dangerously ill person, but not otherwise.
