(38) Not to perform bloodletting — on erev Shavuos the demon Tavuach emerges, and had Israel not accepted the Torah he would slaughter their flesh and blood; Sages decreed on all Festivals because of erev Shavuos. Kaporos some permit except Hoshana Rabbah as judgment day; and on erev Shavuos one should be stringent in any event. On the night before Yom Tov permitted to let blood except Hoshana Rabbah night. Last Yom Tov of Pesach all permitted as not a Festival in its own right. All when wanting bloodletting for health; for danger many have ruled leniently and Hashem protects the simple; even on erev Shavuos Ateret Zekenim permitted when doctors ordered for dangerous illness; but otherwise not.
