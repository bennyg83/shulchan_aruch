Not to perform bloodletting, etc. A decree because of erev Shavuos, for there is danger there because of the demon named Tavuach; and it is implied in Kol Bo that on the last Yom Tov of Pesach there is no concern; and this "not to perform bloodletting" is the same as what they call Kaporos. Mahariv forbids. It is impossible to recite the chapter of Tehillim of thanksgiving on erev Pesach, because the time of thanksgiving is day and night until midday , and accordingly it is impossible, because there is chametz in the loaves of thanksgiving and it will not be eaten in its [proper] time and it is bringing sacred things to a disqualified place; and all the more so in the Temple , one may not say it. The Levush wondered: if so, the Mishnah of thanksgiving and the ram of a nazir too one should not say. And there is no difficulty: the Mishnah is only a mere remembrance, unlike the chapter of Tehillim of thanksgiving which is in place of offering thanksgiving.
