(א) Melacha to earn wages — in this, even if not complete melacha it is forbidden, even for Yom Tov need. Acharonim wrote a worker who has nothing to eat may do melacha to earn wages.

(ב) And it is complete melacha — in this it is forbidden even for Yom Tov need. Laundering one's garments is included in complete melacha and forbidden. Giving garments to an akum laundress is permitted in all respects after midday. Acharonim wrote everything permitted on Chol HaMoed — such as davar ha'aved, or doing an amateur act not a artisan's work with Festival need, etc. — is all the more permitted on erev Pesach after midday; and those permitted to cut hair and launder on Chol HaMoed, such as one coming from abroad and one leaving prison, are also permitted on erev Pesach after midday.

(ג) He repairs his utensils for Yom Tov — meaning old vessels that broke somewhat or garments torn somewhat — permitted to repair and sew for Yom Tov need; similarly permitted for another's need if for free; Acharonim wrote even a artisan's work is permitted in this.

(ד) For himself for his study — since it is his study method and for himself he writes, it does not appear as complete melacha, for certainly he does not intend proper finished writing — it is like mere repair permitted for Festival need. For others, even for free, forbidden in this.
