(b) And they wrote there Tosafot imply in the Yerushalmi it is forbidden by Torah law; and so Rosh in responsa general rule 55 siman 10; but Ran there wrote it is possible it is only rabbinic — see there; see Tosafot Chagigah 17b; and Maggid Mishneh laws of Temple vessels chapter 6 — Tosafot and Ran did not dispute except in explaining the Yerushalmi, but per our Gemara Tosafot too agree it is only rabbinic — see there; and so Rambam laws of Yom Tov chapter 8 law 18: the fourteenth of Nissan melacha is forbidden by rabbinic law like Chol HaMoed and it is lighter than Chol HaMoed, forbidden only from half the day onwards which is slaughter time; but from sunrise until half the day depends on custom — see there; and so Semag; and now too in our time it is forbidden on the sacrifice day by rabbinic law like Chol HaMoed — see there; and Peri Chadash decided melacha prohibition on erev Pesach after midday is only rabbinic per Rambam — see there; Shulchan Gavoah letter 1 wrote Rashi too holds only rabbinic; and so in Magen HaElef letter 3 it is only rabbinic.
