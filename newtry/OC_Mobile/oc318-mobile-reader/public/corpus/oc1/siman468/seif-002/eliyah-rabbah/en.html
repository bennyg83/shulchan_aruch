[2] [Levush] since each one, etc. Rashi there explained the reason: lest he be preoccupied with melacha and forget removal of chametz, slaughtering the Pesach, and preparing matzah, end of his words — implying he does not hold the sacrifice reason as Tosafot Yom Tov wrote there Mishnah 5; and it appears to me he proved thus: if he also held the sacrifice reason, Rashi should not have written the reason of Pesach slaughter and matzah preparation after midday, which is in any case forbidden because of the sacrifice — only the reason of his chametz removal alone, which also applies before midday where they practiced prohibition all day; however one may say he wrote because of erev Pesach falling on Shabbat that melacha is forbidden also on erev Shabbat from being preoccupied with the mitzvah; however where they practiced to do on erev Pesach until midday it appears permitted on erev Shabbat after midday even per Rashi, for he does not hold the preoccupation reason.
