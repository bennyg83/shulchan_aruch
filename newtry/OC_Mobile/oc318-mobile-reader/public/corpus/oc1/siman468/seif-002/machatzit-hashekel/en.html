(Seif katan 2) We excommunicate him — and also he does not see sign 2; and so implied in the Gemara that challenges: we come to teach from midday onwards everywhere forbidden (see Maharsha); and it challenges: why teach specifically erev Pesach — even erev Shabbat and Yom Tov one who does melacha from minchah onwards does not see a sign of blessing; and answers: there specifically from minchah it is forbidden, here from midday; alternatively there it is sign of blessing he is not fit, here we also excommunicate him; Tur ruled both answers are true for law; and since the Gemara says here "we also excommunicate him," the word "also" teaches though he does not see a sign of blessing we still excommunicate him; sign of blessing means from that melacha even if he profits he loses elsewhere — so Taz.
