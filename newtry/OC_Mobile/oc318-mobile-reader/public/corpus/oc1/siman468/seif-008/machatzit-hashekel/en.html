(Seif katan 8) He does, etc., and if he is still, etc., even without intention to return permitted — meaning he has not yet entered the city's boundary when he decided to settle there; but if he already entered the city once, even if he later left and is outside the boundary, nevertheless forbidden since he already settled there; Magen Avraham wrote below siman 496 seif katan 7 outside the boundary is called wilderness.
