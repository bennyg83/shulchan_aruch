To the beis midrash. See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef writes in the name of his grandfather that judging cases without receiving payment counts toward establishing fixed times for Torah, but judging for payment does not. Likewise, teaching children for payment does not count. See there, where he is uncertain whether one who studies in a yeshivah at a fixed time and receives a stipend fulfills this obligation. See there further that when Chazal said, “Whoever studies halachos,” the minimum plural “halachos” means two. Sefer HaManhig writes likewise, and Rashbash cites and agrees with it in responsum 52.
