1) [Seif 1] After he leaves the synagogue, etc. One should not leave the synagogue until the entire prayer service has concluded, unless he needs to relieve himself or vomit. Sefer Chassidim, simanim 18, 779, and 780; see there. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot HaTur. R' Chaim Yosef David Azulai explains there in siman 779 that this means until the end of the final Kaddish, both on weekdays and on Shabbos. He writes that the pious R' Moshe Mizrachi, of blessed memory, was particular about this and would rebuke those who left early; see there.
