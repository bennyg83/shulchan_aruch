2) There: He should go to the beis midrash, etc. Anyone who leaves the synagogue, enters the beis midrash, and engages in Torah study merits and receives the presence of the Shechinah, as it is stated, 'They shall go from strength to strength,' etc. Berachos 64a. Regarding the point that requires clarification here, see my holy sefer Chaim Ad HaOlam at the conclusion of tractate Berachos. The Tur writes: 'He should go to the beis midrash before going to his business and set a fixed time for study, for Rava said: when a person is brought in for judgment, they ask him, Did you conduct your business faithfully? Did you establish fixed times for Torah study?' etc. This is from the Gemara, Shabbos 31a. Regarding the apparent difficulty that in Sanhedrin 7a they said that a person's judgment begins only concerning words of Torah, etc., see the Beit Yosef and Bach, and what I, the lowly one, wrote in my holy sefer Yismach Yisrael, in the discourse on the Torah for parashas Bamidbar.
