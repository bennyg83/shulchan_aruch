Morning bread. The Bach wrote in the name of Mordechai that the benefit of morning bread applies only to grain grown far from inhabited areas, which did not hear the sound of a rooster or the sound of those pulling ships, and those accustomed to it must eat it regularly every day. The Taz and Magen Avraham wrote that one who fulfills the statement according to its plain meaning acts properly. See Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 15.
