(There) In a place where its impurity is great. See my responsum regarding a heated mikveh that was heated and contains vapor and impurity, Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 80 [there it was asked regarding one who has in his room an excavation — a mikveh of hot water to purify the women of his household, covered with a wooden cover — whether it is permitted to study in that room when there is no bathtub there and nothing needed for immersion, only a deficiency in the mikveh that is heated when needed once or twice a month; and in his answer he concludes thus: In the case before us, the room in which the excavation is located is clean and pure; and sometimes once or twice a month a woman immerses in hot water there; and even though the excavation has great impurity, nevertheless the room, which is a domain unto itself, is clean and pure and has no vapor or impurity in it; and if so, at a time when no women are laundering there and the excavation is covered with boards, I know no reason to forbid studying there; and as a good practice, a movable partition should stand outside between the student and the covered excavation. And I know a factual case: the famous pious man R' Zalman Chasid, z"l, in Frankfurt had a hot mikveh in his courtyard in his garden; it was covered, and he studied there during the summer days, and no person objected at all].
