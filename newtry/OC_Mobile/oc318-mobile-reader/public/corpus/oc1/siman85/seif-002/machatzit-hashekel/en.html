(Sk. 2) In the holy tongue etc. — there is on Shabbat etc. that hirhur is permitted; see in the Rosh and Terumat HaDeshen etc. the reason that we establish that hirhur is not considered like speech.

That which is forbidden to engage in hirhur in the bathhouse — that is, in the bathroom it is certainly so that hirhur is forbidden, as is stated there in Shabbat regarding excrement: "and your camp shall be holy," and the word "speech" was not mentioned in the verse — implying that even hirhur is forbidden; and as Rashi explains there, that the intent of the verse is that your encampment shall be holy — implying that since Israel constantly engage in hirhur in words of Torah, it is forbidden. But in the bathhouse, seemingly its prohibition is only because of uncovering nakedness, since people stand there naked; and therefore it is not forbidden in all matters — even greeting is forbidden only in the inner room where everyone stands naked. Therefore the Magen Avraham challenged: behold, hirhur opposite nakedness is permitted!

Because filth is abundant there through use in hot water — and it is like excrement in the bathroom. And therefore I was uncertain about our mikvaot, which are often heated — whether the law of the bathhouse applies, for filth is also abundant there. However, in Yoreh Deah siman 1, seif 14, it implies etc. that it teaches: one who is naked may not slaughter, because he is not permitted to bless; and the Shach and Taz wrote there, saying: although another can bless and he slaughters — even though it is forbidden for another to bless opposite him since he is naked — nevertheless that other one can turn his face and bless. And one must say that in any case the slaughterer is forbidden to intend to fulfill his obligation through another's blessing, since he is naked and hears as one who answers — and on this the Magen Avraham challenged: behold, hirhur opposite nakedness is permitted — so why is it forbidden for the naked one to intend to fulfill his obligation through another's blessing? And one must say that shomea ke'einah is weightier than hirhur, since he has the status as if he himself said it.
