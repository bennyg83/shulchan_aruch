Forbidden — see Ba'er Heitev. See in Megillah page 28: "In my days I did not engage in hirhur" etc., and in the Rashba's novellae there — and this is as stated in Berakhot page 24, that according to Rabbi Yehudah when one is walking it is permitted from the law; see in Avodat HaRokeach and in She'elat Ya'avetz siman 10; see below sk 5; see in Yoreh Deah, end of siman 246, that one may enter the bathroom from an unpunctuated halachic matter; see in Radbaz siman 342, and it was brought in Be'er Mayim Chayim; and he wrote in the name of Lechem Yehudah, chapter 3 of Keriat Shema, that it is forbidden to engage in hirhur within four cubits behind the bathroom, at its sides, and before it — as far as the eye sees — see there; and She'elat Ya'avetz wrote that it is forbidden to examine in the bathroom the weights of the names and verbs of the holy tongue, for there is no way to reach knowledge except through the Scriptures, and one will come to hirhur in Scripture — see there siman 10; and in Birkhei Yosef he learned from this that it is forbidden to arrange there a written aphorism in Talmudic language, for one will certainly come to some hirhur in the Talmudic passage — see there.
