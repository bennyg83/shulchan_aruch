If he encounters, etc., a prohibited matter See Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 14, where I cited a stringent view. That view is from Chayei Adam and Nishmas Adam; see there. Recently the novellae of the Ran on Shabbos were printed, and he states explicitly there contrary to that view. The proofs brought in Nishmas Adam can be rejected, but this is not the place to elaborate. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav likewise rules in accordance with Magen Avraham.
