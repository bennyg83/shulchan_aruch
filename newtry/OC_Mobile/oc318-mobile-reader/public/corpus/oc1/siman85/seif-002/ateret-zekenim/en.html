And similarly nicknames such as Rachum or Ne'eman may be said there. {Rama: See Avodah Zarah, and some forbid the name Rachum (Ra'avad), because we find the name Rachum only regarding the Holy Name, and one should be stringent with the name Rachum; however, in Ashkenaz language or a foreign language, all permit it, as with Shalom (my master in Beit Chadash).} But other words of the holy tongue may not be said in secular language (chapter Kirah).
