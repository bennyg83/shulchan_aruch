Likewise, descriptive names such as Merciful or Faithful may be said there. Gloss: Some forbid the name Merciful, in the name of the Raavad, because we find the designation Merciful applied only to Hashem, blessed be He, and one should be stringent regarding Merciful. Nevertheless, in German or another foreign language, all agree that it is permitted, as with the word peace, according to my teacher in Bach. Other sacred Hebrew expressions, however, may not be said there even in a secular language. (Chapter Kirah.)
