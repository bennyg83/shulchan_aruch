(1) [Seif 1] A person shall not go in filthy alleyways, etc. And even if he was reading and came, he must pause, etc. And if he did not pause, Scripture says: "Also I gave them statutes that are not good and ordinances by which they shall not live." And if he paused, Scripture says: "And through this matter you shall prolong your days." Tur. And this is from the Gemara Berachos 24b. And similarly Levush. And see Eliyah Rabbah, letter alef.
