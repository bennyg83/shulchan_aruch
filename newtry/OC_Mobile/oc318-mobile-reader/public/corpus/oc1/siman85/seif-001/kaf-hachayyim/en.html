1) [Seif 1] A person should not walk through filthy alleyways, etc. Even if he was already reciting and then came there, he must stop, etc. If he did not stop, the verse says of him, ‘I also gave them statutes that were not good and ordinances by which they could not live.’ If he stopped, the verse says of him, ‘Through this matter you shall prolong your days.’ Tur. This is from the Gemara, Berachos, folio 24b. So also wrote the Levush. See Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1.
