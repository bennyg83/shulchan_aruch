In which places is it forbidden to recite Keriat Shema; and in it are 2
seifim. A person should not walk in filthy alleyways and place his hand on his mouth and recite Keriat Shema; and even if he was reciting and came, he must pause when he reaches the filthy alleyway. And when he emerges from there — even if he delayed as long as it would take to complete all of it — he need not return except to the place where he paused.And some say that he returns to the beginning, and this is the essential ruling (see above siman 65).]:
