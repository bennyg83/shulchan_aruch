Even at night, in seif 2 — that when his hands are not clean from touching the body, he may engage in hirhur of the blessing if he wishes to drink; but if actually filthy, hirhur is forbidden. And behold, excrement on his flesh covered by his garments — there are opinions in Be'er Heitev, seif 4; but if the bed is not clean, one should say that all agree that when covered it is permitted. And in Magen Avraham there, letter vav, zayin; and in what he wrote there:

In what he wrote — the Mechaber in seif 1: one should not walk etc., Berakhot 24b, that even while walking it is forbidden, and even when one does not see the excrement — we require "your camp shall be holy," and he returns and recites. See in the Gemara the verse "and through this thing you will prolong your days" (Deuteronomy 32:47); see Derishah — from that it is written "to observe, to perform all the words of this Torah" — for it is not a mere utterance etc., and "to observe" the Torah (the simple meaning of the verse is to observe the Torah and perform positive commandments; but from "to observe, to perform" without a vav before "all the words of the Torah," it implies that even in positive commandments there is guarding) — and this is that sometimes it is forbidden to engage in Torah and to be only from it in a filthy place; and not only this, but "through this you fulfill a mitzvah in this silence, for it is your life if you engage in it, and in this thing that you pause, you prolong your days" — see there. And if God wills, in Sefer HaMagid this will be explained.

If his learning is fluent in his mouth and he engaged in hirhur under duress — it is permitted, Beit Yosef and Avodat HaRokeach and Peri Chadash; see Derishah — even to express with his lips it is permitted, since under duress hirhur is permitted, and so in Zevachim 102: "this law I learned" etc. See there.
