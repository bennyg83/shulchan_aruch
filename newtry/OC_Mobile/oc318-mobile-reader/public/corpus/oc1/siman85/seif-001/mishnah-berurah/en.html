(1) And he places his hand etc. — meaning that he recites so quietly that it is not recognizable at all that he moves his lips; this too is forbidden.

(2) When he reaches — meaning within four cubits; and if it is before him, it is forbidden as far as the eye sees.

(3) And this is the essential ruling — see in siman 65 in Mishnah Berurah, where we explained there all the details of the law.
