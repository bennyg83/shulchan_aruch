(1) And place his hand, etc. Meaning, even if he reads so quietly that it is entirely imperceptible that his lips are moving, it is still forbidden.
(2) When he reaches. Meaning, when he comes within four amos. If the excrement is in front of him, it is forbidden for as far as he can see.
(3) And this is the primary ruling. See siman 65 in Mishnah Berurah, where we explained all the details of this law.
