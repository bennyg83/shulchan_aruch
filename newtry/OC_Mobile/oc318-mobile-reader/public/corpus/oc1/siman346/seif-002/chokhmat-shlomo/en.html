There seif 2 — and the sea and valley are two types of karmelit and permitted to carry from one to the other etc. Note: apparently there is no understanding in this — if it were one karmelit all the more it would be permitted to carry from one to the other within four amot, for such is the law in karmelit; and to say he speaks for emphasis saying even though they are two karmelitot permitted to carry — this is not the implication of his language; therefore it appears to explain that they do not combine for four amot and it is permitted to carry in each within its four amot; and even if combined they are more than four amot — nevertheless they do not combine since each is a domain independently; and permitted to carry in both up to eight amot; but if it were one karmelit it would be forbidden more than four amot — as appears to me; requires study and I have no time to investigate the later authorities whether they noticed this:
