But — see Taz — his words need explanation; in Bach it appears the division of one standing in reshut ha-yachid who removes to reshut ha-rabbim via makom patur while remaining standing in reshut ha-yachid and inserts or removes or passes to his fellow's hand or throws — liable (and he teaches us that caught is not like placed even if the object sees the air of the ground in throwing); and the Tur's text before this division has another division: provided he does not remove from reshut ha-yachid to makom patur and transfer it from makom patur to reshut ha-rabbim — three divisions: the first two for liability — one when walking with his feet for he is not like one standing; second when standing in his place and removes, passes to his fellow's hand or throws — caught is not like placing and liable; and from case 1 we do not learn that one who walks and an object is via him and does not see the face of makom patur; and third — standing in makom patur and removes from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim — exempt but forbidden; but Beit Yosef explained according to this text from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim via makom patur — meaning he stood in makom patur; and in case 1 also exempt and forbidden as he wrote this is like the law in case 3; and there placing of his body is like placing of an object and forbidden since it appears as uprooting — his hand did not rest; and in case 1 greater novelty — even though he rested in makom patur literally and afterward uprooted from there his body and hand — forbidden for he belittles Shabbat; and for Bach and Taz this was not resolved — for if so the order should be case 2 first then 3 then 1 — not this and also that; and in both exempt and forbidden (Avodat HaKodesh); and for Taz also Bach's explanation was not resolved — in case 1 meaning case 2 in his explanation — standing — he did not remain there; and behold from reshut ha-yachid to makom patur via reshut ha-rabbim to permit l'chatchila is difficult from Mishnah like siman 347 — uprooting without placing l'chatchila is forbidden; and to say in Mishnah melacha was done between them — from where? Behold one may say a fortiori uprooting alone is forbidden or d'rabbanan in any case in such a case; and what Beit Yosef wrote perhaps he will stand to rest and be liable — this is d'rabbanan and one decree; or do not hold for one who clears his objects from a corner and reconsidered he is exempt for one uprooting is not uprooting at all — unlike this he intended to uproot from reshut ha-yachid in any case since he rested in reshut ha-rabbim — possibly liable; or perhaps sometimes he intends to uproot from reshut ha-yachid and rest in reshut ha-rabbim; and what is written in siman 348 there — threw from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid with reshut ha-rabbim in the middle — and there he is not walking so why permit? One may say; and in conclusion Taz raised that the old text is primary in the Tur and case 1 for liability when walking for he is not like standing; and second for liability when standing and remaining in reshut ha-yachid and removes or inserts to reshut ha-rabbim via makom patur; and he explains removing and inserting whether passing or throwing; and caught even though it sees the face of the ground is not like placing (see beginning of Shabbat) in this; and nevertheless the main law he agreed with Beit Yosef that he forbade d'rabbanan in any case in such a case when removing from reshut ha-yachid to makom patur via reshut ha-rabbim; and possibly throwing in such a case is also forbidden; and if so in siman 347 and 348 it will be explained further and here one should not expand:
