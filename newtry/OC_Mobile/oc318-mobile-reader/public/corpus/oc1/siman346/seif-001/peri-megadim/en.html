(ב) And know that passing from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim is a type of removing and inserting; however passing from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid with reshut ha-rabbim in the middle that one is liable is mentioned at the beginning of chapter HaZorek and in Rambam chapter 13 law 8 — even above ten it appears all the more below ten; and Rashi Shabbat 2b s.v. "throwing" from passing etc.; and in Tosafos there 3 s.v. "in making it" in the name of Yerushalmi chapter HaZorek that passing from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid with reshut ha-rabbim in the middle one is liable only if the passer passed into his fellow's hand — behold if he passed onto reshut ha-yachid alone he is exempt — all the more when his fellow receives from him — see there; and in Rambam nothing of this is mentioned; and there is doubt whether they decreed passing from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via karmelit d'rabbanan above ten — one may say passing above ten is a novelty as I wrote in Chiddushei HaRim "you have in it only its novelty" and the rabbis did not decree; and I wrote in Chiddushei — passing below ten even though it is not a novelty — nevertheless with two balconies he is exempt — sufficient for one who comes from law to be as the case: derive from above ten which were in the Tabernacle.
