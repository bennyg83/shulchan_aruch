(א) And the Sages forbade etc. A decree because of from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim:

(ב) But one standing in reshut ha-yachid or removes etc. This "or" is a scribe's error and it should read "and removes" — and so in the Tur; and explanation is needed for what is written afterward "or passes" — for the first part also deals with passing — for otherwise how can he remove since he stands in reshut ha-yachid? Perforce what "one standing in reshut ha-yachid" means is not that he remained standing but that he was standing and walked and removed or inserted, or he remained standing but passed or threw; and Beit Yosef wrote that before this division it was written in his book: provided he does not remove from reshut ha-yachid to makom patur via reshut ha-rabbim; and he wrote on this that even though he ruled nearby that walking is not like standing — if so since he did not stand to rest in reshut ha-rabbim it is removing from reshut ha-yachid to makom patur which is permitted — nevertheless since if he stood in reshut ha-rabbim he is liable they decreed lest he stand; and in the old book it is written: provided he does not remove from reshut ha-yachid to makom patur and transfer it via makom patur to reshut ha-rabbim or the reverse — end of quote; and it is difficult on this — for if so what did the Tur write afterward "and likewise one standing in reshut ha-yachid" etc. — its explanation perforce is walking from reshut ha-yachid via makom patur to reshut ha-rabbim as written; and if so this is the first part; and for Beit Yosef's text this works; and Mahari Weil wrote on this text that it is mistaken — we do not find this decree lest he stand to rest; and according to law it is permitted l'chatchila to remove from reshut ha-yachid and pass with his foot via reshut ha-rabbim and place it in makom patur; and his words are very astonishing — for certainly this decree is included in what is written in the Gemara and "provided one not take from the homeowner and give to a poor person" — for he belittles Shabbat prohibitions l'chatchila to cause removing from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim — decree lest he come to remove and insert directly from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim — end of quote; and here too this decree applies; and further it appears to me clear proof to forbid in this — if we permit from reshut ha-yachid to makom patur via reshut ha-rabbim because walking is not like standing — likewise we should permit from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via reshut ha-rabbim since there is no standing in reshut ha-rabbim and uprooting and placing is from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid; and in siman 348 the Tur explains it is forbidden; simply that even though walking is not like standing — this is for liability — but for permission certainly forbidden in all cases because of belittling Shabbat; Heaven forbid we say any permission in this; and nevertheless it is difficult on Beit Yosef's text — we do not find this explicitly in the Gemara; and the cases the Tur wrote afterward are written explicitly and he should have preceded what is written explicitly in the Gemara; therefore it appears that even though from law there is reason to forbid even in Beit Yosef's case — nevertheless Beit Yosef's old text is primary; and what is written afterward "or passes" must be explained — meaning he removes and inserts whether via passing or via throwing — as appears to me:
