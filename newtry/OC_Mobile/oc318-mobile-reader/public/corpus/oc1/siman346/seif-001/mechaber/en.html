The laws of eruvin from the Torah. And in it are
3 seifim: From the Torah one is liable only for hotza'ah and hachnasah and throwing and passing from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim or from reshut ha-rabbim to reshut ha-yachid, and the Sages forbade from karmelit to reshut ha-yachid or to reshut ha-rabbim or from them to karmelit; but makom patur — it is permitted to perform hotza'ah and hachnasah from it to reshut ha-yachid and to reshut ha-rabbim and from them into it; but one standing in reshut ha-yachid who performs hotza'ah or hachnasah or passes or throws to reshut ha-rabbim via makom patur, or the reverse, is liable; and similarly one should not stand on makom patur and take an object from the hand of one standing in reshut ha-rabbim and give it to one standing in reshut ha-yachid, or the reverse; and to exchange via makom patur in rabbinic domains — some forbid and some permit [and see below siman 372 seif 6]:[']:
