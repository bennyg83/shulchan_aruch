"sheep of the house" etc. — see there; except according to Ran Nedarim 36a s.v. "R' Zeira said sheep of the house" etc. there is no necessity for Tosafos' law; and he concluded his responsum: with this we conclude — Heaven forbid to permit a minor to carry in a place without eruv etc.] — and see responsum of our rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 15 and responsum Ketav Sofer siman 77:
