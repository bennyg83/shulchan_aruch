1) [Seif 1] From the Torah one is liable only for removing etc. — meaning when he removes an object with his feet from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim or inserts it from reshut ha-rabbim to reshut ha-yachid, or when standing in reshut ha-yachid he throws it or passes it to reshut ha-rabbim or the reverse.
