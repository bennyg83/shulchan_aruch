(א) And passing — meaning his body stands in one domain and he passes the object in his hand to another domain; and see below siman 347 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 8 regarding passing from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via reshut ha-rabbim:

(ב) And the Sages forbade etc. — for it is somewhat similar to reshut ha-rabbim which has no partitions; and nevertheless it is not reshut ha-rabbim for it is not made for public passage; and therefore the Sages decreed on it not to remove and insert from it whether to reshut ha-rabbim or to reshut ha-yachid lest they come to permit even from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim:

(ג) Permitted to remove and insert — and likewise to pass and throw:

(ד) But one standing — meaning he was standing and walked and removed or inserted, or he remained standing but passed or threw:

(ה) Liable — for since he did uprooting in reshut ha-yachid and placing in reshut ha-rabbim or the reverse we do not care that he transferred via makom patur in between; and specifically if he did not stand in makom patur — but if he stood there a little and then returned and removed to the other domain he is exempt since he did placing in makom patur; and nevertheless there is d'rabbanan prohibition even in this for they decreed lest he come to be lenient and remove directly from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim; and likewise forbidden to remove for this reason from reshut ha-yachid to makom patur via reshut ha-rabbim even if he will be careful not to stand in reshut ha-rabbim:

(ו) And likewise one should not stand etc. — this "and likewise" is not precise for there is no liability here [for since he himself stands and rests in makom patur, once the object reaches opposite him it is as if he placed it there a little]; and he mentioned "and likewise" because of prohibition — for in any case through him removing from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim occurs and he may come to be lenient to remove directly from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim:

(ז) And take an object from the hand etc. — and likewise to take from the ground of reshut ha-yachid and give to the ground of reshut ha-rabbim:

(ח) And to exchange etc. — meaning one standing on makom patur takes from the hand of one standing in reshut ha-yachid and gives to the hand of one standing in karmelit or the reverse; and likewise if he takes an object from reshut ha-yachid and places it in makom patur and returns and takes from makom patur and places it in karmelit:
