1) [Se'if 1] Under Torah law, one is liable only when transferring, etc. This means when one carries an object on foot from a private domain to a public domain, or brings it from a public domain into a private domain, or when one stands in a private domain and throws it or extends it into a public domain, or the reverse. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1. See below, subsection 18.
2) There. The Sages prohibited transferring from a karmelis to a private domain, etc. This was a decree on account of transferring from a private domain to a public domain. Tur. Taz, subsection 1.
3) There. The Sages prohibited transferring from a karmelis to a private domain, etc. Under Torah law, a karmelis is an exempt area, but our Sages imposed upon it both the stringency of a public domain and the stringency of a private domain. Regarding a karmelis by Rabbinic law, an enclosure and a mound larger than beis sasayim are complete private domains under Torah law, but by Rabbinic law they are deemed a karmelis. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1. See below, se'if 3.
