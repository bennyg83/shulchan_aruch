1) [Se'if 1] Under Torah law, one is liable only when transferring, etc. This means when one carries an object on foot from a private domain to a public domain, or brings it from a public domain into a private domain, or when one stands in a private domain and throws it or extends it into a public domain, or the reverse. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1. See below, subsection 18.
