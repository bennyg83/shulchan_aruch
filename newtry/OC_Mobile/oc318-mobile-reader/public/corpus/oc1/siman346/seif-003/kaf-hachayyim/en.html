The Sages prohibited transferring from a karmelis to a private domain, etc. Under Torah law, a karmelis is an exempt area, but our Sages imposed upon it both the stringency of a public domain and the stringency of a private domain. Regarding a karmelis by Rabbinic law, an enclosure and a mound larger than beis sasayim are complete private domains under Torah law, but by Rabbinic law they are deemed a karmelis.
