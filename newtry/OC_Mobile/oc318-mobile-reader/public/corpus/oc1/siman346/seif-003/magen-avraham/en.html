(א) Karpef. From the Torah it is a complete reshut ha-yachid:

(ב) Two partitions. And in the third we say "the edge of the ceiling descends and closes:"

(ג) Two partitions. Requires study — behold in siman 361 he ruled that attached to each other are required; and he answered that the door is considered a third partition — if so one partition from the side suffices; requires study; and it is possible Rama's view is to be stringent l'chatchila — see siman 364 seif 2; and Maharil siman 115 wrote it is good to be stringent as Rabbeinu Chananel wrote — to make lechayayim via plaster on the fourth side and there will be tzurat ha-petach — end of his words; and if the projection has posts one should make from the side adjacent to the house also a pole from this and a pole from that and there will be tzurat ha-petach from all three sides; siman 362 seif 10; and if many houses stand one after another — this correction suffices in two houses standing at the ends and they join an eruv together:

(ד) And a lintel four wide. And in the opening of an alley even if it is not entirely roofed but only the outer half is roofed we say "the edge of the ceiling descends and closes" since there is a space of four from the edge of the beam to the door — the alley is upon it and combines with the alley and the beam permits it; and if the opening is open even if there is no space of four it is considered inside; but in a house there is no distinction between open and locked opening, for a beam does not permit the house unless four wide; and if two beams are adjacent less than three tefachim they combine to four — see Gemara and the Rosh; and if the door is between them when open — inside; when locked — outside:
