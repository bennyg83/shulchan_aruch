(א) More than beit se'asayim — and the measure of beit se'asayim is explained below siman 358 — it is seventy amot and four tefachim by seventy amot and four tefachim in square:

(ב) That was not enclosed for dwelling — such as his intent was to plant or sow and the like as explained below siman 358:

(ג) The Sages forbade etc. — since it is so large it may be confused with reshut ha-rabbim and the Sages made it like karmelit:

(ד) And permitted etc. to another karmelit — even though from the Torah it is reshut ha-yachid for all matters since it has partitions and one who throws from within it to reshut ha-rabbim is liable and from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit is forbidden to remove as above — nevertheless here they permitted lest if this were forbidden people would think it is a complete reshut ha-yachid and there is no karmelit upon it at all and come to carry throughout it like other reshut ha-yachid; and from this others would come to carry also in reshut ha-rabbim because this somewhat resembles reshut ha-rabbim since it is large and was not enclosed for dwelling; therefore the Sages gave it the law of other karmelit for this matter that it is permitted to remove from it to another karmelit such as a valley and the like:

(ה) And therefore it is permitted to take etc. — for even though the garden is reshut ha-yachid from the Torah enclosed by partitions, since d'rabbanan they made it like karmelit they did not forbid carrying from it to the valley before it; and the reason as above:

(ו) To open etc. — meaning even to bring in the garden door with the key inward:

(ז) That there not be between them etc. — meaning at the garden opening there should not be a threshold (called schwell) high such that reshut ha-yachid is upon it — then forbidden to insert the key or remove for he carries from karmelit to reshut ha-yachid and from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit:

(ח) That it is ten high — for otherwise the threshold itself is karmelit and like a valley; and if it were not four wide it is makom patur and certainly permitted to stand on it to open and close:

(ט) Two partitions etc. — and the third is made via the lintel for we say "the edge of the ceiling descends and closes:"

(י) That they are four wide — for with less than this even four complete partitions are not reshut ha-yachid as above siman 345 seif 9:

(כ) That it is not ten high — meaning the threshold; and likewise when it is not three high or there is no threshold at all — reshut ha-yachid is made via the partitions:

(ל) From the threshold to reshut ha-yachid — this does not refer to above where the threshold stands before the garden but to the house threshold standing under the lintel; and he informs us that even if the lintel upon it is four tefachim wide where reshut ha-yachid is upon it as above — nevertheless one should be stringent not to insert from that place to the house or the reverse lest sometimes the lintel not be four tefachim wide and he will also come to do so:

(מ) For we are concerned lest not etc. — and likewise forbidden to remove from threshold to reshut ha-rabbim even if it is not four wide lest it happen the lintel is four tefachim and then it is complete reshut ha-yachid (there in Mordechai):

(נ) The law of that which is before it — if it is reshut ha-rabbim this place is nullified relative to it and also becomes reshut ha-rabbim; and if karmelit — karmelit:

(ס) And even if three high — meaning and if so that place is makom patur — presumably since the lintel over the threshold is not four tefachim wide, also the doorposts from the sides and the threshold itself are not four tefachim wide and automatically it is makom patur; and from makom patur we hold it is permitted to carry to reshut ha-yachid and reshut ha-rabbim — nevertheless since all our domains are karmelit according to several poskim and this threshold is adjacent to it we say "a kind found its kin" etc.; and there is no distinction in all this whether the house opening is open or locked — always forbidden to insert from the threshold inward [Magen Avraham]; and behold according to what is explained in this gloss one must be careful in those places where carrying is forbidden because there is no eruv and the synagogue door faces the street with no room before the door and the gentile brings the key — the beadle should not take the key from his hand until he is together with the gentile under the lintel over the door lest there be four tefachim in the ceiling and it be reshut ha-yachid and he carries from karmelit before the synagogue into reshut ha-yachid; therefore he should take the key from the gentile under the lintel and then open the lock and not open the door until he removes the key and then opens — lest there not be four in the ceiling outside the door, in which case it is karmelit and he inserts from karmelit to reshut ha-yachid; therefore first remove the key and give it to the gentile or hide it at the bottom of the threshold or above before the door swings [and it is fitting for the congregation to make before the door some room that is reshut ha-yachid and the gentile brings the key there — otherwise it is hard to restrict standing together in such a short space]; and likewise one must be careful not to carry from the house outward when open to karmelit only to the extent the door covers there and no more — lest there not be four in the outer portion of the threshold outside the door and it be karmelit and he carries from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit (Taz):

(ע) And likewise if the ceiling — like what is common in roofs before shops where they extend beams outward and cover them with boards:

(פ) Its law is like the domain before it — and perforce forbidden to insert from it inward since it has no partitions; and regarding removing outward — if karmelit were at its side it is permitted; and if reshut ha-rabbim were at its side forbidden to remove for since it is roofed it has the law of karmelit as above siman 345 seif 14:

(צ) And likewise our roofs etc. — Taz wrote here is explained the doubt many people are uncertain about regarding a house that has before it toward the street a roof projecting to the street called in foreign tongue "perliban" — what is their law whether one may carry there from the house; and many err to say the projecting ceiling to the street descends and closes and it is as if built down to the ground; and from here it implies explicitly the area under the roof is like the domain before them — see there; and so all the later authorities; and what is their correction — if the roof projection has pillars toward the street one should place aligned opposite them also poles toward the houses and the roof upon them will have the law of tzurat ha-petach and behold there is tzurat ha-petach from all three sides; and if they are of many houses standing one adjacent to another one need not make for each house but make this correction in two houses standing at the end from this side and from that side — but they need to join eruv chatzerot; and then permitted to insert and remove from the houses under the roofs and likewise the reverse [later authorities]; and in all cases forbidden to remove and insert from reshut ha-rabbim under the roofs and likewise the reverse [Chayei Adam]; and this is when the projection width was four tefachim — then there is upon the area under it karmelit; and see Chayei Adam general rule 53 seif 14 what he further wrote regarding gangways adjacent to inn houses and the roof rests on the pillars:
