(א) That was not enclosed for dwelling. From the Torah it is a complete reshut ha-yachid for all matters; they only permitted carrying from it to karmelit lest if this were forbidden people would say it is a complete reshut ha-yachid and come to carry throughout it; and from this others would come to carry in reshut ha-rabbim because it resembles reshut ha-rabbim; and even though now too he violates d'rabbanan in carrying from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit — nevertheless they did not forbid this for it is not common and the Sages did not decree on it:

(ב) And therefore it is permitted to take etc. For even though the garden is reshut ha-yachid from the Torah they did not forbid carrying from it to karmelit — and this is exactly the law mentioned above:

(ג) Then it is reshut ha-yachid. Meaning it is forbidden to insert there from the karmelit before it; and therefore it would have been fitting to permit inserting from this threshold to reshut ha-yachid — nevertheless it is forbidden lest it not be four wide and it would be karmelit; even if it is not four wide, since karmelit is before it — "a kind found its kin" etc.; and what is written "like reshut ha-yachid" — this "like" is a scribe's error:

(ד) However it is forbidden to insert etc. In Hagahot Mordechai first chapter of Shabbat it is written: our threshold — what is from the door inward when locked is complete reshut ha-rabbim; and what is outside the door — forbidden to insert from atop it inward, for there is no precision now in roofing since we have no roofing four wide; and one who is precise lest he transgress d'oraisa — and also forbidden to remove from atop it outward lest the ceiling be four and forbidden to remove from the Torah outward — end of quote; and Beit Yosef in the name of Kolbo wrote a person cannot stand in reshut ha-rabbim or karmelit and lock a house and insert the key inward under the door or above from the sides because even a little is forbidden to insert and remove from domain to domain — end of quote; and Mordechai wrote first chapter of Shabbat that when a minor brings a key with him it is permitted to take the key and open since the lintel does not project four in reshut ha-rabbim it is like reshut ha-rabbim; but after the lock is opened he must remove the key before opening the door itself, for what is from the door inward is complete reshut ha-yachid — end of quote; I copied all this because I saw many stumble in those places where carrying is forbidden because there is no eruv and the synagogue door faces the street with no room before the door and the gentile brings the key — one must be careful the beadle not take the key from his hand until he is with the gentile under the lintel over the door lest there be four in the ceiling and it be reshut ha-yachid and he carries from karmelit before the synagogue into reshut ha-yachid; therefore take the key from him under the lintel, then open the lock, and not open the door until he removes the key and then opens the door — lest there not be four in the ceiling outside the door, in which case it is karmelit and he inserts from karmelit to reshut ha-yachid; therefore first remove the key and give it to the gentile or hide it in the door at its bottom or side; and a house whose door opens to karmelit must be careful not to carry from the house outward except to the extent the door covers there and no more — lest there not be four in the outer portion and it be karmelit and he carries from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit; and it is fitting for that congregation to make before the door some room that is reshut ha-yachid and the gentile brings the key there and then there is no concern; and it appears from Mordechai there is permission to bring via a minor the key as via a gentile; and even though chapter Cheresh says the keys were lost and R' Pedat said speak to a minor to go there and he will find it — implying forbidden to do via having the minor bring there as we say there "do not add by hand" forbidden — there it deals with reshut ha-rabbim which is d'oraisa; but we have only karmelit which is d'rabbanan — as appears to me:

(ה) And likewise our gardens projecting etc. Here is explained the doubt many are uncertain about regarding a house that has before it toward the street a roof projecting to the street built underneath with boards called in foreign tongue "perliban" — what is their law whether one may carry there from the house; and many err to say "the edge of the ceiling" for that side projecting to the street descends and closes and it is as if built down to the ground; and from here it implies explicitly the area under the roof is like the domain before them; and so in Beit Yosef in the name of Shiltei Gibborim in the responsum he copied — and if there are beams or pillars standing toward the houses open to reshut ha-rabbim under that roofing over the house openings — behold these do not raise or lower; and when we say "remnants the inn settled" etc.; but from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim even via a portico is forbidden — all the more if between the beams and pillars opposite the house openings more than three tefachim etc.; and that which he mentioned from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim — likewise for karmelit for he does not distinguish except regarding making karmelit thereby — see there; and he further wrote there: exert yourself — if reshut ha-rabbim were roofed is there any benefit at all? A ceiling has no benefit for Shabbat prohibition purposes — one who removes from the house key outward etc.; behold it is explained "the edge of the ceiling descends and closes" does not help here; and it is a simple matter — for even under the ceiling of the threshold as above we do not say so — all the more before the door; and the reason we do not say here "the edge of the ceiling descends and closes" since there are no partitions from the sides their law is like the domain before them as Rama wrote here; and see siman 365 what is written at its end:
