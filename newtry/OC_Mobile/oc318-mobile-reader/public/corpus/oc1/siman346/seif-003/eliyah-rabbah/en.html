[3] [Levush] They permitted to remove etc. Beit Yosef raised thus because the Maggid wrote in the name of Rashba that this is his leaning view — end of quote; and behold Knesset HaGedolah wrote our rabbi was not precise — the Maggid did not write Rashba leans but according to Rambam who forbids; and let us further wonder on Beit Yosef — the Maggid did not mention Rabbeinu Tam's view at all — end of its language; and a wonderful wonder on him — behold the Maggid explicitly wrote chapter 16 law 2 — this is its language: Rambam forbids on dry land but Tosafos permit and they permitted from here to a garden etc.; and Rashba wrote that to this his view leans — end of its language; behold he mentioned Tosafos' view who permit; also his intent is clear that Rashba leans to the permissive view; I again examined Maimoni with glosses — there it does not write "but Tosafos permit;" and if so one must say he did not examine the version of Maimoni with Kesef Mishnah that this text was before Beit Yosef; and I also say it is clear and simple there is a scribe's error in Maimoni with glosses and those words "but Tosafos" etc. are missing — for if not, how did he conclude on Rambam "and they permitted from here to a garden" etc. — behold for Rambam it is forbidden on dry land and this permission is only for Tosafos; rather certainly those words are missing in the printing and one must say as printed in Kesef Mishnah; and I also found clear proof — Rashba in Sefer Avodat HaKodesh page 17b wrote explicitly that his view leans to the permissive view; and from this the wonder on Knesset HaGedolah is greater; I also saw in responsum of Mahari Beit chapter 3 siman 48 he stumbled on this — he wrote Rashba leans to Rambam and forbids — but it is not so; rather he left me room:
