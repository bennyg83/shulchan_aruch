(א) Karpef. From the Torah it is a complete reshut ha-yachid; they only permitted carrying from it to karmelit lest if it were forbidden people would say it is a complete reshut ha-yachid and come to carry throughout it, and from this others would come to carry in reshut ha-rabbim because it resembles reshut ha-rabbim. Taz — see there:

(ב) And therefore it is permitted. Meaning that even though the garden is reshut ha-yachid from the Torah they did not forbid carrying from it to karmelit — and this is exactly the law mentioned above:

(ג) Two partitions. And in the third we say "the edge of the ceiling descends and closes." Magen Avraham:

(ד) Reshut ha-yachid. Meaning it is forbidden to insert there from the karmelit before it; and therefore it would have been fitting to permit inserting from this threshold to reshut ha-yachid — nevertheless it is forbidden lest it not be four wide and it would be karmelit; even if it is not four wide, since karmelit is before it — "a kind found its kin" etc.:

(ה) Before it. Taz wrote: I have seen many stumble in those places where carrying is forbidden because there is no eruv, and the synagogue door faces the street with no room before the door, and the gentile brings the key — one must be careful that the beadle not take the key from his hand until he is with the gentile under the lintel over the door, lest there be four tefachim in the ceiling and it be reshut ha-yachid and he carries from the karmelit before the synagogue into reshut ha-yachid; therefore he should take the key from him under the lintel, then open the lock, and not open the door until he removes the key and then opens the door — lest there not be four in the ceiling outside the door, in which case it is karmelit and he inserts from karmelit to reshut ha-yachid; therefore he should first remove the key and give it to the gentile or hide it in the door at its bottom or at its side. And a house whose door opens to karmelit must be careful not to carry from the house outward except to the extent the door covers there and no more — lest there not be four in the outer portion and it be karmelit and he carries from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit; and it is fitting for that congregation to make before the door some room that is reshut ha-yachid and the gentile brings the key there and then there is no concern. And it appears from Mordechai that there is permission to bring via a minor the key as via a gentile, since we have no reshut ha-rabbim but karmelit which is d'rabbanan — end of his words:

(ו) And likewise our roofs. Here is explained the doubt that many are uncertain about regarding a house that has before it toward the street a roof projecting to the street built underneath with boards called in foreign tongue "perliban" — what is their law, whether one may carry there from the house; and many err to say "the edge of the ceiling" for that side projecting to the street descends and closes and it is as if built down to the ground; and from here it implies explicitly that the area under the roof is like the domain before them. Taz; and see Taz siman 365 s.k. 6 where he wrote in the name of Maharil that if the projection before the house has three partitions and a fourth of "the edge of the ceiling" four wide — it is good to be stringent even in this — see there; and one must say it does not help for perliban unless the standing exceeds the breach as written siman 362:

(ז) Like the domain. The custom of women standing under the lintel of the door opening to hide the key under the door even when the door is locked — the lintel is considered outside even though they do not instruct thus; nevertheless one need not protest — Eshel Avraham, Orach Chayim siman 112:
