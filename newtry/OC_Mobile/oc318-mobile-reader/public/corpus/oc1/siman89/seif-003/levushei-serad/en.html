(Ibid.) “Dawn is valid, and it is not comparable.” Meaning, there it is said that the time for Minchah begins immediately at midday, but the Sages postponed it by half an hour lest one err, and it is stated there that even after the fact one has not fulfilled his obligation. We might therefore say that the ruling here means that before the eastern horizon became illuminated one has not fulfilled his obligation even after the fact, because of the concern that one may err.
