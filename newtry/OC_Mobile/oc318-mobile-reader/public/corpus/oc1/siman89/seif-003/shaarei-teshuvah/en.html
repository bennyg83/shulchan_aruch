Today — Av Beit Din. And see above, siman 1, in the name of Shevut Yaakov and Shelomei Tzibbur, that the calculation of seasonal hours is per midday, when the sun stands in the middle of the sky; after twelve hours it is midnight. And accordingly there would be room to say that after counting twelve hours from midday it is the beginning of the day even for Kriat Shema and prayer. But in truth it is not so; for the time of Kriat Shema and prayer we follow the actual beginning of day and the actual beginning of night.
