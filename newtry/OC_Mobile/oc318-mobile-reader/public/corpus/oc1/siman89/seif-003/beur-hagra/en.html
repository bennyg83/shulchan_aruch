And it is good to be stringent, etc. For regarding greeting with shalom, it certainly speaks of Shemoneh Esrei, as written there: they challenged, etc., 'between the sections,' etc.; and the same applies to this, for they are taught together. Terumas HaDeshen.
But, etc., and likewise foods, etc. For in this case arrogance does not apply. Therefore it said, 'Do not eat over your blood,' etc., and it did not state it in its plain sense, to teach that where arrogance does not apply it is permitted. See Beis Yosef.
And between, etc. It is not forbidden because of being before kiddush, even though even water is forbidden then, as written in siman 271, since the time of kiddush has not arrived. Tur in the name of the Rosh.
