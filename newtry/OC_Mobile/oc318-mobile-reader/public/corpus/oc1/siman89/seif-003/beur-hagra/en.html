Section 3, forbidden, etc. Berachos 5b, and per Tosafos there s.v. ella, etc., and all the more so per Rashi.

Or [to travel], etc. Berachos 14a, and per the reading of Rif and Rosh and Shulchan Aruch poskim to set out on a journey; see Remazim chapter 1 seif 7 and chapter 2 seif 6.

And it is good to be stringent, etc., for regarding asking shalom it is certainly in the Amidah of eighteen that it is discussed, as was explained "he challenges," etc., in the chapters, etc.; and the same applies here, for they are taught adjacently — Terumat HaDeshen.

But [water is permitted], etc., and likewise they eat, etc. — for in this pride is not relevant, and therefore Orach Tzedek said "your body," etc., and there is no question according to its plain meaning to teach that in a place where pride is not relevant it is permitted; and appendix BY.

And between [weekday and Shabbat], etc. — and it is not forbidden because of [eating] before kiddush, for even water is forbidden, as was written in siman 271, since the time of kiddush has not arrived. Tur in the name of the Rosh.

And likewise, etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham, as above.
