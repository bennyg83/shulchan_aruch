Or to go on the road. Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari wrote that this is specifically per the view of the Rosh that the Tur brings at the end of the siman, that prayer in the house is preferable; and Beit Yosef made a distinction that here the case is where he has permission in his hand to delay but wants to hurry, etc. And it appears that all this is unnecessary, for here the case is even if he will go on a short road such that the day is still great when he arrives there and he will be able to pray standing — nevertheless it is forbidden from the side of doing personal affairs before prayer.
