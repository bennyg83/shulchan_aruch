And not to drink. See what Baer Heitev cites in the name of R' Chaim Vital. This question was addressed in Responsa Shevus Yaakov, siman 8: May one eat or drink before daylight in order to settle his mind? He answers that a weak person may eat and drink something to strengthen himself for service of the Creator. The siddurim of R' Yitzchak Luria explicitly state that this was R' Chaim Vital's practice. During the half hour before dawn, however, it is forbidden, as the poskim state, because the time for prayer has then arrived. He concludes that all the Zohar's stringent words concern one who eats ravenously for excessive pleasure and arrogantly; the Zohar compares such a person to a diviner and sorcerer. They do not concern one who eats for medical purposes or to keep his body strong and healthy so he can pray with concentration.
