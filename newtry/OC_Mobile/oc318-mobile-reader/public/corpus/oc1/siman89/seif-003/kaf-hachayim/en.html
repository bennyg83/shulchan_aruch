(3) There — if he prayed from when the time of dawn arrived, etc. Peri Chadash wrote in note 1, and these are his words: the author was silent here and did not explain; and the main point is that there are three times for the beginning of the time of prayer — and behold, the main fulfillment of its mitzvah is from sunrise, as the early pious ones, for it is written "they shall fear You with the sun"; and for the outset one may pray from when the column of dawn rose and the face of the east brightened, as was the practice; nevertheless if he prayed from when the column of dawn rose he has fulfilled, as we learned in Megillah — and all who acted from when the column of dawn rose have fulfilled; and also the tamid — if they slaughtered from when the column of dawn rose it is not invalidated; but for the outset, all matters whose mitzvah is by day — their main time is from sunrise; except that in the tamid, since all sacrifices must be offered before the afternoon tamid, they advanced the time slightly before sunrise; but if one acted from alos hashachar, it is obvious he has fulfilled — and not as implied from the words of Rabbeinu Yeruham that the Beit Yosef brought and from the words of the author — and this is obvious — end quote. And the Taz, note 1, wrote that the time of dawn-rise is the time before one can see his friend at a distance of four amot; but from the time one can see his friend one may pray for the outset — only the mitzvah from the preferred way is that it is better to pray immediately after sunrise — end quote. And similarly Maamar, note 1, in the name of Rabbeinu Yeruham, and wrote: according to this understanding of Rabbeinu Yeruham's words one should not object so much to those who advance on the days of Selichos, for they do not advance so much as to pray before the time one can recognize his friend — end quote. It follows that for all opinions the mitzvah from the preferred way requires sunrise; and after the fact, if he prayed from alos hashachar even if the face of the east did not brighten he has fulfilled — and so too Magen Avraham, note 3; and so too Eliyah Rabbah, note 1, in the name of the Ramban in Milchamos, first chapter of Berachos, and wrote: and not like Eliyah Rabbah; and Matteh Yehuda, note 1, who wrote that even after the fact he does not fulfill unless the face of the east brightened — see there; and so too is implied from the words of the poskim that from alos hashachar he fulfills. And from when the face of the east brightened — Peri Chadash holds one may pray for the outset; but Maran holds even this is only after the fact, as explained in Shulchan Aruch; and the Taz holds from when one sees his friend at a distance of four amot one may pray for the outset — and so too Rabbeinu Yeruham, as Maamar wrote; but we have not seen Maran divide on this — see above in Beit Yosef. However see what I wrote in siman 25, note 27, that per the secret of the matter one must don tzitzit and tefillin before Keriat Shema of the sacrifices section, and at least before the portion of the tamid — see there; and the time of laying tefillin is from when one sees his friend, as explained in siman 30, seif 1, and in our words at length on that topic. If so, when one waits until the time of laying tefillin and dons tzitzit and tefillin and begins to pray from the Binding of Isaac section onward, with calmness and settled mind, word by word as fitting so that no letter be missing — and in particular the intent of the Divine Names — his Shacharit prayer will be close to sunrise, which is the emergence of the body of the sun upon the earth, as explained in our words above in siman 58, note 10; and fortunate is one who serves his Master properly and is not particular about a short time for prayer, for they are matters that stand at the height of the world.
