Or to go on the road. It seems to me that if the caravan is not waiting for him, it is permitted to go on the road—as in the end of this section and siman 90 seif 17.

But water is permitted, for pride is not relevant to them.

For healing it is permitted—it implies even eating and drinking something to which pride applies, since he is not doing it out of pride but for healing; even though he is not sick, it is permitted, and so in the Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari'a.
