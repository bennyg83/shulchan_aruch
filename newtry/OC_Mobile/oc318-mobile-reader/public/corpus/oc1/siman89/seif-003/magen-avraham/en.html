Or to set out on the road. It appears to me that if the caravan will not wait for him, he may set out on the road; see the end of this siman and siman 90, seif 17.
But water is permitted. For arrogance does not apply to it.
For healing it is permitted. This implies that even if he eats and drinks something to which arrogance applies, since he does not do it out of arrogance but for healing, even though he is not sick, it is permitted. So it also appears from the Beis Yosef in the name of Mahari Abuhav.
