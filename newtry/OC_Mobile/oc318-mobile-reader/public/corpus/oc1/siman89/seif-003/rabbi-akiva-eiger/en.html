Magen Avraham, note 5: It begins after midnight. And the Tzelach wrote: therefore one may pray after midnight on the road on condition that if the time of Mincha has arrived, this will be for the sake of the Mincha prayer; and if not, it will be for the morning prayer. And after half an hour he prays again on condition that if the first was for Mincha, this is to complete Shacharit; and if the first was Shacharit, this will be for Mincha:
