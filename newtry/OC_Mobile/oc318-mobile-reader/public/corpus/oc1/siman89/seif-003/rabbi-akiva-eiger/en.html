Magen Avraham, seif katan 5. It begins after midday. Tzelach writes that, according to this, one may pray after midday with the following condition: If the time for Minchah has arrived, this prayer shall be the Minchah prayer; if not, it shall be the Shacharit prayer. After half an hour, he should pray a second time conditionally: If the first prayer was Minchah, this one shall be the makeup for Shacharit; and if the first was Shacharit, this one shall be Minchah.
Seif 3. It is forbidden for him. Rambam’s wording is: “It is forbidden for a person to taste anything or to perform work from dawn until he has prayed Shacharis.” See Taz, seif katan 3.
