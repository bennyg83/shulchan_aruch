Taz, seif katan 1. “And the Rosh here did not state a measure, but spoke without qualification.” Meaning, in truth one may pray throughout the day, namely until the time for Minchah, even if he intentionally delayed. After the time for Minchah, he may do so only if the omission was inadvertent. They did not distinguish here, regarding an intentional omission, between before the time for Minchah and after its time because there was no need. They had already stated, in connection with Rabbi Yochanan's ruling that one prays Minchah twice, that this applies specifically where the omission was inadvertent.
