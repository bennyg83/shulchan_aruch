(Taz siman alef.) And the Rosh here [gives] a measure but anonymously. Meaning: because in truth one may pray all day — that is, until mincha time, even intentionally; and after mincha time, unintentionally. And what they did not distinguish here between before mincha time and after its time regarding intentional [transgression] — they did not need this, for they already wrote in the words of R' Yochanan that one who prays two minchas [has fulfilled only] specifically when unintentional.
