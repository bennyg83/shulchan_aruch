(4) 'Which business,' etc. — and it is written in Lechem Chamudos page 12 that the Levush was clever to write 'to pass through the way' there, for it is forbidden for a person to engage himself before prayer; and in people who do a prohibition they did not wish to specify, up to here. But in Nachalas Tzvi he did not mention the words of Lechem Chamudos, and he wrote in his language that not for the need of sleep did the Levush change [the wording], for they were precise in their language to write 'which business,' to teach that specifically to engage in a prohibited business; up to here — and this is to divide between 'which business' and 'business.' And it appears more [correct] to divide that specifically to engage in a prohibited business [is forbidden], but only to see the business is permitted, for mere seeing has no prohibition; and it is possible to intend thus the view of Nachalas Tzvi if one examines his words — understand carefully. And what he wrote 'to pass through the way,' etc. — the explanation is: even when he rises early to his door, since it is because of business that he rises and not in order to greet his face, it is permitted to say 'a good morning to you, sir.'
