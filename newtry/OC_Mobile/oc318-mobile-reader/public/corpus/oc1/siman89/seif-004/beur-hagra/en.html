Section 4, [one who is] thirsty, etc. — the Rambam did not write "if he wishes" but "he shall not pray," etc., and as was said in chapter 6 of Eruvin, whoever's mind is not settled, etc., and in the end of chapter 4 of Berachos "one should always estimate," etc. — except that in siman 98 seif 2 the Shulchan Aruch wrote "and now," etc., and Bach wrote; nevertheless one who wishes to rely here on the Rambam has permission in his hand, and this is why [the text] says "if he wished."
