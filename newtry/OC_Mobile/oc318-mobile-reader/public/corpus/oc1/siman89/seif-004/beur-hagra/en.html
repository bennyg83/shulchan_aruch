Seif 4, one who is thirsty, etc. The Rambam did not write, 'if he wishes,' but rather, 'he should not pray,' etc., as written in chapter 6 of Eiruvin: whoever's mind is not settled, etc.; and at the end of the fourth chapter of Berachos: a person should always assess himself, etc. But in siman 98, seif 2, the Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote, 'and now,' etc., and the Beis Yosef wrote: nevertheless, one who wishes to rely here on the Rambam has permission. This is why he wrote, 'if he wishes.'
