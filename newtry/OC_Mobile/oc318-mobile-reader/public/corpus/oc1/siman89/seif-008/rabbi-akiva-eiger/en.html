Magen Avraham, seif katan 17. Keriat Shema while traveling. In my humble opinion, this is not compelling. It may be said that only when he prays first has the obligation to juxtapose redemption to prayer not yet taken full effect. But when he has not yet prayed, once he recites Keriat Shema he is obligated to juxtapose redemption to prayer. See Rashi's wording in the sugya, and examine it carefully.
