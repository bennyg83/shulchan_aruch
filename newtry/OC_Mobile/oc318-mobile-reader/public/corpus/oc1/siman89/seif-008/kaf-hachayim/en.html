(8) There — its time extends until the end of four hours, which is one-third of the day. If the day is long — eighteen hours — it is six hours of the day; when the day is nine hours long, it is three hours. Magen Avraham siman kaf dalet; Shaar HaTziyun section 4; R. Zalman section 1; Chayei Adam klal kaf zayin section 1 — and R. Zalman wrote there that we calculate these hours from alos hashachar until tzeis hakokhavim; and so too Tanya Achrav there.
