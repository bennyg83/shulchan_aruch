At a time of pressure — see in Magen Avraham sk 17, who wrote that if he is on the road and sees that the time of Kerias Shema will pass, he should recite Kerias Shema on the road with its three blessings, and afterward when he comes to the inn he should pray standing; however, it appears to me that he should recite it on the road without its blessings. And see in the chiddushim of R' Akiva Eiger, who also agreed that in this one should not interrupt between redemption and prayer after he already completed redemption earlier and is obligated to adjoin prayer to it; therefore he must follow the advice of Magen Avraham to recite without blessings. And if he wishes to travel from his house after its time arrived and the caravan does not wait for him only a short time, and there is not enough in this to recite Kerias Shema and pray — it appears in this that even for the Gra it is better that he pray in his house standing and recite Kerias Shema on the road with its blessings as established; however, one can reject this, for behold Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Pe'er Tehillah that one must be concerned in practice to forbid all the matters mentioned in seif 3 even before Kerias Shema and the evening prayer — behold all the more so before he fulfills the morning Kerias Shema there is a prohibition to travel on the road; and if so, when he is compelled to pass and travel before he fulfills the mitzvos of Kerias Shema and prayer because the caravan does not wait for him, it is better for everyone that prayer, which is rabbinic, be deferred then and not Kerias Shema, which is from the Torah; and that [case] of the Shulchan Aruch is different, for the time of Kerias Shema had not yet arrived; and even though in truth the mitzvah is not deferred in this but only delayed by an hour, there is also an advantage in that it is from the Torah, as is proven in Sukkah 38a, and it requires study. And it is possible that in this too he should recite Kerias Shema alone without blessings and on the road pray everything in order, and it requires study. However, according to what we practice today as the poskim who hold that adjoining redemption to prayer is preferable — certainly one should not precede prayer to Kerias Shema.
