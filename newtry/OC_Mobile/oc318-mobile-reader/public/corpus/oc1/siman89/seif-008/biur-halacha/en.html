In pressing circumstances. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 17, who writes that if one is traveling and sees that the time for Krias Shema will pass, he should recite Krias Shema on the road together with its three blessings, and afterward, upon reaching an inn, pray standing. It nevertheless appears to me that he should recite Krias Shema on the road without its blessings. Rabbi Akiva Eiger likewise agrees that one should not interrupt between redemption and prayer after completing the blessing of redemption, since he is obligated to join prayer to it. He must therefore follow Magen Avraham's proposal and recite Krias Shema without its blessings. If one wishes to leave his home after the time has arrived, and the caravan will wait only briefly, without enough time for him both to recite Krias Shema and to pray, then seemingly even according to Rabbi Akiva Eiger it is preferable for him to pray standing at home and recite Krias Shema on the road with its blessings in their proper form. This may be rejected, however, because Eliyah Rabbah writes in the name of Piskei Tosafos that in practice one should be stringent and prohibit all the activities mentioned in seif 3 even before the evening Krias Shema and prayer. Certainly, then, traveling before fulfilling the morning Krias Shema is forbidden. Consequently, where he is compelled to leave before fulfilling Krias Shema and prayer because the caravan will not wait, all would agree that it is preferable to defer prayer, which is Rabbinic, rather than Krias Shema, which is a Torah obligation. The ruling of the Shulchan Aruch is different, because the time for Krias Shema had not yet arrived. Although in truth the mitzvah is not displaced but only delayed briefly, its Torah status nevertheless gives it priority, as is evident from Sukkah 38a. This requires study. Perhaps even in this case he should recite Krias Shema alone, without its blessings, and on the road pray the entire order. This too requires study. In any event, according to our present practice, which follows the authorities who maintain that joining redemption to prayer takes precedence, one certainly should not place prayer before Krias Shema.
