Taz, seif katan 3. “That he has the option.” There, the case concerns a caravan that is departing and is unwilling to wait until the time for prayer. Therefore, one opinion there permits him to pray while traveling. Here, however, he has the option of remaining, because the caravan is willing to wait, but he wishes to hasten his journey. This is forbidden, because he is attending to his own affairs before prayer. So writes Beit Yosef.
