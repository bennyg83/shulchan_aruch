(Taz siman gimel.) For the permission there deals with when the caravan is traveling and does not wish to delay until prayer time; therefore there is an authority who says there that one may pray on the road. But here we deal with when the permission is in his hand to delay — the caravan wishes to wait, but he wants to hurry his journey — it is forbidden, for he is doing his personal needs before prayer. So too Beit Yosef.
