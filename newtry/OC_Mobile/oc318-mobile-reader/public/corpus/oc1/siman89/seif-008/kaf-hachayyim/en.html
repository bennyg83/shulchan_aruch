8) There: Its time extends through the end of four hours, which is one-third of the day. If the day is eighteen hours long, this is six ordinary hours into the day; if the day is nine hours long, this is three ordinary hours. Magen Avraham, paragraph 4; Soles Belulah, paragraph 4; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1; Chayei Adam, rule 27, paragraph 1. Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes there that these seasonal hours are calculated from alos hashachar until the emergence of the stars. Tiferes Adam writes likewise there.
