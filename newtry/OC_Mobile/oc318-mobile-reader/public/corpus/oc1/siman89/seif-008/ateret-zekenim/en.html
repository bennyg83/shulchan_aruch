In a time of pressure one may pray from alos hashachar, etc., for so it is preferable that he pray at home standing and with intention rather than pray at its proper time while walking and adjoin redemption to prayer. However there is no difference whether he walks on his feet or sits in a wagon, even on a ship, because even if he sits in a wagon or on a ship he cannot have proper intention in his prayer — unlike Kerias Shema, in which one can easily stand on the road for the recitation of the first verse and in Shema and its blessings, which is a short time.
