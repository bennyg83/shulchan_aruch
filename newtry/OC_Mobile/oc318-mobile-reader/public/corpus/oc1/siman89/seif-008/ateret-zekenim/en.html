In a pressing circumstance, one may pray from dawn, etc. It is preferable for him to pray at home while standing and with concentration rather than pray at the proper time while traveling, even though he will then be able to join redemption to prayer. In this respect, there is no distinction between walking on foot and sitting in a wagon or even in a boat, because even while sitting in a wagon or boat he cannot concentrate properly on his prayer. Keriat Shema is different: he can easily stop along the road to recite the first verse and “Blessed is the name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever,” since this takes only a short time.
