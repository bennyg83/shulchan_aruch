In a pressing circumstance, etc. This requires study, for most people are not careful about this. Perhaps they follow the other poskim who rule like the Rashba, that joining redemption to prayer is preferable. Also, Rashi explained that prayer while standing is preferable so that he can concentrate; and nowadays, in any event, we do not concentrate so well, so it is better to join redemption to prayer. From Rashi's words it implies that if he can pray sitting on the road, it is better to join redemption to prayer than to pray in his house while standing, for sitting also allows him to concentrate. So too it appears in the Rosh, according to one answer. However, it is stated in the Gemara that one who sits in a wagon or in a ship that is moving is considered like one who is walking for this matter. It is obvious that if he can recognize his friend at a distance of four cubits, he should also read Krias Shema in his house. It is stated in the Gemara that Rav Ashi, on the Shabbos of the festival lecture, would expound immediately at daybreak and pray during the lecture while sitting, while the people would slip away one by one and pray; he would not delay the lecture in order to pray before the lecture. However, it appears to me that this was specifically in their days, when all their deeds were for the sake of Heaven and they would teach and rebuke the people in the lecture. But nowadays one should not cancel praying while standing because of a lecture. Also, it is stated there that when he reached his house, he prayed again while standing.
This is more preferable. According to this view, if he is on the road and sees that the time for Krias Shema will pass, he should read Krias Shema on the road with its three blessings, and afterward, when he reaches the inn, he should pray while standing. However, it appears to me that he should read it on the road without its blessings; see the end of siman 46.
