In Magen Avraham, Maggid Mishneh 20 — note: Here is the rule for this calculation: if you reckon twelve hours from amud hashachar until tzeis hakochavim, and in them forty milin, for everyone it comes out to eighteen minutes per mil; but the Rambam calculates twelve hours from sunrise until sunset alone; and if you hold from amud hashachar until sunrise five milin, and likewise from sunset until tzeis hakochavim, it turns out that from sunrise until sunset there are only thirty milin, and in them twelve hours there will be twenty-four minutes per mil — and this is as Tosafos Yom Tov wrote in his name in chapter 3 of Pesachim. But the truth is that the Rambam holds, as is said, from amud hashachar until sunrise four milin, and likewise from sunset until tzeis hakochavim, and what remains from sunrise until sunset is thirty-two milin, and in them twelve hours it comes out to twenty-two and a half minutes per mil, and this is the main [view]. And so ruled Chek Yaakov siman 459. And what the Rambam wrote regarding prayer, from amud hashachar until sunrise is an hour and a fifth — it should be explained according to what Tosafos wrote in Pesachim 94a s.v. R' Yehuda, that although the beginning of sunset is four milin, nevertheless the last three-quarters of a mil, when the sun has already entered within the thickness of the sky, is called bein hashmoshes; and see also the language of Tosafos in Bava Metzia there; and if so, the same applies at amud hashachar — the first three-quarters of a mil, when the sun begins to emerge, are not fit for prayer at all; and three-quarters of a mil are close to eighteen minutes; when you remove this from four milin, an hour and a fifth remains in approximation. Also the Rambam in chapter 3 of Pesachim holds to say that there, in the Gemara Resh Lakish gave the amoraim the measure of chimutz like from the tower of Ninus to Tiberias, which is a mil; and behold the tower of Ninus and Tiberias — see in the Ran, and it is possible to estimate them; and now, if you would think that that measure is a small mil of twenty-two and a half minutes, then Ulla and Rava in Pesachim 94 would disagree with Resh Lakish, for per them a mil is twenty-four minutes — and for such a thing the Gemara should have said and they disagree with Resh Lakish; rather, perforce from Ninus to Tiberias is also twenty-four minutes, and Resh Lakish calls it a mil. See also the tanna in the beginning of and darkness in the chapter of one who was tamei. And now, although regarding a mil we do not rule like him, nevertheless regarding the measure of chimutz they do not disagree, for it is like from the tower of Ninus, which is twenty-four minutes, which is two-fifths of an hour — see there well and understand carefully. However, I have now found in an ancient book called Kaptor Vaferach, which the author of Tosafos Yom Tov cites well at the beginning of chapter 7 of Sheviis, in Beit Yosef Orach Chayyim at the end of siman 10, and in that book chapter 11 section 68 page 2 s.v. we shall return to Jerusalem, etc., he wrote from Jerusalem to Azazel is like three hours, etc. And behold the rabbi, may his memory be blessed, saw and knew and measured with great precision, and it is one hundred eighty minutes, and in them ten milin for R' Yehuda and R' Yosi in Yoma 67b — if so, perforce a mil is no more than eighteen minutes, and not like the Rambam; and experience and reason will not be denied; and perhaps one may say, a land of deer is written regarding it — so too must be said in what is written there siman 72, the width of the land of Israel is only, etc.; and likewise a land of deer is written regarding it.
