The laws of prayer and its time. It contains 8 seifim: The proper time for Shacharit is to begin with sunrise, as it is written, "They shall fear You with the sun." If one prayed from dawn, after the eastern horizon became illuminated, he has fulfilled his obligation. Its time extends until the end of 4 seasonal hours, which is one-third of the day. If one erred or transgressed and prayed after 4 hours but before midday, although he does not receive the reward for prayer in its proper time, he nevertheless receives the reward for prayer. {Rama: After midday it is forbidden to pray Shacharit. [Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba, chapter Tefillat HaShachar] See above at the beginning of siman 108.}
