Seif 1. The time for the morning prayer. The reason we do not pray with outspread hands, as we find many times in Scripture, in the holy Zohar, and in Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer, is that the nations now do so. This accords with what our Sages said concerning the verse, 'You shall not erect for yourself a pillar.' Be'er Sheva, siman 74.
