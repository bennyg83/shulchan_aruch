(1) 'And the east became light,' etc. — specifically when the entire east became light, and it does not suffice when the dawn sparkles at a point alone; so wrote Bach. But in Magen Avraham he wrote that even if the face of the east did not become light at all he fulfills, for from the law of the rise of dawn it is fit to offer the tamid; only we are concerned lest he come to err — therefore we require that the face of the east became light, as is found in Yoma chapter 3 [28b], up to here. And in my humble opinion, by law we require that the face of the east became light, and so it is implied in the Rambam at the beginning of Berachos; and because of error they go up to the roof to see, and so it is implied in the Gemara there — understand carefully. Also, not like Menachos page 3; and all the poskim who mentioned 'from the rise of amud hashachar' explained in the usual manner that the face of the east became light, which is alos hashachar; and further, because of concern lest he come to err, even b'dieved he does not fulfill, as was explained in siman 233; and even though Magen Avraham wrote that here there is no concern to err except when the light of the moon rises — it is missing to him the words of Bertinoro there, that even on Yom Kippur, when there is no reason because of the light of the moon, we are concerned that another error like it will occur; and in Rambam's commentary on the Mishnah he wrote that an error occurred on Yom Kippur. And study is required for me from the Gemara there page 29.
