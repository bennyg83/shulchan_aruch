Section 1, as it is written, etc. There (Berachos) 9b, 29b.

And if [one prayed from dawn], etc., as was explained there (Berachos) 30a; and Tosafos there s.v. Avuhah, etc., and like Kerias Shema, and likewise everything whose mitzvah is by day, as was written at the end of chapter 2 of Megillah, and its time is at the time of the tamid, as was written there; and we say in chapter 3 of Yoma and in the beginning of chapter 3 of Tamid, "go out and see," etc., and this is what [the text] says "and the east became light," etc.; and so too in the Rosh and Magen Avraham.

And it continues, etc. Berachos 27a, "R' Chanan said," etc.

That it [the time], etc., as was written in siman 58.

And if he erred, etc., the entire [topic] in Yoma, etc., and the same applies to Ri regarding the four hours — the Rif, Rosh, and Shulchan Aruch poskim — except that from the words of the Rif and Rosh [only] forty [minutes] specifically [after sunrise] if he erred, and so it is implied in the Gemara that from the law [that] if he erred and did not pray Shacharit, he may [still pray]; and there regarding intentional [violation] it is not as was written there; but the Rambam and Tur wrote that even if he transgressed he may pray until noon, and appendix BY pushed on this, and the Levush has already challenged him, and it requires study.

And after [four hours], etc., since it says "one who prays Mincha twice" — it is not asked why one who prays all day [may pray Shacharit], rather specifically with Mincha [in mind]; and so in all [cases]. And Magen Avraham — that the time begins from midday, as was written in the beginning of chapter 3 of Yoma 28b.
