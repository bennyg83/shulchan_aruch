Seif 1, as it is written, etc. There, 9b and 29b.
And if, etc. As written there, 30a; and see Tosafot there, s.v. Avuha, etc. This is like Krias Shema, and so too all mitzvos whose time is by day, as written at the end of the second chapter of Megillah. Its time is at the time of the tamid, as written there; and we say in the third chapter of Yoma and at the beginning of the third chapter of Tamid, 'Go out and see,' etc. This is what he wrote, 'and the eastern face lit up,' etc. So too in the Rosh; and see Magen Avraham.
And it continues, etc. 27a: Rav Kahana said, etc.
Which is, etc. As written in siman 58.
And if he erred, etc. There: the whole day, etc. The same applies according to Rabbi Yehudah from four hours, as in the Rif, the Rosh, and the other poskim. However, from the words of the Rif and the Rosh it appears that this is specifically where he erred; and so it appears from the Gemara, for it comes from the law of one who erred and did not pray Shacharis, and there, if he acted deliberately, it is not as written there. But the Rambam and the Tur wrote that even if he transgressed, he prays until noon. See Beis Yosef, who was forced on this point; the Lechem Mishneh has already challenged him, and it requires study.
And after, etc. Since it says that he prays Minchah twice, and it does not say that he prays the entire day, it is specifically together with Minchah; and so in all cases. See Magen Avraham, that the time begins from noon, as written at the beginning of the third chapter of Yoma, 28b.
