And if he prayed from alos hashachar, etc. — that is b'dieved in a time of pressure. The time of alos hashachar is the time before one can see his fellow at a distance of four amos; but from the time one can see his fellow, he may pray l'chatchila. Only the optimal mitzvah is that it is better to pray immediately after sunrise.

That is a third of the day (see above, siman 58). {Rama: And after chatzos it is forbidden to pray Shacharis until the time of Mincha, since it is not a time for prayer at all; he does not pray in any case. And if he prayed, it is a blessing in vain (so writes the Rashba). And there are those who say that one may pray even after chatzos until the time of Mincha gedolah arrives. And once the time of Mincha gedolah arrives, then he must pray Mincha first and afterward make up Shacharis, and both of them together one after the other (Ra'avad ch. 3 halacha 3). And even if he wants to pray Shacharis at the time of Mincha gedolah and afterward Mincha at the time of Mincha ketanah, he is not permitted (my teacher in Bach).}
