(Seif katan 1) Together with sunrise, etc. This accords with what the Tur writes in siman 235, that for this reason they were lenient to recite the evening Krias Shema before nightfall and to pray while it was still day. Since the congregation was already assembled for Minchah, requiring them to gather again for Maariv would involve excessive burden. See there.
Not to rise so early. Meaning, they should not rise so early for Selichos, so that when they finish Selichos it will be sunrise.
