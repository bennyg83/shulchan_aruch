(א) (siman kaf alef) with sunrise, etc. — and as the Tur wrote in siman 235, etc. — for this reason they were lenient to recite the evening kerias shema before tzeis hakokhavim and to pray Maariv by day, since the congregation is already assembled for mincha prayer; and if they would need to assemble again for Maariv, the burden would be great; see there.

(ב) Not to arise so early. Meaning: for selichos they should not agree [to begin early], so that when they finish selichos it will be sunrise.
