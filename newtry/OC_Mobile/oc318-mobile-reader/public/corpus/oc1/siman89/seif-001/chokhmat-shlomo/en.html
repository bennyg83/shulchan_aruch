Seif 1 — the time of prayer, etc. NB — Behold it is explained in the books that one should give charity before every prayer, as it is written, 'and I in righteousness will behold Your face'; and I will explain the reason more, with Hashem's help: behold I saw in Sefer Kav HaYashar from a certain kabbalist that tekhelet in gematria is titain, and therefore it is fitting to look with both eyes at the tzitzis, for 'eye' with 'eye' together with tzitzis adds up thus, and it is as if one fulfilled tekhelet thereby; and in my opinion it appears to correct this with charity, for [it appears it should say tekhelet] which is like the gematria titain — that is, one gives charity and considers it as if he fulfilled tekhelet; and therefore before putting on the tzitzis it is fitting to give charity; and therefore in Midrash on parashas Tetzaveh on the verse 'and it shall be righteousness for you before the Lord your Hashem' — charity ascends to the throne of glory; and this is since tekhelet resembles the sea, and the sea to the firmament, and the firmament to the throne of glory — if so, through tekhelet one is reminded of the glory of the Glory, and charity is in gematria tekhelet — if so, charity is in place of tekhelet; therefore it too ascends to the throne of glory; and therefore it says, 'and I in righteousness will behold Your face,' like with tekhelet — and it comes out well; understand carefully. May Hashem show us wonders in His Torah, amen.
