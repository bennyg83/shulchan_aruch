(2) There — its mitzvah is that one begin at sunrise, etc. I found written in the name of Rashal that on the days of Selichos one should be careful about this, for they are accustomed to advance before sunrise and this is not correct — end quote. And it is possible they relied on it being a burden on the congregation that they go to their homes and assemble again, as the Tur wrote in siman 235 regarding Maariv; nevertheless for the outset one should not advance so much — Magen Avraham, note 1. And they cited Eliyah Rabbah, note 2, and wrote on this that it is not comparable, for in such a short time it is possible to wait in the synagogue and not go to their homes, unlike Maariv — end quote. And Peri Megadim brought this in Eshel Avraham, note 1, and wrote that the same applies in winter — if they advance early they should wait; and see in the note after it.
