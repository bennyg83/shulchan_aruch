2) There: The proper time is to begin at sunrise, etc. I found written in the name of Maharshal that during the Selichos period one should take care concerning this, for people customarily pray before sunrise, which is improper. Perhaps they rely upon the burden to the congregation if they must go home and gather again, as Tur writes in siman 235 concerning Maariv. Nevertheless, initially one should try not to rise quite so early. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2, cites him and objects that the cases are not comparable: for such a brief interval they can wait in the synagogue rather than go home, unlike the case of Maariv. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1, cites this and writes that the same applies in winter: if they pray early, they should wait. See the following paragraph.
