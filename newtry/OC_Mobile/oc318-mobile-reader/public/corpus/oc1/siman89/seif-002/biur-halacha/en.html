Even this is permitted only when he needed, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, where I explained that this means he needed to go there, and so forth. Even in such a case, however, it is forbidden to greet his fellow with shalom. This appears to apply specifically here, because he is at least arising early to go to that person's doorway. If, however, he arises early to go to his fellow's doorway in order to inspect some business there and incidentally also greets him, Peri Megadim writes that it is permitted. Bach and the Gra, however, appear to prohibit it even in such a case.
