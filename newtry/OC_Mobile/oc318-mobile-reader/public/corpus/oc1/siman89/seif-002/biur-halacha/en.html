And even this is not permitted except when he needed [to go], etc. — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote — meaning that he needed to go, etc.; but to give him shalom, even in such a case it is forbidden; and it is implied that specifically in this case, since in any event he is rising early to his door for this; but if he is rising early to his friend's door to see some business there and incidentally he also gives him shalom — it is permitted, so Peri Megadim; but from the words of Bach and the Gra it is implied that it is forbidden even in such a case.
