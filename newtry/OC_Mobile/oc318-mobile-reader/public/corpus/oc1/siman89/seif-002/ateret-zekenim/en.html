Once the time for prayer has arrived, it is forbidden, etc., to give him peace, etc. And if he began the blessings afterward, one need not be so concerned, etc., and then he may even bow when he meets him specifically (Bach). And there are those who say specifically when he is not going out to meet him at his door; but when he goes out to meet him at his door, even if he already began the blessings, it is forbidden. And such is the main view in my opinion — that one should not be lenient in what touches the honor of the Holy One, blessed be He; and even though he began the blessings he may not go out to meet him at his door. And in particular, in the Gemara and Rif it does not seem to divide entirely between whether he began the blessings or not. And to bow even when he is not going out to meet him at his door — and even without giving peace — is forbidden until he accepts upon himself the yoke of the kingdom of heaven, as stated.
