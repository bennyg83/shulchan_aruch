(2) He fulfilled, etc. — it is implied that b'dieved it is permitted even when it is not a time of pressure; and in a time of pressure it is permitted even l'chatchila, as below at the end of this siman; and so ruled Maadanai Yom Tov, Nachalas Tzvi, and Lechem Chamudos at the beginning of the chapter on the morning prayer. Rashal wrote: in the days of selichos it is fitting to be careful, for they are accustomed to precede [prayer] before sunrise, and this is not correct, up to here. And Magen Avraham wrote on this: it is possible they relied on that the trouble of the congregation is that they go to their homes and gather again, as the Tur wrote siman 235 regarding Maariv; and in any case it appears l'chatchila that one should not rise so early, up to here. And to me it appears that this is not comparable, for in such a short time it is possible to wait in the synagogue and not go to their homes, which is not so regarding Maariv.
