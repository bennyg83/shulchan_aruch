(Seif katan 2) From when it rises, etc. Rambam, in his commentary to the Mishnah, first chapter of Berachos, Mishnah 1.
Meaning, before sunrise. That is, do not mistakenly understand the phrase “before the sun rises” as referring to the rising of the body of the sun, which occurs a considerable time afterward. See above, at the beginning of siman 58.
Rather, it means before the beginning of its ascent, namely sunrise.
It appears to me that he rules, etc. Four mil. Meaning, he holds that the measure of a mil is one quarter of an hour plus one twentieth. Accordingly, four mil equal exactly one hour and one fifth. Nevertheless, his words contradict what he writes in the third chapter of Pesachim, that the measure of a mil is two fifths of an hour. According to that calculation, four mil equal one hour and three fifths.
Know that what was written above, at the beginning of siman 58, in the name of Bach, that the interval from dawn until sunrise is one hour, even though it is longer as stated here, is not difficult. Bach actually writes there that it is approximately one hour, meaning that the measure is not exact but is somewhat longer. See there.
