(א) (siman kaf bet) from when it rises, etc. — (Rambam) in his commentary on the mishnah, chapter 1 of Berachos, mishnah alef.

(ב) Meaning: before sunrise. Meaning: so that you should not err from what is written "before sunrise" — meaning the rising of the body of the sun, which is a long time afterward — and see above siman 58.

(ג) Rather, meaning: before the beginning of its rising — that is, sunrise.

(ד) And it seems to me that he ruled, etc., four mil. Meaning: and he holds that the measure of a mil is a quarter of an hour and a twentieth. If so, the measure of four mil amounts to one hour and a fifth, compressed; nevertheless his words contradict what is written in Pesachim chapter 3, that the measure of a mil is two-fifths of an hour — if so, according to that view four mil amounts to one hour and three-fifths of an hour.

(ה) And know that what is written above in siman 58 in the name of Bach — that from alos hashachar until sunrise is an hour — even though it is more, as written here. In truth Bach wrote there approximately an hour. Meaning: that it is not compressed but slightly more; see there.
