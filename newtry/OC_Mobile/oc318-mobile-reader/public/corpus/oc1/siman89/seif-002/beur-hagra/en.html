And even this, etc. Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah there. But greeting with shalom is forbidden even in such a case. Although they said there, 'when one rises early to his doorway,' that is to exclude where he met him incidentally, as Tosafot there, s.v. bemashkim, and Rashi there wrote. Bach in his explanation of Rabbeinu Yonah.
And so it is forbidden, etc. So Rabbeinu Yerucham explained in the name of a Gaon, regarding the above-mentioned giving of shalom.
And if he began, etc. Orchos Chaim there, and see Magen Avraham. With this it is answered that it is not difficult that in seif 3 he ruled until the end of Shemoneh Esrei, although they were stated together in the Gemara there. According to this, it is not difficult, for bowing, according to the explanation of Orchos Chaim, when he did not rise early to his doorway, was not mentioned in the Gemara, as the Magen Avraham wrote.
And if he is not, etc. This refers to above, and according to the explanation of Rashi and Tosafot, that he met him, as above.
And some say, etc. For because of this they said that it is forbidden to attend to one's affairs, etc., as the Rashba wrote.
