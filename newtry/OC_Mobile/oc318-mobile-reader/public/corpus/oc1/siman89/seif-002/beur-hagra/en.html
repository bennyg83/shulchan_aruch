And even this [greeting], etc. Terumat HaDeshen there — but shalom is forbidden even in such a case; and even though they said there "when he rises early to his door" — that is to exclude when he met him by chance, as Tosafos wrote there s.v. bamashkim, etc., and Rashi there. Bach in explanation of Ri.

And likewise it is forbidden [to bow], etc. So explained Rabbeinu Yerucham in the name of a Gaon — giving shalom as mentioned above.

And if he began, etc. Orach Chayyim there and Magen Avraham — and with this it is resolved why there is no question in siman 63 regarding [prayer] until the end of [the Amidah's] eighteen [blessings], and they are contradictory statements in the Gemara there — but according to this there is no question regarding bowing per Orach Chayyim when he is not rising early to his door — it was not mentioned in the Gemara, as Magen Avraham wrote.

And if he is not [going to his door], etc. — this refers to the above, per Rashi and Tosafos that he met him [by chance], as above.

And some say, etc., that for this reason they said it is forbidden to do his affairs, etc., the Rashba.
