And that is, etc. For otherwise the difficulty of Tosafot from what is written there, 14b, and the Mishnah, etc., would be difficult. See Rashi there, who explained that the difficulty is from the Mishnah, etc. They said there: and if you will say it is where it had not, etc.; even if it had reached, it is permitted. Rashi needed this because it asks, 'and if you will say,' etc. If the question were from tefillin, what would he have thought, since tefillin also cannot be placed then, for they have one time? And if you say like the vatikin, the reason of the vatikin is joining redemption to prayer, and Rav was not concerned for this, since according to the assumption he placed tefillin between Krias Shema and prayer. The Gemara did not ask from tefillin because first it asked, perhaps Rabbi Yehoshua ben Korchah said, etc.; then it asked further, 'and does he not,' etc., meaning that even from reading to reading he does not hold at all like Rabbi Yehoshua ben Korchah. Therefore it says, 'does he hold,' etc., and it is not difficult that it should have said, 'and further, did he not say,' etc. There is a scribal error in Rashi there, s.v. umasnisen; where he wrote 'that is action,' it should read 'reading.'
