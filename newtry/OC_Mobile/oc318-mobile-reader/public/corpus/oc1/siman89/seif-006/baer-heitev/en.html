Prayer. See Bach. This is also stated by Maharshal and Maharsha; see there. Olas Tamid was not precise; see there.
Others. Mahari writes this. However, Tosafos implies that they do not accept this distinction. Otherwise, there would be no proof from the incident in which Rav washed his hands and taught us his discourse, for perhaps that case was different because he taught others. This is readily understood. Rokeach writes that a Torah scholar who neglected prayer because of his study, even if he studied the entire day and taught others, is regarded as though he did not study that day.
