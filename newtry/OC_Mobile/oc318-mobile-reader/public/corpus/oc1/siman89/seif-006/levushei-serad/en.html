Seif katan 6. “From dawn.” Magen Avraham disputes Taz in seif katan 3. Taz maintains that all the activities forbidden once the time for prayer arrives are prohibited only from the principal, ideal time for prayer, namely sunrise. Magen Avraham maintains that they are forbidden even once the after-the-fact time for prayer arrives, namely dawn. He proves this from seif 5, where the authorities dispute only whether one must interrupt at dawn, implying that all agree that one may not begin then.
