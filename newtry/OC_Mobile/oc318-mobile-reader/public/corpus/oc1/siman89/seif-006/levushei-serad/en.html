(siman vav.) From alos hashachar. He disagrees with the Taz siman gimel, who holds that all these prohibitions once prayer time has arrived — that is, specifically the principal time of prayer lekhatchilah, namely sunrise — whereas Magen Avraham holds that even once prayer time bedieved, namely alos hashachar, has arrived, all those matters are forbidden; and he proved this from section 5, that they disagree there only whether one must interrupt at alos hashachar — implying that to begin then is forbidden for all [authorities].
