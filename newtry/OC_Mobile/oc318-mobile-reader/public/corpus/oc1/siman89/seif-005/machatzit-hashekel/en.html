(siman kaf heh) And afterward, etc. — for in truth mincha time, etc. — Meaning: behold, mincha time is from six and a half [hours]. If so, it would have been fitting to permit him to pray Shacharis until six and a half [hours] if he transgressed and did not pray before four [hours] — such is truly the view of Bach. And he wrote that "until midnight" written in Shulchan Aruch is not precise. But Magen Avraham holds that it is precise — for in truth mincha time begins at midnight; but because of error they added half an hour and said not to pray mincha until six and a half [hours]; in any case from midnight onward it is already not fitting for Shacharis prayer — and it turns out that Bach disagrees with the view of Rabbeinu Yosef, whose reason is that it is forbidden immediately after midnight. Nevertheless, for another reason it is forbidden after midnight — for mincha time begins immediately after midnight. And see Taz.
