He began to eat. Even if it was permitted, meaning that there was more than half an hour until dawn, for within the half-hour it is forbidden to begin eating, as the Rama wrote in siman 652 regarding lulav, and the same applies regarding Krias Shema, which is according to the Torah. This is specifically if he eats more than the volume of an egg, which is the manner of a fixed meal, or drinks such a measure; but less than this is permitted until dawn.
He must interrupt. When dawn rises. Although regarding Minchah one need not interrupt, as written in siman 232, here it is different, since they attached it to the verse, 'Do not eat upon the blood,' meaning: do not eat before you pray for your blood; therefore he must interrupt. In the name of the Zohar, the later authorities wrote that even if one rises at midnight, it is forbidden to taste before he prays. The Peri Megadim wrote that this is specifically when he had been sleeping, and perhaps drinking is permitted. By strict law, even if he slept, it is permitted to eat and drink. Nevertheless, initially it is proper to be careful about this if he is not compelled. See Machatzit HaShekel, who wrote that in Responsa Shev Yaakov, siman 8, he wrote in the name of the Siddur of the Arizal that Rav Chaim Vital himself, when his heart was weak, ate or drank something to strengthen his body, and forbade only eating to fill one's desire; see there. In Chayei Adam, klal 16, he also wrote that one who rises before daybreak and whose heart is weak, and because of this he is prevented from learning, may eat.
And some say that he need not, etc. Meaning, regarding prayer, even if he began in a forbidden manner; but for Krias Shema alone, which is according to the Torah, he must interrupt if he began in a forbidden manner. He reads Krias Shema without its blessings, for its blessings are from the words of the Sages. After he finishes, he reads Krias Shema with its blessings and prays. The later authorities wrote that the primary ruling is like the first opinion. If he recited Birkas HaMazon and is engaged in drinking, he must interrupt according to all opinions.
