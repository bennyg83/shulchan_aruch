To interrupt. If he blessed Birkas HaMazon and is engaged in drinking, all agree he must interrupt (Magen Avraham). And see in seif kaf alef that I wrote in the name of Maharachav that even if he rose at chatzos at night it is forbidden to taste after prayer. And in Baer Heitev before this he wrote to distinguish between eating and drinking — eating is forbidden and drinking is permitted.
