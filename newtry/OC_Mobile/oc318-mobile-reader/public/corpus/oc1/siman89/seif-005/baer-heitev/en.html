To interrupt. If he recited Bircas HaMazon and is continuing to drink, all authorities agree that he must interrupt. Magen Avraham. See seif katan 11, where I wrote in the name of Rabbi Chaim Vital that even one who arose at midnight may not taste anything until after prayer. The edition of Baer Heitev before me distinguishes between eating and drinking, stating that eating is forbidden but drinking is permitted.
