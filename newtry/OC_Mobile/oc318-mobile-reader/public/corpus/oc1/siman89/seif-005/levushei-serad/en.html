(Magen Avraham siman heh) for in truth he came to resolve all the length of the Taz in siman alef to permit [Shacharis] in the half hour after midnight because mincha time has not yet arrived — to this he answers: since by law mincha time is from midnight, but Chazal were stringent lest one err — but this reason does not help to be lenient to pray Shacharis then, since mincha time has arrived by law; therefore immediately after midnight he has the status of one who prays two minchas, which is specifically unintentional; and also he cannot pray then, since he must precede mincha, and Chazal forbade praying mincha then. Examine well and you will see that all the difficulties of the Taz are resolved.
