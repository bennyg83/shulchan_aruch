Magen Avraham, seif katan 5. “In truth.” He comes to resolve the entire extended discussion of Taz in seif katan 1, who permits praying during the half hour after midday because the time for Minchah has not yet arrived. Magen Avraham answers that according to the strict law the time for Minchah begins at midday, and the Sages merely imposed a stringency lest one err. This reason cannot be used leniently to permit Shacharis then, since according to the strict law the time for Minchah has arrived. Therefore, immediately after midday his case is governed by the rule of praying Minchah twice, which applies only where the omission was inadvertent. He also cannot pray then, because he must first pray Minchah, while the Sages prohibited praying Minchah at that time. Study this carefully and you will see that all of Taz's questions are resolved.
