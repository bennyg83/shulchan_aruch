(Seif katan 7) To see him, etc. “And incidentally, etc.” Meaning, one should not ask: When he did not rise early specifically to go to his fellow’s doorway but merely encountered him on the road, even greeting him with “shalom” is permitted, as written afterward. He therefore explains that this case is intermediate. His primary intention is not to rise early for his fellow’s sake, but incidentally he does go early to his doorway. Therefore, greeting him with “shalom” is forbidden, while saying “Good morning, master” is permitted.
