(siman kaf zayin) To see, etc. — and incidentally this, etc. — Meaning: so that it should not be difficult — behold, they come early to his door and meet him on the road; even giving greeting is permitted, as written afterward. Therefore he wrote that here we deal with an intermediate matter — his main intention is not to arise early to his door, but rather incidentally on the way he arises early to his door; therefore giving greeting is forbidden, and saying "tzafra demare tav" is permitted.
