Seif 7. For it is a common occurrence. However, eating more than the volume of an egg is forbidden during the half hour before daybreak. See the end of siman 652, and see what I wrote above in siman 70 regarding Magen Avraham.
Magen Avraham, seif katan 16. For they ruled in accordance with the Rashba. According to this approach, it appears that even on Shabbos, when there is no obligation at all to juxtapose redemption to prayer, see siman 111, he nevertheless prays while traveling and juxtaposes redemption to prayer. This is because those authorities derive their proof from the statement of Rav Ashi, “I did not see the elder Sages act this way,” and that incident occurred on the Shabbos of the festival assembly.
