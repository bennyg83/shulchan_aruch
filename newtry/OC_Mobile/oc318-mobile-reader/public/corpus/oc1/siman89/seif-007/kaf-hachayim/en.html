(7) There — and if he prayed from alos hashachar, etc., he has fulfilled [his obligation]. Even though the time for kerias shema is from midnight onward, as stated in siman 1 section 7 and siman 46 section 49 — prayer is different, for it corresponds to the morning tamid offering; therefore its time is specifically from alos hashachar onward, as explained in Beit Yosef and in our words above section 3. And all four parts of prayer — namely, from kerias shema of the sacrifices section onward — must be specifically from alos hashachar onward, as known to those expert in calendar calculation, and as explained in Shaar HaKavvanot in the discourse on tefillin and the discourse on Kaddish — and similarly for one who understands the rabbi's words. I already wrote above in section 3 that laying tzitzit and tefillin must also precede all four parts of prayer; see there.
