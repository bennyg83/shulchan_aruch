To take a haircut, etc. Specifically close to Shacharis, meaning before dawn has risen. But once dawn has risen, this too is forbidden. Eliyah Rabbah. The Peri Megadim wrote that judging, entering a tannery, and a meal are forbidden close to Shacharis just as they are close to Minchah. See above in siman 70 in Biur Halacha. Buying needs for the Shabbos meal on Erev Shabbos before prayer is permitted, for they are heavenly needs; but on a weekday it is forbidden.
For it is a matter, etc. Since most people enter there by day. But in the morning it is not common, and they did not decree regarding it. Those labors for which people commonly rise early before dawn, some are stringent and hold that in the half-hour before dawn it is forbidden, as close to Minchah, and as above in siman 70 in Biur Halacha; see there. Therefore it is good to recite the blessings first.
