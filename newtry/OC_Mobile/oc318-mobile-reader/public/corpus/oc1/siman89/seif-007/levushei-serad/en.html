(siman zayin.) And incidentally. A difficulty was posed to him: the author later made in Beit Yosef section 3 that if one meets him on the road, it is permitted to greet with a single opinion even regarding two [people] — and apparently this would be the same case, for this one is going on his way to some business; if so, he has indeed met him. Therefore he explains that he is going to business and incidentally on the way bends the road to open his friend's [door] — therefore here for all [authorities] greeting is forbidden — and this is what Magen Avraham concluded: "but to give him peace," etc. — and this requires study.
