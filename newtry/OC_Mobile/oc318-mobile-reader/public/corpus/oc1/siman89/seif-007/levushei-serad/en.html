Seif katan 7. “And incidentally.” Magen Avraham was troubled because the Mechaber later presents, as a third case, one who encounters his fellow on the road, which one opinion permits even with both forms of greeting. Seemingly, this is identical to the present case, for this person is proceeding on his way for some business and thus encounters his fellow. Magen Avraham therefore explains that he is going to attend to some business and, incidentally, diverts his route to his fellow's doorway. Consequently, all authorities agree here that greeting him with shalom is forbidden. This is why Magen Avraham concludes, “But to greet him with shalom,” and this is readily understood.
