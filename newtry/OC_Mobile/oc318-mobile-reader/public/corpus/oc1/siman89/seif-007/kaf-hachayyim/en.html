7) There: If he prayed from alos hashachar, etc., he fulfilled his obligation. Although the time for Birchos HaShachar begins from midnight, as we wrote above in siman 1, paragraph 7, and siman 46, paragraph 49, prayer is different because it corresponds to the tamid offering. Its time therefore begins specifically at alos hashachar, as explained in Beis Yosef and above, paragraph 3. All four sections of prayer, beginning with Krias Shema in the order of the offerings, must be recited only from alos hashachar onward. This is known to those versed in the hidden wisdom and explained in Shaar HaKavanos, discourse 5 concerning tefillin, the discourse on Kaddish, and many other places for one who understands the Rav's words. We already wrote above in paragraph 3 that tzitzis and tefillin must likewise be put on before all four sections of prayer; see there.
