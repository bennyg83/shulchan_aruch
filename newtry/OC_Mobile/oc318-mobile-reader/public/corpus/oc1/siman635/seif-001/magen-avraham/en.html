For shade. To protect from heat and sun, and not merely for privacy (Rashi). It seems to me that he agrees that if he made it for storage or dwelling it is also invalid, as the Ran wrote; see the beginning of siman 626. But regarding a potters' sukkah, Rashi holds that it is made only for shade, and nevertheless it is invalid because he lives in it all year. The Ran holds it is made for dwelling, unlike the Bach, who made a dispute for no reason. See siman 636.
And he hollowed it out afterward. Whether from above or from below [Beit Yosef].
It is not all valid. It is not like invalid roofing that extends from the sukkah, for one wall does not extend together with it (Tur). Meaning one wall together with valid roofing, for this is invalid roofing.
And one should not make. After the fact it is valid [Bach], but the Levush invalidates.
