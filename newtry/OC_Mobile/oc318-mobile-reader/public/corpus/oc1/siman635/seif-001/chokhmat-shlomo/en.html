Seif 1 A sukkah, although it was not made for the sake of the mitzvah, is valid, etc. Note: See my work on Orach Chayim from the year 601, in my novellae to tractate Sukkah, in the sugya of roofing over an exedra, in siman 633. I was uncertain regarding a ganbach sukkah, which is valid, whether we require that at least before Sukkot, before the onset of Yom Tov, he intend to rely on it as a sukkah, or whether even without having intended this from before Yom Tov it also helps. This question arises only according to those who hold in siman 636 that the renewal is only for the preferred fulfillment of the mitzvah; one can ask whether, if he did not renew, we at least require that he rely on it from before Yom Tov, and without this it does not help, as we rule regarding a lechi that stands on its own, etc., or not. I proved there that we require that he at least intend from before Yom Tov; see there and examine carefully. (There) Such as the sukkah of gentiles, women, animals, etc. Note: Regarding the law whether women and these others may place the roofing on the sukkah at the outset, see Beit Yosef siman 14, Magen Avraham small paragraph 3, and Peri Megadim. In the early decisors it is proven and implied that in practice ganbach may not place the roofing at the outset, and here it permits only after the fact. See my work on Orach Chayim, still in manuscript, siman 14, what I wrote about this. What is written here in the Shulchan Aruch, that we require intent for shade, although this is stated in the Talmud, nevertheless is astonishing, for it appears that in practice we do not rule so, as I wrote above nearby in siman 633; see there and examine carefully.
