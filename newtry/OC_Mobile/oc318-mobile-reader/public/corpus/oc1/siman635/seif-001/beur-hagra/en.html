Seif 1: But, etc. As he writes there.
And if he stacked, etc. See Magen Avraham. This is like the distinction they made there, 4b, regarding building a platform opposite the middle wall, where the entire sukkah is valid, as written in siman 633, seif 5.
And if he hollowed out, etc. For we say the law of a bent wall, as stated there: there is a wall, etc.
And one should not make, etc. This is learned from the above. This is what is stated there, 18b: even if he reduced it, etc. It is not difficult that it never existed, for we do not say the edge of the ceiling descends and seals except when it is four handbreadths wide, as written in chapter 6 of Shabbat. If so, perforce the roofing preceded the walls. What Rashi wrote there in the Mishnah, 16a, s.v. "from above," speaks before he roofed it, and he wished only to explain the order of its weaving; or where there was a handbreadth first.
