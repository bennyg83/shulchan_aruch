(א) Kezayit — and l'chatchila it is good to take two olives: one in commemoration of Pesach and one in commemoration of matza eaten with it.

(ב) (b) Everyone, and no women owe it:

(ג) On a full stomach — see above siman 476 seif 1 in Hagahah and what we wrote there in Mishna Berurah.

(ד) He eats it leaning — b'dieved if he forgot and ate without leaning he need not return and eat if eating is difficult for him.

(ה) He does not bless on it — for it is only commemoration; Shelah wrote: I saw students kiss matzah and maror, and sukkah on entry and exit, and four lulav species — all for love of mitzvah; fortunate is one who serves Heaven with joy.

(ו) And he should be careful, etc. — since it is remembrance of Pesach he must eat it in Pesach's time, and Pesach is eaten only until midnight — and all the more the first olive on which we bless for eating matzah — he must be very careful not to delay until midnight; and b'dieved if delayed, Rishonim are uncertain if he fulfilled; therefore he should eat it and not bless on eating matzah; and maror too, although d'rabbanan, he should be careful to eat before midnight, and if delayed eat without blessing; and if it darkened for him before Haggadah until near midnight — he should sanctify and drink the first cup and wash hands and bless motzi and on eating matzah and eat, and also bless on maror immediately before midnight, and afterward say Haggadah and afterward continue his meal [Acharonim].

(ז) That Hallel too — meaning with its blessing he blesses afterward, he should say it l'chatchila before chatzot.
