(א) Kezayit — and the agreement of Acharonim is that l'chatchila one should eat the measure of two olives: one olive in remembrance of Pesach and one in remembrance of matzah eaten with it; and at minimum one should not reduce below a kezayit. Rokeach wrote: do not make eating afikoman burdensome; see siman 476 in Hagahah — not to eat it with coarse eating, for although Pesach is eaten to satiety, nevertheless it requires desire to eat; Shelah wrote: I saw students of the elite kissing matzah and maror, and so sukkah on entry and exit, and the four species of lulav — all for love of mitzvah; fortunate is one who serves Heaven with joy. See above siman 473 seif kaf tet as Maharshal wrote; and in Pesach Ma'uvin siman 315 he wrote further: my custom is to take a staff in my hand and shoes on my feet and say: so shall you eat it, your loins girded, your shoes on your feet, your staff in your hand, and you shall eat it in haste — it is Heaven's Pesach; therefore I practice not to remove the belt on Passover night until after afikoman — end; and in these countries we do not practice all this.
