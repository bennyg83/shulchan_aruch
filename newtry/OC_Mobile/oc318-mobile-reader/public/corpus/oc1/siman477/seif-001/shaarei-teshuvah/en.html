(In Shulchan Aruch seif alef) And he should be careful to eat it before midnight — see responsa Beit Yehuda on one who slept at Afikoman a midday nap and dreamed he must fast, etc. — I brought his words above siman 488; see Dagul Merevavah whose view is that one with coercion who cannot eat the motzi slice until midnight should not bless on eating matzah — doubtful blessings are lenient; and if it darkened until near midnight — sanctify, drink first cup, wash, bless motzi and on eating matzah and eat; Haggadah afterward; and bless on maror immediately before midnight before Haggadah — see there; and see below siman 491 seif vav on law of onen on Passover night in responsa Maharam of Lubin and other poskim.
