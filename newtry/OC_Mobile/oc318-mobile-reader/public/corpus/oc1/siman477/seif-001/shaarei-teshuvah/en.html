(In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 1) And he should be careful to eat it before midnight. And see Responsa Beis Yehudah regarding one who slept on erev Pesach an afternoon nap and dreamed a dream that requires him to fast, etc.; and I cited his words above in siman 288. And see in Dagul Merevavah, whose view is that one who suffered an ones such that he could not eat the HaMotzi piece until midnight should not recite the blessing over eating matzah, for a doubt regarding blessings we rule leniently. And if it grew dark for him until close to midnight, he should recite Kiddush and drink the first cup, wash his hands, and recite HaMotzi and Al Achilas Matzah and eat; and the Haggadah he should say afterward; and he should also recite the blessing over the maror immediately before midnight, before saying the Haggadah; see there. And see below, siman 548 s.k. 6, regarding the law of an onen on Pesach night, in the name of Responsa Maharam of Lublin and other poskim.
