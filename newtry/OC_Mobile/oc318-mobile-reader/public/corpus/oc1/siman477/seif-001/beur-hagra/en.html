(א) Seif 1: After. There 119b: one does not conclude, etc.; see Rashi there.

(ב) Kezayit. As written there he should eat kezayit matzah at the end.

(ג) Commemoration. Rashi and Rashbam explain this is the main eating and blesses initially like lettuce; Rosh challenged: if so he should eat with it maror and charoset; rather commemoration of Pesach, etc.

(ד) And he eats them. Even per Rosh, since in Temple times this was the main eating.

(ה) And not. As above.

(ו) And be careful. Bahag; even per R' Yehuda nevertheless l'chatchila forbidden as Zevachim 57b; see Tosafot Afikoman there.

(ז) And he prepares, etc. For fourth cup; see siman 472 unlike Tosafot Megillah there.
