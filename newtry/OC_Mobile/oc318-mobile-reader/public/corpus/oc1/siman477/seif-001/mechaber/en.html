Laws of eating the afikoman. Containing 2 seifim: After finishing the
entire meal, each one eats a kezayit from the guarded matza under the cloth, in commemoration of the Pesach offering eaten on a full stomach; and he eats it while leaning; and he does not bless on it; and he should be careful to eat it before chatzot.(And he should prepare himself that Hallel too be recited before chatzot (Ran chapter Afikoman and seif 2 of Megillah}.):
