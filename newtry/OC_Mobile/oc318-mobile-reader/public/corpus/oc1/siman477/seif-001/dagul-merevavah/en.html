It appears one who had coercion and cannot eat the broken piece of HaMotzi until chatzot should not bless on eating matzah, for doubtful blessings we are lenient; if night fell until near chatzot he should recite kiddush and drink the first cup and wash hands and bless HaMotzi and on eating matzah and eat, and say Haggadah afterward; see above siman 483 — except here he also blesses on maror immediately before chatzot, before saying Haggadah.
