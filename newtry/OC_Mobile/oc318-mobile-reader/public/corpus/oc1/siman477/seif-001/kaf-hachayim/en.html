(א) [Seif 1] A kezayit for each in commemoration of Pesach, etc. Bach wrote because for Rosh this eating is only commemoration of Pesach and not for obligation of eating matzah; for Rashbam it is for obligation of matza eaten with Pesach — therefore two olives required and one olive insufficient to remove oneself from dispute — end; so too siman 473. So too Maharil's drashot section 5 Haggadah requires eating measure of two olives for love of mitzvah; if cannot eat all that nevertheless not less than kezayit — see there; Bach in siman 473 wrote on Maharil's reason it has no foundation except main reason to remove from dispute — see there. Acharonim agreed l'chatchila eat two olives; if cannot, not less than kezayit; if eating only one kezayit intend commemoration of Pesach per Shulchan Aruch; so Ben Ish Chai parshat Tzav seif 35. Therefore custom to say when eating afikoman: commemoration of Pesach offering eaten on a full stomach.
