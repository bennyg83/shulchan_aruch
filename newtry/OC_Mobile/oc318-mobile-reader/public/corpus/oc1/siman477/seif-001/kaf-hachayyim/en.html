1) [Se'if 1] An olive-sized quantity for each person in remembrance of the Pesach offering, etc. Bach writes that according to Rosh, this eating is only in remembrance of the Pesach offering and not for the obligation to eat matzah, whereas according to Rashbam it fulfills the obligation of matzah eaten with the Pesach offering. Therefore, two olive-sized quantities are required; one is insufficient to satisfy both opinions. He writes likewise in siman 473. Derashos Maharil, laws of the Haggadah, likewise writes that one should eat the measure of two olive-sized quantities because of affection for the mitzvah. If one cannot eat that much, one should at least eat one olive-sized quantity. See there. Bach cites this in siman 473, but writes that Maharil's stated rationale has no foundation. The primary reason is to satisfy both opinions. See there. The later authorities agree that initially one should eat two olive-sized quantities. If unable, one should eat no less than one. When eating only one olive-sized quantity, one should intend it as a remembrance of the Pesach offering, in accordance with the Shulchan Aruch. Ben Ish Chai, parashas Tzav, subsection 35, writes likewise. Therefore, some customarily say while eating the afikoman, 'In remembrance of the korban Pesach, which was eaten when satiated.'
2) There. An olive-sized quantity for each person, etc. Women are likewise obligated, as stated above in siman 472, se'if 14. See there.
