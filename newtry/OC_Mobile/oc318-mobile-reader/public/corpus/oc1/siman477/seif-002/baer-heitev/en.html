(א) He does not eat. Acharonim agreed that now too he eats afikoman and blesses Birkas Hamazon without a cup (Magen Avraham); see Netziv.

(ב) Reaping. Meaning matza of mitzvah they guard from reaping, not other matzot; and he does not eat from that matza during the meal.

(ג) That he adds. If he remembers after Hallel he returns and says Hallel on the cup; if he did not remember until drinking the third cup he blesses Birkas Hamazon between third and fourth and does not return to drink the third cup (Magen Avraham, Chok Yaakov, Taz). Custom to break a piece from afikoman, pierce it, and hang it; no prohibition concern in piercing on Yom Tov (Magen Avraham siman 500 seif 7).
