He should not eat the afikoman. For if he eats, he will need to recite Birkas HaMazon and take a cup, as the Mordechai wrote at the end of the chapter Arvei Pesachim, and Shibolei HaLeket siman 67, and the Kol Bo siman 50: that one should not recite Birkas HaMazon on this night without a cup, because they enacted the third cup upon it; and if so, it would be like adding to the cups. However, the consensus of the Acharonim is that according to the one who holds that all year Birkas HaMazon does not require a cup, also now he may eat the afikoman and recite Birkas HaMazon without a cup. And see Nagid UMetzaveh.
During the meal. Even though at the end he ate other things, nevertheless after the fact he has fulfilled; so wrote the Abudarham, cited in the Beis Yosef: for they said only "one does not conclude after the Pesach offering with afikoman," and they did not say that if one did conclude [with other food] he must bring a Second Pesach; and matzah is no better than the Pesach offering itself.
In a place where they have the custom to practice shemirah. See the Levush, where it is implied that even an individual who accustomed himself to practice shemirah from the time of harvest likewise has this law.
And he should return and bless over the cup. Even though the cup he drinks now is in place of the third cup, and he has already advanced the cup of Hallel which properly should be last, the order of blessings does not invalidate; so wrote the Rosh in a responsum, cited in the Beis Yosef; and not like the Magen Avraham, who derives rulings in this matter from his own reasoning against the rulings of the Rishonim and Acharonim.
