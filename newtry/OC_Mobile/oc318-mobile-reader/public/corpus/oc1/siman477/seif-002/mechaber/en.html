If one forgot and did not eat afikoman and did not remember until he washed his hands or said 'let us bless,' he eats afikoman without the blessing of HaMotzi. And if he did not remember until he blessed Birkas Hamazon — if he remembered before he blessed borei peri hagafen, he washes his hands and blesses HaMotzi and eats the afikoman(and he returns and blesses Birkas Hamazon and blesses borei peri hagafen and drinks the cup (Beit Yosef)}. And if he did not remember until after he blessed borei peri hagafen, he does not eat afikoman and relies on matza he ate during the meal, for all are guarded from the time of kneading. But in a place where they are accustomed to guard matza of mitzvah from the time of reaping — even if he did not remember until after Hallel, he washes his hands and blesses HaMotzi and eats afikoman. {Rama: And he returns and blesses on the cup, and one need not be concerned that he adds to the cups (responsa of Rosh general rule 24).
