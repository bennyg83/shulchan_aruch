(א) If, etc. Although 'let us bless' is conclusion as Berachot 42a and so 'let us bless' chapter Afikoman 103b — nevertheless here it is not conclusion as Berachot there 'we' etc., although one holds it is conclusion; see Tosafot there s.v. atka.

(ב) And if, etc. As between third and fourth, etc.; see Tosafot there 108a s.v. kulehu.

(ג) He washes hands. Since he diverted attention as Pesachim 115b; see Tosafot 101b s.v. keshehen.

(ד) He blesses HaMotzi. Here 'let us bless' unnecessary, for even if he did not divert attention he must return and bless; even if he drank as Pesachim 103a — he said unnecessary, etc.; see Tosafot 102b s.v. yekaneh; his view, etc.; all the more here 'let us bless' does not help him, etc.

(ה) He blesses borei peri hagafen, etc.; and if not, etc. — this is for those who hold Birkas Hamazon requires a cup even for an individual.
