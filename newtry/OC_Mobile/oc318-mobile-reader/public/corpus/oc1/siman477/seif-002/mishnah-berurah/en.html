(1) (h) Without the blessing of the issuer - Daf Dabsiman Ket 11 11 Dam said Habel len and be blessed, O he is distracted, and if he wants to eat, he should go back and bless Haka Atka, Darhamna Samkinan Dain, it depends on his mind, that he should fulfill the will of the Omnipresent, and surely the forgetfulness caused him to take his hands or say Habel belen, and be blessed, and not be completely distracted:

(ב) (ט) He washes hands — since he blessed Birkas Hamazon he diverted attention; see Peri Megadim who wrote wash without blessing, especially when he knows he did not divert — lest he bless in vain.

(ג) (י) And he blesses borei peri hagafen, etc. — this is the third cup; they did not require drinking this cup adjacent to the first Birkas Hamazon, for they only enacted the third cup at the end of eating; and this case where he forgot to eat afikoman is as one who did not finish his eating (Bach).

(ד) (יא) He does not eat afikoman — for if he eats he must bless Birkas Hamazon, and Birkas Hamazon requires a cup, and then it is like adding cups; Acharonim wrote this is for poskim who hold siman 484 that Birkas Hamazon requires a cup; but per our custom as those who hold Birkas Hamazon ordinarily does not require a cup — now too he eats afikoman and blesses Birkas Hamazon without a cup.

(ה) (יב) He relies on matza, etc. — meaning this will be considered for afikoman even though he did not intend then for that purpose; and although we hold siman 479 that after afikoman one may not eat anything, and he ate other things after the matza — nevertheless b'dieved he fulfilled.

(ו) (יג) During the meal, etc. — for if he had no guarded matza at all during the meal, even if at the start of the meal he ate a kezayit of guarded matza properly, he returns and eats afikoman, for he did not fulfill the mitzvah of afikoman with that, since we require it eaten on a full stomach (Peri Chadash, Peri Megadim).

(ז) (יד) Guarding matza of mitzvah — and he did not eat from that matza during the meal so he could rely on it for afikoman.

(ח) (טו) Even if he did not remember, etc. — and all the more so if he remembered before beginning Hallel he certainly eats afikoman and blesses Birkas Hamazon on the cup; there is no concern of drinking between third and fourth cup which is forbidden as below siman 479, since the third was in error and the cup he now blesses on becomes the third.

(ט) To the other hand, the psalmist, and also greeted him on the fourth cup that followed him:

(י) (יז) One need not be concerned, etc. — since initially the third cup was in error, this cup is considered the third cup; and even though he preceded the Hallel cup which should be last, order does not impede. Know that per our custom all year to bless Birkas Hamazon without a cup, here too he eats afikoman without a cup.
