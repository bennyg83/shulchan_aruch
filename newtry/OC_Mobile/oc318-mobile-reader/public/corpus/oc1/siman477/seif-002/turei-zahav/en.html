(א) From reaping. Meaning matza of mitzvah they guard from reaping, not other matzot — therefore one does not fulfill with them where there is matza of mitzvah.

(ב) One need not be concerned, etc. — since the third was in error, the cup he now blesses on becomes the third; if he remembered after blessing on Hallel which should be last, order of blessings does not impede.
