Without a blessing. For we rely on the Table of the Merciful One; and see siman 179. And the Bach wrote in the name of the Maharshal that he should not eat it.
He should wash his hands. For since he recited Birkas HaMazon, he diverted his attention, as is written at the beginning of siman 475.
He should not eat the afikoman. For if he eats, he will need to take another cup for Birkas HaMazon, and it is like adding to the cups. But the Maggid Mishneh, Rabbeinu Yeruham, the Rashba, and the Raavad wrote that he eats a kezayis and recites the blessing without a cup; end quote. And it appears to me that one who is not careful the whole year and sometimes recites Birkas HaMazon without a cup may even now eat the afikoman and recite the blessing without a cup (see siman 182). And similarly the Semak wrote that one who is not careful the whole year regarding Seudah Shelishis, even when erev Pesach falls on Shabbos, should not eat [it].
And rely on matzah. Even though he ate other things after it, he has fulfilled [his obligation] after the fact.
Guarding for the mitzvah matzah. And he should not eat from that matzah during the meal.
After the Hallel. It appears to me that he should return and say Hallel over the cup; and if he did not remember until he had drunk the third cup, he should recite Birkas HaMazon between the third and the fourth, and return and drink the third cup.
