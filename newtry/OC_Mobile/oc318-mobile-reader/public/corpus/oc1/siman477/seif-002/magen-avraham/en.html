(א) Without a blessing. For we rely on divine mercy regarding afikoman (siman 479), and Bach wrote in the name of Rashal that one should not bless on it.

(ב) He washes his hands. Since he blessed borei peri hagafen his mind was diverted, as written siman 479.

(ג) He does not eat afikoman. For if he eats he must take another cup for Birkas Hamazon and it is like adding to the cups; nevertheless Rashi, Rivan, Rashba, and Raavad wrote he eats a kezayit and blesses without a cup — end; and it appears to me one who is not careful all year and sometimes blesses without a cup may now too eat afikoman and bless without a cup (siman 482); and similarly Semak wrote one not careful all year regarding third meal on Shabbat when it falls on erev Pesach should not eat.

(ד) He relies on matza. Even though he ate other things afterward, he fulfills b'dieved.

(ה) Guarding matza of mitzvah. And he does not eat from that matza during the meal.

(ו) After Hallel. It appears he should return and say Hallel on the cup; and if he did not remember until he drank the third cup, he blesses borei peri hagafen between third and fourth and returns and drinks the third cup.
