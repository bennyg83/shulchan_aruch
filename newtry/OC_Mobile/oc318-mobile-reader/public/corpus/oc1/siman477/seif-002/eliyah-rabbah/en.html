[2] [Levush] Because he would need, etc. Even though in siman 182 there are those who say that Birkas HaMazon does not require a cup, nevertheless the Shulchan Aruch and the Levush stated their ruling in a general way and ruled here that Birkas HaMazon requires a cup, for from the outset it was enacted that the third cup be after finishing eating, as the Mordechai wrote, as above; and so it is implied in the Kol Bo in the name of Rabbeinu Shimon, and Shiltei Gibborim, and Riaz, folio 255; and not like the Magen Avraham and Olas Shabbos.
