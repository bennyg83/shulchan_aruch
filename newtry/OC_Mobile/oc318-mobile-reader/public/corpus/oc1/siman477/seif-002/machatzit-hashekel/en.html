(א) Without a blessing — as explained, etc., meaning we hold siman 479 that even if he only said 'let us bless' and did not say the full formula, if he wishes to eat he must bless; nevertheless there it is written seif 2 that a guest accustomed to the host's table — even if he said 'let us bless' it is not distraction; and similarly on Passover night as the Merciful One established, etc. — even if he said 'let us bless' it is not distraction. However there in siman 479 it is not written regarding a guest that saying 'let us bless' is not distraction, only when he said it; but if he washed hands it is not clear there; and it implies even for a guest it is distraction; and here even if he washed he need not bless — one may say the reason per Magen Avraham there seif gimmel: if at the time of saying 'let us bless' he clearly distracted himself, even a guest is distraction; therefore when washing it is clear distraction.

(ב) I will give thanks to all the things that I do not know. It does not depend on the mind of Hashem’s people, and it is certainly forgotten that He should take His hands, and he did not fully understand:

(ג) Bach wrote in name of Rashal he should not eat it; this is Beit Yosef's view. Bach wrote the reason: if he remembered after Birkas Hamazon and has not yet blessed borei peri hagafen, one should not act as Mechaber wrote — not bless on the cup, wash hands, bless HaMotzi, etc. — for originally they enacted generally to bless borei peri hagafen on the third cup after Birkas Hamazon even if eating not finished, since blessing on food is stated generally; if he blesses on the cup and after eating afikoman blesses Birkas Hamazon again on a cup he adds cups; so too even at 'let us bless' he should not eat afikoman, for some hold siman 479 that even at 'let us bless' it is forbidden to eat again even if wishing to bless HaMotzi until first Birkas Hamazon on what already eaten — if he does so he must again come to Birkas Hamazon; therefore better not eat afikoman and rely on matza eaten during meal, though we do not hold so at 'let us bless' as above siman 479 — nevertheless why enter ourselves into dispute of poskim — end.
