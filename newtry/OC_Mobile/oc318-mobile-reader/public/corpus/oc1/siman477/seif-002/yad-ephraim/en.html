(In Magen Avraham seif 7) It appears he should return and say Hallel on the cup; possibly his reason: since afikoman is commemoration of Pesach and Pesach required Hallel on its eating as Pesachim 35 — he should say it on the cup too since this night's mitzvah is to say it on the cup; lest I say typo here, so read; if he did not remember until after great Hallel on the cup — as explained siman 481 Tur regarding one wishing to drink another Birkas Hamazon cup he says great Hallel on it; from Rosh's words Birkas Hamazon cup is called third cup only order does not impede — see Taz seif 3; 'if he did not remember' — appears typo, should read he does not return and drink fifth cup, meaning on that cup he blesses Birkas Hamazon he also says Hallel until end and drinks it; or Hallel does not require cup specifically and he drinks after Birkas Hamazon and says Hallel without cup; or third cup means cup he now blesses Birkas Hamazon on since prior cup was in error — meaning do not drink that cup separately but leave until fourth cup drinking order; from Rosh's words mistaken Birkas Hamazon cup is not considered at all.
