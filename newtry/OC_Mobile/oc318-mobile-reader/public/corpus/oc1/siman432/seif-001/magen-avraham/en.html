(א) He takes hands before checking (Maharil, Bach): on biur. Because checking is the beginning of biur:

(ב) All the time he did not finish. Explanation — if he finished he does not bless now but blesses tomorrow at burning, for they bless on biur chametz; and although he annulled, nevertheless he is obligated to burn per enactment of Sages; and further — for then he said all chametz I did not see, but chametz he saw he did not annul. And also in Maharik root 174 wrote: Yesh Omrim that he always blesses at burning; and all the more so in such a case. And see in Bach that at the end of his words requires study — he wrote for Rambam one need not bless, and behold Rambam wrote any mitzva with duration one must bless; and see end of siman 214 and Yoreh Deah siman 19 in Shach:

(ג) And he should beware not, etc. And if in matters not related to checking he conversed, he must return and bless as written siman 167 seif 6:

(ד) And good not, etc. With this he need not return and bless:
