Magen Avraham wrote he takes hands — Kaf HaChayim. Bach wrote in the name of Maharshal: although we accept one who takes hands for fruit is grossness of spirit as written siman 158 seif 5 if he did not know he touched an unclean place; nevertheless, since bedikat chametz comes for purification of the festival, and it is an important mitzva, it is fit to be stringent and act with cleanliness.
