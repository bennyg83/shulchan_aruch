(Taz s.k. 2.) Maharsha questioned — it appears to me: per what is brought below siman 491 — Beit Yosef brought in the name of Mordechai: and nowadays they bless on making a sukkah as the Tannaim and Amoraim did, for in their time each made a sukkah for himself; therefore the maker would bless; but now that one makes for all, it does not appear the maker should bless and the others not bless; and we rely on what Rav said — he arranges for them the cup of kiddush; and so the people act — end of his words; the same here for this reason it does not appear the checker should bless and the others not bless; nevertheless he arranges for them also the cup of kiddush; and because of this we also do not find that he would bless Shehecheyanu on slaughtering the Pesach:
