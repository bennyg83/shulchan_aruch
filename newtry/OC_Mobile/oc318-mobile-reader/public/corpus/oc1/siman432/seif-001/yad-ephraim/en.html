In Taz s.k. 2 — this is simple that Rosh did not question, etc. Apparently this was unnecessary, for even if Rosh's difficulty is that he bless again at biur, nevertheless it fits well to conclude from Rosh in his answer that biur is d'rabbanan mitzva in any case; if so his explanation in Rosh's words has no relevance here. And it appears the continuation of his words is thus: Taz's main doubt was whether one blesses at time of finishing mitzva, since Sages' main enactment was only on the beginning; therefore Taz wrote it appears to me he may bless well, since then he does an act in the essence of the mitzva; and therefore although it is only finish of mitzva and not so comparable to what is mentioned all the time he did not finish checking, that the obligation of this checking itself still applies — nevertheless he blesses well; and therefore Taz wrote apparently from Rosh the opposite — meaning the reverse of what is stated that then is essence of mitzva, that this is not called essence of mitzva since it is before its time of prohibition; per this there is still room to say on finish of mitzva alone one does not bless; therefore Taz wrote this is simple Rosh did not question, etc. — meaning certainly if we could explain Rosh's difficulty is that he bless again at biur when then is essence of d'oraisa mitzva; and on this he answers that then also there is no d'oraisa mitzva, therefore Sages did not enact a second blessing; if so one may well say blessing is not applicable at all on finish of mitzva, and the main enactment is only on the beginning. And that he questioned on Rosh that he should bless also on biur — this is because he thought Rosh holds this is not called finish of the first mitzva, since then obligation of d'oraisa mitzva begins and it is like another mitzva; if so it is fit to bless again; therefore Rosh answered that then also there is no d'oraisa mitzva, only all is d'rabbanan mitzva even at time of biur before its time of prohibition; if so not only that one does not bless again, but even if he did not bless initially he has no [obligation] to bless at biur which is finish of mitzva; and on this Taz wrote it is impossible to explain Rosh's difficulty thus — how bless twice — meaning even per what he thought that at biur it is d'oraisa, nevertheless it does not divide into two mitzvot — checking d'rabbanan because of accompaniment of d'oraisa biur — and one does not bless twice; only the main difficulty is that they should have enacted the blessing on d'oraisa mitzva, although finish of mitzva is nevertheless, since essence of d'oraisa mitzva was upon them to enact the blessing on biur; and on this he answered that also at time of biur there is only d'rabbanan mitzva, therefore it is more reconciled for them to enact the blessing on the beginning; if so in any case it is proven from Rosh that also at finish of mitzva blessing applies; if so one blesses on biur when he did not bless initially, although it is finish of mitzva, since it is essence of mitzva — for otherwise Rosh would not question why bless again at biur, and he would not need to say that at biur also there is no d'oraisa mitzva; rather even if you say at biur there is d'oraisa mitzva, nevertheless since it is only finish of d'rabbanan mitzva before it, one does not bless on it. And from where to distinguish here between d'oraisa and d'rabbanan? Rather perforce even in d'rabbanan blessing applies well even at end of mitzva; therefore he questioned that Sages should more have fixed the blessing on d'oraisa mitzva, and although it is end of mitzva, nevertheless since blessing also applies at end of mitzva, they should have followed d'oraisa; and on this he answered that also at time of biur it is d'rabbanan, therefore they fixed more on the beginning — although essence of d'rabbanan mitzva is at the end, nevertheless at the beginning is preferable; if so, since blessing applies at finish of mitzva in any case, we learn that if he forgot and did not bless initially he may bless at the end at biur, since essence of mitzva is then — it is obvious it is fit to compare to blessing at biur, as I explained:
