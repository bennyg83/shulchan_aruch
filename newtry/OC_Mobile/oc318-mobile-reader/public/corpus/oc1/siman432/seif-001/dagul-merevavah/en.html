From the mitzva of milah there is no proof, for there if the father does not circumcise, the court is obligated to circumcise him, and therefore each may bless. But it appears to me there is proof from one who appoints an agent to separate terumah — the agent blesses; for otherwise, if the agent does not bless, it would not help l'chatchila to tell the agent to separate terumah, just as a naked or drunk person does not separate terumah l'chatchila because of the blessing; and see Rambam laws of terumah chapter 8 halacha 8 — examine:
