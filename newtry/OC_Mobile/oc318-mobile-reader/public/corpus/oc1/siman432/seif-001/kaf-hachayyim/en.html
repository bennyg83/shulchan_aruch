1) [Se'if 1] Before beginning the search, etc. One should wash one's hands before reciting the blessing over the search. Maharil. Bach at the end of this siman and Magen Avraham at the beginning of this siman cite his words. Maharshal likewise writes this in Responsa, end of siman 86, but adds that it is preferable for the washing to follow relieving oneself or cleaning droplets of urine, so that it not appear ostentatious. See there. Bach there, Machatzis HaShekel, and Eshel Avraham at the beginning of the siman cite his words. One should not recite a blessing over this washing, because it is performed only for cleanliness. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Chemed Moshe, subsection 1.
2) The same applies when the householder recites the blessing while members of the household stand with him: they should purify their hands before beginning the search, for the same reason. If their hands are not clean, then since they must answer Amen, it is obvious that they must wash them. Eshel Avraham, there. See below, se'if 2. The later authorities write that the person conducting the search should be pure from seminal impurity and from impurity resulting from marital relations. It is therefore proper to immerse beforehand.
