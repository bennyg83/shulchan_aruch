דין ברכת בדיקת חמץ. ובו ב סעיפים:
The law of the blessing for bedikat chametz. Contains 2 seifim. Before he begins to check he blesses Who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us concerning biur chametz (and if he began to check without a blessing he blesses) for as long as he has not finished his checking (Kol Bo). And he should beware not to speak between the blessing and the beginning of the checking; and it is good that he not speak of other matters until he finishes all the checking, so that he may set his heart to check in all the places where chametz is brought in.() ) ויזהר שלא ידבר בין הברכה לתחלת הבדיקה וטוב שלא ידבר בדברים אחרים עד שיגמור כל הבדיקה כדי שישים אל לבו לבדוק בכל המקומות שמכניסין בו חמץ:
