The law of the blessing for the search for chametz. It contains two se'ifim.
Before he begins to search, he recites the blessing "…Who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us concerning the removal of chametz" {and if he began to search without the blessing, he recites the blessing as long as he has not finished his search (Kol Bo)}. And he should be careful not to speak between the blessing and the beginning of the search; and it is good not to speak of other matters until he completes the entire search, so that he set his heart to search in all the places into which chametz is brought.
