(א) Before he begins to check — so the blessing be passing to its performance:
(ב) He blesses — Yesh Omrim that it is good he take hands first, and this is only for cleanliness:
(ג) On biur chametz — for although he does not burn until tomorrow, nevertheless since this checking is for the sake of biur it is akin to biur; and one does not bless on bedikat chametz because this is not the end of his mitzva; and also one does not bless on annulment of chametz since the essence of annulment depends on the heart, and one does not bless on matters of the heart. If he blessed to destroy he fulfilled [Acharonim]:
(4) (d) 27 who did not finish his inspection - a time when he did not finish a case still passes to his doing. And if he has already finished the inspection, he does not bless now but instead blesses for tomorrow during the burning of a daha, who blesses him for burning chametz, and if he canceled it yesterday during the inspection, MM must burn the correction of the sages and another daha, then he said that every hamira without its fronts and the chametz that he saw was not canceled The 21st blessing was fixed during the inspection and per their view he blesses during cremation without a name and a kingdom. And it seems that the one who wants to trust and bless does not have a brain in his hand as to whom to trust:
(ה) And he should beware not to speak — and b'dieved if he conversed in matters not needed for checking he returns and blesses, for he interrupted between the blessing and the mitzva:
(ו) And good, etc. — with this he need not return and bless since he already began the mitzva; and in matters that are needed for checking, even l'chatchila he may speak, for this is not considered interruption at all [Acharonim]:
