(א) Several houses — and walking from house to house is not interruption; and see Chok Yaakov who wrote that if he checks his house and shop and the shop is in another courtyard he must return and bless; however I found in Chok Yosef and in Ma'amar Mordechai that their view is all are one mitzva — he is obligated to go and check all places where there is chametz, and it is not applicable to say walking is interruption in this:
(ב) He stations members of his household — the same applies to another not of his household; he speaks in the present tense, as stated in the Gemara that there are places that hire to check; in any case he should assist, for the mitzva is upon him more than upon his agent. And behold by law he may rely in checking even on women and slaves [and not on our foreign maidservant] and minors; nevertheless l'chatchila it is proper not to rely except on adult men who reached the age of mitzvot — that is, from thirteen years and onward — because proper checking involves great effort, and one should fear perhaps they will be lazy and not check well [Acharonim]:
(ג) At the time he blesses — so they fulfill through his blessing and answer amen on his blessing:
(ד) Relying on blessing, etc. — and if the head of household does not check at all but obligates another to check for him, the other blesses afterward, for he is like his agent also regarding blessing:
(ה) That the head of household blessed — and if they did not hear his blessing, l'chatchila he should not send them to check; nevertheless if it is difficult for him to finish checking himself he may ask another to assist him, and that other need not bless, for all checking is one mitzva and the head of household already blessed on it:
(ו) And the custom is to leave — that is, hard chametz that will not crumble; and also in a place guarded from children and mice:
(ז) However, etc. — Bach disagrees on this, for there is no concern here of blessing in vain, for such is the mitzva — to check chametz and search for it lest he find; and if he does not find, it is nothing. And Taz wrote further that the blessing refers to what he will certainly burn tomorrow from what he leaves for eating, except that from today he begins through checking; and so in siman 431; and Acharonim agreed for the law.
