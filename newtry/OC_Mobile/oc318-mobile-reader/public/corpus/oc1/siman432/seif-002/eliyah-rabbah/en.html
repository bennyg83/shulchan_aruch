(2) [Levush] One need not place, etc. And Mahari siman 193 wrote the reason for the custom — when he has chametz he is reminded tomorrow at the time of biur to annul (Chok Yaakov). It is difficult either way: if he leaves chametz from what he eats tomorrow he will fulfill burning chametz and remember to annul; and if he does not leave — what does he care about annulment tomorrow? I again examined Mahari himself — it is explained there, in order that he remember annulment of the night after checking at the time he finds chametz, and not annulment of tomorrow; however in Maharil and Mateh Moshe siman 555 it implies so; and further — for what they practice burning the vessels, no concern. And Mateh Moshe wrote: they should beware to leave only hard chametz that will not crumble, and in a place guarded from children and mice; and one must conceal the chametz he wishes to eat, and even if the checker finds it there is no objection to this:
