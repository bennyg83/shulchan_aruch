(ב) And all the more so in such a case — meaning we do not accept like Maharik; but in such a case that he forgot to bless at the time of checking, he blesses at the time of burning: any mitzva that has duration, etc. — meaning specifically milah or slaughter, for once he circumcised or slaughtered the mitzva passed and he cannot bless; but a mitzva like this that has duration, such as tefillin and tzitzit — it turns out their mitzva is to bless immediately at placing tefillin and wrapping tallit; nevertheless, since he is still engaged in that mitzva — behold the tefillin and tzitzit are still on him — he blesses if he did not bless initially; and the same with bedikat chametz that has duration until tomorrow at burning — he blesses at burning. And see end of siman 214 there he brought Rambam's language.
