(א) He stations members of his household. In any case he too checks, for the mitzva is upon him more than upon his agent, as stated 82b of Kiddushin; and see siman 431 and siman 470; and in the Gemara it implies there are places that practiced hiring one to check:

(ב) Relying on blessing. Ten who perform one mitzva, one blessing for all — siman 8 implies; if the head of household does not check but obligates another to check for him, the other blesses afterward — requires study: how can he bless? Behold the mitzva is not incumbent on him to check. One may say per what is written siman 585 regarding shofar — the blower blesses, for it is different there because the listener hears the blessing and fulfills through his blessing; but here why should the checker bless since the mitzva is not upon him? One must say nevertheless he performs a mitzva, similar to milah where the mitzva is on the father of the son, and nevertheless if another circumcised him the other blesses; and see Tur Yoreh Deah siman 265.
