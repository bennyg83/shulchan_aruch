(א) From members of his household. The same applies to another who is not of his household (and that the agent also does not bless — see Yoreh Deah laws of slaughtering; and see Eshel Avraham siman 38; and when the houses are distant it does not help to compare for one who blessed on a small tallit at home that he blesses on a large tallit in synagogue). In any case he too should check, for the mitzva is upon him more than upon his agent:

(ב) Relying on blessing. And they answer amen on his blessing; and if they are not standing at his blessing he cannot send them to check — Kaf HaChayim. And if the head of household does not check but obligates another to check for him, the other blesses after — Magen Avraham. And l'chatchila he should seek men obligated in mitzvot; b'dieved women and slaves suffice [and minors], but not our Canaanite maidservant — Kaf HaChayim:

(ג) And the custom. Those who practice leaving should beware to leave only hard chametz that will not crumble, and in a place guarded from children and mice:

(ד) He did not place. And so it is fit to practice l'chatchila, for we are concerned perhaps he will place it where the checker will not find it — Taz. And the Ari wrote this custom and one places ten crumbs — it is according to a great secret. Also Kaf HaChayim reconciles and upholds not to annul a custom of Israel (and Taz in the name of Temim De'im wrote it is women's custom; and in Mahari wrote a reason for the custom, and so Kaf HaChayim gave a reason for the matter there):
