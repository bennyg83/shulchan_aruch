(•) (In Shulchan Aruch seif 2) There is no difference between the first and second etc. — and see in responsum Noda B'Yehuda siman 30 regarding a child born in the diaspora whose eighth or ninth day of circumcision falls on the second day of Yom Tov of the diaspora — in this he wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 266 to be stringent that Yom Tov does not override, and Shach is lenient even when certain, and he wrote that even Rosh holds in a safek that it is permitted, and even on Rosh Hashana Yom Tov, and also certainly milah not in its time on the second day of Yom Tov of the diaspora — one who wishes to rely on Shach to be lenient, they do not protest against him — see there.
