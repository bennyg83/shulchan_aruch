And they place in niddui, etc. For any matter whose essence is from their words [rabbinic], and [violating it] uproots the entire mitzvah, they place in niddui; but regarding a matter whose essential prohibition is D'Oraisa, for example on the first day of Yom Tov, if he did a D'Oraisa melachah they place him under shamta; but if he did a D'Rabbanan melachah regarding a matter whose essence is D'Oraisa, they flog him nevertheless (the Ran). See Yoreh De'ah, siman 334, in the SMaG, and in the Shach there; and Beis Yosef at the end of siman 526 wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yeruham that they flog him and place him in niddui; and if he is a Torah scholar they do not place him in niddui; and if he did melachah through a gentile they place him in niddui (R' Aharon Sasson, siman 196); and if he acted according to a sage's ruling they do not place him in niddui (Mitz, part 1, siman 36); and the same applies to one who acted b'shogeg (Mabit, part 2, 149; so it is).
They flog him. Meaning makkas mardus. Mahara'i wrote in the annotations on Rashi at the end of Parashas Ki Seitzei that it is thirteen lashes; see there; and from the Rambam it does not appear so.
