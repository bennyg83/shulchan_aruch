1) [Se'if 1] In the Diaspora, where two days of Yom Tov are observed, etc. Although we are now expert in determining the new month, the Sages nevertheless instituted that two days of Yom Tov be observed outside Eretz Yisrael. The Sages were concerned that the government might issue a decree forbidding Torah study, causing the method of calculating the calendar to be forgotten. People would then designate a deficient month as full and a full month as deficient, and they might eat chametz on Pesach. Beitzah 4b and Rashi there. See below, subsection 10.
