(1) [Seif 1] In the diaspora where they observe two days of Yom Tov etc. — and even though now we are expert in the fixing of the month, nevertheless Chazal instituted to observe in the diaspora two days of Yom Tov, because Chazal were concerned lest the kingdom decree not to engage in Torah and the secret of intercalation would be forgotten and they would make a deficient month full and a full month deficient and they would eat chametz on Pesach — Beitza daf 4b and Rashi there; and see below note 10.
