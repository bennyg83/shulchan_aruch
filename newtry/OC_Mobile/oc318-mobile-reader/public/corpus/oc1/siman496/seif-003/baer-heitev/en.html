(ב) To settle and so on. Effith will return as if he does not want to return. Red:

(ג) To the boundary. And therefore if his intention is to return, it is permitted to do melacha, and if he has no intention to return he has not yet been fixed to be like them. Children of Eretz Yisrael who came to the diaspora and did melacha — they do not excommunicate them — Orach Chayyim part 2 siman 149; and they pray eighteen blessings like their place and wear Yom Tov garments; and when Yom Tov falls on erev Shabbat they cook without eruv tavshilin. And one who uproots his residence with his wife from Eretz Yisrael to the diaspora — even though his intention is to return, his law is like one who has no intention to return — Radbaz. And whoever has no intention to return, even though his wife remained there, is like one who has no intention to return — Magen Tzvi ch. 2, Knesset HaGedolah — see there distinctions. Avraham Zacut wrote in the name of Yesh Shtar ch. 25: an incident occurred with one who went to Eretz Yisrael and established his residence there, and after several years came to our country and his intention was to return, and he practiced weekday on the second day of Yom Tov and died an unusual death and did not merit to return to Eretz Yisrael — see there. And see in Ganat Veradim Orach Chayyim general rule 4 siman 17, and in responsum Zera Avraham Orach Chayyim siman 9 and Yoreh De'ah, and similarly Maharitatz siman 139, and in Halakhot Ketanot siman 111, and in Yad Aharon. (And know that what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Orach Chayyim and the leniencies of the place he left — there are authorities etc. as long as his intention is to return — end of his words — is a scribal error and must read: and the leniencies of the place to which he went. And see in Sha'arei Efrayim some laws that branch from this.)
