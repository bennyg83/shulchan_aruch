(s.k. 2) They flog, etc. And from the Rambam it does not appear so. For he wrote in chapter 6 of Hilchos Chametz, law 12: the Sages forbade eating matzah on erev Pesach, etc., and one who ate, etc., they strike him with makkas mardus until his soul departs; end quote. See: he gave no fixed measure for the lashes. And the Ran wrote, and this is his wording: even though he holds not like the Rambam, but rather that makkas mardus is lighter than Torah lashes and according to the assessor; however, makkas mardus for a continuing offense: they strike him until his soul departs, or until he accepts upon himself the words of the Sages; end quote. And the Peri Chadash in siman 471 agreed to this.
