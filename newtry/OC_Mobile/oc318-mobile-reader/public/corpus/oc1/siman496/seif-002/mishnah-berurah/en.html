{8} It is forbidden for a Jew, etc.: and even if he feels pain in his whole body it is also forbidden through himself. Orchos Chaim wrote: a person who falls or is bruised and blood collects in the place of his fall or bruise: it is not considered an internal illness; and since he is not in such great distress and can wait until tomorrow without danger, it appears that it is forbidden to draw blood, which they call "keffen" [and in our language "bankes"], even on the second day of Yom Tov of the Diaspora, and even through a gentile; end quote.
