To apply medicine to the eye. Even though it is not an illness that encompasses the whole body; see siman 328, se'if 17, for regarding an illness that encompasses the whole body, even on Shabbos it is permitted to do a shevus; therefore Beis Yosef omitted "or another illness, etc."; and the Rema comes to teach that even according to those who forbid on Shabbos, they permit on Yom Tov. One who fell and blood collected [under the skin]: it is forbidden to draw blood, which they call "keffen," even through a gentile, since he can wait until tomorrow (Orchos Chaim); and see Terumat HaDeshen, siman 420.
