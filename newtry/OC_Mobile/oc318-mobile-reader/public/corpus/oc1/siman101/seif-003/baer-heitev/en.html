To make audible. According to the reason given by the Zohar, it appears that this is forbidden. Therefore, if he can concentrate while praying quietly, that is preferable. Magen Avraham; see there.
Excessively. For then they will certainly disturb one another. Nevertheless, it appears that chazzanim who raise their voices to arouse concentration and to make their words clearly audible act properly, since the essence of the chazzan's repetition was instituted specifically in a loud voice. Taz. The Magen Avraham likewise rules this way, contrary to the Bach and Peri Chadash; see there. Those who do so merely to display their voices, however, certainly act improperly. See Eliyah Zuta, who writes in the name of Ramban regarding the verse, "They called out to Hashem with strength," that from here one learns that prayer requires a voice. See there at the end of Parashas Bo.
