As written at the end of [tractate] Rosh Hashana — because of the noise. Rashi explains: because they are numerous. And so the Rambam explains in chapter 8 of the Laws of Prayer. And as stated in chapter 4 of [tractate] Eruvin: "the noise of the residents." And at the beginning of [tractate] Nedarim: "because of the noise to him" — he explains, etc. But Tosafos explained: since they are not accustomed [to raising their voices], one confuses the other — and this is the meaning of "noise" (avshi), as in chapter 3 of [tractate] Chullin, "the lung that made noise — everyone shouted about it," and in chapter 7 of [tractate] Berachos and chapter 4 of [tractate] Kesubos. "Avshi biney kanei" — in chapter 7 of [tractate] Bava Metzia — see the Gemara there. And so the Aruch explains there.

For this would make one [seem] like the false prophets. Mahariv. And per the Rosh's explanation in seif 2, there is no distinction — since he wrote that if one cannot concentrate, even raising one's voice is permitted; and on Rosh Hashana even in a congregation it is permitted. And Knesset HaGedolah already expressed astonishment at him.
