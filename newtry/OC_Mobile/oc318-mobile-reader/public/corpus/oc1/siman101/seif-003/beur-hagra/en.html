Seif 3, "Some say," etc. As stated at the end of Rosh Hashanah, because they make noise. Rashi explains this as meaning that they are numerous, and Rambam likewise explains it in chapter 8 of Hilchos Tefillah, as in chapter 4 of Eruvin, "the residents make noise," and at the beginning of Nedarim, where "because they make noise for him" is explained accordingly. Tosafos, however, explain that because people are unaccustomed to it, they make their voices audible and one causes another to err. This is the meaning of "make noise," as in chapter 3 of Chullin regarding a lung that made a sound, and "everyone made a commotion about him" in chapter 7 of Berachos and chapter 4 of Kesubos, and "a noise among the reeds" in chapter 7 of Bava Metzia. See the Gemara there, and this is also how the Aruch explains it.
Nevertheless, they should take care, etc. For otherwise he is among the false prophets, as stated by Mahari Weil. According to the Rosh's explanation in seif 2 there is no distinction, for he writes that when one cannot concentrate he may even raise his voice, and on Rosh Hashanah this is permitted even in public. Knesses HaGedolah already expressed surprise at him.
