Seif katan 3, "And he makes audible," etc. The text should read "because he said to him."
As Rama writes in siman 141 regarding one called to the Torah, that he should read quietly along with the chazzan, and he may make it audible to his own ears. Rama writes that even the Zohar agrees. The Zohar compares prayer in this respect to one called to the Torah; see Beit Yosef there.
Bach writes, etc. His words are not compelling. See Taz, seif katan 3.
