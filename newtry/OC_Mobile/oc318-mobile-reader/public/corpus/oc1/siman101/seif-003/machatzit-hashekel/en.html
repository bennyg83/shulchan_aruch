(s.k. 3) And one makes heard, etc. — because it was told to him, as stated.

And as Rama wrote in siman 141 regarding one who goes up to the Torah, that he should read with the prayer leader in a whisper, and it is permitted to make it heard to his ears; and he wrote that the Zohar agrees; and in the Zohar he compares in this regard prayer to one who goes up to the Torah — see there in Rabbeinu Bachya.

Bach wrote, etc., and his words are not compelled; and so Taz, s.k. 3.
