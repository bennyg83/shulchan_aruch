Not to raise one’s voice. Nevertheless, the vidui [confessions] that we say together in a single formula — both the righteous and the sinners alike — on Yom Kippur, it is permitted to say them aloud in order to confess with a full heart (Beit Yosef in the name of Mahariv). And if one makes his voice heard at home so that the members of his household may learn from him, he may make his voice heard more. Therefore one should rebuke those cantors who raise their voices more than is necessary to make the congregation hear, so that they may answer Yehei Shemei Rabbah, Barchu, and Amen (my teacher, in Bach).
