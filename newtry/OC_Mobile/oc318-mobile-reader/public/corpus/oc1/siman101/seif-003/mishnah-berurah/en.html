Even with the congregation. This is permitted in order to arouse concentration, and we are not concerned that it will disturb the congregation, since they have siddurim and machzorim in their hands. Magen Avraham concludes that it is nevertheless preferable to pray quietly if one can concentrate, and many Acharonim rule likewise.
Excessively. One who raises his voice excessively resembles the prophets of Baal, of whom it is written that they called out in a loud voice. Alternatively, he will certainly confuse another worshipper. However, chazzanim who raise their voices to inspire concentration and to make the words clearly audible act properly, since the chazzan’s prayer was instituted specifically aloud. Those who do so merely to display their voices act improperly. See references 100, 260.
