Alternatively, this will certainly cause one to confuse his companion. Nevertheless, it appears that cantors who raise their voices in order to arouse intention and to make their words properly heard act correctly, since the primary function of the prayer leader’s prayer was specifically instituted to be said aloud. However, those who do so to display their voice certainly act improperly.
