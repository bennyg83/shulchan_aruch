(There) Seif 2 — One should not pray, etc., but cut the words on his lips, etc. [n.b.] Chazal learned this from the verse of Chana, that one should pray only in a whisper. And my son-in-law, the sharp and fluent R' Avraham Binyamin, may he live, showed me in Teshuva MeAhavah (siman 13): he was asked by someone about Rashi's explanation in Shmuel 1 on the verse "and he considered her a drunkard" — that they were not accustomed to pray in a whisper; and he was asked: behold Chazal said one may pray only in a whisper — see there how he was pressed on this. And in my humble opinion it comes out well, even though Yalkut Shimoni in Parashat Vaetchanan on the verse "For what great nation has Heaven close to it" — and these are his words: Come and see what is written in Shmuel: they call in a loud voice, as it is written "and the doorposts shook from the voice of the caller" — why? Because the Holy One, blessed be He, is far from them. But a person of Israel stands and prays in a whisper, and the Holy One, blessed be He, hears him — why? Because the Holy One, blessed be He, is close to him, as it is written "for He stands at the right hand of the poor," etc. — until here. If so, it is proven: one to whom Heaven is close may pray in a whisper; but one from whom Heaven is far should pray in a loud voice. And behold the Holy One, blessed be He, stands only by a male and not by a female, for it is not His honor, as written in the midrash and Rashi on Parashat Toldot on the verse "and the the Master said to her" — how many times the wheel turns, not to speak with a woman — until here. If so, here too it is not His honor to stand by her; therefore women pray in a loud voice. And this is what Rashi meant: "they were not accustomed" — meaning the women were not accustomed to pray in a whisper; therefore she, praying in a whisper, he considered her a drunkard. But she answered him: "I am a woman of embittered spirit" — for it is written "the the Master is close to the brokenhearted"; and perforce the intent is: even one who is not worthy, nevertheless if he has brokenness of heart He is close. For if on ordinary people it is written "the the Master is close to all who call Him," why here "to the brokenhearted"? Perforce the intent is: even one not worthy to be close to Him, nevertheless to the brokenhearted He is close; and therefore also for women He is close if she is brokenhearted; and therefore she said, "No, my the Master, for I am a woman of embittered spirit," and therefore I am confident the Holy One, blessed be He, is close to me; therefore I pray in a whisper. And this is what is meant: "and I poured out my soul before the the Master" — specifically that He stands by me; therefore I pray in a whisper. And it comes out well, there's help; examine well.
