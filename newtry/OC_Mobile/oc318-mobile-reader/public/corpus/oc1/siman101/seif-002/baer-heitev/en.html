In his heart. If he prayed only in his heart, whether he fulfilled his obligation requires study. See Magen Avraham.
To his ears. If he did not make it audible to his own ears, he nevertheless fulfilled his obligation. The Arizal would not make his voice audible even during the zemiros; only on Shabbos did he raise his voice slightly.
Quietly. Meaning that although he can concentrate when praying quietly, if he cannot concentrate as well as when praying aloud, this is considered an inability to concentrate. Taz.
