In his heart — if he prayed in his heart, this requires further study whether he has fulfilled his obligation. See Magen Avraham.

To his ears — and if he did not make it audible to his ears, he has fulfilled his obligation. The Ari, of blessed memory, would not make his voice heard even during Pesukei D'zimra — except on Shabbos when he raised his voice slightly.

While whispering — meaning, even if one can concentrate while whispering, but not as well as when praying aloud, this is considered being unable to concentrate. Taz.
