And making it audible to his ears in a whisper — this applies specifically l’chatchila, but b’dieved he has fulfilled his obligation even if he did not make it audible to his ears, as is the case with Krias Shema (Berachos daf 31 and the Rashba). And if he is unable due to illness or some other matter, he may say it in his heart, as it says “speak in your hearts” (Beit Yosef in the name of the Orchos Chayyim).
