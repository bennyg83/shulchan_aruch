(2) There — at least he should have intent in Avot, etc. — Abudraham wrote the reason: because it is more important than the other blessings, since it contains the greatness of God, the merit of the forefathers, and the redemption of the later generations — until here. And Kitzur Lechem wrote: because it has forty-two words corresponding to the forty-two-letter Name — until here. And Bach, Rabbi Akiva Eiger, section 1. And it appears to me further: because most of the intent of prayer is in the blessing of Avot, as explained in Shaar HaKavanot and in the siman of the Rashash.
