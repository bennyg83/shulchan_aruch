That one must direct one's intention in all the blessings, and that one may pray in any language. It contains 4 seifim. (1)(1) One who prays must
(2) direct his intention in all the blessings. If he cannot direct his intention in all of them, at least (1) he should (3) direct his intention during Avos. If he did not direct his intention during Avos, (2) even if he directed his intention during all the rest, he must return and pray again.(And nowadays (we do not repeat [the prayer] due to a lack (4) of intention, since even upon repetition it is likely that he will not direct his intention — if so, why should he repeat?)
