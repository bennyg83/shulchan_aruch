See [tractate] Berachos daf 34b and Tosafos [tractate] Berachos daf 34b s.v. "yechavein."

The Tur: it appears to me that even [if he directed intention in everything else but not Avos, he must return]. And so it is in the Gemara, from the fact that it said “in Avos” — implying in the others, no. And so also [the ruling of] R’ Elazar.

See Tosafos [tractate] Rosh Hashana daf 16b s.v. “v’iyun” — and all the more so nowadays. And so it is stated explicitly in the Jerusalem Talmud, chapter 2 of Berachos: R’ Yirmiah said in the name of R’ Elazar — if one prayed and did not direct his heart, if he knows that he will return and direct his heart, let him pray again; if not, let him not pray again. R’ Chiyya Rabbah said: I never in all my days directed my intention except for one time — I tried to direct my intention and thought to myself: who is entering before the king first — the exilarch or the prince? Shmuel said: I counted chicks. R’ Bun bar Chiyya said: I counted the tax officials. R’ Matnah said: I am grateful to my head, for when it reaches Modim it bows by itself. And Tosafos cited this in [tractate] Rosh Hashana there, [showing] that one does not direct his heart in prayer, as stated in the Jerusalem Talmud — R’ Chiyya, etc.
