At least he should direct his intention during Avos. And one should train himself to direct his intention at least at the conclusion of each blessing, for they contain 113 words, corresponding to the number in the prayer of Channah, and the word “lev” (heart) appears 113 times in the Torah — to indicate that they require the intention of the heart (Tur in the name of Ra’ah). And one should also direct his intention that the roshei teivos (initial letters) of the 18 blessings equal 1,800, and therefore it is not proper to say “v’lamalshinim” but rather “[v’]lamalshinim.” And 1,800 angels receive [the prayer] and place a crown before the Holy One, blessed be He.
