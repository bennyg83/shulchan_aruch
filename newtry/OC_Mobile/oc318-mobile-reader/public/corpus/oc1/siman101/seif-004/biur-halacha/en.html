He may pray in any language, etc. See Mishnah Berurah. Magen Avraham writes in the name of Sefer Chassidim that if one does not understand Lashon HaKodesh, it is preferable for him to pray in a language he understands. Sefer Chassidim, siman 588, makes clear that this applies only when he is Heaven-fearing and his sole purpose is to pray with concentration. Otherwise, he should pray in Lashon HaKodesh. The reason is that Lashon HaKodesh possesses many special qualities beyond all other languages. It is the language in which Hashem speaks with His prophets, as Ramban writes in Parashas Ki Sisa, and Chazal said that the world was created in Lashon HaKodesh, as it is written, "She shall be called woman, because she was taken from man." Furthermore, when the Men of the Great Assembly established the text of prayer, there were 120 elders, including several prophets. They deliberated over every blessing, its words, and its letter combinations, which contain many hidden and exalted secrets. When we recite these words in the language formulated by the Men of the Great Assembly, even if we do not know how to direct all the proper intentions, our prayer nevertheless ascends properly, because the words themselves exert their sanctity Above. This is not so when one prays in a foreign language.
