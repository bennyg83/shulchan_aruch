(4) There, Hagahah: and nowadays we do not repeat on account of lack of intent, etc. — and so Levush, Peri Chadash section 1, Rabbi Zalman section 1; and so Birkei Yosef, section 2, and these are his words: the view of Maran, who omitted the words of Tur mentioned — it appears that even now it is fitting to repeat and strengthen one's intent; but they did not practice according to his words. And so Maharikash in his glosses wrote the words of Tur as our teacher ruled — until here. And so Siddur Beit Yaakov in laws of Shemoneh Esrei, section 85, in the name of the Acharonim: thus we hold, and not like Shulchan Aruch — see there. And so Chasam Sofer, section 1; and he wrote there in Chasam Sofer: in any case b'dieved his prayer is prayer and his blessings are not in vain; only one must understand the severity of the matter and strive with all his strength at least to have intent in the first blessing — see there. And further see in our words above, siman 98, section 15; and examine well; and what Chayei Adam wrote, klal 24, section 2: it appears to him that if he remembered before he said "Elokei" at the end of the blessing, he should return to say "God of Avraham," etc. — see what Pischei Teshuvah wrote, section 2; and from the end of his words it appears he does not agree with him — see there.
