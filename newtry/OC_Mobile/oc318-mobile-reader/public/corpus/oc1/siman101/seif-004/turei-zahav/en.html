Because the ministering angels do not attend to other languages. The same applies to all other languages except the holy tongue. However, a congregation does not need an intermediary [angel].

So wrote Tosafos in the first chapter of [tractate] Shabbos. They raised the objection: are not even the thoughts of man known to the angels? Why then do they say [the angels] do not recognize the Aramaic language? Rather, they certainly understand everything; but this specific Aramaic language is unseemly in their eyes, whereas the other languages are not.
