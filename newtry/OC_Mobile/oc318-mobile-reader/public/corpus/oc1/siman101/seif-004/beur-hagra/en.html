Seif 4, "This applies in communal prayer," etc. Gemara there, 33a.
Some say, etc. As it states there, "his needs"; see also Avodah Zarah 8a. The reason is that the community does not require an intermediary.
Some say, etc. For they understand every language, except that this language is contemptible in their eyes. This is the meaning of "they do not attend to it." This resolves Tosafos' question on Shabbos 12b, s.v. "for they do not," where they wondered how Tosafos knew that the angels recognize thoughts in the heart. It appears that they learned it from the statement that three matters recall a person's sins, including close scrutiny of one's prayer; see Tosafos there. The Zohar, volume 1, 101b, however, states that they know nothing in this world except what is disclosed to them. Although the Gemara in Bava Metzia 87a says, "they knew," the point remains true even according to the Gemara. Likewise, "they do not recognize" means that they do not understand, and therefore it was not entrusted to them. This is the meaning of "they do not attend to it."
