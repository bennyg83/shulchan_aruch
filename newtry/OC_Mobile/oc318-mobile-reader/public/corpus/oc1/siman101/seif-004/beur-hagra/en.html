Gemara there, daf 33a.

As stated there regarding his personal needs. And [tractate] Avodah Zarah daf 8a. And the reason is that a congregation does not need an intermediary [angel].

For they recognize every language, only this Aramaic language is unseemly in their eyes. And this resolves the difficulty of Tosafos in [tractate] Shabbos daf 12b s.v. “she’ein” — they were astonished from where [it is known that] the angels recognize the thoughts of the heart. It appears they derived this from what is stated: “three things are mentioned” etc. — and see Tosafos there regarding iyun tefillah. But in the Zohar, vol. 1, daf 101b: “in this world they know only what” etc. And even though in the Gemara in [tractate] Bava Metzia daf 87a it is stated “they knew,” etc., this is nevertheless true even per the Gemara. And the language “do not recognize” means “do not understand.” Therefore [certain things] were not transmitted to them. And this is the meaning of “they do not attend.”
