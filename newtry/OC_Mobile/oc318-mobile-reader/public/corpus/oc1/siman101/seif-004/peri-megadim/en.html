Only. In Lashon HaKodesh. See Taz. According to this opinion, the ministering angels do not attend to any language other than Lashon HaKodesh. The community requires no intermediary, because Hashem Himself accepts its prayers and knows the thoughts of the heart. Some say this applies to a prayer established for the community even when it is not recited at the time the congregation is praying. Only an individual's personal prayer requires an intermediary, as it is written, "If there is an angel over him, an intermediary"; see siman 90, seif 9. This is the meaning of Tehillim 102, "He turned to the prayer of the destitute and did not despise their prayer." Even when recited by an individual, Hashem Himself turns to it without an intermediary when it is the community's established prayer, because He did not despise their prayer.
