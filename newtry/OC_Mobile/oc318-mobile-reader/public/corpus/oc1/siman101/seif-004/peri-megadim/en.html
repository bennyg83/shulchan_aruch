In the holy tongue — see the Taz. According to this view, for all languages other than the holy tongue the ministering angels do not attend, and a congregation does not require an intermediary — the Holy One, blessed be He, Himself accepts their prayer. Some say the prayer of a congregation — even outside the times when the congregation is praying, if it is only the individual’s prayer for his personal needs — requires an intermediary, as it says “if there is over him an angel advocate.” See siman 90, seif 9. And this is the meaning of Tehillim 102: “He turned to the prayer of the destitute, and did not despise their prayer” — even for an individual, the Holy One, blessed be He, Himself turns [to his prayer], not through an intermediary, when it is the prayer of the congregation, since “He did not despise their prayer.”
