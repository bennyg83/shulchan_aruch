(6)(13) One may pray in (14) any language he wishes. (7) This applies when praying with a congregation, but when praying alone he should not pray (15) except in the holy tongue. (8) Some say that this [restriction] applies only when one is petitioning for his personal needs — for example, one who prayed (16) for a sick person or for some trouble he has at home. But the fixed liturgy (17) established for the congregation, even an individual may recite it in any language. (9)(18) And some say that even an individual petitioning for his personal needs may petition in any language he wishes,(19) except for the Aramaic language.
