He wrote that it is better to pray in a language one understands if one does not understand the holy tongue. And so he wrote in Sefer Chassidim siman 588 and 788.

Because the ministering angels do not attend to other languages. But when a congregation prays, the Holy One, blessed be He, Himself accepts their prayer.

For he holds that the angels recognize every language, only they do not attend to Aramaic since it is unseemly in their eyes.
