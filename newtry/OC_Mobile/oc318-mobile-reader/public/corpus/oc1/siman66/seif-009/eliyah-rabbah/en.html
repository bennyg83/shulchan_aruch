[9] Ga'al Yisrael, etc. — and not Go'el Yisrael, which was enacted for the redemption that passed, as below at the end of the siman; but in prayer one must say Go'el Yisrael, for they are mercies, as stated Pesachim 117; and see below siman 236 sk 4. Taz wrote: even if one says Go'el Yisrael it is effective, for it is present tense and is used for past and future. Maharil wrote: he would stand in the morning for the eighteen blessings to Tehillim of El Elyon; and so wrote the writings of the Ari; and Etz Chayim wrote: then he goes from his place three steps and stands there until he wishes to pray the eighteen, and then goes those three steps by way of drawing near and presentation to the King — see siman 95 sk 2.
