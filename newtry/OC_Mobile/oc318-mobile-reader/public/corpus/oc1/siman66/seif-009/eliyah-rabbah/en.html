[9] Ga'al Yisrael, etc. And not 'Go'el Yisrael,' for it was instituted regarding the redemption that passed, as below at the end of the siman. But in prayer one must say 'Go'el Yisrael,' for it is a request for mercy, as stated in Pesachim 117. See below, siman 236, seif katan 4. The Taz wrote that even if one says 'Go'el Yisrael' it is effective, for it is present tense and is used for past and future. The Maharil wrote that he would stand for Shacharis Shemoneh Esrei at 'tehillos leKel Elyon,' and so wrote the writings of the Arizal. Eitz Chaim wrote that at that point one should walk three steps from his place and stand there until he wishes to pray Shemoneh Esrei, and then walk those three steps back by way of approaching and presenting oneself before the King. See siman 95, seif katan 2.
