(49) And Kedushah — and likewise Barekhu and Modim; and it seems we should limit [this] that one should bow at the beginning of [the Amidah] prayer when the congregation bows in Modim.

(50) Between redemption [and prayer] — some of the Acharonim wrote that on Shabbat it is permitted to answer Kaddish and Kedushah and all those similar to them mentioned above in siman 63 between redemption and prayer; and see below in siman 111.

(51) And what should he do — meaning if he has not yet heard Kedushah or Barekhu.

(52) He waits at "shira chadasha" — after he said "oseh fele" and so forth lekhatchilah; but b'dieved, as long as he has not yet said "Barukh attah Hashem," he may answer, and afterward he must begin again from "shira chadasha" or at minimum from "mi tzur Yisrael," which is the beginning of the matter, as Magen Avraham wrote in sk 3; and also he need not say the closing formula of a blessing adjacent to the closing of a blessing lekhatchilah [Mordekhai].
