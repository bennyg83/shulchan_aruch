Between redemption. Some later authorities wrote that on Shabbos it is permitted to answer Kaddish, Kedushah, and all similar matters mentioned above in seif 3 between redemption and prayer. See below, siman 111.
And how does he do, if he has not heard Hashem’s holiness or blessing:
He waits at Shirah Chadashah. After he said 'oseh fele.' All this is initially. But after the fact, as long as he has not said Baruch Atah Hashem, he may answer, and afterwards he must begin from the beginning of Shirah Chadashah, or at least from Tzur Yisrael, which is the beginning of the subject, like what the Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 3. Also, initially one must say a theme related to the conclusion adjacent to the conclusion, as stated by Ma'amar Mordechai.
