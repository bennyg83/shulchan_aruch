The verse. For if one interrupts to inquire after the honor of flesh and blood, all the more so for the honor of the Holy One, blessed is He. For this reason it seems to me that if he heard thunder, he should interrupt and recite the blessing. Magen Avraham.
Only. Meaning, only 'Modim anachnu lach.' At Barchu he says, 'Baruch Hashem hamevorach le'olam va'ed,' and for Amen yehei shemei rabba he says until 'yisbarach'; he should be silent and listen until the Amen after 'yisbarach,' and answer Amen. From 'tiskabel' onward he should not answer Amen, for it is only a custom. For the blessing over the Torah he answers 'Baruch Hashem hamevorach le'olam va'ed' and also Amen. In Kedushah he should say only 'Kadosh' and 'Baruch,' but not 'Yimloch.' Lechem Chamudos. From the gloss of Yad Ne'eman it implies that one answers also 'Yimloch,' for that belongs to Kedushah; but from the Tosefta in the first chapter of Berachos it implies as I wrote. Lechem Chamudos; Magen Avraham.
Hakadosh. Because it is the conclusion of the first three blessings, and Shome'a Tefillah is the conclusion of the middle blessings. The reason he did not count the Amen of the blessing Sim Shalom is that even an individual answers after the end of Shemoneh Esrei, and therefore he should not interrupt for it, since he can say it privately. Beis Yosef in the name of Mahari Abuhav. The Levush wrote that according to us, since we rule in siman 215 that an individual does not answer Amen after the conclusion of Shemoneh Esrei, he must interrupt during Krias Shema to answer Amen after Sim Shalom. But the Lechem Chamudos wrote that he should not interrupt, but rely on the Amen that he says at Oseh Shalom, and the Magen Avraham agreed with him; see there.
Supplications. One may likewise learn, all the more so, that he may interrupt during Pesukei D'Zimrah. Olas Tamid. It seems to me that ordinary Amens, which are not among those we wrote in seif katan 7 and 8, may be answered if he is standing in Pesukei D'Zimrah or in the blessings of Krias Shema. See siman 51, seif katan 3 in Magen Avraham, siman 59, seif 4 and the gloss, and this siman, seif 9, with Magen Avraham seif katan 14, and examine it carefully. But Baruch Hu u'varuch shemo seems to me forbidden to answer in Pesukei D'Zimrah or in the blessings of Krias Shema; see Magen Avraham, siman 124, seif katan 9.
