The verse. For if he stops to ask out of honor of a person — how much more so out of honor of the Holy One blessed be He. And what is the reason — in my humble opinion if he heard the sound of thunder he should interrupt and bless. Magen Avraham.

Only. Meaning 'modim anachnu lecha' only. And in Barekhu one says 'Barukh the Omnipresent haMevorakh le'olam va'ed' and Amen yehi shemei rabba until Yitbarakh. And he should be silent and listen until Amen after Yitbarakh and answer Amen. And from Titkabbel onward one does not answer Amen, for it is only custom. And on the blessing of the Torah one answers 'Barukh the Omnipresent haMevorakh le'olam va'ed' and also Amen. And in Kedushah one says only 'kadosh' and 'barukh' and does not say 'yimlokh' — Levush Chadash. And in the Hagahot of Yad Nachman it implies they also answer 'yimlokh,' for this pertains to Kedushah. But in Tosefta chapter 1 of Berakhot it implies as his words. Levush Chadash, Magen Avraham.

The holy. For it is the end of the first three and he hears prayer — it is the end of the middle [blessings]. And that which he did not consider the Amen of the blessing 'Shalom Rav' — because the individual also answers after the conclusion of the eighteen; therefore he has no need to interrupt on its account since he can say it as an individual — Beit Yosef in the name of Maharil. And Levush wrote: for us, who hold in siman 215 that the individual does not answer Amen after the conclusion of the eighteen, he must interrupt in Keriat Shema to answer Amen after 'Shalom Rav'; and Levush Chadash wrote he should not interrupt but rely on the Amen one says in 'Oseh shalom,' and Magen Avraham agreed with him in his name.

Supplications. And one should learn from 'all the more so' that one may interrupt in pesukei dezimra — Pri To'ar. And in my humble opinion the Amens of the world that are not among those written in notes 7 and 8 — one may answer if standing in pesukei dezimra or in the blessing of Keriat Shema. And see siman 91 note 3 in Magen Avraham and siman 59 se'if 4 and Hagahah. And siman 7 se'if 9 and Magen Avraham note 14 — investigate. But in Beit Yosef and Beit Shmuel — in my humble opinion it is forbidden to answer in pesukei dezimra or in the blessings of Keriat Shema — siman 124 note 9.
