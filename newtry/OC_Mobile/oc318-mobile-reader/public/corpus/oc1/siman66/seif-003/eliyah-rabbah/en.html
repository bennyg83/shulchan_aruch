[3] In the middle of the verse, etc. The Kesef Mishneh wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach that when he interrupts in the middle of a verse, this means where the matter has concluded; but in the middle of a matter, such as 'and you shall place these words of Mine upon your heart,' he should not. If he interrupted, he returns to the beginning of the verse. End quote. See above, siman 64, seif 2.
