[3] In the middle of a verse, etc. — he wrote in Kesef Mishneh in the name of Rabbenu Manoach that when one stops in the middle of a verse, it means where the matter concludes; but in the middle of a matter, such as 'and you shall place these words upon your heart' — no; and if he stopped he returns to the beginning of the verse — until here.
