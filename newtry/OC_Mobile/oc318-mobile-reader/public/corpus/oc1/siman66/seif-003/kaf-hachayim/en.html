(3) There — one asks peace of a distinguished person, etc. And Rabbenu Yehudah HeChasid wrote: specifically peace and nothing more; and all that he says 'asks' and 'responds' means with new faces — if he does not ask, hatred will result; and in synagogue, where we do not ask peace, God forbid — to respond or ask, and even words of Torah, not between sections nor in its blessings nor in pesukei dezimra. Thus I found in Hagahot Mordechai printed in Krakow. Keneset HaGedolah in Hagahat Tashbetz. Shulchan Arukh HaRav sk 3. Magen Avraham there. Eliyah Rabbah sk 2. Shulchan Arukh HaRav sk 2. Shulchan Arukh Tzedek 97b; and see above siman 51 sk 28. And Eliyah Rabbah wrote there it implies that if he is not engaged in blessings and pesukei dezimra they ask in synagogue; and so Kol Bo in Tashbetz siman 246 — the Rambam asks peace of any person before prayer, and even in synagogue — until here; and Machatzit HaShekel brought it sk 1; and so Shulchan Arukh HaRav there; and so Sha'arei Tefillah sk 1. And nevertheless one should be stringent where possible not to ask peace of a person in synagogue — see below siman 151 sk 8 what he wrote in the name of the Ari z"l, and above siman 1; investigate.
