The word — Taz and Magen Avraham letter 6. Nevertheless, as Magen Avraham wrote that it is forbidden to interrupt, even less than twenty-four [words] is forbidden, for amen is forbidden — and the matter in siman 124 deals with interrupting in silence; but in speech, even one word in tefillin — if he spoke he returns and blesses. And see what Rabbeinu Yehuda wrote on this: that between the sections [saying] "Barukh the Omnipresent haMevorakh" is not twenty-four [words] (for they hold four words — see siman 124), and we hold that one may say "Barukh the Omnipresent haMevorakh le'olam va'ed" — Magen Avraham derives from this letter 6; and I will explain there.
