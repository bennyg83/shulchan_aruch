The word. See Taz and see Magen Avraham, letter 6. However, wherever it is forbidden to interrupt, even less than the time of toch kedei dibbur is also forbidden, for Amen is forbidden. See siman 206; there it speaks of interrupting in silence, but with speech, even one word, regarding tefillin, if he spoke he goes back and blesses. See what the Beit Yosef wrote about this, that between the sections he wrote that 'Baruch Hashem hamevorach' is not the measure of toch kedei dibbur, for he holds it is four words; see siman 206. We rule that one may say, 'Baruch Hashem hamevorach le'olam va'ed.' See Magen Avraham on this in letter 6, and with God's help it will be explained there.
