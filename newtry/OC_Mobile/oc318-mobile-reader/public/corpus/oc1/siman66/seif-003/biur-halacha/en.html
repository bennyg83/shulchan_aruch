For Kaddish and Kedushah, etc. - See Kesef Mishneh at the end of chapter 10 of Hilchos Tefillah, that during a short berachah one may not interrupt even to answer "Yehei Shemei Rabba" or Kedushah. The reason is that, because of the interruption, he will be unable to connect the conclusion of the berachah to the mention of Hashem's name and kingship that he already said. Magen Gibborim cites this as practical halachah. It is clear that according to this, even in a long berachah, regarding which we say that one may interrupt, if the matter occurs after he has begun the conclusion, "Baruch Atah Hashem," he must take care not to interrupt for anything. Chayei Adam, in klal 5, remains uncertain whether after the fact he must return to the beginning. See there.
