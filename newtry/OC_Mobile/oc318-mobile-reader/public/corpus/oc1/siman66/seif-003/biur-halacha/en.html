For Kaddish and Kedushah, etc. — see Kol Bo at the end of chapter ten of prayer that in short blessings one may not interrupt in the middle even for Shema and Kedushah, the reason being that through the interruption he cannot connect the end of the blessing to the Name and kingship he said; they brought this law in Sefer Magen Giborim; and plainly therefore even in a long blessing where we say one interrupts — if this happened after he said the end of the blessing 'Barukh attah Hashem' — he should beware of interrupting for anything; and in Chayyei Adam, regarding the category of five letters, it remains in doubt b'dieved if he must return to the head — see there.
