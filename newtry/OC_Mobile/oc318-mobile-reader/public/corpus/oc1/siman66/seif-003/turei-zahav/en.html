The word Modim alone — meaning we give thanks to You; and it does not diminish, but rather Modim of the rabbis.

After the blessing HaKel HaKadosh — for it is the conclusion of the first three blessings, and after Shema Koleinu is the conclusion of the middle ones; but after the conclusion of the last three it is not considered, for this ascends as the individual blessing after the conclusion of the eighteen; therefore one does not interrupt so he may say this by himself [Beit Yosef in the name of Avudraham].
