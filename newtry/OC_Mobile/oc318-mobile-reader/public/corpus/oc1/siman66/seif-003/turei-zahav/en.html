After the berachah of HaKel HaKadosh. This is because it concludes the first three berachos, and after Shomea Tefillah because it concludes the middle berachos. After the conclusion of the final three, however, it is not considered a permissible point, because this individual passage is recited after the conclusion of the eighteen berachos.
