Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. Regarding what is written in siman 207, see there in Magen Avraham, seif katan 4, as to the distinction in this matter between the blessing of Krias Shema and the blessing of other mitzvos. In siman 104, in Magen Avraham, seif katan 5, regarding returning to the beginning because of this interruption, there is a distinction between Krias Shema and prayer; see there. This is the view of the Rif and Beis Yosef explained at the beginning of siman 65, unlike the Rama there. However, in practice, according to the Magen Avraham's conclusion there in seif katan 2, there is indeed a proper distinction in this between Krias Shema and prayer, for in Krias Shema this interruption is not considered one under compulsion; see there.
