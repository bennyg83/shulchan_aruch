Magen Avraham note 1—see above siman 207, see there, in Magen Avraham note 4, that there is a distinction in this between the blessing of Keriat Shema and the blessing of other mitzvot. And in siman 104, in Magen Avraham note 5, regarding returning to the beginning in this interruption—there is a distinction between Keriat Shema and prayer, see there; and this is the view of the Rif and Rabbeinu Yonah, brought in Rosh siman 65; not so the Rama there. However for the practical ruling, per Magen Avraham's determination there, note 2, there is properly a distinction in this between Keriat Shema and prayer—for in Keriat Shema this interruption is not considered under duress, see there.
