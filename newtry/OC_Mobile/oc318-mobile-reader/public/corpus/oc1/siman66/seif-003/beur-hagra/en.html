And likewise for Modim, etc. As stated there, 'if he can,' etc.; hence it is like Kedushah. Although Rashi and Tosafot explained that it is because of bowing, nevertheless they wrote in the dibbur beginning 'ad': however, initially, etc. This is what he wrote, 'and some say that Amen,' etc.
But not, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'ad,' etc., 'but it does not appear,' etc. This is what is written in the gloss, 'and likewise,' etc., also for this reason.
And some say, etc. Tosafot there, in the Yerushalmi there, etc.; and see Magen Avraham, seif katan 6. What he wrote, 'but in the Tosefta in the first chapter,' etc., he erred in explaining the Tosefta, for there it refers to the Kedushah of Yotzer, as written above, siman 59, seif 13. Nevertheless, the words of the Lechem Chamudos are primary. Also what he wrote, 'and it seems to me,' etc., is not primary, as written above in siman 56, for in the Rambam, Tur, and all the poskim it is explicit that one says until 'almaya.'
