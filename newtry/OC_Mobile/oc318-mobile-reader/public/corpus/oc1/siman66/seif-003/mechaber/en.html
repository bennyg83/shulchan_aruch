For Kaddish, Kedushah, and Barekhu, one interrupts [Shema or its blessings] even in the middle of a verse. And so too for Modim, but one should only say the word Modim alone.And likewise in Barekhu one should not say yitbarakh v'yishtabbach, etc. And there are those' who say that the Amen which we answer after the blessing of HaEl haKadosh and after shome'a tefillah has the status of Kedushah, and one may answer them during Keriat Shema; and that is the main [opinion]. And with all these, the poskim interrupt — and all the more so when saying supplications [responsum of Rashba].]:
