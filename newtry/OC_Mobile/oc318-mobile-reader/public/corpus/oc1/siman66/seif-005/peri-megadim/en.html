There is. See Taz. Krias Shema is according to the Torah; see siman 67 regarding whether 'Vayomer' is according to the Torah or not. See Magen Avraham, letter 8, that the custom is to interrupt. Between Ga'al Yisrael and prayer requires study. What the Mechaber wrote, 'a kohen,' see the Perishah, who explains the Rashba as referring even where there is a concern of blemish and there is no other kohen. But the Knesses HaGedolah, brought by the Peri Chadash, wrote that the Rashba speaks of a kohen whom they called simply, 'let a kohen stand,' and there is no other kohen to bless. Nevertheless, as a matter of law we rule that he interrupts and goes up, even a Yisrael, as we said. See siman 135, seif 5, and the Taz and Magen Avraham there; with God's help it will be explained there.
