To emes veyatziv. Since 'veyatziv' is also an expression of truth, one should not interrupt between 'emes' and 'veyatziv.' Beit Yosef, the Rosh, Lechem Chamudos. So too is written in the commentary of the Yad Moshe according to Kabbalah. But the opinion of the Shulchan Aruch is not so; see the end of this siman. Nevertheless, one should be careful about this.
