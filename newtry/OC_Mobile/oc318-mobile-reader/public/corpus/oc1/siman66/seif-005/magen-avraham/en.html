"To emet and v'yatziv." Since v'yatziv is also an expression of truth, one may not interrupt between emet and v'yatziv [Beit Yosef, Rosh, Levush Chadash]; and so too in the explanation of Hagahot Maimoniyot according to Kabbalah. But Shulchan Arukh's view is not so on this matter; nevertheless one should be careful about this.
