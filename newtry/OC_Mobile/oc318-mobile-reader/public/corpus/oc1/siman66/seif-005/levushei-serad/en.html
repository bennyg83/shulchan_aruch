Note 6—and amen means for another who said "Baruch Hashem haMevorach," etc., and all the more so in Barchu in prayer that he also says this amen; and what Peri Megadim posed as a difficulty—why should he say amen apart from the two amens in the gloss—for according to Lechem Mishneh, the amen of Kaddish is included in Kaddish, and likewise the amen of Barchu is like Barchu itself, and it is included in Barchu.
