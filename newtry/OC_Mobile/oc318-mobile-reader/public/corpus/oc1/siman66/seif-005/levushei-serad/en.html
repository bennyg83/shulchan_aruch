Seif katan 6. 'Le'olam va'ed' and also Amen; meaning after he said 'Baruch Hashem hamevorach,' etc. All the more so at Barchu of prayer he should also say this Amen. What the Peri Megadim asked, why should he say Amen besides the two Amens in the gloss, is not difficult at all. The Amen of Kaddish is included in Kaddish, and likewise the Amen of Barchu is like Barchu and is included in Barchu.
