These are [what are considered] "between the sections": between the first blessing and the second, between the second and Shema, between Shema and Vehayah im shamo'a, between Vehayah im shamo'a and Vayomer. But between Vayomer and emet v'yatziv one does not interrupt, because one does not interrupt between [the Name] the Omnipresent and emet; rather one says "ani the Omnipresent Elokeikhem' emet" and then interrupts as in the case of the middle of a section.
