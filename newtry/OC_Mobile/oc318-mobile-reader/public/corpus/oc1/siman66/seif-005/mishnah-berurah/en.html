Between the sections. The places between the sections in Maariv also have the same law as in Shacharis, as stated by the Magen Avraham in siman 236; see Biur Halacha.
He should not interrupt. The reason is that it is written, 'And Hashem Elokim is true'; therefore one should not interrupt there. It is more severe even than the middle of a section. See the Beur HaGra.
Not to interrupt. Even by mere delay.
Elokeichem emes. This implies that one need not say more. Some hold that since veyatziv is also an expression of truth, one should not interrupt between emes and veyatziv. The Beur HaGra agrees with the Shulchan Aruch, but initially it is proper to be careful.
