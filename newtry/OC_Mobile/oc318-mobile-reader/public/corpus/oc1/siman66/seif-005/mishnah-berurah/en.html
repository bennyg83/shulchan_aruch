(27) "Between the sections" — and between the sections of Ma'ariv as well, their law is like in Shacharit [Magen Avraham in siman 236]; and see Biur Halakhah.

(28) "One does not interrupt" — the reason is because it is written "and Hashem your God is truth"; therefore one may not interrupt in it, and it is more severe even than [interrupting in] the middle of a section; and see Biur HaGra.

(29) Not to interrupt — even for a mere pause:

(30) "Your God is truth" — implies that one need not say more; and there are those who hold that since "v'yatziv" is also an expression of truth, one may not interrupt between "emet" and "v'yatziv"; and in Biur HaGra he agrees with Shulchan Aruch, and it is good to be careful lekhatchilah.
