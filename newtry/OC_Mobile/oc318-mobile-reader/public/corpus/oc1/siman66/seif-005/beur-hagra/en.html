Seif 5, to Emes Veyatziv, etc. This is not precise; rather, only 'Emes,' as written in the Gemara, for it says, 'and Hashem Elokim is truth.' So too Rabbeinu Yonah wrote explicitly, and as written in seif 10 in the gloss and in seif 6 in the Shulchan Aruch. According to Rabbeinu Yonah's explanation of the Gemara there, he returns and says 'Emes,' etc. So too the Tur wrote. Although in the Mishnah it says, 'between Vayomer and Emes Veyatziv,' this is not precise, just as it says 'between Shema and Vehayah im Shamoa.'
But, etc. Rabbeinu Yonah and the Rosh, unlike the Rambam, who wrote that even between 'Elokeichem' and 'Emes' it is permitted, as in the middle of a section, for the language 'he should not interrupt' implies entirely. From this there appears to be proof to what I wrote in seif 1, that all the more so in the first verse. But as practical halacha this does not appear so, for it is no better than the middle of a section, since the main reason is that it is one verse; and wherever it is permitted to interrupt in the middle of a section, it is permitted even in the middle of a verse, as written above in seif 1.
