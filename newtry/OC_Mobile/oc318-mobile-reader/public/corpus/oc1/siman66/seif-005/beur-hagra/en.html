(א) Section 5: "Emet v'yatziv," etc. Their view is only "emet" alone, as stated in the Gemara: it is written "Hashem Elohim, truth," and so too in Targum Yonatan explicitly, and as stated in siman 61 in the gloss and in siman 66 in the Shulchan Aruch; and as Targum Yonatan explains in the Gemara there he repeats and says "emet," etc., and so too in the Tur. And even though in the Mishnah it says between "vayomer" and "emet v'yatziv" one does not interrupt—such as between Shema and V'haya Im Sh'moa.

(ב) Rather, etc. Targum Yonatan and Rosh, unlike the Rambam who wrote that even between "Eloheichem" and "emet" it is permitted as in the middle of a section—for the wording "he may not interrupt" is not to be interrupted entirely; and from this there appears to be proof for what is stated in seif 1, all the more so in the first verse. But for practical halachah it does not appear so, for it is not preferable to the middle of the section—for the primary reason is because it is one verse; and wherever it is permitted to interrupt in the middle of the section, even in the middle of the verse it is permitted, as stated in seif 1.
