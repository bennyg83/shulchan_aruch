A kohen who was reciting Krias Shema, etc. Some say that he should not interrupt, and the halachah follows this view. Rather, they should call another person, for this is not a disgrace to the Torah when he informs them that he is reciting Krias Shema. Therefore, they may even call a Yisrael in his place when no other kohen is present, as stated in Responsa Rashba, siman 465. Nevertheless, one who is concerned for the honor of the Torah may interrupt, as stated by my teacher in Bach, and the custom is to interrupt, as stated by Maharam MiRothenburg. In any event, he should not read along with the chazzan from the Torah at all, and he may rely on the words of the Zohar, Parashas Vayakhel, page 770. Some write that one may also interrupt during Krias Shema to answer 'Baruch Hashem hamevorach' and Amen after one who reads from the Torah, as stated by Lechem Chamudos. But when one prayed alone, Amen is not answered, because the custom is not to answer Amen after the blessing Boneh Yerushalayim.
