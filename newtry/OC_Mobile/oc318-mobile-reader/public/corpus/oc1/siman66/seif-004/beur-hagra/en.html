Seif 4, there are, etc. As written in the previous seif.
And there are, etc. They said to give him precedence only because of the honor of the priesthood, and this person, who does not know, is not a degradation of his honor. Although it is a mitzvah to read from the Torah, they said to interrupt only for tefillin, as written above in seif 2. Rashba, siman 185, and in accordance with the view of Tosafot cited above.
