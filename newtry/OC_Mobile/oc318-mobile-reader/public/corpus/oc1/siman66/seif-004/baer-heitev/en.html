Interrupts. And Israel ascends in his place — and in Levush wrote they are accustomed to interrupt and one should not change the custom because of the dispute; nevertheless he should not read with the prayer leader at all — and so Keneset HaGedolah: he ascends, and all the more he should not interrupt to tell the prayer leader to make Mi sheBerakh. And Magen Avraham wrote: it should be said that even if he delayed in order to finish all of it, he need not return to the head, for this is not coercion — see siman 65. And so Beit Chadash and Levush Chadash agreed that he ascends. And Taz wrote: in Keriat Shema, which is from the Torah, he should not interrupt; but in its blessings, which are rabbinic, he interrupts. And from this in pesukei dezimra too he interrupts; and Pri To'ar wrote: according to those who forbid in Keriat Shema, likewise it is forbidden in its blessings — only in pesukei dezimra one may be lenient. And see siman 135 note 6 in Magen Avraham. And in Keneset HaGedolah he distinguishes: if they call a kohen generically — need not interrupt; but if they called him by name, even if there are other kohanim — interrupt — in his name. And in Hilchot Ketanot part 1 siman 24 he distinguishes: if there is no kohen except him — he ascends out of honor of the congregation — in his name. And Pri Chadash ruled like Rashba that he does not ascend even if called by name and there is no other in the congregation — in his name.
