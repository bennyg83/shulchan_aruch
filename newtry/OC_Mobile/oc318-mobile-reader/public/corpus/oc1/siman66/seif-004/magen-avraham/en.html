"That he does not interrupt." And an Israelite goes up in his place; and it is not comparable to laying tefillin, for tefillin are required for Keriat Shema (Rashba siman 185). And it is written in Levush that the custom is to interrupt, and one should not change the custom because of the dispute; and in any case he should not read with the chazzan at all, and should rely on the plain meaning of the Zohar as written in siman 141 se'if 2 (Levush Chadash); and so too regarding a kohen who goes up. And all the more so he should not interrupt to tell the chazzan to say Mi sheBeirakh; and it seems to me that even if he waited long enough to complete the whole [paragraph], he need not return to the beginning, for this is not [considered] coercion — see beginning of siman 65.
