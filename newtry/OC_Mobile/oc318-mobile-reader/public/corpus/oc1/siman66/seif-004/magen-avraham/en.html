That he does not interrupt. A Yisrael goes up in his place. It is not comparable to putting on tefillin, for tefillin are needed for Krias Shema. Rashba, siman 185. The Levush wrote that the practice is to interrupt, and one should not change the custom because of dispute. Nevertheless, he should not read along with the chazzan at all, and should rely on the simple meaning of the Zohar, as written in siman 141, seif 2. Lechem Chamudos. So too wrote Knesses HaGedolah, that he should go up. All the more so he should not interrupt to tell the chazzan to say a Mi Shebeirach. It seems to me that even if he delayed long enough to complete all of it, he need not return to the beginning, for this is not considered duress. See the beginning of siman 65.
