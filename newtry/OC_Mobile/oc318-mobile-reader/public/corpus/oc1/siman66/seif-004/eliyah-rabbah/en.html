[4] To lay them and bless, etc. — and I saw in the gloss of Shulchan Arukh thus his wording, and it seems to me to emend: he should not bless on them, for below in this siman se'if 8 he wrote one should not bless on them; and one should not distinguish and say here it deals specifically between sections, for Beit Yosef wrote on the words of Shibulei HaLeket that it seemed to him one should not bless on them even between sections; therefore it seems to me he should not bless, neither on tzitzit nor on tefillin — until here. And this is Levush's view, who ruled not like Rama; and apparently it is wondrous, for below siman 80 Levush ruled like Rama; and perhaps he holds one may distinguish between forgetfulness and coercion — so it seems to me. But I wonder they omitted the words of Bedek HaBayit to Beit Yosef, who wrote on Shibulei HaLeket thus his wording, and so wrote our rabbi in siman 80, and wrote he should bless on them — and these are Rosh's words in responsa; and one must pass the pen over 'and it seems to me he should not bless on them, etc., until tefillin' — until here his wording. Behold explicitly Beit Yosef himself erased this and ruled to bless, as Shulchan Arukh wrote. According to this, the view of yeish omrim not to bless is entirely void, for we do not find anyone who holds thus except Beit Yosef, and I already wrote Beit Yosef himself erased it; and there is no doubt in my eyes that if Rama and Levush saw Bedek HaBayit's words they would not have brought the view of yeish omrim at all; and greater than this I wonder at them how it is possible to interpret Shibulei HaLeket's words that one not bless on them between sections — behold Shibulei HaLeket page 4 wrote that even between redemption and prayer one blesses on tzitzit and tefillin as Tosafot wrote in the name of R' Yitzchak bar Yehudah in the chapter 'he was reading' — see there; and so wrote Rokeach siman 321; and all the more and kal vachomer one blesses between sections; and in truth Nachalat Tzvi ruled that even on tzitzit one blesses between sections, since per R' Yehudai even between redemption and prayer they bless; and so ruled in Piskei Tosafot. And so I would wish to establish the law, since so implies in Bedek HaBayit that I brought; and further that even Tosafot Berakhot 14 did not find they disagree on blessing of tzitzit except between redemption and prayer, but between sections or even in the middle of a section one may say they agree. But I found in responsum of R' Meir of Rothenburg siman 73 that he wrote even between sections one may not interrupt for the mitzvah of tzitzit; and it seems his intention is for the blessing of tzitzit, but for wrapping, for everyone it is permitted; and so is explained in Sefer Zichron to the Gaon R' Yehudah HaKohen, and not like Bach in siman 80 who forbids even to wrap between sections. Therefore it seems to me for practical law: one blesses on tefillin between sections but not on tzitzit, as Rama ruled. Again Magen Avraham was printed and he also brings the words of Darkei Moshe in siman 54, who wrote in the name of Or Zaru'a and R' Eliezer of Prague that one should not bless on tallit; and if he remembered in the middle of a section it seems to me he should finish the section and afterward lay tefillin with a blessing, for since it is possible to wait one should be concerned for R' Meir of Kutzi that Tosafot brought there; and so implies in Olah Tamid sk 4. And it seems to me there is a scribal error in his words in sk 17, and one must say 'and then he can lay them with a blessing' — see there; investigate. And it implies the law is likewise in coercion, not like Tosafot who distinguish between forgetfulness and coercion; and regarding wrapping tzitzit he should not wait, but it seems to me he should wrap immediately without a blessing; and all this in Keriat Shema and its blessings before Keriat Shema, but the blessing after Keriat Shema will be explained in se'if 8.
