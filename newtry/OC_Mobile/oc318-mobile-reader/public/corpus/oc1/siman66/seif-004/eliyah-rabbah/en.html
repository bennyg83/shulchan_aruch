[7] That he interrupts, etc. The Levush wrote that this is the practice, implying even if he is not a Kohen. This also appears to be the view of the Kenesses HaGedolah, unlike the Shulchan Aruch; and so ruled the Bach and the Lechem Chamudos there. However, the Lechem Chamudos concluded that it appears he should not read with the chazzan at all. The Magen Avraham wrote: all the more so he should not interrupt to tell the chazzan to say Mi Shebeirach. It seems to me that even if he delayed long enough to finish all of it, he need not return to the beginning, for this is not called compulsion. End quote. I say that even if it were compulsion, in any event it is not compulsion of the person, and he himself rules in siman 65 that he need not return; see there. Regarding Mi Shebeirach, it seems to me that perhaps it can be compared to asking and returning peace because of honor or fear.
