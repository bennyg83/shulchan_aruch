[4] To put them on and bless, etc. I saw in a gloss on the Shulchan Aruch as follows: It seems to me that one should emend and read, 'and he should not bless over them,' for below in this siman, seif 8, he wrote that he should not bless over them. One cannot distinguish and say that here it speaks only between the sections, for the Beis Yosef wrote on the words of Shibbolei HaLeket that it seems to him that one should not bless over them even between the sections. Therefore it seems to me that he should not bless, neither on tzitzis nor on tefillin. End quote. This is like the view of the Levush, who ruled unlike the Rama. At first glance this is surprising, for below, siman 80, the Levush ruled like the Rama. Perhaps he holds a distinction between one who forgot and one who was compelled; so it seems to me. But I wonder that they missed the words of the Bedek HaBayis of the Beis Yosef, who wrote on the words of Shibbolei HaLeket: So too our teacher wrote in siman 80, and wrote that he should bless over them. These are the words of the Rosh in responsa, and one must strike the pen through 'and it seems to me that he should not bless over them,' etc., until 'to prayer.' End quote. It is explicit that the Beis Yosef himself deleted it and ruled to bless, as he wrote in the Shulchan Aruch. Accordingly, the view of the 'some say' that one should not bless is entirely nullified, for we found no one who holds this except the Beis Yosef, and I have written that the Beis Yosef himself deleted it. I have no doubt that had the Rama and Levush seen the Bedek HaBayis, they would not have brought the view of 'some say' at all. Even more, I wonder how one can explain Shibbolei HaLeket as saying not to bless over them between sections, for Shibbolei HaLeket, page 4, wrote that even between redemption and prayer one blesses over tzitzis and tefillin, as Tosafot wrote in the name of Rabbi Yitzchak bar Yehudah in the chapter Hayah Korei; see there. So too the Rokeach wrote, siman 321, and all the more so one blesses between the sections. In truth, Nachalas Tzvi ruled that one blesses even over tzitzis between sections, since according to Ri one blesses even between redemption and prayer, and so ruled Piskei Tosafot. I would have wanted to establish the halacha this way, since so it implies in the Bedek HaBayis I cited. Also, even in Tosafot, Berachos 14a, we did not find that they disagree regarding the blessing on tzitzis except between redemption and prayer; but between sections, or even in the middle of a section, one may say that they agree. However, I found in Responsa Maharam of Rothenburg, siman 73, that even between sections one should not interrupt for the mitzvah of tzitzis. It appears that his intent is the blessings of tzitzis, but as for wrapping, everyone agrees it is permitted. This is also explained in Sefer Zikaron of the gaon Rabbi Y. HaKohen, unlike the Bach in siman 80, who forbids even wrapping between the sections. Therefore practical halacha appears to me: one blesses over tefillin between sections, but not over tzitzis, as the Rama ruled. Afterward the Magen Avraham was printed, and he too brought the words of the Darkei Moshe in siman 54, who wrote in the name of Or Zarua and Rabbi Avraham of Prague that one should not bless over a tallis. If he remembered in the middle of a section, it seems to me that he should finish the section and afterward put on tefillin with a blessing, since it is possible to wait and one should be concerned for Maharam of Coucy, whom Tosafot cited there. So too it implies in Olas Tamid, seif katan 4. It seems to me that there is a textual error in his words in seif katan 17, and it should read: then he may put them on with a blessing; see there and examine it carefully. It implies that this is also the law where he was compelled, unlike Piskei Tosafot, who distinguishes between forgetting and compulsion. Regarding wrapping in tzitzis, he should not wait; rather, it seems to me that he should wrap immediately without a blessing. All this is during Krias Shema and its blessings that precede Krias Shema, but the law regarding the blessing after Krias Shema will be explained in seif 8.
