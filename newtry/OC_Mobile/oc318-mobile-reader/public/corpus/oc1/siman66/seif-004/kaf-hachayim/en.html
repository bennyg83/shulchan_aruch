(4) There — one asks peace of a distinguished person, etc. And in Sefer HaChinukh for Ra'ah, parashat Va'etchanan, he wrote: one whose fellow we have not seen that he objects at all — he should not interrupt even between sections. Magen Avraham there. Eliyah Rabbah there. Taz sk 1. Siddur Beit Yosef in laws of interruption sk 7. And in our time people are not particular about asking peace, and especially if they know he is engaged in Keriat Shema and prayer — therefore one should not interrupt at all, neither in the middle nor between sections, for any person, even not his father and teacher, and even if he is not praying in synagogue.
