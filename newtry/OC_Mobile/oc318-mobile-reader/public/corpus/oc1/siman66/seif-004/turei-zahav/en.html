Some say that one interrupts — this is Baal HaManhig whom the Tur brought. And the second view is Rashba, and Beit Yosef ruled like him, for he is a clear rabbi; and Mahari Weil wrote that one concerned for the honor of Torah may rely and interrupt; and in my humble opinion, in Keriat Shema itself, which is deoraita, one does not interrupt; but in its blessings which are derabbanan there is to rule like Baal HaManhig since the Tur brings him and he is also a clear rabbi for us — therefore one interrupts to read from the Torah.
