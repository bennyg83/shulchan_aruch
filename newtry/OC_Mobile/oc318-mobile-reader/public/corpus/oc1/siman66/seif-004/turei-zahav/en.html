Some say that he interrupts. This is the opinion of the Baal HaManhig cited by the Tur. The second opinion is that of the Rashba, and Beis Yosef rules like him because he is an exceptionally authoritative scholar. My father-in-law, of blessed memory, writes that one who is concerned for the honor of the Torah may rely on the first opinion and interrupt. In my humble opinion, during Krias Shema itself, which is D'Oraisa, he should not interrupt. During its berachos, however, which are D'Rabbanan, one should rule like the Baal HaManhig, since the Tur cites him and he too is an exceptionally authoritative scholar for us. He should therefore interrupt to read from the Torah.
