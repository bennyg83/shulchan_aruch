(sk 4) And some say, etc. — and Levush Chadash wrote that Beit Yosef already erased [it] in Bedek HaBayit; meaning: how did Beit Yosef [write that] Rama in the name of Maharil [held] a law that Maharil himself retracted?

Rather, "obligation of the garment" is not precise. For "obligation of the garment" is per the view that even if it lies in a box and he does not wear it, one must attach tzitzit to it — and we do not hold thus; rather it is the obligation of the person — meaning, only if he wears it is he obligated. Rather "obligation of the garment" means precisely: if he wears a four-cornered garment he is obligated — unlike tefillin, which he is obligated to put on. And similarly wrote above siman 17.
