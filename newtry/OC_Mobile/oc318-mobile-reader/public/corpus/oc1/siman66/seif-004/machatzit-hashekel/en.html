(Seif katan 4) And some say, etc. The Lechem Chamudos wrote that the Beis Yosef already deleted it. Meaning, how did the Rama bring in the name of the Beis Yosef a law from which the Beis Yosef himself retracted?
But an obligation of the garment is not precise. For 'obligation of the garment' refers to the opinion that even if it is lying in a box and he is not wearing it, he must put tzitzis on it, and we do not rule that way. Rather, it is an obligation upon the person, meaning specifically if he wears it he is obligated. Rather, 'obligation of the garment' means that only if he wears a four-cornered garment is he obligated, unlike tefillin, which he is obligated to put on. Something similar was written above in siman 17.
