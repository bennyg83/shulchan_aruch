He has not fulfilled the eighteen[-blessing prayer] mitzvah as enacted. But by [reading] Keriat Shema he has fulfilled [Tur] — see siman 62 and what is written siman 113.

"And he has no leisure." Requires investigation — who permitted him to cut off [prayer] because he has no leisure? And in truth it implies in Rokeach that one who has no leisure reads Keriat Shema alone without the blessings, and afterward when he has leisure he reads the blessings with Keriat Shema and prays — and so in the Yerushalmi that he brought proof from there that he read Keriat Shema a second time after three hours; and so above at the end of siman 46, see there, and in Beit Yosef; and so siman 60 se'if 2. Rama's words require investigation.
