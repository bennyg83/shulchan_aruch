He did not fulfill the mitzvah in its proper manner. But he did fulfill the obligation of Krias Shema. Tur. See siman 62 and what is written in siman 113.
And he has no time. This requires study: who permitted him to interrupt because he has no time? In truth, it appears in the Rokeach that one who has no time should read Krias Shema alone without the blessings, and afterward, when he has time, he should recite the blessings with Krias Shema and pray. So too is it in the Yerushalmi, from which he brought proof, where he read Krias Shema a second time after three hours. So too above, at the end of siman 46; see there, and in Beit Yosef. So too siman 60, seif 2. The words of the Rama require study.
