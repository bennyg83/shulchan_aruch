10) There: And certainly a king, etc. This applies even to a Jewish king, for it is written, "You shall surely place a king over yourself," teaching that his fear must be upon you. Only concerning prayer does the Mishnah say that one does not respond even if a Jewish leader greets him. During Krias Shema one may even initiate a greeting because of fear, and certainly may return the king's greeting. Bach; Ateres Zekenim, paragraph 1; Olas Tamid, paragraph 2. See Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 1.
