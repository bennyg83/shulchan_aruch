[10] And so is the practice, etc. — and Levush directed well that he omitted this, for in Ra'avan's responsum (part 1 siman 6) he disagrees that also in congregation one should not answer; and so wrote Shelah; and so I found in Maggid Meisharim that the Maggid commanded Beit Yosef to beware not to answer; and in Piskei Tosafot it implies even on Shabbat one does not answer — see below siman 111. And plainly that even for Rama one answers only before 'Adonai sefatai tiftach,' but afterward one may not interrupt at all, for that is the beginning of prayer; and so wrote Lechem Chamudot page 6.
