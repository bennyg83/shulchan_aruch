"He does not say Amen." Shulchan Gavoah wrote that one may not interrupt between redemption and prayer even out of fear.

"And some say that they answer," etc. — and the meticulous wait at "tzur Yisrael" in order to answer Amen; and in Sha'arei Teshuvah it implies one should wait at "shira chadasha." And it seems to me that if he wishes he may intend to finish together with the chazzan, and then he is not obligated to answer Amen — see siman 59 se'if 4.
