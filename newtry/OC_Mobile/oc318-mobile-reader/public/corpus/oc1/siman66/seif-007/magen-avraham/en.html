He does not say Amen. The Shiltei Giborim wrote that one may not interrupt between redemption and prayer even because of fear.
And some say that one answers, etc. The meticulous wait at 'Tzur Yisrael' in order to answer Amen. In Seder HaYom it implies that one should wait at 'shirah chadashah.' It seems to me that if one wishes, he can intend to finish together with the shaliach tzibbur, and then he is not obligated to answer Amen. See siman 59, seif 4.
