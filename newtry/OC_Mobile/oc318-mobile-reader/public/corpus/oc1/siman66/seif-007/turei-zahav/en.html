After Ga'al Yisrael. But in prayer one says Go'el Yisrael as below siman 156, and this is taught explicitly in chapter Eilu Devarim in Keriat Shema and Hallel — Ga'al Yisrael, and in prayer Go'el Yisrael, because they are supplications — meaning one prays for the future. And veneheranah — in my youth there was an old doctor named Maharal Shlomo who went at the end of his days to Eretz Yisrael; he asked the Gaon Maharal Meir z"l in Lublin about what is written in Maariv Melekh tzur Yisrael u'go'alo, which is against the Talmud, and he had no answer; and he said that when he came home he would settle this. And so I asked after him Mahari Weil z"l and many rabbis and there was no answer from their mouths. And thus one may say from the Tur's words that he brought here in the name of the Yerushalmi — one must mention in emet veyatziv the Exodus from Egypt, etc., and tzur Yisrael u'go'alo, etc. — end quote. But behold in emet veyatziv he said Ga'al only. And it seems that in truth Go'el Yisrael is fine also in Keriat Shema, for it is past tense, and every past tense is used whether language of past or future, as Rashi explained at the beginning of Iyov, etc. And this is proven here too: for in prayer one must use future tense and nevertheless past tense Go'el works — if so, here too past tense works. And in the Gemara it comes only to exclude that in prayer one should not say Ga'al Yisrael, but the reverse also works — and this is the main point.
