(sk 7) The Amens that they answer, etc. — [this is] the conclusion of the first three [blessings]. For the eighteen[-blessing] prayer is divided into three matters: the first three blessings arrange praise of the Omnipresent; the middle ones are requests for needs; the last three are like a servant who received a reward from his master and praises him for it.

Because this — also the individual, etc. — according to Rambam in siman 215, that thus one answers Amen on one's own blessing; therefore we hold like him.

And who knows, etc. — for even as the poskim hold, to answer these two Amens — we ruled like the Yerushalmi. Nevertheless, since the reason for these two Amens is not explained in the Yerushalmi, it follows that Maharil wrote a reason as stated above; and per Maharil's reason it would be the same for Amen after the conclusion of the eighteen blessings. Perhaps the Yerushalmi has another reason for considering these two Amens important, a reason that does not apply to Amen after the conclusion of the eighteen blessings; therefore out of doubt one should not interrupt.
