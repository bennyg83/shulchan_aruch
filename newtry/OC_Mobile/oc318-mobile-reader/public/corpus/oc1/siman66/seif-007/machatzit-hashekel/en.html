(Seif katan 7) That Amen, etc. The conclusion of the first three blessings. Shemoneh Esrei is divided into three matters: the first three blessings arrange the praise of the Omnipresent; the middle blessings are requests for needs; and the last three blessings are like a servant who received a reward from his master and praises him for it.
Because this, even the individual, etc. According to the Rambam in siman 215, in such a case one answers Amen after his own blessing; therefore we rule like him.
And who knows, etc. For even according to the poskim who say to answer these two Amens, that is because they ruled like the Yerushalmi. Nevertheless, since the Yerushalmi did not explain the reason for these two Amens, although Mahari Abuhav wrote a reason as above, and according to his reason the same should apply to the Amen after the conclusion of the eighteen blessings, perhaps the Yerushalmi has another reason for the importance of these two Amens, and that reason does not apply to Amen after the conclusion of the eighteen blessings. Therefore, because of doubt, one should not interrupt.
