(32) One does not say Amen — neither after oneself nor after the chazzan.

(33) After "Ga'al Yisrael" — past tense because it refers to the Exodus from Egypt; but in the Amidah one says "Go'el Yisrael" because they are mercies and one prays for the future. And in Ma'ariv, where they are accustomed to say "Barukh Atah the Omnipresent Melekh tzur Yisrael u'go'alo," Taz pushed very hard to justify it, and in Degel Machaneh Ephraim his difficulty was resolved; nevertheless, because of this difficulty, people of deeds adopted the practice to say also in Ma'ariv "Ga'al Yisrael."

(34) And some say — this "some say" holds that even after his own blessing one answers Amen, since it is the conclusion of the order of blessings of Keriat Shema and it is not considered an interruption between redemption and prayer; and therefore Rama said that in one respect our custom is also like this "some say," that we do not consider it an interruption between redemption and prayer, and therefore the practice is to answer Amen after the chazzan; but if one prays alone, our practice is not to answer Amen, as below in siman 215, see there.

(35) "To answer" etc. — and specifically if he has not yet begun the verse "the Omnipresent, open my lips," for from there onward it is like prayer. Some are meticulous and want to fulfill according to all opinions, and they wait at "tzur Yisrael" or at "shira chadasha" in order to answer Amen after the chazzan; and all the Acharonim wrote that they are not acting properly, for he is in the middle of the section of "emet v'yatziv," and one may not answer any Amen except the two mentioned Amens in siman 63 in the gloss. Also lekhatchilah it is proper to begin the Amidah together with the chazzan and the congregation; but there is another counsel, and it is correct to do it in order to fulfill according to all opinions: he should intend to finish together with the chazzan, and then he is not obligated to answer Amen; or he should begin Keriat Shema slightly before the chazzan — namely the verse "the Omnipresent, open my lips" etc. — for in this slight [advance] there is certainly no concern, for his precedence is not noticeable at all.

(36) "After the chazzan" — meaning: if it happened that he finished before the chazzan and has not yet begun the Amidah, and in the meantime the chazzan finished — but he need not wait on this account according to all opinions.
