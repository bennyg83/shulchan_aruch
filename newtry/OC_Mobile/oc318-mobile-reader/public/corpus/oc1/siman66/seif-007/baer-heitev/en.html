The Taz wrote: I asked my teacher and father-in-law, of blessed memory, and many other geonim, about what we say in Ma'ariv, 'Blessed are You, Hashem, King, Rock of Yisrael and its Redeemer,' which is against the Gemara in the chapter Arvei Pesachim, that in Krias Shema and Hallel one says 'Ga'al Yisrael,' etc.; see there, for he labored greatly to resolve it. The Shiltei Giborim wrote that one should not interrupt between redemption and prayer even because of fear.
Amen. The meticulous ones wait at 'Tzur Yisrael' in order to answer Amen. In seif 9 it implies that one should wait at 'Shirah chadashah.' The Magen Avraham wrote: it seems to me that if he wishes, he can intend to finish together with the shaliach tzibbur, and then he is not obligated to answer Amen. See siman 59, seif 4. The Re'em, volume 1, siman 6, concluded that one should not answer Amen after 'Ga'al Yisrael,' even after the shaliach tzibbur; see Olas Tamid.
