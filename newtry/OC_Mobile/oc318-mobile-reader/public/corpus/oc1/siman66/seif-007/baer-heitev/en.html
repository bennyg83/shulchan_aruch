Taz wrote: I asked my teacher of blessed memory and also many geniuses on what is written in Maariv in Eretz Yisrael 'Melekh tzur Yisrael uGo'alo' — which is against the Gemara chapter 'Ma'amad' that in Keriat Shema and Hallel [one says] 'Ga'al Yisrael,' etc. — in his name it is very strained to reconcile. He wrote in Seder HaGadolah that one may not interrupt between redemption and prayer even out of fear.

Amen. The meticulous wait at 'tzur Yisrael' in order to answer Amen; and in se'if 9 it implies one should wait at 'Shirah Chadashah'; and Magen Avraham wrote: in my humble opinion if he wishes he may intend to finish with the prayer leader, and then he is not obligated to answer Amen. See siman 59 se'if 4; and Ramah part 1 siman 6 decided one does not answer Amen after 'Ga'al Yisrael' even after the prayer leader — see Pri To'ar.
