And emet ve-emunah — evening Taz; and in Tur only emet ve-emunah; and Taz added that in the morning they say emet veyatziv (translation of "emes yatziv") and "kayam" and "He restored to us the deposit in tranquility" — therefore it is fit to engage in Torah all day; a bar nefesh deposits in his fellow's hand, a bar nefesh returns to him broken and so forth, and the Holy One blessed be He returns in tranquility and rest — see Levush.
