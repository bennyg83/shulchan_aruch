And emes. And emunah in the evening; see Taz. In the Tur it says only 'emes ve'emunah,' and the Taz added that in the morning we say 'emes veyatziv,' meaning that what was last night is now firm and enduring, and that He returned the deposit to us in rest. Therefore it is fitting to occupy oneself with Torah all day. A mortal deposits something in the hand of his fellow mortal, and the mortal returns it worn, etc.; but the Holy One, blessed is He, returns it with rest and calm. See Levush.
