(7) There—and in the middle one asks peace, etc. The Ritva wrote in Yoma 19—that to separate oneself from a prohibition that has no remedy, it is permitted to hint, and even to interrupt; for it is not less than interrupting out of fear and honor, for the honor of Heaven takes precedence.
