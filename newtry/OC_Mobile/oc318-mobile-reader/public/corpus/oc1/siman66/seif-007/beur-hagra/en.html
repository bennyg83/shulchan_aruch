Seif 7, he does not, etc. Meaning, although one must answer even after his own blessing, as written in siman 51, nevertheless here he should not answer even after the blessing of the shaliach tzibbur, because of interruption, according to the Zohar, as written there.
But if, etc. Meaning, according to his approach, as Tosafot wrote that one answers only after Boneh Yerushalayim, as stated there.
