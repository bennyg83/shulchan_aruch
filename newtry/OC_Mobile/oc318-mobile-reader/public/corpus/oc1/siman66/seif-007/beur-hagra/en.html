(א) Section 7: One does not, etc.—meaning, even though regarding one's own blessing there is [reason] to answer, as stated in siman 51—nevertheless here one does not answer, even after the prayer leader's blessing, because of the interruption per the Zohar, as stated there.

(ב) But if, etc.—meaning, according to his view, and as Tosafot wrote that one does not answer except after Boneh Yerushalayim, as stated above.
