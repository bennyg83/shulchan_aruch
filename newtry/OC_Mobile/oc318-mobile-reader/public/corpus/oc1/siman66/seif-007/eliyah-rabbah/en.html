[7] One interrupts, etc. — Levush wrote: so is the practice — it implies even if he is not a kohen; and so appears Keneset HaGedolah's view, and not like Shulchan Arukh; and so ruled Bach and Lechem Chamudot there; however Lechem Chamudot concluded it appears he should not read with the prayer leader at all; and Magen Avraham wrote: all the more he should not interrupt to tell the prayer leader to say Mi sheBerakh. And it seems to me: even if he delayed in order to finish all of it he need not return to the head, for this is not coercion — until here. And I say: even if it were coercion, in any case it is not coercion of a person, and he himself rules in siman 65 that one need not return — see there. And regarding Mi sheBerakh it seems to me one may compare it to asking and responding peace out of honor and fear.
