'u'manach,' etc. The view of Ri is that he puts on and blesses both tefillin and tallis, from what they wrote, 'and the Maharam of Coucy,' etc. The view of Tosafot is that with tefillin he puts them on and blesses, but with a tallis he should not wrap. This is also the view of the Rosh and the Tur. The view of Maharam of Coucy is that for both tefillin and tallis he wraps or puts them on without a blessing. But in the Beis Yosef he was uncertain about the Rosh's view, that perhaps he too agrees with Maharam of Coucy that one should not bless on tefillin, while regarding a tallis he holds like Tosafot that one should not wrap at all. He wrote that this applies specifically between redemption and prayer, but before 'Ga'al Yisrael' it is certainly permitted to wrap in tzitzis, for even work is permitted except during the first parsha, as written in siman 63, seif 7. He concluded that one should not bless on either, like Maharam of Coucy, according to his approach that the Rosh and Rashba agree with him, as he wrote in seif 8 of the Shulchan Aruch. But in Darkei Moshe he rejected his words and explained that the Rosh holds that one blesses over tefillin and wraps in the tallis without a blessing, only that he explained it as speaking before 'Ga'al Yisrael.' After 'Ga'al Yisrael' he agreed with the ruling of the Beis Yosef, as written in Tosafot 13b, s.v. 'sho'el,' etc., regarding between redemption, etc., and as written in the gloss there. So too he ruled in the gloss here, writing, 'and so is the custom,' etc. What he wrote, 'and some say,' etc., is the view of Maharam of Coucy. But the view of the Shulchan Aruch is to decide that in the middle of a section he should not bless on either, while between sections he should bless on both, unlike what he wrote in the Beis Yosef. He learned this from the words of the Rosh in a responsum, klal 4, siman 1, as written in Tur, siman 80, and in the gloss there, that he blesses on them.
