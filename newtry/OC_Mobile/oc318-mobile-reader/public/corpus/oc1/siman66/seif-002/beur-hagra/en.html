(2) If he forgot, etc. See Tosafot Berachot 14b, s.v. u'manach, etc. Behold the view of R' Yitzchak that between tefillin and tallit he puts them on and blesses, as is written, and R' Meir of Rothenburg, etc. And the view of Tosafot is that on tefillin he puts them on and blesses, and on tallit he does not wrap; and so too the Rosh and the Tur. And the view of R' Meir of Rothenburg is that between tefillin and tallit he wraps and puts them on without a blessing—however in the Beit Yosef he was uncertain about the view of the Rosh, for it is possible that he holds like R' Meir of Rothenburg as well, not to bless on tefillin and tallit; he holds per the view of Tosafot not to wrap at all. And he wrote that this is specifically between redemption and prayer; but before "Goel Yisrael" it is certainly permitted to wrap in tzitzit, since even during work it is permitted except for the first section, as stated in siman 63, seif 7. And regarding not blessing on both of them—per the view of R' Meir of Rothenburg, and according to his view the Rosh and Rashba hold like him, as stated in seif 8 in the Shulchan Aruch. But in Darkei Moshe he rejected his words and explained that the Rosh holds one blesses on tefillin, and on tallit one wraps without a blessing—however he explained that it deals with before "Goel Yisrael"; but after "Goel Yisrael" he agreed to the ruling of the Beit Yosef, as stated in Tosafot 13b, s.v. sho'el, etc., between redemption [and prayer], etc., and as stated in the gloss there, and likewise here in the gloss that he wrote "and such is the custom," etc. And what he wrote "and some say," etc., is the view of R' Meir of Rothenburg; but the view of the Shulchan Aruch is to rule that in the middle of the section one does not bless on both of them, and between sections one blesses on both of them—not as stated in the Beit Yosef. And he learned this from the words of the Rosh in responsum, general ruling 4, seif 61, and as stated in the Tur, siman 80, and in the gloss there, and he blesses on them. And according to his view, where he explains the words of the Rosh that one does not bless on tefillin—nevertheless he holds here that one blesses, and he holds the same regarding tallit; and so too in Bedek HaBayit. But according to what is stated in Darkei Moshe, the Rosh holds that one blesses on tefillin even in the middle of the section—therefore he does not distinguish between in the middle and between the sections.
