If he forgot. Or if he was under constraint, for he did not have tzitzis and tefillin from the outset.
Between the sections. If they became available to him in the middle of a section, he should wait from putting them on until between the sections, so that he will be able to recite a blessing on them. This applies if they became available in the middle of the blessing of Yotzer Or or Ahavah Rabbah. But if tefillin became available in the middle of Shema, he should put them on immediately, for each and every word of Shema is a mitzvah to say while wearing tefillin. The putting on should be with a blessing, according to the Peri Megadim.
A tallis. For tzitzis is not an obligation upon the person, since if he has no tallis he is exempt from tzitzis and may read Shema without tzitzis. Therefore the blessing is considered an interruption. But tefillin are an obligation upon the body, and without tefillin he is like one who testifies falsely about himself, Heaven forbid, for he says 'and you shall bind them' while he does not bind them. Therefore we follow the first opinion, that it is not considered an interruption. All this concerns the blessing, but putting it on alone is permitted; after prayer he should touch the tzitzis and recite the blessing. It appears from the Beit Yosef that even in the middle of a section it is permitted to put it on alone, except during the first section of Shema. The Peri Megadim wrote that this is how we rule, in accordance with the Rama's decision. Where he is sitting in the synagogue and is embarrassed to sit without a tallis, according to all opinions he may recite the blessing on it between the sections.
