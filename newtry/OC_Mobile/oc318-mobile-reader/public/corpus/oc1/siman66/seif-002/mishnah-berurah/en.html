(14) "If he forgot" — or was under constraint that he did not have tzitzit and tefillin at the outset.

(15) "Between the sections" — and if they became available to him in the middle of a section, he should wait from laying them until between the sections so he can bless on them. And this applies if they became available in the middle of the blessing Yotzer Or or Ahavah Rabbah; but if tefillin became available in the middle of Keriat Shema he lays immediately, for every word of Keriat Shema is a mitzvah that he have tefillin on him. And the laying should be during a blessing [Pri Megadim].

(16) "Tallit" — for tzitzit is not an obligation of the person, that if he has no tallit he is exempt from tzitzit and he can read Keriat Shema without tzitzit; therefore it is called an interruption through the blessing. But tefillin, which is an obligation of the body, and also without tefillin he is like one bearing false testimony, God forbid, saying "and you shall bind them" and he is not binding — therefore we hold like the first view that it is not considered an interruption. And all this regarding the blessing; but putting on alone is permitted, and after prayer he will handle the tzitzit and bless. And it is implied in Beit Yosef that even in the middle of a section putting on alone is permitted from the first section of Keriat Shema. Pri Megadim wrote that thus we hold as Rama's ruling. And where one sits in the synagogue and is ashamed to sit without a tallit — according to all opinions he may bless on it between the sections.
