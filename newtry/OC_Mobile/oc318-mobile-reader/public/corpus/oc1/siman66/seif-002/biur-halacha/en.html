If he forgot, etc., between the sections - See Mishnah Berurah regarding what I wrote, that if tefillin become available to him during Krias Shema, he should put them on immediately with a berachah. The reason is that we rule like the Rama in seif 8, in the gloss, that even in the middle of the section of Emet V'Yatziv one may recite the berachah. Nevertheless, in the middle of the section of Yotzer Or or Ahavah Rabbah, even the Rama agrees with the Mechaber that it is preferable to wait until between the sections, so that he can recite the berachah according to all opinions. As I wrote in Mishnah Berurah, this is also evident from Peri Megadim and Derech HaChaim, and Rabbi Akiva Eiger in his novellae likewise inclines to this ruling.
