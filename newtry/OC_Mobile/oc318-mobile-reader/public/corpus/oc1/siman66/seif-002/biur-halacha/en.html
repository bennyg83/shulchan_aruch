If he forgot, etc., between the sections — see Mishnah Berurah in what he wrote: if tefillin happened to him during Keriat Shema he lays them immediately with a blessing; the reason is we rule like Rama in siman 25 in the Hagahah that also in the middle of the section of Emet V'yatziv one may bless; nevertheless in the middle of the section of Yotzer Or or Ahavah Rabbah even Rama admits to the author's view that it is better to wait until between sections so he can bless according to all opinions, as I wrote in Mishnah Berurah; so is proven from Pri Megadim and Beit Chadash, and Gra in his innovations also tends to say thus.
