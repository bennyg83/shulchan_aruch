[2] And responds peace to any person, etc. — I found in Sefer HaChinukh for Ra'ah, parashat Va'etchanan, thus his wording: and this is how much — we have not seen one who objects to his fellow at all if he does not stop for him, even between sections — until here. He wrote in the gloss of Mordechai in the name of R' Yehudah HeChasid: one responds out of honor specifically peace and nothing more; and all that he says 'asks' 'responds' means with new faces — if he does not ask, hatred will result; and in synagogue, where we do not ask peace, God forbid — to respond or ask, and even words of Torah, not between sections nor in blessings nor in pesukei dezimra — until here. It implies that if he is not engaged in blessings and pesukei dezimra they ask in synagogue; and so implies Tashbetz siman 246 — thus his wording: the Rambam asks peace of any person before prayer, and even in synagogue.
