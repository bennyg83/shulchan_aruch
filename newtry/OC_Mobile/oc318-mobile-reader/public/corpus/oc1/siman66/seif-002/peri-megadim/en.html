(Regarding) "and thus is the practice" — Taz implies like Magen Avraham letter 4: specifically regarding tallit one may not interrupt to bless, for it is permitted to read Keriat Shema without tzitzit; therefore he dons the tallit without a blessing, unlike tefillin which testify on Shema, and prayer also requires tefillin — see Shulchan Arukh siman 25 se'if 4 and Magen Avraham 24 letter 3 in the name of the Zohar. Nevertheless everyone follows Beit Yosef. And it is possible even if he has no tallit available, since it is not found explicitly in poskim that without tzitzit [one may not read], as Magen Avraham wrote — and if he read Keriat Shema b'dieved they don [tefillin] for the sake of prayer with a blessing. And if it happened that they brought him tefillin in the middle of a section — the Mechaber holds he dons without a blessing, and for Rabeinu Bechor Shor even in the middle he dons tefillin and blesses as explained in siman 98 — and this is where it is impossible otherwise; but before Keriat Shema he waits until between the sections, and this is obvious; but in the middle of a paragraph of Keriat Shema he dons and blesses immediately and does not wait.
