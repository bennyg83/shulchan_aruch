And so. The practice is as stated. See Taz. It implies like the Magen Avraham, letter 4, that specifically for a tallis one does not interrupt to bless, for it is permitted to read Krias Shema without tzitzis; therefore he should put on the tallis without a blessing. This is not so for tefillin, which testify, as stated there; prayer too requires tefillin. See what I wrote above; see siman 25, seif 4, and Magen Avraham 24, letter 3, in the name of the Zohar. Nevertheless, everyone follows the tallis katan. It is possible that even if he does not have a tallis katan, since it is not stated explicitly in the poskim that without tzitzis it is like false testimony, even if he read Krias Shema twice, nevertheless he puts them on because of prayer, with a blessing. If it happened that tefillin were brought to him in the middle of a section, according to the Mechaber he puts them on without a blessing, and according to the Rav, even in the middle he puts on tefillin and blesses, as explained in seif 8. This is when it is impossible in another way; but before Krias Shema he should wait until between the sections, and this is obvious. In the middle of a paragraph of Krias Shema, however, he should put them on and bless immediately, and not wait.
