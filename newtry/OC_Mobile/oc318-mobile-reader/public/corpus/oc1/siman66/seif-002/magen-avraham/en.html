"And some say not to bless," etc. Here Mahari Mintz wrote in Beit Yosef, and Levush Chadash wrote that Beit Yosef already erased the general rule in Bedek HaBayit and wrote that one should bless on them, and so Rosh in a responsum — end quote. And I say: if Maharil retracted, Rama did not retract, for it hangs on a great pillar — for in Darkei Moshe siman 54 he wrote in the name of Or Zaru'a and R' Eliezer of Prague that one should not bless on tallit, and so in Tashbetz in the name of Maharam; and see siman 54 — thus Targum Yonatan: tefillin — because there is an obligation to pray with them — one interrupts; but tallit, which is only "obligation of the garment," does not interrupt in the blessings of Keriat Shema in order to wrap and bless and pray in tzitzit — end quote. And Rosh in a responsum also did not write that one blesses except on tefillin. And Shulchan Arukh copied his words in siman 80; therefore Rama was precise and wrote "and so is the practice" regarding tallit — and so in Hagahot Maimoniyot.
