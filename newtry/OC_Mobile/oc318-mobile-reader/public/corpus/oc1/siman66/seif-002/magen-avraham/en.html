And some say not to bless, etc. Here the Maggid Mishneh and Beit Yosef are cited. The Lechem Chamudos wrote that the Beit Yosef already deleted the principle in Bedek HaBayis and wrote that one should bless over them, and so wrote the Rosh in a responsum. End quote. But I say that even if the Beit Yosef retracted, the Rama did not retract, for the matter depends on great authorities. In Darkei Moshe, siman 54, he wrote in the name of Or Zarua and in the name of R. Avraham of Prague that one should not bless over a tallis, and so wrote the Tashbetz in the name of Maharam. See what I wrote in siman 54. These are the words of Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah: tefillin, because it is an obligation to pray with them, one interrupts; but a tallis, which is only an obligation of the garment, one does not interrupt in the blessings of Krias Shema in order to wrap himself and bless and pray with tzitzis. End quote. The Rosh too, in his responsum, wrote that one should bless only regarding tefillin, and the Shulchan Aruch copied his words in siman 80. Therefore the Rama was precise and wrote, 'and so is the practice regarding a tallis.' So too wrote Hagahos Maimoniyos.
