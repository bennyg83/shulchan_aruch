One must adjoin redemption to the Amidah, and one may not interrupt after saying "Ga'al Yisrael" — only if unavoidable circumstances occurred; for example, one did not lay tefillin and they became available to him between redemption and prayer — he lays them then but does not bless on them until after he prays. But regarding a tallit, he should not put it on then. And if before he said "Ga'al Yisrael" a tallit and tefillin became available to him, he dons them but does not bless on them until after prayer.And some say that before "Ga'al Yisrael" one blesses on the tefillin, and such is the practice [Mordekhai and Tosafot and Hagahot Maimoniyot from Hilchot Keriat Shema].]:
