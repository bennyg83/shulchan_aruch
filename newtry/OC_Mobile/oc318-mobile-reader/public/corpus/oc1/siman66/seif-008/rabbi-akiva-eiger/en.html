Se'if 8 in the Hagahah — he blesses on tefillin. It is possible specifically in this case: although he is in the middle of a section, nevertheless he blesses, since prayer is fit to be said with tefillin and he cannot wait. But for the blessings before Keriat Shema — one may say that in the middle he does not bless, since it is possible to wait until he reaches between the sections.
