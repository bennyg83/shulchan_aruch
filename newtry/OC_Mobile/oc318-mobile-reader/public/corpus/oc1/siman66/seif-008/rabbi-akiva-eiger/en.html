Seif 8, gloss. He should recite the blessing over the tefillin. Perhaps this applies specifically here: Although he is in the middle of a section, he should nevertheless recite the blessing, since the prayer ought to be recited while wearing tefillin and it is impossible to wait. Regarding the blessings that precede Keriat Shema, however, it may be that he should not recite the blessing in the middle of a section, since he can wait until he reaches a point between the sections.
