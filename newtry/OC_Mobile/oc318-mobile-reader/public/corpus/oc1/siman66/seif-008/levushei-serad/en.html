(Shulchan Aruch seif 6) or honor. It is difficult according to what is stated in the previous section that after one said "emet" it is the law in the middle of the section—if so it is forbidden to interrupt out of honor; and it is strained to say that he interrupted in a forbidden manner, for in any case the author permitted him to return—for if not, why did he conceal it if he interrupted after saying "emet"? And this honor here means one's father or teacher, whose law is like out of fear; and fear here means a king and one who compels, as Rashi explained.
