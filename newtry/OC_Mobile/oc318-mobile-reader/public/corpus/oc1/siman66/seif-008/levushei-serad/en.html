Shulchan Aruch, seif 6. Or honor. This is difficult according to what is written in the previous seif, that after he said 'Emes' it has the law of the middle of a section. If so, it is forbidden to interrupt because of honor. It is forced to say that he interrupted improperly, for the Mechaber must be speaking of a permitted interruption; otherwise why did he state plainly, 'if he interrupted after saying Emes'? Or perhaps the honor here means his father or teacher, whose law is like because of fear, and the fear here means a king or violent person, according to Rashi's explanation.
