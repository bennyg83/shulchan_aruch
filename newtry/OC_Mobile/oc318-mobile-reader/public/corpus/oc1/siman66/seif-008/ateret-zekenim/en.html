One should not interrupt after saying Ga'al Yisrael, etc. Gloss: Accordingly, one must specifically say Ga'al Yisrael, and not Go'el Yisrael. Our custom on the evening of Yom Tov requires study, for when reciting the Maaravis poem we conclude, 'Blessed are You, Hashem, King, Rock of Israel and its Redeemer,' which is as though one said Go'el Yisrael. It appears that one should say 'ge'alo,' without the letter vav, for this then properly refers to the redemption that occurred in the past, as stated by my teacher in Bach.
