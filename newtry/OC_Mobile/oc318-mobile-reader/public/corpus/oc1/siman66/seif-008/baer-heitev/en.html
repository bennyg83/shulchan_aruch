Tefillin. He feared that the time for Krias Shema would pass, and he read it. The same applies if he fears that the time for prayer will pass: he prays without tefillin, and when they later come to hand, he puts them on in the middle of the day or at Minchah and says some chapter of Tehillim; see the end of siman 30. It requires study regarding one who has no tefillin, whether it is better to pray with the congregation without tefillin or better to delay until after the communal prayer to borrow tefillin from his fellow, so that he can read Krias Shema and pray with tefillin. The Magen Avraham ruled that it is better that he delay until after the communal prayer and borrow tefillin, so that he read Krias Shema and pray with tefillin; see there.
Over them. The Peri Chadash disagrees and holds that since it is permitted to put on tefillin between redemption and prayer, he may also bless, and it is not called an interruption; see there. In the book Even HaOzer he likewise ruled that one may bless over tefillin after 'Ga'al Yisrael'; see there. If a tallis became available to him, he ruled that it is forbidden to wrap in it at all until after Shemoneh Esrei. But where he is embarrassed to sit without a tallis, it is better to wrap between the sections and bless over it; see there. He further ruled that it is permitted to answer Kaddish and Kedushah between redemption and prayer, and that it is forbidden to wait at 'Shirah chadashah'; see there.
