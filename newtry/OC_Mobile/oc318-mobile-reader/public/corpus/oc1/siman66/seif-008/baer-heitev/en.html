And likewise if he is afraid the time of prayer will pass — he prays without tefillin, and when they come to his hand afterward he lays them in the middle of the day or at Minchah and says some psalm — see end of siman 44. There is question: one who has no tefillin — is it better to pray with the congregation without tefillin, or is it better to delay after the congregation's prayer to borrow tefillin from his fellow so he reads Keriat Shema and prays in tefillin? Magen Avraham decided it is better to delay after the congregation's prayer and ask for tefillin so he reads Keriat Shema and prays in tefillin — in his name.

Upon them. And Pri Chadash disagrees and holds: since it is permitted to lay tefillin between redemption and prayer, he may bless as well and it is not called an interruption — in his name. And in Sefer Even HaEzer he also ruled one may bless on tefillin after 'Ga'al Yisrael' — in his name. And when a tallit became available to him — he ruled it is forbidden to wrap in it altogether until after the eighteen blessings. But where one would be ashamed to sit without a tallit — it is better to wrap between the sections and bless on it — in his name. He further ruled one may answer Kaddish and Kedushah between redemption and prayer; and it is forbidden to wait at 'Shirah Chadashah' — in his name.
