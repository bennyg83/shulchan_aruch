"If unavoidable circumstances occurred," etc. — and he was permitted to let the time for Keriat Shema pass and read [Rashi]. And likewise if he is permitted to let the time for prayer pass, he prays without tefillin, and when they come to his hand afterward he lays them at Minchah, or lays them in the middle of the day and says some psalm. There is a question: one who has no tefillin — is it better to pray with the congregation without tefillin, or is it better to delay after the congregation's prayer to borrow tefillin from his fellow so that he read Keriat Shema and pray with tefillin? And it is somewhat implied in Tosafot that communal prayer is preferable; but one may prove from what we say at the end of siman 111 that adjoining redemption to prayer is preferable to prayer with the congregation, and tefillin are preferable to adjoining redemption to prayer, as written here; if so, a fortiori tefillin are preferable to communal prayer. And one may also prove somewhat from Rashi's explanation that Rav was permitted to let the time for Keriat Shema pass, and he did not explain that Rav read without tefillin in order to pray with the congregation; if so, it must be that it is better to pray with tefillin than to pray with the congregation, as above.

"He does not lay then." — for laying is an interruption; see siman 97.
