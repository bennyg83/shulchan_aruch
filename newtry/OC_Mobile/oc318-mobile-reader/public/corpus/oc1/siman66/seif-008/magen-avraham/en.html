If duress befell him, etc. He feared that the time for Krias Shema would pass, and he therefore read it. Rashi. The same applies if he fears that the time for prayer will pass: he prays without tefillin, and when they later come into his hand, he puts them on at Minchah, or puts them on in the middle of the day and says some chapter of Tehillim; see the end of siman 30. It requires study regarding one who has no tefillin: is it better to pray with the congregation without tefillin, or is it preferable that he delay until after the congregation's prayer in order to borrow tefillin from his fellow, so that he can read Krias Shema and pray with tefillin? It somewhat appears from Tosafot that prayer with the congregation is preferable. However, one can prove from what is said at the end of siman 111 that joining redemption to prayer is preferable to prayer with the congregation; and tefillin are preferable to joining redemption to prayer, as written here. If so, all the more so tefillin are preferable to prayer with the congregation. One can also prove somewhat from the fact that Rashi explained that Rav feared the time for Krias Shema would pass, and did not explain that Rav read without tefillin in order to pray with the congregation. Rather, one must say that it is better to pray with tefillin than to pray with the congregation, as it seems to me.
He should not put them on then. For putting them on is an interruption. See siman 97.
