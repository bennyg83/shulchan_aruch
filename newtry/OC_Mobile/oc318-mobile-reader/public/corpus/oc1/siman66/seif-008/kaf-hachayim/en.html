(8) There—one whom one fears, such as one's father or teacher, etc. Behold what the Sages stated at the beginning of the chapter "Hayeh korei"—in the middle he asks out of fear and responds out of honor; and between sections he asks out of honor and responds peace to any person. There is a dispute among the posekim: Rashi explained—out of honor: a distinguished person whom it is fitting to greet first with peace; and out of fear: a person whom one fears before him lest he kill him. And so too the Rambam, z"l, chapter 2 of Keriat Shema, laws 15 and 16—out of honor, such as one's father or teacher or one greater than him in wisdom; and out of fear, such as a king or one who compels. But the Rosh wrote—out of fear means fear of one's father or teacher, as it is written "a man shall fear his mother and his father," and we learn "the fear of your teacher should be like the fear of Heaven." The Tur brought this and wrote, and so the Geonim explained. And so too Rashba, as stated in Beit Yosef. And so too Rashba in responsum siman 321 in the name of R' Yitzchak Gi'at; and so Ra'avad and Agur, as Eliyah Rabbah wrote, letter 1. And so holds Ra'avad and Nimukey Yosef in the chapter "Yesh nohelin," as Menachem's words in Hagahot Beit Yosef, letter 1. And so ruled Maran, z"l, here in the Shulchan Aruch. And so the Levush. And so ruled Peri Chadash, letter 1. Eliyah Rabbah letter 1. Taz letter 1. Chayei Adam, general rule 39, letter 6. Siddur Beit Yosef in laws of blessings of Keriat Shema, letter 9. And so it appears to be the view of Taz note 8 and Aruch HaShulchan letter 1—from that they resolve the difficulties of the Bach. And not as the Bach, who wrote that the Rosh and Rashba are alone in this matter—see there. However, what Maran wrote—that one greater than him in wisdom is included among those whom one fears—later authorities disagreed on this and considered him included among those [greeted] out of honor. Magen Avraham note 2. Peri Chadash there. Eliyah Rabbah there. Taz there. Chayei Adam there. And so one should be stringent. However, a Torah scholar outstanding in his generation is included in fear, Magen Avraham there. Eliyah Rabbah there. Taz there. Chayei Adam there. And see siman 472, seif 5.
