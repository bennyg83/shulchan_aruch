Taz — and in my humble opinion it appears that in the Gemara he was particular only that one not say in Keriat Shema and Hallel 'Elokei Yisrael Go'el' as in prayer, because Go'el even though it is present tense, nevertheless it implies more the future; but in the Yerushalmi it deals that one says 'Melekh tzur Yisrael uGo'alo' — and tzur is certainly present tense, and also the wording uGo'alo is similar to tzur; with this we see it refers to the past, and if so in Maariv it is also fine.
