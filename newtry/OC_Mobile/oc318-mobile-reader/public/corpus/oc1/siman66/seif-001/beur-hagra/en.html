Seif 1, such as his father, etc. The Rosh, for regarding his father it is said, 'A man shall fear his mother and his father,' and regarding his teacher we learned, 'the fear of your teacher,' etc. This is not like Rashi and the Rambam, who explained it as a violent person; for this need not be stated, as written in the second chapter of Pesachim: learn from it that nothing stands before saving life, etc. Even for prayer one interrupts, as written in the fifth chapter. This is what he wrote, 'and all the more so,' etc.
Or someone, etc. Rashba, and as written in Bava Kama 41b, 'until Rabbi Akiva came,' etc.; see Tosafot there.
And even, etc. Yerushalmi there: Until now, in the middle of a section; even in the middle of a verse? Rabbi Yirmeyah would gesture; Rabbi Yonah would speak. Rav Huna said in the name of Rabbi Yosef: 'and you shall speak of them,' from here that you have permission to speak in them. Rabbi Yirmeyah is a lone view against them.
Except, etc. Kesef Mishneh in the name of Ramach, that so it is in the Yerushalmi. This is what is stated in the verse, 'and Yosef fell,' etc., for Yaakov was then accepting upon himself the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven.
If he did not, etc. As above, and as with prayer.
