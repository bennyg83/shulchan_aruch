(1) Such as his father, etc. The Rosh—that regarding one's father it is written "a man shall fear his mother and his father," and regarding one's teacher we learn "the fear of your teacher should be like the fear of Heaven"—unlike Rashi and the Rambam, who explain [the case of] one who compels; for that goes without saying, as stated in chapter 2 of Pesachim. And we learn from this that nothing stands before [the mitzvah of saving] life (pikuach nefesh), and even for prayer one interrupts, as stated in chapter 5. And he said "all the more so," etc.

Or one who, etc. Rashba, and as stated in Bava Kamma 41b, until R' Akiva came, etc., and see Tosafot there.

And even, etc. Yerushalmi there, until "kedon"—in the middle of a section, even in the middle of a verse; R' Jeremiah alludes, R' Yonah relates in Rosh Hashana in the name of R' Yosef: "and you shall speak of them"—from here you have permission to speak with them; and R' Jeremiah is alone against them.

Except, etc. As brought in the name of Ramach that thus in the Yerushalmi; and he said in the parsha "Vayipol Yosef," etc., that Yaakov would receive him with words then.

If not, etc. As above, and as in prayer.
