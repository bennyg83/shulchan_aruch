[The Laws Regarding] In Which Place One May Interrupt and in Which Place One May Not Interrupt. Containing 10 se'ifim: Between the sections [of
Shema or its blessings], one may greet an honored person and respond to a greeting from any person. And in the middle [of a section], one may greet someone whom one fears, such as one's father, one's teacher, or one greater than oneself in wisdom, and all the more so a king or one who compels. And one may respond to a greeting from an honored person, even in the' middle of a verse, except for the verse of Shema Yisrael and Barukh shem kavod malchuto le'olam va'ed, during which one may not interrupt at all unless it is out of fear that he will be killed.
