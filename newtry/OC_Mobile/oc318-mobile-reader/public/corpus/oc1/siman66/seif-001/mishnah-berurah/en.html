Everything that is forbidden to speak — even in the holy language it is forbidden to speak; and even one word is forbidden [Pri Megadim]. (1) Between the sections — specifically when they meet one another by chance; but when one rises to open [the door] or goes in the synagogue from his fixed place to his fellow's place to give him peace — even to his father and teacher it is forbidden, and even before he began "Barukh she'amar" since he is before prayer [Pri Megadim in siman 89, see there in Perishah, and so is proven from the reason stated in the Gemara 14a, "by what did you consider him worthy" etc., see there in Perishah; and see Kiddushin 33b that one's honor should not be weightier than the honor of Heaven].

(2) "Asks peace" — even in a foreign language. And specifically regarding new faces one asks and responds, for if he does not respond hatred will result. And in Sefer HaChinukh he also wrote: one whose fellow we have not seen that he objects at all — he should not interrupt even between the sections. Therefore according to our custom today, when we do not practice asking peace in the synagogue during prayer, God forbid to ask or respond, even words of Torah, neither between the sections nor in pesukei dezimra. If one transgressed and interrupted in Keriat Shema — even in a place where he is not permitted to interrupt, and he did not wait long enough to complete the whole — according to all opinions he does not return [to the beginning] but only to that verse; and some say only to that word from which he stopped, if it is in a place where the matter concludes.

(3) "Honored person" — one to whom it is proper to give him greeting first, such as if he is elderly or a Torah scholar; and likewise if he is wealthy, whom it is proper to honor on account of his wealth.

(4) "To any person" — who greeted him first. And it is implied in Chiddushei Rashba that it is permitted lekhatchilah to ask peace of the reader even though he knows he will need to respond to him. And see above in sk 2.

(5) "His father or his teacher" — meaning his bona fide teacher from whom the core of his Torah [comes], even if he is now greater in Torah than his teacher; and regarding him applies "fear," as it is written "a man shall fear his mother and his father," and we learned "and fear of your teacher is like fear of Heaven."

(6) "Greater than him" — and if they are equal he is in the category of "out of honor." And if the reader is greater than him he does not interrupt at all, even for a Torah scholar.

(7) "In wisdom" — and the Acharonim ruled that even if he is greater than him, it is only "out of honor"; if he is not a Torah scholar distinguished in his generation, then he is in the category of "fear."

(8) "And all the more so a king" — even a king of Israel is in the category of "out of fear," as it is written "you shall surely set a king over you" — that his dread be upon you.

(9) "Or coercion" — or an informer.

(10) "The verse" — some say in the middle of a verse he may not interrupt unless in a place where the matter concludes; and if he interrupted he returns afterward to the beginning of the verse. And regarding practice, when he interrupts to answer Kaddish or Kedushah as will be explained below — if he cannot hurry to finish the matter, he may stop even in the middle of the matter and rely on the first view that did not distinguish in this for the sake of such a great mitzvah, and answer with the congregation; and afterward he returns to the beginning of the verse.

(11) "Except for the verse" — for there is nothing greater than acceptance of the kingdom of Heaven; and for this reason one does not interrupt also between "Shema Yisrael" and "Barukh shem," since it too is part of unity [Beit Yosef].

(12) "In them at all" — implies that even Kaddish, Kedushah, and Barekhu he does not answer; and see Biur Halakhah. And it is possible that if the reading was after the third hour, when he is only like one who reads the Torah, he must interrupt.

(13) "Unless [out of fear] etc." — and its law is like in prayer, below in siman 104.
