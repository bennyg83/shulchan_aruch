Such as his father or teacher, etc. {Rama: On this — and some say that specifically out of fear of a king or coercion one may interrupt in the middle, but for one's father and teacher one may not interrupt except between the sections (Rashi and Rambam). And so is the practice. Nevertheless, even out of fear of the kings of Israel one may interrupt in the middle of Keriat Shema (R' Yehudah of Regensburg, chapter 'he was reading,' and my teacher in Beit Chadash). But not in prayer where there is no danger, for we derive it from the verse 'and you shall speak them' — and not in prayer.}
