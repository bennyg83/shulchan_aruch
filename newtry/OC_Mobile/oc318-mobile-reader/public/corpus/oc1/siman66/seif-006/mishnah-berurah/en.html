(31) "And one waits" — meaning: if he did not pause long enough, they silence him when he returns and says "emet," as with "Shema shema" and "Modim modim"; but if he paused, even though they do not silence him, nevertheless he need not return, for why should he repeat it a second time. And likewise wherever he stopped in any of the blessings of Keriat Shema — when he returns afterward he need not return [to the beginning] but only to the word from which he stopped; and regarding prayer, see below in siman 104 se'if 5.
