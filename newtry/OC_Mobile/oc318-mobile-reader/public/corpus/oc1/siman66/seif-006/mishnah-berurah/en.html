And waits. This means that if he did not wait, we silence him when he goes back and says emes, as with one who says Shema, Shema, or Modim, Modim. But if he waited, although we do not silence him, nevertheless he need not go back, for why should he repeat it a second time? So too, wherever one stops in any blessing of the blessings of Shema, when he later returns he need return only to the word at which he stopped. Regarding prayer, see below, siman 104, seif 5.
