The verse. See Be'er Heitev. For he wrote in the name of Magen Avraham: upon hearing the sound of thunder one interrupts and blesses; and in Bechor Shor he wrote that Rosh does not disagree, for the a fortiori is a refuted a fortiori, as those who forbid wrote — since one is engaged in praising the Holy One blessed be He, one may not interrupt for other praise. And thus is implied in the Hagahah that wrote amen to HaKel HaKadosh — it implies for other amens not; and the blessing on tefillin is different, for it pertains to Keriat Shema since tefillin are obligatory for Keriat Shema — so in brief. And also in Beit HaRav he doubted Magen Avraham. And see in responsa Noda Biyehuda siman 41 — he brought Magen Avraham's words regarding Kiddush Levanah in the middle of Megillah reading, and brought words of Avi Ezri on Bechor Shor who disagrees with Magen Avraham and did not write a ruling between them.
