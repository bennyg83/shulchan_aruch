After. Ga'al Yisrael; see Taz. See Bach: if he said 'uge'alo' without a vav between the gimel and the alef, it would imply the past. Now, the vav conversive from past to future or from future to past is sometimes with sheva and sometimes with patach. If so, 'vego'alo' without the vav, or with the vav conversive, would be future, as in, 'and he shall redeem his brother's sale,' and, 'I shall redeem you.' If he says it with a patach, then 'vego'alo' with a patach is better. There are textual versions in the Maariv prayers; examine them.
