After Ga'al Yisrael — Taz. And see Bach: if he said "u'go'alo" without vav (between gimmel and aleph) it implies past tense. Behold the vav reverses from past to future or from future to past — this with sh'va na and this with patach; and thus "u'go'alo" without vav, and with vav the reversal would be future, as "u'ga'al et mimkar achiv" (Vayikra 25) and "u'ga'alti etchem" (Shemot 6). And if he says with patach, then "u'go'alo" with patach is even better; and there are nusachim in Ma'ariv — see them.
