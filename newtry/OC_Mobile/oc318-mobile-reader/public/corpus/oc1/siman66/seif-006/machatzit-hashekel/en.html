(sk 6) Modim, etc. — "modim anachnu lecha" alone is unnecessary, for it has no meaning [as interruption] since it is only custom. Meaning: [one should not say] "titkabbel," for by law the end of Kaddish is where "titkabbel" begins.

Only "kadosh" and "barukh," etc. — for these two verses Isaiah and Ezekiel heard from the mouths of the angels who were sanctifying, and "yimlokh" [they did not say] — all the more so what they add on Shabbat and festivals within Kedushah, which he does not have [in scripture].
