(Seif katan 6) Modim, etc. 'Modim anachnu lach,' for the word 'Modim' alone is nothing to him, since it has no meaning. 'For it is only a custom' means to say 'Tiskabel,' for according to the law the end of Kaddish is where 'Tiskabel' begins.
Only Kadosh and Baruch, etc. For these two verses were heard by Yeshayah and Yechezkel from the angels who were sanctifying, but not 'Yimloch,' and all the more so what is added on Shabbos and Yom Tov in the middle of Kedushah, which one should not say.
