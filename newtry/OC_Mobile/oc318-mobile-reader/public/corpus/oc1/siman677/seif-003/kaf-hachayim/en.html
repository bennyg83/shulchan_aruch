Must give perutah, etc. — appears guest giving expense to homeowner — then must participate; if special room must kindle in room; if guest relies entirely on homeowner — eats from his bread, drinks from his cup — no need, for homeowner who provides all needs will also transfer part of candle money — no suspicion since known he relies on homeowner. Gan HaMelech siman 41. Some say there Shaarei Teshuvah letter 1 Shulchan Gavoah letter 2 in Machatzit — if eats with homeowner on account of all he eats and drinks — needs perutah; if eats as contractor so much per day or week for all needs — no perutah. Appears likewise if homeowner gives all free — no perutah; nevertheless better explicitly say transfer some oil in your Hanukkah lights and homeowner says I transfer, etc., whether paid or free; however what wrote even with special room need not kindle — see below letter 9 — appears same here.
