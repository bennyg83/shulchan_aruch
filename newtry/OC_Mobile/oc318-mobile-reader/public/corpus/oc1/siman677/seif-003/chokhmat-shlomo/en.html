When there I wondered greatly why challenge — perhaps intent: one who makes havdalah on this and that means if first on cup then prayer — blessings fall since then not vain; why challenge? Must be intent: then interruption in prayer — difficult on Rabbeinu Yona Bach Orach Chayim 108 brought mentioning other days' events in prayer is not interruption — learned from if he made havdalah in both he fulfilled; here havdalah must be interruption. Without Rabbeinu Yona — no proof from havdalah — certainly mentioning matter evident not of the day is interruption since evident it is interruption; but havdalah at night — if he did not pray at all he could make havdalah in this prayer — not evident from prayer body it is interruption — not known if he prayed; therefore not evident from body is not interruption. This is Taz's objection; I explained his reason more; even so our question remains: in havdalah on cup first also not evident if already made havdalah on cup — therefore appears main distinction: there prayer draws prayer — if not prayer time no place to pray makeup only because prayer time therefore prays makeup too — obligatory prayer time causes second prayer — one prayer considered; support from generally once permission given, given; not interruption; but if say he made havdalah on cup — no need havdalah in prayer — no havdalah in prayer; if made havdalah — interruption; challenge well. If say like R' Yonah who holds wherever mentioning event matter — not interruption even without this reason — challenge well: why not say intent if first on cup; requires study; proves like Taz not Rabbeinu Yona — examine.
