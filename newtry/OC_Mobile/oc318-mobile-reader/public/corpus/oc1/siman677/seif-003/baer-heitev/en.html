And he blesses. There is no blessing in vain, since he does not want to fulfill his obligation with that of his wife, and the obligation takes effect upon him. Responsa Maharil. Some disagree, and where there is doubt about blessings we are lenient. Turei Zahav. Maharshal wrote that one who came to his home and found that they had kindled for him must kindle. Magen Avraham wrote that his words are not compelling; therefore one should not bless. See there, and see Turei Zahav.
