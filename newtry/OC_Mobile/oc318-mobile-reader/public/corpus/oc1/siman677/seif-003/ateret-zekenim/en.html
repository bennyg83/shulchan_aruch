That they kindle for him, etc. Some say that a person for whom they kindle in his home does not need to see Chanukah lamps. Responsa Rema, siman 99, according to the view of the Tur.
And he blesses over them. Some disagree; Maharash. It seems to me that when there is doubt about blessings, we are lenient. Only if he wants to be stringent upon himself, he should kindle without a blessing, as it seems to me.
