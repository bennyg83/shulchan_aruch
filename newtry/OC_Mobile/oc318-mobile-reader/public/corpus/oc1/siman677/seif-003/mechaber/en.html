Some say that although they kindle for him inside his home, if he is in a place where there are no Jews, he kindles with the blessings. Gloss: For he is obligated to see the lamps. Mordechai. So is the practice. Even if he is among Jews and sees the lamps, if he wants to be stringent upon himself and kindle on his own, he kindles and blesses over them, and so is the practice. Terumas HaDeshen, Maharil, and Responsa Maharil, siman 145.
