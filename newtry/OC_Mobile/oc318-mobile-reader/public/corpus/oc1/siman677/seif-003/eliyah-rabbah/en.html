In the place where he eats, etc. This means on a regular basis. But one who eats at his friend's house incidentally, the preferred mitzvah is that he go to his home and kindle. After the fact, when his wife kindles for him, that is enough. Nevertheless, he must bless upon seeing the lamps in the place where he dines at his friend's house. Bach. If he heard the blessings from the homeowner and answered amen, he need not bless upon seeing. Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah. One must say that this is speaking where the homeowner intended to discharge his obligation.
