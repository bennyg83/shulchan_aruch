If he wants. Even though we are not concerned lest he forget since we rely on women even for karet prohibition — nevertheless if he wants to kindle himself he is among mehaderin (Tosefet Yehuda); in responsum Maharil: common custom guests and bachelors kindle since now each kindles — who knows if they kindle for him at home — comes to suspicion; no vain blessing since he does not want to fulfill through wife's — obligation falls on him — end.
