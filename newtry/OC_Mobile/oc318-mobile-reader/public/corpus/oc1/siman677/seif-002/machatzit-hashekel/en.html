Paragraph 2: An entrance, etc. Even if it is open, etc. Meaning: although above in siman 675, section 8, regarding a courtyard that has two entrances, where one must kindle at both of them because of suspicion, if both entrances face one direction he need not kindle except at one entrance, it is different there, because they know that both belong to one person. Since they see that he kindled at one, there is no longer suspicion, except when they face two directions, for one who sees in one direction that he did not kindle will say that just as he did not kindle in this direction, so too he did not kindle in the other direction. This is not so when they face one direction. But here, what does it help that his entrance opens in the same direction as the homeowner's entrance? Nevertheless there is suspicion that the guest did not kindle.
