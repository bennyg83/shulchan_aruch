(ב) There. Must give perutah, etc. — precise to say perutah: guest need not give half candle cost — perutah given suffices. Some say in Hagahot. Mishna Berurah letter 3: when giving perutah say: I give you this perutah so you acquire for me some oil and wicks in your Hanukkah lights you kindle tonight, or if he wishes all eight nights; homeowner says: I transfer to you some oil with this perutah in my Hanukkah lights I kindle all eight nights. Without perutah homeowner may transfer if he wishes as below letter 5 — say I transfer to you, etc., stand by him at blessing, intend to fulfill through homeowner's blessing; homeowner also intends to discharge him as known.
