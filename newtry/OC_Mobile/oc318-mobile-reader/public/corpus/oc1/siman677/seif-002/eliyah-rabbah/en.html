And he participates, etc. I heard that one must add what his friend gives; Agudah. Meaning, if it is not recognizable that he added for his friend, there is suspicion. According to this, even if he did not add everything that the friend gave him but only part of it, that is enough, for it is recognizable that he participated through the fact that he added beyond what he was accustomed to use. Bach. This reason is forced, for what recognition is there? Sometimes one adds a little beyond the required measure. It is also difficult from Responsa Maharil at the beginning of this siman, and the language of Agudah also implies that he adds all of it. Therefore it seems to me that the reason is that the one who kindles is obligated to provide from his own money enough for half an hour; see siman 676. Therefore he must add as though, if not, he has not provided the measure of his obligation. Rashba, siman 542, wrote that the same applies if the homeowner transfers ownership to him of a share in the oil as a gift; that is permitted.
