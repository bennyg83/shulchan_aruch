Paragraph 3. The laws of Mahariv were brought in Darkei Moshe, paragraph 2: nowadays one should not participate with a perutah even if he does not have an entrance open for himself. Magen Avraham explains this as a case where he eats in a separate room. Therefore, in the time of the Talmud, when they kindled outside at the entrance, since there was only one entrance there, participation was enough. But nowadays, when they kindle inside, since there are two rooms, he must kindle for himself. But when there is no separate room, even nowadays participation is enough. However, in Darkei Moshe it does not seem this way at first glance, but it can be reconciled; examine it.
