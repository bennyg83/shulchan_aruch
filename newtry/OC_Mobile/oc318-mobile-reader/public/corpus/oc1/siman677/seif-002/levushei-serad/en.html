Section 503 — the laws of Mahariv brought in Darkei Moshe 2; nowadays one does not participate with a perutah even if he has no open door for himself. Magen Avraham explains that he eats in a room alone; therefore in Talmudic times when they kindled at the entrance from outside, since there was only one entrance, participation suffices — not so nowadays when they kindle inside, and since there are two rooms he must kindle alone; but when there is no room alone, even nowadays participation suffices. However on the face of the matter it does not appear so; there is to resolve — requires study.
