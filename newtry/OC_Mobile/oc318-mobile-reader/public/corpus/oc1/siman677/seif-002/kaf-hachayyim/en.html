2) Ibid. He must give a perutah, etc. The wording precisely says "a perutah," to teach that the lodger need not pay half of the entire expense of the lights. It suffices for him to give a perutah. Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur; Mishna Berurah, letter 3. When giving the perutah, he must say, "I hereby give you this perutah so that you will transfer to me ownership of a portion of the oil and wicks in your Chanukah lights that you will kindle tonight." Alternatively, he may say, "in all that you will kindle during the 8 nights of Chanukah." The homeowner says, "I hereby transfer to you ownership of a portion of the oil, through this perutah, in my Chanukah lights that I kindle during all 8 nights of Chanukah." Even without a perutah, the homeowner may transfer ownership to him if he wishes, as written below, letter 5, and he should say, "I hereby transfer ownership to you, etc." The lodger should stand beside him during the blessing and intend to fulfill his obligation through the homeowner's blessing, and the homeowner should likewise intend to discharge his obligation, as is known.
