A minor who has reached education, etc. According to what the Mechaber ruled in siman 671, section 2, that even those who are mehadrin min hamehadrin kindle only one lamp for all members of the household, only adding one more lamp each day, here it must be speaking of this minor having a house of his own; therefore he must kindle and bless. But according to our practice, that each person must beautify the mitzvah and kindle on his own, this minor too must in every case kindle on his own, and so ruled Rema above at the end of siman 675. But the view of Shiltei Giborim and Meiri is that even according to our practice, one need not educate the minor to kindle on his own unless he has a house of his own.
