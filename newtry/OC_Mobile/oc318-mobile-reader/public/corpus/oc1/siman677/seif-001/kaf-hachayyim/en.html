1) [Seif 1] A lodger for whom they are not kindling in his home, etc. At first glance, it would appear that the expression "they kindle for him" can be explained in two ways. According to the Rambam, of blessed memory, who holds that each member of the household kindles one light, "they kindle for him" means that they kindle one light on his behalf in his home, as though he were standing there. According to the other poskim, who hold that one light suffices for a man and his household, it means that they kindle within his home, that is, his wife kindles one light and he fulfills his obligation through her kindling. However, the words of the gaon, author of Terumas HaDeshen, siman 101, and those of the other later authorities, of blessed memory, do not imply this, and the matter is not conclusive. Maamar Mordechai, letter 1. See above, siman 671, letter 6.
