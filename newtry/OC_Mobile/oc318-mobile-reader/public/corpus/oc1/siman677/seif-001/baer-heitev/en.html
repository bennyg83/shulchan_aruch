A perutah. He was precise to say a perutah, to teach that the lodger need not give half of the whole cost of the lamps; rather, a perutah that he gives him is enough. See the sefer Gan Hamelech, siman 40. Also in our time it is permitted to participate; Darkei Moshe. Responsa Maharshal wrote that yeshivah students also must participate. Perhaps this is specifically when he eats separately, but if he depends on the homeowner's table, he is included among the members of his household, and by the strict law he does not need to kindle unless he wants to be among those who beautify the mitzvah. One who eats at his friend's house incidentally, the preferred mitzvah is that he go to his home and kindle. If he does not want to move from there, he should command the members of his household to kindle for him in his home. Turei Zahav; see there. Regarding a blind person, see siman 675, paragraph 3, and what is written there.
In the oil. The homeowner must add a little oil for him. Bach, Agudah. Rashba wrote that the same applies if the homeowner transfers ownership to him of a share in the oil as a gift; it is permitted. Magen Avraham.
