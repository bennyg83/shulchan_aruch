(א) It is open to itself and the vacant Soma that is not lit in his house and he is in a room by himself is obliged to light Hanukkah candles as I wrote above in the sign of 1911, section 8:

(ב) that he eats (see words of Orach Chayim s.k. 7) and concludes: since known to all they kindle in winter house where they do not eat, and known each has nearby immediately by eating place a sleeping place — sleeping place is called well for kindling in sleeping place; but one who eats occasionally once or even regularly and sleeping place is far from eating place — then kindle in sleeping place; see further in Shaarei Yeshuvah siman 73. Per the distinction I wrote, both are upheld — Rama's Hagahah and words of Chami Zekeni Maharari cited above.
