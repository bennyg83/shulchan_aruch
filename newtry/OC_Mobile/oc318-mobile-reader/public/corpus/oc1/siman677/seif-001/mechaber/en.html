דין אכסנאי בחנוכה. ובו ד סעיפים:
Din an innkeeper at Hanukkah. And in Section 4: An innkeeper who does not light a candle in his house should give a penny to the owner of the house to share with him the oil of a Hanukkah candle, and if he has an open door for himself, he should light a light at his door, even though that house is not special except for sleeping and he eats at the owner's table, and the same is true of the son who eats at his father's house: Hegah 11 Davzman, this person who lights a light inside should light it where he eats, and this is how they used to do it (Response of the Rabbi C. 1922): ):
