Levush: However, with a perutah, etc. This was for them, but for us it is not so; Mahariv, siman 31. So too Responsa Maharil, siman 176, wrote: nowadays most of the world does not participate, and the reason is suspicion. Until here. But Darkei Moshe and Maharshal, siman 85, permit even in our time. Now Magen Avraham wrote that Mahariv and Maharil are speaking when he eats in a separate room. This is not correct, for Maharil wrote that according to the contemporary practice that each and every person kindles, he comes to be suspected, for all guests kindle, and who will distinguish whether he is married, so that his wife kindles for him, or whether he is unmarried? This is explained in Bach. In Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah it is written that one who participates must hear the blessings from the one who blesses; otherwise he must bless upon seeing the lamps.
