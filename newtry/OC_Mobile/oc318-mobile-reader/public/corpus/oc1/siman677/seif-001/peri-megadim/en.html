In Beis Yosef, that one should not bless, this is either because he holds as he wrote in siman 671, like the explanation of Tosafos about mehadrin min hamehadrin, or because a man and his wife are not included among mehadrin, for they are like one body. See Eliyah Rabbah, siman 671. Here, only members of his household are included among mehadrin. Or, mehadrin applies in the house where they live; there is beautification there. But in lodging on the road, there is no beautification in the house where he lodges, because not everyone knows that there is a guest there. See siman 676 in Magen Avraham, regarding if he forgot, etc., for one may say that mehadrin does not bless; only mehadrin means that he does not want to fulfill through his fellow's blessing, and this is certainly so; see there. Maharshal ruled that where he does not know, he blesses, even though doubt is treated leniently, because he may intend not to fulfill through her lighting since he is uncertain. If he knows that she kindled, he is not permitted to kindle and bless unless he has a separate house with a window because of suspicion, and even then he does not bless, like the case in siman 671, section 8. See Magen Avraham here, paragraph 4; with Hashem's help this will be explained there. What he wrote, that where he does not know, etc., the obligation of the Sages has taken effect, requires examination. If she kindled, why; and if she did not kindle, he blesses on her behalf. From Rabbi Zeira it implies that he is not permitted; was he not included among the mehadrin? As halachah, he ruled like the Rav in the gloss. See Magen Avraham, paragraph 3, nowadays, etc.
