Must give perutah to homeowner, etc. Tur HaShulchan siman 101: guest married wanting to kindle with blessing outside house — Gedol A permits — although Gemara: since married I say certainly not obligated; but if he wants may kindle — not vain blessing, among mehaderin. Gedol A: since not obligated called layman if he does — all the more vain blessing since this hidur not in Talmud; TaZ concludes correctly called mehaderin: as hidur of light per person in one house, so hidur man and wife in two places; tanna did not teach this uncommon hidur; since we find mehaderin and mehaderin min hamehaderin — not adding to Talmud detracts — end. Beit Yosef: should not rely to bless unnecessary blessing — end. Rashal in brief rulings: man and wife certainly suffice one light; married guest not knowing wife kindles for him may kindle and bless; if knows clearly she kindles for him — should not kindle unless alone for viewer — kindle without blessing; where he did not know she kindled and came that night — must return kindle and bless — rabbinic obligation fell; where he does not want fulfill through wife's kindling at home — not so important, cannot bless — end. We have not yet seen Rashal's long words; our view TaZ correct: Gedol A's hidur not in Talmud — Gemara mehaderin means each will likely do so, each wants mehaderin; unlike this also in other blessings permission to do as he wishes — if wants fulfill through wife's blessing, if not — bless himself; compare Rosh chapter 3 two who ate must divide — Rashi between zimmun and hamotzi blessings; Rosh: world did not practice; Rashi — not heard practiced hamotzi blessing; incident of bar Kappara, etc. — no prohibition if each blesses hamotzi — Rosh: not practiced; Heaven forbid Rosh say vain blessing — Rashi included even in mitzvah obligation; found in yotzer or blessing — Rosh responsum: I say with shaliach tzibbur calmly though if hears shaliach tzibbur fulfilled — lest not intend well for blesser's words; Beit HaMikdash Beit Yosef 183 in name of Orchot Chayim in name of Rif: each may bless Birkat HaMazon though they invite — cannot intend well, etc.; but like Rambam he would not bless quietly, etc. — we see one may fulfill through friend's blessing and if unwilling — no prohibition, intent for Heaven; one unwilling to bless himself like Rambam — also intent for Heaven not multiply blessings since can fulfill through friend — here too; all the more not with wife, not hearing her blessing — can say I do not fulfill through wife's blessing not in her presence — listener and responder require presence; even at home unwilling through wife's — I know no prohibition if he blesses himself — like minor chinuch age kindling with blessing — plainly fulfills through father's blessing; we see custom all going outside on Hanukkah take Hanukkah lights to kindle where he goes though household kindles with wife — even if household not intending discharge him only themselves — itself difficult why take lights — household with wife can intend discharge him too; rather preferable bless himself as here — Rama ruled so nearby.
