Paragraph 4: To kindle, etc. As written in siman 671, namely regarding a courtyard that has two entrances, where one must kindle at both because of suspicion. Nevertheless, he blesses only over one.
And one may distinguish, for there, in truth, what he kindles in the second direction has no obligation except because of suspicion. This is not so here. Granted, if he participates with the homeowner, there is no obligation upon him by the strict law except because of suspicion. But now that he kindles for himself, he certainly is not participating, and if so he is obligated by the strict law; therefore he blesses. See section 3. Meaning: in the Tur he wrote that even if they kindle for him inside his home, nevertheless if he has an entrance open for himself, he must kindle because of suspicion. In that kind of case, one can properly say that he should not bless, for in that case he is exempt by the strict law and kindles only because of suspicion. Therefore Magen Avraham hinted to see section 3, where Rema wrote that if they kindled for him inside his home and he does not want to fulfill through the lighting of his home, but wants to kindle in the place where he is and bless, he may do so. If so, also in such a case he may bless if he has an entrance open for himself, even though they kindled for him at home, because he can intend not to fulfill through the lighting in his home. See there, section 3.
