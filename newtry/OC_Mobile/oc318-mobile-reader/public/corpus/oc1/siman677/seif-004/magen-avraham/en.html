That is needed for the measure of lighting. For more than the measure of lighting, it is permitted to use it ab initio, as written in siman 672, section 2. Beis Yosef, Mordechai, Maharshal, and Shiltei Giborim. Bach ruled that when he placed the oil without specification, all the oil was set aside, and so it also seems in Hagahos Maimoniyos, Ran, and Tosafos, Shabbos 44. However, this is not so compelled. Therefore it is good to stipulate ab initio that only the measure should become forbidden. Shiltei Giborim wrote that it requires examination: if he placed enough oil for half an hour and it burned more than half an hour, whether one may use from it.
