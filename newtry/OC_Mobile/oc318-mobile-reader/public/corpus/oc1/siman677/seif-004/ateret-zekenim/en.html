Makes a bonfire for him. It appears all leftover oil even more than kindling measure — only oil in the candle designated for kindling requires bonfire as Bach wrote siman 66 — end; so explicitly Ran Bava Metzia and Tosafot chapter Kirah; so Avudraham in name of Rabbenu Shlomo; also another answer: Chazal equated it to holy utensils because reminder of holy oil. What Bach questioned — Beit Yosef wrote in siman 672 if he put more he may extinguish after time passed and may use afterward, etc. — appears difference: certainly may extinguish to kindle other candles and use its light; nevertheless must burn in manner requiring genizah as Avudraham — examine per this Bach's strains unnecessary — this appears plain, finding most poskim agree — Bach's strain void — examine. Some say leftover from day one kindle immediately night two — at end no remedy; also delaying forbidden lest mishap; nevertheless not called shiluach; nullified in sixty; less than sixty no nullification even by kindling. What ruled above after kindling measure may extinguish and do as he wishes — speaks where he put more than measure; where only measure and it went out or wick bad and tangled — forbidden (Rashal siman 85); however what he wrote even if put more requires genizah as I wrote for halacha.
