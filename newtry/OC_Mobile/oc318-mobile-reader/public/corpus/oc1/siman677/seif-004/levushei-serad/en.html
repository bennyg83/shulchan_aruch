{Rama: s.k. 4} As siman 671, etc. In courtyard with two openings — kindle at both, bless only at one not second since only suspicion. One may distinguish: there different since already blessed at one opening — not so here; even though homeowner blessed — in seif 3 Hagahah wrote one may say I do not fulfill through homeowner's blessing — all the more if not partnered with perutah.
