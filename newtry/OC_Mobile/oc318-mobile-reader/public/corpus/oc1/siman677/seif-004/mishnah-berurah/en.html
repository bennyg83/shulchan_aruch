That remains on the eighth day, etc. For on the earlier nights, if any remains, one may use it for the mitzvah of lighting on the following nights. If it became mixed with other oil, its law is as below.
From the oil needed, etc. This means that at first he placed only the measure needed for lighting, and it went out in the middle and some remained. Therefore it is forbidden, because it was set aside for its mitzvah and it is forbidden to benefit from it. But if he placed much oil in the lamp and some remained, it is permitted to use it ab initio, as written above in siman 672, section 2. Know that some authorities hold that if he placed the oil without specification, all the oil was set aside, unless he stipulated ab initio that only the measure should become forbidden.
