(א) What is left on the eighth day, etc. - the devlilot of the previous man, if there is any left over, he can use them for lighting Mitzvahs on the following nights, and if we mix in the oil according to his law as a dalkamia:

(יח) Of oil required, etc. — he gave only kindling measure, went out mid-way and remainder left — forbidden, muktzeh for mitzvah, forbidden to benefit; if he put much in candle and remainder — permitted l'chatchila as above siman 672 seif 2. Know some poskim hold if oil unspecified — entire oil muktzeh unless stipulated l'chatchila only measure forbidden — see above siman 672 Mishna Berurah s.k. 7.

(ג) And they burned it in P.E. - let them leave it for the next year for the New Year. It is forbidden to put it off for trouble and even to put it in a vessel from the dead, he will not come to eat it.
