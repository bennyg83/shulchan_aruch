Now, to add more than sixty and kindle it in the synagogue for honor, one may say that it is permitted even without nullification in more than sixty. Even to pray near it, one may say it is permitted, and there is a double doubt for leniency: perhaps the halachah is that one may add to a rabbinic prohibition, and perhaps the halachah is that sacred use, to learn and pray, is permitted, as in siman 673. See Turei Zahav there, paragraph 4. Still, this requires examination. See Eliyah Rabbah here, paragraph 7, and what I wrote in my Pri on Yoreh Deah there. And if we say that from one mitzvah to another there is a prohibition, see Ran on the matter of lighting from lamp to lamp. Nevertheless, for the honor of Hashem in the synagogue, and not to pray beside it, one may say that it is permitted according to everyone; and this requires examination.
