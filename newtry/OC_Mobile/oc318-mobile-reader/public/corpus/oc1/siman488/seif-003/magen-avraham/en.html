And the maftir reads etc. That which they read the Musafin portion on Yom Tov we do not find in the Gemara; rather the Geonim instituted it (Beis Yosef citing the Rosh and the Ran); see what I wrote in siman 137.
And see above siman 114 se'if 3. For there the Rama ruled that the congregation mentions [rain] in Musaf until they hear that the chazzan does not mention in Musaf; then they cease at Minchah. And the Levush wrote: and if you would say, let them announce before Musaf that they not say "He makes the wind blow," one may answer that this is as if they refuse rains, in the manner of what they said, one does not pray concerning an excess of rains; end quote. And it appears to me that if an individual delayed his prayer until after the chazzan prayed Musaf, he should not say "He makes the wind blow," since the chazzan has already ceased; and this is not comparable to what the Tur wrote in siman 117 regarding "And grant dew and rain," that an individual is drawn after the congregation; see there; for here the main insistence depends on hearing from the mouth of the chazzan that he stopped.
