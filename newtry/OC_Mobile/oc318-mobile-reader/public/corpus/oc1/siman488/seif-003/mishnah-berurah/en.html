(11) And they do not mention, etc. — The Mechaber's view is that this is the custom of Spain, that even in Musaf itself they do not mention rain; therefore the Rama wrote as he did. See above siman 114 seif 3, where in the gloss it is explained that our custom follows the poskim who hold that the congregation mentions in Musaf until they hear that the prayer leader is not mentioning in his prayer — when he repeats aloud they cease in Minchah. The same applies to an individual who delayed his prayer in Musaf until after the prayer leader prayed and ceased saying He makes the wind blow and the rain fall — he too should not again mention rain. See there in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halachah for several details in this law.

(4) From then on one does not mention — meaning in the diaspora one does not again ask for rain; b'dieved if he forgot and transgressed see above siman 117 seif 3 and Biur Halachah. Although poskim agree the prayer leader should not announce in public that they will never again mention rain lest it seem they refuse rain — nevertheless it is proper that secretly before Ma'ariv of the first night of diaspora he remind most of the world to say viten berachah etc., for most people err on the first night; likewise tomorrow in Shacharit until they hear from the prayer leader he stopped — there is concern of several hundred blessings in vain.

(5) And they used to say, etc. - and in retrospect, even if he didn't mention them at all, he came out. And if he made a mistake in the Musfin and mentioned another day, as well as in Sukkot, he said on the first day of the the Master's Day something that does not belong to that day at all, such as he said on the fourth day, if he did not finish the blessing, he should begin with the verses of the sacrifices, and if he finished the blessing, he should repeat [Haa at the end of Rule 20]:
