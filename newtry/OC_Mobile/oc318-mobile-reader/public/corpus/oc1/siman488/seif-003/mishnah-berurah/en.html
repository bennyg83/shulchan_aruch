(9) And the maftir reads etc.: and see above in siman 137 in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 11.
(10) At that time: and there are who begin "And Yehoshua said to the people, Sanctify yourselves," and so is the practice nowadays.
(11) And they do not mention etc.: the view of the Mechaber is that this is the Sephardi custom, that even in Musaf itself they do not mention rain; and for this the Rama wrote. And see above siman 114 se'if 3, where it is explained in the gloss that our custom follows the posekim who hold that the congregation mention in Musaf until they hear that the chazzan does not mention in his prayer that he repeats aloud; then they cease at Minchah. And the same applies to an individual who delayed his Musaf prayer until after the chazzan prayed and ceased saying "He makes the wind blow and makes the rain fall": he too should no longer mention rain. And see there in Mishnah Berurah and Biur Halacha several details in this law.
(12) From then on [they cease] from asking etc.: that is, on Chol HaMoed one should no longer ask for rain; and after the fact if he forgot and transgressed, see above in siman 117 se'if 3 and in Biur Halacha. And behold, even though the posekim agreed that it is not proper that the shamash announce this matter publicly that they will no longer mention rain and rainwater, for it appears as if they refuse the rains, in the manner of what they said, one does not pray concerning an excess of rains; nevertheless it appears to me that it is very proper that the shamash quietly remind most of the people before Maariv of the first night of Chol HaMoed that they say "And grant blessing" etc., for most ordinary people stumble on the first night, and likewise tomorrow in Shacharis until they hear from the chazzan that he ceased, and in this there is concern of several hundred blessings in vain.
(13) And they are accustomed to say etc.: and after the fact, even if he did not mention them at all, he has fulfilled his obligation. And if he erred in the Musafin and mentioned [the verses] of another day, and likewise on Sukkos if on the first day of Chol HaMoed he said something that does not belong at all to that day, for example that he said [the verses] of the fourth day: if he did not finish the blessing, he should begin from the verses of the offerings; and if he finished the blessing, he need not go back [Chayei Adam at the end of klal 28].
