We take out two Torah scrolls, and in the first, five men read {and if additions are made on Yom Tov, see above, beginning of siman 282} in Parashas Bo, from "Mishchu" until "from the land of Egypt by their legions"; and the maftir reads in the second [scroll], in Parashas Pinchas, "And in the first month"; and the Haftarah is in Yehoshua, "At that time." And we pray the Musaf prayer, and we do not mention rain [geshem] from here onward {and see above, siman 114, se'if 3}; and likewise we cease, from there onward, to request [rain] in Bircas HaShanim. {Rama: And the custom is to say on every Yom Tov, in the Musaf prayer, the verses of the additional offering of the day, after saying "through Moshe Your servant, as it is stated."}
