[3] [Levush] For regarding Kedushah etc. it is relevant etc. It also appears to me to give a reason, for Abudarham explained regarding Netilas Yadayim and "and You sanctified us with Your commandments" as the language of betrothal, and the Holy One, blessed be He, betrothed Israel with mitzvos; and in Maariv of Shabbos he explained "be pleased with our rest" because the Holy One, blessed be He, is called bridegroom, and Shabbos and Knesses Yisrael are called bride, and we say to Him: be pleased that we be as Your bride, and that Your bride rest in You; see there. And if so, they properly belong together, and it is easy to understand. But we find in Musaf of Rosh Chodesh on Shabbos that they say "be pleased with our rest" and "Renew" etc., as the Levush himself wrote in siman 505, and even though it is not adjacent to "sanctify us"; and if so, the same law applies on Yom Tov. And furthermore, if one should not attach it to "sanctify us," they would need to double "our God and God of," as the Levush wrote; and this is not reasonable, and we should not fabricate to say "our God and God of our fathers" in a place where they did not institute it. Therefore it appears to me that the main [view] follows the minhagim, and so it is in all the versions, and so is the practice.
