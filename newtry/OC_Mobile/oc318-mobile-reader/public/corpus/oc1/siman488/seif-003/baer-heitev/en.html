(3) Siman 114. For there Rama ruled that the congregation mentions [rain] in musaf until they hear that the prayer leader does not mention [rain] in musaf — then they cease in Minchah; Levush wrote: why not announce before musaf that they should not say Mashiv HaRuach? — one may say this is as if refusing rain, per what they said one does not pray for abundant rain — end. Magen Avraham wrote: it appears to me that if an individual delayed his prayer until after the prayer leader prayed musaf, he should not say Mashiv HaRuach since the prayer leader already ceased — end; so too Chayei Adam.
