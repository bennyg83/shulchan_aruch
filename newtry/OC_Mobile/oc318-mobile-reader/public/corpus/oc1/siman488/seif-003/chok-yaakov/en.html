And if they add on Yom Tov. See above at the beginning of siman 282, and there the Rama wrote that they are not accustomed to add on Yom Tov; and here in Prague the custom is to add.
And the haftarah in Yehoshua, "At that time." And we begin "And Yehoshua said to the people, Sanctify yourselves," and so it is in the machzorim until "and none went out and none came"; in the Chumashim there are versions that begin from "At that time," like the version of the Rosh and the Tur; and some include also in the conclusion the verse "And Hashem was with Yehoshua"; and see in the responsa of Maharam of Padua, siman 78.
And from here on they do not mention rain. Meaning, after the chazzan has repeated his prayer in Musaf and does not mention rain, the congregation also do not mention rain from Minchah onward; or an individual who delayed his Musaf prayer until after the chazzan prayed; and so Magen Avraham concluded.
