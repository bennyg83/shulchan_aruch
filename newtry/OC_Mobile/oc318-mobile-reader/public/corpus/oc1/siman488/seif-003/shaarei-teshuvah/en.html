In Shulchan Aruch se'if 3: they take out two Sefarim etc. See what I wrote in Shaarei Ephraim, gate 5, if he erred and read in the portion "An ox or a sheep," and other laws of a reader's mistake; from there seek it out. And Beis Yosef wrote: if he finished with the verse "a night of watching," and the fifth oleh already said the blessing, he need not read further; so wrote in the sefer Beis David; see there. And regarding saying the Thirteen Attributes at the Ribbono Shel Olam prayer at the time of taking out the Sefer Torah, he wrote in Pri Eitz Chaim, and this is his language: I found in the machzor of my teacher, of blessed memory, written on the outside from a manuscript of my teacher, of blessed memory: on the first day of Shavuos he should recite the Thirteen Attributes three times, and afterward say "Ribbono Shel Olam, fulfill all requests" etc., and likewise "May it be [Your] will," and he should repeat the prayer three times, and afterward say three times "And as for me, may my prayer," and so he should do at Musaf, and on the second day at Yotzer, and likewise of Pesach and likewise of Sukkos etc.; see there. And I have not seen people accustomed to say the prayer three times, only once alone. And regarding what he wrote "and so he should do at Musaf" etc., Rama'z explained in his letters, siman 210, that is when the chazzan says "His glory fills the world," and on the second day at Yotzer at taking out the Sefer Torah, and on Yom Kippur at Minchah it is also at the time of taking out the Sefer Torah; and from this one will understand that in Eretz Yisrael they do not say it three times, for the reason that they have only one [day of] Yom Tov, and at Minchah there is no Sefer Torah etc.; see there. And see in Shaarei Ephraim, Shaarei Yod, se'if 5, where I wrote that there are those accustomed to begin the Thirteen Attributes "And He passed" etc.; and in Pischei She'arim there I wrote that I saw in some sefer that one should not begin "And He passed," and I wrote that perhaps the reason is that they do not say it by way of request; and now I have seen that this matter was asked of Rama'z there, and he, of blessed memory, answered that one should not say it at the beginning of Yom Tov at all, for then the intention is in higher aspects, and it is the secret of saying them three times, and we have no strength even in a quorum to say them by way of entreaty, only by way of praise, in the manner that they say the Thirteen (Attributes) of "Who is like You"; and from this, that the essence of saying them is as one who reads in the Torah, and specifically on the first days of Yom Tov etc.; see there. And nowadays they are accustomed to say even on the last days of Yom Tov. And see there that also if Yom Tov falls on Shabbos, when they say Berich Shemeih, this prayer is not set aside, for the prayer of Berich Shemeih has its main place only at Minchah of Shabbos etc.; see there. And it is implied that on other Yamim Tovim that do not fall on Shabbos they do not say Berich Shemeih; and nowadays the custom is to say Berich Shemeih also on every Yom Tov even if it does not fall on Shabbos, and also on a weekday at the time of taking out the Sefer Torah, as above.
