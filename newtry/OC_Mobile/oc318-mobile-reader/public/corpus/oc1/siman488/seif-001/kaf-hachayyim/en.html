1) [Se'if 1] In the morning, people enter the synagogue. This means they do not arrive especially early, because on Yom Tov people come later and leave earlier, as taught in chapter 2 of Megillah. Bach.
2) There. The Shabbos songs of praise are recited. Maharil writes that according to our custom, the chapter beginning Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbos is recited, but one begins with Tov LeHodos LaHavayah. Abudraham writes likewise in the name of Rav Saadiah, while noting that the custom is nevertheless to recite the entire chapter because Yom Tov is also called Shabbos. This is our custom. Darkei Moshe, subsection 1; Magen Avraham, at the beginning of the siman; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1. It is not recited when Yom Tov begins as it is on Friday evening. Rather, this chapter is recited privately at the time of Kabbalas Shabbos only for Shabbos; on Yom Tov it is recited during Shacharis together with the other chapters. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Eshel Avraham, subsection 1. See above, siman 51, subsection 49.
3) There. The Shabbos songs of praise are recited. Our custom on Yom Tov of Pesach is to add after Yoshev BeSeiser Elyon the chapter of Tehillim 107, beginning, 'Let those redeemed by Havayah say,' and so forth. On Shavuos, chapter 68, beginning, 'Elokim shall arise; His enemies shall scatter,' and so forth, is added. On the first Yom Tov of Sukkos, the chapter beginning, 'As a deer longs,' and so forth, is added. On the final Yom Tov, Shemini Atzeres, chapter 12, beginning, 'Save, Havayah, for the pious have ceased,' and so forth, is added. Shulchan Gavoah, subsection 1; Yafeh LaLev, volume 5, subsection 1. See above, siman 51, subsection 50.
