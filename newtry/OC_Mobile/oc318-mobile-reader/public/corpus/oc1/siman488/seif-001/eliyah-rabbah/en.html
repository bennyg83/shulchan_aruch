[1] [Levush] And there are who say that he blesses etc. "to complete" etc. In the responsa of the Rama, siman 35, he wrote that in a place where there is no custom he should always say "to read," and so the later authorities agreed. And between the chapters etc.: and these are the between-the-chapters: Halleluyah, Praise, servants of Hashem etc.; When Israel went out of Egypt; Not for us, Hashem, not for us; I loved; Praise Hashem, all nations; From the straits. Radak wrote that "From the straits" is not the beginning of the mizmor, but it begins from "Give thanks to Hashem for He is good" (Abudarham). The laws of Rosh Chodesh and the remaining laws of Hallel are explained in siman 422.
