The Laws of the Morning Prayer on Pesach (Three Sections):In the morning, people enter the synagogue, recite the zemirot (Tehillim) of Shabbat, pray the Shacharit (morning) prayer, and complete Hallel (reciting the blessing “to recite the Hallel”). One should not interrupt during Hallel, except in the manner permitted during the recitation of Shema,in the middle of a paragraph, one may greet their father or teacher and respond to a greeting from an honored person. Between paragraphs, one may greet an honored person and respond to any greeting.Gloss: This rule regarding interruptions applies specifically to the first two days of the festival when Hallel is recited in full. On the days when Hallel is not completed, different rules apply (see Siman 422, Section 4). If one interrupted in the middle and was silent for as long as it would take to recite the entire Hallel, they do not need to start over but should continue from where they left off.
