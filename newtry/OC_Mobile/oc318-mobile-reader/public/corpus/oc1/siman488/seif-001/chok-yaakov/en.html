On which they complete the Hallel. And see above siman 422 in Magen Avraham s.k. 16: it is explained that if he came to the synagogue near Hallel, he should read Hallel first with the congregation and afterward pray; but he should not say Hallel with the congregation in the middle of Pesukei DeZimra, for since they complete the Hallel he must bless at the beginning and at the end, and since he said Baruch She'amar and Yishtabach, how can he bless twice; which is not so on days when they do not complete the Hallel, they say Hallel without a blessing, since in any case many posekim hold not to bless when they do not complete the Hallel.
