(1) And they read the zemirot etc.: that is, the mizmorim that they say before Pesukei DeZimra, and they also say Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbos, for Yom Tov too is called Shabbos. And likewise they say the blessing of Nishmas as on Shabbos, and after Barechu they say the blessing of Yotzer Or as on a weekday.
(2) And they complete the Hallel: and if he omitted one verse or one word, he must afterward begin from that verse and complete until the end of the Hallel; for if he says it wherever he remembers, it is reading out of order and he has not fulfilled his obligation.
(3) To read the Hallel: that is to say, he should not say "to complete the Hallel," lest he skip one word or one letter and there be concern of a blessing in vain; and if he said "to complete," he has fulfilled his obligation; and in a place where they practiced to say "to complete," one should not abolish their custom [Peri Megadim]. If he came to the synagogue near Hallel, he should first read Hallel with the congregation and afterward pray, provided that he not thereby pass the time of Kerias Shema and prayer; and see above in siman 422 in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 16.
(4) As they said regarding Kerias Shema etc.: and for the details of these laws see above siman 66.
(5) In the middle of the chapter etc.: and these are the chapters: Halleluyah, Praise, servants of Hashem etc.; When Israel went out of Egypt etc.; Not for us, Hashem, not for us etc.; I loved etc.; Praise Hashem, all nations etc.; From the straits etc. And Radak wrote that "From the straits" is not the beginning of the mizmor, but it begins "Give thanks to Hashem for He is good" etc.
(6) In the middle: that is, even in the middle of the chapter.
(7) And he delayed etc.: since he stated it without qualification, it implies even if he delayed through ones; and see above in siman 422 s.k. 24 in Mishnah Berurah.
