They say Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbos, for Yom Tov too is called Shabbos (Darchei Moshe). To read the Hallel. So it should read, and so it is in some books and in the responsa of the Rama, siman 35; and so it is implied in the Levush and in the Lechem Chamudos; and one should not say "to complete," lest he skip one word.
