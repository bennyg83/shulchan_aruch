(8) Even ten reading as one: that is to say, even where they discharge others of their obligation by their saying, and we do not say that two voices are not heard distinctly, as we find in siman 141 se'if 2 regarding Kerias HaTorah; for Hallel is beloved to them because of remembrance of the miracle, and they apply their minds and hear. And for this reason also the custom is that two or more recite Kiddush together on Shabbos and Yom Tov, or when several householders sit in the sukkah and recite Kiddush to discharge the members of their households, because Kiddush is beloved to them and they apply their minds and hear; and nevertheless it is better that they not recite Kiddush at one time where they need to discharge household members, but rather one after another.
