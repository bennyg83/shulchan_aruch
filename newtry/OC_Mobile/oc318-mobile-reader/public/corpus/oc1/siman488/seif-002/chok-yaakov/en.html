Even ten reading as one. The reason: since it is beloved because of remembrance of the miracle, they apply their minds and hear, even though in general two voices are not heard distinctly (and see above siman 588 for the reason regarding shofar). And it appears to me that for this reason the custom is that two or more recite Kiddush together on Shabbos and Yom Tov, for it is also beloved to them since they mention that the Holy One, blessed be He, chose us and gave us Shabbosos for rest and festivals for joy, a remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt; so it appears to me to settle the custom.
