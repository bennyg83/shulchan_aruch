So that it will be consumed k'zayit, etc. Seif 8 — even if you say that little too he will sell to an Israelite — nevertheless that little an Israelite will eat k'zayit; and accordingly it implies on erev Pesach at the fifth hour in a matter where it cannot be consumed k'zayit it is forbidden to sell to a gentile even a little at a time from concern that perhaps he will sell to an Israelite — and so is the language of the Gemara and Rambam and other poskim; nevertheless if it chametzed for an Israelite much grain on erev Pesach at the fifth hour and it is in a matter where it is impossible for him to sell to a gentile that it be consumed k'zayit and there is great loss — it is permitted to sell to a gentile who knows him and recognizes him that he will not touch it at all but guard it for him until after Pesach; and he may say to him "until you take it for a maneh," etc. as explained above siman 448 seif 3 and 4 — and then there is no concern for anything.
