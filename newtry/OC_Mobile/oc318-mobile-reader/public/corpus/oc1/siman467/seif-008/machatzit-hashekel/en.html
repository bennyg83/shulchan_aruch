(S.k. 8) And grapes, etc. "The grape-blossoms have opened," which refers to the redemption from Egypt; and they also say Shir HaShirim on Pesach.
Here in the community of Kalisz, etc. And even though they soak, etc., they were not stringent from doubt: meaning even though above in siman 447 se'if 4 in the gloss he wrote regarding bread that fell into wine we are concerned lest crumbs remained in it, etc., and the same would apply here: that case above differs, for that doubt of crumbs is near certainty, which is not so here where it is a complete doubt, and regarding a rabbinic any-amount they were lenient in a doubt, as written in se'if 9 in Magen Avraham s.k. 12. And what they practice, etc.: and even apples cut with a knife, and there is concern they were cut with a chametz knife, and it is explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 96 that apples are a sharp food: nevertheless the waters are permitted as Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 447 se'if 8 regarding olive water; see there.
