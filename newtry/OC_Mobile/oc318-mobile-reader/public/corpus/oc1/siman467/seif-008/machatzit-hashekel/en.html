Here is a great difficulty: kalish, etc., and even though the verse, etc., they were not stringent from uncertainty — meaning even though above siman 447 seif 4 there regarding bread into which wine fell we are concerned lest crumbs remained, etc., and the same law here — this case is different, for there this safek of crumbs is close to certainty, unlike here where it is a full safek; and in a rabbinic prohibition in any amount they were lenient in safek.
