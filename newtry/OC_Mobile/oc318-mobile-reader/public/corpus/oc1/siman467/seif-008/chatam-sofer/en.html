(There in Magen Avraham seif 13) Because of any amount. I would say: and in Sefer Avnei Nezer in siman 447 he rejected all the words of the Nehar Shalom in correct matters; and similarly in Peliti siman 92 seif 17 he brought that in any case one should combine the opinion of the Taz with the lenient ones; and so it appears in my humble opinion that for moist with moist the Taz too agrees that it is forbidden — two any-amounts, and a vessel that absorbed any amount is forbidden in any amount as well, as Avnei Nezer wrote; but a dry thing that absorbed any amount and fell into more than sixty — if there are sides to permit, one may combine the words of the Taz — examine well:
