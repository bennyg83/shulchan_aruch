Honey belonging to a non-Jew — we do not presume it to be forbidden and one may eat it on Passover.Rema: However some are stringent. The custom in these lands is to eat only honey brought in barrels from the hive used to make the drink called mead. Dried figs and dried grapes — whether large or small raisins — depends on local custom, as some are stringent not to eat them and some are lenient. Therefore the custom in these lands is to be stringent not to eat any dried fruits unless it is known they were dried in a way that raises no leavening concern. Sugar is forbidden to eat and even' to keep. On the last day) of Yom Tov one eats dried fruits and rock candy sugar which have no leavening concern.
