(Magen Avraham s.k. 22.) If it broke afterward it is forbidden; and that is like Taz's view in Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 83, that chewing constitutes digestion, but if not chewed, even if it afterward crumbled and diminished, it is not digestion; but Shach's view there is the opposite: even according to the one who holds we do not learn prohibition from tumah and chewing is not digestion, nevertheless if less remains than what it swallowed it is digestion; see there.
