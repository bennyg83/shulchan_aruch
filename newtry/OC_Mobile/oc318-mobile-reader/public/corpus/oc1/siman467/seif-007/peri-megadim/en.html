And figs. See the Taz. Regarding flour, one may say it is fruit juice, but fine flour is a concern. See the Levush. In our case, saffron is also forbidden lest people confuse one case with another. The Levush distinguishes honey from barrels, which is common and not subject to confusion, from saffron, which is not common and people may say it came from overseas. The same applies to dried fruits in some cases.
