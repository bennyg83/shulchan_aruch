7) There. One sells a small amount, etc. Only with chametz does selling small amounts help, because even if the non-Jew sells it to a Jew, the Jew will consume it before Pesach. With other prohibitions, selling small amounts does not help, because we remain concerned that the non-Jew might sell them to a Jew or give them as a gift to a Jewish friend. Magen Avraham, subsection 2; Chok Yaakov, subsection 4; Mekor Chaim, subsection 4; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 4. See, however, Zivchei Tzedek on Yoreh De'ah, siman 84, subsection 33, who cites a dispute whether other prohibited foods may be sold to a non-Jew in small amounts and concludes that one should be lenient only where a substantial loss is involved.
