(Magen Avraham s.k. 19.) He forbade it because there were cracks in it; in my humble opinion it is nevertheless proven that the water is forbidden, for otherwise what of it that there are cracks: certainly nothing of the wheat actually entered the cracks; therefore the water is forbidden because it releases in cold; only the meat is perhaps permitted because it does not release and absorb, and it is forbidden only because of the cracks, that the water enters the cracks; and so it appears as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 447 s.k. 9; and if so, automatically if they cooked them in [those] waters it is forbidden.
