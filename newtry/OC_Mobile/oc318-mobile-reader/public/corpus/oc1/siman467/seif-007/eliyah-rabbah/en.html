(7) Honey, etc. And if mixed b'dieved in a cooked dish — the opinion of Magen Avraham is to forbid when there are not sixty; but Chak Yaakov permits even in less for we rely here on Rabbeinu Yona that it dissolved and became honey; and if it is known certainly they mix flour in it he wrote it is permitted to keep it; and if mixed inside the cooked dish — if there is loss and prevention of Yom Tov joy, one who is lenient did not lose even in less than sixty.
