In Magen Avraham s.k. 11 — and as written siman 447; and all the more so according to what Magen Avraham wrote siman 447 regarding prohibition of something, etc. — as it should be. Meaning in siman 447 regarding if something from a matter cooked in chametz vessels mixed — he wrote there one should not establish prohibition in something d'rabbanan — see there; however what Magen Avraham wrote thus in the name of Darkei Moshe regarding honey — requires study, for in Darkei Moshe it is explained thus regarding saffron; and what he wrote "it is fitting" Ra'osh, etc. — because Ra'osh in responsum permitted saffron and Magen Avraham had another version in Darkei Moshe's words; but the version before us is primary; and what is written here in the gloss "they do not forbid their mixtures" — it appears he means only in small mixtures; and it is possible that even though in Darkei Moshe he wrote "a little mixed," etc. — and all the more so according to what Magen Avraham wrote siman 447, etc. — nevertheless here in the gloss he decided to be lenient even when much mixed, from the first reason he wrote there that it is fitting to rely on Ra'osh; and also his opinion in Darkei Moshe is thus — only that otherwise it is not customary they put in a cooked dish matters like saffron and the like in more than sixty to prepare for profit; another reason that in something otherwise one should not be stringent; but for law he is inclined to permit and rely in mixtures on Ra'osh:
