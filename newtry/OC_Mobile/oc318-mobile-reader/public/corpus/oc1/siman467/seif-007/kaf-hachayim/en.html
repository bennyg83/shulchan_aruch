He sells a little, etc. And specifically in chametz a little at a time helps — for even if you say he will sell to an Israelite, he will eat it k'zayit; but in other prohibitions a little at a time does not help — for nevertheless we are concerned he will sell them to an Israelite or give as a gift to his Israelite friend. Magen Avraham s.k. 2.
