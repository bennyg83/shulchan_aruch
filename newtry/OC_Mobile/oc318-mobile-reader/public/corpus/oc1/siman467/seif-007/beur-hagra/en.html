Se'if 7: A Jew, etc. For it is like chametz on which rubble fell, as written in chapter "Kol Sha'ah," and as Rashi wrote in the first chapter regarding one who makes his house a storehouse; and further, there is a double doubt: perhaps they did not become chametz, and even if they became chametz, perhaps they spoiled; therefore it is permitted to keep them, as written in se'if 2 (Rashba).
