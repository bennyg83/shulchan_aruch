(S.k. 15) The whole dough. This is the language of the Rashba, coming to resolve: since it does not spread more than enough to remove, if so what is the reason of those who say the whole dough or matzah [is forbidden]? Behold it is explained above at the beginning of siman 447 that the law of its mixture is like other mixtures, etc.; but if in other mixtures one would not need sixty but only peeling or removal, even regarding chametz likewise, etc. (end quote). And since here in cold it certainly does not spread more than enough to remove, why did those who say forbid everything? On this he brought the Rashba's words. And one should not say in such a case "here it was found," etc.: meaning it fell now and was not in the dough at the time of rolling, for it is explained in se'if 12 s.k. 18 that from time to time we do not say "here it was found," etc. But in a place of sauce, etc.: and the water, even though cold, he holds has the law of sauce, for the Rashba holds that on Pesach cold also forbids, as Magen Avraham wrote at the end of this s.k.
Nevertheless there is no small matter, etc. Meaning, and if so it is not fitting to forbid because of the last reason that the bread itself made the matzah chametz; nevertheless one should forbid because of the first reasons that he wrote.
