(Magen Avraham s.k. 18) If a wheat kernel was found next to the chicken, etc. — meaning Mesoret Binyamin explains it deals that he took a cooked chicken cold (or boiling but he holds all the more it does not forbid on Pesach) and placed it in the bowl; and now there is uncertainty whether the wheat kernel was in the chicken — if so it cooked now and is forbidden — or it was not, only in the bowl, and permitted; and on this he challenged — let us say "here it was found," etc. and it was not in the chicken, for one does not establish presumptive prohibition from place to place; and Magen Avraham resolved it deals with a boiling chicken — and all the more he says on Pesach; and if so even if you say the wheat kernel was in the bowl when placing the chicken there it is forbidden because in any case it is a second vessel; therefore he wrote to permit because of a double uncertainty that perhaps it became digested and perhaps it fell now; and there is no difficulty — on the contrary let us say "here it was found," etc. and certainly it fell to the bowl — and uncertainty whether it was in the bowl at the time of placing the chicken while still boiling — uncertainty — or afterward when it cooled — one uncertainty forbidden; one may say this is the explanation — for in any case we do not say "here it was found," etc. except regarding not establishing presumptive prohibition from place to place — meaning since the second place stands in presumption of permission and presumptive prohibition was not born in it we do not rely on prohibition from this place — for we do not establish presumptive prohibition; and specifically from time to time we are uncertain properly since in this place presumptive prohibition was found — perhaps this badness was from before; and if so specifically regarding establishing presumptive prohibition from place to place we do not say; but not regarding if we come to permit because of this that we rely on the chicken; and see in Mesoret Binyamin there; but still requires study — for what double uncertainty is here; in any case if there is no prohibition only because we said perhaps in the chicken — fine to say if you say from the chicken perhaps it became digested; unlike if there is a side of prohibition even if it fell to the bowl — if so there is only one uncertainty whether before or after; and if they come to permit and rely that it came from the chicken — there is also uncertainty whether it became digested or not; and one must say this uncertainty whether it cooked with the chicken and was not digested is one uncertainty; and the essence of the double uncertainty is uncertainty whether this wheat kernel is chametz — perhaps it was digested; and if you say it is chametz that was not in the chicken's belly or was and was not digested — nevertheless there is still uncertainty perhaps it did not impart taste in the chicken at all since now it fell in cold; and so this double uncertainty reverses — perhaps it fell now and did not impart taste; and if you say it fell before and imparted taste — perhaps the wheat kernel was digested and has no chametzing; but in my humble opinion Mishna Berurah's difficulty is correct according to what appears in Mordechai — that now at the time of finding it does not touch the chicken; if so we have to say we do not establish presumptive prohibition and it never touched it — as Mishna Berurah wrote there regarding pieces salted with milk; and if so even prohibition from second vessel is not here; however in my humble opinion one may say in Mishna Berurah's difficulty — since in any case this wheat kernel fell from elsewhere to the bowl — if so there is properly concern it fell from the chicken since sometimes there are wheat kernels in it;
