Even though they did not split open — forbidden, etc. And even though regarding l'titah they are forbidden only if they split open — that case is different for one is engaged with it — Tur in the name of R' Efrayim. Magen Avraham s.k. 3; and that which is written below in seif 9 that if it did not actually split open the cooked dish is permitted even though they rested inside the cooked dish — nevertheless one may say since the cooked dish is boiling it is like pickling that does not leaven according to Tur siman 461 and 467 — see there — only there is concern it was in water before boiling; therefore we are not so stringent; and further one may say — and this is primary — that in a cooked dish that is only a mixture of something d'rabbanan we are not so stringent, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 12 — see there; and according to what Rambam wrote chapter 5 it appears the reason is simple — for grain that was kneaded or cooked and did not actually split open is not Torah chametz, and also something d'rabbanan they did not forbid two rabbinic prohibitions.
