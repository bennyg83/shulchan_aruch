Its spreading. And it deals with a lean chicken, for otherwise it spreads and all of it is forbidden as written siman 447 seif 1:

Spreads. Specifically regarding rabbinic prohibition of uncertainty we are concerned for this; unlike other prohibitions — see Chayei Adam and in Magen Avraham; and Ateret Zekenim wrote: and if he does not turn the spit, it appears from the opinion of Rama that roasting has the law of salting — end quote:

One in another. But if they touch one another they do not forbid, and we do not say that absorption goes throughout the entire vessel, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 92 — there it deals with cooking. And if so, if the chicken is fatty, even if they do not touch they forbid everything — Magen Avraham. Nevertheless when they are not split open it appears to me one need not be stringent — Chayei Adam. It is written in Shachnahag: an incident came to me on the seventh night of Pesach — they roasted two chickens and a barley kernel that is not split open was found in one of them, and I commanded to cut away the place of its spreading according to estimation and throw it away and eat the rest; and they said they were already cut into pieces with the others and do not know where it is — and I permitted everything see there. And according to his method it goes as written in small seif 40; but the opinion of other later authorities is not so as written.
