And tsuker. Shaarei Teshuvah — and see in responsum Noda B'Yehuda part 1 siman 23 and in Mahadura Tinyana siman 72 that he wrote regarding powdered tsuker brought in barrels and some are concerned for mixtures of flour, and also perhaps the barrels are from barrels in which they carry flour to that country where it grows; and he wrote that if the seller is among the great merchants one should not protest in a place where the custom is to be lenient, and in any case they should be careful to take that powder immediately when the gentile opens the barrels and take from the middle of the barrels — see there; and see there in Chayyei Adam siman 24 that he wrote that in the place of its growth one need not be concerned for forgery because there flour is twice as expensive as tsuker, therefore the custom when it is brought in a ship to Hamburg or Amsterdam is that the rabbi sends his agents to seal the barrels and from there they bring them sealed with certification from the rabbi — see there; and see in She'elat Ya'avetz part 2 siman 65, etc., that kistin tsuker from the note — its permission was released by our teacher the Noda B'Yehuda; and see in responsum Tiferet Tzvi simanim 18 and 19 that he also wrote to permit that brought in barrels from distant places from the place of tsuker growth itself, such as to merchants of the land of Israel, and the tsuker is bronze dye and one need not be concerned for a mixture of flour, which is a distant concern and is not equal in appearance; but if it is not bronze dye one should be somewhat stringent even though from its place of growth there flour is cheap — nevertheless there is a decree lest it be confused; one should also buy from other merchants who store on the rabbi's route and pour from vessel to vessel, and there is concern that perhaps where flour is cheap they mix flour there; we are concerned even for a gentile merchant — see there; and it appears that in a place of need or loss one may permit in this when it is known that all year barrels of tsuker come to him from their place of growth as they are even if not bronze, and they give him a certification on the barrels that come to him on this route, and there is no concern for confusion to buy from one who has no certification, for it is known that certainly no certification was given to him except after investigation that there is no concern — unlike one who has no certification, the buyer should be concerned for himself:
