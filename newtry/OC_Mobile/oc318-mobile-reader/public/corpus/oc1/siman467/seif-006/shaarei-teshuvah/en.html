Shaarei Teshuvah — and he wrote in responsum of Mahari`t Tzahalon siman 45 that a crate full of wheat stood and he passed a bowl of water from place to place and the water fell on the crate, and it was before Pesach; since there was only one bowl that does not contain enough to soak even a tenth of the wheat and they flowed via an incline — therefore if there are cracks in the edges of the box between board and board through which water flows and does not collect so that the wheat becomes soaked, they were permitted; and all the more so here where it fell only on a little, one can say it was nullified before its time of prohibition; and if there are no cracks there, one discards what is at the edges of the box in the place where there is concern that water collected there, and the rest is permitted — see there; and it appears that l'chatchila one should grind the wheat before Pesach:
