And know in Yoreh De'ah 84 and Shach letter 37 honey is accustomed to preserve and sustain things hidden in it; as Rashba wrote in responsum 80: whole things preserve; like Bava Batra regarding Herod who hid seven years in honey; and cut things quickly consumed — see there; therefore whole grains found in honey on Pesach even though long time — soaked preserves; and soaked in soft honey honey becomes chametz and forbids the dish in any amount — no distinction whether there was living soul in it or one grain unlike fine flour long time turned to honey — permitted b'dieved as written. And it will be explained in Magen Avraham 7:

And figs — Taz wrote flour — one might say fruit juice and fine flour concern — see Levush; and in my humble opinion zafarin too forbidden lest substitution; and see Levush: honey from a barrel is common and we are not concerned for substitution unlike zafarin in my view not common — they say it came from overseas; and so dried fruits from Israel people buy known and common to dry for Pesach and no substitution. And fruit a gentile dried with chametz bread in one oven — from aroma one might say b'dieved forbid the mixture — we hold bread aroma does not forbid — see siman 447; nevertheless one might say touched chametz bread. And raisins red — not eaten; small black ones some permit — see there and Magen Avraham letter 8 and it will be explained there:
