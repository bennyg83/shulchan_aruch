(9) That they bring, etc. from the hive, etc. And he did not write in a barrel from the hive as Rama wrote — because that is forbidden in eating in its own form and is not permitted except what they bring with small combs intact as it lies in the hive that has not yet been crushed (Magen Avraham); and now it works well that Levush opened at the beginning of the seif "some forbid" — only there in a barrel; and Rashal and Bach forbade eating honey in its own form in every case lest it be confused; and it appears they agree in this that what they bring thus it appears to permit — for in this it does not come to be confused; and Taz is lenient to eat even in its own form when it comes in a barrel from the hive; and so ruled Chak Yaakov; and I already wrote everything is according to custom in this:
