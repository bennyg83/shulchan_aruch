If they actually split open — even a crack of any size (Peri Megadim):

The entire cooked dish is forbidden — see Mishna Berurah what he wrote that it is forbidden in benefit, and this is from the words of the Acharonim; but in truth according to what the Mechaber ruled below in the next seif that it is permitted to sell to a gentile, etc., here too like him it is permitted to sell the cooked dish to a gentile, for it is not sold for more on account of the release of any amount absorbed from the wheat kernel; and it appears to me that here it deals when the wheat kernel is still inside it, for from the end of the Mechaber's words it appears at the beginning that he forbids the cooked dish even after removing the wheat kernel from it — uncertain:

Even if they did not split open — see Mishna Berurah what he wrote in the name of Kaf HaChayyim; and behold although some Acharonim cited the words of the Taz for practical halachah, nevertheless it appears to me that one who is lenient like the words of Kaf HaChayyim and the Acharonim with him has not lost, after in truth most of the Rishonim and almost all of them agreed to the words of the Rif to permit the cooked dish even in eating, and the Peri Chadash also agreed thus — at least we permit in benefit; and especially where it was not close to splitting but only softened — it appears to me we can rely to be lenient, and we can also combine the opinion of the She'iltot and Razah and Rabbeinu Tam who always hold in sixty — end quote:
