Se'if 13: Used that day. For otherwise [the absorbed taste] gives a spoiled taste and is permitted according to the Shulchan Aruch in siman 447 se'if 10 (Beis Yosef).
During Pesach. For before Pesach it is nullified in sixty within the cistern (Beis Yosef). And even though hagalah does not help during Pesach, as written in siman 452 se'if 1 in the gloss; and even according to the Shulchan Aruch who permits [chametz that] gives a spoiled taste, nevertheless for [a vessel] used that day it does not help; and one should not say as Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Darkei Moshe that they koshered before the time of prohibition and [the water] went down into the cistern on Pesach, and even though it is taste imparted to taste imparted of permitted [food], nevertheless l'chatchila it is forbidden, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 95 se'if 2 in the gloss; aside from the fact that the matter itself is forced, that the case is of that sort: first, according to the Shulchan Aruch there even l'chatchila it is permitted, and even according to Beis Hillel there it is only because of custom, and here it implies it is forbidden by law; and further, even according to those who forbid there, in such a case of three imparted tastes it is permitted, as Tosafos wrote at the end of Avodah Zarah and the Rosh in chapter "Kol Sha'ah," and as above in siman 432 se'if 1; rather he holds like those who say that so long as it is boiling it releases [taste] and does not absorb, as written there; and according to that view even during Pesach it is permitted to do hagalah in any event, only that they should not rest from their boiling, as above there.
Because of, etc. See Magen Avraham.
