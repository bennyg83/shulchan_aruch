During Pesach. The Bach wrote that even if the waters became mixed before Pesach in a manner that they were nullified in sixty, although this is also secondary taste from permitted matter, nevertheless one should not initially use those waters, since other water can easily be found. This is unlike a cooked dish, which is considered after the fact, for if we prohibit it there would be no food to eat on Pesach. The Magen Avraham's words indicate similarly.
