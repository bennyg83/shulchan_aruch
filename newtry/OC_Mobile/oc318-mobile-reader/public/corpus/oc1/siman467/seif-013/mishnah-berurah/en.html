(65) Day-old — and it deals that they performed hag'alah before its time of prohibition; for otherwise one may not perform hag'alah on a day-old vessel, as explained above in siman 452; and the Mechaber cited day-old because if the pot was not day-old, even the hag'elah water itself is permitted on Pesach, since they hag'eled before Pesach and they are not day-old, as explained above in siman 451 and siman 447 seif 5 in the gloss. See there. And know that if the hag'alah was during Pesach, then even if the pot is not day-old, the water in the pit is forbidden according to the Rama above in siman 447 seif 10 in the gloss:

(66) And they descended, etc. during Pesach — that is, the descent was during Pesach, but the hag'elah was before Pesach; and if the descent was also before Pesach, the water was nullified in sixty in the water in the pit; and there are those who are stringent that even if there are sixty against them, one may not use that water l'chatchila, since one can easily find other water. Forbidden to drink its water — and if one transgressed and cooked from them a cooked dish, Peri Megadim wrote one may permit if it is great loss, since the hag'elah was before Pesach:

(67) Because they became mixed — explanation: even for those who permit in cold above because it does not impart taste at all — nevertheless here it is forbidden because through becoming mixed, behold the body of chametz is mixed in it. If grain kernels are found in a water well on Pesach, the Acharonim wrote it is proper to be careful in everything possible not to use that water on Pesach even for those who permit in cold, lest it was pickled in it from the outset and is like pickled; and nevertheless if he has no other water, since his life depends on it, one may permit because of double uncertainty — lest it fell now; and if you say it fell from the outset previously, perhaps it did not split open and soften until within the outset and did not become pickled from when it became chametz; and we rely on the poskim that cold does not forbid without pickling.
