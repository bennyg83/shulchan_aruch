Into a cistern during Pesach. See Mishnah Berurah; and I did not copy what the Taz wrote, that sometimes even after the fact it is forbidden where [the water] rests and stands, etc., for in Sefer Nahar Shalom he questions his words, and also in Sefer Bigdei Yesha he objects to this, that regarding a clear liquid this does not apply; and his view there is that this matter did not come from the mouth of the Taz, of blessed memory; see there.
