If Taz: see seif 8:11 Shach 27 and 105 Taz 4 all the more forbid without pressing circumstances for it purges and absorbs unlike together; siman 447 Magen Avraham 9; 454 letter 3 and 13 see there; chametz in any amount forbids more; one hard grain to chicken if Yom Tov in water; before Pesach equals other prohibitions; this plucking — though Yoreh De'ah 88:11 forbids l'chatchila in any amount all the more here salted on feathers rinsed plucked in any amount:
See the Beit Yosef. At the end of the siman he cites the Mordechai regarding a chicken scalded on Erev Pesach in a chametz vessel, and they forbade the chicken. In truth, on Erev Pesach until night, in a secondary vessel one may permit, and pouring from a primary vessel forbids only the surface layer. Perhaps this follows the opinion that until night even a minute amount applies. Nevertheless, even if a secondary vessel extracts and causes absorption like the Rashba, it does not do both simultaneously. If Pesach vessels were rinsed in a chametz kettle during Pesach in a secondary vessel, in a case of major loss there is room to permit, whether ben yomo and certainly if not ben yomo.
