(41) Permitted to sell to a gentile — after he removed the wheat kernel from it and only the taste of chametz that the wheat kernel released in the chicken remains; and he may not retain it in his house lest he come to eat it:

(42) And in these countries, etc. — and in a place of very great loss the Acharonim agreed to be lenient according to the Mechaber's view, as written above at the beginning of siman 447:

(43) And if it was found, etc. — that is, even if the mixture occurred from before the eighth day; and the same applies even if one transgressed and retained until this day:

(44) Permitted to retain it — because it is an uncertainty of Yom Tov or weekday, and a mixture of any amount is rabbinic; therefore we are lenient to retain it until after Pesach, and then it is permitted even in eating [and some say that even on the seventh of Pesach if a split wheat kernel is found in a cooked dish or in a chicken, one covers them with a vessel until after Yom Tov, and then one eats the cooked dish or the chicken and burns the wheat kernel; and in a place of loss one may rely on this]; and the same applies regarding flavor imparted for deterioration — even though we are stringent on Pesach as explained in siman 447, nevertheless it is permitted to retain it; but complete chametz forbidden d'oraisa — even on the last day it is forbidden to retain it:
