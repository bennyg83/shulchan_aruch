Se'if 10: A chicken, etc. As written in Avodah Zarah se'if 5b regarding that heap of wheat, etc. And in the Mordechai chapter 2; and so wrote the Ramban there from the Yerushalmi, third chapter of Orlah, on the mishnah "A garment that one dyed," etc.; and they brought it in the Milchamos there and in the Ran on Avodah Zarah there, and Tosafos on Avodah Zarah there; and this is its language: R' Chaggai said: When I came down from the ship I heard the voice of R' Yaakov bar Acha: The mishnah [says] a garment dyed with orlah peels must be burned; and we learned: He took from it a weaver's shuttle and wove with it into the garment: forbidden in benefit; and we learned: Yayin nesech that fell into a cistern: all of it is forbidden in benefit; and we learned: Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says: Sell all of it to a non-Jew except for the value of the yayin nesech in it. R' Yaakov bar Acha said: The difficulty is as follows: There, people are not accustomed to buy wine from a non-Jew; but here people are accustomed to buy a garment from a non-Jew; therefore whenever it is not the way to buy from a non-Jew, it is permitted regarding all prohibitions. And so is the view of the Rash on Orlah there, and Tosafos in Zevachim 72b s.v. "all of them," and in Yevamos chapter 1 s.v. "all of them," etc. Only that the Mordechai raised a difficulty: that the drop around the wheat kernel is chametz, and it should be like wine in wine, which is permitted only for rabbinic stam yeinam and not for a Torah prohibition, as written there; but in the Milchamos there he wrote that other prohibitions, even though their prohibition is a Torah matter, we nevertheless compare them to stam yeinam, for we are more stringent in stam yeinam than in all [other] prohibitions, [forbidding] in any amount; and we say in the second chapter of Pesachim and the second chapter of Avodah Zarah: And if you say this is Torah [law], etc.: Whatever the Sages enacted, etc.; and specifically regarding full yayin nesech, which is among the prohibitions of non-Jews, we are stringent, etc.; and so wrote the Ran on Avodah Zarah there; only he wrote that regarding redemption, even for stam yeinam it is specifically barrel by barrel and not wine in wine; and see what I wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 142.
That is not sold, etc. Meaning, one need not say "except for the chametz," etc.; and as written regarding that boat of wheat in the fifth chapter of Avodah Zarah: Rava permitted them to sell them, etc., without specification; Mordechai there siman 811 in the name of R' Yechiel of Paris; see there; and see Tosafos to Chullin 99 s.v. "and the halachah," etc.
And in these lands, etc. For he holds that specifically regarding rabbinic stam yeinam they were lenient, and as written there Avodah Zarah 1 [regarding] practical law, etc.; and see Mordechai at the end of siman 811 and siman 813. And even though regarding other matters we do not distinguish between stam yeinam and chametz, as written there folio 34a and in chapter "Kol Sha'ah": And if you say, etc.: Whatever the Sages enacted, etc.; here the Gemara distinguishes explicitly.
And if it was found on the day, etc. See Magen Avraham; and the same applies in se'if 9 regarding a cooked dish in which wheat and barley were found.
