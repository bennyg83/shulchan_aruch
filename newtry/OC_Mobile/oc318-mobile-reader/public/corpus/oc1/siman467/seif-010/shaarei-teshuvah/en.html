(In the gloss to se'if 10.) They practice to burn everything; and see siman 447 and what the later authorities wrote on this; and see responsa Chacham Tzvi siman 86, whose view is that even on the seventh day of Pesach, if a wheat kernel is found in a cooked dish or in a chicken, one covers them with a vessel until Motzaei Yom Tov, and then eats the cooked dish and the chicken and burns the wheat kernel; and not like the later authorities who strained to be stringent to burn it through a non-Jew, for even on chol ha-mo'ed there is no Beis Yosef prohibition here; and the reason they practiced to burn it is because of a [possible] pitfall, and on the seventh of Pesach covering with a vessel suffices; see there (and see Eliyah Rabbah siman 446, who also wrote to permit). And see there: if the non-Jew himself comes to take it then on the seventh of Pesach, it is forbidden to protest his taking it even regarding a prohibition of any amount; but on the eighth day of Pesach one may protest his [taking it].
