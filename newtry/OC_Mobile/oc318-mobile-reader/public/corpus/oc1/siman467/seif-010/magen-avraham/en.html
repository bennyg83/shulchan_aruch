It may be retained. Because it is uncertain whether the day is Yom Tov or a weekday, as Tur writes. Meaning, the prohibition of even the smallest amount is D'Rabbanan, so we are lenient regarding retaining it. Complete chametz, however, is forbidden even on the last day of Yom Tov, as written in siman 448. Hagahos Maimoniyos, which gives the reason that the prohibition is D'Rabbanan, likewise intended to be lenient only regarding the prohibition of the smallest amount. See siman 447 and Beit Yosef.
