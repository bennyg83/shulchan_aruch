And in a responsum in Yoreh De'ah siman 175 he explained for practice that redemption suffices by force of a double doubt: perhaps the law is that for chametz redemption suffices, and perhaps the law is that any amount does not forbid for benefit; see there. And he further concluded that chametz mixed with matzah as dry with dry according to all has a remedy through redemption; and he further concluded that one should not distinguish between one wheat kernel or even many in such a way that there is not sixty against them, for still there is the reason that it is not sold more dearly because of this; see there and in siman 176.
