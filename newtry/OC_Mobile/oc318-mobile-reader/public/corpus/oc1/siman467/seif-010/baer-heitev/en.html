To keep it. Because it is a doubt of Yom Tov versus weekday, and a mixture of any amount is rabbinic; therefore we are lenient to keep it. And all the more so regarding [chametz that] gives a spoiled taste, for we are stringent on Pesach as written in siman 447: it is permitted to keep it on the last Yom Tov, for that is more lenient than a mixture of any amount; but regarding full chametz, which is forbidden according to the Torah, even on the last Yom Tov it is at least rabbinically forbidden. And this is specifically if found on the eighth day; but if found during Pesach one must burn and destroy everything on chol ha-mo'ed; and if found on the seventh of Pesach, see what I wrote above in siman 446 s.k. 4.
