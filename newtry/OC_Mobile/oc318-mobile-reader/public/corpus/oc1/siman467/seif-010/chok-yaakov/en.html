This implies that even if the mixture occurred during Pesach, and even if one transgressed and deliberately kept it, as explained above at the end of siman 447, we are lenient regarding a minute mixture on the final day, which is D'Rabbanan, and allow him initially to keep it. This is only if it was found on the eighth day. But if it was found during Pesach, one must burn and destroy everything on Chol HaMoed. If it was found on the seventh day of Pesach, see what I wrote above at the beginning of siman 446 as to how he should conduct himself, and see there, where the implication of the later authorities is as written here.
