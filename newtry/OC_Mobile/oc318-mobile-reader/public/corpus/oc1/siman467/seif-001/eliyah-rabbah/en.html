(1) [Levush] And it is not recognizable, etc. So wrote the Tur, and Magen Avraham wrote it implies that if the chametz is recognizable it is permitted and we are not concerned perhaps he will grind them and sell them since flour is not bought from the market except in time of distress — until here; and Chak Yaakov challenged that nevertheless when one keeps it on Pesach he transgresses bal yeira'eh, etc.; rather the Tur's intent in what he wrote "and the chametz is not recognizable" is for amplification that even when it is not recognizable we are concerned for mishap, and all the more so when recognizable. And in my humble opinion he holds that if because of this they did not decree — for one nullifies it in what he says "in something you do not see" — and so in Rabbeinu Yerucham it is explicit in concern lest one eat on Pesach; also what he wrote that the Tur's intent is for amplification is very puzzling — behold he concluded "since chametzing is not recognizable" like Levush; also it seems to me the Tur according to his reasoning based on what Shach wrote in Yoreh De'ah end of siman 86 that the Tur holds one may sell a spoiled egg and we are not concerned they will put it in bread — see there, and there in my books on Yoreh De'ah I expanded; also I can compare to the Gemara Bava Metzia daf 60 — "my measure is known" — and if you say he added water, if so there is no end to the matter, and it is strained to distinguish:
