Laws of wheat upon which water fell, and a cooked dish in which a wheat kernel was found. Contains 16 sections Grain that was submerged in a river or upon which water fell , just as it is forbidden to eat it, so too it is forbidden to keep it. Rather one sells it to a Jew and informs him so that he eats it before Pesach. If he sold it to a non-Jew before Pesach, he sells a little to each one so that it will be consumed before Pesach, lest the non-Jew resell it to a Jew.
