דין חטים שנפל עליהם מים ותבשיל שנמצא בתוכה חטה. ובו טז סעיפים:
The law of wheat upon which water fell, and a cooked dish in which wheat was found. And in it are 16 seifim: Grain that was submerged in a river or upon which water fell — just as it is forbidden to eat it, so it is forbidden to keep it; rather one sells it to a Jew and informs him so that he eats it before Pesach. And if he sold it to a gentile before Pesach, he sells a little to each one so that it is consumed before Pesach, lest the gentile return and sell it to a Jew:
