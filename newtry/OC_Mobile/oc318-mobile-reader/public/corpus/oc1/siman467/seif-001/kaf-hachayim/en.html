(1) [Seif 1] Grain that was submerged in a river, etc. and there is chametzing concern in them and it is not recognizable in them. Tur. Levush. It implies that if the chametz is recognizable it is permitted (to sell even much to one gentile — R' Zalman osef 4) and we are not concerned perhaps he will grind them and sell them since flour is not bought from the market except in time of distress as written siman 453 — and not like Acharonim. Magen Avraham beginning of the siman; however Ran in chapter 25 wrote that even if they split open and chametzing is recognizable in them — nevertheless it is forbidden to sell to gentiles for we are concerned perhaps he will grind them and afterward sell to an Israelite — end of his words; and so Acharonim osef 1; and he wondered at the Tur's language that implies if the chametz is recognizable it is permitted — see there; and this is what Magen Avraham wrote "and not like Acharonim." And the Ran's words mentioned above — Chayei Adam brought osef 1 and challenged Magen Avraham's words mentioned above — for nevertheless we are concerned perhaps an Israelite will take that flour k'zayit and keep it in his house and transgress bal yeira'eh; and he wrote the Tur's intent is that even though the chametz is not recognizable and one knows only there is chametz concern — nevertheless we are concerned for mishap regarding sale, and all the more so in known recognizable chametz we are concerned l'chatchila — see there; however Acharonim osef 1 wrote to resolve Chayei Adam's difficulty — for Magen Avraham holds that if because of this they did not decree — for one nullifies it in what he says "you did not see it" — see there; and Mekor Chayim in Biurim agreed to Magen Avraham's words — see there. And Erech HaShulchan osef 1 wrote to permit selling to a gentile by force of a double uncertainty — perhaps he will sell to a gentile; and if you say he will sell to an Israelite — perhaps he will sell it as wheat that is recognizable — see there; but Rav Beit Din siman 232 agreed to the Ran's words — see there. It is found this matter is in dispute — therefore l'chatchila if possible one should sell a little to each one or sell it in a manner they return it after Pesach as written above siman 448 seif 3; and if impossible and there is great loss — then one may rely on the lenient ones; and so R' Zalman osef 4 that with great loss one may rely on the lenient ones:
