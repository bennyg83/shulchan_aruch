(S.k. 11) They are not, etc. For the Rosh is worthy, etc.; and so the Mechaber ruled: honey of a non-Jew we do not establish [a presumption], etc.; and the Rosh wrote the reason: that it is uncommon that flour be mixed into it, and also it was nullified before Pesach (end of the Rosh's words).
And as written in siman 447, etc. This too is from Darkei Moshe's language; and so it is in Shulchan Aruch above in siman 447 se'if 5: however if any amount of these things mixed, etc.; and see there in Magen Avraham.
Siman 447 regarding dry meat, s.k. 16 in the name of the Bach. And in Yoreh De'ah siman 69 se'if 9 in the gloss: if meat was salted and not rinsed with the final rinse and dried, even if they put it into a boiling kli rishon it is permitted, since it dried there is certainly sixty against the blood.
