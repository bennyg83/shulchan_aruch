The entire dough. The Rashba siman 176 wrote: not in one place alone did the wheat kernel touch, but here and there through the rolling of the dough; and one should not say here it was found here it was (seif 12); and further the water in the dough carries what comes out from that grain throughout the dough — they did not say peeling except dry with dry; and so even in a place of moisture, most of the dough is like circumcision at the time of circumcision, but in a place of gravy they said gravy carries even a thing that does not spread; and so bread found in dough — the bread is apt to crumble and crumbs mixed in the dough, aside from that it itself may leaven others even though it is baked, only my mind does not lean that a small thing leavens all the dough, etc. — see there; it is proven he holds it forbids even in cold — see seif 12:

Here it was found here it was. Explanation: we say now it fell into this matzah and was never in the dough; and if so, if the wheat kernel is full of water and moist through and through, it appears it was in water and in dough — if so one must forbid all the dough; and nevertheless one can say perhaps it was elsewhere swallowed from water and fell now into the matzah. And it appears all is according to the matter: if there is something to rely on, we rely — forever we rely on the common, whether to be lenient or stringent; and it is not called uncertainty except when there is equal reliance on this and that, as in Yoreh De'ah siman 55; and the Bach wrote, and this is its language: perhaps now it fell, and if you say it was before, perhaps it did not leaven — only if softened it is forbidden, but if not softened all is permitted — end of his words; and see seif 12. Mordechai Margaliot wrote in a responsum: one took cabbage in a bowl from a barrel and brought to another house, and there a wheat kernel was found on it — he permitted the cabbage in the barrel, for we say here it was found here it was — end of his words; but if a wheat kernel was found in the barrel, the cabbage in the bowl is also forbidden, for we say perhaps the wheat kernel was before in the barrel, as in seif 12 regarding the chicken; and similarly one who has dough in hand, cut matzot from it and gave to others, and one matzah remained in his hand and a wheat kernel was found on it — all are forbidden, for we say perhaps it was on it before; for specifically from place to place we do not establish prohibition; and according to the Bach who considers it single uncertainty — if so always permitted for this reason; and it appears to me this uncertainty did not leaven is not uncertainty of lack of knowledge, as the poskim wrote in Yoreh De'ah:
