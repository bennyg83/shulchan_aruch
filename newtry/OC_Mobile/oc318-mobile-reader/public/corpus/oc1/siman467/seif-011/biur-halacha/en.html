And we say "here it was found," etc. — Chemed Moshe wrote: if it has not yet been shaped and rolled and a wheat kernel was found on top of the piece of dough in his hand — since we say "here it was found," etc., if so it has not yet given taste in this piece and it is permitted; and this is if it was found on top of the piece and even stuck to the dough; but if it was found inside the dough one must forbid the entire dough, for if we say it fell in this piece, since it has not yet been shaped on the piece it should be found on the piece and not inside it; rather it was certainly in the complete dough, and if so the entire dough is forbidden, and it is not applicable here to say "here it was found," etc.:
