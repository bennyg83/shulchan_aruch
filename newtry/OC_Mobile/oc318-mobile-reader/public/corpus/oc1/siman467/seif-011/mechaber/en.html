If a wheat kernel was found embedded in dough or in baked matzah — one removes from it the amount required for removal and the rest is permitted; and there is one who forbids the entire dough or that matzah, and it is fit to be concerned for his words if not in a place of great loss or in a time of distress:And so they are accustomed during Pesach to forbid that matzah in which the wheat kernel was found and to permit the others, whether it was found in it baked or raw, and we say "here it was found, here it was." And if it was found in dough they forbid the entire dough — specifically during Pesach; but before Pesach they forbid only the amount required for removal, and so is the custom::
