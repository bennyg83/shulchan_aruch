If a split wheat kernel was found in dough or in baked matzah — one removes from it the amount required for removal of the place and the rest is permitted. Some forbid the entire dough or that matzah, and it is proper to be concerned for their opinion except in case of great loss or pressing need. {Rama: Rema: The custom during Passover is to forbid that matzah in which the wheat was found and to permit the others — whether it was found baked in or raw — and we say "here it was found, here it was," meaning it was always there. If it was found in the dough, the entire dough is forbidden. This applies only during Passover, but before Passover one only forbids the amount required for removal. Such is the custom.}
