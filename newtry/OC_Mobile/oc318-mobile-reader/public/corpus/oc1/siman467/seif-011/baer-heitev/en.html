Levush elaborated to challenge the Rabbi on this, and his opinion is to permit in dough in every matter that is not noten ta'am in cold see there; and Taz concludes that even though regarding other cold there is to permit according to Levush's opinion, nevertheless in dough — through engagement with the dough one compresses the wheat and it leavens and forbids the entire dough during Pesach if it softened; and so too in chametz bread one should be stringent even in cold, for there is concern for crumbs. And Ateret Zekenim raised thus, and such is the conclusion of Bach and Maharshal see Magen Avraham and Taz. And Chayei Adam wrote: nevertheless it appears to me according to what is written above that if the wheat is not embedded one may permit in benefit on the festival; if so, all the more so here in cold, for the opinion of many decisors is to permit in every matter; and one who is lenient even in eating has not lost, for it is possible that even when they split open one may be lenient in cold on the festival see there:

Was. Meaning that we say: now it fell into this matzah and it was never in the dough. And Magen Avraham wrote: if the wheat was full of water and moist through and through — if so, it certainly was in water and in dough; if so, one must forbid the entire dough; nevertheless if there is to attribute that it was swallowed from another place from water and fell into the matzah — we attribute and everything according to the matter, for we attribute in the common whether to leniency or stringency, and it is not called uncertainty at all. And he further wrote in Magen Avraham: that which we say here "here it was found, here it was" — this is specifically from place to place or from piece to piece; but from time to time in one piece we establish presumptive prohibition. For example, if he has dough in his hand and cut from it and a piece of dough remained in his hand and a wheat kernel was found on it — all are forbidden, for we say perhaps it was there beforehand; and one may not say that it is a double uncertainty — perhaps it did not leaven — it is uncertainty of lack of knowledge and is not an uncertainty; and so Taz in s.k. 14 see there; and see Peri Chadash Yoreh De'ah siman 41 s.k. 11. And in responsum of Tzitz Eliezer siman 43. And in responsum Panim Me'irot siman 15. And in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 17 and siman 19. It is written in responsum of Maharam of Lublin siman 78 — an incident with one who took cabbage from the barrels in a bowl and brought it to another place and a wheat kernel was found on it, and he permitted the cabbage in the barrels, for we say "here it was found, here it was" — end quote. And Magen Avraham wrote: but if a wheat kernel was found in the barrels, even the cabbage in the bowl is forbidden, for we say perhaps the wheat kernel was there beforehand in the barrels, for from time to time in one piece we establish presumptive prohibition as written; and see small seif 25 in Magen Avraham who wrote: and if they removed it to another place and a wheat kernel was found on it — we say "now it fell," for it is from place to place — end s.k. for Mem-Dalet:
