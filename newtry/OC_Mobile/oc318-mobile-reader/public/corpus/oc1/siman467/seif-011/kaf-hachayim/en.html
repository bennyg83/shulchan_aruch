(11) There. And he will sell it to an Israelite. An incident: an Israelite bought wheat from a gentile and afterward another Israelite came and asked the gentile to sell him wheat too; and he said "I have no wheat — for the Jew to whom I sold is my friend and I sold him the wheat I washed and asked to grind for my household needs and sold them to my friend." It appears to me it is comparable to what is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 16 seif 11 that a gentile is not believed in what he said after he sold them and they left his hand; however if he is believed to him it is forbidden — end of his words.
