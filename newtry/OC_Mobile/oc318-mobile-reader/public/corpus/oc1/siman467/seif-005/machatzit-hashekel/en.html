(seif katan 5) We are not concerned that they go, etc. Know that what Rabbeinu Yosef wrote in seif 5: grain, etc., and one need not suckle, etc., and they receive leavening — these are the words of Rashba on what is written in Pesachim page 33a that if he separated terumah of chametz it is not sanctified, as written: you shall give to him and not to his fire. That is specifically when he had no opportunity, such as if it leavened while attached; and Rashba questioned: if so we would not eat matzah, for certainly rain fell on the grains while attached. Therefore he wrote specifically when they dried and one need not return to the ground — then they receive leavening. And in responsum of Me'il Tzedakah siman 69 he challenged Rashba with proofs and wrote: behold Abbaye said page 46a: this colander in which they roast (Rashi: as they dry nuts in an oven) upside down (meaning the mouth of the pot downward) is permitted, for the water coming out of the nuts does not leaven, for because of lifting them they do not leaven; but right-side up, where the mouth of the pot is upward, is forbidden, for water remains inside the pot and leavens. And Rava said even right-side up is permitted, for fruit juice does not leaven — meaning we hold like Rava; nevertheless it is possible Rava also agrees with Abbaye that because of lifting them they do not leaven; rather he added to permit even right-side up because fruit juice does not leaven. And if so one might say that even while the grains still need the ground they nevertheless receive leavening; only they stated generally that because of lifting them they do not leaven. Unless there was pouring rain until the crop bent and lay on the face of the ground — then there is no because of lifting them. Or we saw that it sprouted or split while attached — such is what the Gemara deals with when it said it leavened while attached, such as when we saw it sprouted while attached; but from the ordinary case, even if suckling from the ground is not needed, we are not concerned lest it leavened even if rain fell, for we say because of lifting them it does not leaven.
