5) There. And inform the buyer, etc. If the kernels are visibly chametz, one need not inform a Jewish buyer, because their status is apparent. Chayei Adam, there. This implies even when selling a large quantity. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1, however, writes that when selling a small amount to a Jew, it is obvious that no notification is required when the chametz is identifiable. See there. This implies that the law for a large quantity is not obvious. Therefore, initially one should be stringent and notify the buyer when selling a large quantity.
