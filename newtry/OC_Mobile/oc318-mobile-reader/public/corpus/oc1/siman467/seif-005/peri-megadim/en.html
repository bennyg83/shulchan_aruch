One does not. See the Taz. The Rashba in responsum 102 and the Darchei Moshe indicate that here it is visible to the eye that the rain did not reach the middle of the stack. Similarly, in the Ran's responsum, it is common that rain does not pass into the middle of the stack when it is covered by ears and straw. Still, the matter requires analysis in each setting.
That which. The Mechaber wrote in seif 3 that if their appearance did not change they are permitted, implying that if their appearance changed they are forbidden. See the Beit Yosef and Levush. They are even forbidden to keep. But if they did not arrive by ship, even if their appearance changed, where other grain cannot easily be found there may be room to permit keeping them.
