There is Taz: behold flour in honey without moistening is like siman 462:6 except only for eating thus; but to make a drink from the start — even flour in honey all that initially lacks sixty then leavens in water before cooking well; however before Pesach permitted and nullified in sixty with water; and from the fifth hour until nightfall if known flour was mixed or small vessels customary to mix flour and concern perhaps no sixty against flour or perhaps in one place not blended well — behold for us flavor is not flavor in other prohibitions; and soaked in soft honey is like soaked — see Yoreh De'ah 105 and 104; if so honey became nevelah and afterward needs sixty against all the honey and there are not sixty in water against all the honey for it is Yom Tov and enhanced. Therefore for one who permits small vessels to make a drink from them before the fifth hour — from the fifth until nightfall not from what we said; however from not nullifying prohibition permitted all where there is safek whether they mix — similar to vinegar — see Yoreh De'ah 114 Shach 21 and 116:28 — see there; except from nevelah such — perhaps fine flour or flour mixed with water as he wrote; and b'dieved if cooked thus one might say in pressing circumstances if not known — perhaps we do not say flavor is not flavor l'chatchila must cook before fifth hour; and large hive barrels not customary to forge — even rooms not recognizable — Taz's view implies permission even to eat during Pesach that honey; and so to cook during Pesach from those large barrels not customary to forge — as appears from Rambam's conclusion that not to eat only from the hive in a barrel — implies permitted to eat on Pesach from it and so drink if fit for drinking on Yom Tov else no melacha of another's Yom Tov. And behold what he proved permitted to make drink on Pesach from large barrels — though obvious since even for eating Rambam permits — all the more so drink — one might say drink is stricter lest flour inside and not blended well and Yom Tov in water in any amount on Pesach unlike eating in their substance fruit juice does not leaven — therefore he wrote no concern at all in large barrels where they never mix flour:

And what he wrote regarding saffron — at first since we see saffron dissolved in the cooked dish and nothing remains — necessarily even if nevelah meat threads are mixed custom is to invert and all becomes like saffron — therefore we follow present state; somewhat strained to explain Taz's words; also there is concern Yom Tov — see there; and Magen Avraham letter 3 and Chayyei Adam here letter 16 wrote novel fat that did not change to a permitted thing — nevertheless one might say we do not permit forbidden fat for it is not such a change as blood which the Torah forbade and did not change to another appearance — and blood that was cooked is permitted for most codifiers and so is the halachah unlike forbidden fat; no verse needed — see there.
