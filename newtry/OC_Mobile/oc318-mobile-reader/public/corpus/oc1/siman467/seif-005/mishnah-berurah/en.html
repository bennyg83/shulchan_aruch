(17) That it dried completely, etc. — Chayei Adam wrote that for this reason the scrupulous are accustomed that all their matzos are guarded from harvest time, for sometimes they leave them attached to dry excessively because they have no time to harvest — and this is what they call shmurah: they harvest them while still somewhat moist.
