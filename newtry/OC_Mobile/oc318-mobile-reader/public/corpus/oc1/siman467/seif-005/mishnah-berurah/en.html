(17) That dried completely, etc. Chayei Adam wrote that for this reason the meticulous practice that all their matzos be guarded from the time of harvest: for sometimes they leave them to dry while attached more than needed because they have no leisure to harvest; and what is called "shemurah" they harvest while still somewhat moist; see there.
(18) If rains fell on it. That is abundant rains; but through a little sprinkling that fell on the sheaves we are not concerned, and it is permitted even for matzas mitzvah (Maamar Mordechai in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach); and see Shaarei Teshuvah what he wrote in the name of responsa Me'il Tzedakah.
