(86) The bird is permitted — for roast does not forbid more than the amount of peeling, and the crop is at the place of peeling and they discard it; and even for us who forbid in roast up to sixty — nevertheless one should not be stringent in this more than the amount of peeling, for singeing is not at all similar to roast, since one does not singe much in one place but passes it constantly back and forth, as explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 68; and if the wheat kernel was found outside the crop after singeing — l'chatchila one must peel at that place; and b'dieved if one cooked thus without peeling it is permitted. The meticulous are careful on Pesach before singeing and before salting to open the chicken well or cut it in two and check well that no wheat kernel is found inside; and one who does so is zealous and rewarded [Knesset HaGedolah]:
(87) One must burn them — for we suspend they became chametzed from the salt found in the crop [Levush]:
(88) For they are not considered digested — for although regarding impurity they are considered digested, regarding prohibition it is different; and see in Biur HaGra who wrote that according to this there is no distinction between whole or broken; but in essential law he tends at the end that one should not distinguish between prohibition and impurity, and even whole ones are considered digested.
