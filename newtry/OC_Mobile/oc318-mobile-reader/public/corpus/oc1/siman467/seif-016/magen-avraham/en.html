For roasting does not forbid more than peeling, and the crop is peeling — therefore one discards it; and the Bach wrote: according to what they practice to forbid in roasting, everything is forbidden — end of his words; and it appears to me in searing all agree it is permitted, as in Yoreh De'ah siman 68; and this is its language of Maharil: one of Maharash — women practice on Pesach to remove the crop before searing it, for they think searing is somewhat close to roasting, and if a wheat kernel is found in it there would be some concern for chametzing; and some are not concerned — end of his words; and it is known Maharil holds to forbid entirely in roasting, and nevertheless he is lenient in searing b'dieved; it is written in Sefer Tanya: all matters of Pesach should not be done through a gentile:
