(16) There. Even though they did not split open — forbidden, etc. Meaning that even though we do not see in it any badness and it is not close to splitting open at all — nevertheless it is forbidden, for it is not comparable to l'titah where one is engaged with it; and therefore even if one leaves it on erev Pesach on a barrel and the like alone to check if it leavened — it does not help.
