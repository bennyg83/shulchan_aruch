(There, se'if 16.) A wheat or barley kernel found in the crop of the bird, etc. Marginal note: Behold regarding what another rabbi permitted when found in the crop on Pesach after salting: to cast it away and not check: see in my work on Yoreh De'ah, third mahadura, in a responsum to the community of Snatin: I wrote that that rabbi instructed well, since there are several double doubts: perhaps in salting peeling [suffices], and perhaps it is like digested; and see the Tur at the end of this siman and you will understand; examine carefully.
