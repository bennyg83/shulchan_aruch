Dry and hard. Nevertheless, if water came upon them, the kernel itself must be forbidden even if it did not change at all, as written in seif katan 7, even regarding hard Cardanian wheat, as stated in Pesachim in the matter of Rav Gidel. See Rashi there.
And their appearance did not change. But if their appearance changed, or they are moist, it is forbidden even to keep them, as the Tur writes in the name of the Baal Halachos Gedolos.
