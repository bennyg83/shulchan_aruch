(4) There. And he informs him, etc. This is the language of Rambam chapter 5; and Beit Yosef wrote that for Rambam we do not say "we sell them keva keva" except when selling to a gentile; but to an Israelite — if he does not inform him, even keva keva is also forbidden for we are concerned perhaps he will leave it in order to eat it on Pesach; and if he informs him, even much is permitted — end of his words. But Maharshal wrote that even when informing him to sell much to him is forbidden; and Bach agreed to his words. And so Taz agreed s.k. 2. Acharonim osef 1; Acharonim osef 3; however Peri Chadash agreed like Beit Yosef's words that whenever he does not inform him, even keva keva is forbidden lest he guard it for Pesach; and when he informs him, even much is permitted. And so Shulchan Gavoah agreed osef 1; Chayei Adam osef 3; Chak Yosef osef 2; R' Zalman osef 3; Chayei Adam general rule 126 osef 2; Mishna Berurah osef 2:
