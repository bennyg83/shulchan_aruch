4) There. And inform the buyer, etc. This is Rambam's language in chapter 5. Beis Yosef writes that according to Rambam, selling one kav at a time applies only when selling to a non-Jew. When selling to a Jew without informing the buyer, even one kav at a time is prohibited, because the Jew might retain it to eat during Pesach. If the buyer is informed, even a large quantity may be sold. Maharshal, however, writes that even after informing the Jewish buyer, selling a large quantity is prohibited. Bach, Taz, subsection 2, Olas Shabbos, subsection 1, and Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 3, agree. Peri Chadash, however, agrees with Beis Yosef: without notification, even one kav at a time is prohibited lest the buyer retain it for Pesach; with notification, even a large quantity is permitted. Shulchan Gavoah, subsection 1; Chok Yaakov, subsection 3; Chok Yosef, subsection 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 3; Chayei Adam, rule 126, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2, agree.
