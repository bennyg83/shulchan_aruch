By force. See the Taz and Magen Avraham, who cite the long Mordechai. With Hashem's help, it will be explained there. The Taz's statement about benefiting from them together means two at a time, not all together, as in Yoreh De'ah 110:7. Nevertheless, where a doubtful prohibition was mixed and one fell into the sea, there may be room to permit all together at once, because there is also a double doubt that perhaps the forbidden item is no longer here.
However. From the responsum of the Ran, siman 59, cited in Shulchan Aruch seif 6, there is difficulty. Yet there too the reason appears similar to the responsum of the Rashba, that it is common when grain lies in straw and rain drips on the top of the stack, while the water does not reach the middle. This differs from the Agur's case of wheat in an attic, where the concern is only doubtful.
