And what the Rambam wrote in seif 12 to permit through safek sefeika — one might say in any amount d'rabbanan; but to eat grains in their substance not; and further one might say safek sefeika born on Pesach permits safek sefeika, for for us chametz is not called davar sheyeish lo matirin — if we forbid we must burn everything and to permit retaining and forbid eating is not seen; therefore we are lenient in everything unlike before Pesach where davar sheyeish lo matirin is such as to permit retaining and not eating — we wrote this in the introduction.
