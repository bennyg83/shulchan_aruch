We are not concerned for ordinary, etc. This is the language of the Rashba in a responsum that Beis Yosef brought, and this is his language: And even those heaps on which we saw that rains fell, we attribute for leniency: perhaps water entered only the upper sheaves but not the belly of the heap; and even if it entered, perhaps they did not manage to become chametz so long as [the grain] did not come into water; therefore it is a double doubt and for leniency (end quote). From this too there is a contradiction to the Rama's ruling nearby, for here it is permitted even for eating.
