We are not concerned for stam, etc. — so wrote the Rashba in a responsum that the Beit Yosef brought, and this is its language: and even those stacks that we saw rain fell upon them — we suspend to leniency that perhaps water did not enter except in the upper sheaves but not in the belly of the stack; and if you say it entered, perhaps it did not have time to leaven as long as it did not come in water — therefore there is double uncertainty, and for leniency — end of his words.
