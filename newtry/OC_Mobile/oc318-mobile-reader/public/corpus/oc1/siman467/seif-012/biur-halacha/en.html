If they scalded, etc. — see in the Taz that he wrote that ordinary scalding is in a second vessel; and behold according to what the Mechaber wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 68 that b'dieved in a second vessel it does not absorb — at first glance it must be that it deals with a first vessel; and if because of blood prohibition it deals that they salted with the feathers before scalding, as I cited in Mishna Berurah in the name of Peri Megadim; but it is possible that the Mechaber's opinion is like those who are stringent that the Rama brought above in siman 447 seif 3 in the gloss that even in a second vessel it is forbidden, and so I found in Peri Chadash who is uncertain about the Mechaber's opinion, and regarding essential law the Peri Chadash is stringent on Pesach regarding a second vessel — see there; and see above in siman 461 in Mishna Berurah seif 21:

And if an embedded wheat kernel was found, etc. — and barley in water, see above in siman 447 in Magen Avraham seif 6 that it imparts taste for spoilage, and the Acharonim do not disagree there except regarding wheat — see there:

If the mixtures became intermingled and there is great loss, etc. — it implies from this that a primary cooked dish may not be permitted even through great loss, and so is among the Acharonim; however in the Biur HaGra it appears that because of great loss alone one permits, even in hot food — then we rule like the first opinion in the Rama that permits in scalding; and it must be according to him that what the Rama mentions mixtures is because through mixtures there is commonly great loss; and if so the Rama who wrote a cooked dish of meat that remained in water — meaning in cold, and there he permits even if the wheat kernel was found inside while the meat was soaking in it — the main reason is also because of great loss; and if so if it happened that there was great loss through a primary cooked dish, it is also permitted in cold, for we rely on the first opinion in the Mechaber that permits in cold; and with this the difficulty of Magen Avraham above in siman 447 seif 9 can be resolved — there in the gloss it appears that in cold it is permitted and here he forbids in cold, for there it deals in a place of great loss — uncertain:
