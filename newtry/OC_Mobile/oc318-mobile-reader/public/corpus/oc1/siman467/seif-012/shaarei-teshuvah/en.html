Regarding ordinary wheat. Shaarei Teshuvah — and he wrote in responsum of Maharan Rotenburg siman 35 that the Gaon Maharam son of the Mechaber answered regarding rains that descended on heaps — there are wheat kernels that sprouted, but if there are sixty against the permitted ones, one may permit even l'chatchila not in a time of distress to grind them, and there is no matter of nullifying prohibition without intent, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 84; only the Bach wrote that one must bake before Pesach; and this matter of siman 467 regarding grain placed in an attic — since it was not ground and baked before Pesach it is dry with dry and returns and revives on Pesach — therefore one should sell it before Pesach like other chametz mixtures — see there; and see in responsum Me'il Tzedakah siman 69 that he wrote in the year 5472 there were rains during harvest days and we saw that wheat harvested a day or two later for matzah of the commandment were finished and fully dried, and we were uncertain perhaps they were thus finished and dried at the time rain was on the ground; the Rashba ruled that it accepts chametzing even while attached to the ground, and he brought that one need not be concerned for this even if they dried completely in their husk, unless when rains were abundant day and night — strong rain that knocks the stalks and sheaves to the ground and rain drips on them strongly, and it is as one who rests barrels full of water — but with rain that is not so strong and does not last a long time one should not be stringent unless we see them split; and know that they required guarding only from harvest time and not from when they finished while attached; and further, behold dew does not stop and falls on them every day, and it is not mentioned in any posek, etc. — see there:
