(12) Tsuker, etc. See in Terumat HaDeshen siman 119 that this was said only regarding spice compounds of quills or spice and fruit coverings; but hot tsuker they are clean and have no concern of mixture at all — so I heard from those engaged in tsuker work (Melechet Yom Tov). And behold this is difficult on Rama who forbids even to leave it; and Levush's opinion appears he holds from the side of custom one may not eat it but permits to leave it; but in spice compounds it all appears Levush too agrees they are forbidden to leave; and the same law applies to fine tsuker like dust — Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah wrote forbidden; and tsuker kandil — Acharonim wrote forbidden to leave them; and some are lenient in tsuker kandil — meaning what is brought from Candia as Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah and Magen Avraham wrote; and Taz wrote it is kaner or hot tsuker; and Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah expanded to resolve Rama's words; and in the margin of Shulchan Aruch a great one expanded to analyze from Mahari'l who mentioned eating tsuker kandia — implying tsuker kandia is permitted to leave and not hot tsuker which has mixture concern; also I saw a question came before my grandfather the Gaon may his memory be blessed — they left hot tsuker and he ruled to sell it to a Samaritan; therefore it is fit to be stringent not to leave it l'chatchila; and b'dieved to sell to a Samaritan; and with loss even for eating:
