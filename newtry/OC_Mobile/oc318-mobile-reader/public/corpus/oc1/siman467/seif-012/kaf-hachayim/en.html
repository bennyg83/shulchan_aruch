(12) [Seif 2] This grain that was submerged in a river, etc. See Beit Yosef who wrote that some distinguish between wheat and barley — that in barley they require splitting open; but in wheat, since there is a crack as one who split open it is considered — only he concluded but the opinion of Rif and Rambam is that there is no distinction between wheat and barley, and so we rule — end of his words; and so appears from the silence of his words here in Shulchan Aruch that in all types of grain it is forbidden even though they did not split open. And so Acharonim osef 2; and so appears from the silence of the Acharonim who used the term "grain" — implying in all types of grain it is forbidden even though they did not split open:
