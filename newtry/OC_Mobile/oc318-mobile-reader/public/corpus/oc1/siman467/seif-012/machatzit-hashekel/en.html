(seif katan 12) They did not split open, etc., one authority says it is permitted — meaning Shmuel holds splitting open is specifically required; but Mar Ukva holds even if they did not split open, if when placed on the opening of the roaster they would split open — meaning they are close to splitting — they are forbidden; and since the halachah was not clarified like whom, all that did not split open literally is a safek. It implies if mixed in less than sixty it is forbidden, for then it is a Torah safek — for we hold the halachah is like all who say d'oraisa. See Yoreh De'ah siman 98 regarding a mixture that was poured out and we know the majority is permitted but we are uncertain if there were sixty — then if it is species in its species, which from the Torah is nullified in the majority, there were sixty d'rabbanan to be lenient; and if it is species in its non-species, which from the Torah requires sixty, it is a Torah safek and therefore we follow the safek to stringency and it is forbidden — the same here.
