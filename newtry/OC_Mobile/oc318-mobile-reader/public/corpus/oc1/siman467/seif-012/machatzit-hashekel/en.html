(S.k. 12) They did not crack open, etc. For some say it is permitted. And that is Shmuel who holds we specifically require truly cracked open; but Mar Ukva holds that even if they did not crack open, but if placed at the mouth of a roasting-tube they would crack open, namely near to cracking open, they are forbidden; and since the law was not clarified like whom: wherever they did not truly crack open it is a doubt. Implying that if mixed in less than sixty it is forbidden, for then it is a Torah doubt, for we hold that taste of a prohibited item according to the Torah [forbids]. See Yoreh De'ah siman 98 regarding a mixture that spilled and we know the majority was permitted, only we are uncertain whether there was sixty: then if it is same-species, which according to the Torah is nullified in majority, sixty is rabbinic for leniency; and if different-species, which according to the Torah needs sixty, it is a Torah doubt and therefore we follow the doubt for stringency and it is forbidden; and the same applies here. For in se'if 2 he ruled even if they did not crack open it is forbidden since they are resting; and here too the cooked dish is resting: so why was he lenient regarding a cooked dish?
And it is difficult since, etc. Meaning what he asked still was not answered. For Taz wrote in Yoreh De'ah, and so Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 465.
