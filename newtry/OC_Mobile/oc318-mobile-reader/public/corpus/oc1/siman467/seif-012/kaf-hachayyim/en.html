12) [Se'if 2] This grain that was submerged in a river, etc. Beis Yosef cites authorities who distinguish between wheat and barley. Barley must split, whereas wheat, which naturally has a crease, is considered split. He concludes, however, that Rif and Rambam make no distinction between wheat and barley, and this is the ruling to follow. This also appears from the unqualified language of the Shulchan Aruch here, which prohibits every type of grain even when it did not split. Olas Shabbos, subsection 2, writes likewise. This also follows from the later authorities' unqualified use of the word grain, implying that every type of grain is prohibited even when it did not split.
