Seif 2 — this grain. Therefore Rava did not ask whether they split open. Hagahot Maimoni and Ra'avan there:

From the force of a double uncertainty. As written in Kesubos 9a:

But not to eat it. Since he has permissible grain, a double uncertainty does not help, as written at the beginning of Yoma 3b; and its uncertainty is forbidden, and if it became mixed, etc. Mordechai end of chapter 3 of Avodah Zarah, and see Yoreh De'ah siman 110.
