Such grain that was submerged in a river or upon which water fell , even if it did not split open , is forbidden, since when it rests undisturbed it comes to leavening. {Rama: Rema: Therefore one should be careful that water or drizzling rain not come upon wheat, for then even if the kernels did not split open they are forbidden. Grain that was stored in an attic and rain came through the roof in some places and moistened it , those that became moist are forbidden, but the rest may be kept on account of a double doubt: perhaps the rain did not fall on them, and even if it did, perhaps they did not become leavened. But to eat them on Pesach is forbidden, and a double doubt does not help for that.}
