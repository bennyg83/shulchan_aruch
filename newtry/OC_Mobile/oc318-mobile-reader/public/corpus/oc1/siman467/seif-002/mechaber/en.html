This grain that was submerged in a river or upon which water fell — even though it did not split open — is forbidden, for since it rested at ease it comes to leavening.And therefore one must be careful that water or rain does not drip upon the wheat, for then even if they did not split open they are forbidden. And grain that was placed in an attic and rain descended through the roof in some places and those that became moist became moistened — those that became moistened are forbidden; but in other places it is permitted to keep them by force of a double uncertainty — perhaps rain did not descend upon them, and if you say rain descended upon them, perhaps they did not leaven — but to eat them on Pesach is forbidden and a double uncertainty does not help (Agur and Kol Bo):):
