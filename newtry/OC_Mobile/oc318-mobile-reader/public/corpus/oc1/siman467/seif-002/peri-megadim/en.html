And we inform you: see Turei Zahav Pesachim 40b and Maharam chapter 5:19 and as written here in Shulchan Aruch. And the Taz understood from the simple sense of Maharshal that he explained the words of Maharam thus in his version: keva keva stam — meaning both for Israelite and gentile; and for Israelite with informing and for gentile without informing because there is safek sefeika with a gentile unlike Israelite on the contrary there; there the prohibition for an Israelite is also with informing because perhaps a stumbling block will come from not informing — therefore they did not mention in the Gemara permission of much with informing; and on this the Taz questioned: certainly Maharam and the Mechaber hold specifically for a gentile a little; for an Israelite whether much or little with informing permitted specifically; and the Tur who wrote and it is reasonable like the author of the Ittur for his view that reads keva for Israelite. Avi HaEzri. However for halachah he agreed to Maharshal and as the Tur's version: for Israelite keva and with informing specifically, for we do not rely that he will eat before Pesach — for in presumption and majority too we do not rely wherever it can be fixed easily like that of R' Papa: ask him — behold much with informing not lest he forget to inform; and for a gentile little permitted because safek sefeika perhaps he will not sell to Israelite and perhaps Israelite will eat before Pesach; even l'chatchila one does not make safek sefeika in one's hands — this is preferable that presumably he will eat for his need.
