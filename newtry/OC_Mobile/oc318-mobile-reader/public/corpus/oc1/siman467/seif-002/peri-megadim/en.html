And he informs him. See the Turei Zahav on Pesachim 40b and the Rambam, chapter 5 of Chametz U'Matzah, halacha 9, as cited in Shulchan Aruch. The Taz understood from the simple meaning of Maharshal that he explained the Rambam's text as selling kav by kav in general, whether to a Jew or to a non-Jew: to a Jew with notice, and to a non-Jew without notice, because there are multiple doubts.
