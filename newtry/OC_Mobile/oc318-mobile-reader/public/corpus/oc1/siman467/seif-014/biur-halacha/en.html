Great loss — poor, large piece, preventing Yom Tov joy — Gra permits Mechaber peel suffices; Peri Chadash deepen and discard surroundings. Others did not mention practically; softened not split perhaps rely; Gra's leniency perhaps only softened; Rama responsum 28 — even softened forbid whole piece; perhaps great loss he concedes; requires study. Doubt softened — not stringent [Acharonim]:

But the essential ruling to forbid, etc. — behold above we wrote that for us even if it is not embedded, only softened, and the Acharonim wrote (Kaf HaChayyim siman 447 seif 6 and Shulchan Aruch HaRav) that specifically if we see the wheat kernel is moist; but if we see it did not become moist from the salt, such as that it is dry — even though it softened it does not forbid, for it does not release at all, for it is pure salted and impure bland; and similarly if chametz fell on salted meat, even on Pesach one may be lenient in a place of great loss as with other prohibitions:

(3) The same whole piece, etc. - refer to Magen Avraham, who heard that heta was found in a barrel of fish that had been salted for Passover, and the Gaon ordered Rabbi Benjamin to throw that row into the river and sell the rest to foreigners, and refer to the Hamed Moshe Shatma 17, and it was not enough to prohibit only the fish on which the hata was found [and such a tama was the half shekel] From Mezekinan it will be forbidden from piece to piece and Amirinan here is found here was Akal and per the 16th chapter we wrote in the Gemara even in this way the whole line should not be forbidden:
