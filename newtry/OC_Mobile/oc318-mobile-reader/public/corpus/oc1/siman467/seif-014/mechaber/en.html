If a split wheat kernel was found on a chicken before salting — washing suffices. If after salting — peeling suffices. Some are stringent to say one must cut deeply all around it and discard it. If other chickens were salted with it, one peels all of them.Rema: Some are stringent to forbid everything salted together, but the primary ruling is to forbid the entire piece on which the wheat was found and to permit the others through slight peeling. If one wishes, one may sell everything to a non-Jew as explained in Siman 447, section 1— even though this is not the custom for other leavened mixtures, so it seems to me.:
