If a split wheat kernel was found in a chicken before salting — rinsing suffices; and if after salting — peeling suffices; and some are stringent to say one must peel deeply all around it and cut and discard; and if other chickens were salted with it one peels all of them:And some are stringent to forbid everything that was salted together; but the essential ruling is to forbid the entire piece upon which the wheat kernel was found and to permit the others with some peeling; and if one wishes he can sell everything to a gentile as explained siman 447 seif 1, even though they do not customarily do so in other chametz mixtures — so it appears to me::
