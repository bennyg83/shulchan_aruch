Se'if 14: If it was found, etc. See Magen Avraham; and as written in Pesachim 76a, cold into, etc.; and see there.
And if afterward. For salted is like roasting-hot, as written in Chullin 97b, and peeling suffices, as written in Pesachim there: one peels its place; and even according to those who are stringent in roasting [and require] enough to remove [a thickness], and compare dripping of its sauce, etc. there; in salting they concede that peeling suffices (Rosh in Chullin there; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 91 and siman 105); and all the more so according to what they explained regarding what is written in Chullin 112a, but roasting requires peeling, for it deals with hot, unlike what Tosafos wrote there 96b s.v. "until," and Rashi in Pesachim 76a that it deals with cold; and so ruled Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 91 that it deals with hot roasting; and if so, even in roasting peeling suffices; and see above siman 447 se'if 1 and what is written there.
And there are those who are stringent, etc. Since its prohibition is in any amount, and specifically around it there is concern for that any amount. Kol Bo in the name of the Ra'avad; and he holds that the peel returns and forbids, unlike what Tosafos wrote in Chullin 96 s.v. "even"; only that in other prohibitions, since the prohibition absorbed in the peel does not forbid because it spreads into its depth, as written here, and there is sixty against it, which is not so here; and if you say, make that depth itself nevelah so that it returns and forbids, as Tosafos wrote in the same entry, he holds as Rabbeinu Tam wrote there 101 s.v. "when he preceded," and Rabbeinu Tam says, etc.
And if they were salted, etc. Meaning, and one does not know perhaps it touched all of them; and see Magen Avraham; and as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 105 se'if 9: and if it is not known, etc.
And there are those who are stringent, etc. See Magen Avraham; and as written in Chullin 97a: fat is different because it spreads, and salted is like roasting-hot; and even though [taste] does not go from piece to piece without sauce, as written in Chullin 108a-b and in Tosafos there s.v. "moist," etc., here there is room to doubt perhaps it touched all of them and forbids all of them in any amount.
The main [law] is to forbid, etc. For by law it is not forbidden even according to those who require sixty in salting because we are not experts; that is specifically for something to which fatness applies, which is not so for chametz, as written in Yoreh De'ah there in the gloss; it is only a stringency; therefore we are not stringent in a case of doubt to forbid all of them lest it touched them, only regarding peeling, which is forbidden by law; and see Magen Avraham.
And if he wishes, etc. For anyway the Shulchan Aruch permits above in se'if 10; and even according to Beis Hillel who forbid there because it is a Torah prohibition, but not regarding rabbinic prohibitions; and all the more so here, where it is only a mere stringency.
