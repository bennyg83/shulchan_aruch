Some say that its law is like a spring. This is the view of Rabbeinu Yonah, who holds that even for vessels, according to the Torah, immersion in a reviis suffices if they are covered, but the Sages required forty seah. Therefore for hand immersion, which is only D’Rabbanan, a reviis suffices. The opinion that forty seah is required is the Rambam, who holds that hand immersion requires the same measure as immersion of the whole body.
