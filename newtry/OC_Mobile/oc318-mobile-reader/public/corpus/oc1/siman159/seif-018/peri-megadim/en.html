Some say that its law is like a spring. See the Taz. The Beis Yosef cites Rabbeinu Yonah that according to the Torah, for a whole body one needs forty seah but for vessels it suffices that the water cover them, and the Sages abolished the reviis measure for vessels; however, for netilas yadayim, which is D’Rabbanan, they did not abolish it. The Levush's language is somewhat difficult, for vessels also require forty seah only D’Rabbanan. A spring is different because according to many poskim it purifies a person with any amount if it covers the body; for hands, therefore, there is greater reason to be lenient. Since the Mechaber accepted the lenient view above, one may rely on it in doubtful cases of netilas yadayim.
