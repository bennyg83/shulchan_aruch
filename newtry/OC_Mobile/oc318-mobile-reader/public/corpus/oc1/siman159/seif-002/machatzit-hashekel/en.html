(s.k. 2) Even an earthen vessel, etc. For it is not considered a vessel. Nevertheless, regarding netilas yadayim they were lenient. An earthen vessel means that it is made of clay, but the artisan did not fire it in a kiln so that it would be called an earthenware vessel. End quote of Tosafot, beginning of chapter 10 of Keilim, in the name of Rashi.
