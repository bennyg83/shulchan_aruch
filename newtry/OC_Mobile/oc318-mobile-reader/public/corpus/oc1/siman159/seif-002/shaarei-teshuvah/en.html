Liquid. See Baer Heitev and Yad Ephraim. Birkei Yosef writes that his grandfather, author of Chesed LeAvraham, cited Zichron Moshe that placing a cloth beneath the opening does not help, but placing it on the other side does help, etc. Admas Kodesh writes that if one coated a copper vessel with tin, or an earthenware vessel with lead, it returns to its original valid status.
