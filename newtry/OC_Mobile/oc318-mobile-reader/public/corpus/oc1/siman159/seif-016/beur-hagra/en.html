Seif 16, forty, etc. As written below from the case of the irrigation channel mentioned above.
And according to the Raavad, etc. For even baalei keri immerse in such water, as stated in the third chapter of Berachos and the eighth chapter of Mikvaos. At first glance the irrigation channel, where the waters are drawn, supports the Rambam; but according to the Raavad one may say that there it is a vessel attached to the ground, as in seif 8, and like the view in the third chapter of Berachos that immersion works in the ground but not in vessels.
And drawn water, etc. As above in seif 7.
