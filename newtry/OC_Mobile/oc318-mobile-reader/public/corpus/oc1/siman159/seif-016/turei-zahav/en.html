If a monkey, etc. Those who invalidate are the Rosh and Tur, who rule like Rabbi Yosei; those who validate are the Rashba and Rambam, who rule like the first Tanna. One should not object from Rav Pappa, who invalidates the irrigation channel because it lacks human force. Beis Yosef answers that according to the Rambam, at least the force of a giver is needed, even if that giver is an animal; Rav Pappa excludes only water flowing entirely on its own. Since this answer is somewhat forced, the Shulchan Aruch presents a dispute here. By contrast, regarding tilting a barrel in seif 10 there is a better answer to Rav Pappa, and therefore he cited only the Rambam's view.
