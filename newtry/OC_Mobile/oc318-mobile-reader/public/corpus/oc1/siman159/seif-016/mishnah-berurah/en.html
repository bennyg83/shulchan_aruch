(89) In the ground. A vessel attached to the ground is not valid for immersion according to any opinion, because one may not immerse in vessels. See above, seif 8.
(90) One may not immerse. Because water valid for immersion of the body is required. Raavad's reasoning is that regarding immersion of a baal keri for Torah study, which is Rabbinic, we find that drawn water collected in the ground is permitted, as cited by Beit Yosef above in siman 88.
(91) If all of it was made to flow. Meaning, even if all the water was originally drawn, if he caused it to flow over the ground into another place, it is valid in this respect, as explained above in seif 7.
