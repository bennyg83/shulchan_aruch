If. See the Taz, who explains that for a monkey the answer must be force of giving, though this is forced, since a monkey and a barrel tilted by itself seem to be one matter. He therefore writes that some invalidate. The irrigation channel may be considered at least force of giving, unlike the case of washing by tilting a person-made vessel where all the water was moved, as explained above; therefore the law may follow the first Tanna here.
