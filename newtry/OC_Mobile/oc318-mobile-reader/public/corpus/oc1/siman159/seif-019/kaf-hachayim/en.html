19) A wooden vessel that has been pierced and wood has been stuck into it with sufficient strength and has a seal, but glass vessels have no seal raised at all to make them a vessel made of tin and lead and the like. Raza letter Yod. It is true that in M.S. there the Rabbinical vessels are not sealed except with tar and brimstone, etc. Refer to the late Rambam, p. By a craftsman with a piece of wood precisely and that there should be a strong seal in the matter that no water came out of it at all and other vessels such as pottery and glass and the like, if they are pierced, the hole should not be crossed to take through the mouth of the vessel so that it does not enter into dispute, but it should be taken through the hole if possible or with another vessel if it is not possible, he should not bless for this taking but rather meditate the blessing in his heart or bless without a name and kingdom.
