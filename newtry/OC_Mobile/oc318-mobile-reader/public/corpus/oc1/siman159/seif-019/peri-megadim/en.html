There is. See the Taz and Magen Avraham s.k. 27. According to the lenient view, if he already washed and has no other water, even in a doubt regarding purity of hands he is valid, as in siman 160. All the more so according to the Mechaber, who rules above like the lenient opinion. Nevertheless, since the Shulchan Aruch writes only that there is room to doubt, perhaps it was not considered a full doubt. This seems to be only with forty seah; with less than that and flowing water there is no doubt.
