(17) [Good] there in a gathering of liquid, etc. — and if they plugged it with a sponge below it does not help, but from the side it helps; and if they plugged it with pitch it helps even though it is not of the material of the vessel, as written in ch. 3 of Kelim. Birkei Yosef letter 4 in the name of his ancestor Maharach Azulai in his glosses, Kol Yehudah in the name of Zekher Moshe on Orach Chayim letter 2; however Taz s.k. 1 wrote that plugging with a sponge, even from the side, does not help to make it a vessel, see there; and with pitch it helps only for earthenware vessels, but not for other vessels, see there. And they brought in Eshel Avraham letter 1, 62 letter 2, Chayei Adam general rule 37 letter 3, and wrote it is not like Magen Avraham s.k. 6; and even though Matteh Yehudah end of letter 1 wrote that a vessel from which one draws — since it is made to receive cold water — plugging with pitch helps in it, and not like Taz, see there — and they brought Hagahot Rabbi Akiva Eiger letter 2; nevertheless for the practical law it appears one should be concerned for the words of those who forbid. And likewise in responsa Sdeh HaAretz vol. 3 siman 3: one should not plug a copper vessel pierced from below or from its side — not with flax, not with pitch, not with glue called titkal, not with dough, not with wine lees, not with sulfur and the like — but specifically with types of metals or with tin, see there; and likewise Chida wrote letter 1, Ben Ish Chai Parashat Tazria, letter 9, and not like Beiur Halacha letter 8; and see in the letters after it and letter 19.**** END BLOCK ****
