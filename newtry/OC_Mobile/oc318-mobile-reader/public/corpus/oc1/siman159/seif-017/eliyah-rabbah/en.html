Since its manner, etc. Eliyah Rabbah does not know the source for limiting this to a monkey; perhaps the Mishnah merely speaks in the normal way and the same would apply to other animals. The Levush may have been answering why the Shulchan Aruch validates a monkey yet earlier rules stringently regarding a spigot, because a monkey seems more like a person. Nachalas Tzvi asks and answers this way, but it is forced, for it is all one dispute between the first Tanna and Rabbi Yosei. The Beis Yosef follows his own view that all agree force of giving from a vessel is required, and therefore the spigot must be returned. The Kesef Mishneh seems uncertain whether other animals have the same law as a monkey.
