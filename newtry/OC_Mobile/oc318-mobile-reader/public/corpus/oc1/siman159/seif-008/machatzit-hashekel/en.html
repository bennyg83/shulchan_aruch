(s.k. 8) Because, etc. And the vessel is attached, etc.; and if it is attached, see se'if 8. That is to say, in se'if 8 he wrote that if one inserted his hand into a vessel, some say the washing counts, which is the view of Baal Halachos Gedolos. According to him, why did immersing his hands inside the pipe not count? Although it is not considered immersion, since the water is drawn, nevertheless it should count as washing, for even if he inserted his hand into a vessel, according to that opinion he fulfills washing. Therefore Magen Avraham wrote that Beis Yosef wrote there in se'if 8 that this applies specifically if the vessel is not attached to the ground, whereas the pipe here is attached to the ground and for this matter loses the status of a vessel. This is the answer of the Ramban. But Tosafot and the Rosh wrote another answer, that a pipe is different because it is not a vessel. As a matter of law, both answers are true. Therefore here he copied the answer of the Ramban, and so too in Shulchan Aruch, se'if 8; while above in s.k. 4, regarding a tube, Magen Avraham copied the answer of Tosafot and the Rosh.
