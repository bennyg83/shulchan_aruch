For they are, etc. Even according to the opinion at the end of the siman that inserting one's hand into a vessel counts as washing, here attachment to the ground is worse, as explained. The Bach concludes that even if there are forty seah it is invalid. Olas Tamid writes that water drawn from a distance through hollow pipes may not be used directly from the pipes, for it does not come by human force; but one may immerse in the water standing before the pipes, for it is as if connected to the river through the flowing stream. If the flow from the pipes stopped, immersion there is ineffective. Those who disagree may admit this when the pipes are very wide.
