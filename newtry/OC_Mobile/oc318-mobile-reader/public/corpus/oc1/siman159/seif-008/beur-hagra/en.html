Seif 8, if he inserted, etc.; and if, etc. Tosafot there in the name of Halachos Gedolos hold that it is permitted inside the vessel, and they bring proof from the second chapter of Zevachim where they asked regarding the laver, from it and not inside it, which implies that this restriction is not present by netilas yadayim. Tosafot disagree from the sugya there, which requires human force; so is the view of most poskim, almost all of them, including the Rambam, for at least the force of a giver is required, as above. Ramban and Rashba reject the proof, as Tosafot explained in their first approach: it is not specifically human force that is required, but a vessel; the irrigation channel is not a vessel, and if it is considered immersion, immersion does not work in drawn water, as the Rif wrote. Rashba adds that the same applies to a vessel attached to the ground, since the water is drawn; this resembles the irrigation channel and refers to where it was carved after being fixed in the ground, as in the gloss to seif 7. See Ramban in Toras HaBayis.
