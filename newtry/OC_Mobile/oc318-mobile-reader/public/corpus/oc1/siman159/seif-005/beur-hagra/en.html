But from the Rosh, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch it implies that there is no distinction between large and small holes, like the Raavad there. The term perutos is like striking strings upon a harp; sometimes water exits and sometimes not. When one closes the top, it does not exit below; therefore it is not considered a perforated vessel, since it is made only for enjoyment, as Rabbeinu Shimshon and the Rosh explain. The Rambam, however, does not explain that placing the finger above closes the hole, but that the hole is small and releases only drops, while it receives at the sides and was made that way originally.
