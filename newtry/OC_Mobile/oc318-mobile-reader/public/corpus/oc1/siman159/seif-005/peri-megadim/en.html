Therefore. See the Taz. A barrel cover is primarily made to cover a barrel and only somewhat for receiving, so it must be made broad and able to stand without support. But a vessel made from the outset only to receive is valid, as in seif 5. A sack and basket, even if a base was made for them, were not originally made to receive liquids. A skin flask and basket are different because without liquid they lack a real cavity, and therefore require a broad base. The Bach's claim that it must no longer be used for a barrel is not correct, for then how could hats be valid? Hats are difficult, for they are not made to receive water but to be worn; perhaps they are also somewhat made for drinking by travelers, and therefore they need to hold a reviis without support like a barrel cover.
