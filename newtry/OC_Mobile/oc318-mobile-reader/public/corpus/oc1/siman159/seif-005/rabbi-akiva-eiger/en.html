Magen Avraham, seif katan 24. “For in Yoreh Deah, end of siman 2, he invalidates slaughter performed by a monkey.” In my humble opinion, this presents no difficulty. Regarding shechitah, the slaughterer must specifically be one who is subject to the mitzvah of slaughtering. For this reason, the shechitah of a non-Jew is invalid. A monkey is therefore automatically invalid, since it is not subject to the mitzvah of slaughtering.
