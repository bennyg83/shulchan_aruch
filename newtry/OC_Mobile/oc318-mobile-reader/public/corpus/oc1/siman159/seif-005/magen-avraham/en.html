It is properly considered. This is not like a barrel cover, which requires repair even if that is its initial manner, because a barrel cover was not made to receive. One cannot compare it to a sack, where repair does not help, for a cover is sometimes turned over and placed on a pot to hold a little gravy, as in Keilim, chapter 2, Mishnah 5.
A spigot below. If a complete vessel became perforated and one inserted another item into it, this helps even though it was not made for that originally, for once it is closed it is considered a vessel. If he designated a spigot to use with it, that is called its initial repair and is permitted, like designating it for olives according to Sefer Mitzvos Katan. See the Tur.
