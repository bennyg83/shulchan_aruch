Full of holes, etc. - See Mishnah Berurah regarding Rambam's view; Mateh Yehudah and Biur HaGra already raised this point. See Taz and Eliyah Rabbah, who cite Maharshal as ruling stringently regarding every titeros for netilas yadayim, distinguishing between tumah and washing because washing requires a significant vessel. In practice, however, it appears one may rely on the Shulchan Aruch, since Rosh and Tur agree to be lenient, and Rabbeinu Yerucham writes likewise in the name of the commentators. If the holes are large, however, or in our type of lever vessel, one should be stringent initially, since according to Rambam it is not considered a vessel even regarding tumah.
