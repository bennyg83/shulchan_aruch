Therefore a barrel cover, etc. Tosafot explain that this cover has a cavity to hold a reviis, but is invalid because it cannot sit without support; therefore repair helps. The difficulty is that if its initial manner is to need support, that should be valid. Beis Yosef answers that a cover was not originally made to receive. The Taz continues to question this, for if so why does any repair help? He explains that because a cover is sometimes turned over and used to receive a little liquid, it has some receptacle function, and therefore proper repair can make it fit for washing.
