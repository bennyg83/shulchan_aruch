If a monkey. At first glance one should ask why the dispute was not stated by a barrel, where the first Tanna and Rabbi Yosei disagree. One can answer that according to the Rambam, who rules like the first Tanna, the Mishnah about a tilted barrel refers to one tilted by hand; if it was tilted by itself, it is invalid because force of giving is required. The Tur, who rules like Rabbi Yosei, explains the Mishnah as a barrel tilted by itself. But when a person tilted it, perhaps even Rabbi Yosei agrees that it is human force. Therefore Beis Yosef ruled this plainly without dispute, while a pipe is invalid according to all because it is not force of giving.
One should be stringent. After the fact it is valid. Although in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 2 the shechitah of a monkey is invalid, there shechitah is according to the Torah and we rule stringently. See seif 18 and siman 164, seif 11.
