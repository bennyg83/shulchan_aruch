A barrel containing water — if one places it on his knees and draws water from it to wash his hands, it is valid. But if it is lying on the ground and water flows from it, or if it is standing and water comes out through a hole in it and one places his hands there, the washing is not valid. However, if there is a stopper (meaning something that blocks the hole — “spina” in the vernacular) in the hole, and he removes it and receives the water on his hands, it is considered as koach gavra (human force), and he must replace and remove it again for each and every pouring.
