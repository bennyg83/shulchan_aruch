Seif 9, a barrel, etc. This is the Mishnah in Yadayim according to all views, for Rabbi Yosei agrees in this case, as stated in the Tosefta cited by Rabbeinu Shimshon.
And if it was, etc. Even according to the Rambam, who rules like the first Tanna, this is specifically when one tilts it, which is considered human force, or according to the Rambam, force of a giver. The case in the Tosefta of opening the boiler was explained by the Raah as force of a giver according to the first Tanna, but not where the vessel was perforated on its own or the spigot fell out.
Or if it stands, etc. According to Halachos Gedolos and the Rashba, however, even in such a case it is valid, for they do not require the force of a giver at all, as above.
And if it was, etc. Sefer HaTerumah brings proof from the laver. This is like the waterwheel in the first chapter of Chullin, where first force is considered human force. This is why he writes that one must return it, etc. See Magen Avraham. Beis Yosef writes that this is so even if there is capacity for a reviis below the hole, as written above at the end of the seif in the gloss.
