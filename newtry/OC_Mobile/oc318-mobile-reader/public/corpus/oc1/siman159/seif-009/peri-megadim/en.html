Attached. See the Taz. Halachos Gedolos does not require force of a giver, only a vessel. The irrigation channel in Chullin and seif 7 is invalid because the water is far from the pouring and the force of the bucket has ceased; if he immerses in that trench, the water had already been drawn by the bucket. Therefore, if one attached a detached vessel to the ground and immersed his hands in it, his hands are tamei even according to Halachos Gedolos, because it appears like immersion in attached drawn water. But where something was fixed and then carved, immersion may help for netilas yadayim, as written above.
