And in a pressing circumstance. See the Taz in the name of Maharshal and Eliyah Rabbah, who applies the rule that doubtful blessings are treated leniently. Hollow pipes that flow water are not vessels, whether round or half-round, if they lack a receptacle; therefore placing the hands under their flow is not netilas yadayim. If the water flows to the ground and he immerses his hands there, such a flow can be a connection, especially where the openings are wide. Regarding pumps, if the internal mechanism contains a vessel and the water is not from a perforated non-vessel, it may be valid to place the hands under the stream; if not, one may need both washing and immersion to satisfy both possibilities, though flowing water and force of giving remain concerns.
