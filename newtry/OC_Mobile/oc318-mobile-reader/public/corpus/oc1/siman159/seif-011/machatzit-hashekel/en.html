(s.k. 11) Regarding immersion, etc. Like the tube of a skin flask, namely two fingers next to the thumb, that is, the index finger and middle finger, passing through the hole.
Unless they stood still. That is to say, but not when they are flowing. Magen Avraham came to answer with this the question of the Shach in Yoreh Deah, siman 201, s.k. 80, which he left requiring study. Therefore Magen Avraham wrote that the reason is because a stream is not a connection unless they stood still; examine this carefully.
