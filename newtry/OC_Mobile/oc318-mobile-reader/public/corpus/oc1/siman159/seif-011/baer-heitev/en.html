And a niddah. Rashal wrote: Fortunate is one who can be careful not to wash from water even if a non-Jew or a niddah drew it, and all the more so not to wash his hands from them. End quote of Rashal in Perek Kol HaBasar, siman 25. The Taz wrote likewise in his name, and Olas Tamid, see there. Magen Avraham wrote: It appears to me that this is specifically when she pours onto his hands, but if she brings the water in a vessel, there is no concern; see there. This requires study, for Maharshal wrote explicitly even if they drew it, etc., and all the more so not to wash his hands from it. See there.
