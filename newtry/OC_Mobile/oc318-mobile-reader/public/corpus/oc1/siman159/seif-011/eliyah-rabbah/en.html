A niddah, etc. Fortunate is one who can be careful not to wash from water drawn by non-Jews or by a niddah, and all the more so not to have his hands washed by them. Yam Shel Shlomo, siman 25, cites Mateh Moshe, siman 260, that a man's wife who is a niddah may not pour water over her husband's hands.
