Magen Avraham, seif katan 15. “Otherwise, how could he eat without washing?” I do not understand. Since it is doubtful whether this is considered washing, he may eat because the doubt concerns a Rabbinic law, as stated below in siman 160, seif 10. Nevertheless, because of the doubt he may not recite a blessing. Magen Avraham himself writes similarly above at the end of siman 12: “Nevertheless, it appears to me that he should not recite a blessing.” Thus, although he permits him to wear those tzitzis, he nevertheless may not recite a blessing. This requires study.
