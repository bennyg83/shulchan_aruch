It is not considered a vessel. The Tur cites Sefer Mitzvos Katan that if he designated it for olives it is a vessel, and the Tur disagrees, saying netilas yadayim is different. Beis Yosef asks that Sefer Mitzvos Katan only permitted using it through the hole, just as he permits by a hole that emits liquid only through the hole. Maharshal and the Bach explain that the Tur also understood Sefer Mitzvos Katan this way, but still objected that for washing even through the hole designation should not help. The Taz analyzes whether the vessel remains important enough for netilas yadayim even if it remains a vessel for other uses.
