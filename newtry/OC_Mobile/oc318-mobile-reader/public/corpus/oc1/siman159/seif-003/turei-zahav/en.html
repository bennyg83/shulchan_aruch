It is not considered like a vessel. In Tur he wrote in the name of Semak that if one designated it for olives it is a vessel, and he disputed this regarding netilat yadayim; Beit Yosef questioned — Semak also only permits via the hole in this case, as he does not permit in less than minimum except via the hole. Rashal and Maharshal explained Tur also explains Semak's words thus, only that it was difficult to him per his reasoning that it does not help even via the hole in this case, since per his view if pierced more than minimum it does not help. And there is a difference per Tur's view that designation for olives does not help to wash via its mouth. Maharshal wrote, and requires study: since per Tur, pierced like all water is valid for netilat yadayim via the hole even though this hole purifies it completely from its impurity, apparently netilat yadayim is more lenient than purification — a fortiori designation for olives that helps for impurity should help for netilat yadayim, end quote. And the answer is: also for impurity it is still a vessel for gistera as stated in chapter HaMitzaniyot, and Tosafot explicitly wrote, in the name of R' Yitzchak, s.v. regarding sealed flask etc., that if below there is the required measure, a hole like all water does not purify from impurity except we require majority or like a pomegranate spout.
