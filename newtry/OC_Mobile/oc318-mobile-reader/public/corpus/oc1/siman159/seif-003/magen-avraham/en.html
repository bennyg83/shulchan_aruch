A barrel cover. Rabbeinu Shimshon and the Rashba explain that a cover is invalid because it is not made to serve as a receptacle but to cover a barrel. The Bach writes that when they say he repaired a cover, they mean he designated it from then on to receive. But Rabbeinu Shimshon states explicitly that he repaired it by carving it to hold a reviis, implying designation alone does not help. The Shelah writes that one should not wash from the protruding spout of a vessel called giskan, because it was not made as a receptacle. This requires study from the laver, which had spouts; one must say that those were tube-like receptacles, whereas an open half-channel is not a vessel, as stated regarding a pipe.
