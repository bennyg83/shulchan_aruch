A non-Jew. See the Taz and Beis Yosef in the name of HaManhig, that some say a non-Jew or a slave cannot effect netilas yadayim because human force is required and they are not called adam for this purpose. According to that, the view that invalidates a monkey would also invalidate them. The Rama did not gloss here. Whether this applies after the fact to a Canaanite slave, who is obligated in mitzvos like a woman, requires study.
