(86) That they are flowing. Rainwater purifies only when collected, not while flowing.
(87) And they contain, etc. Meaning, even if they contain forty seah, it remains doubtful whether they are effective for immersion of the hands. This doubt applies even according to those who were lenient above regarding a mikveh containing less than forty seah, because this case may be worse. There, at least D'Oraisa, forty seah is not required for immersion of utensils. Here, by contrast, flowing rainwater is invalid D'Oraisa even for immersion of utensils. According to those who were stringent above and required specifically forty seah, as for immersion of the entire body, it is obvious that flowing water is ineffective.
(88) It is doubtful, etc. Therefore, he should wash again without a blessing. Biur HaGra rules that according to all opinions flowing rainwater is entirely ineffective.
