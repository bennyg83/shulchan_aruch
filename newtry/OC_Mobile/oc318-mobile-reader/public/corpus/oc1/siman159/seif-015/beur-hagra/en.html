In such a case even for vessels it is invalid, for Toras Kohanim teaches that a mikveh does not purify through flowing water, as stated in Mikvaos, Shabbos, Nedarim, and Bechoros. It appears there is no room for doubt, for Rabbeinu Yonah validated only by comparison to immersion of vessels, and the Tosefta explicitly states the general rule. Regarding the irrigation channel of buckets, the phrase that they are drawn means even if the water was poured from a spring; the Gemara states an even stronger case, that they are drawn.
