(s.k. 15) Without a blessing, etc. In either case: either for the first, etc. For it is explained above in siman 158, se'if 11, that if he forgot to bless, even if he remembered after drying, he blesses. If so, here too he can bless on the first washing, even though he interrupted after the first washing. What was written in Eliyah Rabbah, that this speaks where he already ate HaMotzi and therefore cannot bless on the first washing, as I wrote above in siman 151 in the name of Maharshal, is not sufficient. First, since the Shulchan Aruch here states it without qualification, it implies that it speaks even where he happened to wash in the manner of washing before eating HaMotzi. Also, this law, that if he ate HaMotzi he does not bless, is not mentioned in Shulchan Aruch; rather, Magen Avraham brought it in the name of Maharshal. Therefore one cannot explain that here it speaks where he already ate HaMotzi, for this is not sufficient, since this law, that if he ate HaMotzi he does not bless, is not explicit in Shulchan Aruch. How then can we say that the Shulchan Aruch intended this law?
