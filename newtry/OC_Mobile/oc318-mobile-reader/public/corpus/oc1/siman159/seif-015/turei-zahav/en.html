Gentile and niddah. The reason is that nowadays all water is impure and it is impossible otherwise. Rashal wrote: and it appears to me that although they are lenient in water that a gentile drew — for d'oraisa they do not impart impurity, only the Sages made them like zavim, and in a place where it is impossible to fix what can we do; but niddah whose impurity is definite and d'oraisa — why should we be lenient? And like all water in the responsum of Maharam of Rothenburg: I am careful regarding netilat yadayim from gentiles even though R' Yehudah permitted, and he brought proof that niddot wash their hands even though they are impure; nevertheless one can reject — niddot and yoldot, and we too it is impossible otherwise; but not to receive netilat yadayim from gentiles is possible, end quote. Fortunate is one who can be careful not to take from water even drawn by a gentile or niddah, and all the more not to wash his hands from them, end quote.**** END BLOCK ****
