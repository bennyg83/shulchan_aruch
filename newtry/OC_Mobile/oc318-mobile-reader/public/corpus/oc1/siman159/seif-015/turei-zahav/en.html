A non-Jew and a niddah. The reason is that nowadays all water is tamei and it is impossible otherwise. Maharshal disagrees in part: while we can be lenient with water drawn by a non-Jew because their tumah is only an enactment of the Sages and in a case that cannot be fixed, what can we do, a niddah has definite tumah according to the Torah, so why be lenient? So also appears from the responsum of Maharam Mintz. He writes that he is careful not to receive washing from a non-Jew, even though Rabbeinu Yitzchak permitted it by proof from niddos washing hands, because one can distinguish that with niddos, yoledos, and ourselves today it is impossible otherwise, whereas avoiding washing from a non-Jew is possible. Fortunate is one who can be careful not to wash even from water drawn by a non-Jew or niddah, all the more so not to have his hands washed by them.
