(s.k. 14) Some say forty seah and some say specifically when the vessel has forty seah — Hagahot Ashri and Taz s.k. 10 wrote it is a printing error and it must read: even if they do not have forty seah. For Bahag's view that they are forty seah for washing — his proof is that a verse was needed for sanctifying hands and feet from the laver: "and they shall wash from it" — from it and not inside it — implying that generally even if one puts his hand inside a vessel it is permitted; from this it is implied that even with less than forty seah it is permitted, for the laver did not have forty seah and nevertheless a verse was needed "from it" and not "inside it," implying that generally even without forty seah it is permitted per Bahag; and Magen Avraham's words imply certainly per Bahag it is permitted even without forty seah; but Hagahot Ashri is a third view and does not learn from the laver but from his own reasoning. If so, a vessel is required but it need not come through the force of a vessel — whether pouring from a vessel onto the hand or inserting the hand into a vessel is permitted; nevertheless forty seah is required so it resembles immersion somewhat (see Bach); and if so, understand Taz's difficulty.**** END BLOCK ****
