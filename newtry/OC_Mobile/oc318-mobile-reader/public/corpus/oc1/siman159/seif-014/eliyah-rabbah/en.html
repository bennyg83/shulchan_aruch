And the water flows, etc. See Beis Yosef. Eliyah Rabbah explains Rabbeinu Shimshon: the issue is not literal human force but force of a giver, for even a monkey is called human force in this context when the water comes through a giver. Thus a tilted barrel in the next seif is valid even if it flows all day by itself, whereas an irrigation channel lacks even the force of a giver. Rabbi Yosei, however, requires actual human force. This supports Beis Yosef and Kesef Mishneh against the Bach's emendations.
