If one immersed his hands in spring water, even if it does not contain forty se'ah, his immersion is valid, as long as his hands are covered by the water all at once. And if he immersed them in the water of a mikveh, some say that its law is like that of a spring, and some say that it requires forty se'ah; and we adopt the words of the lenient opinion. {Rama: (But one should be stringent lechatchilah.)}
