If one immersed his hands in spring water, even if it does not contain forty se'ah, the immersion is valid as long as his hands can be covered by the water all at once. And if he immersed them in mikveh water, some say it has the same status as a spring, and some say it requires forty se'ah — and the ruling follows the lenient opinion. {Rama: (But one should be stringent לכתחלה.)}
