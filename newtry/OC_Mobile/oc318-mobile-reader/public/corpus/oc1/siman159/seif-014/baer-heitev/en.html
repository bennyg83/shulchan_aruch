A spring. For a spring purifies with any amount, and even if his entire body immerses in it.
A mikveh. This means rainwater gathered in a pit.
Like a spring. This is the opinion of the Ri, who holds that even for vessels immersion in a revi'is is sufficient if they are covered there, except that the Sages required forty se'ah for them. Nevertheless, for immersion of hands, which is only D'Rabbanan, a revi'is suffices. The opinion that says forty se'ah is needed is the Rambam, who holds that immersion of hands requires the same as immersion of the whole body.
