The Taz challenged this, for regarding netilas yadayim we should be lenient that it is considered a vessel even if he first fixed it in place; see there. The Olas Tamid wrote: It appears that those waters that are drawn through hollow reeds to a distance, called rier kasten in the vernacular, should not be used for washing from the reeds themselves, because they do not come by human force. But it is permitted to immerse his hands in the water standing before the reeds, for it is as though they are connected to the river from which the root of the water emerges, provided that the reeds are flowing with water, so that the stream is a connection with the water that comes from the river to the reeds. But if the flow from the reeds stopped, immersion there is ineffective. See there. Similarly, one may not wash from pumps that are not vessels. Seder Berachos, see there.
