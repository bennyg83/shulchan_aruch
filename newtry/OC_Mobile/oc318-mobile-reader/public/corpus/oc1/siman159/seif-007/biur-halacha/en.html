Because they are drawn water - See Mishnah Berurah. They are considered drawn water here because they were drawn with a bucket, as Rabbeinu Yonah writes. The pipe itself does not render them drawn, because the pipe is not a vessel, as Tosafos, Rosh, Eshkol, and Ritva write. This is also evident from Rabbeinu Yonah and from Raah, who validates such water through hamshachah, and the Mechaber codifies this below. Magen Avraham explains that according to the Mechaber the pipe is a vessel, but the reason for invalidity is that it is attached to the ground and therefore resembles immersion, while immersion is ineffective in drawn water. If so, however, it is difficult to understand why he permits it when the drawn water is conducted into the pipe by hamshachah because drawn water conducted over the ground is valid, for the pipe would still be a vessel. Rabbi Akiva Eiger raises this question, and Peri Megadim struggles greatly with it and leaves it unresolved. According to what we wrote, however, the Mechaber follows the majority of poskim that the pipe is not a vessel, and the difficulty disappears. It may appear from seif 8 that the Mechaber considers a pipe a vessel, since otherwise there is no source for his ruling according to Behag that immersion is ineffective in a vessel attached to the ground, namely one hollowed out and afterward affixed, as Perishah and Peri Megadim explain. This can be rejected, because the Mechaber found only Ramban and Rashba supporting Behag, and they explicitly write this, so he did not wish to be lenient against them. I later found that Shulchan Aruch HaRav also answers this way. All this forced interpretation is necessary only according to Perishah and Peri Megadim's explanation of the Mechaber below in seif 8. According to Biur HaGra there, however, Rashba, Ramban, and the Mechaber refer to a vessel first affixed and then hollowed out, which is invalid according to Behag for agitating one's hands in it because it is like the ground. The channel of drawn water was likewise first affixed and then hollowed out. It then follows simply that the Mechaber validates drawn water conducted into the pipe, because that pipe is literally like the ground. Its invalidity for immersion because of drawn water is due to the water first having been drawn in a bucket, as explained above.
