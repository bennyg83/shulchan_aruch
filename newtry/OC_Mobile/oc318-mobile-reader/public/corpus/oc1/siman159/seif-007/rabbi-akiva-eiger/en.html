Seif 7. “But if he places them.” This is the view of Ra’ah, cited in Beit Yosef. In my humble opinion, the Mechaber’s ruling is difficult. From seif 8 it appears that the Mechaber holds that a pipe is considered a vessel. The reason immersing one’s hands in it is ineffective, according to Behag, who validates immersing hands in a vessel, is that because the pipe is attached to the ground, it resembles immersion, and immersing in drawn water is invalid. If so, what does extending the water through the ground accomplish? The pipe remains a vessel, and the act cannot be considered immersion. It is also invalid as washing, because something resembling an attachment to the ground is not called washing. Ra’ah, who rules this way, may be following Tosafos and Rosh, who explain Behag by saying that a pipe is not a vessel. For the same reason, the Mechaber’s opening language is difficult. He writes that if one immersed his hands in the pipe, they are not pure because the water is drawn. This is taken from Rosh, who follows his own view that a pipe is not a vessel. According to the Shulchan Aruch, however, which treats it as a vessel, immersion in it is invalid in any event, because there is no immersion in a vessel. Even if we say that where it would be valid from either perspective it should be accepted, meaning that absent the defect of drawn water it would be valid according to Behag to immerse one’s hands in a vessel, but a vessel attached to the ground is invalid because it resembles immersion and is treated as though it were ground, then where immersion would be valid if it were actually ground, it should be accepted. Since it is valid either as a vessel or as ground, the only defect is that the water is drawn. If the water was extended through the ground, it would then be valid, because although it resembles ground, actual ground would be valid. Nevertheless, the first difficulty remains unresolved. How could the Mechaber rule without qualification that extending the water validates it? According to the dissenting opinion in seif 8 it remains invalid, since it is in truth a vessel, so there is no immersion, and there is no washing because there is no human force. This requires great study.
