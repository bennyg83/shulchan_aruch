(א) (seif kaf-zayin) Pure — and the Tosafot wrote, etc. — meaning the Rosh holds that if one took one hand from a vessel and poured from it onto his other hand, this is so there is no difficulty, for we rule later in siman 162 seifim 4 and 5 that if one took one hand and rubbed it on the other, the first hand that took also becomes impure. Therefore they wrote that specifically the first hand must take from a reviit of pure water (nevertheless see the Taz that pouring the reviit over the whole hand purifies the first hand; but if he took with his first hand even a reviit, nevertheless since the back of the hand became impure the water also became impure). But the Tosafot wrote that even if he did not give by the first hand but less than a reviit, nevertheless the Rosh holds one may take from it by the second hand. And do not compare to siman 162 that if one took one hand it is forbidden to rub the other before taking the second — for there it is different since he did not intend to take by the first pouring but only one hand, unlike the Rosh law where he intended by pouring to take both hands — that is, to place the water in his palms onto his second hand.
