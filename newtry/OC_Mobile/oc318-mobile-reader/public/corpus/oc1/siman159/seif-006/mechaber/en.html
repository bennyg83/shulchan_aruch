One should not pour water for his fellow using his cupped hands, because one may only wash from a vessel. And the same law applies if one washed one hand from a vessel and then poured from that hand onto the other hand — it is ineffective. However, Rabbeinu Tam permits in such a case, since the initial washing of the first hand was from a vessel, and that is provided the first hand was pure, such as when a quarter-log (רביעית) was poured on it at once.Rema: And the custom is to be lenient like Rabbeinu Tam, but the first opinion is' the main one; therefore, one should be stringent' initially (le-khatchila).':
