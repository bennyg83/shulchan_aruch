One may not pour water for another person from his cupped hands, for one may wash only from a vessel. The same law applies if one washed one hand from a vessel and then poured from it onto his other hand — it is invalid. Rabbeinu Tam, however, permits this, since the beginning of the washing of the first hand was from a vessel; and this is provided that his first hand was tahor, such as where he poured a revi'is upon it all at once. {Rama: The custom is to be lenient in accordance with Rabbeinu Tam; but the first opinion is the primary one, and therefore one should be stringent lechatchilah.}
