A skin flask and basket, etc. The Taz asks why these are not valid as vessels made from the outset not to stand without support. He rejects the Bach's explanation that they are primarily made for dry items, because skin flasks are clearly made for water or other liquids. Rather, leather flasks are made flat like a pouch, and only when liquid is placed inside does the liquid expand them and create a cavity. Since their cavity is not naturally present, they need a later repair to keep the cavity and allow them to sit without support. This differs from a vessel made originally with a cavity, even if it cannot be used without support.
