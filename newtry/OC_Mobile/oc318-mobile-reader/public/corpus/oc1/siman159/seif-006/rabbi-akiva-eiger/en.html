Magen Avraham s.k. 27 explained "to those lenient." See in Beit Yosef that Rashba in Taharat HaBayit disqualifies flowing water. But Beit Yosef wrote on this, and per R' Yitzchak of Corbeil there is room to doubt — meaning since R' Yonah holds a revi'it helps, one could say flowing water also helps. And even though earlier Beit Yosef wrote Rashba and R' Yitzchak hold a revi'it helps, and since Rashba even though lenient regarding revi'it nevertheless regarding flowing water he holds invalid — if so from where do we derive doubt in R' Yitzchak's view to establish it as a dispute? Rather in truth Rashba returned in Taharat HaBayit to what he wrote in his novellae to explain that which they ruled in Beit Birah dealing with a revi'it, and from this it is proven a revi'it helps per R' Yonah's view. But in Taharat HaBayit he wrote it deals with connected to a mikveh as Mechaber brought in Shulchan Aruch 160:7 — it is clear he returned from his words in his novellae, and therefore also holds in Taharat HaBayit flowing water is invalid; but per R' Yitzchak and per Eshel Avraham who held Rashba in the category that revi'it helps — one could say flowing water also helps, and so are Beit Yosef's words.**** END BLOCK ****
