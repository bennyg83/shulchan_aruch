Magen Avraham, seif katan 27. “Meaning, according to those who are lenient.” See Beit Yosef, who states that Rashba in Toras HaBayis HaAruch invalidates flowing water. Beit Yosef then writes that according to Rabbeinu Yonah the matter is uncertain, because Rabbeinu Yonah holds that a revi’is suffices, and perhaps flowing water likewise suffices. Although Beit Yosef had previously written that Rashba and Rabbeinu Yonah both hold that a revi’is suffices, and Rashba nevertheless holds that flowing water is invalid, why should we create a dispute by assuming uncertainty in Rabbeinu Yonah’s view? The answer is that Rashba in Toras HaBayis HaAruch retracted what he had written in his novellae explaining the case of “he interrupted them with the daughter of the palace” as involving a revi’is, from which it follows that a revi’is suffices according to Rabbeinu Yonah. In Toras HaBayis, however, he explains that it refers to water connected to a mikveh, as copied in Shulchan Aruch, siman 160, seif 7. It is therefore clear that he retracted his earlier position in his novellae, and for this reason he also holds in Toras HaBayis HaAruch that flowing water is invalid. According to Rabbeinu Yonah, however, and according to Rashba’s earlier understanding in his novellae that a revi’is suffices, flowing water may likewise suffice. The words of Beit Yosef are therefore correct.
