At once. It seems plain that this is where the water is not gathered in the palm of the hand, but rather he pours over the entire hand and the water flows off. But if it is gathered inside the hand, it is obvious that it became impure, even though it is a revi'is, for the back of the hand is impure and renders the inside of the hand impure. For this reason the Rama wrote well that one should be stringent initially, because the masses err and pour water into the palm of the hand, and then with that water they rub the second hand. This is forbidden according to all opinions. He specifically needs to dry first before washing his hands a second time properly, for this water became impure and has no remedy, even if he pours more water over it, as below. Taz.
