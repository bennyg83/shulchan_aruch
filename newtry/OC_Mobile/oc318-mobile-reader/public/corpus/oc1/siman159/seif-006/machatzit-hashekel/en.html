(s.k. 6) A spigot, etc. And he inserted another item into it, etc. See Taz, end of s.k. 1, who wrote in the name of the Raavad that sealing with clay does not help in any vessel, and pitch helps only for an earthenware vessel and not for other vessels. The opinion that if he designated it, etc., follows Semak, cited above in s.k. 3. The Tur disagreed with him only in that he holds Semak permits even where it does not hold a revi'is from the hole downward, or to wash from the mouth of the vessel, as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 3. But as to what he designated it for olives after it was punctured, its power is not worse than if it had been made this way from the beginning, and in this the Tur also agrees.
