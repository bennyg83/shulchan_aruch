Nevertheless, in a pressing circumstance, etc. Maharshal forbids even in a pressing circumstance, as cited by the Taz, Shiyurei Kenesses HaGedolah, and Olas Tamid. Eliyah Rabbah inclines to leniency like the Shulchan Aruch and Levush, since Maharshal compares it to a sack and basket, but that is not comparable. Here it is made to receive in pressing circumstances for travelers, while a sack and basket cannot receive unless later coated; from the outset this item can receive.
