Vessel See Taz. In the Tur he wrote: a broken vessel or a whole vessel, if it can stand without support, etc. With a whole vessel, he teaches that anything whose initial repair was not that way is not a vessel; and with a broken vessel, he teaches the leniency that if it can stand that way, one may wash with broken pieces if it holds a revi'it. See Magen Avraham 3 regarding the law whether one washes with broken pieces. See siman 183 in Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah ot 5 regarding the cup of birkat hamazon, and there it will be explained, God willing.
