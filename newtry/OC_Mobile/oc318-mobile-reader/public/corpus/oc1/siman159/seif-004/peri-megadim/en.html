A vessel. See the Taz. The Tur writes both a broken vessel and a whole vessel: by a whole vessel he teaches that if it was originally made to stand only with support it may still be valid, as in seif 5; by a broken vessel he teaches the leniency that broken pieces may be used when they can stand in that manner and hold a reviis. See Magen Avraham s.k. 3 for the practical law of using broken pieces, and see siman 183 in Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah.
