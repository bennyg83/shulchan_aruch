In them. Rashal wrote that it does not appear correct to permit even in pressing circumstances, for it is no better than a sack or basket, since a hat is not made to receive. Taz. The Damesek Eliezer wrote likewise. In se'if 2 he is lenient in this, because here it can receive from the outset in pressing circumstances, whereas a sack and basket cannot receive unless one pitches them. See there. So too the Eliyah Rabbah ruled, not like Maharshal.
