A vessel that holds a reviis, etc. The Tur writes both a broken vessel and a whole vessel. It appears he needed both: a whole vessel originally made to stand only with support is valid, as in seif 5, whereas here it speaks of a vessel not originally made that way but later damaged at its base; the law is that it must be able to stand without support. A broken vessel teaches that even fragments may be used if they can stand and hold a reviis.
