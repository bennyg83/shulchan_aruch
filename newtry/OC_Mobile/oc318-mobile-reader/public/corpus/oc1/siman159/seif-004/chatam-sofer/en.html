In the same passage: “A semicircular one is not.” Note: The main point is that in the laver, the spout was not higher than the laver itself, as Eliyah Rabbah also writes. A tube, however, is always invalid. This is likewise implied by Rambam, chapter 18 of Hilchos Keilim, halachah 1, and by the law of pipes there. Therefore, one should not wash from a faucet when the water exits through a tube or pipe, but only from a faucet arranged so that the water exits directly from the barrel itself.
