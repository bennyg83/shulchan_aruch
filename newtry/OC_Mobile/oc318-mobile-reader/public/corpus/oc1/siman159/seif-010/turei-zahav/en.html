Some say that it counts as washing. This is the view of Halachos Gedolos, who holds that only by the laver is there an exclusion from the verse, they shall wash from it and not inside it. It implies from the poskim who cite this ruling that the vessel need not contain forty seah, since the issue is the law of washing. Hagahos Ashri, however, has a text requiring forty seah. The Taz says that must be a scribal error and should read even if it does not contain forty seah; otherwise no verse would be needed by the laver, which itself did not contain forty seah, as the Rambam writes in Hilchos Biyas Mikdash.
