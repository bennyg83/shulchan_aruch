(s.k. 10) It admits, etc. And the same applies, etc., on a ship, etc. See siman 160, se'if 9. That is to say, from the fact that he needed this remedy and did not write that one should take a complete vessel and wash his hands, it follows that he holds one may not wash hands from seawater, because it is not fit for an animal to drink, as it is bitter. But as immersion it is permitted, for in immersion this does not invalidate. However, Magen Avraham wrote there in the name of the Radbaz that it is permitted to wash one's hands from seawater, since through boiling it is fit for a dog to drink. If so, there is no need for this remedy of taking a punctured vessel, etc.
