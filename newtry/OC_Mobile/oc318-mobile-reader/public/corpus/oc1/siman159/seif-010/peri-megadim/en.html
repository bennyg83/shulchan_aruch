Some say. The Taz brings proof from Zevachim 21, from it and not inside it. The Bach suggests that Halachos Gedolos permits placing the hands in a vessel only as immersion in forty seah within a vessel, and all the more so if it is attached to the ground. The irrigation channel is different because the pipe is not a vessel and the water had already been drawn. Thus if one attached a vessel to the ground and placed his hands inside it with forty seah, if he has other water he should wash again without a blessing; according to the Mechaber he would wash again with a blessing. The question why a verse is needed for the laver is not understood, for it excludes even a very large laver of forty seah.
