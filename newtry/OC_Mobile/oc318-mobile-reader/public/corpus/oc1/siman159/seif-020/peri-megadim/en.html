And according to the Raavad. See the Taz. Since netilas yadayim is D’Rabbanan, we are lenient, though he should wash without a blessing if he has water. The Bach's discussion of snow can mean taking snow in a vessel and throwing it on one hand and then on the other, where there is a dispute if it is not melted; or it can refer to immersion in snow. The Taz agrees with the first explanation. For immersion, one does not require melted water for netilas yadayim. But taking snow in one hand and rubbing it onto the other is forbidden even with water, because the second hand did not receive water from a vessel. Since snow can be considered liquid once he intends it for washing, it can become tamei and the hand must be dried.
