20) Those wooden vessels that are not made from a single piece, but whose bottoms are attached with pitch or titkal, may not be used for washing the hands. This applies even according to the Rosh, of blessed memory, for such a vessel is not comparable to a vessel full of holes. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot Beit Yosef, paragraph 9; Olas Tamid, paragraph 6; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 6; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 2; Beis Menuchah, paragraph 17.
