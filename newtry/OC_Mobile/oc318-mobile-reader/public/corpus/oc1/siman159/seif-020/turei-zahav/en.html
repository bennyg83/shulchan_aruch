And according to the Raavad one may immerse. His reason is that baalei keri immerse in such water if it has forty seah in the ground. The Bach cites notes of Maharshal that regarding snow some forbid and some permit, and Rabbi Avigdor did not want to wash with snow even in pressing circumstances. The Taz first explains this as throwing snow onto one hand and then the other, treating snow like water; alternatively, it refers to immersion in snow, as with the dispute about immersing the whole body in Yoreh Deah, siman 201, and this is the primary reading. Taking snow in one hand and rubbing it onto the other is forbidden according to all opinions, even with water. The Taz concludes, however, that where snow is gathered in a hollow with forty seah one may permit immersion of the hands, for even regarding the whole body the Rama rules only that initially one should be stringent; for hand immersion, one may rely on this even not in pressing circumstances.
