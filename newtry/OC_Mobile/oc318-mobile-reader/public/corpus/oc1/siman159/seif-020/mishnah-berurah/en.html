(96) He does not recite, etc. For where were we commanded concerning immersion? We were commanded concerning washing, and the greater includes the lesser: it is because of the obligation of washing that we now immerse the hands.
(97) And some say, etc. The later authorities agree with the Mechaber that one should recite Al Netilas Yadayim. Only when the water is invalid for washing and valid solely for immersion, as below in siman 160, seif 9, should one recite Al Tevilas Yadayim.
