Clods. Meaning, although with regard to impurity they are not vessels, they are valid for netilas yadayim. See Yad Aharon.
Liquid. If he sealed it with clay, it does not help for any vessel, and pitch helps only for an earthenware vessel. See Taz, who discusses this at length. Magen Avraham, s.k. 6, permits when it was punctured and he inserted another item into it. If it drips drop after drop, it is known to admit liquid. Bach. See Yad Aharon, and Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 158.
