(Taz s.k. 1) And its measure (remaining measure) helps regarding... Rabbeinu Yerucham per Rambam... Raavad disputes there similarly in vessels as should be, and he expanded; it appears to explain his words briefly: initially he brought two resolutions of Beit Yosef — in the first resolution Rambam holds even in earthenware there are other measures that are not like all water, such as designated for olives or gistera, and Rambam comes to inform us; in the second resolution, even if you say in earthenware the measure is always equal to like all water and designation does not help as Taz s.k. 3 above in Tur, nevertheless Rambam comes to inform us regarding flushed vessels because Rava specifically deals with earthenware; therefore Rambam says in all vessels each one according to its impurity (but in these resolutions we need to say if we explain thus that Rambam deals with holding a revi'it from the hole downward; but per below in the name of Beit Yosef that Rambam deals with not holding a revi'it — therefore he wrote broken and not pierced per Rava's language, dealing with no revi'it from the hole downward, and then it is like broken that exits its impurity thereby; and accordingly in all vessels the law is equal: whatever has a hole that cannot hold a revi'it in the vessel from the hole downward, behold it is a broken vessel). On this Taz questioned: if there is another measure besides like all water etc.; and further one can question: since Rambam wrote that in a broken vessel even via the hole is forbidden etc., and Beit Yosef wrote Tur holds...
