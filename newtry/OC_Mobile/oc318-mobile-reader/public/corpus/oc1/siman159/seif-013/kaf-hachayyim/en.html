13) There: If it was perforated with a hole that admits liquid, etc. A person must therefore pay attention to copper vessels and similar vessels made from separate pieces joined with tin, for they are prone to develop a hole that admits liquid. Some therefore take care to wash only from a vessel made from a single piece, without a spout, whose rim is even and has no high and low areas. This avoids doubts and also avoids the possibility that it developed a hole that admits liquid without their noticing. In particular, when a person goes to eat at another's home and a vessel full of water is brought before him for washing, he must inspect whether the vessel is valid for netilas yadayim. Perhaps the host is unfamiliar with these laws, and his blessing would be in vain, God forbid.
