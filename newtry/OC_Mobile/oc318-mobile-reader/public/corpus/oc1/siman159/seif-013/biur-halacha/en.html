Initially, he should have intention, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, which explains the case where he has no water, etc. If he has water, Rama above in siman 158, seif 7, implies that he must wash his hands a second time, in order to satisfy the view of Rashba, but he should not recite a blessing. See Magen Avraham there for the reason, namely, that perhaps he already fulfilled his obligation according to Ra’ah and those who agree with him. It appears preferable for him to render his hands impure, so that he will certainly become obligated in a second washing and may recite the blessing.
