And he must. See the Taz and the Magen Avraham, who gives a somewhat different distinction in the name of the Levush. The Bach, because of this question, understands that the Mechaber rules like the Tur and Rosh; therefore a barrel tilted on its own is invalid, but one tilted in such a way that it continues moving from the person's action counts as human force. The Taz rejects this and holds that, according to the Mechaber, even without constant movement it is valid for netilas yadayim, and the Rama would agree; the monkey case is different.
