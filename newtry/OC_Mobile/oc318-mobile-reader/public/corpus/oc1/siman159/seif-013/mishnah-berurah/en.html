(75) Initially he should intend. Magen Avraham writes that even after the fact the washing is invalid if neither party intended at all; see there. Biur HaGra, however, in siman 158, seif 7, agrees with those who hold that washing without intention is valid, and this is also the view of most Rishonim. After the fact, one may certainly be lenient when he has no water with which to wash again, following the Mechaber. I later found that Maamar Mordechai rules likewise.
(76) And the intention of the pourer, etc. If the pourer was younger than six years old, it appears from the poskim that his intention is ineffective.
