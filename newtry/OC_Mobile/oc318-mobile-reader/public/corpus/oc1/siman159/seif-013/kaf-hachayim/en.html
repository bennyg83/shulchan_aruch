13) There and if a drink container is punctured, etc., etc., the person should give his opinion on those copper vessels and the like that they cut into pieces and glue them with tin that may puncture the drink container. And yet there are those who are careful not to take their hands 21 from a vessel that is of one piece and does not have a rim and its lips are straight that there is no high place and no low place in order to be used enough and also lest a drink be punctured in the receiver or otherwise. And in particular, when a person goes to dine with others and a vessel full of water is brought before him to take, he should give his opinion on the vessel as to whether it is kosher for the NT, lest in the Hebrew the Gemara he is not well versed in the laws and his blessing be nullified.
