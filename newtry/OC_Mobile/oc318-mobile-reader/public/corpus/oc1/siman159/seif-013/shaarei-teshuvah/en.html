And pouring. See Baer Heitev. See also Noda BiYehudah, second edition, siman 142, who writes that this proves that the object is considered a vessel even while the stopper is being removed from it. See Responsa Beis Ephraim, Yoreh Deah, siman 53, who writes that this is no proof regarding a mikveh. Here, emptying the contents of the vessel through this opening is its normal manner of use, and this does not detract from its status as a vessel. This is also clear above in seif 5 in the gloss, and all the more so regarding a vessel fitted with a stopper. See there.
