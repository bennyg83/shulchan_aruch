Initially he should intend. It appears that even after the fact he must wash again, since the Rashba wrote that intent is required, and although Beis Yosef disagreed based on Chagigah, the Rashba explains that Chagigah refers to touching chullin, but eating requires intent. The Rama himself ruled in siman 158, seif 7 that even if one washed for dipping in liquid it does not count, for washing must be for sanctity, as Tosafot write in Pesachim 115. The Rashba also brought strong proofs, and although the Raah disagrees, one should rule like the Rashba, Tosafot, and Yam Shel Shlomo.
