(א) (8) One may not scrape, etc. — for it is like weekday activity:
(ב) (9) With an implement — but he may scrape with his hands on the flesh, only he should be careful not to make a chibur:
(ג) (10) Designed for this — we say in the Gemara that if he had an implement designated for Shabbat — permitted; and Magen Avraham wrote that from this virgins practiced having an implement of pig bristles designated for Shabbat, for then it does not appear as weekday activity; and see above at end of siman 303 in Mishna Berurah that even in this it is permitted only to fix a little the hairs to lay them down, but to comb with it is forbidden even in this, for it is impossible not to uproot hair:
(ד) (11) Soiled — for then it does not appear through the scraping as weekday activity:
