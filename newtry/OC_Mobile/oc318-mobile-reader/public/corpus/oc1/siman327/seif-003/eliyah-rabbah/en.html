[4] Made for that purpose, etc. Olat Shabbos writes: I am astonished at all the halachic authorities who omitted the law that if one has a scraper designated for Shabbos it is permitted, for the Gemara, Shabbos 147b, says that Rav Shmuel bar Yehudah's mother made him a silver scraper. End of his words. In my humble opinion, that incident with Rav Shmuel follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who discusses removing mud and filth. For the honor of Shabbos his mother made the implement of silver, not because it had to be specially designated. The Gemara records this to teach that the halachah follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. This also explains why the authorities ruled like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel against the Sages. Examine carefully. The Magen Avraham followed Olat Shabbos and did not notice this. As for his statement that unmarried women have the custom to keep an implement made of pig bristles designated for Shabbos, this has no connection to a scraper, which scrapes the body. Their implement serves instead of a comb. See the end of siman 303, where the concern is lest one uproot hairs from the head.
