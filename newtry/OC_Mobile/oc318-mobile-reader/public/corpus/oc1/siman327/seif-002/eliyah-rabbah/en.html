[3] And not forcefully, etc. This implies that even if one does not anoint himself, but merely rubs his body forcefully, it is prohibited. All this follows Rashi's explanation. According to the Rambam, chapter 21, however, it appears that even forceful rubbing is permitted so long as one does not intend to bring himself to perspiration. The Maggid Mishneh agrees, and so too it appears from the Shiltei Giborim and Malbushei Yom Tov. I am uncertain regarding the rubbing performed for those weakened by fatigue, which is intended to restore their strength and remove their weariness, whether this is considered medical treatment and is prohibited, or is considered pleasure and is permitted according to the Rambam, as stated by the Shiltei Giborim, page 165.
