These substances are forbidden because the Sages prohibited medical treatment on Shabbos as a decree lest one grind medicinal ingredients, as stated at the beginning of siman 328. It is forbidden even through a non-Jew when the person is able to go about like a healthy individual, as stated in siman 328, seif 1. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1.
