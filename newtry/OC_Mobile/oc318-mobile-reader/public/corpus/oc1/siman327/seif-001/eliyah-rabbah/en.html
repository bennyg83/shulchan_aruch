[1] Oil and vinegar, etc. In the Mishnah the wording is wine and vinegar. It is obvious that even without mixing them it is prohibited, because the purpose is evident. So too writes Malbushei Yom Tov.
[2] And in a place, etc. This is as we practice. Therefore it is prohibited to anoint the head when it has sores, or to anoint with oil any swelling anywhere on the body, as stated by the Shiltei Giborim in Perek Shemoneh Sheratzim. See what I wrote at the end of siman 318, that milk and fat are prohibited everywhere because of nolad.
