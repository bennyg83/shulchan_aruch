And we say in the Gemara, etc., as stated in Shabbos daf 147b: "The Rabbis taught: one may not scrape with a scraper on Shabbat," etc.; Rav Shmuel bar Yehuda — his mother made for him a silver scraper, etc.; and Rosh did not cite this that his mother made for him, etc.; end of his words — he was explaining the matters according to their plain sense and it makes no difference for the halacha, therefore he omitted it; but Rashi explained "silver scraper for Shabbat" end of his words; and what compelled Rashi to explain "for Shabbat"? If so, it is not satisfactory to him to explain that they are mere matters; rather he teaches us the halacha that if designated for Shabbat — permitted; and therefore his mother, when she wished to designate a scraper for Shabbat, did so for the honor of Shabbat; if so, when one designates for Shabbat — permitted, the Gemara teaches us this halacha:
