(1) [Seif 1] One whose loins pain him should not anoint with oil and vinegar. In the mishnah at end of chapter 8 of Sheratzim it states: one should not anoint with wine and vinegar, and so is the girsa in all books in our possession, and the same in Alfasi and Rambam chapter 21 halacha 23. But Tur wrote oil and vinegar, and it appears that such was the girsa before him, as Bach wrote. Shulchan Aruch followed Tur's girsa; there is a difference: per the girsa of the books, wine and vinegar — even each alone is forbidden, as Eliyah Rabbah wrote in note 1; but according to Tur and Shulchan Aruch, oil and vinegar — oil alone is not forbidden except when mixed together with vinegar; but oil alone is permitted for all. And it appears that even according to Tur's girsa one should not anoint with wine, for wine is like vinegar that one does not anoint with it except for healing; and for our girsa it is obvious that oil and vinegar, or wine and oil — forbidden.
