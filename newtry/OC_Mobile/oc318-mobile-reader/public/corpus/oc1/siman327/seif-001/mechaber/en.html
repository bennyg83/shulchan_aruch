The laws of anointing on Shabbat. And in it 4
seifim: One whose loins pain him should not anoint with oil and vinegar, but he may anoint with oil alone, but not with rose oil because it is evident that he does it for healing; and if it is a place where rose oil is common and the way of people is to anoint with it even without healing — permitted.And in a place where they do not practice anointing with oil except for healing — in all oil it is forbidden (Beit Yosef).
