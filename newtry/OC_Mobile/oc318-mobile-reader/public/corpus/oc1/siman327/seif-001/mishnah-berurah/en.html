(א) (1) Oil and vinegar — the reason is that it is not the way to anoint with both together except for healing, and all matters that are for healing the Rabbis forbade as a decree on account of grinding spices, which is forbidden from the Torah on account of tochen, as written in siman 328; and know that in our girsa in the mishnah, and so too in Rif and Rambam, it states wine and vinegar — meaning even in one of them alone:

(ב) (2) Oil alone — for even healthy people are accustomed to anoint with it and it is not discernible that it is for healing; and so on a wound it is permitted to anoint with oil for this reason, as below in siman 328 s.k. 82:

(ג) (3) It is evident — for its price is dear and a person does not anoint with it except for healing, and all matters of healing are forbidden as above; and even through a gentile it is forbidden if he is improving and progressing like a healthy person, as below in siman 328 [Peri Megadim]:

(ד) (4) In all oil forbidden — and therefore it is forbidden to anoint with oil on the head in our countries when there are scabs, and so if he has a boil anywhere it is forbidden to anoint with oil in our countries; and in milk and fat it is forbidden everywhere because it congeals and melts and becomes nolad, as above in siman 326 seif 10 in the gloss:
