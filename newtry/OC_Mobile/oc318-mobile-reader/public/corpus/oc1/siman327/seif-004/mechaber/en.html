One should not anoint his foot with oil while it is inside the shoe or sandal (new ones) (Rambam chapter 23) because the skin softens and it is comparable to tanning; but he may anoint his foot and place it in the shoe, and he may anoint his entire body and roll upon the skin, provided there is not on him from the oil wiped from his body an amount sufficient to tan it — even if there is in it enough to polish it, permitted — provided he does not intend even to polish it.
