[4] Designed for this, etc. Olat Shabbat wrote: I wonder at all the poskim who omitted the halacha that if one has a scraper designated for Shabbat — permitted, as they say in the Gemara [Shabbos] daf 147b: Rav Shmuel bar Yehuda — his mother made for him a silver scraper, until here; and in my humble opinion this Rav Shmuel stands on R' Shimon ben Gamliel, who deals with removing mud and excrement, and for the honor of Shabbat he would make of silver and did not require designation for this; and the Gemara explains to teach that the halacha is like R' Shimon ben Gamliel; and now it is satisfactory that they ruled like R' Shimon ben Gamliel against the Rabbis — examine this; and Magen Avraham followed Olat Shabbat and did not descend to this; and what he wrote that virgins practiced having an implement of pig bristles designated for Shabbat, until here — has no relation to a scraper, for a scraper scrapes the body, but this is in place of a comb; and see end of siman 303 that the reason there is lest he uproot hairs of the head:
