(Endnote 6) And even, etc. — nevertheless, etc. — meaning that below, siman 117, it is explained that in such a case they ask "and give dew and rain" in Shomea Tefillah — meaning specifically to ask for rain, since they need it. But they do not mention it, for mentioning is praise; and since the whole world has no need for it, it is not fitting to mention it in praise.
