6) In some countries where they have the custom to mention rain on the day of Hoshana Rabbah, they do not act properly; however, if on Hoshana Rabbah they announce 'Mashiv haruach,' etc., in order that they begin to mention it on the night of Shemini Atzeres, there is some support for their custom. Radbaz, volume 1, siman 240; Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos HaTur; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. However, from the words of the Rav [the Arizal], of blessed memory, in Shaar HaKavanos, in the ninth discourse on Sukkos, and in Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaLulav, chapter 8, it appears that the time for saying 'Mashiv haruach umorid hageshem' begins specifically from the Musaf prayer of Shemini Atzeres onward, and not before then, as seen there, for a reason rooted in the hidden [teachings].
