(Seif 6 in the gloss) Three blessings, and so forth. Note: See Magen Avraham, seif katan 9, who writes that if one erred in the middle, he does not repeat anything. In siman 119, seif katan 5, however, he writes regarding one who erred or omitted, meaning that he omitted the blessing entirely or erred by not reciting it in the formulation established by Chazal. At first glance these statements appear contradictory, but they are not. In siman 119 the intention is that he failed to mention the subject of the opening near the conclusion, or that he concluded or opened improperly. If, however, he opened and concluded properly and mentioned the subject of the opening near the conclusion, then even if he omitted material in the middle he need not repeat it. The Magen Avraham therefore directs one to siman 59, where it is explained that even if one said the words 'Who brings on evenings' in the middle, he has fulfilled his obligation. See there. This is obvious, unlike the Eliyah Rabbah, who writes that the matter requires study. See there, and examine this carefully.
