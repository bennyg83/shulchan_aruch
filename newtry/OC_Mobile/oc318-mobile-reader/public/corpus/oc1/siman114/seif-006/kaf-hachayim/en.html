(6) In some countries where the custom is to mention rain on Hoshana Rabbah, it is not proper conduct; but if they announce on Hoshana Rabbah "Who makes the wind blow," etc., so that they begin to mention it on the night of Shemini Atzeret, there is some support for their custom — Radbaz, volume 1, siman 200; Kanahag in Hagahat Tzioni; Magen Avraham, endnote 1. However, from the words of the Rav, may his memory be blessed, in Shaar HaKavanot, drush 9 of Sukkot, and Pri Etz Chayim, shaar HaLulav, chapter 8, it appears that the time for saying "Who makes the wind blow" and "Who makes rain fall" begins specifically from the Musaf prayer of Shemini Atzeret onward, and not before then; see there the reason in sod. And further, see in the sign afterward.
