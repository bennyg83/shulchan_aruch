In which he erred. For example, he erred in their concluding formula, or he failed to say HaMelech HaKadosh between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, as Tosafos writes. If he merely erred in the middle, however, this is of no consequence. See siman 209 and siman 59. There are many places with variant prayer texts. Beit Yosef writes at the end of siman 113 in the name of Abudraham that each person should not depart from his custom. In siman 122 he writes in the name of HaManhig that if one recited one of the blessings in an abbreviated formula, like blessings over fruit and mitzvos whose opening is their conclusion, he has altered the formula established by the Sages and has not fulfilled his obligation.
