Endnote 3: "Who makes." In truth it is clear in the Beit Yosef that they only say "Who makes dew descend," and similarly it is clear in the Shulchan Aruch from the fact that it writes: if one said "Who makes the wind blow" in the summer days, they do not make him go back — and this is from the Gemara; behold, not l'chatchila. And the Magen Avraham follows the Bach, who wrote so; see there.
