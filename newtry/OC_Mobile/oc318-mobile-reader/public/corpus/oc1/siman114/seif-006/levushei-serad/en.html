Seif katan 3. “Who makes.” In truth, Beit Yosef makes clear that they say only, “Who makes the dew descend.” This is also evident from Shulchan Aruch, which writes that if one said, “Who makes the wind blow” during the summer, he is not required to repeat the prayer. This ruling is from the Gemara, showing that it should not be said initially. Magen Avraham followed Bach, who wrote otherwise; see there.
