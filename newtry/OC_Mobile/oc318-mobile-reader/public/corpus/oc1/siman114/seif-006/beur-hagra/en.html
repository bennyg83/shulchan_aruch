(66) "In what circumstances," etc. — "but [if he remembered before finishing the blessing]," etc. — for the Sages did not establish a [specific] place within the blessing; rather they said simply in [the blessing of] resurrection of the dead; only the custom developed to say it before "Who sustains the living," which is livelihood — as written at the beginning of Ta'anit: "rain" means livelihood; but the main matter is to mention it in resurrection of the dead, as written [there] — "since it is equivalent," etc.; therefore in whatever place one mentions it, it suffices:

And even [if one concluded the blessing but remembered before Ata Kadosh], etc. — as written in Berakhot 49: "If one erred," etc. — "words" — only that not [full repetition], etc.:

Before one began "Ata Kadosh," etc. — Rosh there, in the name of R. Aviyah, and brings proof from "Who is good and does good" [blessing on Chanukah, etc.]; and it is difficult — for there (49a) the opposite [is stated]: that one does not say [the Name] there; for if one says [the Name], why did they need to enact a new blessing? And furthermore, the Rosh himself rejected his proof in tractate Berakhot; and furthermore we say there in Berakhot: if one erred and did not mention [retzé —] "Avodah," one returns to Avodah; and therefore one has not yet begun Modim — for we say there he is [already] mentioned in Hodáh [Modim], etc.:

Without [a] closing [formula]. Hagahot Maimoniyot and Mordechai, as on Rosh Chodesh there — they were uncertain and in uncertainty one does not conclude [with a blessing formula]; and even though it is more apt to compare to Shabbat and Yom Tov as written [above], since it is obligatory — one could say that there, after finishing [with] "Barukh Attah" in the Amidah, [it is different]; not so here in the middle of the eighteen [blessings], where interrupting is different. And so it appears.
