Seif 6: In what case... but... For the Sages did not fix a place within the blessing; rather they said simply [to mention it] in [the blessing of] Techiyat HaMetim. Only that they customarily say it before Mechalkel, which concerns parnasa, as is written at the beginning of Ta'anit: rains means parnasa. But the main point is to mention it in Techiyat HaMetim, as is written there, since it is equal, etc. Therefore, wherever one mentions it, it suffices.
And even... As is written in Berakhot 49: if one erred, etc. - it only applies when one has not yet, etc.
Before one began Atah Kadosh... Rosh there in the name of Raavyah, and he brings a proof from HaTov u'Meitiv - see there. And it is difficult: from there it appears the opposite, that we do not say it there - for if we did say it there, why did they need to institute a new blessing? And furthermore, the Rosh himself rejected his proof in tractate Berakhot. And furthermore, we say there in Berakhot: if one erred and did not mention [it] in Avodah, he returns to Avodah - and necessarily he has not yet begun Modim, for we say there: if he remembered during Hoda'ah, etc.
