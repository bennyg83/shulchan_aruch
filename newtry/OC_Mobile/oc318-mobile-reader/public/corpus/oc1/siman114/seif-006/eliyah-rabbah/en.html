Without a concluding blessing, etc. I am puzzled by the Mordechai at the beginning of Taanit, who writes in the name of the Raavyah that he does not conclude with a blessing, for at the end of the chapter Tefillat HaShachar the Mordechai and the Agudah write in the name of the Raavyah that he does conclude with a blessing. So too the Shiltei Gibborim, folio 22, writes in the name of the Mordechai regarding Atah Chonen. This requires study. The Beit Yosef and all the later authorities cite the passage at the beginning of Taanit and discuss at length why he should not conclude with a blessing; see there. They did not notice this difficulty. This also refutes everything the Taz discusses here at length; see Eliyah Zuta.
In which he erred, etc. This applies specifically to their concluding formulas, or where he did not say HaMelech HaKadosh during the Ten Days of Repentance. But if he erred in the middle, it is of no consequence. Magen Avraham, seif katan 9. This requires study in light of siman 119, seif katan 4 below.
