[6] He finished, etc. Even though he did not begin "Ata Kadosh," etc., and even though in seif 7 we say that whoever did not begin "Ata Kadosh" says "Who makes the wind blow," it appears that there — as the Levush wrote — the reason is that it is like an extended conclusion; but here, where he has already finished the conclusion and the blessing is completed, he must return to the beginning. But the Bach wrote that here too it deals specifically when he began "Ata Kadosh," as there; and it appears that he holds the reason there is because whoever began "Ata Kadosh" is as though the blessing is not finished, and here too whoever did not begin "Ata Kadosh" does not return except to "Ata Gibor." The Tur and Shulchan Aruch were silent about this, and later authorities did not note the dispute; and for the law it seems to me like the Bach.
