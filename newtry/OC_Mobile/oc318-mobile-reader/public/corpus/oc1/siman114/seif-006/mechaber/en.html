When do we say that we make him return when he did not say Morid ha'geshem during the rainy season? When he concluded the entire blessing and began the blessing after it — then he returns to the beginning of the prayer. But if he remembered before he concluded the blessing, he says it at the point where he remembered. And even if he concluded the blessing but remembered before he began Ata Kadosh, he need not return; rather he says Mashiv ha'ruach u'morid ha'geshem, without a closing formula.The first three blessings are considered as one; and anywhere one erred in them, one returns to the beginning, whether alone or with the congregation (Tur).]
