(8) [Seif 2] It is forbidden to mention rain, etc. — it implies; but regarding dew there is no prohibition if one mentions it before the prayer leader mentions it, whether an individual or many, as written in the Beit Yosef in the name of the Yerushalmi and the Ran; and not as the Tur wrote in the name of the Ravad, that even regarding dew it is forbidden to mention until the prayer leader mentions it. And similarly wrote the Peri Chadash, sign 20, and Maamar Mordechai, sign 3. However, it is proper to be careful even regarding dew, to take into account the view of the Ravad and the Tur; and such is the custom, as written below, sign 14; and see the Bach and Taz, endnote 4.
