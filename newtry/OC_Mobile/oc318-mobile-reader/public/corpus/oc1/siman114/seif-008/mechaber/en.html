During the summer months, if one is in doubt whether one mentioned Morid ha'geshem or not — up to thirty days there is a presumption that one mentioned rain, and one must return.And this applies for us who do not mention tal during the summer months. If one is in doubt whether one said Morid ha'geshem during the rainy season — for all thirty days one returns, for it is certain one said what one was accustomed to say; and indeed one did not mention either tal or rain. After thirty days one does not return (his own opinion).]:
