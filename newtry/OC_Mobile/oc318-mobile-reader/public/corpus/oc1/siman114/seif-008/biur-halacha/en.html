"Until thirty days in presumption," etc. — see in Mishnah Berurah that there are opinions among the poskim whether thirty days literally, or like the count of thirty days — that is, ninety prayers. And behold for poskim who hold that it depends on thirty literal days — both for leniency and for stringency — it would seem that the first day is also joined to a complete day even though they did not begin with it except in Musaf or Mincha; but there is [room] to study regarding asking [for rain], where here too the measure is thirty days as for mentioning [rain], as Ohr Zarua wrote — whether the first two days of Pesach are also joined to this. And behold per the view of the Acharonim who disagree with Taz in what he wrote — that if one inadvertently or willfully [skipped] one day and did not pray at all, it joins for the presumption of thirty days — certainly all the more so here it does not join; but I am uncertain regarding the words of Taz — that perhaps Taz deals only if one skipped in the middle and not at the beginning; or perhaps that even in this case, since with the combination of these two days thirty days are completed in which one did not say dew and rain, one has already removed oneself from one's presumption that existed before then:
