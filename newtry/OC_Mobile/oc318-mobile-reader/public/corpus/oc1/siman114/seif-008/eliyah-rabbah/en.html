[8] Without a conclusion, etc. I would wonder about the Mordechai at the beginning of Taanit, who wrote in the name of Raavyah that he does not conclude — but at the end of the chapter "Prayer of the Morning" the Mordechai and Agudah wrote in the name of Raavyah that he does conclude; and similarly wrote Sheltei Giborim, page 22, in the name of the Mordechai regarding "Ata Chonen," and study is needed. The Beit Yosef and all the later authorities brought this [passage] at the beginning of Taanit to elaborate in pilpul why he should not conclude; see there, and they did not notice this wonder. Also in this, all that the Taz extended here is rejected; see Eliyahu Zuta.
