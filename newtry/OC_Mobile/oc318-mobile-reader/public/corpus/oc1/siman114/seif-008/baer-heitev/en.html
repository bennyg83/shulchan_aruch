The rain. Because one is accustomed from winter days [and even] though now one also says "Who makes the wind blow and Who makes dew descend" — it is possible he erred between dew and rain; but in rainy days it is not applicable to say he was in doubt whether he mentioned rain, etc., for even if he said in rainy days as in hot days "Who makes the wind blow and Who makes dew descend" we do not make him go back, as I wrote in seif 5; and for this Rama wrote, and for us who do not mention dew in the hot season, automatically there is room to be in doubt also in rainy days.
