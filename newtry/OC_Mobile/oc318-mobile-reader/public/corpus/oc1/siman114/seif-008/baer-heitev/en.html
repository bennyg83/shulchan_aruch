The rain. This is because his tongue is accustomed to the wording used during the winter. Although he now also says, “Who makes the wind blow and the dew descend,” it is possible that he confused dew with rain. During the rainy season, however, there is no basis for uncertainty whether he mentioned rain, for even if during the rainy season he recited the summer formula, “Who makes the wind blow and the dew descend,” we do not require him to repeat the prayer, as I wrote in seif 5. Rama therefore writes that according to our custom, under which dew is not mentioned during the summer, uncertainty can likewise arise during the rainy season.
