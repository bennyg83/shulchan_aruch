And there is no [separate reason] in letter 7; and in the Levush the reason that it is impossible to say "Who causes the rain to fall" lest it appear as if we refuse rain — they brought Magen Avraham, siman 5; our custom we wrote above — the reason, and it applies in the pause as the practice of Rav Amram; but in the announcement they announce before Musaf the shamash, not like Rav Amram who wrote that in the mention the congregation also begins at Minchah — I brought this above.

The author of the Maor wrote in chapter 1 of Taanis, aggada (Psalms 17:1): "Hear, O the Master, righteousness" — Shema, "Attend to my cry" — prayer; "Give ear to my prayer" — Musaf prayer; "When You judge" — they blow the shofar at Musaf because a person's mind is not settled except in Musaf prayer; therefore they pray for rain at Musaf. See Yerushalmi and the commentator (4:4) — they brought it; see elsewhere.

In Parashat Derakhim, Derekh Heaven, he brought s.v. chapter 3 of Niddah that even Af and Bri, princes of rain, do nothing except by permission of the Holy One, blessed be He. He wondered: all things are done by permission of the Holy One, blessed be He. One can say that certainly all things were initially entrusted to an agent, but His providence, blessed be He, is always upon them, and without Him it is impossible for any existence to exist for even one moment; not so with rain — no agent was appointed over it; only when the Holy One, blessed be He, wishes to rain He sends down influence through Af and Bri, etc. The Maharshal interpreted that rains from the storehouse of good were not entrusted to an agent, as it is written "The the Master will open for you His good storehouse" — but other rains were entrusted to Af and Bri — see there.
