Taz, seif katan 12. He concludes, “I do not know whether he concludes the blessing.” Meaning, whether he concludes, “Who sanctifies Israel and Rosh Chodesh.” On Shabbos and Yom Tov, if one forgot Yaaleh VeYavo in Bircas HaMazon, he says, “Blessed is He Who granted,” and concludes, “Blessed is He Who sanctifies Shabbos,” and similarly on Yom Tov. The reason is that eating bread on Shabbos and Yom Tov is obligatory, since one cannot avoid eating. This law appears below in siman 188, seif 7, where a distinction is made between one who has not yet begun HaTov VeHaMeitiv and one who has begun it. See there.
