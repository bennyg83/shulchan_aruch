Taz, endnote 12: and he concludes, "I do not know whether he concludes." Meaning: whether he concludes "Who sanctifies Israel and the beginnings of months" — for on Shabbat and Yom Tov, if one forgot in Grace after Meals "Yaaleh veYavo," one says "Blessed is He Who gave," etc., and concludes "Blessed is He Who sanctifies the Shabbat"; and similarly on Yom Tov. The reason is because in Grace after Meals there is an obligation on Shabbat and Yom Tov, for it is impossible that he did not eat, and it is obligatory for him to eat; and this law is below, siman 488, seif 20, seif 7; and there is a distinction between one who did not open "hatov vehametiv" and one who opened on the Divine Name.
