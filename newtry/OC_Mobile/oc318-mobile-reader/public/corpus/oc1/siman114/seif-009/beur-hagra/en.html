(69) If on the first day, etc. — as written in Bava Kamma 24: "If he distanced his goring," etc.; but Rashba disagreed with him, that it is not similar to there, for here it depends on habit of speech, and that specifically is over a long period; and proof to his words — for otherwise why would dew and rain be equivalent to "Who makes the wind blow" — behold Shabbat and Yom Tov are extra [prayers]; and he strained himself on this.
