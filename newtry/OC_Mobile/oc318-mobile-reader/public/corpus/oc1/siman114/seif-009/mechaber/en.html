If on the first day of Pesach one says from the blessing Ata Gibor until Morid ha'tal ninety times — corresponding to the thirty days when one would say it three times each day — from that point onward, if one does not remember whether one mentioned rain, there is a presumption that one did not mention rain and one need not return.And similarly for us: if one said until Mechalkel chayim without Mashiv ha'ruach u'morid ha'geshem that we mention during the rainy season; and similarly if on Shemini Atzeret one said ninety times Ata Gibor until Morid ha'geshem — if afterward one is in doubt whether one mentioned it or not, the presumption is that one mentioned it (his own opinion).]:
