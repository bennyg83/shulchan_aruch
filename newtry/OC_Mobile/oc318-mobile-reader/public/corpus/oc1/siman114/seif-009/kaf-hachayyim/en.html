9) There: Until the shaliach tzibbur announces. This means even though he is not praying with the congregation; for if [he were praying] with the congregation, throughout all the days of the year it is likewise forbidden to precede his own tefillah to the tefillah of the congregation, as stated in siman 90, seif 10. So too wrote the Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2; Ateres Zekeinim, ot 2. See below, ot 15.
