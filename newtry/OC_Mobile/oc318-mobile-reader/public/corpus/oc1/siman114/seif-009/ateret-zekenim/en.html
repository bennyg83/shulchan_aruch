If, on the first day of Pesach, he recites the blessing from “You are mighty” through “Who causes the dew to descend” ninety times, corresponding to thirty days in which it is recited three times each day, he is thereafter presumed not to have mentioned rain, etc. This is difficult, for on Shabbos and Yom Tov it is recited four times each day, so that over thirty days there are more than ninety recitations. It may be answered that the same law applies regarding the request for rain, and there are fewer than ninety requests, since no request for rain is made on Shabbos or Yom Tov. They therefore adopted the intermediate number of ninety recitations. Darkei Moshe states that Rama wrote this in a responsum.
