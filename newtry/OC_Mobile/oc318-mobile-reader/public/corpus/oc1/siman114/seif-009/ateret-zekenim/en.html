If on the first day of Passover one said the blessing "You are mighty" through "Who makes dew descend" ninety times — corresponding to thirty days, for one says it three times each day — behold he is in the presumption that he did not mention [rain], etc. And it is difficult: behold on Shabbat and Yom Tov they say it four times each day, and there are more than ninety in thirty days. And one may say that behold regarding petition [for rain] the law is likewise; but regarding petition there are fewer than ninety, for behold on Shabbat and Yom Tov there is no petition; therefore they took the intermediate figure of ninety times (Darkei Moshe — thus wrote the Rabbi Rama; in responsa Magen Avraham).
