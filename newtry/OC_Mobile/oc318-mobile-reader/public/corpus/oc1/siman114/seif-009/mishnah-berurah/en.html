(39) The blessing "Atah Gibor" — and in sefer Shulchan Shlomo he wrote one should begin from Mechayeh Metim Atah and not from Atah Gibor so he not say [God's] name in vain. And if he comes to change the mention of the Name — we are concerned he will come to change afterward because of habit also in prayer.
(40) Until Morid haTal — and "until" is inclusive; and this is for Sephardim who practice mentioning dew in summer days. And for us, until Mechalkel Chayim and "until" is inclusive, as explained below in the gloss. And the reason for all this — that through this his tongue is already accustomed to say properly; therefore the same law applies regarding She'alah: if he said ninety times the blessing of the years until after the word "and give blessing" in summer days, or until after the word "and give dew and rain" in rainy days — that suffices, and he should not begin from the beginning of the blessing "On us" but only from "and all kinds of its grain," etc.
(41) Ninety times — and in the responsum of Chasam Sofer Orach Chayim siman 20 he teaches that lekhatchilah one should say it one hundred and one times. However after the fact, one who said only ninety times — we cannot rule that he must return and pray against the ruling of Shulchan Aruch.
(42) Behold he, etc. — for if he accustomed his tongue when saying scattered [prayers] — kal vachomer when [saying] consecutively. And he wrote in Sha'arei Teshuvah that if he accustomed himself forty-five times — which correspond to fifteen days — after fifteen days pass there is a presumption he said properly.
(43) Until Mechalkel Chayim — and "until" is inclusive; and therefore his tongue became accustomed to say "Who saves" from Mechalkel Chayim. And there are those who disagree with Rama on this, and it is proper to be concerned lekhatchilah not to do so relying on this. So wrote Darchei Chayim, and Chayei Adam does not seem so.
(44) Until Morid haGeshem — and "until" is inclusive.
