Regarding this: See Baer Heitev. The Shalmei Tzibbur writes that if one prayed and forgot a matter whose omission does not require repetition, but mistakenly thought that he was required to repeat the prayer and began praying again, and then remembered that according to law he need not repeat it, he should stop immediately upon remembering, even in the middle of a blessing. So write Michtam LeDavid and Maamar Mordechai, siman 602, regarding the recitation of Al HaNissim. See there.
