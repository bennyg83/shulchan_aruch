For thirty days. Although on Shabbos and Yom Tov it is said four times each day, so that there are more than ninety recitations within thirty days, one may answer that the same rule applies to the request for rain, while the request is said fewer than ninety times because it is not said on Shabbos and Yom Tov. We therefore adopt the intermediate measure of ninety times. Darkei Moshe. Rama likewise wrote this in his responsum, siman 83; see there. Magen Avraham writes that we do not find that this rule also applies to the request for rain; see there. Knesses HaGedolah also remained uncertain whether it is effective for the request as well. Olas Tamid writes plainly that it is effective for the request too; see there. If during those thirty days one inadvertently or deliberately failed to pray altogether on one or two days, his established presumption is not impaired. Taz; see there.
Rain. His proof is from the law of an ox established as prone to gore, regarding which we say: if liability is established when its gorings were far apart, then all the more so when its gorings were close together. The same applies here. Taz and Shelah. Responsa Ginas Veradim, klal 1, siman 101, rejects his proof; see there. See also Devar Shmuel, siman 225, Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 243, Magen Avraham, and Bach, who resolve the matter; see there.
Life. The words “sustains life” are included. His tongue will thereby become accustomed to saying, “abundant in salvation, sustains life.”
