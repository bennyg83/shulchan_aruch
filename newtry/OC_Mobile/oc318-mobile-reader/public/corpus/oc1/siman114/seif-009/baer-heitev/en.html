According to those who hold "day." And that on Shabbat and Yom Tov they say it four times each day, and there are more than ninety in thirty days according to those who hold "day" — one may say that behold regarding petition the law is likewise; but regarding petition there are fewer than ninety, for behold on Shabbat and Yom Tov there is no petition; therefore we took the intermediate expression of ninety times. Darkei Moshe. And thus wrote Rama in his responsum siman 83, see there. Magen Avraham wrote that we do not find it applies to petition, see there. Also in Shakh there is a doubt whether it helps also regarding petition or not, see there; and in Beit Timanim he wrote plainly that it helps also regarding petition, see there. If one erred inadvertently or willfully on some day or days in those [thirty days] according to those who hold "day" and did not pray at all — his presumption is not impaired. Taz, see there.
