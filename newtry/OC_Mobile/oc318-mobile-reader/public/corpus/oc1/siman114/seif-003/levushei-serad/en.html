There: not like the Beit Yosef, etc., "even with the congregation." His intention is that the Beit Yosef explains that this Yerushalmi deals also with the congregation, and from this it follows for him that there is also a distinction with the congregation between rain and dew; and the Taz disagrees on this — rather, the Yerushalmi deals with an individual, but with the congregation there is no distinction. And know that the words of the Beit Yosef in their place need a little study, and one should not elaborate.
