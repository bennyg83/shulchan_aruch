Seif 3, And likewise regarding dew, if, etc. This is stated in the Yerushalmi and cited by Tosafos there, s.v. bimos, and in Berachos 29b, s.v. ha, etc.
Or, etc. The Gemara in Taanis there states: “It was taught,” etc., “and not only that,” etc.
And I, etc. This is implied there from the teaching concerning dew, etc. However, in the Yerushalmi cited above it states that he was standing during the mention of dew, etc., which implies the first opinion.
Some say, etc. The Rosh cites this in the name of Rav Amram. See the reasoning in Magen Avraham. Further, according to the Ran’s explanation, all the Yerushalmi’s questions, “let him mention it in the evening” and “in the morning,” refer to dew, not rain. Regarding rain, it had already explained that this was arranged so the festivals should conclude with dew. It was about this that they said one may not mention it until the chazzan mentions it, in accordance with Rav Amram’s explanation above. The Magen Avraham’s explanation follows the Bach, who writes that it is forbidden to mention it, etc., whether silently or aloud during the repetition. See Rosh there.
