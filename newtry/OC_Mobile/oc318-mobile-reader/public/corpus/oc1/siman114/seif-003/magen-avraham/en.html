And likewise regarding dew. Meaning, the Sephardic custom is to say “Who causes the wind to blow and the dew to descend” during the summer.
He is not required to repeat. Dew and winds are never withheld. Nevertheless, it is proper to mention them so they should be for blessing. It appears to me that even if he has not yet completed the blessing, he is not made to repeat the words, since there is no obligation at all to say them. See the end of siman 294.
They have already heard. They do not announce the cessation by saying “Who causes the wind,” etc., because this would appear as though they reject rain, in keeping with the Sages’ statement that one does not pray concerning an excess of good. Levush.
