(3) One should read "Ata Gibor" with the gimel pointed (dagesh) — Mateh Moshe, siman 125, in the name of the Tzioni, as Kanahag in Hagahat Tzioni, sign 1, Orach Torah there, Atzei Zahav there, Alef Daled, sign 1, Shlah, page 115 side 4, Kaf HaChayim siman 15, sign 22; and in the book Ein Ayah, sign 28. Ein Ayah wrote that one should intend in the blessing "Ata Gibor" for the name AGL"A, which is a segulah for success and favor; see there, Ruach Chayim, sign 3, Ben Aharon, parashat Beshalach, sign 13.
