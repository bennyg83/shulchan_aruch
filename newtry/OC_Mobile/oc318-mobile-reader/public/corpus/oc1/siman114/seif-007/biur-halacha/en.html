"But if intentionally," etc. — see above siman 104, seif 6, regarding speech, which seemingly contradicts this [law] here; and see in the Acharonim who labored to reconcile this; and in Biur HaGra there it is implied that for Rashba one does not distinguish between inadvertent and intentional; but from the words of all the other Acharonim it is proven that they apply it for halakhah as the Tur ruled here without qualification. And Beit Yosef and Ohr Zarua wrote that even if the mistake was in one of the middle blessings, one also returns to the beginning of the prayer — for all eighteen blessings were said in order and all are considered as one blessing for this matter:
