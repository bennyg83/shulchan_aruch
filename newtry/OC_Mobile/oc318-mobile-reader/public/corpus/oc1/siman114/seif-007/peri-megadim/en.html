"Who causes [the wind]" — in letter 7: dew and wind are never withheld; however dew in the blessing is withheld, as Eliyahu (peace be upon him) said, if there was dew, etc. — but only according to his words. In Halakhot Gedolot: if one did not mention wind but mentioned rain, he has fulfilled his obligation — for we require also mention of wind because it relates to rain. See in seif 5 in Taz and Magen Avraham, with God's help.
