Seif 4, And he returns to the beginning of the blessing, etc. This is stated by the Rosh at the beginning of Taanis and by Mordechai in the name of Raavyah. This requires study, for Berachos states that one who errs in the first three blessings returns to the beginning, since the first three are regarded as one unit. It appears that the Rambam’s ruling is correct, that he always returns to the beginning.
And if he completed, etc. As stated in Berachos 34a: “If one erred,” etc.
Even in a place, etc. This is stated by Rabbeinu Yonah in chapter 4 of Berachos, as stated at the end of the first chapter of Taanis concerning the people of Nineveh, who sent a question, etc. They may not alter the formula established by the Sages.
And likewise if, etc. This is stated in the Yerushalmi and cited by Tosafos there: “But was it not taught regarding dew,” etc.? The cases are not comparable, etc.
