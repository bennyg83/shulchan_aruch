(64) "And one returns to the beginning of the blessing," etc. — Rosh at the beginning of Ta'anit and Mordechai in the name of R. Aviyah. And there is a difficulty: for we say in Berakhot [that] if one erred in [the first] three [blessings], one returns to [the blessing of] fire — for the first three are considered as one; and it would seem as Rambam wrote, that one always returns to the beginning [of the Amidah]:

And if one finished [the blessing], etc. — as written in Berakhot 34a: "If one erred," etc.:

And even in a place [that needs rain], etc. — Terumat HaDeshen in chapter 4 of Berakhot; and as written at the end of the first chapter of Ta'anit: "They sent [a letter] to the people of Nineveh," etc., and they did not deviate from the custom of the Sages:

And similarly if [one mentioned rain instead of dew], etc. — Jerusalem Talmud; and Tosafot cited there; and the baraita [regarding] dew, etc., and it is not similar [to mentioning rain], etc.:
