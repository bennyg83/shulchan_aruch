If he said "Who makes rain fall" in the hot season, they make him go back, even though after thirty days from Passover it does not harm; nevertheless he must go back, since most of the time when one ceases mentioning rain it is a sign of a curse — the Rabbis do not disagree (Ran at the beginning of Taanis). For anything for which one must go back on its account, one should intend one's mind and hear from the prayer leader all eighteen blessings.
