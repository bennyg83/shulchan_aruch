If one said Morid HaGeshem during the summer, he is required to repeat the prayer. Although rain is not harmful during the thirty days following Pesach, he must nevertheless repeat it, because during most of the period in which rain is no longer mentioned it is a sign of curse, and the Sages did not distinguish. Ran at the beginning of Ta'anit. Whenever a person makes an error for which he must repeat the prayer, he should concentrate and listen to all eighteen blessings from the shaliach tzibbur.
