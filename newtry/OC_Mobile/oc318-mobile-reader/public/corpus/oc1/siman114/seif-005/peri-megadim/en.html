Also [see] in siman 90, seif 15; however when one is in one's home there is no prohibition, only a mitzvah from a connected [source], as in seif 9 there; here even a sick person is forbidden. If one sees that the hour is passing, how shall we act? To mention [rain] is forbidden — if he prays thus he ends up not having mentioned and must repeat, as in seif 5. It appears he should wait until Minchah and complete both, and this is preferable to do so since Musaf's time is all day, except that after seven [hours] one is called a transgressor, as in siman 286. Since if it is impossible he is an ones, after seven he prays Minchah and Musaf and not from the doctrine of completion — for there is no completion for Musaf, only its time is still available, as in siman 108, seif 66, and as stated. However, to pray Musaf individually before seven and to say "Who causes the wind to blow" and "Who causes the dew to fall" so that he fulfills [the obligation], as in seif 5 — it is possible this constitutes mixing; on Pesach, likewise for us in silent prayer they mention at Minchah, as in seif 3 in the gloss. Sephardim may rely on what is written that dew one mentions, and similarly from the Mechaber that one is forbidden to mention rain, etc. With God's help I will explain further in Magen Avraham.
