5) There, in the Mussaf prayer of the final Yom Tov, etc. We do not begin mentioning rain at Maariv because not everyone is in the synagogue and the congregation would become divided, some mentioning it and others not. We do not begin at Shacharit so that a person will not suppose that it had been mentioned the previous evening and then, the following year, begin at night. There is another reason why we do not begin at Shacharit. Rabbi Chaggai bar Padas said that an individual may not mention rain until the prayer leader mentions it, meaning that the prayer leader must say 'Mashiv haruach' aloud before the prayer. This cannot be done at Shacharit because redemption must be joined to the Amidah. So writes the Rosh based on the Yerushalmi, cited by Beit Yosef; Derishah, letter 1; Taz, seif katan 2; Magen Avraham, seif katan 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. The Yerushalmi gives another reason. Rabbi Avin said in the name of Rabbi Yochanan that Rabbi Yehudah's reason is that the festivals should depart with dew, because dew is beneficial. Rosh explains that this rationale concerns the beginning of the mention rather than its cessation. On Yom Tov everyone comes to the synagogue in the evening, since no melacha is done, so the first reason is insufficient. The arrangement therefore allows dew to be mentioned on the festival also at Maariv and Shacharit. Beit Yosef and Derishah cite this. See the following letter and letter 7.
