Seif 3 in the gloss — that they already heard. If an individual delayed his prayer until after the shaliach tzibur prayed Musaf, he should not say "Who causes the wind to blow" — Magen Avraham below, siman 388.
Seif 5 — they return him. In my humble opinion, on Shabbat night if he forgot to say "Who causes the wind to blow" — they do not return him, for if he prayed only the seven-blessing [version] he has fulfilled, as stated in siman 268 in Magen Avraham, siman 15 — even in the seven blessings one does not mention rain. Therefore in what he prayed the order of prayer properly but did not mention rain — this does not detract from [fulfillment via] the seven blessings. Doubtful for practical ruling.
