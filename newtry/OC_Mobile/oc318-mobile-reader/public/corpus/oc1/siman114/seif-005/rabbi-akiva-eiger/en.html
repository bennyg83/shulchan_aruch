Seif 3 in the gloss: Who have already heard it. If an individual delayed his prayer until after the chazzan had prayed Mussaf, he should not say Mashiv haruach. See Magen Avraham below, at the end of siman 488.
Seif 5: He is required to repeat it. It appears to me that on Friday night, if one forgot to say Mashiv haruach, he is not required to repeat the prayer. If he prayed only the abridged seven-blessing prayer, he fulfilled his obligation, as stated in siman 268, Magen Avraham, seif katan 15, and the abridged seven-blessing prayer does not mention rain. Therefore, when he prayed the regular order of prayer properly but merely omitted the mention of rain, it is no worse than the abridged seven-blessing prayer. This requires further study for a practical ruling.
