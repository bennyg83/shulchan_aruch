[5] And he returns to the beginning, etc. Even though nothing is corrected once they have already said it, nevertheless in his return he reveals his mind that he is not requesting it — Levush. And Levush Yom Tov wrote: "and the skies are revealed," and it is like returning speech, which is effective in all matters; even though in blasphemy it is not effective, here too it is effective even not within the time of speech. And similarly wrote Maadanei Melech, page 33.
