[5] And he returns to the beginning, etc. Although repeating does not correct what he already said, his return reveals that he does not seek rain. This is Levush’s explanation. Malbushei Yom Tov writes that the matter is revealed before Heaven, and it is comparable to retracting one’s speech, which is effective in all matters. Although retraction is ineffective regarding blasphemy, here it is effective even after more than the brief interval required for speech. Maadanei Melech, page 33, writes likewise.
