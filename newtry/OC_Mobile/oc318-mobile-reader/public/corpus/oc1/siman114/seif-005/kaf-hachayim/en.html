(5) There, in the Musaf prayer of the last Yom Tov, etc. — and the reason they do not begin in Maariv is because not everyone is in the synagogue, and they would make groups: this one mentions and this one does not mention. And the reason they do not begin in Shacharit is so that one should not think they already mentioned it in the evening, and the next year he will mention it in the evening. And there is another reason why they do not begin in Shacharit: Rabbi Hagai bar Pedat said it is forbidden for an individual to mention it until the prayer leader mentions it — meaning that the prayer leader must say before the prayer "Who makes the wind blow" in a loud voice, and this is impossible in Shacharit, because one must adjoin redemption to prayer. Page 22, the Rosh on the Yerushalmi, and the Beit Yosef brought it; Derishah, sign 1; Taz, endnote 2; Magen Avraham, endnote 1; Rama Zal there. And further, the Yerushalmi explains another reason: Rabbi Avin in the name of Rabbi Yochanan — Rabbi Yehuda's reason is so that the festivals should go out with dew, because dew is good forever; and the Rosh explained that this reason applies to mentioning, not to ceasing, for on Yom Tov everyone comes to the synagogue in the evening, since they do not do melacha on chol hamoed; therefore the first reason is not sufficient, and therefore he separates so that one mentions dew on the festivals also in Maariv and Shacharit — end of his words; and the Beit Yosef and Derishah brought it there; and see in the sign afterward and sign 7.
