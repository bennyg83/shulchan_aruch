(Seif 6 in the gloss) "Three blessings," etc. NB — see in Magen Avraham sk 9, what he wrote that however, if one erred in the middle, one does not bless at all. And behold in siman 119, sk 5, he wrote: if one erred or skipped — meaning if one skipped the blessing entirely, or erred in not saying it as the Sages enacted, etc. And behold it would seem the matters contradict, but they do not contradict: for in siman 119 the intention is that one did not say something of the opening near the closing, or one closed or opened improperly; but if one opened and closed and mentioned something of the opening near the closing, even though one skipped in the middle, one need not return; and similarly Magen Avraham noted to see siman 59, where the intention is explained — that even if one said in the middle "He Who brings on evenings," one has fulfilled [the obligation]; and this is not like Ohr Zarua, who wrote; and it requires study there; examine well:
