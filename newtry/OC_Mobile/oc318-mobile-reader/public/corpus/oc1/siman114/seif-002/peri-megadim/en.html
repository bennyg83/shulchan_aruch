In Musaf prayer — Taz in Tur and Bach in name of Yerushalmi, see there. And he explained: he also held they mentioned it in the evening and for the year would mention in the evening and there would be agudot (groups). And Bach in name of Semag: they made a mistake thinking they mentioned in the evening when he did not mention, and he prayed Shacharis twice for compensation and his blessing was in vain — see there. And it appears both explanations apply to the custom of Sephardim who always mention dew even in summer days — they say Mashiv haRuach uMorid haTal. If so on Yom Tov: if he mentioned dew and not rain as in seif 5 — they do not return him, only agudot. And for Ashkenazim who do not mention dew on Yom Tov — there is concern of Semag.
