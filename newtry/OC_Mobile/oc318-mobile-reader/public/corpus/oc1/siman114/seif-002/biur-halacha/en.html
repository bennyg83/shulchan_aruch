"It is forbidden to mention [rain]," etc. — see in Mishnah Berurah that he wrote that even the congregation [may not mention rain before the announcement], etc.; thus many Acharonim explained [Taz in sk 6, Peri Chadash, Gra, Mishmeret Shalom, Shulchan Atzei Shittim, and so it is implied from Levush] the view of the Mechaber; and so his view is proven in Beit Yosef — not like Machatzit HaShekel and Yad Efrayim, who sided with explaining the words of Magen Avraham to say that the Mechaber holds like the view of Avi Ezri brought in the Rosh, as Beit Yosef explained there. And for practical halakhah in any case there is no difference, for even according to them the Rama disagrees on this; and Darkhei Chaim, Chayei Adam, Shulchan Shlomo, and many other Acharonim all ruled like the Rama. And know that even though Peri Chadash disagrees with Shulchan Aruch and Rama and holds that only an individual may not pray and mention rain before it was announced — but the congregation and the prayer leader who stand to pray Musaf are permitted to say rain within the prayer as if it had already been announced and publicized — we have nothing but the ruling of Shulchan Aruch, and as the above Acharonim ruled: that if they did not announce beforehand, the congregation and prayer leader also do not mention rain in their prayer silently. And know further that since they did not mention the advice cited in Magen Avraham — that the prayer leader mention within the prayer aloud "Who makes the wind blow" — it is implied that he holds it is not proper to do so; nevertheless it is implied from the words of Chayei Adam that after the fact this advice helps; and even though I do not know its source — for Magen Avraham copied the words of Beit Yosef that for the Rama this does not help — nevertheless it seems one may rely on it, for in any case the view of Darkhei Chaim is that after the fact, even if one mentioned rain on the night of Shemini Atzeret, one does not return; and conversely, according to R. Aviyah and Peri Chadash, who held his view — if one did not mention rain now, one has not fulfilled [one's obligation in] prayer:

"One should not advance [one's prayer]," etc. — I am uncertain: if they prayed a quorum of regulars in this synagogue itself and announced "Who makes the wind blow" as customary — does this help for a second quorum that an individual should be permitted to advance? And it would seem that since there is such an interruption, it is not called an announcement — for behold the Rosh brought in the name of R. Akiva that mentioning [rain] in Musaf helps for Mincha; see in the Rosh there that he does not disagree with this reasoning; and similarly the Rama ruled in seif 3. And it is possible that since it is another quorum, and also that in the morning they will have to say "Who makes the wind blow" — therefore this does not help for the later Musaf prayer. And this doubt applies also to what the Rama ruled in seif 3 — that the congregation is accustomed to mention "Who makes the wind blow" on the first day of Pesach in Musaf because they have not yet heard the prayer leader stop saying "Who makes the wind blow": if in that synagogue they prayed the first quorum and the prayer leader already stopped saying "Who makes the wind blow" in the prayer leader's repetition — does it help that they need not again say "Who makes the wind blow" in the Musaf prayer? It requires study; therefore it seems good to be stringent not to make a first quorum in the synagogue on the first day of Pesach:
