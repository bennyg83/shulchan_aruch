8) [Seif 2] It is forbidden to mention rain, etc. This implies that with dew, however, there is no prohibition if one mentions it before the shaliach tzibbur mentions it, whether an individual or many, as the Beis Yosef wrote in the name of the Yerushalmi and the Ran; and not like the Tur wrote in the name of the Ra'avad, that even with dew it is forbidden to mention until the shaliach tzibbur mentions it. So too wrote the Pri Chadash, ot 20, and the Maamar Mordechai, ot 3. Nevertheless, it is proper to be careful even with dew, out of concern for the view of the Ra'avad and the Tur; and such is the custom, as stated below in ot 14. See the Bach and the Taz, s.k. 4.
9) There: Until the shaliach tzibbur announces. This means even though he is not praying with the congregation; for if [he were praying] with the congregation, throughout all the days of the year it is likewise forbidden to precede his own tefillah to the tefillah of the congregation, as stated in siman 90, seif 10. So too wrote the Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2; Ateres Zekeinim, ot 2. See below, ot 15.
