It is forbidden to mention rain until the prayer leader mentions it, even if one is not praying with the congregation, for if with the congregation — throughout all days of the year one may also not precede one's prayer to the congregation's prayer; and specifically regarding rain it is forbidden to mention it before the prayer leader; but regarding dew, if one wishes to mention it individually one may mention it (Ran at the beginning of Taanis). And some say that dew too is forbidden to mention before the prayer leader (Beit Yosef).
