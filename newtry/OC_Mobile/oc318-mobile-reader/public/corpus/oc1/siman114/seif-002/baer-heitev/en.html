Shatz. That is to say, the prayer leader should say aloud "Who makes the wind blow" within the prayer. And Rama holds that the attendant proclaims before the prayer, and Bach wrote that according to his view, even if the prayer leader mentioned it in a whisper it is forbidden for the congregation to mention unless they first proclaimed aloud; and thus in seif 3 in the gloss, and it is in Beit Yosef; and even one praying at home is forbidden to mention until the congregation mentioned it; and Magen Avraham wrote, and it seems plausible that one residing where there is no minyan should wait until the time when they pray in congregations.
