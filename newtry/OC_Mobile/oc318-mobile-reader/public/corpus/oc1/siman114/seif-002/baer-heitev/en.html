The chazzan. Meaning, the chazzan should say aloud, within the prayer, “Who makes the wind blow.” Rama maintains that the attendant announces it before the prayer. Bach writes that according to this view, even if the chazzan mentioned it aloud during his silent Amidah, the congregation may not mention it unless it was first announced aloud. This also appears from seif 3 in the gloss, and it is stated in Beit Yosef that even one praying at home may not mention it until the congregation has done so. Magen Avraham writes: It appears to me that one who lives in a place without a minyan should wait until the time when the congregations pray.
