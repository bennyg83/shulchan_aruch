And some say, etc. From his words it appears he holds that the Shulchan Aruch holds that "he should proclaim" means the mention by the prayer leader in the repetition of the prayer, and then the congregation mentions in Mincha — and "in order to" etc. means in Musaf; and it is not so, for the Shulchan Aruch also holds thus as they wrote "therefore" etc. And for this reason etc., and thus explicitly in Rosh and at the end; and what Magen Avraham explained, namely etc., is not one [thing] — what is "he should not precede" etc., even with them it is forbidden to precede before the prayer leader mentions; and furthermore what does he say "and for this reason" they began etc. immediately at the start of the prayer? Also the wording "that he proclaim" — and I have not seen anyone who holds thus except R' Chiya and the Geonim, and they hold in the repetition of the prayer leader as written in seif 3 in the gloss — and so explained Taz.
