Seif 2, It is forbidden, etc. This is stated in the Yerushalmi, cited by the Rosh there and by the other poskim.
And some say, etc. His words imply that he understands the Shulchan Aruch to mean that the chazzan should announce it by mentioning it in the repetition of the Amidah, and the congregation should then mention it at Minchah. Accordingly, “so that,” etc., means during Mussaf. This is incorrect, for the Shulchan Aruch also holds this view, as indicated by his words, “therefore,” etc., and “for this reason,” etc. It is also explicit in the Rosh and the other poskim. The Magen Avraham’s explanation, “meaning,” etc., is untenable. First, what does “he should not precede,” etc., mean? Even together with them, he may not precede the chazzan’s mention. Second, what is meant by “and for this reason,” etc., “they began,” etc.? This implies immediately at the beginning of the prayer. The wording “he should announce” also supports this. Nor have I seen anyone who holds this opinion other than Rav Amram and the Geonim, and they refer to the chazzan’s repetition, as stated in seif 3 in the gloss. See Taz.
Therefore, etc. This is stated by the Rosh and Mordechai there in the name of Raavyah.
But, etc.; and for this reason, etc. The Yerushalmi there states: “They rose for prayer as though they had already mentioned it,” etc. It is cited there and elsewhere.
