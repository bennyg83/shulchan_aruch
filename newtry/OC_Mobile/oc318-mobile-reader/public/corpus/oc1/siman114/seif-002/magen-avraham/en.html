That the chazzan should announce. Meaning, the chazzan should say aloud, “Who causes the wind to blow,” during the prayer, or, according to the alternative reading, before the prayer. Rama holds that the shamash announces it before the prayer. Bach writes that according to Rama, even if the chazzan mentioned it silently, the congregation may not mention it unless it was first announced aloud. This is also implied by the gloss in seif 3. Beit Yosef states that even one praying at home may not mention it until the congregation has done so. It appears to me that one who lives where there is no minyan should wait until the time when the communities pray. See the end of siman 90, seif 9.
