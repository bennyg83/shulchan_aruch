The festival. It is not begun at Maariv because not everyone is in the synagogue, and not at Shacharis because an announcement is required, while at Shacharis this is impossible because redemption must be joined to prayer. See Devar Shmuel, siman 149. On the night of the final Yom Tov of Sukkos, if one mentioned rain instead of dew, he does not repeat the prayer. Gan HaMelech, siman 146.
