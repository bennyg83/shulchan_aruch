In the Mussaf prayer. They do not begin at Maariv because not everyone is in the synagogue, and separate groups would result, some mentioning rain and others not. They were not concerned about this regarding requesting rain because in that case not everyone begins on the same day in any event, as the time depends on the place. They do not begin at Shacharis because an announcement is required, and this cannot be made at Shacharis since redemption must be adjoined to prayer. See what I wrote at the beginning of siman 236. In some countries the custom is to mention rain on Hoshana Rabbah. This is improper. If, however, they announce on Hoshana Rabbah, “Who causes the wind to blow,” etc., so that they begin mentioning it on the night of Shemini Atzeres, there is some basis for the custom. Radbaz, part 1, siman 240.
