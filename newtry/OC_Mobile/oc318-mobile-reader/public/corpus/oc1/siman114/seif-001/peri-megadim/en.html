In the blessing — Taz in Tur and Bach. In Tur: this blessing was established on the four keys that are not handed to an agent: rain, livelihood, revival, and life, whose acronym is MeFaT"Ch — see Perishah "permits the forbidden" — hint to revival, that the fetus with the mother is forbidden and Heaven permits. And therefore they say in the word "hodu l'Heaven ki tov" all 18 [letters] of [Heaven's four-letter] name need to give thanks — it emerged from the house of the imprisoned — see there.
