Siman 61 in the prayer, etc. Thus agreed all the poskim, and as is written there 4:2; and Tosafos there s.v. "ha lan" etc. and s.v. "veha lehu" etc. that it refers to petition; and that which is written "and we who have two days" etc. — therefore it is according to all; and it is a textual error in Tosafos there s.v. "ha lan," and one must say there: he explained for the people of Babylonia they do not petition until the 7th of Marcheshvan, etc.; and Rashi who explains that it refers to mention — one should be careful in this, and he wrote that R' Yosi says it and not as the halachah; and it is not so, rather the halachah is like R' Yosi in all places; but one must investigate, for behold below at the beginning of siman 117 the Shulchan Aruch ruled that in Eretz Yisrael they begin to petition on the 7th of Marcheshvan, and this is unlike Tosafos and unlike Rashi who said in the Gemara there "now that you have come to this..." And see Rashi there s.v. "ela"; and see Levush ch. 2 s.v. "tefillah"; and one must investigate; and see Ran and what is written below there.
