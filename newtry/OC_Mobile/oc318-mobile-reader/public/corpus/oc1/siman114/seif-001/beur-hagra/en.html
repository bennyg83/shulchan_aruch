Seif 1, In the Mussaf prayer, etc. This is the consensus of all the poskim, as stated there, 4b, and according to Tosafos there, s.v. ha lan, etc., and s.v. veha lehu, etc., that it refers to requesting rain. The statement, “and we, who observe two days,” etc., must therefore follow all opinions. There is a scribal error in Tosafos there, s.v. ha lan, and it should read: “For the residents of Babylonia, they do not request rain until the seventh of Marcheshvan,” etc. Rashi, who explains that the passage refers to mentioning rain, was careful about this and wrote that it follows Rabbi Yehudah, but not the practical halachah. This is incorrect, however, since the halachah follows Rabbi Yehudah everywhere. Yet this requires study, for below, at the beginning of siman 117, the Shulchan Aruch rules that in Eretz Yisrael they begin requesting rain on the seventh of Marcheshvan. This accords neither with Tosafos nor with Rashi, since the Gemara there states, “Now that you have arrived at this conclusion,” etc. See Rashi there, s.v. ella, etc., and see Lechem Mishneh, chapter 2, s.v. tefillah. This requires study. See also Ran and what I write below there.
