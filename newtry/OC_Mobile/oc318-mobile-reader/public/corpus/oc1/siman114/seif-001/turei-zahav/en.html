In the second blessing, etc. Since it contains resurrection of the dead, and rains are life for the world.

In Musaf prayer, etc. It would have been fitting to begin immediately in the evening, since the festival days have ended during which rain is a curse because of the sukkah; except that not all the people are there then, for they are not present in the synagogue at that time — so one mentions and another does not mention, and they become factions upon factions. It would also have been fitting to mention in Shacharis; except that each one thinks they mentioned in the evening — meaning, he should think that they mentioned from the evening, and next year he will mention in the evening. And another reason why they do not begin in Shacharis: R. Chaggi bar Pedat said it is forbidden for an individual to mention until the shaliach tzibbur mentions — meaning, the shaliach tzibbur must say before the prayer "He Who makes the wind blow" out loud, and this is impossible in Shacharis because one must adjoin redemption to prayer — so too the Rosh, per the Yerushalmi.

And they do not stop, etc. The reason they do not stop in the evening and morning as at the beginning, but in Musaf there is no concern that perhaps he will think they stopped in the evening and come to a stumbling block the next year — for he has already heard that they did not stop in the morning.
