Also in Taz note 14 he was forced [to explain] in what Rama disagrees with the Mechaber, and it is simple from what the Yerushalmi says "until the Shaliach Tzibbur mentions" — the Mechaber explained Shaliach Tzibbur literally, who mentions before Musaf prayer in a whisper, and so it is in Maharil in the name of R' Chaim. And Rama based his custom on Mordechai that Shaliach Tzibbur is not precise but the Shammash announces.
