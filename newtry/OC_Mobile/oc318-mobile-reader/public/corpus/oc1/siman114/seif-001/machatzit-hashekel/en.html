Seif katan 1, In the Mussaf prayer. “But why,” etc., “at Maariv?” Meaning, although throughout Sukkos one may not mention rain because rain during the festival is an ominous sign, on Shemini Atzeres there is no longer any obligation to sit in the sukkah and rain is not an ominous sign. It should therefore be like all other insertions, such as Yaaleh VeYavo on Rosh Chodesh and the like, which begin at Maariv.
And they will form separate groups, etc. Bach concludes: “This is unlike the mentions for Shabbos, Yom Tov, and Rosh Chodesh. Even those who remain at home know and are proficient in mentioning what must be mentioned on each particular day, so no separate groups arise.”
“Why did they not have this concern regarding requesting rain?” Meaning, there they begin at Maariv and did not fear the formation of separate groups.
On one day. As stated at the beginning of siman 117, outside Eretz Yisrael they begin sixty days after the autumnal equinox, while in Eretz Yisrael they begin on the seventh of Marcheshvan.
Because an announcement is required, etc. This explanation works only according to Rama in seif 2. According to Beit Yosef as understood by Magen Avraham in seif katan 2, another reason is needed, as explained there.
See what I wrote at the beginning of siman 236. Meaning, one should not ask: if the reason for not beginning at Maariv is that not everyone is in the synagogue, why is the additional reason needed that one must adjoin redemption to prayer? The answer is that the beginning of siman 236 explains that an announcement needed for the prayer may be made between the blessing of redemption and the Maariv Amidah.
