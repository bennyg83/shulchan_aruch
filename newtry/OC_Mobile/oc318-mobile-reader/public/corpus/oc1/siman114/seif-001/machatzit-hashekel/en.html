(Endnote 1) In the Musaf prayer. And this, etc., in Maariv — meaning that on all the days of the festival one should not mention rain, for rain on a festival is a sign of a curse; nevertheless, in the Shulchan Aruch, where they need not sit in the sukkah and rain is not a sign of a curse, it should therefore be like all the other mentions, such as "Yaaleh veYavo" on Rosh Chodesh and the like, which they begin in Maariv.

"And they will make groups," etc. — and the Bach concluded: unlike mentioning Shabbat, Yom Tov, and Rosh Chodesh, for even those who remain in their homes know and are expert in what they need to mention — each day its matter — and there are no groups there — end of his words.

And the reason he was not concerned about this in the question — meaning that they begin in Maariv, and there is no concern for groups.

"On the first day" — as it is in siman 117: outside the Land they begin, etc., sixty days after the season; and in the Land of Israel, the seventh of Cheshvan.

"That one must announce," etc. — and this helps specifically per the Rama in seif 2; unlike the view of the Rabbeynu Yonah, per the understanding of Magen Avraham, endnote 2, there must be another reason, as written there.

And see siman 236. Meaning: do not be troubled by this — if so, why is another reason needed for why they do not begin in Maariv, since not everyone is in the synagogue? It should also have been the reason that one must adjoin redemption to prayer. For this, siman 236 was written, where it is explained that what the prayer requires, it is permitted to announce between the sections of Maariv.
