A second blessing does not open with "Baruch", because it is a blessing adjacent to its fellow, and likewise every blessing until the end of the prayer; and one begins "You are mighty forever, Adonai" — an acronym for the name Agla, and with this name a person should not meditate unless he is very pure from all impurity and in clean garments; and when Jacob blessed Judah he transmitted this name to him: "You shall be praised," "A lion's whelp," "The scepter shall not depart from Judah," "Binding his foal to the vine." And this name is explained by those versed in Kabbalah as hinted in the Torah, and the reader should take care that the gimmel be stressed (thus wrote the Tzioni in parashat Vayechi), and from this it follows that one should read "You are mighty" with the third [letter] stressed (Mateh Moshe).
