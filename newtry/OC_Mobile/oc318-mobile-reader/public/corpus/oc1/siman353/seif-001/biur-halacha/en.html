(ב) Permitted to throw — Magen Avraham wrote: it requires further study — behold they cannot make an eruv together unless there is an opening between them through which they can move objects together without throwing, as written siman 372 seif 4 — see there his answer; and in Korban Netanel and Maamar Mordechai they answered simply that it deals when there was like a bridge on one side from this house to that house through which they can move objects from one to the other:
