(א) And they made an eruv — for without an eruv behold it is forbidden to move objects from domain to domain even without reshus harabbim interrupting between them at all:
(ב) Level, etc. — meaning since they are level one need not steady his hand for the throw so the object not fall downward:
(ג) Permitted to throw — and specifically above ten, for then it is from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via makom patur; but below ten forbidden:
(ד) Higher than the other — since one is higher than the other he must restrict his hand in throwing and we are concerned perhaps he will not aim well and it will fall to the ground and he will come to bring it:
(ה) Karmelit, etc. — since the prohibition of karmelit is only d'rabbanan they did not decree lest it fall, for that would be a decree upon a decree:
(ו) In all cases — meaning even if one is higher than the other; but plainly specifically when they made an eruv:
