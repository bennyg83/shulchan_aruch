(א) From one to the other. And specifically above ten, for then it is from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via makom patur; but below ten it is forbidden. Rambam:

(ב) Forbidden to throw. Because of a rabbinic decree lest it roll and fall and he come to bring it; and even though in the next seif Rama wrote that regarding a wide thing there is no prohibition with all vessels — here it is different, for he throws from high to low or the reverse and must steady his hand, unlike there where he places.
