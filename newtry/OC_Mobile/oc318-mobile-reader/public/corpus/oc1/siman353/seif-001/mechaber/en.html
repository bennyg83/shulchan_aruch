The laws of zizin in reshus harabbim. And in it are 3 seifim:
Two houses on two sides of reshus harabbim that belong to one person or to two who made an eruv — if both are level, it is permitted to throw from one to the' other; and if one is higher than the other, it is forbidden to throw from one to the other unless they are clay vessels and the like that break if they fall.
