(א) And they made an eruv. And per the view that permits exchanging via makom patur it is permitted even if they did not make an eruv — see siman 355 seif 1 and seif 3 in the gloss: it requires further study — behold they cannot make an eruv together unless there is an opening between them through which they can move objects from one to the other without throwing, as written siman 372 seif 4 and siman 361 seif 5; and it must be said it deals when they stand at the edge of reshus harabbim and their side is adjacent to an alley fixed with a lechi and each also has an opening to the alley through which they can move objects from one to the other:

(ב) Permitted to throw. And specifically above ten, for then it is from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via makom patur (Rambam and Gemara); but below ten forbidden:
