(א) To reshus harabbim — he mentioned reshus harabbim for emphasis that even to reshus harabbim it is permitted, for reshus harabbim does not extend above ten; and all the more so if it projects to karmelit air it is permitted; and see seif 3 that there is a distinction between reshus harabbim and karmelit regarding placing upon it vessels that do not break in their fall:

(ב) Above ten — and needless to say if the window were open to it adjacent to the ziz within three tefachim — certainly permitted to use the ziz from within the window to place upon it and take from it, for it is in the category of chorei reshut ha-yachid and is like reshut ha-yachid; but even if the window were far from it, nevertheless permitted to use the ziz, for whatever is above ten we consider makom patur; and it is implied from Mechaber's conclusion that if the ziz were below ten, even if the house window were open adjacent within three, nevertheless forbidden to use the ziz — the reason: since it is within ten, the people of reshus harabbim also comfortably use the ziz and it is not in chorei reshut ha-yachid but in karmelit, and forbidden to use it to place and take whether from reshus harabbim or from within the window:

(ג) And it has four by four — according to what Mechaber ruled seif 3 that with vessels that do not break it is forbidden lest they fall and he come to bring from reshus harabbim — perforce here it deals with vessels that break by falling; if so even if the ziz has no four by four, since they are likely to fall from it, nevertheless permitted to use it; and one may say with difficulty that he mentioned four by four because he concludes "and the window is open to it" — when the ziz has no four by four an open window is not required, for it is makom patur and in all cases permitted to use it:

(ד) And they belong, etc. — but of one person it is permitted since they are one domain; and if they made an eruv together, even of two people it is permitted [Peri Megadim]:

(ה) Forbidden to use — specifically vessels that rested inside the house; but if they rested at bein hashemashos on the ziz, permitted to move from ziz to ziz:

(ו) One upon the other — meaning between the upper and the lower; and Levush is lenient regarding the lower. Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Maggid Mishneh that even if the lower has no four they also forbid one upon the other; and in Ateret Rokeach he also wrote thus in Avodat HaKodesh:

(ז) Opposite his window — alone, which is like the corner of the window and its extension:

(ח) That made a domain unto itself — and automatically forbidden to use also the lower ziz since they forbid one upon the other as above; and per the aforementioned Levush here certainly permitted regarding the lower:
