2) There. Who established an eruv. This discusses a case in which there are structures resembling porticoes and upper stories above the walls of the houses, or in which firmly fixed boards are laid so that people can pass from one to the other through entrances that open onto them, and they established an eruv by way of that area. Nevertheless, an actual public domain separates between them. Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 1. He writes that the Magen Avraham's interpretation is strained and that the Eliyah Rabbah's interpretation is incorrect. See there. Rav Nesiv Chaim writes an interpretation similar to that of the Ma'amar Mordechai. See there.
