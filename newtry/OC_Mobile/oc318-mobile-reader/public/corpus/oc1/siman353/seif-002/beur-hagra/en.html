(א) To reshus harabbim. To exclude karmelit as written in seif 3:

(ב) Above ten. So too Rambam — below ten it is karmelit; but above ten it is reshut ha-yachid, unlike what Rosh wrote and Shulchan Aruch siman 345 seif 16; and even though Tur chapter 345 also holds like Tosafos that open holes below ten are chorei reshus harabbim — unlike the gloss siman 345 seif 4 — nevertheless according to Rama it requires further study; and Magen Avraham:

(ג) And it has four, etc. So Rashi wrote according to his approach and Tosafos according to theirs; and Tur also according to Tosafos that even with vessels that do not break it is permitted — see there; but according to Rambam there is no distinction between four or not, as written below and in seif 3, except regarding two zizin — here it is not needed; and below he explained Rambam; and it requires further study; and one may say because of an open window — if no four an open window is not needed, for then it should have said "and even if there is," etc.:

(ד) And a window. So Tur wrote according to his approach, as written siman 345 seif 16 — see there:

(ה) If there were. As Rambam's version — the reverse: if there is not, etc., on the entire wall to use; and if there is, etc., even opposite his window, etc.; and Rashba explained regarding two zizin of one person; and Rambam holds that in one of them having four, the upper is forbidden:
