(א) And the house window is open to it — see Biur HaGra that so Tur wrote according to his approach siman 345 — Taz and Peri Megadim; but according to Rambam and the rest of the poskim he brought at the end of the siman, even if the ziz is not opposite the window, since it is above ten tefachim it is not in the category of karmelit but in makom patur, and automatically permitted to place upon it from reshus harabbim as well, as stated in Yerushalmi brought in Tosafos Eruvin chapter 9 page 2 — see there:

(ב) And the upper has no four — it appears to me he means it has no four on its own but combined with the window it has four; therefore forbidden on the two sides of the window like any ziz that has four; and what is permitted opposite his window is because it is like chorei halon — if it has no four even combined with the window permitted to use the entire wall; so explicitly in the Gemara in Abaye's answer; and so implied in Maggid Mishneh the version was not changed according to Rambam except in the Gemara's question — how is it, etc. — see there; but in Abaye's answer nothing was changed:
