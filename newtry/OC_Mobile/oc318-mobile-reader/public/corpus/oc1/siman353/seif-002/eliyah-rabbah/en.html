I examined Shabbos daf 97 and saw there is no proof at all, for what it says above ten is because for R' Akiva he is liable below ten for kaltah k'mi shehunchah dami; but for the Rabbis who exempt, one may say permitted also below when they made an eruv or when they belong to one person; and Rambam chapter 15 — one may say he also mentioned above ten which is for all, or alternatively to include that even above ten forbidden when no eruv when one is higher; and proof for this that Rambam opened "threw from one to the other above ten exempt" and concluded "and he made an eruv, and even garments and metal vessels permitted to throw" — examine; and see siman 354 s.k. 4; but R' Yonatan chapter HaMotzi mishnah somewhat and from Malbo daf 29 in the name of Ba'al HaShleimah somewhat implies forbidden — and one may dismiss:
