A ziz — meaning a board projecting from the wall to reshus harabbim — above ten [tefachim], and it' is four by four, and the house window is open to it — one may use it. If there were two zizin one below the other and they belong to two people, even though both are above ten, if the upper ziz before the window has a width of four' by four, it is forbidden to use it because it is a domain unto itself and the ziz below it is another domain, and they forbid one upon the other; and if the upper has no four and the lower also has no four, one may use both and the entire wall up to the lower ten tefachim. If the lower had four and the upper has no four, one does not use the upper except opposite his window alone; but regarding the rest of the ziz on the two sides of the window it is forbidden to use because of that which is below it, which made a domain unto itself:
