Every ziz that projects into the air of reshus harabbim that one is permitted to use — when he uses it, one may not place upon it nor take from it except clay vessels and glass and the like that if they fall to reshus harabbim they break; but all other vessels are forbidden lest they fall to reshus harabbim and he bring them:And specifically a ziz and the like; but if it is a wide thing such as a roof where the user stands there with the vessel, it is permitted to use all vessels (Beit Yosef in the name of Ritva)} And if there was a single ziz alone projecting into the air of karmelit — as long as it is ten high always, it is permitted whether wide or narrow, and even vessels that do not break, for they did not decree regarding karmelit:) ואם היה זיז אחד לבדו יוצא על אויר כרמלית כל שהוא גבוה י' לעולם מותר בין רחב בין קצר ואפילו כלים שאין משתברין שלא גזרו בכרמלית:
