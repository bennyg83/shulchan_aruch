(א) And specifically a ziz, etc. — for its default is not wide and the user does not stand there with the vessel; therefore the Sages did not distinguish that even if the ziz were wide it would also be forbidden; and regarding a roof likewise they did not distinguish that even if the width were only four tefachim it is permitted to use all types of vessels — so proven in Ritva who is in Beit Yosef:

(ב) Whether wide or narrow — according to what Mechaber ruled initially, also projecting into reshus harabbim air it is permitted whether wide or narrow; and the main novelty is that here it is permitted even with vessels that do not break.
