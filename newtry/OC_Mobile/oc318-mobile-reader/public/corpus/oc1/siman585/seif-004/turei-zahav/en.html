Another blows. So the Tur brought in the name of Rabbeinu Hai from that which we learned [in the Mishnah]: “the second one blows” (meaning the chazzan who is praying Musaf has the blasts blown) meaning that another blows at the direction of the chazzan who says the blessing. And the Beis Yosef explained that according to Rabbeinu Hai the blasts while seated are the primary ones, etc. he forced himself for nothing; for what the Tur wrote, “and Rabbeinu Hai inferred,” is a separate matter and is not connected to the preceding [point] that he said that the blasts while standing are primary; and the proof for this is from the fact that they arranged the blasts for the second one who prays Musaf.
Confident. The Maharshal wrote: according to the Maharam regarding kohanim, even if he is confident we do not rely on him where there is another; here likewise regarding blasting (end quote). And if so, being confident is of no help except where there is no other; and if so, if he is not confident he should not blow at all, even if there is no other. And it does not appear so to me, in my humble opinion: how should they cancel the blasts entirely because of a concern of confusion and muddling in the prayer, which is only a mere concern? Specifically regarding nesias kapayim, which is not obligatory if they did not call him, as is written in siman 128, we say so; which is not so for shofar, if there is no other, he should blow even where he is not confident; rather we say that Heaven will assist him to do [it] properly, for one mitzvah leads to another mitzvah, as it appears to me.
For one who begins, etc. And the custom in many communities is to divide them, and even among the blasts of the sequences themselves; and one may judge them favorably according to what the Tur wrote in the name of the Baal HaItur: it stands to reason that if the chazzan knows how to blow, it is in his hands [to do so]; rather, it was the custom of the early ones, who were eager for mitzvos and cherished them, that each one takes his share in order to receive reward (end quote).
