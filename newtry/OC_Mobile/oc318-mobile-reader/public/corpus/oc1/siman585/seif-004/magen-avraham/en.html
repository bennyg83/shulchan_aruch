And if he is confident. And it appears to me that if he is not confident, even though there is no one else there, he should not blow until after the prayer (see simanim 592 and 593); and all the more so since he has already fulfilled [his obligation] with the blasts while seated; for even nesias kapayim they cancel when he is not confident, all the more so this, as it appears to me. And nevertheless, when he prays from the siddur he is certainly confident; see what I wrote above.
May blow. And it implies in the Tur in the name of Baal HaTurim that even if there is someone else there; and the Bach wrote in the name of the Maharshal that according to what I wrote above in siman 128, se'if 20, that if there is another he should not ascend [for nesias kapayim], here likewise (see what I wrote there). And furthermore it appears to me that the Tur resolved this in his wording when he wrote that blasting certainly is not an interruption, that is to say, since one must blow in the order of the blessings, it is literally a need of the prayer; which is not so regarding Birkas Kohanim, which is not so much of the nature of the prayer; therefore we do not permit him except when there is no [other] kohen.
To call out. The one who prays Shacharis calls out for the baal tokea (Darkei Moshe). Also the first blast he should call out before him (Shlah); see siman 128, se'if 13. Once they were unable to blow, and he turned the shofar over and recited “Vihi Noam” into it, and they were then able to blow (Minhagei Maharil). And it is good that the baal tokea know how to intend the secrets; and if not, then at least the one who calls out should know, and when he calls out he should intend the secrets (Shlah).
