To his prayer. It appears to me that if he is not confident [that he will not become confused], even though there is no one else there, he should not blow until after the prayer, for he has already fulfilled [his obligation] with the blasts while seated (Magen Avraham). And the Taz wrote that if there is no one else, it is permitted to blow, for Heaven will assist him to do [it] properly, for one mitzvah leads to another mitzvah. And the Magen Avraham wrote: nevertheless, when they pray from the siddur, he is certainly confident; and see siman 128, se'if 20.
Finish. And the custom in many places is to divide them, even among the blasts of the [Malchuyos, Zichronos, and Shofaros] sequences themselves.
To call out. The one who prays Shacharis calls out [the blasts] for the baal tokea (Darkei Moshe). Also the first blast he should call out before him (Shlah); see siman 128, se'if 13. Once they were unable to blow, and he turned the shofar over and recited “Vihi Noam” into it, and they were then able to blow (Minhagei Maharil). And it is good that the baal tokea know how to intend the [kabbalistic] secrets; and if not, then at least the one who calls out should know, and when he calls out he should intend the secrets (Shlah) (see there).
