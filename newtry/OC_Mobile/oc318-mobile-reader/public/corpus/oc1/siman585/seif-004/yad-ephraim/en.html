In Magen Avraham, s.k. 5. And in the Levush he wrote, that is to say: since he wrote “a little,” therefore it does not mean regarding the position of the mouth [opening sideways], for if so “a little” would not apply, for it would still also be standing to the sides; therefore he explained that he should raise the mouth a little upward, that is to say, that the shofar should be raised slightly diagonally upward, in such a way that the mouth of the shofar on the wide side is above the mouth of the baal tokea.
