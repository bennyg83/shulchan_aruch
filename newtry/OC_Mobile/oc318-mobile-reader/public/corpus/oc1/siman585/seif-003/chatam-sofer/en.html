(There in Taz, s.k. 7) And this requires further study regarding the Beis Yosef. Note: and in my humble opinion there is nothing difficult; for there regarding one who sells a Sefer Torah, in tractate Megillah folio 26a, it says “he never sees a sign of blessing,” implying that in all his dealings he will be punished, so the Beis Yosef wrote well that we infer that there is a prohibition. But here it is only that he does not see a sign of blessing from that Shabbos wage; one may say that there is no prohibition. And at the beginning of the chapter “A place where they were accustomed,” where we say four perutot never have a sign of blessing, there it refers to the perutah forever (its exchange and the exchange of its exchange) but not to the person regarding the rest of his property.
