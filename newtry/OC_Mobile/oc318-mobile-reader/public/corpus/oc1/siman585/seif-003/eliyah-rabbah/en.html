[3] [Levush:] And we learn this “lachem,” etc. So wrote the Tur and the Semak. And as for that which is written, “Sit here (shevu lachem) with the donkey” “sit” there is a language of delay (Derishah). And in the Responsa of the Geonim I found that we learn [standing] from that which is written, “And you shall blow with trumpets,” and it is [tradition] that trumpets [are blown] while standing; and furthermore we learn from “And when you go to war” until “And you shall blow with trumpets,” and that blasting is while standing; and so wrote the Shibbolei HaLeket and the Tanya; and in the Tanya he added a reason: that the shofar advocates for us in judgment, a remembrance of the binding of Yitzchak, and we hold that litigants [stand] while standing; and therefore the congregation does not stand as litigants, for the honor of the congregation is different, or because their agent is like themselves. And according to the reason of litigants, it implies that after the fact, even while sitting one has fulfilled [the obligation], as the Sema wrote in siman 17; and according to the reason of “lachem” while standing, likewise, as is explained in siman 489; and according to the reason from the trumpets, this requires further study. However, in the Rokeach, siman 203, he ruled explicitly that one has fulfilled [the obligation].
