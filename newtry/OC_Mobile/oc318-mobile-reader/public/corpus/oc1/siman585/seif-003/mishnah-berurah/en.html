(10) And the blessing suffices, etc., For the first one recited the blessing to discharge the entire congregation thereby.
(11) And [this is] provided that they be there, etc., For if they were not there at that time, they must recite the blessing for themselves in a whisper before they begin to blow; but if he has already fulfilled his obligation, he blows without a blessing, since the congregation already heard the blessing from the first.
(12) And it is not a blessing in vain, since the second one fulfills [his obligation] with his blessing.
