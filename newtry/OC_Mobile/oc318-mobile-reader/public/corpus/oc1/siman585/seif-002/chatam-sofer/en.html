In the Mechaber, se'if 3: “And [this is] provided that [they] be there.” Note: meaning, to exclude [the case] where they heard the blessings and half the blasts in another congregation and were summoned here to complete what this one lacked, then the second baal tokea who completes must recite the blessing himself on the half of the blasts that he blows now; even though he already heard the blessing in the first congregation, nevertheless walking from the synagogue constitutes an interruption (so it appears to me according to the view of the Shulchan Aruch. However, the Magen Avraham does not hold so below in siman 589, s.k. 7) see there; and see Eliyah Rabbah here.
