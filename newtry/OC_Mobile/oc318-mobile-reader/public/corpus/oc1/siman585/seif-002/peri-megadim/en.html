To hear the sound of the shofar. See the Taz: one who blows into a pit; the baal tokea himself has not fulfilled [his obligation], for he heard an echo when he is outside the pit and the shofar is inside the pit; and the main [requirement] is hearing (see Beis Yosef); and the Perishah elaborates; and with God’s help in siman 587 it will be explained whether, if the baal tokea is outside and the shofar is inside, those standing inside have fulfilled [their obligation]. One should not say “be-kol shofar,” which implies that Hashem commanded us to obey the voice of the shofar [regarding] whatever it may command, and that is not so; rather we were commanded to hear the sound of a shofar. And if after the fact he recited [the blessing] “be-kol shofar” or “to blow the shofar” see Magen Avraham letter 2: it implies apparently that for “to blow” he has fulfilled [his obligation], for “be-kol” he has not; and see Peri Chadash, who wrote that for “to blow” he has not fulfilled [his obligation], for that is not the completion of the mitzvah (see there); and this requires further study. And a doubt regarding blessings should be treated leniently; and with God’s help in the Magen Avraham it will be further explained.
