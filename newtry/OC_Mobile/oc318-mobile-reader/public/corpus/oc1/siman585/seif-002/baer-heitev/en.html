The Magen Avraham concluded that this custom is specifically for women who do not know [how to recite the blessings]; but one who knows should recite the blessings himself and thereby fulfill [his obligation] according to all opinions (see there); and so it is in Kenesses HaGedolah. And see Yad Aharon: if he is in doubt whether he heard the blast or took the lulav on the first day, he blows and does not recite a blessing, and on the second day he need not blow (Kenesses HaGedolah; Rivash).
