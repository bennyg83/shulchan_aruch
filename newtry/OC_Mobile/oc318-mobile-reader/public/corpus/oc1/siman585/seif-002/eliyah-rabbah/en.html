[2] [Levush:] For it is not, etc. And so wrote the Radbaz, siman 25; and he wrote further that the baal tokea must stand, as is the law of the mitzvah, which is while standing (end quote). And he means to say: even though this is not the [primary] mitzvah of blasting, nevertheless, since we find that for the mitzvah blasts standing is required, they required it in this as well; only for the congregation they did not trouble [them]. I wrote this to exclude [the reading of] Kenesses HaGedolah, who wondered at the Radbaz, and the Magen Avraham followed him; and what I explained is clear.
