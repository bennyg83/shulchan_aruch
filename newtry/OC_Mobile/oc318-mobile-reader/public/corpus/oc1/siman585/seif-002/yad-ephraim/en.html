There, s.k. 1. “And also the hearer,” etc., that is to say, the reason they do not stand for the first blasts is because they are going to blow in the order of the blessings, which are the communal blasts; but [if] he did not hear the blasts of the order of the blessings, and now they are blowing for him to discharge his obligation, he must stand (so wrote the Radbaz). And Kenesses HaGedolah wrote on this: I do not know from where this [comes], that the hearer should be obligated to stand; for if because we learn “lachem” from the omer, that is for the baal tokea himself, but for the hearer, from where? (see there in Kenesses HaGedolah); and [regarding] the end of his words and what is written there, “and if they are not primary, there would be no obligation regarding them,” etc. (it is somewhat unclear) see there.
