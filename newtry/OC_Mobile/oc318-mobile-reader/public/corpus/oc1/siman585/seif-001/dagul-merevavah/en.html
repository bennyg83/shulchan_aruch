The Beis Yosef is dealing also with the reading of the Megillah and likewise with other mitzvos regarding which the Magen Avraham wrote that his opinion is that the listener should recite the blessing; and upon this the Beis Yosef wrote that the custom is not so; and upon this the Magen Avraham wrote that this custom applies to women, and [his remark] refers to the reading of the Megillah and other mitzvos, and not to shofar. And see above, siman 559, se'if 6 in the gloss.
