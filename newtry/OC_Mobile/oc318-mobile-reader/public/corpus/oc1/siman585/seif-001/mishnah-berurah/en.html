(1) To blow. And likewise the blessing must be while standing, like every blessing over the mitzvos, and as above in siman 8, se'if 1 [later authorities].
(2) While standing, For it is written, “A day of teruah it shall be for you (lachem),” and we learn from “lachem” stated regarding the omer; and regarding the omer it is written, “a sickle upon the standing grain (kama)” do not read “bekamah” but “bekomah” [while standing]. And the Magen Avraham wrote that according to this one should not lean on anything in such a way that if that thing were removed he would fall, for that standing is considered sitting; and nevertheless after the fact, even if he blew while entirely seated, he has fulfilled [his obligation], for the verse is a mere support, and [the requirement is] ideally. And the congregation hearing the blasts they did not trouble to stand, because of the honor of the congregation, and also because they are going to hear while standing the blasts in the order of the blessings of Malchuyos, Zichronos, etc. And nowadays the congregation has practiced that all stand also at the time of the blasts blown before Musaf; and even so they are called “blasts while seated,” since permission is given to sit for them. And if an individual hears blasts to fulfill [his obligation] with them and is not going to hear [blasts] in the order of the blessings, by law he must ideally stand.
(3) In the place where they read, So that the merit of the Torah protect us, that our remembrance ascend before Him for good. And it is fitting to give [the opportunity] to blow to a righteous and worthy person, provided that there be no dispute in the matter; and in any case he must know how to intend to discharge the congregation with his blasting.
