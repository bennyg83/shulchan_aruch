While standing. See Baer Heitev; and see in the Magen Avraham in the name of the Radbaz, “and also the hearer,” etc., that is to say, if someone did not hear the blasts in the order of the blessings and now hears those blasts from a person blowing to discharge the obligation, he must stand. And what the Magen Avraham wrote, that Kenesses HaGedolah wrote “and I do not know from where” and see Birkei Yosef and Machazik Beracha, that Kenesses HaGedolah is not referring to this matter; rather it is difficult for him that if the hearer is like the baal tokea, why for the seated blasts does the baal tokea stand and the congregation sit; either all should stand or all should sit. And see Machazik Beracha, what he wrote in the name of the Radbaz, part 1, siman 25: for the honor of the congregation they did not wish to trouble them, since they are going to hear the standing blasts; and they required the baal tokea to stand as is the law of the mitzvah, which is while standing; alternatively, because of the honor of the congregation (end quote; and with this everything is resolved, etc.) see there. And see Eliyah Rabbah, what he wrote on this. And see in the Magen Avraham that the baal tokea should not lean on anything such that if that thing were removed he would fall; and see above, siman 501 and siman 502.
