While standing. See the Taz; Beis Yosef in the name of the Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of the Semak; and see siman 8 regarding tzitzis, and the blessings over the mitzvos are all [said] while standing; and as for the mitzvos themselves, this requires further study; and see Eliyah Rabbah in siman 8, and the Bach and Olas Tamid on this. One who is holding the infant: it is impossible to circumcise because of the blessing; and in Yoreh De’ah at the beginning of siman 265, se'if 1 [regarding] the blessing “to bring him into [the covenant],” and peri’ah; he fulfills [his obligation] with the first blessing, and he must stand; and if he was not in the synagogue, this requires further study.
