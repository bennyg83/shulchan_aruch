[5] [Levush] only through hearing, etc. One should wonder — for in gemara Berachot ch. 2 it is proven that all agree for other mitzvot even if he did not hear in his ears he fulfilled; they disagree only in Keriat Shema because it is written shema — see there. The difficulty is not from Rashi who explained there daf 16 that for other mitzvot it is like all berakhot — meaning specifically berakhot; but this mitzva depends on hearing, therefore we require hearing in his ears. Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Knesset HaGedolah and Rivash: if he is in doubt whether he heard tekiah or took lulav on the first day, he blows and does not bless, and on the second day he need not blow — end quote. One may ask: Knesset HaGedolah wrote in Rivash siman 221 it appears clear he must bless. I again looked in Rivash itself — it is clear from his conclusion he need not bless, for although it is safek d'oraisa, berakhot are d'rabbanan; he also only wrote on the second day one need not take lulav, but to blow without blessing on the second day perhaps he must, since it is a long day; and Knesset HaGedolah also wrote if he wishes he may take lulav:
