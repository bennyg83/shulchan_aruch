In the Shulchan Aruch: One who takes wages, etc. And see in siman 55 regarding whether the congregation must pay the chazzan (see there, se'if 23); and the same law applies to a baal tokea and the like. And see Birkei Yosef here in the name of Sefer Bnei Yehudah: in a village that has thirteen householders and one must give much wages to bring a baal tokea from afar, and six wish [to] and seven prevent; one should not say regarding this “we follow the majority” and the minority coerce those who prevent; and if those who prevent, their reason is because they are poor, one does not coerce, for the verse’s words can be [read] “and the six shall give all that is needed” (see there; and not like Chavos Yair, siman 186, who leaned toward this) see there. And see in Chavos Yair there, who wrote that it depends on whether [it is] a majority of householders or a majority of the poor: even if some of them consent to spend, the law is with those who prevent, for we follow liability and exemption according to the law; and even though they collect according to means, nevertheless a poor person is not obligated to spend even a little more than is proper, etc. (see there). And there he wrote that if the six who consented say that if their fellows do not consent they will give with him so that only they will fulfill [the obligation], if they stipulate so their condition stands, for they are not obligated to provide for their fellows; and if the baal tokea intends to discharge only these and not those, certainly [those] have not fulfilled [their obligation]; and if he transgresses the condition and intends to discharge all who hear, it appears to me that all who hear have not fulfilled [their obligation], for the baal tokea is called an agent like the chazzan, and an agent who changed from his sender’s intent (his agency is void, etc.) see there. And I wondered greatly: what relevance is there of a baal tokea to agency? For one need not blow [as agent] except that he himself [hears], as is said that his agent is like himself and it is as if he blew himself; rather, the mitzvah upon him is to hear, and there is no difference whether they made him an agent to blow for him or not, only that he intend to discharge him; and like a chazzan, whose intent is upon everyone, and even though he was not made their agent to blow, nevertheless since he intends to discharge and they intend to fulfill [their obligation], if so, what does it matter that they stipulated with him not to discharge these? And cancellation of agency has no place here at all, for we do not come to it from the law of agency; and I have abbreviated. And see there that he wrote: if those who bless acted according to the law (that is to say, the matter was as he wrote above that they may prevent); those who stipulated, stipulated not according to the law; if they still wish to contribute according to their ability and the proper wage in a year of peace, when one need not disperse so much (which is not so if those who refuse [do so] not according to the law) see there (that is to say, as he wrote above that the majority of householders, etc. and from the words of Bnei Yehudah it appears he understood “if those who refuse” as that he remains in doubt, with faces both ways (and that is not so). And one should not say that the poor may coerce the householders under the trait of Sodom) this one benefits and that one lacks nothing, and they may not stipulate; there is room to distinguish; and also for the betterment of the world one should not do so, for otherwise many and many would withdraw from the congregation regarding hiring the chazzan and the like, and rely that God-fearers will hire and they will fulfill [their obligation] thereby (see there).
