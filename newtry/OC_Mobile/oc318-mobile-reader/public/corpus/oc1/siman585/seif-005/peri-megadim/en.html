Confident. See the Taz; in [siman] 128, se'if 20, he ruled that if he is not confident, even where there is another, [he should] also not [ascend]; and here, where there is no other, even though he is not confident, he should blow in the order of the blessings; and see Magen Avraham letter 9; and some say that if he is not confident he should blow after the prayer (see there); and with God’s help it will be explained.
