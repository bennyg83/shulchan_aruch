(א) Like half. Magen Avraham ruled it is slightly less than half an egg — see there; likewise Peri Chadash ruled. And see in Kaf HaChayyim and at the end of responsum Chut HaShani: one should be uncertain if one had only slightly less than a kezayit of maror and soaked it in water and it swelled to a kezayit, whether he fulfills with that kezayit on Yom Tov or not. See Yad Ephraim.

(ב) In the air. The same applies if there is a hollow in matzah — it does not combine, per the end of Okatzin. Magen Avraham.
