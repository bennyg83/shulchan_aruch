In the Rama gloss "one must compress the hollow" — see Tosafot Yoma ch. 3 mishnah 4 s.v. teme'im etc., and see well in Rambam ch. 4 of Sha'ar Avot HaTumah halacha 13 — see there — that swelling by rain is only d'rabbanan in prohibitions and tumah, and all the more so regarding fulfillment of a mitzvah that is not biblically obligatory.
