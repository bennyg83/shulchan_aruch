(א) [Seif 1] The measure of a kezayit — some say it is like half an egg. So too Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot Chullin 103b, and so after Eruvin 83b, and so in Peskei Tosafot Chullin s.v. ervav, and so Mordechai ch. "Ataf" in the Passover order, and so Terumat HaDeshen siman 139 and Pesakim uKhetavim siman 26, and so in derashot Maharil seder Hagadah, and so Mahariw siman 123. That which he wrote this in Shulchan Aruch in the name of "some say" is because this is not agreed, as explained there in Tosafot that some hold a kezayit is less than a third of an egg, and so in Magen Avraham, Peri Chadash, and other Acharonim; Shulchan Aruch adopted half an egg stringently to fulfill according to all. So Knesset HaGedolah seif 1: for halacha one should not be lenient in obligation such as kezayit matzah and maror, or for Birkat HaMazon until he eats specifically half an egg — see there; and Eshel Avraham. Rabbi Zalman seif 1 wrote: for maror which is d'rabbanan, and anything d'rabbanan measured by kezayit, one may rely on those who say slightly less than a third of an egg; but matzah which is d'oraisa and anything d'oraisa — be stringent per the first view; for Birkat HaMazon and Birkat HaMazon whose measure is kezayit — safeik berachot lehakel, do not bless until he ate like half an egg; l'chatchila avoid safeik berachot — eat like half an egg or much less than a third where kezayit is not required; be precise where large measure is leniency and small is stringency, e.g. biur chametz — end quote. That which he wrote for maror d'rabbanan one may rely on lenient — appears for b'dieved if he lacks half an egg or cannot eat more than a third; but for berachah on maror do not bless on less than half an egg — kavod Shabbat safeik berachot lehakel; since many hold kezayit is half an egg, how bless "Who sanctified us" etc.; we wrote above siman 468 seif 46: per Rambam and many poskim egg measure is 18 Egyptian drachma; for eating — kezayit matzah Passover night, maror, Birkat HaMazon — all measured by weight: egg 18 drachma, kezayit half egg nine drachma — see there in many poskim; custom fulfills 18 mitzvot lekhol ha'amim and because of safeik berachot.
