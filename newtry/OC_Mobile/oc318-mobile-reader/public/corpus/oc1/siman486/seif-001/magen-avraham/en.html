In Kerisos folio 14 it is stated that a koseves is two olives, and in siman 612 it is stated that a koseves is less than a kebeitzah; if so, a kezayis is slightly less than half an egg. And so it is according to what he wrote in siman 368, se'if 3: a gerogeret is a third of an egg, and so wrote the Rambam; and according to another opinion it is four-ninths of an egg. And we say in chapter HaMatznia that a gerogeret is larger than an olive; see there. If so, an olive is in any case less than half an egg; rather, he was not precise, and for stringency he was not precise; but regarding other matters one must be precise in the matter. However, it is difficult: for we say there in Kerisos that the throat does not hold more than two olives, and elsewhere we say that the throat does not hold more than a kebeitzah; and it appears to me that this is what it means: it does not hold three olives. However, according to the Rambam it is difficult, for three olives are not a kebeitzah; and one must say that that [statement] in chapter HaMatznia deals with a small olive, and we require a medium olive, as Beis Yosef wrote in siman 196 in the name of the Rokeach. To compress a hollow. And the same applies if there is a hollow in matzah: it does not combine, according to [mishnah] 2 of Uktzin.
