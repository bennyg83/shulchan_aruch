In Keritot 14a it teaches that a kotevet is two olives, and in siman 472 it teaches that a kotevet is less than an egg; if so a kezayit is slightly less than half an egg, and so per what is written siman 468 seif 3 a dried fig is a third of an egg, and so Rambam; and for a first view it is four-ninths of an egg. And they say in the chapter Hametzui that a dried fig is larger than an olive — see there; if so an olive is at least less than half an egg, only not precisely; and stringently not precisely, but for other matters one must be precise. However it is difficult, for we say in Keritot there the swallowing organ does not hold more than two olives, and everywhere we say the swallowing organ does not hold more than an egg — and it appears to me this means three olives it does not hold; however for Rambam it is difficult that three olives are not like an egg, and one must say that matter in Hametzui deals with a small olive and we require a medium olive, as Maharal wrote siman 496 in the name of Rokeach: compress the hollow. And the same if there is a hollow in matzah it does not combine, per the end of Okatzin.
