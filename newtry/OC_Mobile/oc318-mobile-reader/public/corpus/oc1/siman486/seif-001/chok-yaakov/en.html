And the difficulty that Magen Avraham raised: Tosafos themselves already sensed there that very difficulty; and see Tosafos Yom Tov, chapter 6 of Keilim, and at the end of the responsum of Chut HaShani in Kuntres HaCheshbon, and in Mahari Ben Lev, siman 27, and in She'eiris Yehudah in his glosses on Terumat HaDeshen; and see Beis Hillel on Even HaEzer, siman 70.
And they should eat together. The Tur wrote this law in brief order in [his] siman, and what the later authorities wrote on this was explained above in its simanim, each one in its place. And he wrote in Piskei Mahari, siman 245, that even if he ate the entire creature, namely one stalk with its leaves, he does not fulfill [his obligation] if there is not a kezayis in it, since the creature is incomplete in that he does not eat the roots that are fit to eat; end quote.
