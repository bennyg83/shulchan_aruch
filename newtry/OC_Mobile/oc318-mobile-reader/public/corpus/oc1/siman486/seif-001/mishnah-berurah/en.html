(א) That it is like half an egg — medium with its shell; and Rambam's view is a kezayit is a third of an egg — see Magen Avraham and Biur HaGra what they wrote on this. And for practical halacha in a d'oraisa mitzvah such as eating matzah one should be stringent and eat at least like half an egg [unless he is sick and it is hard for him to eat like half an egg, then he may rely on Rambam]. And for maror and the like which are d'rabbanan one may rely b'dieved if he ate like a third of an egg; but l'chatchila, since he must bless on maror, it appears one should not be lenient unless he is a weak person who has difficulty — then he may rely and eat only like a third, and so on. And for Birkat HaMazon whose measure is d'rabbanan in a kezayit [and likewise for Birkat HaMazon] safeik berachot lehakel and he should not bless until he knows he ate like half an egg. And l'chatchila it is fit to beware not to bring himself to safeik berachot and eat like half an egg or much less than a third of an egg in a matter that does not require kezayit [Peri Megadim]. And in my humble opinion it appears fit l'chatchila that he eat like an egg of bread, for otherwise there is concern of a berachah levatalah on the berachah he blessed initially on netilat yadayim per some poskim — and so in siman 488 seif 2 — see there. And now we return to our topic: know that what the Mechaber wrote kezayit like half an egg is not a fixed matter in our time, for some Acharonim proved that eggs found in our time have shrunk greatly, up to half compared to ancient days when the Sages measured; therefore wherever the measure is like half an egg one must measure by an egg in our time. And see in Sha'arei Teshuvah who ruled one should distinguish in measures between a matter whose obligation is from the Torah and a matter whose obligation is d'rabbanan, such as maror nowadays, afikoman, and cups — and it is found that in our time one is obligated to eat matzah up to the measure of an egg, and maror one fulfills b'dieved even if he ate only like a third of an egg, and so on; and see what is written above siman 471 in Biur Halacha s.v. shel revi'it.
(ב) And one should eat it together — see above siman 475 in Mishna Berurah end of s.k. 9.
(ג) And for vegetables one must compress the air space — and the same if there is a hollow in matzah the hollow does not combine toward the measure of kezayit and one must compress it; but if there is no hollow in matzah, even if it is soft and made like a sponge, one need not compress it.
