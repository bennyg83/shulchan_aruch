(ב) In Tur it is written etc. — see Taz. The laws of the Seder briefly — see Levush; washing the cup — see siman 471 seif 10 and 483, and Beit Meir siman 129 from Shabbat halacha 7 as Magen Avraham wrote Berachot 51a: a cup of blessing — rinse outside and wash inside; and Bach siman 475: on this night even a clean person should rinse and wash — see there; and siman 474: also cups 2 and 4 are included in a cup of blessing and require rinsing; and if they bring him a new cup for cups 2 and 4 he must etc. — see siman 483: on Passover night if he has only one cup that was not dipped, one can say it is permitted to dip in foul water at night for the mitzvah of four cups; and it is possible if he has no water it is permitted to use it thus, for a d'rabbanan positive act pushes aside the prohibition of dipping in glass d'rabbanan, unlike in metal which is d'oraisa — see Yoreh De'ah 120 seif 16; and it is possible this does not apply in our time — see siman 466 — requires study. Our custom is to bring the ke'ara and everything needed before Kiddush at the time he takes the cup for Kiddush; or therefore, or cover with the right hand the bread and afterward uncover the bread — lechem oni; Hagadah on the bread. And he takes a foreleg of a male lamb for a stretched foreleg and remembrance of Pesach if possible, and it should be roasted; and two cooked dishes for the festival offering — even when it falls on Motzei Shabbat take two cooked dishes, only some change and say from Pesachim and sacrifices when it falls on Motzei Shabbat. One need not remove the egg and meat when saying "this bread" as not written in some siddurim. He should intend in the berachah of eating matzah for the korech and afikoman; therefore one should not engage in idle talk until after afikoman — see Shelah. L'chatchila the whole meal is with reclining except Hallel and Hagadah; and in Birkat HaMazon he sits in awe and fear; and b'dieved it suffices with a kezayit of matzah with reclining and with the cups — Magen Avraham in siman 472 ot 7. And he holds all three matzot in his hand when saying HaMotzi, and afterward let the third matzah fall from his hand and bless on the broken and whole upper one for eating matzah, and afterward break a kezayit from each of the whole and broken and eat — see siman 475. And he takes maror a kezayit, for less than kezayit is not eating, and if he cannot — 18; and it is obvious it is forbidden to bless asher kidshanu for eating matzah; and for korech if possible he takes a kezayit — see siman 475 Magen Avraham 8; and if it is very difficult for him, one kezayit for maror suffices; and when he blesses on the first dipping he should also intend for maror and eat in the first dipping less than a kezayit. And if he has only maror — see siman 475 seif 2; nevertheless if he has a vegetable on which he blesses shehakol he may take it and it is b'dieved, and intend also for maror if he said shehakol for that purpose. He should not make Kiddush until it is night, namely tzeit hakokhavim, for one should not make a d'rabbanan safeik l'chatchila — see siman 489. He should hurry to do the whole Seder before chatzot; and b'dieved he eats matzah after chatzot without a berachah — Magen Avraham. Every mitzvah practiced on the first night is practiced also on the second night; a woman does not lead men in Hagadah even on the second night, lest they treat it like the first night in all its stringencies and not treat it lightly. In Tur here it implies immediately after afikoman he should not drink wine — see siman 475 Magen Avraham 1 and see there; and also after removing the table it is between the third and fourth — he should not drink lest he become intoxicated and not say Hallel; the same after afikoman; and some say wine within the meal that does not intoxicate — see siman 479 Taz 2.
