Musaf. What he wrote "the goats of sin to atone" includes all Rosh Chodesh, as written "the new moons You gave Your people" — for each month "the goats," etc. And what he wrote again "and the burnt offering of Rosh Chodesh is elevated" is as written in Yechezkel "and on the day of the month a perfect bull" which is a burnt offering as written "and on the prince shall be the burnt offering," etc. — in the future Musaf of Rosh Chodesh will be a bull. In the Temple service we rejoice — see there; and the Levite service we all rejoice — see there; "and on your joyous day and Rosh Chodesh," etc. And so what he wrote "a remembrance for all of them they shall be" as written "and they shall be for you a remembrance"; and some explain "for all their offspring" because the Rosh Chodesh burnt offering atones for the offspring of the month's days.
