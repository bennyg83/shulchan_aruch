(א) Musaf prayer. Some read "a remembrance for all of them they were," some "they shall be," some "for all of us it shall be" — one may not change any custom. One must say "and their meal offering and their libations" (Taz chapter 9 of Menachot, not like Rashal in his responsa):

(ב) And one says Keter — per Sephardic custom; in Ashkenaz they say Nekadesh as in Yotzer:

(ג) Bless my soul. In some places they do not practice thus; if one prayed Yotzer in place of Musaf he did not fulfill even if he mentioned Yaaleh VeYavo and must return and pray (so Radbaz part 1 section 201):
