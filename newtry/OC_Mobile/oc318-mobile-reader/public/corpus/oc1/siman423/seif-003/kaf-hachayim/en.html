(3) [Seif 2] And the levi returns and reads, etc. — the reason they needed to skip is because this section has only eight verses; if the first two each read three verses only two remain and one may not leave fewer than three in a section; if they read this section in two six verses remain for two men — impossible because they divide into two sections: "And on the Sabbath day" is two verses and "And on your Rosh Chodesh days" is five; if one reads "And on the Sabbath day" and one verse from "And on your Rosh Chodesh days" one may not begin a section fewer than three; if he reads "And on the Sabbath day" and three from "And on your Rosh Chodesh days" only two remain — therefore they must read three in parashat Tzav; and although only eight verses, when the middle one skips one verse there are nine and the fourth reads "And on the Sabbath day" and Rosh Chodesh section, or the third reads the three remaining and also "And on the Sabbath day" and the fourth reads Rosh Chodesh — as Rambam and Tur wrote and so they practiced.
