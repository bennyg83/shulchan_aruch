(s.k. 3) Musaf prayer — must say, etc. — Taz, etc., not like Rashal responsa 64 who wrote he did not say veniskeihem because he said afterward as discussed; if he says veniskeihem as discussed also unsakeihem; afterward he specifies only meal offerings three tenths per bull, etc., and does not specify libations except 'and wine for its libation' — or he does not say veniskeihem and it refers only to meal offerings; so in parashat Pinchas the verse specifies meal offerings not libations; Taz challenged: oil for meal offerings is also called nesek since it is poured into the meal offering — therefore 'and their meal offering and libations' means oil as discussed three tenths, etc., and omits specifying oil as in scripture; oil is known within the meal offering; then 'and wine for its libation' as in scripture — end:
