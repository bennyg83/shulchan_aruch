1) [Se'if 1] One recites Kaddish Tiskabel. After Hallel, one recites a full Kaddish. The general rule is that on every day having a Mussaf service, a full Kaddish is recited after Hallel. If the day has no Mussaf service, such as Chanukah, one recites half Kaddish. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1.
2) There. One recites Kaddish Tiskabel. Since the Shacharis service has been completed, Tiskabel is recited. On a day without a Mussaf service, however, the prayer service is not completed until after U'Va L'Tziyon, so Tiskabel is not recited until after U'Va L'Tziyon has been completed. After the prayer, meaning after the repetition of the Amidah, half Kaddish is recited. Bach.
