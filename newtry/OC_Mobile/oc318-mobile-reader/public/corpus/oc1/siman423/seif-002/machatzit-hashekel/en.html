(א) (s.k. 2) And the levi, etc. — rather in two sections of parashat Vayedaber there are only eight verses — for those leaving after the levi finishes:

(ב) (s.k. 2) With the three except two verses — lest they think they will not read three with the third but only two at the end of parashat Vayedaber; otherwise why did the levi not read to the section end — or that he left two for the third; you might say even now those entering, etc., and why do we rule above siman 138 one may not begin fewer than three in a section lest one finish with one reader two verses from the section start — those entering between first and second see the second begin two after the section and say the first before him read only those two; answer: those entering will ask, etc.; Taz questioned s.k. 1 — in the Gemara this view exists per Tanna Kama who does not hold decree for entering, but per 'some say' we rule and do not accept this view — end. Truth is with him: Megillah 22a — a section of five verses an individual reads it; we do not say the first reads three and the second the remaining two plus from the next section, lest those leaving between first and second say the second reads only two; if the first erred reading only three the second reads the two remaining plus another from the next; 'some say' the second reads those two and three from the next — lest those entering mid-second or after second think the second read only from the second section start one or two verses; the Gemara challenges Tanna Kama who is concerned for leaving — why not concerned for entering like 'some say' permitting one or two from next; answer: why is Tanna Kama not concerned for entering — those entering ask; 'some say' are concerned and need not this reasoning — we rule siman 138. Nevertheless Taz's grasp does not contradict Magen Avraham from Shibolei HaLeket — certainly we rule like 'some say' and where possible we are concerned for entering; here on Rosh Chodesh impossible — must set aside leaving or entering concern — better set aside entering per Tanna Kama who asks even where impossible; unlike leaving where no leniency. Taz per his method innovated: if the chazzan erred leaving two near the section end, the second should not read one verse from what the first read as we do on Rosh Chodesh with the levi, but read the two remaining plus three from the next; only if the last has only two left must the last read one from the prior — Magen Avraham has no proof but agreed siman 138 and brought proof from Megillah challenging the baraita when the first left two — why does the second read from the next, read one from the first as on Rosh Chodesh; answer: there it is different because possible otherwise, unlike Rosh Chodesh impossible otherwise — Taz wrote there in Megillah the reason the second does not read one from the first is concern for entering who see the second read the last three of the section and say the first read only two from the section start — unlike generally where even reading one from the first with the second the first still has three to the section start — no concern; therefore not the baraita's reason; Taz built on this — end Magen Avraham:
