And the levi returns and reads — see Mishna Berurah on the Gra's view here; and in truth Rashba in his novellae questioned the Gemara per the commentators' explanation why they did not act as the Gra wrote, and it remains unresolved because he did not wish to explain skipping as referring to positions — and so Ramban. And know further that Masechet Soferim as the Gra brought here in our version has the second finish until "and its libation," and per Masechet Soferim we also skip one verse — only that skipping refers to the third; and per Ramban there per his explanation in Masechet Soferim two views are mentioned: per view one the third skips, per view two we pay no attention when impossible; per that masechta the second finishes until the last two verses of the section and the third reads them and finishes until "and on your Rosh Chodesh days," and the Gra in his glosses has a different version there — see there; nevertheless in practice we conclude as Ramban wrote after he protested all commentators erred and innovated that skipping in the Gemara refers only to positions; all the more here where it is not their custom, because of prohibition — and we learned one may not change because of dispute.
