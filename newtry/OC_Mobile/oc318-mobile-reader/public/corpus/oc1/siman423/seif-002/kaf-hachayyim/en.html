On a day without a Mussaf service, however, the prayer service is not completed until after U'Va L'Tziyon, so Tiskabel is not recited until after U'Va L'Tziyon has been completed. After the prayer, meaning after the repetition of the Amidah, half Kaddish is recited. Bach.
