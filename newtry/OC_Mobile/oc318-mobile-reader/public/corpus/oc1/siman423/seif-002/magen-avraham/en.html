And the levi returns and reads. For if he would begin "The one lamb," etc., and read three times, only two verses would remain until "And on the Shabbat day," and it is forbidden to do so lest those leaving think he will not read three with the third but only two; and you might ask: even now there is concern that those entering will say the kohen read only two until "And you shall say" — one may answer that those entering will ask and they will tell them, unlike those leaving (as explained in the Gemara). And Ran asked: if so let the kohen and levi read parashat Vayedaber which has eight verses and the third read "And on the Shabbat day" and two from "And on your Rosh Chodesh days" — what would you say? There is concern for those entering who would say the third read only two — you said those entering ask; one may answer that certainly regarding the kohen they would ask why he did not read more since he still has room, and they would tell him; but here they would not ask, thinking he did not read more so three verses would remain for the fourth:
