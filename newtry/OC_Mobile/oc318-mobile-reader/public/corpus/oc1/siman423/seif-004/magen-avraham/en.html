To remove tefillin. Some are strict not to remove until after Kaddish so there will be four Kaddishim, as written siman 25; and now that they practice spreading the tallit for Shema after the eighteen blessings there are four Kaddishim. In Shelah folio 116 he wrote that in Sefer HaMusar it is the opposite — four kedushot and three Kaddishim, for Barchu is also considered kedusha.
