[4] [Levush] Perhaps one could say, etc. — Malbush Yom Tov wrote and it does not seem to me: if so when the Gemara asks on "and on your Rosh Chodesh days" there are five, how shall we act — let two read and one of those read, we may not begin fewer than three — let it come out one may not divide the day's section; but it seems to me because afterward the baraita teaches a section of five verses an individual reads it — if so there are two laws: one may not begin fewer than three, and second one may not read as an individual. Until here. And initially I would reconcile what he questioned on Levush who did not see Ran wrote the same law — one could say because of the earlier question he asks thus; until here. And if so the same applies that therefore he did not give the reason one may not divide the day's section. And further it seems in my humble opinion Levush's intent in clarifying one may not divide the day's section: since there are only five verses and the prohibition of not beginning, etc., was not made on the day's section itself but better to do it in another section — if so the Gemara was forced to give the reason one may not begin; also what he answered is not there — for reading as an individual is because of not beginning and not leaving, etc. Until here:
