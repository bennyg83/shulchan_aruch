(4) And the reason the levi returns in the kohen's reading and the Israelite does not return in the levi's section: not leaving fewer than three in a section is a decree because of those leaving, and not beginning fewer than three is a decree because of those entering (so the publisher corrected note 1). Here since impossible, the middle one skips — since there is no concern for those leaving there is no concern for those entering, for one who enters will ask.
