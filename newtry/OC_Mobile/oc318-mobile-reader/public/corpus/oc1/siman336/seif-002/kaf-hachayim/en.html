Whether moist or dry — we decree dry against moist; though Tur wrote dry in summer permitted if no fruit or branches to remove — Beit Yosef: we hold like Rif and Rambam not to distinguish moist and dry; therefore did not copy Tur in Shulchan Aruch; so Levush; Or Zarua letter 1 Magen Avraham s.k. 1; Tosafos letter 1 Berakhot on Shabbat letter 1; R' Zalman letter 1; Chayei Adam general 12 letter 5; so other acharonim; see below letter 80.
