(א) Tree roots — that emerge from the ground and look like in old trees.
(ב) Forbidden to use — even though here the concern lest he detach is not so relevant, nevertheless the rabbis decreed.
(ג) Less than that permitted to use — and likewise a tree branch near the ground less than three tefachim.
(ד) Considered like ground — permitted to sit on them; all the more a vegetable not three high is certainly permitted; small tree with fruit perhaps permitted whatever less than three; some say reverse — wherever there is fruit decree lest he detach [therefore roots without fruit — prohibition only lest we not distinguish — less than three permitted]; edible vegetable perhaps forbidden even under three for this reason [Peri Megadim].
(ה) And if they come, etc. — after they were raised three they bent back down.
(ו) Permitted — even though this bent head grew from the forbidden place — three high — and initially forbidden; when it grows more and bends down it is permitted in the low place as ground; therefore all the more permitted to use the tree trunk under three from the ground even if the tree is very tall — that place was never forbidden [acharonim].
(ז) Were three high, etc. — meaning branches go out on the side downward and from them to the ground is three.
(ח) Even though from one side, etc. — such as ground was high on that side and from it to the branch is not three; only from one side — that side is nullified against the three sides that appear three high; if from two sides level with ground — those sides permitted to use.
