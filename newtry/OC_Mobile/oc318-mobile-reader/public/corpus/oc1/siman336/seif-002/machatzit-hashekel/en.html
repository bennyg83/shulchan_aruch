(א) (s.k. 2) Forbidden, etc. — should read on Yom Tov; reason: on Yom Tov no prohibition moving the candle — concern lest he take from tree and use attached; even placing on tree Friday evening forbidden as siman 374 seif 6; Maggid Mishneh implies removal itself is using attached; Parah — such case not using attached on animal; if so same for using attached — Rosh Shabbos: sukkah on tree top kosher in Chullin, cannot ascend Yom Tov using attached; Rosh: even sukkah on ground with s'chach on tree — cannot ascend Yom Tov; decree lest place objects on s'chach — forbidden to place on tree — same forbidden to take; Rosh contradicts himself; Rava Shabbos 155: forbidden sides, same sides of sides; Rav Mesharshia challenged; braita: peg in tree, basket, eruv in basket — above ten not eruv — cannot take eruv Shabbat to eat; basket below ten not reshus hayachid — eruv valid; peg is sides, basket sides of sides — below ten eruv valid, no using attached when taking; hanging basket on tree — sides of sides even below ten no eruv — using attached if taking; this tanna holds even sides permitted; answer: this tanna holds even sides permitted; narrow basket mouth — moving tree not sides; with peg does not move tree; per us even sides forbidden without peg even below ten forbidden to take — using attached; Magen Avraham 374 challenged; perhaps unlike animal — tree sometimes above ten — decree lest climb, forbid below ten lest not distinguish; animal not usually above ten — no decree; Magen Avraham: if removal itself forbidden or lest climb — if tree low no need to climb — permitted to take; per Magen Avraham conclusions even low tree forbidden lest we not distinguish; what difference Rosh wrote — Rosh only rules, not explains; per us if reason climbing forbidden even low; if reason removal forbidden even low.

(ב) And so explicitly in the Gemara Shabbos 155: Rava said whoever forbids using the sides of a tree — likewise holds forbidden to use the sides of the sides; Rav Mesharshia challenged from a braita: one fixed a peg in a tree and hung a basket on it and placed his eruv in the basket — if the basket is above ten tefachim from the ground the eruv is not valid, lest it be fit at twilight of Shabbat to take the eruv and eat it; since he stands in the public domain and the basket is above ten and a plain basket is four wide — the basket is a private domain and forbidden to take the eruv out and bring it to his place in the public domain; but if the basket is below ten — no private-domain status for the basket — his eruv is valid. He inferred the reason for fixing a peg in the tree — it is a peg of the sides of the tree; afterward when he hung a basket it is a basket of the sides of the sides; therefore below ten the eruv is valid — he can take it without using attached — sides of the sides; but if he hung the basket on the tree itself — basket of the sides of the tree — even below ten the eruv is not valid — using attached if he takes the eruv from the basket; this tanna holds sides forbidden but sides of the sides permitted; answer: this tanna holds even sides permitted; why a peg was needed — narrow basket mouth: taking the eruv moves the tree and is not sides; with a peg the tree is not moved — end of Gemara; for us who hold even sides forbidden — without a peg even below ten and not narrow mouth — eruv valid but forbidden to take the eruv — using attached (see Magen Avraham siman 374 who added challenges); perhaps unlike an animal — Rosh's proof that removal alone is not forbidden from comparing rope on a cow — reason is only lest he climb the tree; animal is usually low — no decree; but now below ten also forbidden lest we not distinguish — returns the question why rope on a cow is permitted; Magen Avraham: unlike animal — most are not tall; specifically a tree sometimes above ten — decree lest he climb, therefore forbid below ten lest not distinguish; animal not usually above ten — no decree above ten to forbid below ten; if so what difference what Rosh wrote — Rosh only rules what is relevant; if removal itself forbidden or lest climb — if tree low need not climb — permitted to take; per Magen Avraham even low tree forbidden lest not distinguish; what difference in a tree whether removal forbidden or lest climb — in any case even low forbidden.

(ג) Per Maggid Mishneh — meaning what Magen Avraham wrote in his name s.k. 7: from his words it appears placing on a tree or removal are forbidden in themselves as using attached; if so must resolve for him Rosh's question why permitted to tie rope in a cow's mouth; see Rashi 154b on "sides" — he does not ride on it, not its manner of use — rabbis did not decree on it; implies for one who holds sides permitted — not manner of use; if so even for us who hold even sides forbidden — even not manner of use forbidden; nevertheless tying on a cow's mouth — not manner of use at all and lesser than sides — if so even for us permitted.
