Were three high with a hollow beneath them, etc. — in the Gemara: tree roots three tefachim or hollow three beneath, etc.; Tur and Shulchan Aruch shortened and did not copy the braita language; it appears their intent: even if the hollow itself is not three, in combination with branches where he sits or uses above there are three — forbidden; the braita means with the hollow beneath there will be three total; though strained, for law it is reasonable as they wrote.
