Were three high from the ground, etc. — Rambam chapter 21 writes: if they were three high even if one side is level with the ground or there is a hollow three beneath — forbidden to sit on them. Beit Yosef: the measure of the law is thus. Beit Yosef's words: what is written "even if one side" applies to the first division, not the second; but certainly Rambam after the first division also applies to the second. The explanation: the first division — the tree is three above ground and where the branch ends the ground rises to the branch; Rashi on the Mishnah explains one side level — if three places are high it is forbidden; two places level — permitted per Rabah. The second division — plainly the ground is level but the branch bends down at one end to the ground. Maharshal on Beit Yosef: I do not know why he reversed Rambam's words; Maharshal explains the side level applies only to the first division alone; in the second it deals where level on all sides only hollow beneath — three from ground depth counts as three above. The braita of anigra — Rabbah measures the banks, Rav Sheshet from depth; end of his words. Therefore challenge to Rabbah from hollow braita per Maharshal's explanation; what Beit Yosef reversed — he thought Beit Yosef explains Rambam that side level applies to both divisions; actually it applies to both as I explained; it is plain.
