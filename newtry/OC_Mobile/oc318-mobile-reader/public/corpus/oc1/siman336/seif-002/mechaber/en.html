Roots of a tree that are three tefachim high from the ground — forbidden to use them; less than that — permitted to use them, for they are considered like ground. If they come from above and slope downward — where they are three high they are forbidden, and where they are not three high — permitted. If they were three high with empty space beneath them — even though on one side there is no empty space beneath them and they are level with the ground — forbidden to sit even on the side level with the ground.
