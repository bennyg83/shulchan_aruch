Whether it is permitted to walk on grass and likewise on a tree. And in it are 13 seifim: One
does not climb a tree, whether wet or dry; and one does not hang from it; and one does not use anything attached to the ground at all — a decree lest one climb and detach a leaf from a tree on Shabbat. If one climbed a tree on Shabbat unintentionally — permitted to descend; if intentionally — forbidden to descend. If he climbed while it was still day — in every case permitted to descend after dark. And some say that these words apply only when he intended to descend while it was still day; but if he did not intend to descend while it was still day — he should not descend after dark, since he intended to sit there in a forbidden manner:And specifically a person who climbed there; but if he left an object there while it was still day — forbidden to take it from there on Shabbat (Maggid Mishneh chapter 21). And all this applies to a tree and the like; but soft reeds like a vegetable — permitted to use them even though they are attached to the ground, for there is no prohibition against using a vegetable (Hagahot Ashiri chapter Kol Kitvei in the name of Or Zarua and Beit Yosef).
