(א) (s.k. 1) Whether dry, etc. — Eruvin 100b teaches one braita: moist and dry forbidden (to climb); another braita: dry permitted; answer: in summer recognizable between moist and dry — no decree dry against moist; dry itself has no detaching prohibition — dry permitted; in rainy months not recognizable — even dry forbidden decree against moist; challenge: dry in summer should be forbidden because reeds fall from Mishnah Shevi'is — Rashi: on dry no detaching reason, nevertheless decree against other detaching; answer: no reeds; therefore dry in summer without fruit or branches permitted by law; Rav visited and forbade even in a valley without bnei Torah — we are not better than that place; poskim: we too should forbid in all cases.
