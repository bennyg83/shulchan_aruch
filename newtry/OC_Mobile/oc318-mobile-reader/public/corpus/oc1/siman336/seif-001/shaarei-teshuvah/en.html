(In Shulchan Aruch seif 10) But etrog, etc. — see what Or Ne'erav wrote in Bechor Shor Shabbos 73: Rashi Sukkah 37 wrote one who eats attached — there is no greater detaching; therefore one should protest those who go to gardens on Shabbat and Yom Tov and pick fruit from attached and eat; appoint watchmen until it stops; see there detaching with foot is detaching; Eruvin 100; Har HaCarmel siman 9; Mateh Yehudah brought in Mishna Berurah things to be careful in gardens on Shabbat not to place hands even in another's orchard; not to throw seeds where rain falls; carrying in garden; taking from house to garden — ask a sage which cases permitted (see siman 358 Taz s.k. 5 not to eat in garden on Shabbat; brought in Ba'er Hetev). Also those who tie ropes on trees and seat children — several prohibitions — protest; see Noda B'Yehuda responsa 2:38 on Rambam watering seeds. See there.
