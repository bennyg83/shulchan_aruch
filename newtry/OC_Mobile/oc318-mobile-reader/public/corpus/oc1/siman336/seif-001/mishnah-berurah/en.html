(א) Whether dry — and even if already completely dried with no moisture at all and branches and fruit fell — nevertheless forbidden in every case as a fence and decree; some are lenient in summer months since it is recognizable to all that it is dry.

(ב) And one does not hang — and likewise one does not lean or support oneself on it [Gemara].

(ג) And one does not use — such as placing an object on a tree, lowering something from a tree, tying an animal to it, and the like [acharonim].

(ד) Attached to the ground — specifically higher than three tefachim from the ground as below.

(ה) Lest he climb and detach — leaves, branches, or fruit, and intend to detach them — comes to chatat liability.

(ו) Unintentionally permitted to descend — even though in descending he uses the tree; nevertheless do not forbid him, for in sitting too he uses it.

(ז) Forbidden to descend — until after Shabbat for they penalized him.

(ח) In every case — meaning whether he climbed intending to descend before dark and dark came before he could descend, or climbed intending to sit there on Shabbat.

(ט) Permitted to descend — even though forbidden to climb even Friday evening intending to sit there, for in any case he uses the tree in sitting; nevertheless they did not penalize him, for at the time of climbing he ascended permissibly.

(י) And some say, etc. — and for law we hold like the first view.

(כ) And specifically a person — for in sitting on the tree he does a prohibition.

(ל) Forbidden to take it, etc. — for there is no prohibition in vessels resting by themselves on something attached on Shabbat; in taking it he uses the tree; the reason they forbade taking on Shabbat from a tree — easily he may come to lean on it or a branch; therefore whatever is more than three tefachim high they decreed. Know: nevertheless forbidden Friday evening to place on a tree vessels he may use on Shabbat — since he will use them he will come to remove them from the tree on Shabbat, as above siman 277.

(מ) And the like — meaning reeds or hard canes [Gemara].

(נ) Soft like a vegetable — many poskim hold reeds made to harden even when soft are like a tree and forbidden; only reeds growing in a marsh that never harden, even more than three tefachim, are permitted.

(ס) For there is no prohibition — for they are included in grasses and they did not decree on grasses as explained in seif 3 [Ritva]; poskim wrote nevertheless forbidden to use cabbage and pumpkin stalks — though for vegetables they did not decree, here they are hard and eventually harden like wood [Chullin 127]; know Taz disputes Rama and raised to prohibition reeds and vegetable — Rambam and Shulchan Aruch language at the start after tree law "one does not use attached at all" implies also vegetable and soft reeds; Bach is stringent; Or Zarua challenged and brought many rishonim ruled like Rama [what he brought from Rambam is no proof — all refers to the tree mentioned before; general rule even completely dry without leaves forbidden]; only if the reed is soft like a vegetable; if hard, forbidden to use or take lest it break when using; Tosafos agrees to leniency and divides like Or Zarua; Chayei Adam agrees for law as Rama brought, also Ritva — not like Taz.
