(א) Whether dry — in summer months forbidden because reeds fall; even though branches already fell — forbidden because we do not distinguish; and in rainy months forbidden because one cannot distinguish between moist and dry; but in truth on dry where there is no moisture, detaching is not so relevant — Rashi Eruvin 100; and so Chullin chapter 9; but Rambam's view is that on dry detaching applies as seif 12; and must resolve for him what the Gemara says "and they fell fruit" and does not say lest he climb and detach — see there.

(ב) Forbidden to take it — and why do we not place a candle on it on Yom Tov as siman 277 seif 4 (nevertheless); and Rosh wrote Shabbos chapter 5: if he takes it it is not called using attached, as siman 357 seif 7 permitted to tie rope in cow's mouth; rather the prohibition on a candle is lest he climb the tree to take it; and siman 328 seif 3 implies Rosh holds forbidden to take an object from the tree; and so explicitly in the Gemara — even from a basket forbidden to take; a fortiori from the tree itself forbidden to take; on a basket the reason is lest he climb the tree — since it is below ten he might climb; perhaps he holds since above we decree, below ten also forbidden lest we not distinguish; unlike an animal most animals are not so tall — strained; if so what difference what Rosh wrote that if he takes it not using attached — since anyway forbidden to take; per Maggid Mishneh it must be a tree's manner of use is to take from it and place on it, but an animal has no such manner — therefore sides are lesser; see Shabbos Rashi 154 — forbidden to tie animal to tree as Eruvin 63; rope in cow's mouth permitted; certainly as we said.
