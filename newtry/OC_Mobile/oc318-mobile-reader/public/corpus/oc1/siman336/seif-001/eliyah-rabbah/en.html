[1] [Levush] Even if there is no, etc. — requires study. Beit Yosef ruled thus because Rif and Rambam hold so; why did Bach explain Rif permits on dry — then the essence is like Rosh and Tur that it is permitted when there are not even branches. What Taz challenged — if so Rif would be like Rosh — not precise, for a great difference between them: for Rosh permission is only when no branches; for Rif even with branches and leaves permitted since dry — davar she'eino mitkaven permitted; Perishah explained like Bach and challenged Beit Yosef: but in chapter Meshilin Rif wrote reason lest he climb and detach — implies where detaching does not apply like dry — permitted; if so he should have brought explicitly that even dry forbidden; plain sugya Eruvin 100 implies permission; so siman 372 s.k. 20; what Maggid wrote from Rav in valley, etc. — strained; we find in many places he was stringent and they are not bnei Torah and we hold permission; Tosafos and Agudah: prohibition only where reeds fall; Sefer Tzedei LaDerech; Yam Shel Shlomo chapter Meshilin.
