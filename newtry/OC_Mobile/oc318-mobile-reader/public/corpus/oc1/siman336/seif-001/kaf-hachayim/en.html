a) [Seif 1] One does not climb a tree — even though regarding grass permitted to walk as seif 3, there the reason is mainly concern of uprooting by treading; here question on R' Shimon that davar she'eino mitkaven is permitted; on a tree the reason is lest he detach — R' Shimon agrees forbidden; so Beit Yosef; brought in Taz s.k. 1 see there.
