Psik reisha — and if he eats in another's garden, some permit as written siman 320, since it is not pleasing to him; and Sefer HaZichronot wrote specifically in a gentile's garden we say it is not pleasing to him — and this does not appear so in the Gemara that says he acts on another's land — see there siman 357 seif 7 (Magen Avraham).
