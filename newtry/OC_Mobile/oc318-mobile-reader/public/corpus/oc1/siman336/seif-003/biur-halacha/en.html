(א) Permitted to walk on grass, etc. — see Mishna Berurah; found in Semag a wonder: wrote in melacha of reaping one does not climb a tree lest he detach; also in Eruvin chapter HaMotzi Tefillin forbidden to walk on grass where impossible not to uproot — wonder, for sugya conclusion permits all cases since we hold like R' Shimon; Maharshal in his Biur on Semag also wondered and held all are psik reisha but permitted because no melacha machsheves — to explain Semag; against many rishonim who wrote explicitly not psik reisha (see Or Zarua end Eruvin, Ritva, Tur and Mechaber); Maharshal challenged; even accepting all, Semag is not understood — per sugya all permitted; rather Semag copied from Sefer Yereim in derivative of plowing — explain simply: he did not want to say Rami bar Abba's statement forbidding walking on grass is only per one view; per their view that statement applies even per conclusion like R' Shimon — deals where psik reisha such as walking fast on long grass — certainly cannot avoid uprooting; that is what Yereim concludes "and he hurries with feet — he sins" — hurries to walk on them (like Shabbos regarding Abin of Tzippori not only R' Yehuda but R' Shimon when psik reisha like large vessels — see there).

(ב) Or other liquids, etc. — question: per Semag and Hagahot Maimoni that Magen Avraham copied — watering is liable for plowing too — why write wine permitted and likewise non-growing liquids; though not helping seeds, still softens land by moisture — liable for plowing; perhaps they only said plowing where it also helps seeds; where it harms seeds perhaps land is not improved — strained; found similarly in Maamar Mordechai; examine; in Tiferet Yisrael wrote urine fertilizes land — then urine is derivative of plowing; world not careful; Shabbos 1b proves not so — cannot grow without small; by his words even relieving on plowed field on Shabbat forbidden for plowing — not implied there; see there.
