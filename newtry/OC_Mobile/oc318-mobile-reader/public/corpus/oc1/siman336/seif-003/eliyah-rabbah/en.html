[3] Soft like a vegetable, etc. — Bach elaborated against Rama: Ravina Eruvin 34 establishes decree lest he cut — Rav Nachman's act disagrees with Ravina; Tur siman 499 — in brief; Taz also elaborated dispute from sugya above and added Shulchan Aruch ruled even vegetable and grass forbidden, for he wrote one does not use attached at all from Rambam — until here; not clear, for Rav Nachman's act implies like all; moreover if so Ravina's views would be reversed — soft worse than hard; R' Nachman permits soft forbids hard; rather they do not disagree; Ravina's concern lest he cut is on a hard reed — nevertheless soft relative to a tree; on a reed soft like a vegetable Ravina agrees permitted; Torat Chayim too; moreover even Rambam and Bertinoro: lest he cut — reeds many inserted look attached — therefore decree lest he cut attached thinking detached; if so certainly they do not disagree — soft permitted, no manner to insert; so grass and vegetable; Taz wondered on Rama — perhaps he did not see Shulchan Aruch siman 312 seif 6 permits explicitly like Rama; certainly Shulchan Aruch and Rambam intent like "one does not use attached" means tree mentioned first — even dry without branches forbidden; Levush and examine; if not should only write tree law not plain attached; seif 10 on hadar implies; Taz wrote there he does not take in hand — not so from all poskim; found in Riaz Sheltei Giborim 197 like Rama; Avodat HaKodesh Rashba 35; Beit Yosef siman 312 in name of R' Yehuda — rely on Rama and Levush without hesitation; Rashba concludes reeds made to harden even when soft are like a tree and forbidden; reeds growing in a marsh never harden — permitted like grass small or large.
