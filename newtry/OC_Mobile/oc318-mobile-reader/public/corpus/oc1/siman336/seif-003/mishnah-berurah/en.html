(א) Whether moist, etc. — Gemara implies even if grass is long and he walks barefoot and grass sticks between toes — permitted, not psik reisha to uproot; even if uprooted no prohibition — not intended; nevertheless if after walking grass stuck between toes or on shoes — careful not to remove by hand — forbidden to carry as muktzeh — Friday evening they were attached.
(ב) Since he does not intend — see Biur Halacha what we wrote: where grass is large be careful not to run — psik reisha will certainly uproot; perhaps even walking quickly must be careful.
(ג) That water them — watering seeds is liable for sowing — it helps grow — and for plowing — softens land by moisture.
(ד) Psik reisha — impossible not to help them grow; if eating in a garden not his and not a friend's — some permit, for psik reisha deleina neicha lei is permitted l'chatchila; many poskim forbid even psik reisha deleina neicha lei l'chatchila as end siman 320 — see there.
(ה) Urine — because harsh and burns seeds and prevents growth.
(ו) Or other non-growing liquids — such as wine because harsh and strong and also burns seeds; and other similar liquid; Magen Avraham wrote l'chatchila should be careful even with liquids; see Eliyah Rabbah: strong wine — no need to be careful; Peri Megadim: honey and beer have water and certainly grow.
(ז) Therefore good to be stringent, etc. — Taz wrote in a garden over seventy amot — karmelis, no enclosure helps otherwise must be careful — forbidden to carry four amot; therefore even in another's garden be careful.
