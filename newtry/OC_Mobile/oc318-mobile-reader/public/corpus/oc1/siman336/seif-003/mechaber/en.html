Permitted to walk on grass, whether wet or dry, since he does not intend to uproot. But those who eat in gardens are forbidden to place their hands over the grass to wash them, for they water them — even though they do not intend this — it is psik reisha. However, permitted to urinate on them or other liquids that do not cause growth:Therefore it is good to be stringent not to eat in gardens if he will use water there, for it is difficult to be careful that water does not fall there (Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer HaTerumah).
