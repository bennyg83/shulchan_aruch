(א) Where rain falls — meaning that place is moist from rain that usually falls there; likewise other moist places.
(ב) Will eventually grow — meaning they will grow from softening like all grain put in water as below seif 11; or perhaps in much time will root in ground.
(ג) Or two days — in short time they will not grow; certainly will not root in ground unlike three days or more seeds grow and he is liable retroactively for sowing; also one opinion Yoreh De'ah siman 293 any rooting period is only three days [see there in Peri To'ar]; know: deals with throwing seeds once — otherwise forbidden to prepare for weekdays [Tosafos Shabbos].
