(א) Even unholed — rabbinic detaching concern because it interrupts nourishment slightly; all the more holed — be careful.

(ב) On pegs — even if nothing interrupts between flowerpot and ground; all the more on wood or cloth — prohibition; if on holed flowerpot — d'oraisa detaching and planting [Beit Yosef]; therefore very careful with vessel with spice herbs usually in the house not to lift from ground and place on table or reverse.

(ג) Or reverse — for sowing.
