(א) Seif 8, whether, etc. — Gittin 7b Rashi on "earth of earthenware need not be holed"; Tosafos there on flowerpot wrote the reverse; their proof from what R' Zeira said: holed flowerpot on pegs, etc.; though the boat is not holed; Menachos chapter 4 — flowerpot or flowerpot: here holed, etc.; boat or boat: here wooden boat, etc.; since it does not say boat is also holed — no manner to be holed; even unholed earthenware is like holed; Tosafos there and there wrote reverse; proof from Gittin 21a they wrote on earthen flowerpot holed — implies earthenware requires holed; Shabbos 95b five measures in earthen vessel, holed like a small root — pure from preparing seed; plain flowerpot everywhere is earthenware; they explain reverse: boat is wood therefore no hole needed; see Tosafos Gittin and Menachos; initially Rabbenu Tam was accustomed, etc.; Shabbos chapter 1 — this, etc.; see Rashi on parfisa holed flowerpot, etc.; concern for both explanations; therefore even unholed forbidden whether, etc.; Yerushalmi 40 per Rashi; Rosh brought in laws of Orlah; Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 494 — Yerushalmi Orlah end law 2: one who plants in unholed flowerpot is liable for orlah; R' Yosi said because roots spread; R' Yonah extracts language earthen vessel stands before roots — Rosh there; see Yerushalmi whether also considered holed for seeds; see there; but Rambam chapter 10 wrote perforated; and Noda B'Yehuda and Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah there: plain flowerpot even unholed.
