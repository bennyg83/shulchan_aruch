(א) Even unholed — apparently reason: holed has d'oraisa prohibition per Rambam therefore stringent on unholed rabbinically; but then should not permit pebble with grass in seif 3 to wipe — d'oraisa not permitted for that; unless Mechaber holds like Rashi and Tosafos there as Magen Avraham wrote; correct as Gra wrote: concern for those who hold one vessel unholed like holed; concern for both views; wonder on Bach who noted Mechaber is from gloss — explicitly there holed flowerpot.

(ב) On pegs — see Mishna Berurah on flowerpot with spices; nevertheless like grain standing on ground — even holed permitted to lift to table; proof from Gemara 45 and siman 310: wheat planted not yet rooted — permitted to gather and eat, no muktzeh — interrupt its life; or not yet nourished from ground like on table; per Meiri only if exposed above; if stuck in earth forbidden rabbinically; grass stuck in earth not rooted permitted as seif 6; here not intended for planting, not yet growing — unlike flowerpot sugya where rooted in pot earth; even without earth if intended planting in pot — holed forbidden to lift as seif 16 if intended planting like planted in ground; Mechaber stringent even unholed from doubt wood or earthenware; all this if only a day or two — not yet sprouting; if several days likely sprouting at bottom through hole — lifting cuts nourishment — psik reisha; likewise lifting from table to ground wrong unless intends a day or two; if several days in moist place as seif 4 — will grow and considered planting.

(ג) Split before Shabbat — branch cannot live though still in bark; be careful — world not expert; permit only when fully separated from tree (Peri Megadim).
