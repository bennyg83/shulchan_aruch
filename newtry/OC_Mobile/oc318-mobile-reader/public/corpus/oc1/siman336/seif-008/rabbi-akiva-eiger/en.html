Seif 8, even unholed be careful to lift — sugya of parfisa deals only with holed flowerpot; unholed perhaps no prohibition since on ground too no nourishment when lifting and placing elsewhere, especially on pegs; even holed is only rabbinic; unholed no d'oraisa detaching — perhaps rabbinic decree on decree; perhaps Shulchan Aruch's reason from doubt whether unholed wood or earthenware law.
Magen Avraham s.k. 11: there is no greater detaching — see Menachos 70a "and he bends and eats"; later found so in Bechoros Shor on Shabbos 73b and in Kontres Motzal Me'esh siman 88.
