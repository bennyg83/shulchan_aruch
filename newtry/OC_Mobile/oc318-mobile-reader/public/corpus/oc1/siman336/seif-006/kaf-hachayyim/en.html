6) There: One may not use something attached to the ground, etc. This refers to a tree or something resembling it, such as species of ground produce whose stalks are hard and tall like a tree. This is precisely implied by the wording, 'as a decree lest one climb and detach.' One may use a vegetable, provided he does not move it, as stated above in siman 312, seif 6; see there.
