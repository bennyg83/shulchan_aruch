(א) So they would be moist — such as grass pleasant in appearance and smell; fill a moist earth vessel and stick them in [Rashi]; likewise if stuck in ground since he does not intend they root there.
(ב) To grasp by leaves — if he wants to smell and we do not require inserting and withdrawing Friday evening repeatedly to widen the seat in earth lest earth move when withdrawing on Shabbat — even if earth moves we do not care — he does not carry earth by hand but by the grass — side carrying permitted; see siman 311 Mishna Berurah s.k. 28 what acharonim wrote there.
(ג) And to remove them — and likewise return them if the hole did not close when removed.
(ד) And that they have not rooted — such as before three days when certainly not rooted — if rooted obviously forbidden to remove — uprooting from growth.
(ה) But if, etc. — since stuck and buried in ground like other seeds and he intends to plant them — rabbis decreed lest he come to detach after rooting.
