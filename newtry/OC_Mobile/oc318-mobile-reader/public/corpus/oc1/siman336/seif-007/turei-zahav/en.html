Forbidden to detach even, etc. — it appears there is no liability except in a holed pot; and question from seif 5 grass on vessel lip where he is liable — answer in Gemara unholed flowerpot is his offspring, no manner of planting there; but grass on vessel lip is their main growth; therefore good to be stringent not to eat in gardens; in my humble opinion another reason — prohibition of carrying four amot, garden over seventy amot is karmelis as siman 328.
