From an unholed flowerpot — see Mishna Berurah; Choshen Mishpat siman 242 seif 2: dispute — some say wooden unholed like holed because wood absorbs from ground; no hole needed only earthenware; some reverse — earthenware like dust needs no hole unlike wood; third view in Tosafos Menachos chapter 5 — earthenware needs hole, wood even holed like unholed; can elaborate.
