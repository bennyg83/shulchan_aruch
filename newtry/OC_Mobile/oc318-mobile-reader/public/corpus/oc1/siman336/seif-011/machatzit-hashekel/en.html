(א) (s.k. 11) Lest he detach, etc. — nevertheless perhaps not liable.

(ב) What Rashi wrote "there is no greater detaching" — not precise. Exempt. Must first say he is liable — two matters there: on one who removed dates by throwing a stone from the tree — one for detaching and one for detaching and threshing; Tosafos: dates have a thin outer peel.

(ג) By removing from the tree he removes that peel — detaching and threshing, derivative of threshing; concludes exempt — no manner of detaching or threshing; Rashi: no manner of detaching thus except by hand or vessel. Per plain understanding — whatever is not detached by hand or vessel, even biting with teeth — not its manner and no d'oraisa liability; or Rashi here "greatest detaching" is precise — by biting is complete detaching and liable; what Rashi wrote Shabbos 73b "no manner except hand or vessel" is not precise — also biting; and does not come to exclude except stone throwing which that passage discusses — not manner of detaching; since in any case in one place Rashi is not precise, Magen Avraham was uncertain which is primary — therefore wrote "perhaps."
