(א) Soaking — so they soften and grow like some grain soaked before planting.
(ב) Derivative of sowing — as sowing intends produce to grow, so too [Moed Katan 2b]; Chayei Adam: likewise soaking grain to make malt for beer — liable, known intent to grow; also liable immediately when put in water though grow only after many days; concludes nevertheless permitted to soak grain for animals — no intent to grow, animals eat before growth; know: all this seeds that do not dissolve in water; seeds that mix and cling in water like vetch that soften and dissolve — water becomes like kneaded dough — forbidden to soak for animals for kneading [as siman 340 seif 12 and Mishnah Shabbos 17 and Rambam's Mishnah explanation and Meiri]; dried fruit permitted to soak on Shabbat; nevertheless in pressing olives forbidden [Peri Megadim].
(ג) For any amount — even one grain.
(ד) To place tree branches — cut in summer to enjoy at home [Maharil] — prepared yesterday for this; plain tree branches are muktzeh.
(ה) And see below siman 654 — explained there only permitted to return to water that stood there from Friday evening, not add cold water, all the more not completely change water; therefore what was written earlier permitted to place means return on Shabbat even if already removed — not place l'chatchila [Tosafos; Chayei Adam]; some acharonim hold from Mechaber's silence even l'chatchila permitted in a vessel with water prepared from Friday evening — only adding now forbidden for extra trouble; know: if blossoms open from water — forbidden for all to return to water that stood there.
