(א) And liable for any amount — requires study, for Zevachim 94 says: one who throws flax seed in water — why liable? If because it grows from water it is sowing — if so wheat and barley too; there he does kneading — flax sticks together; see there — appears liable only in flax for kneading; wheat and barley exempt as siman 340 seif 12; perhaps in flax liable immediately for kneading; wheat and barley only after soaking long time like half a day — therefore mentions soaking; practice puts wheat in water for animals — nevertheless requires study from where Rambam learned this.
