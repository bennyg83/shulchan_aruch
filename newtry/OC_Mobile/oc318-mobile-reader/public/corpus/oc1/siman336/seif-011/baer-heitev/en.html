Branches — that were prepared yesterday for use; and see siman 308 seif 23. And see siman 654 that it is forbidden to add water on Shabbat. What the multitude practice to put in a vessel of water roses and the like is a complete prohibition, for those roses open from the moisture of the water; and Rama already wrote in the name of Maharil not to put in water tree branches that have blossoms and roses that open from the moisture of the water (Knesset HaGedolah; and see Yad Efrayim).
