(א) (s.k. 12) Liable, etc. — appears only flax; wheat barley — no liability for kneading; not attached except kneading; flax liable immediately in water for kneading; wheat barley only after long soak — therefore mentions soaking; practice wheat in water for animals; question remains why Gemara chose flax — if liable in wheat barley anyway for sowing — unnecessary to teach flax also for kneading; if so in flax liable two — he did not say two in flax only liable; did not explain why liable.

(ב) As siman 340 seif 12 one who puts flax sesame etc. liable for kneading; no full proof — "and similar" perhaps includes wheat barley; ends kneading — perhaps wheat barley not included — Gemara clear no kneading in wheat barley.

(ג) Therefore mentions soaking — Rambam and Shulchan Aruch changed Gemara "throws wheat in water liable" to "soaks" — Gemara immediate liability therefore "throws"; no sowing liability immediately — only kneading therefore flax; Rambam and Shulchan Aruch soaking takes time — sowing liability in wheat barley too; siman 340 also puts flax — immediate liability only flax for kneading.

(ד) Nevertheless requires study — Maggid Mishneh learned from Zevachim throwing flax; no proof from there for permitting soaking wheat barley — no Rambam necessity from Zevachim.
