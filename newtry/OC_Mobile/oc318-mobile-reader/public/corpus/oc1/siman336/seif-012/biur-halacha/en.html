Liable — dispute among poskim: some say even when fruit and stems dried and tree fully dry (Eruvin last chapter) still liable since tree attached; fruit attached too (Rambam as Magen Avraham and Nishmat Adam); many (Rashi, Tosafos Shabbos 150b, Rashba, Or Zarua, Tosafos) — liable only when fruit and stems still moist or tree dry but trunk still living; if fully dry exempt on fruit and tree as detached; see Mishna Berurah on Magen Avraham dry grass — exempt even per Rambam; nevertheless on his own land not a marsh that improves by this — liable for plowing even small detaching — psik reisha Shabbos 103; Tosafos Shabbos 150b on detaching straw; Rashba; why Magen Avraham omitted — deals with reaping; difference another's land or marsh; rabbinic prohibition — Rashba: moves earth; do not challenge from covering radish under ground siman 308 permitted though moves earth — side movement; poskim require specifically not intended for planting; if intended planting forbidden rabbinically to remove — looks like detaching; no better than there; Rashba's reason moving earth is strained per our words — essence is prohibition.
