One who detaches — and it appears regarding dry grasses all agree considered detached, not like Or Zarua. Rosh wrote general rule 2 siman 4: roof filled with earth and planted — one who detaches is liable; Yoreh De'ah siman 494; proof from Mishnah 2 chapter 5 Maaserot; requires study — Rosh brings Yerushalmi only for impurity but for all matters like detached; nevertheless Taz brought Yerushalmi: when he does not want rooting; but when he wants rooting — fully attached; concludes: if rubble fell and onion tops exposed like field growth — detaching on Shabbat liable (even if he does not want rooting); see siman 311 seif 8.
