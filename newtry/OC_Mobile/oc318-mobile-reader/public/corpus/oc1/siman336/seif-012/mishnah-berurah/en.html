(א) On their branches — meaning attached on the branch.

(ב) And likewise a tree — the language is so; any tree whose fruit dried, etc. — not specifically a fig; in Nishmat Adam printing error should read "and likewise a tree that dried with fruit on it" — initially teaches tree moist only fruit dried; then even tree itself dry still considered attached.

(ג) Liable — Magen Avraham wrote on dry grasses all agree considered detached — detaching exempt; nevertheless rabbinic prohibition even on grasses; sometimes chatat too as I explained in Biur Halacha; onions usually root on a roof with earth — rooted there and he wants rooting — liable though whole onion exposed, not normal growth; if rubble fell covering onion mothers and leaves exposed as normal growth and rooted — even if he does not want rooting liable as if planted in a field [Magen Avraham s.k. 13 in name of responsa Rosh Mishnah 2 chapter 5 Maaserot — see Mishnah and Shenot Eliyahu there; requires study].

(ד) For impurity — food impurity — attached food does not receive impurity; this is considered detached.
