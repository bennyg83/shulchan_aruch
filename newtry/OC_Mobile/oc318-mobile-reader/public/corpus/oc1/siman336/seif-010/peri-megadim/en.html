(א) See note letter 4 vegetable soft even below three — concern lest cut soft reed; muktzeh — hadar even not three forbidden lest cut and muktzeh — only smell not in hand forbidden; etrog smell from afar not in hand — lest come to cut to eat; if entered garden Shabbat and smell came — blessing on forbidden smell; Or Zarua letter 12; R' Zalman letter 8; requires study.

(ב) What he wrote careful not to detach hair from animal hide — liable chatat; those in fox and sable fur liable; forbidden to comb with comb Shabbat siman 303 s.k. 28 and 38 Magen Avraham explained human skin not mostly detached liable chatat; siman 351 Magen Avraham letter 3; therefore requires study why hung on Sanhedrin Gemara — explained in Sha'ar HaTziyun.

(ג) What Mishna Berurah wrote in seif 11 gloss permitted to place — implies l'chatchila if no trouble; Magen Avraham letter 13 palm branches Sukkah Mishna Berurah Rashi not to wither — hadar with grapes opening forbidden; Maharil — if returning to water permitted not necessary if were blooming forbidden even returning; Mishna Berurah gloss seif 13 without moving lest detach forbidden in carrying even partial; deep pit one hundred amot no concern uprooting earth — Beit Yosef; Tosafos.

(ד) See palm branches Sukkah Mishna Berurah 1 — Rabbenu Tam holds woman can bless on muktzeh that her husband set — if she does not want to bless teaches if she wants should not be muktzeh; if all left no man to go out with — forbidden to carry even immediately; explained siman 451 Magen Avraham letter 21; night carrying; Beit Shmuel day two; cannot elaborate here.
