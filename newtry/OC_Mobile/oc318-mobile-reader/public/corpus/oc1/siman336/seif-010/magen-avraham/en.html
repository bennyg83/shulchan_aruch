(א) Permitted to smell — since he can smell while attached he will not come to detach; Yeshuot Shmuel is stringent like Ran.

(ב) Lest he detach it to eat — and you might say he can bite from attached — there is no greater detaching; (Rashi); nevertheless perhaps not liable, for no manner of detaching thus, as they say if he threw a stone on the tree and removed fruit he is exempt — no manner of detaching except by hand or vessel; and likewise in detaching and threshing — see there.
