Permitted to smell it — for we are not concerned lest he detach it to eat, for he only wants to smell and can do so even attached; per what is written in seif 1 one does not use even a vegetable — here he should not take it in hand, for if he takes hadar in hand it is forbidden; see there. A person must be careful in clothing made of animal hide not to detach hair — liable for a chatat as Yerushalmi Sanhedrin great principle: detaching from an animal that died — liable, for that detaching is its shearing; also careful not to detach from one's skin by hand or elsewhere — like attached to ground as Sanhedrin beginning: a person is like ground.
