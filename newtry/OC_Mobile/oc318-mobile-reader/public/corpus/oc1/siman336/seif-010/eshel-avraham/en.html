Permitted — see what Ba'er Hetev wrote in the name of Taz; and in Beit Yehudah siman 11 he raised that what is forbidden to take in hand and smell is per the assumption that using a vegetable is forbidden; when the reason ceased the result ceased — he ruled now permitted to use a vegetable and certainly permitted to hold in hand. Do not challenge per this on Rama seif 13 permitted to touch a tree provided he does not move it — if movement is for use otherwise forbidden to use the tree; if only idle handling why forbidden; also for climbing lest he detach — cannot climb without movement — only a stringency.
