Magen Avraham s.k. 13: and see siman 654 forbidden to add — there only permitted to return lulav to water on Yom Tov and add water; Shabbat law not mentioned since for us lulav is muktzeh on Shabbat; nevertheless true that if they take lulav on Shabbat they return but do not add as Mishnah Sukkah chapter 3; should explain here return permitted but not add water since addition permission not heard; Rashi and Ra'avad on Mishnah Sukkah: return — since they took it today from water — implies only if taken from water on Shabbat; if taken Friday evening — do not return that water on Shabbat since lulav was not in water on Shabbat; found no one to distinguish thus.
