(א) Permitted to smell — no decree lest he detach since he only wants to smell and can do while attached; some are stringent; Or Zarua wrote primary as Shulchan Aruch ruled; regarding carrying in hand — law like soft reeds in seif 2 letter 1.
(ב) Lest he detach to eat — you might say he can bite from attached — no greater detaching; protest those who go to gardens on Shabbat and Yom Tov and pick attached fruit and eat; besides detaching forbidden in eating for everyone on Shabbat as siman 322 seif 3; appoint watchmen until it stops.
