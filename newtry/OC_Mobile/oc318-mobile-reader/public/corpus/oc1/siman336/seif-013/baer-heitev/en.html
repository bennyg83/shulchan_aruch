And the basket — and permitted to take an object from the basket, but the basket itself is forbidden to take, for he uses the peg which is sides; and if the basket hangs on the tree itself — forbidden to take from the basket, for he uses the sides. If the mouth of the basket is narrow so that when taking an object from the basket he moves the tree — forbidden in every case. And to lean on a tree — if the person is healthy, permitted; if weak, forbidden (Yeshuot Shmuel, Magen Avraham see there).
