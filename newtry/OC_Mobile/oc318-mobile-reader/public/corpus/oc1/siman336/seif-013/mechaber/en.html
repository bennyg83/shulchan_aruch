Forbidden to use the sides of a tree, but the sides of the sides are permitted. Therefore forbidden to lean a ladder against the sides of a tree, for when he climbs it he uses the sides. But if there is a peg fixed in the side of the tree — permitted to lean a ladder on it, for it is a peg of the sides and a ladder of the sides of the sides. If he fixed a peg in it and hung a basket on it — the peg is called sides and the basket is like the sides of the sides:And permitted to touch a tree provided he does not move it (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chayim). A deep pit, even one hundred amot — permitted to descend and ascend, and climb down and climb up, and we are not concerned lest he uproot earth in his descent and ascent (Tur).
