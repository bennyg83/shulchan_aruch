(א) Sides of the tree — something stuck or tied to the tree is called sides of the tree; tree faces themselves are not called sides but body of the tree; sides of sides — something leaning on something stuck to the tree; as Mechaber explains below.

(ב) Forbidden to lean a ladder — language not precise; measure of things — do not lean ladder on tree from Friday evening to climb Shabbat — when he climbs he uses sides; leaning ladder on Shabbat certainly forbidden even not on tree itself only on peg fixed in it — that itself is called using sides of the tree.

(ג) Against the sides of the tree — against its face.

(ד) Permitted to lean — meaning permitted Friday evening to lean ladder to climb Shabbat — peg, etc.; careful not to place foot on peg only on ladder rungs lest use sides [Shulchan Shelomo].

(ה) And the basket like sides of sides — therefore permitted to place fruit in it and take from it; basket itself forbidden to take or hang on peg as ladder; if basket hangs on tree itself forbidden to take from or put in — uses sides [Shulchan Shelomo]; if basket mouth narrow when taking object moves tree — forbidden in every case because thereby uses tree; leaning on tree — healthy person permitted, weak forbidden — healthy does not lean much, not called using attached; weak must lean with full force — using attached and forbidden; when moving even healthy forbidden — that itself is use.
