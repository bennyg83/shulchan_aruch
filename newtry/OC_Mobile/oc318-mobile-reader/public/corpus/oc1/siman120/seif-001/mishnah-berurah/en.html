(1) In Minchah — that is, they begin from "and the fire-offerings of Israel." And see in Peri Megadim who wrote that according to what is now practiced everywhere to say Retzei, this is called skipping a word from the coin that Chazal minted, and its law is as the Mechaber wrote in siman 119 seif 3 regarding a mistaken blessing. And in my humble opinion it requires study whether this is considered b'dieved under the name of mistake, even in Shacharit — see above at the end of siman 64 in Magen Avraham in the name of the Kesef Mishneh, and in siman 59, and in siman 114 Magen Avraham seif katan 9, and at the beginning of siman 187. The Tur wrote on what we say "and the fire-offerings of Israel and their prayer," etc., and even though there is now no Temple service, we pray on the prayer that is in place of the sacrifice that it be accepted willingly before the Holy One, blessed be He. And in the midrash there is that Michael the great minister offers the souls of the righteous on the altar above [meaning he presents them willingly before Heaven for a pleasing fragrance]. And on this they instituted "and the fire-offerings of Israel" — meaning the people of Israel. And some explain it regarding what is above it, and that is the explanation: "Restore the service and the fire-offerings of Israel," and afterward "and their prayer with love accept willingly." And see in Taz who wrote that the middle explanation is the most preferable of all, but the Gra wrote that the primary [view] is with the last explanation.
