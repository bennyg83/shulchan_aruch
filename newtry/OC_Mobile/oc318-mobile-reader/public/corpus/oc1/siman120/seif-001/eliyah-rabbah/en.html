(1) Retzei, etc. "And the fire-offerings of Israel," etc. — the sacrifices; and even though there is now no Temple service, prayer is in place of a sacrifice — Tur. The Beit Yosef explained that Retzei means prayer that is in place of the fire-offerings of Israel — end of his words. It appears that he had a difficulty: if so, why the repetition of "and the fire-offerings of Israel and their prayer"? For the fire-offerings of Israel are their prayer. But it appears to me that one should say that "and their prayer" refers to other prayers that are not of the Shemoneh Esrei, and they are called by the language of "prayer," as I wrote above at the end of siman 110. Furthermore, the Tur wrote in the midrash that Michael the great minister offers the souls of the righteous on the altar above, and on this they instituted "and the fire-offerings of Israel." And there are those who explain that "and the fire-offerings of Israel" refers above to "Restore," etc., and "and their prayer," etc. is the beginning of the matter — end of his words. Matteh Moshe wrote: there are books that do not say "mehera," and likewise R' Yehuda of Germiza, because Michael offers the souls of the righteous — one should not say "mehera." And in order to fulfill the count of thirty-seven words — for there were thirty-seven priests who brought a bull and a goat, and the kohen who offered opposite the chazan — we say "Restore the service," end of his words. And in my humble opinion, to uphold our formulas with "mehera": first, there are two more explanations in the Tur as we said; and likewise in Avudraham he copied only these two interpretations and also has the reading "mehera," and likewise in Rokeach. And furthermore, in Tosafot at the end of Menachos [110a, s.v. "and Michael"] they had this reading, even though they brought there R' Meir / some say it should not be said, for this is certainly the portion that Michael offers, and we pray only for acceptance that HaKadosh Baruch Hu receive quickly, and it is not at all like one praying, God forbid, for the death of the righteous; also it is strange to offer the soul of a righteous person who died many years ago. And furthermore, in Tosafot there are those who explain that he offers fire-sheep.
