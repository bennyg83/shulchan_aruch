Seif 1: They say Retzei in all the prayers. Note: see Tur and Taz on the explanation of this formula. And in my opinion the entire intent is about prayer — what is written "Restore the service to the Holy of Holies of Your House" — per their statement of blessed memory: if one prayed in Jerusalem he should direct his face toward the Temple; if he stood outside the Land he should direct his face toward the Land of Israel. If so, it is proven from this that the entire path of prayers is to enter the Temple first, and from there they ascend above. Therefore we ask that even though we pray in other places, nevertheless "be pleased, Heaven, with Your people Israel and their prayer" — that is, "Restore the service," meaning prayer [as they said of blessed memory: what service is in the heart? This is prayer], to the Holy of Holies of Your House — that first you bring the prayer from the place where they pray to the Holy of Holies of Your House, and the prayer enters the Holy of Holies, and there "and the fire-offerings of Israel and their prayer accept willingly" to ascend above. And what is written "and the fire-offerings of Israel" is about prayer in its time and not in its time, as Taz wrote — contemplate this well.
