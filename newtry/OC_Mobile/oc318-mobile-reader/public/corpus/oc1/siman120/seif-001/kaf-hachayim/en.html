(1) [Seif 1] They say Retzei, etc. And what is written "and the fire-offerings of Israel and their prayer accept willingly" — even though we now have no sacrifices, so that we pray on them that they be accepted willingly — the Tur wrote that we pray on the prayer that is in place of the sacrifice that it be accepted willingly before the Holy One, blessed be He. And the Beit Yosef wrote that the explanation of "and the fire-offerings of Israel and their prayer" is the prayer of Israel that is in place of the fire-offerings, which are the sacrifices, that it be accepted willingly — end of his words. And Tosafot at the end of Menachos wrote according to what the Gemara states: "Forever this is upon Israel — this is a built altar, and Michael the great minister stands and offers a sacrifice upon it." And this is its language midrashically: there are those who say the souls of the righteous, and there are those who say fire-sheep — and this is what we say in the eighteenth [blessing] in the [blessing of] service: "and the fire-offerings of Israel," etc. — end of his words. And the Tur brought only "there are those who say" the first [explanation], and the Beit Yosef wrote that the explanation of "and the fire-offerings of Israel" means the people of Israel who are offered through Michael, or it is from the language of "fire" — a pleasing fragrance — meaning the sacrifices of Israel, namely their souls, which themselves are offered through Michael; also the prayer of Israel — all of it accept willingly — end of his words. And furthermore Tosafot wrote there another explanation, and the Tur brought it that it refers to what is above: "Restore the service to the Holy of Holies of Your House and the fire-offerings of Israel." And the Beit Yosef wrote that this means to say: for the two first explanations, "and the fire-offerings of Israel" is attached to "and their prayer" that comes after it; and for this explanation, "and the fire-offerings of Israel" is attached to "Restore the service to the Holy of Holies of Your House," as if saying "Restore the service and the fire-offerings of Israel," meaning their sacrifices, to the Holy of Holies of Your House, and afterward he begins and says "and the prayer of Israel — speedily, with love, accept willingly" — end of his words. And see Taz seif katan 1, who raised a difficulty on the first and last explanations and wrote that the middle one is the most preferable of all — see there. However, Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, ot 2 wrote that what Taz raised as a difficulty on the last explanation — why need he say "and the fire-offerings of Israel" since he already mentioned service, and if you say there is service without fire-offerings — in any case he should not have interrupted with "to the Holy of Holies of Your House" in the middle, and he should have said "Restore the service and the fire-offerings of Israel to the Holy of Holies of Your House" — this is not precise grammar, for we find in Scripture such a case in Psalms 25: "Remember Your mercies, Heaven, and Your kindnesses, for they are from the world," which is as if written "Remember Your mercies and Your kindnesses, Heaven," as is proven from the explanation of Radak there; and likewise here it is as if he said "Restore the service and the fire-offerings of Israel to the Holy of Holies of Your House" — end of his words. And likewise the Gra [wrote] that the last explanation is primary, and Mishna Berurah brought it at the end of ot 1.
