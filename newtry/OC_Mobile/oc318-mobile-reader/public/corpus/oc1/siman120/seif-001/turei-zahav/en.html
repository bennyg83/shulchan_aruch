They say Retzei, etc. The Tur wrote on the formula "and the fire-offerings of Israel and their prayer," etc., and even though there is now no Temple service, we pray on the prayer that is in place of the sacrifice that it be accepted willingly before the Holy One, blessed be He. And in the midrash there is that Michael the great minister offers the souls of the righteous on the altar above. And on this they instituted "and the fire-offerings of Israel." And some explain it regarding what is above it, and that is the explanation: "Restore the service and the fire-offerings of Israel," and afterward begins the matter: "and their prayer with love accept willingly" — end of his words. On the first explanation there is a difficulty of repetition with "and their prayer," and if he came to explain what "fire-offerings of Israel" are, he should have said "their prayer" without the vav. And one can say that there are two types of prayer: one — prayer in its time was in place of a sacrifice, and after its time — as R' Pinchas bar Chama said: reward for prayer they give me; reward for prayer in its time they do not give me — on this "and their prayer" refers, to include also prayer not in its time, which is not in place of the fire-offerings of Israel. And on the last explanation there is a difficulty: why need "and the fire-offerings of Israel," since he already mentioned the service? And if you say there is service without fire-offerings — in any case he should not have interrupted with "to the Holy of Holies of Your House" in the middle, and he should have said "Restore the service and the fire-offerings of Israel to the Holy of Holies of Your House" — end of his words. Therefore the middle explanation is the most preferable of all.
