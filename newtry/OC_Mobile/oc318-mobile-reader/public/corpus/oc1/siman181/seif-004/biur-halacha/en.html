Up to the second joint, etc. See Biur HaGra, from whose words it is evident, according to the explanation in Damesek Eliezer, that this law depends upon the dispute cited above in siman 161, seif 4. According to the first opinion there, the law concerning mayim acharonim would require washing through the ends of the finger joints together with the palm. It is proper to follow this practice initially, so as to satisfy all opinions, when water is available.
