Seif 4: One need not, etc. Terumas HaDeshen, Shaar 1, writes likewise in the name of Raavad. See Magen Avraham. All this follows Rashi, Rashba, Raavad, and the other poskim, who explain that for ordinary food the washing extends until the joint, meaning until the second joint of the fingers. Although regarding terumah we rule stringently in accordance with Shmuel, that is because of the connection to terumah, unlike mayim acharonim. This is the view of Raavad and Rashba here, as explained above.
