1) [Seif 1] The final waters are obligatory. Berachos 53b states: 'You shall sanctify yourselves' refers to the first waters, and 'you shall be holy' refers to the final waters, etc. Regarding the reason, our Sages, of blessed memory, said in Chullin 105b that Sodomite salt is present and blinds the eyes. Abaye said that it is found in the proportion of one grain in a kor. Rashi, of blessed memory, explains that the Sages said in Berachos 40a, 'With everything you eat, eat salt.' Since one touches the salt, if he afterward places his hand upon his eyes it blinds them, and therefore he must wash them. Rabbeinu Yonah writes in the chapter Eilu Devarim that the Rambam, of blessed memory, says that although we no longer have Sodomite salt, one must be concerned about another salt whose nature is like that of Sodomite salt. If one passes his hands over his eyes before washing, it will blind his eyes. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes in the name of Rid that even if one did not eat salt, washing the hands is obligatory, because failure to wash the final waters caused the loss of life, as stated in Yoma 83b. Beis Yosef cites their words. These reasons follow the plain meaning. We have already written in several places that all the words of our Sages, of blessed memory, accord with the mystical dimension, but they clothe them in a plain explanation to make them understandable. The Zohar, parashas Terumah, 154b, reveals the secret of the final waters, stating: After a person has eaten and enjoyed himself, he must give the residue as a portion to that side. What is it? The final waters, the grime of the hands, which one must give to that side as the portion it requires. Therefore, they are certainly obligatory. They are an obligation and rest in the place of obligation, and a person is obligated to give it this portion. Therefore, no blessing at all is required over them, for blessing does not exist on that side. Sefer HaMitzvos, parashas Eikev, likewise writes: Regarding the final waters, know that the Other Side stands at the table, as stated in the Zohar, parashas Terumah, and can then exercise greater control over a person than at other times. This is especially true when a person is alone and three men are not present to recite Zimun, because through Zimun one removes the Other Side from there, as stated in parashas Balak in the account of that child. One must be extremely careful about the intentions of the final waters so that it not prosecute him. Through this gift that one gives it, as is known, the Other Side withdraws and departs. Initially it was a guest, but afterward, if one does not properly direct his intent in Birkas HaMazon, it becomes the householder and prosecutes him, especially when he is alone without Zimun, as stated. We have already written the intention for the final waters above in siman 157, paragraph 21; see there. We thus find that the final waters carry a great obligation according to the mystical meaning, for they drive away the Other Side. Therefore, our Sages, of blessed memory, warned about them and said that they are obligatory. For this reason, it appears to me that one should not place his hand upon his mustache while his hands are still wet from the final waters, since these waters are the portion of the Other Side.
