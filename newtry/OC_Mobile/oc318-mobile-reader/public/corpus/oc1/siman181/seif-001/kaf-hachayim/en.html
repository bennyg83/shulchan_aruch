These Matters: Rambam though no Sodomite salt now—fear other salt like Sodomite; if passes hands over eyes before washing blinds—end quote; Ri Migash in name Ri: even without salt obligatory because mayim acharonim (Yoma 3b) kill the soul—end quote; Bach, Beit Yosef.
