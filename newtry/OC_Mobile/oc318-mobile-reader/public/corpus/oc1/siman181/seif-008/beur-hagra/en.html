Seif 8: Some say. See what I wrote in siman 173, seif 3, that even for mayim rishonim, wherever there is no concern of impurity, such as immersion or pouring an entire reviis at once, drying is unnecessary, as written at the end of siman 128. The same applies here. Rambam, at the end of chapter 6 of Hilchos Berachos, follows his position and does not write regarding mayim rishonim that one who immerses need not dry, nor that washing without drying is not called washing.
