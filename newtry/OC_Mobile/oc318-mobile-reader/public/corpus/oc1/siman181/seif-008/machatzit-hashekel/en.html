Seif katan 8: Some say, etc. Because Sodomite salt is not found nowadays. Maharshal, at the end of siman 10 of chapter Kol HaBasar, writes: Rabbeinu Yonah says in the name of Rambam that although we no longer have Sodomite salt, there is another salt like Sodomite salt, and if one passes his hands over his eyes it will blind them. Rambam was the foremost authority on the natural sciences. It therefore appears that one should not be lenient. These are Maharshal’s words.
