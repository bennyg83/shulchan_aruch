8) [Seif 2] But into a utensil, etc. If he has no utensil designated for the final waters, or if he is too indolent to rise and has no one to serve him, he may wash into an empty food bowl, as stated in Ikarei HaDinim, siman 9, paragraph 17. It is preferable, however, to remove it from before him during the blessing and place it to the side, off the table. Kaf HaChaim there, paragraph 3. We wrote the same above in siman 173, paragraph 15; see there.
