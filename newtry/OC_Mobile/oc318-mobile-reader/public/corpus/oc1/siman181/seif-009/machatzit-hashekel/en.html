Seif katan 9: It is considered, etc. Regarding wine while his hands are soiled, etc. Although one who washes his hands for fruit is considered arrogant, that applies where his hands are ordinarily clean. Since here they are soiled, he must wash them, as above in siman 158, seif 5. There Rama writes that he is permitted to wash, while in seif 8 he writes that he must wipe them.
