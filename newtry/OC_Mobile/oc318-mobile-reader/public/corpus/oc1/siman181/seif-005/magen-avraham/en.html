For the final five. The Sages assessed that during the time needed for four people to wash, the one who will lead Bircas HaMazon can review its four blessings. During this same interval there is no interruption between washing and the blessing. The statement that the blessing must immediately follow washing therefore does not mean literally immediate, but within this interval. It is possible, however, that unnecessarily we do not allow any interruption at all. Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba. See siman 166.
