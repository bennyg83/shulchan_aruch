And if there is no, etc. Ibid., 105a, following the latter version. Terumas HaDeshen and the Tur rule likewise. Rambam, however, rules in accordance with the first version. Kesef Mishneh writes that his reason is that the first version is the anonymous formulation of the Gemara, and further, in a matter of danger we rule stringently, as stated in the first chapter there. Shulchan Aruch likewise rules this way. If he has no other option, however, there is room to rely on Rashba and the Tur.
