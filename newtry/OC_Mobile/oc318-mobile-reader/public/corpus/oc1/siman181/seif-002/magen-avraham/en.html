Thin pieces of wood. The same applies to anything that separates between the water and the ground. Sefer HaChinuch. This implies that it is permitted even where there is a paved floor, because the harmful spirit rests only upon the ground. Hachayat writes that in a place where people do not pass, one may wash directly onto the ground. It therefore appears to me that one may wash beneath the table. Although the table is sometimes removed, the water will dry in the interim.
