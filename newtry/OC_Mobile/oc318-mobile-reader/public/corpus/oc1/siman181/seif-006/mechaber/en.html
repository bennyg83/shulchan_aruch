6. If those reclining [at the meal] are many — up to five, they begin [the washing] with the one who recites the blessing [i.e., the one who will lead birkat hamazon]. And if they are more than five, they begin with the least distinguished [literally: 'the smallest'] and wash in the order of their seating, and they do not honor one another [by deferring the washing, saying 'you first'] until they reach the final five. And once only five remain who have not yet washed, they begin [again] with the one who recites the blessing
