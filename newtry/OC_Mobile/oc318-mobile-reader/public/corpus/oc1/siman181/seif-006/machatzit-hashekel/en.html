Seif katan 6: He dries, etc. Specifically the one who recites the blessing, etc. Maharshal writes there, siman 10 of chapter Kol HaBasar, that it is improper to recite Hashem’s blessing without drying, because of “Prepare to meet your God, Israel,” but the others do not require drying. Accordingly, this applies specifically to their practice, under which only one person recited Bircas HaMazon and the others fulfilled their obligation by listening, as stated in siman 183, seif 6. According to our practice, however, based on what the later authorities write there, that even when they form a zimun together each person recites Bircas HaMazon for himself, everyone requires drying even according to Maharshal.
