Seif 6: Beginning with the one who will recite the blessing, etc. As stated there, “and to the place,” etc., supports this. The reason is only that the most distinguished person recites the blessing, as stated there, 47a: “Begin with the greatest.” See Rashi, s.v. to the place, etc.
And they wash in the order, etc. As stated there, 47a: “We do not honor,” etc. The fact that among the final five they begin with the greatest is not because of his honor, but because of the blessing, as stated there, “supports,” etc., and so that he may review Bircas HaMazon, as stated there concerning Bar Pachsei. Rashba. See Magen Avraham.
