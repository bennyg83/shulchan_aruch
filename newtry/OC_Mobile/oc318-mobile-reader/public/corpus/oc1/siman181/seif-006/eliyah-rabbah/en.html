[6] [Levush] And the reason one does not, etc. This is difficult, for the reason was already given that the blessing must immediately follow washing. In truth, one who examines Beit Yosef will see that the authority who gave this reason did not give the other. Rashi explains that it is because the table was removed, whereas Rashba gives the reason of interruption. Maharshal too, in chapter Kol HaBasar, siman 42, was imprecise when he wrote that nowadays, when the table is not removed, even if there are one hundred people they honor the one who will recite Bircas HaMazon first; he did not note that there is an additional reason, namely interruption. This resolves the difficulty of Bach and Taz against the Tur, who rules this law, while in siman 180 he writes that the table was not removed from before the one who would recite the blessing. There is no difficulty, for the Tur accepts Rashba’s reason. It further appears to me that since their practice was to remove the table, Rashi formulated the matter that way. In truth, however, it is undignified for the greatest person to sit idle and wait for them even when the table is not removed. This is also the view of Levush.
