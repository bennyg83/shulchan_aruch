Which things are considered a lapse of attention during a meal requiring a new blessing. 6 sections: If one finished
his meal and washed his hands with the final waters, he may not eat or drink until he recites Grace After Meals. If he said "let us say the blessing" this constitutes a lapse of attention and he may not drink unless he first recites a blessing over it. Eating has the same law as drinking according to the Rosh; but according to Rabbeinu Yonah and the Ran, eating is different — even though he withdrew from eating, and even if the table was removed, if he wishes to return to eating he need not recite a blessing again, since as long as he has not washed his final waters he has not fully withdrawn from eating.
