A) [Section 1] Finishing his meal, etc., he cannot eat, etc., but it is permissible to stop talking between the last water and the blessing. 22 BKM at the end of P. 5 of the 2nd in the opinion of the late Rambam and brought MA SKA. But in the opinion of the Rabbi, he wrote in the Gemara with this sign that it is permissible to stop even when speaking Yaush. Likewise, the opinion of the MA there, etc. AR, letter 1, AA, letter 1, letter S, etc. above, sign 26, letter 7, Yaush. Two days after he had a severe pain in his shoulder and we looked at him in the late Ari and El because this pain came to him because he stopped between last water and the Behmaz reading a chapter of Mishniyot and immediately moved on to taking a blessing, i.e. taking last water, so he immediately turned his shoulder and felt a pain in his shoulder.
