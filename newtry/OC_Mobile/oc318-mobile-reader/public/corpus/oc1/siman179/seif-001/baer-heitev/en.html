Not to drink. But to interrupt with speech is permitted, as siman 183 seif 6 Magen Avraham. The Arizal forbids—see his Shulchan Aruch Maaseh 11.

And we will bless. Shulchan Aruch's view divides between netilat yadayim and let us bless—for let us bless suffices to bless hamotzi and need not birkat hamazon; but with netilat yadayim must specifically birkat hamazon first. Taz disputes and holds in all cases need only hamotzi blessing not birkat hamazon, see there. So too Magen Avraham no distinction between netilat yadayim and let us bless, see there. See Yad Ephraim and teshuvah Ne'eman Shmuel siman 7. Maharshal wrote: some say when pouring cup for birkat hamazon even without speech it is as saying let us bless. Ateret Zekenim.
