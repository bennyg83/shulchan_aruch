Seif katan 1, he may not, etc. This is implied by siman 183, which states that once the cup has been given to him for Bircas HaMazon, the one reciting the blessing should not speak. This teaches that even before the cup is given, speaking is forbidden, since he has already washed his hands. See what I wrote in siman 166, that even after washing before the meal one may not speak. All the more so after mayim acharonim, which are stricter regarding interruption, as explained there. See also Magen Avraham at the end of siman 165 and what I wrote there, with Hashem’s help. The proof from siman 183, seif 6, cited by Kesef Mishneh to permit speaking, is likewise not a proof.
