Eating is different. Taz raised: if established for eating even said let us bless permitted eat more without first blessing, consequently if wants drink without first blessing if eats more; students of Rav (Pesachim 106) forbidden to drink for you—they did not want eat only drink. Also wrote even to drink alone if beverage people drink not for thirst but pleasure—also did not withdraw hand at let us bless (Taz: as appears from word but should read they did not say so for drinking beverage custom to drink for pleasure, etc.); nevertheless if established only drinking without eating—can say no establishment for drinking and removal—some remove even beverage for pleasure, requires study; seems Mechaber's law like R' Yitzchak and Ran; Eshel Avraham note 4. Seems if established for eating—not only bread but other food—not removed by let us bless final blessing to divide; if wants eat more per R' Yitzchak and Ran permitted without first blessing.
