(2) There he cannot eat, etc.—Shulchan Aruch divides netilat yadayim and let us bless: with netilat yadayim all agree cannot eat or drink until birkat hamazon; with let us bless for drinking need only first blessing on what he will drink; for eating Rosh requires first blessing; Rabbenu Yonah and Ran no first blessing. Tur—whether netilah or let us bless equal—for eating and drinking need only first blessing, see there. Bach Rosh like Tur not Beit Yosef; Taz s.k. 1, Magen Avraham s.k. 2, Eshel Avraham 1 many poskim like Tur; Maamar Mordechai 1. Since dispute, l'chatchila be careful—neither eat nor drink until birkat hamazon. If forced return eat or drink—if netilah bless first on more then return take and bless birkat hamazon covering first eating per Tur; if only let us bless without netilah—for drinking bless, for eating no blessing per Rabbenu Yonah and Ran s.v. birkat lechem, only contemplate in heart.
