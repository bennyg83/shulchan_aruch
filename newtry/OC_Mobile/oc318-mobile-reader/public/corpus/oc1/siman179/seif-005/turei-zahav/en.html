Truth Tosafot as plain—all from some's words—do not heed man's mind thinking will give more need not return bless—depends on inviting host's mind; therefore doubtful if will give or not like here guests R' Gamliel—need not bless each cup because we follow host's mind not guest's regarding removal—here too we follow only host's mind not guest's—must bless each type given since he doubts; Rabbi's words here very wondrous in my humble opinion.
