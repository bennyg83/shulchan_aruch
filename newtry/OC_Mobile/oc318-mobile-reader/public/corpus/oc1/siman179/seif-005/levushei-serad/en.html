There As written there in seif 1. I do not know what this refers to, and perhaps it is a scribal error. His intention may be to refer to Magen Avraham at the beginning of siman 181, who cites Rabbeinu Yerucham that the cup is poured first and afterward one washes the hands. According to that order, it is possible for the cup to be handed to him before washing. If, however, we say that one washes before the cup is poured, this law would have no possible application at all. This is clear.
