Seif katan 6, and each person, etc. Even if he has not yet finished drinking the first cup, he must recite a blessing over each subsequent cup, since the previous blessing had already left his mouth before the next cup was handed to him and he did not have it in mind. See Yoreh Deah 19 regarding slaughter, and siman 8, seif 12, Magen Avraham there, and siman 206, where the matter is discussed primarily.
