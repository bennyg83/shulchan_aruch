From the time he takes the cup - Regarding the other diners, the law depends on who is leading Bircas HaMazon. If the leader is the host himself, or the most distinguished person at the table according to Eliyah Rabbah’s view cited above, it is clear that all the diners follow him. If the leader is one of the other diners, then for the leader himself taking the cup certainly constitutes a diversion of attention. For the remaining diners, the matter depends on the dispute among the poskim below in siman 183 as to whether the diners may converse after the leader takes the cup in his hand. According to those who permit it, the others certainly are not drawn after him so long as they themselves have not withdrawn their attention and have not said, “Let us recite Bircas HaMazon.”
