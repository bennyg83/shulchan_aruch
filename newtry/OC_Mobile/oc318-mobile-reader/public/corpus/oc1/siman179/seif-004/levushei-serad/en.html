Magen Avraham, seif katan 1 To interrupt with speech. Although we say that a blessing should immediately follow washing the hands, ordinary speech does not nullify the required immediacy. Kesef Mishneh writes this and brings proof from siman 183, where conversation is prohibited only after one takes the cup in hand. Magen Avraham responds from siman 165: according to the authority who understands the rule of immediacy after washing to apply also between washing and HaMotzi, it is forbidden to interrupt with speech there. All the more so is speech prohibited between washing and Bircas HaMazon, which is more stringent. Beit Yosef writes similarly in this siman.
