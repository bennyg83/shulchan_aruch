(15) Many—and same in one group when each offers him cup—for each cup is reconsideration as below.

(16) Reconsidered—does not know if they will offer another cup; even if offered another while not finished first cup—same law must return bless separately since at time of blessing did not have intent for this; all this in stam; but if at blessing his intent blessing covers all they will give—need not bless each cup though did not know then if they will give—intent helps; where custom many cups for one entering fixed drinking party—even blessed stam on one cup we say intent on all will give, need not bless each cup; nevertheless better intend at blessing on all will give.
