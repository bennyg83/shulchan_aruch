(4) (Levush) And some say with let us bless, etc.—Shulchan Aruch language; eating law like drinking for Rosh; but Rabbenu Yonah and Ran eating is different, etc. Seems from his words to rule like Rabbenu Yonah and Ran for Rosh alone against them; so Lechem Chamudos 51. I wonder at Beit Yosef—for Tosafot ch. How to Bless 42b s.v. there is, etc., and s.v. immediately wrote explicitly like Rosh; so Mordechai, Agudah, Rashba chiddushim, Kelbo 24, Tashbetz siman 314, Rabbenu Yerucham 159, Tzeda LaDerech, Zichron R' Yehuda Kohen, Avudraham there explicitly—if so Rabbenu Yonah and Ran certainly alone. Also wonder Beit Yosef whence Ran like Rabbenu Yonah—for he wrote in blood cover chapter R' Meshulam: regarding reconsidering must return bless on slaughter—not similar to sitting eating in meal—for even if decided not to eat does not return and cut as we say even finished even removed permitted eat until takes hands—for there is establishment for eating and sometimes sits small meal continuing to large; slaughter has no establishment, therefore reconsidered returns blesss—end quote. If so possibly Ran holds eating forbidden like drinking—all who said let us bless or took hands; removing table alone not diversion; therefore answers slaughter—we hold removing alone is diversion because slaughter has no establishment; understand. Beit Yosef understood he speaks of drinking has no establishment therefore forbidden with let us bless—I do not know whence from Ran's words above clear no distinction, as plain sugya; besides Beit Yosef very strained in explaining Ran see there; for what I wrote welfare for Ran's teaching. Again found Rashba blood cover wrote also Ran's language only instead of word but slaughter no establishment—seems Beit Yosef had this version in Ran causing strain; so Maadanei Melech 52 implies. In my humble opinion clear version mistaken but for drinking who mentions it; besides harsh on Beit Yosef—for Ran wrote let us bless birkat hamazon—if so perforce discusses meal establishment—for to sweeten drink we hold siman 212 no blessing before or after; he himself wrote drinking follows eating; if eating permitted so drinking; anyway his words strained. Correct truth version before me but in slaughter as I explained; must emend Rashba chiddushim. Clear proof: else Rashba contradicts—for I brought in How to Bless even with let us bless forbidden in eating, and wrote worse than netilat yadayim—clear as Rashi explained. Therefore practically should not eat if said let us bless until blesses before him; see siman 196 seif 1. Magen Avraham end siman 208 wrote needs also final blessing, birkat hamazon does not exempt—for not worse than continuing hand from bread siman 177 seif 2. In my opinion can distinguish there—even if food remains drawn from bread, not so in reconsidering like returning to bread—seems Shelah's view there.
