Seif katan 4, that the host should say, etc. This applies specifically where the guests remained silent and agreed. The host has no greater authority over a guest than the guest has over himself. Even if the guest said, “Bring us the cup and let us recite Bircas HaMazon,” his presumed intent is that if the host offers him more food, he will eat. But once the host says it, the host himself may not eat even if the guest wishes to continue, since the host is not dependent upon the guest. Nevertheless, if the guest intends to continue eating, he may do so even though the host said, “Bring us the cup and let us recite Bircas HaMazon.”
