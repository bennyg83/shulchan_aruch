(s.k. 4) And how, etc.—Beit Yosef wrote Rosh in ch. Ayin Peh appears to dispute that must bless on cup and supports—hear now as birkat hamazon interruption, so since impossible drink and recite together must return bless after Hagadah and Hallel end; Hagadah and Hallel not removal and diversion from what ate and drank yet must bless on cup in Pesach Haggadah—so too praying in meal per R' Yitzchak; nevertheless world not practiced like Rif cannot bless every Hagadah cup—we do not hold like him in prayer in meal but like Rosh. Wonder our custom like Rif as Rama 474 Ashkenaz and most geonim—we hold halacha like Rif though no removal and finish must bless if impossible matter pray and eat; should rule R' Yitzchak—cannot rule against settled law prayer not interruption; in this not explicit in Tosafot Tur Shulchan Aruch—if all uprooted went pray in synagogue considered finish and removal and interruption—practically be careful not say no interruption except same room or left friends.
