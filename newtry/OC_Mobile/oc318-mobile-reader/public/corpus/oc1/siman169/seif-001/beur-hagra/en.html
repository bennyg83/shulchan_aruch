Seif 1: Anything, etc., immediately. Kesubos 61a: “They delay everything,” etc. This is the meaning of “immediately,” and there it states the general rule, etc.
And as an act of piety, etc. There, regarding Avahu bar Ihi, etc., and Rashi, s.v. vechad, etc. This is the meaning of “immediately, from every,” etc. Rashi needed to explain it this way, because it cannot mean entirely from one kind, for we say that they leave over, etc., as written in siman 170, seif 3.
And specifically, etc. This is why they said, “to the attendant,” and it is evident from Avodah Zarah.
And it is forbidden, etc. This follows the Rif's reading there, which omits “into his mouth.” See Beit Yosef.
