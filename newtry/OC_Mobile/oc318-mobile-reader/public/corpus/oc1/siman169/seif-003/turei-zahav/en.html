All the bread he needs. The Tur does not write “from bread.” It appears that the Shulchan Aruch was precise in doing so. Specifically regarding bread, the scholar will warn the diners to give to the attendant. This does not refer only to bread, but to anything they are obligated to give him, as stated at the beginning of this siman. Regarding something that is merely an act of piety, however, the attendant must recite a blessing over each item they give him, because perhaps they will not act piously toward him, and the scholar will not warn them about something that is not obligatory.
