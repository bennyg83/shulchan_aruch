[3] As though he had newly decided, etc. If, at the time of the first blessing, he intended to include everything they would give him, he need not recite another blessing, as written at the end of siman 177. Magen Avraham. Taz, however, writes at the end of siman 179 that such intent is ineffective, since he has no certainty that they will give him more. The practical halachah requires study.
