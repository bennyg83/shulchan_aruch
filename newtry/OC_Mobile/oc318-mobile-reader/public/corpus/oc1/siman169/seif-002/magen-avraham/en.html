And some are lenient. It appears to me that if one knows with certainty that he will not recite a blessing, it is forbidden. See Beit Yosef. Bach writes: The prohibition applies specifically to handwashing, because at the very moment one gives the food into his hand to eat, he transgresses “before the blind,” etc. With regard to the blessing, however, he commits no transgression when he gives it to him; if the person later fails to recite a blessing, what can we do?
