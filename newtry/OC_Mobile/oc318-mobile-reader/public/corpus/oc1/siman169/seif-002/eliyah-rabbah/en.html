[2] And some are lenient, etc. Lechem Chamudos, Malbushei Yom Tov, and Shelah agree. Shelah concludes that even if the recipient has committed some sins, one should give to him, unless he is wholly wicked or one knows that he does not recite a blessing. Bach, however, writes: Even if one knows that he is a complete ignoramus and will not recite any blessing, one should not therefore neglect the mitzvah of charity. It appears that they do not disagree. Shelah discusses one who does not recite a blessing out of wickedness, and Bach agrees in that case. See above, siman 163.
