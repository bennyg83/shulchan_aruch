Charity. If he knows with certainty that the recipient will neither wash his hands nor recite a blessing, it is forbidden. Bach writes that the prohibition applies specifically to handwashing, because at the very moment he gives the food into the person's hand to eat, he transgresses “Do not place a stumbling block before the blind.” With regard to the blessing, however, he commits no transgression when he gives it to him; if the person later fails to recite a blessing, what can we do?
