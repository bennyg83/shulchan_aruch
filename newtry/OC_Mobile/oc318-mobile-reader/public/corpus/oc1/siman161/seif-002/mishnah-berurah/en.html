(א) Anything, etc. — meaning if it is in a small amount only; although the Mechaber already mentioned this at the end of the previous seif, he repeated it here because this entire seif is Rashba's words as brought in Beit Yosef, and he wanted to copy them in his language.

(ב) And his hands were dyed — meaning part of the place of netilah; for if most were dyed we hold that even if he is not fastidious it interposes. In the latter part, regarding women accustomed to dye their hands for ornament — even if their whole hands are dyed, since they intend to do so and want it, it is like their body itself and does not interpose.

(3) Dye does not, etc. — and likewise if he is a slaughterer and there is blood on his hands it does not interpose; and if he was selling fat and there is fat on his hands it does not interpose; but after the substance of these things was on his hands it interposes [Acharonim]. If he was both slaughterer and fat-seller and blood and fat are on his hands, Magen Avraham leans to be lenient that it does not interpose.

(ד) That dry ink interposes and moist does not — meaning as there regarding ink that moist does not interpose because it dissolves in water and netilah water reaches his body; likewise colors that are like ink specifically that adhere to him — but if there is no substance, only mere appearance of color, interposition does not apply; and likewise a mark that has no substance does not interpose [Acharonim]. One who writes frequently — even if there is substance of dry ink on his fingers it does not interpose, for he is not accustomed to be fastidious about this [Acharonim].
