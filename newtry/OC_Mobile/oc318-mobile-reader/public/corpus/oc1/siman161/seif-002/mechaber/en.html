Anything one is not fastidious about does not interpose. If this one's custom was to be fastidious and this one's was not — for one whose custom is to be fastidious it interposes; for one whose custom is not to be fastidious it does not interpose. How so? If one was a dyer and his hands were dyed — the dye does not interpose on his hands even if there is substance of dyes on his hands, for he was not a dyer. If his hands were dyed and there is substance of dye on his hands — this interposes; dry ink interposes and moist does not. And likewise women who are accustomed to dye their hands for ornament and the like — that dye does not interpose. ((Scabs of a wound on his hands — if he is not fastidious about them they do not interpose) (Sefer HaTerumah).
