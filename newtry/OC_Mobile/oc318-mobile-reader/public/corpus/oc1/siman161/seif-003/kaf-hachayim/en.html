(א) There — anything that interposes during immersion, etc. The Bach wrote that one must be careful even regarding interposition of any small matter, as with immersion; therefore the Tur wrote afterward, "If he has dough under the nail," etc., because it is a light interposition and very common, so he warned about it more. For other light interpositions that are not so common, he relied on what is explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 198 — see there.
