One must remove the ring from his hand at the time of netilat yadayim. And even if it is loose (Beit Yosef); and even if he is not fastidious about it at netilah, since he is fastidious about it when doing work that they not get dirty (Rosh, chapter Tinoket). Some are accustomed to be lenient if it is loose; but one should be stringent, for we are not expert what is called loose.
