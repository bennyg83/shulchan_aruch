(א) The joint of the forearm. Explanation: immediately after completing the hand is called the joint of the forearm, and netilah is required up to the start of that joint — that is, until completing the hand; thus is the Rif.

(ב) And it is proper to conduct, etc. Beit Yosef wrote it is proper to stipulate and say he does not accept this matter as an obligatory duty, lest once he lacks so much water he need not be stringent as his custom; and likewise one should stipulate in everything a person wants to be stringent upon himself — end of his words. It seems there is concern in this stringency because onlookers will think the essential law is so; and sometimes others who take only per the law will be said to eat without proper netilah, and it will be like two Torahs, Heaven forbid, in their eyes; and they will bring disgrace upon them as stated in the first chapter of Gittin regarding not making a stringency in a get; and likewise in Ashiri, first chapter of Pesachim, regarding the blessing on biur chametz that one may say thus, although it is better to say to destroy, because one must inform that this too is fine and they should not err to say if he blessed thus he did not fulfill — I brought this below siman 467 seif 2. Therefore it appears to me the Shulchan Aruch too did not write thus except if alone, not before many; and in my humble opinion one who is lenient everywhere in this did not lose, since we hold the halacha follows the lenient in netilat yadayim as stated siman 160 seif 11; and this is not in the category of I carry a full pouch of water, etc., for there it deals where netilah is required, unlike where netilah is not required — so it appears to me.
