(20) Up to the joint, etc.: Meaning, up to the end of the palm, which is where the hand joins the forearm.
(21) It is proper to follow, etc.: Meaning, although as a matter of law one who wishes to be lenient in accordance with the second opinion should not be rebuked, since every doubt concerning netilas yadayim is treated leniently, as stated above in siman 160, seif 11, nevertheless, initially it is proper to be stringent in accordance with the first opinion. This is the universal practice. Etzei Almogim demonstrates that many of the great Rishonim hold this way, and one should be careful not to be lenient. See there and Biur Halachah.
(22) In accordance with the first opinion: This implies that in a pressing circumstance, when one has only a limited amount of water, he may rely upon the lenient opinion. The later authorities write that if the palm is soiled with perspiration or the like, all agree that he must wash through the end of the palm.
