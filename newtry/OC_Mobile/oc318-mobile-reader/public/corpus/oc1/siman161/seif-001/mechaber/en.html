Laws of interposition in netilat yadayim. And it has 4
seifim: One must be careful about interposition, for anything that interposes in tevilah interposes in netilat yadayim — such as excrement under the nail not opposite the flesh, and dough under the nail even opposite the flesh, and moisture on his flesh, and Greek mud and potters' mud; but in a small amount that one is not fastidious about, one is not concerned.And therefore they are not accustomed to scrape the mud under the nails for netilat yadayim, because it is like a small amount one is not fastidious about, for people are not fastidious about this for netilat yadayim (Hagahot Semak and Hagahot Ashiri chapter "these matters"). But if he was fastidious he must scrape — and this is essential. And it is possible they are accustomed to be lenient regarding interposition for netilat yadayim because some say interposition is not relevant to netilat yadayim; but the essential law is as below.
