(9) Standing in uchtavtem — and that which [the Gemara] did not say in ukshartem is because there is a difference: in ukshartem the first [occurrence] is with kamatz, which is singular, and the second is with segol, which is plural. And Taz wrote another reason: that there is no way to err in the middle, for one can tell by the time he began to read; but when he is standing at the end there is here a somewhat long time, so he can sometimes err that perhaps he finished the second section.

(10) If he began — meaning, and afterward he became unsure lest he said lema'an yirbu aharekha and uchtavtem in the first section; nevertheless he need not return, for presumably he said according to habit lema'an yirbu in the second [section] and uchtavtem in the second; and all the more if he found himself at a different word in the middle of the section, there is a presumption that he said everything above it and did not skip a verse or word [Acharonim].

(11) He need not return — all this is when reading alone; but if he reads with the congregation and sees that the shaliach tzibbur is standing in uchtavtem of the first section or near it, he should return to uchtavtem of the first [section], for it is evident he erred and did not take the chain and go [continue in order]; and likewise on the other side: if he is standing in uchtavtem that is stated in Shulchan Arukh that one returns to the first uchtavtem — if he sees the shaliach tzibbur and the congregation standing in uchtavtem of the second section or near it, before him or after him, he should also say afterward the verse lema'an yirbu, for presumably he also read the second [section] like them, and support for the matter exists. And the law if he changed in any blessing from what the Sages accustomed it, that Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Rambam, will be explained there in siman 68 in Mishnah Berurah — see there.
