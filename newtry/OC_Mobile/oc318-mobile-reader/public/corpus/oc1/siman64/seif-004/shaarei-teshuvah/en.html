And you shall write them. See Baer Heitev. Shalmei Tzibbur cites Maamar Mordechai: If the reader is before or after the congregation and they are reciting the second 'And you shall write them,' he should recite the second one with them. Conversely, if he began 'So that your days may be increased' while the congregation is at the first 'And you shall write them,' it is evident that he erred rather than merely continuing by habit; see there.
