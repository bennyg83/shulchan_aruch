Uchtavtem. And that which [the Gemara] did not say in ukshartem is because there is a difference: in ukshartem the first [occurrence] is with kamatz, which is singular, and the second is with segol, which is plural.

Sirakhei nekatei ve'ata. And all the more if he found himself at a different word in the middle of the section — there is a presumption that he said everything above it [Beit Yosef Rashba]. Rambam wrote in chapter 1 of Keriat Shema: as a general rule, anyone who deviates from the format that the Sages fixed for blessings — he is mistaken and returns and blesses in the format. Kesef Mishnah wrote in chapter 1 of Berachos: specifically if he opened the blessing in a place they did not ordain to open, or closed in a place they said not to close; but if he changed the wording of the blessing and did not negate that very word, but rather said a different formula on the matter of the blessing, he has fulfilled [his obligation]. See siman 59 and siman 387.
