Beit Yosef in the name of the Rashba. The Rambam writes in chapter 1 of Hilchos Krias Shema: The general rule is that anyone who departs from the formula established by the Sages for blessings has erred and must repeat the blessing according to the prescribed formula. The Kesef Mishneh writes in chapter 1 of Hilchos Berachos that this applies specifically if he began with a blessing where the Sages did not institute an opening blessing, or concluded where they said not to conclude with a blessing. But if he altered the wording of the blessing and did not recite that exact text, but used another formulation concerning the subject of the blessing, he has fulfilled his obligation.
