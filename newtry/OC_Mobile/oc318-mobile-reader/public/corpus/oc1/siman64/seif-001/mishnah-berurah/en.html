(א) He has not fulfilled [his obligation] — for it is stated, "And these words shall be," etc., "they shall be" in their being; and likewise the section of vayomer — when the Sages ordained to say it for the mitzvah of remembering the Exodus from Egypt, they likewise ordained it in this manner.

(ב) The verses — and all the more so if he transposed the words [Peri Megadim].

(ג) That it is not adjacent, etc. — meaning that it is only a rabbinic enactment that they ordered thus, as they said: why does Shema precede v'haya im shamoah — so that he accept upon himself the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven first, and afterward accept upon himself the yoke of the commandments, etc.
