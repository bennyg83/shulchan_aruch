(comment 3) That they are accustomed, etc. — and all the more so in "and you shall find," etc. Meaning: behold even in "and you shall write them" which implies that they are accustomed — he says "in order that they multiply" after the verse "and you shall write them" in the first section; and if so it implies he had not yet read only section one; nevertheless we say presumably he said in order also the second section; all the more so in another word.
Rambam wrote, etc. — and Kesef Mishneh chapter 1 of Berakhot wrote, etc., to resolve that the words of Rambam should not contradict one another — what he wrote in chapter 1 of Berakhot with what he wrote in chapter 1 of Keriat Shema.
