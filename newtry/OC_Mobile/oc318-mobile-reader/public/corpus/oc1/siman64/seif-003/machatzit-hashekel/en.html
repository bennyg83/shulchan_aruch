(Note 3) On sirakhei, etc. — and all the more when he finds himself, etc. — meaning that even regarding uchtavtem that is stated in sirakhei nekatei lo lema'an yirbu after the verse uchtavtem in the first section — and if so it would seem he had not yet read only the first section — nevertheless we say that from the unstated case he said in order also the second section; all the more so at a different word.

Rambam wrote, etc. And Kesef Mishnah wrote in chapter 1 of Berachos, etc., to reconcile so that the words of Rambam do not contradict one another — what he wrote in chapter 1 of Berachos with what he wrote in chapter 1 of Keriat Shema.
