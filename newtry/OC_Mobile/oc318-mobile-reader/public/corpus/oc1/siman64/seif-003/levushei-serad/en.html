Magen Avraham seif katan 3 See siman 59, where it is evident that if the prescribed formula is lengthy and he shortened it, he has not fulfilled his obligation, as the Magen Avraham writes there in seif katan 1. In siman 187, however, the Shulchan Aruch in seif 1 and the Magen Avraham in seif katan 1 cite two opinions on this matter.
