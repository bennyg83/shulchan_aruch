(3) That verse, etc. — or one word. Levush Yom Tov wrote this — his language: the language of the Rosh and of the Tur is not so, and it appears he always returns to the beginning of the verse; and in Kessef Mishneh I found it implies somewhat like the words of Levush — until here his language. However, from Tosafot [Berakhot] folio 16 and Agudah and Semak and Avudraham it appears that even if he skipped a word he begins from the beginning of the verse.
