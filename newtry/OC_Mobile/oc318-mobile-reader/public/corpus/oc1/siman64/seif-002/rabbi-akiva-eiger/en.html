Seif 2 He returns to the beginning of that verse. If he omitted a word, some say he returns to the beginning of that word, while others say he always returns to the beginning of the verse. See Eliyah Rabbah, siman 65, seif katan 3.
Magen Avraham seif katan 1 He returns to the beginning. Since he is uncertain, it follows that he certainly did not concentrate; see there. See Eshel Avraham at the beginning of siman 91, who asks from the law that one who is uncertain whether he prayed does not repeat the prayer. Since he is uncertain, it follows that he certainly did not concentrate, and he should therefore be required by the law of the Gemara to pray again; see there.
