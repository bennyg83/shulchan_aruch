{Rama: Magen Avraham note 1 — siman 63, se'if 4.} His intent is to what is stated there in Magen Avraham note 6: if he remembered after he finished that he did not read the first verse with intention, he must return to the head because if he reads only the first verse it is reading out of order; and he wrote there that thus it implies in siman 64 — and that is the meaning of the words of Beit Chadash: even if he knows he read the whole first section, only if he is in doubt whether he said Shema with intention he returns to the head, etc., the whole section — and that is because lack of intention in Shema is as if he did not read at all and it is reading out of order; and if so, all the more when he remembers he certainly did not have intention — and let it be clear.
