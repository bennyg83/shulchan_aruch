Magen Avraham seif katan 1 Siman 63, seif 4. His intent is to what he wrote there in Magen Avraham seif katan 6, that if after finishing he remembers that he did not read the first verse with concentration, he must return to the beginning, because if he reads only the first verse he will be reading out of sequence. He writes there that this is also implied by siman 64. This refers to these words of the Bach, which imply that even if he knows that he read the entire first paragraph, but is uncertain whether he said Shema with concentration, he returns to the beginning and reads the entire paragraph. This is because lack of concentration during Shema is equivalent to not having read it at all, and he would otherwise be reading out of sequence. All the more so, if he remembers that he definitely did not concentrate, the same applies. This is clear.
