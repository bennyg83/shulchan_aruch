Siman 62, 'if he knows,' etc. — Tosafot there, entry 'one who reads,' etc.; and so in Tosefta chapter 2: one who reads Shema out of order has not fulfilled; and so in Hallel; and so in prayer; and so in Megillah: one who reads Shema and erred and omitted one verse in it should not return and read that verse separately, but begins from that verse and finishes to the end; and so in Hallel; and so in prayer; and so in Megillah: one who enters a synagogue and finds them having read half and he finishes with them should not return and read from the beginning until that place, but begins from the beginning and finishes to the end; and so in Hallel; and so in prayer; and so in Megillah: one who reads Shema and erred and does not know, etc., as stated in the Gemara there. And from here it appears at first glance not like Rambam, who said 'omitted,' etc., 'one who enters,' etc. — even in the first section; and nevertheless he said 'to the end,' and compared it to prayer, Hallel, and Megillah.
