One who read a section and erred, etc. — he returns to the head of the section; and if he is in the first section he returns to v'ahavta, because we establish it on its presumption that he certainly knows the Shema verse was read with intention as required by law. And if he knows he did not read it with intention, he returns to the beginning of Keriat Shema and reads in order, even if he knows where he erred, so that it should not be out of order (Beit Chadash; Maharam of Italy).
