If it is half and half, etc., and some invalidate. See the Magen Avraham, who asked: if so, why did he write in se'if 10 that if all of it is on one side it is valid? It is half and half. In the book Bigdei Yesha he answered simply that there it is speaking where they stand on a minority and do not occupy half, while here it is speaking where they occupy exactly half, since exact measurement is possible; see there.
