Half and half, etc. In the Tur it is written: my brother, Rabbi Yechiel, wrote that since it is in his hand, it is possible to be exact, and it is valid. But the Ra'avad wrote: all invalidations that invalidate when they affect the majority are invalid at half and half, for it is impossible to be exact, as we require a majority visible to the eyes. End quote. In my humble opinion it appears that one should invalidate it for another reason, for the inferences contradict each other. The mishnah teaches: if boil-like discoloration arose on most of it, it is invalid; on a minority of it, it is valid. From the first clause it is difficult, for it implies that half is valid; and from the latter clause it implies that half is invalid. Therefore we go stringently. We find something similar in the Rosh, chapter Behemah HaMaksheh, regarding that which the mishnah teaches: from the knee and above it is a tereifah. The Rosh wrote that one can infer from the first clause the opposite of the inference from the latter clause, and we go stringently. So too here we go stringently, and it is invalid at half and half. This appears correct.
