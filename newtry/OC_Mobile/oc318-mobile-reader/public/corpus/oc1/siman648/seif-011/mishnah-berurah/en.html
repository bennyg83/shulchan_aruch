If it is half and half. This is not speaking of where half the esrog on one side, from its head to its tail, has discoloration, for on its head alone boil-like discoloration invalidates even on a minority, as above in se'if 9. Rather, it is speaking where the half is toward the stem around the entire circumference of the esrog.
Some validate. They hold that exact measurement is possible; and those who invalidate hold that exact measurement is impossible. In practice, one should be stringent like the latter view, since the Mechaber mentioned it last. See the Peri Megadim, who wrote that it is not enough if a majority of valid area is found by measurement; rather, it must be visible to the eyes without measurement. Bikkurei Yaakov disagrees with this; see there.
