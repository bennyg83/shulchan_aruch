Section 11. If it is, etc. Like a breach equal to the standing wall, even though we rule like the Rabbis who say that exact measurement is impossible, as stated in Bekhorot 17, Gittin 78, and Shevuot 32. That applies to matters done by Heaven, but in matters done by human hands exact measurement is possible, from that which is stated regarding a breach equal to the standing wall, and in the third chapter of Chullin: half and half, etc. There they say regarding tereifot that half and half is not like a majority to render it tereifah; rather, we require a majority visible to the eyes. Here we compare it to tereifot. And even though in Bekhorot 18a we conclude that according to the Rabbis even in matters done by human hands exact measurement is impossible, nevertheless regarding matters done by human hands we rule like Rabbi Yose HaGelili. See Tosafot there, who elaborated on this, and in the Rosh there, who concludes as I wrote; see there, chapter 2, se'if 4. See Tosafot at the end of the first chapter of Eruvin, and Chullin 28b, s.v. lefi, etc., and Yevamot 28a, s.v. lo, etc., and infer from there.
And some invalidate. For they hold that the Rabbis maintain that even in matters done by human hands exact measurement is impossible, as stated in the first chapter of Eruvin: what will you say, that the two are equal? It is a doubt, etc. This implies that it is impossible to clarify. They hold that we rule entirely like the Rabbis, even in matters done by human hands. And that case in Chullin, they conclude there that according to all opinions half and half is not like a majority, because we require a majority visible to the eyes; and this is because exact measurement is impossible. This is what the Ra'avad wrote, that it is impossible to be exact, because we require a majority visible to the eyes. The case of a breach equal to the standing wall is because this is how the Merciful One taught it, etc.: that it should not appear as a majority visible to the eyes that is breached. With this the question of Tosafot in Chullin there, s.v. de-kulei alma, is resolved. Likewise by tereifot, half is valid because this is how they received the tradition, that we require a majority visible to the eyes. So too was the initial assumption regarding slaughter, that this is what the Merciful One said: do not leave over a majority visible to the eyes, as stated there.
