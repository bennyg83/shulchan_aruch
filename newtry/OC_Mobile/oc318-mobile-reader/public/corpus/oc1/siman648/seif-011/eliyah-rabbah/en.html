And some say, etc., the size of a sela, etc. This also appears to be Bach's conclusion. It further appears to me that one who examines Sefer Temim Deim there will see that the “some say” maintain that when most of the peel was removed it is invalid even though it retains the appearance of an esrog, contrary to Rosh's understanding of Raavan. Nevertheless, as a matter of practical halachah, he agrees that it is valid if an area the size of a sela remains.
