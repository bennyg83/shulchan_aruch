9) [Seif 2] An esrog that was pierced by a through-and-through hole, etc. If the thick peel was pierced from one side to the other, it is considered through-and-through according to all opinions. Magen Avraham, s.k. 2; Eliyah Rabbah, sec. 6; Chayei Adam, klal 151, sec. 2; Beit HaShoevah, sec. 8. See below, sec. 24.
