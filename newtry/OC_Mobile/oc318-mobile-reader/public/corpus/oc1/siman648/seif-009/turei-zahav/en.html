In two or three places. The Kesef Mishneh wrote as follows: It has always been difficult for me, both in the Gemara and in the poskim: if two invalidate, is three a question? I did not find any author who says anything about this, and it requires investigation. End quote. In my humble opinion, this is comparable to what we say generally: if testimony is established with two, why did the verse specify three? Rather, it compares three to two, etc. So too here: certainly if there are two patches of boil-like discoloration adjacent to each other, but there is a little appearance of esrog between them like a hairbreadth, we do not say it is invalid, for because of this it does not appear spotted. Rather, there must be some measure between them. That is, if there are three patches of discoloration, according to the measure between the two outer ones, where the middle patch of discoloration is, so too if there are only two and there is an empty space between them of that measure, then it appears spotted. This appears correct in my humble opinion.
