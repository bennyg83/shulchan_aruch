In several places he wrote: and these are his words — it was always difficult for me, whether in the Gemara or in the poskim: now two invalid, three uncertain — and I did not find any author anything on this; requires further study, end of his words. And in my humble opinion: it is an example of what we say in general — if testimony is established with two, why does Scripture specify three? Rather it compares three to two, etc.; so too here it is certain: if there are two chazazit adjacent to each other but there is some etrog appearance between them like a hair's breadth — we do not say it should be invalid, because for this reason it does not appear speckled; rather there must be some measure between them — and this is if there are 3 chazazit per the measure that is between the two outer ones from one to the other, where the middle chazazit is; so too if there are two alone and between them empty space of this measure, then it appears speckled; as seems to me, correct:
