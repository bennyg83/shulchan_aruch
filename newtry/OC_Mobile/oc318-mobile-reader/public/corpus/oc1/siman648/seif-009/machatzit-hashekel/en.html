(Seif katan 9) Unless, etc. It appears to me that at minimum it must be removed down to the body of the esrog. This implies that according to Ran even such a case is kosher, unless the part shaped like a pestle in a mortar, which enters into the esrog, was removed and a depression is visible there.
See siman 649, Magen Avraham, seif katan 17.
