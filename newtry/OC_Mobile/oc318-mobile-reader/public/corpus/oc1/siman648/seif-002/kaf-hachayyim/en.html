2) Ibid. A dried esrog is invalid. The invalidation of dryness in an esrog applies only when most of it is dry, as with a lulav and hadas, where dryness invalidates only when it affects most of the leaves. If, however, the dryness is at its chotem, the Rosh and Ran wrote that just as even the slightest blister invalidates at the chotem, so too does dryness, and likewise all invalidations that disqualify an esrog because it lacks beauty. Mateh Yosef, vol. 2, Likkutim to Shulchan Aruch, 62b; Beit HaShoevah, sec. 5. See below, seif 12, and our words there, with God's help.
