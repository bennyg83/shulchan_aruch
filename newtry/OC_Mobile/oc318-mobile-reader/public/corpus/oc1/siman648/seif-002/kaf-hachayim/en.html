2) There. A dry etrog is invalid. Disqualification of dryness on an etrog disqualifies only on its majority, like lulav and hadas, for we do not disqualify in them except on the majority of leaves. But if the dryness is on its pitom, the Rosh and Ran wrote that just as chazazit disqualifies on its pitom in any amount, so too the law with dryness, and all disqualifications that disqualify on an etrog are because of hadar.
