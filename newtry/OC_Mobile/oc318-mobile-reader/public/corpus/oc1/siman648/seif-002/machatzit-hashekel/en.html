(Seif katan 2) Passing through, etc. It is considered a through-perforation according to all opinions. In seif 3 there is a dispute among the poskim. The first opinion maintains that passing through means from one side to the other. Others explain that reaching the seed chambers is enough to be called passing through. One might have thought that according to all opinions the perforation must reach the seed chambers, except that according to the first opinion it must reach the seed chambers and then continue through the entire esrog until it exits on the opposite side, whereas according to the second opinion reaching the seed chambers alone is sufficient. Thus, if the thick peel was perforated from side to side in an area not opposite the seed chambers, one might think that according to all opinions it is not called passing through. Magen Avraham therefore writes that this is incorrect. The reverse is true: in such a case, according to all opinions, even the first opinion, it is called passing through, because it passes from side to side even though there are no seed chambers there.
