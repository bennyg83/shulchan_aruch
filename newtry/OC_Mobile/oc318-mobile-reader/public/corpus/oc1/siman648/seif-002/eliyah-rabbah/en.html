Levush: A needle with a thread in it, etc. Beit Yosef emends the text in this manner. Rabbeinu Yerucham, however, explicitly writes, “a needle or a thread.” If there is moisture in it, this will be visible upon the needle or thread. Thus far his words. He refers to a stiff thread. The difficulty according to the authority in seif 2, who disqualifies a perforation that passes through even where nothing is missing, was already resolved by Tosafos Yom Tov. The phrase “a needle passes through it” does not mean from one side to the other. Bach likewise implies this. Rather, one inserts the needle slightly and withdraws it from the same side. Raavad himself writes in Sefer Tamim De’im: “A needle passes into it, and if it contains moisture, the moisture immediately emerges through the puncture.” Thus far his words. Accordingly, there is no need to pierce it from one side to the other, because the moisture immediately emerges through the opening, and there is no need to identify moisture upon the needle. Taz, however, answers that if the puncture penetrates from one side, meaning through its thickness, it does not disqualify according to any opinion. Thus far his words. This requires study.
