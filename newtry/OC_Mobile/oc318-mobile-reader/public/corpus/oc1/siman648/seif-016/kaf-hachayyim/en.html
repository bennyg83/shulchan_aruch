Some say that even with a through-and-through hole, a deficiency is required, etc. This is the view of the Raavad, who holds that two defects are required, a through-and-through hole and a deficiency. Without a deficiency it is valid in every case. Where there is a deficiency, however, there is a distinction: if the hole is through-and-through, it is invalid even if only the slightest amount is missing; if it is not through-and-through, it is invalid only if the deficiency is the size of an issar. The established halacha is to be stringent, except in pressing circumstances, as the Rama writes afterward. Taz, s.k. 3; Mishna Berurah, sec. 9.
