(Seif katan 16) A chazazis invalidates. Like two small blisters, etc. I saw in Kappos Temarim that he cites this responsum of Mabit. Mabit proves it from Rashi's explanation of the Mishnah's phrase, 'A chazazis arose upon it.' Rashi writes, 'like small blisters.' Since Rashi uses the plural 'blisters,' at least two are required. One blister therefore does not invalidate, and even on the chotem it is kosher. Kappos Temarim disagrees, maintaining that the Gemara implies that on the chotem even one blister of any size invalidates. Rashi used the plural only because he described the usual case and because he was explaining the Mishnah, which discusses a chazazis not on the chotem and therefore requires a majority. It is uncommon for a single blister to spread over most of the esrog, so Rashi described the usual case of many blisters, though they are very close together and therefore still considered one place. He also writes that Kenesses HaGedolah rejected Mabit's inference, and he likewise refutes Mabit's proof from the wording of the Targum. See there.
