Section 16. If it is, etc. There it means spotted, and he explains that it is with invalid colors, such as black or white; but if it is of the appearance of an esrog, or valid, it is valid. This follows from what is written regarding hadas: Rava said to him, two, etc., rather, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. minei, etc.; this shows that spottedness from its own appearances does not invalidate. And it says there that red ones are like black ones, for Rabbi Chanina said, etc. Why do we need Rabbi Chanina's reason? In any event, it is not of the appearance of hadas. This is what the Rosh wrote, that boil-like discoloration is not of the appearance of an esrog, for otherwise we would not say: but in two, etc. If so, that which is taught regarding a black or white one is that even in one place it is invalid on most of it, like boil-like discoloration, which in two or three places is invalid because it is spotted, and in one place on most of it.
