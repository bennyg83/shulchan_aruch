21) [Seif 3] Meaning, it was pierced from one side to the other. Like an alleyway open at both ends. Biur HaGra; Mishna Berurah, sec. 13. If it was pierced only from one side, however, even if it reached the cavity, it is not considered through-and-through and is valid. Beit Yosef in the name of Halachos Gedolos; Levush.
