(There, se'if 21) A green one that resembles the grasses of the field is invalid, etc. Note: regarding the law of a green esrog, see the poskim and the Taz. I found in Tikkunei Zohar, tikkun 21, Ostroh edition, daf 35, this language: and if she is green, she is more praiseworthy, in the image in which Esther was greenish; Esther was. End quote. One should not say that the intent is a yellow appearance, for Esther is called Hadassah after the hadas, as written in the holy Zohar, and hadas is green. And even if we explain his intent as yellow appearance, in any case, from the fact that he wrote that if it is green it is praiseworthy, it is proven that in any case if it is not green it is also valid; and it is proven at least that a green appearance is also valid. Examine this well.
