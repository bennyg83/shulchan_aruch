That it resembles, etc. See the Mishnah Berurah, where we wrote regarding grafted esrogim. This is the language of Bikkurei Yaakov: it is obvious that the esrogim that grow among us in the lands of Ashkenaz in the gardens of nobles are all grafted. But those that come from Italy and are of doubtful grafted status, seemingly they should be valid if it is impossible to decide, for we follow the majority, and most esrogim in the world are known not to be grafted. However, in practice this requires investigation. In any case, where there is doubt whether it is grafted, if he has no other, he should take it without a blessing. End quote. In my humble opinion, one can say that this is why several poskim relied on the signs, even though there is not so much proof, as the later poskim wrote: because together with the majority, one may rely on this. Therefore one should not protest against one who relies on this and blesses, as it appears to my humble opinion.
