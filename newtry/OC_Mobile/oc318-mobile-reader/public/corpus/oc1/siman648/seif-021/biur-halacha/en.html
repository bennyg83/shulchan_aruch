That it resembles, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah what we wrote regarding grafted etrogs, and this is the language of Bikkurei Yaakov: it is simple that the etrogs that grow among us in Ashkenaz lands in the gardens of nobles are all grafted; but those that come from Italy and are possibly grafted would apparently be kosher if we cannot determine — we follow the majority, and the majority of etrogs in the world are known not to be grafted; thus for practical halacha requires further study; and in any case if there is doubt of grafting, if he has no other he should take it without a blessing, end of his words.
