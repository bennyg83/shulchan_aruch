Appearance. See Ba'er Hetev. See Responsa Pnei Aryeh, siman 5, regarding those esrogim common among us in which sometimes there is found an appearance as red as actual blood, where from their greenness they turn red, and many times the redness spreads over most of the surface of the esrog or over its nose, etc. At first he tended to invalidate this, because we have not heard that an esrog appearance inclines to red. Then he concluded to validate it, since we have no proof from the Talmud that this is an invalid appearance; even if we decide that it is a change of appearance with respect to esrog, the verse handed the matter to the Sages, and they invalidated black and white, but red, since they did not mention it as invalid, one can say that this appearance too is called beautiful, as with apples, where it is a beautiful appearance. Also, since this is their manner of growth and many commonly are this way, it is the appearance of an esrog, and is no worse than those boil-like discolorations called mol that the Rema of blessed memory brought.
