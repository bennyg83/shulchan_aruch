(1) Seif 3 — mefulesh. There are those who say, etc. Like an open alleyway, and so in the Tosefta — even in a crack we require from side to side, for we recite there at the beginning of Ohalot, and the Rosh brought there chapter 3 mishnah 4: an etrog that was perforated and they plugged it with a reed — it is not a connection, for human connections are not connections, etc. An etrog that split into two is pure and one need not be concerned that something is missing. And the Rosh wrote: and it appears that it split not that it was completely divided but that it was joined, etc. And know that at the beginning it teaches "perforated and perforated," and here it teaches "split," and he wrote: and pure means kosher. And from here is proof for the latter view that seif 2 holds we require a deficiency, for splitting is no worse than piercing, and this is what "and one need not be concerned" means, etc. And this is because we also consider a crack:
