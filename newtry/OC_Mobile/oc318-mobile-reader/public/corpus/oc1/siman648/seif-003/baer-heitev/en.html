The seeds. See Taz, who discusses this at length and concludes that an esrog has three layers. The first is an extremely thin outer layer, like a film. No defect in it invalidates the esrog unless it was entirely peeled away, as stated in seif 6. The second is called the outer peel, meaning the entire thickness of the esrog until the seed chambers, and it has a sharp taste when eaten. Regarding this layer, Rambam maintains that if even a small amount is missing, the esrog is invalid even without a through-perforation, while Raavad requires a through-perforation as explained above. The law of a split, discussed below, also applies to this layer. The third is the inner membrane between the seeds within the esrog itself; see there.
