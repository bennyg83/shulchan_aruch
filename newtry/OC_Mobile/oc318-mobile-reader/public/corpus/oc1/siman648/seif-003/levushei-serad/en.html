The Shiltei Gibborim holds that partial dryness is valid, but we do not rule that way, as the Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 17. Therefore Hagahos Ashri invalidates it. In any event, he brings proof from the Shiltei Gibborim that partial dryness is called withering, and only in that case does Hagahos Ashri invalidate it. He concludes that one can say that although withering is valid, that is specifically in part; and just as with a lung, dryness invalidates even in any amount, while shriveling, which is the withering here, invalidates only when it is entirely affected.
