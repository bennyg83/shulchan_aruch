Two attached to one another. In Sefer HaAruch in the name of Rabbeinu Hai, of blessed memory: the twin of which they spoke has two forms; one is two attached together in their creation, and one is two halves separated above and joined below by any amount.
Valid. Since they were created as two together, attached and joined to one another such that a person cannot separate them, we read it as one fruit, and it does not involve adding to the mitzvah. See the Beit Yosef, where the Semag invalidated it; therefore one should be stringent when possible. [Eliyah Rabbah]
