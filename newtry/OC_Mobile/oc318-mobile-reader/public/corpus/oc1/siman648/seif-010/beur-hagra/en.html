Section 10. Some say, etc. For this is why he returned and taught in the latter clause, 'if boil-like discoloration arose on a minority of it,' to teach that what he wrote in the first clause, 'on most of it,' does not mean literally most of it in one place. Rather, only on a minority is it valid; but if it was scattered over most of it, even if in combination it is only a minority, it is invalid. This is what the Gemara says: they taught this only, etc. But on an actual minority, it is no worse than if all of it were one patch of boil-like discoloration.
And some, etc. According to the plain meaning of the Gemara.
