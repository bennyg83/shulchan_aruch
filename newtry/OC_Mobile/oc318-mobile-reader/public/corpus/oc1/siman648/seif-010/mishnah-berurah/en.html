In the area of the speckles, etc. Meaning, the place of the speckles themselves is the minority.
That all of them are on one side. This implies that if they spread also to the other side, it is called most of it, for it is most of its circumference, even though it is not most of the whole esrog. [Magen Avraham]
Even on a minority. The Levush and Eliyah Rabbah ruled like the first view, and so it appears also from the Peri Megadim, that there is no invalidity until it spreads over most of it. However, if it also spread to the other side of the esrog, we already explained in the previous se'if katan that this is called most of it according to all opinions.
