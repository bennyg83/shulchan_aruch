And any change of appearance. Such as black and white, as explained below in se'if 16.
In any amount. Specifically when it is visible to everyone; but if it is not visible to the eye because of its thinness and one must look closely, this is not the 'any amount' that invalidates.
And there is one who says that the same applies to dryness, etc. So ruled the Eliyah Rabbah and Sha'arei Teshuvah. It is not specifically dryness; the same applies to other invalidations because of beauty.
There in any amount. This means in the place of its nose. But in the other places of the esrog, its law is like boil-like discoloration and change of appearance: in one place its law is on most of it, and if in two or three places, it invalidates even on a minority.
