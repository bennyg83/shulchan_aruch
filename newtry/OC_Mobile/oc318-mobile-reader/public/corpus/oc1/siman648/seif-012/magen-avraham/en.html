A scab invalidates. This means at least something like two blisters, provided that they are visible to the eye when one holds it in his hand, Mabit, part 3, siman 49.
And any change of appearance. This implies specifically a change of appearance, but not other disqualifications. So wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham, path 8, part 3, and the Rosh, unlike the Ran, who invalidates even if any amount of the nose cracked, Darkei Moshe. See seif 5. According to him, if its nose cracked it is invalid all seven days, for this is not hadar, as it seems to me; and the same applies to all disqualifications of an esrog, as the Ran wrote. It requires study regarding the Rav Beis Yosef, who wrote that some say the same applies to dryness. Since he wrote in seif 5 that a crack invalidates there, if so all disqualifications of an esrog are included, and all the more so dryness, which is invalid because of hadar, for in this even the Rosh agrees that it is invalid. It seems to me that he came to exclude the Shiltei Gibborim, who holds that if it did not dry entirely it is called withered and is valid.
Change of appearance. Specifically when it is plainly visible to most people, Mabit, part 3, siman 49.
