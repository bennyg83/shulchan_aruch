Chazazis. This means at least two blister-like growths, and they must be visible to the eye when one holds the esrog in his hand. Mabit, Knesses HaGedolah.
Appearance. This applies only when the discoloration is clearly visible to most people. Mabit. It also applies only where the color changed to an invalid color. If it changed to a valid color, the esrog remains valid. Bach. See Magen Avraham.
