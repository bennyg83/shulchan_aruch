If it was the size of an issar, etc. Regarding the measure of an issar, see Rambam's Commentary to the Mishnah, Chullin, chapter 3, where he wrote that it has the weight of four barley kernels, with appropriate thickness, and is round in shape. Nekudos HaKesef cites his words in Yoreh De'ah, siman 34, on the Rama's gloss to seif 2; see there. See further there in Shulchan Aruch, seif 2, where he wrote in the name of one authority that we are not expert in the measure of an issar, and therefore measure it as the majority of the width of the windpipe's cavity; see there.
