And one should be stringent, etc. Bach writes that there is no basis for leniency unless there is doubt whether the flower was removed or whether the esrog never had one. It is clear that where there is doubt whether the dad was removed, the esrog is invalid, contrary to Olas Shabbos, as I wrote at length in Eliyah Zuta. Shelah writes that if one has a beautiful esrog without a flower and, because of this, takes another esrog that is less beautiful, he is a fool who walks in darkness. Taz writes similarly. I have not seen people practice this way; rather, they rely upon Rama and Bach cited above.
