Section 12. From its nose, etc. As above from the Yerushalmi: its nose is like its majority. This is what he wrote: and any change, etc. See above, se'if 16.
And there is one who says, etc. For the reason that at its nose any amount invalidates is because the beauty of the esrog is there, and dryness is also invalid because of beauty, as stated there; see the Rosh, se'if 20. It appears from the Tur and Shulchan Aruch that the first view disagrees regarding dryness, and holds that at its nose only those things invalidate which invalidate on most of it, as above. And even though a lung that dried even partially is a tereifah, that is when it crumbles under the fingernail; but if it shriveled, it is specifically when all of it shriveled. See Yoreh De'ah 36:13 and 36:14.
