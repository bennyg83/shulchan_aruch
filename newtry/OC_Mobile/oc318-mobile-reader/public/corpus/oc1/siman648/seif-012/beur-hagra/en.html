“The U.S. is covered.” It is the same as the Lord’s Prayer and the Holy One, and all the things of Hashem. And with St. John:

And there is who is. The taste of the doves is circumcised by Hashem’s Word, because of Hashem’s wrath, and it is also in heaven. And from the first token of Hashem’s Word, Hashem’s promise to Abraham, is the same as Hashem’s people, and Hashem’s promise to Abraham, “I am the Lord of Hashem’s people, and I am the Lord of Hashem’s Word, and I am the Lord’s promise of the Lord.”
