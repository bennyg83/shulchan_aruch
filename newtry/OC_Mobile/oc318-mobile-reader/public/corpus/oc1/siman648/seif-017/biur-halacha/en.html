A place where their esrogim are somewhat black, etc. An esrog that is slightly black and grew in other lands where their esrogim are not black at all, and was brought to this place, is invalid even in this place according to Rashi and those who support him, for it is included in 'resembles,' which is what the Gemara said: one that resembles a dark-colored person is invalid. But according to the Rif and the Rambam, this is not mentioned in the Gemara.
