Pickled. See Taz and Magen Avraham, seif katan 22. The same law applies if it was soaked in water for a full twenty-four hours. See Taz, seif katan 2. Bach may maintain that although soaking for twenty-four hours creates a prohibition in other areas of halachah, regarding an esrog this applies only to a law according to the Torah. He would nevertheless agree that immersion in a sharp liquid for the time needed to place it upon the fire and bring it to a boil is considered pickling and invalidates the esrog, as Magen Avraham writes.
