17) We recite: two disqualifications they are — pierced and not missing, or missing any amount and not pierced — as Maran ruled in the first view that he wrote anonymously, for such is his known view. And if pierced and there is no deficiency, even in time of need take it without blessing in our places where we accepted Maran's ruling; but in other places in time of need one may rely to take it and bless.
