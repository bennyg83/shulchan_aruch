The outer peel was peeled. This is the language of the Ran: explanation, that the entire outer peel was peeled, and this is the thick peel that has sharpness when one eats it. This does not appear correct to me, for if so it would be invalid in any case by the law of a deficiency the size of an issar. Furthermore, the Gemara challenges this from Rabbah, who said: an esrog whose surface was removed is like a red date. If all that thick peel was removed, it would not remain like a red date. Therefore it appears that 'peeled' means that the thin peel like a glaze that is on the esrog was removed from it, and now it is like a red date, and there is no deficiency in the body of the esrog at all. End quote. It is also proven from this that the entire thickness of the esrog until the seed chambers is called the outer peel; for otherwise, what is the Ran's difficulty, that it would not be like a red date? Surely it would be like a red date, since some of the flesh of the esrog until the seed chambers would still remain, as I wrote above.
