The outer peel. This means the thin peel that is like a glaze on the esrog, and there is no deficiency in the body of the esrog at all.
That does not make it deficient, etc. For if it was peeled more deeply, even in one place, and any amount was missing from the body of the esrog, it is invalid because of deficiency according to the first view above in se'if 2; and according to the second view, at least if it is deficient the size of an issar.
Green as it is by its nature. If the peeled place differs in appearance from the appearance of the esrog, even if only most of it was peeled it is invalid. All this is specifically when everything was peeled together. But if it was peeled in two or three places, even on a minority of the esrog, if it does not resemble the rest of the esrog it is invalid, for it is like spotted. If it was peeled at the place of its nose, when the peeled place does not resemble the rest of the esrog, even any amount is invalid, as regarding boil-like discoloration below in se'if 9. Some say that one should not be stringent in all this except specifically where the peeled place changed to an invalid appearance, such as black or white; but if it is a valid appearance, one should not invalidate it. In pressing circumstances one may rely on this. See below in se'if 13 in the gloss.
If all of it was peeled, it is invalid. Like an animal whose entire skin was removed, which is a tereifah, for it does not return to health.
That a sela's worth remain. As there regarding an animal, where a sela's worth must remain. So ruled the Bach and Eliyah Rabbah.
