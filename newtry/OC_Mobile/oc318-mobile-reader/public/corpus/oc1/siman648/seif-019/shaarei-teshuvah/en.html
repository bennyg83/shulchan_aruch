And some invalidate. See Ba'er Hetev. See Magen Avraham, who wrote that it is plainly visible to most people. In Eliyah Rabbah he wrote in the name of the Mabit that if it is not visible to the eye because of its thinness, and one must look closely, this is not the 'any amount' that invalidates; see there. In Peri Megadim he explained more: since regarding its nose the reason is that there a person sees more than in other places, this is not so when it requires examination and looking closely, and from a distance it is not visible; even on its nose it does not invalidate; see there.
