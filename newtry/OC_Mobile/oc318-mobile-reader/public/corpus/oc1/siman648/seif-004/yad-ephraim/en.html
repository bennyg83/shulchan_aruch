Taz seif katan 19 — to be bigger as it should be, and meaning that it will grow more like a tree and its fruit will finish; therefore even now it is considered fruit; and this is explained in the words of the Rosh who wrote: and the Rabbis who permit unripe concede regarding less than an egg that it is invalid, for unripe is more than an egg and in the end will finish and is considered fruit; and the words of Shulchan Aruch here are taken from the Tur's words which are from these words of the Rosh. And since I saw emend in Taz "even though in the end it will be bigger" — in truth that is not Taz's intent regarding size measure, only regarding growing from the tree that it will be finished; therefore even though it is still unripe he considers it fruit; and the examiner will see his intent is per the Rosh's words as written; and see in Beit Yosef what he wrote on the words of Semag in this:
