An esrog whose flesh dissolved, etc. This is Rava's question in the Gemara: if signs of tereifah developed in it, what is the law? The question was not resolved, and he ruled leniently. The Rosh wrote the reason: these invalidations are essentially only rabbinic, and therefore the Rif did not bring the question. The Tur wrote: some are stringent, and this is reasonable, since it is a Torah doubt. End quote.
