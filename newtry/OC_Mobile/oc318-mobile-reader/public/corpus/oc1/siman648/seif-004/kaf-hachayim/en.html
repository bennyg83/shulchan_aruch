And he shall examine by passing a needle through it, etc. And it is difficult — for perhaps there will be a mefulesh hole in it, and it is invalid per the Rosh and Tur, and likewise in Shulchan Aruch in seif 2 per the first view; and one can say that mefulesh does not disqualify except when pierced from this side to the opposite side facing it or to the seed chambers; but if the hole on one side is mefulesh, that is, through its thickness, certainly all agree it does not disqualify without deficiency, for we derive etrog laws from treifot of an animal, and in treifot if there is a mefulesh hole in the thickness of the limb such as in the heart or intestines and it does not reach the cavity, certainly there is no treifah; so too here with etrog. Taz seif katan 1. However Machatzit HaShekel wrote to explain the words of Magen Avraham seif katan 1 — that even if one pierces only the thick peel from side to side it is called mefulesh; therefore one must turn the needle and not insert it in the needle-hole but insert the needle with thread on the side of the needle-hole and not pass the needle through to the other side, and again remove the needle with the thread on the same side and look at the thread whether there is moisture in it — if so there is no mefulesh hole; see there, and so too Eliyah Rabbah letter 1 and Levush. And one must be careful that the needle not enter the seed chambers. Eliyah Rabbah there. And in one made face-to-face (dafa dafa) and one of the protrusions was pierced — one can say for all it is kosher, for side-to-side mefulesh means through to the other side, not one protrusion alone.
