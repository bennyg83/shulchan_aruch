18) Ibid., Rama's gloss: The custom is to validate holes made on the tree by thorns, etc. This applies when we see that a skin formed over the esrog at the place of that deficiency, although a depression and missing portion remain there. This is what was permitted. If, however, we see that no skin formed and there is a hole and deficiency there, it is invalid even though it was made by thorns during its growth. Many people err in this matter, thinking that there is a leniency whenever the hole was made by thorns. Taz, s.k. 4; Eliyah Rabbah, sec. 5; Mishbetzos Zahav, sec. 4. R' Yosef Navon there likewise agreed; Machazik Berachah, sec. 3; Beit HaShoevah, sec. 11; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 9; Chayei Adam, klal 151, sec. 2; Mishna Berurah, sec. 10.
