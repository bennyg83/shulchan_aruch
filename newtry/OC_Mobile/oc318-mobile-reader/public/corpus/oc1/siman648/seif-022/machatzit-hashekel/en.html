(Seif katan 22) Pickled in vinegar, etc. In vinegar, even if it did not stay except enough to boil, it is like cooked. See siman 473 seif katan 14 and siman 497 seif katan 3; and in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote: and requires further study, for Shach and Peri Chadash in Yoreh De'ah end of siman 105 concluded that even in vinegar requires from time to time, and so held the Tur, etc., end of his words. And in my humble opinion it escaped me the words of Magen Avraham in siman 647 seif katan 28 that he wrote: and therefore if it stood in a vinegar vessel, etc., enough that they put water on it and it boils — forbidden by law as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 105; and even though Shach wrote there that also in vinegar requires from time to time — one should not be lenient against Shulchan Aruch, for in the Mishnah, etc.; and even though in siman 104 he wrote that also in vinegar requires from time to time — it must be that it deals with vinegar that is not so strong, and so from Rashi and Tosafos there that it deals with beer vinegar; and the same with wine vinegar that is not so strong, etc., end of Magen Avraham's words. And if so one can say Magen Avraham follows his method that agreed with Shulchan Aruch in Yoreh De'ah siman 105, not like Shach and Peri Chadash; and even though Magen Avraham here wrote anonymously and did not write that it deals specifically with strong wine vinegar — this is a difficulty, for also in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 105 he wrote anonymously "vinegar"; and nevertheless Magen Avraham explained it deals with strong wine vinegar, for such is the anonymous vinegar the poskim mention in most places:
