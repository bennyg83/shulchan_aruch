Less than an egg-bulk. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, siman 181, that an egg-bulk of our time is enough. Even though eggs have become smaller, as the Tzelach testified in chapter Arvei Pesachim, everything follows the time: if eggs have changed, the growth of esrogim has also changed; see there. According to the view of Bikkurei Yaakov, it is proper for one who is careful in mitzvot to seek ab initio an esrog as large as two egg-bulks.
Invalid. All seven days, because its fruit has not finished developing and it is not called a fruit. Even if it had the required measure and during the festival it shriveled and stood at less than an egg-bulk, nevertheless it is invalid because it is not beautiful. [Chayei Adam] If he has no other, he should take it without a blessing, even on the first day. [Bikkurei Yaakov; see there]
Even if it is unripe, etc. See Sha'arei Teshuvah, who brought in the name of the Chacham Tzvi that he challenged the words of the Beit Yosef, who wrote that one can say the Semag holds like what Tosafot wrote at the end of their words, etc. The Chacham Tzvi challenged him that Tosafot's intent is not so; see there. Now however the intent of Tosafot's words may be, nevertheless his reasoning is found in the Shibbolei HaLeket before us in the name of Rabbeinu Shlomo, that because it will eventually grow, it is considered even as large as a white bean; see there. However, in practice we may not move from the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch.
