Some explain that once it was pierced as far as the seed chambers, etc. Even though the seed chambers and the opposite side of the esrog remain intact, this is called a through-and-through hole. The thick peel surrounding the seed chambers is not considered one entity together with the seed chambers, but rather an independent entity for this purpose. Once the entire peel was pierced from one side, it is a through-and-through hole. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 6; Mishna Berurah, sec. 14.
