Any size. Meaning, even if it is so large that he carries it on his shoulder, he has also fulfilled the obligation, since when he has all of the four species they do not prevent one another, and he can take the lulav and afterward the esrog.
Valid. Provided it is not green like leek, for then it is not considered a finished fruit whenever it does not return to the appearance of an esrog, as above in se'if 21. The Semag wrote: it is prohibited to cheapen an esrog and other mitzvah items that non-Jews sell. End quote. Meaning, one must give them a little more than their value, but not a lot, as above in siman 39:7; see there. The intent of the Semag is that one should not cheapen it, because you cause him to stumble in the future, so that he will not bring them another time for purchase from him. [Acharonim]
