Magen Avraham se'if katan 4 And one should examine carefully. In my humble opinion, they disagree about the foundation of the law: if it is revealed before Heaven that the seed chambers remain intact, is it valid? The practical difference would be where he had only this esrog and was obligated to take it without a blessing. Yet there is no practical difference in that case, for even according to those who invalidate, nevertheless in pressing circumstances one takes all invalid items without a blessing, as below in siman 649:6. But if after an hour an esrog became available to him, according to those who validate, he takes the valid esrog but cannot bless, for perhaps in the esrog that he had taken the seed chambers remained intact and he already fulfilled the obligation with it; while according to those who invalidate, he blesses on the valid one. However, this is not correct, for those who invalidate do so only out of doubt, because it is an unresolved question. If so, he cannot bless on the valid one, for perhaps he fulfilled the obligation with the first esrog. Rather, one can say the practical difference is straightforward: if after he took this esrog he cut it open and it was found that the seed chambers remained intact, and afterward another esrog became available to him, then according to the first view, that it is valid, he does not need the second one at all; and according to the second view, he must return and take the valid one out of doubt, lest the first one was invalid, and he takes the second without a blessing.
