He will see it on the thread. The Acharonim ask that inserting the needle would create a through-hole, which is invalid according to the first opinion in seif 2 even without any missing substance. Magen Avraham answers that one inserts the needle only slightly into the thickness at the opening of the existing hole, so that a little of the thread enters with it. When the needle is withdrawn, moisture can be seen on the thread without creating a through-hole. Others explain that a through-hole means one piercing across the width of the esrog from side to side. A hole running lengthwise from the stem toward the tip without reaching the seed chambers is not considered a through-hole according to any opinion.
