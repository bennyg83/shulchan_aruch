Dry. Abir HaTur, and see in Magen Avraham in the name of Shulchan Aruch HaGedolah: chazazit that rises detached is only because of dryness and shriveled and kosher; and therefore partial shriveled is kosher; and see below seif katan 17 that he wrote that one can say in Shulchan Aruch, and there are those who say dry likewise — meaning to exclude from Shulchan Aruch HaGedolah; see there; and see in Peri Megadim there and in seif katan 19 he wrote that the conclusion of all poskim implies that even chazazit that rose detached disqualifies; see there:
