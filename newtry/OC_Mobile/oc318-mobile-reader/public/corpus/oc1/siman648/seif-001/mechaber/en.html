Things That Invalidate An Etrog, 22 Seifim: 1. A dried-out etrog is invalid. The criterion
for dryness is not being able to find any moisture, which can be checked by putting a needle and thread through it. If there is moisture, it should be visible on the thread. [An etrog from the previous year is certainly dry and invalid (Responsa 5 of the Mahari"l)].[ ']:
