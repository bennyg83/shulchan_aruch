(2) Needle. It is difficult — for perhaps there will be a mefulesh hole in it, and it is invalid per the Rosh and Tur, and likewise in Shulchan Aruch seif 2 per the first view — one can say that mefulesh disqualifies only when pierced from this side to the opposite side facing it, or to the seed chambers; but if the hole on one side is mefulesh, that is, through its thickness, certainly all agree it does not disqualify without a deficiency, for we derive etrog laws from treifot of an animal, and in treifot if there is a mefulesh hole in the thickness of the limb such as in the heart or intestines and it does not reach the cavity, certainly there is no treifah; so too with etrog.
