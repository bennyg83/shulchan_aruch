In two or. See Taz. I still do not know how far apart two blemishes may be and still render the esrog mottled. I have not found an explicit source requiring only the width of a straw between them. Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 8, halachah 7 of Hilchos Lulav, discusses this at length and cites many sources. See Kappos Temarim. The expression “a chazazis arose upon it” does not specifically mean after the esrog was detached, as in the phrase “a palm tree arose upon it.” The same law applies while it is attached to the tree. The requirement is only that the blemishes protrude above the rest of the esrog. See there. With Hashem's help this will be explained further in Magen Avraham, seif katan 19.
