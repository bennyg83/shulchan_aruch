Section 14. If it arose, etc. For the reason of these invalidations is because of beauty, and they are not invalidations in its body, like berries more numerous than its leaves, whose invalidity is because of beauty; if he reduced them, it is valid. This is the reason for what is written in Yevamot 76a: and this is, etc., since it is not because of itself, but because the seed cannot flow because of the hole, to exclude, etc.; for there its invalidity is in its body. See the Rosh.
And nothing is missing, etc. See what I wrote in siman 649:5.
