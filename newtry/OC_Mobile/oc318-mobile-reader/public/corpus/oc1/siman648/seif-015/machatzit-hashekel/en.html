(Seif katan 15) And some invalidate, etc. One may answer that there the case concerns, etc. Therefore, even though it is half the circumference, it is more lenient than half the surface. Although in two or three places most of the circumference may have the law of most of the surface, the authorities were more lenient regarding exactly half the circumference than regarding exactly half the surface. The Shulchan Aruch therefore rules without qualification in seif 9 that exactly half the circumference is kosher according to all opinions. Where it is exactly half the surface, he did not need to explain in seif 10 that it depends upon the dispute in seif 11, because he relied upon what he wrote in seif 11 concerning one place, where some invalidate. All the more so would they invalidate in two or three places, which is more stringent. The unqualified ruling in seif 10 concerns most of the circumference, and there, as Magen Avraham writes, all agree that exactly half is kosher.
