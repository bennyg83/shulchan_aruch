Pickled in vinegar, etc. In Rashi it is written: or in mustard. It appears to me that the same applies with honey, for it is certainly included in pickling, which invalidates. My father-in-law and teacher, of blessed memory, wrote above in this siman: I saw that they bury it in honey, for by this it remains moist. But it appears to me that this is prohibited for the reason I wrote.
