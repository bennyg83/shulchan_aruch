Water fell on it while detached and it swelled. In the Gemara it states: swollen and rotten is invalid. Rashi explained two versions. In the first explanation: such as where rain fell on it while it was detached, and it swelled; and rotten means decayed. Another version: swelled means decayed, and rotten means that its smell became foul because of worms that ate it. It implies according to the second version that swelling alone does not invalidate it. The Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote plainly like Rashi's first version. The Magen Avraham, who wrote that 'swelled' means decayed, requires investigation. Now the measure of swelled, rotten, pickled, or cooked was not explained. It is logical that its measure is most of it. Regarding whether here too at its nose any amount invalidates, this requires investigation, since the reason at its nose is that the nose is more visible to the eyes; perhaps that applies specifically to boil-like discoloration and other changes of appearance, which are more recognizable, but not to swelling of any amount, and likewise to the others, except for spottedness. This requires investigation.
