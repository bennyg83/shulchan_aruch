And it swelled. That is to say, it rotted and spoiled. Spoiled means that its smell is bad because worms ate it, Rashi. This implies that being eaten by worms alone does not invalidate it; see seif 2.
Pickled in vinegar. It appears that according to us, who rule that if it soaked in water for twenty-four hours it is like cooked, the same applies here regarding an esrog, and it is invalid. What the Tur and Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote about vinegar is because with vinegar, even if it remained only long enough to bring to a boil, it is like cooked. See what I wrote in simanim 473 and 527.
