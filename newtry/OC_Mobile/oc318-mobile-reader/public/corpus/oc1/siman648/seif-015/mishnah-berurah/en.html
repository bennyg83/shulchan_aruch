Pickled in vinegar. Meaning that in this case, even if it remained only long enough to boil, it is like cooked. The same applies when it soaked in water or other liquids, and in honey, for a twenty-four-hour period; we rule that it is like cooked.
Or spotted. Some say this is specifically if it is spotted with invalid colors, such as boil-like discoloration, or black, or white, or green like the grasses of the field, and its law is as written below in se'if 16. Others say that even if it is spotted with many valid appearances, in any case it is not included in beauty and is invalid. The reason in all this is that it is not beautiful.
