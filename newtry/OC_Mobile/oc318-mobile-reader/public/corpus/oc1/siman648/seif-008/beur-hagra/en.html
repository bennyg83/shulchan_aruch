Section 8. The wood was removed. This is the language of the Rambam, and according to the Aruch's explanation, that he came to add.
But if some was removed, etc. This is the mishnah there: if its stem was removed, etc.
That the entire width, etc. For otherwise it would be deficient. This is the reason regarding its pestle being removed, according to the first view in se'if 2. Nevertheless, etc.
