8) And if he took in his hand an etrog to bless on it and afterward they brought him a more beautiful etrog — he is not obligated to put it down and bless on the more beautiful one. Shulchan Aruch HaRav part 1 siman 37 and part 2 siman 40, and so agreed in responsa Yad Eliyahu siman 72. However in responsa Chacham Tzvi siman 45 he disagreed with the words of Shulchan Aruch HaRav mentioned and concluded the matter is clear that whether regarding etrog, Hanukkah candles, or any mitzvah in the world — one blesses on the beautiful and mehudar and fulfills with it, even though it came last; see there. However if he already began the blessing he should not put it down and take the other; and so too if it is on the first day and this unattractive one is his and he took it in his hand to bless, and meanwhile his friend came and wants to give him a more mehudar gift on condition to return — better to fulfill the mitzvah with his own. So wrote Shaarei Teshuvah below siman 643 letter 1; see there. And in my humble opinion it appears that on day one it is better to bless on one's own even though he has not yet taken it in hand to bless, as long as there is no concern of disqualification, only because of hiddur — for a person prefers to perform a mitzvah with his own property; and also lest the owner of the etrog does not give as a complete gift with all his heart.
