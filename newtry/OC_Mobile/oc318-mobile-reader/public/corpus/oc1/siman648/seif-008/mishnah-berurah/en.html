And its place remained a depression, it is invalid. For it is included in deficiency. If it is a pressing circumstance and there is no other in the city, the Chacham Tzvi concluded in a responsum that one may bless over it even on the first day, since in pressing circumstances it is stated at the end of siman 649 that we bless even over completely invalid species, and all the more so here, where it depends on the views of the Rishonim, as explained in the Beit Yosef. The same applies if its pitma was removed and a depression was made, and there is no other in the city: he may also bless over it even on the first day.
And any thickness remained, so that the entire width, etc. Meaning that a small measure of the thickness of the wood remained inside the esrog, such that its thickness can at least fill the entire width of the depression. Even though above the depression nothing remains, it is nevertheless valid, for it is no longer called deficient.
