The wood. In pressing circumstances, when there is no other esrog in the city, one may rely upon this and recite the blessing over it, even on the first day. Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 9; see there.
Covered. Meaning, some of the wood remains in the depression as it was originally. Responsa Beit Yaakov, siman 142, writes that when the poskim invalidate an esrog whose uketz was removed, they mean that it was removed and no longer exists, so that it cannot be reattached. If it can be reattached with a needle or the like so that the repair is not noticeable, it may be taken on the second day and a blessing recited over it. On the first day, however, one should be stringent. See there.
