S.k. 6. Opposite those chambers, etc. What the Taz validated in s.k. 1 is a different matter: he inserts the needle even opposite the seed chambers, but only through a bit of the peel across its width on one side of the esrog, making a slight hole, and the hole has two openings. This is neither perforated through nor from side to side. Here, however, it discusses a case where above the seed chambers it was pierced from side to side. In one responsum I wrote at length about various matters, but here is not the place to elaborate.
