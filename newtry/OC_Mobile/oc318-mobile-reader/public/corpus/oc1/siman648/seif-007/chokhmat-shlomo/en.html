(There, se'if 7 in the gloss) However, as a matter of law one should not invalidate unless the dad was removed. Note: see Magen Avraham se'if katan 9, in what he wrote: and one can say that in any case we require that all of it be removed, etc., and therefore they wrote the language 'removed,' etc. Indeed, this is implied in Yoreh De'ah, Hilchot Tereifot, in several places, that the term 'removed' implies all of it. A support for this appears from what the poet wrote: 'when he falls, he shall not be cast down, for Hashem supports his hand.' This proves that the language 'cast down' implies entirely. This is a meaningful sign, with Hashem's help; examine it.
