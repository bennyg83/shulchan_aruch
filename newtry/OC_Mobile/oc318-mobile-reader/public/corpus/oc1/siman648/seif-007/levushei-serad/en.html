There. There is a doubt, etc. This is a separate matter, and it deals with a hole that has no deficiency, and it is understandable. But where there is a deficiency, since even according to the Raavad it is not clearly valid, for if it reaches the seed chambers it is invalid if in such a case it is called perforated through, therefore we do not validate it. Even though according to the Raavad there is a double doubt, perhaps it does not reach the seed chambers, and perhaps reaching the seed chambers is not called perforated through, and if so perhaps according to the Raavad it is valid because of this double doubt, nevertheless, since according to those who disagree with the Raavad it is invalid, and even according to the Raavad himself it is only a double doubt, perhaps in such a case even in pressing circumstances one should not be lenient.
