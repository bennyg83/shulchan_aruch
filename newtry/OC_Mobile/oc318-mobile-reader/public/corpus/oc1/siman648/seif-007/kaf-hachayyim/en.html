7) Ibid., Rama's gloss: It is certainly dried and invalid. Regarding an esrog placed in honey to preserve its moisture, one should not rely on this to validate it without examining it with a needle and thread. Bach; Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, sec. 4. However, Taz, s.k. 17, wrote that it is invalid because it is pickled, and so ruled Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 25; Beit HaShoevah, secs. 6 and 67; Mishna Berurah, sec. 54. With congealed honey, however, we do not say that something pickled is like something cooked, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 104, regarding a mouse. Machatzis HaShekel, s.k. 22; Mishna Berurah, Shaar HaTziyun, sec. 61. See further below, seif 15, and our words there, with God's help.
