Its dad was removed. This is the wood on top of the esrog, with the rosette upon it.
Unless the dad was removed. His wording implies specifically that all of it was removed, as the Ran wrote. But the Beis Yosef and Levush wrote to be stringent even if part of it was removed. It seems to me that in any event it must be removed down to the esrog, since the reason is because it is lacking, as written in siman 649, seif 5 in the gloss. If so, when any amount is removed it is not called lacking, for this is not a recognizable deficiency. Therefore all the poskim used the language removed. See the Shelah.
If it did not have a dad. They are recognizable, for at the place of the pitom there is like a cavity from the beginning of its formation, Mabit, part 3, siman 49, and see Radbaz, siman 111, Knesses HaGedolah.
