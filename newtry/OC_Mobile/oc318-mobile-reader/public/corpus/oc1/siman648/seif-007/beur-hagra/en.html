Section 7. Its dad was removed. This is the language of the Rambam, and according to Tosafot's explanation in the name of the Aruch.
And some are stringent, etc. This is the Rif according to the Ran's explanation. And as stated in the Yerushalmi: if its pitma was removed; what do we say? Its shoshanta. Rabbi Yitzchak bar Mekola said: its pika. This is Rabbi Yitzchak bar Elazar, as stated in chapter Arvei Pesachim, and it is its pestle, as written in se'if 8. But the Ran refuted his words, and the Beit Yosef wrote that even the words of the Rif can be explained like the Rambam's explanation, as he wrote, that the shoshanta is on it; and this is also the explanation of the Yerushalmi.
And all this, etc. For this is why he said 'was removed,' and this follows the view of the Rambam in Yoreh De'ah 50:2.
