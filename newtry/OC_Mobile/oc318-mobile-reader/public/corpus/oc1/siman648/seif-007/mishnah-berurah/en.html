Its dad was removed. This is the wood on the head of the esrog, like the point of the dad, inserted into it, with the shoshanta upon it.
Invalid. For it is considered deficient. Now from the language 'the dad was removed' it implies that the wood was removed, including what is inserted into the esrog, and a kind of depression was made there; therefore it is invalid. But if only what is above the esrog was removed, one should not be stringent. The Taz agreed to this in practice. But some poskim incline to be stringent even if only what is above the esrog was removed, holding that according to them this is included in deficiency. If any amount of the wood remains above the esrog, the view of the Magen Avraham is that one should not be stringent in this.
Where possible. Meaning where it is possible to take one more choice than this. But if this one is the most choice, one should not be stringent because of the shoshanta. Nevertheless, it appears that this is specifically if only the shoshanta is missing; but if part of the wood is also missing, even though there is still a little wood above the esrog, it is good to seek another if possible, because some are stringent even in this.
Valid. Since this is how they are created and this is their manner of growth, they should not be described as deficient or as not beautiful. They are recognizable, because in the place of the pitma there is a kind of depression from the beginning of their creation. Bikkurei Yaakov wrote that this is specifically only when there is a slight depression, where there is no invalidity except because of deficiency; and since it was this way from the beginning of its creation and nothing was removed from it at all, it is not called deficient. But sometimes there is a deep depression whose cavity reaches the seed chambers, and this can be recognized by inserting a needle or another thin item into the cavity. In such a case it appears that it is completely invalid, for a congenital hole is invalid wherever a hole is invalid, as stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 43 in the Peri Megadim. Since a hole until the seed chambers is invalid, the same applies to a congenital hole.
