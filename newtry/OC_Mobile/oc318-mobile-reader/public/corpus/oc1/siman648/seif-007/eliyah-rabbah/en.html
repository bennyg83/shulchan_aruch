And the seed chambers remain intact, etc. Bach asks how one can know that they remain intact inside. He answers that when they have dissolved into liquid, this is visible externally from one side to the other. Magen Avraham writes that experience contradicts this and answers that one can determine it by feeling with the hand. See Sefer Temim Deim, siman 230, on this matter. Bach further objects that all the Geonim prohibit it; see there. I found, however, that Baal Halachos Gedolos explicitly permits it, and so does Sefer Tanya in the name of Baal Aseres HaDibros.
