Section 5. If it split, etc. From that which it says, if it split, etc., it implies that we compare it to a windpipe.
And there is, etc. For anything invalid at its nose is invalid in any amount, like boil-like discoloration. The first view holds that when we invalidate at its nose in any amount, this is specifically those invalidations that invalidate on most of it, like boil-like discoloration, and as stated in the Yerushalmi: its nose is like its majority for invalidation.
And some are stringent, etc. According to the Beit Yosef's version in the Ran; and so it is in the Rashba, for all the words of the Ran are copied from him almost letter for letter. This is his language there: split, meaning along its length in most of it, from two sides, and below; for at its nose, etc. And some are stringent even when the crack is not in most of it below, passing from two sides, but it is cracked from one side until its cavity. And some are stringent to invalidate even when it does not reach its cavity, since it passes through all of the thick outer peel or most of it. One must say that he compares it to the flesh that covers most of the rumen; see Yoreh De'ah 48:3.
