Taz concludes as practical halachah that an esrog which split is valid when it did not split entirely, some portion remains intact below, and across its width the split extends through only most of the thickness of the peel and does not reach the seed chambers. This applies only when the split is not at the chotem, for at the chotem even the slightest defect invalidates it. Magen Avraham agrees; see there.
