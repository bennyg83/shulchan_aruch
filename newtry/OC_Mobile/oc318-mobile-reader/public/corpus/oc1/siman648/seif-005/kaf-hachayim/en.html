And he shall examine by passing a needle through it, etc. This examination of the Rosh and Tur and Shulchan Aruch — it is almost impossible that it not make some deficiency in it and disqualify it. Therefore it is better to insert a needle in it slightly and remove it from the same side it was inserted; and if the hole returns and fills after removing the needle, it is moist, and if not, its moisture has already been consumed and it dried — like what is written in Yerushalmi at the end of chapter 2 of Shevi'it regarding gourds: if it was hardened in the seventh year, etc.; and this is a light matter, it does not cause deficiency in an etrog even slightly. From Mo'ach Korkev. From Machberet letter 1, main points of Darkei Moshe siman 33 letter 29. However Beit HaShoevah letter 4 wrote it is possible an etrog is different, for even if the hole does not fill, still not all the moisture has completely finished; see there. And the Raavad in Sefer Tadir siman 232 wrote: pass a needle through it, and if there is moisture in it, it immediately comes out through the hole; see there.
