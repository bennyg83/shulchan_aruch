It is valid. Like the case of an animal's windpipe that split, in Yoreh De'ah siman 34; and even if it split from both sides along its entire length, but on each side some amount remained above and some amount below.
To invalidate when most of it split. The Taz and Magen Avraham challenged the Rema's ruling, and their view is that we do not find anyone who is stringent in this regarding most of it. But the Gra in his commentary proved like the Rema, that some are stringent regarding most of it, meaning even from one side.
