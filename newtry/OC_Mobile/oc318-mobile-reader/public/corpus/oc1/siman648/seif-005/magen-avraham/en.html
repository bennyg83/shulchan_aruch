It cracked. This implies that it discusses one side.
Where most of it cracked. This implies even if it cracked on one side, but this is not precise, for only according to the opinion that it must be on two sides is it invalid if most of it cracked, but according to the opinion that one side suffices, we require all of it, Bach. To me this requires study, for he compares it to the windpipe, and regarding the windpipe we require that all of it cracked. Our version in the Ran is: along its length, across its width, on two sides. It seems to me that this is the explanation: whether it cracked along its length or whether it cracked across its width, we require two sides. Although with a windpipe, if it was split across its width the animal is a tereifah when most of it split, that is because when the animal stretches its neck it tears further, which is not so here. If so, there is no opinion that it is a tereifah by most of it; see there. Therefore one may be lenient.
