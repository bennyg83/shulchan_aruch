And there is one who says that specifically at the bottom, etc. See the Gra, who wrote that the first view holds that when we invalidate at its nose in any amount, this is specifically those invalidations that invalidate on most of it, like boil-like discoloration, and as stated in the Yerushalmi: its nose is like its majority for invalidation. End quote. Meaning, this is unlike here, where we require only that some amount remain above and some amount below. According to this, for the stringent view, which holds that in our matter as well we are stringent to invalidate when most of it split, one must automatically be stringent regarding its nose as well, even in any amount. With this the view of the Ran is understandable, for he inclines to be stringent regarding its nose.
And some are stringent to invalidate when most of it split. Even according to the Mechaber, who compares it to a severed windpipe, it would also be invalid when most of it split in one place and on that side of the place of the split no amount remained, for a windpipe is also a tereifah in this manner. However, there is a practical difference in the case the Mechaber mentioned, where it split from its top to its bottom entirely and only some amount remained above and some amount below. According to the Mechaber it is valid, while according to the stringent view it is invalid, for in any case most of the esrog split. That which the Gemara asks, 'if it split, we learned it,' does not mean to compare it entirely to a windpipe; rather, in our matter too, splitting is a sign of tereifah, for eventually all of it will rot, and it is invalid.
And whenever it did not split, etc. The Rema used negative language, because in this manner it is certainly permitted. Nevertheless, even where most of its peel split there are also opinions; some say it is specifically when the crack reached its cavity. And even though, if so, it is no less than punctured, nevertheless the practical difference is for those poskim who hold that it is not called a through-and-through hole until it reaches from one side to the other. From the standpoint of a hole one would not invalidate it, but when it split along its entire length or most of it, according to the gloss one should invalidate it because of splitting. See the Ran.
