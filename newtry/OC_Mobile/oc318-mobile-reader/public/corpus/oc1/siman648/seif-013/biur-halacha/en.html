Blisters. See the Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote that this is specifically when the boil-like discoloration arose from the substance of the esrog, etc. See the Eliyah Rabbah, who brought from the Agudah that in a place where it lies on thorns, boil-like discoloration grows on it. The Sha'arei Teshuvah was forced in reconciling this, saying that there it is higher than the rest of the esrog, and the language of the Mabit is forced according to this. In Bikkurei Yaakov he reconciled it simply: since boil-like discoloration is that which is produced by rotting of the fruit, whether detached or attached, as the Tosafot Yom Tov wrote in the third chapter of Sukkah, therefore it is natural that in the place where it lies on thorns, since no air dominates there, it rots there and boil-like discoloration is produced there. This is what the Agudah speaks about. But that which becomes crooked and red because of pricking and thorns has no rot or decay, and therefore it is not boil-like discoloration and is valid. End quote.
They are considered the appearance of an esrog. See the Gra's commentary: that is, even though this appearance is not identical to the other places of the esrog, nevertheless since it is considered an appearance of an esrog, it is valid.
