Blisters. Some say specifically two blisters, for otherwise it is not included in boil-like discoloration. But many of the later authorities disagree with this, and their view is that even one blister, whenever it is visible to every person by eye without close examination, is called boil-like discoloration. The poskim wrote that this is specifically when the boil-like discoloration developed from the substance of the esrog; but if its place became crooked and red because thorns prick it, it is valid. See Sha'arei Teshuvah and Biur Halacha.
That they are considered the appearance of an esrog, etc. According to this reason, even if they are higher than the rest of the esrog it is valid, and there is no practical difference whether the boil-like discoloration is on its nose or in other places. See the later authorities, who agreed that one should not be lenient where they are higher than the rest of the esrog except in pressing circumstances.
