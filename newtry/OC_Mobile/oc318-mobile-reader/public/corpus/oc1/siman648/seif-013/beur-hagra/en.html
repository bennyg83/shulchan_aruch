Section 13. Therefore, etc. As he wrote: if it arose, etc.
And there is one who says, etc. As the Rosh wrote, that boil-like discoloration is invalid because it is not of the appearance of an esrog. Therefore, one that was peeled is valid even on most of it, since it is the appearance of an esrog, even though their appearances are not identical. See se'if 17. And these, since they are common, are considered of the appearance of an esrog.
