And similarly if he covered, etc.—and if it is under the bed and the bed's legs do not reach the ground three [tefachim], it is also as sealed and covered by the bed; and from three [tefachim] and above one needs distancing per the measure one must distance from excrement. And if the excrement is behind the bed, it is found that it has no permission from the side of covering, only from the side of interposition that the bed interposes between him and the excrement—it does not help in this unless the bed is ten [handbreadths] high, like other partitions whose law is ten [handbreadths], as the conclusion in the Tur.
