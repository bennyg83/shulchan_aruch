Washed well—in Eshel Avraham his intent is Tosafos Berachos 25b, s.v. "provided that"—in a chamberpot perhaps water does not help; but in a urine receptacle for urine, of glass, at any rate water helps even if not washed well, for urine is rabbinically forbidden, water helps as Turei Zahav, and they mix—the urine with the water; and the Beit Yosef wrote that a revi'it does not raise and does not lower; and therefore he wrote here "washed well" specifically. And I wonder—really for a urine receptacle, why does it not help? And therefore Tosafos's words on a chamberpot—perhaps no dispute, namely regarding glass, for for excrement we require covering or thick saliva. And the Beit Yosef's reason one could say also for a urine receptacle: since filth adheres inside, even urine alone does not mix with water, and no dispute—a revi'it.
