(In Magen Avraham, siman katan 4)—and so too in Turei Zahav that his main difficulty is on the question, etc. It appears to be an explanation of his words that in the Gemara there is a dispute: the Rabbis hold it does not help behind the bed, and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds it helps; and we say there, Rav Yosef asked Rav Huna: a bed three [tefachim]... ten [handbreadths] is not asked of me; Abaye said, you did well that you were not asked; and Rashi wrote that the Gaon explains the question regarding a sealed bed, and he challenged—for if so, why did Rav Yosef need to say ten is not asked of me; and also Abaye need not say you did well, for this is obvious that at ten it is a partition, as we taught: if it was low ten, etc. And behold the Tur wrote that the Gaon holds the question is according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, and about this he says ten is not asked of me—for in this Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel certainly holds it interposes; see Bach. And therefore Turei Zahav should challenge: since the case is a sealed bed, why do the Rabbis who disagree with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel [say] that even at ten [handbreadths] high it does not interpose—why would it not interpose? Behold sealing is a complete partition at ten [handbreadths] high. Rather certainly the case is one that does not interpose from wall to wall; and therefore the Rabbis properly hold it does not help behind the bed, and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds that even in such a case it is a partition. Nevertheless it is properly difficult to me about this that he says ten is not asked of me, etc.—for it is obvious to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel that it is a partition, since according to him even if it does not interpose from wall to wall it is considered a partition; and if so, what do I care whether it is ten [handbreadths] high or much higher—it is obvious that likewise it is a partition at ten [handbreadths] high as at much higher, per the law if the place were ten [handbreadths] high. However, what the Magen Avraham wrote, and it is known the halachah is like the Rabbis—requires study; for if we say this question is according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, then one should rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, for the question follows him, as explained in the Tur that for this reason the aforementioned Gaon ruled like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, and the Taz and Turei Zahav also brought it and ruled like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. And if they do not rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, then necessarily one must explain as the explanation that the question refers according to Rashba—if so, there is no ruling from this at all whether interposing from wall to wall is called a partition, for the case is not discussing that at all.
