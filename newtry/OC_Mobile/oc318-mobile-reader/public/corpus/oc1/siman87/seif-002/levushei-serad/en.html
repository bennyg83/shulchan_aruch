Siman katan 3. Water in them—so wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and the Mordechai, that for a chamberpot and urine receptacle of glass and the like it is not permitted unless he put water in them. However, per the version before us in Tosafos, specifically for the urine receptacle; but for the chamberpot, even pouring water does not help. And per the Maharsha's version in Tosafos there, pouring water is not needed even for the chamberpot. See there.
