And there are those who forbid—for since it is absorbed within it, it is as within it. In the Tur—and the Gaon explains, etc., he gave a reason why the Gaon ruled like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel: because the question goes according to him, and he said to him, yes; for according to Rashi the question goes according to Rashba, and nevertheless the halachah was not like Rashba, for Rava ruled not like him. This is incomprehensible—for whenever there is no halachah decided before us in the Talmud, from the words of amoraim we follow that tanna whom the question follows; whereas not so when there is a halachah decided before us, that an amora has power to rule according to what appears correct in his eyes, even as an individual against many; and we find this many times among the codifiers. There—in the Tur, "as if it is in another domain"—he explains: as if the bed is in another domain, the excrement is found with him in his domain and has no covering; and in vain did the Beit Yosef emend here to "one domain."
