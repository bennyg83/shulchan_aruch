Seif katan 2, coated, etc. Glazed. Beit Yosef implies that coating on the inside alone is sufficient. Bach writes that it must be coated both inside and outside. Taz writes that since Rama rules stringently below in siman 451 that hagalah does not help for glass utensils, just as with earthenware, glass should likewise have the law of earthenware here. Although Taz and Magen Avraham agree there that after the fact, if one performed hagalah and used it, the hagalah is effective, one must say that here it initially retains the stringent law. Eliyah Rabbah writes that since hagalah is effective after the fact, here it is permitted even initially without hagalah, because glass does not absorb as much as earthenware.
