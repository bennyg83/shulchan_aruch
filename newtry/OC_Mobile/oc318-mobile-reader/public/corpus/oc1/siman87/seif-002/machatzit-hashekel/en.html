(Siman katan 2) "Coated"—glazed; and it is implied in Rabbeinu Yonah that it suffices if coated only inside. But the Bach wrote that it must be coated inside and also outside. And the Taz wrote: since the Rama is stringent later in siman 451 that for glass vessels hag'alah does not work like earthenware vessels—if so, here too glass vessels have the law of earthenware vessels. Although there the Taz and Magen Avraham agreed that after the fact, if they did hag'alah and used it, the hag'alah helps—here one must say the law applies lekhatchilah. And in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote: since after the fact hag'alah helps here, even lekhatchilah it is permitted even if he did not do hag'alah, for it does not absorb as much as pottery.
