Taz, siman katan 4—and he said to him, yes. All the Taz's words in this matter I did not understand, for the matter is simple that for Rashi in truth we rule like Rashba, that the bed's interposition does not help—for regarding this matter the Tosefta also holds so; and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel is alone against them, and from the Torah one rules like the individual. And that which Rava said the halachah is not like Rashba—that is regarding that the house is not considered four amot, and this is simple in the sugya. If so, the Taz's difficulty is incomprehensible. Also the Taz's answers are wondrous—for according to his words, why did he need the Tur in the name of the Gaon as proof that we rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel? Behold alongside it we say the halachah is not like Rashba. Rather the main point is as I wrote, and it is simple.
