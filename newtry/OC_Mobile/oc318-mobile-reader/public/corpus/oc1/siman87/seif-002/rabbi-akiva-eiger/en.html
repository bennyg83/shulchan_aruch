Turei Zahav, se'if katan 4: “And if not, this...” I did not understand any of Turei Zahav's words in this passage. The matter is simple: according to Rashi, we truly rule in accordance with Rashba that the partition made by the bed is ineffective, because on this point the first tanna also agrees. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel is therefore a lone authority against them, and why should we rule in accordance with an individual opinion? Rava's statement that the halakhah does not follow Rashba refers to the separate issue of whether the house is considered four cubits; this is clear in the sugya. Turei Zahav's question therefore presents no difficulty. Turei Zahav's answer is also surprising: according to his words, why did Tur need to cite a proof in the name of a Gaon that we rule in accordance with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel? The Gemara explicitly says that the halakhah does not follow Rashba. Rather, the primary explanation is as I wrote, and it is clear.
