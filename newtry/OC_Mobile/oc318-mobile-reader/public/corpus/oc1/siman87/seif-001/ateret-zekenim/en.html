But if they are of metal, etc., or of coated earthenware, it is permitted if they are washed well (and no) odor emerges from them (Beit Yosef in the name of Ohel Moed). And for coated earthenware, it must specifically be coated inside and outside (my teacher, the Bach); then it is permitted even if there is no water in them, since there is no filth whatsoever neither inside nor outside and they are washed well (Beit Yosef). And some say that nevertheless we require pouring a revi'it of water into them (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and the Mordechai). But of [plain] earthenware or wood, even if they are washed well and there is no bad odor in them, nevertheless one must distance as from feces (Berachos 22b).
