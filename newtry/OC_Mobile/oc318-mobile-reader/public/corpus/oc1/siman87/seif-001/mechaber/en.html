How Much One Needs to Distance at the Time of the Recitation of the Sh'ma from a Chamberpot Used
for Excrement, containing 3 s'ifim A chamberpot used for excrement and a receptacle used for urine, [whether made] of earthenware or wood - one needs to distance from them as from excrement, even if one poured water into them. But if they are of metal, glass, or coated earthenware, it is permitted if they are washed well.
