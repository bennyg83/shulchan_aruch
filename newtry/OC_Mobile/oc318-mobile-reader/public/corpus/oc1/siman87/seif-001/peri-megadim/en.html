Even—in Eshel Avraham: behold, in Rabbeinu Yonah the reason that a urine receptacle for urine is forbidden and urine is only rabbinically forbidden; therefore a revi'it of water helps there and not in a urine receptacle for urine (for it is very repulsive and urine is forbidden; and in the Beit Yosef Berlin edition words of Turei Zahav are missing—see there). And therefore urine with a bad smell—a revi'it of water does not help, for urine is forbidden, as written in siman 76 and 79 in the Taz's introduction—see there; and the conclusion of the codifiers does not imply so (and from that he requires much water it also implies that urine is [forbidden] as such). Therefore he chose the Beit Yosef's reason that they do not mix—see there. And from the Torah, for excrement pouring water does not help, for it does not mix; and thick saliva that helps temporarily is because of covering, not because of nullification; and murky water too is because of covering, for the word "and cover" bears two faces: (1) proper covering, namely excrement in a bedpan; (2) covering from sight—therefore thick saliva and murky water, covering from sight, are also covering. And see siman 76 in Magen Avraham, letter 9, and as written here it appears more [applicable]. And in Ba'er Hetev on Magen Avraham 1—see there.
