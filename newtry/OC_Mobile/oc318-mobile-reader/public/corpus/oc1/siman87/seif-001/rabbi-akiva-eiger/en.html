Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1: It is considered like a privy, and they are forbidden. This implies that the prohibition is from the Torah and applies even to a chamber pot made of marble. It is more stringent than the urine itself, as Beit Yosef writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. Accordingly, if one recited, he must recite again. See Magen Avraham at the end of siman 76.
