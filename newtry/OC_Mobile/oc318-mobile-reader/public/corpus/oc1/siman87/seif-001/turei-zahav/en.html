Even if he poured, etc. Beit Yosef explains that adding water permits only urine, because urine is clear, mixes with the water, and becomes nullified. By contrast, what a chamber pot or urinal vessel absorbs does not mix with the water and is therefore not nullified.
Of glass. Because it does not absorb. It appears that according to the Rama in siman 451, who cites an opinion that even hagalah is ineffective for glass utensils, one should likewise be stringent here, since the reason is the same, glass being worse than earthenware according to that opinion.
