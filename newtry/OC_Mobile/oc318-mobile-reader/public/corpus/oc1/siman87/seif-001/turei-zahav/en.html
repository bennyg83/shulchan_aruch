Even if he poured [water], etc.—in the Beit Yosef he wrote the reason that specifically for urine it is permitted with pouring water, because they are clear and mix with water and are nullified; whereas not so with a chamberpot and urine receptacle, for what is absorbed in them does not mix with water; therefore they are not nullified.

Of glass—for it does not absorb; and it appears that according to what the Rama wrote in siman 451, some say that even hag'alah does not help for glass vessels—here too one should be stringent, for it is one reason, since glass is inferior to pottery.

Washed well—in Tosafos at the beginning of the chapter "What is written" he wrote to permit if he put water in them.
