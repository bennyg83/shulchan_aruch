Seif 1, earthenware, etc. Tosafos there and the other poskim, because it absorbs, as stated in the final chapter of Avos D’Rabbi Nosson: Three things are like an earthenware vessel, which absorbs and does not release, etc.
Even, etc. This follows from the statement, “and urine itself,” etc.
Or of glass, etc. As stated there in Avos D’Rabbi Nosson: Three things apply to a glass vessel, it does not absorb, etc. The reason is that it is smooth, and the same applies to the other materials.
If they are thoroughly washed, etc. In the first case, however, washing is ineffective, according to the Rosh and the other poskim. This is like what is stated there regarding a pig, even if it has just come up from the river, and as explained above in siman 76:3; see there.
