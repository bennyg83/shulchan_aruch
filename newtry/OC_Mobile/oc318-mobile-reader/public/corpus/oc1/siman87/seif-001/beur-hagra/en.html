Seif 1, of earthenware, etc. Tosafos there, and Shulchan Aruch [haRav] wrote that it absorbs, as stated in the last chapter of Avos deRabbi Nasan: three things — like an earthenware vessel, it absorbs and does not expel, etc.

Even, etc. From what is written, and urine itself, etc.

Or of glass, etc. As stated in Avos deRabbi Nasan there: three things — in a glass vessel it does not absorb, etc., and the reason is that it is smooth; and the same applies to the others.

If they are washed, etc. But at the beginning, washing does not help — the Rosh and Shulchan Aruch [haRav]; and as stated regarding a pig, even though it came up from the river; and as stated above, Siman 76, seif 3; see there.
