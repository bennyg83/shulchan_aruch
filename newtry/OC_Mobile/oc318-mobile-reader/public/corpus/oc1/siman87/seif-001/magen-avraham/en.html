Even if he poured water into them—for since they are designated for this it is considered like a lavatory and forbidden even if there is no bad smell (Rosh and Rambam); and it seems to me that if the vessel is also designated for another use, such as to pour beverages into it, if there is no excrement and no smell it is permitted; and similarly if designated for urination and every time he urinates he puts a revi'it of water into it, it is permitted.

Coated—it seems to me this means what we call glazed.

Washed well—provided there is no smell in them.
