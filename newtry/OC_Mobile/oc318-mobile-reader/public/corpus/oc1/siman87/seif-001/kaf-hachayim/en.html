1) [Seif 1] A chamberpot for excrement and a receptacle for urine, etc. And so it is stated in the chapter Mi Shemesu (25b): the Sages taught — a chamberpot for excrement and a receptacle for urine, it is forbidden to recite Kerias Shema opposite them even if there is nothing in them. And Rashi explained: a chamberpot and receptacle are both earthenware vessels; only that for excrement it is called a chamberpot, and for urine it is called a receptacle — so far. And the Rosh wrote that it is reasonable that of wood is forbidden because it is absorbed in it and full of filth; but of glass is permitted — and so Tur, and such are the words of our master, may his memory be a blessing. And even though Rashba, may his memory be a blessing, permits regarding wood, since the Rosh and Tur forbid — we consider it like earthenware because it absorbs and is full of filth — and so our master, may his memory be a blessing, ruled; we follow this. And so the later authorities.
