(3) There: One must adjoin Geulah to prayer, etc. — and at Musaf and Minchah, if he wishes to say verses he may say them. Tur; and on it Beit Yosef, may his memory be for blessing, was precise that our teacher wrote "but at Musaf and Minchah if he wishes to say verses he may say" — meaning, but at Maariv not; not because at Maariv too there is no mitzvah to adjoin Geulah to prayer, and so Kol Bo in the name of Rambam, and so is clear from the words of Tosafos in the first chapter of Berachos — end of quote; and so Perishah letter 4, Levush; and it will be further explained in our words to siman 236 seif 2, b'siyata d'Shmaya.
