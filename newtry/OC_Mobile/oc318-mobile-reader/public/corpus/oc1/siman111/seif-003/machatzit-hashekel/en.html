(s"k 3) On Yom Tov, since this day, etc. — for the reason one need not adjoin Geulah to prayer on Shabbat is because it is not a day of trouble; for the reason of adjoining Geulah to prayer is because David concluded Psalm 19 "May they be acceptable," etc., "and my Redeemer," and adjoined to it Psalm 20 that begins "May Heaven answer you on the day of trouble" — that is prayer; therefore specifically on a day of trouble we require adjoining Geulah to prayer, which is not so on Shabbat, as Rambam and Rivash wrote. And see there what he wrote — he divides between Shabbat and Yom Tov.
