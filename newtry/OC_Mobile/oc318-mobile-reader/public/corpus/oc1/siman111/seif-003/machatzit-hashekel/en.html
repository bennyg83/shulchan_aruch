Seif katan 3: On Yom Tov, since this day, etc. The reason one need not juxtapose redemption to the Amidah on Shabbos is that Shabbos is not a day of distress. The requirement to juxtapose redemption to prayer is derived from David, who concluded Tehillim 19 with, “May the words of my mouth be acceptable, etc., Hashem, my Rock and my Redeemer,” and immediately followed it with Tehillim 20, which begins, “May Hashem answer you on a day of distress,” referring to prayer. Thus the juxtaposition is required specifically on a day of distress, unlike Shabbos, as Rabbi Moshe Rivkes writes. See there for his distinction between Shabbos and Yom Tov. See citations 50.
