However. See Taz. According to this, on Shabbos everyone agrees that one may put on a tallis between redemption and prayer. See siman 66, seif 8. If one finds the congregation praying, he should pray with them, for this is no worse than Maariv. I found the same in Eliyah Rabbah, os 4, in the name of Lechem Chamudos. The same applies to responding Yehei Shmei Rabba and the like. See there. On Yom Tov, however, one must join redemption to prayer as on a weekday, as explained in siman 114, seif 2. See there.
