To juxtapose — Taz expounded; Orach Chayyim and Maharshal [explain from] "May Heaven answer you" — the assurance; and it is not necessary [to derive] the verse, for every mitzvah has reward in it, meaning on Shabbat one need not [juxtapose] etc., and therefore there is a practical difference between the Tur's explanation that on Shabbat one juxtaposes and Maharshal that one does not (in Shulchan Aruch in the gloss it is written Tur — perhaps one can intend this, but nevertheless in my humble opinion it is in two matters). And what the Taz wrote, what proof does he bring for juxtaposing? And according to what is written in the name of Maharshal — I do not rely on the end of the verse, and there is a practical difference between both of them: on Shabbat one need not; behold on a weekday one must.

And behold — in our novellae on Berachos 42:1, "immediately after redemption" etc., we wrote twenty-two cubits and now I saw in Peri Chadash twenty-two; and indeed from siman 66, seif 8 it implies that one may not interrupt even with speech of one word like Amen, even in silence to lay down a tallit is forbidden, and I wrote there; and "immediately after Nefilat Apayim" etc. — behold as stated and behold as stated.
