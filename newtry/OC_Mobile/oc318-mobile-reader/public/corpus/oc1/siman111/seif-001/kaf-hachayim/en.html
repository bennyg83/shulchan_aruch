(1) [Seif 1] One must adjoin Geulah to prayer, etc. — explained in the first chapter of Berachos 9b and in the Yerushalmi derives it from what is written "May the words of my mouth be acceptable and the meditation of my heart before You, Heaven, my Rock and my Redeemer" — that is Geulah; and adjoined to it "May Heaven answer you on the day of trouble" — that is prayer. Tur Orach Chayyim letter 1; and there is also a reason in secret as written below letter 6; and see below letter 9; and see above siman 58 seif 1 and in our words there letter 9; and siman 66 seifim 8 and 9 and in our words there, b'siyata d'Shmaya.
