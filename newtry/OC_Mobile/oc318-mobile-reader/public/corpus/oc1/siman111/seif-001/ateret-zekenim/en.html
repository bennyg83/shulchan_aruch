It is necessary to rely on salvation for prayer, and all who trust salvation for prayer is not harmed the whole day. We would say that if one recites Kushitekin Kash before the hot hawk and prays after the hot hawk, and trusts salvation for prayer, then he is not harmed all day long. And he should not say any verse Efi' before the offering prayer and add Nami, he should not say only O, my lips shall open, etc. And for this, Hashem decreed that my lips should be opened at the beginning of the Yachin blessing, because prayers are against always Rule and this verse is written before a sacrifice, after which it is said that you shall not covet a sacrifice (Avodrahm) and it is also an ad for a person from the hearts of the heart and a response to the tongue.
