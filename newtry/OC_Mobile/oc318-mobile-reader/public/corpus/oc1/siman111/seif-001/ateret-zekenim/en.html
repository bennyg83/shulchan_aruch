One must adjoin Geulah to prayer, and whoever adjoins Geulah to prayer is not harmed all that day — that is, specifically if he recites like the vatikin: Kerias Shema before sunrise and prayer after sunrise, and he adjoins Geulah to prayer; then he is not harmed all that day. And he should not say any verse, even before the prayer of Minchah and Musaf; he should say only "Heaven, open my lips," etc. Therefore they established "Heaven, open my lips" at the beginning of the blessing of the eighteen, because the prayers were instituted corresponding to the tamid offerings, and this verse is written before sacrifice, as it says after it "for You do not desire sacrifice" (Abudraham). And also it is in the manner of a person offering the arrangement of his heart, and "from Heaven comes an answer of the tongue" (Abudraham). And this Name is written aleph-dalet, because he speaks corresponding to himself — "my the Master, my Master, my Ruler" — then he writes aleph-dalet, as is the rule for all things a person speaks corresponding to himself that have a yud at their end; and the proof for this is from the prophet's words "Adonai, contend for me" (Abudraham).
