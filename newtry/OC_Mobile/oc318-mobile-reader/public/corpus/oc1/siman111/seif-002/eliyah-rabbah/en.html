The Levush writes that according to the esoteric teaching there is a reason connected to the fact that this Divine Name is written with alef and dalet. Rashban wrote that the distinction between the Divine Name written with alef and dalet and the Name written yud, hei, vav, hei is as follows: when the speaker means that He is Master of all creatures and is not addressing Him directly, the Name is written yud, hei, vav, hei. When the speaker addresses Him directly, saying “my Master, my Mighty One, and my Sovereign,” it is written with alef and dalet.
