"He repeats and says," etc. — see in Mishna Berurah; and one should investigate if a person forgot to say "Heaven, open my lips," whether this counts as a deficiency in the prayer itself, since the Sages established it in the prayer, or not. And one should not bring proof from here that the later authorities wrote that the prayer leader should say it in a whisper — if so, the listeners lack this verse — nevertheless they fulfill the obligation of prayer; for here this verse "Adonai, open my lips," etc., does not apply to them at all since they are not praying themselves; which is not so generally. And it is more reasonable that they did not say it as an extended prayer per se, only regarding there not being an interruption between Geulah and prayer; but not as prayer itself such that one would need to return for it. Know that they also said regarding "Hashkiveinu" that it is like an extended Geulah, and nevertheless we hold that blessings do not impede one another; and if one omitted Hashkiveinu one fulfills through the blessing Emes v'Yatziv [as in Berachos 9a]; if so, necessarily it was said only regarding there not being an interruption between Geulah and prayer — and likewise in our matter.
