(10) He repeats, etc. This verse belongs to the prayer, as explained above, and it is proper to say it quietly. The other verses need not be repeated, though he may recite them if he wishes. Peri Megadim writes that after the chazzan has prayed quietly, he may interrupt only for a mitzvah matter, such as answering Yehei Shmei Rabba and the like, but apparently not for an optional matter.
