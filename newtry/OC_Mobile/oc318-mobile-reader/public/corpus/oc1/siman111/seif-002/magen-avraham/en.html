He repeats and says, etc. He says “Hashem, open my lips” quietly, according to the writings. He need not repeat “Who hears prayer” or the other verses, since he already said them quietly before the prayer. Even at Shacharis and Maariv, when he did not say them before his prayer, he need not say them, as stated by Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari, siman 110. This implies that the chazzan may interrupt after he has prayed quietly.
