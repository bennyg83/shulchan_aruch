8) [Se'if 4] He should not move away from the line opposite the city, etc. This is because mountains and valleys may lie opposite the city, and he must span them with the rope or measure them in successive segments. Beis Yosef in the name of Tosafos and Rosh; Magen Avraham, subsection 3.
9) There. He should not move away from the line opposite the city. This discusses a case in which the obstruction can be spanned opposite the city. If it cannot be spanned there, such as when one reaches a mountain more than fifty amos wide, the Shulchan Aruch writes below in se'if 6 that it may be spanned from the side. Perishah, subsection 4.
10) There. Rather, he should measure opposite the city, and when he reaches, etc. Nevertheless, if directly opposite the city there is level ground without mountains or valleys, he must measure on the level ground. Gaon Yaakov in the name of the Tosefta.
11) There. Such that if a plumb line were lowered. This refers to a builder's plumb line. Rashi.
