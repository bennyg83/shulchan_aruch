6) [Seif 3] One places the rope opposite his heart — the Sages fixed for him a place to set it opposite him at the head of the rope so he should not place one end opposite his neck and one opposite his feet, for then the rope shortens and the techum is reduced — Rashi. Likewise one may measure by placing the rope on the ground; we reduce only that he should not place one end opposite his head and one opposite his feet, and thus the techum is reduced. Chiddushei Ritva.
