6) [Se'if 3] He should position the rope opposite his heart. The Sages fixed a uniform place at which to hold each end of the rope. Otherwise, one person might hold it opposite his neck and the other opposite his feet, causing the rope to be shortened and the techum diminished. Rashi. The measurement may likewise be performed by laying the rope upon the ground. The Sages excluded only a method in which one end is held opposite a person's head and the other opposite another person's feet, thereby diminishing the techum. Ritva's novellae.
