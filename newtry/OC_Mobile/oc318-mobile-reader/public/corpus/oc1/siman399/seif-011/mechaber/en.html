Even a Canaanite bondman or maidservant {who accepted the observance of the mitzvos in which women are obligated} is [considered as] trustworthy when [he or she] says: “The Shabbos limits extend until here.” A minor, by contrast, is not considered as trustworthy [regarding this matter]. Nevertheless, an adult is considered as trustworthy [if] he says, “I remember that when I was a child we would proceed until this point on Shabbos.” His testimony is relied upon.
