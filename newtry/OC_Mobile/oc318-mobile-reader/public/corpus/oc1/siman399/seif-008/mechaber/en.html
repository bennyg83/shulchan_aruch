[The following laws apply when] the Shabbos limits [of a town] had been established and an expert came and measured [them again], maintaining that [the Shabbos limits] should be increased [in one place] and decreased [in another]: His rulings should be accepted even regarding the limits he increased. {Rama: Rema: If [the expert] measured and found that the measure in the northeast [corner] is larger than the parallel measure in the southeast [corner], he should extend the line of the Shabbos limits from [the northeast corner] to the [southeast corner] on a diagonal (Rosh and the Tur). And there are those who say, the resolution or decision will be made based on the level or flatness of the location where the argument occurred. (Beis Yosef in the name of Rashi and the Rashba).}
