2) There. Fifty amos long, etc. This is derived from the verse, 'The length of the courtyard was one hundred amos, and the width fifty by fifty.' The apparently extra letter in the expression 'by fifty' teaches that one should measure with a rope fifty amos long. Gemara. This is only a Scriptural support, for techumin are Rabbinic. Even according to the authority who maintains that twelve mil is of Torah origin, measuring with a fifty-amah rope is nevertheless Rabbinic. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1.
