An enriched [dough]. See Baer Heitev; and see Responsa Noda BiYehuda, part 1, siman 22, regarding a wheat kernel found on Pesach in kremzlich made of fat, honey, and water: one may not rely on permitting and say that fruit juice with water is only noksheh, and noksheh is nullified in sixty, because this depends on that: if we say that fruit juice with water is only noksheh, then automatically it is proven that noksheh is according to the Torah, as is clear to one who studies the Gemara; and if so, noksheh too is forbidden in any amount; see there. And see in the second edition, where he wrote to reconcile these words of his from what was asked of him against this from the words of the Rosh. And in Responsa Beis Ephraim, siman 40, I dealt at length with the words of the Noda BiYehuda and other poskim on this, in an incident where after baking the matzos they checked the wheat that remained and found in it seeds called kneblich that have juice and moisture, about five or six in every quart, and there is reason to be concerned about the matzos made from this wheat: one should be stringent in this like a little fruit juice with water, which hastens to leaven, as the Chok Yaakov wrote in siman 453 regarding the sweat of an animal, and there is a proof from the Semak concerning saliva in a mouse's mouth; see there. And therefore one should be stringent not to eat the matzos even if one baked before Pesach, if there is concern that there was not sixty and it was not nullified; see there. And see Responsa Masas Moshe, siman 2, regarding the law of wheat in which a mixture of salt was found; see there.
