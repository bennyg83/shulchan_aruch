To knead with wine - But with wine lees it is absolute chametz [Acharonim; and as Tosafos wrote on 28b, not like Or Zarua].
Since they were already nullified, etc. - See Mishnah Berurah, where we wrote in the name of most Acharonim that the case is that they mixed while the wine was fermenting. And know that nevertheless there are also several Acharonim who explain the words of the Shulchan Aruch according to their plain sense, that even with absolute wine a minority of water is nullified in fruit juice, as is truly implied from the plain language of the Mechaber, who concluded "since they were already nullified before they kneaded the dough," which indicates that specifically at the time of kneading the dough, if he mixed a little water into the dough kneaded with wine, it is not nullified, because the name "nullification" does not apply, for immediately upon joining with the dough they begin to perform the action of leavening even before the wine has time to nullify the water; which is not so before they came to the dough, when the majority properly nullifies the minority, and when afterward the wine comes to the dough it is like fruit juice alone. And that which the Mechaber wrote in se'if 6, that a wheat kernel found in honey, etc., is permitted provided that water did not mix into them: his intent is not that water did not mix into them even before they kneaded, but rather that water did not mix into them with the wheat and honey while they were mixed, as above; see the gloss in Magen Avraham, where his son-in-law explains thus; and so I found Halacha Berurah agreeing thus for the law; and so it appears that Baal Tzemach Tzedek and the Shelah explained, their words brought briefly in Magen Avraham s.k. 6, and in Peri Chadash he brought their words at length; and so wrote Peri Chadash and Mateh Yehudah in explaining their words. And so is the view of Eliyah Rabbah; and so appears the view of the Gra in s.k. 9; see there, where he proved the Mechaber's words from the passage in chapter "All [must be removed]", "this is with salt and oil," and even though salt is only water according to the Mechaber in se'if 7, and if so it is water with fruit juice, therefore a little salt is nullified [and he does not hold like the reasoning of the Ran, that it is because its form changed, as his words are brought below in siman 463 in Magen Avraham s.k. 1; and see Ritva there in the sugya]. And it is proven that the Gra's view of the Mechaber's intent is not for the reason that fermentation of wine turns the water into wine, for there that does not apply; therefore it is for the reason of ordinary nullification. And so agreed Baal Beis Meir, that this explanation is the most correct of the Mechaber's intent; and therefore even though most commentators did not explain thus (see Levush and Olas Shabbos and Magen Avraham and Peri Chadash in the name of Maharit and his father the Mabit, and Maamar Mordechai and Mateh Yehudah and Mor u'Ketzi'ah and other Acharonim), nevertheless one may rely on these poskim that I brought, at least regarding permitting to knead ab initio and guarding them from leavening like other dough kneaded with water, since according to the Rif and the Rambam in every place it is permitted to mix even much water with wine and to knead ab initio, only that one must guard from leavening like other dough kneaded with water; and if so, at least in our case where a little mixed and was already nullified, we can rely on them; and so ruled Chok Yaakov and Baal Magen Elef.
