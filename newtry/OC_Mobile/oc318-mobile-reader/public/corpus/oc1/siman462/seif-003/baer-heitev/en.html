Cascading stream (nitzok). Meaning: when one pours into a non-Jew's vessel that has liquid dampness, then if there is water in the wine in the upper vessel, we view the wine as if it is not present, and the water nullifies the non-Jew's wine (see Yoreh De'ah siman 166).
Before. It is implied that even if it mixed into it after the time of the vintage, nevertheless it was nullified. Chok Yaakov; and so wrote Eliyah Zuta. But if any amount of water mixed into fruit juice at the time of kneading, it is forbidden. Magen Avraham; and see s.k. 3 what is written there.
