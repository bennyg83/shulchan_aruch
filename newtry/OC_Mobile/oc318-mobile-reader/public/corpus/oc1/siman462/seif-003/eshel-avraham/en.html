A drop of water. And water that they throw into it after the vintage, one should not be lenient, for then it is no longer called fermenting; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 116 and Darkhei Moshe siman 26. And in the Gemara of Avodah Zarah folio 30 it is implied that their fermentation is only three days; and so he ruled in Teshuvos Eretz Yisrael, that afterward it no longer has the power to turn the water into wine; and he also resolves that until forty days there is no concern of gilui, with a broad explanation that they do not contradict one another; see there. And for this reason as well he permitted in a responsum, siman 10, a pot into which they put a fine powder of fine flour, and at the time of the vintage they filled it with wine to drink on Pesach; see there.
Since wine lees The Chok Yaakov wrote that they have the law of fruit juice; and in the Bach he wrote that raisin wine too has the law of fruit juice; and the Mabit wrote that all the more so honey made from raisins that he cooked in water has the law of fruit juice; see there; and see Even HaEzer s.k. 10.
