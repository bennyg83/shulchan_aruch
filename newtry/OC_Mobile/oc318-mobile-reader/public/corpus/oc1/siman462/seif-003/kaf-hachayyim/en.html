3) Vinegar is likewise included among fruit juices. Tosafos, Pesachim 35b; Tur and the Shulchan Aruch below in se'if 6; Chok Yaakov, subsection 7; Peri Chadash below in siman 466, se'if 5; Beis Yehudah, siman 226; Chemed Moshe, subsection 2; Chayei Adam, there; Chikrei Lev, Yoreh De'ah, volume 1, siman 183, page 256b. Erech HaShulchan, subsection 2, however, cites the first Rabbi Yeshayah as prohibiting, but writes that the authorities do not agree, as Maran states in se'if 6. See there. Ruach Chaim, subsection 6, writes that if one kneaded with vinegar after the fact, the dough should not be prohibited, but initially one should not act contrary to Rabbi Yeshayah's prohibition.
