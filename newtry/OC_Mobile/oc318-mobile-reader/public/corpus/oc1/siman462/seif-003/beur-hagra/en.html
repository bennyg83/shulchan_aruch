Se'if 3, And even ab initio, etc. In Hagahos Ashri, chapter 5 of Avodah Zarah, siman 28, s.v. Nevertheless, etc.
To permit a cascading stream (nitzok). For we hold that a cascading stream is a connection, as is written in Avodah Zarah 72a: R' Huna said, a cascading stream, etc.; and there b: R' Chisda said to those, etc.; and there in Tosafos s.v. R' Chisda said to him, and Rashi's explanation, etc. However, if he drew wine, etc. Even though the poskim disagree with Rabbeinu Tam regarding stam yeinam that it is not nullified in sixty, nevertheless if there was water in the barrel it is nullified in sixty, as is written there 73a: wine in water, etc.; and there: when Ravin came, R' Yochanan said, yayin nesech, etc.
And even so, one may not, etc. As is written in chapter "All [must be removed]," 39b: this is with salt and oil. Even though salt is of the derivatives of water and causes leavening, as is written in se'if 7; and therefore Tosafos explained in chapter "All [must be removed]," 35b, and in Menachos 54a, regarding Edomite vinegar, that the barley soaks in water beforehand; and not like the plain sense of the Yerushalmi's language that there is water in the vinegar.
