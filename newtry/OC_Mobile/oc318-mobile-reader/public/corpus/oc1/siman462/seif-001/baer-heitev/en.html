He wrote in Kenesses HaGedolah, and these are his words: And the Ranach, part 2, siman 74, concluded that a God-fearing person should take into account those who hold that fruit juice without water causes leavening, and he should be careful with guarding it like other dough; and my father, of blessed memory, was careful not to eat matzah kneaded with fruit juice except on the last Yom Tov; also in Tummas Yesharim siman 108 he is stringent in the matter; end quote.
Enriched. It is implied that specifically in such a case, where it was kneaded with fruit juice alone, it is enriched matzah; but when kneaded with a mixture of water it is not called enriched matzah; and so wrote the Bach in siman 471, that after the fact one fulfills with it; and so wrote Maharal in Sefer Gevuros Hashem, that with fruit juice with water one fulfills the obligation of matzah after the fact. But the Magen Avraham in siman 471 s.k. 5 challenged them; and so is the consensus of the Acharonim; and see above in Magen Avraham siman 471, that one does not fulfill the obligation of poor man's bread as long as there is in it even a little fruit juice, unless it is a very tiny amount; see there. And it appears to me that anything nullified in sixty such that one does not sense in it the taste of fruit juice is called bread of poverty. Chok Yaakov; see there.
