Se'if 1, Fruit juice. As is written in chapter "All [must be removed]," 39b and 40a, and Menachos 57.
Without water. For with water they cause leavening, as is written in Pesachim 35a: dough that was kneaded, etc., they are not liable, etc., but there is a prohibition; and there 36a: one does not knead, etc.; and there 40b: one does not put, etc.; and there 42a: Median beer and vinegar, etc.; and they said there in the Yerushalmi, and the Rosh brought it there: and all of them through water; and in Menachos 34a: one does not cause leavening with apples because, etc.; and there 57b: R' Levi said, etc., he kneads it with water and it is valid.
And it is permitted, etc. As is written in Pesachim 36a: the first day, etc.; from then on, etc.; and the Rosh wrote that he can well hold like the Rabbis who say one does not knead, only that he deals with without water.
Even if it waited. As is written 39b: and a cake of pressed fruit is permitted, etc.; and as above.
But one may not. As above: the first day, etc.; and there, bread of poverty, excluding, etc.; that is no difficulty: this is on the first Yom Tov, etc.; and see there in the Rosh, that is to say, the first olive-size.
