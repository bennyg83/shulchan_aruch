He wrote in Shiltei Gibborim folio 238: apples and pomegranates that are sour, the liquid that issues from them causes leavening, for every sour liquid causes leavening; end quote. And it appears to me that he derived this from the Mishnah in chapter 10 of Terumos: an apple that he crushed, etc.; but in Menachos folio 54 it is stated explicitly in the Gemara and in Tosafos that they are fruit juice; and so wrote the Rambam and the Semag: apples and pomegranates and olives and bee honey all are fruit juice; and so wrote Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 5 and Semak siman 219. He should bake immediately. And it requires further study if a little fruit juice fell into the dough and it was not baked immediately, whether one should forbid it after the fact. And in the Rosh it is implied that a small amount does not forbid, for he wrote that glazing is a small amount and does not cause leavening; and see the gloss in se'if 4.
