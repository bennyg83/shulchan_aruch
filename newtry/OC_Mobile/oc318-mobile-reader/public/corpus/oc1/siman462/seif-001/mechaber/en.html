דין מי פירות אם מחמיצין. ובו ז סעיפים:
The law of whether fruit juices leaven. And in it are 7 seifim: Fruit juice without water does not leaven at all, and it is permitted to eat on Pesach matzah that was kneaded with fruit juice, even if it stood idle the entire day; but one does not fulfill his obligation with it, because it is matzah ashirah, and the verse states "lechem oni."
