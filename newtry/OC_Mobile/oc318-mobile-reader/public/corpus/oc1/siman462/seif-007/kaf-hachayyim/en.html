7) Human milk has the status of fruit juice. Admas Kodesh, volume 1, siman 8; Birkei Yosef, subsection 3; Sha'arei Teshuvah, subsection 10. Accordingly, Admas Kodesh rules there that if a nursing woman kneaded dough for Pesach and milk dripped from her body into it, the dough has the status of fruit juice mixed with water if it is not baked immediately. See there. See Olas Shabbos there, which writes that the matzah may be eaten with meat. See there.
