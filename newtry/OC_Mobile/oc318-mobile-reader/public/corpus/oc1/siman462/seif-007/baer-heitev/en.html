And the Magen Avraham dealt at length and concluded that after the fact, if a wheat kernel is found during Pesach in salt, he should throw away from it a netilah's measure, and keep the rest until after Pesach; and if he used it during Pesach, one should not forbid the food after the fact unless they put from that netilah's measure into the food; see there. And all this is when the wheat is moistened, that is, the wheat softened; but if it was dry it is permitted in every way; so wrote the Bach (and Magen Avraham left it in further study); only that he concluded that kutoach is different, for it is a sharp thing, and for this reason Beis Yosef in the name of the Rambam forbids placing a jar of salt next to one of kutoach. And Darkhei Moshe wrote that our salt that they cook from water certainly causes leavening; and in the Tur it is implied that salt dug from the ground, according to all, does not cause leavening; see there. Maharshal wrote: one should sort the spices; and it is obvious that after the fact it is permitted if a wheat kernel is found in them, for they are dry. Magen Avraham; and see Taz and Chok Yaakov.
