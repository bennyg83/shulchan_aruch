Before Pesach. Meaning: and he throws it away, and the salt is permitted; but during Pesach the salt is forbidden, for chametz is forbidden in any amount. And my father-in-law, of blessed memory, wrote: Nevertheless, if it is not moistening, that is, it did not soften at all but is dry as originally, the salt is permitted before Pesach even if it imparts taste, for it expelled nothing; and see siman 467. And the law mentioned here regarding salt is obviously the same for spices.
