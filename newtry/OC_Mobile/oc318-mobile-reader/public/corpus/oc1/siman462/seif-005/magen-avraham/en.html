With fruit juice it is good, etc. It is implied in Beis Yosef here that even if he kneaded with wine and honey the law is so, for there are those who say he is not obligated in challah unless it is kneaded with water; and so wrote the Tur in Yoreh De'ah siman 329. And nevertheless it is difficult: and what of it? Let him knead an issaron and separate challah without a blessing. Furthermore, in Yoreh De'ah the Shulchan Aruch ruled that it is obligated in challah; and so wrote Derishah there in the name of Maharshal, that even the Rosh disagrees only regarding egg liquid, but regarding the seven liquids he admits that it is obligated; end quote. Therefore one must say that it refers to fruit juices that are not of the seven liquids, as is written in Yoreh De'ah the reason that it was not made susceptible, and it is forbidden to burn it for it is tahor challah, and also a kohen should not eat it for we are all tamei; see the end of siman 457. However, in a place where they are accustomed to wash the wheat, if so the wheat was made susceptible and there is no insistence in the matter; and regarding the seven liquids see siman 158 se'if 4.
