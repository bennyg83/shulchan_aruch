(s.k. 5) With, etc. And nevertheless it is difficult: and what of it, etc.? For it is not correct to exempt dough from challah and to knead less than a shiur, as it is in Pesachim, which Taz brought; unless there is a necessity. And so wrote Derishah, etc.: this is difficulty 3, for even according to the Rosh who exempts, that is specifically fruit juice that is not of the seven liquids; and if so, why when kneading with wine is it permitted to make less than a shiur? For of that sort, according to all, it is obligated; rather [the ruling] is regarding egg liquid and the same for other fruit juices that are not of the seven liquids, since it is in the Yerushalmi that if he kneaded dough with them it is not a connection regarding tumah; and if a tevul yom touched part of the dough he invalidated only the place of his touch and not the whole dough. And the same applies that it is not a connection regarding challah, and it is like dough less than a shiur; therefore one must say that it refers to fruit juices that are not of the seven liquids. And with this difficulty 3 is resolved. But the first two difficulties are still not resolved.
And as is written in Yoreh De'ah the reason, etc.; and with this he also resolves the first two difficulties.
And it is forbidden to burn it. Granted, if it was kneaded with one of the seven liquids, which make susceptible, then since we are all tamei from the dead the dough and the challah became tamei; and therefore he may burn it like the law of tamei terumah and challah. And even so the Sages were lenient regarding challah of outside the Land, that a kohen from whose body tumah has not issued, if he wishes, may eat it; even though he is tamei from the dead and the challah is also tamei. But if it was kneaded with fruit juices that are not of the seven liquids, which do not make susceptible, the dough and also the challah remain tahor; and tahor challah is forbidden to burn.
And also, etc. And see siman 457. That is to say: even though nevertheless a kohen from whose body tumah has not issued is permitted to eat it; and that is an adult who immersed for his emission, or a minor less than nine years old, as Rav Bezalel also wrote here in the name of Rabbeinu Nesanel; nevertheless it is explained above at the end of siman 457 that the custom is not to feed a kohen even a minor or an adult who immersed; and see there the reason; therefore he should make the dough less than a shiur.
