[5] And even to glaze them, etc. Rather after their baking while they are still hot (Rama); and Levush relied on what he wrote in the next se'if regarding egg liquid; and in Shibbolei HaLeket he wrote that there are those who forbid by law glazing before baking, and so ruled Rabbeinu Yeruham and Sefer Tanya; but on the first days of Pesach it is clear in Shibbolei HaLeket that according to all it is forbidden, for we require bread of poverty; and according to this the same applies after baking on the first days, and even with a small amount, since it is present visibly, and not like Responsa Beis Yaakov there; and so is implied in Chok Yaakov; and so is the custom even with other matzos on the first days, in memory of enriched matzah; and see siman 471 s.k.
