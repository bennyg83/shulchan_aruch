Less than the shiur for challah. The reason, in Beis Yosef, is: regarding dough kneaded with egg liquid or with wine and honey without any water, the Rambam wrote that it is obligated in challah, and the Rosh was in doubt, and he wrote: and it is good to be careful not to knead any dough with egg liquid alone without a mixture of water, in order to leave the realm of doubt; end quote. And accordingly, on Pesach, when it is forbidden to knead with egg liquid mixed with water, one should knead dough less than the shiur for challah; end of Beis Yosef's words. And these same words are also here in the Shulchan Aruch. And I am greatly astonished, for in Yoreh De'ah siman 329 the Shulchan Aruch ruled like the Rambam that it is obligated in challah; and if so, certainly one recites a blessing over them, since he wrote plainly that it is obligated in challah; so why did he rule here that one should not knead with them the amount of a shiur? And if you say that this is a stringency, that is not so, for then there too one should not recite a blessing over them. Furthermore, here it is a stringency that leads to a leniency, for he exempts it from challah, as it is in chapter "These are the things," where Rav Yosef said: these women of ours are accustomed to knead a kapiza by kapiza for Pesach; Abaye said to him: what is your reasoning, [to do so] as a stringency? It is a stringency that leads to a leniency, for you exempt it from challah. And if so, all the more so here, where we hold that by law it is obligated in challah; and this requires further study.
