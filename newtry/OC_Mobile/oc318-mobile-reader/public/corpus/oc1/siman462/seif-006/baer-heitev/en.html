With wine. And the Acharonim wrote in the name of the Rashbatz that raisin wine or raisin honey made with water whose taste changed entirely is like plain fruit juice, for the water in which the raisins soaked changed from their original nature; and so is the consensus of the Mabit and Maharit; and Chacham Tzvi in his responsum siman 101 wrote for law and practice that with our raisin wine it is forbidden to knead on Pesach, for its law is not like absolute wine but like diluted wine; see there; and see Yad Aharon. And see in Magen Avraham, who left in further study a wheat kernel found in mead called mead and the like, whether it is called fruit juice since its taste changed through cooking. And Taz brought an incident: once a servant brought a vessel with honey called mead to drink, and one wheat kernel was found in it, and he permitted the honey to drink, because cold does not forbid; see siman 467 s.k. 17 at length. And regarding if a wheat kernel is found in hot goose fat: in the Shelah he forbids, since it had been soaked in water. And Eliyah Zuta wrote that his grandfather ruled to keep it until after Pesach; and in Responsa Tzemach Tzedek siman 122 he ruled to permit, for the water had already dried; see there; and so wrote Magen Avraham to permit; and he concluded: but with fried dishes where they soak the matzah in water and afterward squeeze them and knead them with fat, and a wheat kernel fell into it, one should be stringent, for water remains in it visibly, for it is impossible to squeeze them completely; until here. But Peri Chadash at the end of siman 467 holds to be stringent like the words of the Shelah; and see Beis Yosef siman 99. I was asked about lekach kneaded with honey without water that he forgot and did not sell before Pesach, what is its law; and I said to sell them to a non-Jew, since he was not careful at the time of kneading to knead it on a board that is not of chametz; and even for eating, one who is lenient has not lost; from Baer Heitev before me; see there.
Water. It is astonishing, for in se'if 3 he wrote that even if water mixed into them it is nullified; and see the Acharonim and Chok Yaakov for what they answered.
