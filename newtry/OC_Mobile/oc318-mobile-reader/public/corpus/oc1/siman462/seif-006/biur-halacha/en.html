And provided that it did not mix, etc. - See Mishnah Berurah regarding what he wrote, that if the wheat found in honey liquid did not crack but only softened, one may be lenient even though there is room to doubt perhaps it stayed twenty-four hours; and that is because it is a double doubt in a prohibition of any amount: a doubt of pickling, and a doubt whether it is not chametz at all, for softening is only doubtful chametz [aside from the doubt of fruit juice]. And although Mishbetzos Zahav below in siman 467 note 18 doubts to be stringent regarding drinking even when it softened, nevertheless in my humble opinion one who relies to be lenient has not lost; see below in siman 467 se'if 12 in Machatzit HaShekel, who questions the very law of Magen Avraham who is stringent in a doubt of pickling; and especially in this, where Beis Meir's view is that a wheat kernel in honey liquid or raisin wine imparts a foul taste (see there in siman 467), certainly one may be lenient at least when it softened, for there are several doubts toward permission.
