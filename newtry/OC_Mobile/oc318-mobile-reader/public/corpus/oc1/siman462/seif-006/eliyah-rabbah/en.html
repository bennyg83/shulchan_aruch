[6] [Levush] For example wine, etc. Even wine lees (Chok Yaakov); and likewise olive oil and apple liquid and pomegranate liquid and all of their kind, and other wines and oils (Rambam), and bee honey, and not like Shiltei Gibborim, as I wrote in Eliyahu Zuta; and so the Acharonim agreed. Raisin wine is among grape [products] that do not cause leavening, for the water in which the raisins soaked changed from their original nature (Bedek HaBayis and Mabit and Maharit and Kenesses HaGedolah); and likewise honey made from raisins, where they soak the raisins in water and squeeze them and cook them, for the water in it changed by fire more than the change through wine (Maharit). And Magen Avraham wrote: and it requires further study regarding mead called mead, for there too the water changed from its original nature. A wheat kernel found in goose fat: the Shelah forbids, since it was already soaked in water; and my grandfather the Gaon, of blessed memory, ruled to keep it until after Pesach; and in Responsa Tzemach Tzedek and Magen Avraham they permitted for eating; and see siman 467 se'if 14, where it is also implied that it is permitted; [but] when they soak the matzah in water and squeeze it and afterward knead it with fat and a wheat kernel fell into it, one should be stringent, for water remains in it visibly, for it is impossible to squeeze them so thoroughly.
