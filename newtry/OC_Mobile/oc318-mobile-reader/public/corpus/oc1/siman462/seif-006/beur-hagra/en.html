Se'if 6, A wheat kernel, etc.; and provided. Yerushalmi, and the Rosh brought it in chapter "All [must be removed]," se'if 13: R' Shmuel had fermenting wine; he put barley into it, etc. R' Chiyya had honey adulterated with fine flour; he came and asked R' Mana; he said to him: You are obligated to destroy [it]. And for our Babylonian Talmud one must say that there was water in them; and so they said in the Yerushalmi at the beginning of chapter 2 regarding Edomite vinegar into which they put barley, and all of them, etc.; so it appears to me.
