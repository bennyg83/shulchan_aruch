(4) Ohr HaChayyim and Kol Bo wrote in the name of R' Yehuda that milk is not fruit juice and it chametzs; and that which Beit Yosef wondered on Kesef Mishneh — see Razah in ch. 21 that such is his view; but Ra'avad in Sefer HaTemimah and Ramban in Milchamot and Ran there proved that milk and likewise liquids are like wine, oil, and honey; and so is the view of Rokeach.
