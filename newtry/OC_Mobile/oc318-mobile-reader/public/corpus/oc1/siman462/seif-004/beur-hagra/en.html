Se'if 4, Egg liquid and other, etc. So it is implied in the Tosefta chapter 3 and in the Yerushalmi there, law 4, that all other liquids are included in fruit juice; and he said egg liquid to exclude from Rashi, who was in doubt because the dough swells from it; but the main point is that since it is not of the derivatives of water it does not cause leavening; and a proof is that they do not make susceptible, as is written in chapter 1 of Sanhedrin; and see siman 466 what is written there.
And in the countries, etc. Because of concern of a mixture of water, and also to be concerned for the view of Rashi, who wrote that that [statement] "one does not knead," etc., disagrees with the view that fruit juice does not cause leavening, and Rashi holds that even without water they cause leavening; and Raavad in the Hasagos at the beginning of chapter 5; and Maggid Mishneh wrote there: And I say this is an old dispute, etc.; and also my teachers, may they rest in Eden, were divided about it, etc.; and the rest of my French teachers, may they rest in Eden, practiced a stringency in it, and I after them practiced a stringency for myself, etc.
And even to glaze. Like the Rabbis there who say: that with which one does not knead, one does not glaze.
