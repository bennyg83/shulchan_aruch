(12) Egg liquid, etc.: for one might have thought it is worse than fruit juice because the dough swells from it; it teaches us that this is not leavening.
(13) And other liquids: for anything that is not of the derivatives of water does not cause leavening; and the details of this matter will be explained below in siman 466; see there.
(14) They are included in fruit juice: and the laws explained in se'if 1 and 2 apply to them.
(15) They are not accustomed to knead, etc.: for ab initio they are concerned for the view of those poskim who hold that fruit juice alone also causes leavening and also hastens to leaven. And they are also concerned that perhaps a little water mixed into them, which according to all causes leavening.
(16) And even to glaze, etc.: that is to say, to smear the faces of the matzos with fruit juice, even though it is a small amount.
(17) While they are still hot: and the Acharonim wrote that regarding this too one should be careful not to take them for the first nights for the olive-size of obligation, for it is like enriched matzah, and especially when afterward they return them again to the oven.
(18) And one should not change: and according to the custom, even if he already kneaded and baked immediately, one should not eat it on Pesach; rather keep it until after Pesach.
(19) For the needs of a sick person, etc.: however, bake them immediately, as above; and specifically with fruit juice alone; but with fruit juice with water one should not knead ab initio even in a time of need.
