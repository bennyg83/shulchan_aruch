And even to glaze the matzos. It is difficult: why "even"? On the contrary, when they knead with water and glaze with fruit juice it is fruit juice with water and they hasten to leaven; and so is implied in the Rif. And one may say that this is what he means: even glazing, which is a small amount, as I wrote in se'if 2. And it is obvious that there is no distinction between milk and other fruit juices; see in the Rif and Rosh, not like Olas Shabbos.
