(s.k. 4) And even, etc. And it is obvious, etc., not like Olas Shabbos, who holds that milk is certainly permitted because R' Yehoshua ben Levi did an act and kneaded with milk; but in truth in all the poskim it is implied that milk is equal to wine and oil and honey. And R' Yehoshua ben Levi ruled like R' Akiva, who permits also with wine and oil and honey.
