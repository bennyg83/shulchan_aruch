With water. And the same applies if it was kneaded first with fruit juice and afterward water came upon it before it was baked, or the reverse; Chok Yaakov; see there (and likewise when the fruit juice dried within the flour and afterward the water came upon them; Ran and Peri Chadash; see there; Rashba permits it).
Immediately. And if it was not baked immediately: since it did not wait the measure of a mil without activity, and the measure of leavening is not recognizable in it, and the like: one should forbid eating and permit benefit and keeping it until after Pesach; Chok Yaakov; see there. And the Magen Avraham wrote: and it requires further study if a little fruit juice fell into the dough and it was not baked immediately, whether one should forbid it after the fact; and in the Rosh it is implied that a small amount does not forbid; end quote. And the Baer Heitev before me wrote, and these are his words: And on the basis of the Taz I permitted in a case where one was kneading the dough and a little sweat from his face fell into the dough; examine carefully; end quote. And all this is unnecessary, for it is explained in siman 466 that human sweat does not cause leavening; see there.
