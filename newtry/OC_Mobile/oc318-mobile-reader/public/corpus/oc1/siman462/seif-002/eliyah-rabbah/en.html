[2] With water, etc. And the same applies if it was kneaded first with fruit juice and afterward water came upon it before it was baked, or the reverse (Ran in the responsum of ninety of the Rosh, and Semag, and Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 5). Magen Avraham wrote: if a little fruit [juice] fell into the dough and it was not baked immediately, it requires further study; and in the Rosh it is implied that a small amount does not forbid; until here. And in s.k. 3 he wrote that if any amount of water fell into fruit juice at the time of kneading it is forbidden (Semag and Ran 72); and it does not appear correct to distinguish between fruit juice at the end and water at the end; and it appears that above he deals with where it fell after it was kneaded, but at the time of kneading even a little forbids, and not like Taz; and see siman 455 s.k. 9. But it requires further study what proof Magen Avraham brings from the Rosh that glazing, which is a small amount, does not forbid; for he wrote this according to R' Akiva, but the law is like the Sages on folio 36, where it is stated that what one does not knead one does not glaze; and see in Bach. And with this is resolved what was asked in Responsa Beis Yaakov siman 68; and see siman 453 s.k.
