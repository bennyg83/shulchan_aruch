(s.k. 2) To permit, etc. Regarding the wine in the upper vessel. For the upper wine is forbidden because of cascading-stream connection, as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 126, that it is as if the wine in the lower vessel mixed with the wine in the upper vessel. And with absolute yayin nesech it forbids in any amount; and with stam yeinam we require sixty against the forbidden wine; and if in the upper wine there is also water, we say: remove the upper wine, and the water nullifies; for for water it suffices if it is six parts like the forbidden wine, as is written there siman 134; and see there siman 126 and in Shach s.k. 11.
