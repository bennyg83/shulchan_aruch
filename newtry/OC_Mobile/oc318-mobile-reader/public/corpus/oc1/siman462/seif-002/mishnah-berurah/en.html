(6) With water: whether the fruit juice is more and the water less, or the reverse. And the Acharonim wrote that there is no distinction between whether it was kneaded first with fruit juice and afterward water came upon it before it was baked, or the reverse. And if the fruit juice dried within the flour and afterward the water came upon them, see Biur Halacha.
(7) They hasten to leaven, etc.: that is to say, even in less than the measure of a mil it is prone to leaven; therefore, etc., for we are concerned that perhaps he will wait before baking.
(8) He should bake immediately: and if he transgressed and waited before baking, one should forbid eating even if he did not wait the measure of a mil; and in Eliyah Rabbah he tends to permit in a case of great loss even for eating, as long as he did not wait the measure of a mil and signs of leavening are not recognizable in it. And it appears that if this happened unwittingly, certainly one may rely to be lenient in a time of need, as above in siman 455 se'if 3 regarding kneading with warm water; see there. And know that the Acharonim agreed that even though we hold that if he baked immediately it is permitted to eat that matzah, nevertheless one does not fulfill with it the obligation of matzah, for anything that has fruit juice in it, even a little, as long as the taste of the fruit juice is sensed in it, is called enriched matzah, and one does not fulfill with it. And not only that, but even if he kneaded with water and afterward glazed it from above with fruit juice and baked it, he also does not fulfill with it the obligation of matzah, for it is then no longer bread of poverty.
