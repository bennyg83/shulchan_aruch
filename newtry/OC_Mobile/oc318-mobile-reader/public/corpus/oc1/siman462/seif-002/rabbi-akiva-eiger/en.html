(Se'if 2) Fruit juice with water hastens to leaven. In the Chok Yaakov he is in doubt whether first fruit juice dried into flour and afterward they put water into it.
(Magen Avraham s.k. 1) And it does not cause leavening; and so is the view of Maharsha, folio 38, to permit; but regarding a little water it is clear in the Ran, brought in the Taz at the beginning of the next siman, to be stringent.
