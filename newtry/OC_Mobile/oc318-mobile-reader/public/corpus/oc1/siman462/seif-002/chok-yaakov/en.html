It should be baked immediately. If it was not baked immediately, but did not remain without handling for the time required to walk a mil and no sign of leavening is visible, in such a case it should be forbidden for consumption but permitted for benefit and may be retained until after Pesach. This is also implied by Kol Bo, cited by Beit Yosef in siman 455 in the name of Raavad. This is especially so according to Rif in Perek Kol Shaah, as Rosh explains his view at the beginning of Perek Eilu Ovrin, and according to Rambam in chapter 5 of Hilchos Chametz, together with Maggid Mishneh in the name of some commentators, who maintain that it does not leaven more rapidly than ordinary dough.
