1) [Se'if 1] Ya'aleh V'Yavo is mentioned in Birkas HaMazon. Regarding the recitation of Ya'aleh V'Yavo in Boneh Yerushalayim, Tosafos on Shabbos 24a write that since Ya'aleh V'Yavo is a prayer and supplication, it was instituted in Boneh Yerushalayim, which is likewise a prayer. In the eighteen blessings of the Amidah, it was instituted in the blessing of Avodah, which is a prayer for the return of the Jewish people to Yerushalayim. Beis Yosef cites their words.
