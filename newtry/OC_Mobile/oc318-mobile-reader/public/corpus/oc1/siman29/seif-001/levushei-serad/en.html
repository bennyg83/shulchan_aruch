Magen Avraham. “And it also.” Meaning, the statement in siman 31 in the name of Midrash HaNe'elam contradicts what is written at the beginning of siman 308. Beit Yosef's statement here, that it is forbidden because perhaps he will go out wearing them, does not contradict the beginning of siman 308. Beit Yosef did not intend an absolute prohibition here, for it is not a formal decree, as is clear from his words. He meant only that it is proper to be careful. Accordingly, Magen Avraham's difficulty is only from the statement in siman 31 against that in siman 308. He resolves it by distinguishing between one who puts them on for the sake of the mitzvah and one who does not.
