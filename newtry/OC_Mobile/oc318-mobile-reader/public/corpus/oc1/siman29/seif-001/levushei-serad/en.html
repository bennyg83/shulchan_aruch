Magen Avraham—and also. Meaning: that which is in siman 31 in the name of the hidden midrash contradicts what is stated at the beginning of siman 308; but the words of Beit Yosef here, who forbids due to concern lest one go out, do not contradict the beginning of siman 308—therefore Beit Yosef here did not state a full prohibition, for it is not a full rabbinic decree as explained in Beit Yosef, and it is only to say that one should be careful—thus is Magen Avraham's intent, only regarding the difficulty of that siman 31 on that of siman 308; and on this he distinguishes between when one lays them for the sake of a mitzvah.
