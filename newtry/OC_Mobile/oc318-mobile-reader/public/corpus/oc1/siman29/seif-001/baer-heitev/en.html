One does not recite a blessing. For this reason, it is proper to remove the tefillin with the left hand, because an item that requires a blessing is taken with the right hand. See what Shiurei Knesses HaGedolah writes. Bnei Chayei resolves the matter; see there.
Twilight. See Magen Avraham, who concludes that if one puts them on during Shabbos for the sake of the mitzvah, he violates bal tosif. If, however, he puts them on not for the sake of the mitzvah, there is no prohibition. See what Magen Avraham writes in siman 308, seif katan 11. According to the distinction he makes here, his difficulty there disappears, and I do not know why he did not answer there with this distinction. See responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 19, and Sefer Yad Aharon.
