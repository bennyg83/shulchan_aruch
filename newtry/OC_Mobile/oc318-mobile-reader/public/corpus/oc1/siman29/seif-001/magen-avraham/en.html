Erev Shabbos bein hashmashos. In Beit Yosef—the reason is that there is no Torah prohibition to wear them on Shabbos except due to concern lest one go out with them to the public domain; therefore a blessing is not relevant, etc. And it requires study—for in siman 31 he wrote in the name of the hidden midrash that one who belittles the king's seal is liable to death; and this also contradicts what is stated at the beginning of siman 308 in the name of Tosafos, Rashi, and Ran, that there is no prohibition at all to wear them on Shabbos. And one must say that when one lays them for the sake of a mitzvah it is as belittling the king's seal, but when one lays them not for the sake of a mitzvah there is no prohibition to wear them, as stated above.
