On Erev Shabbos. Beit Yosef explains that there is no prohibition against putting on tefillin on Shabbos except because perhaps one will go out wearing them into the public domain. Therefore, no blessing is appropriate. This requires study. In siman 31, Beit Yosef writes in the name of Midrash HaNe'elam that one who wears them on Shabbos degrades the King's seal and is liable to death. This also contradicts what is written at the beginning of siman 308 in the name of Tosafos, Rashi, and Ran, that there is no prohibition at all against putting them on during Shabbos. One must therefore say that when he puts them on for the sake of the mitzvah, it is considered degrading the King's seal. If he puts them on not for the sake of the mitzvah, there is no prohibition. This appears correct.
