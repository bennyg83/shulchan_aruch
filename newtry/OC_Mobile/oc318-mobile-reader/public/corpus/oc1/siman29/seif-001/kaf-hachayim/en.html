(1) [Section 1] One should not recite any blessing when removing tefillin, etc.—Explanation: to exclude the people of the West, who would bless "asher kid'shanu b'mitzvosav v'tzivanu le-shomer chukav" when removing tefillin near sunset—for it is not difficult according to them, for they hold that "and you shall observe this statute from days to days" refers to tefillin, and night is not time for tefillin; therefore they would bless. But we find difficult that the verse "and you shall observe this statute," etc., speaks of the statute of Passover, and night is time for tefillin—even according to Rambam, who holds night is not time for tefillin, he did not write to bless this blessing, as the reason is explained in Beit Yosef. And what is written "even when removing them on erev Shabbos during twilight"—meaning that then, according to all, one must remove them, for Shabbos is not time for tefillin according to all—nevertheless one should not bless, because the reason one must remove them then is not derived from "and you shall observe the statute," but because it is written regarding them "and it shall be for you as a sign on your hand"—to exclude Shabbosos and Yamim Tovim, which are themselves a sign—as explained in all this in Tur, Beit Yosef, and Levush. And it will be explained further below in siman 30, note 1 and note 4, with God's help. However, to make a remembrance of the people of the West who would bless le-shomer chukav, one may say when removing tefillin the verse "And He said: If you will surely heed the voice," etc.—Ruach Chayim note 3; Yafeh Lelev note 1. And likewise Chida siman 28 note 3.
