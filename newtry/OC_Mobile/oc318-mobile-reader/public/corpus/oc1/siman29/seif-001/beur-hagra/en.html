Shulchan Aruch: one should not bless, etc. Although in Niddah 11b and at the end of chapter 6 of Berachos they say that the people of the West would bless, etc., from Rabbeinu Tam [it follows] specifically when removing them near sunset; and according to the one who says night is not time for tefillin, one transgresses bal tosif, as written in Menachos 36b. But we rule as the one who says night is time for tefillin, as was written in the halachah, and we do not instruct thus—and like Rabbi Akiva, who [holds that] "ushmartem" in Chukat: the verse speaks of Pesach.

Even when removing them, etc. That is: although Shabbos is certainly not time for tefillin, since we do not derive from "chukah," we do not bless—as with tzitzit; for only regarding tefillin would they bless, because it is written "chukah." But we derive from "v'hayah l'os," etc., as was written—we do not bless. And as written in Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Berachos: when he removes them, what does he say? "Blessed [are You…] Who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to observe His statutes"—and this accords with the one who says in Chukat the verse speaks of tefillin; but according to the one who says in Chukat the verse speaks of Pesach, no. And Tosafot wrote in Niddah: because since we derive from "ushmartem," there is no obligation to remove them. See Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham; and see Tosafot in Niddah and Berachos and Menachos there, and Eruvin 96a.
