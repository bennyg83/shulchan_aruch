[5] [Levush] For he is afraid to leave, etc. I do not know why the Rama ruled thus, since most Rishonim and Acharonim permitted only a key needed for ochel nefesh or an ornament (Taz); and so Maharshal ruled; and so I explained above the view of the Levush. And the Bach wrote: one should be inclined to be lenient regarding a rabbinic prohibition, and so they practiced; except that the masses do not know how to distinguish and are lenient even in taking out a key with no need at all, and one must compel them to heed the words of the Sages; and one who is stringent in all these, a blessing shall come upon him; until here. And even a key of foods that is needed: there is no permit to take it out except when he is afraid to leave them at home, as we said; and then it is permitted even if they are not important; and one who is stringent, a blessing shall come upon him. And the Bach was stringent that he should not take out a key tied in his belt with a strap in weekday fashion, but rather on his finger. Knives: the Taz concludes that it is permitted to take them out after eating, even outside the city, lest some fruit present itself to him to eat with it.
