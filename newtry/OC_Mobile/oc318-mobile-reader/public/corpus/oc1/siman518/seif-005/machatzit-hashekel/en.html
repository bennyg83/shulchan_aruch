(seif katan 5) "And if, etc." — this implies that without an eruv it is forbidden, etc. — to exclude from the opinion of the Rif, who holds that on Yom Tov they did not institute eruv chatzeros. And as he wrote in seif katan 4, in the name of the Rashba which Tosafos brought in Kesubos, we do not rule according to his view; rather, an eruv is required. And for this reason, the Taz wrote in the name of the Maharshal in this siman, and the Magen Avraham above at the end of siman 367, that one should add to the text of eruv chatzeros — which one recites "for all the Shabboses of the year" — and also say "and for all Yom Tov." And there is no concern of changing the custom in this, since he is not performing a new act.
