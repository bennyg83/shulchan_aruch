(s.k. 5) And if, etc. It implies that without an eruv it is forbidden, etc., to exclude from the view of the Rif who holds that on Yom Tov they did not institute eruv chatzeros. And as he wrote in s.k. 4 in the name of the Rashba which Tosafos brought in Kesubos: we do not hold like him, but rather an eruv is required. And for this reason the Taz wrote in the name of Maharshal in this siman, and Magen Avraham above at the end of siman 367, that one should add in the text of eruv chatzeros that one says "for all the Shabbosos of the year," and also say "and for all Yom Tov." And there is no concern of changing a custom in this, since he is not performing a new act.
