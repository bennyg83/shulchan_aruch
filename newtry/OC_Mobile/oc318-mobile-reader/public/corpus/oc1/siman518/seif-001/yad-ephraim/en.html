In Magen Avraham subsection 13: and it is possible that he holds that a lamp made to extinguish is more [muktzeh], etc.; and one can say that he holds that since there is no Torah prohibition in demolishing it, etc. — so it should read. And what Magen Avraham wrote "but the Ran and Tosafos etc." — meaning regarding a sukkah it helps, as Magen Avraham wrote there subsection 7 from the words of Tosafos on Shabbos daf 44 that there is one who says so — see there; and in my humble opinion emend in place of the word "Tosafos" — it should read "Ramban," and it is not in the text. And what Magen Avraham wrote "but the Maor wrote etc." — it is very astonishing that he skipped in his words, for it is there; and the difficulty is: on Yom Tov like R' Yehuda, and muktzeh machmat issur such as the leftover oil in a lamp — even on Shabbat we rule like R' Yehuda, etc. — see there it is explained that he holds a rickety sukkah is not muktzeh machmat issur; rather according to his view on Yom Tov the halacha follows R' Yehuda even on ordinary muktzeh. And afterward he wrote regarding muktzeh machmat mitzva such as leftover oil — even on Shabbat we rule like R' Yehuda, see there. And according to Magen Avraham's words, the Maor's words would contradict themselves — for at first he wrote a rickety one is not muktzeh machmat mitzva, and how did he write at the end that it is forbidden even on Shabbat due to muktzeh machmat mitzva? But in truth this is what it means: certainly he holds this is not muktzeh machmat mitzva; rather he holds we rule on Yom Tov stringently like R' Yehuda who forbids all muktzeh even machmat issur. If so, what Magen Avraham wrote requires study — for what halacha did the Tur write etc.? The wonder is: on the contrary, the Tur wrote like the Maor that it is not muktzeh machmat mitzva, and therefore wrote that for R' Shimon it is permitted. Therefore also what Magen Avraham wrote in the following subsection in the view of the Maor that here there are two [prohibitions] — it is not so, as explained there that he brings from tevel, and there the fruits are muktzeh by themselves, only that he intended to distinguish between where it is possible it will diminish on its own — see there; and I elaborated in my chiddushim to clarify the matter, and here I shortened.
