In Magen Avraham seif katan 13: "and perhaps he holds that a lamp is more prone to be extinguished," etc.; and one should not say that he holds that since its dismantling involves no biblical prohibition, etc.; so it must read.
What the Magen Avraham wrote, "but the Ran and Tosafot," etc., means that regarding a sukkah it helps, as the Magen Avraham wrote there in seif katan 7 from Tosafot in Shabbat 44, that there is an opinion so; see there. In another place someone emended the word Tosafot to Ramban, but this is incorrect.
What the Magen Avraham wrote, "but the Baal HaMaor wrote," etc., is very astonishing, for he skipped in his words. There it says: we hold on Yom Tov like R. Yehudah; and with muktzeh because of prohibition, such as leftover oil in a lamp, even on Shabbat we hold like R. Yehudah, etc.; see there. It is clear that he holds a weak sukkah is not muktzeh because of prohibition. Rather, he follows his own view that on Yom Tov the halakhah follows R. Yehudah even with ordinary muktzeh. Afterward he wrote that with muktzeh because of prohibition, such as leftover oil, even on Shabbat we hold like R. Yehudah; see there. According to the Magen Avraham, the Baal HaMaor's words would contradict each other immediately: at first he wrote that a weak sukkah is not muktzeh because of prohibition, and how could he write at the end that it is forbidden even on Shabbat because of muktzeh because of prohibition? In truth this is not so; certainly he holds this is not muktzeh because of prohibition, only that on Yom Tov we rule stringently like R. Yehudah, who forbids all muktzeh, even not because of prohibition. Therefore what the Magen Avraham wrote, that it requires study for what law the Tur wrote, etc., is no difficulty. On the contrary, the Tur wrote like the Baal HaMaor that it is not muktzeh because of prohibition, and therefore wrote that according to R. Shimon it is permitted. Likewise, what the Magen Avraham wrote in the next seif katan in the Baal HaMaor's view, that here there are two factors, etc., is incorrect, as is clear there, for he brings proof from tevel, and there the fruit is muktzeh in itself; he only intends to distinguish where it might collapse on its own; see there. I discussed the matter at length in my novellae, and here I have been brief.
