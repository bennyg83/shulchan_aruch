(1) [Seif 1] Since carrying was permitted for [Yom Tov] needs, etc. — and likewise kindling: since it was permitted for the need of food for a person, it was also permitted even when not for need, as written above at the beginning of siman 511 — see there. And likewise the other melachos that involve eating, such as slaughtering, baking, and cooking. However, if one performed them with no need whatsoever, or for a weekday purpose — there are those who say that even so one does not receive lashes; and there are those who say that these were not permitted without need except when there is some partial need for that day, such as [the need of] a minor or a lulav, or the need of a mitzva. But when there is no need of a mitzva and no ordinary personal need for that day at all — such as carrying stones — one receives lashes for it, as the Maggid Mishneh wrote in chapter 1, law 1 — see there. And see further below, entry 12, and at the beginning of siman 527.
