Beis Yosef wrote in siman 495 in the name of the Maggid Mishneh: since carrying out is done with the ochel nefesh itself, in that one carries the food from domain to domain, we say regarding it "mitoch," etc.; but other melachos, even though they were permitted for the need of ochel nefesh, were not permitted with the ochel nefesh itself but only with preparatory acts; therefore we do not say "mitoch." And kindling, even though it too is done with preparatory materials, for kindling is not in the food itself but in the wood: nevertheless, since the Torah wrote explicitly, "You shall not kindle a fire on the day of Shabbos," it implies that on Yom Tov it is permitted; therefore we say "mitoch." And see what I wrote in siman 501 in the name of the Ran, that it implies that kindling is like ochel nefesh itself. And it is astonishing: for in Pesachim folio 5 we say that it is forbidden to burn chametz on Yom Tov, for the Merciful One said, "You shall not do any melacha," and we find that kindling is an av melacha, etc.; see there. If so, we learn that even though the negative commandment "You shall not kindle" was not said regarding Yom Tov, nevertheless it is forbidden from the negative commandment "You shall not do any melacha"; and if so the difficulty returns to its place: why do we say "mitoch" regarding it? And one cannot say that this is specifically according to the view that kindling went out to divide [liability], but according to the view that it went out as a negative commandment it is not forbidden on Yom Tov, as Tosafos wrote, and we do not say "mitoch"; if so, what does it say there, "we learn three things: we learn that it went out to divide, and we learn that we do not say mitoch," for they are one matter, for one depends on the other. And furthermore it is forced to say that Beis Shammai and Beis Hillel, who disagree about mitoch, disagree in the dispute of R' Yehuda and R' Nosson whether it went out as a negative commandment or to divide; and this requires further study. And it is stated in the Gemara that regarding cooking as well we say mitoch; and nevertheless one who cooks from Yom Tov for a weekday is liable to lashes, since there is no need at all; see what I wrote at the beginning of siman 527; and see in Sefer Kenesses HaGedolah.
