(In Magen Avraham subsection 1) And what Olat Shabbat wrote here — meaning Olat Shabbat wrote that what is said in Kesubos "perhaps he gave her authority on Shabbat and Yom Tov" speaks of Tosafos on Shabbos that melacha is forbidden — and these are the words of Tosafos in Kesubos there. And Olat Shabbat wrote that the second answer of Tosafos speaks of marriage without a feast, where there is no element of joy — from which it is proven that it is permitted to make a chuppah on Shabbat; on this Magen Avraham wrote that this means plainly that there it speaks of a Torah matter, for there it refers to a verse from the Torah that in truth it is permitted; and this is proven from the fact that Tosafos needed to bring the derasha of "and you shall rejoice in your festival" etc., and they wrote it is a complete derasha, for otherwise it would be obvious where he gave her authority as we teach here that they do not enter except — and certainly the Tosafos' question speaks to Torah law, and on this they answered well that it speaks without a feast; and if because they do not enter — this is only rabbinic, and from the Torah it is permitted.
