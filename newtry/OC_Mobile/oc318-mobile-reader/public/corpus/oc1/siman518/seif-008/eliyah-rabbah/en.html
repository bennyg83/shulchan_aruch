[8] [Levush] And the like, etc. Meaning vessels that are not needed by him: they are comparable to stones regarding carrying out; and nevertheless the prohibition is only rabbinic, even regarding stones. And so it is implied in the Bach; so Beis Yosef concludes, for the reason that from "mitoch" it is permitted even regarding stones; and so Riaz agreed in Shiltei Gibborim; and so it is implied in the Shulchan Aruch and Levush; and not like Magen Avraham who wrote that one is liable to lashes for them.
