Se'if 6: An animal, etc. Per the interpretation of the Rif there regarding a critically ill animal, s.v. "and even R' Yehuda agrees." And regarding a sick animal, R' Yehuda and R' Shimon disagree, as the Ra'az wrote. And we hold like R' Yehuda on Yom Tov, as written in siman 495. And see in the Tur. And these are the words of the Rashba in the book Avodat HaKodesh, 46b; see there.
