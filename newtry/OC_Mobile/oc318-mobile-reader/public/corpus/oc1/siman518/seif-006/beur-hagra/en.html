Seif 6: "An animal, etc." — per the interpretation of the Rif there, regarding a critically ill animal, s.v. "and even R' Yehuda agrees." Regarding a sick animal, R' Yehuda and R' Shimon disagree, as the Razah wrote. And we rule like R' Yehuda on Yom Tov, as was written in siman 495. See in the Tur. And these are the words of the Rashba in the book Avodat HaKodesh, 46b. See there.
