(s.k. 6) It is permitted, etc. Meaning when he needs, etc. And see what I wrote at the end of siman 509. That is to say: to make use of muktzeh is forbidden, for it is like eating muktzeh which is forbidden. And it was only permitted to move muktzeh when he needs its place; but certainly when he still needs it to roast on it again, the Taz wrote that it is obvious that it is permitted.
