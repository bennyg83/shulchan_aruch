If it was critically ill - And the Shulchan Aruch ruled like the Rif and the Rambam and the Rosh according to their version in the Gemara, as explained in Beis Yosef. And the Bach wrote that it is proper to be stringent even regarding a critically ill animal, like the version of Rashi and the Ra'az, that even regarding a critically ill animal R' Yehuda and R' Shimon disagree, and according to R' Yehuda it is forbidden even in this. And even though I also found the Ra'ah in his novellae and the Meiri who also hold like Rashi's version, nevertheless it appears to my humble opinion that one should not be stringent about this, since in any case several poskim hold that muktzeh is permitted on Yom Tov like R' Shimon, as above in siman 495, se'if 4 in the gloss; and all the more so in this, for according to the Rif and the Rosh and the Rambam everyone concedes in this that it is permitted; and so the Acharonim were silent.
Even though it was sick, behold it is muktzeh - It is implied from this language that according to the one who does not hold muktzeh, it is permitted regarding a sick animal. And the Graz wrote that this muktzeh is more stringent, for it is muktzeh of nolad: originally when it was alive it was not standing for dogs, and now it stands for dogs. And therefore, according to what the Rama wrote in siman 495 that regarding nolad we practice to forbid, this too is forbidden, and one should not be lenient except regarding a critically ill animal. And see above at the end of siman 497 in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 51 and in Sha'ar HaTziyun there, and in siman 499 in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 15 and in Sha'ar HaTziyun there.
