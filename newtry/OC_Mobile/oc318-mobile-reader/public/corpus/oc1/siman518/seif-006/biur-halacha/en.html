(א) If it was dangerous — and Shulchan Aruch ruled like Rif, Rambam, and Rosh per their version in the Gemara as explained in Beit Yosef; and Bach wrote it is proper to be stringent even when dangerous, per the version of Rashi and Ra'az that even when dangerous R' Yehuda and R' Shimon disagree and R' Yehuda forbids even in this. And even though I also found Ra'ah in his chiddushim and Meiri hold like Rashi's version, nevertheless it appears to me we should not be stringent here, since in any case many poskim hold muktzeh is permitted on Yom Tov like R' Shimon, as above siman 495 seif 4 in the gloss; and all the more here, for per Rif, Rosh, and Rambam all agree it is permitted, and so the Acharonim ruled stam:

(ב) "Even though it was sick, it is muktzeh" — It is implied from this language that per the one who does not hold the category of muktzeh, it would be permitted in the case of a sick [animal]. And the Graz wrote that this muktzeh is more stringent, since it falls under the category of muktzeh of nolad, for originally, when it was alive, it was not designated for dogs, and now it is designated for dogs. And therefore, according to what the Rama wrote in siman 495 that regarding nolad we practice to prohibit, this also is forbidden, and one should not be lenient except in a life-threatening situation. And see above at the end of siman 497 in the Mishna Berurah, subsection 51, and in Sha'ar HaTziyun there, and in siman 499 in the Mishna Berurah subsection 15, and in Sha'ar HaTziyun there.
