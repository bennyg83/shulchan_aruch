{31} Critically ill, etc. For from the eve of Yom Tov his mind is on it, since it is close to death.
{32} Behold, he cuts it up. And specifically when it is hard for them to eat without cutting; but if they can eat it as is, we do not trouble him for nothing, as above in siman 324, se'if 7 [Acharonim].
{33} And he may not move it from its place. And according to those who say in siman 495 that muktzeh is permitted on Yom Tov, even a healthy animal that died is also permitted, as Magen Avraham wrote; and that is according to the Mechaber's words above in siman 324, se'if 7. And we already wrote there in Mishnah Berurah the view of those who disagree: that regarding a healthy animal, where he had no reason to put it in mind, according to everyone it is forbidden, for it is like dried figs and raisins, which are muktzeh according to everyone; and specifically when it was sick is it permitted according to the one who does not hold muktzeh. And see Biur Halacha.
