Rotten straw. For it is no longer fit for animal food, and it has thorns in it so that it is not fit to mix mortar with them; and then even if it lies in a storehouse it is permitted even according to R' Yehuda, for he designated it only away from animal food, but he did not designate it away from kindling. But wood lying in a room in a storehouse for the winter months: one may not begin with them on Yom Tov according to R' Yehuda, for he designated them away from kindling. And straw that stands for animal food or for another use, and does not lie in a storehouse, is permitted to kindle with, for one kindles with vessels and foods; see siman 501. All this is in the Gemara and Tosafos. And it appears to me that rotten straw that is moist, even though it is fit for a large fire, is forbidden to move, for a large fire is not common, as stated in Bava Basra folio 19b; and it is not similar to moist wood that is designated for kindling, which is not so with straw.
