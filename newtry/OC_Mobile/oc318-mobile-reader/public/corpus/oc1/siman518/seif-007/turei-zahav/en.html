One may begin with piles, etc. In the Tur it is written that it is prepared and standing for kindling, etc. Beis Yosef challenged: for afterward he wrote that we come according to R' Shimon per one version, and R' Shimon permits muktzeh and it is not required that it stand for kindling; and he strained to resolve this. And there is no difficulty: for this "prepared and standing for kindling" he did not mention for the reason of muktzeh, for in this "prepared" alone does not suffice, but specifically that it is rotten straw, as he wrote afterward; rather he needed this because whoever wishes to take straw for kindling must first make it into small bundles and tie them and afterward kindle with them, and this is forbidden to do even according to R' Shimon, for it is making a vessel; therefore he said it is prepared and standing for kindling, meaning there are already small bundles that need not be made on Yom Tov; nevertheless they do not stand specifically for kindling but also for animal food, and that is the essence; therefore it is only permitted according to R' Yehuda regarding rotten straw which is specifically for kindling, and in this specifically it is permitted according to R' Yehuda. And even though here he ruled like R' Yehuda and deals with rotten straw, nevertheless one must say it is already tied so that he need not tie it for kindling, for this is required according to everyone, as appears to my humble opinion. Beis Yosef further challenged: what does the Tur teach us, for it is obvious that according to R' Shimon muktzeh is permitted and according to R' Yehuda forbidden? And there is no difficulty: for there is a great novelty here, that one would have said even rotten straw is forbidden according to R' Yehuda, as Tosafos challenged, and this is their language: and if you say, what is different from wood in a storehouse that R' Yehuda forbade even though it appears fit for kindling? One can say: specifically regarding a straw storehouse he did not designate his mind away from what is fit now, for he only designated his mind away from animal food, and now it is fit for kindling and from this he did not designate; but a wood storehouse he designated his mind away from what is fit now; end quote. And it requires study of Tosafos's words who wrote he designated his mind away from what is fit now; and if so you have no greater muktzeh than this, for he designated from his mind that now it should be rotten straw and specifically for kindling. And one cannot say he is accustomed that there be rotten straw among him; nevertheless spikes are needed there and this is not common. And one can say: in any case the spikes did not fall there on Yom Tov but already came there at the time the straw was gathered; only he did not know of this until today on Yom Tov: it is permitted, for there is no muktzeh here but lack of knowledge; and in truth it was prepared for this from Bein HaShemashos, as appears to my humble opinion.
