One may begin with piles, etc. Tur wrote it is prepared and standing for kindling, etc. Beit Yosef challenged: for he afterward wrote we follow R' Shimon per one version, and R' Shimon permits muktzeh and it is not required that it stand for kindling; and he strained to answer this; and the resolution is this "prepared and standing for kindling" is not because of muktzeh — remember in this "prepared" alone does not suffice, but specifically that it is rotten straw as written afterward; rather this was needed because whoever wants to take straw for kindling must first make it into small bundles and tie them, then kindle with them — and this is forbidden to do even for R' Shimon because it is making a vessel; therefore he said it is prepared and standing for kindling — meaning there are already small bundles that need not be made on Yom Tov; nevertheless they are not standing specifically for kindling but also for animal food, and that is the essence; therefore R' Yehuda permits only rotten straw which is specifically for kindling, and in this specifically R' Yehuda permits; and even though here he ruled like R' Yehuda and deals with rotten straw, nevertheless it must be already tied so he need not tie it for kindling, which all require — so it appears to me. Beit Yosef further challenged: what does Tur teach us — it is obvious that for R' Shimon muktzeh is permitted and for R' Yehuda forbidden; and the resolution is there is a great novelty here, that one could say even rotten straw is forbidden to R' Yehuda as Tosafos challenged; and he wrote: you asked what is different from wood in muktzeh that R' Yehuda forbade even though it appears fit for kindling — and one can answer specifically regarding a straw storehouse he did not designate his mind for what is fit now, for he designated only for animal food, and now it is fit for kindling and from this he did not designate; but a wood storehouse he designated for what is fit now — end quote; and it requires study per Tosafos who wrote he designated his mind for what is fit now; and if so you have no greater muktzeh than this, for he designated from his mind that now it should be rotten straw and be specifically for kindling; and we are accustomed that he regularly has ten rotten bundles; nevertheless spikes are needed there and this is not regular; and one can say in any case the spikes did not fall there on Yom Tov but already came at harvest time, only he did not know until today — on Yom Tov it is permitted, for there is no muktzeh here but lack of knowledge; and in truth it is prepared for this — Beit Hillel — so it appears to me:
