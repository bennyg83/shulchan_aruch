{34} One may begin, etc. The Mechaber abbreviated this, and the explanation of the matter is as follows: according to the one who holds a prohibition of muktzeh on Yom Tov, one may not begin to make use of a storehouse on Yom Tov if he did not designate from the eve of Yom Tov; for example, if he had a pile of straw that he stored there for food for his animals to draw from after some time, or that he gathered wood into a storehouse to draw from in the winter months, it is forbidden for him to draw from them on Yom Tov, whether for kindling or to feed from the straw to his animals. And the Mechaber says that sometimes there is room to permit for kindling even things he put into a storehouse: for example, straw that he put into a storehouse and made into a pile, and they spoiled so that they are not presently fit for animal food: it is permitted for him to kindle with them; even though at first he designated them away by his mind, nevertheless from the hour that they spoiled they stand for kindling every day, even without designation from while it was still day.
{35} If it is, etc. And straw that he did not put into a storehouse to make use of after some time: it is permitted to kindle with it even if it is fine, even if it stands to feed to his animal or to make some other use of it, for the name of muktzeh is not upon them.
{36} Rotten straw. That is when it is very foul-smelling, so that even by crumbling it is not fit for an animal. And the Taz wrote that even so it is forbidden to tie them to make them into bundles of bundles as is the way they do for kindling, unless they were already tied from the eve of Yom Tov. And all this is when the straw is dry; but if the straw is moist, he is not allowed to kindle with it, even though it is fit for a large fire [Magen Avraham]. And in Chemdat Moshe there is hesitation about this; see there.
{37} And it has thorns in it. For otherwise the straw would appear fit to mix mortar with them, and the name of muktzeh would not be nullified from them. And know that this entire se'if is only according to the Mechaber's view above in siman 495 that muktzeh is forbidden on Yom Tov; but according to the view of those who permit, cited there in the gloss, it is permitted to draw on Yom Tov both from straw and from wood even if he put them into a storehouse; and the Rama, who did not emend here, relied on the above.
