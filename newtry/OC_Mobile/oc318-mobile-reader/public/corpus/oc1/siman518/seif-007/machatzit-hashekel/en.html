For in Beitzah 9 they said: that which Beit Hillel permitted there to move a ladder — specifically a dovecote ladder, for he does not need to plaster his roof with it; but an attic ladder that he needs to plaster his roof with it is forbidden in reshut harabbim because of marit ayin; therefore even in the house it is forbidden to move it, for whatever they forbade because of marit ayin is forbidden even in inner rooms; and Tosafos challenged: behold in Eruvin we say even a ladder that is not of a dovecote may be moved on Shabbat; and Rabbeinu Tam answered in Eruvin it deals with a house ladder which is smaller than an attic ladder and it is not likely they will say he plasters his roof with it; and R' Eliezer of Metz answered in Eruvin it deals with Shabbat where he need not take it out to reshut harabbim, therefore in his house it is permitted even an attic ladder, for in the house he does not need to plaster his roof with it; but on Yom Tov since it is permitted to take it out to reshut harabbim and there he needs to plaster his roof with it, therefore even inside the house it is forbidden, for whatever is forbidden in reshut harabbim because of marit ayin is also forbidden in inner rooms; and if so per R' Eliezer of Metz on Yom Tov even a house ladder is forbidden, for Rabbeinu Tam did not distinguish between house and attic except from the necessity of their question from tractate Eruvin; and since per R' Eliezer of Metz in any case we must resolve tractate Eruvin from Torah we distinguish between house and attic; and since Tur in siman 308 ruled like R' Eliezer of Metz and permits on Shabbat even an attic ladder, automatically on Yom Tov even a house ladder is forbidden; and one who is lenient in the house did not lose.
