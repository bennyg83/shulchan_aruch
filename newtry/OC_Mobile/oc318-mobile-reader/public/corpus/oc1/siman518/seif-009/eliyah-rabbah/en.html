[9] A pledge, etc. See there that Kenesses HaGedolah: the Shulchan Aruch permitted only with a garment, but with a pledge that one cannot wear, it is forbidden; and so Maharshal ruled in siman 36, and he explained that we hold that whatever is forbidden for a Jew is forbidden to tell to non-Jews; and so is the essence (Olas Shabbos). And he was not precise: for the Shulchan Aruch in siman 325 deals with non-Jews who are not violent, and since it is by way of wearing it is permitted, even for a Jew it is permitted in that manner; but regarding a violent [non-Jew] it is permitted even not by way of a garment. Also what he wrote that so Maharshal ruled in siman 76: I looked there and did not find it. Also what he wrote "and the explanation of the reason," etc., he was not precise; and that refers to sending to non-Jews through other non-Jews something of his own; but regarding a pledge he concedes that it is permitted.
