Se'if 4: Even in a public domain. See Bahag, that in a matter of doubt we follow the lenient view. The Rosh. And the Ran wrote that we do not hold like the sugya there, but rather like R' Elazar and R' Shimon, who do not distinguish between a private domain and a public domain.
But a ladder, etc. See Tosafos, who strained over this; but the Rashba explained in Avodat HaKodesh that it is forbidden to carry an attic ladder even in a private domain; and not only that, even to tilt it in a dovecote from one window to another is forbidden. And so appears the plain sense of the Gemara, and with this the difficulty of Tosafos there is resolved.
