[4] [Levush] And he is unable, etc. Not specifically; rather even if it is possible to leave it with them, even so it is permitted to take them out by way of carrying about. Yam Shel Shlomo, first chapter of Beitzah, siman 34. And it appears to me that it must be "by way of strolling"; and Kenesses HaGedolah and Olas Shabbos were not precise in this. And so it is implied at the end of Tosafos Beitzah, folio 12, s.v. "lest," etc., and Mordechai and Ran and Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 1 and Hagahos Sefer Mitzvos Katan and Maharil. And it appears to me that the Rosh also concedes this: lest [leaving the child] be worse than playing, as Tosafos wrote; from his writing "and he is unable to leave," etc., meaning that even if he or the child has no stroll, it is permitted because he is unable, etc.; and not like Olas Shabbos who wrote that this requires further study for the law, that the Rosh forbids, until here; he was not precise.
