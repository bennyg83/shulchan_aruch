{16} That is not designated, etc. And he did not prepare it from the evening for this; and nevertheless it is permitted to make use of it for this need, as above in siman 502, se'if 3 in the gloss. And it deals with dry wood fit for kindling, as explained there in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 22.
{17} It is forbidden, etc. For through the roasting the spit becomes repulsive and muktzeh; and even according to those who permit muktzeh on Yom Tov, the view of several Acharonim is that here it is forbidden because it has no status of a vessel.
{18} To move it. If he no longer needs to roast on it or to kindle his oven with it, for wood was given only for kindling and the like.
{19} That is not a vessel. Meaning that through this, that he performs with it an act of a spit, it does not become a vessel.
{20} Rather he removes it. Meaning he is not allowed to take it after his roasting and set it down in its ordinary way; rather he removes it quickly by dragging and moving from the side until they clear it from before him to a corner.
{21} So that they not be injured by it. And like that above in siman 308, se'if 18, regarding a thorn in a public domain. However, there they did not require moving from the side, because it is damage of the public.
{22} Even if there is no meat on it at all. And all this is when it lies in a place where household members pass there, as the Mechaber wrote, so that they not be injured; but if it lies off to the side, it is forbidden to move it.
{23} And for the need of ochel nefesh, etc. For example, stones or earth lying before fruit: it is permitted to move them and remove them in order to reach the fruit, as explained above at the end of siman 509. And the same in our matter: it is permitted to move the wood and remove it from its place where it lies if he needs to set ochel nefesh there and the like for the sake of Yom Tov joy. And all this is for the sake of ochel nefesh and Yom Tov joy; but for the need of its body and its place alone it is forbidden, as above in siman 502, se'if 3. And according to what we wrote there in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 21, the view of several Acharonim is that this is only according to the one who forbids muktzeh; but according to those who say in siman 495 that muktzeh is permitted on Yom Tov, wood that stands for kindling is permitted also for other use. However, in our matter the view of several Acharonim is that wood on which they roasted is muktzeh according to everyone, as above.
{24} Even muktzeh. Chayei Adam wrote: if a key of foods was lying in a chest designated for money, and money is lying in it, it is permitted to take out the chest in order to take the key from it, and it is also permitted to return it if he fears theft (for they permitted their end for the sake of their beginning).
