Rather he removes it, etc. In the Gemara they hold thus according to Rav Malkiyo; R' Chiya bar Ashi said: and that is when there is a ke-zayis of meat on it; Ravina said: even though there is not a ke-zayis of meat on it, it is permitted to move it, just as [one may move] a thorn in a public domain. Rashi explained: full moving, as we say in chapter "Kirah" regarding a thorn in a public domain: he carries it less than less than four amos; and we hold like Ravina. However it is difficult: why did the Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule that we require "he removes it," that is moving from the side? For regarding a thorn we hold even full moving. And even according to the Rosh's version that does not have in Ravina's words "it is permitted to move it," but rather simply "it is permitted": if so one can say it refers only to R' Chiya to disagree with him that a ke-zayis of meat is not required; but regarding Rav Malkiyo he does not disagree and we require "he removes it." Nevertheless it is difficult: for Ravina compared it to a thorn in a public domain, and we hold in siman 308 that even full moving is permitted. One must say that here, since he moves it with full moving at the time he wishes to roast on it, and he may come to err to move it also afterward even without need: therefore they made a distinguishing mark that only by way of removal he should set it down in a corner, as appears to me. And it is obvious that if he wishes to roast meat on it a second time it is permitted.
