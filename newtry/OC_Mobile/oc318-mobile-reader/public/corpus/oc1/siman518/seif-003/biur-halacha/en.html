(א) "He took a piece of wood that is not designated [for roasting], etc." — But if it is designated for roasting, whether it is made of wood or of metal, it has the status of a utensil, and it is permitted to move it in any manner he wishes [Beit Yosef, and so also wrote the Maharshal — see there]. And from the Peri Megadim it is implied that it is only permitted for the sake of its body or its place, like all utensils [that is, whose function is for a forbidden purpose]. And see in the Chiddushei HaRashba and in the Chiddushei Ra'ah, who explain the sugya with regard to a metal spit — see there what they elaborated upon in this matter. And we have explained per the interpretation of the Tur, and as the Mechaber quoted it, and this requires further study.
