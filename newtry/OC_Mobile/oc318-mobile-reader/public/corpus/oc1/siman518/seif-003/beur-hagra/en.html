Se'if 3: That is not, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. "Ravina." And one can say that this case is different, etc. And see in chapter 20 of Shabbos, folio 138b: "this linen garment, etc., and if it is a vessel, etc." And see Tosafos there, s.v. "it hangs," etc., and the Rosh there.
Rather he removes it, etc. The Tur does not have in the Gemara "it is permitted to move it," and like the version of the Rif and the Rosh: "it is permitted; just as [one may move], etc." And the explanation is that it refers to "he removes it" above.
And for the need, etc. See Magen Avraham at the end of siman 509, who wrote: specifically to remove it because of ochel nefesh, but to eat it or to make use of it is forbidden. And this is what Tosafos wrote here, folio 28b, s.v. "Ravina," etc.: and one can say, etc., that after its melacha is finished, etc.; specifically after its melacha, etc.
