3) There: Such as a child, etc. Rabbeinu Chananel explains: a child to be circumcised, a lulav with which to fulfill the mitzvah, and a Torah scroll from which to read. Tosafos there writes that the child need not specifically be carried for circumcision; carrying him for an outing is likewise permitted. The Rosh writes that if one needs to go to the synagogue or take a Yom Tov pleasure walk and cannot leave the child alone at home, he may carry the child with him. Tur and Levush write likewise. Ran, however, explains it as a child carried either for circumcision or for an outing, because that entails Yom Tov enjoyment. Yam Shel Shlomo there similarly writes that the "child" mentioned may be carried even merely for an outing.
