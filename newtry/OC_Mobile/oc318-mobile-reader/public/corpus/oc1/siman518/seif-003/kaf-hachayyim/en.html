3) There, "such as a child," etc. Rabbeinu Chananel explains: a child to bring for brit milah; a lulav with which to fulfill the mitzvah; and a Torah scroll from which to read. Tosafos there writes that it is not specifically a child being brought for brit milah; even carrying him merely for an outing is permitted. Rosh writes that if one needs to go to the synagogue or take a walk for the rejoicing of Yom Tov and cannot leave the child alone at home, he may carry the child with him. Tur and Levush write likewise. Ran explains: either to bring him for brit milah or to take him outside for an outing, because this involves Yom Tov enjoyment. Yam Shel Shlomo likewise writes that the child mentioned may be carried even merely for an outing.
