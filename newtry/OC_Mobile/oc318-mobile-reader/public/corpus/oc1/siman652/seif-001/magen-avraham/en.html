He should take it afterward. See the Beis Yosef, siman 665, in the name of the Ran; it implies that if he did not take it until twilight on the first day, he takes it at twilight, for a Torah doubt is treated stringently. This is not so on the other days, when he is exempt; see there. Nevertheless, it appears to me that he should take it at twilight without a berachah.
From sunrise. If he took it from dawn, he fulfilled the obligation.
Also at the time of prayer. Since taking it is a mitzvah, he is not distracted by it. The Shelah wrote that one should shake it in the sukkah before going to the synagogue; see there.
