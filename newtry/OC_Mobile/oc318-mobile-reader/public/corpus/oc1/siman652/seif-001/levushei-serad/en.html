652 Magen Avraham subparagraph 1 Without a blessing. It seems at first glance that on the first day he does recite a blessing. See siman 67. However, one who examines the Ran will see that it is not at all proven that he should recite a blessing on the first day. It appears that he does not recite a blessing even on the first day, only that one is obligated to take it because of a doubt concerning a Torah obligation. But regarding the other days it seems from the Ran that he is exempt, and about this the Magen Avraham disagrees. His intent is that since he takes it without a blessing and there is no exertion here, therefore even on the other days he should take it.
