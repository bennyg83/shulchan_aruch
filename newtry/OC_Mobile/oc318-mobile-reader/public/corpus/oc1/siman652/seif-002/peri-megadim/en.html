And one who is meticulous. See the Turei Zahav. In the Tur it says, "one who is meticulous in his deeds," meaning that he is always meticulous in his other deeds; but if not, it is like arrogance. However, from his house to the synagogue he should do so; only during prayer should he not hold it if he is not meticulous in his deeds. See Eliyah Rabbah subparagraph 2. And what he wrote, that he holds the Torah scroll during the blessing, see the Beis Yosef: this is the explanation of the Mordechai. But Rashi, of blessed memory, explained that he rolls the Torah scroll and opens it; see there. And see in the laws of a Torah scroll whether one is permitted to hold the columns with his hands, or only with the interruption of a tallis; see there.
