It is prohibited to eat. It implies that mere tasting is permitted, as written in siman 231, the end of siman 232, and siman 431. However, from the words of Tosafos that I wrote in siman 649:5, it is proven that tasting is prohibited; for if not, it should have answered that Rav Chisda only tasted. One can reject this, for the language 'dipped' implies in the manner of dipping in a meal. This requires investigation. One who is traveling and expects to reach the place of the lulav seemingly must let one mealtime pass until he takes it, as written in siman 182:1 and 296:3. However, in Hagahos Maimoniyos it is written that if he has no cup for Kiddush, he eats without Kiddush, because it is prohibited to fast on Shabbos, as written at the end of siman 289. If so, the same applies on Chol HaMoed: it is prohibited to fast. Therefore it appears to me that even one who is stringent should not fast more than half the day; see the beginning of siman 288.
