6) [Seif 2] No detached food, etc. Something attached to the ground is muktzeh even according to Rabbi Shimon, whether it belongs to a Jew or a non-Jew. Beis Yosef. See below, paragraph 14.
7) There: That are intended for sale, etc. Although they are intended for sale, he has them in mind to eat whenever he wishes. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2; Levush Serad.
8) There: Even wheat that he sowed, etc. This does not resemble a lamp that was extinguished in siman 279, where, since it was set aside during twilight, it remains set aside for the entire day. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. See Bach, who writes in the name of Shiltei HaGibborim that wheat one sowed is forbidden like a lamp that was extinguished, although Bach himself agrees with the Shulchan Aruch; see there. The matter nevertheless requires examination, because in siman 311, seif 8, and siman 336, seif 6, Shulchan Aruch forbids it when he intended it for planting. See our words there, with the help of Heaven.
9) There: And they have not yet taken root. If they have taken root, removing them uproots something from its place of growth and is forbidden. Olas Shabbos, siman 311, paragraph 6.
