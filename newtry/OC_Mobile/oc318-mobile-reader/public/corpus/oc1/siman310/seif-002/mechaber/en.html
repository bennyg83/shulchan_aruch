No detached food fit for eating is muktzeh for Shabbat; and dates and almonds and other fruits standing for commerce are permitted to eat from them on Shabbat; and even wheat that one' sowed in the ground and has not yet sprouted; and eggs under a hen are permitted to move; and likewise dates harvested before they are cooked and placed in baskets and cook by themselves — permitted to eat from them before they are cooked; but dried figs and raisins that one places in a muktzeh place to dry are forbidden on Shabbat because of muktzeh, for they spoil before they dry, since he knows they will spoil he removed his mind from them; and since there are two rejections — they reject them with their hands and they are not fit — it becomes muktzeh.And some say designation does not apply to that of a gentile, and even dried figs and raisins in his hand are permitted [Kol Bo siman 55, and so implied in Ran end of chapter 22, and Yerushalmi].]:
