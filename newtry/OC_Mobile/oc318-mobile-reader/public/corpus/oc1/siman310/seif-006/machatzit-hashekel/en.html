(s.k. 6) For the pocket, etc. — and it is difficult, etc., as stated above — meaning that Rama himself wrote that even for the first view it is permitted for the need of his body and its place (and in truth Magen Avraham concluded that even according to what Magen Avraham wrote in the s.k. before this to forbid for the need of his body and its place — nevertheless in this Magen Avraham agrees); and one can say, etc. — even on Rosh Hashana permitted — meaning that which he needed the reason that the pocket is batel, etc., was not in order to permit the prohibition of moving but in order to permit the prohibition of carrying out, even though the pocket is not a garment — nevertheless permitted to wear the garment that has the pocket on it and go out on Rosh Hashana, for the pocket is batel, etc.:

And even according to what is written above in the s.k. before this — that for the Rosh, namely the first view, even for the need of his body and its place it is forbidden — nevertheless in this Magen Avraham certainly agrees it is permitted to wear it, from the reason Rama wrote that the pocket is batel relative to the garment; nevertheless one might think that in any case it is forbidden to place or take an object from inside the pocket, for for the need of its place it is forbidden — on this Magen Avraham wrote this is not so, for from the same reason Magen Avraham wrote it is forbidden even for the need of his body and its place — since he was particular about it, it is muktzeh machmat cheisron kis as stated — but with a pocket we see he was not particular — meaning on a weekday how many times he places other things in the pocket, not only coins, and it is not only like other utensils whose melacha is for prohibition that are permitted for the need of his body and its place:
