There — wheat that he sowed in the ground, this; and similarly eggs under the hen — it is a dilemma in the Gemara whether R' Shimon perhaps concedes because of rejection by hands; and even though a bed and a candle lit on Shabbat and extinguished — even though rejected by hands, that is because when it went out the rejection departed; but here where the rejection is because he sowed them — the rejection has not yet departed — and obvious that it is permitted for R' Shimon and forbidden only where there are two factors as stated in Shulchan Aruch end of the seif. And on this Magen Avraham challenged: behold we forbid extinguished candle not like R' Shimon even though fit — all the more here. And he answered: there because of since it was muktzeh at bein hashemashos, meaning at bein hashemashos it was not fit for anything due to the prohibition; if so at bein hashemashos there are two factors — therefore forbidden all day; unlike here where they are fit also at bein hashemashos:
