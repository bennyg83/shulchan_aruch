(In Magen Avraham, seif katan 5.) What he wrote in Hagahot Ashiri there represents another view. Everyone agrees here that if he designated it, it has the status of a utensil, as stated above. Note: Meaning, according to the Rif, in any event it is permitted only for use of the utensil itself or use of its place. According to Rabbeinu Chananel, however, who compares it to implements used for weaving and pressing, even moving it for use of its place is forbidden.
