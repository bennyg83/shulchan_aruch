(In Magen Avraham s.k. 5.) And what he wrote in Hagahot Maimonides there — this is a different view that all agree to this if he designated it, for it is a utensil, as above. And it appears to me: meaning according to the Rif, in any case it is not permitted except for its body and its place; but according to R' Chananel, who compares it to the wicks of a lamp and a spindle, even for its place it is forbidden.
