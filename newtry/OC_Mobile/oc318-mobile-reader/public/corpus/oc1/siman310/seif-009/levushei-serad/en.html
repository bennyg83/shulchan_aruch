Taz seif 4 — and on Shabbat they became ready, meaning here one can say since it was muktzeh at bein hashemashos — for at bein hashemashos it was not fit; if so it is like dried figs and raisins rejected by hands and also not fit at bein hashemashos; and as I wrote letters vav and alef — nevertheless permitted; and the reason he explained there because it is completed by man — see there. And R' Levi's meaning: we say in Beitzah daf 27 — ordinary pots are boiling at bein hashemashos and not fit to eat; how do we eat from them in the evening? Rather we say since it was muktzeh at bein hashemashos only because it is completed by man; and Rashi explained: since it is in his power to repair it that day he does not muktzeh it from his intent due to its rejection — end of his words. And therefore not specifically meaning in his power to repair it, for at bein hashemashos it is boiling and not in his power to repair it then; nevertheless R' Levi's meaning is: since it is certain it will repair itself it is called completed by man [and similarly from what Rashi explained there regarding completed by heaven, meaning by sun, etc.; and thereby there is doubt whether there will be sun]. Therefore those dates known that their repair will be on Shabbat when the taste of the dates is absorbed in the water — it is completed by man; so appears the intent.
