A bed on which there are coins, or even if there are not on it now but there were at bein hashemashos — forbidden to move it, for since it was muktzeh at bein hashemashos, it was muktzeh all day.And even for its body or its place [Beit Yosef]; and the same law applies to every permitted thing upon which a forbidden thing rests; but if there are not on it now coins and there also were not on it at bein hashemashos — permitted to move it, even if he designated it for coins and placed them on it during the day, since he removed them before bein hashemashos.} {Rama: And some forbid when he designated it for this and placed coins in it even though he removed them before day [Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Tam]; and likewise forbidden to move a purse of coins even though he removed the coins from it during the day, unless he did an act with it — that he opened it from below and removed it from designation; and so is the practice [Hagahot Maimonides chapter 25]; however for its body or its place it is permitted; and likewise a purse sewn into a garment — since the main garment stands for wearing, if he removed the coins from there it is permitted to wear the garment, for the purse is batel against it; but if he forgot coins in it, it is permitted to move the garment, for we do not say the whole garment became a base for the coins, since the coins are not on the main part of the garment; but he may not wear it on Shabbat, for we are concerned lest he go out with it, as above siman 301 seif 33 [Beit Yosef siman 305];
