To wear. Even on Rosh Hashana. Magen Avraham:

Lest. However he can shake the coins from there. See Magen Avraham who raised that if he placed coins intentionally in the pocket — forbidden to put his hand in, for in any case the pocket becomes a base. He further raised that if a pocket full of coins is sewn in a garment — forbidden to move the garment, since the pocket is a utensil in its own right; and the same when there are coins in a box inside a table — forbidden to move the table unless there is also a permitted thing on it — end of his words; and in Sefer Even HaOzair he raised as halacha that even if one can shake the coins from the pocket — one may move a garment with a sewn pocket even if he forgot coins in the pocket; and all the more when he placed intentionally — obviously permitted:
