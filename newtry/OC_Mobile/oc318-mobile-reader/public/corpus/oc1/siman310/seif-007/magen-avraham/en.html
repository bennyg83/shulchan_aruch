A bed that has upon it. It is implied even if they were not upon it at bein hashemashos — forbidden to move it; and you find this where a gentile or a child placed coins on Shabbat with the knowledge of an Israelite [Tosafos daf 44], see siman 309 seif 4, and see siman 308 seif 27 whether it is permitted to shake off the coins, and if he forgot coins upon it see siman 309 seif 4:

However for the need of his body, etc. So too Beit Yosef per the Rosh — requires further study, for behold the Rosh wrote it is comparable to weaver's combs and spindles, for since he is particular about them he designates a place for them — if so, even for the need of his body it is forbidden, as is written in siman 308 seif 1; and so in Hagahot Maimoniyot he wrote it is comparable to dried figs and raisins — see there; and so Tosafos daf 36a; and what is written in Hagahot Ashri — that is a different view; and so the Ra'avan — all agree in this: if he designated it, it is a utensil whose melacha is for prohibition, as is written in siman 308 seif 6 — forbidden except for the need of his body and its place; therefore it appears to me to be strict:

For the pocket is batel to it. And it is difficult — for otherwise it is permitted to wear it, for for the need of his body it is permitted as stated above; and one can say this is what it means: even on Rosh Hashana it is permitted to wear it, for the pocket is batel to it — if so, in any case it is permitted to place an object inside it and take from it, for for the need of its place it is permitted; and even according to what is written in the name of the Rosh — here it is permitted, for we see he is not particular about it:

Since it is not sewn, etc. — meaning the pocket is not sewn along its length to the garment but only its mouth is sewn and it entirely hangs [Beit Yosef]; it is implied that if it is sewn entirely to the garment we say the entire garment became a base; and it is difficult, for we deal with forgetting — if so, even if it rests on the main part of the garment it did not become a base; and one can say nevertheless he needed to shake off the coins, as is written in siman 309 seif 4; but since it is not placed on the main part of the garment he need not shake; but Rambam wrote in tractate Kelim regarding a drawer-board and said: since it is not removable — if so it is impossible to remove the coins unless he moves the drawer with the coins — therefore it is permitted to drag; end of his words; and since this law of a pocket is taken from that of a drawer-board — here too, if it is possible to shake off he must shake off; and know that regarding a drawer-board Tosafos wrote daf 44: even if he placed coins upon it intentionally — permitted to move the drawer since the coins are not on the main part of the utensil — if so, the same applies to a pocket sewn to a garment; and that which Rama wrote "forgot" — that is l'chatchila one should not place it inside, lest we fear he go out with it; and further it appears to me that if he placed coins inside intentionally — forbidden to put his hand in, for in any case the pocket became a base; and further we say in the gemara: when the drawer-board is removable it is an independent utensil, and one does not drag the drawer when there are coins on the drawer-board; and a drawer-board is a board on which the drawer rests [Hagahot Ashri] — if so, here too if a pocket full of coins is tied to a garment — forbidden to move the garment, since the pocket is an independent utensil;
