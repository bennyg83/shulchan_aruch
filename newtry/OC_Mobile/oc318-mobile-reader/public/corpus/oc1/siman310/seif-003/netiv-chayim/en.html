(Taz s.k. 5) And one might wonder at what he wrote here. It appears to me: they are not comparable to each other; and the difference is that in a utensil whose melacha is forbidden, if one places in it a loaf or a child, it appears that he moves the utensil now because of the permitted thing; unlike a candle that was lit on that Shabbat — it turns out that at bein hashemashos he cannot move it at the hour when it burns via a loaf, for there is oil in it; and a candle needed at that hour for light is considered more important than a loaf; therefore it is not a base for a forbidden thing and a permitted thing; and from here you learn that a purse we do not consider via a loaf as a base for a permitted thing and a forbidden thing, for the dinar in the purse is considered more important than a loaf; and likewise a candle burning at bein hashemashos — since it was muktzeh at bein hashemashos, it was muktzeh all day; and so too Magen Avraham in siman 279 — see there.
