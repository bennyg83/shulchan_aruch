Returned to permit. The reason there is no muktzeh for half Shabbat — for at bein hashemashos it was fit. Yoreh De'ah siman 266 I wrote that a knife with which one circumcised on Shabbat — one may not move it after the milah, unlike Rashal's responsum who permits from the reason there is no muktzeh for half Shabbat; for it does not apply to say so here, for at bein hashemashos it was not fit to move — it was muktzeh like a scribe's knife siman 308; and I brought proof from an upper leaf upon which meat was slaughtered in chapter 1 of Beitzah daf 11, and a spit upon which they roasted on Yom Tov in chapter Ein Tzedin — meaning that just as years that have muktzeh due to themselves, meaning the leaf because he is particular and designates a place for it like pins of gold and silver in chapter Kol HaKelim, and the spit is mere wood taken for a spit and there is no utensil upon it as Tur wrote siman 518 — therefore they are inferior to a utensil whose function is for prohibition; and even though at bein hashemashos it stood for melacha for Yom Tov need, nevertheless after the melacha for Yom Tov need it returned to muktzeh prohibition; so too literally with this knife — at bein hashemashos it was muktzeh due to monetary loss like a scribe's knife, for this is obvious that a person is very particular not to use that knife for anything else so it will not be ruined; and especially that one does not eat with it; therefore even though it stands for milah tomorrow, nevertheless after he finishes his need it returns to the prohibition of moving at bein hashemashos; and again I saw in Yeshuos Yaakov chapter Ein Tzedin siman 16 that he upholds his words: and it appears that he did not hold there regarding dried figs and raisins that they are not fit at all for they spoil; but this knife is fit to eat with and to do permitted use even at bein hashemashos — only he prepares it to circumcise and not for other use; nevertheless it is not muktzeh, for there is a doubt whether he prepares it for Shabbat itself; and a spit upon which they roasted is different for it is repulsive; here it is not repulsive for this when he cleans it afterward; and that he hides it for his melacha and returns to be muktzeh due to monetary loss — this is not, for at bein hashemashos it also stands for that — end of his words; and in what is written here all this is hidden, and it is exactly like the leaf and spit we mentioned — forbidden to move except for milah need; and afterward hide it in that room where he circumcises;
