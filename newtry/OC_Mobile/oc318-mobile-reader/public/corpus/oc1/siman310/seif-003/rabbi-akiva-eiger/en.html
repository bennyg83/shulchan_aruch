In my humble opinion it is not all; according to what I wrote above in siman 279 in Magen Avraham Orach Chayim — the Rashba in Chiddushei Shabbat 122, and likewise in a responsum of the Tashbetz in the name of the Ra'avad, that a candle is not called a utensil whose melacha is forbidden, for it does not serve forbidden melacha; rather the forbidden thing rests upon it; if so, here too with a bed that does not serve forbidden melacha, only that coins rest upon it — it is not called a utensil whose melacha is forbidden; and in Levush here he wrote there is a dispute whether the bed is called a utensil whose melacha is forbidden, or since the bed does nothing, only coins rest upon it — it is not called a utensil whose melacha is forbidden; and this is our words.
