Returned [to permit]. Taz wrote similarly regarding a knife with which one circumcised on Shabbat — it is muktzeh at bein hashemashos due to monetary loss like a scribe's knife, for a person is very particular not to use that knife for anything else so it will not be ruined; and especially that one does not eat with it, for that is against human nature; therefore even though it stands for tomorrow's milah work, nevertheless after he finishes his need it returns to the prohibition of moving at bein hashemashos; therefore they hide it in that room where he circumcises there; and so Maharil; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 266. But Bach siman 308 permits moving it; and so Maharshal; and Magen Avraham in siman 336 seif kaf 5 elaborated on this matter and concludes therefore one should be strict not to put it down from his hand until he brings it to his house, for any muktzeh in one's hand one may move it to any place he wishes — end of his words; and see Sefer Beit Yaakov siman 102:
