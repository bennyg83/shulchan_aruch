(18) That are fit and not fit — meaning they dried somewhat on the roof from erev Shabbat:
(19) It is not a matter — for behold they are like stones and earth. The poskim wrote: when do we say it became designated at bein hashemashos — specifically things that are finished by heaven, such as dried figs that are finished only by the sun's heat, lest the day be cloudy on Shabbat and he remove his mind from them; but something finished by human action, such as dates to which he put water on erev Shabbat — even though certainly they are not fit at bein hashemashos, neither the dates nor the water, for the dates had not yet absorbed the water's taste to be fit for drinking and also the dates for now are not fit — nevertheless he did not remove his mind from them for Shabbat day lest they be muktzeh because of this, for it is in his power to delay them until tomorrow when the dates absorb the taste:
