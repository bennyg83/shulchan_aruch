And what is written in Hagahot Ashri — that is the first view, so it should read — meaning Rabbeinu Yehudah brought proof for his words that also in Hagahot Ashri he wrote it is permitted for the need of his body and its place; and on this Magen Avraham rejected that there is no proof from Hagahot Ashri, for Hagahot Ashri holds like the first view that Rabbeinu Yehudah brought, and that is Rif's view who holds we do not rule in this like Rav — therefore he wrote in Hagahot Ashri that for the need of his body and its place it is permitted; and if Hagahot Ashri held like Rif and that is one view — why did he not permit except for the need of his body and its place, even from sun to shade he should have permitted — on this Magen Avraham wrote; and all agree, etc. — it is a utensil whose melacha is for prohibition, etc. — meaning even for Rif who ruled not like Rav and therefore did not permit in Hagahot Ashri except for the need of his body and its place; but according to the view of those who forbid that Rama brought — they are Rabbeinu Tam and the Rosh — for according to them it is muktzeh machmat cheisron kis, even for the need of his body and its place forbidden; and since Rama concluded on those who forbid and so is the custom — therefore Magen Avraham wrote one should be strict to forbid even for the need of his body and its place, for according to what Magen Avraham wrote, for them it is forbidden even for the need of his body and its place:
