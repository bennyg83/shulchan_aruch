Ash that is permitted. But ash that was kindled on Shabbat — forbidden to all, for it is a new entity that was not in the world; see siman 498 seif 15; and it appears to me that if ash kindled on Shabbat became mixed with yesterday's ash — it is batel in the majority, even though dry in dry is called wet in wet and we require sixty, as is written in siman 453 seif 3 — nevertheless here, since the essence of its prohibition is rabbinic, a majority suffices; and specifically when it was not recognizable before it became mixed, as is written in siman 308 seif 2:

That a permitted object is important. And even though it is important to the whole world — if it is not important to him it is not called important, as in the gemara; and the same applies in the reverse — see siman 308:

That it is not possible to gather. Because wood chips are small chips or small coals that it is impossible to take the ash without them [Beit Yosef] — and according to what is written in siman 309 seif 3 in the name of Tosafos, this is unnecessary:
