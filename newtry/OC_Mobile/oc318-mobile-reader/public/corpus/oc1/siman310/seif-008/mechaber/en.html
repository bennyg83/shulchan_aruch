A utensil upon which there is a forbidden thing and a permitted thing — permitted to move it; such as a shovel upon which from before the day there is ash that is permitted to move in order to cover with it spit or excrement, and upon it are also wood chips that are forbidden to move — permitted to move the shovel as it is, when the permitted thing is more important than the forbidden thing; but if the forbidden thing is more important than the permitted thing — it is batel against it and forbidden to move; and the reason for the permission of this moving is because it is impossible to take the soot alone, even if he shakes it from the shovel [or if he needs the place of the shovel, as was explained above siman 305]; and if he needs only the body of the shovel — he should not move it as it is, rather he shakes the ash and the wood chips in their place and takes the shovel.[And likewise if he can shake the forbidden thing alone — he shakes it and does not move it with the permitted thing [Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of the Rosh]; and all this does not deal except when the permitted thing was with the forbidden thing from before the day; but if the forbidden thing was upon it alone, what he placed of the permitted thing with it on Shabbat does not help [Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of the Rashba].] ואם אינו צריך אלא לגוף המחתה לא יטלטלנה כמו שהיא אלא ינער האפר ושברי העצים במקומם ויטול המחתה: הגה וכן אם יכול לנער האיסור לחוד ינערנו ולא יטלטלנו עם ההיתר [ב"י בשם תשובת הרא"ש] וכל זה לא מיירי אלא שהיה ההיתר עם האיסור מבע"י אבל אם היה האיסור עליו לבד לא מהני מה שהניח אצלו ההיתר בשבת [ב"י בשם תשו' הרשב"א]:
