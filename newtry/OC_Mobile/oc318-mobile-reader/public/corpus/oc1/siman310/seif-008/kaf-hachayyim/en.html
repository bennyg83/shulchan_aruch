8) There: Even wheat that he sowed, etc. This does not resemble a lamp that was extinguished in siman 279, where, since it was set aside during twilight, it remains set aside for the entire day. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. See Bach, who writes in the name of Shiltei HaGibborim that wheat one sowed is forbidden like a lamp that was extinguished, although Bach himself agrees with the Shulchan Aruch; see there. The matter nevertheless requires examination, because in siman 311, seif 8, and siman 336, seif 6, Shulchan Aruch forbids it when he intended it for planting.
