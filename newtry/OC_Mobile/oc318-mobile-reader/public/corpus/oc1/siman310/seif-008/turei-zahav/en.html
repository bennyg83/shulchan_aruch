It appears this means from erev Shabbat the prohibition was upon it alone and it became a base for forbidden at bein hashemashos — then it does not help to place permitted also on Shabbat; but if on Shabbat forbidden was placed on it — since at bein hashemashos it was not muktzeh, it has muktzeh for half Shabbat; and we rule there is no muktzeh for half Shabbat, except that as long as forbidden is on it — moving is forbidden; therefore one may place important permitted things on the prohibition and move them as stated:
