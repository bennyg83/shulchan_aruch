S.k. 5 — "rental does not apply," meaning and if so he has still not fully left the Jew's domain; and since it is pledged in the Jew's hand, it is judged like the Jew's own object. And see above siman 252, which deals there regarding selling to a non-Jew — there the reason is because the non-Jew has not yet acquired it; unlike here, where the reason is not on account of the non-Jew's acquisition, but because it is like taking out from the Jew's domain; and if he designated and we say rental acquires, this is not like taking out from the Jew's domain; and nevertheless specifically when pledged — for then we judge it like a Jew's object, since it is impossible to take it without the Jew's permission; unlike if it is the non-Jew's object and not pledged, as stated in s.k. 4. And see in Mordechai, who wrote that Ravyah permits where he designated a place for him from the day before, for it already left the Jew's domain entirely from the day before, as we learned in chapter 1 of Pesachim, etc.; and there is a printing error — and it should read as stated.
