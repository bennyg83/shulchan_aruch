(א) On account of darkei shalom — such as a non-Jew who was ill and sent another with food of a Jew:

(ב) Or the non-Jew is mute — and requests his objects, and even though there is no concern of danger in the matter:

(ג) And the same law for the need of a mitzvah — and even though it is forbidden to give a non-Jew if one knows he will take the objects from his house to reshut ha'rabbim on Shabbat, even not as the Jew's agent, and all the more when it is as his agent — nevertheless they permitted in all these because according to us, for many poskim we have no reshut ha'rabbim d'oraisa, and it is only shevut d'shevut, and they permitted in a place of mitzvah or on account of darkei shalom and the like; and nevertheless one must be careful in all these not to hand into the hand of a non-Jew, lest the Jew perform hotza'ah, for it is possible to ask the non-Jew that he take it himself; and even if the non-Jew requests that they place into his vessel — one should be careful that he place it in the house and after the Jew pours into it he take it:
