To give to him. Nevertheless forbidden to give into his hand literally, as stated in seif 1 above; and it appears to me this is also the intent of Mordekhai in name of Tosafos; and what he wrote at the beginning "permitted to give to them even by hand" — it comes only to exclude one view that forbids giving into their hands, only to place before them and say nothing; and on this he wrote that R' Yitzchak wrote permitted to give into their hands — meaning and permitted to tell him to take them out, for nowadays we have no reshut ha-rabbim, and it is shevut shevut siman 67; but forbidden to give into his hand literally since it is possible without this, as stated; and Rabbeinu Yehuda did not explain thus, and it appears to me as stated; and it is simple that even if the vessel is of a non-Jew one may not give into his hand on account of uprooting — not like Ohel Yesharim:
