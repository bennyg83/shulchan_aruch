(א) Some forbid. For it is nolad; even though wheat is fit for a cup, nevertheless they are not fit to eat in that state in which they were made at the end, after a d'oraisa prohibition was made in it — it became bread [and see seif 11]; and some say the reason lest he tell the non-Jew to cook; even though regarding a non-Jew who kindled a lamp for himself we do not decree, as stated in siman 276 regarding food they decree more [Tur]; and so in Mordekhai ch. 3 of Eruvin and see siman 515; and Tosafos on Avodah Zarah daf 66b wrote, and one should not forbid the bread lest the wood it was baked with was detached on Shabbat and is muktzeh — what of it, for we do not say there is benefit of wood in bread except regarding issurei hanaah; and muktzeh is permitted in hanaah, end of his words; and in Pesachim daf 47b it implies forbidden to cook with muktzeh wood, and see siman 507; and see there siman 507 seif 2; and see siman 246:

(ב) And some permit. For they hold since he finished it by human action it is not muktzeh, as stated in Beitzah ch. 3; and those who forbid hold specifically regarding muktzeh we say thus, that he did not designate it from his mind since a person can finish it — unlike regarding nolad; and in Yeshuot Yaakov in Chullin 132 they wrote — only regarding something that began before Shabbat and was completed on Shabbat is it permitted, see there; and so siman 252 seif 4 — it is also nolad, for at first hide and now a vessel; and Tosafos on Shabbat wrote in Maggid — only regarding pots, even though they were boiling on erev Shabbat, they were not designated for the whole day since it was in his power to cool them, for if he needed them he would cool them; and likewise beans and lentils — even though when they began to cook they were not fit to eat, nevertheless when they finished cooking afterward — permitted, for at first his intent was they cook without any pushing off; unlike when he forces the basket before the chicks and they were upon it on erev Shabbat — he designated them at that hour for their need and they were designated for the whole day, and human finishing does not help, end quote; and so in Rosh ch. 3 of Beitzah — this is not considered human finishing; but in Tosafos Shabbat daf 44 in s.v. "there is upon it money," etc., and it is like human finishing, end quote — it implies that even though he designated it at that one hour it is considered human finishing; and it is difficult there, for if it were human finishing, even if it were upon it on erev Shabbat it should be permitted; and one must say they do not literally mean human finishing; and see in Tosafos Beitzah:
