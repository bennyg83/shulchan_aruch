For himself — Taz: even for one who permits above, and in pressing need — nevertheless here forbidden even in pressing need, for fasting on Shabbat is forbidden; see Tosafos likewise 122a s.v. mishtamesh. And there is further reason: trapping and attached they were stricter because it is like blocking with his hands; and all these, even a shopkeeper in a city whose majority of buyers are gentiles — nevertheless forbidden for these reasons.
