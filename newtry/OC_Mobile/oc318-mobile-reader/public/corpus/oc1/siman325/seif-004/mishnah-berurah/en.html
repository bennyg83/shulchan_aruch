(א) There are those who forbid — lest there was flour or dough in the oven that is not fit for a cup, and it was set aside for the oven; and even if we say there were then wheat fit for a cup — nevertheless they are not fit to eat in that form in which they were made at the end, after a d'oraisa prohibition was made in it when it became bread, and it would be nolad; and there are those who forbid for another reason, lest he say to the non-Jew to cook, and even though regarding a non-Jew who kindled a lamp for himself they did not decree, as stated in siman 276 regarding food they decree more; and Magen Avraham wrote in siman 517 that from the Mechaber's words there it is implied he holds like this reason:

(ב) And there are those who permit — for they do not distinguish between food matter and non-food matter, since nevertheless he made it for himself; and regarding muktzeh he holds that since the non-Jew had ability to finish it, there is no muktzeh and nolad on account of this.
