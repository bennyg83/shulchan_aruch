(א) Seif 4 "some forbid." Perhaps it was from erev Shabbat, flour or dough not fit for a cup; and as stated in the first chapter of Chullin regarding slaughter that it is not fit for a cup; and even according to Ri'f and Rosh that even pouring is permitted because it is not rejected by hand — here it is rejected by hand and is like dried figs and raisins in chapter 3 of Shabbos 145a-b. And furthermore, as they challenged on the mishnah of a non-Jew who lit, etc., what is different from liquids that drip and fruits that fall that they forbade lest he squeeze and lest he detach; and one should explain that only regarding food do we forbid lest a Jew do — not so regarding a lamp and a sheep, etc.; and if so, likewise here it is forbidden; and see Tosafos Avodah Zarah 66b s.v. "he said," and the root of their dispute in Seder Torah parashah 22 at length, see there

(ב) "And some say." For he holds like another textual reading that resolved that squeezing fruits and detaching are a light matter, unlike there at that time and so it is recalled — and as Tosafos stated at the beginning of Yom Tov 3a, and Rashba explains that liquids that drip and fruits that fall — since they come on their own, if you permit him he will come to do by hand what is exchanged for him, and they forbade also what is done by a non-Jew on account of those that come on their own, and it is all one decree, and as stated in Beitzah; and according to this, in his mouth it is permitted, and the same applies to cooking something that has no concern of cooking of a non-Jew there. And also because of muktzeh there is no need, since they were completed by human hands, as stated 27a and 40 — from their words that they were completed by heavenly forces, even by a non-Jew they are forbidden, such as dried figs and raisins, and likewise from what is stated 24b and on Rashi s.v. "if" — not like what is stated in siman 310 seif 2 in the Hagahah
