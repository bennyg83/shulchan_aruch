For himself — forbidden to a Jew. Even according to those who permit bread baked by a gentile on Shabbat in seif 4 — that is only in baking and cooking, for everyone knows there are no melachot heavier than these and one will not come to do with his own hands; but not so with trapping fish and gathering fruit, for it does not appear to people that it is so much melacha, and there is room to decree lest he do it with his own hands; and so Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer HaTerumah; and accordingly it is difficult from the outset regarding water for his animal in seif 1 that it is permitted — but in Tosafos chapter Kol Kitvei daf 122 they distinguished: squeezing and plucking are light to do and a person is eager for them because they are food matters; but with a lamp and water they are not so light to do and a person is not eager for them — end of his words:
