Bread that a non-Jew baked for himself on Shabbat — some forbid and some permit; and in a time of need or for the need of a mitzvah, such as a circumcision feast or for the need of the blessing of HaMotzi, one may rely on those who permit.But it is forbidden to give him money erev Shabbat that he give him the bread on Shabbat, for then he acts with the Jew's intent in mind (Rabbeinu Yerucham part 1).
