(א) That there is no concern — such as other things to which trapping and gathering do not apply, or even in these but it is recognizable they were not trapped and gathered on Shabbat, as above, and as below siman 515:
(ב) Permitted — that is, even in eating, and even for those who forbid in s.k. 16 in all melachos of eating that a non-Jew did for himself in issur techumim — permitted, and even in techum of 12 mil, as below:
(ג) Permitted to move — for on account of themselves there is no muktzeh prohibition, for they were not trapped on Shabbat; and even though in eating they are forbidden to that Jew, since for another Jew they are permitted — there is no name of muktzeh on them, for they are fit for another Jew [Rosh]:
(ד) But to eat forbidden, etc. — and if he brought for two people two things — forbidden to exchange in order to permit in eating, lest if they are permitted they will come to say to the non-Jew l'chatchila to bring for them. And to sell to another for whom it was not brought — permitted [and this is in the manner of permit explained in siman 323], provided he not profit in selling for more than he bought [Knesset HaGedolah]:
(ה) For one for whom it was brought — and explained below in siman 515 that the same law applies to his household, forbidden [and the same law for guests who were prepared with him on Shabbat, see there in Mishna Berurah]; and if he brought to sell in a city whose majority are Jews — forbidden for everyone, for he brought on behalf of all; and in a city whose majority are non-Jews — Shulchan Aruch writes there it is permitted, see there in Magen Avraham. And know that if it was brought on a ship above ten handbreadths — permitted, for there is no techum above ten [Magen Avraham]; and see in siman 404 that this is when it was not on land at twilight, and also was not below ten handbreadths even once; for if it was below ten handbreadths and went out from there outside the techum, even if afterward the entire way was above ten handbreadths, and all the more if it was on land at twilight — forbidden to move outside 4 amot, see there:
(ו) Bichdei sheya'asu — all per the matter in which melacha was done on Shabbat; if the non-Jew brought by way of riding on a horse, one must estimate also in this manner. And if he brought from a distant place and stayed three days — he need not wait only one day corresponding to the time he went on Shabbat, and this is when it is known from which place he brought; but when it is not known — we estimate forever as though he brought them outside the techum:
(ז) And some say, etc. — because it is not customary to bring at night from a distant place, and if so the Jew still benefits from what he brought by day; therefore he must wait the measure of bringing for tomorrow by day; and specifically in this that was brought from a distant place outside the techum they hold thus, because there is no way to bring it at night from there; but above, when it is within the techum but was gathered or trapped on Shabbat — also according to this view it suffices to wait bichdei sheya'asu at night; and Magen Avraham wrote Yeshuot Yaakov ruled like the first view to be lenient, and so is reasonable one should not be stringent in this, for per the second view they permit entirely, and all the more for guests or another matter of mitzvah one may certainly be lenient; but if there was also a d'oraisa prohibition that he detached from the attached outside the techum — one should be stringent nevertheless; for a matter of mitzvah one may be lenient in every manner:
(ח) And for others permitted — for in rabbinic techumim they forbade only for one for whom they were brought, and likewise in other rabbinic prohibitions, as below in this seif [and if he also brought by way of reshut ha'rabbim see below seif 14 s.k. 75]; and even in techum of 12 mil for those who hold it is d'oraisa, since it is not explicit in Scripture they were not stringent in it [Magen Avraham in name of Terumat HaDeshen in siman 515]:
(ט) Permitted at night immediately — for they hold that in rabbinic prohibitions one need not be stringent with bichdei sheya'asu even for one for whom it was brought; and for practical halacha one should be stringent like the first view [Eliyah Rabbah in name of Bach and Maharshal, and he wrote so is implied from the conclusion of Shulchan Aruch below siman 515]; and all the more in techum of 12 mil, for some say it is d'oraisa:
(י) Within 4 amot — for every thing that went outside the techum — forbidden to move outside 4 amot, even if permitted in eating in its place, if not in a city surrounded by a partition that the entire city is considered 4 amot:
(כ) Surrounded by a wall — and the same law when repaired in the form of a doorway, for the entire city is also considered 4 amot; and all the more a house and courtyard that the entire perimeter is considered 4 amot:
(ל) And this is when it is surrounded, etc. — for if it is not surrounded for residence, or the non-Jew left it in the field — the object has only 4 amot; and if one moved it one amah or two, another may not move it again except until completion of 4 amot [Magen Avraham and Taz and Shach and Shaarei Teshuvah]:
