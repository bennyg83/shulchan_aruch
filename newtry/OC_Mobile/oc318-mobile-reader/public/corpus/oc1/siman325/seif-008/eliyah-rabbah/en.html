[15] And for others it is permitted, etc. If it was brought for two people, they may not exchange it with one another, because if exchange were permitted they would not refrain from sending a non-Jew, as stated by Knesset HaGedolah. This requires study in light of the incident of the two pious men who exchanged an ox. The Magen Avraham writes that if it was brought by ship, it is prohibited to take it beyond four amot, unless it was already aboard the ship before Shabbos, in which case it is permitted even for the person for whom it was brought.
