(ב) Even for one for whom it was brought. And if he brought for two — forbidden to exchange (so it is):

(ג) Bekdei she-ye'asu. And one need not wait except bekdei she-ye'asu that the one who brings on Shabbat — if he brought them riding on a horse one need not wait so much (so it is); and it appears one need not wait except bekdei she-ye'asu that he can ride by day, for at night on Shabbat he certainly did not ride; and even though in Rif it states we are concerned perhaps they went at night — that is when they did not eat it on Shabbat itself; but on a weekday one need not wait so much, as stated:

(ד) And some say the night does not count. Because it is not usual to bring at night from a distant place (Mordekhai); and Yeshuot Yaakov ruled like the first view:

(ה) And it must be enclosed.
