(א) That it was brought. And if it was brought for two people — they are forbidden to exchange — Knesset HaGedolah. And it is permitted to sell to one for whom it was not brought without profit if he did not intend for this; and if he intended — it is forbidden, as Knesset HaGedolah — see there. And if it was brought on a ship — it is permitted; and see siman 404 and siman 405 that it is forbidden to take them outside four amot unless they were on the ship from erev Shabbat and were not on dry land. Magen Avraham:

(ב) In the time it takes to do them. And one need not wait except for the time the one who brings on Shabbat tarries; if he brought it riding on a horse — he need not wait so much — Knesset HaGedolah. And it appears to me he need not wait except for the time one can ride by day, for at Shabbat night he certainly did not ride. Magen Avraham — see there:

(ג) For night does not count. In Mordechai chapter Kol Kitvei it gave the reason for this: because it is not customary to bring at night from a distant place, etc.; and accordingly this stringency applies specifically to one who brings from outside the techum; not so with trapping fish and gathering fruit, which is in a nearby place — he can well bring at night; therefore Rama wrote here and not above in seif 7 — Taz.
