An item in which there is no concern of trapping and that is attached, but was brought from outside the techum — if the non-Jew brought it for himself, it is permitted even on that day; and if he brought it on behalf of a Jew, it is permitted to move it even for the one for whom it was brought, but to eat it is forbidden on that day for the one for whom it was brought, and in the evening [one waits] the time it would take to do them.And some say that the night does not count toward the reckoning; rather one must wait on the first day the time it would take to do them (Semak and Mordechai end of chapter 20), and for others it is permitted on that day. And some say that for the one for whom it was brought it is permitted in the evening immediately. And that which it is permitted for a Jew to move it even when the non-Jew brought it for himself — specifically within four cubits or within the city if it is surrounded by a wall and it is surrounded for residence, meaning that it was settled and afterward surrounded; and ordinary towns are presumed surrounded for residence, and ordinary fortresses are not presumed surrounded for residence (and see above siman 401).] ולאחרים מותר בו ביום וי"א דלמי שהובא בשבילו מותר לערב מיד והא דשרי לישראל לטלטל אפי' כשהביאו העכו"ם לעצמו דוק' בתוך ד' אמות או בתוך העיר אם היא מוקפת חומה והוא שתהא מוקפת לדירה דהיינו שישבה ולבסוף הוקפה וסתם עיירות מוקפות לדירה וסתם מבצרים אינם מוקפים לדירה (וע"ל סי' ת"א.
