(א) That he brought on Shabbat, etc. — discusses that he brought them from outside the techum, and also it was by way of reshut ha'rabbim that a Torah prohibition was done; for if it were not by way of reshut ha'rabbim it would be permitted for others at night immediately:
(ב) From a nearby place — that is, from outside the techum; and if he brought them by way of a great highway that is publicized — forbidden to him forever, as stated in seif 14 regarding a coffin and grave:
(ג) And if he knows certainly, etc. — whether to be stringent, such as when it is much more than the techum, or to be lenient, such as when it is within the techum and on account that he brought them by way of reshut ha'rabbim — for this he must wait until bichdei sheyavi them from that place, so he not benefit from melacha of Shabbat:
(ד) He shall wait until evening bichdei, etc. — that is, even if it is a thing available in a nearby place, since it came now from afar he must wait bichdei sheyavi from the place it came [Beit Yosef]:
(ה) And this is when he brought them, etc. — refers within the techum; therefore permitted when he brought them by way of karmelit, even for him at night immediately; for if it were outside the techum, even by way of karmelit it would be forbidden to him bichdei sheyavi and for others immediately, as stam below in siman 515 seif 5; and even though on karmelit there is also a rabbinic prohibition like on techum — nevertheless they were lenient in it because he does not benefit so much from the non-Jew's bringing from within the techum:
