(א) Seif 15 "and not others." Meaning: even though in the aforesaid Tosafos it states that permitted for another deals with "in the time it takes to do them," and when he brings them in his public manner it is forbidden, as stated in seif 14, and as Ran states there; and that which the mishnah distinguishes between a coffin and flutes — because an unspecified coffin is in public and unspecified flutes are in private. (ב) "In the time it takes to come." As Ri'f explains there — unless they came, etc., unless one waits until they come, etc., and likewise they said in chapter 2 of Machshirin — he found vegetables in it, etc., he waits until they come from a nearby place

(ג) "And if he knows." For we do not rely on a nearby place except from sufficiency; and see Tosafos in Eruvin there

(ד) "And afterward permitted." Shabbos there — and why here should he wait, etc.; according to Ri'f's explanation we learn that regarding flutes it is permitted even for him

(ה) "And this applies." It is the view of seif 11 of seif 8, and as stated explicitly that that of Shabbos that requires "in the time it takes to come" deals on Rosh Hashanah; but outside the techum, since it is only rabbinic, it is not required; and it requires further study; and see Taz
