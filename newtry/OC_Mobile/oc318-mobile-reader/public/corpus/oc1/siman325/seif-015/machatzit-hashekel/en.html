(א) (s.k. 15) Outside the techum — and if brought on a ship — permitted; and the reason — no prohibition of bringing from outside the techum was made, for we are accustomed that a ship does not travel unless the water is ten tefachim high; and since it was asked in Eruvin daf 43a whether there is techum above ten tefachim or not and it was not resolved — we follow its doubt leniently, since techum is d'rabbanan; and even outside twelve mil that is d'oraisa according to Rambam — nevertheless on water Rambam wrote it is d'rabbanan, since traveling on water is not its usual use even far — there is only d'rabbanan prohibition; therefore if brought on a ship — permitted; and all this is explained above siman 248 at length, see there:

(ב) For forbidden to take it out, etc. — unless on a ship and not on dry land — meaning on water there is no prohibition of bringing from outside the techum; nevertheless they were at bein ha-shemashot on dry land, or even on a ship and it was grounded — for the water depth there is not ten tefachim, the ship acquired residence on erev Shabbat where it stood in that place; and since now it is outside the techum it has only four amot from where it was placed there or from the ship; but when it was on erev Shabbat on the ship and the water was ten deep — it did not acquire residence at all:
