[10] [Levush] And even though a gentile, etc. So Beit Yosef challenged; and Magen Avraham wrote on it; and see siman 517 where he wrote in the name of Rosh that it is forbidden as a decree lest he go up and pluck; until here; and he meant there is no prohibition on account of muktzeh; and in my humble opinion Beit Yosef aimed well, for at the beginning of chapter Ein Tzedim Rosh wrote the reason for the majority is muktzeh; and one should not challenge that his words contradict, for Tosafos in Beitzah daf 23 and responsum of Ramban 122 already answered that there are two reasons — truly regarding turnip and radish that require digging in the ground, where there is no concern he will do the trouble himself, one must say on account of muktzeh; and regarding fruit he intended they would fall and they are not muktzeh — one must say the reason lest he go up and pluck; see siman 322 seif 3:
