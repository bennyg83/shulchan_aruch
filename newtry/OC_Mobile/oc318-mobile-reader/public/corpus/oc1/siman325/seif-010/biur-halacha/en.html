(א) A gentile who filled, etc. — see Magen Avraham who concludes that the same applies if gentiles filled water — it is not permitted except when they filled for their own need [but for a Jew's need it is forbidden, and we require waiting until evening "in the time it takes to do them" like for a gentile — Peri Megadim]; however this seems preferable to a gentile, for even if he knows him it is permitted, for we are not concerned lest he increase on his account:

(ב) To water his animal from them — in the mishnah it states "he waters after him — a Jew"; and Rashi explained why it says "after him": it is evident the gentile filled for his own need; and Re'em's novellae brought it — see there further; and it appears obvious Rashi deals with stam where we do not know his intent, and from this there is recognition he did it l'chatchila for his own need — permitted; but if we know he did it for his own need — a Jew may benefit immediately; and with this Rama's language in all the poskim is reconciled, and they did not bring Rashi's words, for he deals when we know:
