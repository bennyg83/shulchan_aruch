(א) (s.k. 10) And some permit — for they hold since human finishing, etc. — meaning there is no concern for reason two that Magen Avraham wrote in the previous s.k. lest he tell a gentile, etc. — one may say they hold we are not concerned for this, as we find regarding a gentile who kindled a lamp for his need; and Magen Avraham and Mordekhai do not distinguish between food and lamp. But where does the reason of nolad go — he wrote it is human finishing, that the gentile had it in his hand to bake on Shabbat; and likewise there is no muktzeh concern, as in Beitzah 42b where it was asked whether there is muktzeh for half Shabbat — meaning something that was on erev Shabbat fit and on Shabbat day rejected and not fit, and afterward became fit again; it is impossible — since it was designated for part of Shabbat when not fit, it remains in its prohibition as we say "since it was designated for erev Shabbat it is designated for the whole day"; if not, the question should arise from beans and lentils cooked on Yom Tov that are muktzeh for half Shabbat — nevertheless we eat them, for at first they were fit to eat alive and when put in the pot he rejected them that they are not fit; their cooking finished and again fit to eat — evidently there is no muktzeh for half Shabbat with rejection; and according to you who say no muktzeh for half Shabbat — this should be difficult, for stam pots on erev Shabbat are boiling and not fit to eat, and when cooled we eat them on Yom Tov — let us say since designated for erev Shabbat; rather human finishing is different, for it was in his power to cool them on erev Shabbat; likewise beans and lentils — even if there is muktzeh for half Shabbat, they are permitted, for also human finishing, that he can finish their cooking:

(ב) And in Yeshuot Yaakov, etc.; and so siman 252 seif 4 — from this siman 252 Yeshuot Yaakov brought proof for his words; meaning from Gra's words that Magen Avraham brought in siman 252 s.k. 15, thus: a gentile who made shoes on Yom Tov unspecified to sell, etc., and Ra'avad permitted them since human finishing and he can finish them on Yom Tov — there is no muktzeh concern, end Gra; and Yeshuot Yaakov wrote, thus: and from that he wrote "since he can finish" — it implies l'chatchila it deals that he began from erev Shabbat and finishes on Shabbat or Yom Tov that we permit on account of human finishing since he can finish them, end quote; certainly from the gemara's words there is no proof, for it used human finishing regarding beans and lentils or boiling pots on erev Shabbat where there is only finishing; and so Gra at the beginning of his words "and Ra'avad permitted them since human finishing" — one may explain the gemara's language; but from that he concludes "and can finish them" — from this it proves necessarily specifically when he began in permissibility, for otherwise it should have been a greater novelty that even if he did not begin from erev Shabbat — permitted, and it should have said since he can make them on Yom Tov even though he did not begin from erev Shabbat; nevertheless Gra deals with a case that was; still it should have conveyed the reason of the novelty "since he can do them" — from which we learn even if he did not begin from erev Shabbat — permitted; therefore we require specifically that he began from erev Shabbat:

(ג) That it is also nolad — the language is somewhat garbled; his intent is to combine both explanations together — the distinction Magen Avraham made between muktzeh and nolad, and Yeshuot Yaakov's distinction whether he began from erev Shabbat or not — regarding muktzeh even if he did not begin from erev Shabbat — permitted; and regarding nolad we do not say human finishing permits except specifically when he began from erev Shabbat; and that which appears from siman 252 — that is the above Gra, for there too it is nolad, for at first hide, etc.:

(ד) And Tosafos wrote, etc. — the gemara challenges Rav Yosef who holds forbidden to nullify a vessel from its place, from what was taught: one forces the basket before chicks so they go up and down; and they answer when the chicks descended — permitted to move the basket; therefore it is not nullification so much; and they challenge from another braita that forbids moving the basket even if they are no longer upon it; and they answer on erev Shabbat they were upon it; and "since it was designated for erev Shabbat," etc.; and Tosafos challenged — behold he can cause them to fly and it is human finishing; and likewise we do not say "since it was designated for erev Shabbat" as in Beitzah regarding stam boiling pots on erev Shabbat and beans and lentils as above; and they answer specifically regarding pots even though, etc.:

(ה) But in Tosafos Shabbat daf 44, etc., it appears, etc. — Rav said: a bed not designated for money — if there is money upon it on Shabbat, forbidden to move it; if there is no money — permitted to move it; and this is when there was no money upon it on erev Shabbat; and Tosafos challenged on this that he said if there is money — forbidden to move it — necessarily it deals money was placed upon it on Shabbat; if placed on erev Shabbat, even if there is no money — forbidden to move, for it was upon it on erev Shabbat; if so how do we find it was placed on Shabbat in permissibility; and they answer it was placed on Shabbat by a gentile or Israelite minor with Israelite intent; and they sought to resolve from here there is no muktzeh for half Shabbat, for otherwise even if there is no money — meaning they were removed — forbidden to move, since money was upon it on Shabbat; and they answer it deals money was placed by a gentile intending to remove them after a time — and it is human finishing, end quote; and from this it appears even though he designated it at that hour, etc.; and if so Tosafos' words contradict one another:

(ו) And further difficult there, etc. — meaning otherwise Tosafos' words on daf 44 contradict themselves; and so Maharsha challenged there; and see there what he wrote:

(ז) And it appears they do not literally mean human finishing; and so they answer in the later edition of Maharsha that "since it was designated for erev Shabbat" is stricter than muktzeh for half Shabbat; and know that regarding muktzeh for half Shabbat the gemara was uncertain, unlike regarding "since designated for erev Shabbat"; and also according to some poskim even if not so there is muktzeh for half Shabbat — for eating but not for moving; if so where it was designated that one hour — human finishing is inferior and therefore does not help to nullify "since designated for erev Shabbat" which is stricter; nevertheless it helps to nullify muktzeh for half Shabbat; and with this there is no difficulty in this case of the bed with money — for when money was upon it on erev Shabbat one may say "since designated for erev Shabbat" does not help — inferior human finishing; but if placed on Shabbat where there is only muktzeh for half Shabbat — here it helps even inferior human finishing; and so the basket before chicks is resolved — since it was upon it on erev Shabbat, inferior human finishing does not help:

(ח) And see in Tosafos Beitzah daf 27a s.v. "rather human finishing," etc. — meaning there too it must be as Magen Avraham wrote (and there Maharsha's answer does not apply, see there) — Tosafos challenged it should be resolved there is no muktzeh for half Shabbat from the braita of forcing the basket before chicks — we say permitted to move the basket after they descended even though forbidden to move when upon it; and they answer and one may say it is different there for it is like human finishing in his power to cause them to fly from upon it, end quote; and accordingly the braita that teaches on erev Shabbat at bein ha-shemashot forbidden to move on account of "since designated for erev Shabbat," etc. — should be difficult, for how is it human finishing; even "since designated for erev Shabbat" we do not say; nevertheless one must say between "since designated for erev Shabbat" and muktzeh for half Shabbat regarding inferior human finishing — and this basket before chicks is inferior human finishing as above, for it was designated one hour; therefore Tosafos were precise in their wording and wrote it is like human finishing — it implies literal human finishing is not so:
