A non-Jew who drew water from a pit that is a reshut ha-yachid to a reshut ha-rabim — it is permitted for a Jew to give his animal to drink from them, provided the non-Jew does not recognize him, for there is no concern lest he increase on his behalf. And if he drew for the need of a Jew's animal — it is forbidden for all types of use, even for another Jew. And if he drew from a pit in a reshut ha-yachid to a karmelit — it is permitted after he did not draw on his behalf.And there are those who are lenient and say that even if it was brought via reshut ha-rabim for a Jew's need, a person is permitted to drink from them, since it is possible to go there and drink (Tur in) the name of Rabbenu Tam); and there are those who permit even l'chatchila (Kol Bo); and so they practiced leniency to say even l'chatchila for a non-Jew to bring beer or other things via karmelit or without an eruv; and even though one should be stringent in the matter, nevertheless one should not protest those who are lenient for Shabbat needs and in a time of need, for one may be lenient in instructing a non-Jew for need as explained siman 307; and all the more so in this case.
