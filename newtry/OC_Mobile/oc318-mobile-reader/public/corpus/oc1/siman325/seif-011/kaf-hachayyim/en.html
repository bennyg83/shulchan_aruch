11) There: Even if the objects belong to the non-Jew, etc. If one sold merchandise to a non-Jew but it remains in the Jew's house, and the non-Jew comes to take it on Shabbos, one should be concerned and, when possible, be stringent not to hand it to him because of appearances. Magen Shaul, siman 57. See there, where he cites the responsum of his brother-in-law Rabbi Yosef ben Chason, who forbids it, particularly according to the view that a non-Jew acquires by pulling the item, since according to that view the sale is completed on Shabbos; see there.
