Magen Avraham s.k. 34 — "some say" requires b'chedi she'ya'asu. The language is not precise, for here with flutes it is obvious that all require b'chedi she'ya'asu regarding techumim, as explained in the sugya; and because a corpse is more stringent — Tosafos and Rosh chapter 3 of Beitzah, see there.
