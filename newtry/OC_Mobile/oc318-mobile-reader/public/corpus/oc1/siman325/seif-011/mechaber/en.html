A non-Jew gathered grasses for his animal's need — if he does not recognize him, another Jew standing before the animal feeds it in a manner that it cannot bend except in that direction; for if to stand it upon them it is forbidden, for we are concerned lest he take by hand and eat them, and they are muktzeh. But if he recognizes him — it is forbidden; and likewise in every matter where there is concern lest he increase on his behalf. But in a matter where there is no concern lest he increase on his behalf — such as that he lit a lamp for himself or made a ramp to descend by it — with one lamp and one ramp it suffices for everyone; even if he recognizes him, it is permitted.
