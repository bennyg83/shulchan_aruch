Immediately. In Tosafot Bava Kamma in Beit Va'ad siman 26. He asked: since Beit Yosef concludes not like Semag because it is an individual opinion against Ran and Rabbenu Tam, why did he rule that it is permitted in the evening immediately? And furthermore one should wonder why in Sha'ar HaTevah regarding flutes he did not bring Semag's view at all — for certainly here one should be more stringent, for a d'oraisa prohibition touched it, as Rashba wrote; and see siman 492 s.k. 3.
