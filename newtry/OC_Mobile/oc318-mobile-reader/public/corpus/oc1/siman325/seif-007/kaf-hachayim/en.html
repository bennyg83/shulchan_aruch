G) There — to eat them. Not precise; rather even to give stam is permitted — Olat Shabbat responsum 2; but explicitly to take out — forbidden. Beit Yosef; and accordingly if he had no permission for the gentile to eat them in the courtyard, or he gave him much sustenance that he cannot eat — forbidden to give, for it is as if he specified to take them out.
