And some say for evening one may use immediately — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote in the name of Chayei Adam that one should not rely on this except for mitzvah need, and the same in a place of pressing need; and nevertheless it appears that in stam fruit with a gentile, even if they were not brought as a gift to a Jew — one should examine this whether to be lenient, for the view of those who are lenient here is Rashi's view, who ruled like Shmuel leniently, as Beit Yosef and the Gra wrote; and Ran wrote there, in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah that what they forbade in the gemara "if its kind is attached" — everything attached is as certainly gathered on Shabbat, for most are accustomed to gather them on their day; except Beit Yosef in siman 495 wrote that Semag seems to argue on Rabbeinu Yonah; and see Magen Avraham there what he answered that the gemara's "attached" is not difficult for Semag, since a gift was brought — that is different; but for halacha, even if we say Semag is lenient also here, nevertheless it suffices for us if we are lenient here according to Rashi's view in a complete doubt, such as when it is somewhat evident on the fruit itself that they were gathered from yesterday; but in stam fruit, according to Ran and Rabbeinu Yonah it is as certain — it is impossible to be lenient, for the Mechaber seems to have ruled otherwise like the first view that forbids even in complete doubt:
