There is — Taz deals with a city whose majority are gentiles and bakery bread is permitted; nevertheless some forbid on account of muktzeh or nolad, or lest he tell the gentile to do in food matters — they decreed more strictly; and it requires study regarding filling water for his animal — Magen Avraham responsum 9; and see there will be explained. And regarding siman 497 in Magen Avraham 17: "not so with a gentile's" means muktzeh from intent; but muktzeh like this is forbidden even with a gentile's, like attached and trapping that are forbidden even when a gentile traps for himself, as is written in seif 5; and there are those who permit — they distinguish between trapping and plucking versus here — Taz responsum 10 — see there; and Magen Avraham 517:2 — there the Mechaber ruled stam regarding flour a gentile baked, even if ground today — permitted on Yom Tov; if so, all the more on Shabbat; why was he stringent here without pressing need — therefore it appears the Mechaber holds the reason to forbid is lest he tell him; and in a shop, etc. (for otherwise he makes to sell) — if so, bread of a homeowner is otherwise forbidden to a Jew. And what the Mechaber wrote "for the need of hamotzi" — I do not understand his explanation, for on Shabbat one is obligated to eat an olive-size of bread even if he has other things — siman 288 Magen Avraham 9; and if so, what does hamotzi apply here; and it is possible on account of lechem mishneh, so too Friday night and morning require lechem mishneh — it is also considered pressing need; and what he wrote "most buyers are gentiles" — so too in 517:3: we follow most buyers; and as is written in Yoreh De'ah we follow most buyers — see there; and in Eliyah Rabbah there:
