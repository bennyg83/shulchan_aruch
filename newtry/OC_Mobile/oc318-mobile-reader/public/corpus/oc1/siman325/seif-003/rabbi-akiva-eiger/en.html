Magen Avraham s.k. 13 — for if not so, a non-Jew who brought a gift. That is, he says it deals where they know he gathered for a Jew; and even though nevertheless they do not know he gathered today, as he says — if there is in its species while attached, one must say that for this purpose the gift indeed helps, that presumably he gathered today, only that they need know he gathered for a Jew; but without a gift, even though they know he gathered for a Jew, nevertheless in doubt whether he gathered today — permitted according to those poskim who in doubt do not require b'chedi she'ya'asu, as stated explicitly in seif 7. And therefore Magen Avraham's words are puzzling, who wrote that if not so the gift is unnecessary, etc. — for even though they know he gathered for Israel, nevertheless without a gift why need b'chedi she'ya'asu, since they are only in doubt whether he gathered today? Requires further study.
