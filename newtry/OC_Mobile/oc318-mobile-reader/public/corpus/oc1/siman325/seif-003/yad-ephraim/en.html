S.k. 6 — "and it is permitted to say," etc. — requires further study, for behold he wrote afterward "even if it is known that he will take out," etc.; and if it deals explicitly that he will take this out — even if it is known, it is only permitted. And one may answer that one can say: even if it is known that this non-Jew will fulfill the Jew's words and take out — requires further study.
