And good. Agudah wrote that the same applies if the gentile leaves money and takes the pledge — it is also permitted, provided he does not think about him. He wrote there that he commanded them to fast the next day; and Rama did not concern himself with this since Mahariv is entirely lenient. Sm"a wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 131 seif 10 that if a gentile comes to take his pledge from a Jew on Shabbat — it is permitted for a Jew to be a guarantor, and especially during twilight, etc. — see there.
