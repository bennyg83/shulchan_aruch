It is permitted to exchange collateral on Shabbat if it is a garment and he wears it out as a garment, for this is not commerce; and similarly for a Jew it is permitted in this matter if the Jew needs to wear it.And it is good that the non-Jew take the collateral himself and place another in its place, and the Jew not touch it, so it does not appear as commerce (Agudah); and see above siman 306 at its end regarding the law of a non-Jew who brings something on Shabbat whether it is permitted to accept it.
