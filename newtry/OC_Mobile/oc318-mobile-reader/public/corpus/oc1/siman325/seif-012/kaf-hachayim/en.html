L) One who has in the customs house a document that he paid customs, and on Shabbat he must take it and give it into a gentile's hand — whether it is permitted to go to customs to take the document and hand it to the gentile on Shabbat. See in responsum of Lev Chaim part 3 siman 109 who wrote that if the gentile did not write it on erev Shabbat and comes to write on Shabbat for the Jew — obviously forbidden; but even if he already wrote from erev Shabbat — also forbidden — see there.
