12) If a person has a document at the customs house stating that the duty was paid, and on Shabbos he is compelled to retrieve it and hand it to a non-Jew, may he go to the customs house, take the document, and give it to the non-Jew on Shabbos? See Responsa Lev Chaim, volume 3, siman 109, which writes that if the non-Jew did not prepare it before Shabbos and comes to write it on Shabbos for the Jew, it is plainly forbidden. Even if it was already written before Shabbos, however, it is still forbidden; see there. Orach Chaim, paragraph 1.
