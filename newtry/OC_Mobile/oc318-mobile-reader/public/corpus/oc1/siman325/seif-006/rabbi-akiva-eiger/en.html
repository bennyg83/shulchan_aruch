Magen Avraham s.k. 24 — such as where there is danger on the roads — forbidden. And it appears to me that if the non-Jew brought via reshut ha-rabim and it was also outside the techum, even according to those who hold in seif 8 that on account of techumim it does not require b'chedi she'ya'asu, nevertheless here it requires b'chedi she'ya'asu, because the non-Jew performed d'oraisa melacha on behalf of Israel in that he brought via reshut ha-rabim — for the Jew could not have gone there to drink, since he is outside the techum. See Ran chapter 3 of Sukkah daf 250b who wrote similarly. Requires further study.
