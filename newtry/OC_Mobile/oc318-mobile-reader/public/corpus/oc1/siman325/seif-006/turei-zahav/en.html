For night does not count, etc. In Mordechai chapter Kol Kitvei he gave the reason: because it is not customary to bring at night from a distant place, etc.; and accordingly this stringency applies specifically to one who brings from outside the techum; not so with trapping fish and gathering fruit in a nearby place — he can well bring at night; therefore Rama wrote here and not above in seif 7.
