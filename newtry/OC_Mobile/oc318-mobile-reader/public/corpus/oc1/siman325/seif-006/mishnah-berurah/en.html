(א) If he gathered and trapped — and the same law in all d'oraisa melachos that a non-Jew did on behalf of a Jew, and even if he did from himself without command of the Jew, as below in siman 515, see there in Beit Yosef:
(ב) On behalf of a Jew and a non-Jew — such as that it was half Jew and half non-Jew, or it is known that he also trapped and gathered for a Jew, even though the majority were non-Jews [from below siman 515 seif 6 in the gloss]; and see what is written above in siman 276 in Biur Halacha:
(ג) He must wait, etc. — and this is even another Jew for whom they were not gathered on Shabbat, since he did a d'oraisa melacha on behalf of a Jew — forbidden for everyone to benefit until Motzei Shabbat bichdei sheya'asu [and on Shabbat itself moving is also forbidden, as in siman 515]; and it is not specifically trapping, for the same law in all melachos, as likewise above, for the reason they forbade for everyone until bichdei sheya'asu is so they not benefit from melacha done on behalf of a Jew on Shabbat; and also they decreed that when it will be permitted immediately at Motzei Shabbat they will say to the non-Jew on Shabbat to prepare that it be ready at Motzei Shabbat immediately:
(ד) Bichdei sheya'asu — explained below siman 515 that this is the measure that the non-Jew at Motzei Shabbat go to the place he gathered and gather others there and return here; and if it is not known from where he brought them — their measure is bichdei sheyavi from outside the techum; and if initially on Shabbat he brought them on a horse — we estimate also by way of riding [Magen Avraham in name of Knesset HaGedolah]; and regarding whether a non-Jew is believed with masiach lefi tumo that they were gathered from erev — see what is written there and in siman 513:
