[14] For the amount of time required for it to be done, etc. Everything depends on the manner in which it was done. If it came by caravan and the journey took one day, one must wait one day. If the non-Jew rode a horse and the journey took one hour, one waits one hour. If the place is three days distant, there is no need to wait on Sunday for a period corresponding to Shabbos when the prohibited act was done, as stated by Knesset HaGedolah. This refers to daytime and not nighttime, because on Friday night he certainly did not ride, as stated by the Magen Avraham. This requires study.
