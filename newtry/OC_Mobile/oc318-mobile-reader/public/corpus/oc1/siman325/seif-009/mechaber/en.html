If there is doubt whether it was brought from outside the techum — it is forbidden; and specifically regarding a non-Jew who does not dwell with him in the city. But a non-Jew who dwells with him in the city, and fruits found in the city — one need not be concerned for doubt; and even if the non-Jew has two houses and one of them is within the techum, we rely to be lenient and it is permitted to eat even for the one for whom it was brought.
