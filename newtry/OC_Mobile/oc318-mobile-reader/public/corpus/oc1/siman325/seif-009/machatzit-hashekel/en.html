(ב) Regarding food they decree more, etc. — for food, which one is more eager for, there is reason to forbid; so is Mordekhai's language in ch. 3 of Eruvin in name of Ri of Paris:

(ג) And he said lest they were detached, etc., and they are muktzeh; and there is benefit of muktzeh wood in bread, as stated in Pesachim daf 26b; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 142 that if he baked bread with orlah wood and stubble of kilayim of the vineyard that are forbidden from benefit — and muktzeh is permitted in hanaah, meaning we do not say there is benefit of wood in bread and it is as eating the prohibition, rather as benefiting from the prohibition; therefore specifically when baked beside wood forbidden in hanaah the bread is forbidden; but muktzeh permitted in hanaah — why on account of benefit of wood in bread; and in Pesachim thus it implies forbidden to cook with muktzeh on Yom Tov — later authorities understood Magen Avraham's intent is to prove not like Tosafos who disagree in Pesachim between Rabbah and R' Chiya — one who cooks on Yom Tov for weekday: R' Chiya said he is lashed and Rabbah said he is not lashed, for we say since if guests happen upon him they see this bread — even though now there are no guests, nevertheless he is not lashed; and they challenged him from what was taught: one who cooks gid ha-nasheh in milk on Yom Tov and eats it — lashed five; and R' Chiya taught liable two for eating and three for cooking — two for eating gid and basar be-chalav, three for cooking — one for burning, two for cooking on Yom Tov not for need, three for cooking basar be-chalav even on a weekday he is lashed for it; it is impossible we say "since" on burning he should not be liable, for behold it is fit for his need; and they answer: remove burning and bring muktzeh wood — meaning he cooked with muktzeh wood; and they challenge: is muktzeh d'oraisa; and they answer: yes, we hold muktzeh is d'oraisa, end quote of the gemara:

(ד) And later authorities understood Magen Avraham holds one is lashed for cooking with muktzeh wood on account of benefit of wood in bread; and wrote on this in Sefer Teshuvah, thus: and his words are astounding, for there we do not say thus except according to one who holds muktzeh is d'oraisa, etc., that every thing requires preparation; and since it requires preparation it is called melacha and forbidden on account of a Torah melacha, etc.; but for us who hold muktzeh is d'rabbanan — certainly permitted to benefit from muktzeh wood; and the braita is complete — a gentile gathered grasses for his animal's need, an Israelite feeds after him, as stated in seif 11, end quote; I did not understand his words, for he distinguishes whether muktzeh is forbidden from the Torah or d'rabbanan regarding issurei hanaah; and it appears to me I do not know to distinguish, for all that is forbidden according to one who holds muktzeh is d'oraisa is also forbidden according to one who holds d'rabbanan; and what he wrote "and the braita is complete that muktzeh is permitted in hanaah" — this difficulty is according to what he understood Magen Avraham's intent to prove muktzeh is forbidden in hanaah:

(ה) But in truth even according to his understanding of Magen Avraham's words — certainly Magen Avraham concedes to Tosafos that muktzeh is permitted in hanaah; only here he disagrees with Tosafos — Tosafos hold that which we say benefit of wood in bread — it is only benefiting from the prohibition; therefore with muktzeh there is no reason to forbid on account of benefit of wood in bread; and Magen Avraham proved according to his view there is no distinction between d'oraisa and d'rabbanan regarding issurei hanaah; and even according to one who holds muktzeh is d'oraisa — nevertheless permitted in hanaah, as proven from this braita itself that he brought in Sefer Teshuvah; and therefore one who is lashed there for cooking with muktzeh wood — the reason is benefit of wood in bread, as eating the prohibition; and if so, likewise if muktzeh is d'rabbanan — forbidden on account of benefit of wood in bread d'rabbanan:

(ו) Also in Sefer Chemed Moshe I saw he wrote on Magen Avraham's above words that he was not precise and wrote: how did he say according to what we conclude there, bring muktzeh wood — lashed on account of benefit of wood in bread — behold Rambam ruled one is not lashed if he benefits from issurei hanaah (and see in Mishneh LaMelech in Hilchot Yesodei HaTorah); and to reject Sefer Teshuvah's words — this too is rejected, for on the contrary from this Magen Avraham proved that necessarily what we say benefit of wood in bread is as eating the prohibition — for on hanaah one is not lashed; however he further challenged in Sefer Chemed Moshe: how did he say lashed on account of benefit of wood in bread — if so how did R' Chiya teach two for eating and three for cooking — now it should be the reverse, three for eating — one gid, two basar be-chalav, three Shabbat with muktzeh wood in the dish — and two for cooking, cooking on Yom Tov and cooking basar be-chalav; and similarly the gemara itself challenged on another version, see there; therefore in any case he is not lashed on account of benefit of wood but on account of moving muktzeh per the conclusion that muktzeh is d'oraisa; and he wrote thus is Rashi's language, end quote; and in truth this answer needs inward study; however it appears to me I will add a challenge, for they only sought to reject Magen Avraham's proof; and it appears to me on the contrary proven from there not like Magen Avraham that there is no prohibition to cook with muktzeh wood on account of benefit of wood — for otherwise who compelled the gemara to say remove burning, etc., and it needed to say it holds muktzeh is d'oraisa — it should have said muktzeh is d'rabbanan and not say remove burning but on the contrary liable on account of burning; rather it deals he cooked with muktzeh wood — and now why not liable him on burning, since behold it is fit for his need, for since the wood itself is forbidden to cook with — his need is muktzeh d'rabbanan in any case forbidden to cook with, and it is burning not for need, also according to what they answer remove, etc., and hold muktzeh is d'oraisa — lashed six, three for eating and three for cooking on Yom Tov, basar be-chalav, and cooking with muktzeh wood — meaning benefiting or eating or moving muktzeh wood that is d'oraisa; nevertheless permitted to cook with muktzeh wood; and if so one should not say he is not liable on burning whether muktzeh is d'oraisa or d'rabbanan, since it is fit for his need; therefore it appears what Magen Avraham wrote in Pesachim, etc., that forbidden to cook — the word "forbidden" is a scribal error and must read permitted to cook; and he added according to Tosafos — from Tosafos' words it is not proven except when he already cooked in it it is permitted, for they wrote their words on bread the gentile already baked; but we have not heard permitted l'chatchila on Yom Tov to cook with muktzeh wood when there is no need to move and burn them in place; and so Magen Avraham in Pesachim proves permitted to cook, etc. — meaning even l'chatchila; and this is his above proof, for otherwise liable on burning, for not fit for his need, for in any case l'chatchila forbidden to cook with it, as stated;
