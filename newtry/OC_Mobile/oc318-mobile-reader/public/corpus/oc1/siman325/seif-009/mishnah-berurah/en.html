(א) If it is doubt, etc. — and even though safeka d'rabbanan l'kula — forbidden in this because it is davar she'yesh lo matirin that it is possible to wait until evening, as above [Ramban in Milchamot chapter 22 and Rambam, and so Acharonim cited]:

(ב) Forbidden — stam in this like the view of the Geonim who forbid in doubt, and likewise stam in seif 15 [and not like the view of those who permit brought in Beit Yosef, which is the view of the "some say" in seif 7]; and behold even according to them, even at Motzei Shabbat it is forbidden in this bichdei sheya'asu — nevertheless Acharonim agreed that regarding Motzei Shabbat one need not be stringent in this, for even certainly outside the techum there are those who say one need not wait bichdei sheya'asu, as above in s.k. 40; therefore in doubt at least one may rely on those who permit:

(ג) One need not be concerned from doubt — that these fruits the non-Jew brought outside the techum; on the contrary, we say here "found here, were here":

(ד) And even, etc. — and even though in this there is room for doubt that he brought from the house he has outside the techum — nevertheless we depend to be lenient that until here we did not forbid from doubt except regarding a non-Jew who has no residence at all within the techum we are concerned lest perhaps he brought them outside the techum on Shabbat; but in this, that he has a house within the techum — we do not doubt at all lest perhaps he brought them from outside, for we say "found here, were here," that is, in the house within the techum:
