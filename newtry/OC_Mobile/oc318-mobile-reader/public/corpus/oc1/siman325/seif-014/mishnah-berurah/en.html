(א) Forever — and even though in all melachos done on behalf of a Jew it is not forbidden except bichdei sheya'asu — here, since it is a public matter, as below, the Sages were more stringent in it; and the same law in all public matters done on behalf of a Jew — forbidden to him forever [Magen Avraham in name of Rambam]; but from Taz's words in name of Ran it is implied that specifically here the Sages forbade forever, because it is a disgrace to a Jew that he bury in a famous grave in which Shabbat was desecrated on his behalf; but in other melachos in every manner it is not forbidden except bichdei sheya'asu; and see end of siman 364 and Ran's words; I found likewise in several Rishonim; therefore one need not be stringent at a time of need:
(ב) And this is when he waits, etc. — and even though for others the decree lest they say to the non-Jew on Shabbat to do so it be ready at Motzei Shabbat does not apply — nevertheless they did not distinguish in a matter in which complete melacha was done on behalf of a Jew. And know that in all melachos done on behalf of a Jew that we say he must wait bichdei sheya'asu — even if he told the non-Jew to do for him, even though he transgressed a prohibition in this — nevertheless he need not wait at Motzei Shabbat except until bichdei sheya'asu, on account that he not benefit from melacha of Shabbat and no more [Magen Avraham]:
