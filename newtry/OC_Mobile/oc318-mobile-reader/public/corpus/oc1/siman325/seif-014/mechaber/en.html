If a non-Jew made on Shabbat a coffin or grave for himself — it is permitted for a Jew to be buried in it; and if he made it on behalf of a Jew — he should not be buried in it permanently. And specifically when the grave is in public and the coffin is upon it, so that everyone knows it was made for so-and-so the Jew; but if it is in private, it is permitted to be buried in it in the evening the time it would take to do them. And even when it is in public, it is forbidden only to that Jew for whom it was made; but for another Jew it is permitted, provided he waits the time it would take to do them.
