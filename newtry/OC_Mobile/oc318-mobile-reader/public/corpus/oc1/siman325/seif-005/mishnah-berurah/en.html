(א) Who traps fish, etc. — and in this all agree it is forbidden, because it is certainly muktzeh, for they were trapped on Shabbat and were not prepared from yesterday [and all the more those who forbid also bread in s.k. 16]; and it is not specifically that a non-Jew trapped them, for the same law if they were trapped by themselves on Shabbat in a trap spread from erev:

(ב) Or he gathered fruits — also on account of muktzeh, for they were attached at twilight; and also because they decreed in this lest he go up and detach, as they decreed on fruits that fall on Shabbat, as above in siman 322 [because a person is eager after them, and also it is easy to detach them]; and the same law here that were detached by a non-Jew, for they are not inferior, that both were detached not through a Jew:

(ג) Forbidden to a Jew — whether in eating or in moving:

(ד) And even in doubt — for safek muchan is forbidden, and even those who permit below in s.k. 37 regarding muktzeh in this agree; and as explained below; and specifically in doubt it is forbidden, for we depend that perhaps on that day they were trapped and detached; but if it is recognizable they were detached from erev, such as fruits that withered, and likewise fish in such a case — permitted, as below in siman 515, see there in Beit Yosef:

(ה) Permitted at night immediately — for specifically when he did the melacha on behalf of a Jew they decreed to forbid at night until bichdei sheya'asu, as below in the next seif; but when he did for himself the decree does not apply [Acharonim]:
