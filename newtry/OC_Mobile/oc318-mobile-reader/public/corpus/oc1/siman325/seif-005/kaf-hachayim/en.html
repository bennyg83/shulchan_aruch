And it is permitted to place sustenance, etc. Ra'avad is stringent to forbid in any matter where he can evade it; and what is astonishing about inviting a gentile on Shabbat — we do not feed him, rather we show him and he takes for himself; or we feed for ourselves and he eats with us.
