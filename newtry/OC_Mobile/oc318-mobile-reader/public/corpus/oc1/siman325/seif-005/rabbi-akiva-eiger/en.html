In the Mechaber seif 10 — "it is permitted for a Jew to give his animal to drink." In the mishnah and braita it teaches "waters after him" an Israelite; and so Rambam; and Rashi wrote that it specifically teaches "after him" because it is recognizable that the non-Jew filled for his own need — and I do not know why Rashi's words were not brought to halacha.
