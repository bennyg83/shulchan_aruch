(א) To give sustenance. For its sustenance is considered upon you, as stated in siman 324 seif 11 that we sustain them on account of darkei shalom:

(ב) Before him. But not into his hand, for that is an uprooting (Shiltei Gibborim, Tosafos) and see siman 347 seif 14:

(ג) To eat them. But if he does not have permission in his hand to eat them in the courtyard, or they are many such that he cannot eat them there — forbidden to place before him (Shiltei Gibborim in Mordekhai ch. 1):

(ד) The objects of a non-Jew. In Tur he wrote "or even if the objects are of a non-Jew," and it appears to me this refers to when he stands outside and stretches his hand inside — forbidden; and this is what he means "that one who sees that he gave to him does not know the objects are of a non-Jew" — meaning since he saw an Israelite give to him, he supposes they are of an Israelite; but if the object is of a non-Jew, permitted to give before him in the courtyard, for one who sees him take it out supposes the object is of a non-Jew; and so in Mordekhai — only when he designated a place for him do some be stringent, but when it is of a non-Jew and not pledged at all to an Israelite — all permit; and so in Rambam ch. 6 who wrote: when the non-Jew left his house on Shabbat and an Israelite's object is in his hand, it appears as though he lent it to the non-Jew on Shabbat, end of his words; and see siman 347 seif 21; and so in Rosh and in the hints at the beginning of Shabbat:

(ה) He designated a place for him. Some say rental does not acquire [and see in Hagahot Maharam Padua and see siman 246]:
