(Magen Avraham s.k. 24.) And his proof from Rosh — NB. And it appears that Rabbenu Tam answers the difficulty from the candle, for the case under discussion is not comparable to the proof: for here, since he climbs and descends, he can drink water from that pit that the non-Jew drew on his behalf; unlike where it is said that since he can see by one candle it is permitted to use the light of this candle — for in such a case Rabbenu Tam also concedes it is forbidden; and now the proof of Tur is removed.
