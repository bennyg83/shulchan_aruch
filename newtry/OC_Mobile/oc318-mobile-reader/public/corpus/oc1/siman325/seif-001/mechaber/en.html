A non-Jew who performed melacha on behalf of a Jew. And in it 16 seifim: It
is permitted to invite a non-Jew on Shabbat and it is permitted to place food before him in the courtyard for him to eat; and if he took it and went out, we do not attend to him. And specifically when the non-Jew is in the courtyard; but if he stands outside and extends his hand inside — since it is known that he will take it out, or to give him other objects that one is apt to take out — it is forbidden, even if he stands inside, even if the objects belong to the non-Jew, since the observer does not know that the objects belong to the non-Jew.And even if one designated a place for him from the day before, one should be stringent (Mordechai chapter 1 of Shabbos).
