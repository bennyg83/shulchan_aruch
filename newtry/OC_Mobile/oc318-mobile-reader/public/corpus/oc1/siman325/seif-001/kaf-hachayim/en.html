A) [Seif 1] It is permitted to invite a gentile on Shabbat. And even though it is astonishing (in siman 512) that on Yom Tov it is forbidden to invite the gentile — that is specifically on Yom Tov where they decreed lest he increase on his account; but on Shabbat, where there is no "lest he increase on his account," since everything is cooked from erev Shabbat — therefore it is permitted to invite him to eat.
