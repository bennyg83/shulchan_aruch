(א) Seif 1 "to eat them." Yerushalmi: one does not give food to a non-Jew to go out; and likewise in the gemara that states "he took them and went out," etc.

(ב) "And specifically." Since it states "in the courtyard." Tosafos; and likewise from the Yerushalmi that whenever it is known that it is on condition to go out — it is forbidden

(ג) "Or to give." Since it states "food," and therefore Beit Hillel permit only when it is known, etc., that it is his usual way, etc. — from the aforesaid Yerushalmi, as stated, when it is known, etc., that his way is, etc.

(ד) "And even if." To exclude Tosafos who explain the mishnah of "one who asks on Shabbat" in such a case; rather as Rosh and Ran state there

(ה) "And even seated together." For some permit, as stated in Pesachim 6a, and he rejected that we say only regarding chametz, as stated to say, etc., this case is different, etc., and as stated explicitly in the first chapter of Avodah Zarah that rental is not acquired; and hiring on Shabbat is forbidden — hiring on erev Shabbat when work is done at length
