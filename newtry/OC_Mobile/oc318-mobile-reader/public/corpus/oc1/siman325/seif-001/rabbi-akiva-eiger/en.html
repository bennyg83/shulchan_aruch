Magen Avraham s.k. 9 — and muktzeh is permitted for benefit, end of his words. See in Peri Megadim Yoreh De'ah siman 99 Taz s.k. 12. And see in Rashba's comment Shabbos 29a who wrote that the essence is that muktzeh is permitted for benefit and they only forbade moving it or eating it; and even to use it with one's hands, such as lighting or leaning the legs of the bed on it — even in its place where he does not move it from its place — forbidden, end of his words. And therefore certainly forbidden to sit on a stone; for it appears to me there is no difference between leaning the legs of the bed or leaning oneself on it. See Magen Avraham siman 308 s.k. 40; and requires further study.
