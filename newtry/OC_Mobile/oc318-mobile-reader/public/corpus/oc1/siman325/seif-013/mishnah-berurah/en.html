(א) He need not protest his hand — from gathering and feeding his animal, since he does not do this at the command of a Jew but from himself; and we say he does not intend for the Jew's benefit but for his own intent he does in order to profit; and therefore he need not protest him, as below in siman 334 s.k. 25 regarding fire that he need not protest a non-Jew when he comes on his own to extinguish, unlike in seif 11 where the Jew himself feeds — forbidden, since he benefits by hand; and the same law when the non-Jew feeds at his command — also forbidden, even if he gathered from himself initially:
