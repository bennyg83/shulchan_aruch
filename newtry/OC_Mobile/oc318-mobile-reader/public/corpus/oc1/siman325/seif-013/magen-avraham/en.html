(א) One need not protest. As stated siman 334 s.k. 20; and even though regarding a burning lamp if most are Israelite it is forbidden even if he did not say he did it for an Israelite — there it is different because he himself benefits from the prohibition; but regarding his animal — permitted, end quote Mordekhai and Sefer HaTerumah:

(ב) Accustomed to this. Thus Rosh: but if an Israelite schemes thus and is accustomed to do his matters through scheming via a non-Jew — forbidden, as we say in chapter HaPoalim, end of his words — it implies if the Israelite does not scheme, even though the non-Jew schemes — permitted; and further it implies that even if we know the non-Jew does for an Israelite's need one need not protest, as stated siman 276 s.k. 14; but Mordekhai wrote, thus: provided he does not scheme to do for an Israelite and say he does for himself, end quote — it implies if we know he does for an Israelite one must protest; and so from Shulchan Aruch's words; and the reason — since the Israelite benefits from this melacha; and see siman 292 seif 2:
