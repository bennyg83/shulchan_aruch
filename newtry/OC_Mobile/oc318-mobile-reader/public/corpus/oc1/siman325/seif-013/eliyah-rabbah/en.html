[13] [Levush] And there are those who permit, etc. Requires study why he ruled stam in seif 14 to be stringent; and it is possible that regarding a dead person he was stricter (Magen Avraham); and accordingly it is difficult in the gemara [Shabbos] daf 151: what proof do they bring from the mishnah of the bathhouse to a dead person — see Tosafos there — requires study; and one must say the gemara makes no distinction, only Beit Yosef from himself is stringent regarding a dead person — requires study; however the main difficulty appears to me that one may say it deals in seif 14 when it is known it was done for a Jew even in private; and it appears from Acharonim one may rely on those who permit to be lenient; see siman 495 s.k. 9.
