16) [Seif 2] Where peaceful relations are involved, etc. Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 6, writes this in the name of Or Zarua. It explains there that the permission for peaceful relations is based on the position that today we do not have a full public domain, following the view later in siman 345, seif 7. According to the view there that a public domain exists even today, the act should be forbidden even for peaceful relations. Since the author states the ruling here without qualification, however, it implies that the law applies according to all opinions. It is possible that he holds that peaceful relations permit the act even in a full public domain. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 7.
