Doubt — Taz; and see there responsum 7 on majority and nearby — these conditions apply here too; and Eliyah Rabbah there: one may say here "found here and were here"; and even if he has much outside the techum — he is lenient there; see Chiddushei HaRamban; and what Taz wrote that these conditions apply here too — from honor of the dead they were more concerned and forbidden even in a doubt; for this he said it is not so, for there they learned from here. And for halacha: a dead person and other matters are equal regarding techumim — one for whom brought for a Jew, this is permitted for another without "all the more"; and flutes in stam, etc. — behold brought for one — permitted for another at twilight immediately; see there he brought the Rosh's words in Beitzah 24 regarding techumim rabbinic — one does not need "all the more"; he challenged from flutes and answered that everyone knows and the matter is publicized — therefore they were stringent; it seems nevertheless it is not forbidden forever; and this that the Mechaber omitted in stam seif 16 — this and Taz responsum 14:
