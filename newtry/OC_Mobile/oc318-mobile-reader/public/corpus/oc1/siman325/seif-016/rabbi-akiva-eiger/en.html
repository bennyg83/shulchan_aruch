Seif 16 — "lest they were brought from outside the techum." And they are forbidden until evening with b'chedi she'ya'asu — and specifically for that Israel for whom they were brought; but for another, even if certainly from outside the techum, it is permitted immediately, as in general regarding outside the techum that was brought on behalf of this Israel — permitted for another; for in this a corpse is not more stringent.
