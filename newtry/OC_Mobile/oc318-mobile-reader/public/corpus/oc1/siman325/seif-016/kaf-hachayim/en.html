P) [Seif 2] Where there is darchei shalom concern, etc. So too in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 6 in the name of Or Zarua; and there it is explained that because we permit on account of darchei shalom — for we do not now have a complete public domain, and he holds like one view below siman 345 seif 7; and if so, according to those below who say there is now a public domain — one should forbid even on account of darchei shalom; except since the Mechaber ruled stam here in Shulchan Aruch, it implies for all the law is thus; and it is possible he holds that on account of darchei shalom they permit even in a complete public domain.
