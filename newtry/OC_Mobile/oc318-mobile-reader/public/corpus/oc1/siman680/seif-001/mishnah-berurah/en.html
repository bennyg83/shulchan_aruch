On Shabbos night, etc. The same applies on a weekday: one must be careful not to place the lamps opposite a place where the wind has control. But on Shabbos extra guarding is needed in this.
Anything outside, etc. This refers to a place where they kindle the lamps opposite the entrance of the house from the inside. Also in a place where they kindle on the wall behind the door, one must be careful about this, as above in siman 277, section 1. But in a place where they kindle in the winter room, and there is a house in front of the winter room, there is no concern for extinguishing, for even if it is extinguished by his opening, this is not a pesik reisha, unless the opening of the winter room is exactly opposite the entrance of the house that is open to the courtyard or to the public domain. Then he should be careful to lock the house entrance before opening the winter room entrance. Bach.
Between the lamps and the entrance, etc. If he did not place one, it is forbidden to open the door. See above in siman 271 in Biur Halacha, beginning with in its usual manner.
