Levush: It is a pesik reisha, etc., and he does not want it, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote that it is not a pesik reisha at all, as the Tur and Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote. See siman 276. Bach ruled that if one placed it behind the door, he may open it gently; but on the door itself one should be stringent, and so ruled Hagahos Maimoniyos. This requires examination, for in siman 277 Bach permits it on the door, and it implies there that there is more reason to permit it on the door than behind the door. I wrote there that this is also implied in Hagahos Maimoniyos. It seems to me that he holds that merely as a stringency one should not kindle on the door, but in truth, when there is no place behind the door, or when he forgot to kindle, he may open it gently.
