What the Mechaber wrote, that on the door itself it is forbidden, refers to oil, where he brings it closer and distances it, and this is extinguishing and kindling; not to wax. See siman 277 and Levush here. It is not a base, for the door serves the house and is like something attached. There is one who permits even with oil; see Tur, who cites Rabbi Meir of Rothenburg, of blessed memory, in the name of his teacher, Rabbi Shmuel. One may ask: why did the Mechaber not bring in siman 276 that there is one who permits? One may answer with difficulty that regarding the Chanukah lamp, one may say it is a pesik reisha that he does not want, for he does not want the oil to burn faster. According to all views, the mitzvah of the Chanukah lamp is to burn for half an hour, and he places only a limited measure of oil. In the case of wicks and oils, etc., that may be lit on Chanukah, it is speaking of such a case according to the one who says they are permitted after the burning time, and like the case of burning oil of terumah, where there is a mitzvah to burn it, and it is mentioned in the Mishnah; even so one lights with it on Chanukah when he gives the exact measure. Also when he distances it and extinguishes, he does not want this regarding the Chanukah lamp. Therefore here there is one who permits. See Levush here.
