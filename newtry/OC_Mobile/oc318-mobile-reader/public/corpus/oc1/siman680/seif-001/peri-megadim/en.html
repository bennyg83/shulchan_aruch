What the Mechaber wrote regarding the door itself — forbidden means with oil that brings closer and distances and is extinguishing and kindling, not with wax; see siman 677 and Levush here that the door's base is not a base since the door serves the house and is as attached. Some are lenient even with oil — see Tur, Rabbi Meir of Rothenburg z"l in the name of his teacher R' Shmuel; one may say why did the Mechaber not bring in siman 677? Some permit; one may say with strain that for Hanukkah one may say psik reisha he does not want to kindle the oil quickly; for all Hanukkah lights one must kindle half an hour and puts oil sparingly — in the case of wicks and oils, etc., Hanukkah speaks for the view permitted after burning; like oil burning terumah one must burn and it was mentioned in Mishnah — even so they kindle Hanukkah giving sparse measure; also distancing and extinguishing he does not want on Hanukkah — therefore here some permit; see Levush here.
