That the candles should not go out — written in Hagahot Chadashim to Rif: even on weekdays if he kindled before wind and it went out he must return and kindle where wind is not common, since they cannot stand before wind it is as if he did not give the measure of oil — end; Or Zaru'a wrote similarly one should not wedge candles close to each other until they heat and fall — same reason.
