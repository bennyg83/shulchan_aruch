Kindling performs, etc. Rashi explained that it is similar to the Menorah; if so, this would not apply to a Shabbos lamp. Yam Shel Shlomo and Turei Zahav. This is not correct, for this matter that was taught about the Chanukah lamp, that kindling performs the mitzvah, is obvious from the fact that we bless to kindle. If so, the same applies to Shabbos and Yom Tov lamps, where we bless to kindle, as written in siman 263, section 5, and it is from the Yerushalmi. Mordechai in chapter Bameh Madlikin also ruled explicitly that on Shabbos too the kindling, etc. See siman 263, section 10, that also regarding the law of kindling from lamp to lamp, we hold that it is permitted on Shabbos.
