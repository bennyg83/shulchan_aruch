(א) The fuel does a mitzvah. Pirshi Domiya Damanura and Ak Lash Yes Benner of Shabbat and Asi' Rasg:

That the viewer says for his need. In the Gemara we challenge the view that kindling accomplishes the mitzvah from Rava: he held Hanukkah lights and did nothing — learn placement accomplishes the mitzvah; we answer there: the viewer says for his need — he relies on a baraita Rava said: he kindled inside and took out — he did nothing; unless kindling accomplishes the mitzvah in its place is required, for since this is its mitzvah it must be done in the place of obligation; if placement accomplishes the mitzvah, why did he do nothing? We answer there too: the viewer says for his need — he kindled; if so, it implies for the view kindling accomplishes the mitzvah the viewer reason is not needed, only because kindling must be in its place, as the questioner holds. Why did Tur and Shulchan Aruch write the viewer reason? It appears the Tur deduced Hagahot Maimoniyot's wording there: it also implies it is not so reasonable here as in Rava's first case — therefore the questioner: specifically in Rava's first case held in hand it is reasonable the viewer says for his need, for such is people's way to hold in hand when using anything; but kindling inside it is not so reasonable to say thus — therefore he labored to find for the view kindling accomplishes the mitzvah an answer: because the mitzvah must be outside where the Hanukkah light belongs by Talmud law; but for the view placement accomplishes the mitzvah it is difficult — we answer here too there is nevertheless room to err to the viewer, since he does not do the kindling in the place of obligation which is outside, and it is unnecessary labor, but certainly he has some need inside where he kindles; since so, even for the view kindling l'shem mitzvah the kindling-in-place reason is not needed — we also say this reason because of the viewer, for otherwise one must say he holds the viewer reason from Rava's first case holding Hanukkah lights — therefore our Rabbi mentioned only this viewer reason in both — this is obvious in my humble opinion.
