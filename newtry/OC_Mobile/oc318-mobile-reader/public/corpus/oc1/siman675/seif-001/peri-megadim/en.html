Kindling. See Turei Zahav. I did not understand this, for Rashi, of blessed memory, Shabbos 22b, beginning with if kindling, writes that it is as we find regarding the Menorah, with respect to kindling from lamp to lamp, not as we find there to say that kindling is the mitzvah and that the Chanukah lamp is a remembrance of the Mikdash, unlike a Shabbos lamp; this is not so. Also, from the fact that we bless to kindle a lamp, we learn that kindling is the mitzvah, and the same applies to a Shabbos lamp. See siman 263 in Magen Avraham, paragraph 23, and see Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, who noted this somewhat. This requires examination. See paragraph 2, where I wrote about this with Hashem's help.
