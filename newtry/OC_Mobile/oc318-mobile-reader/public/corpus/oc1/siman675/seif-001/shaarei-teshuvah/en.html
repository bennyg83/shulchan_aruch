Its placement. See Turei Zahav, who wrote from Rashi that it is like the Menorah, etc. Shevus Yaakov wondered about him, for Rashi said this regarding kindling from lamp to lamp, but on Shabbos too kindling performs the mitzvah, as we say in the Talmud from the fact that we bless to kindle, etc. See Mishbetzos, who asked this and wrote that Eliyah Rabbah already noted this as well. See Birkei Yosef, and see Baer Heitev. See Halachos Ketanos, siman 12, who wrote that one who moves a Chanukah lamp from its place within the lighting time perhaps has not fulfilled his obligation, for one who sees may say that from the beginning he kindled it for his own need. See there, siman 280: a Chanukah lamp that he placed on a loose and moving door, perhaps one who sees will say that he kindled it for his own need, since the door is not fixed. See there. This requires examination. It seems that if he goes back and kindles in a fixed place, he should not bless.
