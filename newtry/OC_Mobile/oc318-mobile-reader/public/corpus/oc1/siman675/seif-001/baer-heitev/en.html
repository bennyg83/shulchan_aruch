Its placement. He should leave it there for half an hour. Darkei Moshe. See Beis Yosef, who says that some hold that with synagogue lamps it is permitted to kindle them in one place and place them elsewhere. Nevertheless, one should be careful ab initio. Magen Avraham. And holds it. This means throughout the time of its mitzvah. But if he holds it in his hand for a short time and afterward places it down while it is burning, he has fulfilled. Turei Zahav; see there.
