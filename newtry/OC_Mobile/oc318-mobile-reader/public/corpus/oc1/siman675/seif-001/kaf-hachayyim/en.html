1) [Seif 1] The kindling constitutes the mitzvah. Rashi explained that this is analogous to the Menorah. According to this, the same principle would not apply to the Shabbos lamp. Taz, s.k. 1. This is incorrect, because the distinction regarding the Chanukah lamp, that the kindling constitutes the mitzvah, is evident from the fact that we recite the blessing, 'to kindle.' If so, the same applies to the Shabbos and Yom Tov lamps, over which we recite the blessing, 'to kindle,' as written in siman 263, seif 5. The Mordechai, in the chapter Bameh Madlikin, also ruled explicitly that on Shabbos the kindling constitutes the mitzvah. Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. So too wrote Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1, disputing the aforementioned words of the Taz, that on Shabbos as well the kindling constitutes the mitzvah; see there. So too wrote Birkei Yosef, os 1, and Mishna Berurah, os 1. See above in siman 263, in the gloss at the end of seif 10, and in our words there, os 70 and os 71.
2) Ibid. The kindling constitutes the mitzvah, etc. This is the wording of the Tur. Our teacher already wrote this law above in siman 673, seif 2, and repeated it here only for the law of a lantern. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah, os 1. One can further say that he comes to teach us also the case in which it had been placed, etc.
3) Ibid. The kindling constitutes the mitzvah, etc. For through the kindling, the publicizing of the miracle is recognizable. Levush.
