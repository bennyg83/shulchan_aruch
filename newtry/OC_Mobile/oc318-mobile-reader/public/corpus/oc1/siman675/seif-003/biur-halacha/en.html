(א) A woman who causes inflammation, etc. - refer to M.B. Dikhala to exclude even her husband, but it is well known that in Barakatos page 20 a son blesses his father and a woman blesses her husband, but the Sages said that a woman comes from a man whose wife and sons bless him. And what Daka Madaliki said to me in his house because he was not in his house at the time and the main responsibility rests on the house:

And for us, where each one, etc. — see Mishna Berurah; see below siman 677 in Magen Avraham s.k. 8 who brought Maharash Galante who holds that even for us, where each kindles a light, minors are exempt [for although obligated in chinuch, that is in a matter where the adult is obligated by law; but here for an adult too there is only one because of hidur mitzvah, the minor is not obligated in chinuch for it]. I later found in Meiri who also wrote thus: "Those who are mehaderin in the mitzvah — a light for each one in proportion to the number of adult household members" — end. What Machatzit HaShekel wrote there to reconcile Maharash Galante with the Rama is a great strain. In truth what Magen Avraham brought there — this is because the Mechaber wrote at first that per the Mechaber's view it speaks where he has a separate household, therefore he concludes "and it is possible even for us, etc.," and he is only obligated when he has a separate household.
