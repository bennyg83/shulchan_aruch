A woman kindles. Meaning, on behalf of all members of the household. A blind person, if he can participate with coins, that is better. If he has a wife, she kindles for him. If he has no wife and he has a separate house, he kindles with the assistance of another, for a blind person is obligated in all commandments. This is unlike the Havdalah lamp, from which he is exempt, for there we require that they benefit from its light, whereas here there is publicizing the miracle for others. Responsa Maharshal, siman 77.
