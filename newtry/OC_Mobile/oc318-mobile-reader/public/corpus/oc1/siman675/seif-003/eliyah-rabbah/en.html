[3] The viewer, etc. Bach questioned: in Shabbat 22a the reason is kindling must be in its place, and Rambam wrote so; he answered the Gemara teaches even for us who kindle inside, if he took them outside he has not fulfilled — the viewer, etc.; he questioned on this: in any case he should also write the reason kindling must be in its place, which is also halacha. It appears to me he follows his reason written earlier that there is a practical difference: for the reason kindling in its place — also on Shabbat and Yom Tov it is required; but for the viewer reason — not required for Shabbat and Yom Tov lights, for there is no pirsumei nisa — it fits; I wonder, for Mahariv in Pesach laws wrote the viewer reason is also required on Shabbat and Yom Tov. I saw in responsa Beit Yaakov siman 53 who wrote the practical difference mentioned in the name of Mordechai; he did not examine — Mordechai in chapter BeMa Madlikin did not write it at all, only a law of Shabbat and Yom Tov on the reason kindling in its place, because we hold kindling accomplishes the mitzvah, therefore the viewer difficulty is not needed — as our question. Truly the viewer reason is as Mahariv wrote. Now it fits in Tosafot d.h. de'iba'i, etc., why they did not write this practical difference; with this the Bach's first answer is also rejected — the Taz in his length followed the Tur that in conclusion the kindling-in-place reason is rejected even for the view kindling accomplishes the mitzvah, since there is no practical difference — why say the mitzvah is rejected; and further it is difficult for him, for there is practical difference — if so, why did Rama and Levush rule in siman 663 seif 10 that also on Shabbat and Yom Tov it must be in its place; and in any case Beit Yaakov's responsum is strained with many strains. It is also difficult on what he explained Rashi's view: perhaps for the view placement accomplishes the mitzvah he agrees kindling is also mitzvah, only placement is primary; for the view kindling accomplishes the mitzvah there is no mitzvah in placement at all; therefore the Gemara says for the view kindling, etc., therefore he did nothing — if so, why does the Gemara challenge Rava who said he held Hanukkah lights and did nothing — learn placement accomplishes the mitzvah; still kindling is also mitzvah — what does "he did nothing" mean? Also what he answers Tosafot's difficulty d.h. mechabeh, etc., on 23a — therefore it says kindling since it is also mitzvah — he did not examine, for Tosafot concluded: is a deaf-mute, imbecile, and minor worse than one who kindled? He means: even for deaf-mute, imbecile, and minor the viewer is not required to kindle — this is the practical difference as Tosafot wrote earlier. It should also be difficult why he explained afterward that the Tur rules for the view kindling accomplishes the mitzvah placement is also mitzvah — if so, what does the Gemara say except if kindling accomplishes the mitzvah it fits — still "he must place it" is difficult since placement is also mitzvah, and he himself explained so afterward — he contradicts himself.
