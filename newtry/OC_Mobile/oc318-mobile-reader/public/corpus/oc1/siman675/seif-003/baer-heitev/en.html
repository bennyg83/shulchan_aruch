A woman. She can discharge even a man, unlike Megillah, which is like Torah reading. Sefer Mitzvos Gadol. See Yad Aharon. One who already kindled may kindle for a woman and bless, such as where she stands near him at the time of the blessing, or where she kindles herself. Bach; see there. A blind person, if he can participate with coins, that is better. If he has a wife, his wife kindles for him. If he has no wife and he has a separate house, he kindles with the assistance of another, for a blind person is obligated in all the commandments. Responsa Maharshal, siman 76.
Also. See Responsa Shaar Ephraim, siman 42.
