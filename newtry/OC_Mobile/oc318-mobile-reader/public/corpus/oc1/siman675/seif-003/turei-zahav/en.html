For she too is obligated, etc. Since she was included in that miracle, for the Greeks decreed upon every virgin that she be violated by the governor first, and a woman, the daughter of Yochanan Kohen Gadol, killed the head of the enemies. It appears from the language of the Gemara that a woman discharges even a man, for we say: if a deaf-mute, mentally incompetent person, or minor kindled, he has done nothing; a woman certainly kindles, etc. Since regarding a deaf-mute, mentally incompetent person, or minor it says he has done nothing, meaning to discharge others, it follows that what it says a woman certainly kindles means to discharge others. So wrote Agudah, that if she knows how to bless, she can discharge the man. See siman 689 regarding Megillah.
