Until it went out? — he kindled in his hand and placed it afterward and fulfilled; for the questioner, placement accomplishes the mitzvah — certainly placing afterward fulfills; for Acharonim the essence of the mitzvah is specifically thus, as written in letter 2; for Rashi one could say not at all — he did not fulfill at all if he placed them only a short time even though he did not remain half an hour — b'dieved at least he fulfilled; for conclusion the viewer, etc., is specifically held and stood the entire time — if not fulfilled; the viewer, etc. — see letter 2: if he placed a short time, b'dieved at least he fulfilled; l'chatchila one could say even kindling must be after placement — Eliyah Rabbah. Peri Chadash: known Hanukkah lamps with eight outlets — the viewer, etc.; nevertheless l'chatchila he should appear to place and afterward kindle; b'dieved by holding in his hand the entire time he fulfilled.
