(Same, seif 3) And there are those who say a minor who reached chinuch may discharge, etc. NB: See Magen Avraham below siman 689 and in laws of Megillah — he questioned Shulchan Aruch's contradictory words: there he ruled plainly a minor never discharges in any way and did not bring the Orach Mishpat; here he brought the Orach Mishpat who holds a minor who reached chinuch discharges the adult. He wrote it is strained to distinguish between Hanukkah light and Megillah — in Ran it implies they are equal — see there. In my humble opinion it resolves correctly: regarding the reason one discharges another — there is a dispute if it is because of partnership or agency; in our novellae on Megillah siman 689 chapter 4 we questioned Tosafot's contradictory words: in Berachot they wrote in the evening prayer a minor does not discharge an adult because two rabbinic matters cannot discharge one rabbinic matter; we wrote: granted for partnership it fits, since they are not similar and their obligations are not equal, one cannot discharge the other; but for agency — if so it is as if he himself blesses — why does not two rabbinic discharge one rabbinic? Tosafot in Gittin hold agency applies to a minor in a matter that suits him; if so, since the minor is obligated in Birkat HaMazon, it suits him and he is considered the adult's agent — why is the minor not his agent? — see what is written there. However now it appears well according to Maharit in responsa who holds agency does not work for an oath because words are not entrusted to an agent — see there. True, elsewhere I wondered on him — one could say "words are not entrusted to an agent" means another becomes agent, but one can be agent even for words; nevertheless Maharit holds words are not entrusted to an agent and there is no agency; therefore Berachot fits: Birkat HaMazon is only words, agency does not apply, only partnership — therefore two rabbinic do not discharge one rabbinic; but where there is act, one can be agent — two rabbinic can discharge one rabbinic by agency. Therefore Hanukkah fits, for it is act and agency applies; but Megillah is words, agency does not apply — therefore two rabbinic do not discharge one rabbinic. Therefore Ran and such hold an agent can nullify another's chametz; but Ran brought other views nullification by agent does not help — they dispute Maharit's view if agency applies to words. Therefore Ran for his view that agency applies to words — truly Megillah equals Hanukkah; but for us legally, in Pesach laws he ruled like those who hold an agent can nullify; nevertheless in Megillah he ruled properly no minor discharges, lest we change — perhaps like those who hold even in Hanukkah, being act, two rabbinic do not discharge one rabbinic; and if you say like Orach Mishpat who holds like Hanukkah a minor discharges — the main reason is agency; if so, perhaps the law follows those who hold words cannot have an agent, and Megillah is only partnership, and certainly two rabbinic do not discharge one rabbinic. Therefore since there is room to be stringent, he did not heed the Orach Mishpat at all; but in Hanukkah, being act and agency applies, he brought the Orach Mishpat properly — one can say since it is by agency, two rabbinic can discharge one rabbinic; the reasoning fits as I wrote — correct in this — examine well.
