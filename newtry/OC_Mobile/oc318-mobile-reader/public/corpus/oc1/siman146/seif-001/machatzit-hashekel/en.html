Seif katan 1: It is forbidden, etc. The practical difference is on Shavuos. Meaning: although this law is stated in the Gemara in Berachos, in their times every verse was translated, so there was enough time to leave between one verse and the next while the verse was being translated, and the ruling teaches that this is forbidden. Nowadays, when we do not translate the verses, how is it possible to leave between one verse and the next, since there is no pause? Therefore he says that the practical difference is on Shavuos, when Akdamos is recited. In some places the kohen recites the blessing over the Torah, they read the first verse of the day’s obligatory reading with him, and afterward recite Akdamos. See below, siman 494, Taz. The ruling teaches that it is forbidden to leave during Akdamos, since the reading has already begun, even though this is between one verse and the next. Eliyah Rabbah permits leaving during Akdamos; see there. According to our custom, however, Akdamos is recited before the kohen begins the blessing, and the Sefer Torah is not opened at all, so leaving is permitted in any event.
