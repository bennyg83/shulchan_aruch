(1) It is forbidden to leave, etc. — and on this it is said "those who forsake the the Master shall be consumed," and we translate: "and those who abandon the Torah of the the Master shall be destroyed"; and even if there are ten who listen to the Torah scroll besides him, and he too already heard Keriat HaTorah with ten — nevertheless it is forbidden.
(2) But between one reader and the next — and this is when ten remained in the synagogue.
(3) It is permitted — and this is when he already heard Keriat HaTorah, or his intention is to come immediately; and this too is not permitted except when there is a great need.
