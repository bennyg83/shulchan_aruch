[1] It is forbidden to leave, etc. Even between one verse and the next. Tur. This likewise applies on Shavuos when Akdamos is recited. Magen Avraham. In my humble opinion, however, Levush implies that one may leave during Akdamos, since the Sefer Torah is rolled closed during Akdamos. By contrast, between one verse and the next, even in their times when the verses were translated, it appears that they did not roll it closed. If they had, why would the reader not be permitted to assist the translator? This is also implied by siman 139, seifim 4 and 5.
[2] But between one aliyah and the next. This is permitted only for a great need. Agudah, first chapter of Berachos. Seder HaYom writes that it is permitted only under great pressure or complete compulsion; otherwise, one should not leave. See above, Magen Avraham, siman 53, seif katan 22.
