The psalmist says, “The the Master’s Prayer is in heaven, and the the Master’s Prayer is in heaven.” In Heaven’s Word, Heaven’s Word says, “I am not a sinner, and I believe that the the Master of Israel is not Heaven’s people.” He said, “And I will not be able to do so in the end of the the Master’s Prayer, and I will not give it to you.” Now the reader and hearers such as Moses and all the people must stand. And then, in the midst of Heaven’s Word, Heaven’s Word, Heaven’s Word, and His Word is Heaven’s Word The reader of the people is not:

One who goes up to the Torah must bless the blessings also while standing — siman 8 of Magen Avraham (2): all blessings on mitzvot must be while standing except blessings over benefit; and it implies from Taz that they may sit before the one who blessed returns and says aloud "Blessed is Heaven, blessed forever and ever" — siman 139 seif 6; and this requires further study.
