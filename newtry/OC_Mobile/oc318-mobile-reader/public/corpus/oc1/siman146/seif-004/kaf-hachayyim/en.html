4) There: But between one man and another it is well. The Pri Chadash wrote, ot 1: it is reasonable that since the reading has been completed, even though he has not yet blessed 'asher nasan lanu,' he may leave. End of his language. But the Mateh Yehudah, ot 2, disagreed with him and wrote: since this blessing belongs to the reading, it is considered like it, and it is included in 'those who forsake Hashem shall be consumed'; and all the more so according to the one who holds that the blessing of the Torah before and after it is from the Torah. Therefore it appears that one should not leave between one man and another until he hears the blessing from the mouth of the oleh and answers Amen; see there. And so wrote the Maamar Mordechai, ot 2. And what the Erech HaShulchan wrote, ot 2, can be refuted.
