Seif 4: And some are stringent, etc. As it is stated, “And when he opened it, all the people stood,” etc. The first opinion maintains that “standing” means silence, as we say, “There is no standing except silence.” Likewise, the Yerushalmi forbids the reader and translator to sit, and so too the Gemara at the beginning of the third chapter of Megillah. This implies that according to everyone else it is permitted, and that is the primary ruling.
