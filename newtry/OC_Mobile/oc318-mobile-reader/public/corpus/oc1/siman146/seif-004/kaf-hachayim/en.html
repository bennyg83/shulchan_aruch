(4) There — but between one reader and another — Shulchan Aruch; Peri Chadash section 1 wrote: it is reasonable that since he finished reading, even though he has not yet blessed "Who gave us," he may leave — end of his words; but Matteh Yehuda section 2 disagreed and wrote: since this blessing pertains to reading it is considered with it and is included in "those who abandon the the Master will be consumed," and all the more per view that Torah blessings before and after are d'oraisa; therefore it appears one may not leave between readers until he hears the blessing from the oleh and answers Amen — see there; likewise Maamar Mordechai section 2; what Erech Lechem section 2 wrote can be rejected.
