One need not stand. Even those standing on the bimah need not stand, for one is required to stand before a Sefer Torah only while a person is carrying it, but not when it is resting in its place. See the beginning of siman 141 and the beginning of the chapter HaKorei Omed in Megillah, where specifically the reader must stand. This is also implied by Yoreh Deah 282:2, siman 244, and siman 242:18. The fact that the latter source needed to say that the Sefer Torah was in a separate domain, implying that those standing on the bimah must stand, refers to the time when it is lifted. So writes Beit Yosef there in the name of Rashba, and so write Tosafos, Menachos 33a. During Barchu and the response “Blessed is Hashem, Who is blessed forever and ever,” however, everyone must stand, for this is a davar shebikdushah. Masas Binyamin, end of the work; Responsa Menahem Azaryah, siman 91.
