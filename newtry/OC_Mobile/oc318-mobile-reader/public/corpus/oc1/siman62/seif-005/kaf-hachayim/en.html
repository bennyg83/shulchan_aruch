(5) [Se'if 3] One must make it heard, etc. — provided that he articulates with his lips. But through thought, when he contemplates in his heart, he does not fulfill. Tur and Beit Yosef. Bach. Yad Aharon in Hagahot Taz. Ateret Rosh, letter gimel. And he wrote — and this is not like Olat Tamid, who wrote in them that the author of the Maor, in the chapter "Hayah korei," and Riaz wrote that one fulfills through thought — see there, where he concludes that everyone holds that one does not fulfill. And likewise Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav letter alef, that the view of the Mechaber is that without duress, even b'dieved one does not fulfill through thought — see there. And likewise Razah, letter gimel.
