[3] Provided that he articulates, etc. This means that mere thought does not fulfill the obligation. Olas Tamid writes that Baal HaMaor in the chapter Hayah Korei and Riaz maintain that even through mental recitation he fulfills his obligation. He did not see Sefer Tamim Deim there, where Raavad challenges Baal HaMaor and rules that one does not fulfill the obligation. All the poskim I have seen rule likewise, and above I brought a proof that everyone agrees with this.
