Siman 63, 'one must,' etc. — there, the halakhah is like R. Yehudah who said, etc.; and 'the halakhah,' etc.; and there, the halakhah is as stated by, etc.

'Provided that,' etc. — there, chapter 2, R. Chananel, for he reconciled R. Eliezer and R. Yehoshua to his words; Tosafot there, in the entry 'veR. Chananel'; and likewise the passage in Shabbat 150a is like him; Rashi and Rosh.
