Language. And specifically when one understands the language; the same is true for prayer and Birkat HaMazon. But for Kiddush, Birkat HaP'ri, Birkat HaMitzvot, and Hallel, one fulfills [the obligation] even if one does not understand the language — Tosafot, beginning of chapter 7 of Sotah, folio 41a; and above, siman 193, se'if 1; and see in She'elat Ya'avetz.
