(2) There. If one read and was not precise with its letters — one fulfilled. Sefer HaChinuch wrote in parashat Va'etchanan: the matter is not that one must not mention the words and letters — for anyone who did not read the entire Keriat Shema certainly did not fulfill. Rather, the meaning of "was not precise" yet fulfilled is that one did not leave space between letters that adhere to each other, or did not aspirate the zayin of "tizkeru," or did not lengthen the dalet of "echad" — as is proper l'chatchila to do — end quote. Ateret Rosh there. See letter alef.
