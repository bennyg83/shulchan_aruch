Seif 2: He may, etc. The halachah does not follow Rabbi Yehudah against his colleagues, as stated in chapter four of Eruvin. Furthermore, the first Tanna there on 15a agrees with them, and the ruling there follows the first Tanna, as stated in the seif katan. Also, the anonymous Mishnah in Sotah agrees with them.
He should be careful, etc. As stated there, that your learning should be complete, etc. Beit Yosef.
