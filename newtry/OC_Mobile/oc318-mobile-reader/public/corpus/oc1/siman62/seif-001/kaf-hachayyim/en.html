1) [Seif 1] "Although it is a mitzvah to articulate its letters precisely, if he read without precise articulation he fulfilled his obligation." Nevertheless, one must take care to articulate them precisely. Rav Chama bar Chanina said that whoever reads Kriat Shema precisely in its letters has Gehinnom cooled for him. Tur. Bach explains that one is halachically obligated to be careful about this. If he is careless, he is punished with the heat of Gehinnom; if he is careful, Gehinnom is cooled for him. A great matter therefore depends upon careful articulation of its letters. I heard a mnemonic: from the letters of "Shema" and "Echad" remain the initials of Chananiah, Mishael, Azariah, and Daniel, alluding that one who recites Kriat Shema with proper intention is saved from fire as Chananiah, Mishael, Azariah, and Daniel were. Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1. See what we wrote above in siman 61, letters 1 and 56. 2) There, "If he read without precise articulation he fulfilled his obligation." Sefer HaChinuch, Parashat Vaetchanan, writes that this does not mean that he may omit words or letters, for one who did not read the entire Kriat Shema has not fulfilled his obligation. Rather, failure of precision means such matters as failing to leave sufficient space between adjacent sounds, failing to pronounce distinctly the zayin in tizkeru, or failing to extend the dalet of Echad for the proper amount of time, all of which should ideally be done. Eliyah Rabbah there; Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 1.
