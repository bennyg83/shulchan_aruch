Even though it is a mitzvah to be precise with its letters, if one read it and was not precise with them, one has fulfilled [the obligation]. Nevertheless, one must be very careful to be precise with them; and one who is precise with them — his reward is that they cool Gehinnom for him. This is measure-for-measure: since he arouses himself in this and restrains the warmth of his nature to be precise with the letters, in reward for this natural warmth that he arouses, they cool for him, against it, another heat — which is Gehinnom (Maharam of Italy).
