(1) "And was not precise" — the meaning is not that he did not mention the words and letters, for certainly he would not have fulfilled; rather, the meaning of "was not precise" is that he did not leave space between letters that adhere to each other, or did not aspirate the zayin of "tizkeru," and the like — and as above in siman 61 [Bach and Ateret Rosh in the name of Chinuch; and so is proven from Rashi there on the Mishnah, folio 15 — see there; and from the Gemara there 15b — see there].

(2) He fulfilled — nevertheless one must be very careful to do so optimally; and it is stated in the Talmud: one who is precise with them — his reward is that they cool Gehinnom for him. This is measure-for-measure: since he arouses himself in this and restrains the warmth of his nature to be precise with the letters, in reward for this natural warmth that he arouses, they cool for him, against it, another heat — which is in Gehinnom (Beit Yosef).
