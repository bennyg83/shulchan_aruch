And he was not precise. He wrote in Sefer HaChinuch, parashat Va'etchanan: the matter is not that one should not mention the words and letters — heaven forbid, for certainly one would not fulfill; rather, the matter of 'he was not precise with them' is that he did not give space between the words that adhere to each other; and likewise that he did not aspirate the zayin of 'tizkeru,' or that he did not lengthen the daled of 'echad'; and the like. And it is stated in the Talmud: all who are precise with them — their reward is that they cool Gehinnom for him; and see a hint for this in Magen Avraham.
