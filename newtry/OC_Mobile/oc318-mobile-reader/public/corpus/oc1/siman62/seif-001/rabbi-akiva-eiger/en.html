Se'if 4 — if due to illness. In Perishah he wrote that it is reasonable that if before the time for Keriat Shema passed the constraint passed, he must recite, for thought is not like speech. I have a question: if after the time for Keriat Shema passed, what difference does it make that we say he fulfilled? For even if he did not fulfill, what would he do? What difference is there for practical law whether they erred or not? It is strained to say the difference is for four matters in which Keriat Shema also has compensation — see above, siman 58. In this, since he fulfilled through thought at the time of constraint, he does not need compensation — strained.
