(1) [Se'if 1] Even though it is a mitzvah to be precise with its letters, if one read and was not precise with its letters, one fulfills. Nevertheless, one must be careful to be precise with them, for Rav Chama bar Chanina said: all who read Keriat Shema with its letters — they cool Gehinnom for him. Tur. And Bach explained that one is obligated by law to be careful to be precise with them, for if one is not careful one is punished with the heat of Gehinnom; but if one is careful to be precise with them, they cool Gehinnom for him. If so, a great matter depends on being precise with its letters; therefore one must be careful with them — end quote. And I heard a sign for the matter: 'shema' sounds like 'and one' of 'echad' — 'ash' remains, 'ma' from 'shema,' 'chad' from 'echad' — initials Chananiah, Mishael, Azariah, Daniel — to hint that one who reads Keriat Shema with kavanah is saved from fire like Chananiah, Mishael, Azariah, and Daniel. Ateret Rosh, letter alef; and see what is written above in siman 61, letter alef, and letter nun-vav.
