And he was not precise with them, etc. He wrote in Sefer HaChinuch, parashat Va'etchanan: the matter is not that one should not mention the words and letters — heaven forbid, for certainly one would not fulfill; rather, the matter of 'he was not precise' and fulfilled is that he did not give space between the words that adhere to each other; and likewise that he did not aspirate the zayin of 'tizkeru,' or that he did not lengthen the daled of 'echad' as is proper initially to do in all matters — end quote. And in answer to Beit Yaakov, siman 76, this escaped him. It is stated in the Talmud: all who are precise with them — their reward is that they cool Gehinnom for him, etc. I heard a sign for the matter: 'shema' sounds like 'and one' of 'echad' — 'ash' remains, 'ma' from 'shema,' 'chad' from 'echad' — initials Chananiah, Mishael, Azariah, Daniel — to hint that one who reads Keriat Shema with kavanah is saved from fire like Chananiah, Mishael, Azariah, and Daniel; so wrote Magen Avraham.
