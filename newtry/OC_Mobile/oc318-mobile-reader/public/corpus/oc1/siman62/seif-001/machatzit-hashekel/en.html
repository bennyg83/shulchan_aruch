(note 1) In any language. Specifically when he understands, etc. Tosafos on Rosh Hashana 33a of Sotah — for Tosafos read there "these are said in their tongue" (but our version is "in any language," and see in Beit Shammai) — Keriat Shema, prayer, and Birkat HaMazon; and Tosafos asked why they did not also count Kiddush, Birkat HaP'ri, Birkat HaMitzvot, and Hallel. And they answered that "in their tongue" implies what is unique to them that they understand that language — which is not so for Kiddush, Birkat HaP'ri, mitzvot, and Birkat HaMazon: we do not require that he understand the language he reads. And see what is written in siman 193 regarding one who wishes to fulfill through hearing whether he must understand — see there.
