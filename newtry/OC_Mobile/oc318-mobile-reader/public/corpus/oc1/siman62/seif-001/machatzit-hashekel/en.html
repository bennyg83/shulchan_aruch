Seif katan 1: In any language. This applies only when he understands it, as Tosafos writes at the beginning of chapter seven of Sotah. Tosafos' text there reads, "These are recited in their own language," whereas our text reads, "in any language"; see Beis Shmuel. The list includes Krias Shema, prayer, and Bircas HaMazon. Tosafos ask why it does not also list Kiddush, blessings over fruit, blessings over mitzvos, and Hallel. They answer that "in their own language" implies the language particular to them, which they understand. By contrast, for Kiddush, blessings over fruit and mitzvos, and Bircas HaMazon, it is not necessary that he understand the language in which he recites. See what is written at the beginning of siman 153 regarding one who wishes to fulfill his obligation by listening, whether he must understand, and see there.
