In my humble opinion, siman 65 discusses one who has already recited all of Krias Shema. However, Olas Tamid, citing Kol Bo, adds that if he has said the verse "Shema Yisrael" and the congregation then says "Shema Yisrael," he must answer with them. Although one who says "Shema, Shema" is like one who says "Modim, Modim," in this case, so that he not appear to reject what the congregation is saying, he should say it a second time. This also appears to be the correct meaning of Kol Bo's wording there, "If the individual has already said it," etc. The expression "said it," which refers to the meaning conveyed by the verse, relates to the verse itself and not to all of Krias Shema.
