And in my opinion, in siman 65 it discusses one who read the entire Keriat Shema; but Eshel Torah in the name of Kol Bo added that if one said the verse Shema Yisrael and afterward the congregation says Shema Yisrael, he must answer with them — even one who says Shema Shema, like one who says Modeh Modeh — nevertheless in this, so that it not appear that he does not agree with what the congregation says, he should say them again; and so it appears in truth, for now, from the wording of Kol Bo there, who wrote 'and if the individual already said,' etc.; and the wording 'said' which is the language of force implies the verse, not Keriat Shema.
