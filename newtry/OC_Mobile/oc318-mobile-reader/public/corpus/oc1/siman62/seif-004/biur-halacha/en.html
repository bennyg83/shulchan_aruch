'And even initially,' etc. — this law is not specifically regarding Keriat Shema; the same is true regarding prayer and other berakhot, as explained in Terumat HaDeshen [and it seems from his wording there that by law one does not fulfill prayer through thought; and see below siman 101, Magen Avraham, s.k. 2]. And it is not specifically if the place is not clean; the same is true if he himself is not completely clean — such as if he touched with his hands covered places; for if his hands or other places of his body were actually filthy, certainly even thought is forbidden, as Pri Megadim wrote below, and it is simple. And it appears to me that all this is when he knows with certainty that they are filthy; but in doubt, perhaps one may rely and be lenient in this regarding thought in all berakhot except Keriat Shema and prayer, according to what is explained below in siman 185, se'if 5 — see there; but one may reject that there it is only bedieved; nevertheless, if he is covered as well, one may be lenient, for there are also opinions among the rishonim on this.

'That there should not be,' etc. — see Mishnah Berurah in what he wrote regarding drinking in a bathhouse that is not proper; and the wording of Chayei Adam caused them to err in this. And in truth, in my opinion this matter is very simple: one may not permit because he is under constraint regarding the berakhah that he cannot bless — this would be relevant to say only if the Sages had said that he is commanded to bless; but in truth they did not say so — rather in Berakhot folio 36 it is forbidden to benefit without a berakhah; and similarly from Magen Avraham in siman 13 — see there. And apart from this, he cannot leave the bathhouse and bless and drink; or at least enter the middle room of the bathhouse and contemplate the berakhah there.
