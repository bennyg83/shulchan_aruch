Seif 4. If because of illness. Peri Chadash writes that it is reasonable that if the constraint passes before the time for Krias Shema has ended, he must recite it, because mental recitation is not equivalent to speech. I find this difficult. If the time for Krias Shema has already passed, what practical difference is there in saying that he fulfilled his obligation? Even if he did not fulfill it, what can he now do? What legal consequence is there to whether he fulfilled it or not? It is forced to say that the difference concerns the view that Krias Shema, like prayer, may be made up, as noted above at the end of siman 58. According to this, since he fulfilled his obligation through mental recitation while constrained, he need not make it up. This is forced.
