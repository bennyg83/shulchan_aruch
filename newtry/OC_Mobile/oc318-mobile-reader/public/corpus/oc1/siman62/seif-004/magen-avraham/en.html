That the place not be, etc. For example, the middle chamber of a bathhouse, where contemplation is permitted but speech is forbidden, as stated in siman 84. Shelah, page 66, writes that Rabbeinu Yonah's statement that one should contemplate in his heart means that he should think that he is obligated to recite the blessing and feel distress that he cannot recite it. Rabbeinu Yonah's language does not imply this.
