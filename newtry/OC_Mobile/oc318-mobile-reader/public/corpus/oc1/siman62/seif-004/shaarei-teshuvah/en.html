"Fulfilled." Ba'er Hetev; and see Sha'agat Aryeh siman 7, who expanded on a mute person who does not hear — that he is obligated in Keriat Shema and all mitzvot that depend on speech of the mouth even though he cannot make it heard to his ears — we do not care about this. And if he hears and does not speak, he also fulfills through thought of the heart; and this is not comparable to one who lacks understanding — uncertain.
