If due to illness or another duress one read Keriat Shema in his heart — he fulfilled.And even l'chatchila one may do so if he is in a place that is not completely clean and cannot clean it because of duress — he may contemplate in his heart, provided the place is not completely filthy, for it is forbidden to contemplate words of Torah in a place of filth [Beit Yosef, siman 85].]:
