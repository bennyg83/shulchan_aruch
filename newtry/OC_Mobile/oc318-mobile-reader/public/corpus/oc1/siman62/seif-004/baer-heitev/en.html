He has fulfilled — and all the more so he fulfills by hearing Keriat Shema from another after the fact; and so too from Maharam chapter 1 siman 41; and see Magen Avraham siman 61 seif 1 comment 16 who wrote Olat Tamid, and it appears specifically when he understands the language; and even in the holy tongue we require specifically that the listener understand — see siman 193 seif 1. And in Shakh he brought in the name of Birkat Avraham that specifically an individual who fulfills for an individual; but an individual who fulfills for the many, or an individual who fulfills for two in Birkat Hamazon — even if they do not understand the holy tongue they fulfill — see there. Peri Chadash wrote: and it is reasonable that when the coercion departs, if the time for Keriat Shema has not yet passed, he is obligated to return and read, for we hold thought is not like speech — see there; and Yad Aharon disagrees and ruled he is not obligated to return and read Keriat Shema since he already fulfilled when the time for Keriat Shema arrived through thought, for at that hour when he was coerced thought is like speech — see there.
So — from this one learns regarding one who is thirsty at night in his bed and cannot take his hands and bless — he thinks the blessing in his heart and drinks [Taz]. (And in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he disagrees — see there — and he also brought in the name of Prisha who disagrees with Rabbeinu Yonah that wherever he is not clean it is forbidden to think the formula of Keriat Shema; rather he should think that he is obligated to read Keriat Shema and its blessings and be pained that he cannot do so, and he has reward for this.)
