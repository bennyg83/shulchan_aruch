Siman 64, 'if due to,' etc. — chapter 28, chapter 8, per the Yerushalmi; and it appears from what is written above on that Mishnah: Rav said the halakhah is like the view of both to be lenient, for not everything we say does the anonymous [Mishnah follow]; and R. Yossi — the halakhah follows the anonymous [Mishnah]; R. Yossi and R. Yehudah — the halakhah follows R. Yossi; and why did Rav need to say the halakhah is like the view of both to be lenient? Rather because he heard that R. Chiyya taught in the name of R. Meir — therefore he needed to say the halakhah is like the view of both to be lenient; and this is what is said in our Gemara: R. Meir said to him, 'This is what Scripture says: asher,' etc., after the intent of the heart, etc.

'And even initially,' etc. — for we say in the Midrash: David said, 'Listen, Hashem' — 'Master of the universe, at a time when I can speak, say Listen; and at a time when I cannot speak, understand my thoughts' — and the meaning is like the thought of my heart in my thoughts, etc.; and this is what is written, 'understand,' etc.; Tosafot R' Yona; and in Yalkut Tehillim, siman 427: 'Tremble and do not sin,' etc. — the Holy One, blessed be He, said: I said to pray in the synagogue in your city; and if you cannot go, pray on your bed; and if you cannot, contemplate in your heart, as it is said, 'Speak in your hearts upon your beds,' etc.

'Provided that,' etc. — as written above, siman 85, se'if 2; and such as the middle room of a bathhouse; and Magen Avraham.
