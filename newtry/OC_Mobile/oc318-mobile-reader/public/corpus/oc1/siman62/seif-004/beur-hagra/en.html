Seif 4: If because of, etc. Berachos 28a, according to the Yerushalmi. This appears from what is stated there concerning the foregoing Mishnah: Rav said, the halachah follows both views leniently. The Yerushalmi asks: Do we not always say that where there is an anonymous Mishnah and Rabbi Yose, the halachah follows the anonymous Mishnah, and where Rabbi Yose and Rabbi Yehudah disagree, the halachah follows Rabbi Yose? Why, then, did Rav need to say that the halachah follows both views leniently? It answers that since he heard Rabbi Chiya teach it in the name of Rabbi Meir, Rav therefore needed to say that the halachah follows both views leniently. This is the same as what our Gemara states: Rabbi Meir said to them, the letter heh in the word asher, etc., according to the intent of the heart, etc.
And even initially, etc. The Midrash states: Regarding the verse, "Give ear to my words, Hashem," David said, Master of the Universe, when I am able to speak, give ear to my words, and when I am unable to speak, understand my meditation. "Meditation" means the thought of my heart, as in "my meditation," etc. This is the meaning of "understand," etc. Rabbeinu Yonah. In Yalkut Tehillim, siman 627, on "Tremble and do not sin," etc., the Holy One, blessed is He, says: I instructed you to pray in the synagogue of your city. If you cannot go, pray upon your bed. If you cannot, contemplate in your heart, as it is stated, "Speak in your hearts upon your beds," etc.
Provided that, etc. As stated above, siman 85, seif 2, for example, in the middle chamber of a bathhouse. See Magen Avraham.
