(א) (Note 2) The mother, etc., need not, etc. — for the father's fast on his own behalf also counts for the son. And see siman 550 that also in the three fasts they were lenient regarding pregnant and nursing women.

(ב) That he began, etc. — for even though he is not obligated in half-shekel until he is twenty years old; but if his father began and weighed on behalf of his minor son once, he does not stop again. And he is obligated to weigh from now on.
