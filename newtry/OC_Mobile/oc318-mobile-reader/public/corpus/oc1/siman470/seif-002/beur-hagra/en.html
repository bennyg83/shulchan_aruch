(א) Section 2 — and if it falls. For one does not fast on erev Shabbat, chapter 4 of Taanit.

(ב) And some say. As stated in chapter 1 of Megillah: since it was postponed, it is postponed; and even though in Taanit 12b if so — and they bring proof from Yerushalmi, parashat erev Pesach, that they disagree about one who was fasting on erev Pesach whether because he was a firstborn or an istenis, and it was not resolved whether it fell on the first of the week on Shabbat.

(ג) And they practice. That the death of firstborns was for the sin of their fathers and mothers, as stated "Behold I will kill" etc., to strike Egypt etc.; and similarly in redemption "And I will kill" etc., "And every firstborn among my children" — and even though a woman is not commanded, the Torah exempted her as it exempted a daughter from redemption; nevertheless some say she fasts, and so too in section 1 in the gloss in the name of Maharil, and as stated in section 1 in the gloss, and not etc.
