They fast, etc. Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah wrote: if there is a minyan among them, I instructed that the shaliach tzibur say Aneinu in Shomea Tefillah; and that which they forbade mentioning in public until Nisan has passed speaks of one who is not obligated to fast, end quote. And this escaped his notice: Olas Shabbos siman 220 and Magen Avraham here. Also in Eliyah Zuta I brought a proof from Hagahot Minhagim, who wrote: and they say Aneinu and Birkas Kohanim at Mincha, etc.; see there. And ostensibly it is difficult: it is obvious that they say Aneinu on a fast; rather, it means where there are ten in the congregation. And further, from the fact that he mentions Birkas Kohanim, it is apparent that it is considered a public fast, for on an individual's fast one does not say [it], as is written in Minhagim.
