(2) They fast, etc. Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah wrote: if there is a minyan among them, I would say the shaliach tzibur should say Aneinu in Shema Koleinu; and what they forbade mentioning publicly until Nisan passes — he speaks of one not obligated to fast — end of his words. And I omitted this in Olat Shabbat siman 280 and Magen Avraham here; also in Eliyah Zuta I brought proof from Hagahot Minhagim who wrote they say Aneinu and Birkat Kohanim at Minchah, etc. — see there. At first glance it is difficult: it is simple they say Aneinu on a fast — only it deals with ten in public; and further, since he mentions Birkat Kohanim, evidently a public fast is considered, for on an individual fast one does not say it, as written in Minhagim.
