2) There. Whether a paternal firstborn. This is because the plague also struck paternal firstborn sons. Tur in the name of Avi HaEzri; Olas Shabbos, subsection 1. See below, subsection 16. Chok Yaakov, subsection 2, gives another reason: in any event, such a person is a firstborn either for inheritance or as the first issue of the womb requiring redemption. In other words, if a man without children marries a woman who has already given birth, their first child is firstborn only for inheritance, because that child is the first of the father's strength. If a man who already has children marries a woman who has never given birth, their first child is firstborn only for redemption, because that child is the first issue of her womb, as stated in Yoreh De'ah, siman 305, se'if 17, and Choshen Mishpat, siman 277, se'if 8.
