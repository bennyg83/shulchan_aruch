(ב) What the Mechaber wrote — firstborns fast — is a custom from Tractate Soferim, rabbinic; and if one's wife bore two males and we do not know which emerged first and both are firstborns out of doubt, he need not fast, for we do not come to ask in Beit Din and Kerachum and a city surrounded by doubt is certainly uprooted from rabbinic prohibition — we do not rule leniently out of doubt; unlike here, only a custom. And all the more male and female, rabbinic doubt is lenient. But on erev Pesach on Sunday, if he has a firstborn son or he himself is a firstborn, if he always fasts he fasts also per the reasoning of Shach siman 376 letter 4: if the mourner wishes, permitted to fast on Sunday — see there; and all the more on erev Pesach, for there is no comforting — see in Taanit 26. If there is no firstborn in the house, the eldest in the house need not fast — see Tur and Levush.
