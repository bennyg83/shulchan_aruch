Does the mother fast. And Terumat HaDeshen wrote that she need not fast, and so too wrote in Maharil in the name of Maharash; and where there is distress one should be lenient, and all the more so for pregnant and nursing women; and see siman 550. However, if she fasted once, it is like a vow, as stated in the first chapter of Shekalim: any minor whose father began to weigh [the shekel] on his behalf no longer stops; so wrote in Hagahot Semak, siman 144.
