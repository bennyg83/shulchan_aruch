The mother fasts. Terumat HaDeshen wrote she need not fast, and so too in Maharil in the name of Maharash; where there is distress one may be lenient, especially pregnant or nursing — siman 559. However, if she fasted once, it is like a vow, as stated in chapter 1 of Shekalim, small tractate: if his father began weighing on his behalf, he does not stop — so in Hagahot Semak siman 144.
