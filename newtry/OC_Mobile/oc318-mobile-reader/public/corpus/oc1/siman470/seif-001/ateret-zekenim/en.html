Firstborns fast. In a house where there is no firstborn, the eldest need not fast (Maharil). A firstborn daughter also fasts; and some are accustomed to redeem the firstborns verbally as Hashem redeemed in Egypt (Dagul MeRevavah in the name of Kol Bo). When the father is a firstborn and his son is also a firstborn, the mother need not fast (Maharil in the name of Maharshal), who wrote that the father's fast also counts for his minor son.
