(א) Section 1 — the firstborns. Tosafot 108a s.v. Rav Sheshet in the name of Tractate Soferim.

(ב) Whether, etc. — firstborn from father, for he is sanctified for inheritance; and they said in Midrash: one who was intimate with ten unmarried men and bore ten sons — all died. Firstborn from mother, for he is sanctified to the kohen, as stated "Sanctify to Me" etc., "And it shall be when Pharaoh asks you" etc., "And I will kill" etc., "And every firstborn among the children of" etc.

(ג) And some say, etc. — as stated in Pesikta, parashat Bo: "And every firstborn died" — firstborn to man, firstborn to woman, firstborn male, firstborn in the womb. How so? A man was with ten women and she bore ten sons — all are firstborns of women. Ten men were with one woman and she bore ten sons — all are firstborns of men, etc. R' Avin in the name of R' Yehuda ben Pazi: Bitya daughter of Pharaoh was a firstborn — by merit of what was she saved? By Moshe's prayer, as stated "It shall not go out at night" — "night" is written, etc.

(ד) And the custom is not so — for the Torah did not give sanctity of firstborn status to a female.
