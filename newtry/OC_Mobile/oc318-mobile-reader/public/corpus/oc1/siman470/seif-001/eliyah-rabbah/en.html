(1) The firstborns fast, etc. Baal brit, sandak, and father of the boy are permitted to eat at the milah and repay the fast afterward (responsum Chut HaShani 25); and it appears when fasting on behalf of the son, even when not baal brit — for this is only a custom; in responsum Nachalat Yaakov siman 10 it does not appear so; in any case one may be lenient to join the minyan. Chok Yaakov wrote: when erev Pesach falls on Shabbat and they fast on Thursday, and a milah occurred on Thursday — he fasts and does not complete, as above siman 559 section 9, unless in countries where they eat the milah meal at night.
