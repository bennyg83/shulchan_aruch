(א) From the mother. Since in any case he is a firstborn either for inheritance or for opening the womb for redemption — and for this reason Magen Avraham wrote that one born after miscarriages must fast, for he is a firstborn for inheritance. Accordingly, this requires further study regarding one born by caesarean section and the one born after him, who is not a firstborn either for inheritance or for redemption, whether he must fast — see Kaf HaChayyim. And he wrote in responsum of Maharil siman 14 that firstborn kohanim and leviim must fast on erev Pesach even if he is only a firstborn from the mother, so that he is found not to be a firstborn for inheritance or for redemption — nevertheless he must fast. And see in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 16 that one born after a miscarriage of a nine-month son who died within thirty days need not fast — see there. The eldest of the household need not fast even though the plague was also upon them — we are not so stringent, Tur and Maharil. He wrote in Knesset HaGedolah: if there are ten firstborns fasting together, when the shaliach tzibur repeats the prayer he says Aneinu in Shema Koleinu — and Peri Chadash similarly that he should say Aneinu in Shema Koleinu; and Magen Avraham wrote it is not proper to do so, for it is like a public fast.

(ב) Female. Bach wrote it is proper to do so; but in these countries the custom spread per Rama not to fast a firstborn daughter.
