It appears to me that one born after miscarriages must fast, for he is a firstborn for inheritance. Kenesses HaGedolah, Mahadura Basra, wrote: if there are ten firstborns fasting, the shaliach tzibur says Aneinu in Shomea Tefillah; and it appears to me that it is not proper to do so, for it is like a public fast (Olas Shabbos, siman 220). He wrote, see there, in the name of Maharash HaLevi that the firstborns may eat at a bris milah meal, and one who is stringent, may blessing come upon him, end quote; and in our country they were accustomed to be stringent.
