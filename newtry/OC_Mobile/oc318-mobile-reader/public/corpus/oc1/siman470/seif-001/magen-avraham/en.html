It appears to me that one born after miscarriages must fast, for he is a firstborn for inheritance. Knesset HaGedolah wrote: if there are ten firstborns fasting, the shaliach tzibur says Aneinu in Shema Koleinu; and it appears to me it is not proper to do so, for it is like a public fast — Olat Shabbat siman 280. Maharash HaLevi wrote that firstborns may eat at a brit milah meal, and one who is stringent will come to harm — end of his words; and in our country they were stringent. Thursday: on erev Shabbat one does not fast, as in siman 317; if the fast is difficult for them, they may taste before checking or command another to check (Terumat HaDeshen, Maharil) — see siman 431.
