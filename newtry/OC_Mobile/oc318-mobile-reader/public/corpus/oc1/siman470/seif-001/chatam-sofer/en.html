In Shaarei Teshuvah note 1: thus said the Merciful One. NB: it appears to me this depends on the dispute of Rashi and Tosafot in Bechorot daf 49a — for Rashi it depends on viable offspring and the words of Shevut Yaakov fit well; and Tosafot there hold it is a decree of Scripture and even if we know he is viable offspring there is no obligation to redeem until thirty days — and this is as Birkei Yosef, that it is a decree of Scripture; and we rule as Tosafot. And see Yoreh Deah siman 303 [from the Gaon, end of section].
