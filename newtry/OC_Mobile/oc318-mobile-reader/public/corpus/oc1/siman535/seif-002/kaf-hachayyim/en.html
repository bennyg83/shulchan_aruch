2) There. Articles may not be moved from one courtyard to another, etc. This means through a public domain. Nimukei Yosef on Rif. Accordingly, if there is a doorway between two courtyards, articles may be moved from one to the other. They may even be moved through several courtyards. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2; Shulchan Gavoah, subsection 1; Chayei Adam, rule 114, subsection 1; Mikraei Kodesh, siman 60, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2.
