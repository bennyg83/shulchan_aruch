1) [Se'if 1] Articles may not be moved from one courtyard to another, etc. This is because of the exertion involved. Nimukei Yosef on Rif; Levush. This means that a person should rejoice during the festival and not be burdened with exertion. Bigdei Yesha.
2) There. Articles may not be moved from one courtyard to another, etc. This means through a public domain. Nimukei Yosef on Rif. Accordingly, if there is a doorway between two courtyards, articles may be moved from one to the other. They may even be moved through several courtyards. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2; Shulchan Gavoah, subsection 1; Chayei Adam, rule 114, subsection 1; Mikraei Kodesh, siman 60, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2.
3) There. Articles may not be moved from one courtyard to another, etc. This is forbidden even through our alleyways, which do not have the status of a public domain. Magen Avraham there; Mikraei Kodesh there, Likutei Rima, subsection 1; Tosafos Chaim on Chayei Adam there, subsection 1.
