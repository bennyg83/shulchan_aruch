(s.k. 1) From a courtyard, etc. And it is possible that our alleyways, etc.: because the poskim wrote without qualification in this siman. That is to say: even though above in siman 363, se'if 26, the Rema wrote in a gloss that there are those who say that all our alleyways have the status of courtyards, and therefore they require a tzuras hapesach; see there. But in this siman they wrote without qualification. On the contrary: from the fact that the Beis Yosef wrote without qualification and wrote, "And if the houses, etc., but rather onto a mavoy, there are those who say that from one house to the house adjacent to it is permitted," it implies but not to another house; and he did not write a heter for the present time, which implies that one should not distinguish in this matter. And see Magen Avraham, siman 365, s.k. 4.
