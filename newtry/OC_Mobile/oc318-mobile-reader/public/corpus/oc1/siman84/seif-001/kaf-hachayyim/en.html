(a) [Seif 1] A new bathhouse in which no one has yet bathed; it is permitted to recite in it. Even though in a new privy it is forbidden to recite, as written in siman 83 seif 2; for a privy is different, for it is repulsive. Gemara Shabbat daf 10.
