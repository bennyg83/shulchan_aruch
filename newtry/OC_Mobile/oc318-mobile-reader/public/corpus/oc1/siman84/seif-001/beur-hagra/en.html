Seif 1, bathhouse, etc. And in Nedarim 37b there they invited to a bathhouse, as written in Shabbos there; and see Tosafos in Nedarim and in Shabbos there.

And it is permitted to contemplate, etc. In Kiddushin 33a, 31b: this, etc.; and Shach explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 244, seif dalet — that is, in the middle room, since there is inquiring after shalom there; and so too from Rif and Rosh and Shulchan Aruch, who wrote this statement. And if it were in the outer room, what is there to say — there one puts on tefillin, etc.; and so too Tosafos Shulchan Aruch wrote it. And above the Gemara says: so too it is reasonable, etc. — it implies that contemplation is permitted there; but the Rambam omitted it there; he holds it is in the outer room itself; and see above siman 85.

And it is forbidden, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Shabbos: Shmuel asked Rav — what is the law regarding answering Amen in a filthy place? He said to him: it is forbidden. Forbidden — for I told you it is forbidden. And the intent is that it is forbidden to say and to rule this name.
