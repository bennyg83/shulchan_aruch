Seif 1, “a bathhouse,” etc. The reading in Nedarim there should not be “they designated it as a bathhouse,” as is evident from Shabbos there. See Tosafos in Nedarim and in Shabbos there.
And it is permitted to think, etc. In Kiddushin 33a, the Gemara answers, “This is not difficult,” etc. The Shach, in Yoreh Deah 244:4, explains that this refers to the intermediate chamber, since exchanging greetings is permitted there. This also appears from the Rif, the Rosh, and the other poskim, who cite this teaching. Were it referring to the outer chamber, what novelty would there be? In that chamber one may even put on tefillin, etc. The Tur and Shulchan Aruch likewise cite it, and regarding it the Gemara says, “This is also reasonable,” etc., implying that it is permitted to think Torah thoughts there. The Rambam, however, omitted this ruling there, for he maintains that it refers to the actual outer chamber. See what I wrote at the end of siman 85.
And it is forbidden, etc. The Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Shabbos, relates that Shmuel asked Rav whether one may answer amen in a filthy place. Rav replied, “It is forbidden, and even saying that I told you it is forbidden is itself forbidden,” meaning that one may not state or teach this ruling there.
