And Taz wrote here that this immersion room truly has the law of the middle room of the bathhouse; and ab initio one should not bless there any blessing except the immersion blessing, which is necessary and pertains to immersion; but in the water all blessings are permitted, as written regarding Keriat Shema in siman 74 that it is permitted in murky or clear water if his heart is not in the water. See Yad Aharon and Peri Chadash siman 85. (And see in Eliyah Rabbah a long pilpul on this):
Forbidden. For Shalom is the name of the Holy One, blessed be He. And likewise in filthy alleys it is forbidden to greet with Shalom. And a person whose name is Shalom — Taz permits mentioning his name in the bathhouse and privy, for he does not intend Shalom but only that person's name. Bach and Magen Avraham hold it is forbidden. But in a foreign language it is permitted to call him. And Peri Chadash in this siman disagrees with Bach and holds that the name Shalom is forbidden to call even in a foreign language — see there; but one whose name is Shimshon is permitted — see there in siman 85. If someone asked him a halachic matter, it is forbidden to say to him: one does not answer in the bathhouse. But in the middle room it is permitted to say thus.
