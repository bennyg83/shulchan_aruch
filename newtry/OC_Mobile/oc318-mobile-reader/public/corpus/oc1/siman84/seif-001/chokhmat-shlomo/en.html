Seif 1 — a new bathhouse, etc. NB: see Taz seif katan 2, who wrote in the name of Kesef Mishnah; and it requires study — for regarding mezuzah, an immersion room is like a bathhouse, etc. And in my humble opinion there is an answer: mezuzah is only an obligation of dwelling, and if one does not dwell there, even if it is a beautiful and clean house, he is exempt from mezuzah. Therefore here, since we require dwelling, and immersion — since it is not for honor — is not dwelling; but mentioning the Name is not dependent on dwelling, only on a clean place; and wherever the place is clean of filth, it is permitted to mention the Name there; therefore an immersion place too is not considered filthy, and it is permitted to mention the Name there. And a house in which there is no dwelling at all proves that one may mention the name of Heaven there and is exempt from mezuzah — and this is correct. Again I saw in Eliyah Rabbah that he intended this briefly, and I explained it more, with God's help, until I wondered at Kesef Mishnah that he did not notice this; examine closely.
