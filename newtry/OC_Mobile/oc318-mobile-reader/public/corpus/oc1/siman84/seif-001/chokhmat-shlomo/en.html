Seif 1 A new bathhouse, etc. NB see Taz s.k. 2, who wrote in the name of Maggid Mishneh, and it requires study — for regarding mezuzah an immersion room is like a bathhouse, etc. And in my humble opinion the question is: mezuzah is only an obligation of dwelling, and if one does not dwell there, even if it is a fine clean house he is exempt from mezuzah; therefore here, since we require dwelling and immersion is not for honor, it is not dwelling. But mentioning God's name does not depend on dwelling — only on a clean place; and wherever the place is clean of filth it is permitted to mention His name there; therefore also in a place of immersion it is not considered filth and one may mention God's name. And a house in which there is no dwelling at all proves that one may mention God's name there and is exempt from mezuzah — and such is my view.
