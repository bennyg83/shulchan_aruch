(1) [Seif 1] A new bathhouse in which they have not bathed — it is permitted to recite in it. And even though in a new beit haknesset it is forbidden to recite, as written in siman 83 seif 2 — a beit haknesset is different, for it is more repugnant. Gemara Shabbos 10a. And Rashi explained: it is repugnant and a disgraceful name is upon it. And they brought it in Bach and Derishah ot 1, Taz seif katan 1.
