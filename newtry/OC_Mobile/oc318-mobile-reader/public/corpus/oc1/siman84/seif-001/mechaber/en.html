Whether it is permitted to recite in a bathhouse. It contains one seif. A
new bathhouse in which they have not bathed — it is permitted to recite in it. And in an old one: in the outer room, where all who stand there are dressed, it is permitted; and in the middle room, where some of those standing there are dressed and some are naked, one may inquire after shalom there, but not Kerias Shema and prayer.And it is permitted to contemplate words of Torah there (Ran, chapter 1 of Shabbos and chapter Kol haTzalamim).} And in the inner room, where all stand there naked, even inquiring after shalom is forbidden. {Rama: And it is forbidden to answer Amen in a bathhouse (Ran, chapter Kirah).] ובפנימי שכלם עומדים שם ערומים אפי' שאלת שלום אסור [ואסור לענות אמן בבית המרחץ] [ר"ן פ' כירה]:
