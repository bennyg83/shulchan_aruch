Whether it is permitted to recite sacred texts in a bathhouse. It contains 1 seif: In a new bathhouse in which no one has yet bathed, it is permitted to recite sacred texts. In an old bathhouse, in the outer room where everyone present is clothed, it is permitted. In the middle room, where some are clothed and some are naked, one may exchange a greeting of Shalom, but may not recite Shema or pray. {Rama: It is permitted to think words of Torah there. [Ran, chapter 1 of Shabbos and chapter Kol HaTzelamim]} In the inner room, where everyone stands naked, even a greeting of Shalom is forbidden. It is also forbidden to answer Amen in a bathhouse. [Ran, chapter Kirah]
