Whether It is Permitted to Recite in the Bathhouse, containing 1 s'if A new bathhouse in which they have not washed - it is permitted to recite in it. And in an old [i.e. existing] one: in the outer room in which all who stand there are dressed, it is permitted, and in the middle room, where some who are standing there are dressed and some are standing there naked, one may greet another with the word "Shalom" (which is one of the names of God) , but may not the recite the Sh'ma or the Prayer [i.e. Amidah]. And in the inner room where everyone is naked, even greeting others with the word "Shalom" is forbidden. {Rama: Rem"a: and it is permitted to contemplate words of Torah there (Ran, Perek 1 of Shabbat and Perek "Kol haTz'lamim") And it is forbidden to respond "Amen" in a bathhouse (Ran, Perek Kirah)}
