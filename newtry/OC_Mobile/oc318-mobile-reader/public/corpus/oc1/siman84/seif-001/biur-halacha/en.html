And in an old one in the outer room, etc. — for their custom was that after one donned his robe in the middle room, he would go out to the outer room to finish dressing there (Rashi). And from what Mishna Berurah wrote — even if it were his custom, etc., and in winter days, etc. — this is from the Yerushalmi; and it is straightforward that we deal with a case where the building was not constructed from the outset as a bathhouse; for if not so, even if they bathe in it only occasionally, its law is also like an old beit haknesset.

And in the inner room, where all stand there naked — in the Yerushalmi it states that most people stand there naked.
