And in an old one. Even though there is no person there now (Gemara and Beit Yosef).

Inquiring after shalom is forbidden. For shalom is a name of the Holy One, blessed be He; and the same applies in filthy alleyways — inquiring after shalom is forbidden. And a man whose name is Shalom — it is forbidden to call him by his name, but in a vernacular language it is permitted to call him (Bach). If a man asked him a halachic matter, it is forbidden to tell him: we do not respond in a bathhouse; but in the middle room it is permitted to say so (Gemara 71a).
