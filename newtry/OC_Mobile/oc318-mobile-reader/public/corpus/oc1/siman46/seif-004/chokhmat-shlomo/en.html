Seif 4: One must recite, etc., “Who did not make me a woman.” See Magen Avraham, who writes that if one already recited “Who did not make me a woman,” he should no longer recite “Who did not make me a slave.” See what I wrote incidentally concerning this in the laws of tzitzis, siman 18, in my commentary on Orach Chayim from the year 5609, page 71b.
