One needs to bless every day "Who did not make me a non-Jew," "Who did not make me a slave," and "Who did not make me a woman." {Rama: And even a convert may bless thus [his own view]; but he should not say "Who did not make me a non-Jew," for he was a non-Jew at the outset [Abudarham].} And women bless "Who made me according to His will."
