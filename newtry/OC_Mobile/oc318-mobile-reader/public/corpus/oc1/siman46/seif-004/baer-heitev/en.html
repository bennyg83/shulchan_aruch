A woman. Bach implies that according to the ruling in seif 5 regarding Matir Asurim, the same applies here. If one first recited “Who did not make me a woman,” he should no longer recite the blessings concerning a non-Jew and a slave. Taz writes that the cases are not comparable. See there.
To recite the blessing. Meaning, that a convert should say, “Who made me a convert.” Magen Avraham, Nachalas Tzvi, and Taz. Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah writes that it is more correct to say, “Who brought me beneath the wings of the Shechinah.” Shelah, Bach, and Peri Chadash rule that a convert should not recite this blessing at all, but only the blessings concerning a slave and a woman. Responsa Mateh Yosef, part 2, siman 8, rules likewise. Yad Aharon concludes that a convert may recite, “Who did not make me a non-Jew,” because the intention is, “Who did not make me remain a non-Jew.”
