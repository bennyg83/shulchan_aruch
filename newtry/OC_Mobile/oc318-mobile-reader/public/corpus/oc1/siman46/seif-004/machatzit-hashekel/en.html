(Section 4) "And may it be Your will," etc. — and as he wrote, citing Rashba, Magen Avraham brought it more explicitly in responsum 165, and these are his words, as the Beit Yosef brought in section 165:

A solitary person says "Answer us," for always a person should join with the congregation, etc.

And if he comes to add and say words of supplication, etc. — like what he needs at his time, and in singular, and like the receiver of milk and blood that diminished, etc. — even though he says it in singular, it is acceptable, end quote. And Magen Avraham concludes there, and these are his words: since it is not of the fixed coin of the blessing, end quote.
