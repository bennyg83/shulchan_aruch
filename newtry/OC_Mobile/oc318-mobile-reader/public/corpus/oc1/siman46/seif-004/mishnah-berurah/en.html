(15) "On every day that He did not make me," etc. — and he should beware not to bless "that He made me a Jew," as appears in some siddurim through printing error, for some say that through this he will not be able to bless again "that He did not make me a slave" or "that He did not make me a woman."

(16) Gentile — and if he blessed at the outset "that He did not make me a woman," some say he can no longer bless "that He did not make me a gentile" and "that He did not make me a slave," because a woman is preferable to both of them, and thereby the admission is included — for in general there are two hundred measures. And likewise if he blessed "that He did not make me a slave," he cannot bless "that He did not make me a gentile," because a slave is preferable to a gentile, since he is obligated in some mitzvos. But many Acharonim disagree on all this, and likewise the Eliyah Rabbah agreed, and the Derech haChaim that one may bless.

(17) Slave — and a slave born a slave, some say he likewise cannot bless "that He did not make me a woman," because a slave is more abominable than a woman; and there are disputants. See Peri Megadim.

(18) He may bless — meaning he should say "that He made me a convert," for it is called "making," as written "and the soul that they made in Charan." And there are disputants on this, and their reason is that it is not appropriate to say "that He made me," for conversion was not except through his good choice that he chose the true religion. Nevertheless, all agree he may bless "that He did not make me a slave" and "that He did not make me a woman." And even though a gentile is more abominable than a slave, nevertheless if he had been born a slave perhaps his master would not have freed him and he would have remained a slave — unlike now, when conversion depended on his own will. A mamzer may bless all these blessings, for he is a complete Jew. And likewise a blind person — for we hold he is obligated in all mitzvos. And a tumtum and androgynous, who are obligated out of doubt in all mitzvos, should not say "that He did not make me a woman," for they are doubtful [Peri Megadim].

(19) He shall not say — see Magen Avraham and Beur haTosafos.
