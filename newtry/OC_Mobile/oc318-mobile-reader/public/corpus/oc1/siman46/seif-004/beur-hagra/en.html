Seif 4: "Who did not make me [a gentile]." Thus it is in the Rif and Rambam, and likewise in the Tosefta at the end of Berachos and in the Yerushalmi there; but the textual reading in our books is "Who made me a Jew," and likewise in the Rosh and Tur.

And even a convert [says it] — for he should say "Who made me a convert," for this too is called "making," as it says (Gen. 12:5), "And the soul that they had made in Charan," and as written in Bereishis Rabbah there, parashah 39, and in the first chapter of Avodah Zarah.

And women [do not say it] — Tur, for such was the custom.
