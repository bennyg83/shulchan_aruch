Seif 4: Who did not make me. This is the text of Rif and Rambam. It is likewise the text of the Tosefta at the end of Berachos and of the Yerushalmi there. Our printed editions read, “Who made me an Israelite.” Rosh and Tur likewise have that version.
And even a convert, etc. He should say, “Who made me a convert,” because conversion too is called making, as in the verse, “And the souls they made in Charan,” and as explained in Bereishis Rabbah, parashah 39, and in the first chapter of Avodah Zarah.
And the women, etc. Tur writes that this is the accepted custom.
