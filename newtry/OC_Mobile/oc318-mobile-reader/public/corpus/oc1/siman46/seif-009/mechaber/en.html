One does not recite verses before the blessing on the Torah, even though he says them as supplications. And there are those who say there is no concern, since he says them only as supplications; and it is correct to be concerned with the first view.But the practice follows the latter view—for on the days of Selichot we pray Selichot and afterwards bless on the Torah with the order of the rest of the blessings; and so every day when we enter the synagogue we say several verses and supplications and afterwards bless on the Torah; and the practice is to place the blessing on the Torah immediately after 'who formed,' and we do not change [and so is implied in Tosafot and Mordechai, first chapter of Berachot]. And it is good to say in Shacharit after 'Hear, O Israel,' etc., 'Blessed be the Name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever,' for sometimes we wait with Keriat Shema to recite it not in its time, and one fulfills thereby [Tur].]:
