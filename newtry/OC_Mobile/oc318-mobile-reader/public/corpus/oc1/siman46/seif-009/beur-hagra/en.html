Seif 9: But the custom [follows the lenient view] — and likewise the custom of the ancients, as the Tur wrote and as written below.

They accustomed themselves to arrange [the morning service], etc. The Tur wrote, and in Ashkenazi siddurim he wrote afterward "May it be Your will, Master of all worlds," and this is based on what is written in Tanna debei Eliyahu: "Those wrapped in hunger at the head of every street" — there is no hunger except from words of Torah, as it says (Amos 8:11), "Not a hunger for bread" — and about that generation it says, "Forever let a person fear Heaven in secret," etc. Shibolei haLeket wrote, and our ancestors instituted saying it every day before prayer; and its main establishment was because of the generation of persecution — and this is what he said, "fear Heaven in secret"; and this is what he said, "and they say Shema Yisrael," etc., for they decreed not to recite Kerias Shema, and therefore they also say "You are He" and "Who sanctifies Your name among the many" — and they are mentioned in Tosafos Berachos 46a and in Pesachim 104b. The Tur wrote it was instituted per the Yerushalmi, "You are He," etc., "At that time," etc., and afterward birkas haTorah and parashas haTamid, etc. The Tur wrote, and it was difficult for me on this — the order of saying verses before birkas haTorah; and I myself practiced blessing after Elokei neshamah, etc. Darkei Moshe wrote: and the custom is to bless after Asher yatzar, and they do so per the Tur. And even though he wrote [verses] after Elokei neshamah — that was per the reasoning of not interrupting between Asher yatzar and Elokei neshamah; but we hold that one may interrupt, as written above siman 6. But I have seen in all early and later books that the formula from the days of our fathers who arranged the blessings for us is to bless after all the blessings, before parashas haTamid; and the arranging of Elokei neshamah immediately after birkas Asher yatzar — and likewise R' Yitzchak in Sefer haYashar that Asher yatzar is adjacent to Elokei neshamah; and likewise all the poskim. See Tosafos Berachos 46 s.v. kol, etc., Rosh beginning siman 13, "One who arose early," etc., when reading parashas korbanos, etc., responsum beginning kelal 4, and one who rises to study should bless Elokei neshamah, Asher yatzar, Elokei neshamah, and the entire order of blessings until birkas haTorah including birkas haTorah, etc. — and likewise in the siddur of Rambam chapter 7 of Hilchos Tefillah and in all early books and siddurim, and likewise in Abudraham. What the Tur found difficult was per his reasoning that he holds the first view; but per the other view — and as agreed in the Hagahah — there is no concern. And even per the first view, one should say before the arrangement "Forever let," etc., and likewise in Rambam's siddur for prayer, as written at the beginning of the order: "The people practiced reading," etc., "Forever," etc.; see chapter 7 of Hilchos Tefillah halachah 11. Also that the Geonim wrote one should not interrupt between birkas Asher yatzar and Elokei neshamah, and from Elokei neshamah the order of blessings begins until haGomel, etc.

And it is good [to say Baruch shem] etc. — for without Baruch shem kavod malchuso le'olam va'ed it is only like narrating words.

And one fulfills [the obligation] with this — as written in the second chapter of Berachos; and even per the view that one repeats and completes it, nevertheless when reading out of its time, one reads. It seems to us that it is not proper with this to fulfill without blessings, and also for this reason one should not adjoin geulah to tefillah.
