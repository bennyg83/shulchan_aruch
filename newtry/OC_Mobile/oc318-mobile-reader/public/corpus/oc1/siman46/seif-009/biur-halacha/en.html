"Because sometimes," etc. — see Beur HaGra who wrote: it seems to us that it is not proper with this to fulfill without blessings, nor should one adjoin geulah to tefillah. He holds this per the view of Ra'ah brought in Beit Yosef that it is better for the congregation to say the blessing of Kerias Shema before Kerias Shema which is deOraisa — see there. And apparently his words revolve around the words of Rama, from whom it is implied that he agrees with Beit Yosef in his conclusion, as is evident from him that he holds that even if the congregation will not pass the time of Kerias Shema, nevertheless one says Baruch shem kavod malchuso le'olam va'ed in order to fulfill with this; and the Gra holds that in such a case it is not worthwhile to fulfill without blessings. But if he sees that the congregation will pass the time of Kerias Shema, it is better to intend to fulfill in order that he not remain without Kerias Shema, as Bach, Magen Avraham, and other acharonim wrote. Or possibly the Gra holds that even in this it is better to recite Kerias Shema at its time with its blessings and adjoin geulah to tefillah and pray individually, than to recite without blessings and pray afterward with the congregation — requires investigation. And also requires investigation apparently in what the Gra concluded "nor should one adjoin geulah to tefillah" — does he not adjoin geulah to tefillah afterward? And even though with Kerias Shema he already fulfilled, it does not harm at all for this matter, as brought below siman 66 seif 10 in the Hagahah and in Magen Avraham — see there; and the Gra also agrees there with Magen Avraham. And possibly his intent is thus according to what we know that his opinion is to say "and they say twice every day, Hashem our God, Hashem is One" — and this is exactly the view of Ra'ah brought in Beit Yosef; and therefore the Gra wrote on the words of Rama who holds like Beit Yosef: "it seems to me it is not proper with this to fulfill," etc. — meaning that if they say Shema Yisrael, etc., perforce it will not be done properly: if they say it without blessings, certainly it is not proper; and even if he wishes to say them now with blessings, he should not adjoin geulah to tefillah so that he can pray afterward with the congregation — therefore it is better to skip the words Shema Yisrael. All this the Gra wrote according to what Rama holds, that one fulfills with this; but according to what we explained in Mishnah Berurah that one should not intend to fulfill with this unless he sees the congregation will pass the time of Kerias Shema — possibly he agrees likewise, as we wrote at the beginning of our words.

"And one fulfills with this" — see Mishnah Berurah who wrote one should not intend to fulfill unless he sees, etc. And it seems to me that even if he estimates the congregation will not pass the time of Kerias Shema of sunrise, but he fears they will pass the time of Kerias Shema per the calculation from amud hashachar [as is known there are opinions among poskim whether the Kerias Shema time — until three hours — is calculated from amud hashachar or from sunrise onward, as explained below siman 58 in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 3], he may intend to fulfill now through Kerias Shema — it is not necessary for the view of poskim who calculate from amud hashachar, for he acts well; and even according to poskim who calculate from sunrise, one may rely on the view of Beit Yosef and Rama, and Or Zaru'a agrees with them, that even if the congregation will not pass the time of Kerias Shema, nevertheless it is permitted to intend to fulfill with this — see Beit Yosef.
