[9] He should not bless it, etc. — for it is already included in the blessing of zokef. Bach ruled that bedi'eved one needs a blessing; and likewise if he blessed first shelo asani eved, he blesses afterward she'asani Yisrael; or if he preceded shelo asani ishah, he blesses afterward shelo asani eved — and Nachalas Tzvi and Knesset HaGedolah agreed. But Taz and Magen Avraham are lenient in a doubt of blessings; nevertheless regarding eved, ishah, and Yisrael it seems one should not be lenient. Shelah warned to bless malbish arumim before pokeach ivrim; and in all our book versions pokeach ivrim is printed first, and likewise in our Shas versions; and I also saw in Baal Halachos Gedolos page 11 and Rokeach siman 320 and Abudraham — therefore it does not seem to me to change the versions in this. Taz wrote to bless zokef kefufim before matir asurim — and it requires investigation.
