Because — Taz: and there was not sufficient time to examine it properly. And what he wrote in the wording "You are He," etc. — behold there is in it only a Name in Eretz Yisrael, "Who sanctifies," etc., and not malchus; and we hold that wherever there is no Name and malchus there is no blessing, as explained in Eliyah Rabbah, and an adjacent blessing does not help. And see Levush why it does not open with Baruch, for it is a blessing of thanksgiving; and nevertheless one can say, like Elokai neshamah, malchus is not required, as Beit Yosef wrote in Eliyah Rabbah — so too here, since He created existence from nothing, there is no King of the world more than this. And therefore one can say the Sephardic wording at the head is fitting; and nevertheless we do not change and say "we were created."
