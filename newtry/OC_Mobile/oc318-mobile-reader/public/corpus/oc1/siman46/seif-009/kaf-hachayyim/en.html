9) There. If one forgot to recite Birchos HaShachar before prayer, he should recite them afterward, except for HaMachazir Neshamos, because he has already been exempted through Mechayeh HaMeisim, and except for Birchos HaTorah, because Ahavas Olam has already exempted him. This will be explained, with Hashem's help, below in our discussion of siman 47, se'ifim 7 and 8, and Rema's words at the end of siman 52. If he did not recite them after prayer either, see our discussion below in siman 71, paragraph 4, regarding how long their time extends. Initially, however, it is obvious that one should not delay Birchos HaShachar until after prayer, because that reverses the order of the spiritual rectification they accomplish. See Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaBerachos, chapter 3, and Nahar Shalom, folio 11c in the new edition. See also below at the end of paragraph 32.
