9) There — if he forgot and did not bless Birkhot haShachar before prayer, he blesses them after prayer, except for the blessing of ha-Machazir neshamos — he should not bless it, for he already discharged it with the blessing of Mechayeh hametim. And except for birchas haTorah, for he already discharged it with Ahavas olam, as will be explained in our words below with God's help on siman 47 seifim 7 and 8, and on the words of Morenu veRabbenu at the end of siman 52 — see there. And if he did not bless them even after prayer — until when is its time extended and he can bless — see below in our words for siman 71 letter 4; see Shulchan Aruch Harav. However, lekhatchilah it is obvious one should not omit Birkhot haShachar until after prayer, for it reverses the order of the correction made through them. See Peri Chadash, Shaar haBerachos ch. 3; Nahar Shalom 11b on Niddah 31a. And see also below at the end of letter 32.
