The Selichos. See siman 581 and what is written there.
And he fulfills his obligation through this. He should not intend to fulfill the obligation unless he fears that the congregation will pass the time for Krias Shema. If they will not pass the time, it is preferable to fulfill Krias Shema together with the congregation, reciting it properly with its blessings and juxtaposing redemption to prayer. Bach. Maharshal writes that one should also recite the first paragraph.
