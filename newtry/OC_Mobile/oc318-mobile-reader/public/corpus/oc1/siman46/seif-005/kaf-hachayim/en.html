5) One should not say "tehorah hi" — only "tehorah atah verachtah," etc., because for the most part one sins and it is not pure. Keser Gudal siman 5 letter 8. And likewise Shatz on Berachos 46a for other reasons — see there. Siddur Beit Yosef letter 2. Chayei Adam there. Ben Ish Chai there. And see Yafeh leLev letter 4 what he expanded on this — see there. And likewise in tractate Berachos 60b the word "hi" is absent; Shatz brought it there. And even though in Sha'ar haKavanos 2a it has "tehorah hi" — one can say it is not precise; and such is the custom that we do not say the word "hi."
