(20) He should not bless it — since he gave thanks that he is fully upright, this is included in the category of "who releases the bound," for he is immediately obligated when he sits. And there are disputants on this, and the Acharonim conclude to be lenient; and Peri Megadim wrote it is good that he hear afterward from another the blessing and intend to fulfill, and he fulfills in any case. If at the time he said "Blessed are You," his intention was to bless "who straightens the bent," he should finish "who straightens the bent," even though then he cannot bless "who releases the bound" — and all the more so in the rest of the blessings he should not be particular about the order, but finish as he thought at the time he mentioned the Name [Chayei Adam]. If when he reached the blessing "who opens the eyes of the blind" he wished to finish "who opens the eyes of the blind" but erred in his speech and finished "who clothes the naked," and within twenty-four hours remembered and finished "who opens the eyes of the blind" — some say he fulfills the blessing "who opens the eyes of the blind" through this, because through this he voided his first ending; and some are uncertain [Sha'arei Tefillah on this siman and Dagul meRevavah]. But if at the time he said "Blessed are You, Lord our God," he also thought to finish "who clothes the naked," and after he finished "who clothes the naked" he remembered within the time of speech and said "who opens the eyes of the blind" — for all opinions he fulfills "who clothes the naked" and returns and blesses "who opens the eyes of the blind," for order does not hinder. And likewise in all Birkhot haShachar alone, except the blessings "who releases the bound" and "who straightens the bent": if he preceded the blessing "who straightens the bent" and within the time of speech finished "who releases the bound" — even though through this only one blessing came out, and it is the first blessing of "who straightens the bent" — nevertheless he should not bless again "who releases the bound" [Peri Megadim in siman 59].
