And even — Taz and Magen Avraham letter 10. And behold regarding a convert whose birth was in holiness, Pri Chadash wrote he blesses shelo asani akum; and Beit Yosef in the name of the Rama implies we require his conception and birth in holiness — if his birth was in holiness he does not bless, for through his mother's choice he became a Jew — and needs study; and see siman 268 and 269 seif 4 on this.
