Peri Chadash writes that a convert whose birth occurred in sanctity may recite “Who did not make me a non-Jew.” Beit Yosef in the name of Ramah appears to require both conception and birth in sanctity. If only the birth occurred in sanctity, he should not recite the blessing because he became Jewish through his mother's choice. This requires study. See Yoreh Deah, simanim 268 and 269, seif 4.
