Magen Avraham, se'if katan 14: “A blind person should not recite the blessing.” My grandfather, the Gaon of blessed memory, writes in his work Mishnat DeRabbi Akiva Eiger that the text should be emended: a blind person should recite Poke'ach Ivrim, whereas a deaf person should not recite Hanoten LaSechvi Binah. He brings proof for this from siman 69.
