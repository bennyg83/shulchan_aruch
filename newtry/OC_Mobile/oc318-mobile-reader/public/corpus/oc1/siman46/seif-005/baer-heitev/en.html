He should not recite it. Since he already thanked Hashem for fully straightening him, Matir Asurim is included. The obligation to recite Matir Asurim arose immediately when he sat up. Maharshal and Bach disagree and hold that even if he first recited Zokef Kefufim, he should afterward recite Matir Asurim. See Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 117, who writes that now that the custom is to recite the blessings in order, there is no objection after the fact. Magen Avraham and Taz, however, rule leniently because of doubt concerning blessings. See Responsa Mateh Yosef, part 2, siman 8.
