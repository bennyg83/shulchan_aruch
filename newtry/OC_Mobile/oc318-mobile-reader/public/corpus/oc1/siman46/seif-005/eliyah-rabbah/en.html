(9) He should not recite it, etc. It has already been included in Zokef Kefufim. Bach rules that after the fact he should still recite it. He likewise holds that if one first recited “Who did not make me a slave,” he afterward recites “Who made me an Israelite,” and if he first recited “Who did not make me a woman,” he afterward recites “Who did not make me a slave.” Nachalas Tzvi and Knesses HaGedolah agree. Taz and Magen Avraham are lenient because of doubt concerning blessings. Regarding the blessings concerning a slave, a woman, and an Israelite, however, it appears that one should not be lenient. Shelah cautions that Malbish Arumim should be recited before Pokeach Ivrim. All our printed texts place Pokeach Ivrim first, as do our editions of the Gemara, Baal Halachos Gedolos, Rokeach, and Abudraham. I therefore do not see fit to alter the text. Taz writes that Zokef Kefufim should precede Matir Asurim. This requires study.
