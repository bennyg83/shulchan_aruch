[5] Roke'a ha'aretz, etc. — to give praise to the Omnipresent, blessed be He, that the earth is on the water contrary to nature. And that they say "Ozer Yisrael" and "Oter Yisrael" — because the belt is to separate from nakedness, and the hat is for fear of Heaven, which is not so with the other blessings that are for worldly benefit — all are equal; and likewise explained in Abudraham page 17. And from this reason it seems they needed to bless on them even though they were already included in malbish arumim.
