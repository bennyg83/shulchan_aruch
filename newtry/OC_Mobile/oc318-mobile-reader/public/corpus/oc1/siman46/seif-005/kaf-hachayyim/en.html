5) One should not say, "tehorah hi"; "it is pure," but only "tehorah; Atah verasah," etc., because in most cases a person has sinned and the soul is not pure. Kesher Gudal, siman 5, paragraph 8. Shaliach Tzibbur, folio 46a, writes likewise for other reasons; see there. Siddur Beis Oved, paragraph 2; Chesed LaAlafim there; Ben Ish Chai there. See Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 4, who discusses this at length. The word "hi" likewise does not appear in Berachos 60b, as Shaliach Tzibbur notes. Although Shaar HaKavanos, folio 2a, reads "tehorah hi," this need not be exact. The custom is not to say "hi."
