Even a convert. My father-in-law disagrees with Rama. A convert entered Judaism through his own choice and was not created by Hashem as a convert from the outset, so he should not recite this blessing. Rama's reasoning appears to be that a convert is considered like a newborn child, as stated in Yevamos 23. Upon conversion he becomes a new person. This is therefore a new act of creation, as though Hashem created him anew at that moment.
