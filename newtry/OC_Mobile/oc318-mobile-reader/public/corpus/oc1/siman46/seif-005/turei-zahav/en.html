Even a convert. Machatzis HaShekel of blessed memory disagrees with this ruling of Rama: that entering the religion of Israel is by his own choice, and He did not make him from the beginning of his creation a convert to accept the religion of Israel; therefore a convert should not say this blessing. The reason of Rama appears: a convert who converted is like a child just born, as we say in chapter 2 of Yevamos, page 23: when he converts he is another body — automatically it is well; now it is a new act as if He created him now anew.
