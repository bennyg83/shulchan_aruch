Magen Avraham — before they come to synagogue one says "And I..." etc. And the reason is that the word "ava" in the verse is future tense; therefore the verse must be said before his entry.

Magen Avraham on "And you shall love..." — for if there is, God forbid, division of hearts, it causes that, God forbid, the souls of Israel are not united above. But when each one of the people of Israel accepts upon himself to fulfill the positive commandment of "And you shall love..." and through their mutual love their bodies are united below, and it causes the unity and attachment of their souls above; and thereby their prayers also unite; and then when the prayer of all Israel is joined together, then it is desirable before Him, may He be blessed.
