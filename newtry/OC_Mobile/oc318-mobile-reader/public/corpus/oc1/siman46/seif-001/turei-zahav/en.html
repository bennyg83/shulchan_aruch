His hands over his eyes. It is difficult: afterward he counts "when he washes his hands," which implies that here he has not yet washed his hands — yet then it is forbidden to touch his eyes, as written in siman 4, note 3. One must say he did not list them in order. It may be explained that here we deal with one who places his hands over his eyes after putting a garment on his hand, and the way of the garment is that he places his hand through it. And also from the words of Rama, end of siman 47, it is implied that all these were said in order.
