(Note 1) Neshamah. Ba'er Heitev and Perishah brought proof for this from the wording of the Yerushalmi; and see above, siman 46, what was written; and see what Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of the siman — before coming to the synagogue one says "and I..." And in Pri Etz Chaim it seems he should say first "in the house of God..." He should feel and restrain himself in preparing to enter the synagogue from great fear, and wait and pause a little so as not to enter suddenly; and before he enters he says "and I..." and afterward he enters. When he enters the synagogue he bows toward the sanctuary and says "A psalm of God..." However, there it may be explained that he should not say it at all in "the house of God" but only have intent for it, as explained there — so it seems somewhat from the wording he brought in Shleimei Tzibur. But in Machatzis HaShekel he wrote that one says it and afterward says "and I." And also in Netiv Chayim he wrote to emend in Magen Avraham: before coming to the synagogue one says "in the house..." and upon entering one says "and I," etc. But really in Pri Etz Chaim it is explained that one must say it before entering, and after saying the verse he enters — and likewise in Machatzis HaShekel; and so it is in the Zohar, Bereishis and Vayikra: "A person should not enter the synagogue unless he first takes counsel..." — see there. And see in Sha'arei Teshuvah that he brought the wording of Maharach Or: after you say this verse at the entrance of the synagogue, then enter afterward into the synagogue, and do not say this verse after you have entered, for it is a mistake in the hands of those who practice thus — end quote.
