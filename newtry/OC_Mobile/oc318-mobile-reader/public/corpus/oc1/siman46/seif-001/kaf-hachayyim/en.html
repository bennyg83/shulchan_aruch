1) [Se'if 1] When he awakens from his sleep, etc. How good and pleasant it is if one can avoid speaking at all, so that his first speech upon rising from sleep begins with the mitzvah of blessing the Creator of his soul. This is exceedingly good. Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 2. See Moreh Be'Etzba, paragraph 63, and what we wrote above in siman 1, paragraph 40.
