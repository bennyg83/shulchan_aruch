(1) Elokai Neshamah. One should pause between the words Elokai and neshamah, and likewise between Elokai and netzor. The word tehorah should be followed by a pause, without pronouncing the final heh strongly, and one should not say “tehorah hi.” In “You created it,” the beis is soft, with a sheva, and the final heh is pronounced. The same applies to “You formed it,” with the yod bearing a sheva and the final heh pronounced. The words “You created,” “You formed,” “You breathed,” and all similar forms are pronounced with a kamatz. In the first “it,” the zayin has a kamatz. In “within me,” the second beis is soft. Maharash's siddur records the following vocalizations: in Mah Tovu and VeEchra, the reish has a kamatz. In Yigdal, metziuso has a shuruk under the alef, meaning the existence of Hashem; with a kamatz it would mean a found object. In ne'elam, the lamed has a kamatz. In ne'eman, the mem has a patach. One should not pause between daso and ra; the reish of ra has a kamatz, because ra modifies rasha. Bach writes in siman 38 that he saw the pious Rav Yeshayah read every morning, while wearing tefillin, the passages Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha. Since all four passages are written in the tefillin, he maintained that it is proper to read all four each day while wearing them.
