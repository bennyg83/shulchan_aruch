[1] Elokei neshamah, etc. One should pause between Elokei and neshamah, and likewise between Elokei and netzor tehorah — with an etnachta and without a mappik on the hei, and not "tehorah hi." "Atah verachtah" — both raish letters are soft and with shva na, and with mappik on the hei; and likewise "yetzartah" — vav with shva na and mappik on the hei. "Berachta, yetzarta, nafachta" — and likewise all with kamatz; "otah" the first time, "zeman" with kamatz; "bekirbi" — both raish letters soft. Maharash wrote in his siddur: in "mah tovu" and "ve'achre'ah" — raish with kamatz; in "yigdal" — "metzi'ato" the alef with melafum, meaning "found of" the Name, blessed be He; but with kamatz it implies finding that he found; "ne'elam" with kamatz under the lamed; "ne'eman" — mem with patach; do not pause between "dato"; "ra" — raish with kamatz, for ra attached to rasha, which is "rasha ra." Bach wrote in siman 38: I saw from the pious Maharash who would read every morning with tefillin on his head parashas Kadesh and parashas "Vehayah ki yevi'acha," and said: since four parshiyos are written in tefillin, it is fitting to read all four every day while tefillin are on his head.
