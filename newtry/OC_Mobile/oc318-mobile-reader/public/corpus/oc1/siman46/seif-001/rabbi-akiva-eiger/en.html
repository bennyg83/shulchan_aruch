Magen Avraham, se'if katan 5: “Perhaps they are adjacent.” I do not understand the uncertainty. The two blessings HaMa'avir Sheinah and Yehi Ratzon are adjacent blessings, since the Sages instituted both of them upon washing one's face. What difference does it make that the other blessings were each instituted independently? Every blessing has a specified time, as the Talmud says, “When he reaches,” etc.; similarly, it says, “When he washes his face,” etc. If these two blessings were both instituted upon washing the face, since no other time was assigned to either of them, they are necessarily adjacent blessings. This requires study.
