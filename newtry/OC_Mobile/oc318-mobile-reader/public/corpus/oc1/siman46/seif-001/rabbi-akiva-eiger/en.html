Magen Avraham seif kaf heh: perhaps they are adjacent. I did not understand — perhaps those two blessings, haMa'avir sheinah and yehi ratzon, are adjacent, for the Sages instituted both of them upon washing his face; and what of it that the other blessings were instituted each independently? Behold, each blessing has a known time, as we say — when he reaches "and give," when he washes his face, etc. And if so, if they are two blessings instituted both upon washing his face — for they did not assign another time — if so, they are in any case adjacent, and needs study.
