Soul. If he recited the blessing, “Blessed is He Who revives the dead,” instead of Elokai Neshamah, he has fulfilled his obligation. Peri Chadash.
When he dresses. This does not specifically mean that he slept unclothed. Rather, when he puts on his outer garment he recites this blessing. Peri Chadash.
His eyes. Meaning, through a cloth, because he may not place his hands upon his eyes before washing, as explained in siman 4.
Who prepares. Our custom is to say, “Who prepared.”
