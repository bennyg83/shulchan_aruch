(In Taz, note 6) And the Tur wrote, etc. — meaning, the Tur proved thus: if it were that at the time of the Talmud they were exempt from malbish arumim when saying zokef kefufim, then at this time when blessings are said in order in the synagogue it would suffice with zokef kefufim alone — and he does not hold, as Taz wrote according to R' Amram, that one may not change from the time of the Talmud, since even at the time of the Talmud one could not exempt himself with zokef kefufim alone. And it is possible there is slight lack in the Taz's wording, and one must say: since nevertheless at the time of the Talmud each had a special time, for us too the obligation of each blessing applies at its time, and one cannot exempt himself with a later blessing — and this is not so at the time of the Talmud, etc. And indeed even without emendation his intent is as I wrote.
