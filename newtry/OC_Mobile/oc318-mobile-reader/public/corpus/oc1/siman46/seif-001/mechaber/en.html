The laws of the morning blessings and other blessings. It contains nine sections: When a person
wakes from his sleep, he says Elohei neshamah. When he hears the voice of a rooster, he blesses 'who gives understanding to the heart.' When he gets dressed, he blesses 'who clothes the naked.' When he places his hands over his eyes, he blesses 'who opens the eyes of the blind.' When he sits, he blesses 'who releases the bound.' When he stands upright, he blesses 'who straightens the bowed.' When he places his feet on the ground, he blesses 'who spreads the earth upon the waters.' When he puts on his shoes, he blesses 'who has provided me with all my needs.' When he walks, he blesses 'who prepares the steps of man.' When he girds his belt, he blesses 'who girds Israel with strength.'Or if he wears the sash that separates between his heart and his nakedness (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ravad).} When he places a hat or turban on his head, he blesses 'who crowns Israel with glory.' When he washes his hands, he blesses on the washing of hands. When he washes his face, he blesses 'who removes sleep from my eyes,' etc., and 'may it be Your will,' etc., until 'Blessed are You, Hashem, who bestows good kindnesses on His people Israel.'
