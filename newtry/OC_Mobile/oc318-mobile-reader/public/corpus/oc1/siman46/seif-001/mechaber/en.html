The laws of the morning blessings and other blessings. It contains 9 seifim: When he awakens from his sleep he says Elohai Neshamah. When he hears the rooster's call he recites the blessing Hanoten LaSechvi Vinah. When he dresses he recites Malbish Arumim. When he passes his hands over his eyes he recites Pokeach Ivrim. When he sits up he recites Matir Asurim. When he straightens himself he recites Zokef Kefufim. When he places his feet on the ground he recites Roka HaAretz Al HaMayim. When he puts on his shoes he recites SheAsah Li Kol Tzorki. When he walks he recites HaMechin Mitzadei Gaver. When he girds his belt he recites Ozer Yisrael BiGevurah. {Rama: Or when he puts on the sash that separates between his heart and his ervah. [Beit Yosef in the name of Ra'avad]} When he places a hat or turban on his head he recites Oter Yisrael BeTifarah. When he washes his hands he recites Al Netilat Yadayim. When he washes his face he recites HaMa'avir Sheinah MeEinai, etc., and Yehi Ratzon, etc., through the conclusion, 'Blessed are You, Hashem, who bestows good kindnesses upon His people Israel.' One should not answer Amen after HaMa'avir Sheinah MeEinai until he concludes 'who bestows good kindnesses upon His people Israel,' because it is all 1 blessing.
