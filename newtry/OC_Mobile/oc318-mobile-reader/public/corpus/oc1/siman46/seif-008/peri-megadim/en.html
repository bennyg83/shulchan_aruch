After all — Taz: I did not find a resolution for this; there, letter 5, he wrote only as supplications, and it is mentioned here; and regarding the shaliach tzibbur one can say he does so to discharge others with birchas haTorah. But householders are correct to say immediately when he washes [hands], Elokai neshamah, asher yatzar, and birchas haTorah, as the Rambam wrote here.
