(24) Even so — see in Sefer Eliyahu Rabbah who wrote that the blessing of Elokei neshamah and the blessing of ha-Ma'avir sheinah one should not bless if he was awake all night; and in Peri Megadim and Sha'arei Teshuvah they left his words in doubt, and conclude in Sha'arei Tefillah that one should hear these two blessings from another and intend to fulfill. And if he slept at night sixty breaths — for all opinions he should bless them.

(25) One is not obligated — and even in a desert where one does not hear a rooster crowing one blesses. A blind person blesses the blessing of pokeiach chovrim, and likewise a deaf person — even though he cannot hear a rooster crowing — may bless the blessing of HaNoten laSechvi binah [Derech Hashem]; and in Chayei Adam it is written that a deaf person should wait from blessing this blessing until daybreak.

(26) One blesses them — see below in siman 47 in Mishna Berurah seif kaf alef, and in Biur Halacha there what we wrote in the name of Peri Megadim.
