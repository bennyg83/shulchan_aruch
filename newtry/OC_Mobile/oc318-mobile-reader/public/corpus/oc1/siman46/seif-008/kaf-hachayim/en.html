8) There — one says Elokei neshamah. And if he blessed Mechayeh hametim in place of Elokei neshamah, he has fulfilled his obligation. Peri Chadash. Yad Aharon in Hagahot Taz. Keser Gudal siman 5 letter 9. Shas Berachos 46b. Siddur Beit Yosef in the laws of Birkhot haShachar letter 2; Shitei Zeitim letter 1. And although the Maamer, letter 1, doubted the words of Peri Chadash, nevertheless at the end he concluded there is reason to heed his words — see there. And Bach on Shas there. And in particular, since doubtful blessings are ruled leniently, and thus ruled the later authorities.
