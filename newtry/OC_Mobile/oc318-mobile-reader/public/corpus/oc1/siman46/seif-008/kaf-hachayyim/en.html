8) There: He should say Elokai Neshamah. If one recited Mechayeh HaMeisim in place of Elokai Neshamah, he has fulfilled his obligation. Pri Chadash; Yad Aharon, Hagahos on the Tur; Kesher Gudal, siman 5, paragraph 9; Shaliach Tzibbur, folio 46b; Siddur Beis Oved, laws of Birchos HaShachar, paragraph 2; Shesilei Zeitim, paragraph 1. Although Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1, questions Pri Chadash, he concludes that one should take account of his view. Shaliach Tzibbur there cites this. In particular, doubts concerning blessings are ruled leniently, and the later authorities rule accordingly.
