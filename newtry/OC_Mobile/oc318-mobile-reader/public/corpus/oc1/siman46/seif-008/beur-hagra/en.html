Seif 8: All, etc.; and some say, etc. Ran writes this in the name of Ramban, and Kol Bo in the name of the Geonim. Kol Bo writes that this custom became widespread. Rambam, however, strongly objects and writes that one should not recite any blessing, even Asher Nasan LaSechvi, unless the relevant event occurred. Tosafos there, s.v. “when he spreads,” Rosh, and Tur write likewise. Tosafos, however, writes under s.v. “when he hears” that this blessing may be recited even if he did not hear. Rosh writes likewise. Tur adds Elokai Neshamah, Roka HaAretz, and HaMeichin Mitzadei Gaver, because they too concern the ordering of the world. Beit Yosef writes that because the matter is disputed, these blessings should be recited without Hashem's Name and sovereignty. Darkei Moshe writes that one should not change from the accepted custom, as stated above.
