Seif 8: All [agree one way] and some say [another] — Thus wrote the Ran in the name of the Ramban and Kol Bo in the name of the Geonim, and he wrote that such is the simple meaning of the custom; but the Rambam protested on this and wrote that one should not bless — meaning any blessing, even the blessing "Who has given us," etc. — and likewise in Tosafos there s.v. ki peris, etc., and the Rosh and Tur; except that he wrote in s.v. ki shama, etc., that in the synagogue, even if he did not hear [the chazzan] — and likewise wrote the Rosh; and the Tur added Elokei neshamah, rokea, hamachin, etc., that they too are on the order of the world. Beit Yosef wrote: since there is a dispute, one says it without [intent to fulfill an obligation], etc. Darkei Moshe wrote: and it seems to me one should not change from the custom — and likewise above.
