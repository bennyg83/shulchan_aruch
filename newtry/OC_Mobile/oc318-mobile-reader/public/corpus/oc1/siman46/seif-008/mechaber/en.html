All these blessings—if one was not obligated in one of them, for example if he did not hear the voice of a rooster, or did not walk, or did not get dressed, or did not gird himself—he says that blessing without mentioning the Name.And there are those who say' that even if he was not obligated in them he blesses them, for the blessing is not specifically about himself but rather we bless that the Holy One, blessed be He, created the needs of the world; and so is the practice, and we do not change [Tur and Tosafot haRosh, chapter 'One who sees,' and Ran, first chapter of Pesachim, and Kol Bo].]:
