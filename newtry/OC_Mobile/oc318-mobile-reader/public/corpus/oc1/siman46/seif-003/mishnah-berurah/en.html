(14) Blessings — The reason is that a hundred souls from Israel died every day; David the Holy Prophet, may his memory be blessed, therefore enacted to bless a hundred blessings every day. And behold, every day we bless even more than a hundred blessings. How? At night when he goes to sleep he blesses ha-Mafill; and at dawn he blesses on netilas yadayim and Asher yatzar, and another sixteen blessings in Birkhot haShachar, and three blessings on the Torah [per Rama below siman 47 seif 6 in Hagah] — behold twenty-two. And the blessing of tzitzit and tefillin — behold three more, according to our custom that we bless two on tefillin — behold twenty-five. And Baruch she'amar and Yishtabach — behold two more. And the blessing of Kerias Shema, Shacharit and Arvit, with the blessing "May our eyes see," which are eight — behold thirty-five. And fifty-seven blessings of the three prayers — behold ninety-two. And in two meals that one eats, one in the evening and one in the morning, there are sixteen blessings, because at every meal there are eight blessings: on netilas yadayim and ha-motzi and four blessings in Birkhat haMazon; and when he drinks a cup in Birkhat haMazon he blesses before and after — behold in total 108 blessings. If so, even on a fast day when he lacks one meal he likewise fulfills a hundred blessings. And on Shabbat he lacks thirteen blessings from the hundred, as stated in Magen Avraham — see there; therefore he should make them up with various fruits and delicacies. And if he does not have them, he fulfills through pressing circumstances by intending to hear the blessing of the Torah and the haftarah and answering amen, as below siman 284. And Magen Avraham wrote that one should not put himself in doubt of an unnecessary blessing because of the mitzvah of a hundred blessings. And on Yom Kippur one likewise fulfills through hearing blessings as on Shabbat; but on Yom Kippur, after all the accounts, he still lacks three more blessings. And Magen Avraham wrote that one should complete this with the blessing on spices for fragrance; but all this is when he did not divert his attention from smelling — it is forbidden to repeat the blessing because of an unnecessary blessing. And likewise he can complete them with the blessing of Asher yatzar if the opportunity arises. And it is possible he fulfills through pressing circumstances by intending to hear the repetition of the shaliach tzibbur.
