His eyes. Ba'er Heitev; and see in Avudraham in the name of Perishah, who wrote that this means while his eyes are closed or when he desires sleep — the way to rub is on the skin of the eye, for thereby one removes the bonds of sleep from his eyes — see there. And Avudraham wrote that it is implied one may do this with the hand itself. And see above, siman 4, in Mishnah Berurah in the name of Mor uKetzia that from outside is permitted; and he challenged this from the words of the later authorities here, and wrote that so it seems in the Zohar, Vayishlach, and in Alfasi Zuta to the Rambam that even from outside is forbidden — see there. And see below, siman 249, in Magen Avraham: if one blessing Birchot HaShachar reached pokeach ivrim, erred and said malbish arumim, and returned — there is doubt whether he fulfilled pokeach ivrim; but if he intended malbish arumim, he fulfilled malbish arumim. And in responsa Panim Me'irot part 1, siman 58, he wondered at this, for it is the same law explained in Shulchan Aruch there regarding taking a cup of wine, etc., that he fulfilled; and therefore emend: there is doubt whether he fulfilled malbish arumim — see there in Yad Efraim that one need not emend; and the matters as written, for he is in doubt here, since in truth the obligation rests upon him to say malbish arumim — one should not consider what he said malbish arumim as if it did not exist; unlike there, where in such a case it has no relevance here and is considered as if it did not exist. And therefore what he wrote "he intended malbish arumim" means likewise that he erred and said pokeach ivrim and returned and said malbish arumim — in this he fulfilled, for what he said in the middle pokeach ivrim is as if it did not exist, since he had already blessed pokeach ivrim — see there; and what he wrote in the name of Degel Machaneh Efraim.
