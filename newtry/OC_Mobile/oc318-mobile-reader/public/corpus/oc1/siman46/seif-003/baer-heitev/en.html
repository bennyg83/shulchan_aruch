On Yom Kippur some poskim wrote to take hadasim and spices and from hour to hour smell and bless. But it is impossible to bless each time unless with hesech ha-da'at. And some poskim wrote that it is not proper to smell a pleasant fragrance on a day when the Torah commanded self-affliction; therefore it is better to rely on the poskim who count answering Amen to the chazzan's repetition and answering Amen after those called to the Torah and the haftarah toward completing the hundred blessings, and then he need not take spices or myrtle to smell them. See Magen Avraham and Be'er Hagolah Mitrani part 1 siman 104.
