(Ibid. seif kaf tes) However in Sanhedrin. Author's note: However, we do not abandon what is doubtful to us — for per the Taz's explanation it implies that even if he blessed she'asani Yisrael he still needs to bless shelo asani isha and eved; however if he blessed shelo asani isha he should not again bless for gentile and slave, as the Magen Avraham wrote, for doubtful blessings we rule leniently, and not as Be'er Heitev explained well.
