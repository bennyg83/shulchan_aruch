One hundred blessings — Taz; and Gemara Menachos 43b, Tannaitic teaching: R' Meir says, etc., and Rashi explains "if so, only a hundred." And see there letter 1. And see Pri Chadash what he wrote on this, and in siman 193 and 213 — a ritually clean person fulfills through his fellow's blessing, and Bach implies there is danger of death — an expert should not rely on his fellow and answer Amen; rather he should say "You are..." to fulfill one hundred blessings. And see siman 46 seif 3; and if so, on a fast day and the like where he fears a wonder, he should bless and hear fifteen blessings and answer Amen to Gadol, etc., and fulfill through compulsion in this; and see there, with God's help, in Magen Avraham.
