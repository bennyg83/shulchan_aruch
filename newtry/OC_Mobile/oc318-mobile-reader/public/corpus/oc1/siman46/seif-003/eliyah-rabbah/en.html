[3] When he puts on, etc. — meaning by means of a sheet or tunic, for he has not yet washed his hands; and we hold in siman 4: "The hand to the eye — cut it off"; and likewise wrote Ma'adanei Melech page 73 and Shelah page 128. Perishah wrote in his own words: it seems to me he meant to say while his eyes are closed — the way of the world is to pass his hand over them, or after he opens them and still desires sleep — the way of the world is to close them and touch the skin of the eye, by which he removes ropes of sleep from his eyes — up to here; and it implies one may do this with the hand itself.
