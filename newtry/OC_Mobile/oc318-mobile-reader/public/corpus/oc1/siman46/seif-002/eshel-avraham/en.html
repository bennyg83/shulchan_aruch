Hands. Avraham ben haRambam s.k. 5; and see responsum Maharshadam siman 145; Rada wrote: that they instituted the blessings in second person and third person because the Holy One, blessed be He, is revealed and hidden from the side of divinity; and the soul too appears and is hidden — one blesses in second and third person, etc. — see there. He also wrote: if one can add beyond one hundred blessings, this is praiseworthy, as David said "and I have added to Your praise."
