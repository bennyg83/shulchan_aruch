His obligation. See Baer Heitev, seif katan 5, and Responsa Maharshdam, siman 145. Abudraham writes that the blessings were instituted partly in direct address and partly in third-person language because the Holy One, blessed is He, is both revealed and hidden in relation to His Divinity. The soul too is at times perceptible and at times concealed. Therefore the blessing combines direct and indirect address. He further writes that one who can add beyond one hundred blessings is praiseworthy, as David said, “And I shall add to all Your praise.”
