(2) Sechvi means the heart or the rooster. The beis of binah is soft. The tzadi of tzorki has a kamatz, and the kaf is strong. The lamed of yishlot has a patach. In Mah Chayeinu and Mah Chasdeinu, both mems have a segol. The tav of berisecha has a segol. In ahavcha, the heh has a patach and the beis a silent sheva. The reish of bechorecha has a segol. In ba'elyonim and batachtonim, the first beis has a kamatz and the second a patach.
