[2] Shachvi — meaning a heart or a rooster. "Binah" — the beis is soft; "tzorki hatzaddik" with kamatz; kaf with dagesh; "yishlot" — lamed with patach; "mah chayyeinu mah chasdeinu" — both mems with segol; "berisach" — tav with segol; "ahavtach" — the hei with patach and the beis with shva na; "bechorech" — raish with segol; "ba'elyonim batachtonim" — the first beis with kamatz and the second with patach.
