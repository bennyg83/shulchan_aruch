(11) They practiced arranging them. Whether one may likewise postpone Al Netilas Yadayim and arrange it in the synagogue is discussed above in siman 6 and in Biur Halachah there.
(12) And they answer amen. After the fact, even if they did not answer amen they fulfill the blessing, as explained below in siman 213, provided they intended to fulfill their obligation.
(13) Their obligation. Even one who is proficient may be discharged by another who intends to include him. Later authorities. See above at the end of siman 6, where Magen Avraham cites Levush as requiring ten people for one person to discharge others in the morning blessings. See below in siman 59, seif 4, in Biur Halachah, where this matter is discussed. Nowadays the custom is for each person to recite the blessings independently, and the chazzan does not discharge anyone. Olas Tamid in siman 6.
