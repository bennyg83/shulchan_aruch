(11) The practice is to arrange them— and whether it is also permitted to wait with the blessing on washing hands to arrange it in the synagogue, see above in siman 6 and in Beur Halacha.

(12) And they answer amen — and be'di'eved, even if they did not answer amen, they fulfill the blessing, as below siman 213, since they intended to fulfill.

(13) Their obligation — and even an expert who fulfills the obligation for others, if he intends in his blessing [Acharonim]; and see above at the end of siman 6 in Magen Avraham who brought in the name of Levush to be stricter in Birkhat haShachar that one does not fulfill for others with fewer than ten; and see below siman 59 seif 4 in Beur Halacha there what we wrote about this. And in our time the custom is that each one blesses for himself, and the shaliach tzibbur does not fulfill anyone's obligation [Etz Yosef in siman 6].
