When he dresses. See Ba'er Heitev. See below, siman 223: When one puts on a new garment, he should recite Malbish Arumim. Eliyah Rabbah writes there that even if he already recited the blessing in the morning, he recites it again when he puts on the garment. If he put it on in the morning, however, it is covered by that blessing; see there. According to what Ba'er Heitev writes in the name of Halakhot Ketanot; that one who buys a hat should recite Oter Yisrael, and one who buys a belt should recite Ozer Yisrael; and Menuchah's comment that these should be said without the Divine Name and kingship, a person who is meticulous in his conduct should put them on in the morning and intend to exempt them when reciting these blessings. This is preferable to reciting them without the Divine Name and kingship.
