When he gets dressed. Ba'er Heitev; and see below, siman 223: when wearing a new garment one says malbish arumim; and in Avudraham there — even though he already blessed in the morning, he repeats and blesses when he wears it; but if he wore it in the morning he is discharged with this blessing — see there. And it seems according to what Av Beit Din wrote in the name of Leket Yosher: one who buys a hat blesses "oter Yisrael," and for a belt "ozer Yisrael"; and Minhag writes on this that one says it without God's name and kingship. One who is punctilious in his actions should wear it in the morning and intend to discharge it by saying these blessings — and this is preferable to saying it without God's name and kingship.
