Ozer Yisrael beGevurah. He mentioned Yisrael in this blessing, and likewise in "oter Yisrael" — unlike the others. The reason is that the rest are for worldly benefit in which all are equal, whereas these two are for Yisrael alone, because of modesty: the belt is so one's heart should not see nakedness, as Beit Yosef wrote; and the hat is because of modesty, as explained in Shabbos, chapter Mi sheHechshich: "Cover your head because of the fear of Heaven."
