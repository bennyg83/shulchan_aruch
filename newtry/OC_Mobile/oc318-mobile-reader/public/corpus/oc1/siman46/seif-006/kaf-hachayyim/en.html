This blessing should contain forty-seven words, corresponding to the combined numerical values of the Divine Names Havayah and Ehyeh, which represent Abba and Imma, who are never separated. See Nahar Shalom, folio 11c in the new edition, and what we wrote above in siman 6, paragraph 3. Shaliach Tzibbur, folio 46a, and Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 3, write likewise. This is the text:
6) My God, the soul You placed within me is pure. You created it; You formed it; You breathed it into me, and You preserve it within me. You will ultimately take it from me and restore it to me in the future. As long as the soul is within me, I thank You, Hashem my God and God of my ancestors, Master of all deeds, Lord of all souls. Blessed are You, Hashem, Who restores souls to lifeless bodies. Chesed LaAlafim there. This is unlike Ateres Zekenim to siman 6 and Siddur Beis Oved, paragraph 3, which follows him and says that the text should be "You breathed it," omitting "into me." Shaliach Tzibbur there rejects Ateres Zekenim and writes that the common custom is correct. This is also the text in Berachos 60b and in Shaar HaKavanos, as stated in the following paragraph.
