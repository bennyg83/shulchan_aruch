6) There he says Elokei neshamah. This blessing must contain forty-seven words, corresponding to the numerical value of Havayah Ehyeh, which are Abba and Imma that are not separated forever. See Nahar Shalom page 11b, Niddah 31a, and Shulchan Aruch Harav siman 6 letter 3. And likewise Shatz on Berachos 46a, Chayei Adam letter 3: (and this is the wording)

6) Elokei neshamah shenattata bi tehorah atah verachtah, atah yetzartah, atah nafachtah bi, ve'attah meshamerah bekirbi, ve'attah atid litlah mimeni ulehachzirah bi le'atid lavo. Kol zeman sheneshamah bekirbi modeh ani lefanecha Hashem Elohai ve'Elohei avosai, Ribon kol hama'asim, Adon kol haneshamos. Baruch attah Hashem, hamechazir neshamos lifgarim meisim. Chayei Adam there. And not like Etz Yosef siman 6 and the siddur Beit Yosef letter 3 that followed him, who wrote one must say "atah nafachtah" and not say "atah nafachtah bi"; and likewise Shatz there rejected the words of Etz Yosef and wrote that such is the custom of the world and such is the essential law — see there. And likewise in Shas Berachos 60b. And likewise in Sha'ar haKavanos as written in the next letter.
