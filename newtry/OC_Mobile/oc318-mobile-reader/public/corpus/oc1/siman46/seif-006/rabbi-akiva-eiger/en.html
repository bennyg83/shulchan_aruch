Magen Avraham, se'if katan 16: “Unless he fears that the congregation will let the time pass.” In my humble opinion, if he fears that the congregation will let the time for Keriat Shema pass, he should form a condition: if they let the time pass, he intends to fulfill his obligation with this recitation; if they do not let it pass, he does not intend to fulfill his obligation with it. This is like the ruling of Beit Yosef in siman 489 concerning counting the Omer.
