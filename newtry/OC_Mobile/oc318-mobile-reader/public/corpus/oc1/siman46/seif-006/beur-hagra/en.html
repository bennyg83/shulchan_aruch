Seif 6: Some have the custom, etc. Tur, based upon the Midrash. See Yalkut Tehillim, siman 702, and Tosafos 46a, s.v. “All.”
And one should not, etc. One may not institute a new blessing after the close of the Gemara, as Rosh writes in the first chapter of Kiddushin. This is the basis of the statement in seif 7, “Some have the custom,” etc.
But the custom, etc. As stated in the Yerushalmi and Maseches Sofrim, a custom can override a halachic ruling. This principle appears in several places in the Gemara.
