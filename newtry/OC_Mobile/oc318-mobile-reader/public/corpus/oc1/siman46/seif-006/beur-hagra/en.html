Seif 6: There are those who practice [saying additional verses] — Tur, and per the midrash; see Yalkut Tehillim siman 542, and see Tosafos Berachos 46a s.v. kol.

And one may not [innovate a new blessing] — for one may not institute a new blessing after the Gemara, as Rosh wrote in the first chapter of Kiddushin; and this is what he wrote in seif 7, "There are those who practice," etc.

But the custom [prevails] — as written in the Yerushalmi and in the Mishnah, "custom uproots halachah," and in many places in Shas.
