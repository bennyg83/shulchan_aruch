See Taz, who discusses this at length and reinforces his position in Magen Avraham, ot 4: the blessings Matir Asurim and Zokef Kefufim are not redundant because their obligations arise at different times. This is Tur's reasoning: even if one happened to delay and recited Zokef Kefufim first, the obligation of Matir Asurim did not lapse, because it had already arisen at a time when he was not yet obligated in Zokef Kefufim. We, however, rule in accordance with the Mechaber. See the laws of Birkat HaMazon and blessings over fruit regarding whether Birkat HaMazon exempts Borei Nefashot; with God's help, this will be explained there. Regarding what Taz writes in Bach's name: according to Taz's approach, the same question would apply to Tur, and he must therefore answer that all of these blessings are necessary. When he concludes, 'But the primary order is Zokef Kefufim before Matir Asurim,' he means that Rokeach holds this. We, however, rule like the Mechaber that Matir Asurim precedes Zokef Kefufim; and if one first recited Zokef Kefufim, he does not afterward recite Matir Asurim. See Eliyah Rabbah, ot 9. According to what I have written, this is understandable.
