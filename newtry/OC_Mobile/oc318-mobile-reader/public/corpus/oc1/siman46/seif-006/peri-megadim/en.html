Before — Taz extended and strengthened his words in letter 4 of Magen Avraham; and Eliyah Rabbah [holds] one does not combine blessings that apply at alternating times — and this is the Tur's reason, that even if it happened he waited and said Eliyah Rabbah, the obligation of Magen Avraham did not expire, for he was already obligated at a time when he was not obligated in Eliyah Rabbah. And we hold per the Mechaber's ruling; and see in Hilchos Berachos whether birchas hamazon exempts in a kli rishon, and with God's help it will be explained there. And what he wrote in the name of Bach — behold, on the Taz's method this would be difficult for the Tur; therefore explain that all are needed, and what he concludes "but the main thing" — Eliyah Rabbah before Magen Avraham: the Rokeach held so, but we hold per the Mechaber's ruling that Magen Avraham precedes Eliyah Rabbah, and if he preceded and blessed Eliyah Rabbah he does not bless Magen Avraham; and see Eliyah Rabbah letter 9, and according to what I wrote, if so.
