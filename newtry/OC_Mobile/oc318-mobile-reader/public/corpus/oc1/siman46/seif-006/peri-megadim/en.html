They were incurred at different times. Accordingly, even if one delayed and first recited SheAsani Yisrael, the earlier obligation did not lapse, because it arose at a different time. Magen Avraham rules like the Mechaber. See the laws of Bircas HaMazon and blessings over food concerning whether Bircas HaMazon exempts Borei Nefashos. With Hashem's help, it will be explained there. Taz's challenge from Bach equally applies to Tur. Taz must therefore answer that all the blessings remain necessary. When he concludes that the primary order is first SheAsani Yisrael and afterward the other blessing, he means that Rokeach follows this order. We, however, rule like the Mechaber that the blessing over the earlier distinction comes first. If he first recited SheAsani Yisrael, he should not recite the other afterward. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 9. According to what I have written, the matter is resolved.
