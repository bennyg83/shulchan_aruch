(6) Clean hands, etc. This is difficult. If so, why did they say Al Netilas Yadayim? According to Rosh, the blessing was instituted specifically because the hands are ordinarily soiled, and this is Levush's first reason in siman 4. Rabbeinu Yonah writes: “They were holy, and therefore were careful to wash their hands and stand in a condition in which they could recite the blessings in cleanliness.” See Me'adanei Melech, page 77.
