[6] Ha'yadayim nekiyos, etc. — it is difficult: if so, why did they say on netilas yadayim "this" — it is specifically because of dirty hands for Rosh, and this is the first reason Levush wrote in siman 4. Rabbeinu Yonah's language: they were holy and therefore careful and washed their hands and stood in a state whereby they could say the blessings in cleanliness — up to here; and see Ma'adanei Melech page 77.
