7) There he says Elokei neshamah. The blessing Elokei neshamah shenattata bi, etc., is adjoined to its companion because it corresponds to Imma who is always joined with Abba — and this is the son. And behold, even though not every person merits the part called neshamah, nevertheless he has a part in the neshamah of Adam haRishon who included all creatures, even though this person has not yet merited it himself. And his saying "tehorah" corresponds to the world of Atzilus where Moshe gave neshamah to neshamah. "Atah verachtah" corresponds to the part of neshamah that comes from Beriah. "Atah yetzartah" is the secret of ruach that comes from Yetzirah. "Atah nafachtah bi" is the secret of nefesh in Asiyah. It turns out that by saying "neshamah shenattata bi" he includes all four parts mentioned and calls them all by the name neshamah. "And You will take it from me," etc., is in the secret of mochin, for in the future Malchus will take the souls of the righteous in the secret of "renewed every morning," etc., so they will be in it in the aspect of mochin; and afterward "You will return it to me in the morning" in the secret of "renewed every morning." Sha'ar haKavanos 2a. And see Drushei haLeilah, drush 3 page 53, and you will understand. And see Shatz page 45d and on Berachos 46a who wrote another matter on this — see there. And see in siddur Beit Yosef and Chayei Adam and other siddurim who wrote that when saying Elokei neshamah one should intend the combination of the letters of neshamah with the letters of Shemo. And see Yafeh leLev letter 3; and since the Arizal did not write this and we do not practice it, therefore we wrote nothing on this.
