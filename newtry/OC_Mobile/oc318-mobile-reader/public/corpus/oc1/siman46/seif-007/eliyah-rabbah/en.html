[7] Ha'amei ha'aretz, etc. — it implies he does not know a little; and likewise implied in Tur; and Bach wrote in his own words: do not rely on answering Amen, for there is danger of death, etc. And even though in the remnants of Knesset HaGedolah he wrote in the name of Sefer Berachas Avraham that one fulfills, and likewise implied below siman 58 seif 4 in Yotzer Or — nevertheless one must be stringent in this as Bach's words. And if he hears the blessings from another before he blesses, he should intend not to fulfill; nevertheless he answers Amen. Levush wrote: even though we hold "all blessings follow their performance" — that is for mitzvah blessings; but these are blessings of praise — one may bless afterward — up to here; and it requires investigation. See below siman 224 in the blessing of the king that one does not bless afterward even though it is a blessing of praise; and Ran at the end of the first chapter of Pesachim wrote the reason: because they are blessings on the custom of the world. Shelah wrote page 198: nevertheless reason dictates that we bless them as close to washing as possible and not interrupt between blessing and washing as with other blessings.
