And there is no — Taz; and what he concludes in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, perek 8 of Kiddushin 41a — see there — that women bless on a positive commandment that they are exempt from, and proof from doubtful Kerias Shema — see there. And I say: behold we hold in siman 265 seif daled that whoever blesses a blessing he need not [say], he transgresses "you shall not take [the name in vain]" (Magen Avraham there — whether d'oraisa or d'rabbanan). And that [case] of women blessing on a positive commandment they are exempt from — since men are obligated and doubtful reading of Kerias Shema one blesses, for doubtful Torah [matters] we rule stringently, and one must read Shema in any case they bless upon it — and needs study; and see siman 64 regarding doubtful reading of Kerias Shema that one repeats and blesses, and see Taz and Magen Avraham who extended on this.
