And one does not. See Taz. What he concludes in the name of Rabbeinu Tam in the first chapter of Kiddushin, page 31, is that women recite blessings upon positive time-bound commandments. He brings proof from one who is uncertain whether he recited Krias Shema. I do not understand the proof. We rule in siman 215, seif 4, that one who recites an unnecessary blessing violates “You shall not take the Name,” whether D'Oraisa or D'Rabbanan according to Magen Avraham there. Women may recite blessings upon positive time-bound commandments because men are obligated in them. One who is uncertain whether he recited Krias Shema recites the blessing because a doubt concerning a Torah obligation is ruled stringently, and he must repeat Krias Shema. The blessing then follows. This requires study. See siman 64 concerning one uncertain whether he recited Krias Shema, where he repeats it with its blessings. See Taz and Magen Avraham, who discuss this at length.
