7) There: He should say Elokai Neshamah. The blessing "Elokai Neshamah shenasata bi," etc., is adjacent to the preceding blessing because it corresponds to Imma, which is always joined with Abba. Understand this. Although not every person merits the component called neshamah, he nevertheless possesses a share in the soul of Adam HaRishon, which encompassed all created beings, even if this individual has not yet personally attained it. "Tehorah" corresponds to the world of Atzilus, from which comes the neshamah of the neshamah. "You created it" corresponds to the component of neshamah that comes from Beriah. "You formed it" alludes to ruach, which comes from Yetzirah. "You breathed it into me" alludes to nefesh, which is in Asiyah. Thus, when saying "the soul You placed within me," he includes all four components and calls them all neshamah. "You will ultimately take it from me," etc., concerns the mystical elevation of feminine waters: in the future Malchus takes the souls of the righteous in the mystery of "new every morning," so that they become feminine waters within it; afterward, "You will ultimately restore it to me" in the morning, in the mystery of "new every morning." Shaar HaKavanos, folio 2a. See the discourses of the night, discourse 3, folio 53, and understand. See Shaliach Tzibbur, folios 45d and 46a, who gives another explanation. Siddur Beis Oved, Chesed LaAlafim, and other siddurim write that when saying Elokai Neshamah one should intend an interweaving of the letters of neshamah with the letters of his name. See Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 3. Since the Arizal did not write this and we do not practice it, we have written nothing further about it.
