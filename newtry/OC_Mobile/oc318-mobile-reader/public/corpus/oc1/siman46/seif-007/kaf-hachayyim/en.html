7) There, he should say Elokai Neshamah. The blessing "Elokai, the soul You placed within me," etc., is adjacent to the preceding blessing because it corresponds to Imma, which is always joined with Abba. Understand this. Although not every person merits the specific soul-level called neshamah, every person nevertheless possesses a portion in the soul of Adam HaRishon, which included all created souls, even if this individual has not yet personally merited that level. The phrase "it is pure" corresponds to Atzilut, from which comes the neshamah of the neshamah. "You created it" corresponds to the part of the neshamah that comes from Beriah. "You formed it" alludes to ruach, which comes from Yetzirah. "You breathed it into me" alludes to nefesh, which is in Asiyah. Thus, by saying "the soul You placed within me," he includes all 4 of these aspects and calls them collectively neshamah. "You will ultimately take it from me," etc., alludes to the mystical elevation of mayin nukvin. Malchut receives the souls of the righteous in the secret of "new every morning" so that they become mayin nukvin within it; afterward, "You will ultimately restore it to me" in the morning, likewise in the secret of "new every morning." Shaar HaKavanos, page 2a. See the discourses concerning the night, discourse 3, page 53, and understand. See Shaliach Tzibbur, pages 45d and 46a, who explains the matter differently. Beit Oved, Chesed LaAlafim, and other siddurim write that while saying Elokai Neshamah one should intend an interweaving of the letters of neshamah with the letters of his own name. See Yafeh LaLev, letter 3. Since the Arizal did not write this and our practice does not follow it, we have not recorded it further.
