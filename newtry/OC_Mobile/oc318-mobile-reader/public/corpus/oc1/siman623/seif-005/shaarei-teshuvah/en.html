They raise their hands, etc. See She'eilas Yaavetz, siman 51, regarding what he wrote on this. Regarding the saying of Avinu Malkeinu, the Dagul MeRevavah wrote that in the Darkei Moshe it says that he should extend with hymns until night and afterward say Avinu Malkeinu, implying that initially one should say Avinu Malkeinu at night; see there. In truth, this language is from the Hagahos Maimoniyos, brought in the Beis Yosef there. Nevertheless, it seems that this only means that one may do this, but it is obvious that if it happened that it is still day, they should say it by day, even on Shabbos, as written in the Magen Avraham.
