They raise their hands. Even if they delayed until night, they raise their hands (Maharil, Darkei Moshe). Initially, if time is short, they should say the piyyutim after the prayer so that the raising of hands will be during the day (Hagahos Maimoniyos). The Beis Shmuel wrote that the custom that they raise their hands at night should be abolished; see there, page 57. Nevertheless, we say 'Elokeinu u'Vercheinu,' etc., for in this there is not so much concern. And we say Avinu Malkeinu even if it falls on Shabbos, because Shabbos has already passed (Levush). It seems to me that even if it is still day, we say it, for in siman 622, se'if 3, the Beis Yosef ruled to say it. Although our custom is not to say it, nevertheless at Ne'ilah we say it, because it is the time of the completion of judgment; see there in the Beis Yosef in the name of the Ran.
