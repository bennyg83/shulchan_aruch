[5] And we blow, etc. Tosafos in chapter Eilu Kesharim wrote that the reason for this blast is to show that it is a Yom Tov, so that one should increase in the meal. So too the Semag, siman 69, wrote that since this Yom Tov is not publicized, they made an enactment to publicize it. Mateh Moshe wrote: Therefore each person should visit or greet his fellow as he leaves the synagogue, as people say greetings on Shabbos and Yom Tov.
