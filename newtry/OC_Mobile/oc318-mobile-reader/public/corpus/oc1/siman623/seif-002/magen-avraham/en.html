To sunset. This means the end of sunset, which is the emergence of the stars, as Tosafos wrote; see Beis Yosef, siman 562. The Bach wrote: Now that the custom is to extend the prayer even into the night, one should not protest against them, for they rely on the Rif and the Rosh. However, one must be careful to begin while it is still fully day, and he should also say the stanza 'the day turns,' etc., before the sun sets, for otherwise he speaks falsehood before Hashem.
