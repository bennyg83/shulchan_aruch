The time of the Ne'ilah prayer is when the sun is at the tops of the trees, so that he completes it close to sunset. And the chazzan must shorten the selichos and verses in the middle of the prayer; and also he should not draw out each and every word in the Ne'ilah prayer, in the manner that he draws out in the other prayers, so that he finishes before sunset {and he says, in place of "inscribe us," "seal us" (Tur)}.
