The time for Ne'ilah prayer is when the sun is at the tops of the trees so that one can finish it close to sunset, and the chazan must shorten the selichot and verses in the middle of the prayer, and also he must not draw out every word in Ne'ilah as he draws them out in the rest of his prayers, so that he will finish before sunset (and he says in place of "ketavenu" "chatamenu") (Tur).
