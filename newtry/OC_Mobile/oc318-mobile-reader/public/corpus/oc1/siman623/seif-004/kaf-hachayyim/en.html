4) [Seif 2] The time for the Ne'ilah prayer, etc. From where is the Ne'ilah prayer derived? As it is stated, "Even when you multiply prayer, I will not hear." From here it follows that anyone who increases prayer is answered. Yerushalmi, chapter Tefillas HaShachar, cited by the Avudraham at the beginning of his work, in the second gate; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur.
