Se'if 3, The time, etc. At the end of Yoma: Rav said, the Ne'ilah prayer, etc.; it is a dispute among Tannaim, etc. The halachah is not like Rav, but like that beraisa: for Ma'ariv one prays seven blessings, and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says, etc., for the sugya of the Gemara in chapter 5 of Berachos follows that beraisa. Also in Yerushalmi, chapter 4 of Berachos, and the last chapter of Ta'anis, Rabbi Yochanan disagrees with Rav, and Rav is refuted there. All the Amoraim there challenged Rav and supported Rabbi Yochanan, and also from the Mishnah at the beginning of chapter 4 of Ta'anis. See the Rosh at the end of Yoma. It says there: He said to him, 'When you see the sun at the tops of the palm trees,' etc. 'The view of Rav is reversed,' etc., because Rav would prolong his prayer, etc. This shows that according to Rabbi Yochanan, its time is when the sun is at the tops of the palm trees, and he should not prolong it.
And he says. See the first chapter of Rosh Hashanah.
Se'if 3, But in the confession, etc., and these words apply. Shabbos 24: And the halachah is not, etc., but the halachah is like Rav Gidel. See Tosafos there. The confession is also comparable to Rav Gidel's case, unlike the shaliach tzibbur. See the Mordechai.
