If it falls on Shabbos, one makes mention in it of [the theme of] Shabbos; but in the confession that follows the prayer, one does not make mention of [the theme of] Shabbos. And this applies to an individual; but the chazzan, since he says it within his prayer, makes mention in it of [the theme of] Shabbos; and if he did not make mention of this, we do not have him repeat [it].
