If (the Ne’ilah Prayer) falls on the Sabbath, we remember in it the Sabbath in the prayer (meaning, we mention it), 211 See footnote 196 concerning the added words "of the Sabbath". but in the confession which is after the Silent Prayer 212 Amidah , עמידה ; see footnote 43. we do not mention the Sabbath. This refers to the individual worshipper, but the reader, since he said it in the midst of his Silent Prayer, he mentions the Sabbath in it (even when repeating the Prayer outloud). If he does not mention it (the Sabbath) they do not make him return and repeat it.
