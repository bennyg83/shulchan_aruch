(א) They lift [hands for Birkat Kohanim] etc., and even if he delayed etc. — Maharil: what is the reason? Thus: the reason limbs and fats of the daily offering were burned at night — end of his words. And Rashi means even though they were burned at night, the main mitzvah of lifting hands is by day, as written "on the day the Omnipresent gave" etc.
