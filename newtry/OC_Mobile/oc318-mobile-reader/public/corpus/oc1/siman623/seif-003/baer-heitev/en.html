Not. The reason is that they always finish it at night, and there is no Temple service at night. Levush. The Maharil wrote that even if they delayed until night, the kohanim raise their hands. But the later authorities wrote that the custom should be abolished, because there is no raising of hands at night. Nevertheless, we say 'Elokeinu u'Vercheinu,' etc., for in this there is not so much concern; see the Magen Avraham.
