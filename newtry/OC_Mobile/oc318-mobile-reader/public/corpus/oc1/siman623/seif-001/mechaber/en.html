“The order of the Closing Prayer, Ne’ilah ” - Containing six paragraphs. For the Closing Service we say “ אשרי 203 Ashrei , אשרי ; see footnote 175. ” and Kaddish 204 Kaddish , קדיש ; see footnote 177. but we do not say “ ובא לציון 205 U-Va Le-Ẓiyyon , ובא לציון ; see footnote 187. ”. {Rama: Hagah : I have already written that the custom in these lands is to say “ אשרי ” and “ ובא לציון ” before the Closing Service.}
