1) [Seif 1] For Ne'ilah, one says Ashrei. In order to make an interruption between Minchah and Ne'ilah. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1.
2) Ibid. U'Va L'Tzion is not said. Because it was already said before Minchah, as written above in siman 622, seif 1.
3) Ibid., gloss. I have already written that the custom in these countries, etc. We wrote the reason above in siman 622, sec. 3, see there.
