Before. See Ba'er Heitev. The Noda BiYehudah, siman 33, wrote that one should abolish the custom that a non-Jew lights candles in the synagogue at the time of the Ne'ilah prayer. He wrote that he abolished the custom in the holy community of Yampola and in Prague. I permitted only that he move the candles that were already burning from Erev Yom Kippur, to set them spread out throughout the synagogue, for this is a double rabbinic prohibition in the place of a mitzvah. If darkness has already increased before the congregation prayed the silent Ne'ilah, then it is called a great need, and leave them, etc. But if the congregation already prayed, and it is only for the need of the shaliach tzibbur, certainly one candle lit from Erev Yom Kippur will be found; one should protest against them. It is better that they not say piyyutim and selichos, and that they hear from the shaliach tzibbur, rather than do something forbidden according to most authorities; see there.
