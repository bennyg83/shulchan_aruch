And we blow. This is a sign of the departure of the Shechinah upward, as it says, 'Elokim ascended with a teruah,' and a remembrance of the shofar blasts of the Yovel, which were on Yom Kippur. Tosafos wrote that it is to show that it is a Yom Tov, so that one should increase in the meal; therefore each person should visit or greet his fellow as he leaves the synagogue, in the manner that people say greetings on Shabbos and Yom Tov. Mateh Moshe. We blow even if it falls on Shabbos. Even though they have not yet made havdalah in the prayer, nevertheless, since the day has already passed and blowing the shofar is a skill and not a melachah, they were not stringent about it. Tur. Judgment and wrath do not depart until Yisrael complete their order of prayers. Terumas HaDeshen, siman 271.
