At the end of the selichos we say seven times "Hashem hu ha'Elokim" {and once "Shema Yisrael" and three times "Baruch shem kevod malchuso le'olam va'ed" (Minhagim); and see above, siman 61}. And we sound [the shofar] tekiah-shevarim-teruah-tekiah. {Rama: And some say to sound only a single tekiah (Mordechai; Hagahos Maimoni, end of Hilchos Yom Kippur; Agur); and so is the custom in these lands. And we sound [it] after he has said Kaddish following Ne'ilah; and some places have the custom to sound [it] before Kaddish.}
