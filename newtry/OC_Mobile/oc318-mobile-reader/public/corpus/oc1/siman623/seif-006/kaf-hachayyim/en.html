6) Ibid. The time for the Ne'ilah prayer is when the sun is at the treetops, etc. At the end of Yoma, the Rosh wrote in the name of Maharam that the Ne'ilah prayer must be recited during the day. In the Yerushalmi, chapter Tefillas HaShachar, and at the beginning of the final chapter of Taanis, R' Yochanan disputes Rav and says that the Ne'ilah prayer is recited only during the day. In a dispute between Rav and R' Yochanan, the halacha follows R' Yochanan, see there. The Ran also cited this Yerushalmi, but wrote that the words of the Rif indicate that the halacha follows Rav. Nevertheless, since we add many verses of mercy and words of supplication to the Ne'ilah prayer, we begin it when we see the sun at the top of a palm tree, as stated in the Yerushalmi, see there. So too wrote the Mordechai: The law of the Ne'ilah prayer is that it should be recited during the day. The practice of reciting it at night was certainly not instituted by our ancestors. Rather, because the chazzanim extend it until night, people think that they act in accordance with the law, end quote. So too is written in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 3 of the Laws of Prayer. Hagahos Maimoniyos further wrote there that even according to Rav, the time for the Ne'ilah prayer is from plag haminchah onward, see there. The Beit Yosef cited these words and wrote: From these matters it will become clear to you that according to our practice, in which we add many supplications, it would be necessary to begin and pray it well before nightfall, so that the chazzan will complete the repetition of the prayer during the day, and at the very least that Bircas Kohanim should take place during the day. However, if so, we would need to begin it very early before nightfall, because we add many Selichos, whereas we require that it be recited when we see the sun at the top of a palm tree. Therefore, it appears that the shaliach tzibbur should shorten the Selichos and verses in the middle of the prayer. He also should not draw out every word of the Ne'ilah prayer in the manner that he draws them out in the other prayers, see there. He therefore ruled accordingly in the Shulchan Aruch.
