And some say they did not forbid, etc. — this is Ramban's view who holds that certainly we are not concerned lest he fall and come to bring it — for if so it would be forbidden to stand near a child carrying a dinar in his hand; rather certainly they forbade only carrying a child with a dinar in his hand, lest they err and say: just as it is permitted to carry a child with a dinar in his hand, so too it is permitted to carry the dinar itself in hand.
