And shakes. And permitted to study on the table with coins placed on it from another side even though it is customary to sway while studying — we are not concerned that he will sway the coins. Maharil.

And the coins. If he does this for the need of the coins lest they be stolen — forbidden. Beit Yosef.

Placed. And if he placed them intentionally on a weekday and his intent was not that they remain there on Shabbat and afterward he forgot them — it is called forgetting [Mahariv siman 193].

All Shabbat. And in a place of loss one may rely on this. Bach.

Even. This applies also to the first view — that even if he placed them only for entry of Shabbat to take them afterward, forbidden all Shabbat.

Forbidden. According to this, when we stand the menorah with candles on the tablecloth on the table — we should forbid moving that tablecloth all Shabbat, and we have not heard nor seen anyone careful about this — requires further study. Taz see there.

His fellow. And if he does it for his fellow's benefit — there is room to be uncertain, for presumably he is pleased — see Magen Avraham.
