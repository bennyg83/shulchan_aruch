With intent that they remain. But if he placed them intentionally on a weekday and his intent was not that they remain there on Shabbat and afterward he forgot them there — it is called forgetting (writings of Mahariv siman 193); and he further wrote what you deduced from Rashi chapter Notel — that it does not become a base unless he placed a muktzeh object on it in order that it settle well upon it; but what people place incidentally, as is customary to place objects in a box one on another because he has no room to clear a place for each object at the edges of the box — such a case is not called placing but forgetting; it appears there is no proof from Rashi's explanation to permit except when it already rested upon it and on erev Shabbat he forgot it; but placing on erev Shabbat, even incidentally — since he knows the muktzeh will rest on the object on Shabbat, the object too becomes muktzeh — end of his words; and it appears to me plain as the questioner said, for we move a baker's peel via its dough even though there is ash with wood chips on it, as Rambam wrote chapter 26; and likewise we move pure terumah even though impure terumah rests on it, and it deals with intentional placing — for if forgetting, even impure terumah alone is permitted; rather it deals with placing, as is everywhere in Rambam chapter 25 and Rabbeinu Yerucham and responsum of Rosh; and even so we do not say pure terumah became a base for impure; and Me'or wrote regarding a stone on the mouth of a barrel that specifically when placing with intent of base — forbidden; but when placing with intent of cover it is like forgetting; and in Milchamot he wrote that even when placing with intent of cover it becomes a base — end of his words; but when placing incidentally, as stated — according to all it does not become a base, as is everywhere in responsa 259 and siman 308 s.k. 27 see there; and so too Bach siman 310 in the name of Rashba — permitted to move a movable sack that has a purse of coins on erev Shabbat; Tosafos daf 45 wrote: a chicken coop, since it is made for chickens — if there is a hatching egg in it, forbidden to move it, for this is not like forgetting but like intentional placing; and the same applies to everything similar.
