He forgot a stone on top of a barrel or coins on a pillow — he tilts the barrel on its side and the stone falls, and shakes the pillow and the coins fall; and if the barrel was among barrels in a manner that he cannot tilt it in its place, he can lift it as it is with the stone to another place to tilt it there so it falls off; and if he placed them intentionally with intent that they remain there at the entry of Shabbat — forbidden to tilt and shake; and some say that even if he placed them there with intent that they remain there at the entry of Shabbat in order to take them on Shabbat — permitted to tilt and shake on Shabbat, and they forbade only when placing them with intent that they remain there all Shabbat.And then even if the forbidden object was removed from there, forbidden to move the utensil, for once it became a base for the forbidden object for part of Shabbat, forbidden all Shabbat; and likewise with all muktzeh; and likewise below siman 310 — if a person placed a muktzeh object on his fellow's object, we do not say it became a base for the forbidden object, for a person does not forbid his fellow's object without his knowledge [Or Zarua].]:
