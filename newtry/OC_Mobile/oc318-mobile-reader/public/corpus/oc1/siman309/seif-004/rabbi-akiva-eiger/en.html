Seif 4 forgot a stone. In responsum Mekor Baruch siman 3 he wondered — behold there is wine in the barrel; and according to Rashi why is the barrel not a base for permitted and forbidden? And he strained: since as base for the stone it is only the mouth of the barrel — the mouth of the barrel is not a base for wine; and it is different with a basket and fruits — for the fruits and stone are inside the basket — see there; and see Eliyah Rabbah and Teshuvah Shavet:

Magen Avraham s.k. 6 on ash with wood chips. In my humble opinion there is no proof from this — for wood chips do not cover all the ash, rather placed in several places; and even though in the place of the wood one can say the ash beneath them is a base — nevertheless the ash on the sides is not a base, for the ash is not one mass that everything would be a base:
