[1] [Levush] To bring four cubits, etc. His words are wondrous, for they apply in a courtyard and not in reshut ha-rabbim, as Rashi explained at the beginning of chapter Notel; and so is implied in responsum of Rosh general 22 — this is because of the prohibition of moving; for if he only lifts from place to place — behold that is forbidden; and see siman 308 seif 5 — even on a scale it is forbidden; therefore in Tur and Shulchan Aruch they wrote only "comes to bring" and not four cubits; but I found (in Rashal) [in Rashi] in Alfasi — "comes to bring" in reshut ha-rabbim — until here; and it is also wondrous: "if he does not have," etc. — that is with an ordinary child, as Beit Yosef wrote in Sefer HaTerumot; however, when holding by hand — permitted:
