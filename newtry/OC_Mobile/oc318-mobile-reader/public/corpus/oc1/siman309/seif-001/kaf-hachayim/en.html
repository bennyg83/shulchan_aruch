a) [Seif 1] A person takes his son, etc. — Explanation: permitted for a person to carry his son in his arms in a courtyard even though it is as if he holds a stone in his hand, as Rava said there — he brought out a living child to reshut ha-rabbim with a purse hanging on his neck — liable because of the purse; nevertheless here, where there is no hotzaah but only muktzeh — he moves the stone in a courtyard or house — and the child has longing for his father, that if he does not carry him he will become ill — they did not establish their words in a place of danger of illness even though there is no danger to life. Bach. And if you ask — let him throw the stone from his hand, as is written in seif 3 — one can say the child will cry out and weep. Magen Avraham s.k. 1 in the name of Tosafos; and if so it is implied: if the child does not cry out — one can tell him to throw the stone from his hand and afterward carry him:
