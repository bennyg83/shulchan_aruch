There permitted to tilt and shake on Shabbat. But nevertheless forbidden to lift the barrels where he needs its place. So it appears to me to deduce from Tosafos Shabbat daf 123a s.v. this pigul. However from Tosafos chapter 4 Shabbat daf 51a s.v. or if he buried, etc. — it appears at first glance they do not hold thus — for if so they would not need to explain it deals where he cannot hold the pot; behold in plain meaning it works out — that in truth it is not a base since his intent is to take it on Shabbat; therefore when partly uncovered permitted to shake it; but when not partly uncovered — impossible to shake unless he holds the sides of the pot and lifts and shakes — see Maharsha there — and this is forbidden as complete moving, not permitted, since nevertheless he placed intentionally and should not have placed in a manner requiring complete moving. If so, they hold it is completely like forgetting. Requires further study. And after examination it appears to me: even though initially when they copied Rashi's explanation they thought it impossible to shake by holding the pot except by lifting the pot — for that was the main distinction between partly uncovered or not — nevertheless after he challenged Rashi's explanation from the Mishnah "he shakes the cover" — which implies even when not uncovered; and that it is not a base — that is as Rabeinu Tam explained: since his intent is not for all Shabbat — in truth from this it is also proven that by holding the pot one need not lift it, only tilt and shake; and therefore they needed to answer it deals when the pot is swallowed inside in a manner that there is no contradiction in Tosafos' words — it works out well for practical law: when placing with intent to take on Shabbat — forbidden to lift and shake it; but with a pot it is possible to hold the sides of the pot and shake without lifting — and understand well:
