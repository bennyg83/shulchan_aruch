Lest he fall, etc. — for since presumably he is particular about the dinar — properly we are concerned lest it fall from his hand and he come to bring it, and he ends up moving muktzeh with his hands; and this is according to Rashi's explanation. And Levush wrote "comes to bring" a dinar in reshut ha-rabbim — his words are wondrous, for reshut ha-rabbim is certainly forbidden in every case; rather in a courtyard they permitted, for there is only muktzeh; and it is possible there is a scribal error and one must pass the pen over the words "a dinar in reshut ha-rabbim."
