(9) As it is — even though there is a stone inside, the basket became a base for prohibition and permission; and the permitted is more important and the prohibition is batel against it, and it is as if it is not a base only for the permitted; and even though the stone is moved incidentally — permitted to move the basket and reach wherever he wants; and he need not gather the fruits from the basket in hand lest they fall to the ground; but if the fruits are placed in small baskets inside the large basket — he takes them in hand from inside the large basket:
(10) But if they are, etc. — since he can by some trick fix it so he will not move the stone — they did not lighten this even though it is a base for prohibition and permission; and likewise if he can shake the stone alone from inside the basket and throw it too — he is obligated in this, as explained below siman 310 seif 8 in the gloss:
(11) He shakes them and shakes, etc. — meaning afterward he returns and places them inside the basket if he wants to bring them to the table; but he should be careful they not scatter here and there — for otherwise forbidden to gather them and place them afterward inside the basket because of a weekday act [Tosefes Shabbos]:
(12) For the place of the basket — for then it is impossible to shake, lest the stone fall to the place he needs it; therefore he can take it as it is with the stone and move it to wherever he wants and shake it there, or leave it as it is:
