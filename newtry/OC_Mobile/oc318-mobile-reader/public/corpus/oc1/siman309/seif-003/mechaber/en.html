A basket full of fruits with a stone inside — if they are moist fruits such as figs and grapes, he takes it as it is, for if he shakes the fruits out of it they will spoil; but if they are fruits that are not spoiled, he shakes them out and shakes the stone with them and does not take it with them; and this applies when he needs only the fruits or the basket; but if he needed the place of the basket, he moves it as it is.
