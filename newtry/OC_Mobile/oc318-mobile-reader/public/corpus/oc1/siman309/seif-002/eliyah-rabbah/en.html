[4] As its wall, etc. Since he fastened the stone, he made it into a wall (Gemara). Magen Avraham infers that if he did not fasten it, this is not so. This requires study, because that statement in Shabbos 125b concerns a stone placed in a gourd, and Rashi explains that he fastened it securely. But on 142b, regarding a perforated basket, this requirement is not stated. There one may say that even without fastening, the stone becomes part of the wall because it fills a hole in the side or bottom and the basket cannot be used without it. Yet Beit Yosef writes that Rif omitted the law of the basket because he relied upon the law of the gourd, implying that the cases are equivalent. This can still be rejected.
