In the middle house, etc. Tosafot on 76 write that even if they place the eruv in one of the houses adjacent to the courtyards, or even in one of the houses within those courtyards, these three intervening houses need not contribute to the eruv, because they all become gatehouses for the courtyard that carries its eruv through them to the other courtyard.
The Levush writes: It interrupts between them, etc. From here there is a contradiction to the custom in some places of placing the eruv in the synagogue while houses of non-Jews intervene between them and the eruv, so that they cannot carry to the synagogue where the eruv is located, as stated by the Perishah.
