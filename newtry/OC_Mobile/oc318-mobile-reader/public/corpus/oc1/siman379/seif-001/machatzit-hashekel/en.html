(Siman kof alef) In the house etc. — adjacent to the courtyards, so it should read. Meaning that one courtyard placed its eruv in the third house, and likewise the courtyard opposite it placed its eruv in that same house adjacent to its courtyard; if so, the two eruvin are placed in one house. Or the two courtyards placed their eruv jointly in one house from the houses of one courtyard — at any rate, the two eruvin are placed in one house:

that they all become etc. — meaning this refers to the sifah — when they placed their eruv in a house from the houses of the courtyard. If so, all three houses become a gatehouse for the courtyard that passes its eruv over the three houses and places its eruv in the other courtyard.
