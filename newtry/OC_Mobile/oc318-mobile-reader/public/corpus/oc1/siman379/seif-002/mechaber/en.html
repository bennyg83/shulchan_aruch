Two courtyards with two houses between them, where they did not make a joint eruv but each made its own eruv separately — and one came from his courtyard through the house adjacent to it and placed his eruv in the house adjacent to the other courtyard, and the other did likewise, also placing his eruv in the house adjacent to the first courtyard — they have not acquired an eruv, since each one placed their eruv in the gatehouse of the other courtyard.
