And Tosafos wrote that even if they placed the eruv in one of the houses adjacent to the courtyards, or in one of the houses of the courtyard — these three houses — they need not give an eruv, for they all become a gatehouse for that courtyard that passes the eruv through them to the other courtyard. Magen Avraham siman kuf alef. And see Tosafos, Rashi, and Rabbenu Tam. And he wrote in Ma'orei Or part Be'er Sheva daf 115a that this is reasonable, since the authors of the Shulchan Aruch in Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe did not mention the words of Tosafos, it is a blindfold (s.v.). However, in Etz Chaim siman 377 s"k 19 he wrote that according to Rabbeinu Yerucham it appears he disagrees with this law and holds that for each house they only give one gatehouse, see there.
