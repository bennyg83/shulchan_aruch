(א) Or in four parashiyot. Except parashat Zachor which is for all a d'oraisa obligation — see siman 685; and I saw the custom that a minor does not read maftir in Merkavah on the first day of Shavuot nor on the seventh of Pesach. Mateh Moshe wrote that one should not read maftir on Shabbat Shuvah; and Magen Avraham wrote, and it appears to me that all this is regarding a minor who reached chinuch and knows to cut the letters well, and not like those who appoint many minors to read the haftarah. And see responsum Bach siman 158. And in Knesset HaGedolah and Perach Shoshan general rule 1 siman 8:

(ב) The maftir. That is on Shabbat; but on weekday when the maftir is from the minyan, they say Kaddish after the maftir, for one may not say Kaddish until the minyan is complete:
