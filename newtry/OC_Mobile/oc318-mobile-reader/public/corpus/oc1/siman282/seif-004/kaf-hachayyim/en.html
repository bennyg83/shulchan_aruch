4) There: Seven people read from it. Why did the Sages not decree lest one go to an expert to learn and thereby carry the Sefer Torah four amos in the public domain? One can answer that Torah reading occurs only with a congregation, and all opinions agree that no such decree is necessary. This differs from lulav, shofar, and Megillah, which are handled by each individual. Maharlanch, siman 32, writes this explicitly; see there. Erech HaShulchan, paragraph 1. Regarding the custom of bringing books to the synagogue without applying the decree made for shofar, Tashbetz, volume 2, siman 197, writes that the greatness of the mitzvah requires the decree regarding shofar, since a person is preoccupied with it and might carry it. With other books, whose use does not itself fulfill a mitzvah, he will not forget; see there. Erech HaShulchan, paragraph 2, cites this.
