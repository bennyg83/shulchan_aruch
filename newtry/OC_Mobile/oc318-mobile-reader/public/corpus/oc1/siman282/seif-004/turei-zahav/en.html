(ב) Even though there are disputants. Explanation: they hold a minor cannot read parashat Zachor which is d'oraisa; nevertheless we practice to read him, for he counts even for a minyan of seven:

(ג) And they say Kaddish before, etc. Explanation: to exclude the view of R' Yeshaya that Beit Yosef brings, who distinguishes between one Torah scroll and two; rather in every case Kaddish is before the maftir specifically:
