1) [Seif 1] A Sefer Torah is taken out, etc. Every Shabbos morning, when the Sefer Torah was taken from the ark, the Arizal took care to recite the text written in the sacred Zohar, parashas Vayakhel, namely, Berich Shemei DeMarei Alma, etc. On Shavuos he would ascend and personally read the Ten Commandments aloud, as the sages of Sephardic communities customarily do. Similarly, on the Shabbos of parashas Bechukosai, he would ascend and personally read aloud the curses in that parashah, like the sages of Sephardic communities. On every Shabbos he would receive only the sixth aliyah, as stated in the sacred Zohar, parashas Shelach Lecha, regarding Rabbi Keruspedai, beloved of his Master's heart: on Shabbos he would ascend to the Sefer Torah only as the sixth reader, corresponding to the tzaddik, concerning whom it is stated, 'The Name of Hashem is a tower of strength; through it the righteous man runs and is elevated.' Shaar HaKavanos, folio 73c. We already wrote above in siman 134, paragraph 10, the intentions for opening the ark and the Sefer Torah. Take them from there.
