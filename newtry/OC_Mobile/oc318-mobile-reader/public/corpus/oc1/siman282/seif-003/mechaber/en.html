All count for a minyan of seven — even a woman and a minor who knows to whom they bless; but the Sages said a woman may not read in public because of honor of the congregation:And these specifically join to complete the minyan of seven; but for a minyan of three for reading they do not count. And a minor counts for seven only when he reached chinuch and knows to whom they bless and to cut the letters well; and if there is no kohen except a minor, they call him. And a woman is obligated to hear Keriat HaTorah. A slave whose mother is from Israel counts. And it is forbidden to read one whose knees are visible or his clothes are torn, or one whose head is uncovered; and a mamzer is permitted to go up, but in place of a Levi he may not go up because the sanctity of Levitehood is impaired.
