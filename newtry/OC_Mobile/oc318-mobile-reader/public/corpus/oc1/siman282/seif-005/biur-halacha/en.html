If one is not found, etc. — and on a day when they take out two Torah scrolls and read seven in the first and the second for the maftir, and there is not found one who knows to read maftir — it is forbidden to call the seventh to the second Torah scroll, for he already read in the first; and if he goes up to the second he will appear as if adding for honor, and this is like adding aliyot which is forbidden except on Simchat Torah and the like; and if the seventh knows to read maftir, he goes up to the second scroll and reads maftir and is not counted in the seven, as is explained in the Mishna Berurah:
