(א) (27) If one is not found, etc. — and the same applies on Shabbat of Chazon — even if one is found who knows to read maftir from the Prophets, only he does not know to recite kinot as is customary — the law is also so:
(ב) (28) He must return and read — and even the seventh who wishes to read maftir must return and read, since they interrupted with Kaddish — those entering would not know that this maftir already read in the Torah:
(ג) (29) The one who went up to seventh should read maftir, etc. — meaning he should read maftir from the Prophets and need not return to read in the Torah except what he read as seventh at the end of the parashah [or when they added one who read only for maftir]:
(ד) (30) If he knows — and if the seventh does not know and one of the previous ones must go up for maftir, then in all cases he must return and read and bless in the Torah for the sake of maftir:
(ה) (31) He should not read maftir — and on Shabbat Chazon where the custom is that the rabbi reads maftir, they should not call him earlier for the third [Magen Avraham and Tosafot Shabbat]; and see in Aleph Reish:
(ו) (32) One who already went up — if they called a person to the Torah in another synagogue and that parashah happened to him that day, he must return and bless [Acharonim]:
