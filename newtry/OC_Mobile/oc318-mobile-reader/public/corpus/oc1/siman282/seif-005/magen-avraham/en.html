(א) If one is not found, etc. And the same applies on Shabbat of Chazon — even if one is found who knows to read maftir from the Prophets, only he does not know to recite kinot as is customary — the law is so, Maggid Mishneh there; and Maharik — even if there are others who know kinot, the rabbi may read since the rabbi's way is always to read it:

(ב) He must return and read. For the unspecified wording implies that even the seventh who wishes to read maftir must return and read, since they interrupted with Kaddish — those entering would not know that this one already read in the Torah:

(ג) The one who went up to seventh should read maftir. And he should not say Kaddish after him, so too in Darkei Moshe; nevertheless it appears to me he should say Kaddish after the haftarah, for Kaddish refers to Torah reading and the haftarah is not included:
