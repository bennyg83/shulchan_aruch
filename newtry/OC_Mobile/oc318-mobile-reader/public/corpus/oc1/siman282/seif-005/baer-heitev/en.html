And the same applies on Shabbat of Chazon — even if one is found who knows to read maftir from the Prophets, only he does not know to recite the kinot as is customary — the law is so, Rivash. And even if there are others who know to recite kinot, the rabbi may read, since the rabbi's way is always to read it — it is permitted l'chatchila for the third to go up and be maftir. And it appears to us he should not go up third, as Rama and Magen Avraham wrote. If they called him in another synagogue and that parashah happened to him, he must return and recite the blessing — Maharik:
