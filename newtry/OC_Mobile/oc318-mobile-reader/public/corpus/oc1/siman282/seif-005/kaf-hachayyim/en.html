5) There: Seven people read from it. Whenever the required number of readers read all the material obligatory for that day, the congregation fulfills its obligation. This applies whether the first reader read most of the parashah and the seven readers read a small remainder, or one reader completed the parashah and the second then repeated it from beginning to end, with each subsequent reader doing the same. Maharam di Trani, volume 1, siman 224; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur. See above, siman 137, seif 6, and our words there, and note this well.
