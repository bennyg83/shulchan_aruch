“Who sees me.” And although they say in Shabbat ch. 41 that one who covers the lower part of his face with his hands when going down to wash is as if denying Avraham’s covenant — for it appears he is ashamed of it — that case is different, for there he is compelled to expose himself to wash and therefore the circumcision must remain visible; not so here (Shelah). They say in the Gemara: when his face is toward people he must bow or place his hands on the lower part of his face, provided he does not touch, as explained in Even HaEzer siman 3, see what is written there, and see O.C. siman 23.
