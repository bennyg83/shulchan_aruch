S.k. 7 — “It appears Shabbat 192…” — apparently a scribal error; should read 152 (or 129) where it discusses shoes on a person’s feet. Although Magen Avraham’s end cites “sell what he has” from Shabbat (159a sells roof beams; by implication sell possessions — Rashi Pesachim 112 “one should always sell what he has,” see there) — both texts may be intended and need emendation: Shabbat 129 and 152; the 152 passage is Sadducees to Rebbe Chiya’s son, whereas there they saw Rebbe Chiya’s son not finishing shoes (needs study how he did so, especially Pesachim 113b seven heavenly bans including one with no shoes on feet — perhaps occasional mourning or splinters). Magen Avraham’s midrash Metzora is per Eshel Avraham in name of Ateres Zekenim — there midrash seems about body covering not shoes. “And he shall not walk barefoot” is a separate topic — requires study.
