Bareheaded. On Ba’er Hetev; Bach 112 on the prohibition of walking four cubits bareheaded: not only walking but standing and sitting require care. What Magen Avraham wrote in siman 282 s.k. 8 from Tosefta Yoma — they would cover with their hands — see siman 91. What Ba’er Hetev cited in Maharshal’s name, that only under the open sky is forbidden, misleads readers: Maharshal wrote some say so but in his conclusion did not accept that; still where one’s conduct obviously shows he is cooling off etc. it is permitted. In any case one should avoid walking four cubits [bareheaded]; in bathhouse even saintly conduct does not apply as Derech Eretz implies; yet I have seen careful people cover with a kerchief and I have long done so, see there; Birkei Yosef cites it and calls it a very great stringency, see there.
