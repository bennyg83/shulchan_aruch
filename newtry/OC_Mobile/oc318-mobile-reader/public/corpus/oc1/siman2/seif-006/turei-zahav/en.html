Erect posture. “As if forcing the feet of the Divine Presence”; Gemara forbids walking four cubos erect because of “How awe-inspiring is this place.” Tur and Shulchan Aruch mention the measure of four cubos only for bareheadedness — requires study: not literally four cubos in Gemara for posture, and it fits the reason of awe — no distinction between four cubos and less. Some resolve: Tur was strict to forbid even less than four cubos because Gemara forbids posture; not so bareheadedness where no prohibition is mentioned, only Rav Huna was careful to walk four cubos covered — that is weak: how could Tur be stricter than explicit Gemara from his own reasoning?
And not walk bareheaded. See what I wrote siman 8 s.b.; it seems the same if riding an animal or sitting in a wagon — like walking, for end of Kiddush ch. 1 we say a rider is like a walker.
