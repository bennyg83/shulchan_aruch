6 It is forbidden, etc. Although in Kiddushin they say “four cubos,” in Berachos 43b two texts appear in Rif and Rashba: “and he shall not walk with an erect posture” because of “the whole earth is full of His glory”; so too Rambam and Tur state plainly.
And cover his whole body. Midrash Rabba Metzora 14:4. Four cubos with laziness — “the cold weakens” — by laziness not covering the head properly the cold weakens; “injured” homiletically; with slack hands — by laziness not covering the body properly boils rise on the body, homiletically.
And he shall not walk, etc. Shabbat ch. 18 and ch. 23, Pesachim ch. 10.
And accustom himself, etc. Berachos 62a; Tamid ch. 1.
For that is alacrity and cleanliness. Meaning: although for them alone so explained Tamid, nonetheless they called this alacrity.
