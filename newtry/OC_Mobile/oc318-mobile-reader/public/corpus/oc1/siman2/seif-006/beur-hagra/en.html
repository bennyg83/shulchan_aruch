(א) Seif 6 — it is forbidden, etc. Although in Kiddushin they say four amos, in Berachos 43b two textual readings appear in Rif and Rosh: and he shall not walk with an erect posture because of the whole earth is full of His glory; so too Rambam and Tur state plainly.

(ב) And he shall cover his whole body. Midrash Rabba, parashat Metzora p. 144. Another matter: with laziness the cold weakens — because when a person is lazy to cover his head as he should, the cold weakens; homiletically interpreted as becoming injured; and with slack hands — because when a person is lazy to cover his body as he should, boils rise on the body, homiletically interpreted.

(ג) And he shall not walk, etc. Shabbos ch. 18 and ch. 23, and Pesachim ch. 10.

(ד) And he shall accustom himself, etc. Berachos 62a, and Tamid ch. 1.

(ה) For that is alacrity and cleanliness. Meaning: although this applies specifically to them, as the commentator explained in Tamid, nonetheless they called this alacrity.
