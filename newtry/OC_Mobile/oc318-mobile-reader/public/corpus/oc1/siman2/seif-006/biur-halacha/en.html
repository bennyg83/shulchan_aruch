And he shall not walk four amos — see Mishna Berurah at the end of s.k. 11 as he wrote; if he bows his head it is permitted. It is true that Peri Megadim wrote that if he bows his head in this manner it is permitted, implying only when he covers with his hand; I omitted the words in this manner because Sefer Chasidim implies that in such a case it is permitted in every way. In my humble opinion one need not be strict about this, for concern about their practices does not apply to him according to all, since everyone sees he does this only for his own benefit.
