And he shall not walk four cubos [bareheaded] — see Mishnah Berurah end of s.k. 11 what he wrote; if he bows his head it is permitted. True, Peri Megadim wrote if he bows “like this” it is permitted, implying only when covered with a hand — I omitted the words “like this” because Sefer Chasidim implies in that case it is permitted in all ways; in my humble opinion one need not be strict here, for concern of “their practices” does not apply to him according to all, since everyone sees he does this only for his own benefit.
