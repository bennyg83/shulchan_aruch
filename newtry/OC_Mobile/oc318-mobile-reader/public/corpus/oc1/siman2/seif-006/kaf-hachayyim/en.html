6) Beware not to wear two garments together, for that leads to forgetfulness — Shaar HaKavanos in Drush Bereshis, cited Magen Avraham s.k. 3; the reason is explained in Shaar HaKavanos: each garment has an aspect of “light,” and one who wears two together does not give room for the light to enter between the two garments and surround each garment, hence shells are not driven away — forgetfulness comes from shells, see there. It seems the same if wearing two hats one inside the other — concern for forgetfulness; therefore it is not good for those who wear the small cap on the head that is red (called fez) inside the large bonnet etc. by custom to wear one inside the other — that too causes forgetfulness and is obvious — Ruach Chayim letter 3.
