6) He should beware not to wear two garments together at once, for that leads to forgetfulness — Shaar HaKavanos in the discourse on Bereishis, and Magen Avraham brought this in s.k. 3. The reason is explained in Shaar HaKavanos: each garment and garment has an aspect of light, and one who joins two garments and wears them together does not give room for the light to enter between the two garments together and surround between each garment and garment; therefore the kelipot are not driven away from there — and it is known that forgetfulness is found only from the side of the kelipot, see there. It appears to say that the same applies if one wears two hats one inside the other — there is concern for this reason that it leads to forgetfulness; therefore it is not good for those who wear the small cap on top of the head that is red, called a fez, inside the large bonnet and the like, according to their custom to wear them one inside the other, for this too causes forgetfulness, and it is obvious — Ruach Chayim seif 3.
