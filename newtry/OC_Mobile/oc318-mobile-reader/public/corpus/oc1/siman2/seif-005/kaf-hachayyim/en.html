5) There — not to turn, etc. Taz s.k. 2 wrote there is a distinction between a scholar and others: for a scholar if he wore the robe inside-out he must reverse it; ordinary people should from the outset avoid wearing it inside-out, see there — so Perishah letter 3. A scholar’s reason: every scholar must be dignified for his Torah’s honor — Perishah there. “For this matter every person can make himself a scholar” — Eshel Avraham letter 1; but Rabbi Shmelke Chadashos 112b asks on this and writes even everyone if he wore it inside-out must reverse it at least for prayer — Birkei Yosef letter 1; Mateh Yehudah letter 3 in name of Pri Tzadik: no distinction between scholar and everyone.
