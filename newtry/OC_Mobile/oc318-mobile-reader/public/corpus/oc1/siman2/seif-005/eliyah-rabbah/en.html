[5] And he shall not walk barefoot, etc. In these lands, not in Arab lands where it is customary. Yom Tov garments — see below siman 91 seif 5. Shelah 199 wrote: because of the blessing “Who stamps the ground,” one should intend to touch the actual ground with his feet — stand a moment before putting on shoes; see below siman 46 s.k. 50. Hagahos Hayashrus 130 wrote: if done for repentance mourning sins it is permitted; King David, peace be upon him, walked barefoot [so].
