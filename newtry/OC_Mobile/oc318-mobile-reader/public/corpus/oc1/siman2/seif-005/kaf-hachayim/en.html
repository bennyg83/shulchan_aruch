5) There — that he not turn it inside-out, etc. Taz s.k. 2 wrote there is a distinction between a talmid chacham and other people: for a talmid chacham, if he wore the robe inside-out he must turn it back; but other people in the world should preferably not wear it inside-out from the outset, see there — so Perishah seif 3. The reason for a talmid chacham is that every talmid chacham must be esteemed and dignified for the honor of his Torah — Perishah there. Regarding this matter, every person can make himself a talmid chacham — Eshel Avraham letter 1. But Rabbi Shmelke in Chadashos 112b asks on this and writes that even every person, if he wore it inside-out, must turn it back at least for prayer — Birkei Yosef letter 1. So too Mateh Yehudah seif 3 in the name of Pri Tzadik: there is no distinction between a talmid chacham and every person.
