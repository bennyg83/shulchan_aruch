S.k. 1, while seated, etc. They say in the Gemara Bava Batra 57b.

Today they are not careful, etc. Although in our times, in our great iniquity, we do not have the law of a talmid chacham, nevertheless in matters of modesty it is fitting for everyone to conduct himself with the measure of a talmid chacham, for this law explained in Shulchan Aruch here — that one not don his robe while seated — is what Rabbi Yochanan prided himself in, as explained in Tur, and nonetheless they wrote that we should practice so; and likewise what is stated in seif 3, “be careful,” etc., that he not turn it inside-out, is also stated in the Gemara Elu Kesharim about a talmid chacham, for Rabbi Yochanan said: which talmid chacham is careful with his robe, etc.
