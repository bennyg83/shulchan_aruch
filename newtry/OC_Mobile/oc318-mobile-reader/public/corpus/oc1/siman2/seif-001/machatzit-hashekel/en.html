S.k. 1, while seated, etc. They say in the Gemara Bava Batra 57b.
Today they are not careful, etc. Although in our times we do not have the law of a Torah scholar in this regard, still in modesty matters everyone should conduct himself with a scholar’s standard, for this law explained in Shulchan Aruch here — not to don one’s robe while seated — is what Rabbi Yochanan prided himself in, as Tur explains, and nonetheless they wrote we should practice so; likewise what siman 3 says, be careful not to turn inside-out, is also said in the Gemara Elu Kesharim about a scholar — Rabbi Yochanan asked which scholar is careful with his robe.
