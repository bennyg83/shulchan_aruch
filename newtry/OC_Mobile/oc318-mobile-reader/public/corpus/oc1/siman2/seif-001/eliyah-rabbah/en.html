[1] Let a person be careful, etc. Although the Talmud speaks of a Torah scholar — if already wearing inside-out he turns it back — from the outset every person should be careful with his robe and all the more so other garments, so Perishah wrote. Mahari Luria wrote in Shulchan Aruch: it is not good to wear two garments together; doing so leads to forgetfulness. They also wrote: beware to take both sides of the garment in the right hand, dress the right sleeve, swing the garment behind, then dress the left sleeve.
