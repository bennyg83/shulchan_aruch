(1) [Seif 1] He shall not don his robe — the one touching the skin of his flesh — as is clear from Rashi in parashat Mishpatim on the verse "for it is her covering, it is her garment for her skin," for he explained that his garment here means a robe that is next to the skin. Yafeh Lelev ch. 2 letter 1 — and this is straightforward.
