Taz wrote there is a distinction between a scholar and others: for a scholar, if he wore the robe inside-out he must reverse it; but ordinary people should from the outset avoid wearing it inside-out, see there. One should be careful not to wear two garments together at once, for that leads to forgetfulness. He should hold both sides of the garment in his right hand and dress the right [side] first and then the left, and intend that everything is included in “right” and proceeds from right to left.
