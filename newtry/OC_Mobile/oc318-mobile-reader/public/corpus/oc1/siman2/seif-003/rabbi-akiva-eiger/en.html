Taz s.k. 3 — tefillin’s importance lies in the tying. I saw in Amek HaMelech Lech Lecha a praise-worthy reason per those who explain Chullin ch. 9: in reward for Avraham saying “from a thread to a shoelace” his descendants merited two mitzvos — tekhelet thread and tefillin straps; therefore since they merited tefillin corresponding to “to a shoelace,” importance was given to the shoe’s strap to tie first on the left with which we tie tefillin.
