3) One who places his garments on his head forgets his learning — Horayos, end of the chapter. One who dips his finger in salt and eats restores his learning. One who gazes at the face of the dead or reads an inscription on a grave forgets his learning — Likutei Pri Chadash, and Peri Megadim brought this in O.C. letter 1; but what he wrote about gazing at the face of the dead or reading an inscription on a grave causing forgetfulness of learning requires further study, for in Horayos it states “hard for learning,” and Rashi explains “hard to hear” — one may say that this too is through forgetfulness. Shaar HaMitzvos on Vaetchanan wrote that only raised lettering is a concern, but sunken writing need not be worried about. See there in the Gemara that there are several more things that cause forgetfulness and things that are good for learning. One who eats from broken vessels will have forgetfulness; the reason is because they allude to the aspect of kelipot, and forgetfulness comes from them, as stated below.
