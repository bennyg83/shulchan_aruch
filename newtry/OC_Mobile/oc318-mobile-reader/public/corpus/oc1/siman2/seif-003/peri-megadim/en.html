Put on — Taz. I have five approaches. (1) Beit Yosef: Rav Yosef and Rav Ashi align with Tosafos “who acts like the baraita” vs Rabbi Yochanan — dispute only whether right or left has “big toe”/tefillin-type honor; poskim omitted baraita and R Yochanan (Rif, Rambam, Rashba); each may choose. Tur rules like Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak: baraita supports washing/anointing right, shoe removal left; the baraita’s varied cases mean one may choose which side to honor until the Talmud’s end forces left removal like RNB"Y; Tur omitted Tosafos on laceless shoes, removal, washing — without laces he may not hold like Tosafos: with tying one must honor both — don right, tie left; without laces choose; “remove left” is not absolute per Beit Yosef on Tur; why baraita does not add “when removing may wash left first” — Beit Yosef: from washing not donning — study Beit Yosef and Rashi. (2) Bach: three-way dispute per Tosafos; “loves right” always right, etc.; R Yochanan strict on Rav Shemen; Rav Ashi saw Rav Chisda not strict in optional matters; poskim who rule like Rav Ashi — Tur like RNB"Y. (3) Eliyah Rabba: RNB"Y and Rav Ashi agree; baraita don right tie left when possible; else choose; Rav Ashi explains alternating practice; Tur ruled both; omitted laceless/removal for flexibility; why Tur cited RNB"Y not Rav Ashi. (4) My view: RY “like tefillin like shoes” — parallel to tefillin order and head removal; shoes: left first to avoid “wound” (see Chidushei Ari Shabbat 61, Yerushalmi); Derech Etz Metzuyan; Rav Yosef “as his master” vs RY and baraita — poskim omitted; we rule siman 14 Chiya bar Rav. (5) Taz: no real dispute — Rav Yosef/Rav Ashi lax like laceless/removal; RNB"Y God-fearing honors both with tying — Tur omitted laceless/removal; Rabbei Hagahol brought all; Mechaber brought removal not laceless — resolve accordingly or removal honors left — end.
