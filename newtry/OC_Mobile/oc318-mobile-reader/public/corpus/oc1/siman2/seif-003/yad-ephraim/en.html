(Magen Avraham s.k. 2) See Even HaEzer siman 23 — so it should read. The meaning is: there in seif 6 he wrote that women now cover their nakedness in the bathhouse, and therefore it is permitted to bathe with one’s father, see there. I found Peri Megadim emended similarly, as he wrote, except that he explains his intent on another view regarding holding one’s mother-in-law, etc. — in my humble opinion that is only incidental; he already showed a place for seif 13 on the main point, namely rising from bathing while covered, and for that he cited Even HaEzer. Netiv Chayim wrote that it should read “and see Even HaEzer 12,” and I do not understand his intent.
