(Magen Avraham s.k. 2) See Even HaEzer siman 23 — requires study: there seif 6 he wrote women now cover nakedness in bathhouse hence permitted to bathe with one’s father, see there. I found Peri Megadim emended similarly but explained his intent on another view regarding holding one’s mother-in-law, etc. — in my humble opinion that is only incidental; he already showed a place for seif 13 on the main point — rising from bathing covered — and for that cited Even HaEzer. Netiv Chayim wrote “requires study” and see E.H. 12 — I do not understand his intent.
