(3) With his robe — even though it is under all his garments, and how much more so other garments.

(4) Outward — the ugly seams of the robe will be visible and he will be repulsive in people’s eyes; if he was not careful and turned it inside-out, if he is a talmid chacham he must remove it and return to wearing it properly so as not to be among those who incur hatred, Heaven forbid; other people need not; for prayer, even every person must remove it and wear it properly, for then it is fitting to beautify one’s garments, as explained in siman 91. It is good that he place both sides of the garment in his right hand and dress the right first and then the left, and intend that everything is included in the right and from the right comes to the left. Rambam wrote: the clothing of a talmid chacham should be handsome and clean, and it is forbidden that a stain or grease or the like be found on his garment; he should not wear neither princely clothing that everyone stares at nor poor clothing that degrades its wearer, but middling fine garments; see there more.
