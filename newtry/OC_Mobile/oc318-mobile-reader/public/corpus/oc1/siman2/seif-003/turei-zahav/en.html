To wear it properly. So wrote Tur and cited Elu Kesharim: Rabbi Yochanan asked, “Which scholar is careful with his robe to turn it to its proper side?” Rashi: if he wore it inside-out he is strict on himself and reverses it so the ugly seams and “words” of the robe not show. Question on Tur: he opened with “every person” yet brings proof from a scholar; also first he said “wear properly” and for a scholar said “turn.” One answer: first he said every person should at least be careful when donning; for a scholar we find something greater — even if already wearing inside-out he removes and wears properly, as explained.
