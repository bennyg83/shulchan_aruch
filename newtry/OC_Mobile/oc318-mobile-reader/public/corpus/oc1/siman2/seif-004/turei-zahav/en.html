Put on the right shoe, etc. The right has importance for big toe of hand and foot; the left too for tefillin — therefore the Gemara in Rabbi Yochanan’s name said one may do as he wishes, but Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak said a God-fearing person fulfills both honors: tying on the left, for tefillin’s importance lies in the tying. Tur did not write removing shoes as Shulchan Aruch does here, nor the law of our shoes though mentioned in Talmud — in my humble view halakhah follows Rabbi Yochanan that one may do as he wishes, but when both honors can be fulfilled one should do the preferred mitzvah; where impossible to fulfill both, do as you wish — but Beit Yosef wrote on removing shoes not like Tur’s view, and likewise on our shoes.
