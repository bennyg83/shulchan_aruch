(S.k. 4) The right; when washing, etc. Each time one washes the more honored limb first; the right is more important for the hand’s big toe regarding blood and oil for the metzora and for chalitzah. The left is more important because one ties hand-tefillin on the left. Therefore for shoes one puts the right on first as it is more important; for tying the left is more important so one ties the left first; not so washing and anointing where there is no tying — then right first; but the head is more important, so when washing the whole body the head first.
