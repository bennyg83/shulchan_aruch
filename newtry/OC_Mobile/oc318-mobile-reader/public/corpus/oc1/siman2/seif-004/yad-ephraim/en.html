(S.k. 6) Saintly conduct even less than four cubos. Bach holds even four cubos is only saintliness; Magen Avraham did not cite him here because Eshel Avraham explains Beit Yosef holds four cubos may be actual prohibition — else why mention four at all; strained to say as Bach that it is a stringency that even short walking indoors is forbidden, see there. Yet Shulchan Aruch’s wording suggests: for erect posture it says “forbidden,” for four cubos “he shall not walk” — implying walking four cubos is not strict prohibition; Bach inferred from Shulchan Aruch not saying “cover his head.” Hence less than four cubos might have no saintliness either — else why mention four, as for erect posture where no measure is given; Bach who wrote saintliness even less than four based on Sefer HaYirah and Moreh Nevuchim which do not mention four, see there — therefore Magen Avraham brought his words as “saintly conduct even less than four” to say: for four cubos perhaps prohibition exists, and less than four one should still concern saintliness; see Machatzis HaShekel who elaborated.
