(5) The right — for we find in the Torah that the right is always primary regarding the big toe of hand and foot and for everything we put right before left.

(6) And tie it — for regarding tying we find the Torah gave importance to the left, on which one ties the hand-tefillin. With stockings alone one need not put the left first for tying. A left-handed person who lays tefillin on what is everyone else’s right should put the right first for tying as well.

(7) Right first — and if only a left shoe is available he waits until they bring him a right one. When washing and anointing, the right first; if anointing the whole body, the head first because it is “king” over all the limbs; see Magen Avraham.
