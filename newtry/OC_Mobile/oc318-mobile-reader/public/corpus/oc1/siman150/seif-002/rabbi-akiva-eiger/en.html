Seif 4 — One who builds opposite a window in the synagogue — in teshuva Teshurat Chayim to Maharachash part 1 siman 11 he elaborated on this and concluded it is a lone opinion. And see in teshuva Mahar Elazar Sasson siman 192 and in teshuva Noda B'Yehuda part 2 siman 16 who wrote one should not reject Agudah's words, but in any case widening eight cubits suffices per Keneset HaGedolah Choshen Mishpat siman 154 in Tur letter 71.
