There in mechaber seif 3 in hagahah — Hagahot Maimoniyot. NB: requires study — it is explained there the roof was sloped, and from the main law no repair was needed at all; Rabbeinu Tam said as a mere stringency to raise one cubit from the synagogue; but where the upper room is higher than the synagogue, raising one cubit does not help — see there. Know that in my humble opinion there are three views here: R. Chananel — they compel lowering his house since it was built forbidden, and raising the synagogue afterward does not help; Raosh — requires specifically that the synagogue be raised, as written "to elevate the house of our Heaven," and lowering the house does not help since he does not act on the synagogue itself; Rashi — neither works, and it is crooked that cannot be fixed, for the prohibition at the time of building stands and he receives punishment that it will ultimately be destroyed; rather one must not build it tall initially, and if he transgressed his repair does not help to escape the above punishment — wondrous, and forget.
