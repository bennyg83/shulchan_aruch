They do not build, etc. — it appears specifically in a city where all are Israel; but now in our exile when we dwell among gentiles — only occasionally, and they are jealous of us if we build high — better to fulfill it ourselves; why should we appear proud; we should lower our houses further to satisfy all opinions, heaven and human beings (Bach). One should make the entrance for us in the west, and likewise north and south (in Hagahot Maimonides chapter 11 of Hilchot Tefillah).
