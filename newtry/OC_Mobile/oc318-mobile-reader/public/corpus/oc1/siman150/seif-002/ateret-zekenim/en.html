"One should not build," etc. It appears that this applies specifically in a city whose inhabitants are all Jews. Nowadays in our exile, when we live as a small minority among non-Jews and they become jealous of us if we build tall structures, it is preferable to apply to ourselves, "Why should you make yourself conspicuous?" and to lower our houses further, thereby satisfying all views, both before Heaven and before people. [Bach] For us, the entrance should be made on the west, and likewise toward the north and south. [Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 11 of Hilchot Tefillah]
