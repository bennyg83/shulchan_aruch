Bimah. One should not make more than six steps up to the bimah. Zohar, Vayakhel. It is forbidden to sit between the bimah and the heichal with one’s back toward the heichal, for this is disgraceful. There is also a prohibition, because when the person standing on the bimah bows to recite a blessing, he appears to be bowing to that person. Levush; Taz; Magen Avraham. (Emunas Shmuel, siman 35, writes that if a charity trustee had the synagogue plastered or whitewashed with charity funds, one should not inscribe in the synagogue, “This was done by order of so-and-so, the charity trustee.” Even if it was already written, it may be erased, unless it was done with his own money.)
