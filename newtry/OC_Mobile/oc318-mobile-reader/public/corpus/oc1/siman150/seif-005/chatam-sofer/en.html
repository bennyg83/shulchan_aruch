There, seif 5, in the gloss. In the middle of the synagogue. See Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim 28. There he discusses the placement of the bimah. After citing Rambam and Kesef Mishneh, he writes that logically, since we regard the bimah upon which the Torah portion of the korbanos is read as analogous to the altar, which is why on Sukkos we circle the bimah just as they circled the altar, it should resemble the inner altar that stood in the Heichal before the Aron HaKodesh. That altar, upon which the ketores was offered, stood in the middle of the structure, aligned between the menorah and the table, as Rashi explains in Parashas Terumah and as is stated in the Gemara and Rambam. Since our bimah stands inside like the inner altar, it should therefore be placed in the middle of the synagogue, to model it after the Beis HaMikdash as much as possible. He concludes that even according to Kesef Mishneh we should not alter the practice, and certainly in an old synagogue one may not change it, because a beam that merited being placed in the north, and so forth. He concludes: The principle is that anything novel is prohibited by the Torah in every place. See there.
