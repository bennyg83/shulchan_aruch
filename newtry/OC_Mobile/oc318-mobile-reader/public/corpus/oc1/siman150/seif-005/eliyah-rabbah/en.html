[5] They pray toward the west, etc. For us, who pray as stated in siman 94, it is best, where possible, to place the entrance on the west. Levush. Maharshal writes that since we have a building before the entrance, it should not be placed on the west. Perishah writes that for us there is no concern at all that the entrance be opposite the aron, since we cannot fulfill the verse concerning the Sanctuary, whose entrance faced east. One should not make more than six steps to the bimah. Zohar, Vayakhel. Emunas Shmuel, siman 35, writes that a charity trustee who hired workers to plaster or whitewash the synagogue with charity funds may not inscribe in the synagogue, “This was done by order of so-and-so, the trustee.” Even if it was written years ago, the congregation may object and erase it. Only one who personally performs the work or dedicates his own money may have his name written.
