Note: This requires examination, for it is explained there that the roof was sloped, and according to the strict law no correction was required at all. Rabbeinu Tam merely said, as an added stringency, to raise one corner of the synagogue. Where an upper story is higher than the synagogue, however, raising one corner does not help; see there. Know also that, in my humble opinion, there are three views here. According to Rabbeinu Chananel, they compel him to lower his house, since it was built in violation, and subsequently raising the synagogue does not help. According to Rabbeinu Asher, it is specifically necessary to raise the synagogue, as the verse states, ‘to exalt the House of our God’; lowering the house does not help, since he performs no act upon the synagogue itself. According to Rashi, neither correction is effective, and it is a distortion that cannot be repaired. The violation was committed at the time of construction, and he remains subject to the punishment that the building will ultimately be destroyed. Rather, one must initially refrain from building higher than the synagogue; if he transgressed, a later correction does not save him from that punishment. Examine carefully and you will find this so.
