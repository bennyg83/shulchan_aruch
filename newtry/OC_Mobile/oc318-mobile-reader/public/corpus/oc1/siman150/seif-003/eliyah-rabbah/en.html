Sefer Chassidim, siman 819, writes that one should not pour urine from a window onto a place through which people walk to the synagogue, lest those going to synagogue be soiled by it. See further there from siman 815. Responsa Re’em, siman 79, writes that even if grave sins, such as promiscuity and the like, were committed in a synagogue, it remains permitted to pray there and place a Sefer Torah there. See above, siman 21. She’eris Yosef, siman 27, writes that neighbors whose house emits a foul odor into the synagogue must remove the source, even if their house preceded construction of the synagogue. See Magen Avraham at the end of siman 154.
