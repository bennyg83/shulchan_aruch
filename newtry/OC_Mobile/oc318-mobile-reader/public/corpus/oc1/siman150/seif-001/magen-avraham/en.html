And Kesuvim. So that anyone from the congregation who wishes may read from them. Choshen Mishpat, beginning of siman 163. It appears to me that this applies specifically in their times, when permission was granted to write only Torah, Neviim, and Kesuvim. Nowadays, however, they are also obligated to purchase volumes of Gemara from which both children and adults can learn. See Yoreh Deah, end of siman 270.
