(1) [Seif 1] They compel the residents of the city one another, etc. — and even a minority compels the majority — Choshen Mishpat at the start of siman 163 in the gloss. And this is specifically when there are ten, as written below note 4 in the name of Rambam and Beit Yosef Levush. For this reason note 2; and it appears that in small congregations through coercion one compels another to go to the synagogue to pray on Yom Kippur if he has no constraint — Bach, Taz. So Keneset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur note 1; and see above siman 55 and our words there with Heaven's help.
