Synagogue. The Baer Heitev before me cited Maharam Padua as ruling that construction of a synagogue is assessed per capita. See there. He did not examine Maharam himself, for there he ruled the opposite, that construction of a synagogue is assessed according to wealth. See there, siman 42. Where the congregation benefits from it, they may object to people praying as a minyan in their homes rather than in the synagogue. See Knesses HaGedolah.
And Writings. Nowadays they are also obligated to purchase Gemaras so that whoever wishes may study. See Yoreh Deah, siman 270.
