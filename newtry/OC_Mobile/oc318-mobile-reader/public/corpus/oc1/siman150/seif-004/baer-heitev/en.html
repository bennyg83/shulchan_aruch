It is insufficient. This is where the congregation has acquired a prescriptive right to the windows. See Magen Avraham, siman 154. Rama writes in a responsum, end of siman 32, that where the synagogue courtyard is not used for private activities, the congregation may not object to an individual opening windows toward it. If, however, the congregation opens synagogue windows toward his courtyard, he may object. See Magen Avraham. (In Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he challenges Magen Avraham on this point.)
