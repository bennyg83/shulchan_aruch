Seif 4. One who builds opposite a synagogue window. Responsa Toras Chayim of Maharchash, part 1, siman 11, discusses this at length and concludes that it is a solitary opinion. See Responsa Maharam Alshakar 192 and Responsa Noda BiYehudah, part 2, siman 16, which writes that the Agudah’s ruling should not be rejected. Nevertheless, a distance of eight amos is sufficient, based on Knesses HaGedolah, Choshen Mishpat 154, in the glosses to the Tur, letter 71.
