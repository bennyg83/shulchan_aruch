To eat immediately, etc. Nevertheless, he must eat a kezayit during actual nighttime in order to fulfill the three Shabbos meals, as Sefer Chasidim siman 269 and the later authorities write. Olat Tamid says he does not know the reason, since tosefet Shabbos is d'oraisa; Taz at the end of siman 291 writes similarly. In my humble opinion, the three meals are derived from the three occurrences of "today," implying the actual day of Shabbos and not merely its extension. Shelah writes that some are careful, even in summer, not to eat until close to sunset. See below, end of siman 271. Magen Avraham writes that Tosafot, the Rosh, and Ri imply one may finish the meal while it is still day because after plag haminchah it is halachically regarded as night, though he concludes it is good to be stringent. One who is hungry may certainly rely on the lenient view and begin eating, even if he follows the practice of repeating Keriat Shema at tzeit hakochavim. Since he already recited Keriat Shema with the congregation while it was still day, he may eat. The prohibition applies only to one who did not recite Keriat Shema earlier with the congregation. Although I wrote in siman 235 that during the week it is good to be careful, on Shabbos there is no need for stringency here.
