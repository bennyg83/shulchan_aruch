And to eat immediately. R' Yisrael issued a difficulty: behold we rule that it is forbidden to eat before Shema; and one may say that since he read from before plag hamincha onward, even though there is an opinion that he must return and read when it darkens — nevertheless he is permitted to eat beforehand — end of his words; and he did not see what Rosh wrote, that Rav read Shema after tzeit and did not join geulah to tefillah, and what he ate before Shema was because he had begun eating half an hour before its time — and so too Levush — and see siman 235. In any case, those who practice praying Maariv on Erev Shabbat from before then — nevertheless when half an hour close to the time of Shema arrives they are forbidden to begin eating, for they do not fulfill at all the nighttime Shema.
