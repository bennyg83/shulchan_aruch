3) [Seif 2] They pray Maariv early, etc. In siman 293, Tur writes in the name of Ritz Gias: Since all Jewish people have adopted the view of the Sages that Maariv is not prayed until nightfall, one should not pray the Shabbos Maariv on Friday, nor the weekday Maariv on Shabbos. One should neither recite Kiddush nor Havdalah until the day actually begins or ends, whether wine is available or unavailable, because bread can be used. These are his words. Beis Yosef cites this in the present siman but writes: As a matter of practical halachah, since Rambam and Rosh agree upon one view, we adopt it, contrary to Ritz Gias. These are his words. The Shulchan Aruch therefore rules, 'They pray Maariv early,' etc., in accordance with Rambam, Rosh, Tur, and the other commentators cited by Beis Yosef; see there. Shaar HaKavanos, however, states that the reception of Shabbos should occur precisely at sunset, implying that Maariv should be prayed at night. We cited this above in siman 262, paragraph 32; see there. Therefore, one who follows either authority has acted properly.
