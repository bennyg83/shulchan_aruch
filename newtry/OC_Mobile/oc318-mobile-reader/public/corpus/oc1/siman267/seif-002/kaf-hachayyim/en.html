2) There: They do not fall upon their faces. Even if they pray Minchah Gedolah, they do not fall upon their faces, because the additional sanctity of Shabbos begins to be revealed on Friday immediately after the fifth hour of the day, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 62a; see there. Therefore, one who eats bread on Friday from the fifth hour onward should not recite Al Naharos Bavel, but rather Shir HaMaalos BeShuv, etc. See above, siman 157, paragraph 19.
