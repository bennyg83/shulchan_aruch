1) [Seif 1] During the Minchah prayer, etc. The disciples of the Arizal write that one should not wear tefillin, even if he ordinarily wears them every day at Minchah. Machazik Berachah, paragraph 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1. We wrote the same above in siman 25, paragraph 100; see there. Even if a communal fast occurs that day, and even if Minchah Gedolah is prayed on Friday, tefillin should not be worn. Kaf HaChaim, siman 19, paragraph 5; Petach HaDevir, paragraph 1.
