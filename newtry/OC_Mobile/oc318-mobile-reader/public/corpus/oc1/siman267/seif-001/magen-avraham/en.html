We advance. Tur wrote in siman 293 in the name of Ritz Giat: in a place where they practiced like the rabbis who do not pray Maariv until it darkens — one should not pray the Shabbat [prayer] on Erev Shabbat nor Motzei Shabbat on Shabbat, and one should not make kiddush and havdalah except at the entry of the day, whether wine is available or not, for it is possible with bread in any case; and since Rambam and Rosh agree with one opinion like them, they ruled not like Ritz Giat — end of his words, Beit Yosef. And it requires study, for in the Gemara they say: from the fact that Rav prayed the Shabbat [prayer] on Erev Shabbat — learn that the halachah is like R' Yehudah; it implies that for the rabbis it is forbidden; and so too Rosh there that all hold like R' Yehudah; and so too Shagas Aryeh; and so too Kesef Mishnah chapter 3 of Tefillah that Rambam's view is that Shabbat and weekday are equal — if so his words contradict each other; however in Terumat HaDeshen it implies that on Erev Shabbat one may advance, for specifically regarding Motzei Shabbat we require a matter of mitzvah — see and study, for he is against the Gemara's conclusion. Afterward I found that Halakhot Gedolot wrote thus: Rav prayed the Shabbat [prayer] on Erev Shabbat and R' Yirmiyah prayed behind him the weekday prayer and they did not stop R' Yirmiyah — learn that not only from plag hamincha alone do we pray mincha and from then on Maariv does Rav speak, for they did not stop R' Yirmiyah who prayed mincha; and Rav holds thus: if he wants to pray the Shabbat [prayer] on Erev Shabbat he prays — meaning we add from weekday onto the holy — end of his words. And one must say per him that initially he wanted to resolve from Rav that the halachah is like R' Yehudah, but per the conclusion that if he wants he does as the master, etc. — he may advance on Erev Shabbat even though on a weekday he acts like the rabbis; and so Rambam wrote — see there, that not as Kesef Mishnah wrote. And it appears to me to give a good reason: for Maariv was established corresponding to limbs and fats that digest at night, and on Shabbat one may not offer them, for we say: and not a weekday olah on Shabbat, as explained on Shabbos daf 104; and similar to this in Beit Yosef siman 276 in the name of Kol Bo. And further that Mordechai wrote one may do sometimes thus and sometimes thus — and even though we do not rule thus, nevertheless on Erev Shabbat one may rely on him; however at the end of Moed Katan he wrote that on one day one should not do two leniencies that contradict each other; and therefore one must be careful on Erev Shabbat to pray mincha before plag hamincha — and all this for those who pray Maariv at night; and see siman 273. And in writings it is said that the Ari would pray Maariv at night even on Erev Shabbat; and the Gra wrote that the rabbis agree on Erev Shabbat and he did not examine the Gemara. Also what he wrote that R' Yehudah and the rabbis dispute in the dispute of Terumat HaDeshen and Levush as written siman 273 is not precise at all, for the rabbis did not mention plag hamincha at all and there is no hint to his words. Also what he wrote that for Levush plag hamincha is 2½ hours before tzeit — is not precise, for if we calculate the day from sunrise until sunset it is a mil of 2/5 of an hour, and if so it is 2 hours and ¾ of an hour and a tenth — see siman 261. And what Magen Avraham wrote siman 299; also what he wrote that there is a practical difference regarding an individual who erred, etc. — it appears this is included in that in the Gemara it refers according to Rav who holds like R' Yehudah on Erev Shabbat; and Bach wrote he may accept Shabbat before Maariv prayer. Maggid Mishnah wrote: even though from before the day he made kiddush or havdalah, he is permitted in the evening to eat at this kiddush and to eat and do melacha at this havdalah — and this is the view of some geonim and many later authorities and as our rabbi's words, and such is the essential ruling — end of his words; and so Beit Yosef siman 271 in the name of Rashba; and in Mordechai chapter 2 of Megillah he wrote that the view that Shabbat additions are d'rabbanan — nevertheless one fulfills from nightfall with this kiddush, since at the hour he makes kiddush he will later come to a d'oraisa obligation — see there at length. And it requires study, for a minor does not discharge an adult as written siman 186; and even though he will later come to a d'oraisa obligation on Erev Shabbat — see siman 261 seif 2. It is written in Shelah in the name of Shach that nevertheless one must eat a kezayit at night in order to fulfill three meals on Shabbat; and so Bach siman 472 in the name of R' Levi of Prague; but in Tosafos and Rosh it implies one may finish the meal from before the day; and so Terumat HaDeshen siman 1; and so is clear in Ravyah chapter 1; and the reason is that after plag hamincha we consider it night; and nevertheless it is good to be stringent.
