(1) [Seif 1] One may not demolish a synagogue in order to build another synagogue, etc. — for so it is in Bava Batra 3b: R. Chisda said a person should not demolish a synagogue until he builds another synagogue; some say due to negligence, some say due to prayer; what is between them — when there is another synagogue: per view due to prayer no concern for there is another; per view due to negligence there is concern; likewise Yerushalmi Megillah chapter Bnei HaIr — reason negligence; likewise Rambam chapter 11 Hilchot Tefillah law 12; likewise Raosh there in Bava Batra as the view due to negligence — see there; likewise Tur — reason negligence; maran wrote in Beit Yosef: even though there is a view there reason is prayer, our Rabbi was not concerned with that reason; Rif and Rambam wrote only this reason maran wrote; likewise R' Yosef — as in beginning of Bava Batra we establish reason negligence, even if they have another synagogue for prayer — see there; and so appears maran's view here in Shulchan Aruch; likewise Or Zarua section 1 on maran's words: per this reason even if they have another synagogue to pray, they may not demolish this synagogue due to negligence; Magen Avraham note 1; but Ran wrote: whenever they have another fixed synagogue, for all permitted, for there is neither prayer concern nor negligence — see there; Beit Yosef and Or Zarua brought; Levush likewise.
