[1] In order to build, etc. Some permit demolishing in order to rebuild if there is another established synagogue in the city, while others prohibit it. Taz and Shulchan Aruch hold that all agree it is permitted if there is another established synagogue; they prohibit it only where there is no established synagogue and merely a temporary place to pray. So write Agudah, Tosafos at the beginning of Bava Basra, and Rabbeinu Yerucham, folio 28. Nachalas Shivah, siman 27, writes that if the synagogue is rented from the local ruler, they may not leave it until they have first rented another.
