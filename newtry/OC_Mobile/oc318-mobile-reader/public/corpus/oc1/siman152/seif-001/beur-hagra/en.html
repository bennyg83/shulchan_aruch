Seif 1: Lest, etc. Although Marimar and Mar Zutra hold of another reason, nevertheless this reason is primary, for Rabbi Ashi and Ravina there hold it, as does the Gemara at the beginning of the fourth chapter of Megillah. Ran writes that even Marimar and Mar Zutra hold that the concern is negligence, for otherwise the statements of Ravina and Rabbi Ashi should not have been placed after Marimar and Mar Zutra. Rather, they differ only where there is no established synagogue: there we are concerned for negligence, but where there is an established synagogue, we are not. This follows the reading of the texts, “where there is a place to pray.” See siman 153, seif 7, in the gloss, “All this does not discuss,” etc.
