(Taz note 1) And from this we learn his words need explanation, for the Rosh strained to resolve that which Marimar and Ravina built a summer house in shtei ruchot — for they hold it is because of shade and do not hold because of negligence — and the halacha is not so — see there; and if it were held that it is permitted when they have another fixed synagogue, otherwise this statement would not make sense, as Tosafot and Ran wrote in truth; and it is possible the Rosh was bothered: from where is there concern they will be lax to build more than half a year and they will not have a synagogue in another shtei ruchot.
