One recites a berachah over it. This is imprecise, for the Mordechai wrote this specifically about a myrtle, since he wrote it in the name of Sefer Yere'im, and in Sefer Yere'im it is stated explicitly about a myrtle. But regarding a lulav it is invalid according to all opinions, and so it appears from the Gemara; this is from the Bayis Chadash. This is also the main ruling for one who examines Sefer Yere'im. The Baal HaMinhagim and the Rema did not examine sufficiently. It is stated in the Yerushalmi: if its top was cut off, this is not hadar. If so, it is invalid all seven days. So too wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham: all invalid items are invalid all seven days because they do not have hadar, except for one that split and one whose tiyomes split, which are invalid because of complete taking and are valid on the second day. See siman 649.
