[6] Levush: In pressing circumstances, etc. At the end of siman 649 it says that the custom is to recite a berachah over dry ones even when other moist ones are available. One must say that since he himself has no moist one, even though others have moist ones, it is considered a pressing circumstance, and he recites a berachah over his own.
