If one has only a completely dry lulav, even if it is separated and breaks with the fingernail, one recites a berachah in pressing circumstances, even if others have one, as in siman 649, se'if 6. Here one can say the case is where he can obtain a lulav through effort. One can also say that when it breaks with the fingernail, one does not recite a berachah in pressing circumstances; with Hashem's help this will be explained in siman 649. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 36, se'if 13, where the Rema explains that if the lung dried and one scratches it with the fingernail and a mark remains, but here the explanation is that it breaks; see Perishah.
