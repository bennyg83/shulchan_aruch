A lulav where the majority of leaves have dried out is invalid. The measure of dryness is when the greenness is finished and it whitens. {Rama: [or its spine] [Tur] Rema: Some say that it is not considered dried until a fingernail can disintegrate it due to its dryness (Tur quoting Tosafot) and this is the practice in these countries, where there are not palm trees (Hagahot Maimoni ch. 7).}
