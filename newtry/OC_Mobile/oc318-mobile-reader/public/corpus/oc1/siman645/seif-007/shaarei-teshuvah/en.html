(Se'if 8 in Shulchan Aruch) But if it bent backward, etc. Machazik Berachah wrote in the name of my teacher, the Rav, of blessed memory, in the book Get Mekushar, that initially one should be careful that it not be bent at all, to avoid any doubt. But where this is impossible, one may be lenient when it is bent backward, for our eyes see that this is its manner of growth; see there.
