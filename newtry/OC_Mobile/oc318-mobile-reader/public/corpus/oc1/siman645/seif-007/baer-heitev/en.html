That there is. The Magen Avraham wrote that the Rema and the Baal HaMinhagim did not examine carefully, for the Mordechai wrote this specifically regarding a myrtle, but regarding a lulav it is invalid according to all opinions, and this is the main ruling. It is stated in the Yerushalmi: if its top was cut off, this is not hadar. If so, it is invalid all seven days. So too wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham: all invalid items are invalid all seven days because they lack hadar, except for one that split and one whose tiyomes split, which are invalid because of complete taking and are valid on the second day. See siman 649. These are his words.
