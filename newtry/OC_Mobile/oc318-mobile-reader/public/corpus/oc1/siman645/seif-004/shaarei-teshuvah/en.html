(Se'if 3) In Shulchan Aruch, in the Rema: and if that leaf was not doubled, etc. Machazik Berachah wrote in the name of Zera Emes that one year there were many lulavim whose tiyomes, from the beginning of its formation, was only one leaf. We took a vote about them and invalidated them. It appears that one should validate them on the other days, and invalidate only on the first two days. In pressing circumstances one should validate even on the first day; see there.
