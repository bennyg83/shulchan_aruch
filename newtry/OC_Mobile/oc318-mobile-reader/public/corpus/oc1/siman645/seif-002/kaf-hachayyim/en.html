A lulav whose leaves have separated, etc. As long as its leaves are attached and close to one another, it is the choicest manner of performing the mitzvah, because its leaves must be bound together and not separated, as is derived from the word kapos, and this is its beauty. Levush, seif 2. If one took palm branches while they were still on the tree, before their leaves had separated at all, it appears that this is not considered a lulav without leaves. Derishah. So too ruled Chayei Adam, klal 149, sec. 8.
