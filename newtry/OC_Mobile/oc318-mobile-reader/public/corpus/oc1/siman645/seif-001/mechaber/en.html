The laws of the lulav. It contains 9 seifim: A lulav whose leaves have separated from one another but have not become loose and hanging like the leaves of a palm branch is valid, even if it was not bound. {Rama: Nevertheless, the preferred fulfillment of the mitzvah is with a lulav whose leaves are not completely separated. [Maggid Mishneh and Ran, chapter Lulav HaGazul]}
