[A] Lolev. In Gematria 36 12, that is, 36 righteous in every generation and against 36 Tractates and 12 we were Torah beginning with Beit and ending with Lemad, and Lulev in Gematria Haim we were Torah (Mahiri 15), and in Maharil alluded to those who buy an Atrog with Lulev thus gain the life of this world and the next world, so far. And righteous sons who have a heart for the Torah. And in the book of education Parshat, it is said that an etrog is similar to the heart, which is the dwelling place of the intellect, to imply that he will serve his Creator with his intellect, a rose is similar to an avenue to indicate that he directs his body to his work, blessed be He, a myrtle is similar to the eyes to imply that he will not let his eyes wander in the day of his heart's joy, a willow is similar to the lips to imply that he restrains his mouth and corrects his words and fears the the Master, may he be blessed even in times of joy:
