He answered that the tiyoma did not split down to the spine. See letter 4. According to those who are stringent, as long as the majority did not split, but only the minority or a tefach, it is valid. But when it split until two leaf-heads appear like two separated leaves, like a hemnek, it is invalid even in its minority. This requires analysis, for this is very common, and one must be careful about it.
