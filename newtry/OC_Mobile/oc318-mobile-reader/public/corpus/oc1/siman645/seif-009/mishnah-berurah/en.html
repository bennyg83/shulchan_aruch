(8) In the majority of the leaves. This means the majority of each and every leaf, for the majority is like the whole, and it is as though the lulav were without leaves. This applies specifically within the required measure of the lulav, which is four tefachim, as below in siman 650. However, if the majority of the leaves of the portion of the lulav beyond the required measure broke, but the majority of the required measure of the lulav remained whole, one should be uncertain whether we follow the majority of the whole lulav and invalidate it, or the majority of the required measure and validate it.
