These four species are all mutually necessary. If one lacks one of them, they should not bless on the rest and should just take them for memory's sake . If they are all in front of him and he took them one by one, he has fulfilled his obligation. {Rama: whether on the first or subsequent days (Ros"h, Rabbeinu Yerucham 8:3, Mahari"k Shorsch 41) RAMA: as long as they are all in front of him (Rambam ch. 6). The lulav should be taken first and blessed on, with the intention that it apply to the others. If one talked between them, he must bless on each individually (Hagahot Maimoni).}
