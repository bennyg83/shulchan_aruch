That is not cut off. See Baer Heitev. See Devar Shmuel, siman 84, regarding one congregation that on the first day of the festival did not have any valid hadas species except one branch. Since this matter is subject to dispute, for the Geonim, the Rambam, the Tur, and the Maharik rule like Rabbi Yishmael that three are required, while the Ramban and those who agree with him rule like Rabbi Akiva, the good and proper course is to take that branch with the other three species in order to fulfill the mitzvah out of doubt, without a blessing; see there. It is clear from there that even in pressing circumstances he holds that it does not help with respect to the blessing, even though it is not cut off. See Responsa Knesses HaGedolah, volume 2, that if after the congregation took them it was discovered that there were only two hadas branches, they should go back and take without a blessing. The Birkei Yosef brought this; see there.
