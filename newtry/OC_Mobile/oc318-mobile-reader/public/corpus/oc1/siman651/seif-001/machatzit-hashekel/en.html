The Magen Avraham wrote: it is explained in siman 595. There it is speaking regarding shofar, about one who is not expert in the blasts and not in the Mussaf prayer, and before him are two towns: in one they blow shofar but are not expert in prayer, and in one they pray but are not expert in blowing. He should go to the town where they blow, because blowing is a Torah obligation and prayer is rabbinic. From this we derive regarding lulav, for on the first day it is also a Torah obligation. Its law in this regard is like shofar; and see there for the other distinctions of law in this matter.
