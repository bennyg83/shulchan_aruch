It is explained in siman 595 that on the first day of Yom Tov it is preferable to travel to a place where a lulav is available rather than to a place where there is a congregation for prayer. With one whose tip is not severed. Ramban maintains that even one myrtle whose tip is severed suffices, as Beit Yosef and Ran write. He is worthy of reliance in pressing circumstances; see the end of siman 645, seif 6.
Around the myrtle. Nevertheless, even if he bound it to the lulav with a material of another species, it is not an interposition, because anything added for its beautification is not an interposition. This is stated in the Gemara. The same applies if he bound the myrtle to the lulav, as is implied by Tosafos.
And this is the custom. Even on a weekday. Darkei Moshe.
Higher. The reason is based upon Kabbalah. Darkei Moshe in the name of Mahari Weil. See Levush. The writings of the Arizal state that one should bind three myrtles, one to the right of the lulav, one to its left, and one in the middle, and two willow branches, one on the right and one on the left. Shelah writes in the name of Mateh Moshe that the willow should be on the left, the myrtle on the right, and the lulav in the middle.
Three knots. Corresponding to the three Patriarchs. Mordechai.
