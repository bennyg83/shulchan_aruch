Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. It does not constitute an interposition, etc. Meaning, between the lulav and his hand, because it was added for adornment. When Rama writes that only an object of the same species does not constitute an interposition, he refers to something not added for adornment. For this reason one should take care regarding the string, since it is not for adornment. Magen Avraham therefore concludes that if he bound the myrtle directly to the lulav, rather than with string, this too is considered adornment and does not interpose.
