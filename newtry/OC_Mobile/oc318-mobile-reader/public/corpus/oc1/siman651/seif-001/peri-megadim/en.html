To make. See the Taz. The upper knot of the lulav, in any event, must leave a tefach of lulav projecting above it, the measure required for shaking, as in siman 651. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 7, which implies that there are three knots on the lulav itself, besides the fourth knot that binds the three species together, and above them there should be less than a tefach of lulav. Regarding the two heads, see Taz, Sukkah 37b, and Rava there.
