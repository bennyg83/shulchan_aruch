And they shake. See Taz, Tosafot, Sukkah 37b, s.v. at Hodu, and the Rosh there. In Levush the verse is cited, 'Then shall the trees of the forest sing before Hashem, for He comes to judge,' with the letter beis added, and He gave a sign into our hand; see Tosafot there. One does not shake at the word Hashem. The allusion is 'they shall sing before Hashem,' but not at the word Hashem itself. See Magen Avraham, note 19, and see there.
