When he recites the blessing. Even if he takes the lulav outside the time of Hallel, he waves it in all directions, although he will take it again during Hallel. Rabbi Levi ibn Chaviv; Magen Avraham. He should divide the wavings during the words “Give thanks to Hashem, for He is good,” some during the first part and some during “for His kindness endures forever”; likewise during “Please, Hashem.” Mateh Moshe. He should not wave while pronouncing the Divine Name. Kol Bo, Hagahos Maimoniyos, and Darkei Moshe. Accordingly, during Hodu he should perform one waving on each word, and during Ana two wavings on each word. At the conclusion of each waving he should bring the tip of the lulav toward his chest. Sefer HaKavanos. Maharil remained standing in his place facing east during all the wavings. He merely turned his hand toward the different directions; for the westward waving, he raised the lulav over his shoulder behind him. See there.
