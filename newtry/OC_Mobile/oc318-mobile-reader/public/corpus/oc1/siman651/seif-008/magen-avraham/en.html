At the time he recites the blessing. Even when taking the lulav outside the time of Hallel, he waves it in all directions, although he will take it again during Hallel. Responsa Maharam ben Chaviv, siman 80; Tosafos and Rosh. This is also implied by what is written in siman 657. He should divide the wavings during “Give thanks to Hashem, for He is good,” performing some during the first phrase and some during “for His kindness endures forever,” and similarly during “Please, Hashem.” Mateh Moshe. He should not wave while pronouncing Hashem’s Name. Kol Bo, Hagahos Maimoniyos, and Darkei Moshe. Accordingly, during “Give thanks” he performs one waving for each word, and during “Please” two wavings for each word. During every waving he should bring the tip of the lulav back toward his chest. Writings of the Arizal.
