And they shake at every "Give thanks." For it is written, "Then the trees of the forest will sing," and afterward it is written, "Give thanks to Hashem," etc., and afterward it is written, "And say: save us." Therefore they shake at "Please, save now."
That the prayer leader shakes at "Let Yisrael now say," etc. Even though he does not say "Give thanks" at that time, nevertheless he shakes along with the congregation. The distinction between "Let Yisrael now say" and "Let them now say" appears to me as follows: when he says "Let Yisrael now say," it is like a reminder to the congregation that they should say "Give thanks," and therefore he shakes with them. This is unlike "Let them now say," where he is not speaking about all of Yisrael.
