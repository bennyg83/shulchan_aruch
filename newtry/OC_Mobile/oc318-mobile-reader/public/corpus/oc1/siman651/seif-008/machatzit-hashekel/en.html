(Subparagraph 8) One should take, etc. Similar to tefillin. That is to say, regarding tefillin it is written: "and you shall bind them as a sign upon your hand," and "they shall be totafos between your eyes." We derive from the fact that it first writes "and you shall bind them as a sign upon your hand" that one must first place the hand tefillin.
And from the fact that it is written "and they shall be totafos between your eyes," we derive that all the time they are between your eyes, there should be two. Therefore when one removes them, he must remove the head tefillin first, as is written above in siman 25 and siman 28. Therefore here too, since the verse mentions esrog first, one should take the esrog first, like the hand tefillin. After this we say by reasoning that just as with tefillin one places the hand tefillin first, and when removing them he removes the hand tefillin last, the same applies with lulav. Since he takes the esrog first, when he removes them he should remove the esrog last, even though regarding lulav there is no verse for this as there is with tefillin. Therefore he should not give it to his fellow from his hand, because he must first put down the lulav, while his fellow must first take the esrog. Therefore the first person should put down the lulav, placing it on a vessel or on the ground, and afterward put down the esrog there as well. Then his fellow should take the esrog from that place, and afterward the lulav. The Shelah wrote to be careful about this in a place where they sell the standing near the cantor during Hallel.
See what the Magen Avraham wrote about this in subparagraph 12.
