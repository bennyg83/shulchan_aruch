See Machazik Berachah in the name of Zera Emes, that one who takes the lulav first has what to rely upon; on the contrary, one may say that this is preferable, and this is how the words of Tosafos, the Rosh, the Tur, and the Beis Yosef flow; see there. So too the Dagul Merevavah wrote that the primary ruling is that he should take the lulav first. However, he also disagrees with what the Magen Avraham wrote, that when removing them one should remove the lulav first, for in Menachos, regarding the fact that one removes the head tefillin first, it asks: from where do we know this, etc.; see there. The words of the Magen Avraham are from the Mateh Moshe, who wrote so in the name of Even Shoev, and he gave the reason because the verse put "the fruit of a beautiful tree" first. Regarding tefillin we say: all the time that they are between your eyes, there should be two; and likewise, all the time that the lulav is in the right hand, there should be two in their sanctity; see there.
