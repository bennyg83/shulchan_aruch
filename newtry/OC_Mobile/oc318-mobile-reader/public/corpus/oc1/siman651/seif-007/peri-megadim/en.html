Regarding practice, it seems at first glance from here that one removes tefillin before Mussaf, after Hallel and Torah reading. See siman 31 in the Taz, who writes that on Chol HaMoed Pesach as well one should remove them before Hallel, and Magen Avraham, siman 25, note 31, that on Pesach the shaliach tzibbur removes them after Hallel, and on Sukkos before Hallel, so as not to burden the congregation. But if there is no burden, it is proper for the shaliach tzibbur also to remove them before Hallel, and the congregation should remove them during the repetition of the prayer after Kedushah, and this is the proper practice. See Eliyah Rabbah, siman 25, note 25, that on Rosh Chodesh this is after the Kedushah of U'va LeTziyon and before the Kaddishim. It implies there that the shaliach tzibbur should not remove them on Chol HaMoed Pesach while before the amud, for that is like removing them before his teacher. But on Sukkos, when they sell the esrog, he can step away from before the amud to one side and remove tefillin before Hallel; see there. In Responsa Menachem Azaryah, siman 28, page 2, he writes that it is proper to remove tefillin on Rosh Chodesh when one reaches 'May it be Your will that we observe,' not at the moment he wants to pray Mussaf; see there.
