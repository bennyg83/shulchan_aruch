Se'if 7: if he made, etc. As Tosafos explains there, since he brings proof from hyssop, it is speaking of this sort of case. And see what I wrote below.
And if he wrapped, etc. From what is written there: when you grow the hoshanah, etc.; and Rabbah does not disagree except because it is to beautify it.
Or that he wrapped, etc. From that which is stated at the end of the fourth chapter of Pesachim, that he wrapped his hands in silk, etc.
