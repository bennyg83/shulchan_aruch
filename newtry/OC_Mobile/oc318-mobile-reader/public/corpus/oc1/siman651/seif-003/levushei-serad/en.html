Taz, seif katan 3. The correct reading is: “And even if he is left-handed.” From Tur's word “even,” Beit Yosef infers that a left-handed person follows the same rule as everyone else. See Taz, who explains as follows: not only does the right hand of an ordinary person have precedence, but even the right hand of a left-handed person, whose distinction is only personal to him, nevertheless has precedence for taking the lulav.
