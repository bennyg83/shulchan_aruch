He reversed them. Meaning, if a left-handed person reversed them and took the esrog in the left hand used by everyone else, he has fulfilled the mitzvah. Taz writes: In my humble opinion, even an ordinary person who reversed them, placing the esrog in his right hand and the lulav bundle in his left, has fulfilled the mitzvah after the fact; see there. See Responsa Chut HaShani, siman 28, and Responsa Beit Yaakov, siman 149. Magen Avraham writes that if he took the lulav in his left hand and the esrog in his right, he should take them again without a blessing; see there.
