[Subparagraph 3] And so is the practice even on a weekday; Darkei Moshe. This is the language of the Darkei Moshe: and so is the practice even on a weekday, unlike the implication of the Tur's words below, which imply that specifically on Yom Tov, when it is forbidden to make a complete knot, one should tie in this manner, end quote. The view of the Tur must be explained as follows: just as according to Rabbi Yehudah, who holds that lulav requires binding, we require two knots one upon the other so that it be a complete knot, as stated in the Talmud, the same applies for us. Although we maintain that lulav does not require binding, nevertheless since for the mitzvah it requires binding because of "this is my God and I will beautify Him," on a weekday, when it is possible, one should do as according to Rabbi Yehudah, namely a complete knot, because of "this is my God and I will beautify Him." But the custom is not so. Since for us the knot is only for a mitzvah, for beauty, any knot suffices, since it is beauty.
