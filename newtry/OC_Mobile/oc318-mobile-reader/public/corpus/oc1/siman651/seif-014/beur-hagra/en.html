Se'if 14: because of bal tosif. In the Sifrei: from where do we know that one may not add to the lulav or to tzitzis? The verse states: "do not add," etc. And even though the Sifrei is necessarily in accordance with Rabbi Yehudah, whereas according to the Rabbis this one is by itself, etc., as is written in Sanhedrin, nevertheless, initially it is forbidden.
