Do not add — Tur and Beit Yosef on imprecise language; Gemara teaches we do not add even without binding; even after fulfilling, holding lulav later for love of mitzvah — do not diminish by taking three alone; if he holds them for lulav mitzvah he violates bal tosif; without intent for mitzvah still beware not to add; similar to eating in sukkah on Shemini Atzeret — requires study.
