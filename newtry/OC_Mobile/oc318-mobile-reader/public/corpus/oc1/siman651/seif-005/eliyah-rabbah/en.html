[5] The hadas higher, etc. Because they pull downward, the aravah leaves fall off, and some tear them intentionally. In Eliyahu Zuta I wondered at them, for sometimes most of the leaves fall off, which is invalid in siman 646. It is better to bind it before Sukkos and place the lulav in water, and not to take a new one each day, unless he makes it in a way that the leaves will not fall off. Furthermore, a withered aravah is valid.
