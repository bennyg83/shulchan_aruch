Se'if 5: he should recite the blessing, etc. As is written: all the mitzvos, etc.; and as is written: once he lifted it, etc.
Or, etc. As is written: for example, when he reversed it. But this is not necessary, as is written in the first chapter of Pesachim, 40, that he recites the blessing after they are in his hand and he has fulfilled his obligation with them; and one should not recite the blessing before taking them, as is written in the third chapter of Menachos: from when does he recite the blessing, etc. Also, reversing it does not help, since he recites the blessing over the lulav and they are present before him, they do not prevent fulfillment of one another, as explained in se'if 12. And since the Gemara said that one recites the blessing over the lulav after he has fulfilled his obligation, and it did not find a way to explain how we do this, on the better side he should intend not to fulfill his obligation with it, even according to the one who says that mitzvos do not require intent, as Tosafos wrote there, 39a. See the Ran there.
And he should recite the blessing while standing. For we derive it from counting the Omer. See the Rosh at the end of Pesachim.
And he should not recite the blessing, etc. As is written in Sukkah there: this was taught only, etc. That is to say, at that point there is no mitzvah in it. See Tosafos there, 39a, toward the end of s.v. "before their performance." Nevertheless, not completely, etc.; and once he took it, it has already passed, etc.; and there, 45b, s.v. "one," etc.
