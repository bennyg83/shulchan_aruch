(Subparagraph 5) Three knots corresponding to the three forefathers. The Turei Zahav wrote that if we tie three knots on the lulav, it will be impossible to rustle it at the time of shaking. Therefore one who wants to satisfy the Mordechai, who wrote to make three knots, should count the binding with which he bound the three species together as one knot, and make two knots on the lulav itself, so that together there are three knots. In my humble opinion, perhaps according to his view, if he makes two knots with the three species, one above the other, one knot on the lulav itself is enough. But in the book Eliyah Rabbah he disagrees, and holds that three knots are needed on the lulav itself. It is sufficient if the upper tefach of the lulav has no knot, for then he can still rustle it. He brought proof from the Maharil; see there.
