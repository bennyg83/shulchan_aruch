Se'if 3 A left-handed person takes the lulav in the right hand of ordinary people. See above, siman 183, in the Magen Avraham, subparagraph 9.
Se'if 5 He recites the blessing "on taking the lulav" and Shehecheyanu. Regarding why we do not recite the blessing "on taking palm branches," in the language of the verse, see Responsa Rashba, siman 157.
