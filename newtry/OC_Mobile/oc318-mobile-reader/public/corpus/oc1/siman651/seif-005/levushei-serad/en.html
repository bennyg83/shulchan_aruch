Magen Avraham, seif katan 10. He has only a left hand, etc. Meaning, he follows the same arrangement as before: he takes the lulav with his right arm and the esrog in his left hand. This teaches that although he lacks his right hand, we do not regard his left hand as his right hand. Rather, we follow the ordinary designation of right and left. Magen Avraham concludes, “and likewise a left-handed person,” meaning that for a left-handed person too we follow the ordinary designation of right and left, in accordance with the Mechaber's ruling in seif 3. This is Beit Yosef's explanation of Rokeach.
