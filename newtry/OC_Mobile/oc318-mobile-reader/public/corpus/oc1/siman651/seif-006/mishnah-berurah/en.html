(29) But at the time of taking it - Even though by strict law it would have been proper to recite Shehecheyanu immediately at the time of making the lulav, meaning when he binds it, nevertheless our practice is initially to leave the blessing until the time of taking it. See Chayei Adam, who wrote that if he did not recite Shehecheyanu on the first day, he may recite it even on the seventh day when he takes the lulav.
