The hadas. See Baer Heitev. The Mateh Moshe wrote that one must be careful with the hadas to untie its binding, for sometimes the stem of one is lying next to the top of another, and sometimes they bend the tops inward so that the leaves should not fall off. Therefore he should straighten and stand the branches upright and set them in the way they grow; see there. The same is in the Shelah, and the Eliyah Rabbah brought it.
