Shulchan Aruch, seif 4. The lulav with his arm, and likewise the esrog, etc. This ruling is from Rokeach, cited by Beit Yosef. There the text reads, “He takes the lulav and the esrog with his arm.” This could imply that he takes the lulav in his hand and the esrog with his arm. Beit Yosef, however, understands Rokeach as discussing one who has no hand at all. It therefore follows that he also takes the lulav with his arm. The Mechaber accordingly clarifies here: he takes the lulav with his arm, and likewise the esrog. Meaning, the lulav with his right arm and the esrog with his left arm.
