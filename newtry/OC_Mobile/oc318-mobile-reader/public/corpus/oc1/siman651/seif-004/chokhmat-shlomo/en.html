(Seif 7) If one made a handle and placed the lulav in it, it is valid, etc. See Magen Avraham: if the entire lulav is outside the hand it is invalid through interposition; if fixed with nails there is no interposition — Tosafot Yoma 44b in name of Yerushalmi: with nails fixed, no interposition — the same for lulav. Need not say entire lulav outside hand — case: lulav in hand, handle fixed, no interposition. Shulchan Aruch: if wrapped cloth — see Magen Avraham, Levush, Rambam dispute — see my Chidushei OC on 639. {Rama: But by law there is no concern since the whole hand is not covered etc.} Note: see Magen Avraham what he wrote to resolve Bach's difficulty on wrapping on the hand that it is batel to the hand; proof from Rambam Hilchot Klei HaMikdash chapter 10 — kohen who took with his finger may wrap reed on Shabbat; Raavad challenged; Kesef Mishneh; I prove from Rambam's language he does not hold like Rashi — requires study; Magen Avraham: where the whole hand is not covered it is batel — but if the hand needs it for healing it is not batel — end of his words.
