(21) Who has no hand - That is to say, he has no hands at all. He takes the lulav with his right arm, and likewise the esrog with his left arm, for is it written, "and you shall take in the hand"?
(22) With his arm - This means the armpit. If he has an upper arm, he takes it with his elbow, for this is also called taking. But if he has no arm at all, he should not take it in his mouth, for that is not called taking at all; Peri Megadim. Bikkurei Yaakov disagrees with him; see there.
(23) And likewise the esrog - And if he has one hand, he should take the lulav in that hand; even if it is his left, it is considered his right. The esrog he should take in the opposite arm. If it is impossible for him to take the esrog with his arm, he should take both of them in his hand one after another, and he fulfills his obligation this way, as below in se'if 12.
