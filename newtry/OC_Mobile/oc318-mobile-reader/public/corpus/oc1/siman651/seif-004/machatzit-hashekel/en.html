(Subparagraph 4) Higher. And the reason is according to Kabbalah; see the Levush.
That is to say, the Levush gave a reason according to the revealed level. One of the reasons is that since hadas is mentioned in the verse before aravah, one should bind it higher than the aravah. Importance should be given to the hadas, since the verse placed it before the aravah, and this importance is expressed by raising it in the binding.
