Turning to the right. See Levush. The mnemonic is, “The right hand of Hashem is exalted.” The writings of the Arizal give the order: east, south, north, upward, downward, and west, while the person always faces east. Mateh Moshe. It appears to me that a left-handed person should follow this same order, because we follow the ordinary right side. A proof is the Mishnah in Middos 2:2: Everyone who enters the Temple Mount proceeds to the right, except one to whom an adverse event occurred, who circles to the left. They ask him, “Why are you circling to the left?” If he answers, “Because I am a mourner,” they say, “May the One Who dwells in this House comfort you.” One might ask: perhaps he is merely left-handed. Furthermore, even one circling to the right should be questioned, for perhaps he is a mourner and that direction is his left. It necessarily follows that we do not follow the individual’s own right side.
