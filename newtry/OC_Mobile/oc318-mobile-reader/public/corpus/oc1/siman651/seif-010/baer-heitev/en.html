East. The writings of the Arizal give a different order: first south, then north, east, upward, downward, and west. One should always remain facing east. Mateh Moshe. See Bach, Taz, Responsa Bach, siman 141, and Responsa Devar Shmuel, siman 250. Magen Avraham writes that a left-handed person should also turn in this order, because we follow the universal right side rather than his personal right side; see there.
