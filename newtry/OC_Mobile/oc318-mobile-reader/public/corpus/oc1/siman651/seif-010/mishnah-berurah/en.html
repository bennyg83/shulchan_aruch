(47) He should go around toward the right in his shaking: east, south, etc. - So too the Derech HaChayim and the Chayei Adam copied, that this is the common custom in our countries. The reason is that the Sages always warned us that you should always take the path that is to your right; where there is right and left before you, choose your right. There is no difference between a left-handed person and one who is not left-handed. Magen Avraham. Now I will briefly arrange the order of the shakings according to what is customary in our countries. For the order of shakings toward the directions, he should turn his face eastward, with the spine of the lulav toward his face, and perform three outward movements to the east and three inward movements. That is, he extends his hand with the lulav from opposite himself outward three times. It is proper that he also rustle the leaves during the outward and inward movement. During the inward movement he should bring it very close to his body, opposite the chest. Afterward he should incline the head of the lulav to the south and likewise perform three outward and inward movements, all as toward the east; but he need not turn his face to the side toward which he shakes, only incline the head of the lulav toward the side to which he shakes. Likewise toward the west, he should incline the head of the lulav over his shoulder and perform three outward and inward movements; afterward toward the north, he extends outward and brings inward three times; afterward upward, and afterward downward, all by extending outward and bringing inward three times, as toward the east.
