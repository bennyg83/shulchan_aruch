The shaking — Taz. Ran on Yerushalmi: must shake three times for each matter; R' Zeira inquires whether this one and that one are one, or this and that are one; Ritz Giat explained Yerushalmi holds like our Gemara Sukkah 38a — Rava said so too for lulav: up, down, bring, take; R' Zeira inquires that three times for each matter means each place one shakes — at blessing, Hodu, Ana, and end of Hodu — whether going and bringing count as two or up and down only as one since after raising he cannot lower afterward, therefore three properly and not four (see Rashi 37b and Chida); or going and bringing are also one, therefore need two goings with two bringings and up and down — three, not resolved. Shaking is going and bringing and one need not tear and rustle leaves (Rashi Menachot 62a evil winds east west north south; Iyun Tov Sukkah; evil dews above below — those see to Whose world is His; one could say only one going and bringing; one whose winds are four as Ran wrote and plain going and bringing alone, unlike evil winds needing four goings and bringings per Rashi Menachot; in my humble opinion R' Zeira's inquiry means going and bringing — intent to stop evil dews: in up and down he does nothing except he cannot avoid lowering per Rashi Sukkah and Chida; going and bringing is separate intent to stop evil winds — therefore four goings and bringings; brayta teaches three on each matter meaning three rustlings in each going; or this and that one meaning going, bringing, up, down as one — requires study; Ran, Rif, Rambam, and shaking is going and bringing without tearing leaves — end of Taz summary per Ran and views.

What Tur wrote etc. — Taz to reconcile what he wrote in summary of Rosh — meaning Tur summarized Rosh his father as known; Beit Yosef: Rosh like third view in Ran — difference: third view in Ran on each matter means at blessing and Hodu, and after going bringing up down he makes three small pairs; R' Zeira's inquiry stringently; for Rosh on each matter within large going and bringing he makes three small pairs, or R' Zeira's inquiry — three small pairs or six small pairs, three after going and three in bringing; nevertheless they agree there are at least small pairs in the manner of small going and bringing. Rosh: need three goings and three bringings — small, in large going three small, after large bringing three small — six small pairs with small pairs 60 to 36 as explained above if counted as one. Thus at each of six directions one large going and bringing alone, within it six small pairs in manner of small going and bringing, possibly without rustling at all. Tur explains Rosh differently for Ran — all refinements that Rosh seemed to hold his hand in one place meaning brayta requires shaking three times on each matter, three rustlings and tearing leaves at each direction, or at Hodu and Ana only one going and bringing per direction but R' Zeira's inquiry requires three goings per direction stringently 60 to 36 — end of Taz.
