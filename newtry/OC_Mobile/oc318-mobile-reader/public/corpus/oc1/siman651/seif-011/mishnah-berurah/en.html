(48) One must join the esrog, etc. - For although it is not with them in the bundle, nevertheless it must be joined with it.
(49) He fulfilled his obligation after the fact - And even if he did not shake at all he also fulfilled his obligation, for the shakings do not prevent fulfillment, and once he lifted the esrog he has fulfilled the mitzvah with it. Nevertheless, there it is good to go back and take it and shake it, unlike in this case.
