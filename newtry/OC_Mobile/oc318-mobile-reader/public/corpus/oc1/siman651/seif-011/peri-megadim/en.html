Tearing — Taz: as Ran wrote — second view Rif and Rambam: rustle leaves afterward, meaning after going there three rustlings and after bringing three rustlings; for Rosh and Tur three times on each of 24 — three goings and three bringings, no rustling at all. Mishna Berurah feared to be stringent like both views; lulav four handbreadths for shaking teaches rustling; R' Zeira's inquiry teaches three goings and bringings as in note 10; second difficulty: Mishna Berurah contradicts that going and bringing is the shaking without small motions — how write tearing lulav which contradicts; seems Magen Avraham's difficulty too — see there. Rashal concludes: for east wind three large goings — perhaps this view three times on each of 24; R' Zeira's inquiry stringently also rustle leaves at least in one going and bringing three rustlings in going and three in bringing — heed second view of R' Zeira; also in one going and bringing six small pairs in manner of going and bringing — heed third view like Ran's third view; but second and third large going and bringing to east need no shaking, neither rustling nor small pairs — permitted for all views since one fulfills all opinions as said. Taz further wrote to be stringent on large bringings — see there.
