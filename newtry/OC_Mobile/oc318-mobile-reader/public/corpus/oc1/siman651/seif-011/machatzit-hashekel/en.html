(Subparagraph 11) Before, etc. See siman 25, that with tefillin one recites the blessing after placing them, before tightening them, because one must recite the blessing before the performance, meaning before the tightening; but not before placing them, for that would be before the before. One must at least begin somewhat in the mitzvah before the blessing, and the same applies here. See what is written in siman 166 se'if 8, that if one forgot and ate without the blessing of HaMotzi, he should recite HaMotzi over what he will eat from now on.
