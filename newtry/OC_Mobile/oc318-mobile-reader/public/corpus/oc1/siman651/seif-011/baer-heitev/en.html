To join them. This is the preferred manner of fulfilling the mitzvah. Beit Yosef writes in the name of Recanati: This secret was revealed to me in a dream on the first night of Sukkos. A pious Ashkenazic scholar named Rabbi Yitzchak lodged with me. In the dream I saw him writing the Divine Name and separating the final letter from the first three letters. I asked him, “What have you done?” He answered, “This is the custom in our place.” I protested and wrote the Name in a complete form. I was astonished by the vision and did not understand it. The next day I saw that he waved only the lulav and its accompanying species, without the esrog. I then understood the meaning of my dream, for all four species allude to the Holy One, blessed is He, as stated in the Midrash. See Sefer HaKavanos and you will understand.
