A handle. See Baer Heitev. See Responsa Pnei Aryeh, siman 11, regarding one who held the esrog by its stem at the time of the blessing and taking, and did not touch the body of the esrog at all: he has fulfilled his obligation, for it is no worse than wrapping a cloth, etc., and all the more so since the stem is the body of the esrog, etc. However, initially one should not do so, lest the stem detach before he fulfills his obligation with it; also, it is impossible to perform the shakings according to the law; see there.
