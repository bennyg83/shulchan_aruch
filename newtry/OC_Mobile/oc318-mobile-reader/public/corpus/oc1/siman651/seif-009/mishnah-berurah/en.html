(42) That he extends his hand, etc., and shakes there three times during the outward movement, etc. - That is to say, he performs the outward movement once, but during that outward movement he shakes three times. And likewise during the inward movement, when he brings the lulav toward himself, he also shakes three times. See just ahead in the gloss.
(43) And he rustles the leaves - Meaning a slight rustling. Those who shake forcefully until the lulav is nearly broken by this are mistaken. Chayei Adam.
(44) And the outward and inward movement, etc. - The gloss disagrees with the Mechaber, and its view is that one must extend outward and bring inward three times, as he writes just ahead. And what he wrote, that it itself is the shaking, does not mean that no shaking at all is needed, for above in the gloss he wrote that one rustles the leaves with each shaking. Rather, it means that he should not perform the shakings after completing the outward and inward movement, but should perform them during the outward and inward movement itself, with rustling, as he wrote previously.
(45) Since he holds them in his hand, etc. - Meaning that since the taking with which he initially took them in his hand was in the way they grow, we are no longer particular that the shakings be in the way they grow. However, the meticulous are stringent not to turn it over, etc., because of the concern that this is not in the way it grows. It is not comparable to all the other shakings, where he only tilts it to the sides and does not truly turn it over; unlike here, where if he turns it over, its top will be downward and its end in his hand. Therefore he should not turn it over, but rather lower the lulav downward after raising it three times.
(46) And so appears to me to be the primary view - The Turei Zahav wrote that it is better not to turn it over, for in this way one satisfies all views; and if one turns it over, there is concern that perhaps the opinion that this is not the way it grows is primary. So too the Derech HaChayim and the Chayei Adam wrote that this is correct.
