That the shaliach tzibbur. See Taz. The shaliach tzibbur shakes at Hodu and at Yomar na, but at Yomru na beis Aharon and Yomru yirei Hashem he does not shake. The congregation shakes each time they answer Hodu, thus four times at Hodu. At Ana Hashem hoshiah na the shaliach tzibbur and the congregation shake twice, and at the Hodu at the end the congregation and shaliach tzibbur also shake twice.
