And the extending and bringing back, etc. This requires study, because above the ruling follows Ran that one shakes the lulav, etc., and Beit Yosef already wrote that these are differing opinions. Darkei Moshe implies that the meaning is that one need not shake it three times at the point to which it was extended. Rather, one extends and brings it back three times, and while extending and bringing it back he shakes the lulav, etc. He writes this there in the name of Raavyah. Responsa Maharshal states: One who wishes to be stringent should perform three extensions and three returns, and should also make three shaking motions during the first extension and return. One who adds further is called an ignoramus. It appears that in his view one should make three shaking motions at the point to which the lulav is extended. Bach maintains that it suffices to rustle the leaves while extending it, and this is the custom.
