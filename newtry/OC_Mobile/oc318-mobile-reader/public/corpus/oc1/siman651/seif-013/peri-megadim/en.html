East. See Taz, that one should not bring proof from nature. See siman 677. According to his view there, that one turns in the direction of Hebrew writing, from right to left, on the second night he must begin with the additional candle nearest his right, and necessarily move toward the left, and so each night. Here, however, he begins east, west, north. The cities of the Levites are written in the Torah in the order, 'you shall measure outside the city,' east side, south, west, north; and the same applies to lulav. The Levush distinguishes between the cases.
