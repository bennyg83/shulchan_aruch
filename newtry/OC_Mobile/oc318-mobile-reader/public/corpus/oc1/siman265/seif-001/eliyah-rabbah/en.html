[1] A perforated utensil, etc. The Mishnah refers to a tube made from an egg. Bartenura explains that this means the hard shell of an egg, while Maharil explains that it means a reed from a marsh.
[2] And he attached it to it, etc. This means that even if the homeowner attached it with plaster or potter’s clay, making it as firm as a potter’s vessel, it is permitted.
