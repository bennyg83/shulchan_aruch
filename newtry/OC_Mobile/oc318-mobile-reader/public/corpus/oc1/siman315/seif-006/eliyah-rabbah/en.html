Since it has no partitions — and some say the reason it is not forbidden due to tent is only when one needs the hollow below — bed and chair need the hollow to put shoes, and on a table one does not need it (Rabbeinu Yerucham Netiv 13 part 4); it is explained there is a dispute on this — for the first reason everything depends on partitions — even if one need not the air it is forbidden, and without partitions even if one needs the air it is permitted; and so implied in Rosh chapter 5 of Beitza who wrote: even though a mat in seif 2 is forbidden without partitions — there it is different for it is important building and his main intent is to make a tent; but temporary building here is not even called a tent without partitions — until here; and Taz at the end of this siman omitted this; and for the second reason everything depends on need — and even if the table has four partitions it is permitted; I wrote this to exclude Taz's reasoning that for everyone forbidden if there are four partitions even if one need not the air; and Shulchan Aruch's view appears as Ran's in the name of Tosafos that both are required — partitions and need for air — therefore he permits our bed even though it needs air; also in seif 7 nearby with one book even though there are partitions since one need not the air; and further since it is d'rabbanan he ruled leniently with both reasons mentioned above; and in truth at first glance the essence appears as the second reason as implied from Ran's conclusion and Maggid Mishneh — except I have no authority to be stringent; also not like Beit Yosef — and see siman 502 seif 9:
