In Magen Avraham s.k. 3 "and I did not wish to emend from my own opinion," etc. Behold in the Darkei Moshe before us it states and these are his words: and it is possible that in the Gemara too it does not speak of making a partition before the light of a candle, rather in the manner that the Maharam did; and so it appears to me that there is no distinction between making a partition before a candle or before books — end of his words; meaning that what the Gemara says "possible with a partition" means that he should make it from erev Shabbat; and even though the implication of the language is that even on Yom Tov itself he would spread the partition — that is, that he would fold it and a tefach would remain, and then he could spread it on Yom Tov in the manner the Maharam did; and that which it says there "he may not make a small partition" — it is possible that this means that he did not make it from erev Yom Tov through leaving a tefach; if so, it is impossible to make a partition today:
