(א) That which was written on Shabbat s.k. 2 requires faces on Shabbat in Tosafos — on the contrary it is proven from the case of dikeri that it was on the roof and not in partitions: that it is fixed there. Since it moves back and forth in a common wind and also does not impede those who walk and pass along the way there, it is not called fixed [Beit Yosef]. But it is forbidden to make a permanent ohel addition, as the Ran wrote at the end of siman 313 seif 3:

(ב) And likewise the curtain. This is obvious that it is permitted, for since there is in any case a door to the ark the curtain is not suspended except for modesty on Yom Tov; and it appears to me simple that it is permitted to hang on Shabbat patterned sheets that are decorated even though they are fixed there, since they are not made there for a partition:

(ג) Forbidden to make a partition, etc. And this is the language of Darkei Moshe, and Beit Yosef wrote that the partition of Maharam that was made to separate in order to do one's needs is considered a releasing partition and is forbidden; and it requires study, for it is implied in chapter 2 of Beitza that it is permitted to make a partition before the light of the candle in order to use it, and it does not appear to distinguish between Shabbat and Yom Tov regarding this; and it is possible that in the Gemara also it does not say that it is possible to make a partition before the light of the candle or before books [but only in the manner that Maharam did, as in the Darkei Moshe before us], end of his words; and in any case there is a textual error in his words and I did not wish to emend from my own mind. And this is the language of the Rambam chapter 4 of the laws of Yom Tov: one may not extinguish, etc., except, etc., one makes a partition between him and the candle; and so too the Semag and Rabbenu Yerucham responsum 1 and the Maggid Mishnah chapter 4 of Yom Tov in the name of Tosafos and the Raavad; and so too in the Rokeach laws of Niddah; and this is the language: on Yom Tov one makes a cloth partition; and so too in the rulings of the Rokeach; and so too from the words of Rashi, who wrote "one makes a partition" in a sheet — it is implied that it deals with Yom Tov, for on chol even a complete partition is permitted. And behold it appears to me to analyze from the words of the Mordechai, who wrote that Maharam made every Yom Tov night a partition ten high before the books, etc. — it is implied even though the books were standing exposed above the partition, it is permitted to urinate, for this is as if he is in another domain, as is written siman 79 seif 2; and therefore it is forbidden to make it on Shabbat, for through this he makes a domain by itself; and also one must tie it so the wind will not move it, as is written siman 324 seif 6; and so too in Yoreh De'ah siman 341 in the name of the Rashba. But when one hangs the partition against all the books so that none are exposed at all, it is obvious that even if it is not ten high it is permitted, as mere covering; and therefore it is permitted to make it on Shabbat, for it is not made for a partition but for mere covering and modesty; and the same law applies to a candle as well if one makes a partition ten high and the light is visible above the partition — it is forbidden to make it on Shabbat [on siman 301 seif 11]; but if he hangs it so the candle is not seen, it is permitted, for this is as mere covering; and it is obvious that if there was a partition protruding from the side before the books a tefach wide, it is permitted to hang a sheet before it, for this is a temporary ohel addition, as the Ran wrote at the beginning of siman 313; and see in Even HaEzer siman 25 seif 5, and this is from the words of the Rambam chapter 21 of the laws of Avodah Zarah, and the Maggid Mishnah wrote there that it deals when he has nothing with which to make a partition:
