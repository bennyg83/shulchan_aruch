Matters forbidden due to ohel on Shabbat. And in it 13 seifim: It is forbidden
to make an ohel on Shabbat and Yom Tov, even if it is temporary. And specifically the roof; but partitions are permitted. And a partition is not forbidden unless it was made to permit a sukkah or to permit carrying (tiltul):But a partition made merely for modesty is permitted (Tur); and therefore it is permitted to hang a curtain before a doorway even though it is fixed there (Or Zarua and Beit Yosef); and likewise a parochet) before the Holy Ark, provided that he not make an ohel in the roof of a tefach (Beit Yosef and Kol Bo);) and likewise it is permitted to make a partition before the sun or the cold, or before candles so the wind will not extinguish them (Mordechai beginning of chapter Kirah); but it is forbidden to make a partition before the light of a candle in order to use his bed (his opinion); and likewise before books in order to use them or to attend to his needs, unless there was from before daytime a tefach, for then it is permitted to add to it on Shabbat (Mordechai beginning of chapter Kirah):.
