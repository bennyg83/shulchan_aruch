1) [Seif 1] It is forbidden to make a covering on Shabbos and Yom Tov, etc. This is a decree because of building. Adding to a temporary covering is permitted, as stated in seif 2. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1. Rambam writes in chapter 22: One who makes a permanent covering is liable for building. Therefore, one may not initially make a temporary covering or dismantle one, by rabbinic decree lest one make or dismantle a permanent covering. If one built or dismantled a temporary covering, he is exempt. These are his words. Rashi in the chapter Kol HaKelim explains a temporary covering as one made for the moment and not intended to remain permanently. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1. If one places a sheet upon four poles and ties it there, or even leaves it untied but intends it to remain for an extended time, it is a permanent covering and he is liable to bring a sin offering. This applies when it is at least one tefach by one tefach. One is also liable to bring a sin offering for adding to a permanent covering. It is possible that one is likewise liable for a permanent side partition measuring one tefach by one tefach. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 8; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1. See below, paragraph 14.
