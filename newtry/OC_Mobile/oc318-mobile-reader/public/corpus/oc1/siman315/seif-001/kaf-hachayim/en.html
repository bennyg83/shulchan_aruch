(1) [Seif 1] Forbidden to make a tent on Shabbat and Yom Tov etc. — due to the decree of building. And an addition to a temporary tent is permitted as written seif 2 Tur Orach Chayyim 1 and thus Rambam chapter 22: one who makes a permanent tent is liable due to building; therefore one does not make a temporary tent l'chatchila and one does not demolish a temporary tent — a decree lest one make or demolish a permanent tent; and if he built or demolished a temporary tent — exempt — end of his words. And what is a temporary tent? Rashi explained chapter Kol HaKelim — such as one makes for an hour and not to remain there permanently — see there 1; but if one places a sheet on four poles and ties there, or without tying that it remain for a long time — this is a permanent tent and one is liable for a chatas — that is, whenever there is width of a handbreadth by a handbreadth. And even an addition to a permanent tent — liable for a chatas. And even a partition from the side fixed — possibly a handbreadth by a handbreadth — liable.
