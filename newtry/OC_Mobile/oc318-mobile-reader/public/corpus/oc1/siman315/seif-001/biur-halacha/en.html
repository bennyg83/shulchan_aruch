(א) And a partition is not forbidden etc. — At first glance one should challenge from what is in Beitza 32: stones of the Temple permitted to align them on Yom Tov; and they conclude there the reason — for even though a partition alone without a roof is considered temporary building and forbidden generally — here due to his honor the rabbis did not decree [and so in Rif and this law is brought in Tur Shulchan Aruch siman 312] — behold explicit that even in a temporary partition without a roof it is also forbidden even though there is no tent temporary building forbidden due to temporary building; and one can say Temple stones are different for their manner is permanent building — and when aligning them in the ground for permanent building he is liable due to building as in the beginning of chapter HaBoneh — therefore more stringent; and according to this one may say that what they permit a temporary partition for modesty — this is specifically in something whose manner is not always to make it permanent — such as a curtain or mat and similar; but if one makes a temporary partition of stones and bricks one on another — forbidden; and more plausible to say that specifically a partition always made to separate domains — we say since it does not permit and it is also temporary it is not significant; unlike when aligning stones to sit on them — more significant — not due to partition do we bring it but due to the decree of a bench — see there, in the Gemara:

(ב) If not etc. — See in Mishna Berurah 1 what we wrote in the name of Chayei Adam; and see there in Nishmat Adam general rule 44 letter 5 who tends: where the evil inclination overpowers a person and lamps burn and there is concern Hashem forbid for nocturnal emission, or he will have relations by lamp light — there is danger — then permitted to rely on the poskim Magen Avraham brought in 3 who permit making a temporary partition for this; and permitted to set up the curtain even if it was not spread from erev Shabbat — see there he wrote the reason: for them it is not considered a partition that permits at all because he can hide the lamp by another stratagem such as covering with a utensil — see there he permits when his inclination overpowers even if lamp light is visible above the partition; but otherwise Hashem forbid to be lenient in this:
