Seif 1 "It is forbidden to make an ohel on Shabbat and Yom Tov." It appears to me: behold I saw in responsa Givat Pinchas part 1 Yoreh De'ah siman 77 that he brought in passing what he answered the questioner regarding the matter of the parasol — to stand it against the wall on Shabbat — and it appears according to my humble opinion after forgiving honor of the Torah that a mistake slipped before him in this, for behold in a menorah of sections it is forbidden to carry due to concern lest he assemble; and likewise several matters that they decreed similar to this; and all the more so in this that it is forbidden to carry it altogether, for it is muktzeh due to concern lest it be taken apart and he set it up; and in particular in this that it is common to take it apart and set it up, and also when he needs to go through the doorway and the like — if so, certainly one should decree thus, and it is muktzeh and forbidden to carry it altogether; and all the more so that it is forbidden to go beneath it when it is standing — and investigate well:
