In Magen Avraham s.k. 5 "outside." It appears to me: see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 73 [regarding a partition called spanische Wand that are made of several parts, and when the parts fold one upon the other nevertheless much more than a tefach remains — if this remainder suffices to open it entirely on Shabbat and it will only be like adding to a temporary ohel or not; since its way is thus, one must open at least one of the folds from erev Shabbat; and he concluded his responsum thus: and now according to my humble opinion, those walls called spanische Wand that thus is their way of standing — inevitably folded — and a space of several tefachim width remains in them — it does not appear that they were set there for a partition, rather thus is their way always, and it is not possible otherwise; even Rashi would concede it is forbidden, etc.; and so is my practice in my house not to spread them on Shabbat by a Jew unless they were spread at least folded from erev Shabbat; but through a gentile there is room to permit, for at most there is only a d'rabbanan prohibition because it does not appear like adding an ohel, as stated above; and presumably most making of a partition such as this is for the sake of a mitzvah — therefore there is room to permit through telling a gentile]:
