Magen Avraham wrote: that which was written on Shabbat s.k. 2 requires faces, etc. — and this is on daf 125: R' Eliezer and the Sages disagree; R' Eliezer holds that if it is not tied and hanging, one may not plug with it, for he forbids adding to an ohel; and the Sages permit, for they hold it is permitted to add to a temporary ohel. Rashi explained that there is no ohel prohibition except in a roof and not in partitions; and Tosafos in the name of Rabbeinu Tam proved that ohel also applies in partitions, and their dispute of R' Eliezer and the Sages is regarding a partition — for thus is implied the language of the window plug, which is in a wall, but in a roof it is called a skylight. Nevertheless, specifically regarding a releasing partition, such as side wall 3 for a sukkah; but a partition made for modesty, and such as side wall 4 in a sukkah, is permitted; and Tosafos wrote further, and this is the language: nevertheless regarding an ohel in a roof they also disagree (he intended to say: that per the Sages, even adding an ohel in a roof is permitted), as is proven below, etc.; and so too it is implied in Eruvin 102 regarding the dikeri of R' Huna — by day he needs shade and at night he needs air (and therefore it was not possible to make from erev Shabbat, for at the beginning of Shabbat they need air); and Rav said to him: spread a mat and leave a tefach in it, for tomorrow he adds to a temporary ohel and it is permitted; and it is also implied that this is adding a temporary ohel over which R' Eliezer and the Sages disagree, end of the words of Tosafos (and this law was mentioned in seif 2). And the author of Sefer Or Zarua understood the proof of Tosafos from Eruvin to reject Rashi's explanation — rather that ohel prohibition also applies in partitions; and therefore this case in Eruvin — on the contrary it is implied that it was on the roof; also if one holds that it deals with a partition — behold Tosafos agree that a partition that is not made to release is permitted; and in the case of R' Huna, behold it was not made to release but for shade and air; and therefore Magen Avraham wrote that according to what is before us, on the contrary even Tosafos agree that the case of R' Huna was on the roof; and their intent in bringing proof from Eruvin is not to reject Rashi's explanation but to bring proof for what was written shortly before, namely that the essence of the dispute of R' Eliezer and the Sages is in partitions — since the Mishnah teaches the window plug; nevertheless regarding a skylight in a roof they also disagree, and per the Sages it is permitted; and for this they brought proof from Eruvin from the case of R' Huna, which was on the roof, and nevertheless adding a temporary ohel was permitted:
