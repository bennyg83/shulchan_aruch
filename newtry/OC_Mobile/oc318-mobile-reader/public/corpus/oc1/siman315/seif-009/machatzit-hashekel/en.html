(s.k. 9) He must not arrange, etc. — and even though davar she'eino mitkavein, etc. — to exclude the view of Rashi, who wrote in Beitza 37a that this sugya appears there, and Rashi wrote that this is specifically according to R' Yehuda, who holds davar she'eino mitkavein is forbidden; but according to us, who rule like R' Shimon that davar she'eino mitkavein is permitted even from below to above — and Tosafos disagree and hold that even R' Shimon agrees in such a case that it is forbidden, as Magen Avraham wrote; except that in Sefer Teshuvah he brought in the name of Or Zarua that he challenged in siman 502 — teach us that it is psik reisha; and Magen Avraham in siman 314 proved that not like this, for psik reisha is forbidden even in a rabbinic prohibition; and one can resolve in Sefer Teshuvah that he holds like the Maggid Mishnah brought in Magen Avraham siman 318 s.k. 36 regarding fixing a vessel, that if he does not intend to make a vessel, even though it is psik reisha it is permitted, since he does not intend to make a vessel it is not called a vessel at all; and likewise one can say thus regarding ohel — as of now I have not raised a difficulty on the words of Rashi, who wrote that the sugya deals specifically with R' Yehuda, who holds davar she'eino mitkavein is forbidden — it should be difficult: behold in psik reisha Rashi agrees; and for Rashi there is no need for the view in Sefer Teshuvah that he holds like the Maggid Mishnah, that in not intending to make a vessel, even though it is psik reisha, it is permitted even according to R' Yehuda; and since Rashi has no need for Tosafos' view and therefore it does not come like R' Shimon — it is impossible — unless we say that R' Yehuda disagrees with the Maggid Mishnah; or one can distinguish between a vessel and ohel, and regarding ohel specifically R' Yehuda disagrees — and this is strained:
