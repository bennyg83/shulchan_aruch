(36) A guard, etc. — and it appears that it is made like a kind of sack called logen zak, wide and open above and closed and pointed below; and they insert the pointed end inside the vessel and wrap the wide upper end around the mouth of the vessel, and it becomes a roof for the vessel and is made like an ohel, and forbidden rabbinically; but to hang the guard when it is spread on some peg without a vessel beneath it — permitted, for even temporary ohel is not considered; and all the more so permitted to hang a vessel on a peg:
