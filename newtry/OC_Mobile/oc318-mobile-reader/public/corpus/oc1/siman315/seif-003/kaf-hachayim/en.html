(3) Umbrella made to protect from sun and rain — forbidden to carry it on Shabbat and Yom Tov, and all the more to open it; but to tell a non-Jew to carry it to protect him — permitted. Responsa Michtam LeDavid 8; Berach Yosef 2; however in responsa Givat Pinchas 21 part 5 siman 19 wrote a complete response on all the words of the rabbi above and concludes a clear answer on everything; and an umbrella when open from erev Yom Tov — obvious it is permitted to carry; and so he teaches to use on Yom Tov an umbrella open from erev Yom Tov; and so he saw his rabbis did not protest those who practice thus; but on Shabbat where there is hotza'ah prohibition there is room to decree — see there; and they brought it in Berach Yosef on Yom Tov. And see in responsa Noda B'Yehuda Mahadura Tinyana Orach Chayyim 40 what he expanded on this and concludes that as a rule when arranged from erev Shabbat there is room to discuss forbidding and permitting; but at any rate due to marit ayin one should forbid it — for who knows if it was arranged from yesterday; and especially in this orphaned generation — see there; and likewise Ma'amar Mordechai 4 expanded and inclined to forbid — see there; and Beit Din HaPelet 3 concluded that thus is the world's practice to forbid even when open from yesterday — for thus we are accustomed to forbid; and one who comes to be lenient misleads the public Hashem forbid — see there; and according to this, since the prohibition is due to tent and marit ayin as stated — there is no distinction between Shabbat and Yom Tov. And so wrote Rav Ben Aharon of blessed memory Parashat Shemot 8: and automatically you know — since forbidden to carry it on his head, forbidden also to move it on Shabbat and Yom Tov due to muktzeh — end of his words; and so is the practice of the people:
