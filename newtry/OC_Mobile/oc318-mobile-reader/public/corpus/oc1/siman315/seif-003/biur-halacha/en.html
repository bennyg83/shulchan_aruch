(ב) Attached panels — See in Mishna Berurah what we wrote in the name of Beit Yosef; and behold Tur wrote: and this is his language: and in siman 502 Beit Yosef wrote that partitions reaching the ground are required — and the partitions must be ten handbreadths high or seven and a bit; and less than three from the ground — end of his words; and in truth it was not mentioned there in Beit Yosef except that if they reached less than three from the ground we say lavud and they are considered reaching; but nothing was mentioned at all about ten handbreadths high; and in my humble opinion it is clear the fast scribes confused Tur — for in general there partition when ten high — here for prohibition due to resembling a tent this does not apply at all — since there is on it some form from two small walls and a roof above they forbade the Sages to make this; and know that in seif 7 they permitted placing one book on two books only because one need not the air beneath them; and likewise the Sages forbade on Yom Tov even to place one egg on two to roast them only with a change as in Beitza 32 in Tosafos and Rif and Rosh and Ran there — and although for partitions reaching near the ground at least less than three — the poskim hold also in this we require as generally; but this is clear that even with small partitions in small things like handbreadth height the Sages forbade this. (ג) Our beds etc. — And Rashba's view in his novellae and so implied from Ran's and Rambam's conclusion and their words brought in Beit Yosef — if one needs the air beneath to use — forbidden even without partitions; and he brought proof from what they forbade in a large person; and therefore our beds too forbidden since they use its air for putting on shoes and similar; and brought in Rabbeinu Yerucham both views and did not decide; and Mechaber ruled anonymously like the lenient — because it is a matter of their words; and see in Biur Gra siman 502 who brought strong proof for their words — for otherwise even from above to below would be forbidden; and if so what the Sages forbade in a large person — there must be another reason; and Rama himself who brought Rashba's view there in siman 502 [and Mechaber here in seif 7] did not bring his words except to be lenient — that one requires also standing to use its air but not that this alone suffices; and so implied from Tur similarly. And behold I saw in Ateret Rokeach who wrote: if it appeared at first glance to be stringent like Rashba after Ran's and Rambam's conclusion appears like him — except he has no authority to be stringent; and in my humble opinion one can say after many rishonim — Tosafos in the name of Rabbenu Tam, Hagahot Maimoniyot, Semag, Rosh, and Tur — all stand in one view — he did not want to be stringent against them; and although Rashi's view there in Beitza 32 implies no partition needed — behold his view there this passage goes only according to R' Yehuda and according to R' Shimon entirely permitted; and so Or Zarua and Riv'a as Darkei Moshe brought — except we do not hold like them in this because Rif, Rambam, Tosafos, and Rosh disagree as poskim wrote; and at any rate in something Tosafos, Rosh, and their supporters are lenient — one may be lenient. Nevertheless l'chatchila it appears good to be concerned for Rashba and his supporters to be stringent unless in a time of need.
