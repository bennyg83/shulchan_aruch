And this applies when the legs etc. In Tur and in Asheri and in Remazim — four panels attached; and it is difficult — for behold in two it suffices, as with a barrel nearby "one from here" etc.; and nevertheless this — it appears to me — is the essence: one may say that here Tur wrote in the name of Tosafos that if there are partitions reaching the ground it is forbidden — it is implied they are concerned only with partitions; and below he wrote in the name of Rashba in the name of Tosafos that there is no prohibition if one need not the air below even though it is a complete partition like a book "one from here" etc., as written here in seif 7; and one may say that both are required — specifically complete partitions and one must need the air — and so too Ran; and this is his language: tent matter is forbidden only where it has partitions below and one uses the air beneath it; for in Tosafos chapter HaMeivi Kedei Yayin they mentioned only partitions reaching the ground — and it appears to me the essence certainly depends on needing the air beneath it; if there are four panels below then certainly it is fit to use there for all matters — it is like a box and one can place and conceal there what he wishes; but if there are only two partitions attached to the ground — then it is not fit to conceal there since on two sides it is open — nevertheless sometimes one needs the air beneath, such as beneath the bed for putting on sandals as Beit Yosef wrote nearby; and sometimes one does not need it — therefore Rashba wrote in the name of Tosafos this distinction; and nevertheless in one book "one from here" etc. — permitted since there are only two partitions — and likewise all similar cases; and it turns out that what Ran wrote "it has partitions and uses" — these are not two matters, for Tosafos did not mention use at all but only this is the explanation of partitions — why forbidden with partitions since it is fit to use; and it turns out according to this that with four partitions attached to the ground it is forbidden in every case, and even on a table if so there is prohibition to place the table on them; and when there are only two partitions — then permitted on a table since one need not the air below, and on a bed forbidden since one needs it for putting on sandals; and this that Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Ran: and Rashba challenged — do they make wide partitions for him? Behold they only spread it on poles — and nevertheless he holds spreading the table is permitted because one does not use the air beneath it unlike the bed etc. — end of his words — one must understand: what "nevertheless" that he wrote; and it appears this is his intent: Rashba challenged on Tosafos' version that wrote the matter depends on partitions reaching the ground and thereby specifically fit for use — for regarding a large person there are no such partitions; and on this he wrote "and nevertheless" etc. — meaning: what depends use on air is only without partitions one may say there is a distinction between fit for use of air or not — that is the distinction between bed and table; and Maharachal challenged him — behold we find first between wide partitions and afterward fit for use below in air — and he left it requiring study and dismissed that of seif 7 with one book since there are complete partitions; and according to what I said everything is resolved with Hashem's help — and see at the end of this siman what Tosafos wrote that forbidden due to partitions — that is, one makes them on Shabbat; and see his words:
