And what Taz wrote stringently — we rule like Rashi — that is, if a garment is folded in wrapping in a circle and there are three in its circumference — if its width is a handbreadth forbidden to cover a utensil with it as written in letter 11 at the end: everything not designated for a utensil is forbidden — what does this teach us? At first glance all agree on this — whatever covers a utensil and the covering has a handbreadth width over all of it — what difference if in a circle or not?
