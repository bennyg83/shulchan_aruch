(א) (s.k. 3) Forbidden, etc. — the partition of Maharam — meaning that the Mordechai wrote in his name that since his custom was sometimes to urinate at night, therefore he would make a partition from erev Shabbat ten high and fold it above and leave a tefach in it, and on Shabbat night he would spread it:

(ב) And it requires study, for it is implied in chapter 2 of Beitza 22a that Abba bar Marta asked Abaye: what is the law regarding extinguishing the candle on Yom Tov in order to use his bed? He said to him: is another house possible? He has no other house — what then? He said to him: is making a partition possible? He has nothing with which to make a partition — is covering with a vessel possible? He has no vessel — what then? He said to him: it is forbidden; from this the words of the Gemara — behold, if it were possible to make a partition, he would permit making a partition for the need of use; therefore one must say that only a releasing partition is not called such, but for modesty and to do one's needs it is permitted to make a partition before books. And in any case there is a textual error in his words — namely according to his version that was in Darkei Moshe; but per the version that was before Magen Avraham, the words of Darkei Moshe are not understood, and Magen Avraham from his own mind did not wish to resolve as it stands before us in Darkei Moshe, that in the Gemara it means a partition like that which Maharam made, for the language of the Gemara does not indicate thus — for what Rabba said, "is making a partition possible," means from erev Shabbat and to leave a tefach; and also Magen Avraham brings proof afterward from what Rabba said to him "a partition" — meaning on Yom Tov; and if so, the question of Darkei Moshe is strengthened:

(ג) And so too from the words of Rashi, etc. — it deals with Yom Tov, for if on chol, etc. — he challenges: what proof does Magen Avraham bring from the words of Rashi that it deals with Yom Tov? And therefore one must say that it is not called a releasing partition — perhaps in truth it is Yom Tov, and nevertheless it is permitted according to Rashi even on Yom Tov, for Rashi goes according to his view that he holds there is never ohel prohibition except in a roof and not in partitions; and in truth according to what is before us, behold even on Rashi himself Abaye said to him "a partition" — he forced the language of partition out of the simple meaning that it means a complete partition; and in truth it means on erev Shabbat; and therefore one must say from the fact that Rabba stated anonymously to him "a partition," it is implied that he means today on Yom Tov; and also similar to what he said afterward, "is covering with a vessel possible" — meaning on Yom Tov; and if so, likewise what he said to him "a partition" means on Yom Tov; and therefore according to Rashi, who has a decisive view that what Rabba said to him "a partition" means on Yom Tov — and we do not find Tosafos explicitly disagreeing on this — therefore they also explain "he said to him a partition" on Yom Tov, similar to covering with a vessel; and behold Tosafos forbid the partition of the next day; and therefore one must say that Tosafos hold that this is not a releasing partition; and so too in Sefer Teshuvah:

(ד) And behold it appears to me, etc. — meaning that since now the question of Darkei Moshe is strengthened and resolution is needed, therefore he wrote that it appears from the Mordechai that even though the books were exposed, etc. — for if they were covered, why did he analyze and write that it was ten high, for the covering must be per the height of the books, and why did the Mordechai inform us that the act was thus — behold he should have written anonymously that he would make partitions and not need to mention at all how high it was:

(ה) And as is written siman 79 seif 4 — namely per the view of the Rosh there, not like the Rashba, see there:

(ו) As the one who says mere covering — and before the books one still needs another covering, that the books be a vessel within a vessel, as above siman 301 seif 6:

(ז) And therefore it is permitted to make it on Shabbat — meaning even ten high, for now it is a partition that does not release, for even if it were not a partition it would be permitted, for behold they are covered — not like R' Avraham:

(ח) On siman 301 seif 11 — meaning that since the light is visible above, how is he permitted to use it? Nevertheless it helps that without the partition, even spreading a tallit would not help; and through the partition — except that the light is visible above — the spreading of the tallit helps, as is written there siman 301; and see in Even HaEzer, etc., who wrote, and this is the language: forbidden to use his bed by the light of the candle, etc. — behold it was Shabbat and he had no other house, etc. — he should not use it at all; and you should challenge: behold it is possible to permit through a partition or covering:

(ט) And the Maggid Mishnah wrote there to resolve this — it is not explicit thus in the words of the Maggid Mishnah, but it appears so; and in the Lechem Mishnah there he added that it also deals when he has no vessel with which to cover it:

(י) And it is obvious that if the partition protrudes from the side before the books a tefach in width — meaning from below to above the height of the partition is ten, but in its width it is only a tefach — but it is not ten high, only a tefach; and certainly it is forbidden to make it on Shabbat, for this is not adding a partition but an actual partition:
