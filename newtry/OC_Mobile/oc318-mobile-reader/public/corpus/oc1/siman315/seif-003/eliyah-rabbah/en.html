(3) In order that he may have relations etc. However on Yom Tov it is permitted since by law he could extinguish the lamp — it is only a manner of modesty (Taz siman 524); and even on Shabbat — specifically when they are not covered etc. — only regarding ten handbreadths or above exposed; but when entirely covered above — then permitted even with less than ten handbreadths, both for a lamp and for books — for it is like mere covering (Magen Avraham); and there is room to hesitate — one should distinguish between covering and partition; and it appears that if ten handbreadths high one should forbid, for in any case it is a partition; but with less there is room to permit — Taz wrote: obvious that he can cover the books with garments — two coverings — and see siman 240 seif 14; and it is obvious that if there was a partition protruding from the side before the books a handbreadth wide — permitted to hang before it, for it is an addition to a tent as written at the beginning of siman 313.
