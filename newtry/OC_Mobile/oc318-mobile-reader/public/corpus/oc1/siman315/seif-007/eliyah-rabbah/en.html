(7) A bed that is etc. Beit Yosef's language in the name of Ran: our bed etc.; and so in Maggid chapter 23; and the reason he wrote there — because they use the air beneath the bed for putting on sandals; and Shulchan Aruch and Levush omitted "our" — they follow his reason that specifically with partitions we require as written with one book; Tal Oros — some challenge Semag: why did he write regarding a bed that partitions reach the ground — it implies if not reaching the ground it is not a tent; whereas regarding a bridal canopy he wrote that even with a handbreadth it is a tent?
