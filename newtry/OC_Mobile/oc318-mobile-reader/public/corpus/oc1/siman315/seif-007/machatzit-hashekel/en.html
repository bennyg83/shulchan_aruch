(א) (s.k. 7) Permitted, etc. — and therefore even though he already has partitions in any case, if it is significant building it is forbidden:

(ב) Our stoves — meaning that sometimes the stove is inside the room where one sits, and the pipe is inside the room; above the pipe there is an opening through which the smoke exits; and after the smoke has ceased, so the heat will not exit through the hole in the pipe, they close the hole above by placing on the pole a thick mattress and extend it upward to close the hole:

(ג) For here one can say partitions — meaning that there in siman 313, where the window plug was permitted, since there are partitions except that within the partition there is a window, and when they close the window this is adding to an ohel and permitted; but the stove one can say partitions by themselves, and if he closes above he is not adding but making a complete ohel, for the partitions of the pipe did not have a roof at all from erev Shabbat, and the entire roof he makes on Shabbat:

(ד) And see end of seif 6 — here such a case requires being very wide; and in Sefer Teshuvah he wrote that the hole in the pipe above must be more than three, for otherwise we say lavud and there is no ohel prohibition in the closing:
