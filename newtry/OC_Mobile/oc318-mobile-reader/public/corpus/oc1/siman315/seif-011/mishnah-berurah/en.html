(א) A bridal canopy — one wood stands at the head of the bed higher than the bed, and likewise a wood at the foot of the bed, and a pole is placed on them and a large curtain is spread on the pole, and two ends descend from here and from there like an ohel along the entire bed:
(ב) Since it is prepared — meaning that he prepared it for this from erev Shabbat; and therefore it is permitted in this even though there were no threads wrapped on it from erev Shabbat; but if it is not prepared, forbidden, as the Shulchan Aruch ruled in seif 8, that even an ohel without a tefach in its roof is forbidden to make on Shabbat unless threads were wrapped on it from erev Shabbat, as stated:
(ג) Above the bed a tefach — the reason is that since it is sloped above the bed a tefach in vertical rise, after all the slope this tefach is a partition and all the slope from above downward is considered a roof and it is an actual ohel; however our beds upon which there is no canopy, that they spread a sheet on them — even though it hangs from the bed a tefach, we have no concern for this; and the reason is that what is placed on the bed is not called an ohel, since there is no hollow beneath it:
