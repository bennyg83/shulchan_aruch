Seif 2 "they are considered as an ohel" — that is, regarding that it is permitted to spread a mat over them and to call it an addition to an ohel. But it is not called an ohel regarding that it is forbidden to make it thus; for is it forbidden to go upon two beams when there are not three tefachim between them? Tosafos Eruvin daf 103:
