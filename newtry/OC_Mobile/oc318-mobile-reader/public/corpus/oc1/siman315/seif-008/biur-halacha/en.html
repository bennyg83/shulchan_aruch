(א) Every tent etc. — It appears to me therefore Rambam wrote "every" — for a general rule in this with two extremes — that is, even if made for permanence to last many days — since it is less than a handbreadth it is considered only temporary tent and exempt; and conversely even if not made for permanence there is prohibition due to not distinguishing; and their reason is he follows Rif his teacher who explained doubled tallit when there is not a handbreadth in its roof — and he holds this is certainly not the manner to make it permanent and nevertheless there is prohibition; and see Peri Megadim; and in my humble opinion it appears as I wrote and so implied from Magen Avraham — that Rambam disagrees with Rosh — namely regarding temporary tent less than a handbreadth:

(ב) That there is not in its roof — See in Ran who wrote regarding a canopy that has a handbreadth in the roof — permanent tent and liable; and one examining Rif's language will see so — for he wrote "and because of this" etc. — it implies but if it were handbreadth measure he would be liable; and doubled tallit with canopy is one matter as proven in the Gemara; and so explicitly in R' Chananel — all Rif's words are taken from him as one examining will see; nevertheless it appears to me that if cord or smearing were wrapped on it — even for Rif exempt from chatas and there is only prohibition [and as when there is not a handbreadth in the roof it goes one level through cord and smearing — without cord and smearing there is prohibition and with cord and smearing permitted; so when there is handbreadth there is liability through cord and smearing only prohibition; and although from reasoning one can distinguish — cord and smearing apply only d'rabbanan but not d'oraisa — nevertheless we are compelled to say regarding canopy R' Chananel holds thus]. And proof — R' Chananel himself explained at the beginning of the passage 138 in Abaye's statement there who said canopy exempt but forbidden — canopy is when there is handbreadth in the roof; and behold he himself wrote at the end of the page liable for chatas — or there his intent is with cord and smearing and R' Chananel was compelled to explain thus — for behold the braita similar to a large person is taught; and in a large person Abaye himself on page 2 according to R' Chananel's explanation there — even with cord and smearing forbidden for one person; and if without handbreadth in the roof we hold that if cord and smearing wrapped — permitted — perforce he explained that there we deal when there is handbreadth in the roof; and so proven from Rambam's words copied in Shulchan Aruch seif 12 regarding canopy considered temporary tent at least when there is cord and smearing even if handbreadth in the roof:

(ג) Handbreadth — And behold regarding carrying an umbrella [these are roofs people carry on their heads from sun and rain — in our language called parasol] many acharonim wrote in their books; and generally many forbid not to spread on Shabbat due to tent [they are in Sefer Michtam LeDavid and Sefer Ma'amar Mordechai and Chayei Adam and foremost the Gaon author of Noda B'Yehuda as brought in Sha'arei Teshuvah siman 310 seif 40 see there]; and as poskim wrote wherever one intends for a tent — that is, to protect from sun and rain — even for the roof alone without partitions there is prohibition; and this parasol too — behold he intends to protect from sun and rain; and it is not similar to a folding chair explained in seif 5 for permission — there he does no act on Shabbat only widens and spreads the wrinkles from yesterday; unlike here where he must tie it with straps after spreading or stand it and tighten afterward underneath with iron wire as our custom lest the roof fall from its position — forbidden; and Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham 8 see there wrote they tie with straps and similar; and especially according to what Rambam wrote chapter 22 halacha 28: and permitted to place a bed, chair, and folding chair even though a tent will be made beneath them — for this is not the manner of making a tent neither permanent nor temporary [he explains chair and folding chair are two things — folding chair is like a kind of basket — and so R' Chananel and Rif's version] — certainly no proof to be lenient from this for our case — and the opposite — there is proof for prohibition from what he wrote regarding bed and chair — because this is not tent manner etc. — it implies spreading this parasol is tent manner and forbidden; and in Noda B'Yehuda he wrote that for Rif whom Ran wrote canopy with handbreadth in roof is permanent tent and liable — and Magen Avraham brought it — there is concern of stoning liability [and Ran's words are not a novelty — for so explicitly in R' Chananel that emerged close to light — and from it all Rif and Rambam's words on this revolving according to his view; if so Rif is not alone in this]; and although some dismiss his words and hold even for Rif no liability — nevertheless prohibition does not depart for many poskim both Shabbat and Yom Tov; and therefore one guarding his soul should greatly distance from this. And all we wrote is due to tent concern whose prohibition is wherever one carries it; and sometimes there is also hotza'ah prohibition for all when carried where there is no eruv:
