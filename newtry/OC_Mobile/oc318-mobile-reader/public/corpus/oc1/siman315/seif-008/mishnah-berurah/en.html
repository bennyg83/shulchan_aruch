(א) Every ohel, etc. — namely even if he made it permanent to endure many days — nevertheless, since there is not a tefach in its roof, it is considered only temporary ohel:
(ב) Sloping — and the same if he made the roof level, he is not liable because of it unless a tefach in width — for otherwise it is not considered an ohel; and that which he stated "sloping" is because of the end, to teach us that specifically if it does not widen a tefach until after it has descended three tefachim — but if it widens a tefach within three, it is as if its roof were a tefach:
(ג) That there is not a tefach in its roof — but if there is a tefach in its roof, it is considered permanent ohel and he is liable, as we find regarding tents of impurity a tefach; and specifically when he made it permanent; but for temporary use, such as spreading a mat on four pillars — even if there are many tefachim in its roof, its prohibition is only rabbinic:
(ד) Exempt — namely from a chatas, but forbidden rabbinically, a decree lest permanent ohel; and see in Magen Avraham that all this is per the Rif and Rambam; but according to Rashi and the Rosh, since there is not a tefach in its roof it is not considered an ohel at all and permitted l'chatchila; and the Shulchan Aruch ruled anonymously like the Rif and Rambam; and so too is the view of R' Chananel; and in Peri Megadim he sides that Rashi and the Rosh also agree with the Rambam's view in this, since he made it permanent to endure several days:
