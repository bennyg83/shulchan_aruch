(ב) The author said in letter 1 — we wrote somewhat the laws of tents; and here another moment I will speak. Know that a permanent tent — liable for a chatas; and a temporary tent — exempt but forbidden — Gemara Shabbos 131a; Abaye lists stringencies of mishnayot; and Rambam chapter 22 halacha 27 — chatas 41 see there; and what is permanent and temporary tent? Behold if one places a sheet on four poles and ties there or without tying that it remain a long time — this is permanent tent liable for chatas — whenever there is width handbreadth by handbreadth; and even addition to permanent tent liable for chatas; and even a fixed partition from the side possibly handbreadth liable — see siman 313 Magen Avraham letter 7 and here 1; but if not fixed only for a short time — temporary tent forbidden d'rabbanan; and addition to temporary tent permitted d'rabbanan; however permanent tent less than a handbreadth — one may say for all — meaning between Rif and Rambam and Rosh in 138a they agree exempt but forbidden — for it is a compound rabbinic prohibition — at any rate d'rabbanan forbidden; and temporary tent less than a handbreadth — one may say all agree permitted — for in d'rabbanan they did not decree so much; and this is if we say compound rabbinic prohibition on Shabbat is forbidden not from the Torah — it would be a decree on a decree; and compound rabbinic prohibition is different in meat in milk and similar — for even compound rabbinic they forbade unlike R' Yehuda regarding R' Eliezer; and if compound rabbinic on Shabbat also forbidden — we say in temporary tent it only appears like building — less than a handbreadth does not appear and is not forbidden; and proof from siman 91 310 seif 40 and Gemara Shabbos 138b — handbreadth specifically, less permitted l'chatchila; and if Rambam chapter 22 halacha 41 did not mention handbreadth — behold Rif and Mechaber in 310 mentioned handbreadth and Rif and Rambam disagree; Rif and Rambam hold canopy is permanent tent for a long time and canopy exempt but forbidden without handbreadth; but Rosh and Rashi hold canopy is temporary tent not fixed and even with handbreadth l'chatchila forbidden; without handbreadth even l'chatchila permitted in temporary tent; and bridal canopy for Rif is lighter than other canopy for it is prepared for that — temporary; unlike other canopy — and see Ran there and Rosh;
