(א) Lest he make an ohel. Meaning lest he fold it to a tefach in width and it is an ohel; and it requires study, for we say in the Gemara: if he widened in his garment a tefach, it is not an ohel, since he does not medakdeik, as is written siman 501 seif 60; and one can say that here it is different, for he intends to make a partition, unlike there:

(ב) They hang it with two. For they can hang it as one so it will not fold; and it appears to me that for this reason some are careful not to hang the curtain on Shabbat, as Terumat HaDeshen wrote, see there:

(ג) That it has a roof. One must say that it is sewn like a lip around it:
