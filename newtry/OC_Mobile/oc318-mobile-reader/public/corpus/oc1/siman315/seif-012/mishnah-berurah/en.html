(א) One who spreads a curtain — all this is the language of the Rambam, and he holds that even if presumably there are threads on it from erev Shabbat since it hangs on them — nevertheless according to him it is forbidden to hang it, as the Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 1; nevertheless one must be careful in this:

(ב) When he spreads it — meaning that at the time he deals with hanging it, it is customary that it folds somewhat from its width, and if the fold were a tefach it would be an ohel:

(ג) They hang it with two — for two can hang it entirely as one so it will not fold; and for this reason some refrain from hanging the curtain on Shabbat:

(ד) A canopy — namely a sheet spread above on some thing and made like a folded tallit that we explained in seif 10; and it deals when a thread was wrapped on it or smeared from erev Shabbat — similar to the curtain discussed initially, that per the Rambam there must be a thread wrapped on it from erev Shabbat; and therefore he concludes "that it has a roof" — meaning that at least the roof must be a tefach in width; and all the more so where the roof is very wide — for if there is not a tefach in width above, we rule that it is permitted to spread it according to all when a thread or smearing was on it from erev Shabbat:
