And likewise a curtain before the ark etc. — this is obvious that it is permitted, since there is in any case a door to the ark — the curtain is only for modesty as Beit Yosef wrote. Magen Avraham 2. And Rav Panim Me'iros siman 30 wrote that even without doors to the Holy Ark it is permitted. One says in Hagahot Beit Yosef; and it appears simple that permitted to hang on Shabbat decorated sheets for ornament even though fixed there, since not made as a partition. Magen Avraham there. One says there; and he means even though fixed there — not moving — tied above and below and on the sides — nevertheless permitted since not made as a partition — meaning there is in any case a partition — the wall of the house — and it is similar to a curtain.
