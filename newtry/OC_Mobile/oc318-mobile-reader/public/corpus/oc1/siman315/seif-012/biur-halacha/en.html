(א) Canopy that has a roof etc. — Behold several acharonim were puzzled explaining what he says — see in Levush; and see in Magen Avraham what he explains on this — and this too is very forced — what difference if sewed rim around; and known that Rambam's manner is to clarify and copy Talmud words to halacha but not invent new reasoning in what was never mentioned; but according to what Hashem enlightened our eyes in R' Chananel on this passage from which all these Rambam laws were copied as one examining will see — we understand Rambam's words — there too is written this of Rambam and there written wide roof handbreadth — and now we understand Rambam's intent is also thus; and now we explain R' Chananel's words [and automatically Rambam's words are explained] on Abaye who said: and canopy even ten people forbidden — that is with wide handbreadth roof; he challenged the difficulty Lechem Mishneh challenged on Rambam and remains in difficulty — for even without wide roof it makes temporary tent and forbids; but in truth it is resolved — for earlier in the Gemara: a large person in its clasps permitted to tilt on Shabbat; and R' Chananel explained — cords hung on it are stretched; and Rav said: they taught only with two people but with one person forbidden; and Abaye says: canopy even ten people forbidden; perforce this is one case like a large person — with cords; and nevertheless forbidden in canopy; and if the passage deals with canopy without roof width handbreadth — why forbidden? Perforce R' Chananel explained it deals when it has roof width handbreadth — therefore forbidden even with cords; and R' Chananel follows his view who holds at the beginning of the passage that with width handbreadth cords do not help [and Rashi who holds at the beginning cords help with wide roof handbreadth — perforce here deals when cords were not wrapped from erev Shabbat — and perforce must explain a large person in its clasps not like R' Chananel's explanation — that is, cords]; and now Rambam's words are also explained correctly and Lechem Mishneh's difficulty is removed — for initially he wrote one who tilts a curtain etc. — for according to him known in seif 12 [that Mechaber copied the language from him] that he holds curtain forbidden to tilt only when he has cords hung from erev Shabbat; and on this he concludes: but one person forbidden and learned his law from a large person in its clasps — there too through cords permitted and nevertheless with one person forbidden [or he explains a large person means curtain as Ran explained for Rambam's words]; and afterward he says canopy that has a roof — when the roof is wide handbreadth [for otherwise not called roof] — then cords do not help as Abaye said that if it has no wide roof certainly cords help — as appears to me clearly with Hashem's help:
