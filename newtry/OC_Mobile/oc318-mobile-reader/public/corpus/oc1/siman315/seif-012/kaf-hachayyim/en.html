12) There, gloss: Likewise, a curtain before the Aron Kodesh, etc. It is obvious that this is permitted. Since the Aron already has a door, the curtain is hung only for privacy, as stated in Beis Yosef. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. Panim Meiros, siman 30, writes that it is permitted even if the Aron Kodesh has no doors. Yad Aharon, Hagahos Beis Yosef. It appears obvious to me that decorative painted sheets may be hung on Shabbos even though they remain fixed there, because they are not made as a partition. Magen Avraham there; Yad Aharon there. This means that although they are fixed and do not sway because they are tied above, below, and at the sides, they are nevertheless permitted because they are not made as a partition. The wall of the house already forms a partition, so this resembles the curtain before the Aron. Machatzis HaShekel. See below, seif 12.
