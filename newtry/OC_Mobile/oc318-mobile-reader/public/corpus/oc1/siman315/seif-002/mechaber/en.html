Wooden beams whose one end is inserted in one side of the ship and they bend the other end in the other side of the ship and spread a mat over them for shade — if their width is a tefach, or even if their width is not a tefach if there are not three tefachim between this one and that one, they are considered as an ohel and it is permitted to spread mats over them on Shabbat, for it is an addition to a temporary ohel and is permitted; and for this reason a mat spread for a tefach — it is permitted to spread the remainder of the mat on Shabbat; and the tefach they spoke of is outside the wrapping.
