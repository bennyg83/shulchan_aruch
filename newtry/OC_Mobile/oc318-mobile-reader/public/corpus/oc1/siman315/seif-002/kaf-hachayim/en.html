(2) There. Forbidden to make a tent etc. The naumusia that they practice in the land of Egypt to sleep inside — forbidden to tilt it, spread it, and gather it on Shabbat; and it does not help if it was spread a handbreadth from erev Shabbat. Goren Orach Chayyim general rule 3 siman 26. And Rav Perach Shoshan general rule 3 siman 4 permitted when spread a handbreadth from erev Shabbat. And woe for the sin — the linen sheet in the land of Egypt to spread and gather on Shabbat even when not spread at all from erev Shabbat — through a non-Jew; and some of them even through a Jew; and whoever Hashem touches his heart should be careful that at least a handbreadth width be spread from erev Shabbat and on Shabbat finish through a non-Jew.
