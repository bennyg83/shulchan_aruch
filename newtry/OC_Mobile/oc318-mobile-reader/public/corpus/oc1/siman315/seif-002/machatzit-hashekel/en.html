(s.k. 2) And likewise the curtain — this is obvious, etc. — meaning that he analyzes the language of the Rama, who wrote "and likewise the curtain" — on the contrary, a curtain is preferable and all the more so, since there is in any case a door, etc.; and these are the words of Terumat HaDeshen cited above, that he is lenient by reasoning more regarding a curtain than regarding a valance, as stated.
