In Magen Avraham s.k. 60 see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 72 [there he wrote to refute the words of Noda B'Yehuda second edition siman 244 regarding the parasol that the multitude of people carry on Shabbat and he is concerned for them for chatat liability; and he disagrees with his words for several reasons that he learned from the words of the Yerushalmi chapter Klal Gadol — investigate there well. And in the substance of the matter he wrote that certainly to carry a parasol on Shabbat is not a practice of the pious, and one who guards his soul will distance himself from it, etc.; and at the end of the responsum he wrote and these are his words: and if so, at the very least it is permitted to open them through a gentile, and one need not stir up the world on account of this]:
