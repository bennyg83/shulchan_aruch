2) There: It is forbidden to make a covering on Shabbos, etc. The mosquito net customarily used for sleeping in Egypt may not be erected, spread, or gathered on Shabbos, and it does not help for one tefach of it to have been spread before Shabbos. Ginas Veradim, klal 3, siman 26. Rabbi Perach Shoshan, klal 3, siman 4, permits it when one tefach was spread before Shabbos. Regrettably, the improper practice has spread in Egypt of having it spread and gathered on Shabbos through a non-Jew even when none of it was spread before Shabbos, and some even do so through a Jew. One whose heart has been touched by Heaven should ensure that at least one tefach in width is spread before Shabbos, and on Shabbos have a non-Jew finish spreading it. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1.
