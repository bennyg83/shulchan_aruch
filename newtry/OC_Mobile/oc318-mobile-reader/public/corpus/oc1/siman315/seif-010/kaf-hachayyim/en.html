10) There, gloss: Although it is fixed there. Since the entire curtain moves in an ordinary wind and does not obstruct people walking and passing there, it is considered nonpermanent. If it is fixed above, below, and at the sides so that it does not move from its place, it is forbidden because of building. An ordinary curtain, however, is not fixed so thoroughly and is therefore permitted. Beis Yosef. It is forbidden, however, to add to a permanent covering. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 3. This implies that adding to a permanent covering at least one tefach wide is forbidden by the Torah. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1.
