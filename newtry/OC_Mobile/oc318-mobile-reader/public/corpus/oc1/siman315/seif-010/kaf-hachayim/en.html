Since it finishes moving in an ordinary wind and also does not impede passersby on the path there — it is called not fixed; but if fixed above and below and on the sides in a manner they do not move it from its place — it is forbidden due to building; but an ordinary curtain they do not fix thus much; and therefore permitted.
