(א) A folded tallit. That there is not a tefach in its roof and not within three adjacent to its roof a tefach [Beit Yosef and Maggid Mishnah]; but if there is a tefach in it, the threads do not help:

(ב) Hanging on them. Meaning that he hangs it folded on the pole:

(ג) And it is permitted to remove it. So too the Rosh wrote explicitly, for behold it is a general rule that all that it is permitted to tilt — since it was not an ohel — it is also permitted to remove; and so too Rashi there explicitly regarding the canopy of R' Huna; and the gloss did not analyze:

(ד) And likewise the curtain. Meaning that the curtain is also permitted even though there were no threads on it, because there is no ohel except what is made like a roof (Maggid Mishnah); and it is implied that if it is made to untie, the threads do not help, as in siman 11:
