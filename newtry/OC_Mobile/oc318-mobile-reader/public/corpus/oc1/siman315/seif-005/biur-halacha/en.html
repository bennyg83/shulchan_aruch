(א) Chair etc. — And our beds attached and standing — if upright or leaning on its side permitted to tilt it to sit on its legs for one only does it to support merely [Rashi, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch]; and see in Peri Megadim who is uncertain regarding a box holding forty se'ah whether permitted to invert it on food and drink — possibly since there is a tent on it as above in siman 314 seif 1 in the gloss — more stringent see there [and his doubt is only according to Rashi — for according to Tosafos there the entire braita is due to tent; and therefore even with partitions they permitted tilting the bed because one only does it to support merely]:

(ב) L'chatchila — And according to this at first glance the same applies to our iron beds made folded and leaning on the wall — when they want to sleep on them they spread them — permitted to spread on Shabbat for it stands and endures from itself and he does no melacha only spreads them; but this is an error — for here the roof too — the hide on the chair — is also fixed in it from before; and therefore the roof too does not stand and endure as in Shulchan Aruch; and as Tosafos wrote explicitly in Shabbos 138a passage "chair" — this detail is for exclusion; unlike these beds on which they now spread the sheet on Shabbat after spreading it — and it becomes in tent order — first partitions then roof — forbidden; but if the bed slats lean on each other so there are not three between them — lavud applies — possibly room to be lenient since the roof too is as if already completed from erev Shabbat. Also at first glance room to discuss leniency since it has no partitions — and Shulchan Aruch already ruled above in seif 3 the bed's conditions that partitions are required — at least two partitions — as I explained in Mishna Berurah 22; but also in this one can greatly doubt — for according to what we clarified above in Biur Halacha passage "panels" that in our case we do not require partitions ten high — handbreadth-height partitions suffice; if so the bed has two partitions at its edges — one at the head side and one at the foot side; and even if not reaching the ground — perhaps there are not three handbreadths between them and the ground — lavud applies as Beit Yosef wrote siman 502 and Magen Avraham ruled there 9; but for all these there is a stratagem — even if three handbreadths between slat and slat and even if we say the partitions at head and foot are considered partitions for this — there is a stratagem — that is, first they spread the sheet and hold it in the air and afterward one spreads the bed and stands it beneath the sheet — and this is permitted since not in building order as Shulchan Aruch ruled above in seif 3. Nevertheless the very law of spreading this bed is weak in my hands — for we find in several places the Sages forbade due to exertion on Shabbat [see siman 324 9 and siman 333]; and here too possibly spreading involves exertion — requires study [and if due to honor of guests who lodged with him — explained siman 333 that then we are not concerned for exertion — and he should do the stratagem we wrote first]; but if he has another bed to lie on — certainly not proper to spread these beds — for not better than folding utensils that the Sages forbade where one can switch as explained above siman 302 seif 3:
