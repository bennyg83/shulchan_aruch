Seif 5 "permitted" etc. "since" etc. From what is stated 139b regarding one who deceives Adam etc., and see Rashi s.v. "deceives" etc., and according to his view he goes that which is stated there 138a "I say — Abaye — it is d'rabbanan" etc., and there the stringencies of the mishnah — not all of them are because of ohel; but Tosafos challenged him s.v. "the folding chair" on one matter; and even though they say there it is d'rabbanan — "that one should not make" etc. — nevertheless it is because of a temporary ohel, for the prohibition of a temporary ohel is because of this, as stated in Rambam's responsum and Rashi's proof from what is stated there regarding hanging pomegranates on it — and if because of ohel, what difference to me whether carobs or pomegranates? And likewise as he stated, and he hung etc. — and this they strained to reconcile for Rashi. But it is impossible to say so, for they say there 137b "now — R' Eliezer — we do not add to make a temporary ohel" etc. — learn that it is because of a temporary ohel; and that which they strained for Rashi that hanging pomegranates is permitted fits with what is stated here; and see the aforementioned Tosafos s.v. "chair," and Rashba there, in the name of his teacher that hanging carobs requires hollowing out beneath it, unlike pomegranates; and see Rashba there; and even though he reconciled Rashi's words, he only wrote to reconcile his words; and likewise the aforementioned Tosafos s.v.
