To me he raised three distinctions: (1) if one makes a roof for shade and similar — forbidden whenever there is a handbreadth of space height at the start of making it even without partitions as cited in Eruvin 122 and siman 310 seif 40 and as Taz wrote letter 11 and Magen Avraham letter 7; (2) everything not intending a tent — if one places table legs in one place and there are four panels reaching the ground fit for every use and places a panel on them — forbidden d'rabbanan even if one need not the air beneath; and likewise escutcheons thus if moved from place to place for those who forbid; and Magen Avraham letter 6 — unlike two partitions then it depends if one needs the air beneath; (3) seif 16 the barrel — two partitions that one needs air between them as written in letter 5; seif 7 with two partitions and one need not the air beneath — permitted.
