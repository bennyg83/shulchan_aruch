(4) For shade etc. I wonder — what is the difference from the end of the previous siman where a partition before the sun is permitted, and this one here is in the Talmud at the end of Eruvin, and that one above that Mordechai chapter Kirah inferred thus from the Talmud — there part implies from the Talmud of Kirah that only through a change is it permitted (Levush Yom Tov); and in my humble opinion nothing is difficult — for here it is a roof and forbidden forever; but above we deal with a partition and prohibition only in what is made to permit; and this distinction is proven from Tur and Beit Yosef at the beginning of seif 7 — examine it; also what he wrote that that one above etc. is not so — for he learned from chapter Kirah and did not learn from Eruvin except that it has permission when it was already a handbreadth wide; and one can answer that what he wrote from the Talmud there — that is chapter Kirah; also what he wrote that it implies from the Talmud through change etc. — one can dismiss easily this implication and need not expand; and likewise in Mordechai it is explained permitted even without changes before the sun — as Shulchan Aruch wrote; and Olat Shabbat erred in this matter wondering at Shulchan Aruch — the act at the end of Eruvin deals with a partition — for if not, what does Tosafos Shabbos daf 125 bring as proof from this that even in a partition tent applies — until here; and it is astounding — for on the contrary from Tosafos and Rosh and Ran it is explained we deal with a roof — he brings proof from the end of Eruvin that there is also a dispute in a roof-tent — examine it; and that tent applies in a partition he brings from Sukkah — a temporary wall — and not from Eruvin.
