Not precise, for likewise if it is boiling yad soledes bo — as written seif 5 and seif 29; and so from Rashi's words; and so the poskim in Yoreh De'ah, that a solid item even in kli sheni cooks, and all the more so roasting. And it appears to me permitted to spread fat, for there is no issue of cooking, as written seif 16; and due to nolad there is none, since it is not b'ein — provided he not crush it with his hands but place it upon it little by little and it melts on its own.
