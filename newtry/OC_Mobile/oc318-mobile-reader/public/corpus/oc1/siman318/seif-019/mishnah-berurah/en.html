(118) Opposite the fire — not specifically; the same when passing the spit with the roast on it from the fire — as long as it is boiling where yad soledes bo, forbidden to smear on it; and even if he afterward placed it on a bowl that is kli sheini — also forbidden, for several poskim hold a solid mass where yad soledes bo cooks even in kli sheini; nevertheless b'dieved do not forbid when placed in kli sheini, for we rely on poskim that kli sheini does not cook in every case. All this is with fat and oil that is raw and was not cooked; but fat that congealed — permitted to smear even opposite the fire, for there is no more cooking concern since already cooked, as we wrote above in seif 16 that dry thing already cooked has no more cooking concern; and nolad concern does not exist since absorbed in the meat and not visible, provided he does not crush it with his hands but places a bit on it little by little and it melts by itself. The same permitted to smear for this reason congealed goose fat on flour types and legumes [such as noodles, barley groats, rice] placed on a bowl even if boiling, only that he place on it little by little and it melts by itself, as above. It is stated in Tosefta: one placed the fire, one placed the wood, one placed the pot, one placed the water, one placed the meat, one placed the spices, and another came and stirred — all are liable [meaning the one who places fire is liable for kindling, for when he carries a coal from place to place it ignites from the wind of his going; the one who places wood is also liable for kindling — so Rashi; and the others all for cooking; and stirring too for this reason that whoever does something needed for cooking is cooking, as Rambam wrote. Gemara there; see in Migdol Oz]. One placed the pot, one placed the water, one placed the meat, one placed the spices, one placed the wood, one placed the fire, and another came and stirred — the last two are liable [this is the correct version as Gra emended in his glosses on the Tosefta, and so according to what Beit Yosef found emended in Rambam's books; no need now for the strain that Mahari Bei Rav strained in Kesef Mishneh — see there]. The Rav HaMagid wrote: per the version "whoever has no liability in its cooking," such as fully cooked — no liability in stirring it. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Kolbo: when it is on the fire, liable for stirring even when fully cooked. Our cooking vessel called samovar set up from erev — forbidden to take water from it on Shabbat when not swept of coals [so too in Sefer Tosefet Yerushalaim above siman 253 see there, who brought this in the name of Yerushalmi that says there regarding a boiler not swept one does not drink from it on Shabbat; the reason: because it is made in sections he fears perhaps its fastening will be consumed and he adds water].
