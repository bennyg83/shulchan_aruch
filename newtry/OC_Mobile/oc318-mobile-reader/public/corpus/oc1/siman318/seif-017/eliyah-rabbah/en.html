[17] Because of bishul, etc. And the same law if there is roasting after cooking; therefore forbidden to place a cooked thing next to fire without gravy (Piskei Rekanati in the name of Rosh and Semag, Magen Avraham); but in Shiltei Gibborim chapter Chavitos he wrote Tur's view is there is no roasting after cooking even though he holds there is cooking after roasting — it was astonishing to me, for it appears from Sefer Yere'im daf 38 and the decisors mentioned above they are equal; and it is also clear there that there is baking after cooking, such as one who bakes after scalding; but I found some proof for his words in Kalbo daf 24 — requires study Kalbo there; and so I found in Mas'at Binyamin he ruled thus except he extended the difficulty there and Shiltei Gibborim and Kalbo there were omitted. And further Shiltei Gibborim wrote: there is no baking after baking and roasting after roasting and no roasting after baking and baking after roasting. Magen Avraham wrote: for all, no roasting after roasting; and a thing that was roasted is permitted to place next to fire even if it cooled — and the same for baked — until here his language; and requires study from what Dress wrote in the previous seif "however to warm it on fire forbidden rabbinically" — forced to say there it deals when warming on fire in gravy for it is worse; and it appears there it deals that without gravy it is the law of roasting after cooking; or one may say he holds like Shiltei Gibborim mentioned above; and see Magen Avraham s.k. 26 — requires study.
