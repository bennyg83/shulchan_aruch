(ב) Even though it is kli sheini — for since they are for bathing, presumably he heated much and the cold ones mixed in cook through this even though the bathtub is kli sheini [Tur]; and this is what he wrote "for behold he heats them much":

(ג) One does not place cold water in it — see later authorities who wrote: specifically a small amount of water that can cook there; but if he places much until the hot becomes lukewarm through this — permitted, as below end of seif 12; and it appears if the bathtub were kli rishon there is concern for Ran and Rabbeinu Yerucham's view who wrote in a bathtub forbidden to place even abundant cold:

(ד) But one may place, etc. — and even if the bathtub is kli rishon permitted to pour into cold water if they are more numerous than the hot; and although forbidden to pour on spices as written in this seif that it is like kli rishon — this is specifically on a solid mass; but here the hot mixes with the cold and their heat is nullified since the cold are numerous.
