(ג) But one may place. And even from kli rishon permitted to pour into cold water, for the lower overcomes the upper and cools it [Tur]. And even though forbidden to pour onto spices, as written in that siman, for it is like kli rishon — here it is different, for the hot mixes with the cold and its heat is nullified since the cold are many.
