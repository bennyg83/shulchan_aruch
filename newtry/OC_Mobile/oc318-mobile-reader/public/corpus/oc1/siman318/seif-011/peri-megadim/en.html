Even. Taz: Eliyah Rabbah 22, 23 that the Gemara explains not like Rashi — only from when he removed them — see there. And that Shabbos Mem 2 — a kettle of water opposite the stove not to warm — implying forbidden to warm alone even if impossible to bring to boiling there — forbidden; therefore kli rishon that yad soledes bo is cooking; and if so there is proof likewise one who removed from fire even if it does not bring to boiling — cooking; and the Gemara distinguishes between standing at fire and removing from fire.
