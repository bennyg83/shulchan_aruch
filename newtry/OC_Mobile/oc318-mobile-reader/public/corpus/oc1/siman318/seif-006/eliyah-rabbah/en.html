[19] With dough. But with a substance that smears, it is prohibited, as stated in siman 314, seif 11 (Magen Avraham). Taz, however, writes that dough is like wax and the act of sealing is smoothing. Here, however, the case concerns one who does not wish to seal the opening tightly, only to keep it from remaining completely exposed, and therefore does not press the material strongly against the vessel.
