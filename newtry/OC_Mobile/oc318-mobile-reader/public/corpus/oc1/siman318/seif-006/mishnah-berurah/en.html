(א) Something hot where yad soledes bo, etc. — Mechaber goes according to his view in this seif that if it cooled somewhat until yad soledes bo is not in it, there is still cooking concern; but according to what Rama wrote in the gloss in seif 15 that the custom is to permit if it did not cool completely — then we do not require yad soledes bo:
(ב) In it — and even if not fully cooked, for we said above in this seif there is cooking concern even in boiling — this case is different lest through this he come to complete cooking, only that its heat will be preserved inside [Taz]; but many later authorities disagree and hold it is permitted only when fully cooked, for we fear perhaps a bit of cooking will be added through the heat; and it is written in Sefer Maamar Mordechai that "fully cooked" — even if it shrinks and improves for him through this standing is also permitted:
(ג) To leave it, etc. insulated — in garments from erev; and he informs us that permitted to leave the vessel beneath garments with which they covered the pot; and even though insulating on Shabbat is forbidden even in something that does not add steam — here since the main insulating was from erev for the pot's need this is permitted; therefore if the pot was uncovered, since permitted to cover it, permitted to cover incidentally also the vessel with the hot thing inside:
(ד) Its mouth in dough — so its heat will not escape; and it deals with something not muktzeh, such as he needs the dough for his household chickens. See Magen Avraham: specifically dough that is not subject to smoothing; but in wax, pitch, or clay that are smoothing substances — forbidden because of smoothing, as stated above siman 314 seif 11, forbidden to place wax in the barrel hole to seal it because he smooths; and Taz wrote dough is also subject to smoothing, except there is no smoothing concern because he is not particular to stick it well, only that it not be completely exposed so it will not cool; but there regarding the barrel hole he was particular to stick well and smooth around the hole so wine will not drip out — end quote; and according to this, also in clay and wax permitted in our matter; but for Magen Avraham above — forbidden:
(ה) But one does not leave, etc. — for something that cooled we rule in this seif there is cooking after cooking; therefore forbidden even if he did not insulate it in anything; and this is in something moist; but in something dry, even if cooled completely — permitted, as below in seif 8:
