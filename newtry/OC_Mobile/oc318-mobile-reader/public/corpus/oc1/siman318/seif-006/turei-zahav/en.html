(א) A hot thing that has yad soledes bo. And even if it did not cook completely, for we said above nearby that there is concern of cooking even while boiling — here it is different because it cannot come to complete cooking; and that which we say in the Gemara "and not so that it will be heated but so that its heat will be preserved" — meaning he should not place cold so it will be heated; but something already hot is permitted, for here there is only preserving its heat and it will not come to complete cooking:

(ב) To smear his mouth with dough. It is difficult, for smearing is spreading — in Aruch it is written: smearing, rubbing, anointing, and oiling are one; if so, what is the difference from the case in the chapter "The barrel" brought siman 314 seif 14 that one may not put wax in the hole of the barrel because he spreads it? One must say it deals when he wants to seal the hole — therefore he wants to stick the wax to the vessel's wall around the hole, as is stated there, in the Mishnah: R' Yosi said an incident came before Rabbenu HaGadol and he said I am concerned for it due to a chatas; Rashi explained: lest he spread the wax to stick it to the vessel's walls around the hole — it is implied it is not certain he sticks it, only we are concerned for this because he is particular that the liquid not drip; and here he does not want to seal his mouth so much, only that it not be completely exposed and cool — therefore we are not concerned he will stick much to the vessel, as stated:
