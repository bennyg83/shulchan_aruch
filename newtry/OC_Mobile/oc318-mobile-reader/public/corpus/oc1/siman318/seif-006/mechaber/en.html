A vessel that has something hot in it where yad soledes bo — permitted to place it on Shabbos atop an insulated pot so that it retain its heat and not cool; and one may smear its mouth with dough if he has dough that was kneaded from yesterday; but one may not place a vessel that has something not so hot atop a pot that is so hot that the upper one can heat from its heat until yad soledes bo:
