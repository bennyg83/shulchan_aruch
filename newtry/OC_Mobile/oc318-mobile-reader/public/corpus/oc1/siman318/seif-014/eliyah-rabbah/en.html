[14] [Dress] Even to rinse them, etc. See in Beit Yosef what he wrote that Pardes's words are ambiguous and he resolves Derishah — this is his language: his suggestion of his words is that some explain old salted fish is raw fish that is eaten alive even if not rinsed in hot water but in cold — on this he wrote it is not so, for since it does not need hot water for its repair this is simple that in cold it is permitted; and since it is fit even by cold, the same law applies also by hot water; and what he wrote that they would say raw fish is forbidden etc. — this is what he says: those who forbid raw fish are as if they forbade rinsing cups etc., and this is not so — until here his language. It is clear from this that whatever cannot be eaten even by rinsing in cold unless in hot — forbidden in hot; and in any case in cold permitted; but what is possible in cold — permitted even in hot:
