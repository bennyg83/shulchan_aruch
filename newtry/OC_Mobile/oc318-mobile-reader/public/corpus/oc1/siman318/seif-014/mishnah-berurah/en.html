(א) Regarding something that cannot, etc. — meaning even if the water and liquids stand there a long time they will not heat so much:
(ב) Meaning a place at his belly, etc. — for we cannot measure by hand, for some have yad soledes bo from slight boiling and some do not:
(ג) Since it can, etc. — and we fear perhaps he will forget to take it:
(ד) And the same with fruits — meaning forbidden to leave them in a hot place to remove their coldness lest he forget until they roast; and even though eaten as they are raw — also forbidden, for roasting name applies since they improve thereby; but to place them far from fire, even something not eaten as it is raw — permitted. Nevertheless permitted to place a heating stone and lead vessel near fire to warm [meaning there is food in the vessel that did not cool and he wants it hot] even if so close the vessel can melt there or the heating stone burn, for since he does not want it thus it is davar she'eino mitkaven; and presumably he will not forget and will be very careful to take it before it melts or burns:
