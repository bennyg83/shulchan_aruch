And if Motzei Shabbat was Yom Tov — forbidden, for Shabbat does not prepare for Yom Tov (Likutei Mahariv siman 2; Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tashbetz; Magen Avraham there; Eliyah Rabbah or 3; Tosafos Shabbos or 2; Eliyah Rabbah or 4); and some say preparation does not apply here and one should be concerned for the first view (Rabbi Zalman or 3); and likewise if he cooked for an ill person on Shabbat and Motzei Shabbat is Yom Tov — forbidden for a healthy person; and specifically a thing not eaten as it is alive (R' Akiva Eiger there); and if Yom Tov was day one and day two — day two permitted as written siman 513 seif 5 in the gloss — see there; and water that was heated on Shabbat — if it cooled at Motzei Shabbat and it is Yom Tov — certainly preparation does not apply (R' Akiva Eiger there); and for those accustomed not to drink water unless they heat it, as in the previous or — it appears preparation applies and one may not drink it at Motzei Shabbat if it is Yom Tov:
