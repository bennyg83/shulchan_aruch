(א) One who cooks on Shabbat, etc. — And if it is a rabbinic melacha — see Peri Megadim who wrote they have one law; and b'shogeg he must wait until Motzei Shabbat — there, for he saw from Shabbat 38a that it says there "cooking" — he does an act; but this one who does not do an act, etc., and he did not say because it is rabbinic; and it is astonishing that there the Gemara wanted to say that even b'mezid it is permitted that day for this reason, as is stated there explicitly, and no posek among those who are lenient that day even for a rabbinic prohibition; therefore the Gemara needed this reason; and I searched and found in Biur HaGra that he wrote and concludes for halacha that even according to Shulchan Aruch who ruled like R' Yehuda — this is by Torah law; but in rabbinic melacha he holds they did not penalize shogeg because of mezid; and he brought for this explicit sugyot; and so Rambam ruled that one who immerses vessels on Shabbat b'shogeg may use them, b'mezid he may not use them until Motzei Shabbat [but this requires study, for Gra wrote there that in rabbinic melacha R' Yehuda holds like R' Meir, and it is implied there both for shogeg and mezid; and so it is implied from Rambam who ruled regarding immersing that b'mezid he may not use them until Motzei Shabbat — and it is implied that at Motzei Shabbat permitted even for him; and why — according to Rambam who ruled like R' Yehuda that in Torah melacha b'mezid forbidden forever, also in rabbinic b'mezid should be so, for R' Yehuda holds they did not penalize shogeg because of mezid in rabbinic — but in mezid itself we do not find in the Gemara that R' Yehuda distinguishes between rabbinic and Torah — requires study]; again I found that also in Chayei Adam he wrote: it appears to me that b'shogeg in rabbinic prohibition permitted even for him that day; and Gra in his Biur preceded this:

(ב) One of the other melachos — Chayei Adam general 9 wrote: specifically in a thing where an act was done to the thing itself that changed from what it was — like cooking and the like; but one who carries from domain to domain where the thing did not change from what it was — if b'shogeg permitted even for him and even that day; and if b'mezid forbidden even for others until Motzei Shabbat immediately; and nevertheless one should be stringent in all Torah prohibitions like cooking, end of his words.
