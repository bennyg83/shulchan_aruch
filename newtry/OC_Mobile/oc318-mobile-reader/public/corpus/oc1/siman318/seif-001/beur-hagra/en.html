(א) Seif 1 "one who cooks on Shabbos" etc. He ruled like R' Yosi and not like R' Meir because the anonymous mishnah in Chullin is like him; but Tosafos and their camp ruled like R' Meir and their proof is very clear. And what they wrote that the anonymous mishnah in Chullin is like R' Yosi — this is not relevant, for the anonymous mishnah in chapter 2 of Terumot is like R' Meir, and likewise the sugya at the end of chapter 3 of Shabbos concludes and expounds like him, and likewise Rav Ori rules like R' Meir, etc. And what Ramban and Ran z"l rejected because Rav rules according to his reasoning, for he holds tithe-taker is like R' Yosi, but we rule like R' Yochanan who holds the law follows the stam. This is not in our gemara; on the contrary, R' Yochanan holds in chapter HaKosev that tithe-taker, but there we rule like Rashbag — rather it is a scribal error in their words, and we must say Rav rules according to his reasoning, for these general rules of Eruvin 47a do not apply to him, but we rule like R' Yochanan; nevertheless their words do not appear correct, for if so, why did the Rif and Shulchan Aruch rule like R' Shimon in "one who leaves his house and goes," etc., since Rav holds like him? Rather, as stated below — where it was stated it was stated. And even if you push that this case is different because he did not rule halacha explicitly but only gave an indication, nevertheless it does not appear right to push away, and aside from this general rule there is none here — for even if one would hold halacha like R' Yosi, nevertheless here halacha is like R' Meir, for the dispute is in a braisa and anonymous in the mishnah, as stated in chapter 4 of Yevamos 42b, and as the Rif stated in chapter Kol HaKelim. Even though that of Chullin is also anonymous, that is a precise inference — Shabbos similar to 21 — and even though it binds, etc., we do not abandon the simple meaning of the mishnah. And all the proofs Ramban brought from what is stated in chapter Tolin 120a — wife of R' Zeira, etc., mother of Abaye did, etc., and these are shogegin — and likewise from chapter 1 of Yom Tov: incident — he brought him, etc. — all these compel the opposite, for R' Meir penalizes in rabbinic matters and R' Yosi does not penalize, and all these are rabbinic; only that one can reconcile the words of the Rif in this from what we say in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Orlah, and Tosafos brought in Gittin 54a s.v. naflu, etc. — R' Meir's reason, etc., R' Yosi's reason: R' Avahu said R' Yochanan — all prohibitions for which He increased upon him, shogeg is permitted, meizid is forbidden, etc. — hence R' Yochanan holds like R' Yehuda and R' Yosi, and this is Ramban's words that R' Yochanan disputes Rav; and perhaps according to his view as above in this general rule of R' Meir and R' Yosi, etc. And R' Yochanan found another anonymous position at the end of chapter 5 of Terumot — a seah of terumah, etc., and afterward, etc., if shogeg permitted, etc. — and now it also comes out well: even though the anonymous mishnah is like R' Meir, nevertheless since another anonymous mishnah is like R' Yehuda, we return to the general rule of R' Meir and R' Yosi, and Rav according to his reasoning and R' Yochanan according to his reasoning, as above. And likewise in Zevachim 74 — "and it fell" specifically it fell by itself, as stated in Tosafos that Rav according to his reasoning as above; but for R' Yochanan even if she miscarried — and the words of Shulchan Aruch there are not correct even if Tosafos holds thus for halacha, as Beit Yosef wrote. And also what Razah critiqued the Rif in chapter 3 of Shabbos for what the Rif wrote and infer from this that R' Yossi the sandalmaker's view does not exist, therefore halacha is like R' Yosi — and Ramban strained to reconcile it; and the Rosh wrote in chapter Merubah siman 5 "and my mind is too short to understand his words," for these amoraim dispute according to R' Yossi the sandalmaker — and how did he write infer from this that it does not exist, etc. — nothing is difficult, for his words are very clear from the sugya of the gemara there 71a: all the reasoning of the Rif to rule like R' Yossi the sandalmaker against the general rule of R' Meir and R' Yosi is only because the gemara's conclusion is that "the Sages" is R' Shimon and he holds like R' Yossi the sandalmaker, and if so, in rabbinic matters he holds like him the halacha is like him; but per the one who holds ma'aseh Shabbos is rabbinic, the gemara's conclusion there is that the Sages exempt the rest but for shechitas Shabbos they penalize rabbinically, and it would have been necessary to establish the Sages like R' Yossi the sandalmaker — and if so, for us for whom halacha is like the one who holds ma'aseh Shabbos is rabbinic, there is no necessity that the Sages hold like R' Yossi the sandalmaker; rather we return to the general rule of R' Meir and R' Yosi, etc. And the dispute of R' Elazar and Ravina is a practical difference in the explanation of the mishnah whether it refers to "and the Sages exempt the rest" or not, as above — and this is what he said "therefore halacha," etc. Nevertheless Tosafos are primary for halacha, and one can bring proof for them from what is stated in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Yom Tov, thus: "The house of R' Gamliel would grind pepper in their mill." R' Elazar b'R' Tzadok: once my father ate at R' Gamliel's and they brought before him anigrin and in it was ground pepper; when he tasted them he withdrew his hands. He said to him, do not be concerned — from before Yom Tov they are ground. They asked there and R' Gamliel did before R' Tzadok as shogeg and ate — only not to permit melacha through R' Gamliel. According to this the proof is to the contrary; but in Milchamot at the end of chapter 3 he wrote that since they did not ask in our gemara it does not exist — but one should not reject his explicit words in the Yerushalmi. And one can also say, as stated at end of chapter 3, cooking is stricter — but this, etc., and likewise all his proofs were rejected as above. And further, that explicitly in Yerushalmi chapter 2 of Terumot: an objection was raised before R' Yochanan "what do you say?" He said to him, "I have only the mishnah of maaser and one who cooks on Shabbos — shogeg may eat, meizid may not eat." Hence halacha is like R' Meir that in shogeg he may eat, etc. And also in rabbinic matters he does not penalize, as R' Yochanan said above. And what R' Avahu said there, in the name of R' Yossi "all prohibitions," etc. — this applies both to Torah and rabbinic; therefore Rabbi was anonymous in Terumot there regarding maaser and one who cooks — maaser is rabbinic and likewise immersing vessels, etc., and one who cooks is from the Torah — and with this anonymous mishnah R' Yochanan ruled as above. And what the Geonim and likewise Ran and Ramban wrote that therefore one should rule like R' Yosi because there are many poskim on his side, etc., and likewise the Rosh at beginning of Chullin — this is not so, for Tosafos already wrote at beginning of Yom Tov and infer from this that we are considered students of Torah, and proof from bishul akum, and all the more to be stringent in the ruling of Rav Mori like R' Meir, and all the more that the sugya of chapter 20 of Shabbos is like him and all amoraim there — and this is primary for halacha:
