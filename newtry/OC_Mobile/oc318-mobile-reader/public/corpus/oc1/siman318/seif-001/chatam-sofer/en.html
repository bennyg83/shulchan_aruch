In Magen Avraham s.k. 2 in the matter "if it was," etc. — requires study, for the case is not clear. It appears: see Tosafos Shabbos who explains well the view of Maharam and Rabbeinu Yerucham there, and accordingly they hold like the view of Rosh chapter 2 of Bikkurim; and according to this view the difficulty of Tosafos in Yevamos daf 2a s.v. because of, etc. is resolved; however it is difficult, etc., see there; one can say there the kohen is not impure in its body unlike in a chatas that deals where its owner is impure, see there in Tosafos Shabbos and Rambam who does not learn like the explanation of Rosh in Bikkurim — he explains the words of Rabbah in Yevamos like Rashi there and it does not deal with bodily impurity; if so it is not relevant to say Rav Ashi refers to bodily impurity since it was not mentioned there; therefore Rav Ashi well says it is fiction. Nevertheless it is so that the words of the poskim are resolved per the words of Tosafos Shabbos cited above; and in the addition Nofek Mashal cited above, nevertheless the words of Rama are not resolved, for he decided to be lenient against Rambam and Rashi and Tosafos; therefore it appears to me to add further that here all agree that certainly in other prohibitions one can say that the essence for one who has no matirin should be nullified in any case, since for one who has matirin it is forbidden on account of "until you eat it in prohibition eat it in permission," therefore they are also forbidden on account of "we do not distinguish" for one who has no matirin, as we learn in Bikkurim they are forbidden even to non-priests, as Ra'avad explained there. Here too, since it is said that from the outset the law permitted the cooker himself who has no matirin, how can it be said forbidden to the whole world — behold for them it was only forbidden on account of the penalty of the cooker; and since it was permitted to him, how is it forbidden to other people? Rather, since for this one it was nullified, it was nullified for the whole world; and the law is true law in my humble opinion. And in Tosafos Yoma daf 47 end s.v.
