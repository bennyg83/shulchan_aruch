And we do not say that he combines the vessel, for it is davar she'eino mitkaven and not a psik reisha, as Maggid Mishneh wrote, and this is his language: It appears that whenever he does not intend, it is not fit to call it psik reisha and obligate, for when he intends he performs a melacha and when he does not intend there is no melacha at all, for he does not want to make a vessel from it — similar to cutting a chip: whoever does not cut it to wedge in his teeth is exempt, for whoever is not repairing a vessel is exempt. And even for R' Yehuda who obligates davar she'eino mitkaven, and one who intends to combine — some say he is liable d'oraisa and some say d'rabbanan, end of his words ch. 20; and see siman 334 and siman 471 seif 3:
