(א) Abundant cold water, etc. — but a small amount that can heat from the kettle's heat until yad soledes bo — forbidden; but abundant — permitted even if he fills the entire vessel with cold water and through this the vessel can come to hardening [meaning when a metal vessel is hot and one places cold inside, the vessel is strengthened and this is completing smiths' melacha, for the fire's boiling pulsates and it is near breaking and water hardens its pulsations] — nevertheless permitted since he does not intend for this and it is not psik reisha, for perhaps the vessel will not come to hardening thereby. One who intends to harden — some say he is liable d'oraisa, and some say d'rabbanan:

(ב) Into cold water or cold, etc. — and in both, even if the water is slightly cold so yad soledes bo remains after mixing — also permitted since it is in kli sheini, for we rule kli sheini does not cook, as above in seif 9:

(ג) That they not be in kli rishon — for if the hot are in kli rishon forbidden to pour cold water into them; and the same forbidden to pour from them into slightly cold water that will cook through them:

(ד) And if the water is abundant — it refers to cold into hot; and likewise if hot into cold when the cold are abundant — permitted. See Chayei Adam who wrote: cold into hot specifically when he pours all at once in one large pouring; but little by little forbidden to pour, for it cooks immediately and what help is it that it cools afterward:

(ה) Only that their coldness be removed — and even hot somewhat like lukewarm — permitted, only that yad soledes bo not be in them:
