Provided they are not in a kli rishon — See Mishna Berurah that for hot into cold we also require they be great enough — Magen Avraham and Peri Megadim; and it is implied from Magen Avraham that Tur's view is also thus [and like Tosafos who wrote thus explicitly in the passage "He puts" in their conclusion — there]; and Taz wrote that Tur's view is to be lenient in hot into cold even with small cold amounts; but Rambam is stringent in this — see there; and see Sefer Beit Meir who expanded greatly on this and concludes Tur's view is to be lenient, and so Rashba and Ran; and their reason is this is not considered iruy since they mix with each other; and therefore the hot water poured into the cold is considered a kli sheini and the small cold cannot cook through the hot water; and nevertheless he concludes for practical law one should not be lenient against Tosafos's view, since it touches a Torah prohibition; and he wrote: therefore in my humble opinion for practical law, as is widespread today when they make a drink called punch — namely they squeeze erev Shabbat many lemons for their juice and mix sugar and beverage [called arak] and on Shabbat or Friday night they pour much hot water cooked on Shabbat b'heter into this mixture — in our poverty we certainly must be concerned for Tosafos's view and forbid from a kli rishon unless yad soledes bo no longer applies, for it touches Torah prohibition of cooking on Shabbat; but b'dieved it appears in my humble opinion one may rely on Rashba and Ran who permit explicitly, and on the plain meaning of Rambam's and Tur's language that hot into cold is permitted even with much hot water although their reason is hard for us to grasp. And what people also practice thus with coffee or tea that they cook erev Shabbat a little water with coffee and tea in a way very strong in taste, and on Shabbat pour much hot water cooked b'heter — there is further support to permit due to those who are lenient brought in seif 15 — Rashba's view and those with him who hold even in a moist thing that cooled there is no cooking after cooking, etc. — see there; therefore one should not protest others who do so, but for oneself every person of spirit should be concerned even in this not to pour from a kli rishon as long as yad soledes bo applies, end of his words; and so Gra's view also seems to be concerned for Tosafos's view mentioned:
