In a kli sheini that has water. In the Gemara it is stated in the chapter Kirah that R' said to R' Shimon bar Avdimi: take a kli sheini and put — and we wrote it in Yoreh De'ah end of seif 92; and it is explained there that if he draws with an empty vessel from the kli rishon — that vessel with which he draws has the status of kli rishon stringently and not leniently; and above siman 258 we wrote that one may not put fruits inside the kli sheini of the oven in winter to warm them, for they are not inferior to water; and Mordechai wrote in the chapter Kirah one may not put fruits inside the oven's kli sheini in winter to warm them:
