There in Beit Aharon David "but from erev Shabbos permitted." Chiddushei Rashba wrote: and we did not find anyone who is stringent upon it to leave it; and all the more so per the one who says to return — we learned; end of his words. It is implied to lean toward leniency that even that which is forbidden to leave atop a stove — permitted to leave atop derivatives of the sun; and it appears to me that one should rule that also on derivatives of fire it is permitted, for it is no worse than a swept stove, since there is no concern lest he extinguish:
