(א) Like placing on a kirah. For even in something that has no issue of cooking it is forbidden to place it on a kirah, for we decree lest he stoke. And the first view appears essential: since the pot interrupts between it and the kirah — there the pot stands directly on the fire (Maggid Mishneh ch. 22). And there is difficulty why Beit Yosef did not decide in seif 7 to permit, for he himself so too in siman 253 seif 3. And one can say specifically roasting is permitted l'chatchila on a grufa kirah, but something cooked is forbidden to place on a kirah even if it is hot and has no issue of cooking, for we decree lest he place it when it is cold; and permitted only chazarah. And so too must be said according to Rabbenu Yerucham's words; nevertheless one may be lenient, for it appears from Maggid Mishneh that since the pot interrupts, it is better than a grufa kirah and permitted to place hot food upon it:

(ג) And mitzamek v'yafeh lo. Thus wrote Rabbenu Yerucham, and this is what Rama wrote in Hagahot seif 4. And there is difficulty: since Beit Yosef ruled in seif 4 not like his view, it appears he holds even mitzamek v'ra lo has cooking if it cooled — and if so forbidden to place it on a meicham; and why did he write here mitzamek v'yafeh lo — requires further study.
