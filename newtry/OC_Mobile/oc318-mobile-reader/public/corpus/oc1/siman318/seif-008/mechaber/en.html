To place a cold thing that was fully cooked atop a micham on the fire — some say its law is like placing it opposite the hearth, and anything that is permitted to place opposite the hearth in a place where yad soledes bo, such as something dry — permitted to place it atop a micham on the fire; and some say it is like placing atop a stove l'chatchila and forbidden, even if it was fully cooked, and even if it shrinks and worsens, and even if one places it there to retain its heat; and the first view appears primary.
