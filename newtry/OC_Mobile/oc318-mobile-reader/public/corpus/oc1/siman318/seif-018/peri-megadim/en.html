(א) Of a bathhouse. Taz: missing the word "ein" — it should read: there is no distinction in law between Abaye and R' Abba; and he brought Tosafos Shabbos daf 42a "however" and there they wrote: bath for washing kli sheini — nevertheless no cooking at all; only the viewer thinks kli rishon and therefore forbidden cold bread in it; and it appears b'dieved permitted, for not cooked; but Tur wrote "and they heat much and cook" — requires study, for Yoreh De'ah all decisors concluded kli sheini also does not expel and absorb — kli sheini does not cook; and they did not distinguish whether heated much or little; and such is widespread law — it appears l'chatchila forbidden, for the viewer etc. like Tosafos. And what he wrote dispute between Tur and Rambam — possibly Tur holds according to his method kli sheini for washing cooks; if so or both are equal actual kli rishon for drinking with kli sheini for washing — cold bread forbidden and hot to cold many permitted as Magen Avraham sign 35; but Rambam holds like Tosafos that bath kli sheini only rabbinically the viewer etc.; therefore certainly distinction between kli rishon hot to cold no permission except much cold water that cannot only cool — permitted like a kettle he emptied. And between bath kli sheini for washing hot to cold — even if not so many permitted, for there is no such decree. And to say pouring without ceased spray — doubt; and for Rambam doubt rabbinic permitted; and it is already decree upon decree elsewhere this reasoning is not used. And what Taz wrote "the lower one is strong" — difficult, for nevertheless pouring without ceased spray cooks kli rishon stringently — how did he permit even in kli rishon according to his view even kli sheini.
