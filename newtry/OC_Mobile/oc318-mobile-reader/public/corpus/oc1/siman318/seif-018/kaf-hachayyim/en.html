18) Forgetfulness is considered inadvertent. Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 28; Knesses HaGedolah there; Magen Avraham there; Eliyah Rabbah there; Tosefes Shabbos there; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there; Erekh HaShulchan, paragraph 3. The latter writes the same in his work on Yoreh De'ah, siman 99, paragraph 10; see there. This applies whether one forgot that it was Shabbos or forgot that the act was a prohibited labor. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there.
