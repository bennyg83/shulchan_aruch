(ב) Even in a pot, etc. There Mahariv wrote one may not stir with a spoon, for it is like cooking, as we say in the first chapter of Shabbat regarding an overturned pot that is plastered; and so I saw in the house of Mahari — end of his words. What he brought from the first chapter of Shabbat is no proof, for there it deals with wool for a pot which is stricter, as is written nearby; but here it already cooked completely — permitted; and so explicitly at the end of siman 321 regarding wood chips in the pot — permitted because it already cooked completely; rather certainly due to extra distance he touched on this; and therefore it appears to me that mere removal without turning in the pot — only taking a piece — there is no concern here, even though it did not cook completely — forbidden even in removal if stirred; nevertheless when fully complete one need not be stringent in removal, only that one who wishes to be stringent should turn in the pot; and certainly at the end of siman 321 he permits even l'chatchila — there:
