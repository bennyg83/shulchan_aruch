(א) That he removed them boiling — he stated he removed them for amplification that even in this forbidden when not fully cooked; and when fully cooked, even standing on the fire permitted to remove with a ladle — so implied in Beit Yosef; but Avnei Nezer concludes to forbid in this:

(ב) That was found coarse, etc. — and all the more actual stirring forbidden; and there is chatas liability according to what Shulchan Aruch ruled above in this seif that in this he assists and brings close to cooking and is considered cooking:

(ג) Fully cooked permitted — even to stir in it, for there is no cooking after cooking, as above in this seif:

(ד) Forbidden to stir in it — because of dyeing, for dyeing melacha is constantly stirring so it will not harden; also through stirring the dye is absorbed more in the wool:

(ה) Even in a pot, etc. — rather turn the pot into a bowl and do not remove with a ladle; see later authorities who do not practice to be stringent in this, for truly the essence is as we wrote initially that fully cooked — even stirring permitted, as explained below siman 321 end of seif 9; one who wants to be stringent — be stringent in actual stirring, but to remove with a ladle do not be stringent at all when fully cooked and not on the fire:
