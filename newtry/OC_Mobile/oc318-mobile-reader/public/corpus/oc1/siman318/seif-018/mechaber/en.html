A pot and kettle that one removed boiling from atop the fire — if it was not fully cooked, one may not remove with a spoon from them what is found coarse, and there is an aspect of cooking; and if it was fully cooked — permitted. But wool for a cauldron — even though it absorbed the eye — forbidden to stir it [meaning to agitate it with a spoon]:[And l'chatchila one should be careful even with a pot in every case [Piskei Mahariv]:]:
