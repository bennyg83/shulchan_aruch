5) There: One who cooks on Shabbos, etc. Whenever a dispute creates doubt whether an act constitutes cooking, the doubt concerns a rabbinic matter and is resolved leniently. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 10. This means that the prohibitions imposed upon one who deliberately or inadvertently cooked, or performed other labor, are penalties and therefore rabbinic. A doubt is consequently resolved leniently. See below, paragraph 8.
