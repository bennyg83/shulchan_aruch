There are those who say, etc., due to cooking — See Mishna Berurah that likewise there is roasting after cooking; and see Peri Megadim who brought from Pesachim 41 that the first cooking was not nullified and from this derived that permitted to warm it in hot water on Shabbat [meaning if he did roasting after cooking erev Shabbat] like other cooked things; and it appears according to him the same for cooking after roasting — permitted on Shabbat to leave it without gravy in the oven even if it will roast, for the first roasting was not nullified from it — for R' Eliezer of Metz equates them; however in my humble opinion Peri Megadim's law is not compelled, for although it is proven there the first was not nullified — behold the second certainly exists; and one may well say it is now called cooking after roasting. However this is my humble opinion: if he cooked and afterward roasted it erev Shabbat — permitted to leave it in the oven on Shabbat, for the last action determines more and it is called roasting after roasting:
