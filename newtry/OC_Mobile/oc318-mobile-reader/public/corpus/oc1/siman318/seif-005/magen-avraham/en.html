(א) Due to cooking. Even though there is no cooking after cooking, nevertheless there is cooking after roasting, and likewise there is roasting after cooking; therefore forbidden to place something that was cooked near the fire without gravy (Piskei Rekanati in name of Ra'am and Semag). But according to all there is no roasting after roasting, and something roasted is permitted to place near the fire even if it cooled, and likewise something baked; and something cooked is permitted to warm in hot water, as written seif 4:

(ב) Even in kli sheni. For there are things that cook in it and we are not expert — see Taz:

(ג) They permit in kli shelishi. For they hold kli shelishi does not cook; and according to what is written seif 4 in name of Tosafos one should be stringent, for it appears like cooking; but those who permit hold like the second explanation of Tosafos, and so is Shulchan Aruch's view — and one should be stringent:
