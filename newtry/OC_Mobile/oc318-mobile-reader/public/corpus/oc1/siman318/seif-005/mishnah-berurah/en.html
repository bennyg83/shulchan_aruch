(א) If afterward cooked in liquid — meaning he placed it in the pot while cooking with gravy; if he places it in cold liquids, let it be derived there is cooking concern for the liquids:
(ב) Because of cooking — for although there is no cooking after cooking in dry things, nevertheless there is cooking after roasting, even while still hot from roasting [later authorities]; and the same according to this view there is roasting after cooking; therefore forbidden to place something cooked near the fire without gravy [Magen Avraham]; and not specifically near the fire — the same wherever it can roast from oven heat; therefore careful not to return cooked meat without gravy into the oven in a hot place where yad soledes bo, even if he stands it on a pot that interposes; but according to all there is no roasting after roasting, and something roasted may be placed near the fire even if cooled, provided he does not bring it closer lest he come to stoke; and also not to stand it on the oven floor but on an interposing empty vessel beneath so it will not appear like cooking; see above siman 253 seif 5; and the same in baking like this, for there is no baking after baking:
(ג) And forbidden to place bread, etc. — he added another law; the reason according to this view is there are things that cook even in kli sheini because they are soft and we are not expert; therefore he added to forbid placing baked bread even in kli sheini; and the same according to this view be stringent also in other things not to place them in kli sheini; see below in seif 9 regarding spices:
(ד) They permit in kli sheini — he holds kli sheini does not cook anything:
(ה) Even in kli rishon — he holds cooking after baking or roasting does not apply; and the same the reverse:
(ו) And the custom is to be careful, etc. — meaning they do not place bread in noodle soup in a bowl that is kli sheini; and all the more careful not to pour from kli rishon on them. Those who cut onions and place in the soup in the bowl — only after yad soledes bo is not in the soup, for in bread there is leniency because it was already baked; nevertheless they are careful, all the more in onions one must be careful for we are not expert in what is called soft, as above; and all the more according to what is practiced to put onions into the dish which is a solid mass — certainly be careful, for some poskim hold a solid mass in kli sheini where yad soledes bo is judged like kli rishon. Even if he waits until the soup in the bowl does not have yad soledes bo — nevertheless careful not to pour from the pot in the bowl onto remaining onions in the bowl if yad soledes bo in them, for pouring cooks to the depth of a peel as we rule seif 10, and there is d'oraisa concern. And likewise what some people do when making leg-broth soup — cut a piece of bread in a vessel and pour leg-broth from kli rishon on them — not well done, for even if he does not place bread first until he pours soup first in the vessel to make kli sheini — Rama wrote to be careful in this; rather if he wants to place bread he should wait until yad soledes bo is not in the soup, or at least scoop with a ladle from the pot so the bowl becomes kli shelishi; and all the more if he wants to place onions and spices in this hot soup — certainly be very careful, for if he pours from kli rishon where yad soledes bo there is primary melacha concern, as above:
