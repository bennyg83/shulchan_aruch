[18] Not to place bread, etc. The same applies to other foods, as the conclusion states. This answers Pri Chadash's difficulty in siman 68, seif katan 20, concerning the Gemara's prohibition of placing chicken into a kli sheni. See seif 9, where some say salt cooks even in a kli sheni. Regarding those who put onions into salad, Taz writes that they should not be placed into a bowl while the liquid is still yad soledes bo. Magen Avraham permits onions because they are like spices under seif 9. It appears proper to be lenient, since Shiltei Gibborim writes that common practice is to place baked food even into a kli rishon without concern, following the lenient opinion above. Mas'at Binyamin writes that the accepted practice has already become stringent, but in a kli sheni there is no need to be careful about placing bread.
