(א) Cooking. And know that all laws forbidden due to cooking — even to place on the stove or oven before kindling — are forbidden, as is written at the end of siman 253 — Magen Avraham:

(ב) And some say. And Bach ruled like the first view; and see Sha'arei Teshuvah Motzei Shabbat:

(ג) To warm it. Even in a kli rishon, as is written seif 15 that there is no cooking after cooking — it is implied from Shulchan Aruch's language that if it was not cooked from erev Shabbat but only dissolved in hot water — it is forbidden to warm it in hot water on Shabbat; and good to be stringent if yad soledes bo in the hot water — Magen Avraham:

(ד) They rinse. Meaning they pour upon it from a kli sheini; but forbidden to warm it even in a kli shelishi, for it appears like cooking — Bach. And some permit for spices in a kli shelishi, for spices are made to sweeten the pot and do not appear like cooking; and it appears to me that an onion has the same law as spices, for it is made for taste — Magen Avraham:

(ה) One who salted. It is implied that in cold water it is permitted; therefore permitted to rinse herring on Shabbat in cold water even if very salty — Taz:
