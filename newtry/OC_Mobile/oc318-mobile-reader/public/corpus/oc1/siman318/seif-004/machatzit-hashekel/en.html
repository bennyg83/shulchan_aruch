(s.k. 4) He became ill that day, etc. And there is difficulty: if so muktzeh machubar, etc. — perforce it must be said as Rashi wrote in Beitza: since they did not pick them yesterday from erev Yom Tov, it is as one who set aside with his hands, and even for Rashi forbidden. If so, regarding an animal too one should say thus, since they did not slaughter yesterday it is as one who set aside with his hands and forbidden even for Rashi. And see there another reason in Beit Yosef: specifically regarding machubar, for to pick from the attached is an easy matter — therefore when they did not pick yesterday they set it aside and pushed it away with their hands; unlike shechitah, where we require a checked knife and an expert slaughterer — there is no proof from that they did not slaughter yesterday that they set it aside from their mind and it is pushing away with hands, lest a knife or expert slaughterer not be available; and since they did not trouble themselves for both — it is not pushing away with hands:
