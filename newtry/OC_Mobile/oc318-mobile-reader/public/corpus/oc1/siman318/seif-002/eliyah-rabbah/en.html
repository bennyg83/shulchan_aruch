[6] If the dough became obligated today, etc. Maharshal, Chullin 1:30, prohibits dough that became obligated in challah today because of muktzeh.
[7] [Levush] For he did not actively reject it, etc. He did not personally set it aside; rather, an external prohibition rested upon it. Once the prohibition departs without transgression, it returns to its permitted status (Mishmeret HaBayit, p. 19). This explains why Taz's lengthy objections here and at the beginning of siman 310 are unnecessary.
[8] [Levush] Because the fruit continues growing, etc. This concerns fruit that has not yet completed ripening. Once fully ripe, we do not say that it continues growing in the relevant sense (Beit Yosef; Magen Avraham).
