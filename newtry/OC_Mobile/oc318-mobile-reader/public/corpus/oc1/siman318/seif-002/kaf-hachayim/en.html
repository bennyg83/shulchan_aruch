(b) There. One who cooks on Shabbat, etc. Whether one bakes bread or cooks food or spices or heats water — all are one matter; and the measure for heating water is enough to wash a small limb; and the measure for cooking spices is until they are fit for what they cook them for. Rambam chapter 9 law 1; and likewise one who melts one type of metal, whatever amount, or heats metal until it becomes coal — this is a tolada of cooking; and likewise one who heats wax or fat or pitch and sulfur and the like — these are tolados of cooking and liable; and likewise one who cooks earthenware until it becomes pottery — liable for cooking; the rule of the matter: whether burning a hard body in fire or hardening a soft body — liable for cooking.
