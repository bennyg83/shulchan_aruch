Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Machatzis HaShekel; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1. Mateh Yehudah, paragraph 1, however, disagrees with Magen Avraham and permits the pot. Although Erekh HaShulchan, paragraph 1, challenges Mateh Yehudah, he nevertheless agrees in practice that the pot is permitted for another reason; see there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1, rules stringently like Magen Avraham. Ben Ish Chai, Parashas Bo, paragraph 1, likewise rules this way, as do the later authorities. Kashering is nevertheless effective even for an earthenware utensil. If one cooked in it without kashering, sixty times the absorbed substance is required, because this is more stringent than food cooked by a non-Jew. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1; Likkutei Rema on Nezirus Shimshon, siman 6, paragraph 2; Ben Ish Chai there. Although kashering is effective here for earthenware, it must nevertheless be performed three times, as stated in Yoreh De'ah, siman 113, seif 16; see there.
