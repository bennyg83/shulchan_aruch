(א) As long as, etc. — meaning even if its boiling already ceased from it:
(ב) That yad soledes bo in it — but if yad soledes bo is not in it, even kli rishon does not cook. If they did not remove it from the fire, forbidden to place spices in it, even salt in every case, even if he intends to remove the pot quickly from there lest he forget until it cooks, as below in seif 14:
(ג) Spices into it — but in kli sheini permitted to place spices even if yad soledes bo, for it does not cook there; and if a solid mass is placed in kli sheini where yad soledes bo, be careful, for some poskim hold a solid mass where yad soledes bo is judged like kli rishon:
(ד) Once they removed it — for on the fire even beef tongue cooks:
(ה) Like beef tongue — and even if it softened, even in kli sheini its softening is not its cooking, for even in cold you see thus:
(ו) Salted meat — meaning meat that was salted and rinsed properly:
(ז) Of an ox — for although the meat does not cook, the moisture in it cooks [Tur]:
(ח) Without salting, etc. — meaning not that the reason to forbid is because salt softens the meat and it cooks afterward in kli rishon; rather even without salting at all it would be forbidden to place because of the moisture, as above; and why did Mechaber mention salted — for if it were not salted it would be forbidden to place because of blood prohibition in a boiling vessel without Shabbat prohibition:
(ט) And some forbid, etc. — in the Gemara there are two versions on this; in the latter version salt is lenient because it is hard to cook like beef tongue, therefore permitted even in kli rishon when removed from fire; in the first version salt is stringent, for even in kli sheini it cooks; and most poskim ruled like the latter version, except because some are stringent he wrote that one who is stringent — blessing upon him. And this is quarry salt; but salt made from water they boil — for all there is no cooking after cooking, as below in seif 15 [Magen Avraham and Sh'ar Efraim]; and likewise sugar permitted to place in kli rishon after removing from fire for this reason; some doubt this; good to be careful from kli rishon l'chatchila:
(י) Even if it is, etc. — on the fire, even according to Shulchan Aruch's conclusion — forbidden, as above:
(כ) That the salt is nullified — and even though something done for taste is not nullified — here it is different, for here it was also salted already erev; and it deals that the salt he now placed was not enough to give taste in the pot alone apart from yesterday's salt; but if today's salt alone could give taste — the food is forbidden until Motzei Shabbos if he placed it in the vessel while on the fire; and this is quarry salt; but salt made from boiled water — do not forbid the food, as above:
