(There) s.k. 28 "even if yad soledes bo," and as Maggid Mishneh wrote in the entry sald that dominates — in the Yerushalmi like soledes, and so responsa Beit Yaakov siman 20 — not like Shach Yoreh De'ah siman 105. However it appears to me from the Yerushalmi that for the matter of Shabbos they made a distance for a kli rishon even if yad soledes bo does not apply, as long as it is hot; and also for a kli sheini as long as yad soledes bo; and I cited proof from that rice pan. And all this for Shabbos; but for orlah everything depends on yad soledes bo, whether kli rishon or kli sheini; and from this Magen Avraham does not deal. If so, nevertheless for the law the words of Shach are correct.
