[9] To insulate it on a weekday, etc. Even rolling on top of a weekday [vessel] is forbidden (Rosh and R' Yitzchak); a roof with earth — even if already roasted and he does so to preserve its heat — forbidden even from daytime like a weekday; but dust [without a roof] Rash"l permits in responsum siman 61; and Yeshu'as Yaakov and Magen Avraham forbid; Nachalat Tzvi and Lechem Mishneh chapter Kirah ruled like Rash"l. Ran wrote: forbidden to place food on hot springs of Tiberias; but from erev Shabbat permitted; and to leave inside forbidden as written siman 326 seif 3. Rambam chapter 9 wrote: one who left meat on coals — if it roasted in it like a dried fig he is liable; even at half-cooking according to Ra'avad; and to Rambam not liable at half-cooking until all the meat is half-cooked (Magen Avraham); investigation needed — for it seems Ra'avad agrees with Rambam and only wrote half-cooking is like food at the stage of ben drusai, unlike Rashi who wrote one-third cooking.
