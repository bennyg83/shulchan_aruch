(ב) "All that the hand scalds." For if the hand does not scald there is no bishul, as stated 40b, and see there Tosafos s.v. "and infer," etc., and this is what is stated here throughout the siman yad soledes bo, and even inside the kirah and oven it is permitted if there is no yad soledes bo, as stated in siman 257, and likewise in Yerushalmi beginning of chapter Kirah in the oven see there. And what Magen Avraham wrote in note 48 in the name of the Yerushalmi to the contrary is not precise, for so it is stated there chapter 3 end of halacha 2: R' Zeira in the name of Rav Yehuda — it is permitted to warm in a place where the hand rules, and forbidden to warm in a place where the hand does not rule; in a place where the hand does not rule — until when? Until one can put his hand inside and it is scalded. All concede in a kli sheni that it is permitted. What is the difference between kli rishon and kli sheni? R' Yosi said: here the hand rules and here the hand does not rule. R' Yonah said: here and here the hand does not rule; rather they made a distancing for kli rishon and did not make a distancing for kli sheni. And meaning: and even though in kli rishon one only places to warm, nevertheless they made a distancing so that one not place there until it heats, as the Rosh wrote in chapter 3 seif 10, and in Ran and Maggid Mishneh — and the Yerushalmi calls it "the hand does not rule" what the gemara calls yad soledes bo:
