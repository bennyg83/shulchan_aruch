Magen Avraham s.k. 11 "even to place on the stove" and "to place on the stove" before heating a wet cooked dish while it is still boiling, and after it cools a gentile will kindle the stove — one can say it is permitted l'chatchila, for that which one says one places the water and one places the fire — the first is forbidden d'rabbanan since that fire is there, for then he would be liable for cooking; but here at the time they place it, even if it were there it would be permitted, for there is no cooking after cooking; and after it cools he does nothing; and requires study for the law:
