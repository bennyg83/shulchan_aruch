(c) One who left meat on coals — if it roasted in it like a dried fig (which is one-third of an egg as written below siman 308 seif 3, see there) even in two or three places — liable; if it did not roast in it like a dried fig but all of it was half-cooked (see above siman 253 or 28) — liable; if half-cooked from one side — exempt until he turns it and it is half-cooked from two sides. Rambam there law 5; and Magen Avraham brought it there; and what Magen Avraham brought there, in the name of Ra'avad — already Rabbi Akiva Eiger challenged in or 9 and wrote that Ra'avad also agrees with Rambam in this — see there; and all this is for chatas liability, but prohibition exists in every case — requires study: half the measure is forbidden d'oraisa as stated in Shabbos daf 74a and Rashi there; and so in Yoma daf 74a dispute of R' Yosi and R' Elazar — requires study like R' Yosi; and so Kenesset HaGedolah proved in Hagahot Beit Yosef Yoreh De'ah siman 98 or 67 from many passages etc. that halacha is like R' Yosi who said half the measure is forbidden d'oraisa — see there:
