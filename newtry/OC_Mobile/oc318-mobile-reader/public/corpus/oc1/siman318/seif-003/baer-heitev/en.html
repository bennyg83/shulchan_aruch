And Rashal wrote in a responsum siman 61 that it is permitted to roast on a hot roof from the sun's heat, for it is not exchanged for toldos ha'or — there; and Magen Avraham wrote that his words are not compelling; and also in Sefer Tal Orot they disagree; and in Yerushalmi it is stated explicitly that it is forbidden.
