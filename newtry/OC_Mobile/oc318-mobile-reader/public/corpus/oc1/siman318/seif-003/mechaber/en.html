Just as it is forbidden to cook with fire, so it is forbidden to cook with derivatives of fire, such as to place an egg beside a pot, or to break it on a cloth that was heated by fire so that it roast; and even with derivatives of the sun, such as on a cloth heated by the sun — forbidden as a decree lest [one come to] derivatives of fire. And likewise forbidden to insulate in sand or in road dust that were heated by the power of the sun; but in the sun itself, such as to place an egg in the sun or to place water in the sun so that they heat — permitted:
