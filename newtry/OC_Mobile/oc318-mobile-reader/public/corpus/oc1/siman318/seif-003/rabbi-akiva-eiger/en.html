Magen Avraham s.k. 2. That according to them it is davar she'yesh lo matirin. For behold even in Jerusalem they would not have permission without nullification, and it would be permitted for them also outside Jerusalem (from this it is implied not like what Tzemach Tzedek wrote regarding a treifah born on Yom Tov that nevertheless Yom Tov prohibition is not nullified); if so, since the prohibition outside Jerusalem is yesh lo matirin for priests, it is not nullified also for non-priests; here too with one who cooks on Shabbos, since for others this prohibition is yesh lo matirin — forbidden also for the cooker; and even though terumah that became mixed is yesh lo matirin for an impure priest, nevertheless the prohibition of non-priesthood is not yesh lo matirin; and in truth a non-priest too is forbidden to eat it in impurity, for this prohibition since it is yesh lo matirin for priests — also for a non-priest it is not nullified:
