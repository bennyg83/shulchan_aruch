(א) In a toldah of fire — and one who cooks in it is liable; therefore one who places fruits or water on the oven or inside [the coals] after the oven was lit and they cooked there is liable; and d'rabbanan forbidden to place even before lighting, as below. And all laws of cooking mentioned in this siman apply also to something cooked through a toldah of fire:
(ב) Even in a toldah of sun — and even b'dieved forbidden; and it is possible that after Shabbat it is permitted even for the cook himself [Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger]:
(ג) To insulate it, etc. — and even from erev, for sand is something that adds steam as explained above in siman 257 seif 3, and forbidden to insulate in it even from erev as explained there [Rosh]. And in Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger he brought in the name of Rashba that permitted to insulate in it from erev, for whatever was heated at erev from the sun becomes completely cold on Shabbat night:
(ד) On a weekday — and even if he will only roll it on Shabbat on hot sand so it will roast — also forbidden, a decree lest toldah of fire; therefore forbidden to roast an egg on boiling [from sun]. One who cooks in Tiberias hot springs is exempt from chatas, for it is like toldah of sun; but liable for rabbinic stripes, for there is prohibition in this; therefore forbidden to place food on Tiberias hot springs on Shabbat; but to leave on them from erev is permitted, and to place inside forbidden because of insulating in something that adds steam as written siman 326 seif 3:
(ה) But in the sun itself — and we did not decree lest fire, for fire is not interchanged with sun:
