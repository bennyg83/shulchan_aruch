(א) Empanada, etc. — it is pastida mentioned in several places, bread folded filled with fat pieces placed there to warm it; permitted because since it is dry, even if cooled cooking no longer applies, as in seif 15; and even though the fat returns and melts — nevertheless called dry since when he places it to warm it had not yet melted:
(ב) Opposite the fire — meaning on some interposing vessel, otherwise forbidden, as above siman 253 seif 3 and 5:
(ג) And even though, etc. — and it is not similar to crushing snow and hail explained below siman 320 seif 9 forbidden because of nolad that he creates water; and here too at first it was thick and frozen and now became thin and clear — here is different for he does nothing with his hands, only it melts by itself; and regarding hail too it is taught: but one may place it in a cup of wine to cool and he is not concerned even though it melts, since it is by itself:
(ד) And all the more a pot, etc. — for here when it melts the fat mixes with the gravy and is not recognizable, truly similar to what we say "but one may place in the cup" because the hail is not recognizable when it melts into it:
(ה) That it is permitted — meaning only regarding nolad prohibition that does not apply here; but regarding cooking prohibition here is stricter than above, for gravy is something moist and in moist thing that cooled cooking applies as Mechaber wrote in seif 15; therefore here permitted only if he stands the pot in a place where yad soledes bo is not reached:
(ו) And some are stringent — he holds nolad prohibition also applies here; and even in a pot where fat is mixed with broth and not similar to "but one may place in the cup" — there the hail is not recognizable at all when it melts in the cup; but fat that melted floats on top and is recognizable; and even to leave it in heat so it will melt — also forbidden according to this view. Know that even for those who are stringent, it is forbidden only when there is much fat on the pastida that when it melts there will be much liquid outside recognizable by itself; but if there is not so much fat on the pastida, or he stands it so far from the heat it will not melt until liquid flows outside but only a bit of it melts inside, or if he threw off the fat on it — permitted to warm the pastida for all; and even though still fat from the meat inside oozes — a small amount is not considered significant and permitted for all. Likewise permitted to warm on Shabbat a fatty meat piece even though part of it oozes, since a small amount of oozing is not considered significant and permitted. Later authorities wrote: a pot of water or other liquids that froze on top — permitted for all to stand in a place where yad soledes bo will not be reached, for when the ice melts it will immediately mix within the liquids beneath and is not recognizable:
(ז) To be stringent — nevertheless before the oven was lit permitted to place the pastida, since some permit even afterward; and do not do so before an ignoramus [Magen Avraham and Sh'ar Efraim]:
(ח) In a place of need one may rely — meaning then even l'chatchila permitted; and b'dieved in every case do not forbid:
