A thing that was fully cooked and is dry and has no gravy — permitted to leave it opposite the hearth even in a place where yad soledes bo:And even if it already cooled; but if it is boiling, even in a thing that has gravy — permitted. And some are lenient to say that anything one does not place directly on the fire or the stove itself, only near it — even if it cooled — permitted [Maggid chapter 22].
