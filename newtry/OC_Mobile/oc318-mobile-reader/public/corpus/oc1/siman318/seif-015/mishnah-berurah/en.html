(א) Something that was cooked, etc. — the reason: since it was already cooked, there is no more cooking concern; however on the fire itself forbidden d'rabbanan because it appears like cooking; but in standing opposite the fire where people do not usually cook thus — even rabbinic prohibition does not exist [Rashba]; see what Rosh wrote who holds near the fire is also forbidden, only must distance somewhat:

(ב) Fully and it is dry — for if not fully cooked, even if dry thing and boiling — there is still cooking concern, as above in this seif; and automatically forbidden to stand in a place where yad soledes bo; and if moist thing, even fully cooked there is still cooking concern if cooled, according to what Mechaber ruled at the beginning of this seif:

(ג) Opposite the fire — he stated opposite the fire and not next to the fire to teach one must distance somewhat from the fire; therefore we do not fear he will come to stoke the fire, for since the sages required distancing somewhat he has recognition and will not come to stoke [Rosh]. I saw some later authorities wrote it deals when the fire was made as a fire by itself; but when the wood fire is inside the oven, forbidden to place a pot there even if he distances somewhat from the fire, since it is a place where yad soledes bo and no less than what is explained siman 253, forbidden to place a pot in a stove when not swept, even if not standing close to the fire. However if he does not stand the pot on the oven floor but on some interposing vessel — there is room to be lenient, as above siman 253 seif 3:

(ד) And even if already cooled — to explain Mechaber's words: it deals when cooled, and therefore he permitted only in dry:

(ה) Boiling — meaning where yad soledes bo:

(ו) And some are lenient to say — disputes Mechaber who holds specifically when dry; but in moist thing that cooled there is cooking after cooking; and this view holds there is no cooking after cooking in every case, even if cooled completely:

(ז) On the fire itself, etc. — for if he places on the fire itself, even though no d'oraisa prohibition according to them — nevertheless forbidden d'rabbanan because it appears like cooking; and also lest he come to stoke the coals:

(ח) If not, etc. — even though according to Mechaber since it cooled somewhat from its heat and yad soledes bo is no longer there, there is cooking concern, as we wrote above in this seif — the custom is to be lenient here, relying on those who are lenient whenever it did not cool completely:
