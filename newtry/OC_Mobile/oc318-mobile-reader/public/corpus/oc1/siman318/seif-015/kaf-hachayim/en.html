And Moreinu our teacher z"l wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 102 in the gloss: if it became mixed — it is nullified; and we do not say it is considered a davar sheyesh lo matirin because permitted for others after Shabbat; rather since for this one forbidden forever it is not considered a davar sheyesh lo matirin and it is nullified. And Rav Kohen-Porat z"l wrote there: this specifically for the cook; but for others even if mixed it is not nullified, for it is considered for them a davar sheyesh lo matirin; and so Chokhmas Adam there — see there; however Magen Avraham s.k. 2 wrote: also for the cook forbidden if mixed on that day, for it is not nullified since for others it is a davar sheyesh lo matirin permitted at Motzei Shabbos; unless mixed after Shabbat — then nullified and permitted also for the cook — see there; and so Eliyah Rabbah or 3; Rabbi Zalman or 4; and he wrote nullification is in the majority — see there; and see in responsa Rav Pa'alim ch. 1 siman 17 what he discussed on this matter and concluded to forbid like Magen Avraham — and this is his view but not his reason — see there; and so in his book Ben Avraham chapter Bo 1, 2 — see there; and Rav Kohen-Porat wrote there: if mixed on Shabbat the mixture is forbidden to the intentional cook forever; but per the reasons Rav Pa'alim wrote there it appears one should not forbid the cook except like others; and such is the essence, since without this Moreinu and many Acharonim permit the mixture for the cook even on that day as mentioned above; therefore it suffices for us to be stringent only on that day and at Motzei Shabbos the mixture is permitted for him like others — investigate; and afterward I saw so in Chiddushei Ra'akah that permitted after Shabbat — see there:
