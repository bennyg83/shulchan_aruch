(א) (s.k. 15) One rinses — namely one pours upon it from kli sheni, for from kli rishon forbidden to pour, for we rule iruy from kli rishon cooks to the extent of a peel, as below seif 10; and see there in Magen Avraham s.k. 32:

(ב) But forbidden, etc. — for it appears like cooking — namely even though we rule kli sheni does not cook, nevertheless since it appears like cooking they were stringent for Shabbat:

(ג) And it appears to me onion is like spices and permitted to warm in kli sheni:

(ד) And see seif 5 in Magen Avraham s.k. 19 that some disagree on this and hold it does not appear like cooking; and Mechaber there wrote to forbid even bread in kli shelishi, for there are things that cook in kli sheni and we are not expert which thing cooks in kli sheni — therefore one should be stringent with bread that is soft. And Magen Avraham s.k. 18 added that in other things too one should be stringent.
