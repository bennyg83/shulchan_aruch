For the general rule 40b that anything in which there is no bishul is permitted opposite the fire, as stated 40b for the one who says oil has no bishul permitted opposite the fire, and likewise in toldos ha'or such as on a meicham as stated in seif 6, or in hot water as stated in seif 4; but on a kirah it is certainly forbidden, for they only permitted chazarah under the conditions explained in siman 257 seif 2, and all the more on the fire — they only permitted opposite the fire, which is like supporting.
