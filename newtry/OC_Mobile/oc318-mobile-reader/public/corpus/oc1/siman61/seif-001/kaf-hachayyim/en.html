1) [Se'if 1] One should recite Krias Shema with concentration, awe, etc. Zohar Chadash, Midrash Rus, page 109b, speaks at great length about reciting Krias Shema with concentration. I therefore wished to copy it, to demonstrate the great virtue of Krias Shema. It states as follows:
1) At another window on the southern side stand six lintels, admitting those who engage in Krias Shema willingly and with concentration every day. Rabbi Perachyah said: 'By God, as I gazed at that nearby window, the appointed angel named Na'aniel, who oversees the gate on the southern side, approached me. He said to me: Remain at your station and behold this window. No one has permission to enter there or look through it except those who engage in Krias Shema properly. For all who engage in Krias Shema properly, sixty appointed angels enter before Mashiach, crown him with holy crowns, and say to him: This is the crown of so-and-so, who engaged in Krias Shema properly. I asked him: My master, what constitutes Krias Shema properly? He answered: Pious one, a supernal secret lies here in Krias Shema. It contains four sections. The first is the section of unification, containing two unifications in twelve words: six words in the supernal unification and six in the other unification. The second section, "These words shall be," has forty-two words, in the mystery of the Name of forty-two letters' - this is Ana BeKoach, etc., as Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar Krias Shema, chapter 24, writes in Zohar's name; see there, and Shaar HaKavanos, discourses 5 and 9 on the night. 'The third section, "It shall be if you heed," incorporates the right within the left and the left within the right. In the secret and mystery of the holy Chariot it extends through "You shall place them." This section rules over all, in the mystery of seventy-two words within the mystery of the seventy-two Names of the holy Chariot' - this is the Name of seventy-two derived from Vayisa, Vayavo, and Vayet, as Pri Etz Chaim, chapter 23, and Shaar HaKavanos there explain. 'The fourth section, "You shall place them," contains fifty words corresponding to the fifty gates of Binah. The section of tzitzis incorporates them all, and the sections ascend to the number of the limbs of man, etc. Come and see: Whoever properly completes the unification and concentrates upon these sections every day merits beholding these lintels and windows. He has dominion here, and none can impede him, provided he reads properly, concentrates upon these supernal mysteries, and takes hold of my right hand. He said to me: Pious one, come and see the honor of those who properly unify the holy Name and engage in Krias Shema in this manner. I entered one palace, and within it were 365 palaces, corresponding to the days of the year. Avraham, Yitzchak, and Yaakov rule in that palace, with Adam HaRishon among them. I saw many companies of souls clothed in joy, in the embroidered designs of the Tree of Life and many hues of supernal glory, entering the palace. Yofiel, the great appointed angel over them all and the great appointed angel of Torah, was with them. They began and proclaimed: Adorn yourselves, holy ones of the world! Behold your children, who unify the holy Name and concentrate willingly upon the sections of Krias Shema. I saw Adam HaRishon attending to them. When the Krias Shema recited by a person enters, it first ascends to this palace, and Adam HaRishon senses its fragrance. If one letter is missing from Krias Shema, he does not accept it, for the structure of Adam, which has 248 limbs corresponding to the words including those repeated by the shaliach tzibbur, is incomplete. If he finds it proper, he accepts it; Avraham, Yitzchak, and Yaakov take it from him and kiss it. Who has seen the joy? Who has seen the commotion among all the righteous in Gan Eden who come amid that joy? When that person comes to this world, who can behold his joy? The Patriarchs seat him beside them twice each day. Every day, dew from the King's head descends upon him. Through that dew they perceive and eat the food eaten by supernal angels, and know what was, what will be, and what is destined to be, until the Holy One, blessed is He, resurrects the bodies that decayed in this world and raises them in a properly perfected structure.' You now know the great virtue of one who recites Krias Shema with concentration. A person must therefore take care always to remember these teachings whenever he recites Krias Shema, evening and morning, an established statute that shall not pass. At minimum, he should apply his heart to understand the meaning of the words, omit no word or letter, and be especially mindful of the kavanos of the Names.
