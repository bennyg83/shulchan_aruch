1) [Se'if 1] One should recite Krias Shema with concentration, awe, etc. Zohar Chadash, Midrash Rus, page 109b, speaks at great length about reciting Krias Shema with concentration. I therefore wished to copy it, to demonstrate the great virtue of Krias Shema. It states as follows:
1) At another window on the southern side stand six lintels, admitting those who engage in Krias Shema willingly and with concentration every day. Rabbi Perachyah said: 'By God, as I gazed at that nearby window, the appointed angel named Na'aniel, who oversees the gate on the southern side, approached me. He said to me: Remain at your station and behold this window. No one has permission to enter there or look through it except those who engage in Krias Shema properly. For all who engage in Krias Shema properly, sixty appointed angels enter before Mashiach, crown him with holy crowns, and say to him: This is the crown of so-and-so, who engaged in Krias Shema properly. I asked him: My master, what constitutes Krias Shema properly? He answered: Pious one, a supernal secret lies here in Krias Shema. It contains four sections. The first is the section of unification, containing two unifications in twelve words: six words in the supernal unification and six in the other unification. The second section, "These words shall be," has forty-two words, in the mystery of the Name of forty-two letters' - this is Ana BeKoach, etc., as Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar Krias Shema, chapter 24, writes in Zohar's name; see there, and Shaar HaKavanos, discourses 5 and 9 on the night. 'The third section, "It shall be if you heed," incorporates the right within the left and the left within the right. In the secret and mystery of the holy Chariot it extends through "You shall place them." This section rules over all, in the mystery of seventy-two words within the mystery of the seventy-two Names of the holy Chariot' - this is the Name of seventy-two derived from Vayisa, Vayavo, and Vayet, as Pri Etz Chaim, chapter 23, and Shaar HaKavanos there explain. 'The fourth section, "You shall place them," contains fifty words corresponding to the fifty gates of Binah. The section of tzitzis incorporates them all, and the sections ascend to the number of the limbs of man, etc. Come and see: Whoever properly completes the unification and concentrates upon these sections every day merits beholding these lintels and windows. He has dominion here, and none can impede him, provided he reads properly, concentrates upon these supernal mysteries, and takes hold of my right hand. He said to me: Pious one, come and see the honor of those who properly unify the holy Name and engage in Krias Shema in this manner. I entered one palace, and within it were 365 palaces, corresponding to the days of the year. Avraham, Yitzchak, and Yaakov rule in that palace, with Adam HaRishon among them. I saw many companies of souls clothed in joy, in the embroidered designs of the Tree of Life and many hues of supernal glory, entering the palace. Yofiel, the great appointed angel over them all and the great appointed angel of Torah, was with them. They began and proclaimed: Adorn yourselves, holy ones of the world! Behold your children, who unify the holy Name and concentrate willingly upon the sections of Krias Shema. I saw Adam HaRishon attending to them. When the Krias Shema recited by a person enters, it first ascends to this palace, and Adam HaRishon senses its fragrance. If one letter is missing from Krias Shema, he does not accept it, for the structure of Adam, which has 248 limbs corresponding to the words including those repeated by the shaliach tzibbur, is incomplete. If he finds it proper, he accepts it; Avraham, Yitzchak, and Yaakov take it from him and kiss it. Who has seen the joy? Who has seen the commotion among all the righteous in Gan Eden who come amid that joy? When that person comes to this world, who can behold his joy? The Patriarchs seat him beside them twice each day. Every day, dew from the King's head descends upon him. Through that dew they perceive and eat the food eaten by supernal angels, and know what was, what will be, and what is destined to be, until the Holy One, blessed is He, resurrects the bodies that decayed in this world and raises them in a properly perfected structure.' You now know the great virtue of one who recites Krias Shema with concentration. A person must therefore take care always to remember these teachings whenever he recites Krias Shema, evening and morning, an established statute that shall not pass. At minimum, he should apply his heart to understand the meaning of the words, omit no word or letter, and be especially mindful of the kavanos of the Names.
2) One should bear in mind that the first section, from VeAhavta through its end, excluding the verses Shema Yisrael, etc., and Baruch Shem, etc., contains forty-two words, corresponding to the forty-two-letter Name of Ana BeKoach, etc. The second section, from Vehayah Im, etc., through Vesamtem inclusive, contains seventy-two words, corresponding to the seventy-two-letter Name derived from Vayisa, Vayavo, and Vayet. From Es Devarai Eileh, etc., to the end of the section are fifty words, corresponding to the fifty gates of Binah. The section of tzitzis also contains seventy-two words when combined with the three repeated by the shaliach tzibbur. These correspond to the seventy-two words in the verses Vayisa, Vayavo, and Vayet, as Pri Etz Chaim and Shaar HaKavanos there explain. The Names in Vehayah appear in straight, reverse, and straight order, while those in the tzitzis section are all in straight order, as arranged in Rabbi Rashash's siddur. See Zohar, Beshalach 52a; Shaar Maamarei Razal, page 14a at the end; and Zohar Chadash, page 10, beginning of column 3. Rabbi Rashash's siddur sets them out as follows: In the first section, which contains forty-two words, one letter of the initials of Ana BeKoach - alef, beis, gimel, etc. - appears above each word. In the second section, one of the seventy-two Names derived from Vayisa, Vayavo, and Vayet appears above each word through Vesamtem. From there onward, one letter of the simple Name Ehyeh appears over each word until completing the forty letters contained in ten instances of Ehyeh; above each of the final ten words appears one complete simple Name Ehyeh. In the tzitzis section, one of the seventy-two Names derived from Vayisa, etc., appears above each word. See below, paragraph 16. Together with Shema Yisrael and Baruch Shem, etc., there are 248 words, corresponding to a person's 248 limbs, as Zohar Chadash, Acharei Mos 59a, states. It further says: 'Every person who recites Krias Shema properly causes each word to bestow influence upon its corresponding limb. If a person does not recite Krias Shema by day and by night, each of his limbs becomes filled with an evil spirit and with all the evil and harmful forces in the world.' One should therefore take care to recite the bedtime Krias Shema before becoming drowsy, so that he can say it properly. This will be explained in its place, with G-d's help.
3) There: One should recite Krias Shema with concentration, etc. Yerushalmi states that the three sections instituted for Krias Shema were chosen because they contain the Ten Commandments; examine them and you will find all ten. Tosafos, end of the first chapter of Berachos 12b. Zohar Chadash, Midrash Rus 95a, likewise states that the early chassidim instituted Krias Shema corresponding to the Ten Commandments and to the number of a person's limbs; see there. Yad Aharon writes in Hagahos HaTur: 'Since the works state that one must contemplate the Commandments while reciting Krias Shema, I saw fit to copy the correspondences here. At "Hashem Elokeinu," one accepts the commandment "I am Hashem your God"; at "Hashem Echad," "You shall have no other gods"; in the verse VeAhavta, "You shall not take Hashem's Name in vain"; in UChesavtam, "You shall not covet"; in VeAsafta, "You shall not steal"; in VaAvadtem Meheirah, "You shall not murder"; in Leman Yirbu Yemeichem, "Honor your father"; in VeLo Sasuru, "You shall not commit adultery"; in Leman Tizkeru, "Remember the Shabbos day," which is equivalent to the entire Torah; and at "I am Hashem your God," "You shall not bear false witness against your fellow."' Eliyah Rabbah 1 writes likewise in Kol Bo's name, as do Moreh BeEtzba 2:23 and Chesed LaAlafim 4.
4) Abudraham writes that the ayin of Shema and the dalet of Echad form the word eid, 'witness,' alluding to testimony. Another interpretation: Shema is an acronym for 'Raise your eyes on high - to Whom?' and for 'Shaddai, King of the world.' When should this be done? Shacharis, Minchah, and Maariv. If you do so, you accept upon yourself the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven, the initial letters of which spell Shema in reverse. Eliyah Rabbah 1 and Shalmei Tzibbur, page 95b, cite this.
5) There: One should recite Krias Shema with concentration, etc. Berachos states that one who recites Krias Shema without tefillin is as though he bears false witness against himself, since Krias Shema says, 'as totafos.' From here I infer similarly that one who recites Krias Shema without concentration bears false witness, since it says, 'with all your heart.' Yosef LeChok for the first day of parashas Mikeitz, citing Sefer Chareidim, page 68a; Yafeh LaLev 1.
6) There: With dread and awe, etc. This dread and awe appear to mean that while reciting Shema one should intend to accept the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven and to be killed for the sanctification of Hashem's unique Name. This is the meaning of, 'with all your soul' - even if He takes your soul. Concerning this the verse says, 'For Your sake we are killed all day.' With this intention, he will recite it with dread and awe, trembling and perspiration. Bach; Eliyah Rabbah 3. See below, paragraph 28.
7) There: Trembling and perspiration. Profuse perspiration should come upon a person when he considers, 'What shall I do when God rises, and when He examines my ways?' How many times have passed when he did not fulfill the law stated in Shulchan Aruch to recite Krias Shema with dread, awe, trembling, and perspiration? A living person should take to heart that whenever he recites Krias Shema, he must constantly remember these words that issued from the mouth of our holy master, and not let them pass. Habit becomes nature, and matters to which a person has become accustomed require great vigilance to perform properly. May Hashem always enable us, our children, and all our descendants forever to do His will and serve Him wholeheartedly.
