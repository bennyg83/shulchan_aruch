With kavanah. Tosafot at the beginning of the chapter wrote that in the Yerushalmi they said that those three sections that they enacted in Keriat Shema are because in them are the Ten Commandments. Therefore a person must intend in them when saying Keriat Shema—if he transgressed one of them: in "Hashem Elokeinu" he accepts the utterance "Anokhi Hashem Elokecha"; "Hashem echad"—the utterance "Lo yihyeh lekha"; in the verse "Ve'ahavta"—the utterance "Lo tisa"; in the verse "Uchtavtam"—the utterance "Lo tachmod"; in the verse "Ve'asafta"—the utterance "Lo tignov"; in the verse "Ve'avadtem meherah"—the utterance "Lo tirtzach"; in the verse "Lema'an yirbu yemeikhem"—the utterance "Kaved et avikha"; in the verse "Ve'lo taturu"—the utterance "Lo tinaf"; in the verse "Lema'an tizkeru"—the utterance "Zachor et yom haShabbat," which is equivalent to the entire Torah; in the verse "Ani Hashem Elokeikhem"—the utterance "Lo ta'aneh bere'akha ad sheker." (And see in Kol Bo—they brought in Sefer Eliyahu Rabbah still other allusions.)
