With intent. Tosafot at the end of the first chapter wrote that in the Yerushalmi they said that those three passages which were instituted in Krias Shema correspond to the Ten Commandments. Therefore a person must have them in mind when reciting Krias Shema, if he transgressed one of them. At ‘Hashem Elokeinu,’ he should accept the commandment, ‘I am Hashem your God.’ At ‘Hashem echad,’ the commandment, ‘You shall have no other gods.’ In the verse ‘You shall love,’ he should accept the commandment, ‘You shall not take [His Name in vain].’ In the verse ‘You shall write them,’ the commandment, ‘You shall not covet.’ In the verse ‘You shall gather,’ the commandment, ‘You shall not steal.’ In the verse ‘You will perish quickly,’ the commandment, ‘You shall not murder.’ In the verse ‘So that your days may be lengthened,’ the commandment, ‘Honor your father.’ In the verse ‘Do not stray,’ the commandment, ‘You shall not commit adultery.’ In the verse ‘So that you remember,’ the commandment, ‘Remember the day of Shabbos,’ which is equivalent to the entire Torah. In the verse ‘I am Hashem your God,’ the commandment, ‘You shall not bear false witness against your fellow.’ See Kol Bo, cited in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah, for further allusions.
