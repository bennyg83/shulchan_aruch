Siman 61—one should read Keriat Shema with kavanah, etc. For they did not say that until here one needs kavanah except bedi'eved.

With awe, etc. From what is written in Midrash Rabbah parashat Emor chapter 27: Rabbi Yitzchak compared to a king who sent, etc.—"you do not stand on your feet," etc.—"the One" you do not need [to mention explicitly].
