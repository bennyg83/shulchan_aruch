Seif 1: one should read Shema with intent, etc. For they said that until this point requires intent only with respect to after the fact.
With awe, etc. This follows from what is written in Midrash Rabbah, parashas Emor, chapter 27: Rabbi Yitzchak said, a parable to a king who sent, etc.; 'do you not stand on your feet,' etc. This implies that the other posture is not required.
