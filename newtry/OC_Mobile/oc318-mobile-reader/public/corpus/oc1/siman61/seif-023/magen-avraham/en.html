One places the resting pause. Meaning: shva nach is called "rest," such as at the end of a word or in the middle of a word that comes after tevir ketanah; and meteg is called "moving," such as at the head of a word or in the middle of a word after tevir gedolah. And the mnemonic for tevir gedolah is "petachei chotam"—only the chirik when there is a yud with it is called tevir gedolah, and when there is no yud with it, it is called tevir ketanah.
