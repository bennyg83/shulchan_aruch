He should rest the mobile one. Meaning, a sheva that is swallowed is called resting, such as at the end of a word or in the middle of a word after a small vowel; and a mobile sheva is one that is pronounced, such as at the beginning of a word or in the middle of a word after a large vowel. The mnemonic for a large vowel is ‘pisuchei chosam,’ only the chirik, when there is a yud next to it, is called a large vowel, and when there is no yud next to it, it is called a small vowel.
