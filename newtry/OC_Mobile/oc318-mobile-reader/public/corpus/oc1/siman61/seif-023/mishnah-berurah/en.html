(36) Hanach—a resting shva "nechtefa" is called nach, such as at the end of a word or in the middle of a word that comes after a small vowel; and a moving shva is called the "metunenet," such as at the head of a word or in the middle of a word that comes after a large vowel. The sign is tag: pituchei chotam—only hirek when it has a yud next to it is called tag, and when there is no yud next to it it is called tek.
