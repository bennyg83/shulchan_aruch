The quiescent. A sheva that is snatched is called quiescent, such as at the end of a word or in the middle of a word after a short vowel. A moving sheva is called one that is pronounced, such as at the beginning of a word or in the middle of a word after a long vowel. The mnemonic is that long vowels open the seal; only a chirik when it has a yud next to it is considered long, and when it has no yud next to it is considered short.
