(23) There—so that he not look at something else that prevents him from kavanah; and everything a person does to awaken kavanah is good—Tur; and in Sefer Zera Kodesh and Bach in Derekh Yesharah 21a wrote that in the uniqueness of Keriat Shema one repairs the defect of the sin of the serpent that separated between the cleavings—see there; and they brought Kaf HaChayyim siman 14 note 42. If so, how much must a person strive with all his strength to have kavanah in the uniqueness of Keriat Shema and turn his heart from every kind of thought so it will be help for him to repair the defect.
