The resting one. A sheva that is swallowed, such as at the end of a word or in the middle of a word after a small vowel, is called resting. A mobile sheva is one that is pronounced, such as at the beginning of a word or in the middle of a word after a large vowel. The mnemonic for large vowels is ‘pisuchei chosam,’ only the chirik is called a large vowel when there is a yud next to it, and when there is no yud next to it, it is called a small vowel.
