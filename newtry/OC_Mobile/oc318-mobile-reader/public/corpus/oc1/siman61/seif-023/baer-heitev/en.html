Resting pause—shva nach, such as at the end of a word or in the middle of a word that comes after tevir kadma; and moving shva is called meteg, such as at the head of a word or in the middle of a word after tevir; and the mnemonic for tevir is "petachei chotam." Only chirik when there is a yud with it is called tevir, and when there is no yud with it is called tevir kadma.
