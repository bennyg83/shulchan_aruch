Their hands. Baal HaTov. And what Shulchan Arukh wrote "on their faces"—meaning on the eyes; and so too in Pa'at HaShulchan: he should close his eyes with his right hand, etc. And it is possible that the words "on their faces" should read "on the eyes," but in the Tur it does not imply so. And see in Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Maharil, who placed his hands on his eyes, etc.; and likewise in the language of the Gemara regarding Rabbi—and see in the Tur.
