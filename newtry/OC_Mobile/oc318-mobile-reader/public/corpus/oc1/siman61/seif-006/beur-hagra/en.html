Seif 6: one must, etc. So wrote Rabbeinu Yonah and the Semak; this is what is meant by saying that one should not snatch the ches. But Rashi and other poskim explained that one should direct all the intent in the dalet, and so it is written in the Zohar and Tikkunim.
And some have the custom, etc. So wrote Rabbeinu Yonah, that for this reason he would place his hand, etc., even though the Rosh rejected it, as above.
