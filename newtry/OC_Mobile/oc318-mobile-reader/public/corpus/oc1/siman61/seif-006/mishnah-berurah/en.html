(18) To extend the chet—Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Mateh Moshe that he should extend the chet for a measure of one-third, and the dalet for a measure of two-thirds. And some say not to extend the chet at all but to intend everything in the dalet; so too the Gra in his explanation.

(19) And he should not extend more—according to Pri Megadim in the name of Perach Shoshan.

(20) The thought—although we say one who recites Shema should not wink with his eyes, etc.—there the winking and gesturing are for another matter and nullify kavanah; but here the gesturing is for the sake of kavanah. The inclination should be east, north, west, south—and one should not incline the head east-west north-south, for that would be, heaven forbid, two and evening.
