To lengthen the ches. The Magen Avraham wrote in the name of the Menoras HaMaor that one should lengthen the ches for a third of the time and the dalet for two thirds. Others say that he should not lengthen the ches at all, but should direct all the intent in the dalet; and so wrote the Gra in his Biur.
And he should not lengthen more. See Peri Megadim in the name of the Peri Chadash.
The thought. Even though we say that one who reads Shema should not wink with his eyes, etc., there the winking and signaling are for another matter and cancel intent; but here the signaling is for the sake of intent. The tilting should be east, north, west, south, and he should not tilt the head east, west, north, south, for that would be, G-d forbid, a cross-shape.
