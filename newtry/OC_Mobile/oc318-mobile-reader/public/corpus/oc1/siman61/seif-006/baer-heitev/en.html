The thought. Even though we say one who reads Shema should not wink with his eyes, etc.—there the winking and hinting is for another matter and nullifies kavanah; but here the hinting is for the sake of kavanah—the Rosh. And the tilting should be east-north-west-south, and one should not tilt the head east-west-north-south lest it be, heaven forbid, two and evening—per Taz.
