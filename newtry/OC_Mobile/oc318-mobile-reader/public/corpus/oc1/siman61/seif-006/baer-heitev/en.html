The thought. Although we say that one who reads Shema may not wink with his eyes, etc., there the winking and gesturing are for some other matter and they cancel his intent. Here, however, the gesture is for the sake of intent. Rosh. The turning should be east, north, west, south, and one should not incline the head east, west, north, south, for that would, Heaven forbid, resemble a cross; see Taz.
