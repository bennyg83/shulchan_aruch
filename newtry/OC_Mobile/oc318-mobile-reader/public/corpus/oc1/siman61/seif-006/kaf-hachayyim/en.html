24) [Se'if 6] One must prolong the ches of Echad so as to proclaim the Holy One, blessed is He, King, etc. Zohar, Vayeitzei 158a, states likewise. One should contemplate through the alef that He is one; through the ches, that He is unique in the seven heavens and the earth, totaling eight; and through the dalet, the four directions and that in the future the entire world will proclaim that He is one. Beis Yosef in Semak's name; Ateres Zekeinim 7. Although there are seven lands, the foundation of the earth is one. Yafeh LaLev 6 in the commentators' name.
25) There: This is alluded to by the hump in the middle of the roof of the ches. That is, He who lives is at the height of the world. Beis Yosef; Eliyah Rabbah 10. One should not prolong the ches excessively. Eliyah Rabbah, ibid. See Magen Avraham 5.
26) There: He should prolong the dalet of Echad, etc. This means that when articulating the dalet, he should prolong the breath issuing from his mouth for the time needed to contemplate in his heart, etc. He should not act like those who prolong the dalet by pronouncing it with a tzeirei beneath it, thereby ruining the meaning. This is the tradition I received from my teacher, the great chassid Rav Shmuel of Lublin. Bach. Lechem Chamudos, chapter Hayah Korei 8; Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur 3; Olas Tamid 3; Eliyah Rabbah 11; and Pri Chadash 6 write likewise. See also below, paragraph 31.
