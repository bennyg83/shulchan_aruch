One must prolong the letter chet of echad. One should intend with the alef that He is one, and with the chet that He is unique in the seven heavens and upon the earth, totaling eight. Beit Yosef in the name of Semak.
Some customarily incline the head in accordance with the intended meaning. The inclinations should follow the order of the four applications of blood upon the altar: east, north, west, and south. At the very least, one should not incline the head east, west, north, and south, lest it appear, Heaven forbid, as though one were indicating a cross.
