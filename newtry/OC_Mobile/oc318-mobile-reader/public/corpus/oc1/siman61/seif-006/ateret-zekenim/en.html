One must extend the chet of echad and must intend in the alef that He is one and in the chet that He is unique in the seven heavens and on earth—behold eight (Beit Yosef in the name of Semak).

And some are accustomed to tilt the head as the thought [directs], and the tilting should be in the manner of the four corners of the altar—i.e. east, north, west, south; and in any case one should not tilt the head east-west-north-south lest it appear, heaven forbid, as if hinting at the name beginning shin-vav.
