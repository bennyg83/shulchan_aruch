In beshakmal"v, quietly—Taz, see 219 Yom Kippur in loud voice in Tur and Levush and Magen Avraham 8; and Moses did not say it in the Torah but took it from the angels—and seemingly difficult why do I need the reason that Jacob said it, behold Moses brought it down to Israel. They said quietly, a parable, etc., as is in Tur there 219.

Between—Taz and Rabbeinu Yonah in the gloss ruled two pauses: between echad and beshakmal"v, and between beshakmal"v and ve'ahavta; but from the Mechaber's language without specification it implies only between beshakmal"v and ve'ahavta; see Levush and Bach on this.
