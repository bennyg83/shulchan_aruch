With the congregation, etc. However, Mateh Moshe wrote as follows: my teacher Maharshal would not say El Melech Ne'eman, neither with the congregation nor alone, and this is correct, for if they were concerned because of interruption, what difference is there between an individual and the congregation? End quote. See Lechem Chamudos, page 13. It appears to me to decide that in the morning, when an individual can have intent through the fifteen vavs, he should not say El Melech Ne'eman; but at night, when there is no other remedy, he should say it. However, I saw in the Shelah and Mateh Moshe another remedy: to have intent with the vav of 've'emunah,' that the vav is written full with three letters, and the vav itself is included, meaning from alef until vav has the gematria of Eheyeh, two vavs are two times ahavah, and the form of the hei is twenty-six.
