(6) With the congregation, etc. But in Mateh Moshe he wrote—and this is his language—my teacher Maharash would not say El melekh ne'eman neither in congregation nor individually, and this is correct if they were stringent on account of interruption—what difference individual what difference congregation, so far—and see in Lechem Chamudot page 13. And it seems to me to decide: in Shacharit an individual can intend in fifteen vavins and not say El melekh ne'eman; but in Arvit where there is no other enactment one must say it. But I saw in Shelah and Mateh Moshe another enactment—that he should intend in the vav of ve'emunah, vav full three letters and vav and vav included—meaning from alef until vav in gematria Eheyeh, two vavins two times love, and the form of hei twenty-six thus—meaning he makes yud vav yud against the name of four, behold here three names. And in Tzror HaMor he wrote one should intend in the verse "You redeemed me, Hashem El of truth" which corresponds to "Hashem Elokeikhem emet."
