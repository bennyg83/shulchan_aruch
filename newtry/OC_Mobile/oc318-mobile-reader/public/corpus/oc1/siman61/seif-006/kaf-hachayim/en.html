(6) With awe and fear, etc. It appears that this awe and fear is in this manner: that when he recites Shema he should intend to accept upon himself the yoke of the kingdom of heaven, to be killed for the sanctification of the unique Name—that is "with all your soul," even if He takes your soul; and on this the verse said, "for Your sake we are killed all day"—for then with this kavanah he will recite it with awe and fear, trembling and sweating. Bach, Eliyah Rabbah siman 3. And see below siman 28.
