“During Barukh Shem Kevod Malkhuto LeOlam VaEd.” See Baer Heitev and below, siman 66, regarding interruption. Machazik Berachah writes in the name of Rama MiPano that one may not interrupt in the middle of Shema or anywhere in the middle of Barukh Shem Kevod Malkhuto LeOlam VaEd. Between these two verses, however, the law is the same as between sections; see there.
