So that they should not say. The Bach wrote that since the entire congregation says it, there is no objection, as written in the Beit Yosef; see there. But the Gemara in the fourth chapter of Sukkah escaped him, for they would say, ‘We are to Him and to Him are our eyes,’ and the Gemara asks: but do we not say that one who says ‘Shema, Shema’ is silenced? There, all of them would say it. Rather, it must be that even if the entire congregation says it, it is forbidden.
