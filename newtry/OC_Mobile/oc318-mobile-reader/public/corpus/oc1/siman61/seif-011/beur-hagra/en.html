Seif 11: and there are, etc. For perhaps there too he did not say the first parsha. Mahari Abuhav.
Those who say, etc. One cannot distinguish between an individual and a congregation, for in the Yerushalmi we say the reverse: there, words of Torah are in public, but for an individual they are supplications. Rather, the Tur wrote that since the Gemara did not distinguish, we should not distinguish. See Magen Avraham.
