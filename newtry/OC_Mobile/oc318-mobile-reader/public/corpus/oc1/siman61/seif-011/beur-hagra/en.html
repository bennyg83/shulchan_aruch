Siman 11—and there are those who, etc.—for perhaps there too he would not say the first verse—Mahari Abulafia.

Those who say [Shema twice], etc. And one should not distinguish between individual and congregation, for in the Yerushalmi they say the opposite there—the order of prayer in congregation, but for an individual supplications—except that the Tur wrote that since they did not distinguish in the Gemara, one should not distinguish—and see Magen Avraham.
