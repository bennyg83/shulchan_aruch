Two times amen, etc. However, it is permitted to say 'amen ve'amen' with a vav, as written in the verse. Levush. This is somewhat difficult: if so, what did the Gemara in Sukkah there ask on the Mishnah where they said 'we are to Him and to Him are our eyes,' since 'to Him' was said with a vav? One can distinguish. As a matter of law, it implies from Magen Avraham that one may even say amen, amen, and that Bachya wrote in parashas Beshalach that one who repeats amen, amen merits two worlds. I examined Bachya, and he wrote 'whoever has intent and repeats,' etc., implying that intent is specifically required. Therefore one who is not assured that he has proper intent should not do so, somewhat like the rule of two prayers in siman 107.
