(18) Two times amen, etc. But it is permitted to say amen and amen with a vav as written in the verse—Levush. A bit difficult if so what does it challenge in Sukkah there on the mishnah that says "we are to Him and to Him our eyes"—behold he said "to Him" with a vav—and one may distinguish. And for practical law it seems in Magen Avraham one should say even amen amen, and so Bachya wrote in parashat Beshalach that one who doubles amen amen merits two worlds; and I examined in Bachya and he wrote "and all who intend and double," etc.—it implies specifically we require intent, and if so one who is not confident he intends properly should not do so—similar somewhat to two prayers in siman 107.
