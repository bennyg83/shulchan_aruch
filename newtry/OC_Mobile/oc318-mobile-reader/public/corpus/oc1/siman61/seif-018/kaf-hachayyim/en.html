The fifteen vavs have the numerical value represented by Yud-Heh. Multiply Yud-Heh by vav, yielding the value of the fifteen vavs; together with the heh at the beginning of the word hadavar, one complete Havayah is formed. I also received this kavanah in another form: The fifteen vavs are six times Yud-Heh, totaling ninety. Thus the two letters Yud-Heh are present in each of the six extremities represented by vav. This is the mystery of the Name Yud-Heh-Vav. Together with the heh at the beginning of hadavar, etc., Havayah is completed. One must therefore connect the word hadavar to these fifteen vavs and make no interruption between them.' See there, where another kavanah is given. The practical consequence is that one must take exceptional care not to lose one of these vavs and thereby lose the kavanah. I have seen pious men of deeds count them on a finger so none will be omitted. See also our comments below to siman 66:1, laws of Emes VeYatziv, with God's help.
