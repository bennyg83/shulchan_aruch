(18) There—and they correspond to three names of Havayah, etc. And thus Sha'ar HaKavanot in derashah 8 of Keriat Shema 27, side 4, on the matter of the fifteen vavs that are in emes ve-yatziv, etc.—have kavanah in them toward the name Havayah that emerges from them in this manner, for behold they are fifteen vavs like the numerical value of Yah. If you strike Yah six times they are like the numerical value of the fifteen vavs mentioned, and with the five of the acronym of this matter—behold the Havayah is complete as one. I also received this kavanah mentioned in another manner—that behold the fifteen vavs mentioned are six times Yah, which are ninety—and behold you have two letters Yah in each vav like the numerical value of six—and this is the name Yah-Vav; and with the five of the acronym of this matter, etc.—behold Yahweh; therefore one must connect the word emes with the fifteen vavs mentioned and not interrupt between them at all—end quote. And see there that he brought another kavanah. And the practical upshot is that a person must be very careful and pay attention not to lose one of the vavs mentioned and the kavanah goes with it. And I saw pious people and men of deed who count them on the finger so they are not lacking. And further see in our words below on siman 66, seif 1, laws of emes ve-yatziv—what is written there, with Hashem's help.
