So that it not be swallowed, etc. The Shulchan Aruch listed these verses, and the same applies throughout Shema: one should be careful not to swallow the letters and not to exchange them for other letters. For example, in the verse 've'ahavta,' etc., he should not appear as one who reads 've'ahaphta levavcha nafshcha modecha,' and the like. Throughout Shema he should be careful to read slowly, each word by itself, and to pronounce the word properly from his mouth; through this all 248 limbs will be completed for him, as above in seif katan 6.
