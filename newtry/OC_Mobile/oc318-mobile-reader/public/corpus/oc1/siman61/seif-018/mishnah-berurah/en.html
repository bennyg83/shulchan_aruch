(32) That it not be swallowed, etc.—the Shulchan Aruch mentioned these verses, and likewise in all Keriat Shema one should see not to swallow letters nor replace them with other letters—for example the verse Ve'ahavta, etc., should not appear as if he reads Ve'ahavta levavekha nafshekha modekha, and the like in all Keriat Shema. One should be careful to recite slowly, each word separately, and to pronounce each word from the mouth properly; thereby all 248 limbs are completed, as above in sk"6. And see below in siman 62 in Mishna Berurah sk"1.
