(4) Radak wrote: examine the ayin of Shema and the dalet of echad—it is a sign of testimony; alternatively Shema is a notarikon: "Raise your eyes on high to whom? To the Almighty, King of the world. When? At Shacharit, Minchah, and Ma'ariv. And if you do so, you will receive upon yourself the yoke of the kingdom of heaven"—which is Shema in the future tense—end quote. And they brought Eliyah Rabbah siman 1, Shitah Mekubbetzet 95b.
