A custom. See Taz. It is possible that this resembles Sukkah 53a, since we intend to proclaim Him King over the seven heavens and ruler over them all, so the repetitions concern distinct matters. The same is found regarding Eliyahu HaNavi, of blessed memory, in I Kings 18:39. As for Taz's point from siman 623 that "Blessed is the name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever" is said repeatedly, there Hashem's Name is not mentioned.
