(5) There—one should recite Keriat Shema with kavanah, etc. They said in tractate Berakhot: whoever recites Keriat Shema without tefillin is as though he testifies false testimony against himself, for it is written therein tefillin—from here we learn similarly that whoever recites Keriat Shema without kavanah testifies falsely, for it is written therein "with all your heart." Yosef Lekach, day 1, order Mikketz, Sefer HaCharedim 68a. Yafeh Lelev siman 1.
