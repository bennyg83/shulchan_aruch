(Taz, seif katan 2) "That the chazzan." This is difficult, for what proof can be brought from the congregation to an individual? I saw in Peri Megadim that this passage of Taz should be marked on the second occurrence of "and this is the custom," which concerns a congregation. Nevertheless, according to this, Taz's statement, "and they do not say El Melech Ne'eman," is not well phrased. He should have written, "and they do not answer Amen."
