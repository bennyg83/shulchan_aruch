(Taz, s.k. 2) That the prayer leader—one should say: what proof from congregation to individual? And I saw in Peri Megadim that this statement of the Taz needs to be marked on "and so is the custom," for the second case deals with congregation; however, according to this, what did the Taz write regarding "El melekh ne'eman"—"we do not say"—and it should have written "we do not say amen," and the Bach is not before me.
