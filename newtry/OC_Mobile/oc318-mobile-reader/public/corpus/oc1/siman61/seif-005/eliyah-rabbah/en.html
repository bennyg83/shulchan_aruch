(5) In fifteen vavins, etc. In the chapter Kol Kitvei—what is amen? Rabbi Chanina said El melekh ne'eman. And I found that fifteen vavins equal ninety—it is three lameds and the form of lamed is yud vav yud—behold three times twenty-six like the count of the name of four three times; and see further in Mateh Moshe and Shelah there. And further I found in Sefer HaManhig—this is his language: I heard that on account of the matter of the vavim for pillars Israel suspected Moses our teacher, peace be upon him, and the vavim came before the Omnipresent to remove the suspicion; therefore their enactment in this, so far his language.
