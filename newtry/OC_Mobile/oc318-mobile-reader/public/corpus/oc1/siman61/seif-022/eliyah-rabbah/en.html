(22) Of vezikhartem lest it be heard shin and vesikhartem language of hire (Levush); but in Mateh Moshe he wrote there is no concern to say "and you shall hire all the mitsvot of the Omnipresent"—see there at length. And so in Tur and Abudraham he wrote the reason lest it be heard in dalet mem khaf and its explanation is from the language "and the fountains of the deep were closed"—language of false speakers. Again I found in Rokeach siman 320 that he wrote in the name of Midrash Vayechulu like Levush's words. And one may wonder on Mateh Moshe that Rokeach's words escaped him.
