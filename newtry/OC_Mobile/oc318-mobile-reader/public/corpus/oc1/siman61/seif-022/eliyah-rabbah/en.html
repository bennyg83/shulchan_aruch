Of 'u'zchartem,' so that it should not sound with a shin as 'u'schartem,' language of wages (Levush); but Mateh Moshe wrote that there is no room to err and say 'and you shall hire all the mitzvos of Hashem'; see there at length. So too the Tur and Abudraham wrote that the reason is so it should not sound like 'bedamach,' whose meaning is from the language of 'the fountains of the deep were closed,' or 'the mouth of speakers of falsehood shall be closed.' I later found in Rokeach, siman 320, that he wrote in the name of Midrash Vayechulu like the Levush. It is astonishing that Mateh Moshe missed the words of the Rokeach.
