15) There: If he recites it alone, he should concentrate, etc. According to the Yerushalmi's statement, one who recites Krias Shema alone may repeat Hashem Elokeichem. Bach and Pri Chadash agree with the Yerushalmi. I heard that Maharchu likewise writes that the Arizal followed this practice. Raavad also takes this position concerning supplications and mentioning the Holy One's praises, as cited by Shibolei HaLeket 8; Birkei Yosef 1; Kesher Gudal 11:19; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 99d; Siddur Beis Yaakov 3; Zechor LeAvraham 3, entry kuf 136; Shaarei Teshuvah 2; and Kaf HaChaim by Rav Chaim Palagi 14:49. Shaar HaKavanos, discourse 9 on the night, states: 'After completing Krias Shema, repeat Hashem Elokeichem emes once, as the shaliach tzibbur does. Through this, all 248 words are completed.' Emes LeYaakov, Kuntres Sefas Emes, question 52, page 109b, writes that the same applies to the morning Krias Shema when recited alone: it is like the bedtime Krias Shema, and the rectification is performed by us, although the principal rectification depends upon the shaliach tzibbur; see there.
