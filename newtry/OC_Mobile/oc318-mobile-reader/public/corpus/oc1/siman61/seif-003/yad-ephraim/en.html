In Shulchan Arukh seif 3 in the Rama—Maharik, etc.—one must delete and mark his own opinion. And after the word "Yisrael be'ahavah" there should be "Maharik root 42 and Agur."
(In Magen Avraham s.k. 2) But he does not say "emet" alone with the shaliach tzibbur if he already said quietly "the Omnipresent Elokeikhem emet" without the shaliach tzibbur, etc.—as should be.
