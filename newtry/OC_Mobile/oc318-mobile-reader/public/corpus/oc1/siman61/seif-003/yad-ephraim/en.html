In Shulchan Aruch, seif 3, in the Rama: The reference to Maharik, and so forth, should be deleted and replaced with the words ‘his own view.’ After the words ‘Israel with love,’ the citation should read: Maharik, responsum 42, and Agur.
In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2: “But he does not say the word emet alone together with the shaliach tzibbur if he already quietly said ‘the Omnipresent your God; true’ without the shaliach tzibbur,” etc. This is the correct reading.
