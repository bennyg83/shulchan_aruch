(s.k. 3) In fifteen, etc.—and in Ma'ariv, for there are no fifteen vavins adjacent in the blessing "emet ve'emunah."

One should intend in the vav of "emunah." Shelah wrote that one should picture as if the vav is written full and an alef in the middle—meaning vav-alef-vav; if so, the three letters in the vav can complete three Names: the first vav from alef until vav-alef-vav totals twenty-one; and join also gimel—they are vav-yud-vav; and join also dalet and then it totals ten; and join also heh and it totals fifteen; and join also vav-yud-vav and it totals twenty-one. And it is the number of the Name Ehyeh, and the alef in the vav written as an alef with yud above and yud below and vav in the middle, as the rabbi wrote in siman 36 on the form of the letters—and it totals twenty-six as the number of the Name Havayah; and the final vav, join as one joins in the first vav, it again totals twenty-one as the number of the Name Ehyeh—behold three Names twice Ehyeh and once Havayah.

"That emet alone," etc.—meaning the Name, and it alone suffices for an individual who knows how to intend in it in place of the three words that the prayer leader adds, for it is known in the letters emet that they are rosh toch sof of Elohim alef bet; and likewise the seal of the Holy One, blessed be He, as is written in it: "I am first" above to no end, and "I am last" below to no limit, and "besides Me there is no God" in the middle of the unlimited ends.

And the essence, etc.—end quote.
