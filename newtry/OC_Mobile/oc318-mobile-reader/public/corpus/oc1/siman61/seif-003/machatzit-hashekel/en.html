(Seif katan 3) With fifteen, etc. And at Maariv, for there are not fifteen consecutive vavs in the blessing ‘Emes Ve’emunah.’
He should have intent with the vav in ‘emunah.’ The Sefer Shelah wrote that this means he should picture it as though the vav were written fully, with an alef in the middle, namely vav-alef-vav. Thus the three letters in the written-out vav contain enough to complete the three Names: the first vav, by adding from alef until vav, totals 21, for alef with beis is three; add gimel and it is six; add dalet and it is ten; add hei and it is fifteen; add vav and it is 21, which is the number of the Name Ehyeh. The alef within the vav, written according to its proper form as an alef, with a yud above, a yud below, and a vav in the middle, as stated in the Beit Yosef, siman 36, regarding the forms of the letters, totals 26, corresponding to the Name Havayah. The final vav is joined as with the first vav and again totals 21, corresponding to the Name Ehyeh. Thus there are three Names: twice Ehyeh and once Havayah.
For ‘emes’ alone, etc. This is his language there: and it alone suffices for an individual who knows how to have intent in it in place of the three words that the shaliach tzibbur adds, for it is known regarding the letters of ‘emes’ that they are the beginning, middle, and end of the alphabet. So too it is the seal of the Holy One, blessed is He, of Whom it is written: ‘I am first’ above, to infinity; ‘and I am last’ below, without end; ‘and besides Me there is no god’ in the middle of the ends that are wholly without limit.
