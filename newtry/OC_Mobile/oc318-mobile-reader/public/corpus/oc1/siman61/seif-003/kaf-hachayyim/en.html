3) There: One should recite Krias Shema with concentration, etc. Yerushalmi states that the three sections instituted for Krias Shema were chosen because they contain the Ten Commandments; examine them and you will find all ten. Tosafos, end of the first chapter of Berachos 12b. Zohar Chadash, Midrash Rus 95a, likewise states that the early chassidim instituted Krias Shema corresponding to the Ten Commandments and to the number of a person's limbs; see there. Yad Aharon writes in Hagahos HaTur: 'Since the works state that one must contemplate the Commandments while reciting Krias Shema, I saw fit to copy the correspondences here. At "Hashem Elokeinu," one accepts the commandment "I am Hashem your God"; at "Hashem Echad," "You shall have no other gods"; in the verse VeAhavta, "You shall not take Hashem's Name in vain"; in UChesavtam, "You shall not covet"; in VeAsafta, "You shall not steal"; in VaAvadtem Meheirah, "You shall not murder"; in Leman Yirbu Yemeichem, "Honor your father"; in VeLo Sasuru, "You shall not commit adultery"; in Leman Tizkeru, "Remember the Shabbos day," which is equivalent to the entire Torah; and at "I am Hashem your God," "You shall not bear false witness against your fellow."' Eliyah Rabbah 1 writes likewise in Kol Bo's name, as do Moreh BeEtzba 2:23 and Chesed LaAlafim 4.
