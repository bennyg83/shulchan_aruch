(3) There—one should recite Keriat Shema with kavanah, etc. It is stated in the Yerushalmi that those three parshiyot that they enacted in Keriat Shema are because the Ten Commandments are in them—examine it and you will find everything. Tosafot beginning of Berakhot 12b. And likewise in Zohar Chadash in Midrash Ruth 95a—the early pious ones enacted Keriat Shema corresponding to the Ten Commandments and corresponding to the count of a man's limbs—see there. And Yisa Beracha wrote in Hagahot Tosefta and thus: since they wrote in books that one must have kavanah in them when reciting Keriat Shema, I saw fit to copy it here—in the parshah "the Omnipresent Elokeinu" one receives the utterance "I am the Omnipresent your the Omnipresent"; in "the Omnipresent echad" the utterance "you shall have no other gods"; in the verse "and you shall love" the utterance "you shall not bear false witness"; in "and you shall write them" the utterance "you shall not covet"; in "and you shall gather" the utterance "you shall not steal"; in "and you shall perish quickly" the utterance "you shall not murder"; in "so that your days may increase" the utterance "honor your father"; in the parshah "and you shall not turn aside" the utterance "you shall not commit adultery"; in the parshah "so that you may remember" the utterance "remember the day of Shabbat," which is equivalent to the entire Torah; in the parshah "I am the Omnipresent your the Omnipresent" the utterance "you shall not answer your fellow falsely as a witness"—end quote. And likewise Eliyah Rabbah siman 1 in the name of Kolbo. Mateh Moshe siman 2, siman 23. Knesset HaGedolah siman 4.
