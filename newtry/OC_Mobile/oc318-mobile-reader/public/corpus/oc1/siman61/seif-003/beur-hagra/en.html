Siman 3—concludes, etc. But in the Gemara it implies that the individual concludes emet and does not return, as written "he returns and says," etc.—but the prayer leader does not conclude first with emet, as written that incident when he went down, etc.—"every emet," etc.—and certainly for emet counts toward the number even though it is from the berakhah, and proof that even fifteen vavs count, and all the more so per the rabbi's explanation in the Hagahah—and there is another reason, etc.—thus emet counts toward the number and his words are primary, as written, and so too Magen Avraham in the name of Yeshuot Yaakov not like Beit Yosef whom he strains greatly.

And with this forty-one, etc. As written in Zohar Chadash siman kuf aleph, and whoever says Keriat Shema not with the congregation, etc., and even if he does not say with the prayer leader, as written in the Gemara he returns, etc., and Tur Yehudah explained when he concludes and waits for the prayer leader, and as written above siman 66, 66.

And if, etc.—specifically emet alone it is forbidden to repeat. Derekh Makhol.

And there are those who wrote, etc.—thus too in Midrash Tanchuma and in Tikkunim tikkun 10 chapter 21 part 2.

And this is in place of, etc.—as written in the fifteenth chapter of Shabbat.
