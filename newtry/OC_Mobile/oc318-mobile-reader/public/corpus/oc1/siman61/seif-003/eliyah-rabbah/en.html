(3) As new, etc. Gemara—a new proclamation, etc. Perishah explained: therefore they compared it to a proclamation—as a man who reads the king's written command when he reads with great deliberation each command and command by itself to understand its nature, so he should read Keriat Shema—each command and command, punishment and punishment mentioned in it he should set his heart on it to understand what is the command of the great King, the Holy One, blessed be He.
