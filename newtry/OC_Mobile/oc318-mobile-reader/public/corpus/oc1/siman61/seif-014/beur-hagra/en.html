Seif 14: one must, etc. See Behag. The Tur holds that the same applies for us between 'va'ed' and 've'ahavta'; but the Rokeach explained that it is between 'echad' and 'baruch,' as written in the gloss.
And there are, etc. So Tosafot there explained the above statement of Rabbi Meir, and so wrote the Rokeach.
And between, etc. Rokeach. Regarding all this they said that they did not interrupt, as written, 'in order that,' etc. The view of the Shulchan Aruch follows Rashi's explanation. Both explanations are stated in the Yerushalmi there: how would they 'bind' Shema? Rabbi Acha said in the name of Rabbi Zeira in the name of Reish Lakish: 'Shema Yisrael Hashem Elokeinu Hashem echad,' except that they did not interrupt between word and word; these are the words of Rabbi Meir. Rabbi Yehudah says they would interrupt, etc. Rabbi Yosa said in the name of Rabbi Shimon in the name of Reish Lakish: 'Shema Yisrael Hashem Elokeinu Hashem echad,' except that they did not interrupt between 'echad' and 'baruch'; these are the words of Rabbi Meir. Rabbi Yehudah says, etc.
