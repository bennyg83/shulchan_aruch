14) There, in the gloss. If an individual also wishes to say the words together with the shaliach tzibbur, there is no prohibition. This is the custom of Beis El, may it be rebuilt speedily in our days: Each person repeats Hashem Elokeichem emes for himself together with the shaliach tzibbur. They do not rely upon the shaliach tzibbur's recitation, because each person wishes personally to have the kavanah associated with these three words that is recorded in Rabbi Rashash's siddur.
