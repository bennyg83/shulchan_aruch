One must pause a little between "le'olam va'ed" and "ve'ahavta" in order to separate acceptance of malchut shamayim from the other mitzvot.And one should pause in the first verse between Yisrael and Hashem, and between Elokeinu and the second Hashem, so that it is heard as Shema Yisrael ki Hashem sheHu Elokeinu Hu Hashem echad (Rokeach). And one should pause a little between echad and barukh, for the main acceptance of malchut shamayim is the first verse (Abudarham).
