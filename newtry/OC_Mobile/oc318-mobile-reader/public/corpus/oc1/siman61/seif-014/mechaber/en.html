One must pause a little between "le'olam va'ed" and "ve'ahavta" in order to separate acceptance of malchut shamayim from the other mitzvot.And one should pause in the first verse between Yisrael and the Omnipresent, and between Elokeinu and the second the Omnipresent, so that it is heard as Shema Yisrael ki the Omnipresent sheHu Elokeinu Hu the Omnipresent echad (Rokeach). And one should pause a little between echad and barukh, for the main acceptance of malchut shamayim is the first verse (Abudarham).
