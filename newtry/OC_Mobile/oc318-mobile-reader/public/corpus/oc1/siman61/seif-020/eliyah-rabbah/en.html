With all your heart, etc. The Radak wrote in Sefer Michlol: they did not forbid pausing so as not to place a makkaf between the two lameds as it is written. Rather, even though he reads with a makkaf, he should leave space and distinction in the lamed so that it seems as though he read two lameds. For 'bechol' is vowelized with a kamatz because of the makkaf; if he reads it without a makkaf it would be vowelized with a cholam, and Chazal did not say to change the vocalization given to Moshe at Sinai; end quote.
