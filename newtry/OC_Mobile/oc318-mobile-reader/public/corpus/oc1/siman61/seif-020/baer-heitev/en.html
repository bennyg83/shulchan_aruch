With all your heart. Meaning: he should read in a manner that it is heard that they are two words; nevertheless one needs a makaf between them, for without a makaf one must read "b'chol" with cholam and with makaf with kamatz. And the reason: for a resting pause never appears after a tevir unless the musical accent came under the tevir, as explained among the grammarians; therefore with makaf the accent never comes except under the second word, and perforce the cholam changes to kamatz hatuf which is tevir kadma so that a resting pause can come after it. Nevertheless it does not depart from its decree, as Rashi wrote in the Chumash parashat Yitro verse 24 "lest he burst forth among them," end quote; and so too "et be'tzirei"—and when it comes with makaf it changes to its birth in segol from this reason.
