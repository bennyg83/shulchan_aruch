(17) One should have kavanah in the fifteen vavs, etc. See Magen Avraham note 3. And what Ma'amar Mordechai wrote note 3—that at Ma'ariv there is not so much strictness—per the words of our Rabbi the Ari, may his memory be a blessing, there is no difference between Shacharit and Ma'ariv; and even in Keriat Shema on the bed one must complete 248 words as stated in siman 15.
