(24) With all your heart, etc. Radak wrote in Sefer Mikhlol: they did not forbid interrupting only to give a makaf between the two lameds as it is; rather even if he reads with makaf he should give space and distinction in speech so it appears he read two lameds—for behold in "b'chol" it is voweled kamatz because of the makaf, and if he reads it without makaf it would be voweled cholam, and the Sages did not say to change the vowel movement they gave Moses at Sinai, so far.
