(24) [Seif 6] One must extend the chet of echad so that Hashem will reign, etc. And likewise in Zohar parashat Vayetzei 188a; and one must intend in the alef that it is one, and in the chet that it is unique in seven heavens and on earth—behold eight; and the dalet hints to four winds; and in the future they will say the entire world that He is one—Beit Yosef in the name of Semag, Etz Chayyim note 7. And although there are seven lands, nevertheless the saddle of the land is one—Yafeh Lelev note 6 in the name of the Mapah.
