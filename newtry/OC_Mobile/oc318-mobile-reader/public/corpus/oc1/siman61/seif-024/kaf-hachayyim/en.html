24) [Se'if 6] One must prolong the ches of Echad so as to proclaim the Holy One, blessed is He, King, etc. Zohar, Vayeitzei 158a, states likewise. One should contemplate through the alef that He is one; through the ches, that He is unique in the seven heavens and the earth, totaling eight; and through the dalet, the four directions and that in the future the entire world will proclaim that He is one. Beis Yosef in Semak's name; Ateres Zekeinim 7. Although there are seven lands, the foundation of the earth is one. Yafeh LaLev 6 in the commentators' name.
