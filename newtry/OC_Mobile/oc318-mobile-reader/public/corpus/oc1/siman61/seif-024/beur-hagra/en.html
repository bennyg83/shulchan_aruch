Siman 24—one needs, etc.—thus Tur Yehudah explained what is written in Pesachim there "they did not interrupt" because they did not read with cantillation, and so too Tur in the name of Raavad that thus Yerushalmi explains they did not interrupt between word and word—but it is possible to say not regarding cantillation but like cantillation to pause in the proper place to pause, as written in Yerushalmi, and he explained in the Hagahah "but," etc.
