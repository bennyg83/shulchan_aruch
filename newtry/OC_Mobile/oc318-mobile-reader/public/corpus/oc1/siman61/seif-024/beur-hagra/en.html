Seif 24: one must, etc. So Rabbeinu Yonah explained what is stated in Pesachim there, that they did not interrupt, meaning that they did not read with the cantillation. So too the Tur wrote in the name of Rav Amram that the Yerushalmi explained that they did not pause between one word and the next. Still, one can say that this does not mean specifically the cantillation, but rather something like the cantillation, to pause where it is proper to pause, as written in the Yerushalmi. This is what is meant in the gloss: 'but,' etc.
