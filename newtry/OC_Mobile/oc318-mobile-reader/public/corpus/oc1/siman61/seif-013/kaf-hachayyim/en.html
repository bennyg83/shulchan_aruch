13) There, in the gloss: In this manner everyone fulfills the obligation, since they hear it from the shaliach tzibbur, etc. This too requires the listener and speaker to intend respectively to fulfill and discharge the obligation. Since the Arizal is reported to have repeated these three words when reciting Krias Shema alone, it follows that they were instituted even for an individual. Therefore, if one is uncertain whether the chazzan knows to intend to discharge the congregation's obligation, he should repeat the three words himself and not rely upon the chazzan.
