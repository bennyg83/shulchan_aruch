{Rama: And with this every person fulfills his obligation since they hear from the mouth of the shaliach tzibbur, etc.; and in this too we require kavanah of listener and speaker. And since they wrote in the name of the Ari, may his memory be a blessing, that he was accustomed when reading alone to repeat these three words—for the individual too was enacted—therefore when one is uncertain about the cantor whether he knows and intends to fulfill or not, he should repeat these three words himself and not rely on the cantor. Knesset HaGedolah siman 3. And see below siman 15 and in the words that follow.}
