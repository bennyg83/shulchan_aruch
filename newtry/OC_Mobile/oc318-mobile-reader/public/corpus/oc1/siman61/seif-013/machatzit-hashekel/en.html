(Seif katan 13) He should place, etc. ‘Pisuchei chosam,’ only the chirik, etc. This means that the five vowel points found in the letters of ‘pisuchei chosam’ are five large vowels. Only the chirik under the peh of ‘pisuchei’ is not a large vowel unless there is a yud next to it; therefore the chirik of the peh here is a small vowel.
