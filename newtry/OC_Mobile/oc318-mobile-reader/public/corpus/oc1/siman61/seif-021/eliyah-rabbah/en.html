(21) Lest it be heard, etc. Rav Hai Gaon explained the reason: "you shall remember" in gematria is 613; the kaf remains from them—eighteen against the eighteen mentions in Keriat Shema—two remain against the two times "I am the Omnipresent your the Omnipresent." And he wrote in Mateh Moshe and it seems to me "you shall remember" (with the letters that are five) in gematria is 22, 613—to hint at 613 practical mitsvot included in the letters of Torah. And this is "in order that you remember and do"—for practical mitsvot are meant, which are 613; but the two from the mouth of Gevurah we heard are contemplative and depend on contemplation of thought, and against them He said two times "I am the Omnipresent your the Omnipresent"—and this is correct.
