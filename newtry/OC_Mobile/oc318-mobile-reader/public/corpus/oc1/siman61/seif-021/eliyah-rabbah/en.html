So that it should not sound, etc. Rav Hai Gaon explained the reason: tizkeru has the gematria of 613. Remove the letter kaf from them, eighteen remain, corresponding to the eighteen mentions of the Divine Name in Shema; two remain, corresponding to the two times 'Ani Hashem Elokeichem.' Mateh Moshe wrote: to me it appears that tizkeru, together with its five letters, has the gematria of twenty-two plus 611, alluding to the 611 practical mitzvos included in the letters of the Torah. This is the meaning of 'in order that you remember and do,' for it speaks of the practical mitzvos, which are 611; but the two that we heard from the mouth of the Almighty are intellectual and depend on contemplation. Corresponding to them it says twice, 'Ani Hashem Elokeichem.' This is correct.
