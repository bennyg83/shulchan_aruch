(34) Like one reading motam—R' Akiva Eiger brought in the name of Shelah that likewise with every aleph after mem one must pause—such as Ve'avadtem elohim acherim, eineikhem asher zonim achareihem, Eloheikhem asher, Le'Eloheikhem ani, Eloheikhem emet. Also every word whose beginning is aleph—and perhaps one does not notice it in pronunciation—needs a pause, such as Asher anokhi, matar artzekhem, Ve'atzar et, Asher Hashem, devarai eleh, Vayomer Hashem el, Daber el, mitzvot Hashem, Asher atem, and all the like. Also every word whose beginning is yud one must accentuate in pronunciation so it does not appear attached to the word before it—such as Pen yipateh lo should not appear as if he reads Penifateh or Pen ipateh; and similarly in the word yirbu, and all the like.
