After mem. But in Shelah he wrote in the name of Sefer Be'er Mayim Chayyim: in every alef after mem one must interrupt even when there is no applicable reason of "their likeness," such as "and you shall serve gods your eyes which," "stray after them gods I," and similar. And it is also fitting to be precise in the yud of "yehi" "yiftach" "yirbu" and similar that it not appear as if the beginning of the word is alef "eihei" "eiftach" "eirbu"—see there further other precisions.
