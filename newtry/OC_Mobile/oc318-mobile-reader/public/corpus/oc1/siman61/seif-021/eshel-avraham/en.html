That follows a mem. However, the Shelah writes in the name of Sefer Be’er Mayim Chayim that one must pause before every alef that follows a mem, even where the reason concerning the possible reading of ‘their death’ does not apply. Examples include the phrases ‘and you shall serve other gods,’ ‘your eyes that,’ and ‘straying after them; I am God,’ and similar cases. One should likewise articulate carefully the yod in words such as yihyeh, yifteh, yirbu, and the like, so that it not sound as though the word begins with an alef, such as iyeh, ifteh, or irbu.
