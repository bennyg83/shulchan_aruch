(21) [Seif 5] They are accustomed to place their hands on their faces when reading the first verse, etc. And thus Sha'ar HaKavanot in derashah 5 of Keriat Shema 21, side 3: before you say Shema Yisrael close your two eyes with your right hand, etc., and in your left hand hold four tzitziyot and let them rest on your heart as explained above. Also before you say Keriat Shema you must intend toward the positive commandment that we were commanded to read Keriat Shema twice each day—end quote, Pardes Sha'ar Keriat Shema ch. 6, Eliyah Rabbah note 7.
