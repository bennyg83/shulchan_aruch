With all your heart—Baer Heitev. And see Shnei Luchot HaBrit in the name of Sefer Shetei Yadot, that in order to join words with a makaf one should pause slightly on the preceding word—for example, "venatati matar artzekhem" one must pause slightly on "venatati," and then it is easier for him to read "matar artzekhem" with the makaf. I wrote this because I saw many people who did not know this and did not learn from their teachers as I received it, etc. And for the Rambam Nigrin he wrote that one should pause between af and Hashem; and the reason seems so it should not appear as if he is saying the Name is also af, etc. And I say one should not listen to those who invent pauses from their own heart; on the contrary one must be careful not to pause between af and Hashem at all when there is a makaf, etc.; and it is possible the Mechaber's intent was only to be careful to cut the word af well so it should not sound like one reading "apodni," see there; and it is possible the reason for pausing slightly is so it should not appear as if he is saying "also Hashem will be angry," meaning also Hashem will be angry on this besides another—since in any case one must pause slightly between the word "vacharah" and af so it should not sound like "vacharaf," and therefore one should not join it so much to the word Hashem; nevertheless the pause should not be so noticeable as I wrote regarding "with all your heart."
