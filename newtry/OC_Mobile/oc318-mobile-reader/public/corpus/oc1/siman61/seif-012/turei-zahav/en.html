It is a proper custom. This is because they praise Him as dwelling above the seven heavens. We also find in Scripture the declaration "Hashem, He is God" stated twice regarding Eliyahu. Beit Yosef writes this in the name of Tosafos. Regarding the practice of saying "Blessed is the name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever" three times on Yom Kippur, as stated in the laws of Yom Kippur, one must say that concern for appearing to acknowledge two authorities applies only to the verse Shema, which is the principal acceptance of the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven. Once he has accepted the yoke of Heaven's kingdom one time, there is no concern in saying "Blessed is the name" more than once, for it refers back to the verse Shema Yisrael and no one will be led into error. By contrast, "Hashem, He is God" is an independent verse, so there is a concern. Tosafos therefore needed to answer that Scripture itself records it this way regarding Eliyahu.
