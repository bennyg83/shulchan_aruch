It is a kosher custom—because they praise Him that He dwells above the seven heavens. We also find in Scripture twice "Hashem Hu HaElokim" regarding Eliyahu; so too Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot. And regarding that they say "Baruch shem" three times in the synagogue, as written in siman 5 s.k. 10—it must be that they were not concerned for two authorities except in the verse of Shema, which is the essence of accepting the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven; and since he already accepted the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven once, there is no concern in saying "Baruch shem" more than once, for it refers to the passage "Shema Yisrael," and there is no one who will come to err—unlike the verse "Hashem Hu HaElokim," which is a verse by itself; therefore there is concern, and therefore Tosafot needed to answer that we find it so in a verse regarding Eliyahu.
