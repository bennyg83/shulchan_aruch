To tilt the head, etc. I found that the tilting should be east, north, west, south, but not east, west, south, north. The Levush writes that one should lengthen the alef a little and intend that He is one, but should not lengthen it so that it sounds like 'ei chad,' meaning, G-d forbid, 'not one.' It also appears to me that there is another reason: 'ei' can mean 'woe,' as in the verse 'woe to the one who falls.' Migdal Oz wrote: I received a tradition that the alef is almost snatched, with little contemplation upon it, and one lengthens the ches one third and the dalet two thirds.
