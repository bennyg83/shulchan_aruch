(12) To tilt the head, etc. I found that the tilt should be east-north-west-south, but not east-west-south-north. Levush: extend in the alef a bit and intend that He is one, but do not extend so that one hear "ei echad"—meaning "where is one," heaven forbid, so far. It further seems to me another reason: because if its sound is the language of "oy" as written "oy to him, the one who falls." Migdal Oz wrote: I received the alef almost swallowed in it to minimize reflection on it, and they extend in the chet one-third and in the dalet two-thirds, so far.
