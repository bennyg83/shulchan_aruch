Seif 12, "Hashem, He is God," etc. For we find it twice in the verse.
Some say, etc. Their reason is from what is stated, "amen, amen": amen from this man, amen from another man, etc. This requires study, for in Nehemiah 8 it is written that they answered amen twice, and one cannot say that the two times refer to all the people answering, for in Divrei HaYamim it is written, "and all the people said amen," etc. But in the Yerushalmi at the end of Megillah it is explicit that one should not answer amen twice, as it says there: similarly, "amen, amen" and "Shema, Shema." This requires study; see Magen Avraham.
