Seif 12: Hashem is Elokim, etc. For we find this twice in the verse.
Some say, etc. Their reason is from what is stated, 'amen, amen,' meaning amen from this man and amen from another man, etc. This requires study, for in Nechemiah 8 it is written that they answered amen twice; and one cannot say that twice refers to all the people who answered, for in Divrei Hayamim it is written, 'and all the people said amen,' etc. But in the Yerushalmi at the end of Megillah it is explicit not to answer amen twice, as it says there, and similarly amen amen, Shema Shema. This requires study; see Magen Avraham.
