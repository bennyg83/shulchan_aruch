Siman 12—Hashem hu haElokim, etc.—for behold we find the verse with two [times]:

There are those who say, etc. His reason from what is written amen amen—amen from this man, amen from another man, etc.—and it is difficult, for behold in Nechemiah 8 it is written that they answered amen twice—and it is possible to say that two [times] refers to all the people who answered, for behold it is written in his words "and all the people said amen," etc.—but in Yerushalmi end of Megillah it states explicitly not to answer amen twice as written there, and similarly amen amen shema shema—and it is difficult, and see Magen Avraham.
