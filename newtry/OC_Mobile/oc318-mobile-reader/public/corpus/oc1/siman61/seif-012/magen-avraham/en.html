Amen twice. The Beit Yosef disagrees and wrote that it is permitted, for the verse says ‘amen ve'amen,’ and one should not distinguish between saying it with a vav or without a vav. So too wrote Bachya in Parashas Beshalach: one who has intent and repeats ‘amen, amen’ merits two worlds, and so David said at the end of Tehillim 41; end quote.
