Twice amen. And Beit Yosef disagrees and wrote that it is permitted, for it is written in Scripture "amen and amen"—and one need not distinguish between saying with a vav or without a vav; and so too Bahya in parashat Beshalach—and one who intends and doubles "amen amen" merits two worlds; and so David said at the end of Psalm 41—end quote.
