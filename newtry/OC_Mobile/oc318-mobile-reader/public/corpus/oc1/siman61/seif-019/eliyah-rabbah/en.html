(19) Barukh shem kevod malkhuto, etc. Shelah and Bach wrote that if they did not say it at all they do not return him, so far, and their reason is it is only an enactment since Yaakov said it. But Magen Avraham wrote on this and this is his language: Levush wrote that even if he said it and did not say it with kavanah he must return, so far—and see below siman 63 s.k. 9.
