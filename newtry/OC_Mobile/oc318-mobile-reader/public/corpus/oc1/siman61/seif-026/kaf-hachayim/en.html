(26) There—and one extends the dalet of echad, etc. Meaning: when he emphasizes the dalet he extends the breath of the spirit he expels from his mouth the measure he thinks in his heart, etc.—and not like those who extend the dalet as the vowel tzeirei under the dalet and ruin the kavanah; and thus I received from my teacher the pious great rabbi Maharash, may his memory be a blessing, of Lublin.
