And he should not move the quiescent, etc. Meaning, for example, the second beis in 'levavecha,' which has a sheva and whose proper rule is to be snatched, so that it is quiescent and is called a sheva nach. If he reads it as though it were vowelized like a tzeirei, then it is moved, and this is called a sheva na or a moving sheva. Regarding this he said that one should not move the quiescent, etc. So I found written; see Magen Avraham.
