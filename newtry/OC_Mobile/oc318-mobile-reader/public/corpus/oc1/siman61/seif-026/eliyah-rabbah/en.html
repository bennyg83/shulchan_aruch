(26) And he should not move the resting shva, etc.—meaning such as the second [bet] in "b'levavkha" which is voweled segol and its law is to be chataf-patached, and then it is resting and then called segol resting; and if he reads it as if voweled like tzerei—then it is moving and this is called shva na or segol moving; and on this it said he should not move the resting shva, etc.—thus I found written, and see Magen Avraham.
