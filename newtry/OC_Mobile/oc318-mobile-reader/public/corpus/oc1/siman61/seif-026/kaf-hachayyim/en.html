26) There: He should prolong the dalet of Echad, etc. This means that when articulating the dalet, he should prolong the breath issuing from his mouth for the time needed to contemplate in his heart, etc. He should not act like those who prolong the dalet by pronouncing it with a tzeirei beneath it, thereby ruining the meaning. This is the tradition I received from my teacher, the great chassid Rav Shmuel of Lublin. Bach. Lechem Chamudos, chapter Hayah Korei 8; Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur 3; Olas Tamid 3; Eliyah Rabbah 11; and Pri Chadash 6 write likewise. See also below, paragraph 31.
