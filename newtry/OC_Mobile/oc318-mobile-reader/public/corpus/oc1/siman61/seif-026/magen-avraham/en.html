Aloud. In Responsa Maharam Alashkar, siman 6, he gave a reason in the name of Rav Hai and the Rashba not to read it aloud, lest one rely on hearing and not read it himself; see there. In Kenesses HaGedolah he wrote in the name of the Ra'em, part 1, siman 41, that one who hears and did not read fulfills the obligation; and see Sefer Toras David in the name of the Raavad, end quote. See what I wrote in siman 59, seif 4.
