(40) There are customs, etc.—regarding whether one fulfills Keriat Shema through another who intends to discharge him—see Magen Avraham and Pri Megadim; most acharonim hold one fulfills, and this is preferable to hirhur, for hirhur is not like speech, unlike this where the listener is like the reciter. Eshel Avraham wrote and it appears specifically when he understands the language; and even in a foreign language we require specifically that the listener understand. See siman 193 seif 1. And in Knesset HaGedolah he brought in the name of Berakhat Avraham that specifically an individual discharging an individual; but an individual discharging the many, or an individual discharging the two in birkat hamazon—even if they do not understand lashon hakodesh—they fulfill.
