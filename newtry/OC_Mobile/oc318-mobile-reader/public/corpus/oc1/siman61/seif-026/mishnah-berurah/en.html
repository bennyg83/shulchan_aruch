There are those who have the custom, etc. Regarding whether one fulfills Shema through another, when the other intends to discharge him, see Magen Avraham and Peri Megadim. Most later authorities hold that he does fulfill it this way, and this is better than thought alone, for thought is not like speech, whereas here hearing is like answering. The Olas Tamid wrote that this appears to apply specifically when he understands the language; even in Lashon Hakodesh, the listener must understand. See siman 193, seif 1. The Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah brought in the name of Birkat Avraham that this applies specifically where one individual discharges another individual; but when one individual discharges the public, or one individual discharges two others in Birkas Hamazon, even if they do not understand Lashon Hakodesh, they fulfill their obligation.
