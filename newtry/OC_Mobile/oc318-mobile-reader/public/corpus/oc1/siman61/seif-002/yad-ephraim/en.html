(In Taz s.k. 2) "And so is the custom"—one must delete the notation from here and mark on what the Rama wrote, that in a congregation one should not say "El melekh ne'eman," etc.; and "and so is the custom" on this—the notation applies here. And what he wrote in the name of Bach—that it has already been abolished, etc.—meaning the custom that the Rokeach wrote, that in yeshiva they say "amen, El melekh ne'eman," etc.; and now, etc.; and see there in Bach.
