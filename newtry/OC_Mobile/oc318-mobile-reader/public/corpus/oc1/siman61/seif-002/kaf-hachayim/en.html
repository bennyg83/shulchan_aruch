(2) And one should have kavanah that in the first parshah from "and you shall love" until its end—apart from the verse Shema Yisrael, etc., and barukh shem, etc.—there are 42 words corresponding to the 42-letter name Ana be-khoach, etc.; and in the second parshah from "and it shall be if" until "and you shall bind them"—also included—there are 72 words corresponding to the 72-letter name of Vayisa Vayavo Vayet; and from "these words" until the end of the parshah there are fifty words corresponding to the fifty gates of Binah; and in the parshah of tzitzit there are also 72 words with the three words that the shaliach tzibbur repeats—and they too correspond to the 72 words in the verses Vayisa Vayavo Vayet, as written in Pardes there and Sha'ar HaKavanot there. Only the parshah "and it shall be if" is straight and reversed and straight, and the parshah of tzitzit—all straight—as written in the siddur of Rashash, may the memory of the righteous be a blessing; and see Zohar parashat Beshalach 52a and Sha'ar Ma'amarei Razal 14a at its end and in Chadash 10, beginning of 73—see there. And in the siddur of Rashash they are arranged—namely in the first parshah that has 42 words, above each word is one letter of the acronym Avag, etc.; and in the second parshah above each word is one letter from the 72 names of Vayisa Vayavo Vayet until "and you shall bind them"; and from there onward on each word is one letter from the name Eheyeh simple until completion of forty letters that are in Yud Eheyeh; and in the last words of Yud on each word is the name Eheyeh simple; and then in the parshah of tzitzit on each word one letter from the 72 names of Vayisa, etc.—and see below siman 16. And with the verse Shema Yisrael and barukh shem, etc., they are 248 words corresponding to the 248 limbs of man, as written in Zohar Chadash parashat Acharei Mot 59a; and he wrote further there in Zohar Chadash and thus: whoever recites Keriat Shema as fitting—each word and word influences each limb of his. And if a person does not recite Keriat Shema by day and by night, each limb of his is filled from an evil spirit and from every evil and harmful thing of the world—end quote. Therefore one must be careful in Keriat Shema on one's bed to say it before sleep comes so he can say it properly. And it will be explained in its place, with the Omnipresent's help.
