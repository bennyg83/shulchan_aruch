2) One should bear in mind that the first section, from VeAhavta through its end, excluding the verses Shema Yisrael, etc., and Baruch Shem, etc., contains forty-two words, corresponding to the forty-two-letter Name of Ana BeKoach, etc. The second section, from Vehayah Im, etc., through Vesamtem inclusive, contains seventy-two words, corresponding to the seventy-two-letter Name derived from Vayisa, Vayavo, and Vayet. From Es Devarai Eileh, etc., to the end of the section are fifty words, corresponding to the fifty gates of Binah. The section of tzitzis also contains seventy-two words when combined with the three repeated by the shaliach tzibbur. These correspond to the seventy-two words in the verses Vayisa, Vayavo, and Vayet, as Pri Etz Chaim and Shaar HaKavanos there explain. The Names in Vehayah appear in straight, reverse, and straight order, while those in the tzitzis section are all in straight order, as arranged in Rabbi Rashash's siddur. See Zohar, Beshalach 52a; Shaar Maamarei Razal, page 14a at the end; and Zohar Chadash, page 10, beginning of column 3. Rabbi Rashash's siddur sets them out as follows: In the first section, which contains forty-two words, one letter of the initials of Ana BeKoach - alef, beis, gimel, etc. - appears above each word. In the second section, one of the seventy-two Names derived from Vayisa, Vayavo, and Vayet appears above each word through Vesamtem. From there onward, one letter of the simple Name Ehyeh appears over each word until completing the forty letters contained in ten instances of Ehyeh; above each of the final ten words appears one complete simple Name Ehyeh. In the tzitzis section, one of the seventy-two Names derived from Vayisa, etc., appears above each word. See below, paragraph 16. Together with Shema Yisrael and Baruch Shem, etc., there are 248 words, corresponding to a person's 248 limbs, as Zohar Chadash, Acharei Mos 59a, states. It further says: 'Every person who recites Krias Shema properly causes each word to bestow influence upon its corresponding limb. If a person does not recite Krias Shema by day and by night, each of his limbs becomes filled with an evil spirit and with all the evil and harmful forces in the world.' One should therefore take care to recite the bedtime Krias Shema before becoming drowsy, so that he can say it properly. This will be explained in its place, with God's help.
