On his bed. This implies specifically on his bed, in order to sink into sleep through Shema, for protection; but elsewhere it is forbidden to read even the whole parsha twice. Even on his bed it is permitted only if he reads the parsha, not the first verse alone. This is from the Gra. It appears to me that all this applies specifically if he reads the parsha of Shema alone; but if he also reads the other parshiyos, there is no concern.
