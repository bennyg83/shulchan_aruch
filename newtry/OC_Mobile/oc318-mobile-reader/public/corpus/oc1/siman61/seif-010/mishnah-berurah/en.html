(25) On his bed—it implies specifically on his bed, in order to settle into sleep from within Keriat Shema because of guarding; but elsewhere it is forbidden to read even the entire paragraph twice, and on his bed too it is not permitted unless he reads the paragraph and not only the first verse alone (Gra). And it seems that all this is specifically if he reads only the paragraph of Shema; but if he also reads the other paragraphs, there is no concern in the matter.
