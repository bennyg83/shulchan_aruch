Seif 10: when he reads, etc. Yerushalmi, first chapter: Rabbi Shimon bar Nachmani, when he would go down to intercalate the year, would stay by Rabbi Yaakov Grusa, and Rabbi Zeira would hide among the cupboards to hear how he read Shema. He heard that he would read and repeat it until he sank into sleep. The Semak answered that this is because he repeated the whole parsha. But the Behag wrote that this applies specifically when reciting it on one's bed, and so wrote the Hagahos Maimoniyos. This is what is meant by 'when he reads, etc., the whole parsha, etc.'
