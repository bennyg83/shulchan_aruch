Siman 10—when one reads on his bed, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 1: Rabbi Shimon bar Nachmani—when he would go down to immerse, she would receive him at Rabbi Yaakov the Cook, and Rabbi Zeira would hide between the beams; we learned from her how he would read Keriat Shema and hear that he would read and return and read until he sank from him into his sleep—and Tosafot in Semak on account of repeating the whole passage; but in Beit HaGedolah he wrote specifically on his bed, and so too Hagahot Maimoniyot, and he explained "when one reads, etc., the whole passage," etc.
