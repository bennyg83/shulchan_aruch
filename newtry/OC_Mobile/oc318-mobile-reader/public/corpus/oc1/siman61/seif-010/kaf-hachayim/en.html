(10) [Seif 3] In Keriat Shema there are 245 words; and in order to complete 248 corresponding to a man's limbs, the shaliach tzibbur concludes "the Lord your God" emes and returns and says, etc. And one who is not accustomed thus—it is not fitting to compel him to repeat—R' Eliyahu Mizrachi vol. 1 siman 1, Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef, Magen Avraham note 1. However, in Zohar Chadash in Midrash Ruth 95a he was very strict about this, for he wrote and thus: whoever says Keriat Shema not with the congregation does not complete his limbs because he lacks the three words that the shaliach tzibbur repeats—what is his remedy? He should have kavanah in the fifteen vavs of emes ve-yatziv. And with all this, one who reads upon him "distorted father" cannot repair it, etc.—end quote. And likewise Beit Yosef in the name of Midrash HaNe'elam—see there. And likewise in Sha'ar HaKavanot derashah 8 of Keriat Shema that one must repeat these three words "the Lord your God" emes, and he gave a reason in secret—see there. And likewise in Pardes, Sha'ar Keriat Shema ch. 25.
