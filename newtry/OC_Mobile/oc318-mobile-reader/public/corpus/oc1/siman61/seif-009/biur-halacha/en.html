Between whether one doubles verses, etc.—see in Mishnah Berurah what he wrote, and bedi'eved in doubling a verse, etc., that it is not needed per Pri Chadash that doubling a verse is only disgraceful—certainly the first reading is not uprooted thereby; but even per Rashi who silences him, it is also reasonable that he fulfilled—for on the first statement there was no prohibition and the second statement is not an interruption in Keriat Shema, for he did not pause in this long enough to complete it all—and this is similar to what the Gemara challenges there "perhaps originally he did not intend and later intended," etc., and behold there too afterward he said another word and did not intend—nevertheless it is not called an interruption in this and he fulfills with what he said once; and likewise here he fulfills with what he said once. And regarding doubling words there is to examine—for per Rashi who explained in the Gemara that therefore in this it is only disgraceful and we do not silence him because his reading is considered only as mockery—it is possible that for this reason he did not fulfill; but per Pri Chadash who explained that in this we silence him, it implies Rashi's reasoning does not apply—it seems he fulfills in this. And proof from what the Gemara challenges there "perhaps originally he did not intend and at the end intended"—and per Pri Chadash the difficulty refers to doubling the word in which we silence him—thus it is not considered an interruption what he interrupts between word and fellow word improperly in one word—and likewise in our matter.
