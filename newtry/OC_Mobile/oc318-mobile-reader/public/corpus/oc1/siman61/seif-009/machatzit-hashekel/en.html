(s.k. 9) That not, etc.—and Bach wrote, etc., and the Gemara in Sukkah 53b chapter 5 escaped him.

That they would say, etc.—on the festival of Sukkot at the joy of the Water-Drawing, when they would go to draw water for the water libation.

There—all would say, etc.—and it is possible "all" is not precise, for the joy of the Water-Drawing was done by chasidim and men of deed and great Torah scholars, and the rest of the people and women would come to see and hear as we learned there; and it implies somewhat that also saying "we beseech You" etc. was said specifically by the chasidim and men of deed; nevertheless, in any case, since the utterance was from the mouth of a majority gathering of chasidim, therefore per the Bach it would have been proper to permit.

Nevertheless, even if the entire congregation says it, etc.—and one must distinguish between this case here and that which we say in tractate Rosh Hashana 24b that in a multitude there is no suspicion, and they are permitted to equate types of forms that in their staying there is concern of suspicion of prohibition; and the Rama brought this in Yoreh De'ah siman 141, seif 4, in the gloss.
