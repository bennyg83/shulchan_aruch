(Magen Avraham, seif katan 16) The parenthetical words, "and Kenesses HaGedolah wrote," mean that Kenesses HaGedolah rejected Rashba's rationale, since in fact he has fulfilled his obligation. Magen Avraham therefore concludes, "See what I wrote in siman 59, seif 4, seif katan 5," that one fulfills the obligation only with ten, and there is concern that he may rely upon hearing it even when ten are not present. Siman 62, Shulchan Aruch, seif 3.
