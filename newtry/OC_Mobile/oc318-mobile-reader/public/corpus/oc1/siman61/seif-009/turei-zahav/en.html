It is forbidden to say "Shema, Shema," etc. Sefer Chassidim, siman 18, writes that if one did not recite the first verse with intent, he should recite it again quietly, but not aloud, because that appears, Heaven forbid, as an acknowledgment of two authorities. This is difficult to me, for the Gemara states that Rav Pappa said to Abaye: Perhaps initially he did not direct his mind. Abaye answered: Is there comradeship toward Heaven? If he does not direct his mind, we strike him with a blacksmith's hammer until he does direct it. If it is permitted to repeat the verse quietly, why did Abaye not answer that he should then repeat it quietly? Rather, it is certainly forbidden to repeat it in every manner. One may answer that Rav Pappa's main question was that since it is possible to judge him favorably, why do they rebuke him? Abaye answered that he cannot be judged favorably in this way. See what I wrote in siman 63, seif 4.
