It is forbidden to say Shema, etc. In Sefer Chasidim siman 18 he wrote: if he did not read the first verse with kavanah he should return and read it quietly, but not in a loud voice, lest it appear as two authorities, heaven forbid—and it is simple regarding this, for we say in the Gemara: Rabbi Pappa said to Abbaye, "Perhaps initially he did not intend?" He said to him: "Regarding friendship toward Heaven—if he does not intend we strike him with a bellows until he intends." And if it were permitted to return and read quietly, why did Abbaye not tell him that in that case he should have returned and read quietly? Rather certainly in every manner it is forbidden to double. And one may say that Rabbi Pappa's main difficulty was: since it is possible to judge him favorably, why do they rebuke him? And one should tell him that one may not judge him favorably in this—and see above in siman 63 s.k. 4.
