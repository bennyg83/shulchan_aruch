(9) In the heavens and earth, etc. The language of Semak: he should think that He is unique in seven heavens and one earth—behold eight, so far. And Rabbi David Abudraham wrote: in the three letters of echad are included three worlds—he should intend in alef for the Holy One, blessed be He, Who is first; in chet for eight spheres; in dalet for four elements on which this world is founded, so far. Etz Chayyim wrote: when he says "the Omnipresent Elokeinu the Omnipresent echad" he should intend on the four letters of the Tetragrammaton; when he says "Barukh shem kevod malkhuto" he should intend on the four letters of Adonai; when he says "le'olam va'ed" he should intend on the name Yahadonahi.
