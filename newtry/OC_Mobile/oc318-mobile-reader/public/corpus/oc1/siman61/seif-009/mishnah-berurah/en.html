(22) Shema twice—because it appears as if he accepts upon himself two authorities, heaven forbid. If he did not intend from the outset, he should return and recite quietly; and if there are no listeners, even aloud is permitted. But if he intended from the outset, even quietly is forbidden. In Selichot it is permitted to say Shema every time he says "vaya'avor," for since he pauses much in between it does not appear as two authorities. And see Taz in siman 63 seif 3 who also wrote that if he waits some time between the first reading and the second it is permitted, for they do not silence him unless he reads twice consecutively, and even that is not disgraceful.

(23) "Between if one repeats"—and bedieved with repeating the verse one fulfills, and with repeating the words it is uncertain (tzarikh iyun); see Biur Halakha.

(24) "The first verse"—It is in Beit Yosef that from the first verse onward there is no concern, and from the wording of the Rif it does not seem so; see there. And see in Magen Giborim that the Meiri is also strict about this, but regarding on his bed one certainly should not be strict about this.
