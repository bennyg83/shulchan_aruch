In the dalet. Baal HaTov in Sha'ar HaTziyun, and in the name of Sefer Shetei Yadot he expanded on this that one should not read it with dagesh, for it sounds like "dai" as in "dai machsoro" (enough for his lack), but rather softened, etc. And with dagesh it is heard "achad az esmach" like "vayichad Yitro," and he does not fulfill the mitzvah of yichud Hashem. And likewise what he wrote that one should not extend the alef, for it is in segol which is a small vowel; and one should not snatch the chet which is in kamatz—he should not rush in its reading, which is tipcha, on Shulchan Arukh.
