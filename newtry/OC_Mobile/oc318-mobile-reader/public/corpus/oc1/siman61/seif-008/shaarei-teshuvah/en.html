“With the dalet.” See Baer Heitev and Shalmei Tzibur. Sefer Shtei Yadot discusses this at length and writes that the dalet should not be pronounced with a dagesh, because that would make it sound like dai, meaning “enough for what it lacks”; rather, it should be soft, etc. With a dagesh, echad would sound like echadi in the phrase “then I will rejoice,” like “and Yitro rejoiced,” and he would not fulfill the mitzvah of declaring Hashem’s unity. Regarding the instruction not to lengthen the alef: it has a segol, which is a short vowel. He should also not swallow the chet, which has a kamatz, by reading it too quickly; see there.
