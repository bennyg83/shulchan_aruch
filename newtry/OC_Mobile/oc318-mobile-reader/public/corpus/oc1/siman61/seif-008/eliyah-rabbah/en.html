One must, etc. The Levush writes: one must pause between every word. This is from the Tur and Perishah. It appears that this means that one should not say the word hurriedly, but slowly, as one counts coins. The requirement to leave space between adjoining words is a different matter: one should not say 'al levavecha' in one breath, but 'al' in one breath and 'levavecha' in its own breath; this is the space he leaves between breaths. But the other words may be said in one breath, provided he pauses between each word. See what I wrote above, siman 5.
