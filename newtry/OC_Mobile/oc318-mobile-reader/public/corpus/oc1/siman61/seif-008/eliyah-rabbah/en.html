(8) He must, etc. In Levush: and one must interrupt between every word and word—and this is from Tur and Perishah, and it seems he meant to say he should not say the word hurriedly but with deliberation, deliberation as if counting coins; and that he must give space between the attached words is another matter—and that he should not say "al levavekha" in one breath, but "al" in a breath by itself and "levavekha" in a breath by itself, and this is the space he gives between breath and breath; but other words he can say in one breath only that he pauses between word and word, so far—and see what I wrote above siman 5.
