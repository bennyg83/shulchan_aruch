(16) On his bed, etc. And he was precise: on his bed is permitted as Levush wrote—the reason is only on account of guarding; but in Keriat Shema of Shacharit and Arvit one should not repeat the whole parashah with the first verse. Also in Keriat Shema on his bed he wrote there are those who say one should be cautious even in this. And it implies he holds it is fitting to be cautious as his words, and so too in Beit Yosef one should be concerned for the words of Mahari Abuhav, and some say these are the words of Mahari Abuhav. And from this it will be explained for the inquirer that all the wonderings Shiyurei Kenesset HaGedolah left on Shulhan Arukh in this are for naught, for in what I explained everything is resolved—see there that I shortened. And it implies only the first verse—even on his bed—is forbidden for everyone.
