On his bed, etc. He was precise that on his bed it is permitted, as the Levush wrote, because the reason is only protection; but in the morning and evening Shema one should not repeat the entire parsha together with the first verse. Also regarding the Shema on his bed he wrote that some say one should be careful even about this. It implies that he holds it proper to be careful in accordance with his words, and so it also appears from the Beit Yosef, that one should be concerned for the words of Mahari Abuhav. These 'some say' are the words of Mahari Abuhav. From this, one who studies it will see that all the questions of the Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah on the Shulchan Aruch here are for nothing, for according to what I explained everything is resolved; see there, for I have been brief. It also implies that the first verse alone is forbidden even on his bed according to all opinions.
