(25) Such as velemadtem, etc. But in Shelah in the name of Sefer Be'er Mayim Chayyim he wrote: in every alef after mem one must interrupt even when there is no applicable reason of "their likeness," such as "and you shall serve gods—your eyes which stray after them to your gods I," "your gods which," "to gods I," "your gods truth." And further he answered every word that begins with alef—perhaps one will not feel it in pronunciation—requires interruption, such as "which I," "My mitsvot which I," "the the Omnipresent your the Omnipresent," "rain of your land," "and restrain you," "which the Omnipresent," "words of these," "and the Omnipresent said to," "speak to," "mitsvot of the Omnipresent," "which you are the Omnipresent your the Omnipresent." And likewise in "on the way" and "in your lying down" one must interrupt between "your souls" and "and you shall bind" between these matters, lest it appear as saying these matters from gods; and likewise "and you shall write on." And it is fitting to be precise in the yud of "yehi" "yiftach" "yirbu" and similar that it not appear as if the beginning of the word is alef "eihei" "eiftach" "eirbu"—see there further other precisions.
