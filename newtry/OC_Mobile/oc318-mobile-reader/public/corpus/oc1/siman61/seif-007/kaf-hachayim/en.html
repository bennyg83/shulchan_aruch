(7) There—trembling and sweating. Much sweating is fitting that it should come upon a person when he considers what he will do when the Omnipresent rises and when He calls to account his ways—which many times passed over him that he did not fulfill the law mentioned in Shulchan Aruch to recite Keriat Shema with awe, fear, trembling, and sweating—and the living one will give his heart to remember these things that came from the mouth of our holy master, may his memory be a blessing, always whenever he recites Keriat Shema and shall not pass, for habit becomes nature; and things in which a person is habituated require much caution to fulfill them properly. And may the Omnipresent grant me always to do His will and to serve Him with a whole heart—I and my offspring and my offspring's offspring forever.
