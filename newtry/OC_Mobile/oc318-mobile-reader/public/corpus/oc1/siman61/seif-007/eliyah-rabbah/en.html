To place their hands, etc. So it is written in the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, siman 61, and it somewhat implies that he places both hands over his eyes. But the wording of the Shulchan Aruch of Mahari Luria, of blessed memory, is: he should close his eyes with his right hand, and with his left hand hold the four tzitzis. Perhaps the Shulchan Aruch and the Levush are speaking about the evening Shema, when the tzitzis are not taken in hand, though this is difficult. It is also possible that the expression 'their hands' is merely common language and refers to one hand; so it also appears from the Maharil, laws of prayer: he placed his hands on his eyes until he finished the intent of echad. See Ma'adanei Melech there regarding signaling by opening the eyes.
