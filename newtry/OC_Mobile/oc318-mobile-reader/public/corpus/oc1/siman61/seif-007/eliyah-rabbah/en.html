(7) To place their hands, etc. And so he wrote in Tur and Shulhan Arukh siman 61, and it implies a bit that he places two hands on his eyes; but the language of Shulhan Arukh—Mahari Luria, may his memory be blessed—he closes his eyes with his right hand and with his left hand holds four tzitzit, so far. And perhaps Shulhan Arukh and Levush in Keriat Shema of Arvit deal with that one does not take the tzitzit in hand—and it is strained; also possible the language "their hands" in general is said and deals with one hand, and so it implies in Maharil Laws of Tefillah—this is his language: he places his hands on his eyes until he finishes the kavanah of echad, so far. And see in Ma'adanei Melekh there on the law of hinting of opening his eyes.
