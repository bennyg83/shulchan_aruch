He should emphasize. I found in the name of Moreinu Yosef Kalmenkes that the intent is not to read it with a dagesh, for it has no dagesh; rather, the intent is that he should articulate it well with his mouth. The Rav Beit Yosef also wrote that one should not emphasize it too much. In the Shelah he protested against those who emphasize it too much, so that it appears as though the dalet is vowelized with a sheva.
