One emphasizes. I found in the name of Maharam Yosef Kalmenkes that the intention is not to read it with dagesh, for there is no dagesh in it; rather the intention is to taste it nicely in the mouth. And also Rabbeynu Yonah wrote one should not emphasize it overly. In Shelah he called to quarrel against those who emphasize overly—it appears as though the four points are in shuruk.
