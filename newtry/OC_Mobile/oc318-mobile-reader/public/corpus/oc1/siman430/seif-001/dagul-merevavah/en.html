If because of this it is not satisfactory — for this matter is not agreed that flour is called liquid in liquid; and see Shach Yoreh Deah siman 109 s.k. 3; and on the contrary from Rashal's own words that Shach brought there s.k. 10 it implies he holds flour in flour is called dry in dry. But even so it is difficult: if so one in two is nullified since there is no significant matter here — why must one eat from it before Pesach; and one cannot say he fears lest some are stringent whom Rama brought in siman 109 — for this stringency applies only to one who eats the mixture dry, but this flour one will make matzot from and then it is nullified in sixty, and nullifying l'chatchila is not applicable here as explained there siman 109 seif 2. And it appears to me Rashal feared lest chametz is called a davar sheyeish lo matirin, and see above siman 447 in Magen Avraham s.k. 6; and he is inclined like the Mechaber's view in Yoreh Deah siman 110 seif 7 that even in davar sheyeish lo matirin it helps that one of them was eaten to permit the remaining mixture — examine well; and see in Yoreh Deah there in Shach s.k. 57.
