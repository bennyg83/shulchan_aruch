(1) [Seif 1] The Shabbat before Pesach they call Shabbat HaGadol, etc. — and the reason: because a great miracle was done on it — the Egyptian Pesach they took on the tenth, as it is written "on the tenth of this month," etc.; and the Pesach when Israel left Egypt was on the fifth day, as is found in Seder Olam (Taz: in Seder Olam it is found that on erev [Pesach] they left; but it must be as is found in Shabbat 87b: R. Oshaya, etc.); and it turns out the tenth of the month was Shabbat; and each one took a lamb for his Pesach and tied it at the legs of his bed; and the Egyptians asked why this is for you, and they answered to slaughter for the sake of Pesach by God's command upon us; and their teeth were blunt (and although Israel would slaughter rams and sheep and eat every day — here they informed them that with this their god above too would be slaughtered — Bach) for slaughtering their gods, and they were not permitted to say anything to them; and for that miracle it is called Shabbat HaGadol — Tur. And although according to this reason it should be called so for all the days from the tenth of the month until erev Pesach "great days" — one may say the essence of the miracle was at the beginning, but after the first day passed they were already accustomed — Beit Yosef. And the Rav R' Moshe Eliyahu wrote the reason of the miracle: that they say they wish to slaughter to their gods, and on Shabbat the Egyptian said it is true, and what they did not slaughter because it is Shabbat, not so on other days — end. And they brought it in Avodat HaTorah s.k. 1. And what they attributed the miracle to Shabbat and not to the tenth day of the month — the reason is because on the tenth of the month they ascended from the Jordan; and if they attributed the miracle to the tenth day and called the tenth day "the great day," they would err to say that because of the miracle of ascending from the Jordan it is called great; therefore they attributed the matter to Shabbat, for it was known their ascent from the Jordan was not on Shabbat — Bach and Taz s.k. 1.

(1) And further — the reason they call it Shabbat HaGadol: because a great miracle was done on it, as we say in Midrash Rabbah Parashat Bo: when they took their Pesachim on that Shabbat the firstborn of the nations of the world gathered by Israel and asked them why they do so; they told them: it is a Pesach sacrifice to God who will kill the firstborn of Egypt; they went to their fathers and to Pharaoh to request that Israel be sent, and they did not want; and the firstborn made war and killed many of them — this is what is written "who struck Egypt through their firstborn." Tosafot Shabbat 87b; and they brought Beit Yosef and Levush. And in Kol Bo siman 47 he wrote the reason: when the Egyptians saw thus, each girded his sword on his thigh and sought to destroy, to kill and annihilate all the Jews; and God in His mercy protected them and afflicted Egypt with strange illnesses and they were judged with great suffering and could not harm Israel; and because great miracles were done for Israel on that Shabbat that was before Pesach, every Shabbat before Pesach is called Shabbat HaGadol — end. And see above siman 429 or 3 and in the later authorities further reasons.
