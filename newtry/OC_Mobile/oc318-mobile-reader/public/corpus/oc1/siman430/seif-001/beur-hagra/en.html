Seif 1 — Shabbat, etc. Shabbat 87b; and that day was Thursday, etc.; and see Tosafot there s.v. ve'oto, etc.; and similarly in Midrash Shir HaShirim on the psalm 136; and in Yalkut beginning of Parashat Bo; and in Pesikta there; and Gemara Kol Bo wrote in Even HaEzer.

And the custom — perhaps since then the redemption began through its taking; but it is not a matter [of law], for it is explicit in Mechilta end of Parashat Bo: I would hear from Rosh Chodesh — it teaches "on that day"; if "on that day," perhaps while it is still day — it teaches "because of this" at the time there is matzah and maror placed before you, etc.
