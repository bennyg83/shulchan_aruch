Because of the miracle. Many challenge — if so it should attribute the greatness to the tenth of the month even on a weekday. And I heard from the mouth of the Rav R' Moshe Charif z"l that on that tenth of the month another miracle was done — the Jordan split; and if so they thought the ascent was because of the Jordan miracle; therefore they called it Shabbat HaGadol, for the day of their ascent from the Jordan was not on Shabbat. And I told this before Maharach z"l and he praised it.
