When Rosh Chodesh Nisan falls on Shabbat, then it is the portion HaChodesh, and they say the piyyutim belonging to that portion except for the form "Abir HaGevirot"; and they read as haftarah the portion HaChodesh and not "HaShamayim kisi" — for always they read from what the reading concluded (Maharar"m in Minhagim). And see Taz the reason why they call it Shabbat HaGadol (the Rav relied on Taz for his words).
