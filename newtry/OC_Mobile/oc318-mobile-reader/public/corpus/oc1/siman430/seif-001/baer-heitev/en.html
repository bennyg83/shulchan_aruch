The miracle — that each one took a lamb on the tenth of the month; and that year the tenth of the month was on Shabbat; and they challenged: it should have been established as Yom Tov on the tenth of the month even if it falls on a weekday, like other festivals — see the later authorities and Kaf HaChayim. And what Beit Yosef challenged — that if so all four days should be called "great" — one may say that the miracle was that they say to slaughter to their gods, and on Shabbat the Egyptian said it is true, and what they did not slaughter because it is Shabbat, not so on other days — Maharav R' Moshe, R' Eliyahu. One does not read haftarah "Ve'aravah" except when Shabbat HaGadol falls on erev Pesach, and so too Rashal would preach that each one eat a little before Pesach from Passover flour, lest there was concern of chimutz and he rely on what he ate. (And Magen Avraham wondered at him that this is only in a significant matter mixed dry in dry; see Yoreh Deah siman 110 — flour in flour is a liquid-in-liquid matter — see there; and Kaf HaChayim wrote that he speaks of law and even though it does not help, only if one of them fell in inadvertence — see there.) It is good to give from it to the poor — Mateh Moshe and Shelah.
