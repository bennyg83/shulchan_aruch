Because of the miracle. Each person took a lamb on the tenth of the month, and that year the tenth fell on Shabbos. See Tur. The authorities ask that the festival should have been fixed on the tenth of the month even when it falls on a weekday, like the other festivals. Perhaps this was not done because a fast was established on that date for the death of Miriam, as stated in siman 580. Maharshal would teach that every person should eat a small amount before Pesach from the flour prepared for Pesach, because perhaps there was some concern of fermentation, and it could then be attributed to what he ate. It is proper to give some of it to a poor person. Mateh Moshe and Shelah. This requires study. That principle applies specifically to an important dry item mixed with another dry item, where one of them was eaten, in which case all are permitted, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 110. Flour mixed with flour is regarded as a liquid mixture. If there are sixty parts, everything is permitted, and if not, everything is forbidden. One must say that they adopted this only as an additional stringency. Nevertheless, one should be stringent, since this ruling issued from the mouth of that righteous authority.
