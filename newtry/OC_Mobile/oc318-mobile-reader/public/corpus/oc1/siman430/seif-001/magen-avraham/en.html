Because of the miracle — that each one took a lamb on the tenth of the month; and that year it was on Shabbat — see Tur; and they challenged: it should have been established as Yom Tov on the tenth of the month even if it falls on a weekday, like other festivals; and it is possible because they established on it a fast for Miriam who died on it, as is written siman 580. And Rashal would preach that each one eat a little before Pesach from Passover flour, lest there was concern of chimutz and he rely on what he ate; and it is good to give from it to the poor (Mateh Moshe and Shelah); and there is difficulty — for this is specifically in a significant matter mixed dry in dry and one of them was eaten, then all is permitted as written in Yoreh Deah siman 110; but flour in flour is called liquid in liquid, and if there are sixty everything is permitted, and if not sixty everything is forbidden; and one must say he took it as a stringency in general; and one should be stringent since it came from the mouth of that tzaddik.
