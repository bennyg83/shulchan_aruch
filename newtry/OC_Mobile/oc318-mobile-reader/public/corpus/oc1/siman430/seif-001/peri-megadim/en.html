Because — Taz in Tur that the Egyptian [Pesach] they took on the tenth and it was on Shabbat as is found in Shabbat 87; and Taz [objects] in Seder Olam, for according to one opinion on the sixth day they left and Shabbat was on the ninth — see siman 494 in Magen Avraham, day of giving Torah on the 51st — see there; and Av Beit Din and Beit Yosef brought in the name of Tosafot from tractate Bekhorot in Egypt — therefore it is said "through their firstborn," like "with a stone, with a fist" — by a stone, so too this. And in Levush he wrote that through Shabbat the miracle was that they were occupied with the firstborn — see there that it was muktzeh; and we find that Abraham our father observed even the Torah — so too all rabbinic matters, for Shabbat too at Marah they were commanded and they observed Shabbat on their own; and see Derishah that they asked about the knot they made on Shabbat and did not know how to divide between permanent, etc. And according to Seder Olam one may say on the Shabbat before, Moshe preached to the multitude and they accepted upon themselves Heaven's name, and in Israel His name was great — see Perishah. Shabbat HaGadol — because Shabbat sometimes in the feminine language — Taz, Responsa, sign 2, and Peri there.

They say — piyyutim of Shabbat HaGadol, and also "Avadim hayinu," even when it falls on erev Pesach on Shabbat; and the sermon — if it falls on erev Pesach on Shabbat they advance to the past Shabbat, for on erev Pesach it is impossible to warn and fix what must be before Pesach — Av Beit Din and Rabbi Akiva Eiger. See siman 494 regarding the law of Shabbat erev Pesach, and Even HaEzer there will be explained.

See — Rambam z"l chapter 10 of Hilchot Kiddush HaChodesh: difficult to think visitation of the fourteenth that is not on the second, and the first also needs three groups, not four — since he considered that one does not bring the festival offering, as is changed in the Tosefta — see there. And Rashi Pesachim 96a s.v. kevateih: Pesach dorot need not take on the tenth, but from morning of lambs; and in Yoma "take one of them" — meaning one need not separate from them one for Pesach on the tenth, not so Pesach Mizrayim; and this is per Rashi's two explanations in Arakhin 13 — see Rambam chapter 1 of Hilchot Chalitzah. And if so, one may say Pesach Mizrayim were inspectors of the fourteenth before the tenth, so that on the tenth one selects one and consecrates it for Pesach; and in Seder Olam the ninth of the month was Shabbat and they were occupied with the firstborn to inspect them and ask; and it is answered — also according to Seder Olam as said; and the time does not gather here — Magen Avraham and Even HaEzer there will be further explained.
