He pushes it. Although the chametz is not his and he does not transgress bal yera'eh, nevertheless it is forbidden to keep it in his domain, lest he come to eat from it when he goes up to the roof to use it.
With a reed. But touching it with his hands is forbidden, lest he come to eat from it. Although he moves and burns his own chametz on Chol HaMoed, as above in se'if 1, there it is different, for since he is searching for it in order to burn it, we are not concerned that he may forget and eat it.
For it is forbidden to move it, etc. This means even by pushing with a reed, for moving from the side is called moving. He should keep it until Chol HaMoed, and afterward push it to the non-Jew's roof or to the public domain.
