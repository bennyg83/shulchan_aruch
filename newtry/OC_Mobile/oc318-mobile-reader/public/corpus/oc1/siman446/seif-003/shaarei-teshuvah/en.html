A utensil. See Baer Heitev. See Makom Shmuel, siman 26, regarding what he wrote on the words of the Rambam, the Maggid Mishneh, and the Kesef Mishneh in chapter 3 of Hilchos Chametz. See Responsa Bach, siman 124, that what we say, that when it became chametz on Pesach it was not included in the nullification and it is permitted to burn it even on Yom Tov, is because the formula of kol chamira implies only what had already become chametz. But if he explicitly nullified also all chametz that would become chametz and later enter his domain, he no longer transgresses it according to Torah law. If so, even flour that became chametz may not be burned on Yom Tov. See there regarding the law of a chicken in which a wheat kernel was found, and it is explained below in siman 447 and siman 467.
