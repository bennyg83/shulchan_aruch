(א) Permitted even to eat we follow latter presumption — Rif Rambam Rosh omitted this law entirely; Acharonim strained; Peri Chadash in our edition writes explicitly this sugya entire law only after Pesach — understood reason: within Pesach karet not so lenient eat possible chametz only after Pesach rabbinic; even d'oraisa no karet — perhaps why Rishonim omitted unclear; practically certainly not lenient within Pesach against Peri Chadash whose words are tradition.

(ב) If very moldy — implies if slightly moldy lenient molded day or two of Pesach days (and "if many days of Pesach passed" means many days passed); see Gemara well.
