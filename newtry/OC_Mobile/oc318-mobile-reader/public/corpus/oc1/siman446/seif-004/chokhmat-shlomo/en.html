If he found bread on Pesach in his house, etc., we follow the later one, etc. Note: in my first edition I concluded that the presumption of following the later one is based on proximity, for what difference is there between proximity in place and proximity in time? Now I have found an explicit proof for our words in Tosafos, chapter 9 of Bava Basra, folio 152a, where they wrote as follows: once the doubt arose and the rainy season passed over it, even if he comes to ask in the summer season, it is a private domain for impurity, for one cannot say that we follow the present, since it was closer to the rainy season, etc.; see there. If so, it implies that where the time is close, we follow proximity, and it is stronger than the present presumption, which is strong according to Rabban Gamliel; see there. This is a proof for our words; examine this.
