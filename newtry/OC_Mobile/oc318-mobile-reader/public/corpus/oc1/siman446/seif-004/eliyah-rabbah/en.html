And it is forbidden to move it, etc. Chok Yaakov wrote: it is proper for every halachic authority to be careful to designate a room or closed chest in which to throw what he rules to be chametz. I have seen people who carry it to his house, and this is a complete prohibition. End quote. In my humble opinion, we rely on what was explained in siman 308, s.k. 4, and siman 331, s.k. 5, that whenever one moved something permissibly, while it is still in his hand he may return it to whatever place he wants. Although the halachic authority takes it from his hand, nevertheless, since he does so in order to see it and return it, it is as though it is still in the questioner's hand. See siman 331, s.k. 5, regarding a circumcision knife, for another permission. I also explained another reason for permission there. But his remedy, that the halachic authority should keep it by him, is not clear, for this will lead to suspicion that he is causing the matzah to become chametz in order to keep it. The proof he brings from challah in Responsa Mahariv, siman 193, is no proof, for there the challah is his. See the beginning of siman 445, that there is a remedy when it is still in his hand, namely to throw it into the privy.
