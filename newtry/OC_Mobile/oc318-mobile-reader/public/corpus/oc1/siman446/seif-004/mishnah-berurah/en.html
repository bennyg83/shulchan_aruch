For now they are using only matzah throughout the whole house, and additionally, he checked the house according to the law before Pesach and no chametz remained at all; certainly it is from the matzah that they used later. The later authorities wrote that even if he found the bread in a hollow or hole, we are not concerned that perhaps he did not see it at the time of checking, for presumably he checked according to the law in holes and cracks, as explained above in siman 433. This applies specifically when the hollow is exposed. But if he found bread in a hollow that is not visible to everyone, such as if he found it in a groove at the bottom of a chest, covered by the boards at the bottom of the chest, and it is not visible to everyone, one must be concerned that this bread may be chametz, and he is obligated to destroy it immediately. See Biur Halacha for what we wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel regarding the essential law of this se'if.
