Although one already nullified the chametz, one nevertheless recites a blessing over fulfilling the enactment of the Sages. This is certainly so if dough became chametz in one's possession during Pesach, since it was not included in the nullification; one recites a blessing upon eliminating it. This applies specifically to definite chametz. If a wheat kernel is found in a cooked dish, however, it is not definite chametz and no blessing is recited over it.
