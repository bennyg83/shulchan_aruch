(s.k. 20+) Second yom tov — Taz, Magen Avraham 2–3, Eliyah Rabba — burning in place is better than from within (see there). What he wrote in the name of Rambam chapter 2 law 3 after four hours — see letter 2 and Magen Avraham 3: if nullified, after first yom tov ends he burns (textual variant: nullifies); Gra asked: dispute between Rambam and Mordechai and Rashi — Rambam per Chachamim cast to sea and only rabbinic moving; on second yom tov they permitted moving — if so burning forbidden on second yom tov; Shulchan Aruch HaRav or even if from within they did not permit burning; Mordechai rules like R' Yehuda per Rashi. Nevertheless what he wrote to burn — father of melacha is difficult for us who say from within; Magen Avraham; what Rambam wrote 'first yom tov' means immediately at dark and he calls it first relative to later days (see there); Eliyah Rabba letters 2–3 will explain further. Wheat grain — Chok Yaakov, Sha'ar HaTziyun — does not violate bal yera'eh; even if one holds he violates, nevertheless in nullification or several doubts that we are not expert whether it sprouted, and several stringencies — better not to burn on second yom tov but wait until dark.
