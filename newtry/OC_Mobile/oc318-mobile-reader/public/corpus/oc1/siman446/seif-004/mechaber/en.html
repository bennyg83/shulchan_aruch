If one finds bread in his home during Pesach and does not know whether it is chametz (leavened) or matzah, it is permitted even to eat it, since we follow the most recent presumption , unless it is so moldy that it could not have become that way since Pesach began, in which case it is certainly chametz. However, if enough days of Pesach have passed to account for the mold, and it is one's custom to bake fresh warm bread every day during Pesach, we lean toward leniency even if it is very moldy, attributing the mold to the daily placement of warm bread on top of it.
