Se'if katan 4: Its law is like Chol HaMoed. That is, he should take it to the sea. If it is possible to throw it into the privy, as the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 445, s.k. 2, that is better in my humble opinion, for with the sea, besides the prohibition of moving, there is the prohibition of carrying out to the sea, which is a karmelis. Although we say the principle of since regarding carrying, as stated at the beginning of siman 508, nevertheless according to the Kesef Mishneh, which he cited here in s.k. 3, this is not called even some need.
