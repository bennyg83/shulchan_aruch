Regarding this matter. That is to say, if he found chametz on the second day of Yom Tov, he does not cover it with a utensil, but takes it out and destroys it immediately. The same applies if he found it on the first day of Yom Tov, when he must cover it with a utensil: it remains that way until night, and when the second day of Yom Tov arrives he destroys it. See Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, cited in Eliyah Rabbah, that several halachic decisors disagree with this, for the Sages made the second day of Yom Tov like the first day for every matter. As practical law, the later authorities agreed that if he already nullified his chametz, the second day of Yom Tov is like the first. Only if he did not nullify his chametz, or it became chametz during Pesach and was not included in the nullification, and there was a kezayis measure, so that according to all views he transgresses bal yera'eh and bal yimatzei, may one rely on the reasoning of the one who says this and move the chametz himself to throw it into the sea or into the privy, since according to most early authorities this is permitted even on the first day of Yom Tov.
