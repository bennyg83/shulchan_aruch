Se'if 2: There is one who says. See the Taz. The Beis Yosef wrote, etc.; so he wrote in the name of Orchos Chaim. Orchos Chaim holds like the view of Tosafos, that the prohibition of burning is only because of moving muktzeh; and since it is only a rabbinic shevus, we are lenient on the second day of Yom Tov for a need, just as they permitted a shevus on the second day of Yom Tov for a sick person who is not in danger, at the end of chapter 2 of Beitzah, as written in siman 496.
