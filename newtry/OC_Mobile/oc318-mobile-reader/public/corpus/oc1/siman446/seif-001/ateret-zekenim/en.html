One who finds chametz, etc. But chametz of hekdesh in his house — he need not cover with a vessel; forbidden to move chametz in public domain for it is ownerless and one acquires by lifting (Beit Yosef). Flour on which rain fell on Yom Tov and leavened — R' Yehuda: burn same day even first Yom Tov since not in nullification; Chachamim: cast to river; likewise Gaon Maharam of Lublin and custom; dish with grain on Shabbat or Yom Tov — leave until dark then burn even second Yom Tov; grain in dish on seventh day of Pesach — even R' Yehuda cast to river; by crumbs if possible — if leave until last day permitted; chametz on last day leave until night permitted even to eat; but found chametz on Yom Tov no distinction first/last — if not nullified burn immediately; if nullified as now all nullify — cover even after last Yom Tov; after Yom Tov burn since chametz extant (my teacher Bach).
