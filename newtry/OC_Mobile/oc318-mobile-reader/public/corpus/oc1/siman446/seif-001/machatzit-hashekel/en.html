Se'if katan 1: And he should destroy it immediately; it appears to me that he recites a blessing, etc. Although above in siman 435, s.k. 2, he left this law requiring examination, nevertheless one must say that after he raised the question, he later resolved it, that one should recite a blessing. One should not distinguish between where he found it on Yom Tov and where he found it on Chol HaMoed. See Chok Yaakov.
