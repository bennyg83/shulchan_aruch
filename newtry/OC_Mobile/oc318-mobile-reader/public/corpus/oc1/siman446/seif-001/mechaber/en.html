One who finds chametz during the festival [of Passover]. And it contains 4 sections: One who
finds chametz in his house if it is on the intermediate days of the festival, he should take it out and destroy it immediately. And if it is on a festival day, he should cover it with a vessel until nightfall and then destroy it, because he cannot move it on a festival day (Ribash). Also,) to burn it in its place is forbidden (Ran).):
