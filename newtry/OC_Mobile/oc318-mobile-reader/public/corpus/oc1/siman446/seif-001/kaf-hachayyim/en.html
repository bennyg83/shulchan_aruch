1) [Se'if 1] One who finds chametz in one's house, etc. Chametz in the public domain, however, is ownerless and one may not move it, even if it poses a danger to the public. By lifting it, one acquires it, and it becomes one's own chametz. Responsa Rivash, siman 401; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Peri Chadash; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1.
2) There. One should remove it and eliminate it immediately. This means that as soon as one finds chametz in one's residence, one should take it outside so that it is no longer before one, without leaving it there even briefly until it can be burned, lest one stumble and eat it. Taz, subsection 1; Chemed Moshe, subsection 1. If wheat is found to contain kernels that became chametz, one should sort out everything that can be sorted and burn the sprouted kernels. The remainder may be retained. Whatever cannot be sorted is considered unidentifiable and is nullified in a majority as a dry substance mixed with another dry substance; it may be retained where a substantial loss is involved. Responsa of Rabbi Chaim Kohen, Orach Chaim, siman 9; Pis'chei Olam, subsection 2.
3) There. One should remove it and eliminate it immediately. It is forbidden to move it for a purpose unrelated to its elimination, lest one come to eat from it. This applies even to chametz belonging to a non-Jew. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1.
4) There. And eliminate it immediately. One recites the blessing over eliminating chametz. Although one already nullified the chametz, one nevertheless recites a blessing over fulfilling the enactment of the Sages. This is certainly so if dough became chametz in one's possession during Pesach, since it was not included in the nullification; one recites a blessing upon eliminating it. This applies specifically to definite chametz. If a wheat kernel is found in a cooked dish, however, it is not definite chametz and no blessing is recited over it. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1. Regarding the practical ruling, see above at the end of siman 435, where the opinions of the later authorities are presented. See there.
