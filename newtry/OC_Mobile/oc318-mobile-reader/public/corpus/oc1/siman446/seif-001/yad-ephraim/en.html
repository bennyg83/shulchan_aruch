(Magen Avraham s.k. 2) End of Magen Avraham s.k. 2 and Maharam of Lublin siman 100 also wrote: because it was sit and do not act — the Sages established their word even to uproot from the Torah; likewise Ran regarding shofar, etc., and Ran questioned there: why must it be Yom Tov aseh, etc., and wrote it is not specifically aseh and lav; and in Shulchan Shelah laws of Rosh HaShanah he wrote to answer that aseh of the public overrides, etc. — such is Magen Avraham's intent; and what is written 'and R' Chaim' — in my view it is a textual error, for I examined Shulchan Shelah which wrote: there is wonder why it should be aseh and lav, etc., and we explain aseh of the congregation overrides, etc.; and R' Tam questioned: what of it if they roll away the mound — it is mere moving, etc., and answered: if it is possible to roll by hand, likewise here with a shovel and basket (end of his words). And what Shelah wrote that a rabbinic aseh comes, etc. — meaning even where nullified there is no rabbinic obligation nevertheless it overrides rabbinic lav of telling a gentile; and Magen Avraham 'requires study' there — it appears Shelah does not hold what Nimukei Yosef wrote in the name of Rambam that at the time of moving, etc.; rather here since the way of removal is thus it is properly at its time and the start of removal is effective although not finished until cast to sea; as proven in many places that wherever this is the way of performing the aseh and its start, it is properly at its time — unlike shofar where rolling the mound is not part of performing the aseh itself, only preparation.
