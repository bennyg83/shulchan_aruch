(א) In his house — Rivash 401: found chametz public domain forbidden to lift — from moment lifted acquired and violates bal yera'eh even without intent to acquire — only to remove from place many pass lest stumble — also forbidden; see his words; poskim copied.

(ב) That he cannot move it — see Mishna Berurah; even rabbinic chametz forbidden to move (Acharonim). If moves food to posek ask chametz question and ruled forbidden — Acharonim if may return home to cover or must throw immediately; Kaf HaChayyim; Avnei Nezer; Beit Meir; appears even if may move back since in hand started permitted — 266, 508 — nevertheless not to house only bathroom; covering permitted only because cannot move.
