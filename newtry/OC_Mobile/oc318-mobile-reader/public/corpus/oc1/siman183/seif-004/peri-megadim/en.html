See Taz, who cites the Tur's wording that the reason for raising the cup a tefach is so that those reclining should look at it, while the one reciting the blessing fixes his eyes upon it. See Derishah, siman 182, seif 2. Some are particular not to hold the cup in their hands. Yet does it not require elevation by a tefach? One may answer that the elevation is required when three people are present, so that they may look at it, but not when one is alone. However, from the Tur's words, “and he fixes his eyes upon it,” referring to the one reciting the blessing, it appears that even an individual raises it a tefach. See Taz, siman 484, seif katan 2. Regarding a narrow “gluck glass,” if he has no other cup it is valid after the fact. It appears, however, that if it is impossible to drink a revi'is from it within the required time, so that the quantity should combine, as we will explain below with Hashem's help in the laws of Bircas HaMazon, one should not use such a cup. See siman 472, Taz, seif katan 8. Regarding the statement that there are four requirements, the Mechaber lists more than four. See Nechalas Tzvi on this subject; see there.
