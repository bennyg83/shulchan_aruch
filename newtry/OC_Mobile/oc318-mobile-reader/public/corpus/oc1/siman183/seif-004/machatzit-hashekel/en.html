Seif katan 4: Full, etc. With a crown around the inside of the cup. Meaning, they explain the “adornment” mentioned among the ten matters stated regarding a cup of blessing to mean that it should not be completely full, leaving a crown-like rim within the cup. Magen Avraham rejects this, for on the contrary, the cup must be full to its brim, and fullness itself is one of the ten matters. “Adornment” therefore refers to something else, as explained in the Gemara. Taz, seif katan 4, further rejects this because the halachah is that adornment is not required. Taz writes that those who nevertheless leave space do so lest the cup spill and become degraded, as stated at the beginning of siman 171 that one may not pass a full cup over food for this reason.
