Seif katan 7: Therefore one should not, etc. This is because Sukkah 49a states as follows.
He drinks in sips. Rashi explains that this means large swallows, because of affection for the mitzvah. Maharsha, however, proves that it means fine, small sips, through which the throat derives greater enjoyment and there is therefore greater affection for the mitzvah. This rejects Rabbi Avraham of Prague’s explanation. The wording of the Gemara also implies this, for it says that ten matters were stated regarding a cup and includes among them “he places his eyes upon it,” which implies that this refers to the cup itself, since the Gemara said these matters were stated regarding the cup.
