7) There: To make known the praise of the Land. This is to show everyone present the praise of Eretz Yisrael, whose wine is strong and requires water to be added. Although the person reciting the blessing is outside Eretz Yisrael, since he does not dilute the wine until Birkas HaAretz, it is apparent that he comes to demonstrate the praise of Eretz Yisrael. Beis Yosef; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3.
