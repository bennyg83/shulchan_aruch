Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 3, writes that if the wine is strong and requires much water, while the cup must be full, one should dilute it somewhat before Birkas HaMazon and add more during Birkas HaAretz, intending to sweeten the judgments. Ben Ish Chai, parashas Shelach Lecha, paragraph 21, however, writes that this is questionable, because some hold that at the beginning of the blessing the wine must be undiluted. This opinion appears primary, since Raya Mehemna, parashas Eikev, 273b, states it without qualification, whereas it presents the other opinions in the name of 'some say.' If one dilutes it before the blessing, the quality of being undiluted is absent according to that opinion. Therefore, even if it requires much water and the cup must necessarily have a visibly missing amount at the beginning of the blessing, this does not concern us, since during Birkas HaAretz he dilutes it and it becomes full. Nevertheless, it appears to me that at the beginning of the blessing it must in any event contain a reviis. These are his words.
