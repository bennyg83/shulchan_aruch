A left-handed person. This requires study. In siman 651 Beit Yosef rules without qualification that we follow the ordinary right hand, since taking the lulav in the right hand is D’Rabbanan. All the more so here, where the cup of blessing is fundamentally D’Rabbanan, we should follow the ordinary right hand. Indeed, the Shibolei HaLeket before us says nothing about this regarding Bircas HaMazon, and regarding lulav it rules that he takes it in the ordinary right hand. Perhaps another authority rules this way regarding Bircas HaMazon. Nevertheless, it appears to me that one should practice as Rama rules regarding lulav.
