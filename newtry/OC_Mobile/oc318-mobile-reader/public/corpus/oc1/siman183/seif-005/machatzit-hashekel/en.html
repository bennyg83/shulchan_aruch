Seif katan 5: A cup, etc. Even if it was broken, etc. Eliyah Rabbah further cites Maharil that even if the cup is merely cracked, without any missing piece, one should not recite the blessing over it when another cup is available. See there further.
To require all ten matters, etc. Only adornment is added, for the Shulchan Aruch itself lists eight matters individually: rinsing inside, washing outside, undiluted wine, fullness, receiving it with both hands, taking it in the right hand, raising it a tefach, and looking at it. There are additionally adornment and wrapping. They write that outside Eretz Yisrael wrapping is not practiced.
And Bach wrote, etc. With an outer garment, even when reciting Bircas HaMazon alone. Shelah likewise writes that the awe of his Master should be upon him.
