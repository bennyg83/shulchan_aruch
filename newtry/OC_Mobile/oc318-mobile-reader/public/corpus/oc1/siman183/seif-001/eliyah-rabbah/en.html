Remnants, etc. This means bread was soaked in the cup. The same applies if the cup was wiped with a cloth. Magen Avraham. It is proper to rinse the cup even when there are no bread remnants unless it is already clean and clear, because of the principle, 'Offer it now to your governor.' Berachot Maharam writes that one should not hold the cup while wearing gloves, but should remove them first. See seif katan 7.
