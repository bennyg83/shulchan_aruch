For the sake of the blessing. “Alive” means undiluted wine, called fresko. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that one should draw it from the barrel immediately before reciting the blessing. Accordingly, in our circumstances too one should send to the shopkeeper to purchase it immediately before the blessing, so that it will be “alive.”
