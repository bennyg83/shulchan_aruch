He receives it. See Taz. Levush derives this from the fact that the verse does not write yedekhem with a yod between the dalet and kaf. Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 6, notes that it is in fact written without a yod. Rather, the vowel under the yod is a sheva and the dalet has a tzerei. It should have written the yod with a segol and the dalet with a sheva, which would indicate a single hand, as in the verse concerning tefillin. See there, and see Tehillim 134.
