2) Bach writes: As a practical halachah, since Tosafos and Rosh, of blessed memory, testify that in their times people practiced even spreading a cloth, and the Tur and Rabbeinu Yonah write likewise, every God-fearing person should take care during the blessing to wrap himself in his outer garment and also place his hat upon his head. He should not recite the blessing wearing only a small cap. He should follow this practice even when reciting the blessing alone. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, paragraph 5; Olas Tamid, paragraph 9; Magen Avraham, paragraph 5; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 16. The later authorities write the same.
