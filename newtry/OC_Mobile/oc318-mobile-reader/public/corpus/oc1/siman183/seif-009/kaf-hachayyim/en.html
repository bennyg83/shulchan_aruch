9) There, gloss: He should draw it from the barrel for the sake of the blessing. This is because undiluted wine is called fresh in the vernacular. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that one should draw it from the barrel immediately before the blessing. Even for us, he should send to the merchant to purchase it close to the blessing so that it is fresh. Magen Avraham, paragraph 3. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3, however, writes that it is burdensome to wait while it is purchased close to the blessing. Therefore, it appears to me that one should purchase it beforehand in a pitcher, but not pour it from the pitcher until the time of the blessing, when he should pour it into the cup for the sake of the blessing. I later saw that Eshel Avraham, paragraph 3, writes the same.
