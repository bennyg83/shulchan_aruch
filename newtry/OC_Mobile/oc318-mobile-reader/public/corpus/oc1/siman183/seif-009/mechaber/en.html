9. One must sit at the time of blessing. Whether he was walking about his house when he ate, or standing, or reclining — when he reaches the point of blessing he must sit, so that he can concentrate better, and also so that he will not be reclining, which is a manner of haughtiness. Rather he should sit with awe.Rema: It appears to me that this is not specifically regarding the one blessing, but rather the same applies to all those reclining at the meal — they should not sit with levity but rather with awe. However, if they did not do so, even if he blessed while walking, after the fact he has fulfilled his obligation [Rambam, Chapter 4].]:
