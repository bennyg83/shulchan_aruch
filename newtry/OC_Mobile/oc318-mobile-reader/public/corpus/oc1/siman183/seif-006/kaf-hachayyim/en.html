6) [Seif 2] He then dilutes it, etc. Beis Yosef writes that this is the practice in our country even though the wines are not strong, because it contains a secret according to Kabbalah and also has other reasons; see there. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Zechor LeAvraham, under kaf. Shaar HaMitzvos, parashas Eikev, similarly writes: During the second blessing, when reaching the word es in the verse, 'You shall eat, be satisfied, and bless es,' then dilute the cup with water, etc., as is known. The word es is Malchus, which is called a cup. Look at that water when placing it into the cup and intend that the numerical value of 'water' is ninety, corresponding to the nine letters yud in the four spellings of Havayah: seventy-two, sixty-three, forty-five, and fifty-two. Maavar Yabok, maamar Sefas Emes, writes that one must place three drops into the cup, one after another; see there. Petach HaDevir, paragraph 1; Ruach Chaim, paragraph 1; Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 2; Ben Ish Chai, parashas Shelach Lecha, paragraph 22. It appears to me that the reason is that wine corresponds to Binah, from which judgments are aroused, and it must therefore be sweetened with the waters of kindness. Since Binah is the Name whose numerical value is sixty-three and contains three letters yud, one must place three drops corresponding to them in order to sweeten it.
