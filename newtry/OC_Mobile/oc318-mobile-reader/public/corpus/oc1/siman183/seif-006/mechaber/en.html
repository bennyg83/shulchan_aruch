6. From when the cup is given to him to bless, the one blessing should not speak, and those reclining at the meal should not speak from the moment the one blessing begins. Not only while he is actually blessing — when they must listen and understand what the one blessing says — but even between one blessing and the next they should not speak. And if they transgressed and spoke between blessing and blessing, at a time when the one blessing was momentarily silent, they have fulfilled their obligation.(Rema): Even if the one blessing himself spoke. But if they spoke while he was actually blessing, they have not fulfilled their obligation. אבל אם שחו בשעה שהוא מברך לא יצאו:
