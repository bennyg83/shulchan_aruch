(Seif katan 14) And there are those who say, etc. Since the prohibition, etc. Only with a healthy animal was skinning at the end permitted because of the beginning, for if we do not permit him to skin and move the hide, he will refrain from the joy of Yom Tov and will not slaughter because of concern for loss. But with a dangerously ill animal this does not apply, for he does not slaughter it because of the joy of Yom Tov, but only to save himself from loss.
How did Shiltei Giborim disagree with them? For these words of Rama flow from him.
They bring, etc. If he slaughtered, its meat, as the Mechaber wrote in seif 7.
It is forbidden to pluck the feathers. See the end of seif 13. Meaning, although after slaughter in a healthy bird it is certainly permitted to pluck, and before slaughter it is a dispute among the poskim, see there, nevertheless with a dangerously ill bird, since according to Shiltei Giborim skinning is forbidden, the same applies to plucking feathers after slaughter. All the more so before slaughter it is forbidden in a dangerously ill bird according to all opinions, for the reason of those who permit in a healthy bird is that it is permitted to pluck after slaughter. But with a dangerously ill bird, since it is forbidden to pluck after slaughter, certainly before slaughter it is forbidden.
