A person may not slaughter a wild animal or bird on Yom Tov unless he has earth prepared before Yom Tov. If he transgressed and slaughtered, if he has a pointed implement stuck in loose earth from before Yom Tov, he may cover the blood with it. {Rama: (meaning a type of iron spike with which one digs the ground) (Even if several pokings were required, it is permitted.)}
