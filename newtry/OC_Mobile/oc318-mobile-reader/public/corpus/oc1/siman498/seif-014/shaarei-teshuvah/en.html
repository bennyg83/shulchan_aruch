(In the Shulchan Aruch se'if 14) He should not slaughter, etc. And see Eshel Avraham s.k. 3; and see Ginas Veradim, klal 4, siman 4: that what Rashi wrote, that designation by mouth [suffices], refers to the earth below, but regarding earth above it is forbidden to move by designation alone, and he must do an action to it; and not like the city slaughterers who are accustomed to designate earth by mouth and cover with it from above; and all the more so regarding earth that the feet of the many tread, designation does not avail it, etc.; see there.
