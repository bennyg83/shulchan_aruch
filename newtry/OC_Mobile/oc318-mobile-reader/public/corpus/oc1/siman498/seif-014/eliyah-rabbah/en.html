[14] There is not time to cut it up, etc. The Rashal and Bach ruled that there must be time to eat after skinning and cutting it up. The view of the Magen Avraham is that the first Tanna agrees with Rabbi Akiva in this, but this is not clear. One should not ask what the Talmud challenges from Rabbi Akiva, for it appears to me that it holds that even the first Tanna agrees that if he did not check, it is permitted after the fact, for we do not find a dispute in this. What the first Tanna holds, that he must wait until after skinning, is because they did not wish to permit initially, for if it is later found to be a treifah he will be punished inadvertently, as Tosafot wrote.
