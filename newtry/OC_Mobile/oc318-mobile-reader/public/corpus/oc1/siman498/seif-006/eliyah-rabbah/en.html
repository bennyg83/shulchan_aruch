[6] And if it is doubtful, etc. This requires study, for in siman 515, seif 6, the Geonim ruled that its doubt is stringent, and there is permission only where fruits and non-Jews are common in the city. Although the Beis Yosef wrote this in the name of the Rashba, one can say that he holds like those who are lenient. What the Rashba himself wrote, distinguishing where fruits are common in the city, is according to the Geonim, as the Tur wrote at the end of siman 515, and so it implies in Avodas HaKodesh, page 37. Afterward the Magen Avraham was printed, and he sensed this somewhat. It is also difficult to me regarding the reasoning of the Shulchan Aruch and Levush, that with muktzeh we follow, etc.; for regarding outside the techum and items of non-Jews there is no muktzeh, but the reason for the prohibition is the decree lest they tell him to bring it. See siman 497 and siman 515, seif katan 25, 415.
