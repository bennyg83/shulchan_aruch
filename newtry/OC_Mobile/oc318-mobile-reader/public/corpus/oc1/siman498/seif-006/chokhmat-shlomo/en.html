(Se'if 6) In the gloss: And even if there is not enough time to cut it up and examine it first. Marginal note: See in Magen Avraham that we hold like R' Akiva; and behold we read in the Yerushalmi there: There they said, a wolf came, etc. And see in my work Bigdei Yom Tov on tractate Beitzah, which has already been copied and arranged there; I wrote an explanation of the Yerushalmi's wording on this in two correct ways, with Hashem's help; see there and examine well.
