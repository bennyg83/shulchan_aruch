And even more. It appears in a more spacious way that the Tur holds entirely like the first answer of Tosafot. Therefore the Gemara could not ask against Rabbi Yehudah, for according to Rabbi Yehudah either way it is permitted: if we say the Torah made four signs valid, it was prepared, because he could have inserted his hand into its mother's womb and slaughtered it; and if we do not say the Torah made four signs valid, then the permission of permitting itself through slaughter applies. Nevertheless, for us, who are uncertain whether the Torah made four signs valid, it is not considered prepared. Even if it is revealed before Heaven that four signs are valid, we, because of doubt, cannot slaughter in its mother's womb, and therefore it was not prepared for that. We need the permission that it is prepared incidentally to its mother for dogs, and where she stands for raising, it is forbidden. This is clear.
