A dangerously ill animal that one fears may die — if one has already eaten and does not need it — it is forbidden to slaughter it unless there is enough time in the day to eat an olive's worth of roasted meat while it is still day . When there is enough time and one slaughtered it, one may not remove its hide unless one left one limb attached and brings it along. {Rama: (even if there is not enough time to cut it up and examine it first) Rema: Some say it should not be skinned at all unless it was slaughtered for Yom Tov purposes, and this is the proper ruling.}
