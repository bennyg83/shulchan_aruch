Unless there is enough time in the day, etc. Because of loss of his money they permitted thus; but regarding a healthy animal it is forbidden unless he will truly eat a kezayis of it. And in Beis Yosef it is written in the name of a responsum of the Rashba: "and even an animal of Jews, even if he will eat," etc.; and that is a scribal error, and it should say: "unless he will eat."
A kezayis roasted. So holds the first Tanna in the Mishnah, and R' Akiva says even a kezayis raw; and the Rambam ruled like the first Tanna, and in the Tur "roasted" is not mentioned, and Beis Yosef wondered at him. And in my humble opinion it is astonishing regarding the Rambam, for we hold that the halacha follows R' Akiva in every place, and even against his colleagues, meaning even if many disagree with him.
