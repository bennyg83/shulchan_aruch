He holds that these are the words of the first Tanna, but in the Gemara there it is proven that these are the words of Rabbi Akiva, and so wrote the Rashba. He may not take, etc. There, 11b: three things they permitted, etc.; unlike here. Yerushalmi, chapter 3, halacha 3: It was taught: but he brings it on its hide. How does he do this? He leaves one limb from it and brings it with him.
And there are those who say that one may not, etc. As written in the preceding siman, in the sixteenth chapter of Shabbos, 116b: granted, etc. See Magen Avraham.
