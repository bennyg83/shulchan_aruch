[7] [Levush] We attribute leniently, etc. This is difficult, for that follows Rashi's explanation in Perek Sho'el, page 151, that Shmuel is lenient. But according to the Rif and Ramban there, it is forbidden, and so ruled the Beis Yosef and Shulchan Aruch at the end of siman 325 and the end of siman 515. Although the Beis Yosef wrote this in the name of the Rashba, it appears that the Rashba holds like Rashi. Perhaps for this reason the Shulchan Aruch and Levush permitted only on the second day of Yom Tov, but on the first day they in fact prohibited; so wrote the Magen Avraham. This requires study. It is also possible that the Shulchan Aruch holds more leniently regarding animals, for it is more reasonable that he brought them in from the evening, since they require slaughter and other efforts, although the poskim do not seem to distinguish this way.
