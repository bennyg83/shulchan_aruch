Permitted. See Taz, Beitzah 34a, an unresolved question, and the Rambam ruled leniently in the second chapter, halacha 4, of the laws of Yom Tov. Some say it is stringent. At first glance, it depends on the dispute whether examination is according to the Torah or D'Rabbanan. One may say that according to all, examination is according to the Torah, but on Yom Tov, because of mitoch, even slaughter is only a D'Rabbanan concern, and a D'Rabbanan doubt is ruled leniently. Others say that mitoch applies specifically to carrying and kindling; see siman 518, and therefore they ruled stringently. See Pri Chadash. In Yoreh Deah 54, Taz seif katan 1, I wrote that if most of its ribs were broken, in a bird, one should declare it treifah, though I do not find it now. See there.
