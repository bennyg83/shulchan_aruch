Seif 7, if he slaughtered, etc. Rambam, and according to the explanation that this applies to every animal. He holds that what is written, if he slaughtered it, etc., uses the usual case, for it is not the way to slaughter a healthy animal in the field. Ran. He wrote that some disagree and hold that it applies specifically to an animal in mortal danger, and so wrote the Rashba in Avodas HaKodesh.
