If he slaughtered an animal. Since he stated it plainly, it implies that even with a healthy animal the law is so.
He should bring it by hand. Although this increases his walking, nevertheless it is preferable so that it should not be like weekday practice. See the Peri Megadim, who proves from the Ran that even with a change it is not proper to bring it by means of a pole.
Limb by limb. If he cannot change and bring it limb by limb, such as where he must hurry to bring it to guests, it appears that it is permitted to bring it by a pole and carrying bar. See Or Zarua, where it is evident that with a dangerously ill animal one should be stringent according to the first wording even in this manner, and one may be lenient only with a healthy animal.
