13) Regarding the inspection after slaughter, Levush writes that some forbid it and some permit it, without deciding between them. Rabbi Be'er Sheva, however, in the ruling after the indices, decides in accordance with those who are stringent, and this is the custom. Damesek Eliezer on Chullin, page 44, writes likewise. Mahari Weil also writes in his derashos that since the meat must be rendered valid, this is needed for food preparation. Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, subsection 2. Magen Avraham, subsection 2, likewise writes that anyone may inspect the knife after slaughter, unlike Levush, who was uncertain about this. Pri Chadash; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 3; Kisei Eliyahu, subsection 1; Eshel Avraham, subsection 2; Chemed Moshe, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 3; Chayei Adam, rule 89, subsection 2; Mikraei Kodesh there, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 7, write likewise. See Yoreh Deah, siman 18, se'if 12.
