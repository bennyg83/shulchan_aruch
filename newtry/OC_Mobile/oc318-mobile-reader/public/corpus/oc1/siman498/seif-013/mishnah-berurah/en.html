He should not pluck. Plucking a feather is a derivative of shearing, for the way of a bird is by plucking. Although after slaughter it is permitted to pluck the feathers, and the prohibition of shearing applies even after death, as we wrote in siman 340, we do not say: what difference is there between while it is alive and after slaughter? After slaughter is different, because once it has been permitted through slaughter for eating, and it cannot be eaten without plucking the feathers, it is permitted to pluck them, just as it is permitted to roast and cook it. Similarly, with an animal, after slaughter it is permitted to skin it for this reason, even though skinning is a primary labor. But before it was slaughtered, whatever he does is preparatory work for food that could have been done yesterday, and is forbidden, Rosh.
To make. Nevertheless, clearing it by hand and pulling it this way and that, as above in seif 12, is permitted even with feathers.
For the knife. The Beis Yosef brought the view of the Ramban, who is lenient in this, but he agreed in practice to forbid, like most of the poskim who disagree with him, as he ruled plainly here. Nevertheless, nowadays the slaughterers have the practice to pluck, because they say that it is impossible to slaughter without this, and they rely on the Ramban's view. Certainly, if it is possible to slaughter without plucking, only by clearing it by hand, one should be stringent, as above.
