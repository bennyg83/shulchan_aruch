[8] [Levush] And it stands for dogs, etc. Nowadays, ordinary treifos do not stand for dogs; if so, the offspring of a treifah is forbidden, and this requires study. Magen Avraham. Meaning, they sell treifos to non-Jews. One must say that nevertheless, since it is inexpensive, they throw some of it to dogs; also, if they do not sell them, they do so, and it is considered as standing, etc.
