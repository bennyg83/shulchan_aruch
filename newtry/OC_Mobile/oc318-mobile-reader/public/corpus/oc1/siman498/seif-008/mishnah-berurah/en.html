Therefore it requires waiting a full day. So that it leaves the concern of crushed limbs. It also requires examination, for although certainly its limbs were not dislocated since it waited a full day, perhaps some defect occurred in the inner limbs and it became a treifah thereby.
To slaughter it on Yom Tov. This means after it waited a full day, although it still lacks examination. According to the gloss in Yoreh Deah, siman 58, seif 5, that we are not expert in examining all the limbs, this law is not found for us. The Rama did not note this here, for he relied on what he wrote there in Yoreh Deah.
And we are not concerned, etc. Although it requires examination, nevertheless, since it initially had a presumption of fitness, for most animals and birds are kosher, it is permitted to slaughter it, for we maintain it in its presumption of permissibility.
Although a defect arose in it. The same applies with a doubt whether it was punctured by a thorn and the like, that it is permitted to slaughter it. Certainly where there is no defect at all, only in some places treifos are common like kosher animals, we need not be concerned that it may be found treifah, for we maintain it in the presumption of permissibility, since most animals in the world are kosher. Some disagree with the main law of the Mechaber and hold that one should not slaughter where there is a defect, and the same applies where treifos are as common as kosher animals. See Shaarei Teshuvah, who wrote that certainly if the majority are treifah, even only because of later stringencies, it is forbidden to slaughter on Yom Tov. In practice, the later authorities ruled to be concerned for this view and be stringent even at half and half, provided that the common half of treifos are full treifos according to the law. But if they are only because of later stringencies, it is permitted to slaughter on Yom Tov if it is needed, and the same applies to a dangerously ill animal because of financial loss. Otherwise, the practice is not to slaughter any animal on Yom Tov, even where treifos are not so common, lest it be found treifah. But the practice is to slaughter birds on Yom Tov, Chayei Adam.
