Seif 8. It requires waiting from time to time. If the full time ended on Yom Tov, one may say it is forbidden because of muktzeh according to those who forbid muktzeh on Yom Tov, since at twilight it was forbidden. It was also not prepared for dogs, since it stands to wait a full day and then be eaten. It is similar to what the Maharsha wrote in the first chapter of Beitzah, that if it was born eight days before Yom Tov it is forbidden because of muktzeh; see there.
