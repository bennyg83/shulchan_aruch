Treifah. In Yam Shel Shlomo he wrote that in a place where treifos are as common as kosher animals, it is forbidden to slaughter on Yom Tov, all the more so where a defect has been established. In our time, the practice is not to slaughter any animal on Yom Tov, even where treifos are not common. It appears to me that when he needs it on Yom Tov, one may be lenient; the same applies to an animal in mortal danger. Magen Avraham. See Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 158, where he discusses this at length.
