Koy means, etc. There is also another kind of koy, which is a creature of its own, and it is uncertain whether it is a wild animal or a domestic animal. We give it the stringencies of both: it requires covering like a wild animal, and its fat is forbidden like that of a domestic animal.
From a goat and a deer. Meaning, a deer that came upon a she-goat. But a goat that came upon a female deer, although its fat is also forbidden, nevertheless one may slaughter it on Yom Tov, as explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 28, seif 3, in the later authorities there.
One may not slaughter it on Yom Tov. For he cannot cover its blood until after Yom Tov; perhaps it will be absorbed in the ground and its mark will not be visible, and the mitzvah of covering will be nullified.
Prepared earth. Even if he prepared it explicitly for covering a koy, Meiri. See Tzelach, whose view is that this is specifically with a koy that is a creature of its own, but with one born from a deer that came upon a she-goat, if he prepared it explicitly before Yom Tov for covering it, it is permitted to slaughter it on Yom Tov; see his reason there.
Because one who sees, etc. Meaning, so that people should not err and think that a koy is certainly a wild animal, since they permitted exertion on Yom Tov. The Sages have power to nullify a mitzvah through passive inaction, so that one should not come to a stumbling block involving a doubt of kareis through active transgression.
And in the evening, if its mark, etc. For us, who observe two days, he must wait to cover until after the second day of Yom Tov.
Even the blood of a domestic animal. This implies that certainly the blood of a koy may be covered, since even without the mitzvah of covering he must cover it because of repulsiveness; people will no longer err to permit its fat, for they will say that he covers it so his utensils should not become dirty. See the Magen Avraham, who inclines that one should be lenient to cover only where he truly intends that his utensils should not become dirty. But if he intends only for the mitzvah of covering, it is forbidden. See Biur Halacha.
If he has prepared earth. Such as if he brought in a basketful of earth without specification, as in seif 17, or prepared it to cover excrement; it is prepared also for this.
For it is like a chamber pot, and one must cover. Meaning, it is the way to cover, etc.
