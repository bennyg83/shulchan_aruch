A koy — one may not slaughter it on Yom Tov, and if one did slaughter it, one may not cover its blood even with prepared earth, since an observer will say it is certainly a wild animal — otherwise why would they trouble to cover its blood on Yom Tov — and he will come to permit its forbidden fat. If its mark is recognizable, one may cover it. {Rama: (meaning a creature born from a goat and a deer) Rema: This applies specifically when it was slaughtered in a corner and similar situations. But if it was slaughtered in the middle of the courtyard, even the blood of a regular animal may be covered if one has prepared earth, since it is like an offensive object that must be covered so that one's clothes not be soiled in the courtyard.}
