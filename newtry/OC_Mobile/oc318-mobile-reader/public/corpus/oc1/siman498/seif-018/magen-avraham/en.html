Because one who sees. For this reason it is forbidden to put a little earth in a vessel to receive the blood; see Yoreh Deah, siman 28.
Even the blood of an animal. All the more so that of a koy. If he prepared earth for excrement, it is prepared for a koy, for both are doubtful, as stated in the Gemara. Nevertheless this requires study, for the Gemara concludes that it is forbidden to cover the blood of a koy because of permitting its forbidden fat. If so, even on a weekday it should be forbidden. On a weekday we say that he needs it to clean his courtyard, which implies that on Yom Tov we do not say that he needs it to clean his courtyard. It is forced to say that this discusses a corner, for if so, even on a weekday it should be forbidden. Perhaps according to the conclusion, when he truly intends to clean his courtyard it is permitted on Yom Tov, but when he intends for the mitzvah of covering, it is forbidden because of permitting its forbidden fat.
