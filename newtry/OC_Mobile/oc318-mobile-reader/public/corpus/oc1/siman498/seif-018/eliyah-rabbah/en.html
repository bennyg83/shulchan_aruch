[18] A sage may not, etc. But when he sees the blemish and remains silent, even if he concluded in his heart that it is a blemish, it is permitted. Yam Shel Shlomo, Perek Ein Tzadin 15. The Taz wrote: if liquid prohibition fell into liquid permission in less than sixty, or dry into dry and there is no majority of permitted matter, and afterward more permitted matter was added, it is forbidden to rule on Yom Tov, for it is exactly like a firstborn that has a presumption of prohibition.
