16) There, in the gloss. He should inspect his knife on erev Yom Tov, etc. He should not return the knife to a hard sheath. If the edge or sides of the knife touch the hardness of the sheath, it is no longer considered an inspected knife, as Tevuos Shor writes in Yoreh Deah, siman 18, subsection 14. Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger; Mikraei Kodesh there, Likutei Rima, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 6.
