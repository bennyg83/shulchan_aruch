Dust of ritual impurity. Soft dust — not digging or crushing, only preparation (Rashi); even though above without embedded stake digging applies to permanent ground, here brought dust elsewhere placed in corner — no digging at all (Beit Yosef). Per my words reason embedded stake is preparation — therefore soft dust same, no pit and as written otherwise forbidden only to cover, so Tosafos and Ran explicitly; Ran: all agree fruits and flour no pit concern (siman 511:1) — seems weekday dust hard detached from ground pit; Ran: forbidden to bury egg in soft dust unless so soft when placing egg on dust enters by itself — Tosafos; Shabbos 113 stakes in ground forbidden to remove — pit forms automatically; if not permit removal, taught burying loaf etc. permitted (siman 311:8) answers field pits not uniform, house uniform — learn permitted to remove embedded even if makes pit; forbidden to embed candle in dust making pit as Tosafos and Ran above; Tosafos Shabbos 21 Elazar ben Teddai even if leaves not exposed permitted embed in bundle — bundle unique thin no pit remains when removed.
