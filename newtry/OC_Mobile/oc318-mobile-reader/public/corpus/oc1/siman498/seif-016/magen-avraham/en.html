Much earth. This discusses loose earth, which lacks neither digging nor crushing, but only preparation, Rashi. Although above he holds that if there is no spade inserted, digging applies, that is with earth of the ground. But here, where he brought earth from elsewhere and placed it in a corner, digging does not apply at all, Beis Yosef. According to what I wrote above, that the reason a spade must be inserted is because of preparation, there is no difficulty. It discusses earth so loose that he does not make a pit, as I wrote above; otherwise it is forbidden to do anything else with it, and it may be used only to cover with. So it is explicitly implied in Tosafot and Ran; see there. The Ran wrote there that according to all opinions, with fruits and flour there is no issue of making a pit, as written in siman 510, seif 1. This implies that with sand and earth there can be an issue of making a pit if it is hard, even when detached from the ground. If so, what people thrust knives into boxes filled with earth on Yom Tov is not proper if the earth is somewhat hard.
