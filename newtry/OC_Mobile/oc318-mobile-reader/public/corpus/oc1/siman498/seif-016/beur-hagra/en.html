Seif 16, he brought in, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. hichnis, etc., and s.v. machnis, etc. Meaning, for the needs of his garden he requires much, but if he brought it in without specification, even a basket is enough. See Tosafot there, s.v. ve'amar: and if you will say, etc.; one can say, etc.; alternatively one can say, etc. This is what is written here: to cover it, in a basket, all that he needs.
