Se'if 2. He may bring the knife and the animal to a slaughterer, etc. Marginal note: See in my work on Yoreh De'ah, edition 3, Hilchos Avodah Zarah, siman 155, what I discussed there at length regarding the reason it is permitted to slaughter on Yom Tov, and particularly according to the view that we are concerned for the minority, and in resolving the words of the Rosh who wrote that we require that it spread its legs on the ground, and in the sugya of "mitoch"; see there and examine carefully.
