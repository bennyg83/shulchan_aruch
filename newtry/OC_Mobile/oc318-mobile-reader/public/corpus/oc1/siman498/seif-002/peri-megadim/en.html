And regarding. See Taz, Yoreh Deah 15b, that on Yom Tov too it need not walk; see there. For the practical law, see Magen Avraham, seif katan 9 and 10, and what I wrote in Pri Megadim to Yoreh Deah 13, in the Shach, seif katan 10 and 11, and in Tevuos Shor, that one may say that in D'Rabbanan matters, even nowadays we rely on completion of its months. For the practical law, see there. There in Taz, seif katan 3, we wrote that seven complete days are required. Now I saw in the Levush, it seems there, that seven complete days are not required, and it appears that words are missing there, and this is how it should be corrected there. Where one must wait seven days and the seventh day is Yom Tov, see Tosafot, Beitzah 6a, and Maharsha there, and our novellae there, that if the eighth day is the first day of Yom Tov it is forbidden, for at twilight it was muktzeh. One must distinguish this from what Tosafot wrote in Beitzah 1a, that retroactive muktzeh because of nefel is muktzeh; see Chemed Moshe. See what I wrote in the introduction to the laws of Yom Tov on this, and with Hashem's help it will be explained further in Kuntrus Acharon. The same applies where the eighth day is the second day of Yom Tov in such a case, and it requires study.
