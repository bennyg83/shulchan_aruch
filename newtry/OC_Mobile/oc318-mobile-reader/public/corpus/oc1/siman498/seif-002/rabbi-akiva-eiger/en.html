Fundamentally it implies a shochet who prepared his knife before Yom Tov may not show his knife on Yom Tov to a scholar — must show due to the sage's honor as explicit in Levush — lest he find a defect and sharpen. By law meat may be eaten without a sage's viewing; the sage may waive his honor. Therefore regarding trefah concern we presume after shochet's check there is no defect — therefore no concern lest sage find defect; he will certainly not find defect. Requires study.
