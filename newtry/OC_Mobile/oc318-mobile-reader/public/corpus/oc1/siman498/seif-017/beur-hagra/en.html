Seif 17, he may bring in, etc. These two sections are the language of the Tur. So too in the Beis Yosef there, they distinguished between that case here and seif 16, as above. But the Rashba there distinguished that here it is only after the fact, like a spade inserted, while there it is even initially. The language of the Gemara is specifically like his words, for here it says 'he brought in' only to cover, while there it says 'he may bring in,' etc., and he makes, etc. So too the second answer of Tosafot there inclines this way.
