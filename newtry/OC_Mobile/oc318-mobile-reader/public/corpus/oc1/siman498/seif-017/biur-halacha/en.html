A person may bring in, etc. This means before Yom Tov. It is likewise explained above in siman 308, seif 38, that on Yom Tov itself ordinary earth and sand are muktzeh according to all opinions. This applies even according to Rabbi Shimon, as is evident from siman 308. Even on Shabbos, when we certainly rule like Rabbi Shimon that ordinary muktzeh is permitted, earth can nevertheless have the status of muktzeh.
