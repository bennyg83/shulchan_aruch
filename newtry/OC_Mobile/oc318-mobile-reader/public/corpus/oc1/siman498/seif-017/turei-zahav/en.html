And if it is soft earth. In the Tur he wrote on this that it needs neither crushing nor digging, and nothing was lacking except designation, implying that even if there is no stuck digging-stick there is no concern of digging here. And it appears that this means that that soft earth lies on the ground like a mound, and whatever is taken from there upward has no connection to digging; which is not so if the ground is level: even though it is soft earth, nevertheless whatever he takes from there is digging in that he takes, for a depression remains beneath it. And with this is resolved why in se'if 14 we need soft earth and a stuck digging-stick: there it deals with level ground; therefore there is no permission regarding digging except with a stick stuck from before Yom Tov. And Beis Yosef distinguished here between permanent ground and movable ground, and I do not recognize that distinction except in the way I mentioned.
