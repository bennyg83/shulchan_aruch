[17] It is permitted to slaughter it, etc. The Taz asked: how is this different from dovecote birds in siman 497, seif 10, where designation is required lest he find them lean? This is no difficulty, for here it had a presumption of being kosher, unlike there, where it has no presumption of being fat. However, the Rashal, chapter 4, siman 19, and the later authorities prohibit. Likewise, in a place where treifos are as common as kosher animals, it is forbidden to slaughter on Yom Tov. So explained Raavan, page 77.
