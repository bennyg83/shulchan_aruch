(Seif katan 4) One may not, etc., and according to those who are lenient, etc. Meaning, those who permit muktzeh on Yom Tov.
Yam Shel Shlomo wrote that according to all opinions it is forbidden, for at the end of tractate Beitzah there are three explanations of Rabbi's view, and according to one explanation, wilderness animals are like dried figs and raisins, where Rabbi Shimon agrees that they are forbidden. He ruled stringently like that explanation.
