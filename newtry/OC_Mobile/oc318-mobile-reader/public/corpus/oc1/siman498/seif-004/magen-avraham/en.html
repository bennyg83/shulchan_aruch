On the second day. I found written in the margin of the Shulchan Aruch of the Shach: it is a scribal error and should read, on Yom Tov, for in the Gemara and in all the poskim they wrote plainly that it is permitted, implying even on the first day of Yom Tov. Furthermore, Beis Yosef wrote this in the name of the Rashba, and the Rashba in Avodas HaKodesh, page 45b, did not write second day of Yom Tov, but wrote plainly. These are the words of the Shach. His words are true according to the Rashba, but the Shulchan Aruch, which rules like the view that we are concerned, stringently, that perhaps they lodged outside the wall, as written in siman 515, seif 7, holds that on the first day of Yom Tov it is forbidden, but on the second day of Yom Tov it is permitted, as written in siman 497, seif 4. Orchos Chaim, brought by Beis Yosef, also holds this way, and so too in Yam Shel Shlomo.
