Taz, seif katan 1. And further, what advantage is there that he brought them? This is not understood, for if he brought them in on the first day, they are permitted on the second day after the time needed to do it, according to the Mechaber at the beginning of siman 515. Although one who does labor for a Jew on the first day of Yom Tov is permitted on the evening of the second day of Yom Tov only after the time needed to do it, even for us, nevertheless regarding techumin we hold this way, as the Rama wrote there, seif 5.
