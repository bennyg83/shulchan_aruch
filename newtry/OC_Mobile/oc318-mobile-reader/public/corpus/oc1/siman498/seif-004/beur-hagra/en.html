Seif 4, animals, etc. See Magen Avraham in the name of the Shach. He wrote well that the Rashba follows his own view, as written in seif 3, that in its doubt we follow leniency. He explains the Mishnah at the end of Perek Sho'el, that we are concerned lest, etc., like Rashi's explanation, and this is what he wrote here, lest from the evening, etc., and outside the wall, etc.
On the second day. This is a scribal error, and likewise it is not found there in Sefer Avodas HaKodesh, as the Shach wrote, cited by the Magen Avraham. What the Magen Avraham wrote, that Orchos Chaim and the Shulchan Aruch follow their view, for they hold that a doubt of outside the techum is ruled stringently, etc., is mistaken, for Orchos Chaim and the Shulchan Aruch copied the words of the Rashba verbatim, and he wrote in seif 3 that with muktzeh we follow its doubt leniently. Although the Shulchan Aruch contradicts what he wrote in siman 515, this is of no consequence, as is known.
