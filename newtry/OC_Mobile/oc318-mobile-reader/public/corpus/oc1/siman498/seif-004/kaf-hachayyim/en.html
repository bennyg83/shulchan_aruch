A knife is not shown to a scholar, etc. If, however, a Torah scholar recently studied the laws of shechitah, his teacher gave him full authority and permission to slaughter, and he sharpened and prepared the knife on erev Yom Tov, but when he came to slaughter on Yom Tov he became uncertain about it, he may certainly show his knife to his teacher in such a case. Shulchan Gavoah, subsection 2. It appears that this does not apply only to his teacher; the same applies to anyone greater or more expert than he.
