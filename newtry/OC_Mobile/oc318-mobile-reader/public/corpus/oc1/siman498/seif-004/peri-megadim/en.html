In Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 12, he asked that with a healthy animal it does not help that he will eat an olive-bulk, for that is an artifice; unless he needs the meat on Yom Tov, it is permitted. See there and responsa of the Rashba, siman 681. See Yoreh Deah 39 regarding those who are stringent when the lung was lost inadvertently. Therefore, with a dangerously ill animal that is permitted, this means that they permitted slaughtering on Yom Tov because according to the Torah it is permitted, and they left it on its Torah law regarding slaughter. If he lives in a place where a slaughterer is available but he is not expert in lungs, and he has a dangerously ill animal, it requires study whether we permit him to slaughter and eat without examination because of financial loss, since if he waits it will die or the meat will spoil. See Yoreh Deah there.
