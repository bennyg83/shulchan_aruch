Marginal note: And that we are not concerned thus [elsewhere], the reason is truly because we hold that moist with moist has blending; and see Rosh Hashanah, folio 17b. However, in Chavos Yair, siman 104, he raised a difficulty from the Gemara in Makkos, folio 4a: three log that fell into the Great Sea, etc.; see there. But the Ritva in the name of the Ra'avad wrote there that specifically in seawater, which is salty, it does not mix well, which is not so regarding moist with moist in general.
