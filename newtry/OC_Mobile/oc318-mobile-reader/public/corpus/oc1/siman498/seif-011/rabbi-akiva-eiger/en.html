Magen Avraham, seif katan 13. And further, the Rama wrote. In my humble opinion this is not a proof, for one can say that nevertheless there must be time to skin and examine it. We say ho'il: if guests happen to come, he will skin, examine, and they will eat. But where there is no time, there is no ho'il. Rather, after he slaughtered by means of ho'il, it is forbidden to skin it as long as guests have not come and he does not want to eat, since there is no loss, and we do not permit by means of ho'il. Examine this.
