They may. In a place where they slaughter for the non-Jew and Jews buy meat from them, Shevut Yaakov in a responsum forbids slaughtering a non-Jew's animal on Yom Tov unless he bought from erev Yom Tov something of the animal's body itself, and they should acquire for a Jew a share in their bodies. And do not infer from the Shulchan Aruch, which wrote that there we forbid only by the law of muktzeh but not regarding slaughtering it on Yom Tov; and see what I wrote regarding the law of a firstborn, to remove its sanctity by sale to a non-Jew.
