[11] [Levush] And if there is time, etc. Meaning, it is known with certainty that there is time, not by estimation. In Tosafot Yom Tov, chapter 3 of Beitzah, he wondered about Bartenura, who explained 'he knows that there is time,' saying that the word 'knows,' which he added, is unnecessary. This is astonishing, for Rashi, page 25, and the Ran explained so; why did he ask on Bartenura? Regarding the main question, it appears to me that this is what it means: he must know clearly that there is time, not by estimation, as we say regarding slaughtering the majority visible to the eyes in Yoreh Deah, siman 21.
