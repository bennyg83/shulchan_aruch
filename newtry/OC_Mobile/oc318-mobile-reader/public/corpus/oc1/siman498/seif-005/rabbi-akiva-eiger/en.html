Seif 5: calf born on Yom Tov permitted if mother designated for eating. Apparently difficult in plain sugya: they said to Rav — what difference between this and calf, etc. — implies only Rav needs reason of prepared through mother; per those who permit chick, this reason unnecessary; if so per Tur siman 513 some permit chick born on Yom Tov and geonim forbid — see there. Tur should have brought permitters here for calf when mother designated to raise — those who permit chick; no need for prepared through mother when designated to raise. Sha'ar HaMelech challenged. It appears Tosafos' answer in sugya is necessary: they challenged what difference calf born from trefah — not answered that prepared to reach into animal's womb to slaughter; because R' Oshaya doubts on weekdays whether four signs kosher — they answered initially certainly not slaughter in womb — not worse than slaughter at night. Without their difficulty, our difficulty remains for R' Yehuda: chick permits itself in slaughter by birth qualifying signs — unlike calf born how permit itself — signs were fit before birth by reaching into womb. Therefore must say like Tosafos' answer: not that four signs kosher; R' Yehuda permits itself by slaughter through birth qualifying signs. Therefore must say not like other Tosafos answer that signs kosher but not prepared to slaughter in womb — if so from not challenging R' Yehuda must say even there permits itself because came initial permission to slaughter. But strained to judge for that reason. Since before birth no prohibition in his signs — only night hindered. Therefore Tur must hold like Tosafos' first answer on our difficulty: even R' Yehuda challenged on all sides; if we do not say signs kosher, for R' Yehuda no difficulty permitting itself unlike ruling that matter unresolved whether signs kosher — then even R' Yehuda needs prepared through mother. If automatically permitted because signs kosher side — prepared. End: nevertheless not prepared since initially cannot slaughter in womb like night.
