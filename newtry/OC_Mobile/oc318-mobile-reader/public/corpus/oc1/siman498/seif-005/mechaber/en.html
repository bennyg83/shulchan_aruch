A calf born on Yom Tov may be slaughtered if its mother is designated for eating and it is known with certainty that its months of gestation were completed. If Shabbat and Yom Tov are adjacent, one born on one may be slaughtered on the other.Rema: It is also required that it have stood on the ground, since we are concerned) lest an internal organ defect be found and it turn out the slaughter on Yom Tov was unnecessary — so it appears to me from the words of the Rosh and the Tur.: ואם היו שבת וי"ט סמוכים זה לזה נולד בזה מותר בזה:
