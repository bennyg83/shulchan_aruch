Magen Avraham, seif katan 11. It is permitted to slaughter it for a sick person. This means that if it is permitted for a healthy person raw, as we rule regarding one who squeezes for a sick person on Shabbos, that it is permitted for a healthy person raw, as above in siman 318, seif 2. So it is explicit in the Ran. But because of the permission for the sick person's eating, it is not called preparation for itself, for if so we should say the same with a chick. This is the intent of the Maggid Mishneh, chapter 2, halacha 1 of the laws of Yom Tov, unlike the Mishneh LaMelech, who left it requiring study.
