He cannot slaughter it. For it is muktzeh even according to Rabbi Shimon, for who says that a sage will attend to him?
It was born with its blemish. Since it was prepared at bein hashmashos incidentally to its mother, although it was rejected when it was born, there is no muktzeh for half of Shabbos, Beis Yosef. See siman 310, Bach, and Yoreh Deah, siman 310.
