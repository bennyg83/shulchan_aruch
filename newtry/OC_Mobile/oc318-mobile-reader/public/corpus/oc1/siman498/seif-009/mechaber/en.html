A firstborn animal in our time that cannot be slaughtered without a blemish — a scholar may not examine it on Yom Tov to determine whether it has a blemish. Even if he transgressed and examined it and found a blemish, he may not slaughter it. But if it was born with its blemish and he transgressed and examined it, it may be slaughtered on his ruling. If he examined the blemish before Yom Tov and saw it was a blemish warranting slaughter, he may investigate on Yom Tov whether the blemish arose on its own and thereby permit it. {Rama: Rema: If a firstborn fell into a pit, it is forbidden to lift it out since it cannot be slaughtered — rather one provides for it in its place.}
