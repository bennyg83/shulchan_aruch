One may not slaughter. According to those who are lenient at the end of siman 495, it is permitted to slaughter if they were not brought for him, Tur. Yam Shel Shlomo wrote that according to all opinions it is forbidden.
They are forbidden. This refers to a city whose majority is not non-Jews, for we say that presumably he brought them for a Jew.
A doubt is permitted. One must say this discusses a non-Jew who lives with him in the city and has animals available within the techum, for otherwise it is forbidden, as written in siman 515, seif 7. But the wording does not imply this; see Beis Yosef, and it requires study.
