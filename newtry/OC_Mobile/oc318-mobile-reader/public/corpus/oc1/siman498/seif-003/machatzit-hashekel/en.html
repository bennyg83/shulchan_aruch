(Seif katan 3) And although, etc. Since it is food, etc. Meaning, although with regard to preparatory acts, if they could have been done before Yom Tov, it is forbidden to do them on Yom Tov, as above in siman 495. Therefore the Ran wrote that this is food itself, and it is permitted even if it could have been done before Yom Tov.
And even according to those who forbid, etc. For they hold that one should be stringent even regarding food itself, if it could have been done before Yom Tov and would not have deteriorated at all, so that it is forbidden to do it on Yom Tov except through an alteration.
With regard to the prohibition of carrying, etc. See siman 518. These are the words of Yam Shel Shlomo, first chapter of Beitzah, siman 30: since we say that because carrying was permitted, etc., as Beis Hillel permitted taking out a Torah scroll and a lulav even when it was possible to take them out before Yom Tov. The main reason is that carrying is a weak melachah, etc. These are the words of Yam Shel Shlomo.
