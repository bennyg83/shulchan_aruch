not show a knife to a scholar on Yom Tov to determine whether it is fit for slaughter, lest it be found notched and he tell him it is forbidden to slaughter with it, and he will go and sharpen it' on a whetstone. A scholar who examined a knife for himself may lend it to others.Nowadays since every slaughterer examines his own knife, anyone wishing to slaughter on Yom Tov should examine his knife before Yom Tov and not on Yom Tov lest he sharpen it.
