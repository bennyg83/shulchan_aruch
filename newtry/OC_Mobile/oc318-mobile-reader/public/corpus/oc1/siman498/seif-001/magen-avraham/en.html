And a sage. The same applies that a sage may show it to another sage, for he knows to be careful not to sharpen it, as implied in Maggid Mishneh.
And nowadays. Since Rama stated it generally, it implies that he holds that in our times there is no law of a Torah scholar, as written in several places. Nevertheless this requires study, why, since at least he knows to be careful not to sharpen it, for we find that nowadays we judge the law of a Torah scholar regarding several matters; see Choshen Mishpat, siman 2. Any person may inspect the knife after the slaughter, Beis Shmuel, and so it appears in Darchei Moshe in the name of Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah, unlike the Levush, who was uncertain about this.
