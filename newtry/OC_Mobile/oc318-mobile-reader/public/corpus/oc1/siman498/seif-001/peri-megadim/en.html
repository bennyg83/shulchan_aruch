The Taz holds that one can distinguish between an animal, where it is burdensome to go a long distance at night, and fruit, as written in Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 7. Therefore, even on the first day of Yom Tov, animals of a non-Jew in a city whose majority is Jewish are permitted in a doubtful case, for I say that presumably he brought them before Yom Tov and they lodged outside the wall; certainly this applies when they are slaughtered in the morning. What the Taz wrote regarding the second day of Yom Tov, what advantage is there over the evening, requires study, for in siman 515 it is explained that in such a case, if it was brought on the first day of Yom Tov, it is permitted on the second. See Taz there, seif katan 12, and with Hashem's help it will be explained further in the Magen Avraham.
