To pluck the wool. For this uproots something from its growth. Although it is in an unusual manner, since the usual way to remove wool is not by plucking but by shearing, nevertheless it is forbidden at least D'Rabbanan.
With his hand. But not with a utensil, because it appears like shearing, Peri Megadim.
And if, etc. There is no prohibition in it, for it is a case of davar she'eino miskaven; he does not intend to pluck, but only to clear away.
It was detached. He should not move it from its place, but it should remain there entangled with the rest of the wool of the neck. If the wool on the neck was entangled so that it is impossible to clear it, and one may not slaughter that way, as explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 28, seif 8 in the gloss, and he could not do this before Yom Tov, one may say that it is permitted to do so on Yom Tov, Peri Megadim.
