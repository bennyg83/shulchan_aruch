(Seif katan 12) In order to eat, etc. As written in Yoreh Deah, etc., because it is written, You shall not eat over the blood, and Chazal expounded that one may not eat from an animal before its life has departed.
Even if he does not eat from it, it is permitted, for we rule like Rabbah, that one who bakes from Yom Tov for a weekday does not receive lashes, since if guests happen to come it is fit for him on Yom Tov as well. The same applies here. Although because of the reasoning of ho'il, while he does not receive lashes there is nevertheless a D'Rabbanan prohibition initially, here, because of monetary loss, since it is dangerously ill, the Sages permitted it even initially. So it is in Pesachim 46b.
