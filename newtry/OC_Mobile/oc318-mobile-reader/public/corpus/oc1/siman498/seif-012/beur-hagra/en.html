Seif 12, one who slaughters an animal, etc. Bechoros 24 to 25: they asked Rav Huna: what is the law regarding its corresponding case on Yom Tov, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. vehainu, etc., until: and if you should say that if plucking, etc. The Rambam permits with an animal and not with a bird, while the Ramban permits even with a bird, since its end is to be pushed aside. See Magen Avraham.
