It is possible that even if there is no hot coal, see Rashi Beitzah 8a, but only somewhat hot ash, and in Beitzah 34, anything fit to warm and heat a cold drink and the like still remains in its first function and is not nolad. If it was burned on Yom Tov and cooled and was reheated, see siman 507 in Magen Avraham seif katan 7 and siman 509, letter 15, that it is permitted, as in siman 310, seif 3. Although one could say that there is no muktzeh for half of Shabbos, still there is nolad for half of Shabbos, and one should not distinguish. According to his view, perhaps the version 'to cover with ash' is for pastry, but according to the version 'to bake pastry,' one may say that nolad is forbidden even though it was reheated afterward. Nevertheless, since the Magen Avraham permits it, one can answer him; there it speaks of muktzeh for food preparation, and siman 310 permits moving, not use. Therefore it is forbidden to slaughter initially, for he is using muktzeh. With Hashem's help it will be explained further. See Eliyah Rabbah here, who brought the Maggid Mishneh.
