It is permitted to cover with it. This means even to slaughter initially relying on this, for it is prepared. Since there was ash from yesterday, his mind is on it for anything he needs.
Forbidden. Even according to those who permit muktzeh, and even nolad, nevertheless with complete nolad such as this, which was initially wood and now ash, everyone agrees that it is forbidden to move. If he burned wood today on top of yesterday's ash and the ash became mixed with yesterday's ash, in a manner that it was never recognizable separately without mixture, so that the rule of a davar sheyesh lo matirin not being nullified even in a thousand does not apply, it is nullified in the majority.
Unless it is hot and fit, etc. Yesterday, while it was wood, it was prepared for burning, cooking, and roasting, and it remains in that use. Since it is fit to turn something with it for roasting an egg, he may also take it and cover the blood with it.
Although it is not fit. For the Sages permitted muktzeh after the fact so as not to nullify the mitzvah of covering.
Nevertheless it is preferable, etc. A spade that was inserted before Yom Tov for this purpose has no prohibition of muktzeh; there is only making a pit, which is only destructive in a place where he does not need the pit. He holds that this is lighter than the prohibition of muktzeh. However, several later authorities disagree with this gloss and hold the opposite: it is better to cover with muktzeh ash, which is only mere moving, and not to lift the earth with the spade, by which he makes a pit with his hands.
