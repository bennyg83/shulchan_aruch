Forbidden. If he lit wood upon yesterday's ash and today's ash became mixed with yesterday's ash in a manner that it was never recognizable, it is nullified in the majority, since the essence of its prohibition is from the words of the Sages. Magen Avraham; see there.
Hot. From here we learn that it is permitted to cover with hot ash, unlike some who err and forbid covering with hot ash. Taz.
