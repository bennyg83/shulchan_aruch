[15] Some say may not skin etc. Proper to rule (Rama); Levush omitted — seems first reason Rashba, Ran, Beit Yosef; 'some say' is Riaz in Shiltei Giborim — minority, against mishnah bringing limbs; Magen Avraham challenged from Yerushalmi, answer forced — investigate. It appears to me Riaz only spoke for R' Akiva who permits even without time to cut though some prohibition; therefore permitted skinning; mishnah and Yerushalmi above refer to first tanna; Rama and later authorities did not delve — left me room.
