[15] And some say that one may not skin it, etc. So it is proper to rule. Rama. The Levush omitted it, apparently because the first opinion is the Rashba, Ran, and Beis Yosef, while the 'some say' is Riaz in Shiltei Giborim and is an individual view. Furthermore, it is against the Mishnah, which says he may bring it limb by limb. So the Magen Avraham also challenged from the Yerushalmi, and his answer is forced to one who examines it. In my humble opinion, it appears that even the Riaz said this only according to Rabbi Akiva, who permits even when there is not time to cut it up. Although there is some prohibition, nevertheless he holds that there is also a prohibition in skinning on Yom Tov, and therefore he permitted only that. But according to the first Tanna, who holds that there must be time, he holds that it is permitted. The Mishnah and Yerushalmi mentioned above follow the first Tanna. The Rama and all later authorities did not arrive at this, and they left me room.
