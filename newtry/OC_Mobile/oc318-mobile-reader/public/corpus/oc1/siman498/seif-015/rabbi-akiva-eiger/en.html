Pri Chadash, letter 9 If it was born unblemished and on that day a blemish fell upon it. Maharsha wrote in the name of Tosafot Yeshanim that this is specifically where it was born with its blemish; otherwise not, for a rejection like this, where he certainly did not have it in mind, is considered dichuy according to everyone.
Seif 15 That it is fit to roast an egg in it. This is puzzling to me from what we rule below in siman 502 seif 3, that one may not support a pot with a piece of wood. See Magen Avraham there: even according to the one who permits regarding muktzeh, nevertheless wood is muktzeh for all uses outside of burning. If so, what does it help that it is fit to roast in it? It is like the wood itself. The wood was also muktzeh for everything, and if so, what we want to use the ash for other things, with the law of a utensil, should be like nolad; this requires great study.
