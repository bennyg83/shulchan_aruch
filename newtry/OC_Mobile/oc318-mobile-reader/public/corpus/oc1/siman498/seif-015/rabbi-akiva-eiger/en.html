Pri Chadash, letter 9. If it was born unblemished and on that same day a blemish fell in it. The Maharsha wrote in the name of Tosafot Yeshanim that this applies specifically when it was born with its blemish. Otherwise, no, for such rejection, where his mind was certainly not on it, is considered rejection according to everyone.
Seif 15. That it is fit to roast an egg with it. This is astonishing to me from what we rule below in siman 502, seif 3, that one may not support a pot with a log. See the Magen Avraham there, that even according to one who permits muktzeh, nevertheless wood is muktzeh for all uses outside of burning. If so, what does it help that it is fit to roast with, for it is like the wood itself? The wood was also muktzeh for everything else. Therefore, when we want to use the ash for other things like a utensil, it should be like nolad. This requires great study.
