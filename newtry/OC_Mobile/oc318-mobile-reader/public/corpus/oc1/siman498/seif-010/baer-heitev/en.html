And he employs a device. Because of tzaar baalei chaim they permitted acting by means of a device, but in any event he must slaughter one of them. It implies that if animals that are not a mother and its offspring fell in, it is permitted to raise them even though he does not slaughter one of them, since all of them are fit for slaughter. So it also appears from seif 9 in the gloss, but this requires study. Magen Avraham.
