They may. See Taz. The Beis Yosef permitted where he wants only half an animal, even if the non-Jew wants to give him the larger and fatter one. If he wants the entire animal, then both must be equal. The Taz forbids even when he wants only half an animal; see siman 506. One may say that there, there is no loss, only a small gain, whereas here there is loss. See Even HaOzer, seif katan 22, that he will refrain and not slaughter and will refrain from the joy of Yom Tov; see there.
