It and its offspring, etc. With ordinary animals that fell in, it is certainly permitted to bring them up, even if he does not slaughter any of them, since they are fit for slaughter and he may need them afterward. But with it and its offspring, one of them necessarily is not fit, and he is moving it without need; therefore he must use an artifice.
And he does not slaughter it. He finds a pretext for it, perhaps the other is fatter than it; they permitted this artifice because of tzaar baalei chaim and because of financial loss.
If he wished, he slaughters this one. This implies that in any event he is obligated to slaughter one of them. However, many poskim brought in the name of the Yerushalmi that if afterward he wants not to slaughter either one, permission is also in his hand, for the bringing up was in any event done permissibly. Nevertheless, the Avodas HaKodesh of the Rashba wrote that it is proper to be concerned and be stringent to slaughter one of them, so that the artifice should not be evident.
