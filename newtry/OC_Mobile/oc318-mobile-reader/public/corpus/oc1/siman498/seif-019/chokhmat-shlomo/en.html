(There se'if 19) If he slaughtered a domestic animal and a wild animal and their blood mixed, etc. Marginal note: See in Taz s.k. 21 what he wrote at the end in the name of Maharshal, and what the Taz raised against him. And behold, aside from what I already wondered about him in my works, I further wondered about him in the omissions in my work Mei Niddah, Hilchos Mikvaos, from tractate Zevachim, folio 80, in the sugya of whether there is blending, and from tractate Makkos, folio 4, regarding barrels that fell into the Great Sea, and from the Rambam in chapter 6 of Mikvaos, halacha 10; see there. I further wondered at the Taz: what proof is there from mixtures? Behold dry with dry is also nullified; see there where I elaborated on this, and examine carefully. And see further in my work on Orach Chaim of the year 5612, folio 79, in Hilchos Pesach, siman 448, in my responsum to the holy community of Jaworow, in what I wrote to resolve the Taz's difficulty on Maharshal; see there and examine carefully. And see in my work on Yoreh De'ah, edition 5, siman 193, what I wrote as a proof for Maharshal's words from the Gemara in Chullin, chapter Gid HaNasheh, folio 98b; see there and examine carefully.
