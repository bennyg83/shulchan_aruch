And it became mixed. In a manner in which it requires covering; see Yoreh Deah, siman 28, seifim 13 and 14.
He should cover it. The same applies with a spade inserted in loose earth: it is permitted with one thrust, for he does not trouble himself more because of the animal. Bach; Magen Avraham. See the Taz at length. If he slaughtered in the middle of the courtyard, he may cover even with several thrusts in prepared earth, as written at the end of seif 18.
He should not cover it. Even with one thrust, because he is troubling himself for the animal. Tur; Yam Shel Shlomo; Magen Avraham. The Taz concludes that he should always cover it with one thrust; see there.
