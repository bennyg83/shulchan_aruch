If he slaughtered a domestic animal and a wild animal. The same applies to a koy and a wild animal.
And their blood became mixed. The blood of the wild animal was greater, enough that if the domestic animal's blood were water, the redness of the wild animal's blood would be recognizable; in such a case, on a weekday he is obligated to cover it, as explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 28.
With one thrust. This is not precise; the meaning is that he need not increase the moving of earth because of the added blood of the domestic animal, as he concludes.
He covers it. The same applies if he had a spade inserted before Yom Tov in loose earth; it is also permitted with one thrust, for he does not exert himself more because of the domestic animal. Know further that according to what was explained above in seif 15, if he has ash from a stove that was fired before Yom Tov, one may also permit covering with it in this case.
But if he can, etc. Meaning, where he would need to exert himself separately for the blood of the domestic animal, he should not do so.
