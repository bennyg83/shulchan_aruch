19) There, in the gloss. And not on Yom Tov, etc. If the shochet appointed for the community did not inspect the knife on erev Yom Tov, he may inspect it on Yom Tov. There is no concern that he might find it nicked and sharpen it. This concern was stated only in earlier times, when the butchers themselves performed the slaughter. Now that designated people are appointed, they themselves stand in the place of the scholar, and regarding a scholar himself we do not suspect that he might sharpen it.
