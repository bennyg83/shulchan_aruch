(Seif katan 19) It was born, etc. See siman 310. Meaning, there it is explained that we rule that there is no muktzeh for half of Shabbos. And see Bach and Yoreh Deah. Meaning, Rabbi Meir and Rabbi Yehudah dispute whether, if a firstborn was slaughtered and its blemish was not shown to a sage before the slaughter, and after the slaughter the sage saw that it was a permanent blemish that was not liable to change after slaughter, nevertheless Darchei Moshe forbids it, as above, because he decrees on account of blemishes that are liable to change after slaughter. Rabbi Yehudah permits, for he holds that we do not decree. The Bach brought in the name of Tosafot and Agudah that we rule in this like Rabbi Yehudah. To this Magen Avraham alluded that one should see Yoreh Deah, for there it is explained that we rule like the poskim who follow Rabbi Meir, who holds that this is called a decree. The penalty that Abaye mentioned is not precise, and we rule that the halacha follows Rabbi Meir in his decrees.
