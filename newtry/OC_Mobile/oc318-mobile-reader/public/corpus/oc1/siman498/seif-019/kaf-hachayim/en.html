(יט) In the gloss: not on Yom Tov etc.; the communal slaughterer — if he did not check the knife erev Yom Tov he may check on Yom Tov, and we are not concerned lest he find a flaw and sharpen, for we say so only in their days when the tavchim slaughtered themselves; but now that appointed known people are in place, they themselves stand in the sage's place, and for the sage himself we are not concerned lest he sharpen.
