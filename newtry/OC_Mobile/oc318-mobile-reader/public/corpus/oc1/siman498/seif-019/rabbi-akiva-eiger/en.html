Magen Avraham, seif katan 28. It is called liquid in liquid and requires sixty. In my humble opinion, the reason sixty is required with liquid in liquid is that with unlike kinds it gives taste, and they decreed like unlike kinds. That applies to matters of eating, but here this does not apply. For this reason, the Tosafos Yom Tov in Kilayim, chapter 9, mishnah 1, s.v. if, is difficult to me, regarding dry items of the same kind, etc.; there the concept of taste as primary does not apply, and even with unlike kinds it is nullified in the majority.
