1) [Se'if 1] To sweep the house in accordance with the law on Shabbos, etc. It appears that the custom is lenient and people sweep the house. This may be because some permit sweeping even on Shabbos, and certainly in our houses, whose floors are paved with stones or boards, where leveling depressions is not applicable. They rely upon this to permit it. Shulchan Aruch, however, writes that it is forbidden. Chayei Adam, rule 93, subsection 3. Chayei Adam's statement that Shulchan Aruch forbids it means Rama in the gloss, who refers to siman 337. There Rama writes: 'Some are stringent even on a paved floor, and this is the custom and should not be changed.' According to Shulchan Aruch itself, however, when the ground is paved, sweeping is permitted even on Shabbos. See there and our words there, with Heaven's help.
