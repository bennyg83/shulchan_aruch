1) [Se'if 1] Laundry may not be washed during the festival. This is forbidden even when needed for the festival. The reason resembles the prohibition against removing hair: one should wash his clothing before Yom Tov and not enter the festival unkempt. Tur and Beis Yosef; Levush; Magen Avraham, subsection 1. This does not mean that he must wash the clothing specifically on erev Yom Tov. On the contrary, the earlier he washes it, the better. Rather, he must have freshly laundered clothing prepared for Yom Tov. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1.
2) There. The following may wash: one who returns from overseas, etc. See above, siman 531, se'ifim 3 and 4. All the laws explained there regarding removing hair apply here as well to washing clothing. All those permitted to wash may do so specifically when they have no other garment. Shulchan Shlomo; Tosafos Chaim on Chayei Adam, rule 110, subsection 1.
3) There. And one under a ban whose ban was released, etc. While he remains under the ban, he is forbidden to cut his hair or wash clothing, as stated in Moed Katan 15a. Tur and Levush write likewise. This is also stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 334, se'if 2. See there.
