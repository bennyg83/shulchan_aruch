1) [Se'if 1] Laundry may not be washed during the festival. This is forbidden even when needed for the festival. The reason resembles the prohibition against removing hair: one should wash his clothing before Yom Tov and not enter the festival unkempt. Tur and Beis Yosef; Levush; Magen Avraham, subsection 1. This does not mean that he must wash the clothing specifically on erev Yom Tov. On the contrary, the earlier he washes it, the better. Rather, he must have freshly laundered clothing prepared for Yom Tov. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1.
