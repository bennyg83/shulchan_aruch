Se'if 2: and they did not practice thus. For they hold as the Mordechai and Haggahos Maimoniyos wrote in the name of Maharam, that the law is not like Rabbi Yochanan, for Abaye and Rava do not hold like him. And even though the poskim dispute them, for it is not compelling that they dispute, nevertheless these are [like] "things [that are permitted]," etc., as is written in chapter 4 of Pesachim.
