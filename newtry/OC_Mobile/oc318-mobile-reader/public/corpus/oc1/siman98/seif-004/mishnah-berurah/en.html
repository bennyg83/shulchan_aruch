(10) Between him and the wall — Tur also wrote "between him and the ground." Taz wrote: specifically three tefachim; but less than three tefachim even a thin board is not interposition, though some forbid even less than three. Nevertheless all agree it is permitted on Yom Kippur to spread dry grass on the ground and stand on it to pray, for it is nullified relative to the ground. It is forbidden to pray in a pool.
