And that nothing should be interposed between him and the wall, and similarly that nothing should be interposed between him and the ground, analogous to a sacrifice (Tur). Nevertheless, there is no impediment in the matter; he did not say that nothing should be interposed between him and the ground except as an optimal mitzvah. And so they are accustomed to spread dry grass on the ground in the synagogue, and similarly they are accustomed to spread mats on the Ninth of Av and stand on them and pray (my teacher in Bach).
