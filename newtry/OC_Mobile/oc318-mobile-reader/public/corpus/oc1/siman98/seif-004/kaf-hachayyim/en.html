(ד) Chida wrote in Simchat HaRegel page 17 that the prayers arranged in the order of Kedushah and Rachatz per the intention of the Ari z"l should not be said — even kabbalists do not say them — for apart from printing errors, Ramaz also protested against expressing intention in the manner of prayer, etc.; see there. Also see in his book Yosef Ometz siman 44, where he brought from Sefer Igrot HaRamaz siman 6 page 4 side 1, who wrote: it is not right in my eyes at all regarding the prayers that Maharsha"z corrected — first, that many intentions depend only on thought and not on expression of the lips; also that the main intention is at the moment of performing the mitzvah, such as at the moment of blowing the shofar; if so, how and in what way will one first awaken the higher powers and lights before performing the main action for them; also that there are many names within the prayers — if one mentions them with his mouth, his sin is too great to bear, and if only with intention in them, what use is prayer in expression; the main thing is that the names be in thought alone — this and more, etc. I also heard from Maharash Sharabi before Sefer Igrot HaRamaz was printed that he has more than one reason that Ramaz wrote clear proofs from the foundation of the gratitude of our Rabbi the Ari z"l. I also saw responsum 1 manuscript to the rabbi as Maharaba, and he agreed through Maharar Yano"n to prevent in the manner of prayer, etc., and they expanded in the matter of damages that follows from this; see Beit David HaZaken part 3 in the laws of Pesach end of letter 107 and letter 108.
