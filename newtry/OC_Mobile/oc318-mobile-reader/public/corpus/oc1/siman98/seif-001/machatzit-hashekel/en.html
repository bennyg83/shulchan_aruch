He wrote in Sefer HaGan, etc., and it does not seem right to do this within the Amidah, etc. In Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he brought in the name of Kitzur Shelah, end quote: A segulah for concentration to remove foreign thoughts — before prayer he passes his right hand three times over his forehead and each time says: "A pure heart create for me, O God, and a steadfast spirit renew within me." Likewise if a foreign thought comes during prayer, he should be silent a little, pass his right hand over his forehead, and meditate on that verse, end quote.

(Note 1) His young sons, etc. — those who bring children to beit haknesset, meaning small ones who have not yet reached the age of education. The reason: children play and dance in beit haknesset and profane its sanctity, and also confuse the minds of those praying; moreover when they grow old they will not depart from the bad conduct they were trained in youth to despise the sanctity of beit haknesset. But when they reach the age of education, on the contrary one should bring him along to beit haknesset and teach him proper conduct to sit in awe and fear, not let him move from his place, and urge him to answer amen, Kaddish, and Kedushah — see there at the end of the topics of prayer and Torah reading.
