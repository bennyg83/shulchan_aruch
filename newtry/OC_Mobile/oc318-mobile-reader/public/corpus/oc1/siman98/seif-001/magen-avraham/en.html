It is written in Sefer HaGan: to cancel a bad thought during prayer one says three times "pei pei pei," then spits three times — not a full spit, but gently, with the tongue between the lips when spitting — and certainly the thought will depart, end quote. It does not seem right to do this within the Amidah, for it is an interruption, and who knows if it is a tested remedy. The Zohar (Parashat Beshalach p. 115) wrote: Every person who comes to unify the Holy Name and does not intend it in his heart with desire and awe so that the upper and lower worlds be blessed through him — they cast his prayer outside and all proclaim about him "vile," and the Holy One, blessed be He, calls to him "when you come to see My face," etc., end quote. Therefore it seems that one who does not know himself that he can fulfill this should not intend at all in names and unifications, but pray in the simple sense to understand the words with heart intention. His young sons — see Shelah, who protests against those who bring children to beit haknesset, etc.; see there, his words are correct.
