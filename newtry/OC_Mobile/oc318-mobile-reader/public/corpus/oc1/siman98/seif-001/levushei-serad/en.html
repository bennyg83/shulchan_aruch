(Taz, note 1) Like kohanim — who would walk on the ramp heel beside toe. Beit Yosef asked why he brings this here, for we rule in our Gemara like angels, meaning one beside the other (zeh im zeh). Taz answered that from the Yerushalmi we learn that the intention of angels is one beside the other; since one opinion says like kohanim — heel beside toe — therefore like angels means one beside the other.
