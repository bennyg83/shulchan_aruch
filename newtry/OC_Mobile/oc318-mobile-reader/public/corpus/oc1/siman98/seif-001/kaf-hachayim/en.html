(1) [Seif 1] One who prays must intend in his heart the meaning of the words, etc. Rambam wrote in the Guide regarding prayer, end quote: If when you pray with movement of your lips and turn your face to the wall while thinking of your buying and selling you consider that you prayed — you are then close to one of whom it is said "with their mouth they draw near to Me but their heart is far from Me," end quote. Be'er Heitev in Darkei Moshe, note 1, and the Zohar (Parashat Metzora p. 55a), end quote: What is "without lips of deceit"? Thus we learned: every word of prayer that a person brings forth from his mouth ascends on high, breaks through the heavens, enters the place it enters, and there that word is tested whether it is a fitting word or not; if it is fitting it enters before the Holy King to do His will, and if not they cast it outside and another spirit is aroused through that word, end quote. The Rav wrote in Be'er Heitev gate 40 ch. 2, and Be'er Heitev above siman 60 note 7, that prayer without intention is like a body without a soul — see there. And although the Rav speaks of intention in the Divine names and sefirot, nevertheless intention in the meaning of the words also makes some impression; moreover without intention in the meaning of the words, but thinking other thoughts, the prayer does not emerge pure and clean but is full of rust, good and evil mixed, from the foreign thought placed in it contrary to what must be done for it as soul and to raise it like a bride before her groom before He who created everything and examines the secrets of the heart. See also what I wrote above siman 95 note 11 and below note 5.
