In Shulchan Aruch, seif 3: Like a poor man who asks, etc. — see above in Shaarei Teshuvah what the disciples of the Ari wrote: when he reaches "Helper of the poor" he should think in his heart that he is poor and asking at the door, even if he is wealthy, etc.; and at "Hear our voice" he should confess and set himself to complete being a chassid, and if he wishes to set himself as a servant. Shaarei Tzion wrote that in Mordechai it is written in the name of the later authorities that if he did not pray in a supplicant manner he repeats the prayer — even though for lack of concentration one does not repeat, as stated in siman 101; there the reason is that it is likely he will also not concentrate on repetition, unlike here where saying the prayer in supplicant manner is easy and he can say it again in supplicant manner on repetition.
