In his heart. He wrote in Sefer HaGan that to nullify a bad thought during prayer one should say three times "pi pi pi," and afterward spit three times; he should not spit fully but only downward, and the tongue should be between the lips at the time of spitting, and certainly the thought will depart thereby. And Magen Avraham wrote on this that it does not seem proper to do this in the middle of the Shemoneh Esreh, for it constitutes an interruption, and who knows whether it is a proven remedy.
