One must have intention in his prayer. It contains 5 seifim: One who prays must concentrate in his heart on the meaning of the words he utters with his lips, and should think as though the Divine Presence stands before him. He should remove all distracting thoughts until his thought and intention in prayer are pure. He should consider that if he were speaking before a flesh-and-blood king, he would arrange his words and concentrate on them carefully so as not to stumble; all the more so before the King of kings, the Holy One, blessed is He, who examines all thoughts. This is how the pious and men of deeds would conduct themselves: they would seclude themselves and concentrate on their prayer until they reached a state of detachment from corporeality and strengthening of the intellectual faculty, approaching the level of prophecy. If another thought comes to him during prayer, he should remain silent until the thought passes. He should think of matters that humble the heart and direct it toward his Father in Heaven, and should not think of matters involving frivolity. {Rama: Before prayer he should contemplate the exaltedness of God and the lowliness of man, and remove all human pleasures from his heart. [Rabbeinu Yonah, beginning of chapter Ein Omdin] A person may not kiss his young children in the synagogue, in order to establish in his heart that there is no love like the love of the Omnipresent, blessed is He. [Binyamin Ze'ev, siman 163; Agudah, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin]}
