Sefer Chassidim writes: Do not hasten to bring your words quickly out of your mouth. If you desire livelihood, do not direct your heart only to that blessing, Bircas HaShanim; and if someone is ill, do not direct it only to the blessing for the sick. In Heaven they may say of you, “This person thinks he needs only this.” Therefore, concentrate on all the blessings and do not direct your heart only to the requests, etc.
