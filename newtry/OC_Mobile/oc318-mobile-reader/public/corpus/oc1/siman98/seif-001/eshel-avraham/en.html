And remove [all distracting thoughts]. See Rashbam responsa siman 193 — many divisions and warnings on this matter, and what applies between Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur. Sefer Chassidim wrote: Do not hurry to bring your words out of your mouth faster than your mouth [can form them]; and if you seek livelihood, do not set your heart only on that blessing — for the blessing of the years, or if he has an illness only on the blessing for the sick — because they say above: “This person thinks he needs only this.” Therefore intend all the blessings, and do not set your heart only on requests, etc., end quote.
