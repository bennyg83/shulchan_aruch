(1) One who prays, etc. — Berachos 30b "l'olam," etc.; 31a; Tanna rabbanan, etc.; and Hannah, etc.

And he should think, etc. — Yoma 52b; and see Rashi there s.v. "l's'mol," etc.; Sanhedrin 22a.

And he should remove, etc. — Eruvin 65a.

And he should think, etc. — there 64, regarding a drunk person, all, etc.

And he must think, etc. — as written "ein omadim," etc., they wait an hour.

And he should think beforehand, etc. — there they wait, etc., in order, etc.; and Terumas HaDeshen — it does not mean literally in prayer, for if so it should have said "in prayer"; rather that their heart should be whole to the Omnipresent, that he should think of the exaltedness of the Omnipresent, etc., and he should not speak, etc.; and on this they said there "a sign to the matter, 'Prepare,'" etc., and this refers to the purity of a person from worldly pleasures, which requires assistance from the Omnipresent, as written "and direct their hearts to You," and therefore it says "a sign to the matter" and did not say "proof," because the plain meaning of the verse is not about the intent of prayer, but the end of the verse "hear," etc., teaches this.

And it is forbidden, etc., in order, etc. — see Zohar 1:132b and Tikkunei Zohar, for they established "one should not pray," etc., because, etc., "he should not place," etc., in his name.
