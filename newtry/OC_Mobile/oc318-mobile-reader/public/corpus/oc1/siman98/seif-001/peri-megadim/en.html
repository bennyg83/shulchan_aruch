And standing. See Taz and siman 95 in Taz. The Tur understands our Gemara as referring to angels and cites the Yerushalmi to clarify what its intent is. What the Tur writes here, “like kohanim,” means that prayer and the Temple service are presumably performed in the same manner. Since in prayer one stands like the angels, the same applies in the Temple service. The Tur lists here the respects in which prayer resembles the Temple service. In fact, Rambam, Hilchos Bias HaMikdash 5:16, says nothing about kohanim being required to align their feet during the service. The Tur's statement that prayer must be standing, similar to the Temple service, also excludes walking during prayer, even though we do not find that walking during the Temple service is forbidden, but only that sitting renders the service invalid.
