And standing — Ateret Zekenim on siman 95, Taz: for Tur holds in our Gemara like angels, and brought the Yerushalmi to clarify his intention; and what is written here "like kohanim" means that presumably prayer and service are one kind — in prayer like angels, likewise in service — and Tur considers here that prayer equals service in all respects. In truth Rambam ch. 5 of the Temple laws brings from Makdishei Shevi'i 16 that he wrote nothing requiring kohanim to equalize their feet during service, unlike Tur's "standing like service." In prayer, walking too — even though in service we do not find it forbidden to walk, only improper posture is invalid — Ateret Zekenim siman 94 note 4, and what I wrote in Peri Megadim there.
