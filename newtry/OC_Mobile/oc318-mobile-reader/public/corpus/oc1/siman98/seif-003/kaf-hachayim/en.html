(3) He wrote in the Zohar (Parashat Beshalach p. 57a), end quote: Every person who comes to unify the Holy Name and does not intend it in his heart with desire and awe so that upper and lower worlds be blessed through him — they cast his prayer outside and all proclaim about him "vile," and the Holy One, blessed be He, calls to him "when you come to see My face," etc., end quote. Magen Avraham brought this at the beginning of this siman and wrote that one who does not know himself that he can fulfill this should not intend at all in names and unifications but pray simply to understand the words with heart intention, end quote. He brought Be'er Heitev note 1, Ateret Zekenim note 1 — and all the more one who does not know the order of intentions well, for it is forbidden for him to intend, as written in Parashat Terumah p. 178a at the end, end quote: What is "and despised in their eyes"? One who does not know to unify the Holy Name and bind the bond of faith and draw down to the place needed and honor his Master's Name — better for him that he not be created; and all the more one who does not intend in amen, end quote. Likewise Chida in Machazik Berachah siman 474 note 2, end quote: One without knowledge of Kabbalah should not say or mention or even meditate the secrets written in the siddur of the Ari, may his memory be blessed, or other abbreviations without understanding — he is closer to loss; and it is permitted for them from their Master for worshippers to use the Ari's siddurim without knowing and understanding, for if they intend something they err greatly; if not, it is forbidden due to arrogance and deceiving others, end quote. He brought in his book 483 siman 12 notes 12–13; Eliyah Rabbah wrote that names and unifications were recently printed and everyone handles them, and the sages of the generation must protest, end quote. Peri Megadim in Orach Chayyim note 1 brought this. However one who knows the order of intentions well, learned from scribes and books, and intends the whole order of prayer with joy and trembling without any foreign thought — his reward has no end, as written in the Zohar there in Beshalach and Terumah, and likewise Maharachav, may his memory be blessed, in Shaar HaKavanot end of drush 4 of the night drushim p. 54d — see there. See also our words above siman 61 note 28 and in Rav Pe'alim vol. 3, section Sod Yesharim siman 13 — examine carefully.
