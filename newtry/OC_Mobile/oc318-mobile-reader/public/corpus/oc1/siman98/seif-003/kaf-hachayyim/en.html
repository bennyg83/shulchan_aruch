(ג) He wrote in the Zohar parashat Beshalach page 57a: every person who comes to unify the holy Name and does not intend it in his heart — evil and fear — so that upper and lower be blessed through him — they cast his prayer outside and all proclaim about him "evil," and the Holy One, blessed be He, calls upon him "when you come to appear before Me," etc. Magen Avraham brought this at the beginning of this siman and wrote: one who does not know in himself that he can fulfill this should not intend at all in names and unifications, but only pray simply to understand the matters with intention of the heart — end of his words. He brought Beer Heitev letter 1, Alef-Reish letter 1; and all the more one who does not know the order of intentions well is forbidden to intend, as written in chapter Terumah 178a at the end: what is "and despised in the eyes" — this is one who does not know to unify the holy Name and to tie the knot of faith and to draw to the place needed and to honor the Name of his Master — it is good for him that he was not created; and all the more one who does not intend in Amen — end of citation. So too Chida in Machberet siman 474 letter 2: one who has no hand in Kabbalah should not say or mention or even contemplate the secrets written in the siddur of the Ari z"l or other abbreviations without understanding — he is closer to loss; and it is permitted for them from Heaven for worshipers from the siddurim of the Ari z"l who do not know and do not understand — if they intend something they err greatly; otherwise it is forbidden because of yohara and stealing knowledge — end of citation. He brought in his book Kaf HaChayyim siman 12 letter 12 and letter 13; and A.R. wrote that names and unifications were printed nearby and everyone handles them — the sages of the generation must protest — end of citation. Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham letter 1. However, one who knows the order of intentions well, who learned from scribes and from books and intends the entire order of prayer with joy and trembling without any extraneous thought — there is no end to his reward, as written in the Zohar there in parashat Beshalach and parashat Terumah; and so Maharchu z"l in Shaar HaKavanot end of derush 4 of derushei halailah page 54d — see there; and further see in our words above siman 61 letter 28 and in Teshuvot Rav Pa'alim part 3 section Sod Yesharim siman 13 — examine well.
