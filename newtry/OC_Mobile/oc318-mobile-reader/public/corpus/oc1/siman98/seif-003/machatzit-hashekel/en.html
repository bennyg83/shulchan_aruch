(Note 3) At a time, etc. — one who comes from the road; the Gemara states that according to one opinion one should not pray within three hours of coming from the road. For us, even though in any case we cannot concentrate so much in prayer, nevertheless where one can be careful one should be careful — such as where there is a strong smell and he can pray elsewhere; or if he comes from the road he should not pray while still exhausted from the road unless prayer time will pass in the meantime.
