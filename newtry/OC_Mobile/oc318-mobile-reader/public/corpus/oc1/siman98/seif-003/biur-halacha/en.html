He should pray in a manner of supplication, etc. — A person must be very careful about this, for several poskim require one to return and pray again on account of this; these are Bach and Eliyah Rabbah. And so it is proven from Beit Yosef and the article of Mordechai and Magen Giborim, who demonstrated from Rambam [chapter 4 of Hilchos Tefillah, halachah 16] that he also holds thus. In my opinion there is not so much proof, for in such a case what he said there — "therefore he must sit a little," etc. — is certainly only lekhatchilah; and so Peri Megadim wrote that one should be concerned about a blessing in vain, but one should not be stringent bedieved. In any case, lekhatchilah one should be very careful about this. Furthermore, it seems to me obvious that even according to Magen Avraham and Magen Giborim there is impediment only in the Avot blessings alone, and in the other blessings only lekhatchilah, for according to them the main reason is on account of intent, as they demonstrated from Rambam; if so, in the other blessings it is not an impediment bedieved.
