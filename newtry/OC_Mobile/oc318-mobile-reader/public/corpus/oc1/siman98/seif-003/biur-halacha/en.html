He should pray in a manner of supplication, etc. A person must be exceedingly careful about this, for several authorities require him to repeat the prayer if he failed to do so. These include the Bach and Eliyah Rabbah, and this also appears from the Beit Yosef. Maamar Mordechai and Magen Giborim demonstrate from the Rambam, Hilchos Tefillah 4:16, that he also holds this way. In my opinion, however, this is not a conclusive proof, for the Rambam's statement there, “Therefore, he must sit briefly, etc.,” is certainly only an ideal requirement. Peri Megadim likewise writes that one must consider the concern of a blessing in vain and should not be stringent after the fact. In any event, ideally one must be very careful about this. It also appears obvious to me that even according to Maamar Mordechai and Magen Giborim, this is indispensable only in the blessing of Avos, while in the other blessings it is only an ideal requirement. Their essential reason is concentration, as they demonstrate from the Rambam, and concentration in the other blessings is not indispensable after the fact.
