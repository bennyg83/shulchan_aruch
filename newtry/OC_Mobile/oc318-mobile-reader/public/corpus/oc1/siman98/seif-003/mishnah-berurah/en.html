(8) "Who asks at the door and gently" — this is one matter: he should speak gently in supplicant language like one seeking mercy for himself. He should bear in mind that no creature — angel, constellation, or star — can fulfill his request, only by His will, may He be blessed.

(9) As a burden — meaning: even though he speaks in supplicant language, if he does not think as he should — as one who comes to request before the King — but prays from obligation to discharge his duty, this is not correct and one must be very careful. Nevertheless bedieved, even if he did not speak in supplicant language, he does not repeat the prayer — see Biur Halacha.
