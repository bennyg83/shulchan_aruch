One should not think "It is fitting that the Holy One, blessed be He, should fulfill my request since I concentrated well during my prayer" — for on the contrary this recalls a person's sins (since by doing so they scrutinize his deeds to say he is confident in his merits). Rather one should think that the Holy One, blessed be He, will fulfill it in His kindness, and say in his heart: Who am I, poor and despised, to come request from the King of kings of kings, the Holy One, blessed be He, if not for His abundant kindness that He shows His creatures?
