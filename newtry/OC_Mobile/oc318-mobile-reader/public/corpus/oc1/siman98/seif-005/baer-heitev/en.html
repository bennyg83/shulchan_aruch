To the wall. In Tur he also wrote "and between him and the ground." And Taz wrote specifically three [tefachim], but in less than three tefachim there is not even a mitzvah. And so they are accustomed to spread dry grass on the ground on Yom Kippur, and similarly they spread mats on Tishah BeAv and stand on them and pray. Bach. It is forbidden to pray in a pool — see Lechem Kasir 1:94 in his name.
