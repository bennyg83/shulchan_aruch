To the wall. The Tur also writes, “and between him and the ground.” The Taz writes that this applies specifically when it is three tefachim high, but below three tefachim there is not even an enhancement of the mitzvah. Accordingly, the custom on Yom Kippur is to spread dry grasses on the ground, and the custom on Tisha B'Av is likewise to spread mats and stand upon them while praying. Bach. It is forbidden to pray in a pool. Halachos Ketanos, part I, siman 94; see there.
