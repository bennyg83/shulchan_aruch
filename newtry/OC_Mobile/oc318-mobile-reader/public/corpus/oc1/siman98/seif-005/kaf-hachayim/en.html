(5) There — "and remove all thoughts," etc. Or HaYashar, introduction 14, wrote that one who does not intend in prayer joins with the spirit of impurity and the Satan also enters among them — woe to him and woe to his soul, how much evil he causes himself; this applies to every person, and how much more to the prayer leader. The homileticians of remez explained that the shaliach tzibbur who passes before the ark must intend, for the initial letters of teivah spell "tefillah yesharah" (straight prayer) with heart intention — see there. They brought Rav Chaim Palagi in Kaf HaChayyim siman 11 note 5; he wrote in Chochmas Adam on Mishlei siman 77 that the test whether a person is righteous is when he intends well in his prayer without any [foreign] thought — see there; Kaf HaChayyim brought this there. Rav Batei Knessiyot brought that one who prays with heart intention is saved from chibut ha-kever — see there; Chida note 2 brought Kaf HaChayyim there. In Sefer HaMidot, entry "mamon" vol. 2 note 10, he wrote that one who fulfills "let your fellow's property be as dear to you as your own" thereby merits praying with heart intention — see there; Yafeh Lelev brought this note 2.
