And "nothing should separate," etc. — Ateret Zekenim holds less than three tefachim, even on vessels, is permitted; siman 90 from this that it is nullified relative to the ground. The reason given for three tefachim is height, not interposition on high ground like a mound — interposition does not apply to height; here he said one should also intend that his prayer resembles service. But the separation of two tefachim and Levush here imply that even on vessels less than three tefachim, if there is no concern of disgust, it is forbidden like service where interposition is forbidden. Nevertheless what they spread on Yom Kippur in beit haknesset is not interposition, for it is nullified; one could say a vessel that is significant is not nullified — see Bach. Pri Chadash said: if so it should be forbidden to pray except barefoot with shoes removed and socks on and pray; and it is forbidden to pray barefoot except on Tisha B'Av (siman 51, 65). He brought proof from the first chapter of Shabbos 2 in Rashi "from the kohanim" — there in truth it is forbidden in socks because they are extra garments, and Scripture says (Exodus 21:42) "from the belt to the thighs"; rather they only walk on stone flooring and did not place boards and the like so it would not be too cold. On this he wrote interposition on flooring is forbidden, unlike socks for us which are nullified to the body and are not interposition — Magen Avraham siman 76 note 16 that a sandal is nullified to the body, see there.
