(2) There — one must intend in his heart the meaning of the words, etc. — and likewise one must be careful with words, letters, and vowel points to pronounce them properly and with their correct form, not omitting anything from them. Siddur Nehora HaShalem, introduction 13 p. 14b, end quote: Therefore a person must be careful in every prayer, and habit becomes nature to pronounce fitting words in completeness without omitting anything, for it is known in the Zohar that on every letter and every vowel point hang many thousands of myriads of holy hidden upper worlds, as explained in Zohar (Parashat Acharei p. 73a), end quote: The first word of Torah given to children is alef-bet, etc. — see there: from this, when a person pronounces a word properly from his mouth he shakes all the worlds that depend on it; but when he does not give his mind and heart to feeling the pronunciation well he substitutes this word for another word not at all in the proper order of prayers and causes great damage, heaven forbid, in those holy worlds hanging on the letters of that word, as mentioned in the Zohar in many places that arousal below depends on arousal above. Therefore one should accustom himself to this; at the start of the habit he should do as written in Darkei Moshe — pronounce the words as if counting slowly until he feels himself that he certainly will not fail, and then he may speak the words even with some speed, end quote. See there where he brought a terrible incident about a famous chassid of his generation: after he died they wanted to punish him greatly because he was not careful to pronounce letters and points properly — see there. See also our words above siman 5. Therefore a person must always give his heart and mind — a fixed law at every time he brings forth letters and words from his mouth in Torah study or prayer — not to lose any letter from the beginning or end of words, for some letters are swallowed at word beginnings, some in the middle, and some at the end. People are also not equal in pronunciation: each has certain letters that do not come out well in his speech and require attention; speed necessarily causes loss of some letters without his knowing, etc. In particular one must be careful when bringing forth a name from the holy names — to pronounce it with trembling, holiness, purity, and great intention; one who comes to purify is assisted from heaven. May God grant him and all Israel to do His will and serve Him with a whole heart.
