(4) That negates — such as new beer that smells, and the like.

(5) At a time that negates — such as when his mind is unsettled from some distress, anger, or exertion of travel.

(6) "And nowadays," etc. — It is obvious that nevertheless when one wants to stand to pray from anger and the like he should first remove the thoughts that disturb him, as above in seif 1.

(7) "We are not," etc. — Peri Megadim wrote in the name of Levush: although we do not concentrate, nevertheless what can be done we do; therefore one should not pray in a house where there is new beer or honey-water, and all the more a slightly foul smell.
