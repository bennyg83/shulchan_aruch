(א) Rechavah behind houses — their custom was to leave empty space before houses and behind houses; that before houses is called chatzer and that behind houses is called rechavah; their custom was to encircle them with partitions; and behold the part called chatzer is certainly enclosed for dwelling since customary to do there all house uses; perforce even if more than beit se'ah and even ten korim permitted to move throughout and even from house into it [if one person or two who made eruv together] for one domain; but rechavah presumably not enclosed for dwelling since not customary to use there house uses so much; perforce its law is like karpef regarding more than beit se'ah [karpef is what they make far from the city — an enclosed place to store wood and the like]; and now we explain Shulchan Aruch's words rechavah behind, etc. — namely he enclosed this empty space with three partitions and attached them to his house wall:
(ב) And was not enclosed for dwelling — that he did not open in the wall an opening to the rechavah before enclosing:
(ג) Except within four amot — as law of other karpef not enclosed for dwelling as above siman 358 seif 1; and all the more forbidden to move from it to house; but if the enclosure is only se'ah it has no karpef law and permitted to move from it even to house since not a domain completely separate from the house like karpef even though not enclosed for dwelling:
(ד) Even if there is a threshing floor, etc. — meaning even if there was a threshing floor behind his house before he enclosed with three partitions we do not say that because of the threshing floor an opening was opened in the house wall and there is no opening to the rechavah; rather by default opened to rechavah and behold it is enclosed for dwelling and permitted to move throughout and even from it to his house:
