Rechavah, etc. — know there are four names in this matter: karmelit, karpef, rechavah, chatzer. Karmelit d'oraisa is a valley without public traffic; karmelit d'rabbanan is karpef more than beit se'ah not enclosed for dwelling — for by law it is full reshut ha-yachid and rabbinically is like karmelit [nevertheless one moves from one to another within less than four amot as siman 346 seif 3; but from this karpef to chatzer forbidden as siman 372 seif 1]; and there karpef includes whether more than se'ah or beit se'ah enclosed for dwelling or not enclosed for dwelling. Chatzer before houses is certainly enclosed for dwelling and perforce even many korim is full reshut ha-yachid even d'rabbanan. Karpef that is only beit se'ah precisely and not enclosed for dwelling — even though called karpef — nevertheless permitted to move throughout and even to chatzer permitted; but to house forbidden; and likewise to karmelit or karpef more than beit se'ah also forbidden even of one person. Karpef more than beit se'ah enclosed for dwelling [even if holding many korim likewise] or less than beit se'ah and not enclosed for dwelling — even to house permitted to move, for it is considered like chatzer enclosed for dwelling; and all the more rechavah enclosed for dwelling certainly the law is thus.
