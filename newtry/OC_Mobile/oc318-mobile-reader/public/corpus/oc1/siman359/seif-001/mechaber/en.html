The law of a rechavah behind houses. And in it is one seif:
A rechavah behind houses larger than beit se'ah that was not enclosed for dwelling — one may not move objects in it except within four amot; and if he opened an opening to it from his house and afterward enclosed it, even if there is a threshing floor between it and the house it is enclosure for dwelling:And what we are not careful to move objects in courtyards behind houses — because nowadays they are by default enclosed for dwelling as explained above regarding karpef (private opinion))
