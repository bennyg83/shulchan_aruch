1) [Seif 1] On erev Shabbos one kindles the Chanukah light first, etc. So wrote the Baal Halachos Gedolos, for if he kindles the Shabbos light first, he becomes forbidden to kindle the Chanukah light, since he has accepted Shabbos. Tosafos wrote that acceptance of Shabbos does not depend upon kindling, and he may kindle the Shabbos light first and afterward the Chanukah light, so long as he has not accepted Shabbos. The Tur cited their words. Beit Yosef wrote: As to practical halacha, it appears to me that he should kindle the Chanukah light first, for even according to those who disagree with the Baal Halachos Gedolos, he may kindle the Chanukah light first if he wishes, end quote. He therefore ruled this way in the Shulchan Aruch. So too wrote Darkei Moshe that our custom is to kindle the Chanukah light first, and so is written in our books of customs, end quote. So too wrote the Bach that the universal custom follows the Baal Halachos Gedolos. Birkei Yosef, letter 1, likewise wrote that the principal ruling is as Maran ruled, since in this manner one satisfies all opinions. Radbaz agreed in his responsa, at the end of siman 757, and discussed this at length. Furthermore, there is a fundamental reason according to the true, kabbalistic teaching that the Chanukah light comes first and the Shabbos light afterward, because one ascends in holiness. It is therefore proper to act in this manner, end quote. So too, the sefer Bigdei Yesha wrote that one should not deviate from the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch. So ruled Chayei Adam, klal 154, letter 35. So too is written above, siman 678, at the end of letter 2, in the name of the kabbalistic teachings, and the reason according to the hidden meaning is given there; see there.
