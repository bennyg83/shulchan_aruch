Chanukah candle first — Taz, Magen Avraham 1, and in Acharonim to distinguish between man and woman, and even for a woman he sided there. Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe: the words of Tosafot require Shabbat first because of tadir, and what is not tadir, tadir precedes; and Bach and Peri Chadash: as on Rosh Chodesh Tevet on Shabbat exempted on Chanukah there is pirsumei nisa and tadir precedes; nevertheless since most times they accept Shabbat with lighting Shabbat candles, therefore Chanukah first lest they fall asleep. And are ordinary Shabbatot of the year comparable? In siman 478 one's house candle precedes three house candles and tadir does not precede there — Chanukah candle is entirely deferred; certainly publicity takes precedence if not for shalom bayit. And it appears that on erev Shabbat one should prepare Chanukah first with oil and wicks and not prepare the candelabra with candles on the table for Shabbat, for one could say that whenever both are not before him we do not say tadir precedes, and there is no time of assembly here. Also so it should not appear as eagerness for the Shabbat candle; therefore it is good to do so.
