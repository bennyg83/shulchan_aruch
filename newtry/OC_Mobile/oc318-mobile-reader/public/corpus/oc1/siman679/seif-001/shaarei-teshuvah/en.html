In the Shulchan Aruch one lights etc. See in Shevut Yaakov siman 15 at the end that it is forbidden to leave a non-Jew on Shabbat to extinguish the Chanukah candles at night and relight them, for it is not proper to do so since it is complete melacha d'oraisa; and if for pirsumei nisa it is a mitzvah that comes through a transgression. Also we did not find anywhere this mitzvah requires them to burn evening and morning for publicity of the miracle — see there. And see in Birkei Yosef who wrote in the name of Maharash Abuhav responsum kuntres tav yod: one should be careful to pray Mincha on erev Shabbat of Chanukah and then light Chanukah, for Mincha prayer corresponds to the tamid of bein ha'arbayim and Chanukah is a remembrance of the miracle done in the candles of the menorah lit after the tamid of bein ha'arbayim — end of his words:
