(1) On erev Shabbat etc. Some are accustomed to light before Mincha (Rama siman 471 seif 7), and the Levush omitted this because he wrote in Darkei Moshe that our custom is to light between Mincha and Maariv. Nevertheless it appears specifically while the day is still great; but when he sees that if he prays Mincha the time will pass and there will be some prohibition to light, he should light before Mincha, whether in synagogue or at home. The same applies to one who can pray with a minyan in synagogue: he should light at home before Mincha and then go to synagogue. This is specifically after plag haMincha:
