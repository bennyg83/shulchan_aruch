On erev Shabbos, etc. Some have the practice to kindle before Minchah; Rema, siman 671, section 7. Levush omitted this because he wrote in Darkei Moshe that our custom is to kindle between Minchah and Maariv. Nevertheless, it seems that this is specifically when there is still much daylight. But when one sees that if he prays Minchah the time will pass and there will be some prohibition in kindling, he should kindle before Minchah, both in the synagogue and at home. The same applies to one who can pray with a minyan in the synagogue: he should kindle in his home before Minchah and afterward go to the synagogue. This is specifically after plag haminchah.
