(א) Chanukah candle first — in order to satisfy the view of the Yesh Omrim who hold that if one lights Shabbat first he accepts Shabbat and melacha becomes forbidden for him; but most poskim disagree and hold that acceptance of Shabbat is not dependent on lighting the candle; therefore if he lit Shabbat and did not accept Shabbat in his thought he may light afterward. This is for a man who lights; but a woman who lights, since the custom is that she presumably accepts Shabbat with her lighting, therefore she should not light Chanukah again but tell another to light, and he will bless she'asah nissim to light on her behalf; but the blessing she'asah nissim and likewise shehecheyanu on the first day she may recite herself:

(ב) While the day is still great — specifically after plag haMincha, which is an hour and a quarter before night as written at the beginning of siman 472. We already wrote in the name of the Acharonim that one must put enough oil that it burns half an hour after nightfall; the same for one who lights candles. If he does not have so much oil and large candles, he should see to it that at least one of them burns this amount.
