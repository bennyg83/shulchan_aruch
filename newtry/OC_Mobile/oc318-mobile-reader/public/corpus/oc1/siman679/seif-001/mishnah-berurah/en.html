The Chanukah lamp first. In order to satisfy the view of those who say that if he kindles the Shabbos lamp first, he has accepted Shabbos and labor becomes forbidden to him. But most authorities disagree with this and hold that acceptance of Shabbos does not depend on kindling the lamp. Therefore, if he kindled the Shabbos lamp and did not accept Shabbos in his mind, he may kindle afterward. All this is with a man who kindles. But a woman who kindles, since the custom is that she presumably accepts Shabbos with her kindling, should not afterward kindle the Chanukah lamp; rather, she should tell another person to kindle, and he should recite for her the blessing Who commanded us to kindle. But the blessing Who performed miracles, and also Shehecheyanu on the first day, she may recite herself.
While there is still much daylight. Specifically after plag haminchah, which is an hour and a quarter before night, as written at the beginning of siman 672. We have already written in the name of the later authorities that one must put in enough oil for it to burn half an hour after the emergence of the stars, and likewise when kindling lamps. If he does not have that much oil or large lamps, he should see to it that at least one of them burns for this measure. The later authorities wrote that ab initio it is proper to pray Minchah first and afterward kindle.
