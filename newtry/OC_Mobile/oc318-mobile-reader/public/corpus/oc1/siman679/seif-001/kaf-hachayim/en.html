(1) [seif 1] On erev Shabbat one lights the Chanukah candle first etc. So too Beit HaGadol: if one lights Shabbat first it is forbidden for him to light Chanukah, for he has accepted Shabbat; and the Tosafot wrote that acceptance is not dependent on lighting and one may light Shabbat first and then Chanukah, for thus they did not accept Shabbat. And Beit Yosef and Tur. Beit Yosef wrote: for practical application it appears to me that one lights Chanukah first, for even according to those who disagree with Beit HaGadol, if he wishes to light Chanukah first he is permitted — end of his words; therefore he ruled thus in the Shulchan Aruch. So too in Darkei Moshe that we practice lighting Chanukah first, and so in our customs — end of his words; and so Bach that the world practiced like Beit HaGadol. So too Birkei Yosef letter 1 that the essential ruling is as Maran ruled, that thereby one satisfies all views; and the Radbaz agreed in responsum siman 457 and elaborated on this. There is also a reason as the essential truth: Chanukah first and then Shabbat candle, for we ascend in holiness, and so it is fitting to practice — end of his words; and so in Bigdei Yeshua that one should not deviate from the Shulchan Aruch's ruling.
