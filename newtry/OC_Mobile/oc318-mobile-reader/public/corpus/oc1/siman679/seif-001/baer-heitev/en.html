(א) And then. The Levush wrote that one who lit Shabbat first and did not accept Shabbat with intent that he could light afterward — see there. The Taz disagrees and wrote that he cannot light, since for us there is a widespread custom that a woman who lights the Shabbat candle accepts Shabbat upon herself and is forbidden in melacha, while other household members are still permitted; therefore a man who lights the Shabbat candle and forgot to light Chanukah may not light Chanukah afterward, but should have one of his household members light — see there. It appears to me that for a woman who lights, since the custom is that she accepts Shabbat with her lighting as written in siman 263 seif 10, therefore according to all she should not light Chanukah again but tell another to light.
