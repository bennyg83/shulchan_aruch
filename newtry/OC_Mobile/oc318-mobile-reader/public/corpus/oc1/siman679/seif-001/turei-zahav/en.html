Chanukah first. For if one lights Shabbat first he accepts Shabbat and melacha becomes forbidden for him — so in Beit HaGadol. The Tosafot disagree and hold that acceptance of Shabbat is not dependent on lighting the candle; and for us there is a widespread custom that a woman who lights the Shabbat candle accepts Shabbat upon herself and is forbidden in melacha, while other household members are still permitted; therefore a man who lights Shabbat and forgot Chanukah may not light Chanukah afterward but should have one of his household light.
