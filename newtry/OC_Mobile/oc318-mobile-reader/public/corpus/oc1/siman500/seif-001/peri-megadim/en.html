Forbidden. See Taz, who concludes like the ruling of the Mechaber that even in his house it is also forbidden, and the Yerushalmi also forbids. And "mefeis" means he estimates in his mind by a litra, that is, he estimates by approximation how many litras reached him; and a reckoning of "what of it" is permitted, but to actually weigh is forbidden in his house. And see the Yerushalmi, chapter Ein Tzadin, halachah 6: "mefeis" with two yuds, also to settle his mind; one may also explain thus. However, from that of R. Mana who bought carobs in the seventh year, it is implied at first glance that he was actually weighing in his house; see there.
And know. Know that because of mice it is forbidden if they are hanging; but regarding moving them, utensils whose labor is for a prohibition are permitted for need of their body and place; for otherwise even without this it would be forbidden like a candelabrum; see siman 513. However, regarding meat scales and the like: but perfume scales and the like, one may say are muktzeh because of monetary loss, and he designates a place; forbidden even to cover a vessel. And see the commentary of the Mishnah of the Rambam, of blessed memory; and see Peri Chadash. And in Hilchos Yom Tov, chapter 4, he wrote specifically hanging on a peg; from this it is proven as we said. And at first glance, specifically a butcher in the market; but an individual in his house with no one seeing is permitted, for wherever they forbade even in inner chambers it is forbidden by Torah law, not by rabbinic law; but because of the members of his household it is forbidden; and understand. And in our novellae we elaborated. Nevertheless, specifically how much reached him in thought; but to weigh how much reached him, it appears that it is forbidden; and understand.
