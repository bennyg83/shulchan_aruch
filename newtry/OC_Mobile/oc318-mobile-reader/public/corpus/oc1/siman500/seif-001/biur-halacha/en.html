In apportioning valuation — see in the Gemara 27b, and it is proven that without apportioning one may properly count on the animal. So too is explained explicitly in the Tosefta — see there. But Rashba in Avodat HaKodesh wrote to be stringent in this, and these are his words there: it appears that one does not count even a count of two people on the animal initially, to say we will count five on this animal, for this is like an act of weekday; but if they counted on it from erev Yom Tov, they may add tomorrow and count with them another five others — end of his words. It appears he was concerned for the words of Yerushalmi that he brought in his chiddushim, from which it appears he interpreted the mishnah according to its plain sense; also from our Gemara Shabbat 148 per the version in our books it is apparently proven thus, and the matter that Tosafot wrote there s.v. but we learned, etc., and see Peri Chadash who wrote that although the essential halachah is not established like Rashba's view, nevertheless one who is stringent — blessing shall come upon him.
