Se'if 6. They are accustomed to nikkur the meat, etc. Marginal note: see in my work Sefer Chassidim what I challenged against the Peri Megadim, who forbids peeling on Yom Tov something that requires peeling; see there. But in my work on the Torah from the year 5598, in Parashas Mikeitz, on the verse "and slaughter a slaughter and prepare," I settled my difficulty against him, and one may uphold the words of the Peri Megadim; the matter requires further study; see there with good reasoning, with the help of Hashem, and examine carefully.
