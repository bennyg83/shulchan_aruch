Cuts. See Baer Heitev, and see Responsa Noda BiYehuda, Mahadura Tinyana, siman 95, in a case where there is a non-Jewish butcher who would not sell on erev Yom Tov because it was their festival, only on the morrow on Yom Tov: the posek ruled well that it is permitted to buy from him and to tell him the amount of weight that he needs, for even to a Jew one may say "give me a litra," and he intends by his cutting to cut accordingly; and if so, what does it matter that the non-Jew actually weighs? And even when it is a psik reisha, we do not mind so much regarding instructing a non-Jew, as is explained in siman 203 in Magen Avraham, s.k. 41. And there is also no chillul Hashem here, for they too buy what they need for their festival. Nevertheless, because of degrading Yom Tov one should not carry the meat in public view, and they should cover it when carrying it in the street; see there.
