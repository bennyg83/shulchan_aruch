[6] [Levush] The hindquarters, etc. And it appears to me that if one can guard it after Yom Tov so that it not stink, it is forbidden to sell to a Kuthi a treifah or hindquarters; and if it was slaughtered on erev Yom Tov, in every case it is forbidden, as he wrote in siman 499, se'if 3, unless it was slaughtered close to Yom Tov so that it was not possible to sell before Yom Tov (Magen Avraham at the end of siman 499). And see siman 499, s.k. 7, where he is uncertain about "close to Yom Tov," and one must say that selling is lighter.
