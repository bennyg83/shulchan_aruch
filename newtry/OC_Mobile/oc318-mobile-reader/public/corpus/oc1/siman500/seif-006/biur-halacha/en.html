And it is good to change, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote about rinsing meat that was not pierced: Magen Avraham's view is to be stringent on Yom Tov, that one cannot deceive and pierce, because he should have pierced from erev Yom Tov, and automatically it is forbidden to rinse it as well. Know that the essence of what Magen Avraham decided to be stringent on Shabbat not to rinse it is because he holds that what is stated in the Gemara that raw meat is fit to move because it is fit for a snack applies only to chicken meat, which is tender, but not to ox meat, and it is not fit to move — for the second reason alone, that it softens, there is no prohibition, as Noda BiYehuda wrote in Orach Chaim siman 27. Automatically, according to what many poskim ruled that raw ox meat is also fit to move because it is fit for a snack, it is automatically permitted to rinse it as well, as they wrote explicitly thus in Avodat Yisrael siman 308 and Noda BiYehuda in the above siman 27. If so, meat that was not pierced is also fit to move because it is fit for a snack, for although it is not pierced from blood and from milk, nevertheless it is also fit to eat as a snack — meaning only to rinse the blood from above and to beware of a place where there is concern of milk, and from the strands in which blood is absorbed, as explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 67 seif 2. We never heard from any of the poskim that what is stated in the Gemara that raw meat is fit to move because it is fit for a snack applies only to pierced meat — and the reason is as I wrote, and automatically it is permitted to rinse it as well. Nevertheless, initially one should certainly be careful to rinse the meat through a gentile, as I saw in several Acharonim that after they weighed and debated the essence of the law to disagree with Magen Avraham, nevertheless they concluded that initially one should be careful through a gentile. And know that even according to Magen Avraham who is stringent to rinse, this is only regarding rinsing all the meat; but meat that one wants for Yom Tov needs, it is certainly permitted to rinse and pierce according to all, even if it was slaughtered from erev Yom Tov.
