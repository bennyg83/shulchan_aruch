Se'if 2: And even in his house. Tur. And he learned it from what is written on 29a: "and the baker, in order," etc.; otherwise it is forbidden. This is unlike the Yerushalmi there, which teaches: Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says that a person may even settle himself with a litra to know how much reached him, like this: R. Mana bought carobs of shemittah and came to ask R. Chizkiyah, who said to him: the rabbis practice like this of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. And see Beis Yosef. And it appears to me that from the Yerushalmi the opposite is proven, for it says there: R. Yosi in the name of Shmuel: even to hang it on a scale-pan because of mice is forbidden. Meaning, that is what it says, "we do not attend." R. Yosi bar Bun said: not for this reason; meaning, he did not say "we do not attend" because in such a case it is permitted, but because we learned: R. Yehudah says a person may weigh meat against a vessel or against a cleaver, and the Sages say we do not attend to a scale-pan at all. Thus the earlier beraisa held that we do attend, meaning against a vessel or cleaver, and regarding such a case the Rabbis said we do not attend. It follows that to hang it on a scale-pan because of mice is permitted. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel teaches: a person may even settle himself, etc., as above. And since we do not rule like the Yerushalmi, which concludes that because of mice it is permitted, all the more so we do not rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel.
And even to weigh, etc. There: if like the Rabbis, etc., and we rule like the Rabbis. And although R. Yehudah ruled like R. Yehudah, Abaye rejected him. And see the Yerushalmi there, which also concludes to distinguish between a bekhor and Yom Tov for the reason that we do not attend to a scale-pan at all; except that it says there that even R. Chiyya and R. Shimon son of Rabbi forbid on Yom Tov; see there.
