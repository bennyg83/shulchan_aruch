Meziz on parchment: even to cast a knife and [decide by] whichever side the sharp edge faces is forbidden; end quote. Avodas HaKodesh wrote: and it appears that they do not even number people upon this animal, for that is like a weekday act; but if they numbered upon it on erev Yom Tov, they may add on the morrow and count with them another five.
