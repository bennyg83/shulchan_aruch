It is forbidden to weigh. Even if he is not exacting, but only slightly reduces or slightly adds, it is forbidden; even though regarding a measure it is permitted, as is written in siman 323, for that case is different: it does not appear as measuring, but only as pouring into a vessel; but with a weight, in every case it is forbidden. It is forbidden to take in one hand the weight and in the other hand meat to estimate the weight (Bach).
And an expert butcher. Meaning one who knows how to aim and estimate precisely is forbidden. And it is implied in Yam shel Shlomo that specifically selling is forbidden, but in his house it is permitted; and so it appears, for the Rashba permits actually weighing in his house to know how much he needs.
Cuts in his usual way. And it appears to me that one may say "give me a litra of meat," for he does not intend for the weight, but to tell him how much he needs, as is written in siman 323 regarding a measure.
