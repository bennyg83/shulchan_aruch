See Taz, what he wrote regarding a hide before the tanner: two circumventions; I did not understand, for perhaps there he needs all the meat and salts a great deal for the need of eating, and therefore it is permitted over the whole hide a little here, etc. The Bach wrote that there is no need to mention here that of Rav Adda as regarding a hide, for circumvention is permitted, and the same law applies to meat; and that is where slaughtered on Yom Tov: they permitted their end because of their beginning, and by means of circumvention; and when slaughtered on erev Yom Tov, specifically all at once like Shmuel. And God willing, in Magen Avraham, Yoreh De'ah, it will be clarified.
