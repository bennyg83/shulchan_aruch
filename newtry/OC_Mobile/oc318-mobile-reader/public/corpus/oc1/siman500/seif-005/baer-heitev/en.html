Erev Yom Tov. The reason: meat is better without salt, but fish differ — some types spoil if soaked in salt, then permitted to salt on Yom Tov even if torn erev Yom Tov; types better when soaked may not be salted on Yom Tov if torn erev Yom Tov. Nevertheless it seems one may cook and put salt in the pot since main prohibition is concern for tanning, inapplicable when cooking puts salt in water. Yeshuos Shabbos wrote custom to forbid salting meat slaughtered erev Yom Tov; Magen Avraham raised where certain loss it will spoil, permitted to circumvent and salt even after eating like dangerously ill animal siman 498 seif 6 — only when slaughtered on Yom Tov.
