(s.k. 5) And a butcher, etc. But in his house it is permitted; meaning even for an expert butcher. Granted that they forbade weighing even in his house, that is true weighing; which is not so in such a case, where there is no true weighing.
For the Rashba permits, etc. Granted that we do not rule like him, as the Tur wrote, for we forbid measuring flour even for the need of his house, as is explained at the beginning of siman 506; and even according to Rav who permits measuring flour in his house, that is so that she take challah generously; but to weigh even in his house is forbidden. Nevertheless, in such a case where it is not true weighing, one may rely on the Rashba. And see Sefer Eliyah Rabbah, who disagrees.
