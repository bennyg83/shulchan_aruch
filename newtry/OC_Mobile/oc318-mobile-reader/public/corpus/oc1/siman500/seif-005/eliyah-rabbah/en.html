[5] And some say, etc. Olas Shabbos wrote: but Shiltei Gibborim wrote to permit selling to Kuthi'im, etc.; end quote. And he was not precise, for Shiltei Gibborim, chapter Ein Tzadin, copied from the Tur at the end of siman 497, and so ruled the Shulchan Aruch and the Levush; and if so their words contradict one another. Rather one must say that there [it is] regarding a treifah, as the Levush concluded regarding the hindquarters, and here regarding kosher meat. And according to Magen Avraham it is implied that even with kosher meat it is permitted, only that some say they forbade with Kuthi'im when one says "behold I am a partner," since one is not accustomed to partner with Kuthi'im, it is like buying and selling. And on this too he challenged Hagahos Ashiri; but in truth in all the other poskim no distinction between a Jew and Kuthi'im is mentioned. It also appears to me that there is reason to permit even with Kuthi'im, since they sell to a Jew because of enmity, like a midwife in siman 330, se'if 2; and see siman 497, s.k. 21.
