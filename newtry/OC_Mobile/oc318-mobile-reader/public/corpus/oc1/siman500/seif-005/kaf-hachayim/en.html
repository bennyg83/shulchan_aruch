(5) And Rashba wrote in Avodat HaKodesh in Bava Metzia gate 2 note 4: it appears that one does not count even a count of people on the animal initially, to say we will count five on this animal, for this is like an act of weekday; but if they counted on it from erev Yom Tov, they may add tomorrow and count on them another five others. Beit David, Peri Chadash — and he wrote: it appears he derived this from Yerushalmi; but in our Gemara it implies that without apportioning valuation, for one group to count on it there is no concern, only that he wrote: and although thus it appears, the essential practice is to be stringent — see there.
