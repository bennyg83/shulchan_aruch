(ב) Even though possible to salt erev Yom Tov. Known meat in salt dulls taste; though above permitted to circumvent salt on Yom Tov lest spoil — implies better without salt; say there first Yom Tov if not salted until third day spoils, but erev to Yom Tov better without salt; fish some types improve in salt and need salting erev Yom Tov (Terumat HaDeshen). I say deals with salting to remove blood — if washed and salted erev to remove blood would need second salting after rinsing, then spoils standing without salt, better hanging in air without rinse and salting, therefore permitted to salt on Yom Tov. Yeshuos Shabbos ruled like Maharil to forbid salting meat on Yom Tov; siman 495 in gloss. I say permitted morning on Yom Tov by circumvention lest spoil — salting to remove blood leaving in salt until after Yom Tov (Beit Yosef 70:5 some forbid initially or after salting and rinsing salt again Yom Tov night by circumvention saying from this I eat tomorrow, but morning even before eating after salting and rinsing forbidden to salt again since even if needs eating he cooks immediately without salting). Where certain loss will spoil, permitted to circumvent and salt even after eating like dangerously ill siman 498:6 only when slaughtered on Yom Tov (Rosh).
