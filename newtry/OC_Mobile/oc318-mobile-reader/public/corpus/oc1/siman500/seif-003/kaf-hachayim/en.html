(ג) And a group wanting to buy a whole animal from the butcher — do not set its price; the butcher brings two equal animals and says see they are alike, they buy one, slaughter, divide without lottery as siman 522; after Yom Tov they know both prices and each pays his share. Buyers may not tell butcher what you take tomorrow I pay today for what we slaughter today — forbidden sale mention on Yom Tov. R' Zalman sk 1.
