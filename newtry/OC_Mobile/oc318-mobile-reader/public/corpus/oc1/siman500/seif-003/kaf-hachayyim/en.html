3) A group that wishes to take an entire animal from a butcher may not fix a price with him for it. Instead, the butcher brings two animals of equal value and says to them, 'See that this one is like that one.' They take one of them, slaughter it, and divide it among themselves without casting lots, as written in siman 322. See there. After Yom Tov, they determine the value of both animals, and each person pays the value of his portion. The buyers may not say to the butcher, 'Whatever you will take tomorrow for that animal, I will give you for this one that we slaughter today,' because this is the fixing of a purchase price, which may not be mentioned on Yom Tov. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1.
