One does not pierce. For a change is needed so that it be known that buying and selling is forbidden therein [Rashi]. And if so, it is permitted to pierce a piece of the afikoman on Pesach in order to hang it, and not like those who are stringent.
To make a sign in it. So that a person not switch it. It is stated in the Gemara: a vessel that has notches, and one puts a little water in it and places the meat in the water, and when it reaches the notches he knows how many litras are in it: it is forbidden to do so on Yom Tov, even if he slightly reduces or slightly adds, for the matter is evident that he intends for a measure.
