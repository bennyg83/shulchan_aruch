They pierce. For a change is needed so that it be known that buying and selling is forbidden therein. It is stated in the Gemara: a vessel that has notches, and one puts a little water in it and places the meat in the water, and when it reaches the notches he knows how many litras are in it: it is forbidden to do so on Yom Tov, even if he slightly reduces or slightly adds, for the matter is evident that he intends for a measure.
A sign. Not only a sign and making a mere mark, but even writing words is permitted. Nevertheless, whatever one can change so that it not be like a weekday act, he should change (Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 26).
