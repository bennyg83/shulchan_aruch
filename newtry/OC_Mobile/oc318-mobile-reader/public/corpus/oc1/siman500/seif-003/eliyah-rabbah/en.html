[3] And he brings, etc. And the same law applies that he may go to a Jewish or Kuthi butcher and say to him "give me a leg or a half" and the like, without mentioning buying and selling or fixing a price, and on the morrow he pays him money as he is able (Tanya). And it appears from the Mishnah [Beitzah] folio 25 that l'chatchila it is preferable to fix a price on erev Yom Tov; and so is implied in Shibolei HaLeket, Tanya, and Avodas HaKodesh, and not like the Bach.
