{18} They scald, etc. In boiling water after salting in order to remove the hair; and likewise they singe, etc., for it is melachah for the need of food for the soul. And it is plain that according to the view of those who are stringent above in siman 495 that even regarding food for the soul itself, if it was possible for him to do it on erev Yom Tov it is forbidden on Yom Tov, here it deals with a case where he had no opportunity to scald it on erev Yom Tov [for it is known that it does not spoil if he had scalded it on erev Yom Tov], or it deals with a case where they slaughtered it on Yom Tov itself.
{19} In the fire. And the same law applies that it is permitted to bury it in hot embers.
{20} With lime, etc. In order to remove the hair.
