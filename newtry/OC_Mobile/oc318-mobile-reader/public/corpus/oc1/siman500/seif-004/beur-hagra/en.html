Se'if 4: It is permitted to salt, etc. The Rambam holds that Shmuel and Rav Adda disagree, and Shmuel does not hold that of Rav Adda, and he ruled like Shmuel; Maggid Mishneh.
And some say, etc. See Beer HaGolah, s.k. 30.
Only, etc. Tosafos there s.v. "he circumvents," etc.; and see Rashi 17b s.v. "so that he not circumvent," etc.; and the same applies here.
And it is permitted, etc. Because it dulls the taste when salted on erev Yom Tov; and as what is written in Yoreh De'ah 1, that it is permitted for that reason.
