(s.k. 4) It is forbidden to weigh, etc. But with a weight, in every case it is forbidden. For in every case it appears as weighing; for even to guard it from mice it is forbidden to put it on a scale-pan if it hangs in the place where they usually weigh. And Peri Chadash wrote in the name of the Rambam that even to move the scales to cover something with them or for some other need is forbidden, for they are muktzeh because of monetary loss.
Forbidden.
To take in the hand, etc. That is to say: it was permitted to a householder who is not an expert only to take the meat in one hand, and he does not take the weight in his other hand; for for an expert even such a case is forbidden. Nevertheless, for one who is not an expert it is permitted; but if he takes the weight in the second hand, which more resembles weighing, even for one who is not an expert it is forbidden.
