2) There: He recites a blessing over spices, etc. Regarding fragrant wood, one should make the esoteric intention for fragrance explained by us concerning the fragrance of myrtle on Shabbos night itself; see there. Of the two myrtle bundles prepared for Shabbos, as is known, take care when purchasing them that they contain three valid three-leafed myrtle branches. After Shabbos, when reciting Borei Atzei Besamim, take only those three branches in your hand and smell them. These three myrtles should be tied in one knot as a single bundle. Intend that the three myrtles correspond to nefesh, ruach, and neshamah, and intend to retain some of the additional nefesh, ruach, and neshamah added to you on Shabbos. This is accomplished now through these three myrtles and smelling them. It is already known that all actions performed after Shabbos are intended to retain part of the additional Shabbos sanctity for the coming weekdays. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 60b. One should smell the myrtle three times, corresponding to nefesh, ruach, and neshamah. Pri Etz Chaim, gate 18, chapter 24. It appears that even where myrtle is unavailable and the blessing is recited over other spices, one should take three branches of those spices, intending that they correspond to nefesh, ruach, and neshamah, as stated above. See above, siman 216, paragraph 16, for what we wrote regarding the intention for fragrance; see there.
