We do not make a blessing over spices for a bathroom, nor over those for used for the deceased (and specifically) those which are placed on top of the bed of the deceased, and not over spices used at a gathering for the deceased, because this is simply like a party for a gentile. RAMA: And if one does make the blessing over these spices he has not fulfilled his obligation and he must go back and make a blessing on other spices [Beis Yosef in the name of the Orach Chayim]() הנתונים למעלה ממטתו של מת ולא על בשמים שבמסיבת ע"א דסתם מסיבתן לעכו"ם. הגה ואם בירך על בשמים אלו לא יצא וצריך לחזור ולברך על אחרים [ב"י בשם א"ח]:
