4) There: He recites a blessing over spices, etc. When reciting Borei Atzei Besamim after Shabbos, he should hold them in their natural direction of growth. Magen Avraham there in the name of Mahari Weil. If he recites Borei Isvei Besamim or Borei Minei Besamim, which does not mention trees, he need not hold them in their natural direction of growth. Machatzis HaShekel. Nevertheless, the correct practice is to hold even a species that is not woody in its natural direction of growth while reciting the blessing.
