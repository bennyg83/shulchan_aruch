The spices. Shiyarei Kenesset HaGedolah wrote that one may recite a berachah over crushed pepper, and so wrote the Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 108; so too wrote the Magen Avraham, and so it is in Perach Shoshan, klal 1 siman 13. The Shach in Nekudat HaKesef disagrees and wrote that one does not recite a berachah. (So too Eliyah Rabbah agreed; see there.) The Olat Shabbat wrote that the same applies to ginger, called ingber, that one may recite a berachah, and specifically when it is crushed, but when it is whole it does not smell so much. The Taz wrote that the same applies to herbs that have a good smell, even if they are dry. It is good to place inside it a piece called pizum so that he can recite borei minei besamim; Magen Avraham, see there. One who fasts on Shabbat must recite the berachah over spices, because once the extra soul comes on Friday night, it does not depart until Motza'ei Shabbat, unlike on Yom Kippur. Shiyarei Kenesset HaGedolah.
