(א) He who does not smell was that he had no sense of smell:

(ב) (יב) To discharge, etc. — they need to smell immediately after his blessing.

(ג) (יג) Or to discharge, etc. — all the Acharonim disagree and hold specifically to discharge minor children — that is permitted since it is placed on him to educate them in mitzvot; but not to discharge one who does not know — since he is not obligated in the matter he cannot discharge others from their obligation.
