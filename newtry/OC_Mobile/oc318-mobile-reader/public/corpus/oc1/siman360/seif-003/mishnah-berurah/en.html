(א) (יא) And each enclosed, etc. — meaning they enclosed with inferior partition: lechayim without lavud or lavud without lechayim.
(ב) (יב) And the middle is narrow — like this.
(ג) (יג) Very large — more than bet se'ah.
(ד) (יד) That we view, etc. — for there is no partition to divide between them since it is fully breached to them.
(ה) (טו) And some say, etc. — the halacha follows the first view; nevertheless l'chatchila it is not worthwhile to be lenient.
(ו) (טז) Only up to two se'ah — for they are not one domain with each other because of their walls interrupting toward the middle one.
(ז) (יז) The middle is also forbidden, etc. — but the second outer one opposite it is permitted, for it is not fully breached to the middle [later authorities].
(ח) (יח) Fully breached, etc. — such that the middle is wider than the two outer ones.
(ט) (יט) Only bet se'ah — the outer ones cannot combine with the middle, for we do not consider all dwelling in it since it has walls interrupting and is not fully breached to them; and even though it can combine with each outer one since they are fully breached to it and it is considered dwelling in their domain — as explained — nevertheless in each there are only two people.
(י) (כ) Only that one is forbidden — not the middle, for it is walled and not breached to it; all the more the second outer one far from it.
(כ) (כא) For they are considered, etc. — as we explained the reason above in sk 18.
(ל) (כב) As if in the outer ones — meaning with each outer one; but the middle is given only bet se'ah. All this is only per the second view; per the first view, just as the outer ones become a courtyard to the middle, so in our case the middle becomes a courtyard to the outer ones and permitted for all needs therein too.
(מ) (כג) It becomes a breach and the walled ones, etc. — refers to what is stated above when the middle is walled and an outer one exceeds two se'ah — it is forbidden and the walled one permitted; on this he says: if the breach is more than ten, the partition name is nullified from the walled area and automatically the walled area is also forbidden. See Biur Halacha.
(נ) (כד) And all this when walls, etc. — refers to what is written above: but if one in each outer, etc.
(ס) (כה) Enter into the larger — as we depicted in the diagram.
(ע) (כו) Set back — meaning there is three tefachim between the end of the small walls and the walls of the large.
(פ) (כז) Then the large forbids the small — these words until 'enclosed' in Shulchan Aruch Olat Shabbat are correct; nevertheless with difficulty one can resolve even without enclosure — meaning as we rule below siman 374 seif 3 regarding a small courtyard breached to a large: when does the large forbid the small? Only in this manner — so too in our case.
(צ) (כח) Appears outside and flush inside — when standing inside the large it appears some wall remains at the end of the small; and even standing inside the small the walls are not visible — nevertheless it is as if the small also has walls; and if between the large walls and small walls is less than three tefachim we say lavud and it is as sealed until the end of the small walls — then walls appear from outside and help even for the small.
(ק) (כט) And we treat it as a lechi — a lechi does not apply to our matter; rather meaning: as for lechi we rule that even if flush inside, since it appears outside we treat it as lechi — so here: even though standing in the small it appears fully breached, nevertheless for one standing in the large the walls are visible — we count them also for the small.
