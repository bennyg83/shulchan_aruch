(א) (ג) The middle is also forbidden — but the second outer one is permitted.

(ב) (ד) Walled — such as wider than the outer ones.

(ג) (ה) We give the two outer ones — It seems per the first view above the middle is also permitted, considered a courtyard to the outer ones; so Maggid Mishneh and Bach.

(ד) (ו) And also the walled ones are forbidden — meaning if one enclosed more than two se'ah; and possibly even for leniency a breach more than ten helps, for it is as if all dwell in one karpeif and they are given all their needs; see siman 362 seif 66.
