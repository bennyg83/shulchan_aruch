If there' were three and each enclosed his own area side by side and they made an eruv together — if the outer areas are wide and the middle narrow, such that the middle is fully breached to the outer ones and the outer ones have walls extending beyond it on each side — they are given their full needs in the middle one even if it is very large, since we view it as if they all dwell within it and three are together. Also in the outer ones they are given their full needs, since having become a caravan in the middle, the others are treated as a courtyard to the middle one. Some say the outer ones are permitted only up to two se'ah, and if one of them exceeds two se'ah the middle is also forbidden since it is fully open to it. If the outer ones are fully open to the middle and the middle has walls, with one person in each — each is given only two se'ah; if one outer exceeds two se'ah only that one is forbidden; but if the middle exceeds two se'ah the outer ones are also forbidden since they open to a forbidden area. But if one person is in each outer and two in the middle, or two in each outer and one in the middle — the two outer ones are given their full needs, as everything in the middle is considered as if in the outer ones. When is this said? When they are not open to one another by more than ten tefachim — for then it is a breach and even walled areas are forbidden. All this when the walls of the smaller enter into the larger, and when the walls of the smaller are set back three tefachim from the lengthwise walls of the larger — for then the larger restricts the smaller (see above siman 374 seif 3); otherwise it would appear protruding outside yet flush inside and would be treated as a lechi, and all are considered walled as if none is breached to the other, and each is given only two se'ah.(') דאם לא כן הוי ליה נראה מבחוץ ושוה מבפנים ונדון משום לחי וכולם חשובין מגופפין וכאלו אין א' מהם נפרץ לחבירו ואין נותנין להם באחד מהם אלא מבית סאתים:
