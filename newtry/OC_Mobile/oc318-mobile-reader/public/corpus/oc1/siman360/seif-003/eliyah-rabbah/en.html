[1] [Levush] Even more than, etc. This means when it was enclosed for dwelling, even if only for that one Shabbos.
[2] [Levush] For both of them, etc. Although the Beit Yosef leaves this unresolved, Malbushei Yom Tov and the Bach agree that even for two people only an area of two seah is granted. I found the same in the Raavan, page 69, Rabbenu Yerucham, page 72, and Avodat HaKodesh, page 13. This makes the Beit Yosef's uncertainty surprising.
[3] Although there are two seah, etc. The Bach and Taz rule like Rashi on page 17 that even with fewer than six people, whenever two seah remain unoccupied it is prohibited. This is also the implication of the authorities.
