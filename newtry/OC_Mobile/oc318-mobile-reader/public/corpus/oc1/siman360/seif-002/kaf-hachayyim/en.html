2) There. And he surrounded it with inferior partitions, etc. Under Torah law, these partitions suffice to make the area a complete private domain even if it extends for several mil. One who throws an object into it from a public domain is liable. Nevertheless, by Rabbinic law they do not suffice to permit carrying within the enclosure or from it into a complete private domain unless the enclosure contains no more than beis sasayim.
