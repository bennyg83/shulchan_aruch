But — Taz Rashi Eruvin 17a s.v. lo panui mikelim; Avodat HaKodesh; possibly two se'ah empty is a partition not needed, but six se'ah empty most need them — permitted; nevertheless from where? Avodat HaKodesh: Rama in hagahah — minor: some say does not combine; see there. This minor — if adult in years without two hairs, one may say nevertheless for this matter he is adult and combines; requires study. A woman — one may say she combines; see EH siman 22 seif 5: in a field a woman with men, etc. — if so, since forbidden to seclude with her slave, they do not combine; requires study.
