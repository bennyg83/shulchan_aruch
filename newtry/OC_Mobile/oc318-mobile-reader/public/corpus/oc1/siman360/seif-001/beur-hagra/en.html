(א) Up to inferior partitions, etc. Rashi; and so implied in gemara 100 and Sages — this is the tanna kamma.

(ב) And the same law. From 'l'chatchila three we give them bet shisha' — learn that for two we do not give bet se'ah.

(ג) Even if it is, etc. Yerushalmi: even a kor, even korim; and so in the gemara.

(ד) When is this said. From Rav said they needed only four and enclosed six; and do not challenge 'they needed only four and enclosed six'; and Sages to be lenient on R' Yehuda bar R' Yehuda; R' Yehuda Beit Yosef did not say 'provided'; and so implies 'we give', etc., 'provided' — implying what they come to add on R' Yehuda alone, etc. And Rashi s.v. lo panui, etc., did not write so; and Ritva wrote the end 'provided there not be empty' refers even to R' Yehuda bar R' Yehuda, and the end as at the beginning — impossible; see there where he wrote so plainly.

(ה) Non-Jew. Yerushalmi end ch. 1; and so Rambam and Shulchan Aruch above three Jews.

(ו) And some say the same. Yerushalmi: does a minor complete the caravan? Rambam ruled stringently; Beit Yosef wondered why he ruled stringently; therefore omitted in Shulchan Aruch; and so 'some say', etc.
