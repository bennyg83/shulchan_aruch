(Magen Avraham sk 2) They meant also for a partition. NB: R' Yonasan wrote: even in partitions of lechayim and lavud that are not surrounded lechayim and lavud on all sides — even enclosed for residence — he proved from the case of a tree shading the ground: even though the partition is made with projection and side wall as established in the old chapter, nevertheless one may not carry under it except two se'ah, because his use is only the air — learn that even a complete partition is forbidden to carry except two se'ah; he also brought proof see there; and so Rabbenu Yeruham ch. 1 Netiv 12 ch. 4; and Ritva see there.
