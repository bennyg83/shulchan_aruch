Presumably in the conclusion he did not retract from what he held initially that for two we give only bet se'ah if they spent Shabbat in one enclosure; and one may say three as caravan differs: they enclosed with two se'ah empty — it fits the Mechaber's view; this is the superiority of caravan. Avodat HaKodesh; see Yerushalmi on this. An individual in a field is the same as in his house, as siman 362 seif 5 and Sages Eruvin 17a; and Tosafos Shabbos wondered on Ashkenaz here — truly it requires study; one may say b'dieved only is permitted, but l'chatchila only in a field; in a settled place l'chatchila one needs a complete partition — from where? Magen Avraham 398: four vertical posts make a path of reeds — one may say since there are anyway partitions there; nevertheless for practical law it seems permitted l'chatchila with lechayim or lavud; and possibly even from it to a house, all who enclosed for residence for one Shabbat, even if they do not dwell there afterward — see siman 362 and see here.
