(א) Inferior partitions — but complete partitions are permitted even more than two se'ah, for they enclosed for residence since they enclosed to dwell there on Shabbat (Rashi).

(ב) Lechayim — such as planting reeds less than three apart. Lavud — such as surrounding with three ropes less than three apart, ropes more than a tefach. What Rif and Rosh wrote: residence whose use is air — they meant also such a partition, as Rif states explicitly; see Mahari Berakhah — not like Ashkenaz; his words lack explanation.

(ג) If they are two — we do not give both more than two se'ah (Bach 363).
