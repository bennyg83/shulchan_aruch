(א) Inferior — but complete partitions are permitted even more than two se'ah, for they enclosed for residence since they enclosed to dwell there on Shabbat. Rashi.

(ב) Lechayim — for example he planted reeds less than three tefachim apart. Lavud — for example he surrounded with three ropes less than three tefachim apart and the ropes are more than a tefach.

(ג) Two — meaning we do not give both of them more than two se'ah together; see Taz.

(ד) Six se'ah — and Taz wrote that in all cases, if two se'ah remain empty they are forbidden; see there.
