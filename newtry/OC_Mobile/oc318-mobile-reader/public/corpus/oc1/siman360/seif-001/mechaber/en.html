Surrounding partitions for Shabbat. 3 sections: An individual who spent
Shabbat in an open field and surrounded himself with inferior partitions — such as only vertical elements without horizontal, or only horizontal without vertical — up to two se'ah it is permitted to carry throughout; beyond two se'ah one may only carry within four cubits. The same law applies if there are two people. If there are three Jews they are considered a caravan and it is permitted to carry throughout even if the enclosed area is very large, provided they do not enclose more than they need — meaning there should not remain an empty two se'ah area that they do not need for use. But if there does remain an empty two se'ah area, one may only carry within four cubits throughout the enclosed space. This applies when they enclosed more than six se'ah; but if they did not enclose more than six se'ah, even if there is an empty two se'ah area, since there are three people it is permitted.Rema: A non-Jew does not combine to make a caravan, and some say the same applies to a minor. (Rambam ch. 11).
