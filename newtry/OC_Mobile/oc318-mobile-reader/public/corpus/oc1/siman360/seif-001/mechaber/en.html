Surrounding partitions for Shabbat; containing 3 seifim. An individual who
spent Shabbat in an open field and surrounded himself with inferior partitions — such as lechayim without a lavud, or lavud without lechayim — up to two se'ah it is permitted to carry throughout; beyond two se'ah one may carry only within four amot. The same law applies if there are two people. If there are three Jews they are considered a caravan and it is permitted to carry throughout even if the enclosed area is very large, provided they do not enclose more than they need — there should not remain an empty two-se'ah area they do not need for use. But if two se'ah remain empty, one may carry only within four amot throughout the enclosure. When is this said? When they enclosed more than six se'ah; but if they did not enclose more than six se'ah, even if two se'ah remain empty, since there are three it is permitted.A non-Jew does not combine to form a caravan, and some say the same for a minor (Rambam ch. 11).
