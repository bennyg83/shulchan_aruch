And likewise Borei peri ha’adamah and Shehakol. Eliyah Rabbah writes in the name of Shelah that it is improper for common people, when served brandy together with preserves made from fruit whose blessing is Borei peri ha’adamah or Borei peri ha’eitz, to bless Shehakol over the brandy first. Eliyah Rabbah defends their practice, explaining that initially they have no desire at all to eat the preserves. They are like one who does not presently wish to eat, and only after drinking the brandy do they desire the preserves. A similar idea appears in Beit Yosef. Nevertheless, it is proper always to bless first over the preserves. Otherwise, at times they may be regarded merely as secondary, where one eats them only to temper the sharpness of the drink and has no independent desire to eat them, as explained in siman 212. See there and what I wrote in Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 5, concerning preserves eaten with brandy.
