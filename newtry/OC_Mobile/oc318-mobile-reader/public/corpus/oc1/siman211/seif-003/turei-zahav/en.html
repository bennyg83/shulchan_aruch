All that is earlier, etc. In Tur it is written: if the later is more cherished — and Beit Yosef explained this is per Rosh above that the seven-species type is better than cherishedness when equal — likewise earlier; but for Rambam above, just as the seven-species type is set aside before cherishedness, likewise the advantage of earlier is set aside, and there is no advantage to earlier except when equal in cherishedness. And per Beit Yosef's words, also for Semak who holds in seif 1 that when their blessings are not equal we follow the cherished and set aside the seven-species type — also set aside the advantage of earlier when their blessings are not equal, and we follow the cherished to precede it. And for Rosh, when their blessings are not equal we do not follow neither the seven-species type nor cherishedness, but whichever he wishes — it is difficult why earlier does not help here; let us also say earlier does not help except when equal, like the advantage of cherished. And you might say thus for Rosh too the advantage of precedence is only when equal — this is not so, for Tur finished this siman. But per the view of our teacher of blessed memory that they are equal borei peri haEtz and borei peri haAdamah — it is possible it deals even with chewing wheat; meaning wheat precedes from the aspect of earlier in the verse. From what helps even when they are not equal — one may say since it is important in the verse that deals with the advantage of precedence when equal and when not equal, we learn that earlier is its advantage in every manner; unlike the advantage of seven-species type — we do not find for it advantage for precedence; we say it has advantage only when equal, when one exempts its fellow, but not where he must bless on both of them regarding precedence.
