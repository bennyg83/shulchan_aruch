Everything stated, etc. Requires further study: behold Rabbenu Yitzchak omitted this in his commentary because he wrote in seif 5 that Scripture deals with a cooked dish, and they precede even the vine, as written above in seif 6 — if so, why did Rama write this, which implies specifically when they are equal in importance, such as barley and dates, but olives precede barley; and this is not so, for a cooked dish precedes everything. And if Rama holds Scripture deals even with chewing wheat, he should have emended thus in seif 5 and made known that chewing precedes the vine, fig, etc. — it is fine that Tur holds one can say Scripture deals even with chewing wheat and therefore needed to make known that wheat precedes olive and barley precedes dates; but for Shulchan Aruch it is difficult — see above seif 1 and seif 3, and it is simple that for Beit HaGedolot in chewing wheat and barley even pomegranate precedes them, for borei peri haEtz precedes and Scripture does not deal with chewing at all; and Levush's words in the gloss do not hold properly for one who examines the source — see there; also what he wrote that olive precedes what grows before sunrise, whose blessing is borei peri haAdamah, as written siman 202 — it precedes barley; it does not appear to me we precede the seven-species type except when its fruit is finished, for Scripture is not praised in something unfit — and so too in Rosh, who challenged Beit HaGedolot from that he blesses on wheat and barley before the vine, and there is no answer that one can say it deals with chewing, but perforce as written; and so too Bach wrote that in chewing wheat flour for everyone it is not of the seven species. And in Levush's gloss the questioner wrote: in truth dates precede everything since its blessing is important, and the blessing of borei minei mezonot precedes wine because it is important and also earlier in the verse, and the blessing of olive precedes the blessing of borei minei mezonot because it is earlier in the verse — for precedence in the verse is better than the importance of the blessing of borei minei mezonot, and the importance of wine is better than precedence in the verse; but borei minei mezonot, since it is important and earlier, precedes wine, end of his words. And in the hints it implies that a cooked dish of barley and olive — olive precedes — see there; and so too explicitly in Semak's gloss — see there and seif 5 and seif 6; and in chewing wheat the cherished is better, as written seif 1; and when both are cherished one should precede what is written earlier in the verse.
