Seif 4. Whichever appears earlier in the verse, “a land flowing with milk and honey,” etc., takes precedence. Therefore dates precede grapes, because dates are second to the latter occurrence of the word “land,” while grapes are third to the first occurrence of “land.” Note: At first glance this requires study from the Mishnah in Menachos, chapter Kol HaKorbanos Tzibbur, Mishnah 5, which states that the second item associated with the first category and the first item associated with the second category are equal, and likewise the third associated with the first, the second associated with the second, and the first associated with the third are equal. Why do we not say there that the first item in the second category is superior because it is first in relation to the olive itself? Or here, conversely, that the third species adjoining the first occurrence of “land” should be equal to the second species adjoining the latter occurrence of “land”? Perhaps there the matter depends upon the underlying rationale rather than relative importance, whereas here it depends solely upon relative importance. Therefore, proximity to the latter occurrence of “land” gives precedence. This still requires study. Examine carefully.
