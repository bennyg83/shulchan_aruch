Therefore Taz agreed with the questioner in Levush, and likewise Magen Avraham sign 8, see there — that olive precedes borei minei mezonot of barley; and this is even though borei minei mezonot is somewhat more important against borei peri haEtz of apples as in borei minei mezonot of rice; but it is not so important as borei peri haAdamah relative to shehakol — therefore precedence in the verse is more important, as Magen Avraham wrote sign 8. And Mishna Berurah who wrote in borei minei mezonot "more specific" — and indeed Magen Avraham sign 7 that more specific is difficult: if borei minei mezonot is more specific than wine, even without precedence it should precede like wine, and Tur did not mention more; one may say somewhat more specific with the advantage of precedence in the verse as written in sign 8. And one may say more specific — and wine is important on its own in that it establishes a blessing in several places, and this explanation is not sufficient for borei minei mezonot to be like borei peri haAdamah and shehakol. And what Taz [wrote] from Rosh that borei minei mezonot is important from borei peri haEtz and that Tur does not hold so — ostensibly one may say they are somewhat important but not on the scale of borei peri haAdamah and shehakol, and earlier in the verse is better as Magen Avraham wrote sign 8. Except from what Rosh wrote Magen Avraham sign 1 — one should not bring proof to Rashi from all that is earlier in the verse precedes — thus wheat precedes vine, not like Beit HaGedolot that deals with borei minei mezonot; and still how does barley precede olive — thus borei minei mezonot precedes olive. And this is an error, for "earlier" means first for land 2. However one should not bring proof that olive precedes borei minei mezonot of barley for us, for the verse does not deal with chewing wheat, and necessarily a dish — as the Mechaber wrote in seif 5, and all the more so for Rosh and Tur al hamichyah in Berachot — and necessarily wheat in a dish; and how did he say all that is earlier that olive is earlier to land 2 than barley is earlier? One may say all that is earlier in simplicity refers to this — know that HaMotzi is certainly more important than all, even than olive; and in borei minei mezonot of wheat — nevertheless Rosh wrote the verse may be established on HaMotzi; one need not elaborate.
