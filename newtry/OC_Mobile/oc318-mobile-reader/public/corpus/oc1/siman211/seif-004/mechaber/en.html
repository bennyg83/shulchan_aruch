Whichever species appears earlier in the verse 'a land of wheat and barley' takes precedence for the blessing. The second occurrence of the word 'land' interrupts the sequence, and whatever is closer to it is considered more important than something later in relation to the first occurrence of 'land.' Therefore dates take precedence over grapes, because dates are second to the second 'land,' while grapes are third to the first 'land.' {Rama: This applies specifically when he eats grapes as they are. But if he made wine from them, for which a distinct blessing, Borei Pri HaGafen, is recited, the wine is more important and takes precedence for the blessing. A cooked dish made from the 5 species of grain, however, is more important than the blessing over wine. Anything stated closer to the first 'land' takes precedence over something stated closer to the second 'land' when they are equally distant from their respective occurrence of 'land.' [Tur]}
