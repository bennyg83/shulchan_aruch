They were. Taz on Berachot, Mishna Berurah, and Magen Avraham sign 1. And what he wrote "and now he desires the second he precedes" — these are Bach's words; Magen Avraham brought them sign 2. And even though in sign 3 he answered this difficulty as Magen Avraham wrote in sign 2 — that for the seven species we follow precedence in the verse even when their blessings are not equal, unlike other species against the seven species where we need to say one may precede whichever — nevertheless it stands to reason likewise that one may precede whichever, and he did not write "there is no precedence," meaning it depends on what he desires now; if not so, the seven-species type would be better even when their blessings are not equal (but in Magen Avraham sign 2 he wrote that Rosh's language does not imply so). And Semak: when their blessings are not equal the cherished is better even for R' Yehudah; the reason — when equal it does not depend on the eater's will since one exempts its fellow, it is fit that the seven-species type should exempt other species; but when their blessings are not equal there is no exemption — it depends on the eater's mind regarding what is always cherished to him, and it is fit to precede it — see Prisha. And what he wrote that Semak explained "whichever he wishes" means most of the time — I did not understand at all which explanation fits this, and we do not find in the Gemara the language "whichever he wishes he precedes," for Semak states his own reason. And Rambam (in seif 2) in chapter 8 of Berachot halachah 13 ruled like the Sages of R' Yehudah that the cherished is better, and holds cherished is what is now cherished to him; therefore when one is cherished there is no distinction — whether their blessings are equal he precedes the cherished like the Sages, or when their blessings are not equal, for all agree R' Yehudah concedes the cherished thus is better (like Semak's explanation). And he ruled halachah with no resolution (it is possible he holds the first view anonymously when he writes "some say," unlike where he writes "and for Rambam"; but Rambam has no resolution, and nevertheless Rosh against Semak — "some say" halachah is like Rosh, and one may say Rambam is like Semak in this — understand). If so, the halachah is thus: when equal and one exempts its fellow — if he wants to act like Rosh or like Rambam he may do so; he should not fix halachah one way sometimes thus and sometimes thus. When there is no seven-species type among them — he precedes the cherished per all. And the word that R' Yehudah is "required" — when their blessings are not equal there is only precedence and no exemption; if the seven-species type he desires now and the other species is cherished to him — no resolution: for Rosh he takes the seven-species type that is important and he desires it now; for Semak he takes the cherished; and one who acts per his master acts, etc. And per his method in what Bach brought regarding cherished when there is no seven-species type — no resolution between Rosh and Rambam (Raavad seems a scribal error) — one acts per his master, etc. And if a seven-species type and other species and equal, and he wants now the seven-species type and the other species is cherished to him — ostensibly he takes the seven-species type, for otherwise it is not per any authority; requires study for now.

And to me the poor man — there are reflections here, and I set them forth with God's help. Boser on which one blesses borei peri haAdamah in doubt — as in siman 202 seif 2: if he has boser and borei peri haAdamah, which precedes? For it is possible he should bless on the more specific species on radish with borei peri haAdamah and exempt the boser in doubt, and the certain item would also be exempted. Also pomegranate peel cooked in honey — if we say it has something of the doubt, or borei peri haEtz as Magen Avraham wrote siman 202 sign 17 — if he precedes radish to it, etc. However it is possible this is not praise of the land of Israel for boser or pomegranate peel that is not good except after cooking — Magen Avraham below sign 8; and we came to the dispute of Magen Avraham and Levush there. Also whether one needs intent thus or not — matter of siman 206. And what he wrote there further: I will inform you that a dish from other species with a little wheat flour in it, and a dish of barley alone — one may say the barley dish precedes the seven species for the dish of other species, even regarding borei minei mezonot importance, as in siman 208 seif 2 regarding importance — a dish that is entirely barley is more important — Eliyah Rabbah sign 14, see there.

In Chiddushei there we wrote: Rashba and Rosh held equal etrog and grapes; it is possible they held specifically thus and not wheat and radish, for they are not of the seven species — wheat, for the verse calls it bread; and per his view that al hamichyah and birkat hamazon, and chewing wheat with them — and why learn that bread interrupted the matter and wheat refers to bread (and thus porridge for him too is possible per the Sages). From this it is somewhat difficult per what Tur wrote here that chewing wheat is possibly of the seven species; and in Berachot he holds al hamichyah and chewing one blesses only with them — and Rashi held comparable to olive and apples; and why not vine and apples? Ostensibly when eating one grape as in siman 210 one blesses al hamichyah and exempts with them — apples as in siman 208 seif 13; if so, even for the Sages who ruled the cherished is better here, since the final blessing one blesses al hamichyah necessarily and exempts with them for apples — we should make the first blessing likewise to bless on the seven-species type and exempt apples; therefore he depicts olive that has a pit and is not a whole entity and less than the measure — understand this. And nevertheless it requires study if the law is thus that the first blessing should be on the cherished and the final on the seven-species type and he exempts with them for apples, and they are not related to each other; and still requires study.
