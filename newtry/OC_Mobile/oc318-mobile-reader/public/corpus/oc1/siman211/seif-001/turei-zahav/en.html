If they were before him, etc. In the mishnah they dispute — R' Yehudah [holds] the seven-species type is better; and the Sages say whichever of the five he wishes he blesses. Ula said: dispute when their blessings are equal, and with blessing on one of them its fellow is exempted; but when their blessings are not equal he needs two blessings, such as radish and olive — he blesses on this and returns and blesses on this. Rosh ruled like R' Yehudah and holds R' Yehudah disputes only regarding a seven-species type; but when there is no seven-species type he holds we follow the cherished, and cherished means most of the time — and this is when equal; but when their blessings are not equal we do not follow the seven-species type. And "whichever he wishes" means even if one is more cherished most times and now he desires the second — he takes what he wishes to precede. And Semak explained "whichever he wishes" means the cherished most of the time — automatically it is when their blessings are not equal like when equal; and this is not the name of seven-species type — and this is "some say." And Rambam ruled like the Sages — we follow the cherished; and cherished he holds is everything that is now cherished to him, not most of the time — only when both are cherished or neither is cherished, when one is more cherished than its fellow we all follow the seven-species type or precedence in the verse, whether when equal per halachah that we rule like the Sages, or when their blessings are not equal per halachah that all hold thus. And regarding practical halachah there is no concern here, for in all ways he has what to rely on: if blessings equal and there is a seven-species type — one who wants to take the seven-species type and exempt the second from blessing does well like R' Yehudah and as Rosh ruled. And if he wants to take the second cherished to him and exempt the seven-species type — he does well like the Sages and as Rambam ruled. And if there is no seven-species type among them — it is obvious he may take the cherished; and likewise when blessings are not equal and he must precede one before its fellow in blessing — there is also advantage to seven-species type and advantage to cherished, and he does as he wishes in precedence. And regarding cherished he may rely on two ways: if cherished to him most times like Rosh, or if cherished to him now like Rambam — so it appears to me, since we have no halachic resolution as to which authority.
