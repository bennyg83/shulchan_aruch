Seif 1, they were, etc. Like R' Yehudah, for Rav Yosef holds like him, as written that R' Yehudah, and if you say, etc.; and Tosafot there s.v. amar, etc., and it is not clear, etc. — meaning as R' Yehudah and Rosh wrote that what is the question — perhaps dates are dearer to R' Hamnuna; and per Rosh's view it must be that their blessings are equal, and emend "Rav Yosef said" as written below:

And if, etc. For R' Yehudah explains thus the sages — Rosh there.

And if their blessings are not equal, etc. There they disagree on this; and since Ulla holds specifically when equal — the other is one against two; and so the poskim ruled. And it appears the text should read "R' Yosef," and he holds that R' Yehudah also discusses when their blessings are equal, as in the incident of R' Chisda and R' Huna and the like, and as Rosh wrote there — for otherwise R' Yosef would hold like the other, as Tosafot challenged in s.v. the aforementioned, and one may answer when they are not dear, etc.; and this is per their view, not so per Rosh's view as above.

And even, etc. Rosh there, and not like Tosafot there s.v. aval, and his proof from what is written in s.v. m'vareich, etc., 40 — that there is no superiority in anything from what R' Yehudah concedes to the sages; but the view of R' Yehudah and Rashba and the anonymous passage is like Tosafot that the dearer is preferable, and this is what "some say," etc.; and so there 39a: if your friend, etc. — even though in Bava Kamma it appears that a slice of borei peri haAdamah, as written above, what is it not, etc.; and what is said there that all agree, etc. — there is no difficulty, for all hold regarding borei peri haAdamah for abundance that nevertheless the dearer is preferable, etc.; and on Tosafot there s.v. chaviv:

And it is called dear, etc. R' Yehudah and Rosh, and as our text: the dearer species is preferable.
