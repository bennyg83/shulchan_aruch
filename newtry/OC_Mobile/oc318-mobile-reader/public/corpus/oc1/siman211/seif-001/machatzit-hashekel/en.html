Know that we learned daf 40b: they were before him many species. R' Yehuda says: if among them there is a seven-species type (meaning that the Land of Israel was praised through them), as it is written "a land of wheat and barley, vine, fig, and pomegranate, a land of olive oil" (and olive is only one thing that produces oil) "and honey" (meaning dates) — on it he blesses; and the Sages say: he blesses on whichever he wants. And on daf 41 Ula said: the dispute is when their blessings are equal (for both are borei peri haEtz and one is discharged by his fellow's blessing) — R' Yehuda holds the seven-species type is better and the Sages hold the cherished is better; but when their blessings are not equal (Rashi explained: such as radish whose blessing is borei peri haAdamah and olive whose blessing is borei peri haEtz) s.v. one blesses on this and returns and blesses on this — there is a dispute: Rashi, Rosh, and R' Hai Gaon explain that when their blessings are not equal, both R' Yehuda and the Sages agree that the superiority of the seven-species type and the superiority of the cherished are set aside and they bless on whichever they want; and they also hold that cherished means what is cherished to him always, even if now he desires another species. Semag and those who stand in his method hold that when their blessings are not equal R' Yehuda concedes to the Sages that we follow the cherished, and cherished is explained as above; and all the aforementioned poskim ruled like R' Yehuda — they are the two views brought in Shulchan Aruch seif 1. Rambam — who is in seif 2 — disagrees in two matters: he ruled like the Sages that the cherished is better than the seven-species type, and he also disagrees in the explanation of cherished, holding cherished means what is cherished to him at that moment. From now we stand on the Shulchan Aruch's view and Magen Avraham's view:

(s.k. 1) He precedes, etc. even if it is half a fruit, etc. — deals with siman 168 in the Gemara there, meaning what they said daf 39b: a slice of wheat bread and a whole loaf of barley bread — s.v. one blesses on the slice of wheat bread and discharges the whole loaf of barley, meaning because Scripture preceded wheat; if so, the same law applies here, and likewise the other laws Magen Avraham wrote: if both are of one species he precedes the whole — this too is learned from siman 168, as is explained there; and likewise one whole and one cherished — the whole is better, as in siman 168: if the whole is coarse bread and the slice is fine bread and large, nevertheless the whole is better.
