He precedes from the seven species. And even if it is half a fruit and the other is whole; and if both are of the seven species, or both are not of the seven species — then he precedes the whole. Deals with siman 168 and in the Gemara there; nevertheless, if one is whole and one cherished — the whole is better:

And even if it is not cherished. It is difficult: behold Tur wrote wheat precedes olive, and as written at the end of seif 4 because it is earlier in the verse — all the more so here that radish is not of the seven species at all; and Bach answered: this is what he says — whichever he wants to eat now with more dearness he blesses on it, and if he does not now desire one species more than the other he precedes that which is habitually cherished to him, and if both are cherished he precedes the seven-species type and what is earlier in the verse precedes, end of his words; and his words do not hold up in Rosh's words, who wrote explicitly that when their blessings are not equal, since one is not discharged by his fellow's blessing, there is no superiority in the seven-species type and not in the cherished — for this is not a superiority if he blesses on one before his fellow, but only when the second is discharged by his blessing on its account; and the correct view is that when one is discharged by his fellow's blessing and this is a superiority, then we follow the seven-species type or the cherished, for this too is a superiority; but when their blessings are not equal and one only needs to precede one, whichever he wants he precedes even if both are of the seven species and cherished — for this is not a superiority at all that they precede the other, as Rosh wrote; however, if both are of the seven species we follow what is earlier in the verse, for since Scripture preceded it we too precede it; but what was not written at all in Scripture has no law of precedence — and similarly Shelah wrote; and see in the hints and examine well; and so too in Rosh in what he wrote "and one should not bring proof for their words" from that which we said everything earlier, etc. — it implies for Rashi "if so," and behold Rashi holds there is no precedence at all when their blessings are not equal — rather perforce as written above siman 216 seif 11:
