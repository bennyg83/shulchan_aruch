And some say Taz: borei peri haEtz against shehakol and borei peri haAdamah against shehakol — they precede even though shehakol is cherished to him — Magen Avraham sign 3. And borei peri haEtz and borei peri haAdamah — when both are equal, Yeshuot Yaakov like Beit HaGedolot, and he emerges either way; and regarding cherished, borei peri haAdamah — he precedes the cherished like the first view anonymously and Magen Avraham sign 4, and it will be explained. And behold date honey that shehakol praises the land of Israel — and it is cherished to him; and another species of borei peri haAdamah — it appears borei peri haAdamah precedes likewise in Chiddushei.

And know if there is something whose blessing is in doubt whether borei peri haEtz or borei peri haAdamah — he blesses in doubt borei peri haAdamah; if he has another thing of borei peri haAdamah and borei peri haEtz, it is better that he eat the three together as in siman 202 seif 11 and siman 208 seif 1. And if he has only something whose blessing is borei peri haEtz — he blesses borei peri haEtz and intends not to remove the doubt, for otherwise how can he afterward bless borei peri haAdamah? However one may say he blesses first borei peri haAdamah and immediately borei peri haEtz and eats — he fulfills either way, for there is interruption: if he eats first borei peri haAdamah it is interruption, or if he eats borei peri haEtz and immediately after blessing he must not interrupt in speech one word as in siman 206. And if he said "Blessed are You... Who creates the fruit of the ground and tree" and intended both — if we say even one word is interruption, there is room to ask whether he fulfilled borei peri haEtz; and if we say we require three words, there is room to ask whether he fulfilled both. From Rabbenu Yonah in Magen Avraham sign 8: "Who creates the lights of fire, Who creates the heavens" — he erred; not so here where his intent was for both — it is possible he fulfills; requires study in all this.
