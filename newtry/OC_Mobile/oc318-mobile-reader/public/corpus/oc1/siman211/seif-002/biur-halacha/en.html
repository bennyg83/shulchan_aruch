And if he does not want, etc., from the seven species, etc. — This law when their blessings are equal is according to all; and when their blessings are not equal, such as radish and olive, it depends on the two views brought in seif 1. And per what we wrote there in Mishna Berurah that the halacha follows those who say there that even when their blessings are not equal the law of precedence also applies — if so, when there is no superiority of the cherished, we certainly follow the seven-species type.
