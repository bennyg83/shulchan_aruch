2) There: He gives precedence to a species of the seven, etc. Since their blessings are identical and one is covered by the blessing over the other, a species of the seven is preferable. Tur. The reason is that since Eretz Yisrael was praised through these species, it is fitting to give them precedence for the blessing. Levush; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. The requirement to give precedence to a species of the seven applies specifically if he wishes to eat from both. If he does not wish to eat both, he recites the blessing over the one he wishes to eat, even if a species of the seven is before him, as stated below in the gloss to seif 5; see there.
