And for Rambam. Taz wrote regarding halachah: there is no concern here, for in all ways he has what to rely on — if the blessings are equal and there is a seven-species species, one who wants to take the seven-species species and exempt the second from a blessing does well (like the sages) [like R' Yehudah] and as Rosh ruled; and if he wants to take the second that is dearer to him and exempt the seven-species species he does well (like R' Yehudah) [like the sages] and as Rambam ruled. And if there is no seven-species species among them, it is obvious he may take the dearer one. And likewise when the blessings are not equal and he must precede one before the other in blessing — there is also an advantage to the seven-species species and an advantage to the dearer one, and he may do as he wishes in precedence. And regarding dearness he may rely on two ways: whether it is dearer to him most times, as Rosh — or whether it is dearer to him now (as Raavad) [as Rambam] — as stated above; since we have no resolution, the halachah is as whichever authority — end of his words.
