There, seif 6. They were before him a dish of spelt flour, etc. NB see Magen Avraham s.k. 13 — what he wrote: they were before him wine and a barley dish and an olive — requires further study what he should do, for wine precedes olive and barley precedes wine and olive precedes barley. Behold we find something similar in the Talmud Zevachim daf 90b — they asked: bird sin-offering, animal burnt-offering, and ma'aser — which precedes? Here they interpreted: the sacrifice type is better. In the West they say: animal burnt-offering enters with bird sin-offering, etc. — see Rashi there, who enters with it the importance of animal burnt-offering that comes with it, and it is more important than ma'aser which is kodesh kodashim, etc. — see there in Rashi at length. If so, here too regarding barley — there is in it a superiority that it precedes the vine from everywhere; behold the Torah's decree is that olive precedes it. And behold in barley too there is a superiority that there is wine, which is also an important blessing like borei peri haGafen — for behold what in borei peri haGafen is better than olive is because this is borei peri haGafen and this is borei peri haEtz; and behold in barley too there is this superiority — yet olive precedes it by Torah decree. If so, now too olive precedes barley, then barley, then wine — and it is very similar to there. And so ruled Rambam in chapter 9 of Hilchot Temidin uMusafin. And per Rambam's language it fits better the aforementioned law that he wrote there — since bird sin-offering precedes burnt-offering and burnt-offering precedes ma'aser, etc. — so here too, since olive precedes barley and barley precedes wine, therefore he precedes olive, then barley, then wine. One cannot say the reverse that wine should precede because it precedes olive and olive precedes barley — for there too one could say burnt-offering precedes animal ma'aser and animal ma'aser precedes bird sin-offering; perforce the reason is that since the superiority of burnt-offering is that it is a sacrifice type, the Torah's decree that bird sin-offering precedes is nullified — if so, here too regarding wine that precedes olive because it is an important blessing in itself — this superiority exists in barley too, which is also a blessing of borei minei mezonot — nevertheless the Torah's decree is that olive precedes; if so, now too it is so: olive precedes, then barley, then wine. So it appears clear and correct in my humble opinion, and it is clear proof in my humble opinion; examine well with God's help.
