But chewing wheat, etc. This is Beit HaGedolot's view, who holds borei peri haEtz precedes borei peri haAdamah; and Tur concluded on this. But per our teacher of blessed memory that they are equal — it is possible it deals even with chewing wheat — end of his words. And Beit Yosef explained he wrote the language "possible" because one may say Rosh concedes here that wheat written in the verse is not in chewing but in bread, since the verse highlights afterward "a land in which you will not eat bread in poverty," etc.; and I did not know what arose in his mind, for Rosh wrote explicitly "and even if you want to establish it in chewing wheat," etc. And therefore certainly one cannot establish it in chewing; rather it appears clear therefore Tur wrote the language "possible," because Rosh wrote: "and even if you want to establish it in chewing, one may say thus — for all that is earlier in the verse, whether in things whose blessings are equal like wheat relative to barley," etc. — end of his words. Rosh's intent: the truth is that even when their blessings are not equal there is advantage to precedence, even though then there is no advantage to cherished or seven-species type as above in his view; nevertheless one may say that also the advantage of precedence is only when equal, but not when their blessings are not equal, like the advantage of cherished and seven-species type. Therefore Tur wrote the language "possible" here, since one may say in chewing wheat precedence does not help against borei peri haEtz because their blessings are not equal — as it appears to me, straightforward.
