On mezonot. Even if the bread is of barley and the mezonot foods are of wheat — see there.

From both. And even if he wants to eat from both but did not bring the dearer one — he need not wait for it (Beit Yosef, Magen Avraham). And when they bring before him spiced condiment that is borei peri haAdamah or borei peri haEtz and burnt wine — he blesses first on the spiced condiment even if the wine is dearer to him; and if he also intends to drink the wine he blesses on it first in any case, and this is straightforward — see there, and Taz's Order of Precedence. HaMotzi. On wheat — borei minei mezonot. Olive. On barley — vine, fig, pomegranate. Borei peri haAdamah and borei peri haEtz precede shehakol. For borei peri haAdamah and borei peri haEtz we follow the dearer even if the second is of the seven species. If both were dear we follow the seven-species species and the one earlier in the verse precedes. If both are not of the seven species one should precede borei peri haEtz. And regarding dearness — we Ashkenazim follow Rosh, that the species usually dear to him (as written seif 1). And if both are dear we follow whichever he desires now, as Rambam wrote — Magen Avraham; and see Taz sk 1. And the blessing of eating and drinking precedes smell. If before him were wine, a barley dish, and olive — uncertain what to do, for wine precedes olive and barley precedes wine, and olive precedes barley, as written in sk 7 — see there, Magen Avraham. (And in Eliyah Rabbah he ruled he blesses on the barley dish first and afterward on wine and olive — and he brought proof for his words — see there.)
