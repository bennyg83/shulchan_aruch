But chewing Taz analyzed the word "possible" in Tur — he brought what Rosh wrote Magen Avraham sign 1, and one should not bring proof to Rashi that borei peri haEtz equals borei peri haAdamah from precedence in the verse — thus wheat precedes vine; and one may say in borei minei mezonot, and even if you want to establish it in chewing wheat, one may say thus in things equal in blessings — end of his words. And behold per what Taz wrote in sign 3 — two precedences: one of seven-species type to other species, and when their blessings are not equal there is no precedence, and precedence in the verse within the seven-species type itself is more important, as Magen Avraham also wrote in sign 2. If so, why did he not answer Beit HaGedolot per its plain sense that earlier precedes — wheat borei peri haAdamah precedes borei peri haEtz because the verse preceded them; and that Beit HaGedolot with seven-species type among other species — then there is a distinction between them, meaning Rosh too is not clear that likewise with precedence in the verse when their blessings are not equal there is no precedence, as seven-species type over other species; and he wrote thus to himself — nevertheless it may be resolved in chewing wheat and earlier when equal; and therefore Tur wrote "possible" that he saw his father of blessed memory was in doubt whether equal or whether precedence in the verse applies, etc. However the Mechaber's view is not like Beit HaGedolot but as Beit Yosef wrote that chewing wheat the verse does not deal with, as Magen Avraham wrote sign 8, see there.
