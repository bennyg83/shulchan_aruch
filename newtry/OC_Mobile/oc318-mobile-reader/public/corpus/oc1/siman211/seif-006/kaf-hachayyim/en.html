If neither is a species of the seven, Borei Pri HaEtz precedes Borei Pri HaAdamah, and we do not follow which is favored; see there. This is also implied below at the end of seif 3, which says that some hold Borei Pri HaEtz takes precedence, apparently even when the item requiring Borei Pri HaAdamah is favored; see there. The universal practice is likewise to give precedence to Borei Pri HaEtz over Borei Pri HaAdamah even when the ground produce is favored. It appears to me that the reason is that according to Maran's unqualified ruling here, when the blessings differ, meaning Borei Pri HaEtz and Borei Pri HaAdamah, one may give precedence to whichever he wishes and we do not follow the favored one. Since there is another opinion that Borei Pri HaEtz takes precedence, the practice developed to give Borei Pri HaEtz precedence in order to satisfy the 'some say' opinion in seif 3 as well; see there.
