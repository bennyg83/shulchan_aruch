Since they are important. Meaning they are also included in wheat and barley of the verse, as Tur wrote siman 208 — otherwise why would Scripture need wheat and barley; nevertheless in oat and spelt, which are a species of barley, olive precedes, as written seif 4. This is the general rule of the order of superiorities: hamotzi, borei minei mezonot on wheat, borei peri haGafen, olive, borei minei mezonot on barley, vine, fig, and pomegranate; borei peri haAdamah and borei peri haEtz precede shehakol; for borei peri haAdamah and borei peri haEtz we follow the cherished even if the second is of the seven species; if both were cherished we follow the seven-species type and what is earlier in the verse precedes; if both are not of the seven species one should precede borei peri haEtz; and regarding cherished we Ashkenazim follow Rosh in the species habitually cherished, as written seif 1; and if both are cherished we follow whichever he desires now, as Rambam wrote; and the blessing on eating and drinking precedes smell — see siman 216.
