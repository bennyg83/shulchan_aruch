(6) There, whichever he wants he precedes, etc. — however Avudraham letter 2 wrote that even if their blessings are not equal he precedes the seven-species type; and if among them there is not of the seven species, borei peri haEtz precedes borei peri haAdamah and we do not follow the cherished; see there. And so too below at the end of seif 3 he wrote that some say borei peri haEtz precedes — and it implies that even if borei peri haAdamah is cherished, nevertheless borei peri haEtz is first; see there. And so people practice — to precede borei peri haEtz to borei peri haAdamah even if borei peri haAdamah is cherished; and it seems to me the reason is because per our master's anonymous ruling here, when their blessings are not equal — namely borei peri haEtz and borei peri haAdamah — whichever he wants he precedes and we do not follow the cherished; and since there is also another reasoning that borei peri haEtz is first, they practiced to precede borei peri haEtz in order to fulfill also the "some say" view in seif 3; see there.
