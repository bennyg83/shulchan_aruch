And it is not of the seven species, etc. — behold per what the Mechaber wrote above in seif 1 in the first view, which is the anonymous view, that where their blessings are not equal we do not follow the seven-species type — there is no place for these words; but the truth is that this matter is not from Hagahot Maimoniot in the name of the Rambam; and it is known that the Rambam's view is that even when their blessings are not equal the seven-species type is better.
