Beer of barley and wheat and tatarkei — if there is a law of precedence in them or not — matter of siman 482 in Magen Avraham sign 2 in a dispute stated whether the law of precedence applies to them. And per what Magen Avraham wrote sign 13 in the name of Levush — date honey has no law of seven species, all the more so beer that has no law of seven-species type. Blessing of eating precedes smell as Magen Avraham wrote sign 13 and siman 216. Eating relative to drinking — there is no precedence if their blessings are equal, only regarding cherished and what he desires now. The author said: I saw fit to write here something containing a doubtful dispute — not to eat except within the meal and the like: (1) All five grain species — not to eat them raw or parboiled, and whole kernels only within the meal — R' Chaim Vital seif 64. (2) Orange peel cooked in honey and sugar — three views: borei peri haEtz, borei peri haAdamah, and shehakol — matter of siman 202 in Magen Avraham sign 17 and Taz sign 14 sign 15 — it does not help within the meal, for it did not come to accompany bread; if he has borei peri haEtz, borei peri haAdamah, and shehakol he eats this with them and one may look to bless shehakol. (3) Rice and millet that were crushed — siman 208 seif 66 and Magen Avraham sign 9 — to be stringent, fit to eat within the meal; and one who is not stringent may conduct like the world's custom. But whole-grain rice — one may say there is doubt whether borei peri haAdamah or borei minei mezonot — Magen Avraham there sign 10 without crushing. (4) Fruit water through cooking and soaking above — siman 202 seif 11 — doubt regarding final blessing within the meal, and first it is fit that he have borei peri haEtz and shehakol, etc. (5) Grape skins on which they placed figs — siman 204 seif 66, Taz, and Magen Avraham signs 11 and 17 — final blessing doubtful, and also first whether borei peri haEtz or vine or shehakol, see there. (6) Final blessing on a whole entity or drinking a kezayit of liquids — there is doubt from R' Yeshayah sign 10; therefore he should not eat a whole entity but crush it, or a kezayit, and not drink except less than a kezayit or a revi'it. And a cup of blessing he must necessarily drink a revi'it — siman 190 seif 63; and likewise all liquids. And wine in general — Taz siman 210 sign 1. (7) Kinds of confections from ground fruit through cooking for deterioration — only honey improves; there is doubt in siman 204 Taz sign 3 and Magen Avraham sign 5, see there, whether borei peri haAdamah or shehakol; and one who blesses borei peri haAdamah — they do not rebuke him, see there in Pri Megadim. (8) Kinds of preserves from tatarkei and the like that are not of the five grains — there is doubt, for Taz in siman 208 sign 11 wrote shehakol, and there in Pri Megadim I wrote one may say borei peri haAdamah, see there. (9) Arba kanfot shotim in a garden or field — there is doubt whether borei peri haAdamah or shehakol — siman 204 Taz sign 4 and in Pri Megadim there. (10) Paweedla — siman 202 seif 67 — doubt whether borei peri haEtz or shehakol; and boser too should be prevented because it has no measure there seif 11, see there; and one who is stringent should prepare other species too; and one who is not stringent on the first blessing blesses shehakol — but on the final blessing there is no remedy — let the wise hear and take instruction.
