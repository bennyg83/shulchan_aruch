(א) If the water is deep etc. — for if they are not deep and he can sustain it in its place so that it should not die, they do not permit him to place pillows and beds in order to nullify kelim from their prepared state:
(ב) He brings pillows and beds etc. — and likewise he can place other kelim under it so that it can rise; and he mentioned pillows and beds to include even what is not fit to be used through this while the water is still on it — even when the animal rises it is nevertheless permitted, for tza'ar ba'alei chayim comes, which is d'oraisa [from what the Torah warned regarding the mitzvah of unloading a burden from the animal], and it pushes aside the prohibition of nullifying kelim from their prepared state, which is only d'rabbanan, for they decreed not to nullify on Shabbat a kli from what it is prepared for:
(ג) And he places under it — but it is forbidden to lift it by hand, for all living creatures are muktzeh; and even though there is tza'ar ba'alei chayim it is forbidden, for we do not compare rabbinic decrees one to another [Magen Avraham and Tosafot Shabbati]; and see in R' Akiva Eiger that he brought that there are poskim who are lenient even to lift it by hand if it is impossible by means of pillows and beds; and by means of a gentile all agree that it is permitted to lift it — and this is preferable to placing pillows and beds and other kelim under it:
