19) There, gloss: Meaning a kind of small saddle, etc. Perishah, paragraph 11, writes that it is a kind of garment used to warm the animal. Levush Serad writes that the text should apparently say 'a large saddle,' meaning that it rests upon the entire body. These are his words. It appears to me, however, that it is called a kind of small saddle even though it covers the entire body because it is not as thick as the saddle placed upon an animal when carrying a load or being ridden.
