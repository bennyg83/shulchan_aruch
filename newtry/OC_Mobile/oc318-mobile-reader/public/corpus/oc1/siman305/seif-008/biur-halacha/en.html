(א) Remove mard'at Shabbat horse and donkey — Mechaber stam like Semag; Gra wrote Rashi all animals may remove mard'at and saddle — only donkey cold would freeze if not removed — other animals; depends sugya interpretation; Gra plain sugya like Rashi.

(ב) Saddle forbidden donkey and horse — Avnei Nezer teaches like Tur placing saddle donkey like mard'at; from Gra seems agrees Shulchan Aruch.
