(א) It is permitted to place a mard'at — that is, not to go out to reshut ha'rabbim, but only that it should be in the courtyard:
(ב) Because of cold — that is, that for this reason we are not concerned about Shabbat trouble:
(ג) That he not tie it — that is, even with a knot that is not permanent:
(ד) It is forbidden to place on it — because of unnecessary trouble; and see in Beur HaGra that he wrote that according to Rashi, also on a horse and likewise for all animals it is permitted to place a mard'at in the courtyard, for they too have pain from cold and the warming helps; and all this is stam; but when the cold is great and we see that it harms a horse, and likewise in hot days when flies are many and distress the horse, all agree that it is permitted to leave in the courtyard a mard'at or other garment on it, for it is no worse than a donkey — provided he is careful not to lean on the animal at the time of covering:
(ה) And to remove a mard'at etc. — for it is unnecessary trouble; and even if it already warmed through a burden that it carried from erev Shabbat:
(ו) Whether from a horse — see in Be'er Heitev:
(ז) Whether to take — the reason is as above regarding a mard'at; and what he wrote "whether to place" — the reason is that since it warms only a little, it is unnecessary trouble:
