(א) Seif 8 but on the horse. Semag and as stated: for warming there is pain etc. as people say etc.; and explain that "as people say" refers to the first part "for warming there is pain," unlike a horse; but Rashi's view is that the prohibition is only to remove the mard'at from the donkey, but from a horse whether to remove or to place is permitted, for he explains "donkey" etc.; therefore forbidden to remove it.

(ב) And to remove. There "here for cooling" etc. "for cooling" — why pain? And he explains that likewise for all animals there is no pain from warming — unlike Rashi because of "as people say," but that there is no pain to the animal in this; but in Sefer HaTerumah and Mordechai they were uncertain; and for Rashi it is obvious from his words that it is permitted as above, for he wrote s.v. here for warming etc. but removing the saddle etc. for it will cool by itself as people say etc. — implies a horse is permitted; per Semag's view what is stated in the Gemara "as people say" refers to the first part as above; but for removing the saddle no reason is needed; and even for a horse it is forbidden. But for Rashi it is the reverse: for warming no reason is needed, and therefore a horse is lenient — everything is permitted on a horse; and the plain sense of the sugya accords with Rashi at the end.

(ג) And saddle forbidden. There saddle on etc. now to remove etc.; and see Rashi s.v. what is different from saddle; and even at the conclusion "here for warming" saddle does not warm as stated above and not with saddle even so; and even though with mard'at it is permitted because of warming as Rashi in the Mishnah and see Rashi there s.v. even with saddle etc.
