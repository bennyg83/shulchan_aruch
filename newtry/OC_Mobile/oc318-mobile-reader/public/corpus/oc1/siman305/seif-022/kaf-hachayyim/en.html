22) There: And it may not go out with a saddle, etc. A saddle does not benefit the donkey and is therefore a burden. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 9. Specifically a saddlecloth is beneficial because it rests upon the animal's body and warms it. A saddle warms only the place upon which it rests, and is therefore not clothing but a burden. Levush Serad.
