(א) That a living creature etc. — meaning that the name of burden does not apply to this so that one would need to prevent it because of the mitzvah of resting one's animal; and even though we say "a living creature carries itself," that is only d'oraisa, but d'rabbanan it is forbidden; and therefore it is forbidden for a person to carry a baby on Shabbat — they did not decree this shevut on an animal:

(ב) Its garments on it — but with a saddle there is no concern, for it is batel to the rider:

(ג) And not anything — and if some thing remained on it from erev Shabbat and it wishes on its own to go outside with it, the owner must prevent it so that it should not go out, because of the mitzvah of resting one's animal; but if there is nothing on it, even if it wishes to go outside beyond the techum, he is not obligated to prevent it from this; and only to take it out by hand beyond its techum is forbidden:
