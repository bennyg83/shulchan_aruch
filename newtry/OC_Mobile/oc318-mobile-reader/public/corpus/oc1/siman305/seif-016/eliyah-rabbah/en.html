Regarding what the Levush wrote, ‘and even at its side,’ etc., as a decree, etc., from the Rama it implies that use of a living creature was forbidden without the reason of a decree lest one cut a branch, namely, because of the requirement that one’s animal rest. So too it implies in Hagahos Maimoniyos, first chapter of Eruvin. I saw in the Derishah that they wrote as follows: What Tosafot wrote in Eruvin 43, at the end of s.v. malka, etc., that it is forbidden to use a living creature because of cutting a branch, it appears to me that one must emend it to ‘perhaps,’ and there are two reasons. These are his words. This requires study from Rashi’s explanation in Shabbos 155b, s.v. ka mishtamesh, and from Sefer HaTerumos, siman 225.
