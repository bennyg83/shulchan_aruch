And plugged pair (should read) — and see Magen Avraham note 6 and Eshel Avraham there it will be explained. Behold in a courtyard plugged is permitted even on its neck, for there is concern lest it fall and come to carry in the public domain — nevertheless permitted on an animal, for they did not decree because of the public domain; and in the public domain plugged is forbidden even woven in its garment, for it appears as one going to the market — see Tosafot 54b s.v. because and 58a s.v. lo and 53a s.v. Mahu: on an animal they did not decree because of the public domain — one can say their intent is thus: in a courtyard plugged on the neck is permitted and they did not decree because of the public domain; and this too is not so, for it is also a decree on a decree; but a matter forbidden in the public domain because of a burden — one can say they decreed animal in a courtyard because of the public domain like a person — see siman 308:1; and if so a seal on an animal per Levush 312 would be a burden — one can say in a courtyard forbidden because of the public domain; however from the conclusion of poskim it appears they did not distinguish animal in a courtyard because of the public domain — only a person who is hasty to go out; and even a slave for Tosafot 57b s.v. if (after it there) — unlike an animal that does not go out so much without the owner's permission — there is no decree; see Magen Avraham end and Eshel Avraham in note 6 there it will be explained further on this see there.
