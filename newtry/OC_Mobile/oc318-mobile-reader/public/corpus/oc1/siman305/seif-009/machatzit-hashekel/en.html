(א) (s.k. 9) brings in etc. as written siman 311 that even to move part of a limb from the dead is forbidden — so too Ran there.

(ב) moving by its body is permitted — meaning that he wrote there straw which is muktzeh — on a bed he shakes by its body even though we hold moving from the side was only permitted when moving is for the sake of something permitted; but when moving is for the sake of something forbidden, even moving from the side forbidden — nevertheless moving by its body is lighter than moving from the side, and even for the sake of something forbidden permitted; and if so Ran didn't need to give the reason of tza'ar ba'alei chayim — answer it is moving by its body; as Rabbenu Yonatan wrote there — meaning that even by its body if moving is for forbidden matter forbidden; nevertheless shaking straw called for permitted matter since he intends to shake in order to lie on the straw — if so it is for the sake of a person.
