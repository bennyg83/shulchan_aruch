And luba for a donkey (should read) — behold Rashi wrote there resen has several kinds, but not that it must specifically be promvia. Avodah Zarah and Magen Avraham note 2 and Bach wrote that horse and camel go out with promvia, and mule and donkey are equal — forbidden with promvia which is resen — unlike early authorities who wrote camel only with apsar and horse, mule, and donkey with resen which is promvia — it is reversed — see there; and Taz wrote that camel with promvia — from what is taught Shabbat 51b luvdakim and camel with apsar and not promvia — we learn from camel he cited it; and in our novellae we wrote from this that in the Mishnah one can say luvdakim promvia suffices, and Tosafot that it suffices even with apsar — if so horse with shir is also difficult, and the braita likewise. However that which is forbidden — a donkey whose occupation is bad — what is it to go out with promvia? A plain donkey certainly not; here we cannot elaborate. And what Tosafot wrote horse promvia Shabbat 52a s.v. Amar Shmuel 1 resen — one can say this is not promvia as stated above.
