(א) (s.k. 2) And horse etc. should read thus:

(ב) meaning that in Shulchan Aruch he wrote mule and donkey and horse — for this Magen Avraham wrote it must read only horse alone; but what he wrote mule and donkey is a mistake; and he brings proof that certainly donkey is a mistake, for he wrote seif 3 that a donkey is forbidden to go out with promvia which is a resen. And if so mule too certainly is a mistake, for mule and donkey have one law — so it is in Bach; but what he wrote etc. meaning Bach disputes this too on Shulchan Aruch and permits a camel with promvia; in this Magen Avraham agrees with Bach. And see Taz.
