(א) It is permitted to tell — and likewise it is permitted because of tza'ar ba'alei chayim to tell an idolater to warm geese that were already accustomed to warming and cannot again eat by themselves; but it is permitted only once a day, for afterward there is no tza'ar ba'alei chayim [responsum of Rama]; and it implies there that if there is no gentile, an Israelite may do the warming; and even though below in siman 324 seif 9 we forbade warming geese on Shabbat — here, since they cannot eat by themselves, it is permitted because of tza'ar ba'alei chayim; and it is good to do it through a minor. Rambam wrote: one who milks into a vessel is liable; into food he is exempt [and nevertheless there is a prohibition, for all patur exemptions of Shabbat are patur but forbidden]; and the milk is forbidden even if he milks into food, since the animal is not fit for eating it is muktzeh [Magen Avraham]:
