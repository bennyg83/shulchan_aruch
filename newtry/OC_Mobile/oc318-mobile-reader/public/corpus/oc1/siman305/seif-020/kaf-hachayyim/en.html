20) There: Provided that it was tied upon it before Shabbos. If it was not tied before Shabbos, the person's conduct on the preceding day did not demonstrate that the animal required it as clothing. Rashi. Rosh, however, writes in the name of the Yerushalmi that the reason is that tying it constitutes using a living creature. According to this, if one transgressed and tied it, or if a non-Jew did so, the animal may go out with it. According to the first reason, it may not. Perishah, paragraph 12, writes that Tur intends to forbid it and that Rosh also agrees; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 6, likewise follows those who forbid it and writes that the Shulchan Aruch also agrees. The Shulchan Aruch gives the Yerushalmi's reason in seif 8 but not here, because the Yerushalmi's reason is applied only stringently and not leniently, as in this case. He further writes that this is also implied by Rambam's commentary on the Mishnah and by Bartenura; see there. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 7, likewise follows those who forbid it and writes that this is contrary to Levush; see there. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 5, agrees.
