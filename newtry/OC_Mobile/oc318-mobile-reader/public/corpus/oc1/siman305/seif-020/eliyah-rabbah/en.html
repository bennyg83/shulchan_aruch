[Levush] And similarly more, etc. This requires study from what is written in Teshuvos Menachem Azaryah, siman 112: regarding an animal of a Jew, whose milk belongs half to a non-Jewish sharecropper, what is the law of allowing it to be milked on Shabbos? The responsum of Menachem Azaryah says: One who milks is extracting. Therefore, if its owner was insistent, or stipulated with the non-Jew that he assumes responsibility for it each and every Shabbos, it is permitted; if not, it is forbidden. These are his words. Here, however, they permit even when there is no sharecropper. Perhaps there he is speaking even where the milk does not cause it pain. This requires study. See siman 328, seif 13.
[Levush] And the maidservants are hired for the year. This is unnecessary, for they were hired from the outset for this purpose, as the Bach wrote. See siman 247, seif 7. One must say that since it is permitted to tell a non-Jew because of suffering of living creatures, this case is different.
[Levush] To purchase it, etc. He omitted what the Shulchan Aruch wrote, ‘for a small amount.’ Even more than this, I saw in the Tashbetz, siman 41, as follows: for a small amount, with an apple or with whatever item he wishes, even if it is not worth a perutah.
[Levush] For sometimes, etc. He added the word ‘sometimes’ to teach that if he is accustomed to do this, one must protest. With this, what the Magen Avraham left requiring study is resolved; examine it carefully. See siman 276, seif katan 13. Additionally, here we derive from the permissibility of cheeses after Shabbos. See siman 252, seif 4, and the end of siman 307.
