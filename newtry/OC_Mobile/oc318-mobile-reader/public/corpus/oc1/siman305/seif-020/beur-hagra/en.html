(א) Seif 20 permitted to tell etc. So they learned from the preceding tractate.

(ב) That the milk pains her. As stated regarding a woman 325:1; and see there Tosafot s.v. because.

(ג) And the milk. Yom Tov 3:1 like R' Yitzchak; and even R' Yosef agrees here.

(ד) And some say one must. This version disputes the first reasoning and does not hold that telling to milk is permitted at all; and even if they do it themselves it is permitted only when he does it for himself as stated siman 252 seif 2; but telling is forbidden according to all for this version.
