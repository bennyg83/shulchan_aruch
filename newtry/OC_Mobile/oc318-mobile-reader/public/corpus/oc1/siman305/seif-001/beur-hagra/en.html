(א) Seif 1 "But if." There one cries with apsar — not so.

(ב) Or it is extra guarding. There 52a like Rav and as stated in siman 17.

(ג) In excess. Nevertheless even though it is guarded with less it is permitted, for they say in the Gemara that luvdakim go out with apsar — thus it is guarded with it; and in the Mishnah luvdakim with promvia even though it is extra guarding compared to apsar, since a donkey is forbidden as stated in seif 3 even though it goes out with apsar — Tosafot there s.v. or etc.

(ד) And likewise as stated etc. There.

(ה) Therefore etc. Mishnah and Gemara and Rashi there.

(ו) And mule. Should read: and likewise mule and donkey.

(ז) And a horse may go out etc. And so in the Gemara and Tosafot s.v. Amar etc. and likewise in the Tur and so too Bach; see Magen Avraham.

(ח) Prinivo. It is the briga mentioned above, which is promvia. Beit Yosef: even though it suffices with apsar, nevertheless since its way is thus it is permitted. Those Tosafot and like luvdakim mentioned above.

(ט) But not. Tosafot there — the second is a burden.

(י) And permitted to wrap. Tosafot there from that of the calves of R' Huna etc. and mola'ot etc. there.

(כ) And permitted to move. Tosafot there that it is prepared for the animal and as stated there 53a; and see there Tosafot s.v. Mahu.

(ל) Provided. Rosh in the name of the Yerushalmi on the explanation "tied from erev Shabbat."
