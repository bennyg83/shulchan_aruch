And a camel with a halter, etc. What the Bach wrote, to permit a camel with a prombyah, is not compelling, as the Magen Avraham wrote. He wrote that it is obvious that it is forbidden, for otherwise, since we say in Shabbos 51 that Libyan donkeys and a camel may go out with a halter, it should also have said, ‘and with a prombyah.’ These are his words. I am astonished, for the Bach wrote that he found this in the Semag, and I too saw it in the Semag, in the simanim of Sefer HaTerumos, siman 236, and in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 4. It is also proven from the fact that the Gemara asks, what is the law of a camel with a nose-ring, and it does not ask about a prombyah. As for the baraisa teaching ‘with a halter,’ it appears to me that it states a novelty, that even with a halter it is guarded.
And a mule and donkey, etc. All the later authorities disagree, for a mule and donkey may not go out with a rein, meaning a prombyah, but only with a halter.
[Levush] And if so, it is pleasure, etc. This is not precise, for in the Gemara we derive that pleasure is forbidden. Rather, one must say that he does this so that the animal should not suffer.
