With what an animal may go out on Shabbat. And
in it 23 seifim: An animal may go out with what it is guarded in; but if not guarded in it — then it is extra guarding and is a burden; likewise anything for ornament not for guarding — may not go out with it. Therefore a cow may go out with zamma of iron — iron ring piercing its nose and inserting it; and a donkey with luba — iron peg i.e. briga; and a camel with apsar — headstall; and mule and donkey and horse may go out with apsar or resen — bridle; but not both; permitted to wrap apsar rope around its neck and go out; permitted to move apsar and place on it provided not lean on it.
