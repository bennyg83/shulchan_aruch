(א) (s.k. 1) Ornament — meaning regarding something etc. that a person is commanded regarding resting his animal. Nevertheless what is needed for its guarding is like a garment for a person and permitted to go out with it. But something that cannot be guarded with it, or whose guarding is excessive and one does not need such great guarding but it suffices with less guarding than that — is a burden. And Rabbenu Yonatan wrote in the name of Beit El that specifically with greatly excessive guarding it is a burden; but if only a little more than what it needs it is not a burden, for it is impossible to precisely measure the degree of guarding exactly as needed; and therefore Rabbenu Yonatan was precise in Shulchan Aruch and wrote "or it is greatly excessive guarding" — he was precise with the language "greatly," intending it must specifically be much more than needed and then it is a burden. And also what is only for ornament is a burden regarding an animal according to all. And they conclude daf 52 that animals go out wrapped with a strap around the neck — and this is what is written in this seif — and permitted to wrap the apsar rope etc. And Rashi wrote and so Ran, even though it is only for ornament; and what was for ornament is a burden according to all; and he answered that ornament of usual conduct on it even on a weekday is not a burden. And Tosafot dispute and explain that it deals with the rope around the neck being loose and not tightened on the neck so he can insert his hand between the rope and the neck, and if he wishes to flee he inserts his hand between the neck and the rope and they catch him in the rope — if so it is guarding and permitted. But for Rashi and Ran even if the rope is tightened and there is no guarding, even so it is permitted because of ornament since it is its usual conduct as stated.
