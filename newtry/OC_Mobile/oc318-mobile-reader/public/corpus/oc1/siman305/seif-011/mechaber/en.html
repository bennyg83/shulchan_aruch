Horse may not go out with fox tail between eyes against evil eye; nor zehurit ornament; goats not with pouch on teats tied lest teats cut on thorns; cow not with muzzle blocking mouth from grazing others' fields; no animal with sandal on leg against kicking; permitted bandage on wound and splints on broken bone — boards tied around broken bone; afterbirth partly out hanging; plugged pair on neck may walk courtyard; may not go out to public domain even if plugged on neck or garment.
