(א) (s.k. 11) distress etc. and you might ask what difference from basket etc. for Rashba according to his view who holds nullification of prepared utensils even for part of Shabbat as Magen Avraham wrote in his name above siman 266 — if so it is difficult for him from what they force the basket before the chicks to go up and down, and all the while the chicks are on the basket forbidden to move the basket and it is nullification for part of Shabbat as is clear — until they descend; and there no need for tza'ar ba'alei chayim for there is no distress to the chicks even if they don't force the basket; if so also here only nullification for part of Shabbat, for after the animal ascends he can again raise pillows and sheets.

(ב) One could say there etc. meaning that basket before chicks with these pillows and sheets here are distant from each other end to end; in basket before chicks even for part of Shabbat it is not nullification for all of it since he can scare them away whenever he wishes — not so pillows and sheets — there is doubt of nullification of prepared utensils for all Shabbat, lest the animal doesn't ascend immediately and he'll need to leave the pillows there all day lest it ascends afterward.

(ג) And Rambam holds chapter 25 laws of Shabbat not like Rashba — rather with pillows it is nullification for all Shabbat, for even if the animal ascends and also raises the pillows — nevertheless they are not fit for use all Shabbat, for they are wet from the well water.

(ד) And it appears to me specifically pillows and sheets — meaning for Rambam's reason specifically pillows and sheets made for lying; and all the while they are wet there is no fitness for lying.

(ה) And so is clear siman 301 that wrote there seif 45 one whose garments fell in water walks in them etc. — implies fit for wearing even though wet.

(ו) And siman 302 seif 1 Rama wrote forbidden to shake a garment that fell in water — and this is specifically when he is particular about it to wear without shaking etc. — implies he wears it when wet through shaking; implies even though there is tza'ar ba'alei chayim; and this is from that he was silent to forbid — implies even without need for pillows and sheets to place under them, nevertheless forbidden; and see there in Rambam's wording.

(ז) For we shouldn't liken etc. meaning even though because of tza'ar ba'alei chayim they permitted shevut of nullifying for part of Shabbat — nevertheless to raise it and permit moving muktzeh by hand they didn't permit because of tza'ar ba'alei chayim.
