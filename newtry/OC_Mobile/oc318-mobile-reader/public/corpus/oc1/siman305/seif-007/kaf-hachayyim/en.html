18) [Seif 7] A donkey may go out with a saddlecloth. It suffers from the cold, as the Gemara says: a donkey feels cold even during the summer solstice. Taz, paragraph 5.
19) There, gloss: Meaning a kind of small saddle, etc. Perishah, paragraph 11, writes that it is a kind of garment used to warm the animal. Levush Serad writes that the text should apparently say 'a large saddle,' meaning that it rests upon the entire body. These are his words. It appears to me, however, that it is called a kind of small saddle even though it covers the entire body because it is not as thick as the saddle placed upon an animal when carrying a load or being ridden. See below, paragraph 22.
20) There: Provided that it was tied upon it before Shabbos. If it was not tied before Shabbos, the person's conduct on the preceding day did not demonstrate that the animal required it as clothing. Rashi. Rosh, however, writes in the name of the Yerushalmi that the reason is that tying it constitutes using a living creature. According to this, if one transgressed and tied it, or if a non-Jew did so, the animal may go out with it. According to the first reason, it may not. Perishah, paragraph 12, writes that Tur intends to forbid it and that Rosh also agrees; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 6, likewise follows those who forbid it and writes that the Shulchan Aruch also agrees. The Shulchan Aruch gives the Yerushalmi's reason in seif 8 but not here, because the Yerushalmi's reason is applied only stringently and not leniently, as in this case. He further writes that this is also implied by Rambam's commentary on the Mishnah and by Bartenura; see there. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 7, likewise follows those who forbid it and writes that this is contrary to Levush; see there. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 5, agrees.
21) There: All other animals are forbidden. The cold is not severe for them, so the saddlecloth constitutes a burden. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 8.
22) There: And it may not go out with a saddle, etc. A saddle does not benefit the donkey and is therefore a burden. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 9. Specifically a saddlecloth is beneficial because it rests upon the animal's body and warms it. A saddle warms only the place upon which it rests, and is therefore not clothing but a burden. Levush Serad.
