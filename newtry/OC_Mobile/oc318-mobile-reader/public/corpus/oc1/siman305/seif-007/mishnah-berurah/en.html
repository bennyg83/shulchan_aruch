(א) A donkey goes out in a mard'at — for it suffers from cold; a donkey is cold even in the season of Tammuz, and it is considered like a garment; and specifically when it is tied from erev Shabbat, for then its intent is revealed from yesterday that it needs this; but if they tied it on Shabbat it is forbidden to go out with it, since its intent is not revealed from erev Shabbat that it should be a garment for it:
(ב) Like a small saddle — see Levushei Serad that it should read: like a large saddle:
(ג) And other etc. — because cold is not hard for them, and the mard'at is a burden:
(ד) And it may not go out in a saddle — for a saddle does not help it and it is a burden; for specifically a mard'at helps, since it is placed on the whole body, unlike a saddle that warms only where it is placed:
