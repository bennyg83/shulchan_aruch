(א) A donkey may not etc. and not in a pair etc. — this is already brought above in seif 1 and at the end of siman 11, and they repeated it because all were taught in one mishnah in the Gemara:

(ב) And not with a ladder etc. — the reason for all these [apart from those whose reason is explained explicitly] is that we are concerned for them and if they fall they come to carry:

(ג) And it is like a kind of ring etc. — this is Rashi's explanation; and Rif explained further that when the hoof of the animal's foot splits they tie it with a strap so that it should heal and return to what it was; and likewise Rambam explained the mishnah:

(ד) So that they not break — Chayei Adam wrote that if he tied them to its legs so that they should not be able to flee, it is permitted to go out with them, for everything that is customary to do for its guarding is for it like a garment; and likewise in Sefer Nezer Yisrael in the name of a responsum of Ma'amar Mordechai:

(ה) And not with a muzzle — in Beur HaGra it appears that for this reason it is like a burden, for it is comparable to the block in the mouth of a cow in seif 11, where there the reason is because of burden:

(ו) Whether it is for guarding — for this is excessive guarding for a cow and it is a burden, as in seif 1:

(ז) That does not need guarding — and it is a burden; and if it is accustomed to flee, it was explained in s.k. 17 that it is permitted:

(ח) They are permitted — even if the rope is wrapped around their neck so that he can seize them with the rope if they flee, as in seif 1:

(ט) Although a person etc. — because a person has mazal and an angel advocates for him from above, and his mazal assists him so that an amulet will help him, which is not so for an animal:
