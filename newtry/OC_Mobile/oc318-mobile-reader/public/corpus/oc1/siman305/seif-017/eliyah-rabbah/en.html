That he nullifies, etc. For they are not fit for use while the water is upon them, as the Maggid wrote in chapter 25. It appears to me that this is specifically pillows and cushions, but garments are fit for use, as the Magen Avraham wrote. It appears that he compares it to siman 301, seif 45, that one whose clothes became soaked in water may walk in them, etc. But it is not comparable, for there it is when he has no others, and because of human dignity, as Tosafot Yom Tov wrote in Perek Chaviyos. Here too, since he intentionally places them in water, perhaps it is worse.
