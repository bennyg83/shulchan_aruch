Donkey may not go out with mard'at not tied erev Shabbat; nor pair even plugged; nor ladder on neck — boards around neck lest wound chafe; nor strap on leg — thick ring from rush on short-stepping animals lest legs strike — made to protect; roosters not threads on legs for sign nor strap lest break vessels; rams not cart under tails lest tail drag; goats not known tree in nostrils to sneeze worms from head; calf not small yoke on neck; nor zamma in calf nose lest nurse; cow not hide on udder lest vermin nurse; nor strap between horns whether guarding or ornament; cow or ox not rope on neck — doesn't need guarding; calves permitted as easily rebel.Animal may go out expert amulet for animal not non-expert even if expert for person (Beit Yosef, R' Yerucham ch. 8, Rambam ch. 2).
