(א) And he does not wait for payment from it — meaning that for this reason it is permitted to give it l'chatchila on Shabbat, for the shepherd fears to use it on Shabbat, that he should not be punished for this in their laws, since he did not give it to him for work at all; but to hire and to lend to a gentile is forbidden, even if he stipulates with him that it shall rest on Shabbat, as above in siman 246 seif 3 — for since he hired it to him for work he does not fear so much, for he will say in his heart: for Shabbat I will see to add for him per the value fitting for the day; and likewise with a loan he thinks that he will do him a favor in return for this; unlike here, where he gave it to him only to graze it — then he fears to do any work with it at all, even if he wishes to pay him, since it is not standing for work [Acharonim]; and according to this, what the Mechaber wrote "if he sees him" etc. — likewise when it becomes known to him that he does work with it he must protest:
(ב) And all the more one need not be concerned etc. — and even if he sees, he need not protest; for specifically to take it out by hand is forbidden, as above; and all this is when the gentile takes it out by himself; but to command him on Shabbat to take it beyond the techum to graze there, see in Peri Megadim that he is uncertain about this and it is proper to be stringent, and likewise Chayei Adam; and for this reason, if they lead oxen to another place to sell, it is forbidden to give them to them on Shabbat, even if he already cut the price with the gentile on this matter before Shabbat — for it is like stipulating with him to lead beyond the techum; but to give it to him on erev Shabbat, even if on Shabbat he will lead it beyond three parsas, is permitted:
