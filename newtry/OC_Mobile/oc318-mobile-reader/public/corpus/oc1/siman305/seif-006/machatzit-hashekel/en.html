(א) (s.k. 6) shall not go out etc. because of a burden — meaning since it is not only for ornament it is a burden.

(ב) And for Semag (should read thus) even on dogs etc. meaning even though for them there is no need like one going to the market, for it is not their way thus — that is with a pair as those going to the market — nevertheless forbidden because of a burden.

(ג) And possible etc. that in this Tosafot agree who wrote etc. should read thus and these are the words of Tosafot daf 54b s.v. because it appears etc. but a burden is not annulled regarding the apsar, and because perhaps it fell and he will come to carry — also not needed etc. that here it deals with tied, and they said whatever is tied they didn't decree (meaning lest it fell) — end. And if so one could say Tosafot refer to the words of the Gemara that in any case it deals with tied since forbidden is not because lest it fell; and likewise they wrote that it is annulled regarding apsar and not a burden; but Semag deals with untied — Tosafot agree it is not annulled regarding apsar. And likewise Semag doesn't annul because lest it fell but rather why did Semag need to forbid because of the decree lest it fell; and he wrote for a Torah prohibition even if it didn't fall it is a burden. And meaning that with this it is settled that Semag's words are not against the Gemara which gave another reason from what the Gemara gave — Gemara deals with tied and Semag with untied; but nevertheless difficult why Semag didn't write the law explained in Gemara that even tied it is not a burden, nevertheless forbidden because appears like etc.

(ד) And nevertheless he wrote some say specifically etc. meaning from this reason the Gemara needed to give the reason that it appears like etc. — answer it is a burden because it is for ornament — so says some say specifically etc. and in a pair its usual way even on a weekday.

(ה) See seif 1 — meaning this is the view of Rashi and Ran; but Tosafot are not satisfied with this answer as they go according to their view that even ornament habitual on a weekday is forbidden as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 1; but on the main question Magen Avraham had on Semag — he had in his days that Rashba's novellae on tractate Shabbat hadn't been published; per Rashba it is difficult, for he wrote on daf 23 and these are his words: a horse shall not go out etc. and he said from what they say later a donkey shall not go out with a pair etc. because it appears like one going to the market — evidently because it appears like one going to the market it is forbidden; if because of ornament it would be permitted; and one could say for comprehensiveness they mentioned this reason that even if you say ornament permitted, here forbidden; and some explain etc. — end. And if so Semag too holds like this answer of Rashba; and if so since we hold that all ornament is forbidden, why give the reason because it appears etc. — better its reason is because of burden; and for this reason forbidden even on dogs and birds.

(ו) It appears to me that all these etc. nevertheless in a courtyard permitted — meaning even though in siman 303 seif 18 they say regarding a woman that what is forbidden for her to go out to public domain is also forbidden in a courtyard; nevertheless with an animal it is not so. And so is clear daf 53 in Tosafot s.v. etc. on this that there they asked if a mard'at on a donkey on Shabbat is permitted and they conclude permitted as written above seif 8; and Tosafot wrote in what did he doubt from Torah to forbid, and wrote: and he said that he wants to decree in a courtyard because of public domain as said regarding a woman etc. that regarding an animal they didn't decree as taught immediately regarding a pair and she walks with it in a courtyard (meaning there is no concern it appears like etc. going — nevertheless would forbid because of public domain) — rather it appears to R' Yitzchak the reason is because of Shabbat exertion — end. Behold they wrote explicitly that regarding an animal they didn't decree courtyard because of public domain, even though Tosafot only deal with something that even in public domain there is only a rabbinic prohibition; but what is a burden and there is Torah prohibition in public domain we didn't hear in Tosafot's words that permitted in a courtyard. Nevertheless it seems somewhat from Tosafot that even something that is a burden in public domain they didn't decree courtyard because of public domain in animal — for impossible that also in animal they decreed courtyard because of public domain; if so even something whose prohibition in public domain is only rabbinic it should have been proper to forbid also in courtyard because of not distinguishing, for such is the law in a woman that a thing forbidden in public domain rabbinically lest the thread tear and display — also in courtyard forbidden. If so regarding animal they didn't decree courtyard because of public domain; and also Tosafot's language that they concluded regarding animal they didn't decree — implies in every matter.

(ז) And it appears to me that even etc. whoever's companion's etc. — these are the words of the Gemara there: one who became dark on the road gives his pouch to a gentile; if no gentile with him he places it on the donkey and the Gemara challenges — isn't it driving (meaning when the animal carries a burden and Israel walks after it and leads it there is Torah prohibition, and even if not his animal but his companion's; and Ran wrote why they mentioned driving and didn't mention resting his animal — they mentioned driving prohibition which is more severe, for resting his animal is only with his own animal) — Rav Ada bar Ahava said he places it while it is walking and there is no uprooting, for it already uprooted its leg before he placed his pouch on it — only placing when he places; and it is half a melacha and there is no Torah prohibition. And they challenge — but impossible etc. as above siman 266 see there; and they challenge if so even his companion's too (Rashi explained if so etc. he can do so since not Torah prohibition regarding pouch permitted meaning because of loss; and a person doesn't stand on his money; and if you don't permit him he will come to carry four amot in public domain) — and they answer R' Pinchas: whoever's companion's exempt but forbidden, on his animal permitted l'chatchila; and understood in Sefer Teshuvot the proof of Magen Avraham from that permitted in driving when it only did half melacha that there is only rabbinic prohibition — likewise in karmelit that there is only rabbinic prohibition it was permitted on an animal. And he wondered in Sefer Tosafot Shabbat — for in driving it wasn't permitted there except from distress that a person doesn't stand on his money etc.; and proof for this that if a gentile is with him he must give it to the gentile and not place it on the donkey; and also brought proof from what they say in Pesachim daf 66 if erev Pesach falls on Shabbat and he forgot to bring a knife for slaughter from erev Shabbat — how bring it on Shabbat in public domain; and they said if his Pesach is a lamb he inserts the knife in its wool, and if a kid he inserts it between its horns; and they challenge — isn't it driving, for the animal carries the knife and he drives after it; and they answer it is driving in an unusual manner, for it is not the way of a kid and lamb to carry a burden; and there is only shevut; and in a place of mitzvah they didn't decree; and according to Magen Avraham even without need of mitzvah shevut was permitted on an animal; and he wrote further: and even though also Rabbenu Yonatan wrote in seif 22 and these are Mordechai's words that where there is need it carries itself that there is only shevut prohibition permitted on animal — nevertheless according to them there is room to say that shevut regarding driving is more severe and wasn't permitted even regarding animal except in place of loss or place of mitzvah; and as Ran wrote in tractate Avodah Zarah they forbade selling large livestock to a gentile because of the concern lest he sell it on erev Shabbat near nightfall and the gentile tests it and the Israelite walks with it and the animal obeys the voice of the Israelite and they go because of him and it is driving; and wrote: the reason they didn't mention prohibition of resting his animal is because that concern is a remote concern and we aren't concerned because of resting his animal; but because of driving prohibition which is more severe, for the melacha is done in partnership with the person walking after it, they were concerned; but Magen Avraham who holds that also in driving shevut was permitted on animal — it is difficult; and therefore he wrote that Magen Avraham's words are rejected at first.

(ח) And in my humble opinion I don't know who told him that Magen Avraham — meaning that also shevut of driving was permitted on animal l'chatchila — Magen Avraham didn't bring driving at all, only R' Pinchas's words whoever's companion's exempt but forbidden on his animal permitted l'chatchila; and it must be according to Mordechai and Rabbenu Yonatan seif 22 they explained the Gemara's words thus, that he answered him on the Gemara's question: if so his companion's too — and why needed to place on donkey; and on this R' Pinchas answered whoever's companion's etc. that one's donkey is better than his companion's, for all shevut we hold since with a person it is only exempt but forbidden, on an animal permitted l'chatchila; and it implies completely even without loss or distress or mitzvah, for thus is the implication of the language permitted l'chatchila — implies it is full permission; and if so meaning that shevut regarding driving is more severe than other shevut for the reasons stated, and wasn't permitted except in place of loss — nevertheless better to place on his donkey than give to his companion, for animal is lenient regarding shevut relative to person, for in other shevut permitted on animal even without loss and even l'chatchila; and so also Magen Avraham understood at least it is proven that all shevut except driving are permitted on animal even l'chatchila and even without loss; and so is clear there daf 53 s.v. what, no etc. that there Shmuel said one doesn't hang a traskal on an animal on Shabbat since it is made only for pleasure — and this is explained seif 10; and they challenge: and a braita taught a horse shall not go out with a fox tail etc. and they don't tie traskal in their mouths to public domain; in public domain it is — but in a courtyard what, no — even for large ones and for pleasure even so permitted in courtyard; and they answer: no, for small ones and only because of distress permitted; and Tosafot wrote: and you might ask what is the difficulty — perhaps here it deals with traskal hung from erev Shabbat; but to hang on Shabbat even in courtyard forbidden; and they answered from that it mentioned public domain regarding traskal more than the others — all matters the braita forbade like fox tail etc. — rather it mentioned public domain to teach that in courtyard permitted in every matter — end Tosafot. And still it is difficult — perhaps it always deals with hung from erev Shabbat, and what mentioned public domain is to infer from it that for karmelit permitted — for in other matters even for karmelit forbidden; nevertheless traskal which is for pleasure permitted even for karmelit except public domain forbidden, for it needed to say because of pleasure they permitted to hang on Shabbat — perhaps because of pleasure it was permitted for karmelit; but to hang it on Shabbat forbidden because of exertion; if so the Gemara holds that for karmelit also other forbidden matters are permitted for karmelit; and from that it added the word "to public domain" regarding traskal — it comes to teach that in courtyard in every case permitted even to hang it on Shabbat.

(ט) However regarding something not woven etc. and as Tosafot wrote daf 54 etc. that they asked why need the reason shall not go out with a pair because it appears like etc. — answer because lest it fell he will come to carry as we are concerned regarding a person with zugim as written siman 301 seif 23; and they explained it deals with woven in apsar — in such case no need for lest it fell — end Tosafot. And difficult for Magen Avraham — what difficulty do Tosafot have — perhaps for this reason the Gemara gave the reason it appears like one going to the market, for then even for a mavoy meuchav forbidden as Magen Avraham wrote afterward; not so for the reason lest it fell etc. — if so where there is lest it fell etc. also for mavoy meuchav forbidden, all the more karmelit because of not distinguishing.

(י) Some say there as stated etc. meaning they say there one doesn't lead an animal with a stick (even if he prepared the stick from yesterday that there is no muktzeh) on Yom Tov because it appears like one going to the market; and behold there is no eruv and carrying on Yom Tov, and it is like a mavoy meuchav on Shabbat; and even so they forbade because it appears like etc.
