Rams may go out with bubin — skin tied under genitals so not mount females; ewes go out chevutzot — ram tied upward so males mount them; may go out kevunot — garment tied around to keep wool clean; goats tzrurot — heads of teats tied; specifically' when tied so teats dry and not milked — then tightens well; if tied to guard milk from falling to ground — forbidden — doesn't tighten well; concern lest fall and carry.
