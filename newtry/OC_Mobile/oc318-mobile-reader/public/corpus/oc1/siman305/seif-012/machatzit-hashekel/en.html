(א) (s.k. 12) permitted etc. explanation once a day — meaning even though on weekdays its way is to warm twice, nevertheless on Shabbat it suffices to permit once and no more, for again there is no tza'ar ba'alei chayim.

(ב) for they cannot eat — meaning since they already became accustomed to warming, again if one places food before them they don't eat except when he warms them by hand; and therefore when he began warming them near Shabbat while not yet accustomed to warming — he must first try to give before them to eat whether they eat without warming.

(ג) into a vessel liable — for this is a derivative of extracting; but into food exempt, for we hold liquids that come for food are like food — it is like extracting food from food and not a derivative of extracting.

(ד) nevertheless there is shevut, for in all exempt matters on Shabbat they are exempt but forbidden.

(ה) it is muktzeh — see siman 495 — meaning on Yom Tov specifically when the animal stands to raise offspring or to milk — the animal is muktzeh and then also the milk forbidden.

(ו) and on Shabbat see siman 328 seif 7 and see there in Magen Avraham siman 495.
