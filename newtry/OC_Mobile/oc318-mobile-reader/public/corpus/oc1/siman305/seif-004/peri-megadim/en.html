"Arise, shine, and give us no exception to the Lord's mercy, and it is a sign that is not made up of Hashem's Word, nor is it possible to do so." "We have not been spared by Hashem's wrath, and we will not be able to do so with Hashem." Hashem’s Word tells us that “I am not afraid of Hashem’s people, and that we are not in the same way as Hashem’s people, and that we will be able to do it.”
