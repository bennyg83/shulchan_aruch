(s.k. 3) forbidden etc. because it appears etc. Rabbenu Yonatan z"l that it is like one who needs to load a burden upon it — end of his words; and meaning that in loading a burden he places a mard'at upon it first, and if so by placing a mard'at upon it they will think he wishes afterward to load a burden. And know that what Shulchan Aruch wrote that a donkey goes out with a mard'at — the reason is explained in the Gemara because it needs it at all times, for a donkey even in the season of Tammuz is cold to it, and through the mard'at upon it it warms; but other animals that don't need warming — it is a burden for them and forbidden. And Taz wrote when the cold is great and we see that cold harms even the horse; or in summer days when flies multiplied and stand on the horse and trouble the horse — permitted to place something on the horse to warm or to ward off flies. And what Shulchan Aruch wrote regarding a donkey that it is tied from erev Shabbat — Rosh wrote the reason in the name of Yerushalmi because when not tied it is forbidden to go out with it, and if one ties it he will use it for tza'ar ba'alei chayim as written seif 8; and this reason the Levush copied. And wrote in Sefer Aleph Rabbenu and in Sefer Teshuvot that therefore if he transgressed and tied it on Shabbat — permitted to go out with it. But Tosafot wrote in the name of Rashi the reason that specifically when tied from erev Shabbat it reveals his intent that the donkey must have it; but when not tied from erev Shabbat it is a burden, and therefore even if he transgressed and tied it on Shabbat, nevertheless forbidden to go out with it. And see in Tosafot daf 53a that Tosafot wrote s.v. "and it" in the name of R' Yitzchak Porta another reason. But a saddle is forbidden even for a donkey for it doesn't help against cold.
