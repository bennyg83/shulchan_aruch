May go out, etc. The Magen Avraham wrote: It appears to me that all those things of which we say that it is forbidden for the animal to go out with them to the public domain because they are a load, in a courtyard they are permitted, and even in a karmelis they are permitted. For anything that by a person would be exempt but forbidden, by his animal is permitted initially. However, regarding something not woven into its garment, it implies that it is forbidden, for we are concerned lest it fall and he come to carry it, as Tosafot wrote on folio 54, s.v. mishum, etc. And with something that looks like one going to the market, even in an enclosed alley it is forbidden, similar to Yom Tov in siman 522. These are his words. This requires study, for Tosafot there refers to the public domain, and so it also implies in Tosafot, folio 52, s.v. mai, etc., which wrote that all of them are speaking of the public domain. These are their words. A sandal is one of them, where it is forbidden lest it slip off and he come to carry it. Nevertheless, in a karmelis it is possible to forbid. This requires study from siman 303, seif 18, that according to us there is no public domain. In any case, wherever it is possible to be careful, one should be careful, for many poskim hold that even in our times it is a public domain.
