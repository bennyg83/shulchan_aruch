May not ride on animal nor lean on it nor use beside it; side accessories — one thing on its side using it — permitted; if mounted even intentionally must descend — tza'ar ba'alei chayim; therefore unload burdens — insert head under burden shift aside falls itself.Forbidden sit on wagon gentile drives Shabbat — uses animal; also lest cut vine (Tosafot, Rosh start Mi shehotzi'u, Mordechai Shabbat ch. 1, Hagahot Eruvin, Beit Yosef Semag).
