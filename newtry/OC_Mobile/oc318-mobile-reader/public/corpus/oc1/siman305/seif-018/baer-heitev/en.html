Wagon — if fleeing Shabbat life danger good sit in wagon if ten tefachim high walk on foot — sitting wagon only rabbinic; walking foot concern d'oraisa techum per some; all the more carrying gold silver — better wagon than foot; if wagon not ten tefachim — techum concern (Pri Megadim); likewise Peri Chadash; see Magen Avraham siman 266 seif 2 length on riding horse; Maharadbaz 81 better walk than ride — two prohibitions animal and techum; see Nachalat Shiva 83 permits ride chase runaway son.
