So too the Bach wrote at the end of siman 308. According to this, it appears to me that what the Rambam wrote, that it is forbidden to lift it by hand, refers to when it is possible by means of pillows and cushions, unlike the Magen Avraham, who did not understand it this way and from whom all the words of the great authorities mentioned above escaped notice. It appears that telling non-Jews to lift it is more permitted than placing pillows, as written in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 8, and in Teshuvos Maharam of Rothenburg, siman 49.
