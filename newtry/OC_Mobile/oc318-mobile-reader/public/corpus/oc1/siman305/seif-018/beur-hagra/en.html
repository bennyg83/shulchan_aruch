(א) Seif 18 and one may not lean. Eruvin 101; and "one" 101 tree and "one" etc.

(ב) And if he mounted. Meaning even though regarding a tree they distinguished between unwitting and intentional, here there is no distinction — and it is from Yerushalmi ch. 5 halacha 19.

(ג) And for this reason. There the donkey of R' Gamliel etc. he holds tza'ar ba'alei chayim — but we who hold it is d'oraisa as stated 128:2 and Beit Nasan 32:33 it is permitted.

(ד) How does one act. There it was taught R' Shimon bar Yochai's cow etc. and "mounted" refers to his donkey etc.; and see end siman 266 seif 9.

(ה) That one uses the animal. Sitting in a wagon is like riding as stated in ch. 9 of Kilayim.

(ו) Also etc. In Tosafot ch. 4 they did not write that this alone is the reason as stated in ch. 5 halacha 19; it should read that this is what they mean: even for those who say there is no concern lest he cut a vine, it is forbidden because of using the animal — they do not dispute; and further that "lest he cut" also applies here.
