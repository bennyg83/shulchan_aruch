(א) They may not ride etc. — a decree lest he cut a vine from what is attached in order to drive it, for he is liable for this because of detaching, which is a tolada of harvesting:

(ב) And they may not hang on it — for all use of living creatures they included in the category of riding and forbade; and even in a place where there is no concern lest he cut a vine, such as in a desert where there is no vine, or to stroke a child on the back of the animal in order to amuse him — likewise forbidden, for the Rabbis did not distinguish in their matters:

(ג) And he uses it — that is, that he leans himself on the side of that thing; but if he uses himself on top of that thing placed on its side, behold it is like using on its side directly, as he wrote in the Hagahah below regarding a wagon, see there in Mishna Berurah:

(ד) Even if he descended intentionally — and not like regarding a tree, that if he ascended intentionally we hold below in siman 336 seif 1 that he does not descend until motzei Shabbat because of a penalty — here he descends because of tza'ar ba'alei chayim:

(ה) And for this reason they unload — that is, even if the burden is muktzeh, nevertheless they did not want to be lenient because of tza'ar ba'alei chayim to remove it by hand when it is possible to remove it not by hand:

(ו) A gentile drives it — for if an Israelite himself drives it, there is in any case a d'oraisa prohibition because of michamer:

(ז) That he uses the animal — and it is not called "the side of the side," because the wagon is attached to the side of the animal since he uses himself on the wagon; and its law is like using on the side of the animal directly, as in s.k. 63; and likewise to use himself on the board extending outside the wagon behind it is also forbidden and is not called "the side of the side," for it is all one vessel:
