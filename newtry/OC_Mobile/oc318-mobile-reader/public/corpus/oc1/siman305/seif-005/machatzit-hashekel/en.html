(א) (s.k. 5) And a bell etc. and it appears to me when it jingles etc. meaning the prohibition when not plugged is not because of making sound. Rather like a public domain animal forbidden to go out even if plugged since a pair on its neck appears like one going to the market; but in a courtyard permitted since there are no viewers there and no concern of marit ha-ayin, for we don't hold thus like Rav — whatever the sages forbade because of marit ha-ayin is forbidden even in private domain. And see above siman 301 in Magen Avraham small seif 21. But when not plugged and makes sound, even in a courtyard there is marit ha-ayin; for although in a courtyard the many are not present, nevertheless also the children of the public domain passing by will hear the sound of the pair jingling and know there is a pair on its neck and it will appear to them like one going to the market.

(ב) And Tosafot etc. asked on it regarding noise etc. meaning and if so it is difficult according to their view according to what Rama wrote later siman 338 that an utensil designated for making sound, even if not a sound of song, is forbidden.

(ג) And possible that it sounds etc. and if so in any case it is difficult for all.
