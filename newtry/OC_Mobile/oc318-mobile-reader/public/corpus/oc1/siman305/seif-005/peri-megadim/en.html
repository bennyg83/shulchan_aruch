Donkey (should read) — Gemara Shabbat 53a: what is stated regarding mard'at — small saddle — should read thus, for mard'at implies the whole body and warms all of it, and small saddle only where the saddle sits. And what is stated "tied from erev Shabbat" — in Shabbat Tosafot 53a s.v. vehu for Rashi it is a burden — it appears at first glance from what? and see Maharsha there and Noda BiYehuda 2; and Tosafot wrote it appears as intending to take out the mard'at or leading to a distant place — per this view even if he transgressed and tied on Shabbat, or he has a gentile slave who tied, it is forbidden; for Rashi there is concern of d'oraisa prohibition all the more since he did not reveal in "tied from erev Shabbat" that it is not clothing; and so from the Mishnah explanation: if he transgressed and tied it is forbidden — Yerushalmi and Maggid Mishneh ch. 8 halacha 10 brought Rashba: the reason is use of the animal, and in Mishnah explanation it appears forbidden even otherwise; in a courtyard where these reasons do not apply they say the Yerushalmi reason — therefore in Shulchan Aruch he brought the Yerushalmi reason — namely in a courtyard (and likewise Levush in a courtyard); but Levush in seif 7 also wrote the Yerushalmi reason; see Eshel Avraham note 6 see there. What the Mechaber wrote "and other animals forbidden" — they wrote: since it says donkey specifically and teaches a leniency — even a donkey that is cold to it and needs warming — nevertheless only tied from erev Shabbat, for he revealed his intent; all the more other animals — one can say the tanna returns to the leniency; and in our novellae we wrote from this see there.
