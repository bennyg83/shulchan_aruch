14) [Seif 5] Animals that wear a collar, such as dogs, etc. The same applies to a horse, which may go out with a collar. Mishnah there. Rambam, chapter 20, halachah 8, writes the same.
15) There: And small wild animals, etc. This refers to animals that ordinarily wear such equipment during the week. If it is not their ordinary practice, as when a cat wears a chain, it is forbidden, because a small rope alone suffices. The chain constitutes excessive restraint and is therefore a burden. Rambam there.
