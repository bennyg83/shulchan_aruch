(א) (s.k. 10) uses etc. and if so even if it hadn't written lest he cut etc. and furthermore lest he cut etc. — for Rama divided it into two reasons: he wrote uses animal even without lest he cut etc. — implies prohibition of uses etc. exists even where there is no lest he cut branch.

(ב) But Tosafot wrote in Eruvin daf 43a end s.v. the law is etc. that they forbade etc. a decree etc. and even though even rubbing where there is no lest he cut etc. forbidden — nevertheless the main decree was because of lest he cut etc. and afterward because of not distinguishing they forbade even where there is no lest he cut etc.

(ג) And likewise Rashi on Shabbat daf 154b that there Abbaye found Rava rubbing against his donkey — said behold the master uses an animal for tza'ar ba'alei chayim; and Rashi explained: and we learned one doesn't ride on an animal — and likewise for all use — end. And according to Rama who holds prohibition of using animal for tza'ar ba'alei chayim and prohibition lest he cut branch are two matters — what proof does he bring from Rashi from what we learned one doesn't ride etc. — there it is specifically lest he cut branch; and how derive from it to forbid other uses like rubbing where there is no lest he cut etc. — rather certainly he holds like Tosafot that all are one decree and because of not distinguishing as mentioned above, even though nevertheless in the mishnah only riding prohibition was mentioned because lest he cut etc.; but to forbid rubbing and the like because of not distinguishing — this wasn't mentioned in the mishnah; nevertheless Abbaye could be said to hold thus as simple reasoning that one must forbid even where no lest he cut because of not distinguishing.

(ד) And it implies in Tosafot and Rosh that they wrote their words on all Rashbam's words who wished to permit Israel to sit in a wagon when the gentile leads it, even outside the techum; and Tosafot and Rosh dispute because of prohibition lest he cut and uses animal for tza'ar ba'alei chayim — and didn't mention prohibition outside techum — from which we infer they weren't concerned about that.
