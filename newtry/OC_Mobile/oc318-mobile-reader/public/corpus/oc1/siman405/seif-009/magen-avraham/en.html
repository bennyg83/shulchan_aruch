(א) They are under duress — and also in their place; do not challenge from one who desecrates Shabbat intentionally that it is forbidden — there it is a Torah prohibition; further requires study — for one who immerses his vessels and tithes, whose prohibition is d'rabbanan, we penalize as in chapter 2 of Terumot; it must be specifically where he has other fruit; and as in chapter 2 of Beitzah; so Raosh; here it deals where he has no other fruit.

(ב) Permitted to eat them — if a person made eruv to that side, or another city is adjacent there (Rashi); see siman 495.

(ג) Even one who did not take them out — since the prohibition was done through a Jew, unlike through a non-Jew as siman 325 seif 8; and see responsa 318; and siman 218.
