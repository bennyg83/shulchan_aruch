(siman 9) It is not, etc. — see siman 374 seif 2; and see there, etc., that he challenged — let us say Shabbat once permitted is permitted; on this Magen Avraham hinted to see siman 372 seif 2 where it is explained we do not say Shabbat once permitted, etc., except for a wall between two courtyards each of which made eruv for itself; now when the wall between them fell one might think one courtyard is forbidden to the other — it teaches since it was permitted it remains permitted since the outer partitions remain; but if the outer partition fell and opened to public domain or karmelis we do not say once permitted remains permitted; so too here when the ship's partitions opened to the sea which is karmelis.
