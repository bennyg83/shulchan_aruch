(א) Even intentionally — meaning that regarding a person we hold above that if the leaving was intentional he lost his place and has only four cubits; produce did not lose their place since at twilight they were under duress in their leaving and returning, as below.

(ב) If it is Yom Tov — then it is permitted to carry them throughout the techum; on Shabbat it is forbidden to carry four cubits even within the techum [unless it was a city with eruv, then permitted to carry them throughout the city].

(ג) And if it is Shabbat, etc. — meaning that also on Shabbat there is relevance, at least for eating.

(ד) In their place — meaning in the place where they were returned within the techum, in their four cubits.

(ה) Even for that Jew, etc. — and although one who comes from outside the techum is forbidden for one who comes on his account; here is different since they were returned to the place where they rested at twilight.

(ו) They are under duress — meaning that even though the person took them out intentionally, the fruits themselves are under duress in their leaving and returning; behold they are like a person whom non-Jews took under duress and returned, above in seif 5; so too for permission of eating they were also permitted by their return.

(ז) Permitted to eat them — there, both for other people found there at the place of the fruit and for the one who took them out himself.

(ח) Forbidden to carry them beyond four cubits — as explained above in seif 5 regarding a person, that even if he left unknowingly he has only four cubits.

(ט) Forbidden to eat them — but permitted to carry them in their four cubits; all this when they were in the field; but if he brought them to a walled city, although he violated a prohibition, nevertheless permitted to carry them throughout the whole city, whether he took them out mistakenly or intentionally.

(י) Even one who did not take them out — and although fruit that a non-Jew brought from outside the techum is forbidden only to one for whom it was brought; here, since a prohibition was done through a Jew, it is stricter.

(כ) For one who did not take them out — and specifically if they were not taken out for him; otherwise also forbidden per this view; for law we hold the first view that even for one who did not take them out for him it is also forbidden to eat — this is the view of the majority of Poskim [Acharonim].
