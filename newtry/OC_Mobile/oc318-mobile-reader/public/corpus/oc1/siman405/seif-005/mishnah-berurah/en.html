(א) One whom they took out, etc. — meaning that even in this manner where he was under duress he likewise has only four cubits — all the more when he left intentionally, as above in seif 1.

(ב) Or an evil spirit — his mind was disturbed and afterward he recovered.

(ג) Or who left by mistake — although certainly this is from the law of duress, he stated this because of the end "they returned him" — it helps also in this.

(ד) Only four cubits — see above siman 396 in Hagah how the four cubits are calculated.

(ה) If he returns knowingly, etc. — meaning willingly and knowingly — unlike if he returned by mistake, which is considered as if they returned him.

(ו) Only four cubits — and specifically if he did not enter his city; but if he entered his city enclosed with partitions the whole is like four cubits, as below in seif 8 — see what is written there on this.

(ז) Entered a ship — deals with one who acquired residence on land and entered a ship on Shabbat unspecified to sit in it [not to sail — for if he entered to sail it is like leaving knowingly and he has only four cubits], and in either case the ship sailed.

(ח) Outside the techum — and deals with when that place outside the techum was less than ten handbreadths; then it would be forbidden for him to return knowingly to the first place, as above at the end of siman 404; only because it returned by itself they permit him.
