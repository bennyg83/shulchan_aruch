(א) (siman 5) Entered, etc. — on Shabbat, etc.; if so he was on land and acquired residence there; on this Magen Avraham wrote the same as if he did not leave and he has the first techum; but if he entered from erev and did not acquire residence on land and also did not acquire residence on the ship — for plainly traveling above ten is difficult — besides that the wording "as if he did not leave" is also difficult there, for there too he has no residence anywhere; it is also difficult why specifically that it returned backward — anywhere it reaches he acquires residence there and has two thousand from there, since he did not yet acquire residence anywhere, as above end siman 404.

(ב) To sail is like one who left knowingly — meaning since it is forbidden to enter on Shabbat to sail, if so he entered in violation and whatever is in violation is called leaving knowingly; therefore even if returned he does not have the first techum; even if returned to his city — the whole city is considered four cubits for him, but outside the city is forbidden.

(ג) Or it deals, etc. — meaning always it deals with one who entered from erev; and even if he entered to sail — only it was below ten and they acquired residence there permissibly; it will be explained below in which matter it was permissibly.
