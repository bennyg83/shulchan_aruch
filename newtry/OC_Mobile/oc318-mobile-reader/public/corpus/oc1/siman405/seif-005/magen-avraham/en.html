Entered a ship — on Shabbat; for if he entered from erev he did not acquire residence at all if it is above ten; it must deal with entering to sit in it; if he entered intending to sail it is like one who left knowingly, as seif 7 and seif 8; or it deals when the ship was on erev below ten and he acquired residence there and it sailed on Shabbat and returned backward — it is unknowingly since he entered permissibly; see siman 248.
