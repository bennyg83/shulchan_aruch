(א) Non-Jews placed him outside the techum, etc. — meaning they took him out from within the techum and placed him there.
(ב) In a pen — a cattle fence that they make in the fields, alternating here and there in order to fertilize with animal manure; and a sahar is made for the city's animals. He did not mention here that it must be enclosed for residence because we do not require enclosure for residence except when the area is more than a beit sa'atayim; and plainly a pen and sahar are not so large; but if it was more than a beit sa'atayim we require that it be enclosed for residence, e.g. the animal guard dwells there.
(ג) Enclosed with a wall for residence — thereby it is reshut hayachid and the whole is like four cubits; the same for cities that are not enclosed with a wall but have eruv corrections, as we wrote above siman 401 in Mishna Berurah — see there. Know likewise that if non-Jews brought him on a ship he is also not permitted on his own to leave the ship if it stands below ten handbreadths, unless when the ship entered it was within the circuit of the city's partitions; and some say even if the city is not enclosed with a partition, from the port side the depth of the water at the port place is considered a partition — see what Magen Avraham wrote on this in the name of Maharil, and it is explained in Biur Halacha.
(ד) Or who left by mistake — meaning he was occupied with his matters and from that did not pay attention to his techum and left, and everything like this.
(ה) And he remembered while inside — unlike when he remembered before he entered and then entered — he has only four cubits, for he entered knowingly.
(ו) Even though he is inside one of these — whether he entered knowingly or non-Jews brought him there — since in any case his leaving the techum was knowingly we do not say the whole is like four cubits; see Biur Halacha.
