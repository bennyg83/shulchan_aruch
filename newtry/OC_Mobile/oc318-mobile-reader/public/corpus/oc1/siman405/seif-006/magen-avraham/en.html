Enclosed with a wall — implies even if he came to the outskirts of a city it does not help, as Tosafot and Raosh — not like Hagahot Maimoniot in the name of some; and in Maharil it is written, etc.: where two heads of the city are in the water, e.g. in Mainz where two towers are in the water and the city is between them like a bow — if it came between the two towers he is permitted to enter, end of his words — implies he holds outskirts of a city are like a city; so Agudah wrote and concluded they practiced not to leave the ship but non-Jews carry to the city or to the seashore where it is ten high; see there; some say if he entered a port the depth of the water is a partition as we say regarding an alley, end of his words.
