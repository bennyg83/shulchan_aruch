(siman 6) Enclosed, etc. In Maharil it is written it appears he holds crossing, etc. — meaning the two towers were not built adjacent and flush to the city's houses — for if so what does it teach? Whether towers or houses — the same, he enters the city (and therefore the question was whether city people are considered one, as above siman 399); rather the towers were not adjacent to houses but stand within the crossing; the same law would permit if he came near the towers within seventy amot of the shore — only it is hard to give a measure of amot in water and he gave the boundary of the two towers. Non-Jews carry him to the city — then the whole city is like four cubits as Magen Avraham wrote above siman 404 siman 5. A place where it is ten high — that place will be considered for him like four cubits regarding an alley, as above siman 363 seif 29.
