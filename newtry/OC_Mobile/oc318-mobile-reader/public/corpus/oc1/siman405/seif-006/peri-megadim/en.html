And forbidden — Taz: if he took them to another enclosed city mistakenly it is like leaving unknowingly and permitted throughout the city even though they did not rest in the air of partitions from erev, as we wrote in letter 1; and if he took them out intentionally — although the fruit are like under duress, nevertheless they may not be carried beyond four cubits in another city, for regarding this it is like leaving unknowingly; Orach Chayim letter 14; and in Tosafot Shabbos letter 19 he challenged this — see there; one may say intentionally within four cubits forbidden to eat — nevertheless permitted to carry, for at twilight they were not muktzeh; and for eating only rabbinic penalty, etc.; Magen Avraham 495 letter 2 answered that here if returned to their place it is permitted — therefore even outside their place permitted to carry; and in Beit Yosef on Rambam's words where one may analyze from Raosh's words — see there; and there in Magen Avraham I shall explain further.
