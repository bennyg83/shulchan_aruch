(א) Enclosed with a wall for residence — see Mishna Berurah. Know Magen Avraham brought Riaz's view that outskirts of a city also count as city and a fortiori he disagrees with Mechaber's core ruling that we do not require wall enclosure at all — so explained there explicitly; see also Agudah. In truth I found more Rishonim who hold so — Ramban in his chiddushim to Eruvin expanded there that even without partition enclosure and public domain passing through it also counts as four cubits for this purpose; he brought there that so is also Rabbeinu Tam in a responsum — even without partition enclosure it is doubtful; and so Rabbeinu Yoel brought in Or Zarua in laws see siman 6 he wrote there that were he not concerned he would permit even without partition enclosure; in any case even per his initial stringency we require enclosure, nevertheless he holds there that even if public domain passed through and certainly forbidden to carry there, nevertheless permitted to walk all of it — walking permission does not depend on carrying — see there. Nevertheless it is hard to be lenient against Shulchan Aruch — most Poskim follow Shulchan Aruch: Rashi, Ri author of Tosafot, Rabbeinu Efraim in Or Zarua laws see siman 6 [see there he was stricter still], Or Zarua laws of eruv siman 151, Rabbeinu Yonatan, Ittur laws of Yom Tov folio 144, Raosh, Ritva, Ran in chiddushim to public domain, Shibolei Leket in name of his brother R' Binyamin and R' Avigdor Kohen Tzedek see there siman 111 — and so Tur siman 401 and Ri'u as brought in Beit Yosef. Nevertheless in pressing circumstances one may rely to be lenient per Ramban and supporters even without partition enclosure since it is d'rabbanan — see Ramban who holds outskirts of city also count as city for this — permitted to walk even within outskirts; however what Magen Avraham brought in name of Riaz that even if they brought him into outskirts of city the whole city counts as four cubits — this is extra leniency; even Ramban is strict on this explicitly there and in Hagahot Maimoniot it is reasonable to explain Riaz's words thus.

(ב) But if he left outside his techum knowingly, etc. — see Mishna Berurah; and this is per Rambam and his supporters and as Rashbatz explained; know Rashba, Ritva, and Tosafot Eruvin 29 [s.v. and they seated him in the last house there] disagree and hold in every case we are lenient in an enclosed place to consider it all like four cubits, even if he left intentionally outside the techum and entered one of these; also in Beur HaGra it appears he leans more to this view — see what he expanded on this; nevertheless for practice one should certainly be stringent per the Mechaber, for many Rishonim follow Rambam's view — see Beit Yosef; so Or Zarua in chapter Mi Shehutziah; so too from Rambam's words brought in Raosh and Mordechai and copied in seif 8 since he specified to his city; and so Shibolei Leket siman 111 in the name of his brother R' Binyamin and R' Yeshayah — see there; especially since Rashba himself wrote in Avodat HaKodesh one should be concerned for the stringent words on this.
