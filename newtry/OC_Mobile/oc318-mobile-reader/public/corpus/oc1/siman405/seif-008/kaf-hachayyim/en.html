8) There. It is permitted to make a partition for him, etc. The reason for this permission is that when people surround him to form partitions, the area resembles an enclosed karpeif, all of which is regarded as four amos. He can walk within the enclosure toward the city and thereby enter his techum permissibly. Once he enters his techum, it is as though he never left. Beis Yosef. This means that the entire city then again has the status of four amos for him, as initially, and he may walk two thousand amos beyond it in every direction, as stated in the following se'if.
