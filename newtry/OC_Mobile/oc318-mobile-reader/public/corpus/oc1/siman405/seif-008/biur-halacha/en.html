If they returned him to his enclosed city, etc. — see Beit Me'ir who is uncertain here — behold the core novelty of this law is because he rested there at twilight; if so it is reasonable we do not require partition enclosure around the city here, as we hold generally regarding Shabbat in a city where he has two thousand in every direction even if not enclosed at all — see Tur siman 398; and in truth Rama brought this law only from seif 6 and what is noted in Hagah in the name of Raosh and Ri'u and Or Zarua is also from those Poskim on the law of seif 6; and in truth there is no proof from there that it is different there because they did not rest there at twilight; nevertheless it is not compelled that therefore it is not like Shabbat proper, for he has only his city and not two thousand as at first; also what Beit Me'ir wrote on the core of this law that Rambam does not hold like Rabbi Meir — we need not be concerned to be stringent since Rashba and supporters are lenient even in another city, as we wrote above in Biur Halacha — see there; if so at least when they returned him to the city where he rested Shabbat there is certainly no stringency.
