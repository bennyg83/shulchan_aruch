(ב) Enclosed. As above.

(ג) The whole like four cubits. And even. From what is challenged there — behold we also taught if they returned him, etc.; and perhaps it teaches that even in such a case he has only four cubits; and if not, the challenge at the beginning is obvious — now, etc.; and perhaps it teaches such a case; Rambam and Shulchan Aruch above seif 4 but if he left, etc.; and as Rashba challenged there — one may say per Shmuel the challenge works well for he explains like Rabbi Gamliel on a ship but not in pen and sahar; and if so, even unknowingly he has only four cubits and the challenge works well; but in the end even Shmuel agrees as we wrote for all nevertheless, etc., seif 7 — since he rested in the air, etc.
