(א) And they returned him — to his techum.
(ב) Only four cubits — for we require that the leaving and the return be under duress, as above in seif 5.
(ג) To his city — where he rested from erev; but if they brought him to another city he has only four cubits, since he left the techum knowingly, as written in seif 6.
(ד) Enclosed with partitions — see Biur Halacha what is written on this.
(ה) For residence — and the same if its alleyways are corrected with lechi and korah, as above.
(ו) The whole like four cubits — since he rested there from erev. Nevertheless it is forbidden for him to go outside it even one cubit, since in any case he left knowingly and lost his techum.
