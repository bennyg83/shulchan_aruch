If he left knowingly and non-Jews returned him — only four cubits. However if returned to his city (enclosed with partitions for residence — Raosh chapter Mi Shehutziah, Rabbeinu Yerucham ch. 1, Or Zarua) — whole like four cubits; even if left knowingly and returned knowingly (see law of unspecified towns siman 401).
