He if went out knowingly and returned by way of non-Jews, he only has four amot; however, if he went out of the city walled for dwelling (The Rosh chapter One who went out and Rabbi Yerucham HY''H and Or Zarua) all of it is like four amot; and even if he went out knowingly and returned knowingly. {Rama: Look later of the laws of cities Siman 401.}
