(א) Seif 7 — since he rested. It should read: and he means since he was in the air, etc.; and he stated and rested for the novelty of the end, as Hagahot Maimoniot there s.v. if impossible; and if the walls broke, etc.; Magen Avraham answered it deals with one who entered on Shabbat, which is like leaving knowingly — nevertheless since he rested. At first glance so is the Gemara — all agree nevertheless the halachah, etc.; and even though in pen and sahar duress does not help, but Shulchan Aruch's wording does not imply so and should not leave the mishnah and Gemara there; see Hagahot Maimoniot there s.v. Rabbi Yehudah and s.v. and if he transgressed; and Tosafot there s.v. the halachah and more — Rashbatz ruled, etc., and what Rashbatz explained, etc.; and Rabbi Yehudah holds it is like leaving unknowingly as Rashbatz explained there that a ship goes by itself, etc.

(ב) And if they broke. There 42b and 43a; we rule like Ravah, as below; and although there is no difference since we rule like Rav — there is a difference in such a case, as we wrote requires study 2; and there it implies we rule like Ravah.

(ג) And if. And on its head. Shabbat 7b — and if he carved in it, etc.

(ד) For we say carve. Like Rabbi Meir; and although we do not rule like him in d'rabbanan we rule like him that the sea is like karmelis, as we wrote in chapter HaZorek; and proof from Rabbi who holds like him in Eruvin 43 — and although we do not rule like Rabbi here since the mishnah is not like him they gave it in a bundle, etc.; and Rabbi's reason since he holds like Rabbi Meir is because it is d'rabbanan — see there Tosafot s.v. and every basket, etc.; thereby one need not Rabbi's explanation nor Rashi's explanation in domains that Rabbi does not hold like Rabbi Meir in Torah domains; but requires study — for behold we rule there 93b mound five, etc.; possibly he holds the reason in mound is because of carving — therefore Magen Avraham siman 11 intended; possibly he means, etc.; but it does not appear so — for then he holds like Rabbi Meir; moreover even R' Chisda concedes in such a case, as Tosafot Shabbat 7b s.v. and the halachah, etc., that when there are not ten in one place we do not say carve; so too requires study what is written here that we say carve, etc.; nevertheless the law is true from that mound — and carving is not needed.
