(א) (siman 7) Since, etc. — since he entered on Shabbat, etc., it is like leaving knowingly since he entered in violation; and leaving knowingly — even in a walled city he has only four cubits as above seif 6; but a ship, since he rested in the air of its partitions, even though knowingly it is considered like four cubits as seif 7 and in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he challenged: since it deals with one who entered it on Shabbat — did he not rest in the air of partitions? He answered it deals with one who acquired residence in it on erev and afterward went outside and afterward returned and entered it on Shabbat — in such a case he did not rest in the air of partitions on erev, or also at the beginning of Shabbat; but since he went outside knowingly — Rabbi Zalman above siman 248 again forbidden to enter it on Shabbat; if so his entry on Shabbat into it is in violation and therefore called leaving knowingly; and per Rama's view above siman 248 that if he acquired residence in it, even though he went outside he is permitted to enter it on Shabbat — per him it is leaving unknowingly and even if he came to another city it is permitted.

(ב) For a large ship because of human dignity; and it implies, etc., that he acquired residence yesterday — i.e. for all: per Rabbi Zalman if he did not go out; and per Rama even if he went out, as above.
