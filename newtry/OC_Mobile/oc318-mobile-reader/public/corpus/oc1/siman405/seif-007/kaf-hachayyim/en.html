7) [Se'if 4] Without awareness. For example, his mind was preoccupied and he did not realize that he was leaving the techum, as explained in the Gemara. The same applies if he left under coercion or unintentionally, as explained in the following se'if. It likewise applies if he left for a mitzvah purpose, which is also considered comparable to coercion, as stated above in subsection 3.
