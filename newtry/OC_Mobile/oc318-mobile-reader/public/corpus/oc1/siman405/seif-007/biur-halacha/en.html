(א) Since he rested, etc. — see Mishna Berurah; in Magen Avraham he reconciles Shulchan Aruch's words that he rested in the air of partitions specifically — it deals when he sailed in violation, e.g. he entered there on erev and acquired residence and went out and the next day entered the ship and sailed; the Mechaber follows his view in siman 248 seif 3 that since he left the ship at night it is as if he did not acquire residence and it is forbidden for him to sail the next day; in such a case where he sails in violation on Shabbat he is certainly forbidden to move from his four cubits on the ship itself, like one who left the techum knowingly as explained in seif 6; and here we teach that in his case where he at least rested in the air of partitions at twilight — although it does not help to permit sailing — nevertheless if he sailed he is permitted to carry throughout the ship; only if non-Jews took him from there and placed him in another ship where he did not rest he is forbidden to go there [nevertheless if he needs his bodily needs he may enter a small ship hanging from it to relieve himself and return to the large ship, for he is under duress in his leaving, as siman 406]; all this is per the Mechaber; but per Rama's view there siman 248 that he is permitted to leave the ship at night and sail the next day — then we do not find this law that since he entered permissibly it is like leaving unknowingly [we omitted words of some Acharonim who brought the view of Rashbatz and Ran that even when he entered permissibly we require specifically that he rested — we cannot be so stringent since per Rabbi Yehudah even when he entered in violation it is called leaving unknowingly because a ship goes by itself; if so we may follow Or Zarua and Hagahot Ashiri who wrote there that only when he entered improperly is it called leaving knowingly — see Or Zarua laws of Eruvin siman 246; so Raosh chapter Mi Shehutziah siman 6; examine] — this is the crux of Magen Avraham's words per what Eliyah Rabbah, Machatzit HaShekel, and Peri Megadim explained; so Tosafot Shabbos.

(ב) And if the ship walls broke on Shabbat — and we do not say here once permitted remains permitted, for where there are no partitions we do not say so [Acharonim]; Or Zarua preceded on this.
