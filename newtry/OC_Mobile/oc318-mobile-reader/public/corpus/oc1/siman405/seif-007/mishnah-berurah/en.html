(א) One whose ship sailed, etc. — deals with one who came on erev Shabbat near a port where the ship rests below ten handbreadths and he intended to leave the ship and enter the city; a wind came and sailed the ship outside the techum in a place where the water was less than ten handbreadths from the ground; per what is explained above siman 404 it is forbidden for him to leave the ship and he has only four cubits — nevertheless on the ship itself he walks all of it, for it is like four cubits since it is enclosed with partitions.

(ב) Walks all of it — even when it is standing.

(ג) Since he rested in the air of partitions — the wording is ambiguous and not precise; per what is explained above in seif 6, if his ship sailed unknowingly and brought him to a walled city where he did not rest, or non-Jews placed him in another ship — he also walks all of it since his leaving was under duress; rather the main intent is "since in the air of partitions" — unlike when the walls broke, as he concludes.

(ד) If its walls broke, etc. — and nothing remained ten handbreadths high, thereby the name of partitions is nullified; the same if it was breached in one place a breach more than ten amot wide, or the breach was greater than what stands — in all of these the partition name is nullified from it [Acharonim].

(ה) Traveling — in traveling it is not relevant to say he leaves his four cubits in his walking, for at every moment he stands in different four cubits — the ship always takes him from his four cubits to other four cubits [Gemara].

(ו) Standing — outside the techum in a place that is low below ten handbreadths; above ten we stated there is no techum prohibition and he is permitted to walk as much as he wants even without partitions at all — see siman 248 seif 2 what is called below ten.

(ז) And on its head, etc. — e.g. it is deep in its middle and on its head it is sloped and goes upward like small ships — nevertheless it is all reshut hayachid, for what is on its head has the law of holes of reshut hayachid that are like reshut hayachid, as above siman 345.

(ח) Even if he has none at all — that also in its middle it is not ten deep.

(ט) To carve — meaning the thickness of the planks in its floor has enough to carve in order to join with the partitions to complete the measure of ten.

(י) Carve to complete — and although we hold we do not carve to complete, here is different — included in what we say [siman 372 seif 6] a mound of five and a partition of five combine for the measure of ten handbreadths.
