(א) Since he rested, etc. — but if he came to another city it is forbidden, for he did not rest in the air of partitions; since he entered the ship on Shabbat it is like one who left knowingly (Hagahot Ashiri and Beit Zevulun siman 220); Olat Shabbat was not precise; and he entered under pressure; see seif 8; the same if non-Jews placed him in another ship — nevertheless he may enter a small ship hanging from it to relieve himself and return to the large ship, as siman 406 (Ramban); and it implies from Hagahot Maimoniot if he entered permissibly, e.g. acquired residence yesterday, it is like leaving unknowingly; so too end siman 404.

(ב) Walks all of it — for always he stands within other four cubits — always the ship takes him from the beginning of four cubits and places him in other four cubits.

(ג) He does not walk in it — see siman 374 seif 2; see there — not as Olat Shabbat; see what I wrote siman 355 end seif 1.

(ד) And on its head he has none, etc. — meaning it is deep in its middle and on its head it is sloped like small ships — nevertheless it is reshut hayachid, for what is on its head is like holes of reshut hayachid which are like reshut hayachid as siman 345; but if the partitions on its head were breached we require that the breach not be greater than what stands and the breach not exceed ten, as siman 362 — implies if there are no ten-handbreadth partitions it is not reshut hayachid and we do not say lower the partition; see siman 355 and so Tosafot Shabbat 101b s.v. and he threw, etc.; and so Eruvin 26 and 43b in Rashi.

(ה) Carve to complete — this law requires study; it appears he intended what we say in Eruvin there — see Rashi and understand it deals with a small ship hanging on a large ship that combine — see there; requires study — for we do not rule like Rabbi Meir siman 345 seif 10 and siman 362; possibly he means the thickness of the planks combine, as siman 372 seif 6.
