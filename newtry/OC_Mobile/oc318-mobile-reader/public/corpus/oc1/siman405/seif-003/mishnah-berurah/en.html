(א) And he is outside the city techum — for if he were inside the city techum he is considered like the people of the city since he intends to enter the city and he has the whole city and two thousand cubits on every side; but since it became dark for him one cubit outside the techum he has only two thousand cubits around the place where he rested Shabbat there.
(ב) To be like the people of the city — meaning that although he is permitted in part to enter within the city, since he rested only one cubit outside the two thousand adjacent to the city he may enter three cubits within the city, for the measure of two thousand is measured apart from the four cubits in which he rested as above siman 397 — nevertheless he is not considered thereby to be like the people of the city to walk within it and two thousand around it, since in any case at the time the day was sanctified he was outside the city techum [Gra].
