5) [Se'if 3] And he is outside the techum, etc. If he were within the techum, then since he intends to enter the city, he would have the status of the city's residents and could walk throughout the city and two thousand amos from the city in every direction, as stated above in siman 400.
6) There. He may not enter and acquire the status of the city's residents, etc. He may nevertheless walk three amos within the city, for he has two thousand amos in addition to the four amos that constitute the place of his Shabbos residence, as stated at the beginning of siman 397. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 3.
