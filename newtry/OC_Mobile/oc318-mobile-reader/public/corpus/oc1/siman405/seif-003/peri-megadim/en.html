Enclosed — Taz: our cities' houses are enclosed and require enclosure for residence — he walks all of it; and it appears even if they did not make eruv and forbidden to carry from houses to alley — nevertheless full reshut hayachid and he walks all like four cubits; in the alley itself and courtyard permitted to carry all vessels that rested in the courtyard; I say regarding a karpef more than beit sa'atayim not enclosed for residence — there it is karmelis upon it and forbidden to carry within more than four cubits — one does not walk all unlike such a case.
