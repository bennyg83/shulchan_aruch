(א) Unknowingly — under duress, or who left by mistake; the same for a mitzvah matter.
(ב) Permitted, etc. — meaning they make a remedy for him to return within his techum; and this is since he left — even though he has only four cubits — we hold that if there were partitions there he is permitted to walk the full width of the partitions, for all of it is considered like his four cubits as we wrote below in seif 6; therefore when they make for him a partition of people around him up to his techum he enters thereby; and since he entered within his techum permissibly he is as if he did not leave, as below in seif 5.
(ג) A partition of people — and even if the length of the partitions is more than two thousand he is permitted to walk all of it.
(ד) And he enters among them — that he enters among them up to within his techum; and since he entered within the techum he is permitted to walk to his city even without partitions, as above. Per what we wrote above in seif 2 in the name of the Poskim — even if the people's partitions did not reach exactly into the techum but only near the techum less than four cubits — also permitted, since in any case his four cubits are absorbed within his techum he is permitted to enter.
(ה) And he who did not know — see siman 362 where the particulars of people's partitions are explained.
(ו) Knowingly — forbidden; for even within full partitions he is not permitted in such a case to walk more than four cubits, as below in seif 6 — all the more with a partition of people.
