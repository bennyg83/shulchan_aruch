4) [Se'if 2] He may reenter. We classify him according to the majority of his body. Since only one foot is outside the techum, most of his body remains within it, and he may therefore reenter. Rashi. According to what was written above, that one has four amos outside the techum, the same law applies here: if one went out under coercion and one foot is within four amos of the techum while the other is beyond those four amos, one may reenter. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2; Chayei Adam, there; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 4.
