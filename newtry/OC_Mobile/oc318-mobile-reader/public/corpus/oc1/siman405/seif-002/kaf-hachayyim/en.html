2) There. He may not reenter even if he went only one amah beyond it, etc. The Tur writes this, as does Rambam in chapter 27. Maggid Mishneh writes there that Rashba rules in accordance with Rabbi Shimon, who says that even if one went fifteen amos beyond the techum, one may reenter, because those who mark the measurements do not mark them precisely and leave fifteen amos beyond the marked location. See there. Beis Yosef cites this and writes that the ruling follows Rambam. This is also the opinion of the later authorities.
