(siman 2) Partition, etc. — even, etc., more than two thousand, etc.; as siman 403 states — specifically siman 403 regarding non-Jews who surrounded him with a partition on Shabbat: even without non-Jews' partitions he may walk two thousand amot — they did not permit him to walk more through a non-Jew's partition made on Shabbat; not so here, where without people's partitions he would have only four cubits and it would be impossible to permit him even two thousand except through people's partition made on Shabbat; and since they were lenient for him to walk two thousand through people's partition, the whole length of the partition is permitted — what difference two thousand or more? As Magen Avraham wrote — all this in siman 403.
