He leaned to what Ra'avad challenged — behold we hold swallowing techum is a matter, whether he left for a mitzvah or left under duress (as written siman 397 seif 2); and this too is under duress in the distance of one cubit — why may he not enter? Behold his four cubits are swallowed within the techum; Kesef Mishnah answered that since he is walking and wants to go it appears he clarified toward his face — strained, end of his words; therefore it appears to me to be lenient here if it was duress, as all Poskim wrote — examine well; but for mundane matters all agree it is not a matter; so Me'or wrote and Avodat HaKodesh siman 396; and it seems he did not see Ra'avad's words that imply per Kesef Mishnah's explanation; so too from Rambam's wording; see there; he followed Bach; also Kesef Mishnah's words there require study — examine well.
