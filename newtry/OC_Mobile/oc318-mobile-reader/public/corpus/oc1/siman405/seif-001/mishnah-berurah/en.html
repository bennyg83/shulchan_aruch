(א) May not enter to be like, etc. — meaning do not say one cubit is not considered leaving and therefore he is like the people of the city.
(ב) From where he stands and outward — but toward the inside not at all; for certainly per all he can clarify his four cubits to whichever side he wishes; this case is different since once he began walking this direction he appears to have clarified from himself going forward. The Mechaber follows this in siman 396 that he has only four cubits; but per what Rama brought there in Hagah in the name of those who say he has four cubits in every direction, Acharonim ruled so — if so he may walk his four cubits even toward the city side, i.e. if he left one cubit he may return within the techum three cubits; know all this if he left intentionally; but if he left under duress or from distraction forgot and left, Acharonim agreed that whoever did not leave the techum four cubits may return and enter within the city — in such a case we say since he has permission to return within his techum he is considered as if he never left the techum at all and he is like the people of the city; all the more if he left for a mitzvah matter he is permitted to return within his techum and from there to the city as long as he did not go beyond four cubits within the techum.
