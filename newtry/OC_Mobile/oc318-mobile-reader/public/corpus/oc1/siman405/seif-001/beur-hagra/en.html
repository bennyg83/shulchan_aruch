(א) Seif 1 — one who left. Like Tanna Kamma; and that which we say one amah is per R' Nachman in chapter 2 of Rabbi Elazar — that is under duress, as we wrote above seif 5; and from Rabbi Elazar's words it is proven to the Master that the halachah is not like Rabbi Elazar; and in Milchamot he wrote that sugya of one amah was rejected before this sugya of one amah and the words of the rabbis are correct.

(ב) From where he stands. Rashi there s.v. one who left — intentionally and knowingly, etc.; since he went as we wrote there seif 5 he said to him there is no swallowing of techum, etc.; the same for duress as Magen Avraham wrote seif 2; so too Ra'avad; but Rambam explains even if he left under duress — therefore he wrote from where he stands, etc., for he ruled like Rabbi Yehudah who said if he clarified, etc., there seif 5; and this is since he already began one cubit — he already clarified, etc.

(ג) To be like city people. But to enter the city he is permitted, as we wrote above siman 397 seif 1; and Rashi there s.v. and to measure, etc.; thereby the Kesef Mishnah's question on Rambam there is resolved.
