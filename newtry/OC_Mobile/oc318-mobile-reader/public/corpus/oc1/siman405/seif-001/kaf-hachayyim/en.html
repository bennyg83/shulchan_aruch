1) [Se'if 1] One who went beyond the techum, etc. This means intentionally and knowingly, not for the purpose of a mitzvah. Rashi. Bach writes likewise. If he went out without awareness, see below, se'if 4 and subsection 3. If he went out unintentionally, see Eliyah Rabbah, siman 396, subsection 3, and in this siman, subsection 1, where he writes that an unintentional violation has the status of an intentional one. Se'if 5 below, however, proves that an unintentional violation has the status of one performed under coercion. See there. Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2, writes likewise.
