(א) (siman 1) From where he stands, etc. Swallowing techum, etc. — meaning if part of what he can now walk was swallowed within the techum from which he left, it is as if he left; and here even if we say once he left the techum one cubit he lost his techum, nevertheless at his standing place now he has at least four cubits; and if so per Rabbi Elazar who said those four cubits are in their center and he has two on each side — if so from his standing place toward his techum he has two cubits; and since he left only one cubit he may enter within the techum at least one cubit; and if so his law is as if he did not leave.

(ב) Whether he left — as it should read.

(ג) Under duress; but intentionally he agrees swallowing techum does not help.

(ד) And this too is under duress, etc. — meaning the default of the matter: leaving one cubit has no purpose, as it says he left knowingly; and the default of the matter is leaving unknowingly; and if Rambam deals when leaving one cubit was knowingly, he should explain since the default is not so.

(ה) And Kesef Mishnah answered, etc. — meaning Rambam rules like Rabbi Yehudah that those four cubits he has are in one direction and clarification is in his hand to clarify whichever direction he wishes; therefore it appears he clarified toward his face and he has no walking toward his techum at all and his four cubits are not swallowed within the techum; if so per what Rama wrote above siman 396 seif 1 like Rabbi Elazar who gives those four cubits and he is in their center — if he left only one cubit there is swallowing of techum and it is as if he did not leave; perhaps Rama relied on himself on what he wrote above siman 396 and therefore did not emend here; this is what Magen Avraham meant — the Rav intended, and it is Rambam's wording, from where he stands outward per Kesef Mishnah's words, since when walking it appears he clarified outward.

(ו) Therefore it appears to me to be lenient, etc. — since Kesef Mishnah himself testified his answer is strained and it is reasonable per Ra'avad's view.

(ז) And as it seems he did not see, etc. — Bach means Rambam also follows Rif's way, for his way is to rule like Rabbi Elazar who gives four cubits and he is in their center; only Rambam deals when he left one cubit outside the techum knowingly — in such a case swallowing techum is not a matter; on this he wrote he did not see Ra'avad's words who also understood Rambam deals when he left under duress, etc.; perforce one must say as Kesef Mishnah answered; so too Rambam's wording implies — he also wrote like Shulchan Aruch from where he stands outward, which indicates as Maggid Mishnah wrote as above.

(ח) And also Kesef Mishnah's words, etc. — it appears from his words that he too leans from Rambam's wording; he wrote the reason he may not enter in the four cubits a person has — their beginning is from where he stood, etc.; he holds like Rabbi Yehudah that four cubits are given on one side; on this Kesef Mishnah wrote, etc., end of his words; requires study; and he made for himself as if he did not know Ra'avad's words nor Kesef Mishnah's words.
