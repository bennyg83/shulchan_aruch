(1) (a) We must fasten, etc. - and it is from the words of the prophets as written in the Korah (Zechariah 8) the fourth fast and the fifth fast and the seventh fast and the tenth fast and 3 and 3 and Ahzal the fourth fast is the 17th of Tammuz which is on the fourth month of the new ones and the fifth fast is the 9th of Av which is in the fifth month and the seventh fast is the fast of Gadli' which is in the seventh month and the tenth fast is the tenth of Tebat which is in the tenth month. And all of these days all Israel fasts on them because of the troubles that happened in them in order to awaken the hearts to monitor the ways of repentance and it will be a remembrance of our bad deeds and the deeds of our ancestors which were like our deeds now until it caused them and us the same troubles. As with the people of Nineveh, the Omnipresent saw their deeds, and the Sages said: "They rose, and their fasting is not spoken of, but their deeds, and fasting is nothing but a preparation for repentance. Therefore, those people who, when they are fasting, go on a trip and talk about idle things, took care of the matter and laid down the main thing, and that one should not absolve oneself by repentance alone, because these are the days of the words of the prophets to repent in, and as we wrote above:

(ב) Because of the evil events, etc. — for on the 17th of Tammuz five troubles occurred: the tablets were broken when Moshe descended from the mountain as explained in the Torah; the daily offering ceased from being offered in the First Temple; the city was breached in the destruction of the Second Temple; Apostomus the wicked burned the Torah and set up an idol in the Sanctuary. And on the 9th of Av was the great destruction in which the First Temple was destroyed and also the Second; and on that day it was decreed on our ancestors in the wilderness that they would not enter the Land; and on that day the great city Betar was captured — there were in it thousands and myriads of Israel and all fell into the hands of the non-Jews and all were killed, and it was a great tragedy like the destruction of the Temple; and on that day, the day prepared for punishment, Turnus Rufus the wicked plowed the Sanctuary and its surroundings to fulfill what is said, 'Zion shall be plowed like a field.' And on the 3rd of Tishrei Gedaliah son of Achikam was killed — they had appointed him head after the destruction over the remnant that remained — and the ember of Israel that remained was extinguished, for through his being killed they all went into exile and thousands of them were killed. And on the 10th of Tevet Nebuchadnezzar the wicked king of Babylon laid siege to Jerusalem and brought it into siege and distress, and from this the destruction continued.

(ג) On them — one who erred and ate on them nevertheless must complete the fast after eating, for one who ate garlic should eat garlic again; and if he must fast another day see below siman 568 seif 1 and in Mishna Berurah there.
