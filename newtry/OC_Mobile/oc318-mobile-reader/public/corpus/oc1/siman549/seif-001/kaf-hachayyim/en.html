1) [Se'if 1] One is obligated to fast on Tishah BeAv, etc. These four fast days are explicitly stated in the Prophets: 'the fast of the fourth, the fast of the fifth, the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth.' The fast of the fourth is the seventeenth of Tammuz, which is in the fourth month. The fast of the fifth is Tishah BeAv, which is in the fifth month. The fast of the seventh is the third of Tishrei, which is in the seventh month. The fast of the tenth is the tenth of Teves, which is in the tenth month. This is explained in Rosh Hashanah 18b. Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilchos Taaniyos, law 4, and Tur write likewise. Although this is Rabbi Akiva's view and Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai says in the Talmud there that he does not explain it this way, Beis Yosef writes that Tur records Rabbi Akiva's view because universal practice follows it. Maggid Mishneh writes likewise regarding the aforementioned words of Rambam and says this is the halachah. Ramban, in Toras HaAdam at the beginning of the section on ancient mourning, likewise writes that both halachah and custom follow Rabbi Akiva. Birkei Yosef, subsection 3, cites him. See there. Shulchan Aruch therefore rules accordingly, as do the later authorities.
