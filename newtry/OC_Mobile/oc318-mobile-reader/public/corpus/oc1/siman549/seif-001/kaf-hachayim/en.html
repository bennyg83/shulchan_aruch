A) [Section 1] They must fast on Tisha B'av, etc. and the fourth days of these fasts are explained in Kabbalah (Zechariah 8:19) as the fourth fast, the fifth fast, the seventh fast, and the tenth fast. The weekly fast is the 17th of Tammuz, which is in the fourth month. And the fifth fast is the 12th, which is in the 5th month, and the seventh fast is the 3rd of Tishri, which is in the 7th month, and the 10th fast is the 10th of Tevet, which is in the 10th month, as explained in No. RA, page 18 12.
