1. And on the tenth of Teves, etc. These are the words of the Ramban in Toras HaAdam: and the halachah and the custom follow R' Akiva. And that is to say: [we say] "the halachah," because [the rule that] the law does not follow a disciple in the place of his master applies specifically from Abaye and Rava onward; [thereafter] the halachah follows the later authorities.
