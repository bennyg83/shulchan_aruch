In the Shulchan Aruch: Even though it is written, etc. And see in the annotation of the Taz that we do not recite Kiddush Levanah before Shabbos Nachamu, etc.; and our practice is as written in siman 506 in the name of the Arizal, to recite Kiddush Levanah on Motza'ei Tisha B'Av; and see in Shevut Yaakov, volume 2, siman 11, that he would customarily taste something beforehand, and I brought it in Shaarei Teshuvah there; and see below in siman 551, s.k. 25, that he wrote to announce to the moon, etc.; and in Birkei Yosef, siman 559, he wrote to announce to the Shechinah, which is alluded to as the moon, etc. And see in Darkei Moshe in the name of Maharil: one who forgot and ate on the tenth of Teves should fast Monday-Thursday-Monday; and Eliyah Rabbah wrote that it is obvious that he must complete the tenth of Teves itself after he remembers. In the Selichos for the seventeenth of Tammuz, [the phrase] "lakachtah bechatasah beroshah uvekevel" is a printer's error, and it should read "uvekeifel" with a pei, and it is based on the verse "for she has received from the hand of Hashem double for all her sins"; and the meaning of the word "beroshah" is the principal, as is written "and he shall restore his guilt upon his head"; see there.
