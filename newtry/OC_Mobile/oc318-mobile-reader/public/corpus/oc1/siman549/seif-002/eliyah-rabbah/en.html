[Levush] The words of Yirmiyah, etc. And it concludes "ne'um Hashem," and that is the haftarah of Shemos according to the Sephardi custom. And if he did not recite as the haftarah "Divrei Yirmiyah," it is explained in siman 508, se'if 8. And as to why they did not institute a haftarah for a Shabbos that falls on the seventeenth of Tammuz, one may say that the mourning of Bein HaMetzarim has not yet begun, since [the fast] was postponed; and furthermore, three haftaros correspond to three weeks.
