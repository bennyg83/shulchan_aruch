2) These are the days on which all Israel fasts because of the calamities that occurred on them, in order to awaken hearts and open the paths of repentance. They serve as a remembrance of our evil deeds and the deeds of our ancestors, which resembled our present deeds, until they caused those calamities for them and for us. By remembering these matters, we will return to act properly, as the verse states, 'They will confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their ancestors, etc.' Rambam there, law 1. Therefore, every person is obligated to repent and examine his deeds on these days more than on other days, since this was the purpose of their enactment.
