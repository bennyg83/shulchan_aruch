Although the verse states, “In the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the city was breached,” Midrash Tehillim and Eichah Rabbah state that from the seventeenth of Tammuz until Tisha B’Av a person should take care not to walk alone from the fourth hour of the day until the ninth hour. Hagahos Darkei Moshe associated with the laws of Tisha B’Av cites that Tosafos states that on the Shabbos before Rosh Chodesh Av one should not bless the new month, based on the verse, “Let it not enter into the number of the months.” This is incorrect. On the contrary, the Jewish people especially require the blessing at that time, because it is a period predisposed to calamity. Such is the accepted practice. Kiddush Levanah is not recited before Shabbos Nachamu. It appears that this applies specifically when Shabbos Nachamu is close to Tisha B’Av, such as when Tisha B’Av falls on Thursday. If Tisha B’Av falls on Tuesday, however, Shabbos Nachamu is already the thirteenth of the month. One should not wait until Motzaei Shabbos, but should recite Kiddush Levanah on a weekday after Tisha B’Av, because there is concern that the following nights may be cloudy. Such is the accepted practice.
