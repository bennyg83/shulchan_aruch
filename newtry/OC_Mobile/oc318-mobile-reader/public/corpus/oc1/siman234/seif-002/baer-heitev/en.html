Before Ashrei. The Taz and Magen Avraham wrote to say it after Ashrei of Maariv. And the Ari, may his memory be blessed, wrote to say Pitum HaKetoret after parshat hatamid. One who comes late to the synagogue at the time when the congregation has begun Minchah should pray Shemoneh Esreh with them and afterward say Ashrei (Magen Avraham, siman 108, note 5, unlike Bach — Maariv).
