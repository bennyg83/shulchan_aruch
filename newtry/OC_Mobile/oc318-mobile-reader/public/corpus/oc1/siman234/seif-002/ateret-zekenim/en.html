If one forgot and did not pray Minchah, etc. — even if he intentionally skipped Minchah he can complete it during the time of the prayer after it if he wishes, without renewal; but for Maariv and Shacharis one should not pray them as tashlumin but as nedavah, and he must renew intent therein. When praying Maariv twice if he forgot and did not pray Minchah, one should not say Tachanun, for there is no nefilat apayim at night as above; and it is obvious as written below in siman 237.
