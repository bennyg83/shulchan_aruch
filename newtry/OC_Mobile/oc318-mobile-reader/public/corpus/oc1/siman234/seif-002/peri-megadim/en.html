Parshat hatamid — Taz and Magen Avraham; and in siman 132, note 2, see there (perhaps scribal error [for] siman 136). And see Berachot 26b, braita: Minchah until evening — that the tamid was offered until evening; and according to R' Yehuda until plag — meaning also the limbs that l'chatchila are offered by day (for maharat pushes off offering the fats of Pesach — beloved is a mitzvah in its time). In the Yerushalmi who said corresponding to ketoret — unlike this braita; also not corresponding to Minchah (Magen Avraham, siman 252). And what the Tur wrote in siman 252, braita there — and from Rashi it appears s.v. "and the fats" — it appears they were not consumed — see there. And behold in Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchot Tefillah, halacha 2: the time of Minchah — its essence is from nine and a half, corresponding to the tamid of the whole year at nine and a half; and so the Mechaber explained in siman 233 — if so it implies Minchah corresponds to the limbs at Mincha time; but if corresponding to the blood, presumably it was slaughtered at eight and a half and the blood was cast immediately so that it not congeal; and as concluded in Tamid — "we delay slaughter" two hours, meaning from six and a half until eight and a half that the blood is cast — if so its essence should be eight and a half; rather corresponding to the limbs, etc.; and Maariv — they were not consumed — it appears to me.

If the congregation completes Minchah, they do not repeat the prayer; and with God's help in siman 237 it will be explained further.
