(2) There, in the Rama gloss: one should not say Ashrei, etc. — and the reason: the Taz, note 1, wrote because one should not interrupt l'chatchila between Ashrei and Kaddish; but the reason is not because one does not say Kaddish on what was said without a minyan — end of his words. But Magen Avraham, note 1, wrote: if one said Ashrei without a minyan and afterward a minyan came, they should say another psalm and then say Kaddish. And he wrote that the same applies when learning in the synagogue without a minyan and afterward they summon ten to say Kaddish — not proper, my servant; for the reason of Kaddish on study is because the Name was sanctified, and if so we require ten at the time of study, as we derive from "I will be sanctified among the children of Israel" — end of his words. And we already wrote above in siman 55, note mem alef: if they said Ashrei without a minyan and afterward a minyan came, the chazzan should say some verses and then say Kaddish — see there. And the custom is to say "May my prayer be set as ketoret," etc., or for the sake of yichud, etc., and afterward say Kaddish.
