Before Ashrei. And Menachem Azariah in his responsum, siman 14, wrote in name of the Zohar to say it after Ashrei, because in Sukkah daf 23 it is found that for Shacharis they would pray after the offering of the tamid, and for Minchah they would pray before the offering of the tamid, and Ashrei was like a prayer — end quote; and it is good also to say Pitum HaKetoret. Levush Chayim wrote: it is good to say "Give thanks to the the Master, bless," etc., until "and He is compassionate," because they would say it before the Ark at Maariv.
