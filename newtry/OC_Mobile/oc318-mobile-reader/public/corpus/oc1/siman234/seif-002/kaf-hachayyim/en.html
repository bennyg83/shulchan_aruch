2) There, gloss: One should not say Ashrei, etc. Taz, paragraph 1, explains that the reason is to avoid initially interrupting between Ashrei and Kaddish. The reason is not that one may not recite Kaddish over something said without a minyan. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1, however, writes that if Ashrei was recited without a minyan and afterward a minyan arrived, they should recite another psalm and then Kaddish. He writes that the same applies when people study in the synagogue without a minyan and afterward summon ten to recite Kaddish. They act improperly, because the reason for Kaddish after study is that the Name was sanctified. Therefore, ten must be present during the study, as we derive from, 'I shall be sanctified among the children of Yisrael.' We have already written above in siman 55, paragraph 41, that if they recited Ashrei without a minyan and afterward a minyan arrived, the prayer leader should recite several verses and then Kaddish; see there. The practice is to say, 'May my prayer be established as incense,' etc., or the Leshem Yichud, etc., and afterward recite Kaddish.
