Parshat hatamid before Ashrei. And in my humble opinion it should be said after Ashrei, for this portion is in place of the slaughter and casting of blood of the tamid, and Shemoneh Esreh prayer is in place of its burning on the altar; and proof from what the Tur wrote: Maariv prayer corresponds to the limbs and fats that were not consumed during the day, etc. — from this it is implied that Minchah prayer corresponds to the essence of its burning; and see there siman 136.
