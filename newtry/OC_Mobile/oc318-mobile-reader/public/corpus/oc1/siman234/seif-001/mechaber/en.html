One who wants to pray Mincha gedolah and Mincha ketanah. It contains 2 seifim. One who
wants to pray Mincha gedolah and Mincha ketanah — it is not fit for him to pray the optional one except the gedolah; and if he prays the gedolah as an obligation he should not pray the ketanah except as optional; but it is not fit to pray an optional prayer unless he recognizes in himself that he is careful and diligent and strong-minded to intend in his prayer from beginning to end without distraction of mind; but if he does not intend in it properly, we apply to him "Why do I need your many sacrifices."One should not say Ashrei that precedes Minchah except when there is a minyan in the synagogue so that they say upon it the Kaddish before Minchah prayer (Hagahot Semak and Kol Bo). There are those who wrote that the custom is to say parshat hatamid before Ashrei of Minchah prayer, corresponding to the tamid of between-the-evenings, and it is a fine custom (Abudraham in name of Mahariv and Beit Yosef).
