One should not say Ashrei. And if one said Ashrei without a minyan and afterward they came to a minyan, they should say one chapter of Tehillim and afterward say Kaddish; and it appears to me that the same applies when learning in the synagogue without a minyan and afterward they summon ten to say Kaddish: not proper, my servant; and it is a fortiori, for the essence of the reason for Kaddish on study is because the Name was sanctified, and if so we require ten at the time of study, as we derive from "I will be sanctified among the children of Israel"; and see above siman 157. It is written in Piskei Maharai siman 59: even if part of the congregation prayed in its time and they continued until night, one need not say Kaddish, since day one renewed after they stopped praying: end of his words. And if so, the same applies if they continued in the recitation of Avinu Malkenu and the like, whereby they say Kaddish titkabal; and one must beware not to come to such a situation, and that they say Kaddish before tzet hakochavim: and see there siman 131, seif 3.
