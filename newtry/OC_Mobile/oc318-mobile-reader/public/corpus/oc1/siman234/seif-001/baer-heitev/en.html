Ashrei. And if they said Ashrei without a minyan and afterward a minyan came, they should say one psalm and then say Kaddish; and the same applies when learning in the synagogue without a minyan and afterward they summon ten to say Kaddish — not proper, my servant; rather they require that there be ten at the time of study (Magen Avraham). But the Taz in siman 21, note 5, wrote that whatever is in the saying of Kaddish, ten suffice, and they need not be ten at the time of study — see there, note 7, Mishnat Heaven. And see in responsa Sha'ar Ephraim siman 17. He wrote in Piskei Maharai siman 59: even if part of the congregation prayed in its time and it continued until night, one should not say Kaddish, since the day renewed after they stopped praying — end of his words. And Magen Avraham wrote that the same applies if it continued in the recitation of Avinu Malkenu and the like, whereby they say Kaddish titkabal; and one must beware not to come to such a situation, and that they say Kaddish before tzet hakochavim — see above siman 131, seif 3. And in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he disagrees, that even if it continued until night they say Kaddish.
