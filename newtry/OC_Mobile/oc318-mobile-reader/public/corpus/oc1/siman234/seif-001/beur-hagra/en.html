Seif 1 — One who wants, etc. — as we hold like R' Yehuda who said "would that," etc., and the Rif explained specifically as a nedavah, as written in siman 107.

It is not proper, etc. — Rambam and according to his view, for he holds [Mincha gedolah] only b'dieved; but the Rosh wrote in klal 4, seif 9, that one should pray Mincha gedolah as an obligation; and as written: when Musaf and Minchah were before him, he prays Minchah first and afterward Musaf, for it is more frequent, etc.; and how much more so obligation and nedavah — at Minchah and Maariv one prays as optional. And furthermore we say: one who did not pray Minchah on Shabbat, etc., makes havdalah first, etc. — apparently one prays first what is incumbent upon him and afterward for tashlumin, even though it is also an obligation, and how much more so nedavah. But the Rambam wrote that the law gives [that Mincha ketanah is optional], because it is against something that is not frequent every day; and one should beware from the above regarding Musaf prayer, for we say there "more frequent," etc.; and Minchah and Maariv — both of them are frequent, which is not so here.

And if, etc. — as above, and as written in siman 107.

But, etc. — there.

One should not say Ashrei, etc. — see Magen Avraham, for the essence, etc.

There is [the custom], etc. — as written: prayers corresponding to, etc.; and how much more so per the Yerushalmi, who said that it is corresponding to ketoret, and tamid precedes ketoret, as written in Pesachim, rep"he.
