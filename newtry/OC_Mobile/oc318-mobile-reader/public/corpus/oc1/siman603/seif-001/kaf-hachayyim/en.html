1) [Seif 1] Even one who is not careful regarding bread of non-Jews, etc. Meaning, even one who is not careful throughout the year regarding bread baked by a non-Jewish commercial baker must nevertheless be careful about it during the Aseres Yemei Teshuvah. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 1. Mishna Berurah, os 1. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 112, regarding the distinction between bread of a non-Jewish commercial baker, meaning one who bakes for sale, and bread of a private householder, and also between bread made from the five species of grain and bread made from other types of legumes, see there. See below, os 3.
