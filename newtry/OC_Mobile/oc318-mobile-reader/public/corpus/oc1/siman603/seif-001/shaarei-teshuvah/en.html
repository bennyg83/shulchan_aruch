And what he wrote that one should say the thirteen attributes three times, etc. — see above where I wrote that in Pe'ah it is also stated that the prayer is said three times — see there; and we do not practice thus in the body of the prayer — see there. And Mishna Berurah wrote siman 34 seif 1: one who asked that they give him merchandise to go to a nearby place and they gave him and he was killed on the way — behold Tzemach Tzedek challenged Mahariv regarding one who travels for hire; nevertheless he wrote that he should fast forty days. But in this, that the messenger asked to send him on this mission for his own benefit, there is no concern that he requires atonement. And acts every day that a person takes merchandise on credit or as an agent and goes to fairs — if, Heaven forbid, an accident happens to his life, the seller does not require atonement. And proof from the portion that the Almighty said to David "until when will this be hidden," etc., and Abner is not considered there because Abner was the initiator, etc. — therefore he does not require atonement, only because he instructed, etc., he fasts three days in any case. And if he wishes to give some gift to orphans of the slain, he does well — see there and see there siman 35, order of repentance at length.
