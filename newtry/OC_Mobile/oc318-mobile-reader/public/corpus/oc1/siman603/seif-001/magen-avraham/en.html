It appears to me that one who is traveling need wait only the amount specified in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 112, for they did not accept upon themselves to be stringent in the Ten Days of Repentance more than during the rest of the year. The Shelah, folio 67, wrote that the pious were accustomed to eat ordinary food in purity. Although all of us are tamei from contact with the dead, nevertheless one should eat grain that was not rendered susceptible to tumah and was kneaded with fruit juice, and one should bend down and drink from the well without touching the water and making it tamei. End quote. This requires analysis, for the water becomes tamei in his mouth, as stated in Kiddushin 25 and in several places.
