“Medium.” This requires study: why did he disagree with Baal HaItur—even if there is only enough to wrap eight hairs it suffices, and I did not find early poskim who disputed him. The Gemara says where they are thick all the more they are valid, for the mitzvah is recognizable. At first glance one might learn one should make thick strings—but one can answer: specifically when they snapped, thick strings show the mitzvah more; initially medium is more beautiful, for “I will appear in beauty before Him.”
