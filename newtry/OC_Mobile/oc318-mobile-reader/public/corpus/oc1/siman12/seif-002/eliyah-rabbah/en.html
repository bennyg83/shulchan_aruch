[2] "Two ends—invalid, etc." If there did not remain on any one of them kedei aniva, and even when combined there is kedei aniva—we are in doubt and invalidate. It is implied even when there is doubt whether they are two threads or one; and if you say one, perhaps they combine—and this is proven more in Nachalas Tzvi. But in the responsum of Beit Yaakov, siman 106, he elaborated that they combine even when it is known that it is one thread, and his words are not conclusive.
