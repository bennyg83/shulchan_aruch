[2] Two ends are invalid, etc. This applies when neither end retained enough to tie a bow. Even if together they retain enough to tie a bow, we remain doubtful and invalidate them. This appears to be so even where there is doubt whether the two ends belong to two strings or to one, and even if they are from one string perhaps they combine. This is especially evident from Nachalas Tzvi. Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 106, writes at length that they combine even when it is known that both ends are from one string, but his arguments are not compelling.
