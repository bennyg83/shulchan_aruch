Magen Avraham, se'if katan 7: To go out wearing it on Shabbos into the public domain. Magen Avraham may have specified the public domain precisely; entering a karmelis is permitted. We rely on Ri when no alternative exists, although one could avoid wearing the garment. Since no other tzitzis are available in the city, he cannot make new ones; in such a case there is no Torah-level positive obligation, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 13:8, and regarding a rabbinic obligation we rely on Ri. The same therefore applies to the rabbinic prohibition of a karmelis. One can also reason that even if he intends to remain in the private domain, his permission to wear the garment necessarily relies on Ri; consequently, entering a karmelis is also permitted. There is no distinction between one rabbinic prohibition and two. He may not enter the public domain, however. While he remains home, Ri's view is not needed to avoid a Torah prohibition, because wearing the garment on Shabbos entails no Torah prohibition there. He is therefore forbidden to enter the public domain.
