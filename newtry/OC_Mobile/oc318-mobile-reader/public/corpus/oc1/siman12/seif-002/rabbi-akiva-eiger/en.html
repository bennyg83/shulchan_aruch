Magen Avraham subsection 7 — to go out with it on Shabbos to public domain. One can say Magen Avraham precisely wrote public domain; but for karmelis it is permitted — one can say what they rely on R' Yehuda where not possible — even though he could not wear that garment; but since he cannot make other tzitzis as none are found in the city — in such case there is no Torah positive commandment, as Magen Avraham siman 13 seif 8; and regarding rabbinic law rely on R' Yehuda. If so, likewise rabbinic prohibition of karmelis. Also one can say even if he goes only to private domain, therefore reliance on R' Yehuda — thus permitted also for karmelis. No difference between one prohibition or two prohibitions. Only public domain he may not walk, since at home they need not rely on R' Yehuda's view for Torah law — for biblically on Shabbos there is no prohibition; therefore forbidden to walk to public domain.
