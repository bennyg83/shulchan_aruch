“One may rely on R. Tam.” Above, gloss 61, I wrote: where possible it is good to heed R. Tam’s view—where other tzitzis are easy to find—but one need not chase him; here I wrote where impossible—where no tzitzis are found at all—one may rely on R. Tam. Still it appears to me: one should not bless on them, and it is forbidden to go out with them on Shabbos according to Rosh.
