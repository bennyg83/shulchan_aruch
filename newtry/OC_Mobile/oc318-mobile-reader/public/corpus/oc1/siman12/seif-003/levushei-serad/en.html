{Rama: Magen Avraham, seif katan 3:} There is room to doubt. That is, Beit Yosef himself was in doubt about this; therefore he wrote in Shulchan Aruch that if it was severed entirely—in Halakhot Gedolot it is certainly invalid, and if something remains through combining it is invalid only out of doubt. See Taz, seif katan 1, and what he wrote.
