Magen Avraham, seif katan 3. “The matter remains doubtful.” Beit Yosef himself was uncertain about this. He therefore writes in the Shulchan Aruch that if it was entirely severed it is certainly invalid, whereas if the two ends together retain enough to tie a bow it is invalid only because of doubt. See Taz, seif katan 1, and what is written there.
