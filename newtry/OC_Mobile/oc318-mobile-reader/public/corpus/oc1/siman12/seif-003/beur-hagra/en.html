Section 3, "And according to Rabbi Yitzchak, etc." This is from what is stated there: "Infer from this that one requires, etc.," for if it referred to the loose thread, it would not be permitted. And in Sifrei, "'Cords,' etc.; when was this said? At the outset, etc.," it implies that it refers to the cord. Rashi explained that Rav, who said "Infer from this, etc.," disagrees and does not require this according to everyone. They were pressed to explain this because we rule like Rav, who says that a cord and a loose thread are both required indispensably, as the Mordechai wrote above.
