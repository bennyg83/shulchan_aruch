One may rely upon Ri. At the end of seif 1 the Mechaber writes that, where possible, it is preferable to take Rabbeinu Tam’s view into account. This means where other tzitzis can readily be found, but one need not search for them. Here, “where it is impossible” means a place where no other tzitzis are available at all, and one may then rely upon Ri. Nevertheless, he should not recite a blessing over them, and it is also forbidden to go out with them into a public domain on Shabbos. Magen Avraham. Responsa Ginas Veradim, Orach Chayim, rule 2, siman 1, writes that in pressing circumstances one may validate them even when a small portion of the wound section itself was severed; see there. See also Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 118, which writes that one whose wound strings were severed and who has no replacement strings should undo three windings together with the three upper knots, leaving the bottom knot and its winding so that there remain both a wound section and loose strings, until other strings become available. It is obvious that when the tzitzis become invalid he must remove the garment. Remaining stationary so as not to walk four amos does not help, for the law of four amos applies only to one who walks without tzitzis.
