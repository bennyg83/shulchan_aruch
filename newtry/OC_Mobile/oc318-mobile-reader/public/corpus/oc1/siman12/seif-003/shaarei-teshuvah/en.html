Tore. Eliyah Rabba — see below subsection 4 it does not help validate due to double doubt; Beit Yaakov siman 106 wrote that two sides combining for enough for a loop is questionable — specifically half from this side and half from that; but if from one side little and other side more, even though combined they have enough for a loop — does not combine; and room to say even in such case they may combine — see there. In Leket HaKemach wrote in his name plainly in such case does not combine — but no, for he wrote afterwards indeed in Gemara Chullin regarding liver it implies we do not require half from here — see there. And see there he wrote combining enough for a loop from two sides depends on dispute: for Rashi that enough for a loop from gedil does not combine since string is bound by knots in tzitzis like sewing below; and per R' Yehuda and Rosh the measure of enough for a loop is from anaf without knots — thus stands to unfold and become enough for a loop and combine — see there. In my humble opinion not clear, for there when unfolded they will be joined in his hand, unlike here where one string is on this side and one on that and knots prevent joining. Also what he wrote per Rashi comparing to sewing is not clear; main thing per later authorities to invalidate here; since no practical difference I shortened. And what Beit HaTalmid wrote in name of Taz if they were tied, etc. valid if retied — in Machatzis HaShekel did not intend well Taz's view, for although he discussed it, in conclusion he does not validate where this leads to invalidity except if enough for a loop remained still valid — tying helps; practical difference if also severed from second side afterwards; likewise in Mishbetzos Zahav; see Machatzis HaShekel who brought Eliyah Rabbah and wrote unlike Beit HaTalmid — see there.
