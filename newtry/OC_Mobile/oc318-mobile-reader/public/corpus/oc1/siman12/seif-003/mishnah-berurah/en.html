(13) One may rely on R' Yehuda — and it is permitted to wear the garment. And above in seif 1 where he wrote "whenever possible" — it is better to heed Rabbeinu Tam's view, meaning when another tzitzis can easily be found; but one need not search after them if they are not ready before him or if thereby he would miss prayer with the congregation. And here he wrote "where it is not possible" — meaning where no tzitzis at all are available — one may rely on R' Yehuda. Nevertheless one should not bless on them, and it is also forbidden to go out with them on Shabbos to a public domain. And in such a case one may validate in pressing circumstances even when part of the gedil itself tore [responsa of Ginzburg — see there]. And know that all this does not apply when the string tore at the hole, for there even according to R' Yehuda it is biblically invalid. And it is obvious that when the tzitzis become invalid he must remove them; and standing in place without walking four cubits does not help, for the law of four cubits applies only to one walking without tzitzis at all. Also he should not fix the tzitzis while the tallis is still on him — rather remove it and then fix.
