(13) One may rely upon Ri. It is permitted to wear the garment. Above in seif 1, where the Mechaber writes that where possible it is preferable to take Rabbeinu Tam's view into account, he means where other tzitzis can readily be found. One need not search for them when they are not presently available or when doing so would cause him to miss communal prayer. Here, "where it is impossible" means a place where no other tzitzis are available at all, and one may then rely upon Ri. Nevertheless, he should not recite a blessing over them, and he may not go out with them into a public domain on Shabbos. In such a pressing circumstance one may also validate them when a small portion of the wound section itself was severed, as stated in responsa Ginas Veradim; see there. Know that none of this applies when the string was severed at the hole, for there it is invalid according to the Torah even according to Ri. It is obvious that when the tzitzis becomes invalid he must remove the garment. Remaining stationary so as not to walk four amos does not help, because the rule of four amos applies only to walking without tzitzis. He should likewise not repair the tzitzis while the tallis is still upon him, but should remove it and then repair it.
