(Seif katan 1) "Kedei, etc." The general matter: The first view in Shulchan Aruch is the view of the Rosh, and the second view is the view of Rabbeinu Tam; their dispute depends on this—that we say: "The sons of R' Chiya: stumps of tekhelet are valid," namely, if the threads were severed, the remainder is called stumps. And we say: How much is the measure of stumps? Shmuel said: kedei aniva. And the Rosh holds that both tekhelet and white threads are called tekhelet, since tekhelet is primary; and if so, since the sons of R' Chiya said "stumps of tekhelet are valid," this means even a single stump—and if all the threads were severed, both tekhelet and white, nevertheless they are valid, provided that from each thread kedei aniva remains, as Shmuel said. Rabbeinu Tam holds that the sons of R' Chiya spoke specifically of tekhelet—that if two tekhelet threads were severed and from them kedei aniva remains, it is valid, since two white threads remained completely intact; but if three threads were severed, even though from them kedei aniva remains, nevertheless it is invalid, since only one complete thread remained. This is also brought in the Taz.
