(In Taz subsection 3) Question on tzitzis string, etc. — it implies the reverse, etc. In my humble opinion one need not follow the reverse from this; one can say specifically long and shortened: if there was invalidity because it was long we call telluma, since those tzitzis with which he now fulfills his obligation were already made invalid and no new act occurred in them — only he cut away the invalidating matter; and so placing three for a woman, or cutting string ends after tying. Not so if it was short and lengthened — we do not call "not from that which is already made," since those tzitzis with which he now fulfills his obligation were not made while invalid — only now through a new act that lengthened them they were validated. If so, this is their making now through new making — he acted on them and lengthened them and they were validated to fulfill tzitzis obligation.
