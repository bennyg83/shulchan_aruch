“If they tore,” etc. To understand the dispute — briefly: Gemara sons of R. Chiya say “gardumei tekheles” valid — tzitzis tore and something remained — Shmuel’s measure for gardumei: enough to tie an [aniva] — gather all threads together in what remains — Rav: strand uprooted invalid — no tie measure remained. Rosh explains “gardumei tekheles” means all tzitzis included under “tekheles” — if all four tore and only tie-measure remains valid — but if not even one has tie-measure — only fully snapped invalid — need from each of four strands tie-measure remain — after folding four to eight tips — law if two tips fully tore — if clear each tip from different strand no invalidity — second tip of each remains — concern both torn tips one folded strand — one strand fully uprooted invalid — R. Tam explains “gardumei tekheles” only when there was tekheles but white complete — then two white strands fully twelve thumbbreadths — tie-measure for remaining tekheles — same for us without tekheles taking four white — need two long strands folded four complete twelve thumbbreadths — other two folded four need tie-measure remain — if three tips tore concern each torn tip separate strand — two complete strands lacking — remainder invalid — need two strands folded four complete twelve thumbbreadths. Tur wrote after R. Tam’s view — even two tips torn valid if tie-measure until three tear — difficult: why “has tie-measure” — in one remaining tie-measure other tip fully torn valid — even if two torn tips one strand — since one has tie-measure suffices — Beit Yosef lengthy — Tur’s intent: three tips invalid even tie-measure from each of three torn perhaps three strands — continuation per R. Tam: even what seems valid tie-measure helps only two not three even tie-measure from each tip.
