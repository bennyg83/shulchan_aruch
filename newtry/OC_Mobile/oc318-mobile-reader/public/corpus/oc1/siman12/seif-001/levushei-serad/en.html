{Rama: (Taz, seif katan 1)} It is difficult—why did he say "there is in them"? For if in two ends there is kedei aniva through combining, it does not help; and if so, one cannot explain the wording "in them" through combining. Rather, one should not be concerned lest one thread of them [was severed]; therefore the Tur's intent is that each one requires kedei aniva.
