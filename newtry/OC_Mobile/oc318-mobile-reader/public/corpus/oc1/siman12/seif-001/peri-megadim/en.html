If. At the end of Taz: He cited Gemara Menachot 38b, and in my explanations to the Tur we expanded that there are four explanations here and we cannot expand now. He raised a difficulty on what the Tur wrote for Rabbeinu Tam: if two ends tore, if they have enough for a loop — it implies each end needs enough for a loop (for combined does not help, and see Magen Avraham); if one end suffices with enough for a loop in any case — and further, according to this, if one view it is obvious valid even if eight ends were cut if enough for a loop remained. Beit Yosef expanded and Taz explained the Tur is not returning to permit but clarifying — and according to Rabbeinu Tam who validates with two complete strings of twelve thumbbreadths, two "all agree" does not validate except two ends where one end has enough for a loop; but three ends even if each has enough for a loop — invalid lest three ends are from three strings, and Rabbeinu Tam requires two complete strings twelve thumbbreadths long.
