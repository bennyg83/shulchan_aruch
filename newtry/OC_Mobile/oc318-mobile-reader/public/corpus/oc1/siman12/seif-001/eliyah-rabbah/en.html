[1] Together, etc. Beit Yosef explains that this refers to all the strings that were severed, not to all eight ends when not all were severed. This entire requirement applies where it is possible. Where it is not possible, it is sufficient that each severed string alone retain enough to tie a bow. Taz writes in the name of Beit Yosef that even if enough remained to tie a bow, once the tzitzis were removed they may not be used for another tallis, because their validity is only after the fact. This appears to apply even where enough remains to tie all of the severed strings together. Taz further writes in his responsa that if, before making the tzitzis, one string was severed and retied, and afterward used for tzitzis, it is valid. The same applies after the tzitzis were initially made validly, meaning that enough remained to tie a bow. The prohibition applies only when one ties the string after the tzitzis became invalid through being torn.
