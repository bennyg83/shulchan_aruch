[1] "Together, etc." Beit Yosef explained: from all the threads that were severed, and not from all eight threads unless all were severed; and all this is when possible. But when not possible, enough to tie each one individually suffices. Taz wrote in the name of Beit Yosef that even though kedei aniva remained, if he untied them to make [tzitzis] on another tallit, it is valid only b'dieved—up to here—and it is implied that this also refers to kedei aniva of all of them together. Taz further wrote in his responsa that if initially, before he made tzitzis from the threads, one thread was severed and he tied it, and afterwards he made tzitzis from them—it is valid. And likewise after it has been made valid—namely, kedei aniva—and there is no prohibition except if he ties after making them in the place where they were invalidated by their tearing.
