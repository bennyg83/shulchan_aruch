Two heads that were severed from both sides — if on one there did not remain enough to tie, and on the other there remained enough to tie — it is valid; nevertheless (Beit Yosef in Shulchan Aruch).

And according to our practice to be meticulous when making tzitzit to mark the four heads, etc. — the same applies if all four heads were severed from one side and nothing remained on them enough to tie; it is valid. Twelve [thumbbreadths], end of his words. And one should not cut the heads of the strings with a knife, because it is said, "You shall not lift iron upon them," but rather one should sever them with one's teeth, which are thirty-two, and one should engage with the strings, which are thirty-two (nevertheless, the glosses of Maharshal in the name of R' David bar Nachman). And there are those accustomed to kiss the tzitzit whenever they see them; and so I saw children of the exalted who cherish the mitzvot kiss the sukkah on entering and leaving, and likewise the four species in the lulav — all to endear the mitzvot. And likewise they kiss the matzot and maror on Passover (Luchot HaBrit). And tzitzit [as] notarikon: "A righteous person will examine his tzitzit always."
