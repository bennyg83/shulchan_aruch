Seif 3: One may rely on R' Yehuda. If the gedil is four thumbbreadths long — good to permit the entire gedil (so there not be telluma, as above siman 11 in Magen Avraham margin note 20) and make part gedil and part anaf. For by law four thumbbreadths suffices as Mechaber siman 11 seif 4 appears; especially as Eliyah Rabbah siman 11 seif 6 appears — biblically no measure at all for tzitzis and certainly valid. Thus at least fulfills Torah obligation; and many poskim hold four thumbbreadths even rabbinically suffices. If he leaves them thus one should be concerned for Rashi's view and biblically invalid since there is no anaf, as appears to me.
