Se'if 3: One may rely on Ri. If the wound section is four thumbbreadths long, it is preferable to untie the entire wound section; so as not to violate ta'aseh v'lo min ha'asui, as I wrote above in siman 11 in my gloss to Magen Avraham, se'if katan 20; and then make part wound section and part loose branches. Strictly speaking, four thumbbreadths suffice, as appears to be the Mechaber's view in siman 11:4. This is especially so according to Eliyah Rabbah's apparent view in siman 11:6 that by Torah law tzitzis have no minimum length and even the smallest amount is valid. He will therefore at least fulfill the Torah obligation, and according to the many poskim who hold that four thumbbreadths suffice even rabbinically, he fulfills the full obligation. If he leaves them entirely wound, however, Rashi's view creates concern that they are invalid by Torah law because no loose branches remain. This appears correct to me.
