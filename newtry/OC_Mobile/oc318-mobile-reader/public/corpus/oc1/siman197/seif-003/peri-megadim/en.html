He can. Taz; Tosafot Berachot 48a s.v. until—see there; and Rosh Hashanah 29a; and Magen Avraham 11 that two and one—a Torah scholar blesses even if he did not eat at all. Levush in his commentary brought Yerushalmi that Beit Yosef brought: "and you shall eat and bless"—one who ate blesses; and those who wish to benefit bless—if so there is no place in birkat hamazon that one does not bless unless like a kazayit of grain; even Borei nefashot rabot one does not discharge unless he ate, as we hold such too does not help and he should not bless even though he already ate and is obligated—nevertheless those who wish to benefit apply, and he had no need to eat unless he learned the blessings—see siman 196 Magen Avraham ot 40; and here ot 11; and in Levush he wrote kazayit of bread specifically, not porridge—and if so it will be explained further in Magen Avraham.
