The one who joins must bless the final blessing on what he ate and is not discharged by their birkat hamazon. In what case? That any food suffices to join for ten; but for three he does not join until he eats a kazayit of bread; some say a kazayit of grain even if not bread; some say vegetables or any food. Therefore two who ate and a third came—if they can induce him to eat a kazayit of bread it is better; if he does not want they should not give him to drink or other food; but if they gave him to drink or other food they invite zimun with him even if he does not want to eat bread.
