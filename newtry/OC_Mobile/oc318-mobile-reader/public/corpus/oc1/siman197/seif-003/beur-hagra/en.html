But for three etc. And some say that with vegetables etc. As with ten, for there 45 ruled like Mar Zutra that ten are stricter than three; and further from what is written "to discharge the many" etc. it implies specifically to discharge; and further in Yerushalmi there he said explicitly even with two; and the first view holds that only regarding sitting together we are stricter with ten, but otherwise we say there on the contrary etc.; and so there 47b R' Avahu: and even though etc.; and siman 196 seif 10 in the name of R' Hai Gaon; and from Yerushalmi 40 the reverse of what we say there—it teaches there: and he reclined and ate with them—even though he did not eat a kazayit of grain they invite zimun—the words of the Sages; R' Yaakov bar Acha in the name of R' Yochanan: they never invite zimun upon him until he eats a kazayit of grain; and it was taught: two bread and one vegetable combine—the baraita is like Rashbag; and Tosafot there s.v. nine etc.; and the last view holds that that Yerushalmi argues on our gemara, for there R' Yochanan argues on Rashbag, and in our gemara they answer "joining" etc.; and so that baraita that teaches there etc. is Rashbag etc.; and there is no distinction between three and ten, and R' Yochanan holds in all of them he does not combine.

And some say that with a kazayit etc. As written in the Yerushalmi cited above; and above seif 2 Rambam explained bread—and this is the first view here, which is Rambam's view.
