He can discharge even those etc.—and even though this one who ate a kazayit is obligated only d'rabbanan, for d'oraisa we require actual satiety as I wrote "and you were satisfied and bless"—nevertheless this one can discharge others, for all Israel are guarantors for one another, as we say in chapter "they saw him" in court—all blessings, even if he fulfilled he discharges, and he need not eat a kazayit of grain but that he can say "who has fed us from His bounty"—so Tosafot and Rosh chapter "three who ate."
