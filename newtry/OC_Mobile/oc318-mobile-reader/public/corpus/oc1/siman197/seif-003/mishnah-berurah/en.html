(15) He must bless—after he finishes hearing the zimun blessing.

(16) And he is not discharged—meaning even if he wishes to intend to be discharged by birkat hamazon it does not help, as we hold [below siman 208 seif 17 and Acharonim there] that the blessing of three does not discharge the category of three, nor Borei nefashot rabot; but if he ate dates or drank wine it certainly helps b'dieved, for we hold in siman 208 that if b'dieved he blessed on them in birkat hamazon he fulfilled; and per what several Acharonim ruled there—even for porridge, if he blessed in birkat hamazon he fulfilled—also for porridge the law is thus; and Magen Avraham wrote nevertheless l'chatchila he should intend with them not to be discharged by birkat hamazon, for he must bless on every species its fitting blessing.

(17) To join for ten—for without him there is an obligation of zimun from the bread-eaters, and we do not need him except for mention of the Name.

(18) Kazayit of bread—for he is obligated in birkat hamazon.

(19) That with a kazayit of grain etc.—meaning such as porridge and the like from types of food one must bless on them one blessing of the category of three.

(20) That with vegetables and any food—and the same applies to drinking; and as above in seif 2 regarding ten; and the reason for this view is that for joining zimun they are lenient also for three, since two of them ate bread and are obligated in birkat hamazon, and the third ate a kazayit he is at least obligated in a final blessing.

(21) Therefore two etc.—this is the Mechaber's words to decide between the views: l'chatchila they should urge him to eat bread specifically to fulfill the first view; and if he does not want they should not give him anything so as not to enter themselves into doubt of obligation; but b'dieved if it happened they gave him something that is not bread—they must be concerned for the second view and invite zimun with him.

(22) To drink—and now the custom is if he does not want to eat bread they give him l'chatchila to drink or to eat something, per the other view [Acharonim].
