And he is not discharged. For even the bread-eater, if he ate one thing after the meal he must bless on it, as written siman 178—all the more so this one who did not eat bread at all; and if he drank wine it implies in Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba he is discharged from Borei nefashot rabot—wine too one does zimun; and see siman 208 seif 17 that the same applies to dates; nevertheless l'chatchila he should intend not to be discharged by Borei nefashot rabot, for he must bless on every species fitting for him.
