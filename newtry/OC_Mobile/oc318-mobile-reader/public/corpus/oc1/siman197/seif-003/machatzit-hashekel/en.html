(s.k. 3) And washing etc.—as written there in siman 179 that although Ran holds with "hav lan venevarekh" one may eat, but with washing hands he concedes to Rosh it is forbidden to eat.

And it appears to me he came to teach us etc.—meaning on the contrary—what Rama wrote "and washing etc. like hav lan" means a leniency: that also with mayim acharonim you find they combine, as with "hav lan"—meaning if he wishes to bless first even though it is forbidden etc.

But he must bless birkat hamazon first—for one can say they are always permitted, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 179, not like Rabbeinu Yehudah there.
