They combine. And if he already recited the final blessing he cannot again invite zimun with them for he already departed from them (Hagahot Agudah); and some say for siman 194 seif 1 that there at least he ate bread.

In brine. And nevertheless we require a measure.

Except water. Because one does not do zimun, as Rashi explained in the first chapter of Eruvin (Beit Yosef); and it requires study, for nevertheless he can say "who has fed us from His bounty," for even drinking water is included in eating, as in Yoreh Deah siman 238 seif 2; and Tosafot wrote the reason is because drinking is included in eating, and even though in the gemara it mentions a cup of wine, nevertheless it is not specific, for there are other beverages—but it mentions wine to teach that even so he does not discharge others—see there; and also all poskim wrote generically "who drank"—therefore it seems he combines.
