The one who joins must bless etc.—for birkat hamazon does not discharge Borei nefashot rabot, nor a blessing of the category of three; and Shimon ben Shetach who was drinking a cup of wine and blessed for King Yannai—even in this we say he did for himself, for he holds Borei nefashot rabot discharges the category of three etc., as explained in Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba.
