The one who joins. Taz l'chatchila; also Magen Avraham 7 concedes it is fitting to bless a blessing of the category of three on wine and dates, and b'dieved he is exempt. And Magen Avraham 177:16—there it is b'dieved, for he eats dates after the meal and has birkat hamazon before him—he is exempt even l'chatchila like wine; however in chapter 182 Magen Avraham 8-9 it does not imply thus—see there in Pri Megadim.
