Nine who ate grain and one ate a kazayit of vegetable—they combine to mention the Name, even if he only dipped with them in brine, or only drank with them one cup containing a revi'it of any beverage except water—he joins with them, provided the one blessing is one of the bread-eaters; and even if seven ate grain and three ate vegetable they combine; but six do not, for we require a recognizable majority.
