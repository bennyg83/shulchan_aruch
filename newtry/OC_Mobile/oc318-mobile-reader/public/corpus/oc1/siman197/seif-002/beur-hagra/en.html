Seif 2—kazayit etc. Mordechai; and it appears he learned from what is written "nine who ate" etc., that the one who is not divided from them is only in the species he eats—vegetable—but in measures they are equal; but it requires study, for we say there one dried fig and it is less than a kazayit in what is written in the first chapter of Shabbat 10, and they also said there regarding vegetable.

That contains a revi'it. Mordechai, and as his view as above.

And he is etc. There R' Yitzchak: Beit Shammai hold one who acts for himself etc.—a kazayit of grain etc.; and Rambam explained this is bread over which one blesses birkat hamazon; and in the Rosh there he is uncertain about this.

But six etc. As our text: six certainly etc.; not like Rashi who complained about this.
