(7) And one ate etc.—for although on vegetables one does not bless birkat hamazon, nevertheless he can combine and answer "Blessed are You... who has fed us from His bounty."
(8) Kazayit of vegetable—its reason: since he says "who has fed us" and there is no eating less than a kazayit; or because we require he be obligated in some blessing, and with less than a kazayit there is no obligation in any blessing after his eating, as explained below siman 210.
(9) They combine—and if he already blessed the final blessing they cannot again invite zimun upon him, for he already departed from them [Magen Avraham and Sha'arei Teshuvah].
(10) In brine—meaning he dipped vegetable in brine, and among all of them there was only a kazayit—nevertheless he combines.
(11) One cup—for drinking is included in eating and he can say "who has fed us."
(12) Except water—for water does not do zimun and it is not considered for joining, even if he is thirsty and wishes to drink; and Magen Avraham disagrees and ruled he combines, for all drinking is included in eating—and some Acharonim brought this for the law; however in Bigdei Yeshu'a and Magen Giborim they agreed with the Mechaber, and so sides Chemed Moshe.
(13) The one who blesses etc.—for since he discharges his fellows with the zimun blessing it is stricter.
(14) Vegetable—and the same applies to any beverage, as above.
