Seif 4—companions etc. mitzvah etc. For they are obligated only d'rabbanan, as written 20b, and there a curse comes etc.

And if there are not etc. Tosafot 48a s.v. until etc.; and nevertheless l'chatchila one should conduct per Beit HaGadol and as written in Yerushalmi: every mitzvah from which a person is exempt—he discharges the many with it—except birkat hamazon; and we taught: whoever is not obligated in a matter does not discharge the many with it—one who is obligated discharges; R' Elai said: birkat hamazon is different, as it is written "and you shall eat and be satisfied and bless"—one who ate blesses; but the Rosh wrote it is merely an asmakhta and it is d'rabbanan.

And some say etc. For we hold like R' Meir, as written end of siman 196; and we say 45b that for R' Meir "and you were satisfied" refers to drinking; and Tosafot s.v. R' Meir and as written in Shulchan Aruch—and therefore Shulchan Aruch omitted it.
