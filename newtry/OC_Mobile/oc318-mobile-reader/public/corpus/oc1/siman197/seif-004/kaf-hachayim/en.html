(4) There—and they must give him etc.—because it is a mitzvah they return after a third that he join them for zimun, as written above siman 193 seif 1; and that which is stated below siman 479—mitzvah to return after zimun on Passover nights—which implies on other days of the year there is no mitzvah and no obligation—one can say there one must invite for zimun from before the meal; but here specifically if it occurred during the meal they must give him so he may join. Maamar Mordechai ot 3.
