Taz—behold we hold eating to satiety, then he is obligated—45, and 184:66 and 186:62; and there are poskim like R' Meir with kazayit and "and you were satisfied" refers to drinking; and there is leniency and stringency for each of the explanations; therefore Rama wrote it is good; and nevertheless the essence is as those who say satiety, and he need not drink; and Magen Avraham 11 and Mordechai—it is possible he holds in such a case one obligated d'rabbanan does not discharge d'oraisa, for guarantors apply only to what one is obligated in, and one cannot be exempt from it—unlike optional eating; and nevertheless for us d'rabbanan discharges d'oraisa because of guarantors, as written in ot 3; and therefore only good from two aspects—and one should not elaborate.
