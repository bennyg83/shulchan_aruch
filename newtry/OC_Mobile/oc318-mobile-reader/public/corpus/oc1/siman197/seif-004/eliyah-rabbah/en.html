(4) [Levush] Or even etc. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: what is "even"—one need not say it, as above at the beginning of siman 179—until here. And in the remainder of Kenesset HaGedolah the Beit Yosef wondered at Rama who wrote that washing mayim acharonim is like "hav lan," for it is inferior to "hav lan"; and see above siman 101 where I wrote in the name of Rashba that "hav lan" is inferior to mayim acharonim; and if so one can say Rama means that in this law everyone agrees they are equal. It also seems to my humble opinion to resolve that it is reasonable to him as Bach wrote—that even when they say "hav lan venevarekh" it is a mitzvah they return to eat and wash hands and bless haMotzi so they may invite zimun with him; and therefore he wrote mayim acharonim is like "hav lan," for even though he ruled in siman 179 it is forbidden to eat after mayim acharonim even if he washes and blesses, nevertheless here because of the mitzvah of zimun one may rely on poskim who permit; and see there what I brought that they are permitted to eat even if not for zimun's sake. And in any case the essence of this law Bach wrote—mitzvah to return and eat—and so wrote Magen Avraham—is not clear to me, for I did not find a root for this; and it implies in Talmud and poskim this is a completely separate eating since he must bless before it; and if they eat more and invite zimun it is as if they eat anew and invite; and so implies Derishah—and see above siman 193 s.k. 10.
