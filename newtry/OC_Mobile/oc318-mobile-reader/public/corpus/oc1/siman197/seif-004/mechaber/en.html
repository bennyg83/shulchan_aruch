Companions who ate together—some ate to satiety and some only a kazayit—if all know how to bless, it is a mitzvah that one who ate to satiety bless and discharge the others; if not all know, one who ate a kazayit can discharge even those who ate to satiety.And some say he is not obligated from the Torah if he did not drink and is thirsty to drink (Mordechai chapter "three who ate," Beit Yosef in the name of Kol Bo and Shibolei) Leket). And it is good to be careful l'chatchila: if some drank and some did not, that one who drank blesses (his own words).
