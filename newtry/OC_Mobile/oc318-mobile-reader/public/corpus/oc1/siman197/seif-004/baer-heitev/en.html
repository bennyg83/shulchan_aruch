Satiety. Beit Yosef writes that if each person recites Bircas HaMazon quietly for himself and they join only for zimun, one who ate merely an olive-sized portion may lead even when they all know how to recite the blessings. See Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 277, and Yad Aharon. One who ate excessively, meaning that he did not need the additional food, may discharge others in Bircas HaMazon provided that his throat derived benefit. Shiltei Giborim; see Knesses HaGedolah.
Who drank. If some ate to satiety but did not drink, while others did not eat to satiety but did drink, it is preferable that those who ate to satiety lead. Magen Avraham; see there.
