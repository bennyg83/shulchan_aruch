Satiety. Beit Yosef wrote that if each blesses to himself in a whisper and only they invite zimun, one who ate a kazayit can bless even if all know how to bless. See Lechem Ketanot part 2 siman 477 and see Yad Aharon. One who eats coarse eating—meaning he did not need that eating—discharges others in birkat hamazon if his throat enjoyed it. Shulchan Gavoah; and see Kenesset HaGedolah.

Who drank. And if some ate to satiety and did not drink, and some did not eat to satiety and drank, it is better they bless over those who ate to satiety. Magen Avraham in the name of Peri Megadim.
