(1) And a third came—and wishes to eat and join them for zimun.
(2) They can eat from it—meaning they are not so satiated that if they brought them things that sustain the heart for dessert of the meal, such as fruits and greens and mushrooms and young pigeons and the like, they could eat—even a little of them.
(3) He joins with them—for we consider them as if they have not yet finished their meal, and this is when he fixed with them—meaning he ate with them at the table where they sit, as written in siman 193 seif 2—see there in Mishna Berurah. Ma'amar Mordechai wrote the same applies if one finished his meal at first and afterward two came to him and eat—he also joins with them for zimun if they would bring him something and he can eat from it—see there; he also wrote the same in all this for joining ten to mention the Name.
(4) And they must give etc.—not specifically, but he meant it is a mitzvah to give him, as he wrote above at the beginning of siman 193.
(5) He does not join with them—for since they said so they already diverted their minds from eating and drinking, and it is forbidden for them to eat again as explained above in siman 179 seif 1 per the first view—and therefore he cannot join with them, for they already departed from their first eating; and see Biur Halacha.
(6) And see above siman 179—for there the details of the laws of "hav lan venevarekh" and mayim acharonim were explained.
