The laws of joining for zimun of three or ten. And it contains 4 seifim:
Two who ate as one and finished, and a third came—wherever if they would bring him something they could still eat from it, he joins with them, and they are obligated to give him to eat so he may join with them; and this is when he came before they said "give us and let us bless"; but if they said "give us and let us bless" and afterward the third came,' he does not join with them.And washing mayim acharonim is like "give us and let us bless" (Mordechai chapter "three who) ate"). And see above siman 179.
