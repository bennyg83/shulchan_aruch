The laws of joining for a zimun of 3 or 10. It contains 4 seifim: If 2 people ate together and finished, and a third person arrived, whenever they could still eat something were it brought to them, he joins with them, and they are obligated to give him food so that he can join them. This applies only if he arrived before they said, 'Bring us so that we may bless.' But if they already said, 'Bring us so that we may bless,' and afterward the third person arrived, he does not join them. {Rama: Washing mayim acharonim has the same status as saying, 'Bring us so that we may bless.' [Mordechai, chapter 3, Sheloshah SheAchlu] See above, siman 179.}
