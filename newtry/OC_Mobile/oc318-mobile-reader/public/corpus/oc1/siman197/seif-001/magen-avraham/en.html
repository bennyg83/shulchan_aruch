And washing etc. And it requires study, for on the contrary mayim acharonim is preferable as written there; and one must say he came to teach us that if he wishes to bless and eat they are permitted even though they forbid doing so as written siman 179—nevertheless here because of the mitzvah of zimun they are permitted, for some hold they are always permitted to eat with a blessing as written there; and Bach wrote that here it is a mitzvah they wash hands and bless haMotzi and eat a little—and so is primary.
