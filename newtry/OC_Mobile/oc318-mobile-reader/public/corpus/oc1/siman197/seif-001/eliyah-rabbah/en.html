(1) [Levush] They are not satiated etc. Perishah greatly expanded on Levush's words that aside from his honor he was not precise—that the matter does not depend on his appetite, for certainly every person desires to eat something spiced that is good and pleasant even if he is very satiated; rather the matter depends specifically on whether he has permission, such as if they said "hav lan" etc.; and what Rif and Shulchan Aruch wrote "they can eat" means he has permission to eat—and this is clear; until here his words briefly. And I found in Semag page 110 who wrote: estimate their minds—if they did not eat so much that if they brought them delicacies and a beloved food they could still eat, he combines—until here his language. It implies explicitly like Levush's words, and so it implies in Ba'al Halachot Gedolot that certainly such is the explanation of Rif and Shulchan Aruch—and understand well.
