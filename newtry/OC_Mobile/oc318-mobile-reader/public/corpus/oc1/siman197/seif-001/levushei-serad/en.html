Magen Avraham s.k. 2—for Ran he combines; Ran's view in Mechaber there seif 1 that even if they said "hav venevarekh" they may eat without a blessing—if so they are still within "they can eat" and combine; and Shulchan Aruch ruled that here it is per one view there that if they wish to eat after saying "hav lan venevarekh" they must bless haMotzi—automatically this is not called "they can eat," for when he eats another meal he will eat.
