"As long as he did not sleep." It implies that if one was awake all night he need not bless in the morning—and there is difficulty with this. For the Tur holds that the matter depends on sleep—if he did not sleep he need not bless—but per what is practiced, not to bless when one slept during the day, therefore what one blesses in the morning is because the Sages established this blessing every day, like the other morning blessings. You will know, for Rabbeinu Tam holds that when one stands before dawn he need not bless, and when day breaks one must bless; therefore for us too, even if he did not sleep, when day breaks he must bless—for we did not intend to exempt him only for one day per the enactment of the Sages. And if he slept and awoke before dawn he must also bless, just as with the other morning blessings that one blesses before day; and see end of siman 239.
