Siman 12 — "Even if," etc. — as written in Menachos 43a, and as written "from morning," etc.; and therefore even for the next day one need not [repeat]. See Magen Avraham; and regarding what Magen Avraham found difficult in the view of Yesh Omrim in seif 11 — the Beit Yosef already wrote that it is not the main view, and the Agur himself wrote only to be lenient in blessings, not to be stringent.

"All the time," etc. — not like Rabbeinu Tam in Tosafos there, and as written in Menachos there: if so, "all the time," etc.
