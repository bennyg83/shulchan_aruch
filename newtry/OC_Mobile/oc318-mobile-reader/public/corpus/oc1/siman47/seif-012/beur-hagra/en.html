Seif 12: Even if, etc. As stated in Menachos 43a, “from the morning,” etc. According to this, even on the following day no new blessing would be required. See Magen Avraham. His objection based upon the opinion in seif 11 is not compelling, because Beit Yosef writes that this is not the principal view. Agur himself cited that position only as a leniency concerning blessings, not as a basis for stringency.
So long as, etc. This is unlike Rabbeinu Tam in Tosafos there, as stated in Menachos: “If so, at every moment,” etc.
