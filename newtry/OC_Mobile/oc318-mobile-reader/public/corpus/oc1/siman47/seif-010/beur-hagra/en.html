Seif 10: If he interrupted, etc. This follows the second explanation stated above.
The same applies to sleep and a bathhouse, etc. Hagahos Maimoniyos writes in the name of Maharam that one does not remove his attention from Torah study. Agur writes that even there one must be careful concerning the laws that apply and should mentally review those laws there. Both explanations require study. According to the first explanation, why should this differ from tefillin, where an interruption through entering a lavatory requires a new blessing, as stated in the fourth chapter of Sukkah and explained above in simanim 8 and 25? Agur's answer is also incorrect, as explained below in siman 85 in the gloss: even, etc. See what is written there. Performing an act is not Torah study. Agur and Shulchan Aruch in seif 4 write that one who merely thinks, etc., following their view. Otherwise it would be forbidden to relieve oneself before reciting the blessing, and the same would apply before every mitzvah or act. I have already written there that this is not correct. Whenever one does not intend Torah study, no blessing is required. See above at the end of siman 46.
