Siman 10 — "If he interrupted," etc. — per the second explanation as above.

The same applies to sleep and bathing, etc. — because one is not distracted from study. The Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of Maharam, and the Agur wrote that even there one must be careful about their laws and must meditate on their laws there; the two reasons require study. The first reason: from what was written regarding tefillin — that with an interruption one must return and bless, as written in chapter 4 of Sukkah and as written above in siman 8 and siman 25. Also the Agur's answer is not correct, as written below in siman 85 in the Rama's gloss, and even etc.; see what is written there. An act is not study, and the Agur and Shulchan Aruch in seif 4 wrote that one who meditates, etc. — and according to his view, otherwise it would be forbidden to turn away first, and so with every mitzvah and deed; but he already wrote there that this is not correct, and whoever does not intend study does not need a blessing. See above siman 46.
