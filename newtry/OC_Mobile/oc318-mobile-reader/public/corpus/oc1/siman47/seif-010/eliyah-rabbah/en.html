[10] "The order of blessings," etc., until the parshah of the daily offering; and Shelah likewise wrote on 124 and page 129, and they extended greatly not to interrupt between the blessings of netilas yadayim until the completion of the entire order of the morning blessing except "Who gives understanding to the rooster"; and one who interrupts, whether in study or in another matter — the blessing of netilas yadayim is in vain, so far. He also wrote: it is good to say also the parshah of the binding of Isaac, the parshah of the manna, the Ten Commandments, and other verses of mercy, supplication, and requests, and afterward he should study, so far; and see above siman 1, seif kuf. Mateh Moshe wrote: I found in an old book — before a person begins to study he should say at the outset eight times the eighth [letter] of "Open, O Lord, my lips" — "and it is my portion, O God, I have said," etc., until "Your statutes teach me." And he should say: "May it be Your will before You, Lord our God and God of our fathers, with the eight times that I have said verses before You, that You open my heart to Your Torah, and command Metatron, the minister of the inner [chamber], whose name is Zagzagael — for all hidden wisdom is entrusted in his hand — and to Raziel, master of Adam the first man, and to Yofiel, master of Shem, and to Tzadkiel, master of Abraham our father, and to Raphael, master of Jacob, and to Gabriel, master of Joseph, and to Maltiel, master of Elijah, that they teach me Your Torah; and let my heart be like the opening of the hall to meditate on Your Torah; and let the words of Your Torah be placed in my mouth like a set table and like a flowing spring; and let Your Torah be preserved in my heart that I not forget; and let me be like a lime pit that loses no drop; and let me study day and evening, as written, "This book of the Torah shall not depart from your mouth, and you shall meditate on it day and night," so that you keep all that is written in it — for then you will succeed in your ways and then you will be wise; and afterward he should say the blessing on the Torah, so far. And see below at the end of siman 110 regarding the prayer of one who enters the beit midrash and leaves — saying it for every teacher of ruling: morning — prayer of entry; evening — prayer of exit — even though he studies privately.
