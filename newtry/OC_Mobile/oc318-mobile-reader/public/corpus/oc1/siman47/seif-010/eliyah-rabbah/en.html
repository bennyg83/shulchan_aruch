Matthew wrote Moshe I found in an old book before a person begins to study he will say In the beginning, eight times the chapter of Tamnia Afia and it is the parts of God, I said, etc. until HaKich learned. And he said, May it be your will, O God, our God and the God of our fathers, eight times that I have said verses before you, that you open my heart to your Torah, and command the Metatron, the Minister of the Interior, whose name is Zagzagal, according to which all the Genizi of wisdom are written in his hand, and Raziel, the rabbi of the first Adam, and Yufiel, the rabbi of Shem, and Zedekiel, the rabbi of Abraham our father, and Raphael, the rabbi of Jacob, and Gabriel, the rabbi of Yosef, and to Maltiel, the rabbi of Eliyahu, may they teach me your Torah and my heart may be pleased As the opening of a hall for meditating on your Torah, and let the words of your Torah be placed in my mouth like a Shulchan Aruch and a flowing spring, and let your Torah be preserved in my heart that I will not forget, and I will be like a pit of lime that does not lose a drop, and I will blacken and mix, as it is written, the Torah book will not be consumed from your mouth and you will recite it day and night, so that you may keep all that is written in it, for then you will succeed in your ways and then you will be wise, and then he will say the blessing of the Torah, so far.
