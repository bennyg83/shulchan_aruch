(25) And this is the custom. Lechem Chamudos writes, “In my opinion, one who recites the blessing should be blessed,” and reports that his teacher, Mahari, would recite it. Peri Chadash agrees, Eliyah Rabbah cites many Rishonim and Acharonim who agree, and Gra likewise rules this way. Chayei Adam also records this as the halachah. Although Peri Megadim implies that one may rely upon the custom not to recite a blessing, it appears clear that one who relies upon all the authorities mentioned and recites it has not lost.
