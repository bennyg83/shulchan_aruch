(25) And so they practiced — and Beit Lechem Yehuda wrote that it appears to me that one who blesses should answer Amen; and so my teacher Mahariv practiced to bless; and the Pri Chadash agreed, citing many early and later authorities; and likewise the Gra; and Chayei Adam ruled thus as halacha [and although in the Peri Megadim it implies that one may rely on this custom not to bless, in any case it appears simple that one who relies on all the poskim we mentioned and blesses does not lose]:
