There. “For according to Rabbeinu Tam.” Meaning, Rabbeinu Tam disagrees with seif 13, which says that one who rises before daylight to study recites Birchas HaTorah. Rabbeinu Tam holds that even nighttime sleep is not an interruption. Nevertheless, when daylight arrives one recites the blessing. This shows that the arrival of a new day itself creates a new obligation. Likewise, according to our view that nighttime sleep is an interruption, we may still hold that the new day independently creates a new obligation.
