There: for according to Rabbeinu Tam. Meaning: he disagrees with what is written in seif 13, that one who rises before daybreak to study blesses the blessing on the Torah—for he holds that even if he slept at night it is not an interruption, and nevertheless when day breaks he blesses—behold the day causes the blessing; and likewise for us, where sleep at night is an interruption, it is reasonable for us too that the day causes the blessing:
