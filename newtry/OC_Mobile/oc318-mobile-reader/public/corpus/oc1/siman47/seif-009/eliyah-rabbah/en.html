(6) This is of no consequence, etc. Bach rules that he must recite the blessing again unless he immediately said Bircas Kohanim, the passage of the daily offering, or Eizehu Mekoman. The law of one called to the Torah before reciting the blessing over Torah will be explained below in siman 139, seif 2.
(7) Bircas Kohanim, etc. Lechem Chamudos writes that this applies even when one studies before daylight. Bircas Kohanim is not said because it substitutes for the priestly blessing, which occurs only by day. Rather, the Sages wished to arrange three verses in the manner of one reading from the Torah, and selected these because they contain blessing. This is unlike Maharshal, who followed the poskim and would continue studying afterward.
