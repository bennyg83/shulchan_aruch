[9] And so they practiced, etc. Lechem Chamudos wrote there, and it seems to me that the one who blesses—blessing will come upon him; and so practiced my teacher Mahari Ginzburg, up to here. And so I found written in the margin of Shulchan Aruch—this is his language. And Maharshal in Chochmas Shlomo on Berachos chapter 1 brought the view of Maharai in his writings to rule like the Rosh that established sleep is an interruption even by day, up to here; and so ruled Shibulei HaLeket and Rabbeinu Yerucham and Sefer Zichron to the Gaon R' Yehuda HaKohen. And even though in responsa of the Ra'ah, siman 6, he did not rule thus, his words and proof are not clear—see there, for I shortened.
