Some say that if, in fact, Ahavah Rabbah is not the main blessing for birchas haTorah, then it exempts only if one studies immediately without interruption; but birchas haTorah exempts for the entire day even if one did not study after the morning prayer, only until midday. (Mordechai, end of first chapter of Berachos; Hagahos Rambam; and such is the view of Tosafos, Rabbeinu Yonah, Rabbeinu Yeruham, and the Rosh; and the Tur may be explained accordingly.)
