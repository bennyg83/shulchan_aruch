(Seif katan 9) Since, etc. The wording implies, etc. Taz writes that one who has a fixed daily Torah-study period and spends the rest of the day in business need not recite a new blessing if, after finishing his fixed period and going to work, no business opportunity arises and he returns to study. The obligation to study rests upon him at all times. There is no lapse of attention. His business burdens merely exempt him temporarily. Once no business arises, the original obligation again applies, and the first blessing continues to exempt him. See Taz's extended discussion. Magen Avraham may agree in this case. Magen Avraham discusses a person who has no regular study practice and once decides to begin learning. He had no prior intention that his blessing should exempt that study, and therefore he must recite a blessing. Taz may likewise agree in that case.
