And there is — see Taz, and see what Magen Avraham wrote in letter 6, that the Levush holds in siman 47 seif 8, as Pri Chadash wrote in the name of the Ra'avad and Rashba, that Eliyah Rabbah exempts only what was repeated in place, not what one learns afterward — and therefore he omitted "and one should be uncertain," for there is no practical difference in this. Now I have seen that this is not so, for in seif 10 it is explained there the opposite of what he wrote: "all the more so if he began to study and interrupted and returns to study" — in such a case, even with Ahavah Rabbah, if he began and stopped, when he returns he must bless; even though one could say that this is the difference between Beir Heitev — even if he had no intent at all and changed his mind outright he must bless — and Eliyah Rabbah, specifically when his intent is to return to study it is not an interruption. And the wording in siman 47 seif 8 — nevertheless he should not have omitted "and one should be uncertain," since there is much practical difference, as we said, and see what Magen Avraham wrote in letter 6 on this; there is no need to elaborate, and see what I wrote in letter 8 on this.
