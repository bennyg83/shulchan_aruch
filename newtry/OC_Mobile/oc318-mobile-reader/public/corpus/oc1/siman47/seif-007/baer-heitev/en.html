Immediately. If one studies immediately, Ahavah Rabbah exempts all Torah study for the entire day. This requirement applies specifically to Ahavah Rabbah, because its primary institution was not as a blessing over Torah but as a blessing of Krias Shema. The blessings Asher Bachar Banu and LaAsok BaTorah were instituted specifically as blessings over Torah and exempt the entire day even without immediate study. Rabbeinu Yonah. See Taz.
