(Note 7) "There is no," etc.—and there are those who disagree regarding all blessings; for certainly regarding Ahavah Rabbah, which requires learning immediately—not as Maggid Mishneh wrote, as the poskim wrote that Ahavah Rabbah is different, for they were primarily enacted for Kriat Shema and not for the blessings of the Torah; therefore it requires that one learn immediately, so that it appear he stands also for exemption from words of Torah.

There is no distraction of mind—for if he does not learn immediately, it is as if he is one who diverts his mind from study; and therefore the blessing he blessed would not have exempted the study afterward, for he reconsidered—which is the reason regarding other mitzvos that one may not interrupt—for this means there is no distraction of mind, for there is a mitzvah to meditate day and night, unlike other mitzvos.
