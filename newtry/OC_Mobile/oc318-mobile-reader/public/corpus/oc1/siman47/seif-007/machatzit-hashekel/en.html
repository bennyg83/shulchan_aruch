(Seif katan 7) There is no, etc. This cannot be derived from Ahavah Rabbah, which requires immediate study. The poskim explain that Ahavah Rabbah is different because it was principally instituted for Krias Shema and not as Birchas HaTorah. Therefore, one must study immediately so that it is evident that the blessing also exempts Torah study.
There is no lapse of attention. If one does not study immediately, one might have thought that he removed his attention from study and that the blessing would therefore not exempt later learning, because he had reconsidered. This is incorrect. A person never fully removes his attention from Torah, because he is commanded to contemplate it day and night. This differs from other mitzvos.
