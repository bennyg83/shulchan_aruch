Taz, seif katan 6. Asher Bachar Banu is the principal blessing and exempts Torah study even if one does not study at all immediately after reciting it. Its effect continues until the next morning. This is the basis of our custom not to study immediately after Birchas HaTorah and not immediately after Ahavah Rabbah. Taz then asks why this differs from sukkah, where one must recite a blessing for every meal. Here too, one might have required a blessing each time he studies, and all the more so immediate study after the blessing. This is the sequence of his argument.
