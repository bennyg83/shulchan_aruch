(ז) There: one who ponders words of Torah need not bless. Menachem Aharon, klal 5, siman 34, wrote: through analysis in a book, this is not mere thought, for it is impossible that he not utter something from his mouth through his analysis; and if he utters with his lips, he certainly must bless beforehand, end quote.
