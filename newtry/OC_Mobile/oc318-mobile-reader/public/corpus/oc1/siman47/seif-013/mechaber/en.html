One who rises before daylight to study recites the blessing over Torah and need not recite it again when he goes to the synagogue. One who rises before daylight recites the entire order of morning blessings, except for HaNosen LaSechvi Binah and the passage of the daily offering. See above, siman 1, seif 6. He should wait to recite those until daylight. Rama: Initially he should wash his hands before reciting the blessing in order to study. If he has no water, however, he may study and recite the blessing without washing, just as with the other blessings recited before washing, as explained above in siman 46. Agur.
