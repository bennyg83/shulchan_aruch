Siman 13 — "One who rises early," etc. — the Gemara there: "one who rises early to repeat," etc.

"And one need not," etc. — meaning, the one who reads the Torah aloud: as they enacted there, a blessing after it and a blessing for the maftir — but that applies only in a congregation.

"One who rises early," etc. — as written in the Gemara, "when one awakens," etc. — but because of hands that are not clean, as above.

"And if," etc. — they did not enact hand-washing except for Kerias Shema and prayer, as written above in siman 4; therefore one does not recite "Who has sanctified us" for Torah, as written in siman 7. But lechatchilah one should wash one's hands because of readiness, as written in seif 2 and siman 4, seif 18.

As above, etc. — in seif 1 there he wrote all the blessings, and afterward, when he washes his hands. The Rosh.
