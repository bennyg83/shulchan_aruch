Seif 13: One who rises early, etc. The Gemara there states: “If he rose early to study,” etc.
And he need not, etc. Meaning, this does not resemble one who reads from the Torah publicly, for whom they instituted a blessing afterward, or the maftir, whose blessing applies only in a public reading.
One who rises early, etc. As the Gemara states: “When he awakens,” etc. The concern is only that his hands are not clean, as explained above.
And if, etc. The Sages instituted washing the hands only for Krias Shema and prayer, as explained above at the end of siman 14. Therefore, one does not recite Al Netilas Yadayim merely for Torah study, as explained above at the beginning of siman 7. Initially, however, he should wash his hands because of the requirement of preparation, as explained there in seif 2 and in siman 4, seif 18.
As explained above, etc. In seif 1 there, Rosh writes all the blessings first and afterward discusses washing the hands.
