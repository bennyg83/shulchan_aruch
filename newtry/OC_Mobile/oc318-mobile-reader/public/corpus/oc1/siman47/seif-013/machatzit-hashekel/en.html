(Seif katan 13) Except, etc. It appears to me that this is specifically, etc. See siman 46. According to Beit Yosef there, even after daylight one does not recite HaNosen LaSechvi unless he heard a rooster. Although Rama disagrees and holds that one nevertheless recites it for the world's regular benefit, when daylight has not yet arrived another authority holds that even one who heard a rooster should not recite it. Therefore, when he neither heard the rooster nor reached daylight, both reasons for concern are present, and he should not recite the blessing.
