(Note 13) "Except," etc.—and it seems to me that specifically, etc., siman 46, etc.—that is, for according to Rabbi Yisrael there—even if day broke he does not bless unless he heard the rooster; and this is Rama disagrees there that nevertheless one blesses "who gives good to the world" nevertheless when day has not yet broken—for there is an authority who says even if he heard a rooster he should not bless; and therefore when he did not hear there are two factors aggravating—not hearing, and also day has not broken—he is not fit to bless.
