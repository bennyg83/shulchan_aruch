Understanding — Ba'er Heitev; and see Magen Avraham, who alluded to the words of the Zohar — meaning that it is stated there that when a man read his blessing, etc. And in Shulchan Tzedeck he wrote that for us, even if he did not hear, he blesses; and in the Zohar the meaning is that through the man they would know that it was midnight — see there. And see Machatzit HaShekel in the name of Pardes on Rashi, manuscript, and so the Ari wrote, and so they practiced to bless the blessing "who gives understanding to the rooster" at the watch — see there.
