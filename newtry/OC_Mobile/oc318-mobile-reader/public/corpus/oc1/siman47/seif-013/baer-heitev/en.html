Understanding. And in the writings and in the Zohar it states that one may say immediately after midnight: who gives understanding to the rooster. And Magen Avraham wrote, and it seems to me: specifically if he heard the crowing of a rooster; and nevertheless it is good to be careful l'chatchila. And Olas Tamid wrote that b'dieved he fulfilled his obligation and need not return and bless by day.
