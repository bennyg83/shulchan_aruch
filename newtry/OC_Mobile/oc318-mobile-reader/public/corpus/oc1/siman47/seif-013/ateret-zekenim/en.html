One who rises early, etc. Rosh writes at the end of the first chapter of Berachos that he should recite the blessing over Torah. Mordechai there, however, writes that Maharam held that he need not recite a blessing, because the blessing over Torah from the previous day exempts him until the following morning. Beit Yosef writes likewise. Tosafos in the name of Rabbeinu Tam writes that this does not appear correct. Maharam Mintz likewise rules in responsum 123, and Agur writes that all the poskim disagree with Rabbeinu Tam. This is the universal custom. When he later goes to the synagogue, he need not recite the blessing again. The chazzan who leads the congregation may nevertheless repeat it in order to discharge the obligation of the public.
