(14) [Seif 7] The blessing Ahavat Olam exempts the blessing on the Torah if he studied immediately, etc.; and then it exempts study for the entire day. Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and the Rosh; and so Magen Avraham note 6. And Peri Megadim wrote in Eshel Avraham letter 6 that so is the view of Maran. Raziel letter 6; Shitilei Zetim letter 8. However, there are those who say that even if he studied immediately after it, it exempts only that study and not the whole day—and this is the view of Rashba in the name of Raavad to texts that read "repeated on the spot," and Beit Yosef brought it—see there. And so the view of Peri Chadash on this seif; and so from the words of Levush, seif 8; and see Peri Megadim there. However, according to what was written above letter 2 in the words of the poskim who hold the blessing on the Torah is rabbinic and we follow doubt leniently—here too, one who forgot to bless the blessing on the Torah and blessed Ahavat Olam and studied immediately, we say per the poskim who hold that the blessing Ahavat Olam exempts study for that entire day if he studied immediately after it, and he need not bless again the blessing on the Torah, even if he stopped studying and returned to study.
