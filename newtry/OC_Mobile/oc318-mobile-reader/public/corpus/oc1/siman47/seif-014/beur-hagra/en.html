Seif 14: Women, etc. See Magen Avraham in the name of Agur. Their reasoning is rejected on several grounds. The verse explicitly says, “You shall teach them to your sons,” and not to your daughters. How, then, can women say, “Who commanded us” and “Who gave us”? Rather, the principal basis is what Tosafos and the other poskim write: women recite blessings over all positive time-bound commandments, as I wrote above in siman 17, seif 2. Although we rule that teaching a woman Torah is as though teaching her frivolity, that applies specifically to the Oral Torah, as explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 246, seif 6.
