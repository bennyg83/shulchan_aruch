Siman 14 — "Women," etc. — see Magen Avraham in the name of Agur; their words are rejected from several angles. The verse cries out: "And you shall teach your sons" — not your daughters — so how can one say "Who commanded us" and "Who gave us"? The main point follows what Tosafos and Shmuel wrote: women bless on every time-bound positive mitzvah, as I wrote above in siman 17, seif 2. And although it is established that "as if one teaches her" applies only to Talmud study specifically in the Oral Torah, as written in Yoreh Deah siman 246, seif 6.
