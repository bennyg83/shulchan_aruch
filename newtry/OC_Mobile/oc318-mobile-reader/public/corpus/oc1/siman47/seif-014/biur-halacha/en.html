"Women," etc. — the reason is that they are obligated to learn the laws that apply to them, and also to recite the parshah of sacrifices as they are obligated in prayer; therefore the blessing applies to this. Beit Yosef, Magen Avraham. And therefore the reason [women] can discharge men in the blessing on the Torah — and Peri Megadim wrote this explicitly; and Gra in Biur [HaGra] disagrees on this reason, see there. Rather, the reason of blessings is that although they are exempt from Torah, nevertheless they may bless and say "Who commanded us," for it is no worse than any time-bound positive mitzvah, for it is established that they may bless on them, as above in siman 17, seif 2, see there; and therefore they cannot discharge men. And a minor who reached the age of education certainly cannot discharge an adult in the blessing on the Torah, according to what many poskim hold — that the blessing on the Torah is from the Torah; so too Peri Megadim.
