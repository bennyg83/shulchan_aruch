Women, etc. The reason is that women are obligated to study the laws that apply to them. They are also obligated to recite the passage of the offerings, just as they are obligated in prayer. Birchas HaTorah therefore applies to these obligations. Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham. According to this reasoning, a woman can discharge a man in Birchas HaTorah, and Peri Megadim writes this explicitly. Biur HaGra rejects this reason. He explains instead that although women are exempt from Torah study, they may still recite a blessing and say “Who commanded us,” just as they may recite blessings over positive time-bound commandments from which they are exempt, as explained above in siman 17, seif 2. According to Gra's reason, women cannot discharge men. A minor who has reached the age of education certainly cannot discharge an adult in Birchas HaTorah according to the many poskim who hold that Birchas HaTorah is D'Oraisa. Peri Megadim writes likewise.
