Women bless, etc. — for although they are not obligated in study, and moreover anyone who teaches his daughter Torah is as if he taught her frivolity — meaning oral Torah but not written Torah; and further, they are obligated to say Parashat HaTamid, which is in place of the sacrifice; and all the more so according to Semag, who holds that women are obligated to learn laws that apply to them (Beit Yosef).
