Women recite the blessing, etc. Although women are not obligated in general Torah study, and teaching one's daughter Torah is compared to teaching frivolity, that statement concerns Oral Torah, not Written Torah. They are also obligated to recite the passage of the daily offering, which substitutes for the offering. All the more so according to Semag, who holds that women are obligated to study the laws that apply to them. Beit Yosef.
