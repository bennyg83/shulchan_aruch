14) [Se'if 7] The berachah of Ahavas Olam exempts Birchos HaTorah if one studies immediately, etc. It then exempts all study for the entire day. Beis Yosef in the names of Tosafos and Rosh; Magen Avraham 6. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 6, writes that this is Maran's view. Shulchan Aruch HaRav 6; Shesilei Zeisim 8. Some say, however, that even if one studies immediately afterward, it exempts only that particular session and not the entire day's study. This is Rashba's view in the name of Raavad, according to texts that read 'if he studied immediately,' as cited by Beis Yosef; see there. Pri Chadash agrees in his comments to this se'if, and this is also implied by Levush 8. See Pri Megadim there. Nevertheless, according to what was stated above in paragraph 2; that the poskim who consider Birchos HaTorah rabbinic rule leniently in a case of doubt; the same applies here. If one forgot to recite Birchos HaTorah, recited Ahavas Olam, and then studied immediately, we follow the poskim who hold that Ahavas Olam exempts the entire day's study when followed immediately by study. He need not recite Birchos HaTorah later, even if he interrupted his study and subsequently resumed. See below, paragraph 17.
