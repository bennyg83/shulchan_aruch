Women, etc. Women are obligated to learn the laws that apply to them, as explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 246, seif 6. They are also obligated to recite the passage of the offerings just as they are obligated in prayer. The blessing over Torah therefore applies to these obligations. Beit Yosef in the name of Agur. See what I wrote in siman 177, seif 3.
