Our children. Rambam writes in a letter that a convert may also recite this wording. Magen Avraham and Olas Tamid. The text should read, “And those who study Your Torah for its own sake.” Bach and Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah. One should intend to pray that his children become Torah scholars. The same intention should be present when saying, “That we not labor in vain and not give birth for futility.”
