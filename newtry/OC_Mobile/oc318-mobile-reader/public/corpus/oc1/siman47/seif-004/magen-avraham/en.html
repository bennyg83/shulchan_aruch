For it is one blessing—for if it were a blessing by itself it would open with "Blessed"; and even though it is adjacent to its companion, nevertheless, since the blessings are short, it is appropriate to open with "Blessed," just as with the havdalah blessings, which open with "Blessed" even though they are adjacent, since they are short. And there are those who hold that havdalah blessings are different, for they are not adjacent—for if he wishes he may say each one by itself, over the candle and over the spices.
