With a vav. It is one blessing. If VeHaarev were a separate blessing, it would begin with Baruch. Although it is adjacent to the preceding blessing, because both are short blessings it should still begin with Baruch, like the blessings of Havdalah, which each begin with Baruch although they are adjacent. The opposing view holds that Havdalah is different because those blessings are not inherently adjacent. If one wishes, he may recite each separately, such as the blessing over fire or spices.
