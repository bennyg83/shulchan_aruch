10) [Se'if 5] The formula of Birchos HaTorah is: 'Who sanctified us with His mitzvos and commanded us concerning words of Torah.' The Ashkenazic formula is 'to engage in words of Torah.' Tur; Bach; and Magen Avraham 3. Beis Yosef writes in the name of Abudraham that it is more correct to say 'concerning words of Torah,' because this includes both the occupation of study and the performance of mitzvos; see there. Magen Avraham cites him, and Pri Chadash 5 rules likewise. See Pri Chadash's comments on Bach and Pri Megadim's resolution in Eshel Avraham 3. Pri Chadash further writes that the principal text is 'concerning words of Torah,' and reports that Mahariyah recited this version. This refers to the Arizal, as Yafeh LaLev 4 writes, for Maharchu testifies in Shaar HaKavanos, Nusach HaTefillah, page 50d: 'I also saw him answer Amen between the two berachos; between “Who sanctified us concerning words of Torah” and “Please make pleasant, Hashem our God”; apparently regarding them as two berachos. This accords with Baal HaTurim.' It appears the text should read 'unlike Baal HaTurim'; see below, paragraph 13. 'For “Please make pleasant, Hashem our God” is a second, independent berachah and not the conclusion of the first berachah.' Taz 4 writes, 'Mahari Luria said that one should answer Amen after the berachah “to engage in words of Torah,”' etc. He does not mean that the Arizal recited the formula 'to engage in words of Torah,' which would directly contradict Maharchu's testimony. He means only that the Arizal answered Amen after the berachah 'concerning words of Torah'; Taz used the Ashkenazic formula, as Yafeh LaLev explains there. Minchas Aharon, page 22c, likewise writes that the Arizal's formula was 'concerning words of Torah'; see there. Divrei Menachem, Hagahos HaTur 3, cites him. Our custom likewise follows Maran, the Arizal, and those who agree with them, to say 'concerning words of Torah.' See below, paragraph 13.
11) There: Please make pleasant, etc. One should say: 'We, our descendants, our descendants' descendants, and the descendants of Your people, the House of Yisrael,' etc. This follows our Sages' statement that anyone who sees his son and grandson engaging in Torah is assured that Torah will never cease from his descendants, as it is written, 'They shall not depart from your mouth,' etc. Bach; Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur 1; Solel Boneh at the end of paragraph 2; and the later authorities. The text should conclude: 'and students of Your Torah for its own sake. Blessed are You, Hashem, Who teaches,' etc. This is the version in Rif. Bach; Olas Tamid 2; Perishah 7; Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah 2; Eliyah Rabbah 3; and the later authorities.
12) There: Who chose us. A convert also recites this formula and makes no change whatsoever, whether praying alone or with the congregation. Responsa of Rambam 9; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur; Olas Tamid 1; Magen Avraham 3; Pri Chadash 5; Eliyah Rabbah 11; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 4; Shalmei Tzibbur 56a; Kesher Gudal 5:26; and Siddur Beis Yaakov 23.
