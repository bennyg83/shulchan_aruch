(5) There is room for doubt, etc. Levush is certain that Krias Shema and prayer do not count as Torah study for this purpose. Perishah and Bach likewise imply this. Bircas Avraham, part 4, and Sefer Adam VeChavah, netiv 2, also support this ruling, and this is the practical halachah. It is obvious that if he studies immediately after prayer, then even if he later interrupts, he may resume learning without another blessing, as explained in seif 10.
