And vav. For it is one blessing — for if it were a separate blessing it would open with Baruch; and although it is adjacent to its companion, nevertheless since they are short blessings it should need to open with Baruch, as with the havdalah blessing which opens with Baruch although they are adjacent, since they are short; and some hold havdalah blessings are not adjacent because one may say each separately. See Magen Avraham.
