With a vav. The reason is that VeHaarev Na is one blessing together with LaAsok BeDivrei Torah. If it were a separate blessing, it would begin with Baruch. Although it is adjacent to another blessing, short blessings generally begin with Baruch even when adjacent, like the blessings of Havdalah. The opposing view holds that Havdalah is different because its blessings are not inherently adjacent. Each may be recited separately. See Magen Avraham.
