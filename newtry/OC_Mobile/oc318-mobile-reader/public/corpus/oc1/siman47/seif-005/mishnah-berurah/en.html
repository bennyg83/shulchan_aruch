(8) Over words of Torah. The custom in our countries is to say LaAsok BeDivrei Torah. A convert may recite the blessing Asher Bachar Banu. Later authorities.
(9) VeHaarev Na. Some are precise to add “and the offspring of our offspring,” because when a person, his son, and his grandson are Torah scholars, Torah no longer departs from his descendants, as the verse states, “They shall not depart from your mouth,” etc. This addition is unnecessary, because the word “our offspring” already includes grandchildren. Magen Avraham.
(10) And Who chose. The prayer of a father and mother should always be fluent in their mouths, asking that their children become Torah scholars, righteous people, and possessors of good character. One should concentrate especially in Ahavah Rabbah and Birchas HaTorah when saying, “May we and our offspring,” and likewise in U'Va LeTzion when saying, “So that we not labor in vain and not give birth for futility.”
