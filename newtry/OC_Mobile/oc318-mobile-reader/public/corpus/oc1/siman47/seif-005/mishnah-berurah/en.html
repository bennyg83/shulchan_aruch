(8) On divrei Torah — and the custom in our communities is to say "to engage in divrei Torah." A convert may say the blessing "Who chose us" [later authorities].

(9) Veha'arev na — and some are precise to add "and the offspring of our offspring," for any Torah scholar whose son and grandson are Torah scholars — the Torah never ceases from his seed, as it says, "They shall not depart from your mouth" etc.; and one need not do so, for in the general "and our offspring" grandchildren are also included [Magen Avraham].

(10) Ve'asher bachar — and the prayer of father and mother should always be fixed in their mouths to pray for their children that they be Torah learners, righteous, and people of good character; and they should intend greatly in Ahavah Rabbah and in the Blessing of the Torah when saying "and may we and our offspring be," and likewise when saying in Uva leTzion "so that we not toil in vain nor bear children for panic."
