Seif 6 — "He says," etc. — Rashi there [says] that it is all one blessing, and Tosafos there likewise wrote, in Berachos 46a, s.v. kol, etc., and in the Oral Law 104b, s.v. kol, etc., and in Kesubos 8a, s.v. shehakol, etc.; and like the morning blessings mentioned above, and the Havdalah blessing, and the wedding blessing, which open with Baruch even though they are adjacent — because before them is a short blessing — the same applies here; nevertheless it is one blessing.

And there are those who say, etc. — there is no proof from the morning blessings that each is by itself, as written there, "when one dresses," etc.; and Tosafos there in Berachos likewise wrote. But this blessing, although it is a blessing by itself, does not open with Baruch because it is adjacent to its companion. And what Rabbeinu Tam wrote — that short blessings open with Baruch even when adjacent — is not so, for the Yerushalmi asks: behold Havdalah! And it answers: Rabbi [Yehuda HaNasi] would scatter them. So too Tosafos asked in Pesachim 104b, s.v. chutz, and the wedding blessing — Rashi already explained the reason there; see there.

But, etc. — see Taz.
