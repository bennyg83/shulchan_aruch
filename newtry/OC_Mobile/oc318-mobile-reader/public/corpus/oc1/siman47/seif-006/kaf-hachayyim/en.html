13) [Se'if 6] One says 'Vehaarev' with a vav. The gloss adds: Some say 'Haarev' without a vav, etc. Those who say 'Vehaarev' with a vav reason that it forms one berachah together with 'Who sanctified us,' and one therefore does not answer Amen after 'concerning words of Torah.' This is the view of Tosafos, Rosh, and Tur. Those who say 'Haarev' without a vav consider it an independent berachah and require answering Amen after 'concerning words of Torah.' This is the view of Rambam and Rashba in the names of Baal HaMaor and Abudraham, as Tur and Beis Yosef explain; see there. Beis Yosef writes: In practice, it appears that one should say 'Vehaarev' with a vav. According to those who consider it one berachah, omitting the vav creates an interruption in the middle of the berachah; according to those who consider it an independent berachah, saying it with a vav causes no problem. Maran revealed his view only regarding saying 'Vehaarev' with a vav, but did not reveal his view concerning answering Amen after 'concerning words of Torah.' Maharchu, however, testifies in Shaar HaKavanos that the Arizal answered Amen between the two berachos (between 'Who sanctified us concerning words of Torah' and 'Please make pleasant') as stated above in paragraph 10. This implies that he said 'Vehaarev' with a vav, in accordance with Maran, while also answering Amen after 'concerning words of Torah,' because he considered them two berachos, in accordance with Rambam and Rashba. Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaBerachos, chapter 1, writes likewise, as do Matzas Shimurim; Nagid U'Metzaveh, page 11a; Mishnas Chassidim, Maseches Chatzos 3:11; Taz 4; and Magen Avraham 1, who state that the Arizal customarily answered Amen between them; see there. Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur, also rules this way and writes that Pri Chadash's assertion that Mahariyah said it without a vav is incorrect. See also Chida, Kesher Gudal 5:28; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 5; Shalmei Tzibbur 8c and 56a; Siddur Beis Yaakov 25; Chesed LaAlafim 3; Kaf HaChaim by Rav Chaim Palagi 9:12; Shaarei Teshuvah 6; and Ben Ish Chai, Vayeishev 12.
