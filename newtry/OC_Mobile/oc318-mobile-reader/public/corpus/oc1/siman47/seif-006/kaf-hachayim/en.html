(ו) [Seif 4] One who ponders words of Torah need not bless, for thought is not like speech—Beit Yosef; Taz note 3; Magen Avraham note 2; Raziel letter 2; Chayei Adam klal 9 letter 10 (and see there in Nishmas Adam letter 4 what he wrote on the words of the Gra; and further see Peri Megadim in Mishbetzos Zahav letter 2). And according to this, those who study with deep analysis from a book should be careful to utter some words of Torah with the mouth after the blessing, Taz there—and this is if he does not say the portion of the priestly blessing immediately after the blessing on the Torah, for one should be concerned lest he interrupt with mundane speech before words of Torah come from his mouth; and one should be stringent as Maran wrote in seif 9 that the correct practice is not to interrupt between them.
