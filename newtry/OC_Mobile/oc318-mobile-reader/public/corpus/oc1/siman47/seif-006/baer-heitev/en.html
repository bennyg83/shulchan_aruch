With a vav. In this way one satisfies all opinions. If one says Haarev Na without a vav, it appears to be a separate blessing and creates an interruption according to the view that it is one blessing. The Arizal held that it is a separate blessing and would answer amen after LaAsok BeDivrei Torah. Nevertheless, he would say VeHaarev with a vav. Sefer Nagid U'Metzaveh. Yad Aharon likewise rules unlike Peri Chadash. See Lechem Chamudos, letter 83, who writes that whether to answer amen after LaAsok BeDivrei Torah is uncertain. See Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah and Responsa Mateh Yosef, part 2, siman 60.
