Ba'er Heitev — and in Shulchan Tzedeck he wrote the reason of the Ari, that when he heard from others he would answer Amen, because there is severity from both sides — either in canceling the response of Amen or in answering Amen in vain; and therefore, to decide on one side, his holy and broad mind decided on the side that they are two blessings and one answers Amen — unlike when he said birkat haTorah by himself, when he did not wish to enter himself into this dispute to decide the disagreement, since it is possible to fulfill both, and he would say veHa'arev na, etc., which is a word that suits both; and so it is possible that the Rama's view is also so — see there — and this is correct.
