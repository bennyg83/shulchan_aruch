In the Abhat and in the Shaz, Tama wrote to the late Hari that when he heard from others, he would answer Amen according to the fact that there is material from both sides, either by canceling the prayer of Amen or by asking Amen to invalidate it, and according to this, to decide on one side, his holy and broad opinion decided on one side, and the Master answered, when he said the blessing of the Torah to himself, he did not want to involve himself in this challenge to decide on a flugta, since it is possible to get out by both of them, and he would say And please prove that it is a falsehood for Tarvayahu and as much as possible.
