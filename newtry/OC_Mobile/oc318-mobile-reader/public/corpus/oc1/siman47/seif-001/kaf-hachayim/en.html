(1) [Seif 1] One must be very careful with the blessing on the Torah. As stated in Nedarim 81a: why are Torah scholars not commonly found among their children? Ravina said: because they do not bless on the Torah first, etc.; and the commentator there explained that they do not bless when they rise early for Torah study. And Torah scholars, because they are careful to engage in Torah and are accustomed to it, are not careful to bless when they open, and the blessing is not fulfilled: "that we and our offspring be learners of Torah," end quote. And see Tur and Beit Yosef. And thus Levush: a person must be very, very careful with it to show that Torah is important in his eyes and that he enjoys it as he blesses on all his enjoyments. And if he does not bless on it, he does not merit a Torah scholar son—this is his punishment measure for measure: since he does not bless on it, it shows he does not read it for its sake and does not rejoice with it and does not enjoy it, but in his eyes it was like a mere craft; therefore he does not merit the chain descending to one who engages in it for its sake, as it is written "they shall not depart from your mouth," etc., end quote. And the main care is that he must understand the meaning of the words and say them calmly, word by word, so that no word or letter be missing, as written in siman 5, letter 2. And one must also say it with strong desire and put in his heart that he blesses on the Torah which is our life and length of days in this world and the World to Come:
