The blessing of the Torah—see Taz. And the word Ma'od Ab'ach—that Torah scholars are accustomed in Torah and stand before morning and forget in Birchot HaTorah; therefore one must be very careful, since its punishment is great—that the Land was lost and one does not merit being a Torah scholar. A practical difference—"they shall not depart from your mouth and your offspring"—that is, for its sake. And also "and our offspring," etc. And from the explanation of the Bach, of blessed memory, I understood that which they said—"the Land of the deer" is written: what a deer—its hide does not hold its flesh, etc.—for Eretz Yisrael now is a day's walk in length and width six days, as Rama wrote in Prophets; and this is because truly Eretz Yisrael is small—only when there is an indwelling of holiness it expands, as Rama wrote (Psalms 79) "as a city joined together," and proof—for tribes went up there and stood densely and prostrated with space, etc. Therefore when they engaged for its sake and Torah—the names of the Holy One, blessed be He—and there was indwelling of the Shechinah in Eretz Yisrael, it expanded; and now that not for its sake but for bodily matters, the spiritual departed and automatically only the physical small measure remained—and this is the Land truly lost.
