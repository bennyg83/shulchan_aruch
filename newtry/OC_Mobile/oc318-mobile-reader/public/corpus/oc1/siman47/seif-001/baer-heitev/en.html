One must be careful. Whether the blessing over Torah is D'Oraisa or D'Rabbanan is discussed in Penei Moshe, part 1, siman 1. Peri Chadash, citing the poskim, holds that it is D'Oraisa. A practical difference is that if one is uncertain whether he recited it, he must repeat all the blessings, even though on a Torah level only one blessing is required. Yad Aharon. See Eliyah Rabbah concerning the prayer to be said before the blessing over Torah.
