To be careful. If birchas haTorah is d'oraisa or d'rabbanan — see in Sefer Penei Moshe part 1 siman 1, and Peri Chadash in the name of the poskim that it is d'oraisa; and from this emerges that if he was uncertain whether he blessed or not, he repeats and blesses all the blessings, even though d'oraisa there is only one blessing. Yad Aharon. (And see in Eliyah Rabbah the prayer one should say before birchas haTorah.)
