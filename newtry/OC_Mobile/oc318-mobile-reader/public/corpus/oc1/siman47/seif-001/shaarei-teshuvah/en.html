To be careful — Ba'er Heitev. And see Sha'agat Aryeh siman 24, where he proved that birkat haTorah is from the Torah; and there in siman 25 he wrote that nevertheless, if one is uncertain whether he said birkat haTorah, he should not bless except "who chose us," which is the choicest of the blessings — and this is not as Pri Chadash and Yad Aharon wrote; but Machatzit HaShekel holds that sitting and standing are preferable, and in doubt one does not return and bless, and he brought proof from the Rambam's wording, and so wrote Mateh Yehuda — see there; and see Birkei Yosef, that even for those who hold that in doubt one returns and blesses, nevertheless a woman — even though she also blesses birkat haTorah — if she is uncertain, she does not return to bless a second time — see there.
