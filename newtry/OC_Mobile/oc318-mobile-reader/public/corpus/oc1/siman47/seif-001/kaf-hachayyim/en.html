1) [Se'if 1] One must be exceedingly careful with Birchos HaTorah. Nedarim 81a asks: Why is it uncommon for talmidei chachamim to have sons who are talmidei chachamim? Ravina said: Because they do not first recite a berachah over the Torah, etc. The commentator there explains that they do not recite a berachah when they rise early to study Torah. Because talmidei chachamim are diligent in Torah study and accustomed to it, they are not careful to recite the berachah when they begin, and the blessing 'May we and our descendants be students of Torah' is not fulfilled. See Tur and Beis Yosef. Levush writes: A person must be exceedingly careful with this berachah, thereby demonstrating that Torah is precious in his eyes and that he derives pleasure from it, just as he recites a berachah over all his pleasures. If he does not recite a berachah over Torah, he does not merit a son who is a talmid chacham. This punishment is measure for measure: since he does not bless over it, he demonstrates that he does not study it for its own sake, does not rejoice in it, and derives no pleasure from it, but regards it as an ordinary trade. He therefore does not merit the continuing chain promised to one who studies it for its own sake, as it is written, 'They shall not depart from your mouth,' etc. The principal care required is to understand the meaning of the words and recite the berachah slowly, word by word, omitting no word or letter, as stated above in siman 5:2. One must also say it with intense desire and take to heart that he is blessing over the Torah, which is our life and the length of our days in this world and in the World to Come.
