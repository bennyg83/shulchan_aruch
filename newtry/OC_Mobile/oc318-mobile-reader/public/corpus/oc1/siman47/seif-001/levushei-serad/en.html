Taz note 2—to escape a blessing in vain. It is difficult: behold, the blessing on the Torah exempts all he will learn the entire day, even if he did not learn immediately after the blessing; if so, what concern of blessing in vain is here? And even if we say it speaks of one who writes all day and does not learn with his mouth—still he says the portion of the daily offering and the like, and as Taz wrote in note 6 that this is exempted by the blessing on the Torah. And perhaps it speaks here of one who forgot to say the blessing on the Torah until after prayer, and also did not repeat on the spot and was not exempted by Ahavat Olam, and afterwards came to write and blesses the blessing on the Torah—then there is properly concern of blessing in vain:
