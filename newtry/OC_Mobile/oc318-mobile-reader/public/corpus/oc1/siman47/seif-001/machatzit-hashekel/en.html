Bach writes, etc. The correct text is “strengthen us,” and likewise the Targum to Iyov 21:27.
Bach further writes that one should say, “those who study Your Torah for its own sake.” Eliyah Rabbah and Ateres Zekeinim write likewise.
(Seif katan 1) One who writes, etc. This requires study, because writing is at least thought. For this reason Taz writes: “In practice, every person writing words of Torah should certainly articulate several words aloud, in order to avoid a possible blessing in vain.” See the later responsa concerning whether writing is equivalent to speech; Responsa Shav Yaakov, siman 49; Sema, siman 73, seif 6; and Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 154.
Agudah concludes, etc. It appears to me that this is the explanation, etc. This also supports what I wrote: a scribe who merely copies, etc., need not recite the blessing, as Taz writes.
