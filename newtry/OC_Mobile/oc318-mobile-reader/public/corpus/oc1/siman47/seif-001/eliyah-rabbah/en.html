[1] "Very," etc. — for we find they were punished for not blessing on the Torah first, etc. — meaning, at the beginning of their engagement in Torah each day; so wrote the Prisha. And Lechem Chamudos, page 8, explained the word "first" — that they would bless, but they were not careful not to study at all before blessing; but when it came to hand they studied, and afterward when they remembered they would bless the study afterward; and even so for this the land was lost, so far. And I saw in Knesset HaGedolah an explanation on "students of sages who do not show honor to the Torah and do not ascend first to read in the Torah."
