(4) The writer — holds that writing is preferable to mental reflection, and the reason: some say because he performs an action, and some say because a writer's way is to pronounce words with his mouth while writing. All this is for one writing books for himself as his method of study and who understands what he writes; but a copyist scribe who does not seek understanding need not bless, for this is not study — and how much more if he writes some verse in a permissive letter for casual speech, he need not bless since he does not intend to study. For practical application, the later authorities agreed not to rely on the Mechaber's view to bless on writing alone in all cases, since nevertheless it is only mental reflection on words of Torah; rather, any writer of divrei Torah should pronounce some words with his mouth to avoid a blessing in vain if he does not say the verses of Birkas Kohanim or the beraita of "these are the things..." after the blessing, as is customary.
