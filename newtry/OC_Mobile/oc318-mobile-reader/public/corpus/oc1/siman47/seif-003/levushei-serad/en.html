Note 3: for those who study, etc. It is difficult: behold he says "May He bless you"; and one can resolve as written in the preceding letter, that the Taz is in doubt that perhaps even though he says "May He bless you," nevertheless it is a blessing in vain if he does not study at all. It is still difficult: behold he says in his prayer the portion of offerings; and if so, his blessing is not in vain.
