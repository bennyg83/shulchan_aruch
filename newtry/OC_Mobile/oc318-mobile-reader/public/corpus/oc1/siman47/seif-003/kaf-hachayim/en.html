(ג) [Seif 2] One must bless whether for Scripture, Mishnah, etc.—for all are called Torah, Levush; and so per the Zohar and Kabbalah, and it is simple. And according to what the Kabbalists wrote that these four aspects—Scripture, Mishnah, Gemara, Kabbalah—are in the four aspects of the worlds ABY"A; and see Nefesh HaChaim, Hanhagot HaAdam, folio 37, amud bet. And it is known that through the blessing one draws encompassing light, and through performance of the mitzvah inner light, as explained in Etz Chaim shaar 40, derush 2, and in Shaar HaKavanos derush 11 and 12 on counting the Omer, and in many places. And it is known that all the clarifications refined and intellects drawn through the blessing and performance of the mitzvah belong to the aspect of that blessing and that mitzvah; if so, since Scripture, Mishnah, Gemara, and Kabbalah are in four aspects, and he blesses in the morning on all he will study that day—and it is possible he will study all of them—therefore he must intend at the time of blessing on all of them, so that the clarifications and drawing of intellects belonging to all of them be refined; examine this carefully:
