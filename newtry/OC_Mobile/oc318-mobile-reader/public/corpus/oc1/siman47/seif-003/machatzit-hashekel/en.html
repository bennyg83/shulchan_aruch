(Note 3) "Regarding words of Torah," etc.—regarding engaging in mitzvos. And Beit Yosef concluded, and these are his words: he does not come now to perform them; and when he performs them he will bless on the mitzvah with the blessing unique to it—end of his words. And the Bach wrote, and these are his words: because the essence of the promise that He, may He be blessed, promised us was only regarding engaging in Torah—that is the labor and toil, as is written "and if you walk in My statutes," that you should labor in Torah she-be'al peh in order to observe and fulfill, "and I will give your rains," etc.—therefore they enacted to bless to engage in words of Torah; and this is the essence—end of his words. And so too the Taz.
