(Seif katan 3) Concerning words of Torah, etc. It refers to engaging in the mitzvos. Beit Yosef concludes: “He is not presently about to perform them. When he later performs them, he will recite the particular blessing instituted for that mitzvah.” Bach writes that Hashem's principal promise was specifically concerning toil and exertion in Torah, as the verse says, “If you walk in My statutes,” meaning that you should labor in the Oral Torah in order to observe and perform it, followed by, “I shall give your rains,” etc. Therefore, the formula LaAsok BeDivrei Torah, “to engage in words of Torah,” was instituted. This is the principal explanation. Taz writes likewise.
