And there is room for doubt. See Mishnah Berurah. If this factor is combined with another, namely that he slept a fixed daytime sleep, then according to everyone one may rely upon the combination and recite the blessing, because even without this nearly all poskim require a new blessing for each factor independently. I later found in the novellae of Rabbi Akiva Eiger an even broader ruling: even the Mechaber, who says there is room for doubt, means only that initially one should be careful. After the fact, he too agrees that if one did not study immediately he must recite Birchas HaTorah.
