"And there is room to question" — see Mishnah Berurah: if to this is added also that he slept by day a substantial sleep, all agree one may rely on this and bless, for otherwise almost all poskim require each one to return and bless. Afterward I found in Chiddushei Re'ah more than this — even the Mechaber, who says there is room to question, meant only general caution lechatchilah; but bedieved he too admits that if he did not study immediately he must bless the blessing on the Torah.
