(8) Since he intends, etc. Magen Avraham writes that one who does not ordinarily study and later changes his mind and begins learning must recite the blessing. Rosh writes likewise in responsa, general rule 4, siman 2. Taz, however, understands Shulchan Aruch to mean that no new blessing is required, because we presume that if an opportunity to study arises he intends to do so. Since the matter concerns a doubt regarding blessings, one may be lenient.
