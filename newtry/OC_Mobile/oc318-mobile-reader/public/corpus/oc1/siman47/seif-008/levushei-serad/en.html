Magen Avraham, seif katan 6. “Study” does not mean, as some explain, that Ahavah Rabbah exempts only the study performed immediately afterward. Shulchan Aruch cannot hold that view, because in the following seif he writes that there is room to doubt whether Krias Shema itself counts as study. According to those commentators, there would be no basis for that doubt. This is straightforward.
