(17) There is room to question — for one could say that Shema is only like prayer words, since one does not say it for the sake of study; and see Biur HaGra, who proved plainly that Shema does not help for this. Later I also found in Eliyah Rabbah that he cites thus from many poskim and ruled thus for halacha. And it appears that if he recited Shema after the time of Shema, which is like reading Torah, it is possible that according to all he need not bless again even if he did not study immediately afterward.

(18) One must be careful — lest he forget to study immediately after prayer.
