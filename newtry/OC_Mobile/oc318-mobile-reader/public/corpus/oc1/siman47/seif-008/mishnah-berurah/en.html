(17) There is room for doubt. Perhaps Krias Shema has the status of prayer rather than words of Torah, since he does not recite it for the purpose of study. Biur HaGra proves straightforwardly that Krias Shema does not suffice for this purpose. I later found that Eliyah Rabbah cites many poskim who rule likewise, and he decides the halachah accordingly. It appears that if one recited Krias Shema after its proper time, when it has the status of reading Torah, then according to everyone he need not repeat Birchas HaTorah even if he did not study immediately afterward.
(18) One should be careful. He may otherwise forget to study immediately after prayer.
