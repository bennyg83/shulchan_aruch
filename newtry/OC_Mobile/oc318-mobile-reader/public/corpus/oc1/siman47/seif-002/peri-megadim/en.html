The writer—see Taz; see Levush and see what they wrote: the writer does an action, and Levush wrote contemplation—one does not bless on thought. One could say according to what is written in siman 62: if one is compelled he may contemplate and it is released—for contemplation is like speech. And according to what we wrote in our novellae, Berachot 6b in Tosafot s.v. veRav Chasda—that this is not clear whether contemplation is like speech or not like speech; and as we need to say there in siman 62. And here one does not bless on contemplation, just as one does not bless on nullification of chametz—even though it is a positive commandment; nevertheless one who did not search, one does not bless on matters of the heart (see Taz letter 31). And therefore when writing, for he does an action, one blesses—and see siman 85. And behold, in a place where it is permitted to contemplate, such as the middle room of a bathhouse or a place that is not entirely clean (see siman 84)—requires investigation whether it is permitted to write words of Torah there; one could say writing is exactly like studying, or one could say—and understand. And what is written regarding the copyist scribe—so too Magen Avraham, letter 1.
