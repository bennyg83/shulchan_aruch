Heretic — they are burned on seif 16. Beit Yosef's text in the Tur: "written by a heretic — interred"; this and "some say" here; but Bach's text: burn. For practical law, Shulchan Aruch also admits like the first view anonymously; but Bach concludes for practice: whether written by a heretic or found with a gentile — inter, do not burn, and not valid from the baraita Gittin 45 "sectarian and gentile" — they are one; see Menachot 42b Rashi s.v. "Sadducee gentile" — therefore his text: Sadducee gentile, meaning sectarian gentile expert in writing — one category; therefore Rashi holds this baraita is not decisive, else why say they are not burned?
