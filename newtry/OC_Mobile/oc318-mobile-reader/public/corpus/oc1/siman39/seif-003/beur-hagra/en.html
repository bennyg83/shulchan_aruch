Seif 3, a convert, etc. See Magen Avraham. The Rashash raised a difficulty from what is stated there, and that which was taught, that one may read, etc., was established as referring to a convert, etc., who does it for its required purpose, while the three baraisos that disagree concerning a non-Jew speak of an actual non-Jew. In Mishnas Binyamin he answered that the teaching that one may buy books, etc., speaks of an actual non-Jew, and the incident of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel concerns a convert; see there.
