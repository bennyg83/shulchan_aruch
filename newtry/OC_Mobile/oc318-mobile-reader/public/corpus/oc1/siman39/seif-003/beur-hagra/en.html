Seif 3 — convert, etc. Magen Avraham — Rashba challenged what is written, and the baraita "they read," etc. — we establish it regarding a convert, etc., who acted leshmah; and these three baraitot that disagree regarding a gentile are discussed; and explain in Mishna Berurah on what is written "one may purchase books," etc. — it discusses an actual gentile; and the incident of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel regarding a convert, and he brings proof from it that we do not expound "and you shall bind… and you shall write"; and such is Tosafos on "and the baraita," etc. — meaning: and the baraita "they read" is the view of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who said one may purchase, etc.:
