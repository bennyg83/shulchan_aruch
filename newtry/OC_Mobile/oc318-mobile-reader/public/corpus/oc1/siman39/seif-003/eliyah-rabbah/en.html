[3] More than its value, etc. Nevertheless, one must make an effort and speak persuasively with him, for perhaps he will agree to accept the object’s value, or only slightly more. If he afterward persists in his demand, it is left in his possession. Taz writes similarly there. Beit Yosef writes that one should not tell the non-Jew to sell it for far less than its value, lest he become angry and cast it away where it will be lost. Rashi explains that the small additional amount is half a dinar. Even tefillin that require genizah must be purchased for this amount. See also siman 80.
