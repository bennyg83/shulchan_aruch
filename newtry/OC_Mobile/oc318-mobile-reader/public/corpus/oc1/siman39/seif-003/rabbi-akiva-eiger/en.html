Magen Avraham s.k. 1 But from doubt we do not rely on presumption that he brought two hairs biblically. In my humble opinion, if he wrote them himself he may wear them — for biblically it is a doubt: perhaps he was adult at writing; if not, perhaps still a minor exempt from tefillin.
