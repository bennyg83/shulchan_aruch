Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1: But because this is a Torah-law doubt, we do not rely upon the presumption that he developed two pubic hairs. In my humble opinion, if he himself wrote the tefillin, he may put them on. Regarding the Torah obligation there is a double doubt: perhaps he was already an adult when he wrote them; and even if he was then a minor, perhaps he is still a minor now and is exempt from tefillin. Regarding the rabbinic obligation of education, we rely upon the presumption that he developed two pubic hairs upon reaching thirteen years of age.
