(11) Out of fear — lest they kill him; and although truly he should have given his life for faith in God, nevertheless he did not leave the category of Israel thereby, since it was under coercion. See Magen Avraham, who raised that one should not be lenient even if in private he keeps Torah but is lazy about laying tefillin — for in any case he is not obligated in binding; and all the more if apostate for the entire Torah, unless out of fear of death he would keep Torah even in private. See Biur HaGra, who agrees with Magen Avraham. Magen Avraham also wrote in the name of Darkei Moshe: although in a Torah scroll a mamzer and ger toshav [who accepted the seven Noahide laws] are invalid to write, as Yoreh De'ah siman 281 — nevertheless in tefillin and mezuzot one need not be concerned; and all acharonim agreed a ger toshav is invalid for sacred writing, for he is not obligated in binding.
