Fear. Fear that they will kill him. Although in truth he should have given up his life for faith in Hashem, nevertheless he did not leave the category of Israel through this, since it was under compulsion. See Magen Avraham, who concludes that one should not be lenient where he keeps Torah privately but is lazy in putting on tefillin, and certainly not if he is an apostate to the entire Torah, unless fear of being killed prevents him from keeping Torah even privately. The Gra agrees. Magen Avraham also wrote regarding a mamzer and resident alien, and the later authorities agree that a resident alien is invalid for writing sacred scrolls, tefillin, and mezuzos because he is not included in binding.
