Because of fear. This is the opinion of the Rosh. Rif and Rambam omit it, for the Gemara implies that he is invalid because he is not among those who bind them, and fear does not prevent him from wearing them privately. The baraisa stating "one reads from it" follows the same Tanna as the account stating "one purchases from him." Other authorities disagree. See Tosafos, s.v. "But that which was taught," and the later-edition novellae of Rabbi Moshe, unlike Maharam Lublin. One should not be lenient.
