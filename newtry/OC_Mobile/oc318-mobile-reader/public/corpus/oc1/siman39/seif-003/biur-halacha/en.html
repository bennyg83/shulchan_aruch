Fit to write tefillin — all discusses a righteous convert. For ger toshav, Pri Megadim, Levushei Serad, Machatzit HaShekel [and many more] disqualify — not obligated in binding. Sha'agat Aryeh invented Darkei Moshe validates ger toshav who accepted all mitzvot except nevelah — then obligated in binding. In my humble opinion this too is wrong: he is not warned about binding — know: if woman and slave accepted tefillin mitzvot they would not be fit to write; if rejected because women are not warned to lay tefillin, siman 38 — all stringency lest they interrupt; our Gemara holds we do not warn them, Beit Yosef ibid.; slaves prove no warning, as Mishna Berurah citing Tosafos. If woman accepted tzitzit mitzvot, fit even per Rabbeinu Tam to make men's tzitzit — then Rashba's proof [Gittin 45b] from Rahav's wife's belt for righteous of the future world would be reversed. Besides, no half-conversion — see tractate Gerim; if convert circumcised but not immersed, Rosh Avodah Zarah — not worse than ger toshav who accepted all mitzvot — would be fit for sacred writing and slaughter per Rashi relying on valid slaughterer — he keeps slaughter mitzvot; Gemara Yevamot 46: since not immersed, gentile — not so implied:
