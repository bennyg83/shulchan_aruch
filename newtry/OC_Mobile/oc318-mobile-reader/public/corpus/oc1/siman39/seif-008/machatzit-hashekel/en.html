(Seif katan 8) That he wrote them, etc. Unlike a Torah scroll; see Yoreh Deah. Here he brought two opinions, one that they are burned and one that they are placed in genizah. The reason is that an apostate does not ordinarily put on tefillin and presumably wrote them to sell to a Jew, so some are not concerned that he wrote them for idolatry. But a Torah scroll, which an apostate also reads, may have been written for himself, and then certainly for idolatry, so everyone agrees it is burned. So wrote the Beit Yosef; see Bach.
