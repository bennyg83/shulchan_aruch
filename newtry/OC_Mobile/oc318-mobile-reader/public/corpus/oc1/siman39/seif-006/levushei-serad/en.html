(There.) 'The Ran writes that it is not, etc.' This is not an independent statement and does not refer to an apostate who refuses to put on tefillin. Rather, it refers to the author's words, which derive from the Talmud in Gittin, that an apostate regarding one matter is disqualified from writing tefillin. Concerning this, the Ran writes there that since he is not an apostate regarding the entire Torah, his disqualification is only rabbinic. The objection to this is that the case must concern one who acts in order to provoke, since one who sins to satisfy an appetite remains qualified. If so, the disqualification is by Torah law.
