Only periodically. See Taz. Whenever the leather has been damaged, the tefillin require inspection; see Magen Avraham, seif katan 15. Whenever inspection is required and no one is available to inspect or sew them, one should put them on without a blessing. The principle of maintaining an item in its established presumption does not apply in such a case, because a defect has arisen: they fell into water. This is obvious.
