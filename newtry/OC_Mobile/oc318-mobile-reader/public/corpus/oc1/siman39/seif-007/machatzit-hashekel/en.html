(Seif katan 7) Because of, etc. This is what it means, etc. In Gittin 45b three baraisos are set against one another: one teaches that a Torah scroll written by a non-Jew is burned, another that it is placed in genizah, and another that it may be read. The Gemara resolves them by distinguishing between an assumed intent for idolatry, lack of intent for its required purpose, and a convert who returned because of fear. The Rosh holds that such a convert is still included in binding, since he refrains only because of fear.
So it also appears from Tosafot, etc. Tosafot asked why the baraisa of genizah was not established according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who requires writing for its required purpose. Maharal of Lublin explained one way, but Magen Avraham was not satisfied with it. Therefore he explains that Tosafot meant that the baraisa permitting reading could have followed the first Tanna, who does not require writing for its required purpose and does not require one included in binding, whereas according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel it is invalid because a non-Jew does not write for the required purpose.
Know that according to this, for the Rosh a convert who returned because of fear is considered included in binding, and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel requires one included in binding. Therefore the proof of Magen Avraham in seif katan 2, that even according to the Rosh a Jew standing over a non-Jew does not help for writing, can be rejected. For according to the Rosh, the answerer preferred to establish it as a convert who returned because of fear, thereby resolving both the need for writing for its required purpose and the need for one included in binding.
