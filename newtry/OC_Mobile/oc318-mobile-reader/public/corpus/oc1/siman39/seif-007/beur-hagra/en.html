So that, etc. Rambam. See Rashi on the Mishnah, s.v. because of the enactment, etc., and as stated there, where they asked, etc. Presumably one reason applies to all of them. The Ran in Gittin wrote this in the name of some authorities; see Yoreh Deah, siman 252, seif 5.
