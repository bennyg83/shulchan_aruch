More, etc. But for a little more than their value, meaning up to half a dinar for tefillin and proportionally for a Torah scroll, one must buy them. Even though regarding a Torah scroll found in a non-Jew's possession some say it is placed in genizah, nevertheless one must buy it from them, even for a little more, in order to place it in genizah and prevent the non-Jews from disgracing it. If they are invalid in themselves, one must also buy them from the non-Jew to place them in genizah. But if they were written by an apikores and require burning, one need not buy them.

But when the non-Jew stands by his words, he leaves them in his hand. It is explained in the Gemara that one may not tell the non-Jew to give them too cheaply, lest the non-Jew become angry and treat them disgracefully.
