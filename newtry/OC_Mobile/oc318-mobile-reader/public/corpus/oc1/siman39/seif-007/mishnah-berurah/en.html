(17) More, etc. — but for a little more than their worth [up to half a dinar for tefillin, proportionally for a Torah scroll] one is obligated to buy from them; and although a Torah scroll found with a gentile — some say it is interred [Yoreh De'ah siman 281] — nevertheless one must buy even for a little more, and even to bury them, so gentiles will not disdain them. Plainly, likewise if they are invalid in themselves one must buy from gentiles to bury them; but tefillin written by a heretic require burning — one need not take from them:

(18) Much — and nevertheless the Israelite should not immediately leave him when he says "much," but must engage with him lest he become equal with him; when the gentile insists, he leaves them in his hand.
