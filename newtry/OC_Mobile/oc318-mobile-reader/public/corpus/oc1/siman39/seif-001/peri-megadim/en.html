All on seif 16. In Beit Yosef he sided that an informant and Israelite apostate — per Rashi and Rabbeinu Meir in Gittin 45 — are invalid; others say only rabbinically, as Ran writes in chapter HaSholeach; and Taz made a compromise: apostate for Shabbat or out of spite is Rabbeinu Meir's invalidity, for he does not believe in binding; informants Chazal made rabbinically invalid — specifically for tefillin and Torah scroll, Yoreh De'ah siman 281 s.g. 3; but for other prohibitions he is believed, as the Mechaber Yoreh De'ah siman 119 seif 10 and Shach there 23; and ibid. siman 2 Taz letter 13 — informant like apostate for appetite, see there. And "many will become wise" — Bach and Prisha: why did the Tur omit informant? All the more a gentile who is not lowered is invalid — all the more an informant who is lowered. He challenged: if so, why is informant mentioned in Gittin 45 as forbidden? See Derishah: when we distinguish spite and appetite in commandments between man and God, but for robbers and extortionists not, for he descends to his fellow's life for his appetite, see there. Behold great difficulty: if so, in interpersonal commandments for appetite too it is like spite and he is apostate for the entire Torah and his slaughter is invalid per Gittin 45 — we never heard this; and a robber is invalid for testimony, not for other matters, for he is called wicked, etc. I saw Choshen Mishpat 325 — see Prisha wrote similarly — requires much study:
