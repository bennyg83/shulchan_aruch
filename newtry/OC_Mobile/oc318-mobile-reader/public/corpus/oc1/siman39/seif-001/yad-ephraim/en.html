(In Shulchan Aruch seif 1) From Magen Avraham s.k. 3 it appears he did not write "for idolatry." So too plain from his words that apostate for idolatry is a sectarian; and Beit Yosef implies here: apostate for one sin out of spite, or apostate for the entire Torah without specification.
