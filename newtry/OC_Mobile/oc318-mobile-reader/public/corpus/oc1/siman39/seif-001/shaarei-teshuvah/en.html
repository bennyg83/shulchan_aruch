Be'er Heitev; and Bechor Shor wrote in Gittin 45 that tefillin written by a deaf-mute or imbecile are invalid, although Shulchan Aruch mentioned only a minor alone; see there he wrote to resolve Magen Avraham's difficulty on Beit Yosef from Yoreh De'ah siman 258. Noda B'Yehuda responsa 1 wrote: tefillin written by a youth age thirteen years and one day or slightly more without checking signs of maturity, and the tefillin are not before us — they are valid, for we rely on presumption since he reached the age of years he brought signs; but l'chatchila not. One should rebuke scribes who let youths write without checking signs of maturity, see there. Machatzit HaShekel in the name of Dvar Yosef siman 12: if a tumtum or androgynous wrote — invalid; if a tumtum was torn and found male — valid, see there. In these laws an adult standing over does not help, since the reason is: whoever is in binding is in writing:
