(Seif katan 1) Or a minor. It seems to me that since we derive it from a verse, etc., this differs from a D'Rabbanan matter such as prayer, where once he is thirteen years and one day old we rely on the presumption that he certainly brought two hairs, as below in siman 55, seif 5 in the gloss.
But in a matter that is according to the Torah, such as discharging others in Birkas HaMazon, we do not rely on the presumption; rather, one must know that he certainly brought two hairs, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 199, seif katan 6; see there.
After he is thirteen years old. Meaning that hairs brought before thirteen years do not help, for they are merely a mole-like growth.
Until he is eighteen years old: therefore there is a scribal error here. Eliyah Rabbah also wondered at this, for there are three measures: if he brought two hairs after thirteen he is an adult; if he reached twenty and did not bring two hairs but signs of a saris appeared, he is an adult; and if twenty years passed without two hairs and without signs of a saris, he remains a minor until thirty-six years pass. All this is explained in Choshen Mishpat, but we have not heard of a measure of eighteen years, except regarding one opinion in the Gemara and regarding appointment as a judge.
