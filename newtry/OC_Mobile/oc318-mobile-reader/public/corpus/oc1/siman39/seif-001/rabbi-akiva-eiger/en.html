Se'if 1: “Or a woman.” A tumtum is obligated to put on tefillin because, where a Torah-law doubt exists, Torah law itself requires stringency. Nevertheless, it appears to me that a tumtum is invalid to write tefillin, because perhaps the person is female and not obligated in the mitzvah. Mishbetzot Zahav above, siman 14, se'if katan 1, does not appear to hold this way. I later saw that the author himself writes here in Eshel Avraham as a straightforward matter that a tumtum is invalid to write them.
