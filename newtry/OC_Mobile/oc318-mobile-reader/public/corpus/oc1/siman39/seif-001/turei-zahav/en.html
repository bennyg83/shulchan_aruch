Whoever is not obligated in binding. Although an informant is obligated in binding, since he casts off the yoke of commandments Chazal made him like one not obligated in binding. In the Tur the word "informant" was omitted — a copyist's omission; and many wise men say he need not mention informant, for it is learned a fortiori from a gentile — this is incorrect, for why was it mentioned in the Gemara?
