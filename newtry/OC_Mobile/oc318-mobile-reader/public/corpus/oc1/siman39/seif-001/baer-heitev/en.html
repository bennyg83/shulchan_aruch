Or a minor. We require that he be truly an adult — meaning he brought two hairs after he is thirteen years old; but from doubt he is invalid to write until he is eighteen years old. Magen Avraham:

Or a Samaritan. So too a gentile is in any case invalid, for he does not write leshmah. Magen Avraham:

Apostate. An apostate regarding the entire Torah, or out of spite — even for one transgression; or an informer, even for gain — is invalid. Olat Shmuel, Magen Avraham:

In writing. One whose left hand was amputated — although he is not obligated in binding — is fit to write tefillin, for he is a man subject to obligation; only his mouth hurts him. Magen Avraham:
