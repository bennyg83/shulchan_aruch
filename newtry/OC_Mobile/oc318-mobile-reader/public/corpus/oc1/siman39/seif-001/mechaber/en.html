<b>Who are fit to write tefillin and to purchase from them. In this chapter are 10 seifim:</b><br>Tefillin that a slave,
or a woman, or a minor wrote — even if he reached the age of training (or a gentile), or a Samaritan,) or an apostate for idolatry, or an informant — are invalid, because it is written "and you shall bind them… and you shall write them": all who are not obligated in binding, or do not believe in it, are not fit for writing.
