[1] Minor, etc. Magen Avraham wrote: specifically after bringing two hairs past thirteen; from doubt invalid until eighteen, as Even HaEzer siman 141 s.k. 25 and Choshen Mishpat siman 35 — end quote. His words are astounding: nowhere eighteen is mentioned; some say Choshen Mishpat siman 7 — not law until eighteen only when he also brought two hairs; reason there: power to judge to save from robber — what is eighteen here, especially doubting two hairs — certainly invalid until checked or saris signs, as siman 55. Magen Avraham: left hand amputated, not in binding, may write — man subject to obligation. Maharam of Lublin responsum 65: woman sewing/covering tefillin — invalid; dragging glue between letters — valid b'dieved; likewise sewing Torah scroll — valid. Magen Avraham: Samaritan — read so; gentile invalid, not leshmah, siman 32 s.t. 9 — end quote. My teacher R. Samuel challenged: Tosafos Menachot 42 s.v.
