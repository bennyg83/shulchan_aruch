(Seif katan 9) They are valid, etc. It seems to me that with tefillin there is no concern like there in Yoreh Deah. The Shach gives one reason concerning a mamzer and a resident alien when writing verses that oppose them, but tefillin contain no verses that oppose them. He gives another reason, that a convert and mamzer are exempt from several mitzvos like a slave, woman, and minor. But I do not understand why, under either reason, a resident alien would be permitted to write tefillin, since he is not included in binding and is no better than a woman.
