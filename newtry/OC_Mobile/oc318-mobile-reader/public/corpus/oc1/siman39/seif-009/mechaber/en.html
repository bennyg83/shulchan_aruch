If he bought from one who is not an expert, he must check them. If he bought one hundred compartments from him, he checks three compartments, two of the head and one of the hand, or two of the hand and one of the head. If he found them valid, this man is established and all are valid, and the rest need no checking. If he bought them bundle by bundle, their presumption is that they were taken from many people; therefore he checks from each bundle two of the head and one of the hand, or two of the hand and one of the head. If the seller said they belonged to a great man, he is believed.
