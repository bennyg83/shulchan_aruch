If one purchased from one who is not an expert, he must check them. If he purchased a hundred parchment slips from him, he checks three: two of shel rosh and one of shel yad, or two of shel yad and one of shel rosh — if he found them valid, this man is presumed trustworthy and all are valid, and the rest need not be checked. If he purchased them bundle by bundle, they are presumed bought from many people; therefore from each bundle he checks two of shel rosh and one of shel yad, or two of shel yad and one of shel rosh. One who sells tefillin and says they belonged to a great man is believed, and they need not be checked.
