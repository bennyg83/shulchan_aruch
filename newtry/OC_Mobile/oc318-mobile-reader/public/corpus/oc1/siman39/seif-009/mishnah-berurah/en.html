(20) If he purchased — b'dieved, or when there is no expert in town:

(21) To check them — for missing and extra letters, and likewise the form of letters if halachically correct, as siman 36 s.a. 1; and we are not concerned lest they processed not leshman, for everyone is expert in this:

(22) And the rest — when he says he wrote them himself, or says he bought from one man — we presume that man wrote them all himself, since they are one bundle, as Prisha wrote:

(23) A great man — it seems likewise if he said he bought from an expert:

(24) Believed — for one witness is believed regarding prohibitions. Magen Avraham wrote we in any case require that he be known as presumed valid — see Yoreh De'ah siman 119 s.b. in hagahah; per what is explained there, if we saw him wear them himself he is believed in all respects, for one who transgresses himself is certainly not suspected.
