To check them. For deficient and plene spellings, and likewise the form of the letters, whether they are according to halacha, as above in siman 36, seif 1. There is no concern that they were processed not for their required purpose, for all are expert in this.
And the rest. This is when he says he wrote them himself, or says he bought them from one person, and we presume that person wrote all of them himself since they are in one bundle, as the Prishah wrote.
Great. It appears that the same applies if he said he bought them from an expert.
He is believed. One witness is believed in prohibitions. Magen Avraham wrote that we at least require that he be known as established in observance; see Yoreh Deah, siman 119, seif 2 in the gloss. From what is explained there, if we saw him wear them himself, he is believed in any case, for one is certainly not suspected to transgress himself; see Shach there.
