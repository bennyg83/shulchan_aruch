[5] Bundles, etc. Some say even two or three bundles — check shel yad or shel rosh in first, second, third bundle — if valid, all presumed (Levush). Shulchan Aruch omitted them; reason seems Gemara Eruvin 96. Levush well brought "some say" — Tosafos, Rosh, Tur, Rabbeinu Yeruham ruled so — Tosefta; variant Gemara text — Derishah agrees; this seems primary, great acharonim. "Likewise third" — seems same for a hundred — check each; Levush discusses three; Rosh end Hilchot Tefillin; first view checks all even hundred per each opinion.
