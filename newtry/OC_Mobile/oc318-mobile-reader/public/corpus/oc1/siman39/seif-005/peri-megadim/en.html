More. See Taz and Yoreh Deah 281, Taz, seif katan 2. The Jew is obligated to negotiate with him even if he demands a large sum, lest the seller ultimately agree to the Jew's price. See Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Rashi, who writes that "a little more" means half a dinar. Even when the writings require burial, one must purchase them from him so that he will not treat them contemptuously.
