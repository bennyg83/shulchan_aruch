(Seif katan 10) More than, etc. See Magen Avraham at the beginning of siman 649. The Taz in Yoreh Deah, siman 281, wrote that when the non-Jew wants much money the Jew may not immediately leave it with him and go away. Although the law is that if he wants much, one leaves it in his hand so as not to accustom them, nevertheless he must try to appease and negotiate with the non-Jew, perhaps he will give it for its value and a little more. If after all this the non-Jew insists on much more than its value, then he leaves it in his hand.
