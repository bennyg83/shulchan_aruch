(26) Forever — as long as their covering is intact they remain presumed valid by law, and we are not concerned a letter was erased or punctured. Nevertheless it is proper to check them because they spoil from sweat. If the bayit covering tore or they fell in water, they require immediate checking lest the writing was erased or spoiled. Whatever requires checking by law and he has no one to check or re-sew — he wears them without a blessing, for presumption does not apply in such a case. Chayei Adam wrote likewise if stored in a damp place — all according to circumstances:

(27) Occasionally — we are concerned lest they molded; therefore check them twice per shemittah cycle:

(28) He should wear them thus — regarding blessing, Chayei Adam implies he should bless; the reason seems because we have not seen clear defect on them, unlike if straps fell off or the hide spoiled. For practice requires study. One who finds tefillin cast in a genizah without retzuot, open — concern for hidden disqualification; but if found placed in their pouch in the usual manner, no concern:
