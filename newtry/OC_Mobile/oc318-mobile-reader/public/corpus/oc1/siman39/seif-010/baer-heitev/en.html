Forever. Nevertheless it is proper to check them, for they spoil from sweat — Knesset HaGedolah, Magen Avraham:

Checking. For we are concerned lest they have molded; and likewise if they fell into water they require checking. One who finds tefillin cast in a genizah without retzuot and open — one should be concerned they have a hidden disqualification; but if he found them placed in their pouch in the usual manner, there is nothing to fear.
