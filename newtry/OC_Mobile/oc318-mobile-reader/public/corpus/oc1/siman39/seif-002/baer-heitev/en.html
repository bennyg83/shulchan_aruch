Their making. Meaning he performs an act on the body of the tefillin — but to enslave them is valid. If he sewed or covered the tefillin they are invalid, for this is included in repairing their making; but if he dragged glue between letter and letter, or made another repair, it is valid b'dieved in tefillin, for they require kesidran. In a Torah scroll, if possible to drag the sides of the letters where they were touched and rewrite them, he should do so — for this is called l'chatchila. Maharam of Lublin siman 68. Magen Avraham:
