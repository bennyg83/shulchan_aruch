(Seif katan 2) A Kusi, etc. This should be the reading, for a non-Jew is invalid in any event, as written in siman 32, seif 9, where even a Jew standing over him does not help according to the Rambam. Eliyah Rabbah objected from Tosafot, but it appears that Magen Avraham did not have both 'non-Jew' and 'Kusi' in his text; he had only 'non-Jew' and therefore understood that it was a printer's error and should read 'Kusi.'
Even according to the Rosh, who is lenient regarding processing when a Jew stands over him, in writing he agrees that this does not help. The proof is that the Gemara did not establish the incident in Tzidon as an actual non-Jew with a Jew standing over him, but as a convert who returned because of fear.
