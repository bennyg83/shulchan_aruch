(9) In every — one whose left hand was amputated, although not obligated in binding, may write tefillin, for he is a man subject to obligation; only his mouth hurts him:

(10) Their making — specifically performing an act on the body of tefillin, such as covering batim and sewing them; all the more making the shin on the hide of the batim, or correcting a letter — invalid, and through correction it becomes valid, for that is actual writing. Processing parchment is not included — valid through a gentile, and through a woman, but for a gentile an Israelite must stand over him and see it is leshmah, as above siman 32 s.t. 9; likewise a minor. Blackening batim and retzuot — see above siman 33 where we clarified their laws. Dragging glue between letters — l'chatchila be careful not through them; b'dieved do not be strict, for dragging is not called writing; see above siman 32 s.k. 80. All this in tefillin that require kesidran; but in a Torah scroll, if possible to drag letter sides where touched and rewrite, he should — that is l'chatchila:
