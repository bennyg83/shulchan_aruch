Their making. This is specifically where he does an act in the body of the tefillin, such as covering the batim, sewing them, making the shin in the leather, or correcting a letter that is now invalid and becomes valid through his correction, for that is actual writing. But processing the parchment is not included in this, and is valid through a non-Jew when a Jew stands over him, and certainly through a woman. For scraping glue between letters, one should initially be careful not to have them do it, but after the fact one need not be stringent, for scraping is not called writing. All this is by tefillin, where order is required; by a Torah scroll, if the sides of touching letters can be scraped and rewritten, one should do so.
