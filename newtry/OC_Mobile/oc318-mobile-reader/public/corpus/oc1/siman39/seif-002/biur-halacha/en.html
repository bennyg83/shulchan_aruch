All disqualified, etc. — See Beit Yosef; see Taz: because making includes a shin, therefore invalid for all making. See Pri Megadim: making the shin is biblical, the rest rabbinic — Magen Avraham disputes and holds covering and sewing are like making the shin, not mere decree. Apparently per Pri Megadim's explanation of Taz, Taz could validate a minor who reached chinuch, rabbinically obligated in tefillin — wording "all disqualified" implies the cases above. Truly correct as Maharam of Lublin responsum siman 68: Beit Yosef's intent is not gzeirah on other repairs because of the shin, but since other repairs and the shin are all called making tefillin — one act — do not divide them. Still, a thirteen-year-old whose two hairs are unknown — do not be strict b'dieved for covering and sewing if the shin was made by an adult, for Mishna Berurah is lenient b'dieved even for writing:

In every repair of their making — See Mishna Berurah on covering batim and sewing; plainly likewise wrapping hair on parshiyot — me'akev b'dieved, Rambam ch. 3 Hilchot Tefillin 1:1, and narrowing batim and titura square — all included in repair of making; Maharam of Lublin responsum siman 68: other crafts besides hide repair are included in covering and sewing. Magen Avraham in Maharam's name: dragging glue between letters or other repair valid b'dieved refers to writing — e.g. large gap in a word looking like two words, continuing a letter (as in "to your fathers" continuing the bet) to appear one writing — permitted even in tefillin requiring kesidran, not new writing; permitted even through disqualified persons like dragging. "Or correcting a letter" — invalid intent, excluding tagin on shin ayin tzadi — without them valid b'dieved, siman 36; do not be strict b'dieved. Passing quill over a letter where the letter remains visible but guarded from erasure — not writing. Doubt: yod's alef, shin, ayin that do not touch — child recognizes them; missing left thorn in yod — does repair by disqualified persons help b'dieved? Like out-of-order we are lenient — not new writing since basic form remains — likewise here, not "whoever is not in binding is not in writing"? Or since the letter is now invalid, repair by disqualified persons cannot validate even b'dieved — unlike dragging that does not act on letter form? Do not lighten from Maharam's end: if touches were on letter bodies themselves — feet of hei and kuf, alef legs on roofs, alef face touching roof below, woman separated them — Torah scroll invalid, cannot fix except by dragging all disqualified work and rewriting — complete writing; he did not add the lesser case that before woman's repair the basic form remained invalid for additions — perhaps Maharam answered the questioner about woman's dragging whether valid, answered distinctions between draggings, therefore omitted that case — reasonable. Pri Megadim: knot of dalet and yod in shel yad tefillin — only leshmah, not through woman or minor, included in writing like shel rosh shin. Do not be strict on thirteen-year-old if adult stands over and commands leshmah even if two hairs unknown — many are lenient; Tosafos Shabbat 62a s.v. shin — no place for this; certainly no need for minor to be strict on knot made before thirteen — many hold at thirteen he is a man obligated rabbinically by Rava's presumption; when checked later and two hairs found, presume he brought them at the proper time and became adult retroactively, Choshen Mishpat siman 35. For Torah scroll sewing by women and other disqualified — many doubt; warn strongly unlike some scribes who let women sew at completion — b'dieved if easily possible, permit woman's stitches and re-sew with valid — knowing places, fixable — like full l'chatchila; otherwise Maharam of Lublin lenient b'dieved; Magen HaElef disagrees, requires permitting even b'dieved.
