(Magen Avraham s.k. 9) In tefillin and mezuzot one need not be concerned. It seems this discusses a ger toshav who accepted to keep all Torah commandments except nevelah; as Avodah Zarah 64 — others say "some ger toshav," etc.; therefore fit for tefillin and mezuzot, for he is obligated in binding — only in a Torah scroll the error is he does not write divine names leshmah when writing "you shall not bring a mamzer," etc.; likewise ger toshav does not write the name beside nevelah leshmah — and for tefillin and mezuzot this does not apply, see Yoreh De'ah siman 281:
