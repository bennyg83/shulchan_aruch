To perform a corrective act upon them. This means an act upon the body of the tefillin. Tanning is valid, as stated in siman 33:4. If he sewed or covered them, they are invalid, because forming the letter shin includes the sewing and covering. If he scraped away glue between letters or made another repair, they are valid after the fact; the same applies if he sewed a Torah scroll. It is preferable to scrape the sides of letters that touch and rewrite them, for this is considered their initial formation. This option applies to a Torah scroll; with tefillin it helps only after the fact because of the required order. Maharam Lublin, siman 68. See siman 32:35 and the end of siman 649, paragraph 1.
