Repair of their making. Meaning he performs an act on the body of the tefillin, but to enslave them is valid, as written siman 33 s.d. If he sewed or covered the tefillin they are invalid, for this is included in repairing their making — the shin is included in sewing and covering; but if he dragged glue between letter and letter, or made another repair, it is valid b'dieved; and likewise if he sewed a Torah scroll — nevertheless for clarity, if possible to drag the sides of letters where they were touched and rewrite them, he should, for this is called l'chatchila — specifically in a Torah scroll; but in tefillin it is called b'dieved, for they require kesidran [Maharam of Lublin siman 68].
