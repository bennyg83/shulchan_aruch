Magen Avraham s.k. 2 A gentile in any case. In my humble opinion one can say a practical difference: found with a gentile who says he wrote leshmah — we believe him with miggo he could say an Israelite wrote them, who would be believed as seif 6. Likewise a gentile who wrote them and converted, knowing he wrote leshmah — he may have been permitted to wear them; possibly believed for others too, for one witness is believed in prohibitions. Also when he has no other tefillin — he must wear them from doubt he wrote leshmah. See Even HaEzer siman 123. This needs the reason he is not obligated in binding and certainly invalid.
