Apikores. See Biur Halacha.
They are burned. Together with the Divine Names, as below in siman 334, seif 21; see there, for presumably when he writes, he writes for idolatry.
And some say they are placed in genizah. For an apikores does not usually put on tefillin, and he surely wrote them to sell to a Jew, so perhaps he did not write them for idolatry. The Mechaber rules like the first opinion, and therefore wrote it plainly; so too is the view of the Taz, Prishah, and Gra.
