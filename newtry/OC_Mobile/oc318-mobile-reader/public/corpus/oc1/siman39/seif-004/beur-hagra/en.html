And some say they are placed in genizah. As above, Rabbeinu Yerucham. He holds that they disagree only regarding a Torah scroll, for with tefillin presumably he does not write them for idolatry, since we do not find that they disagree with the baraisa of 'you shall bind' except by a Torah scroll. This is his view, and it is an individual opinion.
