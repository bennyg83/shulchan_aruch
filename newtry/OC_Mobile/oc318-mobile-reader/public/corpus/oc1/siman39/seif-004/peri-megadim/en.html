If they were found. See Taz and Magen Avraham, seif katan 9. This fact alone is insufficient, for if it were sufficient, why would writings found in the possession of an apikores be buried? Rather, the distinction is as Beis Yosef concludes: an apikores is ordinarily proficient in writing, as Rashi writes in Menachos 42b (cited above; see there) unlike a non-Jew, who does not know how to write them.
