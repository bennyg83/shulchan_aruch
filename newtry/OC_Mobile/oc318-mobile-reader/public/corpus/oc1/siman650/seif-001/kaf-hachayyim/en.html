1) [Seif 1] The required measure of a hadas and aravah is three tefachim. If their leaves extend above the wood, the wood itself must have this measure. Chiddushei HaRitva. Mishna Berurah, sec. 1:
2) Ibid. The spine of the lulav must be four tefachim. This is apart from the leaves that rise above the spine. Tur and Beit Yosef. Levush. Levushei Serad. Maamar Mordechai, sec. 1. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1. Chayei Adam, klal 149, sec. 16. Mishna Berurah, sec. 2:
