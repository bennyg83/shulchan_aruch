Se'if 2: they do not have, etc. Menachos 42a.
And there are, etc. This is what he wrote: the spine of the lulav must, etc. He did not say simply that the spine of the lulav is four tefachim. And likewise in the Mishnah it says "enough to shake," and does not say four tefachim; and so too in Niddah there.
