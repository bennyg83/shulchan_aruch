(1) [Seif 1] One does not decree a fast upon the congregation initially on Thursday, etc. — in Beit Yosef he brought a dispute between Ran and R' Yitzchaki, for per Ran not only for a rain fast did they say thus, but for any trouble that might come upon the congregation, and per R' Yitzchaki only for a rain fast they said, for then the market price rises, see above, and in Shulchan Aruch stam like the words of Ran. And nevertheless it is possible that if so Ran did not say except for ongoing trouble similar to rain, such as for blight and for grasshoppers and for locusts and the like where a person's life is not contingent on it moment by moment, but for trouble where human life is contingent on it immediately, such as if a great noise is heard again and again and there is fear lest, Heaven forbid, their home be their grave — Ran also agrees in such a case that there is no restraint to wait. And so I did in practice and relied also on what I saw in Magen Avraham that in a place where price-inflators are not common it may be permitted. May it be for good. And see below comment 5 and siman 576 seif 4.
