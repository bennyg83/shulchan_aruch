In Magen Avraham seif katan 4 — it is not called a day of feasting and joy, etc. — this requires further study, for in truth so wrote Rashi, but this requires further study on Purim — he should have interrupted; and it is possible that he means that even though Rosh Chodesh is called a day of moed from Torah, nevertheless it is not written in it feasting and joy, unlike Purim which is only d'rabbanan — fine that one does not interrupt, even though it is written in it feasting and joy, and see above siman 570 seif katan 9.
