(2) One does not interrupt, etc. And Bach that they practice to interrupt and defer the fast to the coming Shabbat, and see Magen Avraham siman 559 seif katan 11 who questioned in the Gemara Eruvin daf 41 that the people of Senaav fasted on the ninth of Av that fell on Shabbat and was deferred to the tenth of Av which was their Yom Tov — they fasted and did not complete [the fast] — behold even on Rosh Chodesh one completes, until here. It appears to my poor mind that here they had begun to fast, which is not so there from year to year, for all who fasted in one year on this day, for the next year it is before they began; and furthermore one can say that perhaps they did thus like R' Gamliel who does not complete on Rosh Chodesh, even though they did not act like him, for they completed on erev Shabbat, and in this manner it speaks in the Gemara there. And in the responsa printed at the end of Sefer Gevuras Nashim they ruled that if it did not go as they intended, even though they began, they interrupt from the side of law — see there.
