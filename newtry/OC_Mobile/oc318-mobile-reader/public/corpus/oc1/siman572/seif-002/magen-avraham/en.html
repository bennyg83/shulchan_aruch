On Rosh Chodesh. And in Worms they fast for the decree on Rosh Chodesh Sivan (Beis Yosef, siman 579, in the name of the Rokeach); and Rema miPano in a responsa wrote that we do not learn from them as a general rule; and see what I wrote in siman 570.
They fast and complete [the fast]. Even though Rosh Chodesh is called a moed, nevertheless it is not called a day of feasting and rejoicing (Rashi). It is written in Piskei Tosafos that nowadays a community has the status of an individual, and they interrupt [the fast] on Rosh Chodesh and complete it on another day (Beis Yosef); and it seems to me that so is the practice (Bach). See se'if 3.
