(2) There. One does not decree a fast upon the congregation initially on Thursday, etc. — this is when they proclaim in the synagogue; but if it is one day that they decree and the hour presses for it is a time of trouble, they do not delay until Monday but they precede prayer for the trouble to fast on Thursday, for the hour requires it for the matter, and everything per what the eyes of the judges sitting on the scale see. Rav Ovadia Yosef comment 1.
