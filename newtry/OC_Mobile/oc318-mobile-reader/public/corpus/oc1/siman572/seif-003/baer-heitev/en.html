Congregation. Once Maharil decreed not to eat meat every Monday until Rosh Hashana, and the 15th of Av fell on Monday and he did not wish to eat meat, but on erev Yom Kippur and at a seudas mitzvah it is permitted with meat and wine — see Magen Avraham, and it appears to me that since he decreed with his intent in mind, the matter depends [on that] — he did not intend to forbid on erev Yom Kippur nor at a seudas mitzvah; but an individual who accepted upon himself is not obligated to eat meat even on Shabbat and Yom Tov — see above.
