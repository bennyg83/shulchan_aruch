Congregation who asked, etc. Once Maharil decreed not to eat meat every Monday until Rosh Hashana, and the 15th of Av fell on Monday and he did not wish to eat meat, but on erev Yom Kippur it is permitted — see there — and on Thursday, also on [the occasion of] redemption of the firstborn he wrote that he permitted to eat at pidyon haben meat and wine, end of his words; and it appears to me that since he decreed with his intent in mind, the matter depends — he did not intend to forbid on the 15th of Av nor at a seudas mitzvah; but an individual who accepted upon himself, it applies to him that he is not obligated to eat meat even on Shabbat and Yom Tov as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 241 and above at the end of 562 seif.
