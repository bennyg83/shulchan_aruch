A community that asked, etc. Once the Maharil decreed not to eat meat on every Monday until Rosh Hashanah, and the fifteenth of Av fell on Monday, and he did not want to eat meat; but on erev Yom Kippur it is permitted; see there, Hilchos Erev Yom Kippur; and in Hilchos Pidyon HaBen he wrote that he permitted eating meat and wine at a pidyon haben; end quote. And it appears to me that since he is the one who decreed, the matter depends on his intent, that he did not intend to forbid on the fifteenth of Av nor at a seudas mitzvah; but an individual who accepted it upon himself, it takes effect upon him, for he is not obligated to eat meat even on Shabbos and Yom Tov, as is written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 341, and above in siman 552, se'if 10.
