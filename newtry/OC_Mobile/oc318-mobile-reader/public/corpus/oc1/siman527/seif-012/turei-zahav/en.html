And to kindle a lamp. Implies if he did not stipulate this, forbidden to kindle Yom Tov to Shabbat — Tosafot ch. 2 22b: one without eiruv — they kindle a lamp — implies must mention in eiruv blessing: permitted to bake, cook, and kindle; if not stipulated for lighting, here forbidden — only one lamp as lighting had to be mentioned. Question: why mention lighting when he later says and to do all needs? Answer from ch. 2: one may not insulate hot water per Beit Shammai — if he placed eiruv why forbidden for Beit Shammai? Rava: insulating is different — evident it is for Shabbat. Abaye: Chanania says Beit Shammai: no baking unless eiruv bread, no cooking unless eiruv dish, no insulating unless hot water was insulated from before — if he had, he would do it; evident; Abaye: e.g. eiruv for this not that — Rashi: baked/cooked from before for eiruv, did not insulate hot water — Chanania per Beit Shammai. We learn: even Beit Hillel needing only one dish — for blessing must mention; if no blessing at all, helps for all matters — blessings not me'akevim; if blessed but mentioned one thing — worse, shows eiruv only for that; should mention and include all; since R' Eliezer from That which you will bake — bake only on baked, cook on cooked — must mention these two with baking/cooking in blessing since Torah basis for eiruv; must mention also insulating and lighting — like eiruv for this not that for Beit Shammai needing each type — for Beit Hillel in one type must mention all; question on including all — comes to include slaughter not in explicit list; say no need to mention insulating/lighting in formula nor include in and to do all needs — fits Rava: insulating different, evident for Shabbat; though Rava's answer rejected for law, for blessing all agree must mention eiruv even for evident matters — insulating and lamp evident for Shabbat — daylight lamp useless — not in and to do all needs; therefore mention in formula; Hagah Gemara only lighting not insulating — one evident matter enough; Tur and Shulchan Aruch mention both evident; other non-evident matters like slaughter included in and to do all needs — good as Tur explains. Still note Magen Avraham: eiruv permits slaughter Yom Tov to Shabbat though not in formula — obvious in and to do all needs; Rashal responsum 308 forbids slaughter Yom Tov to Shabbat via eiruv — proof eiruv helps only what stipulated per Tosafot and Rosh on lighting — custom: bake, cook, kindle, insulate — we adopt this; do not heed Rashal that cannot slaughter first Yom Tov for Shabbat but on Friday — he wrote per those permitting all melachot via saying bake and cook per Rif — not to forbid slaughter for those who forbid without stipulation; perhaps Rashal when explicitly stipulated slaughter for weekday — see there; says slaughter in and to do all needs — Hagah Gemara bake cook and to do all needs and kindle — if all included why say and kindle; Tur explicitly and to do all needs and insulating — like cooking, must mention though not in and to do all needs — all the more slaughter. Do not answer after death — I say the rabbi said tradition: slaughter permission depends on mentioning eiruv in formula — why not fix blessing like insulating/lighting since slaughter on Yom Tov mentioned often — Rashal also for Rif formula — vain since Rashal cited Rosh formula like Tur; Rashal Beitzah ch. 2 siman 27 himself: although Rif and Rambam did not mention lamp, Rosh is primary. Do not heed forbidding slaughter with eiruv if made — clear to me.
