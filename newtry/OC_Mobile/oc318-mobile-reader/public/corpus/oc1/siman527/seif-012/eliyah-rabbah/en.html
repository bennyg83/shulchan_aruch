[12] [Levush] Even though he remembered, etc. The wording 'even' is not precise, for on the contrary when he did not remember he relies on the leading scholar; and the wording of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch is not thus; and one must say that it refers to his intent that he wishes to rely, and that is the novel point. The Taz wrote: Rashi and Rashba are worthy to be heeded, that relying is effective if the leading scholar intends thus.
