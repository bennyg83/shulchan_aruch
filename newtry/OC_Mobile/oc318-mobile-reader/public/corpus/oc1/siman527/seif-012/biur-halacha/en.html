And if he sets an eruv through agency and the cooked dish is the sender's, whether he must recite a blessing over it: the Alter Rebbe wrote that it depends on this: if the saying is essential, he should recite a blessing; but according to the one who holds that the saying does not withhold at all, he should not recite a blessing since he is doing nothing; see there.
