Our needs. Maharshal wrote: if he did not stipulate explicitly 'and to slaughter,' he cannot slaughter on Yom Tov for Shabbos; and the Taz disagrees with him and concluded that one should not heed him to forbid slaughtering from Yom Tov for Shabbos if he set eruv tavshilin; see there. And so ruled the Bach; and so it is implied in Responsa Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim section, klal 4, siman 4, that one need not specify 'and to slaughter.' And Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah in Hagahos Beis Yosef, letter 10, wrote that l'chatchilah it is proper to say explicitly 'and to slaughter,' and that so he of blessed memory practiced; and see Yad Aharon.
