Need. R' Shlomo wrote: if he did not stipulate explicitly "and to slaughter," he cannot slaughter on Yom Tov for Shabbat. Taz disagrees and raised that one need not be concerned to forbid slaughter from Yom Tov to Shabbat if he placed eruv tavshilin — see there; so too Bach, and so in responsum Ginat Veradim Orach Chayim general rule 4 siman 4 that one need not specify "and to slaughter."
