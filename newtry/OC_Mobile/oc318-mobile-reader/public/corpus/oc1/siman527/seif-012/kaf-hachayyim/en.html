12) If one placed an eruv tavshilin before the congregation recited the Amidah, then although they already said Barechu, his eruv is valid, because one may rely upon Raavan. Chayei Adam there. Mishnah Berurah, subsection 4, likewise writes that in pressing circumstances, even after Barechu, as long as the congregation has not recited Maariv and it is not yet dark, one may prepare an eruv. All this applies in a city with only one synagogue. If there are many synagogues, the individual is not drawn after a congregation that has already prayed, as stated above.
