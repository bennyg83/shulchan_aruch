Se'if 12: He then, etc. The aforementioned Yerushalmi.
And to bury [food to keep warm] and to light a lamp. For all these cannot be done without an eruv. 'To bury [food]': as is written there, folio 21b; and if he did not set eruv tavshilin, what is Beis Hillel's reason? 'And to light': as is written, 'one who did not set,' etc., and they light the lamp for him: meaning one lamp for his livelihood; and see above, se'if 19.
And for So-and-so and So-and-so. So it is implied in the aforementioned Yerushalmi; and likewise in Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Yom Tov, halacha 1, that Rabbi said, 'for me and for one who did not make an eruv,' etc.; and there R' Chiyya in the name of R' Yochanan: the text should read, 'for me and for one who did not make an eruv.'
And one who does not, etc. Whether according to Rava's reason, so that he will clarify, etc., or according to Rav Ashi's reason, so that they will say, etc.: he must understand, and he fulfills his obligation through it.
