(ך) [seif 3] Measure of dish is olive-size, etc. — beware not to take bread and low bone used disrespectfully for the mitzvah; each person happy in the mitzvah should take bread ready for Shabbat meal and an important dish — fish, meat, or food for morning meal. Shelah; Ateret Zekenim note 5; Rashi Beitzah 16a: need something not eaten daily so it is evident it is for Shabbat honor; Magen Avraham sk 2; Ateret Zekenim there. From Rashi's words this is not required — see there; perhaps they mean to learn from Rashi lechatchila where possible; for law, anything used to dip bread is permitted as Gemara, Shulchan Aruch, Peri Chadash; see Noda BiYehuda klal 102 note 2.
