Se'if 20: And even in their house. Since he did not make an eruv, regarding him it is like [transferring] from Yom Tov to a weekday (Beis Yosef). And in the Gemara it is stated anonymously.
And even in his house. From its saying 'what difference is there to acquire,' etc., it is implied that one needs only to acquire, and there is no other practical difference; for otherwise, if so, there would be a further practical difference.
And if there is no, etc. There, the teaching of Rav Huna, and Toras Kohanim; and some authorities wrote [to omit it] because the Rif and the Rambam omitted it and hold that the halacha is not like him, since Rava and Abaye strained to answer with a different resolution.
To bake. Even he himself, for 'they light' is not precise, etc.; and so it is in the Yerushalmi there, not like R' Yochanan (Ran). And so it is implied in the Tosefta at the beginning of chapter 2: one who forgot and did not make an eruv, and afterward those who made an eruv are permitted to do for him, meaning by transferring ownership of his flour; he himself who forgot and did not make an eruv is permitted to fill a barrel of water for him and to light the lamp for him, meaning that he himself is permitted to do [these] for him.
And if he set, etc. And so wrote Tosafos, folio 22a, s.v. 'umadlikin,' etc.; and so it is somewhat implied in the aforementioned Yerushalmi from its saying 'to bake and to cook.'
And one who fasts, etc. Since he cannot cook for himself, and the essence of the eruv is for his sake.
