To rely on it so long as it remains intact. Meaning: that he included in the condition of 'with this eruv' other Shabbosos that will arrive adjacent to Yom Tov.
L'chatchilah he should not rely, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, what he wrote: that there are opinions among the poskim, etc. Know that there is yet a third view among the poskim [and it is the view of the Mordechai and Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of the Yerushalmi]: that for another Yom Tov one cannot make an eruv, but for that Yom Tov itself, for example where Succos falls on Thursday, he may say on erev Yom Tov that it be also for Shemini Atzeres [and see Biur HaGra, who also copied this law in their name and in the name of the Yerushalmi, only that he concludes that nevertheless the Kol Bo is stringent from the Mishnah's wording, 'and he makes a cooked dish from erev Yom Tov,' implying specifically from erev Yom Tov]. However those who are stringent, namely the Kol Bo in the name of Rabbeinu Nesanel, are stringent even in this; see Beis Yosef; therefore one should be stringent l'chatchilah like the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch. And it is also implied from Beis Yosef that according to the stringent ones it is good l'chatchilah to make the cooked dish on erev Yom Tov itself and not earlier, even if they will set it on erev Yom Tov for the sake of an eruv, for so he inferred from the Mishnah's wording, 'and he makes a cooked dish from erev Yom Tov,' etc.; however if he has none, it is certainly valid even l'chatchilah if only he set it for the sake of an eruv; and so I wrote above in se'if 6 in Biur Halacha; see there.
But b'dieved he may rely on it. Behold, the poskim did not clarify whether one kezayis suffices or whether a kezayis is needed for each Yom Tov. And apparently by reason it appears that it does not avail even b'dieved unless there is in it the measure of a kezayis for each Yom Tov that he makes an eruv for; for it is not comparable to eruvei chatzeros, where so long as it remains intact they are all partners in it; but here, where they enacted that he prepare a cooked dish from erev Yom Tov so that he bake and cook relying on this, as above in se'if 1, and if so here, where he wishes to make an eruv now also for another Yom Tov, he needs an extra kezayis for that Yom Tov, for it is considered as if he prepared for that Yom Tov too from today, and where is his preparation if he does not set an extra kezayis for that Yom Tov? However from the Mechaber's wording, 'with intent to rely on it,' it is implied that he relies on the kezayis itself; and so it somewhat appears from the Rosh's wording, who wrote 'one eruv for this Shabbos and for the coming Shabbos'; and all the more it appears from the Yerushalmi, which wrote: a person makes an eruv with an egg's-bulk of bread and a kezayis of a cooked dish and relies on it until the departure of the last day of Yom Tov; and the Mordechai copied it for practical law, and likewise Hagahos Maimoniyos; hence it is explicitly implied that on one kezayis he relies even for the last day of Yom Tov, unless we distinguish that the whole festival until the last day of Yom Tov is considered to him as one, and he needs no extra preparation, which is not so from one Yom Tov to the next, which requires a separate kezayis for each Yom Tov [and in truth so is the view of Hagahos Maimoniyos in the essence of the law: that specifically until the last day of Yom Tov his eruv avails, and not for another Yom Tov, as explained in Beis Yosef]. What emerges for us from our words is that b'dieved one may rely on the Yerushalmi even in this, that one kezayis suffices; only nevertheless one may discuss: perhaps only for that Yom Tov, and since the source of this law is from the Yerushalmi, let us not add upon it, like the view of Hagahos Maimoniyos brought in Beis Yosef. But for the last day of Yom Tov, according to the Kol Bo one cannot make an eruv at all earlier, for he must cook and set on each erev Yom Tov itself; therefore even if we are lenient b'dieved with an eruv that he made now for another Yom Tov, possibly he specifically needs at least an extra kezayis for its sake; and this requires further study. However all this is if he sets and says now 'with this eruv' at the time he sets it for all the Yamim Tovim coming before him; but if the kezayis remains intact and he goes back and says 'with this eruv' on that erev Yom Tov, it is obvious that it avails even with one kezayis, for now it is a new eruv.
