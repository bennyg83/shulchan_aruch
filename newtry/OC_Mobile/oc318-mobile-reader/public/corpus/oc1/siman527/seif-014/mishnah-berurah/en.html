{43} That it remain intact. For example smoked [food] that lasts many days.
{44} L'chatchilah he should not rely, etc. For there are opinions about this among the poskim. Some say that according to the reason of the eruv brought in se'if 1 he may make an eruv on erev Yom Tov and stipulate that it be for an eruv even for another Yom Tov if it remains intact, for even though it turns out that he set the eruv a long time before that Yom Tov we do not care, since at any rate he already began preparing for that Shabbos from an earlier time on weekdays. And some say that its mitzvah is to set it specifically on the eve of that Yom Tov on which he comes to permit cooking; and even if he set it two or three days earlier it does not avail. And the Mechaber decided that l'chatchilah one must be stringent like this view, and b'dieved we rely on the lenient ones regarding a rabbinic prohibition.
{45} He may rely. That is, when he set the eruv for another Yom Tov and did not remember to make an eruv again on erev Yom Tov, he relies on this eruv to bake and cook; but if he remembered before Yom Tov he must make an eruv again and say 'with this eruv,' but he should not recite the blessing again.
