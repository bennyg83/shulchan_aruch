(א) We will eat like a hand. But if we eat it, there is nothing to do with it as a “six-year-old” in the dish:

One need not make eruv — see seif 22, Yeshuot Shani. Meaning one should not think it is l'chatchila, for since one may place eruv tavshilin on the first day conditionally as seif 22 states, therefore he wrote one need not; Yeshuot Shani ends with the reason: since Rambam's view is not to stipulate nowadays at all, brought in Tur siman 528 (his reason: since we are expert in fixing the month and know the first day is Yom Tov and the second is weekday except for our fathers' custom that Chazal established also a second Yom Tov from certain Torah and not doubtful Torah — if so how can one stipulate if today is sanctified, etc., and tomorrow — behold he knows the second day is weekday; see Maggid Mishneh chapter 6 Hilchot Yom Tov who expanded on this reason) — nevertheless we do not hold like him, etc.; nevertheless l'chatchila without need why act against him — end Yeshuot Shani:
