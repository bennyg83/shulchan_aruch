Se'if 14: If he set, etc. See Taz and Hagahos Maimoniyos, whose opinion is that for the last day of Yom Tov it is permitted and for another Yom Tov it is forbidden; certainly one may make an eruv for the second day of Yom Tov, only Beis Yosef was in doubt whether one may also make an eruv on another Yom Tov. And in the aforementioned Yerushalmi as well it is implied that one may make an eruv for the last day of Yom Tov if he stipulated concerning it; but the Kol Bo wrote in the name of R' Nesanel to be stringent, because the Mishnah teaches 'on erev Yom Tov,' implying specifically from erev Yom Tov; and further, the reason 'so that he will choose' and all the reasons do not apply; and see Beis Yosef.
