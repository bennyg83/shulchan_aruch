If he placed, etc. — for it is stated in the Gemara: why did they establish eruv tavshilin? Rava said: so he will set aside a fine portion for Shabbat and a fine portion for Yom Tov. Rav Ashi said: so they will say one does not bake from Yom Tov to Shabbat — a fortiori from Yom Tov to weekday. The two reasons differ: for Rava one must make eruv specifically on Yom Tov so he sets aside fine portions for Shabbat and Yom Tov; for Rav Ashi one may make eruv even before Yom Tov, and on Sukkot Yom Tov that falls on the fifth on Shabbat one may make one eruv on Yom Tov for this Shabbat and the coming Shabbat; since it is not clear which view is halacha, Beit Yosef ruled l'chatchila to forbid and b'dieved to permit:
