If he placed, etc. For it is stated in the Gemara: why did they establish eruv tavshilin? Rava said, so that he will select a fine portion for Shabbos and a fine portion for Yom Tov. Rav Ashi said, so that people will say: one may not bake from Yom Tov for Shabbos, all the more so from Yom Tov for a weekday. The Rosh wrote that there is a practical difference between these reasons. According to Rava, one must make the eruv specifically on Erev Yom Tov, so that he will select a fine portion for Shabbos. According to Rav Ashi, he can make the eruv even before Erev Yom Tov, and if the Yom Tov of Sukkos falls on Thursday, he can make one eruv on Erev Yom Tov for this Shabbos and for the following Shabbos. Since it is not clear to us like whom the halacha follows, the Beit Yosef ruled that initially it is forbidden, but after the fact it is permitted.
