At first glance, according to the Mechaber's ruling in accordance with the Rosh, even after the fact one does not fulfill his obligation through the communal eruv if the one who made it intended to include deliberate offenders. Why, then, is it a mitzvah for the leading scholar of the city to make an eruv for everyone, since in Ezra's time people did not fail to make their own? According to Rashi, however, one may say that in Ezra's time the generation was worthy and people were not negligent, whereas now it is a mitzvah because some may be negligent. This requires study.
