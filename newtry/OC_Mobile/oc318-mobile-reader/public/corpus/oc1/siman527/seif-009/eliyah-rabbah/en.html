[9] And it is also a mitzvah, etc. The Taz raised a difficulty from Beitzah folio 16, 'and they sent portions to one who has none prepared,' to one who does not set eruv tavshilin: for certainly every leading scholar of the city also set [an eruv] on behalf of others, since it is a mitzvah; until here. And in my humble opinion, that mitzvah is what the Tur mentioned because he saw that people are accustomed to forget due to the pressures of the time; but in the days of the Gemara it was not a mitzvah, and this is easy to understand. Further, it is possible that it deals with those who came from outside the techum; see se'if 8.
