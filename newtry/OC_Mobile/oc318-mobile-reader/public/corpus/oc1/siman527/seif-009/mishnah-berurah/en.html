{30} The intent of the one who sets, etc. Apparently it is an obvious matter, for he does not take [others] out unless he acquires through another; and possibly he mentioned it because of the latter clause, 'but the intent,' etc.; and further one may say that he mentioned it for where he takes out anonymously, that it avails only for people included by default in his wording, namely one who is within the city's techum, similar to that of the preceding se'if.
{31} Only that they inform him, etc. Meaning: if it became known to him on Yom Tov that they set an eruv on his behalf, he is permitted to cook even though they did not consult him at the time of setting, for his intent is not needed at all; and the wording 'only that they inform him' that it says is so that they know they may cook for Shabbos.
{32} They rely on him by default. Those who forgot or were forced, for certainly they made an eruv on behalf of all; and even though it was not announced. And some later authorities wished to say that according to us, since in the wording of the blessing of eruv tavshilin it is 'for us and for all who dwell in this city,' whoever forgot may rely, for certainly they made an eruv on his behalf; and they disagreed with them, that the wording avails nothing so long as they did not acquire on their behalf through another with a cooked dish, as below in se'if 10; and most people do not know this and do not transfer ownership at all; therefore one cannot rely on this regarding ordinary people unless it is known clearly that they transferred ownership to them.
