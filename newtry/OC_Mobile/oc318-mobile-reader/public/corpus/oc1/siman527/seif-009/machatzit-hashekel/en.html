(Seif katan 9) He stipulated concerning him and made an eruv tavshilin. The reason given there is that Rabbi Eliezer explains eruv tavshilin as teaching that one may not bake from Yom Tov for Shabbos unless he began preparing for Shabbos before Yom Tov, and the food set aside as eruv tavshilin constitutes that beginning. All the more so may one not bake from Yom Tov for a weekday even if he began before Yom Tov. If the person is outside the techum of the one who made the eruv, how can he rely upon it, and how can the cooked dish set aside by that person be considered the beginning of his Shabbos cooking, when he cannot reach it and eat it on Shabbos? Therefore he must also have made an eruv techumin; see there.
