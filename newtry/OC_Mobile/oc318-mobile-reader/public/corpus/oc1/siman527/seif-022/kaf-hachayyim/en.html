One might ask that this follows a fortiori: if a kezayis is sufficient initially, it is certainly sufficient after the fact. The teaching informs us that even after the fact, less than a kezayis is ineffective. Olas Shabbos, subsection 3; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 9. See below, se'if 15.
