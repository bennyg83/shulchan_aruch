{40} He may, etc. Meaning: he must say.
{41} From the first day of Yom Tov. Meaning: when the first day of Yom Tov falls on Thursday, for he can cook on Friday which is the second day of Yom Tov; and even if he knows he will have a forced circumstance on the second day of Yom Tov so that he cannot cook on it, and he made an eruv initially on condition that he cook on the first day, it does not avail, for they enacted an eruv only from the day adjacent to Shabbos and not from the day before it; and especially in our times, when the second day of Yom Tov is like a weekday according to the Torah, for if there were a weekday in between, an eruv would certainly not avail to cook from Yom Tov for Shabbos.
{42} For Shabbos. And b'dieved if he transgressed and cooked, or erred and cooked, the Taz's view is to permit, as below in se'if 23; and all the more so if he did not cook other foods for Shabbos.
