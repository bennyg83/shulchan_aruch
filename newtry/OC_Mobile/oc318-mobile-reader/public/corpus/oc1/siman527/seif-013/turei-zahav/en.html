First Yom Tov for Shabbat. When Yom Tov is Thursday-Friday — since one may bake, cook, slaughter on Friday (only custom), why do this on holy first Yom Tov? If Yom Tov is Friday-Shabbat, permitted on Friday first Yom Tov via eiruv as required. Rashal: if he transgressed and cooked day 1 (Thursday) forbidden to eat on Shabbat; unlike below seif where transgression permitted when no food — here he can cook tomorrow for Shabbat — forbidden to benefit until after Shabbat; I do not know this distinction; no prohibition eating on Yom Tov anyway. Hagah Maimoniyot: Yerushalmi Yom Tov for neighbor and Yom Tov for Shabbat; Rabeinu Chananel: e.g. two days Shabbat and erev Shabbat — need eiruv for both; Beit Yosef: appears must say in eiruv permitted to cook Yom Tov to neighbor and Yom Tov to Shabbat even if cooking only day 1 for day 1 since that day has two names; difficult wording — implies cooking two days for two future days — rather day 1 for day 1 — should say Yom Tov for neighbor which is Yom Tov and Shabbat, etc.; he elaborated; practically: Yom Tov Friday-Shabbat saying and to do all needs for good day to Shabbat day insufficient — everyone cooks more on Shabbat-Yom Tov than regular Shabbat — if so permitted extras for tomorrow not for Shabbat alone saying Yom Tov for Shabbat excludes extras — say both to include cooking today for Yom Tov under Shabbat permissions — correct for every first Yom Tov on Friday.
