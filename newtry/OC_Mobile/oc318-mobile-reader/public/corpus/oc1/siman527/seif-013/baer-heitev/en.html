We mean that a good Yom Tov falls on the fifth day of the 6th of the Holy Week, if it falls on a Friday and on the Shabbat, it is permissible to cook a peshita on the Friday, which is the first 19th day of the year, according to Irov Tebshilin. And Rashel Dafilo wrote that if he passed and cooked on the Sunday, that is, on the fifth day of Dassor, he should eat it on Shabbat.
