It appears that Yom Tov differs from Shabbos, for reciting Barechu does not constitute acceptance of Yom Tov, whereas on Shabbos reciting Barechu does constitute acceptance; see simanim 261 and 263. This is novel. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. His reference to Shabbos means Yom Tov, where the second day of Yom Tov falls on Shabbos. Alternatively, the text should read “the fifth and sixth days,” and a stipulation is required. This implies that accepting Yom Tov through Barechu is like accepting Shabbos. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 3, and siman 263, seif katan 35. With Hashem's help, this will be explained in Magen Avraham.
