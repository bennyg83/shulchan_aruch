{8} A kezayis. And for the sake of beautifying the mitzvah one should take a whole loaf and an important piece of meat or fish.
{9} Whether at its end. Apparently it is a fortiori: if at its beginning a kezayis suffices, all the more so if there was more and a kezayis remained; rather it comes to teach us that even at its end less than a kezayis does not suffice (Olas Tamid).
{10} L'chatchilah. This 'l'chatchilah' is only before he makes the eruv; but if he already made it, he need not go back and make it again (Chemed Moshe).
