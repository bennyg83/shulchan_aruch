{20} He relies from erev Yom Tov even, etc. Meaning: even though they remained there without intent, since while it was still day he relied on them, we do not say that since they are not significant they are nullified in the pot; rather he scrapes them from erev Yom Tov and designates them for this and says over them 'with this eruv,' etc.; and likewise fat on the knife; and all this specifically if he has no other cooked dish (later authorities). And it is obvious that this is specifically regarding lentils at the bottom of the pot, which is somewhat a disrespect of the mitzvah; but an actual cooked dish of lentils is no worse than any other cooked dish. And even if they were not cooked initially for that purpose. And all this specifically if the practice in that place is to dip bread with lentils (Magen Avraham).
