Lentils, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, what he wrote: that even if they were not cooked for that purpose is clear and obvious; and see further what he wrote in the name of the poskim, that specifically when he has nothing else with which to make an eruv; so wrote Beis Yosef and later authorities. And the Alter Rebbe wrote the reason as disrespect of the mitzvah; however the Bach wrote another reason: that it is because they were not cooked for that purpose, for l'chatchilah the choicest mitzvah is to make a cooked dish intentionally for the sake of an eruv [and perhaps only if he cooked for the needs of Shabbos it suffices, and these are different, for they were not cooked at all for the needs of Shabbos]; and so he brought from the implication of the Mordechai. And all this is only for the choicest mitzvah in general; but the eruv is valid even if he took things that were not cooked for this purpose, as is proven from that of lentils and fat stuck to the knife; and so wrote Olas Tamid, and he proved further from that which is in the Gemara regarding salted fish that a non-Jew roasted, and he certainly did not roast them for the sake of an eruv; and see further in se'if 14 in Biur Halacha.
