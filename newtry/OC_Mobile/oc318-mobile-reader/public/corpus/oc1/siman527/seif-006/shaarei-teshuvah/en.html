Who forgot. See Kenesses Yechezkel, siman 21, regarding one who forgot and the leading scholar of the city told him, 'Rely on my hand,' and again on another festival he likewise forgot: even so he may rely on the leading scholar of the city, even on consecutive festivals, because nowadays the intent of the one who acquires [on behalf of others] is even for one who forgets several times in a row, and all the more so if in between there was a festival or festivals on which he set eruv tavshilin properly; see there. And see Chacham Tzvi, siman 174, what he wrote regarding Rashi's words in Beitzah, and what he wrote in Kenesses Yechezkel about this; see there.
