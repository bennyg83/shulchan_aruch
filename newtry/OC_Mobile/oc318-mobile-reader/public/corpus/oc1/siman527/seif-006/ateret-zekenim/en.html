Trusts from the 10th day of the Efi on lentils. We were to him that the lentils were not cooked in the 10th day and he did not cut with the oily knife on the evening of Yom Tov, but that the lentils and the oily food were left over from 3 or 4 days before the 10th day of the 10th day. At that time, this is a proof that I trust them at all (the column and the Harramii).
