{5} With bread and a cooked dish. For one needs to bake and cook from Yom Tov for Shabbos; therefore they make the eruv from bread and a cooked dish from erev Yom Tov, so that relying on this they afterward bake and cook.
{6} And if he made it only from a cooked dish alone. For according to the essential law one needs only a cooked dish alone, and it avails both for bread and for a cooked dish, for so is the view of most poskim; only they were accustomed like the view of the stringent poskim to require bread and a cooked dish; therefore b'dieved it is permitted. And if he need not bake on Yom Tov and only cook, even l'chatchilah a cooked dish alone suffices according to everyone.
{7} It is permitted. To bake and cook on Yom Tov for the sake of Shabbos; and nevertheless if he remembered before nightfall he should add also bread and say 'with this eruv,' but he should not recite the blessing again. And if he made it from bread alone for a cooked dish, it certainly does not avail; and whether it avails for bread alone there are opinions among the poskim, and the later authorities agreed that it does not avail even for bread, for the essence of the eruv was enacted with a cooked dish.
