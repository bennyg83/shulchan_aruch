2) There. One may not initially cook for Shabbos, etc. In Beitzah 2b, Rabbah derives this from the verse, 'On the sixth day they shall prepare that which they bring.' A weekday prepares for Shabbos, and a weekday prepares for Yom Tov, but Yom Tov does not prepare for Shabbos, etc. Tosafos there ask: Since preparation is a Torah prohibition, how may one bake and cook on Yom Tov for Shabbos? If the answer is that an eruv tavshilin permits it, how can a rabbinic enactment come and uproot a Torah prohibition against preparation? They answer that Rabbah follows his own reasoning in Pesachim 46b, where he applies the principle that since the food would be fit if guests happened to arrive, it is presently considered fit as well.
