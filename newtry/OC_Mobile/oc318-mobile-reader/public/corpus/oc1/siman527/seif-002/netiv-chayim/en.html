(Magen Avraham s.k. 3) It implies salted fish, etc. — I will explain his words: since the Mishnah teaches one makes a cooked dish from erev Yom Tov — excluding salted fish; unlike pickled, roasted, and koleis that fishermen rinse — that is their cooking and one is liable for it on Shabbat — all these are included in cooked dish. One may also say: for stringency alone they say salted is like roasted that is roasted, not for leniency as we find with liver salting that has no remedy except after roasting — from which we learn for leniency we do not say like roasted that is roasted:
