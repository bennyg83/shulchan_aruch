(Seif katan 17) To leave it for Shabbos. It is forbidden to leave it for a weekday. Leaving it for Shabbos is permitted because it was cooked permissibly while the eruv tavshilin still existed.
And it was permitted to cook for Shabbos. Although at the time of cooking he intended it for Yom Tov, that intention does not prohibit the food. Even one who employs a subterfuge by saying that he cooks for Yom Tov and then leaves it for Shabbos would be permitted by the strict law, except that the Sages decreed against this lest everyone circumvent and nullify the enactment of eruv tavshilin. Here there was no subterfuge, so he may leave the food for Shabbos and now cook for Yom Tov. Leaving it for a weekday is certainly forbidden, because eruv tavshilin does not permit preparation for a weekday, and deciding after cooking for Yom Tov to leave it for a weekday is itself an impermissible subterfuge.
