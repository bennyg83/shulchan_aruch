[17] From Yom Tov to Shabbos, etc. And if the first day falls on Friday, then for the second day of Yom Tov which is Shabbos he cooks more than [for] another Shabbos (Taz). And it is obvious that an individual who makes an eruv for himself does not say 'and for So-and-so,' etc., or 'for the whole city,' etc.; and further, that does not avail when they do not place it in another's hand, as above; and the world errs and says the entire formula correctly that one should say 'from one Yom Tov to the next and from Yom Tov to Shabbos.'
