(ז) There. Through eiruv he cooks first for Shabbat. They relied on the verse Remember the Shabbat day to sanctify it — remember it when it might be forgotten. Why? Rava: to prepare a good portion for Shabbat and Yom Tov. Rav Ashi: lest they say one does not bake from Yom Tov to Shabbat — kal vachomer from Yom Tov to weekday. Tanna brings from: That which you will bake, bake, and that which you will cook, cook — R' Elazar: bake only on what is baked, cook only on what is cooked; hence Chazal instituted eiruv tavshilin from Torah. Beitzah 15b. Difference: Rava requires eiruv specifically on Yom Tov to clarify portions; Rav Ashi may eiruv even erev Yom Tov — Sukkot Thursday-Shabbat may make one eiruv for this and next Shabbat — Rosh start Beitzah ch. 2. Yeshayah diShani ch. 2 siman 1 challenged: even Rav Ashi requires eiruv each erev Yom Tov; erev eiruv does not help even for Rav Ashi — so custom; see Beit Yosef dispute; for law l'chatchila strict, b'dieved lenient as d'rabbanan — see Shulchan Aruch seif 14; Levush; Bach; Magen Avraham sk 13; Acharonim as explained there.
