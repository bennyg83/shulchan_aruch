Who forgot. If he forgot because of his laziness, because he was not careful about the word of Hashem, and not because of compulsion, he is called negligent. For one who forgets is called negligent regarding payment of money, and all the more so regarding a prohibition, as the Yam Shel Shlomo writes. This requires study, for in siman 108, seif 8, it says that one who forgets is considered under compulsion, and the same applies to monetary matters, as the Beit Yosef wrote there. One must say that forgetting because of laziness is different.
And it was lost. We do not say that he thereby revealed his intent that he did not wish to fulfill through the eruv of the leading authority, as the Beit Yosef writes.
Who does not know. Or he did not know that each person is obligated to make an eruv and relied on the leading authority. But a learned person is considered negligent, as the Yam Shel Shlomo writes.
