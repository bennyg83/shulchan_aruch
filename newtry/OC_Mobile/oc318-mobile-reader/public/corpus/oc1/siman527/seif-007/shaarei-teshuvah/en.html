(In Shulchan Aruch seif 12) He returns — and the acquirer takes immediately, etc. — see Birkei Yosef in name of Tur Brachot who wrote: our teachers did not practice thus; rather the one who transfers stands with him and the one making eruv takes the eruv in his hand and says "by law may this eruv," etc., for all residents of the province; afterward he gives the eruv to the acquirer's hand and says to him "acquire," etc.; but Shulchan Aruch and Tur wrote as Shulchan Aruch — see there.
