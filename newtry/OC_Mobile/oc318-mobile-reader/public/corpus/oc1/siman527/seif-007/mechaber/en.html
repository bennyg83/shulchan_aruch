7. It is a mitzvah for everyone to make an eruv. It is incumbent on the leader of the city to make an eruv for his townspeople so that those who forgot to make an eruv, those who were forced not to, and those who made an eruv and lost it can rely on his. (This is also the case for ignoramuses who do not know who to make an eruv) (Or Zarua). However, one who can make an eruv and didn't make an eruv but rather wanted to depend on the leader's eruv is called a sinner and cannot rely on it.( ) אבל מי שאפשר לערב ולא עירב אלא שרוצה לסמוך על עירובו של גדול העיר נקרא פושע ואינו יוצא בו:
