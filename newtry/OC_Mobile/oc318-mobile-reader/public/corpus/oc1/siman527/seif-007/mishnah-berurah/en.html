{21} And it is a mitzvah upon every leading scholar, etc. And so we find in the Gemara that several of the great Amoraim were particular about this.
{22} One who forgot. And all this on the first occasion; but if on the second festival he forgot again, he is like a negligent person, for it is evident that he is not anxious about a matter of mitzvah, and his law is explained at the end of the se'if.
{23} Or that he set, etc. And we do not say that by making an eruv he revealed his intent that he does not wish to fulfill his obligation through the eruv of the leading scholar of the city.
{24} Who does not know how to make an eruv. And the same applies if he thought that one may fulfill the obligation through the eruv of the leading scholar even l'chatchilah.
{25} He is called negligent, etc. Because the enactment of the Sages was that each and every one make eruv tavshilin himself, and so that he remember that if from Yom Tov to Shabbos it is forbidden to cook, all the more so from Yom Tov to a weekday. However it need not be specifically he himself, for the same applies if he appoints an agent as well, for a person's agent is like himself; and it comes only to exclude the eruv of the leading scholar, who prepares from his cooked dish and does not consult them at the time of making it, but rather informs them on Yom Tov that he made an eruv and they may rely on him.
{26} And he does not fulfill his obligation through it. For the scholars do not transfer ownership of their eruv to one who can make it himself and is lazy. And know that many poskim disagree with this, and according to them every person may rely l'chatchilah on the eruv of the leading scholar of the city, and even the leading scholars intend with their eruv to transfer ownership to every person in every manner; and who is it that does not fulfill through their eruv? One who every time makes it himself and does not wish to rely on the eruv of the leading scholar, and afterward it happened that he forgot several times and did not make it: in this the leading scholar does not take him out, for it was not his practice to rely on the leading scholar. And possibly b'dieved one may rely on these poskim and be lenient for the sake of the joy of Yom Tov.
