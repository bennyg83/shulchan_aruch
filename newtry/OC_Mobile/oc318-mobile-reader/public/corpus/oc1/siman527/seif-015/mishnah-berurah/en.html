{46} The eruv was eaten. That is, the cooked dish; but if the bread was eaten there is nothing amiss in that. And even if Yom Tov falls on Thursday and Friday and he remembered on the first day that the bread was lost, he need not make an eruv conditionally; rather he relies on the cooked dish alone, which is the essence of the eruv, as above in se'if 2.
{47} A kezayis. For this is the measure of an eruv, as above in se'if 3.
