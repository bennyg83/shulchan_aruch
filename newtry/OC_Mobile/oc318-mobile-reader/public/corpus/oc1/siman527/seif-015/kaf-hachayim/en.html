(טו) One who sat in synagogue and remembered he did not make eiruv tavshilin and there blessed intending eiruv and said: in dish so-and-so at my home it shall be permitted to bake, etc. — clearly erred logically and did nothing; must hold the dish and say the formula, etc. Chaim Shaal ch. 1 siman 29; Zachor leChaim ch. 3 note 27. However Halachah Gevurah on Tiferet Yisrael Mishnayot Beitzah ch. 2 Maor wrote this helps and l'chatchila if in synagogue close to dark with no time to go home, before Barchu, he may say bread and dish I will take when I come home are permitted now l'chatchila in the land of Israel, etc. — see there; Beit Din Makor Chaim Likutei Rima note 16; see below seif 7.
