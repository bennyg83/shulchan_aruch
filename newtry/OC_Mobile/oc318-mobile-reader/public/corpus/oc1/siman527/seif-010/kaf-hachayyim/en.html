10) There, in the gloss. Even during twilight. Shulchan Aruch rules likewise above, siman 393, se'if 2, regarding eruv chatzeros and shitufei mevo'os. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 9, gives the reason: a doubt concerning a rabbinic law is treated leniently, and we presume that it is still daytime. These are his words. Regarding whether one should recite a blessing over an eruv placed during twilight, see above, siman 393, subsection 11. There we wrote in the name of Responsa Noda BiShe'arim, at the end of siman 6, that the question remains unresolved. See there. Therefore, one should recite the blessing without the Divine Name and Kingship, and should say only the Leshem Yichud, etc.
