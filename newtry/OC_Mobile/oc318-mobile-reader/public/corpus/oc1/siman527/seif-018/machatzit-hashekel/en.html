(א) (sk 18) Permitted, etc. — per Rosh and Gemara 22b: R' Hiyya — one without eiruv: one loaf, one pot, kindle lamp; Rosh: kindle lamp means one lamp like one loaf and one flask; therefore custom when making eiruv says and to kindle; although I explained eiruv is only for main meal — bread and dish — still mention everything forbidden without eiruv — end Rosh. If so Rif and Rambam disagree with Rosh — they hold even for mention only main meal — baking and cooking; still agree if no eiruv at all even lighting forbidden except one lamp — as Rosh inferred from Stam candle like one loaf and flask.

(ב) And l'chatchila, etc. — even if Beit Hillel permits lighting per Rif and Rambam, Beit Hillel agrees we do not hold like them except to rely in mitzvah need — Shabbat candle; therefore Magen Avraham one lamp permitted for all; extra candles are not Shabbat obligation though added joy and peace at home — 263:8; not worth violating forbidding poskim.
