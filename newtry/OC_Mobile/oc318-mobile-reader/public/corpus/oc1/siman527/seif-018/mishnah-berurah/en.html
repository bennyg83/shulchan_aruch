{51} He baked and did not cook, etc. Here it comes to teach us a new law: when the eruv was lost before the Yom Tov meal he may leave what he cooked on Yom Tov for the needs of Yom Tov itself for Shabbos, since he cooked them while the eruv was intact and could have cooked them for Shabbos; what he intended in them for the needs of Yom Tov he does not lose thereby, and he may leave them for Shabbos and cook others for the needs of Yom Tov. But what he cooked for Yom Tov after his eruv was lost he may not leave for Shabbos and cook others for the needs of Yom Tov, for that is artifice, since he has cooked dishes for Yom Tov.
{52} Or he cooked and did not bake. The wording is not precise, for the same applies if he baked and cooked, only not for the needs of Shabbos.
{53} To leave it for Shabbos. But to leave it for a weekday in such a case and cook others for the needs of Yom Tov is forbidden, as above.
