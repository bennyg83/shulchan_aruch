[18] [Levush] Forbidden, etc. And even if he transgressed and cooked, it is forbidden to benefit from it until after Shabbos; and it is not comparable to se'if 23, where if he transgressed it is permitted, for this case is different in that it is possible to cook on erev Shabbos (Maharshal); and he does not recognize this distinction at all, and all the more so there is no prohibition to eat it at least on Yom Tov (Taz). And if he did not cook on erev Shabbos, it is implied that according to everyone it is permitted on Shabbos; and so it is implied in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah.
