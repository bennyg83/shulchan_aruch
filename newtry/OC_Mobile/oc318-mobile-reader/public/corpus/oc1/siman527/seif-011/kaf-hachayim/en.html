(11) In the gloss — even doubtful twilight; if he accepted Shabbat such as by saying Barchu — forbidden, as siman 261 s.k. 1; however Taz s.k. 3 wrote in name of Raavan siman 168 that even if he did not make eruv until after Barchu he may make eruv as long as the community has not prayed Yom Tov prayer, for acceptance of Yom Tov is not until Yom Tov prayer — see there; so too Or Sameach in name of the mentioned ones; Netiv Chayim concludes similarly. Eliyah Rabbah in Hagahot Tashbetz distinguishes: Raavan's words apply when the community said Barchu and he has not yet said — then he may make eruv after Barchu; but if he said Barchu he already accepted Yom Tov and cannot make eruv — end quote. R' Zalman letter 9 wrote: if most of the community already said Barchu he is drawn after the majority and Yom Tov sanctity applies to him too, forbidden to make eruv tavshilin as on Yom Tov itself — all the more if he himself accepted Yom Tov such as by praying Maariv of Yom Tov although the community has not said Barchu — forbidden to make eruv — end quote; so too Chayei Adam general rule 102 letter 2: if he already accepted Yom Tov such as Barchu or the community said Barchu — forbidden to place. Nevertheless see above siman 263 letter 15 that we wrote: in a city with many synagogues one is not drawn after another; even if one synagogue has a majority — see there; if so in such a city the individual is not drawn after the majority when he did not answer Barchu, for he can pray in the later synagogue and thereby may also make eruv, as long as he has not answered Barchu; and if he said Barchu and others have not, he may tell them to make eruv for him, as siman 263 seif 17 and our words there letter 100 — see there:
