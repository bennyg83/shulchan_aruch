Pickled. Rashi explained: in vinegar or mustard; in Yoreh De'ah siman 87 it is explained that if it stood a full day in something moist it is called pickled (Darkei Moshe); and Beit Yosef; it appears to me that if it is not fit to eat through pickling or smoking one does not make eruv tavshilin with it; but in salting, even though fit to eat, one does not make eruv, as they say regarding small fish as stated in Chullin: salted is like roasted that is roasted, pickled likewise like cooked — implying salted is not like cooked; nevertheless if it was pickled in brine or vinegar and stood in it long enough that if placed on fire it would begin to boil, it is like cooked if eaten so — see Yoreh De'ah siman 105:

(2) Cooked fruit. But chain is not, and who is it in the mm, which means boiled, it is forbidden if there is no way to wrap a turnip in them.
