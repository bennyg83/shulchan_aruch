(Seif katan 5) Lentils, etc. This applies specifically when he has nothing else. Yam Shel Shlomo infers this from the Gemara's wording that one may rely upon them for eruv tavshilin, echoed by Shulchan Aruch, rather than saying that one may initially make an eruv with them. See what Taz writes. Initially it is forbidden because it demeans the mitzvah.
This must apply specifically in a place where lentils are eaten as an accompaniment. Otherwise, even setting aside an ordinary lentil dish would be invalid, and certainly lentil remnants would not qualify.
