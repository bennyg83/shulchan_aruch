Smoked. Magen Avraham wrote: it appears to me that if it is not fit to eat by means of pickling or smoking, one may not make an eruv with it; but with salt, even though it is fit to eat, one may not make an eruv, for we say salted is like boiling-hot, [and] pickled is like cooked, which implies that salted is not like cooked. However, if it was pickled in brine or vinegar and remained in it long enough that if placed on the fire it would begin to boil, it is like cooked if it is eaten thus; see Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 105 (Magen Avraham).
