[5] A kezayis, etc. One should be careful not to take lowly bread and a bone, for then they do the mitzvah with contempt, and possibly afterward discard it; rather every man who rejoices in the mitzvah should take bread prepared for the Shabbos meal and an important cooked dish, such as an important piece of fish or meat, or a cooked dish prepared for the morning meal (Shlah folio 17). And Rashi wrote that we require something that one does not eat every day, so that the matter will be evident that he is doing it for the honor of Shabbos. Maharil was accustomed to place the meat on a plate and not on the challah because of repulsion, and he also took a whole loaf and used it for lechem mishneh on Friday night and at Shacharis, and at the third meal he would cut over it; until here. And the wording of Baal Halachos Gedolos, folio 36: that they take bread and put cooked meat into it, etc.
