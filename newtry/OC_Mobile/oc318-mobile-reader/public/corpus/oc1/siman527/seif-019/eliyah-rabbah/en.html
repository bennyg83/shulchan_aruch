[19] [Levush] It is better, etc. It is implied when he knows it is impossible for him to cook on Thursday; but on erev Shabbos he forbids; and so Magen Avraham is in doubt about this, that it is like where Yom Tov is on Thursday and Friday is a weekday and there is a forced circumstance that he cannot bake on Friday, that it is forbidden to bake on Thursday for Shabbos by means of an eruv; and so it is implied in Responsa Rivash, siman 15, that they did not enact eruv tavshilin for this.
