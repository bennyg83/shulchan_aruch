It is permitted to light. The Beit Yosef innovated this on his own, since the Rif and Rambam did not mention in the formula of the eruv, 'and to light a lamp,' implying that even without an eruv it is permitted to light. To me it does not appear so, for they hold that one need mention only the essential meal, and all other matters follow it; see the Semak. In truth, if one did not make an eruv it is forbidden to light, as is the view of the Rosh, and so it appears in the Gemara. Therefore one should light only one lamp, as written in seif 20, and so rule most authorities and the Yam Shel Shlomo. The Bach wrote that the Rif and Rambam are worthy to be relied upon to light the Shabbos lamp, since it is a mitzvah. This is not so, first because I have written that the Rif and Rambam do not disagree about this, and furthermore, initially one lamp suffices, as written in siman 263.
