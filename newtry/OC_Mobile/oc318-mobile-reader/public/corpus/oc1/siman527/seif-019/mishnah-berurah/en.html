{54} It is permitted to light. For lighting the lamp does not require an eruv; and according to this view one does not say in the formula 'with this eruv' and 'to light a lamp.'
{55} And some forbid. Many later authorities agreed that the essential law follows this view; therefore we say in the formula 'with this eruv' also 'to light a lamp,' for this too is permitted by means of the eruv; only if he set the eruv and did not say [it], it is not forbidden b'dieved, for this too is automatically dragged along; but if he did not set at all it is forbidden, and only one lamp is permitted to light in any case. And some are lenient like the first view to light whatever he wishes; and l'chatchilah it is proper that he transfer ownership to others and they light for him, or that he make do with one lamp.
