[24] It is permitted to kindle, etc. Their reason in Beitzah 22b: one who did not make eiruv tavshilin — they bake one loaf and cook one pot and kindle a lamp for him; since it did not mention one lamp, it implies all his needs may be kindled even without any eiruv tavshilin — so Rosh, Ran, Mordechai. Hence one might say: only when he made eiruv but did not mention lighting it is permitted, but without any eiruv all forbid — yet Talmud explicitly: without any eiruv it is still permitted, as Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch imply; Avodat HaKodesh 45 implies the same for carrying; Hagahot Ashiri Beitzah ch. 2 eiruv formula for private domain to private domain — we do not say so; thus permitted, for candle lighting depends on this; Rama: even for those who say one does not mention lighting, nevertheless forbidden without eiruv tavshilin (nevertheless forbidden without eiruv); poskim above imply they are linked.
