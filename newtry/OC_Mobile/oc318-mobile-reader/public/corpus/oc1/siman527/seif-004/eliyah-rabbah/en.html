[4] [Levush] But b'dieved, etc. But one should not rely on bread alone even to bake (Maharshal). It is forbidden to make eruv tavshilin before erev Yom Tov, only on erev Yom Tov; and b'dieved it is permitted if he made a cooked dish specially for eruv tavshilin or set it aside for that purpose, even if he did not recite the blessing of eruv tavshilin: one may rely on it b'dieved, whether he made it or his wife made it (Maharshal). And Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 23 and disagrees with him from that of se'if 23, that even if he says 'to do all our needs' it is forbidden; however, when he says 'with this it shall be,' etc., and did not say the blessing, it is permitted; and below I have written on this.
