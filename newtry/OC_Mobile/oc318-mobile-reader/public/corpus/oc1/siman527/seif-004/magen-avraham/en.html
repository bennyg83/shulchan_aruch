Porridge. It seems to me that even in a place where people use it to accompany bread, their view is disregarded, for that is like eating bread with bread, and so it appears from the Gemara. It also seems to me that the same applies to other accompaniments and legumes: in a place where it is not the normal way to eat bread with them, one may not use them for the eruv. Rashi wrote that we require something not eaten every day, so that it is evident that he is making it in honor of Shabbos.
