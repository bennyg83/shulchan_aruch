(4) And Tosafot wrote there in Pesachim: if he cooked near dark so one cannot say guests will happen upon him — he is lashed. So too Mordechai chapter 2 of Beitzah and Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot; Beit David, Magen Avraham beginning of the siman. And wrote: therefore when Yom Tov falls on erev Shabbat they advance to pray arvit early, see there — meaning so they not cook for Shabbat need near dark as Machatzit HaShekel wrote; so too R' Zalman letter 8, Kuntres siman 32 letter 8. On this Mishnat Hasidim wrote siman 259: when Yom Tov is erev Shabbat it is fit to advance and insulate from erev Yom Tov the pot so it cook at least half-cooked from erev Yom Tov, see there. However some say Shabbat needs are done on Yom Tov from Torah as in Sefer Beit Meir and Chayei Adam letter 1 and Mishna Berurah in Biur Halacha; therefore wrote there in Mishna Berurah letter 3: in pressing need one may be lenient on second Yom Tov falling erev Shabbat if cooking for Shabbat was delayed until near Shabbat; and even on first Yom Tov perhaps leniency in pressing need — only l'chatchila certainly must beware, especially first Yom Tov which is d'oraisa, see there:
