(Seif katan 4) Fruit, etc. Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 6 of Hilchos Yom Tov, halachah 3, that only a cooked dish qualifies, but bread does not, and certainly fruit does not. This implies that fruit is excluded because it is not ordinarily eaten as an accompaniment, like bread. Were he speaking of raw fruit, no further reason would be needed, since an eruv requires a cooked dish. He must therefore be speaking even of cooked fruit, yet one may not make an eruv with it because in Maggid Mishneh's locale it was not customary to eat it as an accompaniment.
