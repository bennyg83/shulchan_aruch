Doubt. And Magen Avraham wrote: and if he accepted upon himself (Shabbos) [Yom Tov], for example by saying Barchu, it is forbidden; see the beginning of siman 261. But the Taz and Ateres Zekenim wrote from the Raavan that even if he did not make an eruv until after Barchu, he may make an eruv so long as the congregation has not prayed the Yom Tov prayer; see there. And Yad Aharon agreed with Magen Avraham; see there. And it appears to me that one who is in the synagogue and has not set eruv tavshilin, and if he goes home to set eruv tavshilin the time for Minchah will pass: he should pray Minchah and not set eruv tavshilin, for he can transfer ownership of his flour to others (Magen Avraham).
