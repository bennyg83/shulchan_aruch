In Raavan siman 168 it is stated: an individual who comes to synagogue and finds the community already accepted Shabbat since they prayed "You have sanctified" — the individual prays Maariv twice, for since the community accepted Shabbat he cannot pray weekday. It appears also: one who forgot and did not make eruv tavshilin on erev Yom Tov for Shabbat and remembered when the shamash said Barchu, and made eruv before the community prayed Yom Tov prayer — his eruv is an eruv, for acceptance of Yom Tov is not until Yom Tov prayer — end quote. It is clear: at first he wrote regarding Shabbat that when the congregation prayed "You have sanctified" it is acceptance of Shabbat and the individual may not pray weekday; he then says regarding this too the individual is drawn after the community, for after they prayed Yom Tov prayer he may not make eruv. It is clear he holds Barchu is not acceptance also for Shabbat, no difference between Yom Tov and Shabbat; but per what we hold siman 261 seif 4 that Barchu is acceptance of Shabbat, and likewise regarding the individual not praying weekday as Magen Avraham siman 263 s.k. 28 — so too for eruv tavshilin, as Magen Avraham here s.k. 1 states simply.
