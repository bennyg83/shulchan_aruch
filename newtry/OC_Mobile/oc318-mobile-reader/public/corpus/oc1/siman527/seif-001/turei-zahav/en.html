In a separate pot. For in one pot he may cook as much as he wants. So wrote the Tur, and so wrote Rashi on daf 17: he should not employ evasion after he has cooked for his need, saying, 'I still need to invite others,' and then cook other dishes and leave them for Shabbos. But in one pot, if he adds, we say that even for a weekday it is permitted, as above, that a woman may fill a pot with meat, etc. This means that in one pot, even if he uses evasion and adds more, it is permitted. Tosafot there also cited this. One should not ask, if so, why the blind scholar in the Gemara there was distressed that he had not placed an eruv tavshilin, since there was a permitted device of adding in one pot for Shabbos. One can answer that he needed to cook a dish for Shabbos that could not be cooked in the dish he was cooking in this Yom Tov pot; so it seems to me.
