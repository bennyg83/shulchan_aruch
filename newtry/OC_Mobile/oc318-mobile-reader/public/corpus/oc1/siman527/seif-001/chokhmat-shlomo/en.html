(Se'if 1) Yom Tov that falls on erev Shabbos, etc. Note: Behold, the dispute is clear in chapter 3 of Pesachim: according to Rabbah, the needs of Shabbos are not prepared on Yom Tov, and the main permit is by force of ho'il; and according to Rav Chisda we do not say ho'il, only that the needs of Shabbos are prepared on Yom Tov. And I find difficulty from the Gemara of Menachos, in a beraisa in the chapter of R' Yishmael, where it is clear in the beraisa how many roundabouts the Tannaim circled there to reject the words of the Tzedukim who say that Atzeres is after Shabbos; and the difficulty is: behold, the main claim of the Tzedukim that Atzeres is after Shabbos is because Moshe Rabbeinu loved Israel and saw that Atzeres is one day, so he arose and enacted it for after Shabbos; and they should have rejected their words more simply: for if so, that Moshe Rabbeinu loved Israel, why then did he enact Atzeres on erev Shabbos, so that there would also be two days; and furthermore it is better, for then one can prepare from Yom Tov for Shabbos, but if it were after Shabbos one cannot prepare from Shabbos for Yom Tov; and of necessity their nonsense is not so; so why did they not reject their words with this? But according to the view that the needs of Shabbos are not prepared on Yom Tov except by force of ho'il, it fits well: for Tosafos wrote in Pesachim that wherever it is possible without ho'il one should not act by means of ho'il; if so, here too, if he would enact that Atzeres be on erev Shabbos, he would be able to prepare the needs of Shabbos only by means of ho'il; therefore he enacted it all the more after Shabbos, for then one can prepare the needs of Yom Tov on Yom Tov itself and will not need ho'il. But according to the view that the needs of Shabbos are prepared on Yom Tov without ho'il, the difficulty remains as above, that they should have rejected the Tzedukim with this; and there is a difficulty for that view from the aforementioned beraisa of Menachos; and this requires a bit of further study; examine carefully. And see Magen Avraham here, s.k. 1, what he wrote in the name of Beis Yosef, and what Yad Shaul challenged him about it; and see my composition on Yoreh De'ah, Mahadura Tinyana, siman 157, in my responsum to the holy congregation of Zerawna, where I wrote a bit of proof from the Gemara that we do not hold like the view that we say ho'il, or that one may say that regarding consecrated matters we do not say ho'il; see there and examine carefully. And see also my composition on Choshen Mishpat, Mahadura Tinyana, siman 113, where I brought a proof from the Rambam that he likewise holds that the halacha follows Rav Chisda and not Rabbah; see there what emerges for us from the pilpul, and examine carefully.
