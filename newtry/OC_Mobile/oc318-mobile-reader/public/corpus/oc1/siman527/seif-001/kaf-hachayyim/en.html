1) [Se'if 1] When Yom Tov falls on Friday, etc. There is no distinction between the first day of Yom Tov and the second day observed in the Diaspora. When the second day of Yom Tov in the Diaspora falls on Friday, it is likewise forbidden to cook for Shabbos in a separate pot without an eruv tavshilin prepared before the first day of Yom Tov, as written below, se'if 22.
