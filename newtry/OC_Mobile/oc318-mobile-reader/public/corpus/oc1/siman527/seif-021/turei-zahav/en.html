And he leaves over for Shabbos. In Rashi it is written that even for a weekday it is permitted, as above, that a woman may fill a pot with meat, etc.
Provided that he eats from each one. It seems to me that this certainly refers to the initial practice, that it should be visible to all that he eats from it on Yom Tov. But if it happened that he did not eat at all from one type, it is not forbidden for Shabbos, since at the time of cooking he intended to eat it that day, and his action was therefore permitted. The fact that he did not eat afterward does not cause prohibition. Even with Yom Tov dishes, a person often leaves an entire pot and does not taste it because he is satisfied. In siman 503 the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Rivash that even after eating, if one transgressed and cooked by evasion with an olive's bulk, it does not have the status of forbidden evasion, and so he ruled there in Shulchan Aruch. All the more so here, where he cooks permissibly before the meal. See what I wrote at the end of this siman.
