(1) If he remembered, etc. Binyan Shlomo wrote: if he made a special dish for eruv tavshilin or designated it for that purpose, even if he intended to bless on it and forgot — permitted to cook, for blessings do not obstruct; and even if his wife designated without his knowledge — permitted — up to here; requires study why this is better than one who said and did not specify melachot explicitly forbidden as s.k. 20; and what he wrote blessings do not obstruct means if he said properly "may it be," etc., and did not say the blessing — even though on erev Pesach permitted b'dieved without any statement, there the main action is joint participation; but here speech is primary to clarify a fine portion for Shabbat; therefore it appears one may not rely on this. Further he wrote nowadays if one forgot and did not make eruv — permitted, for the eruv text says "for us and all Israel" — if so each eruvs for all Israel; Bach wrote so; they did not examine people's actions well — we hold one must merit as s.k. 10; no person does this except one expert — therefore do not rely on this, siman 529:
