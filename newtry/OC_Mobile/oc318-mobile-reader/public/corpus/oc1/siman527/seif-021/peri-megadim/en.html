On Yom Tov. See Taz. If he remembered during bein hashmashos at the beginning of the second day, although there is a double doubt, perhaps it is still daytime and his stipulation is effective, and perhaps the second day of Yom Tov is a weekday, nevertheless, for us who are expert in establishing the calendar, one may say that it is ineffective and that an eruv tavshilin may not be set aside in this manner.
