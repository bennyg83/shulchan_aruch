(Seif katan 21) The melachos, etc. Bach writes, and Taz and a responsum of Rosh likewise write, etc. Taz, seif katan 11, states that one may not slaughter on the first day of Yom Tov for Shabbos, but only on the second day. This implies that slaughtering on the second day for Shabbos is permitted, even though slaughter is likewise not mentioned in Rosh's formula.
Nevertheless, it appears proper, etc. Chemed Moshe writes that it is insufficient for the slaughterer alone to mention slaughtering. Every householder should mention it, because if he himself later needs to slaughter and did not mention slaughtering, it is as though he made no eruv for that act. Others would then also be forbidden to slaughter for him except what is needed for basic sustenance, since whatever he may not do, others may not do for him. Although Magen Avraham writes in siman 498, seif katan 16, that nowadays the custom is not to slaughter on Yom Tov, this remains relevant where slaughter is necessary or where an animal is dangerously ill, in which case he writes that one may be lenient.
