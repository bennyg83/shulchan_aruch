21) Mahari Weil would place the cooked meat upon a platter and not upon the loaf, so that the meat would not make the loaf repulsive. Derashos Mahari Weil, Hilchos Eruv Tavshilin; Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, subsection 2; Eliyah Rabbah there; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 11. It is proper to use hard-boiled eggs for the cooked food, because they keep in hot climates, whereas meat will not keep in hot weather for two days and two nights. Ben Ish Chai, parashas Tzav, subsection 1. If eggs are unavailable, one should use roasted meat, because well-roasted meat keeps longer, as is known.
