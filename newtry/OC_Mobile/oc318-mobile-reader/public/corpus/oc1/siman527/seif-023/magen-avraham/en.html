Or unintentionally. This is difficult, for what is the Rama teaching? Since if he acted intentionally it is permitted, all the more so if he acted unintentionally. In the Yam Shel Shlomo he wrote that nevertheless, when it was unintentional, there is concern for evasion, that he will say, 'I was unintentional,' after he intentionally transgressed and baked. Therefore one should penalize him, since it resembles evasion and there is concern that he will transgress regularly. For this reason the Or Zarua wrote that everything depends on what the sage sees fit. Perhaps the Rama came to teach that if it was unintentional it is permitted in every case, unlike the Or Zarua.
