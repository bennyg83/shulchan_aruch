[23] [clothing] and no more, etc. Interpretation For the purpose of Shabbat, one is not allowed anymore, but for the purpose of a good day, such as he has not yet made a need for today, it is permissible, and if he has already done for a good day, he can put it aside for the purpose of Shabbat and make it from then on for a good day, as will be explained in the section after this, and he did not comer except for the need of the Shabbat.
