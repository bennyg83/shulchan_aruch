Seif 3, "even [if]," etc. Magen Avraham.

"The same [applies]," etc., "specifically," etc. For there is no prohibition in his wearing it, for it is not written "you shall not wear," etc., rather the mitzvah is to make tzitzis on his garment; therefore on Shabbos when it is impossible he does not violate, unlike on a weekday. Proof from what I wrote regarding karmelis as rabbinic and they were not concerned for nullification of the positive command.
