If on Shabbat while in a karmelis he discovers that the tallis on him is invalid, he does not remove it until he reaches home — because of human dignity. Rema: Even a tallis katan under his garments he need not remove; the same if one of the tzitzis tore and he is ashamed to sit without a tallis — he may wear it without a blessing due to human dignity (Beit Yosef citing responsum). Specifically on Shabbat when tying tzitzis is forbidden; on a weekday in such a case it is forbidden [to wear invalid tzitzis] (Mordechai first chapter 91a).
