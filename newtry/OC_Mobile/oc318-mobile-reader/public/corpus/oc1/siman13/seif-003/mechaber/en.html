If on Shabbat while in a karmelis he discovers that the tallis on him is invalid, he does not remove it until he reaches home — because of kevod habriyos.{Rama: Even a tallis katan under his garments he need not remove; the same if one of the tzitzis tore and he is ashamed to sit without a tallis — he may wear it without a blessing on account of kevod habriyos (Beit Yosef citing responsum).) Specifically on Shabbos when making tzitzis is forbidden; but on a weekday in such a case it is forbidden (Mordechai first chapter 91a).}.
