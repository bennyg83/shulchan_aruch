If on Shabbat while in a <i>karmelis</i> he discovers that the <i>tallis</i> on him is invalid, he does not remove it until he reaches home — because of human dignity. Rema: Even a <i>tallis katan</i> under his garments he need not remove; the same if one of the <i>tzitzis</i> tore and he is ashamed to sit without a <i>tallis</i> — he may wear it without a blessing due to human dignity (Beit Yosef citing responsum). Specifically on Shabbat when tying <i>tzitzis</i> is forbidden; on a weekday in such a case it is forbidden [to wear invalid <i>tzitzis</i>] (Mordechai first chapter 91a). ) .
