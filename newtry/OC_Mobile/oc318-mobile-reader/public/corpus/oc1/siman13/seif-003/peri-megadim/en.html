And he need not inspect it. See Taz, who follows his own view in siman 8, seif 9, seif katan 8, that inspection is required because of the severe prohibition of uttering Hashem’s Name in a blessing recited in vain. If one owns many garments, the strict law requires inspection of only one of them. See Magen Avraham there, seif katan 11, and here, seif katan 5. The statement concerning one called to the Torah means that he may even recite the blessing over the tallis without inspection. According to the strict law this is permitted; inspection is required only because of the severity of a blessing recited in vain, and the Sages did not impose that stringency where it would delay the congregation. Taz in Yoreh Deah 279, seif katan 2, cites the Gemara in Yoma. Orchos Chayim, siman 8, seif katan 10, in the name of Mateh Moshe, siman 15, writes that he saw Maharshal inspect the tallis every time he was called to the Torah; see there. Nowadays people are not careful about this, in accordance with Taz.
