Mishbetzos Zahav on Taz — and he follows his approach in siman 8:69 note 8 there, on account of the stringency of "do not bear sin"; if he has many garments he need not inspect by law more than one, Magen Avraham there 11; and here 5. What he wrote "one called to Torah," etc. — meaning even to bless on it he may without inspection, for by law it is permitted, only the stringency of "do not bear sin," and burdening the congregation they did not adopt. What he wrote Yoreh Deah 279:56 note 2 brought the Yoma Gemara. Orach Chayyim siman 8 note 10 in Maggid Mishneh 15 saw from Maharash that he inspected the tallis each time he read Torah — see there; nowadays people are not careful like 16.
