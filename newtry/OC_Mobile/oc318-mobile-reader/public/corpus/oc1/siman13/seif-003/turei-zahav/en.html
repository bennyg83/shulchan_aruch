When in karmelis. Meaning in a place where carrying is forbidden only rabbinically, as explained in laws of Shabbos — for nowadays we have no reshus harabim.

Specifically on Shabbos. Per responsa Beit Yosef cites — permitted even on a weekday; Mordechai in the name of R. Yehuda — Beit Yosef also brought — explains specifically Shabbos because one cannot then make tzitzis; proof: if the Torah had written "do not wear a four-cornered garment without tzitzis" one would violate wearing even on Shabbos; but since the positive commandment is only to affix tzitzis when wearing, therefore one does not violate wearing on Shabbos — but on a weekday one violates the positive command to affix tzitzis — requires further study. If so, why did Rama give the reason of kevod habriyos? For R. Yehuda permitted anyway for the reason we mentioned. Perhaps since Mordechai disagrees with R. Yehuda, Rama ruled like R. Yehuda on account of kevod habriyos; kevod habriyos alone does not suffice, for this is not such embarrassment as Beit Yosef wrote. One could answer from end of chapter HaKometz: Mar brei deRav Ashi's tzitzis tore on Shabbos — if not for kevod habriyos he would have removed his cloak even in the market; but on Shabbos there is no prohibition — there it deals with carrying prohibition like a load in karmelis — therefore kevod habriyos was needed; but here it deals with private domain.
