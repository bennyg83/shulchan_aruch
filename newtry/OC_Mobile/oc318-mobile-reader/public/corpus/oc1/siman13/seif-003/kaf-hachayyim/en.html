3) There: It is permitted to go out into the public domain, etc. One who has a four-cornered silk garment in honor of Shabbat and does not recite a blessing over it, but instead recites the blessing only over a wool garment, as Bach writes in siman 9, may go out wearing it into the public domain nowadays. For several reasons, our public domain is treated as a karmelit on Shabbat. Rabbi Shvut Yaakov writes this in Responsa, Orach Chayim, siman 5. Yad Aharon cites him in Mahadura Batra, Hagahot HaTur, ot 2, as do Birkei Yosef, ot 4, and Eshel Avraham, siman 10, ot 2. See below, ot 7.
