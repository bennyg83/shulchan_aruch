(4) Permitted to go out — even at night which is not time for tzitzis, because it adorns the garment. Even tallitot of other materials — even according to those who hold above siman 9:1 that the obligation is only rabbinic — nevertheless they adorn the garment since in any case one is obligated in them as a certainty, and it is not similar to a garment half open and half closed which Shulchan Aruch ruled above siman 10:7 one may not go out with on Shabbos, for there the obligation is only from doubt [Peri Megadim and Orach Chayyim, unlike Shev Yaakov; afterward I found Olas Tamid wrote likewise].

(5) Tekhelet — for we hold tekhelet and white do not obstruct one another, as it is written "and you shall see it" — each one is implied separately.

(6) On his shoulders — it is not an adornment but carrying, for that is not the manner of wearing on weekdays. However if it covers most of his body it suffices. See below siman 301:30.

(7) Before he goes out — since he checked in the morning when reciting the blessing on wearing and found them intact, it is not common for them to sever in such a short time even if he removed it in between [Magen Avraham]. This proves that on Shabbos too one must check tzitzis; Bach wrote explicitly in siman 8, but people are not careful. See Biur Halacha.
