(2) [Seif 2] If it was made tzitzis according to law it is permitted to go out in it to the public domain, etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote in a responsum that it is forbidden to go out in a tallis with tzitzis on Shabbat night, since night is not the time for tzitzis. R. David ben Messer Leon in a manuscript—Knesset HaGedolah brought it in Hagah Beit Yosef, and so Rabbeinu Yeshayah the First in his rulings manuscript chapter Tolim—Birkhei Yosef brought it letter 2. However Beit Yosef siman 18 wrote it is permitted to go out even at night when it is not the time for tzitzis, because tzitzis are not a burden but adornment of the garment and its ornaments—so too there Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Radbaz part 1 siman 244, who is among those who permit: although night is not the time for tzitzis, nevertheless it is a garment for every Jew since it is made tzitzis according to law—end quote. They also brought it in Shach Knesset HaGedolah letter 4, who wrote: although our author in the short book omitted what the Rambam wrote whether by day or night—not because he holds night is forbidden, but he simply wrote it is permitted to go out in it, and his words imply both by day and by night; and so I agreed in my responsum, and so the people spoke—end quote. So too Eshel Orot letters 3 and 4, Magen Avraham s.k. 1, Erech HaShulchan letter 2, Raz letter 2, Ikvei HaDat siman 2 letter 70, Shitilei Zetim letter 3—see siman 18 letter 1 where we wrote more on this; and even in those types where the obligation of tzitzis does not apply in them except rabbinically, it is permitted to go out in them to the public domain—Eshel Orot there letter 3.
