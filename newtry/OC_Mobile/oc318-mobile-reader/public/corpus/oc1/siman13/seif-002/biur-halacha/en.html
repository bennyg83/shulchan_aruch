Perhaps the reason for the custom is: why would inspection help—for even if he does not find them proper he would be permitted to wear on Shabbos in the synagogue, as Rama ruled in seif 3. But this is not resolved except according to Magen Avraham, that the reason for inspection is so he not go in a garment without tzitzis; but according to what Taz wrote, that the reason for inspection is the stringency of "do not take [the Name] in vain" in a blessing, therefore he should inspect, and if he finds them invalid he wears and does not bless—and even for Magen Avraham this reason applies specifically in the synagogue. But if he wears the tallis at home—for example where there is no eruv arrangement in the city and the custom is to wear the tallis at home—there certainly inspection is needed for two reasons: one, so he not go in a garment without tzitzis; and also that he wants to go in it to a karmelis or the public domain. The correct practice is that on erev Shabbos he inspect the tallis when placing it in its bag, and then he need not inspect on Shabbos, as Magen Avraham ruled above in siman 8—see there.
