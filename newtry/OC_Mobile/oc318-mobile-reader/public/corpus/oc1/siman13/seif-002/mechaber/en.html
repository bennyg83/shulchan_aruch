If it is properly “made tzitzis,” one is permitted to go out in it to the public domain — whether a small tallis or tallis gadol, even nowadays when we have no tekheles — provided it is not resting [only] on his shoulders (words of Ran and Rosh Chullin 108b). We uphold the tallis with its presumption [chazaka] that it is properly made tzitzis and need not inspect before going out (Responsa Rosh klal 2; Responsa Ramban siman 215; Kol Bo citing Rambam Shabbat 19). .
