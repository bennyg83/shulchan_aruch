Mishbetzos Zahav on Taz and Magen Avraham note 4 — we conclude that rabbinically a tallis of mitzvah — even if not normally worn that way — a tallis of mitzvah counts as normal wearing and is permitted, we conclude. Or one could say rabbinically tzitzis is carrying, but on Shabbos it is permitted because a positive commandment defers rabbinic prohibition — see R' Meir chapter 19 Mishnah 8 and Maggid Mishneh there — and it is fully permitted even wearing a tallis on his shoulders; we conclude it is forbidden: first, tzitzis cannot apply to another matter, unlike tallis carrying which was not permitted; second, tzitzis does not defer Shabbos. That which is permitted to go out in reshus harabim is because they adorn the garment and its lining, as Rambam wrote there — therefore permitted even at night. Magen Avraham note 1 on this, and there it will be explained, with God's help.
