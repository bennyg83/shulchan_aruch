To go out. See Baer Heitev and Shevus Yaakov, siman 5, who rules stringently against walking on Shabbos in a four-cornered silk garment with tzitzis, even in a city whose eruv has not been arranged to permit carrying. See above, siman 10, where Eshel Avraham cites this at some length. See Responsa Beit Ephraim, Orach Chayyim, siman 26, regarding whether nowadays alleys open at both ends to the royal highway have the status of a public domain. See Shaagas Aryeh, siman 58: In a public domain, the prohibition is not overridden for human dignity. The same applies to kilayim; one must inform the wearer, and human dignity does not override it.
