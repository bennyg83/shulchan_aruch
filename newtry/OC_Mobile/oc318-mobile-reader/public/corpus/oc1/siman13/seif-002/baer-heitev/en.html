To go out with it into a public domain. Even at night, which is not the time for the mitzvah of tzitzis, because they are an adornment of the garment. Beit Yosef, siman 11. See responsa Maharil, siman 102.
A small tallis. Responsa Rama, siman 110, discusses whether one may go out wearing a small four-cornered garment that lacks the minimum size stated in siman 16.
Upon his shoulders. Meaning, not in the manner in which he ordinarily wears it during the week. It is then not considered clothing, but a burden. See siman 301.
Before going out with it. Regarding the blessing, we are more stringent, as stated above in siman 8. See Magen Avraham and Olas Tamid, and below, siman 18, seif katan 9, for what is written there.
