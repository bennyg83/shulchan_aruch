“Permitted to go out.” Even at night, not time for tzitzis, because they adorn the garment (Beis Yosef siman 18).

“A child.” Rema responsum 110 discusses whether one may go out in a small four-cornered garment lacking the measure written in siman 16—see there.

“Tekheles.” We hold tekheles does not hold back the white.

“On his shoulders.” Meaning not the way he wears it on weekdays—see siman 41:36.

“Its presumption.” If you ask: we ruled inspection is needed at the berachah and we do not rely on chazakah, as siman 8:9—one can answer there is different: if not checked at berachah time it would never be checked and certainly one cannot forever rely on chazakah since they snap; but here once checked at berachah he need not check every time he goes out. From this learn: one who left synagogue and removed his tallis, even if he interrupted and needs a berachah when donning again—even so he need not inspect.
