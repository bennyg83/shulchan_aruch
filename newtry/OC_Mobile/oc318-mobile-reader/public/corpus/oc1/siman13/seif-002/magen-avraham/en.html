Permitted to go out. Even at night when it is not the time for tzitzis, because they adorn the garment [Beit Yosef siman 18].

Small [tallis]. In responsa of Rama siman 110 he discusses whether one is permitted to go out in a small four-cornered [garment] that does not have the measure written in siman 16—see there.

Tekheles. For we hold tekheles does not hold back the white.

On his shoulders. Meaning not in the manner of its wearing on weekdays—see siman 301 s. 36.

Its presumption. If you ask: here we ruled inspection is required at the blessing and we do not establish presumption, as siman 8:9 — one can answer that case is different, for if they do not inspect at blessing time they will never inspect, and certainly one cannot forever establish presumption since they are prone to break; but here, since they inspected at blessing time, he need not inspect each time he goes out. From this we learn: one who left beis haknesses and removed his tallis — even if he diverted his attention and needs a blessing when donning again — even so he need not inspect.
