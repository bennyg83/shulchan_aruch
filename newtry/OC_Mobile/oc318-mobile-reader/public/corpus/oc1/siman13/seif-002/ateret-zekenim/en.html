Even nowadays when we do not have tekheles—meaning: when there was tekheles and one was liable for a sin-offering on Shabbos, therefore the tzitzis are significant and are not nullified with respect to the tallis and they are a burden. Even in our time when there is no tekheles, even though one might think the tzitzis are nullified with respect to the tallis since they are not significant—in any event we say they are significant and are not nullified. One who goes out on Shabbos in a tallis that is not made tzitzis is liable for a sin-offering, because the tzitzis are considered a burden.
