Even nowadays, when we do not have techeiles. Meaning, in the time when techeiles was available, carrying invalid tzitzis incurred liability for a chatas. Therefore, the tzitzis were significant and did not become subordinate to the tallis, and they constituted a burden. The same is true even nowadays, when there is no techeiles. One might have thought that the tzitzis are subordinate to the tallis because they are not so significant, but this is not so.
