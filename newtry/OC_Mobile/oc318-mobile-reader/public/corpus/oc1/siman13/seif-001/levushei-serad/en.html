Magen Avraham, seif katan 8. The reason the Shulchan Aruch specifies Shabbos relates to the beginning of Rama’s gloss, “and even a tallis katan,” which refers back to the Mechaber’s discussion of Shabbos. Magen Avraham answers that the ruling teaches a greater novelty. He did not question the Mechaber, for the Mechaber discusses a tallis gadol, whose removal is not so humiliating.
