{Rama: Magen Avraham s.k. 8: That which Shulchan Aruch specifies Shabbos refers to what Rama said at the beginning of the gloss—even a tallis katan, which refers to the Mechaber's words that deal with Shabbos; on this he answered that it teaches an extra case. But on the Mechaber Magen Avraham did not question at all, for the Mechaber deals with a tallis gadol.}
