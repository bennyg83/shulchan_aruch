(1) [Seif 1] Four tzitzis obstruct one another, etc. And one who goes out in it to the public domain on Shabbos is liable for a sin-offering, for it is a burden; and one should not say they are nullified with respect to the garment, for they are significant to it since his intention is to complete them on it. So too Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and the Rambam.

(1) Therefore if all four tzitzis snapped and nothing remains sufficient for a knot, certainly he will make others in their place—they are nullified with respect to the garment and permitted to go out with them on Shabbos—Taz s.k. 1. So too Eshel Orot letter 2. However from the words of Levush siman 301 seif 38 who wrote: if it has no tzitzis at all it is permitted to go out in it, etc.—it implies if it has some, even without sufficient for a knot, it is forbidden to go out in it. So too Eliyah Rabbah s.k. 1, who wrote there: it appears even for Taz this was only because of the Shabbos prohibition it is permitted, but nevertheless because of nullification of the mitzvah of tzitzis it is forbidden, unless in a case permitted because of human dignity as below—and so in Levush there. So too Raz s.k. 1, Shitilei Zetim letter 2; see what Maamar Mordechai wrote letter 1, and Mishnat Hasidim letter 1; and further see in Pischei Olam letter 1 who wrote in the name of Biur Halacha on the aforementioned words of Taz that it is astonishing, since he will snap them—nullification with respect to the garment does not apply—end quote; therefore one should be strict like Levush and Eliyah Rabbah.
