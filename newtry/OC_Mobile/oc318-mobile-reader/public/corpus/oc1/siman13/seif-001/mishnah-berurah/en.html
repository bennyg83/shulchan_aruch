(1) One to another — for all together are one mitzvah; if even one tzitzis is missing, it is as if he affixed none at all and completely nullified the positive commandment of tzitzis.

(2) To reshus harabim — and to karmelis is forbidden rabbinically; the same applies to all other invalid cases, such as if the majority of the garment was closed, or the tzitzis were placed not in their proper place — higher than three fingerbreadths, or lower than a thumbbreadth from the thumb-knot — and all similar cases; or if the four corners were smaller than the measure in siman 16, see there. In all these it is forbidden to go out, even to karmelis rabbinically. Regarding a garment half open in gathering, see above siman 10 in Mishna Berurah 27.

(3) Liable for chatat — for it is carrying on account of the remaining tzitzis, and they are not nullified to the garment because tzitzis threads are significant and not nullified. Even if those tzitzis are also invalid, nevertheless as long as some valid threads remain on them his mind is on them and they are significant and not nullified — unless all threads of all four tzitzis were severed and not enough remained to make a loop, for then they are nullified to the garment. Therefore if he learned this on Shabbos while walking in reshus harabim, he need not remove his tallis.
