(1) One another. All four tzitzis form a single mitzvah. If even one tzitzis is missing, it is as though none were attached, and the positive commandment of tzitzis is entirely unfulfilled.
(2) Into a public domain. Carrying it into a karmelis is forbidden D’Rabbanan. The same applies to every other form of invalidity, such as where most of the garment is closed, the tzitzis were attached in the wrong location, above three fingerbreadths or below the distance from the thumb joint, or the four-cornered garment is smaller than the minimum size stated in siman 16. In all these cases one may not go out wearing it even into a karmelis. Regarding a garment exactly half open and half closed, see Mishnah Berurah above, siman 10, seif katan 27.
(3) He is liable for a chatas. The remaining tzitzis constitute a burden and are not subordinate to the garment, because tzitzis strings are significant. Even if those tzitzis are themselves invalid, so long as some usable strings remain, he intends to retain them and they remain significant. If, however, all the strings of all four tzitzis were severed and none retained enough to tie a bow, they become subordinate to the garment. Therefore, if he discovers this on Shabbos while walking in a public domain, he need not remove the tallis.
