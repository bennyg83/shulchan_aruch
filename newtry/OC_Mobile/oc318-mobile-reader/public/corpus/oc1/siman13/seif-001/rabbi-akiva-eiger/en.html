{Rama: Seif 2.} Whether tallis katan or tallis gadol. See language of Rosh brought in Beit Yosef on our tallitot: even if not made tzitzis properly, permitted to go out in them to reshus harabim, for presumption — if there were tzitzis it would have tzitzis; if not, nullified to the garment since he does not intend to complete them, see there. There is to examine this from the above siman 10:7 regarding garments that are open — we require them stringently and one may not go out with them on Shabbos. Say also here presumption — if not obligated in tzitzis, nevertheless nullified to the garment since he does not intend to complete — requires further study. See responsum Shev Yaakov siman 5; it appears to me the threads are significant and considered intent to complete means opening the garment to obligate certainly in tzitzis — untie the tzitzis, separate them, and affix them on the garment so there not be contradiction.
