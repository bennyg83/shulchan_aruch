He is liable for a chatas. Because they constitute a burden. One cannot say that they are subordinate to the garment, because they are significant to him, since he intends to complete them. Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos. According to this, if all four tzitzis were severed and none retained enough to tie a bow, so that he will certainly replace them, they are subordinate to the garment and he may go out wearing it.
