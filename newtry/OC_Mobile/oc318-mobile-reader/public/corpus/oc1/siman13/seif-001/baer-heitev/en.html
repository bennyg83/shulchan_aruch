Liable for a sin-offering. For it is a burden; and one should not say they are nullified with respect to the garment, for they are significant to it since his intention is to complete them on it—Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos. Therefore if all four tzitzis were severed and nothing remains of them sufficient for a knot, certainly he will make others in their place, so they are nullified with respect to the garment and it is permitted to go out with them on Shabbos. Taz ad loc.
