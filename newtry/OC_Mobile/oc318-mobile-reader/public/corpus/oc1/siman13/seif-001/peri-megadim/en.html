Beit Yosef writes similarly in the name of Tosafos and Rambam in siman 301, though he does not mention it here. Tosafos on Shabbos 139b, s.v. “Tzitzis,” explains that the remaining strings are significant because he intends to complete the fourth tzitzis. Rambam, chapter 19 of Hilchos Shabbos, halachah 20, adds that even if three tzitzis are invalid, so long as strings remain fit for completion they do not become subordinate to the garment, because tzitzis strings are significant. Rashi on Shabbos there, s.v. “They are significant,” and Menachos 37b, s.v. “For Rav Huna,” appears to imply that even if all the tzitzis are invalid, they do not become subordinate. It may be explained that Rashi carefully speaks of strings attached in an invalid manner while the strings themselves remain fit, so that they can later be attached validly. Likewise, on Shabbos, his reference specifically to techeiles follows Rabbi Yishmael’s view that there are four separate mitzvos. When he speaks of a garment not properly fringed, entirely invalid, he may refer to a case where the loose portions were cut away and nothing remained. Even then, techeiles strings are significant, and one will untie and reattach them. According to this explanation, Rashi and Tosafos do not disagree. I discussed this at length in my commentary to Tur. Accordingly, if all four tzitzis were severed and none retained enough to tie a bow, the strings become subordinate to the garment, even though they are not among the garment’s ornaments, just like loose threads protruding from a garment. See Rambam, Hilchos Shabbos, there. One cannot object that he may untie and reattach them, for nowadays, when we have no techeiles and tzitzis are readily available, the severed strings are not sufficiently significant. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 1. According to what I have written, the matter is resolved. This also explains the ruling in siman 10, seif 7, that one may not go out on Shabbos wearing a garment less than half open, because the strings remain significant. Magen Avraham will explain this further. What Magen Avraham writes in siman 12, seif katan 7, concerning all ends being within the wound section refers to a case where three tzitzis remain valid, and the strings therefore do not become subordinate. The same applies if all the ends of all four tzitzis remain within the wound section: one may go out wearing it on Shabbos, for either they are valid or the strings become subordinate, as I have written.
