Liable. Mishbetzos Zahav on Taz: Here the master of blessed memory spoke briefly; in my view the matter needs expansion. For Beit Yosef likewise wrote in the name of Tosafos and Rambam in Shulchan Aruch, and here he did not mention this. Tosafos are on Shabbos 139b s.v. tzitzis — they are significant that one will make a quarter; and Rambam of blessed memory in chapter 19 of Shabbat Laws added that even three invalid ones — as long as there are threads fit to complete — are not nullified, for tzitzis threads are significant (and Rashi on Shabbos there s.v. significant, and Menachot 37b s.v. Rav Huna explains that even if all are invalid they are not nullified; one can say Rashi's precision there is: made in invalid status but the threads valid and one will fix properly; and on Shabbos where he explains tekhelet specifically — that is R. Yehuda's view of four mitzvos, and "not made tzitzis properly" — all invalid — meaning if one scraped them and no knot-measure remained, nevertheless tekhelet is significant and one may untie and retie. See Mishbetzos Zahav 12:3 in a manner that Rashi and Tosafos do not disagree — understand; and in my Biur on Tur I expanded). Accordingly, if all four tzitzis tore and no knot-measure remained — they are nullified even though the garment lacks adornment comparable to threads protruding from the garment — see R' Meir, laws of Shabbos there; and one could say one may untie and tie, for they are not so significant nowadays when there is no tekhelet and tzitzis are adornments. Eshel Avraham on Rama 1, and what I wrote, see there. With this it is reconciled in siman 10:7 — less than half one may not go out on Shabbos, for seeds are significant; Magen Avraham will explain further; and what Magen Avraham wrote 12:7 — knot-measure, palm of the hand — addresses where three valid tzitzis exist that they are not nullified; and likewise if all have knot-measure one may go out on Shabbos in any case as I wrote. Magen Avraham, note 1.
