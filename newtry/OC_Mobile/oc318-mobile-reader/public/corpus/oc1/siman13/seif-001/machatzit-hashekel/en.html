(Seif katan 1) It is permitted to go out. Even at night, etc. Meaning, according to the opinion that even a garment designated for daytime is exempt from tzitzis when worn at night, as stated below in siman 18. Regarding Taz’s statement in seif katan 1 that if all four tzitzis were severed one may go out wearing the garment and there is no prohibition of carrying because the strings are subordinate to the garment, there nevertheless remains the prohibition of wearing a garment without tzitzis. Although it is Shabbos, a D’Rabbanan prohibition still applies, as Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 9, unless human dignity is involved. In any event, there is no Torah prohibition of carrying nowadays, since we have no true public domain, as Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 6, and Taz agrees below in siman 346. The case must therefore concern one in which human dignity does not apply. Even if there remains a prohibition against wearing a garment without tzitzis, there is still a practical distinction regarding a borrowed tallis, where the owner did not transfer it as a gift on condition that it be returned, as is evident from siman 14, seif 3; see Taz there.
