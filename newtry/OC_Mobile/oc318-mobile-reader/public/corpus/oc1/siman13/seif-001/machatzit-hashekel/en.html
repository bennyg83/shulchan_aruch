(s.k. 1) Permitted to go out. Even at night, etc.—meaning for the one who says even a garment designated for day if he wore it at night he is exempt, as below siman 18. And according to what Taz wrote s.k. 1: if all four tzitzis snapped it is permitted to go out in them and there is no issue of carrying, for they are nullified with respect to the garment. And although there is still a prohibition of wearing a garment without tzitzis, and even though it is Shabbos, nevertheless there is a rabbinic prohibition, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 9. And if there is an issue of human dignity. If so, in any case there is no issue of carrying since there is no eruv techumin, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 6, and Taz agreed below siman 346. One must say it deals where there is no issue of human dignity. And if the prohibition of wearing a garment without tzitzis—nevertheless there is a difference for a borrowed tallis; and in our case he did not acquire it for him through a gift—see there, and as understood from siman 14 seif 3, and see Taz.
