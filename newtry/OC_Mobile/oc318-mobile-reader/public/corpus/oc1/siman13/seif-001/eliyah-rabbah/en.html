[1] He is liable for a chatas, etc. Because they constitute a burden. One cannot say that they are subordinate to the garment, because they are significant, since he intends to complete them. Beit Yosef. Taz infers that if all four tzitzis were severed and none retained enough to tie a bow, so that he will certainly replace them, they are subordinate to the garment and one may go out wearing it. If, however, enough remains to tie a bow, they remain significant because he intends to complete them, and they are not subordinate to the garment. See Taz at length.
