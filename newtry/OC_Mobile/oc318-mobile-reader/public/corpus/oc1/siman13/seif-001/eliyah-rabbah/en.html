[1] Liable for a sin-offering, etc. For it is a burden; and one should not say they are nullified with respect to the garment, for they are significant to it since his intention is to complete them on it—Beit Yosef. Taz was precise that if all four tzitzis snapped and nothing remains sufficient for a knot, certainly he will make others—they are nullified with respect to the garment and permitted to go out on Shabbos—until here. And according to what I wrote in his name at the beginning of siman 12, if so it is possible he still needs to tie them after removing them from the garment like other tzitzis strings, and it requires analysis. Also Levush siman 301 seif 38 wrote: if it has no tzitzis at all it is permitted—it implies if it has some, even without sufficient for a knot, it is forbidden. It appears even for Taz this was only permitted because of the Shabbos prohibition, but nevertheless because of nullification of the mitzvah of tzitzis it is forbidden, unless in a case permitted because of human dignity as below—and so it appears in Levush siman 301 there.
