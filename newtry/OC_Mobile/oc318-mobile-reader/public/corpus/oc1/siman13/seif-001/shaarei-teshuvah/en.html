Liable to a sin-offering. See Baer Heitev, Birkei Yosef, and Machazik Berachah. They write that according to Rambam, the Torah does not prescribe a fixed number of strings. If one string was severed, the tzitzis are disqualified only rabbinically. They therefore question whether a person who goes into the public domain wearing this tallis is liable to a sin-offering; see their lengthy discussion.
