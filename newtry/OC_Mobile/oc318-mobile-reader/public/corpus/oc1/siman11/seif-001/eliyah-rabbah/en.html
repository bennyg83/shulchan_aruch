But one could question: from what the Bach wrote in siman 14 — this is his language: "We hold that whether in spinning or in making, even if an Israelite stands over him, they are invalid" — until here. It is also difficult for me: according to this, even with assistance it is invalid, for the Bach ruled in Yoreh De'ah that assistance has no substance and neither raises nor lowers. Yet how can it be valid when an Israelite stands over him alone, even without assistance? See below siman 14, subsection 1. Magen Avraham wrote: regarding whether a deaf-mute, fool, minor, or adult spun and an adult stands over them — it depends on a dispute that Beit Yosef wrote in siman 460. Until here. In my humble opinion, one may be more lenient here, for there the Shulchan Aruch ruled plainly that it is invalid with kutim and an Israelite standing over them, whereas here he wrote two opinions. And thus Rama's view is proven.
