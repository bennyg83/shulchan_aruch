Spin for their sake. See Be'er Heitev, and see there in siman 460 that Taz's view is that although a minor is not thirteen years old, if he has understanding to comprehend what this one tells him — that he should do as God commanded in the portion of Yaakov — that is the main point per Maharil, that they should be thirteen years and one day; and this means that a minor and an adult standing over him do not help, and this is the view of Rashba that Beit Yosef brought there, and Taz is lenient because in Rashba it states that the matter depends on understanding. And this is astonishing, for it appears there that a minor is called such until he reaches years of maturity, similar to slaughter and divorce, see there; and what he wrote that it depends on a dispute, etc. — see Magen Avraham, who wrote that it depends on a dispute that Beit Yosef wrote in siman 460, and he means that Rashba there forbids a deaf-mute, insane person, or minor, see there in his responsum, for he holds that the matter depends on proof, and since there is no proof a deaf-mute, insane person, or minor is invalid, and the same applies to spinning, where there is no proof. And per the view of Tosafos, a deaf-mute or minor who has some understanding is valid when a Jew stands over him, but an insane person is invalid; and per Rabbeinu Yonah, who holds that since it is possible through an agent it helps, the intent of standing over him is valid also for an insane person, see there. And in Machatzit HaShekel he wrote that per Rashba a deaf-mute or minor is valid, only an insane person is invalid, and per Rabbeinu Yonah an insane person is also valid; and it is not so, for Rashba is not so sharp per the view of Tosafos, and he himself also holds that a deaf-mute or minor is invalid, as he likewise wrote in his name; and also per his words Magen Avraham should not have written only an insane person alone; but in truth Magen Avraham intended the dispute of Rashba, who invalidates a deaf-mute, insane person, or minor, as he wrote.
