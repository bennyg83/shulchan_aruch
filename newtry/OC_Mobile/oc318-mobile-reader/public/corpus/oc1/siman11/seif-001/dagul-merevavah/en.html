This is not found in the words of the Rosh regarding tzitzit, and the Tur also did not mention this here — only the Beit Yosef derives spinning tzitzit from the law of processing tefillin below, siman 32. In my opinion, after his honor was forgiven, the analogy does not work well. See the Beit Yosef in Yoreh De'ah siman 271 — he brought the words of the Rosh in Halakhot Ketanot, and there you will see that regarding spinning tzitzit the Rosh also holds it invalid, because it is similar to writing a get. Examine there and forget [this derivation].
