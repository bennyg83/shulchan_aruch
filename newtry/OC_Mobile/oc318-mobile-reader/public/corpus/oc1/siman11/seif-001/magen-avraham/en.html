“The strings.” Rambam’s wording: strings found in the market, even cut and twisted, are invalid, for one would not trouble to make strings like tzitzis for sale—see Beis Yosef siman 12:42 that Magen Mishpat disagrees, and Kesef Mishneh wrote the Rambam retracted in a responsum—see Beis Yosef siman 2. And we are not concerned nowadays that tefillin [straps] are amulets.
