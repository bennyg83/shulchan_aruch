<b>Laws of <i>tzitzis</i> strings — it contains fifteen clauses.</b><br>The strands must be spun for the sake of the mitzvah. Rema: Some are stringent even for carding for the sake ; the practice is lenient for carding (Mordechai; <i>Agudah</i>
siman 23). One says at the beginning of spinning that one does so for <i>tzitzis</i>, or tells a woman “spin me <i>tzitzis</i> for a <i>tallis</i>.” If they were not spun for the sake they are invalid.
