Laws of tzitzis strings — it contains fifteen clauses.
The strands must be spun for the sake of the mitzvah. Rema: Some are stringent even for carding for the sake ; the practice is lenient for carding (Mordechai; Agudah siman 23). One says at the beginning of spinning that one does so for tzitzis, or tells a woman “spin me tzitzis for a tallis.” If they were not spun for the sake they are invalid.
