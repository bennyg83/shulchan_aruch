(13) There, in the gloss: it is good to tie the threads below, etc. Magen Avraham subsection 23 wrote: I heard that it is like adding to the knots, etc. — see there. But Maharach, may his memory be blessed, in Shaar HaKavanot in the discourse on the six of tzitzit wrote: "I saw my teacher, may his memory be blessed, that he did not object to this, though some hesitate about it; on the contrary it seemed to me he favored tying their heads" — until here. So testified our master Shmuel son of Maharach, may his memory be blessed, about his father that he did so. So too Birkei Yosef sign 6, Beit Yosef Yoreh De'ah in the laws of hanging tzitzit sign 16, Kaf HaChayyim siman 16 sign 4, and Ben Avraham Parashat Noach sign 14.
