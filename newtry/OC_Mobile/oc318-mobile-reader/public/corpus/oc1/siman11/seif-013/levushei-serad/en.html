Taz s.k. 9. That it is called a corner. His intent is because the Tur wrote that one should make the hole the measure of a thumb-joint near the corner, and some explained that corner means the angle, namely that one measures diagonally the measure of a thumb-joint from the angle. Therefore he wrote that this is not so; rather, corner means the edge of the garment, whether along its length or its width, and the measure of a thumb-joint is measured from the edge.
