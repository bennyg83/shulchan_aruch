He should be careful — meaning that if he transgressed and did not cut them before inserting, nevertheless he should be careful now before the windings; and see Taz.

One chulya — even of three windings.

And a knot, etc. — meaning if he made a chulya and also a knot after the chulya, even only one knot, he has fulfilled his obligation from the Torah even if he did not make at all the knot adjacent to the corner; and therefore it is "make," not "from that which was already made." But if he did not make the knot after the chulya, even if he made the first knot before the chulya, the making of the tzitzit is not yet complete, for without the knot the chulya does not endure at all and there is no gadil here; and therefore he may cut the strings. And some are stringent, and l'chatchila it is proper to heed them. And know that what he wrote "one knot" means one of the five knots we are accustomed to make; but it must be doubled, one upon the other, as below, for otherwise it is not called a knot since it does not endure.
