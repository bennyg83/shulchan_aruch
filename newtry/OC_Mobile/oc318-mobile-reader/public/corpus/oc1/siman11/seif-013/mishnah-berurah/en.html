(62) He should be careful. That is to say, if he transgressed and did not cut them before inserting them, he should at least be careful now to cut them before the winding. See Taz.
(63) One winding section. Even of three windings.
(64) And tied, etc. That is to say, if he made a winding section and also a knot after the winding section, even only one knot, he has fulfilled his obligation according to the Torah, even if he did not make at all the knot next to the corner. If so, it becomes a case of make it, and not from what is already made. But if he did not make the knot after the winding section, even though he made the first knot before the winding section, the making of the tzitzis has not yet been completed, for without the knot the winding section does not endure at all and there is no tied section here. Therefore he may cut the strings. Some are stringent, and initially it is proper to be concerned for their words. Know that what he wrote, one knot, means one of the five knots that people are accustomed to make, but it was two knots one upon the other, as below, for otherwise it is not called a lasting knot.
