13) There, in Rama's gloss. “It is proper to tie the threads at the bottom,” etc. Magen Avraham, se'if katan 23, writes: “I heard that this is considered adding to the knots,” etc.; see there. R' Chayyim Vital, of blessed memory, however, writes in Shaar HaKavanot, in the discourse on the sixth vav of tzitzis: “I saw that my master, of blessed memory, did not protest this. Although some question the practice, it appeared to me, on the contrary, that he desired their ends to be tied”; this concludes his words. Rabbeinu Shmuel, son of R' Chayyim Vital, likewise testified that his father did so. Birkei Yosef, entry 6, writes likewise, as do Siddur Beit Yaakov in the laws of attaching tzitzis, entry 16; Kaf HaChayyim, siman 10, entry 4; and Ben Ish Chai, parashat Noach, entry 14.
