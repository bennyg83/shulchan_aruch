“One knot.” Meaning if he made a chulyah and one knot he fulfills deoraisa; if so it is “you shall make,” etc.—not like Eliyah Rabbah, for plainly wrapping without a knot does not help—see gloss 13; Beis Yosef cites Mahariya that if he has no time, e.g., erev Shabbos near dark, a chulyah and one knot suffice—see siman 317 [chulyah is the part of the tzitzis between knot and knot].
