“The corner.” See Baer Heitev and what it cites in the name of Ginat Veradim. See there, where he writes that since the prohibition of kilayim applied to them when the tzitzit were made, one can say that the disqualification attached to the tzitzit themselves as well. Removing the offending material would then repair them automatically and create a problem of ta’aseh velo min ha’asui. One should therefore be stringent initially. After the fact, however, one may rely on the authorities who validate them; see there.
