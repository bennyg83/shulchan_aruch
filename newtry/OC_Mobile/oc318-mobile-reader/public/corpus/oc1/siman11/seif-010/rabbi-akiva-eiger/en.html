There, in the gloss, on Eruvin, siman 649. I question this, for there it deals with Yom Tov 2 that does not require “for you,” and only stolen is invalid because of mitzvah by way of transgression. And in this some say that after they did not steal it, there is no mitzvah by way of transgression in it. But here it is invalid because it is not “for you”; and if he sold it after despair — behold, he acquired it through despair and change, and it is not mitzvah by way of transgression, since it was acquired before the mitzvah, as with change of action that is valid here. And perhaps one could say this is Magen Avraham's intent in “if he sold it after despair.” And regarding the blessing, for those who hold in siman 649 that afterward it is not mitzvah by way of transgression — it is not disgraceful and he may bless. Still this requires further analysis.
