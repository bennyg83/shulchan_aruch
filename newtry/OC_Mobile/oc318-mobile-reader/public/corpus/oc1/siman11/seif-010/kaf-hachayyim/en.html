10) There. “And they require twisting.” Be'er Esek, siman 51, cited by Shaarei Teshuvah, entry 2, writes that one should not protest against a person who makes the tzitzis threads as a thick cord; first twisting two strands, and afterward making a thick cord from four, so that each resulting thread contains eight fine strands and each corner contains sixty-four. Rokeach, however, writes that the preferred fulfillment is to make each thread twisted from eight strands. Amudei HaShulchan on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 70, entry 17, cites him. Rema MiPano writes likewise, as Kitzur Shelah cites in Hilkhot Tzitzis. Kitzur Shelah writes that according to Kabbalah it is also proper to do so. Shemesh Tzedakah, Orach Chayyim, siman 22, writes likewise, as does Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berachah, entry 4, in the name of one holy authority. Lev Chayyim, volume 1, siman 86, likewise writes: “The preferred fulfillment is for every tzitzis thread first to be spun as one strand, then for two strands to be twisted together, then the two doubled into four and the four into eight. At the very least, one should take four spun strands, twist them into two, and thereby produce eight; there is no concern whatever with this. Nevertheless, if one found only threads twisted from two or four strands, he fulfills his obligation, and this is clear”; this concludes his words. Ben Ish Chai, parashat Noach, entry 10, writes likewise.
