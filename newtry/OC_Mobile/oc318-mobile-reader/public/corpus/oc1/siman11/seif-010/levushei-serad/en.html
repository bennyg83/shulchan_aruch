(Magen Avraham s.k. 13) We may infer, the upper knot. This means the knot near the braided section, meaning that at least one knot in the tzitzis is required according to the Torah. Therefore he used the term upper, because if he makes only one knot, he makes it at the end of the braided section, since it preserves the braided section. See Menachos, folio 39, in the Gemara and in Rashi.
