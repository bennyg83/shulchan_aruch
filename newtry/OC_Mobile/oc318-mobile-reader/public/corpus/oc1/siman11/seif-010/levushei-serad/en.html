(Magen Avraham subsection 13) From here [we learn] the upper knot. Meaning: the knot next to the gedil — that is, one knot on the tzitzit; nevertheless from the Torah, and therefore he called it "upper," because if one makes only one knot at the end of the gedil, it fulfills the gedil. See Menachos page 39 in the Gemara and in Rashi.
