(10) There: and they require twisting. Baer Heitev siman 51 wrote; they brought Shaarei Teshuva sign 2: one who makes tzitzit threads as a thick cord — that is, first twists two threads, then makes a thick cord from four, which are eight thin threads in each thread, which are sixty-four per corner — they do not object to him. But the Rokeach wrote that it is a mitzvah min hamuvchar to make each thread twisted from eight; they brought Amudei Shlomo on the Shulchan Aruch siman 70 sign 17, and so the Rema; they brought Kitzur Shulchan Aruch in the laws of tzitzit. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch wrote that from kabbalistic sources too it is proper to do thus; so too Shames Tzedakah in Orach Chayyim siman 22. So too Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berakhah sign 4 in the name of a certain holy one. So too Lev Chaim part 1 siman 86: "It is a mitzvah min hamuvchar to make each tzitzit thread spun first as one thread, then twist two threads together, then twist the two into four and four into eight; or alternatively take four spun and twisted threads into two, which are eight — and there is no hesitation about this at all, and this is primary; nevertheless if he did not find each twist of two or four, he fulfills with eighteen" — until here. So too Ben Avraham Parashat Noach sign 15.
