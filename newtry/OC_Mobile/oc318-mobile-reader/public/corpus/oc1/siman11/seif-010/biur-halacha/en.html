And they were detached from the woof threads, etc. See the Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote that this applies even if this happened immediately after the first section and the knot upon it, and this is learned from nearby, seif 13, for through this the tzitzis are rendered valid. What the Peri Megadim wrote in Eshel Avraham at the end of s.k. 13 regarding a tear that occurred, where he is uncertain whether the making is the knot alone or also the section, means that perhaps the halacha follows the Olas Tamid, that even with the knot alone the name tied section is fulfilled. He only joins this doubt regarding a minor, whose obligation in tzitzis is only D'Rabbanan, as is clear there to one who examines. But regarding an adult it is absolutely obvious that we follow the majority of poskim, indeed nearly all of them, that specifically with the section, and without this, he does not fulfill according to the Torah. Apparently he did not have the book Olas Tamid in his possession, except for what was brought from it in the poskim, for Hashem helped me and I obtained the book Olas Tamid, and I saw that he explains explicitly that the knot together with the wrapping, both specifically, is according to the Torah. Only regarding make it and not from what was already made, he holds that since each one prevents validity, both must be made validly. So too the Levush holds; see there. According to this, on Erev Shabbos close to dark, according to everyone one specifically needs a section with the knot. See Eliyah Rabbah in this siman, who brought the words of the Levush in what he wrote that one knot is enough according to the Torah, etc., and he challenged him greatly. In truth, his intent is simply that of the two knots that we make, one before the first section and one after the section, one alone is according to the Torah, except that we make both because of doubt, perhaps the halacha follows the second explanation in Rashi, Menachos 39a, brought in Tosafot there, s.v. knot, or perhaps the first explanation in Rashi, namely the knot after the section, as also brought in the Gra's commentary. But the Levush also holds that we require a knot and the section, both according to the Torah. So it is proven from Olas Tamid that this is the view of the Levush, and so it is proven from his language there that this is his doubt; see there. Eliyah Rabbah therefore challenged him for nothing and was not precise in his wording.
