And they tore, etc. — and likewise if the hole tore until because of that the required measure no longer remained, and even if this happened immediately after the first chulya with the knot, as will be explained shortly.

From the woof threads — and thereby the length of the garment was reduced; and as will be explained shortly, the same applies if the garment was reduced in its width.

At the time — for the verse is written, "and they shall make for themselves tzitzit on the corners of their garments" (Numbers 15:38): the Torah was particular only that at the time of making they be on the corner, not afterwards. And this is specifically regarding those tzitzit that were hung in a valid manner; but if one wishes to cast other tzitzit in it, which is a new act of making, it is invalid until he sews it from the beginning, as below in Siman 15, see there.

Less than the [proper] measure — even though b'dieved it is valid, nevertheless it is good to do so lest onlookers say it is invalid, for not everyone has learned the law [Beit Yosef].

There are those who say that within, etc. — for they hold that the principal application of the name kanaf is to the lower edge along the length of the garment, not along the width of the garment. The width of the garment is called that in which a person wears his stature from head to foot; and the length of the garment is called that in which he wraps himself, for the manner of wrapping is along the length.

It has no [minimum] measure — meaning below, for one may make [the distance] even less than a thumb-knot; but above three finger-widths is invalid [Beit Yosef].
