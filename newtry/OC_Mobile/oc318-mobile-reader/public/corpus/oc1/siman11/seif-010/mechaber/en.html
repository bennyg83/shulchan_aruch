If [the hole] was farther from the corner than a full thumb-knot and [threads] tore from the woof until less than the measure remained, it is valid since at the time one tied this tzitzis it had the measure. Rema: The practice is to sew reinforcement [imra] around the hole so it does not tear there and fall short of the measure; likewise reinforcement on the garment edge below for this reason (Beit Yosef citing Semak and Kol Bo). Some say within the garment’s width there is no measure; some say the width follows the length rule — both views have merit.
