If the hole was a full thumb joint distant from the corner and threads of the woof tore away until the required measure no longer remained, it is valid, because the required measure was present when these tzitzis were attached. {Rama: The custom is to make a reinforced hem around the hole so that it will not tear there and become less than the required measure. For the same reason, a hem is also made along the lower edge of the garment. [Beit Yosef in the name of Semak and Kol Bo]} Some say that there is no required measure within the width of the garment, while others say that the law of the garment's width is the same as its length, and their words appear correct.
