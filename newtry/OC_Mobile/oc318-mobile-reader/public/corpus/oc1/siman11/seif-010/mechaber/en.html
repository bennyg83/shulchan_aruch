If [the hole] was farther from the corner than a full thumb-knot and [threads] tore from the woof until less than the measure remained, it is valid since at the time one tied this <i>tzitzis</i> it had the measure. Rema: The practice is to sew reinforcement [<i>imra</i>] around the hole so it does not tear there and fall short of the measure; likewise reinforcement on the garment edge below for this reason (Beit Yosef citing Semak and <i>Kol Bo</i>).
