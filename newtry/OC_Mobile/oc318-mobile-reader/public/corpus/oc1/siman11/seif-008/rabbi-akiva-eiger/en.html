Taz, se'if katan 5. “It is obvious; why do I need a verse?” This is no difficulty. There, regarding the teaching from “his offering,” the question is that before ye'ush the ruling is obvious, because the consecration does not take effect at all and it is not an offering, as Tosafos writes there. Here, however, a verse is needed even for the period before ye'ush. I afterward saw that Nezir Shimshon writes likewise.
