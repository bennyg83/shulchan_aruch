(8) There, in the gloss: the custom is that the Jew assists a little, etc. Chayei Adam general rule 11 sign 13 wrote: in a time of pressure, when he has no one to spin for their sake, he may tell a non-Jew to spin threads for tzitzit, and the Jew should also assist somewhat in the spinning and stand by the spinning and intend for tzitzit — until here. So too in Sefer Hakadmonim Kol Yaakov there sign 11 on processing — one should not do so unless there is no Jew available; see there.
