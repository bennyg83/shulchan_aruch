"One who bowed to an animal," etc. — and it seems simple to me that the same applies to an animal that was sodomized — its wool is forbidden for tzitzis, for in one verse the Gemara includes them in Avodah Zarah 47, beginning of the page — see there. If so, it is simple that the same applies regarding the mitzvah. Peri Megadim wrote: one under a vow of benefit from wool — wool is forbidden for tzitzis (for we require "make for yourself from your own"). But one under a vow of benefit from his fellow — perhaps he may take his tallis, for mitzvos were not given for benefit, and we are not concerned he benefits from the garment itself (and otherwise kilayim would be permitted on the tallis itself). Perhaps his intent is to take tzitzis from the one under the vow; and what he wrote "his tallis" is imprecise wording.**** END BLOCK ****

"For planted flax" — see Mishna Berurah. Specifically if the uprooted flax is his own; if it belongs to his fellow, even if there is no change at all it is not forbidden, for a person cannot forbid what is not his. Even for Heaven — most poskim hold one cannot forbid. See chapter 4 of Laws of Things Forbidden on the Altar, halachah 5, in Lechem Mishneh there; Yoreh De'ah siman 145, seif 8, in Biur HaGra there; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 12 — close your eye to this.**** END BLOCK ****
