Invalid for tzitzis. See Magen Avraham and the book Bnei Chiya. The Olas Tamid wrote that wool that was upon it at the time of bowing becomes forbidden, and all the more so what grew afterward; see there. Bnei Chiya wrote that what grew after it was worshipped is permitted; see there. So too the Taz wrote in siman 649, s.k. 5, see there. This invalidity means even after the fact. Bnei Chiya.
