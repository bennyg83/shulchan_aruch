Invalid for tzitzit. See Magen Avraham and in Sefer Bnei Chiya. Eliyahu Rabba wrote that wool that was on it during prostration is forbidden, and all the more what grew after, as cited above. And Bnei Chiya wrote that what grew after it was processed is permitted, as cited above, and likewise Taz siman 649 seif katan 5, as cited above; and this invalidity means even bedieved. Bnei Chiya.
