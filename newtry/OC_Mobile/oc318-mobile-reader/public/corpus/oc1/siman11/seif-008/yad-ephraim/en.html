Seif katan 16. The length of the garment is called what one wrapped in. Meaning that in siman 8 seif 3 it states one should place the width of the tallis to his stature — see there in Tur; if so, the edge he wraps in is the edge of the length of the tallis, and the edge facing the ground is the edge of the width of the garment; on this Mahari Ashkenazi holds no measure is required — even if he places it near the edge facing the ground we have no concern, only that it be distant from the edge he wraps in a full thumb-knot; and the Taz holds that even though the manner of wrapping is to wrap the width of the tallis to one's stature, nevertheless here length and width of the garment are called according to how one wraps in it — for then the length is called to his stature and the width is what he wraps in; and the main thing requiring care is that it not be near the lower edge facing the ground, which is now called the edge of the width of the garment; rather he must raise from there a full thumb-knot from the edge near the ground, because measure is required within the length of the garment — not so on the edge of the length of the garment, etc.; one could say he should distance from the edge on its length downward toward the width of the garment he wraps in — this is not required; it is found that the Taz's words are the opposite of Magen Avraham's — see below in Turei Zahav 17.
