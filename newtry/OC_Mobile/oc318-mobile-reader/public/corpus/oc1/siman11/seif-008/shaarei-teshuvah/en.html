Siman 649. See Be'er Hetev; and see Magen Avraham siman 649, who challenged this from the Gemara on avoncheri, from which it is evident that one blesses; and he wrote that perhaps owner despair and change of name is preferable; and therefore according to Bach HaTur's explanation in the seif katan before this—that they acquire through owner despair and change of name—if so one should bless as well; and according to Magen Avraham, who says Rama deals with a case without despair, it fits well.
