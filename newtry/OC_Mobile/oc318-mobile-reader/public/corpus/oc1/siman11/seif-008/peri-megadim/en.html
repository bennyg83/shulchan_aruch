That is. That is, thumbbreadths; see Eshel Avraham on Taz. In Magen Avraham we wrote about this, that Tosafot in Menachos are uncertain about it. Nevertheless, as halachah we rule that wherever the width of a thumb is mentioned, we measure at the broad place, the upper joint at its broad place. The full measure of a thumb joint until the nail means until the end of the nail where it is level with the flesh.
