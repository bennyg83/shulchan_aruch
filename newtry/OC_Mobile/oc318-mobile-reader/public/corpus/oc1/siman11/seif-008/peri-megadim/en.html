That is, thumbbreadths. Eshel Avraham on Taz — and in Magen Avraham we wrote from this that Tosafos on Menachot are uncertain about this; nevertheless for halacha we rule: in a wide place one measures — wherever width of a thumb-joint is mentioned, the upper joint in a wide place. And “full thumb-joint knot until the nail” means the end of the nail, equal to the flesh.
