[8] Because of degradation of a mitzvah, etc. At first glance it would appear from this that b'dieved it is valid — and so the language of the Shulchan Aruch and Levush implies. But the Taz and Nachalat Tzvi explained the words of the Shulchan Aruch that even b'dieved it is forbidden, as Rambam holds. I also found that Baal HaMaor gave another reason: we require the corner (kanaf) to be of a [proper] kind of corner; and these kinds are not fit to make a garment, for they are wool refuse (pesolet). According to this, certainly even b'dieved they are invalid.
