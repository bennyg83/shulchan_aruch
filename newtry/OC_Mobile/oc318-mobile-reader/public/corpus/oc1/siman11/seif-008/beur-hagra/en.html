Seif 8, "one who bowed," etc. This is Reish Lakish's unresolved question. As stated there: techeles of what status, etc. According to Rabbenu Tam at the beginning of Sukkah chapter 3, and as written below siman 586, it is invalid after the fact. But according to Ri's first answer in Tosafos, it is invalid only initially. See siman 649, seif 3.

"One who bowed to flax," etc. There is the question of Rami bar Chama. He holds that what is said there, "how can you compare," resolves the question, as written in chapter 3 of Laws of Things Forbidden for the Altar. In chapter 4 there, regarding the question of techeles for priests, he wrote that it was not resolved, although the Gemara said it is the question of Rami bar Chama. This is as he distinguished here between wool and flax. It did not say, "this is the question of Rami bar Chama," but rather the question of one who bowed to a mountain, etc.; so it appears. Therefore regarding tzitzis he says it is the question of Reish Lakish, unlike Tosafos there, gloss beginning "hainu." So wrote Magen Avraham.
