Three fingerbreadths — in the gloss he wrote that this means thumbbreadths; and in Machatzit HaShekel he brought from Sefer HaKavanos that the three fingerbreadths are the forearm, clenched fist, and little finger, see there; and in Matzat Shemura and in Shalmei Tzibbur he elaborated and wrote that the Ari z"l would also agree, only that we measure at the narrow place. And these three fingerbreadths of forearm, clenched fist, and little finger are one measure, see there; and see in Mishnat Chasidim that they are all called fingerbreadths, see there — the finger next to the thumb, because they are gevurot, etc., see there.
