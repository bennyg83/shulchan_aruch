Seif 12, "the number," etc. There: Mar son of Ravina, etc. See Rashi, gloss beginning "like ours," and as written in the Sifrei and Beis Shammai say: four threads of techeles and four threads of white, meaning after they were doubled. Tosafos, 41b, gloss beginning "Beis Shammai." We rule like Beis Shammai, as written in the Gemara there. Although one has no techeles, he places white in place of techeles, as stated there, 38b: I have no techeles, etc. So it also appears there, 41b: this is not difficult; here, etc.; as Rashi and Tosafos wrote there on the Mishnah at the beginning of 38a. So it also appears from the Gemara there, 40a, which challenges: let it be only white, etc.

"And if," etc. Rashba, as stated in Sanhedrin 89a: and if he holds that a knot, etc.; see Tosafos there, gloss beginning "kesher." So it also appears in Menachos 40b: do we say "make," etc. The Baal HaIttur wrote that initially one may add as much as he wishes, and learned it from what is stated there, 42: Rav Yirmiyah of Difti cast eight, etc.; and there, 41b: this is not difficult; here with a tallis, etc. So too the Sifrei, cited by Tosafos there: how much gedil should be made? No fewer than three, according to Beis Hillel; Beis Shammai say four, etc. So the Baal HaIttur explained what is stated there, 42, that it has no upper measure, whether in length or number; one can say the Gemara's question, "shall we say," etc., refers to both. But Tosafos forced the Sanhedrin passage and wrote that from eight to sixteen one may add, for all this is included in "gedilim," either from one alone or from both together; but more than that is worse and invalid. The Rashba holds it always invalidates, and those Amoraim there, 42a, disagree with one another. See Tosafos at the beginning of the chapter, gloss beginning "HaTecheles," etc. But for halachah the view of the Baal HaIttur, who validates, appears correct; his reasoning is sound and primary. When we invalidate in Sanhedrin there, it is specifically where he added another species, similar to lulav there, where with the same species we validate even initially, allowing him to add as much as he wants.

"He should cut," etc. There, 42a: Rabbi Acha bar Yaakov said, etc.; thus all of them must be doubled, and we act like Mar son of Ravina. See Tosafos at the beginning of the chapter, end of gloss beginning "HaTecheles": "and what we double," etc. He said "he should cut" because initially he should cut them before inserting them, for seif 13 is only after the fact, as stated in Sukkah 11a: if he inserted, etc., they are valid, specifically after the fact; unlike what the Beis Yosef wrote, that it is good advice. This also appears from Rabbenu Yerucham's language, and so wrote Maharai.
