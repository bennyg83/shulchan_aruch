However — Eshel Avraham on Taz, Eshel Avraham on Beit Yosef, and Mishneh Lamelech imply that if he hung [tzitzis] on the gadil it is valid, and rabbinically invalid regarding the gadil lest the launderer take them from here and there and leave the place of tzitzis; in such a case certainly the small remainder was not from the garment. That which is taught in the chapter HaTekhelet, Beit Yosef brought regarding the gadil — invalid rabbinically; but in the Gemara it is clear that nevertheless it is invalid regarding the gadil, for this is not a corner of the garment. Practical difference: carrying out on Shabbos with this tallis — whether human dignity overrides rabbinic [prohibition]; however it will be explained in siman 13, with God's help, that there is no practical difference — see there. What is written in seif 10: if they tore and no thumb-knot remained — permitted, for the Torah was particular about initial making, as Levush wrote: "and they shall make tzitzit on the corners of their garments" — at the time of making we require on corners, and afterward there is no particular concern. It is difficult for me: if so, when he made within three finger-widths and afterward cut the garment more than two finger-widths and sewed what was within three finger-widths, and the tzitzis were spaced on the corner so they now rest more than three finger-widths — shall we say valid, for the Torah was particular only about making? And what is written regarding less than a thumb-knot or more than three finger-widths — one may explain; understand.
