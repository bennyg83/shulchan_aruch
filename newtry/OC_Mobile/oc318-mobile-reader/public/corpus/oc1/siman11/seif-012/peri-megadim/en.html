But. See Eshel Avraham on Taz and Eshel Avraham on Beis Yosef. From the Taz it implies that if he hung it on the tied section, according to the Torah it is valid, and it is invalid D'Rabbanan on the tied section lest the launderer remove threads from here and from there and leave the place of the tzitzis. In such a case, the small amount that remains certainly is not from the garment. As for what is taught in the chapter HaTecheiles, brought by the Beis Yosef, that on the tied section it is invalid D'Rabbanan, but in Beis Yosef and Gra it is clear that according to the Torah it is invalid on the tied section, for this is not the corner of the garment. There is a practical difference regarding going out on Shabbos with this tallis, whether human dignity overrides a D'Rabbanan prohibition. However, it will be explained in siman 13, with Hashem's help, that there is no practical difference; see there. What is written in seif 10 is that if they were torn away and a thumb joint did not remain, it is permitted, for the Torah was particular about the beginning of the making, as the Levush wrote: and they shall make tzitzis on the corners of their garments. At the time of making we require that it be on the corners, but afterward there is no concern. This is difficult for me, for if so, when he made it within three fingerbreadths and afterward cut more than two fingerbreadths in the garment and sewed what was within the three fingerbreadths, and the tzitzis had space on the corner in such a way that now they rest more than three fingerbreadths away, let us say that it is valid, for the Torah was particular only about the making. What is the difference between less than a thumb joint and more than three fingerbreadths? One can answer; understand this.
