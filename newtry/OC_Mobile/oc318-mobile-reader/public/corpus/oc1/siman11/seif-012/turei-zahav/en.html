(א) “Four doubled.” For they say in the Gemara: “gedil” and “two gedilim” are four—meaning, if it were written “gedil” it would imply two, for there is no gedil less than two; now that it is written “gedilim,” four are implied. And when there was tekheles they made two of tekheles and two of white; nowadays white is in place of tekheles and they make four long ones of white.

(ב) “Cut the tips of the strands.” It implies that before threading he cuts them first; and afterward in seif 13 it wrote “before winding”—therefore there it wrote “let one take care,” while here they only mentioned two matters lechatchila: one before threading should do so for good, but need not be careful about this; but lechatchila before winding one should be more careful. And what it wrote “if he wound,” etc., means and tied after winding—for winding without a knot is nothing, and the first knot immediately after threading is not counted at all and is not called an act; rather specifically the winding—that is, with the knot made after the winding—and with this everything is resolved.
