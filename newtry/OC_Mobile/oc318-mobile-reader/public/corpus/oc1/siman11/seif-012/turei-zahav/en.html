“Four doubled.” Gemara says gedil and two gedilim four — if written gedil would imply two — no gedil less than two — now written gedilim implies four. When there was tekheles they made two tekheles two white — nowadays white in place of tekheles and make four long of white.

“Cut the strand tips.” Implies before threading cut first — then clause 13 wrote before winding — therefore there wrote “take care” — here only both lechatchila — one before threading do for good — need not be careful — but lechatchila before winding more careful — “if he wound” means and tied after winding — winding without knot is nothing — first knot right after threading not counted — not called act — specifically winding with knot made after winding — with this all resolves.
