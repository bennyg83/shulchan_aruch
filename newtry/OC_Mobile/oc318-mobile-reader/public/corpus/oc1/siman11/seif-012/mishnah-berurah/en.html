Four doubled — for we say in the Gemara: gadil is two, gedilim is four. Meaning, if it were written gadil it would imply two, for there is no gadil in less than two; now that it is written gedilim, four is implied. And in the time when there was techelet, they would make two of techelet and two of white; and nowadays white is in place of techelet, and they make four long ones all of white.

Which are eight — after one inserts them into the garment and doubles them over, for it is written patil, like the manner of a patil that is doubled.

Invalid — because he transgressed bal tosif; and the Gra in his commentary agreed that the halacha follows the view of the Itur brought in Beit Yosef, who holds there is no bal tosif here. And that which we declare invalid in the Gemara in this — specifically when he added another species to the strings, such as hemp threads or cotton wool; but in the same species we validate even l'chatchila to add as much as he wants. And see in Aruch HaShulchan, who brought several more early great authorities who hold thus; and therefore he concludes that if he already made them and cannot fix them, he may go wearing them; but as soon as he can fix them he must fix them. And regarding reducing from the strings — everyone agrees it is invalid, as in Beit Yosef.

He should cut — meaning l'chatchila he should cut before inserting them into the corner [Magen Avraham and Biur HaGra]; and one should cut them with the teeth, not with a knife.
