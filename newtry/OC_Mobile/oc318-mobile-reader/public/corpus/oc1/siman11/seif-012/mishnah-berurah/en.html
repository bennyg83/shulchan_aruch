(58) Four doubled. For we say in the Gemara: tied section, two; tied sections, four. That is, if it had been written in the singular, tied section, it would imply two, for there is no tied section with fewer than two. Now that it is written in the plural, tied sections, it implies four. In the time when there was techeiles, they would make two from techeiles and two from white; nowadays, white stands in place of techeiles, and we make four long strings from white.
(59) Which are eight. After he inserts them into the garment and doubles them, for it is written, a thread, like a wick, which is doubled.
(60) Invalid. Because he violates the prohibition of adding. The Gra, in his commentary, agreed as halachah with the view of the Ittur brought in the Beis Yosef, who holds that there is no prohibition of adding here, and that when the Gemara invalidates this, it is specifically where he added another kind to the strings, such as hemp or cotton strings. But with the same kind, we validate even initially adding as much as he wants. See Eretz HaChaim, who brought several other great Rishonim who hold this way. Therefore he concludes that if he already made them and it is impossible to fix them, he may go with them, but as soon as he is able to fix them, he must fix them. If he subtracts from the strings, all agree that it is invalid, as stated in the Beis Yosef.
(61) He should cut. That is to say, initially he should cut them before inserting them into the corner. See Magen Avraham and the commentary of the Gra. One should cut them with his teeth and not with a knife.
