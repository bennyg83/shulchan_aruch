And if he added, it is invalid. See the Peri Megadim, who is uncertain if he cut the extra threads only up to the tied section, while in the tied section they remained, what is the law? For if he removed the extras also from the tied section, it is obvious that it is valid, according to what the Rama ruled above in siman 10, seif 6, in the case where one attached tzitzis upon tzitzis, that he cuts off one of them and it is valid in every manner; see there.
