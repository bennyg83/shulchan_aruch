[5] Godelin (thumb-lengths), etc. Beit Yosef in the name of the Mordechai: at the place of the width of the thumb, not from its head which is short. In Bedek HaBayit he wrote that Tosafos in the chapter HaTekhelet were uncertain about this; therefore the Shulchan Aruch ruled without specification. Shach wrote in Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 271 that one estimates by a medium thumb, which is the width of six medium barley corns placed side by side tightly, and in length two barley corns placed loosely.
