Seif 5, "one does not make," etc. Rambam, and so explained the Geonim, as the Razah wrote in their name in Sukkah chapter 1. He wrote that with this the reading in Sukkah there is clear: thus we require spinning for its own sake, and the challenge is from Shmuel. Therefore the Geonim wrote that the law follows Shmuel, since the Gemara's sugya there follows him and we establish the Mishnah and verses according to him. With this it is clear what is written in Menachos 42b: kinds for tzitzis, etc., although a gentile acts for his own intention, even if a Jew stands over him, as written in Gittin chapter 2. Milchamos there follows the Rambam's approach, and the Rambam follows his own view. But the Rosh, who here explained like Rashi that even according to Rav making must be for its own sake, therefore validates it with a Jew standing over him. See seif 14, seif 2. He further wrote: this is what is stated in Sukkah there, "and they shall make for themselves" - from their own; whereas according to Rav the verse would mean: others shall make for them.

"And the reason," etc. Beis Yosef. But the Razah wrote that the reason they are invalid according to Rav is that we require the kind of the corner, and these three kinds are not fit to make a garment from them, for they are wool refuse and garments are not made from such material.
