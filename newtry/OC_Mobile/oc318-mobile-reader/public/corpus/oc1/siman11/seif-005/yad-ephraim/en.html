(In Magen Avraham seif katan 13) Even with one knot it constitutes connection, etc.; and that which we require at least one knot is because otherwise it is not established, as challenged in Shabbos chapter Klal Gadol, and it would be like one who closes with one stitch, which is not a connection because it is not established thus; therefore the challenge in Menachos does not apply, for we rule that one who closes with one stitch is not a connection since it is not established until he ties; and all the more so one knot is at least by Torah law, and he need not raise the challenge in general as Magen Avraham wrote earlier — for we rule it is not a connection until he ties — this is not so, for it is not explicit anywhere in the Mishnah that with two stitches he must tie, only that we say so logically, since with one stitch, since it is not established thus, it is not a connection — likewise with two stitches without any knot at all. But Tosafos Yom Tov at the beginning of chapter Klal Gadol wrote to prove from the Gemara like Bertinoro: one who sews two stitches that require tying is also liable for tying on Shabbos; if so it is proven that with two stitches a complete knot is required; if so the mishnah in Kilayim that distinguishes between one stitch and two stitches is resolved according to Rosh — to say that with one stitch even if he ties two knots there is no prohibition of kilayim; if so it is difficult on the Gemara that says the practical difference is the upper knot, etc. — for we rule one stitch is not a connection, so for what it takes one stitch even a knot does not help; rather it must be his intent that if he ties the upper knot by Torah law it does not apply that he makes two holes, namely two stitches, and the knot helps. If so, for what purpose did it state the general principle that we rule one who closes, etc.? It should have said the principle that we rule there is no connection in kilayim even with two stitches until he ties, but one stitch — who mentions it? If there were only one stitch, even if he tied the upper knot by Torah law it would not apply according to Tosafos Yom Tov that with one stitch it does not help; rather it must be that according to Tosafos Yom Tov we do not derive kilayim from Shabbos at all — for even though in Shabbos he must tie even with two stitches, nevertheless in kilayim he does not need tying at all according to Rambam; and at least one knot suffices according to Rosh for the law, not for his reasoning. For Rosh derives from Shabbos and he did not hold thus; and in any event with two stitches more than one knot is not required according to all; and this is what Magen Avraham wrote "and we conclude from this that according to all with two holes," etc. And perhaps further, in Tosafos Yom Tov from the word "but" until "and we conclude from this" one should write in it "two half-circles"; and what he wrote "and we conclude from this that according to all with two holes," etc., means whether according to Rambam or according to Rosh and Tosafos Yom Tov — one could say they also hold like Rambam that we do not derive kilayim from Shabbos; and see Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 310, seif katan 3, that for Rambam one must distinguish between kilayim and Shabbos — see there; and see there in seif katan 4 that he wrote to distinguish for Rosh there, that one who ties two garments together — tying alone does not help; not so one who ties strands in a garment — see there; only that he did not mention the Gemara words Magen Avraham cites here.
