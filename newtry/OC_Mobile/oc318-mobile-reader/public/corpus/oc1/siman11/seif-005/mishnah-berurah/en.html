(25) We do not make — and even after the fact it is invalid [Taz and Pri Megadim].

(26) That the weaver leaves over — one should not ask that even without this there is invalidity, for the strands were not spun for the sake of tzitzit; one may say that it deals even where initially the strands were spun for the sake of tzitzit and afterward he wove a garment from them, and even so it is invalid because of degrading the mitzvah [Taz]. And in Sefer HaMaor he wrote the reason is because we require that the corner be the same kind as the corner, and these kinds are not fit to make a garment from them, since they are wool waste and a garment cannot be made from their quantity — end of his words. And if so, any inferior wool from which a garment cannot be made is invalid for tzitzit [Magen Avraham].
