(25) We do not make. Even after the fact it is invalid. See Taz and Peri Megadim.
(26) That the weaver leaves over. One should not ask that in any event there is an invalidation because the strings were not spun for the sake of tzitzis. One may answer that it speaks even where initially the strings were spun for the sake of tzitzis and afterward he wove a garment from them, and even so it is invalid because of degradation of the mitzvah. See Taz. In Sefer HaMaor he wrote the reason, because we require the corner to be of the same kind as the corner, and these kinds are not fit to make a garment from them, for they are waste wool and a garment is not made from such material, end quote. If so, all inferior kinds of wool from which a garment is not made are invalid for tzitzis. See Magen Avraham.
