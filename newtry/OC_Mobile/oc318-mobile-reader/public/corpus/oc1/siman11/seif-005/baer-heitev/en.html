At first glance this is difficult, for according to this, since the reason is because of degradation of a mitzvah, the Gemara has no challenge from this against Beis Hillel in Sukkah, folio 9a. It may be resolved according to the version of the Gemara there, which says that this shows that we require spinning for its required purpose, etc.; and it challenges him from Shmuel, not as Rashi explains there, see there and examine carefully. With this the question of the poskim is answered, why the Rambam ruled like Shmuel against Rav: according to his reasoning and his version, the challenge was only from Shmuel, and since he saw the anonymous Talmud laboring to establish the view of Beis Hillel, the halacha following Shmuel is thereby evident. See Baal HaMaor there, Magen Avraham, s.k. 9, Yad Aharon, siman 14, and Beis Yosef there, and examine carefully.
