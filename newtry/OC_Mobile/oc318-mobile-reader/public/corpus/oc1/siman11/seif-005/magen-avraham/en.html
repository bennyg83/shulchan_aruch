“Disgrace to a mitzvah.” Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote this on his own and did not see Sefer HaMaor, who wrote in Sukkah ch. 1 that we need “the corner shall be of the same kind” of cloth—and these kinds are unfit to make a garment from, being inferior wool waste from which no garment is made—end quote; therefore all wool types too inferior for a garment are invalid for tzitzis.
