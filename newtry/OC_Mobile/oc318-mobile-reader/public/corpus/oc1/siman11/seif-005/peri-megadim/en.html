Eshel Avraham on Taz — his holy words have difficulties of understanding, and I will explain according to my ability, with God's help: In Beit Yosef in the name of Nimukei Yosef it implies despair with change of action — wool, threads — and if so, why specify wool, threads? Even from threads that one made twisted, for both revert to their original state (and for us twisting is required). Rather it is certain that despair with complete change of action also does not acquire in theft whenever the name of the stolen item did not change, for Rabbeinu Yerucham says so in Choshen Mishpat siman 360, seif 9; see Sema, note 14, regarding large beams that one made small, and in his words there. And here the reason is because of change of name — wool and threads — and despair with change of name, inferior acquisition, as explained in Choshen Mishpat 361, seif 1 and 2. Specifically wool; but threads that one twisted — there is no change of name, for originally threads and now threads; and despair with change of action (even though it does not revert) — whenever the theft did not change, one does not acquire, as Rabbeinu Yerucham said in Shulchan Aruch, seif 6: And on our master Beit Yosef he challenged two answers on explanation 1, that the wording of Rambam of blessed memory is strained — he stole wool and made it spun thread, for so one does not make wool into thorns, etc. (and therefore Beit Yosef brought Rambam of blessed memory). And according to Beit Yosef's words, why does he take wool before despair, for it is somewhat obvious even without a verse; and if we also require a verse, why did he not inform us of the main law, and the greater novelty is in stolen threads after despair that are invalid, which we do not hear regarding wool without despair (Eshel Avraham on Rama 9; Chemed Moshe was angry at him in Taz, and according to what other later authorities wrote, all of it will be explained below further, im yirtzeh Hashem). Certainly a verse is needed in any case for threads, only as he wrote. And explanation 2 challenged: one, that despair with change, inferior acquisition, is clear in Choshen Mishpat 361:2, and Derishah wondered likewise; and he challenged more that bleaching is presumably the case discussed, and if so it would be a change that does not revert to its original state. And even though we rule in Yoreh De'ah 133, at the beginning of “the shear” — if one bleached and did not dye, he is liable and does not acquire, per the rabbis of R' Shimon at the beginning of Gozel without despair — not so with despair and change of name with bleaching, he certainly acquires, for it does not revert to its original state; and all the more so that inferior change with despair also acquires. And he raised the reason because of mitzvah by way of transgression; and even though in Terumat HaDeshen it is explained that whoever did not assist in the mitzvah has no mitzvah by way of transgression — one could say, one, as Eliyah Rabbah wrote, that spinning leshmah is mitzvah by way of transgression; and further, when does he acquire? At spinning — and before spinning it was not his; further, it was not spinning for its sake, which is called not his and not in his possession. And therefore it is reversed, apparently: in stolen threads that one twisted, with despair he acquires and there is no mitzvah by way of transgression, if we say that twisting not for its sake is not invalid post facto (Magen Avraham 3) — rather, if so, it is despair alone without change of name, for originally threads and now threads, and change of action — whenever the name of the theft did not change, like large beams and small ones in Choshen Mishpat siman 360, seif 6 — he does not acquire. This is what we wished to explain.
