If one took threads belonging to partners and attached them to his garment in a similar manner, it is possible that he also does not fulfill the obligation, for perhaps his fellow did not authorize him for this; this requires study. Regarding the main matter, whether this is called belonging to partners, see below in siman 14, seif 5, regarding a tallis of partners. One can distinguish, for according to what we rule, the third part of Hilchos Tzitzis is speaking of a case where the individual who wears it is obligated, as will be explained below. See Chullin 136b, and see Tosafot, Sukkah 27b, s.v. kol ha'ezrach, and see the Gemara in Sukkah, folio 9a, at the end of the page. All this requires study.
