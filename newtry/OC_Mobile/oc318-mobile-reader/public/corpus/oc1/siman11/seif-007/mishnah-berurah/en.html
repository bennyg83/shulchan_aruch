(33) Borrowed strands — meaning, every expression of borrowing implies that he will return that very item; but an expression of loan is given for expenditure. And these borrowed strands are presumptively a loan, and we call it "from their own."

(34) And it is considered his — but if he lent them to him with intent that he return them to him exactly, he should not bless on them, for we may say that he lent them to him for another work. But if he lent him ready-made tzitzit like ours, which are interwoven and cut, it is permitted to affix them on his tallit and bless on them, for certainly they were given to him as a gift on condition to return, as written in siman 14 regarding a tallit [Magen Avraham].
