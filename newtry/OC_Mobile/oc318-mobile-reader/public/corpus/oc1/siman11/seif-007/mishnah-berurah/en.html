(33) Borrowed strings. This means that any expression of borrowing implies that he will return that very item itself, whereas an expression of lending is given to be spent or consumed. These borrowed strings are presumably a loan, and we apply to them the verse, from their own.
(34) And they are considered his own. But if he borrowed them with the understanding that he would return them themselves, he should not recite a blessing over them, for one can say that he lent them to him for some other use. But if he lent him prepared tzitzis like ours, which are twisted and cut, he may hang them on his tallis and recite a blessing over them, for he certainly gave them to him as a gift on condition that they be returned, as written in siman 14 regarding a tallis. See Magen Avraham.
