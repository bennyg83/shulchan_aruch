“It is as his own.” This implies: if he borrowed tzitzis to return them unchanged, he does not bless, for they are not his—this requires study, for siman 14 he ruled like Rosh that one who borrows a tallis with tzitzis blesses on them. One can answer there the lender lent intending he bless, unlike here if he lent to return unchanged he should not place them on his garment. This requires study: why lend except to wear on a garment—then it is like a loan for return—and see what I wrote; one can say he thought other work would be done with them, hence “strings,” but ready-made tzitzis would indeed be permitted—see siman 349.
