[7] And not from the remnants of the two [threads], etc. One should not challenge that in any case it is invalid because they were not spun for the sake of tzitzit — for one could say he first wove the threads for tzitzit and afterward wove a garment from them. See the Rosh, who wrote that because we require hanging lishmah, it implies that if he plucked them from there and made tzitzit from them, they are valid if they were spun lishmah. Rabbeinu Yeruham page 169 wrote that because we require spinning lishmah — and this is puzzling; examine, for I abbreviated. However, according to Rambam, Shulchan Aruch, and Levush it appears that even if he plucked them from there it is invalid.
