(7) There, in the gloss: the custom is that the Jew assists a little, etc. If the Jew did not assist but only told the non-Jew to do it lishmah — whether one may validate b'dieved, see in Sefer Hakadmonim Kol Yaakov siman 271 sign 15 on processing, for there is a dispute in this; and our master, may his memory be blessed, learned this law from processing — see Beit Yosef and our master's reasoning there in Beit Yosef Yoreh De'ah siman 271: if the Jew did not assist, invalid. So too Shulchan Gavoah there sign 11. Rabbi Zvi here sign 4. See Eliyah Rabbah sign 1; Rabbi Zvi wrote in Korban Aharon sign 1 that even for the Bach, who holds there, as the Rosh holds, that even if the Jew did not assist at all it is valid regarding spinning tzitzit — for all spinning must be lishmah — it is comparable to writing a get, for the Rosh agrees that a non-Jew acts for his own intent; therefore the Bach ruled in siman 14 here that it does not help even b'dieved without Jewish assistance; see there. (By this the challenge of Eliyah Rabbah there on the Bach, and the challenge of Dagul MeRevavah on our master, is resolved.)
