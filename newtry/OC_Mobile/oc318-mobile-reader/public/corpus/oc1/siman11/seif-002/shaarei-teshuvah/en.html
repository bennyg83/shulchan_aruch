Twisted. See Be'er Hetev; and Machatzit HaShekel wrote in the name of Mor uKetzia that if he is not rendering others unfit and it is a time of constraint, he may rely to validate it; and also Ner Tzvi and Eliyah Rabbah validate it b'dieved. Ba'er Sheva wrote siman 51: one who makes the tzitzis strands thick twisted thread — that is, at first he twists two strands and afterward makes thick twisted thread from four, which are eight thin strands in each strand, which are sixteen thin strands in each corner — they do not object to him.
