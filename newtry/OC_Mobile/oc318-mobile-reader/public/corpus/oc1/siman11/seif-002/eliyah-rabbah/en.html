[2] And they require twisting, etc. However, b'dieved, if he did not twist them, he does not invalidate on that account — so ruled Nachalat Tzvi, and he explained that this is the view of the Shulchan Aruch. But Magen Avraham explained the view of the Shulchan Aruch that even b'dieved it is invalid, and he did not mention the words of Nachalat Tzvi. It appears he was precise from what the Shulchan Aruch wrote in the siman after this — that enough must remain of the rest for a coil (anivah); thus twisting is invalid even b'dieved — and so Olat Tamid implies. In my humble opinion this is not proof, for there the reason is that it is like adding to eight threads, which is invalid; but if one takes eight threads without any twisting at all, it may be valid. And so appears the view of the Bach; therefore it seems to me to be lenient b'dieved.
