“According to Rambam invalid.” For the gentile acts with his own intent; Rosh’s view will be explained siman 32 s. 9.

“They require twisting.” For it is taught in Sifrei: “a tekheles strand spun and twisted like cord” — I have only tekheles — white from where? Scripture says give tekheles, give white: as tekheles spun and twisted — so too white.
