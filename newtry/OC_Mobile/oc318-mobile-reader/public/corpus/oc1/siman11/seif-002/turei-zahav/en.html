(א) “According to Rambam, invalid.” For the gentile acts with his own intent; Rosh’s view will be explained in siman 32, seif 9.

(ב) “They require twisting.” For it is taught in Sifrei: “A strand of tekheles, spun and twisted like a cord”—I have only tekheles; white, from where? Scripture said: give tekheles, give white—just as tekheles is spun and twisted, so too white.
