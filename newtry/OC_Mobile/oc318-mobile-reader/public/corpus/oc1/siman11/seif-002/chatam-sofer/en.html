There, Turei Zahav, se'if katan 20: “For it was taught in Sifrei.” Gloss: It appears to me that this is the intent of Rambam's responsum cited by Beit Yosef. Rambam asks that according to Sifrei the thread must certainly be sixfold, because a gezeirah shavah cannot be applied halfway. Yet the unqualified Talmud at the end of Eruvin does not answer that the case concerns one who made it sixfold; a person certainly would not undertake so much trouble. Since the Talmud does not give that answer, it follows that the Sages known to the Gemara disagreed with Rabbi Shimon in Sifrei and did not derive the gezeirah shavah. The universal practice of using spun threads is merely for beauty, not because of a gezeirah shavah at all. Rambam's words are therefore correct in my humble opinion.
