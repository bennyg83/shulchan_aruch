{Rama: (There)} Taz, small paragraph 20: It is taught in the Sifrei, N.B. It seems the intent of the Rambam's answer in Beit Yosef is that the Rambam challenged per the Sifrei — surely doubled threads of six are required, for there is no gezeirah shavah for half; and since the plain Talmud in the last chapter of Eruvin does not say "such as if one doubled six," etc., a person certainly would not trouble himself; and from the fact that it does not answer thus, we learn they were certain the Rabbis disagree with R' Shimon in the Sifrei and we do not derive a gezeirah shavah; twisting is only a worldly custom for beauty, not from gezeirah shavah at all — and the Rambam's words are correct in my humble opinion.
