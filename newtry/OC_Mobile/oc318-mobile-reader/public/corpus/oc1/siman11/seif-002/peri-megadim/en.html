And they require twisting. Eshel Avraham on Taz, and Eshel Avraham on Beit Yosef in the name of Nimukei Yosef — for it is reasonable that he said twisting also requires leshmah (for its sake) (Magen Avraham, note 3), and there it is stated in the name of Baal HaItur: if one spun not for its sake and twisted for its sake, they are valid. The Rosh challenged from “from linsey-woolsey they are invalid” — yet one must twist! One could say that Beit Yosef Tur holds like R' Meir, who holds twisting is not required; linsey-woolsey invalid are bundles when one does not wish to twist. However for us twisting is certainly required, for twisting for its sake does not help for spinning not for its sake from linsey-woolsey, etc. And a similar proof from Eruvin 96b: one who finds tekhelet for ritual fringes invalid, threads valid, and they establish it in twisted ones and verses — why did the extractor not say tekhelet, threads invalid, twisted valid? Rather threads too, perhaps spinning not leshmah; and for Beit Yosef Tur there is remedy in twisting — this is nothing and need not be elaborated.
