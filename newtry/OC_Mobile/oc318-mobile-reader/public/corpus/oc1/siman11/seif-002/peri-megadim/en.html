And they require. They require twisting; see Eshel Avraham on Taz and the Beis Yosef in the name of the Nimukei Yosef, who said by reasoning that twisting too requires being for its required purpose. See Magen Avraham note 3. There it is stated in the name of the Baal HaIttur that if they were spun not for their required purpose and twisted for their required purpose, they are valid. The Rosh challenged this from the law that loose strands are invalid, for they still need twisting. This can be answered that the Baal HaIttur holds like the Rambam that they do not require twisting, and the invalidity of loose strands applies to bundles when he does not wish to twist them. However, according to us, who certainly require twisting, twisting for their required purpose does not help for spinning that was not for their required purpose, as seen from loose strands, etc. A sort of proof may be brought from Eruvin 96b, one who finds techeiles in tongues, they are invalid; strings, they are valid. The Gemara establishes this as when they are twisted and cut. Why did it not say, one who finds techeiles strings, they are invalid; twisted ones, they are valid? Rather, even white strings could have been spun not for their required purpose. According to the Baal HaIttur there would be a remedy through twisting. This proof is not decisive, and there is no need to prolong.
