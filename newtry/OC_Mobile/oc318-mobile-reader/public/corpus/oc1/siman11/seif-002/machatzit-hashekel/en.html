(s.k. 2') A gentile, etc. In siman 331 in Beit Yosef according to Rashba — even though one may disqualify [spinning by] a gentile, standing over him helps, for a gentile acts for his own intent and does not heed what a Jew commands him to do for its sake; nevertheless, regarding a cheresh and minor they are more lenient. (And a cheresh is one who does not hear and does not speak; a shoteh is one who loses what is given to him; a minor is under thirteen years, as stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 1 seif 5.) For cheresh and minor — in any case they have weakened da'at — standing over him helps; but a shoteh who has no da'at at all — standing over him does not help. He can be directed only for a get, for the matter is evident that he has some da'at, hears, and intends to fulfill what he was commanded — for he is commanded to write for its sake and he writes the names of the husband and wife. But for slaughter and making matzah for the mitzvah, where there is no proof, standing over him does not help; and if so, the same applies here regarding spinning, where there is no proof — standing over a shoteh does not help. But according to R' Yosef's view, anything that can be done through an agent — standing over him helps, for through standing over him it is as if he is his agent; accordingly, even though a shoteh is not fit for agency, nevertheless in such a case where standing over him is preferable to ordinary agency, if so, also for slaughter and matzah for the mitzvah and likewise spinning — standing over a shoteh helps; unlike chalitzah, which cannot be done through an agent, therefore standing over a shoteh does not help — see there in Peri Chadash.
