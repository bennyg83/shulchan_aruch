There. In Rama's gloss: “The custom is to be lenient.” Perishah concludes in accordance with the view that requires even the combing to be performed for the sake of the mitzvah. He cites Mahari of Prague, who derived this from the adjacent verses that teach that tzitzis may be made with shaatnez. The word shua refers to combing; it must therefore be done for the sake of the mitzvah.
