A gentile spins and a Jew stands over him and says he should do it for the sake — according to the Rambam invalid; according to the Rosh valid. Rema: The practice is that the Jew assists slightly, as stated below siman 32 s. 9 and Yoreh De’ah siman 271 regarding tefillin and a Torah scroll. They require twisting [shezira] and must be twisted for the sake.
