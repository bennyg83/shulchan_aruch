A gentile spins and a Jew stands over him and says he should do it for the sake — according to the Rambam invalid; according to the Rosh valid. Rema: The practice is that the Jew assists slightly, as stated below siman 32 s. 9 and <i>Yoreh De’ah</i> siman 271 regarding tefillin and a Torah scroll. They require twisting [<i>shezira</i>] and must be twisted for the sake. וצריכין שזירה ושיהיו שזורין לשמן:
