“Not less than four thumbbreadths.” They dispute this: Rif’s halacha — four within three as a triangle of four — meaning four long threads inserted within three fingers in the tallis as stated shortly — hanging four — Rambam and Rosh explain this “hanging four” applies to the whole length of tzitzis — the gdil where winding and knots are and also the anaf issuing after finishing windings and knots — altogether four — others cite Shulchan Aruch from R. Tam who holds this triangle applies only to gdil and anaf must be greater than gdil by two parts — as stated there explicitly — altogether twelve fingerbreadths — “fingers” meaning thumbbreadths — Rif’s handbreadth midoraisa six small five thumb four thumbbreadths — here we need a handbreadth from five fingers which are four thumbbreadths.
