[4] [Levush] And some are strict, etc. From this I wonder about Lechem Chamudos page 28 side a and Malbushi Yom Tov, who wrote that the Levush omitted what Rama wrote in the Shulchan Aruch — that some are strict even to comb the threads lishmah, and the custom is to be lenient regarding combing — because he holds one need not be concerned for this at all, as Beit Yosef wrote that he did not see any posek who mentioned this, nor anyone who was concerned about it — until here. But the Levush wrote Rama's words as they stand, and perhaps when Lechem Chamudos and Malbushi Yom Tov wrote these words they did not look at the Levush in this seif, which is seif 3, but in seif 1, since Rama wrote it in seif 1; and since they did not see it in seif 1, they thought the Levush omitted it. I also wonder at Beit Yosef: how did he write that he did not see any posek who mentioned this, etc., and did not see anyone concerned — yet the Agur, a great authority among the Acharonim, wrote thus and concluded that meticulous people in Ashkenaz practice accordingly; see there. I also found in the Prisha that he wrote in the name of the Gaon Maharal of Prague that Maharam learned from "you shall make gedilim for yourself" — we derive lishmah from "for yourself" — and he linked it to "you shall not wear sha'atnez" and we expound shu'a, tavei, and nuz. Shu'a means combing. The Bach also wrote on Beit Yosef that his challenge is not a [valid] challenge; therefore I say it is fitting for meticulous people to be strict in this.
