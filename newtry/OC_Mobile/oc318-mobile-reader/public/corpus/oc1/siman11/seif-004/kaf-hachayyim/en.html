14) [Seif 4] The length of the eight threads must be no less than four thumbbreadths; the measurement must be taken at the broad part of the thumb, not at its narrow tip. Beit Yosef writes this in the name of Mordechai. See also Olat Tamid, entry 4; Taz, se'if katan 8; Magen Avraham, se'if katan 5; Eliyah Rabbah, se'if katan 5; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, entry 5; Chayei Adam, rule 11, entry 23; and Ben Ish Chai there, entry 12. See our words below, entry 27.
15) The measure of the thumb of an average person is the width of seven hairs placed tightly side by side, or the length of two hairs measured across their width. See Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Sefer Torah, law 9, cited by Magen Avraham there; Shach in Yoreh De'ah, siman 272, se'if katan 1; Birkei Yosef there, entry 1; Shulchan Gavoah there, entry 3; Eliyah Rabbah there; and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, entry 6.
