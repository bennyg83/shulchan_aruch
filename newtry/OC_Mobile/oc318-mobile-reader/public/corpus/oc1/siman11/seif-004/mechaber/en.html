The length of the eight strands is not less than four thumbbreadths; some say twelve thumbbreadths — and such is the practice — and above [that] there is no upper limit. Rema: If one made them excessively long one may shorten — no issue of “you shall make” or “from what is already made” (Mordechai Hilchos Ketanos). One of the strands should be longer to wrap the gdil — the part of the tzitzis that is not woven. Rema: The stated measure applies to the tzitzis after tying, besides what lies on the garment’s corner (many Beit Yosef).
