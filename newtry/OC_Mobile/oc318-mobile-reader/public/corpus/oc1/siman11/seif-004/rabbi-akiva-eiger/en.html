Seif 2. “According to the Rosh, it is valid.” Beit Yosef derived this from the Rosh's words concerning tanning. This is difficult for me. On the contrary, according to the Rosh's distinction between a get and circumcision, from which the Rosh derived that an adult Jew's supervision is effective for tanning because it resembles circumcision, which is performed all at once, it follows that spinning resembles a get: every small portion that is spun must be for the sake of the mitzvah, just as every letter of a get must be written for its sake. For this very reason, a non-Jew's work is ineffective even when a Jew supervises him. This requires further study.
