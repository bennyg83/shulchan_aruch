From four. See Be'er Hetev; and see Magen Avraham in the name of Bedek HaBayit that Tosafos in the chapter HaTekheles were in doubt about the matter. And see in Turei Zahav siman 35, seif katan 34, that one should be stringent; and see in Beit Efrayim on lung treifa that there are two doubts in the matter — whether to measure at the narrow place of the thumb or at the place of the thumb-knot. And see there at length in the responsum of Maharam of Mainz in the laws of judgments, and there it was explained that in a rabbinic matter one may be lenient in this doubt; and there is a practical difference regarding a tallis that is not of wool and linen but of other kinds, for according to many poskim who hold they are only obligated rabbinically — he may rely even though there are not twelve thumbbreadths when measured at the wide place, only at the narrow place or at the knot place, if it is a time of constraint and he has no other tallis, and when he obtains longer ones than these he should untie these and tie the long ones beneath them. There is also a practical difference in this doubt regarding what is explained below in seif 9, that he should not distance the hole three thumbbreadths — for it appears one should be stringent and measure at the narrow place. And see Yad Efrayim what he wrote there.
