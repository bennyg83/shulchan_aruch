(s.k. 4') Enough, etc. Nevertheless regarding twisting he agrees it is valid — this is not Rabbeinu Tam who disqualifies if all broke even if enough to tie a bow remained; he agrees regarding twisting because for Rambam twisting is not required — that does not exist, for certainly Rabbeinu Tam was not concerned with Rambam's words and did not know him, and he was in the generation before him; rather the meaning is: even though Rema in siman 12 is stringent like Rabbeinu Tam, nevertheless regarding twisting they rely to be lenient like Rosh, for there is another side to be lenient, namely Rambam who does not require twisting.
