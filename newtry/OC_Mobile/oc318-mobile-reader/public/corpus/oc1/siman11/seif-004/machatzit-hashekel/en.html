(s.k. 4) So that, etc. Nevertheless, regarding twisting he concedes that it is valid. This does not mean that Rabbeinu Tam, who invalidates if all the strings tore even if enough remained, concedes regarding twisting because according to the Rambam twisting is not required; that cannot be, for Rabbeinu Tam certainly would not have been concerned for the words of the Rambam, nor did he know them, as he lived in the generation before him. Rather, it means that although Rama in siman 12 is stringent like the opinion of Rabbeinu Tam, nevertheless regarding twisting we rely leniently on the opinion of the Rosh, for there is also another ground for leniency, namely the Rambam, who does not require twisting.
