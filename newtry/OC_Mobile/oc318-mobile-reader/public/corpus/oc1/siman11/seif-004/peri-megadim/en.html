On account of combing residue. Eshel Avraham on Taz — leaving two threads is difficult for him, and Eshel Avraham on Bach. And even if one combed them out and spun them for its sake, they are invalid so that mitzvos not be disgraceful upon him; and even post facto it is invalid, and so it is in the Gemara Menachot 42b: from linsey-woolsey it is invalid even post facto. Eshel Avraham on Rama, note 8 — and this is straightforward.
