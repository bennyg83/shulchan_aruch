Because of. See Eshel Avraham on Taz, who had difficulty with the case of leftover warp threads; see Bach. Even if he combed them and spun them for their required purpose, they are invalid, so that mitzvos should not be degraded in his eyes. Even after the fact it is invalid, and so it is stated in the Gemara Menachos 42b, from loose strands it is invalid, even after the fact. See Eliyah Rabbah note 8. This is simple.
