There, se'if katan 13: At the end of Kilayim. Gloss: One may say that Baal HaIttur follows Rambam in chapter 1 of the laws of tzitzit, halakhah 7, where he writes that one of the eight strings should be tekhelet and seven should be white. Beit Yosef likewise writes in his name that one string is made half white and half tekhelet. It follows that the tekhelet is inserted through only one hole and creates only one joining. Were it not for the upper knot, there would be no kilayim. Perhaps Rambam also agrees with Baal HaIttur and was troubled by Magen Avraham's question; from this he derived his position that there is only half a string of tekhelet, contrary to Raavad there. See there. This is easy to understand.
