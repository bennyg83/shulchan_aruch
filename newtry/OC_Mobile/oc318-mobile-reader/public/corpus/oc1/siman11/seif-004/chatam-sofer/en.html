And one could say Baal HaIttur holds like Rambam, chapter 1 of Laws of Tzitzis, halachah 7, who wrote that one of eight threads shall be techeles and seven white; so too Beit Yosef in his name: one thread makes half white and half techeles — thus he does not insert the techeles except in one hole and it has only one wrapping; and were it not that one ties an upper knot there would be no kilayim. Perhaps the Rambam holds like the Ittur, and Magen Avraham's question was difficult for him; from this he proved his method that there is only half techeles, unlike the Ra'avad there — see there, requires investigation.
