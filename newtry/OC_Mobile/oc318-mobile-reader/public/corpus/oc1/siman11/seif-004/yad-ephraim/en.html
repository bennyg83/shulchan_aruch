Seif 4, in Rama: 'That is, the portion.' It appears that his intent is to prevent us from interpreting gedil here as it is ordinarily understood: first one braids and weaves an additional external thread around the eight threads and then winds the long thread around that braid. Rather, here it means the plain portion of the tzitzis, without a braid or weaving around it. It is called gedil based upon its eventual state, because after one winds thread around it, it is called a gedil. This entire phrase is absent from the Shulchan Aruch printed with Olat Tamid; see there. It appears not to be from Rama and may be from the author of Magen Avraham, as is also implied below in se'if katan 19; see there. That passage implies unlike what I wrote; that the word should not be read hachelek with a kamatz but straightforwardly with a tzere. It therefore appears more correct to emend the text to: 'the portion of the tzitzis that is not long.' This requires further study.
In Taz, se'if katan 5. The text should read: 'It was before the theft that he spun it.'
