Seif 4 in Rama — this means the portion). It appears he meant one should not say that here too the explanation of gdil is as everywhere — namely that at first one thickens and weaves around the strands with another strand from outside besides the eight, and afterward wraps with the long strand around that gdil; rather here he means the portion of the tzitzis, etc. — that is, as it is a portion without gdil with weaving around it, and he calls it gdil on account of its end — for after he wraps around it it is called gdil. And in Shulchan Aruch printed with Sefer Olas Tamid this is absent entirely — see there; it appears this is not from Rema, and perhaps it is from the author of Magen Avraham; and so it appears from below seif katan 19 — see there; and from there it appears not as I wrote to read the portion with kamatz but rather plainly with tzerei; therefore it is more plausible to emend "the portion of the tzitzis that is not long" — requires further study.
