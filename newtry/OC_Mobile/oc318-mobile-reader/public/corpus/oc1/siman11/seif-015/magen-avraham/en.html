(א) “Along the length.” There is not so much stringency to invalidate for that; still one should be very careful that tzitzis not hang diagonally on a corner (Beis Yosef). Levush wrote the reason is forbidden: it looks as if he needs eight tzitzis, so he places diagonally to discharge two directions—see there. It appears to me it is forbidden because it is Karaite practice to do so diagonally—and this requires study.

(ב) “That one not place,” etc. Some permit; accordingly even leather is permitted—see siman 10:4 and Mishnah Berurah and Beis Yosef here.

(ג) “And so they practice.” The reason—see siman 15.
