Seif 15, "the custom is," etc. See above seif 3; initially we require whole strands.

"Some say," etc. For so they explain what is said there, 42a: that it should hang, etc.

"Some say," etc. For it is written "the corner," meaning of the same kind as the corner.

"And there is," etc. For we rule: if it is of one fabric and its corners, etc., we follow the main garment. Even for the view that follows the corner, we follow the main part of the corner. The additions are made only to strengthen it. In chalitzah, a shoe covered with leather is valid because it is the main shoe and we are not concerned for interposition. Likewise a folded tallis is obligated per the Rabbis, even though one corner interposes between it and the other. See Beis Yosef.
