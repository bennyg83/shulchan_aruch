Magen Avraham, se'if katan 20. “If he wound all of it, it is invalid.” Even if he later unwound part of it so that some loose ends remain, it nevertheless constitutes taaseh velo min haasui. Since the invalidity was in the tzitzis themselves, the fact that they were valid at an earlier point is of no effect. See Magen Avraham below, siman 23.
