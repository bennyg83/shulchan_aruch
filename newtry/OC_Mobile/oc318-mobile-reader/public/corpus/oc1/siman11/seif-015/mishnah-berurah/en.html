Along the length of the tallit — see above in siman katan 52 what is length, and see Biur Halacha.

For we require, etc. — meaning whether at the time of hanging or afterwards if they slipped downward, he should return them; and all this is l'chatchila, but b'dieved there is no stringency in this; nevertheless he should be very careful that the tzitzit not hang diagonally on a corner point, because it is the custom of Karaites who do thus; and if they hang, it is a mitzvah to return them to their place.

Any garment — for we require that the tzitzit rest on the kanaf and not on something that rests on the kanaf.

And some permit — for they do this only to strengthen the garment lest it tear, and it is annulled relative to the garment; and even of leather it is permitted; and so agreed the poskim.
