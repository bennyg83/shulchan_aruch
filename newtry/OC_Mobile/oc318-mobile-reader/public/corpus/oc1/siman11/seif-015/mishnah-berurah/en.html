(72) Along the length of the tallis. See above in s.k. 52 what is meant by length, and see Biur Halachah.
(73) For we require, etc. This means whether at the time of hanging them or afterward. If they slipped downward, he should return them. All this is initially, but after the fact there is no concern about this. Nevertheless, one should at least be very careful that the tzitzis not hang diagonally over the corner, because this is the practice of the Karaites, who do so. If they are hanging that way, it is a mitzvah to return them to their place.
(74) Any garment. For we require that the tzitzis rest on the corner, and not on something that rests on the corner.
(75) Some permit. For they do this only to strengthen the garment so that it not tear, and it is subordinate to the garment. Even if it is leather it is permitted, and so the poskim agreed.
