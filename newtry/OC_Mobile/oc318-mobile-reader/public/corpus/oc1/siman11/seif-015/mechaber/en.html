Some say one must be careful to hang the tzitzis along the tallis’s length so they “drip” [nutfos] on the corner — hanging on the corner; if along the width they would not “drip” toward the ground. Some say one may not put any cloth in the tallis holes through which tzitzis are inserted; some permit — and such is the practice.
