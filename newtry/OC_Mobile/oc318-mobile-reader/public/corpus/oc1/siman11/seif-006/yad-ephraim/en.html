Seif 14 (in Shulchan Aruch there in Rama) in what Rema wrote "this means thumbbreadths" — apparently according to what Magen Avraham wrote above seif katan 5 that Tosafos were in doubt whether to measure at the wide place or the narrow place; if so, here one should measure at the narrow place stringently. I found in Sefer Machazik Berachah in the name of the Ari z"l that these three fingerbreadths are amah, kammatz, and zeret, and he brought support for his words from the words of Rabbeinu Yeshaya of Trani; and in Sefer Shalmei Tzibbur he wrote that Rabbeinu Yeshaya's words are cited in the words of Shelah Gevurim. He wrote that they hold one should measure at the narrow place, and three fingerbreadths on the thumb at the narrow place is one measure together with the three fingerbreadths mentioned — see there at length.
