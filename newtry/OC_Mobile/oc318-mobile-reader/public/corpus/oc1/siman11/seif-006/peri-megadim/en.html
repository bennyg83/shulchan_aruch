Strings. See Eshel Avraham on Taz. The explanation is that the language of borrowing implies that the item itself returns intact, while the language of lending indicates that it is given to be used up. When Reuven says to Shimon, lend me strings, even so his intent is presumably a loan, so that he may use them in a tallis, and this is not theft. See Kiddushin 47b.
