“Valid.” This implies acquisition through change of form, and from Rema’s plain wording it seems he holds even before yiush they are valid—so too gloss siman 349; but Beis Yosef writes acquisition requires yiush and change to the substance, and perhaps he holds it is a reversible change, as Beis Yosef writes, so he acquires only with yiush—as Choshen Mishpat 353:2 regarding such acquisition—see there siman 360:6 that it is a complete change. Eliyah Rabbah did not [read] there, hence wrote gloss 7 inappropriately. If the thief sold the tzitzis to another—see siman 349.
