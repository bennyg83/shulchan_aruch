If one made them from stolen wool they are invalid, for it says “they shall make from theirs.” Rema: Specifically if he stole the strands; but if he stole wool and made strands they are valid — yet lechatchila it is forbidden to make them (Beit Yosef citing Nimukei Yosef Hilchos Tzitzis).
