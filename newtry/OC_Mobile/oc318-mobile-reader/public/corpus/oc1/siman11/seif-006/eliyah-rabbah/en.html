[6] Twelve godelin, etc. The Levush: the reason is for the adornment of tzitzit. Olat Tamid was precise from this that the Levush holds the obligation of the mitzvah is only four godelin. In my humble opinion this is not so — for if so, how did the Levush not mention the view of the poskim that four suffice, which the Shulchan Aruch brought? Also the language "that the Sages gave" does not read well according to this; rather it is certain that he holds the obligation is twelve. That he wrote the reason is for adornment of tzitzit means he holds that by Torah law we find no measure for the length of the threads, as explained below at the beginning of siman 12 — that by Torah law any amount suffices; but the Sages gave a measure — according to one authority twelve, according to another four godelin — and for both the Sages' reason is adornment of tzitzit: for one authority four constitute adornment, for the other twelve; and the Levush ruled like the Sages — thus specifically twelve is the obligation of the mitzvah; and this is clear. I saw in the Semak and Kol Bo page 15 a reason for twelve godelin corresponding to the twelve tribes. Magen Avraham wrote: if he forgot to leave a thread long enough to wrap everything, he may wrap some coils from one thread and some from the second.
