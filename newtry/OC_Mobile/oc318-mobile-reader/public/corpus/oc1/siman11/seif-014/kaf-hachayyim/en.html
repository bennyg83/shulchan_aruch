14) [Seif 4] The length of the eight threads must be no less than four thumbbreadths; the measurement must be taken at the broad part of the thumb, not at its narrow tip. Beit Yosef writes this in the name of Mordechai. See also Olat Tamid, entry 4; Taz, se'if katan 8; Magen Avraham, se'if katan 5; Eliyah Rabbah, se'if katan 5; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, entry 5; Chayei Adam, rule 11, entry 23; and Ben Ish Chai there, entry 12.
