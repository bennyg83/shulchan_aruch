Seif 14, in the Shulchan Aruch there, in Rama: 'What Rama writes means thumb-breadths.' At first glance, according to Magen Avraham above, se'if katan 5, who writes that Tosafos were uncertain whether one measures at the broad or narrow point, here one should measure at the narrow point as a stringency. I found in Machazik Berachah in Arizal's name that these 'three fingers' are the middle, ring, and little fingers. He supports this from a manuscript of Rabbeinu Yeshayah. Shalmei Tzibbur writes that Rabbeinu Yeshayah's words are cited by Shiltei HaGibborim. These authorities hold that one measures at the narrow point, and three thumb-breadths measured at the thumb's narrow point equal the aforementioned three fingers. See there at length.
