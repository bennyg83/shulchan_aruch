(א) “And two-thirds anaf.” For so they say in the Gemara: the beauty of tekheles—one-third gedil and two-thirds anaf; and it is obvious this is not meakev bedieved.

(ב) “And in the second nine.” In the Arizal’s kavanos it is written “in the second eight,” and he explained there the reason according to kabbalah—and it is fitting to heed his tradition.
