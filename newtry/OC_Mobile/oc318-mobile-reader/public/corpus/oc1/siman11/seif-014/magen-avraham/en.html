(א) “There is no measure.” Even if he wrapped most of it, or wrapped only one chulyah—it is valid (Rambam); if he wrapped the entire gedil it is invalid (Beis Yosef).

(ב) “Four thumbbreadths.” Including the knots (Rosh). Rosh, Parashas Pinchas, Tosafos wrote there should be a thumbbreadth between knot and knot. Rosh wrote all chulyos should be equal—that is beauty for tzitzis—so in the first “air” [segment] the wraps should be farther apart, then in each following segment closer—see siman 32:14.

(ג) “And in the second nine.” Shelah wrote to make eight in the second, then it hints at fifteen as one; so Levush and Kavvanos. Lechem Chamudos siman 48 wrote that in King Shlomo’s tzitzis the first segment had ten wraps then five, matching the Tetragrammaton. Writings say one should make the gedil in chulyos, each chulyah three wraps—i.e., after three wraps leave a small gap—as the Rambam states.

(ד) “A knot,” etc. Levush writes we do not practice so in these lands, and I heard it is like adding to the knots—especially if they are tightly twisted and rarely come undone.
