He shall cut — Eshel Avraham on Taz; see Magen Avraham, letters 18 and 19. He raised that even if he tied near the corner two knots and wound one segment, as long as he did not tie two knots on the segment it is not incomplete, for he has not yet done anything — the mitzvah of tzitzis is gedil, as written "You shall make twisted cords for yourself," and the anaf of tzitzis, as "He took me by the tzitzit of my head" — we require loose ends above. If so, logically in Shulchan Aruch that if he wound after the knot as customary and tied afterward — this agrees with Magen Avraham 19, unlike Olas Tamid — see there. See what is written in Be'er Heitev, siman 15, chidushei din in the name of Damesek Eliezer.

And know that the upper knot of Torah law in tzitzis is two knots distinct from hibbur (joining), for regarding kilayim it is also not a connection, and tzitzis we require similar to kilayim — see below siman 16 in Magen Avraham, letter 20, with God's help it will be explained there. However if he made windings with hibbur as some do — not winding alone — if he tied one knot near the corner and then winding with hibbur, namely he loops the winding thread — the segment is fulfilled properly, it is proper making and one fulfills by Torah law. And on erev Shabbos when there is no time — this suffices; or if he wishes he may wind first and then tie two knots, and that is enough. One need not tie two knots near the corner; also incomplete if several were made invalidly.
