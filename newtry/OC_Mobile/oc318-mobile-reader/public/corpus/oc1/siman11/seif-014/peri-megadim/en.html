He concluded that even if one tied two knots near the corner and wound one winding section, as long as he did not tie two knots over the winding section, it is not considered make it, and not from what is already made, for he has not yet done anything. The mitzvah of tzitzis is a tied section, as it is written, you shall make tied sections for yourself, and the loose thread of tzitzis is like, He took me by the locks of my head, meaning we require separate strands above. If so, this is the explanation in the Shulchan Aruch, that if he wound after the knot in the usual way and also tied afterward. This agrees with Magen Avraham 19, unlike the Olas Tamid; see there. And know. The upper knot, which is according to the Torah in tzitzis, is two knots one upon the other, not a bow, for regarding kilayim too a bow is not considered a connection, and tzitzis must be analogous to kilayim. See below, siman 316, in Magen Avraham note 20, and with Hashem's help it will be explained there. However, if he made windings with a bow, like some do, not winding alone, meaning one knot near the corner and afterward a winding with a bow, that is, he loops the winding string, the winding section is properly maintained, it is a proper act of making, and he fulfills with it according to the Torah. On erev Shabbos, when there is no time, this is sufficient. Or, if he wishes, he may first wind and afterward tie two knots, and that is sufficient; he does not need two knots near the corner. It also becomes a case of make it, and not from what is already made, if some of it was made in invalidity.
