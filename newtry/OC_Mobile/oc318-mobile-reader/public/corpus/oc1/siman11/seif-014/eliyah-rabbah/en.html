[14] To make a lamb, etc. — Beit Yosef and Nachalat Tzvi wrote the reason: not everyone knows the law thoroughly, and they will come to say it is invalid, or they will say that after the threads were repaired he inserted the tzitzit — until here. In my humble opinion the reason is as plainly understood: certainly l'chatchila one should make it so the threads will not disconnect but remain on the corner — for even if disconnected it is valid; nevertheless l'chatchila it is preferable that they not disconnect. This appears to be the Levush's view, and so the Bach.
