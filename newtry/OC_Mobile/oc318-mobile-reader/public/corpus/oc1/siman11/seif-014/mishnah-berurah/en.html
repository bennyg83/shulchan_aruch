For the windings — meaning for the count of windings; and also the count of chulyot and knots does not prevent according to law, only it is the optimal mitzvah, for the five knots hint that through them one will remember the five books of the Torah; and doubling the five knots, which amounts to ten, hints at the ten sefirot of the Holy One blessed be He, as below in Siman 24; and the reason for the chulyot, see Beit Yosef.

Only that they should be, etc. — and what is written in seif 13, that if he wound even one chulya — I will explain the root of the matter: for Chazal received that tzitzit require a gadil, meaning that it be wound, as stated "gedilim you shall make for yourself," and also that there be separate strings, as written tzitzit, like the tzitzit of my head. But from the Torah there is no measure for the length of the gadil; rather even if he wound only three windings and made above them a knot, this too is a halacha to Moshe at Sinai, and through it the name gadil is fulfilled, and with this the mitzvah from the Torah is already complete; and this is the reason for the words of the Shulchan Aruch there. And on this one may rely on erev Shabbos with nightfall when he has no leisure to make it properly, and it is permitted to go out in it on Shabbos and bless (and better that he then also make the first knot before the chulya as we practice, to satisfy also the view of some poskim who hold that on this knot Chazal intended, for it is from the Torah). And all this is from the Torah; but rabbinically one must make a third gadil and two-thirds patil; and since the length of the strings is twelve thumb-widths, as above in seif 4, he should make four thumb-widths gadil, which are the windings, and leave eight thumb-widths of separate strings, for such is the adornment of tzitzit. And therefore if he made one chulya on erev Shabbos with nightfall, he must complete immediately after Shabbos, for otherwise he transgresses the words of the Sages; and some stumble in this.

The width, etc. — meaning l'chatchila; but b'dieved even if he wound most of it, it is valid, for in any case there is also patil here; but if he wound all of it, it is invalid.

Four thumb-widths — with the knots; and in Raya Mehemna it is written that between knot and knot should be a full thumb-width. The Rosh wrote that all the chulyot should be equal, for that is the adornment of tzitzit; and therefore in the first space he should make the windings far from each other, and afterwards in each space more closed [Magen Avraham].

And two parts hanging — see Biur Maharshal on Semag; and everyone agrees it does not prevent b'dieved, as above.

And in the second, nine — and in Kavanos it is written in the second eight; and the later authorities agreed that seven and eight are the name Yud-Heh; and afterwards eleven, and combined it is the name Havayah; and afterwards thirteen, the gematria of echad, and this is Heaven echad. And in the tzitzit of R. Shlomo Molcho, in the first space ten windings and afterwards five, etc., like the name Havayah [Magen Avraham]; and see Levush and Eliyah Rabbah further on windings.

At the end of each string — see above in siman katan 18 as written there. Good to make the strings medium, not thick and not thin, because of "This is my God and I will glorify Him" [Eliyah Rabbah].
