(65) For the windings. That is to say, for the number of windings, and the number of winding sections and knots also does not invalidate according to the law. It is only for the choicest fulfillment of the mitzvah, for the five knots are an allusion through which one will remember the five books of the Torah, and the doubling of the five knots, which totals ten, alludes to the ten sefiros of the Holy One, blessed be He, as stated below in siman 24. For the reason for the winding sections, see the Beis Yosef.
(66) Only that they should be, etc. As for what is stated in seif 13, that if he wound even one winding section, I will explain the root of the matter. Chazal received that we require in tzitzis a tied section, meaning that it be wound, as it is written, you shall make tied sections for yourself, and that it also have separate strings, as it is written, tzitzis, like the verse, by the locks of my head. But according to the Torah there is no measure for the length of the tied section. Even if he wound only three windings and made a knot above it, which is also a halachah received by Moshe at Sinai, the name tied section is fulfilled through this, and with this the mitzvah is already completed according to the Torah. This is the reason for the words of the Shulchan Aruch there, and one may rely on this on erev Shabbos near dark when he has no time to make it properly, and he may go out with it on Shabbos and recite a blessing. It is better then also to make the first knot before the winding section, as we practice, in order to satisfy the view of some poskim who hold that Chazal meant that this knot is according to the Torah. All this is according to the Torah, but D'Rabbanan one must make one third tied section and two thirds loose thread. Since the length of the strings is twelve thumbbreadths, as above in seif 4, he should make four thumbbreadths as tied section, meaning the windings, and leave eight thumbbreadths as separate strings, for this is the beauty of the tzitzis. Therefore, if he made one winding section on erev Shabbos near dark, he must complete it immediately after Shabbos, for otherwise he transgresses the words of the Sages, and some stumble in this.
(67) The width, etc. This is initially, but after the fact, even if he wound most of it, it is valid, for in any event there is also thread here. But if he wound all of it, it is invalid.
(68) Four thumbbreadths. Together with the knots. In Raaya Mehemna it is written that there should be a full thumbbreadth between one knot and the next. The Rosh wrote that all the winding sections should be equal, for this is the beauty of tzitzis. If so, in the first space he should make the windings distant from one another, and afterward, in each space, closer together. See Magen Avraham.
(69) And two parts as loose thread. See the explanation of Maharshal on the Semag. According to all views, this does not invalidate after the fact, as above.
(70) And in the second, nine. In the Kavanos it is written that in the second there are eight. So the later authorities agreed, that seven and eight form the name of Yud Hei, and afterward eleven, and together it is the name of Havayah, and afterward thirteen, the numerical value of one, and this is, Hashem is One. In the tzitzis of Rav Shlomo Molcho, there were ten windings in the first space and then five, etc., corresponding to the name Havayah. See Magen Avraham. See also the Levush and Eliyah Rabbah further regarding the windings.
(71) At the end of each string. See above in s.k. 18 what is written there. It is good to make the strings medium, neither thick nor thin, because of, this is my God and I will beautify Him. See Eliyah Rabbah.
