Initially, it is good to tie the threads. Lechem Chamudot writes that he saw that the tzitzit of Rabbi Shlomo Molcho were not tied. Magen Avraham writes, 'I too have heard this,' because tying them is considered adding to the knots. This is especially so when they are well twisted, in which case tying them would add to the knots rather than merely prevent them from unraveling.
