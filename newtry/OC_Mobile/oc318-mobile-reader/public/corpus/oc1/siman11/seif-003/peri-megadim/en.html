One may not. See Eshel Avraham on Taz and the Gemara Menachos 41b; see there. In Baer HaGolah, the citation to 39a is a scribal error; there it says that the beauty of techeiles is one third tied section, and this is explained below in seif 14. From the Shulchan Aruch it appears like the first view, stated anonymously, while the Levush brought only the second view, that the obligation is twelve thumbbreadths aside from what rests on the garment. If so, the sellers of tzitzis who are exacting and narrow in the measure of their length do not act properly, and the matter is close to a blessing in vain, for according to the Torah any amount suffices, while D'Rabbanan we require twelve thumbbreadths after the tying, aside from what is on the garment. They should be warned about this. See Eliyah Rabbah note 6. Know that if all the strings snapped up to the tied section, which is invalid as explained in siman 12, seif 1, if he is on the road and has no other tzitzis, he should untie the tied section, which is four thumbbreadths or more, and make some as tied section and some as loose thread. According to the Mechaber, he thereby fulfills the mitzvah initially and recites a blessing. Even according to us, who require twelve thumbbreadths, he should wear it, but whether to recite a blessing is doubtful. According to the Torah, any amount suffices, and he does not transgress the positive commandment of tzitzis. However, he must untie the whole tied section, for otherwise it is make it, and not from what is already made, since it was invalid according to the Torah, for we require a tied section and loose thread, as we will say below, with Hashem's help. So it appears to me. Folding the garment helps.
