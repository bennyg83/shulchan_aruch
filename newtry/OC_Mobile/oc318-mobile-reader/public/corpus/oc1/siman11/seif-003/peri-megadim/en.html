There is no [basis for this]. Eshel Avraham on Taz — the Gemara Menachot 41b, see there (Be'er HaGolah 9:39:1 there, nuye tekhelet one-third gedil, and it is explained below, seif 14). And from his words it implies like opinion 1 in the stam, and Levush brought only opinion 2 that the obligation is twelve thumbbreadths besides what rests on the garment; if so, tzitzit sellers do ill who minimize their length measure, and the matter is close to a blessing in vain, for nevertheless any amount suffices, and rabbinically we require twelve thumbbreadths after tying, apart from on the garment. One should warn them about this. Eshel Avraham on Rama, note 6: Know that if all the threads broke up to the gedil it is invalid, as explained in siman 12, seif 1; if he is on the road and has no other tzitzit, he may untie the gedil, which is four thumbbreadths or more, and make part gedil and part anaf; for the Mechaber this is valid ab initio and he may bless. And even for us, twelve thumbbreadths — he may wear it, but to bless there is doubt; nevertheless any amount suffices and one does not violate the positive commandment of tzitzit. However he must untie the entire gedil, for if not so it would be incomplete, since they were invalidated — nevertheless we require gedil and anaf, as we need to say below, im yirtzeh Hashem. It appears to me that doubling the garment helps.
