(3) Regarding b'dieved — when is it called b'dieved? One authority says: once that thing was made, it is called b'dieved — e.g. here, once it was spun it is b'dieved and permitted to insert it in the garment. Another says it is not called b'dieved unless that thing is finished — e.g. here, when he inserted the tzitzit in the garment, as written in Sefer HaGazit on the matter of a two-panel curtain siman 272 sign 7; see there — and the same here. Therefore one who guards his soul should avoid this and not take such tzitzit except in a time of pressure.
