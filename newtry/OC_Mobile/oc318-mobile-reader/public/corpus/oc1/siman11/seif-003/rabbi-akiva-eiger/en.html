There. “Or he should instruct a woman.” This is alluded to in the verse, “Speak to Bnei Yisrael, and say to them that they shall make for themselves tzitzis.” We find that dibbur denotes firm speech and does not pertain to women, while amirah denotes gentle speech and does pertain to women, as Rashi explains in his commentary on the Chumash regarding, “So shall you say to the house of Yaakov,” etc. Thus, “Speak to Bnei Yisrael” means that the mitzvah itself principally applies to the sons of Israel and not the daughters of Israel. Since making the tzitzis (that is, spinning) is women's work, however, the verse continues, “and say to them that they shall make.” This means that one should say to them, “Make tzitzis for my tallis.” Derishat Shalom.
