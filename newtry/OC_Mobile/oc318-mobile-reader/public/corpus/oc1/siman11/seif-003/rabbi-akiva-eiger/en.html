There — or that he say to a woman. Therefore Scripture hinted: “Speak to the children of Israel” and “say to them… and make for themselves tzitzit.” For we find that “speak” is harsh and not applicable to women, and “say” is soft, applicable to women, as Rashi explained in the Chumash: “Thus shall you say to the house of Jacob,” etc. And this is “speak to the children of Israel” — namely, the essence of the mitzvah is for the sons of Israel and not for the daughters of Israel. But since the making, which is spinning, is women's work, therefore “and say to them… and make” — the intent is that he say to them, “Make tzitzit for me for a tallit.” End quote.
