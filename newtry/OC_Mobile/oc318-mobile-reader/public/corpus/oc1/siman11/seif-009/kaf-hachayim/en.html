(9) If a deaf-mute, fool, or minor spun and an adult stands over them. Magen Avraham subsection 2 wrote: it depends on a dispute written in siman 460 in Beit Yosef. But Eliyah Rabbah subsection 1 wrote that here one may be more lenient, for there the Shulchan Aruch ruled plainly that it is invalid in processing by a non-Jew with a Jew standing over him, whereas here he wrote two views — and thus Rama's view is clarified — until here. So too Rabbi Zvi sign 4 to validate. See Korban Aharon sign 2 — it appears one may validate only in a time of pressure, with an adult standing over and assisting, as Chayei Adam wrote regarding a non-Jew, for our master there compared them to each other.
