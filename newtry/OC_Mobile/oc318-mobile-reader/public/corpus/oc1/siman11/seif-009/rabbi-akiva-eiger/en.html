Magen Avraham, subsection 10 — only with despair. Accordingly one may say that what the one mentioned earlier called change of substance means change of name, as Taz wrote, for that acquires with despair, as stated in Choshen Mishpat (siman 353), and its source is from the words of Tosafos on folio 38 of Sukkah regarding avoncheri. But change of action that reverts to its original state with despair does not acquire, as is proven from the passage there, where we answer that it is a change that reverts to its original state, even though there was despair there. It is true that I saw in Lefrasah, Choshen Mishpat in that siman, that he wrote plainly that likewise for change of action that reverts to its original state, despair acquires, see there; but it is astonishing in my humble opinion as above. Afterward a great one came to my hand, Sefer Shaar HaMelekh, and I saw that in chapter 1 of the laws of divorce he challenged him as above.
