“Meaning,” thumbbreadths — at the wide part of the thumb not from the tip at the narrow place — so Beit Yosef.

“Straight.” Meaning along the garment’s length — corner means end of garment — its edges. Beit Yosef cited Mahari Ashkenazi: the reason must be within three [thumbbreadths] because less than three fingers is not called garment — must be full thumb-knot because not called corner except that measure — end quote — he wrote he did not understand his words — even if not called garment less than three — corner of garment it is called — end quote — wondrous he did not explain Mahari Ashkenazi plainly — surely for this very reason less than three is not called garment — therefore they required placing tzitzis where not called garment but corner of garment — beginning right below three and ending thumb-knot measure — afterward not even corner — tzitzis place is on corner of garment specifically — surely Beit Yosef in haste did not look well — all agree.
