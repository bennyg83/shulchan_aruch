(א) “Meaning,” thumbbreadths—at the wide part of the thumb, not from its tip at the narrow place—so Beit Yosef.

(ב) Along the length. Meaning along the length of the garment — for corner means the end of the garment, namely its edges. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Mahari Ashkenazi, and this is his language: the reason it must be within three is that less than three fingerbreadths is not called a garment; and that it must be a full thumb-knot is because it is not called a corner except at this measure, etc., end quote. And he wrote he did not understand his words — that even if less than three is not called a garment, corner of a garment it is called, etc., end quote. It is wondrous that he did not explain Mahari Ashkenazi's words according to their plain sense; surely for this very reason that less than three is not called a garment — therefore they required placing tzitzis where it is not called garment but corner of garment — beginning immediately below three and ending at thumb-knot measure — afterward not even corner is it called; and the place of tzitzis is on corner of garment specifically. Surely Beit Yosef in haste did not look well here — all agree on this.
