9) If a deaf-mute, mentally incompetent person, or minor spun them while an adult supervised: Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2, writes that this depends upon the dispute recorded by Beit Yosef in siman 460. Eliyah Rabbah, se'if katan 1, however, writes that there is greater room for leniency here. There, Shulchan Aruch rules without qualification that tanning by a non-Jew under Jewish supervision is invalid, whereas here he records two opinions. Rama's view also appears to be so; this concludes his words. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, entry 4, likewise validates it; see Kuntres Acharon, entry 2. It appears that it should be validated only in pressing circumstances, when an adult supervises and assists, as Chayei Adam writes regarding a non-Jew, since Maran there compares the two cases.
