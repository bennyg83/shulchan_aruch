Seif 9, "that is thumb-breadths." The Gemara there.**** END BLOCK ****

"Because it is stated," etc. There, in Rav's statement.**** END BLOCK ****

"And if it was," etc. Semag; R' Levi — from where one ties a thumb-breadth until three fingers is called kanaf, and above that is not kanaf but garment, while below is called keren. "On the corners" means on the kanaf itself. Beis Yosef.**** END BLOCK ****

"And we measure," etc. Do not say that the angle of the garment is called keren, and therefore the distancing from the keren mentioned there is from the angle. Rather, the end of the garment generally is called keren. Beis Yosef.**** END BLOCK ****
