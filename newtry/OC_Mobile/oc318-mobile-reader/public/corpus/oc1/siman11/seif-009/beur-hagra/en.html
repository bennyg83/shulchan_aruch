Seif 9, "that is thumb-breadths." The Gemara there.
"Because it is stated," etc. There, in Rav's statement.
"And if it was," etc. Semag; meaning that from a thumb-joint until three fingers is called corner, and above that is not corner but garment, while below is called edge. "On the corners" means in the corner itself. Beis Yosef.
"And we measure," etc. Do not say that the angle of the garment is called the edge, and therefore the distancing from the edge mentioned there means from the angle. Rather, the end of the garment generally is called edge. Beis Yosef.
