In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 20: 'Even if he wound, etc.' The requirement that the entire wound portion measure four thumb-breadths applies ideally. After the fact, even if he wound thread around most of the strands, it is valid. See Beit Yosef, who cites Nimukei Yosef in Rashi's name that there must be a free-hanging portion after the wound section, rather than placing the wound section at the ends of the threads.
