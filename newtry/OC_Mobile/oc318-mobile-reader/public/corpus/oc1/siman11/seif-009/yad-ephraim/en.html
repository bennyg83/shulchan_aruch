(In Magen Avraham seif katan 20) Even if he wound, etc. — only that the entire winding be four thumbbreadths, namely l'chatchila; but b'dieved even if he wound over most of the strands, etc., it is valid; and see in Beit Yosef that Nimukei Yosef wrote in the name of Rashi that there should be anaf after the gdil and he should not make the gdil at the end of the strands.
