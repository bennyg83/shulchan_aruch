Eshel Avraham on Taz — this is the explanation of “see his words” on Tur: make a hole in the length of the tallit three fingerbreadths near the corner; there are those who explained “corner” as measured diagonally. And it is invalid if less than two-fifths of a thumb-joint knot; rather “corner” is the edge of the garment, and likewise in width one measures so that their words appear consistent. He explained Mahari'a's words according to their plain sense, and so Bach wrote; and therefore the hole must be within three fingerbreadths — for if one distanced a full three fingerbreadths and immediately made the hole, it is invalid. Beit Yosef does not hold so; rather a full three fingerbreadths is still called a corner and not a garment — see there, for so it is in the name of Rashi and Rambam, and Bach who wrote that everywhere Rambam disagrees, see there. And in impurity we derive from 46 a garment — one could say a garment is only from 46 that we derive; or one could say three full fingerbreadths is called corner and garment, more than three fingerbreadths is not called corner; nevertheless one should be careful to make it within three fingerbreadths, as Bach wrote, and so it is implied from Mahari'a and Maharan Chaviv, see there in Beit Yosef from this.
