Straight. See Eshel Avraham on Taz. This explains what the Tur wrote, that he should make the hole along the length of the tallis, three fingerbreadths near the corner. Some explained corner to mean that one measures diagonally, but that is invalid, for it is less by two fifths of a thumb joint. Rather, corner means the edge of the garment, and the same applies along the width, that one measures this way, for their words appear correct. He explained the words of Mahari Abuhav according to their plain meaning, and so wrote the Bach. According to this, the hole must be within three fingerbreadths, for if he distanced it a full three fingerbreadths and immediately made the hole, it is invalid. The Beis Yosef does not hold this way, but rather that even a full three fingerbreadths is still called corner and not garment; see there, where he wrote so in the name of Rashi and the Rambam. As for the Bach, who wrote that so it appears from the Rambam, I do not know; see there. Regarding impurity, we include it from the phrase or garment. One may say that it is not a garment, only that or comes to include it, or one may say that a full three fingerbreadths is called both corner and garment, while more than three fingerbreadths is not called corner. Nevertheless, one should be careful to make it within three fingerbreadths, as the Bach wrote, and so it appears from Mahari Abuhav and Maharai Chaviv; see there in the Beis Yosef regarding this.
