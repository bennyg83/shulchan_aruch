The tallis. The Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Baal HaItur that some say one should make two holes like hinges, insert the tzitzis into them, and bring them out to one side, etc. One who comes to be stringent upon himself in such a matter is not among the stringent, but among those who cause wonder, for it appears like arrogance. End quote of the Beis Yosef. Baal HaItur wrote that with a tallis katan one should make two holes, for in this case it does not appear like arrogance, since it is not visible at all. So too it is written in the kavanos that the Arizal conducted himself this way. See Magen Avraham.
More than three fingers. It seems to me that if he made the hole above three, even if, when he tied the tzitzis, he made the threads on the garment short so that the corners folded over and it is below three, it is invalid. The same applies in the opposite direction with the measure of a thumb-joint, that it is valid. Magen Avraham.
Corner. If after he made tzitzis in it he cut at the hole so that the tzitzis would hang lower, it is invalid because of the rule, make it and not from what was already made. A wool tallis had been fitted with tzitzis according to the law, and it was found in the silk braid made at the edge of the tallis that there were linen threads in it, so that they had to pull out the braid to detach it from the tallis. Whether the tzitzis of this tallis are invalid because of make it and not from what was already made, see the responsum Ginas Veradim, Yoreh Deah, at its end, where he wrote that it is proper to be stringent and untie the tzitzis and then tie them again; see there.
