One makes a hole along the length of the tallis — not higher than three fingerbreadths [thumbbreadths — Kol Bo siman 22; Maimonides; Hagahos Semak siman 31] from the corner — for [higher] is not called a “corner”; nor lower than the measure from the thumb joint to the nail tip, because it says “on the corner,” and if lower than a full thumb-knot it would be under the corner.
