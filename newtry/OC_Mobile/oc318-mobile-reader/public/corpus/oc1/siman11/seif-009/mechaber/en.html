One should make the hole along the length of the tallis, no higher than 3 fingerbreadths, meaning thumbbreadths, from the corner (Kol Bo, siman 22; Maimoniyot; Hagahot Semak, siman 31), because above that point it is not called the corner; and no lower than the measure from the thumb joint to the end of the nail, because the verse says 'upon the corner,' and if it were lower than a full thumb joint it would be beneath the corner. {Rama: This measurement is taken in a straight line from the corner, not diagonally (Beit Yosef).}
