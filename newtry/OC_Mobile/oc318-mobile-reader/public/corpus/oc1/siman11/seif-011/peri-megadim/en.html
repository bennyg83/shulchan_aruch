Within, etc. — Eshel Avraham on Taz: at first glance he explains the length of the tallis as a man's height, and in the garment's width as what one wraps — opposite Magen Avraham, letter 10; the reason: in the garment's width, which is the tallis's length [when worn], a man's height — the tallis's end from the edge near the ground is called a corner and requires a thumb-knot; unlike the garment's length along the man's width, which is not called a corner at all, and even less than a thumb-knot is valid. One who examines the Tur and Beit Yosef cannot explain except as Magen Avraham wrote, letter 9, and one must say "and etc." refers to the latter part, and "some say" refers to the reason the main corner applies to width and length is not so much called a corner — as I explained in the gloss; if so, all the more so width requires a thumb-knot. However more than three finger-widths is obviously invalid in length and width — Eliyahu Rabbah and Levush.
