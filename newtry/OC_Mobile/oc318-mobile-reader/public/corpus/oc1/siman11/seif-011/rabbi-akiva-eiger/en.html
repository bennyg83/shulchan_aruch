Taz, se'if katan 7. “He did not commit a transgression.” If so, according to his view it should be invalid. In truth, however, it is clear that this is not included in the disqualification of mitzvah habaah ba'aveirah, because the mitzvah does not result from the transgression, as Tosafos writes at the beginning of the third chapter of Sukkah regarding an esrog of an asheirah.
