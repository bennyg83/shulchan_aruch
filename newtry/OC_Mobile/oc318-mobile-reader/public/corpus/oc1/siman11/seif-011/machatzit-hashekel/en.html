(s.k. 11) And like him, etc. For one who borrowed a tallis with tzitzis recites a blessing on them. Presumably, the lender’s intent is that if the borrower cannot fulfill his obligation with a borrowed item, he gives it to him as a gift on condition that it be returned. If the lender is a person who knows that one may not recite a blessing on a borrowed tallis and nevertheless used language of borrowing, see what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 651.
