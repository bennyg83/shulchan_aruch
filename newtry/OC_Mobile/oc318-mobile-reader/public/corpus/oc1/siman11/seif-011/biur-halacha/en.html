He should not attach it to it. Talleisim that have at the edge of the garment a woven strip resembling techeiles and doubled threads, this is not an edge but the actual garment. Peri Megadim.
And it is good that he measure, etc. Eretz HaChaim wrote in the name of Birkei Yosef and other later authorities that the same applies to our talleisim, which have a blue color at the edge; one measures three fingers from the corner, and this blue color is included in the measure.
