If the gdil — called orlayu — is wide, one does not tie tzitzis on it; if one tied on it, it is invalid, for “on the corners of their garments” is written — and this is not considered part of the garment; but it counts toward the measure of a full thumb-knot and the three fingerbreadths [from the edge] since the hole is within the garment. Rema: Good to measure the thumb-knot without the gdil, and [the hole] within three fingerbreadths including the gdil (Beit Yosef citing Rashi and Rashba).
