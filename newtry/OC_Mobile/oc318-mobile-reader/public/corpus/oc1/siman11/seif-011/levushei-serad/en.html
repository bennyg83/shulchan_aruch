(There) Therefore it is a connection, and the second difficulty is settled — the Gemara proves that one must make two knots; and what he called "upper knot" means tying once, which is two knots one on the other. The first difficulty too for the one who holds that one makes two holes is settled; but according to my view the Gemara proves only one knot. Nevertheless what the Gemara challenged according to my view — "tight, tight once" — is not resolved.
