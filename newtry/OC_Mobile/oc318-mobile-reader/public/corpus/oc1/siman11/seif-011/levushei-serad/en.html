(ibid.) One time is considered a connection, and the second difficulty is resolved. The Shas proves that one must make two knots. What it said, the upper knot, means one act of tying, which is two knots one atop the other. Also the first difficulty, according to the view that one makes two holes, is resolved, for according to him the Shas proves only one knot. Therefore, the difficulty that the Shas asked according to him, one who sews one stitch, is not answered.
