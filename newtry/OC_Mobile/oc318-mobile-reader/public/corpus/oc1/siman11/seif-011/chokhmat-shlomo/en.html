Se'if 11, in the gloss: It is preferable to measure the width of the knot, etc. I do not know why this should be so, because an explicit Mishnah establishes that the hem is included as part of the garment. Keilim 28:7 states: Regarding the measure of three by three that the Sages prescribed, R' Shimon excludes the hem, while the Sages require an exact three by three. This explicitly proves that the hem is otherwise part of the garment. Why, then, should it not be counted within the thumbbreadth measurement? Study this carefully.
