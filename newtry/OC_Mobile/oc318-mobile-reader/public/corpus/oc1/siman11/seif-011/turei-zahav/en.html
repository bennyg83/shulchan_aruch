“But it counts toward the measure.” One could ask: why does it count, since it is not considered part of the garment—for the garment ends near the gdil and it is under the corner, and we require “on the corner” as above? One could say the main point is what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashi at the end of Bava Kamma and Rashba—namely, weavers’ practice at the garment end to weave two or three threads distant from the weave, doing so to save the garment so its weaving not be hidden by laundry; and those threads, if they slip off in laundering, the launderer removes them, etc., end quote. Now one could ask: truly, since he has not yet removed the gdil and intends to leave it with the garment, it is properly included in “the corners of their garments” for the thumb-knot measure; and if so, to make tzitzis in this way—no, for there is concern the launderer will remove the gdil as Rashi wrote; then even if he leaves a place where there will be no tzitzis in the garment at all, for the thumb measure it counts—even if it will later be ruined when the launderer removes it—nevertheless it is valid, since at the time of making it had the measure, as siman 12 stated above on this; only that Rama wrote it is good to measure, etc., and it is valid according to all.
