(א) Even one who needs etc., and they did not say "make your Shabbat ordinary" — meaning only for one whose time is very pressed, who has only what they give him from charity; but when he has nothing and wishes to borrow for this, R' Akiva did not say to make his Shabbat ordinary, rather as chazal said: the Holy One blessed be He said, "My children, borrow on My account and I will pay"; but if it is possible for him, he must honor Shabbat according to his ability; nevertheless he should not borrow unless he has with what to repay, and he will merit to become wealthy, as chazal said in the chapter "All writings" — the wealthy in all lands, in what do they merit to become wealthy? Because they honor Shabbat; from this we learn that one must honor Shabbat before he becomes wealthy, for all his sustenance is fixed for a person from Rosh Hashana to Rosh Hashana except for Shabbat and Yom Tov expenses and the expenses of his children for Torah study (Gemara), and some say also Rosh Chodesh expenses — what they bring the children to their teacher on Rosh Chodesh (Ateret Zekenim in the name of the Yerushalmi), and so too in Pesikta, and the acronym for all expenses fixed for a person in Tishrei except for Tishrei — the acronym is Torah study, Shabbat, Rosh Chodesh, Yom Tov (I heard in the name of Maharan of Krakow):
