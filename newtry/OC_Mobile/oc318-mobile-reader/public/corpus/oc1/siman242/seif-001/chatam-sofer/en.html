In Magen Avraham seif kaf alef "and proof from mishnah." Niddon: and the subject is not comparable to the proof, for there it is not that they hold to forbid, but only that the rest is not obligatory upon her; nevertheless, if she wishes she may also bring the rest, and presumably they did so after the pairs became cheap; not so if Shabbat passed without fish — he cannot repair it. And further, there it is to save the poor person from the prohibition of kares, but here the poor person whose hand does not reach is exempt in any event; it is proper to enact and break from being misled by expensive prices, but to learn from this to another place requires further study; and see in Rambam's commentary on the mishnah end of chapter 4 of Gittin and Tosefot Yom Tov there; and see Magen Avraham later at the beginning of siman 456:
