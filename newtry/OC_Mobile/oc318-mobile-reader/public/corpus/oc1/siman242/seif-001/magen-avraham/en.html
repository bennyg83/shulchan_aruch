(א) To honor Shabbat. And one should not have less than two cooked dishes (Zohar Bereishis page 130), and in Tikunei Shabbat — that one should eat at each meal of the three Shabbat meals fish on erev Shabbos; and it appears to me that each one according to his nature, as is written in siman 288 seif 4. If the pairs become expensive — fish — it is proper to enact that they not buy fish, and proof from the mishnah at the end of chapter 1 of Kerisos (Tzemach Tzedek). It is written in Sefer Olas Shabbos — it appears from the sugya of Pesachim that even though on Yom Tov we require half for Heaven, on Shabbat if he wishes to do everything "for you" he does, provided he not nullify the time of prayer on erev Shabbos; and I say — the matter has been forgotten and is not said, for behold it is also written "Shabbat for the Heaven your Heaven"; rather this is what it means — even though R' Eliezer disagrees on Yom Tov and says either entirely for Heaven etc., on Shabbat it requires also "for you" — so in the Tur and in the Gemara, and he did not copy the word "also," and I am inclined that the version is mistaken — it misled Peri Megadim, R' Tzvi, and Beit Yosef at the beginning of siman 288:

(ב) That the time is very pressed — that is, one who has food for fourteen meals to eat all the days of the week, two meals each day — he should not take from charity to eat a third meal, and so we say in the chapter "All writings" on erev Shabbos; and so too Beit Yosef see there, and Rambam in his commentary on the mishnah wrote that he should not eat on a weekday at all and eat a third meal on Shabbat, and this is as they answer in the Gemara initially; and he holds that we do not establish it in the Gemara according to R' Akiva but according to R' Chidka, see there; nevertheless, if he has only two meals, he should not take from charity to eat a third meal, and so too Beit Yosef; see siman 573 seif 2. One may borrow with interest for the need of a Shabbat meal or a mitzvah meal (Yerushalmi):

(ג) Honor of Shabbat — meaning that they not launder on erev Shabbos so that they be free for the honor of Shabbat:

(ד) To make from them loaves — and he brings in Darkei Moshe adjacent to this from what the Tur wrote in siman 242 that one should eat non-sacred food in purity for seven days between Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur; and there is a difficulty — behold there are nine; if so, on Yom Tov in any event one eats in purity — and it requires further study; on the contrary, from there it is proven that on Shabbat one need not be careful, for otherwise there remain only six when you remove Shabbat and two Yom Tovs of Rosh Hashana; and one may say that R' Chiya would sanctify according to sight as is in tractate Rosh Hashana, and if so there was only one day Yom Tov; and to me it appears proof for this from Taanis daf 25 — the wife of Rav Chanina every erev Shabbos would heat the oven so they would think she baked bread, and nevertheless it is proven from the words of the Rama that the reason is to eat kosher bread; and if so, one must eat the challahs on Shabbat and not other bread; and see siman 574; and also in order to fulfill the mitzvah of challah, because the challah of the world was lost, as is in Yerushalmi Shabbat chapter 2 (32):
