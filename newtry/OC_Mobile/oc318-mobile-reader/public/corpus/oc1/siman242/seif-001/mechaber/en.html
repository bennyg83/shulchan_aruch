To be careful regarding honor of Shabbat. And in
it one seif: Even one who needs others — if he has a little of his own — must exert himself to honor Shabbat, and they did not say "make your Shabbat ordinary and do not need people" except for one whose time is very pressed; therefore one must restrict himself on other days in order to honor Shabbat, from the enactment of Ezra that they launder clothes on Thursday for the honor of Shabbat.The custom is to knead enough for challah in the home to make from them loaves to recite hamotzi upon them on Shabbat and Yom Tov [he relied on the Mordechai at the beginning of tractate Rosh Hashana], and this is honor of Shabbat and Yom Tov and one should not change; some wrote that in some places they were accustomed to eat a dish that they call pashtida on Shabbat night in remembrance of the manna that was covered from above [Maharil, and I did not see reason to be concerned about this]:]:
