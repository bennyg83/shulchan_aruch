In Taz seif kaf alef — some say that "the time is very pressed" etc. — there is here missing language, and his intent is that he was uncertain whether "and do not need people" means that he should not need at all; but if he needs to receive even though he has a little of his own — such as that he earned a selah for the week and needed more — he should not receive except what suffices for his weekday sustenance and he should not burden the community to receive from them what suffices for a third meal, and all the more for a plate of harsana; and what they said that he is obligated in a plate of harsana when the time is very pressed — that is when he does not need others at all but only that he earns his sustenance each week with restriction — he must restrict from weekdays to enjoy with a plate of harsana; and according to this there are here only two divisions — that if he will need people because he wishes to enjoy Shabbat on this R' Akiva said "make your Shabbat ordinary," and when he does not need people — this one must enjoy Shabbat, the wealthy should increase and the average should decrease, and this is only two divisions — must and need not; and according to this he explained "the time is very pressed" — that is, he does not need people but only suffices from the work of his hands with restriction; or perhaps "the time is very pressed" is when he has a little of his own and needs others, and this too is in the category of enjoyment of Shabbat with a plate of harsana; and what they said "make your Shabbat ordinary" is that he has nothing at all of his own — if so, there are three divisions here — need not at all, need a little like the manner of the Tur, and the third division who has nothing of his own and is entirely dependent on charity — this is called "the time is very pressed for him." And he returned and resolved it from the words of Rashi etc. And what the Taz wrote "a short path" etc. "or there are only two here and that of a plate of harsana is included in make your Shabbat ordinary" etc. — his intent is that it is included in what they said there "and do not need people" to indicate that if he does not need people he is in the category of enjoyment of Shabbat, and in this they obligated him in a plate of harsana since he does not need people; but if he needs people, even a plate of harsana he need not make — and this is what he said that also a plate of harsana is included in "make your Shabbat ordinary":
