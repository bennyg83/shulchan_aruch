Magen Avraham seif kaf alef — from the sugya of Pesachim daf 68 — for R' Eliezer on Yom Tov says either "it is entirely for you" (meaning most of it, for in any event one must pray) or "it is entirely for Heaven" (meaning that permission is his not to eat at all and occupy himself with Torah all day); R' Yehoshua says — divide it, half for Heaven and half for you. And he holds that it is forbidden to fast on Yom Tov, and so we rule later siman 529. And Rava says — all agree on Shabbat that we require also "for you" on erev Shabbos. And Olas Shabbos questioned — since we rule like R' Yehuda, why did Rava say this matter, which is not relevant except for R' Eliezer; therefore he explained that Rava speaks even according to R' Yehuda, who agrees on Shabbat that we require "for you" specifically — meaning that he did not divide it but most of it must be "for you" and not half; and automatically he can do the whole day "for you" only that he prays as on Yom Tov according to R' Eliezer — such are the words of see there.
