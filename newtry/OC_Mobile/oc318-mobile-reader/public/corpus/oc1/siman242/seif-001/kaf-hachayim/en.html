(1) [Seif 1] Even one who needs others etc. — one must exert himself to honor Shabbat, for so it is in tractate Shabbat daf 118a. R' Yochanan said in the name of R' Yosi — whoever enjoys Shabbat is given an inheritance without boundaries. Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak said — also he is saved from subjugation to kingdoms. Rav Yehuda said that Rav said — whoever enjoys Shabbat is given the desires of his heart. Tur. And Mahari"a z"l wrote that "is given an inheritance without boundaries" is measure for measure — just as he spends money without limit for the need of Shabbat, so they give him inheritance without limit. And likewise what they said "saved from subjugation to kingdoms" — because he accepts upon himself the yoke of Shabbat, they remove from him the yoke of kingdoms. And likewise "given the desires of his heart" — in order that he be able to fulfill his desire from every enjoyment he wishes. Beit Yosef. And women too are obligated in enjoyment of Shabbat — Perishah siman 242 and Maharshal there. And enjoyment of Shabbat is eating very fatty meat and fish and perfumed drink and drinking wine — Bach. And see later section 10.
