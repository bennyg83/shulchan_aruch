1) [Seif 1] Even one who requires assistance from others, etc. He must exert himself to honor Shabbos. Shabbos 118a states: R' Yochanan said in the name of R' Yose that whoever delights in Shabbos is given a boundless inheritance. Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak said that he is also saved from subjugation by kingdoms. Rav Yehudah said in the name of Rav that whoever delights in Shabbos is granted the desires of his heart. Tur. R' Yitzchak Abarbanel, of blessed memory, writes that he is given a boundless inheritance measure for measure: just as he spends money without limit for Shabbos, so he is given an inheritance without limit. Regarding the statement that he is saved from subjugation by kingdoms, since he accepts the yoke of Shabbos upon himself, the yoke of kingdoms is removed from him. He is also granted the desires of his heart so that he can fulfill his desire for any delight he wishes. Beis Yosef. Women are likewise obligated in delighting in Shabbos. Perishah, siman 280; Mishbetzos Zahav there. Shabbos delight consists of eating especially fatty meat, fish, a fragrant beverage, and drinking wine. Bach. See below, paragraph 10.
