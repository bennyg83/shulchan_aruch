(ג) And behold, like proof that he completely returned from what he said initially, and even per the rabbis he eats one meal on motzei Shabbat not close to evening — for he challenges in Shabbat 118 initially from the end of the mishnah and afterward from the beginning "on Shabbat they give three meals" — one may say according to R' Chidka, since three of the day are explicit in the verses and chazal relied that three meals are alluded to in Scripture — it is a mere asmakhta; not so a fourth meal — there is not so much alluded in Scripture, only by reasoning he holds apart from evening; therefore "on Shabbat they give three meals" and a fourth he does not take — "make your Shabbat ordinary" etc.; not so now he challenges from the end — fourteen meals we see that only two meals he takes according to R' Akiva; and necessarily like the division of Tosafos — if taking from charity he provides all his needs — if so, there is a difficulty for R' Chidka; and what is the difficulty — that he eats four meals?
