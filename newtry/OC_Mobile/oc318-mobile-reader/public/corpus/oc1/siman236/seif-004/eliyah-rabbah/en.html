[4] [Levush] In past tense, etc. — and above end of siman 66 he wrote that emet ve'emunah they enacted for the future — one must say that it refers to the words emet ve'emunah; and here the main blessing refers [to the past]. Bach wrote above in siman 66 that our custom on Yom Tov when saying Maariv and concluding "King, Rock of Israel and its Redeemer" — it is as if he said "Redeemer of Israel," and one must say ve'go'alo without a vav, for it sounds well regarding redemption that passed — so it appears to me — until here. And in my humble opinion it is implied from the Yerushalmi that the Tur brought end of siman 66 that also the language of ve'go'alo is past tense; and see below what I wrote in name of Taz, and it does not imply so in Tur and Levush. Also what Magen Avraham resolves — that Rashi explained evening geulah refers also to the future — is not precise, for behold the Tur also wrote so above; rather as I explained, and so it is implied in Perishah.
