(13) After his own blessing — which is the conclusion of the blessing of Shema; and the Mechaber goes to his reason in what he ruled in siman 215 seif 1 — see there.

(14) And one does not answer, etc. — for it is not related to the blessing of Shema, and it is like any separate blessing where one does not answer amen after one's own blessing.

(15) "The King in His honor" — in saying "May He always reign over us" his intent should not be language of request that he prays that He reign over us — meaning to bring the redemption near, for there is no prayer in the conclusion of a blessing; rather his intent should be language of praise that the Holy One, blessed be He, reign forever (Magen Avraham).

(16) And see above siman 215 — meaning that there Rama wrote that in our lands they are not accustomed to answer after their own blessing, even after the blessing "Who guards His people Israel forever."
