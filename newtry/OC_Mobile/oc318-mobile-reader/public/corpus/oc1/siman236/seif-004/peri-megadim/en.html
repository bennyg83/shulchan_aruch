If he found — Taz: at Maariv, connecting geulah to prayer is pushed aside — prayer with the congregation is preferable; not so at Shacharit — connecting geulah is preferable; and see Levush here and above siman 67 seif 8; and in Magen Avraham note 3 it will be explained further with God's help in this. And what he wrote regarding Minchah prayer, etc. — Magen Avraham note 3: likewise to pray Minchah immediately while the congregation recites Shema and its blessings, and Maariv prayer with the congregation; and if he will have [time for] Maariv prayer at night, he waits in Minchah prayer and prays with the congregation — for nevertheless it is public prayer — see there — even though this one Maariv and this one Minchah. And likewise if he cannot pray Maariv with the congregation — when he finishes Minchah he waits in Minchah prayer. And regarding skipping Tachanun — he skips to begin after Minchah Maariv prayer and say Tachanun afterward, since it is still day — requires study in this; Magen Avraham with God's help in siman 297 from this.
