1) [Seif 1] In the evening, one recites two blessings, etc. The Yerushalmi states that R' Yose bar Bun said in the name of R' Yehoshua ben Levi that this is based upon, 'Seven times a day I praise You.' In the morning one recites two blessings before Krias Shema and one afterward, while in the evening one recites two before and two afterward, totaling seven. Beis Yosef. R' Nachman in the name of R' Mana says that whoever fulfills, 'Seven times a day I praise You,' is considered as though he fulfilled, 'You shall contemplate it day and night.' Tur; Taz, paragraph 1. Regarding why the additional blessing was assigned to Maariv, see our words above at the beginning of siman 58.
