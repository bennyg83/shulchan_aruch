(1) [Seif 1] In the evening one blesses two, etc. — Yerushalmi: R' Yosei bar Avin in name of R' Levi (Shulchan Aruch) "seven times a day I praise You" — meaning at Shacharit he blesses two before and one after, and at evening two before and two after — behold seven (Beit Yosef). Ran in name of R' Mana says: whoever fulfills "seven times a day I praise You" — it is as if he fulfilled "and you shall meditate on it day and night" (Tur; Taz seif katan 1). And the reason they gave the extra [blessing] at Maariv — see in our words above siman 58.
