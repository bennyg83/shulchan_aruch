And the conclusion of Bach on "Your righteous judgments" — in the three sections "Your righteous judgments" — tefillin, mezuzah, and tzitzit — see there; and it is difficult — this is according to R' Yishmael, not so per the Rabbis — one mitzvah; and so is the halachah — see chapter HaTekhelet in Menachot. And one can say further: the mitzvah of tzitzit is one; "and you shall love Heaven" is one; "and you shall speak of them" — Shema, two sections, one by day and one by night — two mitzvot; and Torah study independently, or "and I will give grass in your field" — Torah it is; and one should not extend. And what the Tur wrote, etc.: in Sepharad they say "Who separates between day and night — Blessed are You," etc.; and in Ashkenaz they add "Heaven of hosts is His name; living and enduring Heaven, may He always reign over us forever and ever." And behold "Heaven of hosts is His name" is a kind of conclusion, as Eliyah Rabbah wrote: "He makes dawn and darkness, Heaven of hosts is His name" (Amos 7:13) — "He makes dawn and darkness" — see there; and "living and enduring," etc., is a kind of conclusion — "may He reign" in the final redemption — "and it shall be light for us," etc.; and these are his words: "and He separates between day and night — for the wicked darkness and for Israel light."
