Blessing. They begin "and He is compassionate," etc., before Barekhu of Maariv — for at Maariv they would not offer a sacrifice to atone, and three groups of destroying angels are hinted at in this: destruction, anger, and wrath. And on Shabbat and Yom Tov they are not given permission to destroy — and see further reasons in responsa of Rivash siman 25.
