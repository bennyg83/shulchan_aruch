Two before it. Yerushalmi: R' Yosei bar Avin in name of R' Levi (Shulchan Aruch) "seven times a day I praise You" — Ran in name of R' Mana says: whoever fulfills "seven times a day I praise You" — it is as if he fulfilled "and you shall meditate on it day and night." He wrote: the Tur in the Ashkenazic text that they say "May He always reign over us forever and ever — Blessed are You, Heaven, Who causes evening" — and it was not adjacent to the conclusion, a kind of conclusion; the Rosh on this said that we pray "Heaven will reign forever" and the verse be fulfilled "and it shall come to pass at evening time there shall be light and it shall be light for us and darkness for our enemies" — and it is somewhat like a kind of conclusion.
