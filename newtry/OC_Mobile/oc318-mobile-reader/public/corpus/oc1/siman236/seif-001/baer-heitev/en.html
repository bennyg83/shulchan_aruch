Two before Shema. One must pause between "...in the expanse as He desired" and "Who creates day and night" (Amek Berachah). That which we conclude "King, Rock of Israel and its Redeemer" when saying Maariv is not correct (Bach end of siman 66 seif katan 12); and Magen Avraham wrote: at the end of chapter 1 of Berachot Rashi explained that specifically Shacharit refers to redemption that already passed, but evening geulah refers also to the future — if so, one should not deviate from the custom.
