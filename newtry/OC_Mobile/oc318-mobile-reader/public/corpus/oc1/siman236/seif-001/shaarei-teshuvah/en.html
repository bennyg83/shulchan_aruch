Two before Shema — Av Beit HaTefillah; and he wrote in Shulchan Tzvi that it is written in the writings of the Ari that one must be precise and say "Who causes evening in wisdom, He opens gates with understanding" — and many changed and say "Who causes evening"; end quote. And now I did not find this in the writings of the Ari; and also in Peleh Yoetz in name of the chevrim the order appears as we are accustomed — "in wisdom He opens gates." And indeed the reader will see that the author of Shulchan Tzvi had in his hand accurate writings of the Ari — requires study.
