He should pray with them — even though they pray individually (mebe'eyno yechido), nevertheless public prayer is preferable to connecting geulah to Maariv prayer; and it appears to me that if there is time he should recite Shema and its blessings and skip Baruch Heaven l'olam, etc., and also Yir'u eineinu in order to pray with the congregation — for it is only a custom, and many great authorities hold one should not say it, as the Tur wrote siman 52. And if he came to the synagogue when they begin Barekhu and he has not prayed Minchah, he should pray Minchah while they recite Shema and its blessings, and afterward pray Maariv with the congregation, and afterward recite Shema and its blessings at night; but he should not wait to pray Minchah at the time they pray Maariv and afterward pray Maariv at night — for this is not called "at the time the congregation prays," as is written siman 90 seif 9 regarding Shacharit and Musaf, and all the more so Minchah and Maariv — for they contradict one another; and siman Rashag 95, and furthermore prayer with the congregation is considered more than "at the time the congregation prays" — and siman 109.
