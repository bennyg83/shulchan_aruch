Pray with them. Even though they pray individually (mebe'eyno yechido), nevertheless public prayer is preferable to connecting geulah to the Maariv prayer which is optional. And it appears to me that if there is time, he should recite Shema and its blessings and skip Baruch Heaven l'olam amen and amen, etc., and also Yir'u eineinu, etc., in order to pray with the congregation — for it is only a custom, and many great authorities hold that one should not say it. And if he came to the synagogue when they begin Barekhu and he has not prayed Minchah, he should pray Minchah while they recite Shema and its blessings, and afterward pray Maariv with the congregation, and afterward recite Shema and its blessings at night; but he should not wait to pray Minchah at the time they pray Maariv and afterward pray Maariv at night — for they contradict one another, since the congregation takes that time for night, for behold they pray Maariv, and he still takes it for day since he prays Minchah (Magen Avraham, Taz, Shulchan Aruch).
