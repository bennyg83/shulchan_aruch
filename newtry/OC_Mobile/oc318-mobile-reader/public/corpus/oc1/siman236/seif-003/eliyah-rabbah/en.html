[3] [Levush] And it is somewhat like a conclusion, etc. — "Heaven of hosts is His name" — I found that it is also somewhat like a conclusion, as it is written "He makes darkness and dawn, Heaven of hosts is His name." He wrote (Amek Berachah) that one must pause between "...as He desired" and "Who creates day." He wrote in Perishah: "Living and enduring Heaven, may He always reign," etc.; and likewise in Yir'u eineinu: "The King in His honor — may He always reign," etc. — the word "may He reign" is the beginning of another matter — until here. But Magen Avraham in name of Amek Berachah wrote that one should read the word tamid below.
