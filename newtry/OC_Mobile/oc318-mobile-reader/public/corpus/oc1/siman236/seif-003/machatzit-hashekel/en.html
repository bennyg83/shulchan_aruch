(Seif katan 3) He should pray with them — even though they pray individually (mebe'eyno yechido) — meaning: aside from [the case] that if he prays with the congregation and will not connect geulah to prayer, in addition to this that even with the congregation they pray individually; and if he prays alone he prays at night — even though in siman 235 it is explained that it is preferable to pray in congregation even praying individually — nevertheless it teaches us: even though both apply — that they pray individually and also he will not connect geulah to prayer — nevertheless prayer with the congregation is preferable.

But he should not wait, etc. — for it is an error: since in any case one of two prayers — Minchah and Maariv — he must pray individually; if so, why does he pray Minchah individually and Maariv with the congregation and not connect geulah to prayer, and also pray Maariv individually? Let us say the opposite — that he should wait to pray Minchah until the congregation prays the 18 [blessings] of Maariv, and then pray Minchah — and it is not his prayer with the congregation, for behold they pray Maariv and he Minchah; nevertheless it is at the time the congregation prays, and Maariv he prays at night and also connects geulah to prayer.

At Shacharit and Musaf — meaning: if he prays Musaf at the time the congregation prays Shacharit, it is not called "at the time the congregation prays."

For they contradict one another — since the congregation praying Maariv makes it night, and he prays Minchah — therefore they considered it still day.

And Rashag: one should not pray Minchah in that same synagogue where the congregation already accepted Shabbat — for it is like they contradict one another (Shulchan Aruch).

And furthermore, prayer with the congregation, etc. — meaning: even so, in such a case it is called "at the time the congregation prays"; nevertheless it is preferable that he pray Minchah individually and Maariv with the congregation — for it is prayer with the congregation, which is preferable to praying Minchah at the time they pray Maariv, which is only like "at the time the congregation prays" as above.

And Azsai siman 109: in such a case regarding Kedushah — and these are his words: an individual standing in the 18[-blessing prayer], and when he reaches the place of Kedushah the congregation says Kedushah in their order — he does not say Kedushah with them, for Kedushot are not equal, etc.
