3) In the first blessing, the correct text is, 'Who by His word brings on evenings; with wisdom opens gates; with understanding.' One should say, 'Who brings on evenings with wisdom,' and pause. Afterward he should say, 'Who opens gates with understanding,' and pause. Afterward he should say, 'Who changes the times,' etc. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 51, side 4. We cited this above in siman 48; see there.
