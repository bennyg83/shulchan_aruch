(11) And they wish to stand in prayer — and if they are still engaged in the blessings of Shema and he estimates that until the congregation reaches Shema he can say Shema and its blessings until "Who guards His people Israel forever" — he should do so and skip Baruch Heaven l'olam, etc., and pray Shema with the congregation — for those verses are only a custom, and there are great ones who hold one should not say them; therefore it is better to skip in order to fulfill prayer in congregation with connecting geulah to prayer — so wrote several Acharonim [and behold in the words of Taz and Eliyah Rabbah it is explicit that Baruch Heaven l'olam, etc., and also Yir'u eineinu one says after the prayer — and so cited Chayei Adam, and so is somewhat implied in Magen Avraham seif katan 2; however in Ma'aseh Rav he wrote that after the prayer one does not say the verses of Baruch Heaven l'olam, etc. — and it is implied his reason is that they were enacted only to say in their place and not after one already prayed Maariv; and therefore it appears better not to conclude the blessing of Yir'u eineinu but say until "for we have no king but You" — and even though a blessing without a conclusion is generally not considered a blessing at all, nevertheless since even before the 18 [verses] it is only a general custom, do not add to it]. Further they wrote: in places where they precede praying Maariv individually, and he came to the synagogue when they begin Barekhu and he has not prayed Minchah — he should pray Minchah while they recite Shema and its blessings, and afterward pray Maariv with the congregation, and afterward recite Shema and its blessings at night; however if he will have [time for] Maariv prayer at night, he should wait in Minchah prayer to pray at the time the congregation prays Maariv, and Maariv prayer he prays afterward as its law with the congregation at night (Peri Megadim).
(12) He should pray with them — for public prayer is preferable to connecting geulah to Maariv prayer [and even if the congregation prays individually — nevertheless he should pray with them and recite Shema and its blessings when its time arrives; and see above siman 233 seif 1 when he can pray Shema of Maariv while it is still day]; but all this is specifically when he estimates he will not be able afterward to achieve praying with them — for if not, he should not nullify the mitzvah of connecting geulah to prayer.
