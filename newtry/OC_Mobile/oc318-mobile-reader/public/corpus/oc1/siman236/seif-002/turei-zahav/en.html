Those accustomed to say the 18 verses, etc. — in Tur it is written that this was enacted because in early days their synagogues were in the fields and they were afraid to remain there until the time of Maariv prayer; and they enacted to say these verses that have in them 18 mentions corresponding to the 18 blessings in Maariv prayer. And now that they returned to pray Maariv in synagogues, the first custom was not abolished, etc.; and there are among the great ones who were accustomed not to say them — end quote. And as Beit Yosef — meaning Ramban and Rashbam; and according to this it appears to me: one who came to the synagogue after the congregation began to pray should pray Minchah individually and recite Shema and its blessings until "Who guards His people Israel forever," and the congregation begins Maariv prayer — he can immediately pray with them; and the verses of Baruch Heaven l'olam amen, Yir'u eineinu — he says afterward; for since great ones were accustomed not to say them at all, he can rely on them in this and skip them in order to pray with the congregation and say them afterward as above — simple.
