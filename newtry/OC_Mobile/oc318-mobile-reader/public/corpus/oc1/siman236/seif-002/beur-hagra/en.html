Seif 2 — there is not, etc. — see Tosafot daf 4b s.v. R' Yehuda, etc., and these are his words, and see Taz; and the view of Rashbam and Ramban is primary, and so too in his name Tur Yeshanim at the beginning of Berachot (Shulchan Aruch).

And however, etc. — like "remove [the] blessing" — and all the more so this, which is a need of prayer itself; and all the more so in Maariv prayer which is optional — there siman 293.

And likewise, etc. — Tur in name of R' Akiva, and Beit Yosef from the reason stated above — like "remove [the] blessing," and even though for others [it would be forbidden].

Because they were enacted, etc. — Tosafot there and Tur Yeshanim wrote: at first, when Maariv was optional, they would say these verses in place of the 18[-blessing prayer] and conclude and say Kaddish and leave.
