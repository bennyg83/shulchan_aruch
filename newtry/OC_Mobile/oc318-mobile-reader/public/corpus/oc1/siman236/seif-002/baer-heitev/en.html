To stand. And in Maaseh Rav he wrote that one must sit, and so too Mateh Moshe in name of Maharil and Maharshal; and Taz wrote that if one says them standing, he intends that they be in place of the 18 [blessings] — if so, he has fulfilled the obligation of Maariv prayer — and how will he pray Maariv a second time? And he wrote that in any case one should not stand with his face to the wall as in the 18 [blessings].
