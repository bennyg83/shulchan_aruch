However, what he said — this is the view of Rashba in a responsum, and he wrote the reason: because Maariv prayer is optional (reshut). And Maharshal wrote on this in Hagahot Maharil, and these are his words: and it appears to me, one reason is that they established it as obligatory; and furthermore, even though it is optional, nevertheless wherever you pray you must be careful not to interrupt, as is implied there in Semag; therefore it appears that the shaliach tzibbur should precede himself so he can reach [the point of] Yaaleh VeYavo before the congregation and announce during his prayer — so I found written in the writings of the Gaon the Avudraham in name of Maharil in name of Maharshal; and therefore it is not correct to say the verses such as "and now, let the strength of Heaven be increased," because we may only interrupt for what Chazal established; and one can resolve [this difficulty] at the time they say Baruch Heaven l'olam amen (Maharshal and from Maharam in Sefer Megaleh Amukot, manner of Ran).
