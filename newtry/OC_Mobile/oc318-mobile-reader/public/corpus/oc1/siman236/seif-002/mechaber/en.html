One should not talk between the geulah of Maariv and the prayer; even those who are accustomed to say the 18 verses and Yir'u eineinu should not interrupt between Yir'u eineinu and the prayer. However, what the shaliach tzibbur announces for Rosh Chodesh between Kaddish and Maariv prayer is not an interruption, since it is a need of prayer; and likewise one may say Barekhu to discharge one who did not hear, and it is not an interruption.And see above siman 69. I have seen meticulous people who are accustomed to stand when saying the 18 verses of Baruch Heaven l'olam, etc., and it is a fine custom, for they were enacted in place of the 18[-blessing prayer], and therefore it is fit to stand in them as in prayer.
