They are accustomed to stand. And in Maaseh Rav he wrote that one must sit, and so too Mateh Moshe in name of Maharil and Maharshal; and he wrote that one should say "Save us, Heaven of our salvation," etc., so there not be more than 18 mentions; and Mateh Moshe in name of Maharam Nissim: when he prayed with the congregation and afterward recited Shema and its blessings — then he says Baruch Heaven, etc., standing.
