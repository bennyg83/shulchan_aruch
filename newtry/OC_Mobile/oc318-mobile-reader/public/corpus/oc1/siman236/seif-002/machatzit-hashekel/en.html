(Seif katan 2) They are accustomed, etc., and in Maaseh Rav he wrote that one must sit, etc. — the reason of those who stand: since in earlier days their synagogues were in the field and they were afraid to extend Maariv prayer at night since the synagogue was far from the settlement — therefore they enacted in place of the 18 [blessings] the 18 verses of Baruch Heaven l'olam, etc., and in them are 18 mentions of the Tetragrammaton corresponding to the 18 blessings; and along with this they enacted the conclusion of Yir'u eineinu. And even now that our synagogue is within the city and they pray 18 blessings literally, nevertheless the original enactment to say the 18 verses in place of the 18 blessings stands in its place and has not moved — and this is the reason of Rama who wrote that the meticulous stand, for at first they were enacted in place of the 18 blessings literally, and those who disagree — on the contrary, from the good custom it is fit to sit; for if he stands it appears he intends to fulfill with them in place of the 18 blessings — if so, when afterward he prays 18 blessings, it appears he prays 18 blessings twice; and one can say it is like one long prayer, for they interrupt with Kaddish in between — so too Taz; and he wrote in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah that even those who stand should not stand with their faces to the wall, for then it appears literally as praying 18 blessings.

"Save us, Heaven of our salvation" — to exclude our text "Save us, Heaven our Heaven, and gather us" — for if so there would be in it 19 mentions of the Tetragrammaton; and in my humble opinion this is not sufficient. One can say that one name of Havayah corresponds to the blessing against heretics; and even though in Tur and Rosh it is written 18 mentions corresponding to 18 blessings — one can say they were not precise in their language, since it is called 18 blessings — for such was the beginning of the enactment of Anshei Kenesset HaGedolah; but afterward they added in Yavneh the blessing against heretics — therefore they also count 18 mentions corresponding to them. Further one can say: that which "Heaven — He is the Heaven" twice is counted as one — and that they are counted as one is also written in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah according to his view. And nevertheless one can say as Magen Avraham wrote: "Save us, Heaven of our salvation."

For so is the language of the verse in Divrei HaYamim 1 siman 16 — the Tetragrammaton is not mentioned in it; and so too in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah. However, in Tehillim siman 106 it is written "Save us, Heaven our Heaven, and gather us," etc.

Then he said, etc., standing — since he did not connect geulah to prayer in order to pray with the congregation; therefore when he recited Shema with its blessings he said the verses standing, so it should be similar to connecting geulah to prayer — meaning the verses should be somewhat similar to the 18[-blessing prayer]; and even though he already prayed with the congregation, or that now the verses are in place of the 18 blessings and in the manner of a voluntary prayer — and even though one must innovate something new in it as above siman 107 — this is called an innovation in that he connects geulah to prayer.
