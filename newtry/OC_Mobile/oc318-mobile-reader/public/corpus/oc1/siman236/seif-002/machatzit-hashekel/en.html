(Seif katan 2) They are accustomed, etc., and in Maaseh Rav he wrote that one must sit, etc. — the reason of those who stand: since in earlier days their synagogues were in the field and they were afraid to extend Maariv prayer at night since the synagogue was far from the settlement — therefore they enacted in place of the 18 [blessings] the 18 verses of Baruch Heaven l'olam, etc., and in them are 18 mentions of the Tetragrammaton corresponding to the 18 blessings; and along with this they enacted the conclusion of Yir'u eineinu. And even now that our synagogue is within the city and they pray 18 blessings literally, nevertheless the original enactment to say the 18 verses in place of the 18 blessings stands in its place and has not moved — and this is the reason of Rama who wrote that the meticulous stand, for at first they were enacted in place of the 18 blessings literally, and those who disagree — on the contrary, from the good custom it is fit to sit; for if he stands it appears he intends to fulfill with them in place of the 18 blessings — if so, when afterward he prays 18 blessings, it appears he prays 18 blessings twice; and one can say it is like one long prayer, for they interrupt with Kaddish in between — so too Taz; and he wrote in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah that even those who stand should not stand with their faces to the wall, for then it appears literally as praying 18 blessings.
