But — in place of the Taz, Beit Yosef concluded and it appears that even if one says Yehi L'Ratzon after the supplications it is permitted, like in the blessings themselves one says something akin to the blessing at the end; and behold in Shulchan Aruch he retracted and ruled like the responsum of the Rashba, and I did not merit to understand this — for "end of every blessing" means before Amen, but after Amen it appears forbidden; and if so, if Yehi L'Ratzon is part of the conclusion it is forbidden. And see Levush 114 "Who returns the spirit" — it is like the conclusion of an extended blessing; therefore one can say on us, after the end of Shmoneh Esrei, if we say "end of every blessing" even after the end of the blessing; and the Bach was correct in 119, Taz letter dalet — and all this is not equal to me. And the Taz questioned that with a fixed practice it is forbidden to add; and aside from this it is difficult, for we require specifically something akin to each blessing, and supplications are not relevant to placing peace — and it requires examination.
