He interrupts as well. The Levush wrote that it is good to say Yehi L'Ratzon before the supplications and after them; so too the Bach.

He shortens and rises. That is, he interrupts in the middle of the supplications and steps backward after them; there is no obligation to say supplications every time (Rashba). And if there is no time to step backward, he says Yehi L'Ratzon immediately and answers (Darkei Moshe).
