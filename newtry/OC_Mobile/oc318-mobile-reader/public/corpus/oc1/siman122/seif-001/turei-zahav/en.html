But between Yehi L'Ratzon etc. — it implies in every case it is permitted, and afterwards he concludes "as one interrupts in the blessing of Shema"; and it must be that at the beginning it speaks of one who says supplications in sections, therefore they have no resemblance to the eighteen blessings at all, therefore he interrupts as he wishes. And at the end, one accustomed made it a fixed practice for him and it somewhat resembles the eighteen blessings, therefore he interrupts specifically as in the blessing of Shema.

But in a place where they practice saying supplications — so too Beit Yosef, and it appears even etc. And it is difficult: behold in the Gemara Yehi L'Ratzon is part of the prayer, as stated here — and how is there permission to say supplications there and interrupt? And even though in the eighteen blessings one can add something akin to each blessing, and in Shmoneh Esrei even all one needs — nevertheless this is specifically for the moment what is needed; but to make a fixed practice and add in every blessing every day we have no proof to permit this, for the Gemara only states if one comes to add in every blessing — and this is in a temporary way, not that he establish for himself another nature more than what the Sages established. And so what is written that a man asks his needs in Shmoneh Esrei — meaning what he will need for the moment; but not to make for him a fixed additional formula adding to the enactment of the Sages. And so it is difficult in what is written afterwards that they practice interrupting in Elokai Netzor etc. — why indeed interrupt in a fixed practice before Yehi L'Ratzon, which is part of the eighteen? And so too nearby: it is not proper to say supplications before Yehi L'Ratzon etc. Therefore it seems good and correct to say twice Yehi L'Ratzon — one before Elokai Netzor and one after it — since he wrote afterwards that permission is in his hands to do so; one should practice so ab initio every day when they make a fixed practice from Elokai Netzor, and so with the other supplications he adds, as seems to me.
