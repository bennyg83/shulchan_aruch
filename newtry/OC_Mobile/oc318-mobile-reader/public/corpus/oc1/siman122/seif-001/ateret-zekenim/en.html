But in a place where they practice saying supplications [before Yehi L'Ratzon] — this is not correct, since in the Gemara they said that the Sages instituted Yehi L'Ratzon after the Shemoneh Esrei prayer; therefore it is correct not to interrupt even for Kaddish and Kedusha before Yehi L'Ratzon. Therefore it is correct to say Yehi L'Ratzon immediately after the Shemoneh Esrei prayer, before Elokai Netzor, so that one may answer Kaddish and Kedusha as stated above. So too I saw in the siddur of R. Shabtai of Frankfurt; so it is.
