Laws pertaining between the Shemoneh Esrei and Yehi L'Ratzon. Containing 3 seifim: If one
comes to interrupt to answer Kaddish and Kedusha between the Shemoneh Esrei and Yehi L'Ratzon, one does not interrupt, for Yehi L'Ratzon is part of the prayer. But between Yehi L'Ratzon and the other supplications, it is fine.And specifically in a place where they practice saying Yehi L'Ratzon immediately after the prayer. But in a place where they practice saying supplications before Yehi L'Ratzon, one interrupts also for Kaddish and Kedusha; and in these places they practice interrupting in Elokai Netzor before Yehi L'Ratzon, and therefore they interrupt also for Kedusha and Kaddish and Barchu [his own reasoning, per the Rashba that Beit Yosef brought].} However, one accustomed to say supplications after his prayer — if the prayer leader began to order his prayer and reached Kaddish or Kedusha, he shortens and rises; and if he did' not shorten, he may interrupt as one interrupts in a blessing of Shema, even in the middle.]: מיהו הרגיל לומר תחנונים אחר תפלתו אם התחיל ש"צ לסדר תפלתו והגיע לקדיש או לקדושה מקצר ועולה ואם לא קצר יכול להפסיק כדרך שמפסיק בברכה של ק"ש אפי' באמצע:
