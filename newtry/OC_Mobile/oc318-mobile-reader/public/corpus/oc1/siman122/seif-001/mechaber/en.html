Laws that apply between the Amidah and Yihyu LeRatzon. It contains 3 seifim: If one wishes to interrupt to answer Kaddish or Kedushah between the Amidah and Yihyu LeRatzon, he does not interrupt, because Yihyu LeRatzon is part of the prayer. But between Yihyu LeRatzon and the other supplications it is permitted. {Rama: This applies specifically in a place where the custom is to say Yihyu LeRatzon immediately after the prayer. But where the custom is to say supplications before Yihyu LeRatzon, one also interrupts for Kaddish and Kedushah. In these lands the custom is to interrupt during Elohai Netzor before Yihyu LeRatzon, and therefore one also interrupts there for Kedushah and Kaddish.} One who is accustomed to say supplications after his prayer may shorten them when necessary. If he has completed his prayer but has not yet stepped back, the laws governing interruption are as stated above.
