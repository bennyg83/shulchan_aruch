But — the Taz, from the extra [word] in the language that should have said "but after Yehi L'Ratzon" — if he can shorten he shortens, and if not he interrupts like in Shema in the middle of Shmoneh Esrei, for there is a difference: one not accustomed to say supplications has permission to interrupt even for other Amens; and one accustomed made it a fixed practice and interrupts in the middle of the blessings of Shema as in siman 66 s"3. And supplications after the prayer — like in the middle of the blessings of Shema — for HaKel HaKadosh and Shmoneh Esrei he interrupts, and for other Amens not. One who examines Beit Yosef will see he brought two responsa of the Rashba; he intended what the Taz said, that there is a distinction between accustomed or not.
