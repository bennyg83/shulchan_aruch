(3) There are those who practice saying every day "Elokai, before I was created I am unworthy" etc.; and Maharam of Mintz wrote siman 87 letter hei that one should say it even on Shabbos; see there. And Knesset HaGedolah brought it in Hagahos Tur. Olas Tamid letter aleph, Eliyah Rabbah letter aleph; however now they do not practice saying it except on Yom Kippur alone; and see Prisha letter hei that he wrote that on Yom Kippur one must say Elokai Netzor after "Elokai, before I was created"; see there.
