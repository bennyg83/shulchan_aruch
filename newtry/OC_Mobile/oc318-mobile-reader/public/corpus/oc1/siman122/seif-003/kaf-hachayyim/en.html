3) There, in the gloss: The custom is to interrupt with 'Elokai Netzor,' etc. And the custom is to say it on Shabbos and Yom Tov, even though from its language it appears to be a language of prayer and supplications. And it is written in the rulings of Recanati, of blessed memory, siman 29: Even though we hold that it is forbidden for a person to request his needs on Shabbos, that applies to his making a prayer and supplications on his own and praying; but a fixed formula of prayer is permitted. Therefore one says on Shabbos, after the tefillah, 'Elokai Netzor,' etc. Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos HaTur, ot 1; Pri Chadash, ot 1; Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 195d. And see the Zechor L'Avraham, volume 3, maareches shin, ot 145 and ot 146, what he wrote on this matter.
3) Some have the custom to say every day, 'My God, before I was formed I was unworthy,' etc. And the Maharam Mintz, siman 87, ot 5, wrote that it may be said even on Shabbos; see there. And the Knesses HaGedolah cited him in the Hagahos HaTur; Olas Tamid, ot 1; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1. However, nowadays the custom is to say it only on Yom Kippur alone. And see the Perishah, ot 5, who wrote that on Yom Kippur one must say 'Elokai Netzor' after 'My God, before I was formed'; see there.
