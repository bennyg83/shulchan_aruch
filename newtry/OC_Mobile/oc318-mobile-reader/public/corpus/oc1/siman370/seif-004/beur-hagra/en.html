Seif 4 — courtyard residents. There 72:2 — Mishnah: the brothers, etc., and Gemara: we learn from sleeping place, etc. It implies that if they eat at their father's table literally, all need no eruv — and here he divides three matters that the Mishnah distinguishes when, etc., but, etc., or there are not, etc.

And similarly if, etc. For the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch copied the Mishnah's wording "at one table," etc. And the Rav wrote: he disagrees — even each at another table, since they are in one house, as stated regarding five groups, etc.

And even though, etc. There 102:1. And see there Rashi, s.v. ve'ilon — we learn it is not permanent, for a permanent tent restricts. And there 26:1 — clearly, etc.
