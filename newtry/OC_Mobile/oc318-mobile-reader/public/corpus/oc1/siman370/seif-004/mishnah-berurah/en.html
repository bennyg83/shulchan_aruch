(36) They eat at one table — even though each eats from his own [Beit Yosef].
(37) He has his own house — for sleeping and other uses, as below.
(38) One eruv for all — and whichever of them wishes may give the bread and need not acquire, for he acts as agent for all.
(39) And likewise, etc. — each at his table. The Rama added that content that not specifically at one table — the same applies at separate tables since they are in one house.
(40) It is like five who Shabbos in a hall — and perforce even if there was a partition there but it does not reach the ceiling or is close to it within three — it is as if it is not [so it is in Beit Yosef].
(41) Permanently — for they spread it only when doing something modest.
