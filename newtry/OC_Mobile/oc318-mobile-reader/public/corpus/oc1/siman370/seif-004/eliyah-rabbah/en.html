(4) The two innermost, etc. Meaning: together they give an eruv at twilight when their partitions reach the ceiling or do not reach. My teacher, may his memory be blessed, exempts the inner one if partitions do not reach the ceiling — and he was not precise (Taz). And in my humble opinion the Bach's intention: if they reach the ceiling each makes an eruv independently; if they do not reach, they make an eruv together — and requires further study. And so it appears in Eruvin daf 75 and the poskim. Also what appears from the Taz — that if there are no courtyard residents they are completely exempt from eruv — is not so, for it is explained in the Beit Yosef that specifically according to Shmuel who makes an eruv only for the innermost is the law thus. But according to R' Yochanan that both inner ones make an eruv — even when there are no courtyard residents they make an eruv. And it also appears that each one independently makes an eruv, as we say.
