A guest in a courtyard — even if he lodges in a house by himself — if he did not lodge permanently but for thirty days or less, he does not restrict the courtyard residents, and he and they are permitted both in his house and in their houses.And even if the guests are many and there is one homeowner — and specifically when there is one permanent homeowner, for then the guests are nullified relative to him. But guests together restrict one another immediately (Terumat HaDeshen, siman 77; Gemara end of daf 70). And see below siman 391. (And a non-Jew guest — see above siman 384.)]:
