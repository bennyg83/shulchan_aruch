A guest in a courtyard — even if staying in their own house — if not staying permanently but for thirty days or fewer, does not restrict the courtyard residents, and both he and they are permitted in his house and in theirs. {Rama: Rema: Even if the guests are many and the host is one — but this specifically requires one established host, for then the guests are nullified relative to him. But guests together restrict one another immediately. See below at the end of Siman 391. And regarding a non-Jewish guest, see above Siman 384.}
