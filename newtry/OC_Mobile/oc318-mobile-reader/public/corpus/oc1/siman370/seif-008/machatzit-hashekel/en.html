(Seif kaf-chet) Tutor, etc. And if the place, etc., and each uses, etc. — his intention is that both reasons are required for them to restrict: one, that the place be owned by them — for otherwise one would permit based on the second reason of Tosafos that he does not lend, etc., to restrict him, etc. And we also require that each use independently — for otherwise they are permitted based on the first reason of Tosafos since they all use, etc. — for each alone of Tosafos' reasons suffices to permit. But one cannot say thus, that he disagrees with the Beit Yosef. And Maharik's responsum without proof — and he did not bring their words at all. For the Beit Yosef wrote one should not rely on reason one of Tosafos alone — meaning their baking and cooking place in one place — on this reason alone one should not rely except combined with reason two that he did not lend, etc., to restrict, etc. — the Taz also brought reason two. Perforce Magen Avraham holds that according to the Beit Yosef, perforce also on reason two alone — meaning he did not lend, etc., to restrict, etc. — we do not rely except combined with reason one — meaning their baking place and cooking in one place — for otherwise to what purpose did Tosafos mention this reason of their baking place, etc., since this reason alone does not help except combined with the reason he did not lend, etc., to restrict, etc. And with the reason he did not lend, etc., to restrict, etc. alone it does not suffice except combined with reason one of their baking, etc., in one place. And both are required to be lenient — that their baking place, etc., be in one place and that he did not lend, etc., to restrict, etc. And what Magen Avraham wrote "and each uses, etc." — this vav of "and each uses" is a dividing vav, meaning: either they restrict or the rooms are owned by them — then even if they use according to Magen Avraham they restrict; or when each uses independently, even though the rooms are not owned by them — nevertheless they restrict.
