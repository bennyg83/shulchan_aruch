(8) (Levush) And further that he does not lend, etc. And the main view is like this reason, and one should not rely and permit because of the first reason where the second reason is not applicable (Beit Yosef and later authorities). And prima facie it appears that even from this reason one should not permit. And so it slightly appears in the responsum of the Rama, siman 120. It also appears to me there is proof from seif 2 where he says that even in lending we require a hold on the house, and we do not say he does not lend, etc., and they can evict him — until here. And if so, even from this reason alone there is reason to permit since they can evict him. And above seif 2 must be explained as dealing with when it is impossible to evict them. And see siman 382, seif 18.
