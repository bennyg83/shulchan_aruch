And what is the reason: in our houses, if the eruv was damaged it is forbidden to carry from room to room if the homeowner has no hold, and also from room to house is forbidden if two dwell there — see siman 365, Magen Avraham. And see in the words of Shmuel, siman 482, who ruled both ways regarding two houses of two homeowners attached to each other with one wall separating them from the entire courtyard or other interruption — whether it is permitted to carry from one to the other without eruv, there.
