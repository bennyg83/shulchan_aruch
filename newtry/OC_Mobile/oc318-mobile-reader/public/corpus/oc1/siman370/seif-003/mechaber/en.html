Five groups who spent Shabbat in a large hall and divided it with partitions — if they all pass through one another's sections since there is only one opening to the courtyard, through the outermost section, and all pass through it — only the two innermost need contribute to the eruv, and all the others are considered a gatehouse for them. If each one has an opening to the courtyard — if the partitions reach the ceiling or come within three handbreadths of it, making each one a separate room even if they are made of curtains: if the courtyard residents place the eruv in one of the other courtyard houses, each and every group in the hall must contribute an eruv. But if they placed the eruv in this hall, none of them need contribute an eruv since they all dwell in this house, and the house in which the eruv is placed need not contribute. Similarly, if there are no other residents in the courtyard, they need no eruv. If they divided it with partitions that do not reach the ceiling — even if the courtyard residents place their eruv in one of the other courtyard houses — one eruv suffices for all five groups. If there are residents in actual upper stories, even if the courtyard residents placed the eruv in the hall, each group must contribute an eruv. But one who has a tutor or scribe in his house, and likewise students learning before their teacher and living in his house each in their own room — even if each has an opening to the courtyard and eats and sleeps in their room — they do not restrict.Rema: or they do not pass through one another but each has an opening to a gatehouse in front of them and the gatehouse opens to the courtyard — Rema: If some made partitions and some did not — those who made them are considered separate, and those who did not are considered joint.) אם המחיצות מגיעות לתקרה בתוך ג"ט דהוי כל א' חדר בפני עצמו אפילו הן של יריעות אם בני החצר נותנים העירוב בא' משאר בתי החצר צריך כל אחת ואחת מחבורות שבטרקלין ליתן עירוב אבל אם נתנו העירוב בזה הטרקלין אין צריכין ליתן עירוב כלל שכולם דרים בבית זה ובית שמניחים בו העירוב אין צריך ליתן עירוב וכן אם אין דיורין אחרים בחצר אינם צריכים עירוב חלקוהו במחיצות שאין נוגעות לתקר' (אפי') אם בני החצר נותנים עירובן בא' משאר בתי החצר די בעירוב א' לכל החמש חבורות: הגה אם מקצתן עשו מחיצות ומקצתן לא עשו אותן שעשו הם מחולקים ואותם שלא עשו הם כמשותפין.
