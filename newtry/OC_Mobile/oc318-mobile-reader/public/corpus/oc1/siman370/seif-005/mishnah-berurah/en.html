(42) And sleeps in another place — meaning in another courtyard.

(43) They do not restrict — meaning not at the sleeping place when they did not eruv with the rest of the courtyard residents, and not at the eating place since they are drawn after their father and go out in his eruv.

(44) Allowance — meaning they receive support from their father's house; and there is no difference whether they receive bread or money [later authorities].

(45) They restrict — others; and the same one another when they did not eruv — even if they dwell in one courtyard with their father, since they do not eat at his table literally.

(46) And these matters when they give, etc. — meaning: when do we say that receiving allowance does not help — when besides the brothers and their father there were other residents in the courtyard and they needed eruv and placed it with one of the residents — then we say just as the other residents restrict one another if they did not eruv, they too restrict one another; but when there are no other residents with them in the courtyard they themselves do not require eruv [since they in any case receive allowance from their father — the father joins them and combines them to be as one person]; and likewise when the courtyard residents placed their eruv with one of the brothers we say since he is exempt from giving a share in the eruv because of a house in which eruv is placed — his fellow brothers are also exempt for the reason stated.

(47) In one of these houses — meaning either with the father or with the brothers.
