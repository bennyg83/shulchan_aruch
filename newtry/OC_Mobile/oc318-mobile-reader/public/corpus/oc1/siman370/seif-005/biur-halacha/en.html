And sleeps in another place, etc. — and shepherds sleeping in the field — even though they eat in the city they do not restrict, for we are witnesses that if they could bring food to eat at their sleeping place it would be preferable for them, for there is their guarding place — so wrote Magen Avraham, and so it is explained explicitly in Avodat HaKodesh, and so it is proven in Ritva — see there; and Peri Megadim wrote that the same applies to students who eat in the homeowner's house and sleep in their rabbi's house — we follow sleeping for this reason.

And these matters when they give, etc. they need not eruv — in the Shulchan Aruch it is not explained whether it deals specifically when the brothers and their father dwelled in one courtyard — then they are attached to him — or even when the brothers dwell in another courtyard the law is likewise; and in Ritva he was uncertain on this and wrote that from Rashi's wording it appears specifically when they dwell in one courtyard — see there; however in Rabbenu Yerucham it appears even in another courtyard, and it requires further study.
