One who eats in one place and sleeps in another — his eating place is primary and that is where he creates a restriction. Therefore brothers who eat in their father's house and sleep in their own houses do not restrict. But if he gives them portions and they eat in their own houses, they do restrict. This applies when the other courtyard residents place their eruv elsewhere, but if the other courtyard residents place their eruv in one of these houses, or if there are no other residents with them in the courtyard, they need not make an eruv.
