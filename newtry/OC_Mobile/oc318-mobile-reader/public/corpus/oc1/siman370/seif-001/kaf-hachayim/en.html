(a) [Seif 1] One who dwells in a gatehouse — a gatehouse is when the houses are one inside the other and the inner one has no way to exit to the courtyard or public domain except through the outer house. Chayei Adam, general rule, letter alef. And even though it has four partitions — since people pass through there it is not a dwelling. Magen Avraham, seif kaf-alef. Turei Zahav, letter alef. And even though it is also roofed. Avnei Nezer, letter alef. Chayei Adam, letter alef, part 1.
