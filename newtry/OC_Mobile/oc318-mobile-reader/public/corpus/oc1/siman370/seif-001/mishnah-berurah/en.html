(1) One who dwells in a gatehouse, etc. — there is no distinction between a public gatehouse or an individual's, and even though the gatehouse has four partitions and is also roofed — nevertheless since people pass through there it is not considered a dwelling. And see below seif 7.
(2) And balcony — its meaning is explained below in siman 375.
(3) He does not restrict, etc. — if he did not give a share in the eruv.
(4) That are not considered a dwelling — and the same applies to a sukkah on the festival — it too is not considered a dwelling to restrict. A house that does not have four by four handbreadths does not restrict, for it is not considered a dwelling.
(5) But one who dwells, etc. — for they are considered a dwelling even though they are made of reeds and the like; and it is explained below in seif 5 that if he eats there — even though he does not sleep and lie there — it is called a dwelling, for the place of bread causes.
(6) Restricts — until he gives his share in the eruv.
