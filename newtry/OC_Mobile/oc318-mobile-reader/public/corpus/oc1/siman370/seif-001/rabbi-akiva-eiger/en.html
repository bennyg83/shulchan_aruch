Magen Avraham, seif kaf-vav: he rented it originally. For from the beginning of the rental they agreed to dwell together in the house — even though the other is rented from the non-Jew and not the other — nevertheless the renter from the non-Jew cannot evict his fellow from the house since from the beginning they agreed to dwell together. It turns out this renter from the non-Jew acts as agent for his fellow. Maharik.
