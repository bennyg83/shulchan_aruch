1) [Se'if 1] One who resides in a gatehouse. A gatehouse is a situation in which one house is situated inside another and the resident of the inner house has no way to enter the courtyard or public domain except through the outer house. Chayei Adam, rule 73, subsection 1. Although it has four partitions, since people pass through it, it is not considered a residence. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 1. This remains true even if it is roofed. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1; Chayei Adam, there.
