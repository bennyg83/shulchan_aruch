(Magen Avraham, seif kaf-bet.) And the Magid, in passing, was not precise. Note: He challenged him not according to law — that he understood from his words that when they are not divided no eruv is needed at all — and it is not so. For he holds that when they are not divided, the inner ones are not obligated more than the other rooms in the hall — only they all give one eruv, there.
