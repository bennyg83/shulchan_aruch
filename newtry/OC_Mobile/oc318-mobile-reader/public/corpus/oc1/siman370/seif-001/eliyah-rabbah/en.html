[1] That are not significant, etc. The same applies to a sukkah during Sukkot, as stated in Yoma 10. Regarding a gatehouse attached to a house, the Bach rules that it creates a prohibition. See seif 3.
[2] Because of their weight, since they are, etc. This requires study, because siman 308, seif 2, permits moving even a load requiring ten people. In the Shulchan Aruch, which says 'because of their weight or because of a prohibition, since they are, etc.,' it appears that 'since they are' refers only to the prohibited category, not to weight. The Levush's wording therefore requires study. The Magen Avraham asks what objective limit can be given for weight, since what is difficult for one person may be easy for another. Mateh Moshe says the rule applies only to muktzeh items and one should be stringent. I say that in a comparable context Eruvin 77 and 78 states that great weight fixes an object in place. So too it appears from siman 372, seif 8. As I wrote there in seif katan 8, the standard is whether such objects are ordinarily not moved because of their weight. It also appears that Mateh Moshe may agree concerning truly heavy items and intended only to exclude an object that remains movable when needed for its place. Examine carefully.
