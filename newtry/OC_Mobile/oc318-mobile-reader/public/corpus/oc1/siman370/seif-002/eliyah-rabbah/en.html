(2) Due to weight because they, etc. It requires study — for in siman 308, seif 2, even a load of ten people may be carried. And in the Shulchan Aruch where he wrote "due to weight or due to prohibition because they, etc." — it appears to me that "because they, etc." refers to prohibition and not to weight. But regarding the Levush it requires study. Magen Avraham wrote: it requires study what limit there is to this weight. In Mateh Moshe he wrote: specifically muktzeh items, and one should be stringent — until here. And I say: in such a case we say in the Gemara Eruvin daf 77 side 8: its weight determines it. And so it appears in siman 372, seif 8. And see what I wrote in seif 8 there — that what makes them not routinely carried due to weight is the limit. It also appears to me one may say that Mateh Moshe also agrees regarding weight, and he only meant to exclude something permitted to carry for the sake of its place — and requires further study.
