(Seif kaf-zayin) Or there are not — and what is the reason here: when each has an opening to the gatehouse, even though from gatehouse to courtyard they all have only one opening — nevertheless it is as if each has an opening to the courtyard and each is considered like the homeowner alone and must make an eruv.

What is the reason also: if the eruv was damaged, etc. — forbidden to carry also from room to room (and this is when a different homeowner dwells in each room, as stated in this seif) — just as they are forbidden to carry from house to house. And even though those two rooms have only one opening to the alleyway — nevertheless, since each room has its own opening to the house before the rooms, but from house to alleyway the two rooms have only one opening — even so they are considered as two and forbidden to carry without eruv.

If two dwell there — this applies also to the beginning where he wrote forbidden to carry from room to room.

See end of siman 365 that also if the lechi or beam of the alleyway was damaged, they are forbidden to carry from room to room or to the house when two homeowners dwell in them, for the eruv placed in the synagogue is nullified — there.
