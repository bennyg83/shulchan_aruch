(51) They need give in the eruv — for they alone have the status of a house upon them and they restrict carrying in the courtyard, and likewise carrying from one to another, and likewise from them to the houses before them; and even though the second is a gatehouse for the inner one through which one passes — it is not nullified from the status of a house thereby, since it is only an individual's gatehouse.
(52) That they are all considered, etc. — for each of them is a gatehouse for all the inner ones from him and is not considered a dwelling since many pass through it; and accordingly in a house where there are two rooms inside rented to two people, and in the house before the rooms the homeowner himself dwells and they pass through it — they themselves are obligated to give in the eruv and the homeowner himself is exempt since he is as a gatehouse for them.
