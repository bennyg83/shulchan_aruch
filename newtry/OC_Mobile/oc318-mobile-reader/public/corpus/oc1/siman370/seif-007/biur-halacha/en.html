The two inner ones alone, etc. — see in Taz who wrote that one eruv suffices for the two inner ones since they pass only through one opening, and already later authorities challenged him, as we copied above in Sha'ar HaTziyun letter 11; and know that from the words of Rabbenu Yerucham it appears he holds regarding ten houses that all are obligated to give in the eruv except the outermost of the outers, and several later authorities were astonished at him that he is not like anyone; and one who examines Peri Chadash in the sugya will see he explains thus in the words of R' Yochanan that except the last one who is foot-traffic for all — it is not called a gatehouse of many; and even though for practice one certainly should not deviate from the words of all poskim who did not write thus — I wrote this so as not to make Rabbenu Yerucham, heaven forbid, appear as one who errs.
