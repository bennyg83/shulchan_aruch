(g) There. But one who dwells in a straw house, etc. — and it is made of tree branches and reeds, meaning it is not a permanent house, and nevertheless it is called a house for all matters — Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbenu Yehonatan. And this is if he regularly eats bread there; but if he does not regularly eat bread there, dwelling there does not restrict, as below seif 5. And similarly if the homeowner has a hold in that place, as below seif 2, dwelling there does not restrict, as explained in the Beit Yosef.
