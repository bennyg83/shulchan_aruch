7) There. But one who resides in a straw shed, etc. It is made from tree branches and reeds, meaning that it is not a permanent house, yet it is nevertheless called a house for every purpose. Beis Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yehonasan. This applies if he regularly establishes his meal of bread there. If he does not regularly establish his meal of bread there, one who resides there does not prohibit carrying, as stated below in se'if 5. Similarly, if the householder retains rights of use in that place, one who resides there does not prohibit carrying, as stated below in se'if 2 and explained in Beis Yosef.
