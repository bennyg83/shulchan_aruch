(24) In a groom's home, etc. This means when a minyan prays in the home, and certainly when people occasionally pray with a minyan in a house. If the minyan is established for several days and has a Torah scroll, as at fairs, it resembles a permanent synagogue and the blessing is recited.
(25) For the reason does not apply. Magen Avraham cites Radbaz that one should not protest a place whose custom is to recite it. Pri Megadim questions this, since it may be a blessing in vain.
