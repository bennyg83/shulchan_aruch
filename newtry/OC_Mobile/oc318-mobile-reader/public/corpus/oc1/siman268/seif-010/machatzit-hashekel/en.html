Paragraph 10: And standing, etc.; this requires examination, for Choshen Mishpat 28 says, etc. Se'if 8 there removes everyone and retains the senior witness, asking how he knows the debt; then the second witness is admitted and examined. Thus each testifies alone.
It must be as Sema writes, etc.; after he said, etc. 'After' belongs to what follows. Sema asks why 'everyone is removed' includes the litigants when testimony must be before them. He answers that both witnesses first testify together before the litigants; then all, including litigants, leave during examination, when each witness testifies alone. Magen Avraham's 'as Sema writes' proves testimony occurs twice: once together and once separately. For Vayechulu the order is reversed, as appropriate to prayer. Thus 'after each said' is Magen Avraham's own continuation about Vayechulu, not Sema's wording. Sema is cited only to establish two testimonies. Tosefes Shabbos's emendation is unnecessary.
Nevertheless, according to the conclusion, etc. Sema concludes 'everyone is removed' means everyone except the litigants, who remain while testimony is given. Thus there is no proof of two testimonies, and the original difficulty returns.
Like the first tanna; see Sanhedrin 30. The first tanna requires both witnesses to testify together; Rabbi Nosson allows hearing one today and the other tomorrow. Although monetary law follows Rabbi Nosson, testimony concerning the Omnipresent's honor is performed stringently even according to the first tanna, with both testifying together.
Mateh Moshe wrote, etc., that Kiddush is standing. See Taz and below, siman 271:10, Rema's gloss.
