(2) He completes, etc. Strictly speaking, one should pray eighteen blessings on Shabbos as on weekdays and mention the day's sanctity in Retzeh, as on Rosh Chodesh and Chol HaMoed. Only out of honor for Shabbos did the Sages spare the burden and institute one middle blessing. Therefore, after the fact, once he began a weekday blessing, he completes it because it is fundamentally appropriate, and then begins the special Shabbos blessing.
(3) Of Shabbos. All these laws likewise apply to Yom Tov.
(4) And some say that in Mussaf, etc. They hold that the eighteen-blessing prayer is entirely unrelated to Mussaf, for even on weekdays Mussaf has only seven blessings; he therefore stops even mid-blessing. The first view reasons that after the fact, if one recited eighteen blessings in Mussaf and merely added, 'May we perform before You our obligations; the daily offering and the Mussaf offering,' he fulfilled his obligation, as in se'if 4. Thus, after the fact, weekday blessings also relate to Mussaf.
(5) He stops even, etc. The later authorities rule so because of the possible blessing in vain.
