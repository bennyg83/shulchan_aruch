Paragraph 2: In the middle, etc.; in Atah Chonen, unlike Shiltei HaGibborim. Shiltei HaGibborim on Mordechai says one who began a weekday blessing completes it, except in Musaf: there he completes Atah Chonen but stops in other blessings. He gives no reason. Rosh of Lunel limits completion to Atah Chonen, the first blessing; Rosh disagrees and treats all equally, as Shulchan Aruch rules. Bach explains Rosh of Lunel: Atah Chonen properly belongs even on Shabbos, for the Yerushalmi says it was placed first because without understanding there is no prayer; it is therefore a prayer need, not forbidden personal request. Other blessings are ordinary requests. Rosh disagrees on reasoning, not necessarily another Talmudic text, since the Gemara says only that he is obligated. Shiltei HaGibborim agrees that in ordinary prayers all eighteen are included in this obligation, but Musaf fundamentally does not take eighteen. He nevertheless requires completing Atah Chonen because it belongs to every prayer. Magen Avraham appears to follow Knesses HaGedolah that under the alternative view one stops even in Atah Chonen during Musaf.
