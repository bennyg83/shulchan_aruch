13) One who said Atah Chonen but did not complete the blessing, and instead immediately recited the middle blessing of Shabbos, and upon completing the Shabbos blessing remembered that the law requires one who said Atah Chonen to complete that blessing, should not return and recite the Atah Chonen blessing before Retzei. This is because he already recited the Shabbos blessing instituted in place of the weekday middle blessings. Machazik Berachah, paragraph 3.
