And through the end, he fulfilled his obligation. This is because Maariv is optional. It is preferable to recite this blessing together with the prayer leader. If the prayer leader has already recited it, the individual must pray the seven blessings of the Shabbos Amidah, because an individual is forbidden to recite this abridged blessing. If he nevertheless recited it individually, he fulfilled his obligation and need not pray the seven blessings. Lechem Chamudos; Magen Avraham.
