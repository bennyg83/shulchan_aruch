(27) And he heard from the shaliach tzibbur, etc. We are lenient because Maariv is optional.
(28) He fulfilled his obligation. Initially, he should carefully recite word for word with the shaliach tzibbur from the blessing's beginning through 'Blessed are You, Hashem, Who sanctifies Shabbos.' If the shaliach tzibbur already recited it, he must repeat the Amidah and should not recite the one-blessing-comprising-seven privately. It is also proper that if he remembers before hearing this blessing, he should not rely upon it but should pray the seven-blessing Amidah again in order, because some dispute this entire ruling; see Tur.
