Seif 4. One who prayed a weekday prayer on Shabbat and did not mention Shabbat. NB: I saw in the siddur of the gaon of Lisa z"l who questioned on Shabbat morning prayers why when he reaches Ratzah he returns to V'Titen lecha Moshe — and why is mentioning at Ratzah insufficient since Yerushalmi on the boundary teaches he should pray eighteen and mention Ratzah only that the sages did not burden him — and here having prayed why not mention at Ratzah; see there that he left it requiring study. And his question is like Taz in laws of prayer siman 108 sk 5; see my Sefer HaChaim there to resolve it — and so his question too has an answer, and here it works well: for we hold one who changes from the form sages established for blessings must return — see Magen Avraham siman 213 in the name of Rambam — and so too here certainly had sages not ordained a Shabbat prayer he would have ordained Yaaleh veYavo with Shabbat mention; but since they ordained a separate Shabbat prayer, they did not ordain Shabbat mention in Yaaleh veYavo either; and now if he mentions Shabbat at Ratzah he changes from the sages' established form — and so it appears correct; examine.
