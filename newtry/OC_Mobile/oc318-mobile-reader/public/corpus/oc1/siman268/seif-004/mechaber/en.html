One who prayed the weekday prayer on Shabbos and did not mention [the theme] of Shabbos has not fulfilled his obligation; but if he mentioned [the theme of] Shabbos within the eighteen [blessings], even though he did not establish a [separate] blessing for Shabbos, he has fulfilled his obligation. {Rama: And in Musaf, even if he said only "and let us perform before You our obligatory offerings — the daily offerings of the day and the additional offering [of Musaf]," he has fulfilled his obligation (Beis Yosef, siman 286, in the name of the Rosh, chapter "Mi Shemeso").}
