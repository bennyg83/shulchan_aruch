(9) A weekday prayer on Shabbos. If uncertain whether he prayed the weekday or Shabbos prayer, he must also repeat, for presumably he prayed the weekday text to which he is accustomed [Eliyah Rabbah in siman 422; Chayei Adam].
(10) He did not fulfill his obligation. The same applies on Yom Tov, even its second day.
(11) Only, 'May we perform,' etc. Meaning he said within one of the blessings, 'May it be Your will that we perform before You,' etc. Certainly, if he recited the proper Mussaf text but omitted the verses of the offerings, he fulfilled his obligation [later authorities].
