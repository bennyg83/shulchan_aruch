One of these in place of another. Turei Zahav. The opinion introduced as ‘some say’ maintains, as Shiltei HaGibborim writes in the name of Beis Yosef, that if one recited the Mussaf prayer in place of Shacharis, Maariv, or Mincha, then even though he said ‘Find favor in our rest,’ he did not fulfill his obligation, since he recited the verses of Mussaf. All the more so, if he recited Shacharis in place of Mussaf and omitted the verses of Mussaf, he did not fulfill his obligation. It requires consideration whether, if he said ‘And on the Shabbos day’ within Yismach Moshe as his Mussaf prayer, he likewise fails to fulfill the obligation, since accepting Shabbos is explicitly mentioned there and the words ‘You commanded us to offer the Mussaf offering’ also appear. All the laws in this siman concerning the Shabbos prayers (whether beginning Atah chonen or interchanging Mussaf and Shacharis) also apply on Yom Tov, and even on the second day of Yom Tov. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3. In paragraph 11, Turei Zahav explains the opinion introduced as ‘some say,’ although Beis Yosef cites the Shiltei HaGibborim ruling without disagreement and the Baraisa states the case of interchanging without qualification. For practical law, see Magen Avraham, paragraph 9, and Eshel Avraham there.
