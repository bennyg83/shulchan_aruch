Bridegrooms. This means that they pray with a minyan in their home. The same applies to people who occasionally pray with a minyan in their home. If, however, they establish a place in which to pray for several days, as is commonly done at fairs, it resembles a permanent synagogue and this blessing is recited there. Taz. Knesses HaGedolah writes that in a place without a permanent synagogue containing a Torah scroll, the blessing should not be recited; see there.
