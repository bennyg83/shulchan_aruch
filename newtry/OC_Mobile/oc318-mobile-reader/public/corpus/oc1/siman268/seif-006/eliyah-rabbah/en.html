[Levush] The beginning of the Shabbos prayer, etc. It seems that nevertheless he does not complete either Atah Kidashta or Atah Echad, for which of them should he complete? Rather he says Yismach Moshe, and it is proper to say, 'You, Holy One, blessed be He, gladdened Moshe,' etc.; and similarly, Atah Tikanta, etc. (Lechem Chamudos, page 21). It follows from this that if in Shacharis he began Atah Kidashta, he completes it, and likewise at Maariv, if he began Yismach Moshe, he completes it. In my humble opinion this is not clear; rather, he must stop and begin the appropriate blessing. In seif 15 it says only that he does not go back, meaning if he already completed the blessing. But as long as he has not completed it, he stops. So too it is proven in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 14, in the question there; see there. This also appears from the Magen Avraham.
