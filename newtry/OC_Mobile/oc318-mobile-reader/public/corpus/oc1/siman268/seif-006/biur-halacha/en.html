Mussaf in another prayer. See Mishna Berurah: if he has not yet moved his feet, he returns to Tikanta Shabbos. Derech HaChaim writes that whenever he remembers during one of the final three blessings, he returns to Tikanta Shabbos. Chayei Adam, klal 28, inclines to say that whenever he erred and did not mention Mussaf in Kedushas HaYom, he should say during the blessing of Avodah, when he reaches ‘Restore the service,’ the words ‘and there shall be offered before You the daily offerings and the Mussaf offering’; see there for his reasoning.
Another prayer in place of Mussaf. See Mishna Berurah regarding what I wrote: if he remembered before moving his feet, etc., he begins Yismach Moshe. It is obvious that he begins from the point where he erred. I saw, however, that Derech HaChaim rules that even if he remembers in the middle of the Mussaf prayer, he returns to the beginning of the prayer. This is astonishing. He himself writes above regarding the laws of Yaaleh Veyavo that even if one uttered an absolute falsehood in the middle of the prayer, his law is like one who spoke: he returns to the beginning of that blessing, or, if he is in the final three blessings, to Retzei. This is clear. There must therefore be a scribal error in his words here.
