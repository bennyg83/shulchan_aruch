One may not speak, etc. Tur cites Sefer Chasidim: A pious man once saw another pious man after the latter’s death (that is, in a dream) and his face was green. He asked him, ‘Why is your face green?’ He answered, ‘Because I used to speak while the congregation recited Vayechulu, Magen Avos, and Yisgadal.’ This concludes his words.
