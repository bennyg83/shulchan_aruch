Paragraph 12: It is not, etc.; unlike Ne'ilah. This anticipates the question that when Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos, Shabbos is mentioned even in Ne'ilah, although Ne'ilah has no ordinary Shabbos counterpart. Likewise, when Yom Tov falls on Shabbos, perhaps its subject should be mentioned in Me'ein Sheva despite no relationship to Yom Tov.
The cases differ. Although ordinary Shabbos has no Ne'ilah, this day is Yom Kippur and therefore obligated in Ne'ilah; the Sages required every prayer recited on Shabbos to mention Shabbos. Me'ein Sheva, by contrast, is not inherently required even on Shabbos but was instituted because of danger, as Taz paragraph 6 explains. It therefore needs only the institution's actual subject (Shabbos) not Yom Tov.
