To speak. Sefer Chasidim relates that one pietist saw another pietist after his death, and the deceased's face was green. He asked, "Why is your face green?" The deceased answered, "Because I would speak during Vayechulu while the congregation recited it, during the blessing Magen Avos, and during Yisgadal; and because I was not careful to cut my fingernails on Erev Shabbos." Mateh Moshe writes that Rabbi Kalonymus once forgot to recite Vayechulu. He remembered during the meal, took a cup in his hand, and recited Vayechulu through the word "la'asos." See there.
