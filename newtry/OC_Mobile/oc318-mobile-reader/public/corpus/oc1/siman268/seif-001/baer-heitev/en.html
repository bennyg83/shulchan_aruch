A blessing. Strictly according to law, one ought to recite all eighteen blessings on Shabbos. The Sages did not impose this burden out of respect for Shabbos; therefore, once one has begun a weekday blessing, he completes it. A person to whom this happens should be concerned throughout that Shabbos, for it is a bad sign for him. He should examine his deeds and contemplate repentance. Shelah.
