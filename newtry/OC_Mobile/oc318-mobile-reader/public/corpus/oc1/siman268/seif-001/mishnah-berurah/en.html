(a) Saying and they were able - Damer Rav HaManuna all who prays and says and they were able to become a partner with Heaven in the act of Genesis. And after the fact, if they didn't say, they don't return it, and in siman 4, they say in Kiddush and they could [Peri Megadim]. In the text, you sanctify, you need to pause a little between your name and purpose, so that it doesn't become like the repetition above 16, and also when it was possible, it should stop between Elohim and Heaven [Ar].
