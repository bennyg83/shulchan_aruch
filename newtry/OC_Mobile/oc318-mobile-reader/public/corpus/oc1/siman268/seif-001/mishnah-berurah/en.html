(1) He says Vayechulu. Rav Hamnuna said: anyone who prays and recites Vayechulu is regarded as though he became a partner with the Holy One, blessed be He, in Creation. Do not read vayechulu, 'they were completed,' but vayechallu, 'they completed'; God and he. Nevertheless, after the fact, omission does not require repetition, especially for us, who recite Vayechulu in Kiddush [Pri Megadim]. In Atah Kidashta one should pause slightly between lishmecha and tachlis, lest it appear, God forbid, to refer backward; in Vayechulu, pause between Elokim and es [Eliyah Rabbah]. The custom is to say vayanuchu vah on Friday night, vayanuchu vo by day, and vayanuchu vam at Minchah [Knesses HaGedolah; see there for the reason].
