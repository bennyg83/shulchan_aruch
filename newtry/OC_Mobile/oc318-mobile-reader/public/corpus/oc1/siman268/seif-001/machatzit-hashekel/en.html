Paragraph 1: He completes, etc.; for by law, etc. Berachos 21a says: Rav Yehudah in Shmuel's name rules that one who remembers during prayer that he already prayed stops even mid-blessing. This differs from students who mistakenly mention weekday blessings on Shabbos and complete that blessing. There the person is fundamentally obligated, but the Sages spared him exertion for Shabbos honor; here he already prayed. The passage means as Magen Avraham explains.
For in Musaf one need not pray eighteen, etc. Even on weekday Musaf (Rosh Chodesh or Chol HaMoed) only seven blessings are said. The first view, which also requires completing a mistaken blessing in Musaf, holds Musaf is fundamentally fit for eighteen blessings. The Yerushalmi reports that Rosh Chodesh and Chol HaMoed Musaf once consisted of eighteen with an added request to perform the daily and Musaf offerings. Shabbos too fundamentally warranted eighteen with a Musaf addition, but the Sages did not burden worshippers.
