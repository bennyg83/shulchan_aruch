If one errs and prays the weekday prayer during Shabbat and does not mention Shabbat, if he left the place where he stood in prayer he goes back to the beginning, and if he did not leave his place, even if he finished his prayer, he must return only to the beginning of the blessing for Shabbos {Rama: (and regarding the Chazan that forgets the blessing for Shabbat in the morning prayer, see above Siman 126).}
