(20) One blessing comprising seven. This is the blessing the shaliach tzibbur recites after prayer: 'Blessed are You, Hashem, our God and God of our fathers,' etc., 'Who acquires heaven and earth,' etc. It was instituted because of danger from harmful beings: their synagogues were in fields, and latecomers might remain alone there. The blessing was instituted so that while the shaliach tzibbur prolonged it, they could finish their prayers. Even nowadays the enactment remains.
(21) Without its conclusion. Meaning from Magen Avos through zecher l'maaseh Bereishis only.
(22) Without opening and conclusion. The primary enactment was for the shaliach tzibbur to recite the entire blessing. Therefore, the custom in some places for him to say aloud only through 'Who acquires heaven and earth' and then continue silently is improper. Rather, after the congregation finishes, he should begin Magen Avos aloud.
