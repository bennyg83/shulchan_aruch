[Levush] And according to the 'some say' above, etc. In Nachalas Tzvi he objected that everyone agrees in this case, for how could the Rambam disagree with the Yerushalmi, end quote. This is not difficult at all, because our Gemara and another Yerushalmi contradict this, as the Rosh wrote in the chapter Mi Shemeiso. So too it appears from the Rosh there and from the Chiddushei Rashba that they disagree in this as well. Nevertheless, it seems to me as the practical law that he has fulfilled the obligation, for I wrote above that the main reason we rule in seif 2 like the 'some say' is because of the doubt of a blessing in vain, and here according to the 'some say' it would be a blessing in vain. If he was in doubt on Shabbos or Yom Tov, he does not need to go back (Kol Bo).
