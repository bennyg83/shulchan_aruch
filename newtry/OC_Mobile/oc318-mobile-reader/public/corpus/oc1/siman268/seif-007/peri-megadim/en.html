Yom Tov. Turei Zahav. This differs from Neilah when Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos, in siman 623, seif 3, where Shabbos is mentioned because the day itself created the obligation, etc. Here it is merely a rabbinic enactment. See Magen Avraham, paragraph 12, and Eshel Avraham there. The blessing that encapsulates seven corresponds to the seven blessings: Magen Avos corresponds to Magen Avraham; Mechayeh Meisim to Mechayeh HaMeisim; HaE-l HaKadosh to HaE-l HaKadosh; HaMeniach LeAmo corresponds to the fourth blessing; Lefanav Naavod corresponds to Avodah; Venodeh Lishmo corresponds to Hodaah; and Adon HaShalom corresponds to the blessing of Shalom. Rabbi David Abudraham; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 15. It is recited while standing, as Shaarei Teshuvah writes in the name of the Tanna.
