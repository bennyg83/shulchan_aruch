(18) Which is not recited, etc. Because of this particular Shabbos it was instituted for every Shabbos.
(19) And standing. We thereby testify before God concerning Creation, and witnesses must stand, as written, 'The two men shall stand.' It is best to recite it together with the congregation, since a complete assembly should testify before God; at least two are required. Some say an individual does not repeat Vayechulu because an individual cannot testify. Others say he may recite it but need not stand. It is best that even an individual recite it, not intending testimony but reading Torah. Mateh Moshe writes that one who forgot it in the synagogue should recite the Vayechulu in Kiddush standing. The custom is to recite it in Kiddush standing even after saying it in the synagogue [Pri Megadim].
