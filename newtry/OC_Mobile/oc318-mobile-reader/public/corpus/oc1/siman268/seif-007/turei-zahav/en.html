And while standing. By reciting this passage we testify before the Holy One, blessed is He, concerning the work of Creation, and witnesses must stand. Tur concludes that it is recited by an individual as well. Accordingly, it appears to me that an individual praying alone does not repeat Vayechulu, because testimony cannot be given by one person; a complete body of witnesses (ten) is required to testify before the Holy One, blessed is He. If an individual nevertheless wishes to recite it, he should not intend it as testimony, but should read it as one reads a passage of the Torah. So it appears in my humble opinion.
