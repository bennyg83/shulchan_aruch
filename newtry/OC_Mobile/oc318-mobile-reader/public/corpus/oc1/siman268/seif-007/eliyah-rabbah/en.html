He mentioned Shabbos, etc. This requires study, for in siman 487, if on Yom Tov he concluded 'Who sanctifies Shabbos,' he has not fulfilled his obligation, even though he mentioned Yom Tov in the middle. One must say that it is worse when he concluded with the formula of another day, and this requires study. The Rosh and Rambam imply that he fulfilled the obligation specifically in Mussaf (Magen Avraham). But later he wrote that it refers to where he also did not mention Yom Tov in the middle, and his words contradict one another. Also what he wrote, 'and in the Rosh and Rambam,' etc., is not so, for the Rambam in chapter 10 discusses where he did not mention Shabbos, and the Rosh mentioned Mussaf as a novelty, and also in order to bring proof from it; see there. It also seems to me that later as well it refers to where he mentioned Yom Tov in the middle, and nevertheless he did not fulfill the obligation, since he did not pray a proper Shemoneh Esrei. For one fulfills with Shemoneh Esrei provided that he mentions the day, or that he prays the actual blessing of the day.
