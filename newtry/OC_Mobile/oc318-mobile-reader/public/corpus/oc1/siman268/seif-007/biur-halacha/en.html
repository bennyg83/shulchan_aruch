And while standing. See Mishna Berurah, where I wrote that initially they should endeavor to recite it together. This follows Magen Avraham: initially it is proper to satisfy the view of the first Tanna, who maintains that witnesses must testify together. In truth, however, the law in Choshen Mishpat, siman 28, is that witnesses need not testify together. Magen Avraham further writes that initially it is preferable to recite it with ten men. For this reason, one praying the silent Amidah should hasten to complete his prayer so that he can recite Vayechulu with the congregation. Peri Megadim.
