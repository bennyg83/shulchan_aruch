It is a mitzvah on Yom Tov to call to the Torah those who sustain the community. Therefore, they take precedence over everyone else if there is no exceptionally distinguished Torah scholar present. Maharil. See siman 282, seif 3. In places where the mitzvos are sold, the purchaser may call whomever he wishes, provided that he calls each person in accordance with his honor. Levush. The custom is that the leading member of the community receives the final aliyah. Beit Yosef, siman 135. See Responsa Maharam MiRotenburg, siman 6, who writes that in a place where the custom is that the communal leader receives an aliyah, he may not waive it in favor of another person to be called in his place. Maharik likewise writes, shoresh 9, that in a place where the custom is for the Chassan Bereishis to receive the aliyah in place of a kohen, he may not sell his right to another person. Rabbeinu Levi of Trèves disagrees. See siman 153, seif 12. Knesses HaGedolah.
