The appointees. In a place where they practice that the parnas who goes up may not waive his right for another to read in his place — Rama Mishna Berurah siman 66. And if there are many bridegrooms marrying virgins, they precede a widow; and a widow precedes a chalutzah; and a chalutzah precedes a divorcee — when all are equal in Torah knowledge and there are no kohanim or Leviim among them — Knesset HaGedolah. And likewise in a place where they practice: when an individual has a Torah scroll in synagogue, when he makes a celebration they read from his scroll; and when two householders have a celebration, whichever precedes — a Torah scholar who teaches practical halachic rulings precedes a refined Torah scholar who does not yet know how to teach practical halacha. A Torah scholar precedes a kohen am ha'aretz; kohen precedes Levi; Levi precedes Israel; and if both are equal they cast lots between them. The same law applies to two children to circumcise if they were born at the same hour; and likewise one who wrote a Torah scroll with his own hands or acquired it with his own money precedes one who inherited a Torah scroll from his ancestors — Knesset HaGedolah, see there. If they called one to ascend and read from the Torah and another ran and stood in his place — whether he must pay him the value of the blessing he lost, see Tosafos and the Rosh, chapter Kisuy HaDam; and see in Knesset HaGedolah. And they practice that the elder of the congregation goes up last. Beit Yosef.
