(1) [Seif 1] "On Shabbat and Yom Tov, etc." — meaning that on Monday and Thursday and at Minchah on Shabbat, when they do not add beyond three, this law does not apply; it applies only on Shabbat when they read seven — likewise on Yom Tov and Yom Kippur it also applies, that if they wish to add they may add, as below in siman 282 — see there Bach; and see what Petaḥ HaDevir wrote, letter 1, that Maran, may he rest in Eden, also holds that they add on Yom Tov and Yom Kippur — see there; and what Perishah wrote, letter 1, that on weekdays when they read only three — plainly that they call the elder for the third, etc. — see Rashi Gittin 60a, s.v. mi sheShavet, etc., which implies there that on weekdays not; and in the days of the Talmud — and likewise now one may call on weekdays whoever he wishes — Or Sameiach.
