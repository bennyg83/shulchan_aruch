1) [Seif 1] On Shabbos and Yom Tov, etc. That is, on Monday and Thursday and at Minchah on Shabbos, when one does not add to the three [aliyos], this law does not apply; rather, only on Shabbos, when they read seven, does it apply. And the same applies on Yom Tov and Yom Kippur, [where it] also applies, for if they wished to add, they may add, as [stated] below in siman 282; see there. Bach. And see what the Pesach HaDvir wrote, ot 1, that Maran, of blessed memory, also holds that one adds on Yom Tov and Yom Kippur; see there. And as for what the Perishah wrote, ot 1, that on a weekday, when they read only three, it is obvious that they call up the great one for the third, etc. -- see Rashi, Gittin, folio 60a, s.v. 'one who rested,' etc., where it is implied that on a weekday, no; and in the days of the Talmud, and the same now, whoever wishes may read on a weekday. Eshel Avraham.
