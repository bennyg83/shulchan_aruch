1) [Seif 1] Namely, crumbs of bread, etc. They do not contain a kezayis and also lack the appearance of bread, so their blessing is Borei Minei Mezonos, as stated in siman 168, seif 10; see there. Bach writes that bread covers them because they are brought to stimulate the appetite for food. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1, however, writes that even if they are not brought to stimulate the appetite, bread covers them because they themselves provide nourishment; see there.
