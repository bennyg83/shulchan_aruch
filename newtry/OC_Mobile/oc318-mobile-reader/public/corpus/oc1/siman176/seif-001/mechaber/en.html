That bread exempts the appetizer. 1 section: If one
blessed over bread, it exempts the appetizer, meaning very finely crumbled bread mixed with soup or honey. If one blessed over the appetizer, it does not exempt the bread.Rema: If one blessed over the appetizer it exempts cooked dishes such as porridge and the like, and similarly if one blessed over a cooked dish it exempts the appetizer. (Gemara, ch. Keitzad Mevarkhim, and Rambam in his commentary to the Mishnah).
