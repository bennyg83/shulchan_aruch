Bread exempts the parperet. And in it one seif: If
one blessed on bread, it exempts the parperet, meaning very fine bread crumbs (1) that stuck with soup or honey (2). If one blessed on the parperet, it does not exempt the bread (3).If one blessed on the parperet it exempts maaseh kedeirah such as porridge and the like, and similarly if one blessed on maaseh kedeirah it exempts the parperet (Gemara chapter Keitzad Mevarkhin, and Rambam in his commentary on the Mishnah).
