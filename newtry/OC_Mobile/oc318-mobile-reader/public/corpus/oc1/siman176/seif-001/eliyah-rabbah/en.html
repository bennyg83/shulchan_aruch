[Levush] They did not find, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov writes that according to the Levush's own ruling in siman 177, seif 2, he should have quoted the Tur's formulation that parperet served after the meal but before Birkat HaMazon does not exempt other foods. In my humble opinion the Levush wrote according to our custom, under which no blessing is recited even at large meals, as explained below in siman 177. According to what I write there, however, one who is God fearing and meticulous should be careful to recite the blessing. The same applies here, and similarly above in siman 174, seif katan 4.
