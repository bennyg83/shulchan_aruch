Within the festival. Tosafot's language: from the beginning of the seven until the end of Pesach; and so Semag implies — even on Yom Tov itself he checks, and even though he nullified we decree lest he come to eat from it; and if he finds on Yom Tov he covers it with a vessel as written siman 446; and so Taz, unlike Bach. That we do not decree lest when he finds it he eat from it — because he himself is occupied with burning it, so how would he eat from it (Tosafot)? And it appears to me that even on the last day of Pesach he must check (see Turei Zahav).

He does not bless. Since it is permitted to retain it — even in eating it is not forbidden except because of a penalty — and he does not check except to distinguish this chametz from permitted chametz; therefore he does not bless (nevertheless, and so it appears). It implies that if some definite chametz became leavened during Pesach he blesses at the time of biur; but something that is not definite chametz — one does not bless on it; and if he found chametz in his house — requires study whether he blesses on it since he nullified before Pesach and already fulfilled the Sages' enactment in bedikah.
