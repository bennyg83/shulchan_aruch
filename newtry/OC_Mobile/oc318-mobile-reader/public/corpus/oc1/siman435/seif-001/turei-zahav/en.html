He checks within the festival. It appears that even on Yom Tov itself he checks; and even though the chametz is not known, nevertheless it is as known since he is accustomed to it all year — certainly crumbs of chametz remained there; and proof from Rosh in the first chapter who wrote we are certain crumbs remained — even if he already checked, all the more here where he has not yet checked; and chametz was used there all year. They did not distinguish above between known and unknown chametz except where there is a room not used there regularly all year — as explained.

He does not bless. Mishnah Megillah wrote the reason: since he eats chametz and does not check except to distinguish this chametz from other chametz — how can he bless on destroying chametz? But within the festival, where it is a mitzvah that chametz not be seen in his possession — certainly he should bless — end. And accordingly it appears: if he nullified before Pesach and it comes within the festival — he does not bless on bedikah, since he also does not transgress bal yeira'eh since he already nullified, and bedikah is only so he not eat it; but one may say: since he does not eat chametz at all, bedikah is obligatory because of the prohibition of eating; and a doubt regarding their words is resolved leniently — as above. And Rokeach wrote: even bedikah after the festival should be by candle even by day.
