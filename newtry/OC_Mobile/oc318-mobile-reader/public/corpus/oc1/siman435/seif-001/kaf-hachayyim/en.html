1) [Se'if 1] If one did not search on the night of the fourteenth, one should search during the day of the fourteenth, etc. This search must be performed by candlelight. The same applies to every search, even one performed after Pesach, since no distinction should be made between one search and another. This is also the implication of the authorities cited above in siman 433. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Chok Yosef, subsection 2; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1; Chemed Moshe, subsection 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2; Chayei Adam, rule 119, subsection 28.
