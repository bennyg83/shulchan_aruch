He did not check, etc. However, it appears that on Yom Tov itself he does not check — even if he did not nullify before Yom Tov — for even if chametz is found in his house on Yom Tov he covers it with a vessel though he transgresses bal yeira'eh by retaining it; if so, why impose upon him an obligation of bedikah since it is only d'rabbanan? But on chol ha'moed he checks; and only after Yom Tov specifically by candle even by day (Rokeach siman 267).
