And in the introduction I will now explain briefly:

(2) Know that in the matter of bedikat chametz there are three methods explained in their words, and two more as I will say. Method one: both require biur for known [chametz] and bitul for unknown — Orach Chayim Laws of chametz uMatzah chapter 2, and as Rosh wrote Pesachim 6b: "it shall not be seen" and "it shall not be found" — even per Rambam chapter 9 Laws of chametz uMatzah: doubtful chametz is permitted, it became established presumptively. Method two: Ran — one may say first chapter: chametz suffices either bitul or biur, whether known or unknown — see there. Method three: Tur in Tel'a and siman 366 for unknown chametz bitul only, not bedikah lest some remain; and one who ate milk unwittingly — Magen Avraham siman 434 note 5 — is not called an ones since he could nullify it; and d'rabbanan also requires bedikah lest he come to eat. We have method four: a room sometimes used for chametz — only doubtful chametz — per Rambam all doubtful chametz is permitted; even per those who forbid all doubtful chametz, the Merciful One obligated biur on definite not doubtful — unlike a room used all year where certainly much chametz exists — for this obligation applies per all three methods. Method five: as Tosafot beginning Kol Sha'ah s.v. di: even a room used all year, since "where is it" — no violation unless he placed it with his hands — this seems Rashi's method. "We check lest he transgress 'it shall not be found'" — lest he find on Pesach and pause a moment and transgress — not chametz, etc.; Rashi did not explain lest he come to eat — unlike a loaf in a beam known, unlike doubt therefore Chazal did not require checking. Rashi 12b within the festival until darkening, but not on Yom Tov since karet — Rabbanan feared therefore as Ran; even without bitul no violation since "where is it." Even per Rashi Pesachim 6a s.v. kofeh: without bitul he may burn on Yom Tov, but we fear lest he pause a moment and transgress as Rashi there 6b — and another concern against that: lest when searching he find and eat — therefore R' Yehuda on Shabbat we do not check, Rabbanan from darkening we do not check. Our master Maharsha explained at length: therefore per Rashi for Rabbanan from darkening do not check since main bedikah is rabbinic even in a room used all year lest he find on Pesach and pause — if so better not check lest he not see all Pesach days — unlike an oven where he might attribute and transgress "it shall not be found" b'mezid. Maharsha on crumbs — one may examine; we say perhaps until night there is no "it shall not be found" and pausing a moment is no violation; Rashi 4a s.v. bein. That we say chametz in bitul suffices for known chametz and nevertheless these are trusted unlike Rabbenu Yerucham Yoreh De'ah 127:3 without establishment certainly chametz requires bitul even unknown. That bitul alone for unknown — one may say bitul in heart also helps and he forgets everything unlike a great bedikah act he remembers and thus remembers bitul per Rashi — as I wrote end of siman.
