The law of one who did not check on the night of the 14th.
Contains 1 seif. If he did not check on the night of the 14th he checks on the 14th day at whatever hour he remembers from that day. If he did not check all day on the 14th he checks within the festival. If he did not check within the festival he checks after the festival so he does not fail regarding chametz that passed over Pesach, which is forbidden in benefit. And on the checking after Pesach he does not bless.
