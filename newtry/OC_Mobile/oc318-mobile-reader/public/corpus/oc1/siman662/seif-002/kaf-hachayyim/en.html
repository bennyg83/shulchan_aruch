2) [Seif 2] One does not recite Shehecheyanu on the second day over the lulav, etc. The Tur cites a dispute regarding this, but writes that his father, the Rosh, of blessed memory, agreed that one should not recite it, and this is the custom in Ashkenaz. Therefore, he ruled likewise in the Shulchan Aruch. The reason written there in the Tur is that one should not recite it, because either way he fulfilled his obligation with the Shehecheyanu recited on the first day, even if that day was a weekday, for it is no worse than if he had recited it at the time of preparing the lulav, in which case he would have fulfilled his obligation. This is not comparable to the Shehecheyanu of Kiddush that he recites on the second night, for that is recited on account of the day and not on account of the sukkah. End quote. Although with regard to shofar one also recites Shehecheyanu on the second day, as written above at the end of siman 600 in the gloss, here it is different, because sometimes the primary sanctity is on the second day, as stated in siman 600. Taz, s.k. 1. See above, siman 644, os 8.
