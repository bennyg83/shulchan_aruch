He does not recite Shehecheyanu. See what I wrote at the end of siman 600. See Minhagim, which wrote that Shehecheyanu is from the Torah; this is astonishing. Perhaps his intent is that there is no dispute about it, unlike Rosh Hashanah, where they raised the question in the Gemara. But another reason appears to me: with lulav, if one recited Shehecheyanu at the time of making it, he fulfilled the obligation, as written in siman 648. Therefore, even if yesterday was weekday, he fulfilled the obligation with the Shehecheyanu, for it is no worse than the time of making it. This is not so with shofar, as appears to me clearly.
