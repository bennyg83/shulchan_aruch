3) Ibid.: Unless the first day fell on Shabbos. For he must recite Shehecheyanu on the second day, since this is the first time he takes it, as he did not recite it on the first day. Levush. The same applies if on the second day as well they did not fulfill the mitzvah of lulav, such as when the four species were unavailable. In any event, he must recite Shehecheyanu on the third day. Maharshal, Responsa, siman 8. The same applies if they did not fulfill the mitzvah of lulav until the seventh day; he must recite Shehecheyanu on the day he fulfills the mitzvah. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur. Eliyah Rabbah, os 5; Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 3. Regarding what Mishbetzos Zahav wrote there, implying that if one took it on the first day but did not recite Shehecheyanu, he does not recite Shehecheyanu when taking it on the second day, this is not the view of the poskim, as written above in siman 644, os 7; see there. Mishna Berurah as well, in this siman, Shaar HaTziyun, os 4, wrote to challenge his view; see there.
