1) [Seif 1] On the second day, one recites the blessing over taking the lulav, etc. In the chapter Lulav VaAravah, Sukkah 46a, there is one who says that one recites a blessing over it only on the first day, and there is one who says that one recites a blessing over it all seven days. The poskim ruled according to the opinion that all seven days require a blessing. Beit Yosef. The reason, Rashi wrote there, is that although it is rabbinic, we recite a blessing over it just as we recite a blessing over the Chanukah light and over the Megillah, which are rabbinic. End quote. The Gemara there asks regarding the blessing over the Chanukah light, "Who commanded us," where did He command us? From the verse, "Do not turn aside"; see there.
