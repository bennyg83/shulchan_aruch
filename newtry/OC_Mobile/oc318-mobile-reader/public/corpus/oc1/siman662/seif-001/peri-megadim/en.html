He does not. See the Taz. In the Levush he wrote that with lulav he has fulfilled the obligation whichever way you look at it, if the time of making it, etc., unlike the reasoning of Bnei Eivra. For this reason, with regard to the Shehecheyanu of sukkah on the second night, he also fulfilled the obligation with the Shehecheyanu of the first night, as written in siman 641, and the Shehecheyanu of the second night is only the Shehecheyanu of Yom Tov, not of sukkah. This is not so with shofar, to which a blessing at the time of making does not apply; see there in the Levush. This is difficult, for the making of a shofar does not come from time to time, since one shofar may serve for several years; the same should apply to one sukkah for all his life, if he covered it with sechach; see there. The Taz answered that regarding shofar, sometimes the second day is the primary day: if witnesses came from Minchah and onward when the Beis HaMikdash stood, they would observe that day as holy, and the main day would be the second day. Therefore now too Shehecheyanu is said on the second day, unlike lulav. See siman 600 in the Taz, letter 2. See Eliyah Rabbah and Or Zarua in the name of Knesses HaGedolah, that if one did not take it and recite Shehecheyanu until the seventh day, he says Shehecheyanu on the seventh, for one recites Shehecheyanu also over rabbinic mitzvos. It implies, however, that if he did not take it on the first day and did not recite Shehecheyanu, he does not recite Shehecheyanu on the second day when he takes it. See Magen Avraham, and with Hashem's help it will be explained further there.
What. The Tur wrote regarding the custom of Toledo. The Taz answered that they do not say, 'Save us today in the celebration of the second day,' but only, 'Save us in the celebration of the second day,' meaning: if it is today, and if tomorrow, it is a prayer for the future. Also, there is no degradation in this, for one can explain that it means: save us on the second day of our Yom Tov, etc. The word 'ours' is a scribal error.
