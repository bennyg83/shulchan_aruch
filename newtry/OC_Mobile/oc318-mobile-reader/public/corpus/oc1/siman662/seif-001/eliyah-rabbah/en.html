[1] [Levush] Lulav is different, etc. It is surprising that the Magen Avraham did not examine the Levush or the Matteh Moshe. In Minhagim he wrote that the night does not exempt them. And if you ask: on the first day too, he should not recite Shehecheyanu over the lulav, one may answer that the first night requires Shehecheyanu because of sukkah, apart from Yom Tov, and therefore it does not exempt. End quote. The Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote that according to this, one must say that specifically regarding sukkah it is applicable to say that he fulfilled his obligation with Shehecheyanu, etc. End quote. Meaning, his difficulty was: if so, on the second day too, Shehecheyanu should be required because of sukkah, and should not exempt lulav. He answers that sukkah is exempted by the Shehecheyanu of the first day, even if it was weekday, but not with regard to lulav. See Tashbetz, siman 151.
