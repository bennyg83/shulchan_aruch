In a place where they reverse the order when the first day falls on Shabbos, it would be fitting to recite El Na on the first day and not on the second day. The common practice is not so, because people did not know the reason for the matter. HaRish; Mishna Berurah, sec. 2. See Eshel Avraham at the end of the siman.
