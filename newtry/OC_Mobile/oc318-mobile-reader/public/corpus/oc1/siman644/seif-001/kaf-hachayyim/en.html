1) [Seif 1] In the morning, etc. The Tur states: In the morning they arise early for the synagogue. The Bach explains that the reason is that the zealous hasten to perform the mitzvah of lulav. So too we learned at the end of the chapter Lulav HaGazul: On the following day they arise early and come, each person recognizes his own lulav and takes it; see there. Regarding what the Knesset HaGedolah wrote concerning the aforementioned words of the Bach, see what Eliyah Rabbah, sec. 1, Birkei Yosef, and Maamar Mordechai questioned about his words; see there.
2) Ibid. In the morning, etc. One does not recite El Na on the first day of Sukkos, because in its place he says Az Haysah Chanayas Sukko, which is likewise song and praise like El Na. Levush. In a place where they reverse the order when the first day falls on Shabbos, it would be fitting to recite El Na on the first day and not on the second day. The common practice is not so, because people did not know the reason for the matter. HaRish; Mishna Berurah, sec. 2. See Eshel Avraham at the end of the siman.
