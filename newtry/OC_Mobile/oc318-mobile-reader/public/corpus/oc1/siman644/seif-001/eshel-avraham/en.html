And we complete Hallel. The Maharash wrote that we do not complete Hallel on Chol HaMoed of Pesach because on the seventh day we also do not complete it, due to the reason that the work of My hands drowned, etc., and Chol HaMoed should not be more stringent than Yom Tov. In the Talmud in Eruvin the reason given for distinguishing is that the days differ in their korbanos. See Responsa Rashba. Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 94, answered that certainly the main reason is like the Maharash, and the sugya of the Talmud is also that way, for if so, what is different about Chol HaMoed of Pesach that we complete Hallel? Is it not also more stringent than Yom Tov, namely the seventh day of Pesach? What difference is there whether it is close to it in time or not? He answers that there is another reason as well.
