[1] Levush: In the right hand, etc. This is difficult, for in siman 529 it says that on Yom Tov people come late to the synagogue, and this is from the Gemara in Perek HaKorei Omed. In truth, the Tur there also wrote that they come early. The Bayis Chadash distinguishes: on Sukkos they come early, because the zealous perform mitzvos early, namely the mitzvah of lulav. The Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah asked: if so, on Rosh Hashanah too they should come early because of shofar. These are his words. I am astonished, for in siman 584 it is indeed stated that they come early, and the Bayis Chadash there wrote that the reason is because of shofar, with other reasons as well; see there.
