Magen Avraham, seif katan 5. If he made, meaning, as the author wrote, “a sudar that he designated,” etc. — which implies that even though the pouch was made for mundane use and afterward he designated it by speech alone or by thought, it is likewise forbidden to change it to mundane use if he wrapped in it. That is, specifically when he wrapped tefillin plainly; but if he said explicitly with intent to empty it, it is permitted. Not so when he made the pouch for tefillin — even if he wrapped with intent to empty it, it is forbidden. And in what he wrote, “and if he wrapped once,” etc. — meaning, because in the Beit HaGadol he designated and wrapped. And it deals even if he did not make anything in it and also did not designate it.
