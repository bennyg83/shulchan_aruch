And if he stipulated on them from the outset. — See Mishnah Berurah in what he wrote — that is, at the time of making the head tefillin. Now, although from Bach's words it seems that likewise it helps if the condition was the first time when he tied them — apparently this requires investigation, for it is obvious that making head tefillin in any case is no less than designation through making a pouch for the sake of tefillin, regarding which Magen Avraham compelled from the Tur that even when he narrows it he explained it is only temporarily and afterward he will empty it — forbidden. If so, apparently all the more so it does not help if he explained that if he will need to empty it he may empty it — per the kal vachomer of the Orach Tzedek [cited in Beit Yosef in s.k. 4 from Maharai] — since at the time of designation there was no condition. And what is brought below in siman 154 seif 8 that a condition helps — that too refers to when it was at the time of designation; or there it refers to narrowing with intent of permanence that was the designation and use on the head once, and therefore a condition helps there — unlike our matter. And see Menachot 34, where it also seems the condition is in the designation — but this can be rejected: there, for the view that designation is significant; unlike for us, where designation alone is not significant, only together with tying — perhaps a condition helps even when he tied them on his head. And perhaps Bach would disagree with the kal vachomer of Orach Tzedek — requires investigation. And know: what is written in seif 3 in the hagahah "and if he stipulated on it from the outset, etc." — we can say it refers even if the condition was not only when he narrowed it that he does not narrow it except temporarily — it helps, for there he did not make the pouch for the sake of tefillin. And if the hagahah refers in all cases, it would also refer when the condition was at the time of making.

From head (tefillin) to hand (tefillin). — The parashiyot, and all the more so the straps; but per what is explained in siman 154 and in Pri Megadim there — there is a difference between them in what I will explain: the parashiyot and likewise the compartments, which are the body of sanctity — a condition does not help except to lower them to a lighter sanctity; but to mundane matters, such as if he wants to write on the sheets mundane matters and the like — a condition does not help, even if they are new. But straps, which are only tashmishei kedushah — a condition helps, even to lower them to mundane matters, even if he already used them; and as below in seif 3 in the hagahah, that even if he narrowed it, which is an act — a condition helps; and all this is for Shulchan Aruch there. But according to what we wrote there, lechatchilah one should be concerned for what Bach wrote in Rashi's method, that the place of the knot and the yud are sanctity themselves — their law is like parashiyot if he already used them; for through merely making the knot it is likely sanctity has not yet descended on them.
