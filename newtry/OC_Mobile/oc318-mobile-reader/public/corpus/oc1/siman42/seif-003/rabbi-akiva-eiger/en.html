Seif 3: to wrap money in it. And if he designated and wrapped tefillin in it — it is permitted to wrap other tefillin in it; per the view in Megillah that it does not need raising — end of Rashba's responsum, siman 537. And see above siman 154, seif 9.
In the gloss: for any matter it is permitted. See Machaneh Ephraim, Laws of Charity, siman 12.
