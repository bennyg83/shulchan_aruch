A cloth that he designated for wrapping tefillin permanently, and he wrapped tefillin in it once, may not be used to wrap money. Rama: If he stipulated concerning it from the outset, it is permitted in every circumstance. Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham and Mahari. Parchment processed for the sake of tefillin may not be used for writing mundane matters, because designation of this kind for the sacred object itself is legally effective. Ran, end of the chapter Nigmar HaDin. If he designated it but did not use it, or used it without designating it, it is permitted to use it for wrapping money.
