Seif 3 — "One who designated, etc., forever." See Rashi Avodah Zarah 47b s.v. "he placed, etc."

Forever, etc. As in the hagahah "and if, etc." and as above seif 2; and as the Rosh wrote in the last chapter of Megillah in the name of Yerushalmi — as cited.

And parchment, etc. As Tosafot explain in Menachot 42b s.v. "until, etc." — however, etc., that if it is a significant matter, etc., and nevertheless in processing it helps; thus such a matter. And see Nekudat Kesef there, not like Magen Avraham; therefore Nekudat Kesef wrote. But processing of straps, etc. — it is difficult: what the weaver of a shroud for the dead (learns) from processing; rather as the Razah wrote in the first chapter of Sukkah, that a shroud for the dead is a use of the object, unlike processing. But the Razah wrote there that even the straps are the main sanctity with which one binds. And see Sanhedrin 48a to exclude the heifer, etc., and as cited two tannaim, etc., and Rashi explained that Rava (holds) like the first tanna; and so in Menachot 42b — Abbaye holds like Rashbag, for Abbaye said to Rav Shmuel, etc.; and Tosafot challenged Rashi from Gittin 54b, for we hold there that processing requires leshmah, and tefillin hide too is the body of sanctity. And they further challenged that Rav and Shmuel disagree in Menachot there, and they established it also as (a dispute between) tannaim, and Shmuel like Rashbag. And in the first chapter of Sukkah he asks from Shmuel — thus the halachah is like him. But the Ritva explained there that the first tanna of Rashbag (is) not because of designation to the body of sanctity — certainly a significant matter as above — rather that leshmah is required in tefillin even in their writing; and in this it is easier than a Torah scroll and mezuzah, as in ruling lines. And Rashbag disagrees even regarding tashmishei (kedushah), like Abbaye and the tanna who said to the craftsman "make me, etc." — he holds like Rava that designation in tashmishei is not a significant matter, for a scroll certainly requires leshmah. And we hold like Rashbag in this, that we require leshmah in the body, and like Shmuel; but in tashmishei leshmah is not required and it is not a significant matter — unlike Rashbag and Abbaye. And study is needed: Rashbag mentioned only the body of sanctity, and in this Rava agrees; if so, what is the dispute of tannaim? All (hold) like Rava, and they disagree regarding leshmah. And see Tosafot there and in Menachot — and it implies that even regarding the body of sanctity it is not a significant matter, as in Menachot there s.v. "until"; however Rabbeinu Tam, etc., and some explain, etc., as above.
