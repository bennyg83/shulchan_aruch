Shulchan Aruch, seif 3, in Rama: For the sacred object itself. Although seif 1 teaches that designation of batim, which are themselves sacred objects, is ineffective, as Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 3, that concerns changing them to a lower sanctity. Here the issue is diversion to mundane use. See Responsa Tzemach Tzedek, siman 81.
