Shulchan Aruch, seif 3. In Rama: for the body of kedushah. Even though in seif 1, regarding batim that are the body of kedushah, designation does not count — as Magen Avraham wrote, seif katan 3 — that is regarding changing to minor kedushah; here it deals with mundane matter. See Tzemach Tzedek, siman 81.
