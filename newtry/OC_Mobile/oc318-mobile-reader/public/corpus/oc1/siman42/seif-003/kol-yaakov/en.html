3. There: It is permitted to change arm tefillin into head tefillin. This means that he makes four compartments and places each passage into a separate compartment. Otherwise it is invalid, because if the divisions are not externally recognizable, the head tefillin are invalid. Kesef Mishneh, chapter 3 of Hilchos Tefillin; Magen Avraham, seif katan 2; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 1; Keses HaSofer, siman 25, letter 4; and Emes LeYaakov, general rule 18, letter 4.
