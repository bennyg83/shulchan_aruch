(3) It is permitted, etc. This applies only after the tefillin have been removed. While the tefillin remain inside, mundane use is forbidden. Nimukei Yosef there. Magen Avraham writes that if a minor placed tefillin inside, the pouch becomes forbidden. It is obvious that a person cannot forbid property that is not his. If one stole cloth and made it into a tefillin pouch, he acquires it through physical alteration. Responsa Shaar Ephraim writes that a pouch in which money was stored may not later be used for tefillin. If one adds a new lower lining at the place where the tefillin will rest, it is permitted. See below, siman 144, and the end of siman 153.
