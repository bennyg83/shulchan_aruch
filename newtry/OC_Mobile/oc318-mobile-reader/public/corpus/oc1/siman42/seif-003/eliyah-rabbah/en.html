[3] It is permitted, etc. — This applies when the tefillin were removed; but while the tefillin are on them it is forbidden — so Nimukey Yosef wrote there. Magen Avraham wrote: it seems to me that a minor who wrapped tefillin in it is forbidden; and it is obvious that a person cannot forbid what is not his. If they stole a garment and made a tefillin pouch from it, they acquire it through change of form — so far. He wrote in the responsum of Shaar Ephraim: a pouch in which he wrapped money — it is forbidden to wrap tefillin in it; but if he makes below, in the place of placing tefillin inside the pouch, something new, it is permitted — so far. See below siman 144 and end of siman 153 on this.
