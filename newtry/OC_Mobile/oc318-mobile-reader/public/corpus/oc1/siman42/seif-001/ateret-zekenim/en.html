And if they were new — Rashi explains; Beit Yosef brought from this that even if he only made the knots they are called "old" (used), and this is correct: because the reason is that head tefillin are holy with a stricter sanctity, since most of the Name Shaddai is in them. But in Rashi as it appears in the Alfasi it is written in different language on this matter. However, if he stipulated on them from the outset when tying them on the head that he would make them hand tefillin when he needs them, it is permitted (Rabbeinu Yeruham and R. Yitzchak Abuhab in the name of Or Zaru'a).
