(1) Hand — even if he has no hand tefillin and has two head tefillin.

(2) Strap — and it is obvious that the same applies to the case specifically for head tefillin, for it too is included among sacred utensils like a strap [as stated in Megillah 26b], and so is proven in the Gra's commentary, and likewise the Magen Avraham above at the end of siman 28.

(3) From them — the Magen Avraham wrote that if a hand tefillin strap broke near the knot, it is forbidden to turn the other end upward [where it is strong] and make the hand tefillin knot with it, and the piece that had the knot should now be tied to this one below, for one lowers its sanctity since it had the knot and the yud, and now he will make the finger wrappings with it; rather that piece requires genizah. And if the strap is not so long, then he should not make such wrappings around the hand. And likewise with head tefillin: if what encircles the head broke, it is forbidden to reverse what is inside the knot so that it would be outside the knot, for the reason stated above. However, if the head tefillin strap broke outside the knot and he wants to pull it and lower it so that the strap will be complete in the measure explained above in siman 33 — some say he may lower it even though he lowers it somewhat from its sanctity, for the place of the knot will hang below; this is not considered lowering, for in any case that piece will have the knot. And some say that this too is not correct, for in any case there is some lowering of sanctity. And according to their words, the same applies to hand tefillin: if the hand strap broke near the knot, even if he does not want to tie the severed piece below, nevertheless he should not turn the bottom of the strap upward to make the knot with it, for thereby he lowers the upper part of the strap somewhat from its sanctity, since it was originally close to the knot; therefore one should be stringent not to reverse except in a time of pressing need when he has no other strap, and where it broke it is soft and weak and not fit to make the arm binding from it so that it would be close to breaking — then one may reverse. And see Biur Halacha.

(4) Of head tefillin — for there is the shin and the dalet, unlike hand tefillin in which there is only the knot of the yud.

(5) Permitted — that is, he should make four compartments for it and place each parasha in its compartment; and likewise it is permitted to take the parshiyot and straps from hand tefillin for head tefillin.

(6) He had not put them on — on his head, even though he designated and prepared them for the sake of the head; designation is not significant.

(7) As one compartment — and even though they are written on four parchments and placed in four compartments inside, this is of no consequence b'dieved, as written in siman 32, seif 67; see there, and see in Biur Halacha.
