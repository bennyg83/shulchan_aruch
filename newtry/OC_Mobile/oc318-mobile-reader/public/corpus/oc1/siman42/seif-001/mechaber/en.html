Whether it is permitted to change hand tefillin into head tefillin. And it contains 3 seifim: It is
forbidden to change head tefillin to make them into hand tefillin, and even to take a strap from them and place it on hand tefillin is forbidden, because one may not lower from a more stringent sanctity to a lesser sanctity, and head tefillin have a more stringent sanctity because most of the Name Shaddai is on head tefillin. But from hand tefillin to head tefillin it is permitted to change. And if they were new and he had not yet put them on, it is permitted to change them, even from head tefillin to hand tefillin, for he places upon them a single leather covering and they appear as one compartment.':
