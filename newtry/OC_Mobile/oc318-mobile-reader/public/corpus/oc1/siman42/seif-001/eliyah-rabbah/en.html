(1) From arm tefillin into head tefillin, etc. This means that he makes four compartments and inserts each passage into a separate compartment. Otherwise the head tefillin are invalid, because if the divisions are not externally recognizable they are invalid. Kesef Mishneh, chapter 3 of Hilchos Tefillin, and Sefer Torah. I heard in the name of my grandfather, the gaon, that one may reverse a tefillin strap and place the lower end above inside the passageway. He brought a proof from Rashi on the verse in parashas Vayeitzei: “The righteous man will place his head upon me.” The Holy One, blessed is He, made the stones into one stone. Yet the jealousy would still remain as to which side should be placed above. Evidently, no significance attaches to direction. See above, siman 10, where my discussion of making a tallis somewhat implies otherwise. After Magen Avraham was printed, I saw that he writes that if the strap of the arm tefillin snaps near the knot, one may not reverse the other end upward and make the knot there while tying below the piece that formerly held the knot. This would lower it from its sanctity. If the strap is not long enough, he should make fewer wraps around the arm.
