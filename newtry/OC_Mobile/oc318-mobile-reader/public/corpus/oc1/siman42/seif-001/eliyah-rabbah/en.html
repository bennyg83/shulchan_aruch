[1] From hand to head, etc. — Meaning that he made four compartments and placed all parshiyot in one compartment; for otherwise it is invalid, for if the grooves are not recognizable it is invalid — thus Kesef Mishneh, chapter 3 [Laws of Tefillin] and Sefer Torah. I heard in the name of my ancestor the Gaon, may his righteous memory be blessed, that it is permitted to reverse a tefillin strap and place the lower end upward on the batim; and he brought proof from Rashi’s commentary on Parashat Vayeitzei: “This one says, ‘Upon me shall the righteous one rest his head’” — God made them one stone, and still there is jealousy as to which side the stone will rest, but there is no strictness about this. See above siman 10 (seif 29) [seif katan 14] what I wrote about making a tallit, that it does not quite imply thus. Again the book Magen Avraham was printed, and I saw that he wrote: if a hand strap broke near the knot and one wishes to turn the other head upward and make the hand knot with it, and tie the piece that had the knot below — it is forbidden, for he lowers it from its kedushah; and if the strap is not so long, then he should not make so many wrappings around the hand — so far.
