Arm tefillin. This applies even if he has no arm tefillin. This is implied by the Gemara. Magen Avraham.
From them. If the strap of the arm tefillin snapped near the knot, it is forbidden to turn the other end upward and make the arm-tefillin knot there, while tying below the end that formerly held the knot. This would lower it from its sanctity, since it formerly held the knot and the yod, whereas now it would be used for wrapping around the fingers. If the strap is not long enough, he should instead make fewer wraps around the arm. Magen Avraham. Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 21, permits it. See there.
