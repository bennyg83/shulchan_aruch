Hand (tefillin). Even if he has no hand tefillin — as explained in the Gemara. Magen Avraham.

From them. If the hand strap broke near the knot, it is forbidden to turn the other head (end) upward and make a hand knot with it, and to tie the lower part that had the knot downward — for we do not lower from its sanctity, since it had the knot and the yud; now he would make the finger wrappings on it. And if the strap is not so long, he should not make so many wrappings around the hand — Magen Avraham. And in Halakhot Ketanot part 2 siman 21, to permit — as cited.

Permitted. That is, when he will make for it four compartments — or it refers to a strap.

New ones, etc. For designation (hazmanah) is not a significant matter.
