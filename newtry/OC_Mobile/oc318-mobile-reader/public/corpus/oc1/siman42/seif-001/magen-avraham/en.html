Forbidden to change — even if he has no shel yad [as stated in the Gemara].

From hand to head, etc. — meaning he should make four compartments, as written in siman 32, seif 47.

New ones, etc. — for designation does not count. If a shel yad strap broke near the knot — uncertain whether it is permitted to turn the other head upward and make the hand knot with it, and tie the piece that had the knot below, as written in siman 33; or perhaps we say he lowers it from its kedushah, since it had the knot and the yod, and now he will make the finger wrappings on it. Rabbenu Yonah wrote in siman 33 in the name of the Geonim: if a head strap broke, sew them and turn the strap inside the ma’avarta — so far. If so, where the knot was at first now hangs below, and nevertheless it is permitted — so too here. Nevertheless, one may say it is not comparable, for there at least in that piece of strap there is a knot — what difference does it make whether it was in this place or that? Not so here, where in that piece that had a knot there will now be no knot. Greater than this, Tosafot wrote on Shabbos, page 98: when placing planks on the wagons, it is degrading to place the thick side of a plank near the ground by the short side that was above; and it is comparable to “we elevate in holiness and do not lower” — so far. If so, all the more so here it is forbidden, as above. And if the strap is not so long, then he should not make so many wrappings around the hand, as above.
