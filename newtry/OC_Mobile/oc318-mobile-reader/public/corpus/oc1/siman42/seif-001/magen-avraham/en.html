New, etc. Designation alone is ineffective. If the strap of the arm tefillin snapped near the knot, it requires study whether one may reverse the other end upward, make the arm-tefillin knot there, and attach below the piece that formerly held the knot, as explained at the end of siman 33. Perhaps this lowers it from its sanctity, since it formerly held the knot and the yod, whereas now it would be used for wrapping around the fingers. Beit Yosef cites Hagahos Maimoniyos in siman 33 that if the head-tefillin strap snapped, it may be sewn and the strap turned within the passageway. The section that formerly held the knot would then hang below, yet it is permitted. Our case may be different. There, the same section still contains a knot; it merely changes location. Here, the section that formerly held the knot would no longer hold any knot. Tosafos in Shabbos 98 writes an even broader principle. When placing the Mishkan boards upon the wagons, it was degrading to place the thick lower side of one board beside the thin upper side of another. This resembles the rule that one ascends in sanctity and does not descend. All the more so here, reversal should be forbidden. If the strap is not long enough, he should make fewer wraps around the arm.
