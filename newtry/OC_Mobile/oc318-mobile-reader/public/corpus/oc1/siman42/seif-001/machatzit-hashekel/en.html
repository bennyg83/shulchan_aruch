(Seif katan 1) Forbidden, etc. — even if he said, etc. As stated in the Gemara in Menachot, page 34: a baraita teaches — if he has no shel yad and has two shel rosh, he hangs skin over one of them and dons it. The Gemara objects: but Rabbenu Tam sent in the name of Rabbenu Yitzchak, “tefillah,” etc., and one does not make shel rosh into shel yad, because we do not lower from severe kedushah to minor kedushah. It is not difficult: this refers to old ones, and that to new ones — so too what is mentioned in Shulchan Aruch here. And what is the difficulty? Behold, the baraita plainly deals where he has no shel yad; therefore he may lower shel rosh. Or the Gemara holds: wherever lowering from kedushah does not help — that he has no shel yad — he does not bring from Rabbenu Yitzchak, only that from shel rosh to shel yad is called lowering.
