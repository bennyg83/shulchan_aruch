Magen Avraham, note 3: he should tie below. In my humble opinion, without this it is forbidden, for what is written in siman 33 that one may sew what remains in the measure after stretching it over the middle finger — the meaning is that the wrappings on the finger should be made from the piece that has the knot for the yud, and afterward he returns with the other strap to encircle the arm. But if he encircles the arm first and afterward on the finger — what benefit is the measure that remains in it? And if so, here if he makes with this the arm encirclement, in which there is no element of obligation at all, it is certainly forbidden; and it requires further study.
