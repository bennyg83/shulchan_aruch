Seif 3 in the gloss: Parchment processed for tefillin — it is forbidden to write mundane words on it, for designation for the body of kedushah counts. NB: See in Magen Avraham, who wrote that this means the parchment of the titzah on the shin. And apparently it is difficult: if so, why did Shulchan Aruch write in seif 1 that if they were new and he had not yet donned them, it is permitted to make from head to hand — namely, because whatever he had not donned was only designation that does not count; yet in the titzah, designation counts? It resolves well: the main reason designation for the body of kedushah counts was because it requires processing leshemah; therefore designation counted. If so, it works out if one wishes to make mundane use of it — for since it was processed leshemah, he cannot make mundane use of it. But if he wishes to change from severe kedushah to minor kedushah, since he need not declare at the time of processing for the name of severe kedushah, again it is designation that does not count, since here he does not need processing for severe kedushah. Therefore Shulchan Aruch deals with changing from head to hand — for here he does not need intent for the head compartment; therefore it is permitted to change. But in the gloss it deals with actual mundane need — for here, since it requires processing leshemah, he cannot change to mundane use; and this resolves well. I later found the same in Magen Avraham itself above, siman 32, who wrote thus — and it pleases me. From this I learned the law in what I was asked: he placed head and hand parshiyot of Rashi inside Rashi batim, and afterward reconsidered to make them Rabbeinu Tam tefillin; I was asked whether it is permitted to change them, since from the outset he placed Rashi parshiyot there. I answered that apparently it seems permitted, for from what is written here, that if they were new and he had not donned them, it is permitted to make head into hand — which implies that even though he already placed the parshiyot there, only that he had not donned them, it is permitted to make head into hand; for this is considered only designation, and it is not similar to what we say about a pouch that he wrapped and designated, where it is forbidden to change — for there it is a finished act with no act after it, and therefore it is a [finished] act and forbidden to change. But here the main act is donning on the head; therefore placing parshiyot inside the batim is not a finished act — therefore it is only like designation that does not count; if so, here too it is permitted to change from Rashi to Rabbeinu Tam. But after reconsideration I say it is forbidden, since we proved from Rama’s words that for actual mundane need it is forbidden to change, since it requires processing leshemah. If so, for hand tefillin, which were certainly kedushah, only light kedushah — in this it is permitted to change, since he does not need processing for the severe. But for Rabbeinu Tam, since there is a dispute, and per poskim who hold like Rashi, Rabbeinu Tam tefillin are not tefillin at all and are to them like actual mundane matter — and for mundane matter it is forbidden to change — therefore for those poskim it was like actual mundane matter, and one must be concerned for stringency; therefore, since they were placed for Rashi, it was at least like designation, and designation for the body of kedushah counts, since the change would be for mundane use in their view; one must be concerned for stringency and it is forbidden to change. Perhaps even with intent alone to be Rashi tefillin it is forbidden to change to Rabbeinu Tam, as I explained — correct; examine.
