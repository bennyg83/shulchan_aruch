And if the Taz brought that by default it is as his explanation, since people practice thus — see Even HaEzer 124 in Choshen Mishpat and Beit Shmuel sections 5 and 8; and Eliyah Rabbah here — the intent is not parchment prepared for a Sefer Torah and processed for a Sefer Torah, but rather it was prepared like Sefer Torah parchment. And Alfasi wrote that processing is not required le-shem gittin; see there. And Tosafos wrote that a get has some sanctity, and to lower to lighter sanctity is permitted, but specifically to mundane use is forbidden; see in the Magen Avraham in Even HaEzer. From this I have some inquiry in what the Mechaber and Mishna Berurah wrote that a condition helps while worn and likewise for mundane matters — both learned only from Menachot 34, that per the view of the one who says designation is significant with a condition — perhaps for lighter sanctity yes, for mundane no; and with designation a condition helps, but when he performed an act it is worse. Requires further study; I again saw in Tzemach Tzedek siman 81 that he noted this.

And behold, in Tzemach Tzedek siman 81 it is written: leather processed for the sake of straps — does a condition help to use for mundane purposes? The Tzemach Tzedek wrote: First, that even regarding sanctity itself a condition helps, unlike worn clothing that he placed in Tzemach Tzedek; and further, even to wear — straps are sacred utensils, and he brought proof from a case, see there. And even though a case is only designation by mouth — nevertheless, since he wrapped afterward, it is an act and a condition helps, all the more so in processing such as this. And see further on this in the Magen Avraham in Even HaEzer; and see Tosafos Megillah 26b, s.v. ve-tashmishei, for Rashi's explanation — dalet and yud imply that straps are sanctified; and that which it says straps are sacred utensils means what hangs outside the knot. Requires further study.

And know that the matter that one may not lower from stringent to light sanctity — there is room to question whether it is d'oraisa or d'rabbanan; and see Rashi Megillah 26a on the Mishnah that brought a Tosefta "for they were consecrated," which implies it is d'oraisa, and so it seems. And also, even though it is not fit for stringent sanctity — nevertheless one may not lower to light sanctity, for we learned in Menachot 32, and likewise ruled the Rambam, chapter 5 of Laws of Tefillin: a worn Sefer Torah — one does not make a mezuzah from it; thus even though it is not now fit for a Sefer Torah, nevertheless one may not lower it, etc.
