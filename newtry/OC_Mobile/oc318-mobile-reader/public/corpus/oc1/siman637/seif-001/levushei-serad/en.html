Taz, small paragraph 1 Like that phrase, "it shall not be found," etc. What he writes afterward belongs here: "and likewise, seven days you shall eat matzot," etc., until "for this is not an obligation except on the first night." Meaning: there is no obligation to eat matzah except on the first night. If so, the verse's intent must be that one should not eat chametz, but matzah, when he wishes to eat. Since it speaks specifically about the prohibition of chametz, we explain the verse according to its plain meaning, that it refers to all six days. This is unlike "the festival of Sukkot," where this is impossible, etc.
