Seif 1: Whether, etc. The Yerushalmi there teaches in the name of R. Eliezer: One who did not make his sukkah on the eve of the festival should not make it on the festival. But was it not taught in a baraita: if his sukkah fell on the festival, he may set it up on the festival? They answer: R. Eliezer imposed a penalty because he did not make his sukkah before the festival. Thus they disagree regarding one who acted intentionally. Although this is not implied in our Gemara, nevertheless in practice the rabbis do not disagree with the Yerushalmi, for the rabbis permit in every case.
Even at the end, etc. As written there: and the rabbis say this is what the Merciful One said, etc. See Taz, and see Tosafot there, s.v. "all," etc.
