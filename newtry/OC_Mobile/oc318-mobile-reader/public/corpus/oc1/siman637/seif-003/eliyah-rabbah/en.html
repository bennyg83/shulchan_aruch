[3] Without his knowledge, etc. The Taz wrote that it is permitted to enter, as with lulav; see siman 649, because a person is pleased that a mitzvah be done with his property. This applies specifically when the owner of the sukkah is not there, but when he is there he objects. End quote. In my humble opinion, lulav is different, for one needs it only for a moment, unlike a sukkah, where he objects constantly, because he is embarrassed to enter when someone else is there. A similar case appears in Bava Metzia 22 regarding "go to the better ones."
