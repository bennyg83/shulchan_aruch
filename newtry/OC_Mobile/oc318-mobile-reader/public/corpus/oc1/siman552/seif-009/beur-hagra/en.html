Se'if 9. And the custom, etc. Somewhat implied in the aforementioned Yerushalmi, "Rav when," etc., that "this is the main," etc.
And they are accustomed, etc. As the Rosh wrote in the last chapter of Yoma the reason regarding eating on erev Yom Kippur; see there in se'if katan 22.
And there is some, etc. For it said there: "Those who sit in the gate talk about me, and the songs of drinkers of beer"; these are Israel who eat and drink and become drunk on erev Tisha B'Av, and afterward read Eichah (see Darkei Moshe).
However, etc. As written in the Gemara; and so it should say in se'if 6.
