All this applies to the seudah hamafseket when one does not intend to eat another fixed meal afterward and the meal is after halachic noon. But if it is before halachic noon, or it is after halachic noon and he intends to eat another fixed meal afterward, he need not observe these restrictions. {Rama: The custom throughout the regions of Ashkenaz is to eat a fixed meal before Minchah, then pray Minchah, and afterward eat the seudah hamafseket. [Maharil] The custom is to increase somewhat at the first meal so that the fast will not harm them, since they stop eating while it is still day, as on Yom Kippur. There is some support for this from Midrash Eichah Rabbati. Nevertheless, one who is able to afflict himself, knows that the fast will not harm him, and is stringent upon himself is called holy, as appears to me.}
