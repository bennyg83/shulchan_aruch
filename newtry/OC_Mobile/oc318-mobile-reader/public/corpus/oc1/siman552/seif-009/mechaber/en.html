And all this applies at a meal that interrupts, where one does not intend to eat more afterward in a set meal, and when eaten after halachic noon. But if before noon, or after noon with intention to eat a set meal afterward — one need not be careful in these matters.The custom in all Ashkenaz provinces is to eat a set meal before Mincha, pray Mincha afterward, then eat the interrupting meal [Maharil]. We are accustomed to add) somewhat to the first meal so the fast not harm them, since we stop eating while still day as on Yom Kippur; and there is slight proof from Midrash Eichah Rabbati. However one who can afflict himself and knows the fast will not harm him and is strict on himself is called kadosh, as above.:
