(s.k. 9) That not, etc. They should not invite [to zimmun], etc.; and see in the Tur and Beis Yosef. He means that the Tur wrote on this law, and these are his words: And I wonder, for even a mourner is obligated in zimmun. And on this Magen Avraham wrote: And it appears to me the reason is that it is not considered, etc. See siman 193, end of se'if 3. He brings a proof that even though they sit together, nevertheless sometimes we say that their intention was not to fix together, and it is not called a fixed sitting, and they do not form a zimmun; for there the Rama wrote that the custom is not to form a zimmun in a non-Jew's house, because they cannot fix themselves in a non-Jew's house out of fear of the non-Jew, and it is as if they ate without fixing, end quote. And the same applies here: since it is not proper from the law to fix together, if so even if they fixed it is not called a fixed sitting.
