(9) Ibid. And one should not eat two cooked dishes. The Zera Avraham in his responsa on Orach Chayyim siman 78 wrote that it is forbidden to drink coffee or tea at the seudah ha'mafsekes, because it is considered a cooked dish and is forbidden on account of two cooked dishes, unless it is a case of illness, see there. However, the Machberes os 2 wrote to prove that there is no issue of a cooked dish in this, and also there is no element of luxury or delight in coffee, for recently it has become extremely widespread and even the poor are accustomed to it. But water cooked with cane and coriander seed with sugar, which is a delight and the practice of the wealthy, that is not permitted except for illness, for one who has a stomach complaint, as written by Maharash Sha'ar Aryeh, see there. The Zechor L'Avraham os 9 and the Sha'arei Teshuvah cited his words.
