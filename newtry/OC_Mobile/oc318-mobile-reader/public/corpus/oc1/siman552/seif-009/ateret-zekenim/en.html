And it is customary to increase a bit in the meal, etc. And some say it is proper to be careful not to increase in the meal after midday; and those who eat delicacies on erev Tisha B'Av and several kinds of fried food certainly have them in the category of "the belly of the wicked," and their iniquity is too great to bear (Maharshal in Responsa, siman 30). Nevertheless, one who does not wish to change the custom, as the Rama wrote in the gloss, should increase in the meal that is before midday (my teacher in the Bach).
