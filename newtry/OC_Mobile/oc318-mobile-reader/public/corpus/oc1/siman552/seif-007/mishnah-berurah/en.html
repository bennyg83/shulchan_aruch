(17) On the ground. So that the meal be lowly; and in a place where their custom is to eat reclining, the distinction would be if they now ate without reclining; and for us who all year eat without reclining, the lowliness is recognized only in this; nevertheless if he is a weak person he may place a cushion under himself.
(18) And he need not remove. For mourning does not apply until night; and for this reason it is permitted to sit after the meal on the bench.
