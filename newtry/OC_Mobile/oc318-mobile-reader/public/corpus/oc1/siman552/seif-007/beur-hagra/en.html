Se'if 7. They practiced, etc. Like R' Yehudah bar Elai, etc., a lowly place.
And one need not, etc. For one need not yet practice the laws of Tisha B'Av; and Terumat HaDeshen wrote that this meal is in place of aninut, as written there, and it is like one whose dead lies, etc.; and Tisha B'Av is in place of mourning; and regarding an onen we say he does not recline and eat, and he does not eat meat and does not drink wine; and we who are not accustomed to reclining, the lowliness of the meal is recognized only on the ground; and as written: But all the while that his dead lies before him he does not sleep neither on an upright bed nor on an overturned bed; and he explained in Terumat HaDeshen rather he sleeps on the ground; and see Yoreh De'ah, siman 341, se'if 5, and Tosafos there s.v. "even though." But removing shoes: even an onen is not obligated; and see Tosafos, first chapter of Kesubos. And even according to the Ramban's explanation, what is written there: anything that is because of Tisha B'Av, etc., and it is forbidden to wash; and in the Tosefta: and it is forbidden to wash and anoint; meaning at the seudah hamafseket, for from then onward washing and anointing are forbidden; and he explained like the first Tanna; nevertheless he agrees regarding other things that they are permitted, as written there: specifically washing and anointing, for that is a pleasure that is after a while, and it appears as if he washes for the pleasure of Tisha B'Av; therefore only washing and anointing were mentioned; but other things such as wearing a sandal and the like are permitted; and all the more so according to the Rosh who disagrees with the Ramban; and see above siman 553, se'if 2 in the gloss.
