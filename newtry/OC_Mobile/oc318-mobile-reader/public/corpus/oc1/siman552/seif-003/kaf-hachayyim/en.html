One should not eat meat, etc., this is by the strict law of the Talmud, whereas from the standpoint of custom even beforehand it is forbidden, as stated above in siman 551 seif 9, see there. The practical difference is that even in a place where they did not adopt the custom of prohibition during the aforementioned days, at the seudah ha'mafsekes it is forbidden by the strict law of the Talmud. So too wrote the Levush.
