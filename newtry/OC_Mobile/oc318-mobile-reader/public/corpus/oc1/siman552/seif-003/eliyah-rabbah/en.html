[3] Whose manner is thus, etc. Meaning that most times; but fish it is forbidden to fry with eggs, since most times they cook them without eggs (Magen Avraham). And it is clarified in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah that even though the manner of other places is not thus, it is permitted for one whose manner is thus; lebibos or macaroni in the vernacular cooked with cheese are called two cooked dishes (Shibbolei HaLeket and Tanya).
