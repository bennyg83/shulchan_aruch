In two pots. Meaning that one has a thick batter and one has a thin batter; but if both are equal, there is no insistence on cooking in two pots.
Whose manner is thus. It appears to me that most times they put onions and eggs in peas, as is the manner to fry dough with eggs; but fish it is forbidden to fry with eggs, since most times they cook them without eggs, as written in the Tur, and everything similar; and Shelah wrote it is forbidden to make lebibos filled with cheese.
