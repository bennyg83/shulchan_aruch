[6] [Levush] And he should reduce, etc. That if he was accustomed to drink four cups he should drink three. It is forbidden to drink beer (Agudah); and Maharshal ruled in siman 31 that there is no permission except for water, unless for a person who was not accustomed to drink anything but beer, or a weak person; and in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah he lengthened and was stringent even in this; he also inferred that since the Rama in siman 551, se'if 11 needed to teach us that beer is permitted from Rosh Chodesh onward, it implies that he forbids it at the seudah hamafseket, up to here. And in my humble opinion that is no proof, for this is the Rama's language there: it is permitted to drink all beer even of honey, end of his words; behold his main point even regarding choice beer like mead teaches us that it is permitted; and know that in the Levush beer is not mentioned there at all; and I also saw in Darkei Moshe that they are not accustomed like the Agudah; therefore it appears to me to be lenient like Maharshal, but regarding mead to forbid it at the seudah hamafseket; and the Taz forbids even distilled spirits; and here in Prague one should not be lenient except with white beer; and it is obvious that the cup of Birkas HaMazon is permitted even according to Maharshal.
