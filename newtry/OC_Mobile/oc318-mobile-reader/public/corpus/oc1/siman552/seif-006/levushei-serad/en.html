Magen Avraham, s.k. 17. Siman 562, se'if 2, etc. See there in Magen Avraham, s.k. 5, that he should delay until close to sunset, so that there be some completion; and since on erev Tisha B'Av it is impossible to time so precisely as to delay the eating too much, therefore it is better that he seek annulment.
