(6) There. And he will not drink wine — and the same for beer of the guild; they do not practice thus. Darkei Moshe wrote: 3. Indeed Rashal in responsa siman 31 wrote to forbid per the guild because it is a custom of the place, but wrote all this is for one who is able; one not accustomed to drink except beer, or if weak, is surely permitted to drink beer even at the seudah hamafseket — see there. Bach in Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tosefta. Taz at the beginning of the siman. Eliyah Rabbah in Hagahot Tosefta. However Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tosefta wrote to be stringent even for the weak, lest if he permitted most, all would make themselves weak to drink beer — see there; nevertheless Eliyah Rabbah there doubted this and inclined to leniency like Rashal — see there. Therefore everything depends on the person; and even where there is great need, one should not permit more than a little so he can eat the meal and no more, because it intoxicates and we fear diversion from sorrow over the destruction.
