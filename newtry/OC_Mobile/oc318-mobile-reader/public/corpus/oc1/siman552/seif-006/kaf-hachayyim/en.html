(6) Ibid. And one should not drink wine. The same applies to beer, Agudah. But the custom is not to follow this. Darkei Moshe os 3. However, Maharshal in his responsa siman 31 wrote to forbid it in accordance with the Agudah, because it is considered the "wine of the land" [chamar medinah]; but he wrote that all this applies to one for whom it is possible, but one who is accustomed to drinking only beer, or if he is a weak person, is certainly permitted to drink beer even at the seudah ha'mafsekes, see there. The Knesset HaGedolah cited his words in the glosses. Taz at the beginning of the siman, Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Eliyah Rabbah os 6, Yad Aharon in the glosses. However, the Knesset HaGedolah in the glosses os 1 wrote to be strict even for a weak person, because if he were to permit it most people would make themselves out to be weak in order to drink beer, see there. However, the Eliyah Rabbah there wrote to question the words of the Knesset HaGedolah, and his view is to be lenient like the Maharshal, see there. Therefore it seems that it all depends on the person, and even where there is great need one should not permit drinking more than a small amount, enough to be able to eat the meal and no more, since it is an intoxicating substance and we are concerned that he will come to divert his attention from the grief over the destruction.
