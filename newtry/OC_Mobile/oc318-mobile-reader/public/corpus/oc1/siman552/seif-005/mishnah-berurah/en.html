(13) Cooked in them. For it is considered one cooked dish; and it deals with when he eats them at once. And specifically in a place where that is his manner to cook thus all year, as above in se'if 3; and in the Tur he wrote lentils or eggs; and so wrote the Levush; and so is the custom among us that they eat eggs alone because of mourning, as written in the gloss.
(14) For they are foods of mourners. And they should not eat another cooked dish; and not like the masses who at first eat a cooked kind and afterward sit on the ground and eat eggs, which is forbidden; and all the more so it is forbidden to interrupt between them with Birkas HaMazon, for it causes an unnecessary blessing; and in se'if 9 it will be clarified how to conduct oneself in this.
