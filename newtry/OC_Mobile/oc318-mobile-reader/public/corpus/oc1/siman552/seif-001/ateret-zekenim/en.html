And even other beverages, etc. Maharshal wrote in Responsa, siman 31, in the name of the Agudah, that one should not drink beer at the seudah hamafseket; see there. But we are not accustomed to this. All of this applies to one who is able; but one who is accustomed to drink beer, or who is a weak person, is permitted to drink beer (Maharshal).
