(In Shulchan Aruch seif 1 in the gloss) And likewise he shall not eat, etc. He wrote in Knesset HaGedolah that in a responsum he forbade eating gourds pickled in water and salt or vinegar, and brought it in Eliyah Rabbah; see Mishna Berurah that Zera Emes wrote one should not drink coffee or tea at the seudah hamafseket except when ill; Mishna Berurah wrote the widespread custom in Israel, Egypt, and all lands is to drink coffee even for the healthy. I said to judge favorably our holy rabbis, etc., that eating two dishes alone is forbidden and not drinking; he elaborated: a beverage they heated or boiled is not called a dish, as in Nedarim 49b regarding one who vows not to eat cooked food; also coffee is not luxury and pleasure that spread greatly — even the poor are accustomed; but water boiled with cinnamon and gader with sugar — pleasure and custom of the rich — is not permitted except for stomach illness. See there in name of Zera Emes that putting unripe fruit or vinegar into a dish at the seudah hamafseket is surely permitted — see there.
