(1) He should not eat, etc. In order to increase mourning and to remember the destruction of the Temple and grieve over it.
(2) That he eats after midday. See below in se'if 9.
(3) Meat, etc. That is by the law of the Talmud; for from the custom it is forbidden from Rosh Chodesh, as above in siman 551.
(4) He reduces. That is at the seudah hamafseket: if he is accustomed to drink four cups at the meal, he should drink three cups. And one should not drink beer; and if he is a weak person it is permitted.
