(1) At the seudah hamafseket that he eats, etc. As written above in se'if 9.
And even from beverages, etc. As written there: he was accustomed to drink ten cups, etc.; even though Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel comes to be lenient, and regarding wine; nevertheless the same applies to another thing, as written: he was accustomed to feast with ten people, etc. And even though the halachah is not like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, for we hold like the Sages of the mishnah, and the sugya of the beraisos and Gemara is like the Sages; nevertheless in this we do not find that they disagree with him. So wrote the poskim.
And likewise not, etc. Meaning: even though by law it is permitted, as written there, if he was accustomed to eat, etc., nevertheless what he can reduce is preferable, as written regarding R' Yehudah bar Elai; and these are the words of the Rambam: This is the measure of every person who cannot bear more than that; but, etc., dry bread, etc.; like this it is proper for Sages to do, or close to this, etc.
