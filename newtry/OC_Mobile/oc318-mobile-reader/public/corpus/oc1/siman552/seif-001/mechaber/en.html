דין ערב ט' באב. ובו יב סעיפים:
1. Laws of the Eve of Tisha B'av containing 12 paragraphs. On the Eve of Tisha B'av in the Parting Meal-- that is eaten after Halachic Noon-- a person should not eat meat and should not drink wine, and he should not eat two cooked dishes.Rema: Even other drinks, one should reduce the amount he normally drinks [Tur and Bais Yosef in the name of Tosafos and Rokeach] and similarly, he should not eat after the Meal radishes and salted food that he is used to on other occasions in order to accustom himself in asceticism.
