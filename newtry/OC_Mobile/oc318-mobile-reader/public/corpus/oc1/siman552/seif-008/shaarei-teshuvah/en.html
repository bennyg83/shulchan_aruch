Ground. Ba'er Heitev wrote in the name of the Maharash She'er Ari that one must interpose a garment between oneself and the ground per Kabbalah, for one may not sit on the ground even with one's garments except a garment not designated for wearing — see there. From the words of the Maharil that Magen Avraham brought in siman 559, who wrote that he had a worn-out cloak so as not to ruin his garments — it is clear he was not concerned for this; nevertheless, since it is permitted to sit on sackcloth, one may do so to heed the Kabbalistic words of the Maharash.
