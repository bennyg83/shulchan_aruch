Se'if 8. One should be careful, etc. Because many poskim wrote that even if three ate together they should not form a zimmun; and therefore ideally one should be careful not to come to that. Tur. And in Hagahos Maimoniyos and in the Yerushalmi we recite: And if he wishes to change: if he was accustomed to feast among people, he feasts alone, as it is said, "He sits alone and is silent"; see there. And the reason of the poskim that they should not form a zimmun together even if they became obligated is because we say: if they sat, each blesses for himself; if they reclined, etc.; and here reclining is forbidden, etc.; and even for us fixed sitting is required in the manner of reclining; and all this is forbidden, as above.
