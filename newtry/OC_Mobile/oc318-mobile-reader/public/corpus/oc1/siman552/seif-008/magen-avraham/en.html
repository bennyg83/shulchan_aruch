That three should not sit. If it happened that three ate together, they should not form a zimmun (so wrote in the Minhagim and Maharil; and see in the Tur and Beis Yosef); and it appears to me the reason is that it is not considered a fixed sitting unless they reclined or sat at one table, as written in siman 167, se'if 1; and here their intention is not to fix; see siman 193, end of se'if 3.
