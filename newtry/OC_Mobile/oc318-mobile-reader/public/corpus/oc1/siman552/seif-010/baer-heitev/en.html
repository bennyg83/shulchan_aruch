Meat. And it is forbidden to withhold from him; even though there is no obligation to eat meat on Shabbos, nevertheless since he withholds because of mourning it is a sin; nevertheless he should sit in sorrow of soul; he should not sit at a companions' meal; and if a circumcision falls on that Shabbos, he should make the meal before Minchah (Magen Avraham).
To interrupt. The Baer Heitev that is before [us] wrote, and this requires further study in Rashi, Taanis folio 29, who wrote that he need not interrupt, end quote; see there. But he was not precise, for what Rashi wrote there, "he does not interrupt his meal," etc., means that he does not eat a seudah hamafseket; rather it is permitted to eat meat and drink wine, for at the seudah hamafseket if it falls on a weekday one may not eat meat or drink wine, but on Shabbos it is not so, and this is clear; but in any case he must stop eating and drinking while it is still day, and this is obvious.
