(23) He eats meat. And it is forbidden to withhold from him; even though there is no obligation to eat meat on Shabbos, nevertheless since he withholds because of mourning it is a sin. And behold even though the Mechaber wrote "like Solomon's feast," nevertheless he should sit in sorrow of soul that he not conduct himself with joy; and therefore he should not sit at a companions' meal [Magen Avraham]. In Sefer Bechor Shor he disagrees with this, and his view is that one who every Shabbos is accustomed to feast this meal with his companions and acquaintances, and withholds on this Shabbos, it is like public mourning. And according to everyone it is permitted to eat with his household, and he may bless with zimmun since it is Shabbos [Eliyah Rabbah]; and if a circumcision falls on that Shabbos he should make the meal before Minchah.
(24) While it is still day. That is before sunset. And it is proper to inform the masses of this so that they not err that it is like any other Shabbos.
