10) Two cooked dishes that they forbade at the seudah ha-mafseket: there is no distinction between something eaten as it is raw and something not eaten as it is raw; likewise there is no distinction between roasted and cooked, and likewise no distinction whether he eats them in the manner of a cooked dish or in the manner of fruits as dessert at the meal. Rather, in every case it is forbidden to eat roasted fruits as dessert at the seudah ha-mafseket. The Rav Maharam ibn Chaviv, author of Get Pashut, in manuscript responsum siman 12; Birkei Yosef ot 12; Zekher LeAvraham there; Shaarei Teshuvah ot 3. Even if he cooked dates and made them into honey, or cooked sugar and made it into a drink, it is also considered a cooked dish. Ben Ish Chai, Parashat Devarim ot 19. Likewise, if he cooked cheese, it has the law of a cooked dish and it is forbidden to eat it with another cooked dish. Tur; Levush; Ben Ish Chai there. Likewise, if he cooked milk, it has the law of a cooked dish. Levush. See below, ot 13.
