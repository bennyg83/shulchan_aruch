And fish. Nachalas Tzvi wrote that it is a scribal error and one should say "and kids," and it is forced to say that one error fell in all the books of the Beis Yosef and the Shulchan Aruch and the Hagahos Asheri; and further, for behold the Hagahos Asheri wrote that by law it is permitted, and if you should think that it deals with kids, why would it be permitted, for it is meat according to everyone? Therefore it appears to me that the reading "fish" is the main one, because there is a place where fish are included in meat, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 217, se'if 8; and further, for it ascends upon a king's table and it is joy to a person; and see what I wrote in siman 597.
