(5) Even salted meat, etc., and poultry. Meaning: even though the reason for the prohibition of meat is that the offerings were nullified, and after three days it is no longer called meat regarding consecrated things, for shelamim are not eaten more than two days and one night; and likewise birds were not offered upon the altar except turtledoves and young pigeons; and also there is joy only in animal meat; nevertheless they practiced to forbid.
(6) And fish. Because there is a place where fish are included in meat, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 217, se'if 8; and further, for it ascends upon a king's table and it is joy to a person in its eating [Magen Avraham].
(7) And wine from its press, etc. For it is not significant and there is no joy in it.
