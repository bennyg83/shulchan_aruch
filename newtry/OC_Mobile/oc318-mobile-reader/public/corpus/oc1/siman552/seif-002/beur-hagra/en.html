Se'if 2. Even meat, etc. Meaning that by law it is permitted, as written there; nevertheless it is forbidden because of custom.
And poultry meat. For there is no joy in it, as written in the first chapter of Chagigah 8b: Rav Ashi said, it emerges from "and you shall rejoice," etc.; and we say in Sanhedrin 70a: there it is because of joy, etc.
And fish. So wrote in the Hagahos Asheri; and so it is in Rashi there, s.v. "salted," etc.; and Rashi's words require further study. And it is not so, for what Rashi wrote "after it has aged," etc., does not refer to fish.
