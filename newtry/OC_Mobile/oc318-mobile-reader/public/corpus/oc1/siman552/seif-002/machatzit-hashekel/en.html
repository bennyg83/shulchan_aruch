(s.k. 2) And fish, etc. As written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 217, that if he forbade meat upon himself without specification, and he is in a place where they call fish "meat," fish too are forbidden. And see what I wrote in siman 597: that Maharshal did not eat fish on Rosh Hashanah, for they were very dear to him, and from awe of the Day of Judgment he withheld his desire from something important to him. This implies somewhat that there are people for whom fish are more important than meat, for he did not withhold himself from meat.
