11. One who accepted upon himself to fast every Monday and Thursday all the days of the year, and it happens that the eve of Tisha B'av is on Monday: He should ask upon his vow (i.e. to nullify it) or borrow his fast and pay it back (i.e. fast another day instead).Rema: The Gaonim write that he should fast until after the Mincha Prayers and eat the Parting Meal before the setting of the sun (Hagahot Ashiri and Mordechai and Terumat Hadeshen Chapter 75), and so we are accustomed. And the same rule applies to one' who fasts a "Dream Fast" (Maharil).):
