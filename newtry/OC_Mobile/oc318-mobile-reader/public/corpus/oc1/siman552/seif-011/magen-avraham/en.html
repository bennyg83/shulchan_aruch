And erev Tisha B'Av occurred, etc. And it is hard upon him to sit in a fast of two days and one night.
He should borrow his fast. And even though in siman 568, se'if 2 he wrote that it is forbidden to borrow in such a case, here since it is impossible otherwise it is permitted; and for this reason the Geonim wrote that he need not complete; and so wrote Maharamatal; and it implies that one who is able should fast two days and one night.
The seudah hamafseket. But it is forbidden to feast twice because of his vow (Terumat HaDeshen, siman 271); and if so he must conduct in it all the stringencies of the seudah hamafseket; and so wrote Mateh Moshe in the name of the Geonim, that they only permitted eating a dish of lentils without fat, end quote; and it is a mere stringency, for one cooked dish is permitted according to everyone; however he must eat it close to sunset; see siman 562, se'if 2; and therefore it is better that he seek annulment of his vow.
