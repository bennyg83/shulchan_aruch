(25) And erev Tisha B'Av occurred, etc. And it is hard upon him to sit in a fast of two days and one night.
(26) He should borrow his fast. Magen Avraham wrote: And even though in siman 568, se'if 2 he wrote that it is forbidden to borrow in such a case, here since it is impossible otherwise it is permitted; and for this reason the Geonim wrote that he need not complete, even though in general we hold that every fast upon which the sun has not set is not called a fast; but the Rav Taz in siman 568, se'if 2 rejected the Mechaber's words in this from the law; and likewise several later authorities did not copy the Mechaber's words in this as law.
(27) And he eats the seudah hamafseket. And it is forbidden to feast twice because of his vow; and if so he must conduct in it all the law of the seudah hamafseket stated above.
(28) Before sunset. And since it is impossible to time so precisely as to delay the eating, it is better that he seek annulment of his vow.
(29) A dream-fast. And the same applies to a yahrzeit. And it is good that the first time he stipulate not to fast only until after midday, and he pray Minchah Gedolah and eat a meal, and afterward eat the seudah hamafseket.
