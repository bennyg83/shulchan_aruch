And the Geonim wrote that he fasts, etc. For the Geonim hold that one may not borrow the fast, for accepting Monday-Thursday is like "this day," as below in siman 568; see there. Therefore they fast until after Minchah prayer, and he prays the fast prayer at Minchah. And the same law applies if a yahrzeit falls on erev Tisha B'Av, or if they are fasting a dream-fast (Beis Yosef, Or Zarua).
