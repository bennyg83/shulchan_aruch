Only parshas Shma. It appears to me that if he read kerias shma in the synagogue by day, he must read at night the entire kerias shma in order to fulfill eighteen, as is written siman 235; and if he read it in the synagogue at night, he need not read except parshas Shma, since it is mentioned explicitly in the Gemara, chapter HaRo'eh. And Mahariv wrote that one must say birkas hamapil. And Maharil wrote that one should not lock the door with a strong bolt, and where damage is common we do not rely on a forced circumstance.
