2) There. But only water. Mordechai writes that Rabbi Shmuel of Evreux was personally stringent and would not drink even water. Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 8, likewise writes in the name of Rav Shalom Gaon that one should drink nothing further until morning. See there. Beis Yosef cites these views but writes that this is not the custom. He therefore writes in the Shulchan Aruch that water is permitted. See above, siman 479, subsection 7.
