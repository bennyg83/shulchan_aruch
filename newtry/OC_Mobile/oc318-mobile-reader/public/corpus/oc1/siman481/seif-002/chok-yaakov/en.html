Likewise on the second night. So wrote the Beis Yosef in the name of the Agur in the name of Shibolei HaLeket; and there in siman 67 he wrote, and these are his words: the meticulous among Israel practice the second night like the first night in all its details; but one who wishes to eat and drink on the second night after Birkas HaMazon and after Yehallelucha may eat and drink, and nothing is upon him, except that he excludes himself from the class of the meticulous and transgresses the words of Chazal: a person should never exclude himself from the community; end quote. And for this reason it appears to me that in any case one may rely on the view of the poskim who permit drinking other beverages on the second night after the cups; and see s.k. 1.
