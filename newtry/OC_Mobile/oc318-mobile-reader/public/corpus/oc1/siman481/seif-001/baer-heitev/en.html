(א) Thus. Darkei Moshe wrote that the custom is to permit drinking kreiznach in our country, and even apfel trank is permitted, end of his words. And Magen Avraham wrote that this means that apfel trank that they make in our country by cooking water with apples; but that trank that they make in the land of Ashkenaz where they crush the apples in the press and it has a strong taste, there is to forbid. And ginger water that they call ingber vasser, there is to permit, per Magen Avraham and Kaf HaChayim who brought that it is permitted to drink all beverages that are not intoxicating, and so too in Beiur Halacha and the author of Peri Chadash, see there. Therefore it is clear to permit drinking coffee or tea and the like. And Knesset HaGedolah in his book Pesach Me'uvin wrote that he did not want to practice permitting drinking coffee and drinking tobacco that they call tabak — he wrote that one who guards his soul shall distance himself from it.
