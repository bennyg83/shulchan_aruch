In Magen Avraham, may his memory be blessed, Darkei Moshe etc.; and it is possible that he holds etc.; and Peri Chadash disagrees and wrote that in any case the reason is not that he nullifies the taste of matzah, for behold he already drank the last two cups, and in any case the taste of matzah was nullified. Rather the reason is so that he should not become intoxicated and sleep, and it is a mitzvah to increase in recounting the praises of the Holy One Blessed be He; and according to this, all beverages that are not intoxicating are permitted to drink, and how much more so coffee, as is the current custom.
