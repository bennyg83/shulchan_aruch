1) [Se'if 1] One may not drink wine, but only water. Rif writes at the end of the chapter Arvei Pesachim that after eating all delicacies and fruit, people eat at the end an olive-sized quantity of guarded matzah and taste nothing afterward except the cup used for Birkas HaMazon and the cup used for Hallel. One who is thirsty may not drink wine, but only water. Tur cites this and writes that all the Geonim rule likewise. We already discussed this above in siman 478, subsection 4, together with the dissenting opinions. See there. Beis Yosef writes that people customarily follow Rif. According to all opinions, water may be drunk. Regarding other beverages, however, it appears that the authority who prohibits wine likewise prohibits them. These are his words, and this is his ruling here in the Shulchan Aruch, which permits drinking only water.
