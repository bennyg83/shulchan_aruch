And the Rosh wrote in the name of Rambam that it also works out for the reason of the Yerushalmi, as is written in the Tosefta: a person is obligated to engage in the laws of Pesach and in the Exodus from Egypt all night. And Ramban and Ran wrote because he should not appear to be adding to the cups and appear as one beginning another meal.
