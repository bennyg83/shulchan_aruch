And all — Taz was stringent that other beverages that are not intoxicating are forbidden; however in Darkei Moshe it is explained that the custom is to drink apfel trank or kreiznach; and according to this, his intention in the gloss here is specifically that an intoxicating beverage is forbidden, since one is obligated to recount the Exodus from Egypt; and in Magen Avraham there it is explained.
