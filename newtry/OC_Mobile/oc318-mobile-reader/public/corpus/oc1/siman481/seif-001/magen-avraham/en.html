Thus Darkei Moshe. And Mahariv wrote that water that was cooked, which they call apfel trank, is inferior to wine and forbidden because it nullifies the taste of matzah; and the custom is to drink water cooked with sweet roots that they call kreiznach — and if so, it is possible that apfel trank is also permitted, end of Darkei Moshe's words. And it appears to me that Mahariv forbade only those that they make in the land of Ashkenaz where they crush apples in the press and they have a strong taste; but in our country where they cook water with apples, our law is like kreiznach. And Bach wrote that it is possible that kreiznach is also forbidden; but in ginger water that they call ingber vasser I have not seen to forbid, end of his words. And it is possible that he holds that perhaps anything on which water was placed is permitted. Great Hallel. It appears to me specifically when he did not say the Great Hallel on the fourth cup.
