(1) [Seif 1] He is not permitted to drink wine except water. So too the Rif at the end of the chapter: after they ate all delicacies and fruits, they ate at the end a k'zayis of matzah as dessert, and they taste nothing afterward except the cup of birkas hamazon and the cup of Hallel; and one who is thirsty has no permission to drink wine except water. And Beit Yosef on the Tur wrote, and so too all the Geonim; and we already wrote this above siman 478, section 4; and also the opinion of those who disagree on this, see there — except that Beit Yosef wrote that the world practices per the words of the Rif; nevertheless according to his reasoning it is permitted to drink water; however other beverages — it appears that for one who forbids drinking wine, they are likewise forbidden to him, end of his words; and such are his words here in the Shulchan Aruch, that he permitted drinking only water.
