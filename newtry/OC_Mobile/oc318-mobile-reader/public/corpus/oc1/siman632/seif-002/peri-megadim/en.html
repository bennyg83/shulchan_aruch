As long. See Turei Zahav. It seems that one should emend, and this is the explanation: four handbreadths is a significant place everywhere; therefore one does not sleep beneath it when there is invalid s'chach at the side of less than four amos. But the rest of the sukkah is valid and has walls, for we say bent wall. In the middle too, invalid s'chach invalidates with four handbreadths, for it is a significant place, and this sukkah has no walls, and it separates, as in se'if 2 in the gloss, where it runs across the entire sukkah. One should emend the word "and also it separates," or add "as it separates"; that is, in the middle it invalidates the whole sukkah, as stated.
