For an area of four tefachim is significant enough to constitute a separation, and it therefore separates the valid s'chach on one side of the sukkah from the valid s'chach on the other. Rashi, Sukkah 17a. This applies when the invalid s'chach extends across the entire sukkah and divides the sukkah into two, and no area with the minimum valid dimensions of a sukkah together with walls remains in one place, as written below, seif 2, in the Rama's gloss; see there.
