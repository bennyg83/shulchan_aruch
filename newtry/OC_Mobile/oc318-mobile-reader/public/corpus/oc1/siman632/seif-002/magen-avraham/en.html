And even though from the outside, etc. Look in the Tur and you will find: if it has three walls and the invalid material runs along its length, it is invalid, for on each side there is only a wall and a half. If the invalid material runs along its width, then if a sukkah measure remains there, it is valid. If there is less than four amos from the middle wall until the end of the invalid s'chach, even the outer section is valid, for we say bent wall. If open air interrupts, the outer section is invalid. If it is connected from the sides, the inner and outer sections join to make a valid sukkah. So wrote Tosafos, the Rosh, and Rabbeinu Yerucham, and so wrote the Levush; the Rema shortened his language.
