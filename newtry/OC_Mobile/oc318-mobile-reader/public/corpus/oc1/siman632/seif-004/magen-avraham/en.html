If there is s'chach, etc. However, if the invalid pieces do not have four, we do not say that they should be considered closed because of lavud, for we do not say lavud to be stringent [Tur]. Meaning: although we say that they are considered as if they are adjacent to one another, nevertheless we do not say that it is as though it were separated and closed with invalid s'chach. See what I wrote in siman 502 at the end of se'if 1.
