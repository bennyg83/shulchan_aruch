The Ran writes that the Ra'avad disagrees, maintaining that the statement permitting one to sleep beneath it was said only when it is less than three tefachim, but if it is three tefachim wide, one may not sleep beneath it even in a large sukkah. Bach. So too wrote the Ritva in his novellae to Sukkah 19, stating that his teacher agreed with this view. So too wrote Shiltei HaGibborim in the name of the Riaz. Their words were cited by Chayei Adam, klal 146, os 21, who writes that one should therefore be stringent. So too wrote Mishna Berurah, os 3, that one should ideally be stringent.
