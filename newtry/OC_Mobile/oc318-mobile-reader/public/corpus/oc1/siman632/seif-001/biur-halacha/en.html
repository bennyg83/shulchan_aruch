(א) The wall bends, etc. — etc. See Mishna Berurah what he wrote, that it appears from this wording that specifically when the walls reach, etc. Accordingly he ruled in three faces of lights siman 61 that they place sechach under the roof by uncovering part of the roof covered with shingles or boards when the walls do not reach up to the sechach, for one must uncover from the roof so much that four tefachim do not remain covered within the hollow of the walls, for dofen akumah does not apply since the walls do not reach there; and in a break of air dofen akumah does not apply, and one must be careful about this as it is common. And likewise he invalidates there one who makes his sukkah outside and joins walls built under a shingle roof when the walls do not reach the roof even less than three tefachim and the roof protrudes four tefachim — according to Ran this sukkah has no partitions and one must be careful about this.
