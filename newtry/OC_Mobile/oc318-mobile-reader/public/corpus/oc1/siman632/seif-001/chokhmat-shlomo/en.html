Se'if 1. Invalid s'chach, etc. Note: See Turei Zahav, small paragraph 3, regarding what he wrote in the name of the Levush, that by Torah law one is nullified in two even when the prohibited item is recognizable, and the Turei Zahav wondered about him. Now, I found a proof for the Levush in the commentary of Rabbi Ovadiah of Bartenura to Kilayim 2:1, regarding "every se'ah that contains a quarter-kav of another species, etc.," where the Tosafos Yom Tov there wondered about him, for the prohibited item is recognizable. According to the Levush this works well, for they stand in one approach. See in my work, siman 629, in the sugya of handles, what I wrote regarding the words of the Levush; and see Yoreh De'ah, siman 99, what I wrote about the above Mishnah in Kilayim. I also brought further proofs for the above view of the Levush in several places in my novellae. See my work on Yoreh De'ah, second edition, on siman 371, which is in my work, siman 110, in a responsum to the holy community of Zaleshchik, where I brought proof for the above view of the Levush from the Talmud in Makkos 16b regarding one who crushed ten ants; see there and examine carefully.
