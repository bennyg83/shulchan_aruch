They do not combine. Invalid s'chach measuring less than four tefachim and adjacent open space do not combine, even if together they run through the entire length of the sukkah, because their respective invalidating measures differ. One may sleep beneath invalid s'chach when it is less than four tefachim, but one may not sleep beneath open space even when it is less than three tefachim, as stated in se'if 2.
In a small sukkah. There the measures are equal, for invalid s'chach also invalidates at three tefachim; they therefore combine to a total of three tefachim and invalidate the sukkah.
