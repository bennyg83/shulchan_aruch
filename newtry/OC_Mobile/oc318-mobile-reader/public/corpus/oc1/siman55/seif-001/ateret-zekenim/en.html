They say Kaddish, etc. And one does not reduce from seven Kaddish recitations each day—as the verse says, "Seven times a day I praise You" (Psalms 119:164; Shibolei HaLeket)—that is: (1) after pesukei dezimra, (2) after Yishtabach, (3) after the Shemoneh Esreh blessings, (4) after the kedushah section, (5) after Aleinu, (6) after Ashrei at Minchah, (7) after the Minchah prayer, (8) after the evening Kriat Shema before the Shemoneh Esreh. The Kaddish said after the evening Shemoneh Esreh is not counted among them because it is optional; nevertheless they say titkabel afterward. After the Shemoneh Esreh—only at Shacharit one may not say titkabel until after the kedushah section, because prayer draws after prayer. However, when there is Musaf they say a full Kaddish immediately after the Shacharit prayer. Wherever the shaliach tzibbur does not need titkabel—for since he did not recite the Shemoneh Esreh—he likewise does not complete the Kaddish and only says yitgadal v'yitkadash (Maharam). Lekhatchilah one must repeat after six who did not hear Kaddish and Barekhu; be'di'eved—that is, when six are not present—they say it even for one who did not hear.
