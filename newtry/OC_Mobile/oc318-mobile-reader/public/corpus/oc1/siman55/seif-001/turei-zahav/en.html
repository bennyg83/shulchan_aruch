They say Kaddish. Meaning: after a verse of psalms; and it requires ten, for every matter of kedushah such as Kaddish, kedushah, and Barekhu one may not do it with less than ten, as it says "and I shall be sanctified among the children of Israel," and we derive by gezeirah shavah "among–among" from the spies that ten are called an edah. And the reason for the Kaddishes that they say: one after the Shemoneh Esreh prayer, for it is a mitzvah by itself; and one after keriat haTorah, for it is a mitzvah by itself; and one after the kedushah section, and there one says Titkabel, etc., which also refers to the Shemoneh Esreh prayer. Rabbenu Yerucham wrote that Titkabel is never said except after the Shemoneh Esreh prayer; and this is not a general rule, for on the days of Selichot at the morning watch they say Titkabel after Selichot, for in them too there are many requests like the Shemoneh Esreh. And Kaddish after reciting a psalm or chapter or aggadah—and Beit Yosef did not consider Alenu leShabeach, which they say Kaddish after; and we practice to say Kaddish after it because it has verses; and we do not reduce from seven Kaddishes each day—see there "seven times a day I praise You." And he wrote: Kaddish at Maariv between the conclusion of the blessing of Kriat Shema and the Shemoneh Esreh prayer—for the evening prayer is optional; and there are those who leave the synagogue before they hear the Shemoneh Esreh and do not hear Kaddish.
