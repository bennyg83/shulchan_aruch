They recite Kaddish. Meaning, after Pesukei DeZimrah. Ten are required, because any davar shebikdushah, such as Kaddish, Kedushah, or Borchu, may not be recited with fewer than ten, as it is stated, “I shall be sanctified among the Children of Israel.” Through the verbal analogy of “among” from the spies, we learn that ten are called a congregation. The reasons for the Kaddishes are as follows. One is recited after the Amidah because it is an independent mitzvah; one after the Torah reading because it is an independent mitzvah; and one after the order of Kedushah, where Tiskabel is recited, since it also refers back to the Amidah. Levush writes that Tiskabel is always recited only after the Amidah, but this is not an absolute rule, for during Selichos before dawn Tiskabel is recited after Selichos, since they too contain many petitions like the Amidah. Kaddish is also recited after a psalm, chapter, or aggadah. Beit Yosef did not count Aleinu, after which Kaddish is recited, but our custom is to recite Kaddish after it because it contains verses. We do not recite fewer than seven Kaddishes each day, corresponding to “Seven times a day I praise You.” The Kaddish at Maariv between the conclusion of the blessings of Krias Shema and the Amidah is recited because Maariv is optional, and some leave the synagogue before hearing the Amidah and would otherwise not hear Kaddish.
