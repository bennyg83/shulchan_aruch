In the author, seif 10. See responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 14 [the questioner there wanted to distinguish between bar mitzvah and yahrzeit; and likewise according to this, one who died in first Adar — his yahrzeit is in the month of Shevat of a regular year. And Chatam Sofer answered that we do not exchange Shevat for Adar, as we say in the passage beginning (daf 15), and likewise the reverse; and it is clear that one who died in Adar I of an intercalated year — his yahrzeit is in Adar of a regular year. And see further on this matter in Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 163 at length].
