(1) [Seif 1] They say Kaddish — explanation: after Pesukei DeZimra. Taz s.k. 1. And Agur wrote in siman 98, and thus: Raavad answered the reason for the Kaddishim said in prayer. (1) After Pesukei DeZimra, for there is a pause of verses there. (2) After conclusion of the Shemoneh Esreh, for it is a mitzvah on its own. (3) After kedushah, for it is a matter of kedushah. (4) After reciting a psalm or chapter or aggadah or Mishnah, as we say in Sotah (49a): "Why does the world stand on the kedushah of the order? — because it is 'May His great name be exalted' of aggadah." (5) After Ashrei at Minchah, for it is a mitzvah on its own — that whoever says Tehillah LeDavid three times daily is assured, etc. (6) After Kriat Shema of Maariv, for Maariv is optional, and some go to them before the Shemoneh Esreh and do not hear the seven Kaddishim after Maariv prayer, for prayer is already completed. And one does not reduce from seven Kaddishim each day, for the name "seven times a day I praise You" — so far in Shibolei HaLeket. Beit Yosef and Perishah letter 1. And that he did not count Kaddish after the Shemoneh Esreh of Minchah — namely before beginning Maariv — one may say all the more so it is the Kaddish after the Shemoneh Esreh of Shacharit. And with this it is also settled why he said one does not reduce from seven, etc. — is it that every day they say Kaddish of aggadah? Rather that in any case there are seven Kaddishim with the Kaddish after Minchah prayer; only he did not think to write here except the reason for each one. Perishah there. And see Levush; and per the Arizal z"l there must be twelve Kaddishim each day: six in Shacharit — one before Hodu, and two before Yotzer, and the third after the Shemoneh Esreh, and the fourth after kedushah of the order, and the fifth before Kaddish Yatom, and the sixth before Aleinu l'shabe'ach (and even though the rabbi wrote there that the final Kaddish is not among the Kaddishim, etc. — nevertheless he concluded that this Kaddish is Asiyah, where there is the aspect of death, in order to raise all souls and spirits in the secret of resurrection of the dead; therefore orphans say it for the death of father and mother, etc. — see there). And Tikkunei Zohar 139b wrote that the intention is to raise Asiyah to Yetzirah in the quarry of souls of the dead; and this is drawn from Asiyah to the heights of ascents, for every level is called Asiyah relative to the level above it — so far; and if so this too is obligatory) — and two in Minchah prayer: one before the Amidah and one after the Amidah; and four in Maariv: one before Maariv blessings, and one before the Amidah, and one after the Amidah, and one before Aleinu l'shabe'ach, as explained in Sha'ar HaKavanot discourse on Kaddish and in the prayer text page 51; and Beit David above siman 48 — see there. And further see there in our words to siman 48 letter 1, beginning "and know one introduction," etc., reason for all saying of Kaddishim per the order. And some are accustomed further to say after Minchah prayer Mizmor LeMenatzeach BeNeginot, etc., and afterward they say Kaddish and Aleinu l'shabe'ach — and behold all become thirteen Kaddishim corresponding to thirteen attributes. Fortunate is one who serves his Master with joy and intention. And see below letter 19.
