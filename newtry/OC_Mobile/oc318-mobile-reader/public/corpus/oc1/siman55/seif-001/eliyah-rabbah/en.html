That Kaddish, etc. — it implies he should not begin Yotzer Or; and so Bach explained Tur's words. But Perishah explained that even if he began Kaddish he should finish also the kedushah of Yotzer Or until after the congregation prayed silently. And it seems one may rely on him for law, for I saw in Maharil who ruled in the name of Rosh that even if he began only Yishtabach, he should finish everything.
