{Rama: And if they left after he began aloud and kedushah, they may begin the entire kedushah section and say the full Kaddish. It implies that for Kaddish too one requires that the prayer began aloud. But if some left before the beginning of the repetition of the prayer, they do not say the full Kaddish. And it may be asked: why should they not say the full Kaddish for the silent prayer that was among ten — as is learned from the evening prayer, for Rama wrote afterward "and the evening prayer and Kaddish after it do not belong to Kriat Shema and its blessings" — it implies if Shacharit began, they say Kaddish after it, for Kaddish belongs to the prayer. And why is it not so at Shacharit prayer? And that they enacted repetition of the prayer — is it because of this that Kaddish on the silent prayer does not apply? Uncertain. And regarding the law that if some left they finish if a majority remained — I am uncertain: if after he began with ten, five left, and afterward others came there, and there is now a majority — does it help to finish? Since there is no majority from those ten who began. And even if one of those five who left returned there — there is room to doubt that perhaps already it was a time when there is no remnant of majority, the matter was removed entirely and it was like a beginning anew.}
