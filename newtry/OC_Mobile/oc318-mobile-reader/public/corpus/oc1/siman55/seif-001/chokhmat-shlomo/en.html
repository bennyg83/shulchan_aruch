Se'if 1. Kaddish is recited, and it is not recited with fewer than ten, etc. Gloss: Regarding the law where the majority of a group of ten prayed but only a few individuals did not pray, and one person arose and recited the prayer aloud: may he afterward recite Full Kaddish? In my opinion, he should not recite Kaddish with Titkabel after his prayer; he may recite Kaddish only without Titkabel. See my work Shenot Chayim, in the addenda, page 6, in the responsum to the holy congregation of Breslau, what I wrote there incidentally explaining the reason; see there and examine carefully. Regarding the law of a minor about whom it is uncertain whether he is thirteen years old or younger, we already wrote a responsum on this in one of our works. Now see what I wrote on this in my work Tuv Ta'am VaDa'at on Yoreh De'ah, Mahadura Telita'ah, siman 269, and in the laws of circumcision, siman 266; see there what I wrote on this and my explanation of the words of Tosafos in Yevamos, page 48, showing that they do not contradict their words on page 37. See there, with the help of the Omnipresent, and examine carefully.
