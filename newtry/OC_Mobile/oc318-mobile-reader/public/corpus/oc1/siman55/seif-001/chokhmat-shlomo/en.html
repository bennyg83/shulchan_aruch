Seif 1. They say Kaddish and do not say it with fewer than ten, etc. See note: if by law a majority of ten prayed and only a few individuals did not pray, and one stood and recited the prayer aloud — in my opinion he should not say a full Kaddish after his prayer, only without Titkabel he may say Kaddish. And see in my work Shenot Chayyim in the omissions, page 6, in the responsum to the community of Pressburg, what he wrote there incidentally in his reason — see there, and it requires study. And regarding a minor in doubt whether he is bar mitzvah or younger — we already wrote a responsum on this in one of my works. And now see what I wrote on this in my work Totafot Yoreh De'ah part 5 siman 269, Laws of Milah siman 266 — see there what he wrote on this and the explanation of Tosafos in Yevamos daf 48, that they did not contradict their words on daf 37 — see there, b'ezrat Hashem, and it requires study.
