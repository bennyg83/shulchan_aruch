(17) A convert combines for a minyan of ten for every matter of kedushah; and even though we say "and I shall be sanctified among the children of Israel," and in Sukkah 28b in the matter of "to include" — "children of Israel" excludes converts if there were no verse — nevertheless here it is an asmakhta, for a convert is fit to be shaliach tzibbur as in siman 53 seif 19, and there is no reason he should not combine for ten. Peri Megadim there. And so Petach HaDevir letter 1 — see there. And so Levush on Magen Avraham s.k. 7. And a convert who circumcised but did not immerse does not combine until he immerses. Siddur Beit Yosef in laws of Kaddish letter 16 in the name of responsum Radbaz part 3 siman 578 Florence edition.
