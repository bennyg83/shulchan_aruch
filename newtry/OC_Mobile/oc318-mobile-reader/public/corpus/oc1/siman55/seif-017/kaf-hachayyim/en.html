17) A convert joins a minyan of 10 for every davar shebikdushah. Although we say, "I shall be sanctified among the Children of Israel," and Tosafot, Sukkah 28b, s.v. Lerabot, state that the expression "Children of Israel" excludes converts unless there is an inclusion, here the verse is only an asmachta. A convert is fit to serve as shaliach tzibbur, as stated in siman 53, seif 19, and there is no reason that he should not join the 10. Pri Megadim there. Pithei HaDevir, letter 1, writes likewise; see there. Levushei Serad on Magen Avraham, seif katan 7, also writes so. A convert who underwent brit milah but has not immersed does not join until he immerses. Siddur Beit Yaakov, laws of Kaddish, letter 16, in the name of Responsa Radbaz, part 3, siman 478, Furth edition.
