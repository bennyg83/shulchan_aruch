17) A convert joins a minyan of ten for every sacred matter. Although Scripture says, 'I shall be sanctified among the Children of Yisrael,' and Tosafos, Sukkah 28b, s.v. Lerabos, state that 'Children of Yisrael' excludes converts absent an inclusion, here the verse is only an asmachta. A convert is qualified to serve as shaliach tzibbur, as stated in siman 53:19, and there is no reason he should not join ten. Pri Megadim, ibid.; Pischei HaDevir 1; and Levushei Serad on Magen Avraham 7. A convert who was circumcised but has not immersed does not join until he immerses. Siddur Beis Yaakov, laws of Kaddish 16, in the name of Responsa Radbaz 3:478, Fürth edition.
