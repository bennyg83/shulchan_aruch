Shulchan Aruch, seif 16. If nine are in the large one. The rule is that the large one cannot be drawn after the small one, because it is as though separate. Also, the congregation, being significant, cannot be drawn after the individual, and even five after five cannot be drawn; only the individual is drawn after the congregation. Therefore they cannot combine unless the individual is in the small one and the congregation is in the large one. In this seif it teaches that even a shaliach tzibbur is not preferable to an individual for this matter, and the congregation is not drawn after him; his law is exactly like that of an individual.
