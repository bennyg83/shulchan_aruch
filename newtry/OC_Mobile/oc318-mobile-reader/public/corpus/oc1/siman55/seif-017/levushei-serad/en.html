Shulchan Aruch seif 16—if nine are in the large [room]. The rule is that the large cannot be drawn after the small, for it is as a separate domain. And likewise the congregation, who are important, cannot be drawn after the individual; and even five after five cannot be drawn—only the individual is drawn after the congregation. Therefore they cannot join unless the individual is in the small and the congregation in the large. And in this seif he comes to teach that even the shaliach tzibbur is not preferable for this matter over an individual, and the congregation is not drawn after him—his law is literally like an individual.
