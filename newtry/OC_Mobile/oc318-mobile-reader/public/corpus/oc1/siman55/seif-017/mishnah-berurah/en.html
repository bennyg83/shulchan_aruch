(56) He does not discharge them. Even if there were ten in the small one, he does not discharge them from their obligation. Even nowadays, when all are knowledgeable, nevertheless he should not say Kaddish and Kedushah there, since in the place where he stands there is no minyan. But when there is a minyan in the place of the shaliach tzibbur, he certainly discharges even those behind the synagogue who hear and have intent, and he also has intent to discharge all who hear.
