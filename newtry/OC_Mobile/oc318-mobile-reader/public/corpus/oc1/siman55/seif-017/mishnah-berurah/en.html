(56) He does not fulfill their obligation—even if there were ten in the small, he does not fulfill their obligation; and even nowadays when all are expert, nevertheless he should not say Kaddish and kedushah there, since in the place where he stands there is no minyan. But when there is a minyan in the place of the shaliach tzibbur, he certainly fulfills even those who are behind the synagogue and hear and intend, and he too intends to fulfill all who hear.
