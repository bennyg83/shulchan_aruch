Seif 13, he must, etc. This is from that passage in Eruvin 92b and that passage in Pesachim 85b. Tosafot explained there and there, and in Rosh Hashanah 27b and Sotah 38b, that there it discusses answering Kaddish and Kedushah when there are ten without him, as stated in seif 20, and the passage at the end of the third chapter of Rosh Hashanah there regarding shofar and Megillah, where ten are not required.
And one who stands, etc. Pesachim there. Although we rule like Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, for the halacha follows him, and moreover the sugya in Sotah there follows him, nevertheless in this matter he does not disagree with Rav, as above.
From the place, etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham, and as stated there: this itself is difficult, etc.; it is not difficult, etc. He holds that since it requires sanctification, it follows that it is not automatically considered inside. So too the Rambam wrote in chapter 9 of the Laws of the Korban Pesach, halacha 1, that the threshold itself is considered outside with respect to removing it from the house. At first glance it appears the opposite, that it is presumably considered inside, except that they intentionally left it unsanctified, as stated in the Gemara there; so too wrote the Beit Yosef. Even according to this explanation, the matter requires study, for it should be no worse than a small courtyard breached into a large one, where if a minority or the shaliach tzibbur is there they combine, as written below in seif 16. See the Magen Avraham, and for another reason, as above.
