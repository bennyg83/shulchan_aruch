One. There were nine in the house and one in the sukkah on the festival—they join, according to Rikanti and Peri Chadash disagrees. When there are nine in one place and one beyond the curtain that they spread for modesty—they join, according to Rikanti. Peri Chadash wrote: however, it stands to reason that this is specifically when they spread it for modesty in general; but if there are tefillin or a sefer Torah and they spread a sheet as a partition for the sun from one's bed, it is also a partition for joining ten—and they do not join, etc.

Inner. Meaning: the place where the door closes is considered outside. In Sefer Tanya he ruled it is considered inside—Magen Avraham. In Sefer Even haEzer he ruled: if six stand in the synagogue and four are within the thickness of the doorway, they join; but if five are in the synagogue and five within the thickness of the doorway, they do not join. In the ezrat synagogue, however, even one does not join. See at length in Maginei Eretz, Prague edition.
