One. If there were nine in a house and one inside the sukkah on the festival, they combine, according to Maharikash, but the Peri Chadash disagrees. When there are nine in one place and one behind the curtain that they spread for modesty, they combine, according to Maharikash. The Peri Chadash wrote: however, it is reasonable that this applies specifically where they spread it merely for modesty. But if there are tefillin or a Torah scroll there and they spread a sheet so that it should serve as a partition for marital relations, it is also a partition with respect to combining ten, and they do not combine; see there.
Inside. This means that the place where the door closes is considered like outside. In the Sefer Tanya he ruled that it is considered like inside; see Magen Avraham. In Even HaOzer he ruled that if six are standing in the synagogue and four are within the thickness of the doorway, they combine. But if five are in the synagogue and five are within the thickness of the doorway, they do not combine. In the women's section of the synagogue, however, even one person does not combine. See at length in the work Magenai Eretz, Proops edition.
