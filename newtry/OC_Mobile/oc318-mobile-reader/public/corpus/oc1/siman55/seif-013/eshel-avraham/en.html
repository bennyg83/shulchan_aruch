All ten. In one sacred community, the Sifrei Torah were kept in a chest more than ten tefachim high. Beneath it was a hollow space covered with boards. Because of the city’s distress, two or three distinguished individuals hid there, including one kohen. The question therefore arose whether they combine with those above to form a minyan, and whether the partition exempts the kohen from Bircas Kohanim. Responsa Panim Meiros, part 2, siman 1, concludes that when the partitions do not reach the ceiling, even the Semak concedes to the Rashba that they do not combine. The same applies to Bircas Kohanim, since there are not ten in the chamber.
