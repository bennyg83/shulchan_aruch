All ten — in one community the Torah scrolls are placed in a chest above ten [tefachim], and below is hollow space covered with boards; and because of the city's distress, two or three exceptional individuals and one kohen among them hid there. From now on there is room to question whether they combine for ten, and likewise whether the partition exempts the kohen from priestly benediction. He brought in Tosafot Pesachim 85b chapter 2 siman 1 that if partitions do not reach the ceiling, Semak agrees with Rashba that they do not combine; likewise for priestly benediction, for behold there are not ten in the chamber.
