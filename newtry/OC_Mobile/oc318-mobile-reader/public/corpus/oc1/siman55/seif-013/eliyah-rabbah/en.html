And some are outside, etc. — Perishah wrote: a great matter it teaches us — that even though some are here and some there, and if so I would have said either he is considered shaliach tzibbur for those outside or for those inside — it teaches us we consider him for here and there; and the reason is that since all intend that he discharge them, we combine him for these and these. And so it implies: if nine or the majority are outside or inside and the shaliach tzibbur stands on the platform, he can discharge them — and specifically the shaliach tzibbur, and for this reason; but if another stands on the platform, not only do we not consider him for these and those, but even nine inside he does not combine for them, because from the side outward is as outside — until here; and it requires study.
