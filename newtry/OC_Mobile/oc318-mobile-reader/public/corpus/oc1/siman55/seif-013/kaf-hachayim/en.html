(13) An onen does not combine for ten for any matter of kedushah, as Shakhnehag in Hagahot Beit Yosef letter 4; Olat Tamid letter 3; Alef Rosh letter 10. Peri Chadash letter 8; and so Yad Aharon in Hagahot Beit Yosef in the name of Perach Mateh Aharon part 1 siman 19 and Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 25; and so Beit Yehudah siman 45. And so Chida in Kaf HaChayyim letter 5 and in Kaf HaChayyim siman 8 in the name of the later authorities. And so Shulchan Aruch HaRav page 80 column 1 and page 177 column 2. Beit Yosef letter 25. Ben Ish Chai parashat Vayechi letter 6. But if he has someone who will strive on his behalf — that if he wishes to read Kriat Shema they do not restrain his hand, as written in siman 71 — it is reasonable that certainly he combines. Peri Chadash there. Yad Aharon there. Kaf HaChayyim there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. Beit Yosef there.
