Of the thickness of the door — see Mishna Berurah; and where the door is fixed toward the inside and the door enters only part of the hollow of the opening and the opening is open — here, according to Rabbeinu Yerucham and Shulchan Aruch, who rules like him [as proven in Beur HaGra and Beit HaGadol and Magen Avraham], it seems they do not combine whether he stands inside the place of the thickness of the door or inside the hollow of the opening. But according to Tanya, whom Magen Avraham rules like, it is uncertain; for according to Peri Megadim's explanation regarding the side — that the place of the hollow is called by the name "side" — see there: he would combine according to Tanya. But according to what Yad Efraim explained — the place of the hollow of the opening that is outside the place of the thickness of the door shall be called outside the place of the side — in this, according to all, he must be as outside. But according to what the Gra wrote regarding the place of the side — even if we say it is as outside, nevertheless it does not detract from a small courtyard breached into a large one; one may be lenient here too.

And outside as outside — and if they see one another they may combine even standing entirely outside, similar to what the author was lenient below in seif 14 [Perach; and so it implies from Peri Megadim]; and lekhatchilah it is better to be stringent that they enter inside, for this leniency that "they see one another" helps for combining, as for Birkat HaMazon below in siman 195 — it derives from Rashba's responsum in siman 96; and he did not write there this, only as a possibility.
