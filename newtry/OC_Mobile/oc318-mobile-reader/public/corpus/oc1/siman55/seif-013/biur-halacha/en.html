Of the thickness of the door. See the Mishnah Berurah. Where the door is fixed toward the inside and the door enters only into part of the opening's hollow space, and the opening is open, according to Rabbeinu Yerucham and the Shulchan Aruch, who rules like him, as is proven from the Beur HaGra, the Beer HaGolah, and the Magen Avraham, it appears that they do not combine, whether he stands within the place of the door's thickness or within the hollow space of the opening. However, according to the Taniya, whom the Magen Avraham rules like, this requires study. For according to the Peri Megadim's explanation regarding the threshold area, that the hollow space is called the threshold area, see there, he would combine according to the Taniya. Yet according to what the Yad Ephraim explained, the hollow space of the opening that is outside the place of the door's thickness is called outside the place of the threshold area, and in this, according to all opinions, it should be like outside. However, according to what the Beur HaGra wrote regarding the place of the threshold area, that even if we say it is like outside, in any event it is no worse than a small courtyard breached into a large one, there is room to be lenient in this as well.
And outside is like outside. If they see one another, they can combine even if they are standing entirely outside, similar to what the Mechaber was lenient about below in seif 14, as stated by the Peri Chadash, and so it also appears from the Peri Megadim. Initially, however, it is better to be stringent in this, that they should enter inside, for this leniency, that seeing one another helps for combining as it does regarding Birkas HaMazon below in siman 195, stems from the responsum of the Rashba, siman 96, and he wrote it there only as a possibility.
