In the Mechaber, seif 10. Note: See Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 14. [The questioner there wished to distinguish between becoming bar mitzvah and a yahrzeit, and accordingly maintained that one who died in the first Adar has his yahrzeit in the month of Shevat in an ordinary year. The Chasam Sofer answered that Shevat is not exchanged for Adar, as we say in Rosh Hashanah 15, and the same applies here. It is therefore clear that one who died in Adar I of a leap year has his yahrzeit in Adar of an ordinary year. See also the lengthy discussion of this matter in Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 163.]
