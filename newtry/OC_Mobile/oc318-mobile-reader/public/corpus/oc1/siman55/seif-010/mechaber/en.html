If one youth was born on the 29 of the 1st Adar in a leap year and another youth was born in the 2nd Adar, on its first day, then if the 13th year is not a leap year the one who was born on the 29 of the 1st Adar needs to wait until the 29th of Adar in the 13th year, to be 13 years old. And the one the one who was born after him on the first of the 2nd Adar will be 13 years old since the first of Adar of the 13th year has arrived [already].Rem"a: And someone who was born in Adar and is made a bar mitzvah in a leap year is not made a bar mitzvah until the second Adar.
