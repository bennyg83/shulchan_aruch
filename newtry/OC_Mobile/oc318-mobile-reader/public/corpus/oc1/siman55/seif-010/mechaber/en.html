If one youth was born on the 29th of the first Adar in an intercalated year, and another youth was born in the second Adar on its first day, and the thirteenth year is not intercalated — the one born on the 29th of the first Adar must wait until the 29th of Adar in the thirteenth year to be thirteen years old. And the one born after him on the first of the second Adar will be thirteen years old once the first of Adar of the thirteenth year arrives.And one who was born in Adar and becomes bar mitzvah in an intercalated year does not become bar mitzvah until the second Adar.
