If he has not brought forth two hairs, even if he is advanced in years his law is like a minor until the majority of his years pass—for then it becomes clear that he is a saris. If signs of saris appeared before this, his law is like an adult.However, we are not exacting about hairs; rather, whoever has reached the years in which he is presumed [an adult], we presume him an adult and say regarding this matter that presumably he has brought forth two hairs [Maharik shoresh 49].]:
