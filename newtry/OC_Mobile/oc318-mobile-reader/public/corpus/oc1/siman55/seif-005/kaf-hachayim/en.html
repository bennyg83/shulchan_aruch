There are those who say that the matters over which one says Kaddish must be with a minyan; therefore if there is no minyan in the synagogue, the shaliach tzibbur waits with Yishtabach and is silent until a minyan comes, and then says Yishtabach and Kaddish. And when studying Torah shebe'al peh he says the baraita "The Holy One blessed be He wished to merit Israel," or the last two or three verses of Ashrei, after a minyan has come to the synagogue. And if there is no minyan for Hodu, they say until "Baruch she'amar," and then he says "Rabbi Chanina ben Akavya" and says the Rabbinic Kaddish. Chayei Adam siman 54 letter 3. And see Kaf HaChayyim siman 8 letter 1. And above siman 50 letter 6; and what is written below letter 34 with Heaven's help.
