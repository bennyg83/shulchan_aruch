5) Some say that the words over which Kaddish is recited must themselves be said in the presence of the minyan. Therefore, if no minyan is present in the synagogue, the shaliach tzibbur should wait at Yishtabach and remain silent until a minyan arrives; he then recites Yishtabach and Kaddish. When studying Oral Torah, after the minyan arrives he should recite the baraisa, 'The Holy One, blessed is He, desired to confer merit upon Yisrael,' or the final two or three verses of Ashrei. If no minyan was present for Hodu, they should recite through Baruch She'amar; the shaliach tzibbur should then recite Rabbi Chananya ben Akashya and say Kaddish DeRabbanan. Chesed LaAlafim 54:3. See Kesher Gudal 8:1, what was stated above in siman 50:6, and what we shall write below in paragraph 39, with God's help.
