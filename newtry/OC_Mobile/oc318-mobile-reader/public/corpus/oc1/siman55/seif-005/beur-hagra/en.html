Seif 65, and nevertheless, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 7 of Berachot; Bereishit Rabbah chapter 91 there: a minor they make, etc.; and it taught "they are not exacting with a minor." Rabbi Simon, Rabbi Yehuda le-Bavel: for two minors it is needed — one doubt and one minor: they make the doubt primary and the minor a branch. And he explained what is written in the name of Rashi: they are not exacting whether he reached years; they are not exacting about hairs. And he answered that the doubt who brought is as an adult, and without him they combine for an actual minor, even though in the Gemara he explained that "they are not exacting with a minor" means one who has sprouted. The custom of the world follows Yerushalmi, as above.
