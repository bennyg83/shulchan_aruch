Seif 5, however, etc. The Yerushalmi, seventh chapter of Berachos, and Bereishis Rabbah, chapter 91, state there: a minor may be made, etc. But was it not taught that one is not exacting with a minor? Rabbi Simon said in the name of Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi: this was necessary for two minors, one doubtful and one certainly a minor. They make the doubtful one the main one and the minor an adjunct. The Mareh Kohen explained in the name of Rashi that one is not exacting means that if he reached the age of years, one is not exacting about hairs. It answers that a doubtful one who brought signs is like an adult, and without him they combine even an actual minor, even though in the Gemara they explained the statement that one is not exacting with a minor as referring to a minor who has sprouted. The common practice follows the Yerushalmi, as above.
