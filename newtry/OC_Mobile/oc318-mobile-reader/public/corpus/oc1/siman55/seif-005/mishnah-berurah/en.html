(25) Two hairs—in other places of the body; even if he brought two hairs in the beard [Beit Shmuel in Even HaEzer siman 172 se'if kaf heh].

(26) His law is like a minor—and if he brought two hairs he becomes an adult from the day he brought them, not retroactively.

(27) The majority of his years—and we do not require only one day more than thirty-five years.

(28) Then it will become clear—meaning: and then he becomes an adult retroactively from the year of thirteen and one day.

(29) Signs of a saris—explained in Even HaEzer in siman 172 se'if heh; and even one of the signs mentioned there suffices to equate him to a saris if he did not bring two hairs, also in the beard, as explained in the Rama gloss there.

(30) His law is like an adult—and specifically when he has reached twenty years [that is: thirty days in the twentieth year—so is the opinion of most decisors in Even HaEzer siman 155 se'if yod bet] and did not bring two hairs—for then the signs help to make him a saris retroactively, whether the signs appeared to him in year twenty or before that; but before he reached twenty years, even if he has all the signs of sarisim, we say he is still a minor. And see Peri Megadim who sides to say that regarding prayer, which is derabbanan, perhaps one may be lenient to combine him even before twenty if saris signs are seen on him.

(31) For this matter—meaning: for prayer, which is derabbanan; and even per those decisors who hold prayer is de'oraita, in any case combining ten is not de'oraita—so we rely on this and say: since he came to the full measure of his years, presumably he brought two hairs, for most people once they reach the full measure of years presumably bring two hairs; but for other de'oraita obligations it does not help [and see Peri Megadim who elaborates on this and concludes that because it is a common minority, therefore they were strict derabbanan for a de'oraita matter, as regarding examination of the lung].
