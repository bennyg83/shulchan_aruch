(25) Two hairs. In other places of the body, even if he produced two hairs in the beard, as Beit Shmuel writes in Even HaEzer, siman 172, seif katan 5.
(26) His law is like a minor. If he produced two hairs, he becomes an adult from the day he produced them, not retroactively.
(27) Most of his years. Only one day more than thirty-five years is required.
(28) For then it will be clarified. Meaning that he then becomes an adult retroactively from thirteen years and one day.
(29) Signs of a saris. These are explained in Even HaEzer, siman 172, seif 5. Even one of the signs mentioned there suffices to establish him as a saris if he also did not produce two hairs in the beard, as explained in the gloss there.
(30) His law is like an adult. This is specifically when he reached his twentieth year, meaning thirty days into the twentieth year, which is the view of most poskim in Even HaEzer, siman 155, seif 12, and he did not produce two hairs. Then the signs help establish him as a saris retroactively, whether the signs appeared in the twentieth year or earlier. But before he reaches twenty years, even if he has all the signs of a saris, we still say that he is a minor. See the Peri Megadim, who tends to say that regarding prayer, which is D’Rabbanan, perhaps one may be lenient to join him even before twenty if signs of a saris are visible in him.
(31) For this matter. Meaning for prayer, which is D’Rabbanan. Even according to the poskim who hold that prayer is D’Oraisa, in any event joining ten is not D’Oraisa. Therefore we rely on this and say that since he reached the years of adulthood, presumably he produced two hairs, for most people, once they reach the proper years, presumably produce two hairs. But regarding other obligations according to the Torah, this does not help. See the Peri Megadim, who discusses this at length and concludes that because it is a common minority, the Sages were stringent D’Rabbanan regarding Torah matters, as with checking the lung.
