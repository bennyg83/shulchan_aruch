“And Kedushah.” See Baer Heitev. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah and Peri Chadash disagree and hold that he completes only Kedushah and the remaining blessings of the Amidah, but not the full Kaddish after the prayer. The Shulchan Aruch’s wording likewise implies this; see Beit Yosef. Shalmei Tzibur agrees with this view. Common practice, however, follows the Rama and the Magen Avraham in this matter.
