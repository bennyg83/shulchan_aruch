And kedushah. Ba'er Heitev. And Shakh and Peri Chadash dispute, and hold that one does not finish except kedushah—and similarly the rest of the blessings of the Shemoneh Esreh—but not the full Kaddish after prayer; and thus is implied by the language of Shulchan Aruch; and see Beit Yosef. And therefore Shevut Yaakov agreed, and the world practices as Rama and Magen Avraham on this.
