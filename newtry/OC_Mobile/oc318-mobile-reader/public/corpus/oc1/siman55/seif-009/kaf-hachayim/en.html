Just as it is forbidden to pass before one praying, so it is forbidden to pass before one saying Kaddish. The rabbi the pious Maharar Yaakov Molkho in responsa 21 siman 335. Kaf HaChayyim letter 9 and in his book Kaf HaChayyim siman 8 letter 5. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. Siddur Beit Yosef letter 50. Sha'arei Teshuvah at the beginning of siman 56. Zekhor LeAvraham letter 100. Chayei Adam letter 12. Ben Ish Chai there letter 10. And that it is forbidden to pass before one saying Kaddish is from the beginning of the Kaddish until "be'alma" and they answer Amen; but afterward, when he says "on Israel," etc., and "May there be peace," etc.—we have no concern, for this is mere custom and not from the text of Kaddish, as written above siman 51 letters 10 and 21. And so Ben Ish Chai there.
