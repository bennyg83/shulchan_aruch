Seif 69, forever, etc. Niddah 47b.

And the leap year, etc. As written in Yerushalmi, first chapter of Ketubot: Rabbi Avin read "to God Most High, to God who completes" — a girl of three years and one day; and the court may intercalate for her; the virgins return, and he said to them: the virgins do not return; and this is what is written in this siman in the gloss; and one who was born, etc.
