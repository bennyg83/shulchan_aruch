Seif 9, always, etc. Niddah 47b.
And the leap year, etc. As stated in the Yerushalmi, first chapter of Kesubos: Rabbi Avin said, I will call to G-d Most High, to G-d Who completes concerning me. A girl of three years and one day, if beis din later decides to intercalate the year, her signs of virginity return; and if not, they do not return. This is what is written in seif 10 in the gloss, and one who was born, etc.
