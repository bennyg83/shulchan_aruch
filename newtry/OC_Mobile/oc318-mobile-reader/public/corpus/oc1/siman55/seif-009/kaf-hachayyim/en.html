9) Just as it is forbidden to pass in front of one who is praying, it is forbidden to pass in front of one reciting Kaddish. The pious Rabbi Yaakov Molcho, handwritten responsum 135; Birkei Yosef 9 and Kesher Gudal 8:5; Shalmei Tzibbur, ibid.; Siddur Beis Yaakov 50; Shaarei Teshuvah at the beginning of siman 56; Zechirah LeChaim 100; Chesed LaAlafim 12; and Ben Ish Chai, ibid., 10. The prohibition applies from the beginning of Kaddish through 'daamiran be'alma, ve'imru amen.' Afterward, when he says 'Al Yisrael,' etc., and 'Yehei shelama,' etc., there is no concern, because this is merely a custom and not part of the original Kaddish text, as stated above in siman 51:10 and 21. Ben Ish Chai, ibid., writes likewise.
