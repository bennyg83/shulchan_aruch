“Alone.” See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef likewise writes that one should be stringent in accordance with the Taz, and the sleeper should be awakened so that he is at least dozing. Eliyah Rabbah writes in the name of the Radbaz, siman 141, that if ten people each prayed privately; not necessarily each entirely by himself, but without joining together, with some praying separately from the others; their opportunity for Kaddish and Kedushah has passed. Even if the ten later assemble, they cannot repeat the Amidah, and doing so would constitute a blessing in vain. Only if one person has not yet prayed may he pray while the others respond Amen after him. The Magen Avraham cites this briefly in siman 69. See there regarding his ruling that if there are not ten who have not prayed, the chazzan should not first pray silently, and see Ateret Zekenim there in the name of the Gaon R’ Yehoshua.
