Alone. Ba'er Heitev. And in Be'er Heitev on Rama he also wrote to be strict like Taz, and one should arouse him that he should at least be dozing. And Avudraham wrote in the name of Radbaz siman 141 that if ten each prayed individually (not necessarily each one by himself, but rather they did not join—only some prayed alone and some alone) Kaddish and kedushah have departed from them; and even though ten joined together they cannot return and pray; and if they prayed it is a blessing in vain—only where there is one who has not prayed, he should pray and they answer Amen after him, etc. Magen Avraham brought this briefly in siman 69. See there what he wrote, that if there are not ten who have not prayed, the shaliach tzibbur should not pray silently, etc. And see Ateret Zekenim on this in the name of the Gaon Rabbi Yehoshua zt"l.
