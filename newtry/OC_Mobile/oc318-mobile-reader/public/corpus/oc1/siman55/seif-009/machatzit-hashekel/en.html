(Seif katan 9) He combines, etc. The Shechinah rests only upon adults, but the fact that he cannot respond does not concern us. Taz, seif katan 4, disagrees with the ruling concerning one who is sleeping and holds that he does not combine. See there for his reasoning. In any event, he writes that it is possible to awaken him, so why should we combine him while he is asleep?
