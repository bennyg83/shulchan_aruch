(39) Two hairs. Their location and the measure of their adulthood are explained in Even HaEzer, siman 155, seifim 17 and 18.
(40) After he will be. For Chazal received as a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai that a male is not called a man until he completes thirteen years and also produces two hairs. This is included among all the measures that Chazal received as a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai. However, we rule that once they reach the proper years, there is a presumption that they produced two hairs. Therefore even if we see that they do not have two hairs, we are concerned that perhaps they had them and they fell out, for such hairs sometimes fall out because of thinness and sometimes because of fullness. Nevertheless, we do not rely on this presumption completely, but judge him as a doubt. Therefore we always rule stringently in every matter that is D’Oraisa, and leniently in D’Rabbanan matters. See below in siman 199, Magen Avraham, seif katan 8; it requires some study. See above in seif katan 31 what we wrote there in the name of the Peri Megadim.
(41) Thirteen years old. His father is believed about this, but not another person, as stated in Kiddushin 64a in Tosafot, s.v. ne’eman. If his father died and he does not know when he became thirteen years old, seemingly the law is that once he produced two hairs he must be careful in mitzvos according to the Torah like any other adult, for it is a doubt in a Torah matter; see further.
(42) And one day. This does not mean specifically a full day; rather, once the beginning of the day of his fourteenth year has entered, even one hour or one moment suffices. Even if he was born at the end of the day of Rosh Hashanah, since it was certainly still day, he becomes thirteen years old at the beginning of the night of Rosh Hashanah of his fourteenth year, as the later authorities write. If the birth was at bein hashmashos, we judge him as a doubt and rule stringently in every matter that is D’Oraisa.
