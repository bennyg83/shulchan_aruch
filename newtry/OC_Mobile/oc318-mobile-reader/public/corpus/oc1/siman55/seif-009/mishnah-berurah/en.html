(39) Two hairs—and their place and the measure of their size is explained in Even HaEzer siman 155 se'if yod zayin and yod chet.

(40) After he will be—for thus the Sages received halachah leMoshe miSinai that a male is not called a man until he completes thirteen years and also brings two hairs; and this is among all the measures the Sages received halachah leMoshe miSinai. However, we hold: since they reached the full measure of years there is a presumption they brought two hairs; therefore even if we see they do not have two hairs we are concerned perhaps they had them but they fell out, for such hairs often fall out sometimes from weakness and sometimes from oiliness; nevertheless we do not rely entirely on this presumption but judge it as a doubt; therefore we always go to stringency in every de'oraita matter and to leniency in derabbanan. And see later in siman 199 in Magen Avraham se'if kaf het, and it is uncertain; and see above in se'if kaf lamed alef what we wrote in the name of Peri Megadim.

(41) A son of thirteen years—and his father is believed regarding this and not another man; so it is in Kiddushin 64a in the Tosefta "ne'eman." And if his father died and he does not know when he became a son of thirteen years—apparently his law is: since he brought two hairs he is obligated to be careful in Torah commandments like any other adult, for a doubt in de'oraita matters [and see].

(42) And one day—not specifically a day, but once the beginning of the day of the fourteenth year has entered, even one hour or even one moment suffices; and even if he was born at the end of Rosh Hashanah day, since it is still certainly day, he becomes a thirteen-year-old at the beginning of the night of Rosh Hashanah of the fourteenth year [later authorities]. And if he was born at bein hashemashot, we judge him as a doubt and go to stringency in every de'oraita matter.
