They do not, etc. — for there is a partition, and an opening is like a partition; and the same applies in a large and small courtyard that was not breached — so Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote, page 28; and Perishah's language: for there is a partition and a door that can be closed, and it is considered closed. And what he wrote in seif 20 — an idolater interrupts — is explained in Beit Yosef in the name of Yerushalmi, who wrote a Samaritan interrupts; behold an idolater is like idolaters themselves — and not like Magen Avraham [s.k. 15].
