16) An apostate to idolatry or public Shabbos desecration, or one who transgresses even one matter specifically to provoke, is like a non-Jew and does not join. Se'if 11, which says that a transgressor joins, concerns one who sins out of appetite; otherwise he does not. Pri Megadim, ibid. See what I wrote in Kol Yaakov 39:11; Yoreh Deah 2:5; Rav Zevach Tzedek's comments there on that se'if; Rav Pe'alim 2:11 and 3:12; and below, paragraph 62.
