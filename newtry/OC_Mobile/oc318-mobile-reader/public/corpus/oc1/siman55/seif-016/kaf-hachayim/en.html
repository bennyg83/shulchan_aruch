(16) An apostate — for idolatry or desecration of Shabbos, or to anger in one matter, behold he is like a non-Jew and does not combine. And in seif 11, a transgressor combines — that refers to appetite; if not so, no. Peri Megadim there. And see what is written in Kol Yaakov to siman 54 letter 11; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 2 seif 5; and what Rabbi Chaim Berlin wrote in Sefer Zivchei Tzedek there in Yoreh De'ah on the seif mentioned; and Rav Pe'alim part 2 siman 11; and part 3 siman 12. And further see below letter 62.
