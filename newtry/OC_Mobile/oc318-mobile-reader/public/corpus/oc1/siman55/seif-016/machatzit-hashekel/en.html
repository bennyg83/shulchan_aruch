(Seif katan 16) Half, etc. One who examines, etc. The correct reading is: In this case they pay equally.
All this is regarding prayer, etc. Since they need, etc. Meaning, his connection with the public is a complete connection, because they need him and he needs them. This differs from an esrog, where their partnership is not a complete connection, since each person could purchase his own esrog. See siman 658, where another reason is cited in the name of Maharil, namely that payment for an esrog is assessed according to wealth because the wealthy person derives greater benefit than the poor person. See there. Accordingly, payment follows the benefit received. If one leaves the city, why should he be required to pay when he derived no benefit, since he left? It must therefore be that the payment is assessed per capita, whereas for an esrog payment is not assessed per capita.
