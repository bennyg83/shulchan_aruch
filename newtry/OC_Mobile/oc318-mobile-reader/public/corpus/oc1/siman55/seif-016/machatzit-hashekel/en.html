(Seif katan 16) Half, etc. The source is that in this they pay equally, as it should read.

And all this regarding prayer, etc.—since they need one another. Meaning: his connection with the many is a complete connection, for they need him and he needs them—unlike an etrog, where their connection is not a complete connection, for if he wishes the buyer takes only an etrog by itself. And see siman 658 that he wrote in the name of Maharil another reason: for an etrog they pay according to wealth, for the rich man benefits more than the poor man, etc. If so, the payments are according to benefit; and when one goes outside the city, if he must pay and did not benefit from it—for he went out, etc.—nevertheless he pays according to persons; but for an etrog they do not pay according to persons.
