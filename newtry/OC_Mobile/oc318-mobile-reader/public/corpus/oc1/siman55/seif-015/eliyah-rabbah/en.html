And some of them are outside, etc. The Perishah wrote that this teaches a novelty: although some are here and some are there, and one might have thought that he is considered shaliach tzibbur either for those outside or for those inside, it teaches that we consider him for both here and there. The reason is that since all of them have their intention upon him, that he should discharge them, we combine him with these and with those. So it also appears that if nine, or the majority, are outside or inside, and the shaliach tzibbur stands on the threshold, he can discharge them. This applies specifically to the shaliach tzibbur, for this reason. But if someone else stands on the threshold, not only do we not consider him for both these and those, but even if nine are inside, he does not combine with them, for from the threshold outward is like outside. This requires study.
