Seif 15. He combines them, and all the more so where the nine are either inside or outside and the chazzan stands within the doorway, since he can discharge their obligation. The case in seif 17 is different, because he is in the larger courtyard and is separated from the congregation. This applies specifically to the chazzan, upon whom they direct their attention. If an ordinary individual stands in the doorway while nine are inside or outside, they do not combine. Perishah.
