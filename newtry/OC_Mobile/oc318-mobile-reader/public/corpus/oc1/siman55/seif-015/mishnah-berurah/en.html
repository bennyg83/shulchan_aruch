(54) He combines them. Since he is the shaliach tzibbur, each one directs his attention to him and he connects them together. All the more so if nine are inside and he is in the doorway, he joins with them. The case in seif 17 is different, for since he is in the large courtyard, he is distant from the congregation. But another person who is not the shaliach tzibbur, even if he alone is on the threshold of the house, does not join with them, as above in seif 13. All this is specifically when they do not see one another, but if some see one another, in every case they combine with one another. See below in seif 18, seif katan 57.
