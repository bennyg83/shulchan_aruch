(54) He joins them—for since he is the shaliach tzibbur, each gives his attention to him and he connects them together; and all the more so if nine are inside and he is within the doorway, he combines with them. And the case in se'if 17 is different, for since he is in the large courtyard he is considered separate from the congregation. And with another person who is not the shaliach tzibbur, even if he alone is on the threshold of the house he does not combine with them, as above in se'if 13. And all this is specifically when they do not see one another; but if some see one another, in any case they combine with one another. And see below in se'if 18, se'if kaf zayin.
