(15) An androgynus — there is no side to permit combining him for a minyan of ten. Responsa Orach LaTzadik simanim 2, 3, 4, 5 — not like one who formed the opinion that he combines. Siddur Beit Yosef letter 22. Ikkei HaDat there letter 18; and so Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham letter 4 — that tumtum and androgynus do not combine, and it is not a doubt lest there be nine and a woman or slave combine and perhaps he is male; for according to us certainly slave and woman do not combine, and likewise a half-slave does not combine — see Magen Avraham 128 and Knesset HaGedolah; and the matter of free men teaches thus — so far. And even though Rabbi Yeshuot Yaakov wrote that a half-slave and half-free man combines, for he comes into the category of one obligated in mitzvot, that we compel his master and make him a free man — see there; nevertheless one should be stringent. And if the tumtum was torn and found male, one may validate him, as written in Kol Yaakov to siman 54 letter 8 — see there.
