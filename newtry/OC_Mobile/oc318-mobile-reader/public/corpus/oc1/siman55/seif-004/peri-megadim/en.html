Or the Taz — one who examines Beit Yosef could say that Mahari Bei Rav did not learn this from "the praying one" but rather since in Gemara Megillah chapter on prayer, letter tet, it learns "the praying one" from Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi — that even one who disputes Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi regarding a minor, here regarding one asleep who is an adult who ate in a place of ten the Shechinah rests — and holds that the praying one does not hear and remain silent, for it is like responding and is an interruption (Av Beit Din in siman 124 on this). If so, even according to us that the praying one — certainly it is fine that he hears and remains silent (and it is also possible that fit for soaking applies); unlike one asleep — nevertheless from Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi he learns it, as said see there. And what he wrote "all that is fit for soaking" — specifically ex post facto, not lekhatchilah; see Menachot 18 on this, and there lekhatchilah the Torah said it should be soaked; and see Kiddushin 47:25 letter alef; and see Hilchot Tefillin siman 32 in Beit Yosef on Rashba that adhesion between letter and letter is even lekhatchilah — no fixing is needed for all that is fit for soaking; but according to us it is invalid if twenty-seven days' time it was not dragged, for we require surrounded by parchment — and there elsewhere I will explain further. And what he wrote from siman 125 se'if dalet — likewise Peri Chadash agreed with Taz, and it implies that one praying person they may allow and he may remain silent at the end of the blessing and listen to the chazzan at the end of the blessing; see siman 124 se'if dalet and Magen Avraham letter chet and Sha'arei Teshuvah there, and there it will be explained. Further Peri Chadash wrote proof from Even HaEzer 136 for Rosh: sleep does not divorce like a shotah and deaf-mute is considered likewise for the minyan; however for RAN and Rashba there the reason is that they do not guard — however in Shulchan Aruch there se'if 63 in the gloss the Rosh concedes, as Beit Yosef wrote there at the end of the siman — for otherwise go support the agent of divorce perhaps she was asleep at the time of his receiving — and one need not elaborate. Certainly lekhatchilah it is proper to wake him; and if impossible, one who relies on Shulchan Aruch did not lose. And it is possible in a place where they pray Kaddish and kedushah aloud and the rest silently — the prayer does not repeat, for now there is no difficulty from siman 124. And see what Magen Avraham wrote there in letter chet on this.
