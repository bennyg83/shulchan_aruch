Seif 64, there are those who permit, etc. From what is written in Berachot 47b–48a: even though they said "a minor lying in a cradle," etc., Rabbi Yehuda said "a minor who has sprouted," etc., "we taught," etc., "and the halakhah is not," etc.; and Rashi there explained "a minor who has sprouted" — a minor who brought forth hairs; and Tosafot refuted his explanation — for if so, what does it come to include, etc.; and he explained in the name of Rabbenu Chananel: in the thirteenth year he brought forth hairs, but they are not enough to bend the head to the root — and this is what "sprouted" means, that the hairs sprouted; see there s.v. ketan, etc.; and so explained all the geonim and commentators; and he further explained what is written there "and the halakhah is not," etc., but, etc. — meaning, both require it; and so too Rosh in the name of Rabbenu Chananel and Rif in the name of geonim — except that he explained one need not hairs to have sprouted, but Rif explained one need not even years, for even a nine- or ten-year-old; and so Rambam, and he wrote eight or seven; and Rivash wrote they took the age of children; and so too more than six years — for whoever is less than that, his knowledge is not knowledge, as written in Gittin 59a. Rabbeinu Tam explained that what is written "and the halakhah is not" does not refer to the first two statements of Rabbi Yehuda le-Bavel, for the Gemara interrupted after them with other matters, and there it is for ten, and here for three; and he wrote that so too for prayer they combine a minor lying in a cradle — they are equal from what is challenged there from a baraita, etc.; and nevertheless the poskim disagree with Rabbeinu Tam; and Rabbi Yehuda explained one does not combine neither for prayer nor for Birkat HaMazon, whether for ten or for three, until they bring two hairs, as written in Yerushalmi there: from when do they make him a branch? Rabbi Avina said they disagree — Rabbi Huna and Rabbi Yehuda, both in the name of Shmuel: one — so that he know the nature of blessing; one — so that he know whom he blesses. Rabbi Yehuda said: how many times I ate with Tahlifa Abba and with Rabbi Chanina bar Sisi my beloved, and they did not invite me until I brought two hairs; and there it deals with ten, as written there explicitly, see there; and nevertheless he wrote that one who has sprouted and knows whom they bless combines even for three — for in this there is no one who disagrees; and Tosafot there s.v. velit, etc. But from the words of Rosh and Tur it implies a minor does not combine at all, and they relied on the Yerushalmi cited above and explained specifically after thirteen years, as written in the chapter Yotzei Dofen; and even though our Gemara disagrees with Yerushalmi, the custom of the world follows Yerushalmi — Magen Avraham; and as written, "wherever the halakhah is weak," etc.; and understand that this entire passage is all regarding ten, and it argues against Rabbi Yehuda le-Bavel, as written in Tosafot s.v. ketan — but for three, no; and as written in Yerushalmi there: Rabbi Simon in the name of Rabbi Yehuda le-Bavel, Rabbi Yossi ben Shaul in the name of Rabbi: a minor — they make him a branch for ten, etc. Rabbi Barchia, Rabbi Yaakov bar Zavdi asked Rabbi Yossi bar Rabbi Avin: there, a minor — they make him a branch for ten; say also here they make him a branch for three. What — there, where they mention the Omnipresent, they make him a branch; here, where they do not mention the Omnipresent, all the more so? He said to him: it is not all the more so — there, where they mention the Omnipresent, they make him a branch; here, where they do not mention the Omnipresent, they do not make him a branch, etc. From when do they make, etc., as above; and all this is according to Rabbi Ashi on 48b; but we hold like Mar Zutra, that ten are stricter than three, as Rosh wrote in the name of Rabbi Chaim on Rabbeinu Tam's ruling cited above; and so in the gloss in siman 199 seif 10; but in Shulchan Aruch there he ruled like Rif and Rambam — whoever reached the age of children and knows whom he blesses combines, whether for three or for ten, not like Rabbeinu Tam who distinguishes as above; and according to this reasoning, those who permit here; and as Rabbeinu Tam wrote, prayer and Birkat HaMazon are equal.

And they did not appear, etc. For specifically for Birkat HaMazon, but not for prayer, as written in seif 1; and so in Mishnat Hasidim, chapter 16; but kedushah, etc., fewer than ten, and a minor does not count toward the minyan for Barchu, keriat haTorah, and Kaddish until he is thirteen — and this is Rambam's language and Tur, even though Rambam holds that for Birkat HaMazon he combines; and so many poskim; and as written in Bereishit Rabbah chapter 91 after the statement cited above in Yerushalmi: this teaches they make a branch for Birkat HaMazon, but for Keriat Shema and prayer they do not make him a branch until he brings two hairs — and it is brought in Tosafot there, not like Rabbeinu Tam.

And so too, etc. Not like Rabbi Yehuda le-Bavel, not like Rabbeinu Tam.

And a woman. For her law is as a slave in all places.

And even, etc. Meaning: even though they say in Yerushalmi and Bereishit Rabbah there: they taught "a minor and a Torah scroll — they make him a branch" — it concludes there: Rabbi Yudan said, thus the baraita: a minor for a Torah scroll they make him a branch, as written below siman 282 seif 3; and we do not find combining a Torah scroll except in a leap year, as written in Pirkei Rabbi Eliezer; Tosafot there; and other poskim.

Nevertheless, etc. Magen Avraham; and even without a Chumash — and so those who permit cited above.
