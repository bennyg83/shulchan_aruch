There are those who permit saying a spoken part [of the service] in which [God's] holiness [is proclaimed] (ie. Kaddish, Kedusha, etc.) with 9 men joined by a child that is older than 6 and knows to whom we pray. And their "words were not seen" (ie. not accepted) by the great halachic decisors. And the same law applies: that a slave or a woman is not joined [to the 9]. {Rama: Rem"a: And even if he has a Chumash [printed Torah] in his hand, we do not join him [to the 9]. However, there are those who are lenient [regarding this] in an extenuating circumstance. [The Rosh and Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoni Chapter 9 of the Laws of Prayer]}
