There are those who permit saying a matter of kedushah with nine joined by a minor who is more than six years old and knows to whom we pray. Their words were not accepted by the great decisors. The same' applies: a slave or a woman does not combine.And even if he has a Chumash in his hand we do not combine him. However, some are lenient in a time of pressure [Rosh; Mordechai; Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 9 of Hilchot Tefillah].]:
