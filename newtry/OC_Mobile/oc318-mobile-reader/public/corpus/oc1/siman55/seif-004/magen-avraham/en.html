With nine and combining a minor — however regarding Torah reading, some say they combine him for ten since he ascends for the count of seven [Tashbetz]; and at the end of siman 229 it implies one should be uncertain in the matter.

In a time of pressure — even without a Chumash, thus in Darkei Moshe; however specifically one and not two. And Levush wrote he did not see people act to combine him even in a time of pressure; however in our times people act to combine him through a Chumash in his hand; however specifically to hear Barchu and Kaddish, which is obligatory; but the Kaddish after Aleinu they should not say.
