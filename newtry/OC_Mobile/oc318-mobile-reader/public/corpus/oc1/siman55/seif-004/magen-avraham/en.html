In pressing circumstances. Even without a Chumash, as stated in Darchei Moshe. However, this is only one minor and not two. The Levush wrote that he had not seen the practice to join him even in pressing circumstances. In our times, however, the practice is to join him by means of a Chumash in his hand, but only in order to hear Barchu and Kaddish that are obligatory; they should not say the Kaddish after Aleinu.
