Their words do not appear correct. Although below, in siman 199, seif 10, regarding zimun, the Mechaber rules anonymously like those who are lenient in this, a matter of sanctity is more stringent.
The same applies to a slave and a woman, etc. The wording is somewhat difficult, for they do not combine even according to the permissive opinion mentioned above. So it is clear from the Levush, and likewise from the Beur HaGra and the Peri Megadim. For that permissive opinion is not Rabbeinu Tam's view, which distinguishes between three and ten, following Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, who holds that one may be lenient regarding ten with respect to a minor and a slave. According to Rabbeinu Tam, even a minor lying in a cradle combines, as stated in the Gemara. Rather, their view is that three and ten are equal, only that even regarding three itself the permissive opinion maintains that for zimun a minor who knows to Whom we bless may combine, as the Shulchan Aruch rules below in siman 199, seif 10, and they hold that one should not distinguish between zimun and other matters of sanctity.
