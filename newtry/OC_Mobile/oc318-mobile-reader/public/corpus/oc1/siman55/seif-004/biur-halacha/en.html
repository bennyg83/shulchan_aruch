And their words do not seem correct — and even though below in siman 199 seif 10 regarding zimmun the author follows the lenient view in this, nevertheless a matter of kedushah is stricter.

And the same applies to a servant and a woman, etc. — the wording is somewhat puzzling, for they do not combine even per the lenient view mentioned above [as is proven in Levush and in Beur HaGra and in Peri Megadim, for that lenient view is not the opinion of Rabbeinu Tam, who distinguishes between three and ten, and of Rabbenu Yerucham, who holds one may be lenient for ten as for a minor and a servant; for according to Rabbeinu Tam even a minor lying in a cradle combines, as stated in the Gemara — rather their view is that three and ten are equivalent, only that for three itself they also hold the lenient view, that it is permitted for zimmun with a minor who knows to whom they bless; and as Shulchan Aruch ruled below in siman 199 seif 10; and he holds one should not distinguish between zimmun and other matters of kedushah].
