Pressure. Even without a Chumash—and specifically one and not two; and in Levush he wrote he did not see people join even in a time of pressure, and in our time people join by means of a Chumash in his hand; however, specifically to hear Barekhu and Kaddish, which is an obligation, but the Kaddish after Aleinu they do not say—Magen Avraham. Beit Yosef wrote: this joining by Chumash is specifically when written on parchment like a sefer Torah, but our chumashim, according to his view, do not join; and Ateret Zekenim in the name of his grandfather the Gaon z"l, halakhah lema'aseh, not to join a minor. Onen does not join for ten for any matter of kedushah—as Shakhnehag and likewise Hagahot Maimoniyot this siman; and likewise Peri Chadash chelek 1 siman 19; and responsa Shevut Yaakov chelek 2 siman 25; and Pri Chadash wrote: if he has someone who will strive on his behalf, if he wishes to read [Torah] they do not protest—it stands to reason he certainly joins, etc.; and see Leket haKemach daf 10b.
