Pressing circumstance. Even without a Chumash, and specifically one minor, not two. The Levush wrote that he had not seen the practice to include him even in a pressing circumstance. In our times the practice is to include him by means of a Chumash in his hand. However, this is specifically for hearing Barchu and Kaddish, which are obligatory, but the Kaddish after Aleinu should not be said. Magen Avraham. The Bach wrote that this inclusion by means of a Chumash applies only when it is written on gevil like a Torah scroll, but according to all opinions our Chumashim do not combine. The Eliyah Zuta wrote in the name of his grandfather, the gaon, of blessed memory, as practical halacha, not to include a minor. An onen does not join with ten for any matter of sanctity. Knesses HaGedolah and Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah in this siman, and so wrote Peri Chadash, part 1, siman 19, and Teshuvos Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 25. The Peri Chadash wrote that if he has someone to attend to the burial for him, in which case if he wished to recite, we do not protest against him, it is certainly reasonable that he joins; see there.
