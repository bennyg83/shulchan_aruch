(Seif katan 4) With nine, etc. And at the end of siman 690 it implies there is room to be uncertain in the matter—for Rashbatz learned from what they go up for a minyan of seven readers that one called is somewhat obligated and should be joined to a minyan of ten.

Therefore Magen Avraham—for in Megillah women are certainly obligated in its reading, for they too were in that miracle, as written in siman 689. Nevertheless according to what requires that the Megillah be read with ten, Rama in siman 690 that there is room to be uncertain whether women join; and in Magen Avraham there he wrote that there is a dispute in Tur about this, etc.

By means of a Chumash. And Beit Chadash wrote that even for the opinion that it helps by Chumash, it must be written on a scroll like a sefer Torah, as Tosafot wrote—unlike our chumashim. And therefore a sefer Torah would be needed in the minor's hand. But in Sefer Ateret Zekenim in the name of his grandfather Maharash he wrote that even in a time of pressure he did not want to rely on this; and likewise in responsa Shevut Yaakov siman 6. And he wrote that Rama too does not mean to rely on this even though he wrote "some practice," etc. For in siman 199 seif 6 Beit Yosef wrote regarding a minor that they invite upon him and he joins whether for three or for ten; and Rama wrote: and some say they do not join until he is thirteen years old, etc., and so is practiced and one should not change, etc. Behold Rama himself wrote regarding Birkat haMazon that one should not join him whether for three or for ten and one should not change. And one might say he holds prayer is lighter than Birkat haMazon—on the contrary, Beit Yosef holds the opposite, that Birkat haMazon is lighter, for regarding prayer Beit Yosef wrote plainly that one should not join him, and regarding Birkat haMazon he wrote that he joins whether for three or for ten. How can we say Rama's reasoning would be opposite to Beit Yosef? Rather Rama holds prayer and Birkat haMazon are equal and in both one does not join. And so wrote responsa Shevut Yaakov: also in siman 55 he agrees that one should not join even by Chumash; only he wrote: however, some practice, etc.—meaning those who practice thus rely on the view the author brought in a time of pressure, but Rama did not write that those who practice thus act properly and relied on himself on siman 459:2, etc.—and he expanded; I shortened. And see what Taz wrote seif katan 3; look in Magen Avraham siman 234 that he disagrees.
