Magen Avraham, seif katan 3. The order of Kedushah and Kaddish. Meaning that they do not belong to Kerias Shema and its blessings. This is surprising: what is he teaching us? One may say that he teaches the precision, that it is to Kerias Shema that they do not belong, but to prayer they do belong. Therefore, if they began the Shemoneh Esrei prayer on Motzaei Shabbos with ten and some left, he completes the order of Kedushah and the Kaddish, and so wrote the Peri Megadim, of blessed memory.
