Magen Avraham s.k. 3—kedushah section and Kaddish. Meaning: they do not belong to Kriat Shema and its blessings; and one might ask what he comes to teach us—and one may say he comes to teach the precision that for Kriat Shema they do not belong, but for prayer they do belong. And if they began the Shemoneh Esreh on Motza'ei Shabbat with ten and some left—they complete the kedushah section and Kaddish. And so Peri Megadim z"l.
