One does not say Kaddish at all except in known places in prayers, or after reading some matter of words of Torah, or some commentary or derash on a verse; but after the prayers that Rav Saadiah Gaon arranged and supplications of mercy one does not say Kaddish. The Rambam in his responsa in Sefer Pe'er HaDor, printed edition siman 130. And in Orach Chayyim page 96 top of column 4 he wrote from the R"am that one does not say Kaddish on Torah verses that do not need strengthening, and Ashrei they made like prayer which is from Scripture, etc.—see there. From the words of the Rambam cited it is proven the opposite; and also Raavad in a responsum that Maran z"l mentioned in Beit Yosef wrote that after a psalm they say Kaddish—see there. And so is the custom. Kaf HaChayyim letter 2. And in his book Kaf HaChayyim siman 8 letters 3 and 4. Shulchan Aruch HaRav page 81 column 2. Chayei Adam siman 54 letter 2. Yafeh Lelev letter 2. Therefore it is not proper what those do who say Kaddish after the milah without saying a psalm. Chayei Adam there. And the people practiced to say after all Torah shebe'al peh "Rabbi Chanina ben Akavya," etc., and they say the Rabbinic Kaddish; and after Torah shebekhetav they say "And the salvation of the righteous is from Hashem," etc., and they say Kaddish "May there be abundant peace."
