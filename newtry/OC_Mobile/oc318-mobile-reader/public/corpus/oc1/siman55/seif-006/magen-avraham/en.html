He combines with them — for whenever ten are gathered together the Divine Presence rests. And R' Eliezer Chaviv wrote in siman 15: and perhaps for this reason three or four also, and a majority remains — so far. And it seems to me one should not be so lenient, for behold the main proof of Maharil is since there are those who say a minor lying in a cradle combines, even though he cannot answer; and even though we do not hold thus — that is because the Divine Presence does not rest except on adults, etc. — see there. And if so, it should follow from the law to be as discussed: what about a minor specifically one and not two, likewise regarding one who sleeps specifically one and not two. And Maharil wrote: even though Rosh wrote we require that nine intend for the blessing of the shaliach tzibbur, as written in siman 124 seif 4 — we do not act thus, for we see that even one who speaks and does not hear combines.
