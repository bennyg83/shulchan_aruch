And Avudraham wrote regarding the Minchah prayer: if there were ten when one begins Ashrei and some left, one says Kaddish—for Ashrei is like an obligation, as Tur wrote. See there. From this it is explained that so too for the evening prayer: if there were ten at the hour when they began to pray the silent Shemoneh Esreh and some left in the middle of the prayer, one may say the full Kaddish after prayer, for Kaddish belongs to prayer. And similarly in responsa Noda Biyehudah Mahadura Tinyana siman 7, and similarly in Pri Megadim in section Eshel Avraham. See there. And see Yad Ephraim what he wrote on this regarding Tehillim, and what he wrote that in Tehillim they practiced leaving a psalm, etc.; and the same applies to study—see Avudraham—that one should be strict to study a little when the minyan arrives.
