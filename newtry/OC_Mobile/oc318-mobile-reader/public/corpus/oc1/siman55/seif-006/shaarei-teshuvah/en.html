“And its blessings.” See Baer Heitev. Eliyah Rabbah writes that at Minchah, if ten were present when they began Ashrei and some of them then left, Kaddish is recited, because Ashrei has the status of an obligation, as stated in the Tur; see there. It follows all the more so that at Maariv, if ten were present when they began the silent Amidah and some left in the middle of the prayer, the full Kaddish after the prayer may be recited, since that Kaddish pertains to the prayer. Responsa Noda BiYehudah, second edition, siman 7, writes likewise, as does the Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham; see there. See Yad Ephraim regarding what is stated here concerning Terumat HaDeshen. Regarding the statement that the custom when reciting Tehillim is to leave over one chapter of Tehillim, etc.: the same applies to study. Eliyah Rabbah writes that one should be stringent and study a little when the quorum arrives.
