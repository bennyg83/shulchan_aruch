(32) One of the ten. The same applies to more than one, provided that a majority remains who are not praying Shemoneh Esrei and can answer with them. Some are stringent with more than one. Regarding one who is asleep, certainly one should not join more than one even in pressing circumstances, for in any event many poskim are stringent regarding one who is asleep, as below.
(33) Or he is asleep. See the Peri Megadim, who wrote that the same applies regarding a zimun of ten: one who is asleep joins according to the Shulchan Aruch, as with prayer. Regarding Torah reading and the lifting of the hands, this requires study. However, regarding Megillah reading, certainly one who is asleep does not join ten according to all views, for the Megillah requires ten for publicizing the miracle, and with one asleep there is no publicity.
(34) He joins with them. For with any ten adults the Shechinah rests among them, and we read about them, I shall be sanctified among the Children of Israel. This is not comparable to a minor, who is not subject to sanctity. See the Taz, who disagrees regarding one who is asleep and holds that he does not join, and the Pri Chadash agrees with him, for one asleep is considered like one who lacks halachic competence; see there. Therefore initially one certainly must awaken him, or at least rouse him so that he is dozing. See Biur Halacha.
