(32) One of the ten—the same applies to more than one, provided a majority remain who are not praying silently and can answer with them; and some are strict regarding more than one. And regarding one who is asleep, certainly one should not combine even in the most pressing place more than one, for in any case there are many decisors who are strict regarding one who is asleep, as below.

(33) Or he is asleep—see Peri Megadim who wrote that likewise for the matter of zimun of ten for Birkat HaMazon, a sleeper also combines according to Shulchan Aruch, as for prayer; but for keriat haTorah and nesiat kapayim—uncertain; but for keriat haMegillah certainly a sleeper does not combine for ten according to all, for Megillah requires ten for publicizing the miracle, and in one who is asleep there is no publicity.

(34) Combines with them—for in every ten who are adults the Shekhinah rests among them, for "and I shall be sanctified among the children of Israel" is read regarding them; and it is not similar to a minor, who is not fit for sanctity. And see Taz who disputes regarding one who is asleep and holds he does not combine; Peri Chadash agreed with him that a sleeper is considered like a shoteh—see there; therefore lekhatchilah one certainly must wake him; and at minimum arouse him so he will be dozing. And see in Beit HaLevi.
