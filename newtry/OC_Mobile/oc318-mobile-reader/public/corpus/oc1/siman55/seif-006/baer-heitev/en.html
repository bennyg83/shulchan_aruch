Alone. Specifically one, and not two, unlike the Ralbach. Likewise regarding one who is asleep, specifically one, and not two or three; see Magen Avraham. The Taz disagrees and holds that even one who is asleep does not combine; see there. So too wrote the Peri Chadash, that the words of the Taz are primary; see there. In Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 270, he wrote that one who is asleep counts for a minyan only when his face is uncovered; see there. The Yad Aharon disagrees with him; see there. See siman 124, seif 4.
