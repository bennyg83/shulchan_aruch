Alone. Specifically one and not two—not like Harel Nach. Likewise regarding one who sleeps—specifically one and not two or three—Magen Avraham; and Taz disagrees and holds that one who sleeps, even one, does not join, etc.; and likewise Peri Chadash, that the words of Taz are primary, etc. In Leket Yosher chelek 1 siman 170 he wrote: this regarding one who sleeps counting for the minyan is when his face is uncovered, etc.; and Yad Aharon disagrees with him, etc.; and see siman 124 seif 4.
