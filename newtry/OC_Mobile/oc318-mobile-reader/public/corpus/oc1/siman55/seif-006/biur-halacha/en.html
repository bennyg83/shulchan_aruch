Or he is sleeping — see Mishna Berurah what we wrote, that lekhatchilah one should in any case wake him. And if that too is impossible, it is uncertain in practice; for although Peri Megadim wrote that if it is impossible, one who relies on Shulchan Aruch has not lost, and so Chayei Adam implies — nevertheless Birkei Yosef and Derech HaChaim agreed with Taz and Perach. And it is possible that in a place where they pray Kaddish and kedushah aloud and the rest silently, one may be lenient in a case of need regarding one who is sleeping, for there is no concern of a blessing in vain here; and so it implies from Peri Megadim that this is more lenient.
