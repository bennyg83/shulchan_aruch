Half — Taz and Peri Chadash on the Mechaber there: they pay from the community chest — here the poor person causes that one left and they need to hire another; and therefore the Mechaber too [says] half according to souls. Also per the view that in locales where there is no custom they collect according to wealth — in such places they want a chazzan of pleasant voice, unlike here; and see Rabbi Akiva Eiger and Magen Avraham letter 16, and there it will be explained. And what he wrote "the reasoning of according to wealth so they will not need" etc. — meaning the main enactment is so they will not need, etc. And Levush wrote: presumably the poor person's conduct is not like the rich to profit; and see Rabbi Akiva Eiger.

Regarding bar mitzvah — Magen Avraham letter 10; and wonder at Rama in Mishlei 8 verse 16 "one who sins against me wrongs his soul" — a minor: if one [year] he is liable death, two exempt in these see there well. And one born in a leap year on 28 Adar I — and in gematria "me'uberet" — and he had relations with this one: if he made her a zona, it is uncertain; and in the second likewise one could say rabbinic zona is uncertain to be lenient. Even HaEzer 7 and 21 see there.
