6) There: It is not recited with fewer than ten, etc. Scripture says, 'I shall be sanctified among the Children of Yisrael.' This is derived through the shared word 'among': here it says 'among,' and elsewhere it says, 'Separate yourselves from among the congregation.' A congregation contains no fewer than ten, as the verse concerning the spies says, 'How long shall I bear with this evil congregation?' Yehoshua and Calev are excluded, leaving ten, as explained in Megillah 23b. Bach; Taz 1; Birkei Yosef 3; and Shulchan Aruch HaRav 2. See below, paragraph 17.
