There: one does not say it with fewer than ten, etc.—for it is written "And I shall be sanctified among the children of Israel," and we derive "among" from "among": it is written here "among" and it is written there "Separate yourselves from the midst of the congregation"—and a congregation is not fewer than ten, as written regarding the spies: "Until when shall this evil congregation endure"—Joshua and Caleb remained, as stated in Megillah 23b.
