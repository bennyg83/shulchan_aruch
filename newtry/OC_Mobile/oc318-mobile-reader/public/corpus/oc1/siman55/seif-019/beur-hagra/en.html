Seif 19, the shaliach tzibbur, etc. As stated in Sukkah 51b, and see Rashi there, s.v. platform, etc. Presumably the shaliach tzibbur also stood on it to make himself heard to the people, except that because of those who were far away they waved cloths.
Although, etc. For that case in Eruvin cited above concerns places divided in their usage, but here there is one usage, namely the usage of a synagogue. A proof is from what is stated in Sotah 38b: it is obvious, etc.; the ark does not interrupt. Presumably the ark was large, as is done to make oneself heard to the people, and the same applies here. Furthermore, since they see one another, they combine even in two houses, as stated in the Yerushalmi regarding Birkas HaMazon and as above in seif 14, Rashba there. However, all these proofs can be rejected.
