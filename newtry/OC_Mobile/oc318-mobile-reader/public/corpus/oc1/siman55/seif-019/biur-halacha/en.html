The shaliach tzibbur in the platform, etc., they combine. See the Beur HaGra, who wrote that all the proofs brought by the Beit Yosef in the name of the Rashba for this can be rejected. Nevertheless, it appears to me that even according to the Beur HaGra one should be stringent initially only regarding combining. But regarding prayer with the congregation, as concerns the attendant who stands there, one may be lenient, for in any case the Radbaz is lenient regarding this even in an inner room of the house, as I brought in the Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 58; see there.
These words apply, etc. For they do not hold that it is subordinate to the synagogue. Therefore it is like two separate houses, and this is worse than a shaliach tzibbur in the smaller area with the congregation in the larger one, explained in seif 17, where they combine, for there the smaller area is breached in its entirety, unlike here. According to their view, the same applies if someone other than the shaliach tzibbur is inside the platform, that his law is also so, since without him there are not ten, as is proven in the Beit Yosef. But if the shaliach tzibbur is inside the platform, it is worse, for even if there are ten below without him, he still cannot discharge them in Kaddish and Kedushah, since there is no minyan in the place where he stands, as explained in the Peri Megadim, and I cited it in the Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 56.
