Shaliach tzibbur in the ark, etc., combine — see Beur HaGra, who wrote there is reason to reject all the proofs Beit Yosef brought in the name of Rashba for this; and nevertheless it seems to me that even per the Gra's view one need not be stringent lekhatchilah only for combining; but for prayer with the congregation, regarding the shamash standing there, one may be lenient, for otherwise Radbaz's view is to be lenient for this matter even in a room inside the house — as I brought in Mishna Berurah s.k. 58; see there.

These matters help, etc. — for he does not hold it is nullified relative to the synagogue; therefore it is like two houses by themselves, for this is worse than shaliach tzibbur in a small [room] and congregation in a large [hall], explained in seif 17 that they combine — there the small is breached in its full width, not so here; and according to their view, so too if another who is not shaliach tzibbur is inside the ark — his law is likewise, since without him there are not ten, as proven in Beit Yosef. But if the shaliach tzibbur is inside the ark it is worse — for even if there are ten below without him, he cannot discharge their obligation in Kaddish and kedushah, since there is no minyan in the place where he stands, as explained in Peri Megadim; I copied it in Mishna Berurah s.k. 56.
