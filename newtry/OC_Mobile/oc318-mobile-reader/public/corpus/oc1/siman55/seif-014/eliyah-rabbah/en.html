Even if it is high, etc. So too the entire thickness of the synagogue wall, when it is not roofed, is treated this way, as stated by Rabbeinu Yerucham. Regarding upper galleries that are not enclosed by a full wall, but by posts ten handbreadths high, with a large courtyard below toward which the galleries face, and they see one another, Sheyarei Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that they do not combine.
