(Seif katan 14) They join, etc., for standing before a sefer Torah—for one need not stand, since the sefer Torah is in a different domain (and at the time of reading, see below siman 146).

Nevertheless it is nullified, etc. And Peri Chadash wrote: and the law of nine in the large [synagogue] and an individual in the small, and the law of the shaliach tzibbur in the small and the congregation in the large—stands specifically when they do not see one another; for if they see one another in all cases they join. And what the author wrote in seif 19—that the shaliach tzibbur in the house and nine in the synagogue join because it is nullified relative to the synagogue—refers even when they do not see one another. Therefore a reason that it is nullified is needed, etc.

And see siman 370 seif 3 as it should read; and it refers to the view of some who wrote that the author brought their reason—that they compared it to what is written in siman 370 regarding eruv with five groups that rested in one hall: even though they divided among themselves with partitions, if the partitions do not reach the ceiling and there are three tefachim between the partitions and the ceiling so that lavud does not apply—all five groups are considered as one and they give only one eruv, etc.
