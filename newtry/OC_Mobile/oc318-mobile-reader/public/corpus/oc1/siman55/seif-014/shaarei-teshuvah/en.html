“An inner area.” See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef writes in the name of Malki BaKodesh that one whose house is behind the synagogue, with a small window between them through which he hears everything said by those praying there, is regarded as praying with the congregation. Regarding the Shulchan Aruch’s ruling in se’if 18 that if some are in the women’s courtyard they do not combine: R’ Shimon ben Tzemach, siman 37, writes this even when they can see one another. Machazik Berachah writes that the Rashba, siman 96, does not rule this way. The Rashba, however, writes only that it is possible.
