Inner lip. Ba'er Heitev. And see Rabbeinu Yonah, who wrote in the name of Malchei Bakodesh: one whose house is behind the synagogue and between them is a small window through which he hears everything the worshippers say—for prayer's sake he is considered as praying with the congregation (in Shulchan Aruch se'if 18: and some in the ezrah do not combine). And Rashbatz wrote siman 37: even if they see one another; and in Machatzit HaShekel he wrote that Rashba siman 96 did not say so, but Rashba wrote only as a possible leniency.
