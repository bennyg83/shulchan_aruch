(14) One who is drunk — if he has not reached Lot's drunkenness, it is fit to combine him for a minyan of ten for Kaddish and kedushah and Barchu, even though he cannot speak before the king. But if he reached Lot's drunkenness, behold he is like a shoteh and one does not combine him for any matter of kedushah. Responsa Beit Yehudah siman 45. And they brought Ikkei HaDat siman 3 or letter 3; Siddur Beit Yosef in laws of Kaddish letter 24; Petachei Olam letter 18. However, Shulchan Aruch HaRav page 79 column 3 wrote in the name of Rabbi Beit Yehudah the reverse — that if he cannot speak before the king he does not combine for ten — see there. And so Chayei Adam letter 8. It requires examination on their words; and see below siman 99 seif 1 in the gloss and what is written there. Magen Avraham in the gloss on Shulchan Aruch in the name of responsum Beit Yosef siman 45 — that if he cannot speak before the king he does not combine; and so Chida in Kaf HaChayyim siman 8 letter 7. Ben Ish Chai parashat Vayechi letter 6. However, Rabbi Beit Yosef there wrote they did not act so, but rather they combine him even though he is not fit to speak before the king.
