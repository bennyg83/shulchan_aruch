Magen Avraham, seif katan 3. Until twenty-nine have passed. For he was born on the thirtieth of Cheshvan, and therefore he does not become bar mitzvah until the beginning of the thirtieth of Cheshvan, as the Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 13. If the opposite occurred, that when he was born Rosh Chodesh was only one day, but now it is two days, such as Rosh Chodesh Teves, see Eliyah Rabbah.
