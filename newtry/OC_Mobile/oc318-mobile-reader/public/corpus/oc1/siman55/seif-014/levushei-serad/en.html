Magen Avraham s.k. 3—until twenty-nine pass. For he was born on the thirtieth of Cheshvan—and thus he does not become bar mitzvah until the thirtieth of Cheshvan begins, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 13. And if the case were reversed—that when he was born it was not Rosh Chodesh but one day, and now two days, such as Rosh Chodesh Tevet—see Ateret Zekenim.
