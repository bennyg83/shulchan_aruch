And between them a window. In the Gemara it implies that windows and the thickness of the wall have the law of roofs and upper chambers—they have it, and are not included in "house" unless they are level with the ground of the azarah. And since the poskim were silent in their words, it implies they hold that in a synagogue, since one shows his face from there, in every case he joins, as written in siman 195 seif 2.
