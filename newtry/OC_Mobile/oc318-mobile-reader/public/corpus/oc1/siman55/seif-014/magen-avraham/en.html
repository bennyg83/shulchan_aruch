And there is a window between them. In the Gemara it implies that windows and the thickness of the wall have the law of roofs and upper stories, and are not included in the house unless they are level with the floor of the Azarah. Since the poskim stated their words without qualification, it implies that they hold that in a synagogue, since he shows his face from there, he joins in every case, as written in siman 195, seif 2.
