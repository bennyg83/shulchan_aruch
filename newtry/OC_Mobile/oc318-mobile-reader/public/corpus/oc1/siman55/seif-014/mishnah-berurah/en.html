(52) Joins with them—for although there is an intervening partition between them, since his face is visible to them it is similar to what is explained below in siman 195 regarding zimmun, that if some see one another they combine; and if so, it is straightforward that those standing in the women's section and in an intervening partition, if there is a window and he shows his face to them from there, he joins with them for ten; and all the more so if besides him there are already ten, he is considered to have prayed with the congregation thereby. Nevertheless, it is better if it is easy for him to descend to the synagogue that he descend, for there are later authorities who dispute the fundamental law and hold that our case is not at all similar to zimmun.

(53) Roofs and attics—in such a case where they do not see one another, it would have been an unnecessary stringency for him to say even wide and low windows; whatever is not level with the ground of the synagogue, even if he stands within them, he does not combine.
