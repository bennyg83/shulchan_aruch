(52) He joins with them. Although there is a partition interrupting between them, since he shows them his face, it resembles what is explained below in siman 195 regarding zimun, that if some see one another, they combine. According to this, it is simple that those standing in the women’s section, when there is a window in the partition and he shows his face from there, join with them to make ten. All the more so, if there are ten without him, it is considered prayer with the congregation by this. Nevertheless, if it is easy for him to descend to the synagogue, it is better that he descend, for some later authorities disagree with the essential law and hold that our matter is not comparable to zimun at all.
(53) Roofs and upper stories. In such a case where they do not see one another, it should have stated an even greater novelty: even wide and low windows, as long as they are not level with the synagogue floor, even if he stands within them, he does not join.
