14) A drunk person: if he has not reached Lot's degree of intoxication, it is proper to include him in a minyan for Kaddish, Kedushah, and Barchu, even if he cannot speak before a king. If he has reached Lot's degree of intoxication, he is like an insane person and may not be included for any sacred matter. Responsa Beis Yehudah 45, cited by Ikrei HaDat 3:3; Siddur Beis Yaakov, laws of Kaddish 24; and Pischei Olam 18. Shalmei Tzibbur, page 79c, however, cites Beis Yehudah to the opposite effect: if he cannot speak before a king, he does not join ten; see there. Chesed LaAlafim 8 writes likewise. Their words require further study. See below, siman 99:1 in the gloss, and Eshel Avraham on the margin of Shulchan Aruch there, citing Responsa Beis Yehudah 45, that one unable to speak before a king does not join. Chida, Kesher Gudal 8:7, and Ben Ish Chai, Vayechi 6, rule likewise. Siddur Beis Yaakov, however, writes that this is not the accepted custom; rather, he is included even if unfit to speak before a king.
