The Levush wrote that one never says titkabel tzalotehon except after they have said the Shemoneh Esreh prayer—see there; and they brought Taz s.k. 1, who wrote on this that it is not an absolute rule, for behold on selichot days in the morning watch they say titkabel after selichot, for there too are many requests like in the Shemoneh Esreh, etc. And that one does not say titkabel immediately after the Shemoneh Esreh prayer is because the kedushah section follows prayer—since it belongs to the matter of prayer and praise—therefore they refrain from saying titkabel until after the kedushah section, so that it also applies to the kedushah section. Therefore on a day when there is Musaf they say a full Kaddish with titkabel immediately after the Shacharit prayer, and after the Musaf prayer they say a full Kaddish with titkabel again on the Musaf prayer, which likewise stands on the kedushah section. Levush in the gloss. Etz Chayyim. And see below siman 423.
