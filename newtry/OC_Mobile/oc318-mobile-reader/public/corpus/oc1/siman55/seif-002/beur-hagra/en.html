Seif 2, if he began, etc. The Yerushalmi there says: since we learned that one does not divide the Shema with fewer than ten, did we not also learn that one does not pass before the ark? Therefore it was necessary for that which we learned: one does not divide the Shema with fewer than ten; if they began with ten and some left, he finishes. One does not pass before the ark with fewer than ten; if they began with ten and some left, he finishes. One does not lift, etc.; if they began, etc. One does not read, etc. About all of them it says, Those who forsake Hashem will perish. Meaning, the Mishnah says that each one is a matter by itself, and they finish only that matter. This is what is meant by one does not pass, etc., even though at the start of dividing the Shema there were ten, and similarly for all of them. This is the meaning of that, etc.
But if, etc. So it is in the Yerushalmi cited above.
