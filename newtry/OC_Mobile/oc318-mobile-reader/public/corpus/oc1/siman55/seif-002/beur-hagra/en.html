Seif 62, if he began, etc. Yerushalmi there: since we taught "we do not spread [Hands] over Shema with fewer than ten" — did we not teach "we do not pass before the ark"? Therefore this teaching "we do not spread over Shema with fewer than ten" is needed: if they began with ten and some left — may one finish? We do not pass before the ark with fewer than ten. If they began with ten and some left — may one carry [the Torah]? If they began — may one read? On all of them it says "and those who forsake the the Omnipresent shall be consumed." Meaning: the baraita says each matter is a matter by itself, and one does not finish except that same matter. And this is what "we do not pass" means — even though at the start of spreading Shema there were ten; and so with all of them; and this is what "that same" means, etc.

But if etc. So too in the aforementioned Yerushalmi.
