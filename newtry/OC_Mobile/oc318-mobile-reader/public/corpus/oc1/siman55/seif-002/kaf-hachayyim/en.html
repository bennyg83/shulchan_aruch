2) Levush writes that 'Tiskabel tzeloshon' is never recited except after the Amidah; see there. Taz 1 cites this and objects that it is not an absolute rule: during Selichos before dawn, Tiskabel is recited after Selichos, because they too contain many petitions like the Amidah. Tiskabel is not recited immediately after the Amidah because U'Va LeTziyon is drawn after the prayer, since it relates to prayer and praise. We therefore delay saying Tiskabel until after U'Va LeTziyon so that it applies to that order as well. Consequently, on a day with Mussaf, a full Kaddish with Tiskabel is recited immediately after Shacharis; after Mussaf, a second full Kaddish with Tiskabel is recited for Mussaf, and it also applies back to U'Va LeTziyon. Levush in the gloss; Ateres Zekeinim. See below, siman 423.
