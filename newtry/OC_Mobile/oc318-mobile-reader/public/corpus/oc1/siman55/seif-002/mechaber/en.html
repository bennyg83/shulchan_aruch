If the chazzan began to say Kaddish or kedushah with ten and some left, he may finish that Kaddish or that kedushah that he began, provided a majority remain.Nevertheless it is a transgression to leave, and regarding this it is said: "and those who forsake Hashem shall perish"] [Yerushalmi]. But if ten remain' it is permitted to leave [Mordechai, last chapter of Megillah].]:
