(ibid.) In Magen Avraham s.k. 11 and in Sefer Tanya he ruled that it is as inside. See there, for he brought proof from Shabbos daf 9 amud alef — a threshold serves two domains — and this proof is not decisive in my humble opinion. And see Maseches Shabbos daf 124 amud alef, and it requires study; and see Eruvin daf 8 amud bet — all agree regarding wine between lives that it is forbidden; and see below siman 348 seif 4, and it requires study. It is also written in Magen Avraham, and so it implies in the Gemara — namely in Pesachim daf 25 amud bet — specifically the gates of Jerusalem, and because of lepers their interior was not sanctified. And it seems to me that Rambam ruled regarding those eating Pesachim inside the doorway as outside — the reason being that it was necessary to sanctify the interior of the Courtyard, whereas the gates of Jerusalem that were not sanctified because of lepers were not sanctified at all; learn from this that ordinarily they are as outside. And in my humble opinion there is clear proof from the precision of the mishnah in Nedarim daf 56 amud bet; and one cannot say we follow people's ordinary language, for otherwise the Talmud would not question there.
