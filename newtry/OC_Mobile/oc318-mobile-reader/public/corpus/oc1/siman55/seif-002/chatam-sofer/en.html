Regarding Magen Avraham, seif katan 11, and Sefer Tanya, which rules that it is considered inside: Note, see there, where proof is brought from Shabbos 9a, that a threshold serves two domains. In my humble opinion, this proof is not conclusive. See Shabbos 124a, and this requires study. See also Eruvin 8b, where all agree that the area between the lechayayim is forbidden, and see below, siman 348, seif 4; this requires study. Magen Avraham further writes, “and so it appears from the Gemara,” meaning Pesachim 85b, specifically regarding the gates of Yerushalayim, whose interiors were not sanctified because of the metzoraim. It appears to me that the Rambam rules that, for those eating the Korban Pesach, the area within the doorway is considered outside for the following reason: it was necessary expressly to sanctify the interior of the Azarah, whereas the gates of Yerushalayim, which were not sanctified because of the metzoraim, remained unsanctified as a matter of course. This shows that an ordinary doorway is presumed to be outside. In my humble opinion, there is clear proof from the precise wording of the Mishnah in Nedarim 56b. One cannot say that there we follow ordinary human usage, for if so the Gemara would not have raised its question there.
