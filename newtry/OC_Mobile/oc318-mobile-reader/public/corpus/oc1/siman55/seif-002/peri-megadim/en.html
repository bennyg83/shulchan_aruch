But the Taz — I say: what does this teach us? Here on the day of entry, if they read with ten and left, nevertheless they say "and come to Zion" with the Kaddish of titkabel, which refers to the eighteen-blessing prayer, also at Torah reading — and this is straightforward. See siman 143 se'if 1, and there it will be explained; and Magen Avraham here on this.
