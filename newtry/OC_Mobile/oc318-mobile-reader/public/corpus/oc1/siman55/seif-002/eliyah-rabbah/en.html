In Avot, etc. — and so Bach ruled, and wrote that Rama wrote thus; and it seems to me that according to this, what Rama wrote — after he began aloud and kedushah — he did not specifically mention kedushah, for the same applies when he began only Avot; and so Magen Avraham wrote. Alternatively, for emphasis they mentioned it — that even so one does not read from the Torah; it requires study. Regarding Birkat Kohanim he expanded in Knesset HaGedolah and concludes that one should say Elokeinu, etc. — see there. And I wrote in siman 121 that even an individual may say it.
