That Kaddish, etc. It implies that he should not begin Yotzer Or, and so the Bach explained the words of the Tur. But the Perishah explained that even if he began only Kaddish, he completes also the Kedushah of Yotzer Or until after the congregation has prayed silently. It appears that one may rely on him in practice, for I saw in Maharil that he ruled in the name of the Rash that even if he had begun only Yishtabach, he completes everything.
