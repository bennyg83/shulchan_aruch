(57) And some of them in the ezrat nashim—The same applies even if nine are in the synagogue and one in the ezrat nashim. Peri Megadim wrote that the cases in se'ifim 17, 18, and 19 apply when they do not see one another; but when they see one another, even in two actual houses they combine, similar to zimmun below in siman 195; and some are strict even when they see; and in a pressing circumstance one may be lenient.

(58) They do not combine—because there is no breach there and the doorway itself, even if there is no door, is considered a significant partition, and it is as two houses. Chayei Adam wrote: therefore, when there is a house and before it a room, and that room is not fully breached—the one praying there is as if praying alone, and only Kaddish and kedushah he may answer, as below in se'if 20; and in responsa Radbaz siman 250 I found that he wrote this is specifically regarding joining; but to be considered as praying with the congregation, if the room has no other opening but only through the large house, he is considered as praying with the congregation.
