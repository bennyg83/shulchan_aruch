We do not compel hiring to complete a minyan except on the Days of Awe and when only one or two are lacking—unless there is a fixed custom in the city to compel hiring even when three or four are lacking. If there is' a minyan of residents in the city, they compel hiring a chazzan.And likewise in a place where there is not always a minyan in the synagogue—they compel one another with fines so that a minyan will always come to the synagogue and the tamid not be voided [Rivash responsum 518]; and see at the beginning of siman 150 whether they may compel one another to build a synagogue.
