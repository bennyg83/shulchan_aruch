They compel, etc. — it is written in Derishah: Maharash — one may compel youths and students in a place lacking a regular minyan to go to synagogue, even if they cease from study for this, for Torah study has its [separate] time. And rabbis did not pray in synagogues alone but in large communities that have a majority of people and a larger minyan — until here. Radbaz wrote siman 13: if the ruler gave him permission one day to pray with the congregation in a minyan, he should pray that day immediately and not let the mitzvah grow stale waiting for Yom Kippur and Purim. One who prays individually — see Mateh Moshe page 47, what he should say in place of Kaddish, kedushah, and Barchu.
