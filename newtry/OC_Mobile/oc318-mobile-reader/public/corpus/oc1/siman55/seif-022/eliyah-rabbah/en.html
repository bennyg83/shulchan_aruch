They compel, etc. The Derishah wrote in the name of Maharash that one may compel young men and learners in a place that does not have a constant minyan to go to the synagogue, even though they will be interrupted from their learning because of this, for the time of Torah is one thing and the time of prayer is another. The rabbis were not accustomed to pray in synagogues by themselves except in large communities where there is a multitude of people and an additional minyan. Radbaz, siman 13, wrote that a prisoner to whom the officer gave permission for one day to pray with the congregation in a minyan should pray that day immediately and not let the mitzvah become stale by waiting for Yom Kippur or Purim. One who prays alone should see Mateh Moshe, page 47, regarding what he should say in place of Kaddish, Kedushah, and Barechu.
