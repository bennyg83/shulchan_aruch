Taz, seif katan 6. They will need. Meaning that the essential law, that a settlement without a minyan may compel one another to hire someone to complete the minyan, is so that they will not need to leave their homes and abandon their money. From this law follows the law here, that if they are eleven and two wish to leave, they must hire someone to complete the minyan, since through them the minyan is lacking. If so, the end of the law is like its beginning, and there is reason to say that payment for completing a minyan is according to wealth. This is simple.
