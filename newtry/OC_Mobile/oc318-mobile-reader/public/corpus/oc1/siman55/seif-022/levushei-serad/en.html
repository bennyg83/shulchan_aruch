Taz s.k. 6—they will need—meaning: the essence of the law is that they return when there is no minyan; they compel one another to hire to complete the minyan so that they need not leave their homes and abandon their property. And from this law is derived the law here: if they are eleven and two wish to leave, they must hire to complete, since through them the minyan is lacking. And if so, the end of the law is like its beginning—that there is reason that the hiring payment for a minyan is according to wealth; and it is simple.
