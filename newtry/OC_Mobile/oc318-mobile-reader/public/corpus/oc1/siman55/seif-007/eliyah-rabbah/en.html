And Magen Avraham challenged: behold, Maharil's main proof is since there are those who say a minor lying in a cradle combines; if so, it should follow from the law to be like the case discussed — what about a minor specifically one and not two, likewise regarding one who sleeps, until here. One may say that regarding a minor it is different, for there is no heavenly honor to combine more than one, as Beit Yosef wrote. And Taz expanded to wonder at Maharil, for the derivation from minor to sleeping does not stand for us — see there. And it is not difficult at all, as Beit Yosef wrote, that even one who disagrees does so because he is not fit for kedushah — see there; also other difficulties may be rejected lightly; nevertheless lekhatchilah one should raise the point. He wrote in responsum Radbaz siman 201: if ten each prayed individually, Kaddish and kedushah departed from them, as we say regarding Birkat HaMazon; and since it departed from them, even if afterward ten joined they cannot return and pray Kaddish and kedushah; and if they returned and prayed, it is a blessing in vain — for granted where there is one who did not pray, he prays and the others answer Amen; and even though we hold that even one of those who already fulfilled may return and pray — nevertheless there is finally one who has not yet fulfilled prayer, and all Israel are guarantors for one another; but where all already fulfilled prayer, they are found to be blessings in vain — until here, his words briefly. And so he wrote in Hagahot Magen Avraham siman 69 in the name of the Gaon Rabbi Yehoshua.
