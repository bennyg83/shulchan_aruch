Se'if 15 — he combines them; and all the more so if the nine are inside or outside. And the shaliach tzibbur in the doorway can bring them out. And in that of se'if 17 which is different, since he is in a large courtyard removed from the congregation — and specifically a shaliach tzibbur upon whom they rely; but one standing at the doorway and nine inside or outside do not combine. Perishah.
