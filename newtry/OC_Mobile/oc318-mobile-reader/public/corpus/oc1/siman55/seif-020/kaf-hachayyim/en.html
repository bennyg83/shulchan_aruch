20) Regarding the Kaddish recited by orphans, according to the Arizal it is specifically the final Kaddish recited before Aleinu that benefits the soul of the deceased. Shaar HaKavanos, discourse concerning Kaddish, writes: "The final Kaddish is not included among the ordinary Kaddishim, because all the realms have already ascended. This Kaddish is called Kaddish Yasom, meaning the Kaddish recited by orphans. The matter is that this Kaddish pertains to Asiyah, where there is the aspect of death, in order to elevate all the souls and nefashot in the secret of resurrection. Therefore orphans recite it upon the death of a father or mother," etc. See what we wrote above in letter 1 in the name of Torat Chacham. "It appears to me that I heard from my teacher that it is proper to recite this Kaddish for the death of one's father or mother throughout the entire year, even on Shabbos and Yom Tov. The reason is not, as the masses think, merely that it helps save the deceased's soul from the judgment of Gehinnom. It has another benefit: bringing the soul into Gan Eden and elevating it from level to level. Accordingly, it should be recited even on Shabbos and Yom Tov. I saw my teacher recite the final Kaddish every year on the day his father died, at all 3 prayers." End quote. Thus, according to the Rav, the Kaddish that benefits the soul of the deceased is specifically the final Kaddish. Here in our holy city of Jerusalem, may it be built and established, some have the custom that orphans also recite the Kaddish before Hodu and the Kaddish before Kaveh, while all the other Kaddishim are recited by the chazzan alone. It appears to me that the reason they also recite these 2 Kaddishim is that they correspond to the ascent of Asiyah, the aspect of nefesh, into Yetzirah, and it is possible that they likewise benefit the nefesh of the deceased.
