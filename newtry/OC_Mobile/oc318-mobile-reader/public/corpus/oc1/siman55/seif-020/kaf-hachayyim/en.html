20) Regarding the Kaddish recited by orphans, according to the Arizal specifically the final Kaddish before Aleinu benefits the deceased's soul. Shaar HaKavanos, Derush HaKaddish, states: 'The final Kaddish is not included among the Kaddishim, because all the worlds have already ascended. This Kaddish is nevertheless called Kaddish Yasom, meaning the Kaddish recited by orphans. This Kaddish pertains to the world of Asiyah, which possesses the aspect of death, in order to elevate all souls and nefashos through the mystery of resurrection. Therefore orphans recite it upon the death of a father or mother,' etc. See what was stated above in paragraph 1 in the name of Toras Chacham. 'It appears to me that I heard from my master that it is proper to recite this Kaddish for one's deceased father or mother throughout the entire year, even on Shabbos and Yom Tov. The reason is not, as the masses think, merely that it helps save the deceased's soul from the judgment of Gehinnom. It has another benefit: bringing the soul into Gan Eden and elevating it from level to level. Accordingly, it should be recited even on Shabbos and Yom Tov. I saw my master recite the final Kaddish every year on the date of his father's death at all three prayers.' The Rav's words show that specifically the final Kaddish benefits the deceased's soul. Here in our holy city Yerushalayim, may it be built and established, some have the custom for orphans also to recite the Kaddish before Hodu and the Kaddish before Kaveh, while the chazzan alone recites all other Kaddishim. It appears to me that these two Kaddishim are also recited because they correspond to the ascent of the world of Asiyah, which is the aspect of nefesh, into the world of Yetzirah; they may therefore also benefit the deceased's nefesh.
