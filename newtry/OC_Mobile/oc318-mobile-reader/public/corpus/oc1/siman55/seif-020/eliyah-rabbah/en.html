They pay half, etc. — but in Knesset HaGedolah he wrote in the name of Hagahot Mordechai that they pay equally, since each is obligated to provide a man in his place; and that which they give half, etc., is when they do not dwell in the settlement but only eight, who need to hire two — for then it depends on money: the poor can go to the nearby city, which the rich cannot, for they cannot leave their property; and so Magen Avraham wrote. However, in Agudah first chapter of Bava Batra it implies to him as Shulchan Aruch and Levush; and the reason seems to me that this law that they compel the rest, the poskim learned from a settlement that lacks a minyan, that they hire one or two — it requires study. But according to this, if there are only ten and one wants to leave, he is not obligated to hire one from his pocket if he is poor; and one may distinguish — it requires study. And what Levush wrote — since both go to profit, etc. — is far-fetched. And know that even though regarding a cantor in siman 53 seif 23, in a place where it is not custom they collect according to money — that is as he wrote there, because the rich add to wages according to pleasantness of voice; but here, if the poor remained, the rich would not need to hire in his place; and Levush concluded regarding the cantor's wages, etc., according to their custom in the other years there — he meant to say the cantor's law is unique. Maharil wrote siman 114: if it is true coercion he is exempt; but coercion of monetary obligation or negligence — he is liable.
