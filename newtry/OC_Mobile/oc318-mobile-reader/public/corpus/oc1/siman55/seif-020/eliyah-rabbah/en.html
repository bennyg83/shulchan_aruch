They pay half, etc. But the Kenesses HaGedolah wrote in the name of Gilyon Mordechai that they pay equally, since each one is obligated to provide a person in his place. What is said that they give half, etc., applies when only eight live in the settlement and they need to hire two, for then it depends on wealth, since the poor can go to the nearby city, whereas the wealthy cannot leave their property. So the Magen Avraham wrote. However, from the Agudah in the first chapter of Bava Basra it seems like the words of the Shulchan Aruch and Levush. The reason appears to me that this law, that they compel the remaining residents, the poskim learned from a settlement that does not have a minyan, where they hire one or two. Examine this. But according to this, if there are only ten there and one wishes to leave, he should not be obligated to hire one from his own pocket if he is poor, and one can distinguish; this requires study. What the Levush wrote, that both are going to earn, etc., is far-fetched. Know that although regarding a chazzan in siman 53, seif 23, in a place with no custom, they collect according to wealth, that is as he explained there, because the wealthy add to the wage on account of the pleasantness of the voice. But here, if the poor person had remained, the wealthy person would not have needed to hire someone in his place. This is what the Levush concluded, that the chazzan's wage, etc., follows their local custom in other years, meaning that the law of the chazzan is different. Maharil, siman 114, wrote that if it is a true case of coercion, he is exempt, but coercion due to monetary debt or negligence makes him liable.
