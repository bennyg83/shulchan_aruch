(60) Who is not with them. Meaning even if he is in another house, completely distant, since ten are in one place and the Shechinah rests among them. Then even an iron partition does not separate between anyone who wishes to attach himself to his Father in Heaven, Who dwells among these ten.
(61) May answer. Amen, Yehei Shemei Rabbah, Kedushah, and Barchu. Likewise, he can discharge him from his obligation in prayer if he is not proficient.
(62) And some say that it is necessary. Since the Shulchan Aruch wrote this law in the language of some say, this implies that this law was not conclusive for him. So too it appears from the Rama in siman 79, seif 1 in the gloss, that he does not hold this way, as the Magen Avraham wrote there. See Chayei Adam, who wrote that it seems to him that Yehei Shemei Rabbah and Kedushah one may answer, for they are only verses, but Barchu he should not answer. See Levushei Serad.
(63) That there not be. Even Amen he cannot answer according to this opinion if it interrupts.
(64) That is not clean. Meaning even if he is more than four amos away from it, where ordinarily it is permitted by turning his face away if the bad odor does not reach him. Here that does not help, because since it interrupts between the ten and this one who wishes to join with them, it interrupts between the Shechinah and causes the Shechinah not to rest here.
(65) An idolater. Meaning idolatry or an idolater.
