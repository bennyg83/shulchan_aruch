(60) That is not with them—meaning even if he is in a completely distant house, since ten are in one place the Divine Presence rests among them; and then even an iron partition does not separate between anyone who wishes to join himself with his Father in heaven who dwells within these ten.

(61) May answer—Amen, and Amen yehei shmei rabba, and kedushah, and Barekhu; and likewise he may fulfill their obligation in prayer if he is not expert.

(62) And some say it is required—from the fact that Shulchan Aruch writes this law in the language "and some say," it implies that this law is not decided for him; and likewise it appears from Rama in siman 79 seif 1 in the gloss that he does not hold so; and as Magen Avraham wrote there. And see in Chayei Adam, who wrote that it appears to him that one answers Amen yehei shmei rabba and kedushah, for these are only verses; but Barekhu one does not answer. And see in Levushei Serad.

(63) That there not be—meaning, even Amen one may not answer according to this view if there is an interposition.

(64) That is not clean—meaning, even if he is more distant than four amot; in general it is permitted by turning one's face if one does not reach the foul smell; but here it does not help, since he interposes between the ten and between one who wishes to join with them—he interposes between the Shechinah and causes the Shechinah not to dwell there.

(65) Avodah zarah—meaning, idolatry or an idolater.
