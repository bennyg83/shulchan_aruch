To answer. An individual who enters a synagogue and finds the congregation already beginning to answer Kedushah or Amen, Yehei Shemei Rabbah, as long as they have not completed it, may answer with them. Be'er Sheva and Rama MiPano. So too wrote the Rama in siman 56; see there.
An idol. That is to say, an object of idol worship should not interrupt; but a worshipper of idolatry does not interrupt. Magen Avraham. So too wrote Devar Shmuel, siman 189.
