To answer. An individual who enters the synagogue and finds the congregation that has already begun to answer kedushah or the Shemoneh Esreh—all that they have not completed, he is permitted to answer with them—Beer Sheva and Rama; and likewise Rama siman 56, etc.

Idolater. Meaning: that avodah zarah should not interrupt; but an idol worshipper does not interrupt—Magen Avraham; and likewise Dvar Shmuel siman 189.
