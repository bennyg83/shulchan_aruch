(38) A deaf person who speaks. The view of the Taz below in siman 124, seif 2, is that one who cannot hear is not joined to a minyan, even though he speaks, because he does not know to answer Amen to the blessings recited by the shaliach tzibbur. However, many agree with the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch and hold that this does not prevent joining. If he intends to the blessings, and when he sees them answering he answers with them, similar to the waving of cloths in the synagogue of Alexandria, where one understood when the shaliach tzibbur finished the end of the blessing and answered Amen, he joins according to all views.
