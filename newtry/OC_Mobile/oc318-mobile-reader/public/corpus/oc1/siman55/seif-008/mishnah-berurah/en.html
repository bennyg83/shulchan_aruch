(38) A deaf person who speaks—see Taz later in siman 124 se'if bet that they do not combine for a minyan one who does not hear, even though he speaks, because he does not know to answer Amen on the blessings that the shaliach tzibbur recites. However, many agree with the ruling of Shulchan Aruch and hold this is not an impediment for combining; if he intends the blessings and when he sees they answer he answers with them—it is similar to waving the scarves in the synagogue of Alexandria, who understands when the shaliach tzibbur finishes the end of the blessing and answers Amen—he combines according to all.
