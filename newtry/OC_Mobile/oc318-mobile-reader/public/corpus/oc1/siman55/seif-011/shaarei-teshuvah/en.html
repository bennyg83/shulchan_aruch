Leap year. Ba'er Heitev. And as the Shlah wrote; likewise Me'il Tzedakah siman 21; and in Korban HaEdah first chapter of Megillah he proved there that if one was born in a simple year he does not become bar mitzvah until Adar II — and this is like the words of Rama and Peri Chadash here, who dispute Maharash HaLevi. And see in responsa Beit Efrayim part Even HaEzer siman 59; and see Levush and Av Beit Din siman 475 and Yad Ephraim siman 565. And he wrote in responsa Beit Chadash siman 175 that if one was born on Rosh Chodesh and it was incomplete and now the year is complete — we have nothing in it. And Av Beit Din wrote: if in the year he was born Kislev was incomplete and there was not Rosh Chodesh Tevet but one day only, and now Kislev is full and there are two days Rosh Chodesh — nevertheless bar mitzvah is on the first day of Rosh Chodesh Tevet. And Av Beit Din wrote that according to what Magen Avraham wrote, that the first day of Rosh Chodesh is considered the thirtieth day of the previous month — one could say bar mitzvah is not until the second day of Rosh Chodesh; and there is somewhat to distinguish. And see in Even HaEzer siman 126; and see in Yoreh De'ah siman 55 in Taz and in Kenesset HaGedolah there.
