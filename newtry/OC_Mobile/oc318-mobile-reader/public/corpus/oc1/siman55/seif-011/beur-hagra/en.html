Seif 11, a transgressor, etc. As stated in Sanhedrin 44a, that he remains in his sanctity. Although he is invalid for testimony, women would combine for zimun if not for concerns of impropriety, and a Kusi combines for zimun even though he transgresses D'Rabbanan, as stated there, 47b. What is stated at the beginning of the seventh chapter of Berachos, one who ate tevel, etc., applies specifically to zimun, for one cannot say that this is a fixed meal, as the Raavad wrote, and specifically for that meal, as the Rivash wrote in siman 172; see there.
