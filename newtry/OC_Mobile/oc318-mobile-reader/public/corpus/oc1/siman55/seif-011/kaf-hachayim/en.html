(11) There — with fewer than ten males, etc., one must be careful not to count Israel by their letters to see whether there is a minyan or not, for we say in the first chapter of Yoma: it is forbidden to count Israel even for a mitzvah matter, as written: "and Saul heard the people and counted them with lambs." Peri Chadash letter 1; and so Chayei Adam letter 10, and he concluded: and many err and count alef-bet-gimel, for it seems to them the prohibition is to say "one two" (meaning one, two), but alef-bet is permitted — and the error is in their hands; rather they should appear to count in the eye's view and count in thought — so far. And so in his book Peleh Yo'etz part 2 letter 40, beginning "counting." And so Rabbi Chaim Palaggi z"l in Kaf HaChayyim siman 13 letter 10; Yafeh Lelev letter 3; and see Magen Avraham siman 156 s.k. 2 and Petach HaDevir there letter 3.
