(46) Or he transgressed a transgression. The Peri Megadim wrote that this is specifically a transgression committed for appetite. But if he did so to anger Heaven, even in one matter, or if he is an apostate to idolatry or to desecrate Shabbos publicly, his law is like an idolater and he does not join.
(47) They did not place him in nidui. Even for a transgression for which he is liable to death. The reason is that regarding Achan it is written, Israel sinned; even though he sinned, he is still a Jew and remains in his sanctity. The sect called Karaites do not join ten, for they do not acknowledge the Oral Torah. Anyone who denies the Oral Torah does not join for any matter of sanctity.
