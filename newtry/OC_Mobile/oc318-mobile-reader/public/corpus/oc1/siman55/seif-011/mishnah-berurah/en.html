(46) Or transgressed a sin—Peri Megadim wrote: specifically a sin committed out of appetite; but out of spite—even in one matter—or one who is a mumar for avodah zarah or who desecrates Shabbat in public, his law is like an idolater and he does not combine.

(47) Was not placed under nidui—even a sin for which one is liable to death; and the reason is as written regarding Achan: "Israel has sinned"—even though he sinned, he is Israel and stands in its sanctity. And the sect called Karaites do not combine for ten, for they do not admit the Oral Torah. And whoever denies the Oral Torah does not combine for any matter of kedushah.
