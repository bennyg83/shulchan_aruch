11) There: With fewer than ten males, etc. One must take care not to count Jews by their heads to determine whether a minyan is present. The beginning of the second chapter of Yoma states that it is forbidden to count Yisrael even for a mitzvah, as it is written, 'Shaul summoned the people and counted them with lambs.' Pri Chadash 1. Chesed LaAlafim 10 writes likewise and concludes: Many err by counting alef, beis, gimel, etc., imagining that the prohibition applies only to saying uno, dos (meaning one, two) while counting by letters is permitted. This is their error. Rather, they should assess visually and count in thought. Pele Yoetz 2, entry moneh, rules likewise, as do Rav Chaim Palagi in Kaf HaChaim 13:10 and Yafeh LaLev 3. See Magen Avraham 156:2 and Pischei HaDevir 3 there.
