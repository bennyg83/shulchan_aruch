That is, when he closes the door, etc. It is clear that the threshold area itself, meaning those standing on the threshold of the synagogue in the thickness of the doorway, is like outside, for Rashi explained that the threshold area itself is called from the inner edge of the thickness of the opening until the place where the door strikes. Although the wording of the Shulchan Aruch is 'thickness of the door,' and Rashi's wording is 'thickness of the opening,' nevertheless from the language of the Shulchan Aruch it implies that they are the same, and examine this. So it also appears from Ateres Tzvi, who explained the words of the Shulchan Aruch this way, and so the Magen Avraham explained. The reason appears to me that although the Beit Yosef wrote that the Talmud implies to him that it is like inside, nevertheless since he brought at the end the words of Rabbeinu Yerucham, who wrote explicitly that it is like outside, he nullified his own view before the view of Rabbeinu Yerucham. But Olas Tamid wrote that the Shulchan Aruch did not write the law of the threshold itself, meaning from the inner edge of the thickness of the door inward, which is the whole thickness of the threshold. He was not precise. Furthermore, from the inner edge inward is the synagogue itself, as in Rashi's explanation that the Beit Yosef brought. Moreover, according to him, who understood the Shulchan Aruch as speaking only from the threshold outward and not the threshold itself, it would be clear from the Shulchan Aruch that he holds the threshold itself is like inside, since he mentioned only from the threshold outward. Nevertheless, even Olas Tamid wrote in practice that it implies from the Beit Yosef that he agrees to Rabbeinu Yerucham, that the threshold itself is like outside. Now one must explain the view of the Levush, who wrote anonymously in this matter, and it especially requires study.
