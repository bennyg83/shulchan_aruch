{Rama: seif 10:} He does not become [bar mitzvah]—even though the author already wrote so in seif 9, nevertheless this is specifically when born in another month, such as on Rosh Chodesh Nisan and the thirteenth year is intercalated—he does not become bar mitzvah until Rosh Chodesh Nisan; but one born on Rosh Chodesh Adar—this comes to teach that he becomes bar mitzvah on Rosh Chodesh Adar Rishon; for the first Adar is not called Adar but Shevat for this matter. And so explained Peri Megadim z"l.
