Rama, seif 10. He does not become. Although the Mechaber already wrote this in seif 9, nevertheless one might have thought that this applies only when he was born in another month, for example on Rosh Chodesh Nisan, and the thirteenth year is a leap year, that he does not become bar mitzvah until Rosh Chodesh Nisan. But if he was born on Rosh Chodesh Adar, one might have thought that he becomes bar mitzvah on Rosh Chodesh of the first Adar. Therefore it teaches that the first Adar is not called Adar for this purpose, but rather Shevat. So the Peri Megadim, of blessed memory, explained.
