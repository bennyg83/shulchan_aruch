Seif 63, if he began, etc. Magen Avraham — meaning, every eighteen [verses of Kedushah]; and as written in the Yerushalmi cited above, and it says even the kedushah of "Kadosh, kadosh" that is not a matter by itself; proof that it is not counted in the baraita there, but is included in the rule of "we do not pass before the ark" — Rashba siman 96, see there; and so too for Kaddish, as written in the gloss.

And if they left, etc. But there is no etc. As above from Yerushalmi — each matter is a matter by itself; and this is also what "and the evening prayer," etc., means.

And kedushah. Not specifically Magen Avraham.
