(15) Even Kedushah. The same applies that the shaliach tzibbur completes the entire Shemoneh Esrei, and it mentioned Kedushah only so that one not say that it is a different matter.
(16) After they began. He used this wording to exclude Maharam, cited in Darchei Moshe, who wished to be lenient in this. In truth, according to the consensus of the poskim, even if the silent prayer was also with ten, the law is that the shaliach tzibbur should not begin aloud with fewer than ten.
(17) To pray Yotzer. See the later authorities, who agreed that even if they said only Kaddish and Barchu with ten and some left before they began the blessing of Yotzer at all, it is considered as though they had already begun with ten, and they may discharge others until Shemoneh Esrei even with fewer than ten.
(18) And Kedushah. This is not precise, for even if they have not yet said Kedushah, once they began to pray aloud, even only Avos, they complete even the full Kaddish.
(19) The full Kaddish. All the more so the half Kaddish after Shemoneh Esrei.
(20) After it. But the Kaddish after Aleinu should not be said in this case. Even if he began to say Aleinu with ten and some left, he should not say the Kaddish, for it is only a custom. All the more so with Shir HaYichud, or a Kaddish said for a chapter of Tehillim or after learning, which requires a minyan specifically at the time of Kaddish.
(21) In the Torah. If this occurred on Monday or Thursday.
(22) For Krias Shema and its blessings. Therefore, if they began the Maariv blessing with ten, or even only Barchu alone, and afterwards some left, he may only complete the Kaddish before the prayer, since that Kaddish belongs to the blessing of Krias Shema. But he may not say the Kaddish after Shemoneh Esrei, because the prayer is not attached to the blessings of Krias Shema. All the more so the order of Kedushah and Kaddish on motzaei Shabbos. But if there were ten when they began the silent Shemoneh Esrei and afterwards some left, he may say the full Kaddish after the prayer, for the Kaddish belongs to the prayer. On motzaei Shabbos he may say the half Kaddish after the prayer, and also the order of Kedushah and the full Kaddish afterward, because all this belongs to the prayer, as at Shacharis.
