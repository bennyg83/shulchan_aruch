(15) Even kedushah—and the same applies that the shaliach tzibbur completes the entire Shemoneh Esreh; and kedushah was mentioned only so that one not say it is a different matter.

(16) After they began—this expression was used to exclude Maharash, cited in Darchei Moshe, who wished to be lenient in this; but in truth, per the consensus of the decisors, even if the prayer was whispered, the law is that the shaliach tzibbur should not begin aloud with fewer than ten.

(17) To pray Yotzer—see in the later authorities who agreed that even if they said only Kaddish and Barekhu with ten and some left before they began Yotzer Or at all, it is called as if they already began with ten, and they may fulfill others until the Shemoneh Esreh even with fewer than ten.

(18) And kedushah—not specifically; for even if they have not yet said kedushah, since they began to pray aloud—and even only in Avot alone—they complete the full Kaddish as well.

(19) The full Kaddish—and likewise the half-Kaddish after the eighteen blessings.

(20) That follows it—but the Kaddish after Aleinu one does not say in this case; and even if he began to say Aleinu with ten and some left, one also does not say that Kaddish, for it is only a custom. And how much more so the song of unity, or the Kaddish said for a psalm or after study—for those require a minyan specifically at the time of Kaddish.

(21) In the Torah—if it happened thus on Monday and Thursday.

(22) Kriat Shema and its blessings—and similarly: if they began the evening blessing with ten, or even only Barekhu alone, and afterward some left, he has only to finish the Kaddish before the prayer, for that Kaddish belongs to the blessing of Kriat Shema; but he may not say the Kaddish after the Shemoneh Esreh prayer, for the prayer is not connected to the blessings of Kriat Shema; and how much more so the kedushah section and Kaddish on Motza'ei Shabbat. But if there were ten at the hour when they began to pray the silent Shemoneh Esreh and afterward some left, they may say the full Kaddish after the prayer, for that Kaddish belongs to the prayer; and similarly he may say the half-Kaddish after the prayer, and also the kedushah section and full Kaddish afterward, for all this belongs to the prayer as on Shacharit.
