If he began with Avot and some left, he may finish even kedushah.If some left after they began Yotzer Or, the shaliach tzibbur should not begin the Shemoneh Esreh aloud, for the prayer of Yotzer is already completed. If they left after he began aloud and kedushah, they may complete the entire kedushah section and say the full Kaddish that follows it, for it belongs to the Shemoneh Esreh—in that it says "may their prayer be accepted," etc. But they may not read from' the Torah, for that is a different matter. And the evening prayer and the Kaddish after it do not belong to Kriat Shema and its blessings [Ran on the chapter "one who reads the Megillah"; Shibolei HaLeket siman 15].]:
