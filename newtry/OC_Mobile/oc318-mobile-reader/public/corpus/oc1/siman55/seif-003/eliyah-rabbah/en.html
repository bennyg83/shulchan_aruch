And the evening prayer, etc. Meaning, if they began Barechu, they complete until the Kaddish after the Shemoneh Esrei prayer. If they began Aleinu with ten and some left, they do not say Kaddish, for it is only a custom, and all the more so regarding Shir Yichud and a psalm. There is room to doubt whether, if he began Ashrei at Minchah with ten and they left, he should say the Kaddish over it. It is reasonable that he should say the Kaddish, for Ashrei is like an obligation, as written in the Tur and Levush, seif 1, but this requires study. Regarding the law where they had already said Aleinu without ten, where the Levush wrote that they do not say Kaddish afterward, this is not compelling; and the Taz ruled that they do say Kaddish. Likewise, after reciting Tehillim in the morning and after any learning, when a minyan becomes available immediately, they say Kaddish. See below, siman 56, and Magen Avraham, siman 69, who did not rule this way, and he wrote likewise in siman 234. Initially one should be concerned to learn a little more when they come. In siman 581, seif katan 1, he brought proof for this from Kol Bo, which is difficult for the Taz.
