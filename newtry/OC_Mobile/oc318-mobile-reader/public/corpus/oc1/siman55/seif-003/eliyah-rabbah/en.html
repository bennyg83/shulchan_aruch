And Maariv prayer, etc. — explanation: when they began Barchu they finish until the Kaddish after the Shemoneh Esreh; and if they began Aleinu with ten and some left, they do not say Kaddish, for it is only custom; and all the more so Shir HaYichud and Mizmor. One may be uncertain: if he began Ashrei at Minchah with ten and some left — whether he says Kaddish on it; and it is reasonable that he says Kaddish, for Ashrei is like an obligation, as Tur and Levush wrote in seif 1 — it requires study. And regarding law that they already said Aleinu without ten — Levush wrote one does not say Kaddish after it; that is not necessary; and Taz ruled that one says Kaddish; and likewise in reciting Tehillim in the morning and in every study, when a minyan happens for him immediately they say Kaddish. And see below siman 56; and Magen Avraham siman 69 did not rule thus; and so he wrote siman 234. And lekhatchilah one should be concerned to study a little more when they come; and in siman 581 s.k. 1 he brought proof for this from Kolbo — and it is difficult on Taz.
