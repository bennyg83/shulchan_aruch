If they began with Avot, etc. — all the more so in the Yotzer prayer: even if they only said Kaddish and Barchu with ten, it is considered that they began with ten; therefore if some of them left, the remainder may say the kedushah in Yotzer, even according to those who hold it is not recited with fewer than ten — as is proven in Beit Yosef. And according to what Rama concludes in siman 69, that spreading Shema is Barchu alone. And so Peri Megadim wrote.

And Maariv prayer, etc. — see Noda B'Yehuda Mahadura Tinyana siman 7, who wrote that in Shacharit, if they prayed silently with ten and afterward some left — aside from the shaliach tzibbur not being able to repeat his prayer, he also cannot say any Kaddish after that; and it is not comparable to Maariv — see there, the matter in its reason.
