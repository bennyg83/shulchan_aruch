If he began Avos, etc. All the more so regarding the prayer of Yotzer: even if they only said Kaddish and Barechu with ten, it is considered that they began with ten. Therefore, if some left, those who remain may say the Kedushah of Yotzer, even according to the poskim who maintain that it is not recited with fewer than ten. So it is proven in the Beit Yosef. And according to the Rama's conclusion in siman 69 that spreading Shema is Barechu alone, this is also written by the Peri Megadim.
And the evening prayer, etc. See Noda BiYehudah, Tinyana, siman 7, who wrote that at Shacharis, if they prayed silently with ten and afterward some left, not only may the shaliach tzibbur not repeat the prayer, he also may not say any Kaddish afterward. This is not comparable to Maariv; see there for the reasoning.
