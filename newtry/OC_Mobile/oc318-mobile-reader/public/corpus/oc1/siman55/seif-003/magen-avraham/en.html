Aloud. Kedushah is not precise, for even if they have not yet said Kedushah, once they began to pray aloud they complete even the Kaddish Shalem. So too is written explicitly in Terumas HaDeshen in the name of one great authority; see there.
Maariv and the Kaddish after it. All the more so the order of Kedushah and Kaddish on motzaei Shabbos. The Levush wrote that the Kaddish before Maariv belongs to Krias Shema, for it was instituted on account of people leaving, as written in the Beit Yosef. But if they said Aleinu and some left, they may not say Kaddish, for it is only a custom. All the more so regarding Shir HaYichud or a psalm.
