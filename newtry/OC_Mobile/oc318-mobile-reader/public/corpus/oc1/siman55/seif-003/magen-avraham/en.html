Even kedushah — and all of the Shemoneh Esreh.

In a loud voice — and kedushah is not specific, for even if they have not yet said kedushah, since they began to pray in a loud voice they finish also Kriat Shema; and so explicitly in Terumat HaDeshen in the name of one great authority — see there.

Maariv and the Kaddish after it — and all the more so the kedushah section and Kaddish in what is written; and Levush wrote: the Kaddish before Maariv belongs to Kriat Shema, for it was arranged for people leaving, as Beit Yosef wrote; and if they said Aleinu and some left, one does not say Kaddish, for it is only custom — so far; and all the more so Shir HaYichud or a psalm.
