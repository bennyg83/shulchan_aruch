(Seif katan 12) And between them, etc. In the Gemara it implies, etc., that they said there: Rav said, the upper chambers and roofs of the azarah and of the walls of Jerusalem were not sanctified. And it challenges: but we taught, the windows and thickness of the wall are considered inward—thus they were sanctified; and the same applies to upper chambers and roofs. And it answers: these are windows, these are wall—the case is when they are equal in height to the ground of the azarah; and Rashi explained that the ground of the azarah went up and ascended in steps, etc. Therefore the windows and thickness of the wall have the law of ground, as written in siman 195 seif 2 regarding joining for zimmun.
