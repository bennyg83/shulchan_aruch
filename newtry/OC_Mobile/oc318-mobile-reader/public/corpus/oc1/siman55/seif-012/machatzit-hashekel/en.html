(Seif katan 12) And between them, etc. The Gemara implies, etc., for there Rav says that the upper stories and roofs of the Azarah and of the walls of Yerushalayim were not sanctified. The Gemara asks from the Mishnah, which states that the windows and the thickness of the wall are regarded as inward, showing that they were sanctified, and the same should therefore apply to upper stories and roofs. It answers that both the windows and the wall under discussion were level with the floor of the Azarah. Rashi explains that the floor of the Azarah rose gradually by means of steps. Therefore the windows and the thickness of the wall have the status of the floor, as stated in siman 195, seif 2, regarding combining for zimun.
