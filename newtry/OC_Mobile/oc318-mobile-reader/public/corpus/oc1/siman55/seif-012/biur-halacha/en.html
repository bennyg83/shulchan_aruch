One who is under a ban, etc. If the ban was not because of a transgression but only because of money, it is permitted to combine with him for every matter of sanctity, as the Beit Yosef cites in the name of the Rashba, and so it also appears from Olas Tamid and Or Zarua, not like the Peri Chadash; and so it also appears from the Peri Megadim. But if it was because he violated a communal decree, the Beit Yosef also agrees; see there.
We do not combine, etc. See the Taz in Yoreh Deah, siman 334, seif katan 2, who says that this is specifically if they stated then that he should not combine. But from the language of the poskim it does not appear so; see the Beit Yosef and the Beur HaGra. Afterward I found in Sefer Shevet Yehudah in Yoreh Deah, siman 334, that he too wondered about him in this. The Peri Megadim wrote that for the Megillah perhaps he may combine; see there for his reasoning.
