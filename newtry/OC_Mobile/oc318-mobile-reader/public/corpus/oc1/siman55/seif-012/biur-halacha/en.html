Excommunicated, etc. — and if it was not because of a sin but only because of money, it is permitted to join with him for everything holy [Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba; and so it implies in Be'er Heitev and Orach LaZaddik, not like Perach; and so it implies in Peri Megadim]; and if it was because he transgressed a decree of the community, Beit Yosef also admits — see there.

They do not combine, etc. — see Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 334 s.k. 2, that specifically if they stipulated then that he should not join; but from the language of the poskim it does not imply so; see in Beit Yosef and in Beur HaGra afterward. I found in Sefer Shevet Yehudah, Yoreh De'ah siman 334, that he too was puzzled about this. Peri Megadim wrote that for Megillah perhaps he combines — see there his reason.
