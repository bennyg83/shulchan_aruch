Shulchan Aruch seif 20—that it is required. It implies that the first view is primary, since he wrote this view in the language of eleven. And likewise it implies that Rama holds in siman 76 seif 1, as Magen Avraham wrote there s.k. 3. Nevertheless it is proper to be stringent; but in a time of pressure, one who follows the first view has not lost.
