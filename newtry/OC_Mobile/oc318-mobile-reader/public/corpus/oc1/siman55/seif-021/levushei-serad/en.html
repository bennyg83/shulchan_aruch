Shulchan Aruch, seif 20. That there must be. This implies that the first opinion is primary, since he wrote this view in the language of 'some say.' So too it appears that the Rama holds in siman 76, seif 1, as the Magen Avraham wrote there in seif katan 3. Nevertheless, it is proper to be stringent, but in a pressing situation one who acts according to the first opinion has not lost.
