If the orphans are minors, we have already said above that the chazzan must say the Kaddish with them in order to discharge the congregation for the Shemoneh Esreh. And likewise if the orphan stammers in his speech and does not articulate the letters properly and as they should be pronounced—even if he is an adult—or if he has any of the disqualifications for discharging the many from their obligation, as written in siman 53; or if he has any of the matters we wrote above by which he does not join for ten—the chazzan must also say the Kaddish in order to discharge the congregation for the Shemoneh Esreh. One must be careful about this. And further see below siman 56 letter 30.
