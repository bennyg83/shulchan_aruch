21) If the orphans are minors, we have already stated above that the chazzan must recite Kaddish together with them in order to discharge the congregation's obligation. The same applies if the orphan stammers and does not articulate the letters correctly and distinctly, even if he is an adult; if he possesses any disqualification from discharging the public's obligation, as stated in siman 53; or if he possesses one of the characteristics stated above that prevents him from joining ten. In all these cases, the chazzan must also recite Kaddish in order to discharge the congregation. Attention must be paid to this. See also below, siman 56:30.
