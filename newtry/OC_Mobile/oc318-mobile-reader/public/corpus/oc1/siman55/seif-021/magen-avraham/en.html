Half according to money — one who examines Hagahot Maimoniyot will see it is a corruption: that here they pay equally, for each is obligated to provide a man in his place; and if only eight dwell in the settlement, then they hire two and pay half according to money and half according to persons, for the poor can go to the nearby city, which the rich cannot — leave their house and property — see there; and so Rama explicitly in responsum — see there. And Maharil wrote in responsum siman 114/107: if he is compelled that he is not permitted to come to the country, he is exempt even if the time of the festival has already arrived; and specifically if he is truly compelled; but if because of a debt of money or negligence he cannot come, his fellows do not lose their share. And all this is regarding prayer and a chazzan who holds them back, since they need him to complete the minyan, and he too needs them, as Maharam wrote in responsum — it is not in his power to separate himself from them; but an etrog is not comparable to all these, etc. — see there siman 658.
