Half according to wealth. One who examines Hagahos Maimoniyos will see that this is a scribal error, for in this case they pay equally, since each one is obligated to place a person in his stead. If only eight live in the settlement, then they hire two, and they pay half according to wealth and half according to persons, because the poor can go to the nearby city, while the wealthy cannot leave their homes and property; see there. So too the Rama wrote explicitly in a responsum; see there. Maharil wrote in a responsum, siman 114, or 107, that if he is under duress and is not permitted to enter the province, he is exempt even if the festival time has already arrived. This is specifically if he is truly under duress, but if because of a monetary debt or negligence he cannot come, his colleagues do not lose their share. All this concerns prayer and a chazzan when they can prevent him, since they need him to complete the minyan and he also needs it, as Maharam wrote in a responsum: it is not in his power to separate himself from them. But an esrog is not comparable to all these; see what I wrote in siman 658.
