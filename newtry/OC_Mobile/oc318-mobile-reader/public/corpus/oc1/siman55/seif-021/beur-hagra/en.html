Seif 21, a city, etc. As stated in the Tosefta at the end of Bava Metzia, and below at the beginning of siman 150: they compel, etc. There it speaks of one who was a bathhouse attendant, etc., as stated above in siman 53, seif 25.
And if they are, etc. We do not worry that perhaps one of them will need to relieve himself or that some other compulsion will occur, for if so there is no end to the matter, as stated at the beginning of Yoma. So too in the Tosefta cited above regarding a bathhouse attendant, they are not obligated to appoint another in his place except when there is no one else there, as written above. This is not comparable to what is stated in the first chapter of Sotah and at the end of Kiddushin, they taught this only in a city, etc., for forbidden relations are different. Furthermore, if they began, they finish, as above in seif 2.
