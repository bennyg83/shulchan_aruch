Money. One who examines Hagahot Maimoniyot will see that it is a printing error—for in this they pay equally, that each is obligated to supply a man in his place; and if only eight live in the settlement, they hire two and each pays half according to wealth and half according to persons, because the poor can go to the nearby city and the rich cannot leave their house and possessions—Magen Avraham, etc. Maharil wrote in responsa siman 114: if he is compelled so that he is not permitted to come to the country, he is exempt even if the festival time has already arrived—and specifically if he is truly compelled; but if because of a monetary obligation or negligence he cannot come, his fellows will not lose their share. And all this is for the matter of a minyan; but for the matter of an etrog, see above siman 658 seif 9, Magen Avraham. Settlements that must hire a minyan and a chazzan, and there are surrounding areas accustomed to come there also with their household members—they need not give anything to the settlement, for if they wish they may sit in their place or go to another city—Magen Avraham.
