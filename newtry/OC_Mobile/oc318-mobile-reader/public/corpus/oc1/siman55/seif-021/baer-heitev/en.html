Money. One who examines the Hagahos Maimoniyos will see that there is a textual error, for in this case they pay equally, since each person is obligated to provide a man in his place. If only eight live in the settlement, then they hire two and pay half according to wealth and half per capita, because the poor can go to the nearby city whereas the rich cannot leave their houses and property. Magen Avraham; see there. Mahari Weil wrote in responsum, siman 114, that if he is under coercion and is not permitted to come into the province, he is exempt even if the time of the festival has already arrived. This applies specifically if he is truly under coercion, but if because of a monetary obligation or negligence he cannot come, his colleagues do not lose their share. All this concerns a minyan, but regarding an esrog, see above, siman 658, seif 9; see Magen Avraham. Settlements that need to hire a minyan and a chazzan, and there are surrounding areas whose residents are accustomed also to come there with their households, need not give anything to that settlement, for if they wish they may sit in their place or go to another city; see Magen Avraham.
