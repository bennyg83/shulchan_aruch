A city in which there are only ten, and one wishes to leave on the Days of Awe—they compel him to remain or to hire another in his place. If there are eleven and two wish to leave, they hire one jointly in their place and both pay equally. If one is poor and one rich, they pay half according to wealth and half according to persons. The chazzan's wages are charged to those who leave as to those who remain.. And there is no distinction whether he wishes to leave long before Yom Tov or not, provided he will not return on Yom Tov [Maharil siman 427].]:
