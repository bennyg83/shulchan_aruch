[Levush] Because of a non-Jew, etc. For example, a nation that does not permit light to be kindled on its festival except in its temple. Rashi and Ran in Bameh Madlikin explain "an evil spirit" as a condition in which darkness comforts the person. Rambam explains that "evil spirit" refers broadly to illnesses called melancholy in Arabic, in which the patient departs from the balanced human disposition during episodes of confusion; this occurs frequently among people of certain temperaments. Bartenura calls them people of black bile. Perishah writes that some people imagine frightening things if a lamp burns while they sleep, leading to mental disturbance. He says he found no explicit source, but I found it in Abudarham.
