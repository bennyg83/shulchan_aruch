1) [Seif 1] It is permitted to extinguish the lamp, etc. Beis Yosef writes that Tur rules like Rabbi Shimon, that one is exempt for labor not needed for its intrinsic purpose. This is also the view of Ramban, Ran, and Rashba. Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 1 that this is the view of the later authorities. Rambam, however, rules there like Rabbi Yehudah; see there. Similarly, below in siman 316, seif 8, the Shulchan Aruch rules without qualification like Rabbi Shimon that one is exempt for labor not needed for its intrinsic purpose, although it also cites Rambam's view as 'some say'; see there. A practical difference applies to one who inadvertently or intentionally performed labor not needed for its intrinsic purpose and seeks a rectification for the spiritual damage he caused. One does not impose upon him the same stringencies as upon one who performed labor needed for its intrinsic purpose, because most halachic authorities rule like Rabbi Shimon.
