(א) That he not pass them. And if he passed — he is liable (nonetheless Raavad).

(ב) To the place where he drinks. And it is questioned: when drinking it is hanachah, like urinating and spitting; and we only said in seif 2; and when water is in his mouth it is makom patur, since he is in another place and his mouth in another place — unlike urinating and spitting; and that we did not decree lest he take out explicitly, as written in siman 346 s.k. 1, that here it is impossible to drink without placing his mouth; and it is asked regarding his hand too we should say thus if he extends outward — it becomes makom patur, and when he places the object in reshut ha-rabbim — exempt; and one can say that there he did not place the vessel on his hand from another place after he took it out to reshut ha-rabbim, and the same regarding spit — unlike drinking that he gave the water into his mouth; see in the Rosh.
