דין המוציא ראשו ורובו מרשות לרשות. ובו ג סעיפים:
The law of one who takes out his head and most of himself from domain to domain. And in it are 3 seifim: A person stands in reshut ha-yachid and extends his hand to reshut ha-rabbim and moves objects there that are not needed by him in the place where he stands — taking them from here and placing them here — provided he not pass them four amot; and we are not concerned lest he bring them to him, since they are not needed by him in the place where he stands there; therefore it is permitted to stand in reshut ha-rabbim to take a key in reshut ha-yachid and open there; and likewise from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim; but he should not stand in reshut ha-yachid and take out his head to reshut ha-rabbim and drink there, or the reverse — unless he inserts his head and most of himself to the place where he drinks, for since he needs those waters we are concerned lest he bring them to him; but it is permitted to stand in reshut ha-yachid or in reshut ha-rabbim and drink in karmelit, or the reverse; and some explain that objects needed by him means fine vessels that he needs; but if they are vessels that are not fine — it is permitted to drink with them even if he did not take out his head and most of himself but only extended his neck alone; and other moving besides drinking — even fine vessels it is permitted to move, for they only decreed on fine vessels and on drinking with them, for it brings them to his mouth:
