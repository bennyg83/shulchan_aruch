(א) That are not needed by him in the place, etc. — and even if they are fine vessels.

(ב) That he not pass them four amot — meaning: even though he stands in reshut ha-yachid, only that in his hand he moves an object four amot from the place it rests until the end of four amot in reshut ha-rabbim; and if he passed them — he is liable, and even if he raised it above ten tefachim, since in the end he placed it on the ground.

(ג) To take a key in reshut ha-yachid — that is, the key is placed there from before and he takes it from there and opens the door; and it deals with a manner that through taking the key from its place he does not transgress the prohibition of hotza'ah, such as when there is a ganak outside the house and the ganak is made with partitions as the law of reshut ha-yachid, or that the key is lodged there, in the fixed lock of the door; and see above in siman 346 s.k. 2 in the hagahah what we wrote in Mishna Berurah.

(ד) And to open there — and we are not concerned lest he take it out to himself, for why would he take it out, since he does not need it from outside at all.

(ה) And likewise from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim — meaning that he stands in reshut ha-yachid and takes a key placed in reshut ha-rabbim and opens the shop there; and behold there are divisions of law in this and there is no place here to expand on it, and we already explained above in siman 346 s.k. 3 in the hagahah in Mishna Berurah.

(ו) That he needs, etc. — whether the water is placed in fine vessels or they are not fine; and likewise all similar matters in food and clothing, for they are objects needed by him in his place.

(ז) In karmelit, etc. — for reshut ha-rabbim itself is only because of the decree lest he bring them to himself, and we do not decree a decree on a decree.

(ח) And some explain, etc. — and the primary view is like the first explanation (Avodat HaRav).
