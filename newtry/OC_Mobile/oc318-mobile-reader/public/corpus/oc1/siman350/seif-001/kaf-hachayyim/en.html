1) [Se'if 1] Which he does not need, etc. For example, he takes a key located in another domain and opens something there, in that other domain, such that he does not need the key in the domain in which he is standing. However, it is forbidden to carry objects that he needs in the domain in which he is standing, for we are concerned that he may forget and draw them toward himself. Beis Yosef.
2) There. Which he does not need, etc. This applies even if they are attractive utensils. Avodas HaKodesh by Rashba.
3) There. Provided that he does not convey them four amos. This means that even though he is standing in a private domain, it is forbidden if his hand alone conveys an object four amos, from the place where it rests to the end of four amos in a public domain. Taz, subsection 1.
