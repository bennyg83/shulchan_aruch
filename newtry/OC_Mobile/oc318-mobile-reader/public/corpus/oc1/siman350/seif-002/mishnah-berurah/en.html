(א) And urinate or spit, etc. — for what one expels of urine or spit from his body — this is called uprooting; and if he urinated or spat — he is liable for a chatat, for his intent that he needs this makes it a place for him, and it is as if he uprooted from a place that is four tefachim (Gemara).

(ב) Or in karmelit — for since from domain to domain is a d'oraisa prohibition, automatically in karmelit too it is forbidden at least d'rabbanan; and the same if he urinated or spat in reshut ha-rabbim or in karmelit at a distance of four amot (Avodat HaRav).

(ג) His mouth and his amah outward — for we do not follow the place of exit of urine but the place from which urine is uprooted from him, which is the body standing in the other domain; and with his mouth too we rule it follows the body standing in the other domain, and it is as if he took out from domain to domain; and behold from the language of Shulchan Aruch it implies that in karmelit too it is forbidden even when he took out his mouth and amah outward; nevertheless in Biur HaGra he wrote that since this law that we forbid from domain to domain in Torah domains is only because of the Gemara's unresolved doubt, we may be lenient in this in karmelit, which is a rabbinic doubt, and similar to what Shulchan Aruch ruled in such a case above in siman 303 s.k. 23 — see there.
