(א) And urinate or spit, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah; and the same if he throws into a furnace mouth to burn or into a dog's mouth to eat it — his intent makes it a place, liable; but throwing on sticks or anything even intentionally that it fall in that place — not considered as making it a place [Tosafos]:

(ב) In reshut ha-yachid, etc. — and the same if he stood in reshut ha-yachid and urinates and they roll and descend to reshut ha-rabbim; and likewise when urinating in reshut ha-rabbim and they roll and descend to reshut ha-yachid — also forbidden; however if they roll to karmelit — permitted, for they did not decree his force in karmelit [Avodat HaRaz in the name of Riaz]:
