(א) His spit, etc., and ready, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah that specifically when inverted; and it is possible this is also Taz's intent in what he wrote "ready to cast it":

(ב) In reshut ha-rabbim — I am in doubt whether from the Torah or d'rabbanan, for they said it is similar to one who carries a load on Shabbat; and practical difference to decree this law also in karmelit; however from R' Yehuda who says even his spit, etc., it implies he adds to the first Tanna that not only is casting spit from domain to domain forbidden, but even before he took it out of his mouth it is forbidden to pass it four amot; and another matter, and the same to take it out from domain to domain; and according to this it implies for R' Yehuda it is d'oraisa, and also in karmelit like the beginning; and further from Abaye and Rava's argument regarding karmelit in the second Mishnah — from which we infer in this Mishnah all agree forbidden even in karmelit; nevertheless for practical law there is room to study the matter, for since there are those who hold even in reshut ha-rabbim it is permitted [see Beit Yosef], one may rely on their view regarding karmelit; and so R' Zalman inclines; nevertheless it appears one should not be lenient in this except in something that all agree is karmelit, such as a valley and karfaf; but not in our reshut ha-rabbim, for according to many poskim it is Torah reshut ha-rabbim:
