(א) And ready to cast it — see in Avodat HaRav who wrote that specifically when the spit has already turned over in his mouth and stands ready to exit.
(ב) Some say, etc. — even though the Mechaber wrote "some say," one should not move from this, for so is the view of many Rishonim (Avodat HaRav — see there).
(ג) That he not walk four amot — and the same that he should not then go from domain to domain until he spits; and the reason in all this is since it is considered as resting on his shoulder a load.
