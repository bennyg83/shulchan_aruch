1) [Se'if 1] A place where the custom is to eat roasted meat, etc. Ashkenaz is a place whose custom is not to eat roasted meat. Tur; Levush; Taz, subsection 1; Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Chok Yaakov, subsection 2; Chok Yosef, subsection 1; Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 5; Chayei Adam, rule 130, subsection 9; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1. It is not eaten even on the second night. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2; Mishbetzos Zahav, there; Chayei Adam, there; Mishnah Berurah, there. In countries where roasted meat is not eaten on the nights of Pesach, it is likewise prohibited on the night of the fifteenth of Iyar. Even if one argues that since the matter depends upon custom, the custom was adopted only for the fifteenth of Nissan, a whole roasted kid, which is prohibited everywhere, remains prohibited even on the night of the fifteenth of Iyar. Glosses of Rashash, Pesachim 53a, according to Tosafos' alternative explanation. See there.
