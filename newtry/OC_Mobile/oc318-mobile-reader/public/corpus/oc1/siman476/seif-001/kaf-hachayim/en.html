(א) [Seif 1] Place accustomed to eat roast, etc. — Ashkenaz is place not accustomed to eat roast (Tur). So Levush. Taz seif 1, Magen Avraham seif 1, Chok Yaakov note 2, Chak Yosef note 1, Mishnat Hasidim note 1, R' Zalman note 5, Chayei Adam general 131 note 9, Mishna Berurah note 1; even on second night they do not eat (Eshel Avraham note 2, Mishnat Hasidim there, Chayei Adam there, Mishna Berurah there). In these countries not accustomed to eat roast on Passover nights, forbidden also on night of 15 Iyar; even if we say since matter depends on custom we only meant 15 Nisan — nevertheless kid goat roasted forbidden everywhere, its prohibition even on nights of 15 Iyar (Hagahot Rashash Pesachim 53a per those who wrote Tosafot — see there).
