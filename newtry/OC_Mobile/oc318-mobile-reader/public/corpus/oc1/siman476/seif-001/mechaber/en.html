The custom of eating roasted meat on the night of Pesach. Containing 2 seifim: In
a place where they are accustomed to eat roasted meat on Pesach nights, they eat it. In a place where they are accustomed not to eat it, they do not eat it — a decree lest people say it is the Pesach offering. And in every place it is forbidden to eat an entire roasted lamb in one piece on this night, because it appears like one eating kodashim outside [the Temple]. But if it was cut up, or one limb was missing, or one limb was boiled while still attached, it is permitted in a place where they are accustomed.And one should not eat or drink excessively, so that one not eat the afikoman with coarse eating, or become drunk and sleep immediately (Maharil}.):
