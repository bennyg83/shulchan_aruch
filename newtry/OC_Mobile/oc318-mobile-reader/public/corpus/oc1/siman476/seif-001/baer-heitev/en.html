Custom. In these countries they are not accustomed to eat roast (Levush). Roasted then boiled is permitted; but boiled then roasted pot roast should be forbidden due to marit ayin; nevertheless for a sick person somewhat lenient (Magen Avraham; so Chok Yaakov); Pri Chadash forbids both roasted-then-boiled and boiled-then-roasted — see there.
