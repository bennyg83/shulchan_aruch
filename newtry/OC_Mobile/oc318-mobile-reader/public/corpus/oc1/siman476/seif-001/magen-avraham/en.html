(א) To eat roasted meat. And in these countries they are not accustomed to eat roasted meat (Levush). And it appears to me: if they roasted and afterward cooked it is permitted; but if they cooked and afterward made it roasted in a pot, one should forbid because of marit ayin, for they are stringent even on poultry meat. However for a sick person's need one may be lenient in this, as above; see in the Gemara.

(ב) Coarse eating. Even if he is not desirous of eating — even though Pesach is eaten on a full stomach — nevertheless he must be desirous of eating (Bach siman 471). Beit Yosef wrote in siman 477 in the name of Rokeach: the eating of afikoman should not be with burden, for we say in Nazir: if she ate for coarse eating, transgressors will stumble in them; and if he finished his meal he does not fulfill at all, as written in siman 612 seif 6.
