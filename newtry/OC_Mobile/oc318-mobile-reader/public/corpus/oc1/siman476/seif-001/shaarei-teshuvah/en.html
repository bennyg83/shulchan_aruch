Custom. Baer Heitev; see responsa Beit Yaakov siman 24 which also permits roasted then boiled — see there; regarding pot roast Chok Yaakov wrote even boiled then roasted pot roast is forbidden due to marit ayin — see there; so too Mishna Berurah in name of responsa k'tav yad; he wrote anything in a pot with gravy, even if afterward it condenses and no liquid remains, is not roast but a stew and is permitted.
