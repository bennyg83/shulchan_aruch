Even veal or poultry, anything that requires shechitah — it is forbidden to eat it roasted in a place where they are accustomed not to eat roasted meat.In some places they are accustomed to eat eggs at the meal in commemoration of mourning; and it seems to me the reason is because the night of Tisha B'av is set based on the night of Pesach; and further, commemoration of the destruction of the Temple, when they would bring the Pesach offering. And some are accustomed not to eat any dipping on that night except the two dippings done in the Seder (Maharil}.):
