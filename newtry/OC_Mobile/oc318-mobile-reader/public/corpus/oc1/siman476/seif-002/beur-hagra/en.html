(א) Even so, etc. — Rava asked before R' Ami: even calf meat? He said to him: even calf meat. Even poultry meat? He said to him: even poultry meat. They are inclined to say: even an egg. Even a kuliis. R' Yehuda bar Chanin says: only from slaughtered [meat].

(ב) They are accustomed, etc. — and it appears to me the reason: they make it commemoration of chagigah and must eat; what they do not eat the foreleg because they make it roasted as above siman 473 and they do not eat roast, and they fulfill with afikoman they eat.

(ג) Because of Tisha B'av night. Many drashot on verse 'satiate me with bitterness': this is the first night of Pesach as written 'on bitter herbs they bitterly weep' — what satiated me on the first night of Pesach they bitterly weep on Tisha B'av night; the first night of Pesach is Tisha B'av night.

(ד) And some are accustomed. One may bring support from what is written chapter 5 of Berachot 'if he comes to drink...'
