The night of Tisha B'Av is set on Pesach night. Meaning, just as the first day of Pesach falls, Tisha B'Av will forever be on that same day; and a mnemonic: upon matzos and bitter herbs they shall eat it; and as is explained above in siman 428, in the mnemonic for the calendrical setting of the festivals, Alef-Tav, Beis-Shin; see there. And according to this, on the second night there is no need to eat them; however, according to the second reason one should eat them also on the second night. And see above, siman 473: that they would offer the Pesach offering and now it has been abolished. And another reason: because egg in Aramaic is be'a, [hinting] that the Merciful One desired to redeem us; and this reason applies to the eggs on the Seder plate. And see above, siman 473.
