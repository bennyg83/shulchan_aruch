(א) Even veal and poultry, etc. Tur in name of Avi Ezri concludes it is certainly a kosher custom to eat cooked food where they are accustomed not to eat roast; Beit Yosef explained strained explanation; it appears to exclude some stringency not to eat any meat at all on Passover night lest they err and say it is roast he eats — this is not a kosher custom, for then one nullifies Yom Tov joy; better eat cooked meat and not be concerned for errant ones; siman 486 wrote foreleg of lamb.

(ב) Eggs in commemoration of mourning. Siman 473 Beit Yosef brought Kol Bo: the egg taken for one of the two dishes is also commemoration of mourning; I already wrote there that roasted eggs are forbidden, as Mordechai chapter 20 in name of Yerushalmi: Rava asked before R' Ami — even veal, even poultry, they thought even egg, even kuliis (fish); R' Yehuda bar Chanin says only slaughtered species — end; implies from conclusion eggs and fish permitted, only slaughtered species; though initially they thought egg and fish prohibit roast — not established; Tur and Shulchan Aruch only prohibited slaughtered species; I wonder at Maharshal who wrote even roasted egg forbidden as Mordechai — how bring proof from what was thought and not left in conclusion; appears no roast prohibition on egg and not custom.

(ג) Only two dippings. See siman 479.
