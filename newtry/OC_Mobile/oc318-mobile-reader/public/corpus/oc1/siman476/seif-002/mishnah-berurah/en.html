(8) Even meat of calf and fowl: That is to say, even though the Pesach offering does not come from them, nevertheless it is forbidden to eat it roasted, because people will confuse roast with roast and come to permit also roasted meat from sheep and goats.
(9) That requires shechitah: Excluding fish and eggs, which are permitted to eat roasted in every place, because they do not resemble meat at all and people will not err with them. And even the egg that they place on the Seder plate is permitted by roasting, and to eat it (Taz).
(10) In a place where they practiced not etc.: But in a place where they practiced to eat roast, it is permitted to eat even a calf that was roasted entirely as one, for the Pesach offering does not come from it.
(11) And it appears to me the reason etc.: And the Gra wrote a further reason: because the egg is a remembrance of the Chagigah offering, as is explained above in siman 473, se'if 4; and therefore one must eat it as well. And as for why they do not eat the zeroa, it is because they make it roasted, as is written in siman 473, and they do not eat roast, and they fulfill with the afikoman that they eat; end of his words. And according to this, it appears that one should take care to eat also on the first night that egg which is on the plate; and as for other eggs that they eat, it is because the custom became established.
(12) The night of Tisha B'Av is set etc.: Meaning, just as the first day of Pesach falls, Tisha B'Av will forever be on that same day, as is explained above in siman 428; see there.
(13) That they would offer: And they mourn over this; and according to this reason the custom applies also on the second day (Chok Yaakov). And so it is also according to the Gra's reason mentioned above. And nevertheless one must know that according to all the reasons, the eggs that they eat during the meal are only a mere remembrance, and not like the common people who think this is a mitzvah and for that reason fill their belly until they eat the afikoman as gross eating; therefore a person must know this for himself (Chayei Adam).
(14) Only two dippings: And as for dipping also the maror that is on the korech in charoses, that is not a third dipping, for it is only because of a doubt alone (Taz in siman 475).
