Ornamental trees (ilanei serak). Taz brought that he said here only regarding inferior species that are usual in forest trees, such as small apples and pears that are not fit for eating when raw; but strawberries — when they remain long on the bush — are good to eat them raw; one blesses borei peri haEtz. And we every day bless borei peri haEtz on small nuts gathered from forest trees — end of his words; and so too Magen Avraham. And see Yad Ephraim.
