4) [Seif 2] Over berries that grow on a shrub, one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah. This is the view of R' Yosef cited by the Tur. In the name of R' Yitzchak, however, the Tur writes that one recites Borei Pri HaEtz, and writes that his father Rosh, of blessed memory, practiced accordingly; see there. Bach writes that the present practice follows the Shulchan Aruch to recite Borei Pri HaAdamah, and that this is proper and correct in order to avoid a doubtful blessing; see there. Levush likewise writes that this is the practice, as do the later authorities. See below, paragraph 7.
