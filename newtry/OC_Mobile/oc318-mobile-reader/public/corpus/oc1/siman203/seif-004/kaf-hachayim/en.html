(4) [Seif 2] On strawberries that grow on a bush — borei peri haAdamah. This is the view of R' Yosef, as Tur wrote; but in the name of R' Yitzchak Tur wrote that one blesses on them borei peri haEtz, and he wrote that thus was the practice of his father the Rosh, may his memory be blessed — see there. And Bach wrote that nowadays they practice according to Shulchan Aruch to bless borei peri haAdamah, and so is correct and proper — to remove oneself from safeq berakha — see there. And so too Levush — thus they practice. And so too the latter authorities. And see below note 7.
