Ornamental trees (ilanei serak) — that is, they grow on their own without sowing and planting, and are not fit to be eaten raw but only through cooking and frying; therefore their blessing is shehakol. But those that grow through sowing and planting — even when they are not good to eat raw — after they are cooked, on tree fruit one blesses borei peri haEtz, and on ground fruit borei peri haAdamah [acharonim].
