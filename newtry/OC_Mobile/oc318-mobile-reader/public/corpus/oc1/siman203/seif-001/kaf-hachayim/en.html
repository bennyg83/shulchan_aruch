(1) [Seif 1] On fruits of the ground one blesses borei peri haAdamah. And that which one does not bless borei peri haEretz like the blessing on bread — see the reason in our words above siman 167, note 24. And Birkei Yosef in Sha'ar 2, note 2, wrote that the intent is that the earth should be blessed and continue to receive abundance through the blessing — to repair the curse "cursed is the ground." And he further wrote that he heard there are 55 ground fruits, and therefore they enacted borei peri haAdamah — gimatria 55 — end of his words. And in Shem Mishmuel, parashat Ekev, he wrote that it was explained regarding the blessing on water — shehakol nihyeh bidvaro — for the yesod is called kol and influences below through malchut, which is called devaro; and behold, by this one should intend in all blessings of enjoyment; and the parable in this: in borei peri haEtz one should intend that borei peri is the yesod and the tree is in malchut; and likewise in borei peri haGefen, and so in every blessing of enjoyment — end of his words. And so it is found regarding borei peri haAdamah — that borei peri is the yesod and the adamah is in malchut. And it appears that this is hinted in the word haAdamah, for it is known that malchut is in the aspect of hei (5), because it is hinted in the final hei of the name Havayah; and it is also in the aspect of the name ban (52), which is the name Havayah with the filling of the hei, as is known; and adamah — gematria ban. And adamah includes ban. And therefore they enacted to bless borei peri haAdamah — to hint at the aspect of malchut, which is hei adamah — meaning that it is in the aspect of hei, and also adamah is gematria ban. And therefore one must be careful to pronounce the hei of haAdamah well in speech.
