1) [Seif 1] Over produce of the earth, one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah. Regarding why one does not say, 'Who creates fruit of the earth,' using the same term as in the blessing over bread, see our words above in siman 167, paragraph 24. Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 2, writes that the Sages intended that the ground be blessed and abundance be drawn to it through the blessing, thereby repairing the curse, 'Cursed is the ground.' He further writes that he heard there are fifty-five ground fruits, and therefore they instituted Borei Pri HaAdamah, whose Hebrew words have the numerical value of fifty-five. Shaar HaMitzvos, parashas Eikev, writes that regarding the blessing over water, Shehakol Nihyah Bidvaro, Yesod is called 'all' and bestows below through Malchus, which is called 'His word.' One should direct similar intent in all blessings over enjoyment. For example, in Borei Pri HaEtz, 'Who creates the fruit' is Yesod and 'the tree' is Malchus. The same applies to Borei Pri HaGafen and all similar blessings. We find that the same applies to Borei Pri HaAdamah: 'Who creates the fruit' is Yesod and 'the ground' is Malchus. It appears that this is alluded to by the Hebrew word haadamah. As is known, Malchus corresponds to the letter heh because it is alluded to by the final letter heh of the Name Havayah. It also corresponds to the Name whose numerical value is fifty-two, the spelling of Havayah with letters heh. The numerical value of adamah, with its word unit and the additional collective unit, is fifty-two. Therefore, the Sages instituted the blessing Borei Pri HaAdamah to allude to Malchus, which is the letter heh and adamah, meaning that it corresponds to the letter heh and that adamah has the numerical value of the Name fifty-two. One must therefore take care to pronounce clearly the letter heh of haadamah.
