(1) The strawberries, etc. — that they call in German "moyl," and "malinche"; but in some countries there are types of "moyl" in German, such as in the land of Austria, that grow on a tree — and then one blesses borei peri haEtz — so wrote Orach Chayim. And Beit Yosef wrote siman 204, and this is his language: R' David Abudraham wrote — on sesame one blesses borei peri haAdamah; ground — one blesses shehakol on them; on their oil and waste one blesses borei peri haAdamah — until here his language. And he does not know what reason there is to bless on the waste borei peri haAdamah — until here. And I found like the words of R' David Abudraham in Ba'al Halakhot Gedolot, page 7, and this is his language: on their ointments and husks — borei peri haAdamah. Magen Avraham wrote: on "romberin" and "erpherit" — borei peri haEtz; and likewise black jagodas. But the red ones grow among grasses — borei peri haAdamah. And those red ones found in Tamuz — and there is also a type found in winter that has nothing but mere sap; they suck them out and discard them — one blesses shehakol, even if one swallows them with the peel.
