Mulberries, etc. These are the berries called mulberries and raspberries. In some countries, however, there are types of mulberries, such as in Austria, that grow on a tree, and over them one recites Borei Pri HaEtz, as Orchot Chaim writes. Beit Yosef in siman 204 quotes Rabbi David Abudarham: over sesame seeds one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah; if ground, Shehakol; over their oil and press-cake, Borei Pri HaAdamah. Beit Yosef says he does not know why the press-cake should receive Borei Pri HaAdamah. I found Rabbi David Abudarham's ruling explicitly in Baal Halachot Gedolot, page 7: over their oil and press-cake one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah. Magen Avraham writes that blackberries and certain berries in Erfurt receive Borei Pri HaEtz, as do black currants, whereas red ones grow on herbs and receive Borei Pri HaAdamah. Those red berries found in Tammuz, and another type found in winter, that contain only juice which one sucks out and then discards the berry, receive Shehakol even if one swallows them with the peel.
