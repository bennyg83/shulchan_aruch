(3) They said in Yerushalmi: a person takes a watermelon peduncle and an apple peduncle and grafts them, and they become kilayim — meaning, and fruit mixed of watermelon and apple is produced. And it appears that one blesses borei peri haAdamah; but if its stock changes, it is a complete tree — Radbaz in his explanation to Rambam, end of chapter 1 of Kilayim. Birkei Yosef in Sha'ar 2, note 1.
