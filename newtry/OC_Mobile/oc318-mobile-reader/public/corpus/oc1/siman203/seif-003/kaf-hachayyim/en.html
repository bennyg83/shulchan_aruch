3) The Yerushalmi states: A person takes the stem from a melon and the stem from an apple, grafts them together, and they become kilayim. This means that a mixed fruit of melon and apple is produced. It appears that one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah over it. If its trunk renews itself, however, it is a complete tree. Radbaz in his commentary on the Rambam at the end of chapter 1 of Hilchos Kilayim; Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 1.
