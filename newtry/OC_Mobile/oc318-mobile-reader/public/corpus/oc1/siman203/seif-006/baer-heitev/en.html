Ginger is the main element. And the same applies when they roast ginger and eat it for medicinal purposes — one blesses borei peri haAdamah. And likewise on peppers that they preserve — borei peri haAdamah; but on candied citron (etz citron) one blesses shehakol — end of Magen Avraham's words. Dry ginger and dry peppers that a non-Jew cooked are permitted. Magen Avraham.
