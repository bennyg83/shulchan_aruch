(2) [Levush] Like a tree. Prisha wrote, and one should not challenge: behold we bless on small nuts borei peri haEtz — for one may say that whatever whose type grows in a garden, one does not bless on ilanei serak — until here. And Taz wrote the reasoning to the contrary and concluded that we do not say that on ilanei serak one blesses shehakol except regarding inferior species, such as small apples and pears; and the same law applies to banim asa. But strawberries, small nuts, and other things that are good for eating — one blesses on them borei peri haEtz, even though they grow in the forest; and so is implied in Magen Avraham. Olas Tamid wrote regarding preserved "krein" — it appears to me that one blesses borei peri haAdamah, even though it is not fit for eating before preserving except through a mixture of vinegar; nevertheless, since they are planted for this purpose, it is very similar to dry ginger — until here. And to me this requires study, for it is not comparable to ginger, which can be eaten in its moist state; rather it is comparable to anise and alexanders, which I wrote in siman 204 seif 1 that one blesses shehakol. He further wrote regarding preserved peel of bergamot fruits — one blesses shehakol, like a soft walnut; and Magen Avraham disagrees, for regarding a walnut they were not planted with intent to eat them when soft; but bergamot peel is a complete fruit and one blesses borei peri haEtz. And in siman 202 note 17 he upheld his words at length; and Taz in siman 204 extended and wrote to bless borei peri haAdamah. And in my humble opinion, bergamot peel too was not planted with intent to eat them preserved — rather, for the most part they discard it after scenting with it, since it is not fit for eating at all without preserving; also our eyes see that it is impossible to eat them raw at all — unlike the words of Magen Avraham; rather they pound them after they dry and mix them into spices; therefore, through preserving, it appears to me to bless shehakol and rely on the general rule. And see below siman 204 note 5.
