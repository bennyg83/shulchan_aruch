(2) There — borei peri haAdamah. On eggplant (badinjan) — borei peri haAdamah. Rabbenu Avraham ben haRambam in his novellae at the head of Ma'aseh Rokeach. And so too Maran, may his memory be blessed, in Beit Yosef in the name of responsa of the Geonim. And automatically it spreads that one need not be stringent regarding eggplant on account of orlah. And even though one can reject this — we already wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 294, note 4, that the holy Arizal and Maharchu ate them — see there. Birkei Yosef, note 1: and even though Radbaz siman 266 wrote to bless borei peri haEtz, and Shakh Ne'eman HaGadol brought it in Hagahat Tur, note 1 — we are lenient like the view of those who say borei peri haAdamah on account of safeq berakha; and witness: since the holy ones above were not concerned for orlah, if so they too agree that it is not a tree and its blessing is borei peri haAdamah — and therefore we hold thus. And so the people say. And this is when cooked; but if one eats it raw, one should bless only shehakol, as written below siman 204 — see there. And further: see below note 13.
