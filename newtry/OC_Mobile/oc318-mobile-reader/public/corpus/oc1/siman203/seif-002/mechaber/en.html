On strawberries that grow in a bush one recites borei peri haAdamah:for it is not called a tree except regarding what it produces from its trunk; but what it produces from its roots is not called a tree. And these, since their trunks are completely consumed in winter and again blossom from their roots, one blesses upon them borei peri haAdamah. [Tur and Mordechai in the name of responsa of the Geonim.]]:
