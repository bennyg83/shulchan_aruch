Seif 2 — not like the above — so too Mordechai and Tur gave the reason regarding strawberries; and a Gaon wrote that thus it is in the Tosefta; and so too Rosh in the name of the Tosefta. And this is the language of the Tosefta, chapter 3 of Kilayim: This is the rule — whatever produces from its trunk is a vegetable, and whatever does not produce from its trunk is a tree. And Yerushalmi, chapter 5 of Kilayim — R' Chiya bar Pappa taught: that which grows from its trunk is a tree species; from its roots — a vegetable species. And see Yoreh De'ah siman 206, seif 15.
