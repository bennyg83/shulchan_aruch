Seif 2: Contrary to, etc. So too write Mordechai and the Tur regarding the reason for mulberries, and a Gaon wrote that this is stated in the Tosefta. The Rosh likewise writes in the name of the Tosefta. The language of the Tosefta at the end of chapter 3 of Kilayim is: This is the rule: anything whose leaves emerge from its base is a vegetable, and anything whose leaves do not emerge from its base is a tree. The Yerushalmi at the end of chapter 5 of Kilayim states: Rabbi Chiya bar Pappa taught: that which grows from its trunk is a type of tree; that which grows from its roots is a type of vegetable. See Yoreh Deah, siman 296, seif 15.
