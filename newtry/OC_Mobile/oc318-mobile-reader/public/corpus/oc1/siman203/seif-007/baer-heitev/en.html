The spices are the main element. Sugar with ginger, grated — and even if there is much sugar like the ginger — nevertheless the sugar is considered secondary relative to the ginger, and one blesses on the ginger borei peri haAdamah and exempts the sugar — Taz. On sesame and Holland rose one blesses borei peri haAdamah; ground — shehakol — Shlah. And likewise sesame that they fry in honey — one blesses borei peri haAdamah, unlike some who err to bless shehakol — responsa Pnei Meiros siman 65; and see siman 204 seif 11.
