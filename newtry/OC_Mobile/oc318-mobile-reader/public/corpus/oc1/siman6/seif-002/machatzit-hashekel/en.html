Comment 2, "That is," etc. As our teacher wrote. Because of the above difficulty that a person closes his mouth, etc., as written in Rabbeinu Yonah.

Forty-three words. Whereas if we said "even one hour" there would be three extra words and it would be forty-six words. "Before You" — if so, two words are missing and there would be only forty-one words. Forty-five words — if so, one must say he reads "even one hour." But if so there are forty-six words. It appears the reading is not "before the throne of Your glory" but "before You," and two words are missing — forty-four; except the Agudah reads not "choli kol basar" as the Magen Avraham wrote in the comment after this. Per the ancient manuscripts one must read "choli" and there are forty-five words.
