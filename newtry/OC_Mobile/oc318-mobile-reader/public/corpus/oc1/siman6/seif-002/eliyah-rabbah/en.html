[2] And in any case, etc. The shaliach tzibbur has permission to bless at home and to return and bless in the synagogue because they are not proficient — so wrote the Shelah and the Taz. But the congregation: whoever blesses at home has no permission at all to bless in the synagogue; and whoever wishes to bless in the synagogue should not bless at home. The early and later authorities have already elaborated on this. The Knesset HaGedolah wrote, but in the responsum of Rama of Trani and R' Shmuel of Modena they challenged this custom and wrote: it is better that one person bless and the rest intend to fulfill their obligation and answer Amen, and not insert themselves into a blessing in vain. The Knesset HaGedolah wrote in the name of Sefer Masat Melech: whether in a place that practices like the Shulchan Aruch and Levush, or in a place that practices like R' Moshe of Trani and the Rashdam — one may not prevent them from their custom.
