“Even,” Taz: You will see the master z”l had a unique intent in what he wrote; and others explained it refers to the hour of formation, for Beit Yosef explained to the Tur it refers to emerging into the world’s air; but he z”l explained formation in his mother’s womb — if the navel seals and the mouth opens, as said in letter 1, even a moment he could not survive; and one who does not have the reading “hour” explains like Rashi. Perhaps in Abudarham or Kol Bo he found it written thus, and others explained; I have not found it now.
