Even. See Taz. The master intended a specific point when he wrote that “others explain” the phrase as referring to the time of formation. Beit Yosef explains the Tur as referring to emergence into the air of the world, whereas Taz explains it as referring to formation in the womb: if the navel were closed and the mouth open, survival would be impossible even briefly, as explained in paragraph 1. One who does not have the reading “for one hour” explains it like Rashi. Perhaps Taz found the phrase “others explain” in Abudarham or Kol Bo, though I have not presently found it.
