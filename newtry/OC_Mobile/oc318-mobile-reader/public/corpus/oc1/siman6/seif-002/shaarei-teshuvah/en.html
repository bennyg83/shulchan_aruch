Ei efshar. Ba'er Heitev — and his words are from Ateret Zekenim; but as fixed in everyone’s mouth they say the alef with a hirik, and so in the siddur of Maharaz He'ana; it appears the word ei with a tsere need not be with hirik — it has another meaning, e.g. inhabitants of the island; nevertheless we find ei kavod and ei naki, and though some interpret there differently, one may rely on Rashi there that his explanation is ein (there is not); therefore one should not change from what is fixed in everyone’s mouth to read ei efshar with the alef pointed hirik — “leave Israel alone,” etc.
