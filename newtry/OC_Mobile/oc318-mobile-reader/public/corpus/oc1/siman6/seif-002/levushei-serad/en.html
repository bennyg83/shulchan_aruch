Ibid. Meaning, among the openings. Beit Yosef thus explains the view of the Tur on the basis of Rashi, who writes that “were one of them to open” refers to the hollow organs, while “were one of them to close” refers to the openings. The Tur was troubled by the expression “even for one hour,” and therefore explained it as referring to the moment when a person emerges into the air of the world. Beit Yosef and the Shulchan Aruch explain here that “if one of them were to close” means one particular opening, namely the mouth alone, while “if one of them were to open” means whichever one of them might open, even after birth. The Shulchan Aruch was troubled that the phrase “one of them” would then have a different meaning in the clause concerning closing than in the clause concerning opening. He was also troubled that the closing of the mouth at birth has no connection to relieving oneself. He therefore writes, “Another explanation is,” etc., and concludes, “Since the general meaning,” etc.
