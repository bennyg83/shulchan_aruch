Ibid. Meaning, among the openings. So explained the Beis Yosef — the Tur's view according to Rashi, who wrote that "if one were opened" refers to the hollow organs and "if one were sealed" to the openings; the Tur found difficult the wording "one hour." Therefore he explained it when one emerges into the world's air; and on this the Beis Yosef and Shulchan Aruch explain here that "one of them" regarding sealing means one specific opening — it refers only to the mouth alone; and "one of them" regarding opening means any one of them, even after they have come into the world. The Shulchan Aruch found difficult that there should be a change in explaining one of them between sealed and opened; he also found difficult that sealing at the time of emerging into the air has no connection to relieving oneself; therefore he wrote "and one can further explain," etc., and concluded "and since included," etc.
