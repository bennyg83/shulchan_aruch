Some have the custom to wait to bless on hand-washing until they come to the synagogue and they arrange them with the other blessings; Sephardim do not do so. In any case one does not bless twice: one who blessed them at home does not bless in synagogue, and one who blessed in synagogue does not bless at home (<i>Kol Bo</i> siman 2). One who learns before entering synagogue or prays before [going] blesses [hand-washing] at home and does not bless in synagogue; even in such cases some arrange it with the other blessings in the synagogue and do not bless at home (Maharil, Laws of Prayer).
