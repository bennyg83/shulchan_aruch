Some have the custom to wait to bless al netilas yadayim until they come to synagogue and arrange it with the other blessings; Sephardim do not do so. (In any case one does not bless twice: one who blessed at home does not bless in synagogue, and one who blessed in synagogue does not bless at home (Kol Bo siman 2). One who learns before entering synagogue or prays first blesses at home and does not bless in' synagogue; even in such a case some arrange it with the other blessings in synagogue and do not bless at home (Maharil, Laws of Prayer).
