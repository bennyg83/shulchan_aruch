2) [Seif 2] Some practice waiting, etc. All the later authorities agree that one should not interrupt between the blessing Al Netilas Yadayim and the washing. Rather, immediately after washing one must recite Al Netilas Yadayim. Some write that one should recite the blessing after drying, as stated above in siman 4, but the practice is not so; see what we wrote above in siman 4, paragraphs 1 and 8. If, at the time he washes his hands, he needs to relieve himself; so that even words of Torah are forbidden, and even if he can restrain himself for the time needed to walk a parsah; he should not recite Al Netilas Yadayim until he has relieved himself. He should then recite Al Netilas Yadayim and Asher Yatzar. Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 5; see there. Although in this case he does not recite Al Netilas Yadayim immediately after washing, he must nevertheless raise his hands upward toward his head, etc., while reciting Al Netilas Yadayim, as stated above in siman 4, paragraph 16. This is obvious.
