Seif 2, "some are accustomed," etc. To discharge the obligation of the ignorant, as the Rosh wrote.

"And members," etc. Initially one should recite the blessing before performing the act; nevertheless one should not delay too much.

"And in any case," etc. For everyone is expert in them, and it is only a mere concern.

"And one who studies," etc. For it is a great interruption.
