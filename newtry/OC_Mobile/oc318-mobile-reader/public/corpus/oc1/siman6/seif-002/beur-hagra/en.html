Seif 2, "some are accustomed," etc. To discharge the obligation of the ignorant, as the Rosh wrote.

"And Sephardim," etc. Ab initio one should recite the blessing before performing the act; nevertheless one should not delay excessively.

"And in any case," etc. For everyone is proficient in them, and it is only a general concern.

"And one who studies," etc. For it is a great interruption.
