2) [Seif 2] Some have the practice to wait, etc. All the later authorities agree that one must not interrupt between the blessing on netilas yadayim and the washing; as soon as one washes he must bless on netilas yadayim — except that some wrote one must bless after drying, as stated above, siman 4, and they did not practice so (see our words above, siman 4, seif 1, letter 8). If at the time he washes his hands he needs to relieve himself — even for words of Torah it is forbidden, and even if he can restrain himself up to a parsah — he must not bless on netilas yadayim until he has relieved himself and blesses on netilas yadayim and Asher Yatzar. Shulchan Aruch, letter 5 — see there. And although one does not bless on netilas yadayim immediately adjacent to the washing, one must nonetheless raise his hands upward toward the head, etc., at the time of the blessing on netilas yadayim, as stated above, siman 4, letter 16 of the Taz — and this is obvious.
