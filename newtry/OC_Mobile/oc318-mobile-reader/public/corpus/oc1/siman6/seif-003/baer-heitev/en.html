The thanksgiving [blessing]. And it is not a blessing adjacent to its fellow, therefore one may say it in the morning until Elohai Neshamah. And in the synagogue he should begin with Elohai Neshamah — Tur and Bach wrote that nevertheless one should say Elohai Neshamah immediately; see there, and so wrote Levush. And Be'er Heitev wrote to say all the blessings until "for His people Israel"; and Magen Avraham wrote on this, "I have not seen the elder rabbis act thus"; see there. [Written] in Seder Hayom: one who rises early and will need to sleep again should say Elohai Neshamah and Hamapil chevlei [sleep], etc., without a concluding formula, and when he rises again he should say them with a concluding formula, for some say that even during the day when one sleeps one says Elohai Neshamah.
