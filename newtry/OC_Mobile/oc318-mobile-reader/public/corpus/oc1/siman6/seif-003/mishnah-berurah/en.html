(יב) Blessing of thanks — and it is not a blessing adjacent to its fellow; therefore one may say upon rising in the morning up to Elohai Neshamah, and in synagogue begin Elohai Neshamah [Tur]; nevertheless one should act on the side of better conduct to adjoin Elohai Neshamah to Asher Yatzar; see Biur HaGra siman 46 that his view too is Elohai Neshamah adjoins to Asher Yatzar — therefore correct to be careful ab initio. If he did not bless Elohai Neshamah until after praying the eighteen blessings, he does not bless again after prayer, since he already said mechayeh hametim he is similar to one who restores souls [Peri Chadash]; in Shaarei Teshuvah he greatly questions this law and concludes that in any case if at the time he said mechayeh hametim he had in mind to bless Elohai Neshamah after prayer, he may bless afterward; see there the reason; likewise implied in Peri Megadim siman 52; see what is written there in Biur Halachah. In the text of the blessing one should not say “You created me” with the extra word “in,” but “You created [me], You breathed into me.”
