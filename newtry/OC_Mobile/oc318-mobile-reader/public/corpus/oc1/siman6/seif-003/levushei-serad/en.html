(On Taz, seif katan 1) The Tur writes, "that if it should open." This is difficult, for the Beit Yosef explicitly explains that "should open" applies even after a person has entered the world, as we wrote initially. It is obvious that this is a scribal error and should read, "should close." Taz intends to challenge the Beit Yosef, who treats the Tur's expressions separately, in accordance with Rashi, so that "should open" refers to the cavities and "should close" refers to the openings. If so, the Tur should have written "and." Rather, it is evident that he does not agree with Rashi. Accordingly, "should open" certainly refers only to the cavities, since the openings are always open. By contrast, "should close" implies both the cavities and the openings. Yet according to the Beit Yosef, who understands it as referring only to the mouth and to the beginning of creation, it applies only to the openings. Pri Megadim did not interpret this correctly.
