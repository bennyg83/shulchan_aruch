Taz, comment 1. The Tur's words that "if one were opened" — it is astonishing, for the Beis Yosef explicitly explained that "if one were opened" means even after he has come into the world, as we wrote initially; and plainly it is a scribal error and it should read "if one were sealed." The Taz's intent is to challenge the Beis Yosef, who makes the Tur's words fit one of them like Rashi — "if one were opened" on the hollow organs and "if one were sealed" on the openings — for then the Tur should have written "and if one were opened," since he does not hold like Rashi. If so, it is well that "opened" certainly refers only to the hollow organs, for the openings are open forever; unlike "sealed," which implies both hollow organs and openings. But per the Beis Yosef, who refers only to the mouth alone at the beginning of creation — then it refers only to openings, and the Peri Megadim did not aim well.
