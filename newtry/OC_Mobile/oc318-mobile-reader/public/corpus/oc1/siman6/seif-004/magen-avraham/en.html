“They do.” It appears to me they do so in order to answer amen ninety times, as stated in Zohar Chadash and in a responsum of the Maharam of Prague — see there.

“So as not to be discharged.” Levush wrote because usually there is no minyan in synagogue and the shaliach tzibbur does not discharge fewer than ten—see siman 59:4 in the gloss.
