“Some,” Taz: Abudarham in the name of Mahari Abuhav — al netilas yadayim precedes its performance before drying; and truly for a meal it is fine that drying hinders, as though eating impure bread, unlike morning when dry hands do not hinder — see Magen Avraham 4:6 and 158:12; nevertheless whatever blessing can be adjoined we adjoin, and even a blessing of praise should be immediately after seeing; and where one did not bless, within twenty-four hours one blesses afterward, as Taz wrote Berachot 27b Yerushalmi (see Ran end of chapter Pesachim); and likewise Shekhinah, understanding, and the like — within twenty-four hours the Sages did not give a measure of immediacy; and they arrange them in synagogue to exempt others — unlike al netilas yadayim, which immediately when he takes [water], before drying, he blesses, for that is the measure of immediacy; and all the more so for Rambam siman 46 who blesses according to worldly custom — obviously al netilas yadayim he blesses immediately; therefore he practiced like Sephardim. Abudarham reconciled this: Ashkenazim practice according to Rashba — al netilas yadayim too is a blessing of praise — see there; Taz 4:1:6:1 — understand.
