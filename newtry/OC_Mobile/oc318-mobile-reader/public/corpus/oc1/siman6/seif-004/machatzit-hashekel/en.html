For otherwise how would the spiritual bind to the physical, and how would the soul exist in the body through physical eating — and exit the body through withholding food, for the soul does not enjoy physical eating? On this basis the Arizal explained the verse "for not by bread alone shall man live, but by everything that proceeds from the mouth of the the Master shall man live" — "the mouth of the the Master" means the spirituality in every food, for "by the breath of His mouth all their host" were created. As our Sages said: there is no herb below without a constellation above, etc. — meaning that He influences it through a holy power given from Heaven to influence what is appointed over it.
