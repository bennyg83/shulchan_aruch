(Seif katan 4) Spiritual, etc. Through this they are bound to one another. Otherwise, how could the spiritual be joined to the physical, and how could the soul remain in the body through physical eating? Conversely, why should it leave the body when food is withheld, since the soul itself derives no benefit from physical food? On this basis Arizal explained the verse, “For man does not live by bread alone, but by everything that proceeds from the mouth of Hashem does man live.” “The mouth of Hashem” means the spiritual vitality within every food, for by the breath of His mouth all their hosts were created. As our Sages said, there is no blade of grass below that does not have a mazal above that strikes it and tells it to grow. This means that the mazal imparts vitality to it through the sacred power given to it by Hashem to influence that which was placed under its charge.
