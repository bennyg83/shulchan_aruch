To wait, Ba'er Heitev — and it is from Magen Avraham in the name of Mateh Moshe, who wrote the passage Shema Yisrael; and it requires study, for in my humble opinion the intent is the verse Shema Yisrael; see siman 46 where Tur wrote R. Yehudah he-Hasid would say after a small Keriat Shema that preceded, therefore in Shemoneh Esreh, because he feared its time would pass; and it is close to say the mention in Magen Avraham in the name of R. Yehudah he-Hasid is nothing new — sometimes when he feared the congregation would delay much and the time for Keriat Shema would pass he would recite it before going to synagogue, and sometimes he would wait for the Keriat Shema that followed; therefore it would also appear his intent was the whole passage. See siman 46 what is written in the name of responsum Rashal siman 64 — and Mateh Moshe was Rashal’s student and most of his kabbalah from him — and Rashal there wrote he would say the whole passage; in any case it appears the matters are one style in order to fulfill the eighteen [blessings of] Keriat Shema in its time; but from the implication of their words that they wrote kabbalah from R. Yehudah he-Hasid, etc., from the implication of the words it is not from the side of law but from kabbalah for benefit or preservation; if so one could say this kabbalah came only on the first verse alone; and if it is kabbalah we accept it; and it appears accordingly one need only say the verse Shema Yisrael alone and need not say it in Shemoneh Esreh either; see Tur siman 46.
