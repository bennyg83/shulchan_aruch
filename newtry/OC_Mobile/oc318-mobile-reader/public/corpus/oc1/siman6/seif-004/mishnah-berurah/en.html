(13) Some have the custom, etc. Their intent is that each person should complete ninety responses of Amen each day. See Shaarei Teshuvah, who cites those who question this practice. Responsa Teshuvah MeAhavah, part 2, siman 212, concludes that according to our practice, where we do not intend to fulfill our obligations through the chazzan’s blessings, it is permitted according to all opinions. Peri Megadim writes that nowadays it is proper that only the chazzan recite them aloud. Therefore, each community should follow its custom, for every practice has authorities upon whom to rely.
(14) Who already fulfilled their obligation. Levush implies that one cannot discharge others in the morning blessings with fewer than ten present. See Peri Megadim, who inclines at the end of his discussion to say that this applies specifically to one who is proficient. One who is not proficient does not require ten even according to Levush.
