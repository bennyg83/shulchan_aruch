(יג) Some practice, etc. — their intent is that each completes up to ninety amens per day; see responsa; some question this; in Teshuvah Me'ahavah [vol. 2 siman 212] he raised that for us, who practice not to fulfill through the sheliach tzibbur’s blessing, according to all it is permitted to do so; in Peri Megadim he wrote that now it is fitting that only the sheliach tzibbur say aloud — see there; therefore each place should act according to its custom, for each has whom to rely on.

(יד) That they already fulfilled — in Levush it implies one cannot discharge Birkas HaShachar with fewer than ten; see Peri Megadim who supports at the end of his words to say specifically for an expert one does not discharge with fewer than ten, but for one who is not expert ten are not required — even for Levush.
