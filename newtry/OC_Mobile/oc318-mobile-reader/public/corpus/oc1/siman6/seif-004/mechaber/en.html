Some have the custom that after one person recites a morning blessing and they answer amen after him, one of those who answered amen repeats amen and blesses, and they answer amen after him — in this order they do all those who answered amen first; one should not protest against them nor say they already fulfilled [their obligation] with the amen they answered first, because the one blessing does not intend to exempt others; and even if the blesser intended to exempt others, they intend not to fulfill their obligation with his blessing.
