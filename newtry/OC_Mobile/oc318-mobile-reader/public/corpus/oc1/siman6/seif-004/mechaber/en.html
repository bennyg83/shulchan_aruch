Some have the custom that after one person recites a morning blessing and they answer <i>amen</i> after him, one of those who answered <i>amen</i> repeats <i>amen</i> and blesses, and they answer <i>amen</i> after him — in this order they do all those who answered <i>amen</i> first; one should not protest against them nor say they already fulfilled [their obligation] with the <i>amen</i> they answered first, because the one blessing does not intend to exempt others; and even if the blesser intended to exempt others, they intend not to fulfill their obligation with his blessing.
