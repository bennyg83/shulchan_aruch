1) [Seif 1] When one leaves the privy, he should recite the blessing Asher Yatzar, etc. This blessing contains forty-five words. Magen Avraham, seif katan 2, in the name of the kabbalistic writings. So too in Shaar HaKavanos, in the discourse on washing the hands, and in Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaBerachos, chapter 4; Matzas Shimurim, page 6; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 45; and Yafeh LaLev, volume 1, paragraph 13. Yafeh LaLev further writes there, in the name of Rabbi Nezirus Shimshon in his gloss on Magen Avraham, how he counted the words: "Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe, Who fashioned man with wisdom and created within him many openings and many cavities. It is revealed and known before Your Throne of Glory that if one of them were to become sealed or if one of them were to open"; although he writes there "if one of them were to open" before "if one of them were to become sealed," because he follows the reading of Rashi and those who agree with him, we follow the wording of Rambam, Abudraham, and Maran in Shulchan Aruch, and it should not be changed; see Yafeh LaLev there, paragraph 7; "it would be impossible to survive even for one hour. Blessed are You, Hashem, Who heals all flesh and acts wondrously." End quote. This is also the wording in Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilchos Tefillah, halachah 5, and in the siddur Chesed LaAlafim. As for what Machatzis HaShekel writes concerning Magen Avraham, Yafeh LaLev there has already rejected his words and concludes: "See also what Ashad HaNechalim, page 2b, writes on this matter; his words too can be answered, as discussed."
