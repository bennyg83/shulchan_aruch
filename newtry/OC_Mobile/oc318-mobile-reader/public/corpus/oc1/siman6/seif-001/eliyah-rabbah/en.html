[1] To survive even one hour, etc. Explanation: although one can survive a day or two, nevertheless at the time of formation it was impossible to survive even one hour. The Bach wrote that one must not deviate from what our ancestors practiced — each river according to its stream and custom. So too the Taz wrote that there are two textual versions in the berachos, and one must not erase any nusach found among these authorities. Similarly he wrote regarding the word choli: there are two versions — whether to say Rofeh choli kol basar or Rofeh kol basar; Heaven forbid to delete one and emend it like the other; rather each has authorities on whom to rely. The Rama wrote (OC 93) that they established "openings, openings" based on what I heard from physicians: every person, beast, and animal — his whole body and every limb is full of fine pores that cannot be seen; when he has pain and sweats, the pain exits through these pores; were it not so, healing would be impossible — thus far his words in brief.
