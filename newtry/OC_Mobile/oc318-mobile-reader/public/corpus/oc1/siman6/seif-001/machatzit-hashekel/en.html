Comment 1, "hollows," etc. The language of creation. These are the words of the Tur and Rabbeinu Yonah — that a cavity is only absence. The Taz challenged: if so, it should also have said nekuvim and not nekavim, for in a borehole too the language of creation does not apply. One can say somewhat that we bless on the ultimate purpose of the organs, which concerns relieving oneself. Now the mouth — the ultimate purpose of its creation for the matter of relieving oneself is taking food with the mouth; in this matter the opening is primary and the sides that were created are for the sake of the opening. If so, the language of creation applies to this, even though in the borehole itself creation does not apply; nevertheless creation applies to the sides, whose ultimate purpose is the mouth. If so, it is well to say creation regarding the mouth, for He created the sides so that through them there would be a mouth. Unlike the hollow organs — namely the stomach and intestines — for on the contrary, the ultimate purpose of creating the organs for relieving oneself is the walls, so that food not fall into the body cavity. If so, they were created for their own purpose and the cavity comes about incidentally; even though the cavity too must hold the food, nevertheless since the walls' purpose is for their own sake, one cannot say the walls were created only for the cavity's sake. On the cavity alone the language of creation does not apply, for it is absence.
