Seif katan 1: Hollows, etc. The expression "created" does not apply. These are the words of the Tur and Beit Yosef, for a hollow is merely an absence. The Taz asks that, if so, the text should likewise have said "perforated" rather than "openings," since for the same reason the language of creation does not apply to an opening either. One may answer somewhat that we recite the blessing concerning the purpose of the organs as they relate to relieving oneself. Regarding the mouth, the purpose relevant to this matter is taking food into it. In that respect, the opening is primary, while the walls that were created serve the opening. The language of creation can therefore be used, for although creation does not apply to the opening itself, it applies to the walls whose purpose is to form the mouth. It is thus appropriate to speak of creating the mouth, since He created the walls so that the mouth would thereby exist. This is unlike the internal cavities, namely the stomach and intestines. There, the principal purpose of the created organs in relation to bodily elimination is the walls, which prevent the food from falling into the body cavity. They were therefore created for their own function, while the hollow came about automatically. Although the hollow is also needed as a place where the food rests, the walls were created for their own purpose, and one cannot say that they were created only for the sake of the hollow. Creation does not apply to the hollow by itself, since it is merely an absence.
