Asher Yatzar. But not on netilas yadayim, for one need not wash — it suffices with cleaning of dirt and pebbles (Ra'avad). That we bless on morning netilas yadayim — the reason may be that we say in the Gemara (Chullin): a person washes his hands in the morning and they remain effective all day. See Ramban, responsa siman 153 and siman 154.
