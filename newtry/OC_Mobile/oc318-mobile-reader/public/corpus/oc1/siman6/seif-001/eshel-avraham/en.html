Asher Yatzar. But not the blessing over washing the hands, because washing is not required; cleaning the hands with earth or pebbles suffices. So writes Raavad. The reason we recite the blessing over washing the hands in the morning may be based on the Gemara in Chullin, which states that a person may wash his hands in the morning and stipulate that the washing remain effective for the entire day. See Responsa Ramban, simanim 153 and 154.
