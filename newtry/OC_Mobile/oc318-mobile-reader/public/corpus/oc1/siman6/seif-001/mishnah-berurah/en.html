(א) Chalulim (hollows) — meaning hollow organs; one should not say chalalim (corpses), for “corpse” is not applicable to creation.

(ב) “If one were sealed,” etc. — such is the Rambam’s reading; but our version, and Rashi, Rif, and Rosh, have “if one opens” [Gra].

(ג) “That is, among the openings,” etc. — because his reading was that one could not survive even one hour, and he had difficulty with this since a person closes his mouth for many hours; therefore he says “that is,” etc. But our custom is not to say “even one hour,” as the Rema wrote, and likewise it is proven in Agudah; so too Magen Avraham, and likewise implied in Biur HaGra; see Taz.

(ד) Ei efshar (it is impossible) — ei with a tsere under the alef, because the word derives from ein (there is not); so Ba'er Heitev and Peri Megadim; and in Shaarei Teshuvah he reconciles the reading with a hirik, that the intent is also like ein, as written ei kavod where Rashi explains the intent is like ein kavod.

(ה) Rofeh (choli) — Bach, Shelah, and Mateh Moshe wrote one should not say choli, only rofeh kol basar; and Taz wrote both readings are correct, Heaven forbid to delete one of them; see there.

(ו) Something spiritual — and it is written with this intent: the soul enjoys the spirituality of food and the body enjoys its physicality, and through this they are bound to each other through food [Magen Avraham].
