1) [Seif 1] When he leaves the privy he blesses Asher Yatzar, etc. This blessing contains forty-five words. Magen Avraham, comment 2, in the name of the ancient manuscripts; so too in Shaar HaKavvanos in the discourse on netilas yadayim, and in Sefer Pri Etz Chayyim, Shaar HaBerachos chapter 4. Mateh Moshe, page 6. So too Shaarei Teshuvah, page 45; so too Yafeh Lelev, volume 1, letter 13. He wrote in Or Sameach in the name of Rav Shimshon the Nazir in a gloss on the Magen Avraham — those who would count the words — and his wording: "Blessed are You… Who formed man with wisdom and created in him openings, openings, hollows, hollows, revealed and known before the throne of Your glory that if one of them were sealed or if one of them were opened" (although there he wrote that if "opened" precedes "sealed," it is because his text reads like Rashi and his colleagues; but we read per the text of the Rambam, the Rada, and Maran in the Shulchan Aruch, and one must not change — see Yafeh Lelev there, letter 7) "he could not survive even one hour," Blessed are You… "Healer of all flesh and Who acts wondrously," thus far. So too is the nusach in the Rambam, chapter 7 of the Laws of Prayer, halachah 5, and siman 362. What Machatzit HaShekel wrote on the Magen Avraham — Yafeh Lelev there already rejected his words and concluded; see also in Sefer Eshed HaNechalim, page 2b, what he wrote on this, and one must respond to him as discussed — see there.
