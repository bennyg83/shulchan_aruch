93) People behind the kohanim. This includes those not directly behind them but drawn to the sides; as long as they remain behind the kohanim, they are not included in the blessing, even if nothing separates them from the kohanim.
94) Are not included in the blessing. The blessing specifically requires face-to-face positioning, as stated above in se'if katan 37.
95) But those in front of and beside them. It is obvious that people in front of the kohanim but drawn to the sides are included. Even those directly aligned with the kohanim's sides are included. As explained, those diagonally behind them are not. Accordingly, people standing at the eastern wall behind an ark that projects somewhat into the synagogue, with the kohanim standing before it, are not included. Bach defends them: Nowadays each person purchases a fixed synagogue place, so they are considered unable to move because one cannot displace another person from his place. This is insufficient, however, because one can easily find an open position beside the kohanim, on the bimah in the synagogue's center, or in its western section. So write the later authorities.
96) If circumstances prevent them, etc. Without such circumstances, even people inside the synagogue who stand behind the kohanim are not included. By failing to move and receive the blessing face to face, they show that the blessing is not precious to them.
