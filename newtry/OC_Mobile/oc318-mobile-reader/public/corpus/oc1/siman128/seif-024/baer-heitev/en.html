That is, the sides in front of them; but the sides behind them, such as those standing at the eastern wall when the Ark protrudes somewhat into the synagogue and the priests stand before it — they are not within the blessing. And Bach wrote a leniency in this: now, since everyone acquires a place in the synagogue, they are considered under duress — for one is not permitted to leave his place and displace his fellow from his place, see there. And this is not sufficient — for they can go to the priests' place that is vacant, or move slightly from the eastern wall to be at the priests' sides, or stand in the middle of the synagogue — Magen Avraham. And Taz wrote a leniency here: since the Ark is attached to the wall, it is as if the priests were standing right next to the wall, since they stand next to the Ark; and if so, everything is to the sides of the priests, see there.
