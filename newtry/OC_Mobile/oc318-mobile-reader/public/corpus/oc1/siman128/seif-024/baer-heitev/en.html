And at their sides. This means positions to the kohanim's forward sides. Positions to their rear sides are not included; for example, people at the eastern wall when the Ark protrudes into the synagogue and the kohanim stand before it. Bach permits this today because each person purchases a fixed place and is considered unable to move, since he may not leave his place and displace another. This is insufficient, for he can go to the kohanim's vacant place, move slightly away from the eastern wall so he is at their side, or stand in the middle of the synagogue. Magen Avraham. Taz permits it because the Ark is attached to the wall: The kohanim standing beside the Ark are considered to stand beside the wall itself, making everyone at their sides. Those standing at the southern or northern wall should not face the wall, but should face the kohanim, for the blessing requires face-to-face orientation. Taz.
