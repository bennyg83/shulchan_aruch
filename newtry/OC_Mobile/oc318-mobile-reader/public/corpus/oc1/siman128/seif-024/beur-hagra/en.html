(1) Seif 124: "And at their sides." — see there; Tosafot 39a s.v. "and this one sprinkles," etc. — meaning at their sides literally — the sides behind him are surely like behind him; and likewise before him is like before him; and it is not required except at the sides literally; and it is simple: since it does not say "the sides behind him" like "behind him," we learn that it says sides literally, which are before him; Beit Yosef — who did not distinguish in Yerushalmi; and so Rambam and Tur and Shulchan Aruch.

(2) And behind them too, etc. — Tosafot 38b s.v. "partition," etc.
