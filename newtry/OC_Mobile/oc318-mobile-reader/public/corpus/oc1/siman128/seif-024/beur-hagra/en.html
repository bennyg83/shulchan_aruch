Seif 24: And at their sides. See Tosafos 39a, s.v. "and sprinkle," etc. This means literally at their sides, for the sides behind them are certainly like being behind them, and likewise the area before them is like being before them. Their question concerned only the areas directly at their sides. They resolved it from the fact that the Gemara did not say, "the sides behind them are like being behind them." It follows that it refers to the areas directly at their sides, which are like the area before them. Beit Yosef did not make the distinction found in the Yerushalmi, and neither did the Rambam, Tur, or Shulchan Aruch.
And even behind them, etc. Tosafos 38b, s.v. "a partition," etc.
