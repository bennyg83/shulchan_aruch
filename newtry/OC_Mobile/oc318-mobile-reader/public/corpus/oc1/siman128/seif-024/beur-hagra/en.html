Se'if 24: And at their sides. See Tosafos, Sotah 39a, s.v. Vehizah. This means directly at their sides. The rearward sides are plainly like behind and forward sides like in front; only direct sides were questioned. Since the Gemara did not say 'rearward sides are like behind,' it means direct sides are like in front. Beis Yosef says Yerushalmi, Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch make no distinction.
And even behind them, etc. Tosafos, Sotah 38b, s.v. Mechitzah.
