Seif katan 28 — to settle Beit Yosef's difficulty. For Beit Yosef explained thus it deals with blessing Ribon ha-olamim because he questioned if it deals with Birkat Kohanim behold anyway must wait for the reader — see Taz seif katan 13 and Bach contradicted Beit Yosef's explanation; therefore Magen Avraham wrote must settle Beit Yosef's difficulty and always deals with Birkat Kohanim and answer is he disagrees with Taz seif katan 12 and in Tosafot Taz brought two views; what he concluded and as above is in seif katan 37 — for word yevarechecha all hold must wait on all for all need hear Birkat Kohanim from kohen's mouth:
