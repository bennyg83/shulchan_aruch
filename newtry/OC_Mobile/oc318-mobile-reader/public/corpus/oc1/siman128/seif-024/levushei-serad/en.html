Seif katan 28. To answer the difficulty of Beis Yosef. Beis Yosef explained that it refers to the blessing ‘Ribono shel olam,’ because he asked that if it refers to Birkas Kohanim, they must in any case wait for the prompter. See Taz, seif katan 13. The Bach rejected Beis Yosef’s explanation, and therefore Magen Avraham wrote that one may answer Beis Yosef’s difficulty, and really it refers to Birkas Kohanim. His answer is that he disagrees with Taz, seif katan 12. In the Tosafot cited by Taz there are two views in this. What he concluded, ‘as written above,’ means in seif katan 27: at the word ‘Yevarechecha,’ according to all opinions one must wait for everyone, since all must hear Birkas Kohanim from the kohen.
