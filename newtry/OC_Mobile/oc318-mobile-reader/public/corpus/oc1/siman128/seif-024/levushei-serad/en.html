Se'if kaf kaf chet — to answer the Beit Yosef's difficulty. For the Beit Yosef explained thus — that it refers to the berachah "Ribon ha'olamim," because he challenged: if it refers to Birkat Kohanim, they would in any case need to wait for the reading — see Taz se'if kaf yud gimmel and the Bach contradicted the Beit Yosef's explanation; therefore the Magen Avraham wrote that one should answer the Beit Yosef's difficulty — and in any case it refers to Birkat Kohanim; and his answer is that he disagrees with the Taz se'if kaf bet; and in the Tosafot that the Taz brought there are two opinions on this; and what he concluded "and as stated above" means in se'if kaf kaf zayin — that in the word "yevarechecha" everyone must wait for all, for all need to hear Birkat Kohanim from the kohen's mouth.
