An apostate. Even if he did not worship idolatry. And if he worshipped idolatry, even if under duress, he does not lift his hands — even if he repented — as implied from the Rambam's wording; see in Beit Yosef and Eliyahu Rabbah and Peri Chadash. And Peri Chadash and Bach raised for the halacha that even an idolater, since he repented, lifts his hands, see there; however, if he vowed to apostatize and returned, he is not disqualified — Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Radbaz, see there. Bach wrote: an uncircumcised one whose brothers died from circumcision — it requires investigation whether he is fit for Birkat Kohanim, see there; and in Knesset HaGedolah he wrote: to me the matter is simple that he does not lift his hands; and so Peri Chadash agreed with him, not for his reason, see there.
