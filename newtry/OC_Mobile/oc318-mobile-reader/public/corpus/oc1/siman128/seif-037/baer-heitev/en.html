An apostate. Even if he did not worship idolatry. If he did worship idolatry, even under coercion, he does not perform Nesias Kapayim, even if he repented; so it appears from the precise wording of the Rambam. See Dagul Merevavah, Bach, Olas Tamid, and Peri Chadash. Peri Chadash and Bach conclude as practical halacha that even one who worshipped idolatry, once he repented, performs Nesias Kapayim; see there. However, if he promised to apostatize and then retracted, he is not disqualified; Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Radbaz, see there. The Bach wrote that an uncircumcised man whose brothers died because of circumcision requires study whether he is fit for Nesias Kapayim; see there. In Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah he wrote: to me the matter is simple, that he should not perform Nesias Kapayim; Peri Chadash ruled like him, though not for his reason; see there. Magen Avraham and Olas Tamid disagree with the Bach and rule that even if he deliberately did not circumcise himself, he is fit; see there. Yad Aharon wrote against the Magen Avraham that his words are astonishing; see there.
