Se'if 37: An apostate, etc. Even inadvertently and even after repentance, because Birkas Kohanim is compared to Temple service, as above in se'if 6. Menachos 109a says kohanim who served idolatry, etc.; the law follows Rabbi Shimon even when the sprinkling was inadvertent. Sefer HaZahir agrees.
Some say, etc. See Tosafos, Menachos 109a, s.v. Lo; Taanis 26a; Tosafos, Sotah 39a, s.v. Ki, citing She'iltos, Korach; and Rosh, Megillah.
If he was compelled, etc. Beis Yosef says such a person is not called an apostate. Rambam's statement 'even under compulsion' concerns serving idolatry, not forced conversion. See Bedek HaBayis.
