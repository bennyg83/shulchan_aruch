(1) Seif 137: "An apostate," etc. — even unwittingly, even if he repented — for it is compared to idolatry, as above seif 106; and we say in Menachot 109a — the priests who served, etc.; and we rule like R' Shimon there, that even unwitting sprinkling; and so in Sefer HaZahir.

(2) And some say, etc. — see Tosafot there in Menachot 109a s.v. "no," etc.; and in Taanit 26a; and see Tosafot in Sotah 39a s.v. "for in She'iltot, chapter Korach," etc.; and in Rosh on Megillah.

(3) And if he was coerced, etc. — Beit Yosef: in such a case he is not called an apostate; and what Rambam wrote "even under ones" means regarding a non-Jewish slave, not a convert. See there.
