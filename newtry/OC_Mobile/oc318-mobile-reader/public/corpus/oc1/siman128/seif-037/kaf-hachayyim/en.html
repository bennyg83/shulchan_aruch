37) Orchos Chaim writes in Baal HaTamid's name: We have found no author who requires washing the feet, but this was the custom in those lands, and Jewish custom has the force of Torah. Beis Yosef cites him. Peri Chadash says the custom may have been modeled on the priestly sanctification in the Temple, of which the verse says, 'They shall wash their hands and feet.' It is no longer practiced, correctly so: The present verse says 'your hands,' not 'your feet,' and the Zohar likewise says that he must sanctify his hands. Beis Yosef says we have never seen anyone practice it, and Rama in Darkei Moshe, os 6, says it is not the custom in his lands either. See our words above, siman 4, os 5.
