That there is no nesiat kapayim except on Yom Tov — Beit Yosef in the name of the Agur wrote the reason why there is no nesiat kapayim every day: because it is customary to immerse, and every day it is difficult to immerse; and part of Beit Yosef on this — that immersion for nesiat kapayim was not mentioned in the Gemara. And if they practiced to be stringent and immerse — why should they cancel for this three positive commandments every day, etc.? And many kohanim separate from their wives on Yom Tov because of the duchan — and disruption it is in my eyes: if they are concerned for impurity from keri and to be stringent on themselves, they should immerse beforehand, for behold he already has impurity from keri. And furthermore — in Eretz Yisrael and in all the kingdom of Egypt they practice nesiat kapayim all days of the year; will they separate forever from their wives? Therefore it appears they have no reason; and all the more so if a mitzvah immersion occurs — one should not cancel a mitzvah for this, which has no foundation or principle:
