That there is Nesias Kapayim only on Yom Tov. Beis Yosef in the name of Agur wrote the reason why there is no Nesias Kapayim every day: because the custom is to immerse, and every day it is difficult to immerse. Beis Yosef disputed this, for immersion for Nesias Kapayim is not mentioned in the Gemara, and if they practiced stringently to immerse, why should they nullify three positive commandments every day because of this? Many kohanim separate from their wives on Yom Tov because of the duchan, and this is a mistake in my eyes. If they are concerned for tumas keri and wish to be stringent, they should immerse beforehand, for tumas keri already rests upon them. Furthermore, in Eretz Yisrael and throughout the kingdom of Egypt they perform Nesias Kapayim all year; would they separate from their wives forever? Therefore it appears they have no reason. Certainly if the immersion is a mitzvah, one should not nullify the mitzvah because of this, which has no foundation or basis.
