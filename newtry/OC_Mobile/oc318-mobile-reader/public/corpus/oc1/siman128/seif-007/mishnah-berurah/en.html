24) He should not recite the blessing again, etc. Regarding the blessing, we rely on Rambam, who holds that the morning washing sufficed. This applies only if the kohen neither touched a soiled area nor diverted attention in the interim. If he did divert attention, and certainly if he touched a soiled area, Magen Avraham says all views require him now to recite the hand-washing blessing. Eliyah Rabbah holds that in every case he need not bless, because perhaps no blessing was instituted for this washing at all. The custom is never to bless. Every God-fearing kohen should therefore guard his hands from the morning washing onward, avoiding contact with soiled areas so that he does not enter a doubtful obligation to bless.
