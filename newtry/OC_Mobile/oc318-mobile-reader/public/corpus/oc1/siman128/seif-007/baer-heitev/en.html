To bless. Every kohen who is reverent and trembling shall guard his hands from the time of his morning netilah not to touch an unclean place; if he touched, there is doubt whether he must go back and bless when he washes his hands for nesi'at kapayim — see Magen Avraham. And forbidden to delay the walking of 22 amos between netilah and Birkat Kohanim; therefore he must wash close to Retzeh, and the chazzan should not prolong Retzeh — Magen Avraham.
