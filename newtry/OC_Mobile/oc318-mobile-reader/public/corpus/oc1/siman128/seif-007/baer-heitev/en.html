To bless. Every reverent kohen should guard his hands from the morning washing onward and not touch a filthy place, for if he does there is a doubt whether he must recite another blessing when washing for duchaning. See Magen Avraham. One may not delay between washing and Birchas Kohanim for the time needed to walk twenty-two amos. He must therefore wash close to Retzei, and the chazzan should not prolong Retzei. Magen Avraham.
