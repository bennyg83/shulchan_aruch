45) [Se'if 7] If the kohen washed his hands, etc., he should not recite another blessing, etc. Maran rules this in Bedek HaBayis because doubtful blessings are omitted, rejecting Tur's view that one recites the hand-washing blessing for the Priestly Blessing. Rama likewise writes in Darkei Moshe, os 4, that it is best not to bless, since Rambam holds no washing is needed at all after morning washing; he should therefore wash without a blessing. So rule Bach; Knesses HaGedolah at the beginning of Hagahos HaTur and in Hagahos Beis Yosef; and Eliyah Rabbah, os 13. Eliyah Rabbah adds that perhaps the hand-washing blessing was instituted only for eating or morning washing, as Responsa Rashba, simanim 190-191, implies. The later authorities follow Maran; independently, the settled rule is that a doubtful blessing is omitted.
