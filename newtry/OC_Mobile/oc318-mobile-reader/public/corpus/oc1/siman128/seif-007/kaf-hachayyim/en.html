7) There: A non-kohen may not recite the Priestly Blessing, etc. A child was taken captive among non-Jews and redeemed, and his parentage is unknown. One witness says with certainty that he is a kohen, while another says with certainty that he is an ordinary Israelite. Pesach HaDevir, os 4, rules that he may not recite the Priestly Blessing when he matures; see his full discussion. See also there where he rejects Responsa Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 93, which appears to permit a doubtful kohen to recite it. Mishbetzos Zahav, os 27, likewise rules that one whose paternity is uncertain; whether he is the nine-month child of the first husband or the seven-month child of the latter; may not recite the Priestly Blessing. Pesach HaDevir cites and agrees with him.
