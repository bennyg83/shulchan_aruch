Seif katan 7, "the Levi," etc. We have not found that a firstborn has sanctity, although one who opens the womb requires redemption. Ri writes in the name of the Zohar that the reason a Levi pours is that a kohen is sanctified and the Levi is likewise sanctified, so it is fitting for one who is sanctified to wash another who is sanctified. An Israelite firstborn who opened the womb likewise has an aspect of sanctity. "And if the Levi," etc., "he need not pour," etc. If some of the kohanim are Torah scholars, the question arises whether he must pour for them. Pri Chadash disputes both points and holds that even if some of the kohanim are Torah scholars, not only is a learned Levi not obligated to pour for an am ha'aretz kohen, but he even acts improperly if he pours for an am ha'aretz, and this falls under "all who hate Me love death"; see there.
