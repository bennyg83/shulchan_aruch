A Levi pours water over their hands. Does a firstborn possess sanctity? He must, after all, be redeemed. Ri writes in the name of the Zohar that a Levi pours because both the kohen and Levi are holy, and one holy person washes another. An Israelite firstborn likewise has an aspect of sanctity.
If the Levi is a Torah scholar, he need not pour. Does the presence of some learned kohanim alter the rule? Pri Chadash disputes both points: Even when some kohanim are scholars, a learned Levi should not pour for an ignorant kohen; indeed, doing so is forbidden, for 'all who hate Me love death.'
