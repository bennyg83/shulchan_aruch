Leather. See Baer Heitev. See Panim Meiros, volume 2, siman 28, which states that shoes made of hard felt cloth are not considered barefoot, since they are fashioned like our shoes and one does not feel the hardness of the ground through them. See there regarding one who walked barefoot. Machazik Berachah writes that Responsa Zera Emes rules likewise. See there.
