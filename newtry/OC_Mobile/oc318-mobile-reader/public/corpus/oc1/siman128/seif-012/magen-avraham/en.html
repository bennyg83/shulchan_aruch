They raise. It is written in Maharil that although he raises the right hand somewhat, nevertheless he must be careful to place the finger of the right hand upon the left so that they not separate, in order that the "window" not be broken, for we require five openings. In Hagahos Maimoniyos it is written that between one word and another he may bring the thumbs close to one another. In the Zohar it is stated that no finger should touch another, but this is not the custom. In Bachya it is written on "and his hands were faith" that the practice of the kohanim is to mark the Name Yud-Hei-Vav-Hei with their fingers before they raise their hands.
