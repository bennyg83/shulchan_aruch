[12] And he blessed al netilas yadayim, etc. Magen Avraham asked: He should have said briefly that one does not bless on this washing, for do we not know that if he did not bless in the morning he blesses on his washing even if he does not wish to perform Nesias Kapayim? End quote. In my humble opinion, he comes to exclude a case where he had no water and cleaned with anything that cleanses, to recite the blessing “al nekiyus yadayim,” as above siman 4, seif 22. In such a case it appears that if he afterward washed with water, he need not bless al netilas yadayim unless he performs Nesias Kapayim; examine carefully.
