[27] A little higher, etc. It implies as written in Maharil, that he should be careful not to raise them so much that they separate completely from the left hand, so that the window not be broken, but should only place the fingers of the right hand upon the fingers of the left; end quote. So too wrote Magen Avraham and the later authorities. But in Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah he wrote on this: I saw the pious rabbi, our teacher Rabbi David HaKohen, that he would not join the hands, but only this one and that one separately, and it appears that he held that the air between the two hands is one of the openings, and such is the custom; end quote. Hagahos Minhagim wrote that between one word and the next, or when the congregation says Amen, there is no concern if the thumb touches the finger because of fatigue.
[28] Five spaces, etc. For it is written, “peering through the lattices,” meaning windows. Furthermore, the word “emor” is written with a full vav. Take that vav and place it inside kaf-hei, and it becomes kaf-vav-hei, an opening.
