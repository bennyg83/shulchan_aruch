[12] And he blessed on washing the hands, etc. Magen Avraham questioned: it should have said briefly that we do not bless on this washing, for is it not obvious that if he did not bless in the morning he blesses on his washing afterward even if he does not wish to raise his hands — up to here. In my humble opinion this comes to exclude if he had no water and cleaned with something else — he should bless on clean hands as above siman 4 seif 22; then it appears that if afterward he washed with water he need not bless on washing the hands unless when raising his hands — examine.
