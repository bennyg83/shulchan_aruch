Se'if 12: They raise, etc. The Mishnah there and Tamid's final chapter. 'They raise' follows the scriptural singular 'his hand.' See Zohar 146a.
And they spread their hands. Sotah 39b; see Rashi, s.v. Likhof.
And they separate, etc. Tanchuma, Naso, interprets 'He looks through the windows' as between the kohanim's fingers and 'peers through the lattices' when they spread their hands.
And they direct, etc. Rosh there; meaning five openings.
And they spread, etc. Orchos Chaim.
