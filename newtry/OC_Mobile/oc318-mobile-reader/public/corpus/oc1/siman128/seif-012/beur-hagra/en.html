Siman 12, "they raise," etc. Mishnah there and in the last chapter of Tamid, "and they raise," etc.; Hagahot Maimoniot in his [commentary] wrote, and in the Zohar 146a.

Siman 12, "and they stretch out their hands." 39b, and in Rashi, s.v. "to bend," etc.

Siman 12, "and they separate," etc. In Midrash Tanchuma, parashat Naso: He watches from the windows between the fingers of the Kohanim, peering through the slits at the time they spread their palms.

Siman 12, "and they aim," etc. The Rosh there, and R' Levi [said] five slits.

Siman 12, "and they spread," etc. Avodat HaKodesh.
