And they raise their right hands, etc., as it is written "And Aaron lifted up his hands and blessed them" — "his hand" is written; and so too is proper according to Kabbalah (Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 4). And they spread their hands and separate their fingers, etc.; and it is forbidden to point the fingers upward in vain, for then ten degrees of judgment of the "other side" are aroused against him and he causes a curse upon himself (Zohar, Parashat Nasso).
