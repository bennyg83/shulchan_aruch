They raise the right hand, etc., as it is written, 'Aharon raised his hands toward the people and blessed them.' The word is written in the singular, 'his hand,' and this is also proper according to Kabbalah, as stated in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 4. They spread their hands and separate their fingers, etc. It is forbidden to raise one's fingers unnecessarily, for this arouses against him ten levels of judgment from the Other Side and causes a curse upon himself, as stated in the Zohar, Parashas Naso.
