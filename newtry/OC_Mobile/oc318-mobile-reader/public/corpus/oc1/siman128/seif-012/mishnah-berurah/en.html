42) They raise their hands, etc. We derive the requirement from the verse, 'Aharon raised his hands toward the people and blessed them.' The word for 'his hand' in that verse is written in the singular, hinting that one hand should be raised higher; presumably this is the right hand.
43) The right hand slightly. Nevertheless, he must ensure that the right thumb rests against the left thumb so they do not separate, thereby breaking the 'window,' because five spaces are required. If the thumbs separate, the gap between the right and left hands does not count as one of the spaces. Some write that between words, and certainly between the three blessings, he may bring the thumbs and fingers together because of fatigue.
44) They separate their fingers. Tanchuma, parashas Naso, explains: 'Watching through the windows' means between the kohanim's fingers, and 'peering through the lattices' refers to the time when they spread their hands.
45) They intend to form five, etc. The allusion is that the word hacharakim, 'the lattices,' is written with the letter hei, whose numerical value is five: five lattices.
(46) And they spread their hands properly etc. — meaning not like one performs Nesias Kapayim in prayer toward heaven.
