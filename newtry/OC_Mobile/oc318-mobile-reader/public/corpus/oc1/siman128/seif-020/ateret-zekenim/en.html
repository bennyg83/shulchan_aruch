And if the reader had intent for the prayer leader's prayer from beginning to end — nevertheless for reading alone it is good that he intend his heart for the eighteen blessings from beginning to end; and it is simple that one who chatted while the prayer leader was praying cannot read (Mordechai); and even if the prayer leader returns afterward to the prayer and says Sim Shalom it is good to listen to the entire prayer (Beit Yosef and Mahari Mintz); but in Hagahot Maimoniyot it implies if the prayer leader says Sim Shalom we have no concern except only if the prayer leader does not say Sim Shalom — then one must beware that another not stand in his place to read and say Sim Shalom, rather one who intends for the entire prayer with the prayer leader; and it appears to me it deals with when the prayer leader is not assured to return to his prayer — for if so the prayer leader himself should return and finish the prayer; and we must say that which we say if he is not assured to return to his prayer he does not raise his hands — that is specifically when he himself finishes the prayer; but if there is another who finishes the prayer he may raise his hands (Beit Yosef); and this does not appear so from the plain sense of our Mishna — rather whoever the prayer leader is not assured to return to his prayer is not permitted to ascend the duchan and there is no remedy that the reader intend from beginning to end and the reader finish Sim Shalom since he is not assured — and so my teacher in Bach; and there are those who say now that they pray from the siddur and there is no concern for distraction of the one raising his hands even if he names other kohanim (Levush). Two kohanim who hate each other — even so they are obligated to ascend the duchan together even if there are no kohanim but those two; and even if they vowed benefit from each other they are permitted to perform a mitzvah together for mitzvot were not given for benefit; and one should not say for three who vowed benefit from each other that they do not combine for zimmun — see Yoreh De'ah siman 217 seif 27 and siman 221 seif 13 and Orach Chayyim siman 485 seif 5 and siman 199 seif 11; and one who does not wish to ascend with his fellow cannot compel his fellow to split nesiut kafim with him by saying you ascend Shacharit and I Musaf — for his fellow can say I do not wish to nullify my mitzvah and I ascend the duchan every time, and you are not so important as to nullify my mitzvah because of your foolishness (responsa Maharam Mintz siman 12 din 4); but a kohen who hates the congregation or the congregation hates him shall not ascend the duchan and this kohen has great danger when he ascends the duchan (Zohar Parashat Naso 145b).
