Nachalas Tzvi challenged the Levush, for Beis Yosef cited Agur that Rashi and Maharam do not dispute: according to Rashi the shaliach tzibbur completes Sim Shalom, meaning where the prompter did not have intent, and Maharam speaks where the prompter had intent.
