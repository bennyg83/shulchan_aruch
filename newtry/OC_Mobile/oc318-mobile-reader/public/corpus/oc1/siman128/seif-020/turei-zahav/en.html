And at their sides, etc. Beis Yosef explains that all who are before the kohanim, even if not directly opposite their faces but drawn to the sides, are certainly included among the people before the kohanim. Those who are behind the kohanim, even if not directly behind them but drawn to the sides, are certainly included as behind them. The Gemara’s question was only about those actually at the sides of the kohanim, and it was resolved that they are like before them. One must explain our practice, where the kohanim stand before the ark, with a large space between them and the eastern wall in most synagogues. If so, those standing by the eastern wall are behind the kohanim and are not included in the blessing, Heaven forbid. Indeed some men of deed leave their places at that time for this reason, but Heaven forbid to find fault with those who remain in their places. My teacher and father-in-law wrote that since they buy their seats, they are considered forced regarding changing their place, but this is insufficient, for surely in the times of the Sages they also fixed places for prayer. It seems to me that since the holy ark is attached to the wall, it is as if the kohanim stand right next to the wall, since they stand next to the ark, and therefore all are at the sides of the kohanim. In the times of the Sages, however, the bimah was not right next to the wall, as it says that the bimah does not interrupt, implying that some stood behind the bimah. Therefore ‘behind the kohanim’ applied there. But for us this does not apply, for no one stands behind the holy ark. It appears that those standing by the wall should not turn their faces to the wall, but toward the kohanim, for we require face to face.
