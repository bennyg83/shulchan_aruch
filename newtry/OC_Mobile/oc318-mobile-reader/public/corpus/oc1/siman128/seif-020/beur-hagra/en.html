Se'if 20: If, etc. The Gemara's 'if he is confident,' etc., refers to the case 'if no one else is present.' Therefore it says 'if,' not 'even,' etc.
For, etc. As stated in se'if 1.
And how, etc. Sotah 38b.
And he concludes, etc. Rashi there and Berachos there, as it says 'and there, if,' etc., and he returns, etc.
If the prompter, etc. Maharam says the prompter concludes Sim Shalom. Agur explains the present case this way and says the Mishnah does not address it. See Magen Avraham. All this is strained; Rashi's interpretation is primary.
