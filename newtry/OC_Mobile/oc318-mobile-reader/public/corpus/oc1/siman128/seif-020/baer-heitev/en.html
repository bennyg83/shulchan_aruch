To ascend. That is, before Retzeh. But if he said after Retzeh, he is not permitted to ascend, since he did not uproot at Retzeh, as written in seif 8; and Mahari Weil does not violate when they call "Kohanim," as written in seif 22 — because the calling is after Retzeh. And see Eliyahu Rabbah, who challenged: one always finds that the prayer leader violates a positive commandment, since after one calls the priests, every priest who does not ascend violates a positive commandment, see there — and this is not precise: since the prayer leader did not uproot at Avodah, he does not violate in calling "Kohanim" — see above, se'if katan 11, from what is written.

To his prayer. And according to us, who pray from the siddur, he is assured that he will return to his prayer; and nevertheless, when there are other priests, he should not uproot his feet — Levush, Bach, and Magen Avraham; and most codifiers — see Magen Avraham. And Peri Chadash wrote: if he is assured that he will return to his prayer, he lifts his hands even where there are other priests, see there.

At Avodah. It should read: a little at Avodah — thus in the Tur — Magen Avraham.
