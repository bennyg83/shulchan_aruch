To ascend. This means before Retzei. If he was told after Retzei, he may not ascend, since he did not uproot his feet during Retzei, as stated in se'if 8. For this reason he does not violate the command when Kohanim is called, as stated in se'if 22, because the call comes after Retzei. Olas Tamid asks that the chazzan can always violate the positive commandment, since another person calls Kohanim and any kohen who does not ascend violates it. This is imprecise: Since the chazzan did not uproot his feet during Avodah, the call does not cause him to violate the command. See se'if katan 11.
To his prayer. Today, when we pray from a siddur, the chazzan is assured that he can return to his prayer. Nevertheless, when other kohanim are present he should not uproot his feet. Lechem Chamudos, Bach, Magen Avraham, and most poskim; see Magen Avraham. Peri Chadash writes that if he is assured that he can return to his prayer, he may raise his hands even when other kohanim are present.
During Avodah. The text should read, 'a little during Avodah.' This is the wording in Tur. Magen Avraham.
