And the kohanim. See Taz and Beis Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach. Bach and Peri Chadash write that this is proved by Sotah 38b. Shulchan Aruch cites in se'if 25 the case of ten kohanim who all ascend, proving that those who bless count toward the quorum, for duchaning requires ten. I nevertheless find this difficult. Granted, the kohanim count after the fact, but perhaps initially ten recipients are required, analogous to se'if 25, where one ascends and ten listen. What source distinguishes whether the listeners are Israelites or kohanim? Rambam, Hilchos Tefillah 15:9, and Tur may likewise mean only that after the fact the quorum suffices, while initially ten listeners are required. See Beis Yosef below, Taz, and Magen Avraham, letters 23 and 38; with Hashem's help this will be explained there. The ten must be adult (thirteen years old with two pubic hairs; see Magen Avraham, letter 48) free males. This is clear whether the requirement derives from a sacred matter or from a gezeirah shavah involving 'the Children of Israel,' as Ran writes in Megillah. Nevertheless, it is only a Scriptural support and rabbinic: If some leave after they began with ten, they complete it. Yerushalmi; see Orach Chaim, letter 1.
