And in my humble opinion: even if he washed in the morning and clarified with intention — nevertheless, if he did not wash in the morning except up to the joining of the fingers to the palm of the hand, see below, end of siman 161 — he must bless again on washing for the duchan, for he must wash up to the joint of the forearm; and therefore every Kohen should be careful to wash in the morning up to the joint of the forearm.
