1) [Se'if 1] The Priestly Blessing is not recited with fewer than ten, etc. If they began with ten and some left, they complete it, as stated above in Shulchan Aruch, siman 55, se'if 2. This comes from the Yerushalmi, Megillah, chapter HaKorei, and is cited by Rif and Rosh there and by Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilchos Tefillah, as Beis Yosef writes above in siman 55. Knesses HaGedolah, in this siman, os 3, cited by Olas Tamid, os 1, wonders why the poskim did not explain this. That objection is surprising, for Rif, Rosh, Rambam, and Beis Yosef wrote and explained it above in siman 55. If his objection is that Shulchan Aruch does not state it explicitly, it follows automatically and is already explained in Beis Yosef; why must Shulchan Aruch spell out an obvious consequence? I later saw that Eliyah Rabbah, os 1, also objects to Knesses HaGedolah; see there. His words may nevertheless be reconciled by explaining that he refers to the later authorities after Maran, who did not explain the rule in the laws of the Priestly Blessing. When we say that if some left they complete it, this applies only if a majority remains, as Shulchan Aruch states there. So write Peri Chadash here, os 2, and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 1. See our words there, os 35.
