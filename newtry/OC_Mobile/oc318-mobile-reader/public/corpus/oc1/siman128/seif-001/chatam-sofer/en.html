In Magen Avraham, end of seif kaf-alef. NB: his words are uncertain, and Sha'ar HaMelekh already addressed them, chapter 19 of Hilchot Shabbat; and in a responsum I expanded on this. And the main point, it seems to me, is that one who examines Darkei Moshe will understand the matter securely, without any forced interpretation or doubt; and he holds that if there are Kohanim ascending to the duchan without this, the Israelite ascending with them has hiddur mitzvah — "in the multitude of people is the king's glory" — and wherever there is hiddur mitzvah, there is no bal tosif, as Rambam wrote, chapter 6 of Hilchot Sukkah and Lulav, halachah 7; and see there in Kesef Mishneh. The same applies here: there is no concern because of the positive commandment "so shall you bless"; and this seems to me to be true.
