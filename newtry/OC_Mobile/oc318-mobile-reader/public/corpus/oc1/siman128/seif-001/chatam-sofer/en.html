In Magen Avraham at the end of seif katan 1. Note: His words require study. Shaar HaMelech already addressed them in chapter 19 of the Laws of Shabbos, and in a responsum I discussed this at length. The primary explanation, in my view, is that one who examines Darchei Moshe will understand the matter correctly without any strain or difficulty. He holds that if there are already kohanim ascending to the duchan, then an Israelite who ascends together with them constitutes hiddur mitzvah through 'in a multitude of people is the King's glory.' Wherever there is hiddur mitzvah, there is no prohibition of adding to a mitzvah, as Rambam writes in chapter 6 of the Laws of Sukkah and Lulav, law 7; see Kesef Mishneh there. The same applies here, and there is no violation of the positive commandment 'Thus shall you bless.' This appears to me to be correct.
