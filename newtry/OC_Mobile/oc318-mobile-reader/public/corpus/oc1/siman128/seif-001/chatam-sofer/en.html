In Magen Avraham end of seif katan 1 — note his words require study; and already Sha'ar HaMelech chapter 19 of Shabbat laws stood on them, and in responsa I expanded on this. Main point it appears to me one who examines in depth will understand the matter without strain or doubt: he holds that if there are kohanim ascending the duchan, the Israelite ascending with them has hiddur mitzvah in multitude — glory of the King; and wherever there is hiddur mitzvah there is no "do not add" as Rambam wrote chapter 6 of Sukkah and Lulav law 7 — see Kesef Mishneh; and so here there is no concern of positive commandment "so shall you bless" — this appears to me true:
