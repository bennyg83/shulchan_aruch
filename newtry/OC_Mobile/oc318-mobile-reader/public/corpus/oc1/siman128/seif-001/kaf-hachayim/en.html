(1) [seif 1] There is no nesiut kafim with fewer than ten etc.; if they began with ten and some left they finish as written above in Shulchan Aruch siman 55 seif 2 — from Yerushalmi Megillah chapter HaKoreh brought Rif and Rosh and Rambam chapter 8 of Tefillah as Beit Yosef wrote above siman 55; what Kenesset HaGedolah wrote in this siman note 3 and Eliyahu Rabbah note 1 wondered Poskim did not explain this — puzzling for Rif Rosh Rambam and Beit Yosef above siman 55 explained it; if because not stated explicitly in Shulchan Aruch it is learned implicitly and already explained in Beit Yosef — why need detail in Shulchan Aruch obvious matter. Later saw in Eliyahu Rabbah note 1 also challenged Kenesset HaGedolah — see there; nevertheless may settle his words meaning latter authorities after Maran who did not explain in law of nesiut kafim — requires study.
