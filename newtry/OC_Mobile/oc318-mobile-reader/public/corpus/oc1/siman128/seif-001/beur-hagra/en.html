Se'if 1: And the kohanim count toward the number, etc. Rambam derives this from Sotah 38b: a synagogue consisting entirely of kohanim, interpreted where none remain behind, implies that in a comparable case involving Israelites the kohanim count toward the required number.
And one may not, etc., even, etc. See Tosafos, Shabbos 118b, s.v. Ilu, regarding one who is not, etc. Darkei Moshe asks that Kesubos 24b calls this a positive prohibition. He answers that Tosafos concerns joining other kohanim and objects specifically because of a blessing in vain. See Magen Avraham.
