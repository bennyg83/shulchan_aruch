Siman 1, "and the Kohanim from the minyan," etc. Rambam from what is written in Sotah 38b, regarding "all of it," etc., and they established it that none remained, etc.; from this it follows that for an Israelite such as this, [he is] from the minyan.

Siman 1, "and a non-Kohen should not [raise his hands], even [with other Kohanim]," etc. See Tosafos in Shabbat 118b, s.v. "these," etc.; if not, etc.; and he raised a difficulty in Darkei Moshe, for we say in Ketubot 24b that he violates a positive commandment; and the answer is that Tosafos discusses [raising hands] with other Kohanim, and there [it applies] specifically because of a blessing in vain; and Magen Avraham.
