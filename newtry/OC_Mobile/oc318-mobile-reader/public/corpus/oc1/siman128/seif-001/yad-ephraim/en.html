In Taz, seif katan 2. The correct reading is: 'before their blessing. The same applies if one person blesses, etc., for the plural expression, "to them," need not be expounded specifically, etc.; only with two does violation depend upon being called, etc.' Meaning, we expound 'to them' to exclude a single kohen, who violates the positive commandment even if he was not called. Only when there are two kohanim do they violate it specifically after being called, whereas a single kohen is obligated to go on his own even without being called. The Taz then writes that this also applies so that people not cast aspersions, etc. Meaning, all of the preceding concerns the Torah prohibition of violating the positive commandment. With two kohanim, they violate it only after being called. Regarding the concern that people may cast aspersions, however, the fact that he was not present when 'Kohanim' was called does not suffice. The Taz then returns to his opening statement, that regarding one who regularly resides in the city, he must be stringent and leave the beis haknesses. If he wishes to account for this concern, he must leave until Bircas Kohanim is entirely completed, as stated below in seif 4, where some are recorded as having this custom for that reason.
