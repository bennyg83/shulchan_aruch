Taz siman 127 note 2 — before their blessing; and likewise if one blesses, etc., that one should not expound for them specifically plural language, etc., that specifically with two does the transgression depend on the calling, etc., so it should read. And the intent is that we expound "to them" to exclude one — who transgresses a positive commandment even if they did not call him, for specifically with two he does not transgress except through calling specifically; unlike one who is obligated to go up himself even without calling. And again the Taz wrote: also regarding that they not give rise to slander, etc. — the intent is all this regarding Torah prohibition that he not transgress a positive commandment; then with two he does not transgress except through calling; but regarding not giving rise to slander, etc., it does not help that he was not present at the time of calling kohanim, etc. And he returns to the beginning of his words that he wrote regarding one who is intoxicated in his city — one must be stringent to leave the synagogue, for if one wishes to be concerned for this, one must leave until after the blessing entirely.
