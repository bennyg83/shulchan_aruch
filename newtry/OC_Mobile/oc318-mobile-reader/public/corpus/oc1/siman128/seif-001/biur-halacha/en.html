(1) He wrote in the book Haredim MA to bless a priest of Israel that it is said thus bless the children of Israel and Israel who stand facing the faces of the priests in silence and set their hearts to receive their blessing as the the Master said, they are the names of all the Mitzvah Akal and brought it last:

There is no nesi'at kapayim, etc. — The reason is because it is a matter of sanctity; and the Ran wrote that only Kaddish, Kedushah, and Barchu are called matters of sanctity. And that ten are required for Birkat Kohanim is because we derive it by gezeirah shavah, as it is written "Thus shall you bless the children of Israel," and below it is written "And I will be sanctified among the children of Israel." And even according to our view it is only an asmakhta and is d'rabbanan — Peri Megadim; and see Chayei Adam at the beginning of klal 32.

With fewer than ten — that is, even if at the time when the chazzan began to repeat the prayer there were ten in the synagogue and afterward some left — for we rule above in siman 55, seif 2, that they complete the entire prayer — nevertheless it is forbidden to perform nesi'at kapayim, for that is a matter in itself, except that the Sages established it within the prayer. And if they began nesi'at kapayim with ten and some left, they complete it — Ran in Megillah, cited in siman 55 in the Beit Yosef; and likewise in Shachna"g, cited in Beit Yosef Taz; and he further wrote in his name that whenever there are etc., the congregation and the chazzan together in a loud voice, etc. His explanation is according to what we wrote above in siman 124 in Biur Halachah, s.v. "b'lachash," in the name of Peri Megadim; see there. Therefore I did not copy it in Mishna Berurah, for per our custom this law does not apply.

And a non-kohen may not [go up], etc. — See Peri Megadim, who agreed with Peri Chadash that a chalal who goes up to the platform transgresses a positive commandment like a non-kohen, and even if he went up he must descend. And one who is a tumtum or an androgynous may not go up to the platform; and it seems to me that he should leave the synagogue before Retzeh.
