(41) There, and the Levi pours water etc. — if the kohanim are amei ha'aretz and the Levi is a Torah scholar and does not wish to pour water by the kohanim — no guilt on him even if there is no Levi but he; and even if the Levi was accustomed to pour by kohanim and changed his mind not to pour — no guilt — R' Yehuda HaLevi siman 14 and Maharar Yitzchak HaLevi and his son R' Shmuel HaLevi and Rachash there siman 40 and 41; but Maharar Liv Sruel there siman 40 wrote a Levi should not exempt himself from pouring on kohanim unless the kohanim are unfit to raise their hands such as people of Beit She'an and Beit Chifa and minors — see there; and Beit Din Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tosefta, Or Torah note 14, Magen Avraham seif katan 7, Eliyahu Rabbah note 10 wrote one should be stringent like the latter book — see there; but Peri Chadash note 8 wrote since Levi's pouring is not an impediment — behold a city entirely kohanim all ascend though there is no Levi — the matter is clear that if kohanim are amei ha'aretz and the Levi is a Torah scholar and does not wish to pour by kohanim he acts well even if there is no Levi but he; and on the contrary he is liable death if he pours by am ha'aretz kohen — see there; and so Kenesset HaGedolah further wrote in the name of R' Shmuel HaLevi siman 41 that if there are Torah scholar kohanim and amei ha'aretz he must pour for all — and Or Torah and Magen Avraham and Eliyahu Rabbah brought it; on this Peri Chadash wrote there it is not found for there is liability of death — see there; and Yad Aharon in Hagbei brought it; Shatz on Peri Megadim 2b; Ateret Zekeinim note 7; Chasdei David there Kaf HaChayyim siman 15 note 71 wrote there Chasdei David that even though some permit concern, inquiry and question are better even if there is no Levi but he; and so Ben Aharon wrote there.
