siman 41 — there is one who says etc. Sanhedrin 52 — they treat him as secular; and the Mordechai explains in the name of Rashi from Shemos 52:1, the above-mentioned, that if they would treat him with sanctity etc., Rashi explains to open first and bless first etc.; and my teacher told me: Close relatives of a priest defiled through contact with the dead — it is a homiletical way of saying to treat him as secular etc., for he is profaned from the priesthood. And the Magen Avraham wrote: all the more so for one whose daughter converted to idolatry. And that is why he wrote "they are no longer required to treat him with sanctity" etc.; and see Magen Avraham.
