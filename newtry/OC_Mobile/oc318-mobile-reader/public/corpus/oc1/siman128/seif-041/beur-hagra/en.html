Se'if 41: Some say, etc. Sanhedrin 52b says he is treated as profane. Mordechai in Rashi's name explains the statement on 52a: if he were treated as sacred; given first reading and first blessing, etc. My relative and teacher said permission exists to treat him as profane because he lost kehunah. Mordechai adds: all the more so if his daughter apostatized. This is the meaning of 'they are not obligated,' etc. See Magen Avraham.
