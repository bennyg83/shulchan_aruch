Seif 41: Some say, etc. Sanhedrin 52b states, "They treat him as profane." The Mordechai, in the name of Rashi, explains the statement cited above on 52a, that if they treated him as sacred, etc. Rashi explains this as granting him the first opportunity to open and the first opportunity to recite a blessing, etc. My relative and teacher told me that it is an exposition of the verse that they should treat him as profane, etc., because he has been disqualified from the kehunah. He wrote that this applies all the more so if he converted out of the faith. This is the meaning of "they are not obligated," etc. See Magen Avraham.
