(150) He became tamei from the dead, etc. — and this is only deliberately — see Bi'ur Halachah.

(151) And he accepts — before the court; and for this he need not vow and swear, as above se'if 40, for forbidden relations are different since desire is strong. See responsa Ketav Sofer siman 16 — regarding a doctor visiting the dead who gains profit from money — acceptance alone does not suffice until he vows publicly as with women in transgression see there.

(152) That she apostatized or fornicated — see Magen Avraham who proved she profanes her father only through fornication, for one could have guarded her, but not through apostasy. That he mentioned apostasy — since one who apostatizes presumably fornicated too; "or fornicated" means or fornicated alone.

(153) They are not obligated further to sanctify him — for opening first and blessing first; but not for nesi'at kafayim, for even if he himself transgressed fornication he lifts his hands as above se'if 39 — all the more he is not disqualified through his daughter's fornication [Acharonim]. Explained in Mordechai — nevertheless one may sanctify him, only they are not obligated.

(154) Because her father, etc. — and only when she fornicated as betrothed or married, for the verse refers to that; but an unmarried woman, no. Acharonim wrote that today there is no custom to disqualify a kohen for fornication or apostasy of his son or daughter.
