He shall become impure. He need not take a vow, because forbidden relationships are different: His inclination overpowers him.
To sanctify him. This refers to opening first and reciting a blessing first; it is obvious that he may not perform duchaning.
She profanes. Magen Avraham asks from the passage in Sanhedrin and concludes that Mordechai means that if the daughter apostatized, she is certainly promiscuous; apostasy alone, however, does not profane her father. Mordechai therefore did not mention a son who apostatized. Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 2, disagrees: Whether a son or daughter, and whether promiscuity or apostasy, the child profanes the father and people are not obligated to sanctify him. Knesses HaGedolah writes that today the custom is not to disqualify a kohen because of his daughter's promiscuity or the apostasy of either a daughter or son. Shevus Yaakov agrees.
