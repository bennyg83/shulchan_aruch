Who apostatized. See Magen Avraham, who challenged from the passage in Sanhedrin; and from his answer he raised that Mordechai's intent is: if she apostatized, she certainly fornicated; but by apostatizing alone she does not profane her father — and therefore Mordechai did not mention a son who apostatized, see there. And in responsa Shevut Ya'akov, part 2, siman 2, he disagrees with him and wrote: a son, a son; a daughter, a daughter; promiscuity, promiscuity; apostasy, apostasy — in all of them he profanes her father; and one is not obligated to sanctify him, see there.
