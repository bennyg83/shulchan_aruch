A single kohen is obligated to ascend at least rabbinically. Tosafos on Menachos 44a explains that the Gemara excludes a single kohen only from the call, since the verse says 'kohanim' in the plural, but not from ascending.
Tur identifies Tosafos' view with Rabbeinu Peretz: A single kohen is not obligated by Torah law, though he may be obligated rabbinically; Tur says that perhaps he is obligated by Torah law. Beis Yosef explains that even two kohanim violate the command only if they were called, while one is not called. The difference would arise if one kohen was called by mistake: according to Tur he violates the command if he does not ascend, while according to Tosafos his obligation is only rabbinic. Taz understands Tur to mean that even without a call he violates the command; Tosafos would then impose a rabbinic obligation even without a call. According to Beis Yosef, however, a lone kohen is obligated only if called, yet the law says not to call him. Pri Chadash therefore asks how he can say 'Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us' when he is not commanded even rabbinically.
