57) To bend their fingers. Meaning that their hands must remain spread until they turn their faces.
58) They may not leave. They should be careful not to speak until they descend from the platform, even after lowering their hands. Eliyah Rabbah in Mateh Moshe's name.
59) Until the congregation finishes. This means the majority of the congregation. Peri Megadim.
60) To answer Amen. Before Amen, the blessing has not yet concluded. Now that people customarily say Yasher Koach to the kohanim as they descend, it is proper that the kohanim not leave the platform until after the prayer leader finishes Kaddish. Otherwise, as commonly occurs, the kohanim and congregation may be distracted from answering Yehei Shmei Rabba and the other Amens.
