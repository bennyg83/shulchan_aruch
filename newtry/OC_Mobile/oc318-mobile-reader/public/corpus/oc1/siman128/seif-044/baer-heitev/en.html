Priests. See above, se'if katan 11, from what is written:

On Simchat Yom Tov. The Beit Yosef in the name of the Agur and Hagahot Maimoniot writes the reason: it is the custom of kohanim to immerse for nesi'at kapayim, and it is difficult for them to immerse every day; therefore there is no nesi'at kapayim except on Yom Tov — end quote. Magen Avraham wrote: From this reason it appears to me that when Yom Tov falls on Shabbos they do not raise their hands, for they did not wish to annul the onah stated in the Torah. And the Taz: kohanim were accustomed not to be intimate with their wives on the night of Yom Tov so that they would not again become impure at night through keri; even though it is possible to immerse in the morning, nevertheless they would be tevulei yom. But some are accustomed not to be intimate with their wives on Yom Tov and not to immerse from erev Yom Tov — and this is confusion: if they are concerned about keri they should immerse beforehand, for there is already tum'at keri upon him; and if it is a night of tevilah he may have relations and immerse in the morning — Magen Avraham and Taz — see there. Beit Yosef wrote: And the strength of the children of Eretz Yisrael and all the kingdom of Egypt, who raise their hands every day and do not immerse — and thus it is fit and proper to conduct everywhere.
