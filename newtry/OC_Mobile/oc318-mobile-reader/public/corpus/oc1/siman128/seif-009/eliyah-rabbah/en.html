[9] They return, etc. In Levush: “and afterward he should wash,” etc. This means after removing the shoes, for if before removing them, he must return and wash because of the removal of the shoes. Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah and Olas Tamid wrote that with shoes that can be removed without touching, he may wash before removing them, and that such is their practice. Above in siman 2 I challenged this; see there.
