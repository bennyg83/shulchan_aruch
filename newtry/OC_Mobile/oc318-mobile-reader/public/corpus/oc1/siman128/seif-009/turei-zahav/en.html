In the holy language — for it is written "so shall you bless" in this language. And standing — for it is written "to serve Him and to bless in His name" — what is service? Standing, as it is written "to stand and to serve"; so too blessing. And nesiat kapayim — for it is written "and Aharon lifted his hands and blessed them."
