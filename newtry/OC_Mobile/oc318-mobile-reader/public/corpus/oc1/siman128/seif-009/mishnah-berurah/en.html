30) Until they stand before the ark. This is the current custom: They do not recite it while walking, but only after they are already standing before the sanctuary and have recited Modim; they then say this Yehi Ratzon.
31) Until Amen concludes. This is not exact. They prolong it until the prayer leader finishes 'and to You it is fitting to give thanks,' so the congregation can answer Amen to both. Magen Avraham and other later authorities. Accordingly, it is obvious that the kohanim also answer Amen to the prayer leader's blessing; I found this stated in Shulchan Shlomo.
