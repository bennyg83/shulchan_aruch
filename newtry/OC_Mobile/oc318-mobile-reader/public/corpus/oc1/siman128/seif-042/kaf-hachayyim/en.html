42) There, gloss: The Levites did not customarily wash, etc. If the Levi diverted his attention from his hands, it is proper that he wash first; certainly so if he touched his body or shoes. Bach; cited by Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, os 2; Olas Tamid, os 15; Magen Avraham, se'if katan 8; Eliyah Rabbah, os 11. Knesses HaGedolah says the widespread custom among them is for Levites to wash even while pouring water over the kohanim's hands. Every God-fearing person should follow this: Wash his own hands and then pour for the kohanim, even where Levites did not customarily wash, thereby fulfilling the Zohar's words.
