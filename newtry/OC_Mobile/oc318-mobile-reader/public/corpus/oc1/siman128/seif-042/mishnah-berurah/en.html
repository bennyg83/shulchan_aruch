155) The chalal. Meaning one born from a union forbidden to a kohen; with a divorcee, zonah, or chalalah.
156) May not recite the Priestly Blessing. He does not possess priestly status and is like a non-kohen in every respect, as stated in Even HaEzer, siman 7. Even a rabbinic chalal, such as one born from a chalutzah, may not recite it. Later authorities.
