(155) The challal — meaning one born of kehuna prohibitions: divorcee, zonah, and chalalah.

(156) He does not lift his hands — for he is not in his kehuna and is like a zar for everything, as in Even HaEzer siman 7. Even a challal d'rabbanan such as born of a chalutzah — likewise he does not lift his hands [Acharonim].
