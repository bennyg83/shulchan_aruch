142) Among the matters, etc. Meaning the matters mentioned in this siman.
143) Is not meticulous in mitzvos. This includes even serious mitzvos involving forbidden relationships and the like.
144) People accuse him. Even a true accusation, when his wickedness is publicly known.
145) Other transgressions do not, etc. Meaning except idolatry and bloodshed, as explained above.
146) Prevent. This applies even if he has not repented. Rambam explains: This is a positive mitzvah incumbent upon every kohen fit for the Priestly Blessing. We do not tell a wicked person, 'Add wickedness and abstain from mitzvos.' Do not wonder what benefit can come from this ordinary person's blessing. Acceptance of the blessing does not depend on the kohanim, but on the Holy One, blessed is He, as the verse says, 'They shall place My Name upon the children of Israel, and I will bless them.' The kohanim perform their commanded mitzvah, and the Holy One, blessed is He, in His mercy blesses Israel as He desires.
