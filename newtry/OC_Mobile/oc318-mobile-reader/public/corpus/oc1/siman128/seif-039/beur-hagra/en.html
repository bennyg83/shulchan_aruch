Seif 39: He may not, etc. This is from the Yerushalmi cited above.
For the other, etc. Meaning, even if the matter is publicly known about him. Since it says, "A kohen who killed," etc., this implies specifically where people know of it. This follows his view that the case concerns one who did not repent, as stated in seif 35. Nevertheless, even the Rambam agrees to this, as stated there.
