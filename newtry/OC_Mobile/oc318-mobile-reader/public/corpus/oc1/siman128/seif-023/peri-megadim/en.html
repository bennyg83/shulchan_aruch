And the ten. See Taz; this is from Beis Yosef. In letters 1 and 2 I asked: Perhaps fewer than ten simply are not recipients of the blessing, while there the requirement is only a quorum. One cannot say that fewer than ten kohanim are likewise unfit and that kohanim differ because they could themselves raise their hands, for the same would then apply when there are more than ten. Perhaps Beis Yosef's question is that the passage does not state whom they bless, implying that even one Israelite is fit to receive the blessing and that the kohen violates the command if that Israelite tells him to bless. Ten kohanim are not recipients because they are blessed directly by Hashem, but so that ten people hear and answer Amen, which is preferable to women answering. See letter 22.
