At the time that the Kohanim bless the people, they should not glance [around] nor get distracted; rather, their eyes should face downward in the same way one stands in prayer. And the people should be attentive to the blessing, and their faces should be opposite the faces of the Kohanim, but they should not look at them. {Rama: Gloss: And the Kohanim should also not look at their [own] hands; therefore, it is customary for them to lower their tallit over their faces and [keep] their hands outside the tallit. And there are [some] places where they practice such that their hands are [kept] within the tallit, so that the people do not look at them (Beit Yosef).}
