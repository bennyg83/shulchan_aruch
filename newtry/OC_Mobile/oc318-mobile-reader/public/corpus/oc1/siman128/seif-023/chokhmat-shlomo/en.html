Seif 23. When the kohanim bless the people, they should not look, etc., and should not gaze at them. Note: See Magen Avraham, seif katan 38, who wrote that the language ‘to gaze’ implies looking intently. See Sema in Choshen Mishpat, siman 154, seif katan 14, who wrote the opposite. See also my novellae to the Gemara, Avodah Zarah 20a, where the Gemara asks, ‘Is it permitted to gaze?’ From there the words of Magen Avraham are very astonishing, for according to him the Gemara asks nothing. This requires study; examine it.
