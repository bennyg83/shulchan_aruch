And they should not look at them. This too is so that they not be distracted. If so, all the more so they should not look elsewhere. See siman 229, where it implies that the expression "to look" means that only gazing extensively is forbidden, but mere seeing is not forbidden. Nevertheless, the custom is not to look at all. It is possible that we do this as a remembrance of the Temple, for there even mere seeing was forbidden because of the honor of the Shechinah, and if one gazed much in the Temple his eyes became dim.
