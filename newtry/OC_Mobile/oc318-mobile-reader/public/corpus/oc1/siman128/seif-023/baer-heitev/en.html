That is, so that they not be distracted; even though we say that one who looks at the priests, his eyes grow weak — that is specifically when the Temple stood and they blessed with the Explicit Name; but nowadays it is only due to distraction of attention. If so, all the more so they should not look elsewhere. And from the language "to look" it implies specifically looking much is forbidden, but a mere glance is not prohibited; however, the custom is not to look at all, and perhaps they act as a remembrance of the Temple, where even a mere glance was forbidden due to honor of the Divine Presence.
