Although it is said that one who looks at the kohanim will have his eyes dimmed, that applies only when the Temple stood and they blessed using the explicit Divine Name. Today the concern is merely distraction; all the more so, one should not look elsewhere. The term 'look intently' implies that only prolonged looking is forbidden, while an ordinary glance is permitted. The custom is nevertheless not to look at all, perhaps as a remembrance of the Temple, where even an ordinary glance was forbidden out of respect for the Divine Presence. Magen Avraham.
