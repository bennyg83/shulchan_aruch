And the ten answer after them Amen — meaning that among kohanim alone we require ten, for otherwise they are not counted for blessing; but when there is an Israelite among them, even one counts for the priestly blessing, as above that kohanim are from the count. We see the reason for the distinction: even one Israelite is fit for blessing in his own right, for the language of the priestly blessing is stated in singular; but kohanim do not come into this category except that we must divide them so there will be blessers who are accepted, and they are not accepted upon them unless there are ten.
