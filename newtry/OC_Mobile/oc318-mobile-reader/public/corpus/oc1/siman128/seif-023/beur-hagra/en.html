(1) Seif 123: "At the time," etc., and "not," etc., and "also," etc. — it is brought in Chagigah 16a s.v. "among the priests," etc.; and the same applies to the priests themselves; and as written in chapter 4 of Megillah 24b — because the people look at them; and he said in Yerushalmi there — R' Yochanan: this teaches that it is forbidden to look at the priests while they bless Israel. R' Chagai: did they say one may not look only because of distraction? Moses — meaning an oath by Moses: "I look and do not distract my attention."

(2) Therefore, etc. — and in the gemara in Megillah 40, like the first custom; and see there 31.
