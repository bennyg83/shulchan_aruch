Se'if 23: While, etc.; and not, etc.; also, etc. See Tosafos, Chagigah 16a, s.v. Bakohanim. The same applies to the kohanim themselves, as Megillah 24b says people gaze at them. The Yerushalmi says Rabbi Yehudah derived that one may not gaze at kohanim blessing Israel. Rabbi Chaggai answered that the prohibition is only to prevent distraction, swearing by Moshe that he gazed without distraction.
Therefore, etc. Megillah's Gemara follows the first custom. See se'if 31.
