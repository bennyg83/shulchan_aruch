[32] And bless Your people, etc. So wrote Shulchan Aruch. Olas Tamid wrote that from the Tur it implies that they finish the verse until “milk and honey”; end quote. I am astonished, for the language of the Tur is like the language of the Shulchan Aruch and Levush, implying that they do not finish the verse. So too it is explicit in the Perishah, and this is his language: It seems not to finish the verse, for nowadays it is not relevant to say “and the land,” etc. Also, the Tur and Rambam did not bring the whole verse, only until “and bless Your people Israel,” and they did not write “etc.”; end quote. So too it implies in Sefer Tanya.
