32) There, gloss: Some are stringent if they are leather, etc. Peri Chadash, os 5, writes that Maran holds in Beis Yosef that leather leg coverings are forbidden even without straps and no risk of breakage, because the Sages made no distinction. The custom is nevertheless lenient. Birkei Yosef, os 3, cites him and notes that an unpublished responsum of Radbaz, siman 2264, says the same. Radbaz adds that when it is not cold and the other kohanim are barefoot, he too should stand barefoot. This appears to apply where people stand barefoot before dignitaries; elsewhere, he should wear cloth coverings, as stated in the preceding os.
