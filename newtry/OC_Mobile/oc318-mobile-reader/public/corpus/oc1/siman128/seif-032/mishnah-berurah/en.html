(117) They gaze at them — and in a place where kohanim were accustomed to hang the tallit over their faces and their hands inside the tallit — he lifts his hands, and as above.

(118) Their occupation is in that — and it refers when he is not a crusher in his town; but a crusher by his craft — in his town, even if the townspeople's occupation is not in that, he is also permitted [Beit Yosef, Levush, Peri Chadash, Sha'arei Ephraim; and in some old Shulchan Aruch editions this is written in the Mechaber himself].
