117) They stare at them. Where the kohanim customarily drape the tallis over their faces and keep their hands inside it, he may recite the Priestly Blessing, as explained above.
118) Their trade is such. This concerns one not personally familiar in his city. If he is familiar there through practicing this trade, he is permitted even when the city's inhabitants generally do not practice it. Beis Yosef, Levush, Peri Chadash, and other later authorities. Some old Shulchan Aruch editions include this in the Mechaber's own text.
