When they turn their faces—whether at the beginning or at the end—they should only rotate rightward. {Rama: Gloss: When they go down from the platform, the should not touch their filthy shoes; and if they did touch [them], they must wash their hands for prayer [i.e. the Amidah], for they will continue praying after this. (Agudah - chapter "Haya Koreh" [Brachot Chapter 2])}
