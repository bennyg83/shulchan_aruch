By way of the right. This means that he should turn his face toward his right. If he is standing with his face to the east, he turns his face to the south and afterward to the west; and if his face is to the west, he turns his face to the north and afterward to the east. This is unlike those who explain the opposite, that he turns his face toward his left and thereby turns himself to his right; that is a mistake (Beis Yosef). This is also the custom (Bach, unlike the Levush), and this has already been discussed at length in the book "Yemin Hashem Romemah." When they descend from the duchan, they should turn their faces somewhat toward the heichal, like a student taking leave of his teacher; see the end of siman 132, and see Yoma, folio 36.
