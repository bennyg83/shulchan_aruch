Seif katan 17, they bless, etc. And one does not bless for all, etc. Meaning, although one generally blesses for all because ‘in a multitude of people is the King’s glory,’ as below in siman 298, seif 14, where if they were sitting Motzaei Shabbos in the beis midrash and they brought them fire, one blesses for all; nevertheless here, because of confusion, each one blesses for himself.
