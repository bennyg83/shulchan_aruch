To his right. When they initially stand facing east, they turn south and then west toward the congregation. On returning, they turn north and then east toward the Ark. This is unlike those who explain the reverse, which would have him turn his face left while rotating his body to the right. Bach, Taz, Magen Avraham, Peri Chadash, and Radbaz, part 4, siman 15. See Be'er Esek, se'if katan 6. When descending from the duchan, they should keep their faces somewhat toward the heichal, like a student taking leave of his teacher. Magen Avraham.
