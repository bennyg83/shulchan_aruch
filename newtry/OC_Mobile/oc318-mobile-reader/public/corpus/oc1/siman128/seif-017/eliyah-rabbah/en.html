[34] By way of the right, etc. Meaning, he turns his face toward his right side. If his face is to the east, he turns his face to the south and afterward to the west.
[35] With soiled shoes, etc. It appears that this is what he means: ordinary shoes are like soiled ones; see above, siman 4. Therefore he should have shoes that he can put on without touching. If he does not have such shoes and it is impossible to wash his hands, he should go barefoot until after the prayer. So wrote Agudah, chapter HaKorei Omed.
