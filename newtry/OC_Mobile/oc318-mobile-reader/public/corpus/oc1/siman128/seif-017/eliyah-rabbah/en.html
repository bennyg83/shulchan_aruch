[17] He should not ascend, etc. For it is written, “and he blessed them, and he came down from offering the sin-offering”; meaning, immediately after he performed the sin-offerings, he descended to bless them, and here too, etc.; Levush. I am astonished, for if so, when he uproots his feet immediately after completing the blessing of Retzei, it is also like there, for he blessed after descending and offering the sin-offering. But Rashi explained in Sotah, folio 38, that he blessed while the service was still in his hand, and afterward “he came down from offering the sin-offering.”
