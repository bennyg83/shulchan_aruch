(Sa) Between the beginning and the end, etc. - F. At the beginning, when they come to the duchan and face the hall, which is in the east, and the kohanim shine for them, they begin to turn their faces toward the people to their right, which is in the south, and then to the west, against the tabernacle, and at the end, when the Sha'at begins, saying Shalom, and turning their faces toward the holy place, they begin to turn their faces through their right, which is in the north, and then to the east, against the hall. When they come down from the duchan, they do not turn their backs to the hall but They will side and turn their faces a little towards the temple like a disciple who has passed away from his master who turns his face back to him When he came out in front of him [lasts]:
