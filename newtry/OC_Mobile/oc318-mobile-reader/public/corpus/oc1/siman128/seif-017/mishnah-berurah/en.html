61) Both at the beginning and at the end, etc. At the beginning, when they ascend facing the sanctuary in the east and 'Kohanim' is called, they begin turning toward the people by way of their right, toward the south, and then westward toward the congregation. At the end, when the prayer leader begins Sim Shalom and they turn toward the holy place, they begin by turning to their right, toward the north, and then eastward toward the sanctuary. When descending, they should not turn their backs to the sanctuary. They should angle themselves and keep their faces somewhat toward it, like a disciple leaving his teacher who turns his face toward him as he departs. Later authorities.
(62) Dirty — it appears the intent is plain shoes are like dirty ones [Eliyahu Rabbah and Peri Megadim].
