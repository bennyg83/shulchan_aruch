(Sa) Between the beginning and the end, etc. - F. At the beginning, when they come to the pulpit and face the hall, which is in the east, and the priests shine for them, they begin to turn their faces toward the people to their right, which is in the south, and then to the west, against the tabernacle, and at the end, when the Sha'at begins, saying peace, and turning their faces toward the holy place, they begin to turn their faces through their right, which is in the north, and then to the east, against the hall.
