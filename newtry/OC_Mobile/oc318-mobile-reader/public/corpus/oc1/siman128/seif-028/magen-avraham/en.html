He may raise. There is no prohibition of adding to the mitzvah by performing the mitzvah twice. A kohen who is praying, if there is no other kohen there, interrupts and ascends to the duchan; if there are other kohanim there, he does not interrupt (Radbaz, part 1, siman 253). But it appears to me that if they told him to ascend, in every case he must interrupt, for he is subject to a positive commandment. They said an even greater case regarding one who was called to the Torah, that he interrupts from reciting Shema, and so it appears from the Rashba, siman 185, and from Tosafos in Sotah.
