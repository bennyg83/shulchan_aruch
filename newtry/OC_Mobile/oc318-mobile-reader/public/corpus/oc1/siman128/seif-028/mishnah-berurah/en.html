(106) He may raise his hands — as above there is no "do not add" in performing the mitzvah twice; nevertheless no obligation on him since he already raised hands that day as in seif 93. See above in Mishna Berurah seif katan 11 that although not obligated to raise hands many times in one day, nevertheless each time he raises he blesses "who sanctified us" etc. A kohen praying eighteen blessings if no other kohen in synagogue must interrupt and ascend duchan so nesiut kafim not nullified; after finishing nesiut kafim he descends duchan and finishes prayer; if another kohen there he does not interrupt prayer unless told ascend duchan or take your hands — then even if other kohanim there must interrupt and ascend. Wherever he interrupts within prayer to ascend duchan must uproot feet somewhat within prayer when Shatz says Retzeh; but if he did not uproot feet when Shatz says Retzeh he may not ascend [Magen Avraham in name of Radbaz and Shvut Yaakov]. However Rabbi Akiva Eiger questions the essence of this permission to interrupt mid-prayer even when told ascend — for although prayer is d'rabbanan perhaps the Sages established their words even in place of positive commandment; and so Gaon Yaavetz in his siddur not to interrupt mid-prayer for nesiut kafim when standing in another blessing unless he reached in his prayer the place of Birkat Kohanim — then he holds permitted to uproot feet and ascend duchan for there it is not called interruption — it is like Sim Shalom [when estimating within prayer he will reach that place together with Shatz he must beware uproot feet somewhat toward duchan side when Shatz begins Retzeh as we wrote above]; and this only if assured his mind will not wander and return to prayer — otherwise forbidden in every way to interrupt prayer — this is the content of his words there — end:
