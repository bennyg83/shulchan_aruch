Another. For there is no violation of bal tosif in performing the mitzvah twice, but there is no obligation in it, even if he is in the synagogue when they call ‘Kohanim,’ since he has already performed Nesias Kapayim once. Tosafot. See also Responsa Ginas Veradim, klal 1, siman 13, and Responsa Zera Avraham, Orach Chaim, siman 12.
