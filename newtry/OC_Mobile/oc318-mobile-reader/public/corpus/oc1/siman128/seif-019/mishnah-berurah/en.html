71) After the kohanim's blessing. His concentration might become confused, leaving him unable to prompt the second or third verse. If he prays from a siddur and is certain he will not become confused, he may answer Amen, because this Amen is not an interruption; it is needed for the prayer. Some are stringent that he never answer Amen to the preliminary blessing 'Who sanctified us with Aharon's sanctity, etc.,' because that would interrupt the prayer. The Amen to the Priestly Blessing itself differs: It is not an interruption because it expresses acceptance of the blessing.
