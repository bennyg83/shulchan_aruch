Even if he is assured that he will not be confused, he may not answer Amen — Bach, Taz; and also Tosafos Yom Tov wrote thus, and retracted from what he wrote in his book, part 38, siman 85, mishnah 4. And these words of Tosafos Yom Tov were omitted from the author of Pri Chadash, see there. And Magen Avraham and Peri Chadash hold: if he is assured that he will not be confused, it is permitted to answer Amen, see there. And according to all, it is permitted to answer Amen after the first blessing of the priests, which is to bless His people Israel with love — for here there is no concern of confusion, and he will not be confused, since he has not yet begun — Taz, see there.
