Kohanim. Even if he is assured that he will not become confused, he should not answer Amen; Bach and Taz. The Tosafos Yom Tov also wrote this, and retracted from what he wrote in his Lechem Chamudos, siman 85, Mishnah 4. These words of the Tosafos Yom Tov escaped the author of Peri Chadash; see there. Magen Avraham and Peri Chadash hold that if he is assured that he will not become confused, he may answer Amen; see there. According to all opinions, he may answer Amen after the first blessing of Birkas Kohanim, namely, ‘to bless His people Israel with love,’ for here there is no concern of confusion and he will not become confused, since he has not yet begun; Taz, see there.
