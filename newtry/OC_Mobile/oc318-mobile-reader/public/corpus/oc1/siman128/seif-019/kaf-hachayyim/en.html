19) There: Or to wash his hands. If the person bringing water for the kohanim approaches a prayer leader who is a kohen and reminds him to wash, the prayer leader must recite the Priestly Blessing even when other kohanim are present; if he does not ascend, he violates three positive commands. The water-bearer should therefore be taught not to remind him when other kohanim are present, so that he will not violate if he does not bless. Beis Yosef in Mordechai's name; Rama cites it below in the gloss to se'if 20; Peri Chadash, os 4. The person calling 'kohanim' should likewise be taught not to intend the prayer leader when others are present, in deference to Maharam Mintz's view that an explicit request obligates him even if he did not move his feet during Retzeh, as stated in the preceding os. Olas Tamid, os 42, says excluding the prayer leader from the caller's intent is ineffective, citing the Yerushalmi about Rabbi Shimon ben Pazi standing behind pillars when ill. That proof is not compelling: There the purpose was to avoid being seen as disqualified. No such concern applies to the prayer leader, because everyone knows he does not ascend precisely because he leads the prayer. It is therefore best that the caller not intend him. See Eliyah Rabbah, os 40.
