The chazzan should not. This is so that his mind not become confused and he not know which blessing he must prompt; but it is not considered an interruption, for this Amen is considered a need of the prayer (Tosafos); see siman 104, se'if 7. If he is assured that he will not become confused, he may do so (Lechem Chamudos Rabbah, Parashas Ki Savo). In Divrei Chamudos he retracted and wrote that one does not derive practical halachah from a midrash, etc. The Bach likewise wrote in the name of the long Mordechai that specifically the priestly blessing is permitted, so that the priestly blessing not be entirely nullified, but he should not answer Amen. Also in siman 585, se'if 3, he wrote in the name of Maharshal that it is forbidden for the chazzan to blow shofar even if he is assured. According to the words of the Shulchan Aruch, it appears to me that the priestly blessing is different, since he must uproot himself from his place; we do not permit it if there is another kohen there. But blowing shofar, where he stands in his place, is permitted; if so, Amen likewise is permitted, and all the more so nowadays when we pray from a book, as written above on the beginning of siman 116.
