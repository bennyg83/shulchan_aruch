Alefs as ayins. The same applies to one who reads ches as hei, unless all the people of his city read that way (Radbaz, part 1, siman 45, and Maharit, part 5, siman 16).
And ayins as alefs. Although we do not find the letter ayin in the priestly blessing, nevertheless, when he reads "May Hashem shine," it appears as though he is reading "forest" with an ayin, since ayin and alef are the same for him (Maharshal in his commentary to Semag).
