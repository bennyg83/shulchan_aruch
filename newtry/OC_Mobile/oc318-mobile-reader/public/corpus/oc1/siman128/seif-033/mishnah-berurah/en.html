119) Who does not know, etc. The same applies to one who has difficulty speaking or articulating. Rambam.
120) Alephs as ayins. The same applies to one who pronounces ches as hei or shiboles as siboles. If everyone in his city pronounces them that way, he may recite the Priestly Blessing there. For this reason, the later authorities write that nowadays, when most of our people cannot distinguish the pronunciation of ayin from aleph, he may recite it. Some further say that in Russia, where many pronounce shiboles as siboles, he may recite it even though the general population knows the difference between shin and sin. Taz, cited by Eliyah Rabbah.
