(119) Who does not know [Hebrew], etc. — and likewise one who is heavy of mouth and heavy of tongue [Rambam].

(120) To alefin instead of ayinin — and likewise one who reads ḥetin with a hei or reads shevoles as sevoles; and if all his townspeople read thus, he may lift hands in that place. And for this reason the Acharonim wrote that in our time, since most of our people do not know to distinguish the pronunciation of ayin from alef — automatically he may lift his hands. And some say further that in the country of Russia where many are accustomed to read shevoles as sevoles — even though generally the country's people know the difference between right shin and left shin — nevertheless he may lift his hands [Taz, brought in Avnei Rochel].
