Ayins. The same applies to one who pronounces chets as heis. If everyone in his city speaks this way, they may raise their hands. Knesses HaGedolah in the name of Radbaz and Maharit. Peri Chadash writes: Know that in all these cases where we say that he should not raise his hands, it appears to me that if he ascended, he need not descend.
Alefs. Although no ayin appears in Birchas Kohanim, when he says Ya'er Hashem it sounds as though he says ya'ar with an ayin, since ayin and alef sound alike in his speech. Magen Avraham in the name of Maharshal. I do not understand: This explains one who pronounces alefs as ayins, but not one who pronounces ayins as alefs.
