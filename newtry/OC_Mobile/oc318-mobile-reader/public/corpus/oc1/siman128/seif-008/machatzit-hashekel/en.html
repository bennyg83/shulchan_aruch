(Sk. 8) and not 20 and 22 Chab Dashgar Dasgi in all the hands of Damanki:
It is forbidden to put, etc. So too in siman 511. The Taz added there that even if he put it in before Yom Tov it is forbidden, because he creates a scent in the hands. In the responsa of Chacham Tzvi he disagrees, for not only if he placed it before Yom Tov is it permitted, since with human flesh the issue of creating a scent does not apply, for we maintain in siman 322 that one may crush fragrant woods with his fingers and smell them, even though presumably he intends that his hands absorb the scent. Even to put it in on Yom Tov is permitted, for with food and drink the issue of creating a scent does not apply, since it is permitted to put spices into a cooked dish. Although these waters are for washing and not for drinking, nevertheless, since they are fit for drinking, there is no prohibition of creating a scent.
