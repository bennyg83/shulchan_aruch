But if he did not lift his feet during Retzeh, etc. — Magen Avraham brought in the name of Radbaz that if it was because of compulsion, he also should not ascend, and if he ascended he should not descend; and Eretz Tzvi is uncertain when it was not because of compulsion and he ascended, whether he should descend; and this is cited in Eliyah Rabbah and Magen Giborim, who ruled that when it was not because of compulsion, even if he ascended he should descend.

He should not ascend again. — See Eliyah Rabbah, who brought in the name of responsa of Maharam Mintz, who is uncertain: if they told him to ascend, whether he must ascend.
