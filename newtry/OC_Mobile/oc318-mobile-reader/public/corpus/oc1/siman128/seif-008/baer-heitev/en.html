He shall ascend. I would say at the beginning of the shaliach tzibur's Retzeh; but before the shaliach tzibur finishes Retzeh he must uproot — Pri Chadash see there. Maharikash wants to prove from here that a kohen who came to synagogue after Modim may bless Birkat Kohanim even though he was not in synagogue when they said Retzeh, since it says every kohen in synagogue is uprooted from his place — implying when not in synagogue he need not uproot his feet at Avodah and may ascend the duchan even if he did not hear Retzeh — see there. In responsum Radbaz brought in Knesset HaGedolah not so, who said there: one who uprooted his feet from his house to synagogue is not called uprooting feet to ascend the duchan, therefore he does not ascend — see there. In responsum Ginat Veradim klal 1 siman 13 and siman 22, Pri Chadash disagreed and brought like Radbaz — see there; Knesset HaGedolah wrote it appears to me if his house is adjacent to synagogue and he uprooted his feet from his house before Retzeh to ascend the duchan it is called uprooting — see there; Pri Chadash wrote and this is simple: everything depends on uprooting his feet with intent to ascend the duchan — see there. A kohen who did not intend to ascend the duchan and therefore did not uproot his feet at Retzeh as required by law, and when the shaliach tzibur reached Modim he remembered and came to ascend — he does not ascend; if he ascended he descends — Ginat Veradim siman 22 see there. If he was forced when the shaliach tzibur reached Avodah and did not uproot at Avodah — he does not ascend; if he ascended he does not descend — Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Radbaz see there. The same in the first law: one who uprooted from his house to come to synagogue, etc., likewise if he ascended he does not descend — see Ginat Veradim siman 22. A kohen praying the Eighteen: if there is no other kohen he stops and ascends; if there are kohanim he does not stop — Radbaz chelek 4 siman 293; see Dvar Shmuel siman 67. If they told him "ascend" — in every case he must interrupt — Magen Avraham se'if katan 40. Do not challenge: if everything depends on uprooting at Avodah, why did they rule in se'if 4 that when kohanim do not wish to ascend they must wait outside synagogue while the chazzan calls Kohanim — see there; and Rama likewise in se'if 44 see there. Is it not that even if sitting in synagogue, if he did not uproot at Avodah he would not transgress as we say? One can answer: certainly permitted to sit in synagogue and he does not transgress if he did not uproot at Retzeh; also if they did not tell him ascend, as explained in Yerushalmi: R' Shimon ben Pazi was standing behind the pillar and did not go outside — see Beit Yosef. And proven from se'if 20: if the shaliach tzibur is a kohen he does not raise his hands — see there. That which Shulchan Aruch and Rama wrote to go outside is good advice lest someone in the congregation say before Retzeh "ascend" and then he would transgress. Therefore forced to leave before Retzeh. And after Modim also one should not enter, since there is dispute among poskim: Maharikash — must ascend; Radbaz — does not ascend, and if ascended does not descend as written; therefore they wrote a definitive ruling that one must wait outside while chazzan calls Kohanim — then for all it is fine — end of difficulty. See Magen Avraham se'if katan 4, 34, 69. See responsum Zera Avraham to Mahara Yitzchak siman 14 and Ginat Veradim klal 1 siman 13.
