When the chazzan begins, etc. Our custom is that the chazzan waits at Retzei until the kohanim come up before the ark. This may be because of the concern that perhaps they had not moved their feet from the place where they washed their hands, for presumably it is from there that they are considered to have moved their feet, and not when they moved their feet merely to wash their hands, as stated by Ateres Zekeinim.
