When the prayer leader begins, etc. — our custom is that the prayer leader waits at Retzeh for the priests to ascend before the ark; and it is possible that this is from concern lest perhaps he did not uproot his feet from the place of washing their hands, for it stands to reason that from there it is called "uprooting his feet" — not where he uproots his feet to wash his hands (Eishel Mezuzah).
