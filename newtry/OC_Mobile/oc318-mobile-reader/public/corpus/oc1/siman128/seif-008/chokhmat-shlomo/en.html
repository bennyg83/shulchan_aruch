(Seif 40) A Kohen who married a divorcee should not raise his hands. NB: see Magen Avraham, what he challenged on Rambam; and see what is written in his answer in omissions to my composition Nidrei Zeruzin in Yoreh De'ah, siman 228 — there I settled his words according to Magen Avraham's method in additional explanation; see there in my second composition to omissions on Nedarim; see there; and understand well; and it is in my composition in manuscript, folio 173. And behold, regarding this law of a Kohen who married a divorcee, see what is written in my books Sefer HaChayim, to settle Rambam's approach because they take for themselves light-minded people and do not concern themselves with vows of prohibition; see there. And see what is written on this in my composition to Yoreh De'ah, Hilchot Bechor, siman 314 — proof for this from Shas in Bechoros, folio 36b, regarding R' Eliezer, who brought before him a firstborn and did not know if he was a Kohen; see there — until for the honor of a sage he was concerned about a prohibition, he acted; from there it is proven that perforce we are concerned in such a case that he will take for himself light-minded people, who will not concern themselves for the honor of a sage and will permit him; for otherwise there is no explanation for the passage there: perhaps he was not concerned, only that the three would not permit him, since there is a sage; or that he was cunning, that the three would not permit him, since there is no Kohen with him — and perforce the concern is that perhaps he will take for himself three light-minded people and they will permit him; and perforce he himself was concerned for the honor of a sage; see there; and understand well in this.
