Water. See Baer Heitev, and see below, siman 511, in the Taz and Magen Avraham. There Baer Heitev cites Responsa Gur Aryeh and Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 92. Birkei Yosef, siman 511, cites his teacher in Sefer Nechpah BaKesef that on Shabbos one should be stringent not to place spices into water intended for washing. If they were placed there before Shabbos, however, one may wash with that water. See there.
