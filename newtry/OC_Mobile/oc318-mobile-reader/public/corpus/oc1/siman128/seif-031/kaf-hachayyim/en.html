31) There: But leg coverings are permitted. Maran's view in Beis Yosef is that every kind of leg covering is permitted, even if it has straps and is tied near the knee, provided it is not leather. So write Olas Tamid, os 14 and os 11, and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 6. Olas Tamid says that one who tries to be stringent and goes entirely barefoot without cloth leg coverings acts improperly in places where people do not go barefoot before dignitaries. Chagigah likewise says it is improper to bare one's leg before one's teacher, implying that one should not go barefoot in a synagogue or study hall. Mishnah Berurah, os 18, cites him.
