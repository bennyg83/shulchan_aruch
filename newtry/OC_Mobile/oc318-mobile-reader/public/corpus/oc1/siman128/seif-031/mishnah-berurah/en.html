(114) If the custom [is to drape the tallit], etc. — for they cannot gaze at them; and if the custom is not so but he wants to do so because of his blemishes — it is forbidden; and even if all the kohanim want to do like him so there is no distinction from other kohanim — nevertheless forbidden, for they will gaze at them because they changed their custom and will divert their attention.

(115) To hang the kohanim's tallit, etc. — but it does not help what in some places the congregation covers their faces — for when they know there is a blemish with him they will gaze; and also there are youths who have no tallit.

(116) On his hands — but for blemishes on his face it helps.
