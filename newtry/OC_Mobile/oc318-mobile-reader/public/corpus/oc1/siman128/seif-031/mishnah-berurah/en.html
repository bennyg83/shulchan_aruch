114) If the custom, etc. People then cannot look at the deformities. If this is not the local custom and he alone wishes to cover himself because of his deformities, it is forbidden. Even if all the kohanim wish to imitate him so he will not differ from them, it remains forbidden, because people will stare at all of them for changing the custom and become distracted.
115) For the kohanim to drape, etc. It does not help that in some places the congregation covers their faces. When people know a kohen has a deformity, they may still come to look; moreover, some young men have no tallis.
116) For his hands. It does, however, help for deformities on his face.
