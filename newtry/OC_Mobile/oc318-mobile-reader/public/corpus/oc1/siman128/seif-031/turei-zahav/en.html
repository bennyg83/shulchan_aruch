Specifically only incidentally. In Beis Yosef he brought this distinction in the name of most poskim, and wondered at the Tur for not bringing this distinction, only the distinction between being alone or with other adult kohanim. It appears that our master was in the land of Ashkenaz, where the custom, as in our country, is that Nesias Kapayim is performed only on Yom Tov. If so, all of it is incidental, and it is permitted with other kohanim. This appears to be the primary ruling, that among us there is no need to distinguish with this distinction.
