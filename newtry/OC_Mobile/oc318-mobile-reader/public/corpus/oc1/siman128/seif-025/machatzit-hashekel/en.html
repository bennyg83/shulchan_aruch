They turn to the right. Since they face east, they turn toward the south, which is the right side of one facing east.
This is unlike the reverse interpretation. Every turn one makes must be toward the right. See Magen Avraham 651:21 and siman 676.
The same rule appears in siman 132 regarding leaving the synagogue. Yoma 53a states that the Kohen Gadol left the Holy of Holies walking backward while facing the Ark. Kohanim during their service, Levi'im on their platform, and Israelites at their station likewise moved sideways and did not turn their backs.
