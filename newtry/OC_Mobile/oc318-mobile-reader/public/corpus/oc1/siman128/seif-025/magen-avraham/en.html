To their brothers in the fields. As for why we do not say that they bless women and children, since it is written, "So shall you bless the sons of Yisrael," but not the daughters of Yisrael (Bach), yet in Sotah, folio 38, it says: I know only the sons of Yisrael; from where do we include converts, women, and slaves? Scripture says, "Say to them," meaning to all of them. One cannot say that only when there are males present are they blessed together with them, for if so, let us say the same regarding converts; and if there are only kohanim and converts in the city, not all the kohanim should ascend, but only those in excess of ten. Therefore it appears to me that women and children are not significant enough to be blessed on their own, as Rashi wrote regarding fewer than ten; see there. Nevertheless, the fruit of a woman's womb is blessed from the man.
More than ten. Since the world consists of kohanim, they are not significant with fewer than ten; this is unlike se'if 1, where Yisraelim answer, for even fewer than ten suffice.
