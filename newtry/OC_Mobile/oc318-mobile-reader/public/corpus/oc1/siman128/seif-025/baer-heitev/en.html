Who are in the fields. Why do we not say that the kohanim bless women and children, as stated afterward regarding answering Amen? Bach answers that the verse says, 'Thus shall you bless the sons of Israel,' excluding the daughters of Israel. See Magen Avraham.
Amen. Since those answering are kohanim, fewer than ten answering kohanim are not considered significant. This differs from se'if 1, where those answering are Israelites and even fewer than ten suffice. See Taz.
