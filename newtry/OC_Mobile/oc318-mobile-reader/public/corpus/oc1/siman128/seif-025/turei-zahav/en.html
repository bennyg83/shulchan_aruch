But it is better, etc. It seems to me that they should say them while the prompter first reads the word. In the Gemara there is a dispute about this: Rav Yosef would say them between each blessing, and the other amoraim did not want to say them, and so is the halacha. But while the prompter is reading, all agree, for there it is not considered that he is failing to listen to Birkas Kohanim. The Gemara did not mention this because there the prompter did not chant the word, and it was impossible to say the verse at that time; but now, when the prompter also chants the word, he can properly say it at that time.
