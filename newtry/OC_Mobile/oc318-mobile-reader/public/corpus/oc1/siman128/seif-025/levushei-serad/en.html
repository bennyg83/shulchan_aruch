Taz se'if kaf yud dalet — "but since you were precise." His intent: between berachah and berachah there is no concern of confusion; and as he wrote afterward — there is no concern of confusion if he has not yet begun; and the main concern in answering Amen is between the berachot themselves after beginning; therefore in this case the concern of confusion applies even to a light matter — namely because he diverted his mind to the intent of Amen; therefore "certain" does not apply, for it is not in his power to turn his thought at will. Not so after finishing Birkat Kohanim and beginning "Sim shalom" — there is not so much concern of error, since it is a completely different matter; the concern is only because of fear of the congregation; and in this "certain" applies, because it depends on human nature.
