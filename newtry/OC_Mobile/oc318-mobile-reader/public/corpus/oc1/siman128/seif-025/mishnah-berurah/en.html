97) All of them. This excludes the prayer leader. Even if he is a kohen, we ruled above in se'if 20 that when other kohanim are present, a kohen serving as prayer leader does not recite the Priestly Blessing, even if confident he can resume. Instead, he serves as prompter. As stated above in se'if katan 87, when no Israelite is available, a kohen may prompt.
98) Their brethren in the fields. It does not say the women and children because they are not important enough to justify a blessing addressed solely to them. Nevertheless, they too are included in the blessing.
(99) Women and minors — meaning if they are there; and if they are not there they also raise hands — for answering Amen does not impede the blessing.
100) More than ten, etc. Since ten adult men can remain to answer Amen, it is preferable to fulfill both requirements: Some kohanim ascend and the ten answer Amen.
(101) And the ten answer, etc. — for even though we hold above in seif 1 that nesiut kafim requires ten and kohanim are from the number, and it is found there are not ten answering here — nevertheless it is fine, for that case is different: since the essence of Birkat Kohanim was stated over Israel, they count even with fewer than ten, and even if there is only one Israelite all ascend the duchan and bless that Israelite; not so in our case where all are kohanim — they do not count to bless themselves unless there are ten who will receive the blessing and answer Amen.
