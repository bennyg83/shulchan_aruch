More than ten — From this language it implies that even if there are only eleven [that is, with the chazzan], one blesses and ten answer Amen [that is, the chazzan joins with the nine who answer Amen if he is assured that he will return to his prayer — he is permitted to answer Amen, as is written in Mishna Berurah, seif 71]. And apparently — is it not better in this manner that they all go up and answer Amen, and the women and children fulfill the kohanim's Torah-level positive commandment of nesi'at kapayim? Not so if only one goes up — for according to some poskim it is only a rabbinic positive commandment. But in truth this is not so, for in our case, according to what Peri Megadim wrote, the entire essence of nesi'at kapayim is only d'rabbanan in general, for there is no Israelite here who tells him to bless [and what is written "Say to them," and Onkelos translates "when they say to them" — is only when Israelites say, or even the reciter is a kohen as agent of the Israelite who was there; not so here, where they are all kohanim and the kohen is the reciter]. Nevertheless, it still requires further study.
