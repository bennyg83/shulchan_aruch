One should not say, etc. For is there any servant whom they bless and who does not listen?
Not to say them. So too the later authorities wrote that this is primary. The Magen Avraham and Taz wrote that one who says them should say them while the shaliach tzibbur prompts the kohanim, but the Eliyah Rabbah is uncertain even about this; see there. So too the Vilna Gaon instituted not to say them, Maaseh Rav. All the more so one must be careful not to say the verses aloud, as the masses do, and especially not to repeat the words from “Yevarechecha” onward; this is a mistake, for this custom is not mentioned anywhere, Eliyah Rabbah.
