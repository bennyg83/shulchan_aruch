One who has a blemish. It appears obvious that all those who do not perform Nesias Kapayim must leave the synagogue at the time of the duchan, so that people not say about him that he is the son of a divorcee, as written above in seif 5. So wrote the Mordechai, chapter HaKorei Omed, in the name of Rabbi Yehudah HaLevi of Speyer, that a kohen mourner should leave the synagogue, since the custom is that he does not perform Nesias Kapayim.
