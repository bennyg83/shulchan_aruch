One who. See Taz, se'if katan 4; the reference to se'if katan 5 is a scribal error. One might say that mourning is not outwardly recognizable, unlike a blemish. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 51: Mourning is only a custom. See Peri Chadash: One whose garments do not properly cover him may not raise his hands, as stated in Megillah. Citing Sefer HaMiktzo'os, he also says that a kohen with a niddah in his home may not raise his hands. I do not know whether this means his wife is a niddah or even another woman.
