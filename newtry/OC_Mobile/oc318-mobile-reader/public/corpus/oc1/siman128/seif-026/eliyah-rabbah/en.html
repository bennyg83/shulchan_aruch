[26] With love etc. Be'er Sheva wrote: if you ask where is 'with love' hinted in the Torah — one may say it is in Tanhuma 'say to them' 'say fully' meaning with heart's intent and not like a decree in haste — that is 'with love' with heart's intent — end. And further in Tanhuma siman 1 'say fully' with vowel patach that a person must give peace to his fellow with full mouth; and in Zohar: any kohen who does not love the nation or the nation does not love him shall not raise his hands — and perhaps for this they say 'with love.' The reason appears to me as in chapter Chelek daf 106: trusted are wounds of a lover, etc.; kisses of an enemy are profuse; a good curse — Ahiyah the Shilonite cursed Israel with blessings Balaam blessed.
