(2) To say them. And likewise Magen Avraham siman 95, and likewise Bach, Orach Tzedek, Peri Chadash, and Knesset HaGedolah. And likewise Magen Avraham that they say them while the chazan reads the word; and likewise Taz; however I saw an outrage that the multitude shouts the verses in a loud voice and then return and shout from yevarechecha until the word where he stands, and cannot hear the voice of the priests because of their voice — and they do not act properly, and it is fitting to abolish this. I was asked whether it is permitted to read the parasha shenayim mikra ve'echad targum while the priests bless the people, and I answered that it is not permitted; even per the view that permits reading the parasha shelo bederech keriah while the shatz reads the parasha from a sefer Torah, nevertheless it is forbidden during Birkat Kohanim.
