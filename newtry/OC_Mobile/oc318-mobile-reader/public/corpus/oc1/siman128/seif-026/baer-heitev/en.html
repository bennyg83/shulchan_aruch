To say them. And so Magen Avraham siman 95; and so Bach and Eliyahu Rabbah and Peri Chadash and Knesset HaGedolah. And so Magen Avraham: the one who says them should say them when the cantor prompts the word; and so Taz — but I have seen an outrage, that the multitude shouts the verses in a loud voice and then returns and shouts "May He bless you" up to the word where he stands, and one cannot hear the voice of the priests because of their voice — and they do not act properly, and it is fitting to abolish this. I was asked whether it is permitted to read the parasha twice Hebrew and once translation while the priests bless the people — and I answered that it is forbidden; even for the view of the one who permits reading the parasha of Shema while the prayer leader reads the parasha from the Torah scroll, nevertheless it is forbidden during Birkat Kohanim — Knesset HaGedolah and Peri Chadash:
