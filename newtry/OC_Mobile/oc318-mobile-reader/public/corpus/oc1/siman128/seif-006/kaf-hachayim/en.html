(6) In the gloss: a non-kohen shall not raise his hands etc. One who comes nowadays and says I am a kohen — Maran wrote in Orach Chayyim beginning siman 3 he is not believed and they do not promote him to kehunah on his own word and he shall not read first in the Torah nor raise his hands; but Mor Rama wrote in that gloss in the name of Rama that he is believed to read first in the Torah and raise his hands nowadays since we have no terumah d'oraisa that we fear perhaps they promote him to terumah; and wrote thus is practiced everywhere nowadays where terumah is not practiced — and there is nothing to fear — end; and Chavot Yair wrote there note 2 that even though the rabbi ruled like Rama not in all his words did he rule; and where practiced nowadays even terumah d'rabbanan such as in Eretz Yisrael as written Yoreh De'ah siman 331 seif 2 — then there is concern perhaps from Torah reading they promote him to terumah d'rabbanan; but where terumah is not practiced at all then he may be called to the Torah for nothing is lost per Ran and Maggid Mishneh on the custom — see there; and Bach there note 2 questioned Rama's words and wrote Ran and Maggid did not write he raises hands only that he reads in the Torah since there is no prohibition in the matter; but to raise hands one may say it is forbidden for there is d'oraisa prohibition as written 'so shall you bless — you shall bless' and not non-kohanim; and he resolved every matter without benefit we do not suspect he will do prohibition and he has no benefit from this — therefore we suspect nothing except perhaps he eats terumah; but no concern for disqualification or promoting to Torah; and in Eretz Yisrael where terumah is practiced as Yoreh De'ah 331 he is not believed even nowadays; and he concluded the rule emerging from these laws for lineage all require two valid witnesses and for terumah d'rabbanan one witness suffices and he is not believed on his own — see there; and that the rabbi did not fear perhaps they give him pidyon haben which also has benefit — one may say one who has pidyon haben does not give it to every kohen until he knows he is pedigreed and a Torah scholar to fulfill the mitzvah properly — not so terumah nowadays that goes to burning and is given to any kohen even am ha'aretz as Yoreh De'ah there seif 19 — therefore we are concerned; and see in Sefer Chakkei Chayyim discourse 1 on pidyon and examine; nevertheless Be'er Heitev wrote he is believed nowadays even in Eretz Yisrael — see there; and Ba'er Heitev brought it there seif katan 1; and Rav Maharim Mintz vol. 1 siman 149 wrote: perhaps when one comes from a known place and says I am a kohen they promote him to read first and raise hands — for where caravans are common a person is not likely to lie so his ascent not be a descent; but when he comes from a distant place unknown to us such as these forced converts coming from a distant land where they did not practice religion of Moshe and Yehudah — certainly we do not believe him on his own only through others — end; and see Amud LaMelamed on Aliyat Kessef note 12 and Lechem De'ah siman 5 note 12; and it appears that now such is practiced: if we see he is a Torah scholar and God-fearing and says he is a kohen from such-and-such place — believed on his own to read first and raise hands even in Eretz Yisrael; and if he comes from a distant land where no caravan is found and he is also am ha'aretz — not believed even outside Eretz Yisrael unless through others; and see further below siman 135 note 22.
