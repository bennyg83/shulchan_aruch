They return and wash their hands, etc. — Magen Avraham wrote that it is forbidden to delay for the time it takes to walk twenty amot between washing and the blessing [Tosafos]; and therefore one must wash close to Retzeh, and the chazzan should not prolong Retzeh. And Eliyah Rabbah wrote that we rely on the poskim that one need not wash at all when he washed his hands in the morning. Nevertheless, lekhatchilah it is good to be careful in this as much as possible [Peri Megadim]; and therefore it is good lekhatchilah to be careful not to talk between washing and the blessing.
