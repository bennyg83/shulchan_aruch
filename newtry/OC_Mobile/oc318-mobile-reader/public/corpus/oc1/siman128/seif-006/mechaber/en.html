Even though the Kohanim washed their hands in the morning, they go back and wash their hands again up to the wrist, which is the joint connecting the hand and the arm. The Levi pours water on their hands, and prior to this, the Levi washes [the Levi's own] hands. {Rama: (We do not practice that the Levi'im wash their hands first; rather they rely on their morning washing.)}
