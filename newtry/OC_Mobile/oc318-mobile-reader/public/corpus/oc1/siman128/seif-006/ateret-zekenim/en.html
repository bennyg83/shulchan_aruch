The levi shall pour water over their hands. In a place where there are no leviim, but only kohanim, then if there is a firstborn, the firstborn should give water to the kohanim and pour it over their hands. I found this in an old machzor in the name of Maharari Molin. It is likewise written in one responsum that a firstborn who opened his mother's womb is holy, takes the place of the levi, and pours water over the hands of the kohanim. In a city where there is a levi who is a Torah scholar, while the kohanim are unlearned and some of them have not yet grown a full beard, the levi has the right not to pour water for them, as stated in the responsa of Maharer Levi, siman 39. It appears to me that a firstborn who opened his mother's womb likewise takes the place of the levi and pours water over the hands of the kohanim, as stated above.
