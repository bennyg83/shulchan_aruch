And the Levite pours water upon their hands. And in a place where there are no Levites, only priests, then if there is a firstborn, the firstborn gives water to the priests and pours upon their hands. Thus I found in an old machzor, see there, Mahari Milin; and so too in one responsum — for the firstborn who is the first issue of his mother's womb is holy and enters in place of the Levite and pours water upon the priests' hands. And in a city where there is a Levite who is a scholar and the priests are unlearned, and some of the priests still do not have the beard-sign of maturity — authority is in the hands of the Levite not to pour for them (responsa of Mahari Levi, siman 39). And it appears to me that even the firstborn who is the first issue of his mother's womb may enter in place of the Levite and pour water upon the priests' hands, as above.
