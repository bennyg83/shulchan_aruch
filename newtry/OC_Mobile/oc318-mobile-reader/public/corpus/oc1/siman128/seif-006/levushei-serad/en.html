Taz se'if kaf alef — "they did not refrain." It is difficult — in Yevamot he explains the reason because he informed them; and Rashi explained "and not because they nullified their mind"; and likewise I saw that Eliyah Rabbah challenged. And it appears that the Taz's intent is on what the Gemara says there — initially they did not refrain because they did not act like Beit Shammai according to their words; behold, Beit Shammai in practice nullified their mind against Beit Hillel, even though in halachah they disagreed with them.
