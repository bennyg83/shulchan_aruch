Taz, seif katan 1. They did not refrain. This is difficult, for in Yevamos the reason is explained as that they informed them, and Rashi explains that it was not because they nullified their opinion. I saw that Eliyah Rabbah asked this. It seems to me that the Taz’s intent is to what the Gemara there first said, that they did not refrain because Beis Shammai did not act according to their words; thus Beis Shammai nullified their view in practice before Beis Hillel, although in halacha they disputed them.
