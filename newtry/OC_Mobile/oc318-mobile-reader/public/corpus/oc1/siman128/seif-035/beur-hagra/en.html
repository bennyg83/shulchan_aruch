Se'if 35: Even, etc.; even, etc. Rambam, as with an idolater under his view, as stated in se'if 37.
Some say, etc. Yerushalmi, HaNizakin, says: perhaps one will say, 'This kohen commits immorality and bloodshed, yet blesses me.' The Holy One answers: 'Does he bless you? I bless you,' as it says, 'They shall place,' etc. Rambam limits this to mere rumors. This is the meaning of se'if 36, 'and if,' etc.
