(2) And some say, etc. — as written in Yerushalmi chapter HaNezikin: lest you say — this Kohen exposes incest and sheds blood, and he blesses me — the Holy One, blessed be He, said: does he bless you? I bless you, as it says "and His name," etc.; and Rambam establishes it regarding one who is unknown, only among murmurers, etc.; and this is what he wrote in seif 136: "and if," etc.
