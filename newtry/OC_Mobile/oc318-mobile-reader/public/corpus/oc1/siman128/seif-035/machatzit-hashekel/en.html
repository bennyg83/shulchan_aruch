See siman 229 regarding a rainbow. Only prolonged gazing causes one's eyes to dim. Rosh asks how the congregation can look toward the kohanim to receive the blessing and answers that an ordinary glance is permitted; only prolonged gazing is forbidden. The same applies here.
In the Temple, perhaps even an ordinary glance was forbidden. Chagigah says that one who gazes at the kohanim while they duchan will have his eyes dimmed, and explains that this refers to the Temple, where the explicit Divine Name was used. The word 'gazes' means prolonged looking. Only such looking carries the punishment of dimmed eyes, but an ordinary glance may nevertheless have been forbidden in the Temple.
