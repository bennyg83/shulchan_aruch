What is the law if all the kohanim wish to lower their talleisim? Lowering it for only one kohen certainly does not help, because his difference from the others attracts attention; it appears that even all of them may not lower them. Taz asks that if the congregation lowers its talleisim, it should be permitted even if the kohanim do not. Eliyah Rabbah answers that only lowering by the kohanim helps. The congregation lowers its talleisim voluntarily, and members may raise them and look at a kohen whose appearance is unusual.
