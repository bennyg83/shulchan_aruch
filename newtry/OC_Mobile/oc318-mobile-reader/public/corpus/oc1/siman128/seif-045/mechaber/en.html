The manner in which the kohanim turn during Birkat Kohanim. These are the words during which the kohanim turn toward the south and the north: “May Hashem bless you,” “and protect you,” “may Hashem illuminate,” “for you,” “and be gracious to you,” “may Hashem turn,” “for you,” and “peace.” The custom is to prolong the melody at the ends of the verses, but not while pronouncing the Divine Name. A person should direct his heart to the blessings and not recite verses from Scripture at that time. See simanim 130 and 148.
