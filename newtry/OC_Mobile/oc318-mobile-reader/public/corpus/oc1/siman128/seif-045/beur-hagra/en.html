If he does not [forgive] etc. — as is written in Gittin 59b, according to Mar etc., which is not like Rambam in Sefer HaMitzvos who wrote that one may not forgive, for it is said "And you shall sanctify him" against his will — positive commandment 257; and in Maggid Mishnah in the name of Rabbi Peter he proved that one may forgive, from what is written in chapter 1 of Kiddushin that a Hebrew slave who is a kohen is not pierced, and they disagree whether his master may give him [his Canaanite bondwoman] — evidently that he may make use of her. And see Magen Avraham who proved from the implication of Rava, and Rami b. Chama who made use of Rav Chisda's [bondwoman]; and see Taz, and see Pnei Yehoshua there.
