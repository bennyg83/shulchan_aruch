Se'if 45: And the prompter. Because of confusion, as above in se'if 20.
It is forbidden, etc. Yerushalmi, Berachos chapter 8.
Unless, etc. As Gittin 59b says, 'according to the master,' etc. This is unlike Rambam in Sefer HaMitzvos, who says a kohen cannot waive it because 'you shall sanctify him' applies against his will. Hagahos Maimoniyos cites Rabbi Peter's proof that he may waive it: Kiddushin chapter 1 says a Hebrew kohen slave's ear is not pierced and disputes whether his master may give him, etc., proving service may be taken from him. See Magen Avraham's proof from Rava and Rami bar Chama using Rav Chisda, Taz, and Yefeh Mareh there.
