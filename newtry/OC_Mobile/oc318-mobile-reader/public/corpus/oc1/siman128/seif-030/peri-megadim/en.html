Like alefs. See Taz and Magen Avraham, letter 46. What he wrote that if he is familiar in his city it is permitted: there are two reasons. One is that he changes the blessing, such as saying ‘ya’ar’ and the like. The second is that people may be distracted and not listen, as the Levush wrote. According to the second reason, being familiar in his city helps, but not according to the first reason. This applies specifically to a resident of Haifa or Beit Shean in their places, where everyone speaks that way, as appears from Maharit, Even HaEzer, siman 16, cited by Magen Avraham, letter 46; with Hashem’s help it will be explained there. Peri Chadash wrote that it appears that all those who should not ascend the duchan, if they ascended, should not descend. See Magen Avraham, letter 10.
