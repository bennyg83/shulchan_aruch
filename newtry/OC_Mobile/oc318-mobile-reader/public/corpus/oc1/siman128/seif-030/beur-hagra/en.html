Seif 30: One who, etc., and the same applies, etc. Rambam, in accordance with the version in our texts of Megillah 24b: "It was taught," etc.
And likewise, etc. There, regarding one whose eyes run, according to the Rambam's interpretation.
Or, etc. According to Rashi's interpretation.
And likewise, etc. The Gemara there.
And even, etc. Beit Yosef there, for what practical difference would this make, since a person with a physical blemish is permitted, as stated in chapter 4 of Taanis, 27a.
And anyone, etc. Familiarity depends upon thirty days, as stated in the Yerushalmi concerning "He causes the wind to blow," and similar matters. The same appears in the final chapter of Bava Basra concerning the establishment of a presumption, and in many comparable cases. One might ask: If so, why does it say "in his city," which implies twelve months, as stated in Bava Basra 8a: "How long must one reside," etc.? This is not difficult. It may be answered that the ruling teaches that only residence in his city is effective, etc., because people are otherwise not accustomed to engage in dealings with him, as stated there. This is the meaning of "and even," etc., for in that circumstance people are accustomed to engage in dealings with him.
