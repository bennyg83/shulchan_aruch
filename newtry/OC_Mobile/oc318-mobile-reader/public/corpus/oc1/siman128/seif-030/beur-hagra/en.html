(1) Seif 130: "Who," etc., and "the same," etc. — Rambam; and as our books have Megillah 24b — a baraita, etc.

(2) And likewise, etc. — there, Zevachim; and per Rambam's explanation.

(3) Or, etc. — per Rashi's explanation.

(4) And likewise, etc. — gemara there.

(5) And even, etc. — Beit Yosef there: it is implied, since a baal mum is permitted; and as written in chapter 4 of Taanit 27a.

(6) And all, etc. — for habit depends on thirty days, as written in Yerushalmi regarding "Who returns the wind," and the like; and likewise in the gemara, last chapter of Bava Batra, regarding presumption and much similar; and if you ask — if so, why does it say "in his city" in Yevamot 110b, as written in Bava Batra 8a, "how much should be," etc. — no difficulty; one can say it comes to teach that specifically in his city it helps, etc., as written — because people are not accustomed to marry and give with him; and this is what he said: "and even," etc. — for in this they are accustomed to marry and give with him.
