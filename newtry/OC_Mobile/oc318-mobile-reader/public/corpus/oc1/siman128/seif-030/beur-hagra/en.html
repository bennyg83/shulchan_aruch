And likewise, etc. The Gemara there.
And even, etc. Beis Yosef: what difference can it make, since a blemished person is permitted, as Taanis chapter 4, 27a, states?
And anyone, etc. Familiarity is established in thirty days, as the Yerushalmi states for Mashiv HaRuach and as Bava Basra's final chapter states for presumptive status. Why then specify his city, which requires twelve months under Bava Basra 8a? It teaches that only in his city does familiarity help, because people ordinarily interact with him there. This is why it adds 'even,' etc.; in that case they do interact.
