38) There: Although the kohanim washed their hands in the morning, etc. The Egyptian custom is not to wash again at Shacharis but to wash at Mussaf because attention has meanwhile been diverted. Radbaz, part 1, siman 147; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham, se'if katan 6. Nevertheless, one who fears Hashem and delights in His commandments should wash again even at Shacharis, thereby satisfying Maran, those who agree with him, and the Zohar.
