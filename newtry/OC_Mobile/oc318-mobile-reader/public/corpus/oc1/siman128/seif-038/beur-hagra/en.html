Seif 38: If he drank, etc. All this is the language of the Rambam. It accords with Taanis 26b: "Why was it juxtaposed," etc., and "just as one who ministers," etc. It is also stated in Kerisos 12b: "If he drank," etc.; Rabbi Elazar says, etc. The ruling there, 13b, follows Rabbi Elazar.
And if, etc. There, the Gemara answers: "There is no difficulty; this case," etc. The Rambam holds that the same distinction applies to the first clause, concerning which he issued his ruling.
