Se'if 38: If he drank, etc. This is Rambam's language, based on Taanis 26b's comparison of Birkas Kohanim to Temple service. Kerisos 12b records Rabbi Elazar's view, and 13b rules like him.
And if, etc. There the Gemara distinguishes the cases. Rambam holds the same distinction applies to the opening clause, which he rules.
