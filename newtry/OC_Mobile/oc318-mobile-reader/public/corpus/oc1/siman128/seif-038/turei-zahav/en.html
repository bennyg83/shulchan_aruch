Even on the Shabbatot of the year, etc. — in some places the custom is that even on Yom Tov that falls on Shabbat they do not ascend to the duchan; and I know no reason or rationale why the sanctity of the day should be diminished because it has additional sanctity; and it is fitting to abolish this practice.
