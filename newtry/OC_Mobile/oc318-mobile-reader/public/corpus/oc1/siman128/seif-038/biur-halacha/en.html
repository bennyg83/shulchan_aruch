He drank a revi'it of wine. — See Beit Yosef what he brought in the name of Rabbeinu Yeruham, and see in Sefer HaEshkol in the laws of raising the hands, which he brought in the name of some of the rabbis, that raising the hands is forbidden only when drunk, and not from drinking a revi'it; see there. Nevertheless, there is a difference between this approach and Rabbeinu Yeruham's words, for there he is stringent, at least, regarding one who cannot speak before the king; and for Rabbeinu Yeruham, specifically when he reached Lot's drunkenness. Therefore we need Magen Avraham's explanation; see there. However, for the law, the later authorities agree that even with other beverages, anyone who cannot speak before the king is forbidden to raise his hands, as I wrote in Mishna Berurah.
