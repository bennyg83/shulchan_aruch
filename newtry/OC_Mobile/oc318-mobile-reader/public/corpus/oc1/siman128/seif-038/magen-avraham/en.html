If one drank a revi'it [of wine]. Ri wrote that a drunkard may not lift his hands — I hold only when he reached Lot's degree of drunkenness he may not lift his hands, end of his words. And the Rabbi Yosef ibn Lev disagreed — see there. And it appears to me that Ri's words are clear and correct, for in the Gemara we derive "bless" from nazir who is forbidden in wine — if so, other beverages are permitted as with nazir. And also the Rambam wrote six things prevent nesi'at kafayim… wine… if he drank a revi'it of wine… that even in avodah there is no death penalty in other beverages, as the Rambam wrote — if so, for nesi'at kafayim there is no prohibition at all in other beverages (not like Eliyah Rabbah, and he himself contradicts his words in se'if 90 — see there), unless he is drunk like Lot — then he is exempt from all mitzvot; and it implies that even if he cannot speak before the king he may lift his hands, for it does not apply to prayer. I did not understand what the Rabbi Yosef ibn Lev wrote that Ri compared him to prayer — it does not exist — see there. And it appears to me that if he drank wine from his vat he is disqualified, for with nazir it is forbidden and in avodah also forbidden — therefore the Rambam did not mention it here. And see siman 99 se'if 2 — likewise here.
