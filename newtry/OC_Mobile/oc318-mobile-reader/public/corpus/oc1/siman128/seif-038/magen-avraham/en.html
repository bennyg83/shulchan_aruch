Drank a revi'it. R' Yonah wrote that a drunkard — it appears specifically one who reached Lot's drunkenness — may not raise his hands; therefore the Rabbi disagreed with him — see there; and in my opinion R' Yonah's words are clear and correct, for behold in the Gemara we derive from "bless" from nazir who is forbidden in wine — if so other beverages are permitted as with a nazir; and Rambam wrote six things prevent nesiut kafim, etc., wine, etc., drank a revi'it of wine, etc. — that even for avodah there is no death in other beverages as Rambam wrote — if so for nesiut kafim there is no prohibition at all in other beverages (not as the Beit Yosef, and he himself contradicted his words in seif katan 90 — see there) unless he is drunk like Lot, then he is exempt from all mitzvot; and it implies that even if he cannot speak before the king he may raise his hands — he did not compare this to prayer, and I did not understand what the Rabbi wrote comparing R' Yonah to prayer where it does not apply — see there; and it appears to me if he drank wine from his cup he is disqualified, for in nazir it is forbidden and in avodah too forbidden — therefore Rambam did not mention it here; and siman 99 seif 2 is the same here.
