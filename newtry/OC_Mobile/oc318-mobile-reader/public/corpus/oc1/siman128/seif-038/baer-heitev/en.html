Wine. Taz and Eliyahu Rabbah ruled that even in other beverages — even though regarding Temple service one is liable to death only for wine, nevertheless regarding Birkat Kohanim it is surely forbidden, see there. And Magen Avraham disagrees and ruled: for Birkat Kohanim there is no prohibition at all in other beverages unless he is drunk like Lot — then he is exempt from all mitzvos; but if he has not reached Lot's drunkenness, even if he cannot speak before the king, he may lift his hands, see there; and see Levush Mordechai and Knesset HaGedolah in chapter 1 of Beiat HaMikdash. Leket Yosher, part 2, siman 125, wrote that it makes no difference whether within the meal or not within the meal, see there. If he drank wine from his press, he is disqualified — for in a nazir it is forbidden and in Temple service it is also forbidden — Magen Avraham; and see siman 99, seif 2, and the same here (and see in Eliyahu Rabbah who disagrees with Magen Avraham — for even if exempt and not lashed for it, etc.; and that which it is forbidden for a nazir is no proof, for it is no worse than a nazir who harvests grapes — see Yoreh De'ah, siman 242):
