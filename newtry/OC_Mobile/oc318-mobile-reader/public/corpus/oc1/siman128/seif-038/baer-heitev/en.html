(1) Wine. Taz and Beit Yosef ruled that even in other beverages, even though for avodah he is not liable for death except specifically for wine, nevertheless regarding nesiut kafim it is certainly forbidden — see there. Magen Avraham disagrees and ruled that for nesiut kafim there is no prohibition at all in other beverages unless he is drunk like Lot, in which case he is exempt from all mitzvot. But if he has not reached Lot's drunkenness, even if he cannot speak before the king he may raise his hands — see there. And see Levush Malkhut and Maggid Mishneh in chapter 1 of Bi'at Mikdash. Levush wrote vol. 2 siman 125 that there is no difference whether within the meal or not within the meal — see there. If he drank wine from his cup he is disqualified, for in nazir it is forbidden and in avodah too it is forbidden — Magen Avraham. And see siman 99 seif 2, and the same here (and see in Eliyahu Rabbah who disagrees with Magen Avraham: for even if he is exempt he is not lashed for it, and that which it is forbidden for a nazir is no proof that it is not worse than grape juice — see Yoreh De'ah siman 242):
