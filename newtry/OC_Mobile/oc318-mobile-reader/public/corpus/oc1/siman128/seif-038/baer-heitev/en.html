Wine. Taz and Olas Tamid rule that other intoxicating drinks are also forbidden. Although only wine incurs death regarding Temple service, other drinks are certainly forbidden for duchaning. Magen Avraham disagrees and rules that other drinks entail no prohibition for duchaning unless he is as drunk as Lot, in which case he is exempt from all mitzvos. If he has not reached Lot's intoxication, he may raise his hands even if he cannot speak before a king. See Lechem Mishneh and Kesef Mishneh, Hilchos Bi'as HaMikdash, chapter 1. Leket HaKemach, part 2, siman 125, writes that there is no distinction between drinking during a meal and outside it. One who drank fresh wine from the vat is disqualified, for it is forbidden to a nazir and also for Temple service. Magen Avraham. See siman 99:2; the same applies here. Eliyah Rabbah disagrees with Magen Avraham: Even if one is exempt and receives no lashes, the act may still be forbidden, and the prohibition to a nazir proves nothing, for grape pits are no better.
