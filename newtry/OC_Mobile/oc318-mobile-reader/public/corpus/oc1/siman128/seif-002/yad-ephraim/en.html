Taz, seif katan 31. 'With the other kohanim, and likewise,' etc. Meaning, the Tur holds that even on an occasional basis, a minor may not perform Bircas Kohanim alone, but only together with other kohanim. This is unlike the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch and Beit Yosef that once he has produced two pubic hairs, he may perform Bircas Kohanim occasionally even by himself.
