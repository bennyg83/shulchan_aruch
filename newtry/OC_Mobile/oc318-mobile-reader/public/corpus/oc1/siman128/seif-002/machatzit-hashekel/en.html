(seif katan 2) When they called etc. or told him before Retzeh etc. — therefore he transgresses for he must uproot at Retzeh. And so in Ran Megillah who wrote a kohen does not transgress unless they called him; if so likewise if called after Retzeh he does not transgress for calling after Retzeh is ineffective since he did not uproot at Retzeh he may not ascend — no less than one of the impediments — for even if called he does not transgress:
