(Seif 5) The Kohanim should not ascend to the duchan in shoes, etc. NB: see Magen Avraham, seif kaf-heh. Behold, what he wrote as proof from siman 694 is not proof at all, for there the main obligation is affliction and not pleasure; therefore shoes are forbidden because of pleasure; therefore in a garment that no longer exists, there is no pleasure. But here the main prohibition is because of concern lest a thread of one be severed. And what is written in Beit Yosef, this siman — and it is from Shas — if so, what difference to me whether leather or garment? And see in Beit Yosef, this siman — this law is proven explicitly, that there is no difference between leather or not; any concern lest a thread be severed, etc.; and especially since one view is brought, s.v. "dilkhu yachef l'garmi" — therefore even those who permit, it is only in market shoes that people walk all the way; but otherwise it is forbidden. And I saw in Sha'arei Teshuvah of the Gaon R' Zalman Margaliot, who wrote to look in responsa Knesset Yehezkel, siman 11; and I am not able to look into it; and in my humble opinion it is forbidden; and understand well.
