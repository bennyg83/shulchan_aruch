The kohanim. Specifically, [this applies] where he uprooted his feet [to ascend] during the [chazzan's] Avodah blessing, or where [someone] told him before "Retzeh" to ascend; but if he did not uproot his feet, he may not ascend, as is written in se'if 8 (Magen Avraham); and so [it is written] in Ginas Veradim, siman 13, and in the Responsa Zera Avraham, siman 14; and see subparagraph 11, what is written there.
