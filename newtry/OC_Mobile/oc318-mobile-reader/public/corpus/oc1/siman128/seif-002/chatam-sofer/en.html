(There) seif gimmel, a second blessing. NB: see responsa Chatam Sofer, Orach Chayyim, siman 22 [there he brought the law regarding twenty different prayers, such as Shacharit and Musaf on a weekday, where in Ashkenaz they bless with raising the hands at Shacharit, Musaf, and Ne'ilah, and one does not open his mouth]. And it is correct from law; and in that congregation, in one prayer, one may not raise the hands twice in any manner. However, in another congregation, in that prayer where he already raised his hands, if there are no Kohanim there, he should raise his hands and bless in order to discharge the congregation and bless them. And if there are Kohanim there without them, it seems to me he should not raise his hands and should not insert himself into a doubt of blessing; and it is possible that this is what R' Eliezer meant, who boasted that he did not raise his hands without a blessing. And see what is written on this in Tur Even HaEzer on Megillah, folio 27b, and in Maharsha, part 1, on Sotah, folio 39, and in Sefer Binyan Ariel. And according to what I wrote, it is satisfactory that Rashi meant with other Kohanim in another congregation, as explained above; and see below in the glosses in the passage that follows, in the name of responsa Ketav Sofer.
