(Ibid.) Seif katan 3, “he recites the blessing a second time.” Note: See Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 22. There he concludes as a matter of practical halachah that with two different prayers, such as Shacharis and Musaf, it is obvious, and the daily practice in Ashkenaz is, that the kohanim recite Bircas Kohanim at Shacharis, Musaf, and Ne’ilah, with no one objecting. This is correct according to the law. Within the same congregation and the same prayer, however, a kohen may under no circumstances recite Bircas Kohanim twice. Yet if he is in a different congregation during the same prayer in which he already recited Bircas Kohanim, then, if there are no kohanim there, he should raise his hands and recite the blessing in order to discharge the congregation’s obligation and bless them. If there are other kohanim there without him, it appears to me that he should not recite Bircas Kohanim, so that he does not enter into a possible doubt concerning an unnecessary blessing. Perhaps this is what Rabbi Elazar meant when he praised himself for never having recited Bircas Kohanim without a blessing. See what I wrote on this in Turei Even, Megillah 27b, in Maharsha, Chiddushei Aggados, Sotah 39b, and in Binyan Ariel. According to the above, the matter is clear, for he meant that he did so together with other kohanim in a different congregation, as explained above.
