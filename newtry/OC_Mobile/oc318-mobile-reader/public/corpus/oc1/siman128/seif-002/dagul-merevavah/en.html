The Beit Yosef questions the statement that the kohanim may not begin the next blessing until the congregation has finished answering Amen. Even without that rule, the kohanim may not begin until the reader has finished reciting the word to them. The Beit Yosef therefore explains that “the next blessing” refers to the prayer beginning “Master of the universe.” The Magen Avraham resolves his question, and consequently the ruling does not refer at all to the prayer beginning “Master.”
