Rabbi Yitzchak asked on what they said, that the Kohanim are not permitted to begin another blessing until Amen is finished from the mouth of the congregation; and behold, otherwise the Kohanim are not permitted to begin until the word is finished from the mouth of the mikra. And from this Rabbi Yitzchak explained: this blessing means Ribbon haOlamim; and Magen Avraham settled his difficulty, and automatically it does not refer at all to Ribbon.
