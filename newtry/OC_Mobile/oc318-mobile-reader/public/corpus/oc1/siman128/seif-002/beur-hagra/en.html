Se'if 2: Although, etc., he is, etc. See Rambam's Sefer HaMitzvos, principle 9: 'Know that even if you find,' etc.; and Yevamos 36b, chapter 3.
If, etc. The Yerushalmi says that when Rabbi Shimon ben Pazi's head hurt he stood behind a pillar; Rabbi Elazar went outside.
When he is called, etc. Otherwise he does not ascend. Sifrei, Naso, derives that the chazzan must say to them, 'Say to them,' meaning the call 'Kohanim,' as Sotah 38a and Tosafos, Berachos 34a, explain.
