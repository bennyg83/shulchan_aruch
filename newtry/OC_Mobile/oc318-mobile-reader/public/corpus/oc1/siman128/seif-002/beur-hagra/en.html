Siman 2, "even though he has forfeited one positive commandment, it is as if he has violated three," etc. Sefer HaMitzvot of the Rambam, root 9; and know that even if you find, etc.; and nevertheless, Yevamos 36b.

Siman 2, "if," etc. Yerushalmi, R' Shimon ben Pazi: When he would grasp his head, he would stand behind the pillar; R' Elazar would go outside.

Siman 2, "when they call 'Kohanim,'" etc. For without a caller it does not apply, as wrote in Sifrei, parashat Naso: "From whence [do we know] that the chazzan must say to them? Scripture says, 'Say to them'"—and this is the calling of Kohanim, as wrote in Sotah 38a, and as wrote Tosafos in Berachos 34a, s.v. "no," etc.
