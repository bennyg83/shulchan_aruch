Who has not prayed. It appears to me that if he sees that, should he ascend to the duchan, the time for prayer will pass, he should go outside the synagogue and pray. But if they told him to ascend, he must ascend, as written in se'if 20, for this is biblical, while prayer is rabbinic. So it also appears in Tosafos, Sotah, folio 38, in the name of the Yerushalmi. If he sees that the time for reciting Shema will pass, he should recite the first verse, as written at the end of siman 46.
