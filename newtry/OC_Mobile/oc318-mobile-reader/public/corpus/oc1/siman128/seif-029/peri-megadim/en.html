Istis and puah. See Taz. When he is not familiar in his city, his occupation must involve these dyes. If he is familiar, he is permitted even alone; see Beis Yosef and Shulchan Aruch. Olas Tamid and Peri Chadash write that one familiar in his city is permitted, although Peri Chadash there appears not to have had that reading. Shulchan Aruch, Taz, and Magen Avraham omit this point.
