Prevents him. If he sees that if he ascends the duchan the time for prayer will pass, he should leave the synagogue and pray. But if they told him, ‘Ascend,’ he must ascend, as written in seif katan 20, for this is according to the Torah and prayer is D’Rabbanan. If he sees that the time for Shema will pass, he should read the first verse; Magen Avraham. See also Be’er Heitev, seif katan 27, in what he wrote there in the name of Rabbi Klauzner, etc.; he did not examine this seif.
