[29] In the holy tongue etc. In Sotah daf 38 they learned from a gezeirah shavah of Mount Gerizim that requires the holy tongue, and standing from gezeirah shavah 'these shall stand to bless,' and in a loud voice as written 'say to them' like a person who says to his fellow; and Atteret Tzvi and Levush wrote other derivations — they did not see the source of the law in the Talmud — end. In my humble opinion Levush saw two proofs and also saw what Tur wrote in Sifrei and derived from them the verse 'so shall you bless'; and Beit Yosef wrote I do not know why our rabbi wrote in Sifrei derives — behold in our Talmud too it derives from 'my reading'; end. And Levush resolves that Tur says thus: in Sifrei it derives from the reading of 'so shall you bless' itself and not from another verse learned in our Talmud; and so it appears in Perishah on Beit Yosef's words — and perhaps in Sifrei it derives in the body of 'so shall you bless' that the Name spoke with Moshe in the holy tongue and standing and loud voice, and said to him 'so shall you bless' — only that nesiut kafim is difficult because it is not hinted in the word 'so'; end. In my humble opinion perhaps the Name showed him nesiut kafim and said 'so shall you bless' as Rashi explained regarding 'this month shall be for you,' and 'this they shall give' etc.; and so it seems to me in Rambam chapter 17 and not like Kesef Mishneh and Lechem Mishneh — see there and examine. Levush wrote on standing that above there is no sitting etc. — it is difficult to me for sitting means in the heavenly host but regarding God it is written 'I saw God sitting on a throne' and 'the Name the king sits'; and as we say in chapter Ein Dorshin regarding the account of Acher. Fine per Perishah's explanation he meant to say that at that time the Name stood and proof seems to me from Megillah daf 21 that says Torah standing — from where? as written 'and you stand here with Me' — as it were the Name too standing; but on Levush it is difficult; and perhaps his intent since there is no sitting above, if so perforce Moshe stood and said to him 'so shall you bless.' But difficult — behold it is written 'and I sat on the mountain' and in Megillah difficulties on sitting why behold there is no sitting above — rather perforce one must say the reason Moshe was below ten as in Sukkah daf 5; or one may say that flesh and blood differs so there is no error of divinity; or permission was given him for honor of Torah as we find in Metatron permission was given to sit and write Israel's merit; and such Adoni Abi HaRav explained in Midrash Ruth 'at that time Micha'el stood' — is there sitting above? rather he fell silent from advocating for Israel and meant since he fell silent he has no permission to sit and must stand. Tosafot daf 38 s.v. harei: it appears to R' Yehuda a kohen who blessed sitting did nothing — behold blessing is equated to avodah and one who sits profanes avodah — therefore here too he must return and bless standing — end. Knesset HaGedolah wrote the same for holy tongue and other matters are impediments; and Olat Tamid wrote nevertheless it seems good the kohen go outside when he cannot bless properly; and perhaps a congregation sitting facing the kohanim is nothing — specifically the kohen is warned — and so Magen Avraham wrote; and see above siman 23.
