(107) He lifts his hands — and if he sees that when he ascends the duchan the time for prayer will pass, he goes to the synagogue courtyard and prays there. But if they told him before Retzah to ascend, he must ascend — for if he does not ascend he transgresses a d'oraisa positive commandment and pushes aside d'rabbanan prayer. And if he has not recited Keriat Shema and sees that when he ascends the duchan the time for Keriat Shema will pass, he likewise goes to the synagogue courtyard and recites Keriat Shema. And if they told him before Retzah to ascend, he recites the first verse and ascends and lifts his hands [Magen Avraham and Sha'arei Ephraim].
