107) He recites the Priestly Blessing. If he sees that ascending will cause the prayer time to expire, he should go into the synagogue courtyard and pray there. If told before Retzeh to ascend, however, he must ascend: Otherwise he violates a Torah-level positive command, which overrides rabbinic prayer. If he has not recited Shema and ascending will cause its time to expire, he should likewise go to the courtyard and recite it. If told before Retzeh to ascend, he should recite the first verse and then ascend. Magen Avraham and other later authorities.
