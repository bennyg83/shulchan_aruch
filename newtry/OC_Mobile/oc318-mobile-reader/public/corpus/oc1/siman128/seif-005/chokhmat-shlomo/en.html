Seif 5. The kohanim should not ascend the duchan wearing shoes, etc. Note: See Magen Avraham, seif katan 5. What he wrote as a proof from siman 594 is no proof at all, for there the primary obligation is affliction and not pleasure, and therefore shoes were forbidden because of pleasure; with a garment that is not leather there is no pleasure. But here the primary prohibition is because perhaps one strap will break, and from what is written in Beis Yosef in this siman, which is from the Gemara, what difference is there between leather and cloth? See Beis Yosef in this siman, where this law is clearly proven, that there is no distinction between leather and non-leather wherever there is concern that a strap may break. Especially since one opinion is cited that they should walk entirely barefoot, therefore even those who permit do so only with stockings worn the entire foot; otherwise it is forbidden. I saw in Shaarei Teshuvah of the Gaon Rabbi Zalman Margolios, of blessed memory, that he wrote to see Responsa Knesses Yechezkel, siman 11, and I do not have it to examine. In my humble opinion it is forbidden; examine this well.
