(Seif 23) At the time the Kohanim bless the people, they should not look, etc., and they should not gaze at them. NB: see Magen Avraham, seif kaf-chet, what he wrote that the language "to gaze" implies more; and see in Semag in Choshen Mishpat, siman 254, seif kaf-daled, what he wrote to the contrary; and see in my novellae to Shas on tractate Avodah Zarah, folio 20a, in what is challenged there, "and to gaze — who permitted?" From there Magen Avraham's words are very surprising, for according to his words the Gemara does not challenge at all; and it is uncertain; and understand well.
