Rabban Yochanan ben Zakkai decreed that kohanim may not ascend the duchan wearing shoes, lest a strap break and a kohen sit to tie it while his colleagues ascend, leading people to say that he is disqualified. This concern does not apply to stockings with straps, because there is no disgrace in leaving them untied. The stringent view forbids leather stockings because they resemble shoes. If the stockings reach the leg and the leg and foot coverings are joined, have no strap, and do not resemble shoes, they are permitted.
Compare siman 554 regarding Tishah BeAv, where even footwear covered with leather is forbidden, because the prohibition there concerns comfort and protection. Here the concern is only that it may be confused with a shoe, and a covered leather item is not confused with one.
