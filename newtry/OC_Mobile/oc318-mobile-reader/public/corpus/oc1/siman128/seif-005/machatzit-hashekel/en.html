Seif katan 5, and there are, etc. And tied, etc. This is from the enactment of Rabban Yochanan ben Zakkai that they should not ascend wearing shoes, lest a strap of the shoe break and he tie it, and meanwhile his colleagues ascend the duchan and he does not ascend. But regarding stockings, even if they have straps to tie, Beis Yosef wrote that we are not concerned. Certainly with a shoe whose strap breaks, if he does not tie it there is disgrace, so we are concerned; but with stockings, even if the strap breaks, if he does not tie it there is no disgrace, so we are not concerned. The stringent opinion is because leather stockings may be confused with shoes, since they resemble shoes, as they too are leather. Therefore he wrote that if they reach the elbows, and are stockings and foot-coverings together, and have no strap, and do not resemble shoes, they are permitted.
