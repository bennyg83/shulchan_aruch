(Seif katan 5) And there are etc. tied etc. — this is from the enactment of Rabbenu Gershom that they not ascend in shoes lest the strap break from the shoe and he tie it while his fellows ascend the duchan and he not ascend; but in market boots even if they have straps to tie — Rabbenu Yonah wrote certainly a shoe whose strap broke — if he does not tie it there is disgrace — therefore they are concerned; but market boots even if the strap breaks if he does not tie there is no disgrace — therefore they are not concerned — see Rabbeinu Yonah there. And know some are stringent because market boots of leather may be confused with shoes since they too are leather — therefore he wrote if they reach the ankle and they are market boots and legs together and have no strap and are not similar to shoes — permitted.
