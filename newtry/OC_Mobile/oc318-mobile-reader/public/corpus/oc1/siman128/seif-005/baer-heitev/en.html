With shoes. He shall remove the shoes before netilas yadayim; and shoes that can be removed without touching with the hand, he may wash before removing the shoes — siman 4 se'if 18. They shall hide the shoes under the benches so they do not stand exposed in synagogue out of respect — see Taz. See in Knesset HaGedolah.

Leather. Specifically when they reach only to the knee and are tied with a strap; but if they reach to the wrist — i.e., they are trousers — it is permitted. It appears to me that of cloth covered with leather it is permitted in every case — Magen Avraham. Whether kohanim may ascend the duchan in cloth shoes called "pilutz" — Shach — see responsum Knesset Yechezkel siman 11.
