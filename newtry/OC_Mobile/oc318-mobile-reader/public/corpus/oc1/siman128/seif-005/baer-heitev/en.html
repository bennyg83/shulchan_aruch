With shoes. He should remove his shoes before the washing; and [regarding] shoes that can be removed without touching them by hand, he may wash before removing the shoes — see siman 4, se'if 18. And they should tuck the shoes away beneath the benches, so that they do not stand exposed in the synagogue, out of respect (Olas Tamid); and see the Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah.
Leather. Specifically when they reach only up to the knee and are tied with a strap; but if they reach up to the joints — for they are [then like] trousers — it is permitted. And it appears to me that [footwear] made of cloth and covered with leather is permitted in any case (Magen Avraham). As to whether it is permitted for the kohanim to ascend the duchan in shoes of felt, called "Filtz-Shukh," see the Responsa Knesses Yechezkel, siman 11.
