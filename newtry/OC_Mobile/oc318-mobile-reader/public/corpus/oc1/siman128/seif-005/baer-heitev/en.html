With shoes. He should remove his shoes before the washing; and [regarding] shoes that can be removed without touching them by hand, he may wash before removing the shoes — see siman 4, se'if 18. And they should tuck the shoes away beneath the benches, so that they do not stand exposed in the synagogue, out of respect (Olas Tamid); and see the Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah.
Leather. This applies specifically when they reach only to the knee and are tied with a strap. If they reach the upper legs, so that they are trousers, they are permitted. It appears to me that cloth footwear covered with leather is permitted in every case. Magen Avraham. Regarding whether kohanim may ascend the duchan wearing felt shoes called filz-shukh, see Responsa Knesses Yechezkel, siman 11.
