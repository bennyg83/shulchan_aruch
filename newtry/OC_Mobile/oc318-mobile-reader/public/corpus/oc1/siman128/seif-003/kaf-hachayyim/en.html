3) There: With fewer than ten, etc. The ten must be adult males, thirteen years old with two pubic hairs, free men specifically. This follows either because the Priestly Blessing is a sacred matter or from the verbal analogy of 'the children of Israel,' as Ran writes in Megillah. Even so, that exposition is an asmachta and the requirement is rabbinic. Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1. See above, siman 55, se'if 1, and our words there, os 6.
