There — and it is possible that Rabbi Yosi holds thus. He comes to answer the words of Rabbi Yosi Ba'al HaTosafot, which the Magen Avraham did not answer; rather Rabbi Yosi Ba'al HaTosafot holds that when we say in Ketuvot that he transgresses a positive commandment, we mean mentioning the name of Heaven in vain; and if so, Rabbi Yosi also concludes there in tractate Shabbat "if not because of a berachah le'vatalah." And on this the Magen Avraham ends — see siman 206 regarding berachah le'vatalah, that it is a negative commandment and not a positive commandment; and this answer is rejected. Thus it appears to me is the explanation of the Magen Avraham's words; and likewise he explained in Sefer Noda B'Yehudah siman 6 on the matter. And the other way — that he wished to explain there that the Magen Avraham reconciles in these words of his the words of the Rama — is very forced on the matter.
