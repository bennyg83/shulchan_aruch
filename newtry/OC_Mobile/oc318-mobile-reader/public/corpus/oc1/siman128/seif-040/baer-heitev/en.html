A divorcee. And the same applies to a chalutzah; and even if he cannot remove her due to mortal danger, and he vowed benefit from her from now — he does not lift his hands. And that which we say: one who vowed and a worker goes down and divorces — that is when he goes down from work and immediately divorces; but this one who cannot remove her, even if he vowed benefit from her, is forbidden to lift his hands — Ra'avan, part 1, siman 59. And Magen Avraham did not see these words of Ra'avan in their place, but only from what Knesset HaGedolah copied, see there; for had he seen Ra'avan in his place, he would not have challenged on his own in se'if katan 59 what Ra'avan himself sensed in this and reconciled, as written, see there. (Also an error fell in Magen Avraham's words in what he answers to the difficulty of Beit HaZahav, see there; and in the new Profes edition of Magen Avraham the error was corrected per the corrector, see there.)
