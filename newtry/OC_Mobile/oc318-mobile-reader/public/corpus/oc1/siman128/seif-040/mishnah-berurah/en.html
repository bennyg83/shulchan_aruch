(147) A kohen who married a divorcee, etc. — and likewise chalalah, zonah, or chalutzah. And what was explained nearby that other sins do not prevent nesi'at kafayim — that is only regarding sins for which other kohanim are not warned more than Yisrael; but in what kohanim are warned more than Yisrael, kehuna's sanctity caused him and he profaned it — therefore he is disqualified d'rabbanan for all kehuna matters until he vows before many…

(148) Before many — a vow taken before many has no release; but when he does not vow before many we fear lest he go to a sage and ask about his vow, for the evil inclination is strong regarding forbidden relations.

(149) Of the women forbidden to him — and he must also divorce her — so write the Acharonim [what the Mechaber did not mention this is because it applies also to one who divorced and she died, mentioned at the beginning]. And if he wishes to keep her some time without divorcing — even though he vowed from her benefit — he may not lift his hands and may not read first in the Torah.
