There — rather leniently that grape juice is permitted. Language of Shas there daf 26b: derives that a drunkard is forbidden in nesiut kafim, as we relied parashat nazir to parashat nesiut kafim to say: just as a nazir is forbidden in wine, so too a blessing kohen; and challenges: if from a nazir forbidden in grape juice, so too a blessing kohen; and answers: Scripture said "call to his servant and bless in his name" — just as a servant is permitted in grape juice, so too a blessing kohen; and challenges: if from a servant with a blemish no, so too a blessing kohen no; and answers: behold it is equated to a nazir; and what did you see to equate leniently — equate stringently; and answers: they are mere asmachta and from rabbinic law leniently.
