Mourning. All the more so an onen. If he ascended, he does not descend, whether he is a mourner or an onen. Peri Chadash.
From the synagogue. Even on Shabbos during the seven days of mourning; Knesset HaGedolah. Peri Chadash disagrees with him and wrote that he should fulfill the three positive commandments, perform Nesias Kapayim, and a good blessing will come upon him; see there. If they called him, according to all opinions he must ascend even on a weekday, for otherwise he violates a positive commandment. Peri Chadash wrote that an onen, even if they called him to ascend, should not ascend; see there.
His mother. The same applies to anyone who mourns thirty days for relatives. If they said to him, “Ascend,” he must ascend, as written. The same applies to one who buries his dead on the festival: he is forbidden in Nesias Kapayim, for he is forbidden in joy, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 341. If there are no kohanim in the synagogue except mourners within thirty days or within twelve months, they should ascend to the duchan. Responsa Knesset Yechezkel, siman 12, see there, not like Magen Avraham, s.k. 66.
