(157) He left the synagogue — by law the mourner is obligated to bless, for he is obligated in all mitzvot of the Torah; but the custom is not to lift hands because a kohen must be joyful and of good heart at the berachah, as written "and one of good heart will bless" — therefore he must leave so they not call him to ascend the duchan; and even on Shabbat if he is the only kohen he does not ascend. B'dieved if he did not leave and they called him to ascend — whether Shabbat or weekday, whether alone or with other kohanim — he must ascend, else he transgresses a positive commandment.

(158) Even during twelve months — even on Shabbat and Yom Tov when he is not called dwelling in joy, for he may not go to wedding feasts etc.; and likewise one who buried his dead on a festival — although mourning has not yet begun on him, nevertheless he is forbidden joy all festival days. And likewise an onen even on Yom Tov when obligated in all mitzvot — nevertheless he is not dwelling in joy; and we already wrote nevertheless if they call him to ascend he ascends. Some say regarding an onen even if called to ascend he does not ascend.

(159) For his father and mother — and likewise all thirty for other relatives he must mourn. If there are not two other kohanim in the synagogue besides the mourner, the mourner may lift hands within twelve months for father and mother or within thirty for other relatives.
