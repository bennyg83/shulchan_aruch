siman 43 — after [shiva] etc. Even within the seven [days of mourning] it is fitting that he is obligated in all the commandments stated in the Torah, as is written in chapter 1 and chapter 2 of Berachos and chapter 2 of Sukkah. And the reason for the hagahah is because one must serve with joy, as is written "A joyful heart will bless," and we say in Sotah there "Do not bless" etc. And likewise we say in the Zohar in several places that one must serve with joy. And the Beit Yosef wrote that one should not rely on this except within seven [days], and specifically one who left the beth midrash, for otherwise he transgresses a positive commandment as above, and he must go up [to the platform].
