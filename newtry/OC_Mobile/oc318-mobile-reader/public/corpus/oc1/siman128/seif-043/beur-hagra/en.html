Se'if 43: After, etc. Even during the seven days he is inherently eligible, since a mourner is obligated in all Torah mitzvos, as Berachos chapters 1–2 and Sukkah chapter 2 state. The gloss's reason is that blessing requires joy: 'one with a good eye will bless,' and Sotah expounds 'do not read it as will bless,' etc. Zohar likewise repeatedly requires joy. Beis Yosef says not to rely on this beyond the seven days; during them he should leave the synagogue, because otherwise he violates the positive command and must ascend.
