He should not raise his hands, but in that case he need not leave the synagogue, since no one calls “Kohanim.” The person who washes the kohanim’s hands should likewise not approach him, and he then does not violate the positive commandment, as explained.
The prayer beginning “Master of the universe” is recited while the kohanim prolong the words with their melody. Some write that not every person should recite this prayer when the kohanim ascend the platform. Rather, only one who dreamed a bad dream that night should recite it. Even if he dreamed on another night during the year, the annulment is effective only on the very day of the dream, just as a fast for a dream is effective specifically on that day. Alternatively, he should omit the two words “I dreamed a dream,” and say instead: “Master of the universe, I am Yours and my dreams are Yours; may it be Your will,” etc. This is from Rosh, chapter Shor Shenagach Es HaParah. This appears correct to me. It is also effective when others dreamed a bad dream concerning him, because he annuls it by saying, “whether dreams that I dreamed or that others dreamed concerning me.” Otherwise, it is improper to recite gratuitous supplications on Shabbos or Yom Tov, because one may not deliberately arouse himself with matters of supplication that break a person’s heart. Prayer and request, however, also include asking Hashem to grant a person abundant gifts beyond his essential needs, and such requests may be made even on Shabbos and Yom Tov. This is why those who arranged the order of the Maamados included additional requests for good on Shabbos. The Levush, authored by Maharam Yaffe, writes, based on Rashi in parashas Va’eschanan, that the Zohar in parashas Naso states as follows: When the chazzan repeats Shemoneh Esrei and there is no Bircas Kohanim, upon saying “May Hashem bless you,” he should face the aron; upon saying “and protect you,” he should turn toward his right. Upon saying “May Hashem illuminate,” he should face the aron; upon saying “His countenance toward you and favor you,” he should turn toward his left, thereby joining it to the right. It is forbidden to delay between washing and the blessing for the time required to walk twenty two amos. The kohanim must therefore wash close to Retzei.
