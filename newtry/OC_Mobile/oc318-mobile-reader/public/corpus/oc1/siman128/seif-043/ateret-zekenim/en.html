He left the house of study — and the same if there is no kohen but he: he also shall not raise his hands, only he need not then leave the house of study when they do not call kohanim and also one who washes hands shall not come to him — and then he does not violate a positive commandment as explained. And they say Ribon when they lengthen the melody of the words; and some wrote that not everyone should say the aforementioned Ribon when kohanim ascend the duchan, rather specifically one who dreamed that night a bad dream — then he says Ribon, for even if he dreamed on other days of the year the nullification does not help except on that day when he saw the dream as we find in Taanit regarding a dream specifically on that day; or he should skip the two words "dream I dreamed" and say only Ribono shel olam I am Yours and my dreams are Yours — Maharam etc. (Rosh chapter Shor SheNagach HaParah); and so it seems right to me that this helps if others saw a bad dream about him that they nullify it by saying among dreams others dreamed about me as above — for otherwise it is not right to say supplications on Shabbat and Yom Tov in vain, for it is forbidden to arouse oneself in matters of supplications that break a person's heart; but prayer and request also apply to things one prays that He give him gifts more than his need — and this is permitted to pray even on Shabbat and Yom Tov; this was the reason of the orderers of Seder Ma'amadot who arranged on Shabbat requests more for good. Levush that Mahari Mintz composed on Rashi in Parashat Vaetchanan wrote in Zohar Parashat Naso: when the prayer leader repeats the eighteen blessings, if there is no nesiut kafim — when he says "God bless you" he should look toward the side of the Sanctuary, and "and guard you" toward his right side; "God shine" toward the Sanctuary, "His face toward you" and "be gracious to you" he should look toward his left to join them in the right; and it is forbidden to wait the time of walking twenty-two cubits between washing and blessing — therefore he must wash close to Retzeh.
