54) They say Ribono. On Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, when the day's melodies such as Hayom Te'amtzeinu are prolonged, they should not begin Ribono until near the end, so they finish together with the congregation.
55) What You promised us. That You should assent to our blessing, as the verse says, 'And I will bless them.'
56) So the congregation answers Amen. It is logical that the kohanim too must answer Amen to the prayer leader's blessing. They should say Adir BaMarom and time themselves to finish together with the prayer leader.
