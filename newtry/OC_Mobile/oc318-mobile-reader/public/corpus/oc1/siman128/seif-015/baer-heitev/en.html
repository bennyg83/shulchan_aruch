Ribbono. On Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, when the melodies are prolonged, the kohanim should not begin Ribbono shel Olam until the end, so that they finish together with the congregation. Magen Avraham.
Both. See Responsa Devar Shmuel, end of siman 295, which explains the custom of some to say Baruch Hu uvaruch Shemo when the kohanim and the prompter mention the Divine Name during Birchas Kohanim. Peri Chadash writes simply that one is obligated to say Baruch Hu uvaruch Shemo whenever the Name is mentioned, even when it is merely a mention of the Name, and brings several proofs. See there.
