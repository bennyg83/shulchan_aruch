The Kohanim stand on the platform with their faces toward the Ark and their backs toward the people, and their fingers bent into their palms, until the prayer leader finishes Modim. Then, if there are 2 Kohanim, the prayer leader calls out to them, 'Kohanim.' [Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah and Rambam] {Rama: He should not say 'Our God and God of our fathers,' etc. Some say that he recites it quietly until the word 'Kohanim,' and then says that word aloud. [Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah and Maharam of Rothenburg] He then returns and says 'Your holy people, as stated' quietly, and this is the custom in these lands.} They turn their faces toward the people. If there is only 1 Kohen, the prayer leader does not call him; rather, the Kohen turns his face on his own.
