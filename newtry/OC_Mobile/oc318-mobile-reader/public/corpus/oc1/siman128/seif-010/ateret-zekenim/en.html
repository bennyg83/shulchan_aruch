If they are two, the prayer leader calls "Kohanim"; but if there was only one priest, the prayer leader does not call "Kohanim" — rather he turns his face by himself, even if a minor priest is with him; and see what Mahari wrote. And there are those who say that the custom is to call even for a single one (Beit Yosef in the name of Semak and Semag). And some whisper to him until the word "Kohanim"; and so too Rashi and the Rambam, and thus they practiced — not as the Ran wrote in the chapter "HaKore," that before they uproot their feet he calls them "Kohanim" and they bless "Who has sanctified us with the holiness of Aaron" and "Who commanded us to bless His people Israel with love." And what he wrote "and commanded us to bless with love" — in what place do we find that the Holy One, blessed be He, commanded to bless Israel with love? And it appears, since Rashi explained according to "Say to them" fully: you shall not bless them in haste or while walking, but with intention and with a whole heart; examine this.
