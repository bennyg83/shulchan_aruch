Siman 10, "they stand," etc. Rashi there and Tosafos, s.v. "for," that they do not stand behind them to sanctify except at the time of Ne'ilah because of the congregation's awe, as wrote there in Megillah 1a, and as wrote in 39b, "the Kohanim are not permitted to turn back," etc.; and there 1a, "for he turns back," etc.

Siman 10, "until," etc., "and then," etc. there and there.

Siman 10, "the prayer leader." Rambam, not like Tosafos in Berachos 34a, s.v. "no," etc., who explained that the chazzan who is occupied with the community's needs is the caller, as wrote in the above-mentioned Sifrei, "chazzan," etc.; and furthermore from what is written in Sotah 39b, "from the mouth of the caller," and afterward it says that the prayer leader begins, etc.; from this it follows that the caller is not the prayer leader.
