Se'if 10: They stand, etc. Rashi and Tosafos explain that kohanim do not keep their backs toward the Sanctuary except during Birkas Kohanim, because of the congregation's awe, as Sotah 40a states. See also 39b, that they may not turn back, and 39a, 'when he turns,' etc.
Until, etc.; and then, etc. As stated in those passages.
The shaliach tzibbur. Rambam, unlike Tosafos, Berachos 34a, who identifies the chazzan attending communal needs as the prompter, based on Sifrei. Sotah 39b first says 'from the prompter's mouth' and then that the shaliach tzibbur begins, proving the prompter is not the shaliach tzibbur.
