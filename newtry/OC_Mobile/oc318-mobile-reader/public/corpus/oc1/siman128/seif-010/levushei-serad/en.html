There — "in sheva before 'thus shall you bless'" — for now that it is written "say to them" after "thus shall you bless," it was necessary to write with a kamatz that it should be past tense; and it would have been better to write "say to them" before "thus shall you bless" and it would be in sheva, as is the way of verses everywhere. And it is also known that reading of the kohanim is necessary because "say to them" is redundant, since it is already written "to Aaron and to his sons"; and in Peri Megadim he emended gratuitously in the Taz.
