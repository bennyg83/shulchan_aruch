And the Leviim pour, etc. When there is no Levi to pour, a firstborn who opened the womb, who also has some sanctity, enters in place of the Leviim; Bach. Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah wrote that if there is also no firstborn, it is better that the kohen wash himself than that an Israelite pour over his hands. If the Levi is a talmid chacham and the kohanim are amei ha’aretz, some say there is no guilt on the Levi if he does not wish to pour, and some say that nevertheless he is obligated to pour. It is proper to be stringent, for even according to the first view he only said there is no guilt, but initially it is good to be stringent. If there is one kohen who is a talmid chacham, according to all opinions he is obligated to pour even for an am ha’aretz.
