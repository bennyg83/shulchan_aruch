The Sanctuary. Not to say that for Birkat Kohanim we require a place where there is a Torah scroll, but wherever ten pray there is Birkat Kohanim — see above, se'if katan 1. If the Sanctuary is fixed to the north or south side, the priests do not turn their faces except toward the east, as Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Rabbeinu Shimshon Chakham — see above, siman 94, se'if katan 4; and see in Knesset HaGedolah on this siman.

Priests. One older and one younger than thirteen years — he does not call them "Kohanim" (Mahari Weil, part 1, siman 64); and Knesset HaGedolah agreed. But Peri Chadash wrote: since he is a minor, it is a matter of education — it is properly applicable to call them "Kohanim." Two priests who hate one another, or who have vowed benefit from one another, may ascend together to bless Birkat Kohanim; and one cannot say to his fellow "You ascend at Shacharit and I at Musaf," or the reverse, for he can say "I wish to bless in both of them" — Maharam Mintz, siman 12.
