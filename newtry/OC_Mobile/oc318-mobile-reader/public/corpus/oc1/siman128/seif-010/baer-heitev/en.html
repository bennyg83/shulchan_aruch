The heichal. This does not mean that duchaning requires a place containing a Sefer Torah. Wherever ten people pray, Birchas Kohanim is recited; see se'if katan 1. If the heichal is fixed on the north or south side, the kohanim turn their faces only toward the east. Knesses HaGedolah in the name of Rabbi Shlomo Chakan. See siman 94, se'if katan 4, and Knesses HaGedolah on this siman.
Kohanim. When one is an adult and the other is under thirteen, the chazzan does not call 'Kohanim.' Mabit, part 1, siman 64; Knesses HaGedolah agrees. Peri Chadash writes that since the minor is subject to education, it is entirely appropriate to call them kohanim. Two kohanim who hate one another or are prohibited by vow from benefiting from each other may ascend together to recite Birchas Kohanim. Neither can tell the other, 'You ascend at Shacharis and I at Mussaf,' or the reverse, because the other may say, 'I wish to bless at both.' Maharam Mintz, siman 12.
Quietly. This refers only to the chazzan, not the congregation.
Calls. Nevertheless, the lone kohen recites the blessing even though he is not obligated to ascend, as stated in se'if 2. Maharam Mintz, siman 12, se'if 1; Peri Chadash agrees.
