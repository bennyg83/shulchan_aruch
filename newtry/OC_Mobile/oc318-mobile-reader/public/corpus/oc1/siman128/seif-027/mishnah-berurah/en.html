104) A kohen may not, etc. The Gemara says he should not reason: Since the Torah authorized me to bless, I will add a blessing of my own, such as, 'May Hashem, the God of your fathers, increase you a thousandfold, etc.' The Torah therefore says, 'You shall not add.' The Gemara concludes that even after completing all three blessings he violates the negative command; all the more so if he adds within them. Only adding another verse is forbidden. Repeating the Priestly Blessing several times does not violate bal tosif, because performing a mitzvah twice is not considered adding to it. Later authorities.
105) If he added, etc. Likewise, if he subtracts from the blessings, he violates bal tigra, the prohibition against subtracting.
