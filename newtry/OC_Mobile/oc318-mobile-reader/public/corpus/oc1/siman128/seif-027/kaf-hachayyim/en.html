27) [Se'if 5] The kohanim may not ascend the platform wearing shoes. This is one of Rabban Yochanan ben Zakkai's nine enactments, as stated in Rosh Hashanah 31b and Sotah 40a. Sotah explains the reason: A sandal strap might break, causing him to sit and tie it while his colleagues bless, and observers may say he is the son of a divorcee or chalutzah. Beis Yosef; Levush; Olas Tamid, os 9. Beis Yosef writes that even shoes and sandals without straps are forbidden because the Sages made no distinction and enacted a general prohibition covering every shoe and sandal. So write Levush; Olas Tamid there; Eliyah Rabbah, os 6; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 6; and Chayei Adam 32:10.
