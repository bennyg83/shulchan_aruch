In siman 167 it is ruled that the one breaking bread waits until the majority have finished answering Amen, but need not wait for those who prolong it excessively.
There the one breaking bread waits because Amen is part of the blessing, but those who prolong it improperly may be disregarded. Here the kohanim wait so that the congregation will hear the blessing. They must therefore wait even for those who prolong Amen improperly, for otherwise those people will not hear it.
