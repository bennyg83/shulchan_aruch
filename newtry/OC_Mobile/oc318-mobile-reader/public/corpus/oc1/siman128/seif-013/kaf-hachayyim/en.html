13) There: Although he has neglected one positive mitzvah, etc. This is Rambam's language at the end of Hilchos Tefillah. Sotah 38b, however, says in Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi's name that every kohen who does not ascend violates three positive commands: 'So shall you bless,' 'Say to them,' and 'They shall place My Name.' Why does Rambam say he is 'like one who violates three positive commands' rather than that he actually violates them, as the Gemara says? Maran explains in Kesef Mishneh there and Beis Yosef here that only 'So shall you bless' is phrased as a command. Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi's word 'violates' is therefore not exact; he is like one who violates three commands. So write Levush, Soles Belulah in Mikdash Me'at, os 1, and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 3. See Mishbetzos Zahav at the end of the siman: A kohen who refuses to ascend is compelled until his life is at risk; if the prescribed time has passed, he receives lashes for rebellion. Know also that when possible he must ascend specifically onto the platform, not remain on the floor, because ascent is mentioned in the Gemara as explained above. This also follows from the poskim's unqualified language and should not be changed. See below, os 120.
