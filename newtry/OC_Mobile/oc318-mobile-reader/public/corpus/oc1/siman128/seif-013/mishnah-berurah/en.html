47) The kohanim begin, etc. When there is only one kohen, to whom 'Kohanim' is not called, all agree that the prayer leader prompts him with the word Yevarechecha.
48) To say Yevarechecha. This view holds that the rule requiring each word to be prompted excludes the first word, about which no mistake is likely.
49) Afterward he prompts them. The verse says, 'Say to them,' which the Sages interpret to mean that the cantor tells them what to say. Nevertheless, prompting is only an ideal requirement and does not invalidate the blessing, as proven below in se'if 25 from a synagogue consisting entirely of kohanim. Peri Chadash. He further writes that the prayer leader must prompt from a siddur rather than by heart.
