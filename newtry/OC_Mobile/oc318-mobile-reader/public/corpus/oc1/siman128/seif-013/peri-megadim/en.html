They are not. See Taz. He follows his view in letter 12, like the Perishah and the first answer of Tosafot there, that the prompter may not read the word ‘Kohanim’ until Amen has ended from the mouth of the entire congregation, and certainly for the word ‘Yevarechecha’ the shaliach tzibbur must wait for the entire congregation. The Levush wrote that the prompter does not say ‘Yevarechecha’ until Amen has ended from the mouth of the congregation, like the Rama in seif 13, but omitted the word ‘all,’ like Magen Avraham, letter 28, since the prompter need not wait for the minority in this. Regarding the Rama’s gloss, the Levush corrected that the beginning of the Taz, ‘Sim Shalom,’ should be until the congregation answers Amen, and then the kohanim begin ‘Ribon,’ when the shaliach tzibbur begins Sim Shalom. But the Taz explained that for Sim Shalom the shaliach tzibbur need not wait for the minority, as written in siman 124, seif 9. He holds that here too one must wait until it is from the mouth of the entire congregation, for the kohanim cannot say ‘we have done,’ etc., since they bless even the minority, even one person. As long as the minority has not finished Amen, they have not yet done it. Magen Avraham disagrees entirely in letter 28; see there.
