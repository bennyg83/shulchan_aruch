The kohanim begin by saying, 'May Hashem bless you.' This is the practice throughout Eretz Yisrael and Egypt, where the kohanim begin on their own with 'May Hashem bless you,' because there is no possibility of error in the first word, as the Beit Yosef cites from the Rambam, chapter 14 of Hilchos Tefillah. Mahararam, however, in responsum 3, ruled as stated in the gloss in the name of the Tur, Ran, and Hagahos Maimoniyos. The chazzan then begins Sim Shalom. Some write that the congregation and the chazzan recite Sim Shalom quietly until 'and life and peace,' and the chazzan then begins aloud, 'and may it be good in Your eyes,' etc., as stated by Mahararm MiLublin.
