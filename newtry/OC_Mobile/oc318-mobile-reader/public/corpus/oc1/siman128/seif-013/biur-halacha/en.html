And afterward the chazzan recites [the verses] for them, etc. — See in Mishna Berurah what is written in the name of Peri Chadash, who brought proof from below, seif 20, that the recitation is not indispensable; although in truth there is no proof from there, as Nahar Shalom rejected it — for there it deals with a case where the chazzan is the reciter, and that which it says "they all go up to the platform" means except for the chazzan; and Peri Megadim likewise explained there [and Peri Chadash follows his approach, that he holds in seif kaf that not like Hagah Maimoni, but rather if he is assured of returning to his prayer, even when there are other kohanim, he is permitted to perform nesi'at kapayim; and that which it says "they all" deals with one who is assured; and from this he infers his ruling that the recitation is not indispensable; but according to what the Shulchan Aruch ruled in seif 20, therefore "they all" is not precise]. Nevertheless, in the essential law it is possible that his ruling is a true ruling.
