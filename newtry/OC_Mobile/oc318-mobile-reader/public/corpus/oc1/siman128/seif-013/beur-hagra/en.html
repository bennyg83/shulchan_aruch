Se'if 13: They begin, etc. From Sotah 39b: the kohanim may not begin until the prompter finishes. This means Yevarechecha begins only after the call 'Kohanim' ends, because the blessing preceding Birkas Kohanim is not fundamentally required, as 39a's 'I did not raise,' etc., shows; otherwise that statement has no novelty.
Some say, etc. They interpret the rule as referring to the blessing 'Who sanctified us,' etc.
And afterward, etc. As the Midrash says, cited by Tosafos in Berachos. And then, etc. The Mishnah and Gemara, Sotah 40b and 39b.
