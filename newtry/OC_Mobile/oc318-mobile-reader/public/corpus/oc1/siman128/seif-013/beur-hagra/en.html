Siman 13, "they begin," etc. From what is wrote there 39b, "and the Kohanim," etc., "from the mouth of the caller," and R' Levi [said] "Y'varekhekha" until the caller finishes "Kohanim"—for the blessing is not from the essence of the law, as wrote there 1a, "I did not carry," etc.; and if so, what is its advantage?

Siman 13, "and some say," etc. Those who explain the blessing "Who has sanctified us," etc., it refers [to this].

Siman 13, "and afterward," etc. As wrote in the Midrash, and Tosafos brought it in Berachos there. "And then," etc.—Mishnah and Gemara, Megillah 2b there, and there 39b.
