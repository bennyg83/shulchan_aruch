The Kohanim begin by saying 'Yevarechecha.' {Rama: Some say that the prayer leader should first call out even the word 'Yevarechecha' to them. [Tur, Ran in the chapter HaKoreh, and Hagahot Maimoniyot] This is the custom in all these lands.} Afterward, the prayer leader reads the blessing to them word by word, and they repeat each word after him until they finish the first verse, at which point the congregation answers Amen. The same procedure is followed after the second verse and after the third verse.
