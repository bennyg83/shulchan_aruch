63) To call 'Kohanim' until, etc. This follows the view explained above in se'if 10: Where the Priestly Blessing is recited, the prayer leader does not say 'Our God and God of our fathers, bless us, etc.,' but calls 'Kohanim' immediately after Modim. Under our custom, where the prayer leader always says 'Our God and God of our fathers,' as Rama writes there, this timing law is irrelevant, because that recitation itself creates a much longer pause.
64) From the congregation, etc. This means the majority of the congregation.
65) From the entire congregation. The people are obligated to hear the complete blessing from the kohanim. The Mechaber therefore carefully writes 'the entire congregation': Even if a few people mistakenly prolong their Amen excessively, the kohanim must wait for them too, because everyone is obligated to hear. This follows the Mechaber's ruling above in se'if 13 that the cantor does not prompt the word Yevarechecha, so the kohanim must be cautioned before beginning it. Under our practice, where the cantor also prompts Yevarechecha, the caution applies to him: He must not begin prompting that word before at least the majority has finished Amen. Once the cantor finishes the word, the kohanim may begin Yevarechecha.
66) The Amen answered after the blessing, etc. Likewise, the prompter may not begin Yaer or Yisa until the congregation's Amen has concluded.
67) Until the word concludes, etc. This allows the congregation to hear the word clearly from the kohanim without the prompter's voice mingling with it.
68) From the prompter. It appears that likewise the prompter may not begin the next word until the kohanim finish the preceding one.
69) They answer Amen. This means after each of the three blessings. Later authorities.
70) Until it concludes. The later authorities question this gloss because its case cannot occur. The kohanim do not begin Ribono Shel Olam until they turn their faces, and they do not turn until after the prayer leader begins Sim Shalom, as stated above in se'if 15. By then, the congregation has already answered Amen.
