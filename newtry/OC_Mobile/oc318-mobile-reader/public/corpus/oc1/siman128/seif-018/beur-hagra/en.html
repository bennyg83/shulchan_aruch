Se'if 18: The kohanim may not, etc. See above, se'if 13.
And after, etc. Sotah says not to begin another blessing, etc. Beis Yosef explains this as Yevarechecha according to Rambam. Under those disputing se'if 13, it must refer to Ribbon HaOlamim, as the gloss says, which is strained. He also answers that the Gemara reflects their custom without prompting; nowadays one waits until the prompter finishes the word. This is the meaning of 'and likewise,' etc.
From the mouth of the entire congregation. See Tosafos there, in the comment beginning 'Until, etc.,' for the source and discussion.

And not, etc. See Magen Avraham. The correct ruling is Shulchan Aruch's, as above; see se'if 13.
