Siman 18, "and the Kohanim are not permitted," etc. See above seif 13.

Siman 18, "and after," etc. ibid., "and they are not permitted," etc., in another blessing, etc.; and Beit Yosef explained that this means "Y'varekhekha" per the Rambam, but per those who disagree in seif 13 it must refer to "Master of the Universe," and as wrote in the gloss, and not, etc.; and this is forced; and furthermore the answer is that in the Gemara it speaks per their custom that they would not call out [word by word], but now [we wait] until the word is finished from the caller; and this is what he wrote, "and similarly," etc.

Siman 18, "from the mouth of the entire congregation." See Tosafos there, s.v. "until," etc.

Siman 18, "and similarly they are not permitted," etc. Gemara there.

Siman 18, "and the Kohanim should not begin," etc. Magen Avraham, and the correct [view] is as wrote in the Shulchan Aruch as above, and see above seif 13.
