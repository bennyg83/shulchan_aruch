From the mouth of the entire congregation. This is not comparable to what is written in siman 167, se'if 16, that one need not wait for those who prolong excessively, for the priestly blessing is different, since we require that all hear it; therefore one must wait (Tosafos).
Until Amen is completed. The Bach challenged this: are they not forbidden to begin until the chazzan begins "Sim Shalom," as written in se'if 15? And one cannot say that if some prolong Amen and the chazzan has begun "Sim Shalom," even so the kohanim must wait for them, for the opposite is more reasonable: the chazzan must wait for them so that they hear the blessing, as written in siman 124, se'if 9, while the kohanim need not wait, for what concern is it of theirs if some prolong? It appears to me to resolve the difficulty of the Beis Yosef as follows: if there are members of the congregation who prolong Amen, the one prompting may prompt, since he need not hear the response; but the kohanim must wait until Amen is finished from everyone's mouth, as written above. So it appears to me, and so it also seems from what he wrote above, "the one prompting may not," etc., "until it is finished from the mouth of the congregation," etc.; he did not write "the entire congregation" as he wrote at the end.
