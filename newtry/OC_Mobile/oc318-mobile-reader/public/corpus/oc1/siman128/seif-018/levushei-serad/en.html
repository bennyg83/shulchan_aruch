Se'if kaf yud alef — "specifically in their days." His general intent: among those who hold they should say when they set out — meaning at the time of their walking to the duchan — and among those who hold they should say when they reach the duchan, all hold they should say Modim first, as the Magen Avraham wrote in se'if 17 (and this is what the Magen Avraham concluded, and see above) — in order that the congregation answer Amen to both; but in their days they said Modim while walking; therefore they could properly say the prayer after Modim either when setting out or when reaching the duchan — not so nowadays: he should not say even when reaching the duchan, since they have not yet said Modim.
