The kohanim should not begin, 'Master of the worlds,' and this wording is an error. Above, in seif 15, he wrote that after the chazzan begins Sim Shalom, the kohanim turn their faces and say, 'Master of the worlds,' etc. It follows that even after the congregation has finished answering Amen, they do not yet begin, 'Master of the worlds.' Mahararm MiLublin wrote as follows: The chazzan does not begin Sim Shalom until the congregation has finished answering Amen, and meanwhile the kohanim begin, 'Master of the worlds.' This may be reconciled based on what I wrote above in seif 15, that both the congregation and the chazzan say Sim Shalom quietly. They therefore do not know when to begin, 'Master of the worlds,' and it must consequently be stated that they begin after the congregation has finished answering Amen. Consider this carefully.
