And the priests shall not begin "Master" — and this is an error, for above, seif 15, it is written that after the prayer leader begins "Grant peace," the priests turn their faces and say "Master of the worlds," etc.; thus even after Amen finishes from the mouth of the congregation they do not begin "Master." And Mahari wrote in this wording: and the prayer leader does not begin "Grant peace" until Amen finishes from the mouth of the congregation; and in the interim the priests begin "Master." And it is possible to reconcile this — since I wrote above in seif 15 that the congregation and also the prayer leader say "Grant peace" in a whisper; if so, they do not know when to begin "Master"; therefore it must be said "until Amen finishes from the mouth of the congregation"; examine this.
