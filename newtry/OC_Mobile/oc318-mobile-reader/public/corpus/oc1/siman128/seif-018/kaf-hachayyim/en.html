18) There: Or if they told him to ascend, etc. This applies only if he moved his feet during the blessing of Avodah, or if before Retzeh they told him to ascend. If he did not move his feet, he may not ascend, as stated in se'if 8 and Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2. That is, if he moved during Avodah and was told to ascend but did not, or if before Retzeh they told him to ascend so that he could move during Retzeh, he violates by failing to ascend. If he did not move during Retzeh and was told afterward, he does not violate, because se'if 8 says he may no longer ascend. So Magen Avraham, se'if katan 30; Maamar Mordechai, os 4; Machatzis HaShekel, se'if katan 2; and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 3. Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 12, however, questions whether the rule that one who did not move may not ascend applies only when acting voluntarily, while one explicitly told to ascend must do so. Eliyah Rabbah, os 16, and Eshel Avraham, os 2, cite him. It therefore appears that one who did not move his feet during Retzeh should leave the synagogue, both so no one tells him to ascend and so he does not hear the cantor call 'kohanim' and enter a doubtful obligation, and also so he will not appear disqualified. See below, os 22.
