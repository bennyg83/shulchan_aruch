21) Rabbi Zera Avraham Orach Chayyim siman 12 wrote that a Jerusalem kohen may bless Birkat Kohanim in Musaf on the second day of Yom Tov for diaspora people if they lack a kohen — even though for himself it is not time for avodah, since avodah for him is nothing because he is entirely chol; nevertheless since per law he may bless Israel any hour he wishes, he may bless the diaspora as one who blesses the congregation several times; likewise R' Ben Habib, whose words are brought at length in Goren Orach vol. 1 siman 13, concludes that if they have a kohen from abroad the Jerusalem priests do not ascend with him, and if they lack a kohen a Jerusalem kohen ascends; but Mahara HaLevi there siman 14 expanded and concluded with the rule that this matter has no conclusive proof to resolve the doubt and who would dare act and enter doubt of blessings; to appease Heaven since it issued from rabbis' mouths it is appropriate they bless Birkat Kohanim but not with the explicit Name — see there; Beit Din and Shatz daf 46b–47a and main points siman 5 note 7; also see the correcting rabbi in Shatz daf 235d and 236a who wrote if he can avoid it is better; if other kohanim he ascends with them and blesses without name and malchut; if only he is there he ascends and blesses per custom per Maharam ben Habib and Mahara HaLevi — see there; but to me, a Jerusalem kohen who went abroad intending to return and prays Musaf on the second day of Yom Tov, or prays in Jerusalem with soldiers who come to visit — if diaspora kohanim are with him he may ascend with them and bless without name and malchut since with others his blessing is not conspicuous; if no others are with him he should avoid specifically and go outside before Retzeh to exit the doubt.
