21) The Rav Zera Avraham, Orach Chaim siman 12, wrote that a kohen from Jerusalem may recite birkat kohanim at Mussaf on the second day of Yom Tov for residents of the Diaspora if they have no kohen, even though for him it is not a time of Temple service. Since Temple service is nothing for him then, for it is an entirely weekday day for him, nevertheless, because by law he has the ability to bless Israel whenever he wishes, he may bless the Diaspora residents, just like one who blesses the congregation several times. This is also the view of Mahar"r Ben Chaviv, whose words are brought at length in Ginat Veradim, klal 1, siman 13. He concludes that if they have with them a kohen from the Diaspora, the Jerusalem kohanim should not ascend with him; but if they have no kohen, a kohen from Jerusalem should ascend. However, Mahar"a Halevi z"l there, siman 14, discussed this at length and concluded with this general rule: we have not found a decisive proof to clarify this doubt, and who would dare to act and enter into a possible doubtful blessing? But in order to satisfy the higher view, since it issued from the mouths of the powerful rabbis of Eretz Yisrael, it is proper that they recite birkat kohanim, but not with the blessing "Who sanctified us with His commandments"; see there. Sheliach Tzibbur brought this on p. 146b-c, and Ikrei HaDat, siman 5 letter 7. See also the glossing Rav there in Sheliach Tzibbur, p. 235d and p. 236a, who wrote that if he can avoid it, that is good; if not, if there are other kohanim he ascends with them and blesses "Who sanctified us" without God's name and kingship, and if there is no one there except him, he ascends and blesses in his usual manner, like the opinion of Maharam Ben Chaviv and Mahar"a Halevi z"l; see there. But it appears to me that a kohen from Jerusalem who traveled to the Diaspora intending to return, and they are praying Mussaf on the second day of Yom Tov, or who prays in Jerusalem itself with Diaspora residents who came to visit, if there are kohanim with him from the Diaspora, he may ascend with them and bless "Who sanctified us" without God's name and kingship, for since there are others with him, his blessing is not noticeable. But if there are no others with him, he should specifically avoid it and leave before Retzeh in order to avoid doubt.
