From the left. See Baer Heitev. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, siman 5, concerning a kohen whose hands tremble and who cannot raise them. He may not perform Bircas Kohanim, because raising the hands is indispensable even after the fact. Even if he makes supports attached to the hat on his head and inserts his hands into them so that they remain raised, this does not help. This is true even in a place where the custom is for the kohanim to lower their talleisim over their faces, so that there is no concern that people will gaze at him, as explained below. Raising the hands by means of support is no more effective than standing by means of support. Furthermore, the law is derived from the verse, 'Aharon raised,' etc., which implies that he himself must raise his hands, whereas here the supports attached to his head are raising them. See there.
