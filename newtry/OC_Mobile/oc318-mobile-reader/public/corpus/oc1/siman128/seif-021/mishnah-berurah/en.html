82) Mental confusion. Their preoccupation with changing melodies may leave them unsure which verse or word to begin. Even though every word is prompted, their confusion may make them forget the prompter's words as well.
83) They should not sing, etc. Likewise, wherever cantors sing, it is improper for them to use many melodies for one passage. Taz. See Eliyah Rabbah, who defends their practice.
84) Only one melody, etc. It appears that likewise the kohanim should not each sing a different melody, because this too causes confusion.
