(S.k. 36) And at their sides, this means the sides in front of him. The same applies when one is even with the kohanim; it excludes only the sides behind the kohanim. See above at the beginning of siman 79 in Magen Avraham, s.k. 2.
For Rav would not fall, etc. Although everyone fell on their faces because there was a stone floor before him, upon which it is forbidden to fall, as below in siman 131, while in the place of the congregation there was no stone floor.
Not to trouble the congregation. Rashi explains, that they would stand before him; as they said: From where do we know that an elder should not trouble others, meaning that they should stand before him if it is possible that they will not need to stand before him? The verse states, “You shall honor the face of an elder, and you shall fear your God.” We read “elder and you shall fear,” that the elder should fear Hashem not to trouble the congregation if there is no need. The same applies here, there is trouble in crowding the congregation.
