132) Circumcised a baby and it died, etc. First, he intended to perform a mitzvah. Second, who says the child had completed its gestation? Third, perhaps the wind later harmed the child and caused its death.
133) People accuse him, etc. This does not refer to circumcising the baby; in that case, even witnesses do not disqualify him. It refers to an allegation that he actually killed someone. Even then, without witnesses he cannot be disqualified. If he himself knows the allegation is true, however, he may not recite the Priestly Blessing, whether alone or with other kohanim.
