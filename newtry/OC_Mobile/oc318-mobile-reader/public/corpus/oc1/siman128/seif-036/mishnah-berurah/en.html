132) "Performed a brit milah on an infant and it died," etc. First, he intended to perform a mitzvah. Second, who says that the infant had completed its term? Third, perhaps the wind subsequently affected it and it died. 133) "People accuse him," etc. This does not refer to the case of performing a brit milah on an infant, for in that case even if there are witnesses he may recite the Priestly Blessing. Rather, they accuse him of actually killing someone. Nevertheless, since there are no witnesses, he cannot be disqualified from the Priestly Blessing. If he himself knows that the allegation is true, however, he may not recite the Priestly Blessing, whether alone or together with other kohanim.
