Performed a brit milah on an infant. He intended to perform a mitzvah. Moreover, who says that the infant had completed its term? Furthermore, perhaps the wind subsequently affected him. [Mordechai; Hagahot Maimoniyot] This claim, that the wind affected him, applies even to one who unintentionally killed an adult and the victim did not die immediately, as stated in Gittin 70b; see there, where there is an additional claim when the victim convulsed, and see Tosafos there. "People speak against him." Nevertheless, if he himself knows that the allegation is true, he may not recite the Priestly Blessing. [Ralbach, siman 117] If he pushed a pregnant woman and she miscarried, he may recite the Priestly Blessing. [Ibid., siman 25]
