He circumcised an infant. For he intended for the sake of a mitzvah. Furthermore, who says that his months had been completed? Furthermore, perhaps the wind confused him (Mordechai, Hagahos Maimoniyos). This claim, that the wind confused him, applies even to one who killed an adult unintentionally and he did not die immediately, as we say in Gittin, folio 70b; see there, for there is also another claim when he convulsed, and see Tosafos there.
People murmur. However, if he himself knows that it is true, he should not raise his hands (Ralbach, siman 117). If he pushed a pregnant woman and she miscarried, he raises his hands (ibid., 25).
