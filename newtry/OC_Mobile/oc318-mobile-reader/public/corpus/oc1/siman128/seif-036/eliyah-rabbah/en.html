[36] To call the kohanim until Amen finishes etc. — so it is in the Talmud there. Olat Tamid wrote and there is here a slight contradiction to our custom that we say Elokeinu — end his words. I saw in Tosafot Berakhot daf 34 s.v. lo etc. that they wrote explicitly our custom was not in Talmud times for the reason mentioned above; and accordingly Levush who wrote in seif 10 in general like customs that say Elokeinu — he should not have written 'this time' at all; nevertheless in responsa Maharam Mintz that I brought above that the prayer leader says Elokeinu while the congregation answers Amen — I learned that even though he says Elokeinu, nevertheless sometimes the prayer leader reaches the kohanim before the entire congregation finishes answering Amen; on this perhaps the Talmud warns not to call kohanim until all finish; and so it appears in the body of the responsa — see there and examine; and examination is needed on it from the aforementioned Tosafot words.
