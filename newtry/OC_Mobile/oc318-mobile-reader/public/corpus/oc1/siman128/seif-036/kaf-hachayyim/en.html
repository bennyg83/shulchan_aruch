36) There: Although the kohanim washed their hands in the morning, etc. If he washed in the morning, even only to the ends of his finger joints, and has no water for washing before the platform, Rambam and his followers may be relied upon. He may bless provided he did not divert his attention from his hands. Olas Tamid, os 12; Eliyah Rabbah, os 8; Chayei Adam 32:8; Mishnah Berurah, os 20. If he did not wash even in the morning, he may not bless. Cleaning the hands by another method suffices for prayer but not for the platform, which requires water. Olas Tamid and Eliyah Rabbah there; this also appears to be Peri Chadash's view. See below, os 39.
