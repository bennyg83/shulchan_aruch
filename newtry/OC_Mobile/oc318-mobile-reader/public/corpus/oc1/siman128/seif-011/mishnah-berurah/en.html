40) When they turn their faces, etc. Some rishonim hold that this blessing is recited before they turn. Those careful in their practice satisfy both views: They begin the blessing while facing the sanctuary, turn toward the congregation during it, and then finish. Later authorities.
41) They recite the blessing, etc. Every kohen recites it; one does not recite it on behalf of the others while they answer Amen. Magen Avraham in Mabit's name.
