They bless. This implies that they begin the mitzvah blessing while facing the heichal and complete it facing the people. Peri Chadash. Rambam, however, writes in Hilchos Tefillah 14:12 that before turning to bless the people, the kohen recites 'Blessed are You, Hashem, our God, King of the universe,' and then turns toward the congregation and begins blessing them. Knesses HaGedolah struggles greatly with Rambam's view. The practice follows Shulchan Aruch. Peri Chadash.
In His holiness. All the kohanim recite the blessing; one does not bless on behalf of the others while they answer Amen, because this could cause confusion. Mabit, part 1, siman 180.
With love. This wording is used because the Zohar says that any kohen who does not love the people, or whom the people do not love, may not raise his hands. Magen Avraham. See Be'er Sheva, chapter Eilu Ne'emarin.
