Siman 11, "some say," etc. Tosafos in Berachos, ibid., in the name of R' Yehuda, and so too the Maggid Mishneh; but afterward he retracted, and see Beit Yosef.

Siman 11, "and they turn their faces," etc. 38a, 39a, 39b, Megillah 1a, and Tosefta in Megillah—they brought it in the Rif and the Rosh in chapter 3.

Siman 11, "and if," etc. Gemara there 38a.

Siman 11, "rather," etc. Rashi 39b and Tosafos there 1a, s.v. "for," etc.
