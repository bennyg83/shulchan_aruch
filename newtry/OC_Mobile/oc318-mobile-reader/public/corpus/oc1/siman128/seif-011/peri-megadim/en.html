By way of the right. See Taz in siman 690, letter 13; Magen Avraham, letter 21; siman 676, letter 6; and Levush there. Magen Avraham in siman 651 writes that a left-handed person follows the world's right side. I am uncertain. For a lulav and similar acts, where everyone circles to the right, he too should clearly do so. For kohanim and similar cases, however, his deviation from the world is not apparent. Nevertheless, the other kohanim act according to the ordinary right side, so the left-handed kohen must likewise follow the world's right side.
