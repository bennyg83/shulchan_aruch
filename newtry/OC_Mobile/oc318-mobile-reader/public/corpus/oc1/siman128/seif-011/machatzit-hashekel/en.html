(Seif katan 11) When they uproot etc. — Tur and Rambam wrote the language of the Gemara; nevertheless Rambam may hold like Rashi and Ran and Tosafot; and that Rambam stated plainly — thus is the way to cite Talmud language; but Tur and Shulchan Aruch are not accustomed to state plainly; and so it appears he holds their manner of walking was that they said thus.
He must first recite Modim, so that he will not interrupt in the middle of the prayer. Therefore he says Yehi Ratzon only once he is already on the duchan.
