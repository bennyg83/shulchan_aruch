85) That the prompter be an Israelite. Rambam writes this and supports it from the verse, 'Say to them,' implying that the prompter is not one of them. Accordingly, under our custom that the prayer leader prompts, as stated in se'if 13, one should ideally endeavor that the prayer leader before the ark not be a kohen.
86) He prompts them. Meaning the entire Priestly Blessing, word by word.
87) The cantor stands silently. This means until the end of the Priestly Blessing; from Sim Shalom onward, the cantor himself continues. The later authorities agree that the Mechaber's entire procedure is only ideal. If no one else knows how to prompt, the kohen serving as prayer leader may prompt the other kohanim himself.
