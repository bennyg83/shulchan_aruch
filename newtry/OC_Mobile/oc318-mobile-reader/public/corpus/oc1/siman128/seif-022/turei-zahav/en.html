To their brothers in the fields — and they did not say to women and children, as explained below regarding Amen, for women cannot answer, for "yevarechecha" and all the priestly blessing is in masculine language; the feminine is "aleich" with chirik under the yod and patach under the lamed. And "taf" is not relevant to this blessing for them, for they have not yet reached the age for the blessing to apply to them, only for the future, and the blessing is from now. It also appears that regarding a woman we say in Berakhot that a woman is blessed only from the fruit of a man's womb, and likewise children follow their fathers; yet here the blessing was for the kohanim themselves, and the Torah said "and they shall place My name upon the children of Israel" — but the kohanim are blessed from the mouth of the Holy One, blessed be He, who said "and I will bless them." But for answering Amen they are included properly, as above.
