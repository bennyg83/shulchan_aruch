Israel. P. Dalkathala from interceding that Sha'at Israel be. MA in the name of the preparation III:
And is silent. Meaning until the beginning of Sim Shalom; and it appears that here it is not better that the reader finish Sim Shalom, for only above where the prayer leader left his place and another came in his place and reads — therefore it is better that he also say Sim Shalom; not so here where the prayer leader stands in his place only silent and another stands beside him and reads — how can he say Sim Shalom while on the prayer leader's place. Bach wrote that even here the reader finishes Sim Shalom and the prayer leader says Kaddish; I have not seen this anywhere; on the contrary many times in my youth I was the reader beside the shaliach tzibbur who was a kohen and the shaliach tzibbur said Sim Shalom and Bach may his memory be blessed was in that synagogue — and such is the essence. Taz.
