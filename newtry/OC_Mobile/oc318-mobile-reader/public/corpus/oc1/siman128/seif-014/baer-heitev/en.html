And standing. The congregation may sit, provided their faces are toward the kohanim; Magen Avraham. An elderly kohen, advanced in years, who because of the weakness of age cannot stand when he performs nesiat kapayim: if he can support himself, that is best; if not, he should perform nesiat kapayim seated, for nesiat kapayim should not be nullified, since he violates three positive commandments. Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 1; see there. Yad Aharon disagrees, holding that standing is indispensable, and that it is better that he violate the three positive commandments; see there. This is also the view of Knesset HaGedolah.
