And standing. The congregation may sit, provided that their faces are toward the kohanim; Magen Avraham. An elderly kohen, advanced in years, who because of the weakness of his age cannot stand when he performs Nesias Kapayim: if he can support himself, that is best; if not, he should perform Nesias Kapayim while seated, for Nesias Kapayim should not be nullified, since he thereby violates three positive commandments. Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 1; see there. Yad Aharon disagrees with him, for he holds that standing is indispensable, and it is better that he violate the three positive commandments; see there. This is also the view of Knesset HaGedolah.
