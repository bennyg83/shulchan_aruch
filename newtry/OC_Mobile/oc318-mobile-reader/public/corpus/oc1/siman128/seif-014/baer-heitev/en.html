And standing. And the congregation may sit, only that their faces should be toward the priests — Magen Avraham. An elderly priest, seven days [after illness], and due to weakness of his old age he cannot stand when lifting his hands — if he can support himself, how good; and if not, he lifts his hands while seated, for one does not nullify the lifting of hands; he violates three positive commandments of Shabbat — Ya'avetz, part 2, siman 1, see there; and Yad Aharon disagrees with him that standing is an impediment, and it is better that he violate three positive commandments, see there; and so is the view of Knesset HaGedolah.
