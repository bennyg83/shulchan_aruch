When the kohanim. In the Mordechai it is written that they should leave before they begin Retzeh, and it appears to me that his reason is so that people not say that they are blemished when they do not uproot their feet at Retzeh; and see the Beis Yosef at the end of this siman. The Bach wrote that those disqualified only by rabbinic law need not go outside, for when the chazzan calls, "Kohanim," his intent is not upon those disqualified. This implies that if they were told to ascend they must ascend, for they transgress a positive commandment if they do not ascend. But from the wording of the Beis Yosef, who wrote in se'if 2, it implies that if one of the matters that prevent him applies to him, even if he was told to ascend he does not transgress. This also appears correct, for he is not refraining on his own; rather, the Sages prevented him, and they have authority to do this, as stated in siman 191.
