22) [Se'if 4] When the kohanim do not wish to ascend, etc. Mordechai says they should leave before Retzeh begins. Beis Yosef cites this at the end of the siman and explains that this is the designated time for kohanim to begin ascending, as the Sages say: A kohen who does not move his feet during Retzeh may not recite the Priestly Blessing. Since this is the designated time, we fear a congregant may tell him to ascend, whereupon failure to do so violates the command under se'if 2. Magen Avraham, se'if katan 4, says the reason is to prevent people from thinking they are disqualified when they do not move during Retzeh. Eliyah Rabbah, os 4, says it prevents the person who pours water over the kohanim's hands from approaching him.
23) There: When the kohanim do not wish to ascend, etc. For example, he is weak, his head feels heavy, or the like, as Rosh writes in the Yerushalmi's name and Beis Yosef cites. See our words above, os 15.
24) There: Except while the cantor calls, etc. He no longer violates, because he was absent when the call was made. Levush.
25) There: That they are disqualified. This means the son of a divorcee or chalutzah. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 5. See below, os 27 and os 167.
26) There: Until they finish the Priestly Blessing. This means the entire Priestly Blessing, as Levush writes. So too Eshel Avraham, os 4, in Mahari, siman 22, Beis Yosef's source for this law.
