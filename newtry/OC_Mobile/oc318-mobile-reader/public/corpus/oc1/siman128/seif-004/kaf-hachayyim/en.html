4) There: With fewer than ten, etc. If ten are present in the synagogue, but instead of a separate repetition the congregation and prayer leader recite the Amidah together aloud, and the kohanim finish their Amidah before the congregation reaches Retzeh, they may ascend the platform and recite the Priestly Blessing. Even if the prayer leader calls them, this is not an interruption because it is needed for the prayer. To avoid any concern, another person who has completed his prayer should call them before Retzeh. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, os 2, writes that witnesses reported this as the custom in the western lands; Shaliach Tzibbur, page 142d, cites him. See the beginning of Maaseh Rokeach, page 1b, in the novellae to Rambam composed by his son Rabbi Avraham. It appears there that when the congregation prays aloud together with the prayer leader, the kohanim ascend even if they did not finish before Retzeh. Beis Menuchah, os 8, cites this. However, in light of what we wrote above in siman 124, os 2, in Arizal's name; that the repetition is obligatory and possesses greater stature than the silent Amidah; one should not rely on this to cancel the repetition except when unavoidable, such as before departing on a journey or when insufficient time remains to recite it.
