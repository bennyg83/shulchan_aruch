If. See Taz, letter 3, first occurrence. His view is that a lone kohen must ascend on his own; if he does not, he violates a Torah positive commandment. Peri Chadash and Netziv here agree. Taz above holds that the exposition not to call one kohen is full and that calling him is prohibited. Levush implies it is only a rabbinic support. Tosafos on Menachos 44a, s.v. kol, implies a full exposition that one is not called. See there and what I wrote in letter 8.
