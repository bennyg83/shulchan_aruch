(12) When the kohanim do not want - such as when he is weak and the like. And refer to the MA who wrote in the name of Mordechai that the emigrant should go out first, that the initiates of Ritzah, and the purpose is so that they will not say that they are defective when they do not dislocate in Ritzah, and also that the Levites who pour water will not come and tell them to go up [AR]. And the judges wrote that the invalids should go up to the duchan, even the invalids, Durbanan, to go outside, when the cantor calls kohanim, he does not have the title of the invalids, and they agreed even more if they told him in the commentary, it is a leaf, or when there are only invalids in the court of law. It is upon them that there is no need to go up and they do not transgress the deed, they do not abstain from their own part, but the sages prevented them, and they have the power to do this.
