12) When the kohanim do not wish. For example, he is weak or the like. Magen Avraham cites Mordechai that one who leaves should do so before Retzeh begins, lest people think the kohanim are disqualified when they do not move during Retzeh, and also so that the Levites who bring water will not approach and tell them to ascend. Eliyah Rabbah. The poskim write that kohanim disqualified from ascending, even rabbinically, need not leave: When the cantor calls 'Kohanim,' he does not intend the disqualified. They further agree that even if someone explicitly tells such a kohen to ascend, or if only disqualified kohanim are present so the cantor certainly intends them, they nevertheless need not ascend and do not violate a positive command. They do not refrain on their own; the Sages barred them and possess authority to do so. Still, when no other kohanim are present and only those disqualified rabbinically remain, Eliyah Rabbah inclines that they should leave before Retzeh. Rabbi Akiva Eiger's gloss also implies this.
13) Except while, etc. Meaning: Consequently, no obligation takes effect upon them even if they enter afterward, as explained above in se'if 2.
14) That they are disqualified. Meaning that they are sons of a divorcee or chalutzah.
