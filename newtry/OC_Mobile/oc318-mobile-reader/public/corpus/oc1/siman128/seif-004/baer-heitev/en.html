The kohanim. The Mordechai wrote that they should go out before "Retzeh"; and it appears to me [that] his reason is lest one of the members of the congregation say to him "Ascend!" when the chazzan begins "Retzeh" — see se'if 20 in the gloss. And see subparagraph 11, what is written there, and not like the Magen Avraham. The Bach wrote: those who are disqualified [from the priestly blessing] by rabbinic [law] need not go outside when the chazzan calls "Kohanim," for his intent is not upon the disqualified ones. And the Magen Avraham wrote that even if [someone] told him "Ascend," he does not transgress — see there; and so wrote the Peri Chadash and the Olas Tamid.
