Priests. And in Mordechai it is written that they go out before Retzeh — and it seems his reason is lest someone in the congregation say to him "ascend" when the cantor begins Retzeh; see seif 20 in the gloss. And see se'if katan 11 from what is written, and not as Magen Avraham. Bach wrote that those disqualified rabbinically need not go outside when the cantor calls "Kohanim," for his intent is not toward the disqualified. And Magen Avraham wrote: even if one said to him "ascend," he does not violate, see there; and so Peri Chadash and Eliyahu Rabbah wrote.
