34) The gloss there — some are stringent if of leather, etc. — specifically when they only reach the knee and are tied with a strap or ribbon; but if they reach to the elbow they are like fabric pants and permitted; fabric covered with leather in any anklet is permitted — Magen Avraham seif katan 95, Raz note 6, Chayei Adam general 32 note 10, Mishna Berurah note 17; and even though siman 594 seif 16 wrote regarding Tefillin that fabric covered with leather is forbidden there because of discomfort and anything worn on the leg is forbidden to lock, and leather covering also shields and is forbidden for that reason because of substitution — not so here the reason is because of substitution, and leather covering does not substitute per Chayei Adam seif katan and Peri Megadim Orach Chayyim note 5 and Levush there on siman 594 on Magen Avraham seif katan 16 — thereby removed the uncertainty Eliyahu Rabbah note 7 wrote.
