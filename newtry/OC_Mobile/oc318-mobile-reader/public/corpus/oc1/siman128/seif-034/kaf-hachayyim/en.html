34) There, gloss: Some are stringent if they are leather, etc. This applies specifically when they reach only to the knee and are tied with a strap or ribbon. If they reach the thighs and are trousers, they are permitted. Cloth covered with leather is permitted in every case. Magen Avraham, se'if katan 5; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 6; Chayei Adam 32:10; Mishnah Berurah, os 17. Although siman 554:16 forbids cloth footwear covered with leather on Tishah BeAv, there the prohibition is due to comfort: Anything protecting the foot may not be worn, and a leather covering protects it. Here the concern is confusion with shoes, and leather-covered cloth will not be confused with them. Machatzis HaShekel; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, os 5; and Levushei Serad on Magen Avraham 554:16. This resolves Eliyah Rabbah's question in os 7.
