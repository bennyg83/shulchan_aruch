121) Who has not produced. Once he is thirteen years and one day old, we presume for this purpose that he has produced two hairs, similar to Rama's ruling above in siman 55:5, in the gloss.
122) He may not recite it. Even occasionally, because it is beneath the congregation's honor to submit to a minor's blessing.
123) To learn and be trained. For this reason, he may also recite the preliminary blessing. Magen Avraham; so too Radbaz, part 1, siman 565. This training does not begin at the ordinary ages of five or six, but only when he knows how to recite the Priestly Blessing in accordance with the kohanim's practices.
124) Nevertheless. This too is due to the congregation's honor. It is not respectful for an unmarried-looking youth whose beard has not filled out to bless them alone on a regular basis.
125) Occasionally. In our lands, where the Priestly Blessing is recited only on Yom Tov, he may recite it on every festival even alone, thereby establishing his priestly status, because this is still considered only occasional. Magen Avraham, Taz, and other later authorities.
126) See above, etc. Meaning: It is explained there that even a sparse beard is considered filled out once he is eighteen or older. Before then, an actually full beard is required.
