(121) Who has not brought [two hairs] — and if he becomes thirteen years and one day old, we rely regarding this that presumably he brought two hairs, as Rama ruled above siman 55 se'if 5 in Hagah.

(122) He does not lift — even occasionally, for there is no kavod hatzibur to be subject to a minor's berachah.

(123) To learn and be trained — and for this reason he is permitted to bless also — Magen Avraham; and likewise Radbaz chelek 1 siman 165. And this training is not like other times of training when he is already five or six, only when he knows to lift his hands per kohanim custom.

(124) However — this too is from kavod hatzibur, that it is not their honor to bless them always regularly with an individual man whose beard has not yet fully grown.

(125) Occasionally — and in our country where they lift hands only on Yom Tov, he may lift his hands on every festival even alone, to strengthen himself among kohanim — for this is not called except occasional [Magen Avraham, Taz, Sha'arei Ephraim].

(126) And see above, etc. — meaning it is explained there that even if he had a sparse beard we call his beard full if he is eighteen years or older; and before that we require actual full beard.
