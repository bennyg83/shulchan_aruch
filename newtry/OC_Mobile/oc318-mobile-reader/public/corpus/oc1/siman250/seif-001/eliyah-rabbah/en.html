In the morning, etc. It is a mitzvah to wait until Friday to purchase Shabbos foods even if doing so involves more effort than purchasing earlier in the week. If local sellers market food only in the morning and later it will no longer be available, one should not establish a study session before shopping. Likewise, he should not establish the communal prayer first, provided he recites Keriat Shema within its proper time. If the congregation is already praying, however, an individual pious person should not be more stringent than the community. If on Thursday he can find only lean meat and there is a reasonable possibility of finding fatty, better meat on Friday, he should wait until Friday. If he can buy the lean meat on Thursday and later, should better meat appear Friday, buy that and set aside the lean meat, he should buy the lean meat Thursday, as Seder HaYom writes. Kanfei Yonah writes that one should buy Friday if confident that the item will not be unavailable. Whatever one purchases, he should say that it is in honor of Shabbos.
[Levush] As it is written, "and it shall be," etc. Additionally, the word "vehayah" implies immediacy. Even if he prepared early in the morning, it is a mitzvah to add further preparations near bein hashmashot, as it says they shall prepare double, according to Magen Avraham citing Beit Yosef and Shibbolei HaLeket. Shibbolei HaLeket itself says there is a mitzvah to pursue preparations until bein hashmashot, and Sefer Tanya says likewise. This requires study.
