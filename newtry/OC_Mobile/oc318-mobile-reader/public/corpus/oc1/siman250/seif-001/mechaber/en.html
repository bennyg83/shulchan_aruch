Preparing the Shabbos meal. It contains 2 seifim: One should rise early on Friday morning to prepare the needs of Shabbos. Even if he has several servants to serve him, he should endeavor to prepare something himself in honor of Shabbos, as Rav Chisda would cut vegetables finely, Rabbah and Rav Yosef would chop wood, R' Zeira would kindle the fire, and Rav Nachman would clean the house and exchange the weekday utensils for Shabbos utensils. From them every person should learn, and should not say, "It is beneath my dignity," because this is the honor of Shabbos. {Rama: One should also sharpen his knife on Friday so that it will be ready for eating, for this too is an honor of Shabbos and prepares him for eating. [Kol Bo; Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer Chayei Olam]}
