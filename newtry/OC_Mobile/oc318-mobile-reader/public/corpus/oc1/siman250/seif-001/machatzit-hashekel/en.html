Seif katan 1: He should rise early, etc. “And he should say,” etc., because through speech the sanctity of Shabbos rests upon that item, for human speech is not an empty thing.
And see siman 572, etc. It states there that a communal fast should not be decreed with the first fast day falling on Thursday. For when people see the community purchasing on Thursday for two large meals, one for the evening because they fasted throughout the day, and one for the Shabbos meal, they will think that famine has come upon the world and prices will rise, since the fast decree has not yet become public knowledge. This proves that their practice was to purchase Shabbos provisions on Thursday.
The word “double” serves merely as an allusion.
Although all his deeds were for the sake of Heaven, he possessed this trait, etc. The phrase “although all his deeds,” etc., implies that because all of Hillel’s deeds were for the sake of Heaven, he should in this matter also have adopted the practice of Beis Shammai, for that too would have demonstrated that his deeds were for the sake of Heaven. Nevertheless, he possessed a different trait. Presumably he had a sound reason for it, though we do not know it. This implies that the trait of Beis Shammai is preferable. It was therefore necessary to explain, regarding Beis Hillel, that in this matter Hillel departed from his usual trait. Since we do not know Hillel’s reason, and reason itself indicates that the trait of Beis Shammai is preferable, one should act in accordance with Beis Shammai. This is also evident from the incident involving the butcher in Shabbos 119a, who said that he merited great wealth because he conducted himself in accordance with Beis Shammai.
