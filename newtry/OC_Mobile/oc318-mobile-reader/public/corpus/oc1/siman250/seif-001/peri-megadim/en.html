He should rise early. See Taz and Beit Yosef, citing the Midrash Tanchuma. I do not know why they omitted the Tur’s proof, cited by the Levush: “And they shall prepare that which they bring.” Preparation is compared to bringing, and the bringing occurred in the morning, as it is written, “They gathered it in the morning.” Perhaps “morning” extends until the fourth hour. In any event, there is a mitzvah to begin immediately on Friday, whenever possible, provided that he recites Shema and prays at the proper time. See Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1. Although one may not attend to personal affairs before prayer, as explained in siman 89:3, Shabbos needs are a mitzvah and may therefore be attended to before prayer. A pious person should not act piously by prolonging his prayers excessively on Friday. See Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1.
