(א) [Seif 1] One should rise early on Friday morning, etc. And this is the secret of "and it was on the sixth day and they prepared," etc. — it hints that on the sixth day one prepares for Shabbat, because day six corresponds to Yesod which prepares for Malchut, which is Shabbat — a speculum that has nothing of its own except what Yesod gives it; therefore one must intend to prepare this preparation from Erev Shabbat so that Shabbat day comes and finds it ready, and that acceptance be proper and fitting. Shaar HaKavanot page 61d.
