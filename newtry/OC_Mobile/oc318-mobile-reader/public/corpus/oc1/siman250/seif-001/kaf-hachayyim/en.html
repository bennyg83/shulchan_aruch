1) [Seif 1] One should rise early on Friday morning, etc. This is the mystical meaning of 'And it shall be on the sixth day, and they shall prepare,' etc. This alludes to the fact that on the sixth day one prepares for Shabbos, because the sixth day corresponds to Yesod, which prepares the needs of Malchus, represented by Shabbos, the speculum that has nothing of its own except what Yesod gives it. Therefore, one must intend to make this preparation on Friday, so that when Shabbos arrives it will find everything prepared, and the reception of Shabbos will be properly and fittingly conducted. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 61d.
