1) [Seif 1] One should rise early on Friday morning, etc. This is the mystical meaning of 'And it shall be on the sixth day, and they shall prepare,' etc. This alludes to the fact that on the sixth day one prepares for Shabbos, because the sixth day corresponds to Yesod, which prepares the needs of Malchus, represented by Shabbos, the speculum that has nothing of its own except what Yesod gives it. Therefore, one must intend to make this preparation on Friday, so that when Shabbos arrives it will find everything prepared, and the reception of Shabbos will be properly and fittingly conducted. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 61d.
2) A person must take care to purchase all his Shabbos needs on Friday and not on Thursday. With every purchase he should say, 'In honor of Shabbos.' This constitutes preparation through action. Shaar HaKavanos there; Pri Etz Chaim, gate 18, chapters 1 and 2; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 2. The reason is that through speech, the sanctity of Shabbos rests upon that item, for speech is not an empty matter. Machatzis HaShekel. It appears to me that as he sets out he should also say, 'I hereby go to purchase in honor of Shabbos, in order to fulfill the mitzvah of, And it shall be on the sixth day, and they shall prepare, etc., to rectify the root of this mitzvah in the supernal realm. May the pleasantness, etc.' If he goes on Thursday, he should say only, 'I hereby go to purchase in honor of Shabbos, to rectify the root of this mitzvah in the supernal realm. May the pleasantness, etc.'
