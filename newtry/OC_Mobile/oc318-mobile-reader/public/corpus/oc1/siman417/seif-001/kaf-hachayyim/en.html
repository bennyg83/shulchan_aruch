1) [Se'if 1] Rosh Chodesh, etc. On the Shabbos preceding Rosh Chodesh, after the haftarah reading and before Ashrei, the shaliach tzibbur announces and informs the congregation on which day Rosh Chodesh will occur and whether it will be observed for one day or two. Abudraham; Knesses HaGedolah, siman 284, Hagahos Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham, subsection 1.
