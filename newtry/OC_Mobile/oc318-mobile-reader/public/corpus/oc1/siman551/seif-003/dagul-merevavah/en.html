Both of them, the Bach and the Taz, did not grasp the intent of the Beis Yosef: for what the Beis Yosef wrote, that regarding laundry there is no difference for us between wool and linen, that refers to their [old-style] laundry; and what the Beis Yosef wrote, "however, linen vessels, etc., are permitted," that refers to our laundry; examine carefully in the Beis Yosef and you will find [it].
