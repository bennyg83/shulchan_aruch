(20) They are forbidden to cut hair: and regarding cutting nails there are views among the later authorities; however, for the need of immersion of a mitzvah it is certainly permitted. And likewise for the honor of Shabbos, for example if Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos: it is permitted on Erev Shabbos to cut the nails [later authorities]. And regarding combing the head one should not be stringent even on the other days of the week.
(21) And to launder even, etc.: for it appears as if he diverts his mind from the mourning.
(22) And our laundry: that is, of chutz la'aretz, is permitted, because the laundry is not as fine as the laundry of Eretz Yisrael; and therefore it is permitted to launder and leave until after Tisha B'Av; for to launder and wear is in every place forbidden [Beis Yosef].
(23) And linen garments, etc.: that is, old ones, but not new ones that come out from under the press.
(24) Because of pressing: even in Eretz Yisrael; and the same applies that they have in them no [issue] of their laundry.
(25) But it is forbidden to wear them, etc.: that is, even if he pressed them or laundered them before this week.
(26) Whether to wear or to launder and leave: that is to say, they practiced to forbid even to launder and leave, for to wear is by law forbidden in every case.
(27) That our laundry, etc.: that is to say, what is proven in the Gemara that our laundry is permitted is only for that of the people of Bavel, etc.
(28) Is called that of the people of Bavel: so it should read. And so it is stated in the first printed Shulchan Aruch from the Mechaber himself.
(29) Until after the fast: and one who has only one undershirt, one should permit to launder from Rosh Chodesh until the week.
(30) She is permitted to launder: that is to say, even during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls to be within it.
(31) Only she wears a checked and fine undershirt: and if she does not have [one], many later authorities agreed that she is permitted to wear a white undershirt, for for the need of a mitzvah they did not decree; and see in Derech HaChaim that she is permitted to give to a non-Jewish laundress for the need of wearing whites.
(32) And likewise for the honor of Shabbos they wear linen garments: that are laundered; and it implies from what he said "and likewise" that also for the honor of Shabbos it is permitted to launder on Thursday and Friday; and in Darkei Moshe it implies that they practiced a prohibition in this; however, if he has no undershirt for Shabbos one should be lenient; and through a non-Jew it is obvious that it is permitted; but regarding haircutting it is forbidden even for the honor of Shabbos, for anyway they are not accustomed to cut hair every week [Magen Avraham]; and see in Biur Halacha.
(33) And they spread whites, etc.: that is, on the tables it is permitted to change whites, but to change white sheets they practiced a prohibition.
(34) To launder from Rosh Chodesh onward: and even if he told her to launder after Tisha B'Av it is also forbidden for the reason of custom [Magen Avraham]; and in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he leaned to be lenient to give her from Rosh Chodesh onward to launder after Tisha B'Av, and Peri Megadim brought it; and regarding linen garments one may certainly rely on this.
(35) After Rosh Chodesh: and the same applies even if she launders during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls.
