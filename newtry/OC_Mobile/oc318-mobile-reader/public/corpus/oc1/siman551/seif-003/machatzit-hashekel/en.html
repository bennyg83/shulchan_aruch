(S.k. 3) And Beis Yosef, etc. And even though the women, etc. And in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah it was not logical to him to distinguish between a man and a woman in this. And he wrote that the language of Rav Beis Yosef also implies a permission, for he wrote "mohel" (singular language) and "the sandekim" (in the plural), implying that he also includes the one who takes out and brings in within the permission. And he concluded: it seems that so the people practice.
