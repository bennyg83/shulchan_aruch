To cut hair. I heard that some forbid cutting nails as by a mourner. It appears that this is not comparable, for this is considered a private matter, and regarding private mourning Tisha B'Av is more lenient than mourning. In honor of Shabbos it is certainly permitted, and all the more so for the need of immersion.
And to launder. Because it appears as though he is diverting his mind from mourning.
On Tisha B'Av itself. The Mordechai implies that women may wear white garments, and so it appears from the Bach and Beit Yosef in siman 554 in the name of Maharik.
And likewise in honor of Shabbos. This implies that for Shabbos it is permitted to launder on Thursday, and so it appears from the Tur and the Mishnah. Nevertheless, one should be lenient if he has no shirt for Shabbos, and through a non-Jew it is certainly permitted. Haircutting, however, is forbidden even in honor of Shabbos, because in any case people are not accustomed to cut hair every week.
From Rosh Chodesh and onward. Even if he tells him to launder after Tisha B'Av.
