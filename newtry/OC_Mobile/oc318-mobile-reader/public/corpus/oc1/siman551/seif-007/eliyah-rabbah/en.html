[7] Levush: Haircutting, etc. Regarding trimming nails, I wrote in Eliyah Zuta that it should be prohibited only during the week in which Tishah B'Av occurs, and Taz writes likewise. Magen Avraham appears to permit it even during that week, because private mourning is treated more leniently on Tishah B'Av than for an individual mourner. In Eliyah Zuta I wrote that even regarding a mourner this is disputed in Moed Katan 18. Since no prohibition is mentioned in the Gemara or poskim regarding Tishah B'Av, we should not invent one, for Rosh writes at the end of Taanis that laws of Tishah B'Av may not be derived from mourning by reasoning alone. One may therefore be lenient at least for the honor of Shabbos, even during the week in which Tishah B'Av occurs, for one who does so every Shabbos. Shiltei Giborim writes in the name of Mahari Bruna that combing one's hair is forbidden during the week in which Tishah B'Av occurs. This requires study, for how is it different from a mourner within seven days, for whom it is permitted in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 390?
