(46) That it is forbidden to fix: that is to say, that it is forbidden to make new garments; and likewise stockings that they call "zocken" it is forbidden to knit; and for the need of marriage, one who has no wife and children is permitted to make new garments, for by law it is permitted for him even to marry, as above in s.k. 14; and even on Tisha B'Av itself it is permitted to make through a non-Jew for the need of marriage [later authorities].
(47) And new shoes: and the same applies that it is forbidden to wear them.
(48) From Rosh Chodesh: for in the Yerushalmi it is stated, and it is brought below in se'if 8, that they practiced not to set warp threads of wool from when Av enters; and every fixing of garments is included in this, for the warp is the beginning of the work.
(49) To buy them: even if his intent is not to wear them until after Tisha B'Av; and by analogy to what we forbade above regarding laundry even when they launder them to leave.
(50) It is forbidden to make them for others, etc.: and if he has nothing to eat it is permitted, as regarding a mourner after three days.
(51) To be lenient in this: and it implies that even for a Jew they practiced to be lenient; and it speaks of where they gave them to him before Rosh Chodesh, for after Rosh Chodesh it is obvious that it is forbidden to give to him.
(52) But if it is known, etc.: that is to say, that by law a craftsman who does for a non-Jew certainly does not rejoice from this, and one should not forbid him except because of appearance, that they will think that it is of a Jew [and for this he said at first without specification that it is forbidden to make them for others, namely even for a non-Jew]; and for this he said: if it is known and publicized, etc., it is permitted even to accept the work after Rosh Chodesh and to do it.
(53) To non-Jewish craftsmen: and not to Jews, since it is after Rosh Chodesh, as above. And the reason they were more lenient in this than laundry, which we forbade above at the end of se'if 3, is because they are not yet his and his name is not called upon them.
