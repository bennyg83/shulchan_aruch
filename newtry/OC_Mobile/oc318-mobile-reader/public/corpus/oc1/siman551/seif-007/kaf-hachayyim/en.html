(7) In the Rama's gloss: And the father of the child. And his mother. Chayei Adam ibid. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch ibid. Mishna Berurah os 4. The paternal grandfather and the maternal grandfather also have the practice to be lenient and wear Shabbos garments, they and their wives. Similarly, it is customary to be lenient that other relatives who would normally wear Shabbos garments [for a bris] at other times do so now as well. Sha'arei Teshuvah ibid. However, he wrote that for other relatives one should be strict, see there. It seems that everything stated regarding a bris milah applies equally to a pidyon haben, as is implied from what is written below in the Rama's gloss on seif 9 where he equated bris milah with pidyon haben, see there.
