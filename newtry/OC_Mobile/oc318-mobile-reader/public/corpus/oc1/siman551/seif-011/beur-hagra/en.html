Seif 11, anyone who, etc. As above in seif 9, and as stated at the end of the first chapter of Nedarim, that one who breaches a fence, etc., as in Shabbos and Avodah Zarah.
And it is permitted, etc. The reason for the prohibition is connected with wine libations after the destruction. Even if that explanation was only a rejected reason, the main reason is that we do not impose a decree the public cannot bear; therefore where the custom was not accepted, it is permitted.
