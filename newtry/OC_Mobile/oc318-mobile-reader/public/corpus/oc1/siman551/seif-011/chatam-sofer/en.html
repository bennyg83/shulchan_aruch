There in Magen Avraham in that s.k. that a mourner is forbidden to bless. See Taz siman 471 s.k. 8 in name of Maggid Mishneh that it appears a mourner should not bless Shehecheyanu — requires study, for behold there if he does not bless Shehecheyanu he loses the blessing; and in this manner even between the straits he blesses as explained here. Perhaps Maggid Mishneh did not write thus but only that in public he should not bless Shehecheyanu — requires study [from the Gaon, end quote]:
