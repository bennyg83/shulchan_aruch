There. In Magen Avraham, the aforementioned s.k.: that a mourner is forbidden to recite the blessing. See the Taz, siman 671, s.k. 8, in the name of Maggid Mishneh, that it appears that a mourner should not recite Shehecheyanu; and this requires further study: is it not that there, when he will not recite Shehecheyanu, he will lose the blessing, and in that manner even during Bein HaMetzarim he recites the blessing, as is explained here. And perhaps Maggid Mishneh did not write thus except that in a congregation he should not recite Shehecheyanu; and this requires further study [from the Gaon Ksav Sofer].
