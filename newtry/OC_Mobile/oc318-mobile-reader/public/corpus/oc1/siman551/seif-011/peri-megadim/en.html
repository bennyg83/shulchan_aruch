Permitted. See the Taz in what he wrote in letter 9; and see Magen Avraham, letter 29: nowadays they practice a prohibition even regarding a dish of meat, and all the more so regarding the gravy itself; and see Eliyah Rabbah, letter 22: [if] it was cooked in a pot of meat, it is obvious that he may eat; a nursing woman, whose milk is bad for the infant when she does not eat meat, one should be lenient. And one who cannot eat dairy foods should eat fowl meat or salted meat of three days; see there.
