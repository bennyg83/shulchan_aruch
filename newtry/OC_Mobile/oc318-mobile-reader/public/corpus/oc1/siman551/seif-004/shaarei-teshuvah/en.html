The undershirt. See Baer Heitev; see in Magen Avraham that even the Shabbos tallis they do not change; and see what is written in the siddur Amudei Shamayim in the name of his father the Gaon in new responsa of the Radbaz, siman 693; and in our country they practiced as in the Shulchan Aruch, and one should not change; and Shabbos shoes and stockings, if they are not new, are permitted; see Peri Megadim.
