After the fast. All the more so meat and wine are permitted. Those who are accustomed to forbid meat and wine until Shabbos Nachamu are mistaken and need no release. The Bach wrote that they do require release, since in other matters a person may be stringent. It appears that if the practice was to cut hair and launder immediately after the fast, that remains the practice, for we do not obligate them beyond what they accepted.
That they practiced to forbid. This is when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos and is postponed. If it falls on Sunday, all agree that it is permitted.
From Thursday. If Tisha B'Av falls on Thursday, it is forbidden to launder on Thursday in honor of Shabbos, even from Minchah time and onward. This is unlike Tosafot, who are lenient. The Bach's explanation that laundering is permitted on Wednesday after midday for Shabbos is difficult and rejected, for the baraisa says that when it falls on Thursday, before it is forbidden and afterward permitted. Thus there is no license to launder on Wednesday. One whose thirtieth day of mourning falls on the eighteenth of Tammuz may cut his hair, since this is only a custom and it is a case of consecutive mourning.
