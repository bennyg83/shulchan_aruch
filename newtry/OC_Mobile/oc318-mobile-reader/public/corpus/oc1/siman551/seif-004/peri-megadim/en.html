See the Taz, who brought the words of Maharshal; the Bach also brought them; and the language is doubtful; and nevertheless it implies that regarding a mourner himself, for the honor of Shabbos they spread a laundered cloth ("hand-tucher" and "table-tucher") within seven, even though an undershirt is forbidden for him to wear; see there in Yoreh De'ah 389 in the Taz 1; and here for the honor of Shabbos the undershirt is permitted, and all the more so hand-cloths and table-cloths; but sheets, perhaps they practiced a prohibition; and this requires further study: why regarding a mourner are they stringent about an undershirt for Shabbos, etc.; and one may say differently. And what the Rav wrote in se'if 1: on Shabbos Chazon they wear only an undershirt alone; and here in the gloss it implies that for the honor of Shabbos they wear whites as on other Shabbosos, implying all the garments; and one must say that the custom is only the undershirt; and God willing in Magen Avraham it will be explained further.
