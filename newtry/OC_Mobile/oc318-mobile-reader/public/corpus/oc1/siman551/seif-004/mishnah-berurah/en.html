(36) After the fast it is permitted to cut hair, etc.: and all the more so meat and wine are permitted. And those who practice a prohibition of meat and wine until Shabbos Nachamu: some say that it is a mistaken custom and does not need annulment; but the Bach and Magen Avraham agreed that it needs annulment. And while they have not yet annulled, they are also forbidden to cut hair and launder until Thursday for the honor of Shabbos; and see in Magen Avraham what he wrote further on this matter.
(37) Immediately: and see below in siman 558 among the later authorities, that from the side of custom one should be careful in this on the tenth day until midday.
(38) That they practiced to forbid: that is, where it fell on Shabbos and was postponed; but if it falls on Sunday it is permitted even according to this view.
(39) Except Thursday and Friday: that it is permitted for the honor of Shabbos; and see above in s.k. 32. One who needs to go to distant places during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls is permitted to give to a non-Jewish laundress to launder linen garments for several Shabbosos that he needs for the journey.
(40) From the beginning of Rosh Chodesh: and not earlier, for most of the community cannot stand by it.
(41) From the seventeenth of Tammuz: see below in se'if 15 in Mishnah Berurah.
