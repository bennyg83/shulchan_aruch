Marginal note: by analogy to Adar; and it does not appear [convincing] to me, for there is no proof: for there the days between Purim and Pesach lie between two festivals; see this reasoning in Masseches Ta'anis folio 18a and Chagigah folio 18a; but here, once the days of distress have passed, they have passed; and all the more so after the fifteenth of Av, for there was no festival for Israel like the fifteenth of Av, and a festival came and interrupted it, and grief and sighing fled, and joy and gladness overtook; and in the Yerushalmi at the end of Ta'anis, in the section "and the city was breached," see there: he should make this the head of his reckoning; see there. It appears to me as support for the custom that from the day of Tisha B'Av onward another month begins, and we call it the month of Menachem. And it appears to me more: that regarding Adar the entire month is in joy because the fifteenth of Adar is the beginning of day thirty when they ask and expound before Pesach, so the days of Purim and Pesach are linked to one another; and this does not apply in Av; and this appears to my humble opinion to be true, with the help of Hashem, blessed be He.
