Seif 4, and if it falls, etc. This is from the Gemara there: how is it when it falls on Sunday, etc.
Or on Shabbos, etc. The Yerushalmi there says that when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos, both weeks are permitted.
There is one who says, etc. See Magen Avraham seif katan 17, and so it appears in the Semag and the Tur.
Except for Thursday, etc. As stated that on Thursday they are permitted because of the honor of Shabbos, and the same applies to Erev Shabbos. Although Abaye cursed one who launders then, that is when Tisha B'Av falls on Erev Shabbos; here, however, it is initially permitted.
And the custom is to be stringent, etc. See what is written in seif 9. They did not similarly adopt the laundering stringency because we do not impose a decree the public cannot bear.
