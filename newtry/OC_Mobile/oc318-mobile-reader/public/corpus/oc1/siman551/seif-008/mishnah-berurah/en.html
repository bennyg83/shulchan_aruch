(54) To set wool warp: meaning not to make warp threads from wool [and the same applies from other things]; and they supported it in the Yerushalmi on the name that the Even Shesiyah was abolished; and Beis Yosef wrote that there is no difference between for himself and for others, and between for pay and for free [Taz]; and spinning the threads to sew garments with them is permitted, for it is not included in this; and likewise it is permitted to make "kronen" that they weave with wood, for it is not included as a garment [Magen Avraham].
(55) Until after Tisha B'Av: see above in siman 426, se'if 2, and in Mishnah Berurah there.
