NB see Tur and Beit Yosef siman 513 Yerushalmi not to drink wine and eat meat — as written below seif 69; and it requires study from the end of chazakat habatim that fruit one does not eat and water one does not drink; and it must be said there it means if so one does not drink — for separatists cannot exist forever without water; if so meat too — for separatists can exist without meat; nevertheless it is impossible for most of the community; and since it is impossible for most of the community the decree does not apply at all, unlike our custom which is only a short time from Rosh Chodesh until the fast — therefore meat and wine which most of the community can manage without they forbade; water and fruit which most cannot manage without even briefly — they did not practice forbidding them, and it is strained:
