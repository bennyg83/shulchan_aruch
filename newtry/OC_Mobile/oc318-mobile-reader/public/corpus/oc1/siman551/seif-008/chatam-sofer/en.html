There. In the Mechaber, se'if 8: not to drink. Marginal note: see in the Tur and Beis Yosef, siman 313, in the Yerushalmi: not to drink wine and to eat meat; and as is written below in se'if 9; and this requires further study from the end of [the chapter] Chazakas HaBatim: "fruit we shall not eat and water we shall not drink." And one must say that there the meaning is: if so, shall we not drink water? Necessarily, because it is impossible for the perushim to endure forever without water; if so, regarding meat as well: although it is possible for perushim [to abstain] without meat, nevertheless it is impossible for most of the community; and since it is impossible for most of the community, the decree does not take effect at all; which is not so regarding our custom, which is only a short time from Rosh Chodesh until the fast; therefore the prohibition of meat and wine, regarding which it is possible for most of the community to stand by it, they forbade; [but] water and fruit, regarding which it is impossible for most of the community to stand by it even for a short time, they did not practice a prohibition; and it is forced.
