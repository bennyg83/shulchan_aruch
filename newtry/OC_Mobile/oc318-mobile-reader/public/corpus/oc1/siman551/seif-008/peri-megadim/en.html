That not. See the Taz; and see Magen Avraham 426:6; and in Eliyah Rabbah here, 46, that they are accustomed to recite Kiddush Levanah on motza'ei Tisha B'Av, because Mashiach was born on motza'ei Tisha B'Av, in the name of the Arizal, of blessed memory; and I too have seen people practicing thus; and if it is possible to taste beforehand, that is fine. And it appears that if he is still walking barefoot and has finished [putting on] his shoes, one should not recite Kiddush Levanah thus.
