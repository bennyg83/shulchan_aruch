(S.k. 8) And for a need, etc. That he has no wife and children. And if so he has not yet fulfilled peru u'revu.
And what they are not accustomed, etc. That is to say: how shall we permit for the need of marriage, which is a mitzvah, when marriage itself is forbidden? And how shall we be more lenient regarding needs of marriage than regarding marriage itself? To this he wrote that certainly marriage in such a case, where he has no wife and children, is also permitted. And that they did not practice to marry even in such a case is not for the reason of a prohibition, but rather that the matter is not a good omen.
And if so, just as marriage in such a case is permitted, the same applies that needs of marriage in such a case are permitted if he marries immediately after the fast.
