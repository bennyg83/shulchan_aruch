As is written in se'if 3: it appears to me that it should read se'if 2, namely what is written there, that for the need of a mitzvah everything is permitted; and it appears more that it should read this se'if, and that is to say what the Rama wrote in the gloss, that for the honor of Shabbos they wear linen garments; and even though the Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 14 in the name of Hagahos Asheri that haircutting is forbidden even for the honor of Shabbos, and those who forbid compare cutting nails to haircutting, there is no difficulty: for the Magen Avraham wrote there the reason that anyway they are not accustomed to cut hair every week, whereas cutting nails, on the contrary, they are accustomed to cut every week.
