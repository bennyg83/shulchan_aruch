Magen Avraham, seif katan 14. 'Lest another precede him through prayer.' Rashi explains that the other person might pray that she die before becoming betrothed to someone else; see there. According to this, the concern may apply specifically to betrothal, where he does not contemplate that she might later be divorced and become betrothed to him. Regarding a shidduch, he may not be concerned enough to pray that she die before becoming matched with another. Nevertheless, it appears to me that a shidduch contains less joy than betrothal, and may be permitted even without the reason that another may precede him. See Taz in siman 546 concerning Chol HaMoed, whose view accords with Magen Avraham here.
