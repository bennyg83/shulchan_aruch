(8) In the Rama's gloss: They wear Shabbos garments. Even though the Shulchan Aruch rules below in siman 559 seif 8 that a baal bris wears other [non-Shabbos] garments on Tisha B'Av itself, which has the status of a festival, all the more so from Rosh Chodesh until Tisha B'Av. Nevertheless, here it teaches us that even actual white garments [are permitted], for we only forbid white garments on the day of Tisha B'Av itself. Furthermore, it teaches us that even according to the Ashkenazic custom of being strict on Shabbos Chazon not to change [garments], one might think that the festivity of a bris should not override Shabbos and he should not change his garments according to the custom, therefore it teaches us that the custom is to be lenient for a bris. Mateh Yehuda. The Machberes os 5 cited his words and wrote that this is unlike the Zera Avraham siman 76 who wrote the opposite in the name of this authority, see there.
