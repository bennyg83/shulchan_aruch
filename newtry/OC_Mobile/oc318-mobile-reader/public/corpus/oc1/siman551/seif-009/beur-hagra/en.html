Seif 9, some practice, etc.; and some add, etc. Some versions of the Yerushalmi read that they would not drink wine or eat meat, but the primary version concerns work. The main reason is as stated at the end of Bava Basra: after the destruction of the Beis HaMikdash it would have been proper to decree, but we do not impose decrees the public cannot bear. During these days, however, people accepted a brief practice of restraint, and they added beyond the Mishnah's prohibition at the meal before Tisha B'Av.
And it is permitted. The prohibition is only like a vow, as in Pesachim and the Yerushalmi, where one may not change ancestral custom. Therefore vinegar is permitted to one who vowed from wine, as in Nedarim and Yoreh Deah.
And there is, etc. As below in seif 16.
And they hide away, etc. Like laundering, where the launderers ceased.
