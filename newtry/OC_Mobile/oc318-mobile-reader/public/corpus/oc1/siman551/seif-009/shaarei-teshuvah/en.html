A mitzvah. See Baer Heitev; see in Responsa Shevus Yaakov, who challenged from this Responsa Chinuch Beis Yosef regarding marrying after Lag BaOmer, and wrote to distinguish between this and mourning; see there. And once a certain sage was lenient regarding marriage during the days of the Sefirah, and their pairing did not go well; therefore it appears to me that one who guards his soul should distance himself from marriage in these days, even if there are many sides to be lenient, for danger is more severe than prohibition; end of his words.
