[9] Levush: But on the ninth itself, etc. Responsa Masas Binyamin, siman 5, wonders why he ruled like Hagahos Shaarei Dura against Or Zarua, who permits actual white garments. He further asks that Rama himself rules in Yoreh Deah 381 like Or Zarua, permitting actual white garments within seven days. The later authorities followed him. The second question is not difficult, because Rama there explicitly discusses after seven days. Within seven days, it appears that he agrees with the ruling here that she wears a checked garment, but not a white one. Regarding the first question, I am surprised that they did not examine Darkei Moshe there, who writes that he saw in Or Zarua itself that the permission applies only after seven days. Thus, his ruling accords precisely with Or Zarua. This also explains why Rama there prohibits bathing for the white-garment procedure within seven days, because he understood that Or Zarua agrees. This is indeed the implication of Responsa Maharil, siman 15, unlike Taz there. Masas Binyamin's question that the washing is not for pleasure but for a mitzvah may be answered that Rama follows his own view in siman 196 that washing for this procedure is only a custom, and it suffices that she examine herself and be pure.
