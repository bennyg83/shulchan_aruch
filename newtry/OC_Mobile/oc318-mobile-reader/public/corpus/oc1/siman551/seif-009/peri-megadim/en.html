And it is permitted. See the Taz in Yoreh De'ah 216, se'if 11 (a printer's error: 211); and here it is permitted even though there was wine on the seventeenth of Tammuz and afterward this wine became vinegar: nevertheless it is permitted. And what he wrote, that a prohibition was transformed into a permission: at first glance that is what is written in siman 217 regarding when there is no musk, etc.; see Magen Avraham there; if so, regarding wine, why is [it] forbidden as vinegar? And necessarily it is not similar to meat that became honey, and musk and milk, whose appearance and taste changed; which is not so regarding this, which is still called soured wine; rather the reason is that this is not joy. See what will be written, God willing, in letter 11.
