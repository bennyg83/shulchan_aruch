Taz, seif katan 3. 'And proof from a mourner, where it was ruled, etc.' At first glance there is no proof, for Tishah B'Av concerns old mourning, and Taz himself must distinguish on this basis at the end of his words. One must say that he holds that otherwise betrothal would constitute complete joy, and yet it was permitted because another might precede him. It would then be unreasonable to distinguish between old and recent mourning. Only if betrothal contains merely some joy can one say that, because this is old mourning, the concern that another might precede him is effective. Beit Yosef in siman 546 likewise writes that betrothal contains no joy, as stated in Yevamos, whereas Moed Katan gives the reason that another might precede him. This must be understood as Taz explains here.
