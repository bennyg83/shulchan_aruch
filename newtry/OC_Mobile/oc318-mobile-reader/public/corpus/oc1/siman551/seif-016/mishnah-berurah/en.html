(88) Not to bathe: see later, that even in cold one should be careful. And for healing it is permitted even in hot water, and even during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls [Eliyah Rabbah].
(89) From Rosh Chodesh: and if Rosh Chodesh falls on Erev Shabbos, then it is permitted to bathe even his whole body in hot water for anyone who always bathes on Erev Shabbos for the honor of Shabbos.
(90) And there are those who fast from the seventeenth of Tammuz: and a Torah scholar it is not fitting that he practice this stringency, which reduces the work of Heaven, for it is impossible that his learning not be reduced some hours because of the fast; and one must weigh with scales of righteousness in these matters [Shaarei Teshuvah; see there].
(91) Until Tisha B'Av: see below in siman 562, se'if 2, in the gloss.
(92) To bathe on Erev Tisha B'Av: in her usual way with hot water.
(93) Since, etc., for pleasure: and likewise minors who have sores on their heads: they practice to wash them on their heads.
(94) Even in cold: that is, his whole body; but his face, his hands, and his feet are permitted to wash in cold [later authorities].
(95) And even on Erev Shabbos Chazon it is forbidden, etc.: that is, even if he was accustomed to bathe every Erev Shabbos in hot water his whole body, it is forbidden on Erev Shabbos Chazon to bathe his whole body even in cold. And regarding immersion: one who is accustomed to immerse every Erev Shabbos is permitted; and one who sometimes cancels it because of the press of his affairs or because of the cold is forbidden.
(96) In washing the head in hot water: but not with "zeif" and "loig" that they make from ash.
(97) For one who is accustomed thus: and see in Chayei Adam that the same applies that his face, his hands, and his feet are permitted in hot water for one who is accustomed thus all the week.
