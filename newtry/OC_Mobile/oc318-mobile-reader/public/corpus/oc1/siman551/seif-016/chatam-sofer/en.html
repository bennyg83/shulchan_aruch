Marginal note: and one whose thirtieth [days] of his father and of his mother pressed upon him: my master, my father, my teacher, of blessed memory, wrote in a responsum, siman 158, like the view of Noda BiYehuda, Mahadura Kamma, Orach Chaim, siman 14, that regarding an elderly person the measure of rebuke is at thirty days, for there is in it great disgrace; and nevertheless he wrote not to be lenient with the leniency of the Bach that is in the Taz, s.k. 14, and it is enough to be lenient with a razor and that there no longer be in him a measure for which they would rebuke him [from the Gaon Ksav Sofer].
