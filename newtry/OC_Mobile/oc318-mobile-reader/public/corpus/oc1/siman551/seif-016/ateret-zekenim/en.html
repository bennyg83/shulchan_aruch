There are those who are accustomed not to bathe from Rosh Chodesh. But if Rosh Chodesh happens to fall on Friday, my teacher wrote in the Bach that they bathe for the honor of Shabbos; and so he wrote in the Minhagim that so they practiced, and it is proper; for even on Shabbos Chazon it is an excessive stringency, since even laundry the Sages of the Talmud permitted for the honor of Shabbos (and so wrote Maharshal, siman 92). I found written in Sefer Kenesses HaGedolah, and this is its language: I practiced to refrain from marital relations during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls, to be within it.
