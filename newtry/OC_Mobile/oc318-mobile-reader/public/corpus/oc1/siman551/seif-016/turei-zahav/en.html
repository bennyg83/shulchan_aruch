And some are lenient regarding washing the head. The Bach wrote that if Rosh Chodesh falls on Erev Shabbos, one may wash in honor of Shabbos, and so the customs record. It is not proper to add stringency beyond Shabbos Chazon. One who is accustomed all year never to miss washing on Erev Shabbos may rely on this, even when Erev Shabbos is Rosh Chodesh, but on Shabbos Chazon one should practice the prohibition in every case.
