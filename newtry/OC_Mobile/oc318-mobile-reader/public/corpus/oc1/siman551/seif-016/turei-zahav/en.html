And some are lenient on washing the head. Mora Uchla zt"l wrote: if Rosh Chodesh occurred on erev Shabbat it appears one bathes for honor of Shabbat; so in their customs. Although he wrote there in name of customs of ancestors in Mainz they are careful and bathe early — nevertheless to me one should be stringent, for Mordechai chapter 2 of Shabbat elaborated proofs that it is a mitzvah to bathe on erev Shabbat, and Mordechai concludes: but on Thursday it would not be called Shabbat unless impossible to bathe erev Shabbat. Also on Shabbat Chazon extra stringency — for they permitted laundering in the Gemara for honor of Shabbat even on Thursday when 9 Av is that week; we also practice leniency — how then be stringent on bathing not forbidden in the Talmud at all; Maharshal responsa 92 already exaggerated on this that the custom is not so proper to cancel bathing erev Shabbat — but what can I do, the custom already spread, end of his words. If so give to them not to add: specifically erev Shabbat of Chazon which sometimes falls eve of 9 Av — proper to be stringent from midday as ordinary eve of 9 Av; and due to not separating they practice forbidding on every erev Shabbat of Chazon. But erev Shabbat that is Rosh Chodesh not Shabbat of Chazon — I have not seen forbidding at all; especially one accustomed to bathe every erev Shabbat when possible and now refrains — sin is in his hand, end of his words. And whoever is accustomed all year to be careful not to omit bathing on any erev Shabbat may rely on this even erev Shabbat when Rosh Chodesh falls — provided on Shabbat Chazon one practice prohibition in all manners on erev Shabbat.
