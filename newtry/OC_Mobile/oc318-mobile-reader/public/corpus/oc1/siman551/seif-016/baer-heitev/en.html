From Rosh Chodesh. It appears a woman giving birth may bathe as Yoreh De'ah 481; Berachot 15b — Tosafot wrote even on Tisha B'Av she may bathe — see there; do not be lenient, for we are not so accustomed to bathing. Rosh Chodesh on erev Shabbat — some wash for Shabbat honor, some Thursday for clothing; customs. Bach wrote: one accustomed to wash every erev Shabbat who refrains now commits a transgression; sufficient if stringent on Shabbat Chazon. Knesset HaGedolah: many daughters laundered on Tisha B'Av after chatzot — I overlooked them; if they ask, forbid; on this Shulchan Aruch wrote women practice washing hair from Mincha upward; early ones instituted this because the anointed one was born on Tisha B'Av and a reminder of the redeemer is needed — specifically women who do not know scripture to believe in consolation. If Tisha B'Av is Thursday, forbidden to launder Thursday for Shabbat honor even from Mincha — Ran, Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, Levush; Bach: Thursday of Tisha B'Av after chatzot permitted to launder for Shabbat when impossible to wait until erev Shabbat because of Shabbat honor; likewise one traveling far this Shabbat may launder linen garments for several Shabboses — certainly impossible to wait until after Tisha B'Av; nevertheless better through a gentile — end quote; Magen Avraham s.k. 18 disagrees with Bach, wrote no permission at all Thursday, all the more for a traveler forbidden to launder for Shabbat need after erev Shabbat; see Taz s.k. 14:

(2) the head But it is forbidden to bathe in burit and water and ashes that Korin is wearing. And one who dips every Shabbat eve for the sake of a mitzvah is allowed to dip on this Shabbat of 2015 as well. Who is the one who cancels sometimes because he is busy with his business or because of the cold even now is not allowed to dip.
