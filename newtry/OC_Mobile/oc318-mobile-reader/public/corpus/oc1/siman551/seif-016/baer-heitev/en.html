From Rosh Chodesh. It appears to me that a woman after childbirth may bathe, as written in Yoreh Deah 381. Tosafot on Berachos 15b write that she may bathe even on Tishah B'Av; see there. One should not extend this leniency generally, because we are not so accustomed to bathing. When Rosh Chodesh falls on Friday, some bathe in honor of Shabbos, while others bathe on the preceding Thursday. Levush and Minhagim. Bach writes that one who regularly bathes every Friday commits a wrong by refraining now, and it is sufficient for him to be stringent only on Shabbos Chazon. Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah writes: Many women laundered on Tishah B'Av after midday and I looked away from them, but if they ask, they must be forbidden. He further writes that women customarily wash their heads from Minchah onward. The early elders instituted this because Mashiach was born on Tishah B'Av, and one should make a remembrance of the redeemer. This applies specifically to women who cannot study written works and thereby place faith in the consolations, so they require encouragement. If Tishah B'Av falls on Thursday, laundering on Thursday for Shabbos is forbidden, even after Minchah. Ran, Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, and Levush. Bach writes that on Wednesday, Erev Tishah B'Av, after midday, laundering for Shabbos is permitted, because one cannot wait until Friday without detracting from the honor of Shabbos. The same applies to one leaving on a distant journey that Shabbos, who may launder linen garments for several Shabbosos, since he certainly cannot wait until after Tishah B'Av. Nevertheless, it is preferable to do so through a non-Jew. Magen Avraham, seif katan 18, disagrees with Bach and writes that there is no permission at all to launder on Wednesday, and certainly not for one departing on a journey, since laundering for a Shabbos after that week is forbidden. See Taz, seif katan 14.
The head. It is forbidden, however, to wash with soap and a mixture of water and ashes called lye. Levush. The universal custom is to forbid washing the entire body. Darkei Moshe. One who immerses every Friday for the sake of a mitzvah may immerse on this Shabbos as well. Shelah. If he sometimes omits the immersion because he is occupied with business or because of the cold, he may not immerse now. Magen Avraham. See Taz, seif 7, seif katan 16.
