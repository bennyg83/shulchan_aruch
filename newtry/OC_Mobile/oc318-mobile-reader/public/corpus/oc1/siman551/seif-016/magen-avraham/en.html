Not to bathe. It appears that a woman after childbirth may bathe, as in Yoreh Deah siman 381. Tosafot in Berachos wrote that even on Tisha B'Av she may bathe. One should not be lenient generally, since we are not so accustomed to bathing. When Rosh Chodesh falls on Erev Shabbos, some bathe for Shabbos and some bathe on the preceding Thursday. The Bach wrote that one who bathes every Erev Shabbos and refrains now commits a wrong; it suffices to be stringent on Shabbos Chazon.
The head in hot water. It is forbidden, however, to wash with soap or with water and ash. The custom is that everyone forbids washing the whole body. One who immerses every Erev Shabbos for the sake of a mitzvah may immerse even this week; but one who sometimes omits it because of business or cold may not immerse now.
