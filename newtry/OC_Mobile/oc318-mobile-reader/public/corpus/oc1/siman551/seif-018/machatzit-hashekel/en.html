(ד) And likewise in Mishnah chapter 2 of Taanis: we learned men of mishmar and men of ma'amad were forbidden to launder and iron all days of the week; on the fifth day it was permitted for honor of Shabbat. Magen Avraham's intent is not to refute Bach that if 9 Av falls Thursday to forbid on Wednesday — that case is different since one can launder on the fifth. Rather he brings proof for his view that specifically on the fifth day it is recognizable one acts for Shabbat needs — namely from that they did not permit men of mishmar and ma'amad for Shabbat honor except on the fifth; therefore on the fifth it is more recognizable one acts for Shabbat than on the fourth — if so, it is reasonable one may say on the fourth they do not permit for Shabbat honor.
