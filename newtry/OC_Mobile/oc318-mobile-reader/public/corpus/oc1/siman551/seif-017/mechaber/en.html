It is good to be careful from saying the Sheycianu blessing during Bein HaMitzrim (The Three Weeks) on a fruit or clothing. But on a Pidyon Haben say and do not delay (lit. spoil) the mitzvah. ((And so with a fruit that he won't find after Tisha B'av, it is permitted to bless and to eat it Bein HaMitzrim} (Binyamin Zev Siman 163 and Teshuvas Maharil}.):
