[17] Levush: But to wear, etc., from Rosh Chodesh, etc. This is difficult, since Shehecheyanu is forbidden already from the seventeenth of Tammuz. Magen Avraham answers that it refers to wearing them on Shabbos, when Shehecheyanu is permitted as stated in seif 17. In my Eliyah Zuta, however, I wrote in the name of Malbushei Yom Tov that a garment brings greater joy and is forbidden even on Shabbos. It therefore appears that the teaching includes even shoes and linen garments such as a shirt, over which Shehecheyanu is not recited, as stated at the end of siman 223. Alternatively, it refers to one who purchased a completed garment before the seventeenth of Tammuz and recited the blessing at the time of purchase. According to this, purchasing a completed garment during Bein HaMetzarim is forbidden. One who buys an unfinished garment and gives it to a craftsman does not recite the blessing until he wears it.
