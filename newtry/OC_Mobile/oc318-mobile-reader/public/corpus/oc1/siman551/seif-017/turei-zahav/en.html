Since the authorities permitted writing necessary court documents on Chol HaMoed because the giver, witnesses, or beis din may die, all the more so one should hasten to fulfill a blessing connected with Heaven when possible. The Sefer Chasidim permits reciting Shehecheyanu on Shabbos. The restraint from meat and wine from the seventeenth of Tammuz until Tisha B'Av has several reasons, including Daniel's fast, the continuous distress from the breach of the city until the destruction, and the cessation of the Tamid and wine libation. These reasons do not apply to washing. A vow from meat and wine during these three weeks does not include Shabbos unless he explicitly meant complete weeks, because vows follow the intent of the one who vows and the custom does not include Shabbos.
