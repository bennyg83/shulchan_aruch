To launder, Baer Heitev; and see Yad Ephraim on Magen Avraham here; see Birkei Yosef in name Nachfeh Kesef that even per Pri Megadim siman 568 speech to a gentile on other rabbinic prohibitions is permitted — due to stringency of 9 Av they practiced forbidding even telling a laundress she should take the garments and launder them. See Mishna Berurah in name Zera Emet to give a laundress a flax garment before Rosh Chodesh that she soak them the same day in water, and after Av enters complete whitening by water, ashes, and borit as custom — all in a Jewish house; and even though by law one could permit, nevertheless it is proper to be careful also in this for being in a Jewish house and mar'it ayin — see there; see below comment 35. (•) (Shulchan Aruch seif 4) After the fast: see Magen Avraham comment 16 regarding those who practice not eating meat and wine until Shabbat Nachamu — Bach wrote they need release, and without release even laundering and haircut are forbidden until Thursday; Baer Heitev omitted this — see Magen Avraham that if they benefited not eating meat and wine but practiced laundering and haircut it is permitted to them, for one does not rebuke them more than their practice; also Magen Avraham wrote for practicers prohibition even if 15 Av falls on a weekday they are forbidden meat and wine.
