From saying Shehecheyanu. On Shabbos it is permitted, though the writings of the Ari forbid even on Shabbos. It appears that if the seventeenth of Tammuz falls on Shabbos and is postponed, all agree it is permitted. The reason for the prohibition is that this period is a time of calamity, so one should not bless that He has kept us alive for this time; it is not because of mourning, for we do not find that a mourner may not recite Shehecheyanu.
And he should not let the mitzvah become delayed. Or the blessing. Although one who sees a new fruit delays the blessing, since the practice is to wait until eating it, this is not called delaying the blessing.
That will not be found. According to those who permit on Shabbos, one must say that it will not be found even on Shabbos, and if he takes it during the week and saves it for Shabbos it will spoil, such as small cherries.
