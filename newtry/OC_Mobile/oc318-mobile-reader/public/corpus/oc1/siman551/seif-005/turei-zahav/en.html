And linen garments, etc. The Ramban's statement that linen has no issue of pressing applies specifically to old garments. New garments do have pressing. Thus new garments, whether white or colored, are forbidden. The Beit Yosef's words about laundering refer to pressing garments already laundered before the week, not to forbidding laundering itself in all cases. A woman wearing white garments is discussed in Yoreh Deah siman 381.
