(42) To launder garments: even though by law it is permitted, for there is no laundry prohibition because it is forbidden in melacha, but rather in order to reduce joy and to show mourning, and that does not apply to laundering garments of non-Jews: nevertheless it is forbidden because of appearance, for it is not recognizable that they are of a non-Jew; however, in a place where the non-Jews have other clothing, so that it is recognizable to all that they are of a non-Jew, one should be lenient; and where he has nothing to eat, certainly for his living needs it is at least permitted.
(43) During this week: in this, even for us they did not practice from Rosh Chodesh, from what the Rama did not write that they practice even in this from Rosh Chodesh as he wrote in the following se'if [Chayei Adam].
