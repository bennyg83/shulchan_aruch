Magen Avraham, s.k. 7. And in a place where they practiced a prohibition on all buying and selling, etc. And even though according to everyone a little buying and selling is permitted, it is like things that are permitted and others practiced a prohibition regarding them, which requires annulment, as is explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 214; but those who practice to reduce, since according to several poskim that is forbidden, annulment does not help.
