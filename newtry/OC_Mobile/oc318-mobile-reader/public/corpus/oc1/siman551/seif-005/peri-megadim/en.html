And a woman who wears whites, which the Rav, of blessed memory, wrote in the gloss: see Yoreh De'ah 381:2; it implies there within seven, if she has a clean undershirt she should wear [it], not a white one; but if she has only an actually white one, one may say that it is permitted there, and the same applies here on Tisha B'Av itself for any matter of a mitzvah; and see Magen Avraham, letter 13; and God willing there it will be explained further; and in Responsa Massas Binyamin, siman 5, see there; and see se'if 16.
