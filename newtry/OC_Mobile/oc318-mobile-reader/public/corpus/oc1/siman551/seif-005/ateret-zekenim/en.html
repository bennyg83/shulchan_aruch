It is forbidden for Jewish women, etc., because of appearance: even if she was a laundress for many, for Jews and for non-Jews, nevertheless it is forbidden. However, the work that the women do to scarves after the laundering, that they rub the edges, one should not forbid during this week regarding scarves of non-Jewish women, for even regarding those of a Jew there is nothing here but a mere stringency; and also the women who spin threads, since their livelihood is in this work, it is permitted.
