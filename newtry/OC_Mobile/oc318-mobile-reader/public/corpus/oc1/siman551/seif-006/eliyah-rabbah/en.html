[6] Lest another precede him, etc. Although he previously gave the reason that there is no joy without a meal, one must say that it would nevertheless have been forbidden on Tishah B'Av itself. Taz explains that there is still some joy, though not great joy, and therefore the reason that another might precede him was needed. That reason alone would not permit it if the joy were great. Magen Avraham writes that making a shidduch is permitted, but a meal is forbidden even without dancing or music, and even on Shabbos. Eating confections when the tena'im are written is not considered a meal. See siman 546. Dancing and music are customarily forbidden throughout Bein HaMetzarim in every circumstance, even without betrothal.
