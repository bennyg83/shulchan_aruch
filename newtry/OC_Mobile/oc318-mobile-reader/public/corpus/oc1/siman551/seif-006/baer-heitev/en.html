From Rosh Chodesh. Bach writes that it is forbidden from the seventeenth of Tammuz onward, since Shehecheyanu is not recited, as stated in seif 17. Since it is stated there that Shehecheyanu may be recited on Shabbos, new garments may consequently be worn on the Shabbosos of Bein HaMetzarim. From Rosh Chodesh onward, however, wearing them is forbidden even on Shabbos. See Magen Avraham.
