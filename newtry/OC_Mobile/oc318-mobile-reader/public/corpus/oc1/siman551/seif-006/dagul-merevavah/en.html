And it implies that they forbade eating meat even for minors; for if not so, why did he write without specification that they put away the slaughtering knife, and not like Magen Avraham in s.k. 31; and so it appears by analogy to haircutting and laundry below in se'if 14; and the Havdalah cup is different because of a mitzvah. And what the Magen Avraham wrote from siman 269: I am astonished, for he himself wrote there in siman 269, s.k. 1, that although there are those who say it is permitted even for an adult, and even though we do not hold thus, nevertheless it is at least permitted for a minor; and if so, all the more so the Havdalah cup, which is permitted even for an adult where there is no child.
