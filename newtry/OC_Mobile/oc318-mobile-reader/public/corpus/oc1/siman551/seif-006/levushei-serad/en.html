Taz, s.k. 5. Whether dyed [garments] are forbidden. That is to say, even though regarding a mourner for all thirty days there is a distinction between whites and dyed [garments], here it is more severe and everything is forbidden (see in Yoreh De'ah, siman 390, se'if 5, the aforementioned distinction between whites and dyed [garments]); and from where the Ramban, of blessed memory, learned this matter is in the Beis Yosef at length.
