New garments. It appears that since the manner is to press garments in a press, they are considered ironed and are forbidden according to the law. For this reason the practice is to forbid Shabbos garments as well, for even after several Shabbosos their pressing is still recognizable.
From Rosh Chodesh and onward. The Bach wrote that it is forbidden from the seventeenth of Tammuz onward, since one does not recite Shehecheyanu then. Since on Shabbos one may recite Shehecheyanu, it would follow that one may wear new garments on the Shabbosos between the straits; but from Rosh Chodesh onward even on Shabbos it is forbidden. Alternatively, the case is where he bought a finished garment and recited Shehecheyanu at the purchase before the seventeenth of Tammuz.
