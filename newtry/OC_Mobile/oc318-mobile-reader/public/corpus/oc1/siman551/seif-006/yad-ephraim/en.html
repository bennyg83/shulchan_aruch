Seif katan 20. 'Nothing stands before danger.' It may be answered that the danger permits only demolition. Therefore, the principle does not apply, for although he may demolish, construction remains forbidden. Although on Chol HaMoed such a case permits even rebuilding, because otherwise he would refrain from demolishing, see siman 545, construction for a wedding may be treated more stringently during these days. Therefore the Tur writes, 'According to this, etc.' Meaning, according to the view that only joyful construction is ever prohibited, when a structure is leaning toward collapse the Yerushalmi permits both demolition and rebuilding, as on Chol HaMoed, since otherwise he would refrain from demolishing. According to the other opinion, however, construction of a wedding structure may remain forbidden even for this reason, because the Sages were lenient only on Chol HaMoed and not here.
