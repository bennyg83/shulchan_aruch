(66) New garments, etc. — from their dispute regarding giyutz it is implied that in laundering even Ra'avad concedes there is no difference between white and dyed; and we say there: this teaches that a mourner is forbidden in laundering — meaning even laundering in water alone even though there is no giyutz; and similarly regarding new garments per Tanna Kamma and Rabbi Yehuda it is giyutz even on old ones. And in Yerushalmi: what is giyutz? New wool garments and linen garments whitened — meaning linen specifically new and white, and wool new even dyed; and we do not rule like Yerushalmi here that only of other types is forbidden only white, for we rule like R' Eliezer per R' Shimon that Rava holds like him; nevertheless we learn from Yerushalmi that for new white garments there is no difference between linen and other types; and we say: with what do they rejoice in the Land of Israel? With whitened linen garments — behold there is giyutz in them; and what is written that linen garments have no giyutz — in old ones because sweat does not leave them, and all the distinctions there are in old ones; but in new ones all are forbidden; and see above seif 63 that even linen garments and our laundering are forbidden by law:
