Seif 6, new garments, etc. Since the dispute concerns pressing, we learn that all agree regarding laundering that there is no distinction between white and colored garments. The Yerushalmi defines pressing as new wool garments and pressed white linen garments. Although we do not rule like the Yerushalmi in every respect, we learn from it that with new white garments there is no difference between linen and other species. Since even linen garments and our laundering are forbidden according to the law, all new garments are forbidden.
And we. As written in seif 3, even laundering is customarily forbidden; all the more so new garments, which are forbidden even where laundering is permitted.
