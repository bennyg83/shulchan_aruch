(44) New vessels: and in a place where the way is to press the garments in a press, if so they are included in pressed ones, and they are forbidden even if they are old, for the pressing makes them like new, and they are forbidden to wear during this week; and for this reason they practiced a prohibition regarding Shabbos garments to wear them on Shabbos Chazon: for even though he already wore them for several Shabbosos, their pressing is still noticeable and it is like new ones.
(45) From Rosh Chodesh onward: and it speaks of one who bought a finished garment as it is and recited Shehecheyanu at the time of purchase before the seventeenth of Tammuz; and therefore there is no prohibition before Rosh Chodesh; for one who bought a garment that is not finished and gave it to a craftsman to sew, does he not recite the blessing at the time of wearing, and it is forbidden to wear from the seventeenth of Tammuz because of Shehecheyanu. And according to the view of those who are lenient that on Shabbosos during Bein HaMetzarim it is permitted to recite Shehecheyanu, if so it is permitted to wear new ones on Shabbosos during Bein HaMetzarim; but from Rosh Chodesh onward it is forbidden even on Shabbos; and garments that are not so important that one need not recite Shehecheyanu over them, for example new shoes and stockings and the like, it is certainly permitted to buy them and wear them from the seventeenth of Tammuz until Rosh Chodesh; and see further what we wrote in se'if 17 in the name of the Gra.
