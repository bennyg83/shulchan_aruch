(א) New garments — and where the custom is to press garments in a press, they are included in pressed garments and forbidden even if old, for pressing makes them like new and forbidden to wear this week. And from where did they practice prohibition on Shabbat garments to wear them on Shabbat? It appears: even though he wore them previously many Shabbosim, still the pressing is visible and they are like new.
