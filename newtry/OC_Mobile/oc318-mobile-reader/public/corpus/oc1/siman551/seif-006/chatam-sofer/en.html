There in Magen Avraham s.k. 14 — they are not accustomed to get a haircut every week. NB mourning is not evident if he gets a haircut every Shabbat, for otherwise they get a haircut only on erev Shabbat as above — in my humble opinion. Nevertheless it appears to me that growing beard hair in our time, which is very great disgrace, should be judged like one compelled by his mourning — below seif 15; and if he combines some leniency such as bringing in and taking out the infant per Magen Avraham above s.k. 3, there is room to be lenient that it be somewhat from abundance of his hair:
