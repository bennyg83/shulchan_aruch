There. In Magen Avraham, s.k. 14: they are not accustomed to cut hair every week. Marginal note: and the mourning is not recognizable if he cuts hair every Shabbos, for anyway they cut hair only on Erev Shabbos, as appears to me in my humble opinion. And nevertheless it appears to me that growing the hairs of the beard in this time, which is a very great disgrace, should be judged like one whose mourning periods pressed upon him, below in se'if 15; and if he combines with this some leniency, such as bringing in and taking out the infant, which is in Magen Avraham above, s.k. 3, one should be lenient that it be somewhat from the abundance of his hairs.
