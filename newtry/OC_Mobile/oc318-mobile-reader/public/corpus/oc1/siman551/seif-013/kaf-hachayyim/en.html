(13) In the Rama's gloss: Only the undershirt. And it is permitted even when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos. Levush seif 16. One who is strict about not changing it, that is a foolish custom, for one wears it only because of sweat. Responsa of Maharshal siman 27. Mateh Moshe siman 736. Magen Avraham s.k. 4. However, the Eliyah Rabbah os 39 wrote that those who have the practice not to change even the undergarment have what to rely upon, see there. All this follows the view of the Rama. However, Maran [the Mechaber] in Beit Yosef at the end of siman 552, after citing the words of the Hagahos Maimuniyos and the Mordechai, wrote that this is not the custom. So too the Radbaz in responsa vol. 2 siman 693 challenged this custom and wrote that the practice in Ashkenaz of not changing garments on Shabbos [Chazon], all the regions of Israel did not follow this custom, and rightly so, for it is against the law; and he elaborated further, see there. Similarly, the Gaon R' Tzvi, even though he was Ashkenazic, had the practice of not changing anything from other Shabbosos, even when Tisha B'Av fell on Shabbos. So wrote his son R' Yaakov Emden in his siddur p. 68, see there. The Machberes os 4 cited his words. So too wrote the Chayei Adam rule 133, end of os 21, that in the community of Vilna they practice according to the Gra to wear Shabbos garments, and some change one garment. So too wrote Or Chayyim on Chayei Adam ibid. that in many communities they practice wearing all Shabbos garments and do not change anything, even on Shabbos when Tisha B'Av falls, see there. Obviously, when he wrote "in many communities, etc." he meant Ashkenazic communities, but the Sephardic communities all practice not to change this Shabbos from other Shabbosos, as written by the Beit Yosef and the Radbaz. However, the Shulchan Gavoah os 4 wrote that the custom is not to wear white or distinguished garments like festival garments. So too wrote the Yafeh L'Lev vol. 3 os 2 that their custom is not to wear white as on other summer Shabbosos, but rather other colored garments as is done on winter Shabbosos. This is what we have written according to their custom, but some have the practice not to change at all and wear even white as on other Shabbosos, just not festival garments.
