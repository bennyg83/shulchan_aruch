And regarding an elderly person, whatever impedes eating is permitted; which is not so regarding a mourner within seven [days], even for what impedes eating it is forbidden. And in Yoreh De'ah, siman 390, he ruled like the Rif Geonim, and here like the Ramban; and it is possible that the Beis Yosef holds that because it is ancient mourning, therefore one should be lenient like the Ramban. However, this requires further study in the words of the Beis Yosef himself: in Yoreh De'ah, siman 390, he rules like the words of the Rif Geonim, and he wrote that we hold like the Raavad and the Rambam that it is forbidden all thirty days, even what impedes eating; this requires further study.
