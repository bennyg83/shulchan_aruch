(13) Regarding the gloss — only the undershirt alone; and permitted even if Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbat (Levush seif 16). One who is stringent not to change it is a foolish custom, for it is not a garment but only because of sweat (responsum of Rashal siman 27; Mateh Moshe siman 376; Magen Avraham s.k. 4). However Or Zarua wrote that those accustomed not to change even the undershirt have upon what to rely — see there. And all this is per our teacher the Rav z"l; nevertheless Maran z"l in Beit Yosef end siman 552 after bringing the words of Gema and Mordechai wrote they did not practice thus; for Radbaz in responsum part 2 siman 793 protested this custom and wrote that Ashkenaz practicing not to change garments on Shabbat — all Galilee of Israel did not practice this custom and they did well for it is against the law; and he expanded — see there. And similarly the Gaon Mahar"r Tzvi though Ashkenazi practiced not to change anything from other Shabboses even when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbat — so too his son the Rav Yaavetz in his siddur page 60 — see there. And Bach the Mechaber or 4 and similarly Chayei Adam kelal 133 end seif 21 that in community Vilna they practice per the Gra to wear Shabbat garments and change another garment — end quote. And similarly Or Chaim on Chayei Adam there that in many communities they wear all Shabbat garments and change nothing even on Shabbat when Tisha B'Av falls — see there. And it is obvious that what he wrote "in many communities," etc. — means Ashkenaz communities; but Sephardic communities all practice not to change this Shabbat from other Shabboses as Beit Yosef and Radbaz wrote. Nevertheless Shulchan Gavoah wrote they do not wear white garments or important ones like festival garments; and similarly Yafeh Lalev part 3 seif 2 that their custom is not to wear whites like other summer Shabboses but dyed ones like winter Shabbos dress — end quote; and this is what we wrote per their custom; but there are those who change nothing and wear whites too like other Shabboses, only not festival garments; and see below siman 552 seif 10:
