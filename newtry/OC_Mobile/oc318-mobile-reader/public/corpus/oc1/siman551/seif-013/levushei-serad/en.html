S.k. 19. That there is no joy, etc. The prohibition of laundry when he is not wearing it: the Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 12 that it appears as if he diverts his mind from the mourning; and that is also his intent here in the word "joy," that is to say that there is in this no diversion of the mind from the mourning; on the contrary, he is distressed in his soul; and it is easy.
