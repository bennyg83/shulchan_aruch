Seif 13, and with the beard, etc. As stated in the third chapter of Moed Katan regarding the moustache: from corner to corner, whatever interferes may be removed. Rabbi Akiva says even by the moustache, whatever interferes. The halacha follows Rabbi Ami. See Toras HaAdam, the Rosh, and Yoreh Deah siman 390, seif 1.
