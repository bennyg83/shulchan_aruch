Magen Avraham, s.k. 18. Know, for behold. This is the language of Rav Sheshet in Ta'anis folio 29, to prove not like Rav Nachman who permits to launder and leave [garments]: for if so, why were all the launderers who were in Rav's house idle; and therefore the Magen Avraham wrote this very language to prove that even one who goes out on a journey is forbidden to launder.
