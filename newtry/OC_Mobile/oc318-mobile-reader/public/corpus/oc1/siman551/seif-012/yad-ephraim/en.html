Seif katan 12. 'For it appears that he diverts his attention, etc.' It appears that he means that although a mourner within thirty days may launder, here it is forbidden because it is public conduct, and communal mourning is treated more stringently when it appears that people are diverting their attention. Regarding haircutting, no reason need be given, since every mourner within thirty days is forbidden. See Yevamos 43 and Tosafot there. More likely, it refers to the words, 'even if he does not wish to wear it now,' for this is prohibited because it appears that he is diverting his attention. It is possible that the notation should be placed on the word 'even.'
