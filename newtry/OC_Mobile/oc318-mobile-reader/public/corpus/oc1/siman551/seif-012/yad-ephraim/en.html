comment 12: appears as distracting himself, etc. It appears he means: even though a mourner within seven days is permitted laundering, nevertheless here it is forbidden as publicizing; one should be stringent in mourning of the community lest they appear as distracting themselves; not so for ointment — need not give a reason, since any mourner within seven days is forbidden. See Yevamos daf 43 and Tosafos there. More it appears it refers to what is written "even if he does not wish to wear it now," etc. On this he wrote the reason appears distracting, etc. And perhaps emendation is needed and a mark on the word "even."
