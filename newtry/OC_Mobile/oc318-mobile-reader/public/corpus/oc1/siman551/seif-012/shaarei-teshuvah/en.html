To cut [nails], see Baer Heitev; and see in Responsa Eitan HaEzrachi, simanim 48 and 49, on this matter, who wrote in the name of Maharam of Lublin to forbid during the days of the Sefirah and during Bein HaMetzarim; and likewise in the name of the Gaon Moreinu HaRav Rabbi Avraham Ashkenazi, of blessed memory, that the custom in the land of Ashkenaz is not to cut nails during Bein HaMetzarim; and he himself, of blessed memory, wrote: I was a youth and I have also aged, and I have not seen in our country people practicing [thus], neither in Sefirah nor during Bein HaMetzarim; and one who is lenient has not lost, for since the later authorities of our time did not mention the prohibition, presumably they would permit as well; see there. And see in Yad Ephraim, what I wrote in the name of Eliyah Rabbah on this; and see in Dagul Merevavah that it is permitted to comb with a comb, not like Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, for regarding a mourner it is permitted even within seven; and see in the Shach that even there it is specifically permitted for a woman but not for a man; and nevertheless it appears that here it is lighter, and there is no prohibition with a comb; and so the people practice.
