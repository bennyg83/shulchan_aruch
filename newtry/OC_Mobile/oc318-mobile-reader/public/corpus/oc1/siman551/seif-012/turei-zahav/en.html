All who belong to the meal. This means anyone who would ordinarily go to this meal because of kinship or friendship. Those who come only to drink are doing a mitzvah that comes through a transgression. In the week of Tisha B'Av only the principals of the mitzvah and an exact minyan should eat, though there are lenient views. One who is lenient at a mitzvah meal to allow the invited guests to eat has not lost, and the exact minyan mentioned by the Rama means in addition to the relatives who truly belong to the meal.
