(85) Of a dead person and of this week: that is to say, that day thirty of mourning was completed within the week in which Tisha B'Av falls to be within it.
(86) Is lenient with a razor: because this is not beautification.
(87) But not with scissors: and this is specifically when day thirty falls during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls; but when it fell earlier than this, it is permitted to cut hair and launder even for us who practice a prohibition in this in general: here it is permitted since his mourning periods pressed upon him; so wrote the Taz in the name of the Bach; but Eliyah Rabbah holds that one should be lenient only when day thirty fell before Rosh Chodesh; and so wrote several later authorities.
