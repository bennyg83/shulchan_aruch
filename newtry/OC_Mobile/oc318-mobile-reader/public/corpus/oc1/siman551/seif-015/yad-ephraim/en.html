Seif katan 16. It further appears to me that since Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds in the Gemara that the entire week is forbidden, the custom follows his view. Therefore, even if the fifteenth of Av falls on Friday, the prohibition would continue. This requires study, for according to this, what is the law when Tishah B'Av is postponed? One may answer that even Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel forbids only the actual week in which Tishah B'Av occurs, but not when the fast is postponed. I wrote this in the margin many years ago. Recently the second edition of Responsa Noda BiYehudah was printed, and I found there that the Gaon Rabbi Yeshayah Berlin raised this point. The author responded that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel forbids it even when the fast is postponed; see there. In my humble opinion, this does not appear correct, and I have abbreviated.
