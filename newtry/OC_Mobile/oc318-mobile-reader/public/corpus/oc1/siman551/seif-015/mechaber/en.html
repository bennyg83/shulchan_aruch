One who is doubled by a mourning over a dead relative and this week (i.e. a person lost a relative and the Shloshim feel the week of Tisha B'av increasing his or her time unable to get a haircut) and his hair is heavy on him (i.e. becoming overly long), he can lighten with a razor but not scissors. And wash his clothes with water but not with carbonate nor sand (i.e. cleaning product).
