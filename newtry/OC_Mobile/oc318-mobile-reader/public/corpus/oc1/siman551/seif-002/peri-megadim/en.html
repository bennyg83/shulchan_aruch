See the Taz: permission of a breach: even though there is no danger, only monetary loss, it is permitted; and see siman 540 in Magen Avraham; and it is difficult from Hilchos Choshen Mishpat, [where] something that would be lost is permitted; and one may say: there he dismantles it, but does not build it when there is no danger, for it is not relevant that he would refrain, etc.; and here, whenever there is monetary loss, if it falls he will break the wall and it will be ruined more, it is permitted afterward to build even [a building] of joy as well; and this requires further study.
