They reduce buying and selling, etc. See in Mishnah Berurah that according to many poskim the intent is that he should occupy himself only for his livelihood; and see in Peri Megadim, who wrote that a Jew whose livelihood is musical instruments by a non-Jew in a feast-house: it appears that for his livelihood it is permitted; see there. And see in Derech HaChaim, from which it implies that Peri Megadim permits only from the seventeenth of Tammuz until Rosh Chodesh.

That is, even if he wants to leave over an amah by an amah; for otherwise it is forever forbidden according to the view of the Tur, as below in siman 560; and according to the Rambam, who holds that even through leaving over an amah by an amah it is forever forbidden to build a building of drawing and carving, as is explained there in the Beis Yosef, one must say that here from Rosh Chodesh Av and onward it is forbidden even if he wants to plaster beneath it with a plaster of clay first [Machatzit HaShekel in siman 560].
