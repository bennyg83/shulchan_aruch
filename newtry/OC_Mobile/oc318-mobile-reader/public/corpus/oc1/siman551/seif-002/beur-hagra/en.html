Seif 2, from Rosh Chodesh, etc. In Yevamos, the teaching refers to before the earlier period, and the explanation of from Rosh Chodesh is as stated there, from Rosh Chodesh until the fast, etc.
Of joy. Tosafot writes so there. In the Yerushalmi, at the end of Taanis, Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi said that this applies to a building of joy, but if his wall is leaning he may demolish and rebuild it. The Bavli also teaches that the building meant is a building of joy, and likewise planting. This is why examples are given for building and planting.
Or a building, etc., or types of myrtle, etc. So writes the Rambam. The wedding house is mentioned not because of the wedding itself, but because such a house is decorated. So too, a pleasure pavilion is mentioned because myrtle and tent-like plantings are planted there. The Ran writes that the Yerushalmi's case of a leaning wall implies that not only actual joy is forbidden, but anything for adornment is forbidden. The Tur explains otherwise, and the Rosh explains similarly. Therefore he writes that if it was, etc., although, etc.
And for the need, etc. So wrote the Gaon, since these matters were included together with business dealings; just as business dealings refer to optional matters, so too here.
And even on Tisha B'Av, etc. This is from the Yerushalmi in Taanis and Kesubos and other places.
