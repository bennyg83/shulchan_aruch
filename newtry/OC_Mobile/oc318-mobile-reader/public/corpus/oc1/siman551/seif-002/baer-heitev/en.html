Joy. This refers also to commerce such as wedding needs; but other commerce is permitted as usual — Beit Yosef, Bach; Maharil wrote: brokers should not carry silver vessels to the broker in the city, for joy is for onlookers. Where they practice prohibition in all commerce, release is needed — see Yoreh De'ah siman 214; but where they practice reducing all commerce, release does not help since some poskim already hold so as that view. Siman 554 s.k. 22 implies reduction in all commerce — see there; Beit Yosef in name of Ran: any building not needed except mere profit is forbidden; if he contracted with a gentile to paint his house — permitted to paint; if he can appease him with little to wait until after Tisha B'Av — excellent, Maharil; it appears permitted, for it is a rabbinic mitzvah.
