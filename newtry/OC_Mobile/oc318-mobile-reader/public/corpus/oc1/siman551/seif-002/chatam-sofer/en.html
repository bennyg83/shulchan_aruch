Marginal note: it appears to me that on Shabbos, when his wife goes to the synagogue, it is preferable to a father before his son's wedding, for a husband is under obligation, which is not so regarding the father; although the groom precedes the woman's husband regarding obligation, see Magen Avraham, siman 282; and the husband is not similar to a groom before the wedding when they sing to him; nevertheless he is also not similar to the father of the groom, and it is much preferable, as is understood, as above.
