NB it appears to me: on Shabbat when his wife goes to the synagogue it is preferable to the father before his son's wedding, for the husband is obligated unlike the father; although the groom precedes the husband's wife regarding obligation — see Magen Avraham siman 282; and the husband is not comparable to the groom before the wedding when they sing for him; nevertheless also for the father of the groom it is not comparable and much preferable as is clear above.
