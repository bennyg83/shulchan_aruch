His intent is that there he wrote that it is forbidden to lead the animal with a bell in the public domain, for it appears as one going to the market to sell, and it is forbidden because of appearance; and in a courtyard it is permitted; it implies that we do not hold like Rav who said that wherever the Sages forbade because of appearance, even in innermost chambers it is forbidden; or as the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 301, s.k. 56, in the name of the Beis Yosef, that even according to Rav it is specifically in a place where they would suspect him of doing a Torah prohibition that it is forbidden even in innermost chambers; see there; and here everything is rabbinic; and it is easy.
