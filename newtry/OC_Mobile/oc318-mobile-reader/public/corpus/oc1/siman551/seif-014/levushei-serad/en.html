His intent is that there it is written one may not lead an animal in pairs in the public domain, lest it appear as one going to market to sell, forbidden due to mar'it ayin; in a courtyard it is permitted — implying we do not hold like Rav who said wherever the Sages prohibited due to mar'it ayin, even in innermost chambers it is forbidden; or as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 541 small seif 56 in the name of Beit Yosef that even for Rav, only where they would suspect one is doing a Torah prohibition is it forbidden even in innermost chambers — see there. Here everything is rabbinic — and it is simple.
