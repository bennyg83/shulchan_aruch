Seif katan 15. 'Even if he told him to launder, etc.' Eliyah Rabbah is inclined to permit where he told him to launder after Tishah B'Av. It is possible that Magen Avraham intended to say, 'even if it is impossible for him to launder after Tishah B'Av,' and one letter is missing. The text would then read 'even if it is impossible to launder,' an abbreviation, corresponding to what he writes below in seif katan 18 when disputing Bach regarding one departing on a distant journey.
