The Bach wrote that this is specifically during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls; before then it is permitted in a case of consecutive mourning, when his thirty days ended during these days. Tosafot writes that although linen garments may be laundered on the festival, Rashi was stringent not to give sheets to be laundered during the week of Tisha B'Av before the fast. Where Tisha B'Av falls on Thursday, they allowed laundering and haircutting after midday for the honor of Shabbos, since waiting until Friday would cause Shabbos hardship. The Beit Yosef rejects this, but the Taz explains that after midday belongs somewhat to the following day, as in preparations made then; nevertheless for us there is no practical leniency, since we are stringent even on the tenth of Av. The Bach permitted one traveling far during this week to launder linen garments for several Shabbosos if he will not be able to do so after Tisha B'Av.
