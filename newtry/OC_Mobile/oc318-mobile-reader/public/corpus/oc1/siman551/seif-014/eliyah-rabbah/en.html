[14] Levush: Because of appearance, etc. It is possible to be lenient privately; see siman 305:11. In a place where non-Jews have distinctive garments and it is recognizable that they belong to non-Jews, it is permitted. Magen Avraham. The same applies where the launderer launders only for non-Jews and not for Jews. Terumas HaDeshen, siman 152.
