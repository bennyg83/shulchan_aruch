Seif 14, it is forbidden, etc. See Tosafot in the fourth chapter of Yevamos. The early texts read that even cutting hair and laundering for minors are forbidden during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls. This differs from ordinary mourning, where adults may cut hair and launder for minors. Here even one who has only one garment is restricted, showing the greater stringency.
Nevertheless, etc. Because this is their normal growth need. Even on Yom Kippur adults may do for children whatever is necessary for their upbringing, as stated at the end of Yoma.
