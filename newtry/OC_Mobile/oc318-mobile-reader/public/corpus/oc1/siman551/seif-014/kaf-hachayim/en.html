(14) Custom of Israel not to say on Shabbat Chazon any kinah as in siddurim; so Egypt and large cities in Turkey; even Tisha B'Av on Shabbat they place on the table meat stew aphtora to honor Shabbat — who set a mouth to weep and mourn?
