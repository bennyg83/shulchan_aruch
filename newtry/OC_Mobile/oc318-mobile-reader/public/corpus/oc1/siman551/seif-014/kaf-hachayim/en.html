10) Ai custom not to say on Shabbat a vision of any lamentation in Siddurim, etc., the custom of the land of Egypt and large volumes and towns that were written in Dafi' in the 19th century that fell on Shabbat to our messengers in the Sharam Aftorah Damshalem to do a great deal on Shabbat day and who is there here to weep and Abel also brought when Konan and what is also in Parsia Kabbal with a holy Shabbat day, it is certainly appropriate to cancel this custom and honor Shabbat. In Rabbi O. 2. And also in Tsho'h Zakan Aharon C. 38 extended this and wrote to forbid Ya'ush. And so on in Tsho'h Radbaz 13 C. 1855.
