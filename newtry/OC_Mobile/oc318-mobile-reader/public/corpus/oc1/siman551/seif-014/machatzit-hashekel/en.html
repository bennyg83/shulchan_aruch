(S.k. 14) And likewise for the honor, etc. It is permitted to launder. For behold he wrote: a woman who wears whites is permitted to launder and to wear, etc.; and on this he wrote "and likewise for the honor of Shabbos," implying that it is one law for him with a woman who wears whites.
It implies that regarding laundry as well they practiced, etc. From what Darkei Moshe wrote: they practiced a prohibition even regarding bathing. And from the language "even" it implies all the more so laundry.
