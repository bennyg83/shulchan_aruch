(81) To cut hair for minors: for regarding minors as well chinuch applies to them, whether because of mourning or because of anguish of soul.
(82) During the week in which, etc.: see in Eliyah Rabbah that for us one should be stringent regarding haircutting from the seventeenth of Tammuz and laundry from Rosh Chodesh; and in Chayei Adam he wrote that for the need of minors one should not be stringent even according to our custom except during this week.
(83) However, garments, etc.: and nevertheless they should not launder many together. And they should launder privately if they do not launder upon the river [Magen Avraham].
(84) It is entirely permitted: for in the laundry of these there is no [issue] of joy [Levush].
