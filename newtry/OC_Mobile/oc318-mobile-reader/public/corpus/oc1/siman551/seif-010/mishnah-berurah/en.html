(63) They are permitted in a cooked dish: but fat of meat has the law of meat itself, and it is forbidden to eat a cooked dish that has fat in it; and nowadays the world practices to forbid even a cooked dish of meat; and specifically a cooked dish of meat, but if it was only cooked in a pot of meat, it is obvious that it is permitted [later authorities]. And it appears: whenever meat fell into a cooked dish and there is sixty [parts], it is obvious that it is permitted.
(64) And they are forbidden in poultry meat: and nevertheless one who cannot eat dairy foods is permitted to eat poultry meat or salted meat of three days. And a nursing woman whose milk is bad for the infant when she does not eat meat: one should be lenient even with animal meat.
(65) And salted meat: even from a long time ago, for nevertheless there is in it some joy.
(66) Fermenting: that is, within three [days] of its treading, which is sweet and not strong and disqualified for the altar; and likewise all these aforementioned: nevertheless our fathers accepted upon themselves to forbid all kinds of meat and wine.
(67) And it is permitted to drink, etc.: for Bahag did not accept upon themselves as regarding other seudos mitzvah.
(68) And they practice to be stringent, etc.: since it is possible to have a child taste it, which is not so regarding other seudos mitzvah.
(69) Not to drink wine in Birkas HaMazon: it is obvious that all this speaks of Birkas HaMazon of a weekday; but regarding Birkas HaMazon of Shabbos of the three meals, it is obvious that it is permitted.
(70) Rather they give to a child: who has reached chinuch, and he should drink most of the cup; and specifically if he has not yet reached [the age] to mourn for Jerusalem; and specifically in this which is a mitzvah; but without a mitzvah, even if he does not know how to mourn, meat and wine are forbidden when he is healthy.
(71) To drink Havdalah: but Birkas HaMazon he should recite without a cup, since there are poskim that even three who ate do not need a cup; at least in such a case one may rely on them.
(72) And at a seudas mitzvah, etc.: and in this even the cup of Birkas HaMazon one should permit to drink, for it is no worse than wine within the meal which was permitted.
(73) And a siyum of a tractate: and nevertheless if the siyum did not happen to occur in his learning, he should not rush or delay for this. And also if he would not make a meal on other days, it is possible that he should not make it now either. However, if it happened fittingly, even those who did not learn with them are permitted to eat, if they would go and come also at another time because of friendship.
(74) And a betrothal meal: even though it is explained above in se'if 2 that it is forbidden to make a betrothal meal, it is possible that it speaks not at the time of betrothal, or not in the house of his betrothed, where there is not so much joy; and there are those who delete these two words [Magen Avraham].
(75) All who belong to the meal: that is, anyone who would go at another time to this meal, whether because of kinship or because he is his friend; and women who belong to the meal, in a place where the way is to invite them to the meal, are also permitted. And Chayei Adam wrote that the wife of the host of the meal is permitted even at a siyum of a tractate, and likewise his sons. And one to whom they send to his house is forbidden for him to eat.
(76) Not to add: that is, one who did not come because of kinship or love of friends, only to eat and drink: and it is a transgression in his hand.
(77) Only a limited number: that is, besides the relatives disqualified as witnesses and besides the baalei mitzvah, it is permitted to add ten because of friendship, and the rest eat dairy foods. And behold in the Levush he wrote that from Rosh Chodesh until Tisha B'Av they take a limited number, and there are those who take ten besides the relatives who belong to the host of the meal. And behold in Chayei Adam he copied like the Levush; but in Derech HaChaim it implies that one may practice to be lenient like the view of the Rama, that from Rosh Chodesh until the week in which Tisha B'Av falls it is permitted to invite all who belong to the meal, and even several minyanim. And if the milah or the siyum of a tractate falls on Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos, according to everyone it is permitted to invite all guests that he wants.
(78) On Erev Tisha B'Av it is permitted: provided that he make it before midday.
