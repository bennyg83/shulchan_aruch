In Magen Avraham, s.k. 42; and in my humble opinion one may say according to the reasoning of the Taz to recite the blessing every day during Bein HaMetzarim, lest a person die before he fulfills the mitzvah; and one may say: before we permit on a weekday for this reason, we should permit on Shabbos at least; and then, in such a short time until Shabbos, we are not concerned for death; and therefore on a weekday he should not recite the blessing, and on Shabbos one must necessarily permit; and see Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 116, to distinguish in such a case between a short time and a long time; and see Yaaros Devash, third derashah of part 2, that until Shabbos it is a short time; see there; and since on Shabbos there is in any case more reason to recite the blessing than on a weekday, therefore we permitted on Shabbos and forbade on a weekday. It is well, since until Shabbos we are not concerned for death and he will recite the blessing then, as above it is correct; and see Magen Avraham, siman 568, s.k. 13, and Machatzit HaShekel there. [From the Gaon Ksav Sofer.] And see Responsa Ksav Sofer, Orach Chaim, siman 103.
