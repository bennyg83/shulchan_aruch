(10) In the Rama's gloss: They wear Shabbos garments. Regarding a haircut for a baal bris, it goes without saying that on Tisha B'Av itself it is forbidden, God forbid, to permit it; even from Rosh Chodesh until the fast it is forbidden. Beis Yehuda vol. 2 siman 85, and so he wrote in his work Mateh Yehuda on this siman. So too wrote Eliyah Rabbah os 28, except that he wrote that before the week in which Tisha B'Av falls it is perhaps possible to permit. So too wrote Mish'meres Zahav os 13, and so wrote the Zera Avraham ibid. The Machberes os 2 cited their words. So too wrote the Noda B'Yehuda, first edition, siman 28. R' Yaakov Emden in his responsa vol. 3 siman 2, 3 decisively ruled it forbidden, see there. However, in the responsa of the Chasam Sofer siman 158 he challenged the words of the Noda B'Yehuda and concluded that it is fully permitted even during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls, and wrote that this is the common practice, see there. In the responsa Binyan Tziyon siman 31 he wrote that when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos one may certainly rely on permission to take a haircut even on erev Shabbos, see there. So too wrote in the responsa Or Ne'elam siman 10 that when Tisha B'Av falls on Sunday and the bris is on the preceding Thursday, since there is no "week in which Tisha B'Av falls" and there is no prohibition by the strict law of the Gemara, it is permitted for the mohel and the baal bris to take a haircut in honor of the bris, see there; the Sha'arei Teshuvah os 1 cited his words. So too wrote Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 122 os 15 that before Shabbos Chazon it is permitted, but afterward it is forbidden. It seems that since there are many opinions on this matter, during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls one should be strict since it is the strict law of the Gemara. But from the seventeenth of Tammuz onward, and likewise when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos or Sunday, which does not have the status of "the week in which Tisha B'Av falls" according to the unqualified ruling of the Shulchan Aruch, as stated below in seif 4, one may be lenient if necessary. All this applies to those who have the custom to be strict regarding haircuts from the seventeenth of Tammuz as stated below in seif 4 in the Rama's gloss, or those who have the custom to be strict from Rosh Chodesh. But for those who follow the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch that the prohibition only applies during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls, in any case it is permitted if it is not that week.
