And a siyum of a tractate. See what we wrote in Mishnah Berurah, that even those who did not learn with them are permitted to eat; and that is from Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah; and see in the siddur of Rabbi Yaakov Emden, whose view is that that means those who support the learners with their money, and also the gabba'im who oversee them and their attendants: all these are permitted even if they are many; but to invite others besides these because of honor and love or for some benefit of his is an absolute prohibition. And nevertheless it is obvious that if those who come assist in making the meal, it is certainly permitted, for he is included in what the Gemara said: "May it come to me that when the Rabbis would finish a tractate I would make a festive day for the Rabbis"; and as the Magen Avraham wrote.
