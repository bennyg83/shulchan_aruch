From Rosh Chodesh until the fast. The last chapter of Taanis records a dispute whether the prohibition extends only until the fast or through the whole week, and Rava rules that after the fast it is permitted. Some who continue meat until Shabbos Nachamu act improperly if they present it as the law. Still, one who wishes to be stringent privately may do so, provided he does not say that he follows the rejected view against Rava's ruling.
