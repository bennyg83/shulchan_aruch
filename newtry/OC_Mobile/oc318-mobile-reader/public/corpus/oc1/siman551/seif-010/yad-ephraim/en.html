Ibid. 'Meaning great joy, etc.' This requires study. Why is this needed? Even some joy should suffice, since the concern that another may precede him does not apply to marriage. Perhaps the practical difference is where one cannot become betrothed unless he marries immediately. Even then marriage is forbidden because it contains great joy. Alternatively, if marriage contained only some joy, it might be permitted even without the concern that another may precede him, because of the mitzvah of procreation. He therefore says that there is no permission because marriage contains great joy.
