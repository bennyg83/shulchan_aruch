From a manuscript of Ashkenaz responsa.
And that which is stated above in se'if 2, that they do not make a feast of betrothal: it appears to my humble opinion to distinguish, that here it speaks of a betrothal of a mitzvah, for example where he has no wife and children, as the Ran wrote in the name of Rav Hai, and the Beis Yosef brought it in this siman.
