A circumcision that is, etc. See above, siman 559, seif 8, that even on Tisha B'Av itself this is permitted. As written there, since it is our Yom Tov, they were permitted to eat when Tisha B'Av was postponed; all the more so on the Shabbos that falls in the week of Tisha B'Av. Just as they permitted laundering on Thursday for the honor of Shabbos, even wearing the garments, so too here. See there.
But without this, etc. Certainly during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls it is forbidden according to the law, for even laundered garments are forbidden, all the more so new ones. During the thirty days of mourning laundering is permitted, but a mourner is forbidden laundering all seven days, and the thirty-day restriction applies only to pressing. New garments are forbidden according to the earlier opinions. All the more so where laundering itself is forbidden; and even from Rosh Chodesh, since the custom is to forbid laundering as written in seif 3 in the gloss.
Even, etc. This is merely a stringency. Even if laundering were forbidden from Rosh Chodesh, they permitted laundering for the honor of Shabbos, and according to one opinion even on Tisha B'Av itself. It cannot be more severe than Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos, where even eating, which is more severe, is allowed as at the meal of Shlomo. This is the meaning of one who has a wedding, etc.
If not, etc. This is because of the verse 'He has broken His word,' and is merely a stringency.
