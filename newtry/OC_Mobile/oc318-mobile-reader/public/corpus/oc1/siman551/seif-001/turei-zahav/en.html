We say in the Gemara that Tishah BeAv does not depend on whether people want to fast, because troubles were doubled on it. Tosafot asked: but on the seventeenth of Tammuz too five troubles occurred. They answered that the destruction of the Temple is more severe. One may also answer that on Tishah BeAv one trouble was doubled twice.
(1) We reduce business. The Beit Yosef wrote: nowadays most people do not customarily reduce business at all from when Av enters. It appears that they understand that building and planting were prohibited only when done for joy, like the first opinion our master mentioned, and they understand that business was prohibited only when it is for joy, like the view Tosafot rejected. According to this one must be careful about joyful business, such as wedding needs, etc.; end quote. But this does not follow, for we see that even regarding wedding needs we have not heard that people refrain. Tosafot's rejection of this view, that they prohibited only making that feast, is certainly a true rejection; how could we rely on such a rejected view? It appears that the reason for the lenient custom is like what I wrote in siman 539 seif 4, giving a reason to permit business on Chol HaMoed even when one has money, because the taxes of the king and officials are upon us, and I brought proof from Tosafot in Perek Eizehu Neshekh. Here too we have this permission regarding business from Av onward, and therefore people do not reduce it. I later found this in the words of my father-in-law z"l in the name of Maharshal.
