We say. The Gemara says that Tisha B'Av is not dependent on whether people wish to fast, because calamities were doubled on it. Tosafot asks that five calamities also occurred on the seventeenth of Tammuz, and answers that the destruction of the Beis HaMikdash is more severe, or that on Tisha B'Av one calamity was doubled.
One reduces business dealings. The Beit Yosef writes that most people now do not reduce business from the beginning of Av, apparently understanding that only business of joy is forbidden. Yet we see that people do not refrain even from wedding needs. The custom to be lenient seems based on the allowance for business on Chol HaMoed because of taxes and royal obligations; the same applies here.
