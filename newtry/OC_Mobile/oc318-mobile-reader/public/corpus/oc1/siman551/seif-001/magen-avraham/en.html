One reduces joy. Meaning, one does not rejoice at all, as Tosafot writes in the first chapter of Megillah.
He should evade him. Until Rosh Chodesh Elul, as is implied in the Gemara.
And the participant in the covenant. It appears that this refers specifically to the one who holds the child on his knees at the time of the circumcision, as stated in Yoreh Deah siman 265, seif 11. The one who brings the child in and out is not included. Although women are also accustomed to wear Shabbos garments, that is because this is their primary mitzvah. Certainly during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls, when according to the law one may not wear white garments, permission applies only to the one holding the child. Even the tallis is not changed.
Only the shirt. This is permitted even when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos. One who is stringent not to change it follows a foolish practice, for it is worn only because of perspiration.
The curtain. The same applies to table covers and coverings.
Shabbos garments. New garments, however, are forbidden. Maharil made a celebration for his son and wore a Shabbos garment in honor of his son the groom.
