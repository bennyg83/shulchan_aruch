And there is — Beit Yosef in name of Rosh: perhaps even without blessing he fulfills; halacha follows R' Yosi against Shmuel; therefore on stolen on other days he must return, take, and bless — he does not fulfill at all through stolen; for Rambam on other days he fulfills through stolen only without blessing on it like se'ah of wheat — one blesses only "who forms" and for practical law requires study if he bought stolen on other days whether he may bless afterward; doubt in blessings is lenient; in seif 1 he acquires through change — he fulfills and does not bless on it l'chatchila; if he took he fulfilled and it is forbidden to bless afterward:
