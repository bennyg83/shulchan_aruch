The invalid ones. Meaning because of a blemish; but those that are not of their species and a stolen one are invalid even in pressing circumstances. This is the consensus of the later authorities; see Magen Avraham. An esrog of orlah in Eretz Yisrael is invalid, while orlah of the diaspora is valid. An esrog that became forbidden because it absorbed a prohibited substance should be validated on the other days. A demai esrog is valid; see Magen Avraham, small paragraph 19.
With other. Mishnah Berurah, siman 17, wrote that all four dry species are valid in pressing circumstances. But if one recited a blessing over completely dry ones when completely moist ones were available, even after the fact he has not fulfilled his obligation. See Magen Avraham.
