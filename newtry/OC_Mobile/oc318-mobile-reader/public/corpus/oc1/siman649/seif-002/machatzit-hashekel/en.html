(s.k. 2) He should not bless etc. — perhaps there through change of name and despair it is better. Per simple understanding researchers say Magen Avraham distinguishes change of name from change of action; so in Chayei Adam 494:6 and Avodat Yisrael — he challenged in Avodat Yisrael why Gemara first challenges acquisition through change of action of binding. In Chayei Adam he added: clear in all poskim change of name and change of action are equal for acquisition. But in my heart it is not so — Magen Avraham agrees there is no distinction except his intent to distinguish: in Shulchan Aruch before despair as in s.k. 3 afterward, only acquisition through change, therefore no blessing. But in the Gemara there are two — despair and change of name is better, and so change of action (Maharsha on Rashi brought this in s.k. 3) — obvious in my humble opinion. On Magen Avraham's difficulty on Gemara's difficulty: why acquire through change of name and not change of action — perhaps following Tosafot and not placing Gemara's prior difficulty on change of action; clearer in Bava Kamma at length.
