Section 2. And one who is prohibited by vow from benefit, etc. Since they are prohibited for benefit, we do not read 'for yourselves' with respect to them. See Rashi 35a, s.v. lefi she-ein bah din mamon, etc. And that which must be said regarding an asherah and an idolatrous city, that they are considered crushed, etc., is because of the remaining days, as Tosafot wrote there, s.v. lefi; but on the first day they are prohibited because of 'for yourselves.' The same applies here: even though he gave it to him as a gift, nevertheless it is prohibited, as stated in Nedarim 34b, and it is not his. If he betrothed a woman with them she is not betrothed, as stated there 47: Rami bar Chama inquired; come and hear, one who betroths, etc. This shows that if it is prohibited for benefit, she is not betrothed. There.
Or from his lulav, etc. Meaning, even though he can request release from the vow regarding it, just as with impure terumah, even though he can request release regarding it; and since it teaches the case without qualification, it implies even if they are in the hands of an Israelite.
