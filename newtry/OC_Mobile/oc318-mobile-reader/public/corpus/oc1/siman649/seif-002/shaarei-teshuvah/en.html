(b)
In the gloss: and one prohibited by vow from benefit, etc. See Responsa Shaar Ephraim, siman 38, who challenged the Rashba's proof regarding one prohibited by vow from benefit, that we do not say "since he could ask to be released," from impure terumah, where we do not say it. He objected that with impure terumah, if he asks to be released from it, it reverts to tevel, and one cannot fulfill the mitzvah with it. See Responsa Pri Megadim, vol. 2, what they asked him on this, and he z"l wrote to defend the words of Shaar Ephraim. But now we have merited the hidden light of Responsa Rashba, vol. 4, where he too wrote to one sage and discussed this difficulty at length, that it reverts to tevel; see there. Part of that responsum was copied in Shaar HaMelekh, who had this responsum in manuscript; see there.
