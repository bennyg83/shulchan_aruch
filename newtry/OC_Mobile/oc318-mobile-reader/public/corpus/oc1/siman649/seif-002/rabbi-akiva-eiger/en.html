However it seems impossible to explain so. For Rashi is challenged: why do they challenge and acquire through change of action — it is still mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah; Maharsha answered: since it was already acquired through despair, when he then acquires through change of action it is not mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah — proves Rav Huna holds despair acquires. Therefore what is written "and change of ownership in your hands" is not precise. Though Rashi wrote one side that Rabbah holds despair does not acquire — that is Rabbah's side that we do not say mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah; the other side Rabbah holds despair acquires, Rabbah's side mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah — proves contrary to Rashi that even for others it is mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah, else Maharsha's difficulty remains: why challenge and acquire through change of action — perhaps Rabbah holds despair does not acquire and it is mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah; nevertheless when it came to others in prohibition, it is mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah — proves they do not hold mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah; if despair acquires as Maharshal answered, Gemara's difficulty remains: why challenge — perhaps merchants cut, despair in their hands; proves they do not hold mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah; if despair acquires when he then does acquisition through change of action it is not mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah per Maharshal. Still difficult why they challenge: when merchants shear, let there be despair in their hands — perhaps Rabbah holds despair does not acquire; if R' Elazar holds despair acquires, it is still mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah until change of action — end of his words in brief.
