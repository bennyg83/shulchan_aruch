They are invalid if stolen, etc. If the theft is only rabbinic, such as an object found by a deaf-mute, an imbecile, or a minor, concerning which we rule that taking it constitutes theft because of the ways of peace, or a similar case, the author of Shaar HaMelech, chapter 8 of Hilchos Lulav, was uncertain whether one fulfills his obligation with it. Since according to the Torah it is regarded as ‘yours,’ he discussed the matter, and his view tends toward the conclusion that one does not fulfill his obligation, because it is a mitzvah that comes through a transgression; see there. Machazik Berachah, sec. 1. Shaarei Teshuvah, sec. 1. However, the view of Erech HaShulchan, sec. 3, is to permit it. So too is the view of Beis HaShoevah, sec. 2.
