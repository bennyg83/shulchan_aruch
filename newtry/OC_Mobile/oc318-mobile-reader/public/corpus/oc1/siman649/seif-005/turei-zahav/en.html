It is unreasonable these versions disagree whether one must cut what mice ate — all agree it is repulsive; what proof does the second version bring from R' Chiya who ate from it — how compare to visible mouse eating; why not cut the repulsive part; what is Rav's difficulty in the first version from R' Chiya — Rav forbids while not cut; we did not yet know the reason is repulsiveness and we thought Rav forbids even after cutting the repulsive part — if so why two versions — perhaps both hold mice are repulsive; perhaps the second also holds one must cut; Tur and Beit Yosef disqualify if mice pierced all seven days unless the piercing is not repulsive — see there at length.
