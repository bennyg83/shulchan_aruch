Se'if 5 Because of the blemishes that we explained, or because of robbery and theft. This means disqualifications of beauty and deficiency, and this follows the approach of the Ramban at the beginning of the first chapter, that what we say, that beauty is invalid on the second day, means in the Temple, but not outside the Temple, where it is only rabbinic; see there. What is written in the gloss, that if it was dry or spotted it is invalid, and likewise what is written in the gloss that chazazis is invalid all seven days, is a different approach, the approach of the Rosh, that beauty is invalid on the second day rabbinically. The Rema should have written "and there are those who say." Responsa Pnei Yehoshua, siman 3.
