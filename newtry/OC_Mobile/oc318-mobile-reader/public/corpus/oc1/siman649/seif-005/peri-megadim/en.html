All this. See the Turei Zahav, paragraph 3, that when it changed it did not become repulsive, like planted flax. And if the tree became forbidden, if you will say he planted it and afterward worshiped it, as we intend to say, with Hashem's help, in the Magen Avraham, paragraph 11, here it is speaking where a gentile afterward nullified it by cutting. See siman 11 in the Turei Zahav, paragraph 7, that it is not a mitzvah that comes through a transgression because he did not perform a transgression. This requires analysis, for it implies that even if he performed a transgression, since it has no connection to the mitzvah it is not a mitzvah that comes through a transgression; see Tosafos in Sukkah. And regarding gentile merchants in robbery, anything that he acquired before assistance to the mitzvah is not a mitzvah that comes through a transgression, even if he robbed it and smoothed it, only he should not recite a blessing over it, while others recite a blessing over them. With Hashem's help, it will be explained further in the Magen Avraham.
