Lulav, etc. He was precise and wrote "lulav," to exclude a case where it is for the need of a lulav from birya, which is permitted, for it is also despair together with change of domain. So it is stated explicitly at the beginning of chapter Lulav HaGazul, Sukkah 30b, where it says as follows: ultimately, when gentiles themselves cut it, let there be the owners' despair in their hands and a change of domain in our hands; this was needed only regarding hoshanos of the gentiles themselves. It follows that even on the first day of Sukkos it is permitted. That which he wrote above, "except for the first day," appears to me to be speaking before despair. The Levush distinguishes in another way. Even initially it appears that one need not be particular. So too above in siman 634 se'if 3 in the gloss, it appears that one need not be particular initially if he does not steal it himself.
