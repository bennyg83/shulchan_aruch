Things that invalidate the 4 species. It contains 6 seifim: All 4 species are invalid if robbed or stolen, whether before the owner has despaired of recovering them or after he has despaired. But if one robbed an item and acquired it through an act unrelated to performance of the mitzvah, such as one who robbed a lulav and trimmed it, it is valid, because he acquired it through a physical change. Nevertheless, he should not recite a blessing over it. Some say that a robbed or stolen item is invalid only for the thief or robber himself, but for others it is valid on the remaining days other than the first day. {Rama: For this reason, one should be careful that a Jew not personally cut one of the 4 species for his lulav, because land cannot be acquired through robbery, and ordinarily non-Jews are presumed to have robbed land, so the item may come into his hand through robbery. Rather, the non-Jew should cut it and the Jew should purchase it from him. [Responsa Rashba, siman 852; Hagahot Ashiri, end of chapter Lulav HaGazul] There is no distinction in this respect between Eretz Yisrael and outside Eretz Yisrael. [Or Zarua] A lulav that a non-Jew bound and prepared is valid, just as a sukkah made by a non-Jew is valid. [Mordechai, Hilchot Ketanot]}
