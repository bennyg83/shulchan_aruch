Despair and change of domain is a mitzvah that comes through a transgression. The like of this regarding lulav is, at first glance, through binding it on Yom Tov, even though for us binding is not required. See Tosafos, Sukkah 30a, s.v. "this," and 30b, "let him acquire it initially as myrtle and now as hoshanah," meaning before Yom Tov, when the mitzvah has not yet taken effect.
