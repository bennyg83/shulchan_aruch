(Subparagraph 4) That he should not cut, etc. And it would be tevel. This depends on the dispute that the Magen Avraham wrote in subparagraph 20. It is worse than if there were only a concern of robbery, for regarding concern of robbery the Magen Avraham wrote in subparagraph 5 that after the fact he has fulfilled his obligation. But with a concern of tevel, according to one opinion he has not fulfilled his obligation even after the fact. And perhaps according to the view of the Mechaber in se'if 5, tevel is invalid even on the other days, unlike robbery, which is valid on the other days according to one opinion.
