[4] That he should not cut, etc. With an esrog in Eretz Yisrael, it becomes tevel whose processing was completed in the hand of a Jew. Rabbeinu Betzalel. An esrog of orlah, of impure terumah, or of tevel is invalid. One of demai is valid, for he can render his property ownerless and become a poor person, who is permitted to eat demai. One of terumah or of maaser sheni in Yerushalayim should not be taken, but if he took it, it is valid. Kol Bo. The rest of these laws were explained in siman 637 se'if 3.
