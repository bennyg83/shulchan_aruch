Section 4. The gardens of the youths, etc. For there is no consecration to idolatry, as stated in Avodah Zarah 51b: idolatry that had a garden, etc. See Yoreh De'ah 142:13, where he ruled that it is prohibited, as stated there 44b: Rabbah bar Ulla said, its theft is from here, etc.; at least there is adornment. In the Tur there he wrote two opinions. The first view holds that they are permitted, because they do not worship it, and they were not made for adornment but like an offering; and it is not prohibited since it is not like the service within the Temple, for the planting is the offering, and this is not comparable to the service within. This is as written at the beginning of chapter Rabbi Yishmael there, and Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote similarly here. And the Tur wrote: and some say that it is no less than an adornment, and this is reasonable. So the Shulchan Aruch ruled there, as above.
And even the tree, etc. As stated there 48a: what is an asherah? Any, etc.; Rabbi Shimon says, etc. And even according to the first tanna he said there: if one placed it beneath it and it was removed, etc.; all the more so where it was never beneath it at all.
