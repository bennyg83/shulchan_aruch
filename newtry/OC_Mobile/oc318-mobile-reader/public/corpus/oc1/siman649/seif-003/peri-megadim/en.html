From day — Taz: challenged on 41b on Rava and on 30 it implies Rava establishes stolen only on Yom Tov 1; if so a non-Jew's lulav on Yom Tov 1 is invalid as not his etc. Incidentally in Kapos Temarim 41b on Rashi: a non-Jew's lulav is an offering without nullification as Rambam Hilchot Avodah Zarah 8:9, 14:129 — only in the non-Jew himself there is nullification — see there; see Taz and Shach 34; though not like faces and 613 invalid — perhaps types and quantity required and offerings like faces, perhaps also counted in candles like menorah — see 554:11 Mishna Berurah permits ordinary use of olive oil — see there — thus not considered an offering except animal — "sacrifices of the dead" etc. Know further "for you" — yours: if he found ownerless lulav and lifted without intent to acquire, he does not fulfill — so in Kapos Temarim; must be specifically a non-Jew from day 2 onward. Borrowed does not fulfill because of liability; so too stolen for Choshen Mishpat — where there is liability we read "yours" in it; minority of "for you" excludes borrow without liability or ownerless etc.; and for those who establish stolen only on day 1, stolen on other days is valid for them.
