(א) of Israel. Israel’s report does not allow it to be canceled:

(ב) From the first day onward. This is Rava in the Gemara 41a: a non-Jew's lulav that a non-Jew used for a non-Jew — do not take; if he took, he fulfilled — Ran and Maggid explained this is on Yom Tov 2 when one fulfills through borrowing. Difficulty: they challenge Rava from a mishnah teaching a non-Jew's is invalid and answer: a non-Jew's of Moses etc.; therefore no challenge — for earlier in the chapter Rava held like R' Yochanan bar Nachmani who said on the mishnah stolen is invalid only on first Yom Tov, but on second Yom Tov since one fulfills through borrowing, one fulfills through stolen; there Rava said: always on first Yom Tov etc.; if so, a non-Jew's certainly on first Yom Tov; they are adjacent mishnayot; from Ran it seems Rava did not say "always on Yom Tov" — Rav Ashi wrote and Razah rules like R' Yochanan bar Nachmani because Rav Ashi answered on his view; this is the answer above "always on first Yom Tov"; that valid after first day though forbidden in benefit — mitzvot were not given for benefit.
