And one of an asherah. Even if he planted it and afterward worshiped it. So it appears from Tosafot, daf 32, and in Avodah Zarah daf 47.
One belonging to an Israelite is invalid. For its required measure is considered crushed.
He fulfilled from the day, etc. Even though it is prohibited for eating and benefit, nevertheless, since if it is nullified it becomes permitted for eating, he fulfilled the obligation, as stated in the Gemara regarding demai; see there.
