Siman 1, even though etc., since etc. — from Tosafos s.v. "mamatin" etc., and "since he stands" etc., and proof from the Leket there that he distances as explained regarding excrement; Rashba, and the Tur's view that the same applies to Kriat Shema; and Rambam wrote that even in tefillah one must cover with garments or distance at the required measure; and he ruled like Tosafos in tefillah, but in Kriat Shema like Rambam's view, since one can walk — and this is what "since" etc. means; and "if" etc.

Or wait etc. — as written above, siman 25 seif 1, and as written in siman 82.

And there are those who say etc. — there, and as explained above in siman 65.
