Seif 1, even, etc., since, etc. See Tosafos, s.v. "he waits," etc., and "since he is standing," etc. The proof is that it does not say there that he must distance himself, as it states there regarding excrement. This is the view of the Rashba. The Tur maintains that the same law applies to Krias Shema. Hagahos Maimoniyos writes that even during the Amidah he must cover it with garments or distance himself by the prescribed measure. The Mechaber rules like Tosafos regarding the Amidah, but regarding Krias Shema he rules like Hagahos Maimoniyos, since he is able to walk away. This is the meaning of "since," etc., and "if," etc.
Or he waits, etc. As stated there, 25a, and as written at the end of siman 82.
And some say, etc. There, and as explained above in siman 65.
