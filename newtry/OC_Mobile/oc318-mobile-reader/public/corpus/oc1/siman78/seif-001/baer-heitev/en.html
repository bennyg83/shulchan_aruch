One who has kidney stones, which is a distinct case, constantly drips urine against his will; if so he could never pray — it is permitted to make for him a garment around his limb and the upper garments should be clean, and he may enter the synagogue and pray; at the time urine drips he pauses and then prays — provided only that he did not need to drip at the time he began to pray, even though he knows that during his prayer he will need to drip. Responsum of Rama, siman 98; Magen Avraham.
