Cubits. Or he waits until it is absorbed into the ground, in accordance with the measure that will be explained below in siman 82. This is the correct text. See Magen Avraham. One who suffers, Heaven forbid, from a bladder stone illness, causing urine to drip continuously against his will, and who would therefore otherwise never be able to pray, may be permitted to place a garment around his organ, while keeping his outer garments clean, enter the synagogue, and pray. When the urine drips, he must pause, and afterward continue praying. This applies specifically when he did not need to urinate at the time he began praying, even though he knew that during his prayer he would need to do so. Responsa of the Rama, siman 98; Magen Avraham.
