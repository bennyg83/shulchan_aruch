Or waits until they are absorbed in the ground — for the measure that will be explained below siman 82; and so is written in Shulchan Arukh with the gloss of the author of Shakh. And there is difficulty: at the end of siman 90 he ruled one should not wait until they bring water lest he delay long enough to finish all of it and need to return to the beginning — all the more here if he waits until absorption, which is a large measure. And one could say: regarding Keriat Shema some say that even if he delayed long enough to finish all of it he need not return, as Rama wrote siman 65; therefore we are not concerned he might delay; but in prayer all agree one must return to the beginning — therefore we are concerned he might delay. Nevertheless the opposite seems more reasonable: in Keriat Shema he should not wait, for if he delays there will be doubt whether he must return to the beginning, unlike prayer; therefore it seems in Keriat Shema we are not particular if he returns to the beginning, for it is like reading the Torah. And see in Tur — he wrote Rama in responsum siman 98: one who has an illness whereby urine drips constantly from compulsion — if so he cannot pray ever — one should permit making a garment around his organ and the upper garments will be clean, and he may enter the synagogue and pray; and at the time urine drips he interrupts and afterward prays; specifically that he did not need to drip at the time he begins to pray, even though he knows that during his prayer he will need to drip, etc. — see there where he expanded on his reason; and see siman 76 notes 5 and 6 in the name of Rosh:
