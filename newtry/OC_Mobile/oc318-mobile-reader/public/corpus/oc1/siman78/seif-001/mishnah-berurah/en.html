(1) He was reading — the same law applies to reciting Shema and tefillah: he pauses until the flow stops, even if thereby he must wait a long time in the middle of tefillah — long enough to finish all of it — and thereby afterward need to return to the beginning; nevertheless he waits.

(2) The water — and even though below his knees are wet, he may still [continue] from the urine.

(3) In his garment — meaning the upper garment is not soiled; and if it too is soiled, in Kriat Shema and other holy matters he must go and remove his garments, or at least cover the soiled place with another garment. But regarding tefillah, when he cannot interrupt and deal with this, after urination he prays as usual, since the prohibition of urine is only rabbinic and he is already standing in tefillah — they did not decree.

(4) Distances — see Biur Halacha that regarding tefillah too it is appropriate to be stringent and distance when urine fell on the ground, like Kriat Shema.

(5) Amot — or wait until absorbed in the ground at the distance that will be explained below in siman 82, so states Magen Avraham. He wrote that although through waiting for absorption he might need to wait long enough to finish all of it and need to return to the beginning, nevertheless there is no concern for this in Kriat Shema, for it is like one reading Torah; but if it happened in the blessing of Kriat Shema, it is better to distance than wait for ground absorption; and after the fact, if he waited in the blessing of Kriat Shema long enough to finish all of it, he does not return to the beginning, as above in siman 65 in Mishna Berurah.

(6) And so is the main ruling — see above in siman 65 in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halacha where we explained all details of this law.

(7) Above siman 65 — meaning the main point depends on whether he was under duress at the time of the pause: if so he returns to the beginning; and so here he is under duress. Regarding tefillah: if it happened in the middle and he waited for water to be brought or for absorption in the ground long enough to finish the entire tefillah, it seems perhaps he need not return to the beginning [for after the fact one may rely on Rashba and Tosafos Berachos 22b s.v. "mamatin" who hold he need not distance at all since he is already standing in tefillah — if so he was not then under duress, unless in pausing until the flow stopped alone the measure was long enough to finish all of it; see Biur Halacha and below siman 90 seif 27 in Mishna Berurah there]. One whose illness causes constant dripping — see below siman 80 in Mishna Berurah.
