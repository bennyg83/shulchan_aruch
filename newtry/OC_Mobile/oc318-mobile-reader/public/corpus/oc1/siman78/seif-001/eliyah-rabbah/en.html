[1] Covered by his garment, etc. This means that he must cover it with garments upon which the urine did not splash, as the Beit Yosef writes in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos. Although the Beit Yosef rules contrary to Hagahos Maimoniyos, it appears that this is only regarding distancing himself, because moving away during the Amidah entails some prohibition. Regarding covering it, however, he agrees that one should be stringent. This is also implied by the responsum of the Rama, siman 58. I saw that the Derishah overlooked the responsum of the Rama cited above. Because of the apparent contradiction between the Shulchan Aruch and the Beit Yosef, he explained that when the Shulchan Aruch says "covered," it means that the urine was absorbed into the garments upon which it originally fell, and is no longer visibly present, even though the garments remain moist enough to moisten another object. His analysis is imprecise and entirely unconvincing to one who examines the matter. If the garments are exposed, he must remove them. During the Amidah, however, when it is forbidden to move from his place, he prays in his usual manner. Beit Yosef.
