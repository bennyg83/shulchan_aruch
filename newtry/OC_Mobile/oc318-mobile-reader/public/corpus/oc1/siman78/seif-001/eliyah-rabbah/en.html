[1] Covered in his garment, etc. — explanation: he must cover it with garments upon which the water did not splash; so wrote Beit Yosef in the name of the Gemara. And although Beit Yosef ruled not like the Gemara, it seems this is regarding distancing, for there is somewhat of a prohibition in distancing during tefillah, but regarding covering he admits one should be stringent. So too in Rama's responsum siman 58. And I saw in Derishah that he omitted the above-mentioned responsum of Rama, and from the difficulty of Shulchan Aruch he ruled against Beit Yosef's words — explanation of what Shulchan Aruch wrote "covered": he meant that the urine was absorbed in his garments that fell upon them initially and there is somewhat still visible even though they have enough to moisten further — this is not precise and not clear at all to one who examines. And if the garments are uncovered he must remove them; but during tefillah, when one may not move from his place, one prays as usual. Beit Yosef.
