(Seif kaf-alef) Since etc., that one is forbidden to move from his place etc., and see siman 90; and according to Magen Avraham there, therefore what he writes here that one is forbidden to move from his place is not precise, for that is not the main reason — for on what he writes below in siman 90: one was standing in tefillah and a child urinated in the synagogue — he should be silent until they bring water to pour on the urine or walk before it 4 amot etc.; and Magen Avraham wrote: "Some say according to siman 78 one need not interrupt, for there the interruption would be great — needing to remove garments and wear others; but here silence is not such an interruption, and so walking from his place is not such a great interruption" — end quote. Behold walking is not such an interruption, and the reason here is not because he must move from his place but because he must remove his garments etc.; therefore it means he is forbidden to move etc. to remove his garments etc., for implicitly the garments are not ready at hand to wear, and the interruption is from removing and donning.
