(1) [Seif 1] He was reading and urine began flowing, etc. — one who has kidney stones (Beit Me'ir), which constantly drips urine against his will; if so he could never pray — it is permitted to make for him a garment or sponge around his limb and the upper garments should be clean, and he may enter the synagogue and pray; at the time urine drips he pauses and then prays — provided he did not need to drip at the time he begins to pray, even though he knows that during his prayer he will need to drip. Responsum of Rama, siman 98; so too Panim Chadashot in responsum of Maharam of Lublin siman 98, brought by Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz; so too Shach in Knesset HaGedolah siman 80 in Hagahot Taz comment 2 in Rama's responsum; so too Peri Chadash in the same responsum; Magen Avraham seif 2. Orach Chaim comment 3; Sha'arei Teshuvah section 1; Razah comment 4; She'erit Yehudah letter 5. And our doctors advise tying an animal bladder on his limb so urine drips there. Kaf HaChayyim.

(1) And he wrote — the Rokeach that it is a mitzvah to warn unlearned elders, most of whose garments are not clean to recite Kriat Shema, tefillah, and blessings — for drops of urine descend from them from the remnants of urine when they urinate, and they do not pay attention to wait and clean well or change their pants, for old age makes them prone to this; and who asked them to trample His courtyards and come and pray to the Holy House in garments soiled with urine? Some have this from intestinal illness. One who is a Torah scholar has his eye in his head — he is a pure man and his garments; and if he enters with doubtful suspicion, immediately he should change them and not wear them; and there is no doubt that of him it is said "from the mouth of the Almighty" — "and he smelled the scent of his garments and blessed him and said: See, the scent of my son is like the scent of a field that the Omnipresent blessed." See Sha'arei Challah siman 492 and responsum of Maharam Mintz siman 81 — examine. See below letter dalet.
