(1) In Nisan. And the same for Lag BaOmer and from Rosh Chodesh Sivan until Shavuot, and on days between Yom Kippur and Sukkot; but on Isru Chag, 15 Av, and 15 Shevat he does not fast, since it is mentioned in the Gemara. Magen Avraham — see there.

(2) On Rosh Chodesh. Meaning: even though by law it is forbidden to fast on Rosh Chodesh, nevertheless a groom may fast on this Rosh Chodesh because of, etc., as Bach wrote; and therefore also on 28 Kislev, which is also one of the days they fast on, he also fasts on his wedding day; and Magen Avraham wrote: since there are variant textual readings, therefore he should not fast on 28 Kislev. And it appears to me he need not sit a fast-for-his-fast, for he acts with permission. Magen Avraham — see there. And see in Even HaEzer siman 61 seif 5 what Sha'arei Shamayim wrote on the reason for the groom's fast on his wedding day.
