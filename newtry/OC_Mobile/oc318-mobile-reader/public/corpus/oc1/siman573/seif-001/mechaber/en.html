That Megillat Taanis was annulled. And it has one
seif: The halachah is that Megillat Taanis was annulled, and all days written in it are permitted to fast on them, and all the more so before and after them, except Chanukah and Purim, on which it is forbidden to fast on them themselves; but before and after them is permitted. And similarly Shabbatot, festivals, and Rosh Chodesh are permitted before and after them.One who has a wedding on Chanukah has no [need] to fast; but if he has a wedding in Nisan he fasts on his wedding day,' even on Rosh Chodesh Nisan, which is one of the days they fast on, as below end of siman 580 (Hagahot Minhagim and Lekutim of Maharash).):
