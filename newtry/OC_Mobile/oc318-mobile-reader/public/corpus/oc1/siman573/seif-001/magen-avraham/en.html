(1) Reason for the groom's fast — because on that day they forgive him all his sins; alternatively, lest he be drunk at the time of kiddushin; and therefore one who accepted kiddushin for his minor daughter must fast (Rama MiFano siman 109); and it appears to me she does not fast even for the first reason, for a minor has no sins. In Nisan he fasts — for it is only custom (Bach); and it appears to me the same for Lag BaOmer and from Rosh Chodesh Sivan until Shavuot, and on days between Yom Kippur and Sukkot; but on Isru Chag, 15 Av, and 15 Shevat he does not fast, since mentioned in the Gemara — see siman 429 what he wrote.

(2) Even on Rosh Chodesh. Meaning: even though by law it is forbidden to fast, nevertheless a groom may fast because of, etc., as Bach wrote; and the same in Kislev — end of his words; and per what he wrote there he should not fast because there are variant readings; and it appears to me on Rosh Chodesh Av there is also [reason] to fast, only that initially one does not marry then; and it appears he need not sit fast-for-his-fast — see siman 568 seif 5, for he acts with permission; and see siman 572, 597 — unlike a dream-fast on Shabbat.
