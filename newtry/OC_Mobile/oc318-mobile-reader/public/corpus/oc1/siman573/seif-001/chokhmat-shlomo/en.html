Seif 1 — in the gloss: but if he has a wedding in Nisan he fasts, etc. NB see in Magen Avraham that he wrote on Isru Chag, 15 Av, and 15 Shevat one does not fast since explicit in the Gemara, etc. Behold by law 15 Av and 15 Shevat the law is with him, since we are expert in fixing the month — if so they are the ones explicit in the Gemara; but on Isru Chag it is true it is explicit in the Gemara, but the Gemara discusses if Shavuot is only one day as in Eretz Yisrael when then it was a slaughter day after Shabbat; but for us it is two days and we are expert in fixing the month — if so the third day by law of the Gemara is nothing, and it is as for tachanun we practice like the law of Eretz Yisrael; nevertheless it is not better than Rosh Chodesh Nisan and other days without tachanun — nevertheless a groom fasts; and the same here too, as will be explained — correct, examine well.
