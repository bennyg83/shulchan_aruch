Even. See the Taz: behold, here is the explanation of the word "even," etc., and not according to its simple sense; regarding [whether it is] words of Torah, one can say that Rabbeinu Tam holds it is not words of Torah; see siman 570 in the Magen Avraham, letter 9; and he does not hold to distinguish between [what is forbidden] by law or by custom. And regarding Lag BaOmer, etc., likewise a groom does not fast; only in the days of Nisan he fasts, and they should not be better than Rosh Chodesh itself. And see Eshel Avraham, letter 3, who also concludes in the name of Nachalat Shiva and Kenesses HaGedolah, etc., and he concludes: one who is lenient has not lost [anything] by not having the groom fast on Lag BaOmer and on days on which tachanun is not said. And one can say that on the 15th of Av he should fast, so that it not be better than Rosh Chodesh Av, for he does not hold to distinguish between [what is] by law and [what is] by custom; which is not so according to the Magen Avraham, for whom it works out well. And one can say [this is] specifically when [the day] is adjacent to it; alternatively, since a chuppah on Rosh Chodesh Av is not applicable, [the argument] that it should not be better is not applicable, etc. And [regarding] a yahrzeit fast, see siman 429 in the Taz, letter 3, and siman 568, se'if 5: that on days on which tachanun is not said, one does not observe a yahrzeit fast on them; see there. And the 28th of Kislev: see Magen Avraham, letter 2, which implies not to fast on the 28th of Kislev; see there. And on days when the groom does not fast, nevertheless it appears he should not become intoxicated, for the reason of kiddushin; see Magen Avraham. A widower and a widow fast on their wedding day, even according to the reason of forgiveness of sins; and even if he [already] has a wife, in a place where they marry two wives; see Eshel Avraham, letter 2. The groom and the bride fast; see Even HaEzer, siman 61, and the Magen Avraham here; and one can say it is because of kiddushin, not because of forgiveness of sin, for one can say that where he was saved from sin, etc. And a yavam and a yevamah, in a place where they practice yibbum: according to the reason of drunkenness they need not fast, for even [in cases of] one acting under duress and one acting unintentionally [the act can take effect]; and according to the reason of forgiveness of sin, one can say they do fast; and see Even HaEzer, Laws of Yibbum; and this requires further study. [See] siman 166, se'if 7, and the one who says [in] se'if 63: when [they are] as drunk as Lot, in both of them there is no acquisition; unless regarding a groom one can say that even when his mind alone is unsettled, one also may not effect kiddushin ab initio.
