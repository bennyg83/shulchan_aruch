In Nisan. See Baer Heitev; and see in the Magen Avraham that a father who accepts kiddushin for his daughter must fast, and the minor girl does not fast (and what is marked in the Magen Avraham as Rama is a scribal error, and it should read that [the citation is of] R' Moshe Mintz, which is an acronym for Rabbi Moshe Mintz). And see in Eliyah Rabbah that even a widower and a widow fast, even according to the reason that their sins are forgiven, for it is expounded in the Midrash on the verse "and he took Machalas," etc., and there it is written regarding his wives, etc. And see in the Peri Megadim what he wrote on this regarding days on which they do not fast. And see in Even HaEzer, siman 68, in the Beis Shmuel, s.k. 6, that even on days on which they do not fast, one must take care not to pursue excesses of food and drink.
