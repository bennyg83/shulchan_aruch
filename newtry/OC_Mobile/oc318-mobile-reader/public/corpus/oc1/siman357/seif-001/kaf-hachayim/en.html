(א) [Seif 1] A courtyard less than four amot, etc. — The general principle in this siman is as follows: the Sages estimated that every person needs to use two se'ah of water each day to wash his face, hands, feet, and other needs; and it is known that on Shabbat it is forbidden to pour from reshut hayachid to reshut harabbim or karmelit. It is everyone's way to pour his waste water into his courtyard adjoining his house, which is his domain and also reshut hayachid, taking from reshut hayachid to reshut hayachid. It is known that ordinarily courtyards have dust in summer, and everyone wants to sprinkle his courtyard and settle it so it not become dusty; therefore he pours there the water he uses daily. The Sages estimated that in two se'ah of water an ordinary person can sprinkle a courtyard that has four by four amot — they settle the dust and are absorbed in the ground; and even where he does not want to sprinkle, some say it is permitted anyway because the water is absorbed in the ground and does not soil the courtyard soil, and since so he is not particular that they go outside specifically — and then even if they later go outside it was not his intent and it is not as taking from reshut hayachid to reshut harabbim. But if less than four by four amot, then two se'ah of water are excessive and are not absorbed — the courtyard becomes mud and mire, and his mind is set on this in summer when everyone is particular that his courtyard be clean; therefore he is pleased that they flow outside, and if they go outside his intent is fulfilled and it is as pouring from reshut hayachid to reshut harabbim directly — and they must decree lest he pour directly from reshut hayachid to reshut harabbim. Therefore he must make a pit in the courtyard holding two se'ah; then when he pours into the courtyard he is not particular that they go outside since they can rest in the pit and his courtyard will not be soiled, and it is not as taking from reshut hayachid to reshut harabbim. When there is a pit, there is no difference whether it already had water from before or not, or whether he uses several se'ah on Shabbat — that matter is only a general reminder. All this from Levush. See also (א).
