(א) So that it be a makom patur — for if he does not cover it, it is reshut harabbim; and if it is three deep, it is karmelit; but if it is covered, it has only makom patur.

(ב) He may not pour into it at all — a decree lest he come to pour two se'ah, which is a person's measure; but in the rainy season there is no reason to decree, and the reason is explained in the gemara.

(ג) Even if it is long and narrow — nevertheless there is here measure to absorb two se'ah.

(ד) And the same applies to two upper courtyards before, etc. — thus it should read.

(ה) But if it adjoins karmelit, etc. — It is difficult: if so, why must one hollow the pit outside at the beginning of this siman? The pit is in any case karmelit when it is from three to ten, and all the more if more than ten, which is reshut hayachid. One may say: perhaps mud and mire will rise and it will be even less than three; automatically it is reshut harabbim, and this concern the Beit Yosef brought in the name of the Ritva; therefore they required hollowing. The reason they were lenient in karmelit is because there is only his force and his force — in karmelit they did not decree, as above regarding a ship pouring on the sides of the ship in siman 355.
