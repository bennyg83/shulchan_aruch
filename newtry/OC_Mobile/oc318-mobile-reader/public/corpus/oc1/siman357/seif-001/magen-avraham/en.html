(א) Makom patur — for if it is uncovered, sometimes there is mud and mire in it and it will not be ten deep and will be karmelit; but when it is covered and its mouth is in reshut hayachid, it is holes of reshut hayachid (Beit Yosef, Ritva); see siman 345 seif 1.

(ב) He may pour — even if it filled from before, he may pour into it, for since the Sages fixed it for him as a reminder on Shabbat that he not forget and cast into reshut harabbim, it is permitted.

(ג) He may not pour — for we decree lest he come to pour two se'ah.

(ד) Long and narrow — for example, eight amot long and two wide.

(ה) In the rainy season — for a person is not particular about soiling his courtyard and does not intend that it go outside; and if because he fears a shower pipe and will come to permit in summer, we say: these are rainwater, and even when it is not raining season they are absorbed and follow the rains.

(ו) Plaster ceiling — soft.

(ז) Adjoining — within four tefachim.

(ח) It does not need — see this siman, for some dispute.
