In order — Taz, Beit Yosef in the name of the Ritva: perhaps sometimes there will be mud and mire and its depth will be reduced to less than three and it will be reshut harabbim; therefore one must hollow it. If it is deep from three to nine it is makom patur when covered; if it is ten deep and four wide, it is permitted — it is reshut hayachid. Magen Avraham (1). And even though in seif 3 the Mechaber permits in karmelit because his force in karmelit they did not decree — nevertheless here, since a pit must be fixed, they also required hollowing outside; and this is in Magen Avraham and Taz (2). Taz letter 5.
