(ד) A person. There.

(ז) That any pit. Pesachim 109a-b: whether, etc.

(ח) Rather. Mishnah there.

(ט) And once, etc., he may, etc. Gemara there: 'I might say because it is large', etc.; and Tosafos there s.v. machzik, etc.

(י) And if it does not. There: 'a se'ah does not', etc.

(כ) (יא) And if there is, etc. — for a pit is the measure of four amot in which two se'ah are absorbed, and like a pit one may pour as much as he wishes; all the more per R' Yonasan; and so two upper chambers for combination; and even though much water — permitted as much as there, in the gemara 'I might say because it is large', etc.; and Rashi there.

(ל) (יב) Even if it is. Like R' Zeira who stands in his view against Rabbah, and we rule like Rabbah; and as Tosafos s.v. biv and Rosh there wrote.

(מ) (יג) Or in rainy season. There the tanna taught 'when is this said', and as Rashi explained; and therefore even in rainy season they give a se'ah — and some say a se'ah; there is no distinction between se'ah; and even without a pit at all, per the braita's 'when is this said' — Rosh there.

(נ) (יד) And the same two. Per R' Yonasan's explanation that it refers to projections; and 'two', etc. refers above to combination.

(ס) (טו) And on them plaster. Like R' Zeira, as above.
