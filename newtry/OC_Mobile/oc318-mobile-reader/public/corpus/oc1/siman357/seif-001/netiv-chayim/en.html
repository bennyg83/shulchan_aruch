(Magen Avraham sk 9) And one may ask: behold in Beitza 52, and for our teacher the Maggid Mishneh, thin wood is different, unlike water which is thin and fit to absorb. But if this is difficult, it is difficult for me what the Rama ruled in siman 46 there, in the name of Rokeach that a stone pavement does not absorb. One may distinguish between a pavement of small smooth stones as streets and courtyards are paved, with much dirt between stone and stone to absorb, versus a pavement of wide stones as rooms and upper chambers are paved — and nevertheless Magen Avraham's question is also resolved.
