(א) To cover it — for if he does not cover it, it is reshut harabbim; and if it is three deep, it is karmelit; but if it is covered, it has only the status of makom patur.

(ב) And narrow — for example, eight amot long and two wide; nevertheless there is here sufficient measure to absorb two se'ah.

(ג) The rains — for a person is not particular about soiling his courtyard and does not intend that the water go outside.

(ד) Adjoining — within four tefachim.

(ה) To karmelit — because there is only his force and his force; in karmelit they did not decree, as written in siman 355 seif 1; and see this siman, for some dispute and hold there is no distinction between karmelit and reshut harabbim — see there.
