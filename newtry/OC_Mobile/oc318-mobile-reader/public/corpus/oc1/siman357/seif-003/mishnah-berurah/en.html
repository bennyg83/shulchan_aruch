(א) (כג) When is this said, etc. — This concludes the Rambam's words; and for us too there is relevance: if the pipe was wood, not fit to absorb water, and water certainly goes outside to reshut harabbim — forbidden even per the first view; but when adjoining karmelit it is permitted even of wood, for since he does not pour directly to karmelit but only from his force — his force in karmelit they did not decree.
(ב) (כד) And some say no distinction, etc. — Although we rule that even when water goes to reshut harabbim it is permitted in a pipe, as written in sk 18; nevertheless this view differs regarding a wooden pipe, as in sk 23; or when the pipe lacks four amot coverage in karmelit — for the Shulchan Aruch permitted and this view forbids. Also differs regarding a small courtyard lacking four by four — the Mechaber at end of seif 1 permits when adjoining karmelit and this view forbids [and what is written in hagahah Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and Rosh was not mentioned in Tosafos and Rosh regarding a pipe at all — they rule leniently in a pipe even adjoining reshut harabbim; only from their words does it sound they apply to our matters too].
(ג) (כה) Even in this time when we lack, etc. — meaning per those who require specifically sixty myriad traversing it, which is not found — nevertheless we decree lest a large city happen to have sixty myriad [Magen Avraham].
(ד) (כו) Or a courtyard not combined in an eruv — meaning the courtyard in which the pipe [or small courtyard lacking four by four] adjoins a courtyard not combined in an eruv; when he pours water it descends to that courtyard — permitted for all, since he does not throw directly but only his force — they did not decree and permitted to pour even onto the pipe mouth.
(ה) (כז) Like a courtyard not combined in an eruv — since it truly has partitions, only lacking eruv.
(ו) (כח) And if the conduit, etc. — meaning the shower conduit in karmelit is above ten tefachim, and thus water passes through makom patur — permitted to pour even intending water descend to karmelit; even per the stringent above in siman 346 end seif 1, through makom patur here where he does not throw directly but only his force, one may be lenient [Gra].
(ז) (כט) And this is essential — see Peri Megadim who sides that all the hagahah refers to this, even at the beginning of his words.
