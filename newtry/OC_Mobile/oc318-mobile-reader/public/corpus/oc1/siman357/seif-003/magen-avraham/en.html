(א) (ג) One may pour — for his force in karmelit they did not decree; see siman 355 seif 1.

(ב) (ד) Even in this time — thus Rabbeinu Yosef in the name of the Rosh; the reason is that nevertheless it is fit to decree in a large city that has sixty myriad, not like Ashkenaz.

(ג) (ה) Karmelit that is, etc. — thus in Mordechai, and the Rama brought in siman 325 seif 3, not like Ashkenaz who wrote that Mordechai's words contradict one another.

(ד) (ו) That it is outside, etc. — And even though in a portico one may pour, for there he does not intend that it go outside, unlike here; see siman 314 seif 1 and siman 377.
