When is this said? When the water goes out to reshut harabbim; but if it goes out to karmelit, one may pour onto the mouth of the pipe even in summer.And some say there is no distinction between karmelit and reshut harabbim (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and the Rosh in the chapter Keitzad Mishtatfin). This is specifically a karmelit within the city that is comparable to reshut harabbim itself, even in our time when we have no reshut harabbim; but a karmelit outside the city, or a courtyard that was not combined in an eruv, is permitted (Mordechai in the chapter HaZorek and Semag and his own opinion) (responsa of Maharam siman 90). A city surrounded by a wall without an eruv in it is considered like a courtyard not combined in an eruv (Mordechai end of chapter HaDar and Terumat HaDeshen siman 73 and his opinion). If) the pipe is more than ten tefachim high so that the water passes through a makom patur to the karmelit, one may be lenient (Maharil), and this is) the essential ruling.
