(ב) (כב) And specifically. There and there from that in Shabbos.

(ג) (כג) Even in this time. This is not like R' Baruch's answer in Tosafos Shabbos 64b s.v. R' Anani; and see above siman 363 seif 18; and some learned, etc.

(ד) (כד) But karmelit. As above from Shabbos there; and see Chullin 104b.

(ה) (כה) Or a courtyard. Eruvin chapter 9, 1; and see there Tosafos s.v. lashon; and Tosafos Shabbos 64b s.v. R' Anani, and R' Yitzchak challenged, etc.

(ו) (כו) And a city surrounded. Meaning: even though they say there 101a and the Sages, R' Meir, etc., that since they did not make an eruv it is karmelit; and see Rashi there and in chapter 1, 6b s.v. Yerushalayim — 34 karmelit, only that carrying is forbidden as in karmelit; and as Rashi wrote it is like a courtyard without eruv, etc.; and Tosafos there 47b s.v. dekula; and Or Zarua that it also requires, etc. But without surrounding for residence, since it is like karmelit as written in siman 67b, it has only four amot; and in reshut harabbim 23b Tosafos wrote on the passage beginning 'not after they were breached' — this is before, as 'when is this said'.

(ז) (כז) And if the pipe. Even per those who forbid exchanging in siman 346 seif 1 and siman 353 seif 3 — nevertheless his force in such a case they did not decree.
