(א) That is covered four amot along, etc. — And the same even if in reshut harabbim it is not covered but in the courtyard the length of the pipe with the courtyard is four by four.

(ב) Even onto the mouth of the pipe — And it is reasonable that it is permitted even if the courtyard is less than four amot, and permitted even to pour in the middle of the courtyard since water can descend from there into the pipe — similar to where we are lenient above to pour in a courtyard when there is a pit even if the pit is outside.

(ג) And per the Rambam, etc. — And per this view, above in seif 1 regarding a pit, one may not pour inside the courtyard without making a pit, but only when the courtyard holds four by four square, not like the Mechaber's view above in seif 1 who permits long and narrow [Gra, and so proven explicitly in the gemara]. But for practical law it seems from the later authorities that we do not rule like the Rambam but like most poskim.
