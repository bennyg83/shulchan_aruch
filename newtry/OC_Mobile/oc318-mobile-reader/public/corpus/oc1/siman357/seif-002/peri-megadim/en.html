Not — Taz: the Sages estimated that every person needs two se'ah of water each day for pouring, washing face and hands and vessels; and when one pours in the courtyard and it immediately goes in the ground to reshut harabbim, it is his force in reshut harabbim and they decreed lest he pour directly from his hand to reshut harabbim and there would be a Torah prohibition. Therefore in a courtyard with four by four the water is absorbed there; and likewise in the rainy season, when the courtyard is anyway soiled from rain, he does not intend that water immediately go to reshut harabbim, and melacha requires intent and the Torah forbade — and whatever is without intent, the Sages did not decree lest he pour directly to reshut harabbim. Unlike summer when the courtyard lacks four by four and he intends that water go immediately from courtyard to reshut harabbim — his force in reshut harabbim; they decreed lest he pour directly to reshut harabbim. Therefore a pit holding two se'ah is needed. See Eruvin 88b and Rashi there and Levush here.
