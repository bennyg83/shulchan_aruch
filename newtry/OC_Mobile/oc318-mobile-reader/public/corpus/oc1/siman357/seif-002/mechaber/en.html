A drainpipe covered for four amot along reshut harabbim that has four by four amot — one may pour even onto the mouth of the pipe even in summer, even though the water goes immediately from his hand outside, provided it is not a wooden pipe in which water cannot be absorbed; but if it is made like a stone pavement that absorbs, it is permitted. And per the Rambam, even if the length of the pipe or conduit is one hundred amot, one may not pour onto its mouth in summer; rather one pours outside the pipe and the water descends into the pipe.
