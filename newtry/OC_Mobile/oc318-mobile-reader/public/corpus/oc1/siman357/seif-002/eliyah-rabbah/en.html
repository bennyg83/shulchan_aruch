(ב) [Levush] Such as if it is long, etc. — And the same applies to any other shape, provided it has enough to square four by four (Beit Yosef). Soft plaster ceiling adjoining within four tefachim (Beit Yosef and R' Yonasan). But in Avodat HaKodesh page 16, his language is: the roof built between two upper chambers that are open to one another — if the chambers are opposite one another they combine; if not opposite but one high and one low, they do not combine. Until here — implying that even if distant from one another they combine.
