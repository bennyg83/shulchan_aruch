(א) (טז) Drainpipe, etc. — the channel made to receive water poured in the courtyard toward reshut harabbim.

(ב) (יז) Four amot along — should read: along four amot — meaning the first four amot adjoining the courtyard are covered and also four amot wide [and the same if only two amot wide but eight amot long]; in this measure there is capacity to absorb two se'ah of water a person needs daily; therefore we are lenient to pour onto the pipe mouth standing in the courtyard as in a courtyard holding four by four, as explained.

(ג) (יח) Outside — since he does not specifically intend they go outside, therefore even when they go outside it is permitted, as at the beginning of the seif.

(ד) (יט) That it not be, etc. — for if the pipe is wood it is as if he specifically intends it go outside, and even if one hundred amot long it is forbidden.

(ה) (כ) Like a stone pavement — when paved with small smooth stones as streets and courtyards are paved, with much dirt between stones to absorb.

(ו) (כא) Even if the pipe length, etc. — meaning the pipe length in the courtyard is one hundred amot; at this measure water would certainly absorb in the ground — nevertheless forbidden because water goes from his force to reshut harabbim [there in Rambam]; we rule leniently like the first view (R' Avraham).

(ז) (כב) Or the conduit — conduit means what is placed on the roof and discharge descends through it to reshut harabbim.
