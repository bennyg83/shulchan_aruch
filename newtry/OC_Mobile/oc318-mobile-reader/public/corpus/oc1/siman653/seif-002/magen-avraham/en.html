It appears to me that a hadas is prohibited to smell even on Shabbos, for it was set aside for all seven days, and so it appears in siman 665. But an esrog is permitted to smell on Shabbos, as written in the gloss in siman 658:2, for at least now it is not being used for the mitzvah. It further appears to me that specifically when he smells it at the time of taking it in order to fulfill the obligation, some say he does not recite a berachah; but if he takes it in the middle of the day to smell it, everyone agrees he recites a berachah, like one who takes fruit that stands for eating in order to smell it. See siman 216:1, and so it appears from the language of the Semak; see Beis Yosef. It is permitted for benefit. For designation is not significant. See what I wrote in siman 638:1 regarding a lulav from last year.
