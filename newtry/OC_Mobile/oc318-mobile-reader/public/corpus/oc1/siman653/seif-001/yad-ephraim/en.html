In Magen Avraham seif katan 1 And see what is written in siman 638:10; and the same applies regarding a lulav from last year. This is how the text should read. Meaning, even if one keeps the lulav from year to year, as written in siman 645, nevertheless it is not prohibited until he fulfills the obligation with it in the next year, for after the festival its sanctity has passed, as above in siman 638 regarding a sukkah.
