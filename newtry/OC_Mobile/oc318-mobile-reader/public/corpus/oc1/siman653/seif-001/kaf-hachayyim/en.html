1) [Seif 1] It is forbidden to smell a hadas used for the mitzvah, etc. This is because its primary purpose is only for fragrance, and therefore one sets it aside from such use for all seven days, just as one sets aside the wood of the sukkah. An esrog, however, is primarily intended for eating, and it is set aside only from eating, not from smelling. The authorities disagree only regarding the blessing, for some say that since it was not designated for fragrance, the blessing ‘Who gives a pleasant fragrance to fruits’ does not apply to it.
