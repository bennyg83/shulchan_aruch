(9) There, Rama gloss: that they call them by their names, etc. — and the simple custom in Jerusalem is that the chazan does not call the oleh to the Torah by his name aloud as is the custom in all places; only the shamash, before the oleh finishes the preceding one, goes to him and tells him to ascend; and if that individual does not wish to ascend for some reason, he tells the shamash to call another. And this is an enactment of the early ones so he not be included among those called to the Torah scroll and not ascend; and also through this they are saved from several kinds of stringencies that extend when they call him by name and he is forced to ascend though he does not wish, for a reason known to him — Rav Chaim Shal in responsa part 1 siman 13; and they brought this in Sefer HaTakkanot in the Laws of Torah scroll letter 19. And there are places that practice: one whom they wish to honor to ascend to the Torah scroll — the shamash goes to him and places in his hand a silver plate; and so I saw practiced in the land across the river when I came to Jerusalem; and this custom too is for the above reason.
