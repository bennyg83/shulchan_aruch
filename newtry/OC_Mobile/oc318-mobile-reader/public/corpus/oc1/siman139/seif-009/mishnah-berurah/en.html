(א) He is already exempt from blessing "Who chose us," etc. — for he fulfilled with what he blessed on the Torah this blessing.

(ב) For it is not inferior to one who is exempt, etc. — and as explained above siman 47 seif 7; and the Acharonim wrote that nevertheless it is not completely comparable to one exempted by Ahavah Rabbah, for there he fulfills with that also the blessing "Who chose us" for engaging in Torah, and also "Evening, please"; unlike here, where he is exempt only from the blessing "Who chose us" alone — but the other blessings he must complete, and afterward he says the priestly blessing which they are accustomed to say always.
