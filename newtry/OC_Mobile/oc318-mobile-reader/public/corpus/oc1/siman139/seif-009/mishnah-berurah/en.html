(31) He is already exempt from reciting Asher Bachar Banu, etc. He fulfilled that blessing through the blessing he recited over the Torah.
(32) For it is no less than one who was exempted, etc. As explained above in siman 47, seif 7. The later authorities write that this is nevertheless not entirely comparable to one who was exempted through Ahavah Rabbah. There, he thereby fulfills both the blessing Asher Kideshanu Bemitzvosav Vetzivanu Laasok Bedivrei Torah and Vehaarev Na. Here, however, he is exempt only from Asher Bachar Banu. He must complete the other blessings and afterward recite Bircas Kohanim, which is customarily said each day.
