9) There, in the gloss: That they call them by their name, etc. And the custom is widespread in Yerushalayim, may it be rebuilt and established, that the shaliach tzibbur does not call the oleh to the Sefer Torah by his name aloud, as is the custom in every place; rather the shamash, before the previous oleh finishes, goes over to him and tells him to ascend; and if that individual does not wish to ascend on account of some reason, he tells the shamash to call another. And this is an enactment of the earlier authorities, so that he not be included among those who are called to the Sefer Torah and do not ascend. And also by this they are saved from several kinds of resentment that ensue when they call him by name and he is compelled to ascend but does not wish to, for a reason known to him. The Rav Chaim Sha'al in a responsum, part 1, siman 13; and the sefer HaTakanos cited him in the law of Krias Sefer Torah, ot 19. And there are places that have the custom that one whom they wish to honor to ascend to the Sefer Torah -- the shamash goes over to him and places in his hand a plaque of silver; and so I saw them practice in [my] land when I passed through there, when I came to Yerushalayim, may it be rebuilt; and this custom too is for the aforementioned reason.
