In the name of Taz and Avi Ezri Orach Chayim siman 129 letter 19, and Taz there letter 15, that b'dieved a get is valid written with the name of the father's father; understand well; and if so, the son of an apostate has no objection; unlike the mother's father, who is invalid — for that is not a general rule, for to their families to their fathers' houses it is written. And for uncertain lineage and foundlings they write in a get "son of Abraham our father" specifically — Orach Chayim 129 seif 37; Beit Shmuel letter 39 (or convert); and it is possible that for a Torah scroll too they call similarly to a convert literally — namely "so-and-so son of Abraham our father" or "son of Abraham the convert" (and likewise in gittin one writes "the convert" after Abraham, who was head of converts); and Avi Ezri letter 4. And behold, if his father and father's father are apostates, one can say one should not call him by his father's father's name, for in such a case possibly the get is invalid — sons of sons are like sons, not more than that — "instead of your fathers shall be your sons," Tosafot Pesachim regarding Rav bar Achva; requires study; and if not, elsewhere it will be explained further; and here one should not elaborate.
