(1) He should not go up. This means that even if he was called to go up, he should not do so. Chazal said in the chapter HaRoeh that three matters shorten a person's life, one of which is when he is given a Sefer Torah from which to read and does not read. Here, however, he should not go up because he must first review the reading privately. The Sages found support for this in the verse, “Then He saw it and declared it; He prepared it and also investigated it.”
(2) He must, etc. After the fact, if no one in the synagogue reviewed the reading beforehand, in order not to cancel the public Torah reading the custom is to be lenient and have someone who knows the cantillation read even though he did not prepare. Another person reads quietly before him from a Chumash or siddur. See below, siman 142, in the Mishnah Berurah, where we will discuss this.
(3) Beforehand. It is the preferred mitzvah for the person receiving the aliyah also to review the reading privately beforehand. He fulfills this through the twice-Scripture-and-once-Targum reading that he completed on Erev Shabbos, since that counts as preparation.
