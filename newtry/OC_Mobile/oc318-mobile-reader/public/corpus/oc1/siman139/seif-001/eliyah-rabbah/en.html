This is difficult for the Levush, who requires the oleh to prepare as well. The Bach, however, writes that it is the preferred mitzvah for the oleh also to review it two or three times. Therefore every person should review the reading beforehand, lest he be called to an aliyah the next day on Shabbos. The Shach writes that by reading the weekly parashah twice in Hebrew and once in Targum, one fulfills the obligation to prepare it.
