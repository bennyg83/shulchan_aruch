5) [Seif 2] One who does not know how to read, etc. From the words of the author, of blessed memory, it is proven explicitly that even though, when they read to him word by word, he knows how to read it, they protest against him that he should not ascend to the Sefer Torah; and one may be lenient in this. And so appears from the words of the Tur and the responsum of the Rosh that the Beis Yosef brought in siman 611. Pri Chadash, ot 2; Mishnah Berurah, ot 4. And we already wrote on this above in siman 135, ot 16; take it from there.
