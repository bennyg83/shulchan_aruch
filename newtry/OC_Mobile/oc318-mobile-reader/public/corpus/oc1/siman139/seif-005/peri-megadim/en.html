And after — Taz, Magen Avraham in the earlier letter; and what Mordechai wrote: between one man and another they roll it — from the Tanna "he opens and sees" — in my chiddushim I asked: in the days of the Tannaim the first and last bless; and if so, the first certainly opens, for it was closed at the time of going out; and even per Taz in Turei Zahav letter 5 it is difficult, for perhaps it deals with the first, as was the custom in the days of the Tannaim.
