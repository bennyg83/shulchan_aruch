They practiced to cover, etc. — meaning it is open; and Rama wrote on this that in these countries they practiced that it is rolled — meaning no cover with a sudar is needed; and those who practice rolling it between reader and reader and then placing a cover — that is extra trouble and one should not add trouble, for because of trouble R' Yehuda disagrees with R' Meir. But what they practiced to cover when the chazzan says Kaddish is fine, for then he departs from the reading; and even when saying maftir he returns and opens — nevertheless since the maftir is a Torah reader it is only for honor of the Torah as below — therefore it appears there is a distinction between other readers and the maftir who reads, as explained.
