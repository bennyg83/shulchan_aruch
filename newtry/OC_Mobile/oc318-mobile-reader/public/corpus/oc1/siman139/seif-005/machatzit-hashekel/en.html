(Note 5) They bless before it. Beit Shmuel page 38 siman 37 — that if one erred in the first, etc.; Magen Avraham wrote in his name that the blessing on the Torah before it is d'oraisa — in his chiddushim on Sotah in the sugya in the gemara 41a s.v. because one does not bless, etc., and his proof from Berachot 21a: R' Yehoshua said, Rav said: from where is the blessing on the Torah before it from the Torah? As it says "for the Name of Heaven I proclaim; give greatness to our Heaven" — and the verse implies to him it deals when reading in public; understand well, because of honor of the congregation, for public reading is d'rabbanan except parashat Zachor — and how can the blessing be from the Torah; so Peri Chadash. He wrote further, etc., and see Rabeinu Tam who wrote: how did he take a cup of beer and open and say "Blessed are You... Who creates the fruit of the vine" and concluded "for everything," etc. — he fulfilled, and this is when he knew it was beer; and the same law here; and on the contrary, here it is superior, and similar to what Magen Avraham wrote in the gloss there seif 1: he had a cup of wine and thought it was water and opened and said, etc. — the same here, that even if he finished the blessing "Who gave us," he fulfilled.

(ב) And if he blessed, etc. — he blesses after it, etc., for there is no fixed order to the blessings of the Torah; understand well.
