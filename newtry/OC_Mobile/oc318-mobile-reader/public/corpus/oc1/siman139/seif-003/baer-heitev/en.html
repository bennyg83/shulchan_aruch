A blind person. Bach writes that this applies specifically to a blind Torah scholar, but an unlearned person should not be called. Magen Avraham writes that the custom is not to be particular about this, since presumably every person can read together with the chazzan. Taz, in siman 141, seif katan 3, likewise permits even an unlearned person. Knesses HaGedolah and Rabbi Avraham Yitzchak, in his responsa Zera Avraham, agree.
