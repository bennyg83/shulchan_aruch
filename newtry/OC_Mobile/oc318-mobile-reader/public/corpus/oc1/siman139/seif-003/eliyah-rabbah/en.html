[3] They call him by his father’s name, etc. It appears to me that titles should not be mentioned. Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah writes that Responsa Rama, siman 41, rules that if he comes to a place where he is unknown and they ask him how he should be called, he should be called by his paternal grandfather’s name. Therefore Rama was precise in writing, “and he became accustomed in that city,” implying that even if he became accustomed to it in that city, he should not be called that way in another city.
