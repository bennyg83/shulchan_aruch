(1) In the name of his mother's father — in my humble opinion this is not correct, for perhaps he will come to divorce his wife and write thus "I, so-and-so, that he is the father of his mother" — and in truth he is not called his son, for "to their families, to their fathers' houses" is written — the get is found invalid; therefore they call him by the name ben Avraham as written afterward — and this certainly has no invalidity, for all are sons of Abraham as it is written "father of a multitude of nations," etc., as explained clearly above.

(2) A blind person does not read — this will be explained in siman 141.
