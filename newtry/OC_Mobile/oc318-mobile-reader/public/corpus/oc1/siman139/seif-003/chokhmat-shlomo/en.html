Seif 3 in hagahah — one whose father converted to idolatry: they call him by his paternal grandfather's name, but not his name alone, so as not to embarrass him publicly. NB — see Taz Moed Katan 1; see Avraham Zuckerman who rejected his words that in a get the rabbi must investigate the father's name — his answer did not help: (1) the rabbi need investigate only if he is not established in the city by his name and father's name; but if established in the city by his name and father's name, no need to investigate further — so too here, if he is established to go up to Torah as Abraham, witnesses testify he is established to be called by his father's name thus, and no need to investigate more; (2) is a get required specifically a rabbi? If he divorced alone before witnesses and wrote the get himself in his handwriting and witnesses signed and gave it to her — is she not divorced b'dieved? If so, concern he could write a get himself, sign witnesses, divorce her, write the name Abraham, and she goes and marries another — and the Taz wrote well; see Chidushei HaEzer siman 129 on these words of the Taz, and examine.
