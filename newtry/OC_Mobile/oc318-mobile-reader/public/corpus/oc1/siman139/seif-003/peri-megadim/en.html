All — Taz and Turei Zahav letter 5 explain in Biur HaGra that even in the days of the Mishnah, if they wished, only one blesses; understand well; and see Megillah 21b: R' Chiya bar Gamda said it is permitted, etc.; and per this, if one reads, all need not begin and end with the preceding verses (and in Kitzur Shulchan Aruch only custom, Avi Ezri there and Turei Zahav); and R' Levi said one does not bless on calamities — how is it done? Our Rabbis taught: he begins with a verse before them and concludes, etc.; and a baraita supports R' Levi — for R' Chiya bar Gamda, if one read, all have concern. And Tosafot Megillah chapter 3: one can say he speaks of nowadays; and even though in the Mishnah, nevertheless there is a difference that one does not begin only the preceding verse and conclude with the verse after them; but the baraita is difficult, for it implies like R' Levi, and in the days of the Tannaim only the first and last bless; and if we say it speaks in Ta'anit, as Turei Zahav wrote there, still difficult how it is done — he begins with a verse before them, and behold the kohen (and Levi read in the blessings only to conclude — the Israelite concludes the verse after them; and Turei Zahav letter 5 brought this there, and he can bless beginning and end — more difficult; see there). Magen Avraham in the next letter, if not here.
