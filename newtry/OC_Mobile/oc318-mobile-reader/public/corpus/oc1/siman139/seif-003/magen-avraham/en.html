(א) He should not read until they tell him — because a person does not squander himself; and Avi Ezri in the name of Chayei Adam wrote: and if they are pressed to read (meaning there is no kohen but the chazan), an Israelite takes the Torah scroll from the ark and brings it on the bimah and reads for the kohen (meaning for the chazan) so he does not ascend by himself; and if there is another kohen in the synagogue, the chazan can call him, and there is no flaw, as written siman 135 seif 1 in the gloss as above — meaning not like Bach who explained "pressed."

(ב) And uncertain lineage, etc., in the name of my mother's father — it should read: such as when one woman came before he was gathered from the market and said she is his mother, as written in Orach Chayim siman 4:32.

(ג) A blind person — and in Mishna Berurah he elaborated to prove that nowadays when the chazan reads it is permitted to call a blind person; and in Agudah he permitted if there is no kohen but him; and so is the view of Mateh Moshe and Levush; Bach wrote that they already practice calling a blind person before great ones of the generation and did not protest, for they relied on Mahari Molina who ruled thus, and so appears to be the essence; however an apostate blind person one should not call, for even if he were not blind he would not be called except that they practiced thus — therefore a blind apostate one should not call, end of his words; and they did not practice being precise on this, for presumably any person can read with the chazan.
