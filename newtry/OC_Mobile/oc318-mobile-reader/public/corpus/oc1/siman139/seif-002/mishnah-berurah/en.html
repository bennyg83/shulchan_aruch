(4) One must protest, etc. The Mechaber's words imply that even if he is able to read together with the chazzan word for word from the written text, he should still not be called when another person can be called. The Tur below in siman 141, and the responsum of the Rosh cited there by Beit Yosef, imply that one may be lenient in this regard. Peri Chadash. Particularly in light of what is written in seif 4 in the name of Maharil, one certainly should not be stringent.
(5) He should not go up. This is because it is forbidden to read even one letter not from the written text. See the gloss below in seif 3, according to which our custom is to be lenient in every case.
