Olas Tamid wrote: “I do not know why the Shulchan Aruch omitted the statement in the chapter HaRoeh that three things shorten a person’s life, etc., including one who is given a Sefer Torah to read and does not read.” I saw that Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah similarly wondered why Rambam and Tur omitted it. Accordingly, the difficulty is not specifically against the Shulchan Aruch, who followed Rambam and Tur. It appears to me that the primary resolution is that this applied only in the time of the Gemara, when the person called up himself read, and not the chazzan. This also resolves the question of Magen Avraham above, siman 53, seif katan 22.
