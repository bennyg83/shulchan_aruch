Seif 2: One who, etc. So Rabbi David Abudraham derived from the foregoing passage in the Sifrei. It must be that he holds that even where the chazzan reads, the person called up must prepare the reading, unlike Beit Yosef. Rosh and Rivash, however, derive it from what is stated in the Tosefta, cited by Rif and Rosh in the third chapter of Megillah, siman 10, concerning a synagogue in which there are no people who know how to read, as stated at the end of siman 143.
And if they need, etc. As stated regarding bikkurim: “And anyone who does not know how to read,” etc., and he thereby fulfills the requirement of reading.
