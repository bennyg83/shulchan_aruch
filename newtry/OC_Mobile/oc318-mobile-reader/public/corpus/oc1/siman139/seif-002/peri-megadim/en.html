A blind person — Taz in Orach Chayim letter 3; and behold the Rabbenu Bachya did not emend in Orach Chayim, and on the Mechaber relied Magen Avraham here and Magen Avraham 1. And a blind deaf-mute who does not hear — certainly forbidden to ascend, for "hearing is like reading"; but here, where even a blind person and an apostate ascend, that hearing is like reading; and in seif 1 the Rabbenu Bachya did not emend — Magen Avraham relied: one who sees and speaks but does not hear may read with the chazan; and a fool one should not call to the Torah, for he is inferior to a minor, and one would consider him like an animal (elsewhere we wrote from the beginning of Chullin: all who slaughtered, the slaughter of a monkey is invalid — its place is not here).
