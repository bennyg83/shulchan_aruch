Blessing — the Taz: he pointed to siman 47 letter 5 in the name of the Rashba that the final blessing is on the enactment — they bless as with Hallel and Megillah — not so regarding the mitzvot; see there. But in Beit Yosef in the name of Semag siman 19 (similarly Semak s.v. the reason — because of the honor of the Torah in public a final blessing was enacted; see there) and see siman 143 seif 1 that they finish the concluding blessing; see there.
