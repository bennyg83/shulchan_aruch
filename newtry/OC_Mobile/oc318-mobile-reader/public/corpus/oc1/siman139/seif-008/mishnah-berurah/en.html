(29) And they immediately called him, etc. Meaning, he did not recite any verse afterward.
(30) Because of the honor of the Torah, etc. Meaning, this blessing is not an unnecessary blessing. Nevertheless, so that the first blessing should not have been recited in vain, Peri Chadash and the other later authorities write that he should endeavor to recite at least one verse, such as Yevarechecha, while walking to the Sefer Torah.
