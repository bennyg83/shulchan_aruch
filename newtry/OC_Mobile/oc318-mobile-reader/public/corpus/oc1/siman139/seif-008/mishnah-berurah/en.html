(א) And immediately they called him, etc. — meaning he did not say any verse afterward.

(ב) Because of honor of the Torah, etc. — meaning it is not an extra blessing; nevertheless, so the first blessing not be in vain, Peri Chadash wrote, and so other Acharonim, that it is fit to read at least one verse, such as "May Heaven bless you" on his way to the Torah scroll.
