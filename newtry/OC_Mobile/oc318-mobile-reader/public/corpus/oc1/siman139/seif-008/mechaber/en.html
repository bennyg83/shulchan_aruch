Even if one blessed one's Torah blessing for oneself [i.e., as part of the morning blessings], and right afterwards they called [that person] to read from the Torah, one must repeat and bless “...asher bachar banu...” ("...who has chosen us...") when one reads from the Torah; because it was for the honor of the Torah that [this recitation of a blessing] was decreed when people read [from the Torah] in public.
