(Note 6) And he opens, etc. — for even R' Yehudah concedes, etc., that R' Meir and R' Yehudah disagree. R' Meir holds one must roll so they not say the blessings written in the Torah; R' Yehudah holds one need not roll, for all know the blessings are not, etc.; and we rule like R' Yehudah; and Tosafot wrote that l'chatchila even R' Yehudah concedes to R' Meir, and it deals only b'dieved; and Bach explained that b'dieved means if he does not roll one does not rebuke him, but l'chatchila R' Yehudah concedes it is fit to roll; Avnei Tzedek note 4.
