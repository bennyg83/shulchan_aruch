One should say "Bar'khu" and the blessings out loud; and those who say it in a whisper are making a mistake. Some say [in that case] that one must repeat it out loud.Gloss: so that the people will hear and answer “Barukh [HaShem] haM'vorakh l'olam va-ed” ("Blessed is the Lord, who is blessed for ever and ever.") (Tur). And if the congregation did not hear the one reciting the blessing [i.e., Bar'khu], even though they heard the chazzan answering, they do not answer him [i.e., to the one who whispered[, rather they answer “amen” to the words of the chazzan [i.e.. his response to the one reciting Bar'khu] (Beit Yosef in the name of Ri, Orchot Chaim, and Kol Bo).]:
