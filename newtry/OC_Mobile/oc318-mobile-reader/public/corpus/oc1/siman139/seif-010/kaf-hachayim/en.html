(10) There, in the gloss: and one whose father is an apostate, etc. — and if he converted under duress, and while still an apostate he did great favors for Israel, and at the time of his death he repented and died with his confession — when his son ascends to read in the Torah, it is fully permitted to call him by his father's name, and fit to mention his name for good and blessing; Zekan Aharon siman 95; and they brought this in Pischei Teshuvah siman 38 note 5; see there.
