10) There, in the gloss: And one whose father is an apostate, etc. And if he apostatized under duress, and while an apostate did great favors for Israel, and at the time of his death confessed and died in his confession: when his son ascends to read from the Torah, it is completely permitted to call him up by his father's name, and it is fitting that his name be mentioned for good and for blessing. Zaken Aharon, siman 95; and the Pesach HaDvir cited him in siman 38, ot 5; see there.
