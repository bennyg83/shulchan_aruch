[10] He must repeat, etc. Magen Avraham writes: “Levush wrote that if they were unwilling to wait for him until he read one verse, he should not go up at all, and another should be called in his place.” He then comments that this is a case that cannot occur, since he can easily recite the verse Yevarechecha while walking. I am astonished, for this is not mentioned in Levush, and on the contrary, Levush implies that it is not indispensable. It appears that a scribal error entered his words and that “Bach” should be read instead of “Levush,” for Bach writes: “He should not go up at all, and another should be called in his place, unless they wait for him long enough to recite Bircas Kohanim, or he goes up and recites the blessing.” According to this, there is no difficulty, because Bach requires him to recite the entire Bircas Kohanim, not merely the verse Yevarechecha, as I wrote above in siman 47, seif katan 12.
