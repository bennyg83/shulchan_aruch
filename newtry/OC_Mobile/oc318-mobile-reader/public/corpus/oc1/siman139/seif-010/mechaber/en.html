[In] the blessing [that is made] after [reading from the Torah], [the words]“asher natan lanu Torat emet” (“Who gave us the true Torah”) refers to the "Torah Shebikhtav" (Written Torah), and [the words] “v’chayyei olam nata b'tokheinu” (“and who planted eternal life within us”) refers to the "Torah Shebe-al Peh" (Oral Torah).
