Seif 4: And afterward, etc. They disagree only regarding the blessing beforehand, because of burdening the congregation. See Tosafos there. Rambam rules likewise. This is explicit in Maseches Soferim 13:8: “He opens and recites the blessing.” What is the reason? It is written, “And when he opened it,” etc. And what is written afterward? “And he rolls it and says, Blessed,” etc.
And at the time, etc. As stated above, because here there is no burden upon the congregation.
And it appears to me, etc. For this is the right side of the Holy One, blessed is He, as stated in the fifth chapter of Yoma.
