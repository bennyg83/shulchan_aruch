(11) There, in the gloss: they call him by his father's name, etc. — and it appears to me one does not mention titles (such as chaver or moreinu, etc.) — Keneset HaGedolah in Hagahot Turei Zahav note 1; Rabbi Akiva Eiger note 3; and he wrote there in Keneset HaGedolah that in these places they do not practice mentioning the father's name at the Torah ascent but the family name; and they brought Yafeh LaLev note 4, and wrote: except where in the congregation there are two equal in name and family, then the custom is to mention also the father's name; see there.
