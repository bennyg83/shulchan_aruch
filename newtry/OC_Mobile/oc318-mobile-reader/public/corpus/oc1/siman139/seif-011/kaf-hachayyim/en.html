11) There, in the gloss: They call him by his father's name, etc. And it appears to me that they do not mention titles (such as 'chaver' or 'moreinu' -- Derech HaChaim). Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos HaTur, ot 1; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 3. And the Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah wrote there that in these places they do not have the custom to mention the father's name at the ascent to the Sefer Torah, but rather his family name; and the Yafeh LaLev cited him, ot 4, and wrote: except where there are two in the congregation who are the same in name and lineage, for then the custom is to mention also his father's name; see there.
