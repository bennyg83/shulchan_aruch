(35) The Sefer Torah during the blessing. Meaning, he should hold the handles of the Sefer Torah. See Bach and Taz, who agree that during the reading he should likewise hold the Sefer Torah. Some have the custom then to hold the parchment itself through a cloth; see Magen Avraham. After the reading, the custom is to kiss the Sefer Torah. If saliva comes to his mouth, he should spit, but to the side and not opposite the Sefer Torah, which is forbidden as explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 270, and afterward kiss it. He should not kiss it and then spit. Later authorities.
(36) This Sefer Torah. The phrase implies that he is holding it in his hand.
