(א) One must hold — in writings it is written that before the blessing he should hold the two columns, and at the time of the blessing remove his left hand to strengthen the right; and in writings it is written that at the time of the blessing he should hold with both hands the Torah scroll's mantles by the cloth, and after the blessing remove the left.

(ב) This Torah scroll — for it implies he holds it in his hand; and after the reading he kisses the Torah scroll (Shulchan Aruch HaRav); and when spittle comes to him he spits, and afterward kisses — and he should not kiss and afterward spit [there].
