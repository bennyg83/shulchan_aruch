(Note 7) He rolls, etc. — for after, etc., that before reading, granted; since for R' Yehudah whom we rule like, one need not roll lest they say blessings written in the Torah — if so, he may not roll per Rabbenu Yonah because of bother of the congregation; unlike after reading, where in any case he must roll between one person and another; if so, if he rolls before the blessing there is no extra bother of the congregation — it is preferable to roll before the blessing so they not say blessings written in the Torah, even though for R' Yehudah there is no grave concern — nevertheless it is preferable to fulfill also this concern.
