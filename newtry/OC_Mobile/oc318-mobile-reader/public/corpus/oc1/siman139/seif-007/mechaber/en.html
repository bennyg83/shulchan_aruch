After the people answer to “Bar'khu et [haShem] haM'vorakh” ("Bless the Lord, the blessed One"), the one making the blessing repeats and says “Barukh [HaShem] haM'vorakh l'olam va-ed” ("Blessed is the Lord, who is blessed for ever and ever.") in order to include the one making the blessing among all those who are blessing [Hashem].
