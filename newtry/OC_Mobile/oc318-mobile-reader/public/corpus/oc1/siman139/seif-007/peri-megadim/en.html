And immediately — the Taz: general rule here is Bach's words — he wondered on Beit Yosef from Semag and from there one can say Semag means immediately; and what he wrote "occupation in Torah" means occupation that day — nevertheless now it is immediately; but what Tosafot wrote from there is no proof. What the Mechaber wrote in seif 9 — exempt from "who chose us" but obligated to study and maariv — see siman 47 Ahavah Rabbah such a case; and so Magen Avraham 12.
