(siman 3) The broth of meat (38). And it speaks of siman 202 seif 11 — meaning there is no broth of fruits, for it depends on the dispute of Rashba and Rosh in siman 202 seif 11: for the Rosh, broth that comes from fruits through soaking or cooking — one blesses on them as on the fruit itself, for their law is like the fruit itself; or meaning broth of meat — for after the fact their law is like meat, whereas on meat too one blesses shehakol.
