Seif 5 — and if he found less: per Rashba in Torah HaAdamah and brought in Beit Yosef Yoreh Deah siman 163, even with less than four, whatever is more than three and a half is fit to bless borei pri haAdamah; but per Ran there — four specifically; if so, safek berachot lehakel and one blesses shehakol; but regarding the final berachah one should drink a cup of wine and exempt through it.
