(3) [Levush] And see above siman 202, etc. The explanation is that there it is stated that b'dieved, when one blessed borei peri haEtz on water in which one soaked or cooked fruit — and one must say that it is different there, for the taste of the fruits themselves is sensed in their drinking; but in beer the taste of the barley and dates is not tasted and sensed in their drinking at all — and so wrote Bach.
