That one mixes. This entire seif is the language of Rambam, and it is plain that it speaks specifically of the five species that the passage concludes with — wheat honey, etc.; but regarding other species it is plain we follow the majority, and so in the Gemara we say: whatever has of the five species — one blesses mezonot — and that is specifically when it comes to impart taste in a mixture. If so, regarding other species, even when it comes to impart taste, we follow the majority; and so is reasonable — would one who puts onions or vinegar into a dish bless on the onions? Magen Avraham see there. And see Taz and see Peri To'ar.

The confection. Almonds that they coat in sugar — even though the fruit is completely coated, one blesses on the fruit and not on the sugar.
