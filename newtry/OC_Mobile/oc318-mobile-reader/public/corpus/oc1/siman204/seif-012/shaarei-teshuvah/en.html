Borei nefashot. Ba'er Heitev — and see in Rabbeinu Yonah, who wrote that the Gaon, wonder of the generation, author of Batei Kohanim, wrote in the margin of Yad Aharon that he saw they wait until it cools, etc.; and these are his words: there is no way of drinking it thus, and I do not act thus, and this is not the way of its enjoyment and correction; and these people have voided their minds before every person; and like that of kutach that they say: if it burned, etc.; and see in Menachot daf 70 and in Tosafot Bechorot daf 23 and in Rambam chapter 14 Ma'achalot Assurot and chapter 8 laws 16 in the matter of sheratzim — likewise we require the way of eating as proper; and see what my teacher the Peri Chadash wrote in Yoreh Deah siman 103; and those whom the Rosh wrote in chapter Kol She'ah — it is only a rabbinic prohibition stringently; and see what Maharshal wrote in chapter 8 of Ma'achalot Assurot; and my teacher the Peri Chadash in responsa siman 104; and from all the above your eyes will see clearly that these men of action did nothing — for in any case regarding berachah thus their minds are voided, and this is clear — end of his words. And in my humble opinion requires study: how does voided mind apply to the final berachah of borei nefashot — for even one who drinks water for thirst blesses afterward borei nefashot; and if one says: since there is no way of his enjoyment his mind is voided and he will not bless beforehand shehakol — it is obvious this too is not so, for certainly one must bless on any enjoyment whatsoever, unless it harms him — if not, one must bless; and in our country they are not so particular to drink with excessive heat, and voided mind does not apply; and therefore at the end of the drinking, when it is somewhat cooled, he can drink a revi'it without interruption in the time of drinking a revi'it and properly bless the final berachah; and all the more so that many poskim hold that also for drinking we require interruption in the time of eating a olive-bulk; and one can also say: since the way of drinking it is thus, it is not considered interruption; and see below siman 210 Taz regarding burnt wine; and they already extended on this in books that Ba'er Heitev and other books brought; and it appears that if he was uncertain whether he interrupted the measure of a revi'it or not — even though safek berachot lehakel — nevertheless, since it is possible to correct by drinking a revi'it or eating something whose final berachah is borei nefashot and thereby fulfill also from drinking the hot drink any final berachah; and see in responsa Shemesh Tzedakah siman 2, answers on the matter of Me'ah She'arim and his son; and in their view they conclude to bless at length — see there.
