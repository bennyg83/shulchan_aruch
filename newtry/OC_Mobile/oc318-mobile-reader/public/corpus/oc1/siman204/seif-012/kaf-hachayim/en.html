(12) There — and on chaziz, etc. — grain that did not bring a third; and because the fruit did not finish, they bless shehakol, for it descended one level. And if so, the same applies to all ground fruit that did not finish its fruit — shehakol. Magen Avraham, note 7. But borei peri haEtz is significant, and when it produces fruit one blesses on it borei peri haEtz. Orach Mishpat, note 7. And this is when it is not bitter and dusty and also is eaten without duress, as written above siman 202 seif 2 and in our words there, note 34 — see there.
