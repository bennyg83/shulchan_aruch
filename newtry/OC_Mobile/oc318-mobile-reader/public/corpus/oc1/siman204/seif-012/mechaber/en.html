Whatever is primary and with it is secondary — one blesses on the primary and exempts the secondary. And anything that one mixes to stick or to give scent or to paint the dish — that is secondary. But if he mixed in order to give taste in mixtures — that is primary; therefore kinds of honey that they cook and put wheat milk in them to stick and make kinds of sweetening — one does not bless in any case, because the honey is primary:And it appears that that which if one mixed in order to give taste in mixtures it is primary — that is specifically when there is substance of the thing giving taste and it is an important thing; but spices that they put into a compounding — even though they are for giving taste — one does not bless on them, for they are nullified in their minority even though they give taste; therefore the custom is not to bless except on the compounding and not on the spices in it.
