And everything that one mixes, etc. — this entire seif is the language of Rambam chapter 3. And it is simple it speaks specifically in the five species, for in this the whole chapter speaks; and as it concludes "therefore, etc., lentils, wheat" — but in other species it is simple we follow the majority, and so Rosh. And so in the Gemara we say: everything that has from the five species — bless shehakol — and this is specifically when it comes to impart taste in mixtures, as written siman 208 seif 2. If so, in other species even when coming to impart taste we follow the majority, as written there seif 7 regarding rice. And so it is reasonable — would one who puts onions or vinegar into a dish bless specifically on the onions and forget the words [of the Rambam]? And this requires further study on Rabbeinu Yosef and Rama who were strained in this — and the matter is clear, as I wrote.
