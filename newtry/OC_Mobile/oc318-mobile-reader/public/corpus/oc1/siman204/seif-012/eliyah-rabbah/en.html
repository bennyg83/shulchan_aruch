(12) Because of danger, etc. And Bach disagrees on this, that one does not bless at all on a matter of prohibition; and likewise Derishah wrote that eating prohibition is distress for him and not pleasure, as appears in Choshen Mishpat siman 234. But the Taz and Magen Avraham agreed to bless, and one may rely on them, since for many poskim even without danger one blesses; and furthermore, after this halachah Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yeruham in the name of Rama — and I examined in Sefer Adam VaChavah page 147b that he did not rule according to Rama's responsum except according to what he thought the Rosh ruled like Rambam that one does not bless on a matter of prohibition; but in truth that he holds like Ra'avad, as the author there wrote — Rabbeinu Yeruham did not rule according to Rama's responsum.
