[Levush] But one who was compelled, etc. Taz disagrees and says that this is comparable to one who eats because of danger. The cases may be distinguished. Magen Avraham wonders from siman 475, where one who was compelled to eat matzah fulfills his obligation. In my humble opinion, although compelled eating is considered benefit, we nevertheless do not recite blessings over involuntary benefits.
