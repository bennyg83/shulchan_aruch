And bread that has molded, etc. — that is to say, when it has spoiled completely, since it is not fit for eating, one does not bless on it at all. And likewise a dish whose form has changed and has also spoiled a little; but if it has spoiled completely until it is not fit for eating, one does not bless on it at all, for there is no enjoyment in it (Beit Yosef).

And regarding broth (merak) — meaning broth of meat; whereas broth of fruit — see above in siman 202.

And regarding chaziz — specifically chaziz that grows in the wild; but if it grows in a garden, one blesses on it borei peri haAdamah [chapter 3 of Eruvin].
