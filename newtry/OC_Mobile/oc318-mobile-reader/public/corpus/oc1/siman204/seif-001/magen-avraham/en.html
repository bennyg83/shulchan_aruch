Herbs and mushrooms. From the air they grow.
Palm shoots (kora). People do not plant the tree with intent to eat the kora when soft, only that it should harden and become a tree.
In their shells. And this is unlike regarding boser that one blesses Borei Peri HaAdamah in siman 202 seif 2 , for here the main eating is the outer shell and people did not plant them with intent for the shell (Rashba siman 328).
Spoiled grain. Grain that did not reach a third , because its fruit is not finished one blesses Shehakol, for one descends one level. See siman 202 seif 2. If so, likewise for all fruit of the ground that did not finish its fruit , Shehakol.
Barley flour. In the Gemara we say: since it changed , and this is unlike like olive oil: although it changed, one blesses Borei Peri HaEitz, for there it has no other benefit and the fruit is primary, therefore it was planted initially; not so with barley which has other benefit in bread , therefore it left the category of fruit and did not enter the category of its manner of eating. One might say: since it is hard for worms, one should not bless on it at all , it teaches us: since it has benefit from it, one must bless. See siman 208 in the section beginning.
Date beer, etc. It is difficult , what difference from fruit water that one blesses Borei Peri HaEitz per Rosh, as written siman 202 seif 7. And it is possible that case is different , there the taste of the fruit is sensed in the water, whereas in beer the taste of dates and barley is not sensed at all but a completely different taste (Bach). And in responsum of Maharam siman 154 he wrote that there it speaks when the water is very thick and there is substance from the cooking. And see siman 202 seif 2 another answer. And Aruch HaTeshuvah wrote the barley was nullified in six parts , and not precise, for only regarding wine do we say so , see siman 16.
Wild grasses. They are not significant; and the Ari would bless on tree [type] Borei Peri HaEitz and on ground type Borei Peri HaAdamah (Kavanot).

And see Bach who was strained and I do not know where he likens it , this is unlike specifically those that are not fit to eat in their form one does not bless, for one who eats them on Yom Kippur is exempt; not so anise and coriander which are sweet to the palate, therefore one blesses Shehakol , for it is obvious one who eats them on Yom Kippur is liable.
