(1) [Seif 1] On a thing whose growth is not from the ground, etc. — one who eats an egg yolk as it is, raw, to clear the voice, blesses, since it is his sustenance, even though he does not enjoy its taste in eating. But if one blessed on a lie, thinking it was sweet, and it was found bitter — it does not help if he eats it so that his berachah should not be in vain, for there is no enjoyment. Machatzit HaShekel, note 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, note 17. And regarding the final berachah of yolk — I already wrote in responsum 572 of Be'er Mayim Chayyim that its law is like food and its measure is a kezayit; but regarding albumen there is room to question whether its measure is a kezayit like food or a revi'it like drink — and therefore we wrote there that one should eat less than a kezayit so as not to become obligated in a final berachah, or drink the measure of a revi'it at one time and bless a final berachah; and if one ate the measure of a kezayit of yolk with albumen, one should eat or drink something whose final berachah is borei nefashot and intend to exempt them — see there.
