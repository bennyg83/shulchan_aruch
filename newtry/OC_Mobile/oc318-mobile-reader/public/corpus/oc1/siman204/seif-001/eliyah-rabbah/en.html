(1) The navelot, etc. The language of the Tur — and they are a type of dates that do not ripen on the tree. And Perishah wrote the explanation: on the tree their cooking is not finished; but at the time of the berachah it is already finished and fit for eating, for they place them in straw or dry them in the sun, and the sun burns and renders them fit for eating, as Rashi explained on "bishulei kamarah"; and Beit Yosef brought this at the beginning of siman 202. And what Beit Yosef wrote on Rabbeinu Yeruham — that he explained navelot is a type of fruit whose cooking is not finished, therefore shehakol — he was not precise, etc.; he meant to say that from his explanation it implies that at the time one wants to eat it and bless on it, its cooking is still not finished, and therefore they bless shehakol — and this is not so, as he wrote, end of his words. It implies that he understood that the words of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch are one explanation; but Bach there wrote that there are two explanations, and so I wrote in siman 202, note 13; also the words of Lechem Mishnah that I brought above contradict this, and it requires study — I abbreviated.
