Seif 1: "and it spoiled." Language of Tur:

"They are dates," etc. See above siman 202 seif 9.

And on brine water. This is zamit according to Tur's explanation; and in the name of R' Hai Gaon he explained that it is the scum on the surface of the kettle; and Rashi explained there 36a like mayeira; and likewise Arukh for one explanation, and likewise Rashi and Rosh and Ran in Nedarim 55b, and Hagahot Semak wrote that it is brine water.

And on broth. Thus Arukh explained, and he wrote that in Greek they call broth zomin.

And on kora, etc. — 36a.

And on etc., and on etc. — Tosafot there 36a s.v. lo.

And on chaziz. — Eruvin 28b.

And on kora, etc., and on etc. — Berakhot there, and he said qimcha de'i is a separate matter he held — and siman 208 seif 5.

And on beer, etc. — Bava Batra 96b.

And on water, etc. — Response of Rosh and Tosafot Berakhot 38a s.v. hai; and beer, etc.; and s.v. veha, etc.; and nevertheless, etc.

And on herbs, etc. — Tur and Orchot Chayim in the name of Raavad, and as written in siman 203 seif 4; and this requires further study — for dill and mushrooms are not sown, and we say in Berakhot 39b that mushrooms did not multiply, etc., page 40 — if they had multiplied from the ground they would bless borei peri haAdamah upon them; and likewise he said about dew — and they are made for taste and one blesses shehakol; but we say regarding Shabbat that for taste one blesses borei peri haAdamah, and also on Shabbat itself requires further study — for if so what do they need there 39a "water of barley," etc.; and Rashi there s.v. lemetukei, etc.; and likewise in Shulchan Gavoah that even on their water one blesses borei peri haAdamah, and it is possible specifically their water.

And on vinegar. — Mishnah 40:20, "they mixed," etc. As written regarding Yom Kippur in Yoma chapter 2, and Yom Kippur and berakhah are the same, as written in Berakhot 36b, and this is in seif 2 on, etc. Rosh there.
