Bread. Taz — Bach raised borei peri haAdamah with taste of vinegar and smell of wine without cold spots, or with cold spots and smell of wine borei peri haAdamah and cold spots and taste of vinegar shehakol; or for kiddush taste of vinegar or cold spots alone one does not make kiddush; and that one makes kiddush on flour in snifo or white mold is more than flour that only has white specks, or cold spots that began slightly to sour — see there; and for practical law one should rule like Taz in Raavad 3 and Magen Avraham 2 agreed to this; and therefore the Mechaber did not write there "taste" but that it is obvious one does not bless borei peri haAdamah even without cold spots (and what Ran wrote — one can say even in the morning and it is not like country wine — see there in Shaarei Teshuvah); and white mold for kiddush is invalid; and see regarding flour Magen Avraham Raavad note 4 conceded to Bach on this, and it will be explained there; and here one should not extend.
