Seif 5. If he found less, then according to the Rashba in Toras HaBayis, cited by Beit Yosef, Yoreh Deah, siman 163, even when there is less than four parts, so long as there is more than three and a half, one may recite Borei Peri HaGafen. According to the Ran there, however, four parts are specifically required. Therefore, because of the doubt concerning blessings, one should be lenient and recite Shehakol. Regarding the after-blessing, however, he should drink a cup of wine and thereby discharge this beverage.
