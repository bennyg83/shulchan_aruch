And on kora. Ba'er Heitev wrote that in Pnei Meiros he disagrees with Taz. And see in Eshel Avraham that he too disagrees with Taz, and see what Yad Ephraim wrote that even though one can reconcile Taz's words, nevertheless the common practice is like Peri Megadim and Eshel Avraham that one does not distinguish between legumes and legumes. And in what Taz wrote that even on the shoots one blesses borei peri haAdamah — they also disagree in Peri Megadim and Eshel Avraham, and they wrote it is not fruit and we descend one level to bless shehakol. See Yad Ephraim that the essence is like Taz. And regarding shoots of those called tsuker arbeis, that many chew the shoots together with the legumes — it seems all agree; see there.
