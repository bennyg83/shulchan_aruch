[Levush] And in every place, etc. Rama concludes: provided that the wine is not only one-sixth of the water, for then it is certainly nullified. Olat Tamid writes that nowadays, when we do not customarily dilute weak wines with water, if even a little water became mixed into the wine, although the water is less than the wine, one nevertheless does not recite Borei Pri HaGefen. In practice this requires study. It appears that for this reason the Levush omitted Rama's words. See Responsa Rabbi Yom Tov Tzahalon, siman 22.
Rather, Shehakol. Taz objects and rules that one recites Borei Pri HaGefen, as with figs. He was not precise, as appears from siman 202 seif 11; moreover, this resembles date beer in seif 1. Magen Avraham likewise rules Shehakol.
