Wine lees — one blesses on them borei peri haGafen. If one put water in them: if one put three measures of water and finds four, it is like mixed wine and one blesses borei peri haGafen. And if one finds less — even though there is in it a taste of wine — it is merely acid (kayuha) in general and one blesses only shehakol. And this is regarding their wines that were strong; but our wines that are not so strong — even if one throws three and four come — one does not bless on it borei peri haGafen. And it appears that one estimates by the measure with which they mix wine in that place:provided that the wine is not one part in six of water, for then it is certainly nullified [Agur].]:
