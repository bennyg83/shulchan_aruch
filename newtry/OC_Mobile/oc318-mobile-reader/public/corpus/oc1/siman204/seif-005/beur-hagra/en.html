Seif 5: "he gave," etc. — There, and as Tosafot explain, and as said Rabbah and R' Yehuda, etc.; and Rambam and Tur explained that the first clause is "he bought three" and four came specifically, and so much from Rashbam there s.v. pash lehu. And one portion, etc.; and even though for yayin nesech we hold six there on full wine, they learned from this that even a fifth, two cups, etc.; and this is what is written in the gloss "provided," etc., and refers to full wine; and Av Beit Yosef in the name of Agur, and see siman 472 seif 7.

And this is, etc. — As written at the end of chapter 8 of Shabbat: Shironi wine alone, etc.; and all the more our wines, as written in Tosafot and Rashbam Pesachim 108b, and as written in siman 472.
