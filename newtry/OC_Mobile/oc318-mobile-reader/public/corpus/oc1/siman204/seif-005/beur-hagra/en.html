Seif 5: If he added, etc. This is stated there, following the first Tanna, and as Rabbah and Rav Yosef explain there, etc. Rambam, Tur, and the other poskim explain that the opening case specifically concerns one who poured three measures and obtained four. This is also implied by Rashbam there, at the end of s.v. pash lehu. “And one and a half,” etc. Although regarding yayin nesech we rule that wine is nullified in six parts, there the subject is actual wine. That rule was derived from Avodah Zarah, chapter 5, concerning two cups, etc. This is the meaning of the gloss, “provided that,” etc., which refers to actual wine. See Beit Yosef in the name of Agur, and what I wrote in siman 272, seif 7.
And this means, etc. As stated at the beginning of chapter 8 of Shabbos, specifically Sharonite wine, etc. This is certainly true of our wines, as Tosafos and Rashbam write in Pesachim 108b, and as stated in siman 272.
