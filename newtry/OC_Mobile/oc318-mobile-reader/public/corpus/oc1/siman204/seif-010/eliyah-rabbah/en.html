(10) [Levush] And in every place, etc. Rama concluded: provided the wine is not one-sixth in the water — then certainly it is nullified, end of his words. And Olat Tamid wrote, and it appears that nowadays they do not practice at all to dilute weak wines in water; if a little water became mixed in, even if the water that became mixed in is less than the wine — even so one does not bless on it borei peri haGefen; however, for practical application it requires study, end of his words. And it appears that for this reason Levush omitted Rama's words — and it is easy to understand; and see responsum of R' Yom Tov Tzahalon siman 22.
