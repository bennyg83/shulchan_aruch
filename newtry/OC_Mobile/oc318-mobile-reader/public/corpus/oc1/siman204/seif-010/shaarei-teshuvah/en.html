Ba'er Heitev — and see in Eshel Avraham in the name of the Ari; and it appears the essence is like Magen Avraham, since in any case the view of Rabbeinu Yonah that Beit Yosef brought is to bless on vinegar; and also this is not in the category of safek berachah that perhaps it will sour within three days — for nevertheless, since now it is not so strong, it does not harm; and there in Yoreh Deah 31 one sees the way of libation thus — only because they are not expert they are stringent; see there; and in Yad Ephraim what he wrote on this: in Shulchan Aruch seif 5 in the gloss — provided the wine is not one in six in water; and see in the words of Ateret Zekenim in Beit Chadash in Bava Batra — he wrote: I heard someone who doubted that they are accustomed to put much water on raisins until the raisins are one in six in water, and if so the wine is nullified in water as written in siman 204; but in Menachot daf 55 on dried figs they say: since one can boil them and return them as they were, we estimate them as the quantity they were before they were cooked — if so, likewise raisins: behold we see that after boiling or steeping in water they grow double or more; if so, we estimate thus — only one must be careful that there not be so much water that the raisins, after growing, are one in six in water, for then certainly the berachah would be in vain — end of his words; and see in Yad Ephraim what he wrote on this; and per this, what he wrote there in Beit Chadash that it should not be one in six after they grew — not precise, for even less than this too one should not bless, for the main growth depends on the moisture in the raisins — see there; and see in responsa Chatam Sofer siman 100 regarding kneading in raisin wine whether it is considered fruit water — he wrote that in raisins that are through human action there is no power to change the essence of the water, etc. — see there.
