(א) Bee honey, etc. — for it is in the category of something whose growth is not from the ground [Beit Yosef]:

(ב) Behold it is like other honey — for it was explained in siman 202 seif 8 that one blesses on it shehakol, and he mentioned it only as a sign in general; but in truth even those who dispute regarding date honey agree regarding bee honey, for although bees bring fruit juice into their bodies and suck from them and thereby the honey is made — nevertheless the taste of the fruits is not sensed at all in the honey [Bach]:
