2) There: Milk and cheese. If one recited Borei Pri HaAdamah over cheese, Halachos Ketanos, volume 1, siman 238, writes that he did not fulfill his obligation. See, however, Eruvin 27b, which considers everything produce of the earth except water, salt, and fish. The Rambam in chapter 7 of Hilchos Maaser Sheni, law 5, writes that honey, eggs, and milk are likewise included among produce of the earth because they derive from things that grow from the earth; see there. Regarding truffles and mushrooms, Berachos 40b likewise states that they grow from the earth, and the Shulchan Aruch rules accordingly in Yoreh Deah, siman 217, seif 23 and elsewhere. It therefore appears that since they are considered produce of the earth, after the fact, if one recited Borei Pri HaAdamah, he fulfilled his obligation. Likewise, if one recited Borei Minei Mezonos over any food, he fulfilled his obligation, because in the Torah's language everything except water and salt is called nourishment. Chayei Adam, rule 58, paragraph 3, and Nishmas Adam there; Ben Ish Chai, parashas Balak, paragraph 13.
