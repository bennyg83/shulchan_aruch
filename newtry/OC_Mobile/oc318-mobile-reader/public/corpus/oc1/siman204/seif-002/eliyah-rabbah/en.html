(2) Truffles are called those that are found under the ground and are created from the fat of the earth. And mushrooms are one species that sometimes grow on trees — Rabbeinu Yonah. The Taz wrote that shoots of legumes that are called in Arabic "shotin," which are sown in a garden — borei peri haAdamah; but in a field they sow them to leave them until they harden — even what they uproot while still moist, one blesses shehakol.
