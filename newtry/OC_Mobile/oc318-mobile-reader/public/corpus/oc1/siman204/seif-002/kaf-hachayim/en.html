(2) There — milk of cheese. And if one blessed on cheese borei peri haAdamah, one has not fulfilled one's obligation — HaLeket vol. 1 siman 238. However, see in tractate Eruvin 27b that all are considered "products of the ground" except water, salt, and fish; and Rambam wrote in chapter 7 of Hilchot Ma'aser Sheni, halachah 5, that likewise honey, eggs, and milk — behold they are in the category of products of the ground, for they are products of its products — see there. And likewise regarding truffles and mushrooms they said in tractate Berakhot 40b that they are products of the ground, and they ruled in Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De'ah siman 217 seif 23 — see there. And therefore it appears that since they are products of the ground, b'dieved if one blessed borei peri haAdamah one has fulfilled. And likewise if one blessed mezonot on any thing one has fulfilled, for in the language of the Torah everything is called food except water and salt. Chayei Adam, general rule 58, note 3; and in Ben Ish Chai there. Ben Ish Chai, parashat Balak, note 13.
