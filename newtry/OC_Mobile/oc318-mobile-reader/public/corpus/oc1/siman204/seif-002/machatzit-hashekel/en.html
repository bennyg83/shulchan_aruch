(siman 2) And withered fruit, etc. — Bach ruled likewise if they fell, etc., and in Sefer Ateret Rosh in the name of Perishah that even if he placed them afterward in straw and the like and thereby they were cooked — nevertheless one blesses shehakol on them. But Sdei Chemed brought in the name of Sefer Lechem Mishneh that if they were cooked afterward through placing them in straw and the like, one blesses borei peri haEtz on them, and he wrote that such is the custom.
