(siman 13) He does not, etc. — as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 123: vinegar specifically such — if a gentile touched it, it does not forbid it because of yayin nesech, for it has no wine name; but if not strong — so one forbids through contact.

And that Rosh did not answer thus — he challenged: why do we teach that on vinegar one blesses shehakol, yet we hold one who drinks vinegar on Yom Kippur is exempt? And the Talmud compares to ginger and pepper — blessing on Yom Kippur. And answer one answer as Shulchan Aruch wrote — it speaks when they mixed it in water and drank it for healing, and it is like oil through anigron above. And Magen Avraham Levush holds it speaks of vinegar without water but that is not strong.
