Seif 8: "all," etc. — 36a, 38a, and Tosafot s.v. kiun, etc.

If they forced him, etc. Magen Avraham — it is comparable to a woman [who was ravished], etc., and as written in chapter 4 of Ketubot, and so too here. And it is known that Yom Kippur and a berachah are equal, as written in Berakhot 36b and Yoma 81b, and as the Rosh wrote regarding vinegar as above seif 2. And they say in Yerushalmi and in Midrash Rabbah, parashat Naso, section 9, 172b — that woman who came before R' Yosi; he said to her, "I was ravished by force"; he said to her, "And was it not pleasant for you?"; she said to him, "If one concealed a finger in honey on Yom Kippur and placed it in your mouth, would it not be pleasant for you?"; and R' Yosi conceded to her. Since, etc. — the Taz and Magen Avraham already wondered about this there.
