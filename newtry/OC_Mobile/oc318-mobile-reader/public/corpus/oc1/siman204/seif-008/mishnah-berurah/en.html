(43) If their taste is good, etc. — meaning: even though he does not desire them at all and does not eat them except under duress of illness — since in any case the palate enjoys from them; and to exclude if they are bad, that he has no pleasure from this — even though he is healed from them he does not bless at all.
(44) He blesses on them, etc. — see below in siman 211 in the gloss what is written there in Mishna Berurah.
(45) Even though the palate, etc. — and it is not comparable to what is written below in seif 9 regarding one who eats forbidden food on account of danger, that he blesses on it since in any case he enjoys — for there too the eating is under duress, but it is different there: the duress is not on the eating itself; unlike here, where the eating itself is under duress that they force him to eat — it does not apply to obligate him to bless on his enjoyment since it is against his will. Such is the Rama's view, and many latter authorities agreed with him; and some disagree and hold that since in the end his throat enjoys from this it is considered eating and he must bless on it, like that of the Sages of the Talmud; and see in Chayei Adam who ruled that regarding bread, if he ate the amount of satiation of his Birkat HaMazon obligation, he blesses from the Torah.
