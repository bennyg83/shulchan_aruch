(8) There — and on truffles and mushrooms. Truffles are called those that are created under the ground and are created from the fat of the earth. And mushrooms are another species that sometimes grow on trees — R' Yonah; Derishah, note 8; Eliyah Rabbah, note 2; Orach Mishpat, note 4. And in the land of Babylonia they do not practice eating mushrooms, and I heard the reason — because there are worms in them; and therefore in a place where they eat them one must investigate them, lest there be worms in them.
