(siman 8) Barley flour, etc. — one might say: since it is hard, etc. And see siman 208 — these are the words of the Talmud daf 36a that one must examine: from that Shmuel said barley flour one blesses shehakol — since he did not state the praise of wheat flour which is more important, rather necessarily on wheat flour one blesses borei peri haAdamah, and reject that even wheat flour one blesses shehakol — and so is the ruling in siman 208. And that he specified barley — for if he had specified wheat one might say barley one does not bless at all since it is hard for worms — it teaches us; and so one must say in Shulchan Aruch, since it wrote in siman 208 regarding wheat flour which is more important one blesses shehakol — all the more so barley. And what does it teach us here? And one must say as the Talmud wrote — one might think barley one does not bless at all.
