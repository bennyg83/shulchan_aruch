Seif 8 — all foods and drinks that a person eats and drinks for healing. NB: see what I wrote on this in my work on Orach Chayyim from the year 5644, in responsum siman 196, to my friend the sharp rabbi, our teacher R' Tzvi Hirsh Halevi of Horowitz, in explanation of these laws, and in the words of the Taz there — and study well. In that same seif in the gloss: if they forced him to eat or drink. NB: see in Magen Avraham note 20 regarding a sick person who ate on Yom Kippur, and see in my responsum to the holy community of Lvov in the omissions to the laws of Shevuot siman 239, in the second omissions there — I discussed this halachah at length and made supports for it from the words of the Ran, end of chapter 8 of Shevuot, regarding one who swore not to eat for seven days — see there, and study well.
