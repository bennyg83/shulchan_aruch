Seif 8. All foods and beverages that a person eats or drinks for medicinal purposes. Note: See what I wrote on this in my responsa on Orach Chayim from the year 5604, siman 196, addressed to my distinguished colleague Rabbi Tzvi Hirsch HaLevi Horowitz, where I explained these laws and the words of the Taz. See there and examine carefully.
There, in the gloss to that seif. If he was compelled to eat or drink. Note: See Magen Avraham, seif katan 20, concerning a sick person who ate on Yom Kippur. See also my responsum to the holy community of Lemberg, in the addenda to Hilchos Shevuos, siman 239, in the second set of addenda, where I discussed this law at length and supported it from the words of the Ran at the beginning of the third chapter of Shevuos concerning one who swore not to eat for seven days. See there and examine carefully.
