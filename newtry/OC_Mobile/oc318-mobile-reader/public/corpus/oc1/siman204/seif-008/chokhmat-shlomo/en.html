Seif 8. "All foods and drinks that a person eats and drinks for healing." NB: See what I wrote on this in my work on Orach Chayim from the year 5604, responsum siman 196, to my friend the sharp rabbi, our teacher R. Tzvi Hirsch HaLevi Horowitz, in explanation of these laws and of the words of the Taz; see there and examine carefully.
There, in that same seif, in the gloss. "If they compelled him to eat or drink." NB: See Magen Avraham s.k. 20 regarding a sick person who ate on Yom Kippur, and see my responsum to the holy community of Lvov, in the omissions to Hilchot Shevuot, siman 239, in the second omissions, where I analyzed this law extensively and made supports for it from the words of the Ran at the beginning of chapter 3 of Shevuot, concerning one who swore not to eat for seven days; see there and examine carefully.
