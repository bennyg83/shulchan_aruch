Seif 4: "all," etc. — There 95b: R' Yitzchak, wine, etc., R' Yehuda admits in portzma, etc.; and even though the halakhah follows R' Yehuda regarding wine sold in a shop, as written in seif 3, nevertheless in such a case R' Yehuda admits that this is portzma, and this is what he said "its smell," etc., according to R' Yehuda, "all," etc.

Lees, etc. — Understand according to Rashi there 96b: "rather in a goblet three," etc.
