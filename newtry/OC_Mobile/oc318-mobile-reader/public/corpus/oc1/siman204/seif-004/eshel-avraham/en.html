And these are the words of Eliyah Rabbah: it deals with a case where it does not become complete vinegar until it harms — only a taste of vinegar is in it — and they refrain from it; for if not so, one does not bless on it at all, etc. Furthermore Or Chadash wrote: vinegar that one throws on the ground and it does not boil — it does not have the status of vinegar and one blesses on it — and it is not so, for from Yoreh De'ah it is proven to the contrary — that the reason there is because we are not expert whether it is vinegar, therefore they are stringent regarding yayin nesech until it boils; but regarding berachah we follow the lenient approach not to bless. And furthermore it is explained there that three days before it boils it is certainly complete vinegar — and how can one bless, perhaps it will boil within three days, etc.; also Magen Avraham was not precise on this — see there.
