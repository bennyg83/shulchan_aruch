And regarding the palm shoot, see Taz. Berachos 36a explains the word “kora” as the tender inner growth of a palm tree. It consists of the soft young branches of the date palm, which later harden and produce dates; see Beer HaGolah, letter 2. What was written regarding Arabic thistles requires study. Only a minority plant them to be eaten in this manner, and they resemble peppers as explained in siman 202, Magen Avraham, seif katan 35. Moreover, removing the palm shoot damages the tree, unlike this case. From where do we know that the blessing Borei Peri HaAdamah differs from Borei Peri HaEitz in this respect? See siman 205, Taz, letter 3. This requires study.
