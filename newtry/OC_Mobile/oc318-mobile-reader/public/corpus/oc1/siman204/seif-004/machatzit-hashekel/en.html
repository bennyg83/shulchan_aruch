(siman 4) Herbs and mushrooms — from the air they grow — implies both are one species. And so is evident from Rashi's language on daf 40b on that which the Talmud answers: why does one bless shehakol even though they grow from the ground? Since Rabbi draws from the land — nevertheless they do not draw nourishment from the land. And Rashi wrote: Rabbi draws from the land — from the moisture of the earth they grow on trees and on vessels — end of his words. But Terumat HaDeshen wrote, and these are his words: chamayim are called those found under the ground and created from the fat of the earth; and mushrooms are another species that sometimes grow on trees — end of his words; they brought this in Sefer Ateret Rosh.
