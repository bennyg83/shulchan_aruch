All people — etc. And Magen Avraham wrote that since we are not expert in this measure well — for there are people who drink and there are those who do not drink — therefore one should drink wine in a case of doubt and exempt it with the first berachah and the last berachah. [And see in Machatzis HaShekel who directed Magen Avraham's words to a different intent — namely that the taste in the Tosefta from which the source of this halachah is derived is because of doubt — see there; but it does not seem so in the Tosefta that Beit Yosef brought — see there. And likewise it is proven in Be'ur HaGra, who wrote that this means portami that is mentioned in the gemara — and behold portami is an explanation of boiled wine that was mentioned there earlier. And there certainly it is not because of doubt but because it changed for the worse — they bless initially shehakol and afterward borei nefashot, as the poskim wrote; rather Magen Avraham's intent is that he was concerned with the words of Rabbeinu Tam — see there.]
