(46) On account of danger — such as a sick person who must eat forbidden foods for his healing; and all the more so if he eats permitted food during a prohibited time, such as a sick person on Yom Kippur — they bless on it, as below in siman 618 at the end.

(47) He blesses on it — for since it is danger, the Torah permitted him to eat; and on the contrary he fulfills a mitzvah to save his soul; and this is not in the category of other forbidden matters that we rule above in siman 196 that one does not bless on them.

(48) Initially and finally — for even though willingly he would not have eaten this thing except under duress of illness — nevertheless, since he is already sick and desires healing through this food and drink, it is considered eating that has pleasure. And if it is something his soul loathes — then the palate does not enjoy from it and one need not bless on it.
