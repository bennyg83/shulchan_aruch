Perishah likewise writes that eating prohibited food is pain rather than benefit, as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 234. Taz and Magen Avraham, however, agree that one should recite a blessing, and one may rely on them because many poskim require a blessing even when there is no danger. Moreover, after this ruling Beit Yosef cites Rabbenu Yerucham in the name of Rama. I examined Sefer Adam VeChavah, page 147b, and found that Rabbenu Yerucham accepted Rama's responsum only because he thought the Rosh ruled like Rambam that one does not bless over prohibited food. In truth, however, the Rosh follows Raavad, as the gloss there explains, and therefore Rabbenu Yerucham did not definitively rule in accordance with Rama's responsum.
