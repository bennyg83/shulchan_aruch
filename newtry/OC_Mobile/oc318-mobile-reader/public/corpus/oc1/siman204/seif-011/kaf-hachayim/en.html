(11) There — and on sweet almonds, etc. The reason is because when they are soft they eat the outer peel and it was not planted with intent to eat them thus while soft, but only to eat their kernels when they are cooked; therefore one blesses on them shehakol. Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba; Taz, note 5; Magen Avraham, note 10. And see above siman 202 seif 5 and in our words there, note 49.
