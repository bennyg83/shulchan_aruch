(א) They are primary, etc. — for the honey is only a preparation for the compounded thing that it makes fit and prepares for eating. And likewise almonds coated in sugar (tzuker) — even though they are coated completely, one blesses borei peri haEtz, for although there is much sugar, the fruit is primary [Peri Megadim]:
(ב) Ground very fine — specifically when its nature and form are recognizable; but if it melted completely until its essential form was lost — bless l'chatchila shehakol, as explained above siman 202 seif 7 in the gloss (Shelah); and see above siman 203 seif 7 and in Mishna Berurah there:
(ג) Roses — borei peri haAdamah: namely rozin in foreign tongue; and although they are tree fruit that grow on the tree, nevertheless since they are not the main fruit — for their seed is the main fruit and the leaves that they compound are the flowers — therefore one blesses borei peri haAdamah. And likewise on lavender in foreign tongue one blesses borei peri haAdamah, for although lavender grows on a tree, the main fruit are the black berries that grow on that tree — and it is very similar to the law of roses [latter authorities]. And if you say: since roses are not fit for eating — not raw nor cooked unless cooked with honey — if so it should be that honey is primary? And one can say: on the contrary — since the main preparation of roses for eating is only with honey, it is honey that is secondary, for it only comes to make the rose fit for eating, and it is like fruits not eaten raw that one blesses after cooking with the blessing appropriate to them [as below at the beginning of siman 205] — and we do not say that one should bless on them shehakol because the liquid absorbed in them made them fit for eating [Beit Yosef]:
(ד) But for healing, etc. — the reason is that although one enjoys it, this is not its main enjoyment, since healthy people are not accustomed to eat it thus — it stands only for healing:
