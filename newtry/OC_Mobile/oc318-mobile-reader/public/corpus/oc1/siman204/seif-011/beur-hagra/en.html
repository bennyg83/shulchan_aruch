Seif 11: "preserved," etc. — Rashi Yoma chapter 1, 2b s.v. himeleta, etc., and this is what he said "even they are," etc.

And on rose preserves. — Even though they are tree fruits, as written in siman 216 seif 3, nevertheless since they are not the main fruit one blesses borei peri haAdamah, as with capers; not so regarding smell, as written.

And all, etc. — As written 38a for drinking; but Shulchan Aruch omitted it, as written in s.v. that the reason there is not medicinal, as written in siman 208 seif 6; and Magen Avraham.
