11) There: And over sweet almonds, etc. The reason is that when they are soft people eat their outer peel, but they are not planted with the intention of eating them while soft. They are planted to eat their kernels after they ripen. Therefore, one recites Shehakol over them. Beis Yosef in the name of Rashba; Taz, paragraph 5; Magen Avraham, paragraph 10. See above, siman 202, seif 5, and our words there, paragraph 49.
