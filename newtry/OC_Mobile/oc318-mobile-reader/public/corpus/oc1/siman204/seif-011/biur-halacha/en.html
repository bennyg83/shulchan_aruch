And on roses — borei nefashot — see in Shaarei Teshuvah what he brought in the name of Baruch Kaparah; however, in Be'er Heitev, Chayei Adam, and Sha'arei Adam they copied Shulchan Aruch's ruling. Nevertheless, if one comes to prepare honey and puts crushed spices in it and the main part of his eating is the honey — he does not bless except on the honey, and the spices are secondary to it [and roses and quinces are different, for they are the main element]; but if the main part of his eating is the spices — for example when there are many of them and they are spiced in honey — it is obvious that one blesses with the berachah of the spices, and this is that case of Rabbeinu Gershom, seif 7 [Taz and Peri Megadim].
