Ground very fine. Specifically when its nature and form are recognizable; but if its essential form was lost , bless Shehakol, as written siman 202 seif 7 in the gloss (Shelah). And see there that specifically when they melted completely one blesses Shehakol , and so it implies here in Beit Yosef.
Roses , namely rozin in foreign tongue. And although they grow on the tree, since their seed is the main fruit and the leaves are the flowers (Beit Yosef, Kol Bo). See siman 202 seif 6. And likewise londra , bless Borei Peri HaAdamah, for the main fruit are the black berries (Shelah). And if you say: since roses are not fit to eat , not raw nor cooked unless cooked with honey , if so it should be honey is primary? And this is unlike: on the contrary , since the main preparation of roses for eating is only with honey, it is honey that is secondary, for it only comes to make the rose fit for eating, and it is like fruits not eaten raw that one blesses after cooking with the blessing appropriate to them (Beit Yosef). See siman 202 seif 1.
