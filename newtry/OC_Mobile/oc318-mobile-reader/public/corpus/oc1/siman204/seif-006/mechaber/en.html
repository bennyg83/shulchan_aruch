Tamad (second wine) that they make from grape husks — when they put water on them, their law is like lees. And this is when they were stopped with a beam; but if they were trodden only with the foot — even if one put three measures of water and found only three or less — one blesses on it borei peri haGafen, for it is wine and the water is absorbed in the pomace, and in what emerges from them there is much wine:Pomace upon which they placed figs to strengthen the power of the wine — even though the pomace is the majority, nevertheless all the power of the figs is in the beverage, and one should not bless borei peri haGafen [Beit Yosef in the name of Rashbetz].]:
