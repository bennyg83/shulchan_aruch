(siman 6) In their shells. And one can say regarding boser too — for it is also not finished fruit, and nevertheless it suffices to descend one level to bless borei peri haAdamah — so why does one bless shehakol here?

For here the main eating, etc. — meaning specifically boser: for although one eats it while it is boser, nevertheless one also eats when it is boser the main fruit — namely its interior — only its fruit is not yet finished; therefore one descends a level and blesses borei peri haAdamah. But here with almonds, when one eats them while soft, the main eating then is the shell — if so one does not eat the main fruit that people plant with intent — namely what is inside.
