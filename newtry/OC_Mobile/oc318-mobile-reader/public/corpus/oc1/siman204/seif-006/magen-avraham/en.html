And one should not bless borei peri haGefen. Thus wrote Rabbeinu Yosef in the name of Rashbatz — it would seem to follow the majority, and one should reflect on this because dates — all their strength is in the beverage — therefore he does not rely to bless borei peri haGefen — end of his words. And so Rabbeinu Yosef in Yoreh De'ah siman 123 in his name regarding yayin nesech; therefore from doubt one blesses shehakol. But for the blessing after [the meal] there is to be uncertain — and drink wine and bless on the vine and eat fruit from the seven species and bless on the tree and on fruit of the tree — for date honey does not exempt them with wine, for they are fruits per Rosh as wrote end of siman 202 seif 11; therefore one must bless on the tree. And it is possible this tamad (date beverage) has the law of date beer and blesses shehakol even per Rosh, since it has much water and is exempted with the blessing on wine — as written siman 202 seif 2 and above seif 1.
