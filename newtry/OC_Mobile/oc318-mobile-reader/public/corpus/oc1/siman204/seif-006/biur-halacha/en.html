But on the foot, etc. — see in Teshuvot Tashbetz vol. 1 siman 86 who sides to say that this halachah is specifically regarding grape dregs; but regarding raisin dregs it is possible that this halachah does not apply at all, for even regarding raisins themselves the poskim wrote that we require that they specifically have some moisture such that wine can emerge from them through pressing without soaking [and as appears below in siman 272 seif 6]. And all the more so that the refuse of raisins is not superior to whole raisins — they are inferior — see there, who sides that their law is like the law of beer dregs — we require specifically throw three and four come, as above.

And one does not bless borei peri haGefen — and all the more one does not make kiddush on it. However, regarding havdalah — if it is country wine one makes havdalah on it, for it is beer; and even if it was made without figs one makes havdalah on it — as long as it has taste and smell, for it is not mere vinegar; and even if it is from raisin dregs [about which I was uncertain above regarding borei peri haGefen, as I wrote in his name]. And this matter depends on estimating the strength of its taste and its good smell [Tashbetz there].
