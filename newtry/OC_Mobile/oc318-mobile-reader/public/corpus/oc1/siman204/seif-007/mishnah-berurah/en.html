(40) For his thirst — it appears not literally for his actual thirst, but in general: whoever the palate enjoys from water — presumably he is somewhat thirsty and requires a berachah, for if he were not thirsty at all the palate would not enjoy from it.

(41) A piece of food choked him — meaning food stood lodged in his throat.

(42) And he drank water — specifically water, for which the palate does not enjoy except when drinking for thirst; but when he drinks other beverages or eats a piece of bread that the palate enjoys from them — even though he is not drinking or eating them now for thirst but only to remove the lodged food — he is obligated to bless on them initially and finally, as below in seif 208. When drinking water in the morning for medicinal purposes he does not bless; but if also for thirst he blesses [siman 211 Yaavetz].
