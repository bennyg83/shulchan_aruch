(7) There — and on broth. It is broth of meat; for regarding broth of fruits there is a dispute, as written above siman 202 seif 10. Levushei Serad, chapter 20, note 52; Magen Avraham, note 3. And one who drinks broth with lemon juice for healing — shehakol. And if one mixed into the broth something that one eats in the manner of enjoyment for healing — even though it is a small amount and the majority is broth — that thing is primary and one blesses on it the berachah appropriate to it and exempts the broth, which is secondary. Birkei Yosef in Sha'ar 2, note 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, note 17.
