(siman 7) Spoiled grain, etc. — one descends one level: for if its fruit were finished he would bless borei peri haAdamah; when not finished one blesses shehakol. And it speaks of siman 202 seif 2 regarding boser — if it were finished one would bless borei peri haEtz; now that it is not finished we descend one level to bless borei peri haAdamah.
