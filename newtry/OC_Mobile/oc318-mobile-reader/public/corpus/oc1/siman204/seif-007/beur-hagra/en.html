Seif 7: "one who drinks," etc. — Rashi 45a s.v. mai, etc., and Tosafot s.v. R'T, etc.

And after it borei nefashot, etc. — Tosafot 44b s.v. avidna, etc.

And he drank water. — Specifically water, as written in seif 8 — Rosh.

Not, etc. — Tosafot 45 s.v. dechanketei, etc., and R' Meir, etc.; and likewise Rosh forced that he has no benefit; and furthermore, according to R' Oshaia that the reason is because he was coerced — what does it teach us.
