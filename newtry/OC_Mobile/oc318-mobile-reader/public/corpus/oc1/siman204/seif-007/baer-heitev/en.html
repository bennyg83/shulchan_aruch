Creator of many souls (Borei nefashot rabbot). See in Knesset HaGedolah who wrote in the name of the Rav Maharach Abulafia that one should not recite a final blessing on drinking coffee, for on account of its heat people are accustomed to drink it slowly little by little, and they wait from the beginning of the drinking until its end more than a revi'it. And so agreed R' Yitzchak Rozanes in Mishna LeMelekh chapter 3 of Hilchot Berakhot, and Maharam ibn Chaviv z"l in his novellae on Yoma 12b, and Mahara Halevi in responsa Ginat Veradim Orach Chayyim part 1 klal 1 siman 16. And Peri Chadash in Mayim Chaim siman 1. But Mahariya Ashkenazi and the author of Knesset HaGedolah and Mahara Yitzchaki z"l in his responsum Zera Avraham Orach Chayyim siman 2 and siman 5, and Maharam Galanti agreed to bless. See in HaLeket vol. 1 siman 9 and in Sefer Bnei Chayya; and now in our times most, as all of them, do not bless at the end; and I saw men of action who let it cool and then drink it and bless. Yad Ephraim.
