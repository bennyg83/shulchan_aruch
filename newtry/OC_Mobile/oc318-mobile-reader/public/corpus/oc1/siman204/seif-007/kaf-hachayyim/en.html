7) There: And over broth. This refers to meat broth, for there is a dispute concerning fruit broth, as stated above in siman 202, seif 10. Lechem Chamudos, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin, paragraph 52; Magen Avraham, paragraph 3. One who drinks broth with lemon water for medicinal purposes recites Shehakol. If he mixed into the broth an item that he ordinarily enjoys eating, for medicinal purposes, then even if that item is the minority and most of the mixture is broth, that item is primary. He recites its appropriate blessing, which covers the subordinate broth. Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 17.
