(6) And such is the essence, etc.: and Levush, who omitted it, appears because he cited the remainder of Knesset HaGedolah and Rabbeinu Yeruham and Rosh. One can reject: specifically regarding milah too it is reasonable that it is a matter of holiness that they ruled thus—know that Shulchan Aruch itself ruled in Yoreh De'ah 265 that one need not cover a minor's milah; and Knesset HaGedolah wrote in the name of Radbaz that all agree it is forbidden; and further Bach in Yoreh De'ah was strict even regarding milah where possible. Beit Yosef wrote that after the fact, according to all, he has fulfilled; but for an adult, even after the fact they return him. Magen Avraham wrote: the mohel should not hold ervah at the time of the blessing—until here—and we do not conduct thus.
