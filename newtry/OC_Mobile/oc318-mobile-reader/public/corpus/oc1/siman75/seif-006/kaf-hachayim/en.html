(6) There—it is forbidden to recite Keriat Shema opposite her. Tur wrote: and likewise if her thigh is uncovered it is forbidden to recite opposite her; and Bach wrote: for what purpose did he take thigh more than other covered places on her, to say that even less than a tefach is also considered ervah, as we derive from the verse "uncover the thigh, cross the rivers," and it is written "your nakedness shall be uncovered," which implies all thigh is ervah even less than a tefach—see there. And likewise Taz seif kaf-alef. Eitan HaEzrachi letter alef. But Maran in Beit Yosef wrote on Tur's words cited: that which he wrote "if her thigh is uncovered it is forbidden" means that even though it is not a modest place on a man, on a woman it is ervah—and this is the reason Rashba wrote in the name of Raavad—as explained in Beit Yosef. And if so, according to Maran's words, that which Taz took "and likewise if her thigh is uncovered it is forbidden" is not because he needed to say even less than a tefach is forbidden, but because I would say there is no ervah in it at all since it is not a modest place on a man—therefore he came to say there is ervah in it; and always its law is like other covered places, that it is forbidden to recite opposite it only if a tefach is uncovered—and so is implied from his words here in Shulchan Aruch, that he did not write to distinguish thigh from other covered places, which implies they are equal and it is forbidden only at a tefach uncovered. And so from Eliyah Rabbah letter bet, who after bringing Rashba's words and Beit Yosef wrote: "But Taz wrote that her thigh, even regarding his wife, is forbidden even less than a tefach, since it is the place of the forbidden more than other limbs"—so far. And likewise Razhu letter alef and Chayei Adam klal 4 letter zayin—see there. And likewise Mishnat Hasavim letter alef—see there. Nevertheless, initially one should be concerned for Bach's, Taz's, and Eitan HaEzrachi's words, since the later authorities brought this view and were concerned for it—Eliyah Rabbah there, Chayei Adam there, Razhu there, Shomer Tziyon letter alef.
