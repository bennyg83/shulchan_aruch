From her, etc. Bach rules that only turning one's face away is effective, but closing one's eyes, darkness, or blindness is not effective; see there. This is also the view of Magen Avraham and Taz. (See Eliyah Rabbah for an extensive discussion of this.) Taz further concludes that the permitted turning away applies specifically when one turns one's entire body and stands facing another direction; see there. The author of Yad Aharon concludes in accordance with Shulchan Aruch; see there. It is permitted to think words of Torah even while looking at nakedness. Pri Chadash; and Magen Avraham, siman 85, seif katan 2, writes likewise.
