From her, etc. Bach ruled that only turning one's face away helps, but closing one's eyes, night, or blindness does not help; see there. This is also the view of Magen Avraham and Taz (and see Eliyah Rabbah, where there is a lengthy discussion of this). Taz further concludes that the turning of the face which they permitted is specifically where he turned his entire body and stands facing another side; see there. The author of Yad Aharon concludes in accordance with the words of the Shulchan Aruch; see there. It is permitted to think words of Torah even though one is looking at ervah. Pri Chadash; and so wrote Magen Avraham, siman 85 seif katan 2.
