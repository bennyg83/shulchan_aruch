From her, etc.: Bach ruled that only turning the face away helps, but closing the eyes, or night, or a blind person does not help—see above; and such is the view of Magen Avraham and Taz. (And see in Eliyahu Rabba an extended analysis of this.) Taz raised further that this turning of the face that they permitted is specifically when he turned his whole body and stands to the side—see above. The author of Yad Aharon raised it like the words of Shulchan Aruch—see above. It is permitted to reflect on words of Torah even though he is looking at ervah.
