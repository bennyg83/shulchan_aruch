(27) Nakedness opposite him — see Peri Megadim and other later authorities: when he himself is naked, perhaps even per the Shulchan Aruch none of these strategies help.

And then his face came back, even though he had returned his face alone without his body, he was swayed by their eyes, and looked at her:

(29) Or if he squinted his eyes, etc. — The later authorities agreed that all these strategies except turning the face do not help, for it is not written "and you shall not see" but "and it shall not be seen" — meaning the viewer shall not see. And even turning the face that was permitted is only specifically if he turned his entire body and stands on another side so that the nakedness is from his side; but if he turned his face alone it does not help. If so, when he himself is naked, no strategy can be imagined that would permit speaking words of Torah. Mishbetzot Zahav wrote: if he is in one domain and a naked person is in another domain opposite him, and he squints his eyes from seeing him — one could say this is permitted according to all; and so it seems somewhat in Darkei Chayyim and in siman 79 seif 8 in Eshel Avraham it seems he retracted from this; and so in Chayei Adam klal 4 seif 9 it does not seem so. However, if a glass window separates between him and the nakedness and he squints his eyes from seeing — this helps according to all, since there is in any case some barrier covering opposite the nakedness. It is permitted to meditate on words of Torah when naked, and one need not say opposite another nakedness, for it says "nakedness of a matter" — speech is forbidden, meditation is permitted. Nevertheless he should not listen then to a blessing from his fellow to fulfill his obligation, for it is impossible to say "one who hears is like one who answers" since he cannot answer [later authorities].

(30) For sight is what matters — that is, even though he is close to him within his four cubits — regarding feces we hold in siman 79 that it is forbidden even if behind him; here Scripture revealed that it depends only on sight. There is a practical difference in all this: even according to us who are stringent about squinting the eyes, as above, nevertheless if he turned his face and body away from the nakedness it helps according to all, even if close to him.
