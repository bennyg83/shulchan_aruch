And he turned his face away. The Bach rules that only turning one's face away is effective, because his camp is then holy, analogous to excrement behind him. Closing one's eyes, darkness, or blindness is not effective. This is stated explicitly by the Mordechai, the Rokeach, and the Yerushalmi. Beit Yosef's statement that the contrary appears from the Rashba, as discussed above at the end of siman 74:2, is no proof. There, at least, his private area was covered by water, which is considered a separation with respect to his heart, and the same applies with respect to his eyes. Even so, he may not look at it, just as one may not look at ervah through glass. When the ervah itself is exposed, however, closing one's eyes is ineffective, as stated in siman 74:4.
