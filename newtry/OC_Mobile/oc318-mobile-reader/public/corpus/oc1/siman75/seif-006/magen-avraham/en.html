And he turned his face. Bach ruled that specifically turning the face helps, for his camp becomes holy, similar to feces behind him; but squinting the eyes, or night, or a blind person does not help; and so explicitly in Mordechai, Rokeach, and Yerushalmi. And what Rabbeinu Yosef wrote that so in Rashba—as stated above siman 74 seif 62—is not proof, for there in any case his nakedness is covered in water, for this is called a barrier relative to the heart; the same applies relative to his eyes, and even so it is forbidden to look at her, as with ervah through asheirit; but when the ervah is uncovered, squinting his eyes does not help, as written in siman 74 seif 4.
