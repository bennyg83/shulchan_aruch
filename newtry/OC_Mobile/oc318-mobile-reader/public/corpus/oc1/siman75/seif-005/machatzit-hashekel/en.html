Seif katan 5: “And certainly, etc., a woman may go out, etc.” An ornament of value, or one fastened tightly so that mikveh water cannot enter beneath it, may not be worn outside on Shabbos. When it is valuable, we are concerned that she may remove it to show her companion and carry it four amos in the public domain. When it is tight, we are concerned that she may need to immerse, remove it, and carry it four amos. Strands of hair, however, are neither tight nor valuable, and she may therefore go out wearing them. This is not a conclusive proof for the Magen Avraham, for perhaps the Mishnah concerns strands worn beneath the hairnet and therefore covered. His principal proof is from the latter clause, that a young woman may not go out wearing the hair of an elderly woman, lest people laugh at her and she remove it out of embarrassment, thereby carrying it four amos. That case must concern visible hair, for otherwise there would be no concern that people would laugh at her.
