(Seif kaf-heh) And all the more so a woman with hair emerging, etc.—it is an important matter, for they are tied fast and ritual immersion water does not enter them; she is forbidden to go out with them on Shabbos. For since they are considered significant, we are concerned lest she pull them and show her friend, and carry for her four amot in the public domain; and likewise if they are tied fast, lest she need immersion and need to remove them and carry for her four amot. But hair threads that are not tied fast and also are not significant—she is permitted to go out with them. But from this there is not such strong proof for Magen Avraham, who says perhaps it deals with hair under the basket and they are covered; and his main proof is from the mishnah that a woman after childbirth may not go out with grey ornamental hair, lest they smile at her and she remove them out of embarrassment and carry for her four amot. And therefore it deals with uncovered hair, for we are concerned she will remove them.
