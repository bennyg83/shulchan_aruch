(25) Through asheirit—it is of glass or thin cloth that illuminates until the ervah is visible, or a handbreadth of a woman's flesh in a place where it is her custom to be covered, which is also called ervah, as above in seif 1. And squinting the eyes is permitted in all this according to all, since the ervah is in any case covered [Chayei Adam; and so Peri Megadim regarding asheirit].

(26) Forbidden to recite — even if she is in another house and he sees her through the lens in the window, because the matter depends on sight; and even after the fact he must go back and recite.
