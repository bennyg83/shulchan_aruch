(25) Behind glass. This means glass or a thin translucent garment through which the ervah can be seen, or through which one handbreadth of a woman's flesh is visible in a place she ordinarily covers, which is likewise called ervah, as explained above in seif 1. Closing one's eyes is permitted in all these cases according to all opinions, because the ervah is at least covered. This is stated by Chayei Adam, and Peri Megadim writes the same concerning glass.
(26) It is forbidden to recite. This applies even when the ervah is in another house and he sees it through the glass of the window, because Scripture makes the prohibition dependent on seeing. Even after the fact, he must repeat the recitation.
