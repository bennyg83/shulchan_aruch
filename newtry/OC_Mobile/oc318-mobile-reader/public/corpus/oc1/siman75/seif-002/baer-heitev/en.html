A woman: and hair of a man's ervah that protrudes through his garment is permitted. Gemara, page 24. Magen Avraham.

The head: see Even HaEzer, siman 21, note 5, from Sh"sh. And Magen Avraham.

From their head covering: in the Zohar he was very strict that one should not see any of a woman's hair, and it is proper to conduct oneself accordingly. See Yoma 47a regarding Kimchit.

And all the more so foreign peah, as it should read. The same applies if she cut her own hair and later attached it to her head—see Ateret Zekenim.
