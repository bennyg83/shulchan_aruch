In the name of A. D. even against another woman is permitted, and this means that all the occupiers and no force are exposed to it :
Outside the braid — women from lands where custom is not to expose outside braid, coming where custom is to expose — may expose if not intending to return (Magen Avraham in R' Eliyahu Alshich). To read Keriat Shema opposite them — Shulchan Shelomo: permitted even if intend to return (no arousal). If from exposing land to non-exposing — may not expose even if intend to return (men's arousal) — forbidden to read opposite even if intend to return. Man from exposing to non-exposing — forbidden all cases; from non-exposing to exposing — permitted if not intending to return. See Mishnah Berurah note 14. Hair exposure — Magen Avraham in Tosafot Ketubot: only in market forbidden; in courtyard without men permitted (Avnei Nezer 215 not proven). Zohar Nasso stringent on any hair — practice accordingly. This is not the 'hair is nakedness' of this seif — even at home forbidden per all if custom covers in market due to arousal. Smag, Tur, Bach, Gemara — literal exposure forbidden. Baal HaBayit 215: Jewish custom forbids literal exposure even in courtyard. Chasam Sofer 36: accepted prohibition. Magen Avraham 451:7 expanded. In our lands per Zohar — absolute prohibition. Women immersing must ensure no hair outside — else remains niddah with karet. Friday night immersion — wrap head in thin perforated cloth (tul). See Yoreh De'ah 198:40:
