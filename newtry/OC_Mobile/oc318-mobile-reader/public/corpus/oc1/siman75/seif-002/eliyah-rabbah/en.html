[4] Outside their binding, etc. Maharam Alshakar, responsum 35, writes: “Women have customarily exposed hair outside its binding for adornment, and this is permitted.” The Zohar in parashas Naso is exceedingly stringent that no hair of a woman be visible, and it is proper to conduct oneself accordingly. Magen Avraham. If hair from a man's ervah protrudes through his garment, it is permitted. Gemara, Berachos 24.
