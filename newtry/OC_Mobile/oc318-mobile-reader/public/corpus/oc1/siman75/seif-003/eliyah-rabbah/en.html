[5] One should be careful, etc. Shulchan Arukh as Rosh: woman's voice is nakedness — to hear not Keriat Shema; Darkei Moshe: even without caution concern prohibition since many forbid. Nachalat Tzvi wondered Rama did not write this in Shulchan Arukh gloss. Rama hints: 'but regular voice is not nakedness' — forbidden voice implies absolute prohibition. Be'er Sheva 119 wondered on those who explain voice for Keriat Shema when singing — R' Nachman to R' Yehudah 'send me greetings at night' — Rashi: asking welfare — explicitly hearing not Keriat Shema even not singing. I resolved: 'voice is nakedness' with 'handbreadth' — for wife and Keriat Shema; to gaze at another woman even pinky forbidden — likewise voice hearing even greeting forbidden. Shulchan Arukh hair too — outside braid permitted for Keriat Shema for another woman; to gaze outside braid forbidden. Proof in Rashba chiddushim — see there.
