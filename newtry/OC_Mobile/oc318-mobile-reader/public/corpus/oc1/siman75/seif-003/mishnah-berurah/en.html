(16) One must be careful, etc. — and bedieved if he recited, he goes back and recites without the blessings; see in Biur HaGra and Peri Megadim.

(17) A woman's song — even of an unmarried woman; but not at the time of Shema it is permitted, only he should not intend to enjoy from it so he does not come to lewd thought; and a married woman's song and likewise all arayos — forever forbidden to hear; and likewise an unmarried woman who is a niddah is from the category of arayos; and our virgins all have the presumption of niddah from when they reach time of vest. And the voice of song of an unmarried non-Jewish woman is also included in ervah and forbidden to hear, whether Kohen or Yisrael. Nevertheless if he is on the road among non-Jews or in a city and he is compelled [anus] that he cannot protest — since we do not find that it is called ervah min hatorah — it is permitted to recite and bless, for otherwise since we dwell among non-Jews Torah and prayer would be nullified; and regarding this it is said "a time to act for Hashem, they have violated Your Torah"; but he should strengthen his heart to intend toward the sanctity that he is engaged in and not give his heart to the voice of song.

(18) He who is accustomed to it — meaning, since he is accustomed to it he will not come to lewd thought, even from a married woman; nevertheless it is forbidden to intend to enjoy from her speech, for even in her garments it is forbidden to look to enjoy.
