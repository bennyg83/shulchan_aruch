Seif 3, there is, etc.: there, voice, etc., and Targum Yonatan, Hagahot Maimoniyot, Mordechai, and Rashba Poskim hold that it also deals with Keriat Shema—but only with a singing voice. However the Rosh and Tur wrote that it does not deal with Keriat Shema, and Rambam likewise. Therefore one should be careful to take into account the above poskim.
