Seif 3, some, etc. There it states, “a voice,” etc. Rabbeinu Yonah, Hagahos Maimoniyos, Mordechai, and the other poskim explain that this also concerns Krias Shema, but specifically a singing voice. The Rosh and Tur, however, write that it does not concern Krias Shema, and Rambam likewise does not understand it that way. This is why the Mechaber writes, “One should be careful,” in deference to the former poskim.
