To be careful from uncovered hair and a woman's voice at the time of Kerias Shema, and likewise not to recite it opposite ervah. And in it are 6 seifim: A
handbreadth uncovered on a woman in a place where it is her custom to cover it—even if she is his wife—it is forbidden to recite Kerias Shema opposite her.And some say this is specifically regarding his wife; but regarding another woman,' even less than a handbreadth is ervah (Hagahot Maimoni chapter 3). And it appears from the words of the Rosh that a handbreadth on a woman is ervah even regarding another woman, only that she herself may recite even if she is naked, as above in siman 74.
