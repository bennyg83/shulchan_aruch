If you are dressed in the same way as it is written:
In the midst of Hashem’s Word, Hashem’s word is not revealed, and Hashem’s Word is revealed to you
(Note 1) And some say — see Lechem Chamudot — uncertain. Meaning Berakhot 24a R' Yitzchak handbreadth on woman is nakedness; Talmud concludes — for gazing; R' Sheshet: even pinky forbidden — challenge wife vs other and Keriat Shema. Rosh: specifically other; she herself naked may read. Rama: since forbidden for wife without arousal — 'do not see nakedness' — likewise other woman may not read/bless opposite naked companion. Shulchan Arukh 'handbreadth even wife' — also other woman needs handbreadth. Rama 'some say specifically wife' — Tur/Rambam wording. Rabbeinu Yosef: other woman also needs handbreadth; gazing at pinky is intentional enjoyment — incidental seeing permitted except Keriat Shema. Challenge 'for gazing' — R' Sheshet forbids intentional gaze at pinky; answer: wife and Keriat Shema unintentional seeing; only handbreadth; Keriat Shema stricter.
