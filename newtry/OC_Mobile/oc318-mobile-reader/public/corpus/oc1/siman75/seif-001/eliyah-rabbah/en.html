[1] Handbreadth exposed, etc. — implies for another woman prohibition only at handbreadth; though Rama wrote some say less than handbreadth is nakedness for another woman — Bach wrote Rama also holds we do not establish this. Rama concludes from Rosh: handbreadth on woman is nakedness even for another woman — only she herself may read naked. From 'handbreadth is nakedness' — less than handbreadth not nakedness. Not precise — Rosh specified handbreadth; Rama innovates woman-to-woman nakedness. I think Rama disputes 'some say' on another woman less than handbreadth; 'from Rosh' means handbreadth applies woman-to-woman too. 'Some say' is Ba'al Halakhot Gedolot — no proof: he wrote even wife if exposes handbreadth forbidden — obviously another woman at handbreadth; less than handbreadth no prohibition even for another.
