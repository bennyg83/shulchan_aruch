In Magen Avraham seif kaf-heh in rejected matters. NB: one who examines inside Be'er Sheva will see that the end of his words is correct, and they are in the name of the Gaon R' Ezekiel Katzenellenbogen, who added proof from Nedarim 48b regarding black of the head. And Mahar"i Yaabetz vol. 1 siman 19 and vol. 2 simanim 7–8 raised to forbid. And that which is in Nazir 28b deals with a courtyard and her house and not in the public domain; and that which is "a woman goes out with strands of hair" means not in a place of a gate, such as at her doorway and her back, which are like other ornaments—but a foreign peah is not better than her wig, which is forbidden as stated in the chapter HaMedir—see there.
