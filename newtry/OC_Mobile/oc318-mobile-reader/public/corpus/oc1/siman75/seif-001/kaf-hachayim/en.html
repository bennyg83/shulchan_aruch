(1) [Seif 1] A tefach uncovered on a woman, etc.: if her garment is thin and her flesh appears through it, it is forbidden—as written in seif 5 of Ervah Ba'asirit, etc.; and the same applies here. Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman in the name of Ba'al Halakhot Gedolot and Machatzit HaShekel. Eliyah Rabbah letter alef. Mishna Berurah letter alef, end of seif kaf-bet, letter alef from Mechaber, letter alef, Razhu letter gimmel, Siddur Beit Yaakov letter yud-dalet. Chayei Adam klal 4 letter gimmel—and see Peri Megadim Ateret Tzvi at the beginning of the siman and Mishnat Hasavim siman 74 end of letter alef, who wrote that a thin garment similar to clear water is considered covering for ervah, but for sight it is forbidden because of arousal—see there; and if so it follows that if he squeezes his eyes shut it is permitted to recite even according to those who disagree with Maran regarding squeezing the eyes shut, as will be explained in seif 6—and see our words above siman 74 letters tet and tet-vav.
