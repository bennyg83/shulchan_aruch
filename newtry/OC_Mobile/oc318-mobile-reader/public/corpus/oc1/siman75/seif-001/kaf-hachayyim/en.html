1) [Se'if 1] A handbreadth of a woman is exposed, etc. If she wears a thin garment through which her flesh is visible, it is forbidden, as stated in se'if 5 concerning nakedness behind glass; the same applies here. Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman, citing Baal Halachos Gedolos; Machatzis HaShekel; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Halachah Berurah, paragraph 1; Soles Belulah, paragraph 1; Machazik Berachah, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3; Siddur Beis Oved, paragraph 14; Chayei Adam, rule 4, paragraph 3. See Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, at the beginning of the siman, and Mishbetzos Zahav at the end of siman 74, paragraph 1, who writes that a thin garment resembles clear water: it is considered a covering of nakedness, but looking is forbidden because it prompts improper thoughts. A practical difference follows: if he closes his eyes, he may recite Krias Shema even according to those who disagree with Maran regarding closed eyes, as will be explained in se'if 6.
