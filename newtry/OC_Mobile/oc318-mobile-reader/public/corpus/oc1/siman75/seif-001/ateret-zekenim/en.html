A handbreadth uncovered on a woman, etc.—it is forbidden to recite Keriat Shema opposite her. Even the thigh, which is customarily uncovered on a man, nevertheless on a woman it is ervah and forbidden to recite Keriat Shema opposite her (language of the Gemara, Berachot 24). Even less than a handbreadth—the thigh is also ervah (my teacher in Bach).

And some say only regarding his wife, etc. That which is forbidden to gaze at another woman—even a small finger—is when one intends to look at her; but regarding Keriat Shema there is a prohibition even if he does not intend at all. Rather, one who sees her casually without intention—then he is not forbidden regarding Keriat Shema except for a handbreadth uncovered (my teacher in Bach). And averting the eyes does not help (Mordechai, chapter Mi Shemeto).
