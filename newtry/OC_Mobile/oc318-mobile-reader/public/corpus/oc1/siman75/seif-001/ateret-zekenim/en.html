A handbreadth of a woman that is exposed, etc., it is prohibited to recite Krias Shema opposite it. Even her lower leg, which is ordinarily exposed on a man, is nevertheless considered ervah on a woman, and it is prohibited to recite Krias Shema opposite it. This is the language of the Gemara in Berachos 24a. Even less than a handbreadth of the lower leg is considered ervah. My teacher, the Bach.
And some say that this applies specifically to his wife, etc. That which it is prohibited to gaze at another woman, even at her little finger, applies when he intends to look at her. Regarding Krias Shema, however, there is a prohibition even when he has no intention at all and merely sees her incidentally, without intent. In that case, Krias Shema is prohibited only when a handbreadth is exposed. My teacher, the Bach. Closing one's eyes does not help. Mordechai, chapter Mi Shemeiso.
