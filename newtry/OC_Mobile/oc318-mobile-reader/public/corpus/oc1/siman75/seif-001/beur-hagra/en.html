Seif 1, in a place, etc. For it states there, “the lower leg,” etc. At first glance, why is this necessary? Rather, Rabbi Yitzchak was speaking of such a case, and he teaches us that although the lower leg is not a place that a man ordinarily covers, a woman does ordinarily cover it. It is therefore ervah even with respect to her husband. Rashba in the name of Raavad.
And some say, etc. The Rosh's text there reads: This is not difficult, one case concerns another woman and the other concerns his wife, and it refers to Krias Shema. This is certainly so according to what is written in seif 6, that even when he is not looking at the ervah it is prohibited to recite Krias Shema. It is difficult, then, why the case of his wife is necessary.
And it appears, etc. This requires study in light of what is written above. See also siman 74, seif 1, in the gloss. The language of the Rosh is also difficult, for he says, “specifically with another.” Who mentioned another woman? Rather, this is a scribal error, and it should read, “specifically for others,” meaning both her husband and other men.
