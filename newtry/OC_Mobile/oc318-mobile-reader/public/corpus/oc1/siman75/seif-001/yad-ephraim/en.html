(In Magen Avraham note 3) Regarding the thigh, etc. — At first glance there is no difficulty, for here he wants to recite Kerias Shema, which applies in a house or courtyard there is no prohibition as written; and one must say he holds that if so, even a married woman too — nevertheless, since they are accustomed to cover them in the market, again even in the courtyard it is called nakedness for Kerias Shema. He again raised the difficulty: what goes out in the hinuma, for there she goes so from her father's house to her husband's house; and if there were prohibition even for a virgin — if so, even from courtyard to courtyard through an alley would be forbidden, as stated in HaMedir; and on this he answered that there it deals with one who removes her hairnet — then it is forbidden even for a virgin, for this is not modest conduct, since the head is very exposed, as stated in Sotah; and "she let her hair down" teaches that she removes her hair — and Rashi explained: from much exposure she removes her hairnet; therefore even for a virgin in such a case it is forbidden. And there she goes out in the hinuma with head uncovered; nevertheless she does not remove her hairnet, but the length of her hair rests on her shoulder, as Rashi wrote there. And he again wrote that therefore the prohibition for a virgin regarding hairnet is not biblical from the verse, for the verse does not deal with an unmarried woman — if not, exposure too would be forbidden from the verse "and let her hair down," from which we derive she should not go exposed; since it says "and let her hair down," implying until now it was covered — meaning even without removing the hairnet it was covered, for the plain meaning of the verse regarding exposure deals with cover; only from the exposition we derive she removes her hair. If so, for a virgin too there would be prohibition in exposure alone; but certainly the prohibition in removing the hairnet is not due to the verse of exposure, for it does not deal with an unmarried woman, but due to modest conduct, for a virgin should not go with hairnet removed since it is very exposed.
