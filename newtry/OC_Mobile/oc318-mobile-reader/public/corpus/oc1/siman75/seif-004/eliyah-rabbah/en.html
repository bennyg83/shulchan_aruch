(4) Outside their braids, etc.: language of R' Moshe Alsheikh siman 35: women were accustomed to uncover their hair outside their braids to cool themselves—it is permitted—until here. In Zohar Parshat Naso he was very strict that no hair of a woman should be seen, and so one should conduct (Magen Avraham). Hair of a man's ervah that protrudes through the garment is permitted—gemara Berachos page 24.
