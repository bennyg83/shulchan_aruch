It is forbidden to recite [the Recitation of the Sh'ma] in front of "nakedness", even that of a non-Jew and so too in front of the "nakedness" of a minor it is forbidden. {Rama: And there are those that permit [it] in front of the "nakedness" of a minor so long as [the child] is not considered capable of sexual intercourse (Rabeinu Yerucham - the Rosh on Chapter "Mi Shemeito" and Mahara of Prague). And that is the main opinion. (see Yoreh Deah, Siman 265)}
