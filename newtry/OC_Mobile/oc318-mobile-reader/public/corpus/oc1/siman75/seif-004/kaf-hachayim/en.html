(4) There—even if she is his wife, etc.—therefore one must be careful when she nurses and uncovers her breast, for it is forbidden by Torah law, and all the more so regarding another woman. Chayei Adam klal 4 letter zayin. Amudei HaShulchan on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 5 letter yud-bet. And likewise in Sha'arei Teshuvah siman 110, who wrote that all that is said in Shir HaShirim, such as "your belly is a heap of wheat," "your legs pillars of marble," "your two breasts," etc.—are ervah for a woman to uncover—so far. And that we do not consider it at the time of nursing like uncovered places—even though she takes it out to nurse the child—one reason is because its way is to be covered when she is not nursing; and further, even at the time of nursing its way is not always to be uncovered so we should consider it at that time like uncovered places, but she takes it out only at the time of need to nurse and afterwards returns to cover it; therefore it is not considered like uncovered places even at the time of nursing. And see what we wrote above letter bet in the name of Bach. And Rabbeinu Ben Acharon, Parshat Bo letter yud.
