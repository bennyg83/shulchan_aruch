4) There: Even if she is his wife, etc. One must therefore be careful while a woman nurses and exposes her breast, for it is forbidden to speak words of Torah then, and certainly when she is another man's wife. Chayei Adam, rule 4, paragraph 7; Amudei HaShulchan on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 5, paragraph 12. This is also implied by Sefer Chasidim, siman 110, which writes that all the body parts mentioned in Shir HaShirim; such as "your belly is a heap of wheat," "his legs are pillars of marble," and "your two breasts"; are nakedness for a woman to expose. We do not treat the breast during nursing as a normally exposed area, even though she takes it out to nurse the child. First, it is normally covered when she is not nursing. Moreover, even during the nursing period it is not constantly exposed so as to be treated then as an ordinarily exposed area; she exposes it only when necessary to nurse and then covers it again. It is therefore not considered a normally exposed area even during the nursing period. See what we wrote above in paragraph 2, citing Bach, and Ben Ish Chai, Parashas Bo, paragraph 10.
