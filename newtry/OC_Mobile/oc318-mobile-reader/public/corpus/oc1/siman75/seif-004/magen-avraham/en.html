Nakedness of a minor. And in Megillah page 24, top 72b, Rashi wrote that the minor himself may recite even if he is naked, for he was not warned about this; but to me it requires study that it should be forbidden rabbinically, immediately as with all sins in the Torah; and see siman 343. And for poskim that nakedness of a minor is not called ervah—one must be more careful, and it requires study.

And there are those who permit, etc.—that he is not fit for intercourse, namely one who is not nine years old; and it seems to me that nevertheless the mohel should not grasp the ervah at the time of the blessing, as written in siman 74.
