The private area of a minor. In Megillah 24a, Rashi writes that the minor himself may read even while naked, because he was not warned concerning this. This requires study, for it should at least be Rabbinically prohibited, as with all prohibitions in the Torah. See siman 343. According to the authorities who maintain that the private area of a minor is not considered ervah, the matter is better understood, but it still requires study.
And some permit, etc. This is because he is not fit for intercourse until he is nine years old. It nevertheless appears to me that the mohel should not hold the organ while reciting the blessing. See what I wrote at the end of siman 74.
