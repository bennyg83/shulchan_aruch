And they answer — Taz, Berakhot 191 part 2 in the Mishnah: they answer after Israel and not after Samaritan until he hears the entire blessing; and in Yerushalmi regarding idolater they answer after him — meaning even if he did not hear the entire blessing, for regarding Samaritan too when he hears; and the Mechaber wrote Samaritan — they do not answer, meaning after they decreed; and it implies idolaters too — if he heard the entire blessing they do not answer; and see Rambam chapter 1 of Laws of Blessings halacha 13, see there; and Rabbeynu emended: idolaters — they answer if he heard the entire blessing, and preferable to Samaritan (for they made them like idolaters for degradation, not for elevation — Bach); nevertheless in Yerushalmi it implies even without hearing all they answer regarding idolaters — Bach; and therefore Taz explained that the plain meaning of the Mishnah implies idolaters are not equal to Israel, for otherwise he would have included them together; and this is: otherwise he would have said they answer amen after idolaters and not after Samaritan unless he heard the entire blessing — implying idolaters, no; and that he did not include them with Samaritan — no need, for a fortiori from Samaritan; even before they decreed the Samaritan was more suspect, for idolaters are not accustomed to this — Bach. And in our chiddushim I expanded; here there is no place to expand. Levush wrote in seif 3: an adult Israelite in answering amen from the mouth of a minor bar chinuch, and Israelite — this is for Tosafot in Megillah, and we in chapter 466 hold a minor blesses for his father even though it is only d'rabbanan; and Levush there wrote: when a minor ate — how much more we were not to be two d'rabbanan; and see Magen Avraham 466; and requires study; and see Taz 7 in the name of Lechem Rav also wrote: adult Israelite on a minor's blessing, and Israelite — why? requires study.
