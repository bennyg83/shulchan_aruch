(13) When he learns etc. — meaning: it is not fit to say amen to affirm and uphold the blesser's words after him, since there is no blessing upon it.

(14) In vain — meaning: when the rabbi learns the blessings with them not in their time — nevertheless it is permitted to mention the Name, and even the rabbi may mention the Name in order to teach the child the blessings, for in any case we must learn with them in order to educate them in Torah study and mitzvah performance (as written "and you shall teach them to your children" etc.); and specifically with the child it is permitted to mention the Name; but an adult when he learns blessings in the Gemara says without mention of the Name, and only when learning the verses mentioned in the Talmud may he say them as written with mention of the Name.

(15) To fulfill etc. — such as when he blesses the blessings in their time as a way of education.

(16) For they are bnei chinuch — it implies that on a blessing he blesses on eating and the like before reaching [the age of] chinuch — one does not answer amen on him (Peri Megadim in his chiddushim on Berakhot).

(17) When they recite the haftarah — meaning: then they read in Torah and Prophets and bless before and after them, and one must answer amen on them.
