Amen — Bach and Levush wrote that the adult who answers amen is greater than the blesser, and they reached this from that Rav said to Chiya his son: seize and bless; and see in Ohr Zaru'a and Shakh Choshen Mishpat siman 289 and Peri Megadim letter 1 siman 57 and in responsa at the end of Sefer Sha'ar Yosef siman 7 that brought Mahari ibn Tibbon like Shakh; and see in Berkei Yosef from this.
