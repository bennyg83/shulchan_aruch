Obligated — Taz wrote this to exclude Bach who explained that Tur too holds obligation regarding Israel, only regarding idolaters there is no obligation even if he heard the entire blessing — he may answer if he wishes and there is no obligation, see there; but Beit Yosef explained as Taz wrote here, and it appears after idolaters there is no obligation, only permission — and I will explain further in letter 3. Requires study — parashat Ha'azinu and in the word gema — chiddush of Ramban, amen on Torah blessing and birkat hamazon and similar three d'oraisa matters, see there and Magen Avraham here.
