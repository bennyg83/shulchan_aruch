After Yehalelukha. Because it is forbidden to interrupt in the middle of Hallel and psalms of praise — it is as if the first and last blessing are adjacent to each other; not so in the blessing on fruits; and in the Torah blessing too the enactment of the ancients was that the first blesses before it and the last after it, and they were not adjacent.

In Birkat HaMazon. To interrupt between d'oraisa blessings and d'rabbanan blessings.
