After Yehalelucha. This is because one may not interrupt in the middle of Hallel. Pesukei DeZimrah is therefore regarded as though the opening and closing blessings are adjacent to one another. This is unlike blessings over fruit. Regarding the blessings over Torah reading, the ancient enactment was that the first person recited the opening blessing and the last person recited the closing blessing, so they were not considered adjacent.
