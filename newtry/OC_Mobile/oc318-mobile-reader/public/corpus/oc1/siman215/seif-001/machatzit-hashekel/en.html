Seif katan 1: After Yehalelucha, because it is forbidden, etc. Meaning, although we require specifically that it follow two adjoining blessings, Yehalelucha is considered adjacent to the blessing before Hallel, and Yishtabach is considered adjacent to Baruch She'amar. Although Hallel and Pesukei DeZimrah intervene between the two blessings, they are nevertheless called adjacent because it is forbidden to interrupt between them with speech. By contrast, regarding blessings over fruit, although there are likewise two blessings, one before and one afterward, since one is permitted to interrupt with speech between the first and final blessings, they are not considered adjacent.
And Bircas HaTorah, etc. Although there is a blessing before and afterward and they are adjacent, and ideally one should not interrupt between the first and final blessings, nevertheless, if he interrupted and spoke, it is not considered an interruption, as stated above in siman 140, seif 2. This leniency applies only after the fact. See the Tur there.
