Answering Amen after blessings. It contains 4 seifim: One does not answer Amen after his own blessings except after 2 or more blessings that conclude a series of blessings. The custom is to answer Amen after Yehalelucha and after Yishtabach. {Rama: Some say that one answers Amen only after the blessing Boneh Yerushalayim in Birkat HaMazon, and this is the widespread custom in these countries and should not be changed. [Tosafos and Mordechai, beginning of chapter Sheloshah SheAchlu; Maharik, shoresh 72]} In places where the custom is to answer Amen after Yehalelucha and Yishtabach, one should also answer Amen after the blessing Shomer Amo Yisrael La'ad.
