When he learns — Bach; and in She'elat Ya'avetz siman 81 he wrote that if there is mention in the middle of the coin of a blessing written in the Talmud he may say it, see there; and in Berkei Yosef he wrote that elderly rabbis in Israel practiced not to mention the Name in their reading, and so in Mishna Berurah as Teshuvot Katzan writes in the name of Maharam — and I do not read the four-letter Name of Heaven in the Talmud but "the Name," therefore; and see further in She'elat Ya'avetz there that he wrote: when reading in the Talmud and other words of Chazal and in general their written words coming — he reads in the manner of Scripture the mentions in their reading; and he wrote that so practiced his father, may his rest be in Eden.
