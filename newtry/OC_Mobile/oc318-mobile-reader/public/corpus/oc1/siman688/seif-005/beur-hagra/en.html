Section 5: At the time of reading. Of the day. See Baal Halachos Gedolos and Rashi's explanation there. From here there is proof for Rabbeinu Tam and Baal HaIttur in siman 692, section 1, and the opinion that says "even, etc.," unlike the Rosh, who wrote that everything depends on the night of the fourteenth. They were forced by the fact that the Gemara explains the law of a resident of a walled city, the latter clause of the mishnah, and does not explain the first clause. So too the Rif marks the clause: a resident of an unwalled town who went to a walled city, Rav said, this was taught only, etc. But the Ran already rejected this, because an unwalled-town resident for a day is explicit in the verse, and the Rif marks the clause of the mishnah. There is proof from what is said in the Yerushalmi there: an unwalled-town resident who uprooted his residence on the night of the fifteenth has become obligated here and here, and all the more so a resident of a walled city. See Ran.
And he remained. According to the explanation of the Rif, from what is written: that he is destined to return.
