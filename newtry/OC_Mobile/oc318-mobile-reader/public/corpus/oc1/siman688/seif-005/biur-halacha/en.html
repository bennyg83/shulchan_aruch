A townsman who went to a walled city etc. — this seif has many branches; see Peri Megadim and other acharonim; I copied in Mishna Berurah only Rashi's view that many rishonim agreed, also in brief sharp form; nevertheless in practice I ask the reader not to rely on me regarding beracha — see Peri Megadim; see Shulchan Gavoah and Biur HaGra who copied Yerushalmi for halacha: a townsman who uprooted his residence on the night of the 15th and went to a walled city is obligated in both places.
