And we need to know three things: a) if he belongs to the cities of the farzes, even for one day he is called Proz and recites on the 14th, and also if he belongs to the mokafin even for one day, he is called mokaf and reads on the 12th b) reading time is the most important thing in the day, and since he was in the cities of the farzas at the beginning of the 14th day, he reads on the 14th, and also as 33 for the matter of the mokafin on the 15th c) the reading time that the author writes is the time of reading the place he is now, not the time from where he left And now we will explain the section in order in each detail by itself. A citizen of the city who went to the volume if he had the intention at the time of his journey to return to his place during the reading RL during the reading of the 10th, that is, when the light of the day dawns, he will not be there because he will return from there before daylight, even though he finally sees that he must stay there even on the 15th day, he is not called by a circled name and recites on the 14th day while he is in the volume, but if at the time of his journey he did not intend to return from the volume but after the time of the reading, that is, in the morning of the 10th, then it is already time The reading then applies to him with a circled name and he recites with them on the 15th day, and even if he later returns to his city on the 15th day, he recites there if he did not read, beginning with the volume.
