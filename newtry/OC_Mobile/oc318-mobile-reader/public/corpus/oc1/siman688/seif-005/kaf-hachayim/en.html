And that it was walled and afterward settled, etc. And one could ask: since he said a walled city that settled and was walled at the end is judged like a village — how do walled cities from Joshua's days read on the 15th? We should fear perhaps it settled and was walled afterward. One can say gentile settlement is not settlement, and it turns out when Joshua conquered it there was a wall and it did not settle until Israel settled there, etc. But still one can question walled cities abroad — fear perhaps their settlement preceded their wall and they are as if without a wall; but one can say we follow the majority, that for the majority their wall preceded settlement, since most countries wall first and afterward settle them.
