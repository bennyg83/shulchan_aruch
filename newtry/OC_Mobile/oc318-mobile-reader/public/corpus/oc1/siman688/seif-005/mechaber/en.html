A resident of a town that went to a city or a resident of a city that went to a town - if it was his intention to return to his place at the time of the reading and he was delayed and did not return [to his usual place], reads according to his [usual] location; and if it was his intention to return only after the time of the reading, he reads with the people of the place where he is currently. {Rama: Rema: And if one is in the desert or on a boat, one reads on the 14th like the majority of the world. [Kol Bo]}
