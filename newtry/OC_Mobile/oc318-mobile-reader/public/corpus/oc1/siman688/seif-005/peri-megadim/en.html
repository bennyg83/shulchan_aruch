If 17 — here Rava's statement Megillah 19a has three explanations as Beit Yosef wrote and in Maggid Mishneh chapter 1 of Laws of Megillah halacha 17a: Rava — townsman who went to walled city refers per Mishnah order and from it we learn walled-city resident who went to town; and accordingly townsman to walled city: if his intent is to remain on morning of the 14th in walled city which is his reading time — his reading time is void and he reads on the 15th; and walled-city resident to town: if intent to remain morning of the 15th in town — reading time void, reads on the 14th (and the explanation townsman to walled city as Rosh wrote, and walled-city resident to town not like Rashi and not like Rosh, and matter depends on intent not on act that he will delay there in truth — for then what helps act for walled-city resident retroactively; rather depends on intent of prayer as we need say below — see there). Kessef Mishneh and Beit Yosef rejected this explanation of Rava that asks reading walled city first — see there. Second explanation Rashi: Rava — walled city to town, if intent on day of the 14th in unwalled town reads on the 14th; if intent to go before daylight reads per his place; automatically townsman who went to walled city: if intent to return to his place on the 15th reads on the 14th (being in walled city); if intent to remain morning of the 15th becomes walled and reads only on the 15th. But Rosh and Tur explained Rava on the end and on the beginning — all depends on morning of the 14th: townsman — time voided and departed, reads on the 15th; walled-city resident becomes unwalled, etc.; and Maggid Mishneh and Kessef Mishneh explained for R' Moshe z"l there like Rashi; Mechaber's words are copied from him; and one can say in Taz's words explanation of the 14th which is Rosh and Tur's explanation — he reversed his will who explained like Kessef Mishneh; and there wrote Rif also explains like Rashi; see Levush interprets Shulchan Aruch like Rosh and Tur; and Avodat Yisrael letter 11. And in Rif wrote the citation: townsman who went to walled city — Rava teaches like Rosh and Tur (and if he did not cite the word etc., Beit Yosef and Kessef Mishneh already rejected explanation one — see there); and we say at reading time for majority of world on the 14th; and so Avodat Yisrael; and in Be'er HaGolah he indicated at reading time the place he is in, not the place he left from — see amud haemek.
