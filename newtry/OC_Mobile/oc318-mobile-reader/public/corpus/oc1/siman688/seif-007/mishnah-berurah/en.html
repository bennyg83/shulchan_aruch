(1) And he doesn't find a scroll, etc. - if he found an obligation to take Likha and to do it in the 14th:
(כ) He reads it on the 13th, etc. — in assembly of ten since not in its time requires ten [and see Meiri that b'dieved even an individual fulfills; see below s.s. 692 in Mishna Berurah]. Indeed it is possible that children suffice since it is only for publicizing the miracle. See Ran who is uncertain regarding villagers who advance reading whether one must read also at night or daytime reading suffices; and plainly likewise for sailors at sea and one who travels; and from Tur's words it implies somewhat one must read also at night. And know further that leniency for villagers or sailors is only for Megillah reading, but Purim feast and mishloach manot and matanot laevyonim one fulfills only in its time:
(ג) (b) Even the new chapter, the dictates and the month that has become a protector for joy and suffering:
(ד) He returns and reads it on Sunday – we were in her blessings
