As for the fact that in siman 401 he wrote, 'Ordinary cities are enclosed for residential purposes, for they first build houses and afterwards surround them,' Taz, s.k. 2, wrote that what is stated in siman 401, that they first build houses, refers merely to an initial beginning, which serves only as an indication that a city will be established there. What is stated here, that ordinarily it was surrounded and afterwards settled, refers to the principal settlement and permanent establishment of the place, which come afterwards, see there. Pri Chadash wrote that what is stated in siman 401, that they first build houses and afterwards surround them, means that they first build the houses with the intention of surrounding them afterwards.
