[7] And they do not bless, etc. One could ask: why is there no blessing on two days as with other day-doubts on Yom Tov. And it appears to me that there the sanctity of the day causes obligation even in blessing because of doubt, and further the sanctity of festivals depends on Beit Din and they accepted it thus (Rashal and Bach); also Magen Avraham and Taz answered on this — see there. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah answered that here is different since we follow the majority of the world who read on the 14th — until here. And it is not precise, for Rashal and Bach's difficulty is on Tur who wrote in name of Rabbeinu Yechiel that one does not bless on two days from doubt and he does not accept this view of majority of the world. And I am more astonished at all the above great ones: on Shabbat daf 23 they challenge similarly on doubt regarding tithe that one does not bless on second day of Yom Tov though one blesses, and answer there: so they not treat it lightly — Rava said majority of amei ha'aretz tithe:
