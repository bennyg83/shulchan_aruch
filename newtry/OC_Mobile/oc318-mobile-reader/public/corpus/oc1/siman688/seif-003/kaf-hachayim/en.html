So there Tosafot and Rosh: whoever was walled from the days of Joshua ben Nun — even if there are not ten idlers, read on the 15th; but Magen Avraham wrote there is another view that even in a walled city walled in Joshua's days ten idlers are required, and if not it is like a town — this is Ramban and Rashba's view — end; Beit Yosef wrote this view was not clarified before Ran; nevertheless what is now customary in Eretz Yisrael to read on the 15th even though in most places there are not ten idlers idle from work to stand in synagogue — even for that view one can justify the custom: not specifically idlers, but whoever dwells there ten people who go morning and evening to synagogue — it is as if there are ten idlers there — end.
