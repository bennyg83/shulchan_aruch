So wrote Tosafos and the Rosh there, that any city surrounded by a wall from the days of Yehoshua bin Nun reads on the 15th even if it does not have ten batlanim. However, the Maggid Mishneh wrote that there is another approach regarding this, namely, that even in a city surrounded by a wall from the days of Yehoshua, ten batlanim are required; otherwise, it is considered like a town. This is the opinion of Ramban and Rashba. End quote. Beit Yosef cited his words and wrote that this approach was not accepted by the Ran. Nevertheless, he wrote that the present custom in Eretz Yisrael to read on the 15th, even though in most places there are not ten batlanim who refrain from work in order to remain in the synagogue, can be reconciled even according to that approach. It does not literally require men who are idle from work; rather, whenever ten men live there who go to the synagogue morning and evening, it is considered as though there are ten batlanim. End quote.
