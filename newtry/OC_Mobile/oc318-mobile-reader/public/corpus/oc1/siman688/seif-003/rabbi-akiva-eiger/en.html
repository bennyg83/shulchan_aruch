Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. "It is considered like a wall." See the Ran on that statement of Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, that a city settled and afterward surrounded is judged like a village; he wrote that Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, who compares megillah to walled houses, disagrees with Chizkiyah and holds that the sea is not a wall.
