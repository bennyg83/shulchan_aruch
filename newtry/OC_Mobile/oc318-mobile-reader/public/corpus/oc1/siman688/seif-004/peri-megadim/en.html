And Taz's words "they fulfill on the 14th" — I did not understand, for Pri Chadash at beginning of siman brought Yerushalmi: can a townsman discharge a walled-city resident — like all not obligated does not discharge; can a walled-city resident discharge a townsman — like all not obligated, or comes like R' Chelbo said R' Chiya Rabba: all fulfill on the 14th which is time of its reading — not explained; and it appears on R' Chiya Rabba there is no disputant that walled-city resident discharges on the 14th; and the question: since l'chatchila he was not obligated — if so since b'dieved he is obligated he reads; and therefore the question is resolved leniently like every teiku in d'rabbanan; and Pri Chadash in our Talmud implies he argues on R' Chiya Rabba who said its time is not like its time — say l'chatchila he speaks and to extend dispute — if so Tiberias and Hutzal l'chatchila read on two days and bless only on the 14th not the 15th — departed from necessity and forbidden to bless on the 15th; and no need for Taz's answer nor for his reason since reading time for majority of world as we say. And therefore we derive: walled-city resident who read on the 14th is forbidden to bless on the 15th and reads without blessing like Torah reading; and if he read for a townsman, townsman fulfilled — teiku in d'rabbanan leniently; also many poskim and from them Rashi that for villages they read towns — evidently obligated in the matter he reads since obligated in Megillah reading; and Taz's difficulty on Rashal from what is written 19th of Purim — say beracha of kiddush with sanctity of day is one, which is not so beracha of Megillah with mishloach manot — their matters are not related; and Pri Chadash's answer to this, and it is Ran's answer; and see Magen Avraham letter Dalet explained (for we hold doubt stringently):
