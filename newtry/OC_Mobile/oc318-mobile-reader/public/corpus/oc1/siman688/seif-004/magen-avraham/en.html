That is uncertain. In Teveria one must read on the fourteenth and fifteenth, because there is doubt whether the sea is considered a wall, Gemara. Outside the Land there are walled cities about which it is fitting to be uncertain, Shelah. Levush wrote that in these lands there is no uncertainty, because they are in the north and far from Eretz Yisrael, and it is known that they were not settled in the time of Yehoshua.

And he should not bless, etc. The Bach asked in the name of the Maharshal that he should bless on both days, just as with the two festival days of the Diaspora, etc.; see there. This is not comparable and presents no difficulty, for specifically on a mitzvah that the Sages enacted to perform because of doubt we bless; but mitzvos that we perform because of doubt on our own, we do not bless, even for a Torah-level doubt, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 28; see there.
