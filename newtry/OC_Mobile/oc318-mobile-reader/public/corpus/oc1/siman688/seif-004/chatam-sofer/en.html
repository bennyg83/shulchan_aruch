Note: In Pri Chadash he decided to forbid moving the megillah on Shabbos, while in Shiyarei Keneses HaGedolah in this siman and above in siman 308 he is uncertain about this. See Kol Shmuel at the end of siman 136. Nevertheless it appears to me that they said this only when the fifteenth falls on Shabbos for walled cities, where the concern of Rabbah applies; but when the thirteenth falls on Shabbos, the eve of Purim, and it is not the time of reading, it is permitted. See what Magen Avraham wrote above, siman 34, seif katan 18. According to what Magen Avraham wrote below, siman 692, seif katan 6, one should forbid it also on the thirteenth when it falls on Shabbos. But Pri Chadash disagrees with what is below and forbids reading it even when it falls before its time. This also requires further analysis: regarding that which is below, from where do we know that the concern lest he carry it applies when the public is not occupied with the reading? See what the Ran wrote regarding milah on Shabbos, that since the entire public is not occupied, we do not decree; the same should apply to megillah regarding individuals who are punished or coerced. See Chasam Sofer, Orach Chaim, siman 195.
