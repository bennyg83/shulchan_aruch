NB in Pri Chadash he ruled to forbid carrying the Megillah on Shabbat; and in Knesset HaGedolah in this siman and above siman 308 he is uncertain about this — see in Knesset HaGedolah end of siman 336. In any case it appears to me they said only when the 15th falls on Shabbat for walled cities, since there is concern for Rava's reason; but on the 13th when it falls on Shabbat, erev Purim, and it is not time for reading — permitted; see what Magen Avraham wrote above siman 34 s.k. 18. And per what Magen Avraham wrote below siman 692 s.k. 6 one should forbid also on the 13th when it falls on Shabbat. But Pri Chadash argues on that below and forbids reading it even when it falls before its time. And further requires study this below — from where do we know it applies lest one carry it if the congregation are not busy with reading; see what Ran wrote regarding milah on Shabbat: since the whole congregation are not busy we do not decree; and likewise for Megillah regarding individuals who are punished and compelled; see Chasam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 195:
