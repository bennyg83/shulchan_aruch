4) Ibid., Rama's gloss. Meaning, they refrain from their work and occupy themselves with communal needs. Rashi explained in Megillah 3b that they refrain from their work so that they will be present in the synagogue morning and evening, as we say in tractate Berachos 6b: When the Holy One, blessed is He, comes to the synagogue and does not find ten there, etc. End quote. It is possible that their coming to the synagogue and nullifying anger is called communal needs, because they protect the community. In the She'iltos it is explained that all of them are divided as follows: three judges and their attendant, two charity collectors and one person to distribute the charity with them, a scribe, a chazzan, and a teacher of children.
