Seif katan 4, it is a doubt, etc., whether the sea, etc. Tiberias was surrounded by a wall from the days of Yehoshua bin Nun, except that on 1 side the sea formed its wall. The Gemara was uncertain whether the advantage of a wall causes the city to be judged as a walled city because the wall protects it from enemies, in which case the sea too is called a wall, since it likewise protects; or whether the function of the wall is that it conceals the city's inhabitants from the enemy's sight so that they can hide themselves in a concealed place, which the sea does not do because it leaves them exposed and they cannot hide. According to the latter understanding, the sea does not have the legal status of a wall.
