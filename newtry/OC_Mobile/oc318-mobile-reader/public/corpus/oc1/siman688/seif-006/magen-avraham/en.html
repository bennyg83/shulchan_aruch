The fifteenth day, etc. On the Shabbos of the eighth of Adar it is Parashas Zachor and they read the haftarah of "I remembered," Beis Yosef. The Bach wrote in the name of Rabbi Levi ben Chaviv that on the fifteenth of Adar they also read the haftarah of "I remembered"; see there.
They do not read the megillah on Shabbos. They ask and expound on the subject of the day, Tur, Gemara.
And they do not make the Purim meal, etc. Rabbi Levi ben Chaviv elaborated with proofs that the meal is on Shabbos, and he acted accordingly in Jerusalem; also sending portions is on Shabbos, because the portions are from the meal. End quote of the Bach. Radbaz, part 1, siman 147, ruled like Shulchan Aruch, and so it appears to me, for the proofs of Rabbi Levi ben Chaviv are not compelling, and we do not have the power to disagree with the Yerushalmi. See the Gemara, page 30a, regarding doing and remembering. In any case, gifts to the poor are on the day of reading the megillah, as stated in the Gemara.
