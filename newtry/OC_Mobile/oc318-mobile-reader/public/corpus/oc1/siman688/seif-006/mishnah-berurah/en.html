14) The fifteenth day, etc. For the fourteenth cannot fall on Shabbos.
15) They do not read, etc. For the Sages decreed lest he go to an expert to learn how to read it and carry it four amos in the public domain.
16) The megillah on Shabbos. Then the Shabbos of the eighth of Adar is Parashas Zachor, and they read the haftarah of "I remembered." Also on the fifteenth of Adar, in walled cities, they read the haftarah of "I remembered," but in unwalled towns they read the haftarah of the weekly portion. They ask and expound on that day in the laws of Purim so that they remember the matter of the day; when it falls on a weekday, they fulfill this through reading the megillah.
17) And they say Al HaNissim. On Shabbos and not on Friday, for in truth the time for walled cities is on the fifteenth and not the fourteenth, and only because of the decree mentioned above did they require reading the megillah on Friday. Nevertheless, if he said it, we do not make him go back. So too they do not take out a Torah scroll then. In unwalled towns they say Al HaNissim on the fourteenth and not on the fifteenth. A city about which there is doubt whether it was walled from the days of Yehoshua bin Nun says Al HaNissim on both days.
18) And they do not make, etc. For we say in the Yerushalmi: let them make it on Shabbos. He said to him: it is written "days of feasting"; this excludes one whose joy depends on the court, namely Shabbos, whose joy is from Heaven. Automatically the same applies that sending portions is also on Sunday. Maharal Chaviv elaborated to prove that our Bavli does not hold this way, and his opinion is that the meal is on Shabbos; he acted accordingly in Jerusalem, and also sending portions is on Shabbos, because the portions are from the meal. Radbaz, part 1, siman 147, ruled like Shulchan Aruch, and this is also the opinion of Magen Avraham, Korban Nesanel, and Responsa Mishnah Berurah, part 1, siman 42. Pri Chadash forbids moving the megillah on Shabbos since they do not read it then, but several later authorities disagree with him and permit it.
