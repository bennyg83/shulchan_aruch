Section 6: The fifteenth day, etc. He omitted what is written, that they ask and expound, etc., because he holds that it is not specifically on Shabbos, but was said as a novelty. But the Rambam wrote that it is specifically on Shabbos, and so wrote Tosafos there under "Purim." This escaped the author of Lechem Mishneh.
Rather, they advance. As written there, 5a, like Rav.
And they collect. There, 4b: since, etc., their eyes, etc.
And on the day, etc., they read. 30b: on Purim, etc.; and there: for all of these they interrupt, etc. See Rashi there under "this is as it is, etc." We rule like Rabbi Yirmeyah, as written above in siman 685.
And they do not make. Yerushalmi, chapter 1: the Purim meal is delayed and not advanced. Let them make it on Shabbos? It is written "to make them days of feasting and joy"; this excludes one whose joy depends on the court, namely this, Shabbos, whose joy depends on Heaven. Rabbi Levi ben Chaviv asked from what is written in chapter 4, 30a, "doing and remembering, etc." See Rashi, 2b, under "we find, etc.," and see Magen Avraham. See Pri Chadash, who rejected him. It appears to me that the Rif's text was like the text of the old books: Rabbi Asi says they advance, so that remembering precedes doing; and Shmuel, etc., on the fifteenth, remembering precedes doing. See Rashi there under "this is the text, etc."
