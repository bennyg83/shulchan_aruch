They do not read. Because people are not expert in reading the cantillation notes, unlike with a Torah scroll; analyze this, and with this the difficulty of Tosafos Yom Tov is resolved. See Yad Aharon. They ask and expound on the subject of the day; Gemara.
Meal. Rabbi Levi ben Chaviv, siman 32, elaborated with proofs that the meal is on Shabbos, and he acted accordingly in Jerusalem, and also sending portions is on Shabbos because the portions are from the meal. See Bach. Radbaz, part 1, siman 147, ruled like Shulchan Aruch, and so is the opinion of Magen Avraham. In any case, gifts to the poor are on the day of reading the megillah. But it is forbidden to move the megillah on Shabbos; Pri Chadash, see there. See Shiyarei Keneses HaGedolah in this siman and in siman 308 in the glosses to Beis Yosef. Know that if the fifteenth falls on Shabbos, then the Shabbos of the eighth of Adar is Parashas Zachor and they read the haftarah of "I remembered." Beis Yosef. The Bach wrote in the name of Rabbi Levi ben Chaviv that on the fifteenth of Adar they also read the haftarah of "I remembered"; see there.
