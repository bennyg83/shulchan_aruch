(1) There is no Corinne. Dain Bekain in the reading of the reasons in the SAT and DOK and in this the difficulty of the Toitat is explained and see Yad Aharon. And they ask and pray in the matter of Yom. Cm:

(ב) Feast — meaning: Chaviv siman 32 extended proofs that the feast is on Shabbat and so was done in Jerusalem; mishloach manot on Shabbat too since the portions are from the feast — see Bach; Radbaz 1:147 rules like Shulchan Aruch; Magen Avraham agrees. In any case matanot laevyonim is on the day of Megillah reading. But it is forbidden to carry the Megillah on Shabbat — Peri Chadash see there; see Shakhnehag in this siman and 308 in Hagahot Beit Yosef. Know that if the 15th falls on Shabbat then Shabbat 8 Adar is Parashat Zachor and maftir Pakadti — Beit Yosef and Bach wrote in the name of Chaviv that on 15 Adar maftir Pakadti as well — see there.
