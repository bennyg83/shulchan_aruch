Walled cities 17. Here Ran disagrees with Beit Yosef in Yerushalmi language who said to give honor to Eretz Yisrael — Ran holds not to give honor but lest there be disgrace to Eretz Yisrael, for in Achashverosh's days they were destroyed and would be judged as unwalled without a wall; and his words have two difficulties: (1) why did they uproot walled cities of Achashverosh — fix both walled of Achashverosh like Shushan and walled of Eretz Yisrael lest there be disgrace to Eretz Yisrael, for all walled also on the 14th; and this difficulty I saw in Pri Chadash too; and per Taz's words it is fitting to give Shushan superiority since the miracle was greater, vengeance also on the 14th for Eretz Yisrael too — no difficulty (2); and Yerushalmi language to give honor to Eretz Yisrael too — no. And Ran's "lest there be disgrace" means if walled of Achashverosh on the 15th and Eretz Yisrael as unwalled — still fix all on the 14th except Shushan; and Taz's answer — no, as said; and difficulty (1) still stands. And Taz's explanation for Beit Yosef: without Shushan it is fitting to make remembrance for Eretz Yisrael on the 15th specifically; and that they did not do the reverse — walled on the 14th and unwalled on the 15th — one can say like Shushan; and nevertheless he challenges well: Shushan like whom, since nevertheless walled differ from unwalled; see amud haemek from this; Taz in Chemed Moshe wrote on Taz from gemara that said neither unwalled nor walled — see there; and in truth Taz has no main precision on this and it is not "if not" — yes one can explain neither unwalled nor walled; and from whom did he learn — if so difficulty also on walled cities; but Beit Yosef tied his words to Rashi who explained like one who did not know if walled from Joshua — meaning fitting to read both as Shulchan Aruch; so Beit Yosef challenged well: if not, know from Joshua — difficult no matter how much they did not know Shushan is different, etc.; why divide this mitzvah on the 14th and 15th; but Ran explains plainly and so Taz wrote, and Shushan they did not know — this is Rashi's explanation as we say:
