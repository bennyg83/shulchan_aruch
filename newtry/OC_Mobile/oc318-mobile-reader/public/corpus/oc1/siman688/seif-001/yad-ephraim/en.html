In Shulchan Aruch seif 1: and even if not walled now, etc. — in Tosafot daf 3b s.v. and the baraita is explained: per the view they did not sanctify forever — even if at end he settled it does not help; but Ran at beginning of Megillah explained: even per view they did not sanctify forever, nevertheless since they wished to give honor to Eretz Yisrael that was destroyed then — disgrace to Eretz Yisrael that towns outside the Land are walled, etc. — see there; and therefore must be they were mainly even though not walled now; and so from Rif and other poskim; requires study in Talmud regarding walled cities without ten idlers — Ramban and Rashba read on the 14th as Maggid Mishneh wrote in their name; and Beit Yosef wrote this view was not clarified before Ran; examiner will see he did not begin to reject Ramban's words; and from his words whoever lacks ten who rise early daily to synagogue should heed Ramban to read also on the 14th; nevertheless even if destroyed from ten idlers and afterward settled — judged as walled city as explained there; see Tosafot daf 3b that implies from their words s.v. as Rava: whoever did not dwell in a house until walled around — his law is as walled and did not settle; see Tur Eben that wrote to question Tosafot on this:
