From the days of Yehoshua bin Nun. They enacted it this way in order to apportion honor to Eretz Yisrael.
Presumably it was surrounded, etc. For the way of most countries is to surround them with a wall first and afterward settle them; end quote of the Ran. This is difficult, for it is the opposite of what is written in siman 401, that ordinary towns are surrounded for dwelling. The Rosh wrote in the name of the Maharam that it is not the way to surround with a partition until they first build the houses; end quote. It appears to me that for this reason the Maharam holds here that we require that it was settled first with the intention not to surround it, etc., as the Tur wrote; see there. The opinion of Rama requires analysis.
