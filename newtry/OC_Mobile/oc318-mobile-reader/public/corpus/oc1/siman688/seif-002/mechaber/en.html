And likewise the villages which are visible with them [the "walled" cities], even though they are not [directly] adjacent to them, such as when they are [together] on a mountain. Or they are adjacent to them even though they are not visible to them, such as when they are in a valley, provided they are not farther than a mil. And in Shushan, even though it was not surrounded by a wall from the days of Joshua, they read on the 15th [of Adar] because this is where the miracle occurred.
