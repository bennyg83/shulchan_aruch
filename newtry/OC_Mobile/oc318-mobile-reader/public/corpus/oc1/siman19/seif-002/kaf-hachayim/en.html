(2) On that point, and not an obligation on the garment, etc.—Rashal wrote: some are stringent to check the tzitzis even when folding the tallis, because Shmuel held that a folded vessel is obligated in tzitzis, and there are poskim like him, etc., end quote; and Bach, Perishah, and Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot ha-Turim, letter 1, brought this—see there. The posek who follows Shmuel is the view that Behag wrote, and Beit Yosef brought it; and likewise Raavan in Hagahot Maimoni, chapter 3, letter 3, and Peri Megadim in Mishbetzos Zahav, letter 1, brought it; and likewise the author of the Ittur, and Pitchei Teshuvah, letter 1, brought it; and see Yeshuot Yaakov, letter 1, who brought proof for Behag’s view—see there. However, our master, may his memory be a blessing, wrote in Beit Yosef: since the three pillars of halachic ruling—the Rif, Rambam, and Rosh—agree that it is an obligation on the person, one should not doubt the matter, end quote; and see there in Peri Megadim, who wrote that in Yevamos, page 2, it is obvious to the Talmud that folded vessels are exempt from tzitzis—see there. If so, one need not be concerned for the view that folded vessels are obligated—only one who is stringent may rely on it.

(2) And what he wrote there: Bach wrote in Piskei Dinim that it is correct to check the tzitzis when folding and storing the tallis so that its presumption of being checked remains, for then one need not check again when returning to wear it, etc. Knesset HaGedolah wrote there that those who are meticulous check their tzitzis every time they put them on; and we brought his words above, siman 8, letter 34—see there.
