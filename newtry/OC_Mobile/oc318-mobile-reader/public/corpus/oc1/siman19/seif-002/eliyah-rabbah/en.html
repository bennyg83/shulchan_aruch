[1] Sometimes, etc. Olas Tamid writes that according to the opinion cited above, which obligates a nighttime garment when worn during the day, the same applies here. Accordingly, under our practice one should attach tzitzis to it but should not recite a blessing. Magen Avraham likewise implies this. Clothing merchants who wear garments in order to display them to customers are exempt from tzitzis. The same applies when one wears them to avoid paying customs duties. See below, siman 301.
