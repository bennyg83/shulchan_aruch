(5) Exempt—the latter authorities conclude that specifically per the Rosh, who exempts above in siman 18 a garment designated for night even by day—and this too is like a night garment, since a dead person is exempt from mitzvos; but per the Rambam, who obligates a night garment by day, here too he is obligated. Therefore for us one should make tzitzis on it and not bless. Garment sellers who wear and intend to show to buyers are exempt by their custom; and if one wears them to pass customs duty with them, possibly he is obligated in tzitzis, as with kilayim.
