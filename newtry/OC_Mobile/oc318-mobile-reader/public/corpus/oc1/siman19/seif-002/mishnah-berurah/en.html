(5) Exempt. The later authorities conclude that this applies specifically according to Rosh, who exempts in siman 18 a garment designated for nighttime even when worn during the day. This garment is likewise comparable to a nighttime garment, because a deceased person is exempt from mitzvos. According to Rambam, however, who obligates a nighttime garment when worn during the day, this garment is likewise obligated. Therefore, under our practice one should attach tzitzis to it but should not recite a blessing. Clothing merchants who wear garments with the intention of showing their size to customers are exempt. If one wears them to avoid paying customs duties, it is possible that he is obligated in tzitzis, as with kilayim.
