In his lifetime he is exempt. Since the main purpose of making it is not to cover a living person, it is considered a night garment and is exempt even if he wears it by day; see Magen Avraham and Etz Yosef {Rama: (that in Eliyah Rabbah above, a night garment is obligated when worn by day—the same applies here; and therefore for us one should make tzitzis on it and not bless)}. Tosafos Niddah, page 61, wrote: garment sellers who wear garments intending to show them to buyers are exempt; and if one wears them to pass customs duty, possibly he is obligated in tzitzis. And see Yoreh Deah siman 381, Magen Avraham.
