While alive he is exempt. Since the garment was not made primarily to cover a living person, it is treated like a garment designated for nighttime and is exempt even if worn during the day. See Magen Avraham and Olas Tamid. According to the opinion cited above that a nighttime garment is obligated when worn during the day, the same rule applies here. Accordingly, under our practice one should attach tzitzis to it but should not recite a blessing. Tosafos on Niddah 61a write that clothing merchants who wear garments with the intention of displaying them to customers are exempt. If one wears them in order to avoid paying customs duties, it is possible that he is obligated in tzitzis. See Yoreh Deah, siman 301, and Magen Avraham.
