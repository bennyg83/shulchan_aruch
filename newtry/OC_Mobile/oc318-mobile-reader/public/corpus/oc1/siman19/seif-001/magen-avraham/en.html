“On making the tzitzis.” For there is no mitzvah in making them except in wearing them—see the Gemara, chapter 4 of Menachos. This needs study: what of a mezuzah, where we rule one must bless when affixing it, as Yoreh Deah siman 289 says? There too we rule it is a dwelling obligation, for likewise one who does not dwell in it is exempt from mezuzah—likewise Tosafos Menachos, page 44. Therefore it appears to me that the usual case with a mezuzah is that he affixes it when he dwells in the house, so he blesses on making it; but with a tallis he usually attaches tzitzis before he wears it, so he does not bless on making it. Even so, if he was wearing tzitzis and they snapped for him and he attached new tzitzis to it, he blesses “Who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to make tzitzis,” like a mezuzah. Still, one could say that as soon as they snapped he is obligated to remove it and repair it—see Shabbos, page 118—unlike a mezuzah, where the rabbis did not trouble him to uproot his dwelling. Possibly the same applies to a mezuzah: if he affixed a mezuzah in it before he dwelled there, when he enters to dwell there he blesses “Who has sanctified us… to dwell in a house that has a mezuzah,” as I wrote.
