Obligation. Ateret Tzvi, Beit Chanah, and Drisha, in the name of Rashal: some are stringent to check tzitzis when placing them in a pouch, like Shmuel; and likewise Raavan in Hagahot Maimoni, chapter 3, letter 50. Bach wrote that one should do so, lest tomorrow he forget to check, or he not have free time and rely on yesterday’s [check]—as stated in siman 8, letter 11, in Magen Avraham. And see siman 15, seif 1: it is certainly appropriate when tzitzis are available in the city not to permit [transferring strings] from garment to garment; and one should also be concerned for the view that a folded vessel is obligated in tzitzis—see there. And see Levush: “your garment” at the time you cover yourself with it; and in siman 14, “your garment” excludes a shroud, which is exempt. Now, regarding lifting and spreading [the tallis], there are opinions (in Magen Avraham, siman 18, letter 3) that it is written “you shall not wear fringes”—just as with kilayim, specifically in wearing, so too tzitzis, specifically in wearing; and this is the reason of the view that folded vessels are exempt. And in Yevamos, page 2: “his garments”—require examination; and the Merciful One said “techelet”—perhaps a folded vessel; if so, a folded vessel is obvious to the Talmud as exempt from tzitzis.
