1) [Seif 1] Tzitzis is a chovas gavra and not a chovas mana, etc. See what I wrote above, siman 17, ot 6.
2) There: and not a chovas mana, etc. Maharshal wrote: there are those who are stringent to check the tzitzis even when folding the tallis, because Shmuel holds that a garment kept in a box (kli kufsa) is obligated in tzitzis, and some poskim rule like him, etc., end of his words. Bach, Prishah, and Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur, ot 1, cite this; see there. The one who rules like Shmuel is the view that Bahag wrote, cited by Beit Yosef; and so is the view of Ra'avan in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 3, ot 3, cited by Peri Megadim, Mishbetzos Zahav, ot 1; and so is the view of Ba'al HaIttur, cited by Pitchei Olam, ot 1. See Yeshuos Yaakov, ot 1, who brought a proof for Bahag's view; see there. However, Maran, of blessed memory, wrote in Beit Yosef: since the three pillars of hora'ah — the Rif, the Rambam, and the Rosh — agree that it is a chovas gavra, one should not be in doubt about the matter, end of his words. See there in Peri Megadim, who wrote that in Yevamos daf 2 it is obvious to the Talmud that a garment in a box is exempt from tzitzis; see there. Therefore one need not be concerned for the view that a garment in a box is obligated; only one who is stringent, may blessing come upon him.
2) And that which Bach wrote there: it appears to me, in my humble opinion, that it is proper to check the tzitzis when one folds it and puts it away, so that it will have a chazakah of having been checked, and then one need not check it again when he returns and puts it on, etc. Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah wrote there that the meticulous check their tzitzis every time they put them on, and we cited his words above in siman 8, ot 34; see there.
