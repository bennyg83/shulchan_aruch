“Obligation on the person.” This does not contradict Rema siman 17 that [regular tzitzis] is not an obligation on the person — there it limits that one is not obligated to wear a four-cornered tallis as stated there — but garments lying [folded] that he does not wear one is not obligated to put tzitzis on them — excluding the view in the Gemara that a chest [keli kupa] requires tzitzis.
