(1) And not an obligation on the garment—see above, siman 17, seif katan 5, in his words.

(2) Exempt from tzitzis—even though it is written “and they shall make for themselves tzitzis on the corners of their garments,” nevertheless, since another verse is written, “you shall make for yourself fringes on the four corners of your garment that you cover yourself with,” it implies specifically when one covers oneself with it.

(3) On making the tzitzis—that is, at the time of attaching them to the garment; but afterward, when he wraps himself in it, he blesses on wrapping. And if they snapped after he was wearing it, he must remove it, make tzitzis on it, and bless when he returns and wears it—even if he wears it immediately.

(4) Only in wearing it—and the same applies to mezuzah: as we rule it is a dwelling obligation, one does not bless on the mezuzah except when he enters to dwell in the house immediately—then it is literally similar to wrapping—or when he already dwelled there [Birkei Yosef; and likewise it is implied from Magen Avraham’s words—examine it well; and see there further in Magen Avraham and Birkei Yosef, and in the responsum of Raavan at the end of siman 9].
