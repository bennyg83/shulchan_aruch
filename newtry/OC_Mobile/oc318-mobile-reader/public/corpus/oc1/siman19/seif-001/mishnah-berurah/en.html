(1) And not an obligation upon the garment. See above, siman 17, seif katan 5, and what is written there.
(2) Exempt from tzitzis. Although the verse says, “They shall make for themselves tzitzis upon the corners of their garments,” another verse says, “You shall make fringes for yourself upon the four corners of your garment with which you cover yourself.” This implies that the obligation applies specifically when one covers himself with it.
(3) Over making the tzitzis. Meaning, when attaching them to the garment. Rather, afterward, when he wraps himself in it, he recites the blessing “to wrap oneself in tzitzis.” If the strings became severed after he had put it on, he must remove the garment, repair its tzitzis, and recite a blessing when he puts it on again, even if he puts it back on immediately.
(4) Rather, when wearing it. The same applies to mezuzah, for we rule that it is an obligation upon the resident. One should recite the blessing over the mezuzah only when immediately entering to reside in the house, for then it is truly comparable to wrapping oneself in a tallis, or when he already lives there. Birkei Yosef. This is likewise implied by Magen Avraham when his words are carefully examined. See there further, as well as Birkei Yosef and responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger at the end of siman 9.
