[1] [Levush] Since the law, etc., they ask, etc. — meaning from the fundamental law; but the custom is also before the festival of Shavuot thirty days they ask so as not to distinguish (Bach and Acharonim). And my brother-in-law the Rav in Chok Yaakov wondered why Bach explained the baraita "they deliver on the matter of the day — laws of the festival on the festival," etc., meaning near the festival and not on Yom Tov itself; if so what is the difficulty in Megillah 4 on what it says Purim on Shabbat they ask and deliver on the matter of the day — why specify Purim, even Yom Tov as the baraita teaches, etc.; behold on Purim they deliver that day, and so Magen Avraham wrote it implies first chapter of Megillah on Yom Tov itself, etc. In my humble opinion to settle: Bach indeed concludes and writes however in Tosafot first chapter of Megillah it is clear that on Yom Tov itself they also deliver — until here; and from this Tosafot indeed learned; and further it appears in the Gemara the questioners ask well, for on Purim it also says they ask and deliver on the matter of the day; if so if the explanation regarding Yom Tov is "near," the same for Purim; and they answered even per his understanding what if we decree derashah because of reading, or they answered we decree derashah because of Purim reading; therefore Bach was pleased to bring from Tosafot — examine. Also what Bach thought a complete error in Rokeach siman 244 — see there; it appears to me Bach's intent per what I examined in Rokeach itself — this is his language: even though they deliver festival laws, one must deliver thirty days before — until here his language; he did not write festival laws on the festival as my brother-in-law copied from Mahari — it implies he holds not on the festival itself but near; and this is his explanation: even though they deliver near the festival, namely on Shabbatot, nevertheless one must deliver thirty days before. However for law Bach himself already wrote they deliver on Yom Tov itself; one must say as Bach wrote the reason we do not practice delivering thirty days, only on Shabbat HaGadol because villagers gather to the community — see there; and my brother-in-law challenged that Parashat Parah is like delivering that Pesach be done in purity; in my humble opinion Bach to his reason: nowadays when there is no sacrifice one needs thirty days before to deliver laws of hagalah and other practiced laws; if so they do not fulfill through Parashat Parah and piyyutim that contain no laws; and further on Shabbat HaGadol there are essential laws in piyyutim and even so they deliver:
