(א) They ask, etc. — in Tur it states they ask and deliver — and that is our Gemara's reading in Pesachim 6 and Bekhorot 58 and in Rif the reading is only "they ask"; and so the reading in Avodah Zarah and Sanhedrin 12; and apparently there is no difference, for the intent is one — to marry and give in Pesach laws before Pesach; and so in Rashi Rosh HaShanah 7 and Bava Kamma 113 that they delivered publicly in Pesach laws thirty days before Pesach; however Ran first chapter of Pesachim and first chapter of Megillah said that "they ask" means at that time if two students come and one asks Pesach laws they attend to him first because he is included in "question in context," but there is no obligation to learn thirty days before Pesach except on Pesach itself — and this is what we learned Moshe enacted for Israel to deliver laws of Pesach on Pesach; therefore he was precise here with "ask" alone — and his words are Rashba's in the fourth chapter of Megillah; but several Rishonim disagree — Rashi in several places and Tosafot first chapter of Megillah 4 and Rokeach — all explain explicitly obligation to deliver thirty days before in Pesach laws; and so in Ittur the reading is they ask and deliver; and so Or Zarua copied they deliver thirty days before; also it is very difficult, for in Sanhedrin 12 it implies explicitly this "ask deliver" is what the Gemara says there — behold in Pumbedita we deliver in Pesach laws, as the baraita teaches they ask Pesach laws thirty days before Pesach; and the essence of the matter proves thus, for if only because they attend a student who asks in context it is not relevant to say they would be lax in chametz, for there is no publicity at all when answering a student who asks; and I saw Bach stood on Ran from the sugya of Sanhedrin and so Peri Chadash and so Eshel Avraham wrote from Avodah Zarah 5 regarding sacrifices there also it does not imply like Ran. I further found in She'eltot he also wrote plainly: thirty days before Pesach we deliver Pesach matters as the baraita teaches they ask Pesach laws thirty days before Pesach; and more explicitly in Halakhot Gedolot: before Pesach thirty days Israel must learn Pesach matters and teach household members as the baraita teaches they ask Pesach laws, etc.; if so Ran and Rashba are alone against all the above Rishonim [and also from the sugya itself from R' Shimon ben Gamliel it proves it is not only to answer the asker but to learn and remember the laws; and from R' Shimon ben Gamliel we hear the Tanna Kama disagrees only on time but not on the essence; afterward I found Matateh Yehudah addressed this]. And what is stated at the end of Megillah — Moshe enacted they deliver laws of Pesach on Pesach, laws of Shavuot on Shavuot, etc. — the commentators already answered — see Beit Yosef, Bach, Shach, and Biur HaGra implies the commentators explain it is not specifically on the festival day alone but on the festival day and all the time before as explained elsewhere; and similarly Bach wrote [only that after learning thirty days before one need not learn on the festival itself — many Acharonim disagree]. Peri Chadash — and Chok Yaakov raised a novel matter to answer all the Acharonim's difficulty: Yerushalmi Pesachim chapter 1: they ask Pesach laws on Pesach, Shavuot on Shavuot, festival on festival; in the study hall they ask thirty days before; and so in Tosefta Megillah [Peri Megadim there: they ask Pesach laws on Pesach — some read: and one in his house; but in the study hall where many gather in synagogue to learn they ask thirty days before]; and Peri Megadim there also wrote that the end of chapter Bnei Ha'ir without study hall refers to each in his house, and thirty days before refers to the study hall — like Moshe in the study hall; however in Or Zarua it contradicts Chok Yaakov: since in our Gemara in several places [Pesachim 6, Megillah 29, Sanhedrin 12, Avodah Zarah 5] the Gemara brings this baraita anonymously "they ask thirty days before" and did not omit in one place to say "in the study hall," it is not likely for our Gemara; and so it appears in Avodah Zarah there; and so Bach ruled each must begin Pesach laws from Purim — end of his words; and indeed in Bahag I copied it is as his words; in any case in synagogue in groups certainly there is obligation to learn thirty days before even per Yerushalmi as Peri Megadim wrote:
