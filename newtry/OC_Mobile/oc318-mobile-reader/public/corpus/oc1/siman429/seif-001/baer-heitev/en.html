(א) Pesach. And the same for other festivals. Bach, Magen Avraham, Chok Yaakov:

(ב) Thirty days. And they begin from Purim day itself — Chok Yaakov; and Yad Ephraim — unlike Peri Chadash — see there. And likewise on the festival itself they ask and deliver derashot on the matter of the day. Our custom is that we do not deliver derashot on the festival itself; we also do not deliver derashot thirty days before Pesach — only on the Shabbat before Pesach called Shabbat HaGadol — see Bach and Chok Yaakov for the reason; see Yad Ephraim. Nevertheless it is a mitzva for each person to engage in the laws of Pesach thirty days before Pesach — Bach. And they always deliver derashot on Shabbat HaGadol when erev Pesach does not fall on it, for then they must advance to the previous Shabbat — Maharil:

(ג) Twelve months. And the same — a poor person who resides here twelve months they must give him. Beit Yosef Yoreh Deah siman 256. Semak wrote that nowadays thirty days suffice. And so the Rav in Yoreh Deah there. And if his intent is to settle he is like a city resident immediately as explained there.
