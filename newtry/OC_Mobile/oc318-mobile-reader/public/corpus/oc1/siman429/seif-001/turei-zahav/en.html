They ask on Pesach laws. Ran questioned from what we learned that Moshe enacted for Israel to deliver laws of Pesach on Pesach, etc., and answered there is no obligation to ask and deliver except a practical difference that it be a question in context, etc.; and it appears to me there is a great difference regarding money as stated in chapter HaSho'el — the rabbis said to Rava: our master, ask us — Rashi: our rabbi was asked for our work to teach us Torah — he sits and teaches Torah all day; if we asked him for an animal and it died he is exempt, for it is a question in the owner's presence. Rava was angry: you came to cause me monetary loss; on the contrary you asked me — for I could evade from tractate to tractate when I wish to begin another tractate so I do not forget; you cannot protest; it is not so — he asked them on the days of all forty when they deliver derashot before the festival on festival laws when he cannot evade to another tractate, and they asked him on other days. If so there is a difference if students asked the rabbi for an animal within thirty days before Pesach and it died — exempt, for that time is subjugated to them and it is a question in the owner's presence as stated — correct. In Tur it is written therefore thirty days before Pesach the obligation of biur applies — and that is the Gemara first chapter page 6; what connection is there? It appears per the Gemara's reading "they ask and deliver on Pesach laws," etc., and so in Tur; and it seems the explanation: "ask" is what they will need in the future on Pesach day; "deliver" is what is needed that day — we find this in study where the Tosafot remembered the Tanna Kama from the first Pesach when Moshe stood and warned about the second Pesach; they questioned there that through the question he was compelled to tell them; one may answer nevertheless he need not elaborate except "do not make Pesach" and no more — behold Moshe delivered that they should make second Pesach; from this they learned the sage delivers on his own what is needed now for Pesach in its time — Moshe delivered they should make second Pesach; thirty days are needed because of the sacrifice to inspect blemishes as Beit Yosef wrote; from this they learned for the other festivals without sacrifice as Tosafot Avodah Zarah 5 explained that even without sacrifice thirty days are required as with sacrifice; if so you must say that also in other laws one must now deliver what is obligated from today — and you will not find this except one leaving his house before Pesach who estimated he must biur from that day if within thirty — and this is correct in my humble opinion:
