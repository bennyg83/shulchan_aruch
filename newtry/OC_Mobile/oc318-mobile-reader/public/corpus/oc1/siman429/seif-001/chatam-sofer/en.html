In Magen Avraham s.k. 3 and siman 420. NB Ta'anit s.k. 2 and so it should be. And what many wondered about 13 Nissan — I already said that the peace offerings of the prince Achira ben Einan finished their eating that day if those peace offerings were eaten for two days and one night. I found in Rabbah Parashat Vayera end of parashah 50 that Sodom's overturning was 16 Nissan; Yefet wrote that accordingly the angels were with Abraham on 15 Nissan and it was the third day of his circumcision — thus he was circumcised 13 Nissan; great is that day and fit to make it a festival. Now I found in Keneset Yechezkel siman 117 as the first reason I wrote — see a wondrous matter in Ma'aseh Rokeach on Pesachim citing it in Kikar LaAden chapter 29 from Tractate Soferim — see there Chinuch: in the Temple in the future it will be after Pesach — see there; and further it appears per R' Yitzchak of Corbeil in Rosh chapter Erev Pesach — behold then erev Pesach was Shabbat and all Israel slaughtered their festival offerings on 13 Nissan and it was Yom Tov — end of his words:
