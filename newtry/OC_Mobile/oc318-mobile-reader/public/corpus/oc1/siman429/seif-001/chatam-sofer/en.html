In Magen Avraham, seif katan 3, and siman 420. It should read: Taanit, seif katan 2. Regarding the question many have raised about 13 Nissan, I have long said that the peace offerings of the nasi Achira ben Einan reached the end of their permitted eating on that day, if those peace offerings were eaten for 2 days and 1 night. I also found in Bereishit Rabbah, Parashat Vayera, end of section 50, that the overturning of Sodom took place on 16 Nissan. Yefeh Toar writes that according to this the angels were with Avraham Avinu on 15 Nissan, which was the third day after his brit milah; it follows that the brit milah took place on 13 Nissan. Great goodness attaches to that day, and it is fitting to treat it as a Yom Tov. I have now found in Knesset Yechezkel, siman 117, the first reason that I wrote. See also a remarkable matter in Maaseh Rokeach on Pesachim, cited in Kikar LaAden, chapter 29, from Masekhet Soferim, that the dedication of the future Beit HaMikdash will occur after Pesach. Furthermore, according to the view of R' Yitzchak of Corbeil cited by the Rosh in chapter Arvei Pesachim, the matter is also resolved, for that year Erev Pesach fell on Shabbos and all Israel slaughtered their chagigah offerings on Erev Shabbos, 13 Nissan, and that day was treated as Yom Tov.
