They ask about the laws of Pesach, etc. And now in exile, when we have no sacrifices, the custom is to deliver derashot on the Shabbat before Pesach; nevertheless it is a mitzva for each person to engage in the laws of Pesach all thirty days before Pesach. But according to Tosafot in the first chapter one must deliver derashot for thirty days even now.
