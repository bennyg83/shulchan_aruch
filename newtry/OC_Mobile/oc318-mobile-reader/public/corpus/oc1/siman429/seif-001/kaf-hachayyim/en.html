1) [Se'if 1] One asks about the laws of Pesach, etc. It is stated in Pesachim 6a that one asks and expounds upon the laws of Pesach during the thirty days preceding Pesach. The reason stated there in the Gemara is that Moshe stood on the first Pesach and warned concerning Pesach Sheni. See there. Although the Gemara says, 'One asks and expounds,' the Shulchan Aruch omits the word 'expounds,' because the primary public exposition applied in the time of the Beis HaMikdash for the proper preparation of the offering, as stated in Beis Yosef and Avodah Zarah 5b. See there. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1. Beis Yosef nevertheless writes that although we no longer have the offering, since the enactment to expound thirty days beforehand was instituted when the offering existed, the enactment remains in place. Tosafos on Avodah Zarah there and Peri Chadash write likewise. See below, subsection 3. See also the sacred work Edus B'Yaakov, derush 2 for Shabbos HaGadol, regarding the mystical reason for the thirty days preceding Pesach.
