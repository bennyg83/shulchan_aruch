(א) Nissan. Because twelve princes offered on twelve days and each day of the offering was his festival; afterward Pesach and Isru Chag — thus most of the month came out in holiness. (And in the Gemara Menachot the reason is from the beginning of Nissan the festival of Shavuot is established — see Chok Yaakov for the difference.) Therefore they make all of it holy:

(ב) Tzidkatkha. Also Yehi Ratzon after Torah reading is not said; also souls are not mentioned; also Kaddish after Tziduk HaDin is not said — keep the rule in hand that Tziduk HaDin, Yehi Ratzon, Rachum VeChanun are equivalent. Rama wrote in Yoreh Deah siman 401 that Tziduk HaDin is not said on Motza'ei Shabbat after midnight — the same on erev Yom Tov and erev Rosh Chodesh and erev Chanukah they say after midnight. (And in many Ashkenaz places they say after midnight while walking) — Levush. Eshel Avraham — one should read in Nissan each day its nasi; on the 13th the portion Beha'alotcha until "so he made the menorah":

(ג) In public. Meaning they do not mention the fast in public; nevertheless an individual who is accustomed to fast on a parent's yahrzeit may fast per the Mechaber's view; therefore Rama wrote that one does not fast at all:

(ד) Dream fast they fast. And one need not sit fast-for-fast; and if he fasted on Shabbat a dream fast he sits fast-for-fast in Nissan. The same between Yom Kippur and Sukkot or on days of restriction — Magen Avraham. Nevertheless it appears if he is close to the tenth of the month he fasts on the tenth which is the fast of the righteous as written siman 580. The same if Rosh Chodesh Nissan was on Sunday he fasts — Bach and Shelah wrote that after Isru Chag one may fast Yahrzeit, and the same on Rosh Chodesh Iyar; Magen Avraham, Taz, and Chok Yaakov wrote one should not be strict for these fasts are mere stringencies. (Chok Yaakov concludes: go out and see what the people practice.) And groom — see siman 573 for his law:

(ה) LeTodah. For thanksgiving bread was chametz and even on erev Pesach it is forbidden to offer it lest it become notar:

(ו) On erev Pesach. And they rise early to synagogue on erev Pesach so he finishes his meal before the fourth hour — Maharil:

(ז) Isru Chag. The custom not to fast on any Isru Chag — Magen Avraham. To impose a cherem in synagogue for some great need is permitted in Nissan even if forbidden in Tishrei:
