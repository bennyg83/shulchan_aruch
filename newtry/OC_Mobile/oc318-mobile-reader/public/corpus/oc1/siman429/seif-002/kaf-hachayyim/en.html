2) There. One asks about the laws of Pesach, etc. This provides ample time to attend to grinding the wheat, baking matzah, kashering utensils, and eliminating chametz. If these preparations are not performed properly before Pesach, they cannot be corrected during Pesach. This is unlike the other festivals, and therefore their laws need not be asked about and expounded so long beforehand. Beis Yosef. Levush writes likewise. Bach, however, proves from the words of the halachic authorities that the laws of Sukkos and Shavuos are likewise expounded thirty days beforehand. See there. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Peri Chadash; Chok Yaakov, subsection 2; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1, write likewise. The Gra, however, writes that the laws of Shavuos need not be studied for thirty days, but only from the first of Sivan. See there.
