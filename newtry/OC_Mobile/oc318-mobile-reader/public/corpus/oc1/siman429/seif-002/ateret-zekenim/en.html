We do not fall on our faces, etc., and we do not say Yehi Ratzon after Torah reading; and likewise on every day when Tachanun is not said, Yehi Ratzon is not said, and souls are not mentioned. One should fast after Pesach — Yahrzeit — for it is no less than the fast of students; and all the more so on Rosh Chodesh which is most fixed — specifically as an individual and not in public; in what case? in private, but to mention [fasting] in public is forbidden until Nissan passes.
