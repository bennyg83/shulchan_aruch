We do not fall on our faces throughout the entire month of Nissan, and we do not say Tzidkatkha on Shabbat at Mincha, and we do not deliver eulogies, and we do not fast for communal mention [of fasting], and the firstborn fast on erev Pesach.Also we do not say Tziduk HaDin throughout the entire month of Nissan. And the custom is not to fast at all — even on the day one's father or mother died. But one does fast a fast for a dream. And we do not say Mizmor LeTodah, nor Kel Erech Apayim, nor Lamenatzeach on erev Pesach; the same on Yom Tov (Minhagim). And the custom is to increase somewhat in eating and drinking on the day after the Festival — Isru Chag (Gemara and Rashi chapter Lulav).):
