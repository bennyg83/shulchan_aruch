(א) Seif 2 — we do not fall, etc. Mishnah Soferim last chapter: one does not say supplications all days of Nissan. The rule: every day when Tachanun is not said, Tziduk HaDin and Yehi Ratzon are not said; all the more eulogy, for Tziduk HaDin is forbidden only because of eulogy; and see siman 292 seif 2 and siman 420 seif 2; all this is from custom, but by Gemara law all is permitted, for they are among the days written in Megillat Ta'anit; see Tosafot Rosh HaShanah 19b s.v. from the days, etc.:
