(1) To remove the coverings from the table. Wherever the word teivah appears, it ordinarily means the Aron HaKodesh. Here, however, it means the table upon which the Sefer Torah is placed when it is read. Peri Megadim.
(2) In which it is kept securely. Fine cloths are spread around the table in honor of the Sefer Torah. When they leave and take the Sefer Torah to place it in the building where it is kept securely, the chazzan should not remove the coverings from the table in the presence of the congregation, because it would burden them to remain there with the Sefer Torah until he has uncovered the table. Rather, he carries the Sefer Torah away and they leave after it, as stated in siman 149. He then returns to the synagogue and removes the coverings from the table. All this follows Rashi's explanation there.
