Seif katan 1: To remain there, etc. Rama abbreviated the words of the Tur. The Tur wrote that the Sefer Torah was kept in another secure place, not in the synagogue. Before bringing the Sefer Torah to read from it, the chazzan would cover the table on which they placed the Sefer Torah for reading with fine and attractive cloths. After the reading, he would remove those cloths from the table and store them until the next reading. Since the congregation must remain in the synagogue until the Sefer Torah is taken to the place where it is stored, and the entire congregation must accompany it, as stated in the following siman, if the chazzan were first to remove the coverings from the table and only afterward store the Sefer Torah, this would burden the congregation, for they would have to wait until after the table was uncovered and the Sefer Torah was carried to its storage place. Therefore, they should first store the Sefer Torah and only afterward remove the coverings from the table.
