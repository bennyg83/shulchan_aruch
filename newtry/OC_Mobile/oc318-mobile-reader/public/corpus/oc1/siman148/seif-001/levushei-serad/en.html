(Shulchan Aruch) "In which it is kept secure." Fine cloths are spread around the ark in honor of the Sefer Torah. When they leave that place and take the Sefer Torah to place it in a house where it will be kept secure, one should not remove the coverings from the ark in the presence of the congregation, because it would burden the congregation to remain there with the Sefer Torah until the ark has been uncovered. Rather, he should carry the Sefer Torah, etc. This is cited by Magen Avraham.
