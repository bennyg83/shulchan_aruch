1) [Seif 1] The shaliach tzibbur is not permitted to bare the teivah, etc. Although in every place the meaning of 'teivah' is an actual box in which the Sefer Torah is placed, here the meaning of this 'teivah' is the bimah on which they set up a stand like a table to place the Sefer Torah upon it, and they spread fine cloths around it. Bach; Eshel Avraham, ot 1. And see below in the gloss at the end of siman 150, and our words there, with the help of Heaven.
