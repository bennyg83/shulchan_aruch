Lest he forget them. But from Torah law it is permitted to leave them on.

That the sun sets. That is, the emergence of the stars [Beit Yosef in the name of Mordekhai]; and in siman 25 he wrote in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot that one removes them when he reaches Shema of Maariv for the heavenly bodies upon the earth — and that is when he prays Maariv while it is still day.

And one does not instruct thus. And if so, in public one must remove them, as written in siman 18.

In order to guard them. And he must say he acts in order to guard them [as is explicit in the Gemara].
