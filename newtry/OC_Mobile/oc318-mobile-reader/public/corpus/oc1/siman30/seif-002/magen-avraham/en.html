Lest they forget. But from Dauri' it is permissible to assume:
When the sun sets. Meaning, at the emergence of the stars. Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai. In siman 25 he writes in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos that one removes them during the evening Krias Shema upon reaching the words “as the days of heaven upon the earth.” This refers to one who prays Maariv while it is still daytime.
And one does not rule this way publicly. Therefore, in public he must remove them, as I wrote in siman 18.
In order to guard them. He must state that he is doing so in order to guard them. This is implied by the Gemara.
