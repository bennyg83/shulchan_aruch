(3) At night — and in bein hashemashot, from Magen Avraham's words it seems permitted ab initio to don; but Peri Megadim is uncertain about this unless he has not yet fulfilled the mitzvah of tefillin that day — then he dons them in bein hashemashot.

(4) And sleep in them — and we are concerned lest he pass wind in his sleep; but from Torah law it is permitted to leave them on at night, for we hold night is time for tefillin.

(5) Before, etc. — meaning the Sages forbade only to don them ab initio at night; but since they are already on him from while it was still day, they did not require him to remove them.

(6) All night — meaning as long as he does not remove them; for if he removes them, once removed it is again forbidden to don them.

(7) Permitted — meaning until the time he wishes to sleep; then he must remove them, for it is forbidden to sleep a substantial sleep in them, as below in siman 44. And it is possible that at night, which is time for sleep, even casual sleep is forbidden by law.

(8) And one does not instruct thus — the practical halacha for others: lest they come to don ab initio; therefore one should not do so except between himself and himself, but not in public, for that too is like instruction; therefore one should not pray Maariv in them at a public fast with the congregation.

(9) And they were found on him — specifically when found on him we are lenient and instruct that for guarding one need not remove even all night, provided he does not sleep; but to don ab initio is forbidden for guarding. And there are those lenient in this [Shaarei Teshuvah and the Gra].
