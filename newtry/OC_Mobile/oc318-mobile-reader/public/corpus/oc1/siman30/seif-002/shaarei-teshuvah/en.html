Avnei HaTzedek — and the latter authorities alluded to the verse "Man in honor will not lodge," etc., according to Rashi's explanation: lest he pass wind in them, which is the act of an animal and is not honor for tefillin; and tefillin are called "honor," as we say in Megillah: "honor — these are tefillin"; and this is what "Man in honor" means — in tefillin he shall not lodge; and one must remove them, since at time of sleep he is likened to beasts, etc. — and it is a fine allusion.
