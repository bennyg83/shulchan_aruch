And he sleeps. See Taz. This refers to fixed sleep, for temporary sleep is permitted, as explained in siman 44. Accordingly, one who rises before daybreak to depart should seemingly be permitted to put them on even in a wagon, because such sleep would be only temporary. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 3, Taz here, seif katan 5, and the discussion there, with Hashem's help.
