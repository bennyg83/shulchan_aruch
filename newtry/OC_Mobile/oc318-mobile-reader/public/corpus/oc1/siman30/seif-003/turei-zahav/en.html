Since he rose early and departed. According to this, it appears to me that one sitting in a wagon may not put them on, because we may properly suspect that he will sleep, for sleep is more common in that situation. Beit Yosef cites another reason in the name of Semak for permitting one who rises early to travel: concern that the tefillin may be lost. Shulchan Aruch does not accept this reason, for in seif 2 he writes that only if they were already found upon him may they remain, but he may not put them on initially, and the need to guard them does not suffice.
