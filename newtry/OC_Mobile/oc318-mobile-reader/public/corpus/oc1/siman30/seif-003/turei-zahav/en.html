Since he arose early and set out. Accordingly it seems to me one sitting in a wagon is forbidden, for properly we are concerned lest he sleep, for then sleep is more usual for a person. In Beit Yosef it is written in the name of Semak: another reason to permit when arising early to travel — lest they be lost from him; but Shulchan Aruch does not hold this reason, for in seif 2 he wrote specifically "they were found on him," but ab initio forbidden to don them, and guarding does not help.
