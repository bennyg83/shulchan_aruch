One instructs thus. Avnei HaTzedek — and see what is written in the laws of tzitzit that the Arizal was very careful to remove them after sunset, see there. (This is) the evening prayer. And Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of Dvar Moshe: if one blessed over tefillin at night, or thought it was day and it was night — he need not bless when the time for donning tefillin arrives, see there.
