(10) To set out on the road — and it is difficult for him to don them afterward because of the cold and all such cases [Chayei Adam].

(11) Arising early — even before dawn.

(12) He dons them — before his departure. And he may intend that he wears them for the sake of the mitzvah, since we hold night is time for tefillin; and even if he had a place to guard them, it is also permitted to wear them, since there is no concern lest he sleep in them, as below.

(13) And when their time arrives — for even though the Sages permitted advancing the donning at night where there is no concern of sleep, nevertheless regarding blessing they did not enact to say "and You commanded us" before the time. Nevertheless, if he advanced and blessed at night when he donned them, he need not bless again in the morning.

(14) And he set out on the road — and specifically when he walks on foot or rides; but one sitting in a wagon may not don them, lest he sleep in them; and see in Biur Halacha.
