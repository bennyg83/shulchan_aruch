(10) To depart on a journey. And it would be difficult for him to put them on afterward because of the cold or a similar circumstance. Chayei Adam.
(11) Early in the morning. Even before dawn.
(12) He puts them on. Before departing. He may intend to wear them for the sake of the mitzvah, since we rule that nighttime is a time for tefillin. Even if he has a place to guard them, he may wear them, because there is no concern that he will sleep in them, as explained immediately below.
(13) And when their time arrives. Although the Sages permitted advancing the act of putting them on at night where there is no concern of sleep, they did not institute reciting “Who has commanded us” before the proper time. Nevertheless, if he recited the blessing at night when putting them on, he need not recite it again in the morning.
(14) And he departed. Specifically one who walks or rides. One sitting in a wagon may not put them on, lest he fall asleep in them. See Biur Halachah.
