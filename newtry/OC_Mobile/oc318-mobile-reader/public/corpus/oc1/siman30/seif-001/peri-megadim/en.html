From when he sees. Taz: Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah tied it to seeing, as written (Deuteronomy 28), etc.; but it is only a support, for tefillin is not explicit there; and the main reason is lest he sleep in them until this time, which is included in night — so explained the Levush in letter 1 and 2, see there. And one who sets out on the road and reaches dawn — uncertain whether he blesses then; see seif 3. Rabbeinu Peretz in the Tur holds he blesses when traveling; and we rule he does not bless; nevertheless post factum one may say he blesses. For the Levush, the main reason is that it is night for this matter and lest he sleep by day — if so, there is no distinction, and he does not bless until his time. In siman 18 seif 2, tzitzit between tekhelet and white — apparently until he recognizes at a distance of four cubits is later; Tosafot Berachot 9b s.v. others say, according to "that he see his friend," and in the Gemara "from when he sees," see there; Eshel Avraham 1.
