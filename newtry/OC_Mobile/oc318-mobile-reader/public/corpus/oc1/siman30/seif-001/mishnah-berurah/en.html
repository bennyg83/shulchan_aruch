(1) Donning them — meaning the beginning of the time for donning them is from when one sees [his friend], and their mitzvah is all day, as below in siman 37, see there; and the reason is that until this time we are concerned lest he sleep in them, and it is included in night regarding tefillin.

(2) With him a little — for if there are many [who are accustomed], he will recognize even from afar; and if he is not accustomed at all he will not recognize even from very close.
