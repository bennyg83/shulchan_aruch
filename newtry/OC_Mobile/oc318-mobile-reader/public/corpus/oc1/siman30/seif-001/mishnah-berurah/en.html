(1) Putting them on. Meaning, the beginning of the proper time for putting them on is when one can recognize an acquaintance. Their mitzvah continues throughout the entire day, as explained below in siman 37. Before this time we are concerned that he may sleep in them, and it is considered nighttime with respect to tefillin.
(2) Somewhat acquainted with him. If he knows him very well, he would recognize him even from a distance. If he is entirely unfamiliar with him, he would not recognize him even from very close range.
