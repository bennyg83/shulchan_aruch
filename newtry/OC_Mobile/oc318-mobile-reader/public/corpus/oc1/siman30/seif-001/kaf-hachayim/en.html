1) [Seif 1] The time for donning them in the morning is from when one sees his friend, etc. The reason they tied it to seeing one's friend is because regarding tefillin it is written "seeing," as it says: "and all the peoples of the earth shall see that the name of the the Omnipresent is called upon you," etc. — Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yona; Levush; Taz note 1; Olat Tamid section 1; Avnei Tzedek letter 1. And this is only a mere support; the main reason is: since we hold night is time for tefillin, only the Sages forbade donning them at night lest he forget them and sleep in them, as in seif 2; and the prohibition of sleep is lest he pass wind in them, as Beit Yosef wrote in siman 44 and Taz here note 3 — the Sages supported themselves on this verse "and all the peoples of the earth shall see," etc., not to don them until the time one sees his accustomed friend, etc., for before this time everything is included in night regarding tefillin, and one must be concerned for the concerns mentioned, as the Levush wrote in seif 2 and Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav letter 1; Shitah Mekubetzet letter 3. Also for the Rambam, who holds night is not time for tefillin, as in chapter 4 of Hilchot Tefillin — one may say the reason likewise is not to don tefillin until one sees his accustomed friend, etc., because of the concerns mentioned; and so too per the Arizal in Shaar HaKavanot, drush 8 on tefillin, and in Piskei HaChayim, Shaar HaTefillin chapter 7, who wrote night is not time for tefillin and there is no aspect of tefillin except from morning light onward — one may say likewise not to don tefillin until the time one sees his friend, etc., because of the concerns mentioned — and not as they wished to say per the Arizal that it is permitted to don tefillin ab initio from dawn onward; the Omnipresent forbid to say the Arizal would be lenient against what they were stringent about in the Talmud — the edited Talmud in Berachot 9b — and so ruled the Rif, Rambam, Rosh, and latter authorities. Rather the Arizal came to say the beginning of the time of the aspect of the arrival of the mochin of tefillin is from morning light onward; but now, since one may not don them except at the time the Sages established in Shas and poskim, one may not deviate from their words — and this is straightforward.
