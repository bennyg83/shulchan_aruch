Author seif 3 He should feel them and bless. If he blessed when arising early, in my humble opinion he does not return and bless again, since the Rif's view brought in the Tur is that ab initio one blesses when donning at arising early; if so, when he did so he does not return and bless out of doubt lest the halacha follows the Rif. See in siman 37 note 3.
