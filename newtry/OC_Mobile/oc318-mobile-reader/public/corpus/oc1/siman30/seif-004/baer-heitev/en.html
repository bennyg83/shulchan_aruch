The sun. This deals with a weekday; and even though we say nearby that one need not remove them, for night is time for tefillin — here it is different, for there is concern that one who encounters him will err and say that it is permitted to don them then; but nearby it deals with one sitting in his home. And so that which is written afterward, "the day was sanctified upon him" — meaning the sanctity of Shabbat is upon him, and he must remove them; but on a weekday one need not — that is, since he is in the study hall and there is no concern of erring. Taz; and the Avnei Tzedek disagrees, there.
