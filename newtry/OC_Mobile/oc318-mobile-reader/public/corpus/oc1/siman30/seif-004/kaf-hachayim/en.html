4) [Seif 2] It is forbidden to don tefillin at night lest he forget them and sleep in them. It is explicit from here that he gave the reason lest he forget and sleep in them — the view of Maran is like those who say night is time for tefillin and it is permitted from Torah law, only the Sages forbade not to don them lest he forget and sleep in them. And so Magen Avraham note 1 — and this is the view of Tosafot, the Rosh, the Tur, and other poskim; only the Rambam holds in chapter 4 of Hilchot Tefillin that night is not time for tefillin — see there in Kessef Mishneh and what Birkei Yosef wrote letter 1 and the author on the words of the Rambam cited, and the Kessef Mishneh — see there; and see above letter 1 and in siman 29 letter 1.
