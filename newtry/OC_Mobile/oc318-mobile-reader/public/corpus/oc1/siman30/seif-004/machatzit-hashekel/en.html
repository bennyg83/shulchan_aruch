(Seif katan 4) In order to guard them, etc. This is likewise implied by the Gemara at the end of HaKometz. Ravina said: I was sitting before Rav Ashi, darkness came, and he did not remove his tefillin. I said to him, “Does the master need them in order to guard them?” He answered, “Yes.” I perceived, however, that he did not actually need them for safekeeping, but held that the halachah is that when they were already upon a person before nightfall, he need not remove them even after nightfall, although one does not publicly rule this way. The Gemara's wording requires examination. How did Ravina know enough to say, “I perceived his intention”? Perhaps Rav Ashi genuinely needed them for safekeeping. It must be that if a person intends to keep them on for safekeeping while others are present, he must state that this is his purpose. Since Rav Ashi did not volunteer that explanation before Ravina asked, it was evident that his true intention was not safekeeping. This is Magen Avraham's meaning. Nevertheless, this proof may be questioned somewhat.
