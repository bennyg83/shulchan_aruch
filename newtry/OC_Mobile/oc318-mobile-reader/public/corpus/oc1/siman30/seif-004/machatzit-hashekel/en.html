(note 4) In order to guard them, etc. And so is explicit in the Gemara: we say in the chapter HaKometz, Rabbina said: I was sitting before R' Eliezer and it grew dark and he did not remove the tefillin; and he said to him: "My master wishes to guard them," and he said to him "yes"; and I saw in his mind it was not to guard them he wanted, but he held the halacha (when they are resting on him from earlier one need not remove them even when it grew dark) — and one does not instruct thus; therefore he did not reveal the truth to Rabbina, for since they are already on him he need not remove them, for this is called instruction — end quote from the Gemara. One may question: from where did Rabbina know this, that he said "I saw in his mind" and R' Eliezer denied him? Perhaps in truth he wanted to guard them? Or: if he intends to guard and he is before others, he must say he acts to guard them, since R' Eliezer did not precede and tell Rabbina before they asked that his intent was to guard; or: his intent was not to guard — this is Magen Avraham's intent. However one may somewhat doubt this.
