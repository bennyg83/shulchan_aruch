And the sun set upon him. This concerns a weekday. Although the following ruling says that he need not remove them, this case is different because someone who encounters him may mistakenly conclude that it is permitted to put on tefillin at that time. The later case concerns one sitting in his home. Similarly, when Shulchan Aruch afterward says, “and the day became sanctified,” the meaning is that the sanctity of Shabbos took effect and he must remove them. On a weekday he need not remove them, because he is in the beis midrash and there is no concern that observers will err.
