(15) And the sun set, etc. — the first clause deals with a weekday: to remove them and to carry them in his hand he fears lest they fall from him on the road; they permitted him to place his hand on them and cover them. And even though in seif 2 we say that since they are already on him he need not remove them — here is different, lest one who encounters him err and say it is permitted to don tefillin then; but nearby deals with one sitting in his home. "And the sun set" means the end of sunset, which is emergence of the stars; in bein hashemashot he need not remove them, and one instructs thus in practice — all this by law. In the name of the Arizal it is written he was careful to remove them after sunset.

(16) And it was sanctified, etc. — meaning it became bein hashemashot and he cannot carry them in his hand to his home because of the sanctity of Shabbat; and to leave them there is impossible, for their batei midrash were in the field in a place not guarded from thieves; therefore the Sages permitted carrying them on him as clothing until his house — but he must cover them so they do not see him carrying tefillin on Shabbat.
