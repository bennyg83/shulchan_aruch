(17) While it was still day — and even if the day is still long before sunset, since he already made this time "night and time of lying down" in Shema and the evening prayer — if he dons tefillin in it he would turn it back into day, and behold there are two voices contradicting each other. However the latter authorities agreed to the halacha that he is obligated to don them, without a blessing, as long as it is before the stars emerge; and if he has not yet prayed, even though the congregation prayed, it makes no difference — he dons with a blessing if it is still day, since he does not make two contradictory voices. And specifically if he did not don at all that day; but if he already donned tefillin and only wishes to don again now, it is not proper to don if the congregation already prayed Maariv.
