The later authorities write likewise: Yad Aharon, Hagahot HaTur; Birkei Yosef, ot 2; Shalmei Tzibur, page 9a and page 41a; Siddur Beit Yaakov, laws of the time for putting on tefillin, ot 2; Shaarei Teshuvah, ot 3; and Yafeh LaLev, of blessed memory, ot 2.
