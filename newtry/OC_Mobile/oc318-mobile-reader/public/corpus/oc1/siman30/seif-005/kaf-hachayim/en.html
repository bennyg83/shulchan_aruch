If he donned them before sunset and it grew dark upon him, etc. — Rabbi Shelah wrote in Hagahat Talmid that his custom on a fast day is not to remove tefillin and tallit until after the evening prayer, and he was supported by Lechem Chamudos, Laws of Tefillin siman 179 — see there; and see above in order, siman 18 letters 11–12, that the Arizal was very careful not to be wrapped in tallit and tefillin after sunset, see there; and so the latter authorities.
