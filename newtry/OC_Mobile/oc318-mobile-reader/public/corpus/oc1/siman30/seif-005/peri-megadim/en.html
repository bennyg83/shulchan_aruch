Taz: the author holds the reason night is time for tefillin is only lest he sleep and pass wind (not because of "and all peoples of the earth shall see" alone, as letter 1); and for one traveling — no sleep; and even if he is not afraid lest they be lost, permitted to don before daylight; accordingly in a wagon forbidden — but a rider permitted, unlike Magen Avraham note 5.
