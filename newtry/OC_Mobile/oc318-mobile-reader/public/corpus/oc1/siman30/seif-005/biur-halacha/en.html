And there are those who, etc., while it was still day, etc. — See Mishnah Berurah in what he wrote: "night and time of lying down in Shema," etc. — this is from Shulchan Aruch HaRav; and with this one somewhat resolves the difficulty of Eliyahu Rabba, for the prohibition of tefillin at night is not because of night per se, only because of sleep, as in seif 3; and with this there is some comfort. Accordingly, for those careful people who recite Shema again at its proper time and do not recite it then except to stand in prayer from words of Torah, as below in siman 235:1 — perhaps this law changes; see there. And see again in Mishnah Berurah in what he wrote: if he has not yet prayed, etc. — if it is still day, but if he is in bein hashemashot, it seems to me he should not bless then, for in any case Peri Megadim doubts the words of Magen Avraham in note 2 in what he wrote, that the time for donning tefillin is until the stars emerge — even though certainly he is obligated to don them since he has not yet fulfilled the mitzvah of tefillin, for in matters of Torah a doubt is resolved stringently, and perhaps it is still day; and as Peri Megadim ruled in Eshel Avraham — in any case blessings do not impede.
