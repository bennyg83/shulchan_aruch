1) [Seif 1] One does not read from the Torah with fewer than ten, etc. For it is a davar shebikdushah, and there is no davar shebikdushah with fewer than ten, as we derive from 'and I shall be sanctified in the midst of,' etc. Levush. And see our words above in siman 55, ot 6.
2) There: With ten adult free men. That which he said 'ten adults' is to exclude minors, for although a minor completes the seven readers, nonetheless he does not complete the count of ten; and that which he said 'free men' is to exclude complete slaves. And regarding one who is half slave and half free, there is doubt; and it appears that since we hold that they compel his master and make him a free man, he is obligated in all the mitzvos, for 'whatever is fit for mixing,' etc.; and so it is proven at the beginning of Chagigah; see there. Olas Tamid, ot 1. But the Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1, disagreed with the aforementioned words of the Olas Tamid, and wrote that one who is half slave does not combine; see there. And the same [is stated by] the Eshel Avraham, ot 1. And so wrote [the author] above in siman 55, ot 15. And see our words there, from ot 12 to ot 18, who combines and who does not combine; and the same applies here; see there.
3) There: Ten adults, etc. And if there are only seven who have not [yet] heard the reading of the Sefer Torah, they may combine with them three who have already heard, and take out a Sefer Torah. Lev Chaim, part 2, siman 25. And so wrote [the author] above at the beginning of siman 69 regarding the repetition of the Amidah; see there.
4) There: And if they began with ten and some of them left, etc. And concerning those who leave it is said, 'and those who forsake Hashem shall be consumed.' Bach in the name of the Yerushalmi; Perishah, ot 1; and so it is [written] in the Rama above in siman 55, seif 2. And even between one man [oleh] and another, Perishah there. And that which is stated at the beginning of siman 146, that it is permitted to leave between one man and another, is when ten remain. And so wrote the Eshel Avraham, ot 1. And see below siman 146, ot 5.
5) There: And some of them left, they complete [the reading]. But they do not say Kaddish after the reading of the Sefer Torah, just as the Pri Chadash concurred above in siman 55 that one does not say the Kaddish 'Tiskabbel'; and further, since this custom to say Kaddish on Monday and Thursday after the reading of the Torah is a custom of the later authorities, and now that some of the ten have left, one should not say it. Birkei Yosef, ot 1; and not like the Sha'ar Ephraim, sha'ar 7, ot 38. And see our words above in siman 55, ot 34 and ot 35, and examine it well.
