Seif katan 1: They complete, etc. It is implied there that they read only seven aliyos. Kesef Mishneh, Hilchos Tefillah 8:4, writes that specifically in the period of the Mishnah, when only the first oleh recited the opening blessing and only the last oleh recited the concluding blessing, while the intermediate olim recited no blessings, all were considered like one person and therefore they completed the reading. Nowadays, when every oleh recites a blessing before and after, perhaps only the reader who had already begun and blessed before some people left may finish his aliyah and bless afterward, but no additional olim should be called. Nevertheless, Kesef Mishneh concludes that since the requisite number of olim has not yet ascended, the mitzvah is not complete, and once one person began and blessed before some left, all the required olim must ascend. Since he remained uncertain, even the seven required olim should perhaps not ascend; yet because he concludes that all seven may ascend, being considered one unit, and the mitzvah is incomplete until all seven ascend, no more than seven may be called. Although on ordinary Shabbasos one may add aliyos, once some people have left one should not add. Since the halachah is that the maftir may count toward the seven, although the custom is to call seven besides maftir, that extra aliyah should be omitted. Six should be called, and maftir should be the seventh. The phrase “together with maftir” implies that maftir reads the Haftarah and recites its blessings as usual. Eliyah Rabbah disagrees, arguing that the Haftarah is a separate mitzvah and a separate enactment. Once seven have read from the Torah, that mitzvah is complete, and one should not begin the Haftarah blessings when ten are no longer present. The Haftarah should be read without blessings. According to that, Magen Avraham would agree with him.
