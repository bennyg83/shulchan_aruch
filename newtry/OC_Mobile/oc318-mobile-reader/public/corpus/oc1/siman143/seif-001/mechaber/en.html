דין אם נמצא ס"ת מוטעה ויתר דיני הספר. ובו ה סעיפים:
The Laws Regarding If A Torah Scroll Was Found To Have' an Error and Other Laws Regarding a' [Torah] Scroll. Containing 5 S'ifim:' We do not read from the Torah with less than ten adults who are free [i.e. non-slaves]. And' if they began with ten and some of them left, they [continue reading until they] finish.
