That which he said 'ten adults' is to exclude minors, for although a minor completes the seven readers, nonetheless he does not complete the count of ten; and that which he said 'free men' is to exclude complete slaves. And regarding one who is half slave and half free, there is doubt; and it appears that since we hold that they compel his master and make him a free man, he is obligated in all the mitzvos, for 'whatever is fit for mixing,' etc.; and so it is proven at the beginning of Chagigah; see there. Olas Tamid, ot 1. But the Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1, disagreed with the aforementioned words of the Olas Tamid, and wrote that one who is half slave does not combine; see there. And the same [is stated by] the Eshel Avraham, ot 1. And so wrote [the author] above in siman 55, ot 15. And see our words there, from ot 12 to ot 18, who combines and who does not combine; and the same applies here; see there.
