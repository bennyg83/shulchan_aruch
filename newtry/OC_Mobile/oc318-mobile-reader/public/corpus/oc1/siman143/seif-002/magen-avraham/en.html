One does not bless. Nevertheless they read without a blessing, so that the Torah of reading not be forgotten [Tosefta]; and it implies that the chazzan reads in a loud voice as on other occasions; and so is implied in Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba.

And the one called up reads. And nowadays the practice is that one reads from the chumash and the prayer leader reads from the Torah scroll; and one must say the reason is because there are people who do not know how to read with cantillation and tropes, and even if they prompt them they would be ashamed; therefore they prompt the prayer leader — Eshel Avraham siman 139.
