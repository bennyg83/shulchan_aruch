Seif 2. It may not be read from. The reason is the honor of the congregation. Mordechai writes that the congregation may waive its honor. Beit Yosef likewise infers this from the words of Rabbeinu Yerucham. See above, siman 53, Taz, seif katan 2, and Peri Chadash there.
Seif 4, in the gloss. One must distinguish. This is Rama's compromise. Agur holds that even in the case of a definite error another scroll is not taken out, while Beit Yosef holds that even for defective and plene spellings another scroll is taken out. Rama independently ruled this compromise as practical halachah. This also appears clearly from Darchei Moshe, Yoreh Deah, siman 279. See Responsa Minchas Yaakov, siman 18.
