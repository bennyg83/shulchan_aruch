(1) If an error is found in a Torah scroll, etc. — see Mishna Berurah where we evaluated the three views: the view of Shulchan Aruch, which is the view of Mahari Bei Rav; the view of Mordechai; and Rama's ruling between them; see Magen Avraham who sides to rule like Bach who adopted Mordechai's view [for it appears to me the main law is that when they take out another Torah scroll he must bless on it, and when he finishes reading there there is concern of an unnecessary blessing, for perhaps the law is they fulfill reading even in an invalid Torah scroll, and therefore the first blessing was proper and now it is an unnecessary blessing when he blesses again on those same verses; but his view is also very difficult, for Mordechai himself holds for the law they fulfill in an invalid Torah scroll as is evident in his words — it is only because written matters may not be said orally, not because it is invalid, and therefore he permits finishing reading there; but we who are stringent not to read l'chatchila even where we have no valid Torah scroll, because most poskim hold they do not fulfill reading thereby — how can we permit l'chatchila to bless the final blessing, which for them is certainly a blessing in vain due to causing an unnecessary blessing afterward; I found this difficulty already raised by the gaon Maharaz Margaliot in his Pitchei Shearim]; behold the Gra's view in his commentary is also like Mordechai, that when he takes another Torah scroll he must bless on it; proof for this from that of chapter Ba Lo brought in Mordechai, and on this he wrote that on this proof there is no response; but on the main law of Mordechai who holds he finishes reading in the invalid book and blesses afterward — he certainly does not hold like him, for he holds the main law is like the poskim that they do not fulfill reading even b'dieved in an invalid Torah scroll and must return to the head in a valid Torah scroll, and therefore when he blesses there there is no unnecessary blessing, for the first blessing he blessed earlier is as one who did not bless, as Raosh wrote in his teshuvah; and behold though in early generations the custom in several places was to return to the head, as Tashbetz wrote and as the Gra's view, nevertheless now per how Shulchan Aruch books spread, it appears from later authorities that in all places the custom spread as Mahari Bei Rav taught, not to return to the head of the order, for b'dieved what they already read we rely on some lenient poskim; but if he is still in the middle of reading and an error is found, there are divided opinions and divided customs — some places spread custom only as Shulchan Aruch and Rama's ruling, as Shakh wrote Yoreh Deah siman 279; some places spread custom as Mordechai, as brought in Magen Avraham; and in truth where custom is established it is very hard to change custom, for each has whom to rely on, and we may read for them "and your people are all righteous," as Shaarei Ephraim wrote; but where no fixed custom is established, it appears from Magen Avraham one should conduct as Mordechai, and Chayei Adam and Darkhei Chochmah copied [Chayei Adam's words are very difficult, for he wrote in siman 133: if error found in Torah scroll later authorities ruled (see Magen Avraham siman 143 and Gra's commentary) where no fixed custom, if error in middle of reading even if not yet read three verses, read error orally and complete three times and bless at end, etc. see there — he copied all Magen Avraham's words; what he cited Gra's commentary implies therefore he adopted this view because Magen Avraham and Gra hold thus; in truth Gra does not hold thus to bless afterward, for it is blessing in vain per him; what he wrote that on Mordechai's proof there is no response is only that he must bless the first blessing when taking out Torah scroll, as I wrote above]; however many other later authorities all adopted for law as Shulchan Aruch ruled and Rama's ruling — Levush Yoreh Deah 279, Shakh there [Taz who holds main law like Shulchan Aruch], Avodat HaGershuni in name of several poskim, Nehar Shalom, R' Yehuda Ayash, Sefer Magen Giborim; therefore it is very correct to conduct l'chatchila per Shulchan Aruch and Rama where no fixed custom to the contrary; see Nehar Shalom who extended this; see Shaarei Ephraim who extended in Pitchei Shearim, and his view also not like Magen Avraham; he further wrote if they practiced that if a complete error is found in middle of reading not to read further and not to bless afterward in invalid scroll even if already read three times, but to roll this one without blessing afterward and take out another and bless blessing before it — they have what to rely on [and in truth this is view of most rishonim and Gra's view, except per them must return from head of order]; nevertheless it appears one need not begin from head of order, only begin from head of reading they read with this aliyah where error found; since otherwise he returns and blesses, good that he also read these verses in valid scroll with blessing, etc.; nevertheless not enough to re-read only those verses read in invalid, rather must add and read below them at least three verses — end of his words.
