An error. See Responsa Rashbash, siman 11, and Responsa Beit Yosef, siman 54. See Baer Heitev, seif katan 5, who cites Taz in Yoreh Deah, siman 276, that if the error is found in the maftir reading, the erroneous word is read orally. Meaning, this ruling is correct on ordinary Shabbosos of the year, but not on Shabbos Rosh Chodesh or Yom Tov, because then the maftir reading is part of the day's obligation. See Shav Yaakov, siman 14. See also Responsa Zera Avraham, siman 6, and what I wrote in siman 622, seif katan 1.
One should not take out another scroll. During the kohen's reading at Shabbos Minchah, two letters were found joined together in the Sefer Torah. Responsa Devar Moshe, siman 2, ruled that another scroll should be taken out. Rashbetz likewise writes that there is no distinction at all between Minchah and Shacharis. See Responsa Rosh, klal 3. If, however, the error was discovered before the reading and another scroll was taken out, there is no need to repeat the blessing. Shvus Yaakov, part 1, siman 18. An incident occurred in which the words yeradnah befarech were found written in a Sefer Torah in place of yeradnu. The grandfather of the gaon Eliyah Rabbah, of blessed memory, ruled that another scroll should be taken out. See Eliyah Rabbah, who challenges Magen Avraham regarding this matter. See there.
