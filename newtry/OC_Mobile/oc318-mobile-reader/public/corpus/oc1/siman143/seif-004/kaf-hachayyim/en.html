4) There: And if they began with ten and some of them left, etc. And concerning those who leave it is said, 'and those who forsake Hashem shall be consumed.' Bach in the name of the Yerushalmi; Perishah, ot 1; and so it is [written] in the Rama above in siman 55, seif 2. And even between one man [oleh] and another, Perishah there. And that which is stated at the beginning of siman 146, that it is permitted to leave between one man and another, is when ten remain. And so wrote the Eshel Avraham, ot 1.
