5) There: And some of them left, they complete [the reading]. But they do not say Kaddish after the reading of the Sefer Torah, just as the Pri Chadash concurred above in siman 55 that one does not say the Kaddish 'Tiskabbel'; and further, since this custom to say Kaddish on Monday and Thursday after the reading of the Torah is a custom of the later authorities, and now that some of the ten have left, one should not say it. Birkei Yosef, ot 1; and not like the Sha'ar Ephraim, sha'ar 7, ot 38. And see our words above in siman 55, ot 34 and ot 35, and examine it well.
