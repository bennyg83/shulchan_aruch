Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. According to Mordechai, “if he has not yet,” means even if he has not yet read three verses. In that case he must read the erroneous word orally and continue reading from the invalid scroll until three verses have been completed. Even so, another scroll is not taken out, because doing so would create a doubt whether an initial blessing should be recited over it. All the more so, if three verses have already been read and it is possible to stop, for in that case Rama too rules that the concluding blessing is recited. This is clear.
