Shulchan Aruch, seif 3. One does not recite blessings. Rama, in the following seif, cites two opinions on this matter and rules between them. See there. It is somewhat surprising that this gloss was not written in the present seif, which discusses initially taking out such a scroll and reading from it, rather than in the following seif, which discusses a case where it was taken out and an error was afterward discovered in it.
