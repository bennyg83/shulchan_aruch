Seif katan 3: And the oleh, etc. The reason must be explained. Strictly speaking the oleh himself should read, but because some are unlearned the chazzan reads. Since someone must prompt the chazzan, it would seem preferable to prompt the oleh and have the oleh read after him from the Sefer Torah, as stated in Shulchan Aruch.
Magen Avraham therefore writes that the reason must be because there are, etc.
And they would be embarrassed, etc. This is not precise. Tur and Beit Yosef, siman 141, cite Rosh, who initially wished to explain our custom of having the chazzan read because of unlearned people, so as not to embarrass them, like the Mishnah concerning bikkurim. Initially, one who knew how to read the passage of bikkurim read it himself, while one who did not know was prompted. Because of embarrassment, people refrained from bringing bikkurim, so they instituted that everyone be prompted.
Rosh writes that the cases are not comparable. Regarding bikkurim, embarrassment caused people to refrain from bringing them and thereby transgress the Torah. Here, those who are proficient may read and the others may refrain; embarrassment will motivate them to study the parashah. Rather, the reason is that not everyone is proficient in the cantillation, so the congregation would not fulfill its obligation through his reading. Yet in his own eyes he considers himself proficient, and if he is not called he may quarrel. This is the correct explanation here.
