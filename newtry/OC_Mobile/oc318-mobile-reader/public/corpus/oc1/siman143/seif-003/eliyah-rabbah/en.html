[3] Even when rolled like a Sefer Torah, etc. It appears that in pressing circumstances this is permitted, since the prohibition is only because of the honor of the congregation. Even according to Beit Yosef, who implies from Raavyah that it is forbidden even if the congregation waives its honor, so that they will be distressed and purchase a Sefer Torah, this applies only to reading regularly in this manner. If it occurred occasionally and they have no other Sefer Torah, it is permitted. Moreover, most poskim disagree with Raavyah. It further appears to me that Raavyah discusses our ordinary Chumashim. With one Chumash prepared in the manner of a Sefer Torah, this rationale does not apply, for they will necessarily purchase more, since they possess only one Chumash. This requires study.
