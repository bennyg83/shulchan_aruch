Seif 3: Even, etc. This refers back to seif 2, concerning chumashim written as scrolls, unlike Mordechai, who wrote that since the prohibition was stated because of the honor of the congregation, if they waive their honor it is permitted, and all the more so in such a case. The Yerushalmi, however, in chapter 3 of Megillah states that because their spirits would be distressed, they would purchase a Torah. Beit Yosef appears to understand that this refers to our printed chumashim, as he writes in the name of Responsa Rashba; see there. Yet at the end of that responsum Rashba writes, etc. The Shulchan Aruch, however, does not mention our chumashim. It must therefore refer to an actual Sefer Torah upon which one may not recite the blessings. See seif 4 in the gloss, concerning a pressing circumstance, etc.
