His intent is from Rashi's and Tosafot's explanation — proof that we follow the majority of passersby, from that they forbade in eating; therefore Tosafot there had to explain at the end of "that we follow" that if it belonged to a Jew one could attribute it to after Pesach — but it is better to explain that it deals immediately after Pesach and it cannot be attributed to having occurred after Pesach, and then automatically it is proven we follow the majority of passersby from that they permitted in benefit. But certainly regarding benefit, even if we do not follow the majority of passersby, nevertheless it is permitted in benefit — see Maharsha there.
