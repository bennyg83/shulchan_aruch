1) [Se'if 1] A shop belonging to a Jew and merchandise belonging to a Jew, etc. This is the version of Rashi and Rav Hai. Rabbeinu Chananel, however, has the reverse version. In summary, according to Rashi and Rav Hai, the law follows the shopkeeper. If the shopkeeper is Jewish, chametz found there after Pesach is prohibited for benefit; if the shopkeeper is a non-Jew, it is permitted. According to Rabbeinu Chananel, the law follows the workers. If the workers who enter there are Jewish, chametz found there after Pesach is prohibited for benefit; if they are non-Jews, it is permitted for benefit. This is explicit in the words of Rashi and Rabbeinu Chananel. See there.
