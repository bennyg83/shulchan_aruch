(1) [Seif 1] A Jew's shop and inventory of a Jew, etc. This is the text of Rashi and R' Hai, but Rabbenu Chananel reads the opposite. The rule: for Rashi and R' Hai we follow the shop owner — if the shop owner is a Jew, chametz found there after Pesach is forbidden in benefit; if the shop owner is a non-Jew, it is permitted. For Rabbenu Chananel we follow the workers — if the workers who enter there are Jews, chametz found there after Pesach is forbidden in benefit; if they are non-Jews, it is permitted in benefit, as explained explicitly in the words of Rashi and Rabbenu Chananel — see there.
