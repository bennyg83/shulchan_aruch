This is Rashi's text. In shop and inventory we attribute; and this inventory means bread and wine sold in the shop; therefore it works in a non-Jew's shop, for in any case the workers also bring bread there, and it is a doubt; one can say since we hold like R' Shimon that chametz after Pesach is forbidden d'rabbanan as a penalty, here the penalty does not apply except in a Jew's shop, for we go stringently and need a reason why; and it appears the shop's chametz is certainly fixed and placed there, while the workers' is occasional — one can say what they bring there they eat entirely; therefore we follow the shop. Rabbenu Chananel reads the opposite and follows the workers; it is certainly difficult why we attribute more to workers than the shop, since in the shop the chametz is more fixed; and if for Rabbenu Chananel it deals when there is no bread in the shop only other merchandise, then there is no difference between Rashi and Rabbenu Chananel; it is possible this difference exists between them: for Rabbenu Chananel it deals when there is only merchandise not bread, and if there were bread in the shop we would say it is a doubt; and in every case we follow leniency, for there is no penalty here.
