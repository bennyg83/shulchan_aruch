(א) and merchandise. Meaning: merchandise.

(ב) Forbidden in benefit. For we attribute it to the shop owner; and for Rabbenu Chananel we attribute to the workers. It appears from Bach's words that chametz found after Pesach and we do not know if it belongs to a non-Jew or Jew — forbidden in eating and permitted in benefit — see there; and so in Eruvin daf 64b in Rashi and Tosafot: if we did not follow the majority of passersby and we would attribute to a Jew, nevertheless it would be permitted in benefit; and it is proven from there that we follow the majority, meaning the majority of the city as explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 1; and if it is in a matter where one can attribute that it occurred after Pesach, even where the majority are Jews it is permitted (Tosafot there; and see chapter 1 of Chullin).
