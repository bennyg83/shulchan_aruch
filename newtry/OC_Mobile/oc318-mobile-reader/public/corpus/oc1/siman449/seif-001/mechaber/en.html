The law of chametz found in a Jew's shop where the workers are non-Jews, or the reverse. It contains 1 seif: If the shop belongs to a Jew and its stock, meaning the merchandise and shop utensils, belongs to a Jew, while the workers who enter it are non-Jews, chametz found there after Pesach is forbidden for benefit. If the shop belongs to a non-Jew and its stock belongs to a non-Jew, while the workers who enter it are Jews, chametz found there is permitted even for eating. This is the textual reading of Rashi and Rabbeinu Hai; Rabbeinu Chananel has the reverse reading.
