The law of chametz found in a Jew's shop where the workers are non-Jews, or vice versa. Contains 1 seif. A Jew's shop and its inventory (meaning the merchandise and shop utensils) belonging to a Jew, and the workers who enter there are non-Jews — chametz found there after Pesach is forbidden in benefit. A non-Jew's shop and inventory of a non-Jew, and workers who enter there are Jews —
chametz found there is permitted even for eating.
