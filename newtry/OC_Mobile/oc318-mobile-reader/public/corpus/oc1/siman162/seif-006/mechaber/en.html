If one washed each hand separately and then changed their mind, when receiving the second waters they joined their hands and poured over both at once, the hands are impure. When the hands were joined to receive the second waters, they defiled each other, since the water on one hand defiles the water on the other and the hand itself. When the second hand was washed, it did not purify the first waters (since they were defiled by the other hand); on the contrary, the second waters were also defiled. One must dry the hands and wash both together. {Rama: Gloss: All the more so, one must be careful not to let a hand that received water once touch the hand that received water twice. [Mordechai, ch. Elu Devarim]}
