(6) [Levush] And it is possible the reason, etc. Because Beit Yosef wrote on Or Zaru'a and does not see a reason in this, therefore Levush strained to find a reason. Olat Tamid wrote the reason: since third water comes to purify the water on the hands, we say as if second water went beyond the joint, so third water goes beyond the joint and purifies the second water — end of his words. This is astounding, for water does not purify water that went beyond the joint as explained in seif 1; also in Damasek Eliezer page 336 he strained to find a reason and it is astounding — see there. The apparent reason for Or Zaru'a in my humble opinion follows Rashi's method, for Rashi holds second water purifies even what is beyond the measure, but we concern that with second water the water might not go beyond the measure as explained in Beit Yosef; therefore Or Zaru'a holds if he pours a third time we say presumably it went beyond the measure either in second or third — thus I found in Derishah. Per this, for us who hold water beyond the joint does not purify, it seems raising is required.
