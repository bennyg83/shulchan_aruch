(א) Pouring — Taz per Rashi: for one who holds until forearm there is no impure water; word therefore in Tur and Shulchan Aruch does not settle; Yesh Seder like Ra'ash did not write therefore in chapter 25 — see there; Magen Avraham letter 8 — see there.

(ב) The writer said: I have reflections here and set them forth there's help on the Mechaber's words in seifim 4, 5, and 6 — taken from mishnah chapter 2 of Yadayim 43, reversed order to be not only this but also that — Tosafot there s.v. he took; the Mechaber explains beginning and end regarding taking second water too: beginning — he took both hands one at a time and joined for second impure — this is seif 6; end — he took and rubbed its fellow and also gave second on both hands, nevertheless impure; per Ramban siman 159 seif 6 we learn specifically he rubbed its fellow — if poured from one onto its fellow and they did not touch, pure, since from vessel — see there; Mechaber reversed to be not only this but also that; began: netilah did not avail because they became impure — infer netilah did not avail, not like Ramban 159 first view in stam, and should have said only need to dry; Mechaber: even if poured second on both hands — Bach likewise poured second also on one hand then rubbed — hands impure; he states a greater novelty: even if rubbed its fellow then second on both hands impure — see there; certainly a novelty — gemara teaches second water does not purify first — impurity does not purify impurity from another source. Know Yesh Seder chapter 25 siman 33: I understood from his words there is a difference if he gave first on one hand and touched the second — water has two impurity levels from hand one and hand two; second moment then many kavin do not help, for drawn water purifies only one impurity not two (immersion in mikveh helps — obvious); if he gave first and second on one hand and touched its fellow — only one impurity for water even from second moment helps to pour more to purify. He wrote further per his view: if he poured first water on each hand and in second joined them — permitted, for hands already purified in first — impure water can be purified by second, for no added impurity on water, only unclean waters mixed; specifically where impurity was added on water from first moment touching second — this is his intent; he explains mishnah there per Rambam — see Ra'avad; Tosafot there s.v. he took; we hold seif 6 like Ra'ash and Ra'avad even if took one at a time and joined for second impure — unlike Yesh Seder second division; nevertheless one can say added impurity applies; first division: whatever not added is only one impurity even from another moment — drawn water can purify later; from water impure and pure until joint proves Mechaber seif 6 — need not extend. I say: if he took first and second on one hand then touched the second — must dry first hand while impurity on water from its fellow — a thousand kavin do not help even without added impurity; second hand need only dry — water in second was pure, only impurified in second moment; therefore second has impure water on itself — pour first and second and they purify. See Magen Avraham; see letter 7 seif 8 regarding rubbing on head — Magen Avraham 10.
