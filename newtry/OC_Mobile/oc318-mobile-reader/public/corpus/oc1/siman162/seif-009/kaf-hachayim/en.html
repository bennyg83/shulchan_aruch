(9) There the gloss: some disagree — see what I wrote above letter 4 and Acharonim letter 4 — it seems there is essential disagreement; also what I wrote in letter 1 in Arizal's name: raising helps so externals not cling — see there. Therefore one should raise even if he washed whole hand until forearm joint; raising means he should be careful water not return from forearm and impurify hands from netilah until drying — after second water; per Arizal who requires three after third water, even if they return he pours again to purify; at blessing raise toward head at least toward face; after blessing immediately lower from opposite head, only slightly raised so water not return from forearm to hands, and dry as letters 2 and 3. However b'dieved if he did not raise he need not wash again, since it is only a general concern lest they return; also we practice until forearm joint and per Maran need not raise; also we practice three pourings and some say if three pourings need not raise — from all these reasons b'dieved if he did not raise he need not wash again.
