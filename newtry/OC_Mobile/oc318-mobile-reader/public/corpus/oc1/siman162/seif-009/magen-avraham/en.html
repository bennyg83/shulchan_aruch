But in siman 160, seif 10, it implies that the halacha follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. Beit Yosef. And in Yoreh Deah, siman 201, seif 52, he ruled that although one may immerse in it, nevertheless it reduces the measure with respect to the tube of the waterskin. The Tosafot Yom Tov disagreed with him and wrote that according to the Rosh and the Tur, one may neither immerse in it nor wash with it, and it reduces the measure; and it is also impure if found on the hand. See there, sixth chapter of Mikvaos. To resolve the opinion of the Rav Beit Yosef, one must say that the ruling that one may immerse in small water creatures applies specifically when he crushed them, for they are no better than snow. Even according to the one who disagrees regarding snow, he agrees here that if he did not crush them, it is not called immersion at all. Therefore, if he did not crush them, they reduce the measure, and it is possible that they also render the hand impure. The same applies to snow and hail when they were not crushed and are found on the hand: it is impure. So one should rule.
