He inclines to this ruling based upon Taz at the end of siman 159, who permits initially immersing one's hands in such water even if the hail was not crushed. We have established above that every doubt concerning hand washing is ruled pure. In particular, in pressing circumstances this is the practical ruling in siman 160, se'if 12; see Mishnah Berurah there. Vilna Gaon implies that even initially one may immerse his hands in it, like Taz. His view in Yoreh Deah, siman 201, tends this way even regarding a woman's immersion, and likewise above in siman 160; see there. Especially according to Rambam, who explains the concern as an intervening substance, Rabbeinu Shimshon and the Rosh's rationale does not apply at all. Therefore, if such a substance is later found on his hand, the washing should not be invalidated by treating it as another substance. Nevertheless, initially, if he has water mixed with hail, it is better to pour a revi'is over each hand. There is then no concern at all, as the Mechaber writes.
