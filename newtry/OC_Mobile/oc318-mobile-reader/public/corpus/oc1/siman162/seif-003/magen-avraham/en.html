Moist enough to make another surface moist. This is specifically after the fact, but initially it is forbidden, as stated above. With this, the matter is resolved: why, in the question of Ilfa, does he not bring the Mishnah that one does not add? Rather, the Mishnah is speaking of the initial law, where it is forbidden even when it is moist, whereas Rabbi Yannai is speaking after the fact, and the question of Ilfa is also after the fact. The Bach wrote that from the Tur and the Rosh it implies that a moist liquid does not help, and one should be stringent. Therefore, one should not wash from a vessel whose mouth is narrow, where he cannot pour over the hand all at once.
With the second waters he washes. Meaning, even though part of the hand dried somewhat, he may wash its second part; see seif 8.
