(7) Bach wrote there one must raise hands at first so fingertips too are washed — for when he lowers fingers downward water comes only on finger length from sides around, not on fingertips facing down; therefore raise fingertips first so water pours on their tops too — thus 18 netilot — see there. Shelah likewise in gloss: I saw many minim pour on hand from side and fingertips remain without netilah — as if he did not wash; must raise fingertips — bend hand so fingertips are above palm, not raise whole hand upward, only fingers above palm — see there. It seems even per the above, if one holds hands thus water comes on whole hand; we learn one must pay attention at netilah and turn hands so water comes on whole hand and all sides — understand well.
