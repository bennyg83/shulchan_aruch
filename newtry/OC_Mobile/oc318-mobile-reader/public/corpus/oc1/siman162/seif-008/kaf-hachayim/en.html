(8) Shelah wrote there in gloss: I received after he washed and raised before blessing to say the verse raise your hands in holiness and bless Heaven — this is not called an interruption — end of his words. Acharonim brought letter 1 seif 12 letter 1; however in Arizal's words this verse is not mentioned. Chida in siman 166 letter 1: some concern not to say raise your hands in holiness before al netilat yadayim lest interrupt between netilah and blessing. Ruach Chaim: per Kabbalah one should not say raise your hands, etc., and verse I lift my palms to Your commandments, etc., only before washing. Likewise in his book Kaf HaChayim siman 22 letter 1; see above siman 4 end letter 16 and below siman 166 letter 4.
