He took one hand, etc. — to understand, precede Beit Yosef who wrote: where he took with revi'it and rubbed on head or wall and returned and touched — seems pure per those who hold pouring revi'it as one need not second water to purify — for then not impure by addition but when took one hand with less than revi'it from remainder of purity; but when took full revi'it hands and water on them purified completely — if rubbed head or wall then touched, pure — end; these words are unclear, for since from remainder of purity even two hands too; why did Shulchan Aruch not mention here from remainder of purity; therefore clear there is a textual error — must say: when took one or even two hands with less than revi'it from remainder of purity; explanation: one who has revi'it water and pours on both as one — no impurity at all; deals with pouring on each hand separately — therefore impurity from first water and purity from second; likewise pouring both as one — still not completely pure — deals with less than revi'it from remainder of purity. He said: even though there is purity from second after he rubs — meaning he dries them, for without drying no purity even if poured twice as Rosh Beit Yosef brought above; perhaps rubbing always required; here touched after rubbing — see there.
