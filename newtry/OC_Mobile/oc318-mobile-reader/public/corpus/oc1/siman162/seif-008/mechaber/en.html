If one washed one hand and rubbed it on their head or on a wall, and then touched the water that had come from the hand onto the head or wall — that hand becomes impure, because those impure waters returned and defiled it. Even though rubbing would have purified them before that, once one touched them again it is worse. It has already been explained that one who pours a revi'it at once is exempt from all this and there are no impure waters at all. {Rama: (to dry it — which is why the hand does not become defiled, unlike the case in §164:2 where one scratched their head)}
