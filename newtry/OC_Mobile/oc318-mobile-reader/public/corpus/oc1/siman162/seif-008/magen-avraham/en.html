On his head. Meaning, on the hat upon his head; see siman 104.
It is impure. This is in a case where he washed only with the first waters and not with the second waters. Rash.
They become pure through rubbing. For rubbing purifies like second waters, and second waters are not needed. Beit Yosef, siman 165, in the name of Mahari Abuhav, and so wrote Lechem Chamudos. Nevertheless, even if he washed with second waters, it is possible that he must dry them. Rash; see the end of siman 158. The Lechem Chamudos wrote that if he wiped with a cloth in the manner of those who wipe, even if he then touched again, it is pure. One must say: from where did he know this? Therefore, one should not rely on this.
