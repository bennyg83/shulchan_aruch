Therefore one who washes, etc. Text is missing here. It should read: 'Therefore one who pours from a vessel onto one hand and rubs it with the other must dry his hands and wash them again properly. Therefore one who washes, etc.' So it appears in the Tur and Shulchan Aruch. Malbushei Yom Tov writes likewise, and explains that the plural 'his hands' is used because even the first hand becomes tamei, just as in the case where one hand touched the other.
