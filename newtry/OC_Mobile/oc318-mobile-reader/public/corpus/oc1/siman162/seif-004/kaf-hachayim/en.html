(4) There one must raise his hands, etc. Some explain because hands are not impurified by liquids except until the joint, but liquids that touched beyond the joint are pure; likewise second water does not purify first except while within the joint; if first water within the joint went beyond the joint and then second water was poured, second water purifies that water beyond the joint; if they returned to the hand they impurify it; therefore one must raise so first water that went beyond does not return and impurify the hands — per Ra'ash and Tur as explained in Tur and Beit Yosef. Rashba wrote in a responsum: one who washes until the upper joint — end of palm connected to forearm — and returns and gives second until that joint need not raise, for why concern — second is given where first was given; for what went above that joint on the forearm shaft there is no concern, for they decreed only on hands. That which in first chapter of Sotah he took only until splitting of fingers and therefore must raise — see there; Beit Yosef brought it and wrote thus is Agur's view in name of R. Eliezer Cohen. This is what Shulchan Aruch wrote: if he washes until hand joint he need not raise — Rashba and Agur. Rama glossed: some disagree — Ra'ash and Tur hold even if until hand joint he must raise — see Magen Avraham and Peri Megadim seif 1.
