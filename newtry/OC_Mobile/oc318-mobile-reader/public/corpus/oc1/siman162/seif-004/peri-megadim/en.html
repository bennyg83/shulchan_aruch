He must rub them. See Taz. The purpose of rubbing is apparently to rub one hand against the other, lest the poured water have failed to reach every part of the hand. The rubbing must be performed while the hands are still wet from the first water, so that when they meet, each hand’s water reaches every portion of the other. This also explains the ruling concerning one who immerses his hands. The matter is straightforward and concise.
