(א) Likewise if they lower, etc. — thereby Beit Yosef's question is resolved: why must he raise hands — there is no concern even if lowering from start to end; answer: only then, but warning was not to raise first then lower; Beit Yosef answered in Mahari Avraham's name: if he lowers, concern water touching palm after splitting of fingers becomes impure and water leaves palm and impurifies fingers — therefore not to lower hands downward; this is specifically for one who holds measure of netilah until completion of fingers; Beit Yosef in this siman in Rashba's responsum — I did not understand: even per this view no concern, for hands are impurified only until joint as mishnah Beit Yosef brought; now we hold this until joint means completion of fingers; must say for hand impurity all agree until joint means until forearm joint; Rama's words are Beit Yosef's to explain gemara must raise — not that raising is required, but he caused need to raise — must not lower until drying; Beit Yosef wondered on this explanation — no source writes thus; it seems Rav Yonah too intended on what Beit Yosef wrote in his name — disciples of Mahari Abohav: at netilah beware not to raise hands upward until drying, etc.; Beit Yosef elaborated on this explanation — see there at length.

(ב) Hand and forearm joint. Since the whole hand is presumed impure even though netilah is required only until after splitting of fingers; some disagree — Arukh and disciples of Mahari Abohav hold everywhere they receive impurity; reason seems since water returns afterward into place of netilah — thus I saw Rashal chapter 25.
