(א) The Geonim here — a great difficulty; therefore I needed to extend somewhat. Ra'ash and his followers hold hands are impurified by liquids only until the joint; therefore if second water went beyond the joint it is nothing; if first water went beyond the joint it impurifies the hand, and even if he pours second water on them it does not purify there, for their purification is only until the joint; therefore he must raise his hands so first water that went beyond does not return and impurify the hands — even if he washes until the forearm the law is so. One who immerses his hands or takes revi'it — there is no impure water at all and he need not raise; likewise if he wished to lower from start to end — end of his words. Rashba holds specifically when he took only until splitting of fingers we concern lest first water was taken beyond the joint and impurified there on the palm, and second he took only until splitting, or he poured second more than first and they return and impurify — therefore must raise; but when he takes until forearm need not raise, for no concern for what went on forearm, for forearm is not place of impurity. Arukh holds forearm too is place of impurity; per them forbidden to lower start to end when taking until splitting — water descended from palm and impurified fingers, for impossible to limit precisely within joint. One who immerses or takes revi'it must also raise — see all this in Beit Yosef and Levush. If so, since Rabbi Yosef wrote here — when he does not take whole hand, etc. — this is Rashba's view; how he wrote afterward and likewise if he poured, etc., one who immerses need not, etc. — this is Ra'ash's view; requires study — Rabbi Yosef retracted and holds immersion and revi'it do not receive water impurity at all even outside place of netilah; this is reasonable, for poskim say so in Tosafot's name, and certainly not per Rashba and Arukh on Tosafot — see there seif 7. Rama's words require study: he wrote likewise if he lowers, etc., for per Rashba lowering does not help; if his intent is to disagree with Rabbi Yosef he should not write likewise but some say, etc.; however we find many like him; perhaps this gloss should be after and some disagree — Ra'ash's view. Fingertips: Rashi — Beit Yosef explains his view is one need only wash fingers; Rama cannot explain thus, for he did not write this in siman 161; it seems his intent is not to raise hands entirely lest water go to forearm under clothes and not be dried and at meal return and impurify — as Rabbi Yosef explained per Mahari Abohav — see there; therefore he raises only fingertips. Bach explains Rashi's intent: forbidden to lower start to end because water descends on finger sides and fingertips are not washed; therefore raise fingertips and pour on them; likewise Shelah and Yesh Seder chapter 25 siman 35 and Rekanati Parshat Ekev per Kabbalah; also in Damasek Eliezer itself he wrote preferable to raise to fulfill the verse and take and lift them — see Sha'arei Giborim Parshat Elu Devarim; there is hesitation there.

(ב) Three times — unnecessary. Rabbi Yosef wrote this and I do not know the reason; Levush wrote a reason from himself — Maharil in responsa Ancona edition siman 215 and Drush siman 187, and regarding netilah and rubbing salted meat we are not concerned for remainder of first water; therefore Or Zaru'a wrote one need not concern to raise hands when taking three times — end of his words: when taking three times first water already left the hand and nothing remains; nevertheless requires study, for first impurify second and second third by touching each other; perhaps water on the hand itself does not impurify each other — see Pesachim chapter Kol Sha'ah; or he holds like Rashbi that second purifies first but we concern they did not go there, but when pouring third time we do not concern as above; but per Ra'ash three times does not help — see Be'er Heitev chapter 25.
