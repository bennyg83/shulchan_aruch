Raising and rubbing the hands during hand washing. Containing 10 paragraphs: One who washes their hands must raise them upward so that the
water does not flow past the wrist joint and return to defile the hands. ((meaning the fingertips) Gloss: The same applies if one lowers them from the start of washing to the end — that is also valid — but one must be careful not to first raise the fingertips and then lower them, because then the water would flow past the wrist and return to defile the hands (Beit Yosef in the name of Maharai ibn Haviv). This applies when one does not wash the entire hand up to where the hand meets the forearm, but if one washes up to that point, there is no need to raise the hands (and some disagree). Similarly, if one poured a revi'it (a specific measure) over both hands at once, since there are no impure waters at all, there is no need to raise the hands. Similarly, one who immerses their hands need not raise them.
