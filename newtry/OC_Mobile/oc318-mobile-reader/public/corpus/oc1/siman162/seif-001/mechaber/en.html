Raising and rubbing the hands when washing. It contains 10 seifim: One who washes must raise his hands (that is, the tips of his fingers) upward so that water will not flow beyond the joint and return and render the hands impure. {Rama: And the same law applies if he keeps them lowered from the beginning of the washing until the end, which is also valid; he must only be careful not first to raise the fingertips and then lower them, because then the water would flow beyond the joint and return and render the hands impure. [Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari ibn Chaviv]} This applies when one does not wash the entire hand up to the place where the hand joins the forearm; but if he washes up to there, he need not raise his hands (and some disagree about this). Likewise, if he poured a revi'it over both hands at once, since there is no impure water at all, he need not raise his hands. Likewise, one who immerses his hands need not raise his hands. (And some say that if one pours over his hands 3 times, he need not be careful about all of this, and the custom is to be lenient. [Agur in the name of Or Zarua and Hagahot Ashiri, chapter 25])
