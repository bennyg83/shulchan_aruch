[A] To raise my head, etc. It was interpreted in the tefillah that the hand should be folded and the tips of the fingers should be above the palm and not that the whole hand should be raised up. Another Kabbalah text says that after raising the hand before the blessing, the verse says that your hands come first and bless the the Omnipresent. It is written in the Shulchan Aruch by Mahari Luria that the hands should be raised before wiping against the face.
