Magen Avraham note 18 — when he is not fastidious about it. I ask: here in every minority he is not fastidious we nevertheless require water reach it and it does not interpose between flesh and water; here he need not wash on moisture since flesh does not touch food there — if so even if fastidious it should not harm; how does interposition law apply if he does not wash there? Main difficulty: this here and that in siman 161 are one — if it does not interpose he need not beware not to touch moisture, for since it does not interpose, water in all the moisture is like on his hand and second water purifies first — see Magen Avraham siman 161 seif 4; but since in truth moisture was a minority he is fastidious about and is not nullified to the hand, only he need not wash there — therefore must beware water not touch moisture. If so, here deals specifically when he is fastidious — that of siman 161 interposes regarding needing water not touch moisture — understand well.
