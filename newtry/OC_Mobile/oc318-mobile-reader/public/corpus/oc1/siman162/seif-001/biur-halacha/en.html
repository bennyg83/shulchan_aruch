The same applies if he lowers them, etc.; this is acceptable. See Mishnah Berurah. Even one who wishes to rely upon this method of lowering the hands should do so only when washing the entire hand up to the wrist. In that case, according to Rashba, he need not raise his hands in any event, as stated below in the text; this is Rashba's well-known view. Otherwise, it is proper to take account of Rashba, according to whom lowering does not help. This is implied by the later authorities.
Some say that if he pours over his hands three times, etc. Their reason is that they follow the second opinion cited in Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 1: the principal reason for raising the hands is the concern that the second water did not reach as far as the first water to purify it, and that impure water will subsequently return onto his fingers. When he pours three times, we are not as concerned, because if the second water did not reach the area, the third did. Magen Avraham's second answer. Eliyah Rabbah writes that according to this reasoning one should be stringent and raise his hands even when washing three times, since we do not rule according to that opinion, as explained above. Nevertheless, it appears that one who is lenient loses nothing, for he has authorities upon whom to rely.
