From what has been written, it is clear that the defilement of one hand by rubbing against the other applies only when one washes one hand first and then rubs it against the other. But if one intends from' the start to wash both hands together, both are considered as one hand and do not defile each other. Even four or five people who place their hands side by side or one atop the other are considered as one hand and do not defile each other.
