(5) There the gloss — meaning fingertips. So wrote Rashi; Beit Yosef wrote on this it implies his reason is he holds one need not wash for non-sacred food except until finger joints to palm, therefore need not raise except where netilah is required — see there. Therefore Rama brought here on Shulchan Aruch because here too it deals per those who hold one need not take whole hand until forearm joint as in siman 161; but per those who require until forearm joint, Shulchan Aruch wrote one must raise — see Magen Avraham and Maamar Mordechai seif 1. Magen Avraham wrote his intent is not to raise hands entirely lest water go to forearm under clothes and not be dried and at meal return and impurify — see there; this is uncommon; even if a little water goes under clothes it is absorbed; not common so much water goes under garment and returns; therefore no concern; one may raise whole hand entirely as plain Shulchan Aruch; although we practice until forearm joint as above siman 161, and per Maran if until forearm joint need not raise because water beyond joint is pure as previous letter — nevertheless per Arizal there is reason in raising hand toward head at blessing as above letter 2 — see there. Therefore one should raise whole hand and not concern water under garment will return and impurify.
