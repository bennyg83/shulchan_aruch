10) There: Likewise, if he poured a revi'is over both hands at once, etc. All the more so if he pours a revi'is over each hand at once. Taz, paragraph 3. The Beit Yosef, however, writes that according to the Aruch, who explains that one must raise his hands because water that passes beyond the hand renders the forearm impure, even one who immerses his hands must raise them. There remains a concern that water may pass beyond the joint and then return and render the hands impure. Maran, of blessed memory, did not take the Aruch's view into account here in the Shulchan Aruch because he had already written earlier in the Beit Yosef, regarding the Aruch's words, that the commentators do not agree; see there. For us, however, we already wrote above in paragraph 8 that according to the Arizal, of blessed memory, one must raise the hands in every circumstance, even if he poured a revi'is at once and even if he immersed them; see there.
