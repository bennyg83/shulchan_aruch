One who has a wound on their hand with a bandage it is sufficient to wash the rest of the hand that is not covered by the bandage, and one must take care not to touch the bandage, lest the water on it return and defile the hand. Alternatively, one may pour a full revi'it over the hand at once, in which case the waters are not impure. {Rama: (ungüento in the vernacular) [One pours the first waters whether over a vessel or over the ground. — Tur]}
