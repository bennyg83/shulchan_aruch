2) We will now explain the order of washing, rubbing, and raising the hands according to the Arizal, of blessed memory. First, one should take the vessel in his right hand and give it to his left. With his left hand he should pour three consecutive times over his entire right hand, meaning the fingers together with the palm up to the wrist. He should then take the vessel in his right hand and pour three consecutive times over his entire left hand in the same manner. Afterward, he should rub both hands together three times. He should then raise his hands opposite his head and join his two arms in such a way that the elbow joints do not protrude backward, corresponding to the verse, 'All their back parts faced inward.' He must also open his palms like one who wishes to receive something in them. This alludes to receiving purity. Only the tips of his fingers should be slightly raised upward. He should recite the blessing over netilas yadayim, then lower his hands from opposite his head and dry them. Although Shaar HaMitzvos there, s.v. the matter of netilas yadayim, etc., states that one should pour twice consecutively over each hand and then rub once, nevertheless, from what it writes afterward, s.v. the matter of washing and eating, etc., that when washing the right hand one should have kavanah for Havayah of numerical value 72, and for the left hand Havayah of numerical value 63, etc., it follows that one should wash and have kavanah as in the morning netilas yadayim. This means the simple, filled, and fully expanded forms of Havayah of numerical value 72 for the right hand; likewise, Havayah of numerical value 63 for the left hand; and for the rubbing, Havayah of numerical value 45. That is, during the first pouring on the right hand one should have kavanah for the fully expanded Havayah of numerical value 72; during the second pouring, for the filled Havayah of numerical value 72; and during the third, for the simple Havayah. Although the simple Name corresponds to Kesser and the filled Name to Chochmah, etc., as is known, the third pouring uses the simple Name rather than the first because the order of netilas yadayim is that the final pouring is greatest of all, as written below in paragraph 13. One can also explain that this follows the order in which the mochin are drawn, first in Binah, then in Chochmah, and then in Kesser, proceeding upward from below, as is known to those familiar with the hidden wisdom. Examine this carefully. The same order applies to the left hand, but with Havayah of numerical value 63, as written in Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaBerachos, chapter 3, and in Olas Tamid, Shaar HaTefillah; see there. The Rashash, of blessed memory, likewise arranged the kavanah for netilas yadayim before eating in the same manner as the morning netilas yadayim. The morning washing must alternate, once on this hand and once on the other, because of Shibta, as written in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 1d, which we cited above in siman 4, paragraph 12; see there. Netilas yadayim before eating, however, need not alternate, since there is no concern of Shibta. Rather, one pours three consecutive times over each hand. Regarding rubbing, although Pri Etz Chaim and Olas Tamid imply that once suffices, the Rashash, of blessed memory, arranged that one rub three times both for the morning washing and for washing before eating. He set forth their kavanos: during the first rubbing, one should intend the fully expanded Havayah of numerical value 45; during the second, the filled Havayah of numerical value 45; and during the third, the simple Havayah; see there. R' Shemen Sasson, of blessed memory, volume 4, on Shaar HaMitzvos, parashas Eikev, writes that the passage in Shaar HaMitzvos, parashas Eikev, from s.v. the matter of netilas yadayim through s.v. the matter of washing and eating, is not from the Rav, of blessed memory, but from Maharam Alshich, of blessed memory. The Rashash, of blessed memory, likewise arranged kavanos only for what the Rav, of blessed memory, wrote; see there. See Ben Ish Chai, parashas Shemini, paragraph 4, and examine carefully. See also what we wrote above in siman 158, paragraph 5, and siman 4, paragraph 16. The following is the order of the kavanos for washing and rubbing, both in the morning and before a meal.
2) Right hand, first pouring: Yod: Yod, Vav, Dalet. Hei: Hei, Yod. Vav: Vav, Yod, Vav. Hei: Hei, Yod.
2) Second pouring: Yod, Hei, Vav, Hei, the filled Havayah of numerical value 72.
2) Third pouring: Havayah in its simple form.
2) Left hand, first pouring: Yod: Yod, Vav, Dalet. Hei: Hei, Yod. Vav: Vav, Alef, Vav. Hei: Hei, Yod.
2) Second pouring: Yod, Hei, Vav with Alef, Hei, the filled Havayah of numerical value 63.
2) Third pouring: Havayah in its simple form.
2) First rubbing: Yod: Yod, Vav, Dalet. Hei: Hei, Alef. Vav: Vav, Alef, Vav. Hei: Hei, Alef.
2) Second rubbing: Yod, Hei with Alef, Vav with Alef, Hei with Alef, the filled Havayah of numerical value 45.
2) Third rubbing: Havayah in its simple form.
