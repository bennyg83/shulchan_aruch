One who washes his hands, etc. It is written in Toras HaBayis, bayis 3: if one poured over one hand one large pouring that contains enough for two pourings, this counts as two, and he does not need second waters. But if he poured over both hands one large pouring, it is not pure unless it contains a reviis, and otherwise it does not help even if it comes from leftover pure water. So too the Beit Yosef wrote in siman 160 in the name of Tosafot, the Rosh, the Semak, and the Rash, and so too Tosafot wrote in the second chapter of Gittin. In Toras HaBayis HaKatzar he omitted this, and in the second chapter he wrote like the opinion of the Raavad in the name of the Yerushalmi, that even with a reviis it requires two times; see there and in the Beit Yosef. See also Rambam, chapter 7, law 10.
Much water. Meaning, more than a reviis.
