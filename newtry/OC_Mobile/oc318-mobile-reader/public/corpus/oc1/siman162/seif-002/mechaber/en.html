One who washes his hands pours some of the revi'it over them to remove dirt and anything that intervenes, then pours a second time. These waters are also impure. He then pours a third time in order to purify the water remaining on the hands. If there is no dirt or intervening substance on his hands, he pours a full revi'it over both hands at once and does not need second waters. {Rama: The same applies if he has abundant water. He first washes with a small amount to remove the dirt, then pours a revi'it at once, and does not need a third pouring. One who washes his hands must rub them together. [Rambam, chapter 11 of Hilchot Mikvaot; Kol Bo; Semag; Rosh, chapter Kol HaBasar]}
