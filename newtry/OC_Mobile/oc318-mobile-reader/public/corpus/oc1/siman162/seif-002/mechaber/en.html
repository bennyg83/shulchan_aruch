One who washes their hands pours a little of the revi'it to remove dirt and anything that interposes, then pours a second time (these waters are also impure), then pours a third time to purify the waters on the hands. If there is no dirt or interposing substance, one pours a full revi'it over both hands at once and does not need a second pouring. Gloss: Similarly, if one has abundant water, one first rinses a little to remove dirt, then pours a revi'it all at once and does not need a third pouring. One who washes their hands must rub them together.[Maimonides, Laws of Mikvaot ch. 11; Kol Bo; Semag; Rosh, ch. Kol HaBasar]]:
