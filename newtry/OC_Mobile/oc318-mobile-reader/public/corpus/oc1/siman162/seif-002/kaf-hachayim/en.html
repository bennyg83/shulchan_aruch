(א) And now we explain the order of netilah, rubbing, and raising per the Arizal. First he takes the vessel in the right hand and gives it to the left and pours from the left onto the right three times consecutively on the whole hand — fingers with palm until the forearm joint. Then he takes the vessel in the right hand and pours onto the left three times consecutively on the whole hand as above. Then he rubs both hands together three times. Then he raises his hands toward his head and joins both forearms so the forearm joints do not go backward — in the secret and all their backs homeward. He must also spread his palms as one who wishes to receive something — hinting receiving purity; only fingertips slightly upright upward; he blesses al netilat yadayim, then lowers from opposite his head and dries. Although in Sha'ar HaMitzvot s.v. netilat yadayim he wrote one should enter with two pourings consecutively on each hand then rub once, nevertheless from what he wrote afterward s.v. order of netilah and eating — when taking right hand intend Havayah of 72 and left hand Havayah of 63, etc. — it implies one must take and intend like morning netilah, i.e. peshut, malei, malei of malei Havayah 72 in right hand; likewise Havayah 63 in left; likewise in rubbing Havayah 45; meaning first netilah of right hand — malei of malei Havayah 72; second netilah — malei Havayah 72; third — peshut Havayah 72 (reason third netilah is peshut: first is malei of malei, second malei, third peshut) — see there at length.
