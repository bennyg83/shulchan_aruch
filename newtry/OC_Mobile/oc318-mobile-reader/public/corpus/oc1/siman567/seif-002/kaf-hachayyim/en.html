Therefore, one who is prohibited by a vow from a certain food is forbidden even to taste it. Taz, s.k. 1; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur; Mishna Berurah, ibid. Even if he tastes and spits out any amount, it is forbidden, for he derives benefit. One who is prohibited by a vow from deriving benefit from a cooked dish is forbidden to taste it even in order to know whether it requires salt or seasoning. Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1.
