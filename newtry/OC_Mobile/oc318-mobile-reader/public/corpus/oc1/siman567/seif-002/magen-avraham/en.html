A little at a time. So wrote the Beis Yosef in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos and the Rokeach, and this is their wording: this tasting is on the palate, and to spit it out immediately so that it not pass beyond the palate; and one should not taste a revi'is on the palate at one time, but rather a little at a time and spit it out. And I found written in an old sefer in the name of Ritz Geiat that over the entire day it combines toward a revi'is, and more than that is forbidden, end quote; and see siman 612.
And there are those who say that even etc. And it is permitted to taste several times, each time a revi'is (Rashba, siman 267).
