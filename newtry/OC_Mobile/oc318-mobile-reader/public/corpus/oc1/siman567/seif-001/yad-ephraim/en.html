In Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. "And it is possible that even if his intent is for his own pleasure it is permitted": it appears to my humble opinion that these words belong at the beginning of this s.k., and thus it should read: if it has salt or spice (Rashi), and it is possible that even if his intent is for his own pleasure it is permitted, for he did not accept upon himself etc. eating and drinking, as is written in Yoreh Deah etc.
