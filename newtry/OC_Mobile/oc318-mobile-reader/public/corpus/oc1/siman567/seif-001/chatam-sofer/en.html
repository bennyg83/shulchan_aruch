On the Mechaber, se'if 1, "he may taste." Note: It appears to me that they relied on the verse "and fast for me, and do not eat and do not drink," and therefore the measure of eating is a kezayis and of drinking a revi'is, and not like the measure of Yom Kippur. Nevertheless, tasting in order to swallow is forbidden, for it is a half-measure of eating; and so too a full measure even to spit out is forbidden, for that also is like a half-measure: swallowing of the throat is the measure, and pleasure of the mouth is a half-measure. But less than a kezayis, and he also spits it out: this is permitted even if he has pleasure. This appears according to my humble opinion as the intent of Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; and study carefully Responsa Rivash, siman 288, regarding tasting of forbidden [foods].
