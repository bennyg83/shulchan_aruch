He may taste. If it has salt or spice (Rashi), for he did not accept upon himself that he not derive benefit, but only that he not eat and drink, and this is not called eating and drinking. And it is possible that even if his intent is for the pleasure itself it is permitted, as is written in Yoreh Deah at the beginning of siman 238: that so long as he did not eat a [full] measure, it is called tasting and not eating; and so it appears in the Rokeach; see what I wrote in siman 210.
A revi'is. The log is an egg and a half (Beis Yosef).
On every communal fast. For it is well regarding a fast that they accepted of themselves: one can say that he accepted upon himself only the pleasure of eating; which is not so regarding a communal fast.
