One may, Taz, Berakhot 14; and therefore one who is forbidden benefit from a dish — even if he spits out, all the more so it is forbidden because he benefits. And what is the reason? Some say on communal fasts, and even according to those who permit, they concede here. From Tosafot there it implies that spitting out has no benefit, and therefore one who tastes needs no berachah; and see siman 210. We hold that tasting and spitting out is called benefit and needs no berachah according to some there, even swallowing less than a revi'it since he only tastes (Magen Avraham there, note 6). More than a revi'it is forbidden even to spit out, for such benefit is considered eating, etc. Or it is impossible not to swallow a bit in the throat — this is according to those who permit a revi'it in the mouth; and one forbidden benefit from a dish may not taste even to know if it needs salt or spice (Rashi there, and Magen Avraham in Rashi). Understand. Water in such a case requires study; vinegar — one may say that if it is considered benefit and permitted in such a case (Magen Avraham, note 7). One who says “I will fast tomorrow” so as not to eat and drink — nevertheless it is forbidden all the more to swallow; see Rosh Lechem. Understand.
