1) [Seif 1] One who is fasting may taste, etc. He may taste the cooked dish in order to know whether it requires salt or seasoning. Rashi, Berachos 14a. It is possible that even if his intent is for his own enjoyment, it is permitted, as Yad Aharon emended, for he did not accept upon himself that he would derive no benefit, but only that he would not eat or drink, and this is not considered eating or drinking. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 1.
