(s.k. 3) On every communal fast. "For it is well etc.": that is to say, the view of the Beis Yosef is specifically Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av, and the same applies to the Seventeenth of Tammuz and the Tenth of Teves. For specifically regarding a fast that requires acceptance, we say that he accepted upon himself eating, and not a prohibition of benefit; which is not so regarding the four aforementioned fasts, which do not require acceptance, for they stand already accepted, [and] even benefit is forbidden. But regarding other fasts that the community decrees for any trouble that should not come: since they require acceptance, tasting is permitted, for we say that he did not accept benefit upon himself. And the view of the Rama is that on every communal fast tasting is forbidden: even though it requires acceptance, nevertheless the matter does not depend on his intention, for in any case he must accept it.
