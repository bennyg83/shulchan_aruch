Seif 2 dwellers. Yerushalmi chapter 1 teaches: tents in an encampment need eruv; in a caravan no eruv; but commentators wrote the Yerushalmi is corrupt and it should read as in Tosefta chapter 2: what is the difference between caravan and encampment — except tents in a caravan need eruv, tents in an encampment are exempt from eruv; and the rest without tents are the same as fit in a courtyard — and this is what is stated in the Mishnah, four things exempt in an encampment, etc.; and specifically encampment — as Yerushalmi there: how much is encampment? Ten, as it says "until the great encampment like the encampment of God" — less than this is caravan; and nevertheless:
