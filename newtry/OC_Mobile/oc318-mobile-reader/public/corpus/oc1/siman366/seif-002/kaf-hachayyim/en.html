8) [Se'if 2] Those who dwell in tents, etc. This refers to nomadic desert dwellers who camp there to pasture their flocks or for plowing and sowing, since the land there is spacious.
9) There. Or a camp, etc. It appears that this discusses a camp that is not going out to fight, but is traveling into the wilderness for some purpose and establishes itself there for several days. It must therefore establish an eruv. A camp going out to fight, however, is exempt from an eruv even if established for a long time, for it is stated there that a camp is exempt from four obligations, one of which is establishing an eruv. Olas Shabbos, subsection 2. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2, likewise writes that when people go to war, the Sages exempted them from eruvei chatzeiros and netilas yadayim, even in an optional war. They are exempt specifically from eruvei chatzeiros, but remain obligated in eruvei techumin. Gemara, Eruvin 17b. This follows Rambam's view that the Torah techum is twelve mil. Shiltei HaGibborim at the end of chapter 1 of Eruvin, however, writes in the name of Ri'az that they are exempt even from eruvei techumin, since that obligation too is Rabbinic. See there. See below, at the beginning of siman 397, and our words there, with the help of Heaven.
10) There. Which they surrounded with a partition. The partition must be ten handbreadths high, for otherwise it is not called a partition.
11) There. They may not carry from one tent to another, etc. The same applies to carrying from a tent to the open area within the enclosure, just as one may not carry from a house into a courtyard, as stated in the preceding se'if.
12) There. They may not carry from one tent to another, etc. This is because each person's tent is fixed for him to remain there for many days. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 2.
13) There. Until they all establish an eruv. This is for the reason we wrote above in subsection 2.
14) There. But a caravan that they surrounded with a partition. Each member made a tent for himself within the enclosure.
15) There. But a caravan that continues on its journey. They merely needed to rest there for Shabbos, and those tents are not fixed for them but are only temporary. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 3; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2. It appears that the same applies if they are delayed there for some reason, such as because of bandits until assistance arrives and they can travel with it, or for another reason. Since they urgently wish to continue their journey and are detained only by circumstance, those tents are not called fixed, and they do not require an eruv.
