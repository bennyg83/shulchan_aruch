The Sages forbade carrying, etc. — for by Torah law they should be permitted to carry in the entire courtyard and from houses to courtyard, for it is complete reshut ha-yachid; but Sages forbade carrying from houses to courtyard until they eruv, because houses are separate domains for each and courtyard is shared by all — somewhat similar to reshut ha-rabbim; if they took out from houses to courtyard they would say permitted to take out from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim.
