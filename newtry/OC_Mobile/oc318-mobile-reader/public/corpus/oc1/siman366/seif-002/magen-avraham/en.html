Caravan, etc. They are not fixed there:

Houses. Those who have no houses on the ship are kosher in one courtyard — see siman 366 seif 3:

Partitions. Meaning ten high from the ship's floor; even if not ten high from the water, permitted to carry within (so in Shabbos daf 101) — see siman 355:

Forbidden to carry. It is karmelit, but permitted to carry from the sea into it, for from karmelit to karmelit is permitted. We say in the Gemara there: small ships wide above and narrow below to a knife point — nevertheless reshut ha-yachid if they have partitions of ten, for we say gud achit; and in Agudah siman 113 — if there is not four tefachim width within three tefachim height, then it is like the sea and one may not carry in it except within four amot; and permitted to carry from it to the sea — see siman 345; and Tosafos disagree and explain the ship has sides and floor of boards with hollow between boards and water enters and they sit in it like in water — nevertheless reshut ha-yachid, for we see as if the ship's sides are bent and surround the entire ship and the boards are joined on the surface. And if there is a house on the ship and the ship has no partitions of ten, forbidden to take out from the house to the ship; and if it has partitions of ten, no eruv needed since there is only one house:
