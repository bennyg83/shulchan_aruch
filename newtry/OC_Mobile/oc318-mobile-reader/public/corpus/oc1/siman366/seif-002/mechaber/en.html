Dwellers of tents or sukkot or an encampment that they surrounded with a partition may not carry from tent to tent until they all make an eruv; but a caravan that they surrounded with a partition does not need an eruv because all are combined and those tents are not fixed for them:Houses on a ship need an eruv even though the ship has partitions; and if the ship has no partitions it is forbidden to carry on the ship except within four amot (Beit Yosef in the name of Shibolei Leket) (and see below end of siman 382 and siman 405 seif 7):'.
