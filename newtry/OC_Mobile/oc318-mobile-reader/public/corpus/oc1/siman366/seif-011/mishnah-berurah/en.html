(72) Members of a group, etc. — it teaches two things: one — although they did not place l'chatchila for eruv; also that bread was not originally all of theirs but only the host's who invited them to dine from his own and did not explicitly transfer that bread to them — nevertheless since he invited them to eat with him and they may eat willingly, it is as if they joined with him and have share in it; therefore if it exists until after bein hashemashot they rely on it for eruv:
(73) Bread on the table, etc. — at first glance we require that bread still be with them — one whole loaf, as in seif 16; or possibly since they did not bring their bread but the host invited them from his own, their law resembles seif 7, and so Tosafos Yom Tov implies; but in truth one may distinguish between them — requires study:
(74) But in a courtyard not — for a courtyard is not fit for dwelling:
(75) And the same if they have, etc. — this law is this case, and all the more the other; and Tosafos Yom Tov wrote it teaches one relies on it based on presumption it exists even if they do not know themselves, because we establish it on its presumption:
(76) Rely on it for eruv — and for joining alleys they may rely even when placed in one of the courtyards [Gemara]:
