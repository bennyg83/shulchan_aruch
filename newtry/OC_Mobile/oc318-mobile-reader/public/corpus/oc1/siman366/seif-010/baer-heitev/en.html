And Taz wrote: it appears to me that if the synagogue or other house where the eruv is placed is in a situation forbidden to carry — such as no alley eruv or outside the eruv — that eruv is nothing, since the reason for eruv chatzerot the Tur wrote is we consider as if all dwell in that house and all courtyards belong there; here impossible to say so, for forbidden to carry from courtyards; rather place in a house where carrying is permitted; and all the more where no eruv in the city, forbidden house to house where they cannot make eruv chatzerot unless those permitted from their courtyard to courtyard where eruv is placed — end.
