When he grants to them through another, he may not grant through his minor son and daughter even if they are not dependent on his table, nor through his Canaanite slave and maidservant; but he may grant through his adult son and daughter even if dependent on his table, and through his Hebrew slave and maidservant even if they are minors, and through his wife even though he provides her sustenance and even if she has no house in' the courtyard; and some say he may not grant through his son and daughter dependent on his table even if adults, nor through his daughter even if not dependent on his table as long as she has not reached maturity, nor through his wife who provides her own sustenance or who said to her "go out — your handiwork is for your sustenance," even if she has a house in the courtyard; but he may grant through his son who is not dependent on his table even if a minor, and through his daughter who reached maturity and is not dependent on his table, and through his wife who does not provide her own sustenance even if she has no house in the courtyard; and l'chatchila it is good to heed the second view where possible:And b'dieved one relies on the lenient words regarding eruv; and likewise an adult who has a wife even if dependent on his father's table may grant through him (Hagahot Ashiri and Hagahot Semag and Kol Bo) even l'chatchila; and he need not inform those he granted for before Shabbat — but if he informs them on Shabbat it is permitted to carry (Kol Bo):.
