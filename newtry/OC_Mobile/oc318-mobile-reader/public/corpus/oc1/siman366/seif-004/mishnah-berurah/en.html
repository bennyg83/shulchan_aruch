(31) In one vessel — for eruv is called that they be joined together; if placed in two vessels it appears they are divided and separated; and it appears at first glance specifically if each gives a share in the eruv; but if one acquires for all he may place in two vessels; however since poskim did not mention this it implies they hold the Sages did not distinguish in their matter — requires study:

(32) One became full — meaning in this the division to two vessels was not intentional; however if he knew from the start the vessel would not hold the entire eruv and he had another larger vessel that could hold the entire eruv — some Acharonim are stringent that it is like dividing the eruv into two from the start:

(33) In one house — for at least both were in one domain, but not in two houses; and even if one acquires the eruv from his own on behalf of all, one should be careful in this; therefore the eruv placed in synagogue on behalf of the entire city should be careful not to place only in one synagogue — choose the largest synagogue in the city [responsum in name of Chacham Tzvi]:
