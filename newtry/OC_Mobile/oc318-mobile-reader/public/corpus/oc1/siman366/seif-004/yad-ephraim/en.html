What he wrote and the essence as he said — meaning for another matter: even though the reason one who performs a mitzva blesses is challenged if we say it does not apply to that mitzva and he does nothing per our custom that he need not acquire; nevertheless the essence as he said that also the other is included in the mitzva, etc., and he may bless properly; and he again wrote requires study in what I wrote above s.k. 13 that l'chatchila one should be stringent that the beadle acquire, etc. — this is a matter unto itself — he did not say that because of this reason it would be permitted also for another, and also that the blessing is prior to performance; that is, even though there is reason to do so stringently, nevertheless this does not help to be lenient because of this, since acquisition is essentially superfluous; and as Magen Avraham wrote if they forgot to acquire until Shabbat it is also permitted as Rama wrote; and therefore one should not rely on this to bless afterward or to say the blessing is prior to performance — only that otherwise it is fine, for the reason stated before; see Taz siman 395:
