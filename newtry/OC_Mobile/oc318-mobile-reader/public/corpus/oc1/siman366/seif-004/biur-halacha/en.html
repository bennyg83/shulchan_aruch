In one vessel — at first glance vessel is not precise — likewise if they place all eruvei collected in one place it is also fine; it comes only to exclude two vessels, for that indicates division — requires study [Peri Megadim]; and from Yerushalmi it appears like his words to be lenient, as it says: members of the alley who placed eruvei in two places — if without intent it is permitted [and specifically one house per our Babylonian Talmud]; and if for the sake of dividing their eruv it is forbidden; and it did not mention at all that it must specifically be in a vessel — the essence is one place; and likewise the reverse — if placed in two places without any vessel at all, even in one house, it also does not help; and so proven from Rambam chapter 1 of Laws of Eruvin halacha 18 who wrote if they divided the eruv or the partnership even in one house it is not an eruv, and did not mention at all that the division was in two vessels:
