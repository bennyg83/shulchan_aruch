9) There. Or a camp, etc. It appears that this discusses a camp that is not going out to fight, but is traveling into the wilderness for some purpose and establishes itself there for several days. It must therefore establish an eruv. A camp going out to fight, however, is exempt from an eruv even if established for a long time, for it is stated there that a camp is exempt from four obligations, one of which is establishing an eruv. Olas Shabbos, subsection 2. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2, likewise writes that when people go to war, the Sages exempted them from eruvei chatzeiros and netilas yadayim, even in an optional war. They are exempt specifically from eruvei chatzeiros, but remain obligated in eruvei techumin. Gemara, Eruvin 17b. This follows Rambam's view that the Torah techum is twelve mil. Shiltei HaGibborim at the end of chapter 1 of Eruvin, however, writes in the name of Ri'az that they are exempt even from eruvei techumin, since that obligation too is Rabbinic. See there.
