Or encampment, etc. — it appears encampment means not going out to war but going for some business in desert and settling there some days — therefore obligated in eruv; but encampment going to war, even if fixed for long time, exempt from eruv, for so we say there — four things exempt in encampment, one of which exempt from eruv. See there note 2; and so R' Zalman note 2 — if going to war, Sages exempted from eruv chatzerot and netilat yadayim; even milchemet reshut. And specifically eruv chatzerot; but eruv techumin obligated. Gemara Eruvin 17b.
