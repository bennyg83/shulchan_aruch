(48) On behalf of all — even not in their presence:
(49) Provided he acquires for them through another — for through himself he cannot acquire for them, for as long as it is in his hand it has not left his domain:
(50) Through another — namely he says to him: receive this bread and acquire it for all courtyard members; and he intends in his raising on behalf of all; and even though they did not appoint him agent for this, one may acquire for a person not in his presence, and it is as if they are his agents:
(51) A tefach — and even per those who say in Choshen Mishpat siman 198 seif 4 in gloss that raising less than three does not acquire — here for eruv which is d'rabbanan they were lenient and a tefach raising suffices. Taz wrote likewise if he placed the eruv on his hand suspended in air he must raise his hand a tefach; and see Biur Halacha:
(52) And for whoever will be added from this day onward — for otherwise sometimes residents will be added and they will be forbidden until they add for them; but when he intends for them from the start it is unnecessary, for the measure of two meals suffices even for a thousand, as below siman 368:
(53) And some say even if, etc. — the wording is abbreviated; from essence of law certainly it is not so, but in our time one may rely on this since we practice placing eruv for the entire year — presumably we also intend for people added during the year; and so is the wording in Beit Yosef and the Mechaber's intent here:
(54) Must add because of them — for perhaps the Sages did not say a remnant of any amount (as explained siman 368) except for those present at the start of placing the eruv who acquired then; but those not present at the start did not acquire then and only acquire when they are added; and since at that time it already diminished from its measure, they cannot acquire; and for this reason it does not help even if he stipulates explicitly for whoever will be added; and there is no remedy except to ensure it always has the measure of two meals:
