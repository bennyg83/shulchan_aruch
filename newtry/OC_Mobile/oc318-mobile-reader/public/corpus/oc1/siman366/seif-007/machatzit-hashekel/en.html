(s.k. 7) They may not, etc.; if sold house we have no proof — perhaps alley members did not waive this right except to him and not to sell to another:

Reason because of benefit — Gittin 60a: eruv in old house because of ways of peace; Gemara: why? If honor — this house already acquired honor; this shofar (trumpet for stopping work or voluntary shofar for yeshiva funds placed at head of city) was first in R' Yehuda's house, after death Rabbah, then R' Yosef… conclusion because of suspicion; Rashi: since accustomed to place eruv there, when they see no eruv they suspect alley members carry without eruv; Rambam in Mishnah commentary wrote other reason — benefit: house where eruv placed need not give bread lest lose benefit — cannot place in another house; Rambam's words difficult against Gemara; Magen Avraham wrote specifically for Rambam's benefit reason one may distinguish sale — alley did not waive except to him; but per Gemara's suspicion reason — what difference if in his hand or sold:

Even for Rambam one may say, etc. — Rambam's text in Gemara is benefit; nevertheless proven even if sold waiver to buyer; otherwise what challenge from shofar — different there when left R' Yehuda's domain; or Gemara holds honor inherits to sons — likewise benefit of bread inherits to son if died, likewise buyer:

Between death and sale — not all texts so; perhaps heir more father's presumption than buyer; or while alive alley did not waive except to him — therefore should place in house where he now dwells; unlike death when he cannot benefit — heirs acquire; if sold and left alley, buyer acquires as if died; I do not know his reason; Magen Avraham wrote strained to distinguish:
