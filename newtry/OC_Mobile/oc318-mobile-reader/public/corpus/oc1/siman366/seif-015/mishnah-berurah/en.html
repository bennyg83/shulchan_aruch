(80) At the time, etc. — because all mitzvot one blesses prior to performance; and per what Rama wrote above seif 6 that the custom is to collect flour from city houses — one should not bless at time of collection, for it is prior to prior while the bread is not yet baked:

(81) Who collects, etc. — Beit Yosef and Bach wrote in siman 395 one must bless when he begins to collect, not when collection is finished — for then the mitzva is finished; Acharonim agreed he may l'chatchila bless after collection is finished; the reason: they hold that after collection one must l'chatchila acquire for all city members as we wrote above s.k. 15, and it is still called prior to performance before acquisition; nevertheless b'dieved even if he already acquired he may bless, for the essence of eruv acquisition occurs at Shabbat entry and in every case it is called prior to performance; especially if the eruv was not yet placed in its place — certainly one may bless, for the mitzva is not yet finished:

(82) At the time he acquires for them — meaning he eruvs from his own and gives to others and tells them that after he blesses they should raise to acquire on behalf of city people; and see Biur Halacha we brought that one who blesses after acquisition also has whom to rely on:

(83) For all houses in the courtyard — and he did not mention alley because here it deals where alleys were not fixed with eruvein properly and each courtyard fixes eruv chatzerot only to permit carrying in the courtyard; but where alleys were fixed, the custom is to do eruv chatzerot and joining alleys together as siman 387; therefore one must add alley language, and this is its formula in this eruv: it shall be permitted for us to take out and bring in from houses to courtyard and from courtyard to houses and from house to house and from courtyard to courtyard and from roof to roof and from houses and courtyards to alley and from alley to all houses and courtyards in this city for us and all who dwell in this city and whoever will be added to it; and if he eruvs for all Shabbatot of the year he concludes for all Shabbatot of the year and all festivals [Acharonim]:
