Eruv. Requires study whether another person who does not live in that city may recite this blessing; it appears to me he may bless, for if they had not eruv'd he too would be forbidden to carry — if so he too is included in this mitzva. Magen Avraham see there; and the essence is the beadle gives the eruv to the rabbi and acquires for the entire congregation through him; and so in customs; therefore if night fell on first Yom Tov and he did not acquire, forbidden to acquire on Yom Tov as siman 528 except conditionally; and if Yom Tov is not adjacent to Shabbat, do not eruv until Shabbat; however if they forgot to acquire until Shabbat, permitted to carry as Rama seif 6. In our text from roof to roof see siman 372; and say for all Shabbatot of the year as siman 368; and Yeshu'ot Yaakov first chapter of Beitza 64 wrote to say also for Yom Tov, and this is not change of custom; and see siman 518; and see responsum of Bach part 1 and Maharam of Boton siman 98:
