[8] And give in another house. Even if the homeowner died; and even if the second gives money to courtyard members to place in his house; and even if he is a Torah scholar and the first an am ha-aretz (Radbaz 14); and if at start of giving they stipulated to change, permitted; and if a matter where they need to give money, they may change to give to another; and in all, if they transgressed and placed in another house they do not compel him to return to that house; however if they wish to return and place, the authority is in their hands (requires study — Knesset HaGedolah):
