One does not make eruv in a slice — meaning eruv chatzerot; but for joining alleys, since they eruv with all kinds of food and drink, the Sages did not enact this — specifically with whole loaves, as Rambam wrote; and at first glance per what Rama wrote at end of seif 3 regarding a dwelling house — that today we do eruv chatzerot and joining alleys together — we say migo that it works for partnership it also works for eruv chatzerot, as Gra explained there — possibly here too we could say so; but since Rama did not mention this to distinguish here, it implies even today one does not eruv in a slice; and perhaps one may say Rama holds like other Rishonim [see Sefer Gaon Yaakov stated in the passage] that even for joining alleys one does not eruv in a slice when eruv'ing with bread:

That they should have the measure — namely as explained below siman 368:
