(37) In a slice — because of enmity lest they come to dispute — one says I give whole and you slice; and even if all wish to give slices we fear it will revert to ruin; and see Peri Megadim who is uncertain that perhaps even b'dieved the eruv does not help when made in a slice; and if made whole at first and afterward for another Shabbat it broke and a little remained, see below siman 368 seif 4 see there:

(38) Challah measure — meaning even if not whole it is permitted, for neighbors think challah was taken from the bread:

(39) And if it was sliced, etc. — meaning a little was sliced from the loaf:

(40) If it is not recognizable, etc. — the reason: although in truth it is not whole, nevertheless since it is not recognizable, dispute will not arise:

(41) Meaning all the eruv together, etc. — meaning here too they fulfill what the Sages enacted for eruv chatzerot that it be specifically in whole bread; but they also concede each may give whole bread, and even if this one's loaf is larger than his friend's it is also permitted; and they also concede to the first view that one does not eruv in a slice even if all give equal slices:

(42) From each house a little flour — and the same when they give money to buy flour with; and even if some homeowners did not give, there is no objection, as concluded at the end that each gives his flour for the eruv on behalf of all:

(43) And must be careful, etc. — it teaches us that even though they combine to bring whole bread for the mitzva of eruv, one might think this is more important and does not need measure — it teaches us:

(44) And even though, etc. — meaning there are people without a share in the eruv bread, and therefore these would forbid those:

(45) It is not inferior, etc., and with intent thus, etc. — meaning here too when each homeowner gave a little flour to the beadle on behalf of all he gave; and although one who gives eruv on behalf of his friend must acquire for him through another as will be explained below seif 9 — nevertheless here when each gives his flour to the beadle to eruv with it, we consider as if they give him to acquire on behalf of all, and the beadle need not acquire for them through another or explicitly himself; and some say if the beadle did not acquire for them through another or explicitly himself it is not an eruv, and proper to heed their words l'chatchila [especially if the beadle made it from his own flour he certainly must acquire for the congregation through another]; and so practiced — the beadle gives the eruv to the rabbi on erev Pesach to acquire on behalf of the entire congregation; and if night fell on first Yom Tov and he did not acquire, one may only acquire conditionally — namely he says if today is weekday I acquire it for the entire city, and if holy my words are void; and so he says the next night of second Yom Tov; and all this if Yom Tov was adjacent to Shabbat — if there are weekdays between, wait to acquire until erev Shabbat; and if he also forgot on erev Shabbat to acquire until it darkened, rely on those poskim who hold permitted to carry even without acquisition since already collected on behalf of all [Acharonim]:
