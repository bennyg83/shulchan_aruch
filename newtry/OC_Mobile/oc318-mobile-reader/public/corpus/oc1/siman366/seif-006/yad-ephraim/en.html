Siman 366 seif 14 he blesses on mitzva of eruv; and see in Maharam of Boton siman 38 — one who wishes to eruv eruv chatzerot and eruv tavshilin together — if he did not speak between them he may include them in one blessing and bless on mitzvot of eruvein as Rambam wrote on separating terumot and ma'aserot see there; and it appears l'chatchila he should specify eruv chatzerot and eruv tavshilin; even though if each is done separately he says on mitzvot of eruv in general; nevertheless since he combines them he should specify each separately — better to specify the blessing, as we say give him according to his blessings; and although we do not rule like R' Yehuda for birkat minei desha'im see in my responsum Beit Efraim where I distinguished this; and there I brought Rambam's source from Yerushalmi of demai and words of Rivash siman 385:
