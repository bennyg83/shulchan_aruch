Seif 2 tent-dwellers. A sukkah of the festival during the festival that opens to the courtyard — it appears it does not forbid, and no eruv needed; see first chapter of Yoma daf 10 — R' Yehuda holds a sukkah of the festival during the festival is obligated in mezuzah, and it teaches on this R' Yehuda obligates in eruv, mezuzah, and tithes; it implies per Rabbanan not obligated in eruv. However for a house on a ship the laws differ, for exempt from mezuzah as stated in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 286 seif 1, and he included both in one place with sukkah of the festival — in both the reason is lack of permanence; but here houses on a ship are obligated in eruv as in the gloss; if so, a sukkah of the festival during the festival also appears obligated in eruv; requires study for law.
