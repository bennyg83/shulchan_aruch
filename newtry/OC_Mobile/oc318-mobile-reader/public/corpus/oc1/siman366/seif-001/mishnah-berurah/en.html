(1) Many houses — of designated people:

(2) From their houses to the courtyard — and the same from house to house within the courtyard or within the alley even though the alley is fixed with lechi and korah; and the reason in all this is they decreed not to take out from his domain to his friend's domain lest they come to take out from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim; and in the courtyard itself permitted to carry throughout, and even from courtyard to courtyard also permitted, for the Sages did not decree on this, as below siman 372:

(3) Through this, etc. — for a person's primary dwelling is where his bread is:

(4) And as if the entire courtyard — and as Rambam explained: they should join in one food that they place from erev Shabbat — meaning all are joined and one food for all, and none divides domain from his friend; rather as all our hands are equal in this place remaining for all of us, so all our hands are equal in every place each holds for himself — and behold all one domain; and through this act they will not err and liken that it is permitted to take out and bring in from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim — end:
