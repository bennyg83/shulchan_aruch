דין עירוב לחצר שהרבה בני אדם דרים בו. ובו טו סעיפים:
The law of eruv for a courtyard where many people dwell. And in it are 15 seifim: A courtyard where many houses open into it — the Sages forbade carrying from their houses to the courtyard until they make an eruv, meaning that they collect bread from each and every house and place it in one of the houses of the courtyards; for thereby we see as if all dwell in that one house and as if the entire courtyard is dedicated to that one house:
