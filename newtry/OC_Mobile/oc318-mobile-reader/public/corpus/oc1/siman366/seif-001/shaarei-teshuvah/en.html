Shulchan Aruch seif 4: must place in one vessel, etc.; and see in responsum of Chacham Tzvi siman 112 who learned from this that in two houses it is obvious the eruv is nothing, and he objects to those who err to place a challah loaf of eruv bread in many synagogues in the city, and wrote it is a blessing in vain and they desecrate Shabbat through carrying; and see there he wrote that even if they do not bless on them but hang them for decoration it is forbidden, since the way of those loaves is to hang them for the sake of eruv — one must fear error of children who will think it is permitted to place the eruv in two houses; and further that adults too will err, etc.; and forbidden to hang only one loaf in one synagogue from all synagogues in the city for the mitzva of eruv; and in Mekor Baruch small for Maharav Weisel he wrote to disagree and permit doing so; and that it is better to do so so if one falls the other stands; and also especially since they already practiced so — one must fear children will not see in the synagogue and will forget the law of eruv see there; and his words are not decisive to reject the words of Chacham Tzvi; however there is room to hang it in the largest, most important synagogue in the city that all city members are partners in and related to; and so is the practice:
