Need not place bread — Taz see Tur and Eruvin 49a. I have doubt here and I will set forth briefly with God's help. Rambam chapter 1 of Eruvin halacha 5 and Tur and Levush — reason: each house has separate owners and all houses have domain in courtyard — like houses are reshut ha-yachid and courtyard reshut ha-rabbim, lest they err; therefore they eruv as if all dwell in one house and courtyard subjugated only to that house (and Levush wrote reshut ha-rabbim — Torah forbade, difficult like karmelit d'rabbanan and bread does not help to equate partitions — requires study); but Rashi Shabbos 6a s.v. courtyard that pass through to reshut ha-rabbim implies even rear area behind houses though used regularly, no eruv needed since not similar to reshut ha-rabbim as no passing to reshut ha-rabbim; and s.v. did not eruv — taking out domain to domain — one may say appears like reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim; but Beit Yosef brought taking out his domain to friend's domain — perhaps Rashi's text so, or he explains so; and as Rashi Eruvin 82 in traklin and as Magen Avraham here 1 and siman 372 note 7 from room to friend's room forbidden; and siman 365 note 15. For Rambam and Tur one may say room to friend's room forbidden and courtyard each uses only his own — d'rabbanan there is bererah for that reason similar to reshut ha-rabbim; and even if they divided courtyard and each recognizes and uses his portion, nevertheless breached fully to forbidden place.
