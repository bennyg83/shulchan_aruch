Four amot by four amot. And if it has enough for a quarter of four by four amot, it suffices (Levush Chayyim 106) — see Yoreh De'ah siman 286 seif 13:

They may not change it. Even if there is some reason to change (Maharik s.k. 13); but if there is a great reason they may change (Rashdam siman 60; R' Yehuda Levi responsum 46; R' Shimon Levi siman 40); and if he sold his house to another we have no clear proof the buyer acquires (Ra'avad 1); and this is per Rambam's explanation in the Mishnah that the reason is because of benefit; but in the Gemara it states explicitly because of suspicion — since they will not see the eruv in it they will suspect carrying without eruv — if so, even when selling his house they leave it; and even for Rambam one may infer from the Gemara's challenge from the shofar of Rava etc. see there; and so he distinguishes between death and sale — strained:

And give in one house. Even if the second gives money to place in his house (Radbaz 1:17); and if at the start of giving they stipulated to change, permitted to change; and if it is a matter where they need to give money to this one, they may change to give to another; and in all, if they eruv'd and placed in another house they do not remove from it; nevertheless requires study in practice (so it is):

And even a minor. But a non-Jew is forbidden to collect as above:

House of dwelling.
