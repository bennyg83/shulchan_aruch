[3] Forbidden to carry, etc. It is karmelit; but from the sea into it permitted; and if there is a house on the ship forbidden to take out from house to ship; and if it has partitions of ten, no eruv needed since only one house. Small ships wide above and narrow below to a knife point — even so reshut ha-yachid if partitions of ten, for we say gud achit; and some say if there is not four tefachim width within three tefachim height it is like the sea and one carries only within four amot (Magen Avraham).
