To exclude a gatehouse — the Mechaber ruled anonymously here and did not distinguish between a private gatehouse and a public one, and followed Rambam who ruled even for placing eruv even in a private gatehouse one does not place; and see Beit Yosef siman 366 who expounded at length; however many poskim disagree [Rashi, Tosafos, Rosh in last answer, Rashba in Eruvin] — their view is for placing the eruv one places in a private gatehouse, per our text in the Talmud daf 85b see there; however in Biur HaGra it appears the Mechaber's view is also thus — there gatehouse is only a public gatehouse, but private is not included in gatehouse; and he wrote Rif's view is also thus:

Four amot by four amot — and Tosafos Shabbos wrote if the small room is within a large room, then even if it does not have four by four amot one places eruv in it, for the small room's perimeter ascends and it is as if placed in the large room — like that case at beginning of siman 386 for joining alleys see there; and wrote likewise if the small opened fully to the large, then it is like a corner of the large and as if placed inside the large — like above siman 355 for combining ten see there in Tosafos Shabbos; and in my humble opinion the first law is not required, for certainly Rashi did not write there perimeter ascends, etc. except as reasoning — it is not inferior to the matter because he made partitions, for in the courtyard's airspace it is still inferior, as explained in the Gemara's language — courtyard is not inferior unlike here where we require a house fit for dwelling; therefore one may well say since he placed his eruv within narrow partitions without even four by four amot that do not help, and gud achit does not apply here — certainly it is inferior, for without the walls it would be placed in a place fit for dwelling, and now he placed it within partitions where one cannot dwell; requires study in practice:

And it appears to me the reason our eruvei chatzerot have the law of partnership — Beit Yosef challenged in siman 386: since in this eruv all houses of dwelling join — eruv and partnership together — certainly must place in a house of dwelling and not in synagogue, etc.; and Gra in this siman intends to resolve this — the houses are permitted because of migo that this eruv placed in synagogue to permit the alleyways works also to permit the houses in the courtyard — like in the Gemara see there; and so Peri Megadim also in siman 387 see there:
