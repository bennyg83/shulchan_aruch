(s.k. 5) Forbidden, etc. — for from karmelit to karmelit permitted; that is within four amot:

We say in Gemara, etc.; and in Agudah, etc.; and Tosafos disagree, etc. — since there is room to err in Magen Avraham's words, I must summarize briefly the Talmud Shabbos 101a: Rav Huna said these exits of Mesene (Rashi: small ships wide above…) — one carries in them only within four (Rashi: not reshut ha-yachid…); but if less than three, four (Rashi: before raising three they widened four tefachim) — lavud not needed; R' Nachman challenged even three high before widening four — say gud achit its partition; and even post ten with tray four wide on top — Chachamim exempt because we do not say gud achit — because partition goats breach; but ship — goats do not breach, fish breach — fish breach is not breach per Rashi; and so conclusion of Gemara — if so even without four width within three height, reshut ha-yachid for gud achit; fish breach not breach unlike Agudah; Agudah must hold like Tosafos who challenged Rashi… end; therefore Agudah also may hold ship sides bent…; nevertheless if four width within three height Tosafos agree lavud… Agudah disputes Rashi — not reshut ha-yachid except four within three… Tosafos agree (Agudah not clear unless as Rashi's rejected view); what Magen Avraham wrote afterward and Tosafos disagree — not on Agudah but on Rashi:

Water enters and made so even if overturned never sinks — end Tosafos; would think since water enters and gap between boards below three lavud not needed — karmelit; nevertheless reshut ha-yachid we see bent sides…:
