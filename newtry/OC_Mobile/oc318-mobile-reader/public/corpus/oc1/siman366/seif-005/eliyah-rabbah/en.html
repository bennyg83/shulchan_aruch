[11] Any one person, etc. Certainly, when one grants everyone a share in an eruv from his own bread, he must not be particular about allowing them to use it.
[12] [Levush] For himself, etc. Tosafot, the Rosh, and Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 5, do not contain the word 'himself.' They imply that even regarding his household members, if he is particular about the bread the eruv is invalid, as stated by Malbushei Yom Tov.
[13] [Levush] Because of hostility, etc. So states Eruvin 81. Yet this requires study because the Maharil, Hilchot Eruvei Chatzerot, gives another reason: perhaps one will be particular if he sees that his loaf is larger than his friend's, and one who is particular about his eruv invalidates it.
[14] Even a large one, etc. It is not a valid eruv, as stated by Avodat HaKodesh. This implies invalidity even after the fact. Maharil likewise implies that even if everyone made the eruv with slices it is invalid after the fact. If one mistakenly made the eruv with a slice and, after finishing the formula 'by this eruv it shall be permitted,' remembered the law, then took a whole loaf and repeated the formula over it, he relied on the original blessing so it would not be in vain. This implies that if a substantial interruption occurred, he would need to bless again. Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah cites Maharam Mintz that if the eruv was eaten on Friday night and only a piece remains, one may rely on it, but if this occurred during the week one must make a new eruv. Maharil does not appear to agree, for he writes that even if it was eaten during bein hashmashot on Friday, since it was whole when the eruv took effect there is no concern if it later became a slice. At first glance, however, perhaps there is no dispute: during bein hashmashot there is no time to make another eruv, so Maharam would agree it remains valid, while during the week, when there is time, Maharil might agree a replacement is needed. See siman 368, seif 4, where even a minimal remainder suffices for later Shabbosos. Nevertheless, during the week before the first Shabbos, when this is still the initial eruv, it appears that even if a piece of the proper quantity remains one should remake it with a whole loaf. So too it appears from the Tur in Yoreh Deah there.
