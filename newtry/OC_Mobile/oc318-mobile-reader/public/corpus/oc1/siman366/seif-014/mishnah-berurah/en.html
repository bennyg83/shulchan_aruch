(79) On mitzvot of eruv — and even another person who does not live in that city and wishes to rest there on Shabbat may recite this blessing, for if they had not eruv'd he too would be forbidden to carry — if so he too is related to the eruv; and see what we further wrote on this in Biur Halacha. If he places eruv tavshilin and eruv chatzerot at one time he recites one blessing on both — on mitzvot of eruvein; and recites the formula of this eruv for each according to its formula; and in Chayei Adam general rule 102 wrote to say on mitzvot of eruv tavshilin and eruv chatzerot and say in the formula it shall be permitted for us to bake, etc., and to carry from house to courtyard, etc.:
