Blesses on mitzva of eruv. Nevertheless requires study whether another who does not live in that city may recite this blessing; it appears obvious he may bless, for if they had not eruv'd he too would be forbidden to carry — if so he too is included in this mitzva; and even if he is there on Yom Tov the eruv helps as siman 518; and further one who performs a mitzva for others also blesses as Tur siman 441 and Rambam chapter 11 of Berachos 13 — one who makes mezuzah for others blesses; but one may distinguish — there he blesses for he does an act, but here he does nothing per our custom; and if you ask how one of the city blesses — behold once the eruv was collected he already acquired the eruv as siman 366 seif 6 — if so no longer to bless as Beit Yosef siman 395 and see siman 492; one may say nevertheless since Shabbat has not yet entered he has not yet acquired the eruv — see siman 395; therefore at Shabbat entry the mitzva applies and he may bless; but afterward when no longer related to that mitzva at all, on what does he bless? The essence as said — the beadle gives the eruv to the rabbi and acquires for the congregation through him; and so in customs; therefore if night fell on first Yom Tov and he did not acquire, forbidden to acquire on Yom Tov as siman 528 except conditionally; and if Yom Tov is not adjacent to Shabbat, do not eruv until Shabbat; however if they forgot to acquire until Shabbat, permitted to carry as Rama seif 6. In our text from roof to roof see siman 372; and say for all Shabbatot of the year as siman 368; and Yeshu'ot Yaakov first chapter of Beitza 64 wrote to say also for Yom Tov, and this is not change of custom; and siman 518: The Ari would eruv and recite the blessing and afterward acquire for courtyard members through another, for such is the law — after he eruv'd for himself he then acquires for others (Kavanot); requires study, for he must participate himself with the entire courtyard; perhaps it refers to eruv tavshilin; and what he wrote for courtyard members — not literal, rather for city members:
