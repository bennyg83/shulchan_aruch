He blesses, etc. — and where the custom is to do for the entire congregation orally and one wishes to do for himself every erev Shabbat as several Acharonim wrote — l'chatchila it is good to practice thus — it is not worthwhile that he bless on it; and also proper that his eruv too be given to another to acquire on behalf of the entire city, for if he truly wishes to separate himself from the city there is room to say they would forbid one another; and see siman 392:

On mitzvot of eruv — see Mishna Berurah what he wrote regarding another person who rests in the city may recite this blessing; and even if he will be there only on Yom Tov and not on the following Shabbat the eruv helps him [for matters without any need as Rama wrote beginning of siman 518 in gloss]; and therefore if he does not intend to remain there neither for Shabbat nor for Yom Tov he cannot bless; and it is not like what we rule generally that one who performs a mitzva for another, such as fixing a mezuzah, may bless — for there he does an act; unlike here which is only a blessing, since the eruv was already collected by another.
