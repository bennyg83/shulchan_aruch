[14] Even large, etc. It is not his eruv (Avodat HaKodesh); implies even b'dieved; and even if all eruv'd in slices it does not help b'dieved per Maharil. Forgot and eruv'd on slice; when concluding the formula remembered and took whole and said again — but relied on first blessing not to be in vain — end; implies if he interrupted much he must return and bless. She'erit Knesset HaGedolah: Maharam Mintz wrote if eruv was eaten Shabbat night and only a piece remains they rely on it; if on weekday must eruv again — end; this does not appear so from Maharil who wrote even if eaten erev Shabbat during bein hashemashot, since at time of eruv it was whole, no concern if afterward sliced — end his words; and apparently difficult — one may say no dispute: during bein hashemashot when no time to make another, Maharam permits; on weekday when there is time, perhaps Maharil forbids; and see siman 368 seif 4 that valid for other Shabbatot even if only remnant remains; nevertheless on weekday before first Shabbat which is start of eruv it appears he agrees even if piece remains of eruv measure must return and eruv with whole; and so Tur Yoreh De'ah there:
