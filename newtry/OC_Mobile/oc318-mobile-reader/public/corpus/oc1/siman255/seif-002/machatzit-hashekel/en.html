S.k. 2: It is forbidden, etc. As written at the end of siman 254. Meaning, there in se'if 8 it teaches: 'One may not fill, etc.,' and if he did so, even unintentionally, it is forbidden on Motzei Shabbos until b'chedi she'ya'asu. Thus, although he did not stoke the coals, nevertheless, since he committed a prohibited act, it remains forbidden even on Motzei Shabbos, and all the more so it is forbidden to benefit from it on Shabbos. So it is stated in the Maggid Mishneh there. See what Maharam Lublin wrote in Shabbos, folio 18b, regarding lupines and dyeing vats.
