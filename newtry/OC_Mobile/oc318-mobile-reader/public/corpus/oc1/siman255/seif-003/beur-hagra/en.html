Se'if 3. And likewise. According to the Rif's version there of Rav Huna's statement, in both cases the first clause requires it, whereas the latter clause does not require it; he rules like Rav Chisda. The Rosh had the reverse version, and this is the meaning of, 'And there are those who say.' There is a scribal error in the Rosh before us, and it should read 'kernels,' as it does regarding reeds.
