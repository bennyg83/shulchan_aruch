[Levush:] Olive refuse. The Ran explains that this means the waste of olives or sesame. In the Gemara and the Rif, however, the reading is 'pitch' instead of 'olive refuse.' Rabbeinu Yerucham, folio 68, also includes wax, fat, and anything that melts; and the Rokeach, siman 43, section 2, includes cheese.
