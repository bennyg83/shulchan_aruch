There are those who say, etc. All the great authorities expressed astonishment at the Tur for ruling like Rabbeinu Yerucham; see there. In my humble opinion, he derived this from the Yerushalmi at the end of the first chapter of Shabbos: 'The correct version of the Mishnah is: even coals, in any amount.' The commentator in the margin explained that 'the correct version' means, 'so it is.' End quote. This is strained. It appears to me that the Tur interpreted the expression as related to 'you are correct,' meaning that the Mishnah is lawful and proper.
[Levush:] Olive refuse. The Ran explains that this means the waste of olives or sesame. In the Gemara and the Rif, however, the reading is 'pitch' instead of 'olive refuse.' Rabbeinu Yerucham, folio 68, also includes wax, fat, and anything that melts; and the Rokeach, siman 43, section 2, includes cheese.
