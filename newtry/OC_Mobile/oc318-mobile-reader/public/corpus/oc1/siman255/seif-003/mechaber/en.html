When tar, sulfur, straw, or stubble [are used as kindling for] a medurah, even though the fire only started burning minimally [while it was still day on Friday], it is permitted [to benefit from it, because these substances will continue to burn on their own]. The same [rule applies when] reeds or date pits are used as kindling when they are spread out. If, however, the reeds are bound [in a bundle] and the pits are [collected] in a basket, then the fire must take hold of them to the extent that it ascends on its own accord, and there are those who say the opposite.
