1) [Seif 1] One may not make a fire from wood, etc. It appears that the same law applies to a winter heating oven, unless it is sealed with clay. In that case it is permitted according to Rema at the beginning of siman 254 in the gloss. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1. See Menuchah U'Kedushah, who writes that it is forbidden even through a non-Jew, since we nevertheless remain concerned that one might stoke the coals. This was permitted on Shabbos specifically because everyone is considered ill with regard to cold, unlike on Friday, when it could have been done permissibly; see there. Orach Chaim, paragraph 2.
2) There: Until the fire has caught upon them, etc. Otherwise, we are concerned that on Shabbos he might stoke the fire and move the wood so that the flames will rise upon the remaining pieces.
3) There: Without the assistance of other wood. This means that one should not need to say, 'Bring thin pieces of wood and let us place them underneath to ignite these pieces.' Rather, the flame must rise on its own. Bach.
