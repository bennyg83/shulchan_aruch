One may not make a bonfire from wood, etc. The Birkei Yosef writes in the name of Rabbi Mahari Molcho, in a handwritten responsum, concerning one who arranges piles of wood and ignites a long rope from a distance, so that when the fire reaches the pile the wood begins to burn, whether it is permitted to remove wood from it or add wood to it. There is no issue here of extinguishing or kindling. The Tur likewise writes in siman 502 that, from a bonfire made of thin pieces of wood, it is permitted to remove pieces as long as the fire has not caught them. See the Beit Yosef there. The same applies here. This certainly refers to a case in which there is no issue of muktzeh; see there.
