(s.k. 2) First blessing. But final blessing he blesses—for seemingly this is obvious. And this is one proof of Rav Yosef Karo—for Rambam wrote he swallows them and blesses on them at end, for he holds like the first view Rama brought; and that he blesses on them at end means first blessing, for if final blessing what is he teaching—obvious, see there; nevertheless possible to say therefore Magen Avraham wrote he blesses final blessing because he must say in s.k. afterward that for first view Rama wrote there is doubt regarding final blessing; therefore he wrote for first view obvious he blesses final blessing, and this is strained.
