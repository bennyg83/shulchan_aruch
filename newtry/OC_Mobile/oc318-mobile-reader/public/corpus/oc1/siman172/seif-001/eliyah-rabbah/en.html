[Levush] And this is the principal ruling, etc. So writes the Rama, because the Beit Yosef is uncertain how to interpret the Rambam. I found, however, that Kol Bo, folio 21, follows the Rambam and rules like the first opinion, and it appears there that the Raavad agrees. In truth, the Rambam's words support that interpretation, and Kesef Mishneh says so in the name of Rabbenu Manoach. The Raavad's gloss, with a slight correction, also clearly indicates the same view, and his reasoning proves it. The Rashba likewise writes this in the name of the Geonim, and the Bach rules accordingly. Therefore it appears to me that one should not be lenient where there is uncertainty concerning blessings. If he has additional beverage available, however, he should expel what is already in his mouth and drink other beverage after reciting the appropriate first and final blessings.
