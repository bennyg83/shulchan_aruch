And he does not recite a blessing, etc. This resembles one who ate and forgot to recite a blessing until he finished his meal, in which case he does not recite it, as stated in siman 167 according to Rabbeinu Chananel. The alternative opinion is that of the Rosh, who holds that the cases are not comparable. Here he remembered that he had not recited a blessing while the liquid was still in his mouth. Remembering before swallowing resembles, to some degree, reciting a blessing before performance, except that he was unable to recite it then.
