And he does not recite a blessing over them. This means over the liquids that he swallowed. If, however, he has additional liquid to drink, he certainly must recite a blessing, as stated above in siman 167, seif 8. Ideally, it is proper to take into account the view of the Raavad, cited by the Rashba, who writes that if he has additional liquids to drink he should spit out what is in his mouth rather than benefit without a blessing, as we wrote in Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 2. The other Rishonim did not mention his view, and therefore one should not protest against one who is lenient.
