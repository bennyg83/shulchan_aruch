One who put food or drink into his mouth without a blessing. It contains 2 seifim: If one forgot and put liquid into his mouth without reciting a blessing, he swallows it and does not recite the preceding blessing over it. {Rama: Some say that he does recite a blessing over it, and this appears to be the primary ruling. [Rashba in the name of Ra'avad; Rosh, chapter 3 of Sheloshah SheAchlu; and Rambam, chapter 8 of the Laws of Blessings].}
