(S.k. 7) For lighting, etc. He cited it as appears below in siman 675, se'if 1, that if he lit it inside and brought it outside, he has not fulfilled the obligation for this reason. But as a matter of law, I do not know the practical difference between the reason the Shulchan Aruch gave, that lighting performs the mitzvah and we require lighting in its place, and the reason of the Maharshal, that people will say it is for his own need, etc. If he lit it below twenty, took it from there, and placed it elsewhere within twenty, according to both reasons he fulfills the obligation, for the lighting was in its place, since both places are valid and are called its place; and in such a case we also do not say that people will say it is for his own need, etc., as the Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 263, s.k. 23; see there.
