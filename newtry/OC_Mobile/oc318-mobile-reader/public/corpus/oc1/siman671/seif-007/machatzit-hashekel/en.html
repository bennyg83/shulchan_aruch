(S.k. 7) The kindling, etc. — he cited it as proven below siman 675 seif 1: if kindled inside and took outside — not fulfilled from that reason; but for law I do not know practical difference between reason Shulchan Aruch gave kindling l'shem mitzvah and requires kindling in its place, and Maharshal's reason they will say for his need, etc., and if he kindled below twenty and removed from there and placed elsewhere within twenty — for both reasons he fulfills, for both places are valid called "place," and no need in such case for his need, etc., as Magen Avraham wrote above siman 663 s.k. 23 — see there; see below siman 675 Magen Avraham s.k. 3.
