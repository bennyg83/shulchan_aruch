On the southern wall. As a remembrance of the Menorah, and also because of the arrangement, as written in the gloss, 'and he arranges them,' etc. In Terumas HaDeshen he wrote that this is because we hold that the law follows Rabbi Yehudah against his colleague, and Rashi also holds this way, as written at the beginning of chapter 2 of Shabbos and in his Torah commentary. But the Rambam ruled like his teacher regarding the table, and like Rabbi Elazar bar Shimon regarding the Menorah. This is also the view of the Semag. His reason is that we hold that the law follows Rabbi Yehudah against his colleague, and the anonymous Mishnah in Menachos there is also like Rabbi Yehudah, as Tosafos wrote there on folio 96a, s.v. 'all,' and 98b, s.v. 'east,' etc. The Rambam ruled there like the Mishnah and copied it there in halachah 8. But regarding the Menorah he ruled like Rabbi Elazar bar Shimon because the sugya in Megillah 21b follows him. However, the Raavad there objected to him and wrote that the Mishnah in Tamid is like Rabbi Yehudah. So too Tosafos wrote in Menachos 98b, s.v. 'from the fact that it is written,' etc. The Rambam in his commentary there wrote that we do not hold like that Mishnah.
