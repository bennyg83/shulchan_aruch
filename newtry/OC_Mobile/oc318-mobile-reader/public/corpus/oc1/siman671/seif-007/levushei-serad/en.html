Magen Avraham, s.k. 7 For one who sees says, etc. This reason is effective so that even according to the one who says placement performs the mitzvah, it is invalid. However, we hold that lighting performs the mitzvah, and therefore I do not know why the Yam Shel Shlomo needed his own reason. The book is not before me. See the Taz, siman 645, s.k. 2.
