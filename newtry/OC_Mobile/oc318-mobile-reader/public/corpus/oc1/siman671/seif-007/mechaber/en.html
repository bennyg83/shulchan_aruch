It is a mitzvah to place it within the tefach adjacent to the entrance, on the left, so that the mezuzah is on the right and the Chanukah lamp is on the left. If there is no mezuzah in the entrance, he places it on the right. If he placed it in the doorway itself, he should place it from the midpoint of the entrance toward the left. Gloss: However, nowadays, when everyone lights inside and there is no recognition at all for the people of the public domain, one need not be so concerned if they do not light within the tefach adjacent to the entrance. Nevertheless, the custom is to light within the tefach adjacent to the entrance as in their days, and one should not change, unless the members of the household are many, in which case it is better for each one to light in a distinct place than to mix the lamps together so there is no recognition of how many lamps they are lighting. Nevertheless, he should be careful not to light in the place where lamps are lit all year, for then there will be no recognition at all. Although there is recognition only for the members of the household, at least some recognition is required. In the synagogue one places it on the southern wall, or like the lamps of the Menorah, arranging them from east to west. They light and recite the blessings in the synagogue because of publicizing the miracle. Gloss: A person does not fulfill the obligation with the lamps of the synagogue, and must go back and light in his home. The custom is to light in the synagogue between Minchah and Maariv. Some have the custom to light on erev Shabbos before Minchah. If they want to hurry to pray after the prayer leader recited the blessing and lit one of them, the shamash may light the remaining ones, and the prayer leader may pray.
