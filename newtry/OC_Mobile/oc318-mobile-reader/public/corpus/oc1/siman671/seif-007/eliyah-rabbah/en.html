[7] Which are called lampa, etc. Those who are meticulous are careful not to light in candelabras, because they do not beautify the Chanukah lamp; Maharshal. It is obvious that he is speaking of our candelabras, which are completely permitted, but he says only that there is no beautification in them. The Kenesses HaGedolah understood that he is speaking of other candelabras, but this is not so, as written in the Shelah, folio 249, and in Mateh Moshe.
