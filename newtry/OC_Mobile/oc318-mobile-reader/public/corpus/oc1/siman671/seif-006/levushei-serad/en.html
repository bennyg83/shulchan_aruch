Taz, s.k. 5 For if not, even if the s'chach is below twenty, it should be invalid, etc., if the walls do not reach the s'chach. Perforce, the airspace below twenty does not harm. If so, even above twenty, why must the walls reach specifically to the s'chach? It should only be necessary that there be hanging partitions from the s'chach until twenty amos from the ground. This is easy to understand.
