(6) [Seif 2] How many candles one kindles, etc. In the Gemara (Shabbat 21b) it states: The Sages taught — the mitzvah of Chanukah is one candle for a man and his household; those who are mehaderin kindle a candle for each and every one; those who are mehaderin from among the mehaderin — Beit Hillel say: on the first day one kindles one, from there onward one adds and continues. Tosafot explain that this refers to "a candle for a man and his household" — meaning all members of the household have only one candle on the first night; consequently when they see two candles in the house on the second night they will sense the addition of the miracle. But for those who kindle on the first night a candle for each member of the household — if on the second night they kindle two for each one, there is no recognition here of the addition of the miracle, for they will say there are many household members and each has only one candle there. This is the view of Tur and Shulchan Aruch. But Rambam explained that even for those who kindle a candle for each one, they make for each one two candles on the second night, and so on until eight — and this is the second view that Rama wrote in the gloss. And here there is a novelty in custom: Sephardim practice like Tosafot and Ashkenazim like Rambam, which we do not find in other places. Taz s.k. 1. And this is not a novelty, for Rambam himself (chapter 4 of Chanukah) wrote that they practice thus — and not as some wrote regarding the law per se, see there; if so, this is an early custom.
