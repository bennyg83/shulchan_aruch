One places it higher than three tefachim, and it is a mitzvah to place it lower than ten tefachim. If he placed it higher than ten tefachim, he has fulfilled the obligation; but if he places it higher than twenty amos, he has not fulfilled the obligation. Gloss: Even if he took it while it was already lit and placed it lower than twenty, he has not fulfilled the obligation, for lighting performs the mitzvah.
