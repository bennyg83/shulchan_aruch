[2] [Levush] And it is not applicable to say, etc. Meaning, it is difficult: let us say as we say in Shabbos, that one who is careful with the lamp will himself have sons who are Torah scholars, etc. The Bach answered there that it is not applicable here to say, 'if he himself is not worthy of this, his words should be heard like a Torah scholar,' because only there is it measure for measure. It appears to me that the verse, 'For a mitzvah is a lamp,' perforce is primarily stated regarding the Shabbos lamp, which is according to the Torah, and we hold that the main mitzvah of Shabbos lighting is with women. It is not applicable to say that she will be a Torah scholar, for anyone who teaches Torah, etc.
