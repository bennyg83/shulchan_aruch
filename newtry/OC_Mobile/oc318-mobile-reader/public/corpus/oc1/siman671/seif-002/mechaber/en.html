How many lamps does one kindle? On the first night he kindles 1; from then onward he adds 1 each night, until on the final night there are 8. Even if there are many members of the household, they should not kindle more. {Rama: Some say that each member of the household should kindle. [Rambam] This is the widespread custom. Each person should be careful to place his lamps in a distinct place so that the number of lamps being kindled is recognizable. [Mahara of Prague]}
