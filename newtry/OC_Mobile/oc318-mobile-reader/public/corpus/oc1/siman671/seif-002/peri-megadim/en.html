And from the "cracks there's no light in Lampin even in our rods and in the fire brought a stack of glass and endings he'd seen Likin in them and I mean if you say that your son is a milk candle and a cough within a partition of glass or soak oil:

Layman writer in rabbi's Hagahah words: I saw to explain a little what he wrote; not in circle Darkei Moshe wrote as in synagogue they nail pegs — if meaning nail pegs in wall in circle or around one round board nail pegs in circle — howsoever some difference between candlestick and this — one could say; see Peri Chadash. See Mishna Berurah wrote each separated from fellow not called bonfire; here wrote much separated; Terumat HaDeshen 105 22 not like candlestick where each reed separated by partition; also Ba'al HaItur in bowl two fingers permitted, here far apart — see there. Mechaber forbids in bowl even far apart like Tur; Mishna Berurah silent concedes; except candlestick stringent: reeds must be separated and far more than two fingers — here lacking one: not separated by partitions or separated but reeds not two fingers apart — forbidden as bonfire. Therefore requires study when gluing wax candles to wall not two fingers apart — forbidden; Eliyah Rabbah Tur finger, Terumat HaDeshen two fingers — see Magen Avraham 4 will explain further.
