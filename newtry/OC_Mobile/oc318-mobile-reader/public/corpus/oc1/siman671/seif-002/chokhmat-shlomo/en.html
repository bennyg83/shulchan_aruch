Se'if 2. How many lamps does one light on the first night? He lights one, etc. Note: If on the first night he lit two lamps, whether he has fulfilled the obligation or not, it appears in my humble opinion that he has fulfilled it, for an addition to the matter does not harm in such a case, as the Rama wrote in siman 263 in the laws of Shabbos, that one may add beyond the intended count provided he does not subtract; see there. The same applies here, and analyze carefully.
