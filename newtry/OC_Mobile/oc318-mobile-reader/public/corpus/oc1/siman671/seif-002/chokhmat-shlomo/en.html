(Same, seif 6) But if he places it above twenty amot he has not fulfilled, etc. NB: See Tur and Taz dispute whether walls reaching ceiling helps — R' Yehuda HaLevi holds it helps, Tur disputes — see there. In my humble opinion proof for Tur from Gemara language source of this law chapter 2 Shabbat: Hanukkah light placed above twenty amot is invalid like sukkah and like alley — one should deduce on this lengthy wording: it should say briefly invalid, and one of sukkah or like alley alone suffices — why both like sukkah and like alley? Intent: we hold like Rava sukkah reason from temporary dwelling required; therefore invalid even if walls reach sechach as we hold Shulchan Aruch siman 633; therefore R' Tanchum from whom this law is sourced holds like Rava: in sukkah invalid in every respect even if walls reach sechach — therefore had to cite both: if only like alley, one might think since reason eye does not dominate, if walls reach ceiling valid even for Hanukkah — therefore teaches invalid like sukkah: as in sukkah invalid even walls reach sechach, so here invalid even walls reach ceiling. If only sukkah cited, we would not know invalid reason — in sukkah if walls reach sechach eye dominates, only invalid from other reason temporary dwelling required — then in Hanukkah that reason does not apply, why invalid if walls reach ceiling? Therefore said like alley where reason eye does not dominate — teaches well invalid in sukkah even walls reach ceiling. Alley reason: eye does not dominate — even walls reach ceiling applies — Tur's words and great support for Tur's view — examine. I was uncertain: if he placed the light partly below twenty and partly above twenty, as wax candle — fulfilled or not? In sukkah siman 633 and alley siman 363 seif 26 we hold valid, as Gemara concludes Eruvin 3b: hollow sukkah we learned, hollow alley we learned — here what? It appears to resolve from Gemara language: Hanukkah light placed above twenty amot invalid like sukkah and like alley — why this length — suffice "placed above twenty invalid," and silence implies like sukkah and alley hollow sukkah and hollow alley; if we deepen: should cite only one invalid like sukkah or invalid like alley; one could say sukkah cited to exclude Ra'avyah Tur brought who holds walls reaching ceiling valid for Hanukkah above twenty — Tur disputes; therefore for us who hold sukkah invalid from permanent dwelling — sukkah invalid even walls reach sechach siman 633 — teaches invalid like sukkah even reaching ceiling; what cites alley: as we hold above siman 633 sechach above twenty and projections descend within twenty — if subtract above, shadow majority valid, else invalid; Taz there brought Ran: partial sechach above twenty and partial within twenty valid — implies even without majority shadow within twenty only projections reduce — see there; in my humble opinion not so — only in sukkah we require majority shadow; what Gemara did not explain — relied on explicit sugya regarding projections; in crossbeam appears well: did not distinguish whether that part could stand alone or not — therefore no support here — appears permits even if could not stand alone if top removed; reason: crossbeam since essentially sufficient by majority because of recognition, only it would fall — what difference whether standing by something; now not falling, main validation here it is below and becomes like iron spit; but in sechach main validation only by combination above — if not enough below, does not help — such is reasoning. Therefore in Hanukkah if part of light within twenty and part above twenty, even if below not enough alone, with combination above valid like alley not like sukkah — therefore cited sukkah for walls reaching ceiling not helping, and alley for part within twenty part above; further distinction alley versus sukkah: sukkah temporary dwelling — what if part within twenty, eventually top above is invalid, no combination; crossbeam recognition — if part within twenty and sees by it, sight governs also above — therefore above also valid — very sound. Therefore Hanukkah too recognition — valid if part within twenty part above — this too is alley; cited sukkah walls reaching ceiling not helping even recognition — Tur's view; cited sukkah for us walls reaching sechach not helping; cited alley part within part above. While engaged I greatly wonder at poskim who copied this Gemara language "Hanukkah light placed above twenty invalid" — wondrous, for in Eruvin Gemara concludes hollow sukkah we learned hollow alley we learned — if sechach above twenty and crossbeam likewise valid if hollow only twenty — there they cited body of sukkah and alley themselves above twenty — then invalid when crossbeam and sechach above twenty amot; but here Hanukkah they cited law on body of light placed above twenty invalid — why? behold if hollow only twenty valid as sukkah and alley, and there too valid thus; one who says so would think sukkah and alley invalid even if hollow only twenty; but for us who hold valid — should not cite this language, only "if hollow under light above twenty invalid." Perhaps since in Hanukkah main publicity is light — if crossbeam and sechach from bottom to sechach reaches twenty valid; but Hanukkah since light has some length and main publicity is light — all body above twenty, light is above above twenty — as if hollow body more than twenty — examine, still requires study.
