(There, in Magen Avraham, s.k. 9) From east to west. Note: See Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chaim, siman 186. After he discussed the entire subtle matter there very carefully, he concluded with this language: Therefore one should not change from his custom, that they place the menorah with the face of the lamps toward the southern wall, and the person lighting faces north with his back to the south, and lights.
