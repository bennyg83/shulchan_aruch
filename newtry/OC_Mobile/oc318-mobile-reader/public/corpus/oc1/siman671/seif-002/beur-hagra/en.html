Se'if 2, and even many. Tosafos explain that it does not refer to the mehadrin practice, for if so there would be no recognition. So too the Rambam wrote that this is the custom.
And some say. So wrote the Rambam as practical law. It appears that this is the view of the Rif, from the fact that he brought the statement of Rabbah bar bar Chanah in the name of Rabbi Yochanan, regarding the two elders, etc., corresponding to the bulls of the festival, etc., that one ascends in sanctity and does not, etc. According to this reason, we are not concerned for the recognition of the incoming or outgoing days that Tosafos wrote there. So too it is implied in the Gemara there: one said, etc., and one said the reason, etc., for there is a practical difference in such a case, as appears to me. The conclusion of the two elders is like the one who says that we ascend in sanctity, etc.
And so is the custom. In the Darkei Moshe he wrote in the name of Maharam Padua that for us, who light inside and know how many members of the household there are, even Tosafos would agree. Furthermore, since we light inside, there is no need to be so concerned for the tefach adjacent to the entrance, as written in se'if 7 in the gloss, and each one can light in a distinct place, and there is recognition. This is what he wrote: 'and they should be careful,' etc.; see there in the gloss.
