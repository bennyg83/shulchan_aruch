(S.k. 8) From its midpoint, etc. In the second half, etc. It implies that that entire half is valid for placement, and so it is in the Mordechai cited by the Beis Yosef. See the Taz and Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 227.
Nevertheless, the custom, etc. This does not refer to the above; rather, it is a new se'if katan and refers to what the Rama wrote in the gloss: nevertheless, the custom is to light within a tefach, etc.
