The statement of Rav Huna, and as Rava explains there, folio 23.
That which appears above in siman 90, se'if 8, that where a synagogue has two entrances there is no suspicion, is different there: since if he does not pray, it is as though he casts off the yoke of Heaven, people will not come to suspect him. Alternatively, here is different, because there is financial loss involved, so they will suspect him more; Beis Yosef.
