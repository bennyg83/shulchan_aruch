8) There: Thereafter he continually adds, etc. One who does not have much oil should place the required measure in one lamp and divide the remainder among the others. If he divides it equally among all of them, not even one will burn for the required period. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur; Chayei Adam, klal 154, paragraph 25; Mishna Berurah, paragraph 6. One who lacks enough oil for all eight nights of Chanukah, and has only enough for one night’s kindling, should not divide the oil among all the nights merely to publicize the miracle each night. Instead, he should kindle the required measure on the first night. If nothing remains for the later nights, this is of no concern; the verse speaks of one who possesses the means. Sedei HaAretz, volume 3, responsa 34 and 35.
