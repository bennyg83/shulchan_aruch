And they recite a blessing. See the Taz. It implies that on the other evenings of Chanukah, when one does not recite Shehecheyanu, the mourner may light the Chanukah lamp. The mourner may pray Minchah and Maariv on Chanukah, and Shacharis until Baruch She'amar. On the fifteenth of Av, the fifteenth of Shevat, and Lag BaOmer, when there is no Hallel, one can say that the mourner may pray Shacharis as well. On Chol HaMoed, which is like Yom Tov, I sometimes prevented allowing the mourner to pray Minchah and Maariv. In siman 131 we wrote about this; see siman 581 in the laws of the prayer leader, and with Hashem's help it will be explained further there.
