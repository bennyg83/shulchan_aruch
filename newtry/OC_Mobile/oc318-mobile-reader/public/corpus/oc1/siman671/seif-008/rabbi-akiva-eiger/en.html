Se'if 4 in the gloss That each lamp is greatly separated from the other. Two fingerbreadths, and separated by partitions; Terumas HaDeshen. The Maharshal in siman 85 wrote that if they are very far apart, without overturning a vessel, it counts as one lamp and not as several lamps.
Se'if 8 One of them is enough for him. For we are not concerned about guests; Gemara. See the Magen Avraham above, siman 244, s.k. 6, and see Maharit, part 2, Yoreh Deah, siman 18.
