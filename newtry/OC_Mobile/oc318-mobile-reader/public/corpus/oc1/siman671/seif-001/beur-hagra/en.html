Se'if 1, one must, etc. For we say there: one who is careful with the lamp, etc. Rashi explained there, s.v. 'sons,' that this refers to the Shabbos and Chanukah lamp. So too the Tur wrote.
And even a poor person, etc. For we say in chapter Arvei Pesachim: they may not give him fewer than, etc., even from the charity plate. The Rashbam explained: he asks, or, etc. And we say in the Gemara: from the charity plate is obvious; it was needed only for Rabbi Akiva, etc. Tanna Devei Eliyahu taught: although they said, 'make,' etc. Thus, for a small thing in honor of Shabbos one must ask at the doors; he asks or sells, as appears to me. And we say there that kiddush takes precedence over honor of the day and honor of the night, and we say in Shabbos there that the Chanukah lamp takes precedence over kiddush of the day. This proves that he sells, etc.
