And in a time. See the Taz, Tosafos in Kirah, folio 45a, s.v. 'before the Chavrei,' in the name of Ri, and in Bameh Madlikin, folio 21b, s.v. 'and in a time of danger,' in the name of Ri, like Rashi's explanation, of blessed memory, regarding the Persians. See the Bach, who wrote that if so, he should not place it even on the table because of the danger, unlike if it is a decree, etc.; see there. See the Pri Chadash, who wrote that from Tosafos in Kirah it implies that the Persians allow placing outside; see there. The language of the Taz is uncertain. See siman 656 regarding an esrog, that for a positive mitzvah involving money one should not spend more than one fifth, and the same applies to a Chanukah lamp if there is monetary punishment; see Magen Avraham there, letter 7, one tenth of one's assets. What Tosafos wrote in Kirah, that they were not particular about what was on the table, is difficult, for in Gittin it is proven that they were particular even about what was on the table. One can say that they would leave one lamp, not two lamps. In my novellae on Shabbos we wrote about this.
