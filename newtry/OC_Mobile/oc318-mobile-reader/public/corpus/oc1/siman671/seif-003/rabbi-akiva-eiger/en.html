Se'if 7, Be'er HaGolah Even though it is only a custom. So it is in the Rivash, that this is similar to Hallel on Rosh Chodesh. In Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 87, he challenged this: the Mechaber holds in siman 422 that one does not recite a blessing on Hallel of Rosh Chodesh, for he wrote there that they do not have this custom in all the kingdom of Eretz Yisrael and its surroundings. If so, how did he write here without qualification like the Rivash? In the kingdom of Eretz Yisrael, where they do not recite a blessing on Hallel of Rosh Chodesh, it would be proper that they not recite a blessing on lighting the lamp in the synagogue; see there.
