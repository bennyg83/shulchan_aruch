Magen Avraham, s.k. 2 As written in the gloss, etc., that each person must place his lamps in a distinct place. According to this, this law of the Shulchan Aruch, that it counts for two, applies to one person from the first night onward, for this is the explanation according to Tosafos, which is the view of the Mechaber in the previous se'if: that only one lamp is lit for all members of the household.
