Se'if 3, for two. The Tur wrote 'for two lamps' from the second night onward, according to his view, for he wrote like the opinion of Tosafos in se'if 2, 'and even many,' etc. Therefore he did not explain it like Rashi's explanation, regarding mehadrin, since we practice like mehadrin min hamehadrin. He also did not explain it like Tosafos' explanation, because we light inside. But the Shulchan Aruch, which in se'if 5 copied the language of the Gemara and Tosafos, wrote 'for two,' like the language of the Gemara.
