[4] [Levush] Two people, etc. Meaning, for mehadrin; so Rashi explained. It appears to me that he holds that two people who are involved in the obligation itself, such as two houses, do not fulfill the obligation with this; see s.k. 9. With this a great difficulty in the Tur is resolved, for he wrote, 'such as on the second night,' and the Beis Yosef explained that this is because he ruled that on the first night all members of the household light one lamp. End quote. This is difficult, for he still should have explained it regarding the first night and two houses, as Tosafos explained in Shabbos, folio 21, s.v. 'the mitzvah,' etc. Rather, they do not fulfill the obligation, unlike the Olas Shabbos, who wrote that the words of Tosafos escaped the Beis Yosef. The Magen Avraham wrote: For us, who add each night, two people should not light the first lamp, for then there will be no recognition of how many lamps they are lighting. End quote. In my humble opinion, on the first night recognition is not needed.
