(S.k. 4) That one should not, etc. It implies that with twisted candles, etc., in such a case there is no concern that they will heat up and fall.
And in Mahariv, etc. 'And they attach,' etc., means that they are braided. It is not specifically braided; the same law applies if they are actually attached together, for its law is the same as braided. He came only to exclude that we should not explain 'and they attach,' etc., as what was written above in the name of Or Zarua, that they attach them close to one another. But if they are actually attached together and become one, its law is like braided candles.
And I forbade them. It implies that even twisted candles are forbidden, unlike what is implied above from the Or Zarua, that they are permitted. Although on Shabbos and Yom Tov it is a mitzvah with twisted candles, perforce one must distinguish regarding twisted candles between Shabbos and Chanukah.
And if so, perhaps two, etc. From the fact that Mahariv mentioned four and five, and also according to this Chanukah will be equal to Shabbos and Yom Tov, where there is a mitzvah with twisted candles, for on Shabbos one twists only two candles.
But, etc. That it is forbidden because it is like a torch. So too it is implied in siman 298 regarding Havdalah, where the mitzvah is with a torch, and he wrote that two twisted candles are a torch. In siman 433, where one may not search for chametz by the light of a torch, he forbids two twisted candles; perforce they are a torch.
