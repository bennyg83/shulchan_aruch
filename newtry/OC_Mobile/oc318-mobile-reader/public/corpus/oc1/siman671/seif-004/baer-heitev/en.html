Covered. He must cover it with a vessel before he lights. For if he lights it and afterward covers it with a vessel, the lamps had been burning previously as bonfires and there was no mitzvah in them at all; through covering with the vessel it was rectified to become like lamps, and they must be for the sake of the mitzvah at the time they are lit; Mahara Halevi in Sefer Gan HaMelech, siman 151.
Evenly. And not one protruding inward and one protruding outward; Darkei Moshe and Maharil.
Lampa. The Maharshal wrote: One must hang it within the tefach adjacent to the entrance, and begin with the middle lamp, which is nearest the entrance, and turn toward the right. Those who are meticulous do not light in a lampa, for it has no beautification for the Chanukah lamp. End quote.
