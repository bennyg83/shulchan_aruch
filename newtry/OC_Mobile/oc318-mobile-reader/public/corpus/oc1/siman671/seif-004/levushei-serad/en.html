Shulchan Aruch, se'if 5 That he has no entrance open to the public domain, etc. And he also has no entrance open to the courtyard, but it opens into the house, for now, if he places it at the entrance of the house, it is not recognizable that it is because of the upper story. Therefore he places it in the window adjacent to the public domain; so wrote the Beis Yosef.
