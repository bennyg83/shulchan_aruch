And one needs. See the Taz, and so the Magen Avraham wrote in his name, letter 5. See siman 673, letters 4 and 5, for certainly one benefits more when there are many lamps; see there. In the Rambam, of blessed memory, chapter 4, halachah 8: in a time of danger he places it inside, and even on his table is enough, and another lamp is needed inside his house to use its light. Meaning: even if he placed it inside near the entrance and window, where it is recognizable as a Chanukah lamp, nevertheless another lamp is needed in order to use its light. This is what he wrote, 'inside,' and also 'table,' to equate them. See the Levush and Eliyah Rabbah about this. Now, when Rava said that another lamp is needed, one can say this applies even to an important person who always uses two lamps on his table and the like, as ministers do; if so, even two lamps would not be a recognition on the first night. Therefore he said that another lamp is needed so that he not use the light of Chanukah, even though there will be no recognition, and another lamp is sufficient. With Hashem's help, in siman 673 in the Magen Avraham and Taz, s.v., and below in the Taz, letter 11, and Magen Avraham, letter 14, this will be explained further.
