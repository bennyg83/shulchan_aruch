If one filled a bowl with oil and surrounded it with wicks, if he covered it with a vessel, each wick counts as one lamp; if he did not cover it with a vessel, it does not count even as one lamp, because it is like a bonfire. Gloss: Therefore one should be careful to place the lamps in a straight, even row and not in a circle, for that is like a bonfire. It is permitted to light in candelabras called lampas, since each lamp is greatly separated from the other. When making candles, even of wax, one should be careful not to attach them together and light them, for that is like a bonfire. Even with Shabbos and Yom Tov candles one should be careful not to do this.
