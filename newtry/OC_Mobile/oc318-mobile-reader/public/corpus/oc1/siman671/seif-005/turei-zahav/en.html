And in a time of danger, when he is not permitted to fulfill the mitzvah. This is the explanation of Tosafos in chapter Kirah. But Rashi explained in Bava Metzia that the danger was on a Persian festival day, when they did not allow any lamp except in their idolatrous worship. For if so, it would be difficult: what helps by placing it on the table? Tosafos in Bava Metzia were pressed to say that they did not search so thoroughly in the houses. But according to this explanation it works well that he places it on the table, for there was no noticeable sign in the matter that it was done for a mitzvah.
And another lamp is needed, etc. Although even without danger another lamp is also needed, since it is forbidden to use their light, he teaches us here that you should not say that since perforce he benefits from them, as they are placed on the table, it is therefore entirely permitted. Rather, in any event, another lamp is needed for recognition. So explained the Ran.
