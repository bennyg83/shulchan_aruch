One places the Chanukah lamp outside, at the entrance adjacent to the public domain. If the house opens to the public domain, he places it at his entrance. If there is a courtyard in front of the house, he places it at the entrance to the courtyard. If he lives in an upper story that has no entrance open to the public domain, he places it in the window adjacent to the public domain. In a time of danger, when he is not permitted to fulfill the mitzvah, he places it on his table, and this is enough. He needs another lamp by whose light to use. If there is a bonfire, he does not need another lamp; but if he is an important person, whose way is not to use the light of a bonfire, he needs another lamp.
