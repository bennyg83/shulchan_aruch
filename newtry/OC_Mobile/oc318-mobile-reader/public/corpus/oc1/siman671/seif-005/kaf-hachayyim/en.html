Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, challenges this ruling and leaves it requiring examination; see there. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 2, likewise writes that the words of Olas Tamid do not appear correct and that he is obligated to hire himself out just as for the four cups; see there. Erech HaShulchan, paragraph 1, writes similarly and proves from Responsa Rivash, siman 111, that no poor person is exempt; see there. Raviah rules likewise.
