(2) And one needs another candle to use for the light - P. Rashi because of the Kira and Ral that even if he doesn't want to use the light at all, he needs another candle that he has the ability to use for the light, and Iz Miner that the first candle is for the mitzvah of the the Master, since it is standing on the table, they will say that it needs to be lit. What kind of lamp is it used for? It is indeed the custom of the world, as well as the custom of our sages, that every Hanukkah candle that is lit inside the house needs a candle, otherwise it would not even have to be used unless it is said that it should be lit. And look at the end of the sign of the 18th century in the Shield of Avraham, even if he has only one candle [and he does not have another candle to light the sun for a light, he does not have] MM, he will light the candle and bless it. And since MM, he must be careful not to use it in front of her.
