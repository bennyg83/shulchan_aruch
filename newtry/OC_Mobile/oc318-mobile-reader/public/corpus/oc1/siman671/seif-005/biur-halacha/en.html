(א) The door of the courtyard - R.L. and not as the mind of the Rashidover rejected the open door to the courtyard. And in the light of the old arm, they were called the heir.

And one needs another light to use for its illumination — Rashi explains because of recognition; meaning even if he does not want at all to use its light, nevertheless he needs another light so he has ability to use its light, and thereby it is recognizable the first is for mitzvah — otherwise since it stands on the table they will say he kindled for his need. It appears specifically when placing on his table; but all who place near the entrance do not need another light even though it stands for him, since he does not come to use its light for any use. However world custom and our rabbis' custom: for all Hanukkah lights kindled inside the house, another light is required even if no use needed, lest they say he kindled for his need; so I practice — so Meiri — see there; see Peri Megadim that responsa of Rama also implies thus; see end siman 678 s.k. 3 Magen Avraham: even one who has only one light [and no other to kindle to use its light] — nevertheless he kindles and blesses on it; nevertheless l'chatchila certainly be careful not to use beforehand.
