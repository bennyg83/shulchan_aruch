Since he is already lying — see Mishnah Berurah; and behold, all this is only per the opinion of the gloss in the anonymous [view], following the method of Rabbeinu Yona; but in Beit Yosef he ruled like the poskim who disagree with R. Yona and hold that even lechat-chilah one may lie on one's side and read, for lying on one's actual side is not called a way of pride. And behold for practical halakhah it appears from the words of Eshel Torah and Taz and Magen Avraham that one should adopt [the view] of Beit Yosef, and so ruled Magen Gibborim. However in Sefer Ateret Tzvi he ruled like Rama, and so in Mishnat Zekenim he sides that the law is with Rama, and so in the novellae of R. Akiva Eiger he sides to say that to be stringent one should rule like Rabbeinu Yona — see there. Therefore certainly one should be stringent lechat-chilah.

Inclines a little to his side and reads — see in Peri Megadim who wrote that if sitting and standing are difficult for them, they may even lechat-chilah go and lie a little on their side and read, if they cannot [read while lying] on their actual side.
