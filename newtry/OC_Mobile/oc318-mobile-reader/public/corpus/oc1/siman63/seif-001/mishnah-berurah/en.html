(א) Walking — see below in siman 63.

(ב) Prone — and even if he is leaning slightly on his side, it is also forbidden, because it appears as though he is accepting upon himself the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven in a manner of lordliness and arrogance.

(ג) "Since he was already lying" — meaning: he removed everything that was on him, and there is bother to stand and to put them on again; but if he is lying in his shirt, he must stand; and see in Be'er Heitev Levush.

(ד) "And is not able," etc. — but if he can [recite] on his side, even only the first verse alone, he is obligated to do so; and all the more so if he can recite it sitting.
