And it is burdensome to stand. This means that it is burdensome for him to put his clothing back on. Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah and Lechem Chamudot, unlike Levush. This implies that if he is lying in his robe, he must stand. This also appears from the Gemara's statement that one lying supine may not recite Keriat Shema, implying that even if he is already lying down he may not recite until he stands. Beit Yosef, however, maintains that this applies specifically when he is lying on his back and tilts slightly onto his side. If he lies fully on his side, he may initially recite in that position. Beit Yosef therefore omits the view of Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah, and this also appears to be the plain meaning of the Talmud.
