And there is exertion to stand. Explanation: that the trouble is to return and dress his garments [students of R. Yona and Lechem Chamudot, not like Levush] — it implies that if he lies in his shirt he must stand; and so in the Gemara "prone, one does not read Keriat Shema" — it implies that even though he already lies he should not read until he stands. However, the opinion of Rabbeinu is specifically when cast on his back and inclining a little to his side; but when lying actually on his side it is permitted lechat-chilah to read — therefore he omitted the opinion of the students of R. Yona; and so is the plain meaning of the Talmud.
