Siman 61, 'one reads,' etc. — Mishnah 10:2 and Gemara 11:1, 'the rabbis taught,' etc.

Or riding, etc. — Rambam that he is not inferior to one walking, and it is included in 'in your going on the way'; and see above siman 94, se'if 4.

But not so. See Tosafot Niddah 14a, entry 'efrakid,' etc.; and Rambam took both explanations stringently — Hagahot Maimoniyot.

But one may read, etc. — Rambam; and so Rashi there and Tosafot there, entry 'Keriat Shema,' from what is stated in Berakhot 24a, 'years,' etc.; and so Terumat Yeri in the name of R' Yonah; and as written 'they tilt and read,' and they wrote specifically when one is already lying and removed one's clothes because of effort — and as they said regarding one who is very heavyset, the same here; and this is the meaning in the gloss 'since,' etc.; and he argues on Shulchan Arukh.
