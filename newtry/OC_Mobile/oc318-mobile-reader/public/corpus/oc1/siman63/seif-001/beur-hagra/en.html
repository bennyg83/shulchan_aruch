Seif 1, one reads, etc. Mishnah 10b and Gemara 11a, ‘The Sages taught,’ etc.
Or riding, etc. Rambam, for it is no worse than walking, and it is included in ‘when you go on the way.’ See above, siman 94, seif 4.
But not, etc. See Tosafot, Niddah 14a, s.v. ‘aparkid,’ etc. The Rambam adopted both explanations stringently; Hagahot Maimoniyot.
But he may read, etc. Rambam, and so Rashi there wrote, and Tosafot there, s.v. ‘Krias Shema,’ from what is stated in Berachos 24a, ‘two,’ etc. So too Rabbeinu Yonah wrote in the name of some. This is as stated, ‘they recline and read.’ They wrote that this applies specifically when he is already lying down and has removed his clothing, because of the burden, as they said, ‘he was a heavyset man.’ The same applies here. This is what the gloss means by ‘since,’ etc., and it disagrees with the Shulchan Aruch.
