Magen Avraham, note 1 — and in Levush "his garments" — it implies seemingly that for students of R. Yona there is no distinction between inclining a little on his side or lying actually on his side. And accordingly there is a leniency — if he is ill he can turn on his side but cannot sit without a bed. For students of R. Yona he need not turn on his side completely and may read while inclining a little to his side, since there is no distinction between inclining or actually on his side. But per the author he must turn on his side. And what Rama wrote so much is because this one is concerned for stringency and we adopt two stringencies.
