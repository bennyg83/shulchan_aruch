Magen Avraham, note 1: prone — one does not read. And the Gemara says on it that even while praying it is forbidden; and the explanation of "while praying" is inclining on one's actual side — permitted, for Tur holds that even while lying actually on one's side it is forbidden to read, and it was only permitted where he already lies naked and because of exertion, as Rama emended. But the opinion of Rabbeinu [Yosef Caro] is like Rambam: "while praying" means as Rashi explained — lying on his back and inclining a little to his side as Rashi explained there; but if he lies actually on his side it is permitted to read — therefore he omitted the words of Tur. With this understand the words of Magen Avraham and Taz.
