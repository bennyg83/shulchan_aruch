Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. One may not recite while lying supine. The Gemara states concerning this that it is forbidden even when one is tilted. The explanation of "tilted" is as follows: lying fully upon one's side is permitted according to one view, whereas the students of Rabbeinu Yonah hold that even lying fully upon one's side is forbidden for recitation. It was permitted only where the person is already lying naked, because of the burden involved, as Rama emends the text. Beis Yosef, however, follows Rambam and understands "tilted" as Rashi explains it there: he lies upon his back but is tilted slightly onto his side. If he lies fully upon his side, he may recite. Beis Yosef therefore omits the words of the students of Rabbeinu Yonah. With this, you will understand the words of Magen Avraham and Taz.
