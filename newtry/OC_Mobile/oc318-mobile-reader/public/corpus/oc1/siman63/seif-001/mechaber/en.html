[The Laws regarding the requirement] to sit at the time of the recitation of the Shema and to not
sleep [during it] — and it contains 9 se'ifim. One may recite it walking, standing, lying, riding on the back of an animal, or sitting, but not prone, that is, with one's face into the ground or cast on one's back with one's face upwards; but one may recite while lying on one's side.
