The law of sitting during the recitation of Shema and not sleeping. It contains 9 seifim: One may recite it while walking, standing, lying down, riding upon an animal, or sitting, but not while lying face down or flat on his back with his face upward. He may recite while lying on his side. {Rama: This applies when he is already lying down and it would be burdensome to stand. [Rabbeinu Yonah, chapter Mi SheMeito]} If he is very heavy and cannot turn onto his side, or if he is ill, he should incline himself slightly to the side and recite it.
