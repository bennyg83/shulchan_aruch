But one may read while lying on one's side, since one is already lying and there is effort to stand — even though one lies in another garment, because one is leaning on one's side properly, and there is not all this pride and lordliness. And some say specifically when lying in bed and one removed one's clothes — then it is permitted to read Keriat Shema on one's side, because there is effort, since one removed one's clothes to return and put them on (Rav R. Yitzchak, chapter Mi shemeto). But if there is no effort, one should not read Keriat Shema while leaning on one's side (Beit Yosef). And some say it is forbidden to read on one's side only when lying prone (perakdan) and leaning a little on one's side; but leaning on one's side completely is permitted (thus lean the words of Rashi, Rambam, and Tur).
