And behold, from what he wrote "lechat-chilah" is not necessary — one may say when he already lay down he reads actually on his side, but to lie down lechat-chilah no; and from the language of the author he mentioned five ways — sitting; and Rashi mentioned sitting not riding; and Rambam, chapter 2 of Keriat Shema chapter 2 mentioned riding not sitting; and the author took this to say like sitting lechat-chilah so too leil arvit; and for a matter of mitzvah there is greater mitzvah in sitting (and so Pri Chadash as he wrote in se'if 2) — likewise lying too one may lechat-chilah.
