(1) [Se'if 1] One reads it walking or standing, etc. Nevertheless sitting is preferable so that one can read it in awe and with intention and with more settled mind; and so Hagahot Maimoniot in Laws of Keriat Shema, chapter 2, in the name of Raavyah on that which R. Yehudah [said] "upon your heart" while standing (Berakhot 13b); and all the more so that sitting is preferable. And Darchei Moshe brought it, letter aleph, Taz, Ruth 2; and see further what is written below, letter vav, and letter yod.
