(1) On his back, etc. Rashi explained in the chapter "He was reading" that perhaps his limb would stiffen during his sleep and he would appear to many; and from the aggadah there the reason appears to be because of [improper] thoughts. And on his stomach — Lechem Chamudot explained on folio 11 that it is degrading, for he appears to lie as one who uses his bed [for intercourse], and further lest he come to heat and emit seed in vain.
