And there is burden, etc. The Levush wrote that when one is actually lying on his side it is not a manner of arrogance. He combined two explanations, and this is not correct. According to the one who says it is not called a manner of arrogance, it is permitted even where there is no burden; and according to the one who says the reason is burden, they permitted only a case that involves burden, namely that he removed his clothing and it is burdensome to dress again. All this is clear from Rabbeinu Yonah and the Beis Yosef, and this is also the view of the Rama and Lechem Chamudos. Lechem Chamudos likewise explained the Rama’s words and challenged the Levush. It seems to me that the Levush learned this from the fact that the Rama wrote his words on the Shulchan Aruch, which follows the Rambam, who holds like the first reasoning, and did not write it as a dispute. He holds that even according to the first reasoning, although it is not a manner of arrogance, nevertheless it is not a manner of honor and is permitted only where there is burden. The practical difference is that according to the first reasoning, if he leans only somewhat on his side, even where there is burden it is forbidden, because it is a manner of arrogance, except for a heavyset person, for whom it is permitted even if he lies in his clothing. Rabbi Yehuda, who read and prayed while leaning, was lying in his clothing. With this the question of Olas Tamid on the Gemara is answered; see there. In any event, it is not difficult for one who examines it. According to the second reasoning, even one leaning somewhat on his side is permitted where there is burden, even if he is not heavyset, but the first reasoning is primary. One may also say that even according to the first reasoning, some burden is nevertheless required, meaning the burden of standing once he is already lying down, even though he is clothed. This is also the view of the Rambam, and so Nachalas Tzvi ruled. It appears that even if he can sit up, this is considered burden, and since he is already actually lying on his side, it is permitted. So too it implies in Kesef Mishneh there, and this is primary.
