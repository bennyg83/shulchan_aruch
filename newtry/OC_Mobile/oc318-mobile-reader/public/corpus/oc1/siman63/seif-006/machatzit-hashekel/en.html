(Seif katan 6) And he repeats, etc. That he reads quietly. See the Taz, who wrote another remedy, as follows: one may say that he waits some time and afterward returns and reads, etc. See Magen Avraham siman 61 seif katan 7, who wrote in the name of Shelah that those who say Shema in Selichot every time they say Vayaavor are permitted, since there is much interruption in between.
That he should read the whole paragraph twice; see what I wrote in siman 61 seif katan 8.
And it implies that if he remembered, etc. Since in seif 8 he wrote that he should read the paragraph twice, this implies that he had already read the whole paragraph and nevertheless should again read the whole paragraph. The reason is that if he would read only the first verse, it would be reading out of order, for he first read from Ve'ahavta until the end of the paragraph and afterward Shema. Although he also read Shema Yisrael at first, since he did not intend in reading the verse Shema, that reading of Shema is considered as though it did not exist, and he would have read out of order. The Magen Avraham teaches that this is called reading out of order.
And so it appears in siman 64. Meaning seif katan 1, in what he wrote in the name of the Bach; see there.
