(Note 6) And he returns, etc. — that he reads in a whisper. And see in Taz, who wrote another enactment, thus: and it may be explained that he waits some time and then returns and reads, etc. — until here. And see in Magen Avraham, siman 61, note 7, who wrote in the name of Shelah that those who say Shema in Selichot at every place where they say "and He passed" — it is permitted; thus: since he interrupts the four [verses] in between.

That he should read the whole paragraph twice — and see in siman 61, note 8.

And it implies that if he remembered, etc. — since it is written in note 8 that he should read the paragraph twice, it implies that even though he already read the whole paragraph, nevertheless he should read the whole paragraph again; and the reason is that if he reads only one verse, then it is reading out of order, for he read from "and you shall love" until the end of the paragraph and afterward "Hear" — even though he also read "Hear O Israel" first, nevertheless since he did not intend in reading the verse Shema, that Keriat Shema is as if it did not exist and it is reading out of order; and this is what Magen Avraham teaches us — that this is called reading out of order.

And so in siman 64 — that is to say, small se'if 1 in what is written in the name of Bach — see there.
