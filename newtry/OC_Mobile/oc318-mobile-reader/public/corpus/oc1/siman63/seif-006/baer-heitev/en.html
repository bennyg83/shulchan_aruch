Signaling. It implies somewhat on Yoma folio 19 that even for the purpose of a mitzvah it is forbidden to signal — Magen Avraham.

First. And Rambam did not write in the first paragraph that there is also a prohibition in the second paragraph; rather it is not unseemly except in the first paragraph — so too Ramah, Magen Avraham; and it appears to me that for the purpose of a mitzvah it is permitted to signal in the second paragraph even according to Rambam, for otherwise it is difficult on Rav — how did he signal with his hand to Ben Kappot in the second paragraph, as we conclude on Yoma folio 19, since there is at least a prohibition? Rather, certainly for a matter of mitzvah it is permitted. And let it be clear. And according to this, the author does not question anything — Yedei Aharon on Rambam; and understand.
