There. And see what is written at the end of siman 60. He means that here he ruled that one must return and read the first parsha, and Shema alone does not suffice, for that would be like reading out of order at the beginning of siman 64. Regarding this he cites the end of siman 60, where there are opinions that even if he had intent in the first verse but not in the rest of the parsha, he must return and read it. See there in Magen Avraham, seif katan 4.
