Siman 66 — this is the main [point], etc. Beit Yosef — and as is written in the Yerushalmi: What is the difference between the first chapter and the second chapter? Resh Lakish said: Everything written in this one was written in that one. From now on one should read only one [chapter]. R. Ila said: The first is for an individual; the second for the congregation. The first for study; the second for practice.

Because of [the prohibition against signaling], etc. — for R. Acha son of Rava said: because of intention — for if so, why does it say "and you did not call Me," etc., and it did not bring the verses from chapter 2 of Berakhot; and likewise it says there "and you shall speak of them" — make them permanent, etc., as was explained. And further Rav himself holds that only the first verse requires intention, as is written in Berakhot there — R. Ila, etc., and one who is compelled [to work while reading], etc. Milchamot.
