Seif 6, which is primary, etc. Beis Yosef, and as stated in the Yerushalmi: What is the difference between the first chapter and the second chapter? Rabbi Chiyya said: whatever is written in this one is written in that one. If so, let him read only one. Rabbi Ila said: the first is for the individual, the second for the community; the first for study, the second for practice.
Because, etc. For it cannot be said that the reason is intent, for if so why does it say, ‘You did not call Me,’ etc., and not bring the verses at the beginning of the second chapter of Berachos? So too it says there, ‘and you shall speak of them,’ make, etc., as I wrote. Moreover, Rav himself holds that only the first verse requires intent, as stated there in Berachos: Rabbi Ila said, etc., and he was overcome, etc. Milchamos.
