If he dozed, he fulfilled his obligation. The same applies to another unavoidable circumstance, and this also appears from the Rambam. It is nevertheless obvious that he must recite the entire Krias Shema. Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah maintain that by Torah law one must recite the first paragraph. See there. In siman 67, however, Beit Yosef writes in the name of Rashba that only the first verse is D'Oraisa, and Sefer HaChinuch writes likewise. Minchas Kohen cites a dispute among the authorities on this matter. See there.
