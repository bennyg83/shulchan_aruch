But if he sank into sleep and did not read, he has not fulfilled, for in any event he must read all of it. Know that there are three opinions among the poskim. Some say that only the first verse alone is according to the Torah; some say that the entire first parsha is according to the Torah; and some say that the second is also according to the Torah. The parsha of tzitzis, according to everyone, is according to the Torah in order to mention the Exodus from Egypt, and they enacted mentioning it at the time of Krias Shema.
