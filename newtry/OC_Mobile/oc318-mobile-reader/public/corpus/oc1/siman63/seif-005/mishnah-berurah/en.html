(16) "Drowsing" — and the same applies if another force nullifies his kavvanah — he fulfills, for in any case he read it all; but if he sank into sleep and did not recite, he does not fulfill, for in any case he must recite it all. And know that there are three views among the poskim: some say only the first verse alone is from the Torah; some say the entire first paragraph is from the Torah; some say the second is also from the Torah; and the paragraph of tzitzit — all agree it is from the Torah in order to mention the exodus from Egypt, and they enacted to mention it at the time of Keriat Shema; and see below in siman 67 in Mishna Berurah and in Sha'arei Teshuvah there.
