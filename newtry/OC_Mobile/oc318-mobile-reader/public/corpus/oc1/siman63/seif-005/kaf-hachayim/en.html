(5) [Se'if 2] One who wants to be stringent, etc. Maharshal wrote in Yesh Nohalin, chapter 7 of Bava Kamma, siman 41: one who is stringent before the many in a matter that is permitted, and there is concern for yohara — they put him under nidui; and if it is known that he does so for the sake of Heaven they do not put him under nidui. And if he is stringent before his rabbi and his rabbi is lenient — they put him under nidui even if he does so for the sake of Heaven; and even if it is not so simple to permit, he should not be stringent against his rabbi's words unless he has proof to contradict his words — Magen Avraham, note 2; Chokhmat Shlomo, letter bet; Eliyah Rabbah, letter dalet; Chayei Adam, letter bet. And see Ateret Zekeinim and Machatzit HaShekel, letter bet. And Birkei Yosef wrote, letter aleph, in the name of Maharar Menachem of Beit Meir, brought in Shitah Mekubetzet, first [chapter], folio 2b: a matter that was explicitly permitted — it is not fitting to be stringent before one greater in wisdom at the time the greater practices leniency. And if he did so, the greater one can put him under nidui. And if the stringent one is known for piety, this is praiseworthy. And see Maharach in his responsum at the end of Beit Kohen — see and understand. And see also Gevurat Ari, Yoreh Deah, end of klal 3.
