Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. See siman 58, seif 3. From siman 58 it is proven that even according to us, who require standing only for the first verse, nevertheless it is proper to stand at least until ‘upon your heart.’ If one is going on the road and will not be able to stand until ‘upon your heart,’ it is better to read it at home from the time alot hashachar has risen, as written in Magen Avraham, seif katan 24. We see that there is no prohibition in standing more than what is obligatory. What he brought from siman 94, seif 4, is proof for what the Bach wrote, that one riding or sitting in a wagon can have intent, for that is so there in siman 94 even regarding prayer. Although some are stringent there regarding Avos, that is a mere stringency for another reason, and specifically regarding prayer; see there.
