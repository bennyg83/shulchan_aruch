(12) "At the first verse" — see subsection 11, for the same applies to "shema... malchuso"; and if he did not have kavvanah in it either, he must go back and recite with kavvanah.

(13) "He does not fulfill" — even if before he began to recite he intended to fulfill through the recitation the positive mitzvah of Keriat Shema, nevertheless if in the middle his heart turned to other matters, behold he did not accept upon himself the kingdom of Heaven with the agreement of the heart.

(14) "And he goes back and recites" — in a whisper, so that it should not appear as though he accepts two authorities, heaven forbid; and see above in siman 61 in Mishna Berurah subsection 22. And even if he already recited the entire paragraph of Shema and Vehayah im shamoa, and afterward remembered that he did not recite the first verse with kavvanah, he must go back and recite the entire paragraph Shema, for if he recites only the first verse it is like one who reads backwards and does not fulfill, as below in siman 64. And if he remembered in the middle of the paragraph Vehayah im shamoa, he should finish until Vayomer, and begin Vayomer and recite Shema until Vehayah im shamoa, and return to the paragraph Vayomer.

(15) "Here he agrees" — that even though he does not intend thereby to fulfill the mitzvah, nevertheless he needs kavvanah of the heart in every first verse, so that he accept upon himself the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven.
