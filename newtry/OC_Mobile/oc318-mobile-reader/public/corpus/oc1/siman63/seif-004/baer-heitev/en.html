And he reads. Meaning quietly, as written above siman 61, s.k. 9; or one waits some time and then returns and reads — for they do not silence him unless he reads two portions consecutively — see Taz. And if it is evident that he did not read the first verse with kavanah, after he finished Keriat Shema he must return to the beginning — Magen Avraham; and see Mishnah Berurah siman 64, s.k. 1.
