And recites. Meaning, quietly, as stated above in siman 61, seif katan 9, or he waits for some time and then recites again, since he is silenced only when he recites twice consecutively. See Taz. If it is evident that he did not recite the first verse with concentration, then after completing Krias Shema he must return to the beginning. See Magen Avraham, and see Magen Avraham to siman 64, seif katan 1.
