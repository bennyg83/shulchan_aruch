And he returns and reads. One may say — behold it would appear like two authorities, God forbid; and it must be that he reads in a whisper as written siman 61, se'if 8; and one may explain that he waits some time and then returns and reads — for they do not silence him unless he reads twice consecutively. And that which the Gemara challenged at the beginning of the chapter on Abbaye — why do they silence him, perhaps he did not have intention — and he did not answer that he should wait and read afterward a second time — for I already wrote in siman 61 that the main difficulty is why they silence him since one could judge him favorably even though he could have done it in a manner that would not be suspicion at all.
