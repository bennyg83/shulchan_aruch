And he recites again. One might ask that this will appear as though he acknowledges two powers, Heaven forbid. It must therefore mean that he recites quietly, as stated above in siman 61, seif 8. Alternatively, he waits for some time and then recites again, since he is silenced only when he recites twice consecutively. The Gemara at the beginning of the chapter asks Abaye why such a person is silenced, for perhaps he did not concentrate, and does not answer that he should have waited and recited a second time afterward. I already wrote in siman 61 that the main question is why he is silenced when he can be judged favorably, even though he could have acted in a manner that would have removed all suspicion.
