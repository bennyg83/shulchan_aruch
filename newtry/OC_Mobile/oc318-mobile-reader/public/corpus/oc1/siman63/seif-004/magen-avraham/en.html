And he returns and reads. Even though it is forbidden to read twice, here it deals with one who reads in a whisper [Bach]; and so too in the name of note 8 — that he should read the whole paragraph twice — and see siman 61, note 13; and it implies that if he remembered after he finished that he did not read the first verse with intention, he must return to the beginning, as if he did not read the first paragraph at all — and so in siman 64, and see end of siman 60.

Admits here. That even though he does not intend to fulfill [by mere recitation], one requires intention of the heart to accept the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven, as is the nature of intention in prayer [Beit Yosef, Rashba].
