For the first verse. 'Blessed is the name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever' is included, as stated by the Levush. See what I wrote in siman 61, seif 13.
And he recites again. Although it is forbidden to recite twice, here the case is one in which he recites quietly, as stated by the Bach. I found written in the name of Sefer Chassidim that he should recite the entire paragraph twice. See what I wrote in siman 61, seif 13. It appears that if he remembers after finishing that he did not recite the first verse with concentration, he must return to the beginning, for it is as though he did not recite the first paragraph at all. This also appears from siman 64. See what I wrote at the end of siman 60.
He agrees here. Although he does not intend to fulfill the mitzvah, he must have inward concentration to accept the kingdom of Heaven, in the same manner as concentration in prayer. This is from Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba.
