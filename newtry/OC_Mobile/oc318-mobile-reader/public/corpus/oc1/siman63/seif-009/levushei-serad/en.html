Seif katan 9. And see the end of siman 66. For there it is explained that for a mitzvah matter, such as putting on tzitzis, one interrupts between the sections. If so, the same applies that it is permitted there to gesture for a mitzvah matter. It is difficult, for if so, in Yoma there, where Rav gestured for a mitzvah matter and the Gemara asked that it is forbidden, and answered that he was standing in the second parsha, why did it not answer that he was between the sections? One can answer that ‘between the sections’ is not called so until ‘Vehayah,’ as written in siman 66, seif 5. Therefore it answered a greater novelty, that even in the middle of ‘Vehayah’ he could gesture. This is clear.
