And see siman 66 — there it is explained regarding a matter of mitzvah, such as putting on tzitzit, that one may interrupt between the paragraphs; and if so, the same would apply that there one may signal for a matter of mitzvah. And it is difficult: if so, in Yoma there, where R. Shimon signaled for a matter of mitzvah, they challenged that it is forbidden, and answered that he was in the second paragraph. Why did they not answer that he was between the paragraphs? And one may say that between the paragraphs is not called "until" — and it was as written in siman 66, se'if 5; if so, there is greater novelty in the answer that even in the middle of "until" he could have signaled — and it is simple.
