Siman 69 — the shoulder, etc. Tosafot and Yerushalmi chapter 2, and it concludes there — in either case one should not pray until one puts down [the load], and this means a load of four kav, as written in the Yerushalmi there and in Bava Metzia chapter 9; and from here [we learn] that in Keriat Shema even [while carrying] four kav is permitted.
