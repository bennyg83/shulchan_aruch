Seif 9, the porter, etc. Tosafot and the Yerushalmi, chapter 2. It concludes there, and there: in either case he should not pray until he unloads. This means with a load of four kav, as written in the Yerushalmi there and in Bava Metzia, chapter 9. From here it follows that for Krias Shema, even with four kav it is permitted.
