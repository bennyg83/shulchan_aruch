(7) And he returns and reads, etc. And even though they silence him when he hears "Shema Shema" — see above siman 61, note 13. And that he did not also state that one must return and bless — it appears to me that this is because he wrote below at the beginning of siman 101 that nowadays we do not return because of lack of intention; and according to this, in the first verse of Keriat Shema even nowadays we return. And the reason appears to me to distinguish because Keriat Shema is from the Torah and prayer is rabbinic. And now it is resolved that Shulchan Arukh and Levush did not write here that nowadays we do not return — and it is easy to understand; and so I found in Maadanei Melekh, folio 18, and in Magen Avraham, siman 101, he wrote a strained reason since he was already in one verse and omitted the words of Maadanei Melekh. And it appears that even if he already read all of the paragraph of Shema and was as if he heard, and afterward remembered that he did not read with intention — he must return and read the whole paragraph; for if not, it is like one who reads out of order, who does not fulfill [the obligation], as below siman 64; and see above siman 51, se'if 8.
