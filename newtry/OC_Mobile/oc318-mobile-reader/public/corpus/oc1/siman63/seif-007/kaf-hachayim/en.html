(7) There — and to read it standing, etc. What is written in Sefer HaKaneh, and they brought it in Tzafnat Pa'aneach Chadash: in Eretz Yisrael one must [read] Keriat Shema standing and in the diaspora while seated — see there; it appears that this matter is not agreed upon among the poskim and masters of Kabbalah, and in Eretz Yisrael and in the diaspora all are equal — righteous while seated. Kaf HaChayim to Rav Chayim Palagi, siman 14, letter lamed-bet. And so [appears] from what is written in the previous note in the name of Shaar HaKavanot and Pele Yoetz — that Keriat Shema is in the aspect of the world of Beriah, therefore it must be while seated; and this is no distinction between Eretz Yisrael and the diaspora — examine well.
