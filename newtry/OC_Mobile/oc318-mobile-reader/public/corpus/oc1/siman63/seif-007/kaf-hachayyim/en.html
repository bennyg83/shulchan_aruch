7) There, "And recite it standing," etc. Sefer HaKanah, cited in the newer Tzafnat Paneach, states that in Eretz Yisrael Kriat Shema should be recited standing and outside Eretz Yisrael seated. This distinction does not appear to be accepted by the halachic authorities or by the kabbalists. In Eretz Yisrael and outside it alike, "the righteous sit." Kaf HaChaim by Rabbi Chaim Palagi, siman 14, letter 32. This also follows from what was written in the preceding letter in the name of Shaar HaKavanos and Pri Etz Chaim, that Kriat Shema corresponds to Beriah and should therefore be recited seated. That reason makes no distinction between Eretz Yisrael and outside it. Consider this carefully.
