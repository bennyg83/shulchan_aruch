Siman 67 — one who was engaged in work, etc. Even though in the Gemara they said [one may not signal] because of intention, and if so the first verse would suffice — thus say Tosafot there. Nevertheless the Rif and Shulchan Arukh hold for the whole paragraph, since Rava accepted the answer of Rav Mari; and even though one can reject [this, saying] Rava only accepted the book of R. Yochanan who said "and he," etc., and R. Yochanan according to his view — and further, it is possible the Gemara speaks according to R. Yochanan, as the text "R. Yochanan," etc. Nevertheless in the Yerushalmi they state explicitly that one reason accords with the above in siman 66, for we say there chapter 2: It was taught — one should not signal and read. It was taught: Workers who were doing work for a homeowner, etc. R. Mana said: This teaches that it is forbidden to do work at the time one blesses, etc.
