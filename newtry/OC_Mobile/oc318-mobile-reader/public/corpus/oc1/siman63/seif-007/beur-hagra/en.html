Seif 7, if he was engaged, etc. Although in the Gemara they said the reason is intent, and if so the first verse should suffice, and this is also the view of Tosafot there, nevertheless the view of the Rif and the other poskim is that this applies throughout the entire parsha, because Rava accepted Rav Mari’s answer. Although one could reject this and say that Rava accepted it only according to the reasoning of Rabbi Yochanan, who said ‘and he,’ etc., and Rabbi Yochanan follows his own view, and further, that perhaps the Gemara is speaking according to Rabbi Yochanan, according to the version ‘Rabbi Yochanan said,’ etc., nevertheless in the Yerushalmi it is explicit that the same reason applies to them as to the law above in seif 6. For it says there in chapter 2: it was taught, one should not gesture and read; it was taught, workers who were doing work for the homeowner, etc. Rabbi Mana said: this teaches that it is forbidden to perform work at the time one blesses, etc.
