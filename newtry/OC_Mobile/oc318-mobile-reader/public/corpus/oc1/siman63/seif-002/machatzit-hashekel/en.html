(Seif katan 2) One who wants, etc. ‘And if he is stringent in front of his teacher,’ meaning in a matter whose permissibility has spread. Even if it has not so plainly spread to permit it, but his teacher is lenient in the matter, then although if a student is stringent in such a case they do not place him in nidui, nevertheless it is not proper. It implies there that this is so even not in front of his teacher, when the matter is plainly permitted. He wrote there that this means its permissibility has spread throughout all Israel; see there.
