To be stringent. Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 7, siman 41, writes: One who acts stringently in public regarding something permitted, where there is concern for ostentation, is placed under a ban. If it is known that he acts for the sake of Heaven, he is not placed under a ban. If he acts stringently in the presence of his rav while his rav is lenient, he is placed under a ban even if he acts for the sake of Heaven. Even where the permissive ruling is not entirely clear, he should not act stringently against his rav unless he has proofs that refute his rav's words.
A transgressor. As stated in the chapter Kirah, one who violates a Rabbinic enactment may be called a transgressor.
