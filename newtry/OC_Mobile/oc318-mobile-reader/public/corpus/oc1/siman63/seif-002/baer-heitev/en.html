To be stringent. Shulchan Arukh writes in Bava Kamma chapter 7, siman 41: one who is stringent before the many in something permitted, and there is concern for arrogance — they excommunicate him. And if it is known that he acts for Heaven's sake, they do not excommunicate him. And if he is stringent before his rabbi and his rabbi is lenient, they excommunicate him even if he acts for Heaven's sake. And even if it is not so simple to permit, one should not be stringent against one's rabbi unless one has proofs to contradict his words — end quote.

Transgressor. As we say in chapter Kirah: one who transgressed against the rabbis — it is permitted to call him a transgressor.
