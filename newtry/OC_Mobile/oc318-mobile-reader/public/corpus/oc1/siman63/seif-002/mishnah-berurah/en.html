One who wants, etc. For the halacha follows Beis Hillel, who hold that when it is written ‘and when you rise,’ the intent is not standing, as Beis Shammai hold, but the time when people normally rise from their beds. It is forbidden for him to be stringent and act like Beis Shammai. Even if his intent in standing is not to act like Beis Shammai, but to arouse intent and the like, nevertheless it is forbidden, both by day and by night. Nevertheless, after the fact he need not go back and read again. Peri Megadim; see there, and see the book Mateh Yehudah.
To be stringent. The Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 7 of Bava Kama, siman 41, wrote that one who is stringent publicly in a matter that is permitted, where there is concern for arrogance, is placed in nidui. This applies where the permissibility of that matter has spread throughout all Israel. Peri Megadim. If it is known that he does it for the sake of Heaven, they do not place him in nidui. If he is stringent before his teacher and his teacher is lenient, they place him in nidui even if he acts for the sake of Heaven. Even if it is not so plainly permitted, he should not be stringent against his teacher’s words unless he has proof to refute his words; these are his words.
To stand. Specifically in this case it is forbidden, because it appears to everyone that he is acting out of stringency. But if he is standing, he may sit, for then it does not appear that he is acting out of stringency, but only because he needs to sit. This applies specifically in the morning; but at night he should not recline and sit when he is standing, for it appears that he is acting like Beis Shammai.
He is called a transgressor. As we say in the chapter Kirah, one who transgresses the words of the Sages may be called a transgressor. Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Maharshal that on Yom Kippur everyone agrees it is permitted to stand, for he is not doing so because of arrogance but because the mitzvah is so, meaning to resemble the angels, who are called ‘standing.’ The Peri Megadim forbids even this.
