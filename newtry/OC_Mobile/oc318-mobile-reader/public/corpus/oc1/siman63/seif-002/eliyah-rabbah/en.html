(2) Since he is already lying, etc. On his side — Nachalat Tzvi obtained and wrote that even if one is lying prone one is permitted to incline a little to one's side and read, as with a sick person; and it appears that one should have intention thus according to Levush, for he states a great matter that even while lying one may incline a little to one's side, for standing is not as difficult as in the prone position. And one who is corpulent, for whom standing is more difficult — even so one need not stand; and it appears from the words of Kesef Mishneh, chapter 2 of Laws of Keriat Shema, that if one is sick or corpulent and can sit, they must sit.
