Eliyah Rabbah, letter aleph; and if he transgressed and read while prone, see Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav, letter aleph, who was uncertain about this and inclines to say that bedieved he fulfilled; but Yeshuot Yaakov wrote that even bedieved he did not fulfill — see there.
