One who wants to be stringent. He wrote in Yesh Nohalin, chapter 7 of Bava Kamma, siman 41: one who is stringent before the many in a matter that is permitted — there is concern for yohara — they put him under nidui; and if it is known that he does so for the sake of Heaven they do not put him under nidui. And if he is stringent before his rabbi and his rabbi is lenient — they put him under nidui even if he does so for the sake of Heaven; and even if it is not so simple to permit, he should not be stringent against his rabbi's words unless he has proof to contradict his words — until here.

Since it says "and you shall speak of them" — "and in your rising" — Beit Hillel do not deal with this at all but deal with the time of rising; but regarding Beit Hillel, Beit Hillel agrees with Beit Hillel that it is better to return to his place to bless, only the Sages did not trouble him.
