One who wishes to be stringent and stand when he is sitting, etc. This implies that it is forbidden specifically when he is sitting and wishes to stand. If, however, he is standing, he may sit, for then it does not appear that he is acting out of stringency, but rather because he needs to sit. We learned that every person recites in his usual manner, whether seated or walking. This is obvious. It also appears from Hagahos Maimoniyos that sitting is preferable to standing. On Yom Kippur, however, everyone agrees that it is permitted to stand, for then he is not acting out of ostentation, but rather this is his mitzvah.
