One who wishes to be stringent to stand when sitting, etc. — it implies specifically that if one is sitting and wishes to stand, it is forbidden; but if one is standing, one may sit, for then it appears he is not acting out of stringency but because he needs to sit. And we learned: every person reads in his usual manner, whether sitting or walking (Emet L'Yaakov). And it is simple; and likewise it appears in Hagahot Maimon that sitting is better than standing. However, on Yom Kippur everyone agrees it is permitted to stand, for then one is not acting out of pride but his mitzvah is so (Maharshal in Hagahot).
