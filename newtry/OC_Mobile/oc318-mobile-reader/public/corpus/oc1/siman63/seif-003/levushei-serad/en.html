There: and if you would think that R. Yehudai holds like Beit Shammai — there is some textual corruption here; the intention is: how can he stand for the whole paragraph? If so it would appear he acts like Beit Shammai. Rather in a walker there is no concern, for everyone understands he does so because of intention; likewise for us, even though we do not hold like R. Yehudai, nevertheless if he stands for the whole paragraph we have no concern, for it does not appear at all that he acts like Beit Shammai but rather because of intention he acts.
