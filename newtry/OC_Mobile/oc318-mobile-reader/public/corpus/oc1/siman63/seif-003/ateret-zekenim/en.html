If he was walking, etc., he must stand for the first verse, and from the first verse onward he should recite while continuing on his way, for if he recites while standing he will appear to be acting in accordance with Beis Shammai. This applies specifically when he is walking on foot. If, however, he is riding or sitting in a carriage, he can concentrate properly and need not stand. It appears that the first verse mentioned here also includes, 'Blessed is the name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever,' since it too is part of accepting the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven, and it therefore has all the laws of the first verse in this regard.
