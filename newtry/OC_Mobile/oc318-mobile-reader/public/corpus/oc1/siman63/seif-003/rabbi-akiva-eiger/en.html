Magen Avraham, note 9 — even for need. That is, one who signals with the hands — Gevirol is a matter of mitzvah that he should say properly. And it seems to my humble opinion proof for this that it is called mitzvah — for behold according to what Beit Yosef holds following Rambam that also in the second paragraph one may not signal, only it is not disgraceful — if so how is it difficult how he signaled Gevirol — unless it is a matter of mitzvah. Nevertheless a question: Rambam should have distinguished — in paragraph one even for mitzvah forbidden and two paragraphs permitted — and requires investigation.
