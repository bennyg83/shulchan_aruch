For the first verse. From that point onward it is forbidden to stand, since he appears to be acting in accordance with Beis Shammai. When one is sitting in a wagon or riding, he is considered seated for this purpose and can concentrate on the first verse. See seif 8. This is the Bach's ruling. However, some authorities require one to stand for the entire first paragraph so that he should not appear to recite casually. Accordingly, there is no prohibition for one who stands throughout the entire paragraph. See the end of siman 58, seif 3, and siman 70, seif 4.
