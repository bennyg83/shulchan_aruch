In the first verse. But from here onward it is forbidden to stand, lest it appear he acts like Beit Shammai; and when sitting in a wagon or riding he is like one sitting for this matter, that he can have intention in the first paragraph — siman 66, until here Bach. However, there are poskim who hold one must stand for the whole first paragraph so that it not be like reading casually; if so, there is no prohibition in one who stands for the whole paragraph — siman 58, se'if 3, and siman 70, se'if 4.
