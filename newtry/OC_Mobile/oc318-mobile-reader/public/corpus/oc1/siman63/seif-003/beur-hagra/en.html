Siman 63, 'if one was,' etc. — there, the halakhah is like R. Meir who said, etc.; and 'the halakhah,' etc.; and there, the halakhah is as stated by, etc. And it is so in Midrash Tanchuma, etc.; and so Rif, as we say; and R. Yehudah holds, etc. — the same, Rav Yehudah holds that the mitzvah requires kavanah like R. Eliezer; but Raavad ruled until 'upon your heart' specifically, for his reason is that one should not make it casual, as written in siman 66; and so above siman 58, se'if 3, in the name of Rashba; and so Rashba in Chiddushim in the name of R' Yitzchak Gaon — that otherwise there is a question on Rava; rather, on the contrary, from there onward walking is permitted because it is written 'in your going'; and it requires study — for if so, what is 'and R. Yehudah followed his reason'? Nevertheless, 'in your going' is written; and Tosafot there 16a, entry 'ha,' and so Razah — the same applies to what is in chapter 1 of Yoma, that one should not signal, etc.; but Rif and all the poskim explained in both of them the first paragraph literally; and one must say that each is as stated, and there are three distinctions in the matter.

Walking on the way, etc. — the wording of the Yerushalmi cited above; and Rosh, Rashba, and Tosafot cited above brought it.
