And he recites again. See Taz. I explained this in siman 61, letter 3; see there. Regarding what the Taz wrote, that it appears as though he acknowledges two powers, Heaven forbid, he must nevertheless fulfill the positive mitzvah of Krias Shema. Certainly, initially one should not say Shema twice, but whatever can be done to remedy the matter should certainly be done. It may be asked whether the requirement to recite again is D'Oraisa, because inward concentration is required by Torah law, or only D'Rabbanan. A practical difference arises where he is uncertain whether he concentrated. He certainly does not repeat the blessings, for what connection do the blessings have to this issue? See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 7. I do not presently understand his words. Magen Avraham, seif katan 1, implies that concentration in Krias Shema is likewise D'Rabbanan. See Eliyah Rabbah here, letter 7.
