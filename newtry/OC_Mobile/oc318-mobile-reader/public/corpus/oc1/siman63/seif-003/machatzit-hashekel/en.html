(Note 3) Is called, etc. — what is the nature of standing? Since it says "and you shall speak of them" — "and in your lying down" — that is to say, from it Beit Shammai learn that standing is required; for "in your lying down" means the manner of lying down, and "in your rising" means the manner of rising. But Beit Hillel expound the verse "and in your walking on the way" — even walking. And "in your lying down and in your rising" means on the time — that is, evening and morning, which are time of lying down and time of rising.

But regarding Birkat HaMazon, etc. — that is to say, in the chapter "These matters" they disagree — Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel: one who ate and forgot and did not bless Birkat HaMazon and went on his way and remembered — according to Beit Shammai he returns to his place and blesses Birkat HaMazon, and according to Beit Hillel he blesses in the place where he remembered. And we say there, folio 53b — Rav Bibi bar Abaye was traveling in a caravan; he ate and forgot and did not bless, and remembered and said: what shall I do? If I say to the caravan that I forgot to bless, they will say I blessed here, and he wanted to be stringent like Beit Shammai. He said to them: I forgot a dove of gold (and see there the reason why he said dove). He returned and blessed in his place, and in merit of this he truly found a golden dove; and so it states there regarding a certain disciple who was stringent like Beit Shammai and found golden purses — behold, Heaven agreed by their hand that they did well in what they were stringent like Beit Shammai. Siman 184 — that there are poskim like Beit Shammai regarding Birkat HaMazon.
