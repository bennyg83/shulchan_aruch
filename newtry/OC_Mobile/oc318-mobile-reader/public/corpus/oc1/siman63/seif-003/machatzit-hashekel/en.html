(Seif katan 3) He is called, etc. For what is his virtue, etc., since the verse says ‘and when you rise,’ etc. Meaning, from that Beis Shammai derive that standing is required: ‘when you lie down’ means in the manner of lying down, and ‘when you rise’ means in the manner of rising. But Beis Hillel expound the verse ‘and when you go on the way,’ even while walking; ‘when you lie down and when you rise’ refers to the time, that is, evening and morning, which are the time of lying down and the time of rising.
But regarding Birkas HaMazon, etc. Meaning, at the beginning of the chapter Elu Devarim, Beis Shammai and Beis Hillel dispute one who ate, forgot to recite Birkas HaMazon, left, and then remembered. Beis Shammai say he returns to his place and recites Birkas HaMazon, while Beis Hillel say he recites it in the place where he remembered. It says there, page 53b, that Rabbah bar bar Chanah was traveling with a caravan; he ate, forgot to bless, and remembered. He said: What shall I do? If I tell the caravan that I forgot to bless, they will say that I should bless here, and he wished to be stringent like Beis Shammai. He told them: I forgot a golden dove, and returned and blessed in his place. Because of this he actually found a golden dove. So too it is stated there regarding a certain student who was stringent like Beis Shammai and found a golden purse. Thus, from Heaven they agreed with them that they did well in being stringent like Beis Shammai. See siman 184, for there are poskim who rule like Beis Shammai regarding Birkas HaMazon.
