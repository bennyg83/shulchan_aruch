(A) [Seif 1] One who prays two prayers, etc. — such as during Musaf prayers, or that he forgot and did not pray and must complete it in the time of the prayer after it. Tur and Bach, Levush, Olas Tamid note 1, Eliyah Rabbah note 1 siman 12 in Machatzit HaShekel, Machatzit HaShekel note 1, Raz note 1, Chasam Sofer, and what Magen Avraham wrote in note 1 in the name of the Tur — specifically the Amidah prayer, meaning the Amidah and its equivalent, and it did not come to exclude only prayers of requesting mercy in the world that do not require waiting.
