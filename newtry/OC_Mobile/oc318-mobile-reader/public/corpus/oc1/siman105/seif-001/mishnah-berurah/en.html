(1) Two prayers — such as Shacharis and Musaf, or that he forgot and did not pray and must complete it in the time of the prayer after it, and he prays two. And behold, according to what will be explained in siman 108 in Mishna Berurah — whether he forgot Maariv and prays Shacharis twice to complete it, or whether he forgot Shacharis and prays Minchah twice to complete it — he must say Ashrei between one prayer and the other; this halacha can apply only when he forgot the Minchah prayer and prays Maariv twice to complete it, for there he does not say Ashrei, as explained there in Mishna Berurah, that he must in any case wait for the time it takes to walk four amot.

(2) To wait, etc. — and this measure must be after he has taken three steps back and said "He Who makes peace," etc.

(3) Between, etc. — Shulchan Shlomo wrote so; and likewise if he finished the eighteen blessings even though he has not yet taken three steps back, and he remembered that he erred and must return and pray again, he should wait thus.

(4) For the time it takes to walk, etc. — even if he does not wish to return to his place but rather prays again in the place where he stands there when he stepped after the first prayer; but to return to his place, even if he does not wish to pray again, he must wait, as written in siman 123.
