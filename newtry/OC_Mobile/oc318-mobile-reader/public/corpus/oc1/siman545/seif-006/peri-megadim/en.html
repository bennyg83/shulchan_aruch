Permitted. See Taz on simanim 544 and 541: individual needs not for the need of the moed are forbidden; and for the moed a change is required. And according to the Rashba there are two leniencies: something that would be lost is permitted even after the moed and needs no change. And what is written regarding meshita, see letter 2: that this is not clear, only that meshita is not craftsman's work; and nevertheless one can say that from the Torah it is writing (see the laws of Shabbos), and a Sefer Torah is not [treated] so, for by rabbinic law meshita is writing; and one can say.
