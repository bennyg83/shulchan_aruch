Sharecropping. The later authorities concluded that there is no distinction between a sale and sharecropping and contracting: in both, if they were done before the moed and now he wants to write, if he had an unavoidable delay such that he had no time to write before the moed, it is permitted in both; and if not, it is forbidden in both; see Taz.
