A promissory note during the festival. He can wait to write it until after the festival. If he already gave the money, even before witnesses and with an acquisition, the document may be written, for there is concern that the witnesses may die and the borrower may later deny the loan, as stated in seif 5 concerning gifts. Magen Avraham, and so it appears in the Yerushalmi.
And the borrower needs the money. During Chol HaMoed, even if it is not needed for the festival. If he borrows for use after the festival, it is forbidden. If he will not be able to find money to borrow after the festival, the document may be written, since this is a potential loss.
Or the scribe has nothing to eat. According to Magen Avraham above in siman 542, this means that he lacks even bread and water. According to the other opinion there, it means that he lacks what he needs for the joy of Yom Tov.
To write a deed of sale during the festival. For example, he sold him land for a festival need, such as when he needed to dig clay there to repair his oven for the festival, as stated above in siman 540, or in a similar case, and as stated in siman 539, seif 12. If the sale took place before the festival, it is forbidden to write the deed during the festival, even though there is concern that they may retract, just as a sharecropping contract is forbidden below, unless he was prevented by circumstances or had no time to write it before the festival. Even if the seller or landowner retracts, the purchaser or sharecropper does not suffer a true loss, since he can purchase or lease another field. If the seller needs the money and the purchaser refuses to pay until the deed is written, it appears permitted, as stated above regarding a loan.
But one may not write a sharecropping contract, etc. The same is actually true of a deed of sale if the property was sold before the festival. The term “but” is appropriate because a deed of sale can sometimes be permitted, such as when the sale occurs during the festival for a festival need. This cannot be said of sharecropping or contracting, since accepting a field for such work is not a festival need.
That he accepted before the festival. We do not regard this as a potential loss, as explained above. If he was prevented by circumstances or lacked time to write before the festival, however, he may write even a lease or contract. If he sent an agent to a fair, the sender may write him a letter concerning his merchandise, since all this involves potential loss. Chayei Adam. This applies only if he did not deliberately schedule the work for the festival.
