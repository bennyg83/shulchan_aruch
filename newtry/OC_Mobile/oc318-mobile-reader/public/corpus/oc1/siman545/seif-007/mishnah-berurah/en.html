All the matters, etc. Meaning, the matters explained above in seif 5, or anything involving a potential loss.
It is forbidden even with a deviation. This means a change in the manner of holding the pen or arranging the lines, such as holding the pen between the thumb and finger or making the lines rounded or crooked, while the script itself remains unchanged. This is forbidden. If the script itself is changed, such as making the letters broken in the middle and making the yud like a small circle, it is permitted. This applies to square script. Regarding cursive script or our ordinary writing, see above, seif katan 35.
