[2] And even to proofread etc. But it is permitted to separate words that are stuck together; and even if one complete letter is missing and its trace is visible, it is permitted to pass the quill over it; and a Sefer Torah that was missing sewing at the poles: they should seek one who has nothing to eat and he should sew it (Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah). And where it is forbidden, even if an error is found on the moed it is forbidden (Bach); and it implies even if it is found during the Torah reading in the synagogue one should not proofread, since there is another Sefer Torah, to exclude [the idea that] it is a need of the moed; rather he should take out another.
