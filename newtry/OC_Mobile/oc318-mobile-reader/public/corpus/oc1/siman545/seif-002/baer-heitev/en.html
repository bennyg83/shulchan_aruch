And it deals with one who corrects from reasoning some error, and it is possible that after Yom Tov he will forget this analysis and will not notice the error, and this is something that would be lost (Taz; see there). And where letters are stuck together, or blotted writing whose trace is still visible, to pass the quill over it is permitted to correct.
