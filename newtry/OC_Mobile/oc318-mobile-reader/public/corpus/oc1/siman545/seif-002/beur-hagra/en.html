Se'if 2. It appears to me etc. For its prohibition is because it is not for the need of the moed, as is written in the Rambam. And what is written "even in the Sefer HaAzarah": Ritva explained a Sefer from which the High Priest reads; Beis HaKnesses.
And likewise in books etc. There in Ritva; and see Taz and Magen Avraham; and it is according to Tosafos s.v. "And if," and the other commentators, that because it is something that would be lost it is permitted; and see Nimukei Yosef.
And the same law applies to other etc. As Maggid Mishneh wrote in the name of the Raah, that our Mishnah forbids regarding a Sefer Torah for others' need, but for his own need it is permitted like tefillin; and see Lechem Mishneh; and so Beis Yosef agreed; and as above, that even according to the Rambam's explanation it is specifically because they are not for the need of the moed.
