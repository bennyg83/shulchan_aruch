To read from it publicly. This is a need of Chol HaMoed, and even skilled labor is permitted, as explained in siman 544, seif 2.
It is permitted to correct it. Likewise, if a letter or word is missing, it may be written in.
Likewise books of Scripture, etc. This is difficult, since one could study from them even before correction by marking the error and repairing it after the festival. The Taz explains that the case concerns a correction derived through analysis rather than comparison with another book, and one may forget the insight after Yom Tov, making it a potential loss. Magen Avraham explains that the book contains so many errors that one cannot study from it until it is corrected; the lost opportunity to study is itself considered a loss.
Other books for study. If one needs them for study during Chol HaMoed, he may copy them for that purpose. Beit Yosef appears to permit this only for a communal need, whereas Magen Avraham and the Gra imply that it is permitted even for one’s personal need. See siman 544.
