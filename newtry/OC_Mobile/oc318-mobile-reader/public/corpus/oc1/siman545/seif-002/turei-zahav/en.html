And likewise in books of Scripture etc. This matter is puzzling: granted regarding a Sefer Torah, one must read from it in public, therefore one must proofread before the reading in the synagogue; but regarding other books from which one studies even on the moed, one can read from them even if they are not proofread, only that he make a mark at the place of the error and after Yom Tov correct them. And one must say that he is not proofreading from another book that is already proofread, for that certainly can be deferred until after Yom Tov; rather it deals with one who corrects from reasoning some error, and in this it is possible that after Yom Tov he will forget this analysis and will not notice the error so much, or how to correct it, and this is something that would be lost: so it appears to my humble opinion as a proper distinction.
