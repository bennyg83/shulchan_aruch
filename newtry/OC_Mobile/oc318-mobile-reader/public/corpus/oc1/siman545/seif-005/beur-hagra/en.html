Se'if 5. And judicial rulings. Rashi; and so he explains in the Yerushalmi.
And it is permitted to write etc. From that they permit returning his divorced wife in the first chapter.
And some forbid. As with a promissory note in se'if 6; and from returning his divorced wife there is no proof; and see at the end of chapter 9 of Kesubos, two mishnayos.
And even regarding etc. Not like the explanation of the Raavad and Tosafos that it is because of something that would be lost, but like the Rambam's explanation that it is layman's work, as one must read; and Beis Yosef was precise from that the Mishnah stated it without specification.
And some forbid etc. As Rashi explained there s.v. "And letters"; and Bahag; and see Tosafos there s.v. "And letters" etc.
And they customarily were stringent. See Magen Avraham; and it is a mere stringency, as is written in se'if 1.
