Even to correct one letter, etc. Although one may not keep an uncorrected sacred book in his house, since he could have corrected it before the festival, it is considered work deliberately scheduled for the festival. Even if the error was discovered only now during the festival and was not previously known, correction is nevertheless forbidden because the melachah is not needed for the festival. Bach; Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2; Bigdei Yesha. This implies that even if the error is discovered during the Torah reading in the synagogue, it should not be corrected. Since another Sefer Torah is available, removing this one does not create a festival need; another scroll should be taken out. Eliyah Rabbah there; Bigdei Yesha.
