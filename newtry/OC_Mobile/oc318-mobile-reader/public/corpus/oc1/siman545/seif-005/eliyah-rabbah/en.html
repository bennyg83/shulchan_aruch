[5] [Levush] And they are accustomed to be lenient etc. And Bach: our custom in these countries is not to write at all without a change, and the change should be that the writing itself not be fine in the letters as was his weekday practice; but that he began in a corner is not a change; until here. And where there is no custom one should not be stringent (Darchei Moshe); and see s.k. 14. Judges' decrees and a power of attorney are communal needs even not for the need of the moed (Kenesses HaGedolah in the name of Shach), and the source requires further study; and regarding what he further wrote in his name, that if it is certain he will return to his city one should not permit, see Magen Avraham s.k. 17.
