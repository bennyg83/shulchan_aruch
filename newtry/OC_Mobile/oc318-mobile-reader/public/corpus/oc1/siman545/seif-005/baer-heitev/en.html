To be stringent. It must read: and they customarily were stringent to change even etc.; and so it is in Darchei Moshe. And the change is that they write diagonally; and even though it is not called a change except when the body of the letters is broken, nevertheless, since according to the letter of the law a letter of greeting is permitted without a change, as the Mechaber wrote, except that they customarily change, this change suffices; but writings that are forbidden to write are forbidden even diagonally, end quote of Darchei Moshe. And most poskim wrote that meshita is not called writing at all, and perhaps for this reason they customarily were lenient with all writings, and they have [authorities] on whom to rely. And some are accustomed to make the top line crooked. And Taz concluded that one who is lenient without a change has not lost out, and it is proper to rely on the ruling of Beis Yosef; see there.
