It is permitted to write Kiddushin contracts, Pesikta contracts, bills of divorce (Gittin), proof of divorce, Final Will &amp; Testimony (Datiki), gifts, Prozbul, Assessment Letters, Sustenance Letters. Explanation of pesikta contracts is that a person allocates to give to his son or his daughter such and such (amount).
