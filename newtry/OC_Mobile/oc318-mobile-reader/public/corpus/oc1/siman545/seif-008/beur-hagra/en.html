Se'if 8. One who etc. And as Tosafos wrote that the Mishnah is because of something that would be lost.
And likewise etc. As is written in Temurah 14b: perhaps a novel matter etc., better etc.; thus even at the time when writing was forbidden regarding matters of Oral Law, such a case was permitted; the same law applies on Chol HaMoed. Hagahos Semak, s.k. 194.
