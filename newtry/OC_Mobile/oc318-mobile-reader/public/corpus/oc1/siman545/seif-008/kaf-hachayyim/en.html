He permits erasing the letter because although erasure is a full melachah, it is not skilled workmanship. One may perform unskilled work for a public need even when the public does not presently need it. He proves this from siman 544 regarding repairing public wells and springs. See there. Shaarei Teshuvah cites this. Responsa Zera Avraham, siman 66, writes likewise. Ikarei HaDat, siman 25, subsection 1, cites it. See there. See Shaarei Teshuvah on se'if 8, who writes that if the extra letter is in the middle of a word and must be scraped away, after which the separated letters must be extended or thickened to bring them closer together, it appears that this requires skill and is forbidden on Chol HaMoed. He nevertheless writes that everything depends upon the circumstances and whether the particular act requires skill. If it does require skill, it is forbidden even if an unskilled person presently wishes to perform it, because the nature of the act requires skilled hands. If it would be repaired in an unskilled manner, that too is forbidden because it does not honor the Torah. See there.
