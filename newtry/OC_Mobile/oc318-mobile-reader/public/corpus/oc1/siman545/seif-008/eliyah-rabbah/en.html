(ch) [Levush] or in a place where they set aside documents etc. — meaning that in a place where they do not set aside, permission is only when he has nothing at all to eat; but in a place where they set aside it is permitted even if he has what to eat, only that he wants to have comfort. And therefore the words of the Rama are reconciled that he does not argue with the Shulchan Aruch. And Magen Avraham wondered and forced unnecessarily. The Rama wrote: if he wrote them in order to set them aside on the festival, in every manner it is permitted — until here. It implies even taking pay; but from the Levush it implies permission is only so that his expenses will be more — if he was wealthy it is forbidden as above siman 539 seif 4. And so it appears to me as the main view from Semag that Beit Yosef brought from the Yerushalmi from which the Rama extracted the law — it deals specifically with this. And so it is implied in the Tur and so implied in Beit Yosef siman 542, and so I saw in Shelah Gevurim Ma'arechet 107 daf 391. Also it appears to me proof from the Talmud [Moed Katan] daf 19 that it says: Ori — Rav to Rav Chananel etc. — the halacha is he writes and sells in his usual way for his livelihood, and this case deals with setting aside for the festival as explained in the Yerushalmi there — in Semag even so permission is only for his livelihood. And furthermore from Rabba bar bar Chana whom the Ra"sh brought in his responsum general rule 23 — it is forbidden explicitly for others even to set aside for the festival. And see in the responsum of Menachem Azariah siman 108.
