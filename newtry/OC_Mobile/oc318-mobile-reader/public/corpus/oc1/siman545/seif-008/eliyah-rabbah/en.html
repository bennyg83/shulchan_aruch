[8] [Levush] Or in a place where they put them on etc. Meaning that in a place where they do not put them on, permission is only when he has nothing at all to eat; but in a place where they put them on it is permitted even if he has what to eat, only that he wants to have [livelihood] with ease. And with this Rama's words are reconciled that he does not disagree with the Shulchan Aruch; and Magen Avraham wondered and forced unnecessarily. Rama wrote: if he wrote them in order to put them on during the moed, in every manner it is permitted; until here. It implies even taking pay; but from the Levush it implies permission is only so that his expenses be more; if he was wealthy it is forbidden, as above in siman 539, se'if 4. And so it appears to me as the main view from Semag that Beis Yosef brought from the Yerushalmi, from which Rama extracted the law: it deals specifically thus. And so it is implied in the Tur, and so implied in Beis Yosef siman 542, and so I saw in Shiltei Gibborim from Riaz folio 391. Also it appears to me a proof from the Talmud [Moed Katan] folio 19, that it says Ori taught Rav to Rav Chananel etc.: the halacha is he writes and sells in his usual way for his livelihood; and this incident deals with putting them on during the moed as explained in the Yerushalmi there; in Semag even so permission is only for his livelihood. And furthermore, from Rabbah bar bar Chanah whom the Responsa of the Rosh brought in klal 23: it is forbidden explicitly for others even to put them on during the moed. And see in the responsa of Menachem Azariah, siman 108.
