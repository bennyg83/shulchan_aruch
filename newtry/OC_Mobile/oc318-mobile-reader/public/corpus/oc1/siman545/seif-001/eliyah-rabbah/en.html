[1] [Levush] Of wax etc. Perhaps the same law applies to our writing tablets (Magen Avraham). But writing etc.: Rabbeinu Tam permitted writing in "mashakin" writing, which is fine writing (Beis Yosef); and I saw in Darchei Moshe and Prisha that they understood his meaning as meshita script, and it appears to me that perhaps it deals with actual liquids, as he wrote in siman 340, se'if 4. Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that ruling lines is neither yes nor no, and it is unsettled in his hand.
