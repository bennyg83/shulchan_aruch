And even. See Taz in siman 544, letter 1: to separate stuck-together words, or a letter whose trace is visible, it is permitted to pass a quill. A Sefer Torah lacking sewing: they should seek one who has nothing to eat to repair it (Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah); and that is when they have another Sefer Torah; otherwise everything is permitted. And to make a resh into a dalet is forbidden when there is another. See siman 543, Magen Avraham 1: one can say that melacha of words of Torah is permitted to say to a non-Jew for a mitzvah; and sewing a Sefer Torah requires further study whether it is valid through a non-Jew; see siman 39, Magen Avraham 1.
