In Magen Avraham s.k. 3: that is meshita script. Nota bene: to exclude writing in other languages of the nations, which according to most poskim is a Torah-level [prohibition of writing on Shabbos/Yom Tov]; and see Magen Avraham above in siman 340, s.k. 40, and it is easy [to understand]. And what Beis Yosef wrote in this siman in the name of Sefer Toras Adam: those who erred attributed it to the Ramban, and that is not so; rather it must read Sefer Toldos Adam VeChava, siman 54, see there; and Beis Yosef and Darchei Moshe of this siman disagree with him.
