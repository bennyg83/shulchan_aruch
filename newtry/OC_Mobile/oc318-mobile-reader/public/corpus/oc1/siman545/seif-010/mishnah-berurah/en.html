Between monetary cases. Meaning, Chol HaMoed is not like Shabbos and Yom Tov, on which adjudication is forbidden even if both parties consent. On Chol HaMoed it is permitted if the litigants consent to be judged. To compel a litigant, however, is sometimes impossible, since one cannot always force him to appear during Nisan or Tishrei, as stated in Choshen Mishpat, siman 5; see there.
Capital cases. The Mechaber copied the wording of the baraisa. It refers to the time of the Beis HaMikdash, when they were obligated to fulfill, “You shall eradicate the evil from your midst.”
