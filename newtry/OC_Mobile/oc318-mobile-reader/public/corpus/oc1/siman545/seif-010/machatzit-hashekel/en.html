(s.k. 10) Making them etc. It appears to me that since it is a mitzvah need and also is not craftsman's work, they did not decree so much, and it is permitted even if he need not put them on during the moed, not like Taz; and the words of Taz are puzzling.
And it appears to me that if he does not need etc. It is forbidden etc.: that is to say, if he has what to eat and his intent is only that his expenses be with profit.
