[10] [Levush] Layman's work etc. According to this, if specifically for the need of the moed as he wrote in siman 544, se'if 12, and according to some it is permitted not for the need of the moed, and so we hold. And if his accounts are already written and he only wants to arrange them, it is forbidden (Maharival); and so wrote Kenesses HaGedolah, who preached to the public that it is forbidden. And it appears even accounts that are not written: it is forbidden to schedule them for the moed even though he writes them in meshita script, for they customarily are stringent in se'if 5; and see s.k. 5 and 14.
