(1) (Mz) And if he heard a new thing - and the Omnipresent allowed him to hear it from the beginning in order to write it down. There is also no dispute whether the new thing was heard later or he renewed it himself from a long time ago. And even without a reason for forgetting, since at any time and moment a person is required to labor in the Torah and renew it according to his ability. The late Rabbi who wrote his essays in Hoham [Achronim] and MM will be careful in writing his innovations that he will write a writing from Shita'a and if he has to write a square letter he will write a broken letter and so on:

(2) (Mh) It is permissible to write it, etc. - and the account of the periods is not allowed to be written. This dain takes nothing from his heart and he will not forget a book that he needed at the time for study. It is allowed to correct and bind itself.
