9) If during Chol HaMoed of Sukkos a torn letter is found in the chassan's Sefer Torah, requiring a patch on the outside and subsequent repair of the letter, it appears that this is forbidden. The public does not need this scroll because other sifrei Torah are available. The Sages forbade even correcting one letter, and this repair additionally requires a patch and is the work of an expert craftsman. Another Sefer Torah should be used. Chaim Sha'al, volume 2, siman 43, subsection 1. See there. Ikarei HaDat there cites this. He writes likewise in Yosef Ometz, siman 81. See there for his statement that one should challenge a rabbi only from a work printed during that rabbi's lifetime, which was available to him and which he had read. See there. Certainly, no rabbi should be challenged before the sources are examined in their origins, references, and precise form.
