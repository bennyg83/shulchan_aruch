See Taz and some say: if he married on the eve of the festival, and what of it that he wants it written on the moed; which is not so if the marriage is after the moed and there will be no scribe and he does not trust him: one should not be stringent like those who forbid. And see Magen Avraham 18 and 19; and God willing more will be explained there.
