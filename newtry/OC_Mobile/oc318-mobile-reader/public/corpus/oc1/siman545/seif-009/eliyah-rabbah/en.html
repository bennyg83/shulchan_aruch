[9] [Levush] And also in the place where they said etc. But the making etc. It implies even to make: it appears to me that after the moed it is permitted, and it is not comparable to what Rokeach wrote that it is forbidden to prepare booklets to write after the moed; for here it is a complete mitzvah and they did not decree so much. And further it appears to me a clear proof, for Tosafos folio 19 deduced here from that the Mishnah mentioned only writing, not making; and the Mishnah necessarily deals, per the Yerushalmi mentioned above, with after the moed; and understand well. And so it is proven from Rama; and to exclude what Taz wrote that only for the need of the moed it is permitted: he was not precise. Also what he understood in Rama, that writing for the need of the moed when he has what to eat [is restricted]: he was not precise, for he wrote "in every manner it is permitted," as I wrote above; and further it is proven in Rama there for one who studies carefully. And if so, that writing is permitted in every manner for the need of the moed, then that which forbids regarding writing and permits regarding making necessarily deals even after the moed.
