And the same law applies if he learned a novel point himself: it is permitted to write it; but reckoning the tekufos is forbidden to write, for this brings nothing out of his mind and he will not forget it. It is forbidden to prepare a booklet in order to write upon it after the moed (Rokeach; Beis Yosef; Darchei Moshe). It appears to me that whatever is permitted to write, it is permitted to make a quill and ink for that purpose, as is written at the beginning of siman 536 regarding riding; and see siman 547 in Beis Yosef, that Rashi and the Rambam permit digging in the launderers' pool to launder something permitted; and even though we do not hold so, nevertheless regarding preparing a quill there is not so much melacha, and the same applies to soaking ink.
