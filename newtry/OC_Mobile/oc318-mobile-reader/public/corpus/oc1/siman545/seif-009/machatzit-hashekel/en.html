(s.k. 9) Or that he have. Our Rabbi Beis Yosef holds as Maggid Mishneh wrote etc.: for Beis Yosef wrote that Maggid Mishneh disagrees with Rashi and the Rosh, for Rashi and the Rosh permit even for profit because they hold that one puts on tefillin on Chol HaMoed, and if so it is called a mitzvah (even though he need not put them on during the moed), and therefore they were lenient with them and permitted even for profit; but Maggid Mishneh holds that one does not put on tefillin on Chol HaMoed, and if so writing tefillin on Chol HaMoed is equal to other melacha and is not called mitzvah-work, therefore he permitted only when he has nothing to eat, as with other melacha. And if so, since our Rabbi Beis Yosef above in siman 31 ruled that it is forbidden to put on tefillin on Chol HaMoed, he necessarily holds like Maggid Mishneh that it was permitted only when he has nothing to eat; but Rama, who permits even for profit, follows his own view, for he ruled above in siman 31 like the opinion that one puts on tefillin on Chol HaMoed; therefore he ruled like Rashi and the Rosh that even for profit it is permitted.
