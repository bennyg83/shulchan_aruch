His debts. One who fled with other people's money: it is permitted to write against him the judges' decrees and a power of attorney, and it is good to do so discreetly; but if it is certain that he will return to his city, it is forbidden (Maharashdam). And Magen Avraham wrote upon this: and I do not know why this should be worse than judicial rulings; and see what I wrote at the beginning of this siman, s.k. 1.
