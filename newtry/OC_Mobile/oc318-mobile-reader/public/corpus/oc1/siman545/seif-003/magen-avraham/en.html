Or that he have. Our Rabbi Beis Yosef holds as Maggid Mishneh wrote: specifically when he has nothing at all to eat; and Rama ruled like Rashi and the Rosh and those who say etc., except that this is his usual way, as the Sema wrote in Choshen Mishpat; and see what I wrote in siman 542.
Making them is permitted. It appears to me that since it is a mitzvah need they did not decree so much. Hagahos Ashri wrote: that which is permitted to write and to spin is only when he did not schedule his work for the moed; and Beis Yosef wrote that even if he scheduled his work it is permitted, since it is a matter of mitzvah and there is no place to fine him. And it appears to me that if he does not need to put them on during the moed, it is forbidden for him to write: since he scheduled his work for the moed, we do not permit him because he will find his livelihood with ease; and furthermore, even without this, some say that only when he has nothing to eat is it permitted.
