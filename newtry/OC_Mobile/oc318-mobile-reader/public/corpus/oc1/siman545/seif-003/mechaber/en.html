3. A person can write tefillin, mezuzahs for oneself and spin on his thigh Techelis and Tzitzis (Bais Yosef) to wear. If there isn't to one what to eat, one can write and sell to others in order to sustain oneself. Note (Rema): Or if that there to him more expenses than the profit for the Joy of Yom Tov (Tur). If he writes in order to lay (i.e. put on) on the Festival in all matters it is allowed (Smag).
