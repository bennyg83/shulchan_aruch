Seif 3: A person writes tefillin and mezuzot for himself, etc. It appears to me: Rambam wrote chapter 7 of laws of Yom Tov 13: and it is forbidden to write, etc.; but a person writes tefillin and mezuzot for himself and spins tekhelet for his garment. And Maggid Mishneh wrote there it appears his reason is that one does not put on tefillin on Chol HaMoed, for it is included in Yom Tov; and if so one should not permit writing tefillin more than another melacha, etc. I was asked by a scholar: if so how will he fulfill mezuzah — behold this certainly applies on Chol HaMoed? I answered that this is not need of the festival, for one may give the house as a gift to another and he will dwell in it as a borrower and be exempt from mezuzah. Likewise Magen Avraham asked in the name of his brother above in laws of tzitzit siman 13; and what he answered there does not apply here, for on Chol HaMoed one may give as a gift and thus it is not need of the festival. With tefillin alone, even though he can borrow from others, since he is obligated in tefillin a person wants his own, for one prefers to fulfill a mitzvah with his property — and this is need of the festival. And from this it is proven Rambam holds it is forbidden on Chol HaMoed; but for mezuzah if he gives it as a gift to another the house is fully exempt; therefore it is not considered need of the festival.
