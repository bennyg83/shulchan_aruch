His thigh. Even without a change; and with a wheel it is forbidden, for the matter is very conspicuous (Magen Avraham; and see Taz).
With ease. See Magen Avraham; and see siman 542, s.k. 1.
Permitted. That is, for the need of the moed; but not for the need of the moed it is certainly forbidden even regarding making [them] (Taz; see there). Hagahos Ashri wrote: that which is permitted to write and to spin is only when he did not schedule his work for the moed; and Beis Yosef wrote that even if he scheduled his work it is permitted, since it is a matter of mitzvah. And Magen Avraham wrote: and it appears to me that if he does not need to put them on during the moed, it is forbidden for him to write, since he scheduled his work for the moed; we do not permit him because he will find his livelihood with ease; and furthermore, even without this, some say that only when he has nothing to eat is it permitted, end quote.
