He may write his accounts. This is considered a potential loss, lest he forget them. If no loss is involved, it is forbidden unless the accounts are needed for the festival, such as calculating festival expenses. Even then, according to all opinions only unskilled writing is permitted, as stated in siman 544. See above, seif katan 4. He should therefore write them in cursive script. If the accounts are already written and he merely wishes to arrange them, it is forbidden during the festival. Even where they were not previously written, he should at least avoid deliberately scheduling the task for the festival. Eliyah Rabbah.
The same applies to documents that are written, etc. Because there is a potential loss, lest he forget. The same plainly applies to merchandise that may be sold on Chol HaMoed because it is needed for the festival, as stated above in siman 539, seif 10. Some permit a shopkeeper to record purchases made on credit so that he will not forget them.
