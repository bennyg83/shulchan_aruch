It is permitted to write his accounts. The Rambam gave the reason that in these writings a person is not very careful about perfecting them, and they turn out like layman's work; and the Rashba gave the reason lest he not remember and lose his money. And Beis Yosef wrote that according to the Rambam's reason there is permission only if it is for the need of the moed, as is written in siman 544; and regarding our writing, which is meshita, one may rely to be lenient like the Rashba's reason. And the Rokeach wrote in siman 308: Rabbeinu Shemarya permitted writing mortgages on Chol HaMoed through a change because it is something that would be lost; and even though we say in the Tosefta a person writes his accounts and his expenditures on the moed, one can say [that means] expenditures that he has to spend, and all because of joy of Yom Tov; but mortgages no; even so, because it is something that would be lost it is permitted, end quote. And Beis Yosef asked on this, for something that would be lost does not require a change, as is written in siman 537, end quote. And I do not see a difficulty in this on the Kol Bo, for he holds like the Rambam whom Beis Yosef cites in siman 537, that he holds that R' Yosi said that a change is not required regarding something that would be lost only where if he changes there is a loss; but where even if he changes there is no loss, R' Yosi admits that one must change, end quote. And here too he has no loss if he writes through a change; rather regarding a matter that involves joy of Yom Tov one need not even change; and therefore he establishes that case of writing his expenditures as a matter that involves joy of Yom Tov.
