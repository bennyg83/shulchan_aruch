And he spins. See Taz and Magen Avraham letter 7: even without a change. And what is written "on his thigh": with a wheel called a spinning-wheel it is forbidden, for it makes a sound and the matter is very conspicuous. And regarding tzitzis one can say that when he needs it now on the moed it is permitted for himself and for others for free; and tefillin according to us who put them on on Chol HaMoed are permitted in such a case only; for after the moed one can say it is forbidden. And see Levush and the Mechaber, who ruled above in siman 31 that on Chol HaMoed one does not put them on, and even so he ruled that one writes tefillin for himself: one can say that after the moed they were lenient because of tefillin, and tzitzis is a great mitzvah; and see Magen Avraham 6, and God willing it will be explained there and in letter 5 here.
