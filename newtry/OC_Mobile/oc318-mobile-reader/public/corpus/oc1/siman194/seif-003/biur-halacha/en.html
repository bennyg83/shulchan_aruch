But not in portions, etc. According to what is explained below in siman 210 in the gloss, that the zimun formula extends specifically through the blessing of Hazan, if no one person knows how to recite through the conclusion of Hazan, but one knows only Nivarech and another knows Bircas Hazan, they may not divide it into two. Rather, they should recite Bircas HaMazon without zimun and begin from the opening of Bircas HaMazon. Although Bircas Hazan is indispensable to the zimun, for without it one does not fulfill the zimun, the zimun is not indispensable to Bircas Hazan, which is the beginning of Bircas HaMazon. I later found that Chiddushei HaRaah to Berachos writes likewise regarding this passage.
