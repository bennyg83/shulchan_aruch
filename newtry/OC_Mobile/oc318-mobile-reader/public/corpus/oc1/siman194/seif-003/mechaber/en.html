Three who ate as one and not one of them knows the entire birkat hamazon, but one knows the first berachah and one the second and one the third — they are obligated in zimun and each one blesses the berachah he knows; and even though among them there is no one who knows the fourth berachah, it does not matter. But they may not bless in halves — if one knows only half a berachah he may not bless, for one berachah is not divided' into two.':
