[3] That he does not come, etc. — rather the door of the house opens to the market and he sits opposite and they call him and he hears their voices and they invite zimun; Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba. Bach wrote that he too fulfills zimun. Kolbo wrote: when he finishes his work he should come to the house where he ate and bless birkat hamazon; however, if he tarried there until the end of the berachah and intended in his heart, he fulfills everything and need not bless.
