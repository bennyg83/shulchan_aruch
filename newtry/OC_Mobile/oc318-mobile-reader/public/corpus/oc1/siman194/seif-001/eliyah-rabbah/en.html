[1] Forgot one, etc. — because of the opening he used "forgot," and the same applies if he acted intentionally and blessed, Bach. And there is room to be uncertain with two who ate and the third drank a revi'it, and afterward one forgot and blessed — whether the third who ate is permitted to invite zimun upon those two, or since only one is obligated in zimun, no; and so it is reasonable. It further appears to me that with ten, if three forgot and blessed, he blesses n'varech l'Eloheinu as below siman 197 regarding eating vegetables.
