One forgot, etc. The word 'forgot' follows the wording of the earlier clause, but the same law applies if he intentionally recited Birkat HaMazon. Bach. There is room to question a case where two ate bread and a third drank a reviit, after which one of the two who ate forgot and recited Birkat HaMazon. May the third person now join with the two for zimun, or since only one of the bread eaters remains obligated in zimun can they not join? The latter seems more reasonable. It also appears to me that with ten, if three forgot and already recited Birkat HaMazon, the remaining group still says the zimun with Hashem's Name, as below siman 197 concerning one who ate vegetables.
