1) [Seif 1] Three who ate together and forgot, etc. All the more so, if they did not forget but intentionally failed to make a Zimun, it is obvious that Zimun cannot be performed retroactively. Bach.
2) There: If one of them forgot, etc. The same applies if he acted intentionally and recited Birkas HaMazon; the other two may still make a Zimun with him. Bach; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1.
3) There: The two may make a Zimun with the third, etc. He is no worse than one who ate a vegetable leaf with them. Tur. According to the view in siman 197, seif 3, that a vegetable is ineffective, one must say that this case is superior because he ate bread. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1.
