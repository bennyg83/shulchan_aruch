There is no proof here that three berachot are literally d'oraisa; and it suffices if we say it is like three and is d'oraisa. Therefore it appears to me to emend in Magen Avraham, and so it should read: the matter of three berachot from the Torah; and thereby all the objections of Even HaEzer are removed, who challenged from verbs, and also thereby it is settled that he did not count birkat hamazon in the chapter HaKometz among other things that impede one another.
