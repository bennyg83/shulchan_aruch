Three who ate as one and separated regarding zimun — what is their law. And in it 3
seifim: Three who ate as one and forgot and each one blessed for himself — zimun is nullified for them and they cannot return and invite zimun retroactively. And likewise if two of them blessed. But if one of them forgot and the two blessed, they can invite zimun with the third even though he already blessed; he can say Baruch she'achalnu mishlo and they fulfill their obligation of zimun, but he does not fulfill zimun, for there is no zimun retroactively.And if the one invited zimun with others, even the two remaining cannot invite zimun (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba).
