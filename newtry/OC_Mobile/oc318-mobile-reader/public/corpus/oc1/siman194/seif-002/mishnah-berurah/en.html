(5) So that he can intend — to hear the berachah of zimun.

(6) And he answers with them — but if he does not answer they do not invite zimun upon him, because he does not stand with them; but if he stood with them, even if he does not answer with them they invite zimun upon him against his will, as explained below siman 200 [Shiyurei Berachah and Magen Giborim and R' Akiva Eiger in answer 2].

(7) And they fulfill their obligation — and he too fulfills his obligation of zimun through this.

(8) That he does not come — rather he stands opposite the opening close to them [and see siman 55 seif 20 as explained there that if there is filth between them it interrupts]; and when he finishes his matter he should come to the house where he ate and bless birkat hamazon [Rambam]; however, if he tarried there until the end of birkat hamazon and intended in his heart to leave, he fulfills birkat hamazon and need not bless.

(9) And see below siman 200 seif 2 — and per what Rama ruled there, the responder must wait until they finish the berachah of ha-zan.
