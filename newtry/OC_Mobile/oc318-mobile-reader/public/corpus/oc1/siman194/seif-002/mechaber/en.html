Three who ate and one of them went out to the market — they call him and inform him that they wish to invite zimun, so that he can intend and join with them and answer with them the berachah of zimun, and they fulfill their obligation even though he does not come and sit with them. And this applies with three; but with ten, since they must mention the Name, they do not join until he comes and sits with them. [And see below siman 200 until where is the berachah of zimun.] []:
