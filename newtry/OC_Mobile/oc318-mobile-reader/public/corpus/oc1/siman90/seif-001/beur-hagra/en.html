Section 1 — on top of a bed, etc. Thus is the girsa of the Rif, and thus in chapter 3 of the Tosefta.

And even if they are not high, etc. From what is stated "and not on top of a bed," etc., and "a high place" — since he said "in a high place," you need not [apply it] except specifically there, as the Rambam stated and as stated in section 2. And here, even if it is not three handbreadths high — for he states it explicitly regarding a bed, etc. — all the more so a high place, which is not called "high" in less than three, as stated in many places that the ground is equivalent; and what is stated "unless," etc. — Tosefta there.

Or if he was, etc. Orach Chayyim and Shulchan Aruch poskim.
