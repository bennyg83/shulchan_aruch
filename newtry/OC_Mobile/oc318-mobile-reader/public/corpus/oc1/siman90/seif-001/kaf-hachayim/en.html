(1) [Seif 1] One who prays should not stand, etc. It is explained in the braisa: because there is no height before the Omnipresent — that is, one must show oneself lowly, broken, and humbled; and as we taught: one should not stand to pray except with bowed head — Peri Chadash letter 1. The Levush wrote: one who humbles himself — his prayer is more accepted. And he wrote in Sefer Chasidim siman 393: a person who prays and joy of love of Hashem suddenly fell in his heart — it is bad, because Hashem desires to do His will; and this is what is said "How fair and how pleasant, O love, for delights!" and it is written "Then you shall delight in Hashem," etc. — examine there.
