Nowadays the custom is that the place where the prayer leader stands is lower than the rest of the synagogue, because of the verse, “From the depths I have called You, Hashem.” So too wrote the Agudah. It is possible that for this reason we say everywhere in the Gemara, “he descends before the ark,” and “that one who descended,” etc. See the Beis Yosef, siman 150, who explains it differently.
