Three. The Bach permits even a chair or bench if they are not three handbreadths high, and the Taz writes likewise; see there. From Maharil, Hilchos Tefillah, it implies like the view of the Shulchan Aruch, and so the Levush in siman 98 and Eliyah Rabbah rule. Nowadays the shaliach tzibbur stands lower than the rest of the synagogue, based on 'From the depths I called You'; see Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 122, and Yad Aharon.
