1) [Se'if 1] One who prays should not stand, etc. It is explicit in a baraisa that there is no haughtiness before the Omnipresent; that is, one must present himself as lowly, submissive, and humbled, as we learned: One may stand to pray only in a state of solemnity. Pri Chadash 1. Levush writes that the prayer of one who lowers and humbles himself is more readily accepted. Sefer Chassidim 773 writes that if, while a person is praying, the joy of loving Hashem suddenly enters his heart because Hashem desires to fulfill his will, this is the meaning of, 'How beautiful and how pleasant is love amid delights,' and it is written, 'Then you shall delight in Hashem,' etc.; see there.
