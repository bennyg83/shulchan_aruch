A Place Appropriate to Pray. And to Pray with the Congregation and the Law of One Who Travels, containing 27 s'ifim One who prays should not stand on a bed, a chair, a bench , and not on top of any high place, except if one was old or sick [and thus cannot descend], or if one's intent was to make the congregation hear one [i.e.: a chazzan ]. {Rama: even if they are not 3 [hand-breadths tall] (Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari"ya)}
