(In Magen Avraham end of siman 40.) It is as if he sold it to him. NB: and the prohibition of lo techaneim does not apply, as is explained there; and from his hand one cannot say it is psik reisha that is not pleasing to him to exempt him from work, but only to join him to the count — for this is not psik reisha as complete intent, but only that because it is not pleasing to him it is like melacha she'einah tzricha legufah; and this does not apply in other prohibitions. However, per what I wrote in my novellae on tractate Shabbos, Rashi's view there on 75a is to distinguish that for psik reisha he does not care that it is as intentional, but only melacha she'einah tzricha legufah; but if it is not pleasing to him it is not like lack of intent and is permitted per R' Shimon even in other prohibitions where there is no liability of death. And see my novellae on the Ran at the beginning of chapter HaNechnakim — if so, the Ran can well say it is permitted on account of psik reisha that is not pleasing to him.
