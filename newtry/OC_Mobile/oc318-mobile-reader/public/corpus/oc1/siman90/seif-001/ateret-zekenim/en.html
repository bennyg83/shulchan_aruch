One who prays should not stand upon a bed or upon a chair, etc. This means a bed, chair, or bench, even if they are not three tefachim high, as the Rama wrote, because of distraction: he is preoccupied lest he fall and will not concentrate in his prayer. But other objects must be three tefachim high. Some say that it is forbidden only when it is ten tefachim high (Beit Yosef, siman 211, in the name of Orchos Chaim). One should be stringent, that even if it is three tefachim high it is forbidden to pray upon them (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rambam). There is one who wrote that even a bed, chair, or bench is forbidden only when it is three tefachim high, but less than three tefachim is permitted, for there is no distraction with less than three (my teacher, the Bach). The reason one should not pray in a high place is because it is said, “From the depths I called You, Hashem,” etc.; therefore the practice is that the place where the chazzan stands to pray is a lower place in the synagogue (Agudah; and see below, siman 98).
