One should not pray beside their rabbi/teacher or behind their rabbi/teacher or in front of them. If one distances oneself [from one's rabbi/teacher] four cubits, it is permitted.[[And there are those who say that all of this only applies when one prays alone [with his rabbi], but [when praying] with the congregation, if such is the arrangement of seats, there is no concern if one prays in front of or behind him (ie. one's rabbi/teacher) (Beit Yosef in the name of the Mahary"a and Ohel Mo-ed).
