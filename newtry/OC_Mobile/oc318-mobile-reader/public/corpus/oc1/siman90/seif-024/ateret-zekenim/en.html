One should not pray beside his rebbe, etc. A distance of four amos helps only for one who prays behind his rebbe or opposite him. But one who prays with his back to his rebbe is forbidden even if he is as far as the eye can see (Nimukei Yosef, for so it implies from the Rosh and Rabbeinu Yonah). From the words of the Tur it does not seem so; rather, any distance of four amos is a domain unto itself, and in every case it is permitted (my teacher, the Bach; and see above, siman 103). Even in public, where he need not distance himself, nevertheless he must be careful to distance himself four amos from his rebbe.
