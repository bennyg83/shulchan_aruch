Seif 24, and not before him, etc. This follows a fortiori from Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah and the Tur.
Some say, etc. This is according to Rashi and Tosafot there, s.v. velo, etc., and the other explanation, etc.
And behind, etc. As explained there, because it appears like bowing to him.
And the custom, etc. Since the place is another domain, the custom is to be lenient; see the later authorities.
Seif 25, behind his rebbe. This follows from the same sugya, but in front of his rebbe is more severe.
