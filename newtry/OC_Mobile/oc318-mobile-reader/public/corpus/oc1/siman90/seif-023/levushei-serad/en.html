Magen Avraham, seif katan 40. Siman 78. Meaning, what the Magen Avraham wrote there in seif katan 1 regarding water dripping on his knees: even if they are exposed he should not interrupt his prayer, since it is only a D'Rabbanan prohibition, for according to the Torah the prohibition applies only opposite the stream. So too here, it is D'Rabbanan.
