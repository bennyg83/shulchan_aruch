(w) He should enter, etc. In the Yerushalmi: a person should enter inward from two doors. The Bach explained: every synagogue needs to make a vestibule (ezrah) before the synagogue, so that one enters inward from two doors — like the hall that was before the Sanctuary. Olas Shabbos and the Perishah wrote on the manner of sod — it appears to me they hinted that one should intend that his prayer enter inward from the two names called "doors"; and that is that he should seclude himself in hitbodedut, and the prayer enters and reaches there.
