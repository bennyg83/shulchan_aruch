(70) It is not proper, etc. — that he should not look at their images and not direct his prayer toward them; but regarding interposition there is no concern with garments; therefore if they are not depicted and hung on the wall, it is permitted to pray opposite them.

(71) Images in books, etc. — and likewise one should be careful for this reason not to draw on the wall of the synagogue images opposite a person’s face, but only above a man’s height. It is forbidden to pray opposite a mirror, for it appears as though he bows to his reflection; and this applies even with closed eyes, for when they are open it is forbidden anyway due to nullification of kavanah. Later authorities.

(72) Even to sit on them, etc. — as it says, “You shall not bring an abomination into your house”; and even in his house it is forbidden.
