Pictures in books, etc. For this reason one should also be careful not to draw pictures on the synagogue wall opposite a person's face, but only above a person's height. It is forbidden to pray opposite a mirror, for it appears as if he is bowing to his reflection. This applies even with closed eyes, for with open eyes it is forbidden in any event because of loss of concentration. Later authorities.
