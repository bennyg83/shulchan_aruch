(8) Or windows—and even in a synagogue when praying with the congregation it is proper to be careful about this; and all the more so when praying at home alone, that one must be careful about this initially, as stated in the Gemara that a person should pray only in a house that has windows in it; and even though he must cast his eyes downward, as written in siman 95 seif 2; and what is the practical difference in having windows? Nevertheless when his concentration is distracted he should lift his eyes heavenward to restore his concentration.

(9) Twelve windows—on whichever side he wishes, on condition that it also be on the side where he prays to receive Jerusalem [Peri Megadim].
