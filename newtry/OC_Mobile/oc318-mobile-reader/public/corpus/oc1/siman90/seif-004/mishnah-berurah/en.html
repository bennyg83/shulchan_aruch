Or windows. Even in the synagogue, when praying with the congregation, it is proper to be careful about this, and all the more so when praying alone at home, one must initially be careful about this. As stated in the Gemara, a person should pray only in a house that has windows. Although one must place his eyes downward, as written in siman 95, seif 2, and what difference do the windows make, nevertheless, when his concentration lapses he should lift his eyes heavenward to arouse concentration.
Twelve windows. On whatever side he wants, provided there is also a window on the side toward which he prays, facing Jerusalem. Peri Megadim.
