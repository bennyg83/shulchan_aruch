And his difficulty too is per the view of the Gemara; however, in my humble opinion his words are lacking, and it should read: it is stated in the Gemara (end of 34b), etc.; and Rashi explained: they cause him to intend his heart that he should look toward the heavens and his heart is submissive.

(s.k. 4) And even though it requires, etc. — and this is the difficulty of Mahari Abadi, etc.; Rabbi Yosef in siman 95, who challenged from Mahari Abadi a difficulty on Magen Avraham to Rashi; and wrote: for what reason did Rashi explain Tosafot that the reason for windows is that the house should be arranged with good air (meaning that this causes intent of the heart); and per his explanation one need not look toward the heavens, etc. Unlike per Rashi it is difficult; and likewise per the reason that Rabbi Yosef wrote here in Shulchan Aruch — and these are the words of Rambam: opposite Jerusalem, etc., as we find in Daniel, and open windows toward it, etc.; and as is written that he prayed and they prayed to You toward their land. And the reason for windows is that he should know his prayer must ascend through Jerusalem; also he need not look toward the heavens in prayer, etc.; and to resolve the difficulty. (Unlike per Rashi.) And Rabbi Yosef explained as Magen Avraham wrote here. Nevertheless, when his intent was nullified, etc. — meaning through happenstance it is permitted to look toward the heavens to awaken intent — see there.
