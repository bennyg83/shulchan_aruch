He found in the Gemara more than in Shulchan Aruch, which brought the words of the Gemara. And his difficulty too, at first glance, is also per the Gemara's view; however in my humble opinion something is missing from his words, and it should read: it states in the Gemara (end of 34b), etc., and Rashi explained that they cause him to intend in his heart that he should look toward heaven and his heart is humbled.

(ב) And even though one needs, etc. — and this is the difficulty of Mahari Abuhav, etc., in siman 95. For Mahari Abuhav raised a difficulty on Magen Avraham regarding Rashi, and wrote: therefore the Tur explained that the reason for the windows is that the house be fitted with good air (and he means that this causes proper intent of the heart); and per his explanation one need not look toward heaven — and it is resolved. Unlike Rashi, where it is difficult; and likewise per the reason that Mahari Abuhav wrote here in Shulchan Aruch, which is the language of the Rambam — opposite Jerusalem, etc., as we find in Daniel, and he opened his windows in his upper chamber, etc., and as is written that he prayed, and they prayed to You toward their land. And the reason for the windows is that he know his prayer must ascend through Jerusalem — likewise one need not look toward heaven in prayer, likewise it is resolved. And Mahari Abuhav explained as Magen Avraham wrote here. Nevertheless, when his intent was nullified, etc. — and this is by chance that looking toward heaven is permitted to awaken intent — see there.
