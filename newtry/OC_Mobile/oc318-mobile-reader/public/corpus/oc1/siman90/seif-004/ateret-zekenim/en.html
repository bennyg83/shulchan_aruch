It is good that there be twelve windows in the synagogue. They do not all need to be toward the east; on the contrary, they should be on all sides and should be open (Zohar, Parashas Pekudei). So too I saw from my master, my father’s grandfather, the Gaon, who had a special synagogue in the holy community of Vienna, and he built twelve windows, three on each side. It is proper to be concerned for the words of both.
