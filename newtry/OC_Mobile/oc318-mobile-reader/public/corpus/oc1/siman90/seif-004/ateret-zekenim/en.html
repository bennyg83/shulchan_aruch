And it is good that there be twelve windows in the beit hakneset. And they need not all be toward the east; on the contrary, they must be toward all sides and be open (Zohar, Parshat Pekudei). And thus I saw from my master, my ancestor the Gaon, who had a special beit hakneset in the kehillah of Vienna, and he built twelve windows, three on each side. And one should heed both of their words.
