There, and not on a high place—and likewise he should not stand on a pillow and pray, because it appears as pride. Levush siman 239 seif 1, and Magen Avraham brought it siman 234 seder katan 3, and wrote there Machatzit HaShekel that even if they are not three tefachim high it appears as pride, see there. And likewise one must be careful not to stand on anything that appears as arrogance; but on grass spread in the beit hakneset and the like, and on a mat—it is permitted, Chayei Adam there.
