(d) Because when he, etc. According to this, even in a field where there is no traffic it is forbidden — the first answer of Tosafos end of chapter Ein Omadin [34b s.v. chatzif]. But I saw in the Agudah that he ruled like the second answer of Tosafos that in a field where there is no traffic it is permitted, and it is fitting to rely on him. The Levush wrote: even though it is written "and Isaac went out to meditate in the field" — that is not there, for it was Mount Moriah that was submissive to his heart on account of his binding — up to here. One must say he explained thus the words of Tosafos there; but the Bach explained that Mount Moriyah is called by the name "field," but in truth it was surrounded by partitions. He further explained that he was praying among trees, and therefore he said "lasuach" — the language of bushes, trees.
