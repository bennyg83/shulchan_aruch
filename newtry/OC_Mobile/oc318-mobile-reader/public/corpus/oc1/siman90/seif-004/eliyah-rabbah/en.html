Twelve windows, etc. It appears that they need not all face Jerusalem. According to the reason, it is clear that openings should be on all sides. Lechem Chamudos, end of chapter Ein Omdin. Piskei Tosafot Minhagim wrote that initially one should be careful to pray in a house with windows toward the east, so that whenever he mentions the Name he can lift his eyes toward the windows. If this is not possible, he should at least pray in a house with windows to the north or south, so that he can turn and lift his eyes upward. See below, siman 98.
