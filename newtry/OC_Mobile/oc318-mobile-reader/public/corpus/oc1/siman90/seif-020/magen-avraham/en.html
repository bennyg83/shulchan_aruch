The measure of two entrances. According to all explanations it implies that if his place is by the entrance and it does not open to the public domain, and he waits the measure of two entrances, it is permitted. This requires study, for he omitted the Tur's view that the Yerushalmi derives from the verse 'to watch at My doors,' and so wrote the Bach, so one should always enter within two doors. Therefore the custom is to make an antechamber before the synagogue. At the synagogue entrance, honor is given to the greater person to enter first, since it is fit for a mezuzah. Berachos, chapter 7; see Yoreh Deah, siman 242.
