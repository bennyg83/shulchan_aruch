(61) The measure of two openings — see in the Bach, who wrote that one must make an ezra before the synagogue, modeled on the Ulam that was before the Heichal, and he elaborated on this matter that the measure is two openings; the Magen Avraham likewise, and so they practice today everywhere; therefore it is good to be stringent lekhatchila not to pray in the ezra but only in the synagogue.

(62) One should wait the measure etc.—so his mind settles upon him and he prays with intent, for when he prays immediately upon entering suddenly his mind is not settled upon him. They honor at the entrance of the beit knesset that the elder enter first, since it is fitting for the mezuzah.
