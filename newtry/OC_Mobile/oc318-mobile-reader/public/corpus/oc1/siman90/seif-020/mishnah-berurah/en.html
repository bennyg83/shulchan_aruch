The measure of two entrances. See the Bach, who wrote that one must make an antechamber before the synagogue like the Ulam before the Heichal, and he explained the teaching of the measure of two entrances at length. So wrote the Magen Avraham, and this is today's custom everywhere. Therefore it is good to be careful initially not to pray in the antechamber but in the synagogue.
He should wait the measure, etc. So that his mind settles upon him and he can pray with concentration, for if he prays suddenly immediately upon entering, his mind is not settled. At the entrance of a synagogue, honor is given to the greater person to enter first, since it is fit for a mezuzah.
