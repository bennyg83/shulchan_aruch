That all the more so, etc. Lechem Chamudos writes that it appears to him that for one traveling on the road, they required him to go back only if this does not delay him in such a manner, for since he need not rise early, this follows a fortiori; nevertheless it applies only if he can. The same law applies regarding washing below in siman 92. Magen Avraham asks: since the Gemara says that if one went for optional matters there is no glow for him, for he should have trusted in Hashem, why here is he not required? He answers that there it means he went in his city for business, but one who goes on the road was not burdened so much. It further appears to me that it is called optional only when he went strolling, as below at the end of siman 248, but for commerce and the like it is a mitzvah. Still, one must explain why regarding strolling he should have trusted in Hashem.
