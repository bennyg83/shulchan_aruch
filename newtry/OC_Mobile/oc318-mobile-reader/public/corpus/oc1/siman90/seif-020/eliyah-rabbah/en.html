(t) That all the more so, etc. Lechem Chamudos p. 14 wrote: it appears to me that likewise one traveling on the road — they did not be stringent about repetition unless he is not delayed. In this case, since he will not wake early, there is reason for him in "all the more so"; and even so, specifically if he is able, etc. And the same law applies to washing hands — later siman 92, specifically if he is able, etc. Magen Avraham wrote: and if you say — behold we say on p. 10 that if he went for a voluntary matter it does not concern him, for he should trust in the Name of Hashem? One can say: there, when he went in his city for some business; but one who goes out on the road — they did not burden him so much — up to here. Further it appears to me it is not called "voluntary" except when he went to stroll, as later end of siman 248; but for commerce and the like it is a mitzvah — only they need to resolve that he should trust in the Name of Hashem regarding one who goes to stroll.
