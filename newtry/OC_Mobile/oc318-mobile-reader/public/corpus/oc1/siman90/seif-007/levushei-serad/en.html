Shulchan Aruch, seif 7. The side where the entrance is. Their synagogues were like ours, with the entrance on the west. This opinion holds that 'behind the synagogue' means the west, and therefore he must turn his face toward the synagogue, praying eastward, the direction in which the congregation prays. Thus the west is called behind the synagogue, even though the entrance is there, only because the congregation prays eastward, where the ark is. The second opinion holds that behind the synagogue means the east, and the concern is that one's back should not be toward the synagogue.
