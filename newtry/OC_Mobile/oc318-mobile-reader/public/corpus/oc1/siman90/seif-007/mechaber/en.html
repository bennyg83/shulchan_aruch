And one should not [pray] behind a beit knesset if one does not turn one's face toward the beit knesset. "Behind" a beit knesset means the side where the entrance opens, which is opposite the side toward which the congregation turns when they pray. There are those who explain the opposite, and it is fitting to be concerned for both views. Also when praying on other sides outside the beit knesset, one should be stringent that one's face appear toward the' beit knesset. All this is when it is evident that one turns one's back to the beit knesset; but if one prays in a house adjacent to the beit knesset and one's face is toward Eretz Yisrael as proper and one's back is to the wall of one's house which is the wall of the beit knesset—it is permitted, for it is not evident that one turns one's face away from the beit knesset.
