Section 67 — and behind, etc. This is the explanation of Tosafos.

And some say, etc. This is Rashi's explanation and Tosafos there, first s.v. achorei; and per Tosafos' explanation they call "behind" when the people's faces are toward the west, as Tosafos stated and Rashi brought there — and their backs toward the east; and per Rashi's explanation they call "behind," meaning behind relative to the directions, for the west is called "behind" as stated in many places. And they pressed Rashi because per Tosafos' explanation, why discuss outside the synagogue — even in the synagogue itself if one prays thus he is called wicked. And this is what Rashi said s.v. achorei: "all the openings," etc., meaning one who prays on the side opposite the opening; and he brings proof from the Tosefta that their openings were toward the east, and here it says "behind the synagogue," meaning in the west; and he says that therefore one must explain thus. And that which Rosh Hashana establishes it for Abaye, etc. — and if it were so, why would we need "behind the synagogue," as above.

And also, etc. Maggid Mishnah — that "behind" means from all sides, like behind a fence and the like; and here for all the explanations he is wicked if he does not turn his face, etc. — Terumat HaDeshen.
