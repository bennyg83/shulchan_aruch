There, afterward he was old, etc.—it was hard for him to descend from the bed, Magen Avraham seder katan 2—and this means standing on the bed. And it is also possible that it was hard to stand without a pillow on the ground. However, if he cannot stand, he may pray seated or lying on the bed, Pri Megadim Ateret Tzvi note 2—and see below siman 94 seifim 6 and 9, and our words there in sufficient detail.
