And he faces the opposite direction, etc. The principal concern is that the congregation faces east while he faces west. If he stands on the eastern side of the synagogue, he should turn his face away from the synagogue toward the east, because he must pray in the direction in which the congregation prays. Some explain the opposite. The principal concern is that he turn his face toward the synagogue, and even if he stands east of the synagogue he should face west.
