And he is on the opposite side, etc. For the main concern is that the congregation turns their faces to the east and he is to the west; and if he stands in the east of the synagogue he turns his face from the synagogue to the east, for he needs to pray toward the direction in which the congregation prays. And some explain the opposite: the main concern is that he should turn his face to the synagogue, and even if standing in the east he should turn his face to the west.

According to both opinions. That he should pray only in the west and turn his face to the synagogue.

One should be stringent, etc. And even though he does not pray toward the direction in which the congregation prays, nevertheless since he does not pray in the opposite direction we have no concern (Beit Yosef, Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah); and the Bach writes here what he wrote and I do not know what difficulty bothered him.
