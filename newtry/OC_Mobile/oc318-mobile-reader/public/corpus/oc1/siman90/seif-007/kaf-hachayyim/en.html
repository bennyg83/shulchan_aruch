7) There: Unless he was elderly, etc. This means that it is difficult for him to descend from the bed. Magen Avraham 2. That is, he is standing upon the bed. It may also mean that it is difficult for him to stand on the ground without a cushion. If he cannot stand, however, he may pray while seated or lying upon the bed. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 2.
