Section 18 — and he, etc. And the Rabbis — even though in a beit hakneset there is a multitude, in a multitude is awe of the king — Terumat HaDeshen.

And some say, etc. Terumat HaDeshen in the name of Tosafos, and so it is implied on 30b from that which R' Ami and Rav Assi [said], and in Tosafos s.v. ein, etc.

And specifically, etc. So too in the responsum of the Rosh and Terumat HaDeshen that even per the Rambam he concedes thus, and Terumat HaDeshen wrote that even prayer itself they cancel, as was written at the end of Rosh Hashana that R' Yehuda would pray thirty days for thirty days, and likewise Rashbi would cancel prayer as was written in the first chapter of Shabbos; but per Ri's explanation in the name of Tosafos, even one whose Torah is not his occupation [need not interrupt].
