And even though in the synagogue there is "in the multitude of people is the king's glory," nevertheless in the beit midrash it is preferable that one not cancel his study on account of prayer — for we find Rabbi Yehuda the Nasi, who would not pray except from year to year, as Terumat HaDeshen wrote. And it is difficult — for in siman 298 seif 14 they say that they cancel the beit midrash on account of "in the multitude of people is the king's glory"; and one must say that it is different there, for it is only a short hour — the Sages were not concerned; and so it is explicit in Terumat HaDeshen in the chapter "These are the matters," see there.
