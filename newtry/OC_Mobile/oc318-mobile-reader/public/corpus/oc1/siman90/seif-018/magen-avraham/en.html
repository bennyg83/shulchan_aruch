Provided that he prays with ten. Although in the synagogue there is 'in a multitude of people is the King's glory,' nevertheless the study hall is preferable, for one should not interrupt his learning for prayer. We find that Rabbi Yehudah HaNasi prayed only once a year, as Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah wrote. This is difficult, for in siman 298, seif 14, we say that the study hall is canceled because of 'in a multitude of people is the King's glory.' One must say that there it is only a brief time, and the Sages were not concerned. So it appears in Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah, chapter Elu Devarim.
