A fixed beis midrash is more sacred than a synagogue, etc. Even according to the Rambam, who requires ten in a beis midrash, that applies to one who goes from his home to another place to learn, since he normally interrupts his learning at the time he goes to learn; he should pray with ten or enter the synagogue. But one who learns in his house the entire day in a fixed place, and his Torah is his occupation, should not go to the synagogue even if there are not ten there, because he loses time from his learning when he goes.
