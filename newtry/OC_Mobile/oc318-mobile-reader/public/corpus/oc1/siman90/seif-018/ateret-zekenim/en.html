The beit midrash permanently established is holier than the beit hakneset, etc. And even in the view of the Rambam, who requires ten in the beit midrash — this refers to one who goes from his house to another place to learn, since it is his way to be idle from his learning at the hour he goes to learn; he must pray with ten or enter the beit hakneset. But one who learns in his house all day in a fixed place and his Torah is his trade has no need to go to the beit hakneset even if there are not ten there, because he is idle from his learning at the hour of his going, and it is better for him to pray alone (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Yonah and above, siman 106). And all the more so he should not interrupt Torah in the beit hakneset. And thus in the new Hagahos on Alfasi: those Torah scholars who occupy themselves with Torah in the beit hakneset and synagogue while the congregation prays — they have not acted well (above, siman 69).
