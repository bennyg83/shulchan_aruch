He must go there. If he can arrive while it is still broad daylight and will not need to walk alone, as written in seif 17; this is a kal vachomer. It is stated in chapter Sheloshah She'Achlu that Rabbi Eliezer freed his slave to complete a minyan of ten. Although one who frees his slave violates a positive commandment, a mitzvah of the many is different. Tosafot in the first chapter of Shabbos wrote likewise. The Rosh wrote that the positive commandment of the many is strong, as it says, 'I shall be sanctified among the children of Israel,' meaning the positive commandment to sanctify the Name among ten in public. Even if it is a D'Rabbanan matter, such as hearing Kedushah and Barchu, for which we have not found a source according to the Torah, it nevertheless overrides an individual's positive commandment. It is not reasonable to say that it refers only to a mitzvah according to the Torah, such as reading Parashas Zachor. In Moed Katan he wrote similarly.
