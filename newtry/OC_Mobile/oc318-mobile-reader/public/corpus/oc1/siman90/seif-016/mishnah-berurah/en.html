(49) "And wishes to lodge there"—the same applies in the morning: if on the road ahead within four mil there is a place where they pray with ten, he should wait until he arrives there, provided that thereby the time of Keriat Shema and prayer will not pass.

(50) "If ahead of him"—specifically on the road ahead, for otherwise he will eventually have to go there; but to the sides, his law is like one behind him.

(51) To go there—in a manner that he need not go alone because of this; and it also deals with when the day is still long, that even if he goes there he can arrive while the sun is still on the earth; otherwise he is not obligated, for a person should always enter in good spirits. See Peri Megadim who wrote that on the road, if ten happen to be available to pray Minchah a half hour after midday, he is permitted—even though ab initio it is not proper, as stated in siman 233; here it is permitted.

(52) "And behind him" etc.—see in the previous note; and one who sits in his house, his law is like one behind him. Therefore one who lives in a settlement within a mil of a place where they pray with ten must go every morning to pray with ten, but not in the evening, for one need not travel at night on the road for a minyan. This seif is an open rebuke to those people who are in a city and are lazy to go to the beit knesset to pray Minchah and Maariv.
