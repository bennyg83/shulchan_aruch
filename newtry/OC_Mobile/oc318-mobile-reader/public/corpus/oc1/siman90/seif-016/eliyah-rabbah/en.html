It is permitted, etc. The Bach explains the Levush as not speaking about the Shemoneh Esrei, for during the Shemoneh Esrei it is certainly forbidden to spit, but rather while sitting. In my humble opinion, this is what the Levush means when he writes that otherwise he cannot pray properly, which is permitted even during the Shemoneh Esrei, as below in siman 97, seif 2.
