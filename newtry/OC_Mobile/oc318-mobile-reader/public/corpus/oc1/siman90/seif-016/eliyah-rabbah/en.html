(p) It is permitted, etc. The Bach explained the words of the Levush: he is not speaking of the Shemoneh Esrei prayer — for in the Shemoneh Esrei prayer it is certainly forbidden to spit; rather he speaks of one who is sitting — up to here. And in my humble opinion: that is, what the Levush wrote "and he cannot pray properly" — this is permitted even in the Shemoneh Esrei prayer, as below siman 97 seif 2.
