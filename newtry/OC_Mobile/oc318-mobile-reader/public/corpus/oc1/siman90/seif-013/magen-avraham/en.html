It is permitted to spit. And he throws it behind him, as written in siman 97 seif 2; Darkei Moshe in the name of Or Zarua — that is, there in siman 97 the Shulchan Aruch writes that it is forbidden to spit (that is, during the Shemoneh Esreh prayer); and if he cannot avoid spitting, he should swallow it in his garment. And if he is a stama'er, he throws it behind him — implying that not during the Shemoneh Esreh prayer everything is permitted. However, Darkei Moshe in siman 97 brought in the name of Or Zarua, and this is its language: Or Zarua wrote that even not during prayer it is forbidden to spit before him or to his right — only behind him or to his left, end quote. And Or Zarua certainly does not disagree with the Gemara that says, etc. [Shulchan Aruch Choshen Mishpat 253]. One who wishes not to spit frequently in the synagogue should chew before entering a root called licorice; but in summer and in the rainy season it is forbidden, lest the sweetness remain in his mouth; and when he swallows saliva he swallows the sweetness [Shulchan Aruch siman 253].
