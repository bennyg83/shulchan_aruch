It is permitted to spit. He should throw it behind him, as written in siman 97, seif 2, and in Darchei Moshe in the name of Or Zarua. One who wants not to spit constantly in the synagogue should chew before entering a root called licorice. But on the eve of Yom Kippur and the eve of Tisha B'Av this is forbidden, because the sweetness remains in his mouth, and when he swallows saliva he swallows the sweetness. Sefer Chasidim, siman 253.
