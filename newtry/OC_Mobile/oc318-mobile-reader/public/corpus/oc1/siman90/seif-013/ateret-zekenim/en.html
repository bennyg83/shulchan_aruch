It is permitted to spit in a synagogue, for otherwise he will not be able to pray properly (Maharrami). This is specifically not during the Shemoneh Esrei, but during the Shemoneh Esrei it is forbidden to spit, as below in siman 98; and see below, siman 151, seif 6.
And he treads it down with his foot. Even in his house, in a place designated for him sometimes to pray, he must do so (Rabbeinu Yonah).
