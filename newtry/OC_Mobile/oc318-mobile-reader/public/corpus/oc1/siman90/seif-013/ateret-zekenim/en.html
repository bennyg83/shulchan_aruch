It is permitted to spit in the beit hakneset, for otherwise one could not pray properly (Maharam of Italy). And specifically not at the time of Shemoneh Esreh; but at the time of Shemoneh Esreh it is forbidden to spit, as below in siman 98; and see below siman 151, seif 6.

And one who spits should cover it with his foot. And even in his house, in the place designated for him to pray at times, he must do so (R' Yitzchak).
