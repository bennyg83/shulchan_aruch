It is a mitzvah to run, etc. From Sefer Yirah of Rabbeinu Yonah it implies that the mitzvah to run applies specifically near the synagogue. The Perishah writes that this is logical, for before then it is not evident that he intends for the mitzvah of going to the synagogue. Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, in the name of Shtei Yados, explains that one should run the whole way, and when he comes near the synagogue he should hurry even more. Avodas HaKodesh writes that in the synagogue itself it is forbidden to run; rather, he should walk with awe and fear.
