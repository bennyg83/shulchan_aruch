(m) It is a mitzvah to run, etc. From Sefer Yere'im on Rabbeinu Yonah it implies: specifically near the synagogue it is a mitzvah to run. The Perishah wrote: so is logical — for before that it does not appear he intends for the sake of the mitzvah of the synagogue. Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Shnei Yados explained that on the entire way he should run, and when he comes near the synagogue he should hurry more. Avodas HaKodesh wrote: in the synagogue itself it is forbidden to run; rather he should go with awe and fear.
