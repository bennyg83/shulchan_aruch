There, and likewise if it was surrounded by partitions, etc.—it implies that we require it to be surrounded by partitions on four sides; and so is the language of Rambam chapter 5 of Hilchos Tefillah law 7, who wrote surrounded by partitions on all its sides. And so is implied in Beit Yosef siman 285 s.v. ra'ui and in Bach there—that the language "surrounded by partitions" means on four sides, see there. However, the rabbi Ben Aharon, Parashat Yitro note 3, wrote surrounded by three partitions—and likewise Shevet Tzaddik note 4; and see there in Shoshanat HaMelekh in the margin of Shevet Tzaddik. And in my humble opinion it appears one should not be lenient with three partitions alone without the form of an opening on the fourth, except where there is some compulsion to pray in that place.
