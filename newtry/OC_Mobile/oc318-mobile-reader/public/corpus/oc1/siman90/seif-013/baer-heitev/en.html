And specifically not at the time of Shemoneh Esreh, but such as Kerias Shema and its blessings; but during Shemoneh Esreh it is forbidden to spit, as below siman 97, seif 2 — Bach, see Taz. And he wrote in Sefer Chasidim: one who does not wish to spit frequently in the beit hakneset should chew before entering a root called kreitz, but on Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av it is forbidden, lest the sweetness remain in his mouth; and when he swallows saliva he swallows the sweetness.
