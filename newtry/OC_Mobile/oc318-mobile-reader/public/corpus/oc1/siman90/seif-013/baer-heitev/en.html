To spit. This is specifically not during the Shemoneh Esrei, but during Shema and its blessings and the like. During the Shemoneh Esrei it is forbidden to spit, as below, siman 97, seif 2. Bach; Olas Tamid; Taz. Sefer Chasidim writes that one who wants not to spit constantly in the synagogue should chew before entering a root called licorice. But on the eve of Yom Kippur or the eve of Tisha B'Av this is forbidden, for the sweetness remains in his mouth, and when he swallows saliva he swallows the sweetness. Magen Avraham.
