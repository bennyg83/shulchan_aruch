To spit in the synagogue. Meaning not during the Shemoneh Esrei, but during Shema and its blessings and the like. During the Shemoneh Esrei it is forbidden to spit, as below in siman 97, seif 2; see there.
And he treads it with his feet. So that it not remain exposed, out of respect. One who wants not to spit constantly in the synagogue should chew before entering a sweet root called licorice, and even in the morning this is permitted, for arrogance does not apply and he does not ingest it. But on the eve of Yom Kippur and the eve of Tisha B'Av it is forbidden, for the sweetness remains in his mouth, and when he swallows saliva he swallows the sweetness.
He covers it with reed. Meaning, if there is straw or reed in the synagogue, he spits on it and need not rub it, since the spittle is covered by the reed.
