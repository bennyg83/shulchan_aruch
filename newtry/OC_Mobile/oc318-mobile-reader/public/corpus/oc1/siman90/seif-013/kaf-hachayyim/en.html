13) There: Likewise, if it was surrounded by partitions, etc. This implies that it must be surrounded by partitions on all four sides. Rambam's language in Hilchos Tefillah 5:7 is likewise, 'surrounded by partitions on all its sides.' Beis Yosef and Bach in siman 345 also imply that 'surrounded by partitions' means on four sides; see there. Ben Ish Chai, Yisro 3, however, writes that three partitions suffice, as does Shulchan Tahor HaZahav 4; see Shoshanas HaMelech in the margin there. In my humble opinion, one should not be lenient with only three partitions and no tzuras hapesach on the fourth side, unless some unavoidable circumstance requires him to pray in that place.
