It is forbidden to pass outside the beit knesset on the side where the entrance opens at the time the congregation prays, since it appears like apostasy, for one does not enter to pray. If one carries a load, or wears tefillin, or there is another beit knesset in the city, or this beit knesset has another entrance—it is permitted.
