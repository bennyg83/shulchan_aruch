(Note 8) Because of, etc. — see Aruch HaShulchan siman 22 seif 5. If with two people there is suspicion — that is, according to those who say that unspecified people are called reputable — there is no suspicion. The Rambam holds that unspecified wayfarers are not called reputable, see there and in the Gemara here.

And a torch is like two — that is, like a person.

And therefore in a field and ruin, with presumption and two or a torch it is permitted, for there is no concern at all.
