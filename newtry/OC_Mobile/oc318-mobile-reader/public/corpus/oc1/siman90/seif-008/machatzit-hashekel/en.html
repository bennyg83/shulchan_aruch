Seif katan 8, because, etc. See Even HaEzer, siman 22, seif 5, whether with two men there is suspicion. According to those who say ordinary people are called fit, there is no suspicion; according to the Rambam, ordinary people are not called fit. See there and the Gemara here.
A torch is like two, meaning together with the person.
According to this, in a field and a strong ruin, with two people or a torch, it is permitted, for there is no concern at all.
