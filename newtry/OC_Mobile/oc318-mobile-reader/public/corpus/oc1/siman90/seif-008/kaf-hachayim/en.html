And even according to Perishah that we brought above note 2, who wrote the reason for the prohibition on a bed, chair, and bench is separation—it may be said this applies specifically where it is possible; but in a situation of constraint and compulsion it is permitted, as Rambam wrote chapter 5 of Hilchos Tefillah law 1.
