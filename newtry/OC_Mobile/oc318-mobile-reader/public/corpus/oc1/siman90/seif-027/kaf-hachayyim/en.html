27) There: Such as in a field. It is obvious that travelers may pray in a field. Magen Avraham 6; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 5; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 109b; Kesher Gudal 7:10; Siddur Beis Yaakov, laws of the Amidah 23. They must nevertheless pray off the road, in a place where passersby will not interrupt them. Siddur Beis Yaakov, ibid. Before beginning, they must also take exceptional care to look in every direction and ensure that no excrement or filth is present, so that their prayer not be an abomination. Yafeh LaLev, end of paragraph 6.
