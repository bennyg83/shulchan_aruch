He should be silent, etc. And I lean toward what is written siman 78 that he need not interrupt, for there the interruption would be great — he would need to remove his garments and put on others; but here silence is not called interruption, and likewise walking from his place is not called interruption — so too as written siman 104 seif 3 as above; and see Levush who wrote that urine above is called an ones since they pour for him in the Gemara — it implies one who is bloated; and see siman 65 therein.
