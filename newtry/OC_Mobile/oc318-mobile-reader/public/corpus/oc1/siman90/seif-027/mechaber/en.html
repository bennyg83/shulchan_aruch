If one was standing in prayer [i.e. Amidah] and a young child urinated in the synagogue, he should remain silent [i.e. stop praying] until they bring water to pour over the urine [thus 'removing' it], or he should walk four cubits in front of it or to the side of it, or he should go out from the synagogue and finish his prayer. {Rama: Rem"a: And it is better to go to a different place rather than be silent [and wait], lest one delay as long as [the time required] to finish all of it (i.e. the Amidah) and would [then] be required to return to the beginning (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rokeach, Siman 224. And see above Siman 79).}
