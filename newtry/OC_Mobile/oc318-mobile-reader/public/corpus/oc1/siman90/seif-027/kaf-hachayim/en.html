There, like in a field—and plainly travelers pray in the field: Magen Avraham seder katan 6, Razhu note 5, Shulchan Aruch HaRav 109:2, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 7 note 10, Siddur Beit Yaakov in laws of the Eighteen Blessings note 23. However, they must pray off the road in the field in a place where travelers will not interrupt them, Beit Yaakov there. And they must also be very careful before beginning to look all around that there be no feces or filth there, so their prayer not be an abomination—Hafla'ah end of note 6.
