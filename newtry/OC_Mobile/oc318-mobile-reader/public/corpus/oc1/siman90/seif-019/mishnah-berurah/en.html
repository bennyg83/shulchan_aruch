(59) One should establish a place—for so we find with Avraham Avinu, who established a place for his prayer, as written "And Avraham rose early in the morning to the place where he had stood before the Omnipresent," etc. Also when praying in his house he should establish a place so the household members will not confuse him.

(60) A fixed place — and within four amos is considered one place, for it is impossible to narrow it further.
