He should establish a place. For so we find with Avraham Avinu, who established a place for his prayer, as it says, 'Avraham rose early in the morning to the place where he had stood before Hashem.' Also when one prays at home, he should establish a place where members of the household will not disturb him.
A fixed place. Within four amos is considered one place, for it is impossible to be exact.
