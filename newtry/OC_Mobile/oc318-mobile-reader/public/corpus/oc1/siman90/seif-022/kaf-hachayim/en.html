There, and it is good that there be twelve windows in the beit hakneset. Levush, end of chapter Ein Omdin—it appears certain that these need not all be toward Jerusalem; and also as the reason explained, they should be open on all sides. And they brought Eliyah Rabbah note 3, and the end of Knesset HaGedolah siman 98 note 1 wrote that these twelve windows correspond to the twelve permutations of the Divine Name, and it is good that there be three on each side, so far. And likewise Eitan HaEzrachi note 3—that our grandfather the gaon had a beit hakneset in the holy community of Vienna and built in it twelve windows, three on each side. And likewise Chemdat Shlomo note 2.
