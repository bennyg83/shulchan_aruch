(69) That one should be careful about praying — Maamar Mordechai wrote: it would seem one should be meticulous about this, but it is impossible to be careful about this, for how can all the people standing in the synagogue be opposite the wall without a person interrupting? Therefore it appears that even according to one opinion it is not a complete interruption that would be forbidden for every person, but rather a general carefulness that one should be careful when possible; and in a synagogue and the like, where it is impossible otherwise, it is plainly permitted lekhatchila to pray behind a person — only that one who is meticulous in his actions should see that he has a fixed place opposite the wall; end quote.
