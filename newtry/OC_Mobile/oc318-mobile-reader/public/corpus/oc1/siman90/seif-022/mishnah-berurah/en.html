That one should be careful not to pray. Ma'amar Mordechai wrote that at first glance this requires precision, for it is impossible to be careful about this: how can all the people standing in the synagogue face the wall without a person intervening? Therefore it appears that even according to the opinion cited, it is not a complete interruption forbidden to everyone, but only a precaution to observe when possible. In a synagogue and the like, where it is impossible otherwise, it is obvious that one may initially pray behind another person; only one who is exacting in his deeds should see that he has a fixed place facing the wall.
