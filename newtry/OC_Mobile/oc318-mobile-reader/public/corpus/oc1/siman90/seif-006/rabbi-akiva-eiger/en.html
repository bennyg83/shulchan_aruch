(Magen Avraham siman 32) And a question in siman 298. I did not understand—for here the reason is not on account of nullification of beit midrash; rather on account that it is holier than the beit knesset, as in the gemara "One who loves Hashem, the gates of Zion" etc. And what he wrote in the name of Terumat Yeshanim that one should not nullify one's study—this is what Terumat Yeshanim wrote, even for Rambam; that this is specifically when praying with ten. Nevertheless in one's Torah profession one admits [it's different]. See there—and it is not relevant here.
