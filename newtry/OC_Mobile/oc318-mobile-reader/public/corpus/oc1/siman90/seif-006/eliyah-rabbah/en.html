And because, etc. Even with two fit people and a new ruin, where there is no suspicion of immorality and no concern of collapse, it is nevertheless forbidden because of destructive forces when they are in their place, as we are concerned for them. Berachos 3. Tosafot explain that this is specifically where they are known to be common; ordinarily there is no concern for destructive forces with two people. A torch is like two, and the moon is like three. It appears from the Gemara there that even without prayer it is forbidden to enter, and the poskim teach the novelty that even for prayer it is forbidden.
