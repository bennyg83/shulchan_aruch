Idolatrous matters. See Taz and Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 27. In Yoreh Deah 141, seif 4 in the gloss, there is no suspicion regarding an object belonging to the public. Nevertheless, it is forbidden in a synagogue. Accordingly, the prohibition is not limited to idolatrous matters. Even decorative images that appear connected with idolatrous worship are forbidden in a synagogue. Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 28, wrote in the name of Radbaz that it is forbidden to pray facing a mirror, lest people say that he is bowing to his reflection. See Yoreh Deah 141 for many laws concerning this matter.
