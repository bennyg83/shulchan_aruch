Words of prayer—Taz; and Eliyah Rabbah letter 27 in Yoreh De'ah 141 (seif 4 in gloss)—regarding many people there is no suspicion; nevertheless in a synagogue it is forbidden; and accordingly it is not specifically words of prayer—even to form such drawings that appear like idol worship is forbidden in a synagogue. In Eliyah Rabbah letter 28 in the name of Radbaz—it is forbidden to pray facing a mirror so they should not say he is bowing to his reflection, see there; and Ritva 141 has many laws on this matter.
