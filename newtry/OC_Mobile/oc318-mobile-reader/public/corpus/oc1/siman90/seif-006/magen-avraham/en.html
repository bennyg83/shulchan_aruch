And not in a ruin. It is evident from the Gemara that it is better to pray on the road than in a ruin; and if it is permitted that passers-by interrupt him, he should pray a short prayer — see siman 110.

Because of suspicion. That they suspect him that he has a harlot there; and it is in the Gemara: a ruin standing in a field — there is no suspicion, for a harlot is not common there (see Or Zarua siman 22 seif 5). And in a ruin there is a presumption that one need not be concerned about collapse; and if two enter there one need not be concerned about harmful spirits unless it is known that harmful spirits are regularly there; and a torch counts as two and the moon as three.
