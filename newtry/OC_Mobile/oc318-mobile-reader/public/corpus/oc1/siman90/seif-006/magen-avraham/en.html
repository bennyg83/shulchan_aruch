And not in a ruin. It is evident from the Gemara that it is preferable to pray on the road rather than in a ruin. If he fears that passersby will interrupt him, he should recite a shortened prayer. See siman 110.
Because of suspicion. They may suspect him of having a prostitute there. It is stated in the Gemara that in a ruin standing in a field there is no suspicion, since a prostitute is not commonly found there. See Even HaEzer, siman 22, seif 5. In a sturdy ruin there is no concern for collapse, and if two people enter there, there is no concern for damaging spirits, unless it is known that damaging spirits are commonly found there. A torch is like two people, and moonlight is like three.
