(13) And not in a ruin—it is proven in the Gemara that it is better to pray on the road than in a ruin, even where there is only one reason in it; and if he fears passers-by will interrupt him, he should pray a short prayer—see siman 110.

(14) Because of suspicion—lest they suspect he has a prostitute stationed there; and if the ruin stands in a field, one need not be concerned about suspicion, for prostitutes are not found in a field; the same if his wife is with him—there is also no concern for suspicion; and if it is a sound, sturdy ruin, one need not be concerned about collapse; and if two enter it, one need not fear harmful spirits—for one person they are visible and harm; for two they are visible and do not harm; for three they are not visible at all, unless in a place known to be frequented by harmful spirits; and torchlight is like two, and moonlight like three—therefore if there are two, it is permitted to enter if it is sound, sturdy, and stands in a field [Peri Megadim].
