Magen Avraham seder katan 15—in a multitude of people. Peri Megadim challenged: if so, in any case a beit hakneset is preferable to his house, for even when praying alone the Mechaber wrote that one should pray in a beit hakneset. And it appears the challenge was to Magen Avraham—that the words "with the congregation" are superfluous, for when he said one should pray in a beit hakneset we already know the intent is with the congregation, for it is forbidden to precede his prayer to the congregation's prayer. And on this he answered: he comes to teach that there are two levels—one, his prayer in a beit hakneset is itself a virtue; the second, that it is with the congregation because of "in a multitude of people is the King's glory."
