He is forbidden to precede. Even if he wishes to leave the synagogue. Rivash, siman 75.
The time is passing. Meaning, the time is so short that while he walks home the time for prayer will pass. But if there is time, he should go home and pray alone. Bach. The language of Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah does not imply this, but rather that even in the synagogue it is permitted. It is stated in the Gemara that if his heart is weak, he may pray at home before the congregation, but in the synagogue even this is forbidden. See siman 114, seif 2, that if he is sick or compelled, even in the synagogue it is permitted, as the Bach wrote there, unlike the Beit Yosef.
