It is forbidden for him to precede. Even if he wishes to go out of the synagogue [Rivash siman 75].

The hour passes. That is, the time is so short that while he walks to his house the time for prayer will pass; but if there is time, he should go to his house and pray alone [Bach]. And the language of Terumat HaDeshen does not imply so; rather, even in the synagogue it is permitted. It is stated in the Gemara that if his heart is weak he may pray in his house before the congregation, and in the synagogue even this is forbidden; and see Orach Chayyim 114 seif 2, that if he is sick or under duress, even in the synagogue it is permitted — so too the Bach there, not like the Beit Yosef.
