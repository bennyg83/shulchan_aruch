But where the time is not passing, etc. The Bach and Olas Tamid cite Rabbeinu Yonah as saying that if he sees the time passing, meaning that if he goes home to pray there, there is concern that the time will pass while he walks, it is permitted; therefore they rule that if there is time to go home, he should specifically go home. I am surprised, for Rabbeinu Yonah's language in chapter Tefillas HaShachar is not so; rather, he writes that if he sees that the time is passing and they are not praying, he prays by himself. This implies that even if he could go home, he prays in the synagogue, because once the time is passing he need not wait for the congregation even in the synagogue. So wrote Magen Avraham, and so it implies from the Beit Yosef and Levush. At home, perhaps it is permitted even when the time is not passing, though Rivash, siman 75, states that he would not go out in order to pray before the congregation's prayer.
