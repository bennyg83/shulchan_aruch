(j) With the congregation, etc. Even if there were sinners among them — Rambam chapter 8 of Hilchos Tefillah. And Sefer Chasidim siman 392 wrote: one should not sit in prayer with a wicked person, because he will think evil thoughts and the Divine Presence withdraws from him — and see also siman 393. But in SheLaH p. 249 he wrote that if the righteous person prays with intention not for the sake of the wicked person, [Hashem] will not despise [it]. And know that even though he has ten in his house, he should strive to pray in the synagogue; and so is implied in Knesset HaGedolah in the name of the Rambam; and so is implied in seif 18 regarding the sanctity of the beis midrash. However, when one sees that in the synagogue, with the majority of the people, there is mostly confusion and no person hears neither kerias haTorah nor prayer — Radbaz wrote in responsa siman 18 that it is better to pray with ten in his house. And what the Shulchan Aruch wrote "and if he is under duress," etc. — the explanation is that his strength fails, even though he is not sick; and specifically if it is burdensome for him to gather ten. Beit Yaakov wrote in responsa siman 70 that one should fine the people who prevent going to the synagogue because they are engaged in Torah or because they earn money; and for the wealthy one should fine more — up to here. Even though his proof from siman 108 seif 8 can be rejected — for there the time for prayer has passed — nevertheless we see that a matter of mitzvah is not nullified on account of money; and this too is a mitzvah, and one should consider the loss of a mitzvah against its reward; and especially for a baal Torah — even though he is engaged in study, nevertheless there is suspicion and chillul Hashem, as explained in seif 18. Magen Avraham wrote: it is implied in Avodah Zarah 4a — if one prays Musaf at the hour when the congregation prays Shacharis, he is not called "at the hour when the congregation prays"; and see later end of siman 236. He further wrote: in summer days when the congregation delays Shacharis and then [settles] in the city, it is forbidden even to learn, as explained in siman 89 seif 6 and siman 235; therefore it is better that he pray immediately at sunrise.
