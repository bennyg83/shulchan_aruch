And what he wrote "even if there are many sinners" etc.—in the language of Rambam it is not written thus, rather "even if there were among them sinners" etc. And I did not find it—Maharsha in novellae on aggadot in Kerisos wrote on that which we say "any fast in which there are not wicked ones of Israel" etc.—for behold galbanum etc. that must be read yud outside the wicked, and like incense which had eleven spices outside galbanum, see there. And again I saw in the name of Chemed L'Avraham who likewise wrote in Ayin HaKoreh, Nehar 46, see there—that he wrote specifically when there are ten righteous and one wicked, or twenty righteous and two wicked; but nine righteous have no power etc. And what he wrote from the responsum of Rashbatz and responsum of Chacham Tzvi—see Sha'agat Aryeh and She'elat Ya'avetz siman 18; and see Shabbos Tzaddik in the name of Radbaz part 1 who wrote: but now that I see the majority of the people in majority confusion, and no one hears prayer or Torah reading in beit midrash with ten, I prefer [praying] with the students even though there is no majority of the people, see there.
