(11) There, if it was three [handbreadths] high and has an area of a square cubit by a square cubit, etc. — and if it has enough to quarter a square cubit by a square cubit equally, even though its length exceeds its width or it is round — it is also permitted; and as Maran ruled in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 286 seif 13. And even though the Rosh there holds that specifically it must be a square cubit by a square cubit, as Beit Yosef wrote there and Shach siman 23 — one can say that here he admits, only there must be on the side where he stands to pray the measure so that he can take three steps on it. And see later siman 123 seif 3 and in our words there with the Omnipresent's help.
