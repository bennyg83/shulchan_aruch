(k) But without the hour passing, etc. The Bach and Olas Tamid brought the wording of Rabbeinu Yonah: if he sees the hour is passing — if he goes to his house to pray there, there is concern the hour will pass during the walk — it is permitted; and therefore they ruled: if there is time to go to his house, he should go to his house specifically. And I wonder — the wording of Rabbeinu Yonah in the chapter Tefilas HaShachar is not so; rather this is his wording: if he sees the hour is passing and they are not praying, he prays by himself — up to here his wording. It implies that even though it is possible to go to his house — nevertheless he prays in the synagogue, for since the hour is passing he need not wait for the congregation, even in the synagogue; and so wrote Magen Avraham; and so is implied in Beit Yosef and Levush. And in his house it is possible it is permitted even if the hour is not passing — only that Rivash siman 75 states that he would not go out to precede his prayer to the congregation's prayer.
