That he has, etc. If there are two synagogues in the city, it is good to go to the farther one, for there is reward for the steps.
He is called, etc. For this is the way of bad neighbors, who do not enter their neighbors' house. See the later authorities, who wrote that this applies even if there is no minyan of ten there, for the teaching is stated without qualification; the Peri Megadim is unsure about this. If he prays at home with ten, in any case he is not called a bad neighbor, for wherever there are ten the Shechinah rests. Nevertheless, he has not fulfilled the obligation of the synagogue unless that place where they pray is fixed for sanctity.
A bad neighbor, etc. As it is written, 'So said Hashem concerning all My evil neighbors, etc.: Behold, I will uproot them from their land.' Whoever comes early and stays late in the synagogue merits length of days, as it says, 'Fortunate is the man who listens to Me, watching at My doors day by day,' and after it is written, 'For one who finds Me has found life.'
