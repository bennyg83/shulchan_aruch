(37) "Who has" etc.—and if there are two battei knesset in the city, it is good to go to the farther one, for there is reward for steps.

(38) "Is called" etc.—for such is the way of bad neighbors who do not enter their friends' homes. See in later authorities who wrote that even if there is no minyan of ten, for the Stam Mishnah implies so; Peri Megadim is in doubt about this. And if one prays in one's house with ten in all cases, one is not called a bad neighbor, for in every house of ten the Divine Presence rests; however, one does not exempt oneself from the obligation of the beit knesset unless that place where they pray is established for holiness.

(39) "Bad neighbor" etc.—as written "So said Hashem concerning all My evil neighbors..." And whoever goes early and late to the beit knesset merits long life, as it says "Fortunate is the person who hears Me, to watch at My doors day by day," and it is written after it "for whoever finds Me finds life," etc.
