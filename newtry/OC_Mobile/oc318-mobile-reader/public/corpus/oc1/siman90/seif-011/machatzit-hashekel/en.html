Seif katan 11, there is, etc. And although, etc. Meaning, since he wants to satisfy both opinions, one standing, for example, north of the synagogue must turn toward the synagogue, so that his face is south, and his back is not to the synagogue. Even if his face is east and the side of his body is toward the synagogue it is also permitted, as stated in Eliyah Rabbah. Nevertheless, he also satisfies the view that one must pray toward the direction of the congregation, since at least he is not doing the opposite of the congregation, such as where they face east and he faces west. But in this case, where he faces south, we have no concern.
