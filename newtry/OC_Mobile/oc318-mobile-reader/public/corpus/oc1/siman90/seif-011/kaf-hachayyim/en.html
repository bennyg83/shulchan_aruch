11) There: If it was three handbreadths high and measured four amos by four amos, etc. If it has sufficient area to contain a square of four amos by four amos, it is permitted even if its length exceeds its width or it is circular, as Maran rules in Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah 286:13. Although Rosh there holds that it must actually be four amos by four amos, as stated in Beis Yosef there and Shach 23, one may say that here he agrees. On the side where the person stands to pray, however, there must be enough space for him to take three steps. See below, siman 123:3, and our comments there, with God's help.
