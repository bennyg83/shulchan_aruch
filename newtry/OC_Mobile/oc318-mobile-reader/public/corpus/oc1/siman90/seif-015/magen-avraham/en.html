His fellow is obligated to wait for him. And the Bach ruled in the name of Terumat HaDeshen that if one enters alone at a time when he will not be able to finish his prayer with them, his fellow is not obligated to wait — for the matter is evident that he entered with foreknowledge that this is how he enters, that he is not concerned about it; nevertheless it implies that Rabbi Yehuda would wait even in such a case.
