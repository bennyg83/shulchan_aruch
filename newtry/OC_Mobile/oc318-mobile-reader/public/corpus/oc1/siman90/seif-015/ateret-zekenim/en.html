If one person remains alone in the synagogue, etc. Even if those who remained last did not enter together, one should wait for his fellow. However, if one entered alone at a time when he would not be able to finish his prayer with them, his fellow is not obligated to wait for him, for since he could not finish with them and nevertheless entered, it is revealed that he entered with this in mind, and he is not afraid if he remains alone (Rabbeinu Yonah, first chapter of Berachos, page 4). The Ri was stringent upon himself and would wait even for such a person.
