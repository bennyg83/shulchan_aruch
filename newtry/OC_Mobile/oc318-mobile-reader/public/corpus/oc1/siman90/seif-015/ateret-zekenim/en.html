If a person remains alone in the beit hakneset, etc. Even though those who remained last did not enter together, one should wait for his fellow. However, if one entered alone at a time when he cannot finish his prayer with them, his fellow is not obligated to wait for him, since he cannot finish with them and yet entered — the matter is revealed that he entered with this intent and is not afraid if one remains alone (R' Yonah, ch. 1 of Berachos, p. 4). And R' Yitzchak would be stringent on himself: if one man came to the beit hakneset he would look in a book until he finished his prayer (Mordechai).
