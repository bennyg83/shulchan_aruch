If a single individual remains praying in synagogue in the fields or even in a synagogue that is in the city - if it is the evening prayer , his fellow is obligated to wait for him until he finishes his prayer, in order that he should not mix up his prayer. . And if [the individual] lengthens his prayer with additional requests and supplications, one is not obligated to wait for him. (That is prayed at night. And there are those who are stringent even during the day and in our synagogues that are in the city [Tur and Mordechai in the name of Rashi, and Rashi on the first chapter of Berachot].) חייב חבירו להמתין לו עד שיסיים תפלתו כדי שלא יתבלבל בתפלתו (ויש מחמירין אפי' ביום ובב"ה שלנו שהם בעיר) (הטור ומרדכי בשם ר"י והר"י פ"ק דברכות) ואם מאריך בבקשות ותחנונים אינו חייב להמתין לו:
