If a single individual remains praying in synagogue in the fields or even in a synagogue that is in the city - if it is the evening prayer , his fellow is obligated to wait for him until he finishes his prayer, in order that he should not mix up his prayer. . And if [the individual] lengthens his prayer with additional requests and supplications, one is not obligated to wait for him. {Rama: (that is prayed at night) (And there are those who are strict [and apply this] even during the day and to our synagogues which are in the city) (The Tur and the Mordechai in the name of the R"i, and the R"i on the first chapter of Berachot)}
