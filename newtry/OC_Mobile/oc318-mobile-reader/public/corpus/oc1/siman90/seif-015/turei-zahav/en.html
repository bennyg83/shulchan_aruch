If one person remains alone. Beit Yosef cites Rabbeinu Yonah, who states that if a person entered alone at a time when he could not finish his prayer together with the congregation, another person is not obligated to wait for him. Since he saw that he would not be able to finish with them and nevertheless entered, it is evident that he entered with this understanding and is not afraid to remain alone. My father-in-law wrote that this is also the Tur's view, for the Tur wrote, 'Two people who entered a synagogue,' indicating specifically that they began together at the outset.
