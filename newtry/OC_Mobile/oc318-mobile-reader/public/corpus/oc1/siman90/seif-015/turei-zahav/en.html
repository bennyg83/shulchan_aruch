If a person remained alone. In Beit Yosef he cites the words of R' Yehuda: if one enters alone at a time when he cannot finish his prayer with them, his companion is not obligated to wait for him, since he sees he cannot finish with them and yet enters — the matter is revealed that he entered with this awareness and is not afraid if one remains alone, etc. Maharach wrote that such is also the Tur's view, who wrote two who entered the synagogue, etc. — which teaches this intent that at first they specifically began together.
