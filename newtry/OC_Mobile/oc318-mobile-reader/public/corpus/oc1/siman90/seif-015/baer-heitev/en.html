Specifically if they entered together; but if one entered alone at a time when he cannot finish his prayer with them, one is not obligated to wait for him, since he sees that he cannot finish with them and yet entered — the matter is revealed that he is not afraid if one remains alone. Bach. However, it is a measure of piety to wait even in such a case, and so wrote the Mordechai in the name of R' Yitzchak, who would wait even in such a case.
