To wait. This is specifically if they entered together. But if one entered alone at a time when he could not finish his prayer with them, they are not obligated to wait for him, for since he saw that he could not finish with them and entered, it is revealed that he is not afraid to remain alone. Bach. Nevertheless, it is an act of piety to wait even in such a case, and so the Mordechai wrote in the name of Ri, that he would wait even then. The earlier Baer Heitev abbreviated where he should have elaborated; see there.
