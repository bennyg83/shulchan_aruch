Magen Avraham, seif katan 12. In the courtyard. This refers to a courtyard where the synagogue stands in the middle and is open on every side, so when he turns his face it is recognizable. This is unlike the courtyard mentioned in the next seif katan, which is built on the east of the synagogue with the synagogue wall separating, and he prays eastward. There it is not so recognizable, since he has no other place to stand and necessarily prays eastward, so this is not disrespectful, Heaven forbid. So it is in the Beit Yosef.
