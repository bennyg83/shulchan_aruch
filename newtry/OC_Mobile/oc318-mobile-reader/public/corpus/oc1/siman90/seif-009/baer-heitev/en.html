The congregation. Even if he can pray at home with ten, nevertheless, 'in a multitude of people is the King's glory.' Magen Avraham. Even if there are many sinners among the congregation, he should not refrain from praying with them. Kenesses HaGedolah; Olas Tamid. If one was imprisoned and could not pray with ten, and he pleaded with the officer, who allowed him only one day, the Radbaz, part 4, siman 13, ruled that he should not let the mitzvah become delayed, but should immediately pray with ten. Chacham Tzvi, siman 106, challenged him from Menachos 49 regarding a congregation that has either daily offerings or additional offerings. Leket HaKemach distinguishes between an individual and a congregation, for a congregation does not die, but with an individual we do not delay a mitzvah. Yad Aharon answers that where offering one korban prevents the other, the doubt is which overrides which, but here he does not lose the mitzvah entirely, only that by waiting for Yom Kippur he would perform it more completely. See Responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 42. If one goes early on Shabbos or Yom Tov to pray with a minyan because most of the congregation prays late, and that minyan also prays Mussaf immediately after Shacharis, while there is later time to go to the synagogue, it is better to pray Mussaf with the congregation in the synagogue, because 'in a multitude of people is the King's glory.' This is not like the case of the Radbaz, where much time separates them and there are concerns and causes; within one day we are not concerned. Yad Eliyahu, siman 42.
Compelled. This means that his strength is weakened, even though he is not sick. Magen Avraham. If the compulsion is financial, meaning that by making the effort to pray with the congregation he will suffer monetary loss, Responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 7, rules that according to the law he is not obligated, and he may pray alone at home or in the synagogue when the congregation is not there. Perhaps for preventing profit he is somewhat obligated by law to pray with the congregation, for there is a distinction between loss of profit and loss from his own capital; see there. Whether one may travel in a small or large boat on Shabbos to complete a minyan and pray with the congregation, see Radbaz, part 1, siman 87; Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 115; Responsa Avodas HaGershuni, siman 123; and Responsa Maharam of Rothenburg, simanim 1 and 2.
They pray. This is specifically when it is burdensome for him to gather ten. Magen Avraham. From Avodah Zarah 4b it implies that if one prays Mussaf at the time the congregation prays Shacharis, this is not called the time when the congregation prays; see Tosafot there.
Settlements. It seems to me that in the summer, when the congregation delays Shacharis, one who lives in a settlement is forbidden even to learn, as written in siman 89. Therefore it is better that he pray immediately at sunrise. For Maariv he must wait until nightfall. Magen Avraham.
