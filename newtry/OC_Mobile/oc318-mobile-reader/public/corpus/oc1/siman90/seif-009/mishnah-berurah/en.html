If he has two synagogues and one has a larger congregation, he is obligated to pray there more. The later authorities wrote that if the beit knesset of the larger congregation has much confusion and a person cannot hear neither prayer nor Torah reading, it is better to pray at home with ten. Between praying alone in the beit knesset and at home with ten, communal prayer is preferable; and even if he can hear Kaddish and Barchu, nevertheless communal prayer is preferable [Peri Megadim]. The essence of prayer with a congregation is the Shemoneh Esreh—that ten adult men pray together—and not as the masses think, that the main thing of praying with ten is only to hear Kaddish, Kedushah, and Barchu; therefore they are not strict that there be ten in the beit knesset—and this is an error; therefore it is an obligation on a person to hurry to come to the beit knesset so that he arrive to pray Shemoneh Esreh with the congregation [Chayei Adam]. One who goes on Shabbat and Yom Tov early to pray in a minyan of ten because most of the congregation delay their prayer, and in that minyan they also pray Musaf immediately after Shacharit and there is time afterward to go to the beit knesset—it is better to go to pray Musaf with the congregation in the beit knesset, for in a large congregation is the splendor of the King—so too in responsa Yad Eliyahu siman 42; and specifically if his designated place for prayer is in the beit knesset; but if his place is not designated to pray in the beit knesset, it is better that he pray the entire prayer in one place. Radbaz wrote in responsa regarding one held by the prince who gave him permission one day to pray with the congregation in a minyan—whichever day he wishes, he should pray that day immediately and not delay the mitzvah to wait for Yom Kippur or Purim; and see Chayei Adam klal 68 and Neshmas Adam, who elaborated on several details in this matter.
