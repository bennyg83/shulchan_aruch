With the congregation. Even though he can pray in his house with ten, nevertheless "in the multitude of people is the King's glory" — see Yoma and end of siman 490.

Compelled (anus). This means his strength is weakened; even though he is not sick — for if R' Nachman were sick, then why did he ask him "Why did my master not come to the synagogue?" — unless he was not actually sick.

He should intend to pray, etc. And specifically if the matter is burdensome for him to gather ten; and in Semag it seems R' Nachman did not want to burden ten men to come to him. It seems in Or Zarua p. 4b that if one prays Musaf at the time the congregation prays Shacharit, it is not called "at the time the congregation prays"; and it is stated there in Tosafos that if one prays at the time the congregation prays, his prayer is not rejected but it is not heard except with the congregation in the synagogue; however it is possible they spoke only of a time of wrath — see end of siman 591.

And the same applies to people living in settlements, etc. And it seems to me in summer days when the congregation delays Shacharit and one lives in a settlement, it is forbidden even to learn Torah as written siman 89 seif 6 and siman 232; therefore it is better that he pray immediately at sunrise.
