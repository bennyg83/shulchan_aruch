With the congregation. Even though he can pray at home with ten, nevertheless, 'in a multitude of people is the King's glory.' See seif 18 and the end of siman 690.
Compelled. Meaning that his strength is weakened although he is not sick. For if Rabbi Nachman had been sick, why was he asked why the master did not come to the synagogue? Rather, he was not actually sick.
He should aim to pray, etc. This is specifically if it is burdensome for him to gather ten. In Semag it implies that Rabbi Nachman did not want to burden ten people to come to him. From Avodah Zarah 4b it implies that if one prays Mussaf when the congregation prays Shacharis, this is not called the time when the congregation prays. Tosafot there says that if one prays at the time the congregation prays, his prayer is not rejected, but it is heard only with the congregation in the synagogue. However, perhaps they said this specifically at a time of anger. See what I wrote at the end of siman 591.
The same applies to people living in settlements, etc. It seems to me that in the summer, when the congregation delays Shacharis, one who lives in a settlement is forbidden even to learn, as written in siman 89, seif 6, and siman 232. Therefore it is better that he pray immediately at sunrise.
At the time the congregation. Meaning, at the time when Jewish communities pray. Semag. The Shelah writes that in Ashkenaz, where they customarily pray Maariv early, as written in siman 235, an individual living in a village or traveling must wait until nightfall. See what I wrote in siman 236.
