The congregation. If he was summoned to perform a circumcision, it would seem obvious that the Rabbinic mitzvah of praying with the congregation is deferred. However, Responsa Beit Yehudah, siman 48, concluded that since the obligation of circumcision rests only upon the child's father, it is obvious that the mohel should occupy himself with prayer together with the congregation. All the more so, hearing the reading of the Megillah takes precedence over circumcising another person's son. See there.
