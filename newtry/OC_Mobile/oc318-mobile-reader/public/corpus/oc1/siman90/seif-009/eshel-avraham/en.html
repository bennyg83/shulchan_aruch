The congregation. If they called him for circumcision — apparently it is a simple matter that a rabbinic mitzvah is pushed aside. But in responsa Beis Yehuda siman 48 he raised: since the obligation is not on him but on the father of the child — it is obvious to him that he should engage in prayer with the congregation. And all the more so to hear kerias haMegillah before the circumcision of another's son — with the Omnipresent's help.
