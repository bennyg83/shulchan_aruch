Section 69 — one should strive, etc. Tur: one should not pray except in the synagogue with the congregation, for R' Yochanan said a person's prayer is heard only in the synagogue — explained as with the congregation; and this is the girsa of the Rif and Rosh there 8a, for R' Yitzchak said his prayer, etc., as it says "And as for me, my prayer," etc., "When is the time of favor," etc.

And if he is, etc. There: "and let the master say," etc.

And likewise if he is compelled, etc. There 6a, it was taught: Abba Binyamin, etc.
