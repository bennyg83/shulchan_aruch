A person should strive to pray in the beit knesset with the congregation. If he is an oness such that he cannot come to the beit knesset, he should intend to pray at the' time the congregation prays.[The same applies to people living in settlements who do not have a minyan—nevertheless they should pray Shacharit and Maariv at the time the congregation prays (Semag).} And similarly if he was an oness and did not pray at the time the congregation prayed and he prays alone—even so he should pray in the beit knesset.] וכן אם נאנס ולא התפלל בשעה שהתפללו הציבו' והוא מתפלל ביחיד אעפ"כ יתפלל בב"ה:
