What they pray on the platform—and these are beds made of wood or stone that divide half the house like a pillar and are fixed in the ground and wall—it appears they are considered like the ground, and their law is not like a bed regarding not placing food beneath them, and likewise regarding mourning that one should sit only on the ground. However, when people gather in a house to pray and all or most are below, it appears the individuals standing above do not act properly unless in a place where it is impossible. Likkutei Mahari"a chelek 2 siman 122, and Eliyah Rabbah brought it in Hagahot Tur and wrote on it: I see no concern in this, and I have seen many great ones who were not concerned about it, so far. And Beit Yaakov brought it there note 21 and wrote, and so is the main ruling.
