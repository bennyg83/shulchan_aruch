He should not pray. In the Gemara it says that one who transgresses this is brazen. The Peri Megadim writes that therefore, if he has the choice between a house without windows and an open valley, it is better to pray in the house without windows.
In an open place. According to one answer of Tosafot, if he stands at the side of the road where he is not afraid that passersby will interrupt him, it is permitted. But the Beit Yosef rejects their words. Although the Magen Avraham and other later authorities defend Tosafot, they nevertheless wrote that the Zohar implies that one should pray specifically in a house. Travelers are permitted according to all views to pray in the field. Nevertheless, if there are trees there, it is better that he stand among them and pray, if delaying his journey is not difficult, for it is thereby somewhat private. But when he is at home one should not rely on this.
Like in a field. In a place surrounded by partitions, even if it is not roofed, it is proper.
