(e) And because, etc. — even with two [walls] and fit [for use] and a new ruin where there is no concern of a harlot and collapse — nevertheless it is forbidden on account of harmful spirits, for in their place we are concerned — Berachos 3a, and Tosafos explained: specifically when it is known that harmful spirits regularly [dwell] there; but in an unspecified case there is no concern of harmful spirits with two — see there. And a torch — two; and the moon — three. It appears from the Gemara there that the same law applies even without prayer — it is forbidden to enter; only that the poskim inform us from the amplification that even for prayer it is forbidden.
