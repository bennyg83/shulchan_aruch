For example—Taz expanded, and see in Magen Avraham letter 36 on this. And what he wrote to distinguish when there is need for prayer—Magen Avraham rejected this, see there. And what he wrote to close his eyes—the reason is so he should not look at that thing; but below siman 98 in the author, on account of interposition between him and a vessel, likewise he should not press between himself and a wall—if so closing the eyes does not help; and one can say on this: within four amos it is forbidden, and outside four amos it is another domain and permitted (Magen Avraham letter 36). And with this is resolved what the author wrote in sec. 22 that one should be careful to pray behind a person, and that "one shall not pray behind his rabbi" is also a prohibition; and Eliyah Rabbah challenged, letter 27—Rabbi Yirmiyah, how did he pray behind Rav initially? See Berachos 27b; and one can say Rabbi Yirmiyah was praying outside restricted four amos and did not distance the measure of three steps—Magen Avraham letter 38—and understand this.
