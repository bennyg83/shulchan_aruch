In a place of modesty, etc. Tosafos wrote: You might ask — is it not written And Isaac went out to pray in the field? And one may answer that there the case is Mount Moriah (meaning it was called a field but was not open). And one may answer regarding the passage here that it deals with a valley in a place where people regularly pass and travelers go, etc. And as Beit Yosef — one might say from the second version that the reason was so passers-by would not interrupt him, and that reason is not sufficient to call him impudent, etc. And one might wonder at his wondering — for Tosafos did not intend because of interruption, but since people regularly pass there it is called not in a place of modesty.
