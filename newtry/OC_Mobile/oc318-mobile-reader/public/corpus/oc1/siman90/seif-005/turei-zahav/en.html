In a secluded place, and so forth. Tosafos wrote: One may ask, Scripture states, 'Yitzchak went out to converse in the field.' They answer that this refers to Mount Moriah, meaning that it was called a field but was not open and exposed. Alternatively, the valley discussed here is a place where people and travelers commonly pass. Beit Yosef wrote that Tosafos' second answer is difficult, for it implies that the reason is to prevent travelers from interrupting him, and that is not sufficient reason to call him brazen. His objection itself is surprising. Tosafos did not mean that the concern is interruption. Rather, because people commonly pass there, it is considered a place lacking seclusion.
