(to run). Until the entrance of the beit hakneset; and in the beit hakneset itself it is forbidden to run, but one should walk with awe and fear. See Peri Megadim, Magen Avraham.

(to run). But if one goes out with intent to return, it is a mitzvah to run so that he return quickly. The author in Beit Yosef, and so too Magen Avraham and Knesset HaGedolah, not like the Levush. And the Shlah wrote that one who goes out from the beit hakneset to the beit midrash should run. Magen Avraham.
