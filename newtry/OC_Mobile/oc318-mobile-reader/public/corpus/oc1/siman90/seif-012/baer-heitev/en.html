To run. Until the entrance of the synagogue. In the synagogue itself it is forbidden to run; rather, he should walk with awe and fear. Olas Tamid; Shelah; Magen Avraham.
To run. But if he leaves in order to return, it is a mitzvah to run so that he will return quickly. So wrote the author in Ein Yaakov, and so wrote Magen Avraham and Kenesses HaGedolah, unlike Lechem Chamudos. The Shelah wrote that one who leaves the synagogue for the study hall should run. Magen Avraham.
