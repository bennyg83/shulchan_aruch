Derech Chaim by Maharam di Lonzano writes: When choosing between a congregation in a synagogue and a congregation outside the synagogue, the teaching of Abba Binyamin that 'a person's prayer is not heard, etc.' applies. When choosing between a congregation outside the synagogue and an individual praying in the synagogue, the congregation outside the synagogue is preferable. See Yad Eliyahu, siman 7.
