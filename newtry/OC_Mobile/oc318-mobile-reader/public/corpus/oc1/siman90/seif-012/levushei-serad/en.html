Seif katan 16. It was, meaning Rabbi Nachman. In Berachos, Rabbi Nachman once did not come to the synagogue, and they asked him why the master did not come. He answered: my strength was weak. They said to him: you should have gathered ten. He replied: it was burdensome for me. They said to him: at least you should have arranged to pray at the time the congregation prays. With this, his words here and in the following seif katan are explained.
