Note 16. "Was" means Rabbenu Nissim, who says in Berachos: Rabbenu [the elder] never came once to the beit hakneset. They asked him: why does my master not come? He said: my strength has weakened. They told him: you should have gathered ten. He answered: the matter is burdensome for me. They told him: you should at least have directed yourself to pray at the time the congregation prays. And by this his words are explained here and in the note after it.
