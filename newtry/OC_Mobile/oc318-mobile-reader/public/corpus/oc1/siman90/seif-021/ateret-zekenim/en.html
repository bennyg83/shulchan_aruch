He must have no interposing object, etc. But a bed is not fixed in place; therefore if it is ten handbreadths high and four handbreadths wide it interposes (Tosafos). And it appears this is specifically a bed in the time of the Gemara that was for sitting; but our bed made for lying down is considered fixed and does not interpose (Beit Yosef). And some say even if not made for lying, since sometimes they move it from the place of the bed to another place, it interposes (my teacher the Bach).
