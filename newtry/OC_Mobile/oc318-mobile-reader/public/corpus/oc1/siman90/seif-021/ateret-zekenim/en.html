There must not be anything interposing, etc. But a bed is not fixed; therefore, if it is ten tefachim high and four tefachim wide, it interposes (Tosafot). It appears that this applies specifically to the bed of the Talmudic period, which was used for sitting. But our bed, which is made for lying, is fixed and does not interpose (Beit Yosef). Some say that even if it is not made for lying, since sometimes the bed is moved from place to place, it interposes (my teacher, the Bach).
