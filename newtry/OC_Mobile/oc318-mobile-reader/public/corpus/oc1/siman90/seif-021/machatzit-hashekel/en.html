Seif katan 21, the hour, etc. Meaning, the time is short, etc., and therefore he is permitted to pray before the congregation even in the synagogue.
The language of Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah does not imply this; see the Beit Yosef who cites his language. Berachos 28b states that Rav Avya had an incident and did not come to Rav Yosef's derashah. The next day, when he came before Rav Yosef, Abaye wanted to appease Rav Yosef so he would not bear resentment against Rav Avya. Abaye asked Rav Avya why the master did not come yesterday. He answered that his heart was weak and he could not. Abaye asked why he did not taste something and come. He answered: does the master not hold that it is forbidden to taste anything before praying Mussaf, since Rav Yosef's derashah was before Mussaf? Abaye said: you should have prayed Mussaf alone, then tasted something and come. Rav Avya replied: does the master not hold that it is forbidden to pray before the congregation? Abaye answered that they taught this only in the congregation, meaning in the synagogue.
And in the synagogue even this is forbidden. Meaning, even when his heart is weak, for Rav Avya answered Abaye that it is forbidden to pray before the congregation even though his heart was weak. Abaye answered that they taught this specifically in the congregation, implying even in such a case. But when his heart is not weak, even alone it is forbidden, as it seems. See siman 114, seif 2, where it says that if one is sick or compelled he still may not precede the congregation on the last day of Sukkos regarding mentioning rain until the shaliach tzibbur announces it, because it is forbidden to mention rain before the announcement. The Beit Yosef explains that this teaches even when he is not praying with the congregation, for within the congregation the prohibition to precede the congregation applies all year.
