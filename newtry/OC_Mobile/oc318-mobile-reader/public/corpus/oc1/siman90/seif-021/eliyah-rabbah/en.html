(u) He should not regularly [pray at home], etc. — only occasionally when alone, etc. (Levush). Malbishei Yom Tov wrote on his words: the words of the Rosh in a responsum — each reason by itself suffices, and examine closely — up to here his wording. And apparently it is difficult — for also in the words of the Rama one must say thus, since he also wrote like the Levush. And I examined Lechem Chamudos p. 5 — he wrote in truth thus on the Rama; see there at length — it appears his opinion that one whose Torah is his craft and he does not neglect it may pray always at home. And in my humble opinion the opinion of the Rama and Levush: in the time of the Gemara there were many whose Torah was their craft; and if so, they judged them favorably even if they prayed in their homes. But now that there is not even one from a city and two from a family whose Torah is his craft — we are concerned they will not judge him favorably, for the multitude of people do not know this; and therefore he should not regularly [do so]. And now it is well understood why the Rosh needed all the reasons; and examine closely.
