He should not accustom himself, etc. Only occasionally and for individuals. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote that according to the Levush, each reason of the Rosh in his responsum is sufficient by itself. At first glance this is difficult, for the Rama too must be read this way, since he writes like the Levush. I examined Lechem Chamudos, and he indeed wrote this regarding the Rama, explaining at length that one whose Torah is his occupation and who does not neglect it may always pray at home. In my humble opinion, the Rama and Levush hold that in the time of the Gemara many had Torah as their occupation, and therefore they judged them favorably even when they prayed at home. But now, when there is hardly one in a city and two in a family whose Torah is truly their occupation, we are concerned that people will not judge him favorably, for the masses will not know this. Therefore he should not accustom himself to it, and the Rosh therefore needed all his reasons.
