(q) When he comes, etc. — that even if a hundred come after him, he receives reward corresponding to all of them; also his days and years are lengthened. Rabbeinu Yonah explained: each one of the ten receives reward for all those coming after them. But in the first ten themselves there is no rule of precedence of one over his fellow. But Maadaney Melech p. 3 explained that even in the ten themselves — whoever is earlier prevails: the first receives reward for the nine and all coming after them; the second receives reward for the eight and those coming after them — and so with all ten. It is stated in the chapter Sheloshah She'achlu [47b] that it is permitted to free his slave to complete the ten. And what Magen Avraham wondered on the Ran — see in Chiddushei Rashba chapter HaSholeach [Gittin 38b] and it will be settled.
