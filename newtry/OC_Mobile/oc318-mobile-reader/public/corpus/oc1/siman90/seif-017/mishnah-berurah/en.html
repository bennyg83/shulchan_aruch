(53) To leave the city etc.—this deals with one traveling for a personal matter. See Peri Megadim who questioned that here it implies specifically if thereby he will miss prayer with the congregation, and in such a case it is permitted; but otherwise it is forbidden to set out on the road before Shacharit prayer, as above in siman 89, seif 3. However, per what Rama wrote in that gloss, it is satisfactory that we deal with one who said the berachot before prayer and nevertheless it is forbidden here because of missing prayer with the congregation.

(54) "And that he should not" etc.—but if he now fears and does not wish to wait for him, it is permitted.
