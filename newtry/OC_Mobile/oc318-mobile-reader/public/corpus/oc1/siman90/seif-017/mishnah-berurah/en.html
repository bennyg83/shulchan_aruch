To go from a city, etc. This speaks of one going for an optional matter. See Peri Megadim, who asks that here it implies that only if this will cause him to miss prayer with the congregation is it forbidden, but otherwise it is permitted, whereas even aside from this it is forbidden to set out before the Shemoneh Esrei, as above in siman 89, seif 3. According to what the Rama wrote there in the gloss, it is resolved, for it speaks where he said the blessings before prayer, and even so it is forbidden here because he will miss prayer with the congregation.
And that there not be, etc. But if he now has a caravan and it does not want to wait for him, it is permitted.
