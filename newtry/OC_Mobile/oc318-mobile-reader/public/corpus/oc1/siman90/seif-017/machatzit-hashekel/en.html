(Note 17) He should direct his intent, etc. — and specifically, etc., as is stated in that incident mentioned above: when he said to him "I am unable," he said to him, "Let my master gather ten in his house." He said to him, "The matter is burdensome for me." He said to him, "Let my master tell the prayer leader at the time the congregation prays that he is not present, and inform my master so that he may pray at the time the congregation prays." It implies that if it is possible to gather ten it is preferable — for first he told him to gather ten before he told him to pray at the time the congregation prays.

And in Semag, etc. — per the first explanation, that "trouble of a matter" refers to the homeowner; if so, the matter depends on the homeowner; and if one says there are many servants who can gather ten for him — he must gather ten. But per the explanation of Semag that the trouble refers to the ten men — if so, in every matter trouble applies, unless they came on their own.

It implies in Avodah Zarah 4a: Rabbi Yosef — one should not pray Musaf at the beginning of the year in the first three hours of the day, for then it is the time of wrath above, for sources gaze at his deeds and turn away his prayer. And the Gemara challenges: if so, Shacharis too one should not pray in the first three hours. And they answer: since the congregation also prays then for Shacharis, his prayer is not delayed, since he prays at least at the hour when the congregation prays — therefore. Behold, even at the hour when the congregation prays Shacharis he may not pray Musaf, lest it be delayed. Perforce one must say this is when the congregation prays; and it is stated there in Tosafot on this that they answer: I speak of Shacharis, for the congregation also prays — it implies that it suffices when he prays at the hour when the congregation prays, even though he does not pray with the congregation. They challenged: behold we say in Berachot that prayer is not heard unless one prays with the congregation. And they explain to distinguish between "it is not delayed" and "it is heard." The distinction: "it is not delayed" means the prayer is accepted willingly above and he receives reward for it like one who receives reward for any mitzvah of God; but they do not fulfill his request that he prayed for. But when we say "it is heard," they do his will in what he prayed for that matter. And even though our eyes see that even the congregation's prayer is sometimes not heard — Shelah already explained with several resolutions; and one of them is that they do for them something like what they prayed for, even though they do not fulfill all their requests; nevertheless they fulfill some of their requests — see there, where he elaborated.

However, it is possible, etc. — specifically at the time of wrath. And even though Tosafot brought proof from what is stated in Berachot that his prayer is not heard except with the congregation (meaning that which is stated in Berachot 6a: it is taught, Abba Binyamin says: his prayer is not heard except in a synagogue, as it is written "to hear the song," etc.; and Tosafot hold that in a synagogue means to pray with the congregation; and so Tur explained in this siman — see there; and in Beit Yosef). And per Magen Avraham, Rabbi Akiva Eiger returned to challenge on this: how did he state plainly "his prayer is not heard"; rather, sometimes even when he is not above it is heard, such as when it is not the time of wrath; and perhaps one may say that even when not at the time of wrath it is not heard when he does not pray above, unless he prays at least at the hour when the congregation prays; and Shacharis of the congregation is at the time of wrath; and it deals there with the baraita of Abba Binyamin in Tosafot; and somewhat his proof from "to hear the song," etc. — in place of song there shall be prayer; and Rashi explained: in place of song in a synagogue where they say songs and praises with pleasant evening voice — until here; and this is at Shacharis when they say verses of song; even though one may reject "in place of song" of Shacharis that there all prayers apply, even of Mincha; nevertheless one may say thus; or one may say Abba Binyamin means his prayer is not heard at any time, even at the time of wrath, except in a synagogue with the congregation.

See above siman 491. When is the time of wrath — per Rashi, the first hour of the day; and per Tosafot, the third hour; and per Tur and Shulchan Aruch, all three first hours; even though not all of them are the time of wrath, nevertheless since the Holy One, blessed be He, is then engaged in Torah, He does not show a favorable face; and one should be concerned his prayer will be delayed — see there.
