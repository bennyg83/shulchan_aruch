A person should rise early for the beit hakneset in order to be counted among the first ten. {Rama: And a person should always strive to go early to the beit hakneset even if he will not be among the first ten, for whoever precedes is closer to the root of holiness and the last ones draw from branches of branches. And there are men of deed who have the custom that on the day they do not go to be among the first ten they fast — from here you may infer what penalty you deserve (To'alot Chayyim) — and go to the beit hakneset.}
