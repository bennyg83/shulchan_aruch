A person should rise early to the synagogue so that he may be counted among the first ten. A gloss on this: a person should always endeavor to come early to the synagogue, even if he will not be among the first ten, for whoever comes earlier is closer to the root of holiness, and those who come later draw from branches of branches. Some men of practice have the custom that on a day when they do not go to be among the first ten, they fast. From here judge for yourself what penalty is fitting (Totsaos Chaim). And he should go to the synagogue.
