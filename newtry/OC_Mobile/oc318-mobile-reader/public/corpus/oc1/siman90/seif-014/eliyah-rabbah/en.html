With the people, etc. Even if a hundred come after him, he receives reward corresponding to all of them, and his days and years are lengthened. Rabbeinu Yonah explains that each of the first ten receives reward corresponding to all who come after them, but among the first ten themselves there is no precedence of one over another. Ma'adanei Melech explains that even among the first ten, whoever came earlier merits more: the first receives reward corresponding to the nine and all who come after them, the second corresponding to the eight and those after them, and so on for all ten. In Berachos 47b it says that one may free his slave to complete a minyan of ten. Regarding the Magen Avraham's question on the Ran, see Chiddushei Rashba, Gittin 38b, and it will be resolved.
