That he be counted among the first ten. This is specifically if he prays there with them. The Arizal was not one of the first ten because he needed to relieve himself, since he had an illness, and also so that he could go to the synagogue wrapped in tallis and tefillin, which is impossible before daybreak. See what I wrote in siman 25. In the Zohar, Naso, it says that ten should gather and come together to the synagogue.
