He should count himself among the first ten. Specifically that he pray there with them; and the Ari was not one of the first ten, for he needed to turn aside because he had an illness, and also in order to go wrapped in tallis and tefillin to the synagogue — and this is impossible before day [Kavanos]; see siman 25. And in the Zohar it is written in Parshas Naso, page 239, that ten should gather and come together to the synagogue.
