There, {Rama: and even if they are not three tefachim high}. Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari"a, and the reason Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Mahari"a: because one must be concerned for distraction—that he is distracted, perhaps he will fall and will not have intent in his prayer. But Perishah note 1 wrote that in less than three tefachim the concept of a high place in prayer does not apply; rather this is what it means: not on a bed or any vessel, even in less than three tefachim—because of separation, for we require prayer similar to the Temple service; and in the service, if there was a vessel between him and the ground it was a separation—and as Tur wrote below siman 98. And afterward he said that even on a high place, even if it is solid ground where separation does not apply, it is forbidden—and this prohibition is only when it is three tefachim high; but in less than three it is like the ground and the concept of height does not apply, see there. And see in the note after it and below siman 98 in sufficient detail note 26.
