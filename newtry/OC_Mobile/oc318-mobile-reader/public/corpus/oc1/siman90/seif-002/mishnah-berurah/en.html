(6) A high place, etc. — meaning: even if it is loose ground and its height is three tefachim, it is forbidden to stand on it to pray; but less than that, its height is not noticeable.

(7) Enclosed by partitions — and a new bimah in the synagogue that has not yet been enclosed by partitions, it is plainly forbidden to pray on it; however, regarding a shamash whose place is to stand there regularly for synagogue needs, it appears to be permitted, as with craftsmen in note 63 [Peri Megadim].
