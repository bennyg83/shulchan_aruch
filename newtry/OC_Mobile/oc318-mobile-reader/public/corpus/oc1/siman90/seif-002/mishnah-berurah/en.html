A high place, etc. Meaning, even if it is attached ground, if it is three handbreadths high it is forbidden to stand on it to pray. But less than this, its height is not recognizable.
Surrounded by partitions. A new bimah in the synagogue that has not yet been surrounded by partitions is obviously forbidden to pray upon. However, regarding the attendant whose fixed place is to stand there for the needs of the synagogue, it appears permitted, as regarding craftsmen in seif 3. Peri Megadim.
