(Magen Avraham paragraph 36) And this is proven from Tosafos, and his words are not compelled, etc. Many understood that the Bach challenged like the Taz's question. And one is forced to explain Magen Avraham's words — but in truth this is not needed at all, as is clear to one who examines the Bach: the Bach challenged that Tosafos came to write a ruling, and what difference does it make that it was so in the Talmud's law; and further he challenged — why did Tosafos write a bed seems it is not fixed, implying they innovated this, yet it is explicit in the Talmud; therefore the Bach ruled that regarding our bed as well the law is so. On this Magen Avraham wrote there is no proof from Tosafos — what they wrote a bed seems not fixed means the questioner thought it was not fixed and therefore challenged; but in any event Tosafos deals with their bed and did not write so as a ruling, only as explanation of the questioner — the questioner thought so and held that an ordinary bed of theirs was also designated for sitting, as Beit Yosef wrote.
