Magen Avraham seder katan 1—a high place. For it appears that a high place that nullifies intent—they did not permit it initially for a sick person and elder only where there is no high place, and the reason is not only because of height. This was permitted for a sick person and elder, similar to seif 3 in practice: since he climbed a tree to do his work, it is evident it is not for height; likewise for a sick person and elder it is evident they need to sleep on a bed and the like.
