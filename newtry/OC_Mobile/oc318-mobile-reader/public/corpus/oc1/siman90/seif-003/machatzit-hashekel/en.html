(s.k. 3) To make heard, etc. — Avodah Berurah siman 251, who explained in Or Zaru'a, and these are his words: one descends before the ark — he descends from his place to go before the ark, etc.; and per the words of the Rav, etc. — because he ascended there (meaning onto the bimah) to read in the Torah; and afterward he descends to pray Musaf on account of "from the depths I called You," etc. — until here. And per this one must say that on account of Musaf prayer they called in all prayers "descends before the ark."
