Seif katan 3, to teach, etc. See Beit Yosef, siman 150, who explains otherwise: 'He descends before the ark' means that he descends from his place to go before the ark. According to the Rav, since he ascended there, meaning onto the bimah, to read the Torah, afterward he descends to pray Mussaf because of 'Out of the depths I called You.' According to this one must say that because of Mussaf prayer, all prayers are called 'descending before the ark.'
