(c) Twelve windows, etc. It appears one need not that all of them face toward Jerusalem; also per the reason explained that on all sides they should be open — Lechem Chamudos end of chapter Ein Omadin. Piskei Tosafos Minhagim wrote that one should be careful lekhatchilah to pray in a house that has windows toward the east, so as to lift his eyes whenever he mentions the Name opposite the windows; and if it is impossible to be careful thus, he should pray in any case in a house that has windows on the north or south so that he can turn to lift his eyes upward — up to here. And see later siman 98.
