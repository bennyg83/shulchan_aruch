In a field. See Ba'er Heitev. Batei Kehunah, volume 1, siman 9, writes that a place enclosed by walls is suitable even if it is not roofed. Birkei Yosef writes that this is the widespread custom within Jerusalem, the holy city: The congregation prays in a synagogue in a place that is not roofed, and the rabbis of the yeshivah pray Minchah and Maariv in the courtyards of the study halls. Mizbeach Adamah likewise agrees.
