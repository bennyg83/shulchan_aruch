16) [Se'if 3] The reason the olive tree was treated differently, etc. The same applies to a fig tree, as explained in Beis Yosef; the ruling merely chose one of them as its example. Levushei Serad writes likewise.
17) There: Therefore, they said that the workers should pray there. It appears that nowadays people are not particular about this, as stated below in siman 110 and siman 191:2. See our comments above, siman 63:31.
18) There: A householder, however, must descend even from the top of an olive or fig tree to pray, etc. For Krias Shema, which requires concentration only during the first verse, even a householder need not descend, as stated above in siman 63:7. Eliyah Rabbah 2 writes likewise. See our comments there, paragraph 30.
