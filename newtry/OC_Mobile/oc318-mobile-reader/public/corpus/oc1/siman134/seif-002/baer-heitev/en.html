Sefer Torah. When the Sefer Torah is taken from the heichal to be brought to the bimah, and the heichal is in the east while the bimah is in the west, then when one turns toward the bimah to carry it there, he should take it by way of the north, which is to his right as he holds it. When he takes it from the bimah to return it to the heichal, his right side faces south, and he should carry it by that route. When the Sefer Torah is shown to the people while the person stands on the east side of the bimah, he should turn from east toward south, as written in siman 128 regarding the kohanim; see there. Before taking out the Sefer Torah, Kaddish is recited through “Le’eila.” On a day that has Mussaf, however, Tiskabel is recited before taking out the Sefer Torah.
To see the script. The Arizal would look carefully at the letters until he could recognize and read them. He said that a great light is drawn upon a person by looking closely at the Sefer Torah until he can read the letters clearly. See Nagid U’Mitzvah, page 32, part 2, and Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 255. The Sefer Torah should be rolled open to three columns, then lifted and its writing shown to the people. Maseches Sofrim, chapter 14.
“And this is the Torah.” One should say “And this is the Torah” only while facing the writing of the Sefer Torah. Hagahos Maharil, Maharshal, and Knesses HaGedolah.
“Magnify.” The Sefer Torah should be raised when saying Shema, when saying Echad, and when saying Gadlu, three times in all. Maseches Sofrim, chapter 14.
