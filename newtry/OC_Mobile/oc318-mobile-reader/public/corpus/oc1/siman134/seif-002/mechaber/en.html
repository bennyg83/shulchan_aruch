One shows the writing of the Torah scroll to the people standing to one's right and to one's left, and then turns it to those in front of one and those behind one, for it is a mitzvah for all the men and women to see the writing and to bow and to say "V'zot Hatorah... Torat Hashem Temima etc." ("And this is the Torah... Hashem's Torah is Perfect etc."). {Rama: Gloss: And [Ashkenazim] practiced to do this after they read from the Torah, but when they remove it [from the Ark] they prayer leader says "Gad'lu" and the congregation answers "Romemu... Av Harachamim Hu Yeracheim Am Amusim etc." ("Exalt... May the Father of mercy have compassion on the people borne by Him etc."). And some say to say "Al Hakol Yit'gadal [Masechet Sofrim - chapter 14, and the Tur and Maharil] and this is what we practice on Yom Tov and Shabbat.
