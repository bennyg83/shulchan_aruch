1) [Seif 1] And they say 'V'Hu Rachum,' etc. The reason for this institution of 'V'Hu Rachum' is explained in the Kol Bo, that it was instituted on account of a great miraculous event; and now the custom is to say the supplications, [saying] 'Vaya'avor' three times with one viduy among them, and afterward they say 'V'Hu Rachum,' as written in the siddurim. Beis Yosef. And according to the words of the Arizal, of blessed memory, one must say the viduy before everything, immediately after the repetition of the tefillah, and afterward 'Vaya'avor,' and afterward nefilas apayim and 'Kel Melech yoshev,' etc., 'Anshei emunah,' etc., and afterward 'V'Hu Rachum,' as stated above in siman 131, ot 5, and in siman 48 in the order of the tefillah that we copied from Shaar HaKavanos; see there.
2) There: And they say it aloud. This is not indispensable, and one who says it silently has upon what to rely. Mahar Shmuel ben Ezra in a responsum in the responsa Knesses HaGedolah, siman 4, and the Rav concurred with him, siman 5; Birkei Yosef, ot 1, and in his sefer Kesher Gudal, siman 19, ot 26, and wrote there, 'each river follows its own course'; and so wrote the Beis Oved; Kaf HaChaim, siman 16, ot 23; Mishnah Berurah, ot 5. And see below, ot 5.
