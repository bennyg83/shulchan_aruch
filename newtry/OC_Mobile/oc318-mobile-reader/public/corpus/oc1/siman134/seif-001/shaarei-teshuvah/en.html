Standing. Ba'er Heitev. He wrote in the name of Keneset HaGedolah — and nowadays most of the congregation do not wait for the shatz; therefore it falls when he finishes — an individual need not wait for the shatz, for it is better he not pause so much before Nefilat Apayim; and even in regions that say afterward VeHu Rachum — nevertheless it is fitting to adjoin at least to VeHu Rachum. And see above siman 131.
