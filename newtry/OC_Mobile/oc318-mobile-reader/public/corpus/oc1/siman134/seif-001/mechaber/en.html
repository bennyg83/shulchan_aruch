The Order of "V'hu Rachum" and Lifting The Torah. Containing 2 S'ifim: "V'hu Rachum"; and it is said aloud. And if one did not say it while standing, he transgresses a [Rabbinic] enactment and it is called "breaching a fence". {Rama: Gloss: We practice to increase supplications on Monday and Thursday (Tur) and say Gloss: Indeed, we practice to say it while standing, but we say it quietly. And regarding what we practice - to increase supplications on Monday and Thursday, it is because they are days of [Divine] favor, and therefore we practice also to fast on them [Tur].}
