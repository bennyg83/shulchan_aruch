While it is still. That is Minchah Ketanah (Magen Avraham); see there. And Maginei Eretz wrote, and the same applies to a pidyon haben according to the Maharshal, that it is a seudas mitzvah, so that the father and the kohen need not complete [the fast]; and the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 568, s.k. 10, that it is obvious that where one is permitted to eat for the meal it is not a fast at all, and afterward one may even eat in his house; however before the meal it appears to me that it is forbidden to eat in his house; however the baalei bris are permitted to eat immediately, for it is their festival day; see there. And nowadays the custom has spread to complete even one of the four fasts that was postponed; and so wrote the Radbaz and Magen Avraham that nowadays the custom has spread that most of the meals are made at night; if so, on the day of the fast as well one should not be lenient; see there. (But in the Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he rejected that entirely and concluded with a full permission, only that they should not make a festive meal; see there.)
