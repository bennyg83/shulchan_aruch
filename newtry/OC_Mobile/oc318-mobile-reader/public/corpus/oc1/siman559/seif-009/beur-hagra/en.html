Se'if 9: Tisha B'Av, etc. Hagahos Ashri, chapter 3 of Moed Katan, end of [siman] 84, s.v. And he answered, etc.
They customarily, etc. In the Tosefta at the end of Taanis: they prepare from Tisha B'Av for Motza'ei Tisha B'Av. And the Rosh and Mordechai wrote: and from the end of the day; and further, that melacha is forbidden, but from Minchah onward it is permitted, analogous to trimming vegetables on Yom Kippur, and they crack nuts and they arrange pomegranates. And see above, siman 554, se'if 22 in the gloss: and they did not customarily, etc.
And they go, etc. Beginning of chapter 2 of Taanis.
And if, etc. As is written above in se'if 1 and se'if 4.
