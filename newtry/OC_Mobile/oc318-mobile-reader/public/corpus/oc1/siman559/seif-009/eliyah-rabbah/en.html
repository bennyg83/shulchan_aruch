[9] [Levush] And not "VeAni," etc. And the reason they do not begin every Motza'ei Shabbos with "VeAni," etc., is so that there not be a distinction from the night of Tisha B'Av, lest if they begin on Motza'ei Shabbos an individual err in his house and say it also on Tisha B'Av. Abudraham wrote that they do not say in a mourner's house "VeAni," etc., for a mourner is forbidden in words of Torah; end quote. And it appears that they do not practice thus.
