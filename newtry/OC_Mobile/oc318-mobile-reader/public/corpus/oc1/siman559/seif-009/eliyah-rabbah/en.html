[9] [Levush] and not "ve'ani," etc. The reason they do not begin every motza'ei Shabbat with "ve'ani," etc., is so there should be no difference from the night of Tisha B'Av — lest if they begin on motza'ei Shabbat, an individual err at home and say it also on Tisha B'Av. Abudraham wrote they do not say in a mourner's house "ve'ani," etc., for a mourner is forbidden in words of Torah — end quote; and it appears they do not practice thus.
