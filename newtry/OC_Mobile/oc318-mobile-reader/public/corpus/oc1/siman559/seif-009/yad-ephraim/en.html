There, per the view of the questioner that he does not act, etc. — meaning the Talmud there comes to prove that he does not act like Rabbenu Gershom; for if he acted like Rabbenu Gershom who holds on Tisha B'Av that falls on erev Shabbat they do not complete — if so on every Tisha B'Av they would not need to complete; and per this view the questioner's view is that granted if he does not act like Rabbenu Gershom it is not surprising that on every Tisha B'Av they completed, for they do not heed Yom Tov at all — even on Rosh Chodesh, etc. they complete; only because it was deferred they are lenient; not so if one says he acts like Rabbenu Gershom — if so even when not deferred they do not complete on erev Shabbat; and for us likewise if our Yom Tov is on Tisha B'Av they do not complete — if so on every Tisha B'Av they should not have completed, for their Yom Tov is on it; per the view of the questioner then if he acts like Rabbenu Gershom indeed if their Yom Tov fell on Tisha B'Av itself they also would not complete, per the reasoning of the questioner in Maharam; and if so the same on Yom Tov — he should not have completed like on Shabbat; therefore it answers that even Rabbenu Gershom agrees on Yom Tov that their words, etc. And per this for us who rule not like Rabbenu Gershom we state simply like the questioner's view that on our sixth day too one should be stringent, for it is not better than Rosh Chodesh — only when deferred there is to be lenient. And per the girsa before us in Magen Avraham that he challenged another question — it appears to me some words are missing in Magen Avraham that he challenged another question, and from where to distinguish between deferred or not? For it was necessary to say there that it was deferred because their Yom Tov was the tenth of Av; therefore it appears certainly one must distinguish in this. Per the view of the questioner, etc., as should be — and meaning the questioner there in responsum of Maharam siman 98 challenged there on what Beit Yosef wrote proof that deferred specifically, from that it says "and it was deferred" and does not say simply "and we fasted on Tisha B'Av and did not complete it," etc.; and on this it says it was necessary to say it was deferred since their Yom Tov was the tenth of Av; and for this Magen Avraham wrote the proof is thus: behold the questioner comes to prove he does not act like Rabbenu Gershom — it should have stated simply "and we fasted on Tisha B'Av and did not complete it," etc.; rather necessarily he does not act like Rabbenu Gershom; if so what that they did not complete it is because when deferred they are lenient. And per this for us who rule not like Rabbenu Gershom one is lenient only when deferred. And the view of the questioner that he should not have completed even without deferral — this is if he acts like Rabbenu Gershom; for then since it fell on their Yom Tov itself they complete; for us like if the ninth of Av fell on Rosh Chodesh on Chanukah and Purim — Rabbenu Gershom holds that even though they do not interrupt, nevertheless they do not complete; and so he holds on Tisha B'Av that falls on erev Shabbat; if so also on their Yom Tov they do not complete; and if so the same if it fell on Yom Tov like when it fell on erev Shabbat that they do not complete for Rabbenu Gershom; not so for us who do not rule like him — even on Yom Tov itself they do not defer unless it was deferred, per the questioner's view that he does not act like Rabbenu Gershom; and one need not answer Maharam that specifically for the reason they answered thus.
