(b) Because of the Ninth of Av. And if even the circumcision is not in its time and is deferred until after Shabbat (see siman 266) — if so, he also does not complete, or one could say specifically when they circumcise for eight [days] it is deferred on Tisha B'Av, unlike when it was deferred and its time was not then — behold the Tur wrote specifically when deferred on Tisha B'Av he would be lenient, and brought proof from the statement of Ra'avad that it is unspecified and we fasted on Tisha B'Av and did not complete. But the truth is their Ninth of Av was on the tenth of Av, and see Rashi Eruvin 41a. And see responsa of Maharash Halevi siman 2 and Maharam of Lublin siman 98; if so when it is not deferred they are certainly obligated to fast. See in responsa part 8 chapter 2 siman 8 that he brought for judgment that they are obligated to fast, and likewise R' Meir of Rothenburg siman 53 wrote explicitly that a woman who gave birth on the eighth day from birth is exempt from fasting on the fast of Gedaliah, unlike the father of the son and the sandak — all the more when the circumcision itself is deferred on the eve of Shabbat.
