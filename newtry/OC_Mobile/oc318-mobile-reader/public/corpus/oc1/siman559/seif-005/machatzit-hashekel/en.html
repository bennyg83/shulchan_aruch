(S.k. 5) One does not say, etc. They rise somewhat early; and in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote as follows: if there is concern that many will leave the synagogue before midday, it is good that they not rise so early; end quote.
Before he reads in the Torah; and in the Beis Yosef there are those who say before the reading of Eicha; and since they do not mention in it the Divine Name and kingship, as Magen Avraham wrote, therefore the Shelah wrote that one should say it before Eicha and also each of those called to the Torah.
