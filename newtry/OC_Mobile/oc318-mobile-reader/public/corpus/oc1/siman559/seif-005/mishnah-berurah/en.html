(22) It is forbidden to speak of a matter. And all the more so is the practice ugly whereby some behave lightly and throw things at one another in the synagogue. And it is a great sin, for even not on Tisha B'Av it is forbidden to show lightheadedness in the synagogue, and all the more so on Tisha B'Av and at the time of Kinot, when the Jewish people lament over the House of Hashem that was destroyed and over His people that were scattered throughout the world.
(23) And all the more so not to converse, etc. For in this one certainly diverts his mind from the mourning.
