Before beginning Eichah, one recites the blessing Al Mikra Megillah. Derashos Maharil there; Levush; Magen Avraham there; Ateres Zekeinim; Chayei Adam, klal 131, entry 23. Even when it is printed and written on paper, the blessing is recited. Levush; Machatzis HaShekel. Chayei Adam there wrote that the blessing is recited only when it is written on parchment and rolled in accordance with the law. Eshel Avraham at the beginning of the siman wrote that even if it is written on parchment, one should not recite the blessing, see there. Nevertheless, see above siman 490, entry 81, where we cited the dispute concerning this. Although we rule that where there is a dispute regarding blessings one should not recite a blessing because of the principle of uncertainty regarding blessings, as stated above in siman 18, entry 7, nevertheless, where the custom to recite the blessing has already been established, we do not take this principle into account, as stated above in siman 17, entry 2, see there. Therefore, each place should follow its custom. Where there is no established custom, one should not recite the blessing because of the principle of uncertainty regarding blessings.
