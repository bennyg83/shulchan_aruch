(24) If there is a mourner in the city, etc. That is, even within three days; and even though another mourner is forbidden to go out within three days even to another mourner's house, here since all are mourners it is more lenient. And some are stringent with a mourner within three days; and the Gaon R' Shlomo Kluger ruled that at night, when there are few Kinot, he should not go, but by day, when there are many Kinot and it causes him anguish if he does not go, it is permitted; and after three days he should go to the synagogue even at night. An onen sits in his house at night, and on the morrow after the burial, according to those who are lenient above regarding a mourner within three days, he too is permitted to enter the synagogue until they finish the Kinot, and afterward he goes to his house.
