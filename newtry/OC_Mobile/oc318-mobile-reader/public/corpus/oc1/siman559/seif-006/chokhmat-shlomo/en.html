Seif 6. If there is a mourner in the city who goes at night to the synagogue, etc. N.b.: it appears to me to decide that within the three weeks at night, since they do not increase kinot, he should not go to the synagogue; but by day, since they increase kinot, one may combine this with the rule that also on erev Rosh Hashanah a mourner may go out since they increase selichot and it is distressing for him — and thus the same applies here. But after the three weeks, also at night he goes to the synagogue. So it appears to me to decide as a compromise — and examine carefully.
