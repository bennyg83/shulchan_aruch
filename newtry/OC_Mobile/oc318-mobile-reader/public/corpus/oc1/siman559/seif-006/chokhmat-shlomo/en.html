Se'if 6 If there is a mourner in the city, he goes at night to the synagogue, etc. A side note: it appears to my humble opinion to decide that within three days, at night, since they do not multiply Kinot, he should not go to the synagogue; but by day, since they multiply Kinot, one should join to this that also on erev Rosh Hashanah a mourner is permitted to go out since they multiply Selichos and it causes him anguish; if so, the same applies here. But after three days he should go to the synagogue even at night. So it appears to my humble opinion to decide by way of a decision; and examine carefully.
