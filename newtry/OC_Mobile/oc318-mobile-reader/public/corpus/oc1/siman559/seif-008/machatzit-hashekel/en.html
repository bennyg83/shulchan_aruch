(S.k. 8) But, etc. Forbidden; and if he is after three days, this is the correct reading; and so it is in some printings. Mahara'i asked: for at night, etc.; if so, at night the Rosh would not have needed to give the reason that he is like a mourner after three days; and in the Rosh it implies that even at night this reason is needed. If he goes alone, or one or two with him; so it is there.
