The ba'al habrit dresses in other clothes, but not white ones. {Rama: Rema: and the father of the child and the circumciser and the Sandek are all called ba'alei habrit (Beit Yosef) and it is permitted for them to dress in Shabbat clothes after they have completed the kinnot. And they come to circumcise the child, but they should not dress in white. (Maharil)}
