(31) Other garments. And they are permitted to change the shirt, as on Shabbos Chazon.
(32) And the father of the son. And the same applies to his mother.
(33) Shabbos garments. And if they are new such that the pressing is noticeable in them, it is forbidden to wear them (Magen Avraham and Derech HaChaim); and in Sefer Bigdei Yesha he leans to hold that this is specifically if they are truly new, but otherwise, even if their pressing is noticeable, it is permitted.
(34) And they come to circumcise the infant. And after the milah they remove their garments.
