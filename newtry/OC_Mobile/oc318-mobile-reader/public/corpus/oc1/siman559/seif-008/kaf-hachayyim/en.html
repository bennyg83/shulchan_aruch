Look and see our disgrace, woe, what has happened to us!” He similarly divides all the verses, inserting “woe” in the middle and “woe, what has happened to us” at their ends. At the conclusion of the kinnot, he recites several verses of consolation. Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 5 of the Laws of Fasts; Darkei Moshe, entry 2; Ateres Zekeinim; Mishna Berurah, entry 3. This custom is also mentioned in Derashos Maharil, Laws of Tisha B’Av, Order of Prayer. Knesset HaGedolah cited his words in Hagahos HaTur, entry 2.
