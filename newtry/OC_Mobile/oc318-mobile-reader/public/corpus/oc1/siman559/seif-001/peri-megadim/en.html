One does not say Tzidkasecha; see the Taz. And when Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos they say Av HaRachamim; see Eliyah Rabbah, siman 554, letter 18, and there is a proof from here; see there. And it appears to me that those who customarily mention souls every Shabbos, the same applies to this Shabbos, like Tziduk HaDin on a weekday. In the Levush: before he removes the shoes on Tisha B'Av when it falls on Motza'ei Shabbos, he says HaMavdil; see there; and at present I did not find it explained from this; and see Magen Avraham, and with God's help it will be clarified there. And only the chazzan says HaMavdil, it appears to me.
