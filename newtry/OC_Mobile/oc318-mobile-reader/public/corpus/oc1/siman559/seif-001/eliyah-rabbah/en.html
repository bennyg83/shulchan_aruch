[A] [Levush] and they sit on the ground, etc. It appears this means: when the congregation answered "Baruch haMevorach le'olam va'ed," whether on a weekday or Shabbat. The Maharil wrote explicitly that he sat on the floor and not on the step before the ark, and wore a worn shabby cloak so as not to ruin his garments by sitting on the ground; at minchah he changed from the worse to the better — end quote. It implies somewhat that on Tisha B'Av one should not place a cushion or pillow under oneself; but Magen Avraham wrote it appears permitted to sit on sackcloth, for that is only a custom to sit on the ground — end quote; and he did not note the Maharil mentioned above.
