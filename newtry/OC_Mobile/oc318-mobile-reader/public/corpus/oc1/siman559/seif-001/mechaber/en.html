Customs of Tisha b'Av and rulings regarding circumcision on Tisha b'Av, containing
10 paragraphs: When Tisha b'Av begins on Saturday night, we don't say "Tzidkatecha" during mincha of Shabbat.Rema: and we make Havdalah at night during prayer, like all other Motza'ei Shabbat. (Notes of Mordechai, Halachot Tisha b'Av) And if one forgot to make havdalah, see Siman 294 subsection 3. And one should pray in tranquility, as mourners cry. And so we do in the reading of Eicha (Hagahot Maimoni) and) with all "Eicha"s one should lift up one's voice higher (Maharil). And when the chazan reaches the verse "Hashiveinu", the congregation says it in a loud voice. And after that, the chazan concludes and returns and the congregation and chazan both say "Hashiveinu" in a loud voice. (Hagahot Maimoni)):
