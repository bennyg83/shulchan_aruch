However, from the language of Taz below siman 568 s.k. 5 it does not appear so entirely; nevertheless they practiced leniency. I again found he ruled thus explicitly in Be'er Yaakov siman 568; it appears initially they did not accept the custom upon themselves where the mitzvah of sandak and mohel applies, for there is repair for the dead in that his son engages in milah more than other mitzvos, as Taz wrote Yoreh De'ah siman 341, explained more in responsum of the Maharshal siman 70 — see there.
