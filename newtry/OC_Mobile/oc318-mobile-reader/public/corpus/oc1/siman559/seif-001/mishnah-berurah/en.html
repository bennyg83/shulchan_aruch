(1) (a) One does not say, etc. - from the hand of Dahoi Arah Shachal to be one on Shabbat Sha'a Tzot on Shabbat DTB Nemi Ikri Moed:

(2) And so they do, etc. — and he pauses a little between each verse and a little more between each lament, and the last verse of each lament is said in a loud voice. Regarding the blessing before it, see above siman 554 end of seif 8 in the gloss and Mishna Berurah there. It is good for an individual to read Eichah also by day.
