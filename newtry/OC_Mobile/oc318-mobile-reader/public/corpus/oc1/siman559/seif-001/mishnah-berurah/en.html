(1) They do not say, etc. Similar to a Rosh Chodesh that falls on Sunday, when one does not say Tzidkasecha on Shabbos; for Tisha B'Av is also called a festival.
(2) And so they do, etc. And he should pause a little between each verse, and a little more between each chapter of Eicha; and the last verse of each chapter of Eicha he says in a loud voice. And regarding a blessing beforehand, see above in siman 490 at the end of se'if 8 in the gloss, and in the Mishnah Berurah there. And it is good that an individual also read Eicha by day.
(3) And likewise the chazzan. And afterward the chazzan returns and begins "Zechor Hashem meh hayah lanu," "Oy, habeit u're'eh et cherpasenu," "Oy meh hayah lanu"; and likewise he divides all the verses, their middles with "Oy" and their ends with "Oy meh hayah lanu" (Darkei Moshe).
