One does not say Tzidkasecha, and all the more so [one does not say] Tziduk HaDin if a death occurred on Tisha B'Av; and so wrote the Rama at the end of this siman. And the Maharshal in a responsum, siman 32, wrote that one should say Tziduk HaDin, and so I found in old Minhagim; even though the custom is thus in every place that where Tachanun is not said, Tziduk HaDin is not said, nevertheless it appears that Tisha B'Av is different, for it is no less than other Kinot and calamities; and so is implied in the name of Rashi, who permitted saying Tziduk HaDin before midday like Kinot. And he further wrote in Sefer Agudah in the name of the Rokeach that one should even say Tachanun on Tisha B'Av, because Megillas Taanis was nullified; if so, one should be lenient and not be stringent as on other festive occasions; end quote of the Maharshal.
