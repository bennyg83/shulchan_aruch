And it is written in the glosses of Beit Yosef that the shaliach tzibur does not say "also," and it is written two reasons: the first — since the kohen is a mourner he does not lift his hands, and on Tisha B'Av the kohanim mourn the destruction of the Temple; and the second reason — as it is written "and in spreading your palms" etc. Behold, regarding the first reason one can answer as I wrote above in siman 127; and the second reason is homiletical. However in my humble opinion the reason that since on the Ninth of Av it is forbidden to inquire after peace, how can he say "and place peace for you"?
