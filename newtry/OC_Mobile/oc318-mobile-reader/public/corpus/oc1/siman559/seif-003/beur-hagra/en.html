Seif 3 night, etc. Mishnah Sofrim there — but by law not required as R' Yehuda explained turning the bed; they did not agree; Rava ruled halacha like our tanna therefore we are lenient at minchah as stated. Yerushalmi there 40: by law one must practice thus — we learn there: proper is R' Yehuda's reason that the rabbis made as one whose dead lies before him; likewise Rif: he does not force his bed nor sleep on overturned bed — as stated, when dead lies before him he does not sleep on upright or overturned bed; Tosafot HaRosh explains only sleeps on ground as we say he does not recline — see Yoreh De'ah siman 341 seif 5. But in our Gemara it does not imply so — we say per your view pregnant and nursing women, etc.; if so, for them too it is difficult; moreover Yerushalmi implies only for seudah hamafseket "they made it as one whose dead lies before him" — specifically seudah hamafseket as R' Yehuda bar Elai's case; as stated siman 552 seif 7. But on Tisha B'Av cannot say so — else why practice all mourning customs, and onen need not? Tosafot Ketubot 4a s.v. boel avel — for our Gemara 40 regarding pregnant and nursing. However per Rif who practices onen like all mourning laws except shoe removal because he must attend the dead — fits well and refers to Tisha B'Av; therefore also not obligated in ma'amar avel is not obligated — if so forbidden by law; likewise Mishnah Sofrim there: they eulogize as one whose dead lies before him — evidently considered like onen all day.
