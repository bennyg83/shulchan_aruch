(3) There. They do not say "Tzidkatcha," etc. — and all the more tziduk hadin if a death occurred on Tisha B'Av; Rama likewise in this siman in the gloss. Taz s.k. 1. Rashal in responsa siman 32 wrote they say tziduk hadin on Tisha B'Av even though one does not say tachanun — see there. Bach on Taz there. Likewise Rokeach siman 317 that some places say tziduk hadin on Tisha B'Av — see there. However in Mateh Moshe siman 534 he wrote on Rashal's words above that the custom already spread not to say it — see there. Bach, Eliyah Rabbah seif 14. Likewise Eliyah Rabbah siman 554 seif 18 that they do not practice saying it. Likewise Chayei Adam there seif 9 — see below seif 82.
