(10) They sit, etc. Analogous to a mourner who sits on the ground all seven days; nevertheless it is not as severe as the five afflictions, and therefore it is not required for the whole day.
(11) On the ground. That is, after they answered "Baruch Hashem ha-mevorach," etc., for that must be while standing; and see the later authorities that it is permitted to place under oneself a sack or a small cushion. And one may also sit on a low bench if it is hard for him to sit on the ground.
(12) Immediately after they leave the synagogue, etc. That is to say: afterward they go to the cemetery, as they wrote at the end of the siman; and with this measure it continues until midday, for then one is permitted to sit on the bench.
(13) And they prolong with the Kinot, etc. So that they not come to do melacha at that time.
(14) They do not light, etc. Because it is written "He has set me in dark places," etc.
(15) Each one, one candle. And according to our custom that the entire congregation says Eicha quietly with the chazzan, and also Kinot, one should light one candle in each neighborhood, so that under cramped conditions they can say Kinot by its light. And they do not light a prayer-candle in the synagogue on Tisha B'Av at Shacharis. And for Minchah they light. And for a bris milah they light candles at the hour when they bring the infant to circumcise.
