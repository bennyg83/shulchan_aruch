On the ground. It appears to me that it is permitted to sit upon a sack, because sitting directly on the ground is only a custom. See Yoreh Deah, at the end of siman 387.
After they leave the synagogue. For they go to the cemetery, and this extends until midday, at which point one may sit upon a bench. The same is stated in Minhagim.
One candle. Maharil writes that one should light one additional candle, so that if the chazzan's candle goes out, he can relight it from that candle. This implies that the congregation does not recite Eichah and the kinnot themselves, but listens to the shaliach tzibbur.
