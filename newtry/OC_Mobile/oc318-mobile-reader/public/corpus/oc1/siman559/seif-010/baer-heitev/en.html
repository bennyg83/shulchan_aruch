Midday. And for the needs of a seudas mitzvah it is permitted to slaughter before midday; see siman 551, se'if 9, in the gloss.
The graves. And if there are no Jewish graves, they go to the graves of non-Jews; and one should not go to the graves of non-Jews if there is an idolater there. And he wrote in the writings of the Arizal, of blessed memory, that one should not go to the graves except for the need of accompanying the dead, and especially if one has not repaired the sin of keri, for the external forces cling to him.
