They customarily do not slaughter, etc. It appears that he stated this with distinctions: that is, for those who do not regard the "proper custom" of siman 558 and eat meat at night, they should not slaughter until after midday; and those who follow the "proper custom" certainly should not slaughter, for Tisha B'Av itself is no better than after Rosh Chodesh Av, when they put away the slaughtering knife; however, nevertheless they should not prepare the other needs of their meal until midday. My teacher and father-in-law, of blessed memory, also wrote that according to the "proper custom" one should not slaughter until after the fast. And so it is in Hagahos Maimoniyos until after the fast; but I have seen in all places that they slaughter for the need of eating after Tisha B'Av, and people do not protest against them.
