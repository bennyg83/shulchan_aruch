(S.k. 10) Not white garments, etc. For baalei bris are also permitted, for he compared and equated them with Shabbos Chazon.
And as is written in siman 551, s.k. 4, regarding Shabbos Chazon.
And Shabbos garments that are still new, etc. That is to say: that which the Rama permitted wearing Shabbos garments is specifically when the pressing is not noticeable in them; but when the pressing is noticeable they are forbidden, as on Shabbos Chazon in siman 551, s.k. 20.
