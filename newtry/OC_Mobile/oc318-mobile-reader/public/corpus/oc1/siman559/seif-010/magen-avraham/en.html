Not to slaughter. This applies to those who will eat meat that night. For the needs of a seudas mitzvah, however, one may slaughter before midday. See siman 551:9 in the Rama's gloss concerning slaughtering.
To the cemetery. For the deceased also experience distress, and therefore it does not suffice to visit non-Jewish graves. [Shelah] Nevertheless, if no Jewish graves are available, one goes to non-Jewish graves, as stated at the end of siman 579. The writings of the Arizal state that one should not visit a cemetery except when accompanying a deceased person for burial. In particular, if one has not rectified the sin of a seminal emission, the forces of impurity attach themselves to him.
