The end of chapter 3 of Eruvin, folio 41: Rabban Gamliel holds there that a communal fast that they decreed for twenty or, according to another opinion, thirty days, and Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, and Purim arrived in the middle: they fast and do not complete; and likewise Tisha B'Av that falls on erev Shabbos, they do not complete. And the Gemara there seeks to prove that even in the generation of Rabban Gamliel they did not act according to him, and it proves this from that of R' Elazar son of R' Tzadok, who was in the days of Rabban Gamliel and who would complete every Tisha B'Av even though it was the eve of their festival day; if so, on erev Shabbos they complete; see there.
