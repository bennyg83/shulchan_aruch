They sit; see the Taz. For a mourner it is forbidden midina to sit on a chair and a bench; see Yoreh De'ah 387. And here it is from custom, and they are lenient after midday; but in the Taz it implies that midina all mitzvos practiced for a mourner [apply], and in the latter clause it concludes washing, etc., and not sitting on a bench; this teaches that after midday sitting is permitted, yet other matters are forbidden the whole day. And it implies even during bein hashemashos until nightfall when the stars appear, like eating and drinking; and Tisha B'Av during its bein hashemashos is forbidden. All the more so Motza'ei Tisha B'Av; see Magen Avraham, siman 562.
