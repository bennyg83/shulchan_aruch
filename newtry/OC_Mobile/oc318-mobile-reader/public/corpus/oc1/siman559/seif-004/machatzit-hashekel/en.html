(S.k. 4) One candle, etc. This implies that the congregation, etc.; but in the Taz he wrote: of what use is the chazzan's saying alone? And certainly the entire congregation must say.
And that which was written "one candle" deals with a small synagogue, where in any case under cramped conditions the entire congregation can say [Kinot] by that candle; but in a large synagogue, where one candle does not suffice, they should light one candle in each neighborhood. And the reason is: "He has set me in dark places" (Eicha 3:6).
