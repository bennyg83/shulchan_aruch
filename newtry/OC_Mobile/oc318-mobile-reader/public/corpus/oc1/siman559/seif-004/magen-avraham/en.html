Tachanun is not recited, etc. In the morning they rise somewhat early to go to the synagogue. [Mateh Moshe] They are not summoned to the synagogue, but come on their own. [Maharil] In our lands, however, the custom is to summon them to the synagogue. The custom is to recite "Blessed is the true Judge" before the Torah reading. [Beit Yosef, Levush, Shelah] Darchei Moshe writes that this is not the custom. It is proper for the reader to recite it without Hashem's Name and kingship.
