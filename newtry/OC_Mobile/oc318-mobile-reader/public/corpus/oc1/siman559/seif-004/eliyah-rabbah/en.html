[4] [Levush] He reads Eicha, etc. And a God-fearing person should also read by day; and one who fasts also on the tenth should read on the night of the tenth and by day (Shelah). And if they are ten, they should not read publicly (Magen Avraham). The Maharil would leave a pause between each verse greater than what is done at Torah reading, and between each chapter of Eicha and the next he would pause a little more; and every last verse he says in a loud voice to announce its conclusion; and likewise all verses of merciful appeal he says in a loud voice.
