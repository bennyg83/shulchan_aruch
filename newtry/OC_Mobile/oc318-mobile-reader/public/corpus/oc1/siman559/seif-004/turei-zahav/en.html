(b) Because it is called a festival. It appears to me it is permitted to eulogize a sage who died and to sit on the ground on Tisha B'Av, for otherwise all the righteous are eulogized on that day and even after chatzot, for that entire day is fixed for weeping and eulogy; and for this purpose we pay no attention to its being called a festival, and so from that they did not mention the prohibition of eulogy on Tisha B'Av as they mentioned other matters — as I wrote, so it appears to me.
