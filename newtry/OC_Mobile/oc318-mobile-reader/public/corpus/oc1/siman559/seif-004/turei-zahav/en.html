Because it is called a festival. It appears to me that it is permitted to deliver a eulogy for a sage who died and to sit on the ground on Tisha B'Av, for in any case they eulogize all the holy ones on that very day, and even after midday, for that entire day is fixed for weeping and eulogy; and regarding this we pay no heed to its being called a festival. And so is implied from the fact that they did not mention a prohibition of eulogy on Tisha B'Av as they wrote regarding other matters, as it appears to me.
