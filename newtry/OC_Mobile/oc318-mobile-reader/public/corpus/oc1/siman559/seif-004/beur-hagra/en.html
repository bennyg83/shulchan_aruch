Se'if 4: And they read, etc. Gemara at the end of Megillah.
And all the Kaddishes, etc. Because it is stated "satham tefillati" ("He has shut out my prayer").
And not Pitum. See Magen Avraham.
And one need not, etc. In Masseches Sofrim there: and some change their place, and some go down from their benches below. And he wrote in Toras HaAdam: a place where they change their place, they treated it like the second Shabbos, as is written in chapter 3 of Moed Katan; and a place where they go down from their benches, they treated it like the first Shabbos; and so is the practice now; if so, one need not.
