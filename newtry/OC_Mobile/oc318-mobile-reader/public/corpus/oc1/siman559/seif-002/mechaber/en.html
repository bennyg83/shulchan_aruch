On the night of Tishah B'Av {we pray Ma'ariv and say Eichah and Kinos (Hagahos Maimoni; Maharil)}, and one says after the Kinos and Eichah the Order of Kedushah, beginning from "Ve'atah Kadosh." And if it falls on Motza'ei Shabbos, we do not say "Vihi No'am." {Rama: Nor "Lamnatze'ach Binginos," nor "Veyiten Lecha" (Kol Bo); and we remove the paroches from before the Aron, on account of [the verse] "He has done violence to His word" (Minhagim).}
