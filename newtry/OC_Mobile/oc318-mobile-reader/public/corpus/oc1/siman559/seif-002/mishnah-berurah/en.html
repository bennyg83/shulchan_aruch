(4) They pray Maariv. At the outset. And he says a full Kaddish, that is with Tiskabel, after the Maariv prayer. And if it falls on Motza'ei Shabbos, they do not bless the children [in a place where they practice this] (Maharil).
(5) And they say Eicha and Kinot. And even when he is alone (Chayei Adam).
(6) From VeAtah Kadosh. And he should not say "UVa leTzion go'el," for there is no redemption at night; nor "VeAni zot beriti," which appears as if establishing a covenant over the Kinot; and further, one is forbidden in words of Torah, and it is not fitting to say "and they shall not depart from your mouth" (Tur).
(7) They do not say VeYehi Noam. Because its essence was instituted regarding the erection of the Mishkan, and now it is destroyed.
(8) And not Lamnatzeach, etc. For it is not of the order of the day, and it is forbidden.
(9) And not VeYiten Lecha. For it is not its time on Tisha B'Av.
