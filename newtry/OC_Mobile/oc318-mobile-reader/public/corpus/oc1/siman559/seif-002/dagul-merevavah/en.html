His words require further study, for what connection does this have to a meal? Would it be that if they make no meal at all it is not a festival day for the baalei bris? And are the baalei bris not permitted to eat even in their own homes? See siman 568 in the Magen Avraham at the end of s.k. 10. And further: did the Mechaber permit making a meal? He permitted only for the baalei bris, and the Magen Avraham himself wrote that nevertheless one should not make a meal.
