[2] [Levush] They do not remove [shoes] until, etc. He should remove the shoes of one foot with the other foot immediately (Rokeach); that is to say, so that he not touch them with his hands, for then he would need netilas yadayim; and from this too there is proof that whenever one removes [shoes] and does not touch them, he needs no washing; and see siman 4, s.k. (15) [13].
