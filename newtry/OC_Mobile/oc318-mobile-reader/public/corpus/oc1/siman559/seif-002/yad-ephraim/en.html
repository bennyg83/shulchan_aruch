(ב) Taz note 1 — because Megillat Taanit of fasts was nullified etc.; it requires study what connection the Ninth of Av has to Megillat Taanit; and in Rokeach and Agudah it is explained that it refers to the Ninth of Av, and they brought it in Beit Yosef siman 131; and it is explained in Rokeach laws of Tisha B'Av that one does not say Tachanun on Tisha B'Av; and regarding Mincha it is found in Rokeach siman 316 that they say it.
