(25) After they finish the Kinot. For this mitzvah Jews perform with joy; and therefore they do not circumcise at the time of Kinot and eulogy.
(26) Until after midday. For beforehand mourning still applies and it is not fitting; and the first opinion holds that even so there is a mitzvah to advance it, for the diligent precede mitzvos.
(27) Without besamim. Even though ordinarily too they do not bring besamim for a milah, nevertheless he needs to teach us that today too they do not bring them, to exclude the view of the Geonim who hold that nowadays they do not bring a cup for the blessing even to give to the infant, but bring besamim in place of the cup for the blessing; this teaches us that it is not so, for on Tisha B'Av one must minimize pleasure in every way possible.
(28) And the custom is like the first opinion. This gloss needs to be before the word "And they recite the blessing."
(29) The birthing mother. See above in siman 554, se'if 6, and in the Mishnah Berurah there.
(30) To young children. And we are not concerned that perhaps he will become habituated and drink even in his adulthood, since it is not a fixed matter; even though regarding Havdalah when Tisha B'Av falls on Motza'ei Shabbos we are concerned and do not give to a young girl, as above in siman 556, that is because according to our calendar of years it will necessarily come sometimes once at the end of three or four years on Motza'ei Shabbos, and it is considered like a fixed matter; which is not so with milah, which has no fixed schedule at all, so we are not concerned about this.
