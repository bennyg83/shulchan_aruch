If there is an infant who must receive brit milah, the brit milah is performed after the kinot are completed. Some wait to perform it until after halachic noon. The blessing over the brit milah is recited without spices. The custom follows the first view. If the mother who gave birth is present at the place of the brit milah, the blessing is recited over a cup and she drinks from it, provided that she hears the blessing and does not interrupt by speaking between hearing the blessing and drinking from the cup. If she is not there, the blessing is recited over the cup and children are given a taste from it.
