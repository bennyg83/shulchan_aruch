"To children." We are not concerned that they may become accustomed to doing so, as stated in siman 556, because this does not occur every year. [Tur] Bach writes that the wine should be given to the infant receiving brit milah, as stated in siman 621, seif 3, regarding Yom Kippur. Rama, however, holds that since Tisha B'Av is rabbinic, we are not so stringent. It is preferable to give it to a somewhat older child who is obligated in chinuch for the blessing, as stated in siman 269.
