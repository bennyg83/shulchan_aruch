To children. We are not concerned that they may become accustomed to doing so, as in siman 556, because this does not occur every year. [Tur] Bach writes that it should be given to the circumcised child, as stated in siman 621:3 regarding Yom Kippur. The Rama's view, however, is that since Tishah B'Av is D'Rabbanan, we are not so stringent. It is preferable to give it to a somewhat older child who is obligated in chinuch for the blessing, as stated in siman 269.
