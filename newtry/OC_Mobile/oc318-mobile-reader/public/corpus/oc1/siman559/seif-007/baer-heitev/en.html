To young children. The Bach wrote that one should give [wine] to the circumcised infant, as is written in siman 621, se'if 3, regarding Yom Kippur; but the view of the Rama is that since Tisha B'Av is rabbinic, we are not so stringent, and it is better to give it to a child who is somewhat older, whom one is obligated to train in the blessing (Magen Avraham).
