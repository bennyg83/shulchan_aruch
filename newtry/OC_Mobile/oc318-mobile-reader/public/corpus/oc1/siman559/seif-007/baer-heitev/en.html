"To young children." Bach writes that the wine should be given to the infant receiving brit milah, as stated in siman 621, seif 3, regarding Yom Kippur. Rama, however, holds that since Tisha B'Av is rabbinic, we are not so stringent, and it is preferable to give it to a somewhat older child who is obligated in chinuch for the blessing. Magen Avraham.
