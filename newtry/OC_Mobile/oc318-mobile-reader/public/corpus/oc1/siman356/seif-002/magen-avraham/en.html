(א) permitted to draw from it. Even though it is ten deep and four wide, since it entered the courtyard and does not exit it is like a pit in the courtyard and the partitions of the courtyard permit it; unlike a water channel that enters and exits and divides a domain for itself and is karmelit (Raavad and Ritva; nevertheless Bach).

(ב) from it in the courtyard. For sometimes the water sinks and the partition is visible; but in the house the partition is not visible.

(ג) forbidden even to draw. Meaning it is forbidden to carry in the courtyard; and if the tongue is ten deep it is considered a partition and permitted to carry in the courtyard; but forbidden to draw from it since there is no partition on it between it and the water outside, for it is breached fully and the sea is karmelit (Raavad and Rosh); and one need not be concerned lest the sea rise as siman 363 s.k. 29 — it deals when the embankments are very high or far from the sea shore (Tosefta).
