A courtyard that was breached and a tongue of sea passes over the breach — if it is not full width and the breach is' not more than ten, it is permitted to draw from it and bring into the house; if the breach is full width or the breach' is more than ten — if there remained at the breached place embankments ten high and the water covers them, it is permitted to draw from it in the courtyard; but it is forbidden to bring them into the house unless they made a partition ten high on the water; if embankments did not remain, it is forbidden even to draw in the courtyard.
