If a yard is breached, the sea enters through the breach, it is only partially breached, and the hole is wider than ten [tefachim], one is permitted to use the water and bring it into his house. If it is fully breached or the hole is wider than ten, if parts of the wall remain where it fell, and those parts are taller than ten but covered by water, he can use the water in the yard but it is forbidden to bring it into his house unless he made a ten-tefach tall wall. If no parts of the wall remain, it is even forbidden to use the water.
