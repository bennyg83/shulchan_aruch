(א) permitted to draw from it — even though it is ten deep and four wide; and it is not comparable to the water channel explained in seif 1, for here since the tongue of sea entered the courtyard and does not exit to another side it is like a pit in the courtyard and the partitions of the courtyard permit it [since it is not breached more than ten it is included in the opening as if the partitions exist]; unlike a water channel that enters and exits under the courtyard walls from both sides and divides a domain for itself and is karmelit.

(ב) full width — meaning the entire courtyard wall on the side of the water.

(ג) embankments ten high — for by this it is considered as if the partitions still exist.

(ד) from it in the courtyard — for sometimes the water sinks and the partition is visible; therefore even when the water does not sink the Sages permitted; unlike in the house which is far from the partition — the partition is never visible even when the water sinks.

(ה) ten high — and the width of the partition need not be across the entire breach, only that it seal the area until no more than ten amos remain open, which is the measure of an opening.

(ו) embankments did not remain, etc. — meaning embankments ten high did not remain.

(ז) forbidden even to draw — and the same to carry in the courtyard, since it is breached more than ten to a forbidden place within four amos; when the tongue of sea is not ten deep; but if the tongue is ten deep — water that flooded the courtyard ground and made a pit ten tefachim deep — the sides of the pit itself are considered a partition between the water and the courtyard and permitted to carry in the courtyard; but forbidden to draw from it since there is no partition on it between it and the water outside.
