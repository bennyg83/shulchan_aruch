(א) water channel — and the same applies to a river [Magen Avraham].

(ב) passing through a courtyard — know that per the well-known among poskim it is not specifically that it passes forever; the same applies if a river flooded and water passes through the courtyard from both sides like a water channel — the law is the same; and nevertheless it is possible that if it is a sluice gate that the water will remain only a day or two they did not decree on this that it should be karmelit — requires further study.

(ג) one may not draw from it, etc. — but in the courtyard it is permitted to carry even if they made no repair, and we do not say it should be forbidden because it is breached fully to karmelit, because we say the sides of the water channel which are ten deep become a partition to separate it and the courtyard; and regarding carrying in the courtyard, even if the courtyard wall opposite the channel was breached more than ten on both sides, carrying in the courtyard is permitted, for the courtyard is enclosed from four sides [meaning the three walls of the courtyard and the fourth side is the water channel itself, as stated] — Raavad, and Magen Avraham in the name of Raavad; and it is surprising Chayei Adam siman 53 copied this law only when breached on one side of the courtyard walls — plainly according to Raavad's reason even when breached on both sides. Further Magen Avraham wrote that what we say the sides of the water channel are considered a partition to permit carrying in the courtyard applies even on a slope; however specifically if ten tefachim collect within four amos; but if very sloped that ten collect within five amos it is not half-and-half and therefore forbidden to carry in the courtyard.
