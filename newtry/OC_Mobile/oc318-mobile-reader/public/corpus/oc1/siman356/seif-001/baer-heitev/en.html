(א) ten deep, etc. For at this measure it becomes a domain for itself and is karmelit even in a courtyard; but if it is not ten deep or not four wide it is not karmelit and is like a domain standing there — if in reshus harabbim it is reshus harabbim, and if in reshus hayachid it is reshus hayachid.

(ב) partition. But the partitions of the courtyard that are suspended and pass over it do not help, since they were not made for the sake of water — Rashi; and see Magen Avraham.

(ג) sunk. To distinguish between water outside which is karmelit and water inside.

(ד) in the middle. Meaning at the side of entrance and side of exit he made a partition the width of an amah, except he did not make the partition on the entrance side all in one piece but in two pieces, for in the middle of the partition he left open space; and similarly on the exit side partition; and he did so so that at that open place the water flows; if that open place is not three by three it is lavud — see Taz.
