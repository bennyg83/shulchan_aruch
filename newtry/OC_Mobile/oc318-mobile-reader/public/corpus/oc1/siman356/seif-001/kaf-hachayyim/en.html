1) [Se'if 1] A watercourse that passes through a courtyard, etc. Even in a private domain, it is a karmelis. Rashi. Rabbeinu Chananel explains likewise. Tosafos Yom Tov writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yehonasan that the reason is that it comes from a karmelis, namely, the river from which it is drawn. This implies that if it does not come from a karmelis, but rather one has a pool within one's courtyard, then although it is ten handbreadths deep and four or more handbreadths wide, its status is that of the courtyard.
2) There. A watercourse that passes through a courtyard, etc. The same applies to a river. Chayei Adam, rule 53, subsection 16.
