(א) passing through a courtyard — and specifically when it passes, meaning it enters the courtyard from one side and exits from the other through holes under the walls; but if he has a pool of water in his courtyard and it is not drawn from outside, no repair is needed even if very wide and deep, for everything within the partitions is included in reshus hayachid.

(ב) ten deep and four wide — for at this measure the water becomes a domain for itself and karmelit is upon them like other seas and rivers; therefore when they pass through his courtyard it is forbidden to draw from them on Shabbos; but if they are not ten deep or not four wide it is permitted to draw from them without any repair, for it is nullified to reshus hayachid.

(ג) unless they made, etc. — for since they separate from the rest of the water outside the courtyard, the water within the courtyard is nullified to it; for generally we rule that everything within the partitions is reshus hayachid; only here when connected with water from outside we say they are not nullified and become a domain for themselves; unlike when separated, for then reshus hayachid is upon them; and the partition of the courtyard itself, even if sunk greatly in water, does not help here, for we require that it be visible that it was made for the sake of water.

(ד) at its entrance and exit — meaning at the place the water enters the courtyard and at the place the water exits the courtyard — all inside on the width of the water channel.

(ה) sunk in water — to distinguish between water outside which is karmelit and water inside.

(ו) above the water — so that the partition separating them be somewhat visible.

(ז) at the edge on every side, etc. — meaning at the side of entrance and side of exit he made a partition the width of an amah, except he did not make the partition on the entrance side all in one piece but in two pieces, for in the middle of the partition he left open; and similarly on the exit side partition.

(ח) for we say lavud — and for this reason even if he left many gaps between them, such as he made the partition from reeds and between each and each was less than three, it is permitted [Chayei Adam].

(ט) even if, etc. — for everything less than three we say lavud; and it appears that in this even if on the other side it is three wide, nevertheless no repair is needed since on one side it is completely sealed and like the language of a tongue of sea passing within the courtyard explained below.
