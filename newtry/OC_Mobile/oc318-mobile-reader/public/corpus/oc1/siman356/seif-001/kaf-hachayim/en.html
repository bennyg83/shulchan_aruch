(1) [seif 1] a water channel passing through a courtyard, etc. — even in reshus hayachid it is karmelit. Rashi; and so explained Rabeinu Chananel; and the reason Tosefta wrote in the name of R' Yonatan: because it comes from the force of karmelit — that is, the river from which it flows — end of his words; meaning that if it does not come from the force of karmelit but he has a pool in his courtyard, even though it is ten deep and four wide or more, behold it is like the courtyard.
