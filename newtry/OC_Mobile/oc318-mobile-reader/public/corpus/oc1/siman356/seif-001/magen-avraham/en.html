(א) ten deep. But if it is not ten deep or not four wide it is not karmelit and is like a domain standing there; and if it stands in reshus hayachid it is reshus hayachid (Rashi).

(ב) one may not draw. And nevertheless the partitions of the courtyard do not help to draw since they were not made for the sake of water, as siman 376 (Rashi); and if you ask — behold the channel forbids the courtyard which is breached fully to karmelit — one can say that it itself becomes a partition between it and the courtyard, for it is ten deep and the courtyard is not forbidden because of the channel (Raavad); and see siman 345 seif 8 that if it is not ten deep at its lip but on a slope — if ten collect within four amos it is a partition; but if very sloped that ten collect within five amos it is not a partition; and see siman 363 s.k. 29; and it appears from Raavad that when it is ten deep, even if the courtyard wall was breached more than ten, it is permitted to carry in the courtyard, for the courtyard is enclosed from four sides — see there.

(ג) sunk in water. To distinguish between water outside which is karmelit and water inside.
