דין אמת המים העוברת בחצר. ובו ב סעיפים:
The law of a water channel passing through a courtyard. And in it are 2 seifim: A water channel passing through a courtyard, ten deep and four wide — one may not draw from it on Shabbos unless they made for it a partition ten high at its entrance and exit, and one tefach of it shall be sunk in the water; and if the partition was entirely descending within the water, one tefach of it must protrude above the water; and if one began to make the partition at' the edge on every side and did not connect them in the middle so that the water enters and exits by way of there — if there are not three tefachim between them it is permitted, for we say lavud; if there are three tefachim between them it is forbidden.And if the holes through which the channel enters and exits are not three wide, he does not' need a partition even so (Beit Yosef in the name of Ritva).
