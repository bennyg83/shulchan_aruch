To the right side. Since the sefer Torah is on the bimah, and the entire congregation must turn their faces toward the sefer Torah on the tower before they begin to circle, then the north is their right side (Darchei Moshe, Terumas HaDeshen). It appeared to me that when the chazzan turns himself, he should turn by way of the right, as I wrote regarding kohanim [in siman 128]. But I have not seen people practice this way. Perhaps this is because of the honor of the Shechinah and the congregation; but regarding kohanim this is impossible (Maharil in responsum, siman 40).
