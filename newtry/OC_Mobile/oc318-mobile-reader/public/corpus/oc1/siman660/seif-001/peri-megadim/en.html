Remembrance of the 17th Temple. Sukkah Md Mishna and Maga A Arba We do not make a memorial to the Temple 21 Bhar Yom 7 alone, but the circumambulation we make from Mukhta is not charged from Ds Aq Makipin Almimara and a man stands there holding a Sabbatical in his hand (and wearing a robe and placing a Sach on the ark Ld but holding it YAS) every day of PA because the Sabbatical atones for us as the altar of EBA here And the year 2 and Irayah AD while there is no existing court deals with sacrifices such as those who offered a sacrifice. Me and him without ALF and AAR Shia in ALAF per the HDS YAS and on Shabbat there is no Moshiin Sat and there is no kiffin without Lulav and in HOR even those who do not have Lulav will kifu EBA and we were in the Arabah.
