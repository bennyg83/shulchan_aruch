In remembrance. Of the Mikdash; see the Taz. In Sukkah 44, the Mishnah, and 43a: regarding aravah, we do not make a remembrance of the Mikdash except on Hoshana Rabbah, the seventh day alone. But the circling that we do is a custom, not an obligation from the words of the Sages. Therefore we circle the almemar, and a man stands there holding a sefer Torah in his hand. In the Levush, 'and they place a sefer Torah on the table' is not precise; rather, he holds it. See there. This is done once each day, because the sefer Torah atones for us like the altar. See Bach here and in siman 662. It appears by way of the idea that when the Beis HaMikdash is not standing, one who studies the passages of the offerings is as though he offered an offering. 'Ani vaho' is without an alef; see Eliyah Rabbah, who says that some say it with an alef according to the Hadassim; see there. On Shabbos we do not take out a sefer Torah and do not circle, because there is no lulav. On Hoshana Rabbah, even one who has no lulav should circle; see Bach, and this means with the aravah. With Hashem's help, more will be explained in the Magen Avraham.
