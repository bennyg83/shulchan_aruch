The Order of Circling the Bimah, 3 Seifim: The practice is to bring a Torah scroll to and to circle it once each day and seven times on the seventh day, in memory of the Temple [This is] because they would circle the altar. The circles go clockwise. {Rama: (the bimah) Rem"a: And we take seven Torah scrolls onto the bimah on the seventh day (Mahari"l). There are places that take out every Torah scroll in the Ark (Minhagim) (and that is the practice in these countries). And on Shabbat we do not circle (Tur) nor do we take Torah scrolls on to the bimah.}
