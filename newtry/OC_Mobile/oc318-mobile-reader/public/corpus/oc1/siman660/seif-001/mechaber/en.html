The order of circling the bimah. It contains 3 seifim: The custom is to bring a Sefer Torah to the bimah and circle it once each day, while on the seventh day they circle it 7 times, in remembrance of the Temple. {Rama: On the seventh day, 7 Sifrei Torah are brought to the bimah. [Maharil] In some places they bring out all the Sifrei Torah in the ark, and this is the custom in these lands. [Minhagim]} In the Temple they would circle the altar, and the circles are made toward the right. On Shabbos they do not circle. [Tur] One who has no lulav does not circle, and the same applies to one who is in mourning, according to the customs cited by the authorities.
