(Rema) It appears to my humble mind that the wording is precise: 'to whom mourning occurred during the festival.' Also, that which he concluded, 'and likewise a mourner within twelve months for his father,' etc., implies that for other relatives, even within thirty days, it is permitted. For if not, even if it occurred before the festival it should also be prohibited. Rather, certainly, if it occurred before the festival, then the festival cancels the decree of shivah. The reason appears to me: the main prohibition for a mourner to circle is because of joy, as explained in the Levush. Joy that one is obligated to pay later is permitted for other relatives within thirty days, but for one's father it is prohibited all twelve months. The circling is similar to joy that one is obligated to pay later. See Yoreh Deah siman 391, and all the more so according to the Bach's view, brought in the Shach there, seif katan 3. See there in the Taz, seif katan 3, who explained the Rema's words this way as well. Since the very fact that a mourner is prohibited from circling is itself a novelty, let us not add to it.
