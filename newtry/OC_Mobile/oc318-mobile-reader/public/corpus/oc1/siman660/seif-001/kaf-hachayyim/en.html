1) [Seif 1] The custom is to bring a Torah scroll onto the bimah and circle it, etc. In the Tur it says, "onto the tevah," and so it is in the Levush. The reason for their custom is that they maintain that this tevah, before which the shaliach tzibbur descends to recite the communal prayers that correspond to the offerings, atones for us like the altar upon which an offering is brought. As for bringing the Torah scroll onto the tevah, this also appears to be because we learned: R' Eliezer says that they would say, "We acknowledge to Him; we are His, etc." The Torah scroll consists entirely of the Names of the Holy One, blessed is He; therefore they circle it, saying, "We are His and our eyes are directed to Him." Bach.
