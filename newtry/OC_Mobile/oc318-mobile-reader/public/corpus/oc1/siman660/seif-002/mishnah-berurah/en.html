(7) He does not circle - On the seventh day, if there is no lulav in the city, they should circle with the aravah [Acharonim].
(8) They take the aravah with the lulav - See below, siman 664:7 in the gloss, that it is better not to join the aravah with the lulav at all. So too they wrote in the name of the Arizal, not to join them at all with the lulav; only after Kaddish Tiskabel should you take the aravah in your hand and beat it five times on the ground. The Shelah wrote that when one reaches Taaneh Emunim, he sets down the lulav and takes the aravah.
(9) He does not circle - For it is written, 'You shall rejoice before Hashem your God seven days,' and this is at the time of the circling [Gra].
