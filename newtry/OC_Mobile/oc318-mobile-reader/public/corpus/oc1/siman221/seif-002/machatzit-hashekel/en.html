Difficult what Rama teaches here that even only heard blesses haTov vehaMetiv — perforce Eretz Yisrael partnership obvious; who is lesser than good reports meaning — and so Gemara challenged if hearing implies good tidings; and holds even Eretz Yisrael partner, etc.; if so deals with gloss some say hearer even without partner. This is certainly great novelty — therefore Beit Yosef omitted since deals with Eretz Yisrael partnership — obvious matter.
