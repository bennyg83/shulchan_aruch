[Levush] God of thanksgivings, etc. I am surprised why the Levush changed Shulchan Aruch's text, "God of abundant thanksgivings." Raavad HaLevi, cited by Ran at the beginning of Taanit, indeed rules like the Levush and explains the Gemara's instruction to say both formulations according to Rabbi Yehudah and Rabbi Yochanan. But since Beit Yosef did not cite this, it must be rejected, as Ran rejects it in the name of Ramban. Our Gemara explicitly has the other text in Berachot 59 and Taanit 7, and so it is clear from Rashi and Tosafot there. I also saw that Baal Halachot Gedolot, Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Rashba, and Talmidei Rabbenu Yonah use it. The reason is that "abundant" functions as a title meaning great; additionally, because every drop requires praise, the conclusion should mention multiplicity corresponding to the opening.
