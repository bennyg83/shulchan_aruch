(א) In partnership. Even in partnership with his wife and sons — Sefer Leket part 1 siman 196; and so Shach, and Magen Avraham disagrees — see there. If a gentile is partner in his field he does not bless haTov vehaMetiv but only shehecheyanu, for a gentile's good is not good — Magen Avraham.

(ב) Some say the hearer. Meaning where he heard and did not see, and he has a field in partnership — he blesses haTov vehaMetiv; and if he has a field alone he blesses shehecheyanu, for here there is no distinction between seer and hearer; and the blessing Modim was instituted only for a seer — see Magen Avraham.
