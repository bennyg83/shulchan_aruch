The hearer, etc. — meaning heard and didn't see and has field in partnership doesn't bless Modim, etc., but haTov vehaMetiv; if field alone blesses shehecheyanu — no partner in that good; here no distinction seer and hearer — Modim blessing not instituted except for seer; see Tur siman 223 Eretz Yisrael father died, etc., blesses — see seif 1; so in Rashba chiddushim; difficult what Rama teaches and why wrote some say, etc.; possibly Rama only saw Beit Yosef and thought Rashba innovated hearer even without partner blesses haTov vehaMetiv — examiner in Talmud sees as written and why Beit Yosef omitted; it appears to me if gentile partner in field blesses shehecheyanu — gentile's good isn't good as Tosafot Sanhedrin 37 wrote.
