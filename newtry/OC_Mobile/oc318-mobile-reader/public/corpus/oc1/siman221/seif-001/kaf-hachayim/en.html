(א) [Seif 1] If in distress from rain withholding, etc. — from Maran z"l's words implies no distinction between Eretz Yisrael and other lands: whenever years in order don't bless; whenever withheld and fell — bless; however Rambam chapter 10 laws of Berakhot didn't write when in distress, etc. — see there. Mishnat Zion ot 1; however Acharonim's view like Maran z"l: don't bless unless were in distress — Levush. Avnei Ruven ot 1 ch. 1 klal 65 ot 7; Chasid Yosef ot 1; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 61 ot 9.
