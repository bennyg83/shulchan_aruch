The blessing of thanksgiving for rain. It contains two se'ifim.
If they were in distress on account of the withholding of rain, and rain fell, they recite a blessing upon it — even though there has not yet fallen enough for a revi'ah [soaking rain] — once so much has fallen that [the waters] multiply upon the ground so that bubbles rise {upon them} from the rain and move toward one another. {Rama: And the reason we do not practice reciting the blessing over rain nowadays is because these lands are accustomed to frequent rain and it is not withheld to such an extent. (Semag, Agur, and Kol Bo.)}
