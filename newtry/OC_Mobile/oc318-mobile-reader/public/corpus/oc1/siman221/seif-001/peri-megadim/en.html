Countries — Taz and Levush when withheld even in these countries fit to bless; but Nekudat Tzafon wrote Beit Yosef added even if withheld not withheld so much therefore don't bless. One blessing without Heaven's name and kingship in these countries when dwelling in distress they don't rebuke; from Mechaber's words implies no distinction Eretz Yisrael and other lands — years in order don't bless; withheld and fell — bless; Rambam chapter 10 laws of Berakhot didn't write when in distress, etc. — see there; why Beit Yosef didn't emend seif 2 as Mechaber like Rif and Rambam three blessings on rain fell: no land Modim, has land alone shehecheyanu, partnership haTov vehaMetiv; Rosh, Rashba in chiddushim, Rashi Berakhot 59b dispute — hold on rain no shehecheyanu, whoever has land is partner; Beit Yosef's way to rule like them — in these countries don't practice this blessing; difficult he wrote some say on hearing, etc. — will be explained in Magen Avraham further.
