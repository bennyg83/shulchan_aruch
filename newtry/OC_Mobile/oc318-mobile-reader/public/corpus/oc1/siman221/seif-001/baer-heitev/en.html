So much. Beit Yosef wrote that it is obvious that even in those lands accustomed to rain, if the rains were withheld and the world was in distress and afterward rains fell — one must bless. If one sees the Nile when it is low and sees it when it is high and rejoices at seeing it, he blesses shehecheyanu even if he has no land; but one who sees it every day does not bless — see Kenesset HaGedolah.
