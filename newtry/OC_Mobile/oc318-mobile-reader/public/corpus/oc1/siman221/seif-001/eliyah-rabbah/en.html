Rain is frequent, etc. Rama's wording is "and it is not withheld so greatly." Nachalat Tzvi and Olat Tamid explain that even when there is a drought one does not bless in those lands because rain is never withheld so severely. The Levush, however, implies that when it truly is withheld one does bless, as Beit Yosef writes, and Taz agrees. Regarding Eretz Yisrael when rain is not withheld, the practical law requires study. Responsa Radbaz, siman 319, writes that one who sees the Nile when it is low and later sees it when it rises, and rejoices at seeing it, recites Shehecheyanu even if he owns no land. One who sees it every day does not bless. If he sees water in the canals, if he already saw the water at its place in the Nile he does not bless, but if his first sight of the rising water is now in the canals, he blesses.
