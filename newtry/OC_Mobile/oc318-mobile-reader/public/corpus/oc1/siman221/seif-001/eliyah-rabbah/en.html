[א] Frequent in rain, etc. — Rama's wording and they are not withheld so much; Nachalat Tzvi and Olat Tamid explained even in rain withholding they don't bless because not withheld so much; from Levush it implies if withheld they bless, as Beit Yosef and Taz wrote; in Eretz Yisrael when rains aren't withheld requires study for practical law. Radbaz responsum [219] wrote: one who sees Nile when low and sees when high rejoicing blesses shehecheyanu even without land; one who sees daily doesn't bless; if sees water in canal — if already saw water in place in Nile he doesn't bless; if only now in canal he blesses.
