Seif 2: and if he has a field in partnership — NB see Magen Avraham what Shach wrote in name of Shach that if he has wife and sons he too blesses haTov vehaMetiv; Magen Avraham wondered, for perhaps he will not give them from those fruits; and if so why didn't the Talmud answer thus in Berakhot — his words are astonishing; one may say truly the intent is he has wife and sons considered partnership, etc. Also in his law body that perhaps he won't give them from his fruits — one may say since explained in Ketubot 80 and so ruled in Even HaEzer siman 85 husband cannot sell fruit for many years from riyuv beita force; if so proven even if he supports from elsewhere nevertheless fruit applies to her from riyuv beita; if so likewise for wife and sons good in field fruit success from riyuv beita; and though iron property we rule he may sell and wife cannot protest from riyuv beita — meaning what is not hers she cannot force riyuv beita and support in poverty suffices; but since riyuv beita benefit is benefit, if he has field producing fruit it is good for wife and sons from riyuv beita; Shach well wrote wife and sons count as partnership, resolving Magen Avraham's difficulty why Talmud didn't answer thus — one may say since there Ketubot 80 dispute Abbaye doesn't hold riyuv beita only from perhaps will end, Rava holds from riyuv beita — for Abbaye riyuv beita benefit isn't benefit, wife and sons aren't partners, therefore Talmud answers there per all; it would work for Abbaye too but we rule like Rava from riyuv beita — Shach well wrote wife and sons count as partnership — works well, examine.
