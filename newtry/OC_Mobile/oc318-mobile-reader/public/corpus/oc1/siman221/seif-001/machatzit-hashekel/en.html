(א) (s.k. 1) He blesses, etc. — as Rosh wrote: Rosh truly explains Gemara's answer Eretz Yisrael partnership blesses haTov vehaMetiv — not meaning specifically partnership in his field but all rain good is called Eretz Yisrael partnership for whole world partners in this good; but Shulchan Aruch ruled like Rif requiring specifically partnership in his field.

(ב) And see siman 175 s.k. 4.

(ג) Shach wrote, etc. — siman 223 seif 1 his intent, etc. — like siman 223 male child blesses haTov vehaMetiv since wife has share in good; likewise field — wife and sons dependent on his table count as having share in his field.

(ד) And difficult if so what pressed Gemara, etc. — meaning Berakhot 39b on what we learned on rains and good tidings says haTov vehaMetiv on rains what blesses — R' Yehuda Modim anachnu lach on each drop, etc., and challenges we learned on rains haTov vehaMetiv; answers: hearing implies haTov vehaMetiv, seeing implies Modim; challenges if hearing implies good tidings we learned on good tidings (Rashi: then rains unnecessary, included in good tidings) rather both seeing: has land says haTov vehaMetiv, no land says Modim; challenges if has land haTov vehaMetiv — we learned built house bought vessels says shehecheyanu, his and others haTov vehaMetiv — no difficulty: Eretz Yisrael partnership (here on rain haTov vehaMetiv deals with Eretz Yisrael partnership), built house no partnership, learn; and R' Yosef bar Avin R' Yochanan: even though wine change need not bless separately but says baruch haTov vehaMetiv; answers there too company drinks with him — until here Gemara language. From this Magen Avraham questioned per Shach why needed to establish field blessing haTov vehaMetiv as Eretz Yisrael partnership — should answer e.g. wife and sons like partnership.

(ה) Chemed Moshe questioned: on wine certainly if wife and sons drink with him he blesses haTov vehaMetiv as Tosafot there and Magen Avraham above siman 175 s.k. 4 — then why needed to answer on wine company, etc., should say wife and sons drink with him. Further questioned Magen Avraham siman 175 reason on wine if wife and sons drink — haTov vehaMetiv because their sustenance on him is like Eretz Yisrael partnership; even though not obligated to give them all that wine, all the more field for eating exists and their sustenance on him called partners — end quote. In my humble opinion specifically wine: since already on table husband drinks and wife and sons recline — sustenance on him; men's way to give wife and sons too; unlike field fruits — sustenance on him is specifically on husband not field, not obligated give from that field, may withhold field fruit and feed other fruit bought elsewhere — therefore called partners. What questioned Talmud should answer this case wife and sons drink — not difficulty, must say what troubled Magen Avraham if so what pressed Gemara should answer, etc. — why more pressed Talmud Eretz Yisrael partnership than answer deals with has wife or sons; must say more common man has field with wife or sons than field partner. Mishnah anonymously said on rains haTov vehaMetiv — more fitting to say deals with has wife or sons than Eretz Yisrael field partnership; unlike wine drinking as common man drinks with wife and sons so common drinks with company — not more common than other; since already answered field Eretz Yisrael partnership and per Magen Avraham establish wine case company, etc., since as common as wife — and what Mishnah Berurah siman 223 seif 5 bought vessels household uses blesses haTov vehaMetiv.
