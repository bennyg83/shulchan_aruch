One who sees Nile when small and returns seeing when great rejoicing blesses shehecheyanu even without land; one seeing daily doesn't bless — first sight voided. If sees water in canal — if already saw water in place in Nile no blessing on seeing canal; if only now in canal blesses (Radbaz part 1 siman 130, 25). He blesses shehecheyanu — and although nevertheless whole world partners in that good since many have fields as Rosh wrote — nevertheless we require partners in the good; unlike here each has field separately — see siman 175. Shach wrote if has wife and sons siman 223 seif 1 intent blesses haTov vehaMetiv; difficult if so what pressed Gemara — should answer e.g. wife and sons; rather wife and sons don't help for no share in field and perhaps gives nothing from fruit unlike siman 223 seif 1 wife has share in child three partners in person; and what there seif 5 they benefit using immediately — Bach siman 222 requires study.
