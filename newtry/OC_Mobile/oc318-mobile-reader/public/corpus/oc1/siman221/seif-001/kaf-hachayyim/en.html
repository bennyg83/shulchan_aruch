1) [Seif 1] If they suffered because the rains were withheld, etc. Maran's words, of blessed memory, imply no distinction between Eretz Yisrael and other lands. Whenever the years proceed normally, no blessing is recited; whenever rain is withheld and then descends, the blessing is recited. The Rambam in chapter 10 of Hilchos Berachos, however, does not write that they must have suffered, etc.; see there. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1. The later authorities nevertheless agree with Maran, of blessed memory, that the blessing is recited only if they suffered. Levush; Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1; Chayei Adam, rule 65, paragraph 7; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 1; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 61, paragraph 9.
