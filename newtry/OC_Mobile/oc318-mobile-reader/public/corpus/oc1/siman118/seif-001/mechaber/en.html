The conclusion of the blessing Hashivah. Containing one seif:
Hashivah Shoftenu — one concludes it with Melekh ohev tzedakah u'mishpat. And from Rosh Hashana until Yom Kippur one concludes with HaMelekh HaMishpat.However, if one said Melekh ohev tzedakah u'mishpat, one need not return. And they only said one must return where all year they say HaKel ohev tzedakah u'mishpat (Ri at end of first chapter of Berachos, and Tur and Hagahot Maimoni). And see below siman 582.
