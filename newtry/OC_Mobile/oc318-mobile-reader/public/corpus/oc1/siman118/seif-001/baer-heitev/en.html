To return. So too Magen Avraham, Bach, Mateh Moshe, and Shelah. And Levush wrote that it is customary to return. And Taz raised that one must return and pray again as a voluntary prayer, for there is a difference even in the text "Melekh ohev tzedakah u'mishpat" — for all year judgment is not primary but righteousness is, and therefore they put righteousness before judgment; but from Rosh Hashana until Yom Kippur judgment is primary and therefore they did not mention righteousness. See there siman 582. If during all year he said HaMelekh HaKadosh or HaMelekh HaMishpat, he need not return — see Knesset HaGedolah siman 7 and siman 108, and so too Arukh HaShulchan there. And according to Taz's reason it seems he should return and pray again as a voluntary prayer.
