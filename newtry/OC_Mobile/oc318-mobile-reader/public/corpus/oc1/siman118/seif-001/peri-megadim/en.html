HaMelekh HaMishpat — Taz, R' Menucha — Kesef Mishnah brought them in chapter 2 of Maaseh Torah, section 8, explaining the difference between Melekh ohev tzedakah u'mishpat and HaMelekh HaMishpat: (1) He loves His creatures that they do righteousness and justice; or HaMelekh HaMishpat is He Himself the judgment — not like those who said HaMelekh HaShofet. See section 2 for more difference there.
