(1) Melekh ohev etc. — and if he said during all year HaMelekh HaMishpat or HaKel HaMishpat, he fulfilled his obligation.

(2) HaMelekh HaMishpat — with two heys, and be careful about this, for some say he did not fulfill; nevertheless after the fact if he said Melekh HaMishpat he should not return.

(3) One need not return — see the later authorities who conclude that if he remembered within kedei dibbur from when he said "u'mishpat," he may say HaMelekh HaMishpat as well; and if after twenty-four hours he should not say it again and they do not make him return.
