(20) That normally come, etc.: Meaning, even if he eats them with bread, since they are from others and certainly his mind was not on them at all, they are not exempted by the blessing on bread.
(21) On everything, etc.: This is when they brought him items that come because of the meal, as above in seif 1. But if they brought him kinds of fruit, it does not help even if he had them in mind at the time of the hamotzi blessing so that he could eat them afterward without a blessing, even if they are his own fruit, unless he uses them to accompany the bread.
Summary of the laws of fruit that comes during a meal: If he eats it with bread, it is obvious that no blessing is required at all, for it becomes secondary to the bread, even if he did not explicitly have this in mind at the time of hamotzi. If he eats it without bread, it is obvious that he needs a blessing before it, even if it is cooked, for it is not because of the meal but only as dessert. But if he wants to eat some fruit with bread and some without bread, there are differing opinions; therefore one should conduct himself by first eating a little without bread and blessing over it, and afterward he may eat the rest of the fruit either with bread or without bread. This applies when he establishes his meal to accompany the bread with other foods. But if he establishes his meal to accompany the bread with fruit, since he initially ate fruit with bread, he may eat fruit at the end without bread and without a blessing.
