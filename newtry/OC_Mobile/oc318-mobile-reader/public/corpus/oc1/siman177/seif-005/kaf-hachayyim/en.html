5) There: Such as figs and grapes, etc. The same applies to anything brought as dessert. Even if it was before him when he recited HaMotzi, and even if it is cooked fruit, bread does not cover it, since it has no connection to bread. Shelah and Magen Avraham, paragraph 2, write the same; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2; Beis Menuchah, paragraph 5. This refers to foods that are never customarily brought as part of the meal but are ordinarily eaten throughout the day, even outside mealtime, as Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes there. Fruit-filled pastries, however, such as apple pie or plum-filled pastries and the like, do not require a blessing. One who wishes to act stringently should take another fruit, either raw or cooked, recite Borei Pri HaEtz over it, and then eat those filled pastries as he wishes. Emek Berachah and Shelah; Eliyah Rabbah there. See Magen Avraham, paragraph 3, and our words above at the end of siman 168, paragraph 71.
