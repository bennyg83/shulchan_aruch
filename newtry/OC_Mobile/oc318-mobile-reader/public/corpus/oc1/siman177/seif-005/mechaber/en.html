If after he blessed over the bread someone from another household that he does not depend on sent him a gift, and he had not had that gift in mind — even things that are typically brought to accompany the bread require a blessing, as in the law of one who changes his mind. {Rama: (And I have not seen people careful about this, and perhaps the reason is that a person's default intention covers everything that is brought to him during a meal and see above Siman 206.)}
