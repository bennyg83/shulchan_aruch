There. “So it is in Tashbetz.” I examined Hagahos Tashbetz, siman 251, and there it states: “But regarding this filled pastry, even if one does not eat it during the meal, he need not bless over the apples, because the flour is primary,” end quote. It appears from this that Agudah contains a scribal error. The correct text should read: “Even outside the meal, he need not bless over the apples, and he recites Hamotzi, because the flour is primary.” See there. According to this, Magen Avraham’s words, “not only when he recites Borei Minei Mezonos,” are incorrect. His statement, “So it is in Tashbetz,” requires study, for in my humble opinion this is not what Tashbetz says.
