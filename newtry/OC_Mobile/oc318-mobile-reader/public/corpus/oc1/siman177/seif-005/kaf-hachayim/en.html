(5) There: such as figs and grapes, etc.—the same for anything that comes for dessert; even if it was before him when he blessed hamotzi, even cooked fruits which bread does not exempt since they do not pertain to bread. So Shlah; Magen Avraham s.k. 2; Ohr Zaru'a note 2, etc. And such as are never customary to come because of the meal but their way is to eat them all day even not at mealtime, as the mountain wrote there. But fruit puree such as apple pudding or similar need no blessing on them; one who wants to be stringent takes another fruit, live or cooked, and blesses borei pri ha'etzah on it and then eats those purees as he wishes—Emek Berachah and Shlah HaKadosh, Ohr Zaru'a there. See Magen Avraham s.k. 3 and in our words above siman 168 end note 71.
