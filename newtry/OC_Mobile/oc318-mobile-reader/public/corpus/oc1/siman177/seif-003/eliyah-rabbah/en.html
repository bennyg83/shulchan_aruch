[3] Levush: Types of mezonos during the meal, etc. Olas Tamid writes that this implies that if one ate them before the meal and did not recite an after-blessing, Bircas HaMazon does not exempt them from an after-blessing. Above, siman 174, seif 6, however, it does not appear so; rather, even if he drank the requisite amount, Bircas HaMazon exempts him. This requires study. Chiddushei Rashba, cited by Beit Yosef, likewise explains that one who ate before the meal must recite an after-blessing; see there. Nevertheless, one might say that if he did not recite an after-blessing and then recited Bircas HaMazon, he need not afterward recite the after-blessing. This requires practical study. Taz, at the end of siman 174, seif katan 9, somewhat implies that afterward he must recite both Shehakol and Me'ein Shalosh. With regard to drinking, however, the Rosh at the beginning of Arvei Pesachim explains that even initially no after-blessing is required. One may distinguish between drinking, which opens the intestines as explained above in Levush, and eating. See below, siman 493, seif 6.
