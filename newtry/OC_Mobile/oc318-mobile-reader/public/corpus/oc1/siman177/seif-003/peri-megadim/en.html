And there is. Taz challenged what Mechaber wrote for Mahari Molcho: when establishing meal on fruit must eat first with bread, etc.—this is the difference between establishment and non-establishment that then needs beginning and end with bread as seif 1 per Beit Yosef. Mahari Molcho brings Yerushalmi cited in note 2 and wrote when they require blessing before them, etc., and did not say even when establishing and eating first without bread—implies all who establish, even if ate first without bread, also need no first blessing like Rosh; moreover Rabbi Chiya should have done so—to eat first little without bread and bless at least first on it. Nevertheless in what Mahari Molcho wrote when we say bless before them without bringing to dip at all—I did not understand; for to dip also whenever they are not primary of meal we require bread beginning and end; also know Rosh's text s.v. ate dates with bread means reciprocally, and Terumat Hadeshen above bread means after bread, see there; and therefore must bless here—this must be Rabbenu Yonah.

And the rule Taz wrote: when there is no primary establishment on fruit, what one dips—no blessing before it, main exempts secondary; if eating without bread needs intent as seif 1; if primary on fruit—as said before, even Mahari Molcho agrees to Rosh; nevertheless act to satisfy all: eat first with bread, then when eating afterward without bread even at end, since he blessed at beginning he has blessed.
