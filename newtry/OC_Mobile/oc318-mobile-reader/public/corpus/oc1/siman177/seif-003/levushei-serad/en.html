Shulchan Aruch, end of seif 1 “And at the end he ate them with bread.” This is the view of Rabbeinu Yonah, unlike Beer HaGolah. He holds that the case here is where the meal was not established on fruit, but fruit was brought in the middle of the meal. One must recite a blessing when eating it without bread unless he eats it with bread both at the beginning and at the end. According to him, where the meal was established on fruit, namely the case of seif 3, the law is one degree more lenient, and eating it with bread at the beginning is sufficient. This is the dissenting opinion there. According to the Rosh and Tur, however, in the present case it is enough to eat the fruit with bread at the beginning and one need not do so at the end. In the case of seif 3, which is more lenient, even if he initially eats without bread he need not recite a blessing, provided he later eats it with bread. This is the first opinion there. It requires study why the Mechaber rules here without qualification like Rabbeinu Yonah and does not mention the view of the Rosh and Tur at all, yet in seif 3 treats the Rosh's view as primary. Examine this.
