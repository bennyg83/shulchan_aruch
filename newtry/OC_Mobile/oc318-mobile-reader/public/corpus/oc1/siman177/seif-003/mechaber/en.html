If one fixes his meal's accompaniment upon fruits, the fruits are treated like things that come on account of the meal, and even if he eats the fruits at the beginning of his meal without bread he recites no blessing either before or after them. Some disagree, and therefore it is best that he first eat some of the fruits with bread — for then even if he afterward eats them without bread they require no blessing at all.Rema: And this applies even if he does not return at the end to eat bread with them, since the main fixed eating was upon them. (Beit Yosef in the name of Ri).
