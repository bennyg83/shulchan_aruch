He need not birkat acharonah because of doubt whether he needs birkat acharonah—for if fruits of puree need blessing before them, then with the blessing he blessed before netilah on fruits he exempted fruits of the puree, and therefore fruits before the meal pertain to the meal—hence no birkat acharonah for birkat hamazon exempts them; but if fruits of puree within the meal need no blessing before them, then the blessing before netilah is unnecessary—then fruits before netilah do not pertain to the meal and birkat hamazon does not exempt them and he must bless birkat acharonah afterward; and since it is doubt whether he must bless on puree fruits, therefore he should not eat before netilah an amount that would obligate him from doubt in birkat acharonah. So too Tur, etc., for those who say no blessing needed, etc., meaning per R' Yonah who need not bless on maror eaten on Passover night within the meal because birkat hamotzi exempts it—then if he ate karpas before the meal in sufficient quantity he must bless birkat acharonah after it for it has no connection to the meal; unlike Rashba who holds hamotzi does not exempt maror but borei pri ha'adamah on karpas before the meal exempts maror from borei pri ha'adamah—then borei pri ha'adamah on karpas pertains to the meal even if he ate karpas in quantity—no birkat acharonah after for birkat hamazon exempts. This proof Magen Avraham brought also above siman 174.
