(15) And if he establishes, etc. Meaning, the meal was established on using only these fruits to accompany the bread. They are therefore treated like the other foods discussed above in seif 1.
(16) And some disagree. They hold that if he initially ate some of the fruit without bread, it does not thereby become secondary to the meal, and he must bless over it initially.
(17) And even though, etc., since, etc. Although in seif 1 we said that he must also eat the fruit with bread at the end, where the meal was initially established principally on these fruits, all agree that he may afterward eat them without a blessing even if he does not intend to eat more bread with them.
