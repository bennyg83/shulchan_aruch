Seif katan 1: Without, etc. As stated in siman 212, the primary item exempts the secondary item.
And he eats a little bread. In that case the bread is not primary, so there is no reason for it to exempt the other foods except by virtue of the bread’s importance, just as we rule that wine exempts all other beverages because of its importance even when the wine is not primary, as stated in siman 174, seif 2. Rabbeinu Chananel likewise holds this regarding bread, as stated in Berachos 41b. We do not, however, rule like Rabbeinu Chananel, for on this basis we rule that if items not brought because of the meal, such as figs and grapes, are brought during the meal, one must recite a blessing over them and the bread does not exempt them, as stated in this seif. According to Rabbeinu Chananel, bread would exempt them even in such a case, as stated there in Berachos.
Since it is customary to establish, etc. In this respect, items brought because of the meal are superior, and regarding this we rule like Rabbeinu Chananel.
Therefore, he should recite a blessing over them and not over the bread, except on Shabbos and Yom Tov. This appears to me to be the correct text. If he recites a blessing over the bread, there will remain a doubt whether he must also bless over the fruit. It is therefore preferable to bless over the fruit and not eat bread at all in such a case, since he needs the bread only to exempt the fruit. This differs from Shabbos and Yom Tov, when he must in any event eat bread. On Shabbos and Yom Tov, since he must eat bread, even if he eats only a small amount, perhaps the bread is primary and exempts items brought because of the meal.
