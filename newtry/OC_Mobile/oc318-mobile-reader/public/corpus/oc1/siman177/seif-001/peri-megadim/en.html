At end he eats with them bread. Taz: Levush added to Mechaber's language: if at beginning of his eating from them he eats bread and intends also finally to eat with bread, etc. What he wrote if not—even if he will not eat afterward, etc.—does not help here, etc.; meaning here it discusses one who established meal on other things, and at beginning of eating, namely from them; in middle of meal it discusses if he established on fruit—even though some disagree below seif 3, all who ate at beginning with bread suffices without needing intent at end, etc., meaning does not help, etc.—this regulation in middle: eat little without bread only when established on other things, then needs beginning and intent at end with bread; when established on fruit, beginning alone suffices. From his words you learn this law is words of Mahari Molcho brought in Beit Yosef; so Magen Avraham note 3; Be'er HaGolah note 2 cites Rosh from Yerushalmi and Ein Yaakov that Mahari Molcho should be cited; from Rosh and Tur: all whose beginning of eating is with bread, even if he ate again without bread though he no longer intends with bread to dip—they came as in Beit Yosef. Possibly from what Mechaber did not write and his intent at end implies Mechaber's ruling is somewhat like Rosh—requires study; not here; note 2 will explain more; cannot expand here.
