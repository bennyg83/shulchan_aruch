All kinds of fruit, etc., require a blessing before them, etc. Cooked fruits have the same law as wine. However, fruit-filled pastries made with apples or other fruits are covered by the blessing over bread and are not comparable to pas haba'ah bekisnin. Emek Berachah. Likewise, when one drinks spirits after reciting Hamotzi, he must recite a blessing beforehand. Maharami. Some say that no blessing should be recited. Mateh Moshe, siman 44. I saw in a small work authored by Rabbi Yisrael Ginz, in the name of the Gaon Rabbi Feivish of Krakow, that the spirits should be placed on the table at the time Hamotzi is recited, in which case Hamotzi exempts them. If one did not do so, he should dip a little bread into the spirits and eat it; he will then not need to recite a blessing, as with fruit, and thereby avoid the dispute.
