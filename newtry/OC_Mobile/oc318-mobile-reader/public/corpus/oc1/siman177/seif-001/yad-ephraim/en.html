Magen Avraham s.k. 1: and requires study therefore he should not bless, etc. Some emend to therefore he should bless, etc., if not Shabbat and Yom Tov—the reason: on Shabbat and Yom Tov one is obligated to eat bread, then bread is primary; and it implies so in siman 174 s.k. 12 that his view is when eating little bread he should not bless, see there, that he showed a place to siman 212 meaning there it is explained that whenever bread is secondary one does not bless on it, and if he eats it at the beginning he blesses shehakol, see there; and so appears from words of Even HaEzer in beginning of siman 174 that Magen Avraham ruled, see there; and so from what Magen Avraham wrote siman 168 s.k. 22; and that Terumat Hadeshen wrote, etc., that these are fruits—therefore it discusses such, for otherwise bread would not exempt them as in siman 177, end quote—implies also his view in such a case to bless on fruits; but arrangement of his language requires study for he wrote to side and say bread exempts—requires study how he concluded therefore, etc.; in my humble opinion possibly it should read therefore he should not bless on them but eat them with bread, meaning make them secondary to bread; and what he wrote if not Shabbat Yom Tov means as written siman 295 one may leave fruits after the meal to bless on them to fulfill hundred blessings, see there—in this one may rely on first view to bless on fruits separately and not eat with bread; also possible what he wrote and not on bread—the words and not on are a corruption and should read and to eat bread, a common scribal error, meaning he should not do so—to bless on fruits and eat bread, for although little, one can say it exempts if not Shabbat Yom Tov when obligated to eat bread he may do so and rely on first view—this too requires study.
