Magen Avraham, seif katan 1 We do not rule like Rabbeinu Chananel. Rabbeinu Tam said in Berachos 41a that bread exempts all foods and wine exempts all beverages. The poskim ruled this way regarding wine in siman 174, seif 2. Regarding bread, however, Tosafos write that the halachah does not follow this view because Rav Pappa, who is the principal authority for the laws of this siman, disagrees and holds that foods not brought because of the meal require a blessing beforehand, and when brought after the meal require a blessing afterward as well. See there. It follows that foods brought because of the meal are exempt only because they are subordinate to the bread. In the case of Behag, where one does not wish to eat bread, the bread is subordinate.
