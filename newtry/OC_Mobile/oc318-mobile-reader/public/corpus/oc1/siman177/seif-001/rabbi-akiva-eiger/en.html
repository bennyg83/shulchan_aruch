Magen Avraham s.k. 5: such as meat and fish. If he ate pat haba'ah b'kisanin he is exempted by birkat hamazon—see below siman 208 s. 9 on the gloss; and here it is better to say it is called kvi'ut, for from establishing on bread there is also establishment regarding this bread that comes as pat haba'ah b'kisanin; and by law he should bless on it birkat hamazon as the law of all pat haba'ah b'kisanin in kvi'ut, he answered. But I ask: if so why bless borei minei mezonot when eating for dessert within the meal; and similarly rolls above siman 168 that one blesses before them—say since there is establishment on bread, there is establishment also on them, and automatically by law hamotzi and bread exempts them—requires study.
