And if at the beginning. This law is from Terumat Hadeshen, who says: it discusses such as they were standing in the middle of the meal and the beginning of establishing their meal was with meat and the like, and in the middle of their meal they brought them fruit to dip bread with them, and they ate from them with bread, and now eat them without bread with intent still to eat them with bread afterward—no blessing needed because from the start they brought them to dip bread and eat beginning and end with bread—no need to bless as joined with rest of meal since brought from start for the meal and to dip bread beginning and end, etc. If at first he had nothing else to dip bread and afterward eats from them without bread—even if at end he does not eat with them he does not bless on them since from this he made them primary for his dipping; if they did not bring them from start to dip and he eats them separately he blesses, end quote—meaning when they come for dessert and not to dip: even if he ate beginning and end with bread, if he eats in the middle without bread he must bless since they did not come to dip bread—in this most poskim agree; but there are other views, therefore Hagahot Maimoni wrote: God-fearing person when they bring him fruit in middle of meal to dip bread should eat a little without bread and bless, then eat them with or without bread—implies he holds even when eating beginning and end with bread there is room to doubt; so Bach wrote and Agudah wrote similarly that in middle of meal eating fruit with bread or apple pastry—good to eat a little without bread and bless since fruit is not primary of his meal, end quote. Hagahot Tashbetz wrote inwardly meaning must take out a little fruit from the puree and bless on it; Maharif said need not bless on fruits in puree even if he blessed hamotzi for flour is primary, end quote—meaning not only when blessing borei minei mezonot as siman 168 that need not bless on fruits, but even hamotzi on bread need not bless on fruits separately for flour is primary and bread exempts—so Tashbetz siman 168. However on cooked fruits in themselves even with meat if beginning of establishing meal was not on them—doubt whether to bless; also some say even with bread must bless as Agudah and Kol Bo and Avnei Yisrael; therefore good to eat first without bread and bless—so Semak and Agur 232 that his father practiced; so in Kol Bo in name of Rosh in name of Rashba. But in short rulings of Rosh he doubts if blessing needed since they come for meal purpose and not dessert, therefore wrote he should sit before washing and bless on fruits, end quote—and should eat less than satiating amount so no birkat acharonah as siman 174 s. 7, see there; so Tur siman 473 for those who say no blessing within meal must bless birkat acharonah on those before meal; however Tur wrote plainly that if eating without bread must bless since meal was not primarily established on them at beginning of meal, end s.g.
