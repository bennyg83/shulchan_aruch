And even Taz to explain his words z"l I must copy Tur's language: and wrote in name of Rosh z"l there is room to doubt when establishing meal on fruit if he ate of them first without bread whether to bless on them; reasoning is not to bless for per him it is because of meal for primary meal is on them even little without bread; but when bringing fruit in middle of meal and primary meal is not on them even though dipping bread in them—if he eats little first without bread he blesses on them. Rosh Berakhot 41b: after writing reasoning he brought Yerushalmi Rav Huna ate dates with bread, Rabbi Chiya bar Ashi told him you argue with your teacher who quenches after your food and bless on them beginning and end; he told him they are falsehood—meaning they are primary of meal how can I leave them for end; implies after food before birkat hamazon and as Rabbenu Peretz after meal before and after (implies dates birkat hamazon does not exempt l'chatchila unlike Magen Avraham note 16, see there); but within meal need no blessing after even if ate without bread—for if without bread they also need blessing after within meal why say quench after food—say he should eat in meal without bread and bless beginning and end. However one cannot prove from here that if brought fruit to eat his bread and ate some without bread in middle he would not need blessing before—from that he did not tell him eat within meal, because of birkat acharonah he said quench after your food, end quote—I copied due to need. Bach explained he brought Yerushalmi to prove his law that if they are primary of meal no blessing before or after, added to reasoning he wrote himself; and second law reasoning gives that little without bread blesses first—found for first law there is reasoning and proof from Yerushalmi, and second law when primary meal not on fruit and eats little without bread he blesses on fruit before—reasoning, see Bach; and Rav Huna ate fruit with bread and did not bless before, and truthfully he did not eat some fruit without bread to bless before in any case (as Rabbi Chiya bar Ashi asked quench after food bless beginning end to give praise to Heaven, so too here he should eat little without bread and bless first) implies all whose primary meal even eating without bread some of fruit—no blessing before. Rosh's words need explanation in what he wrote within meal need not after even if ate without bread—discusses if brought to dip and ate some without bread, plain so, for if only when eating and dipping all with bread no need after, then also no need before—all for dipping for main exempts secondary as Rosh on things within meal not because of meal as Shulchan Aruch and Levush here; if discusses fruit brought not to dip at all—should need blessing after within meal, and Rabbenu Peretz in sugya discusses brought to dip and ate little without bread—then what proof from Yerushalmi, though Rabbi Chiya bar Ashi holds no primary meal, nevertheless he saw brought to dip and even if tell him eat little without bread nevertheless no birkat acharonah and said quench after food because of birkat acharonah, and must say nevertheless he eats them without bread at all for Rabbi Chiya bar Ashi thought also for dipping he did not bring only to exempt from blessing, somewhat as Taz wrote—and somewhat strained also Taz in what Rosh wrote; however cannot prove, etc., he should have said without bread at all—requires study as explained in general rules here that in such case bless before, and one can say, see below on this.
