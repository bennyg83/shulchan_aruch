Things that come after the meal but before Grace After Meals — it was the practice in the times of the Talmudic sages that at the end of the meal they would withdraw their hands from the bread, remove it, and settle in to eat fruits and drink whatever was brought before them — whether things that come on account of the meal or things that do not come on account of the meal, they require a blessing both beforehand and afterward, as HaMotzi and Grace After Meals only exempt what is eaten during the main meal. This last ruling is not common among us, since we are not accustomed to withdrawing our hands from the bread until Grace After Meals.
