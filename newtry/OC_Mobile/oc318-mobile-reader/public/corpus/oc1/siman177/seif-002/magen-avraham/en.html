For we do not, etc. Levush writes that at large meals people withdraw their hands from the bread, and although slices remain on the table, they do not intend to eat them. This is also implied by the Tur. Bach, however, writes that our practice follows Tosafos, that our attention always remains on the bread. It appears to me that the reason is as the students of Rabbeinu Yonah write: only in their times, when they removed the tables before Bircas HaMazon, did what followed appear to be a separate meal. We, who do not remove the table, regard everything as part of the meal until Bircas HaMazon, even after the eating has ended.
