[2] And all kinds of fruit, etc. Whether raw or cooked. However, fruit-filled pastries, such as apple fladen or similar pastries, require no blessing. One who wishes to be stringent should take another fruit, raw or cooked, recite Borei Peri Ha'etz over it, and then eat those pastries as he wishes. So write Emek Berachah and Shelah. See above, end of siman 168. Regarding pas haba'ah bekisnin, rolls, and the like, see above, siman 168, seif 9. All this applies even if the fruit was present when he recited Hamotzi.
