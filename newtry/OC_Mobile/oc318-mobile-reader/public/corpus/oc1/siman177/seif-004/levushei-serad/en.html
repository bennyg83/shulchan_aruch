Taz, end of seif katan 1 “For otherwise he certainly need not recite a blessing.” Meaning, if one explains “at the beginning of his eating” as referring to the principal establishment of the meal on fruit, namely the case of seif 3, it would be difficult, for then he would not need to eat it with bread at the end. According to this view, which is Rabbeinu Yonah's as explained above, eating it with bread at the beginning alone would suffice.
