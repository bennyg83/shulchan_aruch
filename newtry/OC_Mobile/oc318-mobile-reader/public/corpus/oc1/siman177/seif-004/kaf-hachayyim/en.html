4) There: Even when eaten without bread, they do not require a blessing, etc. This implies that specifically when one establishes a meal upon bread are these considered foods brought as part of the meal and covered by the blessing over bread. If he eats only a small amount of bread, less than a kezayis, he must recite blessings over them. See Magen Avraham there, who deliberates about this. Therefore, when eating these foods outside a meal, one should take care not to eat bread at all. If he wishes to eat pas habaah bekisnin with them, see above, siman 168, paragraph 99.
