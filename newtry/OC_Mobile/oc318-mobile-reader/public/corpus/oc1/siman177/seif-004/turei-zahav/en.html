Since they are brought to accompany the bread, they require no blessing at all. This means regarding what he eats with bread. The law of what he eats without bread was explained earlier. Here Beit Yosef cites the Rosh in the chapter Arvei Pesachim, that appetizers and wine brought to stimulate and extend the meal are included in the meal and are exempted by Bircas HaMazon. It follows that if one drinks liquor before eating, he need not recite a final blessing even if he drank the minimum measure, since it too stimulates the appetite for the meal that follows. Levush writes here that one must recite an initial blessing over liquor drunk during the meal. Above, in siman 174, I too wrote this, though for a different reason.
