Magen Avraham s.k. 7 — to kindle the candle refers to the words of the Hagahah: before dark he can tell to kindle:
Seif 2, in the Rama's gloss. “If he wishes to begin earlier.” The Levush in siman 263 wrote that it is like a vow, and annulment is effective for it. Eliyah Rabbah questioned this. See what I wrote in my gloss to siman 253, on Magen Avraham, s.k. 26.
