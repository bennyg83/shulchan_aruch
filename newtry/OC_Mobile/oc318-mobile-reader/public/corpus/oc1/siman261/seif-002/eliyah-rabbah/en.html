(2) One may not immerse without need, etc. — Taz was stringent even for need since one can give it as a gift to a non-Jew as below siman 323 seif 7; and similarly Olat Shabbat wrote that one does not separate challah even for need since one can leave a little as below siman 506. They also wrote in Bach's name to immerse through ha'arama such as drawing water, etc., and below it will be explained that even on Shabbat it is permitted in this manner; and perhaps his intent is that since ha'arama is possible, it is forbidden to immerse during bein hashemashot without ha'arama even in a matter where it is impossible to give it to a non-Jew:
