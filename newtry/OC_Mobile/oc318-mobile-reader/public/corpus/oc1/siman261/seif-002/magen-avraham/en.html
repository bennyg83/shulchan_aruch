That one must add. And it is a positive mitzvah d'oraisa:

Three-fourths of a mil. And it is like a quarter of an hour before nightfall; and Bach wrote one should be stringent for himself as written Sefer HaYiraim and Mordechai and Agudah — that from the beginning of sunset it is night, and three-fourths of a mil before that is bein hashemashos. And so I saw elders and men of action who ceased from melacha two hours before Shabbat — end of his words. And these are the words of Maharil responsum siman 163 regarding prayer and accepting Shabbat — it is simple that they go every other day after its hours, for they divide it into two evening and night hours from midnight until evening; and from those they estimate plag mincha and tosefet Shabbat two hours for everything prior, per the words of Maharach. And through this the custom of communities was settled — end of his words. And it implies they calculate until nightfall literally; and so Minchat Cohen; and see what is written siman 233:

From plag hamincha. And it is a walk of four mil before the beginning of sunset (Torat HaAdam); and Machatzit HaShekel wrote it is a printing error and should read four hundred amah approximately; but in truth it is a tenth part of the hour, and is only the time of a walk of thirty-three amah and a third — as Mahari'v wrote — that if he accepted before plag hamincha his acceptance is nothing — end of his words.
