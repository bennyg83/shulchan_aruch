There are those' who say one must add from the weekday to the holy, and the time of this addition is from the beginning of sunset when the sun is no longer visible on the earth until the time of bein hashemashos; and this time which is three mil and a quarter — if he wishes to make all of it addition he may; if he wishes to make part of it he may, provided he adds some time that is certainly weekday over the holy. And the measure of the time of bein hashemashos is three-fourths of a mil, which is a walk of one thousand and five hundred amah before nightfall.And if he wishes to advance and accept Shabbat upon himself from plag hamincha and onward, the permission is in his hand (Tur and Agur in the name of Tosafot chapter Tefilat HaShachar) [and see siman 267].]:
