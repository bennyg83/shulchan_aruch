(s.k. 2) One may not tithe, etc. This is also implied at the end of siman 260. Meaning, since he had to ask while it was still daylight, “Have you separated challah?”, it follows that when it is doubtful whether night has begun it is forbidden to separate challah. Otherwise, he should have delayed asking about challah until bein hashmashos, as explained above in siman 260 in the name of Rashi. One cannot answer as the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 260 regarding an eiruv chatzeiros, that only after the fact was an eiruv chatzeiros permitted during bein hashmashos because of a mitzvah, but not initially. This is not so, because separating challah necessarily involves no shevus at all; otherwise it would not have been permitted even after the fact during bein hashmashos, since separating challah does not involve a mitzvah here, nor is it for the honor of Shabbos, for we rule that with challah outside Eretz Yisrael one may continue eating and separate it afterward. What honor of Shabbos, then, is involved in separating challah? If one were to think that separating challah during bein hashmashos is permitted, it would necessarily be permitted even initially, since it would involve no prohibition at all. The question would then remain why he advanced the inquiry concerning challah to while it was still daylight. Rather, it must be that separating challah during bein hashmashos is forbidden, precisely because no mitzvah is involved in separating challah here, since one may continue eating and separate it afterward, and it therefore does not involve the honor of Shabbos, as stated above. With challah of Eretz Yisrael, however, where there is no remedy of eating and separating afterward, the Magen Avraham agrees that it is permitted during bein hashmashos if he has nothing to eat on Shabbos, as he wrote in s.k. 6 regarding tithing and the like. The Magen Avraham therefore concluded this seif katan, “See siman 506,” meaning s.k. 8, where the Magen Avraham wrote that if erev Pesach falls on erev Shabbos and he forgot before Shabbos to separate challah from chametz bread, there is no remedy even outside Eretz Yisrael of eating and separating afterward. Accordingly, in such a case, if Shabbos falls on erev Pesach, he forgot to separate challah from the chametz, and remembered during bein hashmashos on erev Shabbos, he may separate it during bein hashmashos, because in such a case it is a mitzvah. The Tosefes Shabbos likewise wrote this.
