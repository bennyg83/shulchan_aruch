(s.k. 2) One may not tithe, etc. — and Magen Avraham wrote siman 205. Meaning: since he had to ask at darkening regarding separating challah, from this it is excluded that in safek chashicha it is forbidden to separate challah; for otherwise he would have to wait to ask about challah until safek chashicha, as above in siman 205 in the name of Rashi. And there is no need, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 205 regarding eruv chatzerot — that b'dieved eruv chatzerot on bein hashemashos is permitted on account of a mitzvah but not l'chatchila; meaning: for necessarily in separating challah there is no shevut at all, for otherwise b'dieved it would not be permitted on bein hashemashos, for in separating challah there is no mitzvah and not on account of honor of Shabbat — for we hold one may eat challah of chutz la'aretz and walk and afterward separate. And if so, what honor of Shabbat is there in separating challah? And if one would think it is permitted to separate challah on bein hashemashos — necessarily even l'chatchila it is permitted, for there is no prohibition in it at all; and if so, it is difficult — why did he precede asking about challah at darkening? Rather it is forbidden to separate challah on bein hashemashos. And the reason itself — because there is no mitzvah in separating challah, for one eats and walks and afterward separates, and there is no [prohibition] on account of honor of Shabbat, as above. And regarding challah of Eretz Yisrael where there is no remedy through eating and afterward separating — Magen Avraham agrees it is permitted on bein hashemashos if he has nothing to eat on Shabbat, as he wrote s.k. 6 regarding tithe. And Taz — and therefore Magen Avraham concluded this s.k. and see siman 306 — meaning in s.k. 8 where Magen Avraham wrote that if Shabbat falls on erev Pesach and he forgot on erev Pesach to separate challah from chametz bread for which there is no remedy even in chutz la'aretz to eat and afterward separate — if so, likewise if Shabbat fell on erev Pesach and he forgot to separate challah from matzah and remembered on bein hashemashos on erev Pesach — it is permitted to separate on bein hashemashos, for in such a case it is a mitzvah.
