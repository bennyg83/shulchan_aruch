(א) One does not tithe demai. Rashi — it is a superior enactment; and although it is mere shevut they hold they decreed on shevut even during bein hashemashot; and tevilat kelim was also shevut as it appears one repairs a vessel; and Beit Yosef wrote: if so, since we hold siman 302 that every shevut they did not decree during bein hashemashot, one would tithe demai during bein hashemashot; rather Tur wrote there they did not decree on shevut during bein hashemashot only for mitzvah need or when he was preoccupied; and therefore he wrote here at the end that one may tell a non-Jew during bein hashemashot to kindle a candle for Shabbat need, etc.; and according to this also in tevilat kelim if he needs it for Shabbat and has no other but can immerse during bein hashemashot, except there is another heter — give it to a non-Jew as a gift as written Yoreh Deah siman 120 seif 16 — if so they did not permit shevut even during bein hashemashot:

(ב) And one makes eruv chatzerot. In the Gemara we challenge: this itself is difficult — you said three things a person must say within within Erev Shabbat with darkness, with darkness, safek darkness no; meaning do not say then "your eve" for it is forbidden then to make eruv; and the tanna taught safek darkness one makes eruv; and we answer: here in eruvei techumin it is more stringent for the Sages relied on a verse; here in eruvei chatzerot; and it is difficult — if so why did Tur write in siman 200 with darkness one makes eruv chatzerot, for that mishna we establish here in eruvei techumin; and one could say that certainly for warning his household he must mention eruv chatzerot with darkness, even though afterward in safek darkness he can make eruv chatzerot; nevertheless since on Erev Yom Tov it is forbidden afterward he will also mention eruv chatzerot at that time; and it turns out that establishing it on Erev Yom Tov is regarding that afterward it is forbidden; but regarding amirah to his household they are equal, for one cannot say a distinction here that with darkness he warns about Erev Yom Tov and afterward warns again about eruv chatzerot, for they did not make two times for amirah; certainly l'chatchila one should not rely on safek darkness regarding eruv chatzerot; and that Tur did not mention amirah of Erev Yom Tov is because it is not a fixed thing that is not found on Erev Yom Tov, unlike eruv chatzerot that they were accustomed only every Erev Shabbat: In Hagahot Maimoniyot it is written by Rif that it is permitted to immerse on Shabbat a vessel via trickery, such as drawing water to drink in it or for another need and filling it with water, etc. Rashal wrote in responsa siman 46: one who forgot to kindle a yarzeit candle may tell a non-Jew during bein hashemashot to kindle it:
