(Seif katan 1) Bein hashemashot, and if he is uncertain, etc. — it is forbidden. Bach on erev Shabbat, for Bach inferred thus from Rashi's language and it teaches us that even though it is like a safek sefeika — one doubt perhaps it is still before bein hashemashot, and even if you say it is bein hashemashot, bein hashemashot itself is safek yom — nevertheless it is forbidden; and the reason, as Tosafot Shabbos wrote, is safek heser yeda. And in my humble opinion, even on a cloudy day which is safek for everyone — what is safek for the entire world is not included in safek heser yeda; see Yoreh De'ah siman 110; and even though it is possible to reject that even on a cloudy day it is called heser yeda, for certainly it is not cloudy in the entire world — nevertheless without this I have no answer, and our safek sefeika stands as Tosafot Ketubot wrote in the sugya of an open door — that one compulsion makes both safekot one: is it night or day:
