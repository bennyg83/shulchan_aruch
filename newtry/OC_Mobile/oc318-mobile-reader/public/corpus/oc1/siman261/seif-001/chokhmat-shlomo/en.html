Seif 1 — safek chashichah and it is bein hashemashot, etc.; but one tithes demai and insulates hot food and makes eruvei chatzerot, etc. NB: there is room to doubt whether the intent is also regarding blessing that he spoke to bless as well, as when another made the eruv for him, or the intent is without blessing, since in truth during bein hashemashot it is safek yom safek lailah; and if we had known that now it is night he would not have been able to make an eruv — if so for us it is safek, and eruvei chatzerot are d'rabbanan and it is safek d'rabbanan l'hakel. In any case regarding blessing it is the opposite — safek d'rabbanan l'kula like all safek berachot l'hakel. And ostensibly it appeared to me according to this view that one does not bless; however ostensibly it is difficult for me from the Shas which asks "with darkness" — there is no doubt, "with darkness" no — yet we learned safek chashichah safek lo chashichah one makes eruv chatzerot; and it answers here for a matter of mitzvah need here for eruv chatzerot — why does it not answer that both are eruv chatzerot? Therefore one must warn with darkness so that he not lose the blessing, for if he waits until safek chashichah he loses the blessing, and therefore it warns with darkness — and what is the difficulty? And from this it was proven that one blesses even with safek chashichah. However it appears that through this one may reconcile the view of the Tur who brought the Taz who copied the mishnah "if darkness on erev Shabbat." And according to what is explained one may say that behold this law whether one blesses during bein hashemashot depends on what they disagree in chapter 2 of Shabbat — Abbaye and Rava — whether one blesses on demai: Abbaye said certainly their words require blessing, safek their words do not require blessing; and Rava answers majority of am ha'aretz tithe, and likewise here per the one who holds there that safek their words do not require blessing — likewise when making eruv chatzerot during bein hashemashot one does not bless, for safek their words do not require blessing; but for Rava who answers majority of am ha'aretz tithe, it is implied that on safek their words one blesses properly — if so here during bein hashemashot one also blesses. And behold in my composition Sefer HaChaim siman 483 I proved there that Rambam does not rule like Rava who holds majority of am ha'aretz tithe, and Rambam rules like Ravina in Bekhorot that we do not say majority which depends on action, and he does not hold majority of am ha'aretz tithe — if so automatically one must say that safek their words do not require blessing; if so it fits well to say that the Shas which proceeds here with eruv techumin is for Rava who holds majority of am ha'aretz tithe but safek their words require blessing, therefore no need to warn with eruv chatzerot with darkness but only with a matter of mitzvah need; but for us one should say that the Tur holds like the view of the aforesaid Rambam who does not hold majority of am ha'aretz tithe — if so he holds safek their words do not require blessing, therefore he warns even with eruv chatzerot so that he not lose the blessing — understand well and it fits: And behold from what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the poskim in siman 489 regarding counting, that per the one who holds counting is d'rabbanan it is permitted to count during bein hashemashot — it is implied there counting like a matter of law with berachah — it would be implied that also in such a case one blesses on eruv chatzerot, for there too if it is day it is not counting and one blesses, and the same here. However one may say that they hold like Rava who blesses on safek their words, and on demai is different since majority of am ha'aretz tithe; and according to what is explained that Rambam rules not like Rava — according to him one may say in truth that one does not count during bein hashemashot with berachah even per the one who holds d'rabbanan; and it requires further study thus for practical law now, understand well in this:
