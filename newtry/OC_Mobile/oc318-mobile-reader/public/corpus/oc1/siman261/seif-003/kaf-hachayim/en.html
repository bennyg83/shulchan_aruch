One does not tithe vadai. — it is superior repair, and even though we hold leniently that anything which is due to shevut they did not decree upon during bein hashemashot as is written in siman 302, Beit Yosef wrote that specifically for a matter of mitzvah or when he was preoccupied; and therefore he wrote that it is permitted to tell a non-Jew during bein hashemashot to do any melacha that is for the need of a mitzvah or when he is preoccupied and hurries because of it, see there. Taz seif katan 1 brought and wrote that accordingly also for immersing a vessel, if he needs it for Shabbat and has no other, he may immerse during bein hashemashot; however there is another permission — namely to give it to a non-Jew as a gift as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 120 seif 16; if so we did not permit shevut even during bein hashemashot — end quote. And likewise Acharonim note 1, and the same applies that he may immerse it through ha'arama such as drawing water with it to drink or for some need as is written below siman 323 seif 7, see there.
