One who is not proficient, etc. This is an explicit teaching in Shabbos 35b. Accordingly, in our times, when we certainly are not proficient in the measure established by the Sages either, and especially regarding Shabbos, whose observance is entrusted to everyone, the obligation according to the law, according to all opinions, is certainly to kindle before sunset. I later found this stated in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, who warned very strongly about this matter; see there. He further wrote there that if, God forbid, some unavoidable circumstance arises and she cannot hasten to kindle until sunset, she should instruct a non-Jew to kindle the Shabbos lights, and she should recite the blessing over them. The blessing of goodness will come upon her, and she will receive reward for refraining from kindling herself. End quote. See below, siman 263, regarding what the Mishna Berurah writes in se'if katan 21 in the name of the later authorities, whose view is that a woman should not recite the blessing when the non-Jew kindles.
