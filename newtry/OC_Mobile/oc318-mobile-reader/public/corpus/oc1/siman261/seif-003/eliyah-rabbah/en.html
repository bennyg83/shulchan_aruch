(3) Eruv techumin for a discretionary matter, etc. — there is a difficulty below siman 415 that even during daytime one does not make eruv for a discretionary matter; and one may say that here it deals with eruv by placing feet which is permitted during daytime as Beit Yosef wrote there; however Levush there implies there is no distinction, and it is possible his intent is that even b'dieved it is not a valid eruv; and even though he ruled there seif 2 that b'dieved it is a valid eruv — that is for a matter of mitzvah and is forced; and furthermore according to this l'chatchila it is forbidden regarding eruv techumin for a matter of mitzvah during bein hashemashot, which Levush does not imply:
