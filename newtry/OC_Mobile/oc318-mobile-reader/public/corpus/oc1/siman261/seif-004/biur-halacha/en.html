There is no eruv — this is eruvei chatzerot as I explained in Mishna Berurah, and likewise is explicit in Mordechai at the end of the chapter Bameh Madlikin that this law flows from it [and it is astonishing regarding Peri Megadim who wants to say that here the Mechaber deals with eruv techumin]; and see in Mordechai there that Rabbeinu Yoel disagrees on this and permits, and the Mechaber rules anonymously here like those who are stringent in this [and likewise Rama in siman 253 seif 2 in the gloss rules anonymously to be stringent]; and behold in Shulchan Aruch siman 393 seif 2 he brought the dispute on this, and it is implied there that he leans more toward the view of those who permit, who hold that what they permitted during bein hashemashot applies even if he also accepted Shabbat upon himself; and in order that they not contradict one another many of the latter divide [they are Acharonim for one answer and Peri Megadim and Machatzit HaShekel brought in Sha'arei Teshuvah and Shulchan Atzei Shittim] and they say that there it deals with an individual who accepted Shabbat upon himself, and here it deals with acceptance of Shabbat of Barchu which is through the community and is stricter; and see in Taz in siman 401 who rules that it is permitted to make an eruv even after he accepted Shabbat upon himself, and likewise in Biur Halacha he sides to rule that all shevut for the need of a mitzvah is permitted even after the community accepted Shabbat, and even during the half hour before darkness; and the reason is that this time is not stricter than bein hashemashot, and certainly the intent of the community was not to accept upon themselves the tosefet that it should be stricter than bein hashemashot itself, see there; and nevertheless the Tosfeta which the poskim brought proof from it to be stringent is not resolved in his reasoning, see in Mordechai; and see in Biur HaGra in siman 393, and it is implied from his words that he sides more toward the view of those who forbid, and he brought that the view of Rama is also thus in siman 253 seif 2 in the gloss. And nevertheless it appears in my humble opinion that when another safek joins there is room to rule leniently in this matter, such as when it is two hours before darkness, which for many poskim is before plag haminchah and his acceptance has no effect; and even though Mahari"u above in the gloss mentions an hour or two, it is possible he mentioned it only regarding the d'oraisa melacha of kindling where his acceptance applies and it is permitted only through a non-Jew; but regarding shevut in a place of mitzvah one may be lenient even by himself if it is two hours before darkness:
