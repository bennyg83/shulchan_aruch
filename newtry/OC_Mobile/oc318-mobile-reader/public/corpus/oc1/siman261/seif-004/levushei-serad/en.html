(Shulchan Aruch, seif 1, one may tell a non-Jew.) Instructing a non-Jew is a shevus, and it is explained in siman 342 that the Sages did not prohibit a shevus during bein hashmashos for the sake of a mitzvah, or when one is preoccupied and pressed. The reason it is forbidden to tithe and to immerse utensils is that this is not considered a mitzvah, since if he does not eat or use the utensil there is no obligation. Nor is he considered pressed, because he has other foods and utensils. By contrast, where he has none, one can say that it is permitted. This explains the words of the Magen Avraham in s.k. 6.
