(4) There. One does not tithe vadai even rabbinically mandated tithes. Acharonim note 2, and see below siman 307 note 149. However one may separate challah outside Eretz Yisrael during bein hashemashot, for it is d'rabbanan without support from the verse, so that the Torah of challah not be forgotten. Beit Din siman 103. Birkei Yosef note 3. However the author of Or LeTzion note 1 wrote that one should not separate challah during bein hashemashot if he forgot to separate from beforehand, because it is possible to leave a little as we say 'outside Eretz Yisrael eats and walks while leaving some and afterward separates.' And likewise Magen Avraham seif katan 2. Acharonim note 2, Tosafot Shabbos note 2; and if so Shabbat is permitted during bein hashemashot according to Acharonim. And likewise challah of Eretz Yisrael when he has nothing to eat on Shabbat is permitted during bein hashemashot, for shevut for a mitzvah they did not decree during bein hashemashot.
