4) There: One may not tithe produce that is certainly untithed, even when the tithe obligation is rabbinic. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 2. See below, siman 307, paragraph 149. One may, however, separate challah outside Eretz Yisrael during twilight, because this is a rabbinic institution without Scriptural support, enacted so that the law of challah would not be forgotten. Beis David, siman 103; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 3. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1, however, writes that if one forgot to separate challah while it was still daytime, he should not separate it during twilight, because he can leave over a small portion. As we say regarding challah outside Eretz Yisrael, he may continue eating, leave a portion, and separate challah afterward. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2, write the same. Accordingly, when Shabbos occurs on the day preceding Pesach and this option is unavailable, separation is permitted during twilight. Similarly, regarding challah in Eretz Yisrael, if he has nothing else to eat on Shabbos, separation is permitted during twilight, because a rabbinic prohibition for the sake of a mitzvah was not imposed during twilight. Eshel Avraham there. See below, siman 506, seif 3 in the gloss.
