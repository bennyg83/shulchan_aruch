After the answering of Barchu. So he wrote; there is one who disagrees on this, and he holds that Tosafot end of Sukkah who brings in Mordechai from Shibulei HaLeket — that one is permitted to light the candle after the third tekiah — as is proven in the braita chapter 2 of Beitza — and we hold per the ruling of Rabbeinu Yerucham — end of his words. And he did not examine carefully, for Mordechai speaks of the third tekiah in the last three [blasts]; and he further wrote there that Mordechai forbade only for one who accepted Shabbat upon himself, but one who did not accept Shabbat — even after Barchu he is permitted in shevutot — end of his words. And he did not examine Mordechai end of the chapter who brought proof from walking with a shofar which is a shevut — siman 263 seif 13:

One may not establish an eiruv. It implies even for a matter of mitzvah it is forbidden, for the insulation is for the sake of Shabbat and it is forbidden, for it is worse than bein hashemashos; and the same applies that it is forbidden to enter a bathhouse even to sweat (Berachot chapter 4):

Mizmor shir, etc. And in these countries they are not accustomed to recite Mizmor shir, etc.; therefore the answering of Barchu is accepting Shabbat [Darchei Moshe and so too siman 263 seif 13].
