And likewise Taz — drawn water on the ground in immersion is valid for baalei keri; and nine kav specifically by pouring, not immersion on the ground. Measure of nine kav in Terumat HaDeshen 16 and 16 eggs — there he wondered at what Taz wrote from chapter Kelim, and it should read: from three combine, not from four. These nine kav are explained in Rambam chapter 4 of prayer halacha 8. Plain custom in Spain to wash the whole body on account of preparation — it implies nine kav are required on the whole body so no place remains where water did not come, like immersion; and I do not know — now they pour nine kav and almost part of the body was not touched at all by water; and palms and feet need patting with water beforehand. What the Mechaber wrote — the reason that Torah scholars should not be found constantly with their wives, etc. Pri Megadim wondered: in the gemara the reason is explained as fear and trembling and sweat like at the giving of the Torah, and it is called frivolity; and indeed in Rambam laws of prayer chapter 4 halacha 4 it is explained as the Mechaber wrote (see Nitzotz, who wrote chapter 4 of Keriat Shema, and there is an error in the edition — it should read: in prayer).
