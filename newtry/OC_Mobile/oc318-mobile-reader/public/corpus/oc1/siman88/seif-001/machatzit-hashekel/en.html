(Seif kaf alef) And afterwards, a healthy person specifically, etc.—for they were not stringent now more than at the beginning of Ezra's enactment; and as stated in the Gemara, in such a case he was exempt.

And nevertheless forty se'ah of drawn water are valid—specifically when they are on the ground, but not in a vessel. If he saw split waters, etc.—Ezra 33a—even though below siman 607 it is written that even one who immersed on erev Rosh Hashana should immerse on erev Yom Kippur even if he did not see keri, lest he saw split waters; one must say that regarding erev Yom Kippur they were more stringent. And know that what Taz wrote—that nine kavim may be poured even from four vessels—is an error; and one must say from three vessels they combine, not from four; and likewise in mishnah chapter 3 of Mikvaot, and likewise Rambam and Rav in Beit Yosef here. And the measure of nine kavim—see in Ateret Zekenim.
