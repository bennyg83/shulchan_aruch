Seif katan 1, and afterward, etc., specifically one who is healthy, etc. We should not now be more stringent than Ezra's original enactment. The Gemara states that in a case of this kind he was exempt.
And forty se'ah of drawn water are valid. This is specifically when the water is collected in the ground, but not when it is in a vessel. Regarding one who saw separated drops, etc., Ezra did not decree in this case. Although below, in siman 607, it is written that even one who immersed on Erev Rosh Hashanah should immerse again on Erev Yom Kippur despite not having experienced an emission, lest he have seen separated drops, one must say that a greater stringency was adopted for Erev Yom Kippur. Know that the Taz's statement that nine kav may be poured from as many as four vessels is a scribal error. It should read “from three vessels, but not from four.” This is also stated in the Mishnah at the end of chapter 3 of Mikvaos, and so rule the Rambam and Beit Yosef here. For the measure of nine kav, see Ateres Zekeinim.
