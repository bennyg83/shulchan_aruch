In the gloss to seif 1 See Ramban to Parashas Vayeitzei on the verse, “For the way of women is upon me.” He writes that her gaze, her speech, and the dust beneath her feet are dangerous, and that the nations of the world would distance her. See there. Accordingly, by strict law she should not go to the synagogue or pray, because of the principle, “Present it now to your governor,” as discussed below in siman 272 and in Magen Avraham there, seif katan 10. This is readily understood.
