And such is the practice that spread—see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote, that those who practice immersion—even so, one who is sick against his will is exempt from everything. It somewhat appears that "sick" for this matter means one who is weak; and proof for this from Berachos 22 regarding Torah scholars—see in Rashi there, s.v. efshar. And know further that what Sha'agas Aryeh was uncertain about regarding chatzitzah in immersion of a baal keri—I found in Sefer HaEshkol that we have now merited to have it illuminated, that he wrote explicitly that chatzitzah does not invalidate this. But if there is chatzitzah on most of his body, it is implied there that it invalidates in this case as well.
