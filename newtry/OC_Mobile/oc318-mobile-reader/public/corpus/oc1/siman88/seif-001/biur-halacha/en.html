And this has become the widespread custom - See Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote that even among those who observe immersion, an ill person who experienced an involuntary emission is entirely exempt. It appears somewhat that, for this purpose, an “ill person” means one who is weak. A proof may be brought from Berachos 22a concerning a Torah scholar; see Rashi there, s.v. “Is it possible.” Know also that Shaarei Teshuvah expresses uncertainty regarding an intervening substance during the immersion of a baal keri. I found in Sefer HaEshkol, which has now become available to us, that it states explicitly that an intervening substance does not invalidate this immersion. However, it appears there that if the substance covers most of his body, it does invalidate even this immersion.
