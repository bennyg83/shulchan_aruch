And such is the practice that spread—that is, in a place where there is no filth at all. But in a place of feces or soiled matter it is forbidden to recite either Keriat Shema or other words of Torah. Nevertheless, one who wishes to conduct himself according to Ezra's enactment must immerse in forty se'ah, for even drawn waters are all valid for it, or use nine kavim by pouring upon him specifically, but not by immersing in them. And these nine kavim combine even from three vessels, only that he must pour them continuously one after another—that he pour from the second before the first has finished—and from four vessels in any case they do not combine (Beit Yosef). And one may immerse half [his body] and wash half, even though Ezra's enactment no longer exists today; it emerges from this for one who accepts upon himself to conduct himself according to Ezra's enactment (Beit Yosef). The measure of nine kavim is approximately nine measures and a half in the view of the Rishonim; thus it appears to me.
