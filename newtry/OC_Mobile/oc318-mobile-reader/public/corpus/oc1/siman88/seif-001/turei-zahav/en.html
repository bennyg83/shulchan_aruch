And such is the practice that spread. One who wishes to be stringent and pour upon himself nine kavim—it is explained in Beit Yosef that the law of nine kavim applies even in drawn water and even from three or four vessels, in a manner that one does not interrupt pouring from one vessel until the second begins; but from four and above they do not combine at all. And those nine kavim must be poured upon him, but not to immerse in them, whether in a vessel or on the ground. And likewise one should conduct himself among those who purify on erev Yom Kippur, and similarly by nine kavim.

To go to the synagogue, etc.—Rashal wrote in explanation of Semag that one should be careful not to gaze at the Torah scroll when the chazan shows it to the people.
