One who wishes to be stringent and have nine kav poured over him should know that Beit Yosef explains that nine kav may consist even of drawn water, and may be poured from three or four vessels, provided that one does not pause after pouring from one vessel before beginning the next. Four vessels or more, however, do not combine at all. These nine kav must be poured over him; he may not immerse in them, whether they are in a vessel or collected in the ground. Those who customarily purify themselves on Erev Rosh Hashanah and Erev Yom Kippur by means of nine kav should follow this procedure.
