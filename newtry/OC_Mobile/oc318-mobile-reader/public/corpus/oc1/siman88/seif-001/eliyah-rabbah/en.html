[1] All the impure, etc. Even a zav — and they relied on the verse: are not My words like fire? Just as fire does not receive impurity, so words of Torah (Levush). Nachalat Tzvi challenged: in the gemara they did not learn from the verse except for a baal keri; but other impure persons they learned from: and you shall make them known to your children — so far. And in my humble opinion there is no difficulty: for the verse and you shall make known — we require for the other [view] of R' Yehoshua ben Levi, who holds one who teaches with his grandson is as though he received Torah from Mount Sinai, as in the end of chapter Mi sheMeto [Berachot 21b]; and likewise in Beit Yosef, and likewise explicit in chapter 5 of Hilchot Keriat Shema — it is explained that all impure persons we learn from the verse are not My words like fire; and so I found in Ra'avan siman 105.
