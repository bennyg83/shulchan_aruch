All the impure, etc. Even a zav—and they relied on the verse "Are not My words like fire," etc.: just as fire does not receive impurity, so words of Torah do not receive impurity (Levush). And Nachalat Tzvi challenged: in the Gemara they did not learn from the verse except for a baal keri, but other impure persons they learned from "and you shall make known to your children." Until here. And in my humble opinion there is no difficulty at all—for the verse and "make known" we need for the other [opinion] of R' Yehoshua ben Levi, who holds that one who teaches his son's son is as if he received the Torah from Sinai, as stated at the end of the chapter "Mi shemeisu" (Berachos 21b); and likewise in Beit Yosef, and likewise explained in chapter 5 of the laws of Keriat Shema—that we learn all impure persons from the verse "Are not My words like fire"; and thus I found in Ra'avan siman 165. And with this all the wonderings and objections that Ma'adanei Melech raised on the Levush are settled—see there, for I have abbreviated.
