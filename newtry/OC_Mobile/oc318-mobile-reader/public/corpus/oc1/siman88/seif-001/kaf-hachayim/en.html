(1) [Seif 1] All the impure read in the Torah, etc.—and even impurity that exits from their bodies, such as zavim and zavot, niddot and yoledot; and they relied on the verse "Are not My words like fire," etc.: just as fire does not receive impurity, so words of Torah do not receive impurity (Beit Yosef; Levush; Alef Ra section 1; Ra'avad section 1).
