The practice—Ba'er Heitev: that drawn waters are valid; and see in Levushei Serad on Berachos that even hot water is valid for this immersion, since we say "perhaps in bathhouses"—see there. And see in responsa Ma'amar Mordechai siman 1 and siman 2, who wrote that chatzitzah does not invalidate this immersion of baalei keri—for nine kavim help even though his feet are on the ground and the water does not precede; and proof from the end of Yoma from rubbing of keri, which Rashi explained because of chatzitzah—one can say that was stated regarding the enactment of immersion; see there. And likewise the custom in men's immersion they are not particular; and it requires study.
