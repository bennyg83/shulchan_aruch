Siman 61, "they read," etc.—Berachos 22a.

And "they read," etc.—from what is stated in the mishnah: a zav who saw, etc.—this is not so, it is permitted; and likewise after immersing for keri.

Except, etc.—there and there, and Avodah Zarah.

In order, etc.—there.

And in Keriat Shema, etc., and without, etc.—not like what Rif wrote in the name of Rav Amram Gaon, that it applies only to words of Torah, but for Keriat Shema it is required; and he wrote in the name of Rav Hai Gaon: nine kavim—except like the first version there. And proof from what is stated in the Yerushalmi at the end of Yoma: now washing is forbidden in marital relations—all the more so, in my humble opinion, it is forbidden to pray. And they answer: in a place where they do not immerse—apparently even washing is not required there.

And some say she is permitted in all, etc.—as stated in the mishnah there and Niddah, etc., as above. And even the first version wrote only as a general stringency; see siman 482 seif 9 and Magen Avraham.

On days, etc., because, etc.—as stated in chapter 2 of Chagigah, in order to give comfort to women, etc.; and although it is close to service in kodashim, all the more so here.
