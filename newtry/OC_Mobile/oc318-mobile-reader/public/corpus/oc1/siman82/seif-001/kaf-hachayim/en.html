(1) [Section 1] Dry excrement so dry that if one throws it, it crumbles apart, etc.—specifically into fine crumbs; but if it does not make fine crumbs but only breaks in half, it is forbidden to recite Shema opposite it. Kesef Mishnah, chapter 3 of Kriyat Shema, law 7, in the name of R' Menoach. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. Eliyah Rabbah, section 1; Peri Chadash, section 1; Eliyah Rabbah, section 1. Shaarei Teshuvah in Mikraei Kodesh, section 1; Chayei Adam, section 1; and see below, section 3. Kesef Mishnah wrote in Or Sameach in the name of the Rambam that what is permitted when it crumbles apart is specifically when it has no bad smell—for otherwise, even though it is mere dust, if it has a bad smell with substance to it, thus far; and Kerem Shlomo brought this.
