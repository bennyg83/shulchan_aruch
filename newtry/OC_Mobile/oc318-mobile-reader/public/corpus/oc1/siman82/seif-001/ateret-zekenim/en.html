Dry excrement that if one throws it, it crumbles apart, etc.; but if it does not crumble by throwing, even though it crumbles by rolling, it is forbidden. Therefore one must be stringent that it must be so dry that it crumbles by rolling and by throwing as stated. And specifically if it crumbled into fine crumbs; but if it did not crumble into fine crumbs but only broke of itself, it is forbidden to recite Shema opposite it (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Eliyahu Mizrachi).
