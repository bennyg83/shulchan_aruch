(In Magen Avraham s.k. 2) And nevertheless it requires study regarding excrement like earthenware, etc. — comparable to old Baer Heitev, etc. — it appears the text must be emended; and further "comparable to Baer Heitev" — this is a different proof, and nevertheless it requires study on the proof from old Baer Heitev, for we see that if he threw it and it crumbled there is no excrement in it; and if so, if we say frozen is comparable, there is no proof from old Baer Heitev. And in my humble opinion his intent is regarding what I wrote: even though it can return to its former state, nevertheless we follow the present state; and on this he wrote that study is needed — excrement like earthenware without bad odor is forbidden as long as it does not crumble; if so, we do not follow the present state, for now it is like earthenware and has no odor, but we follow the origin and say the name of excrement was not nullified from it and we judge it by its end. And on this he wrote Magen David old Baer Heitev that it is forbidden: even though there is no excrement in it, since initially it was forbidden by Torah we do not say in it "follow the present" but follow the origin — likewise here one should judge it by its end and not by the present; and it is not comparable to throwing and crumbling, for there it is mere dust and the name dust applies and it is not relevant to follow the origin; and likewise by its end or its judgment — for it will never return to its former state. Unlike when frozen, one cannot say it is dust since when it returns to its former state — therefore the name of excrement was not nullified from it:
