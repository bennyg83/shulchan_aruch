דין צואה יבישה. ובו ב סעיפים:
The laws of dry excrement, containing 2 sections: Excrement that is so dry that if one throws it, it crumbles apart—behold it is like dust and it is permitted to recite Shema opposite it; and this is if it does not have a bad smell.There are those who say it is not like dust unless it crumbles by rolling without throwing, and such is the main ruling (Rashi and the Rosh on the chapter "Mi sheMeito" and Tur).
