"Moist enough"—Eliyah Rabbah: this is the Tur's opinion, and one may rely on this since urine is rabbinically forbidden in any case. See siman 78, for there too they permitted only to remove one's garments; but over ground one requires four amos distance—and see Prisha. The Levush omitted his rabbi's gloss on this; see Netziv and Eliyah Rabbah that the halacha follows "there are those who say," those whom Mishna Berurah brought.
