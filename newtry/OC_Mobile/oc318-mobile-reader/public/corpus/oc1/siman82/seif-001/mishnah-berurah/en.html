(1) "It crumbles apart"—and specifically into fine crumbs, but into two or three pieces does not help. And in cold days when the excrement has hardened greatly like a stone, and also no odor reaches from it then—Magen Avraham was uncertain whether it is permitted to recite Shema opposite it, whether we follow the present state, or whether excrement still remains on it since it can return to its former state in warm weather; and the later authorities all agreed on prohibition in this. And even after the fact, if he recited, he must return and recite Kriyat Shema and pray as a voluntary prayer. And if the excrement is under frozen water, it is possible that it is considered covered, even though water alone does not help [see in siman 76]; nevertheless frost and ice—it is reasonable that they are included and considered like covering and like a clod of earth, and we follow the present state. And excrement covered in snow is considered covered so long as the snow has not melted. Shulchan Shlomo wrote that the same applies opposite frozen urine—one should also be stringent not to recite opposite them; requires study in this.
