1) [Se'if 1.] Excrement that is so dry that if it were thrown it would crumble, etc. It must specifically crumble. If it does not crumble but merely breaks in half, it is forbidden to recite opposite it. Kesef Mishneh, chapter 3 of the laws of Keriat Shema, halakhah 7, in the name of Rabbi Manoach; Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1; Ateret Zekenim, ot 1; Pri Chadash, ot 1; Eliyahu Rabbah, ot 1; Siddur Beit Oved, in Mekor Chayim, ot 1; and Chesed LaAlafim, ot 1. See below, ot 3. Kesef Mishneh further writes there in the name of Rabbi Manoach that the permission when it crumbles applies specifically when it has no foul odor. Otherwise, although it is mere dust, it is a foul odor that has a tangible source. These are his words, and Kerem Shlomo cites them.
