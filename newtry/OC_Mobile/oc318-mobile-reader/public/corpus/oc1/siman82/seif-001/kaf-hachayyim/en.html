(a) [Seif 1] Dry excrement so much that if one throws it it will crumble, etc. — specifically crumbling; but if it will not make crumbs but only break in half, it is forbidden to recite opposite it. Kesef Mishneh chapter 3 of Keriat Shema halachah 7 in the name of R' Menachem. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Taz ot 1. Peri Chadash ot 1. Ateret Tzvi ot 1. Shaarei Teshuvah in Maamar Mordechai ot 1. Chasam Sofer ot 1. And see below ot 3; and Eliyah Rabbah wrote: Kesef Mishneh in the name of Rambam: that which is permitted when it crumbles is specifically when it has no bad odor — for otherwise, even though it is mere dust, bad odor that has substance forbids — end quote.
