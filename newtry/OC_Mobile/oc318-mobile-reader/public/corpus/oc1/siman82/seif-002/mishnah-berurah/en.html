(2) In the ground—or in clothing.

(3) "To moisten another"—Explanation: if one places his hand on them, it raises moisture in it such that if he places it on another object, that object receives moisture from it; and if they are not so moist, it is permitted.

(4) "And one may rely on this"—and so agreed all the later authorities, that specifically it must be moist enough to moisten another, and then it requires four amos distance from it, like excrement. Nevertheless, since they are not visible but only absorbed in the ground or in clothing, one need not pour a revi'it on them according to all opinions; rather it suffices to bring on them only a very little water that also is only moist enough to moisten another, and it is permitted. See above in siman 77 in Mishna Berurah.
