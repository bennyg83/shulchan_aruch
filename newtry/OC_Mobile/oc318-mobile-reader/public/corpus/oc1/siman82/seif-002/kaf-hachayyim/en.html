2) In cold weather, when excrement has frozen hard like stone, it may be said that it still retains the status of excrement because it can return to its original state. Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2; Eliyahu Rabbah, ot 2; Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, ot 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 1; Chayei Adam, klal 3, ot 9; Chesed LaAlafim there; and Petach HaDevir, ot 1. See there, where Petach HaDevir rejects the words of Mor U'Ketziah, who permitted it, and concludes that his words provide no adequate basis for leniency in a Torah prohibition; see there. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 5, ot 7. Pe'at HaShulchan writes that the same law applies to frozen urine. However, excrement covered by snow is considered covered, and it is permitted to recite opposite it. Chesed LaAlafim there; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch there. We wrote likewise above in siman 76, ot 30, in the name of several halakhic authorities; see there.
