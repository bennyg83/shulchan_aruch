(2) In cold weather when the excrement has hardened greatly like a stone—it is reasonable that excrement still remains on it since it can return to its former state: Magen Avraham, subsection 2; Eliyah Rabbah, section 2; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, section 2; Raziel, section 1; Chayei Adam, general rule 3, section 9, Chayei Adam there. Pithei Teshuvah, section 1—and see there what he rejected from Maamar Mordechai, who wrote to permit, and he concluded in a manner that his words are not fit to be lenient in a Torah prohibition—see there. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 5, section 7; and Pe'at HaShulchan wrote that the same applies to frozen urine. However, excrement covered in snow is considered covered, and it is permitted to recite Shema opposite it—Chayei Adam there; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch there; and so we wrote above, siman 76, section 30, in the name of several poskim—see there.
