Siman 82, urine, etc.—for that which is stated "like the tannaim" does not refer to Rava's words; for if so, what is he saying "everyone, etc."? Nevertheless it is like tannaim, and all the more so per the Rambam's explanation that Rava is like R' Yehuda, and initially also like R' Yehuda—only in the dispute of visible inscription and moistening; and Rava's matter is like R' Yehuda and in his wording.

"And there are those who say, etc."—as above, Rava did not decide except regarding visible inscription; and in a dispute of tannaim the halacha follows the first tanna, and all the more so in a rabbinic matter, as stated above in siman 76, and Av Beit Din and Bach.
