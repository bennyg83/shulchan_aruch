Seif 2, urine, etc. The Gemara's statement that the matter is subject to a dispute among Tannaim does not refer to Rava's ruling. Were it so, what could the Gemara mean by saying, 'According to everyone, etc.,' since it would nevertheless remain subject to a Tannaitic dispute? This is certainly so according to the Rambam's explanation, under which Rava follows Rabbi Yehudah, and the initial position also follows Rabbi Yehudah. Rather, the Tannaitic dispute concerns whether a visible trace remains and whether it is moist enough to wet, while Rava's ruling follows Rabbi Yehudah and accords with his formulation.
And some say, etc. As explained above, Rava ruled only concerning a visible trace. Regarding the dispute among the Tannaim, the halachah follows the first Tanna, especially since this is a Rabbinic matter, as I wrote above in siman 76. See Beit Yosef and Bach.
