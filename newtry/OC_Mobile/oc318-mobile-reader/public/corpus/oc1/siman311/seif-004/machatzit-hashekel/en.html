(א) (s.k. 4) From sun, etc. — one can say a remedy as is written in seif 6; and for this Rama intended — meaning in what is written "and see in this siman seif 6" — meaning the Mechaber according to his view that carrying out was not permitted on account of disgrace of the dead (only in a ship where there is great disgrace); therefore it was not permitted in seif 2 except regarding a dead person who already putrefied and on account of dignity of the living; and therefore he did not speak here regarding a dead person lying in the sun that it was not permitted except in that same domain — carrying out was not permitted on account of disgrace of the dead; but Rama who ruled in seif 2 like the Tur — that even if he has not yet putrefied but is close to putrefying — permitted to take him out — thus on account of disgrace of the dead; nevertheless in the law here when lying in the sun Rama agrees carrying out is forbidden because he has a remedy to save him from disgrace without carrying out:

(ב) That there are two domains, etc. — and accordingly in fire Rama disagrees with the Beit Yosef and holds that even on account of disgrace of burning carrying out was permitted, for Rama permits carrying out on account of dignity of the dead — for he already proved that disgrace of fire and disgrace of a dead person lying in the sun are equal:
