And the Beit Yosef wrote: and one can reject, for the garment is batel relative to the dead person; and so in Shlah he challenged on this that David said "I will trample a step from my grave" — and he died; and presumably he was dressed in his garments; and nevertheless it would have been forbidden to move him unless via loaf or child, as is in the gemara — see there; and I heard that David's garments were muktzeh, for it is forbidden for any person to wear them, for they burn them upon kings; but other garments of a dead person are not muktzeh; and what Beit Yosef wrote — it is in Chullin daf 125: a dead person in his garment — meaning shrouds — batels it; this does not batel it — end of his words; if so they follow his intent; and so garments of the dead are not batel except when he intends to strip him and dress him in shrouds:
