But not by means of a loaf. The reason in the Tur is that it is not secondary to the corpse; and it appears the explanation of this matter is that specifically an infant who is a type of person like the corpse — in this one can say they are secondary to each other, therefore one moves this by means of the other; unlike a loaf that has no connection with the corpse and one cannot say this by means of that; and this is the view of Ramban that the Tur and Beit Yosef bring in the name of Tur HaAroch — except that he concluded thus — except that Ra'ah forbids, and also the Shulchan Aruch should have concluded thus — end of his words:
