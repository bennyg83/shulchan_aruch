(א) That it is close [to rotting]. And permitted to sprinkle vinegar on the garments of the corpse on Shabbat so it will not smell foul — Darkei Moshe; and they may occupy themselves with him:

(ב) There is not. per the Tur, if it is close to smelling foul, even if they have a place to go out — permitted to take him out so he will not smell foul:

(ג) On. Specifically when it is somewhat large as stated siman 308 seif 41; and Taz concluded that Ra'ah forbids; also the Shulchan Aruch should have concluded thus — see there:

(ד) To a gentile. Specifically to karmelit; but to reshut harabbim forbidden by means of a gentile — Magen Avraham. And Ateret Zekenim wrote an incident where a house fell on Shabbat on souls of Jews and one infant died under the house, and he permitted there a great sage to split heads with gentiles to clear until they find the infant, for since he slaughters with awareness of his soul he is acting [for the purpose of life]:

(ה) For need. And moving from the side is permitted for the need of priests; nevertheless the priests cannot compel the relatives to take him out even if they need to enter the synagogue, for it is a mitzvah, unless in a collapse — Bach. And nevertheless it appears to me that if there is a sick priest who cannot leave his house they compel the relatives to take the corpse from his house so he will not transgress a d'oraisa prohibition — Magen Avraham; and see what is written Yoreh De'ah siman 372 seif 1:
