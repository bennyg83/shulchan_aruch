(ב) And some say. Rashi there. And he holds that they were stricter regarding the prohibition of moving than regarding carrying out, and as Tosafos stated Sukkah 36b, and Ran answered where one can repair he repairs, not so regarding the prohibition of carrying out:

(ג) And if it was. According to his view — only because of kavod hachayim. But without this — no, for not like Rashi there, and Mordechai already challenged him there — for we say in the chapter "The Stove" that a corpse in the sun specifically via loaf or child, and even from below to below — not per the one who says moving from the side etc., rather Rashi holds that moving is stricter than carrying out, and so too Tosafos, except that Rashi contradicts himself in chapter 3 of Sukkah, and as stated siman 312 in Hagahah, and Tosafos already challenged him, and so too Magen Avraham:

(ד) And some say not etc. As stated 142b and see Rashi there s.v. "he placed" etc. and "they moved it" etc., but Ramban disagreed with him and wrote per the second explanation in Rashi — via a partition of a gentile, and this is the meaning of "there is one who says." He holds that whatever improves in moving harms in carrying out the loaf and child not for a need, and he wrote — for the loaf and child are not subordinate below; and further, carrying out a loaf is closer to coming to a Torah prohibition than carrying out the corpse, for even taking it out for burial is melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah as stated, and further because of marit ha'ayin that they not say he desecrates Shabbos for it, and as stated regarding burial via gentiles on Shabbos; but in a karmelis everyone knows there is no melacha prohibition, and see there in Torat HaAdam; and the First [commentator] holds it is better to multiply one prohibition than to do two prohibitions, and all the more so per the above words of Tosafos that the prohibition of moving is stricter than carrying out, as stated:

(ה) And there is one who permits etc. For from what is stated regarding karmelis — the incident that was, was so; but in truth it is permitted even for reshut harabbim; and from what is stated "who am I speaking to regarding R' Yehuda" etc. — that is because he said there "even for R' Yosi," but for R' Shimon even reshut harabbim is permitted for R' Yehuda, for it is melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah; and he wrote there that Rabbenu Chananel forbids — for they only permitted a thing whose essence is from words of the Scribes, etc., and forbidden because of marit ha'ayin; know that not everyone permitted, for they said in chapter 3 "if you do not permit him" etc., and if so they should permit the extinguishing itself; and in the first chapter of Rosh Hashanah we say regarding Shabbos and Yom Kippur that it is impossible with gentiles, and they did not permit with gentiles except on Yom Tov; see in Torat HaAdam chapter 46 where he elaborated on this:
