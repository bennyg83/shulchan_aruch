Indirect moving for the need of something permitted is permitted; therefore a radish that one buried in the ground and part of it is exposed and it has not rooted, and he also did not intend it for planting — he takes it even though in taking it he moves earth from its place, and even though it increased due to the moisture of the ground — permitted.And even if he placed it there intentionally from before Shabbat to be there all Shabbat, for with food there is no concern of basis for something forbidden (Kol Bo); and moving in the object itself, even for the need of something forbidden, is permitted; therefore straw on a bed that is sealed for kindling — muktzeh — one shakes it in the object itself; and if he placed on it before daytime a mattress or pillow, one shakes it even with the hand, for behold he prepared it before daytime to lie upon it (and likewise if he thought to lie upon it) (Ran end of chapter Bameh Tomnin).) וטלטול בגופו אפי' לצורך דבר האסור מותר הילכך קש שעל המטה דסתמו מוקצה להסקה מנענעו בגופו ואם הניח עליו מבעוד יום כר או כסת מנענעו אפי' בידו שהרי הכינו מבעוד יום לשכוב עליו (וכן אם חשב לשכוב עליו) (ר"ן ס"פ במה טומנין.
