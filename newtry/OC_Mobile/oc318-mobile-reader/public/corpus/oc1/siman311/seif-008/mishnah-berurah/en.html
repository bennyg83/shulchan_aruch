(א) And part uncovered above — for if the leaves were not uncovered at all, behold he necessarily moves dirt with his hands and it is forbidden; and even if he wishes to stick a needle or peg through the dirt in the manner of a radish in the ground and take it — likewise forbidden, as it appears he is making a hole [Peri Megadim]; and some are lenient via sticking a needle and peg:

(ב) And they had not sprouted — for if they sprouted, even if the body of the radish too was uncovered from above — forbidden to move them from their place on account of uprooting:

(ג) And also not, etc. — for if he intended for sowing, even though they had not sprouted — forbidden, since from the beginning he wanted them to take root and they are buried in the ground in the manner of sowing; and see in Magen Avraham who challenged on this from that which we rule in the gemara [brought above in siman 310 seif 2] that wheat one sowed in the ground and they have not yet sprouted — permitted to gather and eat; but nevertheless one should not depart from the Mechaber's ruling, for so is found in several Rishonim [Rokeach and Ramban in his novellae and Me'iri]; and that matter above the Me'iri explained — it deals when the seeds were not yet covered in dirt:

(ד) Even though in taking it, etc. — meaning nevertheless it is only indirect moving; and his intent is for taking the radish which is a permitted object; the Acharonim wrote that the permission to take the radish from the ground deals when buried in a field; but in a house forbidden lest he come afterward to level the holes in the ground of the house made thereby [but if they are buried in sand and dirt placed in a corner and he brought them in for his needs — permitted to take them out even if not uncovered at all from above, as explained above in siman 308 s.k. 38; and on this the gloss hinted in seif 9]; and similarly in the gemara regarding weaver's pillars stuck in the ground of the house — forbidden to move them from their place lest he come to level the holes afterward:

(ה) And even if he placed it, etc. — meaning even though generally if one placed a muktzeh object on some utensil with intent that it remain there all Shabbat — according to all the utensil itself too becomes muktzeh from the reason of base for the forbidden object, as above in siman 309 seif 4; nevertheless here the radish did not become a base for the dirt upon it, because with food items, etc. — meaning in these food items the law of base does not apply, for his intent in burying was not that the food serve the dirt but on the contrary that the dirt serve the food; and the same regarding fruits buried in straw below — even if his intent was that they remain there all Shabbat, the fruits did not become a base for the straw upon them for the above reason:

(ו) And moving with his body — meaning he does not touch it with his hand but only with his body or other limbs — we are more lenient than other indirect moving; and therefore permitted even for the need of a forbidden object:

(ז) For they designated it, etc. — meaning in places where they designate straw for fuel; but in our places the default is for animal food or for lying, and permitted to shake it even by hand; and know that Shulchan Aruch deals when the straw was not placed on the bed for lying — for if placed intentionally for lying, the name muktzeh is removed from it everywhere:

(ח) He shakes it with his body — so that it be loose and soft to lie upon:

(ט) For behold he prepared it, etc. — meaning even if he did not think explicitly to lie upon it, we consider through this act as if he thought; and if he thought explicitly — it helps even without straw and mattress, as Rama wrote:
