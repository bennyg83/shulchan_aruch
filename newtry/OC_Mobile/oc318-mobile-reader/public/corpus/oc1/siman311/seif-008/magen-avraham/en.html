(א) And part of it is uncovered above. But if they are not uncovered — forbidden to stick pegs in them like in seif 9, for it appears as making holes [Peri Megadim]; but on daf 50 in Tosafos it is implied according to one version that here too permitted to stick; and so in Yerushalmi in the chapter of Kilayim — permitted to stick with dust; and requires further study on Maggid Mishneh who ruled not like Yerushalmi — see there in Rashba; and Rambam wrote chapter 26 — weaver's combs forbidden to move lest he fix their holes; and in the gemara daf 113 it challenges: holes come of themselves like in a radish; and it answers: in a field one does not come to level holes — if so in a house forbidden to take the radish from the ground, for he will come to level holes:

(ב) He did not intend for sowing. Requires further study, for in siman 310 seif 2 it is written that wheat that one sowed in the ground is permitted; and in truth Tosafos daf 50 deals only with tithes and shevi'it and kilayim — that if he intended for sowing he is liable in all of them; but regarding Shabbat even if he intended for sowing it is permitted as long as they have not sprouted, as is clear — see there:

(ג) For with food items there is no [concern]. Meaning his intent was not that the food serve the dirt; on the contrary the dirt serves the food; and as Magen Avraham wrote siman 259; and in truth there is no difference between food items and utensils, as is in Kelim and as is in Eruvin daf 77 — see there; and see siman 371 seif 10 and siman 253 s.s.a. what is written:

(ד) Therefore straw. Requires further study — for according to what is written in seif 5, when the essence of his moving is not for the sake of the muktzeh object it is permitted; if so here too permitted, as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Ran; and one must say: since he needs the straw to lie upon it — if so it is like doing it for the need of the muktzeh; and in truth in the Rosh it is implied that if his intent is to move the muktzeh object, even if he needs its place — forbidden; and it is not permitted except when he has no intent at all to move the muktzeh, as with a radish:
