(א) (s.k. 8) Only via, etc. — carrying out that cannot be remedied, etc. — so too the Ran — meaning even for Rambam who holds moving is lighter than carrying out — nevertheless l'chatchila he requires a loaf, since it is possible to move permissibly, why permit muktzeh; even though if they took him out via loaf and child he will increase in carrying out — this reasoning he does not hold; rather since they had to permit carrying out they did not distinguish between much and little:

(ב) If he has no, etc. — permitted for Rambam — meaning for Rambam who holds moving is lighter than carrying out; and since he has no loaf, etc., the prohibition of moving that cannot be remedied — like carrying out was permitted, moving too was permitted; but for the Tur who is the view of one authority that Rama brought, who permits even close to putrefying — one must say moving is stricter than muktzeh, as Tosafos wrote in Sukkah and as stated above in s.k. 5 — see there; therefore if he has no loaf, etc. — forbidden to take him out, for although they permitted the prohibition of carrying out, the prohibition of moving which is stricter according to him they did not permit; and so from the Tur's language — see there:
