(8) There. And all this in that domain. It implies that to another domain forbidden to take him out; however the later authorities wondered on Shulchan Aruch's words here, for even due to disgrace permitted to take him out to another domain as stated seif 2, and if so all the more due to fire; and Levush wrote it is a textual error in Shulchan Aruch's words. Or it means "and all this in that domain" — what is stated initially that he should seek the permitted means to lighten the prohibition by loaf or infant, etc., is in that domain, meaning there is not so much fear of fire; but if the fire is dangerous they should not seek at first to lighten the prohibition lest the fire strengthen and burn — rather they should take him out directly on the ways explained in seif 2 — see there. And so Bach and Taz seif kaf 1 to permit in fire to take out from domain to domain as in smelled foul in seif 2 — see there; and so Eliyah Rabbah section 13 in the name of several poskim, and he agreed with them for practical law; and so Tosafos Shabbos; and so the view of the later authorities. And what Magen Avraham seif 3 wrote there is no disgrace when the corpse is already burned — the later authorities already challenged him; and also what Machatzit HaShekel wrote to resolve Magen Avraham's words according to what Mordechai wrote in the gloss Yoreh De'ah siman 363 that permitted to place lime on him, etc. — Pischei Teshuvah section 1 already challenged him, for there is different specifically to consume the flesh, but here in fire everything will burn and there is no greater disgrace than this — see there; and especially according to Zohar and Ari z"l that remains from the aspect of the soul within the body in the secret of decomposition of bones so it can endure until the time of revival, as written in Orach Chayyim gate 18 gate 288 chapter 1 — see there; and if it burns Hashem forbid there is no greater anguish than this; and see Idra in Sefer HaZohar parashat Vayechi; and examine:
