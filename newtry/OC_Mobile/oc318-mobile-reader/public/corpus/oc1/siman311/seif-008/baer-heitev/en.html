(א) For planting. And Magen Avraham permits even if he intended for planting if it did not sprout; and similarly Yad Aharon:

(ב) Moving. For in a field it does not come to make furrows; but in his house forbidden to take the turnip from the ground, for it comes to make furrows:

(ג) For not. Meaning his intention was not that the food serve the earth; on the contrary the earth serves the food. And there is no distinction between foods and utensils — Magen Avraham. And Taz explained the intention is as long as he wishes to eat from them and what remains he will eat another time; but one who resolves clearly he will not eat from them on this Shabbat and buries them for that intention — forbidden even in moving from the side — see there:

(ד) And moving with his body. Meaning and he should not touch with his hand:

(ה) Muktzeh. And in a place where they do not kindle with straw permitted to shake with the hand — Taz:
