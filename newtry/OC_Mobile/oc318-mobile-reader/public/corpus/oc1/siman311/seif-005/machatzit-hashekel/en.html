(א) (s.k. 5) Close, etc. — and specifically via loaf, etc. — meaning even though the Beit Yosef brought here two views and there is an opinion that whoever takes him out, etc., better to take him out without loaf, etc.; but Rama who rules like the Tur here — that even close to putrefying is permitted — did not permit in another matter more than the Tur permitted, namely via loaf and child, as explained in the Tur:

(ב) And as is written in seif 1 — Tosafos Sukkah — meaning Tosafos brought proof from the law of the gemara brought in seif 1; but in Shulchan Aruch seif 1 it is written the opposite of Tosafos' words — that carrying out is stricter than the prohibition of moving, for there regarding fire they permitted moving and not carrying out, as stated — there in Sukkah they said permitted to bring stones into the bathroom to wipe with them, for great is human dignity that overrides, etc.; and Rashi explained: specifically in a bathroom that has no partitions, for there is only moving of muktzeh stones — this prohibition they permitted on account of human dignity; but surrounded by partitions where there is prohibition of bringing from karmelit to reshus hayachid — they did not permit on account of human dignity; and Tosafos disagree: one, that the language "to bring in" implies surrounded by partitions; and further that moving muktzeh is stricter than the prohibition of karmelit, for regarding a dead person lying in the sun they did not permit to move him except via loaf and child; behold on account of disgrace of the dead they did not permit moving muktzeh, and the prohibition of karmelit was permitted on account of disgrace of the dead in the incident in Drokart; and since on account of human dignity, namely the wiping, they permitted moving muktzeh stones — all the more so they permitted the prohibition of karmelit — end for now; and so the Tur holds, as Tosafos proved that moving is stricter than karmelit; therefore even though carrying out was permitted — nevertheless moving, which is stricter, was not permitted except via loaf and child; and what the Beit Yosef wrote "and there is an opinion that whoever takes out to karmelit, better," etc. — that is because the Beit Yosef follows his view, and one can say he holds like this opinion (namely Ramban) that a dead person close to putrefying — carrying out was not permitted except when he already putrefied on account of dignity of the living, like Rambam's view; and according to him Tosafos' proof can be explained: nevertheless carrying out is stricter than moving; and that which was permitted in the Drokart incident — carrying out — and in chapter Bava Metzia daf 30 and in chapter Kirah daf 43 moving was not permitted except via loaf and child — that is because the Drokart incident necessarily deals with Rambam and the Beit Yosef when he already putrefied; therefore carrying out was permitted and all the more so moving; and that which moving was not permitted in chapter Bava Metzia and chapter Kirah from sun to shade except via loaf and child — there it deals when he has not yet putrefied but is close to putrefying — in such a case neither moving nor carrying out was permitted;
