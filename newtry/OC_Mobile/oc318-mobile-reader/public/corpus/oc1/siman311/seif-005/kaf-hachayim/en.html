That we turned from bed, etc. And if he was alive at bein hashemashos permitted to move the bed afterward, for it did not become a base — Rosh; and Me'ir wrote that even if he was dead at bein hashemashos the bed did not become a base, for the bed is not needed for the corpse and he himself need not place him on it but on the ground as we learn they remove the pillow from under him — Magen Avraham seif 2; Eliyah Rabbah section 1; Tosafos Shabbos section 3; and see above siman 266 section 44 what is written in the name of Mordechai; and if he died at bein hashemashos and his intention was to leave him there all Shabbat — nevertheless turn from bed to bed, for all that is possible in a permitted manner, that is a doubt of a dispute, rather than moving directly; but a corpse lying in the sun where moving from the side is forbidden — there is no relevance regarding the bed — Ateret Zekenim section 2:
