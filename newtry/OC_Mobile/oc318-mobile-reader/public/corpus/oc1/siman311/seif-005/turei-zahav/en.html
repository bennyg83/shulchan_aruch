And one can answer from what is written siman 308 seif 5 that a utensil whose work is for prohibition is permitted from sun to shade by means of a loaf or infant — here is different, since it was muktzeh due to its body it is like a stone, unlike there where in any case it was a utensil; and this is what we learned: a mortar is moved by means of the garlic on it even from sun to shade — so too Beit Yosef siman 308 in the name of responsum of Rosh; and one can answer from what is written siman 310 that they move a utensil in which there is forbidden and permitted matter — meaning wood chips are the forbidden matter, nevertheless they move for the sake of the permitted matter there, and the wood chips themselves are muktzeh like stones and like a corpse; there the prohibition is nullified relative to the permitted matter and as if it does not exist, since the permitted matter is important — and so Ashkenazim and Tur concluded here; therefore those who move a menorah they kindled on it on Shabbat by means of a loaf or infant do not act well — end of his words. And it is puzzling what he wrote from that in siman 308 seif 5 that I mentioned, for here in the menorah also it is a utensil, only whose work is for prohibition — why does loaf or infant help there more than here; and these two rulings of Rosh are separate — except that that in siman 308 is responsum of Rosh and this here is in his rulings in chapter Nottel; and this here is in any case primary, for so Beit Yosef wrote siman 279 in the name of Rivash that one should not move the menorah by means of bread they place on Shabbat, for they did not say loaf and infant except for a corpse alone; and therefore one cannot rely on the custom in siman 308 seif 5. And there is somewhat to resolve that therefore in siman 308 he permits to move it directly for its body and place need; only from sun to shade forbidden — in this loaf or infant helps; unlike here in the menorah where even for its body and place need it is forbidden as above — therefore loaf or infant does not help:
