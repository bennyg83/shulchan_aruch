A corpse lying in the sun and they have no place to move it, or they did not wish to move it from its place — two people come and sit from two sides, hot for them from below: this one brings his bed and sits on it, and this one brings his bed and sits on it; hot for them from above — this one brings a mat and spreads it over himself, and this one brings a mat and spreads it over himself; this one stands his bed upright and slips away and goes, and this one stands his bed upright and slips away and goes; and a partition is found to have been made of itself, for behold this one's mat and this one's mat — their backs are adjacent to one another, and their two ends are on the ground on the two sides of the corpse.
