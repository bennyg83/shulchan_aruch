(א) He brings his bed. But they do not immediately bring a mat, for then it would be recognizable that he is doing it for the need of the dead; and it is forbidden to make a temporary tent for the need of the dead; but now it is recognizable that they did it for their own need, for when it became hot below they brought a bed:

(ב) He sets up his bed. And removes it afterward (Beit Yosef); and if he fears lest the dead person putrefy and they need to go out — permitted to spread a mat over him in a manner that does not make a tent, for it is for the need of the living [Tosafos Eruvin daf 44]:
