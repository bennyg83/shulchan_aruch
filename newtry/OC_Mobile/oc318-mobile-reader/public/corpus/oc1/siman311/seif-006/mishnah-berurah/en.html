(א) He brings his bed — in order to interrupt between them and the ground; and one should not challenge: since in the end he sets up his bed and removes it afterward as below, and only the mat remains spread over the dead person — let them bring the mat immediately and spread over him, and afterward when they remove it will remain over the dead person; one could say then it would be more recognizable that he is doing for the need of the dead, and it is forbidden to make a temporary tent for the need of the dead; but now it is recognizable they did for their own need, for when it became hot below they brought a bed, and they will say they also brought the mat from the beginning only for themselves:
(ב) He sets up his bed and it is removed, etc. — meaning he sets it up and removes it and the dead person is removed from beneath the mat, and the mats are hard and stand of themselves and remain as a tent over the dead person; and see in Magen Avraham that if he fears lest the dead person putrefy and they need to go out — permitted to spread a mat over him explicitly, but without making a tent:
