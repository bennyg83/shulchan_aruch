Permitted to anoint the corpse (and to rinse it)) and to remove the mattress from beneath it so it will not putrefy, provided he does not move any limb in it; and if its mouth was open and continues to open — he ties the cheek in a manner that it will not open further, but not in order to close what has opened or part of it, for if so he would be moving a limb; and for this reason they do not close the eyes of a corpse on Shabbat.
