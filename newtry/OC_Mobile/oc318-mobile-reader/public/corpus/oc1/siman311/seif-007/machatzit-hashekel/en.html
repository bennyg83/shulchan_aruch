(s.k. 7) We do not take him out, etc.; and dignity of the dead too there is not, etc. — this is a mere branch, for nevertheless there is disgrace, only the disgrace was diminished; and the main reason that according to Rambam we require that he already putrefied — because he holds that on account of disgrace of the dead they did not permit, only on account of dignity of the living; and since they have a place to go out — there is no dignity of the living — it was not permitted; therefore Magen Avraham wrote afterward that per the Tur who permits on account of disgrace of the dead — if so, even if they have a place to go out it is permitted, for still on account of disgrace of the dead; and that is specifically when close to putrefying; but if he putrefied and they have a place to go out — there is no [reason] on account of dignity of the living, and also on account of disgrace of the dead there is none, since he already putrefied there is no purpose for the dead person in taking him out; if so in such a case even for the Tur carrying out was not permitted; and so Magen Avraham inferred the language:
