7) There: He may move it directly. Although extinguishing in this case is labor not needed for its own purpose, so there is no concern for a Torah prohibition, the Sages nevertheless permitted direct moving. If one needed the extinguished material to make charcoal, the prohibition would be from the Torah, and not everyone knows this distinction. Ran writes similarly. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 4.
