To be — and see section 10: he raised Tosafos Shabbos 123a s.v. this turnip that he leaves all Shabbat and has no intention to take on Shabbat — it is a base even in foods; and here it deals that his intention is to eat and the rest will remain; and a hot coal presumably likewise that he will certainly take afterward — see there; and Beit Yosef here wrote: and we learn from Tosafos' words regarding one who leaves all Shabbat, and this is base in Tosafos s.v. this turnip and in s.v. hot coal it is explicit thus; and it is possible the permission to turn from below to above is forbidden, but from above to below — no base matter; what is possible rests from the side only on it directly is a base; and this is what we learn from their words: from below to above forbidden; and so hot coal — R' Eliezer ben Teda'i that it is buried entirely they wrote thus; and the same if part is uncovered and the rest has earth resting on it — it is a base if his intention is for all Shabbat; but in Maharsha there it does not imply thus; and what is written regarding turnip "perhaps" in "leaves" implies permitted from above to below that it is not a base what rests beside it from the side; and one can say.
