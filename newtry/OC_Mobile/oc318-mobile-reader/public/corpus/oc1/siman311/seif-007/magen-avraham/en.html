(א) Permitted to anoint. Permitted to sprinkle vinegar on the garments on the dead person so that he not putrefy, for what is written "and they can occupy themselves with his purification" — for there is no prohibition in contact with muktzeh; and also we do not decree here on account of squeezing (Shlah and chapter Kirah Darkei Moshe) — see siman 334 s.k. 24:

(ב) And to remove the cushion. Requires further study regarding what people nowadays practice — to raise the dead person from upon the cushions via a loaf even in a place where there is no concern lest he putrefy — therefore one must protest against them; only to remove the cushion from beneath him — even via loaf or child forbidden to move him to wash him unless he was soiled with mud and excrement that he is repulsive in the eyes of viewers [Radbaz responsa 22 section 4]; even to hint to a gentile that he do it — forbidden unless he cut from erev Shabbat [there responsa 22 section 1 section 4 section 25]:
