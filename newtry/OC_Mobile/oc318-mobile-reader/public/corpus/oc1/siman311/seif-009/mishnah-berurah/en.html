(א) In straw or in hay — straw is the reed cut with the ear, and hay is what remains in the field after harvest:

(ב) Muktzeh — to exclude if he designated them for animal food or for lying; and in our places default straw is designated for animal food; and see above in siman 308 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 118 what we wrote regarding bundles of straw:

(ג) One may stick, etc. — for it is indirect moving for the need of a permitted object and permitted, as above in seif 8; and all this when buried in muktzeh straw placed in its place and designated for fuel; but if he took on erev Shabbat some of it and buried fruits in it — certainly the name muktzeh was removed from that straw, for behold he prepared it to bury fruits in it, and permitted to move the straw explicitly:

(ד) And in dirt — meaning in dirt that is piled in the house for use, for upon it there is no name muktzeh; and know that according to what was explained in this siman that indirect moving for the need of a forbidden object is forbidden — know well that what some people practice on Shabbat to sell and receive money in a utensil or via a garment, or drag with a knife — apart from the great evil of commerce on Shabbat, how many blindings of the eye are in this; for even if we say this is indirect moving, behold it is for the sake of a forbidden object, namely the money, and there is no doubt it is a full prohibition:
