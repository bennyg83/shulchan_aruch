(א) Loaf or child — and the same applies to any other object permitted to move; and the reason they required all this and did not permit to move him explicitly as he concludes at the end — because whatever can be done with a slight permission is preferable:

(ב) By them — meaning one places upon him or with him and moves him with him; and moving the muktzeh is not so recognizable, even though the dead person too is muktzeh, since he moves both together as one; and they did not permit via this device of loaf or child except for a dead person on account of disgrace of the dead, but not for other muktzeh such as a stone and the like, as is written below in seif 5:

(ג) From the side — and even though indirect moving too is called moving when he moves it for the need of the muktzeh object, as is written in seif 8; nevertheless regarding fire they permitted from the reason that a person is agitated about his dead, and if you do not permit him he will come to extinguish. And regarding moving the bed after they removed the dead person from it — it depends: if he was alive at bein hashemashos, according to all permitted afterward to move the bed, for it did not become a base for the dead, since it was not designated at bein hashemashos; but if he died on erev Shabbat — the Rosh forbids and the Me'or permits; and if his intent from the beginning was that the dead person would rest upon it all Shabbat and afterward they removed him from it — according to all the bed became a base and it is forbidden to move it [Tosafos Shabbos]:

(ד) In that same domain — it is implied from this that to take out to another domain is forbidden in every manner; but many Acharonim agreed that even to another domain that was not joined with this one, or to karmelit — permitted to take out on account of disgrace of the dead; and regarding whether one needs a loaf and child for this — it depends on the two views explained in seif 2; and there are Acharonim who wrote that the Mechaber's view too is so; and what he wrote "in that same domain" — meaning that which we wrote, a matter that is decided that l'chatchila one needs a loaf or child, is specifically in that same domain; but when taking him out to another domain [such as when the fire is in the entire courtyard and he was forced to take him out from there] — then it is not a decided matter; some say that on the contrary it is better to take him out without loaf and child in order to minimize the prohibition of carrying out, as explained in seif 2:

(ה) Lying in the sun — deals when he has not yet putrefied but one is concerned lest he putrefy; therefore the Mechaber was strict that specifically in that same domain it is permitted to take him out, and he follows his reasoning at the beginning of seif 2; but according to Rama in the gloss there who brought the view of Rashi and Tur — also when lying in the sun and close to putrefying — permitted to take out to another domain; and so Bach ruled to be lenient:

(ו) It is called moving — and for this reason forbidden to push via a reed something that is muktzeh [Taz]; but if his pushing is because he needs to use the place where the muktzeh rests — permitted, for it is indirect moving for the need of a permitted object, as below in seif 8; and see above in siman 308 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 115:
