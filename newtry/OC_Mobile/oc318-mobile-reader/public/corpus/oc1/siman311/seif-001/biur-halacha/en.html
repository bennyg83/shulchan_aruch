(א) And all this in that domain — see Mishna Berurah seif kaf 4 in what is written there implying thus; so Magen Avraham explained the Mechaber's view, and so appears in Biur HaGra who explains thus the Mechaber's view that he rules like Rambam that it was not permitted due to human dignity except when the living are disgraced thereby, as written at the beginning of seif 2 and as Mishna Berurah wrote; but many later authorities — Levush, Bach, Taz, Tosafos Shabbos, Eliyah Rabbah, and so Peri Megadim in Orach Chayyim — also lean thus in the end; and in truth from the Mechaber's own words at the end of seif 2 who wrote "and the same if there is another disgrace," etc., and so all similar cases — it is proven explicitly that the same applies regarding burning of the corpse, for it is no worse than this [and what Magen Avraham rejected that there it is great disgrace is very forced, for the opposite is so as Tosafos Shabbos wrote]; and therefore we are compelled to say in one of these two: either Rambam himself concedes regarding fire with a corpse that it is permitted to take him out to another domain, as Tosafos Shabbos and Peri Megadim wrote, and what the Mechaber wrote "in that domain" is as I wrote in Mishna Berurah; or the Mechaber himself retracted at the end and adopted Rashi's view as law in seif 2, as Gra wrote explicitly in his Biur that what he wrote at the end of seif 2 "and the same if there is disgrace," etc., is according to Rashi who is the opposite of Rambam's view that the Mechaber adopted in seif 1. And for practical law certainly one should be lenient, for even if we say Rambam is strict in this, from many early great authorities it is proven they disagree with him — Rashi, Ran, Tur as Beit Yosef brought, and so Mordechai proves he holds that fire is preferable to sun; and that which the one who stores permits to karmelit in fire — certainly permitted; and also Tosafos in the name of Rabbenu Tam in chapter HaMitzniyet (Shabbos daf 94) and Ramban in Torat HaAdam permit to take him out to karmelit even from sun to shade, and fire is certainly no worse than this, as Magen Avraham himself wrote that fire and sun are equal; and further many early authorities that Eliyah Rabbah brought; and also that karmelit is a matter of the rabbis — certainly we listen to be lenient:

(ב) One may not move him at all — and even according to Rama who is lenient shortly to take him out to karmelit due to human dignity, nevertheless regarding moving in that domain all agree it is forbidden without a loaf and infant, for taking out is as it is and must be done in another manner, unlike regarding moving where it is possible to fix mostly by means of a loaf and infant; therefore they did not distinguish even where he has none; and some say because the prohibition of moving is stricter than the prohibition of taking out to karmelit [Tosafos Shabbos in the name of the poskim]:
