(א) By means. Meaning he places it next to him and moves it with him; see siman 308 seif 41. What is customary — that they do not move the corpse except by means of a loaf or an infant that is there from erev Shabbat — they have nothing to rely on; Be'er Heitev siman 52; and similarly in responsum Divrei Shmuel:

(ב) From the bed. And permitted to move the bed afterward even if the corpse was [there] at bein hashemashos:

(ג) In that [domain]. Meaning even in that domain one must seek the permitted [means] he mentioned and it does not come to sharpen that in another domain it is forbidden — Taz; and see Magen Avraham and Yad Aharon:

(ד) For its sake. And for this reason it is forbidden to push by means of a reed a thing that is muktzeh — Taz; and see siman 308 seif 34 from Shem Shemuel:
