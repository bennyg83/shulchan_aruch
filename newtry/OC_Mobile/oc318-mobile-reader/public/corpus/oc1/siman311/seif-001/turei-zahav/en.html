(א) And all this in that domain. This is very puzzling, for afterward due to disgrace alone they permitted even from private domain to karmelit, etc.; and here it would be forbidden only in that domain — and Levush already wrote this is a textual error, for certainly permitted even to another domain; and so is the essence, for the reason in the Gemara is that if you do not permit him, since he is distraught over his dead he will come to extinguish — if so, also to another domain there is permission from this reason; also Maharach wrote puzzled on Shulchan Aruch in this. And it appears to me that this "and all this in that domain" means even in that domain one must seek the permitted [means] he already mentioned and it does not come to sharpen that in another domain it is forbidden:
