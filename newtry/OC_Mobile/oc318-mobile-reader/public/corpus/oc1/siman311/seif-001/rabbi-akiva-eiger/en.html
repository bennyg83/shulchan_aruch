Seif 1, in the same domain, via a loaf. It is explained that moving in the usual manner via a loaf or child is preferable to moving not in the usual manner without a loaf or child; and with moving the corpse for the need of its place it is the reverse — indirect moving is permitted as below seif 5, and in the usual manner via a loaf or child is forbidden. See Magen Avraham s.k. 14.
