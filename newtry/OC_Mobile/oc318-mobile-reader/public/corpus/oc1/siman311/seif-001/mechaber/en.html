Laws of a corpse on Shabbat and other indirect moving. And in it 9 seifim: A corpse
lying in a place where one fears for it due to fire — if he has a loaf or a child, he moves it via them; and if he does not have a loaf or a child, if he has two beds he moves it by turning it from bed to bed, which is indirect moving; and if he has neither this nor that, he moves it with complete moving; and all this is in the same domain. A corpse lying in the sun — he moves. it from sun to shade in the same domain via a loaf or a child; and if he does not have a loaf or a child he does not move it at all, even to turn it from bed to bed, for indirect moving is considered moving. And see above in this siman seif 6 what the remedy is.
