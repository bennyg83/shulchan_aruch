Magen Avraham s.k. 2, and if he was alive. And if he was alive at the entry of Shabbat — permitted to move afterward the bed, meaning after the corpse was removed from the bed, for there is no muktzeh for half of Shabbat; and so explained Tosafos Shabbos and Machatzit HaShekel. And to me it is astounding, for necessarily it deals where the bed is not a base at all, such as when he forgot or died on Shabbat it is as placed — otherwise why shake, for behold indirect moving — the bed itself is forbidden to move.
