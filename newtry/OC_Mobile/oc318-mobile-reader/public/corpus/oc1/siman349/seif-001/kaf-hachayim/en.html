(1) [Seif 1] Every person has four amot, etc. — as it is written "a man under him" like under him; and how much is under him? His body three amot and an amah in order to spread his hands and feet — the words of R' Meir; R' Yehuda says: his body three amot and an amah in order that he take an object from beneath his feet and place it beneath his head; what is the difference between them? There is a difference of confined four amot — end of the Gemara; and Rashi explained: for R' Yehuda confined, and for R' Meir large, for spreading hands and feet is more than an amah — end of his words; but Rif and Rosh wrote for R' Yehuda spread — and it is plain like R' Yehuda as is known that R' Meir and R' Yehuda — the halacha is like R' Yehuda; and so wrote the Tur; but the view of the Shulchan Aruch that he did not copy the words of the Tur implies that he holds like Rambam, who wrote in Beit Yosef that here he did not rule like R' Yehuda, and these four amot are confined — see there; and so too Bach that the Shulchan Aruch follows the words of Rambam; and so too Magen Avraham s.k. 1; and so too Tosafos Orach 1, except that he wrote that we are lenient like the Tur — see there; however R' Zalman Orach 1 ruled like the view of the Shulchan Aruch that these four amot are confined; and so too Chayei Adam general rule 53 Orach 8; and such is the view of the Acharonim; and see below Orach 7:
