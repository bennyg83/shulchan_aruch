One who examines the Beit Yosef brings the words of Rabbeinu Yeruham in Chullin that even in karmelit it is forbidden l'chatchila less than four amot when there is no need; for Kol Bo writes that they said less than four amot requires — to sit, for he stood as a walker (in Magen Avraham 6 and in siman 266 letter 9, and with Hashem's help it will be explained there); and likewise karmelit is liable d'rabbanan — he means to say that there too what they permitted from pressure, less than four amot requires sitting; and his main intent is that from pressure only — likewise Kol Bo — that is, in karmelit too less than four amot from pressure is not l'chatchila; from what Rashi z"l explained Shabbat 42a s.v. thorn in reshut ha-rabbim — removes to the sides of reshut ha-rabbim, for it is a d'rabbanan prohibition — and sides of reshut ha-rabbim are karmelit as in siman 361 and siman 364; Eruvin 34a — if so, when he comes to the sides of reshut ha-rabbim, there even when the multitudes are not commonly found he can carry less than four amot — learn from this that there too it is forbidden when the multitudes are not present, and it is l'chatchila; and Tosafos in Eruvin 34a s.v. and why — they wrote in bein hashmashos they decreed less than four amot — behold shevus is strict — likewise karmelit; one can say likewise, for it is a close decree; and accordingly the Taz z"l wrote that in bein hashmashos and karmelit one can permit, for so it is implied from Tosafos there — one places his eruv in reshut ha-rabbim — behold in karmelit it is not so, for he need not carry there for Rabbi — that in bein hashmashos and karmelit they did not permit except in a place of mitzvah; and accordingly a new law emerged as stated — and this is an error, for it concludes a decree of Motzei Shabbos. And for me personally it is difficult from Rashi — there is no resolution that sides of reshut ha-rabbim are forbidden — moving muktzeh, for a thorn is not fit on Shabbat; and according to him, likewise in bein hashmashos it is permitted for Rabbi; and if so for Rashi in karmelit he need not carry his eruv there — for in bein hashmashos he requires carrying less than four amot; and even Motzei Shabbos karmelit is permitted in such a manner; and karmelit that is permitted extensively — that is in a place of damage to the multitudes; and see there he wrote that it is implied from the Mechaber that karmelit and bein hashmashos — less than four amot is forbidden; and in seif 3 the permission even when each one walks less than four amot (for the fourth stands in his place and hands to his fellow near him) as Rashi explained Eruvin 95b in the Mishnah that each one moves less than four amot; and one explanation for one domain that one person carries several amot several times less than less than four amot — they decreed also in karmelit, for it is a close decree in such a manner; and in the way it is implied also in karmelit if there is a gentile with him it is forbidden less than less than four amot via a gentile; and what he wrote about Maharshal — see Eliyah Rabbah; and what the Rama wrote in siman 301 seif 42 regarding tefillin — it is difficult, why specify giving to his fellow? Behold for a matter of reshut it is also permitted in such a manner, even carrying as stated; and why specify tefillin?
