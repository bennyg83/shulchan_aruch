דין ארבע אמות ברשות הרבים. ובו ה סעיפים:
The law of four amot in reshut ha-rabbim. And in it are 5 seifim: Every person has four amot in reshut ha-rabbim within which he may move objects, and we measure for him with his amah; and if his amah is small we give him four average amot of every person, each of which is six tefachim [and see below siman 396]:[]:
