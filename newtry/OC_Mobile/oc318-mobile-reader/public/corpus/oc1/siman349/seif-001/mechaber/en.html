דין ארבע אמות ברשות הרבים. ובו ה סעיפים:
The Law of Four Amot in the Public Domain, 5 Seifim: Every person has four amot in the public domain in which he may carry. We measure these amot by his own forearm ("amah"). If his forearm is small, we allow him four average amot, which are each six tefachim (and see below chapter 396).[]:
