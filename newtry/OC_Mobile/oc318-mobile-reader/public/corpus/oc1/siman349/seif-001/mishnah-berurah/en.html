(א) Every person has, etc. — the reason is as it is written "a man under him," and the intent is that the sitting of every person on the day of Shabbat shall be in the measure beneath him — that is, in the measure of the length of his entire body; and Chazal measured that the length of a person that is the measure beneath him is three amot and additionally an amah in order to spread his hands and feet; and within this measure a person may move objects from here to here:

(ב) Four amot — confined [Magen Avraham]; and that is, one shall measure them tightly; and see in seif 2 regarding four amot that they spoke of — they and their diagonal:

(ג) With his amah — that is, if this person is very tall we measure for him the four amot with his forearm, and it is found that his four amot will be larger than the four amot of other people:

(ד) His amah is small — that is, his body is average like every other person and his forearm is short compared to other people; and if they measure his four amot with his forearm they will be short relative to the needs of his body; and the Torah said "a man under him" — therefore we give him average four amot of every person:

(ה) Siman 396 — that is, there, in the gloss it is explained regarding one going outside the techum that the four amot are measured spread; however here regarding moving four amot in reshut ha-rabbim one must be stringent specifically confined, as stated above in s.k. 2:
