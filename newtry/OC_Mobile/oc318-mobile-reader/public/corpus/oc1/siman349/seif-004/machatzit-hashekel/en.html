(s.k. 4) Outside, etc., and for ownerless objects, etc. — since the reason is that he not take outside the techum is because vessels follow the feet of the owners, and they are not permitted to carry them more than the owners are permitted to walk; and therefore for ownerless objects that have no owners — and it is plain that they did not acquire dwelling — therefore he may carry them as much as he wants; and it is written in Tosafos Shabbat that we require that no person intend to acquire them; for if he intended to acquire them — likewise they follow the feet of the acquirer:
