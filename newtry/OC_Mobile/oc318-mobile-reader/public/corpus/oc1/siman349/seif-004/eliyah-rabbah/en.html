The Taz wrote: Communities that give a sefer Torah on Shabbat to the synagogue and carry it via handing from one to his fellow in less than four amot — and they make a prohibition in what they take it out from the house to the street, for this handing does not help; and via gentiles it is forbidden, for it is a mitzvah that comes through a transgression; and see siman 331 s.k. 5:
