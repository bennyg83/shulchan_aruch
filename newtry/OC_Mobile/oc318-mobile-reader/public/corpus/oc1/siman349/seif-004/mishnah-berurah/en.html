(א) If two people, etc. — that is, one has food and it rests near him at the beginning of his four amot, and likewise the second has food and it rests near him also at the beginning of his four amot; and between them is a gap of six amot — it is found that the second two amot of each one are swallowed one within the other; therefore both are permitted to bring the food within the middle two amot that are still within the four amot of each one:
(ב) And the middle one is swallowed between them — that he stands in the middle of the eight and near him rests food; and it is found that his four amot are swallowed within both of them, for if he wishes he can move two from this side and two from that side; and this is what is written the middle one is permitted with each of the outer ones:
