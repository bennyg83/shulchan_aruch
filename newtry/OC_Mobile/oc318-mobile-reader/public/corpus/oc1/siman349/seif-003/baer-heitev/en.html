(א) To his fellow. And that is specifically within reshut ha-rabbim or within reshut ha-yachid or within karmelit; but to take out from karmelit to reshut ha-yachid or from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit — certainly forbidden. Yad Aharon, and so too the Taz, who wrote: It is plain that this only helps for moving outside four amot in reshut ha-rabbim or in karmelit, but to take out from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit — certainly forbidden; and accordingly I raised a protest against what I saw in some communities on Shabbat — they give a sefer Torah to the synagogue and walk via the street which is in any event karmelit, and carry it via handing from one to his fellow in less than four amot; and they make a prohibition in what they take it out from the house to the street, for this handing does not help; and via a gentile — certainly forbidden to take it out from the house, and it is a mitzvah that comes through a transgression in my opinion; and when they carry on Shabbat a child to circumcise — one should permit that a gentile take the child out from the house, for the issue is not taking out from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit; and afterward Israelites may move him less than less than four amot; and likewise when they bring him into the synagogue — they must have a gentile bring him in, for he brings from karmelit to reshut ha-yachid; all this appears clear, and may Hashem atone for the unwitting. And Magen Avraham in siman 331 s.k. 5 wrote: It is plain that it is forbidden to bring the child to the synagogue via a courtyard that did not join eruv, even via a gentile, for they can circumcise in his house; and afterward he wrote: Since there are those who are lenient even in a d'oraisa melacha for circumcision — one may rely on them. See there; and see what is written in siman 376 seif 7 (and in Eben HaOzer he raised a remedy for this too — that those standing outside extend their hands into the house and his wife who is inside the house places the child into the hands and afterward takes with her hands to the outside, and then this one uproots and that one places — from reshut ha-rabbim exempt but forbidden; and if so, in karmelit they did not decree, etc. — see there at length in Maginei Eretz section Proops):
