There is here a further remedy. One must examine this extensively, for from the words of Rashi Shabbat 101a s.v. but it is explained that a ship if it is not nine tefachim deep is karmelit — behold a ship is a vessel; nevertheless when it stands in karmelit it is karmelit; and as Magen Avraham wrote siman 255 s.k. 10; and if so for us who do not have reshut ha-rabbim according to most poskim — see above siman 303 — if so this remedy is lost; and nevertheless one can say that Rashi holds there is no karmelit in vessels; that is, when a vessel stands in reshut ha-rabbim it is reshut ha-rabbim, for it is nullified to reshut ha-rabbim; and as Tosafos Shabbat 5a s.v. here below ten wrote — therefore a vessel in karmelit is nullified to karmelit; but according to those who hold a vessel in reshut ha-rabbim is makom patur — also in karmelit it is makom patur; for otherwise it must be so for Rashi, for there is no reason for Rashi's reason that karmelit is stricter in this because it found its kind and was shaken — see above siman 346 s.k. 9 in the gloss, for Rashi does not hold so in Shabbat 7a s.v. for it is still karmelit, etc.; if so we find stringency in karmelit — see there; if so the words of Eben HaOzer here apply to those who hold a vessel in reshut ha-rabbim is makom patur — and the first remedy fits well. Afterward I saw that Eben HaOzer explicitly holds that a vessel in karmelit is also makom patur, as explained in his words above siman 345 s.k. 2, and in his novellae to Shabbat 5a Tosafos s.v. here below ten; however in Chayei Adam there he decides that Rashi also holds a vessel in reshut ha-yachid is makom patur, and likewise a vessel in karmelit — see there; but in Machatzit HaShekel — behold in that Rashi above regarding a ship it is explicitly stated that in any event when standing in karmelit it is karmelit:
