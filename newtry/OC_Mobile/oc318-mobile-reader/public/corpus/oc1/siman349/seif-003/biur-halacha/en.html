(א) And give to his fellow near him, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote in the name of the Taz that his view is that it is forbidden to take out a sefer Torah from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit even via a gentile, for it is a mitzvah that comes through a transgression; and it is difficult, for behold it is shevus of Shabbat for the sake of a mitzvah; and it appears the Taz follows his approach that he holds in siman 307 end s.k. 4 that they did not permit via a gentile only to bring via a courtyard that did not join eruv, but not via karmelit, for one comes to exchange in reshut ha-rabbim — see there; but according to what Eliyah Rabbah wrote there, in the name of several poskim that it is permitted even in karmelit [and we copied it there in Biur Halacha — see there] — automatically it is permitted also in this:

(ב) And his fellow to his fellow near him — from this language it is implied that specifically via many people, each one carrying less than four amot; and in Peri Megadim siman 266 it is implied that he holds that even via two people it is also permitted [that is, one carries from the start less than four amot and gives it to his fellow who also carries less than four amot, and afterward takes it from him and carries again less than four amot and gives it to his fellow, and repeats again]; but he does not know whence he has this, for it is possible that since this person carries several times less than four amot — behold he is included in the decree of Chazal who forbade carrying less than four amot unless for one who darkened on the road explained in siman 266:
