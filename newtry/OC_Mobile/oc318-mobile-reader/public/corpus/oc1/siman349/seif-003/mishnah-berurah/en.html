(א) Permitted, etc. — for although below seif 5 it is stated that one person is forbidden to carry an object less than less than four amot, lest he pass it four amot at once — here, since it is via many people, each one carrying only less than four amot, even though via all of them the object was moved a great distance — we have no concern:

(ב) Provided he not take it out, etc. — explanation: he shall not take out the object that has an owner outside his boundary from the place where he acquired dwelling — that is, in the place where its owners acquired dwelling there; and if so, a hundred milin is not precise; or it deals with ownerless objects that have no owners, and we rule that they did not acquire dwelling; and therefore he may carry them as much as he wants; and the Acharonim wrote that we require that no person intend to acquire them by lifting them; for if he intended to acquire them — behold they follow only the feet of the acquirer:

(ג) And some forbid — and according to this view, even in a place where they permitted moving less than four amot, such as one who darkened on the road mentioned in siman 266 seif 9, or Motzei Tefillin mentioned in siman 301 seif 42 — via several people it is forbidden, for it is worse because there is degradation of Shabbat:

(ד) And see above siman 301 — that is, there the Mechaber anonymously follows the first view to be lenient; and nevertheless regarding a matter of reshut it is good to be concerned for the view of those who forbid; and know that what the Mechaber permitted — carrying via many people — only helps for carrying in reshut ha-rabbim or in karmelit, but not to take out from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit; and accordingly some communities do not act properly on Shabbat — they give a sefer Torah to the synagogue and walk via the street which is in any event karmelit via handing from one to his fellow in less than four amot; and they make a prohibition in what they take it out from the house to the street, for this handing does not help; and see in the Taz that his view is that even via a gentile it is forbidden to take it out from the house; and see in Biur Halacha what he wrote about this; and regarding circumcision see what I wrote in siman 331 s.k. 20 in Mishna Berurah:
