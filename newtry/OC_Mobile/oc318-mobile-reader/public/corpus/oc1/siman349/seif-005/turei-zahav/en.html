Even in karmelit. In Beit Yosef in the name of Kol Bo he learned thus from what they said in the chapter Kirah (Shabbat daf 42) that a thorn in reshut ha-rabbim — one carries it less than less than four amot; and Rashi explained until he removes it to the sides of reshut ha-rabbim, for it is a d'rabbanan prohibition — end of his words; it is implied that it is forbidden l'chatchila less than less than four amot; and they were strict in this even at bein hashmashos, for other shevusim are permitted as above; nevertheless here one will transgress easily and take out outside four amot; and accordingly they were strict even in karmelit, for it is close that he will take out outside four amot; and nevertheless it appears to me that for two reasons for leniency — that is, bein hashmashos and karmelit — they were not so strict, for it is two d'rabbanan matters; and the permission he mentioned in seif 3 — one gives to his fellow near him, etc. — is even when each one walks less than four amot; there is no prohibition, for there they did not decree lest he take out outside four amot, for each one is certainly careful; and so Rashi explained explicitly that each one moves within his four amot; and it is plain that this only helps for moving outside four amot in reshut ha-rabbim or in karmelit, but not to take out from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit; and so too Maharshal in his responsum siman 51, and he brought proof for this; and to me it is wondrous that it is plain, for behold an explicit Mishnah regarding from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim in the first Mishnah of Shabbat; and likewise regarding karmelit d'rabbanan; and accordingly I raised a protest against what I saw in some communities on Shabbat — they give a sefer Torah to the synagogue and walk via the street which is in any event karmelit, and carry it via handing from one to his fellow in less than four amot; and they make a prohibition in what they take it out from the house to the street, for this handing does not help; and via a gentile — certainly forbidden to take it out from the house, and it is a mitzvah that comes through a transgression in my opinion; and when they carry on Shabbat a child to circumcise — one should permit that a gentile take the child out from the house, for the issue is not taking out from reshut ha-yachid to karmelit; and afterward Israelites may move him less than less than four amot; and likewise when they bring him into the synagogue — they must have a gentile bring him in, for he brings from karmelit to reshut ha-yachid; all this appears clear, and may Hashem atone for the unwitting:
