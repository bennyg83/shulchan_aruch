(ב) Less less, etc. — it is called less via standing to rest between times, for it is as one who places the object on the ground; but if he stood within four amot to fix a load — it is as one walking all four amot at once, and there is also liability in this:

(ג) And even at bein hashmashos — although regarding less than four amot it is a d'rabbanan doubt and they were lenient in such a case in siman 342 — see there; since it is close to come thereby to a d'oraisa melacha — they decreed more strictly upon them:

(ד) And even in karmelit — the reason is that if we are lenient in this it is close that he will carry four amot at once [Taz]; and in Biur HaGra he wrote the reason that every matter they forbade in reshut ha-rabbim because of shevus — likewise forbidden in karmelit; and if it is at bein hashmashos also — that is two reasons for leniency — the view of the Taz; and Eliyah Rabbah to be lenient in this; however according to what is explained above in siman 342 that they did not permit shevus at bein hashmashos except for the sake of a mitzvah or in a place of pressure — it is possible that here too one may not be lenient except in such a case:
