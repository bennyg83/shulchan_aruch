(א) And even at bein hashmashos — see in Beit Yosef that its source flows from the words of Tosafos Eruvin; and see there in Tosafos and Rosh that according to their approach likewise one may not permit at bein hashmashos this one uproots and that one places — for it is also a strict shevus that is close to come to a d'oraisa melacha; and behold from the words of Tosafos it is implied that shevus of less than less than four amot is stricter than shevus of taking out from karmelit to reshut ha-rabbim; for taking out from karmelit to reshut ha-rabbim [and likewise to reshut ha-yachid] is proven there in Rashi and Tosafos that it is permitted at bein hashmashos for the matter of eruv; and for the matter of less than four amot — Tosafos and their supporters hold it is forbidden at bein hashmashos; and according to this, in a place where they permit less than less than four amot even in reshut ha-rabbim, such as one who darkened on the road and there is no gentile with him, etc. — all the more so we should permit taking out from karmelit to some domain; and it is difficult, for in siman 266 the Taz wrote there [and several Acharonim, and several that I copied there in Mishna Berurah] that it is not permitted only in moving less than four amot, but not in taking out from domain to domain, even to karmelit; and this requires further study:

(ב) And even in karmelit — there are Acharonim who are uncertain about the view of the Shulchan Aruch in this; and what the Taz wrote that it flows from the words of Kol Bo — in truth this is not mentioned in the words of Kol Bo, for he refers to one who does not interrupt with sitting between times, for he is considered as one walking four amot at once — so wrote Mordechai and Nehar Shalom; and they hold it is a decree upon a decree; however the Taz explained that just as we are strict regarding less than less than four amot for bein hashmashos because it is close that he will take out four amot — likewise for karmelit; and accordingly if we hold like Rashi that at bein hashmashos less than less than four amot is permitted — we may be lenient also in karmelit; but per the reason of the Gra that we copied in Mishna Berurah that every matter they forbade because of shevus in reshut ha-rabbim — likewise they forbade in karmelit — automatically also here it is forbidden; and also the Gra brought a source for the words of the Shulchan Aruch that less than less than four amot is forbidden even in karmelit; and also in Tashbetz part 2 siman 281 he wrote plainly that less than less than four amot is forbidden even in karmelit; and they brought it in the novellae of R' Akiva Eiger; and see there further in his novellae that he brought from Rashi Shabbat 127 s.v. and Imar it is implied that he holds that in karmelit less than less than four amot is permitted; however in truth even for Rashi it is not proven except specifically for a matter of mitzvah as was there, in the story of Rabbi — see there; and it is possible Rashi follows his approach that he holds that at bein hashmashos likewise less than less than four amot is permitted; but per the words of Tosafos and their supporters who hold that even at bein hashmashos less than less than four amot is forbidden — and there it deals with eruv, for we rule that one may make eruv only for a matter of mitzvah — at first glance it is reasonable that likewise it is forbidden in such a case even in karmelit; but from the sugya Shabbat 127 it is implied that it is permitted, as stated; and it is possible that even per the words of Tosafos less than less than four amot in karmelit is called shevus deshevus and permitted for the sake of a mitzvah;
