(א) They and their diagonal — that is, the measure of four amot is they and their diagonal; he is not liable unless he moved the measure that the diagonal of four amot ascends; and not only if he moved to the diagonal of the world, for he is not liable in less than the measure of the diagonal of four amot; but even if he moved in the square of the world from east to west or from north to south — likewise he is not liable in less than the measure of the diagonal:

(ב) And some say, etc. — and so agreed the Acharonim:

(ג) From four amot until, etc. — and in less than four amot, also according to this view it is permitted l'chatchila, as stated above in seif 1:

(ד) Exempt but forbidden — this is the view of Rambam; and see in Biur HaGra who wrote that Rambam also concedes that where he chose for himself four amot to move in them and wishes to move in their diagonal of the four amot — he is permitted to move in them even though it ascends until five amot and three-fifths; and he does not argue on the first view except when he wishes to move on the flat and not in diagonal, or he did not designate for himself from the start the four amot to move in them — for according to him in all this he has no permission to move except until four amot without diagonal; and know that what we rule that if one passed an object in reshut ha-rabbim four amot they and their diagonal — he is liable specifically when this measure was between the place of uprooting the object and its placing — that is, that he placed the object outside this measure; but if the place of placing the object was within this measure, even only part of the object — exempt, for behold he did not pass the object four amot they and their diagonal in completeness; so it is proven from Rambam chapter 12 law 19 — see there:
