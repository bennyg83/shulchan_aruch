(S.k. 1) The Magen Avraham wrote: although one does not, etc. See siman 339, for for us, even without this, we are lenient nowadays regarding dancing, because its prohibition is lest one repair a musical instrument. Since we are not expert in making musical instruments, there is no reason to decree.
And see siman 511, se'if 4. The reason is because it is not equally needed by every person, but only by refined people; therefore it was not permitted on Yom Tov. Thus it is forbidden to ignite powder. The Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 514, s.k. 13, that it is permitted to fasten powder into a lamp before lighting the lamp, even though it ultimately causes the lamp to go out through this.
