[1] That we rejoice, etc. Rabbeinu Bachya wrote to protest against throwing fruit to the youths. But there is a Midrash that this too was what Haman said to the king about the Jews; if so, it is a custom of the ancients. Perhaps Rabbeinu Bachya saw in his days that they practiced frivolity and wildness with this; Malbushei Yom Tov in the name of a collection. Some have the custom that the children dismantle the sukkah and burn it on Simchas Torah. My teacher, my father of blessed memory, when I was a youth, protested against me not to dismantle it on Yom Tov; Darkei Moshe in the name of Maharil. Maharil wrote: It is customary to dance, even several elders, at the time when they say praises to the Torah. End quote. But to burn incense is forbidden; see siman 511, se'if 4. The Magen Avraham wrote: all the more so it is forbidden to ignite powder to make its sound for joy. In siman 514 the Magen Avraham wrote that it is permitted to fasten powder into a lamp, even though this causes the lamp to go out because of it. End quote. From all this it is explained that one should gladden the public in every possible way with the joy of a mitzvah, unlike those who strike and push one another until the joy is turned into grief, Heaven forbid; through this they also prevent the joy of the mitzvah, and therefore one should rebuke them.
