Simchas Torah. In honor of the Torah it is permitted to dance on Yom Tov, but to burn incense is forbidden; Maharik. All the more so it is forbidden to ignite powder in order to make a sound for joy, and so I saw that the great authorities in the land protested; Magen Avraham. Nevertheless, it is permitted to fasten powder into a lamp, even though this causes the lamp to go out because of it; so wrote the Baer Heitev before me. An individual who was initially appointed as Chasan Torah, and then a relative died on the festival for whom he is obligated to mourn, may exempt himself from the appointment of being chasan, and should not go to the synagogue that day; Responsa Devar Shmuel, siman 270. One who apostatized to the religion of the Ishmaelites and returned to the religion of Yisrael, repented properly, and from then onward his desire is in the Torah of Hashem, and he conducts himself uprightly, walks blamelessly and acts righteously like all his kosher brethren of Bnei Yisrael, may be Chasan Torah; Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 12.
All the youths. It appears in the Yerushalmi, chapter 5 of Berachos: two should not read in the Torah. Rabbi Zeira said: because of the blessings. This implies that it is forbidden for two people to recite the blessing, and this requires investigation, since they do this on Simchas Torah; Magen Avraham. See Responsa Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, klal 2, siman 102, regarding what he challenged about this custom. Maharil wrote: There are places where the children have the custom to dismantle the sukkah and burn it on Simchas Torah, and they give a reason to permit it. But my father, of blessed memory, protested against me when I was a youth, not to dismantle it on Yom Tov; Darkei Moshe. The Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: I found on parchment that Rabbeinu Bachya wrote to protest against throwing fruit to the youths, but there is in the Midrash that this was what Haman said to the king about the Jews. If so, it is an ancient custom; perhaps Rabbeinu Bachya saw in his days that they practiced frivolity and wildness. End quote.
