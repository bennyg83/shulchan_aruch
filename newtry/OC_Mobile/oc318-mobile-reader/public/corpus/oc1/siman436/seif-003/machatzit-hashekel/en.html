Indeed plain language like Kaf HaChayim; however Magen Avraham concludes even near road if intent return day or two before must check: not forced from Hagahot Maimoniyot reading; Magen Avraham's own reasoning. If so proven Magen Avraham: Hagahot Maimoniyot "long time intent": even caravan long before must check; not only intent return day or two before.
