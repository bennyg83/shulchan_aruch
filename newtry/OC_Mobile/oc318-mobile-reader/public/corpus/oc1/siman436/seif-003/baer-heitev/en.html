(א) Leaving gentile's house — but from Israel's house another Israel dwells — not obligated — second Israel has bedika from house he dwells in (Magen Avraham, Kaf HaChayim).

(ב) Mitzvas biur — per Rav above siman 433 — pockets and sleeves need check; sea/caravan can fulfill biur and bedika; may say since tashbitu se'or from your houses — essence mitzva check and burn chametz from house itself (Kaf HaChayim). All thirty days examine everything lest chametz remain hard to remove (Bach, Maharil).
