(א) He need not burn — since gentile enters house he acquires chametz — Israel declared hefker.

(ב) And must burn gentile's house — implies Israel's house need not burn — second Israel has biur obligation siman 437; Rashi on renter transgresses bal yera'eh if not nullified — first as one without house, chametz not his — declared hefker exempt from burning; Mordechai even in gentile's house, etc. — per Maharam when leaves Israel's house no Israel enters to dwell — plainly must burn for chametz remains in Israel's possession; even gentile's house chametz in gentile's possession gentile acquires — nevertheless must burn as above, not like Bach; also Agudah words — I say Agudah never meant this — only specifically sea/caravan fear delay unlike ordinary traveler as Hagahot Maimoniyot s.k. 1 — investigate well.

(ג) When gentile enters — but when he clears and no one enters — within thirty biur obligation applies as this siman: machzorim — all thirty days examine everything lest chametz remain hard to remove (Bach, Maharil) siman 444 s.k. 3.
