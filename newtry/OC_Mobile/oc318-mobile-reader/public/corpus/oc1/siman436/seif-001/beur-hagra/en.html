(א) s.k. 1 And he does not leave — otherwise biur obligation would not apply — agent like himself as above.

(ב) And he should not bless — as Rosh wrote: enacted blessing on biur chametz because immediately nullifies and conceals known until hour five then burns — blessing enacted for this; not so here he does not nullify and still uses chametz — see there.

(ג) He need not check — Rambam's language — meaning specifically doubtful chametz as Yerushalmi and Ran; in doubt; but definite even from Rosh Hashanah; or alternatively per our Gemara they said to burn; Tur and Shach; but Rambam usually follows Yerushalmi and so Kol Bo, Semag, Semak.

(ד) And when arrives, etc. — otherwise transgresses bal yimatzei ch. 1 unlike beginning where nullifies at bedika as there checker nullifies at four — not end; Magen Avraham not like Taz — s.k. 2 if forgot, etc. — anyway nullifies at bedika as Magen Avraham there.

(ה) And if his intention, etc., before — learned from Yerushalmi ch. 1 traveler: you might say when intent to return; but no intent to return even before thirty must check; R' Abba: even with intent must check lest he reconsider and not return — no intent to return before Pesach; but intent before Pesach need not check then; R' Abba: even with intent lest reconsider — only bein hashemos.

(ו) And likewise before Pesach — also Rambam's language there.

(ז) However after — although Yerushalmi ch. 2 collapse permitted after Pesach — they said there he declared hefker on 13th after Pesach what? R' Yehuda forbidden, Rashb'l permitted; R' Yehuda to R' Pinchas: your teaching like R' Yosi, Rashb'l like R' Meir — not so: R' Yehuda feared trickery, Rashb'l not; outcome: collapse — no trickery permitted; acquisition view — no acquisition forbidden; dispute Nedarim 43a Mishnah Tanna Kamma and R' Yosi; Gemara R' Yehuda's reason; collapse — either trickery permitted as above or acquisition — R' Yochanan like R' Yosi — we do not rule like him — halacha like stam Mishnah; Rav Hai Gaon forbade such case — must say here worse than collapse — resembles trickery.

(ח) And if no known, permitted, etc. — that responsum does not discuss selling after Pesach in doubt — deduced from his words "if clear to us wheat there" as nullified with intent; not sided doubt — need not clear even within thirty lest none there perhaps spoiled; see responsum.
