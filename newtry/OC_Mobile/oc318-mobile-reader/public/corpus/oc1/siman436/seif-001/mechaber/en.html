The Laws Applying to One Who Sets out on a Sea Voyage or Journeys by Caravan. Containg 3 paragraphs: If one sets out on a journey from land to the sea, or in a caravan, and he did not leave anyone at home that could check for him within 30 days, he must check . Before 30 days, he does not need to check . (Tur). If one intends to return before Passover, he must check for Chametz, and afterward he may go out, due to concerns lest he might return on the eve of Passover between twilight periods and will not have time to burn [the leavened bread]. Similarly, one who makes his home a storage place within thirty days is in need of checking, and afterward he may store within it. Before thirty days, if one intends to clear it out before Passover, he must check for chametz, and afterward he may clear it out. But if he does not intend to clear it out before Passover, he does not need to check for chametz. {Rama: [and he does not make the blessing "al biur chametz"] (Kol Bo) and when Pesacdh arrives it is nullified Rema: And a wheat storehouse that has leavened wheat at the bottom of the pit: if the storehouse is made thirty days before Passover, there is no need to burn it (the chametz); rather, one may nullify it in his heart, which is sufficient. However, after Passover, when clearing out the storehouse, it is prohibited to derive benefit from those wheat grains. And if there is no known leavened bread there, only doubt, it is permitted to sell what was stored so it is concluded together. (Responsa of Rashba, Section 70)}
