1. (s.k. 1) He must check: by day, meaning by candlelight as above siman 433.

2. Perhaps he intended Rashi's words, etc.: his words are obscure here; I must offer Gemara words Pesachim 6a: R' Yehuda said R' Haman: one who sets out to sea or goes in a caravan before thirty days is not obligated to burn; within thirty days he is obligated to burn. Rava said: what you said that before thirty days he is not obligated to burn: we only said when he does not intend to return; but if he intends to return, even from Rosh Hashanah he is obligated to burn. Rashi wrote: even from Rosh Hashanah: for when he returns during Pesach days (this is the view in seif 2 that intent to return means during Pesach) he transgresses, and that time it is not in his possession to nullify for it is forbidden, etc.: If so, his proof from Rashi "that time not in his possession" implies if it were in his possession and he could nullify he could rely on Pesach nullification; and if so, within thirty days he could rely on erev Pesach nullification and we are not concerned he forgets. I saw this explanation printed but it is not clear: this is no proof: even if one may not rely on that nullification from a rabbinic decree lest he forget, nevertheless Rashi's reason is not merely rabbinic decree: Rashi holds even d'oraisa nullification then does not help. Moreover from Rashi's words the opposite of Taz: impossible that one may rely on erev Pesach nullification: otherwise Rashi would not say when he returns during Pesach it is not in his possession to nullify; why must he burn now: let him rely on erev Pesach nullification? Perhaps Magen Avraham intended Bach's words on Semak implying Rashi deals before nullification; Bach says Rashi holds like Ran who said obligated to burn not check: certainly known chametz; and why did R' Yehuda mention known chametz not a house for bedika: therefore without nullification, known chametz to teach even before thirty he need not burn unlike if bedika without nullification lest they eat it: no extra stringency in known over house. Bach's difficulty: R' Yehuda's words are for extra that before thirty they mention without nullification, but Rava only teaches prohibition when intent to return even before thirty: should teach extra even if nullified must burn; from this he proved if nullified even with intent to return need not burn: end Bach. If so Taz may hold similarly: within thirty like before thirty with intent to return: same law must burn; as before thirty with intent to return if nullified need not burn, so within thirty if nullified need not burn. And your question: must nullify at bedika: bedika unnecessary, nullification alone suffices: therefore need not nullify, rely on erev Pesach nullification. Rava: if intent to return must burn, not rely on erev Pesach nullification: different there is definite chametz and concern lest forget erev Pesach nullification; here after bedika only concern lest find fine roll: rely on erev Pesach nullification. If so why did Sages obligate one leaving within thirty to check not nullify: should enact opposite, nullify not check? Answer: better here thereby fulfill all enactments to check and nullify: now at departure he checks and on erev Pesach nullifies, for presumably he will nullify erev Pesach chametz then; unlike if nullifies now he does not fulfill bedika mitzva; also must do as enactment began: check first then nullify; they obligated primary obligation: bedika. Magen Avraham rejected. I say no proof: Taz compared within thirty to before thirty with intent to return; Magen Avraham disagrees: Rashi on before thirty with intent to return: Pesach laws not yet apply, only must burn lest return on Pesach and transgress bal yera'eh; if after nullification need not burn for anyway no transgression; not so within thirty when Pesach obligation already applies: nullification and bedika enactment on erev Pesach apply.

3. And moreover in our Gemara, etc.: meaning according to Magen Avraham's first rejection he agrees to Taz for one leaving before thirty with intent to return if nullified need not burn as Rashi: but according to this rejection Magen Avraham also disagrees: even before thirty with intent to return if nullified: Ra'avad in Ran's name from Yerushalmi: before thirty not obligated to burn only for doubtful chametz, not definite; Ran: our Gemara disagrees: says not obligated to burn not check: implies even known chametz before thirty need not burn; Ran implies no distinction known vs doubt within thirty rabbinic obligation applies, not before thirty; Magen Avraham holds Rashi like our Gemara only definite chametz within thirty must burn, not doubt; therefore Semak's question why establish Rashi before nullification: since for doubt no bedika needed no bal yera'eh, same definite if nullified: must be without nullification; Magen Avraham for us who rule even doubt within thirty even if nullified need bedika; same when checking must nullify per enactment; even before thirty with intent to return even if nullified must check and burn.
