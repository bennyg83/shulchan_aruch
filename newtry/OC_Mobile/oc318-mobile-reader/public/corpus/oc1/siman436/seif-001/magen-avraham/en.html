(א) He must check — night before departure by candle as Beit Yosef siman 433; if forgot at night check by day. Taz wrote then no nullification needed, wait until Pesach and nullify as Rashi — perhaps meaning Rashi: when he returns on Pesach days he transgresses, that time not in his possession to nullify. I say no proof — this refers to "if intent to return even before thirty must burn" — Rashi: if he does not burn and returns on Pesach transgresses, therefore must burn; our Gemara is known chametz must burn from world; Shulchan Aruch ruled like Yerushalmi even doubtful chametz needs bedika; since bedika obligation applies, also nullification per enactment lest fine roll there — cannot rely on Pesach nullification lest forget — as siman 434 s.k. 2.

(ב) He need not check — Sages' enactment does not apply; but if known chametz there he must burn before leaving (Bach).

(ג) We are concerned lest, etc. — you asked: all travelers intending return long before Pesach must check — not difficult: specifically sea or caravan we fear delay until near Pesach; ordinary road not concerned (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 2). If so implies in caravan even long before must check before leaving; nevertheless everything by circumstance — even near road if intent return day or two before Pesach check first lest arrival delayed.

(ד) Storehouse — meaning they used chametz there and afterward wish to place wood or other.

(ה) And afterward gathers in — if did not check before must clear store and check (Maharil); possibly same for caravan within thirty who did not check — return home or send messenger to check.

(ו) He must check — concern lest he clear until less than three tefachim remain covered, then busy and not check — transgress bal yera'eh.

(ז) He need not check — but known chametz must burn as above; although chametz on which collapse fell is as burned siman 432 s.k. 2 — different here he places storehouse on it by hand — as hiding l'chatchila as Semak gloss.

(ח) And wheat storehouse — it appears case where leavened wheat there then placed store over; nevertheless if made before thirty need not burn — although we rule Yerushalmi known chametz must burn even before thirty l'chatchila, b'dieved placing store need not clear — some say even l'chatchila before thirty need not burn; but if placed wheat then leavened from pit moisture even within thirty need not clear — when placed no chametz yet, now like collapse; all the more if doubt leavened; so Rashba — no one clears pit for this.

(ט) Forbidden to benefit — even for those who permit chametz on collapse, here worse: there not intent to clear mound after Pesach; here intends clear pit (Rashba).
