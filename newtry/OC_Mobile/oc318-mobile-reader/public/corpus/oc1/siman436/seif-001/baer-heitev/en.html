(א) In his home — but if he leaves someone to check he need not check — one's agent is like oneself; Chayei Adam in Acharonim not like Bach — see end siman 434.

(ב) He must check — night before departure by candle; if forgot at night check by day. Taz wrote then no nullification, wait until Pesach; Magen Avraham challenged Taz — since bedika obligation applies also nullification; Kaf HaChayim agreed with Taz.

(ג) He need not check — Bach and Magen Avraham: if known chametz must burn before leaving; see Kaf HaChayim.

(ד) Storehouse — meaning they used chametz there and afterward wish wood or other.

(ה) And afterward gathers — if did not check before clear store and check; possibly same caravan within thirty — return or send messenger (Magen Avraham).

(ו) He need not check — but known chametz must burn as s.k. 3.

(ז) Leavened wheat — Taz challenged Rav; Kaf HaChayim reconciled — see there.
