1) [Se'if 1] One who sets out from land to sea. Such a person is called one who sets out because one separates and removes oneself from an inhabited place. See above, at the beginning of siman 248.
2) There. One who sets out from land, etc. This applies when one intends not to return home until after Pesach, or when one intends never to return home. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1.
3) There. And does not leave anyone, etc. If one leaves someone to perform the search, however, one need not search, because a person's agent is legally equivalent to that person. Bach; Peri Chadash; Olas Shabbos, subsection 1; Chok Yaakov, subsection 2; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Chok Yosef, subsection 2; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1; Vilna Gaon. Initially, one should instruct adult men to perform the search. After the fact, however, even women and minors are acceptable, as stated above in siman 432, subsection 24. See there.
