Some say that before thirty days, when there is no need to inspect, that is when he does not intend to return during Passover, even if his intention is to return before or after Passover, he does not need to inspect, since it is before thirty days, . But if his intention is to return during Passover, he must inspect, even from Rosh Hashanah. And if he forgets and does not inspect, he should nullify it when Passover arrives and should not recite a blessing upon nullification. and when Passover arrives, he should nullify it (Also, his wife should inspect and nullify in their house, lest he forget to nullify in the place where he is).
