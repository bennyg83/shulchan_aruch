(א) And some say before thirty, etc. — until here Rambam and "some" = Rashi: intent to return means during Pesach; before Pesach presumption he hurries before 14th — even within thirty no burning as Semak; so Maharam of Milan explains Semak per our poet in section 5 biur chametz — textual error end: within thirty burn in every case whether intent return or not because biur obligation — must read after Pesach; and if intent return during Pesach; if intent return before even within thirty no — contradicts above must burn if intent before; certainly error, read after Pesach, good inverted end; wonder Mechaber opened "some say before thirty when no intent during Pesach" — implies within thirty must check even no intent during; end "since before thirty" — one blow for both — need before thirty in both — not as written; great wonder Rama did not notice.

(ב) But if intent return during Pesach — should bless at bedika since reason bedika he may come during Pesach and transgress bal yera'eh — this bedika now like night 14 eve; first view bedika mere decree lest come bein hashemos erev — no blessing; Maggid Mishneh and Beit Yosef imply no blessing unless checking for biur — even intent during Pesach since bedika not true biur no blessing; wonder: necessarily like night 14 eve — why not bless? Kol Bo many views; Maharach ruled: checking within thirty then true biur — bless; before thirty only fear — no blessing; I say need both: within thirty and intent return during Pesach — then bless.
