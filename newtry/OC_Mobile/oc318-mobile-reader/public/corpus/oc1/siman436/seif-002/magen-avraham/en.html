(א) He nullifies — when he did not come home before Pesach; you asked: above s.k. 1 same — may say teaches: if intended return before Pesach and did not come, with intent come during Pesach — need not send messenger before Pesach to check, nullification alone suffices since he left b'heter.

(ב) He must check — when he comes on Pesach not in his possession to nullify — when seen he transgresses (Rashi) — implies before seen no transgression (Bach); difficult — our Gemara known chametz — even unseen transgresses since in his possession; therefore I say: before seeing one may say perhaps eaten, lost, or Israel sold — like returning lost object siman 443 s.k. 2; but coming home and seeing — transgresses as siman 434.

(ג) Not so if he checked — would have had to nullify at bedika time as above.

(ד) He should not bless — essence of nullification in heart; even thought in heart suffices — no blessing on heart matters.

(ה) Also his wife — you asked: above wrote does not leave checker — how here wife checks? May say: even leaving wife must check before leaving — l'chatchila do not rely on women siman 434 (Bach); I say must instruct appoint one to check; here did not instruct wife — nevertheless she should do as siman 434 Beit Yosef — both laws from one source.
