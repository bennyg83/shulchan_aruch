Even if the shopkeeper made an eruv, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah, and it is the conclusion of the Acharonim in explanation of Tur's words and not as Taz wrote in the name of Beit Yosef; but in truth even though such is Tur's view following his father Rosh's approach — that Rosh does not disagree with what he wrote 'and the words are puzzling in my eyes,' etc., but only with Tosafos' words and not with Rashi's words that they mentioned initially, that it deals with one who enabled all the courtyard residents and also enabled this one — but in truth, were it not for Tur's words I would say that Rosh's puzzlement applies to everything he mentioned earlier, and according to him the mishnah does not deal with enabling at all even together with the other courtyard residents, but the acquisition was only through money alone, per the plain meaning of Rosh's words at the end; but if the shopkeeper enabled him, even if he enabled him anonymously together with the other courtyard residents, it also helps, and one need not force a distinction between this and that which is explained at the end of the seif that enabling through another helps. And in truth such is the view of Beit Yosef and Taz for halacha, only they explained thus in Tur's words, and in Tur's words one cannot explain thus, as the Acharonim proved; requires study for halacha.
