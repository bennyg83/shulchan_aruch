In what manner one acquires the eruv. And it contains one seif: One who gives money
to a baker and said to him: if the courtyard residents come, acquire for them from you a loaf for the eruv — enable me in the eruv that I should have a share with them on account of this perutah — it is not an eruv; even if the shopkeeper made an eruv [for all the others] and also enabled this one] with them. But if he said to him: make an eruv for me — he acquires the eruv; and even if he said 'enable me,' if he gave him a vessel as an act of acquisition through which he should acquire for him — he acquires the eruv. And likewise if the baker enabled him through another, or bought bread from the market with the perutah he gave him — he acquires the eruv. And if he gave a perutah to the homeowner and said to him: enable me in the eruv — he acquires the eruv.
