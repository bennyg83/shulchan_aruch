(On Magen Avraham, seif katan 1) He writes that this requires study in light of Choshen Mishpat. Note, however, that there is no difficulty. Regarding Kiddush over wine, the Sages relied upon the verse, “Remember the Shabbos day to sanctify it,” and it is therefore comparable to a Torah obligation. This is unlike an eruv chatzeros. Tosafos explicitly make this distinction in Shabbos 34a, s.v. Lo Kashya, between an eruv techumin and an eruv chatzeros. See there.
