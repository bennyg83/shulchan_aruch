1) [Se'if 1] One who gives money to a baker, etc. The reason the baker does not transfer ownership when the person gives him a coin and says, 'Acquire a share for me,' is that the baker sells the bread, and the owner of the coin does not acquire it until he performs meshichah. Since he did not tell the baker, 'Establish an eruv for me,' it appears that he wishes to purchase some of the baker's bread with the coin, and he does not acquire it. Beis Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh. These are also the words of the Tur.
