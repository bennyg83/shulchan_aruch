(s.k. 1) And also enabled this one. Since he did not intend, etc. — unlike eiruv chatzerot I did not understand his challenge; but regarding this wording 'since he did not intend,' etc., which is Tur's wording — Tosefta chapter 7 and also HaMeir challenged thus following what Beit Yosef emended in Tur and Rashi, that it was written 'and enabled also this one' — meaning 'and enabled' with a yud (one must say 'and enabled' without a yud, for if 'and enabled' why would it not help — behold money does not acquire; nevertheless he enabled through another as stated at the end of the seif; therefore it must read 'and enabled' with a yud, meaning he said anonymously to another that he should enable all the alleyway residents; for if the baker's intent were to give him free gifts he would enable through another like all the alleyway residents; but since he gave him a perutah the baker's intent is not to give him as a gift but only on account of the perutah, and if so even if he told another to enable all the alleyway residents, neither the baker's intent nor the giver of the perutah's intent was on this giver of the perutah but only on the other alleyway residents; and that which is stated at the end 'and likewise if the baker enabled him through another' means that he said explicitly that he should also enable on account of the giver of the perutah — it helps and he acquires eiruv chatzerot, as he elaborated.
