(1) [Seif 1] One who gives money to a baker, etc. — and the reason the shopkeeper does not enable when he gave him a perutah and said 'enable me' is because the shopkeeper sells the bread and the owner of the money does not acquire it until he pulls it, and since he did not say 'make an eruv for me' it appears he wants to buy from his own on account of the perutah and he does not acquire. Beit Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh's explanation; and such are Tur's words.
