(1) [Levush] And money does not, etc. Magen Avraham challenged: why is this different from one who buys wine for kiddush who acquires with money in Choshen Mishpat siman 199, since it is needed for a mitzvah — behold eruv is also a mitzvah as written siman 366 seif 3, until here. And it appears to me that this mitzvah is not at all comparable to that one; furthermore one can say that here the Rabbis did not enact [money acquisition] since it is possible with 'make an eruv for me.' And behold Taz challenged what is written regarding one who lends a ring to betroth a woman — we are witnesses that his intent is to give it with any effective wording — and the same applies to 'enable me,' until here. And one can say that here it is different, since he gives money and says 'enable me'; and see in Even HaEzer siman 28 seif 14 that we require four methods.
