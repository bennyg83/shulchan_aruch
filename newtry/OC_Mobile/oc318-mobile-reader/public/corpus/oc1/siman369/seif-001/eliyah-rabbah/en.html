[1] [Levush] And money does not, etc. The Magen Avraham asks why this differs from purchasing wine for Kiddush, where money effects acquisition according to Choshen Mishpat siman 199 because it is needed for a mitzvah. An eruv is also a mitzvah, as stated in siman 366, seif 3. It appears to me that the mitzvot are not comparable. Alternatively, here the Sages did not institute acquisition by money because one can simply make an eruv for him. The Taz asks from one who borrows a ring to betroth a woman: we are certain that the lender intends to give every legal quality necessary for the betrothal, and the same should apply when one says, 'acquire it for me.' One can answer that here he gives money and says 'acquire it for me.' See Even HaEzer siman 28, seif 14, where four conditions are required.
