[7] To bend, etc. He should not view the inner faces of the fingers [Rema]. Levush apparently omitted this because he writes in the gloss that the fingers are afterward extended, thereby exposing their inner faces. Piskei Tosafos says to bend the four right-hand fingers into the palm, beneath the thumb, facing the lamp, viewing nails and palm at once; he should not afterward extend the fingers to view their faces. He should intend that, just as he distinguishes the smooth nails from the lined palm, he can distinguish between coins. Merely seeing the distinction between nail and finger-flesh does not suffice.
[8] Also the nails, etc. One Aggadah says that light emerged from between Adam HaRishon's nails. Another says he struck two tiles together, producing fire; he looked at his body and saw himself naked except for his nails [Kol Bo].
