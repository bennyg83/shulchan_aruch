They are accustomed to look, etc. The reason is that we require that one be able to distinguish by its light between one coin and another, as below. Therefore they are accustomed to look at the fingernails to see whether one can benefit from its light and distinguish between one coin and another, just as one distinguishes between nail and flesh. Furthermore, the fingernails are a sign of blessing, for they grow and increase always. They also look at the palms of the hands, for in the lines of the hand there is a sign through which to be blessed.
