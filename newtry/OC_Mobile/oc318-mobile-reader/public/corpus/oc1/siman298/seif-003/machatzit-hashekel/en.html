(Seif katan 3) Over a torch, etc. Because its smell is bad. Meaning, we require a pleasant smell to restore the soul, and this, through its smell, disgusts the soul and pains it. In Sefer Knesses HaGedolah I saw that he wrote a reason for the matter, and this is his language: because it appears that he blesses over the spices because of the bad smell, and it is not recognizable that it is because the soul is pained by the departure of Shabbos, end quote. But the language of Sefer Chassidim does not imply this to one who examines it there.
The Bach wrote that if he said, etc. In Berachos 52 we conclude that what was taught, Beis Shammai say, who created the light of the fire, and Beis Hillel say, who creates the lights of the fire, with regard to created and creates they do not disagree, only with regard to light and lights. Beis Shammai hold that one says light, for there is one light, and Beis Hillel hold lights, for there are several colors in a lamp, red, white, and green. Therefore the Bach wrote that if he said created, he fulfilled the obligation, for regarding this Beis Hillel also agree. Although Tosafot wrote that for this reason Beis Hillel used creates, even though created would also be correct, nevertheless creates is preferable because it is the language of the verse. This is specifically initially, but after the fact, if he already said created, he also fulfilled the obligation and need not go back and bless. But if he said light, he did not fulfill the obligation. The Magen Avraham initially thought that the reason was because he changed from the formula established by the Sages, and regarding this the Magen Avraham found it difficult from siman 167 and 187, etc.
