At the palms. Beis Yosef, in the name of Shibbolei HaLeket, writes: The custom is first to fold one's fingers into the palm, thereby darkening beneath them, and afterward to extend them. Light is thus found in the place of darkness, and one recites the blessing, for this is how one derives benefit from the light. In Shaar HaKavanos it is written that one should turn the backs of the fingers, where the fingernails are, toward the light, without extending the thumb outward. One should not act like those who turn the inner sides of the fingers and their palms toward the light. This accords with what Rema wrote. It appears to me that this is why the custom is to fold the fingers into the palm, as Rema wrote, and then extend them and look at their backs, thereby satisfying both opinions. Magen Avraham.
