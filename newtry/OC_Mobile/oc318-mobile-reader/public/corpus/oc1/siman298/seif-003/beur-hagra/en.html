Seif 3: The practice is, etc. The Zohar implies that this is done only with the fingernails, and so it is in the Pesikta. Yalkut Tehillim 723 cites the verse ‘All my bones,’ etc.: the fingernails are used to perform periah and the melikah of a bird, and with them one looks at the light of Havdalah. This is indeed the practice.
