A Jew from a non-Jew, one recites a blessing. For he recites the blessing over the additional permitted flame.
But a non-Jew. It implies in Ma'agalei Tzedek, siman 18, that even if a non-Jew lit a candle for a sick person from his own bonfire, and the same applies when a non-Jew produced fire from wood and stones on Motzaei Shabbos. But if the non-Jew lit another candle from this one, it is permitted. [ibid.]
We do not recite a blessing. This is a decree because of the first column of flame, meaning that one might come to recite the blessing immediately close to nightfall over a fire that was lit on Shabbos, while the permitted addition had not yet been added. [Rashi] It appears to me that after the fact he fulfilled the obligation with this, since it is only a decree.
