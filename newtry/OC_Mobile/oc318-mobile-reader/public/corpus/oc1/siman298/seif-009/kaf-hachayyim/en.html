9) There, gloss: One who has no cup for Havdalah recites a blessing when he sees fire, etc. The cup was principally instituted for the blessing of Havdalah, just as a cup was instituted for Kiddush. The blessings over fire and spices are arranged over the cup incidentally to the blessing of Havdalah. Therefore, one who has no cup for Havdalah recites the blessing upon seeing fire, because this blessing is unrelated to the blessing of Havdalah and is recited only because fire was first created after Shabbos, as stated in paragraph 1. The blessing over spices restores the soul and is a blessing over benefit, and may therefore likewise be recited without a cup.
