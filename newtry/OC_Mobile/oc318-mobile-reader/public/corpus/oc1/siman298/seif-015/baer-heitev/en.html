By its light. A glass pane is still considered a case in which he sees the flame and uses its light; see Magen Avraham. Mateh Moshe writes: I have seen meticulous people light candles in a dark place, for through this Shaul merited kingship. Tzeidah LaDerech writes that on Motzaei Shabbos every person should light one candle more than on other weekday nights.
