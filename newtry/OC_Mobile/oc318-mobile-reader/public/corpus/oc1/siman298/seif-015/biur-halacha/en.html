Or through a aspaklaria, etc. — aspaklaria is called a brightening device through which flame is seen inside; nevertheless one does not bless — as his view in Beit Yosef; he wondered at Ramban lenient on lamp seeing flame inside — against Yerushalmi strict on aspaklaria; and in Yoreh De'ah 298:5 in his notes it is proven even on small stars one may not rely before "the upper covered and equaled the lower" — per what he explained in his Beurim in s.k. 9 there is no resolution; and what is called "covered" — it appears when redness departs from that place and tends to whiteness from language "his face turned" — not necessarily actual blackening lasting long; all redness departing is included in blackening as Ramban proves; similarly Meiri calls it covered when the glare of light passed. One not expert in "covered" or if day was cloudy should estimate if four mil have passed from start of sunset.
