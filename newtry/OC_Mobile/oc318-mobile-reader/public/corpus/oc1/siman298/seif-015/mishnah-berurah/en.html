A lamp in his lap. This means that it was hidden in his lap so that he does not see the flame and does not use its light. The same applies to a lantern in such a case. Then he adds the case of a glass, that even though he sees the fire through the glass, nevertheless this is not called actual seeing with regard to the blessing Borei me'orei ha'esh, for we require the fire to be exposed. The Magen Avraham and other later authorities wrote that this applies specifically to a lantern of iron, where even if it has holes he does not see the flame directly. But with a glass pane, it is called seeing the flame, and he can bless over it when it is close enough that he can use its light. See Biur Halacha, where we brought that several poskim hold like the view of the Shulchan Aruch; therefore initially it is not worthwhile to be lenient in this.
