15) There: Apart from the lamp designated, etc. This implies that one should not join this lamp with the lamp designated for household use to make a torch. Rather, Havdalah is recited over this single lamp alone. Piskei Tosafos writes the same. Magen Avraham, however, implies that they must be attached. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 6. All this concerns only the choicest manner of performing the mitzvah and is not indispensable, as stated at the beginning of the seif.
