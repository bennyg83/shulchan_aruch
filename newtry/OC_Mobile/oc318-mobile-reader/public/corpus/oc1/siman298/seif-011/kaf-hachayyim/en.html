11) [Seif 2] The choicest manner of performing the mitzvah is to recite the blessing over a torch. The word avukah, torch, is explained like chavukah, bound together, because it contains many pieces of wood and many lights. Beis Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh, chapter 29. Kol Bo writes that since the blessing says Meorei, 'lights,' in the plural, a torch displaying many lights is required. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 5. Nevertheless, even when reciting the blessing over a single lamp, one says Meorei HaEish, because light contains many colors: a red, white, and greenish flame. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 4.
