(Seif katan 11) One does not bless, as a decree because of the first non-Jew and the first column, meaning, etc. This should be the text. Meaning, even over the first lamp it would be proper to bless, for even on Motzaei Shabbos an additional flame and new light are added every moment. If so, one could say that he blesses over the permitted addition. Nevertheless, we decree because of the first column, meaning near nightfall, when no permitted addition has yet been added. And although this is a decree upon a decree, Tosafot wrote in Berachos 53a as follows: it is one decree, for the Sages decreed against any non-Jewish fire in a non-Jew's hand, for if not for this, that would not stand, etc., end quote.
