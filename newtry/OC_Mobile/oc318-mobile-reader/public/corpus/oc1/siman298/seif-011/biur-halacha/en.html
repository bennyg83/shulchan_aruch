Candle in synagogue — a candle made for honor and to illuminate; see Peri Megadim who wrote one does not bless on it; and I found likewise in Terumat haDeshen; however in Rashi it implies he does not hold thus. I was uncertain: a candle lit first for honor and later used to illuminate — see his words in Beit Yosef; he wondered at Ramban who is lenient on a lamp when one sees the flame inside — against Yerushalmi strict on aspaklaria.
