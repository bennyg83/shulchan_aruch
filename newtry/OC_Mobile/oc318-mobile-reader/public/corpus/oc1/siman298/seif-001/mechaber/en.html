The laws of the Havdalah candle. And in it
15 seifim: One blesses on the candle Borei me'orei ha'esh if he has one and need not seek one; this is on Motza'ei Shabbat; but on Motza'ei Yom Kippur some say he must seek one.One without a cup for Havdalah — when he sees fire he blesses on it and likewise on spices (Tur).
