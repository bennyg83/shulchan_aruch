1. The Laws of the Havdalah Candle, 15 Seifim: We say the blessing בורא מאורי האש over the
candle if one has one. And if one does not have a candle, he need not seek one out. This applies to the end of Shabbat. But at the end of Yom Kippur there are those who say that one must seek one out. RAMA: Regarding one who has no cup to make Havdalah, when he sees the light he blesses on it and the spices (Tur). .
