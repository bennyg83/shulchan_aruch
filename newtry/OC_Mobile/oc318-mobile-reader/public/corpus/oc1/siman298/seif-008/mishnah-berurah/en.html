One blesses over it. Because the beginning of the creation of fire was also on Motzaei Shabbos by Adam HaRishon in this manner, that he struck stones against one another. This does not apply on Motzaei Yom Kippur, for then it was not the beginning of its creation. The reason for its blessing is because the whole day it was forbidden for him to use the fire, and now it became permitted. Therefore one should bless only over fire that rested, meaning that it was lit from before the day and rested throughout Yom Kippur, so that they could not use it, and now it became permitted.
