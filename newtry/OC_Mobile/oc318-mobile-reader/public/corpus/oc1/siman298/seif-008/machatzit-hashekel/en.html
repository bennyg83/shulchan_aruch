(Seif katan 8) Fire of idolatry, and specifically, etc. But if a Jew lit, etc. For the flame of idolatry is, according to the law, permitted for benefit, as stated in Beitzah 39a and in Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah, siman 142. The reason one does not bless over it is only because it is attached to an ember of idolatry. Although one does not bless over the ember but over the flame, nevertheless Chazal were stringent with the flame as long as it is attached to the ember, as the Rosh wrote. Therefore, when a Jew lit one lamp from a coal of idolatry, one blesses over it. For the flame of idolatry itself, were it not attached to the ember, would be fit for blessing; and the flame of the Jew's lamp is not attached to the ember of idolatry.
