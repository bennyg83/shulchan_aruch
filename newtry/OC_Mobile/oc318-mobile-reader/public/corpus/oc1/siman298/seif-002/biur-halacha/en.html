And a candle that has two wicks, etc. — see Mishna Berurah in what he wrote that in our candle if several wicks are placed there it is like a thick wick, etc., likewise. Levush and Peri Megadim — and therefore before our glass lamp [called lamp] even though it shines much more than several candles, nevertheless it is only a thick wick and is not considered an avukah; however from Agudah's own words one may say his intent is the opposite: not to say if the light is as large as our lamp this is called avukah literally, but even if the light is small, since it is of two candles it is considered avukah; however from another side it appears one should prevent l'chatchila taking the glass lamp for avukah, for one who takes avukah does so to fulfill the mitzvah from the preferred manner [for by law a single small candle suffices] as in Gemara and Shulchan Aruch, and mitzvah from the preferred manner — it appears to me certainly there is none here, for Shulchan Aruch's view below at end of siman is not to bless on Motzei Shabbat on a candle hidden in aspaklaria, and per plain meaning of Yerushalmi; and although later authorities rule like Rashba who is lenient, nevertheless several Rishonim hold Beit Yosef's view and mitzvah from the preferred manner certainly there is none — and see at end of siman what is written on this.
