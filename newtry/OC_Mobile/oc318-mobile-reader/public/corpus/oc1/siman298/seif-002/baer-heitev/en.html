Over a torch. Specifically one of wax, as stated in Sefer HaKavanos. One should not take a piece of wood called "kien" in the vernacular for Havdalah, because its odor is foul. Sefer Chasidim; see Knesses HaGedolah.
A torch. With a wax candle, if two wicks are braided together they are considered a torch, but if each is separate it is not considered a torch. Magen Avraham.
