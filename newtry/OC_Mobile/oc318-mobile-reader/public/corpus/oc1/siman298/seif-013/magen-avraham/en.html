He does not recite a blessing. For we require that one benefit from its light, as written in seif 4. See the end of siman 69. It is implied here that specifically over the candle he does not recite a blessing, but he says the rest of Havdalah, and so it also appears from the Tur and Mordechai, not like the Rashba. See the responsa of Maharshal, siman 77.
