5) There: Or even seek one. Rosh explains that this blessing is merely a remembrance that fire was created after Shabbos. Were it recited because of the benefit of light, one would have to recite it every time he benefited from light. These are his words, cited by Beis Yosef. If you ask why the Sages did not in fact institute a blessing whenever one benefits from light, as they did for other forms of benefit, since one may not benefit from this world without a blessing, Tosafos, Pesachim 53, answers that blessings were instituted only when the body benefits, and not for other forms of benefit. Although a blessing is recited over sunlight each day, that is because it is renewed daily. Bach.
