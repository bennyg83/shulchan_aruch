(Seif katan 5) Bent, etc. This is the language of the Darchei Moshe: and not to extend, etc. Meaning, when the fingers are bent, he should look at the nails while they are bent and not extend them. Certainly, he should not extend them and look at the inner side of the fingers; but even to extend them and look at the backs of the fingers, namely the place of the nails, he should also not do. And this is the language of the Kavanos, etc.: to turn back the backs of the fingers, namely without bending, but as they are extended. He should look at the backs of the fingers, the place of the nails, only the thumb should be covered beneath the four fingers, as he wrote afterward. And not like those who explain: to turn back, etc. This is as the Rama wrote.
And it seems to me, etc. And afterward he extends them, etc., in order to fulfill both views. Meaning, in order to fulfill the view of the Kavanos, he extends them and looks at their backs. If so, even when he extends them, the thumb must be covered beneath the other fingers, as written in the Kavanos, as above.
