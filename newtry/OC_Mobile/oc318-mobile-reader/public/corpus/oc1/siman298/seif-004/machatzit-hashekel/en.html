(Seif katan 4) Two wicks, etc. Braided together. The same applies if they are joined together even if they are not braided, as he wrote at the end of this seif katan and in siman 433.
We say in the Gemara, chapter Arvei Pesachim, folio 103b: Yaakov bar Abba came to the house of Rava. When Rava came to recite havdalah, Rava's attendant arose and lit, etc.
