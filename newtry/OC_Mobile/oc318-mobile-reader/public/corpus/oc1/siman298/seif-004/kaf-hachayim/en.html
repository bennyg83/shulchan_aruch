4) There. Borei me'orei ha-esh, etc. You find that on Motza'ei Shabbat we mention three creations: borei peri ha-gefen, borei atzei besamim, and borei me'orei ha-esh. They correspond to the three creations in the verse, "And God created the human in His image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them." Abudarham. Yafeh LaLev, ot 1. In siman 78, Sha'ar 18 chapter 24, he wrote that the four blessings of havdalah correspond to Atzilut, Beriah, Yetzirah, Asiyah, and to AV, SAG, MAH, BAN; and in "ha-mavdil" one should intend the separation of the kelipot and the weekday from the holy; see there.
