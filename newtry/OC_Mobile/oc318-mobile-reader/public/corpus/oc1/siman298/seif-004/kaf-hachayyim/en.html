4) There: Borei Meorei HaEish, etc. You will find that after Shabbos we mention three acts of creation: Borei Peri HaGafen, Borei Atzei Besamim, and Borei Meorei HaEish. They correspond to the three acts of creation in the verse, 'God created man in His image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.' Avudraham; Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 1. Pri Etz Chaim, gate 18, chapter 24, writes that the four blessings of Havdalah correspond to Atzilus, Beriah, Yetzirah, and Asiyah, and to the Divine Names with numerical values 72, 63, 45, and 52. During Hamavdil, one should intend the separation of the husks and the mundane from the holy; see there.
