They were sitting, etc. This speaks where they already recited havdalah and did not then have fire near them from which they could benefit, and now it became available to them; or where they know that they will not have a cup at night. But if they expect to have a cup, it is better that they wait with the blessing over the lamp and arrange it over the cup, as I wrote above in seif katan 4.
One blesses for all of them. Although they all need to be silent and hear his blessing, which causes neglect of the beis midrash, and if so it would have been better for each one to bless for himself, nevertheless this is preferable because in a multitude of people is the glory of the King, Gemara. The later authorities wrote that even one who already blessed Borei me'orei ha'esh can bless again to discharge the members of his household.
