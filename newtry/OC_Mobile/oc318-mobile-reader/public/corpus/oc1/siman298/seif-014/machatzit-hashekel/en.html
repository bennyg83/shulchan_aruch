(14) They bless, etc. — because on Motza'ei Shabbat was the beginning of Sages' enactment to bless on light on Motza'ei Shabbat because Adam was created then and took fire; therefore on Shabbat when there is Torah reading and time of ratzon one says V'ani tfilati; not so on Yom Tov without Torah reading — no time of ratzon — V'ani tfilati not applicable.
