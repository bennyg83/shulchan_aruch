(Seif katan 14) We bless, etc. Because he took, etc. Meaning, on Shabbos the original enactment of the Sages was to bless over fire on Motzaei Shabbos because it was created on Motzaei Shabbos, when Adam HaRishon took, and not for any other reason. Granted, the reason that applies on Motzaei Yom Kippur also applies somewhat on Shabbos. Nevertheless, the main enactment was because fire was created on Motzaei Shabbos, and why should the Sages attach it to another reason? Therefore, over fire produced from wood, which is also the beginning of its creation, we bless, since through it one fulfills the main enactment. This is not so on Motzaei Yom Kippur, where the main enactment was to publicize that this day is more sacred than other festivals, for the entire day it was forbidden to use a lamp, and now, on Motzaei Yom Kippur, it became permitted. Besides this, there is no other reason. Therefore, one should not bless over fire now produced from wood and stones, for with this one cannot say that the entire day it was forbidden to use it, since it did not exist at all until now. Likewise in seif 3 he wrote for this reason that one does not bless over a lamp that a Jew lit from a non-Jew, for this lamp that the Jew lit now did not exist on Yom Kippur. Furthermore, the reason that applies on Yom Kippur does not apply to Shabbos at all, according to what he wrote in seif katan 2, that on Motzaei Yom Kippur the reason is to show that this day is more sacred than other festivals, etc. This applies specifically to Yom Kippur, which has some connection to the other festivals, but Shabbos has no connection at all to festivals, and there is no need to show a distinction between them. See Perishah.
