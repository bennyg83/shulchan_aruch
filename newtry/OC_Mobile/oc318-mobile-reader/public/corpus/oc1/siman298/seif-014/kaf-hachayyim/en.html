14) There: To recite the blessing over a torch. One should not use resinous wood called kien in the vernacular, because its odor is foul. Magen Avraham, paragraph 3; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 5; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 4. Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes that this applies when another option is available, implying that if no other option is available, it is permitted. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 3, and Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 2, write the same.
