12) There: To recite the blessing over a torch. The Arizal would kindle a wax candle and recite Borei Meorei HaEish over it. He did not recite Havdalah over the lamp in the house, but over a special torch. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 60b. See Pri Etz Chaim, gate 18, chapter 24, which gives an esoteric reason for a wax candle; see there.
