And saw the light. And he can make use of its light, as written in seif 4. [Maggid Mishneh]
If the majority. This requires study, for let us say that anything fixed is treated as half and half, and it should be a doubt, since we hold that anything fixed is treated as half and half, whether to be lenient or stringent. Perhaps it discusses where he saw one man walking with a candle in his hand, for whatever separates is assumed to have separated from the majority. But the Gemara does not imply this. See siman 217, seif 7. Perhaps this is not called fixed, for the light separated, and similarly the scent of spices. The Gemara says: If one saw, immediately close to nightfall, an adult walking with a candle in his hand, he is certainly a non-Jew, for a Jew would not hurry so much immediately close to nightfall to hold the fire in his hand. If he is a child, one must ask whether he is a non-Jew or a Jew. It requires study why the poskim omitted this. Perhaps they hold that nowadays even adults are not careful in this and kindle immediately close to nightfall.
