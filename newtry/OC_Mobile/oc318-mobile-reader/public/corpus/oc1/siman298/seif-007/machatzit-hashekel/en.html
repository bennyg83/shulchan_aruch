(Seif katan 7) For a postpartum woman, etc. Specifically by means of a non-Jew. But by means of a Jew, since there is no danger, it is forbidden for the Jew himself to desecrate Shabbos. If so, when a Jew lit it, it did not rest from forbidden labor, for this was permitted only by means of a non-Jew, as written in siman 328.
