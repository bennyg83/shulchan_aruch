[א] Three malchuyot, etc. We learn in Berakhot daf 49: R' Yochanan said haTov and haMetiv requires malchut—what does it teach us? But R' Yochanan said once: every berachah without malchut is not called a berachah. R' Zeira said: to teach that it requires two malchuyot besides itself. And Ma'adanei Melech daf 61 wrote: I wonder—for above Abba Yosei and the Rabbis dispute—one says it requires malchut holding haTov vehaMetiv is of the Rabbis, and one says it does not require, etc.; and if so we should also say R' Yochanan teaches us he holds like the Rabbis—until here his words. And one could wonder that Rashba's chiddush there escaped him—who resolves: if so we should say explicitly haTov vehaMetiv is of the Rabbis—for R' Yochanan was an amora and needs explanation, see there. And further it appears to me: if so it is stolen theft—why did he need to teach the halachah is like whoever says it requires malchut and not say it from himself that tannaim dispute? And see above siman 188 seif 3; and know berachot of haTov and haMetiv until haRachaman.
