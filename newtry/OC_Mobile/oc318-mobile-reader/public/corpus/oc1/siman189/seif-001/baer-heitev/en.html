The mourner. And if he blesses for himself he says by himself Torah of man (Magen Avraham). When there is a gentile in the house they are accustomed to say all of us together sons of the covenant. And Taz in Yoreh De'ah wrote it is not correct—for since he says "all of us together" everyone is included; rather he should say "us, sons of the covenant, all of us together." When there is an apostate to gentile worship at the meal place one must say sons of the covenant—for an apostate is not included in the covenant (Magen Avraham there). And in responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 83 he challenged this greatly and wrote one should not say it on his authority.
