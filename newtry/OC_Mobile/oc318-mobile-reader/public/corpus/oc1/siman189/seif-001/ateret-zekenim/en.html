And for the three hatovot—He has done good to us, etc.—and some say one should not say "He has done good, He does good" but rather say together: He has done good, does good, will do good to us—to inform that the three hatovot are equally important, namely: haTov is one, haMetiv is two, and "He has done good, does good, will do good to us" is three. And understand—thus I received from my master my grandfather the gaon Maharar Leib Rofe may his memory be blessed; and so I found afterward in the name of Maharshal; and some say on Shabbat magdol with vav and on weekday magdil with yod (unclear matter); and I received that also on Rosh Chodesh one may say magdol with vav and mention the matter of the month; and Shabbat he calls a reading—meaning as written in the prophets—if so.
