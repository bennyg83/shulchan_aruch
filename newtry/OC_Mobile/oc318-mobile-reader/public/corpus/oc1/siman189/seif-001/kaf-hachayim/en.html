[Seif 1] Fourth berachah, etc. This berachah is not d'oraisa—for the Sages enacted it for the slain of Betar when they were given for burial; therefore it opens with Baruch for it is not adjacent to its companion and does not conclude with Baruch for it is of short coinage. And even though the Midrash says one must say therein three hatovot and three gemulot—nevertheless it does not conclude—for initially they enacted only haTov vehaMetiv and afterward added these matters (Tur). And Rashba gave another reason—that they did not see a seal in it and to make it complete like other berachot of Torah above it; and R' Yonah gave another reason—that everything said in this berachah is like one matter—for all are names of attribute: we say He is King and does good and bestows (Beit Yosef, Perishah sign 3). And see above beginning siman 187. And know berachat haTov concludes immediately when he says "and from all good forever may we lack nothing." And haRachaman, etc., is an addition and is not part of the fourth berachah (Shulchan Aruch siman 189 sign 1, Chayei Adam general 87 sign 1)—that is the nusach of Ashkenazim; and the Sephardi nusach concludes "and relief and salvation and all good." And listeners answer amen here as written Chayei Adam there. And so end of siman 187—see there; and see below sign 14.
