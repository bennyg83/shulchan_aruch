Fourth berachah, etc. In this berachah they enacted Name and malchut separately unlike in the first three—for they are d'oraisa and all are considered as one; and since at the beginning he says Name and malchut he need not in the other two except in berachat Racham it was still fitting to say explicitly malchut of heaven since he requests therein request regarding Shabbat malchut of the house of David—except this is not honor for Heaven to request regarding His malchut, may He be blessed, next to requests of malchut of the house of David. And this fourth berachah is d'rabbanan—they enacted it for the slain of Betar when they were given for burial; therefore it opens with Baruch for it is not adjacent to its companion and does not conclude with Baruch because it is short coinage—they did not enact initially except haTov vehaMetiv and afterward added matters; and therein are three malchuyot: one of itself; two to allude to what was fitting to mention in berachat Racham; and since they enacted for berachat Racham they mentioned another one for berachat haAretz so there be equal elevation for all.
