(in Shulchan Aruch) He has done good to us, He does good to us, He will do good to us—so is the essential nusach, unlike one who says "He has done good, He will do good"; however some wrote to say "He does good to us" first and likewise "He bestows upon us" so there be present, past, and future; but our leaders conduct as Maran, and so in R' Yonah; and indeed in Magen Avraham and Taz they wrote they say "He does good, will do good" all together, etc.; and see above in Ba'er Heitev siman 187 and in Avodat Yisrael wrote in name of Kol Bo to precede "He does good," etc., until "the King"; and so Mordechai in name of Pardes and R' Yonah and Abudraham in name of Rosh; and perhaps there is a scribal error in Tur—see there. And in our nuschaot in siddurim: "He has done good, He will do good to us" and the word "to us" applies to all; and so in short birkat hamazon below, see there siman 192; and so in Sefer Or Chadash on laws of berachot—this nusach from short birkat hamazon of Maharam; and in Amudei Shamayim of the gaon R' Yaakov Emden it is found twice for us like Shulchan Aruch. (א) The mourner—see Ba'er Heitev; and see Eben haEzer who disputes Magen Avraham and holds one does not say sons of the covenant—as also written in Ba'er Heitev; and see Even haEzer siman 171 and what is written in Sefer Beit Meir there.
