(*) In Shulchan Aruch: "He has done good to us, He does good to us, He will do good to us." This is the primary text, excluding one who says only "He has done good, He will do good." Some wrote to say "He does good to us" first, and similarly "He bestows upon us," so that the order is present, past, and future; but our custom follows Maran. So writes Birkei Yosef. However, Magen Avraham and Olat Tamid write that we say "He does good, He will do good" all together. See above in Ba'er Heitev siman 187. Eliyah Rabbah writes in the name of Kol Bo to place "He does good" first through "Hashem reigns," and so write Mordekhai in the name of Pardes, Rabbenu Yonah, and Abudraham in the name of the Rosh; perhaps there is a scribal error in the Tur. In our siddurim the text is "He has done good, He will do good to us," and the word "to us" applies to all. So too in the short Birkat HaMazon below, siman 192, and Or Chadash on Hilkhot Berakhot cites this version from the Birkat HaMazon of Maharan. In Ammudei Shamayim of the Yaavetz the word "to us" appears three times, as in Shulchan Aruch.
(1) The mourner. See Ba'er Heitev. See Even HaOzer, who disagrees with Magen Avraham and holds that one should say "members of the covenant," as Ba'er Heitev also writes. See Even HaEzer siman 171 and what Beit Meir wrote there.
