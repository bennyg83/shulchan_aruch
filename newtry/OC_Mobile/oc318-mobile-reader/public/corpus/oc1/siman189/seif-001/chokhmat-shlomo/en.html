Seif 1—in the fourth berachah do not say titbarach. It appears to me—see in Magen Avraham s.k.1 what he debated whether an apostate is called son of covenant; and see in my works on Even haEzer edition 6 in laws of gittin siman 141 and there in my books siman 100 what I wrote on this and understand; and see in my works on Torah from year 5627 parashat Vayikra what I wrote on this with God's help to prove from the midrash that he is son of covenant—there and understand. However see in my works on Even haEzer edition 5 siman 87 for the community of Odessa there I brought proof an apostate is not called son of covenant; and so in my works on Choshen Mishpat edition 5 siman 213 there also I brought proof for this and understand there with God's help; and see in my works Avodat Avodah on tractate Avodah Zarah in my chiddushim on Avodah Zarah daf 27a on Tosafot s.v. aval be'ishah, etc., there what I expanded on this and understand.
