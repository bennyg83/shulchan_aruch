The formula of the fourth berachah and its law. And it contains 2 seifim: In
the fourth berachah one does not say titbarach nor does one say therein haChai except in a house of mourning. And one says therein three malchuyot: Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, the Heaven, our Father, our King… the King who is good,' and three hatovot: He has done good to us, He does good to us, and He will do good to us. And three gemulot: He has bestowed good upon us, He bestows good upon us, and He will bestow good upon us.
