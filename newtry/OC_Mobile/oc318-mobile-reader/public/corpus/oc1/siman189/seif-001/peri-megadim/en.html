Berachah—Taz: therein are three: one for itself because it is not adjacent for it is d'rabbanan and they enacted it afterward and adjoined it to Boneh Yerushalayim because the horn of Israel was cut off in Betar may He speedily in our days and is destined to restore it in building Yerushalayim; and one for Boneh Yerushalayim—for it was fitting to say therein malchut since he mentioned malchut of the house of David, except it is not fitting to mention there next to malchut of the house of David—they enacted here; and therefore they enacted also for berachat haAretz; and haZan has malchut at its beginning; and even on Yom Tov when one says yaaleh veyavo who says "King gracious"—siman 188 Taz and Magen Avraham 2 do not divide. And that therefore we do not conclude with Baruch, etc.—because in name of Rashba another reason that it should not be superior to berachat haAretz and Boneh Yerushalayim which are short (meaning they do not open and conclude with Baruch but only conclude, etc.); and Mahari wrote another time and see there; and see Levush wrote they have no such conclusion—difficult, see there on this. And know berachat haTov concludes immediately when he says "and from all good may we lack nothing." And haRachaman, etc., is an addition and is not part of the fourth berachah—see Tur and Levush.
