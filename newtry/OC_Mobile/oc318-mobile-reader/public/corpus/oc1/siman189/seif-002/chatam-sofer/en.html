In Orach Chayim Sanhedrin daf 30b. It appears to me that per my humble opinion his terumah is not terumah—for where it does not exist in terumat hamishkan as Magen Avraham wrote above siman 154 s.k.18; and see Rashi and Rambam and Ra'avad beginning tractate Terumot that we derive from there for terumat goren; and see Zevachim daf 45b that Rashi brings another verse to exclude ornament from terumah; and they already settled well in Sefer Taharat haKodesh; and nevertheless the truth is as I wrote; and let his honor understand the difficulty of Eben haEzer here from 82 of Kiddushin—and requires study; and see Rashi's language in Gittin daf 23b s.v. sheteramu, etc., from every man, etc., there; and requires study; and see Sanhedrin daf 47a one who ate chelev, etc.; and requires study; and see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayim siman 176.
