(s.k.2) In a house of mourning, etc. And if he blesses, etc. And in Yoreh De'ah siman 379 Rama wrote he wished to settle the world's custom that they are not careful in this Tosafot in a house of mourning—for they rely on the Tosafot Ketubot daf 5b who wrote one says it only in Yoreh De'ah there; however in the nusach of Tosafot it appears from Rabbeinu Yitzchak that one does not say in a house of mourning three hatovot and three gemulot—and so explicitly in Sefer haArukh; but Bach here and in Yoreh De'ah and Shach there wrote one must say also three gemulot and three hatovot; however also Shach there abbreviated the nusach—rather one must say first three malchuyot and three hatovot and when he reaches "He will do good to us" he says "El of truth, Judge," etc., until "He will fence this breach," etc., for life and peace; and afterward he says three gemulot: He has bestowed, He bestows, etc.; and so Bach there.
