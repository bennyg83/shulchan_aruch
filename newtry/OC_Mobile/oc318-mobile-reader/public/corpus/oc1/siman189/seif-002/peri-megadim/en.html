He wrote in Turei Even siman 34 regarding young men who made a fine not to speak in birkat hamazon—it is fitting to enact until he begins haRachaman "He will make peace," etc.; and siman 183 seif 8 that its law is like tefillah—perhaps likewise the fourth berachah that they not treat it lightly. And even in haRachaman he should not interrupt without need—for Tur said haRachaman is not an interruption between birkat hamazon and poteiach levavot; these weekday words are an interruption—requires study if he did not say three malchuyot or three hatovot and gemulot whether he must return. The world says every day "He will make peace," etc., not "on every day He will make peace." And in Magen Avraham 114:9 and 119:5; and in Magen Avraham siman 188 sign 8—in the first three he returns to the head of birkat hamazon and in haTov vehaMetiv he returns only to haTov vehaMetiv—as stated there in Peri Megadim on this.

Three hatovot and three gemulot—past, present, and future; and some say corresponding to three worlds: world of angels, spheres, and the lower world—for all receive abundance from Him, may He be blessed, and need Him—Rabbi 2 in name of Mateh Moshe, see there on this.

See Yoreh De'ah siman 379 in Be'er HaGolah sign 5 what is impossible in mourners' berachah nowadays in a house of mourning—some say specifically with ten, see there; and what he wrote "with ten"—one could say it; however at circumcision some say it is public and one does not say it; and siman 393 there. And in Magen Avraham siman 193 in law of zimmun whether from this—see there.
