[1] Levush: Likewise, if, etc. The Shulchan Aruch implies that the two cases are not equal. Regarding water ahead of him, he must travel back even the full four mil, provided that it is no more than four mil. Regarding water behind him, he must return only when it is within one mil, but if it is exactly one mil away he need not return even when it is no farther. This is also implied by Pesachim 46a. Levush, however, combines them into one rule, implying that even behind him he must return the full one mil if it is no farther than one mil. This is also implied above in siman 92, seif 4, regarding prayer, as Beit Yosef writes there in the name of Rambam, and the words of Shulchan Aruch there also indicate this. According to this, the Shulchan Aruch appears self-contradictory. See what I wrote there, seif katan 5. It appears that here the Shulchan Aruch rules leniently because Rambam holds that one who wraps his hands in a cloth never requires washing, even when water is available. Although we do not follow him regarding the general law, one should not interpret his ruling stringently regarding the exact distance of one mil, since even according to Rambam no prohibition is committed when one wraps his hands in a cloth. The Shach writes similarly in Yoreh Deah, siman 54, seif katan 9, in the name of Darchei Moshe.
