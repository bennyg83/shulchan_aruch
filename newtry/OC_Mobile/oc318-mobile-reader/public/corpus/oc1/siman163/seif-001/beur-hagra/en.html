Seif 1: If, etc. This is from the third chapter of Pesachim and the ninth chapter of Chullin. The words of all the poskim imply that in such a case it is permitted entirely without washing the hands. The Aruch, however, explains that it is permitted specifically with a cloth.
He should wash, etc. Otherwise, eating with a cloth is forbidden, as stated in chapter Kol HaBasar, Chullin 107b: they permitted, etc. This was permitted specifically for terumah because kohanim are vigilant, but not for foods prepared in purity, and all the more so not for ordinary food. Rabbeinu Yonah, the Rosh, and the other poskim disagree with Rambam, who permits it for ordinary food. See Tosafos, Yoma 79b, s.v. ha, etc., “one may say,” etc., and there, s.v. natlu, etc. It appears that Rashi holds that it concerned ordinary food.
Or he eats with a spoon. The Tur writes this as a prohibition, as stated in seif 2. This requires study.
