Mil. If there is uncertainty whether he will find water within four mil, it is possible to rely on Rambam, who permits eating with a cloth even when one has water. One who travels in a wagon among non-Jews journeying as a group, and is afraid to travel alone, is considered to be in pressing circumstances even within four mil. Maharshal, responsa, siman 94. If he has water that is invalid for washing according to all opinions, he should wash with it without a blessing, and we are not concerned that people may mistakenly conclude that such water is valid.
