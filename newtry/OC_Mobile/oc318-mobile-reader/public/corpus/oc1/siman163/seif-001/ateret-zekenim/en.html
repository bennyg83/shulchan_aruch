If water is not available within four mil ahead of him. Some say that one traveling in a wagon among non-Jews who travel as a group, and who is afraid to go alone, is considered to be in pressing circumstances even within four mil. One who is stringent will be blessed. Maharshal, responsa, siman 94. Rambam permits one to wrap his hands in a cloth even where water is available, for they prohibited this only regarding one who eats ordinary food in a state of ritual purity.
