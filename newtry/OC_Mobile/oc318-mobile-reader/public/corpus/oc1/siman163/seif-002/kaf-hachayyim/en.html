2) There: More than four mil, etc. If one is uncertain whether he will find water within four mil, it may be possible to rely upon the Rambam, who permits eating through a cloth even when water is available. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1; Derech HaChaim, paragraph 8; Chayei Adam, klal 40, paragraph 11. Likewise, if one travels with a caravan and fears separating from them to search for water, he may eat through a cloth. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 40, paragraph 14. See above, siman 158, seif 8, and what we wrote there in paragraph 71.
