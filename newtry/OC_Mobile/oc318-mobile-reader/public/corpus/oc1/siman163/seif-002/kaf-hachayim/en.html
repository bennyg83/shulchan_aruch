(ב) There — more than four mil, etc. And if he is in doubt whether he will find water within four mil, it is possible to rely on the Rambam who permits eating with a cloth even if he has water. Magen Avraham seif alef; Eshel Avraham letter bet; Razhu letter alef; Darkhei Chaim letter chet; Klal mem letter yud alef. And likewise if he travels in a caravan and fears to separate from them to search for water — he may eat by means of a cloth. Abbreviated Shulchan Aruch siman 40 letter yud dalet. See above siman 158 seif 8 and in our words there letter ayin alef. The Mateh Yehonatan on siman 161 in the name of responsa Rashal siman 94; see later siman 164 letter alef, and examine.
