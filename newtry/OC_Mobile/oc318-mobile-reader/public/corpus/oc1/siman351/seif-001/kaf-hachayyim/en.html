1) [Se'if 1] One may not stand in a public domain and bring his hand near the drainage channel, etc. Their roofs were flat and coated with plaster, and the roofs had many small gutters to drain the water. Beneath them they would place a board running along the length of the wall at an incline. All the gutters discharged onto it, and it conveyed all the water to one place so that it would not damage the wall. Rashi. This board placed along the length of the wall, onto which the gutters discharged, is called a drainage channel because the water flows along it and it conveys the water to one place.
