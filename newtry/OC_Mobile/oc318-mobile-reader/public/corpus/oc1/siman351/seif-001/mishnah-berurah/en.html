(א) and he joins his hand — and the same applies to his mouth or a vessel:

(ב) to the mezachilah — and all the more so to collect water from on top of the wall, for it is forbidden:

(ג) within 3 tefachim — that is, the mezachilah is placed adjacent to the roof within 3 tefachim and we say lavud and there is roof upon it and it is like taking out from the roof to reshus harabbim, even if the mezachilah is not 4 tefachim wide:

(ד) from the air — the water descending from the mezachilah or from the roof; for air above 10 is a makom patur, and if it is below 10 it is reshus harabbim and his hand is also in reshus harabbim:

(ה) within 3 of the mezachilah — for we do not say lavud here, and also because it is not uprooting from a 4 by 4 place:

(ו) if the mezachilah etc. — mezachilah is called the pipe placed along the length of the roof, and tzinor is called when it stands at one side of the roof and its end protrudes downward greatly so water will not fall on the wall:

(ז) he may join his hand — since it protrudes 3 it is no longer considered like the roof:

(ח) that they do not have etc. — for it is considered a makom patur:

(ט) they have etc. — for it is like karmelit:

(י) even if they do not have etc. and they are above etc. — for we decree lest they have 4 by 4 width and become reshus hayachid and be forbidden:
