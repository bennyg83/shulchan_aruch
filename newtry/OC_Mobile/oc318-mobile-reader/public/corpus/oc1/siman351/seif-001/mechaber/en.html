The law of one who extends his hand to a pipe in the public domain to drink. And in it
is 1 seif: A person should not stand in' reshus harabbim and join his hand to the mezachilah, which is a long pipe placed along the length of the roof within 3 tefachim adjacent to the roof, to receive water from it; since it is within 3 adjacent to the roof it is considered like the roof and is forbidden whether it is above 10 or below 10; however it is permitted to collect from the air even if his hand is within 3 of the mezachilah. If the mezachilah protrudes 3 from the roof, and likewise an unspecified pipe that protrudes 3 — he may join' his hand to them and receive water, such as when they are not 4 by 4 and they are below 10; however if they are 4 by 4, or even if they are not 4 by 4 but are above 10' — it is forbidden:
