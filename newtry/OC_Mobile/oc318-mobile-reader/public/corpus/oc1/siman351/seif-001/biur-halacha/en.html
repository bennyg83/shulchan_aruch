(א) within 3 tefachim — see in Mishna Berurah that it is like taking out from reshus hayachid to reshus harabbim; see in Beit Yosef who brought in the name of Rambam that nevertheless there is only a prohibition and not liability, for the water does not rest there since it flows and goes; and for Rashi there is a d'oraisa prohibition; see in Bigdei Yeshua who questions this and his view is that also for Rashi there is no chatat liability in this:

(ב) or below 10 — and even though according to Rambam it is permitted below 10, the Shulchan Aruch did not bring his view, for the view of Rashi and Tosafos and Rosh and Rashba and Ritva is to be stringent in this:

(ג) within 3 of the mezachilah — see in Taz who brought the view of Rabbeinu Yehonatan that it is forbidden in this; see in Biur HaGra who brought a source for the words of the Shulchan Aruch from the reading of Rabeinu Chananel and I also found in Ahavat Chesed explicitly that he is lenient in this, and likewise from the wording of Ritva and Rabbeinu Yeruham it is implied that we forbid only if he specifically touches the mouth of the mezachilah — see there; therefore one who relies on the Tosafos Yom Tov in this certainly did not lose:

(ד) even if they are not 4 by 4 and they are above 10 it is forbidden — see in Mishna Berurah that we decree on account of a place that has 4 by 4; see in Beit Yosef that according to Rambam it is permitted in this above 10 since it is distant 3 from the roof; however the Shulchan Aruch follows his reasoning that he ruled per the approach of Rashi and Tosafos who are stringent in this as above; and at first glance it is difficult per the words of the Shulchan Aruch — why do we not decree in every place on a post that does not have 4 by 4 standing in reshus harabbim to forbid carrying from it to reshus harabbim because of the decree that it has 4 by 4; and it is possible to say that here we are more concerned because below there are no partitions and they will come to be lenient in this:
