(1) [seif 1] A person should not stand in reshus harabbim and join his hand to the mezachilah etc. Their roofs were divided and they coated with clay, and there were many small gutters on the roof to remove water, and they place below them a board along the length of the wall on a slope, and all the gutters pour onto it and it brings all the water to one place so as not to damage the wall — Rashi; and this board placed along the length of the wall onto which the gutters pour is called mezachilah because it flows and conducts the water to one place:
