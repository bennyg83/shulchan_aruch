"Did not undergo brit milah." Regarding a slave who follows Jewish practice but has not yet undergone brit milah and immersed before a beit din, whether he may be buried on the second day of Yom Tov and whether he must be exhumed if he was already buried in a Jewish cemetery, see Responsa D.A., siman 19. "Complete." See Responsa Beit Yehudah, siman 13.
