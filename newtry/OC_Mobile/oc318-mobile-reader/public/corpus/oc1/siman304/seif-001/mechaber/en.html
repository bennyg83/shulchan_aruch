On which slave one is commanded regarding his rest. And in it 3 seifim: A person is commanded regarding his slave's
rest — one who was circumcised and immersed for the sake of slavery and accepted upon himself the mitzvot customary for a Hebrew slave; but if he did not circumcise and immerse but only accepted upon himself the seven Noahide mitzvot — he is like a ger toshav and permitted to do melacha on Shabbat for himself, but not for his master; and it is forbidden for any Jew to tell him to do melacha on Shabbat for a Jew's need — even one who is not his master. If he did not accept any mitzvah but is still a complete gentile — his law is equal to one who accepted the seven mitzvot; according to this, needs of a sick person without danger — we hold one tells a gentile and he does; likewise a corpse on the first day of Yom Tov — we hold gentiles may attend to it — forbidden to tell a Jewish slave even if he is a gentile, since a slave's melacha is forbidden d'oraisa — it was not permitted in a matter without pikuach nefesh; and some dispute and permit this; nevertheless, if he was doing his master's melacha without his knowledge and it is evident he does not do it knowingly — permitted, and one need not stop him (even if he accepted the seven mitzvot); and for another Jew who is not his master — even if he does it knowingly for the Jew — permitted, as long as there is no Jew's verbal command; provided the Jew does not benefit on Shabbat from that melacha; and some say whoever did not accept the seven Noahide mitzvot — since he is a complete gentile — his master is not commanded regarding his rest; according to this, needs of a sick person without danger and needs of the dead on the first day of Yom Tov — permitted to tell them to do it.(And every slave regarding whose rest one is commanded — forbidden to go out with a seal his master made to show he is his slave; if of clay — permitted to go out with it when hung on his neck; but not on his garment; and of metal in any manner forbidden (Tur); if the slave made the seal for himself — even of clay in any manner forbidden (Magid ch. 9).) ולישראל אחר שאינו רבו אפי' עושה לדעת ישראל מותר כל שאין שם אמירת ישראל ובלבד שלא יהנה ישראל בשבת מאותה מלאכה וי"א שכל שלא קבל עליו שבע מצות בני נח כיון דעכו"ם גמור הוא אין רבו מוזהר עליו ולפי זה צרכי חולה שאין בו סכנה וכן צרכי מתים ביום טוב ראשון מותר לומר להם לעשותו: הגה וכל עבד שמצווה על שביתתו אסור לצאת בחותם שעשה לו רבו להראות בו שהוא עבדו ואם הוא של טיט מותר לצאת בו כשתלוי בצוארו אבל לא בכסותו ובשל מתכת בכל ענין אסור (טור) ואם העבד עשה החותם לעצמו אפי' בשל טיט בכל ענין אסור (המגיד פי"ט.
