Which slave one is commanded to rest on Shabbos. It contains 3 seifim: A person is commanded concerning the Shabbos rest of his slave who underwent brit milah and immersion for the sake of slavery and accepted upon himself the mitzvot that apply to a slave. If, however, he did not undergo brit milah and immersion but accepted upon himself the 7 Noahide mitzvot, he is like a ger toshav. He may perform melacha on Shabbos for himself, but not for his master, and every Jew is forbidden to instruct him to perform melacha on Shabbos for the needs of a Jew, even one who is not his master. If he did not accept upon himself even the 7 Noahide mitzvot, some authorities hold that the same restrictions apply to him, while others permit another Jew who is not his master to instruct him in circumstances in which instruction of such a slave is permitted. {Rama: In any event, the master must not permit his slave to perform melacha for the master's benefit, and if the slave acts on his own initiative the distinctions stated by the authorities regarding benefit and protest apply.}
