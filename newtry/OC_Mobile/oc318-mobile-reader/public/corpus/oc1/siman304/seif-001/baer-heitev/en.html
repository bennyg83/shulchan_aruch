(א) His slave's rest — like his animal's rest; plainly forbidden to tell a mumar to do melacha for him, likewise a Sadducee forbidden; see Magen Avraham.

(ב) For himself — e.g. sewing his garments and the like.

(ג) For his master — even rabbinic melacha (Rashbash part 1 siman 47; see there siman 49); Yad Aharon challenged him — see there.

(ד) Not on his garment — lest it break off, he folds garment and places on shoulder so master not see — similar to folded tallit liable for chatat (Tur).

(ה) Metal — since important concern lest fall and carry; Rambam implies we don't accept slave and ger toshav nowadays; maidservants though acquired permanent acquisition — if they wish enter Ishmaelite religion they go free — perhaps our law like hired gentile — needs study; see Taz and Magen Avraham where no permission per their law to annul themselves by converting — absolute prohibition do melacha Shabbat for Jew; see Kenesset HaGedolah.
