(s.k. 1) On his slave etc. — two verses written: one "that your slave and maidservant rest like you" — refers slave circumcised immersed for slavery accepted all slave mitzvot i.e. all woman obligated; Shabbat included — forbidden melacha Shabbat even for himself; second verse "son of your maidservant and ger refresh" — Sages expounded gentile slave acquired body ownership not circumcised immersed only accepted seven Noahide mitzvot — forbidden all Israel command melacha Shabbat; for himself permitted since Shabbat not in seven mitzvot; slave acquired body not circumcised immersed accepted no mitzvah even seven — poskim dispute if commanded on his rest as will explain.
