(א) A person etc. — to ease reader knowing reasons of laws in this siman I precede small introduction per Gemara and poskim. Two verses: one "that your slave and maidservant rest like you" — slave circumcised immersed for slavery accepted all slave mitzvot i.e. all woman obligated — Shabbat included — forbidden melacha Shabbat; verse teaches slave's rest also on master commanded prevent melacha even for slave's need; second "son of your maidservant and ger refresh" — Sages: gentile slave body-acquired to Israel not circumcised only seven Noahide mitzvot — master must prevent melacha for him Shabbat; forbidden all Israel command melacha for them Shabbat; for himself permitted — Shabbat not in seven mitzvot — law like ger toshav not warned Shabbat; nevertheless forbidden d'oraisa Israel command melacha for him; slave body-acquired not circumcised not accepted even seven — poskim dispute if commanded on rest — as will explain.

(ב) On slave's rest — master commanded guard prevent melacha even for himself as above; permitted rent slave to gentile weekdays if stipulates no Shabbat melacha — unlike animal siman 246 seif 3 forbidden rent gentile such case — gentile not trusted; here gentile won't act against slave will — slave trusted (Peri Megadim).

(ג) Customary for slave — all mitzvot woman obligated.

(ד) He is like ger toshav — ger toshav: accepted seven Noahide mitzvot and not serve idols even in partnership.

(ה) For himself — his own melacha e.g. sewing garments repairing shoes or earning so sustenance beyond master's provision.

(ו) But not for master — even if master didn't explicitly command melacha forbidden — presumably on master's intent — Torah warned prevent as "son of your maidservant refresh" as note 1; even rabbinic melacha forbidden for master; some lenient if master didn't explicitly warn since only rabbinic.

(ז) Forbidden all Israel — d'oraisa since accepted seven mitzvot all agree law like ger toshav — Torah forbade Israel melacha through him.

(ח) If didn't accept — meaning likewise if accepted no mitzvah — since Jewish slave body-acquired to him this view law equal accept seven mitzvot — forbidden melacha for any Israel as above.

(ט) Tell Jewish slave — specifically telling plain gentile — only shevut permitted these; Jewish gentile slave warned d'oraisa — no heter except pikuach nefesh.

(י) Some dispute and permit — when slave complete gentile yet first view equal accept seven mitzvot — forbidden even other Israel tell him melacha; they dispute Torah only forbade melacha for master as "son of your maidservish refresh"; other Israel no worse than other gentile — rabbinic only — therefore sick without danger permitted.

(כ) If he was doing etc. — though commanded slave's rest whether accepted seven or not not do for him — specifically when pleasing him in this melacha — then presumably on his own intent — must stop; when evident not on intent — like his own melacha — not obligated prevent.

(ל) Other Israel etc. — only by master presumed all he does on master; here even knowingly for Israel — as long as didn't command and slave or ger toshav begins himself — presumed for himself to profit — not forbidden unless commanded as note 7.

(מ) Permitted — see siman 252 end seif 2 sometimes must protest rabbinically.

(נ) Provided not benefit — rabbinically — slave not better than other non-Jew doing knowingly for Israel — Israel forbidden benefit that melacha Shabbat as start siman 276.

(ס) Some say all etc. — hold Torah didn't warn Jewish slave Shabbat only if accepted some mitzvot i.e. seven as Beit Yosef see there; see Avnei Nezer brought several poskim practice stringent first view; forbidden tell mumar and Karaites melacha Shabbat and Yom Tov — violates lifnei iver (Peri Megadim).

(ע) Every slave etc. — if also slave not circumcised immersed see Tosafot and Peri Megadim.

(פ) Forbidden go out etc. — first stam then explained if two stringencies i.e. clay seal hung on neck permitted — why concern carry in hand — certainly no concern fears master say removed to show free; why carry; no concern break off carry afterward home — broken clay seal useless; if to show master subjugation holding slavery seal not sign of slavery except hung on neck or garment; if one stringency hung on garment or metal important — forbidden public domain as below.

(צ) Not on garment — lest break fears master say removed show free folds tallit hide seal place carries on shoulder like burden.

(ק) And of metal etc. — important master careful not lose — concern break carry four amot public domain.

(ר) In any manner — even hung on neck — since made for himself not afraid master when removes — concern take in hand carry public domain.
