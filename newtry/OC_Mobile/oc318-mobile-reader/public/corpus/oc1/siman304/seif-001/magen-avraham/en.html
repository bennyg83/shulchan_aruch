(א) On his slave's rest — when slave does melacha master transgresses like animal's rest (Magen Avraham); see siman 246 seif 3.

(ב) For himself — i.e. sewing garments, repairing shoes, or earning his sustenance (Beit Yosef Kol Bo).

(ג) For his master — even rabbinic melacha (Rashbash part 1 seif 4; see there siman 49).

(ד) Forbidden for all Israel — i.e. d'oraisa (Rashi 307); as he concludes since slave's melacha forbidden d'oraisa — likewise explicitly Beit Yosef in name Maggid Mishneh; Eshel Avraham not precise.

(ה) And some dispute — hold like one view end seif Torah didn't forbid; needs study why Rabbenu Yonatan doubled; Eshel Avraham wrote on this but didn't find source.

(ו) Need not stop him — minority like siman 343.

(ז) Permitted etc. — see Ran 52b sometimes must protest.

(ח) That Jew not benefit — i.e. rabbinically as Rashi Ra'avad; see Ran 52b forbidden tell Sadducee melacha Yom Tov (see there 334, Tosefta 161); Yoreh Deah siman 159; plainly mumar law like Israel forbidden tell him melacha.

(ט) Permitted go out with it — no concern bring in hand — fears master.

(י) Not on garment — when breaks folds garment on shoulder so master not see as siman 301 s.k. 29.

(כ) In any manner forbidden — concern bring in hand — not afraid master since made for himself. Nowadays king decreed Jew not buy slave — slaves can do melacha like hired (Rashdam Yoreh Deah 295, Ribash seif 1 and 3); some dispute hold body still acquired; now pay tax for slaves even per Rashdam body acquired even lenient (thus).
