(א) If master said etc. — if doesn't want feed him slave may go begging — authority in his hand — slave does for himself.
(ב) Since stipulated from outset — unlike stipulating on Shabbat forbidden; if master said nothing slave does himself wants feed — Eliyahu Rabbah in name Sefer Masat Binyamin permitted since Israel doesn't tell him do it; Levush beginning this siman implies not lenient.
(ג) He does etc. — even in master's house since discreet as concludes; all the more earns from non-Jew; not for other Israel even discreet — still Jewish slave.
