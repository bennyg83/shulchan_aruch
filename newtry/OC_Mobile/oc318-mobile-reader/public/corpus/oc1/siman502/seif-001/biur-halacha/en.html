One may not produce fire, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah regarding b'dieved that it is permitted to use them; so is implied from Maharam and so wrote Radbaz in name of Rashba, and so agreed the Acharonim Bach, Magen Avraham, Rabbi Akiva Eiger, and other Acharonim — not like Taz who is stringent in this, and all the more so one may not forbid food cooked in this fire. Regarding the essence of the prohibition whether it is d'oraisa or d'rabbanan — Taz's view is d'oraisa, and from Ra'avad's words it is implied d'rabbanan, for he wrote it is similar to melacha; see responsum Ksav Sofer siman 68 where he expands on this.
