(sk 1) One does not produce, etc. — in the Gemara 33: they said, what is the reason? Because he generates on Yom Tov. R' Nissim of Gerona wrote: and it is comparable to melacha that creates this fire on Yom Tov — end quote. And in Rambam's composition he wrote: only kindling from existing fire was permitted on Yom Tov, but generating fire is forbidden, for it is possible to obtain it from erev Yom Tov. Ra'avad wrote: he should say because he generates, and there is no preparation, and that is the reason the Gemara explains; and wrote on this: likewise per the text Taz brought, I do not see he wished to grasp, for this is not like an egg, etc., as Magen Avraham concluded; however in Magen Avraham itself I saw the text "I see he wished," etc., and so Lechem Mishneh brought the text there, which is more correct, meaning Ra'avad's view that the reason the Gemara gave is because he generates and lacks preparation, and it is like an egg born on Yom Tov; for this reason possibly even b'dieved it is forbidden like an egg, and Maggid Mishneh answered his challenge that this is not like an egg, for Sages did not forbid muktzeh and nolad except for eating, but fire itself is not eaten — such fire Sages did not forbid, therefore Rambam had to give the reason it was possible from erev Yom Tov; and this is the Gemara's explanation: because he generates and it should have been done erev Yom Tov, and only existing fire was permitted.
