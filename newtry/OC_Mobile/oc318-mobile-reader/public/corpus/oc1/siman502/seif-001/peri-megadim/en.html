In my opinion somewhat, since Rashi zl Beitzah 33a in the Mishnah "one does not produce light" — because he generates, and in the Gemara 33b generates on Yom Tov, and he did not write an explanation in the Gemara as he usually explains, because the Gemara is like R' Shimon here that nolad is permitted on Yom Tov, and therefore "one does not produce" — like Rambam chapter 4 law 1 that we prepare from erev Yom Tov, and therefore he mentioned the word "on Yom Tov" there; but Rashi, even though he rules there s.v. and the halachah is like R' Shimon in muktzeh in nolad, one can say he holds like R' Yehudah on Yom Tov (see Tur 495) — therefore he said generating alone, meaning nolad, and added "and one does not produce" because it continues like one reason, as one does not winnow in a courtyard because of muktzeh — this is nolad and muktzeh; for Rambam they are unrelated — see Peri Megadim 2 that on Shabbat tithed produce is forbidden b'dieved, not on Yom Tov — see there, and it will be explained in 503 regarding cooking from Yom Tov to weekday; and one can say there it is rabbinic "since," while here producing fire is for Taz, and in our novellae on Beitzah we expanded that apparently for Rambam preparing we do not hold like R' Yehudah — see there, and see Eliyah Rabbah that it appears like melacha creating fire on Yom Tov — from rabbinic lack of preparation and nolad, only it appears like melacha creating fire on Yom Tov, and complete melacha is absent, for one strikes iron on stone, etc., and sparks emerge — the kindling is not the main act, only it appears, etc., and the Mishnah's explanation would be one does not produce from stone because it appears, and all the more so to kindle from it tinder from water, etc. [continues per source]:
