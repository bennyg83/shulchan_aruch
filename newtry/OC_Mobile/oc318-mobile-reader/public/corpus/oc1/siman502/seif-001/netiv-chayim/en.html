(Magen Avraham note 9) To me it requires study — on the contrary, etc. It appears to me: that which he does not challenge the Rosh — because he explains below in siman 632 that the Rosh's intention is that we certainly say lavud even to be stringent, that it is considered as if both are adjacent to one another; nevertheless we do not say lavud to be stringent that invalid sechach should be mixed enclosed — see there. I have great difficulty with this, for it is explicit regarding this that the Rosh is uncertain if there is invalid sechach two handbreadths from here and from there and air less than three interrupts — whether the invalid sechach combines or not; his uncertainty is difficult, for it is obvious they combine since we say lavud — only that the Rosh and Tur hold that even for both to be adjacent through lavud we do not say lavud to be stringent. But it appears possible to resolve Magen Avraham's difficulty: for why did the Talmud not reject it there? This case is different — we do not say lavud to be stringent because there is still a teaching from the end there: we do not say lavud in the middle to be lenient, as it taught there part of the impurity is in the house and part opposite the window — the house is impure and opposite the impurity impure; why not say lavud and what is above it would be pure opposite the impurity? Therefore it rejected: impurity is different. And further it appears that certainly from the side we say lavud even to be stringent; only in the middle the Rosh ruled we do not say lavud to be stringent — for that which we say lavud in the middle is because we rule halachah like Ravina to be lenient, not so if lavud to be stringent the halachah is like Rav Acha who is lenient in such a case; and I once found so in Semag siman 300 — see there. All this for us; but for the one who holds we say lavud in the middle — for him we say lavud both to be lenient and stringent; and for the one who does not say lavud at all — he also does not distinguish between lenient and stringent; therefore the Talmud rejected: impurity is different, its halachah is learned. With this Magen Avraham's difficulty from that which is evident in Eruvin 9 regarding lechi is resolved — we come to the dispute of Rashbag and Rabbanan who say lavud to be stringent — for Rashbag it is lavud from the side. From this it is evident like Beit Yosef's words when the table reaches near the ground, and not as Darkei Moshe challenged him that from the side it is evident we say lavud even to be stringent — with this the complaint of responsum Chacham Tzvi siman 59 is resolved, as follows: and not because I am concerned for what Rama wrote in siman 571 Yoreh De'ah that we do not say lavud to be stringent — in the writer's apology the Talmud was omitted in Eruvin 25: one who distances from the wall less less than three did not help, and Rashi explained it is like a partition on a partition — behold we say lavud even to be stringent — see there. I say: why did he abandon that Eruvin 9 regarding lechi and not raise an objection from it? Because one can say as I wrote that from the side we say lavud even to be stringent, but in the middle we do not say. If so, in that of making pasin too, since it is from the side we say lavud even to be stringent — resolve clearly.
