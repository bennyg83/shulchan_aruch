4) There. Nor from earth. This refers to hard ground that emits fire when dug, similar to our gargishta from the place where it is quarried. Rashi, Beitzah 33a. Ran explains: 'Dung of cattle or sheep is buried in the earth, and when it decomposes, fire emerges from it.' These are his words. This refers to material that emits sparks, beside which some soft substance is placed so that it catches fire from them.
