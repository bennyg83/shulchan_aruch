[4] [Levush] For the fire is not fit, etc. And if you say, how is this different from fragments of a vessel in siman 501, se'if 6, which likewise are fit only to cook with them: one may say that nevertheless one benefits from those [shards] themselves for cooking, for they diminish and go [as fuel] in the cooking before him; but regarding fire, one does not need the newly generated fire itself, but rather that it kindle the wood for heating, even though [the fire] will be as at the beginning (Lechem Mishneh).
