For us permitted to cook in pots, etc. In the Gemara: we do not glaze tiles to roast — what is he doing? R' Yochanan: here with new tiles that need sealing — meaning to harden them and it becomes a vessel; they said further: placing the pot on Shabbat one is liable for libun of tiles — meaning due to this decree it becomes a vessel through first kindling; Tosafot wrote some are careful not to cook initially on Yom Tov in new pot — not so, here with empty pot then libun of tiles applies before bringing water, but when cooking food in it libun of tiles does not apply; R' Yitzchak wrote Taz: it appears in our pots whose entire repair is finished in potter's kiln it is not forbidden; so is public custom; Beit Yosef: custom today is to cook in new pots on Yom Tov without doubt — end quote; therefore it appears although Rama wrote our custom to cook in new before Yom Tov, nevertheless in pressing need if he forgot before Yom Tov or found pots only on Yom Tov one may rely on those who permit cooking on Yom Tov in new pots.
