(א) Prohibitions. And all the groceries that Pinker Glasser holds water in the first oral good cooking so they can be cooked in a ovarian school:

(ב) Pressing need. That he has nothing to eat — Maggid Mishneh, Beit Yosef, Shiltei Gibborim. Taz wrote that if he forgot to cook before Yom Tov or found pots only on Yom Tov, one may rely on those who permit cooking in new pots on Yom Tov.

(ג) On Pesach. For on other Yom Tov days most use is in glazed vessels, but on Pesach one does not use glazed vessels as in siman 451, rather uncoated vessels; therefore one cooks water in them before Yom Tov. But in truth there is no difference between Pesach and other Yom Tov — Magen Avraham. People are not careful to kindle on Yom Tov with a new tube (shofarot), and they do not act properly.
