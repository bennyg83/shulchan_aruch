Constraint. That is, when one has nothing to eat (Hagahos Maimoniyos; Beis Yosef; Yam Shel Shlomo); and Taz wrote that if one forgot to cook it before Yom Tov, or found pots only on Yom Tov, one may rely on those who permit cooking on Yom Tov in new ones.
On Pesach. For on other Yomim Tovim most of their use is with glazed vessels called glaziert; but on Pesach they do not use glazed vessels, as written in siman 451, but rather vessels that are not coated; therefore they cook water in them before Yom Tov. But in truth there is no difference between Pesach and other Yomim Tovim (Magen Avraham). And people are not careful about lighting on Yom Tov the tobacco with a new tube, and they do not act properly.
