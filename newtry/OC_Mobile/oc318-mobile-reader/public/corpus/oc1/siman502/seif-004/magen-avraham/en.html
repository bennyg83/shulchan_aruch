This is the language of Darkei Moshe: it seems to me that in our place they practiced a prohibition, for it is a widespread custom with all pots that they buy for Pesach that they cook water in them before Pesach; end quote. And in Maharil, Laws of Pesach, he wrote that it is forbidden to bury [food to keep warm] in a new pot; end quote. And in truth, on other Yomim Tovim people are not careful and they cook in a new pot; and it appears to me that the reason is as the Rosh wrote: that they are sealed up, for we see that they sweat [leak], and they do not hold water until one has cooked in them once; end quote. And it is known that coated vessels called glaziert hold water well even before cooking; therefore it is permitted to cook in them on Yom Tov from the start, for most of their use is with glazed vessels; but on Pesach, when they do not use glazed vessels, as written in siman 451, but rather vessels that are not coated, therefore they cook water in them before Yom Tov; thus appears to me the reason. But in truth there is no difference between Pesach and other Yomim Tovim.
