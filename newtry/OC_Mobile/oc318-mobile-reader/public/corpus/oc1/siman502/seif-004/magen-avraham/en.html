(א) And so is the custom. Darkei Moshe: it appears to me they practiced in our place a prohibition, for the common custom in all pots bought for Pesach is to cook water in them before Pesach — end quote; in Maharil laws of Pesach he wrote it is forbidden to insulate in a new pot — end quote. In truth on other Yom Tov people are not careful and cook in new pots; it appears the reason Rosh wrote they seal them — we see they are new and do not hold water until cooked once — end quote. It is known glazed vessels called glazirat hold water well even before cooking; therefore permitted to cook in them on Yom Tov initially since most use is glazed; but on Pesach one does not use glazed as siman 451, rather uncoated — therefore cook water before Yom Tov as above — the reason; but in truth no difference between Pesach and other Yom Tov.
