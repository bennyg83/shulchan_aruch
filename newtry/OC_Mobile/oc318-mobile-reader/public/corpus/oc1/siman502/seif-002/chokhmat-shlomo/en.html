Further it appears possible to resolve what Magen Avraham challenged on Rama, that he holds we do not say lavud to be stringent, and Magen Avraham challenged him from several places; in my humble opinion it resolves well if we give a new principle here: where we do not need this partition for permission, if to forbid we would be stringent through lavud we do not follow Torah law to say lavud and forbid — rather we follow the present reality that it is not connected; but if we need this partition to permit, since in truth it is not a proper partition but the Torah or Sages made it a partition — if we say lavud here it will not help to permit; we say lavud, that we do not say lavud to be stringent, nevertheless for permission also one cannot, since from Torah law it is lavud; if we go by truth it is not a partition, if we go by the law the Torah or Sages said it is like a partition — similarly they said and they said that less than three is like lavud and is like one. With this many of Magen Avraham's difficulties on Rama are resolved; in my opinion this principle is correct — examine well.
