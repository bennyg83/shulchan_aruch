2) There. Fire may not be produced, etc. The reason is that fire could have been produced before Yom Tov. One authority infers from this that if a person could not produce fire before Yom Tov, such as where he was imprisoned or in a wilderness and did not obtain the means to produce it until Yom Tov, he may produce it on Yom Tov. This also appears to be the view of the author of Tosafos. Sefer Beis Moed. Rabbi Avraham Azulai cites it in his handwritten glosses. Birkei Yosef, subsection 1; Zechor LeAvraham, subsection 10; Mikraei Kodesh there, Likutei Rima, subsection 2, cite it. Pri HaShulchan, however, writes regarding the aforementioned words of Birkei Yosef that the unqualified rulings of the halachic authorities do not imply this. Rather, creating something new resembles a melachah and was not permitted on Yom Tov. These are his words. Since the matter is disputed, it is possible that in such a case, when one cannot obtain fire from others on Yom Tov, one may be lenient and produce fire from the small sulfur-tipped sticks discussed below, subsection 6.
