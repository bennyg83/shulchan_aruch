(2) There. One does not produce fire, etc. — the reason is "since" it is possible to produce fire from erev Yom Tov. And some inferred from this that if he could not obtain fire from erev Yom Tov, such as in prison or desert and nothing occurred by which to produce until Yom Tov, it is permitted to produce on Yom Tov; and such is the Rav's view in Tosafot, Beit Moed, brought by the Rav Mahara Azulai zl in Hagahot Keneset Yisrael, Birkei Yosef sign 1 and Beit David zl sign 14, and in the holy community there in Likutei Rema sign 2. However Peri Megadim wrote on Birkei Yosef's words above that from the poskim's stam it does not appear so, but only to generate something that appears like melacha, and they did not permit on Yom Tov — end quote.
