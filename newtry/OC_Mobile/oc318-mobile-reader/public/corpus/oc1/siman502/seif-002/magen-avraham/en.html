A bundle, etc. It implies that if they are not bound together, even those that the fire has taken hold of, it is permitted to remove them; and specifically if the wood is thick, but if the wood is thin it is forbidden (Beis Yosef). And this is the language of Hagahos Maimoniyos and Mordechai: it is permitted to take a brand to give light, for it is only a causative extinguishing and one does not intend that, and sometimes it does not extinguish; but people are accustomed to be careful, and it is proper to be stringent; and so wrote the Rokeach; see siman 514, se'if 2; and this requires further study. And therefore Rama was precise and wrote to place it on another side; but to take it entirely away from the stove in order to give light, it is proper to be stringent.
