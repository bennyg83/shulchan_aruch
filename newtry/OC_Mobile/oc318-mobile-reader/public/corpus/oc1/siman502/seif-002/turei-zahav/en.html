Since he does not intend to extinguish. See above siman 514 seif 2: requires study — here it appears even if he intends to extinguish sometimes permitted, such as when fire is great and harms the food and he must remove some; we find in siman 514 permitted to extinguish for ochel nefesh; Beit Yosef in name of Mordechai: if one takes a coal from bonfire to light at night — not like removing from lamp; however people are accustomed to be careful and it is correct — end quote; per the reason here that he does not intend to extinguish, it implies similarly if he places it to another bonfire it is permitted.
