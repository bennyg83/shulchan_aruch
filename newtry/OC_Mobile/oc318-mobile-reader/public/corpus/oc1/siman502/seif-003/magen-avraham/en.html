For wood is muktzeh on Yom Tov as on Shabbos, even according to the one who permits muktzeh, as written in siman 308, se'if 7: for with wood and stones all agree it is forbidden; and Olas Shabbos [likewise does] not [permit otherwise], see there; and they are not permitted to be used except for the need of kindling.
To roast on it. That is, with dry wood; for we say, what difference is it to me to roast on it, that is, by thrusting the meat upon it, versus roasting on its coals; but with moist wood it is forbidden to roast meat; so it is in the Gemara and in the poskim.
And all the more so it is permitted, etc. This is not precise, for at the beginning it deals specifically with dry wood; and it is possible that nevertheless it is an a fortiori, for unspecified wood stands for kindling; and Yam Shel Shlomo ruled like the Tur, that it is permitted to use dry wood for every use.
