Since Taz challenged from siman 514 seif 2 in Taz 4 there forbidding taking a wick from lamp to lamp — what is different here that it is permitted? It appears, etc., and so in vav — and this is the matter independently: sometimes, etc., as written in 514 note 1 that some permit extinguishing for ochel nefesh; partial answer to his difficulty: one can say between here where ochel nefesh is involved and there for lighting which is from an existing source and extinguishing was not permitted through the existing source; or per the view on Shabbat day — on Yom Tov kindling is permitted not extinguishing; therefore taking a coal from bonfire to light perhaps should be forbidden like there; possibly likewise regarding tinder it is forbidden for it diminishes light — Peri Megadim — it appears permitted in such a case. He also wrote similarly from bonfire to another bonfire all is permitted for ochel nefesh.
