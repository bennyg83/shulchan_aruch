(יח) Or two in outer and joined — those two alone, not with inner; teaches we do not decree lest they think two in inner like outer and permit here too — there certainly forbidden per forbidden foot in its place.

(יט) Individual in inner does not forbid, etc. — even without joining together, permitted foot in its place does not forbid outside its place.

(כ) Many like one individual — father with sons receiving sustenance from him, etc. — see siman 370.
