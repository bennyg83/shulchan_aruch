(ז) And inner open, etc. — and in each there are residents forbidding one another.

(ח) For itself — with residents within it.

(ט) Inner permitted — it can close its opening and outer residents cannot prevent it.

(י) Outer forbidden — one who did not give share in eruv forbids the rest unless he nullifies his share to them.

(יא) Both forbidden — we hold forbidden foot in its place forbids outside its place when there is foot-traffic there unless he nullifies to inner residents and also foot-traffic domain in outer to outer residents.

(יב) Each for itself — permitted foot in its place does not forbid outside its place.

(יג) Permitted in its courtyard — inner does not forbid outer residents even though it has foot-traffic on it.
