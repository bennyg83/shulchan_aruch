Two courtyards, one within the other, and the inner one open to the outer one and the outer one to an alley, and the inner one has foot-traffic on the outer one — if the' inner joined for itself and not the outer, or each joined for itself and one of the outer forgot and did not join — the inner is permitted and the outer forbidden; if the outer joined and not the inner, or each joined for itself and one of the inner forgot and did not join — both are forbidden; if each joined for itself, each is permitted in its courtyard.
