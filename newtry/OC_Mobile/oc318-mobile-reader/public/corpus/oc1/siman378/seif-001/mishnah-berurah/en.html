(א) Open to one another — otherwise they could not join together.

(ב) And open to reshus ha-rabbim — likewise open to an alley. Later authorities: likewise if only the outer is open to reshus ha-rabbim or alley and the middle has foot-traffic with the inner second courtyard before it — same law: each permitted with the middle even if outer open to reshus ha-rabbim and inner cannot forbid — permitted foot in its place does not forbid outside its place, as below seif 2.

(ג) Its eruv in this and its eruv in that — then middle residents dwell in the outer and outer residents do not dwell in the middle — not as one entity; but if they placed eruv in the middle, all are as dwelling in the middle.

(ד) In two houses — even filling one vessel and leaving remainder and giving afterward to another vessel — in such a case it would help if placed in one house as siman 366 seif 4; but in two houses the outers are not connected to each other.

(ה) In one house — one vessel or filled one jug and remainder [later authorities].

(ו) All three permitted — as if they joined together.
