דין חצרות פתוחות זו לזו. ובו ה סעיפים:
The law of courtyards open to one another. And in it are 5 seifim: Three courtyards open to one another and open to reshus ha-rabbim, and each of the outer ones joined with the middle one and the outer ones did not join together — the outer ones are forbidden with one another and the middle one is permitted with each of them and they are permitted with it. When does this apply? When the middle' one placed its eruv in this one and its eruv in that one, or the outer ones placed their eruv in the' middle one in two houses; but if the two outer ones placed their eruv in the middle one in' one house' — all three are permitted with one another.
