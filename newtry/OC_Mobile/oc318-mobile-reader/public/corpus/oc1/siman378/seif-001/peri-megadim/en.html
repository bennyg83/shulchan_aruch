(א) Joined — in siman 372. Mechaber wrote open to reshus ha-rabbim — likewise to alley (Tur, Beit Yosef, Bach, Avkat Rochel). Three courtyards — only one outer wall open to reshus ha-rabbim or alley and middle foot-traffic with second outer — same law. Mechaber: eruv in middle in two houses — implies even if impossible in one house; one house as one vessel rule (Tosafos Yeshanim).
