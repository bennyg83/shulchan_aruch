(ג) There. And open to reshus ha-rabbim — each is a domain to itself without foot-traffic one on another; if one inside the other the inner forbids the outer. Rashi. Later authorities questioned: each courtyard joined for itself then with the other — why should inner forbid outer and middle when permitted foot in its place does not forbid outside its place (seif 2); they permit even nested courtyards with only outer open to reshus ha-rabbim or alley. See Eliyah Rabbah, Machatzit HaShekel, Rabbi Akiva Eiger — study.
