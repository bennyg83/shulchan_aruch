(יד) Both forbidden — if the forgetful one is from the inner, certainly forbids the outer since it forbids in its place; even if forgetful from outer, outer is certainly forbidden and inner forbidden too though outer has no foot-traffic on it — since they joined together it is as dwelling in outer and forbids it; not helping that outer residents withdraw and close — eruv is not in their possession; main eruv is because dwelling as if dwelling where eruv is placed.

(טו) Until he nullifies his domain — to both courtyards as siman 381.

(טז) Both forbidden — considered one; unless he nullifies to both; but if nullifies only to inner residents it does not help even if they wish to withdraw from outer and close [later authorities].

(יז) Inner permitted — can withdraw from outer — "for my benefit you opened, not to harm" — uses for itself since eruv is with it; though initially partnered together, can retract and say not intending one of your courtyard would ruin and not give eruv share.
