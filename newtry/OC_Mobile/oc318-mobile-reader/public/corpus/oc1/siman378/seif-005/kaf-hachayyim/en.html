5) There. When does this apply? When the middle courtyard placed its eruv in this one, etc. The middle courtyard then resembles one whose residents live in both outer courtyards, while the residents of the outer courtyards do not live in the middle courtyard, so they cannot be considered equal. Rashi; Beis Yosef. If the outer courtyards placed their eruvei chatzeiros in the middle courtyard, however, it is as though all of them live in that one house, and they all become the courtyard of that house. Taz, subsection 1.
