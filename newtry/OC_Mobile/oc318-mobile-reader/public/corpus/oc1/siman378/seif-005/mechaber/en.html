Three courtyards one inside the other with one individual in each — even though many have foot traffic through the outer one, they do not restrict, since each one is permitted in its own place. But if there were two in the inner one who did not make an eruv, they restrict the individuals in the middle and outer ones. This is the general rule: a foot that is forbidden in its own place restricts in other places, but a foot that is permitted in its own place does not restrict in other places.
