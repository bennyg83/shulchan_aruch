(Taz, seif katan 4) “Old age is different.” This is astonishing, for the Gemara there discusses the day Eli died, when he was ninety-eight years old, as Scripture makes clear. The episode of Channah occurred on the day he was appointed judge, and he judged Israel for forty years. He was therefore only fifty-eight at that time. The later authorities likewise raise this difficulty, and it requires study.
