(18) To pass and stand — meaning he should pass and stand there and not go further, so that he should not be opposite their faces; for whoever can see him, even though they are at his sides before him, it is considered as before him and is forbidden, because he cancels his concentration thereby. And per this, all the more so it is forbidden to go and stand opposite his face within four cubits. So is the view of Magen Avraham. And Rabbi Akiva Eiger holds they forbade only to pass within four cubits opposite his face; but to walk within four cubits and stand is permitted, even before him; and it is possible to be lenient in a case of duress.
