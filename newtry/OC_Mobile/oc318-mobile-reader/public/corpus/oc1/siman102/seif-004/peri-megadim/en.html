Alternatively, according to the view that Eli stood within four amos, he happened to be standing. The Bach writes that if one studies laws of proper conduct, all agree that he may not sit and must stand. This appears from Berachos 21a, where “to study another chapter” refers to proper conduct, showing that it is not considered Torah study for this purpose. See there.
