Those praying. Regarding whether one may pass in front of a person praying when a platform ten handbreadths high and four handbreadths wide stands before him, Halachos Ketanos, part I, siman 84, rules that it is forbidden. If the person praying himself stands in such a place, however, it may be permitted to pass before him. See there, and see Perach Shoshan, Orach Chayim, siman 11.
It is permitted. The Zohar, Chayei Sarah, states that it is forbidden on every side.
And to stand. Meaning, he should remain there and not proceed farther, because he would then be directly before them. Whenever the person praying can see him, it is forbidden, since his concentration is disrupted by him. Magen Avraham. (See Eliyah Rabbah, which resolves the Tur’s wording against the questions of the Olas Tamid, unlike Magen Avraham.)
