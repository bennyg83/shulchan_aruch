[ד] They do not fine his son after him, etc. From the Shulchan Aruch and Levush it implies even for something that is not davar ha-aved, and he follows Beit Yosef who challenged Rabbenu Yerucham who wrote they fined his sons from that which we expound in Bechorot that we do not fine for “one who squeezes the ear,” see there; and I wonder how he did not see what the Ran wrote in chapter “One who overturned”—his wording: “one who squeezes the ear of a firstborn we do not fine his son; and likewise intentional melacha on the festival for davar ha-aved; but for what is not davar ha-aved they fine him; until here Beit Yosef's wording—behold perforce we must distinguish; and it truly appears to me to distinguish: since here they fined him for davar ha-aved which is rabbinic, if so we must be stricter for what is not davar ha-aved which is biblical; and further here he does two prohibitions—intentional melacha on the festival and doing what is not davar ha-aved. I found this distinction now in Perishah and Taz; in Eliyah Zuta I wrote more on this.
