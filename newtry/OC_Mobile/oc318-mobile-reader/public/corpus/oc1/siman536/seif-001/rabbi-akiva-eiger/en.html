Still it is difficult: for even a full melacha is permitted regarding a matter of impending loss, as the Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of siman 537, and all the more so mere exertion; and it is forced to distinguish between a doubt whether without his melacha it would be lost, while through his melacha it will certainly help (for that is permitted), versus a case where loss is certain and the doubt is whether his melacha will help; and this requires further study.
