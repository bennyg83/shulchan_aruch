(1) [seif 1] One who needs to ride on the moed to stroll, etc. — but from Eretz Yisrael to chutz la'aretz it is forbidden to repair riding needs. Magen Avraham sk 1. Eliyah Rabbah letter 1 letter 11 in Hagahot Beit Yosef — and this is when it is not for any need at all. Chayei Adam general rule 156 letter 1 — but when he goes to earn profit or to see the face of his friend it is permitted. Machatzit HaKesef siman 62 letter 2; and see above siman 531 letter 24 and in the gloss afterward.
