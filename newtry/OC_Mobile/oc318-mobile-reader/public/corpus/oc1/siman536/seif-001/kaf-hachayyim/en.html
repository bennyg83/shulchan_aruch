1) [Se'if 1] One who must ride during the festival for pleasure, etc. Traveling from Eretz Yisrael to outside Eretz Yisrael, however, does not permit preparing the riding equipment. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Yad Aharon, Hagahos Beis Yosef. This applies when there is no need at all. Chayei Adam, rule 116, subsection 1. When one travels to earn money or visit a friend, however, it is permitted. Mikraei Kodesh, siman 62, subsection 2. See above, siman 531, subsection 24, and the following subsection.
2) There. For pleasure, etc. The same applies to visiting a friend. If one rides to earn money not needed for the festival, riding is permitted, but he may not prepare the riding equipment. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1.
3) There. For a festival need, and he did not attempt to walk, etc. If the journey is not needed for the festival, or if he attempted to walk, only riding is permitted, but not preparing the riding equipment. Beis Yosef; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2.
4) There. And to repair its horseshoes. This is the correct reading. It is also the text of Tur and Levush, and of the edition of Shulchan Aruch printed during Maran's lifetime in the year 5327. Levushei Serad and Mishnah Berurah, subsection 3, write likewise.
