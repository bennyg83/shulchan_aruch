(א) To walk, and if he is from abroad, he is not allowed to repair the needs of a ride

(ב) And it is permitted to ride — see Mishna Berurah. And behold per Beit Yosef's reason it would seem specifically regarding riding, for even on Yom Tov it is only a rabbinic shvus and we do not decree this on chol ha-moed; but regarding driving (machmer), wherein on Shabbat there is a full Torah prohibition of "you shall not do melacha — you and your beast," as our Sages received that this refers to action done by man and beast, i.e. machmer — it would seem one should not be lenient when it is for free (without festival need). However when we examine the matter, one may be lenient in this based on one doubt: perhaps the halacha follows those poskim who hold a person is not commanded regarding rest of his animal on Yom Tov, for Yom Tov is not equated to Shabbat except for the thirty-nine melachot and not for the positive commandment of resting one's animal and the prohibition of machmer — and as the Rama gloss above in siman 305. And what is written in siman 495 seif 3, that one may not place a load on the animal on Yom Tov, is only rabbinically, as Mishna Berurah writes there. And even if we say per those poskim who hold Yom Tov and Shabbat are equal from the Torah also regarding this, perhaps the halacha follows Ran in the first chapter of Avodah Zarah who holds the prohibition of machmer was stated only regarding the ox wherein when one takes it out the Torah forbade also when placing a load on the animal, but not in a courtyard [and he disputes Rashba there who holds machmer applies in all cases]. If so, per what the world is accustomed to seize in several matters wherein we no longer have Torah-level ro'eh nowadays, machmer from the Torah does not apply at all, only rabbinically, and therefore we need not forbid on chol ha-moed. However in siman 545 we brought in Biur Halacha the view of several Rishonim who hold there is Torah-level ro'eh even in our time — nevertheless we need not protest those who are lenient in this, for they have whom to rely upon.
