To announce — and they practiced announcing a gentile's lost object on Shabbat because of ways of peace; and specifically if there is danger concern in the matter then permitted. Magen Avraham.

Bans — and likewise one excommunicated in a dream they release on Shabbat. Maharadbaz vol. 1 siman 118; see Shachnei HaGan.

Assembly — and what they practice at fairs that the beadle calls from the writing who is under ban for debts — it is like communal affairs; there is no power for a teacher to abolish this custom practiced leniently before many great ones. Bach.

One does not impose bans — and a sage may excommunicate on Shabbat for his honor — disputes siman 305; and permitted to excommunicate one who desecrated Shabbat Rashdam siman 40 (and in Eliyah Rabbah forbids except for Shabbat need see there); and likewise in Shulchan Aruch siman 409.
