It is permitted to announce on Shabbat about a lost object, even if it is something forbidden to move.And it is permitted to release communal bans on Shabbat even though it is not for Shabbat need, since it is a day of assembly for the many — it is like communal affairs that are permitted to discuss) (Agur); but one does not impose a ban on Shabbat except for something needed for Shabbat (Agudah ch. 23).
