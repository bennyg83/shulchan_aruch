(s.k. 12) And to supervise, etc. — and this is proven in the Gemara, and so in siman 399 seif 4; and that which we taught there at the end of tractate Beitzah: one does not betroth a woman on Yom Tov and Shabbat.

And even if he has no wife and children, for there is mitzvah in betrothal in such a case, and even so one does not betroth; and likewise other shevut matters that the mishnah counted there — Maran concludes in the Gemara: even though there is mitzvah, nevertheless forbidden; behold even for mitzvah need shevut was not permitted; and likewise for communal needs; and therefore here they only permitted supervision, etc.
