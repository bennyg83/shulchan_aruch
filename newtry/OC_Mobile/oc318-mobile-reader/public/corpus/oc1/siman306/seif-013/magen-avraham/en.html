To announce on Shabbat — in Constantinople they are accustomed to announce one who wishes to rent a house or buy; and in responsa of the Geonim they forbid this [so it is].

And if not he will lose, etc. — specifically in such a case forbidden, for it is like judging the case; but if he announces stam "whoever has a right, come and tell," even if there is a remedy in town that if he does not tell his right is voided, nevertheless it is not like judging the case; and if there is a place where they practice thus, we do not protest; and nevertheless l'chatchila one should not practice thus, for it is weekday speech (Ribash).

To announce wine — and in Levush he wrote: I did not see practiced thus, that in all places they announce even with purchase sum; and possible they consider it matters of Heaven for kiddush need — end of his words; and according to what is written in seif 3 this is not permission; and in Yeshuot Yaakov ch. 5 of Beitzah he wrote he should announce before Barechu, and if he forgot until after Barechu he should not mention purchase sum — end of his words; and in Maharil he wrote even to inform who the seller is forbidden; and it appears to me: since in his place good wine was common, it was not mitzvah need; and other beverages — plainly forbidden.
