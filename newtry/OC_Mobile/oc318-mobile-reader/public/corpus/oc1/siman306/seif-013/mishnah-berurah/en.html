(53) To announce on Shabbat — and to announce there is a house for sale or to rent in Constantinople they practice leniency; but in responsa of the Geonim letter 145 they forbid even for sacred property and orphans.

(54) And if not he will lose, etc. — specifically in such a case forbidden, for it is like judging the case; but if he announces stam "whoever has a right, come and tell," even though there is a remedy in town that if he does not tell his right is voided, nevertheless it is not like judging the case; and nevertheless l'chatchila one should not practice thus, for it is like weekday speech; but if there is a place where they practice thus, we do not protest, for there is room to say it is communal needs; however it is a surprising matter [Ribash].

(55) To announce wine — that is, to inform that such-and-such and such-and-such have wine to sell; and Magen Avraham wrote that specifically in a place where good wine is common; but in a place where it is not so available, announcing wine is mitzvah need for kiddush; and nevertheless forbidden to mention purchase sum in every manner, as above seif 3 in the gloss; and Rashal wrote he should announce before Barechu, and if he forgot to announce until after Barechu he should not mention purchase sum.
