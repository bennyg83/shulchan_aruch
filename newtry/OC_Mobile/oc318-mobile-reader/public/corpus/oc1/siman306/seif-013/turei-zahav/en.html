And likewise forbidden to announce wine on Shabbat — it appears from comparing it to buying and selling there is prohibition only when he announces he has wine to sell for such a sum; but if he does not remember the sum, only that he has wine to sell, there is no prohibition since in any case it is mitzvah need for Shabbat honor; and similar to what Rama wrote siman 306 seif 3; and Levush wrote: but I saw in all places they announce wine even with purchase sum; and one can say we consider it matters of Heaven to inform to buy wine for kiddush — it appears to me; and nevertheless it appears to me one asked about this should not rule leniently with purchase sum.
