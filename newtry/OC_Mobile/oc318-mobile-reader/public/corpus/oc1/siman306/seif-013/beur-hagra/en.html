Seif 13 to announce etc. and if not. Specifically in such a case it is forbidden for it is like a decision; but otherwise permitted as with the Sicarii who said in Yerushalmi they would announce on the twelfth month four Shabbatot; and likewise a rebellious wife in "Even though" in the Gemara; and Rivash wrote therefore they would send to her before the announcement from weekday and they did not say so within the announcement — she would know etc. — for in such a case it is forbidden.
