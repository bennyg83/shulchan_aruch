With which matters one may speak on Shabbat. And in it 14 seifim: "From pursuing
your affairs" — your affairs are forbidden, even in a matter that does no melacha at all, such as one who examines his property to see what he needs for tomorrow, or one who goes to the edge of the town so he may hurry to leave at night for the bathhouse; and likewise one may not wait at the techum boundary to hire workers; but one may wait at the techum boundary to bring one's animal; and some say that if the animal cannot walk on its legs, such as a small lamb, he is not permitted to wait at the techum, for he is not permitted to bring it, for it is forbidden to move an animal that is muktzeh.And likewise it is forbidden to stroll to find a horse or ship or wagon to depart with (Mordechai end of "One who waited at dusk").
