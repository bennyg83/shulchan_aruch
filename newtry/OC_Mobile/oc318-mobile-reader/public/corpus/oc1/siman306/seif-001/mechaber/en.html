Which activities may be discussed on Shabbos. It contains 14 seifim: From the verse 'from pursuing your affairs,' we learn that one's affairs are forbidden even when no melacha is being performed, such as inspecting one's property to determine what will be needed tomorrow, or walking to the entrance of the city in order to hurry out at night to the bathhouse. Likewise, one may not wait at the techum boundary toward nightfall in order to hire workers. But one may wait there in order to bring his animal. Some say that if the animal cannot walk on its own legs, such as a small lamb, he may not wait there, because he may not bring it, since it is forbidden to move an animal, which is muktzeh. {Rama: Likewise, it is forbidden to stroll about in order to find a horse, ship, or wagon with which to depart after Shabbos. [Mordechai, end of chapter Mi SheHechshich]}
