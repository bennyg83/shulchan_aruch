Seif 1 and some say if the animal cannot walk on its legs — this is obvious that in such a case it is forbidden as explained in the sugya that it is forbidden to wait at dusk to bring something muktzeh such as wet straw; and so Tosafot explain: if the reading is "one may not wait to bring an animal" it refers to a small one that cannot walk on legs; and we explain the reading "they lead" refers to a large one; but the law is certainly that a small one is forbidden — why does Shulchan Aruch write "some say"? Levush did not write "some say" but plainly; and the practical difference is a large animal etc.
