To bring his animal — Taz, and see Shabbat 151 letter Alef in Tosafot s.v. "there is not," and the matter is in siman 203 in Taz 3. And in Peri Megadim, ch. 24 of Laws of Shabbat halacha 3, it is explained that regarding an animal the reason is simpler: he calls to it and it comes — there is permission, and he may walk at dusk; and regarding detached fruits it is because of watchmen; and in Tosafot they hold: whatever he could go there for — calling and its coming does not help. And if a river separates, one can say: if there were a bridge — see siman 339, what the Mechaber wrote.
