(s.k. 1) Examining his property, etc. — where the matter is recognizable, etc., standing within a field that needs plowing — the matter is recognizable that he stands there and examines the needs of plowing; and likewise to go and wait at the town gate, since it is customary that bathhouses are outside the town near the town gate, it is recognizable that for the bathhouse need he waits; and he wrote there Magen Avraham that nevertheless when waiting at dusk at the end of the techum, even if the matter is not recognizable, it is forbidden — see there the reason.
