1) [Seif 1] Even regarding something for which he performs no labor there. If a non-Jew shows a Jewish person a gold coin on Shabbos and asks him to say whether it is good or bad, deficient or whole, and how much it is worth, it is absolutely forbidden to tell him on Shabbos. This is considered a commercial activity and is forbidden on Shabbos. Lev Chaim, volume 3, siman 70; see there. Orach Chaim, paragraph 1.
