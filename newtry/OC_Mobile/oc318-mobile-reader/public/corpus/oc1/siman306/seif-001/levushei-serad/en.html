Shulchan Aruch seif 1: One may not walk at dusk — meaning to draw himself close on Shabbat to the end of the techum and walk at dusk there so he will be near the place of the workers; but regarding a matter permitted on Shabbat — such as bringing his animal — it is permitted to walk at dusk, even though the animal is outside the techum and it is forbidden to bring it on Shabbat; nevertheless there is permission to call to it, as in seif 2; and even if the animal is far from the techum so that it does not hear his voice — nevertheless, since there is a side of permission in the world if there were watchmen, it is permitted to walk at dusk even though there are no watchmen.
