(a) [Seif 1] Even in a matter in which he does no melacha at all. And a gentile who shows on Shabbat to an Israelite one gold piece, that he inform him whether it is good or bad, or whether it is lacking or whole, and how much it is worth — he transgresses against the prohibition; it is forbidden to inform him on Shabbat, for this is called the way of commerce, and it is forbidden on Shabbat.
