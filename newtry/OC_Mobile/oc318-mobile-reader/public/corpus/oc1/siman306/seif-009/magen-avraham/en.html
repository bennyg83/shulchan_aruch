Forbidden to say — and specifically to tell him to do after Shabbat is permitted, as written s.k. 3; but to do on Shabbat, even if he told him from erev Shabbat to do on Shabbat, forbidden even for bride or corpse need (Ran); and it deals with 325 seif 9.

And certainly permitted — even to hire so his mind will not be distraught over him, as written in Choshen Mishpat small seif 254 [Hagahot Maimoniot ch. 2 and Hagahot Alfasi ch. 9 of Bava Batra].
