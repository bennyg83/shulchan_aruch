It is forbidden to say. Specifically, telling him to do it after Shabbos is permitted, as written in seif 3. But to do it on Shabbos is forbidden, even if he told him before Shabbos to do it on Shabbos, and even for the needs of a bride or a corpse, as the Ran writes. See siman 325, seif 15.
It is certainly permitted. Even to hire someone, so that his mind should not be unsettled over him, as written in Choshen Mishpat at the end of siman 254, in Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 2 and Hagahos Ashri chapter 9 of Bava Basra.
