(40) Forbidden to say, etc. — for specifically to tell him to do after Shabbat is permitted, because it is mitzvah need, as written in s.k. 3; but not that he should do or go on Shabbat; and likewise even if he told him from erev Shabbat to do on Shabbat, forbidden even for bride or corpse need [Magen Avraham in the name of the Ran].

(41) Certainly permitted — even to hire a gentile runner who will run several parsangs on Shabbat to bring his relatives on motzei Shabbat — likewise permitted so his mind will not be distraught over him.
