9) There, gloss: The same applies regarding strolling, etc. This implies that it is forbidden even within the techum. From the words of Tosafos cited above in paragraph 5, however, that walking within the techum is permitted when one's purpose is not recognizable, it is possible to permit strolling in order to obtain a horse, etc. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 2. Magen Avraham, paragraph 3, similarly writes that it is forbidden only when the purpose is recognizable. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3, writes the same: only at the end of the techum or when the purpose is recognizable. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2, writes the same. Chayei Adam, klal 60, paragraph 2, however, writes that this is considered engaging in one's affairs on Shabbos and is forbidden even when the purpose is entirely unrecognizable. Nishmas Adam there writes the same; see there.
