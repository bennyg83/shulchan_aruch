To buy a house — and in Mateh Yehudah ch. 1 letter 161 he wrote in the name of Ribash and Rosh that forbidden to buy unless he bought from erev Shabbat, then permitted to tell a gentile to write on Shabbat.

In Eretz Yisrael — and Syria is like Eretz Yisrael for this matter [Rambam ch. 6]; in the Gemara ch. 1 of Gittin the Rambam deduced and wrote for this matter; for if the law of Syria were a private domain, as Rambam wrote beginning Laws of Terumot, thus: Aram Naharaim and Aram Tzova and Achlav and the like are called Syria and are not like Eretz Yisrael, etc., and all are like Syria rabbinically, etc.; what is Syria — from Eretz Yisrael and below opposite Aram Naharaim and Aram Tzova, all along the Euphrates until Babylon, such as Damascus and Achlav and Charan and Mageddo and the like until Shinar and Tzoar — behold it is like Syria; but Akko is a private domain like Ashkelon — end of his words; nevertheless it requires study, for behold the braita holds individual conquest counts as conquest, and if so forbidden to give them residence in full land of "do not give them rest"; therefore permitted to transgress a rabbinic prohibition; but since the Rambam ruled individual conquest does not count as conquest, why push aside a rabbinic prohibition because of a rabbinic prohibition; and all the more questioned, for they conclude in Yoreh Deah siman 151 that even l'chatchila they sell houses to gentiles in Syria — a fortiori not to buy from them on Shabbat; and it requires study; and possible he holds that in any case there is some mitzvah in buying houses, thus Or Zarua ch. 2 of Bava Metzia in the name of Or Zarua: how does he do it — he shows him purses of dinars and the gentile seals, etc. — it implies forbidden to give him money.
