(45) To buy a house — and how does he do it? He shows him purses of dinars and the gentile seals and brings up in their courts; but forbidden to give him money directly.
(46) In Eretz Yisrael — and Syria is like Eretz Yisrael for this matter, so wrote Rambam; and see Magen Avraham what he wrote on this.
(47) That it is not forbidden, etc. — meaning this writing is rabbinic and is a shevut; and shevut via gentile — therefore they permitted because of settling Eretz Yisrael; and see Biur Halacha where we explained that the opinion of Or Zarua is an individual opinion and has no place in the Gemara, and all poskim dispute this, and hold that even in their script it is a d'oraisa prohibition, for in every language he is liable, as in the mishnah [Shabbat 103]; and nevertheless they permitted here via gentile because of settling Eretz Yisrael; and even though the Mechaber ruled below siman 307 s.k. 5 that d'oraisa melacha is forbidden via gentile even for mitzvah need — this is preferable; and therefore forbidden to tell a gentile on Shabbat to write a letter even for mitzvah, unless it is also a great need — perhaps there is room to be lenient; and see above siman 276 seif 2 in the gloss.
