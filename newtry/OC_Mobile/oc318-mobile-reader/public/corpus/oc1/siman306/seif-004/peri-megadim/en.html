And likewise Taz; and in Magen Avraham letter 27: sum of purchase — even matters of Heaven have no permission, as in seif 3, that even for a matter of mitzvah one may not mention the sum of a purchase:

In this siman and the following siman the laws of Shabbat dependent on speech are explained; and Shabbat wages are forbidden to take, and likewise one need not give him — as the Mechaber wrote, seif 4: he does not give him; and in the Tur he wrote: forbidden to take Shabbat wages; and likewise the Levush. And Bach wondered: he is not obligated to give him; and it is possible likewise — but even if he wishes to waive, it is forbidden to take, unless as a gift and not Shabbat wages. And in the Levush, at first forbidden, and at the end he gives — he grants superiority in both. Midwife wages on Shabbat are permitted, as Hagahot Beit Yosef 3 in the name of Mahari of Bruna; and the reason: it is wages of a mitzvah of pikuach nefesh; and see siman 495 in Beit Yosef there it is explained: on Shabbat and Yom Tov it is certainly forbidden to hire a shochet or blower — only from erev Shabbat; and on Yom Tov they did not decree; and likewise a midwife — one does not hire on Shabbat and one does not mention the sum of money:
