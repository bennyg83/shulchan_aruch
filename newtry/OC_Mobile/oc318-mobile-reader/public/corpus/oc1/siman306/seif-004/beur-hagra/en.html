Seif 4 and he should not say. Tosefta end of Shabbat at the conclusion of the braita "one who hires" etc. "and he should not say" etc. but "give me wages for ten days" — and likewise for weekly hire.

And if he hired him. So explicitly there in Mordechai who said it appears like Shabbat wages even though he pays in absorption since a day is added day by day.
