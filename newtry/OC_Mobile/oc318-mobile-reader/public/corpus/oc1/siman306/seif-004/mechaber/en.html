One who hires workers to guard seeds or anything else — he does not give them Shabbat wages; therefore Shabbat liability is not upon him. If he is a Shabbat hire, monthly hire, yearly hire, or weekly hire — he gives him Shabbat wages; therefore Shabbat liability is upon him; and he should not say to him "give me for Shabbat," but he should say to him "give me the week's or month's wages"(and if he hired him for a month and stipulated with him to pay him each day such-and-such, he is called a day hire (Ran end of "Even though")} or he should say to him "give me wages for ten days.") או יאמר לו תן לי שכר עשרה ימים:
