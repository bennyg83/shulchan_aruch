Seif 4: One who hires a worker to guard seeds. NB: Regarding what they wondered here about the Rambam, who omitted seeds, see my work on Orach Chayim from the year 5596, in my novellae to Menachot 84, where I wrote about this and about a new law that emerges from it; see there and examine carefully. See also here in Magen Avraham s.k. 7, what he wrote in the name of the Ran and the Rambam, and likewise regarding one who rents a house to his fellow. See my novellae on Choshen Mishpat, mahadura 5, siman 22, in the responsum to the holy community of Stanislav, where I explained the intent of the Ran's words; see there and examine carefully.
There, in that same seif, in the gloss: And if he hired him for a month and stipulated to pay him each day, etc. NB: See Magen Avraham, who wrote that he may dismiss him in the middle. See Eliyah Rabbah, who disagrees with him and writes at length that he cannot dismiss him. See my novellae to Choshen Mishpat, siman 312, where I wrote about this at length in a responsum, upholding the view of the Eliyah Rabbah; see there. I wrote this in the year 5595. Examine carefully.
