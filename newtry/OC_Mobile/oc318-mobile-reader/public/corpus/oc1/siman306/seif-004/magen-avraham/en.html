It is called a day hire — and so in Bedek HaBayit in the name of Shulchan Aruch; and he wrote on it "it does not appear so from the words of the poskim"; and possible he did not see the Ran's words who wrote the reason that if they remove him in the middle of the week he need not pay him for the entire week, only for the days that passed, and therefore it is called a day hire and it is Shabbat wage — therefore; and it is proven thus from the Gemara; and likewise one who rents a room to his fellow; and it appears to me likewise those who lend with interest must lend in a manner that if he pays in the middle of the week he pays for that entire week or they do not pay at all, and not calculate by days, for even regarding a rebellious wife, where they penalize her all the time she is rebellious she gives such-and-such per day, nevertheless forbidden to give from Shabbat payment, for it is Shabbat wage; and likewise in every penalty like this; but if they reduce from his debt each day such-and-such, it is not called Shabbat wage in such a case; and so in the Gemara ch. 5 of Ketubot.
