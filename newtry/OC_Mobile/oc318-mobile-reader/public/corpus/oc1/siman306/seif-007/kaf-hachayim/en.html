(g) There. To bring his animal. — because there is permission in Levush: if there were watchmen there he goes and brings it. Taz s.k. 1. Magen Avraham s.k. 2. And since it has a remedy through watchmen, therefore it is permitted to walk at dusk even without watchmen. Turei Zahav letter Gimel. But a lamb — for it cannot be brought even if there were actual partitions, for an animal is forbidden to move — therefore it is forbidden to walk at dusk for it.
