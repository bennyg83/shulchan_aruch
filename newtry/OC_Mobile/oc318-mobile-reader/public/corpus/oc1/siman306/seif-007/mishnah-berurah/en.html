(34) A measurement of mitzvah — and even though shevut was not permitted even in a place of mitzvah, as written above s.k. 28 — measuring is different, for it is not so much of a prohibition, only because it is like weekday activity; and therefore permitted in a place of mitzvah.

(35) Such as to measure, etc. — and likewise permitted to measure a hole in a dividing wall if there is an opening of a handbreadth to bring tumah from the room where the deceased is to the adjacent room; and even though the priests can exit from it, nevertheless since he measures Torah measures, it does not appear like weekday activity. And likewise permitted to measure to estimate the measure of sixty to nullify some prohibition, since it is for learning according to ruling, it is not like weekday activities [Peri Megadim].

(36) Of mitzvah — for measuring is healing, and bodily healing is a mitzvah; and the decree against grinding spices does not apply here, only healing with substance, such as a drink or food [Avnei Rozel].
