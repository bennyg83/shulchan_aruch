And to whisper etc. As stated above siman 328 note 45; and Yerushalmi ch. 14 of Shabbat halacha 3 — they whisper for the eye, bowels, snakes, and scorpions, and pass over the eye on Shabbat.

And nevertheless etc. Mekhilta parashat Yitro: "Six days you shall work and do all your melacha" — is it possible for a person to do all his melacha in six days? Rather Shabbat comes as if all your melacha is done.
