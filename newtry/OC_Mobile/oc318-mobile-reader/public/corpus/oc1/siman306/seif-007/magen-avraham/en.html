Such as to measure — and likewise to measure a hole if there is an opening of a handbreadth and tumah enters there; even though they can exit from the house, nevertheless since he measures Torah measures it is not weekday activity; and Tosafot end of ch. 17 wrote that specifically measuring, for it is not so much prohibition, only because it is like weekday activity, and therefore permitted in a place of mitzvah; but other matters — not; Taz ch. 73 in the name of Rosh: Reuven swore to pay Shimon a maneh on 7 Nisan, etc., if he did not pay and that day occurred on Shabbat, he must give him collateral worth a maneh on Shabbat to fulfill his oath, for collateral is like money and it is matters of Heaven that he fulfill his oath; and the Beit Yosef wrote possible to say that even Rosh did not say he fulfills his oath by giving collateral, only when they appraised it and gave it to him as appraisal; therefore he needed to request permission because it is matters of Heaven, etc., for if he does not give it as full payment it is a complete loan, and if he does not trust him he leaves his cloak, etc.; but in Magen Avraham ch. 1 he wrote that even if he swore to pay in money and not collateral, he gives him a collateral of silver, for collateral is like money — end of his words Beit Yosef; and accordingly he ruled in Shulchan Aruch there to permit appraisal; and Sema permits even measuring; and it requires study, for behold Rosh brought proof from that they permitted to arrange a match for a child and teach him a trade — a fortiori permitted to pay on Shabbat to fulfill his oath — end of his words general rule 8 seif 14; and if so, enough should come from the law to be like the case there forbidden to mention, as written s.k. 66; also in seif 3 forbidden to mention a sum; and the reason Maggid Mishnah wrote: because there is no mitzvah in the cutting, only saving his money — end of his words; and if so here too he should give him movable objects he knows are certainly worth more than his debt and will not need appraisal and measuring; and Rosh also wrote afterward, thus: they again said one who swore to give money and not collateral, etc. — it implies that initially he said he would give collateral; and that he needed to say it is matters of Heaven is because one does not pay on Shabbat, and even to give collateral forbidden unless for Shabbat need, as written s.k. 66 and siman 307 seif 11 — see there; therefore it appears plain that appraisal and measuring are forbidden; and in Yoreh Deah 239 seif 6 it appears to me that if he has money he should give it via a gentile, as written siman 307 s.k. 5; and if he has no money he should tell a gentile to appraise, as in Sanhedrin 14a; or do as written siman 500: say this object is worth like that one, and tomorrow they will place it.
