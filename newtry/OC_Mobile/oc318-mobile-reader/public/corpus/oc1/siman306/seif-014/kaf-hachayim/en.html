(n) In responsa R' Akiva Eiger siman 259 he was in doubt about one who makes a kinyan on erev Shabbat conditionally — whether it is permitted to fulfill the condition on Shabbat, for it is like performing the act of kinyan on Shabbat; and his grandson brought it in responsa Ketav Sofer siman 46; and he concludes himself that even to make the kinyan on erev Shabbat so that it takes effect by itself on Shabbat is forbidden — and all the more to make a kinyan on condition that he fulfill the condition, for it is forbidden to fulfill the condition on Shabbat — see there. And in responsa She'elat Shmuel siman 26 he distinguishes: if he said on erev Shabbat "from now," it is permitted to fulfill the condition on Shabbat, for the sale takes effect retroactively from erev Shabbat; but if he said "if" without qualification, that the kinyan is completed at the time of fulfilling the condition — it is forbidden to fulfill the condition on Shabbat to complete the sale; and plainly it is also like the matter of "pursuing your affairs" — see there. And therefore it appears: that which is permitted in "from now" is specifically where the matter is not evident — for without that it is forbidden because of "pursuing your affairs." Peri To'ar:
