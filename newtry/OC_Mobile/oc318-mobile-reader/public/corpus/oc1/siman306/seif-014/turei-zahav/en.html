One to whom they sent etc. — below siman 328 seif 10 ruled that because they want to force him to transgress a great sin they do not desecrate Shabbat to save him; and Rama wrote and see siman 328 seif 10 — at first glance they contradict each other; and it is a dispute in Beit Yosef between Rashba and Tosafot; and one must say this case is different, for they want to convert her and she will remain an apostate forever afterward — therefore he ruled like Tosafot that one must save her by desecrating Shabbat, for this is preferable to saving life — unlike below where he will do once under coercion, for coercion the Merciful One exempts, and afterward there is no concern to desecrate Shabbat for this salvation — as above this is correct.
