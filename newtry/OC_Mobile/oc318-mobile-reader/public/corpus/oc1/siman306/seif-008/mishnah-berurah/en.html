(37) Reflection on business affairs is permitted — for it is written "and speak a matter" — speech is forbidden, reflection permitted.

(38) A mitzvah that he not think, etc. — and all the more if he has through this reflection distraction of heart and worry, he should be careful in this; see Beit Yosef.

(39) And it should be in his eyes, etc. — and as is in the Mekhilta parashat Yitro: "Six days you shall work and do all your melacha" — is it possible for a person to do all his melacha in six days? Rather Shabbat rest is as if all your melacha is done.
