(22) To pray on Shabbat — because he takes Shabbat wage.

(23) And some permit — he holds that in a place of mitzvah they did not decree rabbinically on Shabbat wage here; and nevertheless he does not see a sign of blessing; and nevertheless likewise to hire a shofar blower; and nevertheless forbidden to hire the chazan or blower on Shabbat and Yom Tov itself, only from erev Shabbat.

(24) For a year, etc. — meaning that he prays also on weekdays and pays him for month or week at once; and see Acharonim who wrote that they are accustomed to be lenient to hire for Shabbatot by month; and one concerned for the words of those who forbid should not stipulate at the beginning, and what he takes afterward one can say is a manner of gift; and a midwife is certainly permitted to take Shabbat wage.
