There, seif 5: It is forbidden to hire chazzanim to pray on Shabbat. NB: I was asked about shochetim on Yom Tov who receive some food item, whether this is permitted or falls under wages for Shabbat and Yom Tov. I wrote a lengthy responsum on this permitting it; it is in my work on Orach Chayim from the year 5592, fol. 72b. Whoever wishes to stand on the root of the matter should examine that responsum, and he will find rest for his soul; see there and examine carefully.
There, in the same seif: And there is one who permits. NB: See my work on Orach Chayim from the year 5604, in Hilchot Pesach, siman 450, there in my work fol. 114b, where I wrote in explanation of the reason of the view that permits; see there and examine very carefully.
