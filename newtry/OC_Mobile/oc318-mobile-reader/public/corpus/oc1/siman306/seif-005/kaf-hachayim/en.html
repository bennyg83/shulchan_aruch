Or to go to the city gate, etc. — even within the techum; and the reason Tosafot wrote there is because the matter is evident — that he does not stroll except in order to enter the bathhouse after Shabbat; for at the city gate bathhouses are accustomed to be. But other cases — such as hiring workers and bringing fruits — specifically at the techum boundary it is forbidden, but within the techum it is permitted.
