For Taz wrote in siman 339 seif 4, and for Taz: if he did not dedicate the object on a weekday and wants to dedicate it on Shabbat — a known object is forbidden; but to say without qualification "I vow a Torah scroll," even if he has one in his house — since they do not designate, it is permitted; and if he designated on Shabbat he cannot retract, for "if they did what they did, it is done," as written in siman 339 seif 4.
