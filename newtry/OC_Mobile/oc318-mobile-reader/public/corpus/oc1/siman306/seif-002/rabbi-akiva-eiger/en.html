Magen Avraham s.k. 4 but forbidden to bring it by hand if outside the techum; and permitted only by calling; but to go beyond the animal and chase it to the city — not. Tosafot there; and I saw Eshel Avraham wrote thus; and in my opinion it is not proven — one can say Tosafot said so only to establish the braita like Ravina, that the braita is where its techum is swallowed within his techum; thus the braita teaches he calls to it, not that he goes beyond and chases — proving this is forbidden; but per our view we hold like Ran's changes that it is tannaim; thus the braita literally — he is outside both techums and cannot go there; one can say truly where within his techum it is permitted to go beyond and chase; and why invent a prohibition not proven from the braita, especially one can say Ravina's version from outside his techum forbidden to call lest he go there; therefore within his techum they also decreed going and chasing lest he bring by hand; but for us on an animal they did not decree; one can also say within his techum permitted to go and chase, for they did not decree lest he bring by hand — and understand.
