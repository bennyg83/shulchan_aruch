(s.k. 2) That he is not, etc. — if there were watchmen there, etc., see siman 307 seif 8, for we hold everything for which permission can be found to do on Shabbat, even though now that permission is not present and he is not permitted to do it on Shabbat, nevertheless in any case it is permitted to wait at dusk for it; and therefore for a large animal, if there were watchmen there — and these are within seventy amot and four handbreadths near the town, a house stands, and even if it is not a complete house, only watchmen; and likewise within seventy amot and four handbreadths of this watchman another watchman stands, and so for several parsangs — all is considered like a town and permitted to go until several parsangs without an eruv, and they do not calculate the techum except from the last watchman, as written siman 398 — see there; unlike a small lamb, for even if there were watchmen there it would be forbidden to bring it, since it cannot walk, and a person is not permitted to carry it, and forbidden to move it in a private domain because it is muktzeh. And that he mentioned prohibition of muktzeh and did not mention from the aspect of transferring four amot in reshut ha'rabbim or in a karmelit — for even if there are watchmen, nevertheless reshut ha'rabbim or karmelit would still apply; nevertheless there would be permission, for just as we permit where he has permission through watchmen, likewise we permit to wait at dusk for something that would have had permission if there were partitions, as stated in Magen Avraham daf 157b — so too Magen Avraham below siman 307 s.k. 12; therefore he needed to explain because of muktzeh prohibition.
