(b) There. Such as examining his property, etc. — and specifically where the matter is evident, as stated in siman 397 seif 9 — see there. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Turei Zahav letter Alef. However, in Nishmat Adam, general 60, he wrote that actually making his business affairs — such as examining his property — even within the techum and where the matter is not evident, is forbidden; and he wrote that such is the view of Tosafot and Rambam and Semag and Tur and Shulchan Aruch — see there. But if the matter is not evident at all, and he also does not complete his business on Shabbat, only he prepares himself so he will be ready for commerce — it is permitted.
