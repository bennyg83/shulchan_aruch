(7) He may call, etc. — and we do not decree lest the owner go beyond the techum in order to bring it within the techum. And if it does not heed him to come, it is permitted to bring it via a gentile, but forbidden to bring it via an Israelite, even another Israelite whose owner is not with him within the techum, for we hold an animal and utensils follow the feet of the owners regarding techum; and therefore, since it went outside its techum, it is forbidden for any Israelite to move it by hand because of its techum.
