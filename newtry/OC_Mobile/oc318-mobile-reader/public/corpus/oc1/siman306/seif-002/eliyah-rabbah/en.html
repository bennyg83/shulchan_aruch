[5] Outside the techum, etc. Even outside his own techum, if the animal has its own permitted range, because a person is not warned regarding the techum of his animal. This applies only to calling the animal. One may not go beyond the animal and drive it toward the city, even within his own techum, once it is outside its own techum. All the more so, he may not bring it by hand (Gemara and Tosafot, Shabbos 53a).
[6] [Levush] He calls, etc. This concerns an animal carrying nothing, so that there is no violation of driving an animal with a burden (Malbushei Yom Tov). See the beginning of siman 266.
