[2] At the techum boundary, etc. — specifically at the end of the techum, where the matter is evident; but within the techum it is permitted. And that which is at the city gate — the matter is also evident there, for bathhouses are before the city gate (Tosafot Shabbat 150, and so wrote the Maggid ch. 24) to add that when he is within a field that needs plowing and cultivation the matter is evident — until here. And Magen Avraham siman 397 s.k. 13 wondered at Eruvin 39, which states explicitly that even at the end of the techum the matter is not evident — that if he is a Torah scholar we say that learning distracted him, and if he is an am ha'aretz, etc. And in my humble opinion, there it deals with one who walks at dusk on Yom Tov that occurs on erev Shabbat in order to establish an eruv — then the matter is not evident; but when he walks at dusk on Shabbat near nightfall, certainly we say he walks at dusk to do for weekday need. And what he challenged from Rashi's explanation — that it is forbidden to invite oneself for that which is forbidden to do on Shabbat — it appears to me that is also where the matter is evident; and know that on Shabbat it is forbidden to establish an eruv.
