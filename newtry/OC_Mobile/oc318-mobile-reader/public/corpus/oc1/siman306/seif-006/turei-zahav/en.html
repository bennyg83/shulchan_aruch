For it is permitted to pledge tzedakah — Beit Yosef wrote the Ran challenged for we taught one may not dedicate; and afterward wrote in the name of Kol Bo in the name of a certain gaon that specifically dedication for the altar or Temple maintenance is forbidden because it leaves his domain for the domain of the sacred; but now that they vow to dedicate a Torah scroll or crown and the like it is permitted, for they have not yet designated anything that leaves their domain, and it is not like buying and selling — end quote; and if so, if he already designated the Torah scroll and brings it on Shabbat to the synagogue there is prohibition, for here there is leaving domain to domain when he dedicates it to tzedakah; and it requires study why they did not practice thus; and it appears to me that this one who dedicates a Torah scroll already dedicated it on a weekday between himself and himself — thus it already left to the domain of the sacred, only he publicizes this on Shabbat; and therefore if it came to his heart on Shabbat to dedicate it and not before, he should not dedicate on Shabbat; and likewise forbidden to bring an object to the synagogue to dedicate if he did not think of it before, but hold it in the house and dedicate orally as I wrote.
