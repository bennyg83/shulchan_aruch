6) There: Likewise, one may not wait until nightfall at the edge of the techum, etc. This means approaching the edge of the techum on Shabbos and waiting there until nightfall so that he will be near the workers. He may, however, wait until nightfall for something permitted on Shabbos, such as bringing his animal. Although the animal is outside the techum and may not be brought on Shabbos, he may call it, as stated in seif 2. Even if it is so far beyond the techum that it cannot hear his voice, since a possible permitted method exists in the world if there were watchmen's huts, he may wait there even though no such huts exist. This law is explained at length at the end of siman 203 in Taz; see there. Levush Serad. If a river interrupts the route, one can say, 'If only there were a bridge there.'
