Tzedakah — Taz wrote if it came to his heart on Shabbat to dedicate a Torah scroll and not before, he should not dedicate on Shabbat; and forbidden to bring an object to synagogue to dedicate if he did not think of it before, but hold in the house and dedicate between himself and himself — thus it already left to the domain of the sacred, only he publicizes on Shabbat see there; and Yad Aharon wrote in our opinion even if he dedicated in his heart on a weekday forbidden to dedicate on Shabbat, for matters of the heart are not matters and did not leave to the domain of the sacred see there; Magen Avraham wrote our dedications are profane and every Israelite has a share — thus it did not leave his domain as the dedicator also has a share; therefore permitted to dedicate a Torah scroll on Shabbat, for no prohibition of leaving domain to domain see there; Rashal ch. 5 Beitzah wrote announcement of mitzvah "whoever gives more merits" — more is commerce; the clear view is as pious of Austria practiced: whatever he said he would give he gives to tzedakah even if another acquired the mitzvah — thus only like trimming money for mitzvah need; and every Israelite should be careful in this; and forbidden to buy synagogue seats and etrogim from the congregation after the mitzvah; and forbidden to buy shofar or etrog on Yom Tov; see siman 485 seif 21; and see Choshen Mishpat siman 72 if one swore to pay on 7 Nisan and it occurred on Shabbat what to do; Magen Avraham wrote it appears if he has money he gives via gentile; if no money tell gentile to appraise — meaning say this vessel is worth like this and tomorrow they will appraise see there.
