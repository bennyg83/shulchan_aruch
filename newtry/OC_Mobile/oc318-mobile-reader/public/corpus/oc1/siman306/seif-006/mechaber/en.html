Matters of Heaven are permitted to speak of, such as accounts of a mitzvah, and to pledge tzedakah, and to supervise communal affairs, and to arrange a child's match for betrothal, and to teach him a book or a trade — and specifically to speak if he wishes to hire; but to hire him and mention to him a sum of money is forbidden.. Some say that in a place where they give to the Torah reader one who blessed and vowed tzedakah, or to a chazan — it is forbidden on Shabbat to decide how much he will give (Or Zarua); and the custom is to be lenient, for it is permitted to pledge tzedakah.
