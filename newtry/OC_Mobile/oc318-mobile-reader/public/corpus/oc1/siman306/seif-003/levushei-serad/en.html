Magen Avraham s.k. 5: See there — he wondered: in siman 397 seif 8 it is explained that even for a mundane matter it is permitted to tell his fellow: go tomorrow to such-and-such a city, since there is a side of permission on Shabbat through watchmen. And Magen Avraham answered: for a mundane matter it is forbidden to tell him that he should go tomorrow to buy there some thing — therefore it is not permitted here except because it is mitzvah need; and even though here too he mentioned only "go to the place," etc., and did not mention the purchase — nevertheless, since he concluded "and if you do not find," etc.:
