Go to such-and-such a place. The Olas Shabbos wondered at the words of the Beit Yosef, but he did not realize that this is exactly the language of the Gemara. Its meaning is that since he said, ‘and if you do not find,’ etc., it is evident that he initially told him to take it in such-and-such a place. Therefore, in a matter of optional activity it is forbidden.
And some forbid. Meaning, even if it cannot be done otherwise, it is forbidden to mention the sum of the purchase price. See siman 323.
