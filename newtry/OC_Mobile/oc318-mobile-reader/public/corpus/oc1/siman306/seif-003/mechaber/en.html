One may wait at the techum boundary for the needs of a bride or the needs of a corpse — to bring him a coffin and shrouds; and he may tell his fellow to wait until dark so that he will bring to him; and he may tell him: go tomorrow to such-and-such a place — if you do not find in such-and-such a place, go to such-and-such a place; if you do not find at such a price, take at two hundred — provided he does not mention to him the sum of the purchase, meaning he should not tell him a known sum so as not to add to it; and likewise if he bought from him for eight he may not tell him: give me two more and I will owe you ten.And if it is impossible for him unless he mentions the sum of the purchase, it is permitted in every matter, for it is the needs of a mitzvah (Hagahot Mordechai ch. 5);) and some forbid in every matter, and so is the essential ruling (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ran, Maggid, Mordechai ch. Kira, and Hagahot Maimoniot ch. 1 and ch. 26).
