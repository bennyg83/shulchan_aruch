(8) For the needs, etc., or needs of a corpse — this "for the needs" also refers to a corpse, that he waits at dusk in order to repair and bring quickly; and nevertheless it is permitted, for it is matters of Heaven; and for plain bringing it is obvious that it is permitted to wait at dusk [Gemara].

(9) So that he will bring to him — that is, so that he will repair there and bring to him; and as above.

(10) Go to such-and-such a place, etc. — meaning he should go there and purchase, for it is forbidden to say for the sake of a reshus matter; and for a mitzvah matter it is permitted; but going alone, even for a reshus matter, is permitted to say, as written siman 307 seif 8.

(11) Tomorrow — but on Shabbat itself it is forbidden even though it is for a mitzvah matter.

(12) Known sum — because there is no mitzvah in the cutting [ketzitzah], only saving his money; and therefore it is forbidden.

(13) He should not say "give me," etc. — meaning he should not say to him via this agent that he should give him the needs of this mitzvah later in years and he will be obligated to him in a sum of ten altogether, for combining this sum has no need for the mitzvah.

(14) In every matter — purchasing is meant, for he holds that since it is a manner of actual buying and selling, they did not permit in any manner even for mitzvah need.
