One may not lead an animal with a stick. The reason: it appears like one going to the fair. Rashi: not because of muktzeh it is forbidden but it appears like leading many to sell in the market. And Ran wrote: and you could say why need this reason — derive it from muktzeh, for wood was given only for kindling, and even R' Shimon agrees; one could say the difference is even if designated from yesterday it is not muktzeh:
