A blind person may not. And the same applies to an elderly person.
Goes out with his staff. That is, into a public domain (reshus harabim) or into a karmelis, because it is a weekday manner and a degradation of Yom Tov, since he can walk without this staff and takes it only to adjust and straighten his steps. And what he wrote, "nor a shepherd with his pack," that likewise is [regarding] a public domain or a karmelis, and for the reason of degradation of Yom Tov.
And the law of a lame person, etc. And the same applies regarding a sick person who has recovered from his illness, as above in siman 301, se'if 17.
