(1) [Seif 1] A blind person may not go out with his cane — for it is a weekday manner and there is degradation of Yom Tov. Rashi Beitza 25b. Beit Yosef. Levush. And specifically in reshut harabbim or karmelit similar to reshut harabbim. Yeshuot Shlomo chapter 3 of Beitza seif 14. Taz subsection 1. Eliyah Rabbah entry 1; Chayei Adam entry 1; Razah entry 1; Kaf HaChayyim general 96 entry 5; Maor VaShemesh siman 12 or 14. Mishna Berurah entry 2. But from house to courtyard is permitted without eruv, for there is no eruv and carrying for Yom Tov — Eliyah Rabbah there; Chayei Adam there. And likewise in a city that has eruv it is permitted — Maor VaShemesh there. However in responsum of Radbaz part 2 siman 2963 he wrote that some people who go out with a cane when mud is on the roads are forbidden on Shabbats and Yom Tov to go out with a cane even in an enclosed courtyard, due to weekday activity; and the Beit Din above siman 301 entry 105 — see there. And requires study on the Mechaber in this siman entry 1 who wrote to be uncertain about Radbaz's words whether it applies on Yom Tov — see there, for he explicitly wrote on Shabbats and Yom Tov; nevertheless it appears that for present need one may be lenient to go out with a cane on Yom Tov from house to courtyard or in an enclosed city, for even on Shabbat there are those who permit in an enclosed place as we wrote there — see there.
