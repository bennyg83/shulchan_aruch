[1] One who renounced etc. — meaning he did not renounce the domain of his house; but that he renounced his house as well — it is not relevant to say he reverts from his renunciation (Olat Shabbat). And it escaped me — the words of Levush in siman 380 seif 2, and likewise Beit Yosef wrote in siman 380, and likewise explained in Rashi daf 26a s.v. anshei etc., and likewise implied in Shibbolei HaLeket that we also say regarding renunciation of the domain of his house that he reverts if they had not already held.
