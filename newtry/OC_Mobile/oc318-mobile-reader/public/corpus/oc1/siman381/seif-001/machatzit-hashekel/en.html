(Siman kuf alef) And he carried out, but bringing in is permitted — unless etc., forbidden to bring in, so it should read. For the text before us "but to bring in is permitted" is necessarily a scribal error, for how would it be permitted to bring in to his house when the house is a domain designated for him alone if he did not renounce it, and the courtyard is designated for the courtyard owners and he has no share in it if he renounced; and afterward the text before us that then it is forbidden to bring in. On the contrary, it is more reasonable that when he also renounced the domain of his house, then it is permitted as explained siman 380 seif 2; therefore it must be as I emended. And he said in Magen Avraham in Shulchan Aruch that if he carried out he restricts, for he reverts from his renunciation — meaning specifically carrying out, for using the courtyard it appears he reverts from his renunciation; but carrying out from the courtyard and bringing into the house — in this it does not appear he reverts from his renunciation, for on the contrary he carried out from the courtyard and does not wish to use it; therefore he wrote but bringing in is permitted — meaning permitted for the courtyard members, for his renunciation remains in his renunciation unless he also renounced the domain of his house and brought in — then he brought into the house he renounced and uses there, it appears he reverts from the renunciation of his house and again forbids the courtyard members. And perhaps since in paragraph 1 he renounced the domain of his house and courtyard, and now that he brought in it appears he reverts from the renunciation of his house, even though there is no proof he returned from the renunciation of his courtyard, for he carried out from the courtyard — nevertheless we say since he returned from part of the renunciation he returned from all the renunciation.
