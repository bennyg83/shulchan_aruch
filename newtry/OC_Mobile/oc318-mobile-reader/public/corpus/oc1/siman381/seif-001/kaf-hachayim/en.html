(1) [Seif 1] One who renounced his domain and carried out etc. — meaning that he transgressed and carried out. R' Zalman says in siman 1 that it goes without saying that if he did not renounce only the domain of his courtyard it is forbidden to carry out as written above siman 380 seif 1; but even if he also renounced the domain of his house, which is permitted to carry out as written there seif 2 — how is it after they took possession of what he renounced for them as written there siman 14; but before they took possession he reverts from his renunciation and the courtyard again becomes a partnership that did not make an eruv, and it is forbidden to carry from house to courtyard.
