(1) And afterward he carried out — meaning that he transgressed and carried out.

(2) If deliberately he restricts — on the courtyard members to carry.

(3) For he reverts etc. — for it appears he is reverting and holding the domain he renounced. And nevertheless if he afterward renounces anew it is effective. Magen Avraham wrote that we do not say he is like reverting and holding except through carrying out from house to courtyard; but if he brings in from courtyard to house it does not appear he wishes to revert from his renunciation and hold the courtyard. And we already wrote above siman 380 siman kuf tet that several poskim disagree on this and hold there is no distinction between carrying out and bringing in, and as we do not distinguish between carrying out and bringing in regarding chazakah, as the Mechaber concludes.

(4) And if they held etc. — before he returned and carried out his vessels.

(5) He cannot revert — for in every place chazakah is effective in acquisition, and even though this is not a complete chazakah, nevertheless the Sages were lenient regarding it that it helps like other chazakot; and according to this view chazakah helps even if they held on erev Shabbat after the renunciation.

(6) From nightfall — for from while it is still day the matter is not proven, for even without renunciation they can carry out and bring in; and see Biur Halacha that one should be stringent like this view.
