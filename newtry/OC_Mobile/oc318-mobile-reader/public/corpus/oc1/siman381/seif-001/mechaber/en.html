Laws of one who renounced his domain and transgressed and carried out. Containing 7 seifim: One
who renounced his domain and afterward carried out from his house to the courtyard inadvertently does not restrict. If he did so deliberately he restricts, since he thereby reverts from his renunciation that he renounced. But if they already took possession — meaning they carried out from their houses to the courtyard or brought in from the courtyard to their houses — he cannot revert from his renunciation. And according to Rashi, chazakah is not effective unless they took possession from nightfall.
