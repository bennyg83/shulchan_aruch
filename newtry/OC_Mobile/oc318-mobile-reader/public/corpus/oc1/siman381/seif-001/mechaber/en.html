Laws of one who renounced his domain and then carried anyway. Containing 7 paragraphs:
One who renounced his domain and afterward carried from his house to the courtyard inadvertently does not restrict. If he did so deliberately he restricts, since he thereby retracts his renunciation. But if they had already taken possession meaning they carried from their houses to the courtyard or brought in from the courtyard to their houses he can no longer retract his renunciation. According to Rashi, taking possession is only effective if it occurred after nightfall.
