(Taz siman kuf alef) And it is difficult what is written from siman 371 paragraph 2 — it escaped me, a well-arranged baraita daf 70: this is the rule, whatever is permitted for part of Shabbat is permitted for all of Shabbat, and whatever is forbidden for part is forbidden for all of Shabbat, except for one who renounces domain; and it also refers to the beginning that we do not say once it was permitted it is permitted regarding one who renounces domain, for just as it is not relevant to say since it was forbidden for part of Shabbat it is forbidden for all of Shabbat, we do not say once it was permitted it is permitted; and according to what was concluded, the law of renunciation of domain and inheritor is not included. That is, also regarding renunciation of domain, even though the testator renounced his domain after he died his renunciation ceases — the heir restricts until he renounces, and we do not say once it was permitted it is permitted; and likewise if the testator did not renounce, afterward the heir has power to renounce his domain, and we do not say since it was forbidden etc. And it is possible to give a reason that we do not say once it was permitted it is permitted except regarding something permitted through eruv through an act, but not in another place.
