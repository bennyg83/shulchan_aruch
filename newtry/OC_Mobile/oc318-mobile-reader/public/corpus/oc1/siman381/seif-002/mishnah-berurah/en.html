(7) There is renunciation of domain from courtyard to courtyard — as there is renunciation of domain from house to house in one courtyard.
(8) And each made an eruv for itself — for if they did not make an eruv they do not renounce, as explained in siman 380, that for two they do not renounce, because each one restricts the other.
(9) And it does not say they made an eruv together — it deals with when one of them forgot and did not make an eruv, that he can renounce his domain to the members of his courtyard and also to the members of the other.
(10) To make it permitted etc. and not it — it refers to the beginning, where each made an eruv for itself, that those who renounce their domain are again forbidden to carry out from their houses to the courtyard; and where one forgot and did not make an eruv and renounced his domain to all of them, he alone is forbidden and all are permitted.
(11) But only the outer etc. — for if the outer also did not make an eruv, renunciation is not possible, for one does not renounce domain to two, as above.
(12) Alone — and then the inner is a foot that is forbidden in its place and forbids when not in its place, as above in siman 378.
(13) When it needs to exit — for treading of the foot does not forbid except with making use.
(14) And they placed their eruv in the outer — it should read: likewise if they placed their eruv in the inner, the inner one also needs to renounce his domain to both courtyards, and it does not suffice that he renounce only to the inner ones and he be a guest relative to them and not forbid them; and afterward they themselves completely withdraw from using the outer courtyard, since the restriction comes from them — for one of the members of their courtyard forgot to make an eruv, it is impossible for them to withdraw from the members of the outer after they were initially joined together through one eruv.
(15) And also to each one of the members of the outer — for they become like one courtyard through the eruv; and even if the members of the inner wish to withdraw themselves from the outer as well, renouncing only to the inner ones does not help, as above in the previous siman kuf, and especially according to what the Mechaber depicted, that the eruv is placed in the outer — in any case it is impossible to withdraw from them, for the eruv is placed with them.
(16) And he alone will be forbidden — meaning to carry out from house to courtyard, as above in siman 380.
