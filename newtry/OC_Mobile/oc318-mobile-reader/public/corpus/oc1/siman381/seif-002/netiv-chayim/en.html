(Magen Avraham siman kuf zayin) And their words are puzzling — paragraph 2, Levush already explained: since it is still forbidden for him to carry out from his room to his house, he is not like one holding his domain. And what will you say — behold even if he renounced the domain of his courtyard and house, he should be permitted to carry out from his fellow's house to the courtyard, for there is still a recognizable sign that he is not carrying out and bringing in from his house to the courtyard or from his courtyard to his house. There is no explanation in a case where one holds domain — the courtyard returns to its prohibition because he did not make an eruv; which is not so regarding a house — if he renounced, his renunciation, and he held his domain, who objects to carrying from one to the other. Or alternatively, carrying in a house is a more recognizable sign, for this carrying is more common than carrying from courtyard to house or from house to courtyard.
