And one forgot from the inner — the same applies if one forgot from the members of the outer as well: we require that he renounce to both courtyards, and it does not suffice that he renounce only to the members of the outer while the members of the inner withdraw from them [since the restriction does not come from them] after the eruv is placed in the outer; and if the eruv is placed in the inner, the members of the inner may withdraw from them [even though he did not renounce his domain to them] as explained in siman 378 see there.
