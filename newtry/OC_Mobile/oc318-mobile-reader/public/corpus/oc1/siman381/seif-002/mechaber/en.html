There is renunciation of domain from courtyard to courtyard. Whether two courtyards with an opening between them where each made its own eruv and they did not make a joint eruv — each one may renounce its domain to the other, making' it permitted to use it, but not the reverse. Or whether two courtyards, this one inside that one, where they did not make an eruv but only the outer one alone, and the inner one restricts the outer — the inner one may renounce its domain to the outer so that it neither uses nor passes through it except when it needs to exit, and the outer one will be permitted. Or if they made a joint eruv and placed their eruv in the outer one and one person from the inner one forgot and did not make an eruv — he may renounce his domain, meaning he renounces to each member of the inner' courtyard and also to each member of the outer courtyard, leaving only himself forbidden while all the others are permitted.
