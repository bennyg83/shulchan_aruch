In Magen Avraham siman kuf alef: but to bring in is permitted — paragraph 2, meaning that even though this renouncer wants his renunciation and does not wish to return, nevertheless it is permitted to bring in, for bringing in is not a return (and what he questioned on this: since he did not renounce the domain of his house, how can they be permitted to bring in from the courtyard, which is everyone's domain, to the house, which is his designated domain — one can say that regarding whether he renounced before nightfall it deals with that if he wants his renunciation it is forbidden to carry out even from erev Shabbat, which is not so to bring in from erev Shabbat, which is permitted). But if he renounced the domain of his house so there would be permission to carry out also from his house to the courtyard, it is forbidden to bring in, for regarding the house bringing in is considered; and even though in siman 380 seif 2 we say that he too is permitted to carry out from their houses to the courtyard because he is a mere guest — that is specifically from their houses directly, for he is certainly a guest; which is not so here when he brings in from the courtyard, even though he renounced the domain of the courtyard, nevertheless he is not called a guest relative to it; and when he brings in from it to the house it is as if he reverts from what he renounced the domain of his house, unless they already held. And it is possible that even if they held in the courtyard alone it helps regarding that he be permitted to bring in from the courtyard to the house, and this is not a return from what he renounced the domain of his house, since he brings in to him from the courtyard he renounced and they held in it — he is a guest and it is as written in the said siman that carrying out from their houses to the courtyard is because he is a guest; which is not so if they did not hold, he is not like a guest; and it requires further study.
