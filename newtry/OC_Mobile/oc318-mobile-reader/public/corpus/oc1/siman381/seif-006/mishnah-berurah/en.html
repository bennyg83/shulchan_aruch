(26) And the heir comes to dwell in the courtyard and restricts — but if he did not come to dwell he does not restrict the courtyard members, for residence without owners is not called residence, as above in siman 371 seif 4; and see above what we wrote there in Biur Halacha.
(27) He can renounce his domain — even though if he wished to make an eruv from yesterday he could not make an eruv, nevertheless his renunciation helps because an inheritor is like his father's foot [Gemara].
