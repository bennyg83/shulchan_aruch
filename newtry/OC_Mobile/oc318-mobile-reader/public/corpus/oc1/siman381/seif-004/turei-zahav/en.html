And even the one who renounces is permitted etc. — and there is no concern of returning and holding his domain, for he is not called holding except when he brings into the domain he renounced; but what he carries out from it is not a return. And nevertheless Tosafot and Rosh wrote that the incident came before Rashi in one wedding hall where they forgot and did not make an eruv and needed to carry out from this house to this house, and he permitted them through renouncing their domain to one, end quote. And some say the Rama brings is R' Yehuda and Rabbenu Tam who disagreed with him, that renunciation from house to house is not effective, for then it would be permitted to carry for all of them and the law of eruv would be abolished, since there is a remedy through renunciation unless he leaves for himself one room that he did not renounce, and then he will suffer through the eruv that he will also be permitted in that room. And what the Rama wrote "and even permitted to bring in" — it must be read "and then permitted" etc.
