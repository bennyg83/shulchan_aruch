There is renunciation of domain from house to house — if there were two houses with an opening between them and they did not make an eruv, one may renounce his domain to the other. Even the one who renounced is permitted to carry out from his house to his fellow's house, but he cannot carry out from his fellow's house to his own house, since then he would be returning and holding the domain he renounced. And likewise if he has a room opening into his house and he renounced the domain of his house, he is forbidden to carry from the room into his house.Some say one may only renounce from house to house if he retains one room for himself that he did not renounce, in which case he is permitted to bring in even from his fellow's house to his own. It is good to be stringent about this l'chatchila. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham, Rabbenu Tam, and Hagahot Maimoni chapter 2 of Hilchot Eruvin).
