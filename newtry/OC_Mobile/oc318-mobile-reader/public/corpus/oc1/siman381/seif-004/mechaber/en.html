Renunciation of domain applies from house to house — if there were two houses with an opening between them and they did not make an eruv, one may renounce his domain to the other. Even the one who renounced is permitted to carry from his house to his fellow's house, but he cannot carry from his fellow's house to his own house, since that would be reclaiming the domain he renounced. Similarly, if he has a room opening into his house and he renounced the domain of his house, he is forbidden to carry from the room into his house.Rema: Some say one may only renounce from house to house if he retains one room for himself that he did not renounce, in which case he is permitted to bring in even from his fellow's house to his own. It is good to be stringent about this at the outset. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham, Rabbeinu Tam and Hagahot Maimoni Perek 2 in Hilchot Eiruvin.
