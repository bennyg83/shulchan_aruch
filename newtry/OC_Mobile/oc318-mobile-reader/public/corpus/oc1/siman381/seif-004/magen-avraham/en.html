From his house to his fellow's house. For everything belongs to his fellow.

Returning and holding. For individual relative to individual is not called a guest.

And then it is permitted to bring in. Likewise Rav Beit Yosef, and their words are puzzling, for it is similar to one who renounced the domain of his courtyard to an individual, that it is forbidden to carry out from his fellow's house to the courtyard, for it appears he is returning and holding — individual relative to individual is not called a guest, as written siman 380 seif 4; and if so here too it is forbidden. And if it deals with an individual renouncing to many who made an eruv, then even if he did not leave a room it is also permitted, for it is not relevant to say lest the law of eruv be forgotten, and they will forget.
