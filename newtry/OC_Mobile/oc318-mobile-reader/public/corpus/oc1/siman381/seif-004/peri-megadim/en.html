They cannot — Taz, incidentally; and Gemara Eruvin 70: regarding this we say since it was forbidden for part of Shabbat it is forbidden for all of Shabbat; and specifically in this, but in other matters we do not say thus; see letter 1; and end of seif 3, partition made on Shabbat. And see Perishah who likewise wrote two courtyards with a wall between them without an opening, that it is impossible that they make an eruv together, and the wall fell on Shabbat — non-Jews, members of this courtyard, to renounce their domain to members of another courtyard, see there; and I did not understand, for in siman 378 seif 2 it is explained in such a case each was permitted for itself; and it may be said they did not each make an eruv for itself and the wall was breached on Shabbat — two courtyards to renounce their domain to one of the courtyard members who carries out from his house to the courtyard in such a case — we do not say once it was permitted it is permitted; and it requires further study.
