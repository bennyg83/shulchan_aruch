Even the one who relinquishes, etc. Tosafot on 64 makes clear that even if many households relinquish their rights to one person, it is permitted. One should not object from siman 380, seif 4, where many are not considered guests. There, carrying from one's house to the courtyard appears as a retraction. Here, however, he carries from his own house into his fellow's house. I write this to exclude the Perishah, who discusses at length another strained distinction, namely that each individual house is regarded as a single person.
