[3] For he reverts etc. — Taz questioned: what is different from siman 371 seif 2 regarding an Israelite from this courtyard who went to dwell elsewhere etc., that once it was permitted it is not forbidden; and he answered that there the permission depends on the entrance of Shabbat, for he must remove it from his mind at the entrance of Shabbat that this Shabbat should not be in this house; therefore the permission depends on the entrance of Shabbat alone, which is not so here, where he can renounce domain even from nightfall, as written siman 380; therefore the permission must be for all of Shabbat specifically, until here. And this is not correct, for I already explained at the beginning of siman 371 that even when he goes on Shabbat and changes his mind it is permitted; and further, it is difficult — in the Mishnah daf 79 it is explained that according to all it is forbidden; and if so it should be difficult for Beit Shammai who ruled there that they renounce only from while it is still day; and if we say Beit Shammai holds like R' Meir in the Mishnah daf 86, if so let it say like the tannaim; and in any case his answer is forced. And in my humble opinion, initially it is not difficult at all, for there he changed his mind for all of Shabbat, which is not so here when they renounced — they did not renounce for all of Shabbat but only for as long as they do not need, as written at the end of this siman; and see seif 3 seif 4 in siman 371.
