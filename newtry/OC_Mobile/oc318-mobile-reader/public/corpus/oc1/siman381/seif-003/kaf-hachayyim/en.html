3) There. And afterward he carried from his house into the courtyard, etc. If he brought an object inside, however, it is permitted. This is the correct text, as Eshel Avraham and Machatzis HaShekel likewise write. An exception is when he also renounced his rights in his house, in which case bringing an object inside is forbidden. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2, however, writes that the cited words of Magen Avraham require examination. Even HaOzer, siman 380, se'if 4, likewise challenges these words of Magen Avraham, as does Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 2. See there. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 2, however, agrees with Magen Avraham and resolves the objections of Eliyah Rabbah and Even HaOzer. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1, rules likewise.
