1) [Seif 1] An old sukkah, etc. This refers to old s'chach, but regarding the walls there is no concern according to all opinions. Taz, s.k. 1; Mishbetzos Zahav, sec. 1; Beit HaSho'eivah, sec. 4; Mishna Berurah, sec. 1. They also do not require any renewal. Mishbetzos Zahav, ibid.; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 2; Mishna Berurah, Shaar HaTziyun, sec. 1.
2) Ibid. That is, he made it before entering, etc. For if he made it within 30 days before the festival, since the laws of the festival are studied beginning 30 days before the festival, one who makes it without specification is presumed to make it for the sake of the festival. Rashi, Sukkah 9a; Beit Yosef; Levush.
