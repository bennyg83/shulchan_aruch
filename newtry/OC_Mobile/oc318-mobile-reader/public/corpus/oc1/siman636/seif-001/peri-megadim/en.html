Sukkah - See Taz. Walls are valid from twenty-four, and they too do not require renewal. See siman 638, letter 1, regarding the verse according to Beit Hillel and Beit Shammai; see there. In Sukkah 9a, from it we learn that one must renew something in its body, specifically in the roofing, and what he renewed in it by adding a fourth wall or the like does not help, for renewal is required in the roofing.
