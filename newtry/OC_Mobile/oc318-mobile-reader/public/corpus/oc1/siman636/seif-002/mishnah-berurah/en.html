(8) It is not recognizable - If he lifts the roofing and places it back down for the sake of sukkah, it is valid, for even the ceiling of a house can be made valid as roofing through this act, as explained in siman 631. The same applies to a sukkah that he made for the sake of the festival and afterward lived in it all year: when Sukkot arrives, he must lift the roofing and place it back down for the sake of sukkah for this reason; merely uncovering the roof does not suffice.
(9) In which he lived for the sake of the mitzvah - Mere renewal does not help in this case as above, since it was his actual dwelling there all year, where he ate, drank, and slept.
(10) And with the outer one he fulfills - Nevertheless he must renew something in it, for although he made it for shade, he did not make it for the sake of sukkah.
