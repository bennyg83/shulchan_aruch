But before thirty days it needs renewal, because it was made only for shade. Automatically, regarding ganbach and rakbash sukkot, we require only that it be for shade; for this reason, even within thirty days its law is like before thirty days: according to Beit Shammai it is invalid, and according to Beit Hillel it requires renewal.
