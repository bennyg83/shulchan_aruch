That is - See Taz. One should not say: all the more so within thirty days, similar to before thirty days, and renewal should be required within thirty days. From where would we know this? Within thirty days we interpret it as presumably made for its sake, and it does not need renewal. So wrote the Levush; see there. Even according to the one who says that if it was made at the beginning of the year for the sake of the festival it requires renewal, as the Bach of blessed memory wrote, and as we intend to say below, with God's help, in letter 3, nevertheless within thirty days it does not require renewal. For when else should he make the sukkah? Regarding matzah, some say after midday, but this is not relevant to sukkah. Rather, it teaches us the explanation of the Mishnah: What is an old sukkah over which they disagreed? Thirty days before the festival means before the thirty days. Do not say that "thirty" means within thirty and that in this case Beit Hillel validates; it teaches otherwise. See the Ran and Beit Yosef, and what I will write, with God's help, in the following letter.
