1) [Se'if 1] And he did not know that he was within the city's techum, etc. Although he did not know that the city was within his techum and intended to establish his Shabbos residence at his present location, since he would have established his residence only together with the city's residents had he known that the city was within his techum, he is regarded as one who established residence with them. He may enter the city, walk throughout it, and proceed two thousand amos beyond it. Beis Yosef; Olas Shabbos, subsection 1; Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 1. Perishah writes that Rambam does not hold this view and that Beis Yosef did not see Rashi's wording; see Ma'amar Mordechai there, who challenges his words. Bach likewise writes that according to Rashi's explanation, the person did not intend to establish a Shabbos residence, etc. This requires examination, for Rashi's explanation at the end of the Mishnah explicitly accords with Beis Yosef, as Perishah and Ma'amar Mordechai there also write. Shulchan Aruch HaRav rules in accordance with Beis Yosef, and the later authorities write likewise.
