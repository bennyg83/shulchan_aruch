(ב) All who uphold, etc. Shabbat night meals correspond to Shabbat Bereishit — save from travails of the Messiah; the morning meal corresponds to Shabbat of the giving of the Torah — save from Gehinnom, for it does not rule over Torah learners; and the third meal corresponds to Shabbat of the future — save from Gog and Magog (Bach).
