[5] [Levush:] His hands immediately, etc. Maharil, in Hilchos Sukkah, writes that one should rise, walk about, and wait briefly so that his attention is diverted; otherwise, it is regarded as one continuous meal. Malbushei Yom Tov and all the later authorities rule similarly. Maharshal writes that one should make a brief interruption with words of Torah or by walking about a little. Shibbolei HaLeket writes that his father would make a short interruption by reciting a brief chapter of Tehillim, such as “Hashem is my Shepherd; I shall not lack.” See also siman 8, siman 100.
