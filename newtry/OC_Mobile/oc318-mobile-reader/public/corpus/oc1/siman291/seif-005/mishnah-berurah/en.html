(22) Must make it with bread - Since we derive the three meals from the three times the word "today" is written regarding the manna, one must make it with bread. It is also a mitzvah at the outset to increase delicacies according to his ability [Acharonim].
(23) And some say that he may make it with any, etc. - According to everyone, the choicest mitzvah is with bread and two loaves, as above in seif 4; they disagree only regarding after the fact, whether he fulfilled the obligation with this [from the Bach].
(24) From one of the five, etc. - On which one recites borei minei mezonot. The reason for this view is that since it is not usual to eat the third meal with appetite, this suffices; this is also the reason for the other views that are lenient in this [Tosefet Shabbat].
(25) For it is forbidden to him, etc. - Therefore he should fulfill it with types of fried foods made from crushed matzah, or with meat and fish, or fruit.
