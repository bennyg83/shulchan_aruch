Seif 5: It must be made with bread, etc. Note: Regarding the reasoning behind the opinion of the poskim that bread is not required for the third meal, although they all derive the three meals equally from the three occurrences of ‘today’ written in the Torah, see the sound resolution I wrote concerning this in my commentary on the Torah from the year 5604, in parashas Shelach, on the verse, ‘The Children of Israel were in the wilderness.’ See there, with God's help, and examine it carefully. Regarding Yaaleh V'Yavo: he may begin Yaaleh V'Yavo and complete it from that point onward, but he should not begin Retzei itself. Likewise, regarding Atah Chonantanu, if he recited Atah Chonantanu out of its proper place, he should not begin again from Atah Chonen; since he is not required to repeat, repeating it would constitute an interruption.
