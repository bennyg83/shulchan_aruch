6) There, gloss: One who did not eat on Shabbos night, etc. The same applies to one who did not eat on Yom Tov night: he should eat two meals during the day. He likewise recites the nighttime Kiddush. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. This means that siman 271, seif 8, states that if one did not recite Kiddush at night, he recites it during the day but omits Vayechulu; see there. Magen Avraham teaches that just as Yom Tov is equivalent to Shabbos regarding making up the meal during the day, it is likewise equivalent regarding making up the nighttime Kiddush during the day. Machatzis HaShekel.
