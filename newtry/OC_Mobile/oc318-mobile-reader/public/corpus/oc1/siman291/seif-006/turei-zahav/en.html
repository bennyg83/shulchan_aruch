Women are obligated, etc., for regarding all of them he says 'eat it today,' and among men and among women this eating multiplied. He wrote in Shulchan Aruch siman 269 that on Shabbat night the meal must be completed at night; and Rashal practiced that one who did not eat on Shabbat night did not eat until nightfall.
