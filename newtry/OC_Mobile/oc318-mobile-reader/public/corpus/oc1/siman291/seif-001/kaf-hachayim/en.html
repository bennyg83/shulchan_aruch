(1) [seif 1] One must be very careful to fulfill the third meal. For R' Yehoshua ben Levi said: R' Yehoshua ben Levi in the name of bar Kappara: all who uphold three meals on Shabbat are saved from three punishments: from the travails of the Messiah, from the judgment of Gehinnom, and from the war of Gog and Magog. And R' Yosei said: may my portion be with those who uphold three meals on Shabbat. Tur.
