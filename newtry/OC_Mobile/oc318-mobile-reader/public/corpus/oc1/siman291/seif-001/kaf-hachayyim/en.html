1) [Seif 1] One should be exceedingly careful to fulfill the third meal. Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi said in the name of Bar Kappara: Whoever fulfills the three meals is saved from three calamities, the travail of Mashiach, the judgment of Gehinnom, and the war of Gog and Magog. Rabbi Yosi said, 'May my portion be among those who fulfill the three Shabbos meals.' Tur. The sacred Zohar, parashas Yisro, folio 88a, likewise states: A person must further delight himself three times, because supernal faith depends upon these, upon the holy Ancient One, Zeir Anpin, and the field of apples. A person must delight and rejoice in them. One who omits one of these meals causes a defect above, and that person's punishment is great, etc. Rabbi Shimon said: Regarding one who completes the three Shabbos meals, a voice issues and proclaims, 'Then you shall delight in Hashem.' This is one meal, corresponding to the holy Ancient One of all holy beings. 'I shall cause you to ride upon the high places of the earth.' This is the second meal, corresponding to the field of holy apples. 'I shall feed you the inheritance of Yaakov your forefather.' This is the perfection completed in Zeir Anpin, and corresponding to these a person must complete his meals, etc.; see there. Thus, each of the three meals has a supernal root and an esoteric reason. One who neglects any of them receives great punishment, while one who fulfills them receives great reward. See above, siman 242, paragraph 2.
