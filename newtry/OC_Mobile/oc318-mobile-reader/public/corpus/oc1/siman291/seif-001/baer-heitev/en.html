With an egg[-sized portion]. Not specifically — rather a bit more than an egg, for an egg[-sized amount] is called casual eating, as siman 232 Magen Avraham wrote; and in Sefer Eliyahu Rabbah he disagrees; and likewise it implies in Mordechai, etc., that it is not better than Shabbat night — for we say a person should always set his table on Shabbat night even if he needs only an olive[-sized portion] — one who has food for two meals: if he divides them into three meals he will not have enough for each meal to be satisfied; better he eat two meals to satisfaction (Sefer Chassidim siman 791). And it appears in any case if the morning meal extended until after noon he should divide into two as written seif 3 — for in any case he will be satisfied when he eats again, Magen Avraham. And see siman 444 seif 1 that even before noon he may divide into two — for some say one fulfills thereby, see there. (And in Sefer Eliyahu Rabbah he disagrees with Magen Avraham in what he ruled to divide the morning meal into two — also regarding if the morning meal extended until after noon; see there at length with deep pilpul on this:)
