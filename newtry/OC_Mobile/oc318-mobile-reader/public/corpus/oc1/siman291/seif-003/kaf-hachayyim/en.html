3) There: One should be exceedingly careful, etc. The phrase 'exceedingly careful' is used because in the morning a person desires to eat and drink, delights in abundant foods, fruit, and delicacies, and seeks to complete the count of one hundred blessings. He may fill his stomach so fully that when the time for the third meal arrives, his morning meal has still not been digested. The Shulchan Aruch therefore says that he must be exceedingly careful and look ahead, not filling his stomach to such an extent, so that he will desire to eat the third meal at its proper time. Bach. Machazik Berachah, paragraph 1, writes that perhaps the intention is that this meal requires special care because it demonstrates the honor of Shabbos. It is something one does not ordinarily do during the week, when people principally eat once at night and once during the day. With the third meal, one magnifies the honor of Shabbos by making an additional meal. One who takes care to fulfill it is rewarded even for the night and second meals as though he ate them specifically in honor of Shabbos. If he is negligent about the third meal, however, this shows that he did not eat the first two in honor of Shabbos; see there.
