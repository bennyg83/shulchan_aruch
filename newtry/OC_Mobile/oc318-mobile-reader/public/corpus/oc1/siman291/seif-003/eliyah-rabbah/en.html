[6] If he knows, etc. It appears to me that even if Yom Tov falls on Motzaei Shabbos, if he knows he will be able to eat again before the tenth hour, he should not divide the meal into two (Magen Avraham). This implies that bread is specifically required even on Erev Yom Tov, as is also implied at the end of seif 5 and in Responsa Maharil, siman 99. See siman 529, seif katan 2. It also implies that the matter depends on the tenth hour and there is no distinction between before or after Minchah.
