[4] From six, etc. — because I saw here a dispute of great early authorities not mentioned in Beit Yosef I must mention them. Rokeach siman 54 wrote one fulfills three meals with dessert of a meal eaten at the morning meal — things one does not regularly eat on a weekday such as fish or fruits even before noon; and likewise Mordechai and Agudah in the name of Rabeinu Yechiel; and Ba'al Halachot Gedolot and Ran wrote that only Birkat Hamazon constitutes an interruption; and everywhere they agree one fulfills even before noon; however bread is required. This reasoning Kelbo daf 41 and Tashbetz siman 23 and Shibulei Haleket and a baraita in the name of Targum Yerushalmi wrote; but in Shibulei Haleket there he wrote some practice as written: before four hours one does not fulfill; and proof from that which we learned: on Shabbat eve of Pesach that falls on Shabbat they leave food for two meals; and Sefer Yere'im wrote: that which the Mishnah teaches two meals means two satiations, and those two satiations they divide into three meals, end of his words. And one may ask: on Shabbat 117b we learned on Shabbat night one saves food for three meals — why did it not also teach food for two meals? And it may be said lechatchila they agree one must eat after six and a half — meaning three satiations — only b'dieved one also fulfills where one cannot when dividing the morning meal; and it appears more that Sefer Yere'im holds from there there is no proof; but in truth from the Mishnah of Shabbat in any case there is complete proof — for it teaches in the end: at minchah one saves one meal; and in vessels — since they immerse at minchah, evidently its time is at minchah; and with this Tosafot Shabbat 118 and Rosh and Rabbeynu Yerucham and Sefer Tzeda LaDerech and Avudraham are resolved — that one does not fulfill before minchah; and Shibulei Haleket wrote in the name of R' Yitzchak of Mirinsh who asked dream questions and they answered him and Moses said "eat it today," etc. — how are days divided from each other? So the hours — three meals, etc. And in Sefer Chassidim siman 961: a person who has only food for two meals — if they divide them into three meals he will not have enough for each meal to be satisfied; better he eat two meals of night and day, or eat by day and when he eats enough to bless he blesses and afterward eats, end of his words. And what he wrote "or eat by day" implies he will not eat at night but leave both meals for day — and it requires study, for the night meal is preferable to the third meal — for at night universally bread is required; and likewise if he erred in Birkat Hamazon at night he repeats and not at the third meal, siman 188 seif 8; also it is puzzling to explain what he wrote "and when he eats enough to bless," etc.; and it may be he states the law that Birkat Hamazon constitutes interruption per Ba'al Halachot Gedolot mentioned above — and it is strained. And it appears more to me better not to divide the meal; and if he does not wish to do so he divides the meal — meaning when he eats an olive or egg which is enough to bless Birkat Hamazon on it, he blesses and returns and eats per Sefer Yere'im mentioned above; and Magen Avraham brought it briefly and did not notice all this; also what he disagrees that better to divide — it appears his reason he holds it forbidden to divide the morning meal in half; and this appears to me also the view of Tosafot and Rosh and their supporters; and likewise explained in Sefer HaManhig siman 63 — unlike Magen Avraham who understood that universally there is no prohibition in dividing the morning meal, only because it is fit to eat a third meal after minchah which does not exist; also what he wrote at the beginning of siman 444 on Pesach eve to divide before four [hours] and after minchah eat fish or fruits — and Bach wrote likewise — meaning he acts with stringency of all views; and why I explained stringency that comes to leniency: he divides the morning meal; and Rama and Levush end of this siman and beginning siman 444 imply he should not divide. I also saw in small gloss of Mordechai from parchment copied a prepared responsum of his teacher Yosef Bechor Shor — this is the essence: primarily rely on parperet that they eat at the morning meal; and those proofs (in the Gemara I brought above) that imply they act at minchah deal when making a third meal from bread — for one cannot do before minchah even if he interrupts with Birkat Hamazon, for all is considered one meal with the morning meal; also it is not honor of Shabbat to make his meal in halves; also it is a blessing in vain — they did not finish their meal and bless, end of his words. And even though we follow Rosh and his supporters I brought — nevertheless there is proof for Sefer Chassidim mentioned above and as I explained the view of Tosafot and Rosh; and see Hagahot Tashbetz siman 23. Also what Magen Avraham wrote that universally if he began before noon and ate until after noon he fulfills — for in any case he ate after noon — in my humble opinion it is not clear, for whoever begins before noon is like dividing the morning meal; also we establish siman 188 that everything follows the beginning of the meal; and likewise it implies from proofs of Tosafot and Rosh that at minchah one saves, etc.; and in any case immediately after six it appears one fulfills; and likewise Rokeach end siman 54.
