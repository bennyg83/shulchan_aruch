Magen Avraham seif katan 3 For in any event he ate, etc. Although if the morning meal continued until the time of Minchah he must specifically interrupt the meal, and otherwise he has not fulfilled it because we follow the beginning of the meal, nevertheless if he already ate the morning meal and afterward began another meal before midday and it continued until after midday (not literally, but meaning after six and a half hours), in such a case we do not follow the beginning. This is specifically after the fact; but if he knows that it will continue, etc., and with that intent he began, he commits a prohibition, as written in Magen Avraham siman 232 seif katan 11, and the Magen Avraham compares it to the case at the beginning of siman 471 in Magen Avraham seif katan 1.
