One who drinks on Yom Kippur a mouthful {i.e., a full mouth} is liable; and we measure for each person according to what he is — the large according to his size and the small according to his smallness — and not a literal "full mouth," but rather [enough] that when he pushes it to one side of his mouth it appears as a full mouth; and this is less than a revi'is for an average person. And all liquids combine to [reach] the measure. {Rama: If he drank liquids that are not fit for drinking — such as brine, muryas, or undiluted vinegar — he is exempt; but diluted vinegar, he is liable (Tur).}
