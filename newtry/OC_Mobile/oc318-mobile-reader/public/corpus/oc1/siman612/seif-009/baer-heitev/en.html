And vinegar. Even a large amount, Magen Avraham. The Taz concluded that the exemption with unfit things applies according to the liability measure of fit things, namely foods in their measure and liquids in their measure; with that measure he is exempt when they are unfit. But if he greatly increased the amount, he is liable even with unfit things because of the advantage of the large amount, as we find in Yoreh De'ah siman 103 regarding something that gives a bad taste; see there. The Magen Avraham wrote that the case where vinegar is exempt is specifically when it bubbles when thrown on the ground, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 123, se'if 6.
