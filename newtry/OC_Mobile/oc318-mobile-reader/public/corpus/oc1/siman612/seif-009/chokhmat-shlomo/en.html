(There, se'if 9 in the gloss) "If he drank liquids that are not fit for drinking, such as brine or muryas, or undiluted vinegar, he is exempt." Note: From his language it implies that with brine and muryas he is exempt even if diluted. This is astonishing to me. If so, what forced the Talmud in Yoma 81 and Kerisus 18b to say that this Mishnah, "if one drank brine or muryas he is exempt," since vinegar would make him liable, is according to Rabbi? Perhaps it follows even the Rabbis and speaks when diluted, and teaches that brine and muryas are exempt even when diluted, whereas vinegar is liable when diluted; and the Mishnah would still follow the Rabbis. From this it is proved that when diluted, even brine and muryas make one liable. This is not as implied by the Rav's words here. This requires study; examine carefully.
