And undiluted vinegar, he is exempt - One must say this is specifically when it bubbles when thrown on the ground, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 123, se'if 6. In the Gemara it implies that with a large amount of vinegar he is liable, and the Beis Yosef and the Darkei Moshe pressed to explain this. In truth, reason dictates the opposite: when one drinks a large amount of strong vinegar, it harms him even more. The main view is as the Bach wrote, that this refers to diluted vinegar, where if he drinks a large amount he is liable. So is the textual reading in the Rosh. So too it appears in the Rosh, chapter 7 of Berachos, who wrote that the law that one recites a blessing over vinegar refers to when he puts a large amount of vinegar into a stew, etc.; see there. This implies that without stew, even a large amount is not considered drinking; see there.
