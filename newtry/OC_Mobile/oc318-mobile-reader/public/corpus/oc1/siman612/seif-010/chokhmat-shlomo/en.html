(Seif 10) Some say combining drinks is time to eat half a loaf. In my opinion: first opinion that drinking measure is revi'it contradicts Keritut ch. 3; Beit Yosef — Ravad and question on Rambam; perhaps on Yom Kippur since affliction depends on settling mind, drinking within eating time counts; but for other prohibitions not — see there and my Orah Chayim on Rosh Hashanah 9 and drasha Erev Yom Kippur 543.
