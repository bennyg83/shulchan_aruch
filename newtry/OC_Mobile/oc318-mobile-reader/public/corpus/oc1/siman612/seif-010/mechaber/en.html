If one drank a little and then drank again: if, from the beginning of the first drinking until the end of the last drinking, there is [only as much time as] the drinking of a revi'is, they combine to [reach] the measure; and if not, they do not combine. And some say that the measure for the combining of drinkings is [the time of] "eating a peras," like the combining of eatings. {Rama: It is permitted to touch food and drink on Yom Kippur to give to children, and we are not concerned that he will eat or drink if he touches (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 147).}
