(31) And some say - From the language of the Mechaber it implies that the main view is the first opinion. But the Pri Chadash and the Gra wrote that the main view is the second opinion.
(32) And we are not concerned, etc. - This is not comparable to chametz on Pesach, which is forbidden to touch because he may come to eat it, as is evident above in siman 446, se'if 3. On Pesach he eats other things, and therefore we are concerned that he may also eat this. This is not so on Yom Kippur, when he is separated from every food and drink. Also, the awe of the Day of Judgment is upon him, and therefore we are not concerned that he will forget. Even if the child can take it himself, one should also not be stringent in this.
