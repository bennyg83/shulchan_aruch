Whether for a dwarf, etc. There is no difference whether he eats permitted foods or forbidden foods, such as piggul, nosar, impure food, a neveilah, or a tereifah, because through all of them his mind is settled. Scripture used the inclusive expressions "you shall afflict" and "you shall afflict yourselves" to include that the prohibition of affliction takes effect upon every item, even upon an item to which a prohibition already applies. If he did not afflict himself, even by refraining from forbidden food, he is liable to kares, and with regard to these foods he incurs two liabilities, one because of the food's own prohibition and one because of Yom Kippur. Levush; Pri Chadash; Bigdei Yesha; Mishna Berurah, sec. 12. This is from the Gemara, Yoma 74b and Kerisos 13b. See Chullin 100b and 101, and examine carefully.
