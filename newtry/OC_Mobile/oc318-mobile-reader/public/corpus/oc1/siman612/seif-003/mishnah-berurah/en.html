(6) He ate and then ate again - The same applies if he eats without interruption, but crumbled the food into small crumbs and ate them, and through this his eating took a long time: we also require that from the beginning of his eating until its end there be only the time of eating a peras.
(7) And if not - Meaning that he delayed in his eating more than the time of eating a peras, the beginning of the eating does not join with the end to make him liable to kareis.
