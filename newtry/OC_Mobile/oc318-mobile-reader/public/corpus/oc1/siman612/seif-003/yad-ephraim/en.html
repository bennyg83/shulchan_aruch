Note 6 — we do not decree lest eat, etc. In my opinion: do not nullify Yom Kippur service; siman 588 Taz note 5; on shofar Rosh Hashanah only when happens to be onen we decree; also if onen can use another kohen; if decree lest eat on all Yom Kippur no end; when happens to be onen clear no decree; his proof can be rejected — differs when not fasting; Ran: permitted to touch unlike Pesach.
