Se'if 6. And if he ate, etc. As written in the first chapter of Megillah, 7b.
And it is forbidden, etc. Meaning, although at the beginning of the second chapter of Berachos 14a they say that one may taste and there is nothing in it, that is as stated there: he accepted upon himself eating and drinking, etc. This is not so here. See chapter 7 of Chullin: granted, terumah he tastes, etc.
