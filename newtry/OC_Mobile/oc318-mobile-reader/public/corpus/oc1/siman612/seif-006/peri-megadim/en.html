Until - See Taz. What is written in Yoma 81b: at first glance, if exempt means permitted, from where do we know that the Mishnah is not like Rabbi? Perhaps brine or muryas is exempt and permitted. For if so, it should have said brine and muryas are permitted. He concludes that foods that are entirely unfit, like dirt or mere wood, are exempt and permitted; and foods that are not fit are exempt but forbidden at least rabbinically, or perhaps from the Torah whenever they are not fit for any person. See Pri Chadash.
