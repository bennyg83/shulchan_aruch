If one ate foods that are not fit for eating, or ate in a gorged manner — such as immediately following [another] eating that he ate on Erev Yom Kippur, until he was disgusted by his food — he is exempt. {Rama: And if he ate spiced or seasoned foods following his [previous] eating, he is liable, for there is always room for sweets (Kol Bo). And it is forbidden on Yom Kippur to taste anything and spit it out, even fragrant woods; and see above, siman 567, se'if 3, in the gloss.}
