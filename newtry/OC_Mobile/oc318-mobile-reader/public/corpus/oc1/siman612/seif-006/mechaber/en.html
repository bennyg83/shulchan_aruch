If one eats (on Yom Kippur) food that is not suitable for eating or they eat excessive food immediately after the food which they ate on the Eve of Yom Kippur until one gets sick of his food, he is exempt. {Rama: Hagah : If one eats artificially scented food or spiced food in addition to his meal, he is guilty (for eating on Yom Kippur) because there is always some room for some special delicacy, ( כל בו ). 93 Kol Bo , כל בו , see footnote 34. And it is forbidden on Yom Kippur to taste anything in order to spit it out, even spicy woods.
