[6] It is permitted to touch, etc. This is not comparable to chametz, siman 446, se'if 3, for Yom Kippur is different, since the awe of the Day of Judgment is upon him (Taz). It is somewhat difficult why the Gemara in Yoma 14 did not answer this way. Terumas ha-Deshen, siman 147, answered that on Pesach we are more concerned because he eats other things. Regarding what the Magen Avraham asked from Yoma there against those who forbid touching when one does not need to give it to a child, it seems to me that this is not difficult at all, for kohanim are especially zealous. However, Maharil brings proof from the showbread, which the kohanim did not distribute on Yom Kippur lest they eat.
