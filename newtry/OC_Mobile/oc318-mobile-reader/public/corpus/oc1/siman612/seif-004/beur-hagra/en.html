Se'if 4. Some say. Like Rabbi Yochanan ben Berokah, who teaches it in the Gemara, and the Sages, etc.
And some say. Tosefta and Tur, like Rabbi Shimon, for the sugya of the Talmud everywhere follows him, that food impurity is with an egg's bulk.
Heaping. There: Rabbi Yehudah says two heaping eggs, etc.; the Sages say an egg and a half, heaping.
