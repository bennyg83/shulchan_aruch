[4] Less than a revi'is, etc. In siman 190, se'if 3, and siman 271, se'if 13, he wrote most of a revi'is. However, everything depends on the person. With a large person we require more than a revi'is, even though in the above simanim it is explained that a revi'is suffices for every person. Yom Kippur is different. So too it implies in the Bach there.
