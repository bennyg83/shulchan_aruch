(Subparagraph 4) And it is obvious that it is permitted to smell. This is unlike what the Shelah wrote in tractate Ta'anis and tractate Yoma, that it is forbidden to smell on Tisha B'Av and Yom Kippur. He brought proof from what is written in siman 556, that when Tisha B'Av falls on Sunday we do not recite a blessing over spices on Motzaei Shabbos. The Magen Avraham there in siman 556 rejected his proof; see there.
And it is good to do so, etc. See above, siman 46, end of subparagraph 8, where he wrote as follows: However, one cannot recite a blessing every time unless there was diversion of attention; see siman 217, end quote. So too wrote the Shelah.
