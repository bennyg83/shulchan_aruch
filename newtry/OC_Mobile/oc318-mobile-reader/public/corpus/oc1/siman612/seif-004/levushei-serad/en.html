There And this is what he calls "a little," etc. This is difficult, for Rav Gidel said that even with a little there is no permission at the outset. Perhaps there is a distinction between unfit foods and unfit liquids, for at this point, in this question, the Taz had not yet introduced this reasoning, that there is no difference between foods and liquids, until afterward. Now the Taz asked only that all exemptions of Yom Kippur are only after the fact. To this one can properly say that what was taught, that unfit foods are exempt but forbidden, means with the measure, but less than the measure is permitted. What the Taz wrote, "and this is what he calls a little," means that we see at least that he distinguishes between a little and the measure, and one can say the same distinction applies to this matter too. This is easy to understand.
