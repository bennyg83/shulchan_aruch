To touch - See Taz. It implies that on other fasts, the four fasts, it is forbidden to touch foods unnecessarily, as with chametz on Pesach, where it is forbidden to touch even that of a non-Jew, and one may only push it away with a reed, as in siman 446 in the Taz and Magen Avraham letter 5. It also implies that even rabbinic chametz, nuksheh, is forbidden to touch. The same would apply to the four fasts, which are rabbinic: it is forbidden to touch foods. See Magen Avraham letter 6, and with the help of Heaven it will be explained there.
