(Subparagraph 2) That he ate on the eve of Yom Kippur; but if he ate on Yom Kippur, etc. In the Beis Yosef he wrote in the name of the Kol Bo that one can find such a case with one who eats on Yom Kippur, such as if he first ate foods that are not fit until he was full, for he is exempt for eating them. Afterward he ate fit foods, and he is also exempt for eating the fit foods, since he ate them as gluttonous eating.
