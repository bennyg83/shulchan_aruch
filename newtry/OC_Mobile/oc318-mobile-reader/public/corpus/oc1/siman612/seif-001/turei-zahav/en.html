Like a large date, he is liable. One should examine why the Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote the law of liability, when regarding the prohibition it is forbidden even in any amount, as written in se'if 5. It is not the way of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch to write except the prohibition, and the Beis Yosef is accustomed to be exact about this in Yoreh De'ah many times. It seems to me that here there is practical relevance for the matter mentioned in siman 618, that when we feed a sick person, we feed him in a manner that will not combine to the measure. Therefore we need to know the measure of liability. So too the Beis Yosef answered.
And it is a little less than an egg's bulk. It seems to me that he needed first to give the measure of like a date, and afterward less than an egg, because in the Mishnah it says the measure is like a date, and we ask whether it means with the pit or not. In the Yerushalmi they further said that there is a doubt whether we measure by its hollow, meaning the hollow between the date and the pit inside it. Therefore he ruled for us that the measure is a little less than an egg; through this the measure becomes clear to us.
