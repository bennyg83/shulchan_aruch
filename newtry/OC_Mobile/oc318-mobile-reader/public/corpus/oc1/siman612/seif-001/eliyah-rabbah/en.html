[1] And some say it is three, etc. So he wrote in the Shulchan Aruch. According to what we have received, whenever two opinions are written in the language "some say," he agrees with the latter. So too it implies in the Beis Yosef and Darkei Moshe. It is difficult, for in siman 618, se'if 7, they ruled four eggs. One can say that here, regarding lashes, we are lenient, that with anything that is three he is not liable; but regarding delaying below, he is stringent and requires four. However, this is difficult regarding the Levush in Yoreh De'ah siman 98, who wrote plainly that it is four, even regarding lashes.
