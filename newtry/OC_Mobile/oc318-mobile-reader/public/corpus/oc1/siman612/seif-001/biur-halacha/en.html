Like a large date - It is explained in the Talmud that "like a date" means with its pit. At first glance, if he ate the date itself he would be exempt, for the space of the pit is missing, and the pit itself cannot be joined, since it is hard and not at all fit for human consumption, as is evident in Shabbos 29. According to this, even if he swallowed it together with the fruit, at first glance it does not help, although it is a positive factor regarding a blessing; see siman 210 in Mishnah Berurah subparagraph 7. For here we require settling his mind, and less than the measure does not settle his mind. I found the same in Ma'amar Mordechai, and he wrote to question the language of the Bach, which implies the opposite. However, according to this it is somewhat unsettled that the tanna gave the measure for eating on Yom Kippur as a date, when the date itself is not included. Later I found at the end of chapter Notel in Shabbos, in the Rif there in the first explanation, that there are soft dates that are eaten with their pits, meaning even by people. According to the second explanation, however, they are eaten only by animals. See chapter 11 of Terumos in the Rambam's commentary to the Mishnah, mishnah 5. This requires study.
