To touch; see Ba'er Hetev. See Magen Avraham, who brings proof from Yoma, and see Eliyah Rabbah. See Responsa Har ha-Carmel, siman 21, who wondered about the Magen Avraham in this and wrote to suggest that whenever it is possible for one not to occupy himself with something forbidden to eat, one can say that he is obligated to distance himself, and one should forbid touching his food; see there. In Devar Shmuel, siman 98, he wrote that mohalim who customarily perform suction with wine, on fast days they should not do it by sprinkling with the mouth, but with the hand or with absorbent material, without the medium of the mouth, and all the more so on Tisha B'Av and Yom Kippur; see there. So too the Eliyah Rabbah wrote in siman 626 in the name of Kitzur Shelah; see there. It seems that on Yom Kippur one should not use absorbent material because of concern for wringing, but only sprinkling by hand. See Dagul Merevavah, who wrote that the Magen Avraham's proof from the loaves is from the Yerushalmi, and therefore one must accept the words of the Yerushalmi with awe; see there.
