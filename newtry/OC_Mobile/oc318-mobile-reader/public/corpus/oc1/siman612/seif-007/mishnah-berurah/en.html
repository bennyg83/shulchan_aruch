(19) Reed leaves - Some have the reading "grape leaves." The reason is that they are not fit to be eaten.
(20) That sprouted - Meaning that they blossomed, as we translate, "and it produced a flower and brought forth a blossom," as "and it brought forth buds."
(21) From Rosh Hashanah until Yom Kippur, etc. - Because they are still moist and soft, and fit to be eaten.
