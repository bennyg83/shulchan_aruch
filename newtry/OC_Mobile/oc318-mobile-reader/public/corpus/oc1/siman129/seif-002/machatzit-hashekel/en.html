(Summary 2) Close, etc. — and on a fast they prayed close to sunset, for otherwise they could not eat until sunset because of the fast; and since the time of fast-day Mincha is not equal to the time of Mincha all year, it is not confused; unlike Mincha on Yom Kippur, where they also hurry to pray Mincha like all year in order to pray ne'ilah in its time, as below siman 622 — if so its time equals the time of Mincha all year and it is confused.
