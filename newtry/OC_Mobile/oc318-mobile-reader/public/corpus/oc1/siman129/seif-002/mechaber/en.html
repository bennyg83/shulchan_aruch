A kohen who transgressed and ascended to the duchan on Yom Kippur at Mincha — since it is known there is no drunkenness there, behold he lifts his hands and they do not remove him, because of suspicion lest they say he is disqualified and for that reason they removed him.And therefore on Mincha of Yom Kippur they say Elokeinu and Elokei avoteinu, even though it is not fit for nesiat kapayim; nevertheless, since if he ascended he does not descend, it is called somewhat fit [Hagahot Maimoniyot]. And so they practice in these countries, even though there are disputants.
