(2) And if ne'ilah prayer was delayed until night — Maharil wrote they lift hands, for limbs and fats of the tamid offering were burned at night; and Darkhei Moshe brought in siman 623 note 1, Magen Avraham there summary 3. But Bach page 26 wrote: if ne'ilah extends until nightfall they cannot lift hands, for there is no nesiat kapayim at night; and wrote that even if he ascended he descends, see there; and they brought Magen Avraham there. Peri Chadash in this siman note 1, 11 in Hagahot — however in responsa Be'er Eshek siman 98 wrote: if b'dieved ne'ilah extended until night the kohanim lift hands; and so in the name of Rama of Fano; and they brought 11 in siman 623 in Hagah Beit Yosef and shatz Dikdukei Sofrim, see there; and so Mahari Ukatzin in this siman; and so Birkei Yosef in siman 622 note 2 in the name of She'elat Yaavetz siman 91: what some practice, prolonging ne'ilah and nullifying nesiat kapayim — they have upon what to rely, see there; and they brought the shatz there. Therefore, since there is a dispute in this, l'chatchila one should be careful to finish ne'ilah prayer with sunset, as Maran wrote in siman 623 seif 2; and b'dieved if it extended until night and they wish to perform nesiat kapayim they have upon what to rely as mentioned; and if they mean "our Father, bless us," etc., there is no concern whatsoever, for it is only language of request; and see Magen Avraham there and in our words above siman 128 note 254.
