2) And if one delayed the Neilah prayer until night: the Maharil wrote that they [the kohanim] raise their hands, for the limbs and fats of the daily tamid offering were burned at night; and Darkei Moshe cited him in siman 623, ot 1; Magen Avraham there, s.k. 3. But the Beis Shmuel, folio 26, wrote that if the Neilah is drawn out until the emergence of the stars, they cannot raise their hands, for there is no nesias kapayim at night, and he wrote that even if one ascended [to bless], he descends; see there. And the Magen Avraham cited him there; Pri Chadash in this siman, ot 1; Yad Aharon in the Hagahos HaTur. However, in the responsa Be'er Eishek, siman 98, he wrote that if after the fact the Neilah was drawn out until night, the kohanim raise their hands; and so it is written in the name of the Rama of Fano, and Yad Aharon cited it in siman 623 in the Hagahos Beis Yosef, and the Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 142d; see there. And so wrote the Mor U'Ketziah in this siman. And so wrote the Birkei Yosef in siman 623, ot 2, in the name of the She'eilas Yaavetz, siman 51, that those who have the custom to prolong the Neilah and let the nesias kapayim lapse have that upon which to rely; see there. And the Shalmei Tzibbur cited it there. Therefore, since there is a dispute in this, l'chatchilah one should take care to complete the Neilah prayer at sunset, as Maran, of blessed memory, wrote in siman 623, seif 2; and after the fact, if it was drawn out until night and they wish to perform nesias kapayim, they have that upon which to rely, as mentioned. And if it is a case where they say 'Eloheinu v'Elohei avoseinu, barcheinu,' etc., there is no concern whatsoever, for it is merely a language of request. See the Magen Avraham there, and our words above in siman 128, ot 255.
