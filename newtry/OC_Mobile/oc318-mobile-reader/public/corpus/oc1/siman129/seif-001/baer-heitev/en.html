(On Yom Kippur.) And if ne'ilah extends until nightfall they cannot lift their hands, for there is no nesiat kapayim at night, and even if he ascended he descends. Be'er Sheva, Peri Chadash, and Shakh.

(At Mincha.) Even if he ascended he descends, as is proven in seif 2. Peri Chadash.

(The sun.) Magen Avraham wrote that even if they fast until noon and pray Mincha they say Elokeinu per our custom, see there; and see siman 121 summary 3. Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Darkhei Moshe that the shatz does not say "our Father" even on Rosh Chodesh, for it is not called a fast, and it requires study. And on Taanit Esther the shatz says "our Father" — Rosh HaLevi siman 11: on the seventh of Adar when they fast and gather together and increase selichot and supplications the shatz does not say "our Father," for it is not a fast. Ginat Veradim general rule 1 siman 33 and Yad Aharon. And an individual when he is on a private fast one should not protest when he says it; see above siman 121.
