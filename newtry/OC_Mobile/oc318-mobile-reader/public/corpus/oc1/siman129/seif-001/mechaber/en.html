In which prayers we lift hands. And it has 2 seifim:
There is no nesiat kapayim except at Shacharit and Mussaf and at ne'ilah on a day that has ne'ilah, such as Yom Kippur; but not at Mincha, because drunkenness is common at that hour, lest the kohen be drunk; and they decreed on Mincha of a fast because of Mincha of other days. But on a fast that has no ne'ilah, since the Mincha prayer is close to sunset it is similar to ne'ilah prayer and is not confused with Mincha of other days — therefore there is nesiat kapayim in it. And our custom was already explained above in siman 128.
