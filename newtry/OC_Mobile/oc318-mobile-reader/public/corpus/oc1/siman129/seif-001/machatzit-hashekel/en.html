Summary 1 and Mincha prayer, etc. — and they pray even the great Mincha, even though there is no concern for intoxication; nevertheless, since they do not pray close to sunset, it comes to be confused with Mincha of other days, as he wrote afterward; and since it is not fit for nesiat kapayim they do not say "our Father, bless us" in the blessing, as above siman 127 seif 2. But per Rama's gloss, etc. — he does not mean the gloss in seif 1, for that is not relevant here, for there Rama wrote as explained above siman 128 summary 44 that our custom is not to lift hands except on a festival; and if so for us this law does not apply whether they lift hands at Mincha on a fast. However Magen Avraham intended Rama's gloss in seif 2, where the Mechaber wrote: a kohen who ascended at Mincha on Yom Kippur does not descend — since that there is no nesiat kapayim at Mincha on Yom Kippur is only because of a decree and not everyone knows this, and they will say it is for the kohen's disgrace; and therefore Rama: since if he ascended he does not descend they say Elokeinu, etc. — if so this reason applies also when fasting until noon, for there too there is no nesiat kapayim at Mincha only because of a decree as mentioned; and if so if he ascended he does not descend, and therefore they also say Elokeinu, etc.
