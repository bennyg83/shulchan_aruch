Magen Avraham summary 1: they should not say "we mean per our custom that there is no nesiat kapayim except on a festival at Mussaf" — and it is not relevant to the Mechaber's words except regarding "our Father, bless us" as Rama wrote in the previous siman summary 44; and on this the Magen Avraham speaks, that specifically when they fast the whole day — then they pray Mincha close to sunset and there is nesiat kapayim by law; therefore per our custom they in any case say Elokeinu, unlike when the fast is only until noon — then it is confused with ordinary Mincha. And he concludes: but per Rama's words in the following seif regarding Mincha on Yom Kippur — likewise when they fast until noon, since it is known there is no drunkenness, if he ascended he does not descend — they say Elokeinu.
