And our custom, etc. This refers to the custom explained above, that kohanim raise their hands only on Yom Tov. The Levush writes that they do not do so on fast days because of a general rule not to distinguish between cases. This is surprising. From where did he derive it? He also did not consult the Kol Bo cited above. Moreover, I found explicitly in Kol Bo, siman 128, that all the early authorities write that kohanim raise their hands at Minchah on other fast days, and not one of them was concerned with such a general rule. Clearly that principle does not apply here because the cases are not confused, since Minchah on a fast is prayed near sunset. In addition, the law regarding an intoxicated kohen itself is only d'rabbanan. Therefore I say that in every place where Birkat Kohanim is performed every day, the kohanim should unquestionably raise their hands also at Minchah on Tishah BeAv, the Seventeenth of Tammuz, Tzom Gedaliah, the Tenth of Tevet, and Taanit Esther.
