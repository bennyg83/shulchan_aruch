(1) And our custom, etc. — meaning as explained above, not to lift hands except on a festival; but Levush wrote they do not practice because of lo pelug (not distinguishing); one may wonder whence he has this — he also did not look at Kolbo mentioned above; and further I found explicitly in Kolbo siman 128: all the Rishonim wrote that one should lift hands at Mincha on other fasts, and not one of them was omitted for concern of lo pelug; rather certainly lo pelug does not apply here, for the prayers are not interchangeable since they pray close to sunset; and further that the law of intoxication itself is only d'rabbanan. Therefore I say: wherever there is nesiat kapayim every day, they should lift hands without hesitation — also at Mincha on Tisha B'Av, 17 Tammuz, Fast of Gedaliah, 10 Tevet, and Taanit Esther.
