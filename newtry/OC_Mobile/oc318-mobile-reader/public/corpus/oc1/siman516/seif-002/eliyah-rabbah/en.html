Because it lacks grinding, etc. Even though it is possible to make a cooked dish from them, nevertheless the primary [purpose] of grain is for grinding, and that is [true of] the five species (Maggid Mishneh, chapter 5). And this is a proof for what Maharshal wrote in siman 43, that even with barley that is fit for animal food it is forbidden; however, Magen Avraham wrote in his name the reason that it is forbidden to send for the needs of an animal, as he wrote at the end of siman 512, and therefore he permits sending in a place that is one domain for Shabbos; end quote. And I looked in Maharshal himself, and it is not [stated] that [the prohibition is] for this reason. And greater than this, it appears to me that even when one sends for the needs of an animal it is forbidden, since its primary [purpose] is for grinding; and likewise for the needs of a person, such as wheat to make food from it, it is forbidden. However, the Taz wrote plainly that it is permitted when he needs it, or when he needs [it] for an animal. And the language of the Maggid Mishneh there is that we follow the majority, and this requires further study.
