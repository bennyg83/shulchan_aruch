In the Taz, s.k. 1, that Rabbi Shimon of the Mishnah disagrees, etc. There, namely via the public domain: this requires further study how it is permitted to send for the needs of his animal, for it is stated in siman 512 that specifically carrying [within a domain] is permitted, but to take out on its behalf from one domain to another is forbidden.
