(s.k. 1) And even though they appear fit for an animal, etc. — and wheat too appears fit for an animal; nevertheless, since it is not customary to give wheat to an animal, it is forbidden to send it:

(ב) For here in any case wheat is fit to make roasted grains from them, and nevertheless it is forbidden to send since it is not customary to make roasted grains from them, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 517 s.k. 2. But regarding barley — do not say Magen Avraham's words that barley is animal food; his intent is not to challenge Shulchan Aruch's words but the law comes to inform us, and this is what he means "and even though they appear fit for an animal" — meaning barley, Shulchan Aruch also deals with forbidding to send it. As written in siman 512 that we hold like R' Yehuda HaGlili who expounded "for you" and not for dogs, and it is forbidden to do melacha for an animal; that is one reshut for Shabbat where there is no hotza'ah prohibition it is permitted to send — and that is specifically barley.
