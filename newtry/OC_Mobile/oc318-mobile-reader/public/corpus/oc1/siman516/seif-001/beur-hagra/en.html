Seif 1: "not grain." [This follows] the Tanna Kamma. And even per the Yerushalmi, which establishes R' Shimon's view as following R' Akiva, we rule like Rabban Yochanan ben Gamliel. The Rosh wrote, and it appears to me that the Gemara [states] etc. and also etc. See the Yam Shel Shlomo's explanation there. And it appears to me that there is a scribal error in the Rosh there, and the correct reading should be: that the reason of R' Shimon regarding wheat, the Gemara explains, is because it is fit etc. as human food — therefore the Rabbis forbid [it] etc. See there.
