(s.k. 3) One after another. And Yesh Shtar ruled, etc., and his reason he wrote: even though Rambam and Semag wrote three boxes one after another in one row is not precise, and even in a mixture it is forbidden — from the Gemara's stam and it said there is no row less than three boxes, and the wedding guests going one after another — evidently even in a mixture it is forbidden; however regarding three species which is an unresolved question that was not resolved, the poskim ruled leniently since it is a rabbinic matter; but when going in a row one after another it is reasonable to be stringent even with three species — see there:
