They resemble grain that still requires grinding, as will be explained shortly. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1, writes that a distinction can be made. Grinding is a melachah forbidden on Yom Tov for all grain, whereas slaughtering is permitted for other animals. An animal designated for breeding therefore is not considered to require a forbidden act. These are his words. Maamar Mordechai, subsection 2, however, expresses surprise at these authorities. If the animals are designated for breeding, even moving them is forbidden because they are muktzeh, and sending them is certainly forbidden. He writes that perhaps these authorities speak according to the opinion that does not accept muktzeh, as stated in siman 495, se'if 4, in the gloss. See there. Accordingly, for the opinion that accepts muktzeh, the same applies to a goat designated for its milk or a hen designated to produce eggs: one may not send it to another person on Yom Tov, since it is muktzeh, as written above at the beginning of siman 505 and in siman 497, subsection 32.
