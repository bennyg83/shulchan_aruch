And even live — meaning they stand for eating; but if they stand to raise offspring it is forbidden, for they are not fit on Yom Tov and it would be like grain lacking grinding as written shortly below. Olat Shabbat note 1. And Eshel Avraham note 1 wrote one can distinguish that grinding is a melacha forbidden on Yom Tov for all, unlike slaughter permitted through others — it is not machshir melacha forbidden — end quote; however Maamar Mordechai note 2 wondered on the above rabbis that if they stand to raise offspring even to move them is forbidden because of muktzeh, all the more to send them; and he wrote perhaps these rabbis speak to one who does not hold muktzeh (as written siman 495 seif 4 in the gloss) — see there. And if so, for one who holds muktzeh, likewise a goat standing for its milk or a chicken standing to raise eggs — it is forbidden to send to one's fellow on Yom Tov since they are muktzeh as written above siman 505 and siman 497 note 32 — see there:
