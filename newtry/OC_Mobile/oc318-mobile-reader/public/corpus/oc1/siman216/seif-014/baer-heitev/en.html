And some say that one does not recite a blessing. This is because it was not designated for fragrance, but for the mitzvah. I found written in the name of Maharshal that this applies specifically while taking it for the mitzvah, but before and afterward one recites a blessing. Magen Avraham. He wrote likewise in siman 653; see there. See also Taz.
To smell it. If one smells it, Bach writes that he recites a blessing over it. Magen Avraham disagrees and rules that one should not recite a blessing. The same applies to warm bread.
