One who smells the etrog of the mitzvah blesses on it; and some say one does not bless; therefore it is proper not to smell it.Some say that one who smells hot bread should bless she-natan reiach tov ba-pat (Abudraham; Beit Yosef siman 226 in the name of Rav Hai Gaon). And some say) one should not bless on it (Beit Yosef).) Therefore one should not smell it.
