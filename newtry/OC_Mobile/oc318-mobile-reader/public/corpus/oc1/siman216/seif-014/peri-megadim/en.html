Etrog — Taz, unlike Magen Avraham letter 22; and Eshel Avraham letter 22; and study is required in Tur and 218 and 295 will be explained further, and here we cannot extend. What Acharonim wrote, in the name of Acharon on Hoshana Rabbah — after its mitzvah is done it is permitted to smell it for all it is permitted; for Taz it is forbidden even on Shemini Atzeret, as in siman 295 that it is forbidden in eating — removed from it also the quality of smell; and for Magen Avraham, all seven days except at the time of taking it is permitted and one blesses; and it seems this is the third reason — since it stands for the mitzvah it does not stand for smell; and study is required in 295 on this.
