An esrog. See Taz, contrary to Magen Avraham, letter 22. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 22. With Hashem's help, this will be explained further in simanim 653, 658, and 665, and there is no need to elaborate here. Eliyah Rabbah writes there in the name of Orchos Chayim that on Hoshana Rabbah, after its mitzvah has been completed, all agree that one may smell it. I do not understand this. According to Taz, it remains prohibited even on Shemini Atzeres, as stated in siman 665, because once it is forbidden for eating, its fragrance status is also removed. According to Magen Avraham, during all seven days one may smell it and recite a blessing when it is not being held for the mitzvah. It appears to me that this is a third rationale: since it is designated for the mitzvah, it is not designated for smelling. With Hashem's help, this will be explained in siman 665.
