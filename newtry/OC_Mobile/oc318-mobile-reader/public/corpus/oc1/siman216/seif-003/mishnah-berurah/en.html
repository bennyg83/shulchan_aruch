(17) On the rose — which we call rozaish; and we learn from this that even though it is fit for eating through medicine and one blesses on it as explained in siman 204 seif 1, nevertheless we cannot bless on its smell she-natan reiach tov ba-feirot, since its primary essence is not for eating but for smell; rather one blesses borei atzei besamim. And this is regarding roses that grow on trees; but those that grow in the ground one blesses borei isvei besamim [Beit Yosef]; and in Beur HaGra it seems he sides that if it produces from its wood and persists year to year [i.e. the tree does not dry out and end in winter] one blesses borei atzei besamim, and it is possible Beit Yosef also agrees to this, and study is required.

(18) Or od hendi — textual error; it should read od hendi; and its explanation: a fragrant tree coming from the land of India, for od is tree in Arabic and hendi is from the land of India where they grow [Gra]; and see in Shaarei Teshuvah what Machazik Berachah wrote.

(19) Rose water — whether the moisture that emerged from the rose through squeezing, or what emerged through being left to steep and cook in water; and even for one who holds in siman 202 seif 4 that fruit juice is not like the fruit itself — that is only regarding the blessing of eating and not regarding the smell blessing.

(20) Frankincense (levonah) — it is sap emerging from tree roots.

(21) And meitztichi (storax) — it is a type of tree whose sap gives good smell.
