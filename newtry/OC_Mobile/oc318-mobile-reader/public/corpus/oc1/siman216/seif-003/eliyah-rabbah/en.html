(3) [Levush] There is no substance to it, etc. From this I wonder at what Mahari Pinto wrote in the chapter "How does one bless" — that if one smelled and did not bless and passed from the place of the smell, he can say the blessing immediately; and the reason for the matter is that with food, if it was already eaten one does not bless retroactively, but with smell — even though he already smelled — nevertheless the soul receives benefit and should praise God for the smell that passed, until here. And one could say that if so it should require a final blessing; and it is a fortiori — if with eating, for which one blesses a final blessing, one does not bless a first blessing after eating, then with smell, for which one does not bless a final blessing, all the more so one does not bless a first blessing after it; and similarly the Taz at the end of siman 217 implies thus. Also what he interpreted thus — that these are Abaye's words — is puzzling, for Abaye speaks of three "immediately" [tekef] matters for Torah scholars that require blessing, as explained in the Talmud [Berakhot] daf 42. Also what he challenged — why the derasha "What is something from which the soul benefits..." was not said in the words of Mar Zutra, etc. — is puzzling; for we say in the Talmud, and in Ein Yaakov, and in Ba'al HaHalakhot Gedolot, and Rif, and Rosh, and Tur, and all the poskim, and undoubtedly a mistaken version came to him.
