Whether mastic or the like, etc. Ran in chapter Shemoneh Sheratzim explains that mastic is tree resin from which pitch is made.
[Levush] Because of, etc. Beit Yosef cites Rabbenu Yonah: since it is found in Eretz Yisrael and is important, etc. In Baal Halachot Gedolot I found that fragrant oils which are pressed derive their fragrance from the tree itself. Olat Tamid cites Bach that after the fact, if one recited Borei Atzei Besamim, he has fulfilled his obligation. Although Bach mentions dissenting views, his own conclusion and the primary law appear to be that he has fulfilled it. I saw a dispute about this in the Yerushalmi, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin.
