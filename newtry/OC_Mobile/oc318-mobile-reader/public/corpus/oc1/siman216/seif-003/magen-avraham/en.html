On the rose. Which they call rose — even though it is fit for eating through confectionery, nevertheless its main purpose is not for eating but for fragrance; and one may ask: since they are not the main part of the fruit, not to bless on them "Who creates trees of spices," as written siman 204 seif 11; it appears to me this case is different, for it is impossible to bless "Who creates herbs of spices" since it is not a species of herb, therefore one blesses "Who creates trees of spices":

Rose water. Even for those who disagree in siman 202 seif 11 — because fruit juice does not have taste like the fruits themselves, which is not so here, where rose water smells like the rose itself (Bach):

And storax. Pitch that they make from it (Rashi, Bach):

Trees of spices. And likewise anything whose stalk is hard [Shaarei Teshuvah]:
