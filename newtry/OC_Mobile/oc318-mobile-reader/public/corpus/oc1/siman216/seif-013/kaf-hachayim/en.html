(13) On the smell of mint called in Arabic na'ana, and in the language of Mishnah chapter 7 of Sheviit "dandana," and in the first chapter of Uktzin "minta." And in truth on Shabbat 128 the Gaon Rabbi Chaim Ozer wrote Orach Chayim general rules siman 41 that one does not bless on it. And Rav Perach Shoshan general rules siman 13 disputed him and wrote that one blesses on it "Who gives good smell in fruits"; and so wrote Leket Yosher vol. 2 siman 115 — except that he wrote the world is accustomed to bless on it "Who creates herbs of spices" and he did not know why — see there letter 11 in Hagahat Tashbetz. But Shulchan HaLevi siman 44 wrote: "fig, rue, mint, and all that are grass whose smell wafts — one blesses on them 'Who creates herbs of spices,'" end of his words. And Berkei Yosef brought it letter 2; and so is the custom to bless on mint called in Arabic na'ana "Who creates herbs of spices," as Shulchan HaLevi and Leket Yosher wrote.
