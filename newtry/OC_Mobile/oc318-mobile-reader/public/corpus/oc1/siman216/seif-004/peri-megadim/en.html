On walnut — Taz wrote about its smell — that is, for eating one does not bless at all on cloves — Berakhot 46a, and Tosafot here that the clove mentioned here is not of the species cloves; and Eliyah Rabbah osef 9 to bless on cloves borei minei besamim, and I wonder why not bless atzei besamim; and Magen Avraham osef 4 — since clove is fit for eating through mixtures — one blesses good smell in fruits. And for the law it requires further study regarding dry ginger, dry pepper, and clove — whether one should bless borei minei besamim — see siman 297, Taz 5, Yoreh Deah 108:10, and Magen Avraham there. And in Eliyah Rabbah there — pepper has no smell, and if it smells one does not bless on it — and it will be explained there.
