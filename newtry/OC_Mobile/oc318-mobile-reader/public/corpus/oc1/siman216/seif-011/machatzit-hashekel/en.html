(s.k. 11) Shemen zayit. It appears to me specifically shemen zayit, etc. — that Shulchan Aruch deals with not in its name; but shemen zayit from itself returned to have its smell waft — therefore one blesses on it "Who creates trees of spices," for an olive is a tree; but other oils that do not grow on a tree — if their smell wafts from themselves — one does not bless on them "Who creates trees of spices," since it is not a species of tree. But if in its name and through this their smell wafts — this law is explained in the seif afterward.
