Taz, seif katan 4. This refers to cloves. Eliyah Rabbah concludes that over cloves one should recite Borei Minei Besamim. The Gaon Peri Megadim writes in Eshel Avraham, in the introductory letter, section 100: “Regarding orange peel, the blessing for eating is uncertain. The same applies to one who smells it, who should recite Borei Minei Besamim.” End quote. Regarding pepper and ginger, see Eliyah Rabbah below, at the beginning of siman 299.
