Tree species. Even if it is not the main part of the fruit, as seif 3:

Musk. Some say it is blood that collects in an animal's throat and afterward returns and becomes musk; and Razah was concerned to eat it because of a blood concern; and like R' Yehuda it is possible to say it is mere secretion even though initially it was blood — we are not concerned, for we follow the present state; for behold if a forbidden piece fell into honey, even if it dissolved in it — since honey's way is to return what falls into it to honey, we judge it like honey and permit; likewise here, since it left the category of blood we follow the present and permit, even if it imparts taste to improve a dish. And it appears to me that even his proof requires proof (Tur and Rosh chapter 8 of Berakhot); and in my humble opinion requires study — for we say in Bekhorot daf 6 that milk is a novelty, since blood curdled and became milk it should be forbidden; if so we follow the original state; and one could challenge from milk — we hold we do not learn from a novelty; and he answered that musk is mere secretion and not food at all — if so all permit immediately; unlike nursing from a treifah that is permitted to set cheese in its stomach; also honey is good for food; and what R' Yehuda wrote that it is mere secretion — meaning it has no status of blood, and if so the question returns to its place; and regarding a chick hatched from a treifah egg we say: as long as it has not spoiled it does not congeal, and at the time it congeals it is merely dust — which implies that if not for this it would be forbidden, for we follow the original state; and one may ask: likewise before it becomes musk it is dust — who knows this? And regarding honey what can be said — requires study.
