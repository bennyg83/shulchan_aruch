Like musk — it is in a foreign language phizem. And it is written in Tur that it is the sweat of a known animal, and it is a known animal and has a hump on its neck and gathers there at first like a type of blood, and afterward dries and from it musk is made; and Rama was concerned to forbid it because of blood. R' Yitzchak wrote it is [mere] perspiration even though at first it was blood — for we follow the present state; know that honey — if a piece of prohibition fell into it and dissolved in it, since the way of honey is to restore what fell into it, we judge honey by the present state; so too here — even though at first it was blood, we follow the present state. And Rosh wrote — and it appears to me that even his proof requires proof, end of his words; and in my humble opinion it is puzzling, for there is clear proof for permission from what Rosh wrote in the chapter on forbidden fat in the belly of a kosher animal — that Rabbenu Tam wrote: if it is congealed it is like perspiration; if so, here too, since it congealed on the animal it became perspiration — whether inside the animal or on the animal, in both cases it is perspiration.
