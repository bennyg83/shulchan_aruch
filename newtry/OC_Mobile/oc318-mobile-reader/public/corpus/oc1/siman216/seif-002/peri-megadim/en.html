Like musk — Taz, Magen Avraham osef 2, and Pri Chadash in responsa siman 10 brought that dried milk is permitted per Yoreh Deah and the chapter "one may not make things stand" regarding honey — if prohibited matter nullifies prohibited matter — and in Yoreh Deah he lengthened and removed musk for putting in a cooked dish because it is dirt. However, to put it in a cooked dish in more than sixty — even though some say it is made for taste and forbidden only d'rabbanan, and Pri Chadash in Yoreh Deah in the introduction to Hilchot Ta'aruvot wrote that nevertheless this is a doubt of a rabbinic dispute and lekula. One may not nullify a prohibition l'chatchila — a rabbinic prohibition is as Yoreh Deah 98:7 and Pri Chadash there. And further I say — perhaps even per the view that it is made for taste, nevertheless it is not nullified (Pri Chadash Yoreh Deah 98) — nevertheless perforce, if we follow the original state it is nullified, for there it had no taste in sixty and now it changed to a natural property and gives much taste — nevertheless this taste the Torah did not forbid. Granted such a matter is forbidden — for once forbidden in its essence it does not leave the original prohibition; not so with taste as said. And to put l'chatchila one does not nullify a prohibition — it is d'rabbanan — see Magen Avraham 3 from this.
