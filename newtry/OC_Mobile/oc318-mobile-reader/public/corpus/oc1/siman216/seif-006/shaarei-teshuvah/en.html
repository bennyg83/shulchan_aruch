In Mishna Berurah one fulfills — Abudraham; and see in Machatzit HaShekel who wrote he heard from a sage that if before him are trees and grasses of spices — he includes them in one blessing: Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who creates trees and grasses of spices (and not) to bless in detail on each one; and the sage was troubled in his eyes that we do not find to combine except in the blessing resembling three — for Tosafot wrote this is not the mitzvah of bundled praise on the earth Who brings forth bread and grapevine — how can one include here? And again he found in Jerusalem Talmud regarding one who separates teruma and terumat ma'aser and challah — and so Rambam — that one includes all, end of his words. And in Kenesset HaGedolah there wrote one must settle — for there is no proof from there since they are counted as one matter, see there; and see in responsa Beit Efraim part 1 siman 4 at length on the matter of blessing lehanich and on the mitzvah of tefillin, and see there what he wrote on the words of the Jerusalem Talmud and Tosefta and Rabbenu Shimon in responsa siman 484, and see the words of the Talmud in Sukkah daf 46 there that he hinted to them — Magen Avraham siman 25, see there.
