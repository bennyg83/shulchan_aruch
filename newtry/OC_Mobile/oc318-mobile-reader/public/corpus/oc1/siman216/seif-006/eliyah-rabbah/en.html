(6) [Levush] For his eating and for smelling, etc. Olat Tamid wrote that one blesses first on eating and afterward on smell, as Tur wrote at the end of siman 212 — that if the blessing on smell and the blessing on drink are before him, he blesses first on the drink; and the same applies regarding eating, and one blessing does not interrupt its fellow, as we find on Pesach that one blesses on matzah "haMotzi" and on eating matzah — thus since two blessings obligate before eating, one does not interrupt its fellow, until here. In my humble opinion this is not correct — for there the two blessings are obligatory because of eating, but here smell does not depend on eating, and it is possible to bless on smell before eating; also it is similar to siman 165 regarding the blessings of netilat yadayim and "asher yatzar." And it appears one should not say he should bless first on eating and eat — for if so he will immediately smell, and afterward when he blesses on smell he will not transgress "do not delay" [to perform the mitzvah]; also immediately when he takes it in his mouth before eating he smells. Therefore it seems better to bless first on smell and smell, and afterward bless and eat; and it is not comparable to what Tur wrote siman 212 — for there are two matters, one of smell and one of drink — and examine; and see what I wrote at the end of siman 212.
