Oil that has been infused with fragrance, like the anointing oil (shemen hamishchah): If it was made with fragrant woods, one recites “Who creates fragrant trees” (borei atzei besamim). If it was made with fragrant herbs, one recites “Who creates fragrant herbs” (borei isvei besamim). If it was made with both woods and herbs, one recites “Who creates various kinds of fragrances” (borei minei besamim). If it was filtered and the fragrance was extracted from it (so that the spices themselves are no longer present): Some say one should recite “Who creates pleasant oil” (borei shemen arev). Others say no blessing is recited at all, since it is considered a fragrance without a substance (re’ach she’ein lo ‘ikar). And since this is a matter of doubt, it is proper to be cautious and avoid smelling it.
