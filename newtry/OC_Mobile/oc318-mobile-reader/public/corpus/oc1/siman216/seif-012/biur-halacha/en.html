When its smoke rises — know that it is proven from Rashi and Tosafot that even if the spices were completely burned, one must still bless on them, even though they no longer exist at all and we are not particular that some of the spices remain at the time of the blessing, and it is not called a smell that has no primary source — on the contrary, the smell comes from their bodies; and likewise is the language of Rambam, law 2 — see there; and likewise is proven in Or Zaru'a and Semag in the name of R' Yeshaya — see there; and likewise ruled Taz, and I wrote all this to exclude the words of Ra'ah in his laws, who holds that one does not bless unless its primary source still exists — that after its primary source was consumed by fire one does not bless, for it is a smell that has no primary source — and he explains the entire sugya in this manner — see there; and Shitah Mekubetzet followed him, but this is not so according to all the aforementioned poskim:

(ב) Before the smell reaches him — and even though he does not yet hold the finished item in his hand, it is not called "before," for before is before — and likewise above in siman 167 seif 3 regarding bread: there what is missing is taking it in his hand and eating, which is not so here, where nothing is missing except that he smell, and it is properly called "before" their performance:
