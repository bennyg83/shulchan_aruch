And the word grasses etc. — I the poor one did not see in Job, and in my concordances at the root grass and at the root mountain they did not bring this verse in Job; but in Proverbs 27:28 "grasses of the mountains" I found a version — see with chirik and shin with shva na moving and a weak bet; and accordingly isvei besamim too — the bet is weak of grasses and of besamim; but the great grammarian R' Shlomo Laniado, may his memory be for a blessing, in Shaarei Tefillah siman 314 — isvei besamim the shin with segol like min chalav chalavi — see there; and now there is no commoner like me to decide, and study is required.
