And if. See Taz and the preceding letter. Regarding the word isvei, “herbs,” I, in my limited knowledge, did not find the cited verse in Iyov. Nor did I find it in the concordance under either the root for “herb” or the root for “mountain.” In Mishlei 27:25, however, I found the reading “the herbs of the mountains,” vocalized with a chirik, a shin with a mobile sheva and dagesh, and a soft beis. According to this, in the phrase isvei besamim, both the beis of isvei and the beis of besamim are soft. The great grammarian Rabbi Shlomo Zalman Hanau, of blessed memory, however, writes in Shaarei Tefillah, siman 314, that in isvei besamim the shin has a segol, like the form chalvei from chalav; see there. At present, one as unqualified as I cannot decide the matter. With Hashem's help, it will be explained further. One may perhaps distinguish between one construct form and another. This requires study.
