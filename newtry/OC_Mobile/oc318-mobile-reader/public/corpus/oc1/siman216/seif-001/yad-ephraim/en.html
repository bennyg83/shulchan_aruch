In Magen Avraham s.k. 4 — as written siman 202 — and it appears his intent is to what Magen Avraham wrote there s.k. 17 regarding the shell of mace — that the inside of the fruit too is not good without repair and mixture of sugar (and it appears Delmens says thus), see there; and from this it appears one should bless on the shell of mace or on a lemon when one takes it to smell, or to eat and smell — one gives good smell in fruits; and I did not see people practice thus, and I saw in Sefer Bnei Chiya who wrote it is reasonable to bless on half a fruit and not on the peel of the fruit; and what he wrote in the name of Mar his uncle — see in Mishna Berurah that his view is to bless borei minei besamim on this, as they conclude there on seeds to bless shehakol, see there; and in truth there is no proof from blessing of enjoyment to here — for there the matter depends on whether they planted for this purpose, not so here where we do not care about this; however Mishna Berurah there is concerned for the view of those who hold seeds are not fruit, and the same for peels — and see above siman 202 regarding shell of mace preserved in honey, see there.
