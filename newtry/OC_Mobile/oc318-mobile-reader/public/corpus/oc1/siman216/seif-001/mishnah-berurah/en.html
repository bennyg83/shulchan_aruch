(1) It is forbidden to enjoy [a pleasant smell], etc. — and just as it is forbidden to enjoy eating and drinking until one blesses, so they anchored it in the Gemara from the verse that is written: "Let every soul praise Yah" — which implies that the soul too should praise for its enjoyment; and what is something that is enjoyment of the soul alone? You would say — this is smell.
(2) Until he blesses — the blessing explained below in seif 2.
(3) Before he smells — for we require [the blessing] to precede the act, as with eating and other mitzvot. The Acharonim wrote that if one forgot and smelled without a blessing and remembered after he finished smelling, he lost his blessing — as with eating, after one finished eating he lost the first blessing — as explained in siman 167 seif 8.
(4) But after him, etc. — for they did not enact a final blessing except for eating and drinking, and not for smell since its pleasure is slight. The poskim wrote: if before him were two blessings — one of taste and one of smell — he takes first the category of taste in his right hand and blesses on it, because it is more important since it enters the body; and afterward he takes the smell and blesses on it. It was further explained in the poskim that specifically for smell they enacted a blessing, but not for sound [i.e., pleasant hearing] which has no substance; and likewise they did not enact a blessing for bathing and the like, because they do not enter the body.
