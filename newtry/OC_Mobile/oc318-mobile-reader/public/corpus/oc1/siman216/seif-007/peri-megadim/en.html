And if. See Taz. The Shulchan Aruch concludes that it is proper to avoid smelling it. Levush writes that one should smell another fragrant substance and intend thereby to discharge this one. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 13, who writes that one should take a fragrant wood, and this will also cover it, because regarding balsam too, after the fact, one fulfills the obligation with Borei Atzei Besamim. I do not understand this. Balsam grows on a tree, unlike filtered perfumed oil, over which one recites Borei Shemen Areiv. How would Borei Atzei Besamim discharge it? One would necessarily need actual balsam, which is uncommon. The same issue applies if herbs were placed in the oil. Nevertheless, one may say that he should recite Borei Minei Besamim, meaning that he should take some type of fragrant substance, recite that blessing, and thereby discharge this as well. So it appears to me.
