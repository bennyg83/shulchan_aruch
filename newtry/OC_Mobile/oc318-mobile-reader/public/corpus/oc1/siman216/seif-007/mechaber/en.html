Over simlak and ḥilfei deimā, one recites the blessing: “Who creates fragrant trees” (borei atzei besamim). Regarding simlak: Some explain that it is rosemary (rosmarin), Others say it is jasmine (yasmin), And some interpret it as a herb with three rows of leaves, one above the other, and three leaves on each row. Ḥilfei deimā is identified as spikenard (shibolet nerd), which is called ishpek.
