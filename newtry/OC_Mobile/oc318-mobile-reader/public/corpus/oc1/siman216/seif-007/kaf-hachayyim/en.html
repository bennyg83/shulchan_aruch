7) A rose or myrtle detached on Yom Tov may be smelled without moving it. Responsa Zera Yaakov, siman 8; Machazik Berachah, paragraph 9. The Shulchan Aruch below, siman 336, seif 10, likewise rules that even an attached myrtle may be smelled; see there. Erekh HaShulchan, paragraph 8, writes the same.
