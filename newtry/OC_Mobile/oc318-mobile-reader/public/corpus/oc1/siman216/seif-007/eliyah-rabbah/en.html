(7) [Levush] And it exempts the secondary, etc. I searched through all the early and later poskim and did not find this reason anywhere; and I am further puzzled — if so, regarding incense finished [mugmar] in siman 217, and spices of the dead, and a bathroom, and oil made to remove foul odor — what would one say about it? Behold the primary matter ceases and one smells against his will. Rather certainly anything that is not made for smell — even if it smells — one need not bless at all, since his intent is not to smell, and this is clear; and if so we do not need what he divides between this and siman 212 regarding "it exempts the secondary," for it is very strained and not to be extended; and so it is proven explicitly in Avudraham and Tashbetz daf 29.
