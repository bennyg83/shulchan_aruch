5) It is permitted to smell the blossoms of orlah trees even though they contain the beginning of the fruit. It is forbidden, however, to smell the fruit once it has grown somewhat, since it is orlah. Radbaz, new responsa, siman 44. Responsa Zera Yaakov, siman 7, however, concludes that it is permitted to smell consecrated fruit or fruit forbidden by a vow, but one should not recite a blessing over it; see there at length. Machazik Berachah, paragraph 8, cites this. It appears that since the matter is disputed and it is forbidden to derive enjoyment from the world without a blessing, refraining from action is preferable. One should not smell something from which enjoyment is forbidden, even without a blessing. See Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah, siman 108, seif 7.
