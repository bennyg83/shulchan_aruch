(24) That they ground it, etc. until it returned — meaning they did not put any besamim in it, but through grinding or crushing the olive the oil began to have its smell waft.
(25) Borei atzei besamim — that the olive grows on the tree; and the same applies to other fruits that grow on the tree if they did similarly; and if this was from fruits that do not grow on the tree, that they ground and crushed them and their oil smells — one does not bless on them except borei minei besamim [Pri Megadim]. And that one does not bless on all these that give good smell in fruits like etrog and the like — there it is different, for the smell grows with it; but here the smell does not grow with them but comes through grinding and crushing done by human hands — it is not appropriate to bless she-natan etc., since smell was not given in it from the outset; rather it is more appropriate to bless borei atzei besamim, since the power of besamim was implanted in its interior.
