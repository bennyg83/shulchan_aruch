(5) [Levush] "Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who gives..." etc. Aruchat Chayim wrote, and not like those who say "Who gave" or "gave," because in His goodness He renews daily always, until here — and his words are puzzling. In Shulchan Aruch he wrote sometimes "gave" and sometimes "Who gives," which implies there is no stringency; and so I found in Piskei Rekanati siman 78 seif 79; and further that Rama at the end of this siman wrote "gave," and so wrote Avudraham and Shibolei HaLeket and the baraita. And in Lechem Chamudot daf 61 he wrote to say "Who gave" in past tense; and on daf 54 it appears from his words that the wording "gave" and the wording "Who gave" are one; and it is more puzzling that I saw in the Talmud, and Ba'al HaHalakhot, and Rif, and Rambam, and Rosh, and Tur, and Ravan, and Semag, and Semak, and Rokeach, and Tashbetz, and Rabbenu Yerucham, and Mordekhai, and Agudah, and Kolbo — that they wrote "Who gave" — and who can be light-headed against them? Also what Aruchat Chayim wrote — that Levush was precise to write everywhere "Who gives," and so wrote Lechem Chamudot — is not so, for below siman 653 he wrote "Who gave."
