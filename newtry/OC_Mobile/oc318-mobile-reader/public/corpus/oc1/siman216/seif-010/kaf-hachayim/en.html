(10) There — but afterward one need not bless. And the reason Rashi wrote in Niddah at the beginning of daf 52 — because the benefit is slight — and Bach brought it. And he wrote that Rashi means immediately after the smell ceased the benefit ceased, and it is comparable to one who ate and the food was digested in his intestines — that he does not bless afterward, end of his words. And so too the Taz s.k. 1, and so Machatzit HaShekel s.k. 1 in the name of Kolbo; and Olat Tamid letter 1 wrote another reason — because the fragrant matter does not cease since one smells in it — see there. And it appears to me to give another reason — that one does not bless a final blessing except on something in which satiation applies; not so with smell — for even if one smells day and night he will not be sated, and therefore one does not bless a final blessing on it; and from all these reasons it implies that if he did not bless before he smelled, or at the time he smells, he cannot bless afterward; and so wrote Eliyah Rabbah letter 3 and Eshel Avraham letter 1.
