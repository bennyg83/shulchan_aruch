(38) Were before him — meaning he wishes to smell them. "And if he blessed, etc." — as explained above in seif 2; except there it deals with when he held atzei besamim or isvei besamim in his hand and blessed borei minei besamim, whereas here it deals with when he held only minei besamim and in his blessing intended to discharge also atzei besamim and isvei besamim — nevertheless he has fulfilled, for this blessing applies b'dieved to them as well.

(39) One blesses on each, etc. — meaning he cannot exempt himself with one comprehensive blessing, namely borei minei besamim; rather l'chatchila he must bless on each separately. And he should bless first on atzei besamim and isvei besamim, and afterward bless on the rest of the species borei minei besamim — as we conclude above siman 211 seif 3 regarding the blessing on fruits. If several species were mixed together, or they are in one bundle and one cannot smell each species separately, he blesses on them borei minei besamim [Eshel Avraham and Beit Chadash]. However it implies in Eshel Avraham that it is better to detach from the bundle some species so he can bless on it its appropriate blessing l'chatchila. "He has fulfilled" — meaning when he explicitly intended to discharge all the species; if not, no — as with the blessing on fruits when before him is something whose blessing is haEtz and haAdamah and before him is something whose blessing is shehakol — one does not fulfill with shehakol stated generally on everything, as above siman 206. The Acharonim wrote: just as the blessing of atzei besamim does not exempt the blessing of isvei besamim, so the blessing of isvei besamim does not exempt the blessing of atzei besamim — for herbs are not included in "tree."

(40) See above siman 211 — meaning there is a dispute there similar in seif 3 regarding haEtz and haAdamah whether one must prioritize haEtz; and the same applies in our matter. Know that there the Acharonim agreed that what is always more beloved to him one must prioritize; but if both are equally beloved to him, it is more proper to prioritize haAdamah — and the same applies here: if both are equally beloved to him, it is more proper to prioritize atzei besamim which is the most distinct and specific blessing, and afterward bless isvei besamim.
