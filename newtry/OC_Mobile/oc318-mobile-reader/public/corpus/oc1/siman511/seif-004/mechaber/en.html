One may not make fumigation — meaning scattering spices over coals, whether to smell them or to fumigate a house or vessels. But if one does so in order to give a pleasant scent to fruits to improve them for eating, it is permitted even if one scatters them over a wood coal.Rema: It is forbidden to invert a perfumed cup over garments, since this creates a scent in them. (HaMagid ch. 1 and Hagahot Asheiri, end of ch. 2 of Beitzah)):
