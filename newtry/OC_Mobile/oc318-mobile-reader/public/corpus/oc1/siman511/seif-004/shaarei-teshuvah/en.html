To increase — see in responsa Noda B'Yehuda Even HaEzer siman 24; and so in one who ruled permission to heat the cauldron in the bathhouse through a non-Jew on Yom Tov that falls on erev Shabbat, so that the bathhouse owner should cook from it those hot waters in the cauldron — stew and he should drink from it on Yom Tov, and the rest of the hot water in the cauldron should flow to the mikveh to remove their coldness, and arousers came against him; and he z"l wrote that they do not embarrass him in a place where there is prevention of immersion and nullification of positive commandments; and also there is a side for permission because she needs hot water for shampooing, for which there is permission even on Yom Tov as Shach wrote Yoreh De'ah siman 199; and if so, on Yom Tov for Shabbat need there is also permission through eruv tavshilin — see there; and see in Machatzit HaShekel in the name of Zera Emet that some are accustomed permission in such a case to permit on Yom Tov that falls on erev Shabbat by cooking in the cauldron two or three eggs — one should leave them in their custom; nevertheless bathing her whole body in hot water is forbidden, only shampoo and wash well on erev Yom Tov her whole body in hot water, and on Yom Tov rinse below in hot water and examine well her whole body before immersion as written Yoreh De'ah siman 199 — see there.
