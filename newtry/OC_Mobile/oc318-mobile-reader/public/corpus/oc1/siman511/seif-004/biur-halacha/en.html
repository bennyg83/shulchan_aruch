One may not make mugar etc. — and regarding drinking tobacco on Yom Tov there are views among poskim brought in later authorities; some forbid [Knesset HaGedolah and Magen Avraham in siman 514 and Korban Netanel in chapter 2 of Beitza and Chayei Adam] because this kindling is not for all souls like mugar, and also extinguishing is possible when lighting through paper or ember and throwing as usual on a weekday; and many are lenient [Darkhei Noam brought in Be'er Heitev and Shulchan Atzei Shetim and Birkei Yosef; and see Shaarei Teshuvah who brought more, and the main reason of all is that now that many are accustomed to this it becomes for all souls]. Only one must beware of extinguishing, as written in Shaarei Teshuvah and Peri Megadim — that is, not to kindle from paper but only from a flame; and from an ember it is permitted — that is, to take a burning ember and immediately when the tobacco is lit throw it back on the fire in a manner that there is no extinguishing at all; and also not to cover with a cover (dekel) that is not perforated, and not to add more tobacco while the lower part is burning; and also with cigarettes, on a weekday they are accustomed to remove the burned part with a finger or push it against a wall — one must beware of this on Yom Tov because there is concern of extinguishing. Further Shaarei Teshuvah wrote that some are stringent on the first Yom Tov and the two days of Rosh Hashana Yom Tov and lenient on the second Yom Tov, and so is the practice; nevertheless those who are lenient even on the first Yom Tov one should not protest them, only they should beware regarding extinguishing as above; and all this is specifically in those places where the world is accustomed to this and it is a matter for all souls, for otherwise it is forbidden by law. And even in places where all are accustomed and then there is no prohibition from kindling, nevertheless one should beware and warn the people to be careful of concern of extinguishing as above.
