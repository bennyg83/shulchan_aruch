To sweeten them for eating. For this is ochel nefesh even for the poor, except that it is not commonly available to them; and it resembles one for whom a deer presented itself on Yom Tov, as is stated in chapter 1 of Kesuvos.
Even if one scatters them etc. For there is no concern of extinguishing here, for regarding ochel nefesh it is permitted since it is impossible [to do so] from erev Yom Tov; which is not so with mustard in siman 510, where it is possible from erev Yom Tov.
A scented cup upon the garments. In the Gemara: pressing a cup upon silk is forbidden because one generates fragrance. Rashi explained: to invert a scented cup upon the silk of a garment to put a pleasant smell into them is forbidden, for he generates fragrance in the silk; it implies that he intends that the smell enter the garment. And in the Maharil it is written that it is forbidden to place an esrog on a cloak on the first day of Yom Tov because it generates fragrance, similar to a cup upon silk in chapter 2 of Beitza; and Maharshal wrote on this: and I wonder, for he forbade placing the cup only in order to perfume, as Or Zarua wrote, and this is proof that he mentioned silk and not a plain garment, only because that is how they perfume them; but where one does not intend to perfume, it appears permitted, for it is not relevant here to say psik reisha except with an actual melacha, but the perfuming itself is not a melacha, only extinguishing and kindling are; end quote. And his words are correct, and it is not comparable to what is written at the end of siman 320, that one whose hands are stained from fruit water should be careful not to touch a cloth because of dyeing, and even though he does not intend to dye the cloth, dyeing is an av melacha and therefore it is psik reisha. And I saw in some communities that they pour fragrant oil called spikenard into the water on Yom Tov for washing the hands of the kohanim for the duchan, and they are doing a prohibition, for they generate fragrance in the water; and even if they pour on erev Yom Tov, nevertheless they generate fragrance on Yom Tov through the kohanim, and this is pleasing to them and they intend it; therefore one should refrain from doing so.
