Mugmar. And it is permitted to bring a vessel full of holes and cover it from erev Yom Tov and plug its holes, and the next day they open the holes and it turns out that the house is being perfumed; and even on Shabbos it is permitted. Maharshal.
To sweeten them. Even if they are already good to eat without this, but he wants to sweeten them further, it is likewise permitted. Magen Avraham.
Fragrance. See siman 658, se'if 2; and for this reason the Taz wrote in this siman, and the Magen Avraham in this siman and in siman 128, that it is forbidden to put rose water on Shabbos and Yom Tov into the water for washing the hands of the kohanim; and even if they pour from erev Yom Tov, nevertheless they generate fragrance on Yom Tov through the kohanim; and so wrote Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 19. But in responsa Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, klal 3, siman 9, he wrote that on the hands there is no prohibition at all, only in the water, for it certainly generates fragrance, which is forbidden. And likewise he ruled in responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 92, that on the hands there is no prohibition at all; see there. (But in the Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote that since the oil itself is mixed in the water, it is not generating fragrance; see there.)
