(ג) Odor. Case 558 seif 2, and therefore Taz wrote in this siman and Magen Avraham in this siman and in siman 128 that it is forbidden to put rose water on Shabbat and Yom Tov into the water for the need of priestly hand washing, and even if they pour from erev Yom Tov nevertheless they create odor on Yom Tov through the priests; and so too Leket Yosher part 1 siman 19. But in responsa Ginat Veradim Orach Chayim general 3 siman 9 he wrote that on the hands there is no prohibition at all, only in water it certainly creates odor which is forbidden.
