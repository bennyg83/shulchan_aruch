(כ) A person is permitted to immerse etc. — meaning although for a vessel it is forbidden to immerse it and purify it from its impurity on Yom Tov because it appears like repairing the vessel through this — a person is different because he appears like cooling himself in water [Gemara]; and see above siman 326 seif 8 in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halacha where we explained all details of this law.
