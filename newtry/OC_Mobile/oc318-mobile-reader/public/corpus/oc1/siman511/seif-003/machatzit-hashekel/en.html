(s.k. 3) For sometimes etc. That is to say etc.: for at first glance, from the language of the Rama it is implied that since sometimes the cold is not great, therefore one should forbid even when the cold is great, because of lo plug. Therefore the Magen Avraham wrote that this is not the intent of the Rama, and he came to forbid only when the cold is not great.
And it appears to me that if the cold etc. See siman 639, se'if 5; so it should read. And see there in Magen Avraham s.k. 15, that in such a case one is called a person in distress and is exempt from the sukkah.
