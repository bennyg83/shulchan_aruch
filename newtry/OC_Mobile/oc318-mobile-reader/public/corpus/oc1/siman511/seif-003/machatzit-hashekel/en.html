(א) (s.k. 3) For sometimes, etc. — meaning, etc.; for apparently from Rama's wording it implies since sometimes the cold is not great therefore one should forbid even great cold due to 'do not distinguish'; therefore Magen Avraham wrote this is not Rama's intent and he did not come to forbid except when the cold is not great.

(ב) And it appears to me that if the cold etc. — case 539 seif 5 as it should read. And see there Magen Avraham seif katan 15 that in such a case he is called one who suffers and is exempt from the sukkah.
