To make a bonfire. For since kindling was permitted for the need of ochel nefesh, it was likewise permitted when not for that need.
Some say it is forbidden. It is implied from the language of the Maharil that specifically for Jews it is forbidden to heat it, but through a non-Jew it is permitted.
For sometimes there is no cold. That is to say, when the cold is not great there is a prohibition in the matter; and it appears to me that if the cold is so great that fatty foods congeal, it is permitted to kindle, for it is a need of ochel nefesh; see siman 640, se'if 4.
