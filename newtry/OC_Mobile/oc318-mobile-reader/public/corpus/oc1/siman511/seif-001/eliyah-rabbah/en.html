(א) [Levush] And even on erev Yom Tov, etc. He was precise to write 'they are accustomed,' but for practical ruling it appears to me the main view follows Beit Yosef's ruling that even the whole body at once is permitted, for those who permit are the Geonim, Rif, Rambam, Semag, Semak, Maggid, Ran, and more; I also saw Rabbeinu Yerucham, disciple of Ra'ash, inclines to permit against his teacher; and in Shibbolei HaLeket daf 34 he brought all views and agreed to permit; and so Riaz in Shiltei Giborim. I also wonder at what Beit Yosef wrote that Agur wrote in the name of Rashba's responsum to forbid — but I saw in Avodat HaKodesh to Rashba daf 43 that he permits explicitly; and I examined Agur itself siman 480: Rashba wrote they practiced to forbid and he did not want to permit, end of his words; and it appears his view is to permit, but the question was in a place where they practiced to forbid — he did not want to permit changing their custom; nevertheless Rashba permits is clear; and in Agur itself he brought in conclusion Shibbolei HaLeket mentioned above to permit. I also say it appears from Ran chapter Kirah s.v. it was stated and s.v. and to examine, etc., that to wash limb by limb all permit in water heated erev Yom Tov; with difficulty one could explain his words on another matter, requires further study; and in Kol Bo daf 60: Shibbolei HaLeket in Yerushalmi chapter 2 of Beitzah permits the whole body limb by limb, end of quote; and I also saw in Yerushalmi itself there and in chapter Kirah to permit. Now I will explain the view of those who permit: specifically outside a bathhouse, but in a bathhouse [not]; and even outside a bathhouse Ran wrote specifically to rinse off but not to bathe; however if it was heated, etc., it implies even those who forbid above permit for a child; and so in Agudah; and so Mordechai implies, not like Magen Avraham; and all the more so permitted in water heated erev Yom Tov; and so Beit Yosef establishes it in the name of Mordechai. In Olas Shabbat he wrote Beit Yosef did not understand Mordechai's words therefore he challenged — end of quote; and he erred, for what Beit Yosef wrote 'requires further study' etc. is Mordechai's own language and Beit Yosef challenged nothing; and what Mordechai wrote in the name of Yerushalmi appears to me it refers to an adult not a child; and even so Mordechai's words require further study.
