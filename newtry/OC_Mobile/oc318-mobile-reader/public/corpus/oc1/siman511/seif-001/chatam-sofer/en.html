In the Mechaber seif 2 it is permitted to heat on Yom Tov. Gloss: And to heat a mikveh on Yom Tov — see responsa Chasam Sofer Orach Chayim siman 146 and siman 148; and in siman 146 he wrote regarding the teacher who issued a temporary ruling to heat the mikveh on the second day of Yom Tov for the need of immersion of a sick woman who could not immerse in cold water, and he commanded to cook by the fire and also to take water from the cauldron for eating; and he wrote that since on the second day of Yom Tov the matter came before the teacher, it was in his law well to rule for temporary need; however I did not want that a leniency should come from my mouth to introduce a new leniency that our fathers did not consider and they made a prohibition in it. Therefore from here onward I do not say in this matter neither prohibition nor permission; and after he finished his responsum in which he elaborated to clarify this law as forbidden, he wrote afterward and now the book Noda B'Yehuda second edition was printed — see there Orach Chayim siman 25 — and the elder already ruled. And in siman 148 he returned to speak on this matter and wrote that this question was already asked before him, and now that Noda B'Yehuda already permitted on Yom Tov from erev Yom Tov through a non-Jew; and see also in Peri Megadim siman 511, and it is fit to rely on them, and all the more so in a place of common illness; and although I do not permit, for from fear of ruling I am not, and I hate innovations, but in places where they are accustomed to permit per the ruling of the Geonim — do not embarrass them.}
