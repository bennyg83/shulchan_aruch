(1) [Seif 1] It is permitted to make a bonfire etc. Although it is written in the Torah "only that which is eaten by all souls" etc. — this that they permitted a bonfire is because from when kindling was permitted for the need of food for souls it was also permitted not for that need. Beit Yosef in the name of Ran. Taz seif katan 1. Magen Avraham seif katan 1. However Bach wrote that a bonfire for pleasure of the whole body is similar to food for souls and we permit it without "from this" — see there. And so Or Sameach section 1, and so Rambam in explanation of the Mishnah that the reason for permitting a bonfire for a sick person is because "only that which may be eaten by all souls" — all bodily pleasures are included — see there. And so in Ahavat Chesed section 3 section 5 that the reason is because every matter needed for all souls is included in "that which may be eaten by all souls shall you do for you" — see there. And see below section 10.
