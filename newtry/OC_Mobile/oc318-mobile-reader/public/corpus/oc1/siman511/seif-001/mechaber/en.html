Kindling a fire and heating water is permitted on Yom Tov. 4 sections: It
is permitted to make a bonfire to warm oneself by it.Rema: Some say it is forbidden to heat a winter room on Yom Tov, since sometimes the cold is not great and it is only for the pampered, making it like a bathhouse or fumigation which are forbidden. (Beit Yosef in the name of a German responsum, and the custom is to be lenient)):
