To wash. See the Taz's general rule: whether [the allowance is because it is] ochel nefesh or [by the principle of] mitoch, we specifically require that it be equal for every person; and see siman 518: ostensibly mitoch applies specifically to carrying and kindling, but not to other melachos; and heating for his hands is actual ochel nefesh according to the Ran in siman 501, letter 2; and with Hashem's help, in Magen Avraham here, letter 1, it will be clarified. Now, tobacco on Yom Tov: see Magen Avraham, siman 514, letter 4, to forbid, for it is not equal for every person. And the learned Moreinu HaRav R' Shaul, son of the Rav the Gaon Moreinu HaRav R' Eliezer, grandson of the Gaon Moreinu HaRav R' Hilman, of blessed memory, who was Av Beis Din in the holy community of Metz, showed me a novel point in Penei Yehoshua, Shabbos 39b, Tosafos s.v. And Beis Hillel permit: something that is for healing, such as smoking tobacco, even though it is also for pleasure, when equal for every person is called for healing; see there. Also there he brought whether something that is not equal for every person [is forbidden] according to the Torah or rabbinically; see there. Now I am accustomed to take tobacco on Yom Tov according to the universal custom, for healing; and especially nowadays it is truly equal for every person; and to tear the paper one must [prepare it] from erev Yom Tov; also paper that has letters written on it one must forbid, for it is erasing, at least rabbinically; and tearing paper on Yom Tov, one can say it is an av melacha so long as one is not [merely] destroying; see responsa of the Ralbach, [regarding] one who tore a siddur in his anger; and see the laws of Shabbos, I brought them in the name of the Rambam, of blessed memory; and with Hashem's help in siman 514 more will be clarified.
