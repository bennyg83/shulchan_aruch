To bathe — Taz: the rule is whether from a non-Jew or from this we require for all souls specifically; and case 518 — it appears from this specifically carrying out and kindling, but other melachot not; and heating the hands whether from a non-Jew is literally Ran in 501 letter 2, and it will be explained in Magen Avraham here 1. And behold tobacco on Yom Tov — Magen Avraham 514 letter 4 — to forbid, for it is not for all souls. And the Torah scholar R' Shaul son of R' the Gaon R' Eliezer grandson of the Gaon R' Hilman z"l who was av beit din in community Metz showed me a novella in Penei Yehoshua Shabbos 39b Tosafos s.v. uBeit Ha — they permit a matter that is for healing like smoking tobacco, although also for pleasure it is called for all souls healing — see there. Also there he brought whether a matter that is not for all souls — Torah-level or rabbinic — see there. And behold I am accustomed to drink tobacco on Yom Tov as world custom for healing, and especially as today it is truly for all souls; and to tear paper requires erev Yom Tov; also paper on which letters are written — there is room to forbid, for it is erasing rabbinically at least; and tearing paper on Yom Tov — one can say it is a primary melacha: all that does not spoil — responsa Ralbach: one tore a siddur in his anger; and see Laws of Shabbat that I brought in the name of R' Meir z"l; and it will be explained in 514 further.
