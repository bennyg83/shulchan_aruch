(There) Se'if 3 A person is permitted etc. See in Chut HaShani, section 70: a woman who immersed on the night of Yom Tov after giving birth, inadvertently, is permitted to her husband, since she was inadvertent; and even if she had been intentional, there would be no place to forbid as a matter of law after the fact, only because of migdar milsa; but if inadvertent, she is certainly permitted immediately, even though ideally one should not do so; and if intentional, one should be stringent to separate her for that night, but afterward it is obvious that she is permitted without another immersion; see there; and see in Yoreh De'ah, siman 197.
