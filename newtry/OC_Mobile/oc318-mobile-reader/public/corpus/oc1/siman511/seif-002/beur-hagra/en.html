Se'if 2: It is permitted etc. There, for his feet, and the same applies to his hands; and as is written in Shabbos there: but his face, his hands, and his feet: say [rather] the latter clause etc.; and there, 40a: a beraisa supports him etc.; and that is what [is meant by] but etc. And even etc.
But it is permitted etc. As is written in the last chapter of Yoma, that washing is permitted on Yom Kippur because that is its normal rearing, and the same applies here.
But it is forbidden. As Tosafos wrote s.v. the aforementioned in Shabbos there, that according to the Torah it is forbidden to kindle on Yom Tov for the sake of washing, as on Shabbos; and they said in Eruvin 68a that even through a non-Jew it is forbidden to heat for the need of a minor, even for milah; and even regarding Chol HaMoed they said in chapter 2 of Moed Katan: whoever does not do etc.
But when he needs etc. As is written in Eruvin there: they set it for him incidentally with his mother; and although for an adult it is forbidden, as is written at the end of chapter 16 of Shabbos, and all the more so when a Jew does it, as is written in the first chapter of Chullin; and see at the end of siman 503, that on Yom Tov even for an adult it is permitted.
But with water etc. As is written in Shabbos 40a: Our Rabbis taught: a bathhouse etc. from erev Yom Tov etc. From the fact that regarding Shabbos it said for the next day he bathes, and regarding Yom Tov it said tomorrow, it follows that after they forbade bathing in hot water heated from erev Shabbos, nevertheless on Yom Tov it is permitted; and see Tosafos there s.v. ela etc., explained in the Kuntres etc., and perhaps he would distinguish etc. And in the Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Shabbos and chapter 2 of Yom Tov: hot water heated on Yom Tov, and likewise hot water heated from erev Shabbos for Shabbos: Rav and Shmuel, one said one may wash with them his face, hands, and feet, and the other said one may wash with them his whole body, limb by limb; and we do not know who said this and who said that. From what Shmuel said, one washes with them his face, hands, and feet, it was Rav who said etc. And it states regarding Yom Tov and regarding erev Shabbos [at] 40, that on erev Yom Tov in every respect it is permitted; and likewise in the beraisa supporting Shmuel it did not mention erev Yom Tov.
However, specifically etc. Shabbos there: and he goes out and rinses himself etc., specifically; and it deals with water heated from erev Yom Tov, as is written: they plugged its openings from erev Yom Tov etc.
And some forbid etc. For it is apparent there that all those decrees applied even on Yom Tov, as is written there: and when they proliferated etc.; and it refers to Yom Tov, for on Shabbos sweating was not mentioned there; and in chapter HaMeivi they said regarding sweating, and before the decree, and sweating they forbade only because of hot water heated from before the day, as Rashi wrote there s.v. and still they were etc. And what is written, and he goes out and rinses, they explain as with cold water, as Tosafos wrote there s.v. ela. However, in the Tosefta the reading is etc.; see there; and there is a scribal error there in Tosafos and Rashi, and one must read from erev Yom Tov; and as to why they divided there: they plugged its openings from erev Shabbos etc., they plugged etc., see there in Tosafos s.v. for the next day etc.; and so it appears in the Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Shabbos; and see the Mordechai, chapter 2 of Yom Tov; and the first view holds that the decree of sweating was because they were bathing in hot water heated on Yom Tov.
