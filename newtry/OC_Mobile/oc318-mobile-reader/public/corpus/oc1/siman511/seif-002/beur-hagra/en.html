(ב) But permitted etc. As written in the last chapter of Yoma that bathing in the Temple is permitted because of cooling, and likewise here.

(ג) But forbidden. As Tosafos s.v. above in Shabbos there, that it is forbidden to kindle from the Torah on Yom Tov because of bathing as on Shabbat; and they said in Eruvin 68a that even through a non-Jew it is forbidden to heat for a minor, even for circumcision, and even during twilight they said in chapter 2 of Moed Katan: all who does not etc.

(ד) But when needed etc. As in Eruvin there — one comforts him through his mother; and even though for an adult it is forbidden as written in Shabbat, and all the more so an Israelite who acts as written in the first chapter of Chullin; and case 593 that on Yom Tov even for an adult it is permitted.

(ה) But in water, etc. As written in Shabbat 40a: a braita teaches regarding a bathhouse attendant on Yom Tov need, etc.; since on Shabbat it says 'he bathes' and on Yom Tov it says 'tomorrow' — therefore after they forbade hot water heated from erev Shabbat, nevertheless on Yom Tov it is permitted. See Tosafot there s.v. rather, explanation in Kuntres, etc.; and perhaps one could distinguish, etc.; and in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Shabbat and chapter 2 of Yom Tov: hot water heated on Yom Tov, and likewise hot water heated from erev Shabbat for Shabbat — Rav and Shmuel: one said he may wash with it his face, hands, and feet; and Charna said he may wash with it his entire body limb by limb; and they did not know who said which; from that which Shmuel said he washes face, hands, and feet — Rav said the other, etc.; and it says on Yom Tov and erev Shabbat 40 that on Yom Tov in every case it is permitted; and likewise in a braita like Shmuel — it did not mention Yom Tov need.
