His hands. See responsa in Yoreh De'ah, siman 250; and it is obvious that a woman is permitted to wash herself in her weekday manner in order to put on white [clothes], in cold water; and in hot water heated from erev Yom Tov she is likewise permitted for some limbs; and possibly even in water heated on Yom Tov; but she should be careful not to come to wringing. And see Taz, siman 381, s.k. 2; and responsa [Samech-Ches], siman 46; and responsa Mas'as Binyamin.
