To wash a baby. Since that is its normal rearing, they did not decree (Mordechai). And it appears to me that nowadays, when even on a weekday they sometimes do not wash him for two or three days, even on Yom Tov it is forbidden to wash him.
It is permitted to increase. That is specifically in one go; but it is forbidden to add to it afterward, as is written at the end of siman 503.
And some forbid. It appears to me that according to them it is forbidden to wash a baby, since they hold that Yom Tov and Shabbos are equivalent, and on Shabbos it is plainly forbidden to wash a baby except for the need of milah, as is written in siman 331; and see in the Mordechai; and therefore he arranged the first gloss above as referring to the first view.
