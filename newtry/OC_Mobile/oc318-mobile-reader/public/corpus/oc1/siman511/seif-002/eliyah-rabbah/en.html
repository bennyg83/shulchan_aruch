Also one can say the reason: since adding is only rabbinic as Olas Shabbat wrote siman 503, they were not stringent on bathing, and it requires study. Magen Avraham wrote: nowadays that even on a weekday sometimes one does not bathe it two or three days, even on Yom Tov it is forbidden to bathe it — until here; and for a time of need there is room to be lenient.
