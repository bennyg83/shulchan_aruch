[14] [Levush:] The refuse, etc. One should not rub it by hand in order to remove the refuse, for that is like selecting; so it must be stated (Malbushei Yom Tov). So too it appears in the Tur and Shulchan Aruch. Yet I am surprised, for the Yerushalmi, Perek Tolin, says that soaking is prohibited because of selecting, and rubbing because of threshing. See Rashi's commentary on page 140; this requires study.
[Letter 14*] [Levush:] From the flours, etc. A mistaken printing in Rashi's commentary misled him, for the dibbur was not ended before "wine"; what relevance do flours have to lees? One must insert a break in Rashi's commentary, and "flours" refers to wine (Malbushei Yom Tov). The Rambam wrote in chapter 21 that it is forbidden to hang a strainer in the way one does on a weekday, lest one come to strain. Even if there are, etc. It is obvious that placing water in a strainer that has no lees is permitted for this reason.
