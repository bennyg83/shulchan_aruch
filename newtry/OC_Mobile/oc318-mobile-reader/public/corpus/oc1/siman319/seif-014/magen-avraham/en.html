And the beginning of straining when refuse is not recognizable — it is not borer (Ran); according to this forbidden to pour fat from the dish, for they are two types of foods and forbidden to separate one from its fellow except by hand to eat immediately; and here when he pours from the vessel it is forbidden like beer; and from Rif's words it appears there is no prohibition in beer except when he puts sediments in the mouth of the vessel — then it is like borer through a vessel; but when he pours thus — permitted to drink immediately; and it appears also Tur and Shulchan Aruch concede this, except they discuss one who wants to drink later — then even without a vessel it is forbidden; but for immediate drinking — permitted as is stated seif 1; but regarding fat it is refuse from food and forbidden as is stated seif 3; and possible borer does not apply except in mixed things, but here each stands alone:
