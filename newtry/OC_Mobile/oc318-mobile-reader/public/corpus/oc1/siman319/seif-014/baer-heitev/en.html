When evident. And at the beginning of their floating, when the refuse is not recognizable — it is not selecting — Ran. And accordingly forbidden to pour fat from the dish, for these are two species of food and forbidden to select one from the other except by hand in order to eat it immediately; here, when he pours from the vessel — forbidden like beer; and it is possible that selecting applies only to mixed things, but here it stands alone — see Magen Avraham. And Magen Avraham wrote in siman 510 s.k. 13: it appears to me permitted to collect fat floating on the surface of milk that they call smetana even on Shabbat; and when it reaches near the milk he should leave some with the milk — as is written siman 319 seif 14; and specifically when he needs it that day; and if he does not need it but only fears it will spoil and be ruined — permitted to do it through a non-Jew — as is written siman 307 seif 5, end of his words there:
