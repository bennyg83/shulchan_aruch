(15) It is not called separating — unless some of them are not so good as the other pieces, such as pieces that were scorched from the force of cooking, some fish pieces, and he wants to separate those that were not scorched; or some fish pieces are from live fish and some from dead fish and he wants to separate these from those — in all this, even in one type, separating applies, and he must separate that which he wants to eat now, and it is literally like withered leaves in seif 1. And sour apples and sweet apples — Peri Megadim is uncertain whether it is called one type or not; and if due to its sourness it is not fit to eat — it is certainly similar to refuse, and there is borer prohibition concern in this; and even if eaten through exertion — there is rabbinic prohibition, and it is similar to withered leaves stated above in seif 1. And behold Taz is stringent even in one type not to separate except that which he wants to eat now, or take arbitrarily what comes to his hand to leave for another meal — not the manner of separating; but many later authorities disagreed with him and agreed with Rama who ruled like Terumat HaDeshen. And know that roasted meat and cooked meat are called two types for this matter, and all the more meat of divided types of fowl; therefore they must beware at large meals where several types of fowl are placed together and they separate to leave for Motzei Shabbat — they should separate those they want to eat now and not the reverse. Later authorities wrote the same applies in everything when they are two types, such as utensils and garments — separating applies; therefore one must separate this type he wants to take now and the rest remain in place and not the reverse; and possible if several garments hang on the wall and he searches for his garment he wants to wear now and thereby must first remove all other garments — it is not included in borer; and likewise if several types are in a bowl one on another and the type he wants to eat is below and he removes those above to reach the type below and take it — it is not included in borer; and see in Biur Halacha:
