(16) [Dress] Made for this purpose, etc. Requires study, since we say regarding a garment: its soaking is its laundering — if so, what difference whether made for this purpose or not; and in Semag, etc.; and it appears to me Tur holds that when there is no laundering it is not considered laundering (Magen Avraham); and it is not correct, for in siman 302 it is explained in Tur that he holds like his view that they forbid in seif 9 there; and so explained in Remazim beginning chapter 12; and so Derishah wrote that Tur holds thus; and Semag and Sefer HaTerumot are no proof that he holds like the first view there.
