(א) And he leaves the second, etc. Rama's intent is what Tur wrote: that which he wants to eat now is considered food and the second is considered refuse; if so he must separate this which he wants to eat and leave the second; but the reverse — no, for it is refuse from food, and so Terumat HaDeshen:
