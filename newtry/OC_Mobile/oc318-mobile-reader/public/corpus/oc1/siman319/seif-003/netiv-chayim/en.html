(Taz s.k. 11) that also in a cloth there is on account of ohel. Requires study; and it appears to me that in a cloth ohel does not apply, since Rav Shimi said in Shabbos 139 and provided that he not make a depression — and he did not say provided he not cover the entire vessel like a funnel on the mouth of a barrel; from this we learn that ohel does not apply to a cloth on a vessel, and a funnel is different for it is very wide, as Tosafos wrote. Alternatively, as Ra'avad and Rashba wrote that it appears like a filter — nevertheless change is required that he not make a depression as on a weekday; but on account of ohel prohibition we are not particular in a cloth. And the Egyptian basket that he said provided he not raise it one tefach from the ground — this is on account of change and not from the reason of ohel, as written in Shulchan Aruch seif 12. And so in Rosh in chapter HaMeivi siman 11 that ohel without partitions is not ohel; if so it is difficult from a funnel, and it appears to me that a funnel is different for it is very wide, and one cannot say since there are partitions for the barrel it is ohel — for if so you will have difficulty why it is permitted to return a pot atop a stove in chapter Kirah siman 4, and all the more so since partitions from erev Shabbos are permitted — perforce the law is that ohel does not apply in covering vessels:
