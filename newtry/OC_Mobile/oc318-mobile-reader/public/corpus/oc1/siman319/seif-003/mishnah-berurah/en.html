(2) With a fork and spoon — Rashi explained: a fork is a wooden vessel made like a kind of funnel, wide on one side and narrow on the other; the one separating legumes puts them in the wide side and shakes them and they roll through its narrow mouth and the refuse that are not round remain in the vessel; and a spoon is a large bowl — exempt but forbidden, for it is borer like an unusual manner, for the essence of separating is only in a winnowing shovel and sieve:
