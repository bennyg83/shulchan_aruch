And one can answer since the withered leaves are fit for eating through exertion, and similarly on Shabbat there is only a rabbinic concern; therefore R' Yehuda permitted in the synagogue, end quote — behold it is proven that even when the leaves are eaten through exertion nevertheless there is rabbinic prohibition to separate refuse from food; and that which they permitted rabbinic concern in the synagogue to separate refuse from food and did not say he should separate food from refuse — for even rabbinic concern there is none; one must say the Sages did not want to burden him so much; and perhaps one can say since in the synagogue eating is forbidden, also the food is called refuse; except according to this Maggid Mishneh's answer was unnecessary that the leaves are also food through exertion, and one can say under duress:
