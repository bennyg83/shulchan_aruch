(c) There: in a funnel and tamchei, etc. A funnel is a wooden vessel made like a pipe — wide in the back and narrow in front; metalworkers make it; and one clarifies legumes in it — puts legumes in the wide place and shakes it, and the legumes roll because they are round and descend through the narrow mouth and the refuse remains in the vessel — Rashi Shabbos 74a.
