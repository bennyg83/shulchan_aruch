3) There: With a winnowing tray or large dish, etc. A winnowing tray is a wooden utensil shaped like a channel, wide at the rear and narrow at the front, made by metalworkers. One who selects legumes with it places them in the wide section and shakes it. Because the legumes are round, they roll and descend through the narrow opening while the refuse remains in the utensil. Rashi, Shabbos 74a. A large dish is a broad bowl into which the entire pot is poured and from which portions are transferred to smaller bowls. Bach; Perishah, paragraph 3; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2.
