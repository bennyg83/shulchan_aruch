One may not soak — Magen Avraham 9 — both are forbidden: soaking to float the refuse, or to remove through soaking the refuse from it — meaning their peel; and what Shulchan Aruch wrote that it is like borer — likewise in Tur and Rashi's language that it is borer for him; and possibly there is chatas liability — there is none, for it is k'lachar yad; and see Dress in sieve permitted to put and we are not concerned lest he clarify, for there is no way to clarify as we clarify in winnowing; and it is a decree upon a decree; and it appears a thing whose way is thus — forbidden to put in sieve — requires study. And I the humble one ask a small question: if that which borer by hand to eat immediately is permitted as Shulchan Aruch (that borer is not done thus as Rashi explained Shabbos 74, and likewise cutting vegetables siman 321 seif 2) — whether specifically for a person or also for an animal permitted in such a case; and if he draws in water so the food floats and dirt sinks below — if the refuse floats above and he wishes to eat the food immediately — if permitted like Shulchan Aruch. And behold question one — one may say from what Magen Avraham wrote siman 324 s.k. 15: cutting for chickens permitted — Ran; and he did not learn it except from borer — infer: borer for an animal to eat immediately permitted.
