Know that when selecting with such a utensil, even selecting less than the volume of a dried fig is forbidden as half the prescribed measure because of a sieve or sifter. Half the prescribed measure on Shabbos is forbidden by the Torah, not rabbinically, so this is not a decree added to another decree. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1.
