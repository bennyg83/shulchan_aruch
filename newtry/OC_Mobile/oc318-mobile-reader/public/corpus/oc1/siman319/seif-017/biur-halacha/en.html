(א) One who curdles, etc. — All this is Rambam's language; he explained in chapter 7 meaning he takes milk and puts rennet to curdle it — as I wrote in Mishna Berurah; and what the annotator explained "he removes," etc. — not Rambam's language; nevertheless the law is true, for so Rivash wrote in siman 221 that this too is included in selecting:

(ב) They put sesame seeds and nuts, etc. — This is Tosefta's language [brought in Magen Avraham]: they put water into roasted flour provided they do not knead; they put sesame seeds and nuts into honey provided they do not knead; and see Magen Avraham who expanded; and correct is as they wrote that this baraita follows R' Yosi b'R' Yehuda; and "provided they do not knead" means not knead much but little by little as in the Gemara there 156 — for Rabbi, putting water into flour is forbidden; therefore what the baraita concludes, putting sesame seeds, etc. — also follows R' Yosi b'R' Yehuda; therefore Rambam who copied this law follows his ruling that he ruled like R' Yosi b'R' Yehuda that they put water into bran; therefore on putting water there is no prohibition due to kneading as in the Gemara there; but according to poskim who rule like Rabbi, also putting sesame seeds and nuts into honey is forbidden due to kneading; if so, per the first view brought below siman 321 seif 16 and siman 324 seif 3, there is also prohibition in the putting, as appears to me:

(ג) He scatters, etc. — In responsum R' Akiva Eiger siman 20 he was uncertain regarding pouring a little water from a flask through a window and the wind scatters the drops — one goes here and one there — whether liable due to winnowing; and he raised many sides to be lenient since the poskim omitted it; Yerushalmi implies they do not hold thus, but melacha of winnowing is like selecting that selects refuse from food, but in entirely refuse he is not liable for winnowing; and even according to Rama one may justify that it is specifically in spitting, for just as there is no threshing and winnowing except in produce of the ground, so winnowing only in produce of the ground and a person is called produce of the ground, but not in water that is not produce of the ground; and further it is not pleasing to him and psik reisha that it is not pleasing — some permit, see there; and in Sefer Elef HaManeh he explained Yerushalmi's intent regarding transferring four amot in reshut harabim through wind — and this is correct:
