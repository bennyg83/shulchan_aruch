17. Churning (extracting butter from milk) is a Toldah of Borer and therefore although one may put sesame and nuts into honey he must not churn them with his hand (see Siman 340:11). RAMA: One who spits into the wind on Shabbos and the wind disperses the saliva, is liable for Zoreh – Winnowing.
