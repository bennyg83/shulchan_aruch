(א) And one who churns. Who takes milk and puts rennet in it to churn — liable, for he separated the curd from the milk; and if he curdled and made cheese — liable on account of building, for whoever gathers part to part and all adhered until one body was made, similar to building (Rambam ch. 7). And Rashi explained he makes a kind of leather pouch and puts the curd inside and the milk drips; and so Maggid Mishneh ch. 9, see there:

(ב) Therefore, etc. Maggid Mishneh wrote it is Tosefta ch. 13 thus: they put water into parched flour and provided he not knead; they put sesame and nuts into honey and provided he not knead; they crumble rusks for a sick person and provided he not knead; but churning is an act of cooking and eating, end of his words; it appears the prohibition is on account of kneading, and why did Rambam write on account of borer? And furthermore we do not rule like that Tosefta, for it is R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda, and we rule like R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda as is written siman 321 seif 4 that they knead parched flour; if so sesame and nuts too have no kneading concern, and possible Rambam stated his own reason that it is forbidden on account of borer; but regarding Maggid Mishneh requires further study (and see in Mei HaShiloach with glosses of Maharif). And R' Yehuda Yavo wrote ch. 218: when they make food from ground nuts or garlic they put vinegar from erev Shabbat and tomorrow they grind it, end of his words; it appears he holds it is on account of kneading as is written siman 321 seif 16.
