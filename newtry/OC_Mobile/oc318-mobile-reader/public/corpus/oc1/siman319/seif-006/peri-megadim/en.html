Or — as in sign 8 Avodat HaKodesh in the name of Ran — three distinctions: where the majority do not object at all, only they clarify so it be more clear and the like — then permitted in a strainer; and where the majority object and it is possible to drink under duress — strainer forbidden, cloth permitted; where there is no bleaching — even cloth forbidden. And it appears for one who is fastidious and cannot drink thus — where there is chaff and the like — possibly even where the majority do not object at all we do not say batlah da'ato and for him it is forbidden, for it is borer, etc. — requires study:
