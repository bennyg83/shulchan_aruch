(א) (s.k. 6) To eat, etc., from meal to meal — Rav Hai Gaon brought the dispute of poskim what is called immediately: Mordechai's view — specifically to begin eating after his separating is permitted, but to eat after an hour — liable; and R' Chananel's view — what he separates for the need of the meal at which he reclines is called immediately. And R' Yehuda Yavo brought another view that even to separate three or four hours before the meal, if it is for the need of that meal — it is called immediately and forbidden only to separate from meal to meal, such as separating in the morning that is before the midday meal and he separates for the need of the between-the-evenings meal; or when he stands in the midst of the midday meal and separates for the need of the between-the-evenings meal — in this he is liable:

(ב) Rambam wrote in the language Rav Hai Gaon wrote here in Shulchan Aruch, and Rav Hai Gaon wrote thus: it appears from his words there is a distinction between separating food from refuse and separating one food from another food, that in separating food from refuse if he separated to leave until after three or four hours — liable; and separating one food from another food — he is not liable except in separating in the morning to eat between the evenings (that is, for the need of another meal); except I do not know from where he learned this distinction; and if he did not intend thus — difficult, why did he divide them in two (meaning first he wrote what Rav Hai Gaon stated seif 2: one who separates food from refuse, etc., even to set aside for himself that day, etc., and liable; and afterward he wrote what Rav Hai Gaon stated seif 3: if before him two types of food, etc., and if he separated, etc., such as if he separated in the morning, etc., liable), end quote Rav Beit Yosef; it appears clear to Rav Hai Gaon that perforce he must hold this distinction, except Rav Hai Gaon did not know from where he learned it; and since also in Shulchan Aruch he copied Rambam's language — perforce also Rav Hai Gaon holds this distinction and relied on Rambam's testimony, for Rambam certainly found a place for this distinction; therefore Magen Avraham wrote on this law that it deals with separating one food type from another:

(ג) That specifically from meal to meal he is liable, and all this according to Rav Hai Gaon; but according to Rama certainly even in two types of food it is not permitted except adjacent to the meal; but three and four hours before the meal, even though for the need of the first adjacent meal — liable, for behold it is written in seif 1 "and everything he separates for the need of the meal at which he reclines immediately is called immediately," and presumably it refers to both of them, whether refuse from food or two types of food that the Mechaber wrote there. And likewise in seif 3 he wrote "and two types, etc., to eat them immediately," etc., it appears thus:

(ד) And as is stated regarding fish and between the evenings. He wrote thus: and Terumat HaDeshen wrote in one type, etc., and permitted to take some and leave some for another meal, etc., and was uncertain regarding two types of fish, etc., end quote; it appears it is forbidden only for the need of another meal; therefore he cited this permission in one type. And see in Terumat HaDeshen itself in the language of the question it appears more clearly that such is his view in two types of food; and it is known that the questions in Terumat HaDeshen are Terumat HaDeshen's own words, see there. And all this according to Rav Hai Gaon; unlike Rama as stated. Therefore Magen Avraham wrote in siman 321 on Rama's gloss in seif 12 in s.k. 15 that we require adjacent to the meal literally; and see in Sefer Tosafos Shabbos. And behold Taz disagrees with Terumat HaDeshen and Rama and holds that even in one type separating applies; and that which the Gemara cited "if before him two types," etc., is for amplification it cited two types, for it would have been thought in two types there is no separating since each type stands alone; and he brought proofs for his words, and in Sefer Acharonim and Sefer Tosafos Shabbos and Chayei Moshe agreed with Terumat HaDeshen and Rama and rejected Taz's proof and brought proof for Terumat HaDeshen and Rama, see there:
