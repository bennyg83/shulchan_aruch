(א) Seif 6 "one does not," etc. First chapter of Beitza 12b:

(ב) "Rather." Shabbos 128a:

(ג) "And even though." Per Rashi in Shabbos there who explains how one threshes — for the first chapter of Beitza refers to Shabbos, and proof for this is that in the Tosefta of Beitza it is taught "one who threshes m'lilot from erev Shabbos, and one who threshes m'lilot on Shabbos winnows," etc. Rashba there:

(ד) "And some say." Per Ran in the name of others — specifically to soften is permitted, and this is what is stated in Shabbos "one threshes," etc., and per Tosafos in Beitza 13b s.v. "how," etc. — hence on Shabbos it is forbidden to thresh entirely, and as stated 13b; and the first version holds like Ri'f and the Rosh who hold that it refers to Shabbos as above, and what is stated that on Shabbos it is forbidden — that is without a shinui, and one law applies to all of them — namely with the tips of his fingers on Shabbos:
