With a change, etc. — See Mishna Berurah; and know that in Beitza 12 it is stated they asked before Rava regarding mustard stalks whether permitted to thresh and eat on Yom Tov, and he said they winnow sheaves and thresh legumes on Yom Tov — specifically on Yom Tov, but on Shabbat forbidden to winnow as stated in the sugya there; and the same for threshing legumes, for the Gemara equated them; and only through change is it permitted per the first view — as they proved from the sugya of clearing, see Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham who wondered why the world is lenient — his wonder applies even to the first view, for the world is lenient without change [he means the second view where selecting does not apply — perhaps he holds like the first view as Rama's view truly is, as Acharonim in the name of Maharshal, and requires study; the essence is like the first view]. And behold in Acharonim he brought that Maharash wanted to justify the world's practice — he strained in his words; in truth I know no justification, for the Gemara there proves explicitly forbidden to thresh legumes on Shabbat, as written. And know further from Taz's words it appears if legumes were detached from the stalks inside, permitted to take legumes from them if he wishes to eat immediately — meaning also through change as we proved from the Gemara; and further explanation of Taz see Tosafot Shabbat; and from the Gemara's conclusion it appears no distinction between detached and not — see Peri Megadim who explained another explanation — requires study; and Acharonim and Chayei Adam did not copy this for halacha except Magen Avraham's words, and so Acharonim in the name of Mabit:
