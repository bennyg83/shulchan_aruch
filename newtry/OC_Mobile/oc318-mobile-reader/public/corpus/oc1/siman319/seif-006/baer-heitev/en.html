They are stringent. For they hold that specifically to soften is permitted, but to thresh grain from stalks is a toldah of threshing and forbidden; but when the grain was already threshed from the stalks erev Shabbat — permitted to peel it according to all, see end of siman 321; and legume stalks imply that it is forbidden to thresh them and remove the legumes — requires study, for the whole world practices leniency. And one must say that since they are still moist and even the stalk they eat — it is not threshing but like separating food from food. Magen Avraham. And in Maharil he wondered how they are permitted to eat legumes from their stalks that they call shotein, and so Taz wrote to forbid; and he concluded that even if already detached erev Shabbat one may not take the legumes from the shells except what he wishes to eat immediately — see siman 336 seif 8:
