[10] And some are stringent, etc. Maharshal, Beitzah 6:38, rules like the first view, and Nachalat Tzvi likewise. If the grain was already separated from the ears before Shabbos, everyone agrees it may be peeled. The same applies to peeling nuts from their thin inner skin. See the end of siman 321.
[11] It is prohibited to detach, etc. Maharam of Rothenburg wondered why people eat beans or legumes directly from their pods, since this appears to be mefarek. He answered that each seed is attached to the pod, though I did not understand this. It appears to me that the pods themselves are edible (Malbushei Yom Tov). Magen Avraham writes likewise. Maharam's words appear in Maharil in the name of Maharil. His understanding may be that mefarek applies only where the contents are not attached but merely lie inside the shell. This requires study.
[12] [Levush] And some say, etc. Shulchan Aruch rules without qualification like this view, and this is the primary ruling among the authorities.
