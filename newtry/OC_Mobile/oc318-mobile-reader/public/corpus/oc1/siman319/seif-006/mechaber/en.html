One may not winnow gourds except winnowing with a change — a little on the tips of his fingers.. And even though he separates food from the stalks, since he only separates in an unusual manner for eating — permitted; and there are those who are stringent [Mordechai and Ran] chapter 1 of Yom Tov]. And therefore forbidden to shell walnuts, almonds, or large nuts from their green shell (Maharil); and good to be stringent, since he can eat them thus without shelling:
