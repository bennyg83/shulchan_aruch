And there are those who are stringent. That specifically to soften is permitted, but to thresh grain from stalks is a toldah of threshing and forbidden; but when the grain was already separated from the stalks from erev Shabbat — permitted to shell it according to all, as is stated in the Gemara, and Beit Yosef in name of Tosafos on siman 321, and it implies that to shell legumes from their stalks is forbidden to thresh them and remove the legumes; and requires further study, for the entire world practices leniency; and one must say since they are still moist and even the stalk they eat it — it is not threshing but only like separating food from food.
