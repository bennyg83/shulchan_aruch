In Tur it is written: it is not considered selecting, for the albumen and yolk are both food and they do not put it in to eat but to improve the appearance of the mustard — therefore not considered selecting food from refuse, end of his words. There is extra language here — certainly both are food and there is no refuse here but food from food; therefore they are one species, for otherwise we would forbid due to two species even though both are food; and according to what I wrote in this siman according to Tur's view that even in food from food, even in one species, selecting applies — namely what he wishes to eat now is food and what remains is called refuse — it works well here: since he wishes to eat the yolk, the albumen remaining there is refuse; therefore he wrote not to eat the yolk immediately but to put it to improve the appearance of mustard already there — and it turns out the albumen is also food like the yolk; and from this our words above were supported, as appears to me: In the chapter they wrote Tosafos: "if two species of food were before him" we read, etc., end of his words; and it appears at first glance that they mean selecting applies only in food, but in other things such as selecting a vessel from vessels — no prohibition of selecting applies; and so from all mentioned in the prohibition of selecting they mentioned food; therefore the Tanna did not state simply "two species were before him" but specifically "foods." However, in the Gemara afterward Rava said: one who makes chavitzah is liable for seven sins, and Rashi counts selecting among them — namely selecting the coarse pebbles from them, etc.; if so, one must say that which Tosafos wrote "species of foods" we read — for a leniency, that even in food from food we say the remainder is refuse:
