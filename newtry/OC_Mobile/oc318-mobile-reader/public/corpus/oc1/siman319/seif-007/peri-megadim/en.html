And know: clarifying in a strainer — there is chatas liability in the ways explained among decisors; and in cloths there is no chatas liability, only exempt but forbidden per the ways that will be explained with Hashem's help in sign 8. However Tur wrote the reason of strainer is because of borer; but Rambam chapter 5 of Motza Shabbat 1:1 — borer and he dances like Rabbah; and as Rashi explained Shabbos 138a — Avodat HaKodesh; and there is practical difference: strainer in hand or cloth to drink immediately — one may say permitted as Shulchan Aruch; and one may say more than funnel lest one err as a safeguard for sieve — from the reason of dancing one may say forbidden.
