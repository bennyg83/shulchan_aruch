(s.k. 7) One who separates, etc., for on Yom Tov we are lenient; therefore on Yom Tov if the food is greater than the refuse — he separates refuse from food, since on Yom Tov what is for human food need is permitted, also separating is permitted; if so, to lessen effort is preferable. And from this it is proven we do not rule like Tur who holds if the refuse is greater than the food, also the small amount of food is nullified relative to refuse and forbidden to move it; and Taz brought this, it appears he ruled in this like Tur. However from what is written in siman 507 that on Yom Tov permitted to separate food when refuse is greater — it is proven we do not rule in this like Tur, for regarding muktzeh movement Yom Tov is stricter than Shabbat.
