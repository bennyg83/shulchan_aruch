7. Food peeled before Shabbos must not be sifted with a cone (which Rashi explained to be a utensil whose one end is wide and the other is in the form of a spout. Legumes are inserted into the wide end, and shaking causes the food to roll out the spout and the refuse to remain inside) or with a plate (a large plate), or with both hands.
