And even if the food is greater. In Tur it is written that if the refuse is greater than the food — forbidden even to move it, for the food is nullified in the majority of refuse; nevertheless, nuts and pistachios that broke and are still mixed in their shells — even though the shells are greater than the food — it is not called refuse greater than food, since their manner is thus, like a guard for fruit, end of his words — except that in the green shell it is forbidden to peel on Shabbat, as Rama wrote nearby. Beit Yosef challenged: here it implies that whoever selects by hand, even if he puts it into a vessel, even into a nafah and sieve — permitted if immediate; and it is difficult from what is stated in the first chapter of Beitza that on Yom Tov one selects in his usual manner in his lap, in a basket and scoop — and "in his lap" implies he selects by hand and puts into his lap; and similar to lap are basket and scoop; if so, permitted only to select and leave in his hand, but to leave in basket and scoop forbidden, even though he selects only by hand to eat immediately; and it appears to me that as it is stated — and as is stated — "in his lap" means he selects and puts into his lap; and nafah, sieve, basket, and scoop mean he does the selecting in them, end of his words. And Maharach z"l explained this "in his lap" also as selecting — he spreads his garment on his lap and selects on it. Further he wrote that there was an error in the Mishnah's language before Beit Yosef, who forbids on Yom Tov in basket and scoop — it is not so, for the Mishnah forbade on Yom Tov only nafah and sieve, etc., end of his words; and in truth they were correct together in both — that "into his lap" certainly refers to placing after selecting into his lap, as is stated explicitly in the Gemara there; before this we learned there: one who winnows sheaves of wheat blows by hand and eats; and if he blew and put into his lap — Rashi explained: into the hem of his garments — liable for tithe; and they conclude there that for Shabbat he is exempt; and here too it says selecting into his lap — meaning he puts even into his lap after selecting, and the selecting itself is in basket and scoop; therefore Tur in siman 510 did not mention lap at all, only basket and scoop, which are the vessels of selecting; but in placing after selecting — permitted in every manner; and in the Gemara they mentioned it only because they mentioned it regarding tithe that he is liable.
