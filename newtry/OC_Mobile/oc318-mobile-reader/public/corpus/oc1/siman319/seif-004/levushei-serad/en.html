Seif 3: two species. This seif is superfluous, for he already wrote this law in seif 1; and he mentioned it only because of the latter part "and if he clarified and set aside after time," etc. — for this law was not yet mentioned, since seif 2 deals only with food and refuse and not two species of food; but the difficulty is: just as in seif 1 he included both together regarding eating immediately — likewise in seif 2 he should have rolled food and refuse together with two species regarding setting aside even for his heart that day he is liable; indeed he could not include them, for they differ: in food and refuse, whenever it is not close to the meal he is liable as I wrote in sign 3; but in two species of food it is permitted even far from the meal and he is liable only from meal to meal — all this is Magen Avraham's intent s.k. 6; and so in Beit Yosef:
