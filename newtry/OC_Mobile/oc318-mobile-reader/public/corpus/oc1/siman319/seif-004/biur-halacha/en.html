(א) One who selects refuse, etc. — This is Rambam's language — meaning even immediately, for if for later, even food from refuse is liable; and so in Beit Yosef. And one may be uncertain if "one who selects" refers specifically when he selects beforehand and prepares to eat immediately, but if at the time of eating itself he holds in his hand and wishes to eat, removes the refuse and throws it — selecting does not apply even though he does this before eating, for this is the manner of eating; or perhaps no difference, but he must throw it only after he eats, or throw some of the food with it; and I found in Sefer Berkei Yosef who wrote that Mahari Yehuda Aboltafia and Maharitz Tzahalon disagreed — Mahari Yehuda is lenient and Maharitz forbids; and for this reason he forbids throwing a fly from a cup unless he also pours some beverage with it [brought in Beit Heitev]; and he permitted only for another reason; and see below in seif 16 in Biur Halacha; and Berkei Yosef wrote that from the poskim's conclusion that they did not distinguish in this — it implies refuse from food is liable even in this manner. And I searched in books and found that the Rishonim disagree in this reasoning: behold in Ramban's Chiddushim it is proven like Mahari Yehuda Aboltafia — Ramban in his notes on Shabbat daf 74 in the passage "and is it permitted," etc., explained: from reasoning Rabbah holds, etc., that there is no manner of selecting thus, but like one who eats and finds refuse in a piece he is eating and separates refuse from food — that is permitted; behold Ramban stated this as obvious, that this is not the manner of selecting; and Rav Yosef who argues there on Rabbah is only for another reason as he wrote there explicitly; and Ramban's language was also copied by Ritva in his Chiddushim. However, from Rosh's responsum copied in Shulchan Aruch below in seif 16 — it implies he holds like Maharitz, that he did not permit there regarding water with worms to strain into his mouth through a cloth only because what blocks at the time of drinking the refuse from entering his mouth is not a type of melacha and permitted [meaning selecting does not apply in what he blocks through his mouth]; and since he did not state simply that at the time of drinking itself selecting does not apply — certainly he holds like Maharitz in every case:

(ב) From food, etc. — So Ran in the chapter Kol HaGadol in the name of R' Chiya; and Rambam wrote that Ramban agreed thus, and so it appears from the laws [I do not know whence — perhaps that which Rif brought for toramus is that he is liable for later and food from refuse permitted even for later; at first glance Tosafos explain thus in the passage "and I do not know," etc. — they explain what is said there: if due to holding food from refuse forbidden — meaning for later, and automatically what the Gemara says afterward "let us say Chizkiya holds food from refuse forbidden" — also for later; and on this the Gemara rejects that toramus is comparable to refuse from food; and Abbaye's statement obligating for later refers to refuse from food — see there; and perhaps Rambam's intent is from that which Rif stated simply that one who selects toramus is liable and did not explain — we infer it deals in every case — requires study]; and so in Semag and Semak; all this Beit Yosef brought there; and so Rosh, Ritva, Me'iri; and I saw in Sefer Ma'amar Mordechai who wrote on this seif: from here it is clear one should be careful on Shabbat at large banquets and the like to split fish from the back and remove the spine in the middle — for this is selecting refuse from food, liable even immediately; rather leave the spine there and not throw it outside; and every person should be careful not to throw meat bones outside except in the manner of eating — meaning a bone that has a little meat on it, he should hold it to eat from it and throw it from his hand afterward [from this language it implies that even at the time of eating itself one must be careful not to separate bones from meat and throw outside — only after he already peeled the meat and ate may he throw it, but this is not selecting — as we wrote above]; and that which has no meat on it — he should not touch it but eat the meat and let the bones remain in the bowl, and afterward throw them if he wishes, end of his words. And behold the world is not careful at all to be precise in this — not to mention at the time of eating itself, where in this we possibly cannot protest, for they have whom to rely on, as above; but even before eating, such as men and women who prepare the food to bring to the table — they are also not careful at all and peel bones from the meat beforehand; if so, they stumble in liability to a chatas. And I said to seek merit for them — not to mention if the bones are soft and also fit for eating, for it is common that even after separating them he returns and eats them — certainly there is no refuse on them at all; but even if they are hard, the reason is that since bones and meat are in one connection, our matter applies to what Magen Avraham brought in siman 510 in the name of Yeshuot Shalom regarding nuts and pistachios that broke and are still in their shells — that even for selecting it is one species and there is no refuse on it; and in whatever matter he repairs food from the guard — it is mere food repair and there is no melacha on it, end of his words [and even though Magen Avraham rejected this from that Beit Yosef wrote end of siman 321 in the name of Yerushalmi that one who peels garlic and onions is liable due to selecting — his rejection was only because Yeshuot Shalom stated simply and wrote there is no melacha on it, and it implies even if his intent was for later; and for this Acharonim challenged from that one who peels garlic, etc.; but for immediately Magen Avraham also agrees there is no selecting on it, as Beit Yosef itself states there that to eat them immediately permitted to peel garlic and onions; and with this Magen Avraham's words are also resolved from Acharonim's challenge]; and here too it is mere food repair and there is no melacha on it, even when he peels and removes bones from the meat — provided it is immediate, and like there regarding peeling garlic and onions — even though there too the peel is refuse, nevertheless we rule end of siman 321 that where one peels in order to eat immediately — permitted; therefore because immediately there is no refuse on the peel but mere food repair — and not comparable to ordinary refuse that is absolute refuse separated from food, unlike this which is connected together with the food; and the same in our matter where his intent is to eat immediately — we say it is mere food repair and there is no refuse on them since they are not yet separated and are connected together. And it is strained to say that only there Yeshuot Shalom holds thus because nut shells are a guard for fruit and garlic peel likewise — for Yeshuot Shalom compares the prohibition of selecting to the prohibition of moving mentioned there in Rosh and Tur; and in the prohibition of moving Rosh and Tur explicitly distinguish between bones and this, because bones were already separated — it implies when not separated its law is like nuts; and one should not challenge our words from that stated in siman 320 seif 5 that Rabbenu Tam forbids squeezing unripe grapes into the pot of food because the grape itself is not fit for eating — if so it is like selecting food from refuse; behold even though the grape itself is initially one entity with food and refuse together, nevertheless there he selects because in the end one is selected from the other — and the same would apply here; and one may reject with several reasons: one, that Acharonim wrote there, in the name of Shakhna"g that one who is lenient like the first view who permits even in unripe grapes — did not lose; and even for Rabbenu Tam it appears in Tosafos daf 144b in the passage "one who milks" that its prohibition is due to threshing and not selecting — see there in Biur Halacha what is written; and see in Even HaEzer who wrote that even Ran who copied that it is like selecting food from refuse — his intent is that it is like threshing, and this certainly does not apply in our matter. The conclusion of all the above: at the time of eating itself, one who is lenient and peels bones from meat — we certainly cannot protest; and even to prepare before eating in this manner — according to Rama and Maharshal it is the manner of eating if his intent is to eat the meat immediately; and one may combine with this also the view of some Rishonim brought in Berkei Yosef who hold that immediately permitted even refuse from food — and even though many forbid, as above, and therefore Shulchan Aruch did not rule like them — nevertheless in this matter, where anyway there are many reasons to be lenient, as above, we cannot protest those who practice leniency; but to prepare this for later, as practiced at large banquets — one must be very careful, as we brought above in the name of Ma'amar Mordechai and comparable to peeling garlic and onions forbidden for later, as stated end of siman 321, see there. And all this regarding bones that have meat on them; but those bones that have no meat at all — one should be careful not to select them and throw them from above the plate before eating, for this is refuse from food where selecting applies even immediately; rather take the meat and let the bones remain in the bowl — as we wrote above in the name of Ma'amar Mordechai. And if it is difficult for him to be precise — he should suck in his mouth a little on each bone before throwing it outside, for then there is no refuse on it, as above:

(ג) Liable — See Mishna Berurah that even immediately — for if for later, even food from refuse is liable — so the poskim. And behold what many people practice — they prepare food on Friday morning [made from animal feet] to leave for the third meal — some of them stumble in several prohibitions thereby; therefore I must explain so they know the permitted path. (a) Their manner is to peel garlic and onions and cut them fine and place inside — there are two prohibitions: one on peeling, since it is for later — liable due to selecting as in Yerushalmi brought in Beit Yosef end of siman 321 and Rama there, in the gloss; therefore one who wishes to put garlic and onions for later should not peel them; and prohibition (b) on cutting fine, for there is threshing — as below siman 321 seif 12, see there, that for later all agree liable due to threshing [many are stringent even immediately — see Mishna Berurah; therefore even those who prepare this food to eat immediately must also be careful not to cut onions fine]. (b) If the gravy is hot such that yad soledes bo when putting onions and garlic inside — there is also cooking if in a kli rishon; and even in kli sheini some are stringent, especially if the hand is scalded — certainly be very careful, as we wrote above siman 318 seif 5 — see Mishna Berurah s.k. 148; this is very common among those who eat this food hot in the morning; they also mix chametz and dry bread crumbs — therefore be very careful even in kli sheini when the gravy is hot that the hand is scalded; and proper to be careful even if yad soledes bo not to put anything then. (c) They select bones from meat and throw them outside — one should judge this for all as selecting, since it is for later, unless he sucks each bone a little in his mouth before throwing it — then there is no selecting on it; and perhaps one may say a bone with marrow is considered one species with meat and there is no selecting — but not reasonable, for it is no better than roasted and cooked meat we wrote above is called two species; therefore proper to act as we wrote:
