(א) Therefore — Mishna Berurah rules like the first view for practical law, except good to be stringent where possible to eat thus without separating; and therefore in siman 510 he emended nothing, for there are those who are stringent; how does one rub on Yom Tov — requires change — Magen Avraham there sign 1; but the essence is like the first view and Avodat HaKodesh here; and therefore Taz z"l wrote regarding legumes: not only if still attached on the stalks in the place of their growth — that is separating (if they are closed as must be said below or 8); but even if already detached (from the matter of nittuk) from the place of growth and the stalks still lie closed — it is separating; and there are those who are stringent — only what he wishes to eat immediately then rely on the first view; and the matter is in siman 336 Taz sign 5; and from there we learn that separating is not from the place of growth when exposed — for R' Yeruham learned it there from Chullin 127b and 128 — detached and cannot live is considered detached; nevertheless perhaps it is separating; therefore exposed there is no separating — only when inside its peel whether attached there or not — Avodat HaKodesh sign 11; and if so, when the stalk is open — even if not detached — permitted; and even when the stalk dried — separating for Magen Avraham 8 — in such exposed case there is no separating, as I wrote; and see Rashi Shabbos 74; and consider it like pounding — separating is Dress; and regarding borer it depends on the dispute of Taz and Mishna Berurah in sign 2 in one species whether borer applies; but in dried stalk certainly there is borer — and its time will be explained below in Avodat HaKodesh; and it appears to me as I wrote:
