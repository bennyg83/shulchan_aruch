"the sorter," and it resolves Tosafos' difficulty in Shabbos 74a s.v. "borer," and one cannot say the second version of Tosafos there "or also," etc., but rather the first is preferable, etc., for according to Rambam who explains "is there anyone," etc., the plain explanation is sorting food, and as stated in siman 321 seif 2 the difficulty remains, and therefore according to all sorting refuse is called sorting, and this law Beit Yosef inferred from Rambam's words; but the Tur wrote that everything is like the law of Yom Tov and per the view of Rashi and the Rosh in the first chapter of Beitza in the mishnah "the sorter," etc., and the gemara there, and so wrote Tosafos there and in chapter 6 of Shabbos:
