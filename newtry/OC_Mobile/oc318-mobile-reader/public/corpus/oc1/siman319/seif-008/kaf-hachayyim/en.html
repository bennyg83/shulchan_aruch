8) There: It is permitted for immediate use. This applies specifically when removing food from refuse. Maggid Mishneh; Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. Even if the food is the greater quantity and selecting it requires more effort, he must nevertheless specifically remove the food, as stated below in seif 4 in the gloss; see there. If he selected for immediate use but afterward changed his mind and kept it for the late-afternoon meal, it is improper to do so.
