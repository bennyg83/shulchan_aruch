(א) (s.k. 8) And there are those who are stringent, etc.; but when already separated, etc., permitted to shell it, etc.; therefore Rama wrote and therefore forbidden to shell, etc., from their green shell — because he was concerned for the stringent view, and it turns out the world practices leniency to shell food from nuts, that is, when the green shell was already removed and it is like grain already separated from stalks from erev Shabbat; unlike when they are still in their green shell — they are like separating grain from stalks, forbidden per the stringent view, as is stated in the Gemara and Beit Yosef in name of Tosafos Beitza daf 13b s.v. "and if he shelled," etc., on this that they say there Rav's wife shells for him basket by basket, and so the wife of R' Chananel for R' Chananel — Tosafos wrote this distinction that it deals when grain was separated from stalks from erev Shabbat and therefore permitted:
