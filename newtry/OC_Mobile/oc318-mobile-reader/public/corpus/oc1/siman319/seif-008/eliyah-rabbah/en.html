(8) [Dress] And when the refuse, etc. Bach wrote: since Shulchan Aruch wrote plainly that one clarifies food from refuse, etc. — it appears specifically when the food is abundant; but when the food is little one should be stringent like Rosh not to move it at all — until here; and in my humble opinion it is not correct, for on the contrary from Shulchan Aruch's conclusion it appears that even when the food is abundant it is permitted; and you should know, for he cited the language of Rambam; and from this language Binyamin Zeev inferred it is permitted; and so appears from what Rama wrote above: and even abundant food — one may not clarify the refuse; and I am also astonished, for in Shulchan Aruch siman 510 seif 2 he ruled that clarifying food is permitted; and one should not dismiss and distinguish between Shabbat and Yom Tov — for specifically regarding borer which is melacha they distinguish as Bach and decisors wrote; but regarding muktzeh, on the contrary Yom Tov is stricter than Shabbat as written siman 495; also the primary law is regarding Yom Tov in Beitza daf 14, and from it the decisors learned; and so appears in Bach himself in siman 510 that Yom Tov and Shabbat are equal in this; and so is the essence, for I found that Rabbeinu Yeruham disagrees; and Sefer Avodat HaKodesh; and Shilta Gibborim in the name of Riaz chapter 1 of Beitza; and Maharshal there agreed to be lenient; and further that the primary decisor is from Rashi's explanation; and in Ran chapter 1 of Beitza it is proven to me in what he emended in the name of Rashi that he does not hold this way — requires study.
