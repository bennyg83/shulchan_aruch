[13] One may not soak, etc. Shulchan Aruch writes this. Bach and Taz say it is a scribal error and should read "one may not lift out," as in Shabbos 140b. I examined all editions of the Mishnah and they too read "one may not soak," as does the Yerushalmi in both Mishnah and Gemara, and likewise Rif and Ran. Regarding placing legumes and other seeds into water, see siman 510, seif katan 4.
[14] [Levush] The refuse, etc. The text should read: "and one may not rub it by hand to remove the refuse, because that is like selecting" (Malbushei Yom Tov), and so it appears in Tur and Shulchan Aruch. Yet I am surprised, because the Yerushalmi in chapter Tolin says soaking is prohibited because of selecting, while rubbing is prohibited because of threshing. See Rashi on 140a. This requires study.
[Letter 14*] [Levush] From the flours, etc. A corrupt printing of Rashi misled Levush because the paragraph break before "the wine" was omitted. Flour has no relevance to dregs. The paragraph in Rashi must end earlier, and "flours" refers to wine (Malbushei Yom Tov). Rambam, chapter 21, writes that one may not hang a strainer in the weekday manner lest he come to strain with it. It is obvious that even if there are, etc., placing water into a strainer containing no dregs is permitted for this reason.
