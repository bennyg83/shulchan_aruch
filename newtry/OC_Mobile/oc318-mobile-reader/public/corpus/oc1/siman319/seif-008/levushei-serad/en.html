Magen Avraham s.k. 4: and sets aside the second. At first glance difficult for Rama, for we see that although in the Talmud "he clarifies and eats" forbids but "he clarifies and sets aside" permits — nevertheless in the Mechaber's language "sets aside" is not mentioned at all to permit, because everything "immediately" he calls "he clarifies to eat immediately" as written seif 1; and he does not call the language "sets aside" except regarding prohibition in seif 2 when not immediate; if so, why did he write here the language "sets aside" to permit immediately; therefore Rama explained: meaning he sets aside the second that he does not wish to eat, and what he clarifies is in order to eat immediately. So appeared Rama's intent; but Magen Avraham was not satisfied with explaining thus, for it is no difficulty at all — for granted in seif 1 where there is no permission except truly close to the meal he does not call it "sets aside"; but in this seif where it is permitted even far from the meal as I wrote sign 4 — therefore he calls it "sets aside"; therefore Magen Avraham explained Rama's intent: do not err — meaning he clarifies one from one and sets aside the other to eat immediately; but in truth this is forbidden; rather meaning: he clarifies one from one and sets aside the second, and what he clarifies is in order to eat immediately — and examine:
