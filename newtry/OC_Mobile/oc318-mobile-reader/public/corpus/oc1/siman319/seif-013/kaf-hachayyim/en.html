13) There, gloss: Even if others are eating with him, etc. This is not exact. It is permitted even if he selects everything for others and does not eat with them. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1. Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, paragraph 1, however, writes that this matter remains disputed, because some require specifically that he eat with them; see there. It therefore appears proper to be stringent and eat some of the item that he selects for others.
