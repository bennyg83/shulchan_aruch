(13) One may not soak, etc. So wrote Shulchan Aruch; and Bach and Taz wrote it is a scribal error and one must read: one may soak, as in the Talmud [Shabbos] daf 140b; and I examined all Mishnah editions and it also reads one may not soak; and so in Yerushalmi in the Mishnah and Gemara; and so in Rif and Ran; and regarding putting legumes and other seeds in water see siman 510 s.k.
