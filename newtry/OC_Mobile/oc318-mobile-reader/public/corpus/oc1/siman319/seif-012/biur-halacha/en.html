(א) And he should be careful not to raise, etc. — According to Shulchan Aruch who concludes due to change — even if he set it up erev Shabbat, also forbidden to guard; but according to Rashi and other poskim written below in Taz that the reason is ohel — if he prepared this erev Shabbat, permitted to guard on Shabbat:

(ב) The funnel from the rim of the vessel, etc., due to change — This reason Ran wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah — not like Rashi who holds due to ohel; and in truth it is very difficult to reject this reason, for many Rishonim also wrote thus — R' Chiya [whose light we recently merited], Rambam, Tur, and Me'iri. And regarding sudar, Ran's view and Magen Avraham who brought it: there we do not require less than a tefach from the rim of the vessel beneath it, for the change he is careful about — not to make a hollow before straining — suffices; and regarding concern of ohel there is none, for the vessel beneath is not very wide; according to his view above end of siman 315 seif 21; and Taz disagrees and holds that in a sudar too one must not raise it a tefach from the rim of the vessel beneath due to concern of ohel, or not cover the entire face of the vessel with the sudar — then there is no concern of ohel for all; and see what we wrote above end of siman 315:
