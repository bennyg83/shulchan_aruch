(ב) That the yolk — he proved from what he wrote above sign 2 that in one species borer applies — from Tur needing to say yolk to white; and so in the Gemara Kamma 1a — see there — infer that in one species borer applies, except they do not do so except for appearance and he does not wish to eat at all neither yolk nor white — therefore no borer; and food from refuse — even if he does not wish to eat now plainly borer. And in Dress he wrote yolk and white are two species; and so Bach — Magen Avraham sign 16.
