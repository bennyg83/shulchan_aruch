(11) And liable — therefore one must beware in what the world practices, to prepare onions in vinegar for some food correction for a third meal, and they separate the good leaves from the withered and wormy ones — they should not do this except adjacent to the meal, and also beware not to separate the withered from the good, for this is refuse from food, as we wrote in s.k. 7. And in commentaries he wrote that he practiced to prepare this from erev Shabbat, for it is difficult to beware in matters of separating in this; and behold one who is stringent and does so — good for him [Acharonim]; nevertheless one may not protest against those who are lenient and prepare on Shabbat, for they can rely on Rama stated above in seif 1 in the gloss; but they should beware in all that we wrote:
