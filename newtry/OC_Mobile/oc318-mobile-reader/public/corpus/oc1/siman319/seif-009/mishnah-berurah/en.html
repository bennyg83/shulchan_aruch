(8) From the leaves — and the same when before him legumes or other fruits and there are worms and mold in them, and likewise fruits that fell to the ground and became mixed with dust and pebbles — he should beware not to separate by hand except what he needs for that meal he wants to eat immediately, and not more, for then he is liable; and also beware that he separate specifically food from refuse and not the reverse [Peri Megadim and Chayei Adam]:
