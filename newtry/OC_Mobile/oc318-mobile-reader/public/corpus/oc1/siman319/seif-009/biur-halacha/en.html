(א) A strainer — See Mishna Berurah; and so Rambam ruled chapter 8 halacha 11; and it appears he was uncertain like whom the halacha follows, Rabbah or R' Zeira, and holds Rabbah and R' Zeira argue with each other — not like Rashi; and so Tosafos daf 73b in the passage "due to," see there; and it appears Tosafos and Rambam explain Rabbah's reason: it is not comparable to sifting, for in sifting while it sifts it constantly acts on refuse and food together as the sieve shakes and thereby selects one species from the other; unlike here where at the time of selecting no act is done on the lees resting in place and wine flows from them — therefore more comparable to selecting where one takes the food and refuse remains in place — in such a case too it is the manner of selecting; and know in our words Rabbah's liability in every sifting for selecting is well settled; therefore Rambam departed from Rashi's explanation; and see Beit Yosef what he wrote on Rambam's view — as appears to me I wrote afterward I found in Yerushalmi chapters Kol HaGadol and Tolim that there it is proven explicitly that one who holds due to selecting does not hold due to sifting — not like Rashi; and Korban HaEdah already preceded me on this challenge to Rashi, see there:
