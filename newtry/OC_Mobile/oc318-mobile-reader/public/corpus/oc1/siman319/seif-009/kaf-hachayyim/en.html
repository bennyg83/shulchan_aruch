9) There: It is permitted for immediate use. 'Immediate' does not mean literally at once, but during that meal. Taz, paragraph 1, proves this from the Gemara; see there. Although Rema states this in the gloss, Levush Serad writes that his intent is to say that the Shulchan Aruch also agrees with Rema. The Gemara's expression 'selects and eats' means immediately, while 'selects and sets aside' means shortly before the meal. These are his words.
