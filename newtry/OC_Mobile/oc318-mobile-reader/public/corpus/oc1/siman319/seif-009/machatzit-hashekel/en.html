(א) (s.k. 9) One may not soak, etc. The same applies if there is, etc. — meaning in Shulchan Aruch he did not write to forbid except when refuse is still stuck to the food, for forbidden to put them in water to remove refuse from food; and on this Magen Avraham wrote the same applies if refuse is not stuck to food but mixed with food — forbidden to put in water to separate them one from another:

(ב) And so from Rashi's language who wrote in Shabbat daf 140a on this Mishnah that taught one may not soak, etc., and Rashi wrote he floats water upon them in a vessel to separate their refuse, and as taught in tractate Beitza daf 14 "also rinses and skims," end quote; and since he wrote to separate their refuse it appears refuse is not stuck to the vetch but mixed with food; and so is the proof he brings from tractate Beitza that there it deals thus; and in truth one must say Rashi's text reads also on Shabbat one may not soak, as Taz wrote s.k. 5 and what Magen Avraham wrote; and see siman 507 — he wanted to say there is dispute of poskim whether thus forbidden on Yom Tov, see there in Magen Avraham s.k. 4; nevertheless on Shabbat all agree it is forbidden:
