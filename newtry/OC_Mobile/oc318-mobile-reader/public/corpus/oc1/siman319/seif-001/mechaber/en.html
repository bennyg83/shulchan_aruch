The laws of borer on Shabbos. And in it 17
seifim: One who selects food from refuse, or there were before him two types of food and he selected one type from another with a winnowing shovel and sieve — liable; with a fork and spoon — exempt but forbidden; and if he selected them by hand for immediate eating — permitted:And everything one selects for the need of that meal at which he reclines — immediately is called immediate [Maggid chapter 8 and Rabbeinu Yonah part 4 chapter 8 and] Beit Yosef and Tur and Rosh] and even if others eat with him — permitted [Tosafos chapter Kol HaKadosh].
