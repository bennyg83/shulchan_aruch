(א) To eat immediately — even the Mechaber agrees it is not only immediately but in that meal is called immediately; and specifically for his heart that day forbidden — see Shabbos 74. And what he wrote on Abaye — he wondered at him, for if permitted to bake and cook that day — in my humble opinion it is no difficulty: Ulla who said one who clarifies for his heart that day and tomorrow is liable for chatas; and plainly borer in sieve and basket — he properly challenged: if permitted to bake for his heart that day, even immediately liable in sieve. Unlike R' Yosef who said borer by hand and in funnel — one may not clarify; and in sieve liable; and on this Abaye afterward said borer immediately — meaning by hand; for in sieve too, immediately as well liable, as Tosafos 74a "and it was taught" and as we need to say below or 8; and for his heart that day by hand becomes like storage — and why not that day by hand exempt; and Abaye "and tomorrow liable for chatas" — in sieve certainly liable for his heart that day; and if permitted, etc. — but it is melacha as Rashi explained; unlike by hand; and on this he answered: therefore that day liable by hand — for otherwise borer immediately by hand would be obvious as a decree upon a decree. Nevertheless difficult: immediately means before the meal an hour — what difference for that meal; and certainly needed to inform us it is permitted l'chatchila:

(ב) And know: in funnel and tamchei to clarify less than a dried fig — half the measure forbidden as a safeguard for sieve and basket, for half the measure on Shabbat is from the Torah not rabbinic, lest there be a decree upon a decree; and Rashi there "and if permitted to bake" — half the measure forbidden nevertheless — see there; and I the humble one wondered at what he wrote in responsa Chacham Tzvi siman 86 — it appears he is in doubt whether half the measure is forbidden nevertheless on Shabbat; one may say specifically in Magen Avraham (and in Magen Avraham we wrote there are spices for this if we say it appears fit to combine to an olive-bulk as one piece); and behold it is explained here thus, as written in the comprehensive introduction and or 8 in the introduction to Tur Shulchan Aruch will be explained further; and with this I say to resolve that which it challenged: if permitted to bake less than the measure and it did not mention cooking, while above it mentioned — and if permitted to bake and cook for his heart that day; because one may say less than the measure can be conceived as permitted to cook on Shabbat as I will say when one has an ill person and adds for the healthy less than the measure before putting on the fire in the pot — according to those who say rabbinic, half the measure would be a decree upon a decree; and the matter is in siman 318 in Magen Avraham and 16 and Beit Yosef there whether we say half the measure on Shabbat is rabbinic — therefore it challenged from baking and does not establish it in funnel and tamchei, for less than the measure permitted, at the measure exempt, and sieve liable — for we learned only funnel and tamchei; and one need not extend:
