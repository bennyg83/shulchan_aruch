(א) In a nafah. For this is the manner of its selecting:

(ב) Immediately. And specifically food from refuse — see seif 4. R' Yehuda Aboltafia — his words are brought in Mahariatz Tzahalon siman 243 — wrote that from Rashi's words it appears that even specifically immediately, when one selects for himself it is permitted but for others forbidden; and Mahariatz Tzahalon there rejected his words and wrote that even for others it is permitted. Further, Mahari Yehuda Aboltafia wrote there that Rambam holds that selecting refuse from food is also permitted immediately; and according to this he wrote that to remove a worm from a cup — permitted to remove it when immediate. And Mahariatz Tzahalon there rejected his words and raised that all poskim hold refuse from food forbidden even immediately, and one who takes refuse from food is always liable even by hand and immediately; and Maharitz there permitted removing a worm from a cup on Shabbat for another reason — there is no selecting in liquid. And also "And he shall send away the worm" — selecting applies only to what requires picking through and selecting, but a thing floating on top is already selected and is like skimming cream from milk — this is not selecting, see there. And Yad Aharon wrote on this that this permission is very strained. And Taz in this siman s.k. 13 and in siman 506 s.k. 3 wrote that one may not remove a worm and flies from a cup, for this is selecting refuse from food; rather one should take some of the beverage with them:
