(א) One who selects food from refuse — see in Peri Megadim who wrote that b'dieved if he selected, one may say forbidden for that Shabbat, for he benefits from Shabbat labor — see there what he is uncertain about in several matters in this topic; however according to what Gra wrote above in siman 318 seif 1, one should not be stringent in other melachos b'shogeg for b'dieved — see there in Biur Halacha. And behold, since the Mechaber did not copy Tosafos' view that food from refuse is specifically when food is greater than refuse — if refuse is greater, the manner is to select the food and in general it is selecting — it implies he does not hold thus; and so it appears below in seif 4 in the gloss:

(ב) And selects one species from another, etc. — Regarding liability, in nafah and sieve there is no distinction whether he selected the species he wished to eat and the second species remained in place, or the reverse [and so when his intent is to eat both immediately — Peri Megadim is uncertain about this and it is not clear, for it appears from Rambam's language that the essence of liability is because he selected one species from another; and see what we wrote below further in such a case]; but regarding permission when he selected by hand — permitted only when he selected the species he wishes to eat and not the reverse, for this species is considered food and the second considered refuse — all this we explained according to Rama's view below in seif 3; and see what we wrote there:

(ג) In a basket and scoop, etc. — And even if he selected in them only half the measure of a dried fig — also forbidden, a decree lest half-measure in nafah and sieve, for which there is a Torah prohibition, for we rule half-measure is forbidden by Torah law [Peri Megadim]:

(ד) In order to eat immediately — And even if he selected only in order to feed animals and birds immediately — also permitted [Peri Megadim]:

(ה) And everything he selects, etc. — See Mishna Berurah; and see in Sefer Tosafot Yerushalayim who proved from the Yerushalmi that we require immediate literally, not like Rama; and it appears that nevertheless one should not be stringent in this, for Rama's words have a source in our Talmud, as Gra brought proof in Biur HaGra, see there:

(ו) From the moldy leaves — In this manner of selecting there is a distinction: if they are separate leaves he may select the good ones and leave the moldy; as Magen Avraham wrote; but if they are heads such as lettuce whose outer leaves are moldy — then he removes the moldy extremities, for this is like removing a peel to eat the inside [Sheyurei Berachah; and so Peri Megadim wrote that this is not the manner of selecting but repairing the food]:
