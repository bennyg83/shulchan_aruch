(Taz s.k. 1) And incidentally I will mention what was difficult to me. Requires study; and to me l'chatchila mechaven, since if he selected by hand for storage he is liable for a chatat — if so on that day certainly also liable for a chatat, as R' Chisda challenged Ulla: and is it permitted to bake with it that day, and is it permitted to cook with it that day? And according to Rashi's explanation that which he answers — this with a fork and spoon, this with a winnowing shovel and sieve — and this is that baraita — it is obvious that it is difficult: and is it permitted to bake?
