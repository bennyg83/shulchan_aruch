1) [Seif 1] One who selects food, etc. Selecting is one of the thirty-nine primary labors. One who transgresses deliberately after warning is liable to stoning, and one who transgresses inadvertently must bring a fixed sin offering. The author explains here which form of selecting incurs the death penalty, which leaves one exempt although the act is forbidden, and which is permitted initially. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1. Whether the selected item may be eaten that day, the distinction between inadvertent and deliberate performance, and selecting for an ill person were explained above in siman 318, seifim 1 and 2, regarding cooking. The same applies to selecting, since there is no distinction between the labors, as stated there. Pischei HaDevir, paragraph 1, has already challenged Eshel Avraham's distinction between removing refuse from food and selecting between two kinds; see there. See also below, paragraphs 36 and 37.
