Behold borer is one of the 39 primary melachot of Shabbat, and one is liable for it also for a chatas b'shogeg and death b'mezid like other melachot; and in practice many people stumble in the prohibition of borer; therefore I will explain it, there's help, in all its details. And I precede this siman with a brief introduction. And it is: the prohibition of borer does not apply from the Torah except in one of three modes, for then it is the manner of separating on weekdays. (1) If he separates refuse from food — even if he separates by hand and his intent is to eat immediately — likewise liable, for this is the weekday manner of separating refuse from food. (2) If he separates with a vessel whose manner is to separate therein, such as in a winnowing shovel and sieve — liable even if he separates food from refuse and his intent is to eat immediately, for thus is the manner of the separator with a vessel: sometimes he separates food from refuse, such as when the sieve holes are fine and the food is coarse, and sometimes the reverse [and if he separates with a vessel whose manner is not to separate therein — exempt but forbidden, as below]. (3) Even if he separates food from refuse by hand, but his intent is to eat after time — liable; and he may not separate except in a manner that he beware in all three modes, namely that he separate food from refuse, and also that the separating be by hand and not in a vessel, and also that his intent be to eat immediately — for then it is not the manner of separating but the manner of eating.
