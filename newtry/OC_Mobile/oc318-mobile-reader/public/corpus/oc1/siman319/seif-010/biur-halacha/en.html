(א) Fine spices — See Mishna Berurah who wrote, and the same if there is flour — so Magen Avraham s.k. 10 in the name of Tanya; and he wrote on this "and so Rashi"; and one who examines Beit Yosef will see he holds that due to flour it is not permitted except in sudarim and not in a strainer; therefore Magen Avraham's words are very difficult, for it implies from him permitted even in a strainer; and for this Tosafot Shabbat and Gaon R' Akiva Eiger and other Acharonim wrote there is a textual error in Magen Avraham and one must emend to below that permitted in sudarim [several Acharonim copied Magen Avraham as written]; but in truth this is difficult from reasoning — what is worse about flour than fine spices? And just as fine spices are permitted in a strainer, so too with flour; and I examined Tanya and it is proven like Magen Avraham that wine with flour is permitted even in a strainer; and so I found in Chelbo; and what Magen Avraham wrote "and so Rashi" — he explains that according to Rashi the Mishnah deals with clear liquids, and that which the Mishnah mentions sudarim is to include that even in sudarim there is no concern of squeezing; and afterward I found in Ran's newly printed Chiddushim that he explains Rashi's words thus explicitly. And it is possible that according to what I copied in Mishna Berurah — a little flour, all agree in this — not worse than fine spices; and in any case one may rely on these three great ones who permit even with flour:

(ב) Since they are fit, etc. — And one who is fastidious and cannot drink thus — where there are spices and the like, even though most people are not particular at all — we do not say his intent is nullified; for him it is forbidden, for it is selecting [Peri Megadim]:

(ג) Due to whitening — But in a strainer it appears selecting does not apply; and see Magen Avraham and Biur HaGra who expanded on the reason; and see Mishna Berurah that I concluded to be lenient as Shulchan Aruch's view, even though from Magen Avraham it appears that according to those who hold even without filth we say its laundering is its washing — forbidden to strain water even in a strainer; and we already wrote above siman 302 seif 9 in Biur Halacha one should be concerned for this view; nevertheless here we were lenient due to Ze'eira's statement that he is lenient explicitly regarding clear water, and all poskim copied it; therefore one must say as Tosafot Shabbat explained that even according to this view permitted to strain clear water in a strainer since it is made for this — see there the reason; and for Magen Avraham therefore Ze'eira's statement argues on what we rule that its laundering is its washing — see Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger there — very strained:

(ד) And even in sudarim, etc., in clear — It implies he holds that in clear liquids even water is permitted in sudarim; and Gra wrote the reason: he holds we do not say its laundering is its washing where he does not intend for laundry; and that we are not concerned lest he squeeze deals with sudarim designated for this [as he wrote end of siman 302, see there]; and so Ra'avad on the Mishnah that they strain wine in sudarim — deals with sudarim designated for this; and it implies if not designated, even wine forbidden; but for halacha it appears many poskim and almost all that in wine, even in sudarim not designated, we are not concerned lest he squeeze; therefore Shulchan Aruch was not concerned for his view and ruled anonymously that in sudarim permitted — even not designated, as Beit Yosef proves:

(ה) And wine from the press as long as it ferments — See Mishna Berurah; and this is Rashi's and Rambam's explanation: the reason of the winepress is that then the lees have not yet separated well from the wine and all the wine is one body — therefore selecting does not apply even if he strains; therefore they permitted in a sudar. Taz wrote: the same for vinegar even if thick and turbid, for people's manner is to drink it without straining — therefore its law is like wine from the press and permitted to strain; but if very thick such that it is not fit to drink at all — forbidden to strain even in a sudar due to selecting. And this is plain vinegar; but vinegar that became wormy [and worms are visible against the sun] — forbidden to strain even on a weekday [so Chochmat Adam siman 38]:
