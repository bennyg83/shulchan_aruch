(א) Wine. (See Tosafos Shabbos) And if there is flour in it — permitted to strain (Tosefta and so Rashi):

(ב) On account of libun. But a filter is made for that and he is not concerned for its whitening (Tur); and requires further study, since we say regarding a garment its soaking is its laundering — if so what difference whether made for that or not? And in Semag he wrote thus: and on account of squeezing there is no decree, for behold the filter is made for that and he is not particular about whitening, as we say chapter Beit HaYotzer (and in siman 320 seif 15); but in a cloth with water — forbidden, etc., end of his words. And so in Sefer HaTerumah; and it appears to me for this Tur intended, who holds as Beit Yosef stated siman 302 in name of Tosafos and Rosh that when there is no soiling upon it — it is not laundering, as is stated there in Shulchan Aruch, and so siman 16; except forbidden lest he squeeze and it becomes laundering; therefore in something made for that we do not decree; and what is written in siman 334 that forbidden to put water — that too is lest he squeeze and it becomes laundering; but in other liquids their squeezing is not forbidden except as a toldah of threshing because they do not whiten the liquids, therefore we do not decree lest he squeeze as Tosafos end of chapter 4, Semag, and Maor chapter 4 of Beitza wrote; but for the one who says even when there is no soiling upon it we say its soaking is its laundering — permitted to strain wine specifically, for this is not laundering but soiling, as Maggid Mishneh wrote in chapter 22, see there, and siman 302 seif 9 what he wrote, and siman 507 seif 10:

(ג) And for Rambam, etc., even in cloths, etc., not in cloudy. And requires further study, for in cloudy per the first view of early authorities it is forbidden; and it appears for the first view slightly cloudy are permitted in a cloth since one can drink it anyway even though most people do not drink them, as Rav Hai Gaon wrote in name of Ran and Tur, and Maggid Mishneh in name of Rashba, and commentaries; but for Rambam — forbidden.
