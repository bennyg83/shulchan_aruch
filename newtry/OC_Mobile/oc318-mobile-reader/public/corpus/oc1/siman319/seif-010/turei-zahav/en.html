(א) Or water that is clear. Ran explained: meaning that most people are not particular about this:

(ב) And wine and other beverages. It is difficult from Ran's words that Beit Yosef brought in siman 320 that wine whitens, and he brings proof from the Gemara that they expounded "for eating and not for laundering" — requires study; and see there siman 320:

(ג) And if they are turbid. Meaning completely turbid such that one cannot drink it thus at all — forbidden even in a sudar; and permission in a sudar is only somewhat turbid, and it is possible one drinks it thus; nevertheless most people do not drink it thus — so Beit Yosef in the name of Ran:

(ד) And for Rambam in a strainer forbidden. It is difficult — behold Ze'eira said one may put clear wine into the strainer; and Beit Yosef explained that this strainer of Ze'eira is not a strainer literally — he says "something that guards" — meaning in a sudar or funnel; and this is strained; whence for Rambam to remove the matter from its plain sense? One must say Rambam explains "clear" that Ze'eira mentions as very clear with no lees at all, and he does not guard it except for spices or something that fell there — in this alone permitted in a strainer:

(ה) And wine from the press. It appears to me that in vinegar, which people also use even if thick — one should not forbid in a sudar unless it is very thick such that it is not fit at all to use without straining:
