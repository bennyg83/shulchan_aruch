(ג) Turbid. For it is impossible to drink it thus at all — Beit Yosef:

(ד) Not in turbid. And requires study, for in turbid even per the first view forbidden. And it appears to me that per the first view, somewhat turbid is permitted in a sudar since it is possible to drink it otherwise, even though most people do not drink them — as Beit Yosef wrote; but for Rambam forbidden. A beverage that was crushed erev Shabbat — permitted to strain it since they can drink it without straining — Hagahat Semak, Magen Avraham:
