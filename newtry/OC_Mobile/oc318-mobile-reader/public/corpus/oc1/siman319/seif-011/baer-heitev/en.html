Change. And in a sudar, a change suffices that he does not make hollows — Ran. But regarding an ohel there is none since it is not wide; and Taz wrote that if he covers the entire vessel with a sudar he should be careful that it not be a tefach high — see siman 315 seif 13. And Magen Avraham wrote: it appears to me that in that vessel made like a nafah with which they strain — its law is like a strainer since it is designated for this — see seif 10.
