And whatever he clarifies for the need of that meal, etc. — meaning he clarifies before the meal for the need of that meal; and Beit Yosef wrote in the name of R' Yeruham: if he clarifies after a meal — whatever he clarifies for the need of another meal that same day is called immediately; and if while in the meal he clarifies — he can clarify only for the need of that meal alone; and likewise if he clarifies before a meal he must eat them within the first meal; and if he set some aside until after he rises from his meal — it is not called immediately and he is liable; except that in the name of Mordechai it is written: specifically to begin and eat after his clarifying — permitted; but to eat after an hour becomes like one who clarifies for storage and is forbidden; and he wrote that so appears from Rambam's words like Mordechai's words — see there; and so is his view here in Shulchan Aruch, as written below or 35. And likewise Dress seif 2: one may not clarify except within the meal or close to it before he eats; and likewise Magen Avraham siman 321 s.k. 15; Rabbi Zalman or 3; Chayei Adam general 16 or 2 and or 3. And Rabbi Zalman wrote there: whatever is close to the meal — permitted to clarify for the need of that entire meal even though it lasts some time; and if something remained from his clarifying until after the meal — it is nothing, since he clarified in permitted status — provided he not act deceitfully — end of his words; and likewise Rav Ben Avraham chapter Beshalach or 1: if he clarifies for a meal he begins within less than an hour — permitted; and even though the meal at which he eats lasts many hours — permitted:
