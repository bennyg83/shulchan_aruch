(ב) And it appears for this Tur intended, etc., except forbidden lest he squeeze. And what is forbidden in a cloth on account of libun — the explanation is not through putting water it becomes libun, for it deals with a clean cloth and we do not say its soaking is its laundering according to Tur; rather because he is particular about the cloth to clean it we are concerned lest he squeeze to clean it and thereby be liable on account of libun:

(ג) And what is written, etc., forbidden to put water — he wanted to say a tallit that fire caught on one side — he should not put water on its other side; and Tur wrote the reason on account of laundering, that is, also that it come to laundering through squeezing it:

(ד) But in other liquids, etc., as is written end of chapter 14 that there is no manner to squeeze a garment for the need of liquid in it, end quote:
