The simple person writing said — I have thoughts in Shabbos 138a: one who clarifies is liable for chatas; and challenges R' Eliezer: permitted, etc. — what difficulty; the Mishnah is not regarding lees fit under duress — for R' Eliezer permitted in strainer; and for our master exempt but forbidden; and lees not fit at all in strainer liable for chatas. And for Rambam if we say the majority drink — nevertheless strainer liable for chatas and in cloth permitted in such a case he properly challenged; and Avodat HaKodesh from this; and one need not extend. And in the Mishnah 139 — water on lees to rinse them — Ra'avad explained their wine will flow — it implies there is wine in the lees; and impossible to flow because they are very thick — he puts water and the water flows with the wine; and difficult, for it causes borer; and possibly for this reason Dress explained he wants the lees without flour (for the way is lees without wine — they put flour on lees); and such is not borer — refuse from refuse; and although Taz wrote sign 12 stew is liable because of borer — one may say there the coarse parts are not fit for him at all — like clarifying refuse from the good in it; unlike flour with lees that one can use with flour; Avodat HaKodesh; and what he wrote "and there, in the Gemara the minor ones," etc. — but murky not challenged from Rashbag — behold Rashbag disputes the Mishnah in Chullin and the Mishnah of wine in cloths — behold strainer; one may say Rashbag deals where one can drink under duress; and therefore he said tarud (a person troubled and mixed in thoughts, mind not clear) — a barrel with lees: a whole barrel with a little of its lees fit under duress permitted in strainer; and the Mishnah beginning Tolim: not under duress and liable for chatas; behold under duress permitted (and what Maggid Mishneh wrote chapter 1 from the Talmud under duress shevut — in the conclusion, etc.); unlike the minor ones — specifically clear; murky under duress in strainer forbidden — difficult from wine; and answered: between presses the majority drink thus; and for Rambam this was difficult — therefore he explains strainer of the minor ones and Rashbag as cloths; and challenges murky in cloth forbidden from Rashbag; and answers between presses; and on this and one need not extend.
