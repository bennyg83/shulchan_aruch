Paragraph 2: From, etc.; the entire square of the arc. This means that a city shaped like an arc is certainly assigned corners on the arc side and squared according to the directions of the world, as stated in se'ifim 2 and 3. Its residents may therefore walk throughout that square and around the curve of the arc; they may also walk through the houses to the ends of the arc and measure two thousand amos from there, just like residents at the ends of the arc. The statement that the boundary is measured only from the arc means that if they wish to leave their homes by walking through the empty area of the arc toward the chord opposite it, rather than by way of the houses, they measure from the arc.
