7) [Seif 3] When he squares it, etc. — meaning it was round or a house that narrows and widens and he needed to square it — we give him advice to square it as a world-square; even though if it were originally square and not a world-square we measure only from the square he has, but since he wants to square it, it is good to square it as a world-square — R' Yehonatan on Rif chapter 5 of Eruvin. If they marked the square not as a world-square and the townspeople agreed to this, an individual may not separate from the community to say I will consider it as I wish — Noda BiYehudah Mahadura Tinyana siman 51, see there; they cited Shaarei Teshuvah.
