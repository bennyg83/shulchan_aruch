7) [Se'if 3] When one squares it, etc. If the city was circular, or if one house projected and another was recessed, so that it needed to be squared, the Sages advised that it be squared according to the directions of the world. If it had originally been square but its square was not aligned with the directions of the world, the techum would be measured only from the existing square. Since it now requires squaring, however, it is preferable to square it according to the directions of the world. Rabbeinu Yehonasan on the Rif, chapter 5 of Eruvin. If the square was marked without aligning it to the directions of the world and the city's residents agreed to that alignment, an individual may not separate from the community and say, 'I will calculate my own techum as I wish.' Noda BiYehudah, second edition, siman 51. See there. Shaarei Teshuvah cites his words.
8) There. According to the directions of the world. This means positioning its northern side toward due north and its southern side toward due south, rather than diagonally relative to the directions of the world. Gemara, Eruvin 56a, and Rashi there.
