Paragraph 9: Their law, etc.; this requires further study, for, etc. His statement that their law is like that of a city shaped as an arc must mean that the two houses may not be more than four thousand amos apart, just as the two ends of an arc may not be more than four thousand amos apart. Magen Avraham objects that if the distance between the arc and the chord is two thousand amos, the boundary is measured from the chord even when the two ends of the arc are more than four thousand amos apart. Here, since each of the two houses is within seventy amos and a fraction, the distance between the arc and chord is only seventy amos and a fraction. The boundary should therefore be measured from the chord even if the two houses are more than four thousand amos apart.
It appears to me that all the more so, etc. The correct text is: if the city is longer than four thousand amos.
