A city that sits on the bank of a river that is dry most of the time, and people use it only when it is filled with water during rainy season: If there is a platform of four cubits width on the bank of the river, people can stand on it and use the river. The river is considered part of the city, and it is measured two thousand cubits from the second bank of the river. The entire river is considered part of the city because of the platform built on its side. If there is no platform, only the area of the houses adjacent to the river is measured, and the river is measured from the thousands of cubits of theirs.
