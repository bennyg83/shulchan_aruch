9) [Se'if 4] It is viewed as though it were entirely wide. When measuring the techum on the narrow side, one does not measure from the wall. Instead, it is viewed as though a line extends from the wide side to the narrow side. Measurement begins from the position of that line, as though the second side were as wide as the first. Beis Yosef.
