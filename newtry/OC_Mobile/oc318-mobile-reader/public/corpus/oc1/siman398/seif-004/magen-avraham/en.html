From the bow. Rabbi HaMeiri wrote: those who established Shabbat in the bow may walk the entire square of the bow and its arc until the ends of the bow, and from there onward two thousand amot. End quote. But by way of the chord they are forbidden to enter; Radbaz, part 1, siman 107. So too it is in the Gemara, that if he wants, he can return and come by way of the houses.
From the place. That became shortened, or where less than two thousand amot remain from the bow to there. Tur.
From the chord. See what I wrote in seif 8, that this applies specifically to the outer one, but not to the middle one. It requires study why the Tur wrote without qualification.
