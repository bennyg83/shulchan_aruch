And some say from the place that is shortened — see Tur that his view is even in a place that was not shortened, any place from which to the roof of the arch is only two thousand, that entire place is considered as if filled with houses and we measure the two thousand of the techum from there outward; Magen Avraham copied this. But the language of the Gemara is very forced according to this, for it says that if it is wide in the permitted place more than four thousand, we measure from the arch; according to his words it should have said from a distance of two thousand from the arch. Also from Rashi it appears the measurement must be from the head of the arch, and so Me'or HaGolah and Sefer HaItim cited in Beit Yosef (and what Rama ruled from the place that is shortened — see Ritva who establishes this even according to Rashi; also what the Gemara says from the arch). What Beit Yosef wrote that Tur learned this from what Tosafot and Rosh were lenient regarding when there is not more than two thousand between the excess and the arch — likewise when there is more than two thousand, in any case we measure from a place within two thousand; and as we are lenient when shortened, one may distinguish simply; and especially since this law itself when there is not more than two thousand between excess and arch cited in Rama to be lenient is not a clear law at all — see Nehar Shalom and Maamar Mordechai and Gaon Yaakov who wrote that Rif and Rambam do not hold this since they were silent and did not distinguish; and in Rashba and Ritva in their novellae they wrote explicitly the halachah is not like Tosafot here. Even though one may rely on the Poskim Rama brought because halachah follows the lenient in eruv at least — nevertheless to rely on this additional leniency Tur found to learn from this is very difficult; it appears for this reason Rama and Levush omitted this law in copying Tur's words; so too Chayei Adam omitted this law.
