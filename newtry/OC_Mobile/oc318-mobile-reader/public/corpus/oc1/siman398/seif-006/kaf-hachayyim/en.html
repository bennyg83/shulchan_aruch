6) [Se'if 2] It is squared, etc. If the city is triangular, the narrow side is viewed as though it were filled with houses to the same extent as the wide side. A straight line is drawn, and the two thousand amos are measured from that line. Similarly, if its edge is irregular, every empty indentation is viewed as though it were filled with houses, a straight line is drawn, and the two thousand amos are measured from it.
