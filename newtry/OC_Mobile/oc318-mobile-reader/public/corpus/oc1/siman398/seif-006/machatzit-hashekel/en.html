(note 6) Regarding four by four, etc. — even though it is long, it is not called a house; in tractate Sukkah 3a: a house that does not have four by four on four by four is exempt from mezuzah, etc., and one does not place an eruv in it and one does not make it an extension, etc. Regarding mezuzah, Rambam's view is if it is long and narrow, if it has enough to square four by four in fragments it is obligated in mezuzah; and Rabbeinu Yerucham Beit Din siman 286 anonymously like him; if so, likewise according to him in all matters mentioned in that baraita the law is so; so too Magen Avraham in siman 366 note 6 in name of Ralbag regarding a house where one places the eruv — it suffices if it has enough to square four by four; however Magen Avraham there wrote on this and see Yoreh Deah siman 286 seif 13 — his intention is to disagree, for Shach wrote there one must be stringent like Rosh who requires four by four squared; if so, likewise here one must be stringent that specifically squared is required to make it an extension, for their laws are equal as above. What Magen Avraham concluded see siman 345 is a different matter — meaning there Magen Avraham wrote reshut ha-yachid, and so end of siman, for that which four handbreadths by four cubits enclosed by a partition is called reshut ha-yachid requires specifically that the hollow space be four cubits by four cubits without the thickness of the partitions; he inferred from end of siman from what is written and there is not four between them, etc. — implying the hollow should be four; if so, likewise what is written and there is four by four in them implies the hollow should be four by four without thickness of partitions.
