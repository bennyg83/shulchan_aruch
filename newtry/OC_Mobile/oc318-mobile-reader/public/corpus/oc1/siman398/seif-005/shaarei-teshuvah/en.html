Avodat HaGolah; and Dagul MeRevavah wrote that a watchman's booth need not be four amot by four as written in Sukkah 3, see Rashi there who implies a watchman's booth since it is only for sleeping one night and for one person and made for temporary dwelling does not require four amot; nevertheless it must actually be made for the need of fields or fruits, and so is proven in Sukkah — for a house without four by four, if it were that it helps generally it would be difficult, for a house even though not fit for its purpose would be like a watchman's booth; therefore one who wants to make booths every seventy amot to expand the techum, and those booths are made only for this and not to guard fields or fruits, are nothing; this is very simple in my view.
