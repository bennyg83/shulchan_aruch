(1) "Long and narrow." Its width is less than its length. (2) "And not aligned with the square of the earth." Its north side does not face true north and its south side true south; rather, it stands diagonally. (3) "4 equal corners." This excludes a city that is wider on 1 side and narrower on the other, which does not have equal corners and is not a true square. It is squared as explained in seif 4. (4) "As it is." If it is long and narrow, we do not enlarge its width to equal its length. Likewise, if it is not aligned with the cardinal directions, we do not enlarge it in order to realign its square with the cardinal directions, unlike the case in seif 3.
