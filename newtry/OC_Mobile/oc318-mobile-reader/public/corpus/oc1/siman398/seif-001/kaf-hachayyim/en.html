1) [Se'if 1] One who comes to measure the two thousand amos of a city's techum, etc. A person may walk throughout the entire city on Shabbos, even if it is as large as Nineveh, whether or not it is surrounded by a wall. The person may also walk two thousand amos outside the city in every direction, squared like a square tablet, thereby gaining the corners. Rambam, chapter 27, halachah 2. See below, siman 399, se'if 10. If the city has been abandoned by its inhabitants, however, and it is not surrounded by a wall, one may not walk throughout the entire city, since one did not establish Shabbos residence within the space of partitions. Rashi on Eruvin 61b. This is also implied at the end of siman 396. Tosefes Shabbos, beginning of this siman.
