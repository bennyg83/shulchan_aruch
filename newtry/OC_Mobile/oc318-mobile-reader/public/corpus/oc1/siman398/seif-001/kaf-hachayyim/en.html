1) [Se'if 1] One who comes to measure the two thousand amos of a city's techum, etc. A person may walk throughout the entire city on Shabbos, even if it is as large as Nineveh, whether or not it is surrounded by a wall. The person may also walk two thousand amos outside the city in every direction, squared like a square tablet, thereby gaining the corners. Rambam, chapter 27, halachah 2. See below, siman 399, se'if 10. If the city has been abandoned by its inhabitants, however, and it is not surrounded by a wall, one may not walk throughout the entire city, since one did not establish Shabbos residence within the space of partitions. Rashi on Eruvin 61b. This is also implied at the end of siman 396. Tosefes Shabbos, beginning of this siman.
2) There. Long and narrow. This means that its width is less than its length. Magen Avraham, subsection 1.
3) There. And it is not aligned with the directions of the world. Its northern side does not face due north and its southern side does not face due south; rather, the city stands diagonally. Rashi on Eruvin 55a, cited by Beis Yosef.
4) There. Since it has equal corners. This excludes a city that is wide on one side and narrow on the other, which is discussed in se'if 4. See there.
5) There. It is left as it is, etc. For a city that is long and narrow, its width is not extended to equal its length. When a city is not squared with the directions of the world, it is not expanded in order to align its square with those directions. Rather, it is left as it is and the techum is measured accordingly. Gemara and Rashi there, cited by Beis Yosef.
