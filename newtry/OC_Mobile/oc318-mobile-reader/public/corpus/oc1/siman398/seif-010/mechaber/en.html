Those who dwell in huts, meaning those who dwell in tents made of wood and reeds, do not have the law of a city. Therefore, they are not measured in thousands of cubits from the outskirts of the city, but rather from the threshold of their tents. If they have three courtyards of two houses each, made of stones or beams, they are all considered permanent structures, and they are measured like any other city, with a city boundary of a thousand cubits in every direction. ((However, if they have a kind of enclosure of a wall or ditch that is ten cubits high around their homes).
