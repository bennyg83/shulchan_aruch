And one does not bless upon Keriat Shema etc. — even for one who reads while it is still day and fulfills with bedtime Keriat Shema, nevertheless one does not bless "who sanctified us" to read Shema, for the blessings themselves were not enacted upon Keriat Shema, as is written several times in the name of the Rashba; and all the more for protection etc. that one does not bless. And in truth a reason must be given why we do not bless as on every positive mitzvah; and it is possible that in the general "who sanctified us" upon Torah, Keriat Shema is included in the general category of Torah.
