(א) Before Keriat Shema one should say: Master of the universe, behold I forgive and pardon everyone who angered or provoked me, or who sinned against me, whether under compulsion or willingly, whether unwittingly or intentionally, whether in this incarnation or in another incarnation; and let no person be punished on my account, neither above nor below, neither in this world nor in the world to come. And may it be Your will, Heaven my Heaven and Heaven of my fathers, that You assist me that I not sin again, and what I have sinned before You erase in Your abundant mercies, but not through afflictions and evil illnesses. May the words of my mouth be acceptable etc., end of siman 238. And this is according to what is written in Megillah 28a, that Mar Zutra when he would ascend to his bed would say: I forgive everyone who pained me, and afterward he would say Hashkivenu, our Father, in peace etc. until "and protect us." And on Shabbat and Yom Tov one should not say "Master of the universe" and "may it be Your will," but rather begin directly with Hashkivenu.

(ב) And behold I arrange with divine help the order of Keriat Shema upon the bed according to what the rabbi z"l wrote in Sha'ar HaKavanot and in the siddur of the Rashash z"l.

(ג) First one should say "for the sake of the unification" etc., "behold I come to fulfill the mitzvah of Keriat Shema upon the bed to repair its root in the supernal place" etc., "and may the pleasantness" etc.

(ד) And "may it be Your will" etc. — [kabbalistic kavanot paragraph — full translation of the Hebrew regarding souls, kelipot, 248 words, etc., preserving all technical terms:] And may it be Your will before You, Heaven my Heaven and Heaven of my fathers, that through the power of the segulah of the blessing of the One Who causes sleep and Keriat Shema and the verses that one says and all their particulars and the intention that one intends in them, all the souls, spirits, and neshamot of the drops and seed that went forth from me and from all Your people Israel not in the place of a mitzvah should ascend from within the kelipot to the foundation of the kingdom of Atzilut, and to draw down great abundance with the three havayot of the milui of yudin that ascend, 216, from Da'at of Atik to the eighth mazal of Arich Anpin and from there to Da'at of Abba and from there to its yesod and from there yesod of Imma to establish and repair the vessel of mayin nukvin of hers; and to continue from yesod of Ze'ir Anpin to yesod of Malchut, square of the simple havayah and square of the havayah of milui of the hei to establish and repair the vessel of mayin nukvin of hers; and to draw abundance and great illumination from the upper 216, which is the three havayot of the milui of yudin that are in the gematria of 226 in yesod of Imma to the three mochin of Malchut and to yesod within her to destroy and kill the supernal sword of the kelipah that cuts and severs the holy souls; and to bring down illumination from the lower 216, which is the square of the simple havayah and the square of the havayah of milui of the hei, to kill the dog in the kelipah that corresponds to yesod of Malchut Kadisha of Ze'ir Anpin; and to bring down great illumination from the two 216, upper and lower, to destroy and kill and annihilate all the unclean bodies of the kelipot and the destroyers, children of falsehood, seed of deceit, whom in our sins we raised and exalted, whether by corrupting seed on the ground or those that were not placed on the belly. Please, Heaven, gird Your sword and roar like a lion against them and bring out their bile from their mouths, and pursue, overtake, and deliver, and let none of them be thrust away; and those bodies cast them and a wind carry them and let them be as though they had not been. May they be as chaff before the wind and the angel of the Heaven pursue them. And all the drops and sparks of holiness in them should ascend with the roots of my soul, spirit, and neshamah into the foundation of Malchut Kadisha of Ze'ir Anpin and raise mayin nukvin there and become impregnated from them and bring them to this world. And through the segulah of the 248 words in Keriat Shema that I will read, the 248 limbs of Tiferet and Malchut should be repaired and become 248 limbs for every soul from the souls of the drops that went forth from me and from all Your people the house of Israel, and they should ascend into mem of le'olam va'ed that is within yesod of Malchut Kadisha. And may the pleasantness etc.; and see below letter dalet.

(ה) And afterward one says: Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who causes the bonds of sleep to fall upon my eyes and slumber upon my eyelids, and illuminates the pupil of the eye. And afterward one says the three paragraphs of Keriat Shema with great intention, and repeats "the Heaven your Heaven is true" in order to complete 248 words corresponding to the 248 limbs of a person. And afterward one says: "The pious rejoice in honor; they sing aloud upon their beds. Exaltations of Heaven are in their throats and a two-edged sword in their hands." And afterward one says: "Behold, the bed of Solomon" etc., "all of them grasping swords" etc., "from fear in the nights"; and one repeats them three times (and they contain twenty words, and three times makes sixty words, and the reason is a secret). And afterward one says the three verses of the priestly blessing: "May the Heaven bless you" etc., "May the Heaven shine" etc., "May the Heaven lift" etc., which contain sixty letters. And afterward one says "He who dwells in the covert of the Most High" etc. until "for You, Heaven, are my refuge," which contains sixty words. (And if it is a weekday one says here the confession in alef-bet order. And afterward one says: And may it be Your will, Heaven my Heaven and Heaven of my fathers, that if I sinned before You with the letter yud of Havayah and the letter alef of Adonai, it should be considered before You as if I were stoned by the court through the letter alef of Adonai. And if I sinned before You with the first hei of Havayah and the letter dalet of Adonai, it should be considered before You as if I were burned by the court through the letter dalet of Adonai. And if I sinned before You with the vav of Havayah and the letter nun of Adonai, it should be considered before You as if I were killed by the court through the letter nun of Adonai. And if I sinned before You with the final hei of Havayah and the letter yud of Adonai, it should be considered before You as if I were strangled by the court through the letter yud of Adonai, and appease me in Your abundant mercies.) And afterward one says Ana bekhoach. And before saying "Blessed be the name" etc., if it is the first night of the week one repeats the verse Ana bekhoach three times; if it is the second night one repeats "Accept, O Heaven" etc. three times; if it is the third night "Please, O strong One" etc.; and on Shabbat night "Our outcry, accept" etc.; and afterward one says "Blessed be the name" (and the three times on the first occasion one should intend to raise the aspect of the soul from Asiyah to Yetzirah, and on the second time the soul and spirit from Yetzirah to Beriah, and on the third time the soul, spirit, and neshamah from Beriah to Atzilut; and also each time one says the verse "with two he will cover his face and with two he will cover his feet and with two he will fly" — on the first time the soul flies from Asiyah to Yetzirah, on the second the soul and spirit from Yetzirah to Beriah, etc.). And afterward one says the verse "Into Your hand I entrust my spirit" etc. And afterward one repeats: "Accept, O Heaven, the prayer of Your people Israel when they pray" — and intend the initials of the name with a shva vowel thus: kera satan — for with this name one tears the power of Satan so that he not bring one to keri. And afterward intend the name Elokim yishmereni. And return to intend with the name Abag Yatatz until one sleeps.
