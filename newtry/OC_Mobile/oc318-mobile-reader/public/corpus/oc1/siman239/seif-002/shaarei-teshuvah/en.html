The One Who causes sleep — Av Beit HaTefillah, and it is written in Rabbeinu Yonah in the name of a responsum of Lechem Todah that there are those who wished to say that per the secret, if one was awake until after midnight and then goes to lie down, he does not bless the One Who causes sleep — and this is not true; rather one must bless whenever one goes to sleep — see there.
