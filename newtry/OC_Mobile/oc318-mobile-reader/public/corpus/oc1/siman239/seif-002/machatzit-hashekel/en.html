(א) (Seif kaf bet) And he blesses etc. — one says after Shema, for thus they ordered in Shulchan Aruch: one reads etc. Shema and blesses the One Who causes sleep etc., and so is the essential law as the reason will be explained in this seif kaf zayin.

(ב) And likewise Tosafot at the beginning of Berakhot to reject Rashi's explanation that he wrote to justify the custom that they read Keriat Shema while it is still day, relying on Keriat Shema upon the bed. And they challenged: this Keriat Shema upon the bed is only for protection and one does not fulfill one's obligation with it; and proof that it is for protection is that we say a Torah scholar need not — and if a Torah scholar also fulfills with it, it must be that the reason is protection and a Torah scholar's learning that is fixed in his mouth protects him.

(ג) He holds that one says Shema at the end, and so too this proof in the Yerushalmi — for otherwise it should have said he said "He who dwells in secret" and returned and said, or the other verses that he repeated; therefore since he read Shema at the end, therefore he returned and read Shema.

(ד) R' Yehuda ben Levi holds etc., for we say in Shevuot 15: what is the song of afflictions? "He who dwells in secret" etc. and "How abundant are my foes" etc.; and we say upon it: R' Yehuda ben Levi would read these verses and sleep — implying that adjacent to sleep he would read these verses "He who dwells" etc.; therefore he said them at the end.

(ה) That he wrote "one reads and returns and reads" — and this is like R' Zeira in the Yerushalmi. And see above siman 61 seif 10 that one must be careful not to say one verse, namely Shema, twice, but rather the rest of the parasha (and see Levushei Srad), for the halacha is like R' Yehuda ben Levi even against R' Yochanan.

(ו) That it is "the community of Israel." And in Eliyah Rabbah he disagrees — as if women do not require protection.

(ז) That it is a private domain. And in Eliyah Rabbah he disagrees — do women not need protection?
