(א) First parasha. Rabbeinu Yonah wrote that one should say any Keriat Shema that is 248 words to preserve his 248 limbs, and so too the Ari z"l; and see above siman 235 seif kaf bet as he wrote.

(ב) The One Who causes sleep. It implies that the blessing of the One Who causes sleep one says after Shema so that the blessing should be adjacent to sleep, and so is the essential law. Magen Avraham there.

(ג) Sleep immediately. And if he needs to use his bed he should wash his hands and afterward read, and at minimum he should say the blessing of the One Who causes sleep and Shema after the use (Shelah and likewise in Knesset HaGedolah).

(ד) Lying down. And this is specifically regarding obligatory Keriat Shema; but if he already read after tefillah and now reads upon his bed for protection, it is permitted to read while lying, for R' Zeira read and repeated and read until he fell into his sleep. Magen Avraham.

(ה) Return and read. And it appears to me that the same applies if he is thinking in words of Torah (Maharshal). Magen Avraham.

(ו) In secret. One who sleeps alone in a house — a demon seizes him (Shabbat 151b), and "house" means a room (Maharil). These require guarding from harmful forces: a sick person, a woman in childbirth, a groom, a bride, a mourner, and a Torah scholar at night (Berakhot 4a). There are those who practice saying the confession "before I lie down in bed" before sleep, and it appears to me that this is not correct, for it is stated in Berakhot that one should not say "if I die let my death be an atonement" lest one open one's mouth to Satan; therefore one should not say it, but rather one should search through one's deeds that one did all day, and if one finds that one committed a transgression one should confess upon it and accept upon oneself not to do it again, and especially in common transgressions such as flattery, lies, mockery, lashon hara — these require extra scrutiny. And fortunate is one who is accustomed to say the order of Keriat Shema of the Ari z"l.
