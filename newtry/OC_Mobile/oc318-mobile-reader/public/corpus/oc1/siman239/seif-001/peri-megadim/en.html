He recites, etc. In siman 235, according to the view that one recites Krias Shema early, while it is still daytime, he must intend to fulfill his obligation through the Krias Shema recited at bedtime. It is proper to recite two paragraphs. See Taz there, seif katan 1. Regarding the night of Pesach, see there; with Hashem’s help it will be explained in the laws of Pesach. Women should also recite Krias Shema at bedtime, since it is recited for protection. Are women any less in need of protection than men? See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 4, and there in seif katan 3. With Hashem’s help, this will be further explained in Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. There is no need to elaborate here.
