In Shulchan Aruch seif 1 in the gloss "and one does not bless upon bedtime Keriat Shema" — see in Avnei Tzedek who wrote: the explanation of "who sanctified us regarding Keriat Shema" one does not bless — see there. And according to his words it is explained that one should not say "who sanctified us" upon bedtime Keriat Shema; and the reason is that this Keriat Shema of now is not to fulfill but for protection, and the Keriat Shema indicated in it he already fulfilled when he read with its blessings; and unlike Rav Amram whom the Tur brought siman 235 who wrote that the essential reading one fulfills with what one reads before his bed, and because of this he wrote that one must bless "Blessed are You" etc. And Rabbeinu Tam wrote: certainly that of the synagogue is essential etc.; and Beit Yosef here also wrote in the name of Tosafot that one does not bless "who sanctified us" upon Keriat Shema before his bed etc.; and it is possible the word "of Ma'ariv" is a scribal error, and so in Levush it is absent; and see in responsum siman 319.
