1) [Seif 1] He recites the first paragraph at his bed, etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes based upon the Midrash that one should recite all of Krias Shema, which contains 248 words, to protect his 248 limbs. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 53a, likewise writes that only the early pious people needed to recite merely the first verse. Regarding us, however, the later generations, if only we could specify all the details of the intentions and the unification so that we might cause the supernal union. Therefore, we recite the entire order of Krias Shema; see there.
2) Before Krias Shema, he should say: Master of the universe, I hereby forgive and pardon everyone who angered or provoked me, or sinned against me, whether under compulsion or willingly, unintentionally or intentionally, in this incarnation or another incarnation. Let no person be punished because of me, neither above nor below, neither in this world nor in the World to Come. May it be Your will, Hashem, our God and God of our ancestors, that You assist me so that I sin no more. Erase what I have sinned before You in Your abundant mercy, but not through suffering or severe illnesses. May the words of my mouth find favor, etc. Shulchan Gavoah, siman 238. This follows Megillah 28a, which says that when Mar Zutra ascended to his bed he would say, 'I forgive anyone who distressed me.' Afterward, he should say, 'Lay us down, our Father, in peace,' etc., through, 'and protect us.' On Shabbos and Yom Tov, he should not say, 'Master of the universe,' or, 'May it be Your will,' but should begin, 'Lay us down.'
2) I shall now arrange, with the help of Heaven, the order of Krias Shema al HaMitah according to what the Rav, of blessed memory, writes in Shaar HaKavanos and in the siddur of Rashash, of blessed memory.
2) First he should say the Leshem Yichud, etc.: I hereby come to fulfill the mitzvah of Krias Shema al HaMitah, to rectify its root in the supernal place. May the pleasantness, etc.
2) May it be Your will, Hashem, our God and God of our ancestors, that through the power and spiritual quality of the blessing HaMapil, Krias Shema, the verses that I recite, all their details, and the intention I direct within them, all the nefashos, ruchos, and neshamos of the sparks of seminal emissions and seed that emerged from me and from all Your people Yisrael outside the context of a mitzvah should ascend from among the husks to Yesod of Malchus of Atzilus. May great abundance descend with the three spellings of Havayah filled with letters yud, whose numerical value is 216, from Daas of Atik to the eighth mazal of Arich Anpin, from there to Daas of Abba, from there to his Yesod, and from there to Yesod of Imma, to establish and rectify her vessel of feminine waters. May the squared form of the simple Havayah and the squared form of Havayah filled with letters heh be drawn from Yesod of Zeir Anpin to Yesod of Malchus, to establish and rectify her vessel of feminine waters. May great abundance and illumination be drawn from the upper 216, which is the three spellings of Havayah filled with letters yud, whose numerical value is 626 and which are in Yesod of Imma, to the three mochin of Malchus and her Yesod, to destroy and kill the supernal sword of the husks that cuts and severs the holy souls. May illumination descend from the lower 216, which is the squared form of the simple Havayah and the squared form of Havayah filled with letters heh, to kill the dog among the husks that stands opposite Yesod of the holy Malchus of Zeir Anpin. May great illumination descend from both the upper and lower 216 to destroy, kill, and eliminate all the impure bodies of the husks and the destructive beings, children of rebellion and offspring of falsehood, which through our sins we raised and exalted as children, whether by wasting seed upon the earth or by seed that we did not retain in the womb. Please, Hashem, gird Your sword and roar against them like a lion. Remove what they swallowed from their mouths; surely overtake and surely rescue, so that no one be banished from Him. Scatter those bodies; let the wind carry them away, and let them be as though they never were. Let them be like chaff before the wind, and let an angel of Hashem pursue them. May all the drops and sparks of holiness within them ascend with the roots of my nefesh, ruach, and neshamah into Yesod of the holy Malchus of Zeir Anpin, raise feminine waters there, become conceived within it, and be brought into this world. Through the spiritual quality of the 248 words of Krias Shema that I shall recite, may the 248 limbs of Tiferes and Malchus be rectified, and may 248 limbs be rectified and formed for every soul among the souls produced by seminal emission and wasted seed that emerged from me and from all Your people, the House of Yisrael. May they ascend into the final mem of the words, 'forever and ever,' which is within Yesod of the holy Malchus. May the pleasantness, etc. See below, paragraph 4.
2) Afterward, he should say: Blessed are You, Hashem, our God, King of the universe, Who casts the bonds of sleep upon my eyes and slumber upon my eyelids, and illuminates the pupil of the eye. Afterward, he should recite the three paragraphs of Krias Shema with great intent and repeat, 'Hashem your God is true,' to complete 248 words corresponding to a person's 248 limbs. Afterward, he should say, 'Let the pious exult in honor; let them sing joyfully upon their beds. The exaltations of God are in their throats, and a double-edged sword is in their hands.' Afterward, he should say, 'Behold, it is the bed of Shlomo,' etc., through, 'They all grasp the sword,' etc., and, 'because of terror in the nights.' He should repeat these three times. These verses contain twenty words; reciting them three times produces sixty words, for a mystical reason. Afterward, he should recite the three verses of Birkas Kohanim, 'May Hashem bless you,' etc., 'May Hashem illuminate,' etc., and, 'May Hashem raise,' etc., which contain sixty letters. Afterward, he should recite, 'One who sits in the shelter of the Most High,' etc., through, 'For You, Hashem, are my refuge,' containing sixty words. On a weekday he should here recite confession in alphabetical order. Afterward, he should say: May it be Your will, Hashem, our God and God of our ancestors, that if I sinned before You against the letter yud of Havayah and the letter alef of Adnus, it be considered before You as though I were stoned by a court through the letter alef of Adnus. If I sinned before You against the first letter heh of Havayah and the letter dalet of Adnus, let it be considered before You as though I were burned by a court through the letter dalet of Adnus. If I sinned before You against the letter vav of Havayah and the letter nun of Adnus, let it be considered before You as though I were executed by a court through the letter nun of Adnus. If I sinned before You against the final letter heh of Havayah and the letter yud of Adnus, let it be considered before You as though I were strangled by a court through the letter yud of Adnus, and accept me favorably in Your abundant mercy. Afterward, he should recite Ana BeKoach in its entirety. Before saying, 'Blessed is the Name,' etc., if it is the first night of the week, he should repeat the first verse of Ana BeKoach three times. On the second night, he should repeat the second verse three times; on the third night, the third verse; and on Shabbos night, the sixth verse. Afterward, he should say, 'Blessed is the Name.' During the first repetition, he should intend to elevate the nefesh from Asiyah to Yetzirah. During the second, he should intend to elevate the nefesh and ruach from Yetzirah to Beriah. During the third, he should intend to elevate the nefesh, ruach, and neshamah from Beriah to Atzilus. Each time he recites the verse, he should also intend the verse, 'With two he covers his face, with two he covers his feet, and with two he flies.' Thus, during the first repetition the nefesh flies from Asiyah to Yetzirah; during the second, the nefesh and ruach fly from Yetzirah to Beriah, etc. Afterward, he should recite the verse, 'Into Your hand I entrust my spirit,' etc. He should then repeat the second verse of Ana BeKoach and intend the initials forming the Name pronounced with sheva. He should intend that through this Name he tears the power of Satan so that it not bring him to a seminal emission. Afterward, he should intend, 'God shall protect me.' He should continue intending the first Name of Ana BeKoach until he falls asleep.
