(א) See 61 and "one reads" etc. and "there is no" etc. and "if one read Keriat Shema" etc. Yerushalmi chapter 1: when R' Zeira would go to his bed he would accept upon himself next to R' Yaakov the blind, and R' Zeira would hide among the baskets — we learn from this how he would read Shema and Shema, for he would read and repeat and read until he sank into sleep from him. And why? R' Acha and R' Tachlifa his father-in-law in the name of Rashb"an were angry etc. "and be still, Selah" — and even though there it says a matter of R' Yehuda ben Levi who disagrees, for R' Yehuda ben Levi would read psalms afterward and we hold like R' Yehuda ben Levi, nevertheless we learn from there that it must be adjacent to sleep, and the psalms are like a long Keriat Shema.

(ב) And one does not bless etc. See Tosafot at the beginning of Berakhot s.v. me'ematai and from here etc., and Tosafot on Chullin 105a s.v. mayim etc.
