דין ק"ש על מטתו. ובו ב סעיפים:
The law of Keriat Shema upon one's bed, containing 2 seifim: One reads upon his bed the first parasha of Shema and blesses "Who causes the bonds of sleep to fall upon my eyes" etc.': And one reads Keriat Shema close to one's bed, and one does not eat, drink, or speak after bedtime Keriat Shema except one sleeps immediately, as it says "Speak in your hearts upon your beds and be still, Selah" (Kol Bo, Rokeach, and Rabbeinu Yerucham n.b. ch. 2). And see above siman 63 whether it is permitted to read when lying down. If one read Keriat Shema and cannot sleep immediately, then one repeats and reads several times one after another until one falls into sleep, and one's reading should be close to sleep (Hagahot Maimonides ch. 7). And one does not bless upon bedtime Keriat Shema (Hagahot Maimonides ch. 7 and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot on the chapter Kol HaBasar).} And one says "He who dwells in the covert of the Most High," "How abundant are my foes," "Blessed is Heaven by day, blessed is Heaven by night, blessed is Heaven in our lying down, blessed is Heaven in our rising up," and "May Heaven rebuke you, O Satan" etc.,) "Heaven is your keeper" etc., "Into Your hand I entrust my spirit" etc., "May Heaven bless you" etc. until "and grant you peace," and one says Hashkivenu until close to the conclusion.) ואומר יושב בסתר עליון ואומר ה' מה רבו צרי עד לה' הישועה ואומר ברוך ה' ביום ברוך ה' בלילה ברוך ה' בשכבנו ברוך ה' בקומנו ויאמר ה' אל השטן יגער ה' בך השטן וכו' ה' שומרך וכו' מעתה ועד עולם בידך אפקיד רוחי וכו' יברכך ה' וכו' עד וישם לך שלום ואומ' השכיבנו עד סמוך לחתימה:
