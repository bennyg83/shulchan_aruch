The law of Shema before going to sleep. It contains 2 seifim: One recites in bed the first paragraph of Shema and recites the blessing HaMapil. {Rama: He should recite Shema immediately before sleeping, and it is forbidden to eat, drink, or speak after reciting Shema until he falls asleep, as implied by the verse, 'Commune with your hearts upon your beds and be silent, Selah.' [Kol Bo; Rokeach; Rabbeinu Yeruham] See above, siman 63, regarding reciting Shema while lying down.} If he cannot fall asleep immediately, he should repeat Shema several times until sleep overtakes him. After Shema he may recite the verses and prayers customarily said for protection, including 'Yoshev BeSeter Elyon' and the other passages recorded by the authorities.
