(א) First parasha — and if he prayed Ma'ariv while it was still day he must read all the parashiyot and intend to fulfill through them the positive mitzvah of Keriat Shema and also the mitzvah of remembering the exodus from Egypt. And it is good always to say any Keriat Shema that is 248 words [together with "Heaven, faithful King"] to preserve 248 limbs.

(ב) And he blesses the One Who causes sleep etc. — from the language of Shulchan Aruch it implies that the blessing of the One Who causes sleep one says after Keriat Shema so that the blessing be adjacent to sleep; and what he reads afterward, "He who dwells in secret" and the others, since they are for protection are not an interruption [and some are meticulous to bless the One Who causes sleep at the end after all the verses]. And there are later authorities who agreed that one should bless the blessing of the One Who causes sleep first and afterward Keriat Shema and the rest of the verses of mercy (as printed in siddurim); and it appears that in practice a person should conduct himself according to his nature — namely, if his nature is to fall asleep in the middle of Keriat Shema it is better that he precede the blessing of the One Who causes sleep as much as possible; and if his nature is not so, it is better after the blessing of the One Who causes sleep until the end.

(ג) Close to his bed — and one should not read Keriat Shema when entering to sleep but when one sees sleep coming upon him [Sedei Chemed]; but in Knesset HaGedolah he wrote that one should read immediately lest sleep seize him afterward and he not read; and one need not be concerned about interruption except when doing something else in between; but sitting and being silent is not called interruption even if he waited some time before sleeping.

(ד) After Keriat Shema etc. — and if he wishes to drink or speak some necessary matter it appears permitted, but he should return and read the parasha of Shema; however if he already said the blessing of the One Who causes sleep he should be careful about this, for he will interrupt between the blessing and sleep.

(ה) Sleep immediately — and if he needs to use his bed he should wash himself from the semen upon him and wash his hands and afterward read; and at minimum he should say the blessing of the One Who causes sleep and Shema after the use.

(ו) When he is lying — meaning, that there it is proven in siman 61 in the gloss that l'chatchila it is not worthwhile to go and lie down and read while lying, even when lying on his side literally. Magen Avraham wrote: specifically when reading this Keriat Shema as an obligation, such as when he prayed Ma'ariv while it was still day and now needs to intend to fulfill the positive mitzvah of Keriat Shema — for that he must be careful in all its particulars; but if he already read in its time and now reads only for bedtime Keriat Shema, it is permitted l'chatchila to read while lying. And there are those who are stringent l'chatchila to read standing or sitting; however if he already lay down, all agree it is permitted to turn on his side and read. And a person should accustom himself to lie specifically on his side, and it is a great prohibition to lie face up — namely, cast upon his back with his face upward, or with his face pressed downward.

(ז) Return and read — the first parasha [and it is fitting not to repeat the first verse], or that he say other verses of mercy. And the same applies if he is thinking in words of Torah — it is fine [Magen Avraham].

(ח) And one does not bless etc. — meaning "who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us regarding Keriat Shema" one does not bless; and unlike the view of some poskim who require a blessing. The poskim wrote: one who sleeps by day need not bless the blessing of the One Who causes sleep, and it is good that he say "and may the pleasantness" etc., "He who dwells in secret" etc.

(ט) And one says "He who dwells in secret" etc. — all these verses that they practiced saying are to protect so that no evil thing, Heaven forbid, come upon him. And if he is sick or under duress, it suffices with what he says — the first parasha of Keriat Shema and the blessing of the One Who causes sleep alone [Magen Avraham]. The books wrote that at night before sleep it is proper for a person to search through his deeds that he did all day, and if he finds he committed a transgression he should confess upon it and accept upon himself not to do it again; and especially in common sins such as flattery, lies, mockery, lashon hara, and also the sin of bittul Torah — these require extra scrutiny. Also it is fitting to forgive everyone who sinned against him and pained him, and in the merit of this a person lives long [Megillah 28]. Our sages said: one who sleeps alone in a house — and this is at night — a demon seizes him, and "house" means a room. These require guarding from harmful forces: a sick person, a woman in childbirth, a groom, a bride, a mourner; and a Torah scholar at night [namely when he stands alone in the dark of night].
