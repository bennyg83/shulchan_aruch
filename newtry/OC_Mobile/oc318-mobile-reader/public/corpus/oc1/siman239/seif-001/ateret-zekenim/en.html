One should read Keriat Shema to completion, for it contains 248 words and saves 248 limbs (Mahari Luria and Maharshal). And there are those who wrote to also say after Keriat Shema the chapter of Tehillim "I will exalt You, Heaven, for You have drawn me up" (Semag). One does not bless upon bedtime Keriat Shema, meaning, one does not say "who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us regarding Keriat Shema."
