(1) Until it is stored. It is disrespectful to the Torah for people to leave it behind and depart. This refers to a place where the custom is to store the Sefer Torah after Uva LeTzion. Otherwise, in any event it is forbidden to leave the synagogue before Kedushah DeSidra, as explained in siman 132.
(2) Only an individual. Even two or three may leave. The reason is that the Sefer Torah is still honored so long as most of the congregation remains. See Peri Chadash. Peri Megadim inclines to say that Rama was lenient specifically within the synagogue itself. When the Sefer Torah is being taken to another building, however, under all circumstances even an individual should not leave before the Sefer Torah, but should wait until it is taken out. See there.
(3) They must remain, etc. It is improper for a person to leave through the doorway before the Sefer Torah.
(4) And they should follow it, etc. This is to honor it by accompanying it. The Sages associated this with the verse, “You shall follow Hashem your God.” Gemara.
(5) To leave through another doorway, etc. In that case it is not evident that he is leaving before the Sefer Torah.
(6) Provided that they go, etc. Meaning, although we permitted leaving through another doorway, one must nevertheless wait outside until the Sefer Torah is taken out and then accompany it to its place.
(7) To accompany it. Likewise, when it is taken from the heichal, it is a mitzvah for everyone before whom it passes to accompany it until the bimah.
(8) And this is also the practice regarding the one who lifts it, etc. This implies that our custom applies only to the one who performs hagbahah. Levush, Shulchan Atzei Shittim, and other later authorities, however, state that both the one who performs hagbahah and the one who rolls the Torah must accompany it until before the Aron HaKodesh. The widespread custom is that while proceeding from the table, the chazzan and congregation recite the chapter of Tehillim beginning, “Render to Hashem, sons of the mighty,” etc. The custom is to recite this chapter of Tehillim only on Shabbos or on Yom Tov that coincides with Shabbos. On Yom Tov, and certainly on a weekday, when the Sefer Torah is put away they do not recite that chapter of Tehillim, but rather Tehillim 24, “Of David, a chapter of Tehillim; the earth is Hashem's,” etc.
