Seif katan 1: Until, etc. The correct text is: “And Uva LeTzion; see the end of siman 25.” There Beit Yosef writes that on a day when the Sefer Torah is read, one should not remove his tefillin until the Sefer Torah has been returned to the heichal. Rama explains that this refers to a place where they do not return the Sefer Torah until after Uva LeTzion. Otherwise, in any event one may not remove his tefillin until after Uva LeTzion. The same must be said here, for siman 132, seif 2, explains that the congregation may not leave the synagogue until after Uva LeTzion.
