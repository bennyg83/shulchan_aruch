1) [Seif 1] The congregation is not permitted to leave the synagogue until they put away the Sefer Torah. That is, in a place where they put them away after 'Uva LeTzion.' Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. And he means that if they put them away before 'Uva LeTzion,' in any case it is forbidden to leave, as stated in siman 132, seif 2, that it is forbidden for a person to leave the synagogue before Kedusha d'Sidra. And see above siman 135, ot 2.
