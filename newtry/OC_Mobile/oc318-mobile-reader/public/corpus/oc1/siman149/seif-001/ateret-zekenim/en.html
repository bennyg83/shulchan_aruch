It is forbidden to bring a Sefer Torah to people so that they may read from it, even if they are imprisoned, including on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur. Mordechai, end of the first chapter of Rosh Hashanah. Some say that if a sick person can concentrate, ten people should come and pray with him. If he is a distinguished person, they may bring a Sefer Torah to his home, and the ten who pray with him may read from it. Hagahos Ashri, first chapter of Berachos; this is also stated in Or Zarua itself in the first chapter of Berachos. It appears there that for a distinguished person this is permitted even if he is not ill, and for an ill person even if he is not distinguished. The prohibition applies only to bringing it solely at the time of the reading. It is permitted, however, to prepare an aron or table in his home one or two days beforehand. Responsa Maharam Padua, siman 88.
