It is permitted to move it. Since it is made to go out, a condition helps for it, Maggid Mishneh, chapter 25. Meaning: regarding another base for a forbidden item, a condition does not help, unlike what the Beis Yosef wrote in the name of the Rashbatz. Although at the end of siman 518 it implies that a condition does not help even with a rickety sukkah, that is because on Yom Tov we rule like Rabbi Yehudah. But on Shabbos we rule like Rabbi Shimon. Nevertheless, this requires study, for with muktzeh because of prohibition, even on Shabbos we rule like Rabbi Yehudah; see what I wrote there.
Some say that it does not help. Even according to the one who says that a condition helps with a sukkah, that is because it was made beforehand, and now, at bein hashmashos, he sits and waits for when it will fall. This is not so here, where its essential setting-aside is at bein hashmashos and it was actively rejected for the sake of Shabbos, Tosafot, folio 44.
And the law of the condition, etc. That he should make it before bein hashmashos. But here he cannot say, I am not separating myself from it for all of bein hashmashos, for at bein hashmashos it is burning, and therefore it is forbidden to move it.
The prohibition of instructing a non-Jew. Although in siman 276, seif 3, he permitted telling a non-Jew, that is specifically when it is for the need of his body, where it is permitted because if the Jew wanted, he could move it indirectly. But here he speaks where he acts only for the need of the lamp, so that the lamp not be stolen, and this is forbidden even through indirect moving, as written in siman 311, seif 1 and seif 5; and there is a prohibition of instructing a non-Jew.
