(Paragraph 5) By means of bread, etc. Nevertheless, the bread must be significant, etc., as written below in siman 310.
It is as though he stipulated, for we see that he placed bread on it in order to move it by means of the bread.
This requires examination, for the oil itself, etc. What difference does it make that the oil is more important than the bread, when the oil itself is not muktzeh? That is, it is known, etc., and made for that purpose, etc. See what I wrote in siman 277:3. Magen Avraham writes there, paragraph 8, that if bread and a knife were on the table at bein hashemashos along with the lamp, the table is a base for both a forbidden and a permitted object. Why do we not say the flame, while burning, is nevertheless more important to him than the bread and knife? Magen Avraham distinguishes: a menorah is made for a lamp, not bread, whereas a table is made for bread just as it is made to hold the menorah. Tosafos's words cited there are precise: the Gemara had to say that lamp, oil, and wick become a base for the flame. Otherwise, the lamp would be a base both for the forbidden flame and for the permitted oil and wick, although the flame while burning is more important than the vessel. Evidently, the flame is considered more important only relative to bread, for the menorah is not made for bread; it is made for oil and wick just as for the flame, so the flame is not considered more important than they are.
