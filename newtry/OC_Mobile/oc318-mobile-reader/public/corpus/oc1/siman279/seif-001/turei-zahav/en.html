According to Rabbi Shimon, the prohibition applies only while the lamp is still burning. Levush writes in a responsum printed here: ‘Regarding complete set-aside, such as a lamp lit during that Shabbos, Rabbi Shimon agrees that it is prohibited.’ This is certainly an error. Rabbi Shimon agrees only while it is burning, as stated explicitly in the chapter Kirah: Rabbi Chiya bar Abba said in the name of Rabbi Yochanan that according to Rabbi Shimon there is no muktzeh except something comparable to the oil in a lamp while it is burning. Rashi explains that this is not because of the prohibition, but because of the mitzvah: since it was designated for its mitzvah on Shabbos, it remains set aside for the entire period during which its burning is prohibited, but no longer. This concludes his words. The Rosh, in the first chapter of Chullin, likewise writes: ‘We rule like Rabbi Shimon, who does not accept muktzeh except regarding a lamp lit during Shabbos.’ This is a textual error; it should read, ‘a lamp burning on Shabbos.’
