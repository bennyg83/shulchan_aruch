[Levush] Even Rabbi Shimon, etc. See Nachalat Tzvi's question on the Levush; he did not see the gloss to the Levush.
[Levush] Such as a lamp that was lit, etc. Taz calls this a mistake, arguing that Rabbi Shimon concedes only while the lamp is actually burning, as explicit in Shabbos 45. He similarly emends the Rosh in Chullin from "a lamp that was lit on Shabbos" to "a lamp burning on Shabbos." Taz was not precise. The Levush speaks of a large lamp that one does not expect to go out during the entire day. This is considered actively set aside, and Rabbi Shimon agrees, as Beit Yosef writes in siman 310. According to Taz's understanding, his proof from chapter Kirah is unnecessary, because the Gemara quoted by the Levush itself says that the halachah follows Rabbi Shimon except for muktzeh caused by prohibition, such as a lamp lit on Shabbos. Clearly the Levush means a large lamp. This is also the simple intent of the Rosh's phrase "which he lit on Shabbos, thereby actively setting it aside," namely a large lamp.
