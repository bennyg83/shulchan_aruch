1) [Seif 1] A lamp that was kindled, etc. If a person's eyes hurt, the pain intensifies, and the lamplight harms him, he may not move the lamp and bring it to another room. Admas Kodesh, volume 2, siman 1; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1. See, however, Responsa Sedeh HaAretz, volume 3, siman 15, which permits even the Jewish person himself to move it in an unusual manner; see there. Zechor LeAvraham, Hilchos Shabbos, paragraph 6, cites this.
