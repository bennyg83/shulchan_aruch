(ב) I will copy somewhat from our novellae, Shabbat 44a. In the Rif he brought moving the candle, and so oil, etc., and in the Tur here the oil is permitted, and so the candle, and the Mechaber copied the Rif. And behold with oil there is muktzeh de'oraita in using it, per the one who holds she'eina tzricha legufa — why is he liable? And the candle is rabbinic on Shabbat 37a — per the one who holds she'eina tzricha legufa the candle is more severe in two prohibitions — base and its melacha — forbidden, see there. Regarding a condition — if it helps with oil after extinguishing, per the name of Rashbash who wrote to distinguish between sukkah decorations, which are muktzeh for themselves — a condition does not help, unlike here where the candle is only a base — thus oil — a condition does not help; and one may say sukkah is de'oraita for one authority — a condition does not help, and a candle in rabbinic law — a condition helps; and for this one may resolve the change in wording, and so understand; and if not so in Magen Avraham, letter 16, it will be explained further. And in letter 2, if not so, it will be explained further. And what is written like R' Shimon — it requires study, for R' Yehudah is established, see Turei Zahav and Shiyurei Beracha on this.
