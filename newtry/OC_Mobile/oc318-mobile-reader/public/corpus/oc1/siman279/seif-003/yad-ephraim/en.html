Magen Avraham, note 8. And the law of a condition, etc. — it appears the notation 8 found here is a scribal error, for this that he wrote and the law of a condition belongs to what he wrote before; and it should read: and rejection by hand for Shabbat need — Tosafot Shabbat 44, and the law of a condition in building a sukkah that he make it before bein hashemashot, etc., as it should read; and what he wrote and the law of a condition in building a sukkah is from the Rivash, who wrote in siman 93: for us, who hold like R' Yehudah, we do not say miggo it was set aside a condition does not help, as where a condition does not help in sukkah decoration except when one says I am not detaching, etc., and with a candle — therefore while lit it is forbidden; therefore even after extinguishing it is forbidden because of miggo, see there. And perhaps Magen Avraham intended to explain Tosafot themselves — what he wrote uproots its set-aside, etc. — for at first glance this phrase needs a reason, since he explicitly conditioned and says he is not detaching — if so there is no place to call it rejection by hand for this; therefore Magen Avraham comes to explain and the law of a condition — I am not detaching is only that he make it before bein hashemashot; but here necessarily on bein hashemashot he detaches — what does his speech before bein hashemashot help that he is not detaching? However, in my humble opinion, Tosafot did not settle on this, for they deal regarding the sukkah wood itself whether we say it is permitted through a condition, and there too it is necessarily detached from them because of tent contradiction — only they wrote to distinguish that nevertheless with a candle it is worse, for it is not applicable that one sit and wait on bein hashemashot — therefore a condition does not help; therefore the essence is as written that he comes to distinguish between a condition for sukkah decoration and per the Rivash's words (and so teaches what Magen Avraham wrote before us — and the law of a condition, etc. — for it is not the way of notation in Magen Avraham to come with the word etc. after it; therefore the essence is as written that the notation is a scribal error and the word etc. is also a scribal error and it should read in building a sukkah, as stated). And per the text before us in Magen Avraham it must be that Magen Avraham explains that what Rama wrote and the law of a condition, see below — he comes to give a reason why a condition does not help here, and this is not implied; rather it appears Rama likewise wrote for those who practice like Shulchan Aruch — one must make the law of a condition as below; and were I not afraid, I would say that besides the notation error there is here transposition of upper and lower, etc., and it should read: and one authority says a condition does not help, and on this Magen Avraham wrote the law of a condition is that he make it before bein hashemashot; but here, etc., forbidden to move, and even per the one who says a condition helps with a sukkah, etc., as it should read — and this error happened because Magen Avraham wrote on the gloss words in the margin that the law of a condition is that he make, etc., and the copyist thought it was a notation on Magen Avraham's own words — thus; but rather as stated, etc., and his intent in what he wrote and even per the one who says, etc. — namely that per that view necessarily it must be that even in such a case where necessarily he detaches from it on bein hashemashot, nevertheless a condition helps, for there it was made long before and on bein hashemashot he sits and waits, etc.
