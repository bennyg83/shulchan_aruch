3) There: It may not be moved. If there is fear of non-Jews, it may be moved after it is extinguished. Shiltei Gibborim. Rashi's commentary, however, implies that it is forbidden where there is no danger. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1, writes that this does not mean literal mortal danger, because in that case it would be permitted even while the lamp is burning and even when the danger is uncertain, as stated in siman 278. Rather, the circumstances are somewhat close to danger; see there. Machatzis HaShekel writes the same. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1, writes that 'danger' does not specifically mean danger to life, but includes the prospect that a non-Jew will strike him, financial loss, and the like.
