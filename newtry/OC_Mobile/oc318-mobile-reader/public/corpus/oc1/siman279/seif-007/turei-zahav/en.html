Lest it fall apart, become disassembled, and he reassemble it, etc. This is difficult, for how do we use silver goblets on Shabbos when most are assembled from separate pieces, without concern for this? According to the Tur’s statement in siman 313 that a goblet is not ordinarily tightened firmly, and is therefore permitted, the matter is resolved.
And he will thereby be making a utensil. Although in siman 314 we say that the prohibition of building does not apply to utensils, this case differs because it is as though he makes a new utensil. So it appears in my humble opinion.
