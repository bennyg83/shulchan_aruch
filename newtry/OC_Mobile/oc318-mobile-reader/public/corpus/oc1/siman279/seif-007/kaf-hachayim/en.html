(ז) [Seif 2] Even for his bodily need, etc. — and the reason: since while it is lit it is obviously forbidden to move it in every case, it becomes a base for a forbidden object; therefore it was set aside in such a case for the whole day. Beit Yosef end of the siman, Chokhmat Shlomo, letter 3, Rashaz, letter 1; and what the Taz wrote in note 2 — there is permission for us, since we intend the candle not be on the table only at night — it does not become a base and it is permitted to move it after it is extinguished for his bodily need and its place, see there. See what Turei Zahav challenged, letter 4, and agreed to forbid, see there. And so Mateh Yehuda objected, letter 3, and wrote one should not deviate from the words of the Shulchan Aruch, see there. And so the Taz himself in siman 309, note 1, wrote we cannot be lenient in this since Bach and Shulchan Aruch rule to forbid, see there; and unlike Maharikash in responsa Ahalei Yaakov, siman 103. However, in a place of loss, for all it is permitted to move it in every case, as Rashbatz, part 1, siman 337, and Maharikash there; siman 274, note 1, letter 1, and see below, letter 19.
