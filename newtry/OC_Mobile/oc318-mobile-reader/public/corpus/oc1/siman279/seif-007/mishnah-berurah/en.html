(21) A menorah, etc. - Even if it is new and was never lit.
(22) And he is found to be making a vessel - See Taz, and see below siman 519 seif 2. If its way is to be loose, it is permitted, as written in siman 313 seif 6; see there what we wrote in Mishna Berurah regarding sectional cups.
(23) And it resembles, etc. - Because it can be confused with a sectional one. Know that from what is explained in this seif, it is forbidden to move our candlesticks, called leuchter, even if they were never lit, since they are made of sections. What is written in seif 6 refers to lamps that were not made of sections.
