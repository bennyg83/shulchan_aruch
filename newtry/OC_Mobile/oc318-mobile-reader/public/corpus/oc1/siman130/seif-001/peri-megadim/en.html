(Ribono shel olam) Taz — and see Perishah that he did not say "and if they are bad let them depart," but healing — for all dreams after the mouth, a bad dream if interpreted for good becomes good; and it is good for him that he dreamed, for one who did not dream at all it is bad, as with Miriam through Moshe Rabbeinu — for if they had not existed at all it would be bad; unlike through his prayer they were sweetened. And who can tell — through Elisha they were fixed; and in Miriam great benefit — from the leprosy they took, all Israel received rebuke and saw the magnitude of punishment for lashon hara — from bad came good — until here; and his words are fit for one who said them.
