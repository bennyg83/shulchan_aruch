Summary 1 — one who saw (and did not know whether good or bad) to exclude, etc., every day — for presumably one does not see a dream every day. And it does not seem to me he means what Torat Chayim wrote that it helps only for a dream seen that day — for if so why do they say on the festival; even though Torat Chayim wrote there that in truth they act not according to law, that all Israel say on the festival — but to Magen Avraham it does not seem an error spread through all Israel.

And nevertheless, etc. — every day: if he did not dream, upon what will he say? And if for a dream of days before — behold he also said Ribono on that day.
