(One who saw) — to exclude one who did not dream the night before — he should not say. But in our countries where they lift hands only on festivals one must say Ribono, as I wrote in Mishna Berurah; and see Machatzit HaShekel, who wrote that on the second day of Yom Tov one does not say Ribono for one who did not dream the night before and for dreams of the whole year — he already said from yesterday, see there; and the world does not practice thus; and it is possible they are concerned lest others dreamed about him this night — even so he should not begin from Ribono, for how can he say "a dream I dreamed" when he did not dream at all; rather he begins from "may it be Your will," etc., that all my dreams, etc.
