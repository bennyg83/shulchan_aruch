The Ribono shel olam that we say at the time of nesiat kapayim. And it has
one seif: One who saw a dream and did not know what he saw should stand before the kohanim when they ascend to the duchan and say thus: Ribono shel olam, I am Yours and my dreams are Yours, etc.; and he should intend to finish together with the kohanim when the congregation answers Amen. And if not, he should say thus: Adir bamarom, Who dwells in power, You are peace and Your name is peace — may it be Your will that You place peace upon us.And where they do not ascend to the duchan, he says all this when the shatz says "place peace" and finishes with the shatz so the congregation answers Amen [Ran chapter HaRo'eh].]:
