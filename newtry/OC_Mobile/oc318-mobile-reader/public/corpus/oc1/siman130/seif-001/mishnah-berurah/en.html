(1) One who saw — and in our countries the whole congregation is accustomed to say this prayer at the time of the duchan, even those who did not dream; the reason: since we lift hands only on festivals, it is impossible that he did not dream once between festival and festival.

(2) And did not know, etc. — whether it is good or bad.

(3) And he should say thus — and it was already explained above end of siman 128 in the gloss that the correct time is when they prolong in the tune the words at the end of the verses, see there.

(4) And your dreams, etc. — Magen Avraham wrote one should say: between dreams I dreamed about others and dreams about myself; but in our siddurim the text is reversed — between myself and between, etc.; Eliyah Rabbah agreed. Acharonim wrote: conclude in this prayer the first time u'shmorani when the kohanim finish v'yishmerecha, second v'choneni against v'yichuneka, third u'seratzeni against shalom; Maharshal adds good to say third time: and may the priestly blessing come upon me and satisfy me (Eliyah Rabbah, Magein Giborim, see there). When lifting hands on Yom Tov falling on Shabbat, do not say Ribono, for we do not say tachanun on Shabbat unless he dreamed a bad dream that night (Acharonim):

(5) To finish with the kohanim — so it should read [and the word "also" is a scribal error]; meaning at the end of each verse they finish, three times (Eliyah Rabbah); and so is written in the name of the Gra, who was accustomed to say also at the end of the third verse the Ribono mentioned in the Gemara, not printed in siddurim.

(6) And if not he should say, etc. — meaning the kohanim have not yet finished — he also says Adir bamarom so he finishes with the kohanim the word shalom and the congregation answers Amen on both; and some practice to say Adir bamarom each time when the shatz says place peace, when he says "and may it be good in Your eyes," etc.

(7) And where they do not, etc. — as in our countries where there is no nesiat kapayim all year except on Yom Tov.

(8) To the duchan — duchan means the place where kohanim ascend at the time of nesiat kapayim.

(9) He says all this at the time — meaning the Ribono prayer; and if he finished Ribono before the shatz finished place peace, he also says Adir bamarom as above. Acharonim wrote one should not say Ribono prayer every day, only if he dreamed the night before — how can he say "a dream I dreamed" when he did not dream at all.

(10) Place peace — and if he sees he cannot finish with the shatz, he begins when the shatz says yevarechecha.
