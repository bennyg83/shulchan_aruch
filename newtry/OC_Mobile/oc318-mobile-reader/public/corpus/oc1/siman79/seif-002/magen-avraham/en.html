When he does not see it. And it seems to me that if he closes his eyes or at night, it is permitted in this according to everyone.

And some say: when, etc.—in doubt if they removed feces in the house and a bad smell still remains in the house whether it is permitted to read—for ostensibly it seems permitted according to some, and it is a kal vachomer: here, where the feces is still placed in its place, it is permitted to read even though the smell reaches him—kal vachomer when the feces passed and went; and furthermore regarding passing feces the amoraim disagree, and even though we rule to forbid, that is when he stands in the street opposite it—but here it is outside the room, so according to all it is permitted. However one may say it is different here, since the feces was in this room—it is forbidden to read until the smell ceases, as written s.k. 9; and also the Rashba explains a bad smell without substance means covered feces—see siman 76 s.k. 2; therefore one should be stringent; however it seems to me one who does not smell is permitted to read in this.
