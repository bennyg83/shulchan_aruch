(א) Regarding seif 2, or if it was, etc. As written in chapter 9 of Eruvin and as written in seif 3; and this is why even the opening, etc.

(ב) And one sitting at its side, etc. As written in "if it was high," etc.

(ג) And sees, etc. As stated above, siman 76 seif 1.

(ד) If there is none, etc. As written below.

(ה) Regarding the Rosh. From what was written, "if it was high," etc., and if not he distances as far as his eyes can see, etc. — thus we deal with one who sees it; nevertheless, in a high place, etc., it is permitted. Moreover, feces does not depend on sight, as was written. The Rashba holds that we are dealing with a case where there is a factor preventing one from seeing it; but when it is not high, nevertheless it is forbidden, as written in seif 1.

(ו) And if there is, etc. From what was written: a bad smell that has a substance, etc.; and at the beginning of the baraisa it counts feces and its like from which one distances oneself; and ostensibly this would be the opening passage. Moreover, there it says "as far as his eyes can see," whereas here it is only four amot. Moreover, what is "bad smell"? etc., "one distances from it," etc. — it implies from the smell, and it is not the same as the thing itself until the smell ceases. Nevertheless, we are dealing with such a case where from it itself one need not distance, for example when it is in another house or covered; as the Rashba wrote. One need not distance except because of the smell — and similarly explicitly in seif 3, and in siman 83 seif 1 and seif 4.

(ז) And some say, etc. Tosafos there, s.v. "smell," etc.; and therefore siman 83 seif 4, not like Tosafos.
