Ten [tefachim] high. Meiri wrote, and it is that there is width four on four [tefachim], as Rashbam explained in the first chapter of Beit Kor — only in this is it a domain for itself — end quote. And indeed we find at the beginning of "the thrower" (Gittin 77) regarding a get, that ten high although not four wide is a domain for itself. And it appears regarding feces here one may compare to this, for it is stated in chapter Chaf Heh 56 that regarding Shabbos to divide domains, even a peg helps, as it helps regarding a get — namely ten height without four width, as Rashbam explained there; and it is clear there that whatever is considered a domain to divide for Shabbos, all the more it helps regarding tumah to make an interruption for tumah; if so, certainly tumah is not stricter than feces, for there too there is interruption; and specifically in that [case] of Beit Kor we require ten height and four width, for there the reason is we require that in that height or depth ten height and four width, because there must be on that interruption a field by itself, as stated in the Gemara in chapter Beit Kor that a person does not want to place his money once and be seen in two and three places; not so regarding division of domain — certainly there is division even without four width, as regarding Shabbos, for although regarding carrying from domain to domain one is liable only from private to public domain, nevertheless regarding division of domain this interruption need not be the measure of four amos and height ten like a get; and likewise regarding feces, if it is ten deep or ten high, even though it is not four wide, it is properly an interruption like a get, as above.
