And I am [commenting]: Taz — he analyzed the wording of feces, that there must not be [feces] within four amos from where the smell ceased; but regarding urine, certainly outside four amos, for they have no putrefaction and smell (and if they have smell, their law is like feces — see letter 1 law 9); and in some versions it is emended "and I do not know why not as far as one can see," like Magen Avraham's difficulty; and Magen Avraham already explained he turns his face — do not err in what is written; however urine etc. refers to the Rama's gloss that urine too is as far as one can see, as Rambam wrote chapter 3 Kerias Shema 8; and I explained in Taz 1 regarding law 9 — see there.
