Ten handbreadths. This is provided that it is four handbreadths by four handbreadths wide, so that it constitutes a significant place. Bach. The Taz disagrees and writes that even when it is not four handbreadths wide, it nevertheless constitutes an effective separation. See there. Eliyah Rabbah disagrees with the Taz and rules in accordance with the Bach, and the Rambam likewise rules this way. See there.
One. Meaning, in a single room.
At its side. Meaning, next to the doorway.
It. It appears to me that if he closes his eyes, or if it is nighttime, this is permitted according to all opinions. Magen Avraham.
Its odor. If excrement was removed from a house but a foul odor still remains in the house, one should be stringent. Nevertheless, it appears to me that one who cannot smell it may recite in this situation. Magen Avraham.
