Nineteen [handbreadths]—and this is when there is width of four handbreadths by four handbreadths, for it is an important place (Bach). And Taz disagrees and wrote that even if it is not four [handbreadths] wide it is a valid barrier—see there. And in Eliyah Rabbah disagrees with Taz and rules like Bach, and Rambam too—see there.

One—meaning in one room.

Beside it—meaning next to the opening.

That [feces]—and it seems to me that if he closes his eyes or at night it is permitted in this for all. Magen Avraham.

Its [smell]—if they removed feces from the house and a bad smell still remains in the house, one should be stringent; however it seems to me that one who does not smell is permitted to recite in this. Magen Avraham.
