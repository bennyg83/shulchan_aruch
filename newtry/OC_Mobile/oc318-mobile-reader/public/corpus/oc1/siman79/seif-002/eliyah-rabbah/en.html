Four amot, etc.—see in the Levush the reason for four amot: they acquire for him in every place and it is his camp, and it is written "and your camp shall be holy"; and in Ma'adanei Melech and Nachalas Tzvi they understood that the Levush wrote this reason on his own—see there. I found it explicitly in the chiddushim of the Rashba, folio 20: anything outside four amot is not his camp.
