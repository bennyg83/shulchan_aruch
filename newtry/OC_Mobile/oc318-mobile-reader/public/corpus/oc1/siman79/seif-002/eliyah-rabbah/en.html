Four amos, etc. See the Levush, who gives the reason that four amos acquire for a person in every place, and it is considered his camp, and it is written, Your camp shall be holy. In Ma'adanei Melech and Nachalas Tzvi they understood that the Levush wrote this reason on his own; see there. I found it explicitly in the Chiddushei Rashba, page 20, that anything outside four amos is not his camp.
