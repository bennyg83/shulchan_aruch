If it was in a high place ten handbreadths high and four handbreadths wide, then it is a separate domain because it is an important place by itself, like a private domain for Shabbos (this is the Rosh's view). And some say specifically when he does not see it; but when he sees it he cannot recite [Shema], for it is not in the category of covering and it is not comparable to feces in a lantern (Rashba). And the Rosh's words are primary; however, ab initio it is good to be careful per the Rashba (Beit Yosef).

And some say that just as the barrier is effective for the feces itself, etc., and it is proper to be stringent per the first view (Beit Yosef).
