If it was in a place ten handbreadths high and four handbreadths wide, it constitutes a separate domain, because it is an independently significant place, like a private domain with regard to Shabbos. This is the view of the Rosh. Some say that this applies only when he cannot see it, but if he can see it he may not recite, since this is not considered a covering and is unlike excrement inside a glass vessel. This is the view of the Rashba. The words of the Rosh are primary; nevertheless, initially it is proper to be careful in accordance with the Rashba. Beit Yosef.
Some say that just as a partition is effective for the excrement itself, and so forth. It is proper to be stringent in accordance with the first view. Beit Yosef.
