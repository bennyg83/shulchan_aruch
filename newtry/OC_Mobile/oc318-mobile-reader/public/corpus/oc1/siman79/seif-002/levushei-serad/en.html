Ibid. “And the Rambam,” meaning that according to the Rambam, who writes that the sides are treated like behind him, the case concerns one who cannot turn it to the sides. In such a case, it is explained at the end of siman 81 that placing it to the sides is sufficient. Where it is possible to place it behind him, however, the sides are not treated like in front of him. This is what the Kesef Mishneh writes in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach. The other authorities disagree. The Magen Avraham therefore concludes that all agree that the sides extending toward his front are treated like the area in front of him.
