There—and the Rambam, that is to say: per the Rambam who wrote this law that the sides are like behind him, it deals when he cannot turn to the sides—for in BeHag it is clear at the end of siman 81 that sides suffice; but where possible, the sides are not considered before him—so Kesef Mishnah in the name of R' Menachem; but the poskim disagree on this, and therefore Magen Avraham concluded that the sides before him.
