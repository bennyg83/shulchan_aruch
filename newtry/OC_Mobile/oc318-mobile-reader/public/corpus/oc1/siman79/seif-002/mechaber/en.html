If it was in a place ten handbreadths high or ten low, or it was in one house and he is in another house—even if the door is open and he sits at its side and sees it: if it has no smell, he may recite Kerias Shema. For since it is in another domain, we apply well "and your camp shall be holy" per the Rosh. But per the Rashba, only when he does not see it. And if it has a smell, a partition does not help, nor does a change of domain. And some say that just as a partition helps for the feces itself, so too it helps for its bad smell.
