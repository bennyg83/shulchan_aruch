(5) There—four amot from where the smell ceased. And what the Taz wrote, letter 1—and some wrote that one need not distance except until the smell ceases—that is the view of the Rambam, z"l, whom Maran, z"l, did not rule like, as explained in Beit Yosef. And likewise it is the view of the Acharonim who ruled like the words of Maran, z"l; if so, Heaven forbid to be lenient against them lechatchila, and to rule in this like the Rambam, z"l, except bedieved if he already prayed that he need not return to pray, as written in the previous letter.
