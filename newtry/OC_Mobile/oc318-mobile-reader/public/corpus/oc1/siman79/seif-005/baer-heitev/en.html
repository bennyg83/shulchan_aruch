The soft [feces]—and these typically have a bad smell; therefore even if it happens once that they do not smell, it is forbidden, for it is like human feces. Magen Avraham.

Reaches him—even if they have smell, whatever smell does not reach him is permitted. And what is written in seif 4 "and if they have bad smell their law is like human feces"—not to say one must distance four amos from where smell ceased, rather meaning one must distance until where smell may cease. Ohel Moshe.
