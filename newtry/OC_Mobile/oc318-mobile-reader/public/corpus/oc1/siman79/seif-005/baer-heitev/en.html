Soft. These generally have a foul odor. Therefore, even if on one occasion they happen not to smell, it is forbidden, for they are treated like human excrement. Magen Avraham.
Reaches him. Even if they have an odor, it is permitted so long as the odor does not reach him. The statement in seif 4, that if they have a foul odor their law is like that of human excrement, does not mean that he must distance himself four amos from the point where the odor ceases. Rather, it means that he must distance himself until he reaches a place where the odor ceases. See Magen Avraham.
