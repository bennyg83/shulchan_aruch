But Taz and Magen Avraham 15. And regarding feces covered in a vessel or interruption of domain — whether it is like bad smell with substantial source or without — see Eliyah Rabbah letter 10 and Magen Avraham 9. Indeed in chapter 3 Kerias Shema halachah 12 Rambam wrote: bad smell with substantial source — one distances four amos; Rabbenu Menachem explained: it deals with covered (or) behind him or to his sides; behold covered is bad smell with substantial source; and it requires study for the law. And the pit of dimyon until the smell ceases — likewise one distances from where the smell ceased, and that suffices, and he need not four amos; see Kesef Mishneh there.
