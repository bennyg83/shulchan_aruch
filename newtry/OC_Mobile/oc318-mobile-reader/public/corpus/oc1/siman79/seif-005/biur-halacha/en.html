Donkey excrement, etc. — see in the Mishna Berurah what he wrote in the name of the Magen Avraham; so too the Darkhei Chayyim. However, for the essential law there is room to question this, for one may say that the entire substance to which the name of feces applies is only when they left the animal while putrid; but when initially there was no bad smell in them, the name of feces does not apply at all, and it is not similar to human feces that is forbidden there in all cases. What compelled the Magen Avraham to this seems because otherwise what distinction does the author make between these and the excrement of other domesticated animals, wild beasts, and birds? Did not the author rule in seif 4 that if they have a bad smell their law is like human feces? So too the Machatzis HaShekel — this is the reason of the Magen Avraham. If so, according to what the Magen Avraham afterward ruled himself like the Tur, that excrement of other domesticated animals, wild beasts, and birds is forbidden only until the smell ceases — we do not need all this; it requires investigation.
