Donkey excrement, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote in the name of the Magen Avraham, and so wrote Derech HaChaim. However, regarding the essential law, there is room to question this, for one can say that the name of excrement takes effect upon them only if, when they emerged from the animal, they stank. But if from the outset they had no bad odor, the name of excrement does not take effect upon them at all, and this is not comparable to human excrement, which is forbidden there in all cases. What forced the Magen Avraham to this appears to be that otherwise, what distinction is there for the Mechaber between these and the excrement of other domesticated animals, wild animals, and birds? In all of them the Mechaber ruled in seif 4 that if they have a bad odor, their law is like human excrement. So too the Machatzit HaShekel wrote that this is the reason of the Magen Avraham. If so, according to what the Magen Avraham himself later ruled like the Tur, that the excrement of other domesticated animals, wild animals, and birds is forbidden only until the odor ceases, we do not need all this, and it requires study.
