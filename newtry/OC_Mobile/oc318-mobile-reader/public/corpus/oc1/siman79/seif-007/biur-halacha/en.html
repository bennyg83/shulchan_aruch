But their coop, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote in the name of Chayei Adam regarding an animal pen. In Kitzur Shulchan Aruch he wrote that his words do not appear correct to him, and the poskim also did not mention this. One may distinguish it from a chicken coop, meaning that besides the stench, it is also repulsive, as the Rosh and Tur wrote; who says that this reasoning also applies to an animal pen? One should not bring proof for the Kitzur Shulchan Aruch from what we rule in Yoreh Deah, siman 286, that a cattle barn requires a mezuzah, while regarding a bathroom we rule there that it is exempt, which would show that it is not considered like a bathroom, for chicken coops prove otherwise, since they require mezuzah. Rather, the reason must be that although regarding a blessing it is forbidden to bless there, as in a bathroom, nevertheless regarding mezuzah it is not included among places not made for dignified dwelling, so as to be exempt for that reason; see there. See Chayei Adam, klal 15, din 12, where he leaves this requiring study. However, there is another proof for the Kitzur Shulchan Aruch: according to the Chayei Adam, that it is not specifically chicken excrement, but the same applies to animal excrement, because their abundance makes it considered like human excrement, if so the same law should also apply to the excrement of dogs and pigs in their pen. But this does not appear from the Gemara there according to Tosafot, s.v. and the halacha is not. For if the baraisa that forbids chicken excrement speaks of a coop where there is much of their excrement, as the Rosh wrote, then we could say that the opening clause of the baraisa, which forbids the excrement of dogs and pigs, also speaks in such a case. Why then did Rava say there, the halacha is not, etc.? Let him establish it in such a case. Rather, certainly in all cases, when no hides were placed in them, their law is not like human excrement. Thus, the animal pen remains requiring study, and one who is stringent initially has upon whom to rely.
