Ten handbreadths high, etc.—the Bach wrote that we also require that its width be four handbreadths by four handbreadths, like a private domain for Shabbos; and the Taz disagrees with him from that which is stated in chapter HaMotzi, page 80: for Shabbos, to divide domains, even a board suffices—that is, ten high and not four wide—until here, his words briefly. The halacha is not precise like Rava who said there thus, but like Rabba, who requires a private domain—and so ruled the Rambam chapter 18 of Hilchos Shabbos, and Kesef Mishnah there.
