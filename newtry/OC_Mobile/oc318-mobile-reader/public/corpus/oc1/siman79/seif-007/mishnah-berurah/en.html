(27) That go in the house—even if most of their sustenance is from the five species of grain, one may likewise be lenient in this, since we do not know that their feces putrefies. Birds sitting on their eggs—it is known that then their feces putrefies greatly; therefore plainly their law then is like human feces, as above.

(28) Their coop — it is a small structure made for geese and chickens; and because much feces accumulates in it there is putrefaction and it is disgusting; therefore its law is like human feces. And Chayei Adam wrote that an animal pen is likewise forbidden for this reason; and see Biur Halacha for what we wrote on this.
