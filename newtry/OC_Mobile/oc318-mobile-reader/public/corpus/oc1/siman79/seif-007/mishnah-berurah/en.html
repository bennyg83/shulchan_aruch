That walk about the house. Even if their principal nourishment is from the five species of grain, one may still be lenient so long as we do not know that their excrement is foul. It is known that when birds sit on their eggs their excrement is especially foul. It is therefore obvious that at that time its law is like human excrement, as explained above.
Their coop. This is a small structure made for geese and chickens. Because much excrement accumulates there, it has a foul stench and is repulsive. Therefore, its law is like human excrement. Chayei Adam writes that the same applies to an animal pen, which is likewise forbidden for this reason. See what we wrote in Biur Halachah.
