(4) There—four amot from where the smell ceased. If he prayed within four amot of a bad smell, even though by law he must distance four amot from where the smell ceased, nevertheless bedieved he fulfilled, for it is only derabbanan; and for Kerias Shema he returns to read without its blessings. Neshamas Adam klal 3 letter 6. See in Chayei Adam there letter 33, and in our words above siman 76 letter 38.
