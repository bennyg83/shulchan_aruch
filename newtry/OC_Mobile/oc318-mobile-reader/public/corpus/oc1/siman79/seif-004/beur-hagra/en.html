(א) Regarding seif 4, as the law, etc. As written in Yerushalmi there: they distance from animal dung four amot. The Rashba says: from the knees, and only of a donkey; R. Chaviva says: and one coming from the road — these, no.

(ב) And if there is, etc. Thus he explained the words of the Rambam, that one must distance from all animal feces; his reason is that it is no less than a garbage heap with a bad smell; and likewise a stinking carcass and moist donkey excrement and the like.
