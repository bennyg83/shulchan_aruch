Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar says that this applies when they are soft, provided they are from a donkey. Rabbi Chiya bar Abba said that this refers to a donkey coming from a journey, but not otherwise.
And if there is, and so forth. This is how the Rambam’s words are explained, for he requires distancing from the excrement of every animal. His reason is that it is no less severe than a refuse heap with a foul odor, a putrid carcass, soft donkey dung, and the like.
