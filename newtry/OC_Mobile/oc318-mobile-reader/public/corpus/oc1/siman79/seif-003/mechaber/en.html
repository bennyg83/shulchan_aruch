A small courtyard completely breached into a large one, and the large one extends beyond it on the sides—the small one is not considered a house in itself since it was completely breached into the large one. But the large one, since its walls extend beyond the small one on this side and that side, is considered a house in itself. Therefore, if there is feces in the large one, it is forbidden to recite Kerias Shema in the small one until one distances as required. And if the feces is in the small one, it is permitted to recite in the large one without distancing if a bad smell does not reach him.
