(3) There—one must distance four amot from where the smell ceased, because it is written "and your camp shall be holy." The measure of four amot: the reason is because a person's four amot acquire for him in every place he walks regarding Shabbos, gittin, lost objects, and other matters; therefore a person's four amot are in his domain and his house where he lodges there—and this is "your camp," language of lodging—that is, "and your camp shall be holy" means every place of your lodging shall be holy; that is, all four amot from where the smell ceased—for if bad smell were within four amot, not all his lodging would be holy. Levush. Eliyah Rabbah letter 2—and see in the letter after it.
