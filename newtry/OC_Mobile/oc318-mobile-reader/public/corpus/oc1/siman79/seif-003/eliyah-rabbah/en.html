And before him, etc.—regarding covering, the Merciful One made it dependent on clothing; it is somewhat difficult, for regarding this the Merciful One made it dependent on covering: if he covered it, even though it is within his four amot it is permitted—but that it should be forbidden outside four amot before him if he did not cover it, from where do we know this? I again saw in chiddushim there that he wrote: because of "nothing indecent shall be seen by you," it is forbidden before him as far as his eyes can see—even though the verse refers to actual ervah; nevertheless, since the Merciful One combined them and wrote them together, "nothing indecent shall be seen by you" also applies to feces; and thus I explain the words of the Levush himself in seif 2. And one may push and explain that here too his intent is likewise.
