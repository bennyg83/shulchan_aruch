Taz, seif katan 2. “Where the odor ceases,” for this is the law explained at the beginning of the seif. He then concludes, “but regarding urine,” and so forth. This means urine that has no odor, for if it has an odor its law is like excrement, as explained in siman 86. The Taz’s point is to ask why this law was written regarding excrement and not also regarding urine. He therefore writes that in the case of urine, the entire congregation can distance itself four amos, since one need distance himself only from the urine itself, as it has no odor. With excrement, however, one must distance himself from the place where its odor ceases, and they cannot all do so.
