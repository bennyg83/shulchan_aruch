Taz s.k. 2—ceased: for such is the law, as explained at the beginning of the seif; and for this reason he concluded regarding urine, etc.—that is to say, they have no smell; but if they have smell, their law is like feces—see siman 86. The intent of the Taz: why did he write this law regarding feces and did not write that the same applies to urine? Therefore he wrote that for urine one distances the entire congregation four amot, since one need only distance from the urine itself, which has no smell—unlike feces, where one must distance from the place of the smell; they cannot distance—and understand clearly.
