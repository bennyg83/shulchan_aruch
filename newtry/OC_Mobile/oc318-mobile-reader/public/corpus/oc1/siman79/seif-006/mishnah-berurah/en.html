(26) Red—from his wording it implies that he means because it is red it putrefies greatly; and the later authorities wrote that red chickens are what they call in English "young chickens" [indik], for their feces putrefies greatly; and it is proper not to keep them in a house where one studies and blesses, and especially in a Torah scholar's house, for he cannot be without Torah thought [Minchat Shmuel, Halachah Berurah].
