In the Yerushalmi it forbids, etc., red. See Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote regarding large chickens, called turkeys. In Derech HaChaim it appears regarding this, and likewise regarding a chicken coop, that if one transgressed and read opposite them, he should go back and read, and regarding prayer he should stipulate, as above in the Mishnah Berurah. It appears to me that his reason is that from Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah and the Rosh it appears that this law was not so conclusive even regarding a chicken coop, that its law is like human excrement. Therefore his view is that regarding prayer one must stipulate. Initially, a person certainly must be stringent, for it is a doubt according to the Torah, and therefore the Mechaber wrote that its law is like human excrement.
