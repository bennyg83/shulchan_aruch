In the Yerushalmi it forbids red, etc.—see in the Mishna Berurah what he wrote regarding large chickens [called in Yiddish indik or young chickens (yungere)], and in Biur Halacha it is implied likewise; and likewise regarding a chicken coop—if one passed by and read Kerias Shema facing it, he must return and read again; and regarding prayer he must make a condition, and likewise in the Mishna Berurah. It seems to me that his reason is because of the Yerushalmi, and the Rosh implies that this law is not conclusively established even for a coop, since its law is like human feces; therefore his view is that for prayer one must make a condition, and lechatchila a person must certainly be stringent, for it is a safek deoraysa; therefore the Mechaber wrote that its law is like human feces.
