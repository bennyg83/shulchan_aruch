Red. His wording implies that the excrement itself is red, as Rabbeinu Yonah writes. The Rashba explains that the chickens are red, referring to what is called an English hen. Bach. The Tashbetz explains that these are chickens from the land of Edom that are fed barley, and the Agudah writes likewise. In Kesubos, chapter 4, it states that red barley is harmful.
