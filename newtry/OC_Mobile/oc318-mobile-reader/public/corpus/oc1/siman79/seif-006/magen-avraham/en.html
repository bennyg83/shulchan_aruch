Red. From his wording it implies that he means the feces is red, as Tur wrote; and Rashba explained that the chickens are red—that is what they call in English "hens" [Bach]. And in Tashbetz he explained red chickens of the land of Edom that they feed them barley; and likewise Agudah. And in Kesubos chapter 4 there is that red barley is bad.
