And opposite red chicken feces, etc. And some say that putrefying red chicken feces are the chickens in the land of Edom that eat barley there and their feces putrefy exceedingly; and it appears that they are those same large chickens called "English hen," which are known to have exceedingly putrefying feces (my teacher in Bach). And some say that any animal, beast, and bird whose feces has a bad smell—it is forbidden to recite [Shema] opposite it; therefore there is no distinction between them, between red and others, for anything that has a bad smell is forbidden and what has no bad smell is permitted (Darkei Moshe). And some say that any whose sustenance is in filth—forbidden to recite opposite its feces, such as dogs and swine; and any whose sustenance is not in filth is permitted (so too in Orach Chayyim).
