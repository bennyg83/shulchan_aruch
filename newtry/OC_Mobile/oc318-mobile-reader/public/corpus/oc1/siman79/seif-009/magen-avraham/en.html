It is forbidden to recite Krias Shema. This is because he can go outside and recite it. Rashi. According to this, the same should apply to Torah study when one is learning alone and can go elsewhere. It may nevertheless be that since the Sages did not decree against Torah study in this circumstance, he need not leave, as the Beit Yosef writes regarding a tallis in siman 13. See there.
