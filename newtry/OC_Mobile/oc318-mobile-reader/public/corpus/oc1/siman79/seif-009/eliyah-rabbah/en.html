Malbushei Yom Tov wrote this is his wording: his eyes misled him—he saw in the words of the Rashba when he does not see the ervah. But it seems to me this is a scribal error, and it must say "the feces," for we derive in the matter of "You shall have a place outside the camp" and not from the verse "nothing indecent shall be seen by you," etc.—until here. Behold, he corrected well that it must say "the feces," for here it deals with feces; and likewise I saw in the chiddushim of the Rashba himself there explicitly. Nevertheless the words of the Levush are correct, for the Rashba wrote explicitly there that the verse "nothing indecent shall be seen by you" also applies to feces, as we said. Moreover, from the verse of "outside the camp" we learn only that feces in a latrine is permitted—for even when it is visible from everywhere, covering suffices; and furthermore it comes to teach that regarding covering, even within four amot, as I explained above—but that it should be forbidden as far as his eyes can see is learned from "nothing indecent shall be seen by you," and this is clear.
