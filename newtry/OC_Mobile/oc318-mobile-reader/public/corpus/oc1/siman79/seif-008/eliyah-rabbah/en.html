And he sees it, etc. Berachos 25: if there was a place ten tefachim high or ten tefachim low, he sits at its side and reads Krias Shema, and if not, he distances himself as far as his eyes can see. The Rosh explained that it speaks even when he sees it. Nachalas Tzvi wrote that his reason is that otherwise, why did it need to teach, and if not, etc.? For if he sees it, even at a height of ten tefachim he distances himself as far as his eyes can see. Regarding the Rashba, it appears to my humble opinion that he holds that it needed to teach, and if not, etc., in order to inform us that where it is ten high and he does not see it, he need not distance four amos. Thus far. His words are astonishing and very forced to explain. How is this heard from, and if not, etc.? Furthermore, it already taught, he sits at its side and reads. Also, he overlooked the Rashba's words in his Chiddushim, where he explains the Gemara: here it says that if it is ten high, he sits at its side and reads, since it is not actually in his place and he does not see it, because the height or depth of the place causes him not to see it. But if it is opposite him, even though there is another cause preventing him from seeing it, whenever it is in a place where he could see it, he should not read until he distances from it as far as his eyes can see, since it is not another domain. This is explained even more clearly in Responsa Rashba, siman 473. Also the Beit Yosef, with all due respect, did not reach the depth of the Rashba's words in this, for he rejected his words from what is stated in seif 3, that it is permitted with excrement in the smaller room, which necessarily speaks where he sees it, for if not, even if it is in the same room it is permitted, and there is no proof, as explained.
