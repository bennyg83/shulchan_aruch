(29) Bad smell — and its law for everything is like human feces; therefore one must distance before it as far as one can see, even if he knows there is no feces or urine in it, and it is from the Torah. This is the rule: any bad smell that comes from something that putrefies, like those explained [above] that have a substantial source — one distances from it as the law of human feces, for they are included in "and your camp shall be holy" — meaning that if the bad smell reaches him, his camp is not holy and it is forbidden to recite [Shema] [Levush].
