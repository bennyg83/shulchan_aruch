(8) There—gloss: or he is blind. That even though he does not see, if he is in a place where, were he not blind, he would see it—it is forbidden; Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah and the Rashba. And the Acharonim wrote likewise. That which Maran, z"l, did not write it in Shulchan Aruch is because it is learned from night, as Maran, z"l, wrote in Beit Yosef—see there, R' Yehuda and Rashba.
