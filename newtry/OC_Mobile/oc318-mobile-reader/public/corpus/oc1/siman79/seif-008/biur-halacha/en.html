A garbage heap with a bad smell—see in the Mishna Berurah that even if it is checked for feces and odor, likewise the Peri Megadim; and it is simple that if not so, even if there is no bad smell, behold we rule that safek feces in a garbage heap is forbidden. It seems to me that if on most days the bad smell ceased from it, it is permitted if it is checked for feces; and even though regarding feces we rule in siman 82 that it is forbidden until it is decomposed, for then it is considered like ordinary dust—nevertheless a garbage heap is not dust from the outset, but is only forbidden and considered like feces because of the smell; and automatically, when the smell ceases, it returns to being dust, as seems correct to me.
