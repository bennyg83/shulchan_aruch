A refuse heap whose odor is bad. See Mishnah Berurah, that this applies even if it was checked and found free of excrement and urine. So wrote the Peri Megadim. This is obvious, for otherwise, even if its odor is not bad, we rule that doubtful excrement in a refuse heap is forbidden. It appears to me that if on most days the bad odor has gone away, it is permitted if it was checked and found free of excrement. Although regarding excrement we rule in siman 82 that it is forbidden until it crumbles, for then it is considered mere earth, that case is different, for a refuse heap is essentially earth from the outset; it is forbidden and considered like excrement only because of the odor. Automatically, when the odor is gone it returns to being earth. So it appears to my humble opinion.
