8) There, gloss: Or if he is blind. Although he does not see it, if he stands in a place from which he would see it were he not blind, it is forbidden. Beis Yosef, citing Rabbeinu Yonah and Rashba. The later authorities write likewise. Maran did not state this separately in Shulchan Aruch because it is learned from the law of night, as Maran writes in Beis Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah and Rashba.
