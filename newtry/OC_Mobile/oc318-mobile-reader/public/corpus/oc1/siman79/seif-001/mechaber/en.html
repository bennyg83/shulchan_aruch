One who happens upon feces at the time of recitation. And it contains 9 seifim. If there was human feces behind him, he
must distance four amot from where the smell ceases. Even if he has an illness such that he does not smell, he must distance four amot from where the smell would cease for one who smells. And before him he must distance as far as his eyes can see, even at night [or if he is blind] [responsum of Rashba siman 168, Beit Yosef in the name of Ri]—where he does not see it—he must distance until a place where he could not see it by day. And if it is at his side, its law is like behind him.[A cantor praying and there is feces in the synagogue or house where he prays—even if it is behind him anywhere in the house, he must be silent until they remove it, since he fulfills the obligation of the many; and it is impossible that one of the congregation is not within four amot of the feces [Hagahot Mordechai Chadashim, chapter Tefilat haShachar].
