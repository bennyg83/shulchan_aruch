(In Magen Avraham siman kuf bet) like before him, as stated regarding a crooked lulav. His intention: there we say Rava said, I have nothing except before him, but after him it is a baraita; Rabbenu Nissim said the sides are like before him, and some say like after him, and they ruled stringently like words of Torah — the sides are like before him. Accordingly we see that although Rava said I have nothing except before him, it is included in his wording to disqualify the sides too; if so, also here where it states before him as far as one can see, we may say that in the wording "before him" the sides are also included for distancing at this measure; and Rambam deals where it is not possible — then it is better he turn to the sides.
