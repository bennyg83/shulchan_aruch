Even at night, and so forth. This is because “your camp shall be holy” is required. See the end of siman 75.
At his side, its law is like behind him. The Kesef Mishneh writes in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach that it appears the sides are like the area in front of him, as is stated concerning a bent lulav, while the Rambam refers to a case in which no other arrangement is possible. See also the end of siman 81. What I wrote there appears correct, that the sides extending toward his front are treated like the area in front of him, as stated in siman 128, seif 24. See there.
Within four amos. This implies that if no person is sitting within four amos of the excrement, it is permitted to pray even though the excrement separates between them and the chazzan, since they can turn their faces away. Nevertheless, in siman 55, seif 20, some say that one should not answer Amen when excrement separates between them.
Of the excrement. The listener and the one reciting must both have intent. It appears to me that if the chazzan is in Pesukei DeZimrah, where he is not discharging the congregation’s obligation, he may recite in his usual manner if he is outside four amos.
