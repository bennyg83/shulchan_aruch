Within four amot. It implies that if no person sits within four amot of the feces, it is permitted to pray even though feces separates between them and the shaliach tzibur—they may turn their faces; however in siman 55 s.k. 11 that one should not answer amen when feces separates between them.

Of the feces. And we require the intent of listener and speaker (there); and it seems to me that if he is in Pesukei deZimra, which is not motzi the many, the shaliach tzibur is permitted to say as usual if he is outside four amot.
