(1) [Seif 1] If human feces was behind him, he must distance four amot, etc.—in the Tur he wrote that a bad smell that has substance, such as human feces, one distances; and if it was behind him he must distance four amot, etc. The Rashba explained that a bad smell that has substance means exposed feces, and Beit Yosef brought it and wrote on it that it is simple. Perishah letter 1 wrote that his intent is when covered: even a smell that has substance does not require distancing for Kerias Shema except until the smell ceases, for since it is covered it no longer has substance—we call it thus. Proof from flatulence emerging from the body, which also comes from feces, but since the feces is covered in the body it has no substance—we call it thus. Likewise Beit Yosef at the end of this siman, and these are his words: in Orach Chayyim he wrote in the name of the Rashba that a bad smell without substance means covered feces—until here. However, the Rokeach, z"l, in siman 323 wrote, and these are his words: a bad smell that has substance, such as covered feces, and a wafting smell—one distances four amot from where the smell ceased and prays—until here; and Eliyah Rabbah brought it letter 10 and wrote, and likewise in Kesef Mishnah chapter 3 of Hilchos Kerias Shema din 12 in the name of R' Menachem, and likewise in the Rambam himself there, and Rashi, Tur, and other poskim—that a bad smell without substance is specifically flatulence emerging from the body. So it seems in Shulchan Aruch, since he did not write this law that covered feces is called without substance, and in this siman he mentioned only flatulence—until here; and likewise in siman 76 letter 1—see there. Likewise Chayei Adam klal 3 letter 22. Therefore, where possible one should be stringent and not read facing covered feces from which smell emerges, except with distancing of four amot from where the smell ceased; and if impossible, one may rely on the Rashba and Perishah who wrote that one need not distance except until the smell ceases. See also below letter 19.
