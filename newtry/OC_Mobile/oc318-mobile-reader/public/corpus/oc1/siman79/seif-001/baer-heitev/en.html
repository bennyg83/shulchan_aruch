The excrement. This implies that when no person is sitting within four amos of the excrement, it is permitted to pray, even though the excrement separates between them and the chazzan, since they can turn their faces away. Nevertheless, in siman 55, seif katan 20, some say that one should not answer Amen when excrement separates between them. The Taz writes that in the case of urine, it may certainly be permitted when the entire congregation distances itself four amos from it. See there. The Magen Avraham writes that if the chazzan is in Pesukei DeZimrah, where he is not discharging the congregation's obligation, he may recite in his usual manner, provided that he is more than four amos beyond the point where the odor ceases.
