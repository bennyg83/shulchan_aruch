As behind him—and the sides before him are considered as before him. Magen Avraham.

The feces—it implies that if no person sits within four amos of the feces, prayer is permitted even though the feces separates between him and the sheliach tzibbur; they may turn their faces. However, in siman 55 s.k. 20 some say one should not answer amen when feces separates between them. And Taz wrote regarding foot urine that it is fine to permit when the entire congregation distances four amos from them—see there. And Magen Avraham wrote that if he is in psukei dezimra where he does not fulfill others' obligation, the sheliach tzibbur may speak as usual if he is outside four amos from where the smell ceased—see there.
