From the place where the smell ceased, etc.—a dispute between Rav Huna and Rav Chisda. The Semag wrote that the halacha follows Rav Chisda, as it is taught in accordance with him. It is difficult: why was it necessary to teach in accordance with him? It should follow because it is stringent, as he himself ruled regarding feces on one's flesh above in siman 76 seif 4—it is also a dispute between Rav Huna and Rav Chisda.
