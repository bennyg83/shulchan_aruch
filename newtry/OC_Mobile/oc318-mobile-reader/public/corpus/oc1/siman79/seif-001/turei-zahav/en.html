Even at night. The Rosh, chapter "from when," brought from Avodah Zarah, Jerusalem Talmud: Rabbi Ilai and his colleagues were sitting before him in an inn at night. He said: What is the law to say here words of Torah? He told him: Since if it were day you would see what is before us, but as it is, it is forbidden. We learn from this that even other words of Torah are forbidden, not specifically Kerias Shema; and even in a place where it is doubtful whether there is feces there — namely before houses where feces is commonly found, and all the more so in a courtyard.

And it is impossible that there not be etc. of the feces — meaning from the place where the smell ceased [and I do not know why he did not write that one must distance as far as one can see]; but regarding urine it is properly permitted when the whole congregation distances from them four amos.
