With stolen matzah. When they bake many in one oven, they should say, "Anyone into whose hands my matzah arrives, it shall be his as a gift" (Beis Yosef). And it appears to me that it is good to say so at the time of milling, for sometimes the flour is exchanged. If he stole and gave it to another, that is shinui reshus and one fulfills with it (and specifically after ye'ush); and if the other returned it and gave it to the thief, the thief too fulfills with it (Maharil). And see what I wrote in siman 649 regarding theft from a non-Jew.
