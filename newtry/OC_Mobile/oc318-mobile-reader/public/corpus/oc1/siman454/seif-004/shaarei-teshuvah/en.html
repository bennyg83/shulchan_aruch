Stolen. See Baer Heitev, and see in Shaarei Teshuvah of the newer Bach and in the Kuntres Acharon there, that it is good to say, "Anyone who arrives," etc. See in Shaagas Aryeh, simanim 92 and 93, who discussed this at length, that even according to the one who holds that bererah does not apply for him, it works; and also one need not be concerned here because of what we say, "he stole and the owners have not despaired," etc., and this is because it is not in his possession, since it was exchanged by mistake; and further, there too it works for him to forgive and to give it as a gift to the thief himself. And see there, siman 94, whose view is that with stolen maror one fulfills, since maror nowadays is rabbinic, and we are not concerned for a mitzvah that comes through a transgression, except according to the Rama, siman 649, where we are concerned; and nevertheless with stolen matzah one does not fulfill on the second night of Yom Tov, even though it is rabbinic; see there. In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 1, "one does not knead," etc., even matzah that is not of the mitzvah, for the concern is of chimutz: the Rashbatz in Maamar Chametz and in his responsa, part 2, siman 87; and see there that he wrote that water drawn in a pool at night, one should not knead with it; and the Beis Yosef brought it.
In Shulchan Aruch seif 1, "one may not knead, etc." This applies even to matzah that is not mitzvah matzah, because the concern is chimutz; see Tashbetz in Ma'amar Chametz and his responsa part 2 siman 87. There he writes that water drawn from a pool at night should not be used for kneading, and Birkei Yosef cites him.
