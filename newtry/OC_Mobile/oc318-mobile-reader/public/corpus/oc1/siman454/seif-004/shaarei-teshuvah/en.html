Stolen. See Ba'er Heitev. See the new Responsa Bach and the kuntres acharon there, that it is good to say, "whoever it reaches..." See Sha'agat Aryeh simanim 92-93, who discusses at length that this helps even according to the view that there is no bererah, and there is no concern here because we say "stolen and the owners did not despair," since it is not in his possession after being exchanged by mistake. Moreover, even there, forgiveness and gifting to the robber himself helps. See siman 94, where his view is that one fulfills with stolen maror because maror today is rabbinic, and we are not concerned for mitzvah haba'ah ba'aveirah except according to the Rama in siman 649. Nevertheless, with stolen matzah one does not fulfill on the second Yom Tov night, although it is rabbinic.
In Shulchan Aruch seif 1, "one may not knead, etc." This applies even to matzah that is not mitzvah matzah, because the concern is chimutz; see Tashbetz in Ma'amar Chametz and his responsa part 2 siman 87. There he writes that water drawn from a pool at night should not be used for kneading, and Birkei Yosef cites him.
