In stolen matzah. In Ashkenazi he derives ch. 20 from that which they say regarding ma'aseh sheni one does not fulfill — R' Meir plainly; but regarding etrog not so plain, for one can say "for you" excludes borrowed and stolen; but ma'aser sheni not excluded — not so matzah where written twice "your doughs" — excludes also ma'aser sheni; if so all the more borrowed and stolen. Therefore we should say: just as stolen matzah invalid, so borrowed matzah — for regarding etrog they are compared; except truth is not so — etrog is different, for it returns in kind; even so in his hand not called his — general borrowing in his hand; but one who borrows matzah from friend intended to eat it and payment he owes is in place of it or another matzah — this is now truly his, only debt in hand to pay — like one who borrowed money from friend for expenditure and betrothed with it — surely betrothed, since money does not return in kind and he has money in hand; so in my humble opinion one fulfills with borrowed matzah; I do not know for etrog here; and it is a fortiori from stealing flour with guilt in hand:
