Stolen. But regarding theft Taz wrote one fulfills; and if he stole and gave to another, it is shinui reshus and one fulfills with it — specifically after ye'ush; and if the other returned and gave to the robber, the robber also fulfills with it — Maharil. One may not recite the blessing at the outset; Acharonim wrote it is good to say at grinding or baking: whoever receives my flour or my matzah — behold it is his as a gift. Kaf HaChayyim wrote specifically regarding stolen matzah one does not fulfill because the matzah itself comes from a transgression; not so if one carried out matzah from reshut hayachid to reshut harabbim on Pesach, for there it is transgression of carrying and not the matzah itself in transgression. Nevertheless one who carried them out — if it is forbidden for him to eat as one who cooks on Shabbat — see above siman 318 and siman 307. Kenesset HaGedolah in the name of Maharil wrote one fulfills with stolen maror — see there. Peri Chadash siman 475 s.k. 1 ruled that just as with stolen matzah one does not fulfill, so stolen maror; see responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 24.
