[1] Sovin etc. Magen Avraham: saw mursan called shafiryar does not chametz — but hard hull; wheat hull itself perhaps causes chimutz; impossible no wheat substance adheres. Chok Yaakov wondered his uncertainty — mishnah: do not soak mursan for chickens — causes chimutz siman 465. In my humble opinion Magen Avraham uncertain only wheat hull (sovin), as he wrote Rashi: sovin wheat hull at threshing, mursan remains in sieve; Rambam opposite per Ramban Shabbat ch. 7 — nevertheless no difficulty for Magen Avraham who wrote per Rashi only. Apparently difficult on Rashi Terumot ch. 11: mursan permitted, new sovin forbidden — if Rashi why mursan permitted and sovin forbidden if sovin inferior? Also difficult Rambam — if mursan inferior, mishnah Shabbat ch. 7 mursan and sovin do not join — Tosafot Pesachim 39 clear not taught so; can resolve first on Rashi: sometimes call sovin what is mursan and vice versa from above difficulty. Resolves Ramban Terumot did not bring Rashi, stam like Rambam; Shabbat like Rashi; stam ch. 2 Challah like Rambam because there sovin and mursan join — difficult per Rashi "this and need not say this"; Tosafot Yom Tov did not address. Practically: hard hull certainly causes chimutz; thick hull all new within thirty days causes chimutz; after thirty days no flour mixed per Ramban Terumot ch. 11; however Shibolei Leket 62 Rashi: also sovin causes chimutz.
