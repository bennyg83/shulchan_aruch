Mursan. See Baer Heitev, and see in the responsa Beis Efraim, siman 31, regarding a case where a husk of oat was found in a cooked dish and it is not known whether it is the upper husk that falls off at the time of threshing or the thick husk that falls off at the time of pounding: one should permit that case; and see there for the explanation of the words "sovin" and "mursin." And see in Tiferes Tzvi, siman 21, who wrote to be stringent with a husk like the Magen Avraham, lest a bit of flour remain there; but with a whole wheat kernel that is found, and the husk has softened and the inside has not softened, one should likewise be stringent, whether with barley or with wheat. And see there, for it implies that with a small amount of water one may be lenient; and this too requires further study, for they are not stringent about wetting even with a small amount, except that regarding a large amount they wrote that it is absolute chametz; see there. And see in Chok Yaakov and in the responsa Shevus Yaakov, siman 32, what he wrote to reply to what is written in Derech Chaim against the Chok Yaakov regarding sovin and mursan; see there. And see in the responsa Bach, siman 107, in the middle of the responsum, who wrote that the husk itself, which is the sovin, does not forbid, for it has no taste at all and is considered merely like wood and ash; and if because of the flour that adheres to the sovin, etc.; see there. And from this it appears that specifically sovin, to which flour sometimes adheres, is of concern; but when it is apparent to the eye that it is an actual husk with no adhesion of flour whatever, one need not be concerned. And if one were to say that perhaps at the time the husk fell a bit of substance was stuck to it and dissolved afterward in the dish, we do not establish a presumption of issur; and as is written in the responsa at the end of the Sefer Minchas Yaakov, regarding a milk dish in which a hollow bone was found: we do not establish a presumption of issur that perhaps there was marrow in it; see there.
