Sovin. Rashi explained: the husk of wheat that falls off at the time of pounding, and mursan remaining in the sieve; see there. It is somewhat implied that it can come to chimutz, since according to Rashi one fulfills the obligation of matzah with them. And one can say that since it is of a species that can come to chimutz, one fulfills his obligation with it. And I have seen those who rule that a husk they call "shprayr" does not cause chimutz; however, that is a husk of straw, but the husk of the wheat itself possibly causes chimutz, and also it is impossible that some substance of wheat did not adhere to it.
But if he kneads it, etc. And if he removed mursan from it and returned it into it and kneaded with it, one should be in doubt whether it joins toward a kezayit; see Yoreh De'ah siman 324, se'if 3.
