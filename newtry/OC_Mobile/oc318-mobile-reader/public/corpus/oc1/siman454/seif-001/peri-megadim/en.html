Not. See Taz: that is, even though it is fit for eating, like new [produce] that is fit for eating; see Magen Avraham (1). Or he removed sovin and mursan and returned them into it, and there is flour in it fit for human food: even so, since regarding challah, if there are not five quarters of flour aside from the sovin, he is not liable for challah when he removed the sovin and returned them into it; see Magen Avraham (2): for "lechem" is written, and this is not bread for most of the world; or "your doughs," etc. Further he learns matzah by a gezeirah shavah of "lechem" from there, that one does not fulfill with it. However, even when he removed sovin and returned them into them, and there is a kezayit of flour within the time of eating a pras, he fulfills with it; for taste-as-essence according to us is a matter of Torah; see siman 493 in the Magen Avraham (4) and in the introduction; and in Challah chapter 2, mishnah 6: he removed sovin and returned them into it: he is exempt when there is not the measure of challah aside from flour; and as the Bach wrote: but a kezayit within the time of eating a pras, he fulfills with it, as said; and see Peri Chadash.
