(1) One does not fulfill, etc.: because they are not called bread, and as is explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 325, that for this reason they are also exempt from challah.
(2) Sovin: that is, the husk that falls from the wheat at the time of pounding; and mursan is the fine husk that remains in the sieve after the flour has gone out; and some explain the opposite. And regarding soaking sovin and mursan in water, it will be explained below in siman 465, se'if 1; see there in the Mishnah Berurah.
(3) With the sovin and mursan that are in it: for so is the way of a poor person to eat his bread from flour that is not cleaned, and they also join toward the measure of a kezayit of matzah; and specifically if he did not separate them from it, but if he separated them from it and then mixed them back into the flour and kneaded them together, they do not join toward the measure of a kezayit.
(4) And he fulfills with it: and the same law applies, that one fulfills with bread made from grain that worms have eaten.
(5) That it is like an ashishah: and in the Gemara we say, "lechem oni" excludes ashishah, meaning a large loaf.
