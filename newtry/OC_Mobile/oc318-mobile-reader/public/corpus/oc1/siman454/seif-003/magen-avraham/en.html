For chalita. For they would chalut the dough in boiling water, and thereby it does not become chametz; and nowadays we are not expert in chalita. And so it is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 73 regarding liver. And apparently, just as he ruled there that after the fact it is permitted, so too if a piece of dough fell into boiling water at the time of their boiling and they removed it immediately, after the fact it is permitted. And so it is somewhat implied in the Tur, siman 461 and siman 467, who wrote that the wheat was in the water before boiling and became chametz, which somewhat implies that at the time of boiling it does not become chametz. Nevertheless one should not be lenient, since it is not found explicit in the posekim.
