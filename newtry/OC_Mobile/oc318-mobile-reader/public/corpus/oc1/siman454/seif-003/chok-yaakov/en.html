Chalita is forbidden. See in the Magen Avraham, who is in doubt whether it is forbidden after the fact or not; and my teacher and father-in-law the Gaon Moreinu HaRav Rabbi V. may his lamp shine, ruled to be lenient with a mixture of any amount, to permit in benefit or to keep it until after Pesach; and so he ruled as practical law regarding challah that they wanted to throw into the oven and that fell into a boiling cooked dish; and see above siman 465, s.k. 2.
