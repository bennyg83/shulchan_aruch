That it is not etc. Rama ruled like Rosh etc. if he mixed much mursan in it — his intent is nullified. Language of "mixed" implies he already removed sovin and returned and mixed them; also from writing "much mursan" implies more than its mursan; also if only its mursan it would not need say his intent is nullified, for such is not called complete bread; rather he mixed other much mursan — if so it must be he ate a kezayit of flour aside from mursan. To join mursan without that Magen Avraham is uncertain even a little mursan — i.e. its mursan — whether such joins. From this it implies they disagree. Peri Chadash wrote they do not disagree — also Rosh agrees to Rambam for matzah it requires meshumar l'shem matzah; he brings proof from challah of thanksgiving and nazir wafers made for themselves — we say daf 38b one does not fulfill, for matzah meshumar for sacrifice not for Pesach; all the more here not guarded except for dogs.
