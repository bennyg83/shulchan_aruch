Acharonim further wrote: one with stomach illness whose cure is chaluting barley or oats and placing on stomach — if this illness occurred on Pesach with danger, Israel may do via chalita — boil water well then put grains in — even with danger, wherever possible on lenient side do so; if no danger — forbidden even via chalita, for since not expert when chaluting there is concern it will be seen became chametz; if a non-Jew does from his own or Israel transfers barley before chalita — permitted even without danger; if possible chalut in fruit juice — permitted even by Israel without danger, for in fruit juice it does not chametz — see Biur Halacha.
