That this is not guarded for the sake of matzah. According to this, even if it is fine bread, since he did not have in mind food for a person, it is not guarded for the sake of matzah; but if at the time of kneading he had in mind that a person would eat from it, he fulfills with it even if it is coarse bread (so wrote the Rambam in his commentary on the Mishnah in chapter 1 of Challah). And the Rama ruled like the Rosh, who holds that the reason it is not called bread is if he mixed much mursan into it and it is not fit for shepherds, even if he had in mind to eat it, his intent is nullified. And even according to the Tur, Yoreh De'ah, who ruled that even if it is nice it is exempt, that is because it is recognizable on it that it is for dogs; and see there and in the Shulchan Aruch.
