(s.k. 2) But if he took etc. — see YD siman 324 s.k. 3 it is clear there that in such a case it does not join for challah; therefore Chayei Adam plainly holds also for matzah it does not join. Peri Chadash wrote in the name of Beit Mo'ed to the contrary that for matzah it joins; but Peri Chadash wrote on it: such is not the poskim' view. Magen Avraham's uncertainty: that which it does not join for challah — the reason is not because it is not bread, rather because it is written "your doughs" and such kneading is not the way — see there. If so one may say specifically for challah where it is written "first of your doughs" — not so for matzah where "your doughs" is not written — it joins; and although we derive matzah from challah via gezeirah shavah lechem lechem as in Pesachim daf 38 — nevertheless uncertain perhaps for this they do not learn from each other; specifically regarding challah whose obligation is at rolling the dough, and since then it is not called such dough — therefore it does not join; not so matzah which depends at eating, then it is bread — and such he distinguished above between challah and hamotzi blessing on dough that began as sfoganim and ended thick — see there in Rabbi Akiva siman 168:
