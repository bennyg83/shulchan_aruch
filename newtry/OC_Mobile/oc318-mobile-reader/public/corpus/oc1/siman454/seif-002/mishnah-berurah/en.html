(T) That this is not a guardian of the name of a cave – the tongue is not really cracked down, because it is not thought at all that a person has no bread at all, and even for the blessing of the groceries, but because of the arky for the Passover matter, it is no longer mentioned:

(10) Thus Rambam — Rav abbreviated; his intent: Rambam deals whether fine bread or bread mixed with much mursan; main dispute whether he thought only for dogs or also human eating. Rav says there is another view: not dependent on his thought — where much mursan mixed and not fit for shepherds to eat, there is no bread; even if he explicitly thought also for shepherds — nullified intent, for owners do not eat such bread.
