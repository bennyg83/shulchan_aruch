In the first three [blessings]. That which they mention resurrection of the dead six times — for four are considered as dead, see Talmud; and also natural death, and a wicked person is called dead in his lifetime. And that which is written "Who gives life to the dead in great mercy" indicates regarding amei haaretz that by law they would not have resurrection except by supporting talmidei chachamim in perakmatya — see chapter 13 of Kesubos. That which is written "See, please, in our affliction" is not redemption from exile, for it was already stated in the blessing of Boneh Yerushalayim and Tzemach David; rather, from frequent troubles — see beginning of chapter 3 of Megillah; and in the blessing Hashivenu it hints to this: "And I will betroth you to Me in righteousness and in justice and in kindness and in mercy," as written in the midrash: because Avraham did righteousness and justice, He gave his children kindness and mercy, as it says, "Execute justice and righteousness," etc.; and when they return to do righteousness and justice, etc., it says, "Zion shall be redeemed through justice" — the Holy One, blessed be He, adds for them mercy and kindness. And that which is written "Sound the great shofar" — that it is great in His eminence, that it is a sign of freedom for Israel and the return of the Land; and see in the omissions in Tur, Orach Chayyim, from siman 565 through 569 at length.
