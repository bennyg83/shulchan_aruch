(1) [Seif 1] One should not ask for one's needs in the first three [blessings], etc. — for R' Chanina said: the first are comparable to a servant who arranges praise before his master; the middle are comparable to a servant who requests a reward from his master; the last are comparable to a servant who received a reward from his master, takes leave, and goes his way — Berachos 34a; Tur and Levush; Orach Tzadikim, letter 1; Rebbe Tzaddok, letter 1.
