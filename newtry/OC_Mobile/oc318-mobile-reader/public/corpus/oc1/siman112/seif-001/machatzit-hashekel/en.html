That which is written in Shulchan Aruch to distinguish between individual needs and communal needs. Tur wrote that for this reason one does not ask in the first three and the last three. For the first three are comparable to a servant who arranges praise before his master; the middle are comparable to a servant who requests a reward from his master; the last are comparable to a servant who received a reward from his master, praises him, and goes his way. And for this reason there is no place to request needs in the first three and the last three.

For there is no relevance to asking within a narrative of praise. And for this reason communal needs are permitted, for this is praise and honor to the Master that many need Him, and it is included in the category of narratives of praise.
