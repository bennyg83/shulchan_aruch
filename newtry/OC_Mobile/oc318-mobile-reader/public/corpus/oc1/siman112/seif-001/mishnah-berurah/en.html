(1) One should not ask, etc. — for the first three, why are they comparable to a servant who arranges praise before his master; and the middle to a servant who requests a reward from his master; the last to a servant who received a reward from his master, praises him, and goes his way. And since the first are the arrangement of praise, therefore it is not fitting to ask one's needs in them; and the same applies to the last.

(2) It is permitted — for this too is a kind of praise and honor to the Master that many need Him.
