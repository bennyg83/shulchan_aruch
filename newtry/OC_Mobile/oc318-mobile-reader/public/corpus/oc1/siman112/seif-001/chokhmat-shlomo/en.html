Seif 1: One should not ask for one's needs, etc. Note: This law is from the Talmud, that one must arrange praise of the Omnipresent and afterward pray; and see the reason for this in the midrash on parashas Vayikra [end of chapter 5], where it is written: There is a woman who is wise to ask and there is a woman who is not wise to ask, etc.; there is a husband who is wise to ask and there is a husband who is not wise to ask, etc. — until R' Chiya said: David from Aram was wise, for he began saying, "The heavens recount the glory of Heaven," etc. — from there you will understand the reason why a person should arrange praise of the Omnipresent and afterward pray; see there. And I wrote at length in my composition on the Torah, parashas Vayikra, in the year 5548, on this; and part of the words of this midrash were brought in Tur, siman 562 — examine carefully.
