That which the author wrote regarding krovetz — Pri Chadash warned very much not to pass the time of Kriat Shema and prayer on account of this, and also not to converse; and see siman 68. Eliyah Rabbah, in the name of Rabbi Eliezer HaKallir, in Aruch, chapter Kallir: "ugah" (cake) — that an amulet was written in it and he ate and became enlightened. And that which we find on the second day of Pesach he composed a piyyut — one can say that he composed two piyyutim for day one, and the world divided and chose one for day one and one for day two — see there.
