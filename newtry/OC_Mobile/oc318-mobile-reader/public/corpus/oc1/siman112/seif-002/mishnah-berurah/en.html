(3) And there are those who permit, etc. — and in the responsum of Radbaz, part 3, siman 532, he also elaborated to permit (and he wrote that he had in hand some questions that one of the early authorities asked from Heaven through seclusion, prayer, and mentioning names, and they would answer him his questions; and he also asked about this and they answered him that it is permitted); and see there that he warned very much not to change the custom. And see in Bach, siman 68, who elaborated on this; and likewise in responsum Chavat Yair, siman 238, that one should say the piyyutim and not change.

(4) To say them — Pri Chadash warned very much not to pass by them the time of Kriat Shema and prayer, and one should not wait for them until after midnight — see there. And if he sees that the congregation will pass the time of Kriat Shema through their length, he should not wait for them; rather, once the shaliach tzibur begins Yotzer Or, he should begin for himself in a whisper to pray in order until after the fourth [blessing], "Elokeinu veElokei avoseinu," and there he should wait in silence and not speak even words of Torah until the shaliach tzibur reaches that place and he prays with him in order.
