(א) Seif 10 "and some say." Magen Avraham specifically, etc., and the explanation of the mishnah, etc.:

(ב) Some say that all, etc., and even, etc. For he holds that what is stated regarding an eruv chatzerot deals specifically with that, but in a house or where no eruv is needed it is permitted according to all, and even though there is no proof, nevertheless from what we say there 120 seif 1 "and there is no difficulty here for that," etc., and even though we do not rule like him in practice regarding that courtyard, nevertheless one can learn that there is a distinction between a courtyard and on account of concern of hotza'ah for Rosh Hashana:

(ג) And some say.
