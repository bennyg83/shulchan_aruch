(א) All rescue, etc. — whether rescue of food for three meals and his needed utensils, or rescue of garments; and even though he ruled above in siman 301 s.k. 36 that it is permitted to go out with two garments one on top of the other because it is the manner of wearing — here, since in rescue we are concerned, we are more concerned lest he forget it is Shabbat and come to extinguish; and those who are lenient do not hold this reasoning, that we are more stringent regarding garments here than generally; and therefore they are lenient, and regarding garments it stands; but regarding food for three meals or needed utensils — they too hold that it is permitted only to a joined courtyard; and the garments they permit specifically when he wears them in the manner of wearing, but when he carries them in his hand they are no better than other vessels:
