[7] And some are lenient, etc. The Bach limits this to garments and not food. It appears to me that utensils are likewise prohibited; the Semag writes 'food and utensils,' unlike the Shiltei Giborim's understanding. The Magen Avraham explains that the leniency rests on wearing in the normal manner, but this does not answer the Shiltei Giborim's precision from the Rambam. According to the Rambam one must likewise include garments, since the Semag rules in his name that garments are prohibited in a courtyard without an eruv. Since garments were mentioned first, the later reference to utensils also appears to be included in that prohibition. I resolved the difficulty above differently. For practical law, Nachalat Tzvi and the Perishah follow the lenient view, and in siman 340 I likewise demonstrated that this is the Levush's position.
