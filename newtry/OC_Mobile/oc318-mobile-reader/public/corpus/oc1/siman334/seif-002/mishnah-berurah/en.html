(א) There are those who permit — meaning specifically in another house where they are not agitated, and not in the house of the fire, as stated in seif 1:
(ב) To move, etc. — their reason is they learned from that which they permitted one who was darkened on the road to carry his pouch less than four amot so he not do a more severe prohibition and carry them explicitly; the same applies here — they permitted the prohibition of moving muktzeh so he not come to extinguish the fire; and likewise regarding compulsion they permitted so he not come to take them out to reshus harabim explicitly; and the reason of those who forbid is they hold that specifically regarding one who was darkened on the road they permitted him so he not do a great prohibition; but regarding compulsion there is nothing to be concerned about regarding taking out, for he will certainly not take it out before the compellers, for he fears to show them; and likewise regarding fire there is no concern lest he extinguish, since the fire is still in others' houses and he is not so agitated over them, as stated in seif 1; and see in Bach who ruled like those who permit [and several Acharonim cited his words] because there is concern, since a person does not place himself on his money — if they do not permit him he will think in any case I am doing a prohibition and come to bury them in the ground through digging and do a d'oraisa prohibition; and it is stated in Sefer Maharil in name of Maharash, and Eliyah Rabbah brought it, that at a time of alarm when they fear enemies will come and seize what is his — it is permitted for an Israelite to move his money, even outside the eruv — see there; and see above in siman 307 s.k. 33 in the gloss and Taz and Magen Avraham and Peri Megadim there that it is permitted only when one carries them not in the manner of carrying out — that is, between his garment and his flesh, and the like:
(ג) For in a place of loss, etc. — and all this is specifically on account of fire or bandits where he is greatly agitated; but if rain fell on muktzeh merchandise — certainly forbidden to move via an Israelite [so too Magen Avraham and Tosafos Shabbos, and so it appears in Biur HaGra]:
