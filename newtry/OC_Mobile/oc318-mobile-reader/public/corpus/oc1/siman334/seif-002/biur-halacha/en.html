(א) There are those who permit, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah and Eliyah Rabbah that from what he brought in name of Orach VeShalom it implies that even in the house in which the fire broke out it is permitted to save everything, even money; and therefore he holds like the view of Maharai, and they cited it in Taz, that regarding a matter very important to a person, such as money, one should be concerned for the opposite — that if they do not permit him to save them he will come to extinguish the fire; but in Neshmat Adam he expanded on this matter and showed that even Beit HaTerumot himself, who is lenient, is not lenient at all in the house in which the fire broke out; and also in Peri Megadim he concluded on the words of Orach VeShalom "requires further study"; and nevertheless we cannot protest against those who are lenient, since Taz too leans this way even though there is no source for this:

(ב) In a place of, etc., regarding the prohibition of muktzeh — from the words of Maharash that I brought in Mishna Berurah it appears at first glance that even regarding the prohibition of karmelit one need not be concerned then; but from the words of Sefer HaTerumah brought in Beit Yosef, and likewise from what is brought in Ran chapter Mi sheHechshich, it is proven explicitly that we permit this only to a joined courtyard; and likewise from the words of Taz and Magen Avraham above in siman 307 s.k. 33 it implies that we do not permit taking out beyond the eruv except when one carries them not in the manner of carrying out:
