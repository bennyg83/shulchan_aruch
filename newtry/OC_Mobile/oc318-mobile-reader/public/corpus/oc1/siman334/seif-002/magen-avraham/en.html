(ב) One need not be concerned. And that which we say — one forgot his purse in a courtyard — forbidden to move it even via a loaf or a child — that case is different there since he was negligent in forgetting, as is written siman 267, 268; and furthermore that he could sit and guard it or give it to a non-Jewish guard:

(ג) And some forbid. For specifically one for whom it became dark — permitted, so that he not come to a d'oraisa prohibition, as is written siman 266; unlike here where he fears to take them out from before him; and furthermore even if he takes them out there is no d'oraisa prohibition since we have no reshus harabim in our time (Tur); if so, one for whom it became dark is also forbidden in our time, and requires further study; and Bach ruled like those who permit; nevertheless it appears to me that specifically on account of bandits or on account of fire they permitted, for the matter is urgent and we are concerned lest he come to a d'oraisa prohibition; but if rain fell on muktzeh merchandise — forbidden for all to move via an Israelite; and so siman 308 seif 37; and in the gemara there regarding millstones; and so somewhat in Ran ch. 24; and so siman 338 seif 7 — that even fit fruits some forbid to cover, all the more so muktzeh fruits; and so in Rif Beitza — that which it says "likewise" — that even the airspace of bricks; and since the beginning teaches fit fruits etc., it implies that the beginning specifically fit fruits are permitted; and see other Acharonim seif 5; even though from the words of the questioner in Rashba it is not implied as I wrote — what the main point is:
