Some permit to move money, etc. This is the view of Sefer HaTerumah that Tur brings; and the other view is Tur who disagrees and holds they did not permit one for whom it became dark to move it less than four amot except so he not come to a d'oraisa prohibition, etc., to move it directly; but here where there is no concern he will come to a greater prohibition than this — he fears to take out from before them, etc.; and one cannot infer against Ba'al HaTerumah, for here too there is great concern of prohibition — lest he do some d'oraisa melacha to hide the money; and furthermore Tur wrote: and also in fire there is no concern lest he extinguish as long as it has not reached him. The language "and also," etc., implies he disagrees in both — meaning in coercion he holds like Tur that in every manner forbidden since there is no concern of great d'oraisa prohibition; and in fire there is concern of d'oraisa prohibition — meaning lest he extinguish — only this is in a house where the fire is, then permitted in muktzeh even much and we are not concerned lest he forget it is Shabbat, for here there are two opposing concerns — there is concern of intentional and perforce one must be lenient, and there is concern lest he forget and perforce one must be stringent; and it is more reasonable to be concerned for intentional since he is greatly agitated over his money he will extinguish intentionally than to be concerned if it were permitted lest he forget it is Shabbat on account of tircha of saving; but in adjacent houses certainly we are concerned lest he forget it is Shabbat since there is no other concern against it; and it appears for this reason Beit Yosef wrote in name of Mahari Abo that he deduced from Ran's words that Ba'al HaTerumah permits even in a house where the fire is and Tur did not challenge him except for other houses — for this is not comparable to a corpse where there is concern of d'oraisa prohibition; if so, this "some permit" is even regarding fire in every manner even in adjacent houses; and "some forbid" is Tur's view to forbid in fire in adjacent houses; but in a house where the fire is — permitted for the reason that if forbidden lest he extinguish like a corpse — and in coercion forbidden in every manner; and this distinction is in muktzeh items that are important and he is agitated over them like over a corpse, as siman 311; but in permitted things that are not so important — no concern of intentional at all, only concern lest he forget it is Shabbat; and perforce forbidden more than food for three meals — in my humble opinion thus; and so explained in Mordekhai's words that I brought siman this seif 10; and not as Maharach of blessed memory wrote that there is no posek who permits in a house where the fire is in a permitted and important thing that is not muktzeh — it appears that for all permitted even much in adjacent houses and not in the house of fire:
