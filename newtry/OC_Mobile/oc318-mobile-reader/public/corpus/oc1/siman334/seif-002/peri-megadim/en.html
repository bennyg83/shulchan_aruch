(א) There is, etc., in Tur he brings Beit HaTerumot's words that permit money and important items — forbidden moving muktzeh lest he come to a Torah prohibition in compulsion; and proof from his pouch in siman 266; and Tur wrote that here there is no Torah concern; and all the more for us who have no reshus harabim; and also regarding fire they did not decree lest he extinguish as long as the fire has not reached him. Beit HaTerumot permits in compulsion and fire — both in that house and in adjacent houses; and Tur argues in two ways: in compulsion there is no Torah concern; and in adjacent houses he is not agitated; and he conceded to him in the house where the fire broke out that he is agitated over an important matter like the pouch in Mi sheHechshich — permitted even much; for there are three concerns, each opposing the other: if they permit much he will forget and extinguish inadvertently; and if they do not permit he is agitated over an important matter and will come to extinguish intentionally — almost certainly; therefore they permitted much, even muktzeh, all that is important; but what is not important — neither agitated nor did they permit much permission, only two meals; but in another house Tur holds even a little muktzeh they do not permit (dealt with in siman 311 in Magen Avraham 3; and all this in that domain; and in Rambam's view — in another courtyard he is not agitated); and what Taz wrote in other houses — lest he forget, since there is no other concern opposing it — meaning there is no agitation in other houses and certainly they do not permit muktzeh, for he is not yet agitated; and all the more if they permit muktzeh he will forget Shabbat, seeing muktzeh moved, and come to do inadvertent Torah prohibition of extinguishing and the like (see letter 1, for Rashi and R' Shimon); however what he concluded in an unimportant matter in other houses — permitted for all, much permission — not in the house of the fire — I did not understand; in other houses it is obvious they are no worse than unimportant permission in other houses — permitted much as in seif 1; therefore one can say do not read "important — more troubled"; if you permit him it teaches us:

(ב) And what he wrote "and in that house forbidden" — requires study, for behold he wrote in that house even important muktzeh — permitted for all, even for Tur who permits; and one can strain there is a scribal error and emend "and not in the house of the fire" specifically; and one can say for practical law they did not permit in that house and rules like Beit Yosef here, for Tur argues even in that house; and Magen Avraham see letter 7 and there it will be explained.
