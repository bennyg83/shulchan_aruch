Seif 22 in the gloss: in a place of loss. In the simple sense it appears it refers to the entire seif, and even spreading a kid's hide so it will not actually extinguish but only not ignite further — nevertheless forbidden not in a place of loss; but from Magen Avraham's words siman 514 s.k. 4 it is clear he holds it refers only to the end of the law of new earthenware vessels that the fire will actually extinguish; but I did not know the necessity for this, for what Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 17 "and he said to him" — there it is on account of loss, for Ra'ash holds, etc. — and from this it implies that even without loss it is permitted in his view is not compelled; for the essence of Ra'ash's difficulty was on that tube where we decree lest he come to extinguish — Ra'ash holds that since we decree lest he come to extinguish it implies he holds uncertain extinguishing is full extinguishing d'oraisa, and since permitted in a place of loss — perforce from the Torah it is not extinguishing; and more so that if we say nevertheless they decreed even on rabbinic extinguishing — nevertheless the sugya in Shabbos 29 would be difficult: "if the permitted part is in these" — R' Yehuda said, etc. — why not in the simple sense that those who say rabbinic is permitted only in those two where even if he comes to extinguish it is only rabbinic that does not dim the light — therefore no decree lest he extinguish; but with a bowl where the wick head is inside, which dims the light and is extinguishing d'oraisa — they decree; a fortiori at the beginning it is also d'oraisa extinguishing — see Tosafos Beitzah s.v. one who is uncertain who wrote explicitly that grama extinguishing is forbidden; nevertheless even though there it does not actually extinguish but only not ignite further — and since from Ra'ash's words there is no proof to the contrary — therefore Rama's words stand in their simple sense that it refers to everything mentioned in this seif; examine well:
