(א) (s.k. 24) The margins cut, etc. — for if not cut — must say saved via the book:

(ב) Or erased, etc. — erased straightforward to him the gemara one does not save — initially this place was not sanctified except via the letters; and since letters erased — also sanctity of margin written on it goes; except margins from the side and similar that those margins were sanctified on their own — in this the gemara is in doubt if cut whether saved or not and not resolved; therefore suffices one does not save:

(ג) And if so one does not save. Meaning in it there is no doubt but certainly one does not save — for certainly not sanctified:

(ד) In new ones permitted, etc. — possibly in new ones there is heart of court stipulation on them — for in any case need to bind and cut margins to equal pages; but old ones already cut once — to cut again in this possibly no heart of court stipulation; but custom now also cuts old ones — must say heart of court stipulates each time need arises to cut what was need of the book — at minimum one should be careful in old books where they glue in torn place not to glue on words especially Divine Names:

(ה) And Taz wrote s.k. 16 in name of Bach those coins stamped with the four-letter Name heretics stamped for idolatry — forbidden to keep in one's possession rather melt immediately; and Taz added what is practiced in some communities when blessing sick and volunteering money for the sick placing money inside aron kodesh — there is additional prohibition forbidden to place mundane matter into aron kodesh which is lowering from sanctity — see there what he wrote regarding lowering from sanctity; see Magen Avraham siman 154 s.k. 14; and what he wrote in name of Bach regarding coins stamped with four-letter Name — wrote in responsum Chavot Yair siman 16 (page 26a), and this is his language: and according to this in coins stamped in lands of Sweden with four-letter Name there was doubt; nevertheless it appears to me they are permitted since stamped to circulate them and it is like writing the Name for mundane purpose — end of his words; behold opposite Bach's words; and perhaps there is to distinguish between coins stamped by kingdom about which Chavot Yair spoke in that responsum — and Bach deals with coins made by craftsman smith for private non-Jew; and therefore Bach wrote heretics stamped for idolatry; and also perhaps distinguish between unspecified non-Jew and heretic; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 481:
