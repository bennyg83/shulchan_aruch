[23] Except for water, etc. The Shiltei Giborim, page 145, writes that the Semag states the rule without distinction, and perhaps holds that here soaking is a manner of soiling rather than laundering. This is imprecise, because the Semag explicitly rules that with a clean garment even water is permitted, as the Beit Yosef cites in siman 302. In seif katan 25 there I further explained another difficulty with the Shiltei Giborim on page 161. His statement that this is the Rambam's view is also surprising because it contradicts what he himself writes there. Since the Shulchan Aruch says here that the second view appears correct, it is clear that he takes account of the prohibiting opinion in siman 302, seif 9. One can still say that he does so specifically here, because according to the first view even other liquids are prohibited due to indirectly causing extinguishing.
