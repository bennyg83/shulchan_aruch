Baer Heitev; and see in Dagul MeRevavah who wrote that if he transgressed only muktzeh tiltul one need not be so stringent, for at the beginning of this siman some say permitted to move muktzeh in fear of great loss; and tiltul of a lit lamp to save property is stricter than muktzeh tiltul where there is also concern of extinguishing; it appears to me to be lenient in a three-day fast, and perhaps also Maharam who was stringent on a forty-day fast — that deals when there was no fear of great loss:
