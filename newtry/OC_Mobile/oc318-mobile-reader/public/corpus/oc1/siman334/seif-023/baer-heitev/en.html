Intend. Meaning that he not run and jump and shake back and forth in order that it extinguish, rather he covers himself in its manner Bach; and so Taz raised; and he wrote shaking the cloak that fire seized in it in order that it extinguish is a complete prohibition and an Israelite should not do so — end of his words, see there; and likewise a sefer Torah that fire seized in it — he spreads it and reads in it; and if it was extinguished it was extinguished.
