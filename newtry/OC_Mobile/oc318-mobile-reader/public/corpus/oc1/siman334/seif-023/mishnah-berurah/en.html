(א) He spreads it — so it not be folded, lest the fire catch all of it through this [Taz]:
(ב) That he not intend for this — it is from Tur's words; and see in Taz who explained meaning he should not intend in his action to extinguish what was already lit, but that it not ignite more; and if it happened that thereby the light was completely extinguished he is not concerned, since he did not intend for this. And for practical law it appears from the words of the Acharonim that we adopt that even if he intends for this it is permitted, only he should beware not to run and jump and shake himself here and there so it be extinguished, or shake the tallit so it be extinguished — for all this is doing an act of actual extinguishing and is not grama extinguishing, and an Israelite should not do so; rather cover with the tallit in its normal manner. And likewise a Torah scroll that fire seized — spread it and read in it; and if it was extinguished, it was extinguished [Gemara]; and it is permitted to read to a non-Jew and he will extinguish it, as above in seif 18 and below in s.k. 26:
