And some say, etc. This is Tur who wrote in this — since he does not intend for that — from this Beit Yosef learned Tur's view that he must not intend to extinguish via this; and even though afterward he wrote permitted to place liquids on it, etc. — there it was only gerama of extinguishing, for behold he does no act on the body of the fire — rather when fire reaches the place of water it will not rule there and extinguish on its own; but here when he covers with a cloak he shakes the fire itself and thereby it is extinguished — forbidden if he intended for that; but other poskim hold even if he intended only to extinguish — permitted; and this is the dispute written here; and it is wondrous to me why forbidden according to Tur when intending so it extinguishes — behold the main thing the Sages gave advice to extinguish the light with this cloak; and furthermore it is difficult for me — if a cloak is more stringent than placing water, as Beit Yosef wrote — what does the gemara challenge from "a cloak that fire seized in it, etc." — meaning specifically in such a case permitted; but placing water not, as Beit Yosef wrote — on the contrary a cloak has more stringency according to Beit Yosef's words; and perforce the challenger challenges well; and furthermore it appears very wondrous what Beit Yosef wrote regarding a cloak that shakes the fire itself and thereby is extinguished — this is certainly forbidden that he do an act of extinguishing — for even opening a door is forbidden near a lamp, siman 277, lest wind extinguish it; and all the more he should not shake with hands to extinguish fire — what difference his intent; and in my humble opinion certainly forbidden to shake the lamp so it extinguishes — this is actual extinguishing; and here it deals with one who does not extinguish what burns before him — only he acts so the fire not increase, for via joining of the cloak the fire increases; and when he scatters the joining there will be no increase; and that which they mentioned in the gemara "he spreads and covers with it" — they did not mention covering in order that via it the fire extinguish — for they said there "and likewise a book that fire seized in it — he spreads it and reads in it" — and there certainly one cannot say that via reading it extinguishes — rather certainly the manner of the matter is he opens and places it — meaning he covers with it; and so in a book; if so it is literally like placing water on a garment not in the place of fire — nevertheless more lenient in water which is actual extinguishing; and perforce the challenger challenges well; and in this they said permitted to spread the cloak — and via this he scatters the joining and the fire will not go afterward to the place adjacent to it; and if it happened that via this the light was extinguished he is not concerned since he did not intend to extinguish the fire already lit — only that it not go further; and for this he said "since he does not intend for that" — meaning that what was already lit be extinguished; and there is no dispute at all between Tur and other poskim; and shaking the cloak that fire seized in it in order that it extinguish is a complete prohibition in my humble opinion and an Israelite should not do so — so in my humble opinion:
