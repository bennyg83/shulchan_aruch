[6] And if they do not wish, etc. Since they know he declared the property ownerless against his true wishes, returning it is conduct of a God-fearing person. A particularly pious person should not take payment, as stated in the Gemara. The Magen Avraham cites the Shiltei Giborim that if they already returned it and afterward request payment, they may not take it, since they can no longer say they acquired it from ownerless property, as in Choshen Mishpat at the end of siman 183. This can be rejected, because there he returned it without knowing of the mistake, and perhaps returned it expecting payment.
