(א) Amulets — the reason of the first view is that in amulets the reason of "et la'asot" does not apply, etc., for there is no Torah to study or pray from them; therefore it is forbidden to write them; and some hold since permission was given to write blessings, there is also permission to write amulets:
(ב) One does not save, etc. — it deals with a sick person who has no danger; and for a sick person who has danger one saves his amulet and takes it out on him in the manner of wearing [Peri Megadim]:
(ג) And some say they save — and the halacha is like the first view [Acharonim]; and nevertheless when there are Divine Names in them one can say they save [Peri Megadim]:
