Olas Shabbos writes that if a sack containing money is tied at its opening when enemies come to take it, one may cut it with a knife and break the knot to save it even on Shabbos, but may not untie the knot. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2, writes that one may be lenient only regarding muktzeh, not carrying through a karmelis, and only in a house near the fire, not the burning house; see there. All this follows those who permit. According to those who forbid, even moving muktzeh is forbidden.
