There are those who permit, etc. — as is written in letter 13 in Semag in name of Yerushalmi — permits via loaf and child muktzeh in fire like a dead person; and this is certainly even l'chatchila to place on Shabbat; and a loaf is nullified to the pouch — nevertheless permitted; all the more a ring; and Beit HaTerumot also holds this Yerushalmi; and nevertheless he wrote a ring is forbidden — meaning possibly to a courtyard that is not joined; for if the money were nullified to the ring it would be permitted to a courtyard not joined — see seif 10 in Taz and Magen Avraham 7, 14; except the ring is nullified and is muktzeh and they did not permit like a dead person in fire except to reshus harabim not to a courtyard that is not joined; but according to Taz and Magen Avraham there — requires study; and there it will be explained further in Magen Avraham letter 24 and 21.
