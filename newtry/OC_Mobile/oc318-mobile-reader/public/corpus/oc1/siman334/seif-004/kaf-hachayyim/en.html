4) There: In the morning, food for two meals. It is obvious that one may save two whole loaves for each meal. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2. Regarding beverages, Tosefes Shabbos writes that even where wine is very common, one may save no more than enough wine for three meals, because we follow the majority of the world. Other beverages, however, may plainly be saved in the amount needed to drink throughout the entire day; see there. See below at the beginning of siman 335.
