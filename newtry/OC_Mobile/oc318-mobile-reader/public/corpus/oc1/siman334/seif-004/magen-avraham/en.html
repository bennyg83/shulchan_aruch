(א) From Shabbat to Yom Kippur. Such as when Yom Kippur falls on Sunday — for he does not eat until evening, and in the evening he would toil and bring; and Tosafos wrote that Yom Kippur itself — to feed his children — likewise forbidden to save; it appears to me that even though Yom Kippur and Shabbat are not adjacent to each other they wrote this law to teach us that if Yom Kippur is on Thursday and he knows he will not find to buy on Friday — we save:
