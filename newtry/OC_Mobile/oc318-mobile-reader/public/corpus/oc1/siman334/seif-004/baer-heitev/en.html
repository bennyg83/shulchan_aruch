For Yom Kippur. Such as when Yom Kippur falls on Sunday — for he does not eat until evening; and in the evening they would toil and bring; one can say that even though Yom Kippur and Shabbat are not adjacent they wrote this law to: if Yom Kippur is on Thursday and he knows he will not find to buy on Friday — we save.
