(s.k. 4) He saved, etc. — members of his household permitted, etc.; and so Rabbenu Yehuda wrote, etc. — for what was not permitted to him except three meals — the reason since a person is agitated in saving he will forget it is Shabbat and come to extinguish; and since not permitted except food for three meals that each needs on Shabbat — tell him recognition and he will not extinguish; therefore even if one would say what will he eat — nevertheless permitted since in any case he has recognition:
