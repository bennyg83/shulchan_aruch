(א) From Yom Kippur to Shabbat — for on Shabbat day he cannot prepare; unlike from Shabbat to Yom Tov, for he can prepare on Yom Tov itself; and likewise from Shabbat to Yom Kippur; and for example when Yom Kippur falls on Sunday — likewise forbidden, for he does not eat until evening at Motzei Yom Kippur, and in the evening he will trouble himself and bring; and even though for us Yom Kippur and Shabbat are not adjacent, Shulchan Aruch wrote this seif to inform us that if Yom Kippur falls on Thursday and he knows he will not find to buy on Shabbat on day six — they save:
