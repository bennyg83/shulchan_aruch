[12] Ashurit script, etc. The Magen Avraham argues at length that the Mordechai prohibits rescue specifically when written with sam or sikra, because one may not read such a text where ink is common, while a text written in any language on paper may be rescued even if it is a Megillah. In a small Mordechai manuscript on parchment I saw no mention of sam or sikra, only that it was not written according to its required form, and so too it appears in the large Mordechai. It appears to me that if there is a difficulty, it is this: perhaps the Mordechai discusses the original Gemara law, or teaches that other sacred books too are invalid when not written in ink, while the Gemara discusses only rescue, as appears from the Agudah. But nowadays, when all sacred books may be read, even a Megillah written with sam or sikra may be read. None of the halachic authorities distinguishes for us between languages or types of writing except regarding public reading. See the previous seif. So too it appears from Shiltei Giborim in Perek Kol Kitvei. He further writes that according to the Rif and Rambam, even under the original law a Megillah may be read in any language and if written with sam or sikra. I am astonished, for on Shabbos 115 Rav Ashi says the rule follows the Mishnah in Megillah that it must be written according to its required form. Yet in Tosefta, chapter 17, I found explicit that even a Megillah written with sam or sikra is rescued. Perhaps the Rambam understood 'if it was taught, it was taught' as referring to that Tosefta and did not have the continuation attributed to Rav Ashi. This resolves Tosafot's questions there. Incidentally, the Gemara itself raises a difficulty: what was the Exilarch asking if he knew the baraita? Rashi writes, 'But Rav Hamnuna already said, a tanna taught, etc.' It appears to me that Rav Hamnuna was an amora, not a tanna, so how can the Gemara say 'the tanna Rav Hamnuna'? Maharshal in Chochmat Shlomo deletes the word 'said,' but this requires study. Also, according to his text, what did Rashi add?
