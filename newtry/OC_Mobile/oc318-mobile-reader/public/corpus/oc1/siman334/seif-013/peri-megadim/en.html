Tefillin, etc. — Bach challenged that Beit HaTerumot holds money is permitted; and one can answer it deals in that house or to a courtyard that is not joined as in seif 17; and letter 2 implies Beit HaTerumot permits even in that house at the time of placing; except Taz concluded there in that house forbidden; and Magen Avraham letter 14; and what he wrote there is no dispute — Magen Avraham letter 24 from this; and a ring in a pouch full of money — one does not save, for the ring is not as important as money and it is muktzeh — dealt with in siman 310; and it also deals in that house where fire broke out — if so for Beit HaTerumot permitted like another house according to one view; but if in another house it would be permitted via a ring except it is muktzeh on account of money and forbidden according to view 1 who did not permit muktzeh; and Magen Avraham further on this; and there it will be explained.
