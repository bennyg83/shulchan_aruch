(ב) As required, etc. — and in Magen Avraham he concludes that all this is only in Talmudic times, when it was forbidden to save it unless written as required, since it was not given to write and study in it; but nowadays, since on account of "et la'asot laShem" it was given to study in it even on paper and in every language — automatically they save it from fire as well, like all holy writings; and only regarding if it was written in the holy tongue and not in ink but only in dye and red paint there is a difference: in all holy writings, even if written in dye and red paint, for one does not read in them [because ink can be bought easily, unlike dye and red paint, and "et la'asot" does not apply here] — nevertheless one saves them from fire on account of the Divine Names in them; unlike Megillah, which has no Divine Names — if written in dye and red paint one does not read in it and one does not save it from fire; and all this in the twenty-four books of Scripture; but other books — it is obvious they are written in dye and red paint, whether written in the holy tongue or in every language:
