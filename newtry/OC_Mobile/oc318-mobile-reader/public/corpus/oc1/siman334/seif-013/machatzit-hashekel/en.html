(א) (s.k. 13) And it is not, etc. — from hefker they acquired — meaning for if they wished they could acquire all the things since it is hefker; only since they know not with his will he declared hefker — they did not wish to acquire; nevertheless for wages they acquired from hefker — not from the law of hire but from the law of acquisition from hefker; for according to known wage measure it is pleasing to the homeowner that it be hefker, as Magen Avraham wrote afterward:

(ב) As is written siman 307 seif 4. One who hires workers to guard seeds does not give sachar Shabbat even though guarding seeds has no melacha prohibition — nevertheless called sachar Shabbat:

(ג) As is written siman 266. One for whom it became dark erev Shabbat on the road with money — on account of loss they permitted him shevut — for if we do not permit the shevut since a person is agitated over his money he will carry it four amot and do Torah prohibition; but if he found a lost object — shevut was not permitted since it has not yet come to his hand he is not agitated and no need he do Torah prohibition — likewise here if the Sages were permitting shevut — namely according to Ben Beteira who holds daf 120 permitted to save even to a courtyard that is not mixed — then specifically for himself permitted on account of loss; and likewise others if they saved for the homeowner's need — permitted even to a courtyard that is not mixed; but if they wish to acquire from hefker for themselves and others — it is like a lost object that has not yet come to hand and shevut was not permitted for their own need — if so l'chatchila not permitted to save and acquire from hefker even for their wages to a courtyard that is not mixed:

(ז) (And see in Sefer Ateret Rosheinu) and in siman 122 who wrote one who gave permission to one to collect his debt for him — if he wrote in the power of attorney "all who are exempted from judgment upon me return" — there are two practical differences — if he did not write thus the authorized agent could acquire the money received from the borrower for himself; unlike when he wrote thus he is not permitted to withhold for himself; another practical difference — via this writing he must return to the agent all expenses he spent on this; and Semag challenged — all the more if he did not write thus he must return his expenses; for if the agent wishes he can acquire the entire debt — all the more he can acquire the money received for the expenses; and one can say practical difference if the agent already returned the money to Reuven and afterward claims expenses — he can no longer acquire in the money since he already returned to Reuven; nevertheless if he wrote in it "all who are exempted," etc. — must return the expenses; and it is literally the case here:

(ח) As is written in Choshen Mishpat siman 264 — if one did good for his fellow and his fellow did not command him — nevertheless must pay him at least according to what he benefited; and specifically, etc. — permitted to accept wages — so Rambam? It is a mistake — rather they are Shulchan Gavoah's words; and the matter is not explicit in Shulchan Gavoah except the implication of his words thus according to Magen Avraham's understanding; and possibly meaning Rambam according to what Rambam wrote who gave reason there is no sachar Shabbat here since there is no melacha, etc.; and according to Maggid Mishneh as stated; and Magen Avraham's intent — all the time it is in others' hands whether they saved permissibly or in violation he can say I acquired for myself for my wages from hefker — for even if he did violation nevertheless he acquired it as Magen Avraham wrote above; but regarding l'chatchila there is a difference — where there is no violation he can l'chatchila save for himself from hefker for his wages; but where there is violation he is not permitted l'chatchila to save for his own need as stated; but b'dieved if he says I acquired for myself in violation he acquires; and all this while still in his hand; but if already returned — if there is violation even if the owners wish to give him the measure of his wages — we say forbidden for him to accept — for we are witnesses since he returned that his intent was not initially to save for his own need since there is violation — if he were saving for his own need it would be that all the time it was in his hand he would be believed that he did violation and saved for his own need since he was presumed; but when he returned — there are indications that he returned and also he is not presumed — we establish him in presumption of integrity until now that he did not do violation and did not save for himself; and now he turns back; but when he saved permissibly — meaning he cannot extract from owners in court since they are presumed — the owners can say from when did you save for yourself and there are indications he returned to me — presumptively he did not save for himself; nevertheless if the owners wish to give him willingly — permission is in his hand to accept; however in my humble opinion for us there is no practical difference whether he saved in violation — if he saved food or vessel to a courtyard that is not mixed — why said certainly he did not save for himself so he not do violation — and what difference between saving for himself or saving for the homeowner's need — for according to what we rule like the first Tanna of Ben Beteira even for the homeowner's need forbidden to save to a courtyard that is not mixed; and since either way he did violation — why not believe he did violation to save for himself; and according to Pri Megadim who clarified Rama — even to one that is not mixed — behold specifically in garments and manner of wearing — then there is no violation even if he saved for himself; and likewise holy writings permitted to save even to one that is not mixed also saving for himself — there is no violation — for behold the reason of permission in holy writings is not on account of monetary loss but on account of disgrace of holy writings; if so what difference whether saving for the homeowner's need or for his own need — in all cases there is reason to permit; therefore you will not find it except for poskim like Ben Beteira who even food permitted to save to one that is not mixed — on account of monetary loss the Sages permitted; if so when saving for the homeowner's need to one that is not mixed he did no violation — for this the Sages permitted; unlike when saving for himself — for regarding himself since he has not yet acquired it shevut was not permitted as Magen Avraham wrote above — and he did violation then — properly one can say we are witnesses he was in presumption of integrity and did not do violation to save for himself; therefore if he saved food or vessels not in the manner of wearing he did no violation to save for himself and certainly saved for the homeowner's need — for he did no violation the Sages permitted him; and therefore if he returned to owners even if owners wish to give him for his wages — we do not allow him; but for us there is no practical difference and Magen Avraham did not need to conceal his words so much; and also Terumat HaShulchan copied all Magen Avraham's words — it appears he holds the law is thus; and in my humble opinion it appears as I wrote — for us there is no practical difference:
