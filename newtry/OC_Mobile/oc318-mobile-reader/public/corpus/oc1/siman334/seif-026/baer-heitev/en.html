(א) The extinguisher. Even his hired servant for a set time Gemara Beit Yosef; but forbidden to tell a non-Jew to extinguish except holy writings which is permitted Magen Avraham — see there; a corpse and books — doubt which to save first — a definite one and a doubtful one — the definite one first. Sha'arei Chayim siman (523) [5724]:

(ב) He transgressed and desecrated. That is in other matters; but in extinguishing — no need for repentance that they not prevent him from extinguishing. Shulchan Gavoah, Knesset HaGedolah:

(ג) To fast. Even for techumin d'rabbanan; and he can postpone it to winter days, as is written siman 571 seif 3; it is in Shabbat 12 that if one transgressed a sin for which he is liable a chatas he writes it in a notebook that when the Temple is rebuilt he will bring it:

(ד) Wine. It appears he speaks of the night after the fast, as we find regarding the night after Tishah BeAv siman 558. Taz; and see siman 134 seif 1:

(ה) Eighteen peshitim. They are eighteen viner. Minhagim Laws of Rosh Chodesh Elul; and in Levush siman 568 he wrote they are like a grosz and a half Polish; and see Magen Avraham; Sha'arei Chayim wrote one desecrated Shabbat and borrowed money — the sage told him: is it money or worth money?
