Because it is forbidden to eat before one gives food to one's animal; and therefore one may also save for an animal food for three meals. Tosafos Shabbos note 1; and likewise in Sefer Tiferet Yisrael in wondrous questions on Mishnayot Perek 16 of Shabbos note 2 — see there; and likewise Maor VaShemesh and Beit Din; Piskei HaDat note 2 wrote this is a homiletical method of drash, but per the law one may not permit saving for an animal three meals if it is not accustomed to eat three times a day but only according to its eating measure that day and no more; and what he wrote "forbidden to eat before giving to one's animal" — when both are hungry he brought proofs for his words — see there.
