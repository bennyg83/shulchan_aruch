3) There: And what is fit for an animal, for the animal. Since one may not eat before feeding his animal, he may also save three meals of food for the animal. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1. Tiferes Yisrael writes the same in Kuntres Niflaos on the Mishnah, chapter 16 of Shabbos, number 42; see there. Mor U'Ketziah writes likewise. Pischei HaDevir, paragraph 2, cites him and writes that this is a homiletic interpretation. In law, one may not save three meals for an animal that does not ordinarily eat three times a day, but only the amount it eats that day. The rule forbidding a person to eat before feeding his animal applies when both are hungry. He adduces support for this; see there.
