(א) He saved bread — and even if he saved coarse bread, the members of his household may also each save only food for three meals, even though they can eat from their own; for to each one they permitted saving three meals; and likewise Rabbeinu Yehuda: even if he has what to eat he may save [Magen Avraham]; and likewise even if he is fasting on Shabbat as a Torah scholar he may save, for Chazal did not distinguish [Tosafos Shabbos]:

(ב) Coarse bread — for there is no applicability of trickery to say "coarse bread is fine for me":

(ג) Permitted — because he can say on account of my agitation I forgot and took coarse bread, but in truth I want to eat clean bread; and even l'chatchila he may save coarse bread and afterward clean; and the same applies to meat and fish or other two species — even if he already saved one species he can say regarding the second species "I prefer" [Acharonim]:
