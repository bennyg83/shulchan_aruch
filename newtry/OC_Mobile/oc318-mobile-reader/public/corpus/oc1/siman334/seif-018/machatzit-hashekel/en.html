(א) (s.k. 18) Ashurit, etc. — this is, etc.; and also Tosafos so too; and it is difficult, etc. — need to present briefly Tosafos' words — in first chapter of Megillah the Sages and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel dispute — Sages hold books are written in every language; Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel requires specifically holy language except sefer Torah permitted to write also in Greek and not another language; and we learned ch. 22 all holy writings one saves from fire; and they dispute if written in another language that is not holy language — if one saves it from fire; and for Sages who hold written in every language and read in them — straightforward one saves; only for Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel who holds in other languages not written and if written in another language not read in them — if nevertheless one saves from fire — R' Huna said one does not save since one does not read in them; and Rav Chisda said one saves on account of disgrace of holy writings; and they conclude there tannaim dispute in dispute of R' Huna and R' Chisda; and they say there the Exilarch asked Rabba bar R' Huna — if written in holy language in ink and red paint — if one saves; and the question was asked both according to R' Huna and R' Chisda — for R' Huna although he holds if written in another language one does not save — nevertheless if written in ink and red paint since written in holy language it is preferable and one saves; or according to R' Chisda who holds if written in another language one saves — nevertheless if written in ink and red paint even written in holy language it is inferior and he agrees one does not save; and Rav Ashi brought a braita from which we hear one saves — for it teaches there is no difference between books and Megillah except books are written in every language and Megillah until written Ashurit on parchment and in ink — implies books do not require Ashurit and ink and one saves; and Tosafos there daf 115b s.v. Megillah, etc. — they challenged — proven from tractate Megillah that also other books require parchment and ink; therefore Tosafos ruled this braita regarding saving was said — other books even written in other languages and not on parchment and ink — nevertheless one saves; but Megillah one saves only in Ashurit writing on parchment and ink; and reason to distinguish between them — since also books require parchment and ink like Megillah — why in other books even written not on parchment and ink nevertheless one saves and not Megillah; and they resolved — books are different on account of Divine Names in them one saves; and wrote in Sefer HaTerumah; and you should challenge from braita here against what was said above "wherever not given to read one does not save" (meaning books one does not read if not written on parchment and ink and nevertheless one saves; and even though in this the Exilarch was in doubt if written in ink and red paint preferable — R' Huna agrees one saves if written in holy language — for superiority of language causes permission to save; but in braita that teaches regarding saving other books written in every language not on parchment and not in ink — you should challenge it comes according to Sages against Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel — books if written in other languages one reads in them — nevertheless if written in other languages and ink and red paint — in any case there is no superiority of holy language and since one does not read — if so according to R' Huna one does not save; and behold braita teaches one saves) — and one can say this concludes above it is a tannaitic dispute — end of Tosafos; and according to R' Huna whom we rule like him — if written in ink and red paint (even written in holy language) according to him the question is not resolved — for this braita holds not like R' Huna; and in every case where one does not read nevertheless one saves; if so R' Huna who holds like the other tanna who thus one does not read one does not save — the Exilarch's doubt remains if written in holy language and ink and red paint if one saves; and doubt fitting to be stringent; and so in Chiddushei Rashba — for Tosafos the question is not resolved according to R' Huna:
