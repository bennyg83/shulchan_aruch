[19] A partition, etc. This implies specifically a vessel separating between the water and the fire. A partition made directly of snow or hail is prohibited. See siman 265 in Magen Avraham, seifim ketanim 8 and 10, and seif 24 here.
[20] In a case of loss, etc. Olat Shabbos questions the Rama because at the end of siman 265 he permits even without loss. There is no difficulty, because there the case is before Shabbos. See above.
[21] [Levush] And some say that certainly when he intends, etc. The first view is primary. Nevertheless, one should not run, jump, or shake the garment in order to extinguish the fire; rather, he covers himself normally. Likewise, if a Sefer Torah catches fire, he spreads it out and tears it, and if the fire goes out, it goes out, as stated in the Gemara.
[22] [Levush] And it is not an inevitable consequence, etc. The Shiltei Giborim holds that even an inevitable consequence is permitted when extinguishing occurs only indirectly.
