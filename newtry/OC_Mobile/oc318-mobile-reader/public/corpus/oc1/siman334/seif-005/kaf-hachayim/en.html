And see in Machatzit HaShekel note 1 who wrote that even though he already ate something in the morning he may save two meals, for that morning eating is not the main meal and the main meal is at noon — see there; and Piskei HaDat note 1 agreed with him and wrote from this great ones relied on those accustomed on Shabbat day: when they leave the synagogue in the morning they eat a casual meal with haba bekisnin bread without netilat yadayim, and the one who takes out and blesses after it "al ha-michya," and at noon they set a table and establish there a second meal; except afterward he brought the view of Peri Megadim and Maharil that it must be done immediately after prayer; and he concluded that for every custom there is support; only one who eats casually in the morning should beware not to fill his stomach with delicacies and afterward not be able to make the main meal at noon; and if he cannot restrain himself he should change his custom and establish the meal in the morning and eat delicacies to satiety:
