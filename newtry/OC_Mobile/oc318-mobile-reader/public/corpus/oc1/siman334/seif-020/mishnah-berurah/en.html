(50) The margins — that were cut from the book, or the book was erased and fewer than 85 letters remained — for its sanctity of the parchment beneath it is gone, and also of the margins around it. And Maharam wrote in a responsum there was an enactment in Crete not to cut a book margin even to write on it; and in Mishna Berurah siman 100 he expanded on margins of books that binders cut and discard; and the reason is since they are accustomed thus it is as if they stipulated initially — see siman 154 seif 8; and if so one does not save them; and in Agudah Moed Katan chapter 6 one who cuts in new books — permitted; in old books — forbidden [Magen Avraham]; and the reason: in new books it is as if stipulated initially, and ordering too is not significant; but when they already learned from them, even the margins were sanctified with the sanctity of the books:
