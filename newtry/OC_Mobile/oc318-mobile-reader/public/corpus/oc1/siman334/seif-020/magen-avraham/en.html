The margins. That were cut from the book or the book was erased, as is written end of seif 12; and Maharam wrote in responsum that they enacted with a ban not to cut a book margin even in order to write on it; and in Mishna Berurah siman 100 he expanded on book margins that bookbinders cut them and discard them; and the reason — since they practiced thus it is as if they stipulated on them from the beginning; see siman 134 seif 8; and if so one does not save them; and in Agudah Moed Katan ch. 6 he wrote: one who cuts in books — in new ones permitted, in old ones forbidden:
