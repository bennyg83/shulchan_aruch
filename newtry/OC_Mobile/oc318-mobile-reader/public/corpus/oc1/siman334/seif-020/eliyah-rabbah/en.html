[18] The margins, etc. This means margins cut from a book or a book whose writing was erased, as at the end of seif 12. Maharash writes in a responsum that a ban was enacted against cutting the margin of a sacred book even to write on it. The Agudah writes that cutting new books is permitted but old books is prohibited. Mabit writes that bookbinders cut and discard margins because it is as though this was stipulated from the outset, as cited by the Magen Avraham.
