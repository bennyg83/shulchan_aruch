(א) (s.k. 20) And some say — and it appears to me there is no dispute here — meaning not as Rabbenu Yehuda understood that there is dispute among poskim he brought in name of Mordekhai who wrote forbidden to place tefillin with money on Shabbat day even to save them from fire and from thieves; but from Semag's language it appears even if tefillin were placed on Shabbat permitted — for Tur wrote, and this is his language: Ba'al HaTerumah wrote if he placed tefillin in a purse full of money he can save it like law of tefillin; and since he did not write if he placed them erev Shabbat — implies even if placed on Shabbat permitted to move the money — not like Mordekhai; and therefore Shulchan Aruch wrote two views; and on this Magen Avraham wrote there is no dispute between them as will be explained:

(ב) To save the money (to a courtyard that is not mixed, etc.; and Bach did not understand thus, etc. — they contradict each other) — what is printed in two half-circles is a separate matter as is customary; and Magen Avraham comes to resolve Bach's difficulties according to Rabbenu Yehuda's understanding that there is dispute among poskim whether permitted to place tefillin with money on Shabbat — according to this Bach challenged the two seifim contradict each other — in this seif he brought two views whether permitted to place them on Shabbat; and in the seif after he wrote some permit to save, etc., via loaf and child, etc.; and since language "some permit" is because some say they did not permit via loaf and child except for a corpse alone — and certainly placing loaf or child is on Shabbat like holy objects mentioned by a corpse — deals on Shabbat; if so in any case there is dispute whether permitted to do thus on Shabbat via placing tefillin — all the more via placing loaf and child on Shabbat — forbidden for those who forbid with tefillin; and for this Magen Avraham explained — in this seif deals with wanting to save money via tefillin like law of tefillin to a courtyard that is not mixed — in this poskim dispute whether permitted to place on Shabbat; but in the seif after deals with wanting to save to reshus hayachid or mixed courtyard only the money is muktzeh — therefore wanted to move via loaf and child — in this all agree even to place on Shabbat permitted except there are disputants who did not say loaf, etc., except for a corpse alone:

(ג) For behold it is in the gemara (daf 117a) that by law was, etc.; and as is written end siman 310 — even though we rule if forbidden object and permitted object placed together permitted to move the vessel that is basis for forbidden and also for permitted; nevertheless if possible to shake the forbidden from the vessel one must shake; and therefore Mordekhai wrote one should place them erev Shabbat — comes to exclude not to place them on Shabbat at time of fire; but agrees if placed there before fire and afterward fire fell — permitted to save money via tefillin; and also those who permit do not permit to place on Shabbat at time of fire; and therefore Tur was unspecified in name of Ba'al HaTerumah and wrote if he placed tefillin, etc.; and did not write if he placed tefillin erev Shabbat — for in truth even if placed on Shabbat permitted; only behold forbidden to place at time of fire; but placed on Shabbat not at time of fire — permitted to save via them also the money; and this Mordekhai also agrees permitted and there is no dispute here:
