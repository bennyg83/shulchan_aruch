The laws of a fire on Shabbat. And in it
27 seifim: If a fire broke out on Shabbat — if it is at night before the meal, he may save enough food for three meals: that which is appropriate for a person for a person, and that which is appropriate for an animal for an animal; in the morning, food for two meals; at minchah, food for one meal.
