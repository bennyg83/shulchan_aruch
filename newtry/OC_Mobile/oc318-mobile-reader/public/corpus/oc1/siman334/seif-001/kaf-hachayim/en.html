A) [Seif 1] If it is at night before the meal, etc. And all that is before eating — even at the end of the night he may save food for three meals; unlike the morning, which is specifically before the meal, meaning the time of the meal until the end of the sixth hour; but after the sixth hour its time has passed and he does not save two meals even though it is before eating. Bach; however Orach Mishpat note 1 wrote on Bach's words "requires further study"; and likewise Machatzit HaShekel note 1, for behold three meals are obligatory even though their time has passed, as is written siman 291 — see there.
