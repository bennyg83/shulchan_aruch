(א) What Shulchan Aruch wrote — fire fell, etc., for food for three meals:

(ב) Terumat HaShulchan wrote that likewise permitted to save lechem mishneh for each meal; and it is not comparable to above siman 294 — for here he does no prohibition at all; we are concerned from the side of agitation he will forget and extinguish; and they made an enactment that he save only for three meals; at minimum what is needed for each meal permitted to save (and see s.k. 4); and in drink — all other liquids — all he needs he saves, for there is no fixed amount for drinking; but wine he may not save more than the need of three meals even in a place where most of their drinking is wine — see there:

(ג) Magen Avraham s.k. 1 — some permit. Specifically in another house as is written seif 1 Bach; in a house where the fire is — not more important muktzeh money than permitted things — permitted only food for three meals needed for Shabbat; and the reason — if we do not permit him to save — tell him lest he desecrate Shabbat intentionally; and if we permit him to save — tell him from tircha he will forget it is Shabbat and desecrate Shabbat; therefore better not to permit him to save so he not desecrate inadvertently — more fitting to watch he not come to inadvertence than he not come to intentional — for one who desecrates intentionally is wicked and rebellious and we are not responsible for rebels; and better to save him from error; unlike in a house where there is no fire — there he is not agitated as Shulchan Aruch writes — then we permit to save so he not desecrate intentionally since there is no inadvertence since he is not agitated — fitting to save also the intentional; if so, those who forbid — that is Tur who forbids even in another house — not like Rabbenu Yehuda; and Taz — see there — wrote the opposite:
