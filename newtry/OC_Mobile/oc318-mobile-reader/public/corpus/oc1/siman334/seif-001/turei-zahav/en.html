On account of there is concern, etc. Here they forbid the permission lest he do extinguishing; and above siman 311 we permit prohibition regarding moving a corpse on account that if you do not permit him prohibition of moving he will come to extinguish since he is agitated over his dead — Tosafos already wrote in parsha Kirah (Shabbos daf 44) regarding this, and this is their language: and even though regarding money we say the opposite — if you permit him he will come to extinguish — and this is because one is not as pained over his money as over his dead; and if you permit him, since he will be preoccupied with saving he will forget it is Shabbat and come to extinguish inadvertently; but over his dead he grieves greatly and if you do not permit him he will come to extinguish intentionally — end of his words; it is clear his intent that in a matter where he is greatly agitated we are concerned for intentional extinguishing if we do not permit him a light prohibition like that of a corpse; but in what is not greatly agitated we are not concerned for intentional, only lest he forget it is Shabbat — and this is on account of tircha, like that of fire here; and see nearby after this:
