Seif 21: they do not save them. And if found in the hand of a min and we do not know who wrote them — we rule in Yoreh De'ah siman 281 that they require genizah; nevertheless on Shabbat even where there is no concern of extinguishing, such as to save from collapse and water — forbidden to move them and take them out; Tosafos Shabbos 116 s.v. books of minim and in s.v. thus they do not save; and see in Kesef Mishneh chapter 6 Laws of Torah scrolls — it appears he explains not like Tosafos' explanation but the baraita deals with scriptures of minim; and we rule like R' Tzadok — if so there is no proof for this law to forbid moving them and taking them out if found in his hand; also from Rashi on Shabbos there it appears like Kesef Mishneh's view from what he wrote "thus they do not save" even on a weekday — if so impossible to explain "if found"; if so the baraita in Gittin is not like those who hold for R' Tzadok they burn, and likewise R' Yehuda regarding Divine Names, and the Tanna Kamma at least does not require genizah since permitted to destroy them in collapse and water — a fortiori when found in the hand of a min; and the baraita in Gittin is like R' Tzadok as I explained, with Hashem's help:
