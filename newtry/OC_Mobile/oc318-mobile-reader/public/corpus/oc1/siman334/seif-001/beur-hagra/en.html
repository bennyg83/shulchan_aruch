(ג) And some say. From what we say there 116 seif 2 "they save the book case," etc., and even though, etc., meaning that without a non-Jew it is forbidden; and furthermore specifically they save but to extinguish is forbidden even though it is only a shevut, and even for the Yerushalmi who said that for R' Shimon it is permitted even to extinguish on account of holy writings because a shevut does not stand, etc. — they only permitted on account of holy writings, and even through a non-Jew it is forbidden, for we say "a non-Jew who comes to extinguish," etc., and know that when he reaches the outer courtyard they said there 154 seif 2 that it is forbidden even with loss, and one must say that they only permitted in a place where it is close to coming to a d'oraisa prohibition, unlike there; and likewise in such a case.
