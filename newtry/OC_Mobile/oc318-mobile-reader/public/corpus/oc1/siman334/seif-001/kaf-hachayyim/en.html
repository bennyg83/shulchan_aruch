1) [Seif 1] If it is nighttime before the meal, etc. Whenever it is before eating, even at the end of the night, one may save food for three meals. In the morning, however, this applies specifically before the time of the meal, meaning until the end of the sixth hour. After the sixth hour, its time has passed and one may not save food for two meals even if he has not yet eaten. Bach. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1, writes that Bach's ruling requires examination. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1, writes the same, because one remains obligated in three meals even after their proper times have passed, as stated in siman 291; see there.
