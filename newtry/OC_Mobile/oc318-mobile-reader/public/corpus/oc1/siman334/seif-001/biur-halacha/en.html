(א) For food for three meals — see in Peri Megadim s.k. 10 that he is uncertain whether perhaps the homeowner can save food for three meals for each and every member of his household; and so it appears to me from his words; but not from Shabbat to Yom Kippur, for they wrote there that even in order to feed the children on Yom Kippur it is forbidden to save; and it implies that for Shabbat itself it is permitted; and so is the law, for it is no less than what is permitted to save in order to feed an animal:

(ב) And that which is appropriate for an animal — for an animal — because it is forbidden to eat before one gives food to one's animal; and therefore one may also save for an animal food for three meals [Tosafos Shabbos]:
