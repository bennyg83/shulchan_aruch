[14] And there is one who says, etc. The Taz and Magen Avraham hold that there is no actual dispute. Everyone agrees that if tefillin were placed beside money on Shabbos and a fire later broke out, one may rescue them together. The prohibition is only to place tefillin beside the money during the fire for the purpose of saving the money. Yet this requires study regarding the Beit Yosef and other great authorities, because they cite Sefer HaTerumot as the source for permission to place them there even on Shabbos. I examined Sefer HaTerumot, siman 248 and its summary, and it explicitly discusses placement before Shabbos, like the Semag. See siman 310, seif katan 15.
[15] And some permit, etc. The later authorities explain this as permitting even placing the tefillin there on Shabbos, and so it appears from the Beit Yosef. At first glance Sefer HaTerumot and the Semag do not imply this. See the Beit Yosef in siman 279. This requires study.
