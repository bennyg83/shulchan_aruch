And it appears to me there is no dispute here — for all agree forbidden l'chatchila to place tefillin with money at the time of fire in order to save the money (to a courtyard that is not mixed; but to reshus hayachid permitted as is written in the seif after, and Bach did not understand thus and therefore challenged that the two seifim contradict each other) — for behold it is stated explicitly in the gemara that by law one had to shake the money from the tefillin case, as is written siman 310; only they are concerned lest meanwhile the fire fall and the tefillin burn; but if a money bag is placed elsewhere and he wants to bring it to place tefillin in it — if possible to shake the money in that manner one must shake it; if so, all the more forbidden to place the tefillin case at the time of fire in a money bag in order to save the money; and as Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rashba; and therefore Mordekhai wrote one should place it there erev Shabbat, etc. — see there; and if he placed on Shabbat tefillin with money and afterward fire fell — straightforward permitted to save for all; and one need not shake the money, as stated; examine and forget not as Rabbenu Yehuda wrote:
