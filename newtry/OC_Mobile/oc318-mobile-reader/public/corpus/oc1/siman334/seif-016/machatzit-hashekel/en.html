(s.k. 16) Nowadays for the heart has diminished — meaning from the aspect of law Written Torah is written only Ashurit and holy language and Oral Torah forbidden to write; and if in alteration they are Written Torah written not properly or if one wrote Oral Torah — one does not read in them and also does not save them on Shabbat on account of fire; but nowadays for the heart has diminished when intellect and memory diminished — permitted to write everything even Oral Torah; and likewise Written Torah as explained in Magen Avraham in the s.k. after who wrote "a time to act for the Heaven, they have violated Your Torah" — therefore one reads in them and saves them on account of fire on Shabbat:
