Tefillin in a purse, etc. This is in a house where the fire is; for if in an adjacent house permitted even in muktzeh for one view in seif 2; and Beit Yosef wrote it implies from this even on Shabbat; only in name of Maharam in Mordekhai he wrote forbidden to place on Shabbat, etc.; and perforce wrote here "and some say," etc.; and it appears to me there is a difference in the matter — if he places for this purpose that he have permission to save — this is not permitted only if he does it erev Shabbat; but if he placed beforehand not for this — then there is permission even if placed on Shabbat, since he did not place it there for saving; and perforce Tur wrote "he placed tefillin in a purse" — meaning not for saving — he can save it; and this even if placed on Shabbat; unlike a ring — even if not placed for saving — forbidden; and afterward he wrote in name of Sha'arei Chayim that in Yerushalmi permitted via loaf or child — meaning even placing on Shabbat on account of saving permitted as is written regarding a corpse; and on this he said perhaps in fire they permitted like a corpse; and Maharam in Mordekhai deals with placing on Shabbat on account of saving to resolve this matter — and there is no dispute here; and this ring has no prohibition to save in adjacent houses for all, for it is not muktzeh:
