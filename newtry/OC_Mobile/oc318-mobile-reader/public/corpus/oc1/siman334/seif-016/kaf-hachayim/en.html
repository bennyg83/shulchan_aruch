P) There. And there are those who forbid, etc. And Bach ruled like those who permit. Magen Avraham s.k. 3, Eliyah Rabbah note 2, Tosafos Shabbos note 7; and see above siman 13 note 7 what we wrote that wherever Shulchan Aruch writes "there are those who permit and there are those who forbid," his intent is to rule like those who forbid, and only in pressing circumstances may one rely on those who permit — see there; and nevertheless it appears to me specifically on account of bandits or on account of fire they permitted, for the matter is agitated and we are concerned he will come to a d'oraisa prohibition; but if rain fell on muktzeh merchandise — forbidden for all to move via an Israelite.
