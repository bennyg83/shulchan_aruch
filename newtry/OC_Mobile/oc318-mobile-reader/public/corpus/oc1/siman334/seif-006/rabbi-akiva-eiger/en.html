Thus the Or Zarua and Orchot Chayim: a general principle 59 — he wrote that in our time we have no public domain and there is nothing to decree a fortiori from d'oraisa; if a fire fell even in his house he may save everything even money, for in a place of loss one need not be concerned about muktzeh; and likewise on account of thieves or coercion or fear of seizure or siege — therefore I shortened most laws in this siman; and see this siman in the gloss of Orchot Chayim who wrote that specifically for himself or his wife and children one need not be concerned about karmelit and techumin, etc.; but for his fellow it is a safek; nevertheless regarding muktzeh which has no root from the Torah — permitted for every person to assist and move and take out to a courtyard even if not joined — see there where he expanded; and all this is d'rabbanan prohibition; but to dig under the ground or desecrate Shabbat and Yom Tov with any melacha d'oraisa for property — altogether not — end of Or Zarua s.k. 1:
