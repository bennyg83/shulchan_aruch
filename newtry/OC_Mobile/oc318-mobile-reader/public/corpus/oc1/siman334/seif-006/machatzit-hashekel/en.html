(א) (s.k. 6) Yom Kippur, etc. — it appears to me that even though Yom Kippur does not fall on erev Shabbat or on Sunday, as is written below siman 428 seif 1 — Yom Kippur does not fall on erev Shabbat or on Sunday:

(ב) And he knows he will not find, etc. — and it is as if Yom Kippur falls on erev Shabbat — permitted to save on Thursday; and likewise the reverse teaches us — if Yom Kippur falls on Monday and he knows on Sunday he will not find to buy — nevertheless not permitted to save on Shabbat for Yom Kippur:
