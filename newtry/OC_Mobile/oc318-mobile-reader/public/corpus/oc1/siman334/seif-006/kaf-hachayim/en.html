And specifically the members of the household, etc. Likewise Rosh in name of Rabbeinu Tam; however in Tosafos chapter Kol Kitvei they wrote in name of Rabbeinu Tam "in that house or in that courtyard"; and likewise Ran in his name; and so is implied from Rambam's language, as Maggid Mishneh wrote; and Bach wrote Rosh deals when the houses each stand separately as our houses; and Ran deals when many houses stand in one courtyard — then all are considered one house; and Tosafos deal with both — therefore they wrote "in that house or in that courtyard," etc.; and see siman 345 where there is a distinction between house and courtyard.
