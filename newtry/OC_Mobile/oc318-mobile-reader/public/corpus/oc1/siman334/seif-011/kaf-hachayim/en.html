K) [Seif 2] There are those who permit moving, etc. This is the view of Sefer HaTerumah that Tur brings; and "those who forbid" is Tur's view that argues on him; and Bach wrote Sefer HaTerumot refers to Rabbeinu Tam's words that permitted adjacent houses to the fire to save more than he needs; and therefore they can also save even money and muktzeh items, for they are not so agitated, since the fire is in another house — see there; and likewise Perishah note 1; and likewise Orach Mishpat note 2, Magen Avraham s.k. 1, Ateret Zekenim note 1, R' Zalman note 2, Chayei Adam general rule 45 note 6; however Maharai strained from Ran's words in chapter Mi sheHechshich that even in one's own house in which the fire broke out he permitted, and Tur did not challenge him except regarding other houses where the fire is not; but regarding the house in which the fire broke out he did not challenge, for it is fine to permit him muktzeh so he not come to extinguish, end of his words; and Beit Yosef brought it; and so is Taz's view s.k. 2 — see there; however Beit Yosef wondered at Maharai's words and wrote to resolve with difficulty — see there.
