11) [Seif 2] Some permit moving, etc. This is Sefer HaTerumah's view, cited by Tur, while the prohibiting opinion is Tur's disagreement. Bach writes that Sefer HaTerumah addresses Rabbeinu Tam's permission for nearby houses to save more than needed. Accordingly, they may even save money and muktzeh objects, because they are not so panicked since the fire is in another house; see there. Perishah, paragraph 1, writes the same, and this also appears to be Beis Yosef's view. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 2; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2; Chayei Adam, klal 45, paragraph 6. Mahari Abuhav, however, infers from Ran in the chapter Mi Shehechshich that Sefer HaTerumah permits this even in the burning house itself. Tur objects only regarding other houses not burning, but raises no objection concerning the burning house because moving muktzeh may properly be permitted lest the owner extinguish the fire. Beis Yosef cites him, and Taz, paragraph 2, agrees; see there. Beis Yosef, however, questions Mahari Abuhav's words and offers a forced resolution; see there.
