(א) (s.k. 11) Since, etc. — but if he did not say, etc. — to exclude Ran's view who holds even if he did not say to you — nevertheless automatically it is hefker immediately like one who saves from the edge of the sea and from the power of the river — for behold it is his since the owners despair since they cannot save — likewise here; but Bach wrote here it is different — presumptively one can say he does not despair — for he himself is not permitted to save more; nevertheless he can say I would have saved via a non-Jew that I would have told all who save should not lose, as is written siman 326:

(ב) And it appears to me without, etc. — I would have found for myself, etc. — meaning according to Bach if he is in a place where no non-Jews live or no non-Jews are found there except him and the rescuer — even if he did not say to you it is hefker; and on this Magen Avraham wrote nevertheless he can say I would have found, etc.:
