Some say all the saving, etc. This is Rabbenu Baruch's view in Sefer HaTerumah that Tur brings; and he challenged him — since the reason is lest he be preoccupied and extinguish — if so what difference courtyard and house; furthermore Sefer HaTerumah wrote there is a difference between his courtyard and his friend's courtyard even in mixed — whereas in his courtyard permitted to take out all his objects; and Beit Yosef wrote Tur's difficulty applies also to this distinction; and so here in Shulchan Aruch; and "some say" there is no difference — implies also in his courtyard one must be stringent; and it is difficult for me — if so Tur should have written this novelty that even to his courtyard forbidden to take out more than three meals; rather certainly Tur permits in this; and it appears his reason — for his courtyard that is close to him presumptively he has no such tircha as to his friend's domain; and on Sefer HaTerumah it is difficult for me from what we learned in ch. 22 — one saves a basket full of loaves even though there are one hundred meals in it; and we challenge: but the beginning taught three meals and no more; R' Huna said there is no difficulty — here one comes to save meaning in one basket; here one comes to fold meaning many baskets; R' Abba said both are for folding — here for that courtyard; here for another courtyard; and perforce for R' Huna even to fold in that same courtyard forbidden; for he holds R' Huna permits even in that courtyard even to fold; and likewise to save permitted even in another courtyard; and prohibition is only in two together — bad combination — meaning in another courtyard and to fold; for otherwise this is R' Abba; rather certainly R' Huna is stringent even in that courtyard; and we rule like R' Huna; if so why did Sefer HaTerumah permit in his courtyard to take out all he wants — meaning folding — and requires further study; but on Tur there is no difficulty as is written — since the reason is lest he forget Shabbat on account of tircha of saving — what difference courtyard and house; if so why permitted to fold in that courtyard for R' Abba — one can say for another courtyard there is more tircha presumptively and perforce more preoccupied and there is concern lest he forget:
