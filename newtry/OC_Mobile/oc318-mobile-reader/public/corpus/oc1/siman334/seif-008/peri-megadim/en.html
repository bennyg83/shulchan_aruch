There is no distinction, etc. — Shabbos 120a challenged Sefer HaTerumot that we rule like Rav Huna to fold — forbidden to that courtyard; and on Tur who rules in law 5 in a fellow's house — forbidden: what difference is there? If so, what is R' Abba's reason that to that courtyard it is permitted? What difference is there? One can say there is reasoning for this; and one can say Sefer HaTerumot possibly rules like R' Abba for this reason; and possibly Tur concedes it is close and he is not troubled; but the difficulty is Sefer HaTerumot and Tur in this, for the halacha is like Rav Huna — see Rif; and from the doubt there he explained "he spread his tallit and folded" — it implies the halacha is so; and this is what Tosafos there s.v. "in any case" means: for R' Abba there is no distinction between folding and saving; but if we say for R' Abba there is a distinction — then the question applies also for R' Abba; and therefore one can say for Rav Huna to that courtyard permitted in every manner; and he disputes R' Abba regarding saving: for Rav Huna to another courtyard permitted; and for R' Abba forbidden — if so, the main dispute is missing from the book.
