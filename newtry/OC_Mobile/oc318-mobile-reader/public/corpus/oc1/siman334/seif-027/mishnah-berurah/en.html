(א) He may extinguish it — and it deals when he has no advice how to move it from this place to clear it to another place where many are not found; for if not, certainly it is better to clear it and not extinguish it:

(ב) Of metal — and there is no concern of mekhabeh [strengthening the vessel through the cold water poured on it], since he does not intend for this:

(ג) Whether, etc., of wood — for Torah-level extinguishing one is liable specifically when extinguishing to make charcoal; but plain extinguishing is melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah and is only a rabbinic prohibition; and in this place of public damage where many can be harmed in their bodies they did not decree; but in a place of property damage it is forbidden, as is written seif 25; and Rambam holds one is liable for melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah; therefore it is forbidden in wood; but in metal there is no Torah-level prohibition of extinguishing for all, for he does not burn:
