Of wood. For extinguishing is melacha she'ein tzricha l'gufah; and in a place of damage they did not decree; but in a place of damage to money — forbidden; and as is written siman 265; and Rambam holds liable for melacha she'ein tzricha l'gufah; and see siman 318 seif 12; and in metal there is no extinguishing, for it does not burn:
