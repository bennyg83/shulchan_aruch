(א) A non-Jew, etc. — for a non-Jew acts for his own intent; and even if he knows it pleases the Israelite — he intends for his own benefit, knowing he will not lose; but in siman 276 regarding lighting a lamp for an Israelite, when the Israelite himself benefits from it — we do not say he acts for his own intent, and it is forbidden to benefit from its light:

(ב) Who comes to extinguish — even if he is his hired servant for a set time — we also say he acts for his own intent:

(ג) One need not protest his hand — but it is forbidden to urge him to extinguish, even though he came initially on his own for this:

(ד) But a minor who comes to extinguish — even a minor who has not reached chinuch:

(ה) One must protest — for a minor lacks the reasoning to act for his own intent, and acts according to his father's understanding, knowing this extinguishing pleases his father, and acts on his behalf — and it is forbidden, for he is commanded regarding shevitah. And see in Magen Avraham who wrote in name of Ran that even according to one who says melacha she'einah tzerichah legufah is rabbinic — one must protest his hand; and see in Peri Megadim what he wrote on this:

(ו) In his hand — see in Beit Yosef that even if he comes to extinguish a fire in another's house — one must protest his hand, since he acts on behalf of an adult:
