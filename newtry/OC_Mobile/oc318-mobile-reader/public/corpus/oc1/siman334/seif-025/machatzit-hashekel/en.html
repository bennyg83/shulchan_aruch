(s.k. 25) That he not intend — meaning not, etc. Bach not like Beit Yosef — for in Tur he wrote he is not concerned if it extinguishes (and therefore permitted) since he does not intend for that; and Rabbenu Yehuda explained specifically if his intent without extinguishing is to cover and read in the book and his mind is not at all that it extinguish — then permitted; but if his mind that via covering with cloak and reading in book it will extinguish — forbidden; but Bach disagrees necessarily — see there — even if his mind is not on wearing or reading but only that it extinguish — also permitted; and forbidden only that he not shake or run and jump, etc. — for these acts cause extinguishing:
