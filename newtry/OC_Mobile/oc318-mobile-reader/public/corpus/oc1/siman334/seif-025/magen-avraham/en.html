(א) A non-Jew who comes, etc. For a non-Jew acts for his own intent; and even if he knows it is pleasant for an Israelite — he intends for his own benefit, knowing he will not lose (Rashi); but in siman 276 regarding lighting a lamp the body of the Israelite benefits from it — we do not say he acts for his own intent, since the non-Jew intends for his benefit (Tosafos daf 112):

(ב) Who comes to extinguish. Even his hired servant for a set time (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 3):

(ג) One must protest. For a minor lacks sound judgment — he acts according to his father's intent, knowing this extinguishing is pleasant for his father and he acts for his sake; and therefore even per the one who says melacha she'ein tzricha l'gufah is d'rabbanan — one must protest his hand (Ran); and see siman 343 and siman 362 seif 7 in the gloss:
