(א) That they wrote, etc. — even Assyrian script on parchment and in ink:

(ב) With Divine Names — since they are attached, they certainly wrote them for the sake of idolatry; and from this one should warn regarding those gold and silver coins that heretics minted for idolatry — forbidden to hang them on a Torah scroll; and also forbidden to keep them in one's possession — rather melt them immediately so there be no remembrance of their deeds [Bach and Taz]; and Rav Morenu HaRav Yehuda Miller doubted whether it is permitted to melt them, lest they are not items of idolatry; and in responsum of Chavot Yair siman 316 he permits melting the coins of Shem ben Noach made in Sweden countries, because they were minted to spend them, and it is as if written explicitly for mundane use; and so it appears in Peri Megadim — see there. Further Maharam wrote what is customary in some communities when they bless a sick person and collect money from each one and place the vessel with the money into the ark of the Torah — they do not act well — see there; and in any case it is forbidden to place a mundane item into the ark of the Torah; therefore one should stop this custom:
