And even on a weekday one burns them, etc. Maharach of blessed memory wrote from here one should warn about those gold and silver coins on which is the four-letter Name that heretics stamped for idolatry — forbidden to hang on the sefer Torah and also forbidden to keep them in one's possession, rather melt them immediately — until here his language; and I add to say that what is practiced in some communities when they bless a sick person and collect money from each and place the vessel with the money into the aron kodesh — they do not act well, for there is impurity on the coins; and even otherwise forbidden to place mundane matter into the aron kodesh — perforce one must stop that custom — so I wrote:
