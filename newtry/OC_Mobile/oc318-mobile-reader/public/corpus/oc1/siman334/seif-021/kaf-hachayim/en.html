U) There. He saved clean bread, etc. And even if he saved coarse bread, the members of his household may each save food for three meals. Magen Avraham s.k. 4, Tosafos Shabbos there, R' Zalman note 3; and what he wrote "and even if he saved coarse bread" — Ateret Zekenim note 4 wrote this means if he transgressed and saved more than three meals; or one can say it means he may save three meals for every member of his household accustomed to feed them, end of his words; and Tosafos Shabbos there wrote, for example, when he saved much bread in one vessel, as is written seif 6.
