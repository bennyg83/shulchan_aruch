With the Divine Names. Bach wrote from here one should warn about those gold and silver coins on which is the four-letter Name that heretics stamped for idolatry — forbidden to hang on the sefer Torah and also forbidden to keep them in one's possession, rather melt them immediately — until here; and Rav R' Yehuda Miller was in doubt in this lest they are not heretics for idolatry and he expanded in responsum; and in responsum Chavot Yair siman 316 who permits to melt the coins that there they make the four-letter Name in the land of Sweden — see there the reason; and Taz wrote what is practiced in some communities when they bless a sick person and collect money from each and place the vessel with the money into the aron kodesh — they do not act well, for there is impurity on the coins; and even otherwise forbidden to place mundane matter into the aron kodesh — perforce one must stop that custom:
