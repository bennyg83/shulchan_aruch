[7] And there are those who are lenient, etc. Specifically regarding garments, but not regarding food (Bach); and it appears to me the same law applies to his needed utensils; and likewise Semag wrote food and vessels — not as Shelatei Gibborim understood and left it as a difficulty; and the reason is since it is the manner of wearing (Magen Avraham). And in my humble opinion he does not resolve anything at all, for Shelatei Gibborim strained that it is the language of Rambam; and if so, perforce one must say the same law applies to garments, for Semag ruled from him that garments are forbidden to a courtyard that is not joined — requires study; and likewise from what they mentioned garments at the beginning it implies that the utensils they mentioned afterward also refer to it, that they are forbidden to a courtyard that is not joined; and I resolved the difficulty above in another manner; and for practical law Nachalat Tzvi and Perishah ruled like the view of those who are lenient; and so I proved in siman 310 the view of Levush:
