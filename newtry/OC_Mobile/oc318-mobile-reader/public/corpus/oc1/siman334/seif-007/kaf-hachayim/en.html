Because there is concern, etc. And even though regarding a dead person they permit saving so it not be extinguished, as Rama wrote siman 311, and here we forbid saving so it not be extinguished — the reason Tosafos wrote: according to R' Yehuda, because a person is not as distressed over property as over his dead; and if you permit him, while he is busy saving he will forget it is Shabbat and come to extinguish inadvertently; but regarding his dead he is very distressed, and if you do not permit him he will come to extinguish intentionally, end of his words; and they brought it in Or Zarua s.k. 1 and Chavot Yair note 4 — see there:
