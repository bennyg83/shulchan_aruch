7) There: Because there is concern, etc. Regarding a corpse, the Sages permit saving it lest one extinguish the fire, as stated at the beginning of siman 311, whereas here they forbid saving property lest one extinguish. Tosafos on page 44a explains that a person does not suffer over his property as he does over his deceased relative. If permitted to save property, his preoccupation with saving it may cause him to forget that it is Shabbos and inadvertently extinguish. He suffers much more over his deceased relative, and if not permitted to save the corpse he may deliberately extinguish. These are their words. Taz, paragraph 1, and Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 4, cite this; see there.
