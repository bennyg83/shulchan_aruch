Letter 17, etc. — Gemara Shabbos 120a challenged that it is permitted to place water from one side — from the baraita "a tallit that fire seized — spread it and cover oneself" — it implies water is not as Beit Yosef wrote; on the contrary, a greater matter — even covering, etc., is more severe, etc.; and Magen Avraham letter 25; and this covering Taz explained is not covering that causes extinguishing (take language falling on covering the body in order that the fire be covered) — this is forbidden; only spread it and it is similar to water and lighter; but via covering and shaking — forbidden; and Magen Avraham; and there it will be explained further.
