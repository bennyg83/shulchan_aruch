L) And in Orach VeShalom general rule 59 siman 41 he wrote: And now in our time we have no reshus harabim (see below siman 345 seif 7, for there is a dispute on this) and there is no reason to decree on account of d'oraisa — if a fire broke out in his house on Shabbat he may save everything lest he come to extinguish because a person is agitated over his property; and all the more if a fire broke out in another courtyard — it is preferable; and he can save everything, even money and other muktzeh items can save in a place of loss, and one need not be concerned for muktzeh, etc.; and specifically in a rabbinic prohibition; but to bury them in the ground or desecrate Shabbat in any melacha of avot or tolados for money — absolutely not, end of his words; and in the gloss there he wrote: and since the reason is because a person is agitated over his property lest he extinguish — the same applies to himself and his sons and wife: they need not be concerned for karmelit and techumin and a courtyard that is not joined, etc.; but for his fellow, since this reason does not apply — certainly forbidden to take out, etc.; however regarding muktzeh, which has no root in d'oraisa — it appears permitted for every person to assist him, to move muktzeh and take out to a courtyard even if not joined; and also to take out to karmelit is uncertain there whether permitted, for it is possible permitted for another person to assist him — see there; and they brought it in Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz note 1, Eliyah Rabbah note 1, Chayei Adam general rule 45 note 8; however Chayei Adam wrote there on Orach VeShalom's words that all his words require study, for this is not found in Sefer HaTerumah or any posek, end of his words.
