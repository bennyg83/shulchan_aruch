[10] Sacred writings, etc. Written Torah takes precedence for rescue over Oral Torah. Even borrowed or rented sacred books must be rescued and one remains responsible for payment, as stated in Sefer Chasidim 861. Rescuing a corpse takes precedence over books.
[11] With sam and sikra, etc. The Magen Avraham explains this specifically where the text was written in another language. But if written in Hebrew script with sam or sikra, one may not read it. The Shulchan Aruch and Levush do not imply this, and the Shiltei Giborim implies that nowadays one may read such texts. This will be further explained.
