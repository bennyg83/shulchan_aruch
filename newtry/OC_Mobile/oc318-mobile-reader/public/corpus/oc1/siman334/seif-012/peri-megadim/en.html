Amulets, etc. — Beit Yosef implies so; and likewise Levush — the reason for amulets is they did not give permission to write verses of Torah even nowadays; but Bach in name of Rashi wrote even though permission was given to write, it is like jewelry; and dealt with in siman 301 seif 25; and in the Gemara 61b; and for a sick person with danger they save on account of fire; and for a sick person without danger it deals; and Magen Avraham letter 14; and Bach 69 — a ring on his finger is likewise an amulet in the manner of wearing — see there.
