(א) Seif 12 "all," etc. "nowadays," etc. Meaning even though per the gemara's law we rule like R' Yehuda who said that writings not in the holy tongue are not saved because they were not given to be read, but nowadays we say in Gittin 60 seif 1 "but it is not so — permitted," etc., and likewise they said in chapter 17 that some say the words of the Sages are like hora'ot, etc., and all save, etc., and likewise aggadic works, etc., as all this see there and Rosh. And Ran wrote that according to our gemara specifically they save but do not extinguish, even for R' Shimon — meaning that in our gemara the distinction between R' Yehuda and R' Shimon was not mentioned, but in the Yerushalmi it says that for R' Shimon one may even extinguish since it is melacha she'eina tzricha legufah and a shevut does not stand before holy writings; and at first glance the words of the Yerushalmi appear correct for the halachah since the gemara does not explicitly argue with them, and it is not so — the Yerushalmi speaks specifically of R' Shimon who holds in the last chapter of Eruvin that a shevut does not stand, etc., but we do not rule like R' Shimon there, and even though we rule regarding melacha she'eina tzricha legufah according to him. And likewise a coin. Meaning even though per the gemara's law one does not save, as above. And likewise a translation, etc. Baraita there, and even though that baraita is according to those who say they save, as above, nowadays, etc. That people.
