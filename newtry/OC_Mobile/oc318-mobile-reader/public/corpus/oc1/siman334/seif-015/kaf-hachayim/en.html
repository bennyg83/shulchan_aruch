For in a place of loss one need not be concerned, etc. And that which we say one forgot his pouch in the courtyard — forbidden to move it even via a loaf or child — there it is different because he was negligent in forgetting, as is written siman 267 seif 8; and further he can sit and guard it or give it to a non-Jewish guard.
