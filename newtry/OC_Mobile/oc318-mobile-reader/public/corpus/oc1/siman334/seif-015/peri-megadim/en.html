Provided, etc. — emended "they save books to a courtyard that is not joined"; in Levush this was omitted; and if so "provided," etc. refers to money via a dead person forbidden to not joined; or to an alley that did not participate. And "provided," etc. — even if he wishes to study on that Shabbat; and what he wrote "alley that is not open" — apparently a scribal error, must read "that did not participate"; and in Eliyah Rabbah letter 17 Levush wrote "provided" refers to an alley that did not participate — forbidden; if they participated — permitted; provided there are three partitions and a lechi; and likewise it is difficult why permission without three partitions and lechi; and also in seif 16 he did not write the place where one saves tefillin — therefore emended; and see Be'er HaGolah letter 50.
