(s.k. 4) That fell etc. Regarding one permitted to kill him — meaning excluding father tunneling into son's house, forbidden for son to kill father; for the reason one tunneling who may be killed is because he is a rodef — a person knows that one does not see his money being taken and remain silent, and certainly the tunneler intended if the homeowner stands against him he will kill him, therefore the Torah said one who comes to kill you — kill him first; and since by default a father has mercy on his son and certainly the father did not intend to kill his son if he does not allow him to take the money — therefore forbidden to kill the father; but regarding another, even the son of one tunneling into father's house — permitted to kill, and therefore if during tunneling a collapse fell upon him they do not dig out. And regarding goses they wrote Tosafos in Niddah daf 44a they dig out, and similarly in responsum Shevut Yaakov vol 1 not like responsum Beit Yaakov; also in Sefer Torah VeHaShabbos; unless he was already goses three days — then surely already dead; also wrote in name of responsum Chavot Ya'ir regarding a captive commanded by his master to cook for him on Shabbat or similar — even if he does not rebel at all, if he pleads and they do not heed his pleading — permitted to do it, for no doubtful life matters emerge, for a captive — all are in him:
