(8) And see in Even HaEzer siman 4 seif 34 that an infant found in a city whose majority is gentiles — one does not desecrate Shabbat for him, since every day they all separate from their place of establishment we follow the majority (nevertheless Pesak Torah from Ach Pri HaEtz and Beit Yosef there). Magen Avraham there; however Mahari Molcho there, in the gloss wrote one view that even among a majority of Kutim we dig out the collapse on Shabbat, and the reason Beit Yosef wrote there is because the city's residents are not considered as travelers and it is as if one departed from their place of establishment, for we do not follow the majority — see there; and Bach and Sefer Torah VeHaShabbos there wrote in pikuach nefesh we go to leniency and wondered at Magen Avraham who did not bring this — see there. And all this when found in a city; but if found in a house it is an established place and according to all they dig out — Beit Yosef there.
