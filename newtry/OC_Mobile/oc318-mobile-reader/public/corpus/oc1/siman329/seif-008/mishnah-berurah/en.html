(א) Because of gentiles — and the same applies to an Israelite when one estimates he will come to danger, for it is permitted to save him with his life; and similarly when a person is pursued because of a snake or bear — likewise a mitzvah to save him, even through doing several melachot on Shabbat [Rambam]:

(ב) In order to save them — nevertheless if there is danger to the rescuer he is not obligated, for his life precedes his fellow's life (Orchot Chayim); and even doubtful danger — his doubt is preferable to his fellow's certainty; however one must weigh the matter well whether there is doubtful danger and not be overly precise like the one they said "one who is overly precise in this comes to this" [Pischei Teshuvah Choshen Mishpat siman 426]:
