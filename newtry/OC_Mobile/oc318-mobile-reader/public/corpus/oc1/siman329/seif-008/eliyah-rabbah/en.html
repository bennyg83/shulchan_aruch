[8] It is a mitzvah, etc. Nevertheless, if he would certainly also place himself in danger together with the other person, he should not endanger himself, since he is presently outside the danger. This is derived from 'and your brother shall live with you.' We find no distinction between danger and certain death, as stated by Issur VeHeter, rule 59. This apparently contradicts what the Sema writes in siman 426, that one must enter a situation of possible danger to save another, supporting the Shulchan Aruch there.
