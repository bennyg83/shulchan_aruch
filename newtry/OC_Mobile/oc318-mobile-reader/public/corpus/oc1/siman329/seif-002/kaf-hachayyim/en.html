2) There: And it will lead to danger, etc. For example, ill people or children are present and there is insufficient time to remove them before the fire reaches them. Beis Yosef. It appears to me that even if they can be saved by carrying them through a public domain, it is preferable to extinguish the fire. Extinguishing is labor not needed for its own purpose, whereas carrying them would be a Torah prohibition, since they cannot walk and the principle that a living person carries himself does not apply, as stated in siman 308, seif 41. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. This means carrying them through the public domain into another private domain. Carrying them merely into the public domain would also be labor not needed for its own purpose, like carrying out a corpse. Bringing them into another private domain, however, is labor needed for its own purpose, because he needs the ill people and children to be in a private domain. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1. He writes that even according to our view that we do not have a full public domain today, as stated in siman 345, seif 7, carrying is nevertheless more stringent than extinguishing; see there. Pischei HaDevir, paragraph 1, cites him and appears to agree; see there.
