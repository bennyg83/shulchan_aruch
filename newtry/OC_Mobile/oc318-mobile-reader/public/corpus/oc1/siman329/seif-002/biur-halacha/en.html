(א) As half and half — and I saw in Ritva's chiddushim to Kesubos 15 that he wrote we do not say this except where this one who departed intended to return to the group; but if he did not intend to return — we say of him whoever separates from the majority separates; and I did not see this among other poskim:

(ב) The initial establishment — behold the Mechaber was silent in this like Rif [and as Ran and Hagahot Maimoni explained him], Rambam, and Rosh; and Rashi's approach in this is that all this is specifically if those men did not all go to that second courtyard; but if they went via the second courtyard [even though they did not join together to be considered as established, rather each went his way — Ritva in explanation of Rashi's words], and a collapse fell upon one of them — they dig out, for due to the stringency of life matters we judge them as established. And Tosafos there in Yoma also hold Rashi's approach, except they added in this that even if they passed through the courtyard one after another, and in that one upon whom the collapse fell they had already passed the majority or 9 and only one remained — they also dig out, for since once they were in that courtyard we judge them as truly established, that even if only one remained the establishment is not nullified.
