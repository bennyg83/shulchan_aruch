(א) Even etc. — meaning not only if in that courtyard where there were 9 gentiles and 1 Jew a collapse fell — certainly they dig out, for every established group is considered as half and half and there is no majority of gentiles; but even if there were 9 etc.:
(ב) And one departed — not specifically one, rather even if the majority departed, and as he concludes the reason since the initial establishment remained etc.:
(ג) The initial establishment in its place — and even though in general they said whoever separates from the majority separates, even where the establishment remained in its place — regarding pikuach nefesh the Merciful One was lenient, for it is written "and live by them" and not "and die by them," implying one may not come in any manner to the death of an Israelite:
(ד) As half and half — and doubtful life matters are for leniency; and see in Even HaEzer siman 4 seif 34 that an infant found in a city whose majority is gentiles — one does not desecrate Shabbat for him, since every day they all separate from their place of establishment we follow the majority; and Rama in the gloss disputes there and holds we dig out the collapse for him, for the city's residents are considered as established and comparable to the law here; and Gra's view there is to rule like Rama — see there; and if found on the road and the majority of passersby are gentiles — according to all we follow the majority [Peri Megadim]:
(ה) If they all uprooted — meaning they uprooted to disperse each to his way and their establishment was nullified; but if they departed consecutively together one after another, since they are still in one group they are called established and the law is as initially:
