On the Mechaber seif 2 "we do not follow." See Eliyah Rabbah who brings in responsa Beit Yaakov that for goses one does not desecrate Shabbat; and see inside who distinguishes that certainly if the doctor says he can still be completely healed — certainly one desecrates Shabbat for him; but due to life for an hour one does not desecrate, for it helps only [so that] he not die quickly; and see Tosafos Shabbos who brings responsum Shevut Yaakov disputing him — even due to life for an hour one desecrates for him; and see in Mishbetzos Zahav s.k. 2; and see in Dagul MeRevavah Even HaEzer siman 17 s.k. 94 [from the Gaon may he rest in Eden]:
