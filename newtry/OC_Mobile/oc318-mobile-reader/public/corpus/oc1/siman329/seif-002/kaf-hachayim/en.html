Beit Yosef; and it appears to me that even if it is possible to save them via reshus harabim, better to extinguish, for that is melacha she-einah tzerichah legufah but this is d'oraisa (since they cannot walk we do not say of them "a live person carries himself"), as written siman 328 seif 41 Magen Avraham s.k. 1; and meaning to carry them via reshus harabim to another reshus harabim, for reshus harabim itself is also melacha she-einah tzerichah legufah like one who carries out the dead; but to another reshus hayachid it is melacha tzerichah legufah since they need to be in reshus hayachid like the way of ill and minors. Sefer Torah VeHaShabbos 1; and wrote that even according to us who do not have reshus harabim as written siman 345 seif 7 — nevertheless carrying is more stringent than extinguishing — see there; and Petach HaDevir brought it 1, and it appears he agrees with his words — see there.
