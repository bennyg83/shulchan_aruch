We do not follow the majority in pikuach nefesh. Even if there were 9 gentiles and 1 Jew in a courtyard, and one departed to another courtyard and a collapse fell upon him there — they dig him out; since the initial establishment remained in its place, we consider him as half and half. But if they all uprooted themselves, and at the time of their uprooting one departed to another courtyard and a collapse fell upon him — they do not dig him out, for since the initial establishment was uprooted from its place, we say whoever separates from the majority separates:
