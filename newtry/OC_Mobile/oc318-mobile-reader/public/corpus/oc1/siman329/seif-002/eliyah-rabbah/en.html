[2] One separated, etc. The same applies if most of them separated, or even all of them except one who remained in the original location. The status of a fixed group remains because they did not all separate, as stated by the Bach. Regarding danger to life the Sages were lenient. See siman 439 and Yoreh Deah siman 110. Even if all left, Issur VeHeter writes that this applies specifically when they left one after another. If they left all at once, it is still considered fixed.
