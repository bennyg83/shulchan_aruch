(א) Regarding monetary matters etc. — for on account of monetary loss the prohibition of Shabbat was not permitted:
(ב) And even without specification — for doubtful life matters are for leniency:
(ג) Near the border etc. — a city that separates between the border where Israelites dwell and the border of gentiles, and we are concerned that if they capture it from there the land will be easy to conquer before them:
(ד) Even if they did not come etc. — meaning when rumor goes out that they wish to come, even though they have not yet come — permitted to don weapons to guard and make noise in the city so they will not come, for we are not precise in pikuach nefesh [language of Or Zarua]; and this law applies to the beginning as well [Acharonim]:
