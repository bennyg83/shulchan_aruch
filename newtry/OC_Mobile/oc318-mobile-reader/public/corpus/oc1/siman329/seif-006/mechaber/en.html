Gentiles who besieged Jewish towns — if they came regarding monetary matters, one does not desecrate Shabbat for them. If they came regarding life matters, and even without specification — they go out against them with weapons and desecrate Shabbat for them. And in a city near the border, even if they came only regarding straw and hay matters — one desecrates Shabbat for them.And even if they have not yet come but wish to come [Or Zarua].]:
