(ב) He is there — and it deals with one who was there at the time of the collapse and it is not known whether he succeeded in exiting from the overturning; and it appears the same even if we did not see him initially explicitly, only that people are designated in this house at this time — we also desecrate and dig out the collapse. One who comes to tunnel in a case where it is permitted to kill him — if during his tunneling a collapse fell upon him, they do not dig him out, for he is a dead man (poskim in name of the Gemara). An Israelite who sins for pleasure — as long as he does not deny the Torah — it appears we desecrate Shabbat to save him; but if he is to provoke — forbidden to save him even on a weekday, and all the more forbidden to desecrate Shabbat for him in digging out the collapse or other healing; and the same for all those stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 158 seif 1 and 2 — see there:
