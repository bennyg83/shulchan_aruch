Doubts. And similarly regarding healing an ill person — even though we do not know with certainty that this is the medicine with which he will be healed by natural means, rather the matter still depends on doubt — we desecrate Shabbat; even seemingly from Rambam who holds like the Rabbis who dispute R' Matya ben Charash, and we do not feed one bitten by a mad dog from the lobe of his liver, because the cure is not certain — see there that all healing is not from nature and reason does not agree it should help — what is written regarding healing; and it is possible the times and practitioners and recipients are not equal; but in my humble opinion the doubt is from the side of reasoning — certainly it is included in the category of doubtful life matters for leniency. Ginat Veradim siman 2; and see R' Yehuda siman 112; and Eliyahu in Hagahot Maimoni chapter 4 Laws of Forbidden Foods that forbids one with seizures to heal with an impure bird, since the cure is not certain — if so, the cure in that creature is not clear, unlike here where the cure is certain except we doubt in the medicine whether it is that medicine or not — permitted according to all; and see in responsum Rashal siman 285, Ran chapter "With what," Radbaz siman 66, responsum Rashba siman 481:
