One upon whom a collapse fell — doubtful alive etc. Even with several doubts, for we do not follow the majority in pikuach nefesh, nor after several doubts; even according to one who says rov is preferable, and also chezkah with a doubtful case does not help even to transgress a Torah prohibition except idolatry, adultery, and bloodshed, as in siman 179 Yoreh De'ah and Shach there. There is to doubt whether the law that we do not follow the majority in pikuach nefesh is specifically regarding Shabbat melacha she-einah tzerichah legufah according to one who says the Rabbis were lenient in it — but in melacha d'oraisa like wounding for healing and the like, or in other Torah prohibitions like tying a knot and the like — we do not follow the majority in pikuach nefesh; and the proof seemingly is that they all departed — we do not dig out, and in the remaining initial establishment we dig out, and in other prohibitions it is not so — 9 shops etc. see Perishah here and Bach Even HaEzer 4 note 58; but one may answer that Shmuel said so and he ruled like R' Yehuda that melacha she-einah tzerichah legufah is d'oraisa, chapter 1 Laws of Shabbat halacha 7 and chapter 10 halacha 17 in Maggid Mishneh there and Tosafos Yoma chapter 5 1 s.v.
