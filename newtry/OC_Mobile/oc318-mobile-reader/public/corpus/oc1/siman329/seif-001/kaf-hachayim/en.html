(1) [Seif 1] All pikuach nefesh overrides Shabbat. A Canaanite slave — if he did not accept upon himself the mitzvos applicable to a slave, one does not desecrate Shabbat for him; but if he accepted upon himself mitzvos — one desecrates Shabbat for him. The pious rabbi Mahari Molcho in responsum 21. Berkei Yosef 1; and see further on this "for whom one desecrates" above siman 328 21 and 91 — see there; and if one who desecrated Shabbat for pikuach nefesh needs atonement — see below 25.
