[1] [Levush] Such as sick people, etc. Even if they could be rescued by carrying them through a public domain, it is preferable to extinguish the fire, because extinguishing is a melachah not needed for its own purpose, whereas carrying in a public domain is a Torah prohibition, as explained in siman 308, seif 41, according to the Magen Avraham. This requires study, especially according to those who hold that we do not presently have a true public domain. See Magen Avraham, siman 330, seif katan 3. Beit Yaakov responsum 59 writes that one does not desecrate Shabbos for a person who is dying. This requires study.
