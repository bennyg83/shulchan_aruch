1) [Seif 1] Any danger to life overrides Shabbos. If a Canaanite slave did not accept the mitzvos that apply to a slave, Shabbos is not desecrated for him. If he accepted the mitzvos of Shabbos, Shabbos is desecrated for him. The pious Rabbi Yosef Molcho, responsa manuscript; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1. See above, siman 328, paragraphs 21 and 91, regarding those for whom Shabbos is desecrated; see there. Regarding whether one who desecrated Shabbos to save a life requires atonement, see below, paragraph 25.
