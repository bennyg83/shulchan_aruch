Section 1, "And there are those who are stringent, etc." As is stated in the chapter "HaSholeach" regarding tefillin: "and you shall bind them" and "and you shall write them," etc. The first opinion holds that this applies specifically to tefillin, where Scripture wrote an indispensable requirement. A proof is that the Beit Yosef needed a derivation to exclude a gentile. Also, there is the case of a sukkah made by gentiles, women, animals, or Cutheans, and in the chapter "HaSefinah" the case of the wife of Rabbi Chanina ben Dosa. But the Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote in the name of Maharam that from "the children of Israel" we exclude the daughters of Israel, as in several places. And the case in the chapter "HaSefinah" concerns spinning tzitzit, for even regarding tefillin, in such a case, a woman is valid, as stated in Avodah Zarah 39a; see there, chapter 1 of the Laws of Tzitzit.
