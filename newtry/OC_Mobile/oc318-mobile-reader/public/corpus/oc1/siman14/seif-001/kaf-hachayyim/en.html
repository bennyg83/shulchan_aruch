1) [Seif 1] Tzitzit made by a non-Jew are invalid, etc. This means that if the non-Jew attached them to the garment, they are invalid, even if a Jew stood over him supervising. Regarding spinning, however, they are valid according to the Rosh, as stated in siman 11, seif 2. Magen Avraham, seif katan 1; Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 1; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, ot 1; Chayei Adam, klal 11, ot 30. Even if the non-Jew merely inserted the threads into the corner, or made one winding or one knot, they are invalid. Peri Megadim there in the name of Levush; Pithei Olam, ot 1. They are invalid even if a Jew assists him. Eliyah Rabbah there, cited by Shaarei Teshuvah, ot 1, unlike Olat Tamid, ot 5. If, however, a Jew hung the threads in the corner and made one winding and one knot in a manner that makes them valid by Torah law, and the non-Jew then completed them, they are valid. Eliyah Rabbah there; Peri Megadim there; Shaarei Teshuvah there; Pithei Olam there. See below, ot 9.
