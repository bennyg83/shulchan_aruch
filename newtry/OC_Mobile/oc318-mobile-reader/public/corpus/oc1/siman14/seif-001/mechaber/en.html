Laws of tzitzis made by gentiles and women, and a borrowed tallis — it contains five clauses. Tzitzis made by a gentile are invalid, for it says
“Speak to the children of Israel,” excluding gentiles; a woman is fit to make them. Rema: Some require men to make them — good to do so lechatchila (Mordechai Hilchos Ketanos; Hagahos Maimoniyos Hilchos Tzitzis; Tosafos 42a).
