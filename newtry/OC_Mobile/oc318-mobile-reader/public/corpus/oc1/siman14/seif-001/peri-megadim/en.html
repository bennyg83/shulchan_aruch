The woman. Taz (s. 33, and it should read s. 39 seif 1). And according to Rabbeinu Tam, all those disqualified there — a minor, etc., and an apostate, etc. — are disqualified here too. And there is another reason: "Bnei Yisrael" and not a woman, and a minor is fit; therefore Magen Avraham — what was written from this, and what was written that according to Rabbeinu Tam a woman also spins, etc. — so it certainly implies in siman 11 seif 1 letter 2 regarding a woman and a non-Jew, since Rabbenu did not emend anything there, and I concede to the Mechaber. And what was written that specifically tying he is stringent (scripture), as stated shortly in letter 2: "Speak to Bnei Yisrael and make on the corners of their garments" — meaning to attach to the garment, not spinning and interweaving; and so the Levush explained in siman 11 seif 14. But here, except that the Levush according to his view that a woman is disqualified — "sons" and not daughters — if so, here "fringes you shall make for yourself" is not written "sons" nor "daughters"; unlike Rabbeinu Tam's reason that whatever is not in wearing is not in making — we exclude from the verse "fringes you shall make for yourself on the four corners of your garment" — whatever is in wearing, etc. — to exclude spinning and interweaving, for from here we derive that spinning and interweaving require intent, from "do not wear shaatnez" (only insertion is fit by women; and the rule is that the verse "and they shall make" — therefore "their garments" — refers only to insertion and tying alone; and the verse "you shall make on the four corners of your garment" refers to spinning and interweaving similar to "do not wear shaatnez," and tying too from the word "gedilim" as the Levush wrote in siman 11, and here there was no insertion). And if so, apparently Taz of blessed memory benefited two parties, and requires study now. And know: according to Rabbeinu Tam a tumtum is fit to make them, for a Torah doubt we are stringent; nevertheless they exist in wearing (and Rambam in chapter 9 of Hilchos Tefillin requires study). And per the one who says "sons and not daughters" — one can say definite sons and not doubt; and an androgynus depends whether it is a creature — and if God wills, in siman 39 this will be explained from this. And a non-Jew, for the Mechaber, is fit in spinning per the Rosh.
