Magen Avraham seif 1 — meaning that he affixed them, etc. In Noda B'Yehuda he wrote, and I do not know from where he derives this; for since in spinning all the authorities hold that it requires intent, it is valid for the Rosh with a gentile standing over. All the more so in making — for per the Rambam it is not needed that it be for the sake, for it is valid for the Rosh with a gentile standing over. And from your sharpness — not precise, for making is different, for they are not disqualified because of not for the sake, but rather it is a decree of Scripture that excludes from the verse "Bnei Yisrael and make" and not a non-Jew; therefore a gentile standing over does not help. And it is straightforward. And with this the Rosh is settled who holds making requires intent — why do I need the verse "Bnei Yisrael"? For automatically it is disqualified because of not for the sake; see Taz seif 2. For per the Rosh in his view that with a gentile standing over it is called "for the sake," he needs the verse "and make" to exclude a non-Jew with a gentile standing over; and the Rambam, for his reason that holds that where it requires intent a gentile standing over does not help — it is well proven his view that making does not require intent, since he needed the verse "Bnei Yisrael" to exclude a non-Jew.
