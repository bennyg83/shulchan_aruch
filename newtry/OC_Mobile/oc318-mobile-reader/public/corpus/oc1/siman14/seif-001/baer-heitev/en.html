If a gentile made them: that is, if he inserted them into the garment—they are invalid even if a Jew stands over him. For in spinning it is valid per the Rosh, as stated in siman 11, seif 2. Magen Avraham.

To require men: that is, to attach them to the garment; but to spin is permitted for everyone, and a minor's law is like a woman's. Magen Avraham.
