To require men—see in Mishnah Berurah; and this is the reason of Maharam cited in Hagahot Maimoniyot, and I did not copy the reason because of what Rabbeinu Tam said, and as stated in Magen Avraham, because anything not in wearing is not in making—and this applies also to other mitzvot, lulav and sukkah, because it is implied in the words of the Mechaber that he was concerned only for Maharam; and so I found in Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham. If so, one need not be stringent also regarding a minor; and so I found in Arukh HaChaim. Nevertheless, since Pri Megadim and Darkhei Chaim copied this as law, to be careful ab initio through a minor, it is fitting and proper to do so. And it is simple that a minor for himself may insert tzitzit according to all, if he has only reached chinuch that he is obligated rabbinically in the mitzvah of tzitzit and is called one fit for wearing to fulfill himself with his tzitzit. And more—it seems to me that even if he has already become an adult of thirteen years he need not remove his tzitzit, for they are as b'dieved; and b'dieved one need not be concerned for what Rabbeinu Tam said—all this if he knows himself that he inserted them then with intent.

"And it is good to do," etc. — See in Mishna Berurah: here I must explain the words of Daat, for even according to what the Rama was lenient regarding b'dieved with a woman — and certainly she is permitted in every manner like a man — but with a minor, at minimum specifically an adult must be standing over him. And in Chayei Adam he wrote: a minor who knows how to act l'shemah may be validated b'dieved like a woman; therefore this too speaks specifically of an adult standing over him — for even the Taz, who holds below in siman 460 that a minor who knows how to act l'shemah is still not in the category of a minor, meaning that standing over him helps, as is clear to one who examines there. And I wonder at the Aruch HaShulchan cited above, who is lenient lechatchila through a minor — for even if we do not be concerned at all for that of Rabbeinu Tam, nevertheless on account of l'shemah one should be stringent lechatchila if possible, for it was ruled in seif 2 that attaching requires intent l'shemah, to be concerned for the view of Rashi and the Rosh and other early authorities who hold that l'shemah is required here by Torah law; and below in siman 460 it was ruled that kneading the matzah of the mitzvah that requires l'shemah — one does not knead lechatchila through a minor boy even with an adult standing over him, as is stated among the later authorities and in the Gra there.
