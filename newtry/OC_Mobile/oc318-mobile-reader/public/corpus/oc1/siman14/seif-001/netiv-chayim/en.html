Taz, seif katan 1. “Even according to Rabbeinu Tam, who maintains that the Torah's insistence applies specifically to attaching them to the garment.” This is incorrect. Rosh in Gittin, chapter 4, siman 46, brings proof against Rabbeinu Tam from the passage in Bava Basra 74a; see there. According to Taz's understanding, that passage would provide no proof, because it concerns spinning. Rather, according to Rabbeinu Tam, once the comparison to wearing teaches that wearing itself is not considered making, every stage of preparation is subject to the restriction, both spinning and attaching the strings to the garment.
