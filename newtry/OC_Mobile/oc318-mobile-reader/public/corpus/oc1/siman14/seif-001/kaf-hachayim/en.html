(1) [Seif 1] Tzitzit that a gentile made are invalid, etc. Explanation: that he tied them on the garment — invalid. And even if a Jew is standing over him — for regarding spinning it is valid per the Rosh, as stated in siman 11 seif 2, Magen Avraham seif katan 1, Eliyah Rabbah seif katan 1, Peri Megadim, Ateret Zekeinim letter 1, Chayei Adam klal 11 letter 30. And even if he inserted alone into the corner, or made a chulyah or knot through a gentile — invalid; Peri Megadim there, citing the name of the Levush. And they brought Shaarei Olam letter 1. And even if a Jew assists him — invalid; Eliyah Rabbah there, and they brought Shaarei Teshuvah letter 1, and not like Orchos Tzadikim letter 5. But if a Jew attached the threads in the corner and made one chulyah and knot in a manner that is valid by Torah law, and afterward a gentile finished — valid; Eliyah Rabbah there, Peri Megadim there, Shaarei Teshuvah there, Shaarei Olam there — and see below letter 9.
