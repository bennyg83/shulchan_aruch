Seif katan 1: "that he made," etc. Even if a Jew is standing over him — per the Rosh, the Rambam proved that attaching tzitzit does not require l'shemah; for otherwise why is the verse "children of Israel" needed to exclude a gentile worker? We could learn from spinning, where the exclusion is not written and nevertheless spinning is forbidden through gentile workers, as above end of siman 11 — and this is because since spinning requires l'shemah and gentile workers are not fit for l'shemah, if so the same would apply to attaching — if attaching required l'shemah, there would be no need to exclude gentile workers from the verse "children of Israel," but certainly attaching l'shemah is not required. But the Rosh, who holds attaching l'shemah is required — so is ruled, as stated in the seif after this. The Rambam's difficulty remains: why is the verse "children of Israel" needed? And to resolve this, Magen Avraham: the Rosh, according to his view, holds regarding spinning that if a Jew is standing over gentile workers it is called l'shemah — therefore the verse "children of Israel" was needed for attaching, for even in such a case where it would be l'shemah, attaching is forbidden by decree of Scripture. But the Rambam, according to his view, properly proved that he holds regarding spinning that even a Jew standing over gentile workers is invalid, for it is still not l'shemah; if so, the same with attaching — if attaching required l'shemah, why "children of Israel" to exclude gentile workers?
