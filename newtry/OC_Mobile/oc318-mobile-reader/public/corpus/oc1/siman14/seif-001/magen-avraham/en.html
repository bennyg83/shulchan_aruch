It is valid. This requires study, for throughout the Gemara the phrase “the children of Israel” is expounded as excluding Jewish women, as in Kiddushin 36a and Menachos 61a, where both non-Jews and women are excluded from “the children of Israel.”
Some are stringent. According to them, a woman may not prepare any mitzvah in which she is not obligated, such as lulav or sukkah. See what I wrote at the end of siman 649, seif 1. It appears to me that a minor has the same status as a woman; see siman 39.
