“He made them,” etc. Meaning he attached them to an invalid garment—even if a Jew did it—whereas if he merely spun the threads invalidly they are valid for Rabbeinu Oshaya, as siman 14:1 says.

“Valid.” This needs study, for everywhere we expound “the sons of Israel” and not “the daughters of Israel,” as in Kiddushin 36 and Menachos 61, which exclude gentiles and women from “sons of Israel.”

“Some are stringent.” According to them, for any mitzvah a woman is not obligated in—such as lulav and sukkah—she is not permitted to perform it; see siman 589:1. It appears to me a minor’s law is like a woman’s; see siman 49.
