3) There. 'A woman is qualified to make them.' Taz, seif katan 1, writes that this excludes the view of Rabbeinu Tam, who disqualifies women from making tzitzit just as they are disqualified from making tefillin in siman 39, seif katan 1, etc. The fact that our women spin tzitzit is permitted even according to Rabbeinu Tam, for he objects specifically to attaching the tzitzit to the garment, as stated nearby; spinning is a different matter. Levush, seif 1, likewise writes that preparing, spinning, and twisting the tzitzit may be done by women even according to the stringent authorities; see there. Shiyurei Kenesses HaGedolah, Hagahot Beit Yosef, ot 1, and Maamar Mordechai, ot 2, cite this. Olat Tamid, seif katan 3; Birkei Yosef, ot 3, and in his Machazik Berachah, ot 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 2; Chayei Adam there; and Pithei Olam, ot 2, write likewise. This is unlike Korban Netanel at the end of chapter HaSholeach and his Netiv Chaim, who write that according to Rabbeinu Tam even spinning by women is prohibited. Machazik Berachah there likewise proves the contrary from the words of the halakhic authorities; see there.
