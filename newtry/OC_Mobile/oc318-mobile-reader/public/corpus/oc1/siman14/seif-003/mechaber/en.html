One who borrows from his fellow a tallis without tzitzis is exempt from tying tzitzis on it for thirty days, as it says “your garment,” not another’s; but after thirty days he is obligated rabbinically because it appears his own. Rema: If he returned it within thirty [days] and borrowed again it does not combine — we require thirty consecutive days (Nimukei Yosef Hilchos Tzitzis). A loan when it already has tzitzis — one blesses on it immediately.
