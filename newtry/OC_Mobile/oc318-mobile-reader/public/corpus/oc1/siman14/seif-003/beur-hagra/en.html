Section 3, "For it is written, etc." There, 136a.

"Because, etc." Tosafot there.

"And if, etc." For then it does not appear to be his.

"He borrowed it as it is, etc." For he lent it to him with this understanding, as is stated in Bava Batra 137b, "If he goes out, etc.," and in Moed Katan 26b, "And Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, etc."
