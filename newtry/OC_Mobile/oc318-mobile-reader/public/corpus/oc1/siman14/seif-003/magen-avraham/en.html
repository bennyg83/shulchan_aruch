He recites a blessing. Yam Shel Shlomo, Chullin, chapter 8, siman 53, writes that if one wears the tallis merely to receive an aliyah or to lead the prayers, he does not recite a blessing, because he is not wearing it for the sake of the mitzvah. Rosh at the beginning of Hilchos Tzitzis, however, implies that one who leads the prayers recites a blessing. Piskei Tosafos on Menachos, siman 161, likewise states that kohanim who wrap themselves in talleisim that are not theirs when ascending to duchan recite a blessing.
