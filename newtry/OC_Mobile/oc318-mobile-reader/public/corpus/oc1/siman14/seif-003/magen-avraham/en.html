“Exempt.” If he attached tzitzis to it, Tosafos and the Rosh wrote that one who recites the blessing has not lost out, like women who bless on positive mitzvos they are not commanded in—see there.
“He blesses.” Yesh Shalom in Chullin chapter 8, siman 53, wrote that if he wears it to receive an aliyah or to pass before the ark he does not bless, since he is not wearing it for the sake of the mitzvah. But in the Rosh, beginning of Hilchos Tzitzis, it implies that one who passes before the ark does bless; likewise in Menachos in Pesakim Tosafos siman 161, that kohanim who wrap in a tallis that is not theirs when they ascend to the duchan bless.
