(Seif katan 3) Some are stringent, and according to them every mitzvah, etc. Their reason is derived from tefillin. The Torah juxtaposes “you shall bind them” and “you shall write them.” Whoever is obligated to bind tefillin may write them, but one who is not obligated in binding may not write them. The stringent authorities derive tzitzis from tefillin and extend the principle to every mitzvah: one who is not obligated in that mitzvah may not prepare its object.
A minor has the same law as a woman. See siman 39. For this reason a minor is invalid to write tefillin, and according to the stringent authorities who derive tzitzis from tefillin, the same applies to tzitzis. This continues until he is thirteen years old and has produced two hairs, as stated at the beginning of siman 39.
