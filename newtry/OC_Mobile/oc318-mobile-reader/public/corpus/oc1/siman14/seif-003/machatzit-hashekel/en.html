(Seif katan 3) "And some are stringent, and according to them every mitzvah," etc. The reason of the stringent is that we expound regarding tefillin, as is written regarding them "and you shall bind them," and similarly "and you shall write them" — whoever is in tying, namely one obligated in the mitzvah of tying tefillin, is in writing and may write them; but one who is not in tying is not in writing. And the stringent hold that we derive tzitzit from it — likewise every mitzvah may be derived from tefillin for this matter: one who is not obligated in that mitzvah is not permitted to repair it.

"And a minor's law is like a woman's." In siman 39 — the reason a minor is unfit for writing tefillin, and the same for tzitzit per the stringent, who derive tzitzit from tefillin — namely until he is thirteen years old and brings two hairs, as stated end of siman 39.
