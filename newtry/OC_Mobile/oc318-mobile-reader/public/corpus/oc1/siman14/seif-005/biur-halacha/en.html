"A partners' tallit is obligated," etc. — See in Mishna Berurah in what he wrote; and it speaks of when his fellow is willing, etc. — this is from the Peri Megadim. Although in the essence of his law at first glance much may be settled — for what is lacking in this compared to an ordinary partners' tallit? For there too half of the garment is not his in essence, only borrowed to him, and a borrower the Torah exempts from tzitzit — if so, only half the garment remains with two tzitziyot; nevertheless the Torah revealed to us that we require only that he have a share in the garment itself — and the same applies in our matter. Nevertheless his words are true for another reason, for we rule that even where we do not require "for you," as in tefillin, nevertheless theft is invalid because of "a mitzvah that comes through a transgression," as below in siman 25 seif katan 26 in Magen Avraham; if so, the same in our matter: since the prohibition of theft became mixed into his wearing, his mitzvah of tzitzit comes through a transgression and he does not fulfill the mitzvah with it.
