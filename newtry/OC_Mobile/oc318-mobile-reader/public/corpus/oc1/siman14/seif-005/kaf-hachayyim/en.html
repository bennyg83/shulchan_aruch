5) A tumtum. A tumtum is qualified to make them, because a Torah-law uncertainty is treated stringently by Torah law and the tumtum is obligated to wear tzitzit. According to the authority who expounds 'sons, but not daughters,' it may be said that this means definite sons, but not one whose status is uncertain. The law of an androgynos depends upon the dispute whether the androgynos is a distinct created category. Peri Megadim, Mishbetzot Zahav, ot 1; Pithei Olam, ot 1.
