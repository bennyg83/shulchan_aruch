(subsection 5) exempt, etc. Tosafos and the Rosh wrote that one who recites the blessing has not lost out, like women, etc.—so wrote the Rosh, chapter Kol HaBasar, siman 26. To bless on a borrowed tallis it seems better not to bless; even though Rabbeinu Tam said women bless on a sukkah and lulav though they are exempt, etc.—nevertheless a borrowed tallis is not comparable, for in other mitzvos another person is obligated in them while these are exempt because of a change in them, such as a blind person (according to one view a blind person is exempt from all mitzvos) and a woman; but here everyone is exempt when it is not his, yet one who blesses has not lost out, end quote. Tosafos there likewise—from this it is clear that it is better not to bless, for it is not truly comparable to women; rather since it is somewhat similar to women, therefore one who blesses has not lost out.
