(Seif katan 5) Exempt, etc. Tosafos and Rosh write that one who recites the blessing has not lost, like women, etc. Rosh writes in chapter Kol HaBasar, siman 26: “Regarding a borrowed tallis, it appears preferable not to recite a blessing. Although Rabbeinu Tam held that women recite blessings over sukkah and lulav even though they are exempt, a borrowed tallis is not fully comparable. Regarding other mitzvos, another person is obligated and these individuals are exempt because of a difference in their status, such as a blind person according to the view that he is exempt, or a woman. Here, however, every person is exempt when the garment is not his. Nevertheless, one who recites the blessing has not lost.” Tosafos writes the same. This shows that it is preferable not to recite a blessing, because the comparison to women is only partial.
