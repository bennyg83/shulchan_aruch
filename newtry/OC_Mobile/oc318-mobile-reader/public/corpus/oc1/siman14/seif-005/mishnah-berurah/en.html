(17) Jointly owned. A tallis jointly owned by a non-Jew and Jew, or by a man and woman, is obligated in tzitzis, but no blessing should be recited over it. Artzos HaChayim, unlike Damesek Eliezer; see there.
(18) Is obligated. This refers to a case where the other partner consents to his wearing it. If partners own a tallis with tzitzis and one objects to the other's wearing it, then even if the garment is divisible and each half contains the minimum tallis size, one who wears it through theft and recites a blessing violates the prohibition against taking Hashem's Name in vain, because the half containing two of the tzitzis does not belong to him. This is all the more true if tzitzis have not yet been attached. Peri Megadim. Accordingly, regarding a tallis inherited from one's father, if the other heirs object to his use, he should not recite a blessing until they reach an agreement.
