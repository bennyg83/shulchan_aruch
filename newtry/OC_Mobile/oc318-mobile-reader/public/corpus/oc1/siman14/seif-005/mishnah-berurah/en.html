(17) Of partners — and a tallit of a partnership of a non-Jew and a Jew, or a man and a woman, is obligated in tzitzis and one does not bless on it. Aruch HaChaim, not like Damesek Eliezer; see there.

(18) Obligated — and we are dealing with where his friend is pleased that his friend go in it and it has no tzitzis; he is obligated to make tzitzis on it. But partners, when there are tzitzis on it and one is particular about his fellow that he not go in it — even if there is enough in it for division and in each part the measure of a tallit — nevertheless, if he wears it through robbery and blesses on it, he transgresses "You shall not take [the name of Hashem your God in vain]," for the portion of one in which there are two tzitzis is not his; and all the more so if there are not yet tzitzis on it [Peri Megadim]. And therefore, regarding a tallit left to the heir from his father and the rest of his brothers are particular about him, he should be careful not to bless on it until they compromise among themselves.
