Women. One does not recite a blessing where the tallis is inherently exempt or the tzitzis are invalid for everyone. Women are different. Although they themselves are exempt, men are obligated, and therefore a blessing applies to women as well, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 7. The same applies to a borrowed tallis. Since the garment is obligated when worn by its owner, the borrower may also recite a blessing over it.
