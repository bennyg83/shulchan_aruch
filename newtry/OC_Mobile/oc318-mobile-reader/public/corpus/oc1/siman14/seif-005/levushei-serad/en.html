{Rama: Women.} For that which they do not bless where the tallit is not one obligated, or the tzitzit are invalid — this is specifically when the invalidity or exemption applies to the whole world; unlike women, who although exempt, nevertheless since men are obligated, blessing applies to them too, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 7. The same here with borrowing: regarding the owners it is his borrowed garment of one obligated — the borrower too may bless on it.
