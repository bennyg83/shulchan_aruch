On the Rambam. Taz, and see there from this. Magen Avraham (1): according to all, this that excludes a non-Jew is not because it was not for the sake, but from a decree of Scripture; and even a Jew with a gentile standing over, which helps for the Rosh in siman 11 seif 1 — here is invalid, and see there. And behold, it is implied from the language of the Mechaber and Rambam of blessed memory that an act of a Jew without intent — even all the knots and windings — is valid; and do not ask why, for behold "gedilim you shall make for you" for the sake of your obligation applies to spinning and interweaving from shaatnez, and all the more so tying and windings from the word "gedilim" as the Levush wrote in siman 11, that from it we learn "you shall make" and not from what was already made applies to tying (and not insertion); if so, all the more so it also applies "for you" for your obligation to tying and insertion without intent — it would be to validate only; requires study.
