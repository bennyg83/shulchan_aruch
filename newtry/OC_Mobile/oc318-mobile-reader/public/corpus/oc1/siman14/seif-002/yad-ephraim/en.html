In Taz seif 5 — since it is written "your garment" and not of others, etc. In my humble opinion this is not a difficulty, for there "your garment" excludes if he borrowed from his friend a tallit that is not properly tzitzis, for he is exempt; and there this reasoning that he intended in the effective manner to fulfill his obligation does not apply — unlike here, where we deal with one who borrows from his friend a tallit with tzitzis to fulfill his obligation, which is like kiddushin, for certainly he intended in the effective manner. Also what was written — and further it is difficult, etc. — for our case according to what I wrote; however his reasoning is a mere supposition: that in lulav borrowing is invalid from a decree of Scripture, etc. — it is a great stretch, for perhaps Scripture deals with a case such as where the lender does not know that this is needed to fulfill his obligation.
