2) If. If a Jew and a non-Jew attached the tzitzit together, the law depends upon the dispute between Beit Yosef and the Shach concerning slaughter in Yoreh Deah, siman 2, seif 11, where both hold the knife and slaughter: Shulchan Aruch rules the slaughter invalid, while the Shach rules it valid; see there. Pithei Olam there in the name of Responsa Peri Tevuah. Accordingly, for us, who rely upon the rulings of Maran, the tzitzit are invalid.
