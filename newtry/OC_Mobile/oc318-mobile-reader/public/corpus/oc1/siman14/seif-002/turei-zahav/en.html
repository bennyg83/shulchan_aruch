“On Rambam who validates.” Meaning Rambam holds even though spinning requires “for the sake” as siman 11 — tying on garment does not require “for the sake” — derives from “Bnei Yisrael” excluding gentile — Jew without intent valid — verse speaks tying — written “corners of their garments.” Beit Yosef difficulty: Rav expounds not gentile — Rav holds spinning without intent valid — say Shmuel disqualifies spinning without intent — also not expound here gentile — invalid Israel spinning without intent — appears to me answer: Talmud should say “and Shmuel this Bnei Yisrael what expounds” — surely Shmuel also expounds Bnei Yisrael not gentile for act on garment — tying — as above.

“But one does not bless,” etc. R. Shimon and Rosh hold tying also requires “for the sake.”
