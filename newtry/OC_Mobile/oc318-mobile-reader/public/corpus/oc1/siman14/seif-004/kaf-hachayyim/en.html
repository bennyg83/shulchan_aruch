4) There, in the gloss. 'Some are stringent and require men to make it, etc.' A minor has the same law as a woman. Magen Avraham, seif katan 3; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there; Chayei Adam there; Chesed LaAlafim, ot 1; Shitilei Zeitim, ot 3. This is so even if an adult stands over him and instructs him to make it for the sake of the mitzvah of tzitzit. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. According to this view, he is disqualified from making them until he is thirteen years old and has grown two hairs, as with writing tefillin in siman 39. Machatzit HaShekel; and Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, ot 3, writes likewise according to Magen Avraham. Although Peri Megadim writes there that this is a forced interpretation, since all the authorities cited above rule like Magen Avraham, and since we initially take Rabbeinu Tam's view into account as Rema writes, one should not initially allow a minor to make them until he is thirteen years old and it is definitively established that he has grown two hairs. This is unlike Pithei Olam, ot 3.
