On the Rambam. See Beer HaTov, and what was written that Taz made himself, etc. — likewise in Birkei Yosef in the name of Yad Aharon, and he wrote that one who examines Taz will see a slight leaning from Beit Yosef, even though everything goes to one place; see there. And what was written in the name of Magen Avraham that we are dealing with Shabbat — they questioned this, for in Shulchan Aruch he wrote if there are no other tzitzis. See in Yad Efraim that one should emend "close to Shabbat," and so I saw emend — and the explanation is that with others one can say he will make only one knot and one winding; but to untie these that require care and to return and tie at least a knot and winding — for this there is no time. See in Machatzis HaShekel who wrote in the name of some scholars that they answered that also on a weekday one finds when prayer time will pass. And what was written — one scholar that one should be concerned lest they be ruined and remain without any mitzvah at all; therefore it is better to rely on the Rambam. And it is not so, since on a weekday he can make — there is no concern they will be lost; and it is better to perform the mitzvah according to all; see there. And according to his words one can say that close to Shabbat, even if there is still time to untie and return and tie — nevertheless, since one cannot do it with care again, one should not do so, for one should be concerned that by untying them without care they will be ruined; see there in Machatzis HaShekel further, that one can say we are dealing with thin and worn tzitzis that are close to being lost if he unties them; see there.
