Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. “As stated at the beginning of siman 15.” Here that reason is unnecessary. Even if the law permitted returning them to the garment because they had once been validly made, the present case is still understandable. If we take Rosh's view into account, the tzitzis were never validly made at all, since they were attached without intention. Reattaching them would therefore constitute the initial making, at which point the required length would be lacking.
