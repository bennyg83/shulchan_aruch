One may take his fellow’s <i>tallis</i> and bless on it provided he folds it if he found it folded. Rema: So too for tefillin (Noda BiYehudah <i>Perek HaSfinah</i>); but one may not study from his fellow’s books without permission lest he tear them in his study (Noda BiYehudah <i>Hilchos Ketanos</i>).)
