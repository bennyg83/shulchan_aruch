Borrowing. Taz — what was written; and this is the reason of seif 4, that one may take a tallit without his friend's knowledge, for a person is pleased and with intent of a gift on condition to return — this is Magen Avraham letter 8 and Taz letter 5 from this. And the Rosh wrote that one blesses on a borrowed tallit with its tzitzis specifically for a tallit designated for tzitzis; but for a garment one does not bless — one can say that borrowing a garment is not a gift on condition to return; see in the next letter.
