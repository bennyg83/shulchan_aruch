Borrowed. See Taz for what he writes. This is also the reason for seif 4, which permits taking a tallis without the owner's knowledge, because a person is pleased for a mitzvah to be performed with his property, and the tallis is treated as a gift on condition that it be returned. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 8, and Taz, seif katan 5. According to Rosh, the blessing over a borrowed tallis with tzitzis applies specifically to a tallis designated for the mitzvah. Over an ordinary four-cornered garment worn as clothing he does not recite a blessing, because one may say it was lent merely as clothing, not as a gift on condition that it be returned.
