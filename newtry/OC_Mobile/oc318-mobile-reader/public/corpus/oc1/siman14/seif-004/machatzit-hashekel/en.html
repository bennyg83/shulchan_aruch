(Seif katan 4) Other tzitzis, etc. Magen Avraham says that the case concerns Shabbos. People ask: if so, why does Beit Yosef write, “if other tzitzis are not available,” implying that if other tzitzis are available they should be attached? On Shabbos that is impossible. They answer that Magen Avraham's reference to Shabbos is not exact. The case is shortly before Shabbos, with insufficient time to remove and reattach the existing strings with proper intention, but enough time to cut them off and attach different strings. Alternatively, Magen Avraham may mean Shabbos literally. The printed word “available,” placed in parentheses in Shulchan Aruch, is an error. The text should read, “if there are no other tzitzis with which to validate it.” Beit Yosef's meaning is then not that other strings are unavailable, but that the time, namely Shabbos, prevents using them.
Alternatively.
For example, one end was severed, etc. Enough remained to tie a bow, so the tzitzis are presently valid under siman 12. If they are removed and reattached, however, doing so is forbidden initially, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 15.
