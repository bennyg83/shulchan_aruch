(א) (subsection 4) tzitzis, etc. They seem to deal with Shabbos. People challenge: if so, why does the Beit Yosef write “if no other tzitzis are available”—implying that if other tzitzis are available one must attach other tzitzis to it; but if it refers to Shabbos that is impossible. They answer: what Magen Avraham wrote—that it refers to Shabbos—is not precise; rather close to Shabbos, so there is no time to untie the tzitzis and reattach them with intention. But there is time to sever the tzitzis and attach other tzitzis to it. One can also say further that what Magen Avraham wrote—that it refers to Shabbos—is precise, but what is printed in the Shulchan Aruch, the word “available” between two halves of a circle, is a misprint; it should read “if no other tzitzis to validate it.” And if so one can say that this was indeed the Beit Yosef’s intent in what he wrote “if no other tzitzis to validate it”—not because they are unavailable but because time causes it to be Shabbos.

(ב) Alternatively.

(ג) For example if one snapped, etc. Enough remained for a knot, so now they are valid as explained in siman 12. If he unties them and reattaches them it is forbidden in such a case lechatchila, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 15.
