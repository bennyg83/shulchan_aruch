Section 4, "It is permitted, etc." As is stated in Pesachim, page 2, and in Bekhorot 18.
"And the same applies to tefillin." This means: although we say in Bava Metzia 29b, "One who finds tefillin, etc.," that does not mean that otherwise it would be forbidden to put them on. Rather, it comes to teach a permission, unlike the Nimukei Yosef.
"But it is forbidden, etc." As is stated there: "One who borrows a Torah scroll, etc.; one might have said, etc." The reason is that in a case of loss we do not say, "A person is pleased, etc." This is what was stated above, "provided, etc.," and as stated in Bekhorot 18b: "Rabbi Tarfon holds, etc., and Rabbi Akiva holds, etc."
