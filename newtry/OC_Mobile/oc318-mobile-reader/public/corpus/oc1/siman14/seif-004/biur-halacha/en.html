"One may take his fellow's tallit," etc. — A question: is a person obligated to lend his tallit to one who lives in a village so that he will need to borrow a tallit from others in his city? He ruled in the responsum of Beit Yaakov in siman 214 that he is not obligated to lend his tallit to another, and he may bless on a borrowed tallit — see there.
