Shulchan Aruch, seif 2. “But he should not recite a blessing.” It appears to me that he likewise may not go out wearing them in a public domain, since this resembles reciting the blessing. The same is implied by Magen Avraham at the end of siman 12. Although all the tzitzis are invalid, and according to Taz at the beginning of siman 13 one might say that they are insignificant and become subordinate to the garment, these strings remain significant because they can be removed and reattached with intention. They are also significant because he may rely upon them when no other tzitzis are available, as I wrote regarding the cited Magen Avraham. Consider this carefully. Magen Avraham, seif katan 5.
