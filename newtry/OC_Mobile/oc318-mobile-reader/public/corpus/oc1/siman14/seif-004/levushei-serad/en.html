{Rama: Shulchan Aruch seif 2:} but he does not bless. It seems to me that likewise he does not fulfill through them according to Rabeinu Tam, for this is comparable to a blessing, as is found in Magen Avraham end of siman 12. And although all the tzitziyot are invalid — if so, one might say per the Taz end of siman 13 that they are not considered and are nullified — nevertheless one may say that these are properly considered, since he can permit them and tie them with intent; also one may say they are considered because he will rely on them when he does not find other tzitzit, as stated on the words of that Magen Avraham, see there, examine well: Magen Avraham seif katan 5.
