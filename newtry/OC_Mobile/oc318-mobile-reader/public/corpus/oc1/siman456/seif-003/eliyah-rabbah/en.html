He will not knead it well, and the flour will enter a cooked dish and become chametz, as is written at the end of siman 459. The Rama wrote: and it is good to say, at the time of putting the flour into the measuring vessel, that he is doing it for the sake of the mitzvah; end quote. And this is the language of Mateh Moshe: and one must say [it is] for the sake of matzas mitzvah, whether in making the matzos of the mitzvah, or in drawing the water, or in measuring the flour, or in putting water into the flour.
