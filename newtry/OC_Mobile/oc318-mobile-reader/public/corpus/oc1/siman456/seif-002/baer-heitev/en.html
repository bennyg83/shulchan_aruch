And Magen Avraham wrote that the world practices even l'chatchila to be lenient and one lacks power to protest in their hands because there are those who permit; and Kaf HaChayyim wrote, nevertheless it appears to me that in any case if they make more than three kav one should protest, because perhaps in such a case even b'dieved one should forbid; and in any case it is good to warn and urge and hurry its making and not to delay with it — end quote. And Magen Avraham wrote: and if they left it for the measure of a mil before the helpers began to assist at the time of shaping, it is forbidden — Bach end of siman 459.
