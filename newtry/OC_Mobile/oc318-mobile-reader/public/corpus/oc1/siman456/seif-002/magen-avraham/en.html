It is permitted after the fact. For even though working it is of no benefit, nevertheless they do not leave it the measure of a mil (the Rosh and the Ran). And if they left it the measure of a mil before the helpers began to assist at the time of shaping, it is forbidden (Bach, siman 459). And one should be stringent, for the Rosh's proof from the Lechem HaPanim, which were two issaron measures, is no proof; for if they said [it] regarding those who are diligent, would they say [it] regarding those who are not diligent, as is stated in Beitzah and in Pesachim; see siman 460. And the world practices even ab initio to be lenient, and there is no power to protest against them, and there are those who permit.
