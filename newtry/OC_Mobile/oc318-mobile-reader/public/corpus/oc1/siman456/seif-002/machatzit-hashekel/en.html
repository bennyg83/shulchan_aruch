(s.k. 2) And likewise etc. Not like R"av etc.; and Tur wrote further a matter how to measure. Surely it is impossible to fill a vessel with 43 eggs and the vessel holding them will be the measure for flour — for such a vessel is more than the measure, because the air added between egg and egg; and also impossible to measure by weight, because weight of eggs and flour is not equal. But Tur wrote that it is possible to fill a vessel with water and put in it 43 eggs, and the water displaced is the measure of 43 eggs; and even so Tur wrote to measure as written here in Shulchan Aruch is preferable. And these are Tur's words: so one cannot aim precisely in putting the eggs into it when it is full — end quote.

And so in the name of Maharik etc. See Shach Yoreh Deah. And Kaf HaChayyim and Alef Resh wrote in the name of Taz that it is the measure of a pint Praeger. And I measured and it is nearly seven small zeidelach from the old Mahari"as; and therefore since measures change in every generation, each one must measure per the value of the measure in his days and time.
