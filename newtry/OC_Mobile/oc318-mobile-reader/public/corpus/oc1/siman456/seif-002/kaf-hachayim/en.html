(2) There. One does not knead on Pesach a large dough etc. — see in Tur and Beit Yosef that some say this is specifically for the early ones whose ovens were small and there is concern in every dough to warm it; but our ovens that are very large, it is preferable more that one knead much together and they be occupied with the dough always so it not come to chimutz — except that the other geonim were stringent in the matter — see there. And so Ri'az that even many women who bake in many ovens — it is forbidden for them on Pesach to knead in one house more than the measure of challah, because perhaps the abundance of dough will come to chimutz even in a brief stay — rather each one kneads separately at the measure of challah. Bach in Beit Yosef chapter Kol Sha'ah. And so in Sefer HaZichronot daf 24. Kenah"H in Hagahot Tzafnat. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Pri Chadash nevertheless ose 3; and even if he has many helpers he should not knead more than this measure. Bach. Pri Chadash there. R"an ose 5; except he wrote there R"an that now the world relies on the first reasoning and we lack power to protest in their hands since they have upon whom to rely; but each person should be stringent for himself — see there.
