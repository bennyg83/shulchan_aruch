(In Magen Avraham s.k. 6 "and one should be stringent") — meaning this law of kneading more than the measure regarding permission b'dieved, since they do not leave it the measure of a mil, one should be stringent in it because Rosh's proof to reject the words of those who are stringent is not a proof etc.; and what Bach interrupted in the words of Bach was according to Rosh's explanation that in this that they left the measure of a mil — also Rosh holds to forbid; but what Magen Avraham wrote "and one should be stringent" refers to the essence of Rosh's law; and so Magen Avraham wrote that the world practices l'chatchila like those who permit — see in Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Maimoniot that they would practice kneading large doughs and rely on the words of Ra'avad etc.; and Mahari Segal wrote Beit Yosef there to permit b'dieved kneading intentionally since there are those who permit even l'chatchila — see there.
