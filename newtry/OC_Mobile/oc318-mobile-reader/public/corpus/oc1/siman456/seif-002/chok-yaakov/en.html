It is permitted after the fact. And the Beis Yosef wrote that even if he transgressed deliberately and kneaded more, one should not forbid after the fact, for there are those who permit even ab initio; and see in Magen Avraham regarding what he challenged against the proofs of the Rosh from the Lechem HaPanim, which were two issaron measures: for if they said [it] regarding those who are diligent, would they say [it] regarding those who are not diligent, as is stated in Beitzah; until here. (And see Tosafos, chapter Kol HaMenachos (Menachos, folio 55a), s.v. Kol HaMenachos niloshos etc., [who say] that the Lechem HaPanim were not made by the diligent nor in a place of the diligent; rather one must say that a communal [offering] is different, as is stated in chapter Kol Sha'ah (Pesachim, folio 36).) And in truth one who looks into the words of the Rosh will see that this is no challenge; for this is what the Rosh means: since the view of those who forbid is for the reason that they hold that more than this measure, working with the hands is of no benefit at all, for they cannot suffice to work it because of its size; therefore the Rosh raises a proper difficulty from the Lechem HaPanim: even though they were made by the diligent, nevertheless working is of no benefit at all in a dough this large; and this is understandable. And Magen Avraham wrote, and this is his language: and the world practices even ab initio to be lenient, and there is no power to protest against them, and there are those who permit; end quote. And nevertheless it appears to me that in any case if they make more than three kavim, one should protest, for it is possible that in such a case even after the fact one should forbid; for I found in the Yerushalmi, chapter Elu Overin, halacha 4, and this is its language: even up to three kavim is the measure of a dough to guard it from chimutz; end quote; and see there in the commentary. And if so, it implies that larger than this it is impossible to guard it from chimutz. And nevertheless it is good to warn as much as possible, as implied by our Talmud and the poskim, [to use] only the measure of one kav, as I wrote the measure in s.k. 3; and in any case to urge and hasten their making and not to leave delay in it; see in the Bach at the end of siman 459.
