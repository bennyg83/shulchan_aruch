(1) [Seif 1] One may not knead a large dough for Pesach, etc. — because sages fixed measure: occupation with kneading, arranging does not save from leavening when larger than isaron; hand does not suffice even if zealous from kneading to baking without pause of mil — forbidden l'chatchila more than isaron. Raz note 1: even if splitting immediately to helpers — concern leavening at kneading and perhaps poor kneading leaving flour, cooks later. If small doughs later combined — forbidden, concern in arranging. Iggerot Moshe note 1; see below note 22.
