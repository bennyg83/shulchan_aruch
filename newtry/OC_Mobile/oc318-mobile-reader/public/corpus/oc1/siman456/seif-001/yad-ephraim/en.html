(Taz s.k. 2) His hand will not suffice to do all the need etc. and it will be like free etc. The word "like" is a textual corruption. And it appears more that it should read it is like somewhat free from hand-work which is forbidden l'chatchila; and likewise more than the measure as it should read; and it is like the words of Rosh brought in Beit Yosef — see there.
