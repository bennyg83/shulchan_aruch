(s.k. 1) Large dough. Even if he bakes in many ovens etc. — excluding the view of R' Yitzchak bar R' Yehudah that Tur brought and in Beit Yosef that wrote this measure was said only in the time of the sages of the Talmud whose ovens were small and easily cooled and perhaps the matzot would need to stand a long time; but our ovens are very large etc. — end quote the Gemara'ei that Beit Yosef brought. Therefore Magen Avraham wrote one should not distinguish, because for another reason they were strict about this measure — that in a measure greater than this they cannot deal with it well; and for this reason one should not distinguish between oven and oven.
