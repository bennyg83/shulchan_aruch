(1) One may not knead etc.: because the Sages measured that the working which one person works with his hand in the dough, in kneading and shaping, does not save it from chimutz when it is larger than the measure mentioned here, because the hands do not suffice to work it from all its sides on account of its size.
(2) Forty-three eggs: as they are, with the shells.
(3) One should measure it etc.: and not by weight; and the later authorities wrote that it is a measure of about three quarts.
(4) And one should not pack [it] down etc.: that is, even if the amount of flour only holds an issaron or less than that; and because if so, he will not knead well in several places where water will not enter there, and it will become chametz when it enters a cooked dish; and one should measure loosely, as they measure when selling; and even to place one's hand upon the flour is not proper ab initio.
(5) At the time of putting etc.: see below in siman 460 in the Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 2.
