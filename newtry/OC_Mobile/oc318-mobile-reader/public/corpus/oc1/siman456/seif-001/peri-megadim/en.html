Leveled — Taz. In Menachot 87a: R' Meir — two isaron: one heaped, one leveled; Rabbanan — one isaron leveled per Rav Chisda; Rashi, Tosafot: between them only one isaron compressed flour from 43 eggs medium hen; other meal-offerings: vessel holds two-thirds isaron heaped one-third; high priest: vessel itself holds 43 eggs and fifth, no heaping; 90a all measures heaped except high priest's pressed into vessel per R' Meir per Rav Chisda — leveled means removal; see above; Rambam Hilchot Klei HaMikdash 1:16 ruled one isaron leveled like Rabbanan without explaining leveled — see Maggid Mishneh; truth follows R' Meir and halacha follows stam then stam; say heaped pressed in because leveled outside (actions: heaped, leveled, heaped, leveled, sealed); Rambam in Mishnah commentary this for R' Meir — heaped into that isaron while leveled as one isaron holds when heaped — needs explanation; appears as Beit Yosef: compressed flour holds more than loose — third view: two isaron in Gemara: one for high priest, one for all offerings, both vessel two-thirds plus heaping one-third; high priest's heaping outside lest fall — Rashi, Tosafot 87a — forced active pressing; say three disputes and this Mishnah for R' Meir per his way — forced. Rav Chisda per his view: R' Meir and Rabbanan all high priest leveled; Rabbanan one leveled for all; if Rabbanan one heaped for all and high priest heaped in — forced compressed flour — Mishnah all measures heaped except high priest necessarily heaped in — forced; Rambam Klei HaMikdash ruled so sometimes or found elsewhere. Rosh: challah measure 43 eggs grain ground much — needs heaping for omer from stalks; likewise rich meal-offering isaron fine flour — all measures heaped, high priest heaped in — no proof against Rosh; not corrupt explanation — Peri Chadash, Peri Megadim 6, Bach study. For law: flour only 43 eggs and fifth for challah and Pesach. Yoreh De'ah 324: author wrote measure not heaped — appears no warning needed; Pesach warning — say measure holds 43 leveled not two-thirds with heaping like high priest lest scatter below measure — Riva in Mordechai; forced Peri Chadash, Kaf HaChayim; Riva: do not heap Pesach measure because after isaron like high priest; what I did not sense: human cannot measure precisely; eggs vary — easy see Chullin 28b s.v. lefi. For challah add if can fulfill Tur, doubt Rosh challah 324; Pesach take compressed measure 43 eggs not more, take challah after in basket not heaped — all the more Pesach; see Magen Avraham note 2.
