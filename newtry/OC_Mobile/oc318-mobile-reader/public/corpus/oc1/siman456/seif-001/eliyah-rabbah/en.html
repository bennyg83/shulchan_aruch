And likewise one should measure etc. And I measured it, and it is a Prague pint, which is two and a half quarts of Ladmor, and which is three Viennese achterling (Malbushei Yom Tov). And what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Mahari, that it is three zeidelach, that means a quart, as the Shach wrote in Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 324; and that is in the lands of Poland, against [the view of] one teacher who stumbled in this. And in places where there are other measures, every sage may measure [accordingly]. However, almost more than this there is no concern, as the poskim wrote; and see the end of this siman.
