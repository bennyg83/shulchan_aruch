(א) And likewise one measures etc. And I measured and it is a pint Praeger and it is two and a half quarts for Ladmer and it is three Viennese kreutzers (Malbush Yom Tov); and what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Mahari that it is three zeidelach — that is quart, as Shach wrote in Yoreh Deah beginning siman 324, and that is in the lands of Poland — unlike one sage who stumbled in this; and in places where there are other measures each sage may measure; however almost more than this there is no concern, as the poskim wrote; and see end of this siman.
