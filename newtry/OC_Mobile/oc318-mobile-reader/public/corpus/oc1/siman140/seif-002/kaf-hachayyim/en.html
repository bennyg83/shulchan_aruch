Maharam Mintz, siman 85; Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos HaTur; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2. And as for what the Machatzis HaShekel emended, 'and even if he did not delay,' etc. -- this happened to him because he did not see the aforementioned responsum of the Maharam Mintz, as he himself testified; and this is astonishing, that they emend and raise difficulties on the words of the poskim before they see the matters at their root. And this difficulty falls also upon the Chayei Adam, ot 23, and the Kerem Shlomo, for they too wrote to emend so; see there.
