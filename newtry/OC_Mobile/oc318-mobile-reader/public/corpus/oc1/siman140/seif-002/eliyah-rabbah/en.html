(b) And he read partially, etc. — it implies that if he interrupted before reading at all he must return and bless; and so is implied in Bedek HaBayit who compares it to blessing of a meal; see there. But it is difficult — for above siman 47 seif 9 regarding blessing on the Torah we say that even if he interrupted before studying he need not return and bless, and it is not similar to blessing on bread and fruits because he is obligated to study always; and it is possible to distinguish between blessing on the Torah of shacharit which is for study one is always obligated, and between blessing of the oleh to the Torah whose main intent is for this — and since he did not begin he returns and blesses; and furthermore I already wrote there that Bach concludes he returns and blesses; see there.
