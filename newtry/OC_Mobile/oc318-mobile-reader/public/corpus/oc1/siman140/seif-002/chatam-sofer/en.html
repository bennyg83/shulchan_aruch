(there) note 4 — lest he cause a blessing in vain. N.B. meaning according to their view he must return and bless; if he had not begun, then he would not cause an unnecessary blessing; but if he already began one verse of Chanukah — then according to all he need not return and bless — then certainly he returns and reads on Rosh Chodesh; and with this Shulchan Aruch and Magen Avraham later siman 484 deal; and not as some explain there; and this requires study.
