(1) The reader, etc. — Yerushalmi chapter 5 of Berachos, Rosh Chodesh in the name of R' Yehuda: he was reading in the Torah and lost his voice — the one standing in his place begins from where the first began, and do not say from where the first stopped — the early ones were blessed before them and not after them, and the later ones were blessed after them and not before them — "The Torah of Heaven is perfect" that all of it be perfect. And Rosh explained, and so is simple, that the last one also blesses before — and this is why he begins from where the first began, so he bless on everything before and after. And R' Yehuda there.

(2) And according to Rambam, etc. — that he explains he relies on the first one's blessings; and if he had begun from where the first stopped then it would appear his reading is separate from the first reading — not so now when it appears as one reading.

(3) And even, etc. — for we judge the chazzan's reading as if he read. There.
