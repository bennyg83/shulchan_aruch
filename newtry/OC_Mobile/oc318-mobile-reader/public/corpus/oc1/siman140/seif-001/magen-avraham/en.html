From the place where the first reader began. If the replacement reader began only where the first stopped and did not recite a new blessing, the earlier verses would have a blessing before them but none after them, while the later verses would have a blessing after them but none before them.
Where the first reader began. This applies even if the interruption lasted long enough to read the entire portion. In Talmudic times only the first reader recited the opening blessing, because the others intended to fulfill their obligation through his blessing. Today each reader recites his own blessings, so the replacement must begin again and bless.
According to Rambam. The first reader’s blessing also covers the replacement. See Beit Yosef.
