(Note 1) From the place, etc. — the early ones were blessed, etc.; for the one who blesses did not intend to exempt the congregation with his blessing but only to exempt himself — if so the first one's blessing does not help for the second reading; and even if the second begins from where the first stopped and blesses before and after — nevertheless the last verses were blessed before them from the "nevertheless" that the first one's verses that he read were not blessed after them; therefore he begins from where the first began and blesses beginning and end.
