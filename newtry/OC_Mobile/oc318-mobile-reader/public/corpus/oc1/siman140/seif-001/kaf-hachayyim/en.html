1) [Seif 1] One who reads from the Torah and becomes unable to continue -- the one who stands in his place should begin, etc. For if you say [he begins] from the place where [the first] stopped, it turns out that the first verses were blessed before them but not after them, and the latter ones were blessed after them but not before them. Tur and Beis Yosef; Taz and Magen Avraham, s.k. 1.
2) There: He should begin from the place where the first one began. And even if he delayed long enough to complete all of it, he need not return to the beginning. Maharam Mintz, siman 85; Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos HaTur; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2. And as for what the Machatzis HaShekel emended, 'and even if he did not delay,' etc. -- this happened to him because he did not see the aforementioned responsum of the Maharam Mintz, as he himself testified; and this is astonishing, that they emend and raise difficulties on the words of the poskim before they see the matters at their root. And this difficulty falls also upon the Chayei Adam, ot 23, and the Kerem Shlomo, for they too wrote to emend so; see there.
3) There: And he blesses at the beginning and at the end. And the same applies if he blessed but did not manage to begin reading before he became unable to continue -- the one who stands in his place blesses at the beginning and at the end. Levush; Mishnah Berurah, ot 1.
