(1) That one must — in Perach Shoshan Orach Chayyim part 1 general rule 1 siman 1 he brought as halachic ruling: if it was the day's order but the mistake was that they showed him above what was already read or below and he must return further up — in each of these cases he need not return and bless, as Mabit and Baal Tikkun Yissachar and Radbaz wrote. And where it is a different portion not from the reading order — Mabit and Baal Tikkun Yissachar hold he must bless, and so Magen Avraham and Taz. And Taz concluded that if the mistake is in a place where one need not roll the sefer Torah he need not bless, for since the sefer Torah is unrolled before him his mind is on all that is unrolled; and so Perach ruled — when it is not the day's obligation he must return and bless; and if it is the day's obligation, if they showed him from what was already read he reads from what they showed so the blessing not be in vain; and if it is below he begins reading from where they stopped until that verse they showed him, including it. See responsa Chut HaShani siman 62 and responsa Ginat Veradim Orach Chayyim general rule 1 siman 22, and Yad Aharon and Eliyah Rabbah siman 484.
