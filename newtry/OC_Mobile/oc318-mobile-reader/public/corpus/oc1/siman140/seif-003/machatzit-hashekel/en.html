(Note 3) And according to Rambam the first one's blessing helps — for he holds the blessing the oleh blesses his intent is to exempt the entire congregation; but specifically from that portion he reads; therefore if the second begins from where the first began — then he fulfills with the first one's blessing since he blessed to exempt the congregation; not so when he begins where the first stopped — then it appears he is an oleh by himself and has no connection to the first aliyos, for such is the way of olim that each begins from where the first stopped; and since the second's reading is a separate reading he is not exempted by the first one's blessing, for the first one exempted only his aliyah and his portion as explained. For even if we say the second should bless before — still the first verses were not blessed after them as explained; therefore he begins from where the first began and then he need not bless before, being exempted by the first one's blessing that exempted the congregation from blessings of this portion, and the second reads that portion and the second blesses after; and the verses were blessed before them and after them. See Beit Yosef and Bach.
