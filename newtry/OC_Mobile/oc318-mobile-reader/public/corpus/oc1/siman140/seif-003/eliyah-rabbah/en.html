(c) And he need not, etc. — even if he already read three verses — so is implied in Beit Yosef. And Olas Tamid wrote it is implied from Rabbenu Ephraim that even if he diverted his mind from reading further and afterward returns and reads again he need not bless again; and for practical law this requires study — until here his words. And indeed according to Bedek HaBayit that I wrote who compares to law of meal blessing — if so also regarding diversion of mind the law is so, and as below siman 178; however since Taz wrote the reason that presumably he intended in the initial blessing that even if he diverts his mind he will return and read — it implies that even when he diverts his mind from reading he need not bless, and one can be lenient in doubt of blessings. He wrote in Drashos Maharil: once the reader erred and blessed "who gave us the Torah of truth," etc., at the beginning and they rebuked him and he returned and began "who chose us" and Maharil rebuked him again and required him to return and begin "Baruch Atah" at the beginning of the blessing — until here. And in Be'er Sheva page 102 — no author among the later ones brought Maharil's words, and it is a matter — therefore I toiled and found he has no reward at all — until here. And he extended greatly to distinguish against him; and his main proof that we already learned order of blessings does not prevent regarding blessing of Kerias Shema — and the same law for other blessings; therefore he concludes if he did not remember until after he finished the blessing then he should be completely silent and say "who chose us" at the end after the reading, for there is no order to blessings; and Magen Avraham brought this for practical law in siman 139 — until here. And I set to my heart to settle the words of the great Maharil who did so in practice; and specifically I found in composition Mateh Moshe he brought Maharil's words as halachic ruling. Therefore it appears to me to distinguish that it is not similar to blessing of Kerias Shema — for we hold blessings are independent and were not enacted on Kerias Shema as Beit Yosef wrote siman 46 and siman 60; and similarly I questioned my brother-in-law the rabbi R' Yaakov on what he wrote in responsa wondering on what Taz wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 279 that one should not say a blessing on what he will recite by heart — and he wondered: but Kerias Shema is said by heart and they bless on it — until here. And I questioned him that blessings were not enacted on Kerias Shema. And he answered me that since Levush siman 235 writes they are not blessings on Kerias Shema — for if so we should bless to read Shema as they say to engage in words of Torah, etc. — apparently regarding the Torah that one does not bless except "giver of the Torah" also was not enacted on the Torah — until here his words. And I questioned that if so let us say one blesses blessing on the Torah even if he read without blessing, as one blesses blessing of Kerias Shema without reading Shema; and furthermore the source of the law of blessing of Kerias Shema Beit Yosef wrote in name of responsa of Rashba, and I found in responsa siman 319 he wrote these words: for he does not bless to read Shema or giver of the Torah as they enacted in Torah reading — until here his words; behold it is clear blessing on the Torah was enacted and Kerias Shema blessings are different; and furthermore I found in Abudraham page 31 these words: the reason one does not bless on Kerias Shema as other mitzvot is because parshas Shema has mention of Heaven and also is considered like malchus and behold it is important as a blessing — until here his words; and this does not apply to blessing on the Torah; and even Rabbeinu Yonah whom Beit Yosef brought siman 59 who wrote one should not interrupt Amen between blessings of Kerias Shema and reading Shema — it appears to me he also holds they are blessings of praise and incidentally they attached them to blessing of Kerias Shema; and understand well. In summary there is no proof from blessing of Kerias Shema; and furthermore consider yourself that if he blessed on beer initially "borei nefashos rabos" he fulfills — behold blessing on the Torah is also like blessing on enjoyment; and furthermore I found in R' David Abudraham page 49 these words: they did not enact in the blessing "life of the world planted within us" because it is not a tree of life until one grasps it — until here; if so the first blessing is not appropriate at all. Therefore I say one should rely on Maharil's ruling; however if he remembered before he finished "Baruch Atah Heaven noten haTorah" — it appears he begins "asher bachar banu" and not "Baruch Atah"; and as Be'er Sheva wrote in his conclusion there — these words: if he erred and blessed "who gave," etc., and remembered immediately within kedei dibbur — it is forbidden to begin from the beginning of the blessing "Baruch Atah Heaven" — until here. And indeed this requires study — apparently what is "remembered immediately within kedei dibbur" when he says he did not finish the blessing; and within the blessing itself it is strained to distinguish within kedei dibbur; and one cannot say it deals when he finished, for he wrote he should be completely silent as I brought above; and it is strained to say it deals after kedei dibbur when he finished but within kedei dibbur he begins "asher bachar." But from siman 206 there is proof — he blessed "borei pri ha'adamah" on beer and within kedei dibbur remembered he erred and said "shehakol nihyeh bidvaro" — he fulfilled; except one can distinguish there he does not seal the blessing, but here he seals the blessing immediately when he finishes — perhaps it is an interruption; and so is implied in siman 59. In summary: immediately when he finishes he returns and begins "Baruch Atah Heaven" and mentions — and with this Maharil deals; but all who did not finish — it appears Maharil agrees he begins "asher bachar banu" and not "Baruch Atah Heaven" as I brought Be'er Sheva proof from blessing Yotzer Or in siman 59 and blessing on beer later siman 209 that one does not begin at the beginning of the blessing; also in this I can reject his proofs — Yotzer Or is different as they say in Berachos page 12 in order to mention attribute of night by day, etc.; and blessing on beer one can distinguish since it is an unresolved question and poskim ruled leniently that one need not begin beginning of blessing because doubt of rabbinic law is lenient as Beit Yosef wrote siman 209; but blessing on the Torah — behold Be'er Sheva himself page 58 wrote it is d'oraisa; and nevertheless for practical law it appears since he did not finish he does not begin from "Baruch Atah" — for later in siman 144 I questioned his words and raised that they are d'rabbanan.
