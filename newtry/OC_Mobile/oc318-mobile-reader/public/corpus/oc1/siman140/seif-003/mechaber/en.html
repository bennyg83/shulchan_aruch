One who went up to read from the Torah, and they showed [the person] the place where [that person] should read from, and [the person] blessed over the Torah and started to read or [even] did not start to read, and then they reminded [the person] that [the person] is supposed to read a different parasha [section of the reading], and [then the person] rolled the Torah scroll to the place [the person] is supposed to read: there are those who say that [the person] does not need to go back and bless [again], and there are those who say there is a need [to do so].
