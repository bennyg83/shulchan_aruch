(3) Seif 3 — the oleh, etc. — in Yerushalmi chapter 6 of Berachos: R' Zerikan said — R' Zeira asked: one who took a turnip and blessed on it and it fell from his hand — what is the law? Do they bless on it a second time? What is between him and the truth of the water? They say there: for this, since his mind was initially; but here not so, since his mind was initially; and so too he did not intend on this portion. And he taught: R' Chiya taught — one does not bless on bread except when he spreads it. R' Chiya bar Aba said this teaches: one who took a radish and blessed on it and it did not come to his hand — he needs to bless on it a second time; and the reason is because of the interruption of his taking, even though he interrupted in silence; and so too here he interrupted in rolling. And the reason of the first section — in the question there he holds as Raavad wrote that the question is lenient; also the end of R' Chiya bar Aba — halachah is not like him, for he holds like R' Chiya; and we hold like Rava in Berachos 39. But poskim hold the question is strict as written in siman 206 seif 6; and he also explained this of R' Chiya bar Aba as written seif 5; and see Tosafos and Rosh there and Terumat HaDeshen who explains Yerushalmi — perhaps the slice will fall from his hand, etc.; and all the more so it comes like Rava; and even though the Gemara disagrees in the reason, nevertheless the law is true — only they explain this of R' Chiya bar Aba also that if it fell from his hand as in the first case; and R' Levi spreads the question that one must bless a second time; and Terumat HaDeshen there. But Rambam chapter 4 of Berachos explains as Shulchan Aruch there seif 5 — only he explains not because of interruption but because it is not before him; and also for Rava it is not so — to exclude before they bring it before him. And also what Raavad wrote that we hold the question lenient; and so Peri Megadim in name of Rabbenu Tam, and he learned from a cup of blessing that all drink from cups before them in the blessing of the one who blesses, as written in chapter Ekev regarding that elder who bowed and drank, and end of chapter 7 of Berachos — all are included in the blessing; and Beit Yosef there poskim hold if it fell it is different; see there. And he began — and even though interruption in the middle does not harm as written in seif 2 — nevertheless here, since the reason is because his mind is not on it even in the middle, he must return and bless, as written in siman 8 seif 12; see there.
