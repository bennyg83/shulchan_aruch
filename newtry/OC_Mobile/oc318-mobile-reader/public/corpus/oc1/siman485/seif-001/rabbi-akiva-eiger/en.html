Seif alef — matzah without specification is forbidden to eat. And one who says an oath that he will not eat in the sukkah of mitzvah — even though all seven days except the first night are permission, we do not say since it takes effect on the rest of the days, for on the rest of the days it also does not take effect, for even though he can eat casually outside the sukkah, nevertheless since he eats fixed meals in the sukkah it is a mitzvah and is called swearing to nullify the mitzvah — Tashbetz part 1 siman 100. And in Chokhmat Shlomo that also on the eighth they sit — if so the language "sukkah of mitzvah" includes the eighth as well; and one may say it is inclusive since it takes effect on the eighth — this requires further study.
