(א) On Passover night, etc. — but if he said Konam upon me on Passover night, it is forbidden, for a vow takes effect on a matter of mitzvah; see Yoreh De'ah siman 239. Bach wrote: one who swore he will not eat maror or will not drink the four cups on Passover night — since it is d'rabbanan, his oath takes effect and we compel him to request annulment of his oath; and if he swore affirmatively, we do not compel him, but it is fitting he should request annulment of his oath.
