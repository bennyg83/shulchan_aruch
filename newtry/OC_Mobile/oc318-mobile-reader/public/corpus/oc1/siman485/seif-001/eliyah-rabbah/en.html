On Pesach night, etc. If he said, “This food is forbidden to me as a konam on Pesach night,” he may not eat it, because a vow takes effect even regarding a mitzvah. See Yoreh Deah, siman 239. Bach writes that if one swore not to eat maror or not to drink the four cups on Pesach night, the oath takes effect because these are Rabbinic obligations, and he is compelled to seek annulment of the oath. If he took an oath but did not use the force of an oath upon himself, he is not compelled to seek annulment. Nevertheless, it is proper that he do so.
