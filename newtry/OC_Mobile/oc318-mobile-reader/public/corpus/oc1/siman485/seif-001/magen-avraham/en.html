(א) Matzah without specification — if so, since it applies to matzah of rabbinic origin, it also applies to matzah of mitzvah; and it appears to me that one who did not recite the Haggadah on Passover night has no compensation, for it is written "for this" — I did not say except at the hour when matzah and maror are placed before you; and it is written "in the evening you shall eat matzot" — from here onward is permission, as Eliyah Rabbah wrote, unlike Peri Chadash.

(ב) He receives lashes and eats, etc. — for it is a vain oath, since an oath does not take effect on a matter of mitzvah; but if he says Konam be my eating matzah upon me on Passover night, it is forbidden, for a vow takes effect on a matter of mitzvah; see Yoreh De'ah siman 239.
