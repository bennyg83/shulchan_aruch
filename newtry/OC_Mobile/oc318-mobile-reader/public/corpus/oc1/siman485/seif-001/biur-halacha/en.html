Oath that one will not eat matzah on Passover night — see in Mor U'Ketzia who wrote that if one said he will not eat this matzah, it is forbidden even if he has no other matzah, and they annul his oath for him. And know that if he said he will not eat any matzah of Passover, the oath takes effect inclusively per the view of most poskim, even though he mentioned Passover explicitly; and therefore one should examine per those poskim that the time for eating matzah is from the Torah only until midnight — if so, when one says he will not eat matzah on Passover night, behold he includes the time of exemption with the time of obligation and the oath takes effect inclusively and it is not a vain oath, unless we strain to say that since he mentioned matzah of Passover night his intent is only on the hour when people fulfill the mitzvah of matzah and not afterward — and that is strained, for per that view he would be permitted to eat after midnight; this requires further study.
