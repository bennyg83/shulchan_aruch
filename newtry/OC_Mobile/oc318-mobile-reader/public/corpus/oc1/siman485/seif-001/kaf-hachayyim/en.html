1) [Se'if 1] One said, 'I swear that I will not eat matzah,' etc. The reason is that an inclusive oath can take effect to negate a mitzvah through passive nonperformance. Since this oath takes effect upon matzah eaten throughout the year, it likewise takes effect upon matzah eaten on the nights of Pesach. Beis Yosef in the name of Ran; Taz, subsection 1; Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1.
