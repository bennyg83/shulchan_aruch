(א) [Seif 1] He said an oath that he will not eat matzah, etc. — and the reason is because the oath came to nullify the mitzvah of sit and do not do inclusively; therefore this oath, since it takes effect on matzah of the whole year, also takes effect on matzah of Passover night — Beit Yosef in the name of Ran. Taz seif kaf alef. Magen Avraham seif kaf alef. Chayei Adam letter alef.
