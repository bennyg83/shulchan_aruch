Even if he scheduled his work - For with a food need they did not decree about one who scheduled his work.
He should not use artifice - It appears to me that if he used artifice, it is permitted to eat it; see siman 503 seif 3.
Or old beer - Rambam's view is that even though he has old beer, he may use artifice and drink from the new, for this artifice is not recognizable to one who sees it; and the same applies to anything similar, end quote. The commentators asked against him; see Beit Yosef. It appears to me that Rambam holds this from the fact that the anonymous Talmud in Perek Tolin asks from this and answers: here the matter is not evident; and Rashi explains that not everyone knows he has old beer. We see that the halachah is so, and this appears clear to me. So too it appears in siman 335 at the end of seif 3; see what is written there and in the Gemara.
The same applies with beer - And with wheat and with anything.
