(15) He may pickle them: that is to say, even though afterwards they will not be fit for all the days of the festival, and if so he toiled only for the weekday days, even so it is permitted, for it is a davar ha'aved.
(16) And if it is something that is not found etc.: and the same law applies if it will become more expensive afterwards, as with merchandise below in siman 539, se'if 5.
(17) It is permitted to buy it and pickle it: even though they are not fit for the moed itself; and the reason is that we compare it to merchandise, which is permitted in such a case, as below in siman 539, se'if 5. And see Biur Halacha in the name of the Acharonim that this law is not agreed upon by all opinions.
