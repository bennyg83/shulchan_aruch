(s.k. 3) Or beer etc. And the opinion of the Rambam etc. And the commentators raised a difficulty against him. And this matter, whether it is permitted to use a device, is a dispute of the first Tanna and Rabbi Yehudah bar Rabbi Yosei in Moed Katan (folio 12b): according to the first Tanna it is forbidden, and Rabbi Yehudah bar Rabbi Yosei permits. If so, why did he rule like Rabbi Yehudah bar Rabbi Yosei against the first Tanna?
In chapter "Tolinin." In Shabbos folio 139b. Regarding that which is taught there, that the Sages say one does not hang the strainer on Yom Tov to filter wine lees with it, and the clear wine in it flows, because it appears like weekday labor; but if it is already hanging, one may put lees into it to filter the wine. And Rabbah bar Rabbi Huna said: a person may use a device with the strainer on Yom Tov to hang it in order to put pomegranates into it, for that is permitted; and afterwards he hangs lees in it. And Rav Ashi said: and that is when he [actually] hung pomegranates in it; but otherwise it is ha'aramah and forbidden. And the Gemara objects against Rav Ashi: how is this different from that which is taught: one may cast beer on the moed for the need of the moed, etc.; even though they have old, they use a device and drink from the new. Behold, it is permitted to use a device. And it answers: there the matter is not proven; here the matter is proven. And this beraisa the Gemara establishes in Moed Katan according to Rabbi Yehudah bar Rabbi Yosei mentioned above. If so, rather than objecting from Rabbi Yehudah bar Rabbi Yosei, one should support [the law] from the first Tanna. Therefore one must say that we hold like Rabbi Yehudah bar Rabbi Yosei. Therefore the Rambam ruled like him.
And that which the Rambam wrote, that this ha'aramah is not noticeable: that is as the Gemara answered above, "there the matter is not proven."
