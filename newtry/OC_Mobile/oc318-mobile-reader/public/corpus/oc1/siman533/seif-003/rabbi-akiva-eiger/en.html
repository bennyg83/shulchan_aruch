Se'if 3. It is permitted to buy. This is according to the first answer of the Rosh at the end of the first chapter, who distinguished: that which is permitted regarding a caravan, and regarding fish we need the reason that they are eaten along with the charred matter; for a find is different, since there is no loss, only mere profit; but regarding merchandise, its cancellation is a loss. If so, here to gather them is forbidden, but to buy them, which is merchandise, is permitted. But according to the other answer, we permit only by way of buying and selling, where there is no melacha and no exertion; but salting fish is called a melacha and exertion, and is forbidden. If so, to buy them and pickle them is forbidden, for pickling involves great exertion and is not permitted on account of profit. If so, according to the Ran, who copied only the second answer of the Rosh mentioned above, it is forbidden to buy them and pickle them. And in truth one must examine why the Tur ruled to be lenient, which is according to the first answer of the Rosh; for in most places the latter answer is the primary one. And in Magen Avraham, s.k. 6, he brought the beginning of the Ran's words that a complete melacha is forbidden, and upon this he wrote: but to buy it is permitted; and in my humble opinion, according to the Ran it is forbidden to buy them and pickle them.
