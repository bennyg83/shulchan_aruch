(א) The stock. The artists who do a lot together and seem to be doing for sand, therefore, are swayed:

(ב) Animals and birds — the Rama's intent is to inform that fish are permitted, as he wrote in seif 4 in the name of Mordechai; nevertheless it requires study from where he derives this, for Mordechai's words deal with one who is not a craftsman as the gemara he cited states; but Rabbeinu Yonah ruled like the Rambam that specifically a craftsman is forbidden because he does much at once — if so, fish too would be forbidden publicly. And it appears to me that one who has a large pond is forbidden to dig on Chol HaMoed so the waters flow in order that fish be trapped — for the matter is more evident that it is a weekday need; and so in the gemara first chapter of Moed Katan that Rava permitted him only to trap after gentiles dug — it implies that l'chatchila it is forbidden to do so if it belongs to an Israelite; and requires study — one could say it was an actual case; and see siman 537 seif 8.
