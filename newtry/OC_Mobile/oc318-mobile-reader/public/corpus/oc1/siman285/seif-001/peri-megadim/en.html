Even Taz — it appears to me specifically Atarot and Divon that have Yerushalmi translation — one must return after that translation and read their names; but Reuven and Shimon that have no translation — twice is enough; and for Rashi Reuven and Shimon also need three times; and the custom is like Rashi; and likewise Magen Avraham letter 2. And what he wrote "perhaps they read them" — Eliyahu Rabbah letter 4 in the name of Bachai — that they are names of gentiles and these are acrostics "life to Adam to complete the parashah twice Scripture and once translation." And in Mishnah Berurah he deduced from the word "twice" masculine and not "twice" feminine (for parashah is feminine) that he reads each verse three times — twice Scripture and once translation; Eliyahu Rabbah letter 1; Magen Avraham letter 1. And in siman 283 in Hagahot Tur letter 22 in the name of Rabbeinu Bachai: even though they are gentile names, see there; in such a case in Hagahot Tur in the name of Seder HaYom one does not read translation and afterward Scripture, and also not out of order — rather one reads Scripture first and afterward translation, as brought in Eliyahu Rabbah; and see Magen Avraham further on this:
