Twice Scripture. Shelah writes that one should read the entire parashah twice and then its Targum. Maharshal writes likewise, and this also appears from Megillah 4. Lechem Chamudos, siman 41, however, rules that one should read each verse twice and then its Targum. Hagahos Yesh Nochalin writes this in the name of the Arizal, as does Seder HaYom. The kabbalistic writings state likewise. This also appears from siman 145, for this was the practice when the Targum was publicly recited. They would repeat the final verse after its Targum so as to conclude with Torah. If one is familiar with the cantillation, he should read from a Torah scroll. Radbaz, volume 2; see Sefer Chasidim, simanim 301 and 306. He should immerse after the reading. Shaar HaKavanos. See Shelah concerning immersion; I have no concern here with hidden matters. He should cut his fingernails before the reading. Shelah.
