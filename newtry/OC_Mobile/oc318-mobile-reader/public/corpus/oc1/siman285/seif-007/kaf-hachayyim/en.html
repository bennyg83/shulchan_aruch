7) A person who has a Sefer Torah in his home and also has a Chumash should act as follows. If he is proficient in reading a Sefer Torah with the correct vowels, cantillation, and verse endings, he should read the two Scripture readings from the Sefer Torah, which possesses greater sanctity. This is the principal purpose of the mitzvah of a Sefer Torah: to read from it. If he is not proficient, it is preferable that he read the Scripture twice and the translation once from a Chumash rather than read incorrectly from a Sefer Torah and thereby strip the word sefer of its meaning. Radbaz in the new responsa printed in Furth, siman 529, cited by Knesses HaGedolah; Machazik Berachah, paragraph 2. Even if he is proficient and reads from a Sefer Torah, he should not recite the blessings over Torah reading, because they were instituted only for a public reading, as stated above in siman 139, seif 8; see there.
