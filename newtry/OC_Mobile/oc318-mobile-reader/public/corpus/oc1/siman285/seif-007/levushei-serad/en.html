Per the explanation of R' Chaim. R' Chaim holds the reason for shenayim mikra ve'echad targum is so one will be familiar with what the congregation reads; and if so, likewise for the Festival portion. But the other poskim hold the reason is shnayim mikra ve'echad targum so that each person of Israel reads the entire Torah for himself from beginning to end. And for this reason he ruled in Shulchan Aruch that one need not read the Festival portion — because Magen Avraham s.k. 10 wrote; and per this view what is practiced in reading the haftarah as Rama wrote is also so one completes each year with the congregation every Shabbat what they read — if so, even with many weddings one must read what the congregation reads. And Magen Avraham resolved: the reason of shnayim mikra ve'echad targum for the other poskim does not apply to haftarah at all; and the poskim agree the custom of haftarah is for the reason of R' Chaim lest they call him for maftir and he not be familiar.
