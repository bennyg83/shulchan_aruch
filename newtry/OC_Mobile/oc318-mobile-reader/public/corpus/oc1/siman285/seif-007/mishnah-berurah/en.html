(18) Need not; Meaning that he need not separately read shnayim mikra v'echad Targum, since he has already read the entire Torah, each Shabbos in its proper week. On Hoshana Rabbah he should read shnayim mikra v'echad Targum for V'Zos HaBerachah; if he read it on Shemini Atzeres, he has not lost [Shaarei Teshuvah].
(19) The custom is to read the Haftarah; The reason is that perhaps he will be called the following day for maftir, and he should be proficient and practiced in it. This does not apply to the Torah readings of Yom Tov, which the chazzan reads [Magen Avraham].
(20) That of Shabbos, and not, etc.; Some communities publicly read, besides the Haftarah of the sidrah, several verses from the Haftarah beginning Sos Asis, because it says, 'like a bridegroom who dons splendor,' etc. The ruling teaches that when reading the Haftarah privately, one need not read those verses, because they are common, fluent in everyone's mouth, and known without this reading. On each of the Four Parshiyos, he should read its special Haftarah.
