He need not read, etc. This is because he has already read the entire Torah, week by week, each Shabbos. Terumas HaDeshen. On Erev Simchas Torah he should read Vezos HaBerachah. Shaar HaKavanos.
The Haftarah. He should read it once as Scripture and once in Targum. He should then read a chapter of Tehillim from Kesuvim (the Tehillim of the Day) once as Scripture and once in Targum or another vernacular, followed by one Mishnah. Shelah in the name of Rabbi Moshe Cordovero. It is preferable not to interrupt at all with conversation. Knesses HaGedolah.
But not Sos Asis. The reason for reviewing the Haftarah is to know what will be read publicly the next day. If many weddings occur on consecutive weeks, must one review Sos Asis every Shabbos? This requires study, for that reasoning follows Rabbeinu Chananel's explanation. According to his view, one would also have to review the Yom Tov Torah portion so as to be familiar with what the congregation will read, as stated in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 23. Beis Yosef has already ruled, however, that one need not review the Yom Tov portion. One must say that the Haftarah nevertheless requires review for this reason because the person himself may serve as maftir, unlike the Torah reading, which the prayer leader reads. This appears correct. Levush omits this gloss. That itself requires study: if one never reviews the Haftarah Sos Asis, perhaps he will be called to read it and will not be familiar with it, as stated in siman 139. It therefore appears to me that he should review it; if many weddings occur consecutively, however, he need not review it again each week. It is implied that regarding the Four Parashiyos, it is obvious that one should read the Haftarah of the particular special parashah. Knesses HaGedolah writes that his practice is to review the Haftarah of the weekly portion instead; I do not know the reason. See citations 100.
