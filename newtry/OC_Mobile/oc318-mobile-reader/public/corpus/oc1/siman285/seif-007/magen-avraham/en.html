(1) OT to happen 20. He has already recited the whole turn from the day of Shabbat on his Shabbat [15] and on the eve of Shatt he will recite and this is the blessing [Konot]:

(ב) The haftarah — once Scripture and once translation, and afterward a psalm from Writings from the song of day one, once Scripture and once translation or in another language, and afterward one Mishnah [Shelah in the name of Maharik]. And it is good not to interrupt at all in conversation [Maharil]:

(ג) And not Shushan Ashish — for the reason to repeat the haftarah is that he know what they read tomorrow for maftir; and if many weddings occur one after another will he repeat every Shabbat Shushan Ashish [there]? And it requires study — for this is per the explanation of R' Chaim; and according to my view he must repeat parashat Yitro to be accustomed to what the congregation reads, as Tosefta writes siman 23; and the Rabbeynu already ruled one need not repeat parashat Yitro; and it must be said that nevertheless the haftarah must be repeated for this because he himself is maftir, unlike in the reading where the chazzan reads as above; and in Levush he omitted this gloss that requires study — for if he never repeats, perhaps Shushan Ashish haftarah will be read for him and he will not be accustomed to it as siman 139 writes; therefore it appears to me he should repeat it; but if there are many weddings one after another he need not again; and it implies in four parshiyot it is obvious he reads the haftarah of the four parshiyot; and in Kenesset HaGedolah he wrote his custom to repeat the haftarah of the sidra and I did not know the reason:
