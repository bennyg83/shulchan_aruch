(s.k. 2) Atarot and Divon — and likewise Reuven, etc. — meaning that which we say in tractate Berachot: one should always complete, etc., even Atarot and Divon — Rashi explained it is for abundance even though there is no translation on it, the Scripture must be three times. And Tosafot challenged him: if so why did he mention Atarot and Divon — he should have said Reuven and Shimon that have no translation; therefore he explained he mentioned Atarot and Divon that have no Onkelos translation but have Yerushalmi translation. And it teaches us that nevertheless one must read the Yerushalmi translation three times since they lack Onkelos; and Tur and Rosh wrote he mentioned Atarot and Divon for abundance — for one might think since they are names of cities, even though there is Yerushalmi translation, nevertheless one need not translate them; it teaches that even though they are city names one must translate since there is translation in them. And he wrote accordingly: Reuven and Shimon that have no translation at all need not read Scripture three times; nevertheless Rosh concluded to be stringent like Rashi and also Reuven and Shimon need three times Scripture. Shelah wrote: since they write Targum Yerushalmi on Rashi — and these words are not plausible, for we find many other verses that have no translation such as verses of priestly blessing and many others — why did he not mention them, etc., end Targum Yerushalmi. And Shelah wrote on this: and even though in our chumashim there is translation on these verses, it is possible this translation is not received from Sinai — therefore one reads Scripture three times in priestly blessing; and also perhaps the translation is received from Sinai for priestly blessing and there was no megillah in the days of Targum Yerushalmi — therefore one also reads translation of priestly blessing at this time; and if so, likewise for Atarot and Divon per Shelah one reads Scripture twice as Rashi explained and also reads their translation, lest their translation of Atarot and Divon also is received from Sinai but was not revealed in the days of Rashi as he said regarding priestly blessing:
