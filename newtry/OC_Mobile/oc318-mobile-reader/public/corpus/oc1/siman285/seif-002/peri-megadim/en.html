(א) The phrase of the verse of the Gemara, and the second time, with the sign of the verse, is once again mentioned in the word with the reader’s ear, and the book is a sign of “What is written to him” You may say to them by saying, “I will tell you what you are saying in the book that you are saying, ‘I will say in an exact manner that you are doing, but I will say, ‘No, I am a sign.’

(ב) The reason the Sages obligated shenayim mikra ve'echad targum corresponding to Sinai, the Tent of Meeting, and Mishneh Torah was explained well — and this translation is Magen Avraham Talmud Torah always letter 2; and see Knesset HaGedolah. In Magen Avraham Talmud Torah one should be stringent as Rashi explained apparently; according to Tosafos' explanation there is also a stringency that one must repeat after the Yerushalmi translation at Atarot and Divon; and Bach and in Shulchan Aruch omitted this — only three times Hebrew, and likewise Reuven and Shimon, and one need not write for it is understood.
