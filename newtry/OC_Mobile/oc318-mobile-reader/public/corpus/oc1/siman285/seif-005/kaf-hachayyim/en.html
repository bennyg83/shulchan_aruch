5) When reading the Ten Commandments, one must take care to read them twice in the Scripture and once in translation according to the cantillation used for the ordinary division into verses, in accordance with Responsa Masas Binyamin, siman 6, and Shtei Yados. To avoid error, he should consult the Targum in a Mikraos Gedolos, which contains only the cantillation for the ordinary division into verses, the form prescribed for an individual's reading. Regarding the words Lo Sirtzach, Lo Sinaf, Lo Signov, and Lo Saaneh, however, one must take care to read them together twice in the Scripture and then once in translation, because they all constitute one verse, as Masas Binyamin writes there. One may not divide a verse into sections and read each section twice in the Scripture and once in translation. Rather, he must first read the entire verse twice in the Scripture and then once in translation. Beis David, siman 111; see there.
