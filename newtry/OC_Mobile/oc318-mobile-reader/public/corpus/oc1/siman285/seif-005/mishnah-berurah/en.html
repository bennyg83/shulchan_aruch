(14) During the Torah reading; In siman 146, Magen Avraham 5 cites the Shelah, who is stringent and requires everyone to hear the reading from the shaliach tzibbur. This is also the view of the Vilna Gaon recorded in Maaseh Rav: one must hear every word, look in a Chumash, and not assist the reader at all. They evidently regard as primary the view of Shibbolei HaLeket in the name of his teacher and of Maharam, cited in Hagahos Maimoniyos and Beis Yosef there, that reading is permitted only between aliyos. Nevertheless, one need not be stringent against reading quietly word for word together with the shaliach tzibbur, since he also intends to hear every word from him. Magen Avraham cites Mateh Moshe that this is initially proper, and several later authorities agree.
(15) See above, siman 146; There the Mechaber concludes in se'if 2 that one who is meticulous in his conduct should concentrate on hearing the words from the reader, because he also took into account the authorities cited above.
