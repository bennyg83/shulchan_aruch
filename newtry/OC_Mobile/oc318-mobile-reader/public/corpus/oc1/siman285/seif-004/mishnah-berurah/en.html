(9) That he complete it, etc.; The Midrash relates that Rebbe instructed his sons: 'Do not eat bread on Shabbos until you have completed the entire portion.' Nevertheless, it is obvious that one should not delay eating beyond midday for this reason, as explained below in siman 288, for this is only a mitzvah rather than an absolute bar. Ideally, if one did not read shnayim mikra v'echad Targum on Friday, he should arise early on Shabbos morning and read it before going to the synagogue [Or Zarua, beginning of Hilchos Shabbos; likewise implied by Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah, beginning of Berachos].
(10) Until Minchah; For from Minchah onward the congregation begins reading the next week's portion.
(11) Until Wednesday; For the first three weekdays still have some connection to the preceding Shabbos, as explained below in siman 299:6 regarding Havdalah; therefore, after the fact, he may complete it then.
(12) Until Shemini Atzeres; This refers to Eretz Yisrael, where Shemini Atzeres is observed for only one day and the congregation completes the Torah then. Therefore, after the fact, completing his portions by that day is considered completing them with the congregation. Initially, however, everyone agrees that he should not delay beyond that Shabbos.
(13) When the congregation completes it; He should then read twice Scripture and once Targum, unlike those who read Scripture alone, since he is also missing the Targum.
