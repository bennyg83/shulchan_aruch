6) One should not read the Scripture once, then the translation, and afterward the Scripture again. Rather, he should read the Scripture twice and then the translation once. Lechem Chamudos, paragraph 41; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 2. Shelah, folio 131, similarly writes that the translation must be recited last, after the two readings of the Scripture, and not between them. This is the implication of the Gemara's expression, 'the Scripture twice and the translation once,' and is also demonstrated by the Zohar and the words of the kabbalists; see there. Mateh Yehudah, paragraph 1, cites this.
