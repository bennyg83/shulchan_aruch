(16) Teachers of children; This applies specifically in their times, when teachers studied the explanation of Scripture with the children, thereby also fulfilling Targum as described above in se'if 2. If he teaches only the meanings of the words, he has not fulfilled Targum, but he has certainly read Scripture twice with the children; he must therefore recite Targum himself [Machatzis HaShekel; Pri Megadim likewise inclines to this view].
(17) They need not, etc.; From here it follows that the cantillation melody is not indispensable for shnayim mikra v'echad Targum [Pri Megadim].
