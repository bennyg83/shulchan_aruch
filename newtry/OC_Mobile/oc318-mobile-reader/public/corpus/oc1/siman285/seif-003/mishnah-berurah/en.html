(7) From Sunday onward; Since the coming week's portion begins to be read at Shabbos Minchah, one who reads from then onward is already considered to be reading together with the congregation. Thus, the Mechaber's phrase 'from Sunday onward' is not exact.
(8) Is considered together with the congregation; Several later authorities write that the ideal is to read it on Friday. See Shaarei Teshuvah. Mateh Yehudah writes that reading it from the beginning of the week onward is likewise included in the ideal practice, as Beis Yosef shows from Rambam. Maaseh Rav similarly records that the Vilna Gaon would read a portion of the sidrah, twice Scripture and once Targum, immediately after prayer each day, and finish on Friday.
