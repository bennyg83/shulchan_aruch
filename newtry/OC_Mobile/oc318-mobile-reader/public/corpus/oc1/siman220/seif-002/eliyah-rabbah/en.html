[2] [Levush] For good seven etc. Mitzvah to attend with three to improve because restores soul of faster (end of section). Levush Yom Tov wrote for one explanation of Tosafot — do not say seven times for not part of saying it but Talmud says they must say seven times — and so is custom — until here his words; and from all other poskim does not imply so. Bach wrote in name of Rashal to practice saying three times chalma tava chazai and they answer four times tava hu until seven times; and afterward say again three times chalma tava chazai and they answer three times tava hu etc. seven times — and by this fulfills both explanations in Tosafot — until here. Nevertheless Bach concluded world practices saying all seven times and Perishah challenged Rashal's words and concluded not to change from saying all together seven times. Beit Yosef and Achronim in name of Kolbo — do not tell him eat in joy your bread for acronym aval; rather thus they tell you in joy eat your bread — until here. I examined Kolbo itself siman 40 — and wrote this language: they tell you eat your bread in joy.
