(Seif 2) Seven times etc. Some say way etc. — meaning in Gemara it states improvers answer after dreamer said chalma tava chazai — they answer tava hu and tava lihyei Rachmana leshavyei letav seven times etc.; therefore some say these two words seven times are also words of incantation — meaning improvers must also say these two words seven times (and meaning unknown as all incantations hidden); if so when they answer incantation three times since not stated in Talmud how many times — ostensibly means like all incantations three suffices; but some say these two words seven times are not from incantation and incantation is only tava hu and Rachmana leshavyei letav; and what Talmud mentioned seven times are Talmud's words that must say incantation seven times.

Therefore four times — meaning and do not conclude two words seven times; and afterward etc. with seven times — meaning say incantation three times and conclude each time also two words seven times; and by this fulfills both views — for necessarily if must say incantation seven times then need not say two words seven times — then first four times without two words seven times are seven times; and if also two words seven times are from incantation and answerers must say — then for them three times suffice and they said at end three times and concluded each time seven times — and need not first four without concluding seven times.

And this when they improve for him in evening — meaning from go eat in joy etc.; therefore improve in evening — for if at dawn how say in joy eat — he must fast for anonymous dream one improves custom that they fast — and this reason wait to improve until evening so merit of fast helps improvement.

For immediately upon accepting fast etc. — as said in Taanit 8b — in days of R' Zeira he decreed not to sit in fast; R' Zeira said we accept it upon us; when decree nullified they sat in fast; he said whence? He said for written to Daniel do not fear Daniel for from first day you gave heart to understand and fast before your God your words were heard — end Gemara.

One who dreamed etc. — he should fast (Shulchan Aruch siman 644); and wrote there — for his friend rebuked therefore did not show him; and if he does not fast good to tell him so he pray for himself — end quote.
