Beautiful is fasting. And repentance with it (Shulchan Aruch); and see Rabbah Kohelet; nevertheless not obligated to fast for Shmuel said dreams speak vanity (Rashba siman 332).

And even on Shabbat. Olat Tamid wrote in name of Kolbo Maharik — if ten fast on Shabbat they pray Minchah aloud and say our affliction at end of prayer; and in his opinion not correct to do so — end quote; and see there siman 219 and Tur this siman and siman 566.
