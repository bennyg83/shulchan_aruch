(1) And his soul troubled over it — meaning even if no implication of evil.

(2) They improve for us etc. — even when he fasts; and good to improve at dawn for zealous precede mitzvah; but then they do not tell him go eat in joy etc. unless when not fasting (Peri Megadim); and remember dream in thought at time of improvement.

(3) Before three etc. — mitzvah to attend with three to improve because restores his soul thereby; and even on Shabbat may improve dream (Avnei Nezer).

(4) And it will be good etc. — seven times for decree etc. — this language answerers must say three times; and some say seven times; and at end of improvement they say go eat your bread in joy and drink with good heart etc. One who dreamed difficult dream about friend should fast (Shulchan Aruch siman 644 see there reason; and further seems sometimes matter hints about dreamer himself but in dream matter not shown only in likeness).
