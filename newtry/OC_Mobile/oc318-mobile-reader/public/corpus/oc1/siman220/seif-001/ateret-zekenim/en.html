One who sees a dream etc. — it is fit to improve the dream at dawn. One who sleeps afternoon sleep and dreams a dream — he should not taste anything immediately after the dream even if midday, even on Shabbat; he should separate from his wife even on Shabbat; forbidden in third meal; if on Shabbat he must fast the day after for he nullified enjoyment of Shabbat; if tomorrow is public fast he must sit in fast another day and public fast does not count for him per nullifying Shabbat enjoyment (She'elot uTeshuvot Masat Binyamin siman 93); and it does not seem so — only public fast counts for both as above; and I wrote these matters in good taste and understanding from words of Talmud and poskim.
