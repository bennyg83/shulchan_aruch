1) [Seif 1] One who sees a dream, etc. Derishah, paragraph 3, writes that the time for ameliorating the dream is at the end of the day after leaving the synagogue; see there. The Shelah, folio 200b, however, writes that the custom is to ameliorate the dream in the morning. Regarding the argument that the merit of the fast assists him, once he has begun fasting it is considered a fast; see there. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2, and Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, cite this. Ateres Zekeinim, paragraph 1, writes the same. This also appears correct according to the Kabbalists, because the first half of the day is a time of greater mercy than the second half. During the first half, the aspect of forty-five within forty-five governs, while during the second half, forty-five within fifty-two governs. As is known, forty-five represents kindness and fifty-two severity, and prayer is more readily accepted during a time of mercy.
2) There: He should ameliorate it before three, etc. He should recall the dream in his mind during its amelioration. Birchos Maharam Mintz; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 1.
