(Seif 1) Improve for us etc. And if they improve two at one time etc. — in my humble opinion his intent is what we say in Sanhedrin 73a — from where one who sees friend drowning in river or bandits coming on him that he is obligated to save him — Scripture says do not stand on your brother's blood. And challenged — was not already derived for loss of his body? Scripture says and you shall return it to him. If from there — here is when in danger to himself but trouble and endangering lodging we do not learn — behold obligated to save friend in body and property; if so automatically if one dreamed bad dream for another and asks after he is among three improvers who must attend to improve him. And so in Sefer Chasidim — mitzvah to attend with three to improve because restores soul of faster — end quote; however one may say in my humble opinion if he asks one to wait in one place and he will seek two others to bring there and be three in refinement of three and improve his dream — so they did not require waiting. Also perhaps two more will not chance; and if one already ready and asks second also to wait here to join when he seeks and third comes — also need not wait. And if they improve two — meaning two improvers already here — meaning two agreed to improve for him; and third standing with two — third must join two who agreed so there be three and improve for him — for this is restoring his body. Or at one time — meaning two who agreed are at one time standing together before us; and added even though he did not dream that time — certainly improvement on day he dreamed is better as written beautiful is fast for dream — specifically that day; but they disagree whether improve at dawn or Minchah as Magen Avraham seif 2; nevertheless if did not improve that day but next day — it is that improvement does not help so much; nevertheless somewhat certainly helps; and therefore not required if dreamed that time — for saving is likely and certainly must attend; but even though not dreamed that time but yesterday — remedy of improvement not so precise — nevertheless must join two for nevertheless somewhat restoring body exists — so seems in my humble opinion.
