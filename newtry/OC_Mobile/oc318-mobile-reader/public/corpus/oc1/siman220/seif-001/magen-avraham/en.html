Improve for us. And remember dream in thought at time of improvement (Berakhot Rema); and if they improve two at one time also third must improve for him even though not dreamed that time (there); when he goes to sleep from joy they show him good dream (Shabbat 30).

And etc. Seven times etc. Some say thus is incantation and need not say only three times like all incantations; some say must say tava hu and tava lihyei therefore say tava hu and tava lihyei four times and afterward say it with seven times three times and by this fulfills both views (Rashal and so Magen Avraham) and so practice; Bach wrote say all seven times and they say go in joy eat your bread but not eat in joy your bread for acronym aval (Beit Yosef R' Yehuda HaChasid); and this when improve for him in evening after fast; and in Shelah wrote improve at dawn for immediately upon accepting fast accepted before God; one who dreamed difficult dream about friend should fast (Shulchan Aruch).
