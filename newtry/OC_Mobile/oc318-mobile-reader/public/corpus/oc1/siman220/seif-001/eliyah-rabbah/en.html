[1] And his soul is troubled etc. Derishah wrote he inferred to say and his soul is troubled — for although not among dreams one fasts for and also not hinted in dream any bad matter — nevertheless since his soul is troubled over it he should fast because although he does not see his fortune his fortune sees him — therefore his soul is troubled — until here. I did not know how he inferred he should fast — for not mentioned in Tur troubled soul except in improvement siman 288; however below will be explained for law that Bach also rules so. Further wrote time of improvement is end of day after Maariv — until here; and so Shelah daf 200 in name of R' Shimshon — although at night after fast they do improvement; but he wrote one should improve immediately at dawn for zealous precede mitzvah; and do not say merit of fast helps for immediately upon accepting and beginning it is as if he fasted — until here. But truly not such proof — for nevertheless his merit is better since already done; however from what he wrote daf 134 in name of Seder HaYom to improve dream on Shabbat even though asks needs — nevertheless life-threatening overrides etc. — clear they improve immediately at dawn; however possible to explain when not fasting on Shabbat — strained. He should remember dream in thought at time of improvement; and if they improve two at one time also third must improve for him even though he did not dream that time (Berakhot Maharam of Rothenburg).
