Improve for us. At dawn — Shelah. And he should remember the dream in his thought at time of improvement — Maharam. One who dreamed a difficult dream about his friend should fast. Shulchan Aruch.

And it will be good etc. Some say thus is the incantation and need not say it only three times like all incantations; some say must say tava hu and tava lihyei three times therefore they say tava hu and tava lihyei four times; and afterward say it with seven times three times; and by this fulfills both views — Rashal from Magen Avraham and so practice. Bach wrote to say all seven times and they say to him go in joy eat your bread but he should not say eat in joy your bread for it is acronym aval. Beit Yosef.
