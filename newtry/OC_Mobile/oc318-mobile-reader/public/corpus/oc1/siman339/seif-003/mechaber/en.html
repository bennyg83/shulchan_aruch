One does not clap hand to hand nor slap hand to thigh nor dance — a decree lest one fix a musical instrument; and even to tap with a finger on the ground or on a board or one against one as singers do, or to rattle a nut for a child or to play with it with two nuts so he will be quiet — all this and the like is forbidden, a decree lest one fix a musical instrument; and slapping with the back of the hand is permitted:And this that they clap and dance nowadays and we do not protest — because it is better that they be unintentional transgressors etc.; and some say that in our time all is permitted because we are not proficient in making musical instruments and there is no room to decree lest one fix a musical instrument because it is uncommon; and possibly on this basis they were lenient in everything. (Tosafos end of chapter HaMevi Kadei Yayin):.
