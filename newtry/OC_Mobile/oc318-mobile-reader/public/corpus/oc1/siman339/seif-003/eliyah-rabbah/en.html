[1] [Levush] In all clapping, etc. This implies that the leniency is specifically for clapping and dancing, not for shaking bells or other sound-making activities. The Rama's words can also be interpreted this way, and the Taz in Yoreh Deah siman 282 writes so. See the beginning of siman 338 above. Even regarding clapping and dancing outside a mitzvah context, Yam Shel Shlomo writes that the only basis for leniency is to leave those who already practice it.
