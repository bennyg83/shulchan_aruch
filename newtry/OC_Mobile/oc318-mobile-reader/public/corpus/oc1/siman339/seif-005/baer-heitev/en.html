On Shabbat — and in Maharil it is written that there is danger of death in the matter; and Mishna Berurah wrote that even a virgin one should seclude before Shabbat since some say our chuppah is not called chuppah and before the chuppah seclusion is forbidden. Chelkas Mechokek wrote in Even HaEzer: a widow who married Thursday night and was a niddah and secluded with the groom through two guardians — she is permitted to immerse on Shabbat night since she entered his house he acquired her etc. — see there; and Magen Avraham disagrees with him and raised that even if the marriage was on Thursday it is forbidden to have intercourse with her on Shabbat — therefore one should not rely on Chelkas Mechokek on this. Right: and on Yom Tov night she is permitted to immerse — it appears to me; and so ruled Mahari Weil; and she should not immerse also at Motzei Shabbos — see in Yoreh De'ah section 197 seif 2; and if her immersion fell on Motzei Shabbos she is permitted to immerse — and so ruled R' Shalom of Prague; and if she secluded with him not in presence of a person for intercourse after the chuppah — permitted by law.
