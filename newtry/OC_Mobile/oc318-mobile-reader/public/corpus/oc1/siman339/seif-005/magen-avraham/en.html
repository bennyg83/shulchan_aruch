The widow — and in Maharil it is written that there is danger of death in the matter; and Mishna Berurah wrote that even a virgin one should seclude before Shabbat since some say our chuppah is not called chuppah and before the chuppah seclusion is forbidden; Chelkas Mechokek wrote: a widow who married Thursday night and was a niddah and secluded with the groom through two guardians — she is permitted to immerse on Shabbat night since they entered his house he acquired her etc.; however according to Rambam who holds that chuppah of a niddah does not acquire — it is possible the same applies to seclusion and entering the husband's house does not help as long as she is not fit for intercourse; and I will write more on this below siman 60 — end of his words in siman 55 and siman 60 he elaborated to decide not like Rambam and concluded: and if so it appears for practice a widow who entered the chuppah before Shabbat is permitted to immerse on Shabbat for he acquired her for everything etc. — end of his words; and his words are not accepted to me, for even though the first question deals with the matter of writing a ketubah — nevertheless the second question is also regarding inheritance, for since he requires in chuppah that it be fit for intercourse — a fortiori in handing over to the husband's agents; but if she is not fit for intercourse he acquired nothing even in chuppah; and furthermore it appears to me that even per the one who says chuppah of a niddah acquires — nevertheless regarding a widow where chuppah does not help, only seclusion — we require specifically seclusion fit for intercourse, for Rosh holds that chuppah of a niddah acquires and nevertheless wrote regarding a widow one requires specifically seclusion fit for intercourse as is written at the beginning of Kesubos; and Tur and Rabbeinu Yerucham copied his language in Even HaEzer siman 63 seif 5; and for this reason it appears to me one requires seclusion where there is no person; and so too in Ran who brought it in Even HaEzer in Beit Yosef siman 61: some say chuppah is seclusion, for if not seclusion — behold a niddah too is fit for chuppah etc.; and since we rule chuppah is not seclusion — if so it is permitted to bring in a niddah — end of his words; if so it is proven one requires actual seclusion without any guardian, for otherwise behold a niddah is fit to seclude too; or one requires actual seclusion without a guardian; and so too Mishna Berurah siman 90 requires seclusion after the chuppah fit for intercourse; and so too Rama in Yoreh De'ah siman 196; and Taz there; and so too Maharil in Laws of Shabbat that even if the marriage was on Thursday it is forbidden to have intercourse with her on Shabbat — therefore one should not rely on Chelkas Mechokek on this:
