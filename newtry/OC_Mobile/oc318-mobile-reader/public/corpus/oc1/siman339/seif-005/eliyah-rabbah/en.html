[5] [Levush] They seclude etc. — specifically after the chuppah, for before the chuppah it does not help and it is also forbidden for betrothed man and betrothed woman to seclude (Meshech Binyamin). Furthermore there they wrote one requires only seclusion for complete intercourse but he should not yet have intercourse; furthermore there that some communities practice to seclude even a bachelor marrying a widow because some question that our chuppah does not acquire even in a virgin — and so it is correct — until here; and this deals with a place where they have intercourse first on Shabbat in siman 280. Maharil wrote: if immersion of a widow falls on Shabbat night she should not immerse also at Motzei Shabbat and should wait until the second night; and Chelkas Mechokek siman 60 permits to immerse and to have intercourse because even handing over by the father to the husband's agents acquires; and his words do not appear as explained in Meshech Binyamin there; and so Magen Avraham challenged him. And it appears if her immersion falls on Motzei Shabbat she is permitted — and so ruled my grandfather of blessed memory; and to bring her in on erev Shabbat when she is impure is also permitted for he does not have intercourse with her on Shabbat:
