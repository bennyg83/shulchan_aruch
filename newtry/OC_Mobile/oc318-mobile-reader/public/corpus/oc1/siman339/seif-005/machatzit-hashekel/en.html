(א) (s.k. 5) Entering the chuppah — for by law it is fit to forbid for it is like acquiring acquisition on Shabbat; it is stated in Yerushalmi that through the chuppah the husband acquires the woman and acquires the work of her hands and her found objects and her inheritance and for all matters the husband acquires in his wife; and because of the mitzvah of procreation since he has no wife and children Rama holds to be lenient:

(ב) And in Semak etc. — for they did not permit the High Priest — meaning at the beginning of Yoma we learned R' Yishmael: before Yom Kippur they would prepare for the High Priest another wife lest his wife die, as is written "And he shall atone for himself and for his household" — his household is his wife; for the High Priest on Yom Kippur had to have a wife and therefore he would betroth and bring to chuppah that other wife — for even if his wife dies behold he has another wife; and the Gemara challenges: but is it not written "and for his household" — meaning one household and not two households; and now perhaps she will not die and he will have two households; and they answer he divorces conditionally; and see there chapter 1 how many times the Gemara rolled on that condition; and it was impossible — he should not betroth her and not bring her to chuppah before Yom Kippur except if his wife dies on Yom Kippur he will betroth and bring in that other wife prepared for him (and per the one who says chuppah of a virgin is seclusion fit for intercourse as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 11 — this proof does not exist, for on Yom Kippur seclusion fit for intercourse is impossible, for marital relations is one of the five afflictions forbidden on Yom Kippur; and I wrote from this at length in Magen Avraham) — rather forbidden because of shevut; and it is impossible that they permitted this shevut because of the mitzvah of procreation — all the more so there should be permission for the High Priest on Yom Kippur since the atonement of all Israel depends on the service of the High Priest and it is not needed for any condition:

(ג) In the name of Yerushalmi that it implies it is permitted — for in truth he did not betroth her on erev Yom Kippur except if it happened his wife died on Yom Kippur he would betroth and bring to chuppah that wife prepared for him — behold in a place of mitzvah it is permitted; the same that there is room to permit because of the mitzvah of procreation even though it is not necessary; there regarding the High Priest it is more preferable since the atonement of all Israel depends on him — on the contrary there Yerushalmi challenges and it is not like acquiring acquisition on Shabbat; and they answer there is no shevut in the Temple; and they do not answer that in a place of mitzvah it is permitted — it must be one of two reasons he cited; in any case it is not compelled to permit because of procreation; rather because it is possible on erev Yom Kippur — namely through a condition in order to equate the two students together — namely our Gemara with Yerushalmi:
