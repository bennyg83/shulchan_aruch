Note: behold in tractate Shabbos daf 139a it is explicit that a Torah scholar is permitted to use a rabbinic ruse such as to sleep in a boat etc.; and Rambam omitted this law that for a Torah scholar it is permitted; and Rosh and Tur brought it; and see in Rosh what he distinguishes between ruses — there are three distinctions in the ruse — see there. And his bearers of vessels answered that therefore Rambam omitted it because there is no law of Torah scholar in our time — and this is a great strain, for Rambam's way is to write all laws even if they are not practiced and not relevant at all in our time — and why did he omit this law? Therefore it appears Rambam learned this from the Gemara that we do not rule thus; and it is from what is stated in chapter 2: the Gemara challenges "what — one and one who is believed?" etc.; and it says there: come and hear — an incident with R' Nehorai who went to the witness in Usha to testify for him on Shabbat; and they answer R' Nehorai was another with him; and this one was not counted because of honor of R' Nehorai; and it is difficult to me why he needs to answer because of honor of R' Nehorai — he should answer more: according to what is stated in Yerushalmi in that chapter: it says there — his acquaintances — what if they desecrate Shabbat for him? Let us hear from this: R' Nehorai said: here is different — an incident that I went down to testify for one witness in Usha and they needed him only as a pretext — I requested to receive the face of my fellow — see there and in K'nei Pnei. And it is difficult: if he knew they did not need him — end of discussion it is a ruse and how did he desecrate Shabbat to go outside the techum? Perforce since it is outside the techum only rabbinically and permitted to use a ruse — end of discussion if so what does the Gemara challenge that perhaps for this R' Nehorai was cited — it teaches that a Torah scholar is permitted to use a ruse; but the other was not permitted to use a ruse — perforce it is proven that the Gemara's default view is there is no distinction in the ruse between Torah scholar and other — only if the ruse is permitted it is permitted for every person; and where the ruse is forbidden it is forbidden for every person; and there is no distinction between Torah scholar and other. From here Rambam extracted his law that we do not rule like the sugya in Shabbos mentioned — and understand. And with this one can resolve Rav Ashi who answers R' Nehorai was another witness in Usha — if so what is the reason to say etc.; and Tosafos there sensed why does he challenge specifically on this version and they strained there that he challenges actually on two versions; but per the above it comes out well that for the first version one can say R' Nehorai knew they did not need him for there are several witnesses there — only what he went was only to receive his fellow's face; and what he said that he went for testimony of the month was only a pretext by way of ruse so it not appear as desecrating Shabbat — end of discussion if so what is the matter that there were others in Usha — end of discussion if there is no witness with him too it appears as desecrating Shabbat and there is concern for marit ayin — therefore he must answer there was another witness with him so there is no concern for marit ayin; for it is not difficult "what is the reason to say" — it teaches that by way of ruse it is permitted to desecrate Shabbat rabbinically; but this is if the ruse is that he says for a mitzvah matter — then permitted; but if the ruse is for a optional matter — forbidden as with a boat — therefore he does not challenge "what is the reason to say."
