[7] Tied, etc. There must be a knot with one end attached to the boat and the other to land, as stated by Binyamin Ze'ev. A person had a large debt owed by a non-Jew and learned that the debtor was ill. He may not hire a small boat to travel to him because the financial loss is not certain; perhaps the debtor will not die, or perhaps he will instruct that the debt be paid, as stated by Rabbi Yosef Levi, siman 32. The Magen Avraham writes that even if the loss were certain, it would be prohibited, because even for a mitzvah it is prohibited, as in siman 513, seif 6.
