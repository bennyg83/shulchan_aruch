Permitted — see explained well what is written in responsum Maharam Alashkar and Avodat HaGershuni; and behold regarding crossing on a flying bridge for a mitzvah matter such as to the synagogue and the like — Shev Yaakov permitted in responsum siman 107 since we rely on Behag to enter a boat on Shabbat through acquisition of residence — not like R' Yosef in chapter Mi sheHotzi'u; and according to Behag it is possible it is permitted without any acquisition of residence, for perhaps the reason is because he went outside the techum and Shabbat did not come in the air of the partitions from while it was still day — and this is not relevant here; see siman 228 in the gloss; and furthermore he sided to permit that on a bridge like that always most gentiles who go there go without a Jew too from now; and certainly it is permitted to travel far; and even though Beit Yosef wrote a person of spirit should be concerned for himself — this is when it goes outside the techum — see there; and especially in a place where the Jew does nothing — permitted; and furthermore he brought proof that even according to R' Yosef who forbids he permits in a place of mitzvah — see there; except he concluded in his words: since no explicit permission is found only from what we rely on Behag mentioned — give not to add to it; and in any case with acquisition of residence it is simple there is room to permit; and the gaon Mahar R' Gavriel of blessed memory debated this matter until he wrote: I am not among those who permit etc. until: but regarding a matter practiced as forbidden and they have upon what to rely — impossible to permit before them; however Shev Yaakov answered everything Mahar R' Gavriel challenged on him; and with Hashem's help I wrote all this as give and take to escape his challenge; but heaven forbid to raise my head and feet regarding a matter that even small rabbis of my rank do not agree with me, all the more so against his exalted greatness — and what is it to me to insert etc.:
