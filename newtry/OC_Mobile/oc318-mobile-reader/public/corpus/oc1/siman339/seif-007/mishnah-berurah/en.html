(א) And it does not float at all — but if it floats forbidden to enter the boat; and so too on a ferry called "ferry" because it appears as if he swims himself:

(ב) Permitted to enter it — and forbidden for any person to use a ruse to enter to sleep in it knowing the gentile will convey it on the other side on Shabbat, even within the techum; and see siman 248 seif 3 in the gloss that if he entered from erev Shabbat and acquired residence there it is permitted for his status is like his house and he does not appear afterward as swimming; and even if they convey it outside the techum it is permitted; and nevertheless forbidden to go out from boat to boat, for only in the first boat he traverses all of it since Shabbat came in the air of its partitions from while it was still day; and above in siman 248 and below in simanim 404 and 405 all these laws are explained. One person had a large debt with a gentile and it became known he is ill — forbidden for him to rent a small boat to go to him because the harm is not certain lest he not die or lest he command to pay [R' Yosef Halevi]; and this is even within the techum; and Magen Avraham wrote that even if the harm is certain it is forbidden, for even for a mitzvah matter it is forbidden — see siman 513 seif 6; and in Chayei Adam general rule 44 letter 20 he wrote that if one needs to cross on a ferry for a mitzvah he crosses and it is impossible otherwise; and so one who has a debt with a gentile and the harm is certain if he not cross on Shabbat in a boat — it is possible to rely and permit; however when it is outside the techum it appears one should not be lenient at all; and see in Shaarei Teshuvah in this siman and in siman 248:
