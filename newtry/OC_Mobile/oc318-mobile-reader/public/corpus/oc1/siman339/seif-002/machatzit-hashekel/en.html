(א) (s.k. 2) One against one etc. before the High Priest etc. on Yom Kippur night if he came to doze the young priests would tap before him with a hard finger so he not sleep; and even though one could say the reason is there is no shevut in the Temple — Baal Tosafot HaRash rejected this, for if so the Gemara should have troubled and weighed this — behold there is a rabbinic prohibition; and to change — there is no shevut in the Temple except that Rabbeinu Yonah wrote there is no such precision to divide according to Rambam who forbade it as is written in Shulchan Aruch here — therefore Magen Avraham wrote a resolution for this:

(ב) That case is different etc. and it was not a sound of song etc. and therefore he wrote "as singers do." Magen Avraham wrote thus to resolve the view of Rabbeinu Yonah who holds that it is forbidden only a sound of song; and in truth also according to those who are stringent and forbid even a sound that is not of song — one can reject Baal Tosafot HaRash's proof from this case of the High Priest as Acharonim wrote on this; also one can say the stringent ones' reason is because they disagree on this whether a sound that is not of song is permitted or forbidden — Rava and Ulla end of Eruvin: Rava permits and Ulla forbids; and the lenient ones ruled like Rava; and the stringent ones ruled like Ulla — end of discussion one can say the stringent ones for law adopted the essence like Ulla; nevertheless it is not difficult on the Gemara that it should have stated the reason in this case of the High Priest that they tap before him with a finger because there is no shevut in the Temple — for according to Rava this was not needed, for according to Rava even in the province it is permitted; the Gemara did not trouble itself to state the reason because of there is no shevut in the Temple — since it depends on the dispute of Ulla and Rava — end of discussion since we rule like Ulla the stringent one:
