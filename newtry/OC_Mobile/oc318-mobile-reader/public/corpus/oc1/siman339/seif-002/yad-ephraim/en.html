In Magen Avraham s.k. 11 one should seclude after the chuppah before Shabbat etc. — end of his words in siman 55 as it should be. And what he wrote "and before the chuppah seclusion is forbidden" — meaning because it is seclusion of an unmarried woman; and even though she is betrothed to him — even a betrothed woman is forbidden to seclude with the betrothed before the blessing of groom and bride as is written in Even HaEzer siman 55 — therefore one must seclude from while it was still day in a modest place after the chuppah on erev Shabbat in Meshech Binyamin siman 95:
