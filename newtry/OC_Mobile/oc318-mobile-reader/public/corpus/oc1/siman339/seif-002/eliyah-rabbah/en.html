[2] One does not judge etc. — one does not arrange sessions of claims on Shabbat etc. one should not cancel the tamid unless to accept regarding a claim of virginity or a claim of the community (Acharonim). Yerushalmi: "in all your dwellings" — from here for courts not to judge on Shabbat (Sefer HaChinuch parashat Vayakhel); and to Magen Avraham this was omitted and the reason in Sefer HaChinuch is that Heaven wanted to honor this day that everyone find rest on it, also sinners and those liable — parable to a great king who called the people of the country one day for a feast etc.:
