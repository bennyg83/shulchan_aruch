Seif 6 forbidden etc. and therefore etc. Yerushalmi chapter 5 of Yevamos: and one does not swim — R' Yaakov in the name of R' Zavdi: this teaches that it is forbidden to float something to convey it from himself; and likewise it is forbidden for a person to float something and bring it to himself — R' Abba Mari and R' Matanya were sitting and saw a certain man spreading water here and there and drinking; R' Abba Mari said to R' Matanya: this is what R' Yochanan bar Zavdi said — it is forbidden for a person to float something and convey it from himself:
