(א) One does not judge — one does not arrange sessions of claims on Shabbat. And R' Gamliel enacted not to cancel the tamid on Shabbat; but to accept such as regarding claims of virginity — permitted (Semag); and see in Tosafos Kesubos daf 3. And for claims of the community it is permitted to cancel the tamid. Rambam wrote in chapter 23 that one does not punish on Shabbat even though the punishment is a positive commandment — it does not override Shabbat; how so? If one was liable in court to lashes or death — they do not lash him and do not put him to death, as it says "You shall not kindle fire" etc. — see there. And even though from the Gemara it appears specifically murder does not override Shabbat — nevertheless since lashes stand in place of death, death is death and lashes too are included — Yad Aharon; and see Magen Avraham and see in responsum Emunat Shmuel siman 54 who stood on this at length — see there; and see what was questioned on this in Mishneh LaMelech. And monetary laws — one does not judge on Shabbat, a decree lest one write. It is stated in Sanhedrin daf 88b that on Shabbat the Sanhedrin did not sit in the Chamber of Hewn Stone but on a weekday so it not appear as if they judge on Shabbat.
