Judgment — see explained well; and see responsum Beit Yehudah siman 29; and see responsum Maaseh Rav siman 17: Yerushalmi "in all your dwellings" — from here for litigants not to judge on Shabbat. Sefer HaChinuch parashat Vayakhel. To Magen Avraham this was omitted and the reason in Sefer HaChinuch is that Heaven wanted to honor this day that everyone find rest on it, also sinners and those liable — parable to a great king who called the people of the country one day for a feast etc.:
