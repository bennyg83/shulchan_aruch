(א) One does not judge — one does not arrange sessions of claims on Shabbat; and R' Gamliel enacted not to cancel the tamid on Shabbat; but to accept such as regarding a claim of virginity — permitted (Semag chapter 72); and see in Tosafos Kesubos daf 3; and for claims of the community it is permitted to cancel the tamid (there): Rambam wrote in chapter 24 one does not punish on Shabbat — even though the punishment is a positive commandment it does not override Shabbat; how so? If one was liable to death or lashes — they do not lash him, as it says "You shall not kindle fire" etc.; and the same for other punishments; and Maggid Mishneh wrote that Rambam counted it in the count of mitzvot — end of his words; and in truth in the count of mitzvot siman 322 it implies specifically regarding a matter that has desecration of Shabbat it is forbidden d'oraisa, as is clear in the Gemara Shabbos daf 106 regarding burning — see there; and it is possible that in lashes too there is desecration of Shabbat such as if he makes a bruise — requires study; and it is possible that from the superfluous word of the verse "in all your dwellings" we derive that one does not judge at all — requires study; in Sanhedrin daf 35 it implies specifically regarding a matter that has desecration of Shabbat; and so too in Tosafos there; and monetary laws — one does not judge, a decree lest one write. It is stated in Sanhedrin daf 88b that on Shabbat the Sanhedrin did not sit in the Chamber of Hewn Stone but on a weekday so it not appear as if they judge on Shabbat; and it appears to me that for this reason it is forbidden to set a place for chalitzah on Shabbat; and see in Even HaEzer in explanation of the order of chalitzah siman 17 — he strained in the matter:

(ו) And one does not redeem — even with a vessel that resembles buying and selling; and even if he says he will give the money to the kohen on erev Shabbat — nevertheless how will he do with the blessings? And the feast he will make on erev Shabbat — the mitzvah has not yet fallen upon him and how will he say "and commanded us"? And on Shabbat too not, for now he does nothing — therefore he waits until day 1 (Terumat HaDeshen); and in Yoreh De'ah siman 305 Bach wrote that if thirty days were completed for him on day 6 they redeem him on day 6 for it is a month; and so too Shach s.k. 19 there; and in my humble opinion one should not rely on this, for in Bechoros daf 49 they say if he died on day 30 it is like the day before it etc. — why? Because the rabbis learn month month from the wilderness: just as there and more, so here and more etc.; it is difficult what is the question "why do the rabbis learn" — for the month has not yet been completed for him? Rather it appears here since thirty days and 793 parts of an hour were completed for him it is a month; and they answer that it requires "and more"; therefore it must reach day 31; and so too where it says if he died on day thirty it is like the day before — and it did not distinguish between twenty-nine days twelve hours and 793 parts; and furthermore Tosafos wrote in Sanhedrin daf 11 end of 1a: in Torah language a month is thirty days; and for the other opinion even in the language of the sages it is thirty; and so too in Yevamos daf 48; and so too in Rosh Hashana daf 20b and in Megillah:
