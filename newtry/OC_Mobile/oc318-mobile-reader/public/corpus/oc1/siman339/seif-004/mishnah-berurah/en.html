(א) One does not judge — meaning even monetary laws lest one come to writing; and the same regarding arranging claims alone before the judges — also forbidden lest the judges write their claims; but to accept and propose matters such as regarding claims of virginity — permitted as is written in Even HaEzer siman 62; and R' Gamliel enacted not to cancel the tamid [meaning the fixed prayer of the congregation in the synagogue] on Shabbat unless they already canceled three times on weekdays and it did not help, or for claims of the community:
(ב) Because it is etc. — and also he can come to a gathering on Shabbat:
(ג) Included in judgment — and it is stated in Yerushalmi on what is written "You shall not kindle fire in all your dwellings" etc. — from here for courts not to judge on Shabbat [meaning one who was liable in ancient times to burning — the Torah excluded that they not punish him on Shabbat and from this we derive the same for every punishment; and the Mechaber deals initially with monetary matters that in this too it is forbidden rabbinically as explained above in s.k. 11] and it is stated in Sefer HaChinuch the reason that Heaven wanted to honor this day that everyone find rest on it, also sinners and those liable — parable to a great king who called the people of the country one day for a feast etc.:
(ד) And if he flees etc. — and not included in this if one wants to flee in order to betroth his wife — in this it was permitted to imprison him; and so permitted to accept testimony from a man in danger and near death regarding a woman whose husband died where we are concerned lest they not find other testimony afterward [Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger and other Acharonim]:
(ה) And one does not betroth — also because of a decree lest one write; but not because it is like acquiring acquisition on Shabbat, for in kiddushin alone without chuppah he has not yet acquired her for inheritance and for her found objects and for the work of her hands; and so too if even the kiddushin were from while it was still day — it is explained in the Mechaber below that it is forbidden to bring her to the chuppah on Shabbat — and that is because through the chuppah he acquires her for all these matters mentioned and it is like buying and selling which is forbidden on Shabbat:
(ו) Where he has no wife and children — and the same if he has children and has no wife — also permitted according to this view, for he is performing a mitzvah as we say in Yevamos [62]: "Sow your seed in the morning and in the evening do not rest your hand" etc.:
(ז) And it is possible the same etc. — meaning even though through the chuppah he acquires her for several matters as explained — nevertheless since he has no wife and children and there is a mitzvah of procreation it is reasonable to him to be lenient in this:
(ח) That we do not rule thus — meaning that in essence we rule like the view of Rashi and the other Rishonim that kiddushin and even chuppah are forbidden on Shabbat and Yom Tov:
(ט) Since etc. — meaning and there is great loss through this; and see in Chayei Adam who wrote one should not be lenient in all this unless the three particulars were fulfilled — meaning if he has not yet fulfilled procreation and there is great loss and also embarrassment for groom and bride if he not enter then; and see what we wrote in s.k. 16 that according to Rabbeinu Tam even if he has children since he has no wife he is performing a mitzvah and it is permitted; and it is possible he holds one does not rely on this. And at twilight certainly one should not be stringent b'dieved for they did not decree shevut at twilight for the sake of a mitzvah for all; however l'chatchila one should be very careful not to delay so much because several transgressions come thereby — muktzeh of the candles and also many women going to the chuppah after candle-lighting time thereby and light in the synagogue — and better had they not lit then at all; and therefore what is correct is very much for one who has the ability not to make the wedding at all on erev Shabbat and especially near dark, for even if the wedding is at its time nevertheless several corruptions branch from this as explained. If the chuppah was delayed until dark and the groomsmen have candles — although we rule muktzeh as long as it is in his hand he may take it where he wants — nevertheless here he should give the candles to a gentile, for whatever is possible to remove muktzeh from his hand he is obligated to remove [Peri Megadim]:
(י) And one does not perform chalitzah — lest he write a chalitzah document for the woman who performs chalitzah [Rashi]; and even to set a place for chalitzah is forbidden so it not appear as if they judge on Shabbat; and he can set on Motzei Shabbat night that the chalitzah be on Sunday; and if there is great need he can set even at twilight on erev Shabbat for Sunday if it is impossible otherwise [Peri Megadim in Orach Chayim s.k. 3]:
(כ) And one does not perform yibum — for in what he performs yibum she is acquired to him to be his wife for everything like a married woman and it is like acquiring acquisition on Shabbat; and furthermore lest he write a ketubah:
(ל) And one does not bring in — meaning even if he betrothed her from while it was still day it is forbidden to bring her on Shabbat to the chuppah, for through the chuppah he acquires her for several matters as explained:
(מ) And one does not dedicate — any thing to Heaven that he say "behold this is hekdesh" — because since he dedicates with his statement the object to hekdesh, behold he removes the object with this statement from his domain to Heaven's domain and it resembles buying and selling; unlike when he vows charity to the poor — permitted; and so too when he says "behold upon me to dedicate such and such" — also permitted; and see above in siman 306 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 27:
(נ) And one does not vow valuations — that he say "my valuation upon me" or "the valuation of so-and-so upon me" that he give to hekdesh according to his age — for it is like buying and selling:
(ס) And one does not declare cherem — an animal or any thing to Heaven that he say "behold this thing is cherem" — all from the reason mentioned; and what is written "and one does not separate" etc. — that it resembles dedicating those fruits he separated; and furthermore that it is like fixing something not fixed; and if he transgressed and separated intentionally he should not eat whether for himself or for others until Motzei Shabbat because of a penalty; and if it was unintentional — permitted even for himself immediately:
(ע) Terumot and maaserot — and the same for challah; and see above in siman 261 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 4:
(פ) And one does not redeem — even with a vessel worth five sela that resembles buying and selling; and one should not say he will give the money to the kohen on erev Shabbat and when day 31 arrives on Shabbat the redemption will take effect — for then he cannot bless, since the mitzvah has not yet fallen upon him how will he say "and commanded us"? And on Shabbat too he cannot bless since then he does nothing — therefore he waits until after Shabbat; and even if thirty days and twelve hours and 793 parts of an hour were completed for him on erev Shabbat — nevertheless they do not redeem him on day 16 because the month in Torah is thirty complete days; therefore it must reach day 31 at the time of redemption; and since day 31 is on Shabbat it is postponed until after Shabbat:
(צ) And one does not divorce — even with a get written before Shabbat lest he come to writing:
(ק) Because the world weighs heavily upon him — meaning he wants to divorce his wife so she not be bound for yibum — they permitted him to divorce on Shabbat so his mind not be disturbed if they not do his will; and he deals with a case where there is no disqualification because of being premature such as when the get was written from erev Shabbat the date of the day was not written in it but it is written "this week" unspecified — which is valid in such a case as is written in Even HaEzer siman 127:
(ר) And all of them etc. — all that is explained in this seif applies except for separating terumot and maaserot which we explained above that if he separated them intentionally it is forbidden for him that day:
(ש) Unintentionally etc. — unintentional is such as he forgot it was Shabbat or he thought this thing was permitted to do:
