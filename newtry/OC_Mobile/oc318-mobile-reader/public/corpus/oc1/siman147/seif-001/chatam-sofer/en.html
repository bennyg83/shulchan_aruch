In Magen Avraham seif 5 — from the polished mirrors. Note: see Chavat Yair siman 161 and 162 and what he wrote there that for this reason Moshe accepted the kumaz in the donation of the Mishkan because it was changed by melting, unlike the polished mirrors. And it requires investigation, for they too were melted. But Ramban in parashat Vayakhel wrote that they were nullified in the majority, unlike the polished mirrors, for nothing was mixed with them in making the basin — therefore Moshe did not wish to accept copper per the utterance [see Chatham Sofer OC siman 40 and 42]:
