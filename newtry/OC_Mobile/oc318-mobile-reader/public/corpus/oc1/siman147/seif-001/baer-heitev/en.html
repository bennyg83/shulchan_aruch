Bare. Bach and Magen Avraham wrote that even the rollers may not be held except through a cloth or some other intervening object; see there. Peri Chadash and Yad Aharon disagree and write that although one may not hold the rollers when the Sefer Torah is bare, when it is wrapped in its cloth it is plainly permitted to hold the rollers even without an intervening cloth. Taz likewise writes that we have not seen even meticulous people take care not to touch the rollers of a Sefer Torah without an intervening object; see there. See also Mahari Mitrani, part 1, siman 136, and Responsa Panim Meiros, siman 76.
