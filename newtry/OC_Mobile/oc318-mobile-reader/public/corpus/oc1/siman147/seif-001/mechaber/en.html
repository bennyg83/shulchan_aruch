The laws of rolling a Sefer Torah. It contains 8 seifim: It is forbidden to hold a bare Sefer Torah without a cloth. {Rama: Some say that the same law applies to other sacred writings. [Agudah and Tosafos, chapter 1 of Shabbos] This is not the custom, but it is proper to be stringent if one has not washed his hands. Regarding a Sefer Torah, even in such a case it is forbidden. [Rama's own ruling; Agudah]} The greatest among those who read from the Torah performs the rolling, and the custom is to purchase this honor for a high price in order to endear the mitzvah. {Rama: Some say that if the mantle is linen on 1 side and silk on the other, the silk side should be turned toward the Sefer Torah when it is rolled. [Mordechai, end of Megillah] This is not the custom. [Beit Yosef, siman 123] One should not roll the Sefer Torah with a torn cloth if another is available. [Maharil] One should not make coverings for a Sefer Torah from old items that were previously used for an ordinary purpose. See above, siman 153. [Agudah, Menachos; Responsa Maharil, siman 114; Beit Yosef; Rashbatz]}
