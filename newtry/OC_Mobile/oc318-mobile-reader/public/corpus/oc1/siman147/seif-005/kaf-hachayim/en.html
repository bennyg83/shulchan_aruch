(5) There — Rama gloss: and some say the same applies to other holy writings. So too Tosafot Shabbat 14a — it somewhat implies not specifically a Torah scroll, for all holy writings we learned in tractate Yadayim (chapter 3) that they defile the hands — end of his words; and Magen Avraham wrote in note 1 — and if so, also tefillin straps and all the more so tefillin themselves — one may not hold them while naked, for they defile the hands. However in Chagigah 24b Tosafot wrote that tefillin straps with tefillin — Rav Parnok etc. does not apply — so too the Rosh in tractate Yadayim; and if so it implies one may hold them naked. However holy writings specifically when written in Assyrian script on hide with ink as in the mishnah (there chapter 4) and so too Rambam (chapter 9 of Laws of Fathers of Impurity). However regarding a megillah written properly one must be careful — for from the Rambam's plain meaning one may say we do not laugh at Shmuel (Megillah 7a) who said Esther does not defile hands — end of his words; and so the Mo'ach in siman 491 that Esther megillah requires a cloth as in Chelkas 100a. And they brought from Machberes there note 3, and so in Sefer HaEshkol Laws of Torah Study siman 12 that one may not hold the megillah naked — see there. However in Radbaz responsa part 2 siman 11 he wrote this is for one who explains "naked" refers to the person; but for one who explains it refers to the Torah scroll — other holy writings are permitted to be held when they are "naked." And they brought this in Keneset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef and wrote "and so is the practice of the people"; and so in responsa Shev Yaakov Orach Chayim siman 11 he elaborated on this matter and concludes that other holy writings are permitted to be held when they are naked, per the words of Radbaz — see there; and they brought this in Elef L'Mateh in Mishna Berurah in Hagahot Beit Yosef note 1, Birkei Yosef note 1, and so Aruch HaShulchan note 1 — what the Rama wrote that they did not practice to be strict regarding holy writings — one may say it refers to the megillah, for in other books of ours it is always permitted. And so in responsa Panim Me'irot part 1 siman 76 that people are not careful to hold a megillah naked and they have upon what to rely. And they brought from Machberes note 4 — see there.
