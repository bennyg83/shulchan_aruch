(5) The writing facing the one who lifts, etc. — and when I roll I practice bending the Torah columns toward myself so they are level as when laid until fully rolled; then the writing faces me and faces the one who lifts; I found support in Sheyarei Kenesset HaGedolah; and it is simple that if they honor one person with lifting and rolling, he takes lifting; if they honored rolling and handed the mantle, he takes rolling. See Yoma 70: a mitzvah to see performance of mitzvot due to multitude of people is glory of the king; nevertheless one may leave it and go see another mitzvah since he is not occupied with it.
