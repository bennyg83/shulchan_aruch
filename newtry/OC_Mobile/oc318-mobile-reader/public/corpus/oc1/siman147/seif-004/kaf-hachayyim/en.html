4) There: Bare, without a covering. And I have heard that there are those who explain that this 'bare' does not refer to the Sefer Torah but to the person holding the Sefer Torah, that if he is bare of a fringed garment he is buried bare. Mahar"t Mildola, of blessed memory, in the sefer Mayim Rabbim, Yoreh De'ah, siman 53; Yad Aharon [in the] Mishnah Berurah, in the Hagahos HaTur, ot 1. And further see the Maamar Mordechai, ot 2, who wrote that there are those who explained 'bare' as referring to the person, that a person who is bare should not hold the Sefer Torah, and they rejected their words, and he wrote that all the poskim wrote like the words of Maran, of blessed memory, that 'bare' refers to the Sefer Torah without a covering; see there. And the practical difference is that it is proper to be stringent also according to the 'some explain,' even though according to the law we rule like the words of Maran, of blessed memory, and like the agreement of all the poskim, that 'bare' refers to the Sefer Torah. And so wrote the Yafeh LaLev, ot 1, that this matter provides support for what the Rav Ma'aseh Avraham wrote, Orach Chaim, siman 36, that one should not ascend and read from the Sefer Torah, nor perform any mitzvah connected to the Sefer Torah, without being adorned in his garment, with a tallis on his head, etc.; however, he wrote there that one should not hold back the mitzvah on account of this; see there.
