Magen Avraham, seif katan 7 “He means this specifically.” Rama gives a reason for fastening the end of the cloth on the inside, namely, so that one will not need to turn the Sefer Torah over. Regarding beginning to roll, meaning to wrap the cloth from the outside, he gives no reason. One might therefore have thought that he means only that it is permitted to begin rolling from the outside if one wishes, whereas fastening must specifically be on the inside. Magen Avraham therefore writes that this too is meant specifically, because of the concern that the Sefer Torah might, Heaven forbid, fall.
