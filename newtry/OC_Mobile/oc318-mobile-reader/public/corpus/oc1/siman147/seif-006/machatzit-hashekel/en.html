(s.k. 6) For there is no, etc. — he was entitled, etc., from the reason that one who begins a mitzvah is told to finish; and if so, if he could not read in the Torah except the prayer leader, then prayer and reading in the Torah would be one matter, and we would say: since he began to pray, he should finish the mitzvah with taking out and bringing in the Torah scroll. But since sometimes one prays and one reads, and we see that prayer and reading are not related to each other; and since when he finished prayer his mitzvah already passed and he has no connection to bringing in and taking out the Torah scroll — and likewise the reader, who has not yet begun the mitzvah, also has no connection to taking out and bringing in the Torah scroll; if so, even if the one praying reads in the Torah, nevertheless he has no acquisition in taking out and bringing in the Torah scroll, for he has no connection to it.
