[6] He should not roll, etc. Because this is degrading to the Torah. For the same reason, it appears proper to be stringent not to hold the straps of tefillin and allow the boxes to roll around the straps. Rather, one should hold the tefillin in his hand and wind the strap around them. Although the straps possess sanctity, the boxes possess greater sanctity, as explained above in siman 32.
