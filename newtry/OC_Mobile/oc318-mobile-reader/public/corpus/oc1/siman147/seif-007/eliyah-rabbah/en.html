[7] The maftir begins, etc. Levush explains that this means the blessings over the Haftarah. Although the Gemara and Shulchan Aruch mention only the Haftarah itself and not its blessings, he holds that this is the more logical reading, since the underlying reason governs the law. So too rules Be’er Sheva, page 58. Magen Avraham writes that on Monday and Thursday, when Yehi Ratzon is said after the reading, one need not wait for the person rolling the Sefer Torah, since its recital is only a custom.
