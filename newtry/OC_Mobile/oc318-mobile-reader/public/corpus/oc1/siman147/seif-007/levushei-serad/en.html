Magen Avraham, seif katan 11 “And that case of Yoma may be explained.” Meaning, in Yoma the blood of the goat was first passed upward because he carried the blood in his right hand, since that hand was already full. If he had first taken the blood of the bull, he would have needed to take it in his left hand, and it is burdensome to take something in the left hand when both hands are full. In Pesachim, however, he receives the full vessel first in his right hand and the empty vessel in his left. Our custom is therefore understood.
