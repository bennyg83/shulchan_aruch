(s.k. 3) And they are accustomed, etc. — this requires study, etc.; and one must say: nevertheless, one who contributes more money, etc. — if so, the gabbaim act improperly, for sometimes there is not found in the synagogue one who bought [the privilege of] rolling, and the gabbaim honor [someone] to appear upright in their eyes; for it is fitting for gabbaim to honor the great one.
