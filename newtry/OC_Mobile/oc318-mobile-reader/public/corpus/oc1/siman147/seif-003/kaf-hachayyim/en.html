3) There: Without a covering. The explanation is that washing the hands does not suffice. Olas Tamid, ot 2; and it is from what the Beis Yosef wrote in the name of the Agudah, whose words we brought in the previous ot, that even close to the washing it is said [to be forbidden] to hold a bare Sefer Torah; and so is the view of the Rama, of blessed memory, in the gloss, who wrote 'and with a Sefer Torah even in such a case it is forbidden' -- meaning that washing the hands is of no avail. And so wrote the Levush. And the view of Maran, of blessed memory, is that even to hold the pillars, washing the hands does not suffice, as he wrote in the Beis Yosef, and we brought his words in the previous ot, where he wrote on the words of the Mordechai, 'and it is possible that upon this the Ashkenazim rely, who hold a bare Sefer Torah at the time that they raise [it],' etc.; and the raising of the Ashkenazim is by the pillars, because they do not have the custom to make a wooden case for the Sefer Torah as the Sefardim do; and since he said 'upon this the Ashkenazim rely,' it is implied that he himself does not hold so. And so is the view of the Levush, that also to hold the pillars, washing the hands does not suffice, only if the Sefer Torah is wrapped in a covering.
