The greatest — Ateret Zekenim: Shulchan Aruch ruled per the second explanation in Tur: ten [people] associated with reading; and nowadays there is no translator, as in siman 145; on Shabbat there are only nine; and I do not know why he wrote plainly "those who read" — behold, the prayer leader is related to this, for he is the segan. And see Perishah, who questioned: behold, the greatest among those who read is the greatest in the synagogue, as in siman 136; or [perhaps] the greatest was not in the synagogue, or he did not arrange the parasha.
