(Magen Avraham note 11) And we say, etc. — meaning: it appears from this that even though the coal pan was handed — fire to burn incense upon it — the incense itself is preferable to him even though the work of the coal pan precedes the burning; we do not say in such a case one does not pass over mitzvot, since he wants to do the essence of the mitzvah; and similarly even though handing the mantles precedes rolling, he is permitted to honor this one later and leave for himself the later mitzvah — and this is Magen Avraham's intent in what he wrote "one to whom they give to lift and to roll," etc.; but when they honor him, etc. — at first glance this contradicts, but it is not understood that it is not a matter and its opposite; and per what he wrote, his intent appears: regarding lifting and rolling it is fine for him to take the prior mitzvah — that is lifting — not because of precedence but because it is the essence of the mitzvah; but one whom they honor to hand — rolling is preferable even though it is later, since it is the essence of the mitzvah. However Magen Avraham wrote it is not necessary, and therefore he had to bring proof from the coal pan.
