Anyone who wants, may purchase [the privilege] of handing the covers to the one who rolls [the Torah scroll], and the one rolling may not protest because even though [the latter] purchased the [privilege of] rolling, [the latter] did not purchase [the privilege] of the taking of the covers. (And similarly, in those places that practice to purchase [the privilege] of removing and returning [the Torah from/to the ark], the prayer leader may not protest, because that [privilege] does not belong to chazzanim). (Mordechai - end of Tractate Megillah; and Ohr Zaruah)
