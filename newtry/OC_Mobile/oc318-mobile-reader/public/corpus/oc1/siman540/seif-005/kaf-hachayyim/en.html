5) There. He does not plaster it with clay. This implies that plastering with clay is skilled workmanship. This applies here specifically because he performs it together with the work of building the fence, unlike when he performs the plastering alone. Therefore, one may make a ladder and plaster his roof on Chol HaMoed if needed for the festival. If it is not needed for the festival, even unskilled work was not permitted. Taz, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur; Halachah Berurah, subsection 1; Mikraei Kodesh, siman 48, subsection 3. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1, writes that Taz's permission to plaster a roof does not refer to cracks in the roof, whose law is explained in se'if 3. His permission to make a ladder means through unskilled workmanship. See there. If one beautifies it in the craftsmen's manner, it is certainly skilled workmanship and is forbidden even when needed for the festival. Mishnah Berurah, subsection 3.
