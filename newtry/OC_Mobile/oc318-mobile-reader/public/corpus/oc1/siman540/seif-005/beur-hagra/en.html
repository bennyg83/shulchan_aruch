Work of an amateur. For anything that is for the need of the festival is permitted only as work of an amateur, as is written [in Moed Katan] 10b, and there 11a, and there 5a; and see above, siman 544, se'if 2. And the rule is that anything for food needs on the festival is permitted even as craftsman's work; and therefore making an oven and the like is permitted, as is written below; and harvesting wheat and grinding and the like, as is written in siman 533; and likewise something that would be lost, as is written in siman 538 and siman 537, se'if 15, and other places; and likewise because a worker has nothing to eat, as is written at the end of siman 542; and likewise for the need of the public on the festival, as is written in siman 544, se'if 2; but for the need of an individual, only work of an amateur is permitted; and [work] not for a need, even work of an amateur is forbidden, as is written there; see there.
