See Taz regarding plastering his roof (this is not about cracks in the roof) and a ladder: it is work of an amateur and is permitted on Chol HaMoed for the need of the festival; but [work] not for the need of the festival is forbidden even as work of an amateur. In Choshen Mishpat, siman 314, regarding this; and see Eliyah Rabbah here. And nowadays we do not have a roof plastered with clay; and if his roof cracked, he may repair it on Chol HaMoed.
