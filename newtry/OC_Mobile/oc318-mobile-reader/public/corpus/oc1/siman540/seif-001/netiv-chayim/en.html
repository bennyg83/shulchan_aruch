(Taz sk 3) And requires study, for behold the Rambam, etc. NB: It is a fortiori that this "and they plaster" refers to the dispute of R' Eliezer and the Sages on daf 10: "and the Sages say plaster" — meaning even in rainy season one finishes the plaster around the oven; and this is that the oven was already made from erev Yom Tov, but the plaster one finishes on Yom Tov even in rainy season, since the oven was already dried from erev Yom Tov — it does not matter to him that the plaster was not dried on Yom Tov; nevertheless it is fit to bake in it; and this is what the Rambam wrote "in any case they build"; and so the Rav HaMagid in explanation of the Rambam's view — see there.
