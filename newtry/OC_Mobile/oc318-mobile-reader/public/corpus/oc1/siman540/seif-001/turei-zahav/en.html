And it is not plastered with mortar — meaning plastering with mortar is professional work. Question: in the chapter "One who hires craftsmen" daf 101 we say in the mishnah: one who rents a house to his fellow — the landlord is obligated for the door by a carpenter and lock and everything that is professional work, but something that is not professional work the renter does; and we say in the baraita: the renter is obligated to make a ladder and railing and to plaster his roof — learn from this plastering is unskilled work. And one could say that here the plastering was professional work because he does it together with fence work, unlike there where he does only plastering alone, as it appears to me; and one can learn from there that it is permitted to make a ladder and plaster his roof on chol ha-moed, meaning for festival need, for if not for festival need it would not be permitted even unskilled work as Beit Yosef wrote in the siman after this. And I wonder why the poskim did not bring this here.
