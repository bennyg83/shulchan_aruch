And he does not plaster with clay. This implies that plastering with clay is craftsman's work. This is difficult, for in the chapter "One Who Hires Craftsmen," folio 101, we say in the Mishnah: One who rents a house to his fellow, the landlord is obligated regarding a door, a bolt, and a lock, and anything that is craftsman's work; but something that is not craftsman's work, the tenant does it. And we say in a Baraisa: the tenant is obligated to make for him a ladder and a railing and to plaster his roof; from this we learn that plastering is work of an amateur. And one may answer that here the plastering is craftsman's work because he does it together with the work of the fence, which is not so there, where he does only the plastering alone, as it appears to my humble opinion. And one may learn from there that it is permitted to make a ladder and to plaster his roof on Chol HaMoed, and that is for the need of the festival; for if it is not for the need of the festival, even work of an amateur was not permitted, as Beis Yosef wrote in the following siman. And I am astonished that the poskim did not bring this here.
