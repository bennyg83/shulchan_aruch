3) There, in the gloss. Between his property and that of another person. There is no concern about thieves there. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. This means that all the residents are presumed honest. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1. This implies that if they are not presumed honest and there is concern about thieves or the like, he may build in the usual manner. The general rule of this siman is as follows. When the work is needed for the festival but is not a matter subject to loss, it must be performed with an alteration, meaning through unskilled workmanship. If it is a matter subject to loss, such as where thieves are a concern, no alteration is required and even skilled workmanship is permitted. Demolishing and rebuilding, however, were permitted only when danger is a concern. Levushei Serad. See below, subsection 10.
