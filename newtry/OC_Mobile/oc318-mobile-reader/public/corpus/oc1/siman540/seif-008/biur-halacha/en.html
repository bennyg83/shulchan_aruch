And they open an eye for them. See Beis Yosef, that the Rosh in the name of the Raavad forbids this, and so the Tur stated without qualification; nevertheless, for the practical law, the Shulchan Aruch transcribed to be lenient like the opinion of the Rambam, because the Maggid Mishneh brought in the name of the Geonim and the Ramban to be lenient in this; and it is somewhat difficult, for Rabbeinu Yeruham brought that the opinion of most of the poskim is like the Raavad.
