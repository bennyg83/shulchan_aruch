(21) They score, etc. For when it is smooth and the wheat cannot be crushed, they score it so that it not be even, in order that the wheat be crushed beneath it; and it is permitted even by opening an eye, even though it is craftsman's work; and ordinarily even for the need of the festival it is permitted only by means of a change, but here craftsman's work is permitted because it is considered like preparatory work for food needs.
(22) The millstones. And even new ones are permitted, since it is for the need of the festival.
(23) And they set them up. That is, they repair them well in their place in order to use them (Rosh).
(24) And they build the water channel, etc. For by means of it they grind.
(25) The donkey. That is, the donkey that turns the millstones; for the growth of its hooves harms it and it cannot walk so well; and all these are because it is for the need of the festival; and see above in siman 536, se'if 1.
(26) And it is permitted to sharpen, etc., and a knife, etc. For since it is melacha of food needs, it is permitted even as craftsman's work, and no change is needed.
(27) That was broken on the festival. And it implies that if it was broken before the festival it is forbidden, since it is only preparatory work for food needs; and it is proven from the Taz that this entire [restriction] is where he could have done it from before Yom Tov and deliberately left the work for Yom Tov; but if he did not deliberately [delay it], it is permitted.
(28) To make another. And even if it is possible for him by borrowing (Magen Avraham and other Acharonim).
