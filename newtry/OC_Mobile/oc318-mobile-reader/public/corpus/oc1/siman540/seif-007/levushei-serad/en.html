Taz, s.k. 3: of Rabbah bar Shmuel. Who forbids making an oven, and reconciles it as referring to the rainy season, when one cannot dry it on Chol HaMoed; and in the first clause it taught "they set up," etc., that is to say, to bake a coating; and on this he challenged: perhaps the reason for the permission is because they can bake in it since the oven is already dry; and this is easily understood.
