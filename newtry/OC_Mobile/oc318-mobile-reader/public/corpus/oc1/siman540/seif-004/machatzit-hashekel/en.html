(S.K. 4) And he builds it according to his custom. For otherwise he will refrain, etc. — this refers to what Rama wrote "and even if it only leans to the courtyard," etc., for there is no concern for thieves; therefore if not that otherwise he will refrain it would be forbidden to build it; unlike Mechaber who deals when it leans toward the public domain where there is concern for thieves — nevertheless it is permitted to build it since it is davar ha'aved.
