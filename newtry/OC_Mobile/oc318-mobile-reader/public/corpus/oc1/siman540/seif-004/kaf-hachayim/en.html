D) A congregation and committee from Israel who vowed not to marry in chol ha-moed, even lost merchandise, and even one who has nothing to eat and will not open a shop at all—may they be blessed from heaven who forbade and accepted thus as a fence. Mahari ibn Migash, responsa 25, siman 145. Beit Chayim, letter 2; see above siman 530 letter 7.
