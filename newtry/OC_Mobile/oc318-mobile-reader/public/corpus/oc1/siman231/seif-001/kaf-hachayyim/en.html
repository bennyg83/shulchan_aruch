1) [Seif 1] Gloss: When he awakens from sleep, he need not recite Elokai Neshamah. Agur, siman 3, writes in the name of R' Yaakov of Speyer that when one awakens from daytime sleep, he must recite Elokai Neshamah. Shibolei HaLeket writes the same in the name of Rabbeinu Simchah and R' Avigdor HaKohen. Beis Yosef, however, writes that this is not the practice. Bach likewise rules that one need not recite Elokai Neshamah. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1. This also appears correct according to the Kabbalists, for this blessing has no place during the day. Likewise, according to the Kabbalists, the blessing HaMapil has no place during the day, as will be explained below in siman 239, paragraph 8; see there.
