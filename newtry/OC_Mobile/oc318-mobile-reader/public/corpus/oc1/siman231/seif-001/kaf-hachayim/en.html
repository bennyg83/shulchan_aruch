(1) [Seif 1] Rama gloss. And when he awakens from his sleep he need not bless Elohai Neshamah. HaAgur siman 3 wrote in the name of R' Yehuda Meshpira that when one awakens from afternoon sleep he must bless Elohai Neshamah; and so in She'erit Yehudah in the name of Rabbeinu Simcha and R' Avigdor HaKohen; except that Beit Yosef wrote they did not practice thus; and so Bach that he need not bless Elohai Neshamah note 1; and so per the kabbalists that there is no place for this blessing by day; and likewise blessing HaMapil according to kabbalists has no place by day, as will be explained below siman 239 note 8 — see there.
