(1) If it is impossible for him etc. — see above siman 4 seif 16 and Mishnah Berurah there.

(2) And when he awakens from his sleep etc. — the reason is possible that this blessing was established for the world's custom that the Holy One blessed be He returns souls to people in the morning.

(3) And Yehi Noam — because of danger of harmful spirits; and Levush wrote they do not practice thus.

(4) More than a horse's sleep etc. — see above siman 4 seif 16 what is written in Mishnah Berurah in the name of Machatzit HaShekel.

(5) Rather he should intend to eat etc. — and I saw men of action who before eating would say: 'Behold I wish to eat and drink so that I be healthy and strong for service of Heaven' [Chayei Adam].

(6) In the council of the upright etc. — it is not praiseworthy; nevertheless it is good that through this he comes for the sake of Heaven.

(7) He should intend to have children etc. — and see other intentions in siman 240 seif 1; and after the intent of the heart are the matters, and the Merciful One requires the heart.

(8) Even to speak words of wisdom — and all the more if his conversation is in matters of business he must intend for the sake of mitzvah — meaning that he have wherewith to earn a living so he not come to theft; and even if he is very wealthy sufficient in his money to support himself and his household all his life — it can also be imagined sometimes in what he strives to expand his business that it is a mitzvah matter, such as his intent to increase charity and gemach thereby, or his intent to take businesses under his supervision to provide work for poor of Israel who will earn to sustain themselves — all this is included in charity and more excellent than it, as Rambam wrote and is brought in Yoreh De'ah siman 251; and see there that one who supports the hand of Israel and gives him work so he earns and need not ask creatures — fulfills thereby the positive commandment 'You shall surely open your hand to him and strengthen him'.
