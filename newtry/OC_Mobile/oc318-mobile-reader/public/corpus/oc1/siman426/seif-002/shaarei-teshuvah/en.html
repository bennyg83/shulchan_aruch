In Shulchan Aruch seif 2 "Blessed is your Creator." And Machatzit HaShekel wrote he heard from the holy mouth of Or HaChayim he would say thus in parashat Vayetzei; and Maharik Shapira explained this formula per the secret; and in Iggerot HaRamaz siman 10 he disagrees and wrote one should say per the secret "Your Maker, Your Creator, Your Fashioner" which is like the order of ABY"A from below upward; and so the Ramaz found and so he practiced between himself and himself but does not inform others — see there.
