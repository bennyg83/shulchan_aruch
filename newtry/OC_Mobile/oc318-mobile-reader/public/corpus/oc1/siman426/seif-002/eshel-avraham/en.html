Alive and established. That his kingdom is compared to the moon — as the moon sometimes shines and sometimes darkens, so his seed when they are good shine, etc.; and what it says "alive and established" is even though it was dark last month it returns to shine — so Israel will be redeemed in the near future and his kingdom will return; and his kingdom is alive and established for it returns to his seed and it is as if he is alive. And see midrash parashat Bo and Rekanati end of Pinchas expounds the intent on Knesset Yisrael; and see Levush and Rav in Beit Din wrote siman 555 expounds per Yerushalmi Shabbat that David asked to dwell in Your tent forever, etc., that they say halacha in his name and his lips murmur — see Yevamot 96 and Bechorot 91.
