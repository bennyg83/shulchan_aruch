Or. Taz — meaning on the night of the 15th at the Purim meal he will be busy and become drunk from wine and will not sanctify it; but its time is until half of 29 days 12 hours 793 parts from the hour of the molad, as Mishna Berurah wrote in the gloss and Magen Avraham 6; and if sometimes immediately it is fine, and all the more so those who sanctify at its time and fear lest afterward there not be a minyan of ten to sanctify on a fast if he tasted something, etc., as I saw practiced that they sanctify on the night of a fast even if the time passes. Eshel Avraham 67.
