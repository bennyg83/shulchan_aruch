(2) There: one who sees the moon, etc. A blind person is obligated in Birkat HaLevana — Rashal in responsum siman 77. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur. Magen Avraham there. However Maharikash wrote from what they say "one who sees the moon," etc. — it falls on seeing, and a blind person does not bless — end of his words. And Birkei Yosef brought it siman 224 letter alef, and likewise in his book Machazik Berachah siman 229 letter vav, and he wrote: although Maharshal in responsum siman 77 and his colleagues hold a blind person blesses Birkat HaLevana, nevertheless it is a doubt in blessings — end of his words; and so Shatz page 231b — the straight way is not to bless and hear from the blesser to remove oneself from dispute. And so Chasam Sofer part 2 Ben Aharon parashat Vayikra letter 23. However other utterances said at kiddush levana he may say himself.
