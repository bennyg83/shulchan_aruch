One recites, 'Who, by His word, created the heavens,' etc. 'So that they do not alter their assignment' means the mitzvah that He commanded them to conduct themselves according to the movement of their cycles. 'Joyful and glad' is as written, 'It rejoices like a mighty man.' 'Workers of truth' means that they do not alter their order. 'And to the moon the Holy One, blessed is He, said that it should renew itself each month.' 'A crown of glory it is for those borne from the womb' means that it is a sign for the Jewish people, who count by the moon, that they too are destined to be renewed from their exile like it and to glorify their Creator. Bach's text reads, 'in their greatness.' Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2, likewise writes that this does not refer to the destruction of the Beis HaMikdash but to the renewal of souls. Rashi on Sanhedrin 42a. Tosafos there write that some versions read the singular, 'Worker of truth,' referring to the Holy One, blessed is He, Who diminished the moon in truth and justice. The blessing therefore continues, 'And to the moon He said that it should renew itself.' Rabbeinu Yonah likewise writes that 'Worker of truth, Whose work is truth' refers to the Creator. He is truth, and the work itself is also true because it endures, as it is written, 'He established them forever and ever.' If the wording referred to the heavenly hosts, it would have sufficed to say, 'Workers of truth.' Why would it also need to say that the work they perform is truth? Beis Yosef. Bach likewise writes that this blessing should be recited according to the version of Tosafos. See there. Birkei Yosef, subsection 3, also writes that the correct wording is, 'Worker of truth, Whose work is truth,' and that this is the version of the Arizal's disciples, who explain it according to the hidden dimension of Torah. Rabbeinu Bachya in parashas Ha'azinu writes likewise. Ruach Chaim cites this, as does Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 232d. This is also the version in Mevo She'arim by the Arizal, folio 60c; Peri Etz Chaim, gate 19, chapter 3; and the siddur of Rashash. See there for the explanation of the entire blessing according to the hidden dimension of Torah. One should pause between the words 'that it should renew itself' and 'a crown.'
