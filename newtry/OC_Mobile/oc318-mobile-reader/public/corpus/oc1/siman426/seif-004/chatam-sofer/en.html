On the Mechaber seif 4: seven days upon it. Gloss: see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chaim siman 102: [Regarding what occurred in the holy community of Krakow, that in the month of Kislev the moon was covered, and since the cholera disease prevailed there and they were greatly distressed over this - for it is an omen (simana milta) - and it is known that the doctors warn greatly during a plague, meaning that people should not distress themselves and should not take any worry to heart; therefore the gaon, the Av Beit Din there, was clever to calm the hearts of the worried by ruling that even on the night of the 16th they could recite Birkat HaChodesh, and he relied on the words of Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah, who found written in one shitah of a later authority in tractate Sanhedrin in the name of R' Peretz and Rav HaMeiri that the 16th too is included in the fitness of Birkat HaLevanah. However, in responsa Devar Shmuel end of siman 216 he wrote about this, and this is his language: And the reason has not been clarified for us; and the questioner asked the opinion of the late author what he says on this matter; and after he toiled to find the opinion of the holy ones and to clarify and explain the reason of R' Peretz and the Meiri, he concluded with this language: And if so, after the fact, since the elder [sage] of your honor has already ruled, we do not reject him.]
