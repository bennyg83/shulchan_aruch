In Shulchan Aruch: one who sees the moon in its renewal, etc., wrote in Sefer HaChinuch siman 83: those who descend to sea do not sail after five days for its renewal; and they are careful not to let blood near its renewal; and flax found in a trough or inside a kettle to cook at the moon's renewal — lashes; and how many melachot need guarding not to do when the moon renews — see there; and they brought R' Yehudah; and see Baer Heitev in the name of Devar Shmuel, and see there from his words that this is only l'chatchila — one should be concerned for doubt lest from brightness of the telescope it warns him a figure of the moon; but if people outside see the moon itself and testify to him — he blesses well, since the moon's shining is evident to all at that time; and all the more so per Rashal that even a blind person blesses — see there; and see in responsa Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 126 regarding seeing through an interposition via eyeglasses called spectacles — he brought there words of Divrei Shalom, and wrote there is no proof from that in Rosh Hashanah that deals with one who did not see it in the thickness of the sky; but one who sees through a lens to the sky and sees the moon — it is actual seeing and he blesses; and perhaps Divrei Shalom deals with one who saw only inside the telescope or glass and not in the actual thickness of the sky; and in my humble opinion certainly it deals thus, for then there is relevance to say "perhaps it is a thick white circle"; but when he sees through a window to the sky and sees the moon's form in the sky through the glass — there is no mistake at all; and it seems simple one who sees through eyeglasses may bless even if his eyes cannot see without eyeglasses, for per Rashal even a blind person blesses. And even though Divrei Shalom wrote one should doubt his proofs, nevertheless they do not disagree; and if he wants to sanctify through a window and can open the window — one should do so l'chatchila as implied from Rashal's case.
