Seif 1: one who sees the moon in its renewal, etc. Note — see in Magen Avraham s.k. 1 who wrote Birkat HaLevana is a time-bound positive commandment and women are exempt, and wrote so in the name of the holy Shelah. And in my opinion this is very astonishing and has no relevance to a time-bound positive commandment at all, for this principle is said only regarding a mitzvah whose body applies always in every matter and at every time — such as matzah or sukkah and lulav and the like, that this mitzvah can be fulfilled in Cheshvan as in Tishrei and Iyar as in Nisan, and nevertheless the Torah said on those days one is obligated and from then on exempt — it is proven it is time-bound and therefore women are exempt; but regarding the moon — does it have prevention from the force of time? This prevention is from the body of the mitzvah that does not apply then, for necessarily if he already blessed once on seeing it, another blessing is not relevant, since he already blessed once for him — it would be like new fruit: if he blessed on it at time of seeing or eating he need not bless again, and in any case when it renews in the new year they bless new fruit again — shall we say in this time caused it? Certainly not, for the prevention is not from the force of time; and even if he did not bless on it this month, nevertheless another blessing is not relevant because it begins to diminish afterward — if so the prevention of blessing is that there is no body of the matter to bless on, and the prevention is from the thing that there is nothing to bless on and not from the force of time — shall we say in fruits that are not common only in summer a woman would not bless on eating or seeing shehecheyanu since it is time-caused that in winter those fruits are not available? Certainly not. So too the moon each month like fruit each year; therefore since this prevention is not from the force of the body's time but from the force of the moon — at time of renewal they bless and at time of its waning they do not bless, and the prevention is not from time but from the moon itself — the moon is literally month in its month like fruit of a year, and this is not a time-bound positive commandment as it appears to me clearly; and the rabbis did not analyze this matter — in forgiveness of their exalted honor — and understand.
