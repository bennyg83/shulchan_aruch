(1) [Levush] And he fixes his eyes, etc., and straightens his legs (Tur and Shulchan Aruch). He should gaze at the moon until he finishes the blessing, and from then on it is forbidden to look at it as if looking at a rainbow (Knesset HaGedolah from Sefer Charedim); but in Shelah 74b he wrote one may look at it when he wishes to bless, and when he begins he does not look — until here. And the wording "he fixes his eyes," etc., that all poskim wrote from Tractate Soferim does not imply this slightly; and he further wrote one must be very careful not to bend his knees to dance but rather lean on his toes and dance. Knesset HaGedolah wrote: that one does not bless "time" because it is less than thirty days — until here; but I found in Sefer Tanya that one need not bless "time," for one blesses on its renewal.
