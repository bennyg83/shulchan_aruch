(1) [Seif 1] One who sees the moon in its renewal, etc. Women are exempt — a time-bound positive commandment; and even though they fulfill all time-bound positive commandments such as sukkah, etc., nevertheless this mitzvah they do not fulfill because they caused the moon's flaw — Shelah. And in Sanhedrin (42a) we say on Birkat HaLevana "our women too bless" — it implies somewhat they bless; and it is possible it does not mean women but he used ordinary language — Magen Avraham at the head of the siman; and therefore it seems since the Gemara implies they bless, they may hear the blessing from others and intend to fulfill, or say only "Blessed… Who renews months" as explained in the Gemara — that is women's blessing. And it is possible Shelah too would agree — there is no stringency except in men's long blessing formula, but in this there is no stringency.
