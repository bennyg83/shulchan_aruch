Until. Taz — it implies specifically in motza'ei Shabbat after three days, but on a weekday after three days not; and Eshel Avraham letter 14. And behold what Shulchan Aruch wrote "one does not bless on the moon except on motza'ei Shabbat" is from Tractate Soferim, and there is another version in the name of the students of Rabbenu Yonah and Rambam hold even on day one, and it implies until seven days per him even on a weekday; and Peri Chadash implies likewise even on a weekday — see there; therefore one who practices to sanctify the moon after three days from the molad even on a weekday — one should not rebuke him, and so I practice, for sometimes it is trouble to gather ten and one comes to forgetfulness and a cloudy day, and in winter and rain days even on a weekday after three days one should not rebuke one who practices thus. And Avraham Chaviv wrote seven days means after six full days from the molad. And Eshel Avraham siman 78: nine days is the time for kiddush levana — meaning after six until 15 inclusive; and for us add 12 hours 793 parts.
