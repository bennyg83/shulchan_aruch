(3) [Levush] He says "Blessed is your Creator," etc. It implies only once; and what Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote "and he says three times: a good sign for all Israel, Blessed is your Creator," etc. — one must say that "three times" does not refer to "Blessed is your Creator," and so it implies in Abudarham; and see in s.k. 5 it is proven thus.
