3) A congregation that came to recite Birchas HaLevanah but found no one who knew the blessing except one blind person, who then stood and recited it, may have fulfilled their obligation even according to Maharikash, who maintains that the blind person is not personally obligated. He may nevertheless discharge the obligation of others, similar to the ruling in siman 297 that one who cannot smell may recite the blessing on behalf of others. Sdei HaAretz, volume 3, siman 9; Ikarei HaDat, end of siman 16.
