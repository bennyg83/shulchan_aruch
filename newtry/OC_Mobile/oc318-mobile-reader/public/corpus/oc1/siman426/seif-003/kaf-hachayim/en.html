(א) And one who sees through eyeglasses may bless even though he cannot see without eyeglasses — Sha'arei Teshuvah letter alef Ben Aharon there.

(ב) A congregation who came to bless Birkat HaLevana and found no one who knows to bless except one blind person who stood and blessed — it is possible even per Maharikash that he is not obligated, nevertheless he fulfills others; and it is similar to what is written siman 297 that one who does not smell fulfills others with his blessing — Sdeh HaAretz part 3 siman 9, Ikvei HaDat siman 16.
