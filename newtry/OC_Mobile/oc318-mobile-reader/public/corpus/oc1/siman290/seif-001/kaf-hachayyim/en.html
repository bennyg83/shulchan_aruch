1) [Seif 1] He should increase his consumption of fruit, etc. This does not constitute an unnecessary blessing, because each time he again derives benefit, and he may not benefit from this world without a blessing. It is therefore a necessary blessing. Bach.
2) If the fruits require the same blessing, he should instruct that they be brought before him one after another. Maharam Galanti, siman 38. Knesses HaGedolah above in siman 46, however, cites Sefer HaZichronos, which disagrees and maintains that this is forbidden because it creates an unnecessary blessing. Or Chadash similarly questions Maharam Galanti. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1, cites these authorities and writes that Sefer HaZichronos and those who follow it state the primary ruling; see there. We wrote the same above in siman 215, paragraph 30; see there.
