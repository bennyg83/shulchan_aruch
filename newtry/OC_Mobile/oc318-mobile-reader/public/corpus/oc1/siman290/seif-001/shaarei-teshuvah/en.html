In fruits, etc. See Eshel Avraham from Responsa Maharam Galanti and Birkei Yosef siman 47:2, that Sefer HaZikhronot disagreed with Maharam Galanti and the Birkei Yosef agreed with Sefer HaZikhronot, unlike Maharshal Halevi. See Magen Avraham above siman 215, who cites the Shelah and Kenesset HaGedolah in the name of Sefer HaZikhronot. See what my grandfather wrote in Tevuot Shor siman 19: nevertheless one should not bring before him many kinds of fruit and bless on each separately by intending not to exempt the other with this blessing, for in that way it is a berakhah levatalah; there is no proof from siman 174.
(1) Noon. See Ba'er Heitev. See Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Sefer Chasidim that one should not say so even if he wants to write Torah on Motzaei Shabbat; rather he should say, "for today is Shabbat." See there in the name of Shelah that one should not increase, etc. On the contrary, a person is obligated to innovate Torah insights, as stated in the Zohar, that on Motzaei Shabbat, when the extra soul returns to its place, the Holy One asks it what new Torah insight it said. Machazik Berakhah writes in the name of Da'at Chokhmah in the name of the Ari that if one innovates in Torah, his father is crowned in that world; therefore honoring parents is juxtaposed to Shabbat. One who is not capable of innovating should study things he has not yet learned. Birkei Yosef writes that some wrote in the name of the Ari that he would sleep two or three hours on Shabbat; Machazik Berakhah writes that many fulfill the Ari's custom here, and if only they would fulfill the rest of his holy practices and warnings.
