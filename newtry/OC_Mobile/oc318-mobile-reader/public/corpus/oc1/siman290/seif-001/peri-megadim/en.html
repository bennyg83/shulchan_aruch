It is customary to deliver a sermon before Minchah to state the laws of Shabbat and a bit of mussar. And Eliyahu Rabbah letter 3 in the Midrash: if you assemble on Shabbat to deliver a sermon it is as if you crowned Me — for three testify one against the other: Heaven, Shabbat, and Israel. And in Kiddushin: anyone who lacks knowledge of Scripture and Mishnah is disqualified from testimony — they assemble amei ha'aretz to deliver a sermon to them from Tanach and they are fit for testimony. Magen Avraham: one should not say he will sleep in order to do our work in the evening.
