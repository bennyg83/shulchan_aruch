Even though he nullified. Even though he did not violate from the Torah, we fear he will deceive and say he nullified though he did not (Tur); and see siman 447; likewise one who declares chametz hefker — so Rosh and poskim in the name of Yerushalmi, unlike one opinion (end of his words). R' Shimon HaLevi siman 12 wrote: one on a ship wanted to permit declaring all his chametz hefker and re-acquire after Pesach and sell — but not to eat, for this is uncommon and because of chametz stringency I was not comfortable to act (Knesset HaGedolah). It is obvious that even if the chametz is not with him he can sell to a non-Jew by one of the acquisitions mentioned, and then the non-Jew acquired the chametz wherever it is; see above siman 426 seif 3.
