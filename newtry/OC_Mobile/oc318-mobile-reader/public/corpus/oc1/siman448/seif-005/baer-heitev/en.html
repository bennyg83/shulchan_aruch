That he nullified. Even though he did not violate from the Torah, nevertheless we fear he will deceive and say he nullified though he did not, since he acted not as required — not burning chametz or selling to a non-Jew. Even absolute hefker — the law is so; even if there are witnesses that he declared hefker and nullified, it does not help if he left chametz in his house in his domain knowingly all the days of Pesach — certainly in such a case nullification or hefker does not help; but whenever he did not know of it all the days of Pesach and there are witnesses that he nullified or declared hefker, or there is absolute coercion in the sale matter and all Pesach he thought he sold and after Pesach it became known he did not sell because he was coerced — so great coercion that he said he nullified though there are no witnesses — we do not suspect evasion.
