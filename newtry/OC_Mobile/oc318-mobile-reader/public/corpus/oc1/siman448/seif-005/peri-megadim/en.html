If he sold chametz in the sixth hour to a gentile — one can say it is a sale and l'chatchila depends on dispute whether work helps; see Choshen Mishpat 207 and siman 208 in rabbinic too; see Kiddushin sold and betrothed with its value; see responsum Pnei Yehoshua Orach Chayim part 1 siman 12 and 14; above I brought it; see siman 431 in Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah in responsa of rabbis of Venice.
