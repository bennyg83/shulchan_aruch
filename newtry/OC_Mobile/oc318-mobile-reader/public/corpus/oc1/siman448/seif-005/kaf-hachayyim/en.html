5) Two Jews were partners in chametz. One sold the share that would be allocated to that Jew to a non-Jew before Pesach, while the other did not sell. After Pesach they divided the chametz. The portion allocated to the one who sold a share to the non-Jew before Pesach is permitted. The portion of the one who did not sell before Pesach is prohibited. Sha'agas Aryeh, siman 91; Machazik Berachah, subsection 2; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1; Sha'arei Teshuvah, there.
