5) Two Jews were partners in chametz. One sold to a non-Jew before Pesach the share that would fall to him, while the other did not sell. After Pesach they divided it. The portion that fell to the partner who sold his share to the non-Jew before Pesach is permitted. The portion of the partner who did not sell before Pesach is prohibited. Shaagat Aryeh, siman 91; Machazik Berachah, letter 2; Mekor Chaim, letter 1; Shaarei Teshuvah there.
