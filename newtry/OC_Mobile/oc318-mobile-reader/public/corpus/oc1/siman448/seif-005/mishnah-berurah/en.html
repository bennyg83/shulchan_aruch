(25) Forbidden even though he nullified — or declared hefker; forbidden even in benefit. Even when nullified he does not violate bal yera'eh, nevertheless Sages feared if we permit when nullified, everyone will leave chametz until after Pesach claiming hefker before Pesach. Know many Acharonim wrote even if he checked per our custom and found chametz after Pesach — forbidden in benefit; some lenient when checked, nullified, then found — he did all required; their view: at least for benefit do not forbid; in great loss rely on them (see Biur Halacha seif 3 s.v. even). One on a ship or road with chametz and no gentile to sell — declared hefker before witnesses whether he may acquire and benefit after Pesach — Acharonim dispute, majority forbid; in great loss permit selling to gentile or exchanging. If on road remembered chametz at home, no one to sell on eve — declared hefker before witnesses — may rely on lenient view to re-acquire after Pesach, since not in his hand and could not sell or burn as required.
