If a non-Jew brings, etc. If the non-Jew received it when the Jew was not present, it is permitted after Pesach. A person prefers to acquire something permitted rather than something prohibited. Thus, the Jew had no intention to acquire chametz during Pesach, but only after Yom Tov. This is from a responsum of Rashba.
It is proper that he say, etc. Strictly according to the law, it is permitted even without an explicit statement, because a person prefers to acquire a permitted right rather than a prohibited one, as stated in Bava Metzia.
