(there seif 2) If a gentile brings a Jew a gift, etc. We learn in Talmud first chapter of Chullin: Rava said — a baraita supports you: chametz of sinners is permitted immediately because they exchange. They thought: whose view is R' Yehuda who holds chametz over which Passover passed is forbidden from the Torah? How was it not established perhaps Rabbanan? It appears per what we learn in Sheviit chapter 5 mishnah 5: from when may one buy turnips after shemittah? R' Yehuda: immediately; Chachamim: when the new increases — if so proven for Rabbanan we do not rely on leniency though not established there for sinners; likewise this presumption that they do not abandon.
