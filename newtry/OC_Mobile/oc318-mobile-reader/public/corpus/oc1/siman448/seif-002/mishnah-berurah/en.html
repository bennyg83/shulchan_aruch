(א) (c) On the last day, even though it is filtered and circumcised in its body:

(ב) (d) We shall not receive Israel, for it shall not be seen, and if it is not received by pleasure after Passover, because of the wrath of Israel that has passed through it:

(ג) He should not show, etc. — if it would be apparent he desires it, such as telling him place it in a certain hidden spot in my courtyard — his courtyard acquires for him; but if not apparent, even if the gentile leaves it as a gift in his house it is permitted, for presumably he does not want a prohibition to be acquired and his courtyard does not acquire against his will; therefore even if this were on the first yom tov it is permitted.

(ד) And he said that he did not want to do so, and so on, even if he showed him a place to lay down on him until the evening when he or she was not, and so on, even if he showed him that he should not be able to do so, and that he should not be able to do so with him
