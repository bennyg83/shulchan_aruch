[2] On Yom Tov. See Taz. After the fact, even on the first Yom Tov it is permitted, even if he was silent, because he does not want to acquire a prohibition; see Beis Yosef and see there. On the eighth day, if he accepted it, it is forbidden for benefit; see Tur and Levush, se'if 3. Even though we are expert in fixing the calendar and he does not transgress bal yera'eh because of benefit from it, they made a fence, and everywhere the second day is like the first day. If he accepted it during twilight of the first day, at first glance after Pesach it should be a rabbinic doubt and lenient. One can say that the prohibition was established, as in siman 342 in Magen Avraham. See Magen Avraham here, note 1, and Yoreh De'ah 106 regarding this, and see Chok Yaakov 6. On the last Yom Tov Chok Yaakov says that covering with a vessel is not required. I do not know why, for in siman 446, and there even on one day, covering with a vessel is enough; at least on the last Yom Tov, which is rabbinic, it should require it so as not to treat it lightly. It appears that even noksheh requires covering with a vessel, and this requires examination. See what I will write, with Hashem's help, in note 3.
