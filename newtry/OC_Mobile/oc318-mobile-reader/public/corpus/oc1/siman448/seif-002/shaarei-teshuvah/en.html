Non-Jew. Baer Heitev; see responsum Beit Efraim siman 34 — one had malt in Vienna belonging to a gentile to make burnt wine; the gentile said he would make burnt wine on Pesach; on the eve he sold the malt properly and the gentile threw the malt into boiling water to make burnt wine; some doubt: since he did not inform the buyer the gentile would throw the grain into water, it would be mistaken sale. I raised that one should not void the sale for this; see there copied from manuscript of the gaon Baal Peri Yehoshua — his view is malt is hard chametz; explained there everything depends whether spoiled greatly until unfit for human food as explained siman 442 — then hard; and.
