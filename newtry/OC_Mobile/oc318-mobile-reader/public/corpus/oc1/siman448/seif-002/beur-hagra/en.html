Seif 2 he should not, etc. — and also, etc., and it is good. For perforce he does not acquire his domain, as in Bava Metzia 117a — the reason is their faces, etc.; but on the first part one does not listen — no challenge; and even without saying, stam we do not acquire his domain, as written 96b daf 2: leniency, etc., and there 102a, and Chullin 139a: wherever he, etc.; and this is what "it is good" means — for clarity; but if the Jew accepts, even though it is a prohibition, it acquires for him, as in Avodah Zarah 42a: perhaps he will lift it, etc.; and this is why he wrote he should not, etc., and likewise his domain, as in chapter 6 of Demai: convert and gentile, etc.; and if they come, etc.; and this is why he also wrote he should not — for domain acquires immediately when he desires it, as in Bava Batra chapter 5: in the buyer's domain, once, etc.
