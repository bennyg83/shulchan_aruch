[4] On the last day, etc. So too it is implied regarding the first day; rather, it teaches us that even on the last day it must not be recognizable, for if he accepted them they are forbidden for benefit, and if it was not recognizable, then even on the first day it is permitted. Chok Yaakov wrote: on the last day, covering with a vessel and a partition of ten handbreadths are not required, but during Pesach one needs covering with a vessel or a partition of ten handbreadths. This is the end of his words. This seemingly contradicts what he wrote in siman 440, that he must destroy it. As a matter of law, I wrote in se'if katan 8 that the main view is that he does not need to destroy it; rather, if it is on Yom Tov, when he cannot make a partition, he should cover it with a vessel, and on Chol HaMoed he should make a partition. On the last day too, it appears that he should cover it with a vessel, for he wrote there that if he left it only for half a day he should cover it with a vessel. This is the end of his words. However, if he brings it after minchah, he certainly need not do so. This is the language of Tzeidah LaDerech: on the last day, one should not show the bringers that he wants to acquire it; rather, the non-Jews should leave it in a corner of the house. This is his language.
