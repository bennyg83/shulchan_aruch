Before you purchase chametz for a maneh. Rambam’s wording in chapter 4 of Hilchos Chametz is: “Before you purchase from a non-Jew, purchase from a Jew.”
And I will purchase it from you. Rashba, responsum 850, concludes: “I or another Jew will purchase it from you.” See Responsa Maharam of Brisk, siman 51. The later authorities write that he should say, “And afterward I will give you a profit.”
