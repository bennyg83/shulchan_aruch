(א) (h) Until you take a lot of snails in your life, and so on, you will take a lot of snails from you, and you will be given a blessing for all the following:

(ב) I will have to do so, and the others have written a sermon even to ensure that it is returned and bought from it

(ג) On condition — when he said in language of "if" — if you do such-and-such during or after Pesach the chametz is given or sold to you; as long as the condition is not fulfilled the chametz is not sold or given, and therefore he violates bal yera'eh. But if he said: I give you from now on condition that you do such-and-such, the chametz belongs to the non-Jew immediately if the condition is fulfilled later, and forbidden only because of chametz stringency, as below end of seif 3. Alternatively, even with "from now" the condition was that when he brings money he returns it — with this condition sale and gift do not take effect at all, since every hour the Jew can redeem it, as explained in Choshen Mishpat siman 207 seif 6. Know further that they agreed.
