(ב) And if he has much chametz, etc. — it implies specifically when he cannot take it out it is permitted; but if he can take it from the house he must take it out; likewise in Sefer Ateret Rosh — so is custom in Prague that beer sellers must take all chametz from their houses; they asked why they differ from wine sellers who may keep chametz in the house and sell to a gentile. He answered: beer sellers differ for they cannot take out the chametz (end of his words).

(ג) But with a deed — meaning requires both: money and also deed; no concern whether money first or deed first — so there in Choshen Mishpat; Shelah wrote even if the deed is written in our script, i.e. Hebrew — he writes to the gentile: I sell room such-and-such with all inside (end of his words); what he wrote with all, etc. — see below.

(ד) And behold, etc. with money alone — and Shelah concluded on this; nevertheless one must inform the gentile and explain well so his mind relies on the leniency (end of his words).

(ה) Bach. In truth here Bach wrote it suffices if he gifts that he acquire with money alone; but in Choshen Mishpat he brought Shach in the name of Bach who disagrees on this law and holds that stipulating to acquire with bare money does not work; it is possible Bach is lenient regarding chametz since otherwise it is not in his domain but Scripture made it as if in his domain, as Magen Avraham wrote afterward.

(ו) And siman 449 seif 5 — meaning Bach wrote that though one can rent the room to a gentile and land-rental is acquired by a gentile through money alone, it is not possible for two reasons: one, we hold rental does not acquire; and further Yerushalmi forbids renting a room to gentiles to use chametz — and Pri Chadash rejected these reasons: what they said rental does not acquire — nevertheless for chametz it helps, as Pesachim daf 4 teaches chametz is different because it is written lo yimatze; also through rental it is not in his possession. What Yerushalmi forbids — we do not hold like that Yerushalmi; nevertheless for the law he agreed with Bach that there is no remedy through rental, for it is profiting from benefit prohibitions which is forbidden; and for this reason he wrote in siman 449 seif 5 in Magen Avraham s.k. 16.

(ח) And Mishna Berurah, etc. with money and pulling, etc. — for regarding this gold R' Yehuda and R' Levi dispute: R' Yehuda holds d'oraisa money acquires for a Jew, and Scripture wrote "when you sell to your fellow" and excludes a gentile — if so for a gentile money does not work except pulling is required; R' Levi holds d'oraisa for a Jew pulling acquires — if so for a gentile the opposite: specifically money acquires and not pulling. Rashi and those of his school ruled like R' Levi; Rabbenu Tam and those of his school ruled like R' Yehuda. (י) And requires study — in Yerushalmi regarding acquisitions, place of confusion — Kaf HaChayyim wrote in the name of responsum Avnei Nezer: the intent is not that by transferring place it becomes the gentile's courtyard and the courtyard acquires the chametz, for courtyard is from the reason of agency and there is no agency for a gentile; rather the intent is through agav movable property is acquired with land (and therefore the language should be agav and not "with," as Choshen Mishpat siman 210 per one opinion there). However in responsum Avodat HaGershuni siman 120 he wrote — therefore Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 317 wrote regarding annulling firstborn sanctity that the clear acquisition is that he acquires the place and gave in that responsum a reason why this acquisition is clear, that through pulling.

(כ) And I already wrote it is difficult to transfer to a gentile through agav, for a gentile's mind does not rely on acquiring through agav; and where his mind is not to acquire through this acquisition he did not acquire, as written in the name of responsum Avnei Nezer in the name of poskim. Therefore it occurred to me better to sell to a gentile woman who has no husband — for in such a case he concedes to responsum Avnei Nezer that her courtyard acquires for her, as explained in Choshen Mishpat siman 243 that a minor has no courtyard — for a man, courtyard is from agency and there is no agency for a minor; but a minor girl has a courtyard, for regarding a woman courtyard is from the reason of "hand," and a minor girl has a hand. If so a gentile woman — one can say "hand," and even though a woman's courtyard from "hand" we learn from divorce as in first chapter of Bava Metzia, and divorce does not exist for a gentile — nevertheless one can say.

(ל) And so merchants practice, etc. — meaning Jewish merchants; and this is the law of situmta explained in Choshen Mishpat siman 210; but Shach wrote in the name of Bach that for land situmta does not acquire unless it is known clearly that the custom in that place is to acquire land through situmta; all the more acquisition between gentile and Jew which in their law is obvious.
