4) A Jew and a non-Jew who jointly own chametz and other items may not say to the non-Jew, 'You take the chametz and I will take the other items.' Mekor Chaim, subsection 3; Chayei Adam, there. Eliyah Rabbah there, however, appears to rule leniently because chametz does not transfer its prohibition to the money received for it. See there. It is possible that one may rely upon this where a substantial loss is involved.
