To a non-Jew outside the house. Bach, Beit Yosef — it implies from Terumat HaDeshen's words that giving chametz to a non-Jew does not work (unless he is a non-Jew) outside the house; the intent is not that the non-Jew be a stranger not living in a Jew's house, but regarding the chametz — if it is in a Jew's house there is no concern; this certainly has no taste or smell, and further it is worse than chametz in his house; and further Terumat HaDeshen did not write outside the house but outside his house — implying he spoke of the chametz being outside the house, and we are not concerned about the non-Jew even if in the Jew's servant's house, for below he permits even one who recognizes him and is accustomed, etc. All this I wrote per Beit Yosef in the name of Terumat HaDeshen; but the essence of the matter we do not lean — for it is explicit in first chapter of Bava Metzia that I brought in siman 440.
