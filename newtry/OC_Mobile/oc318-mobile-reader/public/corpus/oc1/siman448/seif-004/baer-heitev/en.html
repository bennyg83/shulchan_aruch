[4] And I will buy it. He is permitted even to promise that he will buy it back from him and will give him a profit. Bach and the later authorities.
[4] On condition. It appears that the meaning is: on condition that he return it to him after Pesach, for then it is neither a sale nor a gift. But if he said to him: on condition that you keep it for yourself and do with it as you wish, only that you not sell it to any other person, this is a complete sale. Taz; see there.
