(א) And I will take it. And I will promise that he will come back and buy from him and give him a sigh. In and on:

(ב) On condition. It appears the meaning is on condition that he return it after Pesach — for then it is neither sale nor gift; but if he said on condition that he hold it for himself and do with it as he wishes, only not sell to anyone else — this is an absolute sale (Taz, end of his words).
