Magen Avraham s.k. 4: but gentile to gentile makes an agent — likewise Rikanti Choshen Mishpat siman 188; but from words of Baal HaTanya brought in Beit Yosef Yoreh De'ah siman 169 and glosses there 11 — proven gentile to gentile does not become agent, as Tiferet Lemoshe there; see Beit Shmuel Even HaEzer siman 5; responsum Maharam Mintz siman 5 wrote: but gentile to gentile possibly there is agency.
