[4] Magen Avraham, se'if katan 4. "But a non-Jew can make another non-Jew an agent." So too wrote Rikash, Choshen Mishpat, siman 188. But from the words of Baal HaTerumos, brought in Beis Yosef, Yoreh De'ah, siman 169, and in the glosses there, se'if 11, it is proven that one non-Jew does not become an agent for another non-Jew, as Tefillah LeMoshe wrote there. See Beis Shmuel, Even HaEzer, end of siman 5. In Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 5, he wrote: but perhaps a non-Jew can be an agent for a non-Jew.
