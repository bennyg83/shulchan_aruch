(ב) He thereby violates bal yera'eh. One who deposits chametz with a non-Jew violates, as siman 440 (Mishna Berurah). It must deal when the Jew himself attached the condition, e.g. that if the Jew has money he will return it — in such a case it is not a sale at all, as Choshen Mishpat siman 207 (end of his words), and worse than a pledge where he said if I do not redeem before Rosh Chodesh Nisan it is yours from now; but here redemption is always in his power. Alternatively, the Jew attached the sale to a condition, e.g. if you do such-and-such the chametz is yours — as long as the condition is not fulfilled it is not his sale; but if he said: I give you from now on condition to return or on condition you do such-and-such, this is an absolute sale.
