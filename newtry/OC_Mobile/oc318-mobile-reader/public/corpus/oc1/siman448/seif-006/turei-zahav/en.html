And he may not give on condition. It appears the meaning is on condition that he return it after Pesach — for then it is neither sale nor gift; but if he said on condition that he hold it for himself and do as he wishes, only not sell to anyone else — this is an absolute sale even all year; it appears anyone may say so explicitly to the non-Jew and it helps that no one can buy that chametz from the non-Jew because he sold on this condition, and the sale returns to the non-Jew and the Jew owner can buy it back; but then the owner is forbidden to sue the Jew who bought from the non-Jew and say give me the chametz at that sale price, for thereby he reveals his mind that the sale was void and the chametz returns to.
