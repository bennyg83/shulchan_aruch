And even though. See Taz; Mesoret Binyamin siman 43 expanded — Magen Avraham brought (letter 5) because in everyone's mind he did not declare hefker. Forbidden to sue the gentile if he does not want — to sue he can sue the Jew who bought improperly. It appears one may sell the room and chametz for a large sum much above value and add as loan — in their law no concern for void sale; see Choshen Mishpat 227:26 — if so no one will give this sum. However from seif 3 selling for small amount no proof — there owners declare hefker and no void-sale concern (see Chak Yakov, Taz; Terumat HaDeshen 120). In any case here likewise one can say so; if slightly less than.
