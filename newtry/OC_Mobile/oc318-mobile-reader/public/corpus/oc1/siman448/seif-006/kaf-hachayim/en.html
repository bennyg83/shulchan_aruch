(6) There. Permitted even for eating. That which the Mishnah teaches (Pesachim 28a) gentile chametz over which Passover passed permitted in benefit — not to imply forbidden in eating; rather because it needed to teach Jew's forbidden in benefit, it taught regarding gentile permitted in benefit (Rif and Rosh). In the name of Yerushalmi Rosh wrote: Mishnah gentile chametz permitted in benefit — where they did not practice eating gentile bread; but where practiced, permitted even in eating (Beit Din, Beit Yosef; Razah 2). See Yoreh De'ah siman 112 seif 2 which gentile bread they were lenient (see there).
