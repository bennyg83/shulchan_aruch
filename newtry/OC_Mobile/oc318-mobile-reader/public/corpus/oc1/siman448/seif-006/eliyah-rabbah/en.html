[6] [Levush] and Samaritans, etc. Even his servant (Bach and Taz), unlike his Canaanite slave and maidservant — his hand is his master's (Sha'ar Ephraim); likewise to Samaritan apostate one may not sell, for though he sinned he is a Jew. Incident: sold burnt wine for a large sum, hard to lose — close the Jew would not comply; I said the Samaritan apostate should exchange with another Samaritan and take other burnt wine, then permitted to the Jew, or sell and money permitted to ask — chametz does not bind its value (Taz); see end siman 463. In responsum printed end Sha'ar Ephraim I wondered on Sha'ar Ephraim siman 4 who permitted the burnt wine itself after.
