(א) (s.k. 7) He thereby violates, etc. — if the Jew will have money, etc. — meaning he did not set a time for this, but whenever the Jew gives money the gentile must return the chametz — if so no hour is clarified that it belongs to the gentile even one hour, and therefore it is not a sale; similarly Semag wrote in siman 207 s.k. 11 (end of his words).

(ב) Alternatively the Jew attached the sale to a condition — meaning he did not say from now but the sale takes effect only at the time the condition is fulfilled; but if he said: Here it is for you from now, etc. — and even though Magen Avraham wrote above siman 441 s.k. 2 that even from now does not work except for a loan and pledge (see there the reason) and not for a gift — that is for us who hold in Choshen Mishpat that from now does not remove asmakhta and there is a dispute in Darkei Chaim (see there siman 207 seif 9 and Semag s.k. 44); but Rambam holds from now alone even for a gift removes asmakhta, as explained there in Choshen Mishpat. Here Shulchan Aruch wrote "he thereby violates" in Rambam's language; and Magen Avraham here brought Maggid Mishneh explaining Rambam's words — if so what Magen Avraham wrote here.
