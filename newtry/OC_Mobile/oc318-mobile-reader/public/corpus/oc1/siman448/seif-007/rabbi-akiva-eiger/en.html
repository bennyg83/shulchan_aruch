Magen Avraham s.k. 7: only because of chametz stringency forbidden in seif 7 dealing with primary law l'chatchila — forbidden by law lest the condition not be fulfilled and the chametz be the Jew's and he violate bal yera'eh; but b'dieved if he did and the condition was fulfilled, by law chametz permitted afterward; however one can say it is in his power to fulfill the condition — if the gentile does not return he can say I accept as if received — which helps l'chatchila in gift (end of his words); if so one can say even l'chatchila permitted — requires study.
