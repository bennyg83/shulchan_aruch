Baer Heitev; and what he wrote in the name of Kaf HaChayyim — Jew and gentile partners — see responsum Sha'agat Aryeh siman 89 and 90 who wrote: if they divided after Pesach even though he did not sell to the gentile before Pesach, nevertheless the Jew's share is also permitted; see responsum Beit Efraim siman 46 on this; see Sha'agat Aryeh siman 91 — two Jewish partners, one sold his chametz to a gentile before Pesach wherever it was to the gentile and the other did not sell — after division the seller's share permitted and the non-seller's forbidden (see there).
