Non-Jew. It appears to me that one who has a partnership with a non-Jew in chametz over which Passover passed — even though half belongs to a Jew and is forbidden in benefit — nevertheless when he divides after Pesach with the non-Jew, the non-Jew's share (since after Pesach it is only d'rabbanan we say there is clarification) is permitted in benefit. For eating requires study (Kaf HaChayyim, see there). A convert who died and left chametz over which Passover passed — whoever precedes after Passover and acquires it (for when he died it became ownerless and no one acquired it to violate) — it is permitted to him (Kaf HaChayyim, see there).
