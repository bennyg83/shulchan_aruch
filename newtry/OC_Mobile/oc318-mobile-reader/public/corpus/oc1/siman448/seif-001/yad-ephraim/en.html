In Magen Avraham s.k. 5: but here if the gentile wants to fulfill, etc. — meaning perforce we do not say "since," etc. as his own — only when it is in his power to acquire we say it is as if he already acquired; unlike a matter not his and not in his power to acquire, only dependent on others — we do not say as his own; therefore if he returned after Pesach and the condition was fulfilled, permitted; nevertheless it appears l'chatchila obviously forbidden, lest the gentile not return and retroactively the chametz was the Jew's; only Radbaz came to give reason to forbid chametz after Pesach by law; on this Magen Avraham wrote this does not apply since he cannot acquire; nevertheless I lean Radbaz.
