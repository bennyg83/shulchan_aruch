[1] Of Samaritans, etc. One who has partnership with Samaritans in chametz over which Passover passed — when he divides after Pesach, his share is forbidden in benefit and the Samaritan's share permitted in benefit, for there is clarification in d'rabbanan; for eating requires study (Chak Yakov); see Yoreh De'ah siman 146 seif 4: convert and Samaritans who inherited Samaritan fathers — the convert can tell the Samaritan take your idolatry and I take money, etc., because convert inheritance is d'rabbanan; but if they partnered, forbidden; if so here likewise for chametz; however there the reason is he wants its existence (see Tosafot Avodah Zarah 64); if so likewise if partnership in chametz and money — forbidden; however chametz does not bind its value (see end siman 443). Incident.
