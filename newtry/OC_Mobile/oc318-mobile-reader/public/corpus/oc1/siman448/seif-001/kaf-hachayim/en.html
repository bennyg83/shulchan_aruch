(1) [Seif 1] Chametz of a gentile, etc. Yerushalmi chapter Kol Sha'ah: convert died and left chametz over which Passover passed — whoever precedes after Pesach and acquires, permitted per his view (end of his words). This does not contradict our Talmud Bava Kamma 110b — robbing convert's chametz over which Passover passed forbidden in benefit — there the convert did not die until chametz prohibition applied, or even if he died before Pesach, it was in the robber's hand during chametz prohibition — unlike here when the convert died the chametz remained ownerless, so no one violates bal yera'eh of yours — you see of hefker. Kaf HaChayyim 1; Mekor Chayyim 1; Chayei Adam general 124 24. If so likewise hefker chametz permitted after.
