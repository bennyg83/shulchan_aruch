Permitted (Taz). Bach challenged from baraita 41b; Pri Chadash challenged from seif 4 from Tosefta that one may tell a gentile; and Pesachim 1a: go out and acquire chametz of soldiers. Taz explained everything; one can say for Chizkiyah who does not eat leaven within its time — before its time permitted in benefit, only in eating; nevertheless for R' Yehuda in Tosafot Pesachim 28b s.v. one; Rif and Rosh appear they ruled like Chizkiyah — R' Eliezer Pesachim chapter 8 not like R' Meir (Pri Megadim chapter 8 from Magen Avraham letter 5). In Gemara 29a it challenges: if R' Yehuda, gentile's too — R' Avahu challenges; for Chizkiyah one can answer: Mishnah — R' Yehuda: gentile's permitted after Pesach in benefit, in eating forbidden; nevertheless Jew's forbidden — penalty too; Beit Yosef rules like R' Yehuda and these baraitot like R' Shimon; what Beit Yosef rules like R' Shimon before its time — Tur in his name in Pri Megadim — one can say after its time he ruled like R' Yehuda, for for us too before its time like R' Yehuda and after like R' Shimon; Pesachim 28b implies from when forbidden in eating forbidden in benefit like R' Yehuda — a fortiori from orlah not worked as sin — see Peri Chadash; however after Pesach permitted in eating also for R' Shimon — nevertheless no a fortiori from orlah as stated. What Bach wrote: one must check within the festival and after the festival will be explained in letter 7; in Magen Avraham 8. Gentile chametz during Pesach forbidden in benefit, for we do not hold like Rav Acha (Beit Yosef; Pri Chadash); therefore forbidden to smell gentile bread (see Eruvin 64b; Tur what Beit Yosef wrote: nullified after Pesach forbidden in eating and permitted in benefit — no reason, etc.). And what Taz wrote: even hefker forbidden in eating and permitted in benefit for R' Yehuda and Chizkiyah — requires study; see letter 3.
