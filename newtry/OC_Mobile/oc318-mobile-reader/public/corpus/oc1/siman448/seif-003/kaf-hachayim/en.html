(3) There. Chametz of a gentile, etc. One who has partnership with a gentile in chametz over which Passover passed — even though half of a Jew is forbidden in benefit, when he divides after Pesach the gentile's share is permitted in benefit, since chametz over which Passover passed is only forbidden d'rabbanan as penalty we say there is clarification (for eating requires study; however in Chok HaPesach 1 s.v. it appears permitted even for eating — see there; likewise Razah). But the Jew's share is certainly forbidden in benefit (Kaf HaChayyim 2; Ateret Rosh 1; Yad Ephraim 1; Mekor Chayyim 1; Razah 27; Chayei Adam there 25; see Mekor Chayyim who leans the gentile's share is permitted specifically when placed in the gentile's house).
