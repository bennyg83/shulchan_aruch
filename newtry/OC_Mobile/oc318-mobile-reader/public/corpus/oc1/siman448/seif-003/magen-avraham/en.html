(א) Not in pleasure. Dexo Hoyle and went on to Bell to see B.C.:

(ב) Outside the house. Meaning he must also take the chametz outside the house — for if it remains in his house it appears he accepts the non-Jew's chametz as a deposit; but by law it is permitted since the Jew is not liable; but if when the chametz is lost the Jew must return the sale price to the non-Jew, it is forbidden. If he has much chametz and cannot take it outside, he may sell him the room too; but since we hold a non-Jew does not acquire land by money alone without a deed, he should stipulate at the start of the transaction that he transfers with money alone, as in Choshen Mishpat siman 194 and 207; and he must give the key so he can enter, otherwise it is mere evasion (Bach). See siman 449 seif 5; see Shelah. Mishna Berurah siman 59 wrote he must transfer with.

(ג) Does not work. Even though elsewhere it is a gift as in first chapter of Kiddushin and Sukkah chapter 3 — because of chametz stringency it does not work, and it is an extra stringency and they did not add to compare it to another matter (Terumat HaDeshen and Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 6 of Shabbat). Radbaz part 1 siman 140 wrote the reason: as long as the condition is pending it is like a deposit; if the condition is fulfilled, even though retroactively the chametz was the non-Jew's, before fulfillment the Jew already violated and fulfillment of the condition cannot cancel the violation already committed (Knesset HaGedolah). I would say unlike siman 441 with a non-Jew who lent on his chametz and said if I do not pay by Rosh Chodesh Nisan it is yours from now — permitted, for there one can say as above that since he will certainly pay.
