[3] Provided, etc. This is from the Tosefta and Yerushalmi there.

[3] Or that he sell it, etc. According to the reading of Maharam and Agudah in the Tosefta: "Instead of your buying it for two hundred, buy it for one hundred, and I will go back and buy it from you after Pesach." This means that even if he tells him that he will buy it from him for a higher price and give him profit, it is permitted, since he sells it to him without evasion. See Bach.
[3] But as a gift. According to the Gemara's explanation of the Tosefta mentioned above, "provided, etc." And even though elsewhere this works, here it does not because of the stringency of chametz. Hagahos Maimoniyos.
