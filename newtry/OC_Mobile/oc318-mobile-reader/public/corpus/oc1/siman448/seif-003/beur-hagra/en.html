(א) Seif 3 even if he left it. Meaning do not say the penalty does not apply to R' Shimon — proof from Israel who pledged with a gentile, forbidden if he did not say from now — there 1a; nevertheless.

(ב) And if he sold, etc. Tosefta and Yerushalmi chapter 2 halacha 2.

(ג) Outside the house. Terumat HaDeshen; Magen Avraham; Magen Avraham in siman 449 seif 5; and resolved Taz's difficulty; and see end.

(ד) Even though, etc. There; and he brings proof from what is written in Gittin 22: Rami bar Chama asked, etc., learn: even he, etc., rather, etc.; and see Rashi s.v. rather, etc.

(ה) As long as he is. The addition and the impressions there:

(ו) Without, etc. Rambam; and as explained on the Tosefta: provided, etc.

(ז) Or that he sell, etc. As Maharam; and Agudah in Tosefta: until you acquire for two hundred take for one hundred and I will buy from you after Pesach — meaning even if he tells him to acquire expensively and give him profit, it is permitted since he sells without evasion (Bach).

(ח) But a gift. As the Gemara explains in that Tosefta: provided, etc.; and even though elsewhere it works because of chametz stringency (Hagahot Maimoniyot).
