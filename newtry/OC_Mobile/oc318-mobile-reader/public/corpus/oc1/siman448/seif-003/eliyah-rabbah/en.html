[3] A gift of chametz, etc. The same applies if a non-Jew's chametz was lost by a Jew (Chok Yaakov). Rokeach, siman 297, wrote: on Pesach one cannot accept a chametz deposit at all. This is his language. It implies even if he specified that he accepts it without responsibility and designated a place, which is permitted before Pesach, as written in siman 440. But from Responsa Rema, siman 177, this does not appear so, at first glance.
