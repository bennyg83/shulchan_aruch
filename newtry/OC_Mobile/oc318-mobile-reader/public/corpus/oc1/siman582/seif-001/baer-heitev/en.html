And it appears to me that here it does not help to say it ninety times as in siman 114, se'if 8; for here one is not permitted to say "Blessed are You, Hashem," for that would be a blessing in vain, and if he says it without the Name, then in prayer when he says it with the Name he will revert to his habit of saying "Blessed are You, Hashem, the holy God"; therefore he has no remedy. Magen Avraham.
