In the Ten Days of Repentance, etc. And it is good to recite during the Ten Days of Repentance, between Yishtabach and Yotzer Or, the psalm "From the depths I called You, Hashem"; for "depth" (omek) in gematria is 216, the numerical value of gevurah, and the numerical value of the harsh wrath that is the attribute of Esav; and each day one depth among the ten depths mentioned in Sefer Yetzirah is aroused, and one must intend one each day, for all of them are in the secret of 216 (Lechem Min HaShamayim). And there are pious ones who refrain from marital relations on the two nights of Rosh Hashana so that their immersion should count for them until the morrow; and one who needs it should immerse again in the morning so that he may pray in purity at the time of judgment (Sefer Seder HaYom). From his words we learn that if it is an immersion of a mitzvah, he does not refrain from it, and specifically when it was not possible to immerse earlier; and possibly in this manner it was said, "And on Rosh Hashana Sarah, Rachel, and Chana were remembered," and it is readily understood.
