The Order of Prayers for the Ten Days of Repentance and Rosh HaShana. And in it is 9 paragraphs. In the Ten Days of Repentance say The Holy King and The King of Judgment. If one made a mistake or even if one is uncertain, (the rule is as follows:) If it is with "HaMelech HaKadosh" he should go back to the beginning. If it is HaMelech HaMishpat if prior to uprooting his feet, he should go back to the Birchas Hasheveinu and say from there and on according to the order. If he did not remember until he uprooted his feet () he should got back to the beginning (i.e. repeat). [.] {Rama: see before the end of Siman 117 Also see Siman 118}
