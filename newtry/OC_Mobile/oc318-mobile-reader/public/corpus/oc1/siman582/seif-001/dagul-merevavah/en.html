That the Mechaber did not mention "with love" (be'ahava) is because he follows his own view that also regarding the other festivals (in siman 487) he did not mention "with love," and he goes according to the wording of the Rambam, who does not mention "with love" until after he has explained the festival as "such and such a day" etc., "a time" etc., "with love," as is clear in the Rambam's order of the prayer. And with this you will see that the author of Eliyah Rabbah was not precise, and wanted to learn from the words of the Shulchan Aruch that we do not say "And You gave us with love"; and this is not so; rather, only when it falls on Shabbos we do not say "zikkaron teruah" with "be'ahava."
