Zikkaron teruah. And we do not say "with love," for teruah indicates judgment, and there is no love in judgment; so wrote the Levush. He further wrote that one should not say "melokh al kol ha'olam kulo," for that is a doubling; yet in all the books it is so; and it appears to me to resolve this that we find many times that the majority is like the whole, and here it teaches us "entirely" in the full sense; and there is a proof for this above.
