If he did not remember until he moved his feet, etc. See above, siman 117 and siman 422, where it is explained that this applies specifically when one is accustomed to recite supplications after the prayer. If he is not accustomed to do so, once he has completed his prayer, even if he has not yet moved his feet, it is considered as though he moved them. According to our custom to recite "My God, guard my tongue," etc., after the prayer, followed by "May the words of my mouth," etc., the prayer is not considered finished until he recites the second "May the words of my mouth," etc., which precedes "He Who makes peace." However, once he has completed the second "May the words of my mouth," etc., even if he has not yet said "He Who makes peace in His heights," the prayer is considered finished, as written above, siman 117, os 40, see there.
