[ז] They must stand erect, etc. Meaning when one reaches "the King who loves righteousness" one stands erect; although in Avot one bows at the end of the blessing, nevertheless so it appears he returns and bows because of obligation. At the beginning of the blessing "You are mighty" one remains erect, for one does not bow at the beginning of a blessing; afterward one bows until reaching "Blessed," and remains erect until after beginning "You are holy" — from this learn for other blessings. Shelah wrote that even when standing erect one should not be in full upright posture but with bowed head and slight inclination, and this is not included in prostration; even when the head is bowed, throughout the year it is fitting to act so, all the more on the Days of Awe.
