9) [Seif 2] If he said "the holy God" and remembered within the time required for a brief utterance, etc. This means that from the time he said "the holy God" until he said "the holy King," he did not pause longer than the time required for a brief utterance. Regarding the measure of that time, see above, siman 206, os 15. If he paused longer than that measure, it no longer helps to say "the holy King," and he must return to the beginning. Mateh Yehudah. However, see above, siman 206, os 16; siman 487, os 12; and this siman, end of os 6. Therefore, it appears that after the fact, even if he paused longer than the time required for a brief utterance, he may say "the holy King" as long as he has not yet begun the following blessing, because of the rule concerning doubtful blessings. But if he did not remember until he began the following blessing, even if he said only one word, he must return to the beginning, as written in the following os.
