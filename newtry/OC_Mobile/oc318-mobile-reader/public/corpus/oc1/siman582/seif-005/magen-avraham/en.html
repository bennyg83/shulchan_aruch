There is no return. He said, “We are not allowed to return to the Lord’s Prayer, and we will not be able to live in our hearts, and we will not be able to do so with Hashem’s mercy, and we will not be able to live in the world.” And Hashem’s people, who believe in Hashem’s Word, and Hashem’s Word is not Hashem’s Word, and Hashem’s Word is not Hashem’s Word, and Hashem’s Word is in every way
