They do not make him return. And even if he wishes to return, it is forbidden, in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 144; and see siman 107 (Darkei Moshe). Zochreinu and Kosveinu with a chataf-kamatz. In Zochreinu one should not say "lechaim tovim," only "lechaim," until "and write for good life," all etc. (Levush); but some say that also in "and write" we do not say "tovim" until "in the book" (Mateh Moshe). "Lechaim": the lamed with a sheva and not with a patach, so that it not be heard as "not life"; and specifically on Rosh Hashana we are particular, but throughout the year we say in Hashkiveinu "lechaim" with a patach under the lamed according to grammar, for when the sheva comes at the beginning of the word with letters that are not alef-hei-ches-ayin, they read it with a patach (Bach; Mateh Moshe); and see what I wrote in siman 8. "Shehasultan" with a kamatz under the second shin. And one should not say "be'ahava mikra kodesh," but rather "yom teruah mikra kodesh." And "udevarecha emes," and one should not say "malkenu." And they double "le'eila" in all the Kaddishes that they say. (All this in the Minhagim, Levush, and Bach.)
