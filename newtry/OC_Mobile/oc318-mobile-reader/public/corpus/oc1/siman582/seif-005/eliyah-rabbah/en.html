[5] And if he is in "HaMelekh HaMishpat," etc., he repeats, etc. And so he wrote in siman 118, not like the Rama who ruled that one does not repeat if he said "Melekh ohev," etc.; and the Taz decided that one should pray a second time by way of a voluntary offering without adding something new; but Magen Avraham there agreed with the Rama; and so ruled Hagahos Maimoniyos, Mateh Moshe, and the Bach; and so concludes Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah at length. And one must be careful to say "HaMelekh HaMishpat," and not "Melekh HaMishpat," for some say he has not fulfilled; and the reason is that he wished to say that He is the King and He is the judgment, as Beis Yosef wrote; and in Kol Bo, Hilchos Tefillah, it is explained further; and it escaped the notice of Kenesses HaGedolah.
