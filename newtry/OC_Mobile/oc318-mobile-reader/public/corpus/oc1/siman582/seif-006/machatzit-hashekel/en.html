(s.k. 6) Yom teruah. Even in Kiddush at night; for in any case the day is a day of teruah (Beis Yosef). And the Peri Chadash wrote: if it falls on Shabbos and he said "yom teruah," or it falls on a weekday and he said "zikkaron teruah," he has fulfilled; and see there the reason.
On Shabbos they say "Our God and God of our fathers, be pleased," etc. But on Yom Kippur one does not say "Our God and God of our fathers, be pleased," etc., as it should read; and so it is in the Levush. And he wrote the reason: because it is a day of affliction and is not complete rest.
