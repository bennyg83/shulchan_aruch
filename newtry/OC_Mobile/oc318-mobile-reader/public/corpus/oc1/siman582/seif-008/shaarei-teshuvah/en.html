And see Machatzis HaShekel: according to the reason of the Zohar and the Ari, of blessed memory, who forbade it on a weekday, all the more so one must be careful on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur, when judgment sits; see there; and see what I wrote in Yad Ephraim above, siman 101, and there I wrote that if he cannot intend in any other manner it is permitted; and see Shaarei Chochmah who wrote in the name of Sefer Beis David, simanim 371 and 378, that the custom of the congregation to say the entire repetition of the Amidah and Musaf with the chazzan, even though it is explained in siman 124 that the congregation must be silent, etc., one may say that the prohibition is only throughout the year, but here, since they do not do so every day and now they do so, we have no concern; but as for those who raise their voice louder than the chazzan, that is certainly improper, and it is fitting to rebuke them; see there. And in our country I have not seen this custom; rather the congregation is silent and listens to the words of the chazzan; but in the verses of Malchuyos, Zichronos, and Shofaros some say them with the chazzan in a whisper, and even though he also mentions the holy Names, since they are verses of Tanach it is like one reading in the Torah; and so wrote the Taz, siman 621, s.k. 3; and see Shaarei Teshuvah above, siman 215; and so wrote R' Yosef regarding saying Ochilah that the congregation says with the chazzan: it is permitted, and not like the Kitzur Shelah; see there. But one should say it in a whisper so as not to distract the chazzan from his prayer; and he should also be careful if he wishes to say with the chazzan also from the rest of the prayer wording in order to focus his heart with arousal of prayer and supplications: nevertheless he should not mention any Divine Name; and when the chazzan begins to say "Our God and God of our fathers, reign," etc., he should begin from the word "reign," and also in the rest of the wording he should skip when he comes to mentioning a Name; and it is possible that even this he may say by way of request, as they say in Shacharis before the passage of the Binding by way of prayer and supplications; and nevertheless he should at least be careful not to conclude the blessing; and also one should not do so except when he senses in himself that thereby his heart will be aroused to intend toward his Father in heaven.
