Se'if 8. He does not say "moadim lesimcha, chagim uzmanim lesason," etc. Note: see Magen Avraham s.k. 7. And behold, in the haftarah the wording is, "that You gave us, Hashem our God, for holiness and for rest, for honor and for glory," etc.; and in my eyes it is astonishing how it is fitting here to say "for holiness and for rest," since melachah of food preparation is permitted as on other Yamim Tovim; and why on other Yamim Tovim we do not say "for holiness and for rest," and here on Rosh Hashana we do say it. And if I had the strength I would emend the wording thus: "that You gave us, Hashem our God, this Day of Remembrance, for remembrance, for honor and for glory"; and that is because this day was given for remembrance, for which reason it is called the Day of Remembrance, as Chazal said, "so that your remembrance should ascend before Me for good," and as the paytan wrote on Rosh Hashana in Shacharis of the first day, "and it is made a statute of remembrance for the first day and for the last day," and likewise in the Zichronos of Musaf prayer; see there. If so, it is explicit that this day was given for remembrance; therefore it is fitting to say, "that You gave us, Hashem our God, this Day of Remembrance, for remembrance, for honor and for glory"; and examine this well.
