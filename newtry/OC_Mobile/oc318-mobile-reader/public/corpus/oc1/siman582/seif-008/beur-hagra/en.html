In the Midrash, and the Mordechai brought it at the beginning of Rosh Hashana: "Forever, Hashem, Your word stands in the heavens": fulfill Your word; just as You settled with Adam, just as You judged him with mercy, so You will have mercy on the generations that come after him forever. Was it not so that You decreed upon Adam, "for on the day you eat of it you shall surely die"? He said to Him: perhaps I explained "day" as a day that You have of Mine; I said, today I give him nine hundred thirty years and leave for his descendants seventy years. Therefore David said: Master of the worlds, had You not judged Adam HaRishon with mercy at the hour that he ate from the tree, he would not have lived even one hour; and You settled with him with mercy; so You settled with him that You judge his children with mercy.
