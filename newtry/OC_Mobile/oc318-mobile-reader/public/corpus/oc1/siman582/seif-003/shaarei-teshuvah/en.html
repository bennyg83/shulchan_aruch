In the Shulchan Aruch: on Shabbos in between. And Machatzis HaShekel wrote that if in the blessing me'ein sheva he erred and said "ha'El HaKadosh," he wrote in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah that he returns, and the Peri Chadash disputes and holds that he need not return; and so wrote in Gan HaMelech, siman 149; and in Mateh Yehudah he wrote like the Peri Chadash; and the view of Machatzis HaShekel in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah is that he returns; and he wrote that according to the words of the holy Ari, of blessed memory, it is an obligation, and also our Sages of blessed memory intended a hidden matter, only that they gave a reason according to the plain sense and hid it by hint; see there.
