(11) If Rosh Hashana falls on Shabbos. That is to say, he should also say in the blessing me'ein sheva of Friday night "HaMelekh HaKadosh."
(12) And likewise when it falls, etc. That is to say, also that he does not conclude with that of Yom Kippur but with that of Shabbos; and like every Yom Tov that falls on Shabbos, where he does not mention Yom Tov in it; and as above in siman 268, se'if 9; see there.
