Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. Beit Yosef writes an even stronger ruling: even in the case there, repeating ninety times from “You are mighty” through “Who brings down dew” is ineffective, because during prayer he says “You are mighty” after the blessing of Magen Avraham, and he has not yet become accustomed to that sequence. See there. Although we do not rule that way there, here, where there is a change during the prayer within that same blessing itself, all agree that he has not become accustomed.
