Zikkaron teruah; see Taz; see Magen Avraham 4 and what is written there; and what he wrote "and there is a proof for this above" possibly refers to what is written above, "vechol ha-risha kulah"; see there. See Peri Chadash: if one said on a weekday "zikkaron teruah," they do not make him return; and the same applies on Shabbos if he said "yom teruah," that they do not make him return; see there. However, anyone who concluded properly with "and the Day of Remembrance," even if he did not mention it in the middle, has fulfilled; he spoke only regarding whether they make him return in the middle; see there.
