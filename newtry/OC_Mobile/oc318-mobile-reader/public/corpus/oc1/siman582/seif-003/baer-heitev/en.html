To stand erect. Before he says "Blessed," and in Zochreinu he must stand erect a few words before those adjacent to "Blessed," so that he can bow afterward when he says "Blessed are You"; but for the erect stance of "Mi kamokha," he need not stand erect until he says "Blessed"; and likewise regarding all blessings that have no bowing at their end (Taz; see there). And when he stands erect he should not be fully upright, and it is not included in prostration when the head is bowed; for even throughout the year it is proper to conduct oneself thus, all the more so on the Days of Awe (Shelah). And every person should review and learn the prayer and the piyutim so that they are fluent in his mouth on Rosh Hashana, and likewise he should teach his children the order of the prayer so that he need not interrupt. Mahariv.
