To stand erect. Before saying Blessed and in zochreinu must stand erect a few words near Blessed to bow afterward when saying Blessed are You; for 'like You' no erect until Blessed; same blessings without bowing at end — Taz see. When standing not fully upright, not full prostration head bowed — proper all year especially Days of Awe (Shelah). Each should relearn prayer and Kaddish fluent for Rosh Hashanah; teach children order so no interruption — Mahariv.
