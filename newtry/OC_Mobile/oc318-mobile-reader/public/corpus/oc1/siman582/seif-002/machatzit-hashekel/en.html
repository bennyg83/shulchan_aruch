(s.k. 2) He need not, etc. As written in siman 487: that if he erred in the Yom Tov prayer and concluded "Who sanctifies the Shabbos," and remembered within toch kedei dibbur and concluded "Who sanctifies Israel and the times," he has fulfilled. And specifically when he knew that it was Yom Tov when he said "Who sanctifies the Shabbos," only that he stumbled in his speech. And likewise in siman 209: if he had a cup of beer in his hand and said the blessing of the fruit of the vine, and within toch kedei dibbur he concluded "shehakol," something created by His word, he has fulfilled. Beis Yosef also wrote specifically that he knew when he said the blessing of the fruit of the vine that it was beer; but if he did not know, he has not fulfilled, for everything follows the intention: if the intention was not according to law, what he concluded within toch kedei dibbur according to law does not help. If so, the same applies here. However, we hold, etc., as Magen Avraham wrote there. Nevertheless, upon Beis Yosef it is difficult: since he holds that we follow the intention, he should have written here, "and that is when he knew," etc.
