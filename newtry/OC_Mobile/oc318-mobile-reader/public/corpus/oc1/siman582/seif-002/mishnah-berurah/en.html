(7) And within toch kedei dibbur, etc. That is, that he did not pause after finishing "ha'El HaKadosh" more than the measure of three words; and if he paused more than this measure, his returning does not help; and all this is when he has not yet begun another blessing; for otherwise, even within toch kedei dibbur he returns to the beginning.
(8) He need not return. That is, even if he did not remember that these are the Days of Repentance, and at the time he said "Blessed are You, Hashem" it was also in his mind to conclude "ha'El HaKadosh," even so, since in the end he remembered and said "HaMelekh HaKadosh," it helps.
(9) And the same law, etc. He follows his view in the previous se'if; and according to what the Rama ruled in siman 118, in every case he need not return, unless he said the first time "ha'El ohev tzedakah u'mishpat" and did not mention "melekh."
