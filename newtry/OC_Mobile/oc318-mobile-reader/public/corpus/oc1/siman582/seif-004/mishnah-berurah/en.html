(13) With bowing. Because we depend on judgment, therefore he should intend to pray with dread and awe. And from this wording it implies like the other bowing mentioned in Scripture regarding Daniel, that he was kneeling on his knees; and likewise regarding Shlomo it is written, "he rose from kneeling on his knees," etc.; but people do not have that custom; rather he should pray with bowing of his head together with his body only [later authorities].
(14) To stand erect at the end. Meaning, before he says "Blessed are You"; and the same applies at the beginning of the blessing; and the reason is not to add upon the enactment of the Sages, who enacted only at the beginning of Avos and Hoda'ah and at their end; and see above in siman 113 and in Mishnah Berurah there.
