Nevertheless here regarding Zochreinu it is possible that he is not permitted; for Terumas HaDeshen wrote that even according to the poskim that wherever they said they do not make him return, nevertheless before he uprooted his feet, if he wishes to return he is permitted; nevertheless regarding Zochreinu and Mi kamokha they agree that he is not permitted, since it was not mentioned at all in the Gemara to say Zochreinu and Mi kamokha; if so, the same applies also regarding a voluntary prayer, one may say that it is worse.
Until "and write for life," etc. But some say, etc., as it is in the Midrash regarding David, peace be upon him.
For such is the way of those who beg at the doorways: first they ask for a little and afterward they add to ask. Therefore they first ask for remembrance and afterward for writing, and afterward they add "tovim." "Lechaim" with a sheva, etc., not "not life," as it is in the first chapter of Nedarim, that a patach under the lamed means "not"; and on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur, which are days of judgment, one must clarify the wording of his prayer more.
According to grammar, for it should have been "la-chaim." With a he after the lamed, and the intent would be "to the known life," namely good life; and since the he of knowledge is missing, they place the patach that was fitting to be pointed with the he under the lamed, as Rashi wrote on parashas Beshalach on the verse "lenachosam haderech"; see there. But on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur it is implied that also in Hashkiveinu he should say "lechaim" with a sheva under the lamed.
And see what I wrote in siman 8, s.k. 7, regarding the blessings for wrapping oneself in tzitzis.
"Shehasultan" with a kamatz, etc. For with a chirik it implies that man rules by the hand of Hashem; but with a kamatz the intent is that the sultanate and the dominion are in His blessed hand; so wrote the later authorities.
And one should not say "with love," etc. But rather "zikkaron teruah mikra," etc., as it should read; for teruah indicates judgment, and there is no love in judgment. Taz in the name of Levush.
And one should not say "malkenu," etc. For this wording was established corresponding to the verse "Forever, Hashem, Your word stands in the heavens." And see Beis Yosef; and there are two explanations of this. (1) According to what we hold, that the world was created in Tishrei, and Adam on the day he was created sinned, namely on Rosh Hashana; and the Holy One, blessed be He, said to him: You are a sign for your children: just as you entered before Me in judgment and went out on this day with mercy, so your children will enter before Me in judgment on this day and go out with mercy. And according to this we entreat God, "and Your word is truth"; therefore let Your word be true to have mercy on us. (2) Because Hashem promised us many times through His prophets to gather our dispersed; and according to the one who says that they were redeemed in Nisan and in Tishrei they are destined to be redeemed; therefore we ask that Your word be fulfilled, for Your word is truth, and gather us speedily. And because in the verse the wording of kingship was not mentioned, one should also take in the prayer the language of the verse, not to mention "malkenu."
And they double "le'eila," etc. The Levush wrote the reason: because on these days all His creatures pass before Him like sheep of a counting; all of them look to His kindness; they are precious to exalt Him and praise Him for His kindnesses; and He is elevated and exalted from the mouth of His creatures double and redoubled more than all year.
