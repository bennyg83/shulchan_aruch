Refa'einu — and it is the eighth blessing, because the word was given with a chet and the word needs healing; therefore it was established as the eighth. And one should not say "Heal us, Heaven our Heaven," but rather "Heal us, Heaven, and we shall be healed." And one must say "rofeh cholei" with a tzerei under the tzadi instead of kamatz under the lamed (thus too Mahar'r Shabtai of Przemysl in his siddur).
