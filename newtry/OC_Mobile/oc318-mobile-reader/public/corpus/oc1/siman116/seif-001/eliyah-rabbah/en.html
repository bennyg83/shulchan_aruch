[1] Refa'einu, etc. Rashal wrote that one should not say "Heal us, Heaven our Heaven," but rather "Heal us, Heaven, and we shall be healed." And Ma'adanei Melekh wrote that this is proven from Tur and Kol Bo, who wrote that there are twenty-seven words in them — and this follows the version "Heal us, Heaven, and we shall be healed," etc. — until here. And per my humble opinion there is no proof from this; for one could say that he holds that they do not say the word "melekh" in "for You are King, Healer," but rather "for You are Healer," and so I saw the version in Rambam and Avudraham; and if so there are twenty-seven words even if they say "Heal us, Heaven our Heaven," and so in Rambam there the word "Eloheinu" appears. Also it seems to me there is clear proof from Avudraham's words that I brought at the end of siman 114, that they do not say "melekh" in the closing [of blessings] except in four blessings. He wrote in the siddur of Beit Shmuel that one must say "rofeh cholei" with tzerei under the lamed — see there. When a person wishes to pray for any sick person he should say the prayer "Heal please So-and-so son of So-and-so, a complete healing among the rest of the sick of His people Israel"; and one praying before him need not mention his name.
