And we shall be healed. See Baer Hetev. And see Eliyah Rabba who wrote cholei with tzerei under the lamed; and see in Ne'ot Yaakov that it is possible to read [it as] "a sick one" and "raises a healing cure for all ailments of our soul, spirit, and life" — for in Megillah it is proven that this blessing was established for healing bodily ailments; and in Be'er Yitzchak he wrote that several great ones of Israel whisper on their lips to read it thus, and reconciles the words of the Gemara — see there. And in responsa of Ri Malko he wrote that if one must pray for a sick person, it is preferable in repentance, meaning explicit prayer, and in Refa'einu in thought alone; and Be'er Yitzchak wrote concerning this that he heard from elderly rabbis who would pray in the blessing Refa'einu, and so I practice — end [of his words].
