(1) (a) Rafano D. - And he will not say Rafano D. is our God and He is healed because Lishna Dekra is like that Rafani D. and Arpa. It should be said that I am sick in the middle of the day. 11. He should not say, Please see in our case, only see in our case, but the MA and AR and the rest of the greats wrote that one should not deviate from the custom that says, Please see in our case:

(2) One may not adapt it, etc. — and the same applies to other changes, such as from "speaking on his behalf" to "speaking in his presence."

(3) As prayer, etc. — and one may pray for the sick in Refa'einu and say: "Heal please So-and-so son of So-and-so, a complete healing among the rest of the sick of Israel"; and this is when [the sick person] is not before him, but when before him, one need not mention his name.

(4) It is forbidden, etc. — meaning even if they say it as prayer; and when one does not say it as prayer, it is forbidden even for a single verse per Rama's gloss.
