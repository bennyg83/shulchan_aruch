1) [Seif 1] Heal us, Hashem, and we shall be healed, etc. One should not say 'Heal us, Hashem our God, and we shall be healed,' but rather 'Heal us, Hashem, and we shall be healed,' for it is a verse in Yirmeyah, 'Heal me, Hashem, and I shall be healed,' etc., except that they changed it from singular to plural. Maharshal in his responsa, siman 64, and Knesses HaGedolah cited it in the Hagahos HaTur; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Ateres Zekeinim, ot 1; Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 128b. So too it is written in Shaar HaKavanos, the sixth discourse on the Amidah, and it states that their initial letters spell a hidden Name, giving a reason based on the concealed [teachings]; see there. So too wrote the Yafeh LaLev, ot 1; see there. And one should say 'rofei cholei' with a tzeirei under the lamed. Ateres Zekeinim there; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1; Shalmei Tzibbur there; Shaarei Teshuvah, ot 1. And one should not say 'ki Kel Melech rofei,' but rather 'ki Kel rofei.' Eliyah Rabbah there. So too wrote the Shalmei Tzibbur there.
