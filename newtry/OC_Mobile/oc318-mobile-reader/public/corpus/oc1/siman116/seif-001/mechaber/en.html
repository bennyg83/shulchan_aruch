Commentary on the blessing Refa'einu. Containing one seif: Heal us, Heaven, and
we shall be' healed — even though the verse is in the singular, one may not adapt it to the plural; this is when one intends to read [it]. But when one says it as prayer and request, it is permitted.Nevertheless, if one recites a complete psalm, it is forbidden to change from singular to plural or vice versa. (Tur and Rosh, chapter HaKoreh Omed, in the name of Ri)]:
