The wording of the blessing Refa’einu. This siman contains one seif.
One says, “Heal us, Hashem, and we shall be healed.” Although the verse is written in the singular, one may not alter it to the plural when reciting it as a verse. When, however, one says it as a prayer and supplication, this is permitted. Nevertheless, when reciting an entire chapter of Tehillim, one may not change singular wording to plural or plural wording to singular. This is cited by the Tur and the Rosh in the chapter HaKoreh Omed in the name of Ri.
