1) [Seif 1] Heal us, Heaven, and we shall be healed, etc. And one should not say "Heal us, Heaven our Heaven, and we shall be healed," but rather "Heal us, Heaven, and we shall be healed," for it is a verse in Jeremiah: "Heal us, Heaven, and I shall be healed," etc., only that they changed from singular to plural — Rashal in responsa siman 64, and they brought it in Knesset HaGedolah in the hagahot of Taz, Magen Avraham seif katan 1; Eliyah Rabba letter 1, Shulchan Aruch section 288 page 2 side b; and likewise in Sha'ar HaKavanot derush 6 of Amidah, and he wrote that their acronym is ri"v and gave a reason in the secret of Yaakov; and likewise Pri Megadim letter 1 Yaakov. And one must say "rofeh cholei" with tzerei under the lamed — Eliyah Rabba there; Eliyah Rabbah letter 1 Shulchan Aruch there; Sha'arei Teshuvah letter 1; and one should not say "for You are King, Healer," but rather "for You are Healer," Eliyah Rabbah there. And likewise Shulchan Aruch there.
