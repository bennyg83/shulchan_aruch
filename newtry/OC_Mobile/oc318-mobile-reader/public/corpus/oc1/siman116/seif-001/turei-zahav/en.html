As prayer and request. Thus too Tur in the name of Rama; afterward Rabbeinu Yonah wrote specifically when reading the entire psalm complete, etc. — as Rama copied. And it seems to me there is a difference between these two answers regarding the verse they say in Havdalah: "My strength and song is Heaven, and He has been for me salvation" — if one wishes to change and say "and let Him be for us salvation" — according to Rama it is forbidden, since this verse is not language of request, for it is past tense as implied by the word "vayehi"; and according to Rabbeinu Yonah it is permitted, since it is only one verse. And from Rama's words it implies there is no dispute here, and so I wrote in my humble opinion.
