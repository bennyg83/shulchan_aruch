A practical difference concerns the verse recited at Havdalah, “Hashem is my strength and song, and He has become my salvation.” If one changes it to “May He become our salvation,” according to Ramah it remains forbidden when used merely as a scriptural quotation, because the original verse is not formulated as a request. According to Rabbeinu Yonah, a single verse is permitted. The proper course is to recite verses in the established liturgical manner, as prayer and supplication, and not to introduce unnecessary alterations.
