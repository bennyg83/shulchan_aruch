In the manner of. See Taz. According to his explanation, each opinion contains both a leniency and a stringency. According to Ramah, when one recites a verse merely as Scripture, even a single verse is forbidden, whereas an entire passage is permitted when recited as prayer. According to Rabbeinu Yonah, a complete passage is forbidden even as prayer, but a single verse is permitted even when not stated as prayer. Rama did not introduce the second view with “some say” because the matter is not explicit. Eliyah Rabbah rules that one should observe the stringencies of both. Levush, however, implies that Rabbeinu Yonah adds only a stringency and otherwise agrees with Ramah. Accordingly, we follow Rabbeinu Yonah as presented in Rama’s gloss. The blessings Hashiveinu and Selach Lanu present no difficulty, because they do not quote the verse verbatim. A change of wording is permitted, as Bach explains; therefore even an entire passage is permitted when its language is altered.
