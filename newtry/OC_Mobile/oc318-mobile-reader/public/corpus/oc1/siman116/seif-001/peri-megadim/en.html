Path. Eliyah Rabba — behold, according to what Rama wrote, each has a leniency and stringency relative to Rama: as a psalm — even one verse is forbidden; as prayer — a complete psalm is permitted. And according to Rabbeinu Yonah, a complete psalm is forbidden in prayer, and the leniency is one verse is permitted even not as prayer. And what Rama did not write — some say since it is not in Be'ur [HaGra] and Eliyah Rabbah, one should adopt the stringency of both; but Levush implies Rabbeinu Yonah only took a stringency and concedes to Rama; and if so, we rule like Rabbeinu Yonah and Rama in the gloss. And likewise the blessings Hashiveinu and Selach lanu — for in my humble opinion, when one does not say the exact language of the verse, it is permitted to change even regarding a complete psalm when one changes it.
