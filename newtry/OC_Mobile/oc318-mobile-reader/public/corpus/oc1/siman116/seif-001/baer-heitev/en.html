And we shall be healed. And one should not say "Heal us, Heaven our Heaven, and we shall be healed"; also one should not say "See now our affliction," but only "See our affliction" — thus Rashal and Knesset HaGedolah and Pri Chadash. But Magen Avraham wrote that one should not deviate from the custom that they say "See now our affliction," and so it is in Magaleh Amukos.
