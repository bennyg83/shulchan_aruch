Seif 1 One may not adapt it. And the same applies to other changes, such as from "He speaks from behind him" to "before him"; and therefore Rosh wrote in Berachot 17: "Your mouth shall speak wisdom" in the language of a blessing — for they would say thus so that it would not be difficult how he changed from the verse in Tehillim 48: "Your mouth speaks wisdom." Therefore he explains that he did not intend to read but only to bless. Tzelach there.
