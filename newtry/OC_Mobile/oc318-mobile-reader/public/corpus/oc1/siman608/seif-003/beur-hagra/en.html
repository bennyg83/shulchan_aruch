Se'if 3. If he stopped, etc. As written at the end of chapter 3 of Eruvin: since he said the time blessing, he accepted it upon himself, etc.; this implies that before then it is permitted. And as written in Eichah Rabbasi, brought by Tosafos there, comment beginning "if," and the Rosh in Ta'anis, chapter 1, siman 14.
And see above siman, etc. But in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 3 of the Laws of Fasts, he wrote that it is acceptance, and so the Shulchan Aruch ruled in siman 562, se'if 6, regarding acceptance of a fast. See what is written there.
