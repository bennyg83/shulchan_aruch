That he did not accept. If he accepted upon himself before plag ha-minchah, his acceptance is nothing (Rabbenu Yerucham and Beis Yosef, siman 553, in the name of Ramban). However, if he explicitly accepted upon himself not to eat anymore, it is forbidden; see siman 562, se'if 11. The Maharshal wrote: the people have already become accustomed that when they stop eating, they accept the fast, and it is as if they accepted it verbally; this is an extra stringency, until here. Maharil wrote that it is forbidden to eat afterward, and one should not be lenient (Bach).
