There And so it implies from the incident of the tailor, and the reason, etc. From the incident the Tur brought in siman 603, that he bought a fish, there is no proof at all, for where is it mentioned that fish are among things that increase seed? The Levush explicitly wrote to eat fish. In the Rambam's commentary to the Mishnah on Yoma, he wrote that they would not feed him fish, but this is not found in the Gemara. However, in my humble opinion there is proof from Eichah Rabbah, which the Ran and other authorities brought in the incident of Rabbi Yehoshua ben Beseira, who drank wine in the meal with which one stops eating on the eve of Yom Kippur; see their words in tractate Ta'anis. According to some authorities, this was at least the morning meal. It is necessarily true that at the morning meal it is permitted, for old wine is mentioned in the Talmud, and certainly there they did not give him new wine from the vat to drink.
