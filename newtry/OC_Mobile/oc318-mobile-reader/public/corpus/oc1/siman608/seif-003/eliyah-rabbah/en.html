[3] [Levush] Although, etc. This gloss escaped the Taz; see there. Also what he asked about deriving from "in plowing and harvesting," that if so the same would apply to Shabbos and Yom Tov, while the Tur holds that there is no addition on Shabbos, until here are his words. In my humble opinion this is not difficult at all, for the Beis Yosef wrote in siman 261 that the Rif and Rosh disagree with the Tur and hold that there is addition on Shabbos and Yom Tov, and so ruled the Shulchan Aruch and Levush there. I also wrote above that perhaps even the Tur agrees with them.
