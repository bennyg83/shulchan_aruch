Before twilight. It is written in the Tur that from Scripture it appears to him that one must add from weekday onto the sacred, both regarding the prohibition of eating and regarding the prohibition of doing labor. It appears that he intends the verse from which they learn in the last chapter of Yoma that there is a gezeirah shavah of "etzem, etzem," meaning that they learn that there is no punishment and warning for the addition from weekday through the gezeirah shavah, that specifically "on the day itself" there is punishment and warning, but not for the addition. Consequently we hear that in any event there is an obligation to add from weekday onto the sacred on Yom Kippur. So too it is stated there in the Gemara in the chapter Yom ha-Kippurim, that the one who learns from "etzem, etzem" does not learn the addition from weekday from the verse "and you shall afflict yourselves on the ninth in the evening," but that verse of "and you shall afflict" comes for the obligation to eat on the eve of Yom Kippur, as written at the beginning of the laws of Yom Kippur. If so, since the Tur took the verse of "and you shall afflict" for the obligation of eating, as above, he necessarily learns the addition from weekday from "etzem, etzem." Therefore he did not mention adding from weekday onto the sacred regarding Shabbos in siman 261, nor regarding Yom Tov, as the Beis Yosef wrote in siman 261, that the Tur holds there is an addition from weekday only regarding Yom Kippur. But in the Levush it is written that he learns here from the verse "and you shall afflict, in the evening." This is difficult, for if so, there would not be an obligation of eating from this verse, since the one who expounds one does not expound the other, as explained there in the chapter Yom ha-Kippurim. He further wrote in the Levush that we learn from "in plowing and harvesting you shall rest," and through this there is a difficulty, for according to the one who learns from this, he holds that the same applies to every Shabbos and Yom Tov, that addition is required, as stated in the chapter Yom ha-Kippurim. If so, why did our teacher the Tur write that specifically on Yom Kippur there is addition, while regarding Shabbos and Yom Tov he wrote nothing in siman 261? Rather, it is as I explained.
