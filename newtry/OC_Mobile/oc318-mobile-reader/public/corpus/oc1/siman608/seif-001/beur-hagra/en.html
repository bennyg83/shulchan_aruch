Se'if 1. One eats and stops, etc. Kesubos 63a; see Rashi there, comment beginning "not, etc." And in chapter 4 of Pesachim: "and its twilight is forbidden." And in Yoma, chapter 8, 81b: the addition of affliction, etc. Necessarily this is before twilight.
And the addition, etc. The Gemara there. See Tosafos, Rosh Hashanah 9a, comment beginning "and Rabbi Akiva." Perhaps because, etc. But the Rosh in Berachos, chapter 4, siman 6, wrote that necessarily there is a measure, since we say that this addition of Yom Kippur is from the Torah, as written in se'if 3; and presumably they did not eat until literal night, for they could not be exact, etc.; see there.
