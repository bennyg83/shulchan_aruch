On the ninth of the month one might think, etc. Rif chapter Yom Kippur brings this baraita and concludes: one might be liable for additions; the Talmud must say on the essence of the day, on the middle of the day you are liable, etc., until here. One might ask: in the Gemara it challenges, and the tanna of essence essence — this on the ninth of the month what does it do? Rashi explained: since it was necessary to exclude additions from punishment, we learn they add; and it answers it is needed for all who eat on the ninth as if they fasted, etc. If so, how did Rif bring the derashah of "and you shall afflict" and also tanna of essence? I saw in Lechem Mishneh chapter 1 of laws of Shevitot of the fast — he expanded greatly and did not succeed in a correct path — see there. Therefore I examined to explain it with three difficulties we pose on it: first, why does the Gemara challenge — perhaps without derashah on the ninth of the month on the main prohibition of additions we would not learn from essence to exclude additions from punishment but for another derashah; second, perhaps in any case we need on the ninth of the month because of Shabbat and Yom Tov to understand "your resting" refers to additions; third, why did the tanna double essence essence though one is for affliction and one for melacha — in any case mentioning the tanna once suffices. Therefore it appears not like Rashi's explanation; rather I explain what the Gemara says there before this: warning for affliction — from where? Ravina said: this tanna learns from essence essence a gezeirah shavah with mufneh, etc. — see there at length. Therefore it appears the challenge on tanna essence essence means he learns a gezeirah shavah with mufneh and did not learn to exclude additions from punishment; perforce one must say because he has no other verse on the main law of the prohibition of additions; if so on the ninth what does it do? And for the first tanna who learns from essence to exclude additions from punishment, derashah on the ninth of the month for the addition is contrary to Rashi's explanation. If so, Rif who brings both is settled and correct — examine well.
