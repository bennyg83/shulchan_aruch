Women who eat and drink until dark and do not know it is a mitzva to add from chol onto kodesh — one does not rebuke them lest they come to do so intentionally.The same law for any prohibited matter — we say better they be inadvertent than intentional; specifically what is not explicit in Torah even if d'oraisa; but if explicit in Torah one rebukes him (Ran chapter 4 Beitzah and Rosh in name of Ittur).
