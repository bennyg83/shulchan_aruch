(Magen Avraham s.k. 2) Asmakhta with a non-Jew is not because kinyan by their law is effective, but since in their law they do not judge the law of asmakhta, he finalizes and acquires — so wrote Magen Avraham below siman 448 seif 4. In our matter I did not merit to understand: behold the foundation that asmakhta does not acquire — even though through the condition he should have acquired and he knows that if there will not be money this will acquire — nevertheless we say he thinks in his mind that certainly he will have money and it will not come to this that this will acquire, and he did not finalize and acquire; if so, what difference does it make that in their law they do not judge asmakhta — nevertheless they judge only that if he does not pay he will acquire, and he thinks in his mind he will pay and did not finalize and acquire; behold also in their law he should have acquired if he does not pay him, and nevertheless we say he did not finalize and acquire because he thinks clearly he will certainly pay — and what is the wonder in this that with a gentile they do not judge asmakhta, and if he does not pay this will acquire — nevertheless in his mind he will certainly pay and he did not finalize and acquire; requires study.
