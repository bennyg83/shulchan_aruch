(א) In his hand. Specifically, for we hold like Bach that from here and onward he collects — meaning we do not consider it retroactively as if it were the non-Jew's on Pesach.

(ב) Belonging to the non-Jew. And he said to him: since it is in his hand to redeem, he shall transgress on it, as written regarding challah: since he lacks money we do not say since. And Chayei Adam explained: how could he redeem it and it would be forbidden in benefit? And see Tosafot Pesachim 42b. And Taz raised: if his intent is to redeem after Pesach it is forbidden, for it is like hiding; and Magen Avraham challenged: is it not that when the pledge is compelled he must pay the loan value and it will be forbidden? And one can explain: in any case it is no worse than one who accepts liability for a non-Jew's chametz in a non-Jew's house — permitted, as written Choshen Mishpat siman 207.
