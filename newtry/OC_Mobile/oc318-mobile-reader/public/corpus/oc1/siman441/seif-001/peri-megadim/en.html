Taz: it appears to emend and thus read from now, for otherwise Bach in an unspecified case does not acquire retroactively per Rava, and therefore we require from now; and the words that it is not etc. have no explanation; and we also require leaving as pledge, for otherwise liability is on the Jew — Rosh Pesachim 41. And it is possible to reconcile the text from what implies in Pesachim for Abaye one need not leave as pledge — see there Maharsha and Gittin 40:1 in Rashi s.v. chametz and Penei Yehoshua there. And at first glance one would say for Abaye without leaving as pledge he collects retroactively, and a non-Jew acquires a pledge through money per R' Yochanan — see Tosafot Kiddushin 14b s.v. he'eil per Rashi's explanation; and for Rava he establishes per the halachah like R' Yochanan that a non-Jew acquires only through pulling — and with what will he acquire? Therefore specifically when left as pledge (and see Magen Avraham 1 in the name of Shach, and it will be explained there). And it is possible for Abaye without leaving as pledge, and even a non-Jew from a Jew acquires retroactively, for lien of debt is stronger than acquisition through money — know that money alone even with a Jew does not acquire by rabbinic law, and nevertheless if one consecrated, the lender cannot retract — and why, since by rabbinic law he did not acquire, is it not his, analogous to one who consecrated chametz on the sixth? And even if you say this — nevertheless it is difficult; rather we strengthen lien of debt more than acquisition through money; if so for Abaye money-kinyan is not required. And that which Taz wrote the Rosh meant Rava need not etc., and he transferred to him through kinyan — nevertheless forbidden because liability is on the Jew; and with a condition that there is no liability on him, and through kinyan it is permitted b'dieved even without leaving as pledge, as Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Shach — thus one can explain Taz's words. And such is Taz's language: the pledge must be in his hand, for we hold like Rava and from now a non-Jew does not acquire a pledge — and this teaches as we wrote that for Abaye one need not leave as pledge; and if a non-Jew acquires a pledge the borrower does not transgress, as Magen Avraham note 6 wrote, and it will be explained there; also in Taz Magen Avraham note 3 disagrees on this — see there. I again examined in Milchamot: he wrote for Abaye in the conclusion also leaving as pledge is required; and that which one consecrated and the lender sold — for Abaye consecration applies in real property that he acquired through document, not in movable property — see there he elaborated, and it will be explained further in Magen Avraham.
