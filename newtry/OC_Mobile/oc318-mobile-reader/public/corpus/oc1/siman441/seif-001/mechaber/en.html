דין מי שהלוה על חמץ. ובו ב סעיפים:
The law of one who lent on chametz. Contains 2 seifim. A non-Jew who lent a Jew on his chametz and pledged it in his (the non-Jew's) hand, and said to him: from now it shall be yours if I do not repay you by a set time, and the time arrived and he did not repay him — it is permitted, even if the time he set did not arrive until after Pesach; for when the time arrived and he did not repay him, it is acquired by him retroactively, and it is his chametz belonging to the non-Jew.
