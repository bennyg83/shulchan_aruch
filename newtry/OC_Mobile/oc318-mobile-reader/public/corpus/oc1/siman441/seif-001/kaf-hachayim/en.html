(1) [Seif 1] A non-Jew who lent a Jew on his chametz, etc. L'chatchila it is forbidden to lend a non-Jew on his chametz to redeem it after Pesach, for it is like hiding in the non-Jew's hand and he transgresses bal yeira'eh; but if he intended to redeem before Pesach and afterward it continued that he does not redeem before Pesach, he is not obligated to destroy, for if he does not redeem before Pesach he cannot redeem after Pesach, for then it will be forbidden — Taz s.k. 2. Chok Yosef vol. 1: and it appears from his words that if in his mind he will not redeem at all even after Pesach, it is permitted l'chatchila since he set a time and said from now; and likewise R' Akiva Eiger vol. 2, and likewise Peri Chadash that it is permitted even l'chatchila on condition that when the time arrives he will finalize in his mind not to redeem further — see there. And likewise Chayei Adam vol. 1: if at bi'ur time he said to the non-Jew he will not redeem again, he does not transgress and there is no prohibition at all — see there. And they brought R' Akiva Eiger there. Mekor Chayim vol. 1: however Chayei Adam general rule 128 end of vol. 7 wrote one should be stringent and redeem before Pesach.
