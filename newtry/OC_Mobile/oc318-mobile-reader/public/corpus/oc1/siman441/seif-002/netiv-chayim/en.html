(s.k. 6) He does not acquire a pledge. Nemukei Yosef: Nevertheless one must condition that there is no liability on him — meaning he conditioned explicitly that even if the pledge is lost; for if he did not condition explicitly, even though he did not acquire a pledge by Torah law, nevertheless it is called double payment on him because of that which Shmuel [said]: a lost sack that rolls away — a lost thousand zuz, even though Shmuel deals when he specified — nevertheless regarding a pledge it is like specification, as Tosafot wrote in chapter HaUmanin 2 s.v. lo etc. — see there.
