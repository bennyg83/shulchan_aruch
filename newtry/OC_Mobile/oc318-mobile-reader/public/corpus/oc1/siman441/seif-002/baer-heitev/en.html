It appears to me that on Pesach the Jew is not obligated to destroy it, for it is still in the non-Jew's possession since perhaps he will redeem it; and even if the non-Jew came on Pesach and gave its value, it is permitted to let the non-Jew take it. Magen Avraham and Kaf HaChayim disagree — see there. And a Jew who takes a pledge from his fellow who lent him transgresses, for a Jew does not acquire a pledge from his fellow without from now. Magen Avraham. And Taz in siman 443 s.k. 4 wrote the borrower is obligated to destroy.
