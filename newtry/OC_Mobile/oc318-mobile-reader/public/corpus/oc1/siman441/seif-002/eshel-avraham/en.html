Permitted. In Rosh Yosef he challenged from siman 440: a Jew who deposited with a non-Jew with liability etc. — Chayei Adam explained: certainly when there is no pledge in his hand it is nothing, for even in renting money one does not acquire from a non-Jew — Choshen Mishpat siman 154. And even if they acquired, nevertheless here it is only asmakhta and is not effective without a pledge in his hand; and he had no view regarding immersing vessels because in any case it appears as his; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 120. And know: also if his intent is to leave it with him he does not transgress as long as he did not sell it to him explicitly — Magen Avraham; and see Choshen Mishpat siman 73 — many laws and disputes regarding pledge.
