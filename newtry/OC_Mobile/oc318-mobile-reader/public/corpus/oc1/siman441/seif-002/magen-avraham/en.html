(א) He is not liable for it, forbidden. It appears to me that on Pesach the Jew is not obligated to destroy it, for it is still in the non-Jew's possession since perhaps he will redeem it; and even if the non-Jew came on Pesach and gave its value, it is permitted to let the non-Jew take it, as written siman 440.

(ב) Because he acquired it for him. If so, even if he did not pledge it in his hand, if he transferred it to him with from now it is forbidden, as we wrote.

(ג) But if he did not say to him etc., permitted. For we hold a Jew from a non-Jew and a non-Jew from a Jew do not acquire a pledge (Rosh). And he had a view regarding what is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 120 seif 9 regarding immersing vessels that it is a doubt, for there the reason is because it appears as his; and in Choshen Mishpat siman 253 end of Hagahah: it appears to me that even though it appears from the non-Jew's intent that he intends it to remain in his possession, nevertheless he does not transgress as long as he did not sell it to him explicitly; and in Choshen Mishpat siman 73 — and from this it implies a Jew who takes a pledge from his fellow who lent him transgresses, for a Jew from a Jew acquires a pledge; and see what we wrote siman 443.
