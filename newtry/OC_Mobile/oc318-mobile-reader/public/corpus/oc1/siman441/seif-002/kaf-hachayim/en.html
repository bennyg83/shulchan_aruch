But if he did not pledge it in the non-Jew's hand, only the non-Jew lent him on it and the chametz still rests in the Jew's possession — although he said to him from now, it is no worse than a non-Jew's chametz in the Jew's possession with liability on the Jew, which is forbidden — Rosh, Taz s.k. 1, Magen Avraham s.k. 1. And if he said to him from now and transferred it to the non-Jew through kinyan or via land, even though they did not deliver it to the non-Jew until after Pesach, it is permitted provided it was not on the Jew's responsibility all the days of Pesach — Riaz and Beit Yosef in Shach chapter Kol Sha'ah, Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur, Magen Avraham there. And he wrote there Magen Avraham that one must acquire through the non-Jew's sudar as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 123 seif 4. However Shach there s.k. 30 wrote chalipin and sudar-kinyan with a non-Jew are not effective except only with money or pulling per one opinion and per another — see there; and Nekudat HaKesef agreed to his words in this note 1; and see below note 9.
