[2] Permitted, etc. It appears that if his intent is that he will not redeem it by the time set after Pesach, it is permitted l'chatchila to lend on it in this manner, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 120 seif 11 regarding immersing vessels; but to lend in an unspecified manner in this way is forbidden l'chatchila. And if he set a time before Pesach and did not redeem it, it is obvious it is permitted; and even if he redeemed after Pesach it is like a new acquisition. Chok Yaakov wrote: if at the time bi'ur arrived he said he would not redeem it, he does not transgress at all.
