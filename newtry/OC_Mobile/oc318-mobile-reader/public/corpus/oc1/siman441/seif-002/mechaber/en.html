A Jew who lent a non-Jew on his chametz — if he pledged it in his (the Jew's) hand and said to him from now, and the time arrived even after Pesach and he did not repay him, even if he is not liable for it — it is forbidden, because the Jew acquires it retroactively. But if he did not say to him from now, and he is not liable for it — it is permitted.
