An Israelite who lent to a non-Jew on his Chametz, if he took it as a pledge in his hand and said to him: "from now," and the time arrived even after Pesach and he did not repay him, even if the responsibility for it does not fall upon him, it is forbidden, because he acquires it retroactively.
