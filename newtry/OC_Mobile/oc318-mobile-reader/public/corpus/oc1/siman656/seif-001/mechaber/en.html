That one must pursue hiddur mitzvah when purchasing an etrog. It contains 1 seif: If one purchased an etrog that is only minimally fit for fulfilling the mitzvah, such as one exactly the size of an egg, and afterward found a larger one, it is a mitzvah to add up to one-third of the price of the first in order to exchange it for the finer one. One authority says that if 2 etrogim are available for purchase, 1 more beautiful than the other, he should purchase the more beautiful one if its price is not more than one-third greater than the other. {Rama: One who does not have an etrog, or another mitzvah object whose opportunity will pass, is not required to spend a great amount of his wealth for it, for the Sages said that one who spends should not spend more than one-fifth, even for a time-bound mitzvah. [Rosh; Rabbeinu Yeruham, netiv 13, part 2] This applies specifically to a positive mitzvah. To avoid transgressing a negative commandment, however, one must give all his wealth rather than transgress. [Rashba and Ra'avad] See the Rama at the end of siman 658.}
