(s.k. 3) Even etc. That it become moist afterward: that is to say, even though the woman's body itself is moist now at the time of rubbing because of sweat, that is of no consequence, for human sweat does not cause chimutz, as written in siman 466 se'if 5. Rather [the concern is] because after the rubbing it is her way to rinse with cold water, and we fear that she may not remove all the bran from her body, and some bran will remain on her body when she rinses with cold water and it will become chametz. And the words of the Taz on this are mistaken and need a gloss.
