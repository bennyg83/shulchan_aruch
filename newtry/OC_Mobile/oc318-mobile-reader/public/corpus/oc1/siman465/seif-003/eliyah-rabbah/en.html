[3] [Levush] When they wish to wash etc. It implies that when they do not wish to wash it is permitted; and in the Remazim, chapter Kol Sha'ah, it implies that it is forbidden in every case. And this is the wording of the Taz: And it appears that even if she does not rinse afterward with cold water they were stringent, lest she not rinse well; end quote. And perforce there is a scribal error in his words, and this requires study. And it appeared to me to emend: And it appears that even if she rinses etc.; and he meant that if before she washes she rinses with cold water, which does not cause chimutz as above in siman 457, one would have thought that afterward it is permitted to wash, for by rinsing she removed all of it; and he says that is not so, for perhaps she will not rinse well. But in the Rosh and Ri they wrote that they must remove all the bran before she rinses with cold water.
