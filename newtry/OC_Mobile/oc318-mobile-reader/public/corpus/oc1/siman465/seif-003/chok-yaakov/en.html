And so is the practice regarding a prohibition of chametz, which is in any amount. That is to say: even though the Rosh who forbids, his main proof is from what is stated in chapter Kol HaBasar, that if there are cracks in it, it is forbidden; and he follows his own view, for he ruled even regarding other prohibitions to forbid the whole of it if there are cracks in it, and as explained in Taz Yoreh De'ah siman 91. But we hold like the view of the poskim there that one forbids the whole of it only when roasted or boiling, as explained there in Shulchan Aruch siman 91 se'if 7 in the gloss. Nevertheless here, regarding a prohibition of chametz which is in any amount, we are stringent like the view of the Rosh and forbid the whole of it; for aside from the skin there are cracks in goose flesh on every side. And so it is implied in Darkei Moshe and in my brother-in-law the Rav in Or Zarua; not like the cited authority who erred in this. And see above siman 467 s.k. 31, that even one who permits when cold concedes here, since there are cracks in it, and also the rubbing causes it to enter into it; see there. And on Erev Pesach from the sixth hour onward, since there is here no stringency of a prohibition of any amount, one may be lenient, it appears to me.
Not to singe. And after the fact, if he singed, it is valid; Taz. And Magen Avraham wrote: and specifically when it is unspecified; but if it is known that there was chametz grain in it and he singed with it, it is forbidden; see Yoreh De'ah siman 92. And if they singed with straw and afterward chametz grain was found in the remaining straw, we do not presume a prohibition in it; but if three [kernels] were found in it, one should be in doubt, and this requires further study. And if a stalk of straw is found and there is no grain in it, see siman 454 s.k. 1.
