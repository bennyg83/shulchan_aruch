See Taz: it implies that his view is that even if wheat is found among them it is permitted, for they are fruit juices; even though he cooks the fowl afterward, it was already nullified while still permitted in the fowl before the chimutz. And what he wrote that one need not fear perhaps the wheat had already become chametz: for we do not presume a prohibition; so it is certainly; and see responsa Tzemach Tzedek 122, and above siman 462, wheat in honey is permitted and we do not fear that it was already chametz. But chametz wheat and they singed [with it]: it implies that his view is to forbid, and as Magen Avraham wrote in 5. But in Eliyah Rabbah letter 4 his view is to permit even without peeling, since nothing touches it but the smell of chametz; see there. And after the fact one can say regarding this that by peeling at any rate one may permit, as in siman 467 in Magen Avraham 37 regarding a craw in such a case; and God willing it will be explained further there. And here one can distinguish between the singeing here, that the smoke that rises from chametz is like chametz, and that is what Magen Avraham wrote in 5; see Yoreh De'ah 92 se'if 8, that the vapor rises; and as written in siman 447 regarding dry meat under which wood (holtz) smoke was rising, that it is forbidden; and see Magen Avraham in siman 451 according to that view. And what is written there: and for that reason one should forbid the whole fowl when chametz wheat is found; which is not so regarding pitch, that we should say that the fire acts to emit the wheat into the fowl: we do not say so. And see siman 467 in Magen Avraham 37 and Taz 21, and God willing it will be explained there regarding this. And the view of Peri Chadash in siman 467 there implies to forbid when it is known that there is chametz wheat; see there regarding this.
