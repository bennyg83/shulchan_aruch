Flavor. And after the fact it is permitted; Taz. (For it is fruit juice, and one should not say perhaps water came upon them, for they permitted in siman 463 to singe two stalks of grain, even though even one would be forbidden; see Rashba and Chok Yaakov.) And Magen Avraham wrote: and specifically when it is unspecified; but if it is known that there was chametz grain in it and they singed with it, it is forbidden. And if they singed with straw and afterward chametz grain was found in the remaining straw, we do not presume a prohibition in it; but if three [kernels] were found in it, one should be in doubt, and this requires further study. (For according to the view of the Taz in the name of Maharshal in Yoreh De'ah siman 84, we say so only regarding worms that multiply from it; and see Magen Avraham siman 467, se'if 7 and se'if 9; and see Chok Yaakov.)
