And it imparts flavor into the poultry. And after the fact one should not forbid, for these are fruit juices and they do not cause chimutz. And one should not say, perhaps water once came upon that straw: for if so, why did they permit in siman 463 to singe two stalks of grain, given that even one would be forbidden for this concern? Rather, certainly we do not presume a prohibition, and as Beis Yosef wrote in the name of a responsum of the Rashba in siman 467, that we are not concerned for chimutz in stalks of grain. And here he came only to caution ideally; and it is an excessive stringency.
