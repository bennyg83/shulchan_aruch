They scald. Because we are not expert in chalitah, as written in siman 454 se'if 3. And it is written in a responsum of Emunas Shmuel, siman 27, regarding one who has an illness in his belly that they call in the Ashkenazic tongue "heben Mutter," whose remedy is that they scald barley or oats and place them on his belly: that if this illness occurs on Pesach and there is danger in it, it is permitted for a Jew himself to make the chametz and heal him with it; but if it is possible through a non-Jew, he should do so; and if it is impossible, he should boil the water thoroughly and afterward cast the barley into it, and it will not come to chimutz through chalitah. And if it is possible to do it with fruit juice, he should do so, for then it does not become chametz; end quote. And see siman 328 se'if 13 and in the Taz there, s.k. 6.
