Because we are not expert in scalding as in siman 454 seif 3; Maharash of Kaidenburg responsum 27 wrote: one with stomach illness called hiben motir—remedy is scalding barley or oat sheaf placed on stomach; if this illness occurs on Passover with danger, a Jew may himself make chametz and heal; but if possible through a gentile he should; if impossible, boil water well then put barley so it does not leaven through scalding. If possible with fruit juice, do so for then it does not leaven—end. See siman 528 seif 13 and Taz there small seif 7:
