(1) One may not soak, etc.—because they leaven like grain itself:

(2) Bran—and likewise coarse bran leavens. [Above siman 454 Mishna Berurah s.k. 2 explained bran and coarse bran.] Husk of straw holding grain called spreyer does not leaven, like ordinary straw:

(3) In any case—meaning not effective to stand watching so bran not in water mil measure—for that measure is only b'dieved; l'chatchila forbidden even one moment as above siman 459 seif 2—see Biur Halacha:

(4) And not scald—scalding means boil water very well then put flour or coarse bran; though scalding does not leaven, forbidden because we are not expert in scalding as above siman 454 seif 3—see Mishna Berurah there many details:

(5) Not to throw them, etc.—from sixth hour onward; some wrote even before Passover beware [unless standing over them while eating until finished and remainder not left before them] lest leaven and remain during Passover:

(6) Wet place—even if placed in dry place, good to beware not giving water to drink while barley before them; rather clean place well first then give water:
