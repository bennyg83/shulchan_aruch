Seif 3 in gloss: beware not singe fowl with straw lest wheat or leavened grain—note see Taz and Magen Avraham even if grain found do not concern perhaps more were there. I was asked by Rav of Kraske: in practice hollow upper husk of oat sheaf found in water, checked no kernel—what law? I answered: per questioner's wording husk on strainer lid—if actual oat sheaf in crevice obviously permitted, for we do not presume prohibition place to place; can attribute to bucket drawing water to pour barrels briefly, not this pit water—no prohibition. Even per Maharam if husk in water also no prohibition as only doubt soaked; thus doubt perhaps no kernel; even if say perhaps not soaked long—still only rabbinic smallest doubt unlike Beit Lechem Yehudah bone marrow case d'oraisa doubt. Besides logic: even husk without kernel in food permitted—great rule we do not presume more evil than evident; certainly per Minchat Yaakov disputing Beit Lechem Yehudah hollow bone—forbidden only because common marrow in bone, custom marrow dissolves in pot, senses testify—if full marrow in food later comes out clean; here kernel in husk uncommon, not removed without hands, if falls occasionally kernel uncommon—not presumed especially rabbinic smallest amount—permitted without doubt; so I ruled several times permit even in food—requires study:
