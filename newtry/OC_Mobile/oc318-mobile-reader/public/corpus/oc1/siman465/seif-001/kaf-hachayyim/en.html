1) [Se'if 1] One may not soak coarse bran, etc. This is because soaking causes it to become chametz. Rashi, Pesachim 39b. Bran likewise becomes chametz. Eliyah Rabbah, siman 454, subsection 1; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur, there; Erech HaShulchan, there, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there, subsection 3; Chemed Moshe in this siman, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2. See Sha'ar HaTziyun there, which cites this in the name of several great authorities. See also our words there in subsection 3. Bran and coarse bran were already defined there in subsection 2. See there.
2) There. One may not soak coarse bran, etc. This applies from the beginning of the sixth hour onward. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1.
3) There. In any manner. This applies even when it will not remain for the time needed to walk a mil. Initially it is forbidden to leave it unattended even for a single moment, as stated in siman 459, se'if 2.
