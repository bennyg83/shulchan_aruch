1) [Se'if 1] One may not soak coarse bran, etc. This is because soaking causes it to become chametz. Rashi, Pesachim 39b. Bran likewise becomes chametz. Eliyah Rabbah, siman 454, subsection 1; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur, there; Erech HaShulchan, there, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there, subsection 3; Chemed Moshe in this siman, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2. See Sha'ar HaTziyun there, which cites this in the name of several great authorities. See also our words there in subsection 3. Bran and coarse bran were already defined there in subsection 2.
