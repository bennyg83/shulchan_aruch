One should not ask why this differs from the ruling at the end of siman 450, where it is permitted on Pesach to heat water in a chametz utensil for washing. There, one derives no benefit from the absorbed chametz in the utensil. Here, however, actual chametz remains present, and the woman benefits from it through rubbing. It appears that they were stringent even if she will rinse afterward with cold water, lest she fail to rinse thoroughly.
