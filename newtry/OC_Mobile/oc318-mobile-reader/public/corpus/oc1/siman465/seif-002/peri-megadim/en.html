That the goose. See Taz and see Peri Chadash. And the Mechaber in Yoreh De'ah 91 se'if 7 permits regarding cracks when roasted cold with rinsing, and all the more so raw; [but] on Pesach with any amount he was stringent, and as the Rav wrote in the gloss, and for that reason he wrote "there is one who forbids." And so is the way of the Mechaber in many places: that when he writes "there is one who forbids" and does not write that one disputes this, the law follows that. And what he wrote in the name of "there is one who says," either he found it so only in one posek, or as here: even though Avi HaEzri and the Rosh and the Tur wrote to forbid the goose according to their view in Yoreh De'ah in the Tur, that even raw with cracks the whole is forbidden, which is not so [for] the Mechaber, who ruled that even roasted cold is permitted with cracks, and all the more so raw; rather it appeared to him to be stringent regarding chametz of any amount, and for that reason he wrote so. And cold roast which the Rav forbids there when that [leniency] is absent: if so regarding chametz one can say regarding this matter that it is possible by law, namely during Pesach. And one must look into benefit from a raw goose with cracks: whether it is permitted to sell to a non-Jew in such a case, since it appears only from custom, and it is possible that it is forbidden by law; and this requires further study. And see siman 467, and God willing it will be explained further there.
