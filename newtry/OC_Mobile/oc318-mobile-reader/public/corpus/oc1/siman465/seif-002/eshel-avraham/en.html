Although siman 450 permits heating water in a chametz utensil for washing, because one does not benefit from what is absorbed in the utensil, here the body benefits from the forbidden substance. If she rubbed herself with it, she should wash herself with fruit juice, or wait until it is certain that twenty-four hours have passed. He further writes that those who refrain from eating poultry during the first days of Pesach but eat it during the final days have no sound basis unless they are careful to feed the birds only matzah throughout Pesach.
