Crackers. Above siman 168 seif 8 I wrote that specifically if he eats the crackers with the spread; but if he ate the spread from above and left the crackers and eats them by themselves — they require their proper blessing, for then he has made them primary as well.

Bread. Only that they come to attach the spread to them so the hands are not soiled with honey. Magen Avraham wrote: but in these countries they place the spread on honey cakes that are fit for food — if so, his intent is also for eating the honey cakes and he blesses on them.
