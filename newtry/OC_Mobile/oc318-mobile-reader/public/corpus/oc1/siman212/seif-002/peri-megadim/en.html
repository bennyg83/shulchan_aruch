Taz: in the Gemara "and is there anything," etc.; and Rashi explained: Sea of Kinneret fruits are more important than bread; and Rambam chapter 3 of Blessings halachah 7 — behold one must eat salty food because of them; and in explanation of the mishnah it is only regarding garden guards; and it implies to some fruits of Ginosar only — but all who ate sweetness and needs salty food to cut the sweetness sticking in the esophagus; but Tur and Mechaber wrote even salted fish alone. And for Rashal — in truth one may say: is there anything where bread is secondary to fruits and the fruits are relish, etc.; and it requires study.
