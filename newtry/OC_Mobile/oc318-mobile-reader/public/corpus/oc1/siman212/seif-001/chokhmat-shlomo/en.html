Seif 1 in the gloss — even though it is secondary to drinking and he does not bless on it only shehakol since it is secondary to another thing. NB: see in Taz sk 7 what he wrote in the name of Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe and challenged them; and this is his language: this distinction is not sufficient to nullify Beit Yosef's words, for even in lack of the required quantity one is not called enjoying properly from the essence of its enjoyment — for whatever does not have the required quantity, the enjoyment is not complete; if so, what difference to me whether the enjoyment is not complete because of quantity or because it is secondary to this — he said that in both there is some enjoyment — end of his words, see there. And his words are wondrous, for behold we see in prohibitions that we hold half the required quantity is forbidden from the Torah, and in not the usual manner of enjoyment we hold it is entirely permitted from the Torah and forbidden only d'rabbanan; and perforce enjoyment of half the required quantity differs from enjoyment not in the usual manner; if so, likewise regarding what is secondary — granted, when eating half the required quantity, which is enjoyment of the thing itself that he eats for itself since he desires it — therefore it is the usual manner of enjoyment, and because of lack of quantity it is not considered not in the usual manner and it is enjoyment and therefore requires a blessing; but if he ate it only for another thing to sweeten the drink — since this is food and he ate it to sweeten the drink — if so, regarding eating it is not in the usual manner and therefore it is not enjoyment and therefore a blessing is not required. And in the body of Taz's and Darkei Moshe's words, who disagree whether not in the usual manner is considered anything at all or anything more dear — see this in Tosafot chapter 2 of Shevuot daf 23b in Maharshal and in Maharsha there, who disagree whether not in the usual manner is like half the required quantity or not — see there; and in my novellae in Ma'amar Katan I wondered there on Maharsha, and there is more to expand — think well.
