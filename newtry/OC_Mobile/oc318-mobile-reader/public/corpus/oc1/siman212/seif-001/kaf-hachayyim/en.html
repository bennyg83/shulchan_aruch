1) [Seif 1] Whatever is primary, etc. Whatever constitutes the majority is primary, as stated in siman 208, seif 10. Whenever a mixture contains one of the five grain species, we follow the grain, as stated there in seif 2, provided that it has substance. If it was mixed with water until it became thin, one recites Shehakol, as stated there in seif 6.
