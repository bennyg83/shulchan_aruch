Se'if 7: Wherever, etc. Shabbos 39b, as Tosafos explain there, s.v. 'From the incident,' and as stated by Rabbeinu Yonah, etc. This means, etc., completely, from twenty-four [hours]. It implies that the same applies when it is fully cooked, even though hot water is mitzamek v'ra lo, as explained above, and in accordance with Rabbeinu Tam on 47b, Tosafos s.v. 'With what,' regarding ordinary pots, etc. This is based on the Gemara in Beitza 27a.
And there are. There, in the name of R' Shmuel, who holds that in both cases the reason is lest one stoke the coals; the statement above refers to insulation performed before Shabbos.
