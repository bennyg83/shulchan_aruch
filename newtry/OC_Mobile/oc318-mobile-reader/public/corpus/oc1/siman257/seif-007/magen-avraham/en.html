Even in a cooked pot. Ordinarily insulation is intended for the following day, and more stoking would be needed so that it not cool, as stated by the Tur. A small amount of stoking is effective to preserve the heat because the pot is insulated, as stated by the Rosh and Mordechai. With leaving food on the fire, however, it is permitted even when intended for the following day, because a small amount of stoking is ineffective. See seif 8.
