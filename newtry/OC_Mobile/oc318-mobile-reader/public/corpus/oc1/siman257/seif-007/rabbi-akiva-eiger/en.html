Seif 7 in the hagahah. One should not act thus; but b'dieved it appears to me it is permitted, for Rama wrote in seif 1 that when insulating for tomorrow's needs, b'dieved it is permitted, for we rely on those who permit this even l'chatchila; and for those poskim shehiyah is not stricter than hatmanah — for per what we hold like Chananiah regarding shehiyah, hatmanah too is permitted like me'ein habein.
