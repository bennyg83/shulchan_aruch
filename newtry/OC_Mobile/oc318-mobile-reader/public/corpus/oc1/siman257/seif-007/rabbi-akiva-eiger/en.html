Seif 7, in the Rama's gloss. One should not conduct oneself this way. B'dieved, however, it appears to me that it is permitted, for the Rama writes in seif 1 that when one insulated for the following day, it is permitted b'dieved because we rely upon the authorities who permit this even l'chatchila. According to those authorities, insulation is no more stringent than leaving food on the fire, and just as we rule regarding leaving food on the fire in accordance with Chananya, insulation is also permitted once the food has reached ma'achal ben Derusai.
