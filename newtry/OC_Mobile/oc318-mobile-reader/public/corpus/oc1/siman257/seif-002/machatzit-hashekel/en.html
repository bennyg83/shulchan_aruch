(s.k. 2) One may insulate, etc. See siman 609, etc., regarding preparation for Motzei Shabbos according to the Rama. That is to say, the Rama forbids there insulating before Yom Kippur for after Yom Kippur, because this constitutes preparing on a sacred day for a weekday. Although on Yom Kippur the preparation occurs by itself, since he does nothing on Yom Kippur and had insulated it before Yom Kippur, it is nevertheless forbidden. If so, the same applies here: one may not insulate before Shabbos for Motzei Shabbos, for this reason.
If so, this requires further study, etc. Bread for Motzei Shabbos should likewise constitute preparation that occurs by itself.
It is a novel ruling, for it is permitted, etc. Even though it is for weekday use. For the Baraisa teaches that it may continue to be processed throughout the entire day; necessarily, he will benefit from it only on Motzei Shabbos. Nevertheless, it is permitted, although this constitutes preparation occurring by itself on Shabbos for weekday use. One must therefore say that the Sages were more stringent regarding insulation, and this itself is a novel ruling. Accordingly, apply the principle that we should not extend a novel prohibition beyond its stated scope, and do not forbid every case in which preparation for weekday use occurs by itself.
