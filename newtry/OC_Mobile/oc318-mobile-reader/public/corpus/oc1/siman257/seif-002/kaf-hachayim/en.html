Beit Yosef in the name of Rashi. Even according to the one who says he is not liable for cooking (meaning, who holds there is no cooking after cooking even if it cooled entirely, as written in siman 318 seif 15), nevertheless one must decree lest he heat it on the fire and stoke. Magen Avraham there in the name of the Rosh; Tosefet Shabbat there.
