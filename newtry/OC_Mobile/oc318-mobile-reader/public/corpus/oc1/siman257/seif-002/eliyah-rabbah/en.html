And it improves it, etc. It appears to me that here, b'dieved, they permit the food when it is mitzamek v'ra lo, because his action did not benefit it. In truth, however, it is forbidden l'chatchila to insulate it, and seif 7 discusses that case. Olas Tamid raised a contradiction between the rulings of the Shulchan Aruch and was forced to explain that “improves it” was not stated precisely. There is no difficulty at all, and this is also implied by Magen Avraham.
