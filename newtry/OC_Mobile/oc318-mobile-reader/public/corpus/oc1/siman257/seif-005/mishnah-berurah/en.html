If he transferred it, etc. The Sages forbade insulation only while the food remains in the kli rishon in which it was cooked. If he transferred it to another vessel, it is permitted even if it is still at yad soledes bo heat. Even if he afterward poured it back into the original kli rishon, it is also permitted, because it no longer has the status of a kli rishon and is treated like a cold item, which may be insulated. It also appears that even in the original kli rishon, if it cooled below yad soledes bo heat, it may be insulated in a case of need.
To another pot. There is no practical difference whether he expressly intended to cool it or transferred it without such intent for some other reason.
In something that does not, etc. In a material that adds heat, even a completely cold item may not be insulated, as explained immediately below.
