And b'dieved, etc. I am astonished how the expression b'dieved can apply together with “provided he is not accustomed to doing so” in a case of unintentional insulation. The Rama, who wrote this, had first written: “There are those who say that all this is forbidden only when he does it for the nighttime meal, but when he insulates for the following day, it is permitted to insulate before Shabbos with material that adds heat.” It is concerning that case that he wrote, “And b'dieved, etc.” I subsequently saw that Malbushei Yom Tov writes that words are missing here, etc.
