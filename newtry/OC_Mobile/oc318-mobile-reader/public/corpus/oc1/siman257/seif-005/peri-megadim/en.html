It is permitted. See Taz, who quotes the language of the Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilchos Shabbos, halacha 5; see there. Even if he did not reveal that he wished it to cool, once it was poured out of the kli rishon in which it was cooked, it may be insulated in material that does not add heat, like a cold item, which may be insulated in material that does not add heat. See Levush; his phrase “he revealed his intent” may not be precise. See Beit Yosef. It appears that whenever it was poured out of the kli rishon in which it was cooked, even if he placed it into an empty kli rishon and it remains at yad soledes bo heat in that kli rishon, so that it does not have the law of a kli sheni and the water is still considered cooked, nevertheless, because it is no longer in the pot in which it was originally cooked, there is no decree lest he find his pot cold. This applies specifically if it remains at yad soledes bo heat. If it is below yad soledes bo heat, it may certainly be insulated in material that does not add heat. See Magen Avraham, letter 14.
