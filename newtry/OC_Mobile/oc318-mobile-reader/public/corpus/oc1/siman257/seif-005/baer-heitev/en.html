To insulate it. The Sages forbade insulating on Shabbos only a hot item that remains in the kli rishon in which it was cooked. If one transferred it to another vessel, it is considered like insulating a cold item, which is permitted. It appears from the Taz that even if he returned it to the first vessel, it is also permitted, since it no longer has the law of a kli rishon. It is possible that even while it is in the original kli rishon, if it cooled somewhat, it is permitted to insulate it. See Magen Avraham, and see siman 318.
