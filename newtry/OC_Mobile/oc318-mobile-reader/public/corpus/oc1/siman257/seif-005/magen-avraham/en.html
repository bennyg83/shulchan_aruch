It is permitted to insulate it. It is like a cold item. The Sages forbade insulating only a hot item that remains in the kli rishon in which it was cooked, but if he poured it into another vessel, it is permitted, as stated by the Rambam and Ran. For this reason, it appears that even if he returned it afterward to a kli rishon, it is still permitted, because it no longer has the status of a kli rishon. This is not comparable to what is written in siman 253, seif 2, that matters are more stringent when one transfers from one urn to another urn. There the reason is that it is forbidden l'chatchila to place a pot on a stove, whereas when it remains in the original pot this is considered returning and is permitted. Here, by contrast, once it was poured into another vessel it is considered cold and may be insulated. It is possible that even in the original kli rishon, if it cooled somewhat, it may be insulated. See what I wrote in siman 318, seif 4.
