It is permitted to insulate it. Like a cold thing — they forbade insulating only a hot thing that was in a kli rishon where it was cooked; but if he emptied it out it is permitted (Rambam and Ran). For this reason it appears that even if he returned it to a kli rishon it is permitted, for it no longer has the law of kli rishon; and disagree with what is written siman 253 seif 2 that it is worse when pouring from kettle to kettle — there the reason is prohibition to leave on a stove l'chatchila, not so in the first pot where it is a return and permitted; not so here when emptying it becomes cold and permitted to insulate; and possible even in kli rishon if it cooled somewhat it is permitted to insulate — see siman 318 seif 4.
