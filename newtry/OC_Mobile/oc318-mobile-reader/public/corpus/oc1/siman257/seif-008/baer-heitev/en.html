Among the coals. According to the Rama in siman 253, it is permitted even if the pot touches the coals, since it is not insulated in cloths. See Magen Avraham.
The mouth of the oven. This applies specifically to a pastry or kinds of legumes that cook quickly. Raw meat, however, does not require sealing. In places where the practice is not to seal the oven with clay, it is forbidden to place kinds of legumes or dough in it unless they will cook to the stage of ma’achal ben Derusai. Magen Avraham.
It is permitted according to all opinions. One must take care not to position the pot against the wall of the oven, for then it appears like insulation. The primary basis for permitting it is that there is an airspace between the pot and the wall. The board that closes the oven should likewise not touch the pot, for the same reason. Taz; see there.
