And the insulation that they make, etc. It appears to me that one must take care not to position the pot against the wall of the oven, for then it appears like insulation. The principal basis of the permission is that there is an airspace between the pot and the oven wall. Likewise, the board that closes the oven should not touch the pot, for the same reason. This is similar to what the Tur writes concerning the form of insulation that the Rosh customarily used, placing the pot in a hollow and covering it from above, where the permission rested upon the separation between the pot and the wall.
