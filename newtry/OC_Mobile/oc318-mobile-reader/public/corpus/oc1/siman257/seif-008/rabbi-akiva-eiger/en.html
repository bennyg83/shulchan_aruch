Seif 8. “To place the pot in our ovens.” I do not understand the precision of the expression “our ovens.” Even in the ovens discussed in the Gemara, for which raking out or covering the coals is ineffective, raw food is nevertheless permitted. Moreover, in siman 253 the Mechaber does not mention at all the leniency that our ovens have the law of a stove. This requires further study.
