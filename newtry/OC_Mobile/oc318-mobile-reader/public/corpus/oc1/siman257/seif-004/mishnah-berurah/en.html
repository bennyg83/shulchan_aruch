And it became uncovered after nightfall. The phrase 'became uncovered' is not precise, because it is permitted even l'chatchila to uncover it, as stated immediately afterward. Rather, the ruling teaches that even when it became uncovered on its own, it may be covered only if this occurred after nightfall. If it became uncovered while it was still day, it is forbidden to cover it on Shabbos, because this is like insulating it initially on Shabbos. All the more so, if he uncovered it by hand before nightfall, even intending to cover it again after nightfall, it is forbidden to cover it on Shabbos.
Even, etc., with a sheet. This means that one should not say that covering it with a sheet is not considered insulation because it provides no benefit, and therefore covering it on Shabbos with a thick cloak would be considered initial insulation. The ruling teaches that even a sheet provides at least some benefit.
Causes it. See Biur Halacha.
