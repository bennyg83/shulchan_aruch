And it became uncovered after nightfall. If it became uncovered before nightfall, it is forbidden to cover it on Shabbos, because this is like insulating it initially on Shabbos. See siman 253, seif 2, in the Rama's gloss. The Bach is lenient in this case.
Causes it to cook. It is obvious that when it is insulated in something that does not add heat, this does not cause any cooking at all. Rather, the case is the manner described in seif 8; see there.
