(s.k. 4) The dish, etc. So too is implied by the gloss. That is to say, since the Rama writes, ‘One can say that if he forgot,’ etc., it implies that the Mechaber forbids even when he forgot. Yet he permits in siman 253, meaning in a case of mitzamek v'yafeh lo. Since the Mechaber discusses even an unintentional case and teaches two cases, a cold item that was heated and an item that became mitzamek v'yafeh lo, it follows that both are forbidden even when he forgot.
