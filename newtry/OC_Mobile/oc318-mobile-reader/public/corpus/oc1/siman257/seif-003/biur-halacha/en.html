When all three are moist. See Mishna Berurah. Magen Avraham rules in accordance with the Rambam, who holds that it is forbidden in every case. See Bach, who writes that the Tur states the matter without qualification in order to be stringent because he rules in accordance with the Rambam. However, many rishonim disagree, and therefore several later authorities are lenient. It appears to me that the Tur's reason is that although the Babylonian Gemara remains in doubt concerning this matter, the Yerushalmi in the chapter Bameh Tominin regards it as plainly forbidden in every case. We therefore follow the Yerushalmi, as stated in Yad Malachi.
