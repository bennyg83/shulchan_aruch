Of olives, etc. If a pot wrapped in a basket of wool is placed on top of olive refuse, the arrangement is forbidden because the refuse produces heat. Sesame refuse is permitted, as stated in the Gemara and Rabbenu Yerucham.
And grape skins, etc. Rambam and Bartenura in chapter Bameh Tomnin explain that zagim are grape skins and chartzanim are grape seeds. There is room to doubt whether they mean that grape seeds have the same law as skins for hatmanah, or whether they mention the seeds only to distinguish and teach that zagim specifically means skins. This requires study.
[Levush] All three of them, etc. Shulchan Aruch also lists straw, which the Levush omitted because Rambam does not mention it. In practical law Shulchan Aruch appears correct, for straw is listed in the Mishnah and all the poskim. Maggid Mishneh also writes that some Rambam manuscripts include straw, and Rambam's Commentary to the Mishnah explicitly includes it. Perhaps even the text that omits straw treats it as included in the same category as grape refuse.
