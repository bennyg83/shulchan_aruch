These are they. If one insulated a pot in a basket of wool, it is forbidden to place it on olive dregs, which add heat. It is permitted, however, to place it on sesame dregs, as stated in the Gemara.
When all three are moist. One must say that straw and grape pomace are counted as one item. In truth, the Rambam did not mention straw. From the unqualified wording it follows that they are forbidden even when moist because of an external liquid.
In stones. It is entirely uncommon to insulate in stones, because they break the pot or spoil the food. But regarding something in which people sometimes insulate, even though this is uncommon, the Sages issued their decree, as Tosafos states there.
