And straw and chaff — Taz, Bach, and Magen Avraham letter 10; and in Rabbenu Meir chapter 4 of Mahadura Sheni omitted straw, and in Levush also omitted; but the Mechaber mentioned it, and Peri Megadim letter 8: for such is the law, that straw too, if moist, adds heat; and straw appears whether long or cut into fine pieces; and in our novellae at the beginning of Kirah we expanded on the explanation of kash and gaba and straw. And salt — since it is sealed, it implies whether they dig or draw from water, they add heat; and sand — Shabbat 38b: not that sand, cold cools, hot heats; and sand heated in the sun, even though cold, heats. And see Yerushalmi beginning Bameh Tominin: those sands — Peri Megadim explained (dalet-dalet): it neither cools nor heats; and this teaches according to Rabbenu Meir, who omitted straw — for if dry straw is meant, why mention straw; and in Ran beginning Bameh Tominin, the Krakow edition implies the reading: tannan — guardian — meaning sand in the mishnah that adds heat is a guardian, as a watchman keeps according to what is entrusted to him, so too sand — not straw with beit-beit; see there, and in our novellae we wrote from this. What Peri Megadim wrote — many laws on the matter of stones, and it is not the place in Magen Avraham letter 10, will be explained.
