And straw and chaff, etc.; and according to most poskim they hold that specifically moist from themselves is forbidden, and not moist from something else, because they do not heat so much, as concluded at the beginning of chapter Bameh Tominin. And some say it is permitted to insulate on Erev Shabbat in dung, which is hot ashes and this is not rametz, which is forbidden, for rametz Rashi explained is ash mixed with coals; but dung, even hot, does not add heat. But in some places I have heard that they insulate and place pots even on coals, and they say that since the oven is sealed there is no concern — and one should protest against them and excommunicate them for this until they listen (Maharshal in responsa siman 60):
