Specifically, etc. This follows from the Mishnah's wording: 'like hot water that was heated,' etc.
There are those who say that if. This is like the law of leaving food on the fire when it is fully cooked, according to the Rif, as stated in siman 253, se'if 1.
And there are those who say that all this. So wrote the Shiltei Giborim, who holds that anything permitted with respect to leaving food on the fire is permitted with respect to insulation. He also holds that raw food is permitted, and the same applies to food that is partially cooked when it is intended for the next day.
Provided, etc. His words were rejected, for insulation is forbidden even with raw food, as stated in the Tosafos cited above and in the words of Rabbeinu Yonah, etc., and as stated in se'if 6. Even with respect to leaving food on the fire, there is no distinction once it is cooked, as explained above in siman 254, se'if 5. Likewise, the opinion that distinguishes where one forgot is rejected, for that view also holds that insulation is equivalent to leaving food on the fire. But according to our ruling, even mitzamek v'ra lo is forbidden, as stated in se'if 7, and this is comparable to a Jew returning the pot in siman 253, se'if 1.
