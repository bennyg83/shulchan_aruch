(In Magen Avraham s.k. 2.) And it requires study why Mishna Berurah wrote; and according to what is written above siman 254 seif 9, that on Motza'ei Shabbat they are forbidden while there is still time for them to be done — and Rashba and Ramban wrote the reason: since he places them in order to knead with them for Motza'ei Shabbat, we decree lest he kindle coals on Shabbat so they will be ready to knead with them immediately on Motza'ei Shabbat on Erev Shabbat; but not regarding bread, which finishes by itself, and we do not decree lest he kindle so it will be ready for Motza'ei Shabbat — understand well:
