1) [Seif 1] One may not insulate food on Shabbos, etc. This means that after a dish has been cooked, one wishes to insulate it in a material that will preserve its heat, as stated in seif 2 in the gloss. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1.
2) There: Even in a material that does not increase heat. The reason is that when he wishes to insulate his pot, he might find that it has cooled and first bring it back to a boil, thereby cooking on Shabbos. Beis Yosef in the name of Rashi. Even according to the opinion that he would not be liable for cooking, meaning the opinion that there is no cooking after cooking even if the food cooled completely, as stated in siman 318, seif 15, there is nevertheless reason to decree lest he heat it over the fire and stoke the coals. Magen Avraham there in the name of Rosh; Tosefes Shabbos there.
3) There: During twilight, however, one may insulate in it. This is because pots are ordinarily boiling hot during twilight, and there is no concern that the pot may have cooled and that he will bring it back to a boil. Beis Yosef; Taz, paragraph 1; Magen Avraham, paragraph 2.
4) There: Even while it is still daytime. This is a decree lest one insulate the food in ashes containing coals and stoke them on Shabbos. Beis Yosef; Taz there; Magen Avraham, paragraph 3.
5) There: The dish is forbidden, etc. This applies even if it was already fully cooked. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1. See seif 2. It applies even to a pot containing entirely raw food. Tosafos, Shabbos 39b, beginning with the words 'From the incident.'
6) There: The dish is forbidden, etc. This applies even if the act was inadvertent. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1. Magen Avraham, paragraph 4; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 4; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 4; and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1, write the same. Erech HaShulchan, paragraph 3, rules this way and writes that this is contrary to Eliyah Rabbah; see there. Although siman 253 permits food that one inadvertently forgot and left in place, the laws of insulation are more stringent. Magen Avraham there; Tosefes Shabbos there; Eshel Avraham there; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there.
7) There: The dish is forbidden, etc. It is forbidden both to him and to others until after Shabbos. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. Meat that was already fit to eat, however, is permitted. Piskei Tosafos, Shabbos, paragraph 155.
8) There: Or it contracted, etc. This applies only after the fact. Ab initio it is forbidden even if continued cooking causes it to contract and deteriorate, as stated in seif 7, contrary to Olas Shabbos. Magen Avraham, paragraph 5; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 5; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 5; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there.
