(א) Magen Avraham wrote there is doubt, etc., as written end of siman 306: if on erev Shabbat he said "today is weekday and tomorrow is sacred" — they make an eruv for him with it even though sacredness is not fit for him and a fit meal is required during twilight, since during twilight it is a doubt and from a doubt sanctity did not take effect on the loaf and it is fit for him during twilight and he may eat it during twilight; but if he said "today is sacred and tomorrow is weekday" — they do not make an eruv with it, for perhaps it is already night and the next day entered — sanctity did not cease and it is forbidden to eat during twilight and it is not fit for him during twilight. And see siman 462 in Magen Avraham who wrote in the name of the Gemara: although at the beginning of twilight of a fast the fast enters, nevertheless at the end of the fast during twilight eating is forbidden, for perhaps the fast did not cease — this is from the reason stated:

(ב) And see in Sefer Teshuvah who wrote to examine what Magen Avraham wrote on this in siman 307 — Magen Avraham omitted Tosafos in Eruvin daf 34a that also on motzei Shabbat during twilight regarding shevut; and I examined his words in siman 307 s.k. 49 where he brought proof from Eruvin: if he left his eruv where he cannot take it during twilight from that place — nevertheless if there is a way to bring it to a place where he can take it from there it is a valid eruv; and Rav bar Shabba objected to Rav Pappa from what we learned: how does he act — if Yom Tov falls on erev Shabbat and he wants to make eruv for Yom Tov and Shabbat — since Yom Tov and Shabbat are two sanctities he must each day make eruv for that day — from erev Yom Tov for Yom Tov and on Yom Tov for Shabbat; and it teaches how he acts: he brings it on Yom Tov to the place where he will acquire eruv for him and waits until after twilight, for during twilight he acquires eruv; and after dark he brings it home, for if he leaves it there lest it be lost and he have no eruv for Shabbat — on Yom Tov for now he brings the eruv to that place and waits on it until after twilight; and since on Shabbat it is forbidden to bring it home — the Tanna gives good advice that he eat it there in that place, for he already acquired his eruv during twilight; and if he leaves it there lest it be lost — better he eat it himself; if so, why must he bring the eruv to the place where he acquires eruv — even if he does not bring it there it suffices, for since he has in hand that he can bring it, it is as if he brought it. R' Zeira said: a decree on account of Yom Tov that falls after Shabbat — if he wants to leave eruv for Yom Tov he must bring it from erev Shabbat to that place and leave it until Yom Tov darkens, for then he cannot acquire eruv for Yom Tov unless the eruv rests in that place — for there is no twilight on motzei Shabbat; behold if he wishes he can bring it, for he cannot bring it while it is still doubt of Shabbat. Rashi wrote: likewise one can challenge every erev Shabbat why must he bring it to that place since it is possible for him to bring it — it is as if he already brought it; but according to what we do not find explicitly anywhere that one must bring the eruv — perhaps in truth one need not bring it, only here on Yom Tov falling on erev Shabbat they taught explicitly that one must bring the eruv; therefore he challenges from it; but in truth the challenge applies to every erev Shabbat, and Tosafos "but why," etc., disagreed with Rashi: for every erev Shabbat there is room for doubt, for he cannot bring it on erev Shabbat during twilight which is the time of acquiring eruv on account of the prohibition of carrying; and if the challenge still stands why must he bring it only to the place where he acquires eruv — he should only bring it close to that place for twilight — for us siman 261 three-quarters mil — from there onward he can bring it during twilight, meaning less than four amot until the resting place; and they wrote this too is not a challenge to the Rabbis who hold they decreed on shevut during twilight — if so it is forbidden to bring it during twilight less than four amot; and for Rabbi in truth in such a case it is permitted and we do not find for Rabbi that he must bring it to the resting place except when he wants to acquire residence in a private domain and a public domain separates between his house and the resting place — and the gloss on Tosafos wrote: and therefore it must be that in truth according to Rashi (and for Rabbi unless he acquires residence in a private domain with public domain separating) he need not bring it to the resting place; if so the challenge applies to Rabbi and he cannot answer what he answers — a decree lest Yom Tov after Shabbat; the gloss understood in Sefer Teshuvah that what the gloss wrote "he cannot answer the decree," etc. — behold also when Yom Tov falls after Shabbat it is permitted to bring it during motzei Shabbat twilight for Rabbi less than four amot — behold according to this it is explained explicitly that also on motzei Shabbat they did not decree shevut on twilight:

(ג) And in my humble opinion Tosafos' intent is not thus, but their intent is another matter: for they wrote in that passage to challenge according to their view that every erev Shabbat he must bring it — and the Gemara is not challenged except on erev Yom Tov; if so, why did it need to teach a decree lest Yom Tov after Shabbat — it should have said a decree of Yom Tov lest Shabbat, for in many places they decree Yom Tov lest Shabbat; and their second answer: "in Eruvin we are more lenient we do not decree Yom Tov lest Shabbat" — end of their words. If so, this is the intent of the gloss in what he wrote "he cannot answer the decree," etc. — specifically when Yom Tov falls on erev Shabbat he must bring the eruv needed for Yom Tov from beforehand, for they decree lest if Yom Tov falls after Shabbat and he wants to make eruv for Yom Tov he must bring from erev Shabbat from beforehand as stated — in this they decree Yom Tov lest Yom Tov — if he wants to make eruv for Yom Tov falling on erev Shabbat they decree lest Yom Tov — if he wants to make eruv for Yom Tov after Shabbat; but according to Rashi if we say that for Rabbi too on every erev Shabbat if he wants to make eruv for Shabbat he must bring it — a decree lest Yom Tov after Shabbat fall and he want to make eruv from erev Shabbat for Yom Tov — in such a case they did not decree Shabbat lest Yom Tov; the gloss holds like Tosafos' second wording and Magen Avraham wrote below siman 491 s.k. 16 regarding lamps — see there; and even for the first answer there, that likewise the teacher who decrees Yom Tov lest Shabbat — only that according to Rabbi there must be this decree — if so it should have said immediately "they decree Yom Tov lest Shabbat"; and one can say that even the gloss holds that on motzei Shabbat during twilight they decreed on shevut:
