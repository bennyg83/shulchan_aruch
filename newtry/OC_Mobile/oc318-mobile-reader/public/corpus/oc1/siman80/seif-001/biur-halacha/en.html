Better that the time for Shema and prayer pass — see in Mishna Berurah the reason that it is disgraceful, etc.; I collected this from the words of Chayei Adam. And above in siman 38 seif 2 in Biur HaGra it implies that the reason of Shulchan Aruch is mainly that Shema and prayer must be with tefillin, and here it is impossible with tefillin — see there. And accordingly in Minchah prayer, where in any case we do not put on tefillin, and likewise in the Shema of Maariv and its prayer — even though he cannot guard himself from belching, nevertheless he recites Shema and prays in its time; and when he needs to belch he stops until the odor passes. But according to everyone's words the reason of Shulchan Aruch is difficult: per Chayei Adam it is difficult — shall he nullify the mitzvah entirely on account of concern for belching? Is he not an ones in this? Let him pray, and when the ones occurs let him stop in the middle until the odor passes — as Magen Gibborim indeed challenged. And likewise for the reason of the Gra it is also difficult — shall he nullify Shema and prayer entirely because he cannot fulfill them with tefillin? Yet the Levush expanded for us on this in siman 58 and it is plain to him as a bolt in its socket: in a place where a person is compelled and cannot fulfill the mitzvah of tefillin, he does not nullify Shema and prayer in their time on account of this — see there. And it is plain to me that for this reason the Gra wrote in this siman that it does not appear so for practical law. However, according to everyone this ruling of Shulchan Aruch is stated only if he will certainly belch in the middle; but from mere concern that perhaps he will belch, it is certainly not correct to nullify on account of this — even public prayer — and a person has only to check himself initially, and beyond that he has no obligation at all:
