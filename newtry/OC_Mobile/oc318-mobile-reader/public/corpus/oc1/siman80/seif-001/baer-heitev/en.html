(א) From flatulence, etc.—all this applies specifically when this incident of being not ritually clean occurred because of some food that harmed him and the like, so that in the evening he will certainly be able to pray with a clean body. But if he has an illness because of which he must flatulate every hour, he should pray; and when flatulence comes upon him he should be silent and wait until the odor passes, then resume praying—unless he waited long enough to complete all of it. Pri Chadash. And see above, siman 78, s"q 2, from Shulchan Shlomo regarding urine. (And see in responsa Rama, siman 98, and in Eliyah Rabbah what he challenged on him.)

(ב) To Keriat Shema. But he may wear a tallit and bless before he begins, for it is permitted to enter the synagogue with tzitzit. Taz—not like Bach; see there.
