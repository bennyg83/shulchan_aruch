One who belches, etc. All this is specifically when this incident occurred without purity on account of some food that harmed him and the like — so that in the evening he can certainly pray with a clean body. But if he has an illness on account of which he is compelled to belch every hour, he prays; and when belching comes to him he is silent and waits until the odor passes, and returns to pray — unless he waited long enough to finish the entire [prayer].
