He should remove — Taz. Certainly a tallit is permitted all day; even a large tallit is permitted for flatulence and for the bathroom it is forbidden. However if he did not remove the tallit and flatulence came upon him between Ahavah Rabbah and Keriat Shema — he may remove it without a blessing, for donning is not an interruption except in prayer; see siman 66 note 4 in Magen Avraham what he wrote there; and see Perishah here. And know that flatulence is a rabbinic prohibition, yet a bad smell with no substance is permitted even for him and for Keriat Shema too — and the Sages decreed; nevertheless it is better that he let pass, for prayer and Keriat Shema have compensation — siman 58, opinions, Eliyah Rabbah stood on this. But one who has an illness that he cannot manage without flatulence — Peri Chadash and Eliyah Rabbah in Rama's responsum 98 — he is permitted to pray and read Keriat Shema, for rabbinic law does not come and uproot Torah law — see there; and Peri Chadash wrote that he waits until the flatulence finishes and the smell — see there. And prayer according to the view that it is rabbinic is a public mitzvah. Behold tefillin require a clean body; and if one has flatulence illness — one may say rabbinic law does not uproot forever a public mitzvah, similar to musical instruments as in siman 575 in Magen Avraham note 4; and one may distinguish between individual and congregation — requires investigation.
