He should don tefillin between Ahavah and Keriat Shema. The reason is that he is under duress. And Mateh Moshe wrote: but a tallit, which one is not obligated to wear—it is forbidden for him to interrupt between Ahavah and Keriat Shema, even not to bless but only to wear it, is also forbidden, as above in siman 66—so far his words. He was not precise here, for one may wear a tallit and bless before he begins the blessings, and even all day this one may remain wearing a tallit, for it is permitted to enter the synagogue with tzitzit, as written in siman 21; and this is not similar to siman 66, for there he did not have the tallit before he began.
