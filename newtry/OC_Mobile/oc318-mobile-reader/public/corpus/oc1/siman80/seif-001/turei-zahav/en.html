He puts on tefillin between Ahavah and Shema. The reason is that he is compelled; and Magen Avraham z"l wrote: but a tallit, which he is not obligated to wear — it is forbidden for him to interrupt between Ahavah and Shema, even not to bless but only to wear it alone is also forbidden, as above siman 66. And he was not precise in this, for a tallit he may wear and bless before beginning the blessings, and even all day this can be — wearing a tallit, for it is permitted to enter a synagogue with tzitzit, as written in siman 21; and this is not comparable to siman 66, for there he did not have the tallit before he began:
