See the Taz, who writes in the name of the Bach that it is forbidden to wrap oneself in a tallis, and that even if he does not recite a blessing it constitutes an interruption. We do not, however, rule this way, since wrapping oneself does not constitute an interruption, as stated above in siman 66, seif 2, in the gloss, and in the Magen Avraham there, seif katan 4.
