(15) At the top of the camel - This means that he made the walls there between its humps and placed s'chach over them.
(16) At the top of the tree - He prepared his seat at the top of it and made partitions and s'chach there.
(17) And one may not ascend to it - Because it is forbidden on Yom Tov to use a living creature or attached vegetation, as written at the beginning of siman 336. Even if the sukkah is on the ground, but the reeds of the s'chach are supported by the tree, it is forbidden to ascend to it on Shabbos and Yom Tov, as a decree lest he use the s'chach by placing his objects there or hanging something on it, and thus be using the tree indirectly. Perhaps this applies specifically in earlier times, when people were accustomed to use the s'chach to place and hang their objects there. But now that people are not accustomed to do so, we are not concerned for this, and it is permitted to enter it even on Shabbos and Yom Tov. Nevertheless, initially one may not support the s'chach on the tree, for initially the s'chach may be supported only with something fit for s'chach, as written in siman 629; see there for the reason. If he inserted poles into the tree and supported the s'chach on them, it is permitted even initially.
(18) On Yom Tov - If he transgressed and ascended on Yom Tov, he fulfilled his obligation. See the Peri Megadim, who is uncertain whether, if on Yom Tov he has only a sukkah on top of an animal or a tree, he may initially ascend to it; see there. The Bikkurei Yaakov concludes that it is forbidden, for the Sages exempted him; see there.
(19) Part of it on the tree and part of it on another item - This means that he made part of the floor of his sukkah and part of its walls on top of the tree, and part of its floor and part of its walls on another item, and placed s'chach over the walls, if it is made in such a way that if the tree were removed it would remain standing on the other item and not fall, etc.
(20) One may ascend to it on Yom Tov - This means by a ladder, not by way of the tree.
(21) One may not ascend to it on Yom Tov - Since its support is on the tree, when he enters it he is using the tree.
