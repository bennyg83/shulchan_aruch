The tree. This is where the s'chach can stand without the tree, because he supported it on poles. Otherwise it is forbidden, for we decree lest he use the tree itself as s'chach, as written in siman 629:7. Ran; Magen Avraham.
One may ascend. If he transgressed and ascended, he fulfilled his obligation. If the sukkah is on the ground and the reeds of the s'chach are supported by the tree, it is permitted, and we do not decree lest he place something upon the s'chach. See the Magen Avraham there.
