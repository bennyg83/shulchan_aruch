(Se'if katan 3) Higher than twenty. This too is marked incorrectly in the printing, and refers to the first clause, which validates even when it is higher than twenty, since the lower sukkah by itself, without joining the upper one, has more shade than sun. On this the Magen Avraham wrote: and we do not say that what is opposite the invalid s'chach, etc. This is even according to the first some say opinion in siman 626, for here we speak where the upper s'chach has more shade than sun, and nevertheless it is valid, because the upper s'chach in itself is valid; only its location causes it to be invalid.
