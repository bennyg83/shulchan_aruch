There is no Taz — Rosh chapter 3: if one transgressed and went up and fulfilled — some say this is mitzvah through transgression since it is impossible except through transgression; Tosafot beginning of Lulav stolen, and even for rabbinic mitzvah through transgression one does not fulfill; and l'chatchila also on the first night there is a positive commandment that one eats an olive-size of bread there — the positive commandment does not come and override rabbinic, as shofar in siman 681 seif 21 and 22 — requires study. See Gemara 23a for R' Yehuda: the festival of Sukkot seven days — fit for seven; nevertheless Mishnah implies for all it is a full derashah; and 27b positive commandment to make sukkah on the festival — for Rabbanan of R' Eliezer, nevertheless a sukkah fit for seven is also expounded, only it should not be conceived; and if one built sukkah in public domain on an animal and on Shabbat its rest is biblical — in any case this is carrying, whether public domain to private or private to private, not better than a person as explained. I saw in Teshuvot Divrei Chamudot: sukkah fit for seven and Shabbat within seven — requires study, for otherwise on the first day forbidden as Rashi explained; perhaps for what is written in siman 514 that his house burned and he has nowhere to eat — permitted to extinguish as life-threatening — one could say similarly no prohibition to climb a tree and animal such on Yom Tov as life-threatening the Merciful One permitted; therefore he explained Shabbat always within seven is not fit for seven unless for what is permitted. Requires study for us: if he has no other sukkah except in a tree or on an animal, whether they climb on Yom Tov as life-threatening; however it burned. And according to his innovations in what he wrote one does not climb on Yom Tov — Bach 514 for R' Yehuda validates, and here for R' Yehuda it is, and Bach in Teshuvot Divrei Chamudot, and for Rashi one could say, and perhaps to make from egg — see Beitzah on this.
