Even if the upper one, etc. This implies even though the upper one also has more shade than sun. Although in siman 627 everyone invalidates if the invalid s'chach, namely the tree, has more shade than sun, here is different, for the upper s'chach is not called invalid s'chach, since its invalidity is not intrinsic but only because of the measure of height. This is Rabbeinu Tam's reasoning in Tosafot.
