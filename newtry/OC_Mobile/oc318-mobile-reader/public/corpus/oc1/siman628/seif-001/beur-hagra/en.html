Se'if 1: And there is between them, etc. Like Shmuel there, for the sugya there follows him, and the western scholars also follow him. For if it does not require ten tefachim, all the more so it need not be able to receive cushions and pillows, since it is not fit for dwelling. Rashba. See the Rosh, and the Rambam's commentary, with which the Raavad agreed.
Even if, etc. According to the text of Tosafot there, 9b, s.v. ha; and Rabbeinu Tam reads: the lower one, etc.
