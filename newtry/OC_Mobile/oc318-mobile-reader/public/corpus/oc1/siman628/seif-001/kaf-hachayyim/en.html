1) [Seif 1] The upper sukkah is valid and the lower one is invalid. For the word sukkos is written defectively, without a vav, and we expound: not in a sukkah beneath another sukkah, etc., as written above at the beginning of siman 626, see there.
2) Ibid. And the lower one is invalid. This refers to a case where the upper sukkah is within twenty amos. However, there is one who was uncertain according to Rava, who holds that the reason a sukkah higher than twenty amos is invalid is that we require a temporary dwelling, and above twenty amos it cannot be made as a temporary dwelling. In a case of a sukkah beneath another sukkah, may the upper one be built within twenty amos of the roof of the lower sukkah, even though it is more than twenty amos above the ground? See Sefer Bikkurei Yaakov, who wrote that it is permitted if it is within twenty amos of the roof of the lower sukkah. So too wrote the Mishna Berurah in Biur Halacha, see there.
3) Ibid. This applies when the lower sukkah can support, etc. For then the lower sukkah is called a sukkah beneath another sukkah, since the upper sukkah is fit for dwelling. Tur; Levush.
