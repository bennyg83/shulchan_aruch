And the lower one is invalid. For it is written basukkot, in the singular.
Higher than twenty. We do not say that what is opposite the invalid s'chach is considered as though it is absent, because s'chach that is higher than twenty is not called invalid s'chach so as to invalidate what is opposite it. Tosafot and Rosh; see siman 626.
The s'chach of the upper one. And the upper one has more shade than sun. But if its sun is greater than its shade, even if the lower one can receive it, it is valid. See siman 631:5.
