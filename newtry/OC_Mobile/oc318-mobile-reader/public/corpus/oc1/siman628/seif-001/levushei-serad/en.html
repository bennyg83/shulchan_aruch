Magen Avraham se'if katan 3 The s'chach of the upper one, etc. The reference mark is mistaken, and it should read: and cushions of the upper one. On this the Magen Avraham says that if its sun is greater than its shade, it is valid even if it can receive them. Also the reference to se'if katan 3 is marked by mistake; its place is on what the Mechaber wrote, even if the upper one is higher than twenty, in accordance with the Taz, se'if katan 2.
