The Laws of Building One Sukkah Under Another Sukkah. Containg 3
paragraphs: Regarding a sukkah that is underneath another sukkah, the top one is valid and the bottom one is invalid. This is so if the bottom one is able to receive pillows and mattresses of the upper one, even with difficulty, and there are ten tefachim between them. However, if there are not ten tefachim between them or there are ten tefachim between them but it is impossible for [the floor of] the upper [sukkah to support a person together with his] pillows and mattresses even with difficulty, the bottom one is valid if it is covered per the law, even if the upper sukkah is' higher than twenty tefachim. If it is not covered per the halachah and valid due to the s'chach of the upper one, the s'chach of that upper sukkah cannot be higher than twenty amot off the ground.
