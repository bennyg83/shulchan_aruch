[2] To mention the souls, etc. This does not refer to the present custom of mentioning souls in the prayer “Kel Malei Rachamim,” for we recite “Kel Malei Rachamim” on every festival. It necessarily follows that this is permitted even during the festival itself, since it is not a eulogy but a prayer on their behalf. Beis Hillel to Yoreh Deah, siman 347. The Levush writes there that our custom is to mention them even for the first time during the festival itself. The Shach cites his words, but this requires study.
