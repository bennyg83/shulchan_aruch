Although Tzidduk HaDin is less severe than a eulogy, it is not recited on Erev Shabbos after midday, and perhaps the same applies on Erev Yom Tov. Certainly on Erev Pesach after midday, when the Pesach offering was brought, there should be no eulogy except for a Torah scholar, whom one may eulogize on Erev Shabbos and Erev Yom Tov, and even on Erev Pesach after midday, since a Torah scholar may be eulogized even on Chol HaMoed, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 401.
