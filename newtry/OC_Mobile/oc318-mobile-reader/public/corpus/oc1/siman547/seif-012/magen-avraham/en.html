Tziduk HaDin. See Yoreh Deah, siman 403, for it implies that when one does not say Tziduk HaDin, one also does not say the praise of the dead, for that too prompts a eulogy when one mentions his praise; and if so, it is forbidden to preach about him, except for a scholar in his presence. And so it is implied in the Levush, siman 696; and all the more so on Yom Tov it is forbidden. And it appears to me that in our time there is no status of a Torah scholar who knows halachah in every place, as Mahariv wrote; see the end of the siman.
