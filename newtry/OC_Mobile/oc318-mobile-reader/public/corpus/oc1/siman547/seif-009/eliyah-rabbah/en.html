[9] Boards discreetly, etc. If it is impossible to do so discreetly, they may be prepared publicly. This applies, however, only to publicity before Jews, but not to publicity before non-Jews, even when no other option is available. Knesses HaGedolah and Magen Avraham in the name of Tumas Yesharim, siman 117. In my humble opinion, one may be lenient even before a non-Jew, and this is also implied there in siman 115.
