[5] And for an upright person, etc. It appears that he wrote this because of the conclusion, “and upon hearing the report,” etc. At the moment the soul departs, however, one tears even for a person who is not classified as upright, and this is also implied by the Shulchan Aruch. Yet this requires study, for the Rama writes there in Yoreh Deah 340:6 that the custom is to be lenient and not to tear for an upright person unless one is present at the moment the soul departs. This applies even on a weekday, and all the more so on Chol HaMoed. Perhaps the authorities here relied upon that ruling. It therefore appears correct in practice not to tear on Chol HaMoed even in the cases mentioned above, unless one is present at the moment the soul departs and would be required to tear even on a weekday.
