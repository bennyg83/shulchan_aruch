1) [Se'if 1] It is forbidden to deliver a eulogy or to fast on Chol HaMoed. If one swore to fast on Chol HaMoed, the oath takes effect upon him. Responsa Ralnach, siman 103; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos on the Tur; Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1. See above, siman 418, paragraph 4.
2) There: One who fasted a dream-fast on Chol HaMoed must observe a compensatory fast, as will be explained below in siman 568, se'if 5. See above, siman 288, paragraph 22.
3) There: Women should not accustom themselves to lamenting on Chol HaMoed. This means that they should not increase their lamentation. Tur; Levush.
4) There: Until the grave is prepared. For if they bring the deceased earlier, they will eulogize until the grave is prepared. Levush. This applied in their locales, where the custom was to eulogize at the cemetery. In our locales, where the custom is to eulogize at home, it is preferable to remove the deceased immediately after the eulogy, so that the eulogy will not be prolonged. Yad Aharon, Hagahos Beis Yosef.
