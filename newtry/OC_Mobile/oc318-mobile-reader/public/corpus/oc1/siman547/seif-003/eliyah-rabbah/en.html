[3] And for a Torah scholar, etc. In Yoreh Deah 340:7, the Rama writes that the custom is to be lenient and not to tear one’s garment for a Torah scholar unless he was one’s teacher or one knows the explanations that he originated. The Shach there writes that the custom is to be lenient even when one knows them. Therefore, on Chol HaMoed, when one should not tear even in a place where there is no established custom, notwithstanding the Levush’s concluding statement that one should tear for all of them, one should tear only for one’s teacher.
