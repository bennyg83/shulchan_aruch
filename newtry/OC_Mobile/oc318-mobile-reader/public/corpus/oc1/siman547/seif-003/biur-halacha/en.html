For the dead who died for him before thirty days before the festival - From this wording it implies that even if he is still within thirty days of the death, nevertheless, since from the time of the eulogy until the festival is less than thirty days, it is forbidden; and see in Yoreh Deah, siman 347, in the Pitchei Teshuvah there.
That it is permitted to eulogize him even if he died on the eve of the festival - And if it is after midday, and likewise on erev Shabbos, it requires further study whether it is permitted to eulogize him; for it is possible that just as one does not say Tziduk HaDin on erev Shabbos and on the eve of a festival after midday, as is clarified in Yoreh Deah, siman 401, so too regarding a eulogy, except for a Torah scholar, whom they eulogize on erev Shabbos and on the eve of a festival, and likewise on erev Pesach after midday, for it is no better than Chol HaMoed, when they do eulogize him (Peri Megadim). And even though we are stringent on Chol HaMoed not to eulogize even a Torah scholar, as the poskim wrote, nevertheless regarding erev Shabbos and the eve of a festival one certainly should not be stringent in this.
