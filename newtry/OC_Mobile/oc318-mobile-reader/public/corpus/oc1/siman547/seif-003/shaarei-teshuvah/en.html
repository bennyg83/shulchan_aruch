(In Shulchan Aruch, seif 3) All thirty days before the festival, etc. See what I wrote in a responsum that I printed at the end of Kuntres Mispar Rav, and also in my responsa Beis Ephraim, at the end of the Yoreh Deah volume, that there is room to distinguish between for free and for pay; and there I wondered at the distinction clarified in the Shulchan Aruch between one who died before thirty days, for that is not the intent of the Ramban in Toras HaAdam. And now I saw in Shevus Yaakov, volume 2, siman 25, that he wrote that this is specifically regarding one's own dead, for whose heart is greatly bitter, etc.; but if it is not his own dead, and he eulogizes him only for the honor of his Torah, the eulogy does not stir his heart so much, and we are not concerned; and one may further say that the honor of the Torah overrides the honor of the festival, etc.; see there. And the matter requires further study, and it is difficult to act upon this unless in a place where it appears that there is a need of the hour, as I wrote in the responsum there.
