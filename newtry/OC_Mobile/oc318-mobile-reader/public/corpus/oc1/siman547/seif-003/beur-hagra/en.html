Seif 3: All, etc. Yerushalmi there: What is "arousal"? He mentions him among the dead. And what is a eulogy? That he makes a separate eulogy for him. This was said regarding an old [death], but for a recent [death] it is permitted. What is recent and what is old? Recent is within thirty days; old is after thirty days. And this accords with Shmuel, who said "because," etc. And even though the halachah follows Rav in prohibitions, since according to one version Rav Kahana said as in the reading of the Rosh, and in the Rif the text has only Rav Kahana alone, we act like Shmuel, for the Yerushalmi accords with him.
