Seif 3, all, etc. Yerushalmi there: what is "arousal"? He mentions him among the dead. What is a eulogy? That he makes a separate eulogy for him. This was said regarding an old death, but for a recent death it is permitted. What is recent and what is old? Recent is within thirty days; old is after thirty days. This follows Shmuel, who said: because, etc. Even though the halachah follows Rabbi in prohibitions, since according to one version Rav Kahana said as in the Rosh's reading, and in Rif the text has only Rav Kahana, we act like Shmuel, for the Yerushalmi agrees with him.
