Seif 8: They provide the mourning meal on Chol HaMoed, and it is, etc. Tur; and he explains what is stated in the mishnah, "and they do not provide the mourning meal," as meaning they do not provide it in the [public] square; and as is written there regarding a scholar, that for him everyone provides the mourning meal in the square; and it implies that without a square it is in any case permitted; and it speaks of Chol HaMoed, as above. But in the Rambam he wrote simply that they do not provide the mourning meal, etc.
