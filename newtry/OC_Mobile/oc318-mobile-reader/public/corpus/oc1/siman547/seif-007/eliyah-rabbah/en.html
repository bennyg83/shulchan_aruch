[7] [Levush] The custom is, etc. Nevertheless, upon receiving a recent report concerning another relative, which will become a distant report after the festival, one tears on Chol HaMoed, for if not now, when? For one’s father or mother, one tears even if the report will not become distant after the festival. Upon receiving a distant report, however, one does not tear even for one’s father or mother. So write the Shach and Taz in Yoreh Deah. This escaped the notice of Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah and Olas Shabbos.
