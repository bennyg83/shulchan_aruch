7) [Se'if 2] Thirty days before the festival. The reason is that the deceased is not forgotten for thirty days; if one eulogizes within thirty days of the festival, he will come to grieve during the festival. According to Shmuel, even a free eulogy is forbidden. Rav prohibits only a paid eulogy, because one thereby spends festival funds, but permits a free eulogy. The halachah follows Shmuel. Taz, paragraph 1. There is no difference whether the eulogizer is an outsider or one of the mourners. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1; Mikra'ei Kodesh, siman 58; Chayei Adam, rule 3; Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 2. If the person died within thirty days of the festival, even a paid eulogy is permitted, for the halachah follows Shmuel. Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 5.
