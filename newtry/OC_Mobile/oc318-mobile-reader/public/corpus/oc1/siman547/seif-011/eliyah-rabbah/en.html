[11] One may not pluck. I likewise found in Malbushei Yom Tov, part 1, siman 250, that he disagrees with this and concludes that the ruling concerns one who plucks the grass for the needs of the cemetery, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 368. The grass that is plucked after burial as a remembrance of the resurrection, however, may be plucked. The laws of Tzidduk HaDin were explained in siman 420.
