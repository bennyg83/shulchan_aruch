A scholar. Whom they ask a matter of halachah in every place, and he answers, even if he does not know how to give and take in it (Nimukei Yosef). There is no such Torah scholar in our time (Magen Avraham).
Ashkenaz. That is Ashkenaz proper; but in Poland they customarily tear for all of them (Masas Binyamin, siman 72; Magen Avraham). And even in Ashkenaz, upon a recent report that will become distant after the festival, they tear. But upon a distant report they do not tear, even for one's father or mother; this is the consensus of the later authorities; and see in Yoreh Deah, siman 340. And if one's father died and was buried on Yom Tov, one should not tear on Chol HaMoed, for it is neither the time of death nor the time of mourning; rather he should tear after the festival (Maharam of Lublin, siman 74).
