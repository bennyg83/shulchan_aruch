And there are those who disagree. This is the view of the Semak, who holds that there is no tearing at all on Chol HaMoed; and the people of Ashkenaz customarily made a compromise, distinguishing between father and mother and other relatives; and so too the Terumas HaDeshen wrote that this is the custom in Ashkenaz. And the Rama concluded regarding this that in this matter we do not follow the people of Ashkenaz as in other places, unless it is clear that such is their custom; but by default we rely on the main law, that one should tear for all the relatives. And so are the words of the Rama in Yoreh Deah, siman 340; and see there in our words on matters belonging to this further.
