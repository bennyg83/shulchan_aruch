They do not tear, etc. If he was buried on Yom Tov, one should not tear on the festival until after Yom Tov, for that time is neither a time of emotion nor a time of hearing the report (Maharam of Lublin, siman 74). Rabbi Yitzchak Tyrnau wrote in seif 65 that one who heard a distant report on the festival tears; and in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah on Yoreh Deah, siman 340, seif 31, he wrote that the Maharshal and the Bach disagree; and so too Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that there are those who disagree.
The people of Ashkenaz. That is Ashkenaz proper; but in Poland they customarily tear for all of them (Masas Binyamin, siman 72).
