[6] And in Ashkenaz, etc. This means Ashkenaz proper, but in Poland the custom was to tear for all of them. Masas Binyamin and the later authorities. Maharam of Lublin writes in a responsum that if one’s father died and was buried on Yom Tov, one should not tear on Chol HaMoed, since it is neither the time of burial, the time when the report was heard, nor the time of mourning. Rather, one should tear after the festival.
