Ashkenaz, see Baer Heitev; and the Mishneh LaMelech wrote in chapter 11 of Hilchos Avel, regarding an upright person, or one who was present at the moment the soul departed (it appears that he means according to the view of the Mechaber in Yoreh Deah, siman 340; but the Rama there wrote that they customarily follow the other opinion, that even for an upright person one is obligated only when he is present at the moment the soul departs; see there), that he does not tear at all, even after the festival, since once it was deferred, it remains deferred; see there. And Machatzit HaShekel cites him.
