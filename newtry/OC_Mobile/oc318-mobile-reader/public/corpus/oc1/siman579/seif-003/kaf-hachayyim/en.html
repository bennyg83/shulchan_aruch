Accordingly, in a place where they cannot pray in the street because of the non-Jews, they should pray in another house, in accordance with the second reason. Although the Gemara states that they went into exile from one synagogue to another synagogue, this is not precise; the same applies to another house, which is no less suitable than the street. It is called a synagogue because at that time it became a synagogue. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 2.
