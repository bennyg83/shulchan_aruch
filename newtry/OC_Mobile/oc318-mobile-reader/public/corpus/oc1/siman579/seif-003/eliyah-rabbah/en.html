Soft ash. Ash of the stove. And the Tosafos wrote: ash burned from a human bone. And Magen Avraham wrote: and we do not practice so regarding a groom, as he wrote in siman 560, se'if 2; it appears to me that the same law applies here; end of his words. And this requires further study, for there the case is different, since the reason is to remember the destruction of Jerusalem, which is not so here, where the reason is to remember the Binding of Yitzchak, so specifically ash is required.
