(Se'if 1) They take out the teivah etc. And see the Responsa Noda BiYehuda, Mahadura Tinyana, who ruled that one should not take a Sefer Torah out to the cemetery in a time of distress, for since they are not expert in defective and plene spellings, there is reason to fear that they will cause a defect, as is written in the Zohar that once they did so and there was missing in the Sefer Torah a vav from "shosa'as," and they nearly caused a great destruction; see there. And see Birkei Yosef, siman 135, who wrote likewise in the name of great authorities that one should not do so. And see what I wrote in Shaarei Ephraim, shaar 9, se'if 45, that even though they read from that Sefer Torah and it is presumed kosher, one should not take it out to the cemetery; see there. And in Pischei Shearim, what is written on this.
