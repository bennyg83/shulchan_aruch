To the town square. In order to be publicly humiliated; alternatively, on account of exile (Gemara). And if so, in a place where they cannot pray in the street before gentiles, one should pray in another house, according to the second reason. And even though it is stated in the Gemara that they went into exile from synagogue to synagogue, that is not precise; rather, the same applies to another house, for it is no worse than the street; rather, he called it a synagogue because at that hour it becomes a beis haknesses.
In sackcloth. To say: behold, we are regarded before You as an animal. And Rashi explained: sackcloth is of animal hair, of goats' fleece; end of his words. And it appears to me that he came only to explain that ordinary sackcloth has that meaning, as the Tosafos wrote in Shabbos, folio 27; but in truth they may cover themselves even with other kinds of sackcloth, for nevertheless one is likened to animals, as they were compared, for it is customary to cover an animal with a sack; therefore it appears to me that one should cover it on top of the clothes.
Soft ash. Ash of the stove. And the Tosafos wrote: ash that comes from the burning of a person. And we do not practice so regarding a groom, as is written in siman 560, se'if 2; it appears to me that the same applies here.
And one of the people etc. And the reason they do not take [ash] themselves and place it on their heads is so that they will be ashamed before others, that their shame be greater.
The Nasi. Even though in [public] disgrace we begin from the lesser one, here it is an honor for them, for we say: you are important enough to seek mercy upon us (there).
And after he finished. And the same applies on erev Rosh Chodesh, in a place where they say words of rebuke; they should say them before the prayer.
Accustomed to prayer. So that he not err.
And burdened. With young children; and he has none, for through this he will pray with a whole heart.
And acceptable to the people. So that they will assent to the prayer.
A pleasant [voice]. That draws the heart.
