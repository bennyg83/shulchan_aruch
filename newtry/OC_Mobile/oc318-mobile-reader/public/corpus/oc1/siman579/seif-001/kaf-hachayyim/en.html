1) [Seif 1] They take out the ark, etc. This means the ark in which the Sefer Torah is kept. Rashi on the Rif, beginning of chapter 2 of Taanis. So too wrote the Rosh there: They take the Sefer Torah together with the ark to the city square. It may not be left alone without a guardian sitting and watching it. Jerusalem Talmud, beginning of chapter 2 of Taanis.
2) Ibid. They take out the ark, etc. If the community is in distress, they should not take a Sefer Torah to the cemetery, as written above in siman 135, os 77. See there. See below, os 19.
3) Ibid. To the city square. This is in order to be humiliated publicly. Alternatively, it is because of exile. Gemara. Accordingly, in a place where they cannot pray in the street because of the non-Jews, they should pray in another house, in accordance with the second reason. Although the Gemara states that they went into exile from one synagogue to another synagogue, this is not precise; the same applies to another house, which is no less suitable than the street. It is called a synagogue because at that time it became a synagogue. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 2.
