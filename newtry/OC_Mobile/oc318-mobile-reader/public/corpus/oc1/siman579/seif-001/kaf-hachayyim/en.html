1) [Seif 1] They take out the ark, etc. This means the ark in which the Sefer Torah is kept. Rashi on the Rif, beginning of chapter 2 of Taanis. So too wrote the Rosh there: They take the Sefer Torah together with the ark to the city square. It may not be left alone without a guardian sitting and watching it. Jerusalem Talmud, beginning of chapter 2 of Taanis.
