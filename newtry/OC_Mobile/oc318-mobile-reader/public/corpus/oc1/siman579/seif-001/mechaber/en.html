The order of Taflu' in those who fast, and in it 3 sections: on each day of the seven fasts, the last of the rains, they pray for this order, they take the ark out to the street of a city and all the people gather and cover it with sacks and put ashes from a stick (pl. ash of a thing that is burned to ashes in the Alma, which is also called ashes)
on top of the ark and on a Torah scroll to increase the crying and subdue their hearts and one of the people takes the ashes and gives on the head The president and the head of the court place a tefillin so that they will talk and return and each A and A take and give at his head and then they place a wise old man between them and they sit. There was no wise old man there. the Omnipresent will see their deeds and their fasting, but the Omnipresent will see their actions and in acceptance he will say tear your hearts and your clothes and add to these matters according to his power until he subdues their hearts and return complete repentance and after this is over the words of captives stand in prayer and set up a massed messenger who is worthy to pray in fasting' these and if it was he who said words of conquest' he is worthy of praying he prays and if not someone else is brought down and who is he who is worthy to pray in fasting' these are a person who is accustomed in prayer and is accustomed to read the Torah, prophets and scriptures, and is treated, and he does not have and he has a foot in the field, and let not his children and the members of his household and all his relatives and companions have a transgression, but let his house be empty of his transgression, and let not a bad name come upon him In his childhood he was humble and pleased with the people and he has a pleasant voice and a deep voice.
