Seif 5. But if he has not yet finished his prayer he may not return. NB: it appears this is only if he does not wish to return to the beginning — for example if he forgot Yaaleh veYavo and finished the blessing — if he wishes to say it in its place if he erred and returned — but if he returned in Yaaleh veYavo to the last three and likewise in havdalah to Atah Chonenanu — what of it that there is interruption — if he stopped, if he returned in the last three to Ratzah and in the middle to the beginning of the matter he erred, there is no delay and he may do so l'chatchila; why should this be worse. Therefore it appears in such a case there is truly no interruption and he need not pray again — as in my opinion correct and clear for practical law; examine well.
