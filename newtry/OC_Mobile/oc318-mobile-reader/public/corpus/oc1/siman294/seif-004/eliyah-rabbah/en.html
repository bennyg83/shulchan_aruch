(ד) There is no need. And Maharash wrote one should say it in shome'a tefillah (Malbushei Yom Tov); and likewise wrote Hagahot Maimoniot in the name of Maharash; and Maharil implies even if he has a cup; and Magen Avraham wrote specifically when there is no cup one may rely on them — but in Pesakim Tosafot wrote even without a cup one does not say them in shome'a tefillah but must return to Atah chonen — for specifically a request one says in shome'a tefillah and not a question and mention as Beit Yosef wrote — until here. And in my humble opinion the essence is as Magen Avraham — for I saw Levush in the name of R' Noson wrote like Maharash; and moreover Beit Yosef wrote that "k'shem shehabdalatanu" is also prayer; and although Perishah wrote that since they are not accustomed to say k'shem shehabdalatanu therefore Beit Yosef agreed not to say it — therefore it appears to me that since they are accustomed to say Avinu Malkenu "hachal," etc., it is also prayer; and in Sefer Tanya he wrote this language: one need not say it in shome'a tefillah because he says it on the cup — until here his language implies he agrees when he has no cup:
