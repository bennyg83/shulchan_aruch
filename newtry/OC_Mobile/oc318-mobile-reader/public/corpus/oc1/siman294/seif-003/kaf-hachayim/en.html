And if he erred and did not make Havdalah, etc. — he was anus and did not pray on Motza'ei Shabbat — he prays on Sunday two prayers, one obligatory and one makeup, and does not say Atah chonenatanu even though he did not make Havdalah. Radbaz 1:176 (and in Chadashot 561) as Nachalat Tzvi in Hagahat Tashbetz. Olat Tamid letter 1, Birkei Yosef letter 1; and although Magen Avraham and R' Akiva Eiger questioned Radbaz's words and hold one must say Atah chonenatanu in the makeup prayer — and Mateh Yehudah letter 2 agreed — nevertheless it appears since there is a dispute here, "return and do not do" is preferable so as not to interrupt prayer — for per the view that one need not say it, if he says it that is an interruption. And this is when there is remedy on the cup, such as he made Havdalah on Motza'ei Shabbat on the cup or he has a cup to make Havdalah on Sunday; but if he has no cup as in seif 2, Havdalah must be in the makeup prayer even per Radbaz — Mateh Yehudah there; and see above siman 108 letter 6:
