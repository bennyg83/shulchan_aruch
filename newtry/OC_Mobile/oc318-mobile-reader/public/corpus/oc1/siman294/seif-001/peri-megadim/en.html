They say — Taz: Levush and Bach agreed he should begin "Atah chonen" and also say "Atah chonenatanu," etc.; and the repetition — Levush wrote "Atah chonen" is intermediate every day for every person of the world, and "Atah chonenatanu" passed at the time of matan Torah when He gave us abundance of sechel — see there; and therefore they also repeat "v'chanenu" a second time at the end of the blessing — see there; and the Bach wrote that "noten haTorah" who teaches Torah to His people Israel is also intermediate; and explained the repetition: "Atah chonen" to man da'at even for other nations, "Atah chonenatanu" to know Your Torah — He separated us from other nations; and one should not say at first only "Atah chonen l'anos da'at" and "melamed l'anos binah" "Atah chonenatanu," and at the end conclude "v'chanenu me'itkha" and not say "v'chanenu" at first — see there; and Taz agreed here. And the repetition is on wisdom of Havdalah. And likewise Ohr Chadash letter 2 that one should not request his needs before he makes Havdalah — see there. If he forgot to pray Maariv on Motza'ei Shabbat he prays Shacharit twice — if one holds Havdalah is in Chonen haDa'at at Shacharit — Ohr Chadash letter 3 in the name of Olat Tamid in the name of Radbaz that even if he did not make Havdalah on the cup at night he need not say Atah chonenatanu at Shacharit; and R' Akiva Eiger left it requiring study; and see above siman 108 from this.
