They say Havdalah, etc. — meaning one must say immediately "Atah chonenatanu," meaning he begins "Atah chonenatanu" and not begin the blessing "Atah chonen" (Maharshal); and it appears not to begin "Atah chonenatanu" until after "V'chanenu me'itkha" da'at binah vehaskel — and afterward he begins "Atah chonenatanu"; and not as printed in all siddurim after "l'anos binah" first "v'chanenu me'itkha," etc., for the Sages said an individual inserts at the end of the blessing and not in the middle as above in siman 119; therefore an individual adds only at the end of the blessing as I wrote (Lechem Rav).
