1. The Laws Governing the Recitation of Havdalah in the Shemoneh Esreh, 5
Seifim: We say Havdalah in "He who grants wisdom", and if one erred and did not say Havdalah [in that blessing], he continues his prayers and does not return, since he needs to say havdalah on a cup [of wine]. And if he ate prior to saying havdalah on the cup [of wine], he must pray again and recite havdalah in the prayer.
