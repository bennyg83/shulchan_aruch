[4] [Levush] He need not. Maharam of Rothenburg writes that he should say it in Shomea Tefillah (Malbushei Yom Tov), and Hagahot Maimoniyot likewise cites Rabbi Shimshon. Maharil implies this even when he has wine for Havdalah. Magen Avraham writes that one should rely on them specifically when he has no cup. Piskei Tosafot, however, writes that even when he has no cup he should not say it in Shomea Tefillah, but must return to Atah Chonen, because only a petition may be inserted in Shomea Tefillah, not a request or mention of another matter, as Beit Yosef writes. In my humble opinion the primary ruling follows Magen Avraham, for I saw that Kol Bo in the name of Rabbi Natan writes like Maharam of Rothenburg. Furthermore, Beit Yosef writes that "just as You distinguished us" is itself a prayer. Although Perishah writes that because our custom is not to say that formulation, Beit Yosef agreed not to recite it, it appears to me that since our custom is to say "Our Father, our King, begin for us, etc.," this too is a prayer. Tanya writes: "He need not say it in Shomea Tefillah because he says it over the cup." This implies that Tanya agrees that when he has no cup, he may say it there.
