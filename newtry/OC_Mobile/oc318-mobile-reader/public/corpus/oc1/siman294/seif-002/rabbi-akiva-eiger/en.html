Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. Nowadays this is also required for the same reason. It appears that in the first Shacharis prayer he should mention Atah Chonantanu. This applies also when there is no compensatory prayer at all, such as one who did not pray Maariv and at Shacharis prays only once. I saw a manuscript addition to Magen Avraham stating that in the second Shacharis prayer, which compensates for Maariv, he should mention Atah Chonantanu. This addition does not appear in Magen Avraham here, and the principal ruling is that it is mentioned in the first prayer.
