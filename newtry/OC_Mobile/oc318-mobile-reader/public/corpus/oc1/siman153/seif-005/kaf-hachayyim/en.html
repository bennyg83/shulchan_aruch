(21) [Se'if 5] If money was collected for a synagogue, etc. If money was raised to demolish and rebuild a synagogue wall but they later feared they could not complete construction, it may be used for a mikveh [Tashbetz II 170]. He gives two reasons: designation alone has no effect, and the consecration was mistaken. Later authorities agree [Birkei Yosef].
(22) One donated a sum to build a synagogue at a specified site. After his death his sons tried unsuccessfully to build there, while another site contained a dilapidated synagogue. They may rebuild it there, since construction at the designated site is impossible and this does not contradict the deceased's intent; he would approve [Shevus Yaakov, Orach Chaim 12; Yad Aharon].
