(5) There: But a study hall may not become a synagogue. This concerns a synagogue without a fixed study program. If the newly built synagogue replacing the study hall contains a permanent daily yeshivah teaching Scripture, Mishnah, halachah, and aggadah, so it retains the name study hall and is known as the great house where prayer and Torah are magnified, conversion is permitted [Davar Shmuel 346]. Yad Aharon and Baer Heitev attribute to Davar Shmuel that one hour of fixed study gives study-hall status, but he did not write that and his quoted wording does not imply it. Tosefes Chaim says a one-hour study hall has no advantage over a synagogue.
