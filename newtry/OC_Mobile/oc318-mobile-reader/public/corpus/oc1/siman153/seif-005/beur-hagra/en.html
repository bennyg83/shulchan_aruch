And if when they collected, etc. Hagahos Ashri there, in the name of Or Zarua, and this requires study, for why did it not answer there in such a case?
But if not, etc. There: what are the circumstances? If we say, etc.
And if they bought, etc. There, 26b: but with new ones, etc. The Beis Yosef asked why this differs from collected money, which may not be changed, as above, and likewise when they bought bricks and wood with the money. He answered that what is stated, with new ones, etc., means before it came into the hand of the treasurer, as stated in Arachin 6; see Tosafot there. And he wrote: it seems more correct that what is stated, with new ones, etc., does not mean to redirect them permanently, but to lend them out. See Tosafot, Bava Kama 36b, s.v. yad, etc., and Rabbeinu Tam explained, etc.; see Yoreh Deah, siman 258, seif 6, and siman 259, seif 1. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 5, and Taz, seif katan 2.
