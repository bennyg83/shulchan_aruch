Seif 19, there is, etc. As written there: one who finds a vessel, etc., and written on it korban, etc.; kof means korban, etc. Even according to Rabbi Yehudah, who says there, last year, etc., and likewise the Yerushalmi, Rabbi Yonah, etc., which Tosafot cited on 115b, s.v. eimur, etc., that is not the case here. Similarly, what is stated at the end of the first chapter of Bava Metzia regarding a receipt that emerges from the creditor's possession, etc., all this does not apply here.
