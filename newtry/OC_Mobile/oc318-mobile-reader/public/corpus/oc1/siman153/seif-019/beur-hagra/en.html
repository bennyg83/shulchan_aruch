Seif 19 — there is, etc. As written there: one who finds a vessel, etc., and written on it "offering," etc., "holy offering," etc.; and even per R' Yehudah who said there "last year," etc.; and so Yerushalmi R' Yonah, etc.; and they brought Tosafot 115b s.v. emur, etc. — not relevant here; and likewise what is written in the first chapter of Bava Metzia regarding a receipt that comes from under the hand of a lender, etc. — all this is not relevant here.
