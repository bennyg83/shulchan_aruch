That if it is found, etc. Meaning that it is found in his house among his documents.
It is consecrated property. For he certainly consecrated it, since he wrote this on the Megillah. See the Magen Avraham, seif katan 45, who raised a point from Choshen Mishpat, siman 81, seif 17. In the words of the Shach there, his difficulty is resolved, as the Machatzit HaShekel wrote. But if so, this is not a clear law for extracting money, for many later authorities there do not hold like the Shach. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 255, in Pischei Teshuvah.
