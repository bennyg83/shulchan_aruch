Note: it is explained here in Beit Yosef that in any case, for all, he does not see a sign of blessing; and the difficulty of Magen Avraham is resolved; and so Avodah Berurah note 22; and further it appears to me that they dispute only when he has a Torah scroll and the like, but when he does not have the like — from this the Talmud and poskim do not deal; and even only a synagogue is not sold by the seven good men of the city in the presence of the people of the city if they do not have the like; and if so, this case in Bava Batra deals thus; and since all his days he cannot sell it, it is not included in his property in general to live — it is given to another in a gift, as Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah end of siman 270; and to resolve the difficulty of Magen Avraham note 23 — and this is clear in my humble opinion. However, in any case, it implies there in Bava Batra that Rashba had a different version, and so in Chemed Moshe.
