There, seif katan 22. Note: it is clear here in the Beis Yosef that in any event, according to everyone, he does not see a sign of blessing. The difficulty of the Magen Avraham is thereby resolved, and the Eliyah Rabbah wrote similarly in seif katan 22. It further appears to me that they disagree only where he has another Torah scroll or the like. But if he has nothing similar, the Talmud and poskim did not discuss that case. Even a synagogue may not be sold by the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople if they have nothing similar. If so, that case in Bava Basra discusses such a case. Since he could not sell it during his lifetime, it is not included among his ordinary property that was given for his livelihood to another as a gift of a deathly ill person. That is also the case discussed in Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah, end of siman 270, and the difficulty of the Magen Avraham in seif katan 23 is not difficult at all. This is clear in my humble opinion. However, in any event it appears there in Bava Basra that the Rashbam had a different text, and so wrote in Sefer Chemed Moshe.
