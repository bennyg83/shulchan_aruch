It is explained here in Beit Yosef that per all opinions he does not see a sign of blessing; and this settles Magen Avraham's difficulty; and so Eliyah Rabbah s.k. 22; and further it appears to me they dispute only when he has a sefer Torah or the like, but if he has no substitute the Talmud and poskim do not deal with this; and even for beit haknesset alone it is not sold by seven good men in the presence of the people if they have no substitute; and so this Bava Batra passage deals thus; and since all his life he cannot sell it, it is not included in his general assets for living expenses — it may be given to another as gift of shekhinah; and so Meiri in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah; and this resolves Magen Avraham s.k. 23 — clear to me. However in any case Bava Batra implies Rashbam had a different version, as in Chemed Moshe.
