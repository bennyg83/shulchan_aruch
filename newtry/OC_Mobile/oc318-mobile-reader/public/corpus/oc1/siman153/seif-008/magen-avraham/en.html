They built a house without specification. This means that they built it from non-sacred money, as written in seif 4. However, it appears to me that even if they built it from synagogue money, nevertheless it does not have the sanctity of a synagogue for all matters; only it is forbidden to change it, like the law of money. An individual who built a synagogue cannot retract, as written in seif 14, for it is as though he vowed charity. But if he made a covering for his Torah scroll that he has in his home, and it is not read publicly, as long as it was not used for the Torah scroll he may retract, as written in siman 42 and in Yoreh Deah, siman 282, for designation is not effective.
