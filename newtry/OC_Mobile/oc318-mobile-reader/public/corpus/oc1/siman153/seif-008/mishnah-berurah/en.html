Its law is like a synagogue. Meaning, although at first they used it for common use, nevertheless it becomes like a synagogue for all matters. It appears that even initially this is permitted. See below, siman 154, seif 21, and above, siman 147, seif 1, in the gloss. There the matter is sanctity, whereas here a synagogue is an accessory of a mitzvah, as the poskim wrote.
It is not sanctified. For we rule that designation alone is not significant, and it is permitted to change the house for mundane use. See above in Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 18, that this discusses a case where the community built it from ordinary communal funds; but if they collected money specifically for a synagogue, it is forbidden to downgrade the building to mundane use, though it still does not yet have full synagogue sanctity in all respects.
Even if they built it, etc. For even though there was also an act, it is still considered only mere designation.
Since it was designated. Meaning, the designation was a strong designation, for he designated it forever to pray in it. This is like what is stated above in siman 42, that where one designated a cloth forever to wrap tefillin in, and wrapped tefillin in it even once, it is then forbidden to wrap money in it. This excludes where he designated it as a house of prayer only temporarily, namely until the festival or the fair passes, for then people customarily gather. Such a designation is not considered designation at all, and even during the festival it is permitted to eat and drink in it.
And if he consecrated it temporarily. This means that he said without qualification, I designate it for the festivals. The wording implies whenever there is a festival. Therefore it is considered a permanent designation, at least for that time, and it is forbidden during that time. But an unspecified temporary designation was already written by the Beis Yosef in the name of the Mordechai as not being considered designation at all, as we wrote at the beginning. Regarding a condition, see above, siman 151, seif 11, and the Mishnah Berurah there.
