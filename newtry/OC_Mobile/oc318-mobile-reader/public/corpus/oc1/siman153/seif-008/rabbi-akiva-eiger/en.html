(Magen Avraham note 27) — it is written in Mishna Berurah: if they built a new synagogue — at first glance astonishing, for he already wrote in the gloss seif 7 that all this deals when they have another synagogue; and see Magen Avraham there, that now they built for this purpose another synagogue; but otherwise, even to sell with zt"h in the presence of the people of the city is forbidden. And if so, that which they dispute regarding a gift is also that they built another synagogue, and nevertheless, were it not for this reasoning that if they had not done for them, rest for the soul, it would be forbidden; and we do not say that sanctity ceases on account of another synagogue; and if so, Mishna Berurah's reasoning is hidden; and one must say for the examiner in Ran, that the essence of Mishna Berurah's reasoning above is from Ramban's words, that he wrote everything per his foundation, that for one who rents a house — because they erred in the presence of the people of the city, they may nullify sanctity of a synagogue in order; and if so, it must deal when they built another synagogue, for otherwise what he wrote that to destroy is forbidden; and in his words, the dispute regarding a gift deals only with zt"h alone. But per Ran's view, that permission for one who rents a house — sanctity applied to the money, and that sanctity is weak, and they have in their hand to take out to secular use; and otherwise, no difficulty from that which it is forbidden to destroy, for it is different there, for there is no exchange on which sanctity can apply; and if so, one can say the dispute regarding a gift deals without building another synagogue, and therefore the reason is needed, that if they had not done for them, rest for the soul; but if they built another synagogue, one can say that in any case sanctity ceases on the other synagogue; and therefore one can say this law of Mishna Berurah is from the first principle: permitted per Ramban, permitted with zt"h in the presence of the people of the city, etc. — see there.
