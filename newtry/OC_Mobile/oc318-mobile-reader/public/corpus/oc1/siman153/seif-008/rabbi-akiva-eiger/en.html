Magen Avraham s.k. 27 — it is written in Mishna Berurah: if they built a new beit haknesset, seemingly puzzling, for already in the gloss seif 7 all this deals when they have another beit haknesset; and see Magen Avraham there that now they built another beit haknesset for this; but in any case even to sell in the default case in the presence of the people is forbidden. If so, the dispute regarding gift is also when they built another beit haknesset and nevertheless without this reason that if not for making it restful for them it would be forbidden; and we do not say the holiness expired on the other beit haknesset. And if so Mishna Berurah's reason is hidden; and per him truly Meiri's dispute is gift in the default case alone. But per Ran who permits drinking beer there, holiness applies to the money and that holiness is minor and they may remove to unconsecrated. Otherwise the difficulty why forbidden to demolish, for that case is different since there are no substitutes on which holiness can apply; and one may say the dispute regarding gift deals without building another beit haknesset, therefore the reason is needed that if not for making it restful for them. But if they built another beit haknesset, one may say that in any case holiness expired on the other beit haknesset; and per this one may say this law of Mishna Berurah is from Manip permitted per Ramban, permitted to annul holiness for nothing if they built another beit haknesset; and per Ran annulment is on the other beit haknesset. And that they ruled in the gloss seif 6 like Ramban and here ruled gift specifically default case in the presence of the people like Ran — meaning we go here stringently and there stringently; but Mishna Berurah's law is permitted from Manip; see.
