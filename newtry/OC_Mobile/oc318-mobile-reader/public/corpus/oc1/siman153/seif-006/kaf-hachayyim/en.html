(6) There: But a study hall may not become a synagogue. Rosh, principle 5, permits a congregation, with its leaders' agreement, to rent a study hall as a residence and change it even to secular use. A study hall exceeds a synagogue only insofar as it may not be converted into one, not so greatly that townspeople can never change it. Maran cites this in Bedek HaBayis, as does Knesses HaGedolah. Rabbi Shmuel Boton, however, says a public study hall may not be sold, like a public synagogue [Davar Shmuel]; if so, it may likewise not be rented as a residence. See below, paragraph 51, beginning 'who come there,' and se'if 11.
