Even because of financial pressure, they may not sell even a portion, although the congregation will retain enough for its synagogue needs. Responsa Beis Yehudah, siman 9. For this reason, Responsa Mahari Bazan, siman 52, rules concerning one who vowed to arrange the marriage of a certain orphan girl but has a poor female relative whose father traveled overseas and who is poor and blind, that he may fulfill his vow by arranging the marriage of this poor relative; see there.
