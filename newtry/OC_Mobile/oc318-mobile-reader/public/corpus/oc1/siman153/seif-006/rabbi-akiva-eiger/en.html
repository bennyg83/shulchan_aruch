Magen Avraham s.k. 18 — I did not understand and I am puzzled at their puzzlement. It seems simply Mishna Berurah's intent from Ran and Ramban who wrote they built it with everyone's intent, meaning if at the time of building it was a village, even though later many gathered from other places to pray, nevertheless it is a village and we follow the beginning; but from Rosh who wrote presumably they consecrated — meaning it does not depend at all on time of building but whatever was done later as villages they consecrated later with everyone's intent, as it appears simply in Mishna Berurah's intent; and in the essential law this was already mentioned in Beit Yosef in the name of Nei regarding the plain sugya of R' Ashi: this town comes with my intent, meaning since they came only with his intent to study and later became villages, but at time of building it was a village permitted to sell — see there.
