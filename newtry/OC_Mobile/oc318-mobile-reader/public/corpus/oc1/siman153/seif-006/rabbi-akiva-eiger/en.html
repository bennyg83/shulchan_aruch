Magen Avraham, seif katan 18. I did not understand, and I am astonished at their astonishment. It appears obvious that the Mishnah Berurah's intent is from the language of the Ran and Ramban, who wrote, they built it with the intent of everyone, implying that if at the time of building it was a village, even if afterward many people gathered from other places to pray, nevertheless it is considered a village and we follow the beginning. But from the language of the Rosh, who wrote that presumably they consecrated it, it implies that it does not depend at all on the time of building. Rather, whenever it later became like a large city, they later consecrated it with the intent of the many. This seems to me obvious in the intent of the Mishnah Berurah. Regarding the main law, this was already mentioned in the Beit Yosef in the name of Nimukei Yosef in explaining the sugya, where Rav Ashi said that this city, Masa Mechasya, they come with me in mind. That means that since they came only with his intent, to study, and it later became like a large city, but at the time of building it was a village, it is permitted to sell it; see there.
