Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. After the fact, if he sold them. The Bach holds that even with other sanctities one distinguishes between initially and after the fact, so that if he already transgressed and sold them, it is permitted to buy something like them. This is unlike what the Taz wrote, that with other sanctities, since it is possible to elevate them, their after-the-fact case is like initially.
