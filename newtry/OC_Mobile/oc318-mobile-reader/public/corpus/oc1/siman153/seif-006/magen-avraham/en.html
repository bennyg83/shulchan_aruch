To marry off orphans with its proceeds. The same applies to orphan girls. Rabbi Moshe Alashkar, siman 42. So too it appears to me, as is proven from the Gemara’s statement that the reason is because 'He did not create it for emptiness, He formed it to be settled,' and it did not say because of being fruitful and multiplying. Being fruitful and multiplying does not apply to a woman, unlike 'He formed it to be settled,' as Tosafot wrote in Gittin 41b. So it appears to me, unlike what was written in Chelkas Mechokek, siman 1, and I elaborated on this in a responsum. The same applies that one may sell a Torah scroll for redeeming captives. Beis Yosef in Yoreh Deah, siman 252, and Shach, unlike the Derishah.
