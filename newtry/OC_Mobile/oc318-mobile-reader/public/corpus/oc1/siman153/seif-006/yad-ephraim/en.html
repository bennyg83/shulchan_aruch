S.k. 22 — and as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 259 seif 2 in the gloss. See what my grandfather the gaon wrote in Torat Chaim on Sanhedrin 21a, etc. — it appears an individual who writes a sefer Torah for himself and gives it to a synagogue to read in public and sanctifies it did not act properly — meaning, since he sanctified it behold it is hekdesh and not his, and he no longer fulfills with it the mitzvah of "for you" — "for you" / "for you" we require, as we find regarding four types, etc. And we cannot say the matter depends on writing alone, etc. — meaning, an individual who wrote a sefer Torah for himself and then lost it — obviously he must write another. Therefore it appears one should not sanctify a sefer Torah unless he writes another for himself — end of his words, see there. And see in responsa Beit Ephraim part 14 siman 62 regarding writing a sefer Torah in partnership whether they fulfill the mitzvah — see there.
