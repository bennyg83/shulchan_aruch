The congregation may not change it, etc.: Reuven had a house in the village, standing many days without going to the village; therefore the villagers prayed in Beit Shimon's house; later Reuven sold his house to Tuvia and the villagers returned to pray in it; Shimon acquired presumptive right of his mitzvah; nevertheless he cannot prevent one coming with presumptive right of Reuven from establishing a prayer place in his house as well (She'elot Teshuvot Maharam Azarya ben Chaim part 2 siman 70).
