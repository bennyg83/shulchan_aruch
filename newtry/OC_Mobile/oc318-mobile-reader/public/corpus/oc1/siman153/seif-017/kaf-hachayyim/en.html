(17) There, gloss: See above, siman 143. Siman 143:4, gloss, permits reading under pressure from one error-free Chumash in a Torah scroll even if other Chumashim contain errors. This implies a scroll usable with a blessing in such circumstances has more sanctity than ordinary Chumashim. Maran disagrees, as explained there in paragraphs 43–44, unlike Olas Tamid's unresolved objection. Pri Megadim says that even if one complete Chumash permits reading when no other scroll exists, an entirely valid Torah scroll may still have higher sanctity, and remains uncertain. Therefore, where possible treat it with Torah-scroll sanctity; where impossible, follow Maran that it has Chumash status.
