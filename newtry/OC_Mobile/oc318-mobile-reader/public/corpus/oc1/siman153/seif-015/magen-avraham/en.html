It is nothing — meaning from the regulation; but by law one can forbid as in Yoreh De'ah siman 224 — see there. It is written in Hagahos Maimoniyot in the name of R' Shmuel: a community that had a box in which they placed tithes and one forbade his portion and permitted them for the rest — end of his words. The difficulty is what he wrote from beit haknesset; possibly what they place in the box was already set aside from his mind and stands to be taken per the will of the community leaders, and it is as not his own to forbid them; not so in beit haknesset that stands forever and each seeks to merit forever in his share; nevertheless it is good to ask about his vow (Yeshuot Shach 5:165); it appears to me tithe is not his own as in mishnah chapter 1 of Nedarim.
