Regarding partners who dedicated a synagogue, a Torah scroll, and its furnishings, and afterward their association dissolved and one wished to retract from the dedication, see Responsa Zera Avraham, siman 27. A beis midrash burned down and the congregation feared that the Ishmaelites would seize the land, as they had taken all the surrounding property. The responsum cited above, siman 113, permitted its sale so that they would not lose everything. The proceeds, however, should be used to purchase other land in its place. If some members of a congregation decided to separate and build themselves a new synagogue, and then demanded a share of the sacred vessels and lamps belonging to the old synagogue, see there, siman 59.
