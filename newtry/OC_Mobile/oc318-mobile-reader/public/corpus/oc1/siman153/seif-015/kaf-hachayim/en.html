(15) There — to lower them from their holiness is forbidden, etc.; and this is when the people of the city sold without the knowledge of their leaders; and the same if the seven good men of the city sold not in the presence of the people of the city; but if the seven good men of the city agreed to that sale and were in the presence of the people of the city, they may spend the money for whatever they want — see Mishnah Berurah.
