Seif katan 21, they built a house, etc. From non-sacred money. For if they collected money for the need of a synagogue, it is explained in seif 4 that even the money may not be changed, and certainly when they built a synagogue from that money.
It does not have synagogue sanctity in all matters, meaning those explained here in siman 151. According to this, it would not have been necessary to say that here the case is where they built it with non-sacred money, for one could say that here the Shulchan Aruch does not speak about changing it, but only about whether it becomes sanctified with the sanctity of a synagogue, and for this it was necessary that they pray in it.
For it is as though he vowed tzedakah. This is the language of the Mordechai brought by the Beis Yosef regarding the law of seif 14: It is no worse than one who says, “I will give to such-and-such a wealthy person,” if his intent was that it be a vow, in which case it becomes a vow; all the more so here, where there are several poor people in the city, it becomes a vow and he cannot retract. End quote.
But if he made a cloth, etc. He can retract. For in such a case, where he did not say he would give it to others, there is no vow. Nevertheless, this requires study, for it involves “his statement to the Most High” even though it does not involve tzedakah. In any event, it is no worse than another mitzvah, as we rule in Yoreh Deah, siman 213, seif 2: one who says, “I will study this chapter,” is as though he vowed to give tzedakah or to do other mitzvos, and his vow stands. The Shach explained that what it says, “to do other mitzvos,” means that when he says he will do other mitzvos, his law is also like one who says “I will study this chapter,” and it is a vow. The Mordechai, who gave the reason of tzedakah, mentioned the stronger reason. In truth, I saw that the Eliyah Rabbah left this law requiring study and did not explain his reason. If the case is where he said nothing at the time he made it, and only his thought was for this, then in such a case why can he not retract with a synagogue? See Orach Chaim.
