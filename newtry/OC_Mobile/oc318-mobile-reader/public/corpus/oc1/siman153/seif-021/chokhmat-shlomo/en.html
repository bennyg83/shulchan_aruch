Seif 21. In the gloss: It is forbidden to make an object used for a mitzvah from the payment of a prostitute or the exchange of a dog. Note: Regarding one who had relations with a woman for whom beis din would impose a capital penalty, whether the payment is permitted, see my work on Bava Kamma, chapter Merubah, in the sugya of “pluck a fig,” etc. There I proved from Rambam's wording that where the act incurs a beis din death penalty, the payment is permitted. See there for the correct reasoning, with Hashem's help, and examine carefully.
