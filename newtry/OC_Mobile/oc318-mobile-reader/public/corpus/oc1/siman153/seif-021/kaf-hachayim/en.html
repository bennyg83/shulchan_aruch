(21) [seif 5] If they collected money for the needs of the synagogue, etc. — and if they collected money to demolish a wall of the synagogue and rebuild it, and afterwards they feared perhaps they would not succeed in building it, it is permitted to make from the money a bathhouse. Rashbetz volume 2 siman 170; and it is derived from two reasons: that designation is not a matter, and it is a mistaken consecration; see there. And as the later authorities wrote, Birkei Yosef section 3.
