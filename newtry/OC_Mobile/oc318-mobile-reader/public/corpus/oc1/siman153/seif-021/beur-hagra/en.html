Seif 21, one may not, etc. As written above in siman 147, seif 1, in the gloss there, and in the Tosefta at the end of the second chapter of Megillah: utensils that were originally made for mundane use may not be made for use on high; stones and beams, etc.
It is forbidden, etc. As stated in Temurah 30, that even beaten plates are forbidden.
And specifically, etc. As stated in the Mishnah there: if he gave her money, etc.; and it is not called, etc., in the Gemara there. And as written in chapter HaBa Al Yevimto, that we do not rule like Rabbi Eliezer. So too wrote the Rambam, who wrote that all those liable for negative commandments are called zonah, but only when the esnan came through a transgression. Tosafot, however, in Temurah 30 and Shiltei Giborim, do not understand it this way, but rather that anyone already called a zonah, even if now she was with one permitted to her, and they wrote that the same applies to a woman who is hefker; see there at length. See Lechem Mishneh and Magen Avraham, and his words are not compelling.
