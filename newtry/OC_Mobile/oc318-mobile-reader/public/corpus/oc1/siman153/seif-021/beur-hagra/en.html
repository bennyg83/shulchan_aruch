Seif 161 — there is not, etc. As explained above siman 147 seif 61 in the gloss there, and in Tosefta chapter 2 of Megillah: vessels that are made from the outset for mundane use — one does not make them for holy use; stones and beams, etc.

Forbidden, etc. — as is written in Temurah 30: that even beaten sheets are forbidden.

And specifically, etc. As written in the mishnah there "he gave her money," etc., and it is not called, etc.; gemara there; and as written in chapter HaBa'al Yachid that we do not hold like R' Eliezer; and so Rambam; and he wrote that all those liable for negative commandments are called zonah, but specifically when the etnan comes through a transgression; but Tosafot's view in Temurah 39 is not so, and it implies rather that whoever is already called zonah, even if she now had relations with one permitted to her; and he wrote that the same applies to a woman left unrestrained — see there at length; and see Choshen Mishpat and Magen Avraham; and his words are not definitive.
