Ordinary use. See Chavos Yair, siman 161. Yad Aharon raises many objections to his words; see there. See also above, siman 147, seif katan 4, and what is written there.
From the hire of a zonah. If he had relations with her and afterward gave it to her, it is permitted. If she gave him a hire, it is permitted. If he gave it to her and said, let it remain with you until I come upon you, when he comes upon her it is forbidden. If she first consecrated it to the synagogue, it is permitted. If he gave a hire to a male, it is forbidden. If he said to a zonah, take this lamb and have relations with so-and-so, it is forbidden. If she made a change, such as wheat and she made it into fine flour, and likewise their offspring, they are permitted. If he stipulated one lamb and gave her many, all are forbidden. See Magen Avraham and see Yad Aharon.
