Taz — behold he holds as written in note 13, that the distinction is between when they have a synagogue (per my method siman 152:1, and per Magen Avraham when they have one and built another with reduced expense) — it is permitted to sell a synagogue and a Torah scroll to learn Torah and marry a woman, and all the more so to redeem captives, as Tosafot Bava Batra wrote, brought by Taz and Shach in Yoreh De'ah 252:1, and Rama in Avodah Berurah there — see there; but when they have only one, they may not sell even for redemption of captives, and all the more so to marry a woman, etc.; and the same applies to a Torah scroll thus — one can say whenever the public has only one, they will cancel its reading, etc.; see siman 132 that negligence applies even in all communal needs; see Megillah 26a negligence, etc. — see there; and therefore to resolve the difficulty of Perishah in Yoreh De'ah 252: at first glance Rama in Avodah Berurah there brought s.v. Bava Batra that they sell a Torah scroll for redemption of captives — he validated a document for two partners; for here in seif 13 the Mechaber was silent for him, for he wrote "they may not sell" in building — implies prohibition, etc.; and all the more so a Torah scroll, which is more stringent than a synagogue (granted Tosafot can say a person's dwelling they do not sell — they should not do so, Taz 252:1; unlike Tur there who wrote prohibition, and the Mechaber's wording here and there implies to him too that it is forbidden) — if so, it is difficult; and per his words one can say: here when they have another, and here when they do not have another; and Taz in Yoreh De'ah 252 answered that the prohibition is on account of proper conduct — a person's dwelling they do not sell, all the more so one should not do so in a synagogue; unlike a Torah scroll, which does not use the term "dwelling," etc. — see there. But Magen Avraham here note 17 wrote see in the gloss, and all this does not deal, etc., that for a severe mitzvah they may sell l'chatchila, sons of the city may sell a synagogue even if they have no other — and so from Ramban that Ran brought regarding a gift Megillah 27b, that he wrote zt"h in the presence of the people of the city, and nevertheless when they have no other synagogue it is forbidden — and this is money to secular use; but sons of the city alone, who elevate to severe sanctity, are permitted even if they have no other synagogue — and so from Tosafot HaRosh he wrote that they sold b'dieved and the mishnah does not teach "they sell," for it is not sufficient that they do not sell and he does not answer except when they have one synagogue, and he began with it; nevertheless he began with its street, etc.; understand, and if so that case of seif 13 to seif 66 is difficult; and one must say as Levush and Shach in Yoreh De'ah 252:1 wrote, that certainly if they do not sell, the mitzvah will be nullified — they may sell synagogue and Torah scroll to marry and to learn, and all the more so redemption of captives; unlike in seif 13 they collect from the public, for there is ability, etc.; and see in Magen Avraham note 9, it will be from this.
