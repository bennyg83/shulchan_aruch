Taz, seif katan 1. And in the Gemara there. All these words of his until the word “rather” all continue the question. Meaning, in truth, in the Gemara it wanted to prove this from the Mishnah and distinguishes between initially and after the fact. Consequently, regarding other sanctities, where its after-the-fact case is also like initially, since one can elevate and purchase a more stringent sanctity, we certainly should be precise, etc. If so, why did Mahari Abuhav not prove this from the Mishnah? This is the intent of the Taz. Afterward, when he writes “rather,” etc., he begins to answer this second question.
