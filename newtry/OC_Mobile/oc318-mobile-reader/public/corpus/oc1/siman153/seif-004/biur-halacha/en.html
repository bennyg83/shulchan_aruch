There are those who forbid. See Mishnah Berurah. It is obvious that this speaks where they have another synagogue in which to pray, for otherwise it is more proper that they purchase a synagogue so that they have a fixed place to pray, as I wrote in Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 3. See there.
And there are those who permit. See Peri Megadim, who leans that this is the halacha, since the Mechaber wrote this permissive view at the end, and it is a D'Rabbanan matter. But in Meiri I saw that he wrote that most of the Geonim hold that it specifically requires an elevation in sanctity.
