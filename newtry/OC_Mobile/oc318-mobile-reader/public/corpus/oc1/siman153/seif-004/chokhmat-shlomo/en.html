(Seif 21) In the gloss: forbidden to make from a prostitute's fee or a dog's price a matter of mitzvah. Note: behold, if one had relations with those liable to death by court — whether the fee is permitted; and see in my composition on Bava Kamma in chapter Merubah in the sugya of "Akutz Te'enah," etc., what I wrote to prove from Rambam's wording that one who had relations with those liable to death by court — the fee is permitted — see there; his reason, for it is correct, with God's help; and investigate.
