Permit. These opinions discuss other sacred items, but regarding a Torah scroll the Shulchan Aruch rules at the beginning of the laws of Torah scrolls in Yoreh Deah that it is forbidden to sell one in order to buy another with its proceeds. See there, and see Magen Avraham. The author of Yad Aharon challenged him. The Taz wrote that in our times we see many people sell sefarim and buy others with their proceeds; apparently, when they bought them originally, they had this in mind, even if they did not state it explicitly. See also Eliyah Rabbah, who challenges the later authorities on this matter.
