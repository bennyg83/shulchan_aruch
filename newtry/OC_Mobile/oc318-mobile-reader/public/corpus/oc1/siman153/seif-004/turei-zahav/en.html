Another sanctity of the same level. Regarding a Torah scroll, Rama ruled at the beginning of chapter 5 of Hilchot Sefer Torah that it is forbidden, since one cannot elevate it, even though it is impossible in the world to elevate it — nevertheless it is forbidden; and this is an unresolved question that was not resolved in the gemara. And if so, in other sanctities where elevation is possible, it is obvious it is forbidden to sell in order to buy another of the same level; so Ran wrote. But Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari Avuhav wrote: from that which Tur wrote "but conversely to take them down from their sanctity" — it does not imply that with "another of the same level" it is permitted; and this is "there are those who permit," and I cannot understand Mahari Avuhav's view, nor Shulchan Aruch's view that brings it; for if he refers to Mahari Avuhav on a Torah scroll too, and permits buying another Torah scroll — difficult, for it is an unresolved question, and we rule stringently: old in order to buy new; and if you say he holds leniently, as Yoreh De'ah beginning of chapter 5 Sefer Torah in the name of R' Menachem — difficult, for Tur wrote there for prohibition, and how does he infer here from his words that with "another of the same level" it is permitted; and also difficult on Shulchan Aruch, for he ruled for prohibition in Yoreh De'ah regarding a Torah scroll, and here wrote a dispute. And if we say Mahari Avuhav refers to other sanctities, and regarding a Torah scroll he agrees to prohibition, and Shulchan Aruch deals here only with other sanctities, not a Torah scroll — difficult, for all the more so in other sanctities it is forbidden, since one can elevate, as Ran wrote; and further, why did Mahari Avuhav infer more from Tur's words than from the mishnah, for in the mishnah we also say "books — one does not buy a wrapper," implying that with "another of the same level" it is permitted; and in the gemara there it seeks to resolve the question of old Torah scroll to buy new, from that which we taught "but a Torah scroll one does not buy," etc.
