These words are very astonishing, for Magen Avraham himself later note 33 wrote it is a mere stringency, but by law it is permitted. And even if we say Magen Avraham's intent from Rambam's wording chapter 11 Hilchot Tefillah, that he wrote "they wished to sell," etc., implies even no stringency exists, and on this he wrote to distinguish that later it deals when they have no other synagogue. But it is really difficult for me, for Magen Avraham per his method in siman 152 note 1, his view is that even if there is a synagogue it is forbidden, not like Taz there — see there; and so it is also clear from Magen Avraham's view in this siman note 16; investigate — behold the sugya in Bava Batra deals even when they have another synagogue, and nevertheless it is forbidden to sell; and requires study.
