Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. Meaning, when they have no other synagogue. The words are very astonishing, for the Magen Avraham himself below, seif katan 33, writes that it is merely a stringency, but by law it is permitted. Even if we say that the Magen Avraham's intent is that from the language of the Rambam, Laws of Tefillah 11, who wrote, they wanted to sell, etc., it implies that there is not even a stringency, and regarding this he wrote to distinguish that below it speaks where they have no other synagogue, and so it appears from the words of the Magen Avraham, seif katan 17, where his words seem to mean that he brings proof that for stricter sanctity it is permitted to sell even when they have no other synagogue from that sugya in Bava Basra, which is the law of seif 13, implying that it is not proper to do so, for we say that a person's dwelling is not sold, which implies that this is not the normal way, but there is no prohibition, and there it speaks where there is no other synagogue; see there. But in truth this is difficult to me, for the Magen Avraham follows his own view in siman 152, seif katan 1, that even where there is another synagogue it is forbidden, unlike the Taz there; see there. This is also clearly the view of the Magen Avraham in this siman, seif katan 16; examine it carefully. Thus the sugya in Bava Basra speaks even where they have another synagogue, and nevertheless it is forbidden to sell. This requires study.
