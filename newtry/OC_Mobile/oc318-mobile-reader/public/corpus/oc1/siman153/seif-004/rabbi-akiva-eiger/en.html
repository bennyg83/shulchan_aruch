Magen Avraham s.k. 4 — meaning when they have no other beit haknesset. The words are very puzzling, for Magen Avraham himself below s.k. 33 writes it is a mere stringency but by law it is permitted. And even if we say Magen Avraham's intent from Rambam's language chapter 1 of Laws of Prayer that they wanted to sell, etc., implying even no stringency — he wrote to distinguish that below it deals when they have no other beit haknesset. And so from Magen Avraham s.k. 17 it appears clearly from his words there that he brings proof that for weightier holiness it is permitted to sell even when they have no other beit haknesset, from that sugya in Bava Batra which is this law of seif 13 implying it is not proper to do so, for we say a person's residence is not sold implying it is not customary; but there is no prohibition, and there it deals when there is no other beit haknesset — see there. But truly it is difficult for me, for Magen Avraham per my method in siman 152 s.k. 1 holds that even if there is a beit haknesset it is forbidden, not like Taz there — see there; and so is also clear Magen Avraham's view in this siman s.k. 16; examine — behold the sugya in Bava Batra deals even when they have another beit haknesset and nevertheless forbidden to sell; it requires study.
