Name from GA s.k. MT and their words. P.S. And it appears to reconcile their words: meaning in other appointments the first does not return at all; and so if the first high priest returns to his work, the second will not resent it if he too serves, since in other appointments the first has nothing; but R' Yossi in all appointments judged most that the first returns, so they resented — therefore halachic ruling was needed; see responsa Chasam Sofer Orach Chayyim simanim 12–13.
