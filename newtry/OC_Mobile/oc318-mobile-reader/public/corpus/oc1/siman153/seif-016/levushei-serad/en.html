Taz, seif katan 2. It implies that in any event. From the language “one lowers” that he wrote, and also from the proof he later brings from the Shulchan Aruch, which is from the language “one changes.” So too it further implies from what the Mechaber writes nearby, “it is permitted to lower the surplus,” and he did not write “it is permitted to use the surplus for whatever they want.”
