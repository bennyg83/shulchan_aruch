Seif 16, one who, etc. This too is from his enactments.
And specifically, etc. So he learned from the language, that he did not enact in such a case. As written in the ninth chapter of Bava Metzia, where we expound common language, and as Tosafot wrote in the sixth chapter of Eruvin on the Mishnah, s.v. chavurot, regarding a teacher or scribe, that in such a case he did not lend it to them.
