There, seif katan 12. And if they are not. Note: this ruling requires study, and he contradicts himself from what he wrote in seif katan 17. I looked inside the Mabit, and it has no relevance here at all. There, they had already left that synagogue and had made it into a dwelling for many years, unlawfully, and now it was permitted to sell it after the act had already been done. See there. See also Responsa Chatam Sofer, Orach Chaim, siman 31. There he was asked about a synagogue that they demolished after seeing a defect in it, and now they wanted to build a new synagogue nearby, close to the place of the old ruin, in such a way that the old place would be a courtyard for the new synagogue, and the eastern wall where the bimah and aron kodesh had stood would now become the entrance to the new one, whether it was proper to do so or not. In his responsum he concluded: since this place is not being sold, it has no remedy except either actually to build a synagogue and expand the place to its height, or to make a fence or some enclosure around it; for to make it a synagogue courtyard is a lowering of sanctity. Moreover, in a synagogue courtyard people commonly do degrading things and treat the holy place lightly, for they urinate there. Therefore, it is impossible without fencing that place around.
