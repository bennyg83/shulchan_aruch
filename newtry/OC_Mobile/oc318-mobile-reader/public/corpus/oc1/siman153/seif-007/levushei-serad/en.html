There. Regarding a Torah scroll we adopt. It seems to me that the words “regarding a Torah scroll” should read “the question,” and it continues what he just concluded, where he wrote, “and so is the opinion of Mahari Abuhav.” Meaning, there is no dispute, for the Ran who forbids is speaking initially, and the Tur who permits is speaking after the fact. About this he wrote that so too is the opinion of Mahari Abuhav, specifically to permit after the fact. But the question, which speaks initially, Mahari Abuhav agrees that we adopt the stringent view, whether regarding a Torah scroll or all matters. So it appears to me, and this is easy to understand. Therefore the words “regarding a Torah scroll” have no explanation at all, and must be emended as I wrote.
