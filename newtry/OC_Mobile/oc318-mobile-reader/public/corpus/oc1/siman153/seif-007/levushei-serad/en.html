(there) Regarding a Torah scroll we are stringent. It appears to me the word "regarding a Torah scroll" should read "that was asked" (ha'iba'i), and it continues from his dismissal, for he wrote "and so Mahari Avuhav" — meaning there is no dispute: Ran who forbids means l'chatchila, and Tur who permits means b'dieved; and on this basis he wrote that Mahari Avuhav specifically permits b'dieved, but regarding the question that deals with l'chatchila, Mahari Avuhav agrees we are stringent; and this applies both regarding a Torah scroll and regarding all matters — so it appears to me, and requires study. Therefore the word "regarding a Torah scroll" has no explanation at all and must be emended as above.
