(50) To change it, etc. — This follows Rashba; and apparently per Magen Avraham we do not establish minor holiness in its light — specifically when it is forbidden to change entirely even to weightier holiness [such as when the dedicator commanded thus], then proclamation is required; but if they have permission to change to weightier holiness, such as through the seven good men of the city not in the presence of the people of the city, as explained, then it is considered as their own and no proclamation is required; and truly Rashba's intent is proven, for he hangs the reason on one can give it as a gift, and he holds in his novellae in chapter Bnei HaIr that for a gift we require only the seven good men alone, and so too Ramban. However there is a difficulty: per this, per Magen Avraham's own ruling below s.k. 26 that for a gift one needs specifically seven good men in the presence of the people, it follows that for sale through seven good men not in the presence of the people proclamation should be required; and it is possible Magen Avraham relied mainly on Ran's reason cited in s.k. 18, that we find proclamation only in a matter incumbent on the court, such as the court selling to collect for others; but in a matter we do of our own accord without needing the court, proclamation was not mentioned — end of his words; and this implies clearly that seven good men alone also do not require proclamation, for they are the people of the city themselves and sell on their own; and so too Tur and Rama in Choshen Mishpat siman 103 s.k. 1 in the gloss. And truly it is explained in Ran and other rishonim that at minimum seven good men alone are not weaker than all the people of the city without seven good men; and if we say that through seven good men alone proclamation is required because it is forbidden to change to minor holiness, then if all the people of the city sold without seven good men proclamation should also be required, for they too may not change to minor holiness — and this is unreasonable that the people of the city need proclamation on their own; rather it does not depend on this: the sages were stringent regarding sanctity not to lighten for them or their money unless the people of the city agree in the presence of their leaders — nevertheless this is not a reason to require proclamation for sellers on their own to upgrade holiness; and likewise regarding seven good men who are the core of the people of the city — the sages decreed not to annul holiness unless all the people of the city stand at annulment, but from this there is no proof regarding sale money; and it appears Rashba agrees with Ran's reason but did not need it for his view regarding gift; we elaborated because I saw R' Shmuel objects to Magen Avraham and forces our version to require proclamation when forbidden to change to minor holiness; I disagree — the essence is as Magen Avraham and as we wrote.
