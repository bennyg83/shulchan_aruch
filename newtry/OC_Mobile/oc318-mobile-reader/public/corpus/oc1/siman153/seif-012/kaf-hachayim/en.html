(12) There. If they sold a cloth wrapper, one buys with its proceeds sefarim — meaning each chumash by itself, and likewise Nevi'im and Ketuvim; and so Levush wrote. And it implies he holds that chumashim, Nevi'im, and Ketuvim are of equal status. This requires further study, for in Yoreh De'ah siman 282 seif 19 it states that chumashim have more severe kedushah than Nevi'im and Ketuvim, and so is explicit in the gemara daf 27a; therefore it appears to me it is forbidden to buy Nevi'im and Ketuvim with the proceeds of chumashim, but Nevi'im and Ketuvim are equal to each other, as written there in Yoreh De'ah in the gloss; Magen Avraham s.k. 2. Avnei Nezer section 4 wrote that also Shulchan Aruch and Levush spoke specifically regarding a Torah scroll wrapper, that they are equal, for even Nevi'im and Ketuvim are like chumashim, for they are more sacred than a Torah scroll wrapper; and with the proceeds of Nevi'im and Ketuvim it is forbidden [to buy] a wrapper for a Torah scroll. But regarding chumashim versus Nevi'im and Ketuvim, chumashim are more sacred, and this is clear — end of his words. And so too HaNei Shalom section 1, Ta'ut on Magen Avraham s.k. 2.
