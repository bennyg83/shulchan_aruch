Magen Avraham, seif katan 5. And it is forbidden to change it. This answer of the Magen Avraham is not like the answer of the Taz in seif katan 2, for the Taz explained that “one may not change” means with respect to not building a synagogue at all, but as a loan it is permitted. According to the Magen Avraham, even as a loan it is forbidden, for the intent of the townspeople is that a mitzvah be done specifically with this money that they gave.
