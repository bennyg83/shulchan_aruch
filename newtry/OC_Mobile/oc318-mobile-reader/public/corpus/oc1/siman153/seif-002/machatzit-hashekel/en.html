Seif katan 2, and so, etc. It implies, etc., that they are equal to each other, for if Chumashim were more stringent, it should have said: if they sold books, namely Prophets and Writings, they should buy Chumashim; if they sold Chumashim, they should buy a Torah scroll.
And it requires study, etc. For Chumashim have a stricter sanctity, as he wrote that it is forbidden to place Prophets and Writings on top of Chumashim.
In the name of Tosafot and the Ran. Tosafot wrote that it is permitted to place them one upon the other, and the Ran added that they are also equal regarding purchase. The Beis Yosef brought this in siman 591, that on Rosh Hashanah one must say ten verses of Malchuyos, and likewise Zichronos and Shofaros, and it is explained in the Talmud that one must begin with Torah, meaning to say three verses from the Torah, and likewise conclude the tenth verse with Torah, while the six middle verses are three from Prophets and three from Writings. The practice of the world is to precede with three verses from Writings. The Ran wrote the reason: since one must begin with Torah, afterward it is proper to ascend from sanctity to sanctity. Therefore he begins with Writings, then ascends in sanctity to say three verses from Prophets, and afterward ascends in sanctity to say one verse from Torah.
Or to buy, etc. This is according to the “some say” in seif 4.
Only that it is permitted because we say it is impossible. In Megillah 27 they say that it is permitted to place a Torah scroll on top of another Torah scroll, since it is impossible otherwise. For if you do not say so, even with one Torah scroll, how may we roll it? One page rests upon the other. Rather, it is because it is impossible. See its explanation in the words of the Magen Avraham, siman 28, and in what is written there in the name of the Rashba.
And it implies from the Ran in Megillah that he retracted, etc. According to the Ran in Rosh Hashanah, it is forbidden to place Writings on top of Prophets, and likewise regarding purchase, unlike what he wrote in Megillah.
Only that we give precedence because David, etc. This reason was written by the Rosh, and it is also brought in the Beis Yosef, siman 591; see there.
