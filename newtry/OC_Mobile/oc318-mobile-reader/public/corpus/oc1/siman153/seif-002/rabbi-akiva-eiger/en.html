In the AD — therefore it appears to me forbidden to take, etc., with ak; even per those who permit buying a sefer Torah to purchase other holiness like it, nevertheless this is forbidden as lowering from holiness. But truly there is clear proof to permit, for it is explained in Megillah daf 27 that it is permitted to roll in a handkerchief for chumashim — see in that sugya that they compared them to one another for the matter of sale; and per Ran who forbids buying like it, and where there is upgrading — certainly forbidden to roll in a handkerchief; therefore here where it is permitted to roll chumashim, which is upgrading, it is also permitted for sale as it appears to me.
