Note: and in the mishnah too it is difficult that it did not mention the distinction between chumashim and books; and likewise wondered in Tur Even there. And in my humble opinion it appears there are three distinctions. One: regarding saying the verses — words of prophets are holier, as Ran wrote in Rosh Hashanah; however, in any case regarding the sanctity of the book there is no distinction at all, for that holiness with which the parchment is sanctified through words of prophets is also sanctified through Writings; and for this reason Ran wrote in chapter Bnei HaIr that a wrapper of prophets and Writings is one. However, chumashim — the parchment too is holier, for it is fit to complete it to a complete Torah; and all the more so a complete Torah scroll, only lacking one letter that can be repaired; and therefore the baraita forbids rolling in a wrapper of chumashim; and all this regarding a wrapper; however, to sell and buy — certainly it is one, for there is no distinction between one engaged in the Torah of Moses or in books of prophets and Writings, and even only in commentators on the Gemara; for this reason it did not distinguish in the mishnah at the beginning of chapter Bnei HaIr at all regarding sale between chumashim and books; and nevertheless in a complete Torah scroll there is another holiness to read in public, and further a positive mitzvah of "and now write for yourselves" — certainly it is elevated in its reading from chumashim and books to a complete Torah scroll; and this is clear and settles all the difficulties. And a similar distinction must be said also in selling a synagogue: it is lenient — meaning selling the house when there is no cancellation of prayer; but without doubt it is permitted to sell a wrapper for the need of prayer with ten, to sanctify the Name of Heaven with ten; and for this reason, when there is no other synagogue, it is forbidden to sell, as Rambam wrote, whom Magen Avraham brings at the end of siman 101; and investigate all this.
