There, Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. And it requires study. Note: in the Mishnah as well it is difficult that it did not mention the distinction between Chumashim and other books, and the Turei Even there also wondered about this. In my humble opinion, it appears that there are three distinctions. First, regarding reciting verses, the words of the Prophets are more sacred, as the Ran wrote in Rosh Hashanah. Nevertheless, with respect to the sanctity of the book, there is no distinction, for the same sanctity with which the parchment becomes sanctified through words of the Prophets also becomes sanctified through Writings, for what difference is there? Therefore the Ran wrote in chapter Bnei HaIr that a covering for Prophets and Writings has one status. Chumashim, however, have greater sanctity even in the parchment, since it is fit to complete them into a whole Torah, and certainly a complete Torah scroll that is missing only one letter, which can be repaired. Therefore the baraisa forbids wrapping with a covering of Chumashim. All this is regarding a covering. But regarding selling and buying, this and that are one, for there is no distinction between one who occupies himself with the Torah of Moshe Rabbeinu or with books of the Prophets and Writings, and even only with commentaries on the Talmud. Therefore the Mishnah at the beginning of Bnei HaIr did not distinguish at all regarding sale between Chumashim and other books. Nevertheless, a complete Torah scroll, which has an additional sanctity for public reading and also the positive commandment of “and now write for yourselves,” is certainly elevated beyond Chumashim and books by reading from a complete Torah scroll. This is clear, and all the difficulties are resolved. A similar distinction must also be said regarding the sale of a synagogue, that when it is considered lighter, that means selling the building where there is no cancellation of prayer. But without doubt it is permitted to sell a covering for the sake of prayer with ten, to sanctify the Name of Heaven with ten. Therefore, when there is no other synagogue, it is forbidden to sell, as the Rambam wrote, cited by the Magen Avraham at the end of seif katan 101. Examine all this carefully.
