There in Magen Avraham s.k. 2 and 12. P.S. And in Matani' there is also a difficulty why he did not mention the division between chumashim and books as Taz in Tur there. And it appears there are three divisions: (1) regarding reading verses — words of prophets are holier per Ran in Beit HaRif; (2) regarding sanctity of the book there is no difference whether sanctified by prophets' words or Scriptures, and therefore Mechaber wrote in chapter Bnei HaIr that prophets and Scriptures are one; (3) chumashim are also sacred since they can be completed to a full Torah, and it appears the entire Shas lacks only one letter repairable, and the Gemara forbids wrapping chumashim in a handkerchief; and regarding selling there is no difference between Torah scroll dealers and prophets/Scriptures books except Shas commentaries where he did not divide — all difficulties resolve with this division; and similarly regarding beit haknesset sale when there is no cancellation of prayer but selling for prayer at need when no other beit haknesset exists is forbidden per Rambam as Magen Avraham s.k. 1 and 27 bring.
