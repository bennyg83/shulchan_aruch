There. “Therefore Beit Yosef wrote, ‘some permit.’” The Shulchan Aruch’s statement, “and it is permitted to read,” etc., means that one may sell with the intention of purchasing another object of equivalent sanctity. Beit Yosef therefore writes “some permit,” referring to Rambam’s view that this is permitted even initially. If the object was already sold, everyone agrees that one may purchase another object of equivalent sanctity.
