(2) There: A synagogue may be converted into a study hall, etc. Magen Avraham says siman 90:18 implies a synagogue may become an individual's home study hall; Kol HaRamaz agrees. Eliyah Rabbah says its sanctity is apparently diminished. Beis Yosef, siman 151, says an individual fixing a study place in his home for personal use certainly gives it less sanctity, implying it is below a synagogue. Pischei HaDevir cites Meiri on Megillah, rejects Magen Avraham, and agrees with Eliyah Rabbah; he also defends Eliyah Rabbah from Bigdei Yesha. Maharsha, Taavah LaEinayim at the end of Megillah 244, explicitly says a synagogue is superior to an individual's Torah-study house. Chukos Olam on Magen Avraham and Tosefes Chaim agree.
