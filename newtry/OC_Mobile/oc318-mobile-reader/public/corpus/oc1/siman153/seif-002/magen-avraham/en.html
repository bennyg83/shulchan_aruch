And likewise Prophets and Writings. It implies that he holds that Chumashim, Prophets, and Writings are all equivalent. This requires study, for in Yoreh Deah, siman 282, seif 19, it is stated that the sanctity of Chumashim is more severe than that of Prophets and Writings, and so it is explicit in the Gemara, page 27. The Olas Tamid did not look there. Therefore it appears to me that it is forbidden to use the proceeds of Chumashim to purchase Prophets and Writings, while Prophets and Writings are equivalent to one another, as written there in Yoreh Deah in the gloss, in the name of Tosafot and the Ran. It requires study, for the Ran himself wrote that the sanctity of Prophets is more severe than the sanctity of Writings, as the Beis Yosef brought in siman 591, see there. Perhaps nevertheless it is not so severe that it would be forbidden to place one upon the other or to buy one with the proceeds of the other. Yet this requires study, for even regarding an item whose sanctity is equal, the Gemara wanted to say that it is forbidden to place one upon the other, and it is permitted only because it is impossible. If so, regarding something that has an aspect of greater severity, why should it be permitted? See what I wrote in seif katan 8. It implies that the Ran in Megillah retracted from what he wrote in tractate Rosh Hashanah and holds that they are equivalent; only Writings are given precedence because David preceded the Prophets.
