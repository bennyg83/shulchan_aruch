That they sold, etc. Even initially it is also permitted to sell in order to purchase something of higher sanctity, as is implied in seif 6. The reason it says “that they sold” is because of the latter clause.
All the stringency, etc. For a Chumash in codex form and a Chumash made as a scroll are equal, as the Olas Tamid wrote. See above in siman 143, where it is clear that our Chumashim, if the five Chumashim are together, are inferior to one Chumash made according to the proper form of a Torah scroll.
And likewise Prophets, etc. The Olas Tamid wrote that it seems from here that Chumash, Prophets, and Writings are equal. This is difficult from Megillah 27, where it is clear that it is forbidden to buy Prophets and Writings with the money from Chumashim, but with the money from Prophets and Writings one may buy Chumashim, and so ruled the Magen Avraham. One must say that the Shulchan Aruch and Levush spoke specifically regarding a Torah scroll covering, that they are equal in that even Prophets and Writings are like Chumashim, for they are more sacred than a Torah scroll covering. With the money from Prophets and Writings, it is forbidden to buy a covering for a Torah scroll. But regarding Chumashim, Prophets, and Writings themselves, Chumashim are more sacred, and this is clear. The Ran wrote that Prophets and Writings are equal in every respect. Regarding what the Magen Avraham wrote about this, see Tosafot Yom Tov, chapter 4 of Rosh Hashanah.
