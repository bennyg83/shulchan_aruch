As a gift. This means the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople; in large towns it is forbidden. Magen Avraham.
Like a sale. But to give it to a distant city, where they certainly have no benefit from them, is forbidden. The Mishnas Binyamin wrote that if they built a new synagogue and demolished the old one by the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople, the sanctity leaves the old one and takes effect upon the new one, and they may change the old one for whatever they wish, for it is no worse than a gift. But without this, even the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople have no power to use them for a mundane matter, for there is no other thing upon which their sanctity can take effect. See Magen Avraham.
