(11) There. If they sold a cloth wrapper, one buys with its proceeds sefarim, etc. — even [if they sold] wrappers of Torah scrolls and chumashim, they may buy with them Nevi'im and Ketuvim; but in the latter case, if [they sold] sefarim, they do not buy wrappers — even with Nevi'im and Ketuvim one does not buy wrappers of Torah scrolls and chumashim; Ran in the name of the Yerushalmi, brought in Beit Yosef. And Beit Yehuda, Orach Chayyim 54:27 wrote that the same applies if they sold wrappers — they buy tefillin, and this is an elevation in holiness, for tefillin are equal to other sefarim; but to make from wrappers of a Torah scroll pouches for tefillin is obviously forbidden, for it is a lowering; see there.
