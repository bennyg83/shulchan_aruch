And if so, Taz — Rama ruled like both answers of Beit Yosef: if they came into the treasurer's hand, sanctity applies to that money and it is forbidden to take it down, even if he gives others afterward; and also before they came into the treasurer's hand he must fulfill his vow in any case. And Taz asked: for what purpose did Rama write regarding stones, etc., that a fortiori it is from money; and Beit Yosef by way of a fortiori said that which regarding building a house (in seif 8) through donation only wood, and they did not come to the treasurer — for otherwise a fortiori from collected money that came to the treasurer. And behold one can say it is not a fortiori, for one can say money is like a weaver, as Tosafot Sanhedrin 48a s.v. mutar wrote, and therefore designation like this when they come to the treasurer is effective, unlike stones that are inferior, like spinning for a weaver that lacks building and is not designation at all — and I would say even if they came to the treasurer, sanctity does not apply to them; he teaches us this. And what he asked from Megillah 27a — if even per Taz's method their vow is not fulfilled, etc. — if so, one can say when they come into the treasurer's hand we need stipulation on what is permitted; and if so, what does it ask — perhaps Rava deals without coming into the treasurer's hand, only they collected among themselves and did not hand to the treasurer, that the money itself one may not change to fulfill his vow, and what is permitted one may change; and when they came into the treasurer's hand, one can say we need stipulation; and if so, it is difficult for Rama who ruled like both changes of Beit Yosef — what does it ask, etc.; and if so, Rava even when they came to the treasurer changes what is permitted, since he does not establish it, and likewise in their permitted thus, and he establishes only in "if they sold," etc. — one can say, and so it implies that permitted equals the body of the property, and behold regarding permitted stipulation helps — we learn it deals in "if they sold," etc.; only we learn there is no elevation at all from coming to the treasurer. And Avodah Berurah note 8, and per his words it is fine; understand this, and it will be explained further in Magen Avraham.
