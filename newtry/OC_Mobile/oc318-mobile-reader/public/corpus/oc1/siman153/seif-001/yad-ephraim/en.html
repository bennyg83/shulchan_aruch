In Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. “And this is also Mahari’s view.” The passage beginning with the word “However, regarding a Sefer Torah,” through “in siman 270” belongs below, after the words, “Therefore Beit Yosef wrote, ‘and some permit.’” The reference “siman 74” is a scribal error and should read “siman 270.” The correct text is: “Therefore Beit Yosef wrote, ‘and some permit.’ However, regarding a Sefer Torah,” etc., “in siman 270.
