Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. And see siman 153, seif 2. Meaning, there it is proven that it is forbidden, for it is explained there that everything forbidden in a synagogue is permitted in an individual’s beis midrash. Thus it is proven that the sanctity of an individual’s beis midrash is lighter than that of a synagogue. By contrast, in siman 90 it implies that it is permitted, for the sanctity of a beis midrash is more stringent than that of a synagogue, since it is explained there that it is a greater mitzvah to pray in it than in a synagogue.
