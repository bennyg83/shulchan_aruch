Seif katan 1, it is permitted, etc. See siman 151, seif 2, where it implies that a private beis midrash has a weaker sanctity than the sanctity of a synagogue, for it is explained there that some say that in a private beis midrash there is more room for leniency to use it than to use a synagogue. If so, it is forbidden to make a synagogue into a private beis midrash, for that is a lowering of sanctity.
And in siman 90, seif 18, it implies that it is permitted, for he wrote that it is preferable to pray in a fixed beis midrash than in a synagogue. This implies that the sanctity of a beis midrash is greater than that of a synagogue. Since he wrote this plainly in siman 90, it implies that he speaks even of a private beis midrash. So too it appears from the proof that the Beis Yosef brought there in siman 90 for that law, for from the proof it implies even one belonging to an individual; see there.
