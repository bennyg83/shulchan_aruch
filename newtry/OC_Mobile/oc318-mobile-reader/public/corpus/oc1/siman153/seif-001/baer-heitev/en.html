Beis Midrash. In siman 90, seif 18, it implies that it is permitted to make a synagogue into a beis midrash for an individual in his home. See Magen Avraham.
Synagogue. This applies specifically to a synagogue in which they do not establish regular study. Our synagogues, however, where a fixed study session is held, even for one hour, have the law of a beis midrash, and it is permitted to make such a synagogue into a place of study. This is from the responsum Devar Shmuel, siman 346.
