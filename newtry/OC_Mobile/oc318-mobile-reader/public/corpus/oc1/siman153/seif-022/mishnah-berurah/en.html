(1) (א) (Kiv) that he is accustomed - RL who held it with the permission of the audience [and in the answer of Radbaz 44 SIA he wrote that even he did not permit it in the interpretation, but all that held it for a long time and kept silent and did not move is a possession]:
(2) (ב) (Kig) There was satisfaction - we mean that Onso or his poor also had satisfaction in his hand to give as usual:
(3) (ג) (Kidd) And we will be satisfied, etc. - not exactly we will be satisfied in the interpretation, but anyone who has satisfaction in his hand and does not wish to give has lost his right and will resort to be satisfied because of a sipa:
(4) (ד) (Keto) goes back to its purpose - and it should not be changed even from OT to OT unless there is a complete argument for the appellant. But if there is clear evidence that we have already given up on it, the presumption is lost. The arbitrators wrote that a pensioner who passed away because of rape and appointed another under him Dakhshifor Onsu returns to his livelihood and the second is not to be given any share in the pension because of an initial enmity and that the second is not to be treated with contempt and they will not give him any honorary appointment due to accumulated needs. If he was removed from his office because of an offense that he committed even by mistake, he does not return to his office as in the case of a murderer who exiled himself to the city of refuge and then returns to his office that he had before [MA] and the Rabbi is satisfied with this.
