Seif 10, some say, etc. For in what is his own, he is like the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople regarding communal property, as written in seif 7, and the same applies to all matters, etc.
And there is one who, etc. As stated in the Gemara there. The Rivash wrote in siman 286 that the Rambam holds that they said this only regarding a synagogue, and not regarding other sacred items, since they cannot lower them themselves to a lesser sanctity, unlike a synagogue, as written in seif 9, and all the more so according to the reading of the texts that read, even to drink beer in it and to spread fruit in it, which refers to a synagogue. Nevertheless, the law of seif 7 is also true, as stated in the above Tosefta, and also since it permits a gift, which is an exchange for a benefit already received. The first view holds that what is stated, he may not sell a Torah scroll, etc., refers to one belonging to the public, for upon this the question there is based, and regarding this they said: come and hear, etc. So too wrote the Mordechai in the name of Raavyah.
