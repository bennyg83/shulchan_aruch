Seif 10 — there are those who say an individual in his own [beit haknesset] — in responsa Ra'ananach part 2 siman 64 he doubts whether one may say selling a sefer Torah is forbidden only to one commanded regarding its writing; but a woman who is not commanded regarding writing, perhaps she is not included in the prohibition of sale — end of his words.
