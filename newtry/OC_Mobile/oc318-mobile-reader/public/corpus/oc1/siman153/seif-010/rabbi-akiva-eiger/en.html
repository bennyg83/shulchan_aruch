Seif 10. There are those who say that an individual with his own property: in responsa Raanach, part 2, siman 64, he is uncertain. One can say that the prohibition against selling a Torah scroll was said only regarding a man, who is commanded in writing it. But a woman, who is not commanded in writing it, is perhaps not included in the prohibition against selling it. This is his language.
Magen Avraham, seif katan 38. Meaning, because of the enactment. See responsa Maamar Mordechai, simanim 3 and 4.
