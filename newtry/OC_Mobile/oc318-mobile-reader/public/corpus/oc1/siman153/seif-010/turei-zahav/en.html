And there is one who forbids. This is Rambam, as Beit Yosef explains. It appears to me that if the person originally acquired the Sefer Torah in order to resell it, and certainly if he accepted it in payment of a debt, he may sell it. Otherwise, a scribe too would be forbidden to sell a Sefer Torah. This case is comparable, since it did not come into his possession in order to remain his personal Sefer Torah.
