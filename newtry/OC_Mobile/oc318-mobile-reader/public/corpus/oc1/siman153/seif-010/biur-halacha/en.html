And there are those who forbid, etc. — see Magen Avraham that this refers only to a sefer Torah and its tashmish; and in Beur HaGra it appears the one who forbids is Rambam's view that even sacred tashmishim may not have their money taken for secular use except through seven good men in the presence of the people, and only for beit haknesset was this permitted; and Peri Megadim holds one cannot attribute this to Rambam — see there; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 282 seif 17 Rama ruled likewise that through seven good men in the presence of the people one may take money of sold sacred tashmishim for secular use, and did not bring disputants; as he brought in seif 18 it appears all agree it is permitted.
