And there is one who forbids, etc. See Magen Avraham, that this applies only to a Torah scroll and not to its accessories. But from the Beur HaGra it implies that according to the one who forbids, which is the view of the Rambam, even regarding accessories of sanctity it is also forbidden to spend the money for a mundane matter, even through the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople, and only regarding a synagogue was this permitted. The Peri Megadim holds that we cannot attribute this to the Rambam's view; see there. In Yoreh Deah, siman 282, seif 17, he also ruled that through the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople we may take the money of sold accessories of sanctity out to mundane use, and he did not bring differing views there as he brought later in seif 18. This implies that he holds that in this everyone agrees it is permitted.
