(18) [seif 4] whether it is permitted to buy, etc. — some forbid and some permit. The opinion of those who forbid is the opinion of Ran that Beit Yosef brought, who wrote that since we ask in the Gemara regarding a sefer Torah that there is no upgrading in it, we infer that in the other [objects] where upgrading is possible, it is forbidden to sell them and buy with their money something of the same holiness; and the opinion of those who permit is that of Rambam and Tur — see there at length.
