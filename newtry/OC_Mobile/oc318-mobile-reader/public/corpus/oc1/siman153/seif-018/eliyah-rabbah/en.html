It is forbidden to sell it, etc. This is the language of the Magen Avraham: unless they want to buy another with the money in a manner where there is no concern for negligence, or they want to buy something of greater sanctity; and so it implies in the Ran in the name of the Ramban. This is not clear, for since it is of large towns, we are concerned that perhaps there is someone who does not want the greater sanctity, and so wrote Responsa Masas Binyamin there. One must say that the Magen Avraham was speaking about villages, and even this requires study. Masas Binyamin wrote there that even in large towns, where they built another better one, it is permitted to demolish the first one, for certainly everyone is pleased with this; and the wood and stones from the first one are also permitted for use. This is because they built another one, and it is like selling this for that, where presumably everyone is pleased. But if they did not build another one, even the stones and wood are forbidden, and the Magen Avraham challenged him for no reason; see there. The Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 37 as follows: In Responsa Maharam Lublin he permitted selling the land of the synagogue of the holy community of Lukowa, because when they built it as a synagogue they did so with permission and counsel of the leaders of the province, and everyone follows the leaders of the province. Also, presumably it is agreeable to all to sell it, since there is no way to rebuild a synagogue on that place. Nevertheless, to be extra safe, they should leave the place of the aron kodesh empty and make it a green garden, for it is not as disgraceful as other uses. It requires study, for planting is a disgraceful use, as written in siman 151, seif 11. One must say that it discusses vegetables that do not require plowing, for it is not so disgraceful. This is the language of the Magen Avraham. I did not reach the end of his intent, for Maharam wrote explicitly there that although planting is a disgraceful use, nevertheless it is not repulsive, etc. Thus even planting vegetables is a disgraceful use; rather, he meant that it is not repulsive like the four things listed in seif 9, which are worse than planting, as is explicit there. I also wonder at him for copying “they should leave the place of the aron kodesh empty,” etc., but that is not so. Rather, this is his language: the place on which the men’s synagogue stands is the main sanctity and should remain empty, etc.
