That they practiced bringing them, etc. Such as menorahs to light with, or ornaments to hang on the Torah scroll.
They cannot. Since they did not explain explicitly that they were giving this only as a mere loan, once they used them, sanctity took effect upon them. The Magen Avraham wrote that nowadays the simple custom is that they remain in the possession of their owners. If so, those who bring the vessels bring them with the custom in mind, and it is as though they stipulated explicitly that sanctity should not take effect upon them. Nevertheless, it is better to stipulate explicitly at the time of bringing them that it is only as a mere loan.
