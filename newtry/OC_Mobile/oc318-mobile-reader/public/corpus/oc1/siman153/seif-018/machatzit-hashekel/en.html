Seif katan 18, without announcement. For even giving it as a gift is permitted. If so, certainly an announcement is not required. The reason for announcement is that it is as though they are slighting part of its value, for perhaps if they announced it, someone else would add to the price. Since it is permitted to give all of it as a gift, certainly it is permitted regarding part of it.
Only when he comes to collect for others, etc. This is the language of the Ran: such as beis din that sells in order to collect for others, as is stated in Eruvin in the language of announcement, in order to give to a woman for her kesubah or to a creditor for his debt. But in a matter that we do at our own discretion, and we do not need beis din for it, announcement was not mentioned. End quote.
