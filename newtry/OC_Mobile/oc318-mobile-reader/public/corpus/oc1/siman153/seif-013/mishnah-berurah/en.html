A mitzvah matter. Since he wrote this without qualification, it implies any mitzvah matter, such as tzedakah and the like, even if it is not more severe than the sanctity of a synagogue. Although above in seif 5 he ruled, regarding money collected, that one may change it only to a stricter sanctity, here he speaks through the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople, where it is permitted to use the money even for a lighter sanctity and even for non-sacred use, as stated above in seif 7. The Taz explains it even where it was not through the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople, but where they take the money as a loan and afterward return other money for building the synagogue; he follows his own view in seif katan 2, where he holds regarding collected money that it is permitted to take it as a loan.
They should not sell them for a mitzvah matter. Although according to the law it is permitted through the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople even to use the money for non-sacred purposes, all the more so for a mitzvah matter, nevertheless they should not do so, but should collect from the public.
They built and completed. Specifically where they completed it; but if they did not complete the building, its law is like stones and beams mentioned earlier. See above, siman 152, in Biur Halacha.
They should not sell a synagogue. Meaning, it is not proper for the townspeople to do so. But according to the law it is permitted for redeeming captives or for a stricter sanctity in every case, even without the seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople.
