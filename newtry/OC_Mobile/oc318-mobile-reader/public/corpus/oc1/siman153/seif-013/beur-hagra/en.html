Seif 13, they collected, etc. Bava Basra 3b: Ravina said to him, etc. The Rambam's version in the first clause is, perhaps a mitzvah opportunity will arise immediately, and in the latter clause, redemption of captives. This is what is meant by rows of bricks, etc.; meaning, in such a case they cannot sell it for a mitzvah purpose. What, etc.
