They will not sell it in a court of law — see Mishna Berurah that the law is to sell, and even to the rest of the people of the city it is permitted when the seven good men sold in the presence of the people; and in any case when the seven good men sold not in the presence of the people it is forbidden to sell to the rest of the people of the city, for they did not reveal their intent that they have no more need for it [as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 37, and so Meiri] — see there.
