They should not sell a synagogue. See Mishnah Berurah, that by strict law it is permitted to sell, even for another item of higher sanctity, and this is from the Magen Avraham. Similarly the Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 17 that for higher sanctity it is permitted to sell a synagogue, even when they have only one synagogue. Eliyah Rabbah wrote there that this law requires study. It appears that even according to the Magen Avraham, although a higher sanctity is called even an ark and the like, as in the Mishnah in Megillah 25, nevertheless it stands to reason that one should not sell a synagogue for this. For we hold that the townspeople can compel one another to build a synagogue, and the reason is so that Jews should have a small Mikdash to enter and pray there before Hashem. How could they nullify this for an ark and the like? The Mishnah in Megillah, chapter Bnei Ha'ir, does not speak regarding an initial sale, but regarding the money of a synagogue already sold, that it should not be lowered from its sanctity. The case in seif 5 above may also speak where the synagogue will not be prevented from being built by their taking a little money for the need of an ark and the like. In every place where the poskim mention the language initially, they speak where they have other synagogues, or where the other sanctity was very necessary, such as if they lack a Torah scroll for public reading, or they lack Neviim and Kesuvim for study. So it appears in my humble opinion.
