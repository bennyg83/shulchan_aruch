To retain it. Even testimony is not effective from members of that city, as written in Choshen Mishpat, siman 37. A settlement whose public did not have a Torah scroll, and they read from an individual’s Torah scroll, and afterward the public bought two Torah scrolls, and the individual claims that he has established possession that they should read specifically from his Torah scroll, he has no chazakah, for it has no accompanying claim. Nevertheless, one should not diminish the honor of the individual’s Torah scroll; rather, they should read each Shabbos from one Torah scroll. B.Z., siman 182.
