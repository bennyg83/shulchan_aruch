[20] They built a house, etc. — that is, they built it from unconsecrated money, as explained in seif 4; however it appears to me that even if they built it from beit haknesset money, nevertheless it has no beit haknesset holiness for all matters, only that it is forbidden to change it (Magen Avraham). And see below siman 42 that in all tashmishei kedushah, etc.
