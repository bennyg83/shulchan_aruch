They built a house, etc. This means that they built it from non-sacred money, as explained in seif 4. However, it appears to me that even if they built it from synagogue money, it nevertheless does not have synagogue sanctity for all matters, only that it is forbidden to change it. Magen Avraham. See below, siman 42, that in all matters we require designation verbally and by an act. However, one who donated a place upon which to build a synagogue, or a house for a synagogue, or a covering for a Torah scroll, see Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 59. He wrote that he cannot retract, for it is considered like a vow, but he may use it for ordinary use, for sanctity does not take effect on it through designation. In siman 147, seif katan 3, I wrote even more than this. The Magen Avraham wrote that if he made it for a Torah scroll that he has in his home, as long as it was not used for the Torah scroll he may retract. This is not clear, and requires study.
