Seif 10. Some forbid selling it unless for Torah study or marriage. Note: See Yoreh Deah 270, where I resolved the Magen Avraham's question here from siman 248 and his question on Beit Yosef concerning other sefarim. I understood support for this from Rashbam's commentary, that even tefillin may not be sold except for Torah study or marriage. See there and examine carefully.
Seif 20. Some say that where a Sefer Torah was established as having belonged to Reuven's ancestors, the community cannot take possession of it. See my work on Even HaEzer, tenth edition, siman 4, in its place in Even HaEzer 125. There I wrote, incidentally, with Hashem's help, to the community of Sniatyn concerning one who had a Sefer Torah in one synagogue, moved to another synagogue and took the Sefer Torah with him, and then died, whether his son-in-law may take the Sefer Torah and return it to the first synagogue where he had originally prayed. See there and examine carefully.
