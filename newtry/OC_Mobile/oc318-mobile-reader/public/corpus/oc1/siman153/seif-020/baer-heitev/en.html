In it. Even testimony from members of that city is not effective, as written in Choshen Mishpat, siman 37. A settlement where the community had no Torah scroll and they read from an individual’s Torah scroll, and afterward the community purchased two Torah scrolls, and the individual claims that he was established to have them read specifically from his Torah scroll, he has no chazakah, for there is no claim with it. Nevertheless, one should not diminish the honor of the individual’s Torah scroll; rather, they should read each Shabbos from a different Torah scroll. Beis Yaakov, siman 182.
