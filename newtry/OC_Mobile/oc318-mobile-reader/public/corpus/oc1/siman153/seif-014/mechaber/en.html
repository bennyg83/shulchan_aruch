If Reuven said, "I am giving this land to build a synagogue upon it," but the [gentiles] do not let them build a synagogue; and the community says to [instead] build a school upon it, but Reuven says, "I did not donate [it] with this in mind" - Reuven is not able to go back upon it. And if Reuven does not live there, they may change [the use of the land]; but if he is among the residents of that city, they are not allowed to change it if he gets up and screams (yells), unless there is a Torah scholar in the city for whom any [communal] decision that is made, is done through his authority.
