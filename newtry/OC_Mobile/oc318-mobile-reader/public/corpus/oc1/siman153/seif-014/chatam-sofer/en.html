There, seif katan 46. And he gave her much. Note: this is like “exchange price” in Bechoros 54a, and below in seif katan 47, where he gave ten lambs and the buyer will choose one in exchange for the dog. Then, according to the opinion that there is no retroactive clarification, all are forbidden. But if the seller clarifies and says, “One of ten lambs that I have I will give you as the hire,” then when he later clarifies one, the hire does not take effect retroactively, as Tosafot wrote in Avodah Zarah 63a, s.v. kegon. It is possible that the same applies to an exchange price: if he said, “One of ten that I will choose from my lambs shall be the exchange price of your dog,” it is not forbidden as an exchange price. But perhaps one can say that an exchange price is different, because the dog is still in existence; if it did not take effect before it was clarified, it takes effect now when he clarifies and becomes the exchange price of the dog that still exists. This is unlike hire, for since it did not take effect before it was clarified, it no longer takes effect, because the benefit of relations has already passed. This requires study for practical halacha.
