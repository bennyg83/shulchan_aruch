(s.k. 14) His words, etc. — meaning: if they sold, etc.; that is, what Shulchan Aruch wrote "and the same for all holy objects," etc. — do not say they are equal for every matter to a beit haknesset; rather they are not equal regarding the body of the matter: for a beit haknesset that is sold, the buyer may treat it with secular practice; but holy objects that are sold — even the buyer may not treat them with secular practice, only the money is permitted for secular needs.
