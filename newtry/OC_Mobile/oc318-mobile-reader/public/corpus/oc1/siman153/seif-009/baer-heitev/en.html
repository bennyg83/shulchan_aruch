The seven leaders of the city in the presence of the townspeople. This does not mean literally all seven in every case; whenever there is a majority, it is called the seven leaders of the city, and we follow the majority. See Penei Yehoshua, siman 4, and Maharshdam, Yoreh Deah, siman 175. The seven leaders of the city does not mean the wisest or oldest men, but those most involved in communal affairs. See Re’em, part 1, siman 53.
