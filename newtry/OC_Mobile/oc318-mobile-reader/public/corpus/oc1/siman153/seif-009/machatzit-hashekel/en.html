For since they did not pray in it, etc. Meaning, the sanctity of a synagogue does not take effect through construction alone, but through prayer in it.
But if they already prayed in it. Then it acquired synagogue sanctity, and it may not be changed to a lighter sanctity except through the methods explained above.
And there is no distinction. Whether it was built from communal money or from an individual’s donation, once they prayed in it, it has the law of a synagogue.
And so it appears from the Mordechai. For he wrote that a house built to be a synagogue does not become sanctified until they pray in it.
And this is what the Magen Avraham wrote. Meaning, that the difficulty from seif 8 is resolved by distinguishing between before they prayed in it and after they prayed in it.
Seif katan 10, but if he lent it, etc. Meaning, if one lent an item for synagogue use, it does not become absolutely sanctified, for his intention was only for temporary use.
