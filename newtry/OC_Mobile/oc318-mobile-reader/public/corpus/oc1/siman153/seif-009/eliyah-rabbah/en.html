For whatever they want, etc. Even if it has no sanctity at all, but is for other public needs, as in the Talmud and later authorities. As for the Taz’s wonder about the one who asks, “The reason is that he stipulated,” etc., perhaps because it is a mundane matter a condition is required, in my humble opinion this is not difficult at all, for if so it should not have said “but if they stipulated, even for duchsusya,” since it would be discussing specifically duchsusya, meaning the city guard.
