That is near the fire. This implies that with pot-roasting it is permitted.
For it cooks quickly. The same applies to all types of legumes that cook quickly, see Rabbeinu Yerucham and see seif 8.
And the meat will harden. This implies that if it is in a pot, smearing the opening with clay is ineffective, since it will not harden. See the gloss and the end of the siman, gloss 2, and the end of siman 257.
That was cooked somewhat. If it did not reach the level of the food of Ben Derusai, it is permitted. Where it is forbidden, it is forbidden even if it reached the level of the food of Ben Derusai according to those who prohibit at the beginning of siman 253. However, in a pot even kid meat is forbidden according to the Rif and those who follow him, as written at the beginning of siman 253.
Everything is permitted. Even in a pot, since he is not ready to stoke, for it is sealed.
For it is uncovered. This implies that even if it is in an oven, whenever it is not covered at all, it is forbidden, unlike the first view, which holds that kid meat is permitted. However, once it is roasted to the level of the food of Ben Derusai, it is permitted even according to those who prohibit in siman 253 regarding a pot. For they explain that which we learned, one may not roast, etc., unless it will be roasted while it is still day to the level of the food of Ben Derusai, as referring to meat on coals, meaning that it is uncovered. If so, once it reached the level of the food of Ben Derusai, at least it is permitted. This is unlike the first view, for which there is no permission at the level of the food of Ben Derusai unless it is placed directly on the coals, as written in seif 2. However, it appears from the Baal HaMaor that even according to the second view, this is specifically in their ovens, which were small, and they were concerned that it might become scorched, as stated in tractate Beitzah. But in our ovens, there is no permission for roasted meat at the level of the food of Ben Derusai according to those who prohibit in a pot in siman 253.
