Regarding food in a pot, or pot-roasted meat, whose law is also like food in a pot, there is a distinction between something raw and something partially cooked. When it is raw, we are lenient and permit placing it close to nightfall, whether it is beef, kid meat, or poultry. When it is partially cooked, however, it is forbidden even with kid meat and poultry, lest he stoke the coals. The leniency that stoking would scorch it does not apply, since it is in a pot and requires substantial cooking. What is considered 'partially cooked' is explained above at the beginning of siman 253. The first opinion there maintains that it remains in that category until it is fully cooked and further cooking would make it mitzamek v'ra lo. The second opinion there maintains that once it has cooked to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai, it is regarded as fully cooked and may be placed on a stove or in an oven that is neither swept out nor covered with ash.
