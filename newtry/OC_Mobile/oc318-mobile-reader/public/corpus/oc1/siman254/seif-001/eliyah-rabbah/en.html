Everything is permitted, even in a pot. Magen Avraham means even where the food has cooked only partially.
[3] Everything is forbidden, etc. Nevertheless, once it has roasted to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai, it is permitted even according to those who are stringent at the beginning of siman 253, as stated by the Taz and Magen Avraham. The Maor, however, implies that this applies specifically to their small ovens, where there was concern that the food might scorch. In our ovens, however, those who forbid leaving food in a pot do not permit roasted meat merely because it has reached the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai, as stated by the Magen Avraham. One may nevertheless rely here upon the lenient authorities.
[4] [Levush:] But with other meat, etc. Nevertheless, if one transgressed or forgot and left it in a covered oven, even though the opening was not sealed with clay, it is permitted, since there are authorities who permit this even initially. The same rule applies in every comparable case, as stated by the Bach and later authorities.
