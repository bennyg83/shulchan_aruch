Se'if 1 in the Shulchan Aruch. 'If it is in an oven and he sealed its opening with clay, etc.' Responsa Panim Me'iros, part 1, siman 80, discusses those who distill liquor from grain when, close to nightfall, the liquor had not yet finished distilling, etc. See there, where he writes that because the droplets produced from the grain's vapor emerge on Shabbos, they have the status of nolad. According to the Baal HaTerumos, cited by the Beit Yosef in siman 318, such a substance is not nullified even in a mixture of one thousand. Tosafos, Shabbos 19b, s.v. 'Oil,' likewise write that the leniency applies specifically to garlic and unripe grapes that one crushed, etc. Nevertheless, since these are rabbinic matters, it is proper to rely upon the lenient view in a case of financial loss, etc.; see there. I am astonished. Do the Baal HaTerumos and Tosafos maintain that this remains forbidden forever? Their ruling is only that it is forbidden for that Shabbos. It is clear there that the discussion concerns only drinking it on that Shabbos. In that respect, financial loss is not relevant. This requires further study.
