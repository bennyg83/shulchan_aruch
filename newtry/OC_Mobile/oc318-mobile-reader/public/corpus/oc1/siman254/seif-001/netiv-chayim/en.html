In the Rama, se'if 1. 'And there are those who are stringent and forbid it.' Note: Although with respect to sealing the oven with clay this opinion is lenient, nevertheless regarding roasting, which is the subject under discussion, it is stringent. The Taz's objection, see there, is not difficult at all. According to the Rama's ruling in siman 253 that food cooked to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai may be left, the Rama here needed to record only the stringent consequence and not the lenient one, for even according to the concern that the meat may scorch, it is permitted once it has reached the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai. Examine this carefully.
