If he transgressed. He intentionally placed it there so close to nightfall that there was not enough time for it to roast before nightfall to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai, and it roasted during the forbidden time; or he forgot the roast on the stove and it roasted on Shabbos. Even after the fact it is forbidden, so that he should not deliberately leave it there and then claim that he forgot.
It is forbidden. Both for him and for others until Motzei Shabbos, after waiting b'chedi she'ya'asu, as stated above in siman 253, se'if 1.
