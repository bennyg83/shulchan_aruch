Se'if katan 3: And it will toughen, etc. In a pot, it is of no consequence, etc.; see below in the Rama's gloss. That is to say, according to the gloss, which follows Rashi, if the oven was sealed with clay it is permitted even when the meat is in a pot. Rashi's reason is that the clay seal serves as a reminder. Therefore, there is no difference whether the meat is in a pot or not. Magen Avraham wrote likewise in se'if katan 5.
