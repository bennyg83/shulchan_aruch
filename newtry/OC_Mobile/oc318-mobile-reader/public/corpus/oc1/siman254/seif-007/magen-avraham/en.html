More than three meals. This is similar to a cake on top of coals, as written in seif 5, from Rabbeinu Yerucham. It is speaking where he stuck it in permissibly, as written there. It seems to me that this is specifically when he wants to eat from it on Shabbos, but it is forbidden to remove it for weekday use, for even mere carrying for that purpose is forbidden, as written in the Hagahot Maimoniyot, laws of Pesach, that it is forbidden to bring wine on Shabbos for after Shabbos. See what I wrote in siman 323 and at the end of siman 666. It further seems to me that specifically with bread they permitted the food of three meals, for on Shabbos one is obligated to eat bread. But other cooked foods, if he placed them in violation, even unintentionally, are forbidden until after Shabbos, as written at the beginning of siman 253. See seif 8. However, if he has nothing at all to eat, it is permitted, since it is forbidden to fast on Shabbos, as written in siman 288. If he has other bread for the food of three meals, it is forbidden to save more, even though each meal requires a whole loaf and lechem mishneh. Nevertheless, this is not so absolute an obligation. See siman 334, seif 1.
