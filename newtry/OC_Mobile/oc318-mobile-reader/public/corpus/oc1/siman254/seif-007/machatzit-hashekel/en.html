(s.k. 7) On coals near coals, etc. Mordechai — and even though Magen Avraham wrote in siman 253 in the name of Mordechai s.k. 18 that if the pot is not buried in coals but on coals it is permitted even for a Jew to remove it — that is b'dieved; but the mishnah 'we do not roast' unless etc. deals with l'chatchila; therefore it must be near coals. Per Rambam — Mordechai can explain 'meat on the coals' per Rashi, but Rambam explains meat on the coals literally as in Shulchan Aruch seif 2. Mordechai's question: how may one take the meat off the coals?
