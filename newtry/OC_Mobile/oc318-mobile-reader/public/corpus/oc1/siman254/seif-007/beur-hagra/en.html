Se'if 7, in ovens, etc.; with a knife or... Shabbos 123a: 'And a cake that one concealed, etc.; one inserts, etc.'
More than three meals. This is how the Beit Yosef explains his words, for otherwise even using their implement would be permitted.
Nevertheless, it is... This means an implement like their baker's peel, as the Rif explains, because it is a weekday activity. Our peel is likewise a weekday activity, since we use it to remove bread during the week.
