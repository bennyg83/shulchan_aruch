(ט) [Levush] For they are fit to eat, etc. — not comparable to onion and egg above: an egg is not normally swallowed, and onion though sometimes eaten raw is not as good raw as apples (Rosh chapter Bameh Tomnin). The same for anything not good to eat raw — forbidden. This is Magen Avraham's wording; so wrote in Agudah on apples, etc.
