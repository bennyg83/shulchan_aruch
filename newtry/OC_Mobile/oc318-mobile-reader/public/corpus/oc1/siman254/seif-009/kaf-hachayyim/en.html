9) There: And the meat will become tough, etc. Raw squash, since exposure to the wind is harmful to it, has the same law as kid meat. Shabbos 18b, cited by Beis Yosef. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 9, however, writes that this applies specifically according to Rosh and Rabbeinu Yerucham, who maintain that the principal reason regarding kid meat is that exposure to the wind harms it. According to Rambam, the reason regarding kid meat is not harm from the wind but singeing. This reading is therefore incorrect according to him, and Rambam omitted this case; see there. Accordingly, the Shulchan Aruch, which adopts Rambam's reason of singeing, likewise rejects this ruling. Rather, squash is like other produce, and the Shulchan Aruch therefore omitted it.
