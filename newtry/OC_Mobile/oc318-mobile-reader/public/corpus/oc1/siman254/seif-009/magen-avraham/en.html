Of water. Although water is fit to be drunk raw, nevertheless it is not as good as fruit, as written in seif 4. Alternatively, one may say that here it is speaking where he needs it for rinsing, and then it must be hot. According to this, it is forbidden to put water into the pots that are in the ovens, even though he intends that the pot not crack, since he needs it for rinsing and we are concerned lest he stoke. Therefore he must put it in while it is still day. See what I wrote at the end of siman 253.
