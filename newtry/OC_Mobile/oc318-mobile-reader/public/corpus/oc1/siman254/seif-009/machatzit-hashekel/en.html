(ס"ק ט) And even, etc. — even smeared is forbidden for Rabbah, for smearing for Rabbah is not that it is recognizable as stated, but because it is smeared — if one opens the oven the meat will harden from hot air leaving — not relevant if on coals; on the contrary opening the oven ignites coals; as below siman 259; unlike Rama who holds like Rashi and leans, etc.
