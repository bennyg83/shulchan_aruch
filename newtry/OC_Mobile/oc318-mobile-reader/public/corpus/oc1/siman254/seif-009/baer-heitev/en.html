Water. Although water is fit to be drunk cold, nevertheless it is not as good that way as fruit is when eaten raw, as written in se'if 4. Alternatively, this case concerns water needed for rinsing, for which it must be hot. According to this, it is forbidden to place water into pots in ovens, even if his intent is to prevent the pot from cracking. Since he needs the water for rinsing, we are concerned that he may stoke the coals. It must therefore be placed there while it is still day. Magen Avraham. See also siman 253, se'if katan 24, and what is written there in the name of the Magen Avraham.
