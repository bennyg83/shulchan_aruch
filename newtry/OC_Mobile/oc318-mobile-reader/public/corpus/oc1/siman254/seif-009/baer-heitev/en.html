(ט) Water. Although water is fit to drink raw, nevertheless it is not as good as fruits as siman 64; or one may say here it speaks when he needs it for rinsing, then it must be hot; therefore it is forbidden to put water into pots in ovens even though he intends the pot not crack — nevertheless he fixes it for rinsing and we fear lest it cook; therefore one must put them from before the day, etc.
