One may not roast, etc. See the Mishna Berurah, where we wrote that regarding food placed near the coals, the Mechaber treats an onion and an egg like kid meat. This is obvious, because the reason kid meat is permitted is that when roasted it cooks quickly and does not require stoking, while stoking would spoil it. All the more so does this apply to other foods besides meat, which cook even more quickly than kid meat, so that stoking would spoil them. A proof is that regarding a pot of completely raw food, even kid meat is permitted because one puts it out of mind, knowing it will not cook that evening, whereas for other foods besides meat the fact that they are raw does not permit leaving them, as Magen Avraham writes in se'if katan 4.
But once it has roasted, etc. Even the authorities who forbid food at the beginning of siman 253 with respect to cooking, holding that it must be fully cooked, agree that with respect to roasting the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai is sufficient.
