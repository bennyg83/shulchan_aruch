(ב) Stuck to the oven. Levush corrected this per Beit Yosef: Rambam and Tur ruled like R' Eliezer stringently as Rashi — thick bread needs faces stuck to the oven to scorch; charara which is thin — scorching faces toward the fire; so Perishah explained, or on coals alone suffices as Levush; therefore specifically on coals, etc.
