One may not roast meat, etc. Although he already wrote at the beginning of the siman that roasted meat placed near the fire may not be set there close to nightfall even when completely raw, he nevertheless also wrote the law that one may not roast, in order to teach that it is permitted when there is enough time for it to roast to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai. One should not object that our teacher wrote above in siman 253 that according to the Rif, with regard to cooking, it is forbidden to leave it even when it has cooked to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai, whereas here, regarding roasting, which is more severe, it is permitted. It may be answered that here the meat and onion themselves are resting on the fire. Therefore, once they have reached the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai, if he stokes the coals he will scorch them, and for this reason we are not concerned that he may stoke. According to Rashi and Ri, however, the discussion here applies even when they are merely near the fire, and it teaches that roasted food may be left once it has reached the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai, just as food cooked in a pot may be left.
