On top of coals. Meaning close to the coals, and not literally on top of the coals, for how would he take the meat from the coals, perhaps they have not yet been extinguished. This is from the Mordechai. See what I wrote in siman 253, end of seif 1. According to the Rambam, one must say that indeed he waits until they are extinguished. See seif 4.
Half its cooking. See Yoreh Deah siman 113, where some say this means one third of its cooking. Here he ruled stringently because of the stringency of Shabbos.
Even if it is kid meat. Even if the opening is plastered, it is forbidden according to the Beit Yosef in seif 5. This is unlike the Rama there, who permits when it is plastered.
Why would he stoke and ruin it? But when it is not placed directly on the coals, the level of the food of Ben Derusai does not help according to the Beit Yosef, for he does not ruin it by stoking, as written in seif 1.
