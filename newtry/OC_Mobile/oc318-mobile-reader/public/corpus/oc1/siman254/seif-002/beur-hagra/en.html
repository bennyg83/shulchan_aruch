Se'if 2: On coals. This qualification applies specifically according to the Rambam's explanation.
Half cooked. This follows the Rambam's explanation. See Magen Avraham.
Even if it is. As stated there concerning meat, etc. According to Rav Ashi, there is no distinction between a kid and a mature goat. See what the Rama writes in se'if 1.
Since. This is the rationale according to his approach, because the concern is that the meat will become scorched.
But when it has roasted. Meaning, although according to the Rif and Rambam there is ordinarily no distinction between food that has reached ma'achal ben Derusai and food that has not, here, when it is directly on the coals, reaching that stage is effective.
