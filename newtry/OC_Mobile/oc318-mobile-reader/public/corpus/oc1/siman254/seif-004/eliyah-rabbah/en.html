[Levush] For they are fit to eat, etc. This differs from the onion and egg mentioned above. An egg is not ordinarily swallowed raw, and although onions are sometimes eaten raw they are not as good raw as apples, as the Rosh writes in chapter Bameh Tomnin. The same applies to anything not good raw, which may not be placed to cook close to Shabbos. Magen Avraham cites Agudah as saying that raw apples may not be placed to roast. I am surprised, because Agudah there says only that if apples were removed from the oven after dark and were not yet roasted they may not be returned, whereas placing them near the fire or in the oven shortly before dark is explicitly permitted. Perhaps there is a scribal error in Magen Avraham and he intended to cite the gloss to Agudah that certain apples are not edible raw and therefore must be roasted while it is still day.
