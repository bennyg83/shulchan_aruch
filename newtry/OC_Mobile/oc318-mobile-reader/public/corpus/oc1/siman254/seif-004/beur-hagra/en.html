Se'if 4: Fruits. Since they are fit to be eaten raw, they are no worse than food cooked to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai. See Tosafos, folio 48a, s.v. 'for olives'; according to their approach, after reaching ma'achal ben Derusai it is permitted.
Nevertheless, etc. See there. That is to say, although it is permitted to uncover it and add insulation, as stated there on folio 51a, in such a case it is forbidden, as written in siman 257, se'if 4, namely, when it has been cooked, etc.
