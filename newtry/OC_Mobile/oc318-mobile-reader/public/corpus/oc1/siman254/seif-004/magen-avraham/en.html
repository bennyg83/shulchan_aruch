Around the pot. This means close to the coals. Otherwise it is forbidden according to the Rif and those who follow him, as the Beit Yosef wrote in siman 253 and at the end of siman 254. See seif 2.
Even though it is impossible, etc. For they are considered as though they were cooked to the level of the food of Ben Derusai, since they are fit to be eaten raw. This is not comparable to an onion in seif 2, which is forbidden even though it is sometimes eaten raw, for nevertheless it is not good to eat raw like apples. The same applies to anything that is not good to eat raw, which is forbidden. So too the Agudah wrote that wild apples, which are not eaten raw, may not be roasted.
