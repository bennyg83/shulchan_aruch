Around the pot. The pot is standing on the stove supports, with a fire beneath it. The Mechaber teaches that before nightfall one may place fruit around it, even though the fruit touches the fire and roasts on Shabbos from the fire's heat. This is not comparable to meat, an onion, and an egg mentioned above, which may not be placed there unless they roasted before nightfall to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai. Since these fruits are eaten raw, they are equivalent to a cooked dish that has reached the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai. Indeed, they are better when entirely uncooked than another dish that has cooked to that degree, and he will therefore not come to stoke the coals. The Mechaber says 'around the pot' rather than stating generally that one may place them on the fire because he describes the common practice of their time. Alternatively, he uses the case of a pot because of the law of the cover mentioned at the end of the se'if, which applies to a pot.
Although it is impossible, etc. This is not comparable to an onion in se'if 2, which is forbidden even though it is sometimes eaten raw, because it is nevertheless not as good raw as apples are. The same applies to anything that is not good to eat raw, such as wild apples and the like; it is forbidden like the onion and egg mentioned above.
That he not replace the cover. With regard to the pot itself, if on Shabbos its upper cover, which was placed over it to prevent it from cooling, was removed, it is permitted to cover it again and even to add to the covering, as stated below in siman 257, se'if 4. There, the food in the pot was already cooked before nightfall. Here, however, the fruit continues cooking on Shabbos, and the cover also extends over it. Since the cover hastens the completion of its cooking, replacing it is forbidden.
For it hastens completion. All the more so, it is forbidden on Shabbos itself to place the fruit near an oven in order to roast it. Even if he places it there before the oven is lit, it is forbidden, as stated above at the end of siman 253. Before nightfall, however, it is permitted to place it on a hot oven or in a tiled stove. This is the ruling of the Peri Megadim. Know that the permission to place it there initially close to nightfall means before sunset. See siman 261 regarding the law of bein hashmashos.
