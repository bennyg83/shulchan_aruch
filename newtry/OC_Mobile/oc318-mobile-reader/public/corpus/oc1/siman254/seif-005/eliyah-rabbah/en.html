[5] Since he placed it, etc. This follows the first view in the preceding se'if. According to the alternative view, whenever the oven is not covered, it is considered as though the food were directly over the coals and is forbidden even with kid meat, as stated by the Malbushei Yom Tov. The Mordechai wrote that the phrase does not mean literally upon the coals, for how would he then remove the meat? Rather, it means near the coals. See siman 253, se'if katan 1.
