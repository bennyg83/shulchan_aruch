Se'if 3. 'If he transgressed or forgot.' It is difficult to me why the ruling does not distinguish as follows: If he transgressed intentionally, it should be forbidden in every case. If he forgot, however, and the food had been fully cooked before Shabbos but was then roasted in a prohibited manner, such as when it was not placed near the coals, it should nevertheless be permitted after the fact, as above in siman 253, se'if 8. This requires further study.
Se'if 5. 'But not for a need of that day, which is forbidden.' This means more than what is needed for three meals. See Magen Avraham, s.k. 20 and s.k. 23.
