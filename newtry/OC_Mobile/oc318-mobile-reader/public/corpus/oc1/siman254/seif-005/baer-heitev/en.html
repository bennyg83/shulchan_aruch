Filled pastries. It is obvious that on a stove that is swept out and covered with ash, everything is permitted. A cake or types of dough baked in a pot have the same law as bread. Bach. This is provided that they are not filled. Magen Avraham. In Even HaOzer, he resolves the difficulties raised by the Bach, Taz, and Magen Avraham, and concludes as practical halacha that one may not bake bread on erev Shabbos close to nightfall unless its lower or upper surface has formed a crust. If it is baked in a vessel, both its upper and lower surfaces must form a crust, and its interior must also reach the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai.
Unintentionally. Although we do not impose the penalty even when it was done unintentionally, unlike what is written in se'if 8, this case is different, since he otherwise has nothing to eat, and it is therefore permitted.
Meals. If he has enough of one loaf for three meals, it is forbidden to rescue additional bread on Shabbos merely for lechem mishneh. The primary permission concerning bread is specifically because one is obligated to eat bread on Shabbos. Accordingly, other cooked foods may be rescued only when he has nothing at all to eat. See Magen Avraham.
In an unusual manner. Removing bread from an oven is a shevus, a rabbinic Shabbos prohibition.
