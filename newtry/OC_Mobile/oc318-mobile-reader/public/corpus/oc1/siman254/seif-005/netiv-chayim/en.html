Se'if katan 22 'He raised a difficulty in the sefer.' Note: See the Ran, who states that the Sages did not forbid removing bread from an oven as a rabbinic labor prohibition, but because it is a weekday-like activity. It may therefore be said that in this case they did not uphold their enactment. See below, Magen Avraham, siman 306, se'if katan 17, and see there.
