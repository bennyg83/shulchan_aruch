Se'if katan 8: Half, etc. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 113, se'if 8, regarding bishul akum. If food was cooked by a non-Jew to the degree of ma'achal ben Derusai, it does not help if a Jew later assists in the cooking. Magen Avraham writes in the name of the Beit Yosef that this measure is one-third cooked. This is a dispute between Rashi and the Rambam: according to Rashi it is one-third cooked, while according to the Rambam it is half cooked. To this Magen Avraham answers that regarding Shabbos the Mechaber was stringent in accordance with the Rambam.
