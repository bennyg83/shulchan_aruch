With a mill. In any event, a Jew should not sit there in order to remain and supervise. In Jerusalem, however, the mills that grind wheat for Pesach are locked. In Bruloni, where the practice is that every householder grinds there an equal fixed weight and no accounting or writing is required, they grind on Shabbos while a Jew stands there and supervises. See Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 4.
