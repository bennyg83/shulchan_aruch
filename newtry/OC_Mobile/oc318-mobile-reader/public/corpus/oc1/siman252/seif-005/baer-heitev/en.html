(ה) Sound. There is degradation of Shabbat. If the mill belongs to a non-Jew it is obvious one may put grain in from before the day; if a Jew gives wheat to a non-Jew from Erev Shabbat, even if the non-Jew grinds on Shabbat it is permitted as seif 2, provided the Jew does not stand by him lest it appear as his agent; if the hour requires it, such as close to Passover, a Jew may watch the mill as siman 467; but if the Jew gives the grain, etc.
