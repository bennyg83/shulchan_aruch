The oil - See Taz. The Bach also discusses this at length; see there, and we elaborated in our novellae. See below, siman 320, seif 2, that when wine is already present in the vat, even fruit not crushed before nightfall is nullified into it. Here the case is where no wine is present. Therefore, if the fruit was crushed before nightfall and wine emerges on Shabbos, it is permitted; if it was not crushed, it is forbidden. See Magen Avraham seif katan 19.
