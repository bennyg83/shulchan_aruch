[6] Without payment, etc. Beit Yosef writes that this appears from the Hagahot Mordechai cited shortly below. This is his wording. It appears that his intent is to what he wrote, that if the non-Jew works on Shabbos one must protest, which necessarily concerns a case where the article was already given to him on Friday. Mitzvat Shemurim understood this as referring to the phrase preceding the preceding one and was forced into a strained explanation; see there, but that is incorrect. Nevertheless, in siman 247, seif katan 1, I raised a difficulty against Beit Yosef and wrote that the primary view follows those who prohibit work done without payment.
