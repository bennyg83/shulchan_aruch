(51) With his needle - The same applies when he holds any other object in his hand.
(52) In his hand - The same applies when another object rests in his pocket, for carrying it in that manner is the ordinary manner of transferring and would incur liability on Shabbos. The Sages therefore prohibited leaving one's house with it close to nightfall, lest he forget and carry it after dark. It is permitted, however, to enter the public domain close to nightfall with a needle stuck into one's garment, in a place where this is not the ordinary weekday manner of carrying, and similarly with any object whose carriage on Shabbos would not incur Torah liability, as explained below in siman 301. Although this remains rabbinically forbidden on Shabbos, the Sages did not enact one safeguard upon another. Accordingly, nowadays, when there is no biblical public domain but only a karmelit, one may go out with an object in hand close to nightfall, meaning before sunset, because this too is only one safeguard upon another [Magen Avraham]. Many poskim, including the Mechaber, maintain that a biblical public domain exists even today, as is evident in siman 345 [Tosefet Shabbos]. According to the Gra's explanation, it is forbidden immediately before sunset even into a karmelit.
(53) Lest he forget and carry it out - Meaning, he may forget and, after dark, carry the object from one domain to another or transport it four amot in the public domain.
(54) Wearing tefillin - Meaning, tefillin on his head. We do not fear that he will forget to remove them before dark, later need to relieve himself, remove them and carry them four amot in the public domain, as described in siman 301, seif 7. A person ordinarily does not forget tefillin on his head, because it is a mitzvah to touch them frequently and forbidden to divert his attention from them. If he holds them in his hand, however, he may not go out close to nightfall, just as with other objects, because he may cease thinking about them and forgetfully carry them after dark from one domain to another or four amot.
