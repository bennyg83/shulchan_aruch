Magen Avraham, seif katan 9 Regarding his words, ‘for it is forbidden to give them to him’: Shulchan Atzei Shittim writes that this refers to a case in which it was otherwise permitted to give them to him, such as on Wednesday or Thursday, or when he could have completed the work before Shabbos. In such a case, with respect to the obligation to protest, paid work without a fixed price is more lenient than work performed without compensation.
