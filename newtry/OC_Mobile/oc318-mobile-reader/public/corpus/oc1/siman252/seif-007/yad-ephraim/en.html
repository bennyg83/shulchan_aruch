Seif katan 19. It is known that squeezing is a derivative of threshing, etc. It appears that he means as follows. Although this particular squeezing does not itself incur Torah liability because threshing is not ordinarily performed in this manner, manual squeezing will cause kernels to separate from their ears, which is an act of threshing. Although that result might ordinarily be viewed as unintended and not inevitable, since he has no interest in it, and therefore would not incur liability as explained in siman 320, here he intentionally performs squeezing, which is itself a derivative of threshing, and some kernels cannot fail to separate from their ears. He is therefore regarded as intending the labor of threshing, and if he does so by hand he is liable to bring a sin-offering.
