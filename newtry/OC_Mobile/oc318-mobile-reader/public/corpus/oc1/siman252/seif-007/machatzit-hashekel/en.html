Seif katan 7: For a favor, etc. This means gratuitously. If the non-Jew initiated the matter and asked the Jew to permit him to do a favor and perform the labor for him, all agree that it is permitted. If the Jew initiated and asked the non-Jew to perform the labor, it is prohibited according to the Rama. According to the Beit Yosef, as the Rama understands him in siman 247, it is permitted even when the Jew initiated. See the Magen Avraham there, who writes that the present passage likewise supports the Rama's understanding. The Beit Yosef states without qualification that work performed as a favor is permitted and does not limit this to a case where the non-Jew initiated. This implies that he permits it in all circumstances, even when the Jew initiated. The Rama's statement here, 'some disagree if he does it for him gratuitously,' therefore refers specifically to a case where the Jew initiated.
