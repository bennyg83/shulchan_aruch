If he sold it on Sunday and the non-Jew comes on Shabbos to remove it from the Jew’s house, the author of Mishpat Shalom, responsum 57, concludes that it is proper to be concerned and stringent where possible. Rabbi Moshe Yehudah ibn Chasson was even more stringent, particularly according to the authorities who hold that a non-Jew acquires from a Jew only through meshichah, for the sale is then completed on Shabbos.
