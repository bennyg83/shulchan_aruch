Maharam A.S. did not allow grain to be given in a bowler who cooks and walks all Shabbat, but we have to assume that he was able to cook it before the beginning of Shabbat, as the correction of the above-mentioned khela, but the question was there if he was wrong in his lesson and when Shabbat came, everything was still not over, if he had to remove the hat and lose the Shabbat. And to this the late Gaon replied, since it is at the end of the Shula, when the oven is not opened and the lid is closed, therefore it is good for him to think about the macabre and the burner, and not for Shama Yahata, and he allowed it.
