Or that he does, and some disagree if. Their dispute will be explained below.
And he. As above in siman 247 seif 1.
And also. Yerushalmi, as I wrote above in siman 244.
Forbidden on erev Shabbat. According to his view in siman 247 seif 1. According to Shulchan Aruch it is forbidden in every case, and therefore he wrote simply: specifically if he fixed a payment.
And if they saw him, etc., if. Yerushalmi. From this the Beit Yosef proved that for a benefit not involving payment it is permitted only if he sees him on Shabbat, as written there; otherwise, how could he leave it in his hand on Shabbat? This is specifically according to his view, that where he did not fix a payment it is always forbidden. But according to the Rama's view, that on Wednesday and Thursday it is permitted, one may say that he gave it on Wednesday or Thursday. This is what the Rama wrote: "and even if he gave them to him," etc. He wrote this so you should not say: but he wrote above that some disagree if he does, etc.
