(ס"ק ב) Because of cooking — it implies if it is already cooked katzatz it is permitted as siman 318. He wrote on this in Sefer Tosefet Shabbat: his words are astounding — how could he understand from the Mechaber that he holds it is permitted when cooked katzatz, and he showed a place in siman 318 seif 18; but there the Mechaber wrote explicitly that in a pot it is permitted when cooked katzatz, but in a kettle even if cooked katzatz it is forbidden, see there. Rosh wrote the reason: because dyes need much cooking, or because of dyeing — when one stirs he introduces dye into the wool (meaning even if cooked and dyed katzatz there is still dyeing concern); Tur and Mechaber seized the first reason, therefore wrote the reason is cooking and even if cooked katzatz it is forbidden. One may also wonder on Magen Avraham in siman 318 who wrote on the Mechaber's words the reason is dyeing — how did he miss the Mechaber writing here the reason is cooking? It is a great wonder on such a sage for his error here, end of Sefer Tosefet Shabbat. I cannot in good conscience rule against him; I will uphold the sage's words per Rabbenu Yerucham in siman 318 on Tur's words there: as long as it did not cook katzatz, even if cooked like ben drusai there is still cooking concern; and why did he write unlike Rosh in chapter Kirah who wrote: it is reasonable that until it reaches ben drusai food, all who advance its cooking are liable; and if it reached ben drusai, if it was dyed, etc.
