A person is permitted to give his garments to a non-Jewish launderer and his hides to a non-Jewish leather worker [to work on] close to nightfall [on Friday] if he established a price or he) does them for a favor, and he does not tell him to do it on Shabbat, and the non-Jew performs the work in his house. If he sees that he performs his work on Shabbat, if it was being done as a favor he must tell him it cannot be done on Shabbat.RAMA: If he did not fix (a price) it is forbidden on Erev Shabbat, and see Siman 247 that there is a difference if one does it for free, for this is a favor.
