It is permitted to give one's garments to a non-Jewish launderer and hides to a tanner (meaning the craftsman who tans and prepares the hides) close to nightfall, if he fixed a price with him or the non-Jew does them as a favor, provided that he does not tell him to do it on Shabbos, and also that the non-Jew performs the melacha in his own house. {Rama: And if he did not fix a price, it is forbidden on Friday. And see above, siman 247, for there are those who disagree when he does it for free, that is, as a favor.} And if he sees him performing his melacha on Shabbos, if he was doing it as a favor he must tell him not to do it on Shabbos. {Rama: And this is so even if he gave them to him several days before Shabbos.}
