All this - See Taz. In a case of uncertainty it is permitted even without any evidentiary basis; 'to attribute it' means that a mere doubt suffices. See Magen Avraham seif katan 13. Although some maintain that even a double doubt does not help regarding an item that will become permitted with time, here the matter is more lenient.
