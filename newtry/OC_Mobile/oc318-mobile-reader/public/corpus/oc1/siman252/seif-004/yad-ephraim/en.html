Seif katan 13. As stated in siman 253, seif 1, in the fourth gloss. This means the Rama's fourth gloss in that seif: if a non-Jew returned the pot on Shabbat, its law is like one who forgot it, etc. The Magen Avraham there, seif katan 13, explains that the Jew did not benefit greatly because the food was already fit to eat. See there and the Taz, who permits it even when cooling improves the food. The same applies here.
