3) There: And placing bundles of flax, etc. Three explanations have been given. Rashi explains that these are bundles of flax placed in a pot inside the oven so that they become warm and whiten. Hagahos Mordechai writes in the name of Riva that it means flax removed from its soaking pit and placed by the fire to dry, which involves cooking. Aruch explains that it means spun fibers. Bach; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 1.
