Magen Avraham, seif katan 5 Because the non-Jew has not yet acquired it. This is difficult for me: even according to the authorities who hold that money does not acquire from a non-Jew, once the non-Jew lifts the object he acquires it, and it becomes the non-Jew’s property before he carries it out. Furthermore, when the Jew designates a place for him, by what act does the non-Jew acquire that place so that it should become his courtyard? This requires study.
