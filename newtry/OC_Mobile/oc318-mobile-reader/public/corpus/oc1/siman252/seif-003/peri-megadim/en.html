If - See Taz. His proof is from the Mishnah in Shabbos: the poor person extended his hand inside and took the object from the homeowner's hand, in which case the homeowner is exempt and the act is permitted when the poor person is a non-Jew and the homeowner designated a place for him. The Taz writes that according to Rambam this should be forbidden, implying that under the Mechaber's reason in siman 246, seif 2, it is permitted. See siman 325, seif 1, Magen Avraham there seif katan 4, and Magen Avraham here seif katan 5, where this will, with God's help, be explained.
