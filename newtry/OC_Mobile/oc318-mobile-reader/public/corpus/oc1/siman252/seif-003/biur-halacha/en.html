It is proper to be stringent and prohibit it. This means that if the non-Jew performs the work on Shabbos, the Jew should protest even where a price was fixed. Initially, however, he may give him the work and need not suspect that he will do it on Shabbos. The Peri Megadim writes that if the Jew knows with certainty that the work will be done publicly on Shabbos, he may not give it to him; leniency applies only where it is doubtful whether he will do it on Shabbos.
