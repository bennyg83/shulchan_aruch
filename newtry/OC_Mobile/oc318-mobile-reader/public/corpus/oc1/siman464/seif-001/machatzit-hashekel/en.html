(s.k. 1) Even if mixed—meaning they make it, etc.; do not err that mixed means with flour, for in that it is obvious it is forbidden whether mixed before Passover or on Passover; rather it means mixed with wine. And the novelty that even though at mixing they crush it and it does not appear like legumes when Passover enters, nevertheless it is forbidden. Nevertheless b'dieved if mustard fell into a dish it does not forbid; and also if mixed with wine without flour it is permitted to leave it, as Taz also wrote; and likewise mustard in its visible form is permitted to leave.

And as above in siman 453 regarding legumes. And possibly Taz cited as a novelty mixed with wine is permitted to leave, for sometimes when mixing with wine they also put flour in it. Nevertheless when there is no flour in it, it is permitted to leave; and Maharshal wrote perhaps one might come to confuse.
