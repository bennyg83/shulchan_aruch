[1] Even if mixed, etc.—that was placed in it when it was still fermenting, for mustard prevents it from fermenting further and preserves it in its sweetness; and Maharil permitted leaving it but not drinking it on Passover. And see siman 453 seif 4 regarding nullification in majority; Chok Yaakov answered that here it is not called a mixture since it is made for its taste. One can also say that there it fell by itself and here it is mixed by hand. It also appears to me that there he takes it back out, which is not so here—it remains in it.
