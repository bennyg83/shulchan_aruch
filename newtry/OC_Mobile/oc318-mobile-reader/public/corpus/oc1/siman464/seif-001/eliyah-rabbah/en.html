Even if it became mixed, etc. The mustard was placed in the wine while it was still grape juice. The mustard prevented it from fermenting and preserved its sweetness. Maharil permitted retaining it over Pesach but prohibited drinking it during Pesach. See siman 453, seif katan 4, where the mixture is nullified in the majority. Chok Yaakov answers that here it is not considered an ordinary mixture because the mustard was added for flavor. One may also answer that there it fell in by itself, whereas here it was mixed intentionally. It also appears to me that there the prohibited substance is later removed, while here it remains within the wine.
