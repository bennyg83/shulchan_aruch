Laws of mustard into which flour has been mixed. Containing 1 seif: Mustard or other
types of condiments that contain water—one may not put flour into them. And if one placed [flour] into the mustard, he must eat it immediately and must not leave it to stand, lest it become leavened. And with other types of condiments, since they are not as sharp as mustard, it has certainly become leavened and must be destroyed if it is after the time when chametz becomes forbidden.And the custom is not to eat mustard at all on Passover,' even if it was mixed before Passover, for it is like types of legumes which people customarily forbid. (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 5 and Terumat HaDeshen siman 112)):
