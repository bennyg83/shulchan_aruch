Even if mixed before Passover—this is l'chatchila; but b'dieved it does not forbid the dish, and likewise it is permitted to leave it until after Passover if one has mustard made for dipping without flour, for there is no chametzing in it at all, only a mere stringency. And Rokeach wrote in his work that they eat mustard on Passover only that it should not be ground in a chametz bowl.
