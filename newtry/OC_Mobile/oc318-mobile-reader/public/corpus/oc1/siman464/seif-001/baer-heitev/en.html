(1) Water—for if not so it would be fruit juice and they do not cause chametzing, even though they are sour things in themselves, as stated in siman 462 seif 1.

(2) Mixed—meaning they make it with wine, crush it, and grind it until it is not recognizable (Terumat HaDeshen). And wine into which mustard was placed while still new, which preserves it in its sweetness—Rabbeinu Yerucham permitted it (and Maharil forbids it, for it is a substance that causes chametzing, and even if one could leave it to stand they do not permit it; see Chayei Adam) to leave it in the house but not to drink. And Taz wrote in the name of Rokeach that they eat mustard on Passover only that it should not be ground in a chametz bowl—end of his words.
