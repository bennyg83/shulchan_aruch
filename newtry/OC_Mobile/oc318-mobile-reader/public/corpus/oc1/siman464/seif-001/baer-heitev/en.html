Meaning, they prepare it with wine, crush it, and grind it until it is no longer recognizable. Terumas HaDeshen. Maharshal permitted retaining in the house, though not drinking, wine into which mustard had been placed while the wine was still new, where the mustard preserved its sweetness. Maharil forbids it because it acts as a setting agent, and it may perhaps not even be retained. See Chok Yaakov. Taz writes in the name of Rokeach that mustard may be eaten on Pesach, provided it was not ground in a chametz bowl. See there.
