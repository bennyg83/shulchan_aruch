Se'if 3. And if he tore for Yerushalayim first: seemingly this is obvious, for Yerushalayim too is included in the cities of Yehudah, regarding which he already wrote that if he tore for one of the cities of Yehudah, he does not tear again when he sees the other cities of Yehudah. And the Algazi, in the sefer Ta'avah LaEinayim, proved from this that what he wrote at first, that he does not tear again; nevertheless he must add to the tear. But if he tore for Yerushalayim first, he need not tear at all for the remaining cities of Yehudah, and even with an addition he does not tear.
