The author of Chesed LeAvraham, of blessed memory, wrote in handwritten glosses: Concerning the common practice not to tear one's garment upon seeing Chevron, may it be speedily rebuilt, I heard in the name of great authorities that this is because it was among the cities of refuge and among the cities given to the Leviim, and is not called one of the cities of Yehudah. End quote. Maharar Avraham Yitzchaki, of blessed memory, wrote in the margin: I do not know who these great authorities are; however it may be, these are weak arguments. End quote. We too, in our poverty, say that one should not rely upon this. Birkei Yosef, os 1. Shaarei Teshuvah:
