(7) From the scouts — meaning that he does not consider it seeing from a distant place, but once he reaches the scouts, which is a place near Jerusalem, he considers it as having seen; so too Beit Yosef. But several poskim wrote that scouts means any place around Jerusalem from which one can see from there. They further wrote that once he reaches the scouts he must tear even though he has not yet seen it. Eshel Avraham wrote: whoever tore and saw a bit, even if he did not see it properly — when he arrives he need not tear again; for l'chatchila he should wait until he reaches the place of the scouts.
