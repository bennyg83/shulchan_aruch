Se'if 2. [For] the Beis HaMikdash one says "Beis Kodsheinu VeSifarteinu," etc. Marginal note: See Magen Avraham, s.k. 2, who brought the dispute of the Rambam and the Raavad regarding whether the sanctity of Yerushalayim and the Beis HaMikdash remains sanctified for the future or not. And see my work on Yoreh Deah, edition 6, siman 163, in the laws of the firstborn, in Yoreh Deah siman 320; there I brought a difficulty on the Rambam from Maseches Megillah, folio 10; see there, and examine carefully.
