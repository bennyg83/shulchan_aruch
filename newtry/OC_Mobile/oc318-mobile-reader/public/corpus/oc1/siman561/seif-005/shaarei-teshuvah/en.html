He was going, etc. And the Radbaz wrote, in siman 646: one who dwells in Yerushalayim, and thirty-four days have passed over him without his having seen the Beis HaMikdash, is obligated to tear. But they were not careful about this; see there. And the Birkei Yosef also wrote that they did not practice thus. And see the Radbaz, siman 691, who wrote that it is permitted to draw near to the entrances of the Beis HaMikdash and to enter the upper chambers that surround the Beis HaMikdash; and see the sefer Derech HaKodesh of Moreinu HaRav Avraham, of blessed memory.
