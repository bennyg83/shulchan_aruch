5) Ibid. And he tears his garment. When he tears it, he recites a blessing and says: "Blessed is the true Judge, for all His judgments are righteous and true. The Rock, His work is perfect, for all His ways are justice; a God of faithfulness and without iniquity, righteous and upright is He. You are righteous concerning all that has come upon us, for You have acted truthfully, while we have acted wickedly." Bach in the name of the Likkutim. The Bach wrote concerning this that the statement that he should recite "Blessed is the true Judge" means without Hashem's Name and kingship, for this blessing was instituted only for bad tidings that newly occur and arrive. This, however, is an old mourning, and it has no direct connection to his seeing its destruction; see there. The later authorities cited his words. Afterwards he should say: "Gladden us according to the days You afflicted us, etc.; let Your work be seen by Your servants, etc." He should also say: "May it be Your will before You that, in Your abundant mercy, You privilege us to see the rebuilding of the Beit HaMikdash, Yerushalayim, and the cities of Yisrael and Yehudah, amen, may it be His will."
