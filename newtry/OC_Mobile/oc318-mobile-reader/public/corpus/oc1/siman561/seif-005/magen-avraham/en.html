And he came to Yerushalayim. It appears to me that one who sees Yerushalayim within thirty days, even though he did not tear (for example, because he dwells within it), need not tear for the cities of Yehudah. And one who was born in Yerushalayim, even after he has grown up, need not tear; for in his minority he was exempt, and when he grew up he need not tear, since he has seen them within thirty days. And see what I wrote in siman 224, and in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 396.
