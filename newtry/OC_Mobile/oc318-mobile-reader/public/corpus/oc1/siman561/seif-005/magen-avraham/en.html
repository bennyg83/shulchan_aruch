Coming to Jerusalem. the Omnipresent’s promise to Abraham: “He who is born in the city of Jerusalem is not a single day, but who is born in the midst of the Omnipresent’s people, who is born in the Holy Spirit, who has been raised in the midst of the Omnipresent’s mercy, and who has been commanded by the Omnipresent’s glory, has seen them in the midst of the Omnipresent’s Word, and who has commanded him.”
