1) [Seif 1] One who sees the cities of Yehudah. Specifically the cities of Yehudah, but not the cities of Yisrael. As for that which is written, "Men came from Shechem, from Shiloh, and from Shomron, with their beards shaven and their garments torn," although these were cities of Yisrael and not cities of Yehudah, one can say that they did not tear their garments until they saw Mitzpah, which is among the cities of Yehudah.
