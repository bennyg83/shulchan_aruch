Many. See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef records a received practice to study several chapters of Tehillim each day with concentration and submission, which will benefit him. He should endeavor to have someone escort him, for one who is escorted is not harmed, and people may be compelled to provide an escort, as Rambam writes in Hilchos Avel 14. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 427. One who parts from his fellow should do so only amid discussion of a halachah.
