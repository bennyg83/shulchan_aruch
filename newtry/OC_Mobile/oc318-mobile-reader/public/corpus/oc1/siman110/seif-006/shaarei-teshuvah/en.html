Many [cite] Avodas HaTefillah, and in Be'er Heitev: one who accepted upon himself to study each day a portion of the psalms of Tehillim with intention and humility — it is good for him. And he should strive to make it for him a levaya, and all who accompany are not harmed, and they compel for a levaya. As the Rambam wrote chapter 14 of Hilchos Aveil, and see Choshen Mishpat siman 427. And one who passes away from his friend may not exempt [others] except through a matter of halachah.
