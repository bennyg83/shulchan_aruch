(Seif 6) Therefore it does not begin with Barukh. There, and all in the name of, and Rabbenu Yonah explained as the Rosh and Geonim said — that the blessing Elohei neshamah does not open with Barukh since it is juxtaposed to "who formed"; but R' Yehuda explained that there it is thanksgiving and here it is prayer. See Tur.
