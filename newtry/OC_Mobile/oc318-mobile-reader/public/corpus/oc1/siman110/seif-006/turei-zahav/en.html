But less than a parsa, etc. For as he is in a city it is reasonable for him; and Rabbenu Yonah wrote: there are those who question from the Yerushalmi that says all roads are presumed dangerous, and one can say that this is not close to a city but when traveling between villages where everything is presumed dangerous — end quote. It implies from this that when traveling in a place of danger one should pray the traveler's prayer even in less than a parsa, for we follow the reason. And proof further from what is written in siman 219 regarding Birkas HaGomel on this even in less than a parsa, and all the more so here.
