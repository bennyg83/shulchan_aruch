But for less than a parsah, etc. For he is considered as though he were still in the city. Rabbeinu Yonah writes that some question this from the Yerushalmi, which states that all roads are presumed dangerous. It is possible to answer that this refers not to a place close to a city, but to one traveling among villages, where the entire route is presumed dangerous. His words imply that when one travels through a dangerous place, he should recite Tefillas HaDerech even for a journey of less than a parsah, because the reason for the ruling governs. Further proof comes from siman 219 concerning Bircas HaGomel, where one recites it in such circumstances even for less than a parsah, and all the more so here.
