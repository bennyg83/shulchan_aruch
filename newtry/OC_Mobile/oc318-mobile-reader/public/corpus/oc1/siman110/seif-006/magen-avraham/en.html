(א) "For the road in the morning." That is, he says the blessings on the road; in any case it is forbidden to say it until he has set out on the road, as written in sec. 7 [Beit Shmuel].

(ב) "Juxtaposed to its fellow." That it does not open with "Blessed"; and if one travels in the middle of the day, one juxtaposes it to another blessing — such as if one eats or drinks something and blesses a concluding blessing, or one relieves oneself and says "Who formed [man]" [Shelah, note 90, and Agudah].
