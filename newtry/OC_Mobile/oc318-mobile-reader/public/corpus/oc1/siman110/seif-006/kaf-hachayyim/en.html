6) There: One must recite it while standing. Since it is brief, standing will not distract him, unlike during the other prayers. Magen Avraham, seif katan 3; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1; Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 3. Maamar Mordechai writes that this is contrary to Pri Chadash, who writes that while traveling one may recite it even while walking; see there.
