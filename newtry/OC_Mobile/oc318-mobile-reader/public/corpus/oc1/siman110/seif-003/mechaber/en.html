One who walks in a place of troops of wild beasts or bandits prays "The needs of Your people are many, etc.," and one need not [pray] the first three blessings nor the last three, and one may pray it on the road while walking. And if one can stand, one stands. And when one arrives at a settled place and one's mind has settled, one returns and prays the prayer of eighteen blessings. (And if one did not return to pray, it is as if one forgot to pray entirely, as explained above in siman 108. (And so too is implied from the Beit Yosef).
