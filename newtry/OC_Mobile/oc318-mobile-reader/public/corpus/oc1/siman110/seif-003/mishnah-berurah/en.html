(13) A wild animal, bandits, etc. Meaning, because in such a situation his mind is entirely unsettled, they exempted him even from reciting the abbreviated prayer, Havineinu.
(14) He returns and prays. Provided that the time for that prayer has not yet passed.
(15) To pray at all. Meaning, even though he recited the short prayer, it is treated as though he did nothing with respect to the essential mitzvah of prayer. He must therefore compensate for it during the next prayer, as explained above in siman 108. This applies only if his failure to return and pray was unintentional, because he did not know that he was obligated to pray again, because he forgot, or because the time expired while he was traveling and he could not pray upon reaching home. If he deliberately failed to pray, however, this is a distortion that cannot be corrected. If the times of two prayers passed while he was traveling and he was compelled to recite the short prayer, there is no compensatory prayer for the first prayer.
