Taz sec. 2 — to decide between [types of] pressing need. That is, because the permission for workers is not at all within the category of a time of pressing need; rather they permitted them because of the loss to the proprietor. And from this, since the ruling in the Gemara is that one may not pray Havinenu except under pressing need, there is no permission for workers — even though Rabban Gamliel himself concedes regarding workers. And if so, although when not at a time of pressing need we rule like Rabban Gamliel, nevertheless it is permitted for workers — this is not so. For since Abaye objects to it, it is implied that Abaye holds one may not rely at all on the leniency of Havinenu, even in a place where Rabban Gamliel agrees and did not permit except under pressing need; from this, for workers — no. And so hold the Rif and the Rosh; and therefore the Rambam, who ruled permission for workers — the Tur wrote on it that he holds the permission for workers is also within the category of a time of pressing need; this is the intention of the Taz. And in vain did the rabbi Peri Megadim of blessed memory question this.
