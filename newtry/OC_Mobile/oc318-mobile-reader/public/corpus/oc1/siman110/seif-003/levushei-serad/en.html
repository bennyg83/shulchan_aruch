Taz, seif katan 2. To decide that it applies only under pressing circumstances. The meaning is that the dispensation for workers is not included in the category of pressing circumstances at all. Rather, it was granted because of the employer's financial loss. Consequently, since the Gemara decided that Havineinu may be recited only under pressing circumstances, there is no dispensation for workers. Although Rabban Gamliel himself concedes regarding workers, and therefore, even though outside pressing circumstances we rule like Rabban Gamliel, workers might still be permitted, this is incorrect. Since Abaye cursed one who recites Havineinu, it implies that Abaye held that one may not rely on the leniency of Havineinu at all, even in a case where Rabban Gamliel concedes. He permitted it only under pressing circumstances, and consequently not for workers. This is the view of the Rif and Rosh. Therefore, regarding the Rambam, who rules that workers are permitted to recite it, the Tur writes that the Rambam considers the workers' dispensation itself to be included within pressing circumstances. This is the Taz's intent, and Peri Megadim's difficulty is unnecessary.
