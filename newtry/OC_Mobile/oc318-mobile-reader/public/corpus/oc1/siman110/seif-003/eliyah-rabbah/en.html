Regarding what he wrote, "They should be judged, and not according to their constellation, etc.," the Beit Yosef wrote similarly. This is puzzling, for what connection is there between "those who err in judgment" and "not according to their constellation"? I saw in Malbushei Yom Tov that he writes that there is a scribal error in the Beit Yosef, and the text should read: "According to Your will they should be judged," meaning that those who err should be judged according to Your will and not according to their constellation, etc. This is the correct reading. I also found this text in the Kesef Mishneh, chapter 2 of Hilchos Tefillah.
