Seif 3, If he is able, etc. This is the Rambam's view, for he explains that the Gemara's question, 'How does he pray?' refers to the short prayer. This is the meaning of, 'and the halachah is...' The Rif wrote that the halachah follows Rabbi Shimon. Nevertheless, the formulation of Rabbi Chiya is preferable, as stated there, for Rabbi Shimon himself said, 'From being good, do not be called evil,' etc.
