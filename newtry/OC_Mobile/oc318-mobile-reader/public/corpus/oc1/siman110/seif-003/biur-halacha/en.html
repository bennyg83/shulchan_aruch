One traveling in a place frequented by bands of wild animals and robbers, etc. See Mishnah Berurah. The Rambam likewise implies that this ruling concerns one whose time for prayer arrived while he was traveling, and nevertheless he is exempt from the regular prayer. Still, it is possible that the same applies when one is compelled to set out on a road frequented by bands of wild animals and robbers after he has already prayed. He need recite only this short prayer, and not Tefilas HaDerech. This is also implied by the Or Zarua. If he set out and recited Tefilas HaDerech, and then learned during the journey that the place was frequented by bands of wild animals and robbers, it appears to me that he need not recite this short prayer as well, for it is included in what he said initially: 'Save us from every enemy and ambusher on the road, and from all harmful occurrences,' etc.
