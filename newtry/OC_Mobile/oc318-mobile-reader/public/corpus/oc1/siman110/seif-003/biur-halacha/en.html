"One who walks in a place of bands of wild animals and robbers," etc. — see in Mishna Berurah; and similarly it is implied from the Rambam that this law deals with when his prayer time arrived in the middle of the road and nevertheless he is exempt. However, it is possible that the same applies if he must set out on a road of bands of wild animals and robbers after he already prayed — he need not pray again, only this short prayer and not tefillat ha-derekh. And similarly it is implied in Or Zarua. And if he set out on the road and prayed tefillat ha-derekh, and in the middle of the road it became known to him that it is a place of bands of wild animals and robbers — it seems to me he need not pray this short prayer again, for it is included in what he said initially: "and save us from every enemy and ambush on the way, and from every kind of evil mishap," etc.
