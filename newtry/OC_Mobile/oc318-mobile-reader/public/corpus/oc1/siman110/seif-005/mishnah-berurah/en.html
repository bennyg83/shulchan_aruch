(24) "By day" — meaning each and every day; thus one who is on the road is obligated to say it each day.

(25) "If he rests" — meaning that he stays there some hours to rest and afterward returns and goes on his way; since when he rested in the city his intention was to return and go on, he need not go back and recite the blessing.

(26) "[One who lodged on the road] must," etc. — for there was distraction of attention; and all the more so if he lodged on the road at an inn at night, that he must recite the blessing in the morning. However, if he travels all night — that is, he does not lodge fixedly at an inn but only rests a short time at an inn and afterward returns to travel — he should recite Tefilat HaDerech in the morning without the concluding blessing; see Biur Halachah.
