(24) During the day. Meaning, every day that he remains on the road he is obligated to recite it.
(25) If he rests. Meaning, he delays there for several hours in order to rest and then resumes his journey. Since when he rested in the city he intended to continue traveling, he need not recite the blessing again.
(26) He must, etc. Because this constitutes a diversion of attention. Certainly, if he lodges overnight at an inn, he must recite Tefillas HaDerech in the morning. If, however, he travels throughout the night, meaning that he does not lodge there in a settled manner but merely rests briefly at an inn and then resumes traveling, he should recite Tefillas HaDerech in the morning without its concluding blessing.
