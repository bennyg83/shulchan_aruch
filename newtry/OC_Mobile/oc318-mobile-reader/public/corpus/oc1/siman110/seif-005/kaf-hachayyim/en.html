5) There: After the first three blessings, he recites Habineinu, etc. It is one blessing that encompasses all the middle blessings. There are several versions of the text of this blessing, as may be seen in the Gemara, Berachos 29a; Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilchos Tefillah, halachah 3; Tur in the present siman; Levush; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1; and Shiurei Berachah, paragraph 1. It appears that this is why the practice nowadays is not to recite Habineinu. See below, paragraph 8.
