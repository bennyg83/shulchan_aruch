One who enters — Taz — it is possible "or and" means monetary law in Magen Avraham. And issuing rulings applies regarding Shabbat and similar, and in Even HaEzer regarding gittin and similar, and in study even if it does not relate to a legal ruling. See the explanation in Beit Yosef and Levush and Eliyah Rabbah Taz. "I toil and they toil; I receive reward and they do not receive reward; I run and they run; I to the life of the World to Come and they to the pit of destruction" — in my humble opinion in the name of the Rebbe Rabbi Yitzchak, for he doubled saying "I" — when I see days that have passed I am careful in running, I prepare provisions for the road (and this is what he meant "I run to the World to Come" — in the manner of "to its place it aspires, there it shines"); and they do not prepare provisions (because they hold there is no reward and punishment only in the pit — the grave is their home forever, and why should they do mitzvot; and this is what he meant "their nearness is their grave" — in their home forever, so they believe; but it is not so, rather they extract the soul and judge). And see Levush.
