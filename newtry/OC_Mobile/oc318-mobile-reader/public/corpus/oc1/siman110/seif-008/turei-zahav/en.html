One who enters the house of study. It appears to me that the same applies to one who sits to learn even alone, and especially one who has reached hora'ah. And one short formula includes much — and this is it: "May it be Your will, Heaven our the Master, that You enlighten my eyes in the light of Your Torah and save me from every stumbling block and error, whether in laws of prohibition and permission, whether in laws of monetary matters, whether in hora'ah, whether in study — reveal my eyes and I will behold wonders from Your Torah, and what I have already erred — establish me on the truth, and do not remove from my mouth a word of truth exceedingly, for the the Master gives wisdom; from His mouth knowledge and understanding."
