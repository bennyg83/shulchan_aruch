To the study hall. It seems that the same applies to one who sits to study, even alone—and especially one who has attained the status to issue halachic rulings. And one short formula includes much, and this is it: "the Master our Heaven—may the light of Your Torah illuminate my eyes and save me from every stumbling block and error, whether regarding prohibition and permission, whether regarding monetary law, whether regarding issuing rulings, whether regarding study; open my eyes and let me behold wonders from Your Torah; and what I have already erred in—establish me on the truth; and do not remove from my mouth a word of truth for very long, for the the Master gives wisdom; from His mouth come knowledge and understanding." So far Taz. And Magen Avraham likewise wrote that the Ari, may his memory be for a blessing, would say it every morning. And Magen Avraham wrote: the prayer "I give thanks," etc., one should say every evening—everyone who engages in Torah all day.
