The one who enters the Beit Midrash shall pray, May there be a will from You, O the Omnipresent our God and the God of our fathers, that I may not fail in matters of Halacha, etc.,' and when he leaves, he shall say, I thank You, O the Omnipresent, my God, that you have placed a portion of the residents of the Beit Midrash, etc.:':
