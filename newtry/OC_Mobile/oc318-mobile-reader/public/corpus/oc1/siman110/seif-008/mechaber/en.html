The one who enters the Beit Midrash shall pray, May there be a will from You, O Hashem our Hashem and the Hashem of our fathers, that I may not fail in matters of Halacha, etc., and when he leaves, he shall say, I thank You, O Hashem, my Hashem, that you have placed a portion of the residents of the Beit Midrash, etc.:
