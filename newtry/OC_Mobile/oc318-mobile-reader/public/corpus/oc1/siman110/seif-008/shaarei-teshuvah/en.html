On the day [mentioned] in Avodas HaTefillah. And see Be'er Heitev in the name of the responsum of the Radbaz (manuscript): if one lodged in a settled place and is obligated to pray the eighteen[-blessing prayer], when he departs he must say the traveler's prayer as if he were departing at the outset. And if he lodged not in a settled place, or they were walking by day and by night — the first time he blesses and concludes, and on the other days he blesses and does not conclude — end quote. And the Be'er Heitev wrote on this that the same applies by the seas: one says the prayer of the sea the first time with conclusion, and on the other days they bless without conclusion — end of words.
