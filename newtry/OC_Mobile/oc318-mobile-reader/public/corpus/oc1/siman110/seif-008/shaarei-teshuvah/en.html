During the day. See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef cites a manuscript responsum of Radbaz: If a traveler lodged in an inhabited place, where he was obligated to recite Shemoneh Esrei, then upon departing he recites Tefillas HaDerech as though beginning a new journey. If he lodged outside an inhabited place, or if the travelers continued by day and night, he recites the prayer with its concluding blessing only on the first occasion; on subsequent days he recites it without the concluding blessing. Birkei Yosef adds that the same applies to a sea traveler: He recites the prayer for sea travel with its concluding blessing the first time and without the conclusion on subsequent days.
