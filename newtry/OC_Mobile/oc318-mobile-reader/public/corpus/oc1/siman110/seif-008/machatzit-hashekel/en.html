(א) "He returns," etc. — even though [he prayed] standing. That is, in a short prayer the Shulchan Aruch said one may pray even while walking; but if one can stand, it is preferable.

(ב) "And therefore it comes to inform us that even if he stood, he must pray again." For if while walking — thus the Beit Yosef wrote above in siman 94, that even if he prayed the Eighteen while walking he must pray again. And all this Magen Avraham wrote to explain the Beit Yosef's words. But Magen Avraham himself wrote above in siman 94 that the custom of the world is not to pray again when one prayed while walking.
