(Seif katan 8) He returns, etc. Although, etc., while standing. Meaning, regarding the short prayer the Shulchan Aruch states that he may recite it even while walking, though if he can stand still, that is preferable.
Accordingly, it comes to teach us that even if he stood, he must pray again. For if he prayed while walking, the Beit Yosef already wrote above in siman 94 that even one who recited the full Shemoneh Esrei while walking must pray again. All this was written by Magen Avraham to explain the words of the Beit Yosef. Magen Avraham himself, however, wrote above in siman 94 that the common practice is not to pray again when one prayed while walking.
