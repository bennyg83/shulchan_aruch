4) There: And he fears that they will interrupt him. This also refers to the preceding case: he is traveling and fears that they will interrupt him. Without that concern, he should pray while walking or while seated, as stated in siman 94, seifim 4 and 6. Bach likewise writes this in the name of Tosafos; Magen Avraham, seif katan 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1.
