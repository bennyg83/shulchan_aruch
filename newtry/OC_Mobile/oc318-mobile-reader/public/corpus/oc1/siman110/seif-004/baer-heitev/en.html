For peace. And a person should not say to his fellow "go in peace," but rather "go to peace." So too [writes the author elsewhere].

Many. Specifically the prayer fixed in plural language, as written in siman 565, end of seif 1; but the phrase "and grant us favor" he should say in singular language—Sefer HaKaneh per the secret [tradition]. And he should engage in Torah on the road, but he should not study halachah, lest he come to be interrupted—Gemara Taanis. And Magen Avraham wrote: and it is possible that one sitting in a wagon while another person drives the horses is permitted even to study. And he should not eat more than twice [his] hunger on account of intestinal illness (there). And he should enter with good crying and exit with good crying. See Tosafos there.

To dismount. However, if possible for him he should stop the animal, for we hold that a rider is considered like one walking. Shaarei Teshuvah.
