(א) "To peace." And a man should not say to his fellow "go in peace," but rather "go to peace" (Gemara).

(ב) "In plural language." Specifically a prayer established for many, as written in siman 565, section 61; but the words "and grant us favor" one says in singular language (so too Sefer HaKaneh, per the kabbalistic tradition). And one should engage in Torah on the road, but one should not study halacha, lest one become distracted (Gemara Taanis). And it is possible: one sitting in a wagon and another man leading the horses — it is permitted even to study; and one should not eat more than two meals because of intestinal illness [there]; and one should enter with a good cry and exit with a good cry. And the Tosafos wrote at the beginning of Pesachim that in one's city it is permitted at night if one is not traveling alone, for there is no concern for damaging spirits nor for demons.

(ג) "Riding." However, if possible for him, he should stop the animal, for we rule a rider is considered like one walking (Shlah).
