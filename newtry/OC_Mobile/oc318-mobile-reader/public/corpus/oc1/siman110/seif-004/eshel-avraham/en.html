"Lead us and return us to our homes in peace" — thus it is in the Rosh, and in the novelae of Rashba, R' Yehuda, and Abudraham; and he wrote in a book I have not seen: some are accustomed after tefillat ha-derekh to say "yoshev ba-seter Elyon," Shir la-ma'alot "Esa einai," etc.; and similarly men of deeds say when leaving their homes: "Beit Ya'akov lekhu venelkha," etc. And see in Eliyah Rabbah what he brings in the name of Shelah.
