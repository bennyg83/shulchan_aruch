Seif 1, In a pressing circumstance, etc. On 30a: 'According to the one who says... what is there to say...' and 'when he arrived...' The Rif and the Rosh wrote: From this we learn that one may pray in this abbreviated manner only in a pressing circumstance, such as while traveling and the like. This is what is meant there by, 'Abaye cursed one who did so,' even though the entire discussion indicates that it is permitted, as stated there, 'throughout the entire year,' and likewise on 30a, as above. Rather, Abaye cursed one who did so when it was not a pressing circumstance. See Tosafos there, s.v. 'Abaye cursed,' and see there 3a. This is the meaning of, 'or if he was...' See Rashi and Tosafos there, s.v. 'if he was.'
Or if he was not able, etc. As stated in the Mishnah on 28b, in accordance with Rabbi Akiva, since the halachah follows him, as the Rambam wrote in his commentary.
He recites, etc. See 30a.
Havineinu. This follows Shmuel, since the entire discussion accords with his view.
And he does not, etc. See 29a.
