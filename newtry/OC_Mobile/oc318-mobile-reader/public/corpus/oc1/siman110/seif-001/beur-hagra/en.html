Seif 1—at a time of urgency. According to one statement—what is there [to pray Havineinu], etc., and when one reaches [home], etc. Rif and Rosh wrote: from this we learn that they pray only at a time of urgency, such as on the road and the like. And this is what [the Gemara] says there: Abaye rebuked him, etc., even though in the entire passage it teaches that it is permitted, as written "the whole year," etc.; and there is one statement as above—except that Abaye rebuked him specifically at a time of urgency. And see Tosafos there, s.v. "rebuked," etc., and see there three statements; and this is what [the Mechaber] says "or that he was," etc.; and see there Rashi and Tosafos, s.v. "that he was," etc.

Or that he was not able. As written in the mishnah, Berachos 28b, and [the halacha is] like Rabbi Akiva that the halacha follows him; and as Rambam wrote in his commentary.

He prays, etc. There, statement 1.

Havineinu. Like Shmuel, for the entire passage follows him.

And he does not [pray Havineinu], etc. Berachos 29a.
