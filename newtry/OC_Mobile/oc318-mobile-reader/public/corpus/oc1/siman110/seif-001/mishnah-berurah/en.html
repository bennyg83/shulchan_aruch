(א) At a time of pressing need, etc. — But when not at a time of pressing need, we say in the Gemara that Abaye cursed one who prays Havineinu; nevertheless, after the fact one has fulfilled one's obligation and need not return to pray. And the Rosh is strict even after the fact. And the Acharonim wrote that if one sees the time for prayer passing, if one will pray at length it is permitted lekhatchilah to pray Havineinu — and this is during the summer months.

(ב) "And he fears they will interrupt him" — This also refers to the above, when he is on the road and fears that passersby will interrupt him; for if not, even on the road it is better that he pray all eighteen blessings while walking or seated, as above in siman 94 regarding praying Havineinu standing. And see in the Peri Megadim.

(ג) Havineinu — which comprises all the middle blessings. And see in the Tur and Beit Yosef its text and explanation.

(ד) Standing — since it is in place of the prayer of eighteen blessings at a time of pressing need, since it comprises all of them. And they gave greater weight to the strength of this prayer for this matter than what is implied that one may pray while walking on the road, because if he would need to stand he would be harried and unable to have kavanah; not so here, since it is short — he will not be troubled by standing. And if he transgressed and prayed it seated, the Peri Megadim remains in doubt — perhaps he must return and pray it standing when he comes home.

(ה) And one does not pray, etc. — because one must say "grant dew and rain" in the blessing of the years, and at Motzei Shabbos and Yom Tov one must say havdalah; and even if one wishes to include them in the Havineinu blessing one is not permitted. And for the reason, see in the Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham. And see in the Peri Megadim who is uncertain to say that even after the fact, if one prayed Havineinu on those days and included them within it, perhaps one did not fulfill one's obligation, for one transgressed the enactment of the Sages. And see in Biur Halachah what he wrote in the name of the Gra.

(ו) Havineinu — see in Chayei Adam who wrote that specifically Havineinu is forbidden, but to shorten each blessing and include within them the rain request and havdalah [see there for the formula in Klal 24] is permitted if one is sick or there is great pressing need.

(ז) During the rainy season — rather one prays eighteen; and if they interrupted him, see above in siman 104 sec. 5 and in Mishnah Berurah there.
