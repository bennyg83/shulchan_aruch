1) [Seif 1] In pressing circumstances, etc. The same applies if the time for prayer is about to pass. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef; Olas Tamid, paragraph 3, who writes that this means during the summer; Magen Avraham, seif katan 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Solet Belulah, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1; Chayei Adam, rule 24, paragraph 31; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 1. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1, explains that the reason is that one must complete the entire prayer within its proper time. Pischei HaDevir, paragraph 3, likewise writes that one must complete the entire prayer within its proper time. He writes that this is contrary to the work Batei Kenesiyos, siman 89, which remains uncertain on this point; see there.
