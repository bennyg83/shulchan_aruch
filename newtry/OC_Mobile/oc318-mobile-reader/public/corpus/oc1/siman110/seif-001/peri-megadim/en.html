In the rainy season — Taz — and this is the explanation of the Rif's words that Beit Yosef brought; and it appears that it is as if he is adding to the blessings, for Havineinu is every word in place of a blessing. And there is uncertainty if one transgressed and prayed Havineinu at Motzei Shabbos and Yom Tov and in the rainy season — whether he returns and prays the eighteen [blessings], for he transgressed the enactment of the Sages. I will cite what I wrote in our novellae that from Taz it appears that the derivation regarding havdalah applies also to "May He Who causes..." [the rain prayer], and there is much practical difference: (1) if certain, as Magen Avraham wrote siman 4, it appears that adding is forbidden and the Gemara holds according to what was resolved in the question. (2) After the fact — and Magen Avraham siman 108 seif 3, and Rabbi Levi Yitzchak siman 1 — one could say that perhaps one will come to err; after the fact he has fulfilled. And see Berachos 16, which asks and teaches eighteen and "May He Who causes..." in the rainy season, and it appears after the fact he has fulfilled; and laborers are considered after the fact, and it is implied from the Mechaber in seif 2 that laborers in the rainy season pray the full [Shemoneh Esrei] — and one need not extend.
