During the rainy season. See Taz. This is his explanation of the words of the Rif, cited by Beit Yosef. It would appear that this is considered adding to the blessings, for in Havineinu each phrase stands in place of an individual blessing. It requires study whether one who transgressed and recited Havineinu on Motzaei Shabbos, Motzaei Yom Tov, or during the rainy season must return and recite the full Shemoneh Esrei, since he violated the enactment of Chazal. I will copy what I wrote in my novellae. The Taz appears to indicate that the answer given regarding Havdalah applies equally to the request for rain, which yields several practical differences. First, if he is certain, as Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 4, it would appear that adding is prohibited, and the Gemara states its answer only according to the conclusion that remains difficult. Second, after the fact, see Magen Avraham, siman 89, seif katan 3, and siman 233, seif katan 1. It may be that the concern that he might err applies only ideally, but after the fact he has fulfilled his obligation. See Berachos 16, where the Gemara objects from the teaching that workers recite the full Shemoneh Esrei, and does not distinguish the rainy season. At first glance, after the fact one fulfills his obligation, and workers are comparable to an after-the-fact situation. Yet from the plain ruling of the Mechaber in seif 2, even workers during the rainy season recite the full Shemoneh Esrei. There is no need to elaborate.
