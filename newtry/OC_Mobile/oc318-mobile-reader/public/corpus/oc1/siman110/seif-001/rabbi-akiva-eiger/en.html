Seif 1 — And not at Motzei Shabbos and Yom Tov. It is possible this refers to the extenuating circumstance of the Mechaber — that is, he fears passersby will interrupt him; in this case at Motzei Shabbos and Yom Tov, even though havdalah is not essential — for behold if he did not make havdalah in prayer he does not return and pray — nevertheless it is better to pray on the road in the proper formula, and if they interrupt him they interrupt him and he completes afterward the prayer. But in the extenuating circumstance that Magen Avraham mentions at the beginning of the siman, or when the hour passes — one could say that in this case at Motzei Shabbos and Yom Tov it is better that he pray Havineinu and not mention havdalah, than that he not pray at all. And in the rainy season one could say that without mentioning "He Who causes the wind to blow" it is not prayer at all; but at Motzei Shabbos and Yom Tov without havdalah one fulfills the prayer — one could say it is preferable to that he not pray at all; and there is uncertainty for the halacha.
