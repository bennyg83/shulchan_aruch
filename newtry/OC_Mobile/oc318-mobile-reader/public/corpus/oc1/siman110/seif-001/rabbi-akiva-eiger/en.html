Seif 1. But not on Motzaei Shabbos or Motzaei Yom Tov. It is possible that this refers to the pressing circumstance mentioned by the Mechaber, namely, that he fears travelers will interrupt him. In that case, on Motzaei Shabbos or Motzaei Yom Tov, although Havdalah does not prevent fulfillment, since one who omitted Havdalah in prayer does not repeat the prayer, it is nevertheless better that he recite the full prayer while traveling, using the proper text. Should they interrupt him, they will interrupt him, and he can afterward complete the prayer. Regarding the pressing circumstance mentioned by Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman, however, namely, when the time for prayer is passing, it may be that on Motzaei Shabbos or Motzaei Yom Tov it is preferable that he recite Havineinu without mentioning Havdalah rather than not pray at all. During the rainy season, it may be that if he omitted Mashiv HaRuach, it is not considered a prayer at all. On Motzaei Shabbos or Motzaei Yom Tov, however, since one fulfills the obligation of prayer without Havdalah, this may be preferable to not praying at all. This requires practical clarification.
