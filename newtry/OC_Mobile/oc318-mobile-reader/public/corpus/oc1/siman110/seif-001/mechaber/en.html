One who goes on the road and laborers — what they pray, and the order of the traveler’s prayer and of the beit midrash. Containing
eight seifim: At a time of pressing need, such as when one is on the road or when one was standing in a place where one is harried, and one fears they will interrupt one, or one cannot pray a long prayer with kavanah — one prays after the first three blessings Havineinu, and says after it the last three, and one must say them standing. And when one arrives home, one need not return and pray. And one does not pray Havineinu during the rainy season, nor at Motzei' Shabbos, nor on Yom Tov.
