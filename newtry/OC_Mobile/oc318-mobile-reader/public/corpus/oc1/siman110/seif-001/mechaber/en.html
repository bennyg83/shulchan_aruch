The Traveler and Laborers - What Should They Pray? And the Order of the Prayer During Travel and of the Study Hall. Containing 8 Seifim: In a extenuating circumstance, such as when
one is on the road or when one was standing in a place where one is distracted, and one fears that they will interrupt one, or if one is not able to pray the full [Amidah] prayer with intention - one prays "Havineinu" [i.e. the digest version of the middle 13 Amidah blessings] after the first three [blessings of the Amidah] and, after it, say the last three [blessings of the Amidah], and it is necessary to say them while standing. And when one arrives at one's house, it is not necessary to go back and pray [again]. And one does not pray "Havineinu" in the rainy season, and not at the departure of Shabbat' [i.e. Saturday night] nor a holiday.
