And lechatchila, etc. Meaning he should not delay from saying it after the first parsa, and he may say it even in the city before he goes on the road. And what is written that he should hold on the road — meaning he should be established with certainty to travel. And I saw some who are strict not to say it until he is specifically outside the city in the field, and this has no root or principle from all the commentators; and behold Maharam would pray it in the morning on the day he wanted to travel by road. Nevertheless there appears to be a reason why they pray it outside the city — because then he says it with others, and the merit of the many is preferable. Nevertheless he should be careful to say it in a house of prayer that is outside the town mentioned in siman 231.
