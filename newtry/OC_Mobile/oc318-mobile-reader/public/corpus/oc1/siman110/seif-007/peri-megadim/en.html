And ab initio — Taz — and Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah and other Acharonim disagree that one does not say tefillat ha-derekh until he has left the city; and see Bach and Perishah and Maharam — when he left in the morning from the city he would adjoin it — see in their words, of blessed memory. And the matter of siman 231 implies immediately when he left the city he says tefillat ha-derekh and includes with it tefillat ha-derekh with a closing blessing; therefore within the city's bounds one says tefillat ha-derekh — not as Magen Avraham wrote here siman 14; and one could say as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 231 seif 4 — see there; and Magen Avraham here siman 14; and if so in siman 231 it will be explained.
