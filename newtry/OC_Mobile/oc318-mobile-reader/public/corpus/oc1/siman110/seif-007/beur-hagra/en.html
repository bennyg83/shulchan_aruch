(Seif 7) And one does not say it, etc. — as explained in Halakhot Gedolot.

He does not conclude with Barukh. Tur and Kol Bo.

And ab initio, etc. — to be concerned for Rashi's explanation, ab initio.

And if, etc. — for the essential point is as Halakhot Gedolot explain, not like Rashi; and as written in Shulchan Aruch 1:1; and similarly regarding the blessing on mitzvot, and as Tosafos wrote in Sukkah 39a, s.v. "over." Terumat Yeri'im.

Provided that, etc. — as Halakhot Gedolot explain, that within less than a parsah there is no danger, as above.
