(7) There — one does not pray Havinenu during the rainy season, etc., because one must say "and grant dew and rain," and at Motzei Shabbat and Yom Tov one must say havdalah; and everything said in Havinenu in brief words is a type of the blessings of the Eighteen, and havdalah is not a blessing on its own, therefore one does not mention it — Tur, Beit Yosef, and Levush; Taz sec. 1; Rabbi Zerachiah there. If one transgressed and prayed Havinenu during the rainy season or at Motzei Shabbat and Yom Tov, b'dieved it counts — Shelah sec. 3.
