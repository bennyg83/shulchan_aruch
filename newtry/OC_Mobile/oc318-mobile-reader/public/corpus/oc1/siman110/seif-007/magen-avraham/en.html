(א) "After he has set out." It seems one should not say it within the city's outskirts — that is, within seventy cubits and remnants from the city, for the outskirts of a city are like the city, as we say in Nedarim; and see Rabbi Levi in siman 565 sec. 61. And in Amek HaBerachah it is written that this means the boundary from the city's outskirts.

(ב) "Parsah." That is, eight thousand amah.
