7) There: One does not recite Habineinu during the rainy season, etc. This is because one must say "Grant dew and rain," while on Motzaei Shabbos and Motzaei Yom Tov one must recite Havdalah. Everything stated in Habineinu in abbreviated form corresponds to the blessings of Shemoneh Esrei, whereas Havdalah is not an independent blessing; it is therefore not mentioned in Habineinu. Tur, Beis Yosef, and Levush; Taz, seif katan 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. If one nevertheless recited Habineinu during the rainy season or on Motzaei Shabbos or Motzaei Yom Tov, he fulfilled his obligation after the fact. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1.
