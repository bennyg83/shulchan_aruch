Laborers who do melachah for a baal habayit — if he does not give them wages beyond their meals, they pray the Shemoneh Esrei; but they do not go down before the ark and they do not lift their hands [for Birkat' Kohanim]. And if wages are given to them, they pray Havineinu. And nowadays it is not customary to be strict about this, and it is presumed they were hired with the understanding that they would pray the full Shemoneh Esrei.
