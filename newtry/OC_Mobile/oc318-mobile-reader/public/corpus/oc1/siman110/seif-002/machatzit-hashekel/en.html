(Seif katan 2) And he fears, etc. This also refers back to the preceding clause, when he is traveling, etc. One should not mistakenly think that when traveling, even if he does not fear that others will interrupt him, he may recite Havineinu so that he can stand, since it is short, as the Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 3. The ruling therefore teaches that for this reason he was not permitted to recite Havineinu; rather, he should recite the full eighteen blessings while walking. It must therefore be that the words, “and he fears that they will interrupt him,” also refer to one who is traveling, and for that reason he was permitted to recite Havineinu.
