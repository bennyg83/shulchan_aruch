(Siman katan bet) "And he fears [they will interrupt him]" — this also refers to the above, when he is on the road, etc., so you should not err: when he is on the road, even if he does not fear they will interrupt him, he is permitted to pray Havinenu so that he can stand, since it is short — and as Magen Avraham wrote in sec. 3. This informs us why it was not permitted for him to pray Havinenu but rather to pray the Eighteen while walking; nevertheless, "and he fears they will interrupt him" refers also when he is on the road, and therefore he was permitted to pray Havinenu.
