"And nowadays," etc. "that they pray eighteen" — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote, that the same applies to the entire prayer formula as any other person; thus it is evident from Eliyah Rabbah, and it is obvious they should be careful to pray the prayers at the time of prayer; and as Rashi wrote in Berachos 16a, s.v. "korein"; and unfortunately there are laborers who fail in this, who delay the time of prayer until twilight itself.
