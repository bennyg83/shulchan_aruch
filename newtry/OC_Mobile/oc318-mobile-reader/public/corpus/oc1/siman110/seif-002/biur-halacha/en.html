And nowadays, etc., that they should recite Shemoneh Esrei. See Mishnah Berurah, where it is written that the same applies to the entire text of prayer, which they recite like everyone else. This is evident from Eliyah Rabbah. It is obvious that they must be careful to recite the prayers within the proper time for prayer, as Rashi writes in Berachos 16a, s.v. 'they recite.' Due to our sins, some workers stumble in this matter and delay prayer until actual twilight.
