We remove flies, etc. — The source of this law comes from the Mishnah daf 23 mikardedin, etc. And in Shitah Mekubbetzet I found something new: this Mishnah applies only according to R' Akiva, who holds that nefesh even of an animal is included; not so R' Yosi haGalili — one need not trouble oneself for an animal. And Rambam and Tur clearly do not hold this, for Rambam in ch. 1 of Hilchot Yom Tov and likewise the Tur above in siman 512 ruled explicitly like R' Yosi haGalili, yet here they ruled to permit. It appears the reason is tzaar baalei chayim through flies that bite them. Also from the Gemara it is proven not like the previous inference, for otherwise why would the Gemara strain to give reasons for those who forbid combing as a gezeirah lest one comb — [indeed this is forced, for combing too is not forbidden for R' Yosi except derabbanan, per what Tosafot wrote in Yoma daf 34 that a davar she'eino mitzva for R' Yosi is only derabbanan] — say he holds like R' Yosi haGalili.
