1) [Se'if 1] Flies may be removed, etc. This means by hand or with a wooden scraper, which does not make a wound, as Tosafos write in Beitzah 23a. See there. Pischei Olam, subsection 1, writes likewise.
2) There. Although one does not make a wound. This means that although some of them cannot be separated from the animal without making a wound, he does not intend to do so. It is an unintended act and the prohibited result is not inevitable. Levush.
3) Tosafos write in Shabbos 53a, according to one resolution, that this was permitted only on Yom Tov but is forbidden on Shabbos. This also appears to be the view of Rashba in Avodas HaKodesh. See there. Mordechai in the name of Rashba, however, and Rabbeinu Yerucham, path 13, page 83, write that just as it is permitted on Yom Tov, it is likewise permitted on Shabbos. See there. Erech HaShulchan, subsection 3.
