[Levush] And likewise it is permitted, etc. In the Gemara this comes earlier, and I do not know why the Tur and Levush changed their order; also in siman 332 they wrote them as they are in the Talmud (Malbushei Yom Tov). And in my humble opinion, it is possible to rule that they are of equal weight, so that one should not say that soaking the placenta must specifically come first because that [remedy] is preferable, that she will have compassion and he will not need the second, and if they see that it does not help, then he should also do the second; it teaches us that whichever he wishes, he may put first. And so the Rambam, chapter 4, wrote: it is permitted to sprinkle from her placenta and to give all of them, etc.
