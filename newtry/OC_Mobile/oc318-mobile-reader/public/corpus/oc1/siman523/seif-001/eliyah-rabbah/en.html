[1] [Levush] And so it is permitted, etc. In the Gemara this is presented first, and I do not know why the Tur and Levush changed their order; also in siman 532 they wrote as in the Talmud (Malbushei Yom Tov). In my humble opinion it may be explained that they are equal — we should not say sprinkling afterbirth water specifically comes first because it is better that she have mercy and not need the second; and if they see it does not help, then do the second too. It teaches us whichever one he wishes may come first. And so Rambam in ch. 4 wrote it is permitted to sprinkle from the afterbirth and give all of them, etc.
