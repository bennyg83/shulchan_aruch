(ג) And Tosafot wrote in Shabbat daf 53a: in one version it is only permitted on Yom Tov but forbidden on Shabbat; and so is the view of the Rashba in Sefer Avodat HaKodesh; see there. But the Mordechai in the name of the Rashba and the Ri and Netiv ch. 13 daf 83 wrote that just as it is permitted on Yom Tov, so too on Shabbat; see there. Erekh HaShulchan seif 3.
