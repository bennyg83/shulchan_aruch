3) Tosafos write in Shabbos 53a, according to one resolution, that this was permitted only on Yom Tov but is forbidden on Shabbos. This also appears to be the view of Rashba in Avodas HaKodesh. See there. Mordechai in the name of Rashba, however, and Rabbeinu Yerucham, path 13, page 83, write that just as it is permitted on Yom Tov, it is likewise permitted on Shabbos. See there.
