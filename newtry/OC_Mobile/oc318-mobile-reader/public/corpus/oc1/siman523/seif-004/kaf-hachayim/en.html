(ד) [seif 2] One does not comb an animal with a comb, etc. — meaning with an iron comb that plucks hairs, and psik reisha, and it will not die. So Tosafot there. And so Mishna Berurah note 3; and the Levush wrote: although he does not intend to pluck but to scrape — it is puzzling, for davar she'eino mitkaven is permitted, nevertheless psik reisha that cannot help but pluck is forbidden. And so Magen Avraham sk 2; Razah note 2; Makor Chaim siman 42 note 4; Mishna Berurah there.
