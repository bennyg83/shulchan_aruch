4) [Se'if 2] An animal may not be scraped with a scraper, etc. This refers to an iron scraper, which pulls out hair, making the prohibited result inevitable. Tosafos there write this, as does Mishnah Berurah, subsection 3. Levush writes that although the person does not intend to pull out hair but only to scrape, and we rule that an unintended act is permitted, here the prohibited result is inevitable, because it is impossible that he will not pull out hair, and it is forbidden. Magen Avraham, subsection 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2; Mikraei Kodesh, siman 42, subsection 4; Mishnah Berurah there, write likewise.
