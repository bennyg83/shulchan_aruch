And to eat. See Taz. There is a slight omission of language: in Yoma 27b it objects to Rava, who said that a date is more than an egg's bulk: how did he eat two dates outside the sukkah? It answers: without their pits. Rava himself answers that the reason is that fruit does not require a sukkah. The Taz wrote that the first answer also agrees with Rava that fruit does not require a sukkah, but it did not occur to him to answer that way, and so he answered that it was without pits; see there. What he wrote about the text in the Tur I explained in the preceding letter.
