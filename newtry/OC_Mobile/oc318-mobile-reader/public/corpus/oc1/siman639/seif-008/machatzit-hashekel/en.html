(Small paragraph 8) A man, etc. At the time of service, etc. For it was taught: all are obligated in sukkah, priests, Levites, and Israelites. The Gemara asks: is this not obvious? It answers: priests were needed, for one might have thought that "you shall dwell" means in the manner that you live, just as a dwelling includes a man and his wife, etc.; and at the time of service it is impossible for him to be with his wife. It teaches us that although they are exempt at the time of service, when not at the time of service they are obligated. See Responsa Sha'ar Ephraim. Thus, in any case, at the time of service they are exempt because it is impossible during service to have a man and his wife.
For in Sukkah 28 it asks: why do I need a halachah to exempt women from sukkah? Let it follow because it is a positive commandment caused by time. Abaye answers: one might have thought "you shall dwell" means in the manner that you live, etc. Thus it says that the halachah given to Moses at Sinai teaches that in this matter we do not require "you shall dwell" in the manner that you live.
And one may say that nevertheless, etc. That he is distressed is another reason, not like what the Rema wrote. Granted, in this matter we do not require the manner of dwelling of a man and his wife; nevertheless, the husband is exempt because he is distressed. If we say that the Magen Avraham wanted to insert this reason into the Rema's words, one must greatly force the language of the Rema. There is another difficulty: in the Gemara we say (27b), from the verse "all the native-born in Israel shall dwell in sukkot," that all Israel can fulfill their obligation in one sukkah. Perforce this does not mean with partitions separating them, for then it would not be one sukkah. If so, either there is a difficulty on the law in siman 640, for it is not fit for sleeping, since it is not fit for a man and his wife, as all Israel sleep there and it is forbidden to have relations with one's wife in such a case; and one must say that they fulfill only eating. If so, it is proven that even if it is not fit for sleeping, nevertheless he has fulfilled eating (and so the responsum of the sage Chacham Tzvi objected). Or one must say that even if it is not fit for sleeping for a man and his wife, as long as it is fit for sleep for a man alone, it is called fit for sleeping. If so, this is difficult here.
