(There, seif 8) The custom is that we recite the blessing over the sukkah only at the time of eating. Note: Regarding the dispute between the Taz and the Magen Avraham, if one was sitting in the sukkah and returned to eat, whether he must recite the blessing or not, and also whether one must recite the blessing over sleep, see my novellae on Orach Chayim from the year 589, in the laws of Rosh Chodesh, siman 592, where I wrote about this; see there and examine carefully. See Magen Avraham, siman 17, s.v. "why we do not recite a blessing over matzah all seven days," because there is no mitzvah in eating it, only that one does not eat chametz, unlike sukkah, etc.; and see what I wrote regarding his intent and explanation in my work on the Torah, a discourse for the festival of Sukkot, year 590; see there and examine very carefully.
