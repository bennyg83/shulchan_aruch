(37) He was eating in the sukkah - The same applies when rain was falling while he was in his house and he ate in his house: he need not interrupt his meal in his house, but finishes it in his house.
(38) Until he finishes his meal - But if he has not yet sat down to eat in his house, even though he stopped eating in the sukkah and went home, he must return to the sukkah if the rain stopped.
