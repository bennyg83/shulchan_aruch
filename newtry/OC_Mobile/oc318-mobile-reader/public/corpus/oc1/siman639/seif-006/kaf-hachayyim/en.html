6) It is fitting for every God-fearing person not to speak inside the sukkah during the days of the festival of Sukkos except matters of holiness, and not words of utter vanity, for the mitzvah of sukkah possesses great sanctity, as is known to all wise-hearted people. The proof is that sanctity rests even upon the wood of the sukkah, and it is forbidden throughout all eight days, as stated in the preceding siman. Kitzur Shelah, tractate Sukkah. See the Shach on the Torah, Parashas Emor, who wrote as follows: 'The seven righteous ones are alluded to as coming and dwelling in the sukkah. Therefore, one must sit in it with awe and fear, shame and humility. He should not be drawn after eating and drinking, lest he come to disgrace. He should not bring a non-Jew into it, for the sukkah is the shade of faith, and a non-Jew has no faith; then the holiness flees, and the seven righteous ones utter severe curses, etc.' See there. Beit David, siman 444, cited his words.
