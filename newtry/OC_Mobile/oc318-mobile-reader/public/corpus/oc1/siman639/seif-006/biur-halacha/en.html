He was eating in the sukkah and went to his house, and the rain stopped, etc. In the Levush it says: when he began to eat; and so it is in Kol Bo and Rabbenu Yerucham. However, in Rashi it says: when he sat down, it stopped; and so the language of the baraita implies. In any event, if it stopped before he sat down, it implies that even according to Rashi he is obligated to return to the sukkah. So wrote the Eliyah Rabbah, and so I wrote plainly inside.
