Seif 7: until it rises, etc. According to the reading of all the Geonim, Rabbenu Chananel, and the Rif: say "until it becomes light" means from when dawn rises. That is to say, do not say "until it becomes light" means when the day is bright, which is later, but rather from when dawn rises, when it begins to become light.
And he awakens from his sleep. The Rashba wrote: even according to the reading of the Geonim, "until he awakens" is no less than a meal, and all the more so according to our reading.
One who is sleeping, etc. For everything depends on his distress, as stated there regarding Rav Yosef: "for me, etc."
And anyone exempt, etc. The Yerushalmi in the first chapter of Shabbat, halachah 1.
And when he leaves, etc. The first chapter of Avodah Zarah.
He is doing, etc. Sukkah there.
