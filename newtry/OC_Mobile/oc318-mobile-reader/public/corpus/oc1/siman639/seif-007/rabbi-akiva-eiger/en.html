Seif 7 Rain at night. In Tzlach, Berachot 49, he wrote that one should wait an hour or two until the rain stops and then eat in the sukkah. If he sees that the rain is not stopping, he should eat in the sukkah and not recite the blessing. If afterward the rain stops, he should go back and eat more than an egg's bulk in the sukkah and recite the blessing; see there. This is because if he eats less than that, he cannot recite the blessing, for perhaps the halachah follows the decisors who hold that on the first night one is obligated even while rain is falling; if so, he already fulfilled his obligation with what he ate while it was raining, and there is no obligation of a blessing unless he eats more than an egg's bulk, as on the other days of Sukkot.
