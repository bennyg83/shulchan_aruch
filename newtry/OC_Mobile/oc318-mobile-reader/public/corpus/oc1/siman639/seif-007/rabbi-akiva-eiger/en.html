Seif 7 — rain at night. In Tzelach on Berachot daf 49 he wrote to wait one or two hours until the rains cease and then eat afterward in the sukkah. And if he sees that the rains are not stopping, he should eat in the sukkah and not recite the berachah. And if afterward the rains stopped, he should return and eat more than a k'beitzah in the sukkah and recite the berachah — see there. And this is because if he eats less than that he cannot recite the berachah, for perhaps according to those who hold that on the first night one is obligated even while it is raining — if so, he already fulfilled [the mitzvah] with what he ate while it was raining, and there is no obligation of berachah except when eating more than a k'beitzah, as on the other days of Sukkot.
