Rain, etc. Just as one leaves because of rain, so too one leaves because of heat and mosquitoes. Hagahot Ashiri. Even if the person is not distressed by this, but the food is spoiled by it, the same applies. Likewise, if the weather is cold and the fatty foods congeal before him, he eats his whole meal in the house, and all the more so if he is distressed because of the cold. Bach and Levush; see siman 640, seif 3.
The first. On the second night he need not eat an olive's bulk in the sukkah if rain fell, for we are expert in the fixing of the month, and one need not eat in the sukkah when distressed. One who wants to be stringent need not recite kiddush in the sukkah, for he recited Shehecheyanu yesterday; rather, he should eat an olive's bulk there after his meal. Terumat HaDeshen, Darchei Moshe, Hagahot Minhagim, Magen Avraham. The Taz wrote that on the second night too there is an obligation of an olive's bulk when rain falls, but on the second night one should not recite "to sit in the sukkah" if rain fell; see there.
