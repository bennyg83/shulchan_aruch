Even. See Taz; Sukkah 26a, like Rava. Even if he rests his head between his knees, it is forbidden. Rashi wrote that Rava holds we are not concerned lest he fall asleep, and we rule in siman 44 that only between his knees is permitted; but with sukkah, even between his knees is forbidden. According to Rashi, it implies that when it answers, "one teaches that one may not sleep, whether temporary or fixed," and does not establish it as where his head is not between his knees, this implies that according to Rava this is the answer. See siman 44: some say temporary sleep is the time of walking 100 cubits, but less than that is not even temporary sleep. It is possible that the same applies regarding sukkah in such a case.
