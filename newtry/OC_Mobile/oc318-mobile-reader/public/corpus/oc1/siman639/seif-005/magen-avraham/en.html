If rain fell, etc. Just as one leaves the sukkah because of rain, so one leaves because of oppressive heat or mosquitoes, as Hagahos Ashiri writes. This applies even if the person is not distressed but the food is being spoiled. The same applies when the weather is cold and fatty foods congeal before him. He may eat his entire meal in the house, as stated in seif 7, according to Levush and Bach. See siman 640, seif 3. On the second night he need not eat an olive-volume in the sukkah, because we are knowledgeable regarding the fixed calendar and may rely upon the authorities who exempt one who is distressed. One who wishes to be stringent need not recite Kiddush in the sukkah, since he already recited Shehecheyanu the previous day. Rather, after his meal he should eat an olive-volume there, as stated by Terumas HaDeshen, Darchei Moshe, and Hagahos Maimoniyos.
