c) And the Dayin who blesses Ashina to sit in the Sukkah because he did not sleep and the blessing will be nullified. Rabbi P. Batra Dasukha and P. K. Dabrakot. And so on the Shabat C. Km2. And the Tur was written in the name of Rabbi Davrakt Sukkah who blesses the eating and exempts everything else. MTM C. Tathkid. Therefore, when he blesses the sukkah at the day meal, he will waive all sitting and sleeping and the eating and drinking of the day from here until he blesses another meal, and when he blesses at night, he waives the night's sitting and sleeping until he blesses the day meal:
