He has fulfilled his obligation. Although on the other days the amount of bread that may not be eaten outside the sukkah is an egg-volume, on this night even if he wishes to eat only an olive-volume, he may not eat it outside the sukkah. He therefore also fulfills his obligation with that amount, as Tur writes, and he must recite the blessing.
Fruit and roasted grain. This refers to Chol HaMoed. On Yom Tov and Shabbos, however, he must eat an olive-volume of bread, though it need not be eaten in a sukkah. See the ends of simanim 188 and 529.
And he should not eat, etc. This implies that he may not eat until nightfall. Hagahos Maimoniyos likewise writes that he may recite Kiddush slightly before night upon wine, but the blessing “leishev basukkah” and HaMotzi should be recited at night. Even on Pesach this would have been permitted were it not for the four cups, as stated at the beginning of siman 472. Nevertheless, because he recites the blessing over the sukkah and then Shehecheyanu, Kiddush too must be recited at night.
From midday onward. This requires study, for Maharil rules the same regarding matzah, as Ran also writes in the chapter Arvei Pesachim. According to what is written at the beginning of siman 471, however, on Erev Pesach the prohibition begins only from the tenth hour. Why should we be more stringent on Erev Sukkos? Perhaps because on Pesach he cannot eat bread, but only matzah ashirah, which is not as filling, whereas here bread is forbidden from midday. This requires study, for what is the source for this distinction? Furthermore, what is meant by saying that it is comparable to eating matzah? See the beginning of siman 529.
