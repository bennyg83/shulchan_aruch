A small sukkah. See Taz; Rashi, Sukkah 29a. In Tosafot there and in the Levush: in a small sukkah it is not the way to bring it into such a narrow place; the same applies in a sukkah. Also, perhaps the sukkah will burn and he will have no other. When it is not lit, even in a large one it is permitted. This is somewhat difficult from what is said in Sukkah 48a: "A large sukkah, what?" Shall we say that he brings in an unlit lamp there? In the Rosh, 29, it also implies that in a small sukkah it is forbidden, but in a large one it is permitted in every case; this requires examination. See Bach, who explains that he becomes frightened lest his sukkah burn and he leaves; with God's help, in Magen Avraham letter 2 this will be explained further. From the words of the Bach in seif 3, it appears that sukkah decorations reduce from the side only when they are set aside for all seven days and it is forbidden to remove them from there; but when he stipulated that he may remove them, they do not reduce the measure in a small sukkah. See simans 638 and 634, and this requires examination.
