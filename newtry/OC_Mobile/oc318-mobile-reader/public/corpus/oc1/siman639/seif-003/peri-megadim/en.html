Small Sukkah 17 Rashi Sukkah 29 A. And when you are there and dressed in a small one, there is no way to enter such a narrow place in the Sukkah and more, lest he burn a Sukkah and have no other, and if it is not lit, even in a large one (melts), and it is a little more difficult than what Damer Sukkah MH A. And I.E. B.A. letter B. will come again. From the words of the Bah in siman 19, it will be seen that there is a Sukkah from a small place on the side.
