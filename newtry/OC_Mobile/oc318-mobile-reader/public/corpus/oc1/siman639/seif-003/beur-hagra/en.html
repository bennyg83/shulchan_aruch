Seif 3: even if he ate, etc. The Yerushalmi there, cited by Tosafot and the Rosh: "Perhaps, just as there, only when he eats an olive's bulk of grain, etc." And he was uncertain whether one fulfills it with kinds of targima; therefore it says "an olive's bulk of bread" stringently.
If he wishes, etc. Tosafot there and in Berachot chapter 7, for fasting is forbidden, as written in the Yerushalmi.
And he should not eat, etc. As Tosafot wrote at the beginning of chapter Arvei Pesachim from the Tosefta, and from what is written there: until it becomes dark. And he should eat, etc. As written at the end of chapter Arvei Pesachim.
And he should not eat, etc. The Yerushalmi, cited by Tosafot and the Rosh.
From midday. He holds as the Ran wrote in chapter Arvei Pesachim, that the question was not resolved, and therefore one is stringent; for near minchah gedolah we learned in the Mishnah. But we do not rule so, as written at the beginning of siman 471 and in the Magen Avraham.
