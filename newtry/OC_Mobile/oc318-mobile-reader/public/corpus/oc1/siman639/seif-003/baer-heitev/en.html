Obligation. For "the fifteenth" is stated here, and "the fifteenth" is stated regarding the festival of matzah. Just as there the first night is obligatory and from then onward optional, so too here the first night is obligatory and from then onward optional. The Rosh explained that this refers to when rain fell, for otherwise there is an obligation on Yom Tov to eat bread and automatically one is obligated in sukkah. See Tosafot there, s.v. "if he wishes to eat," page 27a, and Berachot 49b, s.v. "if he eats," and examine carefully.
Bread. Although on the other days the measure of eating outside the sukkah is an egg's bulk, the first night, which is more obligatory, is different: even if he wants to eat only an olive's bulk, it is forbidden to eat it outside it; therefore he also fulfills his obligation of sukkah with it. Tur. He must recite the blessing. Magen Avraham; see there, and see Ateret Zekenim, seif 5.
Fruit. This refers to Chol HaMoed. But on Yom Tov and Shabbat one must eat an olive's bulk of bread, though it does not require sukkah; see siman 529, seif 1.
That eating. See siman 471 and the Magen Avraham.
