4) Two brothers or partners who have no place to make a large sukkah and made a small sukkah that does not suffice for all of them: Regarding eating, each may eat alone, one after the other. Regarding sleeping, one sleeps one night and the other sleeps another night. If the mitzvah is dear to them, they are deserving of blessing if one sleeps part of the night and the other sleeps part of the night. If watchmen of gardens are exempt, all the more so one who has no place. Maharar Avraham Azulai in handwritten glosses, in the name of a responsum of Rav Hai Gaon; Birkei Yosef, sec. 1; Shaarei Teshuvah.
