MA SKI Damokh Hatem. On Yoma, page 10, there is a name for Shina, leaves, not in the sukkah, and the most conclusion is there, and he interpreted it, Maha Damer Rabbi, when we were studying with Rabbi Ben Shmoe, they brought figs and grapes before us, and we ate them eating arai outside the sukkah, eating arai, there is no regular eating, no, and secondly, we ate them as eating arai. He established that he is more than an egg and also established his feast on them and did not sag in that he is more than an egg alone:
