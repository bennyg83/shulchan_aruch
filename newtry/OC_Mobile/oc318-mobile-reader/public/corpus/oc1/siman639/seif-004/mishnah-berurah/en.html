(28) And when he understands and examines carefully, etc. - Meaning that then it is better for him to study in the house, where the air is pleasant for him and broadens his mind.
(29) Settled upon him - Everything depends on the circumstances: if his mind is settled in the sukkah, where he has rest, he studies in the sukkah. Sometimes it is cold, and if he is distressed by sitting there long to read and analyze his study, he is exempt. If it is very burdensome for him to bring many books for his study into the sukkah, it is possible that he is exempt. However, if he has a place to leave them there for the entire festival, he is obligated, for this is not a burden.
(30) If he wishes, he prays, etc. - Meaning that in whatever place he has greater calm to pray with proper intention, there he should pray. If he has a synagogue in his city, he leaves his sukkah and goes to the synagogue, for during the rest of the year too he leaves his dwelling and goes to the synagogue. Havdalah he recites in the sukkah, for throughout the year he recites havdalah in his house.
