And studies. Everything depends on the circumstances: if he has peace of mind in the sukkah, he studies in the sukkah. It requires examination if he needs many books and it is burdensome for him to bring them up to the sukkah; one should be stringent. Magen Avraham. In Levush, siman 640, seif 4, it implies that he need not bring them there, as with pillows, and so it also appears there in the Shulchan Aruch, that he need not go to his fellow's sukkah to eat for this reason.
