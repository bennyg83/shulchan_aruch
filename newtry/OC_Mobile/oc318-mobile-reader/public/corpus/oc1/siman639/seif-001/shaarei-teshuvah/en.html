In the Shulchan Aruch: How is the mitzvah, etc. The Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of his grandfather, who brought a responsum of Rabbenu Hai Gaon, who wrote as follows: You asked about brothers or partners who have no place to make a large sukkah, and made a small sukkah that is not sufficient for all of them. He answered: regarding eating, each one can eat alone, one after another; but regarding sleeping, this one sleeps one night and that one sleeps one night. If the mitzvot are beloved to them, it is praiseworthy if this one sleeps part of the night and that one part. If watchmen of gardens are exempt, all the more so one who has no place to sleep in the sukkah. End quote. From this there is proof to what I wrote below, siman 660, letter 5, in the name of the Chacham Tzvi and Zera Emet; see there.
