And sleeps. See Taz and Darchei Moshe in the name of Maharil, and in Mahari Weil: one who wants to play dice should play in the sukkah. See Eliyah Rabbah 1: it is proper not to speak in the sukkah except words of Torah, and all the more so idle matters, which are forbidden even outside the sukkah; but dice in a manner that has no prohibition, one who is careful is praiseworthy. One should not cover the table with a cloth on Shabbat and Yom Tov in the house, but only in the sukkah. It implies that on Chol HaMoed one does not cover the table at all, even in the sukkah; and on Shemini Atzeret he should cover it also in the winter house; see there. It seems to me that people do not practice so, but cover the table even in the house, and also on Chol HaMoed cover the table, for it is called a festival; see siman 490, for in any case it is Yom Tov.
