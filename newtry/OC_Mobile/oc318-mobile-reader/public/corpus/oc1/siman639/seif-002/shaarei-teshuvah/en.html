Seif 2, in the Shulchan Aruch: And one who is stringent, etc. See Birkei Yosef, who wrote in the name of Tzeidah LaDerech that it was an excessive stringency; so too wrote Maharil. See below, siman 642. Rabbenu Yerucham wrote that with less than an egg's bulk there is no one who is stringent. In Eliyah Rabbah he objected to this, and the Birkei Yosef wrote to resolve it somewhat; see there. See Eliyah Rabbah, who brought Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah and Tanya in the name of R. Avigdor, that temporary eating on Shabbat and Yom Tov is considered fixed; and he objected to this, for if so, what is the Gemara asking from an incident that contradicts? Perhaps it was on Yom Tov, etc. See Birkei Yosef about this. See above, siman 168, what I wrote there in the name of Ginat Veradim regarding the blessing, that there is no distinction between Shabbat and weekday; if so, it appears the same applies here. So too it is implied in the Birkei Yosef there from what the decisors wrote regarding eating on the first night below, seif 17: it implies that only because eating on the first night is obligatory is one obligated even with an olive's bulk; without this, he would not be obligated, even if no rain fell. See Taz below, small paragraph 11; and so is the view of the Magen Avraham below, small paragraph 10, that on Yom Tov and Shabbat one must eat an olive's bulk of bread, but it does not require sukkah. This was brought in Ba'er Hetev below.
