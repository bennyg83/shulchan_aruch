And to eat. This is the language of the Bach: An individual person who sits to drink, there is reason to say that it is not considered fixed; but members of a group who established themselves to drink, this is complete fixedness, especially in a place where the practice is to drink together in a fixed manner without any eating, for this is complete fixedness and requires a sukkah and the blessing "to sit in the sukkah," as appears to me in halachah. But in practice it is proper to be stringent not to drink in a fixed manner except during the meal, to remove oneself from dispute; and the same applies to fixed eating of meat and cheese and the like. End quote. The Taz concluded that one should not recite "to sit in the sukkah" over drinking at all, even in a fixed manner, for even if afterward he is obligated in a sukkah, nevertheless the blessing he recites over eating exempts it entirely. Even if he establishes himself to drink before eating, such as drinking spirits in these lands, nevertheless the blessing he will recite afterward at the time of eating exempts him. Therefore one should bless only over eating, as written at the end of the siman. End quote; see there, and see Taz, small paragraph 20.
Special. One should not cover the table in the winter house, but only in the sukkah, on Shabbat and Yom Tov; however, on Shemini Atzeret he should also cover it in the winter house. Hagahot Minhagim. The Eliyah Zuta wrote: it implies that even in the sukkah there is no obligation to cover it on Chol HaMoed, and the fifth day is during Chol HaMoed of Pesach.
