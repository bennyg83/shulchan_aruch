The lamp. See Taz. In Rashi, Sukkah 29: in a small sukkah one does not bring in an earthenware lamp; hence in a large sukkah one may bring it. The Beit Yosef, in the name of the Rokeach, etc. If it is coated with glaze, one may say it is permitted, for there is no repulsiveness; see Darchei Moshe about this.
