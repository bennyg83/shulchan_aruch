Note: Even though one who is occupied with a mitzvah is exempt from gladdening his wife, even when setting out on a journey, as is explained in Tur and Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De'ah siman 184, seif 10, in the gloss, and so the book Nishmat Adam objected, there is no difficulty. Here we require dwelling in the manner that one lives. In his dwelling, when he would need to gladden his wife for a mitzvah matter, he would be obligated to leave the house for a room to attend to her. If so, there is no mitzvah of sukkah here at all. For this reason one who is distressed is exempt from the sukkah, even though he is obligated in all the mitzvot. Therefore it is not like a dwelling, for one who is distressed leaves it, and automatically there is no mitzvah of sukkah here.
