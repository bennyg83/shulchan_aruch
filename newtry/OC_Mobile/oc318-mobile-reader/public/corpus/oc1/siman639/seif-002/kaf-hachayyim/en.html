What is the manner of the mitzvah of dwelling in the sukkah, etc. The reason we recite a blessing over the sukkah each time, whereas over matzah we recite a blessing only on the first night, is that through eating matzah one fulfills a positive commandment only on the first night, as it is written, 'In the evening you shall eat matzos.' Regarding sukkah, however, every moment that one dwells there he fulfills, 'You shall dwell in sukkos for seven days.' Derashos Maharil, Laws of Sukkah; Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, sec. 3; Magen Avraham at the end of the siman. See above, siman 475, secs. 70 and 71.
