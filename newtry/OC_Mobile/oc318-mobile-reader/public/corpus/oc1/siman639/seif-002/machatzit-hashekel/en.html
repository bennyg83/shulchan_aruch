(Small paragraph 2) He places it, etc. So that it not ignite, etc. The Bach wrote that according to this reason, even if it is not exactly seven by seven handbreadths, it is forbidden, excluding the reason that the lamp, since it is fixed there, reduces from the valid measure of the sukkah. See the Taz, small paragraph 3.
The custom, etc. See the end of siman 673. Meaning: there he wrote that it is forbidden to light a Chanukah lamp in an old earthenware lamp, because it is repulsive; but if it is glazed or made of glass, it has the law of metal utensils, for it is not repulsive and is permitted. The same applies here.
