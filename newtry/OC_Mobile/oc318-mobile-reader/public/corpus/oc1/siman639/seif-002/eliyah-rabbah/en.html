[2] [Levush] And he studies, etc. Everything depends on the circumstances: if he has peace of mind in the sukkah, he studies in the sukkah. It requires examination if he needs many books and it is burdensome for him to bring them up; in Levush, siman 640, seif 4, it implies that he need not, as with pillows, and so it also implies there in Shulchan Aruch, that he need not go to his fellow's sukkah to eat for this reason (Magen Avraham). It seems to me that this is specifically when he needs to bring them out every day, but if he can leave them there all the days of Sukkot and study there regularly, he must bring them up, similar to the matters mentioned above.
