6) There. One may knead only with water that rested overnight. This means water left overnight in a utensil after being drawn. Rashi, Pesachim 42a, explains that during Nissan the springs are warm because the rainy season has not yet ended. During the rainy season the sun travels low in the sky, so the world is cold and the springs are warm. The water is therefore left overnight to cool. These are his words. Others explain that the sun travels beneath the earth at night and warms the springs. The only remedy is therefore for the water not to remain connected to the ground at night, as explained by Beis Yosef. See there. Derashos Maharil writes that the water must cool for twenty-four hours before kneading: twelve daytime hours while still in the well, when the sun is distant from it, and twelve nighttime hours after it is drawn. See there.
