Even without pressure, etc. R' Eliyahu Mizrachi, Semag, and Semak permit even l'chatchila when impossible otherwise; appears first night one may rely so as not to nullify eating matzah mitzvah since b'shogeg permitted; not other days — eat other things. Beit Yosef brought Mordechai: incident Pesach Sunday, tekufah fell Thursday night before; nevertheless not concerned, kneaded with water drawn at night; R' Yehuda HaChasid wrote no concern — keeper of mitzvah; Mordechai after: baked only for day 1, Yom Tov does not prepare for fellow; Rokeach brought this law but there writes tekufa fell Thursday night only. It appears he understood tekufa fell Shabbos night, drew at night, baked Motzei matzah — night 1 Pesach; thus Mordechai explained; they did not want draw Motzei night per Rashi requiring 12 hours always, not concerned tekufa at drawing hour; R' Yehuda HaChasid said well done keeper of mitzvah — implies bake Pesach night when Sunday; otherwise only Motzei bake no place for Mordechai; Beit Yosef further even so Mordechai not settled — wonder why crossed tekufa danger, not draw Friday day; answered perhaps hold by day no remedy per R' Eliyahu Mizrachi and Mizrachi — Beit Yosef; perhaps hold such not mayim shelanu — Mizrachi; Bach asked why concerned Shabbos drawing tekufa — implies without tekufa no doubt; even without, Shabbos drawing prohibition; you will say such permitted Shabbos — then how draw afterward at night; why concerned tekufa at all since not at kneading hour; answered hold all water detached at tekufah hour dangerous even after — not answering other questions to explain Beit Yosef per his understanding. Mahari Weil other explanation per Beit Yosef — I did not reconcile; truth in Mordechai unlike Beit Yosef: drew Motzei Friday night only after drawing that night or Shabbos night tekufa — no difference; why Mordechai wrote Thursday night not Thursday 6: drew as law Motzei Friday bein ha shemashot, baked Yom Tov night — teaches two laws: tekufah does not harm what they kneaded for mitzvah though passed in detached; second that night baked only for Yom Tov 1 — two laws independent, both occurred — therefore Mordechai wrote also fell, etc., another novelty there; Shabbos obviously no drawing as Rokeach like filling garden end Eruvin — all Bach's questions inapplicable — in my humble opinion.
