Matzah. And in Rambam it implies it is rich matzah. And for everyone one must eat a kezayit of matzah aside from spices. Magen Avraham.

And pepper. Even one grain makes the entire dough chametz; and the same applies to a little lime — Darkei Moshe. It appears specifically when kneaded into it; but if it fell on it one removes it and it is permitted. It appears ginger is treated like pepper, and possibly the same for anise and saffron. Magen Avraham and Ateret Zekenim ruled to permit in eating all kinds of spices except pepper. And even in pepper one forbids only in eating like salt, as written in s.k. 15; and so Bach, Ateret Zekenim, and Kaf HaChayim. Dough kneaded in wine into which beer became mixed — Hagahot Beit Yosef wrote that since there is no water there is no need to be particular — Ateret Zekenim; see responsum Darkei Noam Orach Chayim siman 6 and Ginat Veradim general rule 4 siman 9.

Lime. This is live lime; but lime in a wall that already dried and hardened like stone — it appears plainly it does not forbid. Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 13.
