And peppers. Magen Avraham wrote: even one speck forbids; and specifically when kneaded into it; but if it fell upon it, remove it and it is permitted; and ginger has the same law as peppers; and possibly the same applies to cloves and saffron; end quote. And my brother-in-law the Rav in Sefer Ateres Zekenim ruled to permit for eating with all kinds of spices; and so it appears to me is primary, for the poskim mentioned only peppers; and so we say, "good is sharp pepper," and the Aruch explained because it is sharper than all kinds of spices; and if so one should not compare other kinds of spices to it; and even regarding peppers one should forbid only for eating, as with salt, as I wrote in s.k. 18; and so wrote Bach and Olas Shabbas that they are alike.
