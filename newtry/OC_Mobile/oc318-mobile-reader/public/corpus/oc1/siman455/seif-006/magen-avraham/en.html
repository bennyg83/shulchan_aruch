Sarah. And who should eat as an exalted olive, except the spice, look at the landings of a page as a “g” in his daughter:

Since it has. Implies one fulfills with it on the first nights; however in Rambam it implies rich matzah; and so Maggid Mishneh chapter 6.

And pepper. Even one grain makes entire dough chametz; likewise a little lime (Darkei Moshe, Mahariv). It appears specifically kneaded into it; if fell on, remove and permitted. Ginger like pepper; possibly anise and saffron too. In hints it implies pepper permitted b'dieved; nevertheless do not be lenient — see above seif 65 regarding salt.
