(6) There. One may knead only with mayim shelanu — meaning ours in another vessel after drawing. Rashi explained (Pesachim 42a): because in Nissan days the springs are hot since the rainy season still applies, and in the rainy season the sun travels in the lower sky; therefore the whole world is cold and springs hot; therefore we leave overnight and they cool — end quote. Some explain the reason: because at night the sun travels under the earth and heats the springs; therefore their only remedy is not to be attached at night, as Beit Yosef explains — see there. In derashot of Maharil he wrote the water must cool 24 hours before kneading: 12 hours by day while attached in the well when the sun is far, and 12 hours at night after drawing — see there.
