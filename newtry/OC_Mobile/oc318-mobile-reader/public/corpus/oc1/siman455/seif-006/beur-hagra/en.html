Seif 6: since it has. In the Gemara there they say specifically that he added flour to it; and Tosafot wrote this sugya argues on that of Zevachim where they said even though the majority is rice, etc. — majority of Torah law; and we rule like the sugya of Zevachim that taste in a kezayit in a place of Torah law obligates, as Tosafot wrote; and likewise Tur explained in Yoreh De'ah; and this is what Shulchan Aruch wrote: since, etc.

Nevertheless. The Ra'avad in chapter 12 of Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot forbids in cooked dishes that become chametz; and what is written in Menachot "seasoned," etc., and so in Tosefta and Yerushalmi chapter Kol Sha'ah — matzah seasoned is discussed when seasoning is after baking; but in Yerushalmi chapter 2 halachah 4 it is not so, for R' Simon in the name of R' Yehuda bar Levi said: that kezayit with which a person fulfills on the 15th of Nissan must not have liquid come into it, etc.; and it was taught: one fulfills with seasoned matzah even though it has no taste of grain — they thought to say seasoned means liquids; it was said seasoned means spiced; nuts — therefore permitted even before baking, for after baking rich matzah is not relevant; and as written siman 462 seif 4; and so Ra'avad in laws of matzah chapter 6 because of rich matzah it is forbidden.

And pepper. For it is not called spice as Tosafot wrote chapter 1 of Yoma in the name of Tosefta; and pepper is likewise as spice — see Tosafot Pesachim 105a s.v. atei, etc.; and it heats as written in Niddah: she ate pepper and saw — see there in Shulchan Aruch siman 129.
