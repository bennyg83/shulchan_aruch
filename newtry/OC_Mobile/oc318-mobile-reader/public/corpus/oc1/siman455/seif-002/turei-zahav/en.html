L'chatchila one should draw, etc. Rashi wrote main stringency of water not left overnight is in springs and cisterns that boil, but not rivers — in Nissan sun does not travel in water, but sages were concerned for most towns that have only cistern and spring water; R' Avigdor wrote this is nothing, for even flowing rivers sun strikes by day and heats them; therefore better l'chatchila to draw from river when possible.
