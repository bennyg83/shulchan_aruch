And this is what the language of the Talmud calls a dut: gathered water in a structure in the ground where the building intervenes between it and the ground, and it is also covered above, so that they are as if drawn; and one should not compare to this what we call a pipe-castine: even though it is covered above, one should not draw from it to use them immediately upon drawing, because every hour and moment it flows into it from the river; however, if one wants to leave them overnight like other water and to draw at its proper time, it is permitted to draw under the pipe, or what is called a flowing pipe, as from the river or the well from which it flows; and likewise from the castine itself if it is covered.
