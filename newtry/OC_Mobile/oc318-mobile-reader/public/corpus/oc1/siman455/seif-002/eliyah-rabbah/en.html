[2] Bein ha shemashot, etc. Or close to bein ha shemashot (Rama). Bach wrote: if drawn while it was still day and left overnight, or drawn after the beginning of the night and left overnight, it is permitted even l'chatchila — until here. It appears to me specifically before midnight; but after midnight it is forbidden, for we rely on Semak that overnight standing is from midnight until dawn — and he was precise and wrote "after the beginning of the night." Also it appears to me that after dawn it is permitted immediately even though not yet twelve hours from drawing — and so Bach end of siman 4 explicitly; Chak Yaakov did not understand so. Semak wrote in Hagahot: it is proper to wait to knead until the day brightens much lest one err by moonlight.
