While it is still day — know that what Rama wrote in the gloss "close to bein ha shemashot" is not the Mechaber's view but his own view, as explained in Darkei Moshe; but for the Mechaber it is permitted l'chatchila to draw by day at any hour he wishes, provided he waits to knead with them until the morrow, as his view is explained in Beit Yosef; and so Gra, Peri Chadash, and Maamar Mordechai ruled as the main view; therefore Maamar Mordechai wrote that where there is some pressure one may fill by day while the day is still long, since according to Maran, may he rest in Eden, one may do so l'chatchila.
