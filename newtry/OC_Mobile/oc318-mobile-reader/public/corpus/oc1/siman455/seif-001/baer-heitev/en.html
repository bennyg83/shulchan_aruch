Close to bein ha shemashot. By right one should draw them at bein ha shemashot itself; and since not everyone knows how to measure bein ha shemashot, therefore one draws them close to bein ha shemashot after sunset (before moonrise). B'dieved, if one drew them while it was still day or drew after the beginning of the night, it appears from the words of the later authorities that one may be lenient to knead with them after 12 hours; and in responsum of Magen Avraham siman 32 he wrote one should not be lenient unless in a time of pressure or great loss. See Taz who wrote it is better after drawing than to draw early. However Maharil wrote the opposite — end quote: it is better to draw a little early than to delay — see Ateret Zekenim; his words appear correct and so implies Magen Avraham. Kaf HaChayim.

Not harmful. And even if they stood in the sun. Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham.

To cover them. Specifically an earthenware or wood vessel and the like — covering helps; but if they are in a glass vessel, covering does not help. Kaf HaChayim — see there.

On the night of the 5th. Explanation: for those who bake at Motzei Shabbos and cannot draw at Motzei Shabbos nor on Friday evening when they need to draw at bein ha shemashot and Shabbos does not prepare for Yom Tov (and if not for preparation it would certainly be permitted, for all shevut is permitted at bein ha shemashot) — therefore they draw on the night of the 5th that belongs to the 6th day, Beit Yosef; if so, also those who bake on the second day of Yom Tov are forbidden to draw at bein ha shemashot on Yom Tov, for one Yom Tov does not prepare for another and they need to draw on the eve of the 14th. It appears to me that if he forgot to draw on the night of the 5th he may draw on Friday while it is still day on this Erev Pesach. Or he may command a gentile to draw at bein ha shemashot — see siman 307. Or he may take water drawn in his house even though not drawn for the sake of the mitzvah, since it is impossible otherwise. Magen Avraham; see Kaf HaChayim.

Iron. One takes new or clean iron and hangs it on a cord in the water, but one does not put his hand in the water, for the hand heats the water. In the rest of the year one places salt or a seal. Darkei Moshe, Magen Avraham. Taz wrote: it appears to me that on other nights, if one did not place iron in them to guard from the tekufah, one may pour them out if one can easily be without that water — which is not so with these waters of matzah of the mitzvah: it is forbidden to pour out, for it appears contemptuous of the verse "a keeper of a mitzvah knows no evil" — see there; and Kaf HaChayim practice filtering water — Maharil. Mateh Moshe wrote one should not take a vessel to draw water that had honey or fruit juice in it, for fruit juice with water hastens to become chametz as siman 462. (And likewise one should not take a copper vessel — Magen Avraham — and I have not seen practice so.) It appears to me that through boiling it is permitted. Magen Avraham.

A gentile. This is l'chatchila.

Designated. One should say at the time of drawing for the sake of matzah of the mitzvah — Maharil and Mahariv; and Maharil wrote further that some are accustomed to take designated water each night independently.
