One may not knead, etc. One should point the lamed in shelanu with kamatz and not patach, for otherwise it speaks of him alone like u-lanu in Gibeah (so too in his Shulchan Aruch). If one kneaded, it is forbidden only in eating; but in benefit it is permitted immediately before it comes to leavening. Even where it is impossible, one should not permit even b'dieved since there is concern for becoming chametz — unlike what Shulchan Aruch wrote to be lenient in a time of pressure. However, water drawn while it was still day and left overnight, or drawn after the beginning of the night and left overnight, is permitted even l'chatchila.
