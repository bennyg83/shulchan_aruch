1) [Se'if 1] One may knead only with water that rested overnight. This applies even to matzah not intended for the mitzvah, because the concern is that the dough might become chametz. Tashbetz, Ma'amar Chametz, page 30, and Responsa Tashbetz, volume 2, siman 87; Birkei Yosef, subsection 1; Kisei Eliyahu, subsection 1; Sha'arei Teshuvah, at the beginning of this siman. Rosh at the end of the chapter Kol Sha'ah and Maggid Mishneh, chapter 5, halachah 11, write likewise, as cited by Beis Yosef. Kol Bo, page 38a, writes that the Geonim and most Sages agreed to this, contrary to Rabbi Yitzchak ibn Ghayyat. See there. Erech HaShulchan, subsection 1, cites this. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1, writes likewise.
