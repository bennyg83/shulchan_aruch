Near bein hashemashot. See Baer Heitev; and see Peri Chadash, siman 459, se'if 5, who wrote that even though in Yoreh De'ah, siman 36, it is stated that when they stood a bit in the house they are called lukewarm, nevertheless mayim shelanu are colder still; and nevertheless, if they became heated like the warmth of saliva, or like water that one draws in the summer from the rivers, they have the status of lukewarm and must be burned immediately; end quote. And see Sefer Beis Ephraim on terefos, in the kuntres of the responsum, siman 20, at length in clarifying this matter; and there it is explained that specifically when they stand in the house in the summer do we take care about this, which is not so in the days of Nisan, of which Rashi wrote in Pesachim that they are still the rainy season, etc.; see there. And there it is explained that one may say that whenever they became even somewhat hot, even though not like the warmth of saliva, one should not knead with them; see there. And one should be concerned for this and set them in a cold place so that they do not come to any warmth at all. And see Mor UKetzi'ah, that in our countries, which are northern lands, where in the days of Nisan it is a cold time, one may be lenient at least with water that did not rest overnight, after the fact in a time of pressure; see there. And it appears that everything depends on the judgment of the posek according to the season and the time.
