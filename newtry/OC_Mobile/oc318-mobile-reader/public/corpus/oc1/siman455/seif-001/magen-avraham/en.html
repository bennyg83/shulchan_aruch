Close to bein ha shemashot. By right one should draw at bein ha shemashot itself so water cools 12 hours attached by day when springs are cold, and 12 hours in the air at night as Maharil wrote; but since impossible to measure bein ha shemashot precisely, permitted to draw close to bein ha shemashot. One should not be lenient to draw by day, for per R' Eliyahu Mizrachi it is not correct — all the more so not to draw at night, for in this all agree. Beit Yosef wrote: if drawn after beginning of night they have no remedy and the water is forbidden forever per R' Eliyahu Mizrachi — end quote Darkei Moshe; Bach wrote b'dieved if drawn at night one may be lenient to knead by day; see siman 261 measure of bein ha shemashot.

Not harmful. And even if they stood in the sun (Beit Yosef, Eliyah Rabbah); siman 459 seif 65.

They must be covered. Nothing will fall into the content, but it is not to be felt that it has already sunk

Thursday night. Explanation: for those who bake at Motzei Shabbos and cannot draw at Motzei Shabbos nor Friday when they need bein ha shemashot and Shabbos does not prepare for Yom Tov — therefore draw Thursday night belonging to Friday (Beit Yosef); see siman 467; if so, also those baking on second day of Yom Tov forbidden to draw at bein ha shemashot on Yom Tov, for one Yom Tov does not prepare for another and they need eve of 14th; see siman 458. It appears if he forgot Thursday night he may draw Friday while still day on this Erev Pesach, or command gentile at bein ha shemashot — see siman 307 and 464; or take water drawn in his house even if not drawn for mitzvah since impossible otherwise.

From rivers. Because the sun travels in Nissan days under the ground and therefore wells boil; but melting of snow and rain always boils.

And not from wells. Because the sun travels under the ground.

One may not pour out. For "a keeper of a mitzvah knows no evil."

Or falling tekufah. And Yoreh De'ah siman 116 wrote there is danger only when drinking at the hour tekufah falls (and so Shulchan Aruch HaRav).

Iron. One does not put his hand in the water, for the hand heats — only hang on a cord (Maharil); rest of year salt or seal (Hagahot Maimoniyot, Darkei Moshe). Practice filters water (Maharil). Mateh Moshe: do not take vessel that had honey or fruit juice — fruit juice with water hastens to become chametz siman 462. (Boiling permitted — it appears.) Also not in copper vessel that heats — end quote; however I have seen kneading in copper troughs — Semag see there.

Wooden vessel, etc. — the same for glazed earthenware; see siman 473 seif 3.
