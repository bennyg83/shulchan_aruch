One in two. It is implied specifically one in two; and see in my work mem-yud, klal 51, s.k. 6; and see Magen Avraham; and it is implied from the language of the poskim brought in Beis Yosef that if it became mixed during the kneading it is not nullified, for specifically if mixed before the kneading do we require [bitul]. And my brother-in-law the Rav in Sefer Ateres Zekenim concluded after the fact to permit even in such a case; and see what I wrote in s.k. 16; and Magen Avraham wrote that it is permitted to mix them even ab initio in order to cool them; and this requires further study; and the same applies to fire-heated waters: this cooling helps according to everyone; and so wrote Olas Shabbas; and see s.k. 13. But after baking it is certainly permitted to brush the faces of the matzos with salt water and return them to the oven, as they do on Chol HaMoed. And specifically on Chol HaMoed is it permitted to brush; but on the first two nights it is forbidden (Ateret Zekenim).
