Implies less than that is not nullified (Beit Yosef); but Semag and Semak wrote nullified in majority, and so Yoreh De'ah siman 98; we rule see Ateret Zekenim; it appears permitted to mix them l'chatchila, for the reason is not prohibition but when not left overnight they are hot and when mixed they become cold; if so permitted to cool l'chatchila — requires study.
