(4) One who collected rainwater — permitted to knead matzah with it and no overnight standing is required according to all, provided he does not leave them where their coldness will dissipate. Radbaz part 5 siman 2002; brought by Birkei Yosef note 4, Shaarei Teshuvah note 9.
