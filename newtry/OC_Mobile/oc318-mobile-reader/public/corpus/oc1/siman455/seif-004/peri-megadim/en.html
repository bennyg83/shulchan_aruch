Iron. See Taz; and salt helps for the tekufah; see Yoreh De'ah 116 and Peri Chadash at the end of the laws of Rosh Chodesh; and in mayim shelanu it is forbidden to put into the water salt that rested overnight, for that becomes fruit juices with water and hastens chimutz; see siman 460 and 472 in Magen Avraham 7; and God willing it will be explained there. It is forbidden to draw through a gentile for matzas mitzvah; Peri Chadash wrote the reason: that preparations for a mitzvah are like the mitzvah, as in siman 460; and after the fact it is permitted; and when one draws one should say for the sake of matzas mitzvah; see Chok Yaakov, letters 8 and 9. And see what I write, God willing, in Magen Avraham letter 16 about this. And Darkei Moshe wrote: iron, or to seal it with a cloth; it is implied that when sealed with a cloth there is no power to harm, as we find regarding anything tied and sealed, etc. What is written in the gloss, new earthenware vessels, and of wood one need not insist, will be explained, God willing, in Magen Avraham letter 10; and the words of the Levush se'if 7 require a bit of further study.
