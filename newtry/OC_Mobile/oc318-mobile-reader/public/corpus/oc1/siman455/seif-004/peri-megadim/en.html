Iron — Taz. Salt helps for tekufah — see Yoreh De'ah 116 end ch. 5 Ra'ah Chadash. In mayim shelanu forbidden to put our salt in them — fruit juice with water hastens to become chametz siman 462, 472 in Magen Avraham 7; explained there. Forbidden for gentile to draw for matzah of mitzvah — Peri Chadash: reason like preparing for mitzvah siman 462; b'dieved permitted; when drawing say for matzah of mitzvah — Kaf HaChayim notes 8, 9. And see Eliyah Rabbah in Magen Avraham note 16 from this. Iron or seal with cloth — implies sealing with cloth cannot harm as we find all engraved and sealed, etc. — see gloss 24 months; wooden vessel no concern — explained Magen Avraham note 10; Levush seif 7 requires study.
