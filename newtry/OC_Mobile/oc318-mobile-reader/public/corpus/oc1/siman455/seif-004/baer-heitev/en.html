One in two. It implies specifically one in two, Beit Yosef; but Semag and Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote it is nullified in the majority, and so too in Yoreh De'ah siman 98, Magen Avraham; and it is implied from the words of the poskim that if it became mixed during kneading it is not nullified — we require specifically that it became mixed before kneading. (And Kaf HaChayim ruled in the name of his brother-in-law the Gaon, may he rest in Eden, that he permitted even at the time of kneading.) And Ateret Zekenim raised that b'dieved one may permit even in such a case. And Magen Avraham wrote it appears permitted to mix them l'chatchila, for the reason is not because of prohibition but when not left overnight they are hot and when mixed they become cold; if so, it is permitted to cool them l'chatchila — requires study. And Kaf HaChayim wrote the same applies to copper-kettle water — this cooling helps according to all — and so too Ateret Zekenim.
