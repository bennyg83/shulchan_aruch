(s.k. 4) On Thursday night, etc. Explanation: for those who bake, etc.: that is to say, for it is explained in siman 458 that when Pesach falls on Sunday one kneads on Friday after midday; and obviously one should draw on Thursday night; rather the Rama needed to write this for those who customarily bake at Motzei Shabbos, as the Tur wrote in siman 458.
And see siman 667: that even a light preparation is forbidden from one Yom Tov to its fellow.
See the beginning of this siman: that one may rely in this on the view of Beis Yosef; see there.
And after the fact the Bach too is lenient if they were drawn at night; and this is called after the fact.
See siman 307: that a shevut of a shevut is permitted in a place of mitzvah; therefore it is permitted to command a gentile.
And siman 460: that is to say, although the Rama wrote in this se'if that it is forbidden to draw through a gentile. To this he brought proof from siman 460, where he wrote that kneading and baking are certainly forbidden through a gentile; and even so Magen Avraham wrote there that where it is impossible otherwise it is permitted through a gentile when a Jew stands over him; all the more so drawing. Or one may take water that was drawn in his house, that is, mayim shelanu, even though they were not drawn for the sake of the mitzvah.
