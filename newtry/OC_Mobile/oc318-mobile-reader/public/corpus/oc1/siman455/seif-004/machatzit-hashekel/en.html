(s.k. 4) On the night of the 5th, etc. Explanation: for those who bake, etc. — meaning it is explained siman 458: when Pesach falls on Sunday one kneads Friday evening after chatzot; obviously one draws Thursday night. Rama needed to write this for those who bake at Motzei Shabbos, as Tur siman 458.
