4) One who collected rainwater may knead matzah with it. According to all opinions, it need not rest overnight, provided that it is not placed where it will lose its chill. Radbaz, volume 5, siman 2,172; Birkei Yosef, subsection 4; Sha'arei Teshuvah, subsection 9. Halachos Ketanos, volume 1, siman 97, however, distinguishes: water collected during the day must rest overnight, while water collected at night need not. See there. Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur and Birkei Yosef, there, cite his ruling. See also below, subsection 64, regarding this matter.
