[4] And when carrying, etc., under the air, etc. Requires study: if it deals with kneading, he already wrote at the end of the seif; if when drawing, he wrote one need not cover them. Perhaps here it deals at night when he wants to place them in an open place he should cover, for then he might forget tomorrow — no concern. People always leave them covered at night even under the ceiling, and we are not concerned they will heat from this covering; the reason appears to me we are concerned something might fall in; and although Terumat HaDeshen wrote this is extra caution, it appears that is when carrying from river to house while engaged with it; but where they stand all night there is more concern.
