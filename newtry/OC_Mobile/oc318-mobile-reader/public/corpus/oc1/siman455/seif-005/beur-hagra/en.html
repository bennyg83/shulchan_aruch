Seif 5: it is customary, etc. And even. They bring proof to forbid from what is written chapter 8 of Shabbos and in Gittin: matzah that is not salted; and in Chullin they said salted is like boiling and is like kneading in hot water — as above seif 3; and chapter 4 of Shabbos; and not regarding salt that adds vapor; and chapter 2 of Bava Batra: they distance salt because of heat — as written in the Gemara there. And this is what Baer Heitev wrote: and even b'dieved, etc.; and so Mordechai; and as above seif 3. But many poskim permit and reject their proofs: that which "not salted" means it lacks preparation like "not sharpened"; and that which "salted is like boiling" — they state explicitly only when it is not eaten because of its salt — see Yoreh De'ah siman 98 and siman 105; and likewise that of Shabbos and Bava Batra where there is much salt; and Rashba siman 224 that it is not like boiling but to absorb and extract, etc.; but to heat, etc. And Ran brings proof to permit from end of chapter Kol Sha'ah from what is written: and soaked in water and salt is forbidden — implying specifically when cooking it heats; but for bread, no — and so Rashba, except he wrote people are accustomed to be stringent.
