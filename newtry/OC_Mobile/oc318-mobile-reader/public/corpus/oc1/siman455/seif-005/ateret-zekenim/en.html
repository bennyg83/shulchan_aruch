It is customary not to, etc. But after baking it is certainly permitted to scrape the faces of the matzot with salt water and return them to the oven as they do on chol ha'moed with salt water and eggs; and specifically on chol ha'moed it is permitted to scrape, but on the first two nights it is forbidden; and even to dip them in salt that the Yerushalmi permitted as below siman 475 — people refrain; and in this too there is concern because of do not forsake, etc. Nevertheless in this too b'dieved one need not be concerned even if he scraped after baking in salt water and eggs and spices as on chol ha'moed, for this scraping is no worse than spices with which matzah was baked through which one fulfills the obligation of matzah (my teacher in Bach).
