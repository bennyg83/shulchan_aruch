5) It is permitted to knead with water when a non-Jew makes an incidental statement that it rested overnight in the non-Jew's house. No prohibition has been established, and the matter is rabbinic. One who is stringent will be blessed. The pious Rabbi Yosef Molcho in manuscript responsum, siman 103; Birkei Yosef, subsection 5. It appears that one certainly may not fulfill the mitzvah obligation with these matzos, since the water was not guarded for the mitzvah. Even for other matzos there is reason for concern, since perhaps a substance that causes leavening or something similar fell into the water. In pressing circumstances, one may permit it after filtering. Sha'arei Teshuvah, there.
