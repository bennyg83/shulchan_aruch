Salt. But after baking it is certainly permitted to scrape the faces of the matzah in salt water and return them to the oven as on chol ha'moed. And specifically on chol ha'moed scraping is permitted, but on the first two nights it is forbidden — see Taz.

To forbid. That is, in eating; but in benefit it is certainly permitted. And if he put a little salt in it, if it is baked immediately it is permitted in eating, for a little salt does not heat it entirely — Magen Avraham. And all this if one kneaded with water; but if one kneaded with fruit juice, Bach ruled one should not forbid b'dieved.
