In a cistern. See Taz, who wrote that is near their drawing; and so it is in the Levush; at first glance the meaning is: when they are lenient in a time of pressure, that is with what was drawn near bein hashemashot; and according to the Mechaber in se'if 1 while still daytime and it did not stand twelve hours, then one may be lenient in a time of pressure; for according to R' Eliezer of Metz, when one drew at bein hashemashot it is permitted to knead with them immediately; which is not so when one draws at the beginning of the night: according to R' Eliezer of Metz there is never a remedy according to the second explanation in Beis Yosef; if so one should not rely on this even in a cistern in a time of pressure in this; and nevertheless it appears that in a time of pressure according to everyone it is permitted. And see Chok Yaakov letter 11; and in Eliyah Rabbah 6: this cistern, or that there is a pipe there called a ra'er, or a hole at the place of the springing from which the water issues; and one may say that the ra'er waters are nullified with the water in the cistern; see Magen Avraham 15; and God willing in Magen Avraham letter 11 it will be explained further. And those wells with a ra'er that are inside houses where they cook beer and honey there, and they always put vessels dirty with honey and beer into the well: one should prevent drawing within the well from water, for honey is fruit juice, and some say even in a thousand it is not nullified; see siman 462; and the Tur prefers more to draw from the pipe called a ra'er, to place the vessel there and let it flow there.
