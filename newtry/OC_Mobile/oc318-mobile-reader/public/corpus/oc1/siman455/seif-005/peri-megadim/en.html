Cistern Taz wrote meaning close to drawing; so Levush — apparently when lenient in pressure it is drawn close to bein ha shemashot; for Mechaber seif 1 while still day and did not stand 12 hours, then lenient in pressure — for R' Eliezer of Metz when drawn at bein ha shemashot permitted immediately, unlike drawing beginning of night per R' Eliezer of Metz no remedy forever per second explanation in Beit Yosef — therefore do not rely on this even in cistern in pressure; nevertheless in pressure all permit. Ateret Zekenim 11; Eliyah Rabbah 6: cistern or pipe called ra'er or hole at source — perhaps ra'er water nullified with cistern water — Magen Avraham 15; see Magen Avraham 11. Those wells with ra'er in houses where they brew beer and honey and put always dirty honey-beer vessels in well — prevent drawing from well, for honey is fruit juice; some say not nullified in 1000 — siman 462; Tur prefers drawing from pipe ra'er to put vessel there and drip there.
