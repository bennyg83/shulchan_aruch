(s.k. 3) There is, etc. So that it not fall, etc.: and in Chok Yaakov he wrote that at the time of drawing, where there is no other reason but that something not fall in, it is only a stringency by itself and extra carefulness; rather the Rama's intent is: when one carries them by day under the open sky to the kneading house, then one must cover them so that they not become heated.
