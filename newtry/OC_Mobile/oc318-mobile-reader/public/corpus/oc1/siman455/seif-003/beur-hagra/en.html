Even. There Pesachim 42a:

And not in water. There:

Whether with water. According to Rif's explanation, drawn water means water not left overnight, and the question also refers to water not left overnight.

And there are those who disagree. According to Rashi who explains drawn water means from a kettle, etc., as above — thus the question does not apply at all to water not left overnight:

And some say that b'shogeg, etc. For one transgressed intentionally as written chapter 2 of Yoma 17: they asked — if one transgressed and baked, etc.; and there it deals with intentional as written 99b, etc.; provided he does not deceive, etc.; this case of deception is different, etc.; 99b: one who cooks, etc.; and the first reasoning holds we also find one transgressed b'shogeg, as written chapter 3 of Shabbos: they asked — if one transgressed and stayed, what? And according to Rif's explanation there they asked regarding metsamek ve-yafeh whether it is forbidden b'shogeg — see Tosafot there; and proof from the pastries of Eretz Yisrael; and we are not concerned for such a case; but Rashi and Rosh rejected there and said because it is close to intentional; and all the more so for those who baked in our presence — see R' Eliyahu Mizrachi siman 226.
