Large kettle. The copper of the kettle retains heat (Rashi); it does not deal with water heated on the fire, for then even if completely cooled it is forbidden even not in the kettle — merely putting water in the kettle without fire is forbidden because the kettle retains heat; so Agur in his responsum (Rama siman 35).
