Its copper rim. Rashi explained the rim is thick and there is no difference between copper and earthenware — Magen Avraham. Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote R' Yitzchak the White would not allow kneading in water that was in a copper vessel that heats the water — there was an incident and other rabbis forbade likewise. However, practice is to knead in copper vessels, as in Knesset HaGedolah and Kaf HaChayim s.k. 10. Or Zaru'a wrote an incident came before my elders, may they rest in Eden: one placed mayim matzah in a warm winter room; he asked if the vessel was hot when they took from it; he said no — and permitted.

Lukewarm. That is, like the warmth of saliva; and it is explained in Tur and Beit Yosef that in benefit it is permitted for everyone even if one kneaded with hot water.

B'shogeg. And he has no other dough — Magen Avraham. Taz wrote on the first night one may rely so as not to nullify the positive mitzvah of eating matzah, but not other days — rather he eats other things. (And Kaf HaChayim implies the opposite for matzah of the mitzvah in any case one should not take it — see there.)
