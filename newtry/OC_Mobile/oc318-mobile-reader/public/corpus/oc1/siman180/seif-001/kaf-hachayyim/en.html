1) [Seif 1] One should not remove, etc. A blessing rests upon something only when an object is present, as in the account of Elisha and the Shunammite woman. Zohar, parashas Lech Lecha, 88; parashas Yisro, 86b; parashas Terumah, 157b; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. Levush writes that the reason is so that it will be apparent to everyone that we bless Hashem, may He be exalted, for His great kindness and goodness in preparing food for all His creatures. See another reason below in paragraph 4.
