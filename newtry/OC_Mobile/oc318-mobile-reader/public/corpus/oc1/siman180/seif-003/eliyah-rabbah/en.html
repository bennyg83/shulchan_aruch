[3] [Levush:] And it appears as though he is preparing, etc. Mahari Weil gives an additional reason: blessing is not found in a whole object, because it resembles something measured. Be’er Sheva writes that this is unconvincing, for if so, why do we recite Hamotzi over a whole loaf? In my humble opinion, there is no difficulty. For Hamotzi, one blesses Hashem over it, and a whole loaf is required because of “present it now to your governor,” as stated above in siman 177. When one leaves bread for Bircas HaMazon, however, the purpose is that the blessings and prayers we recite should rest upon it, namely that we should never lack food and that Hashem should sustain us honorably. As stated in Midrash HaNe’elam on Rus, the power of Bircas HaMazon is great, for it adds blessing to the work of one’s hands. The Zohar there therefore properly teaches that blessing does not rest upon something whole.
