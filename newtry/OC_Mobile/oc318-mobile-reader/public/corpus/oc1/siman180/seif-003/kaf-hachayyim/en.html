3) There: One should not remove, etc. Some Kabbalists write that nothing should be removed from the table, even bones and peels, until after Birkas HaMazon, because the sparks of holiness attached to the bones and peels emerge through Birkas HaMazon. Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 1; Ben Ish Chai, parashas Shelach Lecha, paragraph 2. The salt likewise should not be removed from the table until after Birkas HaMazon, so that the table resembles the altar that atones, regarding which it is written, 'Upon every one of your offerings,' etc. Salt also protects from calamity. Kitzur Shelah; Beis Menuchah, paragraph 1. We have received a warning, Heaven forbid, not to remove anything from the table before Birkas HaMazon in order to feed an impure domesticated animal, wild animal, or bird. Chesed LaAlafim there; Ben Ish Chai there. See above, siman 167, paragraph 141.
