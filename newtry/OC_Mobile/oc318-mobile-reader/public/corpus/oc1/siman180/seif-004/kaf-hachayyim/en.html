4) [Seif 2] Whoever does not leave bread, etc. As it is stated, 'There was no remnant of his food; therefore, his prosperity shall not endure.' Sanhedrin 92a. The Zohar there, parashas Yisro, is extremely stringent about this and associates it with the verse, 'You shall not take the Name of Hashem your God in vain,' meaning that one must not raise the Name of Hashem upon his lips to recite Birkas HaMazon over a table that is vain and empty. Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 2.
