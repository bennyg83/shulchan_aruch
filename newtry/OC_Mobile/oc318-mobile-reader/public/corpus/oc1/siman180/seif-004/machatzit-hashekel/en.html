Seif katan 4: That one should not, etc. “You shall not raise iron upon them” applies while building the altar. Since the construction of the Beis HaMikdash does not override Shabbos, as stated at the beginning of Yevamos, there is no allusion to the altar on Shabbos.
And Levush writes that one does not, etc. This is because he follows the second reason cited by Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbi Simchah: once, when a person reached the blessing Boneh Yerushalayim and remembered the destruction of the Beis HaMikdash, he thrust a knife into his abdomen. They therefore adopted the practice of removing the knife during Bircas HaMazon. According to this reason, there is no distinction between Shabbos and weekdays, as Beit Yosef writes.
Levush further writes that even according to the reason that the table alludes to the altar, one may say that the knife should also be removed on Shabbos and Yom Tov, since one should nevertheless allude to the construction of the altar, which occurred on weekdays. See there.
So that it should not see its shame; see siman 473. There this applies specifically when the cup is in his hand. Accordingly, when one recites Bircas HaMazon alone, where according to Rama in siman 182, seif 2, he does not hold the cup but leaves it on the table, perhaps the bread need not be covered.
