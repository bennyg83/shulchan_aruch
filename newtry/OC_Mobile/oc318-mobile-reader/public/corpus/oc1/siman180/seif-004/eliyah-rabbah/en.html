[4] Outside the table, etc. This implies that one whose attendant brings the water to him at the table must be careful. Bach writes likewise. Taz disagrees and says that the concern applied only in the time of the Gemara, when they removed the table, because shaking the table might cause crumbs to fall. He was not precise, for at the beginning of the chapter Eilu Devarim it states that when they recline at a table, they sweep the table of food remnants scattered upon it. Lechem Chamudos likewise writes that at large meals, where they wash at the table, even for the first washing one must be careful to remove the crumbs. See there. Presumably they wash into a vessel, yet we are concerned for splashes.
