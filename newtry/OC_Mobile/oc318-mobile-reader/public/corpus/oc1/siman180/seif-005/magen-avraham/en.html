Not to cover it. The reason is that the table resembles the altar, and concerning the altar it is written, “You shall not raise iron upon them.” Since the altar is not built on Shabbos, there is no allusion to the altar then. According to this, only an iron knife must be covered. Shulchan Arba. Levush writes that in our countries the custom is not to distinguish between Shabbos and weekdays. Shulchan Arba also writes that the bread should be covered during Bircas HaMazon so that it should not witness its own shame. See siman 473.
