The knife. Iron shortens a person’s life, and it is therefore improper for it to lie upon the table, which resembles the altar and lengthens a person’s life. A second reason is that once, when a person reached the blessing Boneh Yerushalayim and remembered the destruction of the Beis HaMikdash, he thrust a knife into his abdomen. They therefore adopted the practice of removing it during the blessing. Beit Yosef. Taz writes that the incident occurred on a weekday, and therefore the decree applied on weekdays as well. According to the first reason, one might say that because the altar is not built on Shabbos, there is no allusion to the altar then. In our countries, however, the practice is not to distinguish between Shabbos and weekdays. See Responsa Be’er Eisek, siman 77. Shulchan Arba writes that the bread should be covered during Bircas HaMazon so that it should not witness its own shame. See siman 473.
