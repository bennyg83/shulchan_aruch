5) Meticulous people take care that no empty utensil remain upon the table during Birkas HaMazon. Tiferes Shmuel on Rosh, end of the third chapter of Berachos; Birkei Yosef, siman 183, paragraph 1; Zechor LeAvraham, under kaf; Chesed LaAlafim there; Ben Ish Chai there, paragraph 4. One should likewise take care that no spoiled or repulsive food be present, because it is written, 'This is the table that is before Hashem.'
