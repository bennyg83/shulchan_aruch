(11) To cover knife—in Beit Yosef two reasons: first iron shortens man's days, improper on table similar to altar extending days—for so written do not raise iron on them; therefore after finished eating wants birkat hamazon covers knife. Second reason in name Rabbenu Simcha: once reached blessing Builder of Jerusalem, remembered Temple destruction, stabbed knife in belly—therefore custom remove at blessing time.

(12) Custom not to cover, etc.—reason not particular about this on Shabbat and Yom Tov—see Acharonim.
