NB: It is written in Avot deRabbi Natan in the name of Tzalach that likewise in Adar, and it appears to me all the more in Iyar; and see what Beit Yosef wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 201 regarding cheese in the days of Tishrei — since he did not say "in the month of Tishrei" but "in the days of Tishrei," it implies likewise Elul — see there; and likewise here; and from my teacher z"l I received all this: that the days of the month of Nissan when the tekufah extends into the month of Iyar — for example, if tekufat Nissan falls on 20 Nissan, then there is time to bless until 20 Iyar.
