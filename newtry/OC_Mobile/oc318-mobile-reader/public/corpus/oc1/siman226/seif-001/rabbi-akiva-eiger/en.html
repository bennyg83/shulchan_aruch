And sees trees — if the trees are within three years of planting, I am in doubt whether he blesses, since it is impossible to enjoy their fruit; and likewise in the law of siman 225 seif 3: one who sees new fruit — if the fruit is under the prohibition of orlah, whether he blesses; and where there is doubt, it appears to me he blesses and it is not called a vain blessing, since the law of doubt regarding orlah outside the Land is permitted — if so, it is fitting to enjoy from it and he blesses properly.
