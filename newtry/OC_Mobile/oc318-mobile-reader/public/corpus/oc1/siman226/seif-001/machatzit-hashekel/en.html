In Shulchan Aruch "one who goes out in the days of Nissan" — orcha deMilta he cited, for then in warm lands it is customary for trees to bloom; and the same in another month — whenever one sees blooming the first time he blesses. Further there: and if he delayed to bless, etc. — but before the fruits grew he may bless, even if he did not bless at the time of first sight. And this is per the view of Rama in siman 225 seif 3. But per those who disagree in siman 225, and as Magen Avraham wrote there s.k. 9 — likewise here one does not bless unless he blessed at first sight; see Tur, Bach, and Perishah.
