One who sees blossoms on the tree — what does he bless? It
contains 1 seif: One who goes out in the days of Nissan and sees trees that put forth blossom says: Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who has left nothing lacking in His world and created in it good creatures and good trees for people to enjoy. And he blesses only once each year; and if he delayed to bless until after the fruits grew he does not bless again.
