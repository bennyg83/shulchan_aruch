(א) That put forth blossom — meaning malbivin as they said there, as written "and behold the rod of Aaron bloomed and blossomed," and Onkelos renders "and brought forth blossoms," etc.

(ב) And if he delayed, etc. — so Tur; but Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote that if he did not see until after the fruits grew he blesses afterward — and so is the main ruling.
