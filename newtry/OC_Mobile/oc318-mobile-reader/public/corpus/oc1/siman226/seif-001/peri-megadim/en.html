Only once — although in all those above one blesses from language to language, here one does not act thus, Bach — for from the way of nature one knows that after they began to grow they continue growing, and there is no joy except at first sight. See siman 227 in Taz letter 2 in what he wrote that here, if he delayed, nevertheless on all that have not grown one blesses — one may say the joy still exists, and there it will be explained if so in this.
