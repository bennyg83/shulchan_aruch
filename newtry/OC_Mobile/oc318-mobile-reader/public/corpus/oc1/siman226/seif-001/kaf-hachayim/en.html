(א) [Seif 1] One who goes out in the days of Nissan, etc. — it appears from the words of Tur and Shulchan Aruch that they specified "in the days of Nissan" because specifically in the days of Nissan it is fitting to bless this blessing, and not before and not after; and so from the language of the Gemara that says "this man who goes out in the days of Nissan and sees trees that are blooming"; and so Sefer Leket part 2 siman 228 — from that they said "one who goes out in the days of Nissan" it implies that on almond blossoms that hurry greatly before Nissan one does not bless — see there. However, some say that "days of Nissan" is not precise and the same applies in another month — Avnei Ruven ot 1 in the name of Sefer Tzalach, and so Machatzit HaShekel and other Acharonim. But Birkei Yosef ot 2 wrote that this blessing in truth applies specifically to the days of Nissan — end of his words. And so in his book Moreh BaEtzba ot 198: the meticulous insist it be specifically in Nissan, and that there be two trees, and they go out to the field to bless — end of his words. And so in Sefer Moed LeChol Chai siman 1 ot 9; and so Petaḥ HaDvir concludes ot 2 and rejects one who wrote in the name of Likutei HaAri that one may bless also after Nissan — see there. Therefore, since there is a dispute in this and it is clear from the Gemara's language that specifically in Nissan, and also it is a matter of doubt where one may be lenient — one should not bless except specifically in Nissan; and so is the practice. However, one who delayed and did not bless in Nissan — it appears he may bless afterward without Heaven's name and kingship.
