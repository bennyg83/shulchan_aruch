[2] [Levush] To recite the blessing, etc. Those women who change the challah blessing on Pesach are mistaken; rather they recite the blessing as is their custom the whole year (Taz and Shach, Yoreh De'ah, siman 328). Mateh Moshe wrote that it is proper to make from the challah a small cake the whole year, and not only on Pesach. He wrote in the Shlah that one should protest women who fold the dough and take from both ends; rather she should take from one end, and another should arrange with the rolling-pin at the other end so that it not be left idle.
