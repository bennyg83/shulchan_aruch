One who kneads dough on Pesach Yom Tov should not pronounce the name upon it for challah until it is baked. For if he pronounces the name upon it, he is not permitted to bake it; and if he leaves it so it will become chametz, and he is not permitted to burn it on Yom Tov. If he forgot and pronounced the name upon it, he places it to cool and prevents it from becoming chametz.And if there is a minor kohen who has not seen keri, or an adult who immersed for his keri, it is permitted to bake the challah for him (Tur). And some say one does not feed challah at this time to any kohen (Mahariv). Some say it is permitted on Yom Tov to knead less than the measure in order to exempt oneself from challah (Ran beginning chapter Elu Overin).):
