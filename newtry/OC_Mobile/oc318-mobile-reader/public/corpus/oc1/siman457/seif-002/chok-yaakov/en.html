And in truth this is a great error; for indeed from the language of Riv brought in Beis Yosef he understood at first glance thus, but the truth is not so, and the sugya of the Talmud at the beginning of chapter Benos Kusim escaped his notice, and Rambam explicitly in chapter 5 of the laws of Avos HaTumah: one less than nine years old, even if he lay beside a woman and saw a seminal emission, does not convey tumah, and that is from a decree of Scripture. And that which Riv wrote that tumah does not issue from his body: this is what he means: even if a seminal emission issues from him he is not tamei.

For it is permitted on Yom Tov to knead less than the measure. And Taz wrote: and afterward he should not knead at all; and if he kneads another dough less than the measure, there is concern lest they afterward combine in one vessel and come to obligation and eat tevel, end quote. And this concern is remote regarding challah of chutz la'aretz which is rabbinic; for certainly if they combine he will also separate its challah, as I wrote above in s.k. 2: we are not concerned for this except where he once separated from a dough that was less than the measure, as the Rosh wrote, which I brought above in s.k. 2.
