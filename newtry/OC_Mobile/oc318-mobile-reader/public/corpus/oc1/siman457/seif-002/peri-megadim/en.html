Lest perhaps — Taz's general intent: kneading two doughs, and in each the measure — he must surround one to the other somewhat, adjacent, that he separate from one for its fellow; not so when there is not in each the measure for challah, and through vessel-combination they became obligated — he need not from the surrounding batch; therefore they did not mention surrounding here. And Tosafot in Elu Overin 46b s.v. heveil: in a basket touching is required — when there is in each the measure for challah that requires surrounding, then basket and touching are required; not like Rosh that surrounding alone suffices; or Tosafot also hold basket is not required — only touching, for basket does not help and touching is also required; and this in challah of chutz la'aretz that is d'rabbanan — then there is a difference between when there is in each the measure for challah, surrounding is required to separate from one for all, and when there is not in each the measure for challah, only through combination — then surrounding is not required; therefore challah of chutz la'aretz: eat and go and afterward separate, since it was from one dough; and Kaf HaChayyim letter 2; and from challah of chutz la'aretz of eat and afterward separate we are not concerned he forgot, for he is mindful. Not so if he separates from each dough alone, lest among them there is one not obligated in challah and the separation is void, and afterward in the basket they become obligated and he will not separate and eat tevel; see Ra'avad's explanation chapter 2 of Challah mishnah 4: surrounding does not require touching; and nevertheless impure and pure require touching — and implied even when each has the measure; and implied when there is not in each the measure, actual basket and touching are required to combine the measure for challah; and on Yom Tov there — understand; see R' Shimshon there; and see in letter 5 it will be explained further:
