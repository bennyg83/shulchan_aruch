1) [Se'if 1] Because one must be precise, etc. In most places, the custom is to appoint a supervisor over matzah production, to observe the work and urge the workers to act promptly. It is obvious that one should appoint an intelligent, learned person who fears Heaven. A group composed of women and minors should not be entrusted with such matters. Sefer HaZichronos, page 24b; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur. See below, siman 460, subsection 11.
2) There. Not to make too much dough, etc. The early authorities explain that even if one has many assistants, two ovens, and similar advantages, one should nevertheless not increase the quantity of dough. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1. All this was already explained at length in the preceding siman.
