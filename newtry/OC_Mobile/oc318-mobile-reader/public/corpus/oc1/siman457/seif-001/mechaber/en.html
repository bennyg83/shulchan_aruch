דין הנהגת חלה בעיסת מצה. ובו ב סעיפים:
The law of conduct regarding challah from matzah dough. It contains 2 seifim. Because one must be careful with the measure of the dough not to make it too large, from concern for chimutz, and it is better to reduce it somewhat; therefore it is good to bring the doughs together at the time of separating challah so they touch one another, lest one of them did not have the requisite measure. If it is impossible to separate challah while still dough because of haste, he separates it immediately after baking, when he places all the matzot in a basket — and the basket combines them for challah; and this is the most correct way.And if there was the measure for challah and he forgot to separate, he separates afterward; and even basket-combination is not required. If he took from part of the doughs and from part he did not take and they became mixed, he must take challah from each one individually, or knead another dough and take from it also for those that became mixed. If he recognizes one matzah that is obligated in challah, he takes from it for the others (Hagahot Maimoniot chapter 3, and Mordechai beginning of chapter Elu Overin, and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot first chapter of Beitzah).):
