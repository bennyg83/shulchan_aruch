Battle. The curse should be discarded from the compression of your cribs. B:

(3) In a basket. A vessel without receptacle — doubt if combines; covering with cloth counts as vessel and combines — so practice (Taz). Shlah: protest folding dough taking challah from two ends — take one end, one rolls at other end exposed. Custom separate from each dough (Darkei Moshe). Must be per custom of large doughs — Magen Avraham: one blessing suffices all if no unrelated speech between kneading. Now majority separate each dough, each woman blesses. Blessing: no difference Pesach and year (Darkei Moshe; Taz Yoreh De'ah 328). Derishah: reason saying on Pesach "terumat challah matzah from dough" — reason making challah first like matzah then throw to fire; Maharshal house burned without repair — Mateh Moshe; if challah fell in boiling food — besides mixture laws Yoreh De'ah 324, discuss chametz siman 454 seif 3. Kaf HaChayim in father-in-law's name: benefit or keep until after Pesach — see there.

(ד) Not necessary. But both must be placed before him — for although we hold challah of chutz la'aretz: eat and go and afterward separate, nevertheless l'chatchila he requires that it be from the surrounding batch (Magen Avraham). And Chayei Adam wrote he need not be from the surrounding batch in challah of chutz la'aretz: eat and go and afterward separate — see Acharonim. He wrote in Mishnah Berurah and customs and Levush: if he forgot to separate from the doughs of matzah of the mitzvah he may wait until night so he places them whole on the plate; and when he breaks the second matzah for the two to keep half for afikoman, then he places the two whole matzot and the two broken pieces in a vessel to combine them, covers them, takes a small piece from the broken portion, blesses to separate challah, keeps it for chol ha'moed {it is permitted to benefit while burning it (Maharil); nevertheless it is proper to make a separate bonfire (Mishnah Berurah)} and they burn on chol ha'moed; and it appears to me it is better that he break from it first a small piece immediately by day lest he return and forget — for in any case he will break it without a blessing — end quote. And Kaf HaChayyim wrote so too it is fit to practice not to wait until night — for in any case dough kneaded from erev Yom Tov is forbidden to separate from it on Yom Tov, as written siman 506 — see there {and if there was not in it the measure, so on erev Yom Tov he was not obligated in challah at all, but on Yom Tov through combination they became obligated — certainly he separates from it on Yom Tov}:
