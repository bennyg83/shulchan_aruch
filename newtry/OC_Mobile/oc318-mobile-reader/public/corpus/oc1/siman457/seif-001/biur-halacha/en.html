(א) And it is better to reduce it — see Chok Yaakov; and I copied above in siman 456 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 7 that if he has only one dough it is good not to diminish so as not to lose challah, and he is the number of terumah — see there in Sha'arei Tzedek:

(ב) That he place all the matzot in a basket — and a tray that has no waste, such as a table, is in doubt; therefore when combining them in a vessel he should be careful that nothing go above the sides of the vessel, as stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 325 seif 1 in the gloss. And b'dieved if he did not cover them and took from the upper ones — challah on the whole basket: Peri Megadim is uncertain whether it helps; and in my humble opinion it appears one need not be stringent b'dieved, since in any case the view of Semag is to be lenient that covering is not required, for the air of the vessel combines them since the vessel has an interior even what protrudes above is considered as placed below — and his view was brought in Biur HaGra there — see there:

(ג) And the basket combines them for challah — the same applies through covering with a cloth; that is, he places them on a cloth and places this cloth also over the matzot from above; and even though in the middle some are exposed, all combine since from the sides they are covered — end quote Peri Megadim; and this flows from Shach Yoreh De'ah in the name of Maharil; and Peri Megadim concludes: therefore if placed on a tray and above with a cloth — no. And in my humble opinion one should study the fundamental law, for in Semak it is implied that plain covering also helps — see there; and so in Machatzit HaShekel it is implied that through plain covering it helps — requires study for practice. Indeed in a vessel that has an interior, only that the matzot protrude above — in this it certainly appears one may be lenient through plain covering, after Semag is completely lenient, as above. And know: if we say plain cloth covering helps to combine, the same if he inverted a vessel over the matzot it also helps to combine; indeed per this, what R' Eliezer said — even one who winnows and puts into a basket etc. — is not precise, for the same if he inverted the basket over them:
