Good to bring the doughs together. And some place the dough folded at the time of separating challah — and this is not correct (Shlah). The custom is to separate from each dough and each dough alone (Darkei Moshe). And nevertheless one blessing suffices for all if he did not interrupt between them with speech unrelated to kneading (Yoreh De'ah siman 19; and see what is written siman 330). Regarding the blessing there is no difference between Pesach and other days of the year (Darkei Moshe section 38). And in Derishah Yoreh De'ah siman 328 he wrote the reason for those who practice saying on Pesach "terumat challah matzah from the dough" — see there; and he also gave a reason why they practice making it first like matzah and afterward throwing it into the fire; and he concludes that in the house of Maharshal they practiced burning it without repair — and so Matanot Kehunah:
