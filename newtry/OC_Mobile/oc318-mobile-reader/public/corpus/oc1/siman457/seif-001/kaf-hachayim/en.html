(1) [Seif 1] Because one must be careful, etc. The practice in most places is to appoint a supervisor over making the matzot to stand over their making and to urge those engaged in them; and it is simply that this requires a wise, understanding, Heaven-fearing man; and for such matters one does not make a group of women and minors.
