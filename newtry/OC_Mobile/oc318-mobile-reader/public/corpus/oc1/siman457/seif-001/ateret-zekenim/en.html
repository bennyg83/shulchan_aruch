Therefore it is good to bring the doughs together so they touch one another, as in Yoreh De'ah siman 325; and if each dough has the measure for challah and he wishes to separate from one for the other, he need not combination nor touching — only that both are before him and they separate from one for the other; also in Yoreh De'ah siman 325. And if he wishes to separate challah when they are in a basket or vessel, he should be careful that nothing protrude beyond the sides of the vessel outward, or cover with a cloth — then it is considered like a vessel to combine them. And if he forgot to separate challah from the dough of the matzot, he may wait until night so they will be whole to place on the seder plate; and when he breaks the second matzah for the two [participants] to keep half for afikoman, then he places the two whole matzot and those two broken pieces in a vessel to combine them, covers them, takes a small piece from the broken portion, blesses to separate challah, keeps it until chol ha'moed, and burns it on chol ha'moed (Tur). But it is better to break a small piece for challah immediately and not wait until night, lest he forget also at night — for in any case he will break without a blessing at night (Lechem Rav):
