In Magen Avraham s.k. 5 he reconciles it and it is non-sacred. Note: one who makes an agent to separate — who reconciles? See responsa Chatam Sofer Yoreh De'ah siman 320 {and he wrote the matter is simple that both may ask the agent and the homeowner, except if the agent asks about his vow and regrets from the outset about what he gave his mind to separate — from this side the vow is uprooted from its root and he returns to tithe it; then he returns and separates another terumah, for he still did not perform his agency at all, for the first separation is like the act of a monkey in the world; not so when the homeowner regrets — he must regret the essence of what he gave his mind based on the mind of this agent, and if so automatically the first agency is void and he must appoint an agent anew, etc.; and from being good they should annul vows for both of them, the agent and the homeowner}:
