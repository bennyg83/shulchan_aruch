[1] That they kiss, etc. Meaning that if they separate, one tears a little from its fellow; and if so, any hard dough, even when placed one upon the other, does not adhere together, and there is no combination here; therefore one should act through vessel-combination, or cover with a cloth (Taz). And Chok Yaakov wrote in his father's name that they practice to cut one piece from two doughs together, for through the cutting they adhere to one another. And see the responsa of Maharam of Rothenburg, siman 322, that some practice to take a little from each dough and make one cake; that is not a proper custom.
