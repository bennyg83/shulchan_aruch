And in Yoreh De'ah there, specifically min b'mino — see there in siman 325 in Taz and Shach 1, and in siman 324 in Taz 2. And it appears the same for vessel-combination — specifically one species; two species — no; and all the more so from dough, for per the first tanna second chapter of Menachot the basket does not combine; however one may say in dough he is particular, not so in basket-combination after baking, and touching does not matter — it is implied even in two species it combines; and see section 4 Magen Avraham — it is implied basket-combination too we require min b'mino; and it is forced to say per the view that we require biting — see section 2 Menachot — see there; requires study. And Ra'avad there explained min b'mino — basket-combination; and again in several places in chapter 6 of Laws of Bechoros section 17 in the name of Rashba z"l that in a basket too min b'mino specifically; and it appears from what Ra'avad wrote there impure and pure — see there. A vessel that has no waste is in doubt — end quote Menachot section 17; and challah of chutz la'aretz that is d'rabbanan — doubt for leniency; and the same now in challah of Eretz Yisrael — end quote there; and in Yoreh De'ah 322 in Shach 4. And if he placed matzot in a basket and the upper ones are not within the partitions of the basket — there is doubt whether it is like siman 325 seif 1 in the gloss and Shach 4 and Taz 2; and if he took from the upper ones — challah on the whole basket: seemingly doubt d'rabbanan for leniency, for perhaps a tray that has no waste combines all upper and lower together, and he took obligation upon obligation; and one wise man told me there is no doubt removing from certainty, for the lower ones were already obligated through basket-combination, and doubt whether separated from obligation upon them. And behold in Shulchan Aruch he wrote: some say in d'rabbanan doubt removes from certainty — see Mikvaot: an impure person descended to immerse — see Kaf HaChayyim.
