But Taz siman 101 note 2, and this is in Beit Yosef citing Mordechai, and there is another reason because they are beloved and one gives his attention; and Beit Yosef and Eliyah Rabbah wrote to prepare must be fluent (how preparation helps; and for Rosh Chodesh there is a doubt perhaps the halachah is like Tur that need not prepare, unlike festivals. And Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan is like festivals — see Bach. And know Yaaleh VeYavo also needs preparation or from the siddur, and similarly Chanukah and Purim on days etc. And Pri Chadash wrote they practice first prayer from the siddur and afterward enough — see there. And know Rambam chapter 4 of Laws of Prayer law 19 Musaf Rosh Chodesh implies yes Yaaleh VeYavo and on weekdays they were not concerned for complete blessing perhaps he erred in it (not because it should be fluent etc.) although the mishnah slightly diverges, nevertheless lekhatchilah not so unlike Yaaleh VeYavo and the like; but from Bach and Pri Chadash it is clear similarly Chanukah and Purim — see there. In some editions of Shulchan Aruch (alternative reading) R"ה means Rosh Hashanah, and so in Pri Chadash edition, and proven there the girsa he pointed: and Rosh Chodesh prayer too — thus in Shulchan Aruch R"ה is Rosh Hashanah, and this is like Rif and Rosh — see Beit Yosef (and Tur who cites Rosh's language — see Bach). And this is possible the view of Olat Tamid and Eliyah Rabbah; however in many places in Beit Yosef his view seems they agree to Rambam, and so in Levush Rosh Chodesh and in Netsiv — see there on this. Nevertheless Taz also wrote the meaning of difficult words must be prepared.
