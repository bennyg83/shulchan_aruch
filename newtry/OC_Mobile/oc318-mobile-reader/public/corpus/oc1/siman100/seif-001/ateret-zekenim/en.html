Prayers of festivals one must prepare his prayer; and some say prayers of Rosh Chodesh do not need to be prepared (Rosh and Tur).

But when they pray from the siddur it is permitted; and one should be strict even when praying from the siddur, even on Rosh Chodesh (Beit Yosef); and all the more so one must prepare the prayer of Chanukah and Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur and of Tisha BeAv and of festivals, even if praying from the siddur (my teacher in Bach).
