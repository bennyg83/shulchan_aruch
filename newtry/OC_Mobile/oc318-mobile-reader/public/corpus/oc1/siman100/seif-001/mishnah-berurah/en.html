(1) And of Rosh Chodesh — thus is the version in many books and in Biur HaGra, and it means Musaf prayer; and the reason is because one does not pray them frequently but only periodically, one is not so accustomed to them. And in Peri Megadim in the name of Bach and Pri Chadash wrote similarly Yaaleh VeYavo of Shacharit or on days etc. of Chanukah and Purim also need to prepare beforehand or pray from the siddur. And in Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah wondered at the custom of people who are lenient in all this to pray from memory and do not prepare beforehand — one does not know on what they relied; and see in Shaarei Teshuvah. And lekhatchilah certainly it is correct to be careful to pray on Chanukah and Purim and festivals and Rosh Chodesh at least the first prayer from the siddur, and enough.

(2) When they pray from memory — Machatzit HaShekel wrote not proper is the world's practice that they say Kiddush Levanah blessing from memory and do not prepare it beforehand; and see in Shaarei Teshuvah.

(3) From the siddur — and prayers and piyyutim whose meaning is weighty need to be prepared first, and a book does not help for this [Taz].
