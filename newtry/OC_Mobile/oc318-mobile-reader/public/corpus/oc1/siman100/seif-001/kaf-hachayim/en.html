(1) [Section 1] Prayers of festivals and of R"ה must be prepared, etc. Rambam wrote, chapter 4 of Laws of Prayer, law 19, and these are his words: chapter prayers such as Musaf of Rosh Chodesh and festival prayer one must prepare his prayer and then stand and pray so that he not err in it; until here. But Tur wrote in the name of his father Rosh that Rosh Chodesh prayer need not be prepared, and our master in Beit Yosef rejected his words and wrote that even Rosh's words may be explained like Rambam's — see there. And Bach compelled that Rif and Rosh hold like Rambam that Rosh Chodesh prayer must be prepared, and wrote "thus we hold" — see there. And if so, what is written in Shulchan Aruch "and of R"ה" must be a typo and should read "and of Rosh Chodesh," for our master rejected the Tur's words and holds like Rambam, and therefore wrote in Shulchan Aruch like Rambam "of festivals and Rosh Chodesh"; and furthermore, why write R"ה — is not R"ה included in festivals? And it should also say Yom Kippur — rather it is compelled as we said. And so is the version in old Shulchan Aruch printed in the year 5387 in our master's lifetime "and Rosh Chodesh"; and so in Shulchan Aruch printed with Magen Avraham, and likewise Levush, and see Mekor Chaim. And so from the words of Eliyah Rabbah — see there. And afterward I saw Maamar Mordechai siman 1 and Nishmat Adam siman 1, Yefe Lalev siman 1, and so versions R' Zalman siman 1; and with this the difficulty of Birkei Yosef departs, and what was forced to answer in siman 1 and 3; and Olat Tamid siman 1 wrote similarly if it happened he did not pray for 29 days and prays on day 30 he must prepare his prayer beforehand — see there.
