Seif 1: Concerning the festivals, etc. Rashi explains the expression there, “at intervals,” in this manner. The Rif and the Rosh, however, write that it refers to a prayer recited once in thirty days. The Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch write that it applies to the festivals and Rosh Chodesh, because they maintain that the Gemara’s statement there, “once in thirty days,” as also quoted by the Rif and the Rosh, is not exact, but means close to thirty days.
