The Beit Yosef himself writes that even when the prayer is written before him, he should first review it. Nevertheless, the Rama ruled correctly that praying from a siddur is effective. We find that a siddur is effective even in a more serious matter, namely that one may pray aloud on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, as stated in the following siman. According to the Gemara, praying aloud is strictly prohibited because one may cause another person to err, yet we rule there that this concern does not apply when he has a siddur in his hand. All the more so does a siddur suffice here. Nevertheless, regarding a prayer whose meaning must be understood, particularly piyyutim whose meaning is difficult unless one gives them considerable attention, he certainly must review them beforehand, and having a book does not suffice for this. This is clear.
