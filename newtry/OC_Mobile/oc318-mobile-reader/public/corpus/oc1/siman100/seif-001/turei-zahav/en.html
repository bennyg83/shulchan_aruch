And Beit Yosef himself wrote that even when it is written before him he should prepare first, etc. Nevertheless Rama ruled well that prayer from the siddur helps, for we find something better than this: we find what helps prayer regarding one who prays on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur aloud, as written in the next siman; and this is from Talmudic law — a complete prohibition that one may mislead his fellow — and nevertheless we rule there that one need not be concerned for this since he has a siddur in his hand; how much more so here. Nevertheless it seems that for a prayer whose meaning one must understand, especially in piyyutim whose meaning is weighty, if not with great attention to them, certainly one must prepare first, and a book does not help him for this; and this is simple.
