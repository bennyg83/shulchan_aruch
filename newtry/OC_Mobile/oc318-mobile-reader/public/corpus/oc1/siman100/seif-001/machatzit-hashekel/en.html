In Rama's gloss: but when they pray from the siddur it is permitted. And Taz wrote nevertheless in piyyutim and weighty prayers to understand the intent certainly requires preparation first; and some say that which he prays from a book — not proper is the world's practice that they say Kiddush Levanah blessings from memory and also do not prepare beforehand; and in my humble opinion more preparation is required for Rosh Chodesh prayer, for at least thirty days should not pass without praying Rosh Chodesh prayer (and see in Rabbeinu Yonah); unlike Kiddush Levanah blessings, for sometimes between one and another there is more than thirty days according to when the moon is seen.
