The recitation of "And on your Rosh Chodesh days" in Pesukei Dezimra. Contains 1 seif. The custom
in Ashkenaz is to recite the passage of "And on your Rosh Chodesh days" in the morning service after the passage of the daily offering. In Sepharad the custom is not to recite it, since they will read it later from the Torah scroll.
