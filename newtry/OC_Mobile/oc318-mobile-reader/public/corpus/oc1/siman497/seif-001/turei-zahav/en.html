(א) One does not trap etc. I will set forth briefly the Gemara needed for this and the view of the authorities who said in the mishnah Eruvin one does not trap fish from ponds on Yom Tov (Rashi explained because it was possible to trap from yesterday and leave them in a trap in the water and tomorrow retrieve it; and Tosafos gave the reason that trapping is like harvesting, but there is no distinction in ochel nefesh if it was possible from yesterday) — but one traps animals and birds from ponds (Rashi explained that a fish pond is pools of water and an animal pond is enclosures surrounded by a fence and they gather wild animals into them and they bear and raise there) — Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says not all ponds are equal — this is the principle: whatever requires trapping (that one must trap it) is forbidden, and whatever does not require trapping is permitted; and the halacha is like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel; and we establish that which is permitted for animals and birds — namely in small ponds specifically; and we explain in the Gemara that "requires trapping" which Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel mentioned means whatever we say "bring the trap and we will trap" (meaning one must seek stratagems to catch it); and it is stated afterward in the Gemara: Rav said a water channel that he blocked (so fish from outside do not enter from evening, and these he prepared and designated through this act) — they are permitted. And behold Rambam holds Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel refers also to fish; therefore he ruled in ch. 2 of Yom Tov: fish in large ponds and likewise animals and birds in large ponds — whatever requires trapping etc. — this is muktzeh and one does not trap them on Yom Tov; and Ra'avad wrote on this: this is incorrect, for animals and birds that they permitted is only in small ponds, and nevertheless fish are not; and the reason is because they are covered in water from the eye — end of his words; and the fifth Maggid explained Rambam holds Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel refers also to fish and there too it depends on requiring trapping; and Ra'avad and Rashba hold Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel refers only to animals and birds; and Rambam relied on that which we permit in blocking the water channel as written in this siman — and what is the difference between this and a small pond: if in a small pond that does not require trapping its fish are forbidden, why are these permitted? Rather certainly whatever does not require trapping is permitted; and a water channel even if very long, since it is not very wide, the fish within are considered trapped; and the same law applies in small ponds — end of his words; and if so certainly it is difficult on Ra'avad and Rashba from blocking the water channel as the fifth Maggid wrote — support from this for Rambam; and the words of Beit Yosef: and so in ponds it is different for they are very wide, and even though no trap is needed to trap the fish, nevertheless because they are covered from the eye they slip here and there and therefore are not considered trapped; but a water channel, since it is not wide it cannot slip — therefore it is considered trapped — end of his words; and I cannot reconcile at all Beit Yosef's words here, for since according to Ra'avad animals and birds are permitted when not requiring trapping — automatically fish are forbidden even when not requiring trapping, even though they are considered trapped — nevertheless forbidden because they are covered from the eye; if so what helps blocking the water even though it is narrow and does not require trapping — nevertheless it would be forbidden because of covering the water, and it would not help that they are trapped and standing; and there is no place to distinguish between them; and it appears to me to answer the fifth Maggid's question on Ra'avad and Rashba thus: when trapping is required it is forbidden because of muktzeh as written nearby in the name of Rambam; and even though in ch. 1 of Yom Tov Rambam wrote that on Yom Tov is stricter than Shabbat, for muktzeh on Shabbat is permitted and on Yom Tov forbidden, and Ra'avad disagrees and equates Yom Tov to Shabbat — nevertheless it is obvious that in such muktzeh even on Shabbat it is forbidden and even R' Shimon agrees; and this is the reason Ra'avad wrote "because they are covered from the eye" — it is difficult at first glance: is covering from the eye what permits the prohibition of trapping? Behold there is already no trapping since no trap is needed; rather certainly he speaks because of muktzeh; except that for Rambam muktzeh is specifically because of lacking trapping, and for Ra'avad muktzeh is specifically because of covering from the eye; and if so according to both, blocking the water channel is permitted, for the essence of muktzeh reason is since from yesterday he designated his mind away from it and did not prepare it; and this that he blocked from yesterday the water channel — certainly there is no muktzeh here. And this is explained from Rashi's words we mentioned who wrote "and these he prepared" and from outside they will not enter — behold literally as stated; and this is also proven from Rav's words who said a water channel that he blocked on erev Yom Tov, and did not say briefly "a water channel that is blocked is permitted," rather it is clear a person must do an act on erev Yom Tov or before it and remove from muktzeh; and this is very correct in my humble opinion; and Tur's words imply he holds like Ra'avad, for he wrote regarding fish plainly to forbid, except that plain water permits; and for animals and birds he distinguishes between requiring trapping or not — rather certainly forbidden regarding fish even when not requiring trapping because of muktzeh like Ra'avad we mentioned; and so Rashal in chapter Eruvin ruled like Ra'avad and Rashba; and in my humble opinion there is full permission regarding fish that one prepares from erev Yom Tov and puts them into a large perforated box in water, since all do not require trapping and there is no muktzeh among them — it is literally like blocking the water channel we mentioned; and Rashal there in chapter Eruvin also wrote to permit in this box even if a small trap is needed to reach there — there is no trapping on this; except he wrote on this that it appeared to him to distinguish specifically if he wants to take all and cook them it is permitted, but if he wants to take one or two it is forbidden unless he ties from beforehand with a string, for it is no better than dovecote pigeons and upper pigeons that even if they do not fly are forbidden, and the reason lest they be weak and he leave them and move in vain as stated in this siman; and afterward he rejected this and said that there regarding pigeons there is concern lest he move muktzeh, but here where he took them by hand and put them in the box on erev Yom Tov there is no muktzeh at all, for all are fit for him even without designation — thus; and I wondered why he needed to elaborate — it is obvious that it is permitted to take whatever he wishes, like blocking the water channel we mentioned; and further why was it not difficult to him on the mishnah that one traps animals and birds from ponds and we are not concerned lest they not please him and he move in vain — rather certainly only regarding pigeons is there muktzeh prohibition and the essence of permission is through designation — therefore he must designate properly as written in this siman, which is not so with these that do not require designation and there is no muktzeh since they are in small ponds; and similarly regarding fish if he blocks the water channel from yesterday it is as though he takes each fish individually on erev Yom Tov and leaves it there, and tying a string is not needed in my humble opinion; and see in this siman from this: again I saw my honored teacher may he rest in Eden also followed Beit Yosef's distinction according to Ra'avad and Rashba regarding the water channel, and concluded on this that since the container is somewhat wide the fish are forbidden because of trapping according to Ra'avad and Rashba; and so it appears from Rashi's words who found permission in a pond for fish only to trap them from beforehand and leave them in his trap; and I would say regarding the water channel that it is not wide, and whatever is wider than six handbreadths in the measure of the water channel the fish are forbidden — thus. And it appears to me they did not insist the water channel be short, rather since it is long — if it were wide the fish could slip along the length which is very great, not so in a box that is square — even if it slips somewhat from its place it has no place to escape; and the principle they gave here: whatever we do not say "bring the trap" — Rashi explained one will need stratagems to trap it, meaning he spreads food there or spreads it across from end to end; not so when reaching with a trap to the box to take it — even reaching several times until he removes it from there — there is no concern in this, in my humble opinion.
