One may not trap, etc. And regarding whether the prohibition of trapping on Yom Tov is according to the Torah or rabbinic, see above in siman 495, se'if 2, and in the Mishnah Berurah there. And see Tosafos, Shabbos 106, and the novellae of the Rashba at the beginning of chapter "Ein Tzadin." And in Shabbos 106 regarding "one does not place food before them," it is implied there that the prohibition of trapping fish even from a small fishpond is according to the Torah, because they slip away into holes and crevices; and therefore they decreed not to place food before them, lest one come to trap them; see there.
