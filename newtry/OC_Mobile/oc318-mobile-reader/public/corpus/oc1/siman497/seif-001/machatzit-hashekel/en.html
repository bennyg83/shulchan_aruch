(א) (seif katan 1) And the fish etc. — because they are covered etc. We learned in the first chapter of Eruvin: one does not trap fish from ponds on Yom Tov, but one traps animals and birds from ponds; Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says: not all ponds are equal — this is the principle: whatever requires trapping is forbidden, and whatever does not require trapping is permitted; and the halacha was decided like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. And the Gemara raises: it teaches ponds of animals and birds — one does not trap from them on Yom Tov; and it answers: animals — there is no difficulty — here in a small pond, here in a large pond; birds — there is no difficulty — here in a covered pond, here in an uncovered one; and it proved there that a sparrow even in a covered pond is not considered trapped — thus is the essence of the Gemara needed here. And Rambam and Ra'avad disagreed: Rambam holds Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel argues also on the first clause, and also regarding fish — if they are in a small pond it is permitted to trap them, for they are considered trapped, and animals, birds, and fish are equal; but Ra'avad holds Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel argues only on animals and birds, but regarding fish he agrees with the first tanna that even in a small pond it is forbidden to trap them; and Rama in Shulchan Aruch ruled like Ra'avad.
