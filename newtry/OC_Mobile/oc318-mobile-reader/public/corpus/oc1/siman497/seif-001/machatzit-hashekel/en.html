Seif katan 1, and the fish, etc. Because they are covered, etc. We learned at the beginning of the chapter Ein Tzadin: one may not trap fish from ponds on Yom Tov, but one may trap wild animals and birds from enclosures. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says: not all enclosures are the same; this is the rule: anything still lacking trapping is forbidden, and anything not lacking trapping is permitted. The halacha was ruled like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. The Gemara challenges this from a baraisa that states that one may not trap from enclosures of wild animals and birds on Yom Tov. It answers: wild animal against wild animal is not difficult; this speaks of a small enclosure and that of a large enclosure. Bird against bird is not difficult; this speaks of a roofed enclosure and that of an unroofed enclosure. It proves there that a deror bird, even in a roofed enclosure, is not considered already trapped. This is the summary of the Gemara needed here. The Rambam and Raavad disagree. The Rambam holds that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel disagrees also with the first clause, and that even with fish, if they are in a small enclosure, one may trap them, for they are considered already trapped, and wild animals, birds, and fish are the same. But the Raavad holds that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel disagrees only regarding wild animals and birds, but regarding fish he agrees with the first Tanna that even in a small enclosure it is forbidden to trap them. The Rama in Shulchan Aruch ruled like the Raavad.
However, the Maggid Mishneh challenged the Raavad from what we say on 25a: one who dammed a water channel before Yom Tov, the fish are permitted, as written in seif 5. But according to the Raavad, even in a small enclosure fish are forbidden. The Beis Yosef answered that the reason a water channel is permitted is that since it is narrow, even though it is very long, they are nevertheless considered trapped. This is unlike an enclosure, which is wide, and the fish are also hidden from sight and can more easily slip away; therefore it is forbidden even in an enclosure. Perforce, even according to the Rambam one must say this, for he permitted only a small enclosure, yet in a water channel it is permitted even if it is very long. It must be because a water channel is narrow. Through this the words of the Rama and Magen Avraham, and other places in this siman, will be understood.
If they are not lacking trapping, meaning in a small enclosure.
