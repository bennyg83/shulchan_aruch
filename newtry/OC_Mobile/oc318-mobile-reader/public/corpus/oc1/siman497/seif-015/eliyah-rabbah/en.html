That is near the city, etc. Rashi explained (Beitzah folio 25) that he knew of it and his mind was upon it. Therefore he explained: since it is near, he knew and also his mind was upon it; but when it is not near, even though he knew, his mind is not upon it. And you should know [this is correct], for if not so, why does the Talmud say that when it is not near it needs designation: knowledge alone would suffice. And so it is implied in Avodas HaKodesh there and on folio 46; and not like the Magen Avraham.
