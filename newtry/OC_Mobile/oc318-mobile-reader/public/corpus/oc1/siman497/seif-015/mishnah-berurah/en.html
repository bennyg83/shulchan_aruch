{47} Since they do not require trapping. They are young and cannot fly, as stated above in seif 9.
{48} They are permitted. They are permitted even to the person for whom they were brought. Where such birds belong to a Jew, their only prohibition is that he did not designate them and removed them from his mind before Yom Tov. Here, since they belong to a non-Jew, the non-Jew did not remove them from his mind, and they are therefore prepared for everyone. If the non-Jew brought them from outside the techum, they are forbidden to the person for whom they were brought and permitted to others, as stated below in siman 515, seif 5.
