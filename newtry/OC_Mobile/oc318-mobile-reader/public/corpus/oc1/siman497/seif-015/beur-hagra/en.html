Seif 15, a non-Jew, etc. As written in siman 524: if there is none of its type attached to the ground, etc. So too here, where they do not require trapping and are within the techum, even though he did not designate them before Yom Tov, the non-Jew does not require preparation, as written in siman 498, seif 3. See Magen Avraham.
