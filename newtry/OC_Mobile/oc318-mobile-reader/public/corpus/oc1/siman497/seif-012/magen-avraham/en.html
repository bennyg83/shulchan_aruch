(א) And found three. And if so there is one that is not designated mixed among them, and one cannot say one in two is nullified, for it is a davar sheyeish lo matirin (Ran); and according to this if one of them was eaten or fell into the sea, all are permitted as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 110 seif 7 note 8 — see there; but in the Gemara there is another concern lest perhaps all went elsewhere and these are others, and one must say Ran holds that per the conclusion that we say young pigeons are different since they are made to fly apart — therefore we are not concerned that all left, rather we say one came here; and specifically with money we are concerned for this, that if he left one hundred and found two hundred we say they are others, for there is no way to leave money beside another's money; but with pigeons we say one came from elsewhere; and see in the gloss and what is written siman 499.
