Seif katan 12, that they do not fly, etc. Therefore it appears to me that the Rosh in his responsum, who holds that they must specifically fly outside the techum, follows his own reasoning. Meaning, as the Magen Avraham wrote in the preceding seif katan, the Rosh holds like the first reason, that the matter depends on their coming to their coop and their sustenance being upon you. For trapping itself does not require that their sustenance be upon you; it is enough that they come to their coop. Rather, they must be prepared and not muktzeh, and therefore their sustenance must be upon him. For this reason they must specifically fly outside the techum: although they fly, and through this their sustenance is not upon him, what of it? Since they come to their coop in the evening, they are considered trapped. Their sustenance must be upon you only so that they be designated and not muktzeh. Since they do not fly outside the techum, they are still designated and not muktzeh, as we say regarding wilderness animals in siman 498, seif 3. But if they fly outside the techum, they are muktzeh. However, in his rulings the Rosh retracted and held that even so the reason is trapping, meaning that even if they come to their coop, if their sustenance is not upon him they are called lacking trapping. For otherwise, designation should help them; why do we rule that designation does not help them if their sustenance need not be upon him except to make them designated and avoid muktzeh? Rather, perforce, their sustenance must be upon you, and if not they are lacking trapping. Therefore even if they fly within the techum, since through this their sustenance is not upon him, although nevertheless they are designated as above, they are forbidden because they lack trapping. This is apparently the intent of the Magen Avraham. But his words require explanation. The core of the Gemara's words is as follows. Regarding Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel's statement that anything lacking trapping is forbidden, the Gemara asks: what is considered lacking trapping? Rav Yosef said in the name of Rav Yehudah in Shmuel's name: anything about which one says, bring a trap and let us catch it. Abaye said to him: what about geese and chickens, regarding which one says bring a trap, and yet a baraisa teaches that one who traps geese and chickens is exempt? Rabbah bar Rav Huna said in Shmuel's name: these come to their coop in the evening, etc. But what about dovecote doves and upper-story doves, which also come to their coop in the evening, and a baraisa teaches that one who traps dovecote doves and upper-story doves is liable? Rather, Rabbah bar Rav Huna said in Shmuel's name: these come to their coop and their sustenance is upon you, etc. Rav Mari said: these tend to flee, etc. Thus far the Gemara's words. If so, according to Rabbah bar Rav Huna in Shmuel's name, the reason why the baraisa teaches that one who traps dovecote and upper-story doves is liable speaks where they do come to their coop in the evening; yet because their sustenance is not upon you, he is liable. According to the Magen Avraham's understanding of the Rosh's responsum, that we need sustenance upon you only so that they be designated and not muktzeh, why is he liable for dovecote and upper-story doves when they come to their coop and do not lack trapping? Granted that their sustenance is not upon him and they are not designated, why should he be liable for muktzeh? Perhaps one can say, and so is implied by Rashi's language there, that Rashi wrote: and their sustenance is upon you, therefore they are like an animal and considered designated. Thus Rashi too wrote that because his sustenance is upon you it is considered designated. But what does designation have to do there, since the discussion concerns liability, which depends on trapping and not designation? Rather, one must say that although through their coming to their coop they are trapped, nevertheless if their sustenance is not upon him, they are not designated and his mind is not upon them; it is as though they are not in his possession, and through this they are considered lacking trapping, and because of this he is liable for trapping. This is the view of the Rosh's responsum. Therefore if they do not fly outside the techum, although their sustenance is not upon him, they are nevertheless considered designated and as though in his possession, and do not lack trapping. But when they fly outside the techum, they are as though not in his possession. In his rulings, however, the Rosh holds that even if his mind is upon them, whenever their sustenance is not upon him, meaning even if they do not fly outside the techum, they nevertheless lack trapping. For otherwise, if the problem were muktzeh because their sustenance is not upon him, designation should help even when they fly outside the techum. Still, this is difficult.
