(12) That they do not fly etc. — therefore it appears to me that Rosh in his responsum (who holds we require specifically that they fly outside the boundary) follows his reason, meaning as Magen Avraham wrote in the seif katan before this that Rosh holds like reason one that depends on coming to the coop and their sustenance upon you, and because of trapping he does not require sustenance upon you and it suffices that they come to the coop; rather they must be designated and not muktzeh, therefore we require specifically that they fly outside the boundary, for even though they fly and thereby their sustenance is not upon him — what of it, since they come to the coop in the evening they are trapped, but he requires sustenance upon them so they are designated and not muktzeh; and therefore we require specifically that they fly outside the boundary, for since they do not fly outside the boundary they are still designated and not muktzeh, as we say regarding wild animals in siman 498 seif 3; but in his rulings he retracted and holds that even so the reason is because of trapping, meaning even if they come to the coop, if their sustenance is not upon him it is called lacking trapping, for otherwise designation would help for them, and why do we rule that designation does not help for them since their sustenance is not upon him — rather that they be designated and not forbidden because of muktzeh; if so sustenance upon him is required, for otherwise they lack trapping; therefore even if they fly within the boundary, since through this their sustenance is not upon him, even though they are designated as stated, nevertheless they are forbidden because of lacking trapping — such is Magen Avraham's apparent intention; but his words require further study, for behold the essence of the Gemara's words is thus stated: on that which Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel said whatever requires trapping is forbidden — we say how is trapping defined? R' Yosef said R' Yehuda said: whatever we say "bring the trap and we will trap"; Abaye said to him: but geese and chickens — we say "bring the trap" etc., and it is taught: one who traps geese and chickens is exempt; Rabbah bar R' Huna said R' Yehuda said: these come to the coop in the evening etc., and behold dovecote pigeons and upper pigeons that come to the coop in the evening — and it is taught: one who traps dovecote pigeons and upper pigeons is liable; rather Rabbah bar R' Huna said R' Yehuda said: these come to the coop and their sustenance is upon you etc.; Rav Mari said these are frequent etc. — end of the Gemara; and if so to establish Rabbah bar R' Huna's words: the reason the baraita teaches dovecote pigeons and upper pigeons — one who traps is liable — deals with coming to the coop in the evening; rather since their sustenance is not upon you he is liable, for per Mishna Berurah's responsum to Rosh who does not require sustenance upon them but only that they be designated and not muktzeh — if so why is one liable for dovecote and upper pigeons since they come to the coop and are not lacking trapping; they are that sustenance is not upon them and they are not designated — why should he be liable because of muktzeh prohibition? Perhaps one can say, and so is Rashi's language there: and their sustenance upon you — therefore they are like an animal and are considered designated — end of his words; behold Rashi also wrote that through sustenance upon him they are considered designated; and what difference to me whether because of prohibition of designation or because of liability — we deal with whether trapping applies, not with designation; if so one must say: even though through coming to the coop they are trapped, nevertheless if their sustenance is not upon them, since they are not designated, his mind is not upon them and it is as though they are not in his domain; and through this it is called lacking trapping and through this he is liable because of trapping — and so is the view of Rosh's responsum; therefore if they do not fly outside the boundary, behold sustenance is upon him, nevertheless they are like designated and as though in his domain and are not lacking trapping; not so when they fly outside the boundary it is as though they are not in his domain; but Rosh in his rulings holds even if his mind is upon them, all that their sustenance is not upon him — namely they do not fly outside the boundary — nevertheless they lack trapping, for otherwise designation would help for them even if they fly outside the boundary; and nevertheless designation does not help for them since their sustenance is not upon him; rather they must be designated and not forbidden as muktzeh; if so prohibition of trapping caused it, since sustenance is not upon him — designation would help for them even if they fly outside the boundary; and nevertheless it is difficult.
