{21} In an orchard. Meaning, an enclosed and guarded place from which the young animal cannot leave and where it is easy to catch.
{22} They are permitted without designation. His intention is ordinarily upon them whenever he may need them.
{23} Within seventy. Meaning, seventy cubits and four tefachim, as stated above in siman 358.
{24} His intention is upon them. Since the place is nearby and he sees them constantly, he does not remove them from his mind even without an explicit designation. If it is not nearby, designation is required; without designation they are forbidden.
{25} Since it requires trapping. The case is one of a large orchard where one must say, 'Bring a net and let us trap it.' Even if it becomes trapped on its own on Yom Tov, it remains forbidden because of muktzeh, since his designation before Yom Tov never took effect.
