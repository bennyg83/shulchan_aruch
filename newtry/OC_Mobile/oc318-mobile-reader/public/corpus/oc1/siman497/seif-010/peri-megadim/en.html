Before the nest. See Taz, Beitzah 10 and 11: and Abaye establishes it regarding a board, and Rava regarding two nests, each to push aside so that it not be a support for Rav Chinena; but for us, who hold that majority and nearness: follow the majority, it can be established as its plain meaning. And Rava does not establish it where there are small rooms, for what would that teach us: it is obvious there is majority and nearness toward prohibition. And the poskim did not bring that of Rava. And behold, there is a practical difference where there is no nest nearby within fifty, and regarding those that fly a bit: that they only walk about, he should have taught us two nests that extended or cling by clinging, and it is forbidden, for there is a great practical difference. And see Beis Yosef's answer on this: one may say that according to the conclusion that reasoning is not accepted, and regarding those that walk about and do not fly we are not concerned in such a case, and it is permitted when he finds [them] before the nest. And what he wrote, "whatever he found in their place": not specifically before the nest; those too are called in their place. And see Yam shel Shlomo, first chapter, siman 28; and according to what I wrote it is fine. And see Bach: one may say that since according to the Torah, why say that Rava's words are not accepted. And the Taz wrote that the word "in their place" is difficult; and I already explained it in the name of Yam shel Shlomo; and in our novellae on Beitzah we wrote about this.
