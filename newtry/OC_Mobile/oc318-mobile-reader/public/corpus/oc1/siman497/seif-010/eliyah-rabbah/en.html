It is permitted to take, etc. Specifically to take all of them is permitted, but it is forbidden to select, as he wrote in se'if 11 regarding pigeons (Yam shel Shlomo). And it is possible that it is similar to geese and chickens in the house, which do not need designation, as he wrote in se'if 6 (Magen Avraham). And in Yam shel Shlomo, se'if 12, it appears to me to distinguish: there he knew about them from yesterday, but here he did not know from yesterday which kinds of fish are in it. And that is why in s.k. 2, regarding one who bought fish and placed them in a box, the Maharshal was stringent only for a God-fearing person, for there he knew about them.
