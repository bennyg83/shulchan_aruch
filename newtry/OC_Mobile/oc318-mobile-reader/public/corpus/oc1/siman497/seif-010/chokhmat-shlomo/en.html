Se'if 10. How is the designation? He says, "This one and that one I am taking," etc. Marginal note: see what I wrote on this in my work Bigdei Yom Tov on Maseches Beitzah in the omissions, where I explained the wording of the Yerushalmi in this sugya; see there and examine carefully. (There, se'if 14:) If he designated [birds] inside the nest and found [birds] in front of the nest, they are forbidden. Marginal note: see what I wrote in my work on Even HaEzer, third edition, siman 17, in the Takana, to reconcile the view of the Rashba who copied the sugya of the Talmud as dealing with a board; and I settled, with God's help, on a correct path; see there and examine carefully.
