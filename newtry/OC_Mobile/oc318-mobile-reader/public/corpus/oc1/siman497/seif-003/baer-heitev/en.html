Doubt. Even though we hold that a rabbinic doubt is judged leniently, here it is forbidden because it is a matter that has a [later] permit; see Yoreh De'ah siman 110. But in the evening they are permitted even if they were certainly trapped today of themselves; see siman 325. (And see in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah what he answered to the difficulty of the Bach, who wanted to disagree with the Shulchan Aruch that permits when one finds [nets] disordered, and he agreed to the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch; see there, where he elaborated with upright words.)
