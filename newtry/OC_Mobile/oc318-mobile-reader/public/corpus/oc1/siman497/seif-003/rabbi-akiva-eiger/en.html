Magen Avraham, s.k. 14. "If one of them was eaten or fell into the sea, all of them are permitted." Even though before one of them fell into the sea the law was that all of them are forbidden and it is not in his power to permit them, for if he cast [one] down by hand we hold that they are forbidden, and if so at that time they became muktzeh: nevertheless now that one of them fell, and we attribute that the forbidden one fell, those [remaining] are permitted, for there is no muktzeh for half of Shabbos, as above in siman 308 and below in siman 496, se'if 9.
