Even if it is doubtful. Although we generally rule leniently in a doubt concerning a D'Rabbanan prohibition, here it is forbidden because it is an item that will later become permitted. See Yoreh Deah, siman 110. Alternatively, the Sages chose to be stringent in this matter. See Mordechai in Yevamos, where we find several D'Rabbanan doubts ruled stringently, and therefore one case may not be compared to another. In the evening they are permitted even if they were certainly trapped today without human action. See siman 322, seif 3, and siman 365, seif 5.
