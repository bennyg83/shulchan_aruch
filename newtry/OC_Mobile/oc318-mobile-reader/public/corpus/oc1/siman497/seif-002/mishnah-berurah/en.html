{4} Fish and birds, etc., that are muktzeh. Since the reason given is muktzeh, it implies that the same applies to an animal, such as a wild-grazing animal discussed below in siman 498, seif 3, or a goose or hen kept for producing eggs. Although trapping does not apply to them, since they are muktzeh it is forbidden to give them water or place food before them, lest one come to eat from them on Yom Tov. Below in seif 7, however, the Mechaber rules without qualification in accordance with the authorities who make the matter depend upon trapping, so that one may place food before any creature that does not require trapping. Pri Chadash writes that this is the common practice and the principal halachah; see there.
{5} One may not give them water, etc., or place food before them. The later authorities write that it is forbidden only to place the food directly before them. If one places it somewhat distant from them and they come and eat, there is no concern, because the unusual manner serves as a reminder and he will not come to take one of them.
{6} Lest he come to take one of them. Meaning, to eat it. Regarding a non-kosher animal, where this concern does not apply, all agree that it is permitted.
