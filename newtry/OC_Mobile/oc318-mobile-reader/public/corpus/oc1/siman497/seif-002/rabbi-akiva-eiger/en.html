Magen Avraham, s.k. 4. "And so is implied in siman 513, se'if 6." Regarding this one can say that there, where it refers to the second day of Yom Tov that falls on Sunday, since there is in this a Torah prohibition of preparation, it is a double doubt in a Torah matter. But here, preparation involving trapping, which is rabbinic: in that we are lenient with a double doubt. And so the wording of the Ran, chapter 3 of Beitzah, is precise, for he wrote: therefore a doubtful case of something prepared on the second day of Yom Tov, since muktzeh was forbidden only by rabbinic law, it is a double doubt in a rabbinic matter; end quote. And it is all the more difficult from what the Mechaber wrote in siman 513, se'if 3, and likewise on the second night of the second day of Yom Tov of the Diaspora it is clear that on the second day it is forbidden to take from a gentile; and this requires further study.
