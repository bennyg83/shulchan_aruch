6) [Se'if 2] Fish, birds, and animals that are muktzeh, etc. This is Rambam's wording in chapter 2, law 17. It shows that even without a catching prohibition, one may not feed muktzeh creatures, as a decree lest he move one. Although moving is only rabbinically forbidden and this would seem one decree upon another, the concern that he may take one is evidently not a formal decree but a likely concern, which applies even where the underlying prohibition is rabbinic. Maamar Mordechai 3.
7) There: One may not give them drink, etc. This applies specifically to species fit for eating but presently muktzeh. There is no prohibition against watering and feeding nonkosher animals or birds. Maggid Mishneh there, cited by Magen Avraham 2; Eliyah Rabbah 4; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 6; Mikra Kodesh 37:16; Mishnah Berurah 6. This is only where their food is his responsibility. Shulchan Aruch HaRav and Mikra Kodesh there. See below, paragraph 10.
8) On Shabbos, which is more stringent, the decree lest he take one does not apply; therefore, even kosher species may be fed. Eliyah Rabbah and Mikra Kodesh there, Likkutei Rema 21. See above, siman 324:10–11.
9) There: And one may not place food before them, etc. One may place it a short distance away. Magen Avraham; Eliyah Rabbah; Chemed Moshe 4; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 5; Chayei Adam 88:7; Mikra Kodesh 37:15; Mishnah Berurah 5. Shulchan Aruch HaRav explains that requiring distance provides a reminder, so he will not forget and take them. Mishnah Berurah agrees.
10) This applies only when their food is his responsibility, where the sole prohibition is lest he take them, so distance permits it. If their food is not his responsibility, it is forbidden in every manner. Dagul Merevavah; Mikra Kodesh there, Likkutei Rema 22.
11) There: And one may not place food before them, etc. Rashi on Beitzah 23b explains that fish can live without supplied food: they eat plant roots and earth, and larger fish eat smaller ones. Tosafos say feeding fish is forbidden lest one catch them. Beis Yosef cites both and says Tur holds that even where taking fish is permitted, one may not feed them because, as Rashi says, they can live without it. Darkei Moshe 1 writes that even according to Rashi, fish purchased before Yom Tov and kept in water inside a vessel may be fed, since they have no other food. Levush and Ateres Zekenim agree. More is explained below in se'if 7.
