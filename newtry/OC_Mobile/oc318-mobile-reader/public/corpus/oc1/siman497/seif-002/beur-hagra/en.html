Seif 2, one may not water, etc. As stated at the end of Beitzah: one may not water, etc. And when the Gemara there asks why it needed to say watering, meaning why it mentioned watering specifically, this is unlike the Ran there, who wrote that watering is permitted. This is why the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch used the language of watering; see Magen Avraham.
And anything that is forbidden, etc. The Gemara there asks: permitted for what purpose, etc.; and according to your reasoning, etc.; rather, etc.
