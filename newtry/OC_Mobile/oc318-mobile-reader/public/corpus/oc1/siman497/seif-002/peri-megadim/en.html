Even. See Taz: I explained it in letter 1. And he raised a slight difficulty on the Rema in what he wrote "very wide," for that certainly lacks trapping, and that is like the Rambam, of blessed memory. And however, the Mechaber rules like the Raavad and the Rashba, since he did not include fish with wild animals and birds in se'if 7; and if so he should have said that it is not specifically an irrigation channel, for even a small fishpond is somewhat wide, according to the answer of Beis Yosef and the Bach. And according to what the Taz wrote in letter 1, the reason is because of muktzeh: even if it is narrow like an irrigation channel, as long as he did not perform an act, they are forbidden because of muktzeh.
