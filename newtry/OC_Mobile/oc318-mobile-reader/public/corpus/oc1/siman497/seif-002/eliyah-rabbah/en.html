That the fishpond is wide, etc. The Magen Avraham wrote: and the Maharshal took a different view for himself, for he wrote that regarding fish one is not liable because of trapping, for it is not comparable to the Mishkan; rather one is liable because of reaping, for it is uprooting a thing from its growth, as is in the Yerushalmi; and therefore one is liable even on Yom Tov; and specifically in a fishpond on the ground, but a perforated crate in the water: trapping does not apply to them at all, even if one needs a small net; until here. And I do not know, etc., for the Yerushalmi deals with when one causes a fish to die; but it is obvious that trapping applies to fish, as all the poskim wrote. And what he wrote, "and therefore one is liable even on Yom Tov," meaning that reaping is forbidden on Yom Tov, is not a proof, for trapping too is forbidden on Yom Tov; until here. And according to my humble opinion, certainly even R' Shimon agrees that trapping applies to fish; rather he refers explicitly to this fishpond that does not lack trapping, for trapping of this sort was not in the Mishkan, for wild animals and birds in the Mishkan lacked trapping; rather one is liable because of reaping, and therefore it is forbidden even on Yom Tov, for trapping of this sort he holds they certainly did not forbid on Yom Tov, for specifically complete trapping they forbade. Also what he wrote in explaining the Yerushalmi, the Maharshal already rejected there; and so it is implied in the Kol Bo, folio 19. And all this is clear to one who examines Yam shel Shlomo, siman 2, and I abbreviated. And regarding the law of the crate mentioned above, the Taz agreed that even if one reaches with a net into a square box to take it, even if one reaches several times until one removes it, there is no insistence. And so it appears to me to be lenient, for according to the Rambam even in a fishpond it is permitted. The Maharshal wrote there: specifically if one wants to take all of them and cook them, or there are not many fish there; but when there are many fish there and on Yom Tov one wants to take one or two, a God-fearing person should be careful in the matter l'chatchila to tie with a string the one he needs; until here. And the Taz challenged this; this is his wording: And I wondered: it is obvious that it is permitted to take everything; what difference is there from damming an irrigation channel in se'if 5?; until here. And the words of the Maharshal in siman 11 escaped him, for he wrote also regarding an irrigation channel that it deals with when one wants to take all of them; and see s.k. 7. Afterward I saw in the Taz, s.k. 7, that he brought the words of the Maharshal and strained to explain them; and also in this his words there contradict [his words] here.
