Geese. See Taz: in the Gemara Beitzah there are two answers; alternatively, perhaps he finds them all lean and refrains from the joy of Yom Tov. One may say regarding those in the house: he feels about constantly and knows they are fit; see Peri Chadash. And see letter 1: one may say that whatever needs designation needs proper designation; which is not so here: when he took them without specification they too are prepared, unless he took it to raise eggs, then they need proper designation, possibly; and see letters 6-7, and God willing there it will be clarified further. And what he wrote: whatever is permitted on Shabbos: all the more so the Sages did not decree on Yom Tov; but regarding muktzeh they were more stringent on Yom Tov; see Tosafos, Beitzah 8a, s.v. "ve'eino"; see there.
