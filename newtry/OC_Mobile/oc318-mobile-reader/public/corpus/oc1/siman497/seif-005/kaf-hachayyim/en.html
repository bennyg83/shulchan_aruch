5) There, gloss: And the fish evade, etc. Because they are hidden from view. Raavad on Rambam, chapter 2, law 7. Thus, if the water is clear and the fish visible, it is permitted when they do not require catching. Magen Avraham 1; Eliyah Rabbah 3; Eshel Avraham 1. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, however, forbids even clear water in Kuntres Acharon 1. Since Rambam permits a small enclosure whenever no catching is needed, if the fish were designated before Yom Tov, the enclosure is closed on all sides, and the water is clear enough to seize them quickly without evasion, one may be lenient. More is explained below in se'if 5.
