(ב) As written seif 10 regarding pigeons — one must say "this and this I will take," and it does not suffice to say "from here I will take." And the reason as Magen Avraham seif katan 13 wrote, and these are the words of the Gemara: sometimes he leaves the weak ones etc. and moves something unfit; or sometimes all are weak and he will come to withhold Yom Tov joy — from these reasons Yesh Shtar holds that regarding fish too there is concern, and therefore he must take all of them. And it is possible it is similar to geese etc., that it is only a stringency of words — "from this Yom Tov this and this I will take" or take all of them, as Magen Avraham seif katan 8 wrote. And the reason to distinguish between pigeons and geese — Yesh Shtar wrote in chapter Eruvin siman 2: reason one — lest he leave the weak ones etc. and move something not fit for him — this applies specifically regarding pigeons that require designation because of muktzeh; and if after moving he leaves them because they are weak, it is clarified retroactively that he did not designate the weak ones, and thus he moved muktzeh; not so geese and chickens that do not require designation for they are not muktzeh — this concern does not apply. And even so there is concern lest it be clarified retroactively that he exerted effort not for Yom Tov need in moving the weak ones — most authorities did not concern for extra effort such as this, only Ra'avyah and Rashbatz; and for practical law one may rely on most authorities; only one who fears Heaven should clarify on erev Yom Tov to satisfy Ra'avyah and Rashbatz. And reason two — lest all be found weak and he withhold Yom Tov joy — does not apply regarding geese, since they walk through the house and he feels them every hour whether fat or weak.
