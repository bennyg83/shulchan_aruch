(Taz, s.k. 9) Rather, this matter should be learned from a firstborn. Marginal note: and I have never seen elder Sages who were concerned for this. And the proper [comparison] does not resemble the proof, for according to Rashi the reason is that it is like one who judged a judgment: that applies only regarding a firstborn, which is like monetary laws, where through the judge's ruling one takes money from this domain to another domain; here similarly he would take the firstborn from the domain of hekdesh to the domain of the kohen through his ruling. Which is not so regarding the laws of forbidden and permitted. And if [the concern is] because of muktzeh, yesterday it was prepared for dogs, and what is prepared for dogs is prepared for man. And one can find [such a case] only regarding the prohibition of meat in milk that is wet into wet, which was forbidden for dogs from yesterday: regarding meat in milk we say that a piece becomes like a nevelah, and one must say that permitted [matter] sixty times against the mixture was added; if so, we are judging about this majority, and this had a presumption of being permitted, as it appears to me according to my humble opinion. And see Terumas HaDeshen, siman 94; see there.
