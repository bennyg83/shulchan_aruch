And the Maharshal, chapter "Ein Tzadin," also wrote to permit with this box, even if one needs a small net to reach in there: there is no trapping in that. Except that he wrote there that it appears to him to distinguish: specifically if one wants to take all of them and cook them, it is permitted; but if one wants to take one or two, a God-fearing person should tie with a string the one he needs; see there. And the Bach wrote that if the kastin is wider than six tefachim, it is forbidden to take from it; see there. And the Taz concluded and permits without tying a string, and even if it is wider than six tefachim, provided that he does not return to the water what is not good for him; see there. And the Magen Avraham wrote that one should be stringent in any case to have it done by a gentile when the crate is wide; see there.
