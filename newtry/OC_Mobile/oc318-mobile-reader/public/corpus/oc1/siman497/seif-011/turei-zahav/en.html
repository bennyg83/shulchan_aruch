If he designated the whole dovecote, etc. It is difficult for me regarding what is written above about damming an irrigation channel, that it is permitted since there is no permit for muktzeh except through what one prepares, as above in our words in se'if 1; and if so it is like preparing all the fish in the water, and this does not help. And it appears to me to answer: that regarding fish this concern certainly does not apply; even if the fish is not good in his eyes, he will not return and throw it into the water; rather he will go back to trap another and will eat all of them, as is the way of the whole world. And regarding a wild animal and a bird above, where it is permitted, there is no such concern, for all of them are already prepared for him, like geese and chickens, as above. Afterward I saw the Maharshal, chapter "Ein Tzadin," who wrote that even in water there is no permit to take all the fish, but to select from them according to need is forbidden, lest he leave, etc. And this is very puzzling: how is that possible? And it is possible that his intent is as I wrote: that he take everything without selecting, and return to the water what is not good for him.
