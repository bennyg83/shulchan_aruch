Seif katan 11, forbidden, etc., Gemara. Meaning, these are the two answers of the Gemara.
And in the Rosh it implies like the first reason. For he wrote the reason for permitting the trapping of geese and chickens: since they come to their coop and their sustenance is upon you, and therefore they are easy to catch. This implies that it depends on their coming to their coop and their sustenance being upon you. But in the Ran it implies, etc., for the Ran gave the reason and permitted trapping geese and chickens because they are easy to catch, implying that the matter depends on this alone.
One should be concerned for both reasons in order to satisfy the Rosh and the Ran: that they come to their coop, their sustenance is upon you, and they are easy to catch.
