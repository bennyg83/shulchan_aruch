11) There: And one may not place food before them, etc. Rashi on Beitzah 23b explains that fish can live without supplied food: they eat plant roots and earth, and larger fish eat smaller ones. Tosafos say feeding fish is forbidden lest one catch them. Beis Yosef cites both and says Tur holds that even where taking fish is permitted, one may not feed them because, as Rashi says, they can live without it. Darkei Moshe 1 writes that even according to Rashi, fish purchased before Yom Tov and kept in water inside a vessel may be fed, since they have no other food. Levush and Ateres Zekenim agree. More is explained below in se'if 7.
