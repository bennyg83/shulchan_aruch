A double doubt. And in Yoreh De'ah siman 110, se'if 8, the Rema wrote that some say that for a double doubt, [the leniency of] a matter that has a [later] permit does not help; and here he wrote nothing, and this requires further study (Taz). And see the Shach and the Peri Chadash there, and see Magen Avraham.
