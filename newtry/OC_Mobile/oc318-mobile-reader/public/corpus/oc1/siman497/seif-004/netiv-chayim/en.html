(Taz, s.k. 9) And I have reason to scrutinize why R' Avraham needed [this]. Marginal note: below, that he compelled R' Avraham to establish the dispute even regarding [items that are] tied, from that statement in Pesachim folio 10: if he left ten and found nine, that is the dispute of Rabbi and the Sages, and there it deals with tied [items], as Tosafos wrote there.
