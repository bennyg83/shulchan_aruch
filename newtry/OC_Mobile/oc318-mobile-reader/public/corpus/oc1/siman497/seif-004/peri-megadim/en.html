The traps — Taz; and see Peri Chadash and Bach and Eliyah Rabbah and Avodat HaGashmi — see there; and for halacha Peri Chadash and Eliyah Rabbah ruled leniently like Shulchan Aruch. And behold if the trap can trap several at once and he found two or three there — it appears one can say there is a double safek: perhaps all were trapped yesterday; and if one today, perhaps this one was trapped yesterday; and if three — one can say two were trapped today, the majority is prohibition and all are forbidden, for even if we say the leniency of one in two prohibition, if he ate one of them he is not lashed, as written in Pri Chadash in the introduction Sha'ar HaTa'aruvot in Yoreh De'ah — nevertheless there is prohibition as though all are forbidden, and requires further study.
