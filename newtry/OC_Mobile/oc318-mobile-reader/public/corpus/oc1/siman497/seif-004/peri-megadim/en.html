The traps. See Taz; and see Peri Chadash and Bach and Eliyah Rabbah and Or Zarua; see there. And for practical law the Peri Chadash and Eliyah Rabbah ruled that it is permitted, like the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch. And behold, if it is possible for a trap to catch several, one after another, and he found two or three there: at first glance one may say it is a double doubt: maybe all were trapped yesterday, and even if you say one today, maybe this is among those trapped yesterday. And if three: one may say, even if you say two were trapped today, the majority is forbidden, and all are forbidden; for even if we say a permit of one in two forbidden, and he ate one of them, he does not receive lashes, as is written in my Pri [Megadim] in the introduction, Shaar HaTaaruvos in Yoreh De'ah: nevertheless there is a prohibition as if all are forbidden; and this requires further study.
