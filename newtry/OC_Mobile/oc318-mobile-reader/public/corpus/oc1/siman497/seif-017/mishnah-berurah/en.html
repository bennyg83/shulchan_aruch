{51} It is forbidden to move it. Even if it stands in the sun and will spoil, it is forbidden to place it in the shade. This was not permitted because of concern that he might refrain from slaughtering, since he can slaughter it in the shade or at least place it there before the inspection. Some write that the concern that he may refrain from slaughtering was not considered because tereifos are uncommon. Accordingly, some write that nowadays, when we are stringent regarding many possible lung defects and tereifos are common, there is reason to fear that he will refrain from slaughtering if we do not at least permit him to conceal the tereifah in a protected place so that it does not spoil. Therefore, nowadays one may conceal it. This leniency applies only to geese and chickens, where lung tereifos are not relevant; regarding them the original law remains and they are forbidden to move. All this follows the Mechaber's view that muktzeh is forbidden on Yom Tov, as stated above in siman 495. According to the opinion cited there by the Rama that muktzeh is permitted, it is likewise permitted here, as several poskim write. Some maintain, however, that an animal found to be a tereifah is nolad, since initially it was fit for human consumption and now is fit only for dogs. According to this view, it is forbidden even according to the lenient opinion, because the Rama writes there that we are stringent regarding nolad.
{52} But it is permitted, etc. The later authorities write that the same applies to a kosher animal in a place where there is no expert available to remove the forbidden fats from the hindquarters. It may be sold to a non-Jew in the manner explained here. This applies only if there is concern that it will spoil before the end of Yom Tov. If it can be preserved, sale on Yom Tov is not permitted, whether it is a tereifah or consists of hindquarters. Even where spoilage is feared, the sale is permitted only if it was slaughtered on Yom Tov or shortly before evening on Erev Yom Tov, so that there was no opportunity to sell it before Yom Tov. If there was sufficient time, it is forbidden in all circumstances.
