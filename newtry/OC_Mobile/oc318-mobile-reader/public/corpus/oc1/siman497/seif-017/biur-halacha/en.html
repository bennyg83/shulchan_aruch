It is forbidden to move it. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav wrote: one who inspects an animal on Yom Tov should inspect it in a place where it will be possible to leave it there if it is a treifah, because it is forbidden to move it when it is found to be a treifah from this place to another place. And likewise a sage who issues a ruling regarding a matter that would be forbidden to move if he forbids it should not rule until they first place it in its designated place, for after he forbids it, it will be forbidden to move it; end quote. And that is from the sugya of Shabbos 156 in the incident of Levi; see there. And the Rashba in Avodas HaKodesh also copied this, and this is his wording: It appears that one should not examine a treifah except in a place where if it is found to be a treifah one will not need to move it from its place; but I have not seen any of the Rishonim who were concerned for this matter; end quote. And I found in Eliyah Rabbah that he brought the words of the Rashba and wrote that it is possible those Rishonim held that muktzeh is permitted on Yom Tov; and his words are difficult, for he wrote that he did not see [it in] any of the Rishonim, and there are several Rishonim who hold that muktzeh on Yom Tov is forbidden. And according to my humble opinion one can say that they saw that many doubts multiplied in the laws of treifos and we are stringent out of doubt, and through this treifos are common, and if we forbid moving afterward one will refrain from slaughtering, as is the reasoning of the Maharshal.
