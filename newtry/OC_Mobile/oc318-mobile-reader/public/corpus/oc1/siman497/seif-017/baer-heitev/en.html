And the Levush wrote in siman 500 that in a place where one does not remove the sinews it is permitted to sell the hindquarters to a gentile in this manner, for otherwise they would not slaughter animals for Yom Tov; end quote. And the Magen Avraham wrote: and it appears to me that if one can keep it until after Yom Tov so that it not spoil, it is forbidden to sell a treifah or hindquarters to a gentile. And if it was slaughtered before Yom Tov, in every case it is forbidden, as is written in siman 499, unless it was slaughtered close to Yom Tov such that it was not possible to sell before Yom Tov; see there.
