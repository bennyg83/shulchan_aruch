If one designated birds inside the nest and found them in front of the nest, they are forbidden. If there is no other nest nearby, they are permitted. This applies when they are hopping, but if they can fly they are forbidden. And even if there is another nest within fifty cubits, if it is around a corner such that one cannot see it from this nest — since they are only hopping and not flying, wherever it hops and does not return, it is evident it belongs to that nest and has not hopped away from it. {Rama: (If one designated birds inside the nest and found them at the entrance of the nest, they are permitted.) (Or Zarua)}
