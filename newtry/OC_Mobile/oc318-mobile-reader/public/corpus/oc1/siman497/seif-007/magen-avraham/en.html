That require trapping. Meaning in a pond that is not covered; but in a covered one trapping does not apply at all to birds even in a very large one, only to a sparrow that trapping applies even in a covered one; and regarding animals where one animal reaches him, there is no trapping and even those that rebel are permitted; but in a place where Torah trapping applies, even to drive them to a house is forbidden (Mishna Berurah). To me requires further study, for wherever one cannot catch it in one pursuit, even in a covered one it is forbidden regarding an animal and all the more so a bird; and further Tosafos wrote explicitly that which Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel said "that which we say 'bring the trap and we will trap'" refers to birds in a covered pond — see there; and so in Yesh Shtar and in all the authorities who did not distinguish between covered and uncovered but whatever requires a trap is forbidden; and so in Rambam ch. 10 who wrote one who drives a bird to a large pond is exempt — and necessarily it deals with a covered one, for when it is not covered even for a small pond he is exempt for it can fly outside; rather necessarily it deals with a covered one and nevertheless for a large pond it is exempt for it is not considered trapped; and if so if it is in a large pond trapping applies — and this is the intention of the authorities who omitted the matter of sparrow, rather they gave a general rule for a place where it is trapped etc., meaning that no trap is needed; and so Shulchan Aruch at the beginning of siman 316 who wrote "to a house where they are trapped in it" etc. — see there; and so in Rambam who wrote if he brought a bird into a tower he is liable, and if he brought a sparrow into a house he is exempt, and afterward wrote one who drives a bird to a large pond is exempt — end of his words: at the beginning he said "bird" to include every bird considered trapped in a tower, and in the middle he said "sparrow" meaning specifically sparrow is not considered trapped in a house, but other birds are considered trapped because a house is small; and at the end he said "bird" to teach that in a large pond every bird is not considered trapped even if covered as stated above; and so in the Gemara.
