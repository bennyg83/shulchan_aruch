A doubtful case of something prepared, etc. Even though we hold that a rabbinic doubt is judged leniently, here it is forbidden because it is a matter that has a [later] permit, or because the Sages wished to be stringent in this matter, for we find several rabbinic doubts [judged] stringently, and one should not compare one to another. But in the evening they are permitted even if they were certainly trapped today of themselves; see siman 316, with God's help.
