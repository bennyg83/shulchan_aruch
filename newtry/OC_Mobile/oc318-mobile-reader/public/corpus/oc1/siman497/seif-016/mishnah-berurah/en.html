{50} He will not be permitted to skin it. Skinning is a melachah not needed for eating. Likewise, if a bird is found to be a tereifah after slaughter, it is forbidden to pluck its feathers. Even skinning in a manner prohibited only as a shevus is forbidden. We do not permit it because of the concern that otherwise he may refrain from slaughtering and thereby diminish rejoicing on Yom Tov, as in siman 499, seif 3, because he can skin the animal before the inspection.
