Geese and chickens, etc. One should not say lest lean ones come to his hand and he move them for nothing: since there is no muktzeh here, there is no concern in this; which is not so regarding pigeons in se'if 10. And Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote in the name of Ravyah: however, a God-fearing person should set his eyes from the outset to take, and not select and leave; even though it is not muktzeh, nevertheless they were concerned for excessive exertion; and so wrote the Rashbam; end quote. And so wrote Beis Yosef in the name of Maharai. And the Maharshal wrote that one should be careful in this to select while it is still day, to say "this one and that one I am taking." And regarding what is written here that geese are permitted to trap: in the Gemara in a baraisa it is stated regarding this "exempt"; and Tosafos found it difficult, for all exemptions of Shabbos mean exempt but forbidden. And the Maharshal wrote that there is nothing to ask, for since on Shabbos it is exempt, automatically on Yom Tov it is permitted l'chatchila. And there is a proof for this from what Beis Yosef wrote in the name of the Ramban, siman 498, s.v. "katav haRambam," etc.; and so wrote Tosafos in the first chapter of Beitzah, folio 5.
