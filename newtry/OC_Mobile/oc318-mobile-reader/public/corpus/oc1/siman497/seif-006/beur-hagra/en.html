Seif 6, geese, etc. There, 25a and there 24a, and it was taught: one who traps geese, etc. See the Rosh there, and one must say that what he says, etc.; and according to this, etc. Meaning, in a house it is certainly permitted, as above, but geese, etc.; and there in the Rosh and in Baal HaItur, etc.; and even according to Tosafot, etc. This is why he wrote: in a house or in a courtyard.
