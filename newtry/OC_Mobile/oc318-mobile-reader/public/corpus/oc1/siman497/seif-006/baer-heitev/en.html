Geese. And the same applies to other birds that come to their coop: they are considered already trapped, and it is permitted to catch them even outside their coop; and even if one must say "bring a trap and we will trap them," it is permitted; and even outside the courtyard it is permitted to catch them. But those that he bought newly and that are not yet accustomed in his house and do not come to their coop toward evening, their law is like other wild animals and birds: whatever is forbidden according to the Torah, there is no distinction between Shabbos and Yom Tov. And the Maharshal wrote that a God-fearing person should select before Yom Tov and say, "This one and that one I am taking." And when one does not need to eat them, it is forbidden to trap them; see Magen Avraham and Taz.
