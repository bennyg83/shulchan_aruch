And even. See Taz: he elaborated and distinguished that here [regarding] a matter that has a [later] permit, its doubt is forbidden, and even regarding a doubt that is a matter of dispute; for that reason the Tur omitted. And one may say [it is] from a doubt regarding tied [items]; and in Tal Tohar there are those who explain regarding untied ones, that is regarding nullification. And see Peri Chadash on this.
