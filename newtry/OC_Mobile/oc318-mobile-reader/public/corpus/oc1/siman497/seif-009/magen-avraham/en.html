(1) It is not allowed to the outsider. And you must sin and taste even though they are not caged for the evening MM, their food is not on you and I do not consider it as cash AN because they must escape and run away from humans and it is not convenient to catch them (Gam') and in the Rash Gabi Ouzin it means like the first taste and in the Ran it means like the second taste and both tastes must be felt Essi' Shi'u:

(ב) That they do not fly. Outside the boundary (Yesh Shtar ch. 1 siman 28 and Beit Yosef siman 517 in the name of responsum of Rosh) — but Magen Mishneh and Ran wrote specifically regarding those that fly apart it is permitted, for it does not fly more than fifty amot, or they fly a little; and also in Tur siman 517 and Rosh in his rulings did not write the words "outside the boundary" — therefore it appears to me Rosh in his responsum follows his reason who holds that since pigeons come to the coop in the evening they are not lacking trapping, but because their sustenance is not upon them they are not considered designated; and therefore if they do not fly outside the boundary they are considered designated; but in his rulings he retracted and holds that nevertheless the reason is because of trapping, for otherwise designation would help; and therefore if they fly — for trapping is needed to trap them — it is forbidden; and see Magen Mishneh.
