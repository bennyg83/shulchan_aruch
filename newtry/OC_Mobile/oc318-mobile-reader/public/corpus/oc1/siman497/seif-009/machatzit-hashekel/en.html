(א) (seif katan 9) That require trapping etc. — to me requires further study etc., meaning what Mishna Berurah permitted regarding birds in a covered pond — even in a large pond except sparrow, even in a covered pond it is forbidden, for regarding an animal it cannot fly away, there is no reason to distinguish between covered or uncovered; and further Tosafos wrote etc. daf 24 that which is stated there: that which Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel said this is the principle — whatever requires trapping is forbidden — how is this defined? R' Yehuda said: whatever we say "bring the trap and we will trap"; and Rashi challenged: for above from him he said another measure of what appears not trapped, namely whatever cannot be reached in one pursuit; and it appears Rashi's explanation that these two measures are the same, namely what is reached in one pursuit or does not require a trap are equal; and Tosafos wrote that this is strained; therefore he wrote to answer, and this is his language: above it deals with an animal and here with a bird; and even though above he said (meaning regarding a bird, for they raise a contradiction: but we trap animals and birds from ponds, and there it teaches ponds of animals and birds — one does not trap from them on Yom Tov; and it answers birds — there is no difficulty — here in a covered pond, here in uncovered — it implies that in a covered pond it is permitted) — nevertheless he did not explain the measure of the pond in how much it is not lacking trapping — end of his words Tosafos; behold they wrote explicitly that even in a covered pond regarding a bird a pond measure is required; whatever we say "bring the trap" — nevertheless it is forbidden; however in responsum of Mishna Berurah there he brought Ran's language (also Rabbenu Yeruham brought it here) — it appears as stated, and this is Ran's language: how is "requires trapping" defined? For example that which we say "bring the trap" etc.; and he wrote in his novellae: and birds that are not domesticated — it is proven in the Gemara that wherever they are in a covered place and have no place to exit from it they are considered trapped except sparrow — end of Ran's words; it appears like Mishna Berurah's words. And regarding Tosafos' question one can say Ran holds like Rashi's explanation above, and holds that this measure of "bring the trap" refers to an animal; but a bird suffices in a covered place.
