The order of Torah reading on Sukkot: We take out two Torah scrolls.
From the first we read "When an ox or lamb..." from Emor [Leviticus 22:27], and from the second we read the maftir from the section on mussaf sacrifices "On the fifth day of the seventh month"[Numbers 29:12]. The haftarah is from Zachariah "Behold a day is coming..." [14:1].':
