1) [Seif 1] Two Torah scrolls are taken out, etc. The practice of taking out two scrolls on every Yom Tov and reading the day's offering from the second is not found written in the Mishnah or Gemara. Rather, the Savoraic Rabbis instituted it, relying upon what we say at the end of Megillah 31b: Avraham said before the Holy One, blessed is He, "Master of the universe, perhaps my descendants will sin before You and You will do to them as You did to the generation of the Flood, etc." He said to him, "I have already established for them the order of the offerings. Whenever they read them, I consider it as though they offered an offering before Me." See there. Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, os 1. See above, siman 488, os 29.
