Seif 1: The eighth day of the festival, etc. This is the reading in Rashi, Tosafos, and all the texts, unlike Maharshal.
And we, etc. Regarding the festival offering, it serves as a compensatory day for the first day. Maseches Soferim, chapter 19, similarly uses this expression regarding the seventh day of Pesach, yet we do not follow that custom there, since it is merely a compensatory day, just like Chol HaMoed. All the more so here, where Shemini Atzeres is a festival unto itself. As for the statement that this expression is not found, Sukkah 48 speaks of “the final Yom Tov of the festival,” and the expression appears in many other places. Arachin and the end of Taanis likewise speak of “the eight days of the festival,” etc.
