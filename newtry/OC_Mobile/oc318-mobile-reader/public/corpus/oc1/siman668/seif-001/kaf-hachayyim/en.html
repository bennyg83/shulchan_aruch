1) [Seif 1] The night of the eighth day, etc. It is forbidden to establish a meal from Minchah time onward, as written above in siman 529, seif 1, in the gloss. One should not act like those who eat, drink, and become intoxicated on Hoshana Rabbah until they are unable to recite Kiddush at night. Magen Avraham, beginning of the siman. In siman 529, the Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 1 that on the first day of Yom Tov it is likewise forbidden, because it is the eve of the second day of Yom Tov. According to this, on Shemini Atzeres it is also forbidden before Simchas Torah from the ninth hour onward. Eshel Avraham, beginning of the siman. See our words there regarding the aforementioned gloss.
