1) [Seif 1] The night of the eighth day, etc. It is forbidden to establish a meal from Minchah time onward, as written above in siman 529, seif 1, in the gloss. One should not act like those who eat, drink, and become intoxicated on Hoshana Rabbah until they are unable to recite Kiddush at night. Magen Avraham, beginning of the siman. In siman 529, the Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 1 that on the first day of Yom Tov it is likewise forbidden, because it is the eve of the second day of Yom Tov. According to this, on Shemini Atzeres it is also forbidden before Simchas Torah from the ninth hour onward. Eshel Avraham, beginning of the siman. See our words there regarding the aforementioned gloss.
2) Ibid. This eighth day, the festival of Atzeres. Maharshal wrote in responsum 64 that one should say, 'Shemini Atzeres, the festival.' The Taz, s.k. 1, agreed. However, the Levush wrote in accordance with the words of the Shulchan Aruch, 'This eighth day, the festival of Atzeres'; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, os 1, wrote that it is proper to follow the Levush, for this is the primary view among the early and later halachic authorities; see there. So too wrote Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur regarding the aforementioned words of Maharshal: 'However, our custom is to say, This eighth day, the festival of Atzeres.' End quote. So too wrote Mateh Yehudah. So too is written in the siddur of Rav Yaavetz. So too wrote the Vilna Gaon in his commentary, because this is the textual reading in Rashi, Tosafos, and all the texts, unlike Maharshal. End quote. Sefer Bigdei Yesha likewise agreed with the words of the Shulchan Aruch; see there. See further below, os 4.
