The order of the prayer on the night and day of Shemini Atzeret. It contains 2 seifim: On the night of Shemini Atzeret one says in the prayer, 'And You gave us this eighth day, the festival of Atzeret.' {Rama: Our custom is not to say the word 'festival' regarding Shemini Atzeret, because we do not find it called a festival anywhere; rather, we say, 'the day of Shemini Atzeret.' [Minhagim]} In the Diaspora, one eats in the sukkah both at night and by day because of the doubt, but does not recite the blessing over sitting in the sukkah. Afterward, on the ninth day, one does not sit in the sukkah.
