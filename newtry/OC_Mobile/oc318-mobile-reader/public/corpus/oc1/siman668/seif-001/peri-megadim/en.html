In Yoreh Deah 220, se'if 20, that one who vows regarding 'the festival' is forbidden on Shemini Atzeres, there is no proof, for one can say that we follow the language of people. But there is proof from Sotah, folio 41, in the chapter Hakhel, that on the first day of Chol HaMoed Sukkos in the eighth year, meaning the end of shemittah and entering the first year from the beginning of shemittah; see there. See Eliyah Rabbah and Eliyahu Zuta. What is this, that it is not called Yom Tov? But the first day of Chol HaMoed for Bnei Eretz Yisrael is called Yom Tov, since Bnei Chutz LaAretz make it Yom Tov; see siman 652, letter 3. This works well in Sukkah at the beginning of Lulav HaGazul: there is no difference between the first Yom Tov and the second Yom Tov, and folio 38, first Yom Tov and second Yom Tov, meaning for Bnei Eretz Yisrael, Chol HaMoed is called Yom Tov; see Eliyah Rabbah. As a matter of law, everything depends on the custom. In Eliyahu Zuta and Eliyah Rabbah he wrote, like the Levush, to say, 'this day of the eighth, festival of Atzeres.' The Taz agreed with the Maharshal to say, 'this day of Shemini Atzeres, this festival.' But certainly one should not say a third formula, 'this festival day of Shemini Atzeres'; see the Levush. The Taz wrote that one should not say this, for if so it would imply that it is a festival fully in its own right, which is not so; rather, nevertheless it is subordinate to the main festival. Therefore he should say 'this day of Shemini Atzeres, this festival,' meaning the eighth day called Atzeres of this festival of Sukkos, or the eighth day, Atzeres of the festival, which is the eighth day, etc. According to the one who says 'this eighth day, festival of Atzeres,' it works better: the eighth day of the festival of Sukkos, which is called Atzeres. Nevertheless, one should not interrupt the language of the verse, 'on the eighth day Atzeres'; so too in the blessing, one should say the two words 'Shemini Atzeres' together. At first glance, there is no difference between 'Shemini Atzeres, this festival' or 'eighth, festival of Atzeres' regarding saying the word Atzeres without the letter hei at the beginning.
