(Beer HaGolah on daf 41.) In my humble opinion this requires further study in the simple reading. NB: In my humble opinion it may be reconciled per what Tosafot wrote on Megillah 36b s.v. veshe'ar etc. — see there. If so, one may say in their days they did not read from the second scroll; therefore they had to read from the festival theme 'tithe your tithe.' But after we accepted upon ourselves the obligation to establish the second scroll regarding the festival, there is no longer such obligation to read 'tithe your tithe.' Similarly one may answer Tosafot's question on Megillah 31b s.v. lemachar etc. regarding Rabbeinu Hai; per the above it may be reconciled; requires study.
