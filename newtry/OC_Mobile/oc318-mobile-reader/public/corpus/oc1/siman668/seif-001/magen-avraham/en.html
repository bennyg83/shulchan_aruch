It is forbidden to establish a meal from Minchah time and onward, as written in siman 529, unlike those who eat, drink, and become intoxicated on Hoshana Rabbah until they cannot recite Kiddush at night. Eighth, festival. The Maharshal wrote in a responsum to say, 'this day of Shemini Atzeres, this festival,' and the Levush wrote as the Beis Yosef wrote.
We eat in the sukkah. But we do not sleep in the sukkah, for granted, regarding eating there is recognition, since he does not recite the blessing; this is unlike sleeping, where there is no recognition; Mordechai. For this reason they hurry to leave after the morning meal; Maharil and Levush. There are those who do not sit in it at night and do sit in it by day, but this is not a custom; Tur. In our country there are some people who practice this way because they saw their fathers do so. The Beis Yosef wrote that their reason is that since at night they say Shehecheyanu over Shemini Atzeres, if they sit in the sukkah their sitting contradicts the saying of Shehecheyanu. This is difficult, for by day as well they say in the synagogue 'this day of Shemini Atzeres,' and nevertheless they sit. It appears to me that the reason is that we are concerned for bal tosif; therefore they interrupt one night, and afterward there is no concern, as written in siman 666 in the gloss. Nevertheless, the law is with the Tur, as the Beis Yosef wrote, for the Gemara said without qualification: the halachah is that we sit, and so all the poskim wrote without qualification, implying that there is no distinction between day and night. It is implied in all the poskim that one must eat all meals of that day in the sukkah, and even if a meal happens for him during bein hashmashos he must eat it there, for this is the reason that we forbid sukkah decorations on the ninth day. What Hagahos Minhagim wrote to somewhat justify the custom of the masses to eat only half the meal in the sukkah so that they pray for rain with a full heart, etc., see there, is not a proof; one can say that after eating they are exempt from there, as written above; see there. He himself also wrote that if it is Shabbos one must eat seudah shelishis in the sukkah, and so wrote the Bach in the name of the Mordechai, and this is the custom of the meticulous.
And we do not recite a blessing. Therefore one should not eat at night until it is dark; Hagahos Minhagim, Maharshal in Responsa, siman 68, Orchos Chaim, and Levush. Nevertheless, it appears to me that if he ate after the Maariv prayer while it was still day, he should not recite leishev basukkah, as written at the end of siman 30 regarding tefillin. Furthermore, he has already accepted Shemini Atzeres in his prayer and Kiddush.
