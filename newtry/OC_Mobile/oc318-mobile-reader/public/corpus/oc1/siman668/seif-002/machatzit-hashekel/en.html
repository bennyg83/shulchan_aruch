(S.k. 2) We eat, etc. And we do not sleep, etc.; for this reason they hurry, etc. That is to say, above in siman 659 it is explained that on Sukkos one must do all his affairs in the sukkah as he does throughout the year in his dwelling, unlike Shemini Atzeres, when after eating he goes to his house and does all his affairs there. Similarly, people who are stringent and do not eat even fruit or drink water outside the sukkah, on Shemini Atzeres should eat them in their homes, since one does not recite leishev basukkah over these things; they are similar to sleeping, where there is no recognition. Nevertheless, perhaps one can distinguish: since fruit and water do not require a sukkah at all except as a stringency, if he eats them in the sukkah on Shemini Atzeres, it does not appear as adding.
For this is the reason that we forbid, etc., as the Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of siman 667.
And what is written in Hagahos Minhagim, etc. Is not a proof. That is to say, Hagahos Minhagim brought a proof from Midrash Tanchuma, which wrote as follows: Why did the Sages permit one to exempt himself from the sukkah on the final Yom Tov of the festival? Because, etc., and on the final day they pray for rain; therefore they are exempt from the sukkah so that one will pray for rain with a full heart. End quote. Hagahos Minhagim understood that they are exempt in the middle of the meal. About this the Magen Avraham wrote that it is no proof, for one can say that they are exempt after the meal and do not do other things, such as strolling, learning, and the like, in the sukkah, even though on Sukkos they do these things in the sukkah.
