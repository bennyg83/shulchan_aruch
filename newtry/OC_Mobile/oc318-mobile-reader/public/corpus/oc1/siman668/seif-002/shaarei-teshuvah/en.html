The festival of Atzeres. See Baer Heitev. See the words of my grandfather in Bechor Shor, folio 115c, who brought proofs that Shemini Atzeres is called a festival, and the Birkei Yosef wrote that his words are good and correct. See there regarding his discussion of his words, of blessed memory. See Me'il Tzedakah, siman 8, who also wrote from the Yerushalmi of Moed Katan, 'I will turn your festivals,' etc., and from the Mishnah in Bikkurim, 'from Atzeres until the festival.' The Ran in Nedarim brings a Yerushalmi that in Torah language it is not called a festival, but in the language of people it is. Nevertheless, although prayers and blessings were founded on Scripture, still, for what is not in the wording of Scripture, the language of people is preferable in prayer; see there. See Eliyah Rabbah and Be'er Yaakov regarding what they wrote about this. See Birkei Yosef, who brought from Responsa Kenesses HaGedolah, part 2, Nezir HaKodesh, folio 481, and Beis Yaakov, simanim 78 and 142, that they hold like the Rama, that one should not say 'festival.' The Birkei Yosef challenged them from the words of all the early poskim, and so it is implied from Maseches Sofrim, chapter 19, halachah 3. See there in Shiyurei Berachah, where he found in the order of Rav Amram that it is written, 'this day of the eighth, festival of Atzeres.' See Birkei Yosef in the name of the Beis Yosef, that one who erred in the Maariv prayer of the night of Shemini Atzeres and said 'this festival of Sukkos' and uprooted his feet has fulfilled the obligation after the fact. The Birkei Yosef disagrees and brings proofs that he has not fulfilled the obligation and we send him back, for certainly one who says 'the festival of Sukkos' intended actual Sukkos, not Shemini Atzeres, even though we find that it is included in the festival of Sukkos. This is because also what he had said until now in the prayer, he certainly intended actual Sukkos; see there.
