Preparation. Magen Avraham wrote that specifically to arrange them is forbidden, but to set them in place is permitted because of kavod Yom Tov; other preparations are forbidden — likewise in Minhagim, Pesach laws, that one may not bring wine from Yom Tov for a friend. Also Maharil forbids searching for the Torah scroll from Shabbat to Yom Tov because of preparation. Magen Avraham — see there.
