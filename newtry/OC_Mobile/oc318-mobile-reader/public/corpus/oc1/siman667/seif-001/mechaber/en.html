The sukkah and its decorations are forbidden throughout Shemini Atzeret as well. It contains 1 seif: The sukkah and its decorations are forbidden on Shemini Atzeret. Outside Eretz Yisrael, where 2 days of Yom Tov are observed, they are also forbidden on the ninth day, because it may be the eighth. If Shabbos begins immediately after the final Yom Tov of the festival, the custom is not to make use of the sukkah decorations until after Shabbos; some permit it. {Rama: Some have the custom, when leaving the sukkah, to say, "May it be Your will that we merit to sit in the sukkah of Leviathan." [Kol Bo] It is forbidden to prepare on Yom Tov for the following night of Yom Tov. Therefore it is forbidden to set up the tables and benches in the house for the night, because this constitutes preparation. [Hagahot Maimoniyot and Maharil]}
