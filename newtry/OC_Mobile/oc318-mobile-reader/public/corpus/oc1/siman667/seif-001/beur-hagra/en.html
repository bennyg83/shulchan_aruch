Se'if 1, and if, etc. Yerushalmi, first chapter of Yom Tov, end of halachah 1: Rabbi Yirmeyah asked: what is the law of sukkah decorations? Rabbi Yirmeyah retracted and said: all seven days they are subordinate to the sukkah; from then onward they are in their preparation. Rabbi Yosi said: all seven days the sanctity of sukkah rests upon them; from then onward the sanctity of Yom Tov rests upon them. What did he come to permit? Yom Tov that falls on erev Shabbos, and according to the one who says that they are two sanctities. This is a scribal error, and it should read: what did he come to permit? Yom Tov that falls, etc. For above this it says: Rav Huna said in the name of Rav that the halachah follows the four elders. Rabbi Chanina asked: has Rav's approach changed? There, etc., leftover wick, etc. See the Rosh, first chapter of Yom Tov, se'if 1. However, he wrote there in the name of Rabbi Baruch that the Yerushalmi does not accept the concept of preparation, but for us, leftover wick is forbidden because of preparation. This is the reason of those who forbid here. But the Rosh holds that here preparation is not applicable. See what I wrote above at the end of siman 501, and see what I wrote above in siman 665.
