Magen Avraham, s.k. 3 To search out the Sefer Torah from Shabbos to Yom Tov. It implies that from Yom Tov to Shabbos it is permitted by means of an eruv tavshilin. See Magen Avraham, s.k. 28, se'if 2, in the name of the Ran, and what I wrote there in the margin. According to my reason there, here too, from Yom Tov to Shabbos it is permitted through an eruv tavshilin, because the permissive principle of 'since guests may happen to come' also applies, for they may want to learn the parashah that they will read on Shabbos by rolling the Sefer Torah. But according to the reason of the Magen Avraham there in the name of the Ran, that an eruv tavshilin permits only meal needs, if so, here it is forbidden to search out the Sefer Torah from Yom Tov to Shabbos. See what I wrote in the margin above, siman 302, on Magen Avraham, se'if 6.
