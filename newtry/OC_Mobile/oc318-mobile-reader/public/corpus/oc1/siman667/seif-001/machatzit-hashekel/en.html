(In s.k. 1) Also on the eighth. For if his meal happens, etc. He comes to resolve that although we hold that we say miggo that it was set aside during bein hashmashos because of the doubtful previous day, as written above at the beginning of siman 665, about this he wrote that here it is worse, because even during bein hashmashos he must perform an act with it, meaning that if a meal happens for him during bein hashmashos, he must eat in it. This is stricter; so wrote Tosafos and the Rosh.
And it requires investigation in siman 638. That is to say, the prohibition also rests upon the decorations.
Regarding wall decorations, see there in the Rama and in the Magen Avraham, s.k. 9.
