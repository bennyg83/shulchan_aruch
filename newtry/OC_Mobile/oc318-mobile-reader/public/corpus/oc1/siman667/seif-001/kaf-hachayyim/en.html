1) [Seif 1] The sukkah and its decorations are forbidden, etc. For the Name of Heaven rests upon them, as written above in siman 638, os 1; see there. This refers to a sukkah that fell, for otherwise it is in any event forbidden because of dismantling an ohel. Levushei Serad; Mishna Berurah in Biur Halachah. See above siman 638, seif 1, in the gloss, and our words there in os 11 and os 12.
