(19) Even wheat flour. Wheat is important; all the more so barley flour.
(20) One recites Shehakol. Whole wheat receives Borei Pri HaAdamah, but grinding changes it and prepares it to rise into bread. Before reaching that form it has left the category of fruit without reaching its normal mode of consumption.
(21) Very finely. Raw flour is not good food, so its blessing is certainly only Shehakol, for the slight benefit it provides. Even if ground only somewhat, its blessing remains Shehakol.
