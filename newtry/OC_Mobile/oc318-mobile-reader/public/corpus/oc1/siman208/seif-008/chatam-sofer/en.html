Seif 8 in the gloss. Note: Regarding tatraki, Turkish wheat, see Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 50. There he was asked concerning bread and cooked dishes made from the flour of the species of legumes known as kukuruz or Turkish wheat, whether the blessing is Shehakol or Borei Peri HaAdamah. The substance of my response was that if it were clear that it is a species of legume, then in my opinion the blessing would be Shehakol. However, although it is certainly not one of the five grain species, there remains room to doubt whether it is a species of rice, in which case its blessing would be Borei Minei Mezonos. He discusses there at length the law of bread made from rice and millet regarding blessings. Concerning the case at hand he concludes: Since I have no clear determination, and so forth, one should eat neither the Turkish wheat nor the tatraki except within a meal.
