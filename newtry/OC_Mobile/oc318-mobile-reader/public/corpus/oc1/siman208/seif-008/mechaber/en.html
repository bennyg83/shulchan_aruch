On bread of dochan and polizo or of other kinds of legumes one blesses shehakol and afterward borei nefashot.One who makes a dish from other kinds of legumes — if they remained whole and good when cooked like beans, one blesses borei peri haAdamah; and if they were completely crushed or are not good when cooked like beans, one blesses shehakol.[And so Beit Yosef explained the words of the Tur]:]:
