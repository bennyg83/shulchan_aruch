4) [Seif 2] The five grain species, etc. These are wheat, barley, spelt, oats, and rye. They too are significant because Eretz Yisrael was praised through them: spelt is a type of wheat, while oats and rye are types of barley. They possess a further virtue, because a person lives through them, and if one makes bread from them he recites HaMotzi. Therefore, even if he did not make bread but made a cooked dish, such as a pot-cooked food, etc., he recites Borei Minei Mezonos and afterward the single blessing that summarizes three. Tur; Taz, paragraph 2. What we call buckwheat, however, is not included among the five grain species. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1.
