If one ate grain raw, or made into parched kernels [kali] or boiled, with the kernels whole, he recites only "Borei Pri Ha'adamah," and after it "Borei Nefashos." {Rama: And this — that one recites "Borei Pri Ha'adamah" before it — refers to one who eats wheat and the like, which are fit to be eaten thus; but one who eats whole barley, even roasted in fire, they are fit to be eaten only with difficulty, and one recites before them only "Shehakol." [Kol Bo; and so it is implied from the words of the Rashba, for the fact that he equated wheat with barley for the after-blessing implies [only for the after-blessing] and not for the first blessing.]} And Tosafos were in doubt whether one should recite after it the after-blessing "Al Hamichyah"; therefore they wrote that it is proper not to eat it except within a meal, so that Bircas Hamazon will exempt it.
