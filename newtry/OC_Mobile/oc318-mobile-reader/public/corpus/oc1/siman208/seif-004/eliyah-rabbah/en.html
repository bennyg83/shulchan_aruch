[4] [Levush] Types of honey, etc. This is repeated above in siman 204, seif 12, but Shulchan Aruch did not repeat it here. It also appears that it is specifically regarding almonds, where there is a majority of the five grain species, that it is proper to be stringent. This does not apply where there is only a minority of grain. One may further say that since it is also commonly made for satiation, it is proper to be stringent.
