(4) [Levush] Types of honey, etc. Repeated above siman 204 seif 12, and in Shulchan Aruch they did not repeat it here. Also it appears specifically regarding almonds where there is a majority of the five species that it is good to be stringent, which is not so if there is a minority of grain; and further one may say that since it is customary to make it also for a meal, it is good to be stringent.
