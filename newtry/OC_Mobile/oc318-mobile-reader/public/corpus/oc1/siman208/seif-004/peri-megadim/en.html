See Taz, who writes that this applies whether the flour is the majority, etc. Bach, however, writes that even if it is the majority, when it is included only to bind the mixture it is subordinate. In lekach, however, the flour is primary. Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 2, writes similarly. See Chemed Moshe, who writes that lekach is pas habaah bekisnin. There is uncertainty whether pan kuchen is considered made merely to bind the mixture; see Eliyah Rabbah there. Levush, seif 3, implies that whether the mixture contains almonds or flour used to bind it, one should eat it during a meal because it is uncertain whether the flour serves only as a binder. See there. This will be discussed, with Hashem's help, in seif katan 5.
