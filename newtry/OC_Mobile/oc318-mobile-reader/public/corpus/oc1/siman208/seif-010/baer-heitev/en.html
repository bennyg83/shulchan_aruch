(1) Kind of C. The wording of the blessing in I Ama on the living and the economy and the yield of the field and on the good and wide greedy land that you wanted and gave to our ancestors to eat from its fruit and for a good week, O the Omnipresent, have mercy on us and on Israel your people and on Jerusalem your city and on the Zion of the tabernacle of your honor and on your altar and on your temple and build Jerusalem the holy city quickly in our days and we ascended into it and rejoiced in it because you, O God, are good and good to all and we thank you for the land and for the life, blessed are you, O God, for the land and the livelihood and the economy. And about fruits he says about the tree and the fruit of the tree and the produce of the field and the land and so on. And the wine, the vine, and the fruit of the vine, and the produce of the field, and the land, etc., and a seal on the land and the fruits in 28. But the custom to seal the land and the fruit of the vine:

The land. And in chutz la'aretz adjacent to Eretz Yisrael, one need not change the coin of the blessing from doubt lest the fruits be from Eretz Yisrael — and we are not concerned for this — R' Yonah, Magen Avraham. (And in Sefer Eliyahu Rabbah he ruled for practice that if they definitely come from Eretz Yisrael to chutz la'aretz, one blesses on its fruits; and if they definitely come from chutz la'aretz to Eretz Yisrael, one blesses on the fruits; but in a case of doubt, one always blesses on the fruits alone.)
