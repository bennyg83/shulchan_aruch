Accordingly, if one wishes to eat less than a kezayis, he should recite Shehakol; see there. Nehar Shalom, paragraph 3, however, writes that according to Tosafos and the halachic authorities, even regarding the initial blessing it is proper to avoid the doubt and eat it during a meal rather than recite Shehakol in a doubtful situation; see there. This is also implied by Mateh Yehudah's words cited above in paragraph 12; see there.
