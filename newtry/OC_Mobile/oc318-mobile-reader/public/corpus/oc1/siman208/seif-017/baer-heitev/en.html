Exempts him. See in Be'er HaGolah that he wrote to his view that it is forced that certainly deisa is more sustaining than dates, etc. — see there; and so Peri Chadash — that if he blessed shehakol on deisa he has fulfilled — see there. And Magen Avraham wrote that deisa is different from dates and wine, because it has other importance as bread — see there; and see in Ginnat Veradim klal 1 siman 48, and in Halaket vol. 2 siman 154, and in Sefer Avnei HaEzer — see there.

Exempts him. And likewise the al haMichya blessing exempts wine and dates — Lechem Rav, Ateret Zekenim.
