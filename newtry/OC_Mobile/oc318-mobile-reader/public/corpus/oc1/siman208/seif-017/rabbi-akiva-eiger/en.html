Seif 17. That if he ate porridge. This refers specifically to porridge, which has no connection to Birkas HaMazon, for even if he made a fixed meal over it he does not recite Birkas HaMazon. But pas haba'ah b'kisnin is complete bread, except that without establishing a meal on it the Sages did not require him to recite Birkas HaMazon. For according to the Torah, even with complete bread there is no obligation of Birkas HaMazon unless he ate to satiation; see Even HaOzer, siman 168, on this. In this case Birkas HaMazon exempts it, since in truth it is bread. So too wrote Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, rule 53.
