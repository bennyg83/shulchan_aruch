(74) That if one ate, etc. Outside a meal; during a meal, the three blessings of Birkas HaMazon certainly exempt them even before Birkas HaMazon.
(75) Porridge. The same applies to other dishes from the five grains. Although they nourish more than dates, grain has the further elevation of becoming bread, so the three blessings were assigned only to bread. Several later authorities reject Shulchan Aruch and rule that Birkas HaMazon does exempt porridge and all grain foods after the fact: since they nourish, they are no worse than dates. Certainly it exempts pas habaah bekisnin.
(76) But wine, etc. Even when drunk outside a meal, Birkas HaMazon after the fact exempts it because wine nourishes and sustains the heart like bread. See Biur Halachah.
(77) Over dates. Dates also nourish somewhat like grain, so Birkas HaMazon can apply after the fact. Other fruits, even of the seven species, are not exempted by Birkas HaMazon.
(78) Only the blessing HaZan, etc. Although it lacks all three facets, once he completed HaZan he cannot recite the remaining blessings, which were not initially instituted for wine or dates.
(79) If he remembered, etc. Initially he must certainly recite the proper blessing containing all three facets.
