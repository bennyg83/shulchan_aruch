(69) If after the fact, etc. The same applies if he drank wine explicitly intending to exempt grapes; he fulfilled it under siman 206:2 [later authorities]. See the Mishnah Berurah there. This differs from se'if 14, where he did not explicitly intend HaGefen to exempt grapes, so they are not automatically included; initially each receives its specific blessing.
(70) Over grapes, Borei Pri HaGefen, etc. If he recited Borei Pri HaEtz over wine, poskim dispute whether he fulfilled it. Doubt concerning blessings is treated leniently.
