The five species of grain. These are wheat, barley, spelt, oats, and rye, all of which are especially significant. The Tur writes: spelt is a species of wheat, while oats and rye are species of barley. They have an additional distinction, for human beings live upon them. Therefore, if one makes bread from them, he recites HaMotzi; if he cooks or crushes them, and so forth, the other laws stated here apply. See what I wrote in siman 168, seif 4, regarding spelt.
Even if he mixed with them, etc. With the five species of grain, because of their exceptional importance, we do not follow the majority whenever they were added as food. With other ingredients, however, the blessing follows the majority, as stated at the beginning of siman 208. See there. This is also implied in siman 208, seif 7: here, even if the grain does not impart a discernible taste, it retains its importance because it was added as food, for in a mixture containing honey one may perceive only the taste of the honey.
