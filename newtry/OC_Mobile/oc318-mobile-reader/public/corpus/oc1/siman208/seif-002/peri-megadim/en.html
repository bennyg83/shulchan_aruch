The five species of grain. See Taz. So writes the Tur. The phrase “and it appears to me” should read “meaning,” because this is a Gemara in Menachos. Taz refers to siman 168, seif katan 4. See also siman 211, seif katan 6. Both Taz and Magen Avraham, in siman 168, seif katan 7, and siman 211, seif katan 12, agree that spelt, oats, and rye are included in the wheat and barley mentioned in the verses praising Eretz Yisrael. Bircas HaMazon over bread made from them is D'Oraisa. According to the Tur, Me'ein Shalosh over porridge made from them is likewise D'Oraisa, while some hold that Me'ein Shalosh is D'Rabbanan even for the five species of grain. The order of precedence will be explained, with Hashem's help, in siman 211 in Taz and Magen Avraham, seif katan 13.
