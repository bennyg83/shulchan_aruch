Seif 9: If he mixed, etc. As stated above in seif 2, and as stated, “Anything containing,” etc. This applies all the more regarding Hamotzi. This is the meaning of “and if he made,” etc.
And if he made, etc. As stated in Challah, chapter 3, mishnah 7: “One who makes dough from wheat,” etc. Raavad writes that this applies only when there is a kezayis of grain within the quantity eaten in kedei achilas peras. Even according to Ramban and Rashba, who disagree and hold that this is unnecessary because the rice is drawn after the grain, that rule applies specifically to rice. See siman 453, seif 2, and what is stated there.
Specifically, etc., but, etc. None of the halachic quantities combine when consumed over more than kedei achilas peras. See above at the beginning of siman 210: “One who eats,” etc., and see Magen Avraham.
Within the time needed to eat. Meaning, specifically when he also eats it within kedei achilas peras.
Initially he recites Hamotzi. Here it is not necessary that there be a kezayis within kedei achilas peras, as stated, “Anything containing one of the five species,” etc., provided that the grain was not added merely as a binding agent, as stated above. As stated, “If he cooked it in a pot, he recites,” etc. The same applies regarding Hamotzi.
And afterward Al HaMichyah. This is a scribal error. It should read Borei Nefashos, as stated at the end: “If he cooked it,” etc. Why should this case differ? This is the correct ruling, for with respect to every after-blessing one requires a kezayis within kedei achilas peras.
And if he cooked it, etc., and afterward Borei Nefashos. See Rashi, 41b, s.v. pas habaah, etc., and “since,” etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. ella, who disagree with this and write, “This does not appear correct,” etc. Rabbeinu Yonah there, however, although he too explains like Tosafos, does not do so because of this difficulty, which implies that as a matter of law he does not disagree with Rashi.
