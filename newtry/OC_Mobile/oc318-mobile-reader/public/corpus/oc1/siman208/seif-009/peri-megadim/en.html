But. See Taz. Beit Yosef cites the language of the Rif in Berachos 37a. The Rif writes that Rav and Shmuel's rule, “Anything containing one of the five species is Borei Minei Mezonos,” was not refuted with respect to grain, but the same was not established regarding rice. Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchos Berachos, halachah 10, discusses rice by itself. The Rif may have written this lest one think that once Rav and Shmuel were refuted regarding the statement that rice nourishes, rice no longer possesses any of the special status of the five species even when it is the minority. He therefore teaches that the rule “anything containing” was not refuted regarding the five species, whereas rice has such status only when it is the majority. This is also the view of Magen Avraham, seif katan 11; see there. See Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchos Berachos, halachah 4, who first cites the rule, “anything that is one of the five species of grain,” meaning where the majority is grain, and afterward cites the separate rule, “anything containing one of the five species of grain,” even when only a small amount is present. Why did he need the first rule? At first glance, it serves to exclude rice, which would not receive this status even when it is the majority, though this is strained. Taz writes that according to the opinion that one should recite Shehakol over rice, the distinction has no practical consequence. He means that the poskim are uncertain whether orez is rice and dochan is millet, or the reverse, and therefore rule that one should recite Shehakol over both. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 9. There is no practical consequence because Shehakol covers everything. The special importance of Borei Minei Mezonos may have been accorded to rice only when it stands entirely by itself, not when mixed with even a small amount of another species. However, where the blessing is Borei Peri HaAdamah because it remains whole, or Shehakol when mixed with fruit, its majority is not nullified, for why should rice be inferior to other foods? Its loss of status applies only regarding Borei Minei Mezonos, because it is not one of the seven species and therefore becomes subordinate through mixture, as explained.
