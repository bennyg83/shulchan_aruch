For eating and for chewing it. It implies that if it is not fit for chewing it — even if it is thick — one blesses shehakol; and afterward he says if it is fit for drinking, etc. — it implies that if it is thick one blesses borei minei mezonot; and so is primary, and so is implied in the Gemara that on thick one blesses borei minei mezonot — Magen Avraham; and see further in Magen Avraham siman 205 seif kaf vav.
