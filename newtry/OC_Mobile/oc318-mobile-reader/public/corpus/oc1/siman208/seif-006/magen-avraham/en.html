For eating and chewing. This implies that if it is not fit to be chewed, even if it is thick, one recites Shehakol. It then states that if it is fit to drink, and so forth, implying that if it is thick one recites Borei Minei Mezonos. The latter is the principal ruling. This is also implied by the Gemara, which states that over a thick mixture one recites Borei Minei Mezonos, by Rashi’s explanation regarding cooked porridge, and by Agudah.
