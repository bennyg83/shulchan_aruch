6) There: If he cooked or crushed them. This implies that the first clause concerns cooking without crushing. The statement in seif 4 that one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah over whole cooked kernels may refer to a case here in which he cooked them until they became mashed. This is also implied by Levush, seif 4, and below, seif 7; see there. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. Yaavetz, however, writes that the Shulchan Aruch's word meaning 'cooked them' is a scribal error and should read 'split them.' This is both a common error and an evident one, since the entire seif follows the Rambam in chapter 3, whose text reads, 'split or crushed them,' etc.; see there. Beis David, siman 532, writes the same. Regarding practical halachah, however, there is no difference: whether he split them or cooked them until mashed, he recites Borei Minei Mezonos, as stated in Beis Yosef. If they remain whole, he recites Borei Pri HaAdamah, as stated in seif 4.
