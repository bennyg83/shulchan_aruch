1) [Seif 1] Over the five species, etc. Because of their significance, since Eretz Yisrael was praised through them, the Sages established a distinct blessing for them. Tur; Taz, paragraph 1.
2) There: An olive and a date. The honey mentioned in the verse is date honey. The verse therefore means a land containing dates from which honey is made. See, however, Rashi on Sukkah 6a, who writes that all sweet things are called honey and dates are sweet. According to this, the honey mentioned in the verse refers to the dates themselves, which are called honey because of their sweetness.
3) There: One recites after them, etc. If one ate the requisite measure of fruit for a concluding blessing and immediately after eating, when it entered his stomach, vomited all of it, he does not recite the concluding blessing. This is no better than food that has become digested, over which one does not recite the blessing. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1. Divrei Menachem, Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 1, writes the same and challenges Kol Eliyahu, volume 1, siman 9; see there. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 51, paragraph 15.
