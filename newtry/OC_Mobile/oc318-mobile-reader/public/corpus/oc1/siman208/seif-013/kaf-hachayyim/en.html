13) Regarding lekach, the flour is primary. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 4. The Shelah writes that in pancakes the flour is considered a binder, but Tashbetz, siman 322, writes that one recites Borei Minei Mezonos and afterward the blessing that summarizes three. Eliyah Rabbah there.
