Seif 13. That they too are included in the blessing of Al ha'etz. This is specifically apples themselves. But if he squeezed them and drank their liquid, or even cooked them in water, according to the Rashba above, siman 202, seif 10, it is not exempted by the blessing of Al ha'etz, for it is not fruit at all; Magen Avraham above, siman 202, seif katan 25.
