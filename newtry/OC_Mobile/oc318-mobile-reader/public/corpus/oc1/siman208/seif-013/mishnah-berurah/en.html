(62) For they too, etc. Otherwise an abridged three-faceted blessing does not exempt Borei Nefashos, nor the reverse [later authorities].
(63) They are tree fruit. Because they are less important, no such blessing was separately instituted; when it is already being recited, it exempts them. This applies to apples themselves. Their squeezed juice is not fruit and is not exempted by Al HaEtz.
(64) And he drank wine. Even according to those who conclude the wine blessing 'for the land and fruits,' its opening mentions only vine, so apples are not included [Magen Avraham]. Some later authorities suggest that if he actually concluded 'for the land and fruits,' apples may be included. Therefore, when eating apples and drinking wine, first recite Borei Nefashos on the apples or conclude the wine blessing 'for the land and fruit of the vine.'
(65) All the more so if he ate meat, etc. Wine and apples are at least both tree products; how much more so something not from a tree.
(66) The same applies if, etc. See siman 212 concerning primary and subordinate foods and our comments there.
