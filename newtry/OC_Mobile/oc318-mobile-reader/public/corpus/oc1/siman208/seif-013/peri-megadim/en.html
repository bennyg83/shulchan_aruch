In the blessing. See Taz. This blessing contains the substance of four blessings: “for the sustenance” corresponds to HaZan; “for the land” corresponds to Bircas HaAretz; “Have mercy” corresponds to Boneh Yerushalayim; and “for You are good and do good” corresponds to HaTov VeHaMeitiv. It is called Me’ein Shalosh because the first three are Torah-level. See Tur and Levush. If one omitted mention of the Land, he must repeat the blessing. See Levush at length regarding the wording of the blessing. With Hashem’s help, see what I write about this in letter 18.
