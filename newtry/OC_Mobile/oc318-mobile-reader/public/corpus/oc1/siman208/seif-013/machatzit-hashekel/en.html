(sk 13) They were mashed, etc. But when one mashes them in a spoon, the primary way of eating them is thus — meaning, and therefore one blesses borei peri haAdamah.

And furthermore, the Mishnah stands, etc. And it deals with Mishnah Berurah seif 6 — meaning, that it is written there: even if they were crushed much, if their form was not lost and the form of the fruit is still upon it — in its language it stands — to bless its blessing as if it were not mashed.
