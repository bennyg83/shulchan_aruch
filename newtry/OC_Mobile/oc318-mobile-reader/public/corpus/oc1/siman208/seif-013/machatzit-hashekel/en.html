(Seif katan 13) They became mashed, etc. But when one mashes them with a spoon, this is their normal manner of eating. Accordingly, one recites Borei Pri Ha’adamah.
Furthermore, their substance remains intact, etc. See siman 202, seif 6. There it is stated that even if fruit was crushed extensively, if its form was not lost and it still retains the appearance of fruit, its original blessing remains and one blesses as though it had not been mashed.
