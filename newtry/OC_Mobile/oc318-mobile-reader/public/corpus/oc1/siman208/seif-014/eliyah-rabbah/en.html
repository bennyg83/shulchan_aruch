(14) [Levush] Ki Atah Heaven tov u'metiv, etc. Lechem Chamudot and Sheirei Kenesset HaGedolah wrote that Levush did not have the text "u'metiv lakol"; and I say: since Levush afterward wrote "and some say: after he said Ki Atah Heaven tov u'metiv lakol" — evidently he had this text, except that here the word lakol fell from the printing; and so it implies in seif 12.
