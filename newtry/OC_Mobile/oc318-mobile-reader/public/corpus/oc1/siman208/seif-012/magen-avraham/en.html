Mention of the event. One says v'samachnu bah, ratzah v'hachalitzanu beyom haShabbat hazeh; and on Yom Tov he says v'zachreinu letovah beyom chag peloni hazeh; and likewise on Rosh Chodesh. And if he did not mention it, he has fulfilled — see in Tur, see siman 188.

On the land, etc. And it is not concluding in two, for the land brings out all of them.

And upon the fruit of the vine. And in Mordekhai it is written to conclude on the land and on the sustenance and on the fruits; and so too Agudah; and so from what is written in seif 10 and seif 11.
