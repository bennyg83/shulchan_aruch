(57) It is mentioned in it. Whether the blessing is for grain foods, wine, or fruits of the land.
(58) A reflection of the occasion. Before 'for You are good and do good,' on Shabbos say, 'Favor us and strengthen us on this Shabbos day'; on Yom Tov, 'Remember us favorably on this festival of ...'; and on Rosh Chodesh, 'Remember us favorably on this Rosh Chodesh.' Omission does not invalidate after the fact.
(59) But not, etc. Even Birkas HaMazon's mention is legally only customary, while no custom exists here [Gra].
(60) He should place sustenance first. Its blessing, Mezonos, is important and specific, and grain precedes in the verse. Wine follows, also important and possessing a specific blessing.
(61) And fruit of the vine and the fruits. Magen Avraham inclines to say only 'and the fruits'; the Gra also implies this. Popular custom follows Shulchan Aruch. This does not create an unnecessary blessing because no Divine Name is added, so mentioning vine and fruits is unobjectionable. One who omitted fruit of the vine certainly does not repeat [Chayei Adam].
