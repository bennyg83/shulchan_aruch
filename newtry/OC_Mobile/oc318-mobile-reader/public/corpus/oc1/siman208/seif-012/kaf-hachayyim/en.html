12) There: Only to bind it, etc. Bach writes that whenever flour is included as a binder, even if the flour is the majority and other items are the minority, one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah, Borei Pri HaEtz, or Shehakol, according to the blessing of those other species; see there. Olas Tamid at the end of paragraph 2 writes the same. Taz, paragraph 4, however, writes that one should not rule leniently in this manner, since in any event he derives substantial enjoyment from the large amount of flour; see there. Mateh Yehudah, paragraph 1, similarly writes that using flour as a binder negates only its significance, not the grain's majority. He writes that the same applies to the law in seif 3, which refers only to almonds constituting the majority. Therefore, if flour is added as a binder, one recites only the blessing over the almonds. The uncertainty there is that perhaps the flour was added for nourishment, contrary to Taz, paragraph 5; see there. Therefore, if most of a mixture is grain added as a binder, one should eat it specifically during a meal, or recite Borei Minei Mezonos over another item and the blessing appropriate to the other species over another item, intending to cover this mixture whichever ruling is correct, thereby avoiding doubt.
