(12) They were completely mashed, etc. Specifically when they were mashed through a perforated utensil that they are very thin, or that he made a dish from flour of legumes, as Lechem Chamudot and Shelah wrote; but when he mashed it in a spoon, one blesses borei peri haAdamah — end of Magen Avraham's words. I wonder at Shelah daf 92, who wrote that on dik'i arbizan one blesses shehakol; and for practical halachah the words of Magen Avraham appear correct, and so it implies in Olat Tamid — study is required.
