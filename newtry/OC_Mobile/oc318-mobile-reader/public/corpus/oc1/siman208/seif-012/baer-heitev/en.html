Me'ein haMe'orei. That is, before he says "Ki Atah Tov u'Metiv." He says "V'samachnu bah, ratzah v'hachalitzanu beyom haShabbat hazeh"; and on Yom Tov he says "V'zachreinu letovah beyom chag peloni hazeh"; and likewise on Rosh Chodesh. And if he did not mention it, he has fulfilled — Magen Avraham.

The fruits. And in Mordekhai he wrote to conclude on the land and on the living and on the fruits — and so Agudah, and so it appears to me — Magen Avraham.
