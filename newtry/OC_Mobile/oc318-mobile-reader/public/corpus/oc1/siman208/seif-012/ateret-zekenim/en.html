One mentions in it me'ein [haMe'orei], etc. — that is, before he says "Ki Atah Tov u'Metiv," on Shabbat he says "Vehe'elinu letokha v'samachnu bevinah, ratzah v'hachalitzanu beyom haShabbat hazeh, Ki Atah Hu Heaven Tov u'Metiv," etc.; on Yom Tov he says "V'samachnu bah, Zachreinu letovah beyom chag peloni hazeh"; and on Rosh Chodesh he says "Zachreinu letovah beyom Rosh Chodesh hazeh" (Beit Yosef in the name of Teshuvat HaTashbetz).
