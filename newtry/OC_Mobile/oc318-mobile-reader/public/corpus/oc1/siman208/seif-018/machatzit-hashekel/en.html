(siman katan 18) Me'ein, etc. See in Tur — meaning that the Beit Yosef brought many poskim that in this time one should not mention mention of the event; if so we hold like the poskim who mention mention of the event — l'chatchila — but b'dieved we rely on the poskim who need not mention mention of the event; but for Chanukah and Purim, for all one need not mention mention of the event, as written in Shulchan Aruch. And the reason — Levush wrote — since miracles are thanksgiving, it is fit to mention them in thanksgiving — that is, in prayer in Modim "v'al kulam," and in Birkat HaMazon "v'al hakol," where thanks is mentioned — unlike one blessing me'ein shalosh, which has no thanksgiving that it is fit to adjoin thanks for miracles except in "v'noda lecha al ha'aretz," etc., and there it is not fit to interrupt so there would be a mention of the event adjacent to the conclusion — and Lechem Chamudot chapter 48 siman katan 135 wrote on him that it is forced. And Lechem Chamudot wrote the reason — since we do not find to mention mention of the event except in Yerushalmi, and there Chanukah and Purim were not mentioned — possibly the reason, since they were not mentioned in the Torah as of now.
