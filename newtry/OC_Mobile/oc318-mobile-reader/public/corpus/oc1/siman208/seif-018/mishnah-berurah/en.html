(80) And an item whose blessing, etc. He has no wine; if he did, he could resolve the doubt by blessing HaGefen over wine and Borei Nefashos over another species. He wishes to take another item with an abridged three-faceted blessing and include 'for the vine' in it. This is forbidden because perhaps the doubtful beverage requires Borei Nefashos, making mention of vine irrelevant; better omit it. If he has something requiring Borei Nefashos, he should bless over it and repair what he can.
(81) And include with it, etc. If one ate tree fruit but does not know whether it is among the seven species, and lacks another seven-species fruit, Magen Avraham permits taking wine or a food from the five grains, whose blessing is also three-faceted, and adding 'for the tree and its fruit.' Either way this wording applies, even if the doubtful fruit is not one of the seven, as in se'if 13.
(82) Because of doubt. Taz and several later authorities hold this rationale applies only initially: one should not drink the doubtful beverage planning to rely on a later addition to another blessing. After the fact, once he drank it, it is better to include the doubtful phrase than leave it with no after-blessing at all.
