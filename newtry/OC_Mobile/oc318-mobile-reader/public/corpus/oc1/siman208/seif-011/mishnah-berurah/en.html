(55) He does not conclude, etc. Just as grain concludes 'for the land and sustenance,' and tree fruit 'for the land and fruits,' wine should mention land in its conclusion. Magen Avraham says omission of land does not invalidate after the fact.
(56) For the land and fruit of the vine, etc. Some Rishonim require wine's distinction in the conclusion as in the opening, while others say a general 'for the fruits' suffices and its text should not differ from other blessings. Shulchan Aruch leaves either choice. Later authorities imply custom concludes 'for the land and fruit of the vine.' One who concluded 'for the land and fruits' certainly fulfilled it.
