(9) One recites Borei Minei Mezonos. Even when grain is the minority, flour from the five species makes the almonds subordinate.
(10) He does not recite Borei Minei Mezonos. The flour is subordinate to the fruit, as above in se'if 2.
(11) It is proper to be stringent. Almond dishes are ordinarily also made to nourish, so it is difficult to determine whether his intent was only binding; therefore stringency is proper.
(12) And to swallow it. Meaning eat it: it was thick and food-like. If thin and fit only for drinking, even outside a meal and intended to nourish, one recites only Shehakol, as below in se'if 6.
(13) And he is exempt from it. The text should read mima nafshach. Later authorities ask: if the flour only binds, it is subordinate to the almonds, and fruit normally requires a blessing during a meal. Since the patient needs the almonds and initially bases his meal upon them, however, this resembles establishing a meal on fruit, which receives no separate blessing, as above in siman 177:3.
