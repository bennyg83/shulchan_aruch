3) There: One recites after them, etc. If one ate the requisite measure of fruit for a concluding blessing and immediately after eating, when it entered his stomach, vomited all of it, he does not recite the concluding blessing. This is no better than food that has become digested, over which one does not recite the blessing. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1. Divrei Menachem, Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 1, writes the same and challenges Kol Eliyahu, volume 1, siman 9; see there. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 51, paragraph 15.
