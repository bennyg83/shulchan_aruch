14) [Seif 3] When flour is added, etc., one recites Borei Minei Mezonos. Afterward, if there is a kezayis of flour within the time required to eat a pras and he ate the amount of a pras, he recites the blessing that summarizes three. Otherwise, he recites Borei Nefashos, as stated above in paragraph 9; see there.
15) There: If it is merely to bind, etc. According to Bach, even if flour is the majority it is called a binder. According to Mateh Yehudah, this applies specifically when it is the minority, as stated above in paragraph 12; see there.
16) There: It is proper to act stringently and swallow it, etc. This applies specifically if it is thick and must be chewed in the mouth. If it is so thin that it is fit to drink, in any event one recites only Shehakol and afterward Borei Nefashos, as stated below in seif 6.
17) There: It is proper to act stringently, etc. Taz, paragraph 5, writes that the uncertainty concerns the concluding blessing. Accordingly, if one wishes to eat less than a kezayis, he should recite Shehakol; see there. Nehar Shalom, paragraph 3, however, writes that according to Tosafos and the halachic authorities, even regarding the initial blessing it is proper to avoid the doubt and eat it during a meal rather than recite Shehakol in a doubtful situation; see there. This is also implied by Mateh Yehudah's words cited above in paragraph 12; see there.
18) There: And swallow it during a meal, etc. Alternatively, he should first recite a blessing over pas habaah bekisnin and over something whose blessing is Borei Pri HaEtz, thereby covering this mixture whichever ruling is correct, as stated above in paragraph 12; see there.
