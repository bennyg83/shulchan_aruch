Even. See Taz. Magen Avraham in siman 204, seif katan 25, implies that with the five species the taste must be perceptible. See also Magen Avraham here, seif katan 15. Bach likewise writes that the grain must be included specifically for its taste, and Levush appears to agree. See Perishah, who distinguishes between this case and seif 9. There, where flour is mixed with flour, its taste is not perceptible, and when there is not a kezayis within kedei achilas pras one does not recite Bircas HaMazon. Here, its taste is perceptible initially, even if one later added a large quantity of flour from other species. This is unlike Taz. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 3, and Peri Chadash, Responsa, siman 8. Although the honey is the primary object of eating, if the semolina flavors and improves it, one recites Borei Minei Mezonos. See Chiddushei HaRashba, Berachos 36b, who writes that the honey is primary. In siman 204, Taz seif katan 16 and Magen Avraham seif katan 25, we discussed this at length.
