(71) And water. The same applies to every other beverage, even the most important.
(72) He need not bless, etc. This concerns one who established himself over wine, which exempts drinks even absent during the blessing, or drinks present during the blessing, which are exempt even after only one cup. If he neither established himself nor had the drinks present, he must bless afterward as well as before, as above in siman 174, Mishnah Berurah paragraph 3.
(73) For the wine blessing exempts them, etc. Even if drinks arrived only after he changed his mind and diverted attention from wine, requiring a new first blessing on them, they need no Borei Nefashos. Since he initially established himself over wine, all drinks are subordinate and covered by its after-blessing.
