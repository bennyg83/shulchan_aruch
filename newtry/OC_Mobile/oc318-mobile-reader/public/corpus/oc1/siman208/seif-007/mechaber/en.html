One who chews {i.e., eats} rice recites upon it "Borei Pri Ha'adamah," and after it "Borei Nefashos." And if he cooked it {Rama: until it became mashed [Beis Yosef in the name of the Rosh and Rabbeinu Yonah]}, or ground it and made bread from it, he recites upon it "Borei Minei Mezonos," and after it "Borei Nefashos" — provided that it is not mixed with anything else, but is rice alone. And if he mixed some of it into another cooked dish, and the other dish is the majority, he recites upon it the blessing of that dish.
