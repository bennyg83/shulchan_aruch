(7) [Levush] And to chew it, etc. The wording of Shulchan Aruch: "and to chew it" — in Semak, and so in Rambam chapter 3 of Hilchot Berachot; except Magen Avraham challenged the beginning against the end, that it implies specifically when it is fit for drinking, but if it is thick even though it is not fit to chew, one blesses shehakol — see there. And perhaps because of Beit Yosef, Levush changed and wrote "to chew it," which is from the language of chewing; and so all other poskim wrote only: whatever is fit for eating, one blesses borei minei mezonot. Again I found in Abudraham daf 130 in the wording of Levush: "to chew it."
