Magen Avraham, seif katan 22. To go back and recite Borei pri hagefen. I am astonished at this, for the name of fruit is upon it, since one recites Borei pri hagefen. If so, he properly recited the blessing of fruit of the tree, except that he did not clarify which tree; if so, this should not be worse than if he recited shehakol, where he has fulfilled the obligation.
