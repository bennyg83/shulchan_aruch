(Note 22) If he erred, etc. — and see in Eben HaEzer who disagrees on this; and in the aforementioned kuntres I wrote to settle the proof of Bach. And see in Leket Tov vol. 2 siman 149, who rules that whether he blessed borei peri haEtz on wine or borei peri haAdamah on bread, he has not fulfilled — and the reason is that since every species and species has its general blessing except bread and wine, which were singled out to learn a new matter, that they established for them a separate blessing because of their importance — you cannot return them to the general category of other fruits, for it is called a change from the coin that the Sages stamped in particular and removed from the general category — end of his words — see there. And the proof of R' Yehuda HaLevi from Tosafot daf 12, who explained in Eben HaEzer in another way — the plain meaning of the matters shows like Mahar"i HaLevi. And see in Penei Yehoshua daf 40 and in Peri Megadim vol. 1 siman 59 and in responsa Ginat Veradim general 1 siman 19, who also hold that b'dieved he has fulfilled; and I expanded on this — I wrote that to remove himself from doubt he should say 424 in borei peri haGefen and he fulfills according to all — see there.
