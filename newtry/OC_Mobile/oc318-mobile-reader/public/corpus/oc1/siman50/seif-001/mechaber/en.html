The reason they say the mishnah Eizehu Mekoman. And in it one seif: They
established to recite after the section of the daily offering the chapter Eizehu Mekoman and the baraita of Rabbi Yishmael, so that every person should merit to study Scripture, Mishnah, and Gemara every day; for the baraita of Rabbi Yishmael counts in place of Gemara, since midrash is like Gemara.
