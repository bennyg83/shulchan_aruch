(Seif katan 1) Eizehu, etc. “But this is incorrect.” See siman 554, seif 4. Meaning, it is explained there that even on Tisha B'Av, when Torah study is forbidden, the entire established daily order is permitted. The same rule applies to a mourner. However, in siman 559, seif 4, Rama writes that Pittum HaKetores and the other passages concerning the offerings are not recited on Tisha B'Av, because they are not considered part of the fixed daily order, since not every individual recites them. Accordingly, the same should apply in a mourner's house.
