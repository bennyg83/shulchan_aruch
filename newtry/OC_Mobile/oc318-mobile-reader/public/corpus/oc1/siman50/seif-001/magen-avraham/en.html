Eizehu Mekoman — some places were accustomed not to recite it in a house of mourning, and this is incorrect (Knesset HaGedolah); and see siman 554, seif 4.

To study every day — it seems to me specifically in their time they understood the language of the Aramaic translation, but now that they do not understand they need to study to understand, for otherwise it is not considered study. Even in prayer, it is better to pray in a language one understands, as written in siman 101. Nevertheless, one could say that even though he does not understand, the Holy One, blessed be He, knows his intent and understands; but if he recites the mishnah and does not understand, it is not called study; therefore one must study the commentary.
