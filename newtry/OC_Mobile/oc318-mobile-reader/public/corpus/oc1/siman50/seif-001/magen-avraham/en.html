Eizehu Mekoman. Some places have the custom not to recite it in a mourner's house, but this is incorrect. Knesses HaGedolah. See siman 554, seif 4.
To study every day. It appears to me that this applied specifically in their time, when people understood Aramaic. Nowadays, when people do not understand it, they must study its meaning. Otherwise, it is not considered Torah study. Even in prayer it is preferable to pray in a language one understands, as stated at the end of siman 101. Nevertheless, one may say that even when the worshipper does not understand, the Holy One, blessed is He, knows and understands his intention. If, however, one recites a Mishnah without understanding it, this is not called Torah study. One must therefore study its explanation.
