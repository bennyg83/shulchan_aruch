SA 1 — they established, etc. Tosafos Kiddushin 31 and Avodah Zarah 19b; they chose these because they concern the sacrifices, and all the sacrifices are included there, and it is a halachah given clearly to Moses at Sinai; and the baraita of Rabbi Yishmael — because it is the beginning of Torat Kohanim, which is the head of the entire section of sacrifices.
