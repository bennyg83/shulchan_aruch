The custom is that the shaliach tzibbur says, etc. In the glosses to Derashos Maharil there it is written that the leading authority of each city says, "By authority of the Heavenly Court," etc., and he wrote that this is indeed the common practice, see there. The Bach, Taz, s.k. 1, and Magen Avraham, s.k. 1, cited his words. The Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur, sec. 4, wrote that the custom is for the leading authority of the city or the leader of the congregation to say it. Accordingly, each place should follow its custom.
