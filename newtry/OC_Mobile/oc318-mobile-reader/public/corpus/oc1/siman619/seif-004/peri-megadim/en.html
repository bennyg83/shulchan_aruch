When Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos, we do not accept Shabbos, but we do say 'Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbos,' Eliyah Rabbah 18 in the name of Sheyarei Kenesses HaGedolah. This is proper, as when Yom Tov falls on Shabbos, and Shabbos is more severe than Yom Kippur, involving stoning, etc. It seems to me that the world does not practice this way, and I do not know anything clear about this.
