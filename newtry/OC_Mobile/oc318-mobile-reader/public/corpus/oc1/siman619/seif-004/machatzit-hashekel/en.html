(Se'if katan 4) Without a cup, etc. See siman 559, Magen Avraham, se'if katan 9.
See siman 18. Meaning, regarding a tallis designated for daytime use, if he wears it at night, whether he recites the blessing over it is a dispute between the Rambam and the Rosh. Therefore, to satisfy everyone, the Rema wrote there that on the night of Yom Kippur one should wrap himself in the tallis by day and recite the blessing over it. For the reason we wrap ourselves in a tallis on the night of Yom Kippur, see there in the Magen Avraham.
