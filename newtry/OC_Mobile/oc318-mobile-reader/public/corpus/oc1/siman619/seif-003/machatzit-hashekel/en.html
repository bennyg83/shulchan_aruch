(Se'if katan 3) He says, etc. As written in siman 298, se'if 14: They were sitting in the study hall on Motza'ei Shabbos and fire was brought before them. One recites for all of them the blessing 'Borei Me'orei HaEish,' and they are silent and intend to fulfill their obligation with his blessing. Although through this they are interrupted from words of Torah, nevertheless it is better than each person reciting the blessing for himself, because 'in the multitude of people is the King's glory.' The same applies here.
Therefore he should recite the blessing for himself. The Eliyah Rabbah concludes: He should hurry to finish before the shaliach tzibbur and answer amen to the blessings of the shaliach tzibbur, as above, siman 59, se'if 4.
