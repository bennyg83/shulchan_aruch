If it falls on Shabbos, one says "Vayechulu" and the single blessing "Me'ein Sheva," and concludes "mekadesh haShabbos," and does not mention [the theme] of Yom Kippur. {And we do not say "Avinu Malkeinu" on Shabbos; but the other selichos and supplications one says as on a weekday (Rivash, siman 512; Minhagim).}
