Se'if 3, He says Vayechulu. As written in chapter 16 of Shabbos; see there.
And one does not say Avinu Malkeinu, etc.; but, etc. This is from the Yerushalmi mentioned above, which is written in siman 602. But the selichos, since they are in the middle of the blessing and when it falls during the week we say them, are considered part of the blessing formula. This is unlike Avinu Malkeinu. There.
