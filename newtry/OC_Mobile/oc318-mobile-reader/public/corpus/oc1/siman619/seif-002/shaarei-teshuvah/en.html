The transgressors. See Ba'er Heitev. See Sheyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, who wrote that he heard from our teacher Rabbi Shlomo Saba, of blessed memory, that he would say 'the transgressors,' and the intent is that we release them from their status as transgressors in order to pray with them. This version is correct, and so I have the custom to say. The Bach wrote that we should say only, 'in the earthly yeshivah we permit,' but this is not the custom. See Eliyah Rabbah, who explains that the meaning is that in the yeshivah they agree with us about the release, unlike the Magen Avraham, who brought the words of the Bach on our version; see there. According to this, what the Taz wrote, that the shaliach tzibbur alone is effective, also appears not to be so, because the three are the ones who permit by the authority of the Omnipresent and by the authority of the congregation, meaning with their agreement, but not as agents to permit. It also appears that agency does not help for an individual in such a case. I have shortened.
