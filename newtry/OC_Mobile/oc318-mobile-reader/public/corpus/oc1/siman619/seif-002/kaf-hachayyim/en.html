2) Ibid. The night of Yom Kippur, etc. The custom is to wrap oneself in a tallis while it is still day and recite the blessing over it. Levush; Bach; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur. There are places where the custom is not to wrap oneself in a tallis. Levush; Bach; Ateres Zekeinim. If he forgot until night, he should not recite the blessing, for we do not rule that a daytime garment is obligated even at night. Levush; Yad Aharon, ibid.; Eshel Avraham, sec. 4. Likewise, if it is bein hashmashos, he should not recite the blessing. Mateh Ephraim, sec. 7. See further above siman 18, seif 1 in the gloss, and our words there, with the help of Heaven. A fringed tallis that is decorated with gold may be worn on Yom Kippur. Ritva, Rosh Hashana; Pischei Olam, sec. 1.
