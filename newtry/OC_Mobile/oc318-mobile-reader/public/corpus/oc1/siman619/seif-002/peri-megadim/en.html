By the authority of the Omnipresent. See the Taz. The Bach, of blessed memory, explained 'by the authority of the Omnipresent' to mean that the transgressors should not think they are already exempt from their punishment. The Taz explained as written in letter 1, to inform that the entire congregation agrees to permit praying with them, and what it says 'by the authority of the Omnipresent' means that Heaven too should agree. What was written in explanation of Kol Nidrei, see the Tur at length. We practice like Rabbeinu Tam, that it is a stipulation from this Yom Kippur until the coming Yom Kippur, upon us for good. Regarding the precision of the words, see the Beis Yosef too on this. In our machzorim they are all with an alef. What is written, that the alef is from the root in Targum and is not first person, means that 'asarna' lacks the root alef. See the Levush.
