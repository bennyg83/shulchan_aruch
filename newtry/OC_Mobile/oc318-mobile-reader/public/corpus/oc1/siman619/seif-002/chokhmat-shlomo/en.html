Se'if 2 On the night of Yom Kippur and the next day we say 'Baruch shem kevod malchuso le'olam va'ed' aloud. Note: See my work on the Torah, Parashas Vayechi, year 5600, where I brought an explicit proof for this from the Midrash Tanchuma there, that where they are similar to the ministering angels, one should say it aloud. See there and examine carefully.
