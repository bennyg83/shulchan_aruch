Who stand. This means during the Ma'ariv prayer. But one who stands all night will be unable to pray with concentration during the day (Beis Yosef). They should be careful not to delay relieving themselves. If they become weak, they may lean on something (Maharil, Darkei Moshe). See the beginning of siman 585. The reason for standing is to be similar to the angels, and therefore women should not stand, as written at the end of siman 610 (Mateh Moshe).
