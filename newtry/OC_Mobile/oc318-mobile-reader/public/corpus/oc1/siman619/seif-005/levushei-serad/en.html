There: See the beginning of siman 585, etc. Meaning, although there regarding shofar one should not lean, as the Magen Avraham wrote there, this is not as severe as shofar. Alternatively, weakness is different. Alternatively, he may lean, but not so much that if the object were removed he would fall; in such a case even regarding shofar it is permitted, as the Magen Avraham wrote there.
