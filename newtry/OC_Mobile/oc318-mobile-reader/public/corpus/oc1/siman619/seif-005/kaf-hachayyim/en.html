5) Ibid. The custom is that the shaliach tzibbur says, etc. He did not mention here that another two people should stand with him. In the Minhagim it is written that another two must join him. The Taz, s.k. 1, cited these words and wrote that specifically when the Rav or the leading member of the congregation says it, two others must stand with him. However, a shaliach tzibbur, who is appointed by the congregation, does not need two others with him, see there. So too wrote Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1. However, Nehar Shalom, sec. 1, wrote that there is no basis to distinguish between whether the shaliach tzibbur or the Rav says Kol Nidrei, and he challenged the aforementioned words of the Taz, see there. This is also the view of Shaarei Teshuvah, sec. 2, that even when the shaliach tzibbur says it, two people must stand with him, see there.
