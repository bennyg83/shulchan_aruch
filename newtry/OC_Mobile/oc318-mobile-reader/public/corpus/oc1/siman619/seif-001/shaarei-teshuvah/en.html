(In Shulchan Aruch 61) They are accustomed to say Kol Nidrei. She'elat Yaavetz 145: early text: say "that I vowed" referring to past; Rabeinu Tam: future — answer "that I vowed" or "that I will vow" — patach on vow word, dagesh on door letter; likewise all "that I swore," etc. He writes whisper for Rabeinu Tam's words — both wordings; in Poland text: vowed/will vow, swore/will swear, forbade/will forbid, accepted prohibition/will accept, from last Yom Kippur, etc., this Yom Kippur — all to satisfy everyone — see there. Individual may say whisper alone; prayer leader in congregation should not change printed widespread text.
