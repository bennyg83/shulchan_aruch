A) [Section 1] the night of Yoh HaK, etc. and before the coming of the sun, before they begin to say in the Yeshiva of Ma'ale, etc., each and every one will say in P.E. For the sake of Yehud Kabbalah, etc., I have come to hold a holy month of fasting on the tenth day of the month of Tishrei, and also not to do any work on this day as the seventh month, and on the tenth day of this seventh month, you shall have a holy reading and you shall answer your souls for any work you shall not do. And I also accept the addition of the the Master to add a dance on the holy place to correct the root of all these mitzvos and all the mitzvos that depend on them in a higher place. And there will be Noam, etc. And before that, the above saying will be said.
