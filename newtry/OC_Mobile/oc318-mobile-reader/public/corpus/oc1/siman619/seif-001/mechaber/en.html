The order of the night of Yom Kippur. It contains three se'ifim.
On the night of Yom Kippur, the custom is that the chazzan says, "In the yeshivah on high and in the yeshivah below, by the authority of the Omnipresent and by the authority of the congregation, we permit praying with the transgressors." And the custom is that he says "Kol Nidrei," etc., and afterward says "Shehecheyanu" without a cup. {Rama: And afterward we pray Ma'ariv. And the custom is to say "Kol Nidrei" while it is still day, and to draw it out with melodies until night; and we say it three times, and each time he raises his voice more than the first (Maharil). And likewise the chazzan says three times, "And it shall be forgiven to the entire congregation," etc., and the congregation says three times, "And Hashem said: I have forgiven according to your word" (Minhagim). And a person should not deviate from the custom of the city, even in the melodies or the piyyutim that they say there (Maharil).}
