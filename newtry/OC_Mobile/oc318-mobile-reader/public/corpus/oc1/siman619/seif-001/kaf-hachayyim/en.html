1) [Seif 1] The night of Yom Kippur, etc. Before sunset, prior to beginning, "By authority of the Heavenly Court," etc., each person should say privately: For the sake of the unification of the Holy One, blessed is He, and His Shechinah, etc., behold, I come to fulfill the positive Torah commandment to fast on Yom Kippur, on the 10th day of the month of Tishrei, and also not to perform melacha on this day, as it is written, "On the 10th day of this seventh month, there shall be a sacred convocation for you, and you shall afflict your souls; you shall not perform any melacha." I also hereby accept upon myself the additional sanctity of Yom Kippur, to add from the weekday onto the sacred, in order to rectify the root of all these mitzvos and all the mitzvos dependent upon them in the supernal realm. "May the pleasantness," etc. Before this, he should say what is written above in siman 606, sec. 45, see there.
