Per the place, etc. Maharshal interpreted they say thus so criminals do not think they are already released since they already prayed with them; therefore per the place, etc., that they return in repentance to the place. And it requires study on this: if they hear that we say we permit to pray, etc., it implies prayer alone; and furthermore if so at the place of closeness there would be hinted distance if they do not return. Before Heaven the future is revealed and this becomes a prosecution. Rather it appears per the place as we wrote: these three announcers are in place of the whole congregation and act on their view as if they did themselves, except the addition of the place, blessed be He, must come first so He agrees with this statement; therefore it appears the correct girsa is as written in the yeshiva, etc., and afterward per the place, etc., and not as in our machzorim first per the place and then in the yeshiva — how would he mention first the congregation's agreement and then the yeshiva's agreement? Rather the opposite is reasonable: the yeshiva agrees to join the place's view to the congregation's view to permit.
