The custom is to sleep in the synagogue and to say songs and praises the entire night. {Rama: And it is good to sleep far from the Aron (Mordechai); and one who does not wish to say praises and songs should not sleep there (Mahari Weil); and the chazzanim who pray the entire day should not stay awake the whole night, for they lose their voice when they do not sleep (Maharil).}
