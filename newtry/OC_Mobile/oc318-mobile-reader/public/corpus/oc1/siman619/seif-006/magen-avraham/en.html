To remain overnight in the synagogue. Levush writes that it is preferable for one to go home to sleep, because a person who remains awake at night will sleep during the day and will not recite the prayers with proper concentration. If the concern is guarding the lamps, they should hire a watchman. Shelah writes that before going to sleep one should recite the first four psalms of Tehillim, because they protect against a seminal emission.
