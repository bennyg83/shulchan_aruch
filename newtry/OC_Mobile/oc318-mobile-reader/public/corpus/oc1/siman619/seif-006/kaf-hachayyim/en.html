6) And the Levush wrote that two distinguished members of the congregation go and stand beside the chazzan, because this is needed to permit praying with transgressors. However, because of Kol Nidrei, three are not needed, since it is only a stipulation concerning the future, etc. Nevertheless, the Taz there wrote that they must remain until the conclusion of Kol Nidrei, see there. So too wrote Eliyah Rabbah, secs. 2 and 4. So too wrote Mateh Ephraim, sec. 10, that the two remain standing until after the chazzan says Barechu, etc., and Einayim Beruch, etc., and then they return to their places. If Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos, when Mizmor Shir L'Yom HaShabbos is said before Barechu, they may return to their places at the beginning of Mizmor Shir L'Yom HaShabbos, end quote. See below, seif 4.
