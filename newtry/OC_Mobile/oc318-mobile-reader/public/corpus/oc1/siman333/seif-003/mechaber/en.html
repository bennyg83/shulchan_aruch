These four or five kufot that he empties — that is for one guest; and if many came to him — he empties according to this measure for each and every guest, provided that one person should not empty for all of them, for there is extra tircha; rather, each one empties for himself, or another empties on his behalf.Wine barrels that were brought on a wagon — it is forbidden to unload them on Shabbat unless for guest honor or for mitzvah need, for it is like emptying the storehouse (Or Zarua).
