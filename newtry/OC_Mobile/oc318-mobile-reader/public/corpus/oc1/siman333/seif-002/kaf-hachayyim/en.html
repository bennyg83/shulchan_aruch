Beis Yosef; Olas Shabbos there; Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. Taz, paragraph 1, however, raises many questions and concludes that even if one began before Shabbos, it may not be cleared on Shabbos for an optional purpose; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, agrees with him. Chemed Moshe, paragraph 1, and Nehar Shalom, paragraph 1, however, reject Taz's questions and agree with Beis Yosef; see there. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2, and Chayei Adam, klal 40, paragraph 4, likewise rule this way.
