A) [Seif 1] Even though it is permitted to take supplies from it, etc. Explanation: even though we rule like Rashi who has no muktzeh except in dried figs and raisins that are set aside by hand, as is written siman 310 — that is when they wish to take supplies from it; but if he does not wish to take supplies from it but only to begin emptying it alone — forbidden unless for a matter of mitzvah; see there letter 1; and the reason is on account of tircha (Beit Yosef, Tosafos there).
