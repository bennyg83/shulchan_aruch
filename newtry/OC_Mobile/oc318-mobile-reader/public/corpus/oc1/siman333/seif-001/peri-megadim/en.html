(א) Forbidden — see Taz in siman 310 seif 1 regarding produce standing for commerce, permitted in tilting and eating per Rashi; and fodder fit for an animal is likewise permitted in tilting (Shabbos 127b in the mishnah, and in Rambam chapter 26). Fifteen — storehouse, etc.; rather he makes a path with his feet — at the beginning of his words this refers to that for reshus it is forbidden to begin, for it is extra tircha (one can say the reason is kufot — what difference if he began from erev Shabbat; rather the reason is weekday activities as Bach wrote); rather he makes a path with his feet — and this is from the braita there 127a; and even to make a path there is no permission on account of weekday activities, rather only with his feet; and per Rashi there it implies that with his feet it is permitted even muktzeh (Magen Avraham siman 308 s.k. 7). And behold, for Rashi there who establishes the mishnah per R' Yehuda — muktzeh — the storehouse that he should not begin even for mitzvah is forbidden as muktzeh; and from this for reshus, even if he began, it is forbidden (for therefore the mishnah taught "they empty on account of beit midrash and guests" and did not say "they empty for guests and beit midrash," to say that even for these more than the measure it is forbidden; or the anonymous teaching implies that specifically on account of these they empty — but for reshus, no). And the braita "they do not begin" is per R' Yehuda, and for mitzvah yes — for reshus, even if he began, forbidden; and nevertheless he makes a path with his feet — it teaches that it is permitted; and even for Shmuel who rules like Rashi and the braita for reshus — nevertheless he does not argue on Rashi in this that with feet it is permitted. And the other braita "gathered grain — if he began from erev Shabbat, permitted to take supplies" — that is for Rashi and for mitzvah, etc.; and therefore it says "I would say Rashi permits" — but for Shmuel he establishes that braita for reshus, and likewise that Rashi forbids, and like the anonymous mishnah "on account of beit midrash," etc. — for reshus he should not begin. But Taz was not satisfied to say there is a dispute between Rashi and Shmuel, and all agree that for mitzvah if he began it is permitted for R' Yehuda and Rashi without beginning; but for reshus, for all, even Rashi they do not begin, even to empty a little; and if he began for reshus — forbidden, possibly even less than the measure likewise; and even from sun to shade and similar lest it spoil — permitted in siman 308 — but to empty to sit there a person who is not a guest — forbidden if possible, on account of tircha; and still requires study; and for the reason of weekday activities — all that has fifteen se'ah, if he began in it and there are no longer fifteen se'ah, permitted for reshus; and from this: all that does not have fifteen se'ah, even if he did not begin in it — permitted for reshus from erev Shabbat. And know that not every shevut they permitted for mitzvah need — for shevut of shevut specifically they said permitted for mitzvah; but shevut alone certainly not — rather specifically such as weekday activities and similar (Magen Avraham siman 307 letter 7):

(ב) Furthermore, briefly I will speak on this that gathered grain — there are in it three explanations: (1) Rashi explains muktzeh, and the text reads "to take supplies" to feed his animal — and how much gathered grain for a lethech? If less — anonymous teaching is not for a storehouse and not muktzeh. The second explanation: Beit Yosef in Rif, and the text reads "they move" — that is, to empty; and for Rashi for mitzvah, and for Shmuel for reshus — how much gathered grain for a lethech? That is, up to a lethech — all that is less than a lethech, that there are not fifteen se'ah, even beginning for reshus is permitted for Rashi, and halacha is like him; and the reason: all that is less than a lethech, there is no storehouse upon it; and likewise grain in kufot and similar standing for animal food — even more than fifteen se'ah — permitted to begin for reshus; for what are kufot? And specifically a storehouse for reshus — forbidden to begin; and likewise if they designated it expressly for a storehouse — forbidden for reshus even less than a lethech.
