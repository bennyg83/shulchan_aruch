1) [Seif 1] Although one may make use of it, etc. Although we rule like Rabbi Shimon, who holds that muktzeh applies only to dried figs and raisins that one actively rejected, as stated in siman 310, this permits using the storehouse's contents. If one does not wish to use them but merely to begin clearing the storehouse, it is forbidden unless needed for a mitzvah. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1. The reason is the exertion involved. Beis Yosef; Tosefes Shabbos there.
2) There: It is forbidden to begin with it, etc. If one began clearing it before Shabbos, he may continue clearing it on Shabbos even for an optional purpose. Beis Yosef; Olas Shabbos there; Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. Taz, paragraph 1, however, raises many questions and concludes that even if one began before Shabbos, it may not be cleared on Shabbos for an optional purpose; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, agrees with him. Chemed Moshe, paragraph 1, and Nehar Shalom, paragraph 1, however, reject Taz's questions and agree with Beis Yosef; see there. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2, and Chayei Adam, klal 40, paragraph 4, likewise rule this way.
3) There: It is forbidden to begin clearing it. One may nevertheless make a path through it with his feet in either direction while walking in and out. Gemara; Magen Avraham, paragraph 3; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 3. See above, siman 308, paragraph 28.
