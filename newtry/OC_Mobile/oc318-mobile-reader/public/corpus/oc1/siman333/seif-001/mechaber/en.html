That one should not empty a storehouse on Shabbat. And in it 3 seifim: A storehouse of grain or
of wine ingredients — even though it is permitted to take supplies from it — it is forbidden to begin emptying it except for a matter of mitzvah, such as that he emptied it for hosting guests or to establish a beit midrash in it. And how does he empty it? If the storehouse was large — he empties from it five kufot (that in each kupa there are three se'ah) (Hagahot Mordechai and Ran in the chapter Mefanin); if there were in it only five kufot — he empties from them four, but he may not empty all of it, lest he come to level the bumps.(And every shevut that they permitted on account of mitzvah need is also permitted for guest need; and they are not called guests unless they lodged with him in his house, or that he invited guests who lodged with others (Terumat HaDeshen siman 72); but when he invited his friend to dine with him — they are not called guests, and it is not a seudat mitzvah but only a seudat reshus.) לא היה בו אלא חמשה קופות מפנה מהם ארבעה אבל כולו לא יפנה שמא יבא להשוות גומות: הגה וכל שבות שהתירו משום צורך מצוה מותר ג"כ לצורך אורחים ולא מקרי אורחים אלא שנתארחו אצלו בביתו או שזימן אורחים שנתארחו אצל אחרים (תרומת הדשן סי' ע"ב) אבל כשזימן חבירו לסעוד אצלו לא מקרי אורחים ואינה סעודת מצוה רק סעודת רשות:
