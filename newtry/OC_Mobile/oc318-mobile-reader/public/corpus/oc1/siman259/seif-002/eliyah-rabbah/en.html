It is forbidden to move them, etc. The Taz asks why this differs from siman 309, regarding a stone upon the mouth of a barrel, where, if one needs the place occupied by the barrel, one may move the barrel; see his lengthy discussion there. In my humble opinion, that case is different because he moves the permitted object, namely the barrel, and the stone is merely carried along with it. Here, however, he touches and moves the forbidden object itself. His difficulty from Tosafos at the end of Bameh Tomnin is also not difficult, because we rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, as is implied in Shabbos 142b and by all the poskim.
