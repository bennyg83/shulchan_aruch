Ibid., s.k. 9: “As stated at the end of se’if 1.” Note: The meaning is that the Ran explains like the Baal HaMaor, that the “covering” mentioned here is not the lid of the pot, but an additional covering placed above it on Shabbos to preserve the heat. It is therefore exactly comparable to the wool fleeces discussed above at the end of se’if 1. Rashi, however, at the end of Bameh Tomnin, explains that it refers to the pot’s lid, which covers the mouth of the pot and is considered part of the utensil itself. The utensil therefore becomes a base for it, like a stone placed upon the mouth of a barrel, where the barrel becomes a base for the stone because the stone serves to cover the barrel’s mouth. Magen Avraham alluded to this distinction above in s.k. 6, distinguishing wool fleeces from the case in siman 309, se’if 4. In Maginei Shlomo at the end of Bameh Tomnin, he seeks to explain that the stone upon the barrel concerns a case where it did not serve the barrel at all; see there. This requires study, for Shabbos 125b clearly indicates that it did serve the barrel. What I have written appears primary, and the difficulty of the Shiltei Gibborim at the end of Bameh Tomnin, letter 4, is therefore resolved; see there. This is readily understood.
