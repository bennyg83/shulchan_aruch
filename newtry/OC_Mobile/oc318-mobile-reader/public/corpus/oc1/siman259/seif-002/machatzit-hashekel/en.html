S.k. 2: He designated them, etc. See what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 308, se’if 22: it is forbidden to cover the mouth of a barrel with a stone unless one designated it permanently for that purpose. With a stone used to crack nuts, however, the Ran holds that designation for one Shabbos is sufficient. In siman 308, Magen Avraham cites two opinions regarding cracking nuts. The Ran explains that because it is not customary to designate a stone as a barrel cover, permanent designation is required; but because it is customary to use a stone to crack nuts, designation for one Shabbos suffices. R. Yonah writes here that the same applies to stones designated for insulation: permanent designation is required, since it is no more customary to place stones around a pot than to use a stone as a barrel cover. Their law is therefore like that of a stone upon a barrel, which requires permanent designation. If so, the same applies to stuffing: since it is not normally used for insulation, permanent designation is required according to all opinions, even according to the Ran. The Bach writes that designation is required only when one insulated with them for a single Shabbos. If he insulated with them for two Shabbosos, he thereby revealed his intention to designate them for insulation, and no further designation is necessary. He infers this from the wording of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, “and he insulated with them incidentally,” which implies one occasion. He also brings some support from the wording of the Gemara; see there. Magen Avraham omitted this view. I do not know whether he disagrees with the Bach.
