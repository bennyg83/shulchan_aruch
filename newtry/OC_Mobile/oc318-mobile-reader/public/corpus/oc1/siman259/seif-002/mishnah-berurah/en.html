Similarly, one should take care not to use wood designated as fuel to seal the oven opening on erev Shabbos, because otherwise he will be unable to move it on Shabbos due to muktzeh.
And he throws them away. After they are used, it is customary to throw them outside. Since they have neither the status nor the importance of a utensil, they are included in muktzeh, because a person removes them from his mind. Stones or bricks that are permanently placed on top of a stove, however, may be moved because they are designated for use.
