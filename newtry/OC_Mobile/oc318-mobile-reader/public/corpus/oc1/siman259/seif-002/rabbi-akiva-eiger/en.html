Magen Avraham, s.k. 12: “The same applies on Friday night, and to placing a hot cooked dish,” etc. This refers to returning it. If it was removed before Shabbos, it must still be in his hand and he must have intended to return it. There is no additional leniency for placing it nearby, except that with a stove that is not grufa u’ketumah, placing it nearby has the same law as placing it on top of a grufa u’ketumah stove, as Rama writes in siman 253. Likewise, the permission obtained by placing a brick or another object upon the oven renders it like grufa u’ketumah, and helps only for returning, just as with grufa u’ketumah. Accordingly, Magen Avraham’s statement that a food without liquid has the same law when cold as when hot is not entirely clear. Once it has cooled, the act of returning no longer applies, and it is considered an initial placement, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 253, s.k. 26; see there and see s.k. 19. It is therefore forbidden even on a grufa u’ketumah stove, and even when merely placed nearby.
