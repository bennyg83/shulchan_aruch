3) There: Provided that part of it is exposed, etc. If no part is exposed, but he can insert a knife and shake it off, it is permitted, as stated below in siman 311, seif 9. Magen Avraham, paragraph 5; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 7. Similarly, if he can shake the cover so that the insulating material falls away, it is permitted even if the cover is entirely covered. Tur and the Shulchan Aruch discuss a case in which it cannot be shaken unless part of it is exposed. Nehar Shalom, paragraph 1.
