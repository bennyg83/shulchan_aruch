Se’if 7: It is permitted to dismantle. See Shabbos 48a: “What difference is there between this,” etc. See Rashi, s.v. “the stopper,” etc. Although the cases are not entirely comparable, because there the discussion concerns a utensil, while regarding something attached to the ground Beitzah 34b states, “Seals attached to the ground,” etc., that applies only to something intended to remain in place, as stated in siman 314, se’if 10. Even regarding the ground, the prohibition is only a rabbinic decree, as Rashi states in Eruvin 35a, s.v. “and he requires,” etc.
