It is permitted to dismantle that sealing. The Beit Yosef writes in the name of the Mordechai that the reason is that only if the seal were made to remain in place, so that it would not be removed on Shabbos, would it be forbidden because of demolition. This board, however, is not made to remain. He then concludes in the name of the Hagahos Mordechai that an object becomes a base for a forbidden item only when one did not intend to remove the forbidden item, etc. He writes that this accords with the view of Sefer HaTerumos in siman 309.
