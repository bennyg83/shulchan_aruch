To insulate with stones. This applies even if he designated them previously for that purpose, so that they are not muktzeh.
Because it is, etc. Arranging the stones resembles forming a wall. It is therefore included in temporary building and is forbidden rabbinically on Yom Tov.
And some permit it. They maintain that the Sages did not issue the decree where the honor of Shabbos is involved. If it is possible to insulate in another manner, all opinions agree that it is forbidden. Eliyah Rabbah writes that the custom follows the permitting opinion, and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav rules likewise.
