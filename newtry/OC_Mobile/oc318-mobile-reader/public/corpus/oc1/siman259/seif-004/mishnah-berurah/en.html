In something that may not be moved. He placed muktzeh material around the pot. If he insulated it in something that may be moved but covered its opening with something that may not be moved, he clears the material from around it, grasps the pot, and removes it, because the pot did not become a base for the covering, as stated at the end of se'if 1. He then shakes the pot, and the object that may not be moved falls away by itself.
