(Sq. D) To the treasurer for goods and precisely when he is careful about them as in C. Sh. S. A. and B. Ibn Hauzer part of the sabra precisely in S. Sh. He is assigned a shekel of wool, which are themselves assigned because they are not worthy of anything, and they are like bundles and stones, according to Rash Dahan, they are assigned, except that the Taman' says that they are designated for this, and they are allowed to shake the shacha while standing for the goods.
