S.k. 4: For storage, for commerce. This applies specifically when he is particular about them, as stated in siman 308, se’if 1. Even HaOzer disagrees. He explains that only in siman 308, regarding utensils designated for commerce, must the owner be particular about them, because we rule like R. Shimon, who does not accept muktzeh, even regarding dates intended for commerce, as explained in siman 310, se’if 2, following Shmuel on Shabbos 19b. Particularity is therefore required to render them muktzeh. Wool fleeces, however, are inherently muktzeh because they are not fit for any use, like pebbles and stones, which R. Shimon agrees are muktzeh. Insulating with them can indicate that they were designated for that purpose and thus permit moving them. If they are intended for commerce, however, they remain inherently muktzeh; no particularity is required, and the act of insulation does not help. See there.
