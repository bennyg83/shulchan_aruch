And if he designated them for this, etc. — Magen Avraham s.k. 2 wrote: this means that he designated them for this forever; but Mishna Berurah or part 2 wrote: since it does not say here "forever" as in seif 2, it implies that here he does not require forever, for they are not muktzeh like stones; and this is proven from Maran's precise wording in Beit Yosef, and likewise from Levush — unlike Magen Avraham; see there. And likewise Eshel Avraham or part 2: in Levush seif 2 and Netziv or part 2 it is explained that straw suffices if designated for this Shabbat alone from Erev Shabbat — see if so, since there is a dispute and also many poskim hold that one insulation suffices and it is permitted to move them like wool fleece as written above or part 1 — therefore for practical law it appears one should be lenient, and whoever prepared them for insulation, even if not forever but for one Shabbat, it is permitted to move them:
