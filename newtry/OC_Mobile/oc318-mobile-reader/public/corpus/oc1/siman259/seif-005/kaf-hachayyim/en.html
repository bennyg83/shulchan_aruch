5) There: If he designated it for this purpose, etc. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2, writes that this means he designated it permanently. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 2, however, writes that since the word 'permanently' is not stated here as it is in seif 2, it implies that permanent designation is unnecessary here, because this material is not inherently muktzeh like stones. This is also demonstrated by Maran Beis Yosef's precise wording and implied by Levush, contrary to Magen Avraham; see there. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 2, similarly writes that Levush, seif 2, and Nezer Yisrael, paragraph 2, make clear that for soft material, designation before Shabbos for this Shabbos alone suffices; see there. Accordingly, since the matter is disputed and several halachic authorities maintain that using it once for insulation suffices and permits moving it just like wool shearings, as stated above in paragraph 1, it appears that one may be lenient as a matter of practical halachah. Whenever he designates it for insulation, even if not permanently but only for one Shabbos, it may be moved.
