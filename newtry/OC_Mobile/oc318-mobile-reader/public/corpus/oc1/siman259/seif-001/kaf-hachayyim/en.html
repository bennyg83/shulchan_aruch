1) [Seif 1] Soft material in which one insulated food on an incidental basis may not be moved, etc. This is because it was set aside for labor and may not be moved. Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, however, writes that there is no distinction between soft material and wool shearings. Whenever one did not place them in storage, if he insulated food in them, they may be moved; see there. Erech HaShulchan, paragraph 1, similarly writes in the name of several halachic authorities that there is no distinction between soft material and wool shearings, and once one has insulated food in them, they may be moved; see there. Bach writes that soft material used incidentally for insulation need not be expressly designated for that purpose permanently. Only on the first Shabbos that he incidentally insulates food in it may it not be moved unless he designated it before Shabbos. If he uses it again to insulate food on another Shabbos, it may be moved, because his repeated use on another Shabbos is regarded as though he expressly designated it for insulation; see there.
