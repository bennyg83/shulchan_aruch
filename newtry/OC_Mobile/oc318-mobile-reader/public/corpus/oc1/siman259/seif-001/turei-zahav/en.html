It is forbidden to move them. Because they were not rendered subordinate to the insulation, they remain muktzeh.
For example, some of them are exposed. This is difficult to me. Why does this differ from the case in the chapter Notel, and below in siman 309, of a stone upon the mouth of a barrel, where, if one needs the place occupied by the barrel, one may move the barrel? Tosafos at the end of Bameh Tomnin likewise write in the name of the Rashba that according to the Sages who disagree with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, even when the forbidden object is completely covered, one may remove it directly by hand, and they bring proof from the chapter Notel. One must answer that there the case concerns one who forgot it there, whereas if he deliberately placed it there, the permitted object becomes a base for a forbidden object. Here, although he placed it there intending to remove it on Shabbos, he is nevertheless considered to have deliberately placed it there, following one opinion in siman 309 and not the view of Sefer HaTerumos there, which holds that it is considered deliberate placement only when he intends to leave it there for the entire Shabbos. Yet this is difficult, because in se’if 7, regarding an oven, the ruling follows Sefer HaTerumos. This requires further study.
