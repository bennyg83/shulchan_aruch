It appears that he did not have before him the wording found in our text, because these are in fact designated for their use, yet Magen Avraham omitted this. He apparently omitted it intentionally. Although Rashi writes this in his commentary to the Mishnah, that applies to the law of hatmanah. Here, however, since the text states that stuffing is regarded as wool shearings, it necessarily does not refer to stuffing designated only for making felt.
But if he placed them in storage as merchandise. See Magen Avraham, who writes that this applies specifically when he is particular that they not be used for any purpose. I did not copy this because Even HaOzer challenges him; see there. Eliyah Rabbah likewise questions his ruling, though Peri Megadim cites it. Know that Eliyah Rabbah’s view accords with the Rishonim who maintain that stuffing has the same law as wool shearings whenever it is not held as merchandise, and that mere use for hatmanah permits moving it on the following day. He cites Behag as following this view. In my humble opinion, this is also the view of Rabbeinu Chananel, for on page 48 he does not cite Abaye’s discussion concerning stuffing. He therefore evidently rules like Behag: since the law follows Rava regarding wool shearings (that mere hatmanah permits them to be moved) as Rabbeinu Chananel concludes there because Rava is the later authority, the same applies to stuffing, and Abaye’s ruling is rejected. Many Rishonim, however, rule like Abaye, as Beis Yosef records. Nevertheless, in pressing circumstances it appears that one may rely on this position.
