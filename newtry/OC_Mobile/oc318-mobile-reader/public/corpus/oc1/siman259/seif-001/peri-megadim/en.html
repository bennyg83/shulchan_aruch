It is forbidden. See the Taz. Wool fluff is valuable, and felt is made from it. See Rashi to Shabbos 48a and the Levush here. See siman 257, se'if 3, regarding dry wool fluff. See also the Yerushalmi at the beginning of chapter 4, which states that one must leave a small amount lacking. See the commentator there, who explains that the pot should not be completely full, lest it spill and the insulating material become moist from an external source, which would then add heat. See there regarding olive waste that, after twelve months, is considered dry and no longer susceptible to impurity. The commentator explains that the same applies to insulating with it after twelve months. He expresses surprise that the poskim did not cite this ruling.
