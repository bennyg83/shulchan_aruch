11) [Seif 2] One who traps a sleeping deer, etc. These creatures ordinarily try to escape when they sense a human hand, so capturing them constitutes actual trapping. One who captures a lame, sick, or old deer, however, is exempt because it already stands trapped, since it cannot escape. Beis Yosef; Levush.
12) There: One who traps a sleeping deer, etc. It appears that the same applies to all other species that are ordinarily trapped. This is also implied by Rambam and Semag. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3.
