Asleep or blind. It can escape when they feel a person's hand; and in the Gemara they distinguish regarding an ill person: if due to fatigue — he is tired and cannot move from his place — trapped and standing, exempt; if due to fever — ill, not trapped, liable. Tur and Shulchan Aruch did not mention this since they equated him to an old man — it is implied that an ill person also cannot escape like an old man — that is, due to his fatigue.
