[2] That they are trapped there etc. Meaning: wherever it runs before him and he reaches it in one bound — that is, in one running without artifice and trouble (Mas'at Binyamin there); and Avodat HaKodesh of Rashba wrote this language: in one running until he does not need to exert himself until the trapper reaches it; the lion-trapper is not liable until he brings it into his cage where it is forbidden to it (Talmud; and see seif 10). One who scoops fish from the sea into a bowl of water — this is trapping; but into a pool of water — not trapping (Rambam):
