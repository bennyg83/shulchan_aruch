(א) Exempt. For they are trapped and standing:

(ב) It is trapping. Thus wrote the Rambam: one who sends dogs to trap deer, and the deer fled before the dog and he was pursuing the deer or stood before it and frightened it until the dogs arrived and seized it — he is liable; and the same applies to birds, end of his words. It is implied that if he did not do an act himself he is exempt; and all this he speaks only regarding rabbinic prohibition:
