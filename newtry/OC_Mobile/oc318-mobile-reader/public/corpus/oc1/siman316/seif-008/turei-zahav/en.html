Eight creeping things etc. Ran wrote: one who wounds one of the eight creeping things until the blood coagulates is liable — since they have skin, the skin holds back the blood from exiting; and were it not for the skin it would exit; therefore liable due to removal of the soul in that place, for the blood is the soul. But other abominations — since they have no skin to hold back the blood, if uprooted from its place it would exit outside; therefore as long as it did not exit outside — exempt; but when it exited outside — liable even in other abominations due to removal of the soul:
