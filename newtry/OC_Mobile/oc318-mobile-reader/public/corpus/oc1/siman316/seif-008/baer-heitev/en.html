(א) The skin. Some say one who wounds is liable due to uprooting, which is a tolada of threshing; and even though threshing applies only to "great ones of the ground," an animal is called "great ones of the ground" and he is not liable until blood the size of a dried fig goes from place to place. And some say due to dyeing; and some say because of removal of the soul in that place; and for this reason most commentators agreed. And one who wounds a person or his fellow's animal out of vengeance — Rambam obligates, for he gives satisfaction to his inclination; and Ra'avad exempts — see there; and see in Magen Avraham siman 278 regarding this; and the same applies to tearing — they disagree; and it appears to me that one who wounds for healing is liable according to all — see siman 328 seif 1:

(ג) Exempt. For it is melacha she'ein tzricha l'gufah. One who raised a fish from a bowl of water and left it until it dried like a rock between its fins — liable due to removal of the soul, for it can no longer live — Rambam, Gemara.
