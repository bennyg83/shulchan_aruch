(s.k. 8) And there are, etc. — even though the Tur wrote, etc. — meaning that the Tur rejected Beit Yosef's proof from bees, for a beehive is different because it is small — it is implied that the Tur agrees that in a small one it is forbidden, namely specifically regarding bees; but regarding flies the Tur holds that even in a small one it is permitted, for the first reason that if he opens the box they will flee; and in a beehive this does not apply, as stated; or for another reason, as Taz wrote; and he did not write that a beehive is different except to reject Beit Yosef's words per the Tur's understanding, as stated. And Beit Yosef ruled to be stringent according to Beit Yosef's view. And Taz wrote that the Tur also does not explain Beit Yosef in this, and agrees that if he sees flies in the box he must drive them away before he closes the box; but he need not check whether there are flies there as long as he does not see flies:
