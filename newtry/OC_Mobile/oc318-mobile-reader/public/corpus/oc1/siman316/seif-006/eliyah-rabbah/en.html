[6] It is trapping etc. So Rama wrote in the name of Kelalei HaChodesh; and this is Rambam's language: one who sends dogs to trap, and it fled before the dogs and he pursued the trapped animal or stood before it and frightened it until the dog seized it — liable; and the same with birds — until here. It is implied if he did not act himself he is exempt; and Kelalei HaChodesh speaks only of prohibition in general (Magen Avraham). And I wonder why he said "it is trapping" — implying liability; especially astonishing that he did not see in Kelalei HaChodesh itself this language: one who sends dogs to pursue the trapped animal and pursued so the dog reaches to seize it — liable — until his language. Behold he wrote liable; and it appears what he wrote "and pursued so" etc. is an act, and he followed Rambam as is his way. If so, Rama's and Levush's words also explain thus — and it is clear. Rivash siman 245 wrote: he saw a deer and said "come, come" and did no act and did not bring it to a guarded place and it came and sat until evening — no trapping here since he did not touch it:
