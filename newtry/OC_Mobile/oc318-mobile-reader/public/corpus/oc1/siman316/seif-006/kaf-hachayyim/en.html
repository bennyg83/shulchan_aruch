6) There: And other birds, etc. Levush writes the same. Bach writes that no distinction should be made between a free bird and other birds, and that this is the view of Rif, Rambam, and Rosh; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2, writes likewise. Even HaOzer, siman 497, paragraph 9, however, writes that Rambam and the halachic authorities also agree with the Shulchan Aruch that a distinction exists between a free bird and other birds; see there. This is the view of the later authorities.
