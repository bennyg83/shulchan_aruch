(א) Not in its species trapped — and even if he traps them for some purpose — it is also not considered trapping:
(ב) Therefore flies — and bees are implied in Beit Yosef that they are in the category of something in its species trapped; but other poskim disagree with him; and so it is implied from the Gra s.k. 6. Grasshoppers — according to Rashi it is something in its species trapped; the Rambam does not hold thus. Fish are in the category of something in its species trapped [later authorities]:
(ג) Not to close — but rather place a knife or garlic or something else between the cover and the box in a manner that they can exit from there; and likewise if there is a small hole in the box — even if not visible to flies explicitly — nevertheless permitted, for it is not psik reisha:
(ד) A small box — but a large box — the Rama holds that according to all it is permitted, for even if a person were standing inside he could not catch it in one stride; and also it is something not in its species trapped:
(ה) And there are those who are lenient — this is the Tur's view, who holds that even though in the Gemara it is stated explicitly regarding a beehive that if he covers it in a manner that has no hole it is forbidden because of psik reisha, as below in seif 4 — nevertheless flies are different, for it is not called trapping at all, for due to their smallness usually when one opens the vessel they all flee from there; unlike in a beehive of bees that is full of them and they are somewhat large — impossible not to catch one of them; and therefore it is considered trapping thereby. And Beit Yosef ruled to be stringent per the first view; and so Magen Avraham wrote that such is essential; and therefore one should drive away the flies; and see in Taz who ruled that once he drove away the flies he saw with his eyes — he need no longer check and be precise whether perhaps there are more flies there, since this is only doubt of psik reisha in a rabbinic matter that is something not in its species trapped — one need not be so stringent:
