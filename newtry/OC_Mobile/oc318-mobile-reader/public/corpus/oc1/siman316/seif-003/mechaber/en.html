Any [creature] whose species is trapped — liable for it; whose species is not trapped — exempt but forbidden. Therefore flies — even though their species is not trapped — it is forbidden to trap them:And therefore one should be careful not to close a small box or to seal vessels that have flies in them on Shabbat, for it is psik reisha that they will be trapped there [Baal HaTerumah and Mordechai beginning of chapter Kirah and Agur].
