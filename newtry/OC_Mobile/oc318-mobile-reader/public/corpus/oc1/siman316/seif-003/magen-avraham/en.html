(ג) And there are those who are lenient. Even though the Tur wrote regarding a small beehive that it is forbidden — nevertheless regarding flies the Rama holds that it is permitted, for if one comes to take them they will flee, as stated; and Beit Yosef ruled to be stringent, and so is the essential law:
