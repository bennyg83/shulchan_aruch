[3] One who traps a deer etc. So is the Talmud's language — it appears the same law applies to other trapped species; and so implied in Rambam and Semag, who wrote one traps the asleep and blind and did not mention a deer — requires study. I found in Rokeach siman 78: Yerushalmi — a sleepy deer is liable, for it closes one eye and opens one — until here. It appears Rambam holds the Yerushalmi disputes our Gemara, since he exempts the blind; and he holds asleep is like blind.
