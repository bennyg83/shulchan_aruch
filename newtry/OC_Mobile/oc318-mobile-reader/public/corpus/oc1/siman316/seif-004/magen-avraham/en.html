Psik reisha. And even though bees are not in their species trapped, as is stated in Beitza — and so too Rashba in Bach wrote — if so there is no d'oraisa prohibition; nevertheless it is forbidden because of psik reisha — see siman 314 seif 1. And if there is a small hole but it is not visible — permitted, for it is not psik reisha [Tosafos in Beitza]. And see in Rashba there, who wrote that the text "and you will understand" is not in the text. The one who spreads a trap and at the time of spreading an animal enters into it — liable; but if it entered afterward — exempt but forbidden, for he does not know whether it will be trapped or not [Tosafos daf 17].
