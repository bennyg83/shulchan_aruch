[11] [Levush] Bees whose species is trapped, etc. Beit Yosef writes this. Maharsha objects from Beitzah 36b, where the Gemara says one might have thought that trapping a species normally trapped is prohibited but trapping a species not normally trapped is permitted, therefore the Mishnah teaches otherwise. This seems to show bees are not normally trapped. Bach and all the later authorities likewise object to Beit Yosef. It appears to me, however, that Bach understands the Gemara's conclusion to mean that bees too are considered a species normally trapped. Moreover, Rashi and Ran in chapter HaOreg explain that locusts and similar insects are normally trapped like wild animals and birds, and at first glance bees should be the same. Yet Rambam chapter 10 and Kol Bo, p. 108, write that locusts and such insects are not normally trapped, and Rabbenu Yerucham, p. 80, likewise writes so, though his text says fish instead of those insects. That appears to be a scribal error, because the Gemara at the beginning of chapter Ein Tzadin, Rambam, and all the authorities explicitly treat fish as normally trapped. There is proof for Rashi from Tosefta Shabbos chapter 13: "One who traps flies or mosquitoes is liable; Rabbi Yehudah exempts." This implies they disagree only regarding flies and mosquitoes, while all agree regarding locusts and similar insects. Yet I found clear proof for Rambam in the Yerushalmi, chapter Shemonah Sheratzim. This still requires study, because the Bavli says that one who traps locusts during dew is exempt but during heat is liable unless they are arriving in large numbers. Even if Rambam holds the first baraita indicates no distinction and is authoritative, the Tosefta and Yerushalmi explicitly imply the other rule, and the Bavli analyzes that baraita as normative.
