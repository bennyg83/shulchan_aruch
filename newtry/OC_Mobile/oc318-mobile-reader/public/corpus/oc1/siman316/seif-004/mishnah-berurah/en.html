(א) One spreads a mat — in summer because of sun and in rain because of rain [Gemara]:

(ב) In a manner that is not necessary — such as that he not press the mat tightly on the beehive so the bees can at least exit with difficulty; or that there is some small hole in the beehive and they can exit from there; and even though not visible explicitly to bees — nevertheless permitted, for we rule like R' Shimon that davar she'eino mitkavein is permitted. But when there is no hole at all — then it is psik reisha and forbidden even in a rabbinic matter. One who spreads a trap and at the time of spreading an animal entered into it — liable to a chatas; but if it entered afterward — exempt but forbidden.
