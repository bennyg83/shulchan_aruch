(א) (s.k. 4) It is trapping. Thus wrote the Rambam, etc. — for in the Gemara daf 106b the Gemara raises two baraitot against each other: one teaches one who traps a sick person is liable, and another teaches exempt; and it answers — R' Shimon said there is no difficulty: here with a sick person from heat, here with a sick person from weakness. And Rashi explained: weakness — faintness from which he cannot move from his place — he is trapped and standing, end of his words. If so, from heat — liable according to Rashi; and for the Rambam he explained the reverse — that from heat he is exempt, and this is with a sick person, for he is as trapped; and from weakness he is liable — namely that he caused this faintness, that he pursued it until the dog arrived; because from the weariness the pursuit was such that he had to stand, and thereby the dog seized it — liable. Unlike if he was weary on his own and did not cause the weariness for it — the Rambam agrees he is exempt.
