4) There: One who traps a bird, etc. If a bird entered a house through an open window, although it is not trapped there, one may not close the window or block the opening. It appears to me that in cold weather, when the cold or another discomfort causes suffering, one may close it if his sole intent is protection from the cold. Since this bird is not subject to Torah-level trapping, this is an inevitable rabbinic consequence. With another animal or bird to which Torah-level trapping applies, however, it is forbidden even when he has no such intention, because it is an inevitable consequence. Chayei Adam, klal 30, paragraph 2.
