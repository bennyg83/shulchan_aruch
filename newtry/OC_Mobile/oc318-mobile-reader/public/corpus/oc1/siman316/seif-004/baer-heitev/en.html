Its head. Even though bees are not of a species that is trapped — so too Maharsha and Bach — if so there is no d'oraisa prohibition; nevertheless forbidden as psik reisha. And if there is a small hole that is not visible — permitted, for it is not psik reisha — Tosafos Beitza; and see in Maharsha there. One who spreads a trap and at the time of spreading an animal enters it — liable; but if it entered afterward — exempt, but forbidden, for he does not know whether it will be trapped or not — Tosafos daf 17.
