(א) Dror for the tower, etc. Because the dror bird — even though it is in a small house — they cannot catch it:

(ב) That they are trapped. To exclude a large house in which they cannot catch it — and see siman 697. And the Rambam wrote that he can catch it in one stride. And one who scoops fish from the sea into a bowl of water — [11:8] — it is trapping; and one who traps a lion is not liable until he brings it into his den in which it is enclosed:
