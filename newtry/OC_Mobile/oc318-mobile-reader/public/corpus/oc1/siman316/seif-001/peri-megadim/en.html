Free wild bird. Taz Shabbos 106:2 and Avodat Yisrael; and in Gemara it challenges: perhaps birds too in a covered aviary? But a house is covered — whether for R' Yehuda or Rabbanan, for a house — no; evidently even for R' Yehuda a house with closed windows — otherwise certainly difference between house with open windows and covered aviary without windows. And what he wrote "both bird according to what it is" — difficult: in a large coop even a free wild bird is trapped; what bird is not trapped in a small coop? It appears his intent as Magen Avraham wrote in 397:9 — therefore they omitted free wild bird; once they gave the rule trapped there, we know everything per the bird — geese in a house with open window are trapping. And what Tur wrote "deer for house and aviary" — he mentioned house for the precision: bird — even geese not with open window; Mechaber deals with closed and trapped there — even a house, a large house is like a large aviary (Magen Avraham 2). Not like Bach who wrote decisors hold geese too for a house exempt. And behold in Mishnah R' Yehuda says bird for coop and deer for house — geese for house not? So in open ones; per Bach and Taz Rabbanan dispute in both: deer to aviary liable, free wild bird to closed house exempt for them; for R' Yehuda closed house — free wild bird too liable. If so why "bird for coop" — precision: for house even closed exempt unlike R' Yehuda; but Rashi Beitza 24a "and Rabbanan say bird for coop as you said" — requires study.
