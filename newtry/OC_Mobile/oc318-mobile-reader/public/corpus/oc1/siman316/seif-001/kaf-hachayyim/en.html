1) [Seif 1] One who traps a free bird in a cabinet, etc. The Gemara, Shabbos 106b, explains that one is not liable for bringing a bird into a house specifically when it is a free bird. Although this species ordinarily lives in houses, it is not trapped there because it slips from corner to corner. With any other bird, however, once one brings it into a house and locks the door before it, he is liable because it is trapped. Beis Yosef. See below, paragraph 6. Ramban in Parashas Metzora implies that the general term 'bird' includes turtledoves, pigeons, geese, and chickens. Free birds are the very small birds that live on tree branches, sing, and chatter. They atone in the purification of a metzora for the sin of chatter. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1.
