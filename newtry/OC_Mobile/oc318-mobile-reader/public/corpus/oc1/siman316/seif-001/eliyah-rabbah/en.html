[1] And other birds etc. Rif and Rambam chapter 10, Ra'ash and Tur did not distinguish between a free wild bird and other birds; Chiddushei Maharshal and all the later authorities wondered, and their answer is forced; and it appears to my humble opinion that he holds like Tosafos in Beitza daf 24: in a covered aviary it is trapping specifically when one does not need a trap; but when still needing a trap, such as in a house or large aviary — it is trapping. According to this, why should the Talmud distinguish between a free wild bird and other birds — let it also distinguish large aviary from small like with an animal. Therefore it appears to me that the explanation of Shulchan Aruch now that we arrive at this conclusion — an animal, an animal too is not difficult: in a large aviary etc. they returned to the answer of a free wild bird — requires study. And behold Ran in Shabbos and Beitza distinguishes a free wild bird; therefore Rif explained — and it appears he holds that even in a large house it is permitted as long as it is not a free wild bird. And what is forbidden here as trapping — they explained like Rashi's explanation in Beitza — requires study. With this one can resolve responsa Mas'at Binyamin siman 87 — see there; or one may say they agree that all needing a trap are forbidden; only that even when not needing a trap, such as a small house, nevertheless forbidden with a free wild bird; and this appears Shulchan Aruch's view, who opened "a free wild bird for a coop" — implying other birds are trapping even in a house; and afterward wrote other birds and a deer for a house that are trapped there — it is forced to say "that are trapped there" refers only to a deer; and in truth this appears Levush's view, who distinguishes two in a house; and other birds even for a house; and so a deer etc.; and he also holds like my first explanation in Ran; and nevertheless in the conclusion of the authorities mentioned above one must say there is no distinction between a free wild bird and other birds; and so implied in Avodat HaKodesh of Rashba daf 38; and it appears all this with closed windows, but with open ones it is not trapping; and so implied from Rashi's explanation in the Mishnah there.
