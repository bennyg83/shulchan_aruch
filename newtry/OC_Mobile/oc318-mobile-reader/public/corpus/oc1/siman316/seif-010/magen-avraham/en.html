(א) Even if they do not run. For there is pikuach nefesh in merely showing them; and therefore permitted even per the one who obligates melacha she'ein tzricha legufah:

(ב) And even b'mechaven. Even for the Rambam — permitted [Machatzit HaShekel]; and a reason is needed for the matter. And it appears to me specifically regarding a snake and scorpion, for per the majority they kill; and therefore even in a place where they do not kill — permitted to trample them because of concern for danger to lives. But a thing that never kills — obvious that it is forbidden to trample it for the Rambam. And those people who kill the house gecko that they call spider in foreign language — it appears to me that it is a complete prohibition, for it does not harm; and even though there is concern that it will fall into food — nevertheless it is an uncommon matter and one can cover the food; and also one in a thousand that it is dangerous in food — therefore one should protest against them:
