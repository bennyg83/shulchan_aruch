[10] [Levush] And some permit etc. Bach was stringent like the first view; Taz is lenient when there is doubt whether there are flies — see there. In my humble opinion one should not be lenient in summer where there is food; and so Agudah wrote in chapter Meshilin. And behold Beit Yosef challenged that flies are not similar to bees, for it is like bringing a lion into his room; and Bach wrote and gave him permission — he speaks of liability; but for rabbinic prohibition there is even when bringing into a courtyard. It appears to me Beit Yosef's intent is specifically a lion of a trapped species, unlike flies that are not of a trapped species — a double d'rabbanan; and nevertheless one may say a lion is not guarded elsewhere unlike flies guarded in every box:
