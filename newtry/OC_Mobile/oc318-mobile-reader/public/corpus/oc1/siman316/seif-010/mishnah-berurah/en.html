(א) That they, etc. — such as a mad dog and the like:
(ב) After him — and even if they flee before him:
(ג) If they run, etc. — per the Rambam who obligates melacha she'ein tzricha legufah — it must be said that thus he speaks: they do not kill with certainty but it is doubt of pikuach nefesh; and therefore when they run after him there is concern of danger and permitted. However for us who hold melacha she'ein tzricha legufah is rabbinically forbidden — permitted because of mere pain so he will not be harmed. And specifically these and all such that harm by nature and their bite is a stinging bite — except that it is not their manner to kill in this place; unlike a flea above and all small creeping things such as these — where even through their stinging there is not so much pain — forbidden to kill them even when they sting him; only drive them away from upon him [Beit Yosef]:
(ד) To trample them — and the same applies that he turns a vessel over them [Or Zarua]:
(ה) And even b'mechaven — for melacha she'ein tzricha legufah applies; and here when they harm — even rabbinically they did not decree when he does not kill them directly but appears before observers as if he does not intend, so they will not say this one takes a soul on Shabbat intentionally and they will not know to distinguish. However regarding ants and other vermin and creeping things — even by walking it is forbidden to trample them. Forbidden to kill on Shabbat the house gecko that they call spider; and even though people say it is dangerous when it falls in food — nevertheless the harm is not certain; and also one can cover the foods; therefore one should protest against them; and all the more so one should be careful not to kill other creeping things and worms found in fruits [later authorities]:
