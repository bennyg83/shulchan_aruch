And Magen Avraham wrote: and it appears to me specifically regarding a snake and scorpion that per the majority they kill; therefore even where they do not kill, permitted to trample them due to concern for danger to life. But a thing that never kills — it is obvious forbidden to trample it according to Rambam. And those people who kill the wall-lizard called "spider" in foreign tongue — it appears to me it is a complete prohibition, for it does not harm; and even though there is concern it may fall into food — nevertheless it is an uncommon matter and he can cover the foods; and also one in a thousand that it is dangerous in food — therefore one should protest against them — end of his words:
