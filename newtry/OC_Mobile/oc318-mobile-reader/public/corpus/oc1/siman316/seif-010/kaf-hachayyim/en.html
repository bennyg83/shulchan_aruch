10) There: In which they are trapped, etc. One who removes fish from the sea into a bowl of water is liable. If he removes them from the sea into a pool of water, he is exempt. One who traps a lion is not liable until he brings it into its enclosure, where it is confined. Rambam, chapter 10, halachah 19; Magen Avraham, paragraph 2; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Tosefes Shabbos there. Similarly, one who traps an animal in a weak net that it can tear and escape from is not liable to bring a sin offering. He should leave it in the net and not take it in his hands. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 6.
