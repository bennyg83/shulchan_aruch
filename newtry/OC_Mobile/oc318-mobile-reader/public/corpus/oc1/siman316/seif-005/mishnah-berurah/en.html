(א) That entered a house — on its own; and all the more so if he brought it into a house and locked before it — liable:

(ב) And he locked, etc. — namely that he closed the door, even without a lock, since the deer cannot flee:

(ג) Exempt — for it is written "and if one soul sins unwittingly from among the people of the land in doing it," etc., and we derive "in doing it" — the one who does all of it; and not one who does part of it; and if so, since each one by himself can lock it — behold each one does only part:

(ד) One cannot and, etc. — it is its manner to lock with two; and behold for each one it is melacha that without him it is not done [Rashi]. And if one can do it by himself and the second cannot, and both did it — the one who can is liable, for assisting has no substance and it is as if he did everything alone; and this one who cannot is exempt but forbidden:

(ה) From itself — meaning closed in a manner that the deer cannot flee; perforce permitted to lock, for the lock is only guarding and not trapping, for it is already trapped and standing. And likewise when the deer was tied in the house — permitted to close the door, even if the deer was released afterward — he need not open the door so it will exit, since he closed the door permissibly:
