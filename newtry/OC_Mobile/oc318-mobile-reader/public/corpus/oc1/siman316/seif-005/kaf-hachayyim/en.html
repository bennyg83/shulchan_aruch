5) There: One who traps a bird, etc. If one encounters a nest on Shabbos with the mother resting upon the chicks, is he obligated to fulfill the mitzvah of sending away the mother bird? Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chaim, siman 100, was asked this question. He answers that according to the reason of the sacred Zohar, that the mitzvah is intended to arouse the lament of the supernal Mother over her children, it is obvious that on Shabbos and Yom Tov one may not arouse lament above. Even according to the revealed Torah, it is improper. He writes there that even when no liability for trapping applies and there is only a rabbinic prohibition of muktzeh, it is nevertheless forbidden; see there. Pischei HaDevir, paragraph 1, cites him; see there for his comments. See also Rashash on Eitz Chaim, Gate 15, chapter 3, who writes that one must take care not to perform this mitzvah when Ze'eir Anpin and Nukva possess expanded consciousness, such as during prayer and similar times, during the forty-nine days of the Omer, or from Rosh Hashanah through Shemini Atzeres, because expanded consciousness is present throughout these times; see there. Accordingly, this mitzvah also has no place on Shabbos, because expanded consciousness enters on Shabbos and is not withdrawn, as is known.
