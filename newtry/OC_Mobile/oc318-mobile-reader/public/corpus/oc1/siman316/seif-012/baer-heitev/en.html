He traps them. See in responsa Mishna Berurah siman 60, which concludes that even geese and chickens not yet accustomed to the house — when they do not come on their own to their coop in the evening — one who traps them is liable for a chatas; and if they were accustomed and come to their coop in the evening, even other birds — exempt but forbidden; and if they left the house and he fears they will be lost — even though forbidden to trap them, we push them back to bring them into the house due to loss — nevertheless forbidden to move and seize them; but birds not accustomed — forbidden to push them — Eliyah Rabbah and Magen Avraham.
