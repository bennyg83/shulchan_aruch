An animal and bird in his possession — permitted to trap them, provided they do not rebel. But if they are rebellious — forbidden to seize them even in a courtyard if the courtyard is large such that if they had not grown up among people they would need trapping:And some say it is forbidden to trap an animal and bird in his possession, and if he traps — exempt [Hagahot Alfasi]. But a cow and] horse, and all the more so other animal and bird that rebelled — if he traps them, liable for a chatat; and so is the essence [Maggid chapter 10]. A cat — its law is like other animals, and forbidden to seize on Shabbat [Hagahot Alfasi chapter 3 daf 180]:]:
