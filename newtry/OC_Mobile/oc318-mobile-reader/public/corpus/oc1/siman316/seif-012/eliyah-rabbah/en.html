[12] [Levush] Permitted to spread etc. For his intent is not to trap them (Levush Yom Tov). And in truth so appears Shulchan Aruch; but requires study from Beitza daf 36: what do we deal with here that it has etc. — why not say "provided he does not intend to trap" but rather "provided he does not make it a trap" — until here. Why do I need "and do not say" etc. as its plain meaning also fits well that he not intend, even though there is place for R' Shimon — nevertheless he must not intend; also what does it challenge there — obvious it teaches much that even here he must not intend. R' Yom Tov Tzahalon responsa wrote: when locusts flee from the hive and sit on the wall nearby, they take one bed and anoint it with maimon and place it opposite them and speak good words until they all gather on the bed, then take the bed and bring them to the hive — even a Jew possibly permitted on Shabbat, for speaking to them is not trapping since he does not tie them and gives no glue on Shabbat so they not flee — only they can still flee; all the more to tell a non-Jew:
