And Maharil wrote that permitted to place his feet on it provided he does not rub; and so too Hagahot Maimoniyot:

(ב) Disgust. It appears to me that nowadays when no one is particular about spittle due to disgust, he should be careful not to rub when trampling on it; and possibly this is Maharil's intent.
