(א) On the ground because of, etc. It is implied that on a paved floor — permitted according to those who permit honor in a paved courtyard; see siman 337 seif 2; and so too in the Yerushalmi. And on a bench — according to all permitted; and there is doubt whether it should be forbidden because of smoothing himself; and one can say that smoothing applies only when his intent is that something should be smoothed upon another, but here he wants it to be absorbed in the ground. Maharil wrote: permitted to place his feet upon it, provided he does not rub; and so too Hagahot Maimoniyot:

(ב) Because of repulsiveness. It appears to me that nowadays, since people are not particular about spittle because of repulsiveness — one should be careful that there be no rubbing at all when he tramples upon it; and it is possible that this is Maharil's intent. But saliva that comes from the mouth or nose — certainly there is repulsiveness; and in Beit HaMikdash even spittle is permitted — see there siman 153:
