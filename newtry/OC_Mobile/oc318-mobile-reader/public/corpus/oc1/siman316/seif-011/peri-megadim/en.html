Permitted. Taz: behold in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 10 laws of Shabbat 24 (Magen Avraham note 26) implied: animal and bird in his possession exempt — somewhat rebellious and large courtyard: exempt but forbidden; in his possession and not rebellious, or rebellious and small courtyard: permitted l'chatchila; because rebelled liable chatas in Reshut HaRabbim not in courtyard; thus animal in his possession not rebellious permitted even large courtyard l'chatchila to trap; rebellious forbidden, no chatas in large courtyard; and horse in Reshut HaRabbim liable chatas. Mishna Berurah disputes: in his possession and not rebellious forbidden; rebellious liable chatas in courtyard too, large too. Taz to Mechaber: needless to say in his possession not rebellious permitted in large courtyard; even rebellious in small courtyard permitted; animal and bird in possession exempt forbidden when rebellious and large courtyard. For Mishna Berurah forbidden when not rebellious; possibly small courtyard permitted even for Mishna Berurah on Shabbat (Magen Avraham note 26; and if Hashem wills explained there; Mishna Berurah siman 8; and if Hashem wills there).
