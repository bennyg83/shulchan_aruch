[11] [Levush] Bees whose species etc. So Beit Yosef wrote. Maharsha challenged in Talmud Beitza daf 36: perhaps you would think in its species trapped — forbidden; not in its species not trapped — permitted — it teaches us; behold bees are not of a trapped species; and so Bach and all later authorities challenged Beit Yosef. To me it appears Bach explained it teaches that bees too are of a trapped species; and further Rashi and Ran in chapter HaOreg explained grasshoppers and locusts are of a trapped species like animals and birds — and seemingly the same for bees. But requires study — I saw Rambam chapter 10 and Kelalei HaChodesh daf 108 wrote grasshoppers and locusts are not of a trapped species; and so R' Yehuda daf 8 wrote; only regarding locusts he wrote fish — it appears to me a scribal error and must say locusts; for otherwise from this it would be clear in Talmud beginning of chapter Eino Tzadin and Rambam and all decisors that fish are of a trapped species. And it appears to me proof for Rashi from Tosefta (Beitza) [Shabbos] chapter 13: "one who traps flies and mosquitoes on Shabbat — liable; R' Yehuda exempts" — until here. It appears they dispute only flies and mosquitoes; but regarding grasshoppers and locusts all agree. But in Yerushalmi chapter Eight Creeping Things I found proper proof for Rambam — see there; but requires study — for in Talmud it says Tosefta: one who traps grasshoppers at dew time exempt, at heat liable — unless they came many together; and even though they wanted to say Rambam holds from the first baraita no distinction and so is the law — nevertheless requires study, for in Tosefta and Yerushalmi it appears explicitly thus, and so implied where the Talmud argues on this baraita:
