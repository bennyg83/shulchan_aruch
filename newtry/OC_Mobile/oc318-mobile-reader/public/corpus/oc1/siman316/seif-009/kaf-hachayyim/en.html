9) There: In which they are trapped, etc. This means that they can be caught with one chase, one can reach them in a single run, and one need not sit and rest in between. So writes the Maggid Mishneh. This is also the measure for creatures that require no further trapping, as stated in siman 497. The Gemara gives another measure: wherever the shadows of the walls meet. Tur and the author omit this measure because it is not uniform, since everything depends upon the height of the walls and width of the house. In the time of the Gemara there was a known standard for this, as Tosafos writes, and the authorities therefore cite only the other measure. All this applies when the windows are closed. If they are open, it obviously is not considered trapping because the bird can fly through the windows. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 2. This refers to a bird. A deer or other animal that cannot fly is considered trapped and one is liable even if the windows are open and the house is unroofed.
