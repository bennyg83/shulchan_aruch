(א) Louse. That is, the black one that jumps; and it breeds and multiplies — we derive from the Mishkan: "rams dyed red" — just as rams breed and multiply, so all that breed and multiply; therefore one is liable for killing a louse. But the white louse does not breed and multiply but comes from human sweat — one is not liable for killing it, only forbidden due to a decree lest one kill a louse — and see in responsa Chavot Yair siman 164:

(ב) Forbidden to trap it. But one is not liable for trapping it since it is a matter whose species is not trapped:

(ג) And it bites him. It is implied that if it is on his flesh and not biting him, or if it is on his shirt inside — forbidden to trap it. But some say permitted to take it from his garments inside — Magen Avraham. And Taz wrote regarding the practical law: whoever can knock it to the ground without taking it by hand easily should do so if it is on his shirt inside; but if he is wearing outer garments and it is hard for him to knock off the louse without first taking it by hand — one who relies on the lenient authorities has not lost; and so we see most of the world do — end of his words, see there:

(ד) To kill it. And one who kills it is liable; and in responsa Chavot Yair siman 164 he forbids throwing it in water — see there:

(ה) He should not kill them. Since lice are found there, they decreed lest he also kill lice — nevertheless it appears to me permitted to throw them in water — Magen Avraham. One should not take lice from fox skins and the like because it detaches from the wool — Shulchan Aruch siman 268; and see siman 340 seif 2 regarding this. At night when one does not know if it is a louse or a flea, he should not kill it — Shulchan Aruch there.
