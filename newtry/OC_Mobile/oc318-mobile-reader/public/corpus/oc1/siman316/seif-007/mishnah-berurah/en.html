(27) And if for the sake of, etc. — namely even in a place where snakes are not accustomed to kill and do not run after him but stand in their place — in this it is forbidden to go kill them, as below in seif 10; nevertheless to trap them is permitted — namely that he turns a vessel over them or ties them; and the reason is that this is melacha she'ein tzricha legufah, for he does not need to trap but that it not put him to sleep; and if he knew it would stand and not harm him — he would not trap.
