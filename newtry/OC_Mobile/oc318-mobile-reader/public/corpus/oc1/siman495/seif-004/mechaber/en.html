Regarding muktza, even though it is permitted on Shabbos, Chazal were more stringent on Yom Tov and prohibited it. REMA: There are those who lenient regarding muktza even on Yom Tov but nolad is prohibited according to this opinion, even on Shabbos. Any muktza which is not an animal, for example, a storehouse of fruit or wood, it is sufficient to declare that one will take from that place on Yom Tov and it is not necessary to mark it.
