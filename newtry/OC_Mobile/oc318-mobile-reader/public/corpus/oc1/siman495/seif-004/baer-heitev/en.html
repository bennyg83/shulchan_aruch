A storehouse. And the same applies to fruits that stand for commerce.
He takes. And according to those who permit muktzeh, one needs a verbal statement only regarding dried figs and raisins, as is written in siman 310, se'if 5. Magen Avraham.
That he mark. See Magen Avraham, who concluded that for fruits that are [partly] fit and [partly] unfit, according to all opinions he must mark and say, "From here until here I will take tomorrow," for there is concern that perhaps he will leave the bad and take the good and carry something unfit, as is written in siman 437, se'if 10; see there.
To prepare. Even though below in siman 503 he ruled that it is forbidden to prepare from one Yom Tov for its fellow, here is different, since one needs only a mere statement, regarding something that is not a living creature (Taz); and so wrote Magen Avraham, that this refers to what is above it, that in a storehouse it is permitted to prepare etc.; see there.
