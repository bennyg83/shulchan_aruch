And they forbade it. Since Yom Tov is lighter [in people's eyes], the Sages were concerned that one not treat it lightly; therefore they were stringent in it to rule like R' Yehudah that muktzeh is forbidden. But on Shabbos, which is more severe, they ruled like R' Shimon that it is permitted.
And it is permitted to prepare, etc. Even though below in siman 503 he ruled that it is forbidden to prepare from one Yom Tov for its fellow, here is different, since one needs only a mere statement, regarding something that is not a living creature.
