See Baer Heitev; and see Responsa Pnei Yehoshua in manuscript, Orach Chaim section, siman 14, regarding the custom on Yom Tov to take honeycombs and dip the piece of the hamotzi bread in them, where one sage wished to forbid it, [holding] that honey from the combs is like liquid that comes out of olives and grapes, which is forbidden even when they stand for eating; and he wrote that this is not so, for certainly to dip the bread is permitted, for it is a liquid that comes for food, and it is even better than that, for it is food that comes for food, as is stated in the Gemara; but without bread there is a leniency, specifically when they are still attached to the hive and one needs etc., as is stated in siman 321 etc.; see there; and see siman 321 and Magen Avraham there.
