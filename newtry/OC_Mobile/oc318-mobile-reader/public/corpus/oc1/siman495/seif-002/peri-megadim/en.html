Harvesting. See Taz. Trapping: even though with one exertion it becomes weekday-like work; see Beit Yosef. But harvesting and grinding: it is difficult why we need the reason that he will refrain from the joy of Yom Tov; without that, one who harvests for many days is [doing so] not for the need of the day, which is forbidden. And one may say the reason of "ho'il" [since]; and see Magen Avraham, letter 4. And baked matzah: see siman 510, letter 9, like the Rashba of blessed memory. Sorting is forbidden on Yom Tov; and see siman 321, se'if 12, that on Shabbos too there is no grinding after grinding; nevertheless in the work of R. Eisen there it is forbidden, and on Yom Tov it is permitted with a vessel; and see Maggid Mishneh, laws of chametz and matzah, chapter 1, law 3, who emends that one who grinds until fine like flour: one may say there is grinding after grinding; see there. If so, on Shabbos by "mitoch" it is forbidden, and Yom Tov is permitted according to the view that grinding is rabbinic, because of concern that he will refrain from the joy of Yom Tov; and in such a case they were not concerned, for it is not the way to increase so much in grinding peppers with a mill; see Beit Yosef. And according to the view that grinding is [forbidden] by "mitoch," this requires further study regarding grinding peppers and matzah and the like; and God willing below it will be explained further.
