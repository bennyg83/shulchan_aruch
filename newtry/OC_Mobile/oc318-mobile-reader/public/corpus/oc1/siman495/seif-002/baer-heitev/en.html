And grinding. The reason is because a person is accustomed to harvest his whole field and pick his whole vineyard as one, and to tread all his grapes as one, and to grind a great amount at once; and the Sages were concerned that if these were permitted on Yom Tov he would harvest his whole field and pick his whole vineyard and tread all his grapes and refrain from the joy of Yom Tov; and likewise they forbade trapping for this reason. And Maharil wrote: on Yom Tov of Pesach it is permitted to grind baked matzah, for there is no grinding after grinding; but one should be careful not to separate large crumbs from small ones, for sorting is forbidden even on Yom Tov; and see siman 510.
