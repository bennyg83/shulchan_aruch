{11} Harvesting, etc.: and the same applies to threshing and sifting and the like.
{12} And squeezing: that is to say, to squeeze the fruits to extract their oil and their wine for eating on Yom Tov.
{13} The Sages forbade them: for according to the Torah every melacha that is for food preparation is permitted; rather the Sages forbade things that are a great labor, such as these: for a person is accustomed to harvest his whole field and pick his whole vineyard as one, and to tread all his grapes as one, and to grind a great amount at one time; and if so he would be occupied all day with his work and refrain from the joy of Yom Tov; therefore the Sages forbade them, and even by means of a change it is forbidden; and they left in their permitted status only things that typically are only for the eating need of Yom Tov. And many poskim disagree and hold that the aforementioned melacha of labor is forbidden according to the Torah even for food preparation, and the Torah permitted for food preparation only what is the way to do for this day, such as from kneading and baking onward and the like, melachos that are for eating need on Yom Tov; and the details of these laws will be explained in the coming simanim, each in its place.
