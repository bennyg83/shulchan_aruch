[2] [Levush] As it is written, 'but only that which,' etc. This was written regarding the Festival of Matzos, where it says simply "every melacha"; but on other Yamim Tovim, where it says "every melacha of labor," there was no need to permit the melacha of food preparation, for it is not called "labor," but rather a melacha of benefit (Ramban). And in Sefer Yereim, siman 115, he answered that we learn the other Yamim Tovim from the Festival of Matzos by the hekesh, as it is written "the Festival of Matzos" and "on the Festival of Shavuos," he equates etc. Further one may say that we learn [it] from a gezeirah shavah of "a sacred convocation."
