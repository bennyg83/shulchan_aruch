Se'if 3: One does not carry out. As is written at the end of chapter 3: "his cow" etc., and "they card" etc. on Yom Tov: it appears that on Yom Tov it refers to both of them. And further, all three matters in which Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov and the Sages disagree are all regarding Yom Tov, not like Tosefos Yom Tov. And they said in the Yerushalmi, chapter 4 of Yom Tov [Beitza], and the Rif and Rosh brought it there: one may not ride etc. on Yom Tov because of resting one's animal; hence he is commanded [regarding his animal] on Yom Tov. And regarding an animal we do not say "mitoch" [since carrying was permitted for need], for [animal rest] does not relate to [personal] need.
