{14} One does not carry a load, etc. — per the Hagah above siman 246 seif 3 that a person is not commanded regarding resting his animal, it must be that here it is only a rabbinic prohibition in general that one not do as on a weekday with his animal, even for Yom Tov needs, even in private domain from place to place is also forbidden; therefore the wording "one does not carry" is not precise. And above in siman 246 we brought the view of those who dispute that a person is commanded regarding resting his animal on Yom Tov as on Shabbat, and all the more when he is strict with another's animal — per this view it fits plainly and the Mechaber states a Torah prohibition. And know: if we say there is resting of an animal on Yom Tov, all the laws stated on this matter above in Shabbat laws siman 305 apply here.
