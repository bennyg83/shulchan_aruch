{14} One does not carry out a load, etc.: according to the view of the gloss above in siman 246, se'if 3, that a person is not commanded regarding resting his animal, one must say that here it is only a mere rabbinic prohibition, so that he not do as he does on a weekday with his animal, even for the need of Yom Tov, and even in a single domain from place to place it is also forbidden; and according to this, the wording "one does not carry out" is not precise. And above in siman 246 we brought the view of those who disagree, that a person is commanded regarding resting his animal on Yom Tov as on Shabbos, and all the more so when he is goading after his animal; and according to this view it fits according to its plain meaning, and the Mechaber means a Torah prohibition. And know: if we say there is resting of an animal on Yom Tov, all the laws stated on this matter above in the laws of Shabbos, siman 305, apply here.
