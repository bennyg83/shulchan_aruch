One does not carry out. One should not explain [this] as because of resting one's animal, for a person himself is permitted to carry out on Yom Tov; rather as the Rambam wrote, it is forbidden so that he not do as he does on a weekday; and see siman 246 at the end of se'if 3, and what is written that one should forbid resting one's animal on Yom Tov, not like the Rama.
