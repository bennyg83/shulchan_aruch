A load. One should not explain [this] as because of resting one's animal, for a person himself is permitted to carry out on Yom Tov; rather the reason is so that he not do as he does on a weekday; and see siman 246, se'if 3, whether one must forbid resting an animal on Yom Tov or not; and see Peri Chadash and Magen Avraham and Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah there; and see Yad Aharon here.
