One does not. See Taz. The Rambam of blessed memory, chapter 5 of the laws of Yom Tov, law 2; and nevertheless some say one may not use living creatures. And behold one may say that resting an animal is permitted on Yom Tov; yet goading [driving] an animal is [still] forbidden by "mitoch"; see Peri Chadash here and in Beit Yosef. And that is a load that is not needed at all on Yom Tov, which for a person himself is forbidden by "mitoch"; the same applies when he goads after an animal; see Magen Avraham, siman 246, letter 12. And the wording of the Mechaber appears, ostensibly, that it is forbidden by "mitoch"; and that is, one may say, [the case of] goading; and see Beit Yosef; in Magen Avraham letter 5 it will be explained further.
