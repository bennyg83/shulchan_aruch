Whatever is forbidden to do, it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew to do it (Beit Yosef; and see the beginning of siman 543). Except for the melacha of food preparation. He intends what is written in siman 521, that it is permitted to lower fruit because of loss, since they are fit for eating; and see in the Gemara.
And some are stringent. And for this reason one should cook dried fruits before Yom Tov, for they are better when cooked from the day before (Shlah).
By means of a change. And it is permitted ideally to wait until Yom Tov to do it with a change (and so it appears in siman 504). Nevertheless Maharil wrote to knead the vermicelli ["verimizlish"] before Yom Tov, for the ones [kneaded] earlier are better; and it is also forbidden to crush almonds on Yom Tov to extract milk from them, since it is possible to do it on erev Yom Tov even though it loses a little flavor; end quote. And even though with other foods, if it loses a little flavor it is permitted, here it is similar to squeezing fruit; and even though it is not similar at all, for there the moisture is stored inside it, but almonds are dry and from much crushing the liquid is squeezed out: nevertheless in common speech the name "milk" is upon them, and it resembles squeezing fruit to make a beverage, and it is forbidden; end quote of Yam shel Shlomo, siman 38; and see siman 506, se'if 8.
