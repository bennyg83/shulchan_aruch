Which melachot are forbidden on Yom Tov. It contains 4 seifim: Every melacha forbidden on Shabbos is forbidden on Yom Tov, except melacha needed for food preparation, carrying, kindling, and preparations for food that could not have been done on the preceding day. {Rama: Some are stringent even regarding food preparation itself when doing it on the eve of Yom Tov would not diminish its taste at all. [Or Zarua; Maharil] Nevertheless, if he did not do it on the eve of Yom Tov and it is needed for Yom Tov, he may do it with a shinui. [Semag and Ran]}
