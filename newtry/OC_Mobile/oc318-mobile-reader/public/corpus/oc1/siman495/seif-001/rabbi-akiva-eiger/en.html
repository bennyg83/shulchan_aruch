Se'if 1. Except for the melacha of food preparation. Whether one may cook at twilight at the departure of Yom Tov in order to eat it at twilight, and likewise to take a coal to kindle tobacco at twilight: I wrote about this in the responsa of Moreinu HaRav Rabbi Chaim Alfandari. In the kuntres Mutsal Me'esh, siman 12, he is uncertain whether it is permitted to cook on Yom Tov in order to eat a coarse, large meal [achilah gasah].
