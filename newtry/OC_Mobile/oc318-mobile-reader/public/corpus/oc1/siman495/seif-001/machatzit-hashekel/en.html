Magen Avraham: Whatever is forbidden, etc. Beit Yosef. From what we learned in Beitza folio 36b: whatever one is liable for because of shevus on Shabbos, one is liable for on Yom Tov; and since they forbade shevus [restrictions] on Yom Tov as on Shabbos, the same applies to telling a non-Jew [to do melacha], for which there is a shevus, which is forbidden on Yom Tov.
And see the beginning of siman 543. That is to say that even on Chol HaMoed it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew [to do] what is forbidden for a Jew; only that the Rama there is somewhat more lenient on Chol HaMoed than on Yom Tov; see there.
