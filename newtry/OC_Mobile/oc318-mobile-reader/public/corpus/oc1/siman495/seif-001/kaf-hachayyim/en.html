1) [Seif 1] Every labor prohibited on Shabbos is prohibited on Yom Tov. The reason, rooted in the mitzvah, is so that the Jewish people will remember the great miracles that the Holy One, blessed is He, performed for them and for their ancestors, speak about them, and make them known to their children and grandchildren. By resting from worldly occupations, they will be free to engage in this. Were labor permitted, even light labor, each person would turn to his occupation, and the honor of the festival would be forgotten by children and adults alike. There are also many other benefits to resting: all the people assemble in synagogues and batei midrash to hear words of Torah, and the leaders of the people guide them and teach them knowledge. This resembles the Sages’ statement that Moshe instituted for the Jewish people that they expound the laws of Pesach on Pesach and the laws of Atzeres on Atzeres. Sefer HaChinuch, mitzvah 315; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1.
