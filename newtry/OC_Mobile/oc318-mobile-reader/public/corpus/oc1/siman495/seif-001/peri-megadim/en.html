And except. See Taz; and see what I wrote in the introduction. And behold, in [siman] 518 in Magen Avraham he lengthened [discussion]. And if we say that carrying out and kindling, by "mitoch," were permitted entirely, and only by rabbinic law are they forbidden when there is no need at all: see Levush; and the meaning here would be "except for carrying out and kindling," as its plain sense. And according to this, apparently one need not [make] an eruv chatzeiros on Yom Tov, for it would be a decree upon a decree; and see Magen Avraham, siman 518, letters 3 and 5. And to say that we apply "mitoch" only regarding carrying out and kindling, but regarding others not: and that case of siman 511, and Beitza 21b, hot water for his feet, is not because of "mitoch," but rather kindling is permitted by "mitoch," and there is no cooking there, for hot water for his feet is not yad soledes bo, and there is no cooking; and see Yoreh De'ah, siman 105, in Shach, letter 5, and in the laws of Shabbos; and this is forced. And it appears that in any case regarding cooking we say "mitoch"; see Magen Avraham, siman 518; and the meaning would be "except for carrying out and kindling," even though they are not themselves food preparation, all the more so cooking etc.; and see Magen Avraham; and the matters are lengthy; and God willing in [simanim] 501 and 518 I will explain further on this. And see Olas Shabbos.
