If it was given with no specification, this is considered as if they said "on the condition that you return it to me," because we assume that with this intention it was given because it is needed for the obligation and they have no other. If it is not returned, the obligation is not fulfilled. That said, even if one did not give it back directly but rather to another and another to another and the last person gives it back to the owners, the obligation is fulfilled.Rema: One must return and give it back to its owner as a gift so that the owners will have it to use for the obligation (Ros"h and Rabbeinu Yerucham 8:4).] ומיהו אפי' לא החזירו לידו אלא לאחר ואחר לאחר והאחרון מחזירו לבעלים יצא:
