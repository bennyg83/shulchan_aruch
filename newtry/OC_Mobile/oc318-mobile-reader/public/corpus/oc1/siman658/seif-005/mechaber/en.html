If it was given to him without any explicit condition, it is as though the giver said, 'On condition that you return it to me,' because it is presumed that he gave it with that intention, since he himself needs it to fulfill the mitzvah and has no other. If the recipient did not return it, he did not fulfill the mitzvah. {Rama: He must give it back to its owner as a gift, so that it again belongs to the owner and the owner can fulfill the mitzvah with it. [Rosh and Rabbeinu Yeruham, netiv 8, part 4]} Nevertheless, even if he did not return it directly to the owner's hand but gave it to another person, who gave it to another, and the last person returned it to the owner, he has fulfilled his obligation.
