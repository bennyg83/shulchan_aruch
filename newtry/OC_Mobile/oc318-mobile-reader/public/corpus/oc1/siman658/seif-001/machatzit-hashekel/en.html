(s.k. 1) That one should smell. See siman 653 that one should smell etc. — to resolve that it is explained there one should not smell etrog because of doubt in blessing. On this Magen Avraham wrote: on Shabbat he concludes from Magen Avraham above that one may smell and bless on it according to all. If so he can bless etc. — meaning even if not like Magen Avraham there, perhaps on Shabbat poskim disagree whether one blesses on it; nevertheless he can fulfill through another device: bless on another fragrant species and intend to include the etrog in his blessing; therefore it is not muktzeh.
