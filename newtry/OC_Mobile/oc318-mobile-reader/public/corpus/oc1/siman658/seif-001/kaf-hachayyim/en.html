1) [Seif 1] According to the Torah, the mitzvah of lulav outside the Beit HaMikdash applies only on the first day, etc. For it is written, "You shall rejoice before Hashem your God for seven days," and this refers to the Beit HaMikdash; but throughout the rest of Eretz Yisrael it applies only on the first day, as it is written, "on the first day." The Sages instituted seven days as a remembrance of the Beit HaMikdash. Sukkah 41a. Tur and Beit Yosef. Levush. Taz, s.k. 1.
