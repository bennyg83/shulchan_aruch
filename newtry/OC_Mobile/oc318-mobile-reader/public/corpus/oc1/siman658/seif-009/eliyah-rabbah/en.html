[9] Levush: He must give it, etc. Meaning, even if the first recipient ultimately returns it to the owner, and even if the owner explicitly said, 'on condition that you return it to me,' each recipient must give it as a gift to the next, as is also implied by Rabbeinu Yerucham. Darchei Moshe writes in the name of Ri that if one gave an esrog as a gift and said, 'after you, it shall belong to so-and-so,' the first recipient fulfills the mitzvah even if he ate or sold it, because the later recipient acquires only what the first leaves over. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 248.
