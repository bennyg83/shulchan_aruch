Exempt. The Taz wrote that nowadays, when our women recite berachos, they are obligated to contribute to this according to their wealth; and so wrote the Magen Avraham; see there. One who is not in the city need not contribute toward the esrog. Magen Avraham; see there.
Properly. For most people do not know how to transfer ownership to their fellows, and also how to perform the shakings according to halachah. A person who has his own esrog, while in another city they have none at all, is better off sending it there, and he should recite the berachah over the communal one. Matteh Moshe, Mitzvos; and see Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 114, and Birkei Yosef.
