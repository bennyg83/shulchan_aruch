A woman is exempt. If she wishes to recite the blessing, she may. One who is not in the city need not contribute. This is unlike the quorum discussed in siman 55, because there they need him to complete the minyan and he himself also requires the minyan, so he cannot separate himself. It is also unlike a lechi, korah, or other communal needs, where each person requires the mitzvah equally. An esrog, however, is assessed according to wealth even when both people are in the city. Where one is not in the city, there is no basis to require a per-capita contribution (Maharil).
The mitzvah properly. Because most people do not know how to transfer ownership to one another, and also do not know how to perform the wavings correctly (Maharil). If a person owns a private esrog and another city has none at all, it is preferable that he send his esrog there and recite the blessing upon the communal esrog in his own city (Mateh Moshe, gloss in Maharai Katz, Mitzvos).
