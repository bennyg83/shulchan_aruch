[2] And it is forbidden, etc. The reason it may be placed in flax tow is that the tow absorbed the fragrance before Yom Tov (Maharil), since it does not become more fragrant through this act (Magen Avraham, end of siman 511). This is not conclusive. Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah writes that if it was wrapped in flax tow before Yom Tov, it may be wrapped on Yom Tov in a clean cloth with the tow intervening. This does not appear correct, for if the fragrance passes through the tow into the cloth, he is still creating a new fragrance.
