On Shabbat — Taz Sukkah 43a: likewise lest he take from private to public; even for those who today have no public domain, the decree stands in its place — see 588 Taz 5 and Magen Avraham 4. See Tosafot 42b s.v. tilul, Rashi and Chiddushei HaRim: lulav is muktzeh though fit for mitzvah — not a vessel, like a bundle of reeds not overridden by "rest"; therefore on other days in rabbinic-era lands they decreed muktzeh on Shabbat, except Temple or Yom Tov 1 — why see there. One could say: what Magen Avraham wrote in 651:28 from Yerushalmi — muktzeh after fulfilling does not apply, there is no muktzeh for mitzvah, for tamid is always muktzeh; rather they did not learn even in the land on other days remembering the Temple; nevertheless after fulfilling it is muktzeh. Tree branches they designated for waving are vessels — unlike lulav forbidden in benefit — see Shabbat 123; requires some study.
