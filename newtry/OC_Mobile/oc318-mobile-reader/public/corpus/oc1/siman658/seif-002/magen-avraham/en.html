(א) Dare to smell. Dr. R. R. D. R. D. A.D. can bless another fruit:

(ב) That it creates scent. Yesh Seder in Beitzah 2:34 wrote: one might wonder — forbidden only to place the cup to finish as in 511, therefore he mentioned barrels usually finished; but where he does not intend to finish it is permitted, for psik reisha does not apply except to complete melacha; finishing is not melacha, only extinguishing — end of his words. Meaning what they said on 23: even on pottery forbidden because it creates scent on pottery, forbidden even without intent because of psik reisha — there is anyway kindling which is complete melacha; but to create scent without intent is permitted as above. Nevertheless I think Maharil and Rokeach are primary: there is no prohibition of kindling there, for it is for food need; nevertheless forbidden because it creates scent on pottery — therefore Maharil and Rokeach are primary. If it was on the garment from yesterday it is permitted to place on Yom Tov — see siman 322 s.k. 5.
