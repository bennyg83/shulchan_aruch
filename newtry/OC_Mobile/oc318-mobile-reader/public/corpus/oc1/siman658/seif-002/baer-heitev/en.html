Like a stone. In siman 588:5 the Rema brought regarding shofar that it is prohibited to move it except for use of its body or place. If so, here too it is permitted to move the lulav for use of its body or place. Taz.
For it is fit. That which is stated in siman 653, that one should refrain from smelling it, is because there the reason is that he does not know whether to recite a berachah. Consequently, if he has before him another thing fit for reciting the fragrance blessing over it, then he should recite the berachah over that item and afterward he may smell this too. Therefore it is not muktzeh. Taz.
