2. And it is prohibited. From what is stated in Sukkah 42a: according to the one who says, 'since,' etc. See Rashi there, s.v. 'kamashma lan'; this is not so here, for it is not fit even for men. And there, and Shabbos 131a, a verse was needed to permit moving it; see Rashi, s.v. 'itztarich,' etc. In the Tosefta at the end of chapter 2, R. Yehudah says: if the first Yom Tov of the festival occurred on Shabbos, once he has fulfilled his obligation with it, it is prohibited to move it. So too in the Yerushalmi at the end of chapter 3: if he placed it on the ground, it is prohibited to move it. R. Zerika said: this teaches that it is prohibited for benefit, for if not, it has the status of a utensil, as hadas is for smelling, and likewise all of them. This is what it says: but the esrog, etc.; and as I wrote above, siman 653.
And it is prohibited, etc. As written at the end of chapter 2 of Yom Tov: overturning a cup, etc.
