Seif 2: On Shabbat one does not take it even on the first day. Note: In the Gemara the reason is explained lest one carry it four cubits in public domain. Tosafot asked why it does not say lest one take it from private to public domain. In my Orach Chaim work on Mishnah Torah siman 471, responsum to Krakow community, in the sugya miggo it is a partition etc., I brought that Yom Tov and Shabbat are positive and negative commandments; nevertheless for carrying out, which is a weak melacha, it is not in the category of real melacha and is not excluded by "rest," but only a negative prohibition of not doing. If so, one could say: even if he takes it out, what of it — positive overrides negative; and though not at the time, one could say it is a public mitzvah — see Magen Avraham 266. Also those who hold preparatory mitzvot count at the time — see there at length. Therefore it is reasonable to say: even if he takes it from private to public, what harm — positive and negative; this fits what was chosen "lest one carry four cubits." Clear proof for our words that carrying is only negative — end of his words.
