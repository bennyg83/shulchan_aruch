On Shabbos he does not take it. This is a decree lest he go to an expert to learn and carry it four amos in the public domain.
And it is prohibited to move the lulav. In siman 588 the Rema brought regarding shofar that it is prohibited to move it except for use of its body or place. If so, here too it is permitted to move the lulav for use of its body or place.
The esrog is permitted. For it is fit to smell. This is difficult, for in siman 653 he wrote that one should refrain from smelling it. One may say that the main prohibition there is because he does not know what to do about the berachah, for some say not to recite a berachah. Consequently, if he has before him another item fit for reciting the fragrance blessing over it, he should recite the berachah over that item and can afterward smell this one as well. Therefore it is not muktzeh.
