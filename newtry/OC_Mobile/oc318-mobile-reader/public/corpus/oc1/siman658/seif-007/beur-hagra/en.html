Seif 7: Specifically that, etc. As written in seif 9: what, etc. The Maggid Mishneh wrote in the name of the Rashba: since they knew at the time that it was not fit to be divided, and they acquired it with that in mind, we say there is retroactive clarification; at the time he takes it to fulfill the obligation, it is entirely his. This is like partners who vowed not to benefit from one another, who may enter a courtyard that is not subject to division, because we say there is retroactive clarification: when they acquired it, they did so with this understanding, that at the time each of them enters, the place of his walking is his. So too with lulav and the like, and no transfer of ownership is required. In Bava Kamma 51b, the Rashba explained in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel: from when he hands him his bucket. He took it, and the matter became clarified from when he handed him the bucket, that the pit stands in his domain and he draws from his own. This is what it says there: and they follow their own reasoning, etc., as written in Nedarim, that they dispute where it is not subject to division, and we say that the matter is clarified at the time he uses it, that all of it is his.
