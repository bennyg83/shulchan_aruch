None of them, etc. Because they are particular with one another, as stated in seif 8. They purchased it with the understanding that each share would be regarded as selected for its owner, relying upon the principle of bereirah, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 226 (Maggid Mishneh in the name of Rashba). In Responsa Rashba, siman 62, he writes that he stated this as a matter of law but not for practical ruling. It therefore appears proper to announce that each person should give his share to the others as a gift, because siman 226 contains many opinions on this matter. See Beit Yosef there and what I wrote at the end of this siman.
