Seif 8: The brothers, etc., and although, etc. This is the Rambam's language, according to the version in the Gemara there.
Because anything that is not, etc. The Maggid Mishneh there. This is the Ran's wording: and if you say, even if there are other esrogim there, who divided it for him, as written in chapter 5 of Bava Metzia? One can say that here it speaks of brothers who are not particular with one another, and even if this one took the beautiful one, they are not particular about it. But when they have only a quince or a pomegranate, they are certainly particular, for they want to fulfill the mitzvah just like him.
