And a woman. See the Taz. See siman 17:2 and Taz, letter 3; see there, that since women recite a berachah over lulav, they are obligated to contribute to this. In Sukkah 37b, according to R. Yochanan one moves it outward and inward for the One to whom the four directions belong, and according to R. Yose bar Chanina, to stop harmful dews. If so, she too benefits from the men taking the lulav, and therefore she should contribute to this. In siman 651 we wrote that the shakings we perform are according to R. Yose bar Chanina, and so it appears in the Gemara; if so, the woman should also contribute to this. That is if she is a widow or her husband is not in the city; but if her husband is in the city, he alone should contribute. If there is a custom that everyone who recites a berachah over the esrog gives esrog money, she too must give, together with her husband. See Magen Avraham, letter 11, and with Hashem's help it will be explained further there.
