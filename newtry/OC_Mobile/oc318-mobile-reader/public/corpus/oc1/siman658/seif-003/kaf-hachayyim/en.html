3) Ibid., gloss: It is forbidden to move the lulav on Shabbos, etc. For use of its body or its place, it is permitted, as with a shofar at the end of siman 588. Taz, s.k. 3. Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. However, Maamar Mordechai, os 1, wrote that from the language of the poskim, who wrote that it is like a stone, it is implied that even for use of its body or its place it is forbidden, according to the law of all muktzeh machmas gufo. He expressed astonishment at the words of the aforementioned Taz, who brought proof from a shofar, for a shofar is different, since it is a utensil whose primary use is forbidden; therefore it is permitted to move it for use of its body or its place, according to the law of a utensil whose primary use is forbidden. A lulav, however, is forbidden according to all opinions; see there. So too wrote Mishbetzos Zahav, os 3; Nehar Shalom, os 1; Erekh HaShulchan, os 2; Bigdei Yesha; Beit Meir; Chemed Moshe, os 2; Machatzis HaShekel, s.k. 2; Chayei Adam, klal 66, os 13; Mishna Berurah, os 4. So too I wrote above, siman 308, os 51; see there.
