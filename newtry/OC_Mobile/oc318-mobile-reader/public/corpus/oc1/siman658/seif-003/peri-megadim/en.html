And it is prohibited. See the Taz. I did not understand, for a shofar on Shabbos was not set aside for the mitzvah; see Magen Avraham 588. This is not so with a lulav on Shabbos during the seven days, and perhaps even in a year when the first Yom Tov occurs on Shabbos, it was set aside for all seven. It is prohibited only for use of its body and place? Only a utensil whose work is prohibited was permitted for its place; but stones are prohibited even for use of their body and place, unless he designated them for this. See siman 308 in Magen Avraham, letter 12. This requires investigation.
