There Explanation: that which we say in Beitzah 23 regarding the dispute about incense, it says there that to smoke fruits with fragrant smoke so they absorb the fragrance, Rav prohibits, because it is not called food preparation, since it is not something equal for every person but only for delicate people. It also contains a primary melachah, extinguishing and kindling, for at first he extinguishes the coals when he places the spice powder on them, and afterward he kindles them. Rav Yehudah said that specifically on a coal it is prohibited, but on a heated earthenware shard it is permitted. Rashi explained that there is no extinguishing, and the kindling is backhanded, through an unusual manner, and there is no Torah prohibition. Tosafos explained that he holds that since kindling was permitted for the need of food, etc. Rabbah said: even on an earthenware shard it is also prohibited, because he creates a fragrance in the shard. End quote. This person does not intend to create a fragrance in the shard, and nevertheless it is prohibited because it is an inevitable consequence.
