(There, seif 3.) And if he gave it to him as a gift, it is permitted. Note: Regarding the law whether a gift for a time helps with an esrog, see the Talmud in Bava Kamma 68 regarding 'excluding when he transferred ownership for thirty days.' It is proven there that a sale for a time is not called a sale at all, and the same applies to a gift. See Rashi there, who wrote that it is only like mere rental. This requires investigation regarding those who hold the opposite. Examine this carefully.
