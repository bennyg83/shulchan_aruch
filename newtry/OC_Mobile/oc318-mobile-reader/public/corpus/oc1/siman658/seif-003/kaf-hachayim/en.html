(3) There [Hagahah]. It is forbidden to move the lulav on Shabbat etc.; for bodily need and its place it is permitted as with shofar in siman 588. Taz s.k. 3. Acharonim note 1. But Maamer Mordechai 1 wrote: from commentators' language that it is like a stone implies even for bodily need and place it is forbidden like all muktzeh machmat gufo; he wondered at Taz who brought proof from shofar — shofar is a vessel though its melacha is forbidden, therefore permitted to move for need like a vessel whose melacha is forbidden; but lulav all forbid — see there. So wrote Mishnat Hasidim 3, Nehar Shalom, Or HaRim 2, Bigdei Yesha, Beit Meir, Chayei Adam 66:13, Mishna Berurah 4; and above siman 308 note 51 — see there.
