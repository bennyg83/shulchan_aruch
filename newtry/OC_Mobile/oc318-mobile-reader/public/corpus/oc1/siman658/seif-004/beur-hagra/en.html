Seif 4: Even if he gave him, etc. Although Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel and the Sages disagree regarding a cloak, and the Sages agree in other places that returning money is considered a return, as stated there because it is meant to distress him, etc., here everyone agrees that it is not a return, as written in Bava Basra: brothers who bought an esrog, if one can, etc.
And even if it was taken from him by unavoidable accident. As it says there regarding a cloak that was lost, etc. Just as there, since he intended specifically to distress him, his statement was precise and this does not help, so too wherever his statement is precise it does not help, and all the more so here.
And similarly, etc. For we assess his intent as specifically the time of its mitzvah. See Rashbam in Bava Basra 137b, s.v. 've'im lav,' etc., and there, 137b, that he referred to something fit for him. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. So too the Rashba wrote in siman 1000, and as written in the Gemara there.
And it is permitted, etc. See Tosafos there, 134a, s.v. 'kol matanah,' etc.; 'and if you say,' etc.; and according to Ri it appears that it is not, etc. And in Sukkah there, s.v. 'heilach,' 'and if you say, since,' etc.; and further, etc.
