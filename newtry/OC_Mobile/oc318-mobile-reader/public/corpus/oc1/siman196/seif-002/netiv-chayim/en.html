(there) And further in siman 649 Bach is uncertain in the name of Ran whether to bless; if so for birkat hamazon we hold stringently since it is d'oraisa—as it should read; and see the same in chapter 6 of matzah, etc.—as it should read. And it appears to me I do not understand—for if so with stolen matzah too one fulfills b'dieved, for he acquired through change; and proof from siman 649 is nothing—for there he acquired through change without support of the mitzvah, unlike here where the change comes through support of the mitzvah and it is mitzvah coming through transgression; and according to everyone one does not bless—and understand well.
