(5) Forbidden item—even if he ate a d'oraisa prohibition in a place of danger such as due to illness; and the reason is since it is danger the Torah permitted him to eat, and on the contrary he fulfills a mitzvah to save his soul, as it is written "and live by them"—and they expounded "and live by them" and not die by them.
