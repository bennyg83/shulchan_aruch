And the non-Kohen—Taz in Arakhin 2: Kohanim, Levites, and Israelites are obligated in zimun—lest you say a non-Kohen does not eat terumah and kodashim, Kohanim can eat with a non-Kohen—if not; and Taz added that it is not d'oraisa that it is possible through asking; and it may be it refers to three under vows of benefit—and see Tosafot Arakhin 3a who wrote terumah one can ask; and that is after it came to a Kohen's hand.
