And the non-kohen. See Taz. A Baraisa in Arachin teaches that kohanim, leviim, and Yisraelim are obligated in zimun. One might have thought that because a non-kohen may not eat terumah or sacrificial meat, kohanim can join a non-kohen only where they can eat ordinary food together. Taz adds that one should not say terumah can be permitted through annulment of the designation. It is possible that the teaching refers to three people prohibited by vows from benefiting from one another. See Tosafos, Arachin 4a, who write that terumah cannot be undone once it has reached the kohen. I later saw that Eliyah Rabbah and Peri Megadim, part 2, siman 7, raised this issue from Nedarim 59a. This requires study.
