One does not invite zimun, etc. In the Mishnah they say: until where does one invite zimun? Until a kezayit. Rashi explained: how much should he eat with them and become obligated with them in zimun; and there is practical difference to discharge them if they give him the cup to bless, end of his words; and if so this is what is written afterward siman 199 that the blesser should be one of the bread-eaters.
