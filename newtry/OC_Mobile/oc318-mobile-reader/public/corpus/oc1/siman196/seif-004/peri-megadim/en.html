They do not. See Taz and Rashi, Berachos 45a. Perhaps Rashi was troubled because according to the Rosh, for joining zimun even an olive-sized portion suffices, as in siman 197. He therefore explains that an olive-sized portion of bread is required specifically for discharging others. Alternatively, otherwise the ruling would not belong here, for it should simply state how much food obligates one in Bircas HaMazon, without reference to zimun. If so, the Mechaber’s rulings are difficult: in siman 197:2 he writes that the one leading must be among those who ate bread, while in siman 184:6 he rules that an olive-sized portion obligates Bircas HaMazon. One may answer that the Mechaber explains the Mishnah literally: “How much food is required for joining zimun? An olive-sized portion.” One might otherwise have thought that even less than an olive-sized portion of bread joins; the Mishnah therefore teaches otherwise.
