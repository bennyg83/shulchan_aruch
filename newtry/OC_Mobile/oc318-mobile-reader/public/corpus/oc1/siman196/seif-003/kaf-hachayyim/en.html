3) There: One ate a prohibited item, etc. See Taz, paragraph 1, who concludes that one who unintentionally ate a prohibited item should recite the concluding blessing; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2, cites this and writes that one may rely upon it because many halachic authorities hold that blessings are recited over prohibited foods; see there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1, rules likewise. Responsa Olas Shmuel, siman 3, however, challenges Taz and writes that even one who unintentionally ate a prohibited item does not recite a blessing; see there. Responsa Ksav Sofer, siman 22, similarly discusses this at length and concludes as practical halachah that even one who ate it unintentionally does not recite a blessing; see there.
