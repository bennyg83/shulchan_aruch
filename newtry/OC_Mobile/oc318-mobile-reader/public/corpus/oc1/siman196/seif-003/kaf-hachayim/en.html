(3) There—one who ate a forbidden item, etc. See Taz s.k. 1 who ruled that one who eats forbidden food b'shogeg blesses afterward—see there; and they brought Rabbi Akiva Eiger ot 2 and wrote one may rely on him since many poskim hold one blesses on forbidden foods—see there. And so R' Zalman ot 1; however in responsum Olah Shemuel siman 3 he challenged Taz and wrote that even if he ate forbidden food b'shogeg he does not bless—see there. And so in responsum Katav Sofer siman 22 he expanded on this and concludes for the law that even one who eats b'shogeg does not bless—see there.
