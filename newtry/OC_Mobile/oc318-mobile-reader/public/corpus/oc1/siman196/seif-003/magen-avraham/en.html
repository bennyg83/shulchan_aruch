Rabbeinu Yosef wrote the same regarding one who steals wheat and grinds and bakes—even though he acquired through change he does not bless neither at the beginning nor at the end; and so responsum 649; and so from the words of Rama siman 454 and siman 11 section 6; however in Rambam Laws of Matzah chapter 6 law 7 one does not fulfill with stolen matzah—this is the rule: anything one blesses birkat hamazon on one fulfills his obligation, and anything one does not bless birkat hamazon on one does not fulfill; and he wrote the same that if he acquired through change he fulfills—if so it is proven they bless birkat hamazon; and so one should instruct, for birkat hamazon is d'oraisa and we go stringently; and it is possible even one who stole bread blesses birkat hamazon, for when he ate it he already acquired through change; and further in siman 649 he was uncertain whether to bless; if so for birkat hamazon we hold stringently since it is d'oraisa as mentioned; and see chapter 6 of matzah in explanation of Yerushalmi—not like Bach. It is difficult—for Yoreh Deah siman 112 chapter Darkei permits eating bread with them because of enmity; and one could say there specifically when they eat in one group enmity applies, but here each eats from his loaf—as is explicit there. Agudah wrote chapter 3 they ate: one eats cheese and two meat—they combine, for the cheese-eater can eat with the others; and so in Arakhin in the name of Semak; and so Rokeach laws of Berachot siman 334; and on Shabbat on which 9th of Av falls they do not invite zimun together those eating meat and those careful from meat and eating cheese, since none can eat with his fellow (Agudah chapter 4 of Taanit); and Hagah Maimoni challenged—for behold they can eat bread together, end of his words; and so Rashba on Semag explained they can invite zimun together; and it is difficult to be lenient against all these Geonim—one could say he holds they need to eat together that same bread his fellow eats—for otherwise Kohanim and non-Kohen could also eat other bread as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Agur; rather we require that same bread, and here they cannot eat meat and cheese from one bread since it is soiled with meat as Yoreh Deah siman 89; and so Rokeach and Semak—they do not combine unless the cheese-eater can eat meat; and so Hagah Maimoni in the name of Maharash: two eat meat and one milk—the milk-eater blesses and discharges the other because he can eat with his fellow and not the reverse (gloss in small Mordechai, end of his words)—it implies specifically milk; but if he eats hard cheese they do not combine, for the custom now is not to eat meat after cheese; and on Shabbat on which 9th of Av falls even with milk they do not combine; and there is practical difference at a mitzvah meal; and from Hagah Maimoni's words it seems even if many they cannot invite zimun together, for one does not discharge his fellow and each group must invite zimun separately—and who will be lenient against all these great ones; and even Hagah Maimoni did not disagree but challenged them, and every difficulty has an answer; therefore one should not be lenient; and so is common speech.
