Three who ate as one: one is careful from gentile bread and one is not careful, or one of them is a Kohen and eats challah—even though the careful one cannot eat with the one who is not careful, and an Israelite cannot with the Kohen—since the one who is not careful can eat with the careful one, and a Kohen with an Israelite, they combine. But if Kohanim and a non-Kohen ate as one and the Kohanim eat challah and are careful from gentile bread while the non-Kohen eats gentile bread—they do not combine. And the same applies to three who vowed benefit from each other that they do not combine for zimun.And specifically when each eats from his own loaf; but if they eat from the host's loaf they combine, for behold they eat from one loaf (Avraham Zalman).
