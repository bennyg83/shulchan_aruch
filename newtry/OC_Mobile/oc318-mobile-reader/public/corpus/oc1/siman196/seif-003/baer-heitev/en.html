They combine. Agudah wrote chapter 3 "they ate": one eats cheese and two eat meat—they combine, for the one eating cheese can eat with the others. And on Shabbat on which the 9th of Av falls (within it) one does not invite zimun together those eating meat and those careful from meat and eating cheese, since none of them can eat with his fellow, end of his words. And Hagahot Minhagim challenged on this—for behold they can eat bread together; and so Rashi on Semag wrote they can invite zimun together. And so is the view of Taz—see there. Magen Avraham wrote one could say he holds they need to be able to eat together that same bread his fellow eats—and here they cannot eat meat and cheese from one bread, for it is soiled with meat as written in Yoreh Deah siman 89; and so Rokeach siman 65—that they do not combine unless the one eating cheese can eat meat; and so Hagah Maimoni in the name of Maharash: two eat meat and one milk—the one eating milk blesses and discharges the second because he can eat with his fellow and not the reverse—it implies specifically milk; but if he eats hard cheese they do not combine, for the custom now is not to eat meat after cheese; and on Shabbat on which 9th of Av falls (within it) even with milk they do not combine; and there is practical difference at a mitzvah meal; and so is the custom—see Magen Avraham. And Baer Heitev before me wrote on this: this matter has no end, etc.; and I do not know why—and see Yad Ephraim. (And see in Eliyah Rabbah who expanded on this, see there).
