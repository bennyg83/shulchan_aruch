But if a Kohen and a non-Kohen ate as one and the Kohen eats and is careful, etc.—this is the view of Mordechai at the beginning of "three who ate" in the name of Maharam; and so too in Hagahot Maimoniot chapter 5 of Berachot. But the Agur wrote in the name of Shibolei Leket that even so they combine for zimun, since if it happened to them at their meal with ordinary bread of Israel both can eat together; and the view of Shulchan Aruch is primary, end of his words.
