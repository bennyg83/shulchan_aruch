(1) (Levush) One who steals an item, etc. And Bach rules regarding one who steals or robs wheat and grinds and makes it into bread—he is obligated to bless at the beginning and end, for he acquired through change; but one who steals bread or baked matzah is forbidden to bless on it at the beginning, but at the end he may bless—and see Magen Avraham that for the law his conclusion too is thus.
