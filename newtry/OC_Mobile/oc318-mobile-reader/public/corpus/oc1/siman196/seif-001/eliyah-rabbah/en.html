[Levush] One who steals an item, etc. The Bach rules that if one stole or robbed wheat, ground it, and made it into bread, he must recite both the first and final blessings because he acquired it through a change. If he stole already baked bread or matzah, he may not recite the first blessing over it, but after eating he may recite the final blessing. See Magen Avraham, whose practical conclusion is likewise.
