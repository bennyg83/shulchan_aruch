D'rabbanan. See Baer Heitev s.k. 1; see under responsum Be'er Eshek; and the inquirer there—the reason is that the decree of stam yeinam is later than the enactment of blessings in the days of Ezra, and he compares it to Megillat Taanit where we say his vow preceded our decree; but in responsum Beit Yehudah he refutes this proof and also challenged him on the essence of the law.
