Seif 1—even though, etc. 47b.

And one does not, etc. As written in the first chapter of Demai: demai, etc.—they bless on it; but on other [forbidden foods] they do not bless; and as written in Mezamnin; and in Tosefta chapter 2 of Demai: one said to his fellow, give me this loaf and I will eat it; this wine and I will drink it—they do not give him, for one does not feed pure foods to an am ha'aretz. If he was under a vow from the loaf and they said give me and I will eat it—a watermelon and they pierced it and said give me and I will eat it; wine and it was uncovered and they said give me and I will drink it—they do not give, for one does not feed a person something forbidden to him. Similarly a Jew may not hand a limb from a live animal to a ben Noach, nor a cup of wine to a nazir—for one does not feed a person something forbidden to him; and on all of these one does not bless, one does not invite zimun, and one does not answer amen after them. And in the first chapter of Sanhedrin and in the last chapter of Bava Kamma: behold one who stole a se'ah, etc.—this one does not bless, etc.; and in the first chapter of tractate Challah in Yerushalmi it teaches: stolen matzah is forbidden to bless on it, etc.; and see Beit Yosef.
