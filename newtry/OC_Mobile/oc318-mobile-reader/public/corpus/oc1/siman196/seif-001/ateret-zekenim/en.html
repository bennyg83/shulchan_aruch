Even though it is not [d'oraisa], etc. From this we learn that those who do not separate challah from their dough—even though it is only d'rabbanan—there is no zimun among them (Rashba).

And one does not bless on him, etc. Even without zimun; and the same applies to three who are under a vow of benefit from each other; but if one of them was under a vow of benefit from the two, they combine—for although he is not fit to eat with them, they are fit to eat with him. And so Tosafot wrote in the first chapter of Eruvin.
