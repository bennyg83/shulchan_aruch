(Sk. 6) To happen, etc., and the like, he wrote in the Pesach Haggadah as explained in column 3, TP Damarinan in Pesachim, cup 4, saying the blessing of the song over him. And the interpretation of the blessing of the song. R. Yehuda says praise be and seals with praise. And R. Yochanan, the soul of every living being is praised. And the Rashbam wrote, because it was not a priest, nor a priest, Abdi', as Tarvayahu, and they seal it in Hallel with the King of Hallel in praise. And he says the Great Hallel and Neshem 28 and seals it a second time with the king of praises. and Rabbi Haim Cohen. There was no seal in the space of Halleluch in siman 20, but there was a seal after the soul of siman 28. Because why would B sign twice in one matter Akal.
