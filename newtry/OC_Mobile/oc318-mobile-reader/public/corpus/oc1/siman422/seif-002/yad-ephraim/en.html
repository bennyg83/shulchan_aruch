There s.k. 4: even on Shabbat and Yom Tov, etc. Gloss: perhaps he means on the second day of Yom Tov when he already said the Yom Tov prayer three times; for otherwise Rosh Chodesh is more regular, for it comes every learned day — unlike Yom Tov prayer where between Yom Tov and Yom Tov there is much [gap].
