(2) There: and one says Yaaleh veYavo, etc. There are places accustomed to say Yaaleh veYavo in Kaddish like "Atah chonenanu" and like "Vetodeanu" — whether to mention Rosh Chodesh or for the community that is not accustomed to it; and it is a good custom and one need not another custom; and to interrupt that the shaliach tzibur say before beginning the Amidah [Rosh Chodesh] — and the custom is the psalm "Barechi nafshi" before Arvit and within the zemirot it helps that remembrance follows remembrance so he not forget to mention Rosh Chodesh — Rokeach letter 1; however what is written "and within the zemirot" — this custom is not among us to interrupt between zemirot to say the psalm "Barechi nafshi"; and the custom of the pious of Beit El Yakov who follow the Ari z"l — also on moedim they do not interrupt between zemirot to say the psalm of the moed; and so on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur they do not add to say any psalm except the psalms of Shabbat specifically. And what is written "and one need not interrupt that the shaliach tzibur say," etc. — this is already explained above siman 236 seif 2; and in our words there letters 16 and 15 we took from there.
