(א) Seif 2: whether individual or congregation. That is, even though they said there and in Erchin 10 and in Berakhot — on days when the individual does not complete Hallel — an individual is as written in Taanit: one reason is that they skip by leaps; and furthermore we learn: even though they said there an individual should not begin — that he need not begin — nevertheless one may recite since they practiced; and as Tosafot wrote in Berakhot 14a s.v. days; and wrote there that even the congregation is not obligated except because of custom.

(ב) They bless on it at the beginning. Sukkah 38a.

(ג) To recite. Since they do not complete — Tosafot cited above.

(ד) And if he blessed. There — from what Rav did not understand in Taanit from the blessing.

(ה) And at the end. Sukkah there; and in Yerushalmi Tosafot cited above brought it.

(ו) And the individual does not. From the Gemara of Taanit cited above — an individual should not begin; and since there is no obligation he does not bless "asher tzivanu"; and Tosafot wrote in the name of R' Chananel that the meaning of "should not begin" is with a blessing. In Erchin there s.v. eighteen, etc.

(ז) And there are those who say even. There they said: the custom of their ancestors is in their hands; and by custom we do not bless — as written in Sukkah 38b.

(ח) And there are those who say also. Tosafot cited above — from what we say on days when the individual does not complete: even in the middle of the chapter, etc. — it implies that on another matter he should not interrupt, as written in Shulchan Aruch; and if without a blessing, what interruption applies? And furthermore from Rav there: he saw them skipping by leaps — why did he not notice they did not bless? And what Rashi wrote — that by custom we do not bless — is not precise; for there only moving alone; but otherwise we bless, since we bless on a good day the second even though it is custom, as written in chapter 1 of Yoma. And Rabeinu Yechiel wrote: the Gemara brought Sukkah chapter 2 of Shabbat Rosh Chodesh — an individual should not begin with a blessing; and if he began with a blessing he completes; and since they practice l'chatchila they practice — therefore we must explain per the words of Magen Avraham that they explain: an individual should not begin to recite; and behold they practiced to recite even as an individual — therefore the same with a blessing per R' Chananel as cited above.

(ט) And in any case. In order to fulfill the first book.

(י) And there are those who say, etc., that if. That three constitute a multitude, as written in Gittin 46a like R' Nechemya — we hold like him there.

(כ) And they practice. That Hodu alone they call "heads of chapters," as written in Sukkah 38b — see there; and there one must answer heads of chapters; and see in Raosh chapter 10 of Pesachim 132 in Midrash Tehillim: those who recite need to be three in order that one say to two Hodu; and see Berakhot 45.
