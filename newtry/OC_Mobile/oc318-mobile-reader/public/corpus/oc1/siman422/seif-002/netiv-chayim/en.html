(Magen Avraham s.k. 4.) As Rabbeinu Yosef ibn Ezra wrote in Bava Batra regarding what Beit Yosef wondered that he did not look, etc. — Heaven forbid to say so about a great man like Rabbeinu Meir; rather R' Meir establishes R' Yossi against R' Yossi — two statements in Yerushalmi in the name of R' Yossi — and therefore there must be a distinction between mentioning rain and saying the event of Rosh Chodesh and chol hamoed; since there are not thirty days when one does not mention Yaaleh veYavo in prayer and in Birkat HaMazon, as Rama wrote; and Moshe is true and his Torah is true.
