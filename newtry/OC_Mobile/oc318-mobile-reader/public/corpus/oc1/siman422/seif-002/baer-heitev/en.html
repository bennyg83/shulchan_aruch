Hallel. Hallel is not recited in a mourner’s house, because it contains the verse, “The dead do not praise Hashem,” which would be like mocking the impoverished. See siman 131, seif katan 10, where I discussed this. Women are exempt from every form of Hallel because it is a time bound positive mitzvah. See Magen Avraham and Yad Aharon. The congregation. If one arrives at the synagogue close to the congregation’s recitation of Hallel, he should first recite Hallel with the congregation and afterward pray. This is from Lechem Chamudos. It is possible that while reciting Pesukei DeZimrah, he may interrupt in the middle to recite Hallel with the congregation, because Hallel is no less than the additional psalms recited on Shabbos. Nevertheless, it appears to me that in that case he should not recite the blessing over Hallel, either before or after it. Therefore, he should do this only for the Hallel of Rosh Chodesh, because many authorities hold that no blessing is recited over it. On days when the complete Hallel is recited, he should not recite it in the middle of Pesukei DeZimrah. This is from Magen Avraham; see there. He says.
