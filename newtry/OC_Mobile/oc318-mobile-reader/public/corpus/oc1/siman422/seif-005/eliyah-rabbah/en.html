(5) Before he uprooted, etc. And the same law: if his attention was diverted from prayer it is as if he uprooted; and it appears to me specifically when he said "yehi ratzon" — for that is the end of prayer (Magen Avraham). And it appears to me: why he wrote in siman 122 s.k. 3 that one should say before "Elohai netzor" "yehi ratzon" and also afterward — we do not consider the end of prayer for this matter as the first "yehi ratzon" but the second.
