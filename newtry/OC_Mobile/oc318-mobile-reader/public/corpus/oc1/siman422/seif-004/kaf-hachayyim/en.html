4) There. At Ma'ariv, one is not required to repeat. Radvaz writes in Responsa, siman 561, that even where one need not repeat the Amidah, such as at Ma'ariv of Rosh Chodesh, if one remembers before beginning Modim, one does not repeat the blessing but recites Ya'aleh V'Yavo there, between the blessings. Maran, however, rules in siman 294, se'if 4, that immediately upon completing the blessing, one may not go back. This implies that one does not mention it at all, and this is the primary ruling. Eliyah Rabbah, siman 114, subsection 7, and Birkei Yosef, subsection 1, agree. Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 5, and Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 220c, write likewise. See above, siman 294, subsection 17.
