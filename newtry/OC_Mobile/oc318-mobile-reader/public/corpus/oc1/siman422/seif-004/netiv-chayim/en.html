(s.k. 5 and wondrous in my eyes.) Gloss: and he did not examine well — for in Sukkah he does not distinguish between expert and non-expert as Rashi and Ran explained; whether he fulfilled or did not fulfill — each time the listener must answer "Halleluyah"; and that [sugya] of Rosh Hashanah is regarding blessings — indeed regarding reciting Hallel he did not speak there at all; and he relied on the mishnah of Sukkah — therefore Rabbeinu Yerucham's words are wondrous.
