(4) There: in Maariv we do not make him return. Radbaz in a responsum siman 561 wrote: even in a place where they do not make him return — such as Maariv of Rosh Chodesh — if he remembered before he began Modim he does not return to the blessing but says it there between blessing and blessing. But Maran siman 294 seif 4 ruled that as soon as he finished the blessing he has no return; and it seems he does not mention at all; and so is the essence; and so Eshel Avraham agreed siman 114 letter 7, Birkei Yosef letter 1; and so the article letter 5, Shulchan Aruch 200:3. And see above siman 294 letter 15.
