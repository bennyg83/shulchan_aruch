Before he uprooted. Avraham Chaviv; and see Shulchan Aruch in the name of Machatzit HaShekel: if he said in "Elokeinu" even though he did not finish "Hamachazir shechinato leTziyon" — it is as if he finished the blessing completely and he must finish and say Yaaleh veYavo before he begins Modim. And Shulchan Aruch disagrees on this per Ritva of Taanit; and his view is he finishes "Lamedeni chukecha" and returns to his law; and if he must return to the beginning he need not open "Adonai sefatai" — see there. And see in Rosh Yosef in the name of Mahari Molcho: one who remembered in R'tzeh that it is Rosh Chodesh and when he finished his prayer was in doubt whether he said Yaaleh veYavo — nevertheless he returns, for presumably he forgot; and Sirkis held thus — see there.
