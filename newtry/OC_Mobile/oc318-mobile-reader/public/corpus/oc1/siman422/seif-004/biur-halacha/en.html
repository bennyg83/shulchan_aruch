But regarding another matter, etc. — see Eshel Avraham in the name of Radbaz; and it seems there that per the poskim who hold an individual does not bless on Hallel, the law of interruption when reciting alone does not apply. However from the Mechaber's words brought above — the view of Rambam that one does not bless at all on Rosh Chodesh Hallel and they practiced like him — and here he states anonymously in this law — it seems he holds this law applies to all; and it is not comparable to one who recites from Tehillim who may interrupt for any matter — for here it is different because it is publicizing the miracle and is more important; therefore one should not interrupt for the sake of it; and this reasoning is mentioned in Berakhot 14a; and even per the conclusion there, in any case this reasoning of not interrupting for the sake of it helps.
