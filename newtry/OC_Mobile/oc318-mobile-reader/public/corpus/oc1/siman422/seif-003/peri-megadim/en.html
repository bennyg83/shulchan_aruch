To recite. Taz — the text is "to recite the Hallel"; and Taz "to recite Hallel"; and see in letter Pesach what is written there; and they answer amen after the blessing; and b'dieved he fulfills without completing even on Rosh Chodesh — for it is like "to recite," etc., like Keriat Shema; and the ancients complete — meaning "and we recite."
