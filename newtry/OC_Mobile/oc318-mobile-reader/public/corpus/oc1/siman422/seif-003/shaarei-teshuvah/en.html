We make him return. Avraham Chaviv — see Peri Chadash and in Rosh Yosef; and there in She'irei Berachah in the name of Rashba: even if he already prayed Musaf he must return; and when Minchah arrives he prays Minchah twice; and even if he remembered at the time of Musaf prayer he does not pray makeups except in Minchah — see there. And if when he was praying the first three with intent to pray Musaf it occurred to him that he did not say in Shacharit Yaaleh veYavo — he finishes the prayer of Shacharit and afterward prays Musaf — see there; so Machzik Berachah in the name of Zera Emet; and see below siman 124 regarding whether one may rely on intent in the shaliach tzibur's blessing. And Shach wrote: one whose house is near the synagogue and has a window open to the synagogue and prays with the congregation and forgot Yaaleh veYavo — he does not fulfill through intent in the shaliach tzibur's prayer; for the reason he wrote "he wrote everything in it so he not be shamed in the congregation" — does not apply here where he is in a place they do not see — see there.
