Standing. It is forbidden to support oneself upon a pillar or wall, because leaning is not considered standing. See Magen Avraham, who discusses at length his difficulty with Rama in Choshen Mishpat, siman 17. Examine the matter carefully. Hallel is recited while seated on Pesach night because it is divided into two sections, and one is therefore not burdened to stand. Taz.
