(7) There: but if they did not say in Shacharit, etc. One who did not mention Yaaleh veYavo and remembered after saying the first three [blessings] with intent to pray Musaf — the matter is simple that he finishes Shacharit prayer even though he said the first three with intent to say Musaf.
