But if one omitted it at Shacharis, etc. If one omitted Ya'aleh V'Yavo and remembered after reciting the first three blessings while intending to recite Mussaf, it is obvious that one should complete the Shacharis Amidah, even though the first three blessings were recited with the intention of saying Mussaf. Responsa Zera Emes, Orach Chaim, siman 47; Machazik Berachah, subsection 1. Nehar Shalom in siman 424, Ma'amar Mordechai in siman 423, subsection 3, Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 222b, and Shaarei Teshuvah in this siman, subsection 3, write likewise.
