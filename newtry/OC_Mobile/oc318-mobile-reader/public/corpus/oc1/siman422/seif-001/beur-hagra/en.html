(א) Seif 1: and if not. Between. Berakhot 30b.

(ב) And if he remembered. Raosh and Mordechai in chapter 1 of Taanit. From Midrash HaGadol — before HaTov VeHaMetiv. Berakhot 29b.

(ג) And if he did not remember until after, etc. There 29b.

(ד) And if it is doubtful. Yerushalmi chapter 7 of Berakhot — and Shulchan Aruch omitted it because it does not stand in the conclusion there; and thus it is stated there: three who ate as one, etc. — taught: one was sitting and eating on Shabbat and forgot and did not mention Shabbat — Rav said he returns and Shmuel said he does not return. Shimon bar Abba in the name of R' Yochanan: if in doubt whether he mentioned Rosh Chodesh or did not mention — we do not make him return. He found a tanna who argues: every day that has a musaf offering, such as Rosh Chodesh and chol hamoed — one must mention me'ein hama'aseh; and if he mentioned, we make him return, etc. And in chapter 5 there — R' Yochanan bar Acha and R' Shimon bar Abba in the name of R' Elazar: if in doubt whether he mentioned Rosh Chodesh or did not — we make him return; to where does he return, etc. And furthermore from what is stated there that in Birkat HaMazon it deals and argues against our Gemara — but in prayer it does not deal at all. And furthermore from the challenge "if certainly he did not mention" from doubt we learn they have one law — and not like Magen Avraham; and Peri Megadim and Taz and Levush, etc.
