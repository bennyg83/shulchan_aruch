(1) Yaaleh veYavo, etc. Abudarham wrote in the laws of Passover: if it were said "yaaleh" alone I would say "yaaleh" but not "yavo"; therefore it says "yavo." And if it were said "yavo," etc. And if it were said "yirtzeh" I would say "yirtzeh" but not "yishma" in the world that we desired — therefore, etc. And if it were said "yishma" I would say once; therefore it says "yifaked." And if it were said "yifaked" I would say twice but not forever; therefore it says "veyizacher" forever — behold we needed all these expressions; they are eight corresponding to the eight virtues in "You have chosen us." How so: corresponding to "and Your great name" it said "yaaleh" — upward; and corresponding to "upon us You have called" it said "veyavo" — upon us; and corresponding to "and brought us near, our King, to Your service" it said the time of nearness and service has come; and corresponding to "and sanctified us" it said "yeraeh" — and holiness, for in our sins no sanctity is seen with us; and corresponding to "and exalted us" it said "yirtzeh" — to exalt us; and corresponding to "and desired us" it said "yishma" — for all You desire of us; and corresponding to "You loved us" it said "yifaked" — the charge from before; and corresponding to "You chose us" it said "veyizacher" — the choice You chose in Avraham, etc.; and it mentions five remembrances corresponding to the five times Israel called in one verse "and I shall give the Levites," etc. — that the Holy One, blessed be He, desires to mention Israel at every time and hour. She'irei Knesset HaGedolah wrote: the world interrupts between "zochreinu Adonai Elokeinu bo letovah" and "ufkedeinu bo levrachah" to answer amen; but in Sefer Tolelet Yaakov it is written that "zochreinu" is said on the masculine and "ufkadeinu" on the feminine — therefore one should not interrupt — until here. And Abudarham wrote "zochreinu," etc., for "zochrah li Elohai letovah"; and "ufkadeinu," etc., for "pakod pakadeti etchem"; and "vehoshienu," etc., for "hosheinu Elohei yisheinu" — until here; it seems one should interrupt; and see siman 236 seif 2 that the shaliach tzibur announces in Maariv "yaaleh veyavo" between redemption and prayer; however between Keriat Shema and its blessings it is forbidden to interrupt.
