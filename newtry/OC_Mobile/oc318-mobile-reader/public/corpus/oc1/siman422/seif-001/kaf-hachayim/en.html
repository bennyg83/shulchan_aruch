(1) [Seif 1] And one says Yaaleh veYavo, etc. In the text of Yaaleh veYavo we say "zochreinu Adonai Elokeinu bo letovah ufkadeinu bo levrachah"; and the world interrupts between "zochreinu Adonai Elokeinu bo letovah" and "ufkadeinu bo levrachah" to answer amen; but in Sefer Tolelet Yaakov it is written that "zochreinu" is said on the masculine and "ufkadeinu" on the feminine — therefore one should not interrupt — as Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur letter 1 — see there letter 1; however in Abudarham it seems one should interrupt. Eshel Avraham letter 1.
