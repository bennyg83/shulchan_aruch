The order of prayer and Hallel on Rosh Chodesh. Contains 7 seifim. At Arvit, Shacharit, and Mincha one prays the 18 blessings and says Yaaleh v'yavo in Retzeh. If one did not say it at Arvit, we do not make him go back — (2) and at
whatever point he remembers, he does not go back — see siman 294 seif 4 and 5 — whether Rosh Chodesh is one day or two days, because we do not sanctify the month at night. (3) But if one did not say it at Shacharit, we make him go back at Mincha. (4) And if he remembered before he began Modim, he says it at the place he remembered. (5) And if he did not remember until after he began Modim — if he remembered before he finished his prayer, he returns to Retzeh. (6) And if he did not remember until he finished his prayer, he returns to the beginning. (7) And if he is accustomed to say supplications after his prayer and remembered after he finished his prayer but before he uprooted his feet, he returns to Retzeh.((8) And if it is doubtful whether he mentioned it or not, he need not go back — Kol Bo, Laws of Prayer.
