Seif 1, "he should intend," etc. Berakhot 47a: and he should not throw, etc.

"The meaning of its reading." End of Pesachim chapter 3 and beginning of Kiddushin chapter 4.

"That He is," etc. As stated in Berakhot 7b: from the day that He created, etc.

"And he should intend," etc. The Tur derived this from Rabbenu Yonah in Sefer HaYirah, who wrote regarding the first verse of Shema that at each of the two Names mentioned in the first verse he should prolong until he thinks: He was, is, and will be, etc. Afterward he wrote that every time he mentions the special Name he should think of the meaning of its reading, that He is Master of all. But it appears there that one need think only of the meaning of its reading; only in Shema do the two Names indicate "was," etc. This is the main point in strict law, for in all matters we follow the reading, and one need not contemplate the writing at all, even though the writing contains great secrets, which are halachah to Moshe from Sinai. So here too we follow the reading with its vocalization. When the prefixes vav, kaf, lamed, and beis come before it, the alef is quiescent and the kaf/lamed/beis has a patach; unlike the writing, where it would have had to be with a chirik. So too the lamed before it is weak, and likewise the letters beis-gimmel-daled-kaf-pei-tav after the Name always have a dagesh. So the Masorah counts "Adonai bam" as three soft mappik forms, while in the reading the alef has a chataf-patach; for the Name's vocalization follows its reading, as with all readings in the Torah. So too, "laHashem Adonai lamaves totzaos," the lamed has a tzerei, and this is very simple. Therefore, when two Names are adjacent, sometimes it is vocalized with the vocalization of Elokim; but the Name itself has a hidden vocalization like all written forms in the Torah, and its vocalization is the secret of the explicit Name. Also, if one comes to think of the meaning of its writing, its meaning is not that, but rather "existing and enduring," like the name of his wife Chavah, and as the Rash and Shita Mekubetzes wrote.

"Who was," etc. As it is written, "and the man called," etc.; see Rashbam, parashas Shemos. These twelve letters, when divided into equal letters, make three Names corresponding to "He reigned, He reigns, He will reign," and so too their vocalizations, only without the vav of action and with the interchange of their ending with their beginning. They are ten letters, and the matter of ten and twelve is known to one who understands.

"And when mentioning," etc. As in Targum Onkelos: "sons of God" means "sons of the great ones," and likewise all judges: "to the judges he shall come," etc. In the work of Creation everything is with the Name Elokim. This is what he wrote: "and Master of all powers."
