(1) [Seif 1] One should intend in blessings the meaning of the words. And likewise in all his blessings and prayers, for otherwise one is like a chirping bird that does not understand what it chirps. Levush. And likewise in siman 271, laws of netilas yadayim, letter tet; Shitulei Zetim, letter alef; Yafeh LeLev 61, letter alef; and this is the language of Sefer Chasidim siman 46: when he washes his hands or blesses on fruits or on mitzvos habitual in everyone's mouth, his heart should intend to bless the name of his Creator who wondrously showed him kindness and gave him fruits or bread to enjoy them and commanded him regarding mitzvos — and he should not act like one who does a thing by custom and utters words from his mouth without the heart's understanding. For this Heaven was angry with His people and sent to us by Isaiah saying: "Because this people drew near with its mouth and lips honored Me but its heart was far from Me, and their fear of Me was a commandment of men learned by rote." The Holy One blessed be He said to Isaiah: Isaiah, see My children's action — it is only outward show — they hold it like one who holds and practices his ancestors' custom in his hand. They come to his house and pray the fixed prayers as their fathers' custom, not with a whole heart. They wash their hands and bless al netilas yadayim, break bread and bless hamotzi, drink and bless as the matter habitual in their mouths — even when they bless they do not intend to bless Me. Therefore His anger burned and He swore by His great Name to destroy the wisdom of His sages who know Him yet bless Him by custom and not with kavanah, as written afterward "Therefore I will add..." and "the wisdom of its sages perished..." Therefore His sages warned about this and said: do things for the sake of their Maker. And a story happened: one died after Yom Kippur and was revealed in a dream to one of his relatives who told him: every day they judge me for not being careful to bless hamotzi and blessings on fruits and birkas hamazon with heart-kavanah. They asked him: is there not judgment of wicked in Gehenom except on Yom Kippur? He said: they do not judge him with strong punishments as on Yom Kippur's first [days] — end quote. And they brought his words: Chayei Adam klal 5, letter 26; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 6, letter alef; Yafeh LeLev there. And Kitzur Shulchan Aruch wrote there it is fitting for a person to accustom himself to say blessings in a loud voice, for the voice awakens kavanah — end quote.
