One should intend in blessings the meaning of the words. When mentioning Adonai, one should intend its reading and its writing. The reading of the Name is with kamatz under the nun and not with patach, for with the vowel-points of kamatz it will be like the numerical value of one five (echad heh), and they are three Names — unlike patach. And he should prolong slightly the kamatz under the nun so that he can think in his heart "one," which the kamatz indicates for this purpose.

And when mentioning etc., one should intend that He is Master of all powers. His intention is to resolve the meaning of the plural in the word Elohim. If you erred in which blessing to nullify it, say after it in Shulchan Aruch 6:6: And read the alef of Adonai with chataf patach — and some say thus: when he reads the Name in its writing, such as Adonai sefatai (my lips) or le'olam Adonai and the like, he shall read patach under the alef; and God forbid to read patach under the nun, for he substitutes the holy with the profane.
