{Rama: Siman 5, saif 1} When he mentions the Name: if one recounts God's favors done for him and from the start mentions the Name and wishes to finish his words of what God did for him, it is forbidden to interrupt him with words lest he not finish and it turn out he mentioned the Name in vain. But if he wishes to curse any person and began with the Name, it is a mitzvah to interrupt him so he not finish his words and not sin. Sefer Chasidim siman 65 and siman 449.
