Explained in Bachye, parashas Emor, that before he mentions the Name he should intend thus. And see Eliyah Rabbah regarding reading the Name, citing the responsa of Maharam of Lublin z"l. Also according to kabbalists of truth, always one should intend to combine the Tetragrammaton with Adonai in a combination such as YehoAdonai, and by day one should intend thus the beginning letters of the Tetragrammaton which is masculine; at night the beginning letters of Adonai which is feminine.
