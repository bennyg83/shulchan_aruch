It is explained in Rabbeinu Bachya, parashas Emor, that before mentioning the Name one should have this intention. See Eliyah Rabbah concerning pronunciation of the Name, in the name of the responsa of Maharam Lublin, of blessed memory. Furthermore, according to the sages of the true wisdom, one should always intend to interlace Yud-Hei-Vav-Hei with Adonai in an alternating sequence. During the day, one should begin with the letters of Yud-Hei-Vav-Hei, which represent the masculine aspect; at night, one should begin with the letters Alef-Daled, which represent the feminine aspect.
