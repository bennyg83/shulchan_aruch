Saif katan alef, "with yud-hei," etc. And one should read with patach — meaning under the alef, and on the dalet a holam one need not explain; and kamatz under the nun, but not chirik, for it implies God forbid he is master to him alone — unlike kamatz whose implication is Master of all. And in the responsum of Rabbi Meir of Lublin siman 83. But Eliyah Rabbah wrote that Maharam at the end of his days retracted and admitted the alef should be read with chataf patach. But one should not prolong the vowel motion much on the sheva nor on the patach.
