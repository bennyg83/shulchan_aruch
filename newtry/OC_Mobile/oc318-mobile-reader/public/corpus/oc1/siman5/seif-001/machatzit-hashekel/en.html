Seif katan 1: With a yud, etc. It should be pronounced with a patach, meaning beneath the aleph; the cholam over the dalet requires no explanation; and with a kamatz beneath the nun, but not with a chirik, for that would imply, Heaven forbid, that He is master over him alone. With a kamatz, however, it means Master of all. See Responsa Maharam of Lublin, siman 83. Eliyah Rabbah writes, however, that toward the end of his life Maharam retracted and agreed that the aleph should be pronounced with a chataf patach. One should not unduly prolong either the sheva sound or the patach.
