With yud-hei vav-hei. Be'er Heitev in Machzik Bracha in the name of Luch Arush for HaGaon Maharam Eibeschutz wrote from now that reading the Name — whether where the Tetragrammaton is written or where Adonai is written — is always the same: to read it with chataf patach — and not as Magen Avraham wrote citing Bach, and also not as he wrote citing Maharam of Lublin's responsum that one read in a manner the patach pronunciation not be noticeable, per the Name — in siman 83. And Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Or Chadash that Maharam retracted, and so Machatzit HaShekel cites a small book called Teshuvas Geonim printed Amsterdam year 5627 where a responsum answering Maharam is written; also responsum of Rama of Panu — and when he saw Rama of Panu's responsum he retracted, and Shlah agreed. And see Noda Biyehuda responsa siman 2: there is no doubt regarding reading Adonai to mislead the melody milra and not mil'el — and Machatzit HaShekel brought this in the name of Luch Arush per the Name.

“And He will be.” Be'er Heitev and Magen Avraham wrote on the word "shehayah" that the vav is in place of yud and showed a source for Tosafos Yom Tov on Sukkah; and there Tosafos Yom Tov wrote on "vayehi" and it seems Be'er Heitev emended Magen Avraham's words on "vayehi" — and so in Eliyah Rabbah, and I saw proofreaders in Magen Avraham. In my humble opinion there is no need to emend, for in Tosafos Yom Tov there he wrote "and likewise it was" in removing the future yud — meaning to remove the future yud from the word yihyeh per the Name; and if so, when we remove the yud at the beginning of the Name, the word hayah remains with vav in place of yud — and Magen Avraham's arrangement of Shulchan Aruch's language refers to what it cites to explain "shehayah," namely through the vav in place of yud; and automatically the same applies to the word "vayehi."
