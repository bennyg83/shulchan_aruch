The intention [required] in blessings — it contains one clause.
One should intend in blessings the meaning of the words. When he mentions the Name [of Four Letters], he should intend the meaning of reading it as mastery — that He is Master of all — and intend His spelling with the letters yud-hei-vav-hei as He was, He is, and He will be. When mentioning Elohim, one should intend that He is mighty, possessed of power and of all strengths.
