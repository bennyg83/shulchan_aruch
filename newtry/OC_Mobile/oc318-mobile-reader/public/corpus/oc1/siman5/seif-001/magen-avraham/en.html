“With yud-hei.” But when it is written with alef-dalet one should have intent only that He is Master of all, and one must read it with patach and kamatz under the nun; when it is written with yud-hei, some read with patach and some lean toward tzeirei—and such is Ashkenazi custom (Bach). Maharam of Lublin’s responsum says that even when it is written with alef-dalet one should read in a way that the patach pronunciation is not conspicuous, for the sake of the Divine Name.
“Vehayah.” For we find a vav in place of a yud—see Tosafos, Sukkah ch. 4.
