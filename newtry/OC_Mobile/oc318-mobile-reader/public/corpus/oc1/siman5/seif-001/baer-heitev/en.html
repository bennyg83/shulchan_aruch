With yud-hei. When the Name is written alef-dalet, however, one should intend only that He is Master of all. It must be pronounced with a patach beneath the alef and a kamatz beneath the nun. When it is written yud-hei, some pronounce the initial vowel as a patach and others as a tzerei; the latter is the Ashkenazic custom. Bach.
