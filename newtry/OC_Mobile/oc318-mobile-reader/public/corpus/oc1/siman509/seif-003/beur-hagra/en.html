Seif 3: Birds, etc. For these are makhshirim that one could not do from yesterday.
And that is, etc. Rabbeinu Yeruham in the name of the Yerushalmi; and see Beis Yosef; and that is what he wrote, "and one should be careful," etc.; and that is before the sewing, for it is possible to do from erev Yom Tov; and see Magen Avraham.
And likewise they were accustomed. Certainly there is no distinction between burning and cutting, as written in seif 2; only that they were accustomed regarding what remains, for cutting it is impossible from erev Yom Tov. Darkei Moshe; and see Magen Avraham, s.k. 9.
