To burn. See Magen Avraham, who concluded that the general rule is: whatever is forbidden to cut is also forbidden to burn, and whatever is permitted to burn is also permitted to cut. And here it is permitted even to cut, for one could not do it from erev Yom Tov; only that they were accustomed to do this by burning the thread; see there.
