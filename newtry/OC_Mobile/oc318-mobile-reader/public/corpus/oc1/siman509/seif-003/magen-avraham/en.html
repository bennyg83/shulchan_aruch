(1) And put in a needle. If we put a BIT with a cutting needle, lest the thread be cut (Ri 5 Yerosh'), it means that if we put a BIT with a needle, it is forbidden to put it with a BIT needle, and ASA in the Haga:

And likewise they were accustomed to burn, etc. — thus wrote Darkei Moshe: and according to what is written in seif 2 that it is forbidden to burn the spit, similarly it is forbidden to burn the thread; but they were accustomed to be lenient in this, and it also appears from Mordechai who forbids burning — that is, before it is filled it is forbidden to burn the thread for the need of sewing; but after it is sewn and one wishes to remove from there the needle or the remaining thread, it is obvious that it is permitted even to cut, for this cannot be done from before Yom Tov, only that they were accustomed to do this through burning the thread — end of his words. The general rule: everything that is forbidden to cut is also forbidden to burn, because of fixing a vessel as stated in the gemara page 32; and everything that is permitted to burn is permitted to cut, only that they were accustomed to burn it; and see siman 314 seif 9.
