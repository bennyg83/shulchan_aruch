(s.k. 3) Permitted etc. — and as implied at the end of siman 502, so it should read. Meaning that Beit Yosef there brought in the name of Maggid Mishneh who wrote as follows: a knife is certainly permitted to sharpen in its normal manner (meaning through a gentile) even through expert artisan melacha and to do so l'chatchila when it broke on the festival — possibly it is forbidden, because it is a matter to be asked and there is room to permit, end of his words; nevertheless, behold even through a gentile one might think nevertheless initially to forbid since it is possible through asking. And he who concludes to permit — and thus we establish like Maggid Mishneh; nevertheless on Yom Tov we do not find anyone who permits explicitly — one should forbid where it is possible through asking.
