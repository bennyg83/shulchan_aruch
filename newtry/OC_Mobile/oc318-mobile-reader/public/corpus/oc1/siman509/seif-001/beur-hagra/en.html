Seif 1: And specifically, etc. See Rashi there, 28b, s.v. "a spit that became bent," and s.v. "it is forbidden to repair it," etc. But, etc.: there, "a spit that became slightly bent," etc.
And it broke on Yom Tov. There: and there is no difficulty here, etc. And we hold like R' Yehuda, as written there: they derash from "for you," etc.; and see Rashi, 34a, s.v. "and one may not repair," etc.
And one may not, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. "the halacha is like R' Yehuda," etc. But the Shulchan Aruch at the beginning of siman 455 did not write "and we do not rule thus," for according to the Rambam we do rule like R' Yehuda; and see what I wrote in seif 2; and therefore the Rambam did not write that we do not rule, but rather holds completely like R' Yehuda; and see there in chapter 1, law 8. And likewise in the Rif he brings what is there, 28a: Rava said to Rav Chisda, We derash, etc., not like Beis Yosef; and see what I wrote in seif 2.
