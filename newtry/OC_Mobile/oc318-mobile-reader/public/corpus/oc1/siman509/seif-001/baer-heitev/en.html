That became bent. Meaning that it became warped and bent. A needle that became curved, even slightly, one should not straighten it. Sefer HaZichronos.
On Yom Tov. And the same applies if it broke on erev Yom Tov and he did not know of it, or he had no time to repair it; but if he forgot to repair it from erev Yom Tov, it is forbidden; and likewise if it was possible to repair it on the first day, it is forbidden to repair it on the second day of Yom Tov (Maharil; Magen Avraham). And if one can borrow another for him, it is forbidden to repair it in any case (Magen Avraham); and see the end of siman 540, what is written there.
