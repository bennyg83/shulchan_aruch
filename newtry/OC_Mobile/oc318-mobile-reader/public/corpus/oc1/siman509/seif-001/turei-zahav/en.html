A spit that became bent. Meaning that it became warped and bent; and the reason is that we hold like R' Yehuda, who said: preparations for ochel nefesh that one could do from erev Yom Tov are forbidden on Yom Tov, and what one could not do from erev Yom Tov is permitted on Yom Tov; and see what I wrote in siman 514, that we do not hold like R' Yehuda in every case.
