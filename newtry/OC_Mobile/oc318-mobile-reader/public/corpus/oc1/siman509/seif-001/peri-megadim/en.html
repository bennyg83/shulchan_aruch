See Taz on what the Rav wrote: that even if it became bent on Yom Tov it is forbidden, for one can roast that way; and regarding one that became bent meaning it broke, if on Yom Tov it is permitted, and on erev Yom Tov it is forbidden. And see the Ra'am, Parashas Bo, according to R' Yehuda, whether there is a distinction between makhshirim and ochel nefesh itself according to the Torah or only rabbinically regarding ochel nefesh; see siman 495, where some are stringent about ochel nefesh itself, which implies rabbinically. And what Rashi wrote from "akh" like R' Yehuda, with "for you" for makhshirim, see Taz; and in Eliyah Rabbah [1] in Tamim Deim there are two "for you" written; see there. And see Tzelach, Parashas Bo; and with Hashem's help in siman 512 in the Taz I will explain further.
