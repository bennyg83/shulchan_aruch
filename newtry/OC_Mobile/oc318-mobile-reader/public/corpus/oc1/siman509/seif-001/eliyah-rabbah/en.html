(1) [Levush] "For you" to include all etc. One might ask: if so, how did Tur rule in siman 492 that Samaritan needs are forbidden? But in the Gemara [Beitzah] daf 28, the first tanna learned to forbid in food-preparation items "for you" and not for a Samaritan; but for R' Yehuda who learned for food-preparation items to permit, Samaritan needs (Olas Shabbat). And one might ask about his wonder, that in the Gemara daf 21 it is explained that everyone forbids Samaritan needs, and likewise from every incident brought there, and likewise on daf 20 it says Beit Hillel thus. Therefore it appears to me that R' Yehuda holds two teachings from "for you," and in this manner Tosafot wrote on Pesachim daf 47 s.v. "for you." I further found in Temim De'im daf 25d that he resolves this difficulty: two "for you"s are written — one "Atzeret shall be for you" and it comes to exclude Samaritans; and the other is "he alone shall do for you" and it comes to include all their needs; and for the rabbis one to exclude Samaritans and one to exclude dogs; and R' Yehuda holds like R' Akiva who said "soul" — even the soul of an animal; and therefore we establish the halacha like R' Yehuda, until here. It further appears to me that R' Yehuda holds Samaritan needs are learned from "ach" as Rashi explained in Parashat Bo, and that is from Mekhilta; and Ra'avam and all Rashi's commentators wonder on Rashi that he did not bring the Gemara's derivation mentioned above from "for you"; and in my humble opinion it is not difficult at all, for the Gemara says thus per the first tanna; but for R' Yehuda who requires "for you" for food-preparation items, therefore he learns Samaritan needs from "ach"; and Rashi there according to R' Yehuda — for he explained that food-preparation items are permitted.
