(1) [seif 1] A spit that was bent etc. — and know that food-preparation items that cannot be made from erev Yom Tov are a dispute between R' Yehuda and the rabbis, for the rabbis forbid and R' Yehuda permits as stated in Beitzah 28b, and there Rav Chisda ruled in practice like R' Yehuda — see there. And so Tur wrote here and in siman 495, and so Shulchan Aruch wrote there in siman 495; however according to Rif and Rambam there is a dispute whether one holds like R' Yehuda or like the rabbis as explained in Beit Yosef — see there. And R' Yehuda and the rabbis dispute only regarding repair of a utensil that was impossible to make from erev Yom Tov; but to make a utensil for food need, even R' Yehuda agrees it is forbidden as explained in Beit Yosef — see there. And further know that even to rule like R' Yehuda, there are matters they did not permit except to do for oneself and not to instruct others lest they be lenient excessively as explained in Beit Yosef and here in Shulchan Aruch; and further see in Bach and Taz s.k. 1 and in Beit Yosef siman 514 that we do not establish halacha like R' Yehuda in every case — see there.
