(s.k. 7) She cannot repair it because it is repairing a utensil. Rambam gave a reason as Shulchan Aruch wrote: a decree lest he come to sharpen it on a sharpening implement. And Ran and Tur gave a reason because it is repairing a utensil. And see in Beit Yosef; if so, how did Magen Avraham cite Ran and Tur's reason on Shulchan Aruch's words that wrote Rambam's reason? It does not appear to me to emend Magen Avraham's words; and further because it is repairing a utensil — and perhaps Magen Avraham's intent was to give a reason for Rambam's words who forbids lest he sharpen it on a sharpening implement, and if he sharpens it on a sharpening implement what has he accomplished? Therefore Magen Avraham said it is repairing a utensil. And in this Rambam and Ran disagree: Rambam holds if he sharpens on wood there is no repairing-a-utensil concern, only we decree lest he sharpen it on a sharpening implement and then there is repairing-a-utensil concern. And Ran holds that even sharpening on wood has repairing-a-utensil concern. And Yeshuot Shalom wrote: even for us who establish halacha like R' Yehuda that food-preparation items impossible to make from erev Yom Tov are permitted on Yom Tov from "for you" — the Torah certainly permitted even complete melacha; nevertheless anything that makes a utensil is forbidden, because it is impossible to permit in food-preparation items more than "for you" itself. And since grinding and sifting are forbidden, clearly food-preparation does not permit melachot done for a long time — whether you say it is d'oraisa or from their words (see there siman 495); and since so, it is impossible to permit in food-preparation items more than this, and anything that makes a utensil — the matter is clear that it is melacha done for many days etc., end of his words Yeshuot Shalom.
