And it broke on Yom Tov — and likewise if it broke on Yom Tov and he did not know of it or he did not have leisure to repair it; but if he forgot to repair it from erev Yom Tov it is forbidden. And likewise if it was possible to repair it on the first [day] it is forbidden to repair it on the second Yom Tov day — for the first Yom Tov day is considered a weekday relative to the second for this matter, even though we are proficient in fixing the month. Maharil. Magen Avraham s.k. 2. Eliyah Rabbah page 3. Chamudei Daniel introduction 1. R' Zalman page 1. Chayei Adam klal 94 page 2 and page 3. Mekor Chaim page 5 and page 6. However in Sefer Bigdei Yesha he wrote that if he did not need the spit on the first Yom Tov, it is permitted according to our teacher Rama may he rest in Eden to repair on the second Yom Tov when he needs it — not like Maharil; and likewise in the novellae of R' Akiva Eiger he questions like the words of Maharil mentioned — see there.
