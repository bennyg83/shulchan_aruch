(s.k. 2) And it broke on Yom Tov, etc. But if he forgot, etc. And see in siman 527 in Magen Avraham, s.k. 16; and one must distinguish between them.
It is considered a weekday, etc. And even if we say that the first day of Yom Tov is like a weekday, what of it? One may forbid on the second day of Yom Tov only where it was possible to do it from erev Yom Tov on a weekday. And if so, granted that we reckon the first day of Yom Tov as a weekday, nevertheless it was not possible to repair it on the first day of Yom Tov for the need of the second. One must say: since by the strict law it was possible to repair on the first day of Yom Tov, only because of a stringency or a doubt, or for us who are expert and know that the first day is primary, that stringency does not create a leniency on the second day of Yom Tov. And the book of the Maharil is not before me; and see what I wrote in siman 499 in Magen Avraham, s.k. 5, who brought in the name of the Maharil that which we hold: one gives the hide before the tramplers, and that is specifically when it was slaughtered on Yom Tov, for they permitted its end on account of its beginning; but if it was slaughtered from erev Yom Tov, when it was possible to put it before the tramplers, it was not permitted to put it before the tramplers on Yom Tov. And the Maharil was in doubt there whether, if one slaughtered on the first day of Yom Tov, it is permitted to put it before the tramplers on the second day of Yom Tov, for the first day relative to the second is like a weekday. And if so it is difficult: there he is in doubt about this, and here it is obvious to him to forbid; and see in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah, who wrote a partial resolution.
