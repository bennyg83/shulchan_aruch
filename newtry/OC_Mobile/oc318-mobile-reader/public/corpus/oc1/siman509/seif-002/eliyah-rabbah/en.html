[2] And it broke, etc. And there is one who ruled to forbid when it broke or became completely bent, even on Yom Tov, for one does not make utensils on Yom Tov, and they permitted only when it became slightly warped (Avodat HaKodesh); and so the Ran agreed. And this appears to me to be the interpretation of the Rambam in chapter 14, who explained "niratzef" as completely bent; and automatically, when it is slightly warped, such as "niratzam," it is permitted; and the Maggid Mishneh and Beis Yosef forced his words needlessly. And so it appears to me to explain the Mordechai regarding a long spit in seif 2, which implies even if it was impossible from erev Yom Tov, and that is because one is making a utensil; and so it appears to me to be stringent.
