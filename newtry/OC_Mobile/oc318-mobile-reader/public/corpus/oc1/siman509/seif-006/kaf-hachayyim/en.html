6) There, in the gloss. And it broke on Yom Tov. This means that its point broke. Tur writes this, and Rashi explains it likewise. Rabbeinu Yerucham, section 7, also writes this, and Beis Yosef cites him. Rashba writes likewise in Avodas HaKodesh, gate 2, subsection 8. Nevertheless, one authority ruled that when it broke or became entirely bent, it is forbidden even if this occurred on Yom Tov, because utensils may not be made on Yom Tov. Permission was granted only when it became slightly bent. Avodas HaKodesh there. Ran agrees. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2, cites this and writes that it appears proper to be stringent. See there.
