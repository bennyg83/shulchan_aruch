Seif 5: It is permitted to perform libun, etc. But, etc. See Magen Avraham, s.k. 10-11: a libun such that straw burns from the outside is like hagalah, since it goes from one side through to the other; and when permitted matter was absorbed, even something whose use is by fire, hagalah suffices; which is not so when issur was absorbed; and as written at the end of Avodah Zarah.
But if, etc. As written: one may not perform libun to the tiles, etc., for that is repairing a utensil; and likewise the reason for hagalah; but in the first clause it is permitted, as above. And that is, etc., as written: and one may not cool them in cold water in order to harden them; and if for the sake of, etc. And the Ran wrote there: and it is not comparable to libun of tiles, because at the time of its use, etc. And he already wrote that whatever makes a utensil, even where it is impossible to do from erev Yom Tov, R' Yehuda agrees that it is forbidden; and see Tosafos of Pesachim 5a, s.v. "and he says"; or alternatively, etc.
And it is permitted. See Magen Avraham.
