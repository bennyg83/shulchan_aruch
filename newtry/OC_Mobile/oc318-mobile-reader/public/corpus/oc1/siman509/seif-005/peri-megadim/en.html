A pastry. See Taz; see Yoreh De'ah 121 in the Shach, 8; and this requires further study from here; see Magen Avraham in Yoreh De'ah. And the same applies if permitted matter was absorbed through liquids: a light libun of a kli rishon suffices. And know regarding issur, even rabbinic, and even according to Beis Yosef, nevertheless whatever is possible from erev Yom Tov is forbidden on Yom Tov; see Magen Avraham 11; and it is not comparable to the spit there, which is only a custom. And with Hashem's help in Magen Avraham 12 it will be explained further. And kashering an oven from issur to permitted on Yom Tov, see siman 461 in Magen Avraham 1, and Chok Yaakov and Eliyah Rabbah there, that one may say one heating suffices; see there. And one should not heat twice.
