It is permitted to perform libun. For since permitted matter was absorbed, a minimal libun suffices for it, such that straw burns upon it from the outside, as written in siman 451, seif 4, and in Yoreh De'ah, siman 121, seif 4; see there, and in the Shach and in the Taz.
And that is provided that when it has been heated. So that it appears like warming it to cook beneath it (Mordechai); and even if it was possible to do it from erev Yom Tov, it is permitted (Darkei Moshe). But what absorbed from neveilah requires libun until their coating flakes off, and it is recognizable that he is doing it to kasher it; it is forbidden, just as it is forbidden to do hagalah to a vessel on Yom Tov (Beis Yosef; Hagahot Maimoniyot). And if it was not possible to do it from erev Yom Tov, it is permitted to do hagalah (Darkei Moshe; Hagahot Asheri, chapter 2). And see seif 1 in the gloss. And from here it is implied that on a weekday it is permitted to do hagalah to a milchig vessel to eat meat in it, or the reverse; and the world practices a prohibition in the matter. And I found written in the name of the Gaon Moreinu HaRav R' Binyamin of Posen, who heard from Maharam Yaffe the reason for the custom: that if one does so, he will forever have only one vessel and will do hagalah to it every time he uses it, and that is forbidden, lest he come to err, as stated in Chullin, folio 9b. And as for why we permit here, one may say that since they never use it except by way of libun, there is no room to decree, as above.
To perform libun to a spit. For no repair is apparent, unlike kashering tiles (Ra'af). And it appears to me that it is permitted for another reason: because one could not do it from erev Yom Tov (Darkei Moshe). And it appears to me that according to the reason of Ra'af, even if it was possible to perform libun from erev Yom Tov, it is permitted to perform libun on Yom Tov, since by the strict law it needs no libun, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 76, seif 4; only that they were accustomed to perform libun to it, so it is not considered a repair, like a vessel that became tamei through a toldah of tumah from erev Yom Tov, which one immerses on Yom Tov; and see what I wrote in siman 323.
