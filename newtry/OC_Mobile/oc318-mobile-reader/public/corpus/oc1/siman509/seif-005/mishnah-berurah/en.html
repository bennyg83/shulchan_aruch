{24} A meat pastry. For since permitted matter was absorbed, it does not need a thorough libun until its coating is burned; rather a somewhat light libun suffices until straw burns upon it from the outside; therefore it is permitted, for with such a libun it does not appear like repairing a utensil, but like warming it to bake in it; which is not so regarding what absorbed from neveilah, which needs a thorough libun until sparks fly from it, and that appears like repairing a utensil; and even if it was not possible for him to perform libun from erev Yom Tov, for example if it became treifah on Yom Tov itself, it is likewise forbidden for this reason. And some later authorities are lenient when it is impossible for him to perform libun from erev Yom Tov; however, in any case according to all opinions one should not rule thus for others, as above in seif 1 in the gloss.
{25} Immediately. And then even if it was possible for him to perform libun to the vessel from erev Yom Tov, it is likewise permitted, for he needs the heating of the vessel now for baking the food; but if he waits from baking until it cools and warms it anew, it turns out that the first libun was only to kasher the vessel, and it appears like repairing; and even if it was not possible for him to perform libun from erev Yom Tov, it is likewise forbidden. And according to the "some are lenient" above, this too is permitted by the strict law, but one should not rule thus for others, as above. Magen Avraham wrote: from here it is implied that on a weekday it is permitted to do hagalah to a vessel from milchig to fleishig or the reverse, and the world practices a prohibition in the matter; see there the reason; and Peri Megadim wrote that the custom is to make it treifah first; and see what we wrote above in siman 451 in Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 19, in the name of the Chatam Sofer.
{26} For it is forbidden to do hagalah to a vessel on Yom Tov. Even if he wants to use the vessel afterward for the need of Yom Tov; for cooking the hagalah water is not for the need of ochel nefesh, but only to kasher the vessel to use afterward, and that one could have done from erev Yom Tov. And if it was not possible to kasher the vessel from erev Yom Tov, for example if it became treifah today and the like, many later authorities are lenient by the strict law, but one should not rule thus for others, as above. However, if he had boiling water that he boiled for the need of ochel nefesh, it is permitted to rule for others to do hagalah with them.
{27} To perform libun to a spit, etc. For since by the strict law it needs neither libun nor hagalah, only that they were accustomed to perform libun to it, the libun is not considered repairing a utensil; and it is permitted even if it was possible to perform libun before Yom Tov [Magen Avraham and other later authorities].
