It is permitted on Yom Tov to heat an iron utensil in which cheese flatbreads were baked, and after the heating to bake in it a meat pastry, provided that when it is heated, it is placed on the food immediately. However, if it has absorbed [flavor] from non-kosher meat or the like, it is forbidden to heat it even to bake permitted food in it. {Rama: Rema: Similarly, it is forbidden to purge vessels on Yom Tov (Mordechai end of Yoma, Or Zarua, and Hagahot Maimoniot chapter 1). And it is permitted to heat a spit on which unsalted meat was roasted when one wishes to roast with it again on Yom Tov (Maharai of Prague).}
