(s.k. 4) And not to burn it, even though it is permitted to burn, etc. For there there is an element of repairing a utensil, as explained there and in this siman, s.k. 9.
And one must say we are dealing with a case where it is possible, etc. For otherwise we hold that makhshirim that one could not do from erev Yom Tov are permitted on Yom Tov; only that we do not rule thus [publicly], as written in seif 1.
