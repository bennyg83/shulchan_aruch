Seif 4: It is permitted to cut, etc. As written at 31-32 regarding seals on vessels, etc., and one cuts; and even though regarding the ground it would have been permitted were it not for demolition in the ground; and even on Shabbos it is permitted, as written in siman 314, seif 9.
And likewise one may, etc. For burning is permitted in a place where cutting is permitted, as above.
