{20} To cut on Yom Tov a binding, etc. And even though he wrote at first that one should be careful not to cut the thread, that is before the sewing, because by that he prepares it for sewing and it is considered repairing a utensil; which is not so here, after the sewing, where there is no repair; and even on Shabbos it is permitted, as above in siman 314, seif 9; see there the reason in Mishnah Berurah.
{21} Mekulasin. Roasted with its head upon its legs and upon its innards is called mekulas [see Rashi, Pesachim 53, s.v. "mekulasin"].
{22} And threads that are sewn. Meaning that they were sewn one to another at the time of roasting.
{23} And likewise one may burn. For there is no repairing of a utensil in this.
