That one should not. See Taz; Shabbos 34. But what remains after the sewing one may cut; only they were accustomed to burn; see Magen Avraham 9. And the word "and likewise" means they further practice, etc.; and see Beis Yosef and the Levush: at first glance it is difficult, and one may say that this is the meaning: and he should set the thread l'chatchila on erev Yom Tov, but after the fact one may say it does not prevent, and he may set it on Yom Tov; but severing the thread requires extreme care not to cut on Yom Tov; and on this the Rav commented, "and likewise what remains, they are accustomed not to cut," even though by the law of the Torah what remains is permitted. And may his garments be white as snow; and with Hashem's help in Magen Avraham it will be explained further.
