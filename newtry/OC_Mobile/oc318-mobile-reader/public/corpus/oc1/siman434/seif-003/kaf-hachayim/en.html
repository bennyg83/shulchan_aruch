(3) There — chametz he leaves, etc.; in derashot Maharil he wrote guard chametz found at bedika and chametz left to eat — guard well in a barrel or box, take out at eating time and return to guarding and conceal. All the more when the 14th is Shabbat, for then several meals remain that he must guard from crumbling chametz; children should not be brought to the checked place for they crumble. Proper to have a special place to eat there, guarded from every stumbling block — end; see below end of note 10.
