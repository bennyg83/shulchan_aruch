On the night of the Lord’s Prayer, the psalmist said, “I will eat milk in the roof, and as a result of the Lord’s Prayer, and as a result of the Lord’s Word, I will be able to do so.” And the Lord’s Prayer in the Holy Spirit, which he says, “I am not afraid of my brother, Lord, and I will not go away with him, but rather as a verse that he has written on the page, and that the Lord is not a light of his soul that is left in the house, and that he shall be forgotten in one hour.” This is what you have done, and that you have forgotten about it, because Hashem’s Word will be found there. And Hashem’s Word says, “You will not see Hashem’s presence, nor do you know that they are in His house difficult, nor will it be seen in Hashem’s Word, nor will He be able to find a little harder and less difficult, nor will He be able to do so.”
