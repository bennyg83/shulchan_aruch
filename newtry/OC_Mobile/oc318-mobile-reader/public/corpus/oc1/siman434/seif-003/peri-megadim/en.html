At night Taz Magen Avraham 5 explained Tur's words simply: as if one ate milk b'shogeg, as siman 446; likewise 431 regarding unintentional. Taz holds per Tur chametz unknown — no transgression b'shogeg; Tosafot beginning Kol Sha'ah s.v. vai — see there; in Talmud one may say they are words of his brother R' Yechiel z"l who did not hold except like Rosh Pesachim 6:2: if you say crumbs — it is established they remained in the house; Pri Chadash explained fine roll in one corner forgotten at removal time — implies such case: all that was known one hour and forgotten is like milk b'shogeg; if so what does he ask "do you find to nullify" — therefore Taz: he will find afterward; per Tosafot there. Rosh's words there difficult: he wrote lo yera'eh and not written lo tireh — although not seen, fit to be seen; even if unknown in his house — difficult also 5:2 implies lo yera'eh concealment permitted, also lo yimatzei — somewhat difficult; see Kessef Mishneh chapter 1 of Chovel uMazik dispute of Rambam on this; dealt with 438 Magen Avraham 4; and I wrote more; in novellae we wrote from this.
