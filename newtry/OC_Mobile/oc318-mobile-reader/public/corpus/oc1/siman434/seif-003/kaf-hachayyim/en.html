3) There. The chametz that one retains, etc. Maharil writes in his Derashos that one should carefully guard the chametz found during the search. One should likewise carefully guard the chametz retained for eating inside a barrel or chest, remove it at mealtime, and afterward return it to safekeeping and put it away. This is especially necessary when the fourteenth falls on Shabbos, since several meals still remain and one must be careful not to scatter crumbs of chametz. Children should not be brought into an area that has been searched, because they commonly scatter crumbs. It is proper to designate a particular place in which to eat, so as to avoid every possible mishap. These are his words. See below, at the end of subsection 10.
