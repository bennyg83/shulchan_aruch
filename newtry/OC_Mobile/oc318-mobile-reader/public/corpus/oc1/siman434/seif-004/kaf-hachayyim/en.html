So that another search will not be required, etc. The same applies to nullification: one must nullify again, because chametz that a weasel dragged away from the house was not included in the original nullification. When nullifying, one does not intend to include chametz that one plans to eat after the search. Therefore, a second nullification is required. Beis Yosef in the name of Ran. Chok Yaakov, subsection 3, explains that the Shulchan Aruch did not mention this because in any event one nullifies again during the day at the time of burning. Magen Avraham, subsection 2, also appears to hold that a second nullification is unnecessary at this point, because one relies upon the daytime nullification. Chok Yosef, subsection 2, writes likewise.
