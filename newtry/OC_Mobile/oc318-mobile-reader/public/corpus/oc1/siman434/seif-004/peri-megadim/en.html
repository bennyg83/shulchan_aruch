That is in possession Taz Rashi Pesachim 4:1 s.v. obligation of return; 6:2 s.v. checker, etc. in this house; Bach 5 implies chametz unknown needs no nullification only intent on it when he sees he transgresses; suffices to say in this house — in borders where people do not live no nullification needed; Tur per his view as Magen Avraham 5 wrote. Taz challenged: nevertheless language is superfluous — see note 3. Nullification formula Pri Chadash: all chamira (se'or) and all chamia (chametz) in my possession (to include all borders) that I did not see and did not remove — meaning whether I did not see or saw and forgot to burn. He added there "and I do not know" — one may say he says so to exclude what he left for food from inclusion; and "did not remove" — in our siddurim it is absent; and I do not know.
