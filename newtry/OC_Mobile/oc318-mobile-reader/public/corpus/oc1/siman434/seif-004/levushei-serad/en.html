Shulchan Aruch seif 2 after bedika immediately, etc. — one may say the reason: if he nullifies before bedika, even chametz he finds afterward is in the scope of nullification since he did not see it — he would burn chametz not his, as in the gloss "and he will reacquire" per Rosh's words on what he takes for burning there is no acquisition; otherwise also nullification by day could be before burning; however one may say another matter as I wrote elsewhere Taz s.k. 4.
