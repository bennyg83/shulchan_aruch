There "that is in his possession" — appears his intent: if he said in his possession alone, it implies chametz he saw that is in his possession and his hand controls to burn; not included chametz he does not know where it is; therefore must say "that I did not see" — intent to nullify also this chametz; therefore he challenged: should have said "I have" and no more — in that certainly included also chametz he did not see; therefore enacted in my possession that I did not see, etc.; from this unnecessary to say "I have" at all. What he wrote "and if he did not say whether I saw and did not see" — appears not specifically in nullification by day; main intent on saying "I did not see" that apparently he need not if he said "I have."
