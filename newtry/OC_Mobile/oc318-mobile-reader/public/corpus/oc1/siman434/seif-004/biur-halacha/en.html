(א) His agent, etc. — one should not make a minor an agent [Peri Megadim].

(ב) So-and-so's chametz, etc. — see Taz who wants to say this is unnecessary because presumably he gave it as a gift; I did not copy this in Mishnah Berurah, for several Acharonim challenged: how did the agent acquire the chametz?

(ג) Shall be nullified — see in Mishnah Berurah what we copied in the name of Magen Avraham and Sha'arei Teshuvah: specifically when commanded to nullify; if commanded only to check he cannot nullify. In Magen HaElef he questions: why not nullify — presumably he made him agent to check as usual; therefore he sides that the agent should nullify in any case, lest the sender rely on him, and the sender must nullify too; without this it does not help — see there more. I later found Gra Zalman also questions Magen Avraham's words that presumably intent is also for nullification; nevertheless it seems all this when he traveled and made an agent before travel to check his chametz; but when he is home and asks another to check, certainly he must nullify himself unless he explicitly commanded also on nullification.

(ד) That his wife nullify — see Mishnah Berurah from Magen Avraham. Know the main law of his wife's nullification where he did not command her — Acharonim question this; nevertheless in a pressing situation one may rely, for his wife is like himself for this, and presumably just as he gave her permission for damim so it seems from Beur HaGra; likewise in Bigdei Yesha, Mor, and Ketziah; per their words it appears she may also make an agent; likewise Sha'arei Teshuvah in the name of Ram"a; likewise Ketziah. Nevertheless l'chatchila better she nullify herself as I wrote in Mishnah Berurah, especially since some are stringent — see Taz and Machatzit HaShekel.
