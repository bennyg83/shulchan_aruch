(א) (s.k. 4) He may press, etc. — although on Pesach covering with a vessel does not suffice, and Chok Yaakov understood his intent: one who finds chametz on chol ha'mo'ed — we hold siman 446 that he must burn immediately. From this Magen Avraham was puzzled: why does covering not suffice there on chol ha'mo'ed? He wrote on this: per the Rambam there he must burn; why press a vessel — behold zealous people precede, etc.; not so here, etc. — see there. In my humble opinion Magen Avraham did not intend this; rather his intent was to reject Beit Yosef's proof, who wrote: it appears one may not rely l'chatchila on pressing a vessel — in one version Rashi on one who finds chametz on Yom Tov presses a vessel because it is impossible; but where possible we do not rely on pressing a vessel because the vessel is taken for need — end Beit Yosef. Meaning on what R' Yehuda said in the name of Rav, daf 6a: one who finds chametz in his house on Yom Tov presses a vessel; R' Yehuda said in the name of Rav: for a non-Jew's chametz he makes a partition ten tefachim — on this Rashi wrote covering with a vessel does not suffice for a non-Jew's chametz because the vessel is taken for need. However the case above (finding chametz on Yom Tov) is impossible with a partition, for on Yom Tov it is forbidden to make a partition; Rashi gave another reason — see there. Therefore Magen Avraham intended in what he wrote: although on Pesach covering does not suffice — meaning for a non-Jew's chametz; and what he wrote as in siman 446, or it is a textual error and it should read as siman 444 s.k. 2; or since Rashi's question is from siman 446 he wrote siman 446, but it appears more a textual error and it should be siman 444.

(ב) As the Rosh wrote in chapter 1 of Parah, on the baraita brought in Chullin daf 10b: a flask of sin-offering water left uncovered and found covered is impure; left covered and found uncovered is pure. R' Yehuda said: why? Because creeping things are accustomed to uncover and are not accustomed to cover. Therefore in the first case where found covered, a person covered it and we are concerned perhaps an impure person was there and made it impure. But in the latter case where found uncovered we attribute it that certainly a creeping thing uncovered it, for they are accustomed to uncover and there is no impurity; but we are not concerned for a person, for people are accustomed to cover and not leave something uncovered because of creeping things — so the Rosh explained. Magen Avraham brought the Rosh's words because from this his proof is simpler; but also according to Rashi there and Rambam chapter 15 of Hilchot Parah Adumah, certainly sometimes people uncover, and nevertheless in the latter case it is pure, for it is one doubt — perhaps a creeping thing uncovered, and if you say a person, perhaps he was pure — the same here one may rely l'chatchila; we need not be concerned perhaps a person will uncover and mice will drag, for that is also one doubt — perhaps it will not be uncovered, and if uncovered perhaps he will cover, and if not cover perhaps a mouse will not take, and even if a mouse takes perhaps they will eat it. Although one should not do so l'chatchila, a doubt in such a case where there is much doubt and a general concern we rely on, as Chok Yaakov wrote — see there; and what Shulchan Aruch wrote in Ba'er Heitev on what Shulchan Aruch wrote "or place it in a box" — from what Choshen Mishpat siman 461 wrote that an ordinary box is frequented by mice; per the Rambam everything is according to context, and here for an hour until tomorrow it is certainly not frequented regarding mice.
