(א) His agent — meaning he commanded him to nullify; but if he commanded only to check he cannot nullify. In Darchei Moshe it is written in the name of Maharil that he must stand beside him, as end of siman 476; Bach ruled one may not nullify through an agent.

(ב) If he does not do so — meaning he is not accustomed to do so; if so we are concerned he may forget; therefore it is good his wife nullify. But in truth the wife's nullification does not help so much since it is not hers — see Levush siman 436 s.k. 2.
