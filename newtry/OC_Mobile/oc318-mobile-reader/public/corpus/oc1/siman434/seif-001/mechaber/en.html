The laws practiced immediately after bedikat chametz. Contains 4 seifim. After bedikat chametz he should
be careful with chametz he leaves to conceal, so that he does not need another bedikat chametz — for example if a mouse takes it before him, or if his bread is diminished, such as if he left ten loaves and finds nine. But if he simply does not know whether any was taken from it or not, no. If he pressed a vessel over it and did not find it, he need not check again, for certainly a person took it. Therefore he may press a vessel on it, hang it in the air, or place it in a box in a place where a mouse cannot come.
