(א) (s.k. 1) After bedikat chametz, and the same at the time of bedikat chametz, etc. — meaning: certainly before bedikat chametz he need not be careful, for even if a mouse takes it before him and he needs bedikat chametz — what of it? He truly must check, for he has not yet checked, as the Taz wrote. But at the time of bedika he must be careful, for there is concern that after he finished checking one corner and goes to check another, a mouse may take it before us and drag it to corners he already checked.

(ב) And people, etc. — they also conceal chametz they leave for eating. Nevertheless the multitude errs and thinks the checker may not see that chametz, for according to their mistake every chametz the checker sees at bedika must be burned; therefore they hide it so the checker's eye not gaze on it — they blind their eyes from seeing; it is from lack of their knowledge.
