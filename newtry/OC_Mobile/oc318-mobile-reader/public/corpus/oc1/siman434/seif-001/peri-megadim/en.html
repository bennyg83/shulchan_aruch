In chametz Taz and Magen Avraham 1; in Mishnah Pesachim 10:2: if one leaves he places in concealment lest he need bedika after him; 10:2 in the question he raised the word as Darchei Moshe, for he holds it is not good advice but necessity, for they fear, etc.; in Shulchan Aruch he wrote he should be careful — warning from taking a weasel before us — and chametz he leaves for eating or at bedika leaves to burn tomorrow, for at night they do not burn similar to notar or he will forget nullification by day as Darchei Moshe 1 and I wrote in Magen Avraham 1; see Hagahot Maimoniot chapter 3:1. That he did not say chametz he checks — it is not fixed he will find chametz in bedika; chametz before bedika need not conceal, etc.; Bach, Chok Yaakov, Eliyah Rabbah wrote he must, for during bedika he will need check the place checked again; Magen Avraham 1. Perhaps "may take before us" properly and come to the checked place — Rav Mari: perhaps his bread count will be diminished during bedika — no concern apparently, for perhaps he placed where not checked and will still check, as apparently from Rashi 9:1 "behold we see he brought it here"; one may say bread will be diminished, he will check and not find — good, for he recognizes it — he must check even the place already checked. But the questioner thinks it is an obligation not good advice; Rav Mari also said a decree lest it be diminished — implies even Rava holds it is obligatory; that is if he permits chametz scattered, even if taken before us he will not pay attention and not check again — only after bedika when the house is checked in his eyes; not so before bedika, even if during checking it takes, he checks again the checked place since he is engaged in bedika; they did not decree he be careful from the law — warning from mice because of concern of mouse before him requires warning — understand this; and I wrote in note 2 more. In Darchei Moshe 1 it implies from Hagahot Maimoniot's explanation simply — see there.
