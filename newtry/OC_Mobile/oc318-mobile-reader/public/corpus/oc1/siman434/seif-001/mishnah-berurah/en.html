(א) After bedikat chametz — and the same applies at the time of bedikat chametz: he should conceal chametz he wishes to eat, for there is concern that after he finished checking one corner and goes to check another corner, a mouse may take it before us and drag it to a corner he already checked.
(ב) That he leaves — for his eating; and the same applies to chametz he finds during his bedika and leaves to burn tomorrow. People only conceal chametz they find at the time of bedika, but with other chametz they are not careful and carry it here and there, which is not proper.
(ג) So that he does not need bedikat chametz, etc. — nevertheless he need not bless again on this bedika. If he checked and did not find it, he must nullify again, for this chametz that the mouse dragged after bedikat chametz was not included in the nullification, since he left it for his food or to burn tomorrow. However, we practice in any case to nullify again at the time of burning.
(ד) He need not check — specifically when he placed it in a high place where children's hands do not reach.
(ה) He may press a vessel on it — but to place chametz in a vessel and cover it is forbidden, because creeping things are accustomed to uncover; but when he presses a wide vessel on it they cannot uncover [Magen Avraham and Sha'arei Teshuvah].
