(א) After bedikat chametz — some say the same before beginning bedika: he conceals chametz he wishes to eat, lest his bread be diminished during bedika and he need to check again what he already checked (Bach, Chok Yaakov, Yad Ephraim, Magen Avraham). The Taz attacked him on this — see there. People only conceal chametz found at bedika; other chametz they carry here and there, which is not proper [Acharonim].

(ב) Again — (and likewise he must nullify again then. Beit Yosef in the name of the Ran.) Nevertheless he need not bless again [Chok Yaakov].

(ג) Need not — if a child cannot take it [Magen Avraham].

(ד) In a box — in Choshen Mishpat siman 461 s.k. 13 the Rambam ruled a hollowed box regarding mice — see there; requires study.
