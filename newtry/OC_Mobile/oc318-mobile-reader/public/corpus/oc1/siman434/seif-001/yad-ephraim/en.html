Taz s.k. 1 "and he has not yet checked," etc. — apparently difficult from Taz "and he has not yet checked" — plainly Bach's intent is concern the mouse may take before him during bedika and he need check again part of the house already checked; perhaps Taz holds for this no warning needed — one who wishes can do so to avoid trouble of checking again; nevertheless no obligation and warning, for if a mouse actually takes, since he is engaged in bedika we are not concerned he forgets, unlike after bedika he needs warning to conceal lest he need bedika again, and there is concern perhaps after finishing bedika he forgets to check again; therefore they fixed a limited time immediately at night start when there is change, as Taz wrote beginning siman 431 that main enactment of Sages is bedika immediately adjacent to night. Further it appears certainly no concern mouse takes a large loaf, only concern it takes a small loaf; therefore one may suspend perhaps they ate it; nevertheless needs bedika again since we see it took — no doubt removes certainty; but specifically if taken after bedika and nullification, then needs bedika again since this chametz was not in nullification as Magen Avraham s.k. 2; not if mouse takes during bedika — automatically included in nullification he will nullify after bedika and need not check again — rabbinic doubt perhaps it ate; this is difficult: since at dragging time he has not yet nullified, he cannot exempt himself from bedika again because he will nullify afterward — nevertheless he did not fulfill bedikat chametz per Sages' enactment; otherwise if mouse takes after bedika why need bedika again — say nullification alone suffices; nevertheless he should not rely on nullification and make rabbinic doubt l'chatchila; likewise during bedika since he did not fulfill Sages' enactment requiring bedika he should not rely on nullification — see siman 438 Taz s.k. 4: Bach holds what he nullifies after doubt arises does not help; Tur holds it helps — see there.
