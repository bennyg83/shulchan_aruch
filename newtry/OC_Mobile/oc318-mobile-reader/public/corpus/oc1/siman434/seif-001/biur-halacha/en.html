(א) If a mouse takes it before him — we do not say the mouse ate it, for a doubt of eating does not remove certain chametz [Gemara].

(ב) Or place it in a box where a mouse cannot come — meaning ordinary hollowed boxes are frequented by mice, as Choshen Mishpat siman 461 s.k. 13 in the gloss; therefore he was precise to write a place where, etc. — that he should be careful to place it so a mouse cannot reach there [Gra; this resolves Ba'er Heitev's question].
