(א) Regarding chametz he leaves, etc. — the law is so both for what he finds through his bedika and conceals to burn tomorrow, and for what he left from his eating. He did not mention here only what he left for eating and not what he found in bedika, for it is not a fixed matter that he will find chametz in bedika, for presumably they cleaned all rooms and check only out of doubt; but the remainder for eating is certain, and in all cases he needs warning to guard it from mice taking it before him. Mahari Beri'an expanded here at length and made a custom to conceal chametz needed for eating before bedika — there is no reason like the essence, for before bedika he need not warn, for even if a mouse takes it he only needs bedika, and he has not yet checked; rather after bedika he needs warning lest he need bedika again, as explicitly in his customs that one need not conceal chametz for eating.

(ב) If a mouse takes it, etc. — we do not say the mouse ate it, for a doubt of eating does not remove certain chametz.
