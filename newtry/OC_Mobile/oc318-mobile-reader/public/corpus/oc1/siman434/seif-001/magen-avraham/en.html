(א) After bedikat chametz — and the same law at the time of bedikat chametz: he conceals chametz he wishes to eat (Beit Yosef). People only conceal chametz found at bedika, but other chametz they carry here and there, which is not proper.

(ב) If a mouse takes it — the Ran wrote he must nullify again, for this chametz the mouse dragged from the house was not yet nullified; therefore. It appears to me according to what the Tur wrote that one should not say all chamira in this house but in my possession, to include chametz in other houses — if so, also chametz the weasel dragged is nullified again and one need not nullify again — end Darchei Moshe. It appears to me the Ran did not intend this; rather his intent is this chametz dragged after bedika was not in the scope of nullification, since he left it for his food; if so, if he checked again and did not find, he needs nullification again. However we practice in any case to nullify again at the time of burning.

(ג) Need not check — it should read: placed in a high place where children cannot take it; otherwise he needs bedika again — see end of siman 439.

(ד) He may press a vessel — likewise the Rosh and Mahariv in the name of Yerushalmi, not like Bach who forbids l'chatchila pressing a vessel; although on Pesach covering with a vessel does not suffice, as siman 446 — there we are concerned he may forget and eat it; but here there is no concern perhaps a person will uncover and afterward a creeping thing come and drag it, for a person's way is to cover, as the Rosh wrote chapter 1 of Parah. It appears forbidden to place chametz in a vessel and cover it because creeping things are accustomed to uncover, as in Chullin daf 10; but when he presses a wide vessel on it they cannot uncover.
