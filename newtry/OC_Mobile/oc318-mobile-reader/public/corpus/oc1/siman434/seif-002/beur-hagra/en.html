(א) Immediately — there: and let it be nullified, etc.

(ב) All chamira — so Rif and Geonim and Yerushalmi chapter 2 halacha 2: Rav said one must say all chametz I have in my house that I do not know — let it be nullified. Hagahot Maimoniot and Tur: he should not say in this house but in my possession, to include all places, as Gemara: I have only in houses, etc.; therefore "that is in my possession" — so Rif and Rosh.

(ג) And he says — as in Terumot and Shevuot: mouth and heart aligned [Magen Avraham s.k. 7].

(ד) If they said — meaning in the language of Targum.

(ה) But in other, etc. — Kol Bo wrote to say chamira and chamia — chametz and se'or are two things; Terumat HaDeshen wrote the word chamira includes chametz too, as chapter 1: chamira of warriors is his chamira; chapter 2 they bought warriors' chamira — therefore in the gloss "but in other." But in that Yerushalmi it is not so, for in foreign language chametz and se'or are certainly two things as in the Torah; nevertheless Yerushalmi only said all chametz I have, etc.

(ו) It is good — Magen Avraham; the Gemara did not require it because they said and what he leaves, etc.

(ז) When the sixth hour arrives — Gemara there.

(ח) One should not nullify — Magen Avraham; although in the Gemara it implies they burned at six, as "when is its removal," see Tosafot and Rosh there; likewise here since it is not time of removal, etc., because they did not nullify by day.

(ט) In order to fulfill, etc. — Rashi's view: nullification is d'oraisa and not bedika, and helps even for known chametz, as below s.k. 7: student sitting before his teacher, etc.; end chapter 3 in the mishnah "one who goes," etc.; there s.k. 6 Tosafot challenged: if so how did R' Yehuda and Rabbanan disagree on removal of chametz? R' Yehuda derives from verses and Rabbanan answered "he did not find," and the Torah said tashbitu, etc.; there s.k. 1 R' Akiva says, etc.; we find, etc.; further asks: bedika by candle — whence? He explains bedika is d'oraisa and nullification from hefker; Tosafot daf 2 "from the Torah," etc.; but Ran wrote d'oraisa one suffices, but because this nullification depends on people's thoughts and minds are not equal the Sages were stringent specifically on bedika and nullification is not needed for the mishnah's law; afterward Rav Yehuda innovated nullification lest he find, etc. — per Rashi — see Ran beginning of Pesachim; the Rav here follows Tosafot whom Acharonim followed — likewise Mordechai, Hagahot Maimoniot, Semag, and supporters.
