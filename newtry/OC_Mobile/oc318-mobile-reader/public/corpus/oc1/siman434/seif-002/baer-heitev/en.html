(א) Chamira — because bread is human sustenance and one should not degrade its honor; therefore they enacted the language of Targum so harmful beings and accusers not understand [Siddur He'arot Yosef].

(ב) Language — in some versions: if they said in Hebrew he fulfilled — specifically when he knows at least the matter of nullification that he declares his chametz hefker; if he does not know he did not fulfill — likewise Maharil, Mahariv, Magen Avraham; see Chok Yaakov.

(ג) Each one — one careful in his deeds should say this formula: all chamira and chamia in my possession that I did not see, did not remove, and do not know — let it be nullified and hefker like the dust of the earth [Bach, Chok Yaakov].
