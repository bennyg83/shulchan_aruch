After bedikat chametz, etc. It is fit for everyone careful in deeds to also remember hefker in nullification — meaning he should say let it be nullified and hefker like the dust of the earth; such is the nullification formula: all chamira and all chamia in my possession that I did not see, did not remove, and do not know — let it be nullified and hefker like the dust of the earth (my teacher in Bach); he wrote there the reason for the matter.
