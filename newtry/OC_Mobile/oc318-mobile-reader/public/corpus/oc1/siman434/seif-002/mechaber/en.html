After bedikat chametz, immediately at night he shall nullify and say: All chamira that is in my possession that I have not seen and have not removed shall be nullified and be like the dust of the earth.He should say the nullification in a language he understands (Mahari Beri'an). If they said it in Hebrew, "all chamira" includes both chametz and se'or (Terumat HaDeshen) siman 434). But in other languages one must mention each one separately (Darchei Moshe).} It is good to nullify again on the 14th at the end of the fifth hour, before the sixth hour arrives, for once the sixth) hour arrives it becomes forbidden and he cannot nullify. {Rama: One should not nullify during the day except after burning the chametz, in order to fulfill the mitzva of burning his chametz (Mahariv). וטוב לחזור ולבטלו פעם אחרת ביום י"ד סוף שעה חמישית קודם שתגיע שעה ששית שמשתגיע שעה ששית נאסר ואין בידו לבטלו: הגה ואין לבטל ביום אלא לאחר ששרף החמץ כדי לקיים מצות שריפה בחמץ שלו: (מהרי"ו)
