Seif 2: After bedikat chametz immediately at night he nullifies, etc. — note: the source of this law is Talmud chapter 1 Pesachim: R' Yehuda said in the name of Rav the checker must nullify, etc.; "do you find it to nullify" — it is difficult: what does he ask? Perhaps the intent is when he removes chametz he must nullify, and what we teach is so — it is a doubt, since he found it he delays removing and does not transgress and need not nullify; therefore we teach that delaying removal does not transgress if he could not remove immediately; but if he could remove immediately and does not, he transgresses; therefore we teach that certainly if he finds it when he can remove from presumption he will remove and nullification unnecessary — only concern when he cannot remove. But if he does not nullify he transgresses since it was in his power — what does he ask? Per Rashi who explains he spares it from burning it works that he cannot answer thus, for then what do we teach — obvious he must nullify; we cannot say the doubt is he delays intending to burn, for he spares not burning. Per Tosafot who hold he did not nullify by itself — why does he ask? Perhaps we teach precisely that even if he intends to delay intending to burn it does not help since he can nullify. But difficult: "the checker must nullify" — why not say briefly "must nullify"? It proves the intent is at bedika time he nullifies — understood more per Rabbeinu Yonah chapter 2 of Berachot regarding returning to chapter beginning: although doubt of biblical verse Keriat Shema he does not return, nevertheless since he began he must finish, etc.; likewise here: without bedika he would not be obligated to nullify for nothing from doubt lest he find after prohibition; since the Sages enacted bedika from doubt, from concern lest he come to eat — since he began to check and be concerned from doubt he must also nullify from doubt; therefore "the checker must nullify" — works better; especially per Rosh who brought Yesh Omrim that nullification is only at the sixth hour — then it is not at bedika time; all the more our question: perhaps intent when he finds it he must nullify, and we cannot infer from "checker" that at bedika time, for they hold nullification is at the sixth hour; but per our explanation it is fine that nullification is by day — nevertheless if he did not first be concerned from doubt he need not afterward nullify from doubt; since he checks and fears from doubt he must afterward nullify; tomorrow at the sixth hour fear from doubt — examine well.
