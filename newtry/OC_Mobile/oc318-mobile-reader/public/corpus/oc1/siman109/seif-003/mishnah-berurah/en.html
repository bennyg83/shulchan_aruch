(15) "D'Sidra" — this is U'va Letzion, and the same applies to Kedushah of Yotzer.

(16) "Keter" — for Sephardim this is [the Kedushah of] Keter, and for Ashkenaz custom it is Nakdishach; and the reason of the Mechaber is because he is not standing with them in one matter, for they stand in Musaf and he stands in Shacharit — this is not preferable to if they were standing then in Kedushah D'Sidra. And although the Geonim wrote explicitly that one joins with them, the Mechaber holds that Rashba, who wrote "an individual standing in prayer..." disputes them. But Rama in his gloss holds that Rashba said only regarding Kedushah D'Sidra, which is only a narrative of how angels sanctify; but regarding two Kedushot of one matter, such as of Shacharit and of Musaf, perhaps he agrees to the words of the Geonim; therefore one should not reject their words, and so ruled Peri Megadim that one should not deviate from this.

(17) "To say with them" — and he says the entire text of Keter or Nakdishach that the prayer leader says in Musaf, and this is called one matter because the essence in Kedushah is Kadosh Kadosh Kadosh, and they are always equivalent.
