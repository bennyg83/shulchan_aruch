If the time is not passing, etc. — So wrote Beit Yosef in the name of Semak, that if the time is passing it is fine. But it is somewhat difficult, because Semak refers to the end — if he cannot finish before the shaliach tzibur reaches Modim — but regarding Kedushah certainly there is still time when the shaliach tzibur begins to pray; and it is forced to say that the case is where the shaliach tzibur prays when the time is passing. And see above what I wrote in the name of Rama. And one cannot say that he means thus — that if he waits until after Kedushah he cannot finish before Modim, and if he does not begin before Modim the time will pass — therefore he begins before Kedushah. For this is not [correct]: it is better that he answer Kedushah and begin praying after Kedushah even if he will not reach Modim, as Magen Avraham wrote that one can bow in the middle of a blessing [for Modim]. And furthermore one can say the reason that answering Kedushah is preferable to Modim — for according to Rambam one need not wait at all for Modim, as Beit Yosef wrote.
