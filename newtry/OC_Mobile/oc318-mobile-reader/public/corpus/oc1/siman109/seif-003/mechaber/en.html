An individual standing in prayer — when he reaches the place of Kedushah and the congregation was saying Kedushah D'Sidra, he does not say "Kadosh" with them, for the Kedushot are not equivalent. And it seems the same applies if the congregation was saying Keter — he does not say "Kadosh" with them, but rather remains silent and intends what they say, for one who hears is like one who responds.And some say that Kedushah of Keter, namely Kedushah of Musaf, and the individual is praying Shacharit — he may say with them, for both are Kedushah of Shemoneh Esrei and their Kedushah is equivalent; and so it seems to me to be the principle [and Rashba does not dispute the Geonim that Beit Yosef cites].]:
