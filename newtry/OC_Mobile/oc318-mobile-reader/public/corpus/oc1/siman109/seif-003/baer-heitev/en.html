Of the order. That is, U'va Letzion. And the same for Kedushat Yotzer. Magen Avraham.

Keter. Meaning: the congregation says the Kedushah of Musaf and he prays the Yotzer prayer—he also does not say with them. This is Beit Yosef's view, who holds Rashba disagrees with the Gaon; and Rama holds Rashba said only regarding Kedushah of the order, but regarding two Kedushot of one subject, such as of Shacharit and of Musaf, perhaps he agrees with the Gaon's words—therefore one should not reject the Gaon's words, end quote Darkei Moshe; and this is the "some say" that Rama brought.
