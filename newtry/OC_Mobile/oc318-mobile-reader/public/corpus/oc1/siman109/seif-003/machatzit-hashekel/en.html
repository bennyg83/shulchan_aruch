(Siman kaf gimmel) And the same [applies to] Amen, etc. — that if he already heard, etc., he need not wait; and do not say that it is preferable that he hear [Amen of] HaEl HaKadosh. For from the outset he need not wait, and it suffices on his own.

For the view that an individual [may say Kedushah], etc. — meaning because one could say Kedushah as an individual; and even though now he will hear Kedushah with the congregation, and he will also say afterwards Kedushah as an individual — nevertheless once suffices, and he need not wait because of a second time.
