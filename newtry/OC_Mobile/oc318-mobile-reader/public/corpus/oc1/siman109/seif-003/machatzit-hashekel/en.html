Seif katan 3, The same applies to Amen, etc. If he has already heard it, etc., he need not wait. One should not say that it is preferable to hear it an additional time. For this reason he need not wait, since hearing it once is sufficient.
For according to the view that an individual, etc. The reason is that he may recite Kedushah individually. Even though he would now hear Kedushah with the congregation and later recite Kedushah individually, the Gemara still says he need not wait. Evidently, one occurrence is sufficient, and he need not wait for a second.
