Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. “This implies that he may.” Meaning, he need not finish when the chazzan begins Yisgadal in order to answer Amen. Even if he finishes when the congregation is answering Amen, yehei shemei rabba, and because of his prayer did not hear the Kaddish from the chazzan, in which case he does not answer Amen but only yehei shemei rabba, that is sufficient. The Magen Avraham concludes by referring to the gloss at the end of siman 96, meaning what he wrote there in seif katan 5. See what I wrote there, that if he paid attention when the chazzan began Yisgadal, he may also answer Amen. The same is meant here. He may not intentionally forgo answering Amen, since Amen has the law of Kaddish. Mordechai and Agudah mean only that he may continue praying until the chazzan begins yehei shemei rabba, but he must still answer Amen because he paid attention, as explained.
