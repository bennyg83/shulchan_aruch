And with this I finished; and the matter of siman 96 in Hagahah — his intent is to what is written there in Magen Avraham siman 5, and as explained: when he gives attention that the shaliach tzibur says yisgadal he can also answer Amen — and here too we deal in such a case. But it is forbidden for him to neglect answering Amen, because Amen is like Kaddish; and the Mordechai and Agudah did not say [otherwise] except that he may pray until the shaliach tzibur begins yehei shmei rabba — and always that he answers Amen too, because he gave attention — as stated.
