(Siman kaf bet) To Kaddish, etc. — and it implies he can, etc. — meaning it is evident that one need not finish before the Kaddish begins — that is, yisgadal, etc. — and see siman 56 in Hagahah. Meaning: yehei shmei rabba, etc. — there begins a new praise, and Amen refers to what preceded it — that is, yisgadal.

As written siman 56 in the name of Beit Shmuel, and it is explained there that Amen yehei shmei rabba [comes] before Kedushah too — see Magen Avraham there. And even though regarding Kedushah they disagree — meaning folio 21b — Rav Huna holds that one must finish before Modim, for otherwise it would appear, Heaven forbid, as a denier of the one to whom the congregation bows in Modim. And even if he cannot finish before Kedushah — he does not need it [to finish before Kedushah]. And Rabbi Yehuda bar Lavai holds that one must finish before Kedushah; and they conclude there that they disagree whether an individual says Kedushah — Rabbah holds an individual says Kedushah; and if so, even if he cannot finish before Kedushah he does not need it, for he can say Kedushah as an individual. And Rabbi Yehuda bar Lavai holds that an individual does not say Kedushah; therefore one must finish before Kedushah. But regarding Modim all agree — because of the reason that he not appear as a denier, etc.
