Word for word. Meaning, he should recite with the chazzan Nakdishach, Ledor Vador, and so forth, until HaKel HaKadosh. He may then continue independently if he can finish before the chazzan reaches the conclusion of Shome'a Tefillah. This appears from Terumas HaDeshen and Rabbi Levi ibn Chaviv, siman 66. On a public fast, he should not recite Aneinu with the chazzan as a separate blessing, but should insert it in Shome'a Tefillah as an individual does. Mateh Moshe, Maharil, Hagahos Minhagim.
The blessing of HaKel, etc. Meaning, he should intend to conclude these two blessings together with the chazzan. He should then not intend to answer these Amens, as stated in siman 59, seif 4, for where correction is possible, Beit Yosef agrees that one corrects whatever one can. See seif 1.
He should not begin. Because he must remain silent and listen, as stated at the beginning of siman 125. Darchei Moshe.
