Word for word — meaning that he says with him "nekadesh l'dor vador," etc., until HaEl HaKadosh; and afterwards he prays on his own if he can finish before the shaliach tzibur reaches the end of Shomea Tefillah [Kol Bo in Tosafot, and Rabbi Levi Chaviv siman 66]. And in Tzitz Eliezer he should not say with the shaliach tzibur "answer us with blessing" on his own, but in Shomea Tefillah as an individual (Magen Avraham Torah Mitzvah, Maharil, Hagahot Maimoniyot).

The blessing of HaEl, etc. — meaning he should intend to finish these two blessings together with the shaliach tzibur; and then he should not intend to answer these Amens, as written siman 59 — that where it is possible to fix, the Rabbeynu Yonah [holds] we fix all that is possible — end of matter.

He should not begin — for one must be silent and listen, as written at the beginning of siman 125 [Darkhei Moshe].
