(11) "Word by word" — meaning he should say with him "Nekadesh le-dor va-dor..." etc. through "HaEl HaKadosh," and afterward pray independently if he can finish before the prayer leader finishes "Who Hears Prayer." And in communal prayer he should not say with the prayer leader our matter as an independent blessing — even at Minchah — but only "Who Hears Prayer" as an individual.

(12) "The blessing of HaEl etc." — meaning he should intend in these two blessings to finish together with the prayer leader, and then he need not answer these Amens like the prayer leader himself.

(13) "And also he should intend..." — see above in comment 11; and in Magen Giborim he wrote it is better to pray word by word to fulfill bowing and Amens; one should hurry to pray before the prayer leader reaches Modim; and so is implied in Eliyah Rabbah.

(14) "But l'chatchila..." — and in Eliyah Rabbah he elaborated from many poskim that even l'chatchila it is permitted to begin together with the prayer leader; and see Chayei Adam that implies from him that at least at Shacharit certainly one may be lenient in this so as not to need to wait so long in Shirah Chadashah; see there. And even at Minchah one may be lenient in this with a slight need, so too in Maamar Mordechai; and similarly wrote Magen Giborim: one who cannot estimate that he will reach Modim together with the prayer leader if he begins after Kedushah, and perhaps he will become confused thereby — he may begin with the prayer leader and say with him Kedushah and the entire prayer word by word; end quote.
