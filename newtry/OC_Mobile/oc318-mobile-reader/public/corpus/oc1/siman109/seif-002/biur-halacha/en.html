If one begins to pray, etc.—see what we wrote in Mishna Berurah note 14 in the name of Eliyah Rabbah, who holds this is permitted even ab initio; but if he stood to pray before the prayer leader and through his great concentration or for some reason reached Kedushah with the prayer leader, or the opposite—that he began after the prayer leader began and because the prayer leader lengthened he reached Kedushah—it is implied from there that for him too it is not permitted except b'dieved. And it appears to me the same applies when he has no other way to hear Kedushah.

But l'chatchila, etc. — see in Magen Avraham the reason that one must be silent and listen, as we wrote at the beginning of siman 125 — meaning, how can he say on his own "nekadesh"? Therefore for us, who practice not thus (lo amrinan hakhei) like the Taz below in siman 125 to say "nekadesh" — then it is no longer applicable to be strict about this. But in truth the essential law is not like the Taz, as we wrote there.
