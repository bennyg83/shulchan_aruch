(2) There — if one can begin and finish before the shaliach tzibur reaches Kedushah, etc. — for Kedushah it is forbidden to say it as an individual, and similarly Kaddish. Modim — the reason is that one must bow with the congregation so he not appear as a denier of the one to whom the congregation bows — Tur and Beit Yosef, Bach, Taz siman 101, Mishnat Zakanim siman 1; and similarly wherever the congregation bows and prostrates, such as Aleinu, one must bow with them — Remez siman 2.
