When he is. See Taz, who writes that bowing unrelated to the prayer is considered somewhat of an interruption. Nevertheless, if the situation occurred, he bows according to Rabbeinu Tam. Certainly, between redemption and prayer, which is less severe than the middle of the Amidah, he should bow with them and say nothing, as I wrote in seif katan 1. See Beit Yosef, siman 66, and Terumas HaDeshen, siman 11, who cites an opinion that one answers yehei shemei rabba between redemption and prayer. Bach gives a different answer; see there.
