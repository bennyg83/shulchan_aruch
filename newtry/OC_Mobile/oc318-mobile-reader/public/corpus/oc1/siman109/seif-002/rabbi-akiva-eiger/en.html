Magen Avraham note 2 — that it is better to say Kedushah with the congregation. And how is it — if he waits until after Kedushah he will not finish his prayer until after Kaddish; it is better that he begin before Kedushah and say "Amen, yehei Shmei Rabba...," for Kaddish is preferable to Kedushah, as Magen Avraham wrote siman 56.
