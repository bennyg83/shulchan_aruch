{Rama: And the same law [applies to] Amen of HaEl HaKadosh.} It is a dispute of the author, for he is not concerned about these Amens — for he wrote: if one enters after Kedushah he estimates until the shaliach tzibur reaches Modim, and he was not particular about Shomea Tefillah. The reason is because he wrote in Beit Yosef that the Bavli argues with the Yerushalmi, and he is not concerned for those Amens — see there.
