If one begins praying along with the prayer leader, when one reaches with the prayer leader "Nakdishach," one should say with him word by word all of Kedushah as he says it. And similarly one should say with him' word by word the blessing of "HaEl HaKadosh" and the' blessing of "Who Hears Prayer." Also one should intend that when the prayer leader reaches Modim, he too reaches Modim, or "ha-tov shimcha u-lekha na'eh l'hodot," so that he bows with the prayer leader at Modim.But l'chatchila one should not begin until after the prayer leader said Kedushah and "HaEl HaKadosh," unless he was compelled to begin because the hour is passing or in order to juxtapose Geulah to prayer — then the law is thus [Tur and his own opinion].]:
