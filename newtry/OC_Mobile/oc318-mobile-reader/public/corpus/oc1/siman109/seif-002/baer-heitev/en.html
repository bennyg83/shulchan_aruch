Word by word. Meaning: he should say with him "nekadesh le-dor va-dor," etc., until HaEil HaKadosh; and afterward if he wishes he may pray independently if he can finish before the prayer leader reaches the end of Shomeah Tefillah. So in Terumat HaDeshen; and R' Levi Chaviv. And in Tashbetz one should not say with the prayer leader "annenu berakhah" independently but as repetition of the prayer leader like an individual. Maharam and Maharil. Magen Avraham.

Prayer. Meaning: he should intend to finish these two blessings with the prayer leader—Magen Avraham.

He should begin, etc. Magen Avraham wrote, and it appears to me the same applies to Barekhu, for the individual does not say Barekhu; and if he already heard Kedushah or Barekhu or knows he will hear afterward, he need not wait. However for Kaddish he must wait, for it is a set time; and perhaps Kaddishes from "above us" onward are not in this category. Magen Avraham.
