For Kaddish. Meaning: until the prayer leader reaches "yehei shmei rabba"—Mordekhai and Agudah. And it implies he may pray until they answer "yehei shmei rabba," but he should not answer Amen, since he did not intend for what he will say—this is an orphaned Amen. One who comes to the synagogue immediately after Kedushah, and if he waits until after Modim he will not be able to answer Amen yehei shemei rabba after the Kaddish after [the Amidah of] eighteen [blessings]; and if he waits until after the Kaddish the time of prayer will pass or he will not be able to pray Maariv with the congregation. It appears to me he should begin immediately, for it is better to forgo Modim than Amen yehei shemei rabba, as written in siman 56 in the name of Beit Shmuel; and further, Modim one may skip in the middle [of one's Amidah] if he came close to Kaddish. And if he prays immediately he will cancel Amen yehei shemei rabba; and if he waits until after Kaddish he will not be able to pray Maariv with the congregation. It appears to me that public prayer is preferable. And if he came close to Kedushah—if he begins immediately he will not be able to say Kedushah; and if he begins after Kedushah he will not be able to say Modim; and if he waits until after Modim the time will pass. It appears to me he should begin after Kedushah, for it is better to say Kedushah with the congregation; and further, Modim one may skip in the middle of a blessing. Magen Avraham.

For Modim. And for Rama one must finish before the prayer leader's Amen of Modim; and if not, he should not begin until after Modim. Magen Avraham.
