And Tosafos Berachos 40b s.v. "veR' Yehuda," etc.; and likewise the plain reading of chapter 3 of Megillah is like him; and all the more regarding Modim, for R' Yehuda Levi concedes to R' Hillel, as written in "what do they argue about," etc.

Or to Kaddish, etc.—for it is not inferior to Kedushah—Mordekhai in that passage in the name of R' Yehuda, and Sha'ar HaPesukim.
