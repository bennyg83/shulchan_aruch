When the prayer leader reaches Kedushah — Kedushah is forbidden to say alone, and similarly Kaddish and Modim; the reason is that one must bow with the congregation so that one should not appear as a heretic toward One to Whom the congregation bows.

When he is in one of the blessings in the middle — in siman 113 Beit Yosef asked in the name of Mahari Abuhav: why did Tur write there that one may bow in the middle of the blessing in all cases, but here said specifically if he needed to juxtapose Geulah to prayer, then he may bow in the middle of the blessing? He answered what Beit Yosef wrote — two notes there, another note — and in my humble opinion there is a difference: certainly if he comes to bow in the middle because it seemed to him proper to bow there, it is permitted in all cases since he is in the middle and there is no interruption here; but here, needing to bow on account of the congregation that bows and not from the aspect of the prayer itself, it is considered somewhat an interruption; therefore one should not rely on this l'chatchila, in my humble opinion.
