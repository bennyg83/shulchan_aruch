1) [Seif 1] One who enters the synagogue and finds the congregation praying: meaning they are praying the silent Amidah. Olat Tamid, ot 1; Solet Belulah in Maginei Chaim, ot 1; Yafeh Lalev, ot 1.
2) There: if he can begin and finish before the shaliach tzibbur reaches Kedushah, etc. For Kedushah may not be said by an individual, and likewise Kaddish. And Modim: the reason is that he must bow with the congregation so that he not appear as one who denies the One to Whom the congregation bows. Tur and Beit Yosef; Bach; Taz, se'if katan 1; Mishbetzos Zahav, ot 1. And likewise wherever the congregation kneels and bows, such as in Aleinu, he must bow with them. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 2.
3) There: before the shaliach tzibbur reaches Kedushah, etc. And if he found the congregation already beginning to answer Kedushah or Amen Yehei Shemei Rabba, so long as they have not finished, he may answer with them. Rama of Fano, siman 112; Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef; Ateret Zekeinim, ot 1. And so I wrote above, siman 56, ot 26.
