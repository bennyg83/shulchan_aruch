If one can begin, etc. — This implies that even l'chatchila, when the time is not passing, and regarding a matter where he does not need to juxtapose geulah to tefillah, it is also permitted; and such is the implication of all poskim. I wrote thus to exclude what I saw in Shulchan Aruch printed with Be'er HaGolah and with Olas Tamid, where Rama's glosses are arranged here: "but l'chatchila he should not begin," etc. — and that is an error and should be after seif 2; and so it is in all old Shulchan Aruch books.
