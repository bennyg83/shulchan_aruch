Shulchan Aruch: "or to Kaddish." It appears to explain what was asked — or if he entered after Kedushah he must estimate whether he will finish before the shaliach tzibur reaches the Kaddish after the prayer. But the meaning is: here we are not dealing with one who enters after Kedushah until afterwards; and [the Mechaber] wrote there that he must estimate until the shaliach tzibur reaches Modim, which is before the [post-]Kaddish. Therefore Magen Avraham explained siman 101 "or" — or if he entered when they pray Maariv, where there is no Kedushah and no Modim, he must estimate until the shaliach tzibur reaches Kaddish. In all these the reason is because it is impossible to say them alone. And regarding Modim — even though an individual says it — there is another reason, because one must bow when the congregation bows; and it is not fitting that a person bow in the middle of his prayer, as Taz wrote siman 2, or as Magen Avraham wrote siman 6. Therefore there is permission regarding Modim if he estimates he will reach Modim together with the congregation, or will reach one of the blessings in which one bows at the time the shaliach tzibur reaches Modim — for automatically he will bow with the congregation. All this is l'chatchila — it is forbidden for him to rely and say that when the shaliach tzibur reaches Modim he will bow in the middle of the prayer. But b'dieved — such as if he finished Go'eil Yisrael and needs to juxtapose geulah to tefillah — then he bows even in blessings in which one does not bow, but specifically in their middle. This is the continuation of the author's words.
