Or to Kaddish — meaning the Kaddish after the prayer, such as in Maariv, which is not like [the morning service].

To Kaddish — meaning until the shaliach tzibur reaches yehei shmei rabba (Mordechai and Agudah). And it implies that he may pray until they answer yehei shmei rabba, but he should not answer Amen — since he did not intend for what he would say, it would be an orphaned Amen, as written siman 124 and in siman 56 in Hagahah. And one who came to the synagogue immediately after Kedushah — if he waits until after Modim he cannot answer Amen yehei shmei rabba after the Kaddish after [the eighteen blessings]; and if he waits until after the Kaddish the time for prayer will pass, or he will not be able to pray Maariv with the congregation. It seems to me that he should begin immediately — for it is better to omit Modim than Amen yehei shmei rabba, as written siman 56 in the name of Beit Shmuel. And furthermore, regarding Modim one can bow in the middle of [the blessings]. And if he came close to Kaddish — if he prays immediately he will omit Amen yehei shmei rabba; and if he waits until after Kaddish he will not be able to pray Maariv with the congregation. It seems to me that communal prayer is preferable. And furthermore it seems to me: if he came close to Kedushah — if he begins immediately he cannot say Kedushah; and if he begins after Kedushah he cannot say Modim; and if he waits until after Modim the time will pass. It seems to me that he should begin after Kedushah — for it is better to say Kedushah with the congregation; and even though regarding Kedushah they disagree, regarding Modim all agree — meaning because they hold that an individual says Kedushah; but according to us, who hold that an individual does not say Kedushah — Kedushah is preferable. And furthermore, regarding Modim one can bow in the middle of the blessing.

And the same [applies to] Amen — it seems to me the same [applies to] Barchu, for an individual does not say Barchu. And it seems to me: if he already heard Kedushah or Barchu, or knows he will hear afterwards, he need not wait — for the view that an individual says Kedushah, he need not wait. However for Kaddish he must wait, for it has no fixed [text the individual recites alone]. And it is possible that the Kaddishim from "above us" and onward are not included in this.

Before the shaliach tzibur reaches Modim — and for Rama one must finish before the Amen of Shomea Tefillah; and if not, he should not begin until Modim — end of matter.

In order to juxtapose [geulah to tefillah] — meaning that he erred and said Go'eil Yisrael, [thinking] that l'chatchila he must wait for a new song, as written siman 66.

In their middle he bows with him — but l'chatchila one should not begin with intent to bow in their middle, lest he forget to bow since there is no obligation there to bow [Darkhei Moshe siman 113]. And in Beit Yosef there are further resolutions.
