Laws of Rosh Chodesh that falls on Shabbat. Contains 3 seifim. When Rosh
Chodesh falls on Shabbat, for Maariv, Shacharit, and Mincha one prays seven blessings and says Yaaleh VeYavo in Avodah, and one does not mention Shabbat in Yaaleh VeYavo. They take out two Sifrei Torah and read from the first seven in the order of the day, and from the second the maftir reads "And on the Shabbat day and on your Rosh Chodesh days" until the end of the section, and the haftarah is' "The heavens are My throne" (Isaiah 66).(And if one mentions Rosh Chodesh there, see siman 284 seif 2.} Except Rosh Chodesh Elul that falls on Shabbat, on which the haftarah is "Unhappy, storm-tossed one" (Isaiah 54). {Rama: And some say "The heavens are My throne" (Tur and Mordechai chapter Bnei HaIr and Minhagim), and so they practice in these lands; but when Rosh Chodesh Av falls on Shabbat the haftarah is "Hear" (Avudraham and Mordechai chapter Bnei HaIr and Minhagim), and some say "The heavens are My throne," and that is primary where there is no custom (Terumat HaDeshen siman 19 and customs of Maharak). And if Rosh Chodesh occurs on one of the four [special Torah portions], they read the haftarah of that portion (Minhagim); and see below siman 685.) חוץ מראש חדש אלול שחל להיות בשבת שמפטירין עניי' סוערה: הגה ויש אומרים השמים כסאי (טור ומרדכי פרק בני העיר ומנהגים) וכן נוהגים במדינות אלו אבל ראש חדש אב שחל להיות בשבת מפטירין שמעו (אבודרהם ומרדכי פ' בני העיר ומנהגים) וי"א השמי' כסאי וכן עיקר במקום שאין מנהג (ת"ה סי' י"ט ומנהגי מהרא"ק) ואם אירע ראש חדש בד' פרשיות מפטירין בשל פרשה (מנהגים) ועיין לקמן סי' תרפ"ה:
