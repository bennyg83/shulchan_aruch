And although the Gemara explicitly said one does not read "The heavens are My throne" on Rosh Chodesh Av that falls on Shabbat — one may answer as Tosafot in Megillah that R' Haim holds like 'some say' that mourning applies from Rosh Chodesh, and we rule like 'some say' mourning does not apply on a week that contains Tisha BeAv, therefore we read "your new moons" on Shabbat when Tisha BeAv follows; likewise in Terumat HaDeshen a great one initially ruled to read "Hear" and a student told him Tosafot chapter Kol Shaah writes to read "The heavens are My throne" and he returned and said since from Tosafot's days we drink their words we should practice as they said — Terumat HaDeshen wrote; and it appears that is primary although I heard Tosafot Megillah wrote "Hear" — I did not know perhaps those Tosafot are a different edition, see there.
