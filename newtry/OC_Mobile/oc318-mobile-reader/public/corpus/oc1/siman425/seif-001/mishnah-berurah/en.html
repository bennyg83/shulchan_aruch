(א) And one does not mention, etc. — for he already mentioned Shabbat in the fourth blessing:
(ב) Seven in the order of the day — for ideally the maftir does not count toward the seven, as above siman 282 seif 4:
(ג) And from the second one reads — and they place the second Sefer Torah beside the first and say half-Kaddish on both:
(ד) And the haftarah is "The heavens," etc. — for Rosh Chodesh is mentioned there; if he forgot and read the week's haftarah he reads "The heavens are My throne" after it; if he remembered after he already blessed the concluding blessings he reads it without a blessing:
(ה) They read "Unhappy," etc. — because one may not skip one of the seven consolations — see below siman 428 seif 8:
(ו) And some say "The heavens," etc. — for in this haftarah there are two matters — remembrance of Rosh Chodesh and consolation of Jerusalem:
(ז) And so they practice — and the Acharonim wrote that nevertheless one may not skip one of the seven consolations; therefore when they read in parashat Ki Tavo "Sing, O barren one" they also finish with "Unhappy, storm-tossed" written after it:
