Seif 1 — and the haftarah is "The heavens are My throne," etc. Note — from all these haftarot that we override the week's haftarah before them and we do not say the frequent overrides the infrequent — it is proven that the frequent has only priority to precede when one can do both; but if one cannot do only one of them, the obligation of the day that was renewed now takes precedence and overrides the frequent. And I saw in Tosafot Sukkah 54 s.v. and further frequent and infrequent, etc., that likewise without [the case of] two cups they could ask thus; but it is truly astonishing, for without two cups since he would say only one of them the frequent is not relevant — only the day's obligation is preferable; since he answers that he says both, the intent is only to precede the frequent. And one cannot question the Gemara itself, for here it implies plainly that frequent precedes, and later 56a Rav holds the day's obligation is preferable — one may say: either the Gemara asks per the view that there too time precedes and holds frequent precedes; or the day's obligation is preferable specifically there where sukkah and time are separate matters — sukkah can be called the day's obligation against time since sukkah is a mitzvah by itself unrelated to the festival blessing; but Shabbat song and Rosh Chodesh song are both song matters — one cannot call Rosh Chodesh the day's obligation against Shabbat, therefore frequent applies. And what I wrote above that frequent priority is only to precede but not to override entirely — I wrote thus in my novellae in the omissions siman 463 plainly. But now I saw that in truth it is a question in Menachot regarding musafim nowadays and tomorrow's tamidim — if they lack enough for both, whether frequent or sanctified is preferable — and it is an unresolved question there. From the beginning there is no proof from today's tamidim and today's musafim, for there it is both frequent and sanctified together; but frequent alone one might say the day's obligation is preferable — only from the question on today's musafim and tomorrow's tamidim there is proof. In any case this resolves Tosafot's question — therefore he only asks initially one might say when overriding entirely the day's obligation beats frequent; therefore he only asks for two cups who says both — here he asks it is fit to precede the frequent. And one might say from Menachot there is no proof, for one might say when the frequent will also be done today the frequent overrides the other entirely; but there since frequent is only tomorrow and musafim today one might say musafim precede; nevertheless from the beginning there it is plain that today's tamidim and musafim — tamidim which are frequent and sanctified — one does musafim, implying frequent alone does not override musafim; and from our custom reading "tomorrow is the new moon" or "The heavens are My throne" and leaving the week's haftarah although frequent — it appears our custom follows the side that the day's obligation that renewed now beats frequent, like musafim today beat tamidim tomorrow. And I divided that for preceding frequent is preferable and for overriding entirely the day's obligation is preferable — we find similarly Yoreh Deah siman 335 regarding a wedding feast and a recovery feast — when he has both, recovery precedes; when he lacks both, wedding feast precedes; likewise in Midrash on eating and Bach there in Orach Chayim; and we find similarly elsewhere; if so one may say here likewise — investigate.
