In Magen Avraham s.k. 5 "then they say part of Joy and gladness" — meaning such as Shabbat Rosh Chodesh when the haftarah "The heavens are My throne" is also in Isaiah — they say "The heavens are My throne" and also part of "Joy and gladness" since it is one prophet; and likewise on other Shabbatot where the haftarah is not overridden because of "Joy and gladness" when it is in Isaiah they say with it part of "Joy and gladness." And see Levush 424 and Magen Avraham there that also on Chanukah they say part of "Joy and gladness," and this is not Rama's view as explained in Darkei Moshe end of siman 428 who brings Teshuvot Radbaz that one should not practice thus except for the seven haftarot in book one — see there; and perhaps Magen Avraham hinted there — see siman 225 seif 3, meaning per what is explained there one does not say "Joy and gladness" on Chanukah; but his wording is difficult; and see below in the gloss siman 428 what he wrote, and it was already explained siman 225 that it also implies one does not read "Joy and gladness" at all on Chanukah when it is another prophet:
