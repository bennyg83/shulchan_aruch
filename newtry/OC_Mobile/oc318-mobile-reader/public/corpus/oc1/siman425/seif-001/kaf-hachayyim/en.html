1) [Se'if 1] When Rosh Chodesh occurs on Shabbos, etc. It is stated in Beitzah 17a: When Shabbos occurs on Rosh Chodesh or Chol HaMoed, at Ma'ariv, Shacharis, and Minchah one recites seven blessings and mentions the occasion in the blessing of Avodah. If one omitted it, one must repeat the Amidah. Beis Yosef cites this. However, the statement that one must repeat if it was omitted, which appears to include Ma'ariv, applies specifically to Chol HaMoed. On Rosh Chodesh, one who omitted it at Ma'ariv does not repeat, as stated above in siman 422, se'if 1. See there.
