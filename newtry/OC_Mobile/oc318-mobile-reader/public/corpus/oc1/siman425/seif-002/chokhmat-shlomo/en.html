There seif 3 — Rosh Chodesh that falls on Shabbat, etc. Note — I was asked: if one prayed Musaf of Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh mentioning only Shabbat Musaf, is he obligated to return and pray? I answered plainly since he did not mention Rosh Chodesh Musaf he must return, as if he omitted Yom Tov or Shabbat Musaf — Shulchan Aruch siman 268 is explicit; and further proof from what Mabit wrote in a responsum brought in Baer Heitev laws of Rosh Hashanah siman 591 — if he said on the second day Musaf he need not return — implying if he prayed day one and said Musaf he returns; so it is explicit if he did not mention Rosh Chodesh in Musaf he returns — so it is settled and correct.
