(4) His eyes downward to the earth, etc. In Tzror HaMor he wrote that one who prays the eighteen blessings — the Shechinah is opposite him; therefore it is forbidden to open his eyes and look at the wall — end quote. And She'eirei Knesset HaGedolah wrote: I heard in the name of the Zohar that anyone who does not close his eyes during the prayer of the eighteen blessings does not merit to see the face of the Shechinah when his soul departs; and from then on my practice has been to close my eyes — end quote. And thus warned in this Matteh Moshe and Shelah page 255. And a little investigation is needed in siman 90 seif 23 why one may not pray opposite illustrated garments — see there. And I saw in Pesakim of Tosafos he wrote: I saw pious men and men of action — when they mention the Name within the prayer they lift their eyes upward; but during the rest of the prayer they do not look even incidentally at the wall — end quote. And through this is resolved the matter that one may not pray opposite illustrated garments, because when they mention the Name they will look at them. However from the words of the Levush it does not imply so, for if so he would resolve the difficulty why windows are required because of mentioning the Name; rather it implies only at the beginning of prayer one looks upward — and thus implies from the Bach. Also according to this one may say that because of the beginning of prayer one may not pray opposite illustrated garments; and see above siman 90 seif 4. In Sefer Devar Eliyahu on Psalm 123 I explained further; and the Arizal would pray within the book in order to concentrate greatly (kavvanah). And Magen Avraham wrote siman 53: Everything is according to what a person feels in his holiness — end quote. And Maharil would take the siddur in his right hand and pray from it, and in his left he would take the coat over his heart.
