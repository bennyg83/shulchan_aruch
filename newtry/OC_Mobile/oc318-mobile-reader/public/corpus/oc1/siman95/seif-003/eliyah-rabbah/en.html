(3) Some say when standing, etc. And Nachalas Tzvi wrote: I do not know why the Levush omitted this — end quote. And it is possible that he holds this applies when standing in his place in his house; but when he has already gone from his place in order to pray — and all the more when going to the synagogue — it is not required. It also appears that one need not turn backward in order to walk before himself — that is, when standing at the wall. And in Rokeach he wrote the reason, for it is written "three approaches to prayer" — "and Abraham approached," "and Judah approached," "and Elijah approached"; and it is further written "and their feet were a straight foot" — "their feet" are two, "foot" is one; and afterward "straight" — he straightens his feet; "straight" (yesharah) has the letters of shirah (song) and its gematria is tefillah (prayer).
