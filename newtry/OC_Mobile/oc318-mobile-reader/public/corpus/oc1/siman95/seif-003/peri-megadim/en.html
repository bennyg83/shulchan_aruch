The right - See Taz. See Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 5 of Hilchos Tefillah, paragraph 6, which implies like Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh. Rambam likewise writes that one stands like a servant before his master, with dread, awe, and fear; see there. In Tur, siman 91, he explains that during a time of distress, etc.; this is cited in Tzemach Tzedek, seif 6. The Mechaber omitted it because he follows his own view and rules like Rambam. Rama did not add a gloss here because he maintains that this is understood logically. The wording with which the Mechaber copied Rambam's words does not explicitly indicate the opposite, and therefore Rama remained silent.
