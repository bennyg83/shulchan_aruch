To resemble [angels] — Ateret Tzvi in Tur ch. 1: as angels, this next to this; and ch. 2: as kohanim who walk on the ramp with heel beside big toe. Beit Yosef challenged in our Gemara "as angels" — why did he bring the Yerushalmi? Taz answered to explain "straight" that Onkelos translated as "positioned" — meaning this next to this; ch. 2 as kohanim, etc. — implying angels this next to this, and Bach; and in Bach the Tur wrote as kohanim in the service of prayer, and service is one — see there. And in Rambam ch. 5, Laws of Prayer, it is brought from the Temple that there is no mention of this that in service we require equating the feet. And if one did not position one's feet at all but only they are far from each other — it is explained in Rambam ch. 5, Laws of Prayer 1:8 that eight things do not invalidate [prayer], and Bach and Eliyah Rabbah; and when sitting in a carriage nevertheless position one's feet — see Ateret Tzvi.
