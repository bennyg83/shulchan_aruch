(1) [seif 1] He should align his feet, etc. — And the reason one must align his feet appears to be that since he stands to speak with the Shechinah he must remove all bodily thoughts from his heart and see himself as among the ministering angels. And Mahari Abohav, may his memory be blessed, wrote another reason — that it is a hint that movement has departed from him to flee and not to attain any object apart from God (Beit Yosef, Levush). And the Levush wrote another reason — that when standing thus he does not stand firmly on his feet, and this indicates that he stands in awe and fear and submission before his Creator, may He be blessed. And it seems to me another reason — that he stands thus to show as if his legs are bound together and he cannot walk; and through this he will come to pray slowly and return his mind word by word, and no letter will be lacking, and the meaning of the words and the kavvanot of the Names.
