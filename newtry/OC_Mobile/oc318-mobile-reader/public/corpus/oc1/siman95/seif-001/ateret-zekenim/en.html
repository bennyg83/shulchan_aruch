He should align his feet, etc. And some have the practice of leaving space above at the toes because it is written "and their feet were like a calf's foot" — but this is nothing, for in the Gemara they only mentioned that one should align them in a manner that they resemble one (R' Yitzchak).

And some say: when standing, etc., he should walk before himself three steps, as it is written "three approaches to prayer" — "and Abraham approached," "and Judah approached," "and Elijah approached" (Rokeach). And he should stand: at Shacharis for prayer when the prayer leader begins "Tehillos to God on High"; at Minchah when the prayer leader descends before the ark; and at Maariv when the prayer leader begins Kaddish (Maharil).
