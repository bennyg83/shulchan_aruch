A) [Section 1] The preparation of his feet, etc. and the reason that his feet need to be prepared seems to be per the fact that one who is about to speak with the Shekinah should remove all thoughts of the body from his heart and see as if he is one of the angels of the servant, and from the late Rabbi I wrote another reason which is a hint that the movement away from him is to flee and not to obtain any object apart from Hashem, 22, clothing, and the clothing is written further 11 per the fact that the Omar does not stand firmly on his feet and this is a teacher who stands In fear and awe and in humility before his Creator 10, and it seems to me that he stood like this to show that his feet were tied together and he would not be able to walk.
