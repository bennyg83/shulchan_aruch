Seif katan 1: His hands, etc. In the concluding words, the text should read: "Orach Chaim, part 1, siman 33."
Within the palm. This means the right thumb within the right palm and the left thumb within the left palm. He concludes there, after citing the words of the Arizal and the Ramak: They all ascend to one place, to incorporate judgment within mercy, and a God-fearing person should satisfy both opinions.
