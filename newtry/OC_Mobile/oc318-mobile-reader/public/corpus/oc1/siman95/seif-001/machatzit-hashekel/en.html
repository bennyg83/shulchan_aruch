(s"k 1) His hands, etc. And in Shulchan Aruch Mechaber and commentaries, ch. 1, siman 33, thus it should be read.

Within the palm of the hand — that is, the right thumb within the palm of the right hand, and the left thumb within the palm of the left hand. And he concluded there, and thus: After he wrote the words of the Ari to the Rama M'Pano, and everything ascends to one place to include judgment in mercy, and one who fears Heaven fulfills both of them — end quote.
