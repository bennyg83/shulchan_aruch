To resemble the angels. The Tur cites the Yerushalmi, where there is a dispute, one opinion maintaining that the feet should be positioned like those of the kohanim. Beit Yosef asks why the Tur cites this. It appears to me that he derives from there how to explain the Targum, which renders "straight" as "aligned." What does aligned mean, one foot beside the other or the heel of one beside the other? He therefore cites the Yerushalmi, for the opinion that says one should resemble the angels requires one foot beside the other, and this is the meaning of the angels' "straight" feet. Thus, the Targum's intention is also this, since it comes to explain the phrase "a straight foot." See what I wrote in siman 98, with Hashem's help.
