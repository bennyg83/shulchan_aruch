(1) In aligning them as if they were only one, etc. — Explanation: to exclude one who says in the Yerushalmi that it suffices with the heel beside the big toe; he teaches us that this does not suffice, but we require actual alignment one beside the other. However if one transgressed and prayed with the heel beside the big toe — the Bach and Olas Tamid wrote that he need not return and pray again; but if he prayed without aligning the feet at all he must return and pray — end quote. In my humble opinion it appears from the Rambam chapter 5 of Hilchos Tefillah that aligning the feet is only lechatchilah and one need not return and pray even if he did not align at all; nevertheless it appears he has permission in his hand to pray a voluntary prayer without renewal, as above siman 94 s"k 9. Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: Some are accustomed to leave space above at the toes because it is written "and their feet were like a calf's foot" — end quote. And Olas Tamid wrote: Even though he concludes that this is not required because our Gemara did not mention anything of this, nevertheless it appears that one who practices this is acting with piety — end quote. In my humble opinion, from the language of Rabbeinu Yonah "and this is nothing, for in the Gemara they only mentioned that they should resemble one" — end quote — it is implied that they act improperly when they leave space above, for we specifically require that they resemble one; and thus it also appears in Abudraham page 35.
