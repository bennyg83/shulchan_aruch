Downward — Be'er Heitev. And what is written in Shulchan Aruch Knesset HaGedolah "I heard," etc. — Mishna Berurah wrote that it is in the Zohar in Vaetchanan: one who opens his eyes during prayer — woe to him, how can he open his eyes, etc. And if one prays from a siddur with eyes open to see in the book — we have no concern; thus they wrote in the name of Sefer Tzitzos Chaim and Minchas Tam z"l, and likewise implies the language of the Zohar; see below siman 96 and below siman 98. Mishna Berurah wrote there are those who are accustomed that when they mention the Omnipresent within the prayer they lift their eyes upward, and Mor uKetziah wrote that it is not necessary.
