Downward. Regarding those who raise their heads and eyes upward as though looking at the ceiling, the angels mock them. Sefer Chasidim, siman 18. Before the Amidah, however, he should look toward the windows in that wall. See Ateres Zekeinim. One who opens his eyes during prayer brings the Angel of Death upon himself prematurely. Taz, siman 91. The Knesses HaGedolah writes: I heard that anyone who does not close his eyes during the Amidah will not merit seeing the face of the Shechinah when his soul departs; from then onward, my practice has been to close my eyes.
