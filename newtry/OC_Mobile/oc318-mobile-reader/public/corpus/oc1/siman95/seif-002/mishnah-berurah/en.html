(4) That his eyes should be, etc. — see above siman 90, skh 8 in Mishna Berurah; and Bach wrote that before beginning to pray one should look through the windows toward the heavens so that one's heart will be humbled.

(5) Downward to the ground — and those who raise their heads and eyes upward as if looking at the roof — the angels mock them [Shulchan Aruch haRav 8, siman 8]. And Peri Megadim wrote that one should not make unusual movement; for an individual it is permitted and not in congregation, and especially to raise one's voice and the like. Later authorities wrote that anyone who does not close his eyes during the Eighteen Blessings will not merit to see the face of the Shechinah at the departure of his soul; however if one prays from a siddur with one's eyes open in order to see in it — we have no concern with this. And see above siman 91, skh 6 in Mishna Berurah.
