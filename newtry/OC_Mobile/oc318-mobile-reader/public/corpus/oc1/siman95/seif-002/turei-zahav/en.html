That his eyes should be downward. Rashi explains in the chapter Ein Omdin, as cited by the Tur in siman 90, that a synagogue should have windows so that one can look toward Heaven and humble his heart. This is difficult, for here one is required to direct his eyes downward. Mahari Abuhav answers that the intent is not that he should look toward Heaven throughout the entire prayer, but that if he glances toward Heaven momentarily, his heart will be humbled.
