That his eyes should be downward. And that which Rashi explained in the chapter "Ein Omdim" — the Tur brought in siman 90 that there must be windows in the synagogue so one looks toward the heavens and one's heart is humbled. It is difficult, for here it is necessary that one's eyes be downward. He answered in the name of Avuhav that the intent is not that one should look toward the heavens throughout the prayer, but by way of metaphor: if he looks toward the heavens his heart will be humbled.
