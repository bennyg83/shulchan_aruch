(2) There, he should align his feet one beside the other, etc. — Explanation: to exclude one who says in the Yerushalmi, brought in the Tur and Beit Yosef, that it suffices with the heel beside the big toe; he teaches us that this does not suffice, but we require actual alignment one beside the other. However if one transgressed and prayed without aligning the feet at all and did not even place the heel beside the big toe — it appears from the Bach that he must return and pray; and Olas Torah ot 1 wrote there is doubt for practice — see there. However from the words of the Rambam chapter 5 of Hilchos Tefillah law 1 and law 4 it appears that aligning the feet bedieved is not me'akev — see there. And thus too Eliyah Rabbah ot 1; and thus too Mahari Abohav, may his memory be blessed, in Sefer Lev Chayyim page 19a and in Sefer Mateh Yehuda siman 94 ot 1; Mishnat Zakkenim ot 1; and thus agreed Maamar Mordechai ot 1, Noda BiYehuda ot 1. However what Eliyah Rabbah wrote there that he has permission in his hand to pray a voluntary prayer without renewal as above siman 94 s"k 9 — we do not accept this, because a voluntary prayer requires kavvanah from beginning to end, and if he cannot concentrate well we apply to him "Why do I need your many sacrifices" as written at the end of siman 107; and now it is likely that he cannot concentrate from beginning to end as written in the Rama at the beginning of siman 101; therefore in this era we do not pray voluntary prayers, as will be explained in its place be'ezras Hashem. But bedieved if one transgressed and prayed without aligning the feet at all — neither this beside that nor heel beside big toe — he does not return and pray at all, even a voluntary prayer; and see above siman 94 ot 34.
