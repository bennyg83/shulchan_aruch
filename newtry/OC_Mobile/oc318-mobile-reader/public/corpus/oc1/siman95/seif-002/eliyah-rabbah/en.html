(2) To resemble the ministering angels, etc. And furthermore when standing thus he does not stand firmly — and this indicates that he stands in awe and submission. And Mahari Abohav wrote: It is a hint that movement has departed from him to flee and not to attain any object apart from God — end quote. And the opinion of Rabbeinu Bachya on the parsha of Korach agrees with this, that he wrote to show as if his legs are bound — his desire does not depend on himself and it is not in his power to carry out his will if the Master of the house does not agree by His hand. And since the movement of the legs is greater than the movement of the hands to advance his benefit and to repel his harm, therefore they practiced aligning the feet and not the hands — end quote.
