That they should be — Ateret Tzvi and Bach interpret: before beginning prayer one should look through the windows and one's heart will be humbled — meaning the verse "Let us lift our hearts to our palms," etc. See Prisha in the name of R' Refael Yosef and see Rashi in the first chapter of Taanis on "his inside like his outside" (as in his hands spread out, so his heart) — see interpretation of R' Refael Yosef; and see Parashat Derechim, derech aravah, that brought this — see there.
