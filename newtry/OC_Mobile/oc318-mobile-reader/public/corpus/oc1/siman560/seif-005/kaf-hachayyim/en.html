However, Responsa She’eilas Yaavetz, siman 169, wrote that leaving one area of one cubit by one cubit is sufficient for all the rooms of the house, even if they are numerous, see there. Birkei Yosef, entry 3; Shaarei Teshuvah. Nevertheless, when I added for myself a pleasant room for the mitzvah of sukkah, I left opposite the entrance several handbreadths that were not covered with white plaster, of the sort applied over the plaster already coating the walls. It is possible that even when building a new house, it is sufficient to leave a space without plaster, because its place is clearly recognizable. It seems to me that the custom has become widespread to be lenient in this manner, and all the more so regarding this room. I was additionally lenient not to insist upon the measure of one cubit, for a stringency is sufficient, as stated in the Shulchan Aruch, seif 2, that even a small amount suffices. She’eilas Yaavetz there; Shaarei Teshuvah there.
