(There in the Mechaber, se'if 2.) In the gloss, to break a cup. Marginal note: The Maharit wrote in Tzafnas Paneach, parashas Devarim, to take specifically glass ones, for it has a remedy after it is broken, as a hint to the redemption, which is not so of an earthenware vessel; and see the responsa of Moreinu HaRav R' Getz in Avnei Shoham, siman 51.
