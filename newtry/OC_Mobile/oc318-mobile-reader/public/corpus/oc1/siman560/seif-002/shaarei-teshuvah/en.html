(In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 2) They customarily break a cup, etc. And Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Matteh Moshe that this is the reason they break a pot at the writing of the tenaim; and see Mishbetzos Zahav, that it is in order to startle and to remember that the joy is not complete nowadays; and see Even HaShoham, siman 51, that under the chuppah one should break specifically a glass cup; and so in Tzafnas Paneach of the Maharit he wrote that it is a hint; see there. And the source of the matter is from Berachos, chapter "Ein Omdin," at the wedding feast of Mar the son of Ravina, that he broke a white glass before them and they were saddened; and the Tosafos wrote: from here they customarily break a cup, etc.; see there.
