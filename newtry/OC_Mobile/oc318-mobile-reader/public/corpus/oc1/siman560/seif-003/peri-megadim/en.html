An empty place. See the Taz, Bava Basra 60b; and see the Levush, who did not write "an empty place," but only to leave over one cooked dish of those that are customarily made, in accordance with the view of the Tur. And the Taz disagrees: for if one left over one cooked dish of those that are customary in that place at a meal, then it is recognizable that something is missing, and afterward one may place on the table and arrange all the plates he has without an empty place; but when one omitted one cooked dish that is not his practice to make at every meal, then a recognizable empty place on the table is required. And accordingly, in a place where they do not arrange everything on the table at once, and an empty place is not a recognizable sign, one must omit a cooked dish that is customary at every meal.
