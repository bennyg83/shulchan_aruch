From the destruction, Taz and Magen Avraham: the world practices like Tur that even in building whitewashed and engraved, whatever remains amah by amah is permitted. Eshel Avraham letter 1: one may say in each room and room one must leave amah by amah. And one who blackens and paints King Zedekiah and the act of the Talmud — even though there is great remembrance of the destruction, nevertheless it is painting and forbidden, for we require that one's desire not be filled — Magen Avraham on this. And if so even if afterward he covers the rooms with panels and plaster and leaves empty amah by amah — all that initially he plastered his entire house, it is forbidden to fill his desire as we say. And this amah by amah — it appears we require a square, not long and narrow. Also opposite the opening — meaning near the opening; Magen Avraham on this; even though the word "negd" its meaning per the majority is opposite and facing — see Meir Netiv; nevertheless sometimes its meaning is from the side — see Targum Yonatan on "vateshev mineged," Mishlei "lekh mineged l'ish kesil" 14:7, Rashi there.
