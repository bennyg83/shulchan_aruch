(s.k. 1) Whitewashed and engraved. These are types of painting. Meaning: that which is called whitewashed does not mean plastered with lime; for if so it would be difficult, for in whitewashed and engraved it teaches that it has no remedy even if one leaves an amah by amah on the upper story it does not help; and in the conclusion it teaches that leaving an amah by amah on the upper story helps — therefore it wrote that also whitewashed means a type of painting; therefore it has no remedy. But when he plasters with lime and there is no painting, an amah by amah on the upper story helps. (And per this explanation it must be that even plastering below with clay, since he plastered over it and there are paintings on it, it is forbidden.) And it is possible, etc. — meaning always whitewashed means as literally plastered with lime without painting, and nevertheless there is no remedy — it deals with when there is no clay at all. And in the conclusion that teaches plastering with clay below — therefore leaving a remnant helps. And both explanations Rav Bet Yosef wrote against Rambam who is like the language of Shulchan Aruch. But in Shulchan Aruch the main point is per the second explanation; for per the first explanation, how when there is painting there is no remedy — and therefore even if below there is clay as stated, where does it write above siman 551 seif 2 that from Rosh Chodesh they diminish building of painting and engraving — behold it is forbidden to build them at all? And certainly in Rambam it does not say that the words above siman 551 are Rambam's language; nevertheless Rambam did not write thus regarding crafts of a father, only regarding fast that they decree on rains (and from this Rav Bet Yosef learns for Rosh Chodesh Av); if so one may say it deals with when the Temple stood that it was permitted to paint and engrave; but for Rav Bet Yosef the main point must be per the second explanation; and if so if clay below is permitted through leaving an amah by amah on the upper story — this passage siman 551 is not relevant:
