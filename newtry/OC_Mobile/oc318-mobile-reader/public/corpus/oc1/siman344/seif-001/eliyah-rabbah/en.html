[1] [Levush] In order to leave quickly, etc. The Magen Avraham writes this in the name of Tosafot and the Rosh, and infers that other rabbinic prohibitions remain prohibited for him, even though ordinarily a doubt concerning a rabbinic prohibition is ruled leniently, because once Shabbos restrictions have taken effect upon him he is bound by all of them, like the second day of Yom Tov. This requires study. Tosafot in Perek Klal Gadol do not write this. The Rosh gives this reason only to show that even according to the opinion that techum is d'oraisa, leniency exists because of danger; according to the opinion that techum is d'rabbanan, no such reason is needed because a rabbinic doubt is lenient. The Ran and Taz indeed explain the permission because techum is rabbinic.
[2] [Levush] In order to sustain him for preservation of life, etc. Therefore, if he has what to eat, such as bread, it is prohibited to cook another dish for him, as stated by the Taz.
