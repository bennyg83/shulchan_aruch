1) [Seif 1] One traveling in a wilderness, etc. The same applies if one was held captive among non-Jews and forgot which day is Shabbos. Yerushalmi, chapter Klal Gadol. In such a case, however, he may sometimes determine from their calculations on which day Shabbos will occur. He must therefore pay attention so that he does not desecrate Shabbos.
2) There: He counts seven days, etc. Those who live or travel beneath the pole, where daytime extends for one month, two months, or even six months, must count six days of twenty-four equal hours according to our reckoning. Mor U'Ketziah; Machazik Berachah, paragraph 4; Shaarei Teshuvah. See above, siman 261, paragraph 19.
