Seif 1. One who travels in a desert, etc. — we learn in the first chapter of Shabbat daf 5b: Rava asked about a nut in a vessel floating on water — do we follow the nut or the vessel, for the nut is at rest but the vessel moves; and similarly in Nazir daf 64a regarding a corpse in a vessel floating on water — do we follow the vessel or the corpse; I wondered what he asks — behold this is an explicit mishnah in tractate Parah chapter 10 mishnah 3: ashes of a sin-offering that a pure creeping creature touched — one placed it on top of them; R' Eliezer purifies and the Sages defile — Rashi, Rosh, and Ra'avad there: they dispute whether we follow the vessel, which is pure and rests in a pure place, or the creeping creature, and it rests in an impure place; if so the dispute is explicit in Parah — why does he ask in these two places. One can answer that Parah is different — for afterward Rambam and Ra'avad wrote on the dispute of R' Akiva and the Sages regarding "caught" that it is like "placed" — therefore they compared it to Shabbat; and likewise in Zevachim as Tosefta wrote there; and one cannot say this is a decree upon a decree as Tosefta wrote later regarding defiling even when standing on an oven with ashes in his hand outside the oven — a decree upon a decree — it requires study; also difficult for me mishnah in chapter "placed" chapter 5 mishnah 4: one placed a vessel on a vessel — they did not disqualify the upper one; and it is explicit there in Tosefta and in Tosafos Yoma regarding "on it" for oil — that if the verse had not written "on it" it would be considered placing oil even in a vessel; it is proven we follow the oil and not the vessel — examine well:
