דין ההולך במדבר בשבת. ובו ב סעיפים:
The law of one who travels in a desert on Shabbat. And in it are 2 seifim: One who travels in a desert and does not know when it is Shabbat counts seven days from the day it entered his mind that he forgot, and sanctifies the seventh with kiddush and havdalah; and if he has something with which to support himself it is forbidden for him to do melacha at all until what he has is finished — and then he does melacha every day, even on the day he sanctifies, for his limited livelihood; and he is permitted to walk on it every day, even on the day he sanctifies:
