In Shulchan Aruch seif 2: if he knew the day, etc. — see responsum of Maharam of Padua siman 28: even in such a case he counts seven from Shabbat; he gave reason why not permitted melacha three days near departure — people do not travel three days before Shabbat; see in Mor U'Ketzia those traveling under the pole where day lengthens a month and two months and six months — count six days of twenty-four hours like ours:
