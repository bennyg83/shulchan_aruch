In Magen Avraham s.k. 1: and even though most days, etc. — I heard a challenge to Rambam: Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 183 — a woman from birth counts seven days of niddah and eleven days of zivah — zivah days outnumber niddah days — how find an opening for a mistaken woman following majority zivah days, not applicable as fixed since not recognizable in place, unlike Shabbat. In my humble opinion no difficulty: zivah days mean she is clean of blood — Scripture calls it "outside her regular time"; how say "if you see blood" — follow majority days she is clean and it would be zivah law; rather say this blood is from days likely to be niddah; since zivah days are majority, doubt and mistaken; this is clear to me; see Niddah daf 57b beginning chapter "One who sees a stain" — if majority of her days are impure, see there; see Chasam Sofer Orach Chayim siman 178:
