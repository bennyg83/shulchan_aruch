"And there is one who disagrees." This refers to the dispute whether women are obligated in Havdalah. The view that exempts them treats Havdalah as a time-bound positive mitzvah that is not inseparably connected with the mitzvah of Shabbos observance. The view that obligates them compares Havdalah to Kiddush, for whoever is included in "Shamor" is included in "Zachor," and women are therefore obligated in the sanctification that marks both the entrance and departure of Shabbos. According to the stringent opinion, a woman should hear Havdalah from a man who is obligated. Where that is not possible, the authorities discuss whether she may recite Havdalah herself. Since women customarily recite blessings over time-bound positive mitzvot, there is a substantial basis for permitting her to recite it. Nevertheless, where a man can discharge her obligation, that course is preferable.
