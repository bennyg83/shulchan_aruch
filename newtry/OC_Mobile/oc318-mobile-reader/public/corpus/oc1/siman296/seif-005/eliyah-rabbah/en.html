Levush: Nevertheless, it is preferable, etc. Magen Avraham objects that beer may perhaps be preferable to defective wine. After all, seif 3 permits him to eat before Havdalah rather than recite Havdalah over a defective cup, and on Motzaei Pesach Havdalah is recited over beer because it is beloved. It appears to me that Rama speaks of a place where beer is not considered the local beverage. Thus far his words. This does not appear correct. Rama and Levush refer to beer that is the local beverage, which they mentioned earlier. Levush implies this even more clearly from his words, “except on Motzaei Pesach,” etc., because there too he speaks of beer that is the local beverage, as he himself writes afterward. I also find proof from Mordechai in the chapter Arvei Pesachim, who writes that it is preferable to recite Kiddush over bread rather than over beer, even when beer is the local beverage, because beer nevertheless does not possess the status of wine. There is an additional difficulty. Seif 3 speaks only where the cup contains less than a reviis. Regarding a defective cup, however, it appears that one may not eat, but must recite Havdalah over the defective cup. This is explicit in Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh, according to our ruling in siman 182 that after the fact one may recite a blessing over a defective cup. Rabbeinu Yerucham, end of netiv 62, writes similarly. Avudraham, however, implies somewhat otherwise. His words are very difficult when examined against Rosh in the chapter Elu Devarim and require study. It is also astonishing that this contradicts what Magen Avraham himself rules in the laws of Bircas HaMazon, that a cup is required only as the preferred manner of performing the mitzvah.
