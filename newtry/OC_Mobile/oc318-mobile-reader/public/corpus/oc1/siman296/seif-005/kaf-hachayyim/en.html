5) There: The cup must not be blemished. The same applies to the requirement that it be whole, meaning that the cup must not be broken, as Tur writes. The same also applies to the other requirements for a cup of Birkas HaMazon. Maran Beis Yosef writes regarding the Kiddush cup above in siman 271: Rambam further writes there that it is subject to all the laws of a cup of Birkas HaMazon. It requires rinsing inside and out, must be full, held in the right hand without assistance from the left, and raised at least one tefach above the ground. These are his words. The Shulchan Aruch rules this way there in siman 271, seif 10; see there. This is obvious. Mateh Yehudah, paragraph 2.
