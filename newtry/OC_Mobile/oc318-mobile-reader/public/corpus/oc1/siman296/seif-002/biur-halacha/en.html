If it is chamar medinah — and even per the poskim who are stringent above in siman 472 seif 9 regarding Kiddush, for Havdalah all agree they make Havdalah as stated explicitly in the Gemara — Ameimar made Havdalah on beer because it is chamar medinah. And it appears from the Rama above in siman 182 seif 2 in the gloss that where wine is available, even if expensive, it is called available and one makes Havdalah on wine; however if beer is more beloved to him than wine he may make Kiddush on beer as below in the gloss. Peri Megadim in siman 459 wrote regarding those careful about drinking beer because of chadash — it requires study when he has only beer how he makes Havdalah; in my opinion there is also study here if he is concerned for himself that this cup is a cup of prohibition — how does he discharge Havdalah by what another blesses on it unless he accepted stringency as a chumra not as true prohibition.
