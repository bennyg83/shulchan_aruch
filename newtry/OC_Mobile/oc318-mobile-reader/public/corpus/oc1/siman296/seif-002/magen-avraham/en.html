And it is better, etc. This requires study. Avudraham states that it is better to recite Havdalah over a defective cup than to recite Havdalah without wine. His meaning is that if one has no wine at all, he must recite the Havdalah formula without wine, as stated at the end of siman 299. If he has a defective cup, it is preferable to recite Havdalah over it, as stated at the end of siman 182. Beer, however, may perhaps be preferable to defective wine. We see that one may eat before Havdalah rather than recite a blessing over a defective cup, as stated in seif 3. On Motzaei Pesach people also recite Havdalah over beer because it is beloved. It appears to me that Rama speaks of a place where beer is not considered the local beverage. Olas Shabbos overlooked this gloss. And the custom is to recite Havdalah. It appears to me that this applies specifically where beer is considered the local beverage. In Germany it is forbidden, because even if one has no wine at all one may not recite Havdalah over beer. All the more so where he has wine and simply does not favor it.
