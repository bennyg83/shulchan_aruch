The Rambam, etc. And see the end of this siman. Meaning, the dispute at the end of this siman depends on this, whether women are obligated in havdalah. According to the one who says havdalah is Torah law, women are certainly obligated; but according to the one who says havdalah is rabbinic, the view of the Maggid Mishneh and those with him is that nevertheless women are also obligated, for every rabbinic enactment was instituted in the likeness of Torah law. The Orchot Chayim and those with him hold that since it is a positive time-bound commandment, they did not institute it for women.
