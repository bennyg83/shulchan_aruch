Before finishing Borei peri hagafen. There is no explanation for this; Levush wrote not to spill until beginning the blessing first so the beginning of the blessing not be on a blemished cup — and there is no reason, for if you consider this blemished, the end of the blessing must also be on an unblemished cup, which is obvious the whole blessing should be on a fit cup; better to wait until end of blessing. Further, blemished applies only if he drank from it; and it does not seem a fine custom to bless and spill on the ground — there is no greater degradation of a blessing; likewise Sefer Chasidim siman 111 after blessing on drinks forbidden to spill except it appears to practice as found in a book Yesh Nochalin — the chassid Mahari Weil wrote when filling the Havdalah cup they fill it to the brim and still spill somewhat onto the ground because the vessel is very full — for omen wine spilled like water — afterward spill nothing — very correct and so I have practiced. Main explanation of "any house where wine not spilled like water" — I do not think Sages commanded spilling wine, rather the opposite there is prohibition; and as siman 171 — they draw wine in pipes before the groom receiving in vessel at pipe mouth; rather intent of "wine spilled like water" is that even if damage occurred — wine already spilled inadvertently by someone in the house and people tend to anger over this — therefore they said any house where spilled wine is not considered like water but they get angry — then no sign of blessing, for they say Sotah ch. 1 every angry house is sign of poverty; therefore no proof to spill wine for destruction even with intent for blessing; therefore lessen spilling at Motza'ei Shabbat Havdalah cup due to waste of drink.
