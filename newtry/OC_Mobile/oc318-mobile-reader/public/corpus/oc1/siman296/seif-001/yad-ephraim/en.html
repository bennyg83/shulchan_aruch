In Magen Avraham, seif katan 4, on the words "because," etc. This requires study, because above a different reason was given, namely that pouring first would constitute a defect and a degradation of the mitzvah. Perhaps the text should read, "and also because of an interruption." Although above it appears that the pouring itself does not create a concern of interruption, it is possible that pouring together with extinguishing the lamp constitutes a slight interruption. It appears more likely, however, that there is a scribal error here and that the text should read, "as stated above in the name of Bach," referring to the words of Sefer Chasidim cited above in seif katan 2, which Taz also cites.
