The laws of Havdalah on wine. And in it 8 seifim:
The order of Havdalah — wine, spices, Havdalah candle; and your mnemonic is yud-beit-nun-hey. And one must take care the cup not be blemished.And the custom is to say before Havdalah at home: Hinei el yeshuati, etc.; Kos yeshuot esa, etc.; LaYehudim hayetah orah, etc., for a good omen. At Havdalah they set eyes on cup and candle. Custom to spill wine on the ground before finishing Borei peri hagafen so the cup not be blemished; reason for spilling — they say any house where wine was not spilled like water has no sign of blessing; done for good omen at week start. Also spill after Havdalah, extinguish candle in it, moisten eyes with it from affection for the mitzvah.
