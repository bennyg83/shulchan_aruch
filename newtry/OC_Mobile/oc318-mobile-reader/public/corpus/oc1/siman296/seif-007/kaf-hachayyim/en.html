7) Shaar HaKavanos writes that under no circumstances should water be added to the Havdalah cup. Pri Etz Chaim, gate 18, chapter 24, writes the same. It appears that this applies when another option is available. If it is impossible, such as when one has less than a revi'is and must add water to complete the revi'is, he should not omit Havdalah for this reason.
