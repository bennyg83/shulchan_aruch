1) [Seif 1] Every person is obligated to give, etc. Even a poor person who is supported by charity is obligated, as with the four cups of Pesach. This differs from other charity, regarding which he is obligated only once a year in order to fulfill the mitzvah of charity, as written in Yoreh De'ah, simanim 248 and 249. Bach; Taz, s.k. 1; Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur, os 1; Olas Shabbos, os 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. The Pri Chadash wrote that a poor person supported by charity is exempt from gifts to the poor. However, Mateh Yehudah wrote that the primary ruling follows the Bach and those who agree with him, analogous to the mitzvah of mishloach manos, regarding which the Pri Chadash himself wrote below, siman 695, that a poor person supported by charity is obligated; see there. So too agreed Birkei Yosef, os 2, and in his sefer Machazik Berachah, os 1; Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, os 2; Nehar Shalom, os 1; Chemed Moshe, os 2; Bigdei Yesha; Chayei Adam, klal 155, os 28; Mishna Berurah, os 1.
