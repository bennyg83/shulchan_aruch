1) [Seif 1] Every person is obligated to give, etc. Even a poor person who is supported by charity is obligated, as with the four cups of Pesach. This differs from other charity, regarding which he is obligated only once a year in order to fulfill the mitzvah of charity, as written in Yoreh De'ah, simanim 248 and 249. Bach; Taz, s.k. 1; Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur, os 1; Olas Shabbos, os 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. The Pri Chadash wrote that a poor person supported by charity is exempt from gifts to the poor. However, Mateh Yehudah wrote that the primary ruling follows the Bach and those who agree with him, analogous to the mitzvah of mishloach manos, regarding which the Pri Chadash himself wrote below, siman 695, that a poor person supported by charity is obligated; see there. So too agreed Birkei Yosef, os 2, and in his sefer Machazik Berachah, os 1; Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, os 2; Nehar Shalom, os 1; Chemed Moshe, os 2; Bigdei Yesha; Chayei Adam, klal 155, os 28; Mishna Berurah, os 1.
2) Nevertheless, they may exchange gifts, so that this poor person gives two gifts to two poor people and they then give back to him, analogous to mishloach manos. Mateh Yehudah; Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1; Yafeh LaLev, volume 2, siman 695, os 17; Ben Ish Chai, Parashas Tetzaveh, os 76; Mishna Berurah, os 2. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 251, seif 12, where the same law is stated regarding charity; see there.
3) Ibid. Every person is obligated, etc. A woman is also obligated in gifts to the poor and mishloach manos, just as a man is, as written below in siman 695, seif 4, in the Rama's gloss; see there.
4) Ibid. Every person is obligated to give, etc. Regarding why no blessing is recited over mishloach manos and gifts to the poor, as is recited over reading the Megillah, the Rashba wrote in Responsa, siman 18, that this is because the recipients may refuse to accept them. See further there for the reason regarding all mitzvos over which no blessing is recited; see there. Nevertheless, before giving, one should say 'For the sake of the unification' in the following formulation: 'For the sake of the unification of the Holy One, blessed is He, and His Presence,' etc. 'Behold, I come to fulfill the mitzvah of gifts to the poor, which Mordechai and Esther instituted, to give on this day two gifts to two poor people, to rectify the root of this mitzvah in the supernal realm. May the pleasantness,' etc. He should likewise say this before mishloach manos and before the daytime meal.
