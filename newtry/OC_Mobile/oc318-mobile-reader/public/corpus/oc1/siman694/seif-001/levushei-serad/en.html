Magen Avraham s.k. 5 from Rama's language. It is difficult — on the contrary it seems from Rama specifically the collectors, and from the Mechaber he should have proved; but the poor etc. he omitted city dwellers — it appears since he omitted there is not so much proof; but Rama who emended the beginning of Mordechai's words and did not emend also their end — he should have emended "but the poor and city dwellers" etc. — we learn that in this he does not hold like Mordechai, and it requires study.
