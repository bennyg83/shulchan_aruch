(ב) "And there is none" — meaning there is no obligation but there is a custom; Levush wrote that the custom is to give Purim money for each and every member of the household, even for children, and pregnant women also give for the unborn child per the Gemara; and it is stated in a mishnah: every minor whose father began to weigh coins on his behalf does not cease. In a place where the custom is to give the money as half-shekel to the chazan there is no prohibition in the matter, for whoever gives gives with intent for the custom — Mishna Berurah siman 694. But they do not fulfill matanot laevyonim through this unless they divide some of it for the poor — Magen Avraham. Shelah wrote that half-shekel and certainly matanot laevyonim and mishloach manot do not come from maaser money, and if one wishes to add he adds from non-maaser — Maharil in responsum siman 56; see Kanfei Nesharim and Yad Aharon. Money one planned in his heart to distribute on Purim day he may not change — Beit Yosef in name of Hagahot Asheiri; see Beit Yosef siman 258.
