A city dweller who sent portions to a country dweller and they reached him on the 14th — he fulfilled if he knew he would not eat them except on the 15th, for we follow the joy of the recipient — likewise in Yad Aharon siman 694 who wrote that one who sent portions abroad and they arrived there on Purim he fulfilled even at the time he sent from his house the time had not yet arrived — the same law here; see Darkei Moshe what he wrote in name of Mahari Weil and Maharil and Shaarei Chaim siman 693. The same if he sent portions and the sender was prevented — he did not fulfill, for everything depends on the joy of the recipient — so concluded in responsum Masat Moshe, unlike what Bnei Chayei wrote — see there.
