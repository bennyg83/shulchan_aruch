(1) [Seif 1] Every person is obligated to give etc., even a poor person supported from charity, as with four cups of Pesach — unlike other charity where he is obligated only once a year to fulfill the mitzvah of charity as written in Yoreh Deah siman 248 and 249. Bach. Avodah Zarah s.k. 1. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Turei Zahav letter 1 — see there letter 1 letter 1 and Peri Chadash wrote that a poor person supported from charity is exempt from matanot laevyonim, but Mateh Yehudah wrote the main law is as Bach and his colleagues and similar to mishloach manot as Peri Chadash himself wrote below siman 695 that a poor person supported from charity is obligated — see there. So agreed Birkei Yosef letter 2 and in his book Machber letter 1 Shulchan Gavoah letter 2 Nehar Shalom letter 1 Chayei Adam letter 2 Bigdei Yesha. Chayei Adam general rule 165 letter 28.
