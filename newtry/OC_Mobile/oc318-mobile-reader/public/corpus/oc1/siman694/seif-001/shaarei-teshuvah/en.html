(א) Two gifts. Avodat HaTumah; see Peri Chadash who holds a poor person is exempt from matanot laevyonim and obligated in mishloach manot, and Birkei Yosef wrote as Bach and Taz as ruled in Yoreh Deah siman 248 that even a poor person supported from charity must give from what was given him; in Machatzit HaShekel he wrote so in Mateh Yehudah; see there in name of Zera Yaakov that if he gives money he must give measure that acquires three eggs' worth of food, and if he gives foods he must give measure of three eggs of bread — so appears per straightforward reading; per this if he distributes to poor people a portion to each of a perutah he does not fulfill, and still needs to be settled — till here his language, and I do not have the book itself to know his reason; in every place of giving a perutah see Pesachim daf 32 and Gittin daf 47 in Tosafos there, and perhaps he learned from what is written in Chullin perek Reishit HaGez that he give him weight of five selaim to make from it a small garment as it says "you shall give him" that there be enough for a gift, and so Rambam wrote in chapter 10 Laws of Bikkurim and Shulchan Aruch Yoreh Deah siman 353 "give him that there be enough for a useful gift"; per this one could say the same here it is not called a gift unless there is enough for a useful gift, namely enough for meal measure; indeed there daf 138 it is stated that what we derive enough to make a small garment from scripture "to stand and serve" is derived, and see responsum Noda B'Yehuda mahadura tinyana Yoreh Deah siman 201 who wrote regarding challah separation there are two mitzvot: one to remove tevel prohibition which has no Torah measure, and second to fulfill giving mitzvah to kohen which has Torah measure as written "you shall give" enough for giving etc., and there he debated Tosafos in Gittin daf 47 and wondered on them — see there at length (see Ramban in Gittin there who wrote for a monetary matter a perutah suffices and get is different — see there); and I wondered he did not mention mishnah perek Reishit HaGez and from Mishneh LaMelekh one could learn matanot laevyonim is the same, and the examiner will see the matters are close — see there; and what is written in name of Leket Yosher one who gives to one measure of two gifts etc. — it appears from gemara language and poskim certainly he does not fulfill with less than two poor people; see Peri Chadash who challenged saying two gifts to each poor person as challenged in Yoma regarding "and he shall place on the two goats" etc. — see there; in Birkei Yosef he extended on this — see there in Eliyah Rabbah in name of Rambam: better to increase matanot laevyonim than to increase his meal and mishloach manot to friends, for there is no great and splendid joy except to rejoice the heart of poor, orphans, widows and converts, similar to the Divine Presence of whom it is said "to revive the lowly spirit and restore the broken heart" — till here his words.

(ב) And there is no Avodat HaTumah. What is written in a place where they practice giving the money as half-shekel to the chazan etc. — in Machatzit HaShekel he erred through mistake that fell in Magen Avraham and the reference to seif 2 "one may not change Purim money" etc. is missing, and on this Magen Avraham relies as explained in Mishna Berurah and as written in Yad Efrayim; it never entered the mind there would be a custom to give half-shekel to the chazan, and Avodat HaTumah who added on his own the words half-shekel did not aim well and it is a stumbling block causing charity collectors who are the hand of the poor to diminish the power of the poor from this charity given for half-shekel to give to the chazan — thus Mishna Berurah: what is practiced in several communities that they give all Purim money to the shaliach tzibbur who reads the Megillah alone and do not distribute Purim money to the poor at all — is it permitted to do so or must the custom be abolished because we learn collection Purim to Purim? Answer: certainly it is not proper to do so, and the kosher custom is to distribute to the poor; however this is specifically how they collected explicitly for the poor etc. or the city's custom etc.; but in those places that already practiced giving all Purim money to the chazan who reads the Megillah I do not see prohibition, for everyone who gives etc. has intent for the chazan, for there is no law of Purim money here, and though it rolls on people's tongues to call them Purim money, the matter does not depend on the name but on the collection — the matter depends on collection, and so Rashi explained collection Purim: money the collectors gathered from city members to distribute to poor for Purim meal etc. — not so in our case they never collected with intent for the poor etc. — end of his words; it is clear their way in those places was to collect from beit kenesset to give to the chazan who reads Megillah and it was called Purim money, and Mishna Berurah ruled thus; but one must not be lenient regarding half-shekel, and one who says custom per Avodat HaTumah — we do not listen, for it is mistaken custom and if they practiced they must abolish it; to me it appears God-fearing shlichei tzibbur who wished to merit half-shekel because of these words of Avodat HaTumah — I showed them it is error and they retracted and withdrew their hand from it henceforth; and what Magen Avraham brought Avodat HaTumah "but they do not fulfill through this" etc. — this too is clear it refers to that money given to collectors called Purim money.
