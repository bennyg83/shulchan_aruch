Every person is obligated to give, etc. Regarding why no blessing is recited over mishloach manos and gifts to the poor, as is recited over reading the Megillah, the Rashba wrote in Responsa, siman 18, that this is because the recipients may refuse to accept them. See further there for the reason regarding all mitzvos over which no blessing is recited; see there. Nevertheless, before giving, one should say 'For the sake of the unification' in the following formulation: 'For the sake of the unification of the Holy One, blessed is He, and His Presence,' etc. 'Behold, I come to fulfill the mitzvah of gifts to the poor, which Mordechai and Esther instituted, to give on this day two gifts to two poor people, to rectify the root of this mitzvah in the supernal realm. May the pleasantness,' etc. He should likewise say this before mishloach manos and before the daytime meal.
