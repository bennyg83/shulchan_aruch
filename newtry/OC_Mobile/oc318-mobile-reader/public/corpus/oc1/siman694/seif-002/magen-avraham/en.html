(א) One may not change — Beit Yosef wrote in name of Hagahot Asheiri: money one planned in his heart to distribute on Purim day he may not change it — end of his words; see Beit Yosef siman 258 and Choshen Mishpat siman 212 Shaarei Chaim.

(ב) And specifically the collectors, but etc. In Tur it implies even city dwellers may not change, and so from Rama's words here; but Mordechai wrote explicitly city dwellers may change, and if so their law is like other charity as written in Yoreh Deah siman 256 s.k. 4.
