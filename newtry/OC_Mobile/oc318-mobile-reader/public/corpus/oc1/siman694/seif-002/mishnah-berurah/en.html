(א) (6) One may not change Purim money — meaning money the collectors gathered to distribute to the poor for the Purim meal; he cannot change them to say enough with less and the remainder falls to the charity fund, but they buy calves in quantity with all the money and what remains that they will not eat on Purim they sell and it falls to the charity fund [gemara]. Beit Yosef wrote in name of Hagahot Asheiri: money one planned in his heart to distribute to the poor on Purim day he may not change it, and the reason is he holds charity has sanctity law and he must fulfill his thought even though he did not express it with his lips.
