Ochimin 17 Shabbos 17 A for known things, O Ithamai these things and go even to RAR in them, and also a whisper, even though it is doubtful whether it heals, and they do not go out on Shabbos in a permitted sand or on Shabbos in a house, it is permitted, and there is no putting on it in the way of Amorites, but if they check and there is no cure in it because of the ways of Amorites, even in a whisper.
