Into which the foot enters. Although the Gemara implies that wooden footwear is prohibited lest it slip off because it is not tight, that concerned footwear in their time, whose principal attachment was tied around the lower leg and into which the foot did not enter, unlike ours. Regarding a cork sandal, according to Rabbeinu Yerucham it may certainly be worn outside according to all opinions, even those who prohibit wood. Beis Yosef cites this at the end of the siman.
