Pantanines. Since they are covered with leather, they fit tightly and do not slip off. Rashba.
Some are stringent. Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 19, likewise indicates that they were customarily prohibited in the Roman realm. See Pesachim 51. Shibolei HaLeket writes likewise. Nevertheless, if one forgot and went out with them into the public domain, he need not remove them. See what I wrote in siman 308:15.
In a place where people do not ordinarily, etc. Even if they ordinarily walk barefoot during the week, one should wear shoes on Shabbos to remember that it is Shabbos. Darkei Moshe and Kol Bo imply this. See the end of siman 2 regarding the standards of modesty, that one should never walk barefoot.
