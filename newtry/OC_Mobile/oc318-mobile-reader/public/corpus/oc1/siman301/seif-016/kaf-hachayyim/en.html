16) There: It is forbidden to cross it, etc. If he cannot go around and cannot leap over it, but can only cross through the water, it is forbidden, since he is not going for a mitzvah purpose. Bach; Eliyah Rabbah 8. Magen Avraham 6, however, implies that if he can neither leap nor go around, he may cross through the water. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 6, writes likewise. The later authorities nevertheless follow Bach. It appears that one may be lenient in pressing circumstances.
