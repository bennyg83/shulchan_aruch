(ה) And likewise pantofle — see Magen Avraham and he also holds like Tosafot there in responsum, therefore forbidden Yom Kippur as Yoma and equal etc.; but siman 554 s.tz. and 314 s.b. explained wooden permitted, only forbidden covered leather, so Rif and Geonim, Rashi view; difficulty on Rashi from Yoma — Ramban: apparently difficult Shabbat Rav Yosef who did not concede etc., Ramban girsa Rava said who etc. why lean on Shmuel's establishment rather Rava follows his view Yoma all hold shoe; therefore Rabbanan of testimonies Ribbi Nachman; Rava Yoma all shoe only R' Meir and R' Yehudah on whom said equal, Rabbanan dispute, therefore Yevamot stam also Rabbanan; therefore stump on leg is R' Meir, anpilya of cloth we come to Rabbanan, no difficulty R' Yehudah; R' Meir explains because shoe; we hold Rabbanan Yom Kippur and chalitzah; nevertheless here true forbidden because slips as Rava Yoma; Shabbat we hold R' Yosei except leather-covered tight view chalitzah reason not slips but shoe and not shoe; Rashba wrote even though Yevamot as Ramban must say from anpilya of cloth we come to Rabbanan; but surely R' Yosei forbids chalitzah wooden shoe because slips, else why Rava who did not concede Ribbi Nachman to mishnah of testimonies and not difficult on Yevamot s.v. it rather there R' Yosei forbids as explained unlike plaster not slips; see Rashba; must say leather-covered tight as Tosafot else R' Yosei would forbid and not s.v. it. General matter: Yom Kippur we hold Ribbi Nachman as explained not shoe; Shabbat we hold R' Yosei because slips; possible Ribbi Nachman concedes to me; chalitzah Ribbi Nachman and R' Yosei equal invalid unless leather-covered tight; therefore Shabbat we hold Tosafot all slips forbidden all tight permitted; Rashba responsum siman 327 pantofle because tight; thus all Shulchan Aruch here and Yom Kippur proper; for halacha appears main Ramban, need not leather-covered tight view, unlike Rashba.
