18) [Se'if 4] Such as to greet his teacher. One is obligated to greet him on a festival. On Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh there is no obligation, but there is a mitzvah. A woman is equivalent to a man in this respect, as learned from the Shunammite woman. Magen Avraham 7. See siman 554:12, where Magen Avraham writes that there is a mitzvah even on a weekday; see there. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, second edition, siman 94, however, which writes that nowadays, if one does not go to study from his teacher during the festival but only to greet him, there is no obligation, although it is proper to do so; see there.
