39) Unless, etc. Unless the ring can be used to seal documents.
40) Engraved. The same applies when the letters on the ring are raised, so that their impressions in wax are recessed; this too is a ring with a seal. Engraving was mentioned because of pictorial forms: a raised complete human figure may be forbidden even to keep in one's home on a weekday, as stated in Yoreh De'ah 141:5. Some explain that the forms here are not human figures, or that only a face is depicted from one side; all agree that such a form may be made either raised or recessed. Accordingly, 'engraved' merely describes the usual case.
