173) A person may dry himself, etc. Magen Avraham implies that nowadays it is preferable to use a material whose wetness does not concern him. Gra implies that an ordinary towel is such a material.
174) With a towel. He may dry even his entire body after bathing in cold water, as below in siman 326.
175) And bring it in his hand. He may bring it home where there is an eruv. Magen Avraham explains that since drying was permitted as unavoidable, carrying the wet towel home was also permitted; after he sets it down, it may no longer be moved. Eliyah Rabbah cites Sefer HaTerumah that a towel absorbs only a small amount of water, so there was never a squeezing-based prohibition on moving it. Gra likewise implies that it may be moved.
