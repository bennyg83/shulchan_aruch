Rama's ruling concerning bells. According to Rabbeinu Tam and Rambam, an ornament made only for men is not subject to the concern that its wearer may remove it to show it. Bells are used only as male ornaments, so why are they forbidden? The answer is that they are not truly ornaments; rather, they make the wearer appear arrogant.
They appear arrogant because bells are suitable for princes, not ordinary people. Although the present case concerns a minor, the word 'sons' itself indicates minors. Shabbos 66 says, 'Sons may go out with knots.' The Gemara asks why the law is limited to sons if the knots are medicinal; this proves that 'sons' means children. Magen Avraham's words in se'if katan 35 likewise require the case to concern a minor.
Even if the child comes to carry the bells, this is like a minor eating forbidden food: The court is not commanded to stop him.
Shabbos 67 states that princes' sons may go out with bells, and that everyone is permitted to do so. Rabbi Oshaya says this follows Rabbi Shimon, who holds that all Israel are princes. Rashi explains that bells are suitable even for the poor, so people will not ridicule them and cause them to remove and carry the bells. Rava says the permission applies only when the bells are sewn into the garment, and all agree with this. Tosafos asks on Rabbi Oshaya that there should still be concern that a bell may break, as there is regarding a servant's seal on page 58. Riva answers that the Yerushalmi establishes the case as one of minors, and if the bell breaks and the child carries it, there is no prohibition requiring intervention. Tosafos then asks why Rabbi Oshaya needs the principle that all Israel are princes: Even if the child is ridiculed and carries the bell, the court need not stop him. Tosafos answers that the father may hear the ridicule and remove the bells himself. Later authorities ask why Magen Avraham cites Tosafos, since we rule like Rava that the bells must be sewn in, eliminating concern that they will break. Magen Avraham cites it to support his explanation that sewing is required because ridicule may cause removal. Breakage alone is not a concern with minors; mockery is.
