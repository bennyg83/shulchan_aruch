One who goes out with money ; Maharil permitted carrying pierced coins with him on Shabbos in time of danger, but not taking them beyond the eruv; and some say he permitted thus even when not pierced; and if he fears robbery, such as when the governor's agents search, permitted to take them beyond the eruv, as siman 334 ; end quote; if so, what he wrote initially not beyond eruv deals when no person comes to rob, and one cannot say he is agitated only in time of danger ; if Gentiles notice his money they will rob or he will be endangered, as Or Zarua ; see there; nevertheless from his words it implies carrying in hand forbidden, only carrying with him, which is tiltul kele'acher yad; so Beit Yosef in Agur and Maharil; and what is written siman 303 note 2, permitted to go out ; is because they designated for use and are batel to the garment; but mere piercing does not help unless pierced to hang on daughter's neck if not time of danger.
