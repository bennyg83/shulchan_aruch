119) He is liable. This is the ordinary manner of carrying. The same applies when he goes out with coins tied in a pouch inserted into his garment. At home or within an eruv, where carrying is not prohibited, it is permitted as explained below.
120) It is permitted. He may wear the sheet if he needs it. Some explain that this means he fears leaving it lest it be stolen; merely needing the sheet for use does not permit moving it.
121) Provided they are perforated. Perforated gold or silver coins are not muktzeh because they can be hung around his daughter's neck as an ornament. Gra applies this also to coins tied in a sheet: they too must be perforated, for otherwise the sheet would be a base for muktzeh coins placed in it before Shabbos.
