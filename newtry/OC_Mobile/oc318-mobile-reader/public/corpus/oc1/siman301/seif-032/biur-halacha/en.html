If he needs it. See Mishnah Berurah: Some say this means he fears it will be stolen. At first glance, since we conclude below that in a case of loss one may carry money in an unusual manner, why does Rama require the coins here to be pierced even to carry them in his hand? Is the muktzeh prohibition more severe than carrying with a deviation? Perhaps Rama here intends to be lenient according to all opinions, whereas the ruling there follows only the permissive authorities.
