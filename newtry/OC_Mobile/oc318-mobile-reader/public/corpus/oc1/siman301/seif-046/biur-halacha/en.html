Garments soaked, etc. The unqualified word 'garments' includes even wool garments. Pri Megadim. That is, one should not say that this bleaching applies specifically to linen, which is ordinarily placed in an oven to be whitened, as stated in the Mishnah there. The prohibition of cooking certainly applies to all garments. See Biur Halachah to siman 302:9, s.v. mutar.
