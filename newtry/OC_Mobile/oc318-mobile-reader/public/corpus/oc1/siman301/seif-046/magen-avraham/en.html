(א) Near fire — because of bleaching or cooking like flax pods [there]; if so, even slight roasting forbidden; but where there is no pot, no cooking concern, as siman 318 seif 14; and where no cooking, no bleaching — else sun also forbidden for bleaching; rather as written.

(ב) And forbidden to move them — Mordechai wrote: one dries with a tithe alontit and does not bring in hand — from this Mishnah we learn everything soaked in water forbidden to move, etc. — requires study, for we hold halacha is not like that Mishnah, as Semag; it should be as Ran: bathing and drying different, impossible without bathing and drying, as chapter Kirah — end quote; implies nevertheless ten people may move, reminding each other, this Mishnah not rejected; and apparently nowadays when possible without bathing, forbidden to dry; nevertheless one may say since Sages did not decree, permitted, as notes siman 13 in Beit Yosef; nevertheless good to dry with something one is not particular about its wetness.
