Se'if 46: Garments, etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Shabbos, halachah 2: Rabbi Mana was lenient for women who spread their garments in the air of an oven.
It is forbidden to dry them, etc. Folio 18b; see Tosafos, s.v. 'bundles of flax,' and folio 74b. This is what it says there on folio 146b, that one may spread them in the sun. See the Mordechai, chapter 22 of Shabbos, who wrote that it is forbidden because of whitening and because of cooking. These are the two explanations of the bundles of flax in Hagahos Mordechai, chapter 1 of Shabbos, where Riva wished to explain that it is because of cooking, as stated in the chapter Klal Gadol, 'one who throws a peg into an oven,' etc. There he wrote: but in the chapter Bameh Madlikin we say that bundles of flax become liable when they whiten, which indicates whitening. These are the words of Tosafos cited above, s.v. 'bundles,' etc. It is possible that both are true, except that Riva initially wished to explain it specifically regarding moist flax from the soaking vat, and he retracted, for even if dry one is liable, as above.
