[Levush] Or for that one. This seems to me to be an outright error, Malbushei Yom Tov. The explanation is that with one person, establishment of the man does not apply, but only with three people, as below. According to this, it is difficult regarding what the Levush wrote before this, but one amulet healed one person three times. One can say that this is what he meant: one amulet healed one person three times, and a second amulet healed a second person three times. When he wrote earlier, they healed three times for two people, he meant that each amulet healed two people three times. If so, each amulet healed six times. In this way one must explain what he wrote afterward, or for one person, etc., but this is forced. Therefore it seems to me that he was saying, if you wish to say: even if you say that the man is established through one person as well, we have still not found establishment of the man and establishment of the amulet with one person in this manner. Another possibility is that he holds there is a distinction: specifically when three written amulets healed one person, each one time, he is not established. But when each one heals three times, the man is established and the amulets are established, as when he wrote one incantation three times for one person and for three people, and they were healed. Up to here is his language. This is a proof to my words; examine it carefully.
