(א) Soaked in water — even slight soaking.

(ב) Forbidden to dry them, etc. — even in sun forbidden there because of maris ayin alone; here teaches opposite fire is d'oraisa prohibition of cooking and bleaching [flax pods bleach by oven as Shabbat 27a]; also forbidden to dry even while wearing if standing against heat where yad soledes bo.

(ג) Near fire — likewise forbidden to place on stove where yad soledes bo; people err in this unwittingly in winter placing wet clothes on hot stove to dry; where yad soledes bo not applicable, permitted to dry, provided not spread in usual manner, for if spread as usual forbidden in all cases, as previous seif.

(ד) And forbidden to move them — specifically when soaked much in water; when little water came on them no squeezing concern, as below siman 302 seif 9 in gloss.

(ה) Who is particular — meaning he does not want water in them; but cloths always soaked in water, no concern, moving permitted.
