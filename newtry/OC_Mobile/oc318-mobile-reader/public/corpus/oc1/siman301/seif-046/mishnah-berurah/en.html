168) Soaked in water. Even only slightly soaked.
() Forbidden to dry them, etc. ; even in sun forbidden there because of maris ayin alone; here teaches opposite fire is d'oraisa prohibition of cooking and bleaching [flax pods bleach by oven as Shabbos 27a]; also forbidden to dry even while wearing if standing against heat where yad soledes bo.
() Near fire ; likewise forbidden to place on stove where yad soledes bo; people err in this unwittingly in winter placing wet clothes on hot stove to dry; where yad soledes bo not applicable, permitted to dry, provided not spread in usual manner, for if spread as usual forbidden in all cases, as previous seif.
171) It is forbidden to move them. This applies when soaked with much water. If only a little water fell on them, we do not fear that he will wring them out, as below in siman 302:9 in the gloss.
172) About whose wetness he is particular. He does not want water in them. Cloths that are always kept soaking in water present no concern and may be moved.
