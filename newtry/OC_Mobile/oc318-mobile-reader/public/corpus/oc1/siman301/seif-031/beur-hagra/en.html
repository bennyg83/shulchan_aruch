Se'if 31: One who goes out. So Rashi also explained, s.v. 'gutter.' However, Rashi explained that it is done by means of strings according to his own view, for he explained the issue as repair of a utensil. But according to the Rambam's explanation that the concern is carrying, it applies even if he holds it in his hand.
This applies only to cloaks. So it is proven in Yoma 77b according to the explanation of the Rosh and the Tur above, se'if 4, that the reason is the unusual manner; otherwise it is permitted even though it is folded over his shoulders, as Rashi writes there and below in siman 613, se'if 5. So too it appears in the Gemara there, which mentioned it in connection with crossing through water; otherwise it would be forbidden in any case.
