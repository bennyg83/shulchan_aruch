(א) And folds from here and there — Rambam wrote until "and this is in tallitot," etc.; he explains R' Yochanan's folded tallit not as Rashi raising garment hem, but wrapping then gathering corners in hand or on shoulder — for prohibition law they are equal.

(ב) And this is in tallitot, etc. — requires study: good, from where did our Rabbi Ba'al Shulchan Aruch this distinction without foundation? On the contrary illogical to distinguish: why does taking hands out help when not worn as clothing, but raising and folding garment not in its way forbidden? And all poskim wrote this law — were there no garments like ours with sleeves? Beit Yosef wrote: any holding part in hand raising lest soil — even their tallitot permitted; forbidden only placing on shoulder as burden — end quote. Astonishing: Rambam at start of this seif regarding folding in hand or shoulder — prohibition applies in hand like on shoulder, gathering causes prohibition like gutter in Gemara; Beit Yosef ended: simple matter, wrote only because I saw doubters, explained above they have nothing to rely on — end quote. I did not know our Rabbi's basis to be lenient with our garments gathering in hand lest soil or on shoulder — requires study; see end seif 34.
