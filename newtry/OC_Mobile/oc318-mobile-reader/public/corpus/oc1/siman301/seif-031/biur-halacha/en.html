He folded it on both sides in his hand or upon his shoulder. See Mishnah Berurah. Gra appears to explain Shulchan Aruch's words, which copy Rambam, differently: He doubled the hems, raised the garment, and held it that way in his hand or upon his shoulder, creating a gutter shape inward. The Sages prohibited carrying it this way when he does not intend adornment, because it is not sufficiently a manner of wearing.
