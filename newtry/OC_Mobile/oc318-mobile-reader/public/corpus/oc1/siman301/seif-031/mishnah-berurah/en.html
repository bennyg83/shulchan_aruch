(16) We will prepare to gather his wings, etc. - that is, he will take the two edges in front of him and behind him on the right side as well as on the left side and fold them up and put them on his hand or on his shoulder and there is no blood for the skat, all the edges of the tallit after him are folded and placed on his shoulder.

(Kiz) But from a dress, the meaning is that the garment itself remains on it through a dress, and it means in the latter cases that it should not be made lighter except by raising it a little, but not raising it much, and putting it under the arm is done as a gutter:

(Kih) and takes his hands out of it - meaning that he does not take his hands out of it, such as from a women's antalik or an Ashkenazim's shul from an antil, it is forbidden to raise them even through clothing.
