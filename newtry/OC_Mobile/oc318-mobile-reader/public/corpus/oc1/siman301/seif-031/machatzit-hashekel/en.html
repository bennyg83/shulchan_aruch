() (s.k. 31) Forbidden ; Rambam: do not explain Shulchan Aruch literally forbidden only tie thread or salve on wound; on pad permitted like binding ibid.s. Bach truly. Magen Avraham brought Rambam proving forbidden even on pad.
The plural expression 'them' proves that the prohibition applies even when the coin is placed over lint or a sponge used as a dressing, and not only when it lies directly on the wound. If the law concerned only direct contact with the wound, the singular 'it' would have been appropriate.
