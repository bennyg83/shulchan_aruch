And extends his arms from within it. This implies that if he does not extend his arms from within, as women or Ashkenazim wrap themselves, he may not lift it. Maharil writes: 'Women who pull up and fold their garments under their arms so as to lift them and prevent their hems from becoming soiled may not do so, because the garments form a gutter shape in the middle. They may, however, lift them in their hands without folding them.' This implies that Maharil does not distinguish based on whether it remains the manner of wearing. Rather, lifting it slightly remains a manner of wearing, but lifting it completely is forbidden. Beis Yosef also implies this.
