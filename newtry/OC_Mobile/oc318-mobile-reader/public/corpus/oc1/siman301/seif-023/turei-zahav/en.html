(א) In something whose custom is to be attached — this explanation is per what Beit Yosef wrote in this siman in the Rashba's name that I brought in seif 11 regarding making a key at belt end — they were accustomed to permit batel to the belt and I forbade them: anything not needed for the vessel and not use of the vessel is not batel to the vessel — end quote; and although above Rama wrote some permit, that view holds this is proper custom there since made like a pendant; here he deals with what is not like a pendant — all agree not batel to the belt.

(ב) Handkerchief if attached to garment — Shach in Semag's name: anything attached to garment is like garment; no concern lest it fall and he carry, for those stitches count as woven; and although handkerchief is not clothing nor ornament, since not important it is batel to garment — end quote. Mateh Moshe wrote: specifically sewn to garment, then batel; but those who attach handkerchief to belt with knot forbidden on Shabbat — full prohibition, we say the belt is batel to the handkerchief — end quote; therefore one who wants the handkerchief with him has no permission to make it like a belt, since he has a belt anyway and belt over belt is forbidden, as this siman; rather tie head of handkerchief to head of belt with non-permanent knot like long belt and gird while walking in street, as above.
