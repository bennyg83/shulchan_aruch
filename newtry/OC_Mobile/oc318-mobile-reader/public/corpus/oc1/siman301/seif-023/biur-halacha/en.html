Specifically when they have no clapper, etc. The same applies if the bell is plugged. Gra appears to imply this. Magen Avraham concludes that the restriction applies specifically when the sound is desired, such as for children. Adults may go out even when the bell has a clapper and makes sound, since they do not need the sound. See siman 305:5, where Magen Avraham is uncertain because perhaps a bell's sound is considered that of a musical instrument. In siman 338 he concludes that an instrument specifically made for music is forbidden. Eliyah Rabbah also questions Magen Avraham's words; see there. Gra likewise appears to agree with Rama; see there.
