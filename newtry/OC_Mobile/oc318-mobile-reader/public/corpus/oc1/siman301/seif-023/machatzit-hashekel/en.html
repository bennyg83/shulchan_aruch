Se'if katan 23: Over her scarf, etc. This implies even pieces of linen. Although the piece is not itself a garment, it is permitted because she wraps herself in it in the manner of clothing. Even linen pieces insignificant to wealthy women are permitted.
But to place, etc. Maharil agrees in this case. Responsa Avodas HaGershuni explains why we do not fear that when the rain stops she will remove the covering from her head and body to display her beautiful ornaments and clothing, then carry it four amos in the public domain. Weekday garments are generally not important enough for her to show off; only costly Shabbos garments are. When she wishes to remove the covering to display her clothing, she will necessarily remember that it is Shabbos and refrain from carrying it four amos by hand. He proves this from the first Tosafos in Bameh Ishah. See there.
