Se'if 23: It is effective only. Tosafos on folio 58a, s.v. 'with what are we dealing,' as above in se'if 11.
Those round items, etc. This is like a seal, which they did not forbid on his garment when it is not made of metal; the only concern is that he may be afraid and fold it. Otherwise it is permitted. There, even if it would not be rubbed away, it is as written there, 'What is different about a bell,' etc., and the initial assumption in all of them was that they were not woven. In the Yerushalmi: anything attached to one's garment is like his garment; see there.
And likewise it is permitted. Here, when sewn well, and like a bell there, since its way is to be attached, as above.
