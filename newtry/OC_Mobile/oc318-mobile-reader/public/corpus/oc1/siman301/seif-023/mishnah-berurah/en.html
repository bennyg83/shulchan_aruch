(Pen) The woven ones - therefore they did not feel a dilemma with a stop and I with my hand, and even if they are a zagin of gold that is not made except for the sons of kings, they did not feel a dilemma with me smiling on it and I with my hand in his hand since it is woven.
() They are not woven ; and even if tied to veils, and eestablishedly a pair on his neck, forbidden to go out, lest it tear from the veil or neck and he come to carry; and eestablishedly if gold, lest they mock him to hold him like a prince made only for the wealthy and he come to take and carry in hand; Tosafotafot in the ibid. of Riv'a: even small children who are not wealthy forbidden to go out with unwoven gold pair on clothing or hanging on neck, lest they mock them and impossible their father not hear and take them, and meanwhile come to move them d'oraisa in reshut ha-rabbim; Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah brought this.
(Fa) Only in the matter, etc. - i.e. such as a couple whose way in their time was to be oribid.ntal in all their clothes, therefore the ministers of a fabric did not rule that it is not a fabric because it is an oribid.nt to the garment, so the Sages did not bother to lose it and defend themselves.
(Fab) that there is no way in this - we would say that it is connected with it, it is not connected and refer to Mordechai, where he concluded that it is possible, that it is permissible, and the obligation of the sin of Nami is null and void with regard to the garment, since there is no way of doing so, and it also means in the answer of the Rashba that was brought in the Bible that anything that does not need to be clothed and there is no use for the garment is not void with regard to the garment, since it is as if it is taken out alone without the garment and must:
() Permitted to go out in them ; for considered clothing since customary to go out in them all weekdays; and we do not worry lest he be ashamed and take it and come to carry from fear of the kingdom; also we do not worry lest it tear and he come to carry, unlike unwoven buttons which are not important.
() If attached, etc. ; here requires sewn well to the veil, or at least sewn with two stitches, then like woven together, as at the start of the seif [otherwise we worry lest it tear and he come to carry like buttons; and though not clothing nor oribid.nt, nevertheless not considered burden since in their time customary to sew thus to veils, therefore batel to garment; thus in our time where no custom to sew patshil in garment, not batel to garment and forbidden, as in Sefer Zichru Torat Moshe and Sefer Mateh Eser; some wrote since patshil is unimportant thing, batel to veil when sewn]. Tying alone to veil or belt, even tied from before Shabbos with permanent knot, also forbidden to go out, since he does not gird himself with the patshil; one who wants the patshil with him and did not sew to veil from before Shabbos has no permission to make it now like a belt to gird, since he has a belt anyway, and belt on belt forbidden as below seif 36; rather attach head of patshil to head of belt with a knot and it becomes like a long belt and he girds himself while walking in the street [Taz and Shulchan Aruch]. Some Acharonim doubt this and hold a non-permanent knot does not help; therefore initially good to do this advice itself from erev Shabbos and tie with permanent knot to belt at the head, considered a long belt to gird on Shabbos.
() To cover ; specifically to cover; but if sewn to belt it does not help, not batel to belt, unless sewn at head of belt and he girds himself with it, as above; see below seif 36–37 what we wrote in Mishnah Berurah on this matter.
