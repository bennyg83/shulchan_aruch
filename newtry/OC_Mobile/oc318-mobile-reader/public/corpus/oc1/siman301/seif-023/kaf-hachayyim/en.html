23) There. See below, siman 613:5 and the gloss to se'if 8. Rama writes there that wherever crossing water is permitted, one may cross even if a route around it on dry land is available, because minimizing the distance walked is preferable. Taz and Magen Avraham there, however, write that one should be stringent and not cross through water if a dry route is possible, even though it lengthens the walk; see there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav 13 rules likewise.
