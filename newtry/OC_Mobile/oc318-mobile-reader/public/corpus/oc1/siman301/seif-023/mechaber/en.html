Boys may go out with bells {i.e., like small chimes} woven into their garments; but if they are not woven [in], no. {Rama: And this — that it is attached to the garment — helps only with something that is customarily attached there; but if he attached there something not customarily [attached] thus, it is forbidden (Hagahos Maimoni; Beis Yosef in the name of Responsa of the Rashba; Mordechai). And those green circles that the [ruling] kingdom decreed that every Jew must wear on his garment — it is permitted to go out with them, even if it is not sewn onto his garment but only attached there somewhat (Or Zarua). And likewise it is permitted to go out with a cloth used to wipe the nose, called a fatzolet, if it is attached to his garment. And this — that it is permitted to go out with woven-in bells — is specifically where they have no clapper and do not produce sound (Hagahos Alfasi, end of chapter "Bameh Ishah").}
