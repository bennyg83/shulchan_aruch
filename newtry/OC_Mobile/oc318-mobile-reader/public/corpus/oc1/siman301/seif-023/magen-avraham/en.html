(א) If they are not woven, no — and according to what is written in seif 49, we do not decree on a man lest he show off — it should be that here the reason is lest they mock him, for he appears like a prince and may come to carry; and even though here it deals with a minor where even bringing in hands has no prohibition, one may say they decreed lest princes' sons mock him constantly and his father cannot avoid hearing and taking them and father comes to carry; but if princes' sons do not mock, even if he stops, no concern lest father bring — not common that father be beside him when he stops — end quote, Tosafos.

(ב) Whose custom is to be attached there — on all garments for adornment, the Sages did not trouble to decree and adorn themselves.

(ג) Attached to garment — specifically sewn with two stitches; tying does not help [Beit Shmuel]; requires study what is written regarding green circles permitted when somewhat attached — it should be those are more batel to the garment; Bach wrote: specifically when attached to garment, then batel to garment; but on belt it does not help, not batel to belt — so Shach; and likewise permitted to go out with cap sewn behind; but forbidden to go out with cloth tied to hat wick to wipe eyes [Shach chapter 6, note 25].

(ד) And they do not make sound — requires study, for they ruled in siman 338 only song sound is forbidden; and Tur wrote there all permit placing hollow ring with stone that makes sound since he does not need that sound — Hagahot Maimoni chapter 22; and proof from Gemara daf 28: slave goes out with bell even with clapper since he does not need the sound — end quote; it should deal specifically with children — Shach: if made to make sound, forbidden; so Tosafos one does not rattle bell for child — end quote; implies specifically child who needs song sound, as siman 339 seif 3; and even though child does this, nevertheless it should be as feeding prohibition to his hands — see above; and see siman 339 in Hagahot.
