(א) And also Taz wrote not like Bach who permits running for later pleasure — Magen Avraham note 4. Tosefta "to exercise for pleasure not healing" as Magen Avraham note 5: Zal holds not dealing with healing; if so why Beit Yosef brought Tosefta here, not in siman 328 implying for pleasure, nevertheless running forbidden because pleasure comes afterward; but stroll permitted, pleasure applies here; youths do not exercise to sweat; here deals with hunger to eat — Taz there chapter 28.

(ב) Tur wrote he did not elaborate measures of each melacha, for no practical difference, prohibition exists in any amount; Levush copied; implies half measure forbidden d'oraisa, else practical difference for sick without danger d'rabbanan permitted — should have explained measures, implies even here half measure forbidden; Eshel Avraham part 86 sides whether carrying less than measure still d'oraisa prohibition.
