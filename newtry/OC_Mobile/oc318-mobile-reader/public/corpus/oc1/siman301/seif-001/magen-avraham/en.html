One may not run. Even during the week, a large stride is forbidden, for it takes away one five-hundredth of one's eyesight.
More than an amah. This means that there should be one-half amah between one foot and the other, while the foot itself is also one-half amah, as stated in siman 397:2 in Mahari Weil's name. Agudah writes in Tosafos' name, first chapter of Taanis, that one should not lift his second foot before placing down the first.
