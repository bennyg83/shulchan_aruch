(א) One must not run — and even on a weekday coarse stepping is forbidden, for it removes one three-hundredth from the light of one's eyes.

(ב) More than an amah — meaning there should be half an amah between foot and foot, and the foot itself is also half an amah, as written in siman 397 seif 2 in the name of Mahari Veil, and in Agudah he wrote in the name of Tosafos in the first chapter of Taanit that he should not uproot his second foot before placing the first.
