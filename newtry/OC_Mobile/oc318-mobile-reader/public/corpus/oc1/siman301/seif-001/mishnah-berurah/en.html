1) One may not run on Shabbos. As it is stated, 'And you shall honor it by refraining from pursuing your affairs.' The Sages expounded that your manner of walking on Shabbos should not be like your manner of walking on a weekday, when a person normally hurries and runs after his business. Even on a weekday one should not take a broad stride, for it diminishes one five-hundredth of his eyesight; on Shabbos there is the additional prohibition derived from 'pursuing your affairs.'
2) And it is forbidden, etc. The same applies to jumping.
3) More than a cubit. This means that there is half a cubit between one foot and the other, while the length of one foot is also approximately half a cubit. This applies to an average person, whose ordinary stride measures a cubit; for an exceptionally tall person, the measure follows the length of his own stride.
4) If possible, etc. For when it is impossible, se'if 3 explains that it is permitted.
