Se'if katan 1: One should not run, etc. 'Even on a weekday, etc.' Maharsha in Chiddushei Aggados, page 113, asks: If so, what difference is there between Shabbos and a weekday? He answers that on a weekday there is no prohibition, only harm to the eyes; Shabbos adds an actual prohibition. Another practical difference is a blind person, for whom running is nevertheless forbidden on Shabbos.
