(1) [seif 1] One may not run on Shabbat — as written "if you turn back your foot from Shabbat," Chazal expounded one's Shabbat walking should not be like weekday walking (Tur). Though Gemara derives from "your ways," Tur cited beginning of verse intending end — so Tosafot Shantz letter 1, see there. Even short steps less than an amah, if running, forbidden on Shabbat — Eshel Avraham letter 1.
