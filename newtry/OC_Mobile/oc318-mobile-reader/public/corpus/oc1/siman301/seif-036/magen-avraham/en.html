With two garments. Some women have robes with waistbands sewn into them for fastening, and afterward fasten another belt of silk or fine silver. It is preferable that they not fasten the robe's waistbands. Nevertheless, those waistbands are not considered a burden; since they are attached to the robe, they are subordinate to it. Beis Yosef; Shibolei HaLeket. See se'if katan 23.
