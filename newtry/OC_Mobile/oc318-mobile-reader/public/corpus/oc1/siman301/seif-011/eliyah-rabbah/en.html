[Levush] And he is liable for carrying it out, etc. Know that wherever it says liable, if he acted intentionally he is liable to kareis, and if unwittingly, meaning he forgot that it was Shabbos or knew it was Shabbos but did not know that this labor was forbidden, he is liable to bring a chatas. But if he performed a labor without intent, such as if he forgot an item and went out to the public domain, he is exempt.
