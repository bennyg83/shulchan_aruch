Se'if 11: An item made. This follows the explanation that it is made for both this and that; see the Rosh.
Such as keys. This is from the Yerushalmi there, in the incident with Rabban Gamliel, etc.
And some permit. For he explains it according to the explanation above in se'if 9, that it is for both a man and a woman.
Nevertheless it is forbidden. Like coins wrapped in one's sheet, folio 147a.
And if the key. There, folio 62b, it speaks of a key in her hand, meaning on her finger, as written in the Yerushalmi cited above.
Even attached, etc.; and there are, etc. The reason of those who permit is like a bell, of which they said there that anything woven was not included in the decree. Their words were rejected, for if so even a seal should be permitted. See Tosafos there, s.v. 'With what are we dealing.' And we say on folio 139b that one who goes out with a tallis that is not, etc., is liable, etc.; these are significant and are not nullified. See se'if 38 and se'if 39.
