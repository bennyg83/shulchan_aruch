Magen Avraham, seif katan 56. And perhaps Tosafot hold. In my humble opinion one may say that the sugyos do not disagree. This is according to what Tosafot asked on 55, why the reason is needed that he is going to a fair, and why not prohibit because it is a burden; see there. In truth, one may say that it gave the reason of a fair to prohibit even in an alley with an eruv, as the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 305, seif katan 6. According to this, one may say that it appears he is going to a fair, and the way of those going to a fair is to go with animals to markets and streets, and there would be a prohibition according to the Torah, for when he leads it in the marketplace, which is a public domain, there is a prohibition of resting one’s animal, since the bell is a burden. Therefore we say that according to Rav it is forbidden even in a courtyard, for it is maris ayin regarding a Torah prohibition, which is forbidden even in private rooms. Nevertheless, we rule that in a courtyard it is permitted, because regarding the prohibition of maris ayin we rely on those who hold that nowadays we do not have a public domain. See below, siman 303, seif 18. Thus the maris ayin is only regarding a D’Rabbanan matter, and in that case it is permitted in private rooms. Consider this.
