Se'if katan 21: She ties it, etc.; unlike Olas Shabbos, etc. Shabbos 65 discusses the Mishnah permitting a woman to go out with absorbent material prepared for her menstrual flow; Rashi explains that she presses it in place to absorb the blood. Rami bar Chama thought this was only if tied. Rava said it is permitted even untied, because it is repulsive and she will not carry it. Olas Shabbos therefore objects to Tur and Shulchan Aruch, who say 'if she tied it,' contrary to Rava's conclusion. Magen Avraham answers that Olas Shabbos did not consult siman 303, where Tur and Shulchan Aruch themselves permit the absorbent material even untied. Here, tying is not required by law; it merely describes the usual case. Material pressed tightly in place can remain without tying, whereas an apron wrapped around her body would fall if untied.
