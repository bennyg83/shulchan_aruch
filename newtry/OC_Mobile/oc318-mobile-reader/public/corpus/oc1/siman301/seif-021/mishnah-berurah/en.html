71) In a box, etc. For example, he uses it to protect himself from rain. These are not worn as clothing but are burdens.
72) In sackcloth. Shepherds ordinarily wear it against rain. Since it is considered clothing for them, anyone may go out wearing it, even when not protecting himself from rain.
73) Meaning coarse garments. Rosh so explains in Nedarim, and Rashi on Shabbos 62 similarly explains a garment of sackcloth, implying that an actual sack is not permitted. This requires study nowadays, when shepherds wear actual sacks. Moreover, why would the baraisa mention a box and basket instead of teaching the greater novelty of an actual sack? Shiltei Gibborim cites Rashba as permitting even an actual sack; this requires further study.
