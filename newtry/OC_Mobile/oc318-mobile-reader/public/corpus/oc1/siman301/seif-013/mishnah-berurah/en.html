48) A zav may not go out, etc. Although this is the ordinary weekday manner of carrying for this person, it is not clothing, because it serves only to protect him from soiling. Anything used merely to protect from soiling is a burden unless it is an actual garment, as below in se'if 14.
49) With a pouch. Similarly, it is forbidden to tie a cloth to a hat cord in order to wipe one's eyes with it.
50) A garment. It covers her in front and behind like trousers without bottoms. Since it is worn as a garment, it is permitted in every case, as below in se'if 14. If it is only in front, or only behind and tied by straps in front, it is not considered clothing and is forbidden.
51) So that it will not hurt. That is, so the discharge will not fall on her skin, dry there, and cause pain. Since her intent is to avoid pain, it is considered clothing like other garments made to protect the body, even though it also protects her from soiling.
