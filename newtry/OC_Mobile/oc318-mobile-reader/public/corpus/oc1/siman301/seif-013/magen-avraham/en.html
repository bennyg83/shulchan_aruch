(א) Apron in front and behind her — but if only behind and tied with straps in front, forbidden [Hagahot Maimoni chapter 19].

(ב) She ties it — he used "ties" because without tying it does not hold at all since it hangs in front, unlike the gloss writer who made it a burden for Tur and the rabbi erred and did not look at siman 303 seif 175.

(ג) That it not hurt her — meaning that blood not fall on her flesh and dry on her and cause distress [Tosafos].
