An apron in front and behind her. If it is only behind her and she ties it with straps in front, it is forbidden. Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 19.
She ties it. The ruling specifies tying because without a tie it would not stay in place at all, since it hangs in front of her. This is unlike Olas Shabbos, who describes Tur and Beis Yosef as mistaken and did not examine siman 303:15.
So that it will not hurt. This means so that the discharge will not fall upon her skin, dry there, and cause her pain. Tosafos.
