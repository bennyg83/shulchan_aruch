[Levush] An iron helmet, etc. So explained the Beit Yosef. This requires study, for Rashi explained that it is a leather cap under a metal helmet, and so wrote the Maggid in chapter 19 of Hilchos Shabbos and the Semak. I later saw in the Rambam’s commentary to the Mishnah and in the Bartenura like the explanation of the Beit Yosef. In Sefer Tanya: a copper, iron, or wooden helmet. This requires study.
