A zav (a man who has an abnormal penile discharge that is a source of ritual impurity) should not go out [to the public domain] wearing his sack that is made to protect him from his discharge so that his clothes will not become soiled. Similarly, a menstruating woman who ties a cloth in front of her so that her clothes will not become soiled by the blood of her discharge is forbidden to go out [to the public domain] wearing it, unless it is like a girdle that is fashioned like a garment, covering both her front and back. If, however, it covers only from behind and its front is merely tied with straps, this is not the manner in which clothes are worn and [doing so] is forbidden.
