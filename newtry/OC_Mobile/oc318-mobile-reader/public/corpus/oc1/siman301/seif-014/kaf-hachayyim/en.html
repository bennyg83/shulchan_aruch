14) There, in the gloss: It is likewise permitted to stroll, etc. A responsum at the end of Chazeh HaTenufah states that strolling on Shabbos in an alley with an eruv without a head covering or outer cloak depends upon local custom. If the custom is not to stroll this way, it is a praiseworthy custom and should not be treated leniently, as proven by Pesachim 50b, chapter Makom Shenahagu. Beis Yosef. In my humble circumstances, these responsa reached me in manuscript. Chazeh HaTenufah is an abridgment of Rosh's responsa. At its end is an abridgment of responsa of Rosh that were not printed. This responsum is number 55, where the text contains a variation and addition: 'If this is their custom, it is a praiseworthy custom and should not be treated leniently, for it is a proper custom to make one's Shabbos manner of wrapping different from that of weekdays. The custom of our ancestors is Torah. Proof comes from the chapter Makom Shenahagu.' Machazik Berachah 2.
