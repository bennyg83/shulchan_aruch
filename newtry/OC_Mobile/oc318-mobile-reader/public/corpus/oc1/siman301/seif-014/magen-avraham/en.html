Over her scarf. This is the language of Maharil: The practice of women to carry on Shabbos pieces of linen cloth over the scarf when rains fall, to protect from the dirt of the rain, is permitted even for wealthy women to take out into the public domain, since it is fit for poor women, and is considered clothing even for wealthy women and not a burden. This implies that even ordinary pieces of cloth, whenever she wraps herself in them in the manner of clothing, are permitted. But to place them on her head so that they rest on her without wrapping herself in them is forbidden, and so it appears in the Beit Yosef. If her intention is that the rains should not distress her, it is permitted in every manner, even if thereby she is protected from dirt. So too it appears in Shiltei Giborim, chapter 6, in Mordechai, and likewise in seif 13, seif katan 21, and seif 35.
