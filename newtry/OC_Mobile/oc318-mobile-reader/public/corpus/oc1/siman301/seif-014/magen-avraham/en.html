Over her scarf. Maharil writes: 'Women customarily carry pieces of linen cloth over their scarves on Shabbos when it rains, to protect themselves from rainwater's dirt. Even wealthy women may take them into the public domain, since they are suitable for poor women and are therefore considered clothing rather than a burden even for the wealthy.' This implies that even ordinary pieces of cloth are permitted whenever she wraps herself in them as clothing. Merely placing one on her head so that it rests there without wrapping herself in it is forbidden. Beis Yosef also implies this. If her intent is to avoid discomfort from the rain, it is permitted in every manner, even if it also protects her from dirt. Shiltei HaGibborim, chapter 6, and Mordechai imply this, as do se'if 13 and se'ifim ketanim 21 and 35.
