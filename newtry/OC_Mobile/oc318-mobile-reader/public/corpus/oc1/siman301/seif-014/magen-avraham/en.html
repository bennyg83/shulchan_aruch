Over a scarf — Maharil wrote: women are accustomed on Shabbat to carry linen pieces over the scarf when rain falls to save from mud; also the wealthy may go out to the public domain, for regarding poverty it appears important, also for wealth it is clothing and not a burden — end quote; implies even linen pieces, whatever is wrapped as manner of clothing permitted; but to place on her head resting on it without wrapping, forbidden — so Beit Yosef; and if her intent is that rain not distress her, permitted in every case even saved from mud thus — Shach chapter 6, Mordechai, Mishnah Berurah seif 13, Eliyah Rabbah, Shelah.
