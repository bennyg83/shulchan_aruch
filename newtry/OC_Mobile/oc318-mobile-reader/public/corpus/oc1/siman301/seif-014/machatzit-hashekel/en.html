Se'if katan 14: In his garment, but in his hand, etc. Shabbos 11b states explicitly that carrying it in one's hand is the ordinary manner of carrying. Rashi on 62a, s.v. veha hotza'ah kele'achar yad, likewise writes that any item not serving as an ornament is carried by hand rather than worn as clothing. In one's garment, however, it is sometimes not the ordinary manner of carrying, as will be explained.
