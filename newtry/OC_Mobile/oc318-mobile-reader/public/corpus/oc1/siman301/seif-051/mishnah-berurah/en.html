180) One may go out, etc. None of these are considered burdens for such people; they are like ornaments.
181) In his hand. This is the correct reading, as printed in Olas Shabbos and Tosefes Shabbos. The reading 'on his head' found in some editions is a scribal error.
182) Wrapped around the hand. The same applies when wrapped directly on the wound. Magen Avraham. See above, se'ifim 22 and 28, where many later authorities agree. The Mechaber's unqualified wording implies that this is considered clothing even if he did not wear it for any time before Shabbos, unlike the preceding se'if 50. Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah incline this way. See Biur Halachah.
