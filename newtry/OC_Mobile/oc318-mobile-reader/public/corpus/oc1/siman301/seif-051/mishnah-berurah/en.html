(א) Permitted to go out, etc. — all these are not considered burdens for them but like ornaments.

(ב) On his hand — so it should read; so printed in Or Zarua and Tosafot Shantz; what is found in some editions "on his head" is scribal error.

(ג) Wrapped on the hand — likewise wrapped on the wound itself [Magen Avraham]; see above seif 22 and seif 28 what is written there, many Acharonim agreed; implies from Mechaber's conclusion in this considered clothing even if he did not go out one hour from erev Shabbat, unlike previous seif 50; so Magen Avraham; Shulchan Aruch HaRav sides; see Biur Halacha.
