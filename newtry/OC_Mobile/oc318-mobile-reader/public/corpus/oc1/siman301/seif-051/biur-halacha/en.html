Wrapped around the hand. See Mishnah Berurah for what we wrote in Magen Avraham's and Eliyah Rabbah's names. Chayei Adam likewise rules leniently concerning insignificant rags. Gra writes that according to Shulchan Aruch in the preceding se'if, here too the person must have gone out wearing them for some time before nightfall. Taz writes likewise. Gra clarifies that Shulchan Aruch omitted this detail in se'if 7 because he there followed the other Rishonim, who hold that in order for something to be considered clothing for carrying, one need not have gone out wearing it before nightfall. The Gemara mentions this concerning pikorin because otherwise handling them would be forbidden. Yet in se'if 50 Shulchan Aruch includes this detail while copying Rambam, who holds that the Gemara concerns carrying, and omits it here, thereby contradicting himself. Accordingly, one certainly should not be lenient against Rashi, Rambam, Rabbeinu Yonah, Ran, and Rashba, who all follow one approach: The prohibition is because of carrying, and in our case too he must have gone out wearing them for some time before nightfall. In practice, however, the matter requires examination, since Shulchan Aruch rules unqualifiedly leniently here.
