Se'if 51: It is permitted. To go out, etc., and likewise with rags. He learned it from the combed flax mentioned above, and therefore concluded there, 'provided that he went out.' He explains it like Rashi there, regarding a wound, and it follows the Rambam's method in the preceding siman; this is also the view of Rabbeinu Yonah, as I wrote above in siman 308, se'if 24. But here he stated it anonymously according to Tosafos' explanation, that the matter concerns the prohibition of moving muktzeh. According to Tosafos' explanation there is no proof from there, and with a wound it is permitted even if he did not go out before Shabbos, as written in Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 19, in the name of Rabbeinu Yitzchak, and as Tosafos write, s.v. 'and if,' that 'he took it out before Shabbos' is because of the prohibition of moving the fleece, unlike rags, as above. Therefore they omitted this condition, 'provided that he went out,' etc., and this contradicts the preceding se'if.
