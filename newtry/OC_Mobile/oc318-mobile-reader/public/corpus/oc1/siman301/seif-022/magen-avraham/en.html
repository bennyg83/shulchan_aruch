One may not wrap a thread. Rambam writes: 'One may go out with cotton or a sponge upon the wound, provided he does not wrap a thread or cord around them, for the thread and cord are important to him and do not benefit the wound.' This implies that even around the sponge one may not wrap a thread. Rif and Tosefta likewise imply this, unlike Bach. A thread or cord is significant and is not subordinate to the sponge, unlike a rag bandage: when one removes it, he discards it, so it is insignificant and subordinate to the sponge. See se'if katan 51.
