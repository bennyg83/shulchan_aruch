Forbidden to wrap a string — Rambam wrote: with down and sponge on the wound, provided he not wrap string or ointment on them, for string and ointment are important to him and do not help the wound — end quote; implies even on the sponge forbidden to wrap string; so Rif and Tosefta, unlike Bach; reason: string or ointment important and not batel to sponge like rag wrapping which one throws away and is batel to sponge — Eliyah Rabbah.
