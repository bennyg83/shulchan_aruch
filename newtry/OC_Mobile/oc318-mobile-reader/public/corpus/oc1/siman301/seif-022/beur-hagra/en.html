Se'if 22: And likewise with a peel, etc.; and if they fell off, etc. This is from the Tosefta there. From here there is a difficulty against Rashi's explanation in the last chapter of Eruvin, where he explained that the prohibition of returning a bandage is because of smoothing. For this reason Tosafos had to explain that which is stated in chapter 6 of Shabbos, 'provided that he not place,' etc., and 'if,' etc., as referring to the entire mishnah and as being because of carrying. But this is not so.
But it is forbidden, etc.; but with a bandage, etc. The language of the Tosefta is: one may go out with wool, etc., provided that he not wrap a thread or cord over them, etc.; one may go out with a bandage that is on a wound, etc. The Tur explains that the first clause refers to the wound and the latter clause to the dressing. See se'if 51 and see the Bach, who was pressed to explain there also that it refers to the dressing. But this is the Rambam's method, who explains it the opposite way: the first clause refers to the dressing and the latter clause to the wound. His language is: with wool and a sponge that are on a wound, provided that he does not wrap over them a thread or cord, because the thread and cord are significant to him and do not help the wound; and one may go out with garlic peel, etc., and with a bandage that is on a wound. His statement 'they are significant,' etc., requires both factors: they are significant and they do not help; only then is it forbidden. He is precise in the Tosefta's wording: in the first clause it says 'that he not wrap over them,' etc., and says 'a thread or cord'; in the latter clause it says 'that is on a wound' and says 'a bandage.' The prohibition applies only on top of the dressing, and specifically with something significant; something insignificant is nullified to the dressing, as written in siman 39. In the latter clause it says a bandage, that any bandage is permitted since it is on a wound and helps prevent the wound from being scratched, as Rashi writes on folio 50a, s.v. 'at the time,' etc.; see there.
