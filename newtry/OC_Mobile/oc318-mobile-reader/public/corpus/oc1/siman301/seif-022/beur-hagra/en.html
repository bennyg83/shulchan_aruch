(ב) But forbidden etc. but in bandage etc. — Tosefta language go out in wool etc. provided not wrap thread or smear etc. go out in bandage on wound etc.; Tur explains first on wound, end on scab; see 51a and Bach strained there on scab; but Rambam reverses: first scab, end wound; wool and sponge on wound provided not wrap thread or smear, thread and smear significant to him and do not help wound; go out in garlic peel etc. and bandage on wound; what "significant" etc. needs both significant and not helping then forbidden; deduced Tosefta language first said not wrap etc. said thread or smear, end said on wound said bandage; prohibition only on scab and only significant item, insignificant batel to scab as siman 39; end said bandage any bandage permitted on wound helps not wound, as Rashi 50a s.v. when see there.
