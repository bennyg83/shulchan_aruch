Or if he went out with them, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote that regarding moving them it is permitted even if he merely had them in mind. Regarding going out, see Beit Yosef, that this depends on the views of the Rishonim: according to the Rosh and Tosafot it is permitted, while according to the Ran, Rabbeinu Yonah, and the Rashba cited by the Beit Yosef in siman 308, it is forbidden, because the matter is not evident and it appears as though he is carrying on Shabbos. The Gra wrote that this is also the view of the Rambam. Know that the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Tosafot that it is not specifically if he went out with them for one hour while it was still day; the same applies if he sat in them. See the Gra in siman 308, seif 24, who wrote that according to those Rishonim who are stringent that thought does not help, sitting likewise does not help regarding carrying out.
