177) People with scabs. They do this so the covering will look like hair on their heads.
178) Which he dyed and wrapped. He dyed it for beauty and wrapped it with a cord, so it is considered clothing.
179) Or he went out, etc. This demonstrates that they are intended as clothing. If he neither dyed nor previously wore them, they are muktzeh and may not even be moved. If he designated them before Shabbos for wearing, see siman 308:24, which permits moving them.
