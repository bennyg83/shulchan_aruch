(א) Lime carriers — and do this so it appear like hair on their heads.

(ב) That they colored and wrapped — meaning colored for adornment and wrapped in ointment, then considered clothing.

(ג) Or that he went out, etc. — reveals his intent they stand for clothing; if not colored and did not go out, forbidden to move because muktzeh; but if thought from erev Shabbat to go out in them see siman 308 seif 24, moving permitted.
