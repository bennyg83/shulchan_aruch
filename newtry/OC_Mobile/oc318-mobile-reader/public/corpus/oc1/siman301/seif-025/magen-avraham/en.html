(א) For every person — meaning every person may wear that amulet even if it did not heal except one person three times; but if he writes another amulet, it is forbidden.

(ב) Both proofs together — such as he wrote an amulet and healed Reuven, and another for Shimon, and another, and healed Shimon twice, and man's expertise not yet established since he did not heal three people and not the amulet; afterward healed Levi with that amulet — then both proofs came together.

(ג) Do not rely, etc. — practical difference: if the healer lost his proof, such as wrote three amulets and they did not help, then the amulet is also forbidden; which is not so when both came together, or amulet proof came first — then even if healer lost his proof, amulet proof remains.

(ד) Not the man — for we say the sick person's fortune caused; if so, he himself is also forbidden to go out with it.

(ה) Of writing — and if from holy scriptures, forbidden to go out with it unless covered, as seif 7 — see there (Darkei Moshe, Hagahot Maimoni); and so in the Gemara.
