For every person. This means that every person may carry that same written amulet, even though it healed only one person three times. If the writer produces another written amulet, however, it is forbidden.
Both proven statuses occur together. For example, he wrote one amulet and healed Reuven, another for Shimon, and a third amulet that healed Shimon twice. The writer is not yet proven, because he has not healed three people, nor is the amulet proven. If that same amulet then heals Levi, both proven statuses arise together.
It is not dependent, etc. A practical difference arises if the writer loses his proven status, such as by writing three amulets that prove ineffective. The amulet then also becomes forbidden. If the two proven statuses arose together, however, or the amulet became proven first, then even if the writer loses his proven status, the amulet's proven status remains.
