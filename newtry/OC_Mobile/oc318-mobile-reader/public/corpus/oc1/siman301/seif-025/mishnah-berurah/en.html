87) One may not go out, etc. See what we wrote below in Mishnah Berurah, siman 308, se'if katan 129.
88) One may go out wearing it. Only when worn in the manner of clothing is it permitted, because it is like an ornament and garment for the sick person. He may not hold it in his hand and carry it four cubits. Gemara.
89) Whether, etc. A writer becomes established only after healing three people of the same illness with three documents containing the same incantation, even if each was healed only once. He may then write that incantation for anyone with that illness, but is not established for other incantations. An amulet becomes established when one document containing one incantation heals three times; anyone with that illness may wear that same document. The same incantation in another document is ineffective for this status, even if written by the same person, because the writer was not established through three documents.
90) One incantation. If he wrote three different incantations, the writer does not become established, since each incantation helped only one person.
91) Whenever he writes it. Even for another person. Likewise, anyone with that illness may wear one of the original documents written by the established writer; once the writer is established for this incantation, there is no difference between a new document and one he already wrote.
92) And it healed three times. Even if it healed only one person on all three occasions.
93) For any person. Anyone with that illness may wear that document, even though it healed only one person, since its effectiveness was established three times. The same incantation in another document remains forbidden, even if the first document healed three people, because only that document, not the writer, became established.
94) Each helped three people. If each document helped only one person, the writer alone, not the amulet, would be established. Since each document helped three people once, each document also became established.
95) Or one person three times. Three times is specified so that the amulet too will be established, as explained above.
96) Both forms of establishment together. For example, one document healed Reuven three times; another healed Shimon three times; and a third healed Shimon twice. At that point neither writer nor third amulet was established. When the third document then healed Levi, both forms of establishment arose together. Magen Avraham.
97) We do not attribute it, etc. The practical difference is where the healer later loses his established status by writing three more ineffective documents. If his status preceded that of the amulet, the previously effective amulet also becomes forbidden, for it had not independently become established. If both statuses arose together, or the amulet's came first, the amulet remains established despite the healer's loss of status.
98) Three amulets for one person. Meaning three documents, even if all contain the same incantation.
99) Not the writer. He may not write another document even with this incantation, because the three documents healed only one person and perhaps that patient's fortune caused the cure. Magen Avraham says even that patient may no longer wear the documents that healed him, since they are classified as amulets not made by an established expert. Other later authorities question this.
100) Nor the amulet. An amulet becomes established only when one document heals three times.
101) A written amulet. If it contains sacred writings, one may not carry it into the public domain unless covered with leather or another material. Otherwise, he may need to relieve himself, remove it, and carry it four cubits. If removing it would endanger the patient, he may wear it in every case.
102) Of roots. Meaning roots of medicinal plants.
