And there are those who permit, etc. This is specifically when it is fixed at its end, which is called a zenkil. But in the middle of the belt it is forbidden. With a strap fixed in the belt, even at its end, it is forbidden, even though it is impossible to remove it from there on Shabbos. So it appears from the Bach. It also implies that the practice follows those who permit, and so it implies in Shiltei Giborim, pages 125 and 173.
