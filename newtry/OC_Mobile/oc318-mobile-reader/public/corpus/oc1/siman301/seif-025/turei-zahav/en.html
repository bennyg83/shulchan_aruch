With an amulet that is not proven — the rules and explanation are thus: this seif is the Tur's language per Tosafos in R' Yitzchak's name, disagreeing with Rashi: Rashi holds an expert man helps his expertise even for other things not yet tried, comparing to a goring ox; and in an amulet that was expert, it helps even if another writes afterward. R' Yitzchak and the Rosh hold: no expert at all except what was verified; three divisions here: (1) expert man, not amulet — wrote three amulets of one incantation, each healed one person once — then these amulets have virtue from the writer who made three cures in this writing, but the amulet is not expert, meaning the writing itself has no virtue without the writer; Tosafos in Rashba's name asked: what difference? It teaches the amulet is not expert here, for whenever he writes this incantation it helps from the expert man; they answered: difference if this man lost his virtue — wrote for this incantation afterward three times and did not help — if virtue were from the amulet itself, each healing three times, permitted from amulet expertise though man's declined; now he did not heal each writing only once, man's virtue lost and amulet's not yet reached — therefore even without losing man's virtue, "expert man not amulet" though no difference there, because Ashiri and Tur wrote another difference: another may not write that incantation; Tosafos did not want to answer from that difference, for then difficulty even expert man and amulet — another may not write per Tosafos who requires only what was verified; here even expert man and amulet not verified for another — Tosafos only mentioned what helps if amulet has virtue, and all the more man and amulet; if man's expertise lost as above. But surely while man's virtue remains, the amulet he wrote already has virtue from the man though not from the amulet, and permitted for anyone, as in the end: he can write this incantation anew — why lessen what he already wrote since established as expert man? Second division: expert amulet not man — one incantation healed three times, amulet expert for everyone; all the more expert man and amulet — one incantation in three amulets, each helped three people or one person three times — man expert for that incantation in every amulet he writes, and those amulets expert for everyone. Third: expert man and amulet — healed in three writings of one incantation, each three times even one person — nevertheless only for this incantation and this person, not another writing. Now explain Tur's divisions: "and healed the three" — three people specifically, else one person's fortune caused acceptance of writing, as here in Shulchan Aruch; "that amulet expert for every person" — certainly those amulets already written permitted for everyone from man's expertise; difference only if lost, as above; Beit Yosef brought Mahari Chaviv — "not expert amulet" understood from what he explains below in expert amulet, etc. — these words need no explanation, for there too man's expertise helps; he listed all virtues and we know the differences in "lost," as above; Tur needed third division — two qualities — to teach even here only for this incantation, not like Rashi; "but if he wrote three amulets for one person" — amulet obviously has no virtue, each healed once only, and man not expert even for this incantation since one person's fortune helped — we say where no amulet virtue; you want to give virtue from scribe since from man — I say perhaps this person's fortune caused the amulet to help, why lessen this one's fortune from that — clear in my humble opinion; I saw other explanations not possible in my humble opinion.
