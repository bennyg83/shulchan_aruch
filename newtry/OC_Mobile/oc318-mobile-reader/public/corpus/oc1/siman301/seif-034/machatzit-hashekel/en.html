(Seif katan 34) Attached, etc. The Rosh permits when it is attached somewhat, and being tied is no worse than a partial attachment. It is not nullified to the belt; on the contrary, since the belt is not so significant, perhaps the belt is nullified to the handkerchief. Only with respect to a garment, which is significant, is the handkerchief nullified to it. The Taz wrote in seif katan 14 that the beginning of the remedy is to attach it to the end of the belt so that it will be like a long belt. However, the Taz wrote that he should attach it with a knot that is not permanent. This must be when he wants to tie it on Shabbos, for it is forbidden to make a permanent knot. In Eliyah Rabbah he disagrees, for if it is not a permanent knot it does not help, since it is not considered attached. In Tosefes Shabbos he emends the Taz to read that he ties a permanent knot, meaning from erev Shabbos. But if it is not permanent, even the Taz agrees that it does not help.
