127) Folded on his shoulder. Although this is forbidden for a tallis, as in se'if 29, it is permitted for a scarf because scarves are ordinarily worn folded over the shoulders on weekdays. This applies only where some people sometimes wear them this way.
128) Even without a thread, etc. One opinion in the Gemara requires a thread protruding from the scarf and wound around his finger to keep it from falling off his shoulders, but we do not rule that way.
129) If the scarf does not, etc. If it is too short to cover his head and most of his body, its two ends must be tied below his shoulders so it resembles a sash. If it is large enough to cover his head and most of his body, it may be folded over his shoulders because that is a manner of wearing it. See Biur Halachah.
