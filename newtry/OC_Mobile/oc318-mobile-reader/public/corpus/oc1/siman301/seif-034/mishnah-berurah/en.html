(127) The folded over his shoulder - A'Ag Dabatlit is forbidden in a folded kamsh in the skat in a soder, a desuder, through wearing it like that on weekdays, to fold it over his shoulders, and 17, Myri, in places through which some people go, yes, sometimes.
(rejected) Even if there is no nima, etc. - the Madd in the Gemara, according to them, should have a nima that comes out of the order and wraps around his finger to hold it so that it does not fall off his shoulders, but not the ki'l most.
(Kecht) And if there is no soder, etc. - and we mean that it is short and does not have a hem to cover his head and most of it, then both of his heads should be tied below his shoulders, and it is like a sash and shri, but if it is short, but if the soder has a hem to cover his head and most of it, then even if they fold over his shoulders a shree, etc.
