Se'if 34: With a folded scarf. From the fact that it says, 'one who goes out with a folded tallis,' etc., and 'one may go out with a scarf that is on his shoulder,' etc., it must be similar to a tallis that is folded; otherwise, even with a tallis it would be permitted. See the Aruch, who explains 'hartanim' as runners, with a folded scarf so that it not impede their running. So it is also written in the Rambam. See the Rambam, and see the Ran, who cites the Aruch.
