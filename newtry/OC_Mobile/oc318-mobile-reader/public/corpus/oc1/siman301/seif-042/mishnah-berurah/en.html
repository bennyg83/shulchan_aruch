(Kano) that they are teflin - they don't have strips of teflin, because they are inherent. Sometimes a person bothers to make a kami like a teflin, and the NAM is to keep them and darken them at all, and refer to Siman 11 of the Magen Avraham who wrote in our time.
(Kenz) and they are tied - Ral that there is a shar knot on the head and also due to a hand knot of a hand properly corrected according to its size [and not larger] that he may wear it and if they are not tied and on Shabbos it is not permissible to tie a knot, he cannot put them in through wearing a pair A couple should wait until it gets dark and bring them.
(Kanakh) A pair of pairs - R.L. One hand-on-hand teflin and one head-on-head teflin is inserted to the point where it is preserved and taken out, and then goes back and puts another pair in this way, and even a DKIL Shabbos, the time of the teflin is MM as the way he wears it on weekdays. Magen Avraham Dasha, who finds a tafflin, is forbidden to bring them in on Shabbos in any way from a regular Dachion Dayna garment, to lay them down on weekdays of the hol, from the back, and there are others who disagree about it, and Sal Dachion Damdina Dagmara there is no prohibition against them in this, only as a starting point, it is not correct to assume that they are not coincidental, and refer to Sha'ar Hamelech Pit of the Laws of Shabbos, which he found in Shita 21 Dahrabad and the Rashba'a disagree on this. The Magen Avraham also wrote that according to his method, if a woman who wears a talit wears a veil [we mean a talit of a mitzvah that one is not accustomed to wear for the sake of a mitzvah only], then a woman who is accustomed to wear such a talit is guilty of the sin of dakin, and if a woman is accustomed to wear such a talit, it will be worn on her back.
