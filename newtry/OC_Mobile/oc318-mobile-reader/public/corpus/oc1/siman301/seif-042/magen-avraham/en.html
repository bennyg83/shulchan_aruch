(א) And not amulets — for if they have no straps we say perhaps they are amulets, for a person troubles to make an amulet like tefillin [Beit Yosef]; and see end siman 301.

(ב) Pair by pair — Beit Yosef challenged: since we hold Shabbat is not time for tefillin, he could bring two pairs at once without violating "do not add," since it is not their time, as Gemara — end quote; it should be: since we are not expert in precise placement, as siman 34; if placed not in its place it is a burden; therefore place only one pair; and see notes siman 451; Rashba wrote: even for the view Shabbat is not time for tefillin, forbidden to wear more than one pair, not permitted more than weekday clothing, Gemara; so Ran; and what we establish as R' Meir who holds Shabbat is time for tefillin is because our Mishnah is R' Meir — see there; but a woman forbidden to put them on, even though we hold permitted to go out in sack because shepherds go out in them — called clothing for everyone; women are different, alone (Shabbat 62b); therefore it appears if she went out in affixed tzitzit she is liable for chatas; but complete man's garment, woman may go out; Yoreh Deah siman 182; not so a ring, not called complete clothing.
