And not amulets. If they have no straps, we say that they may be amulets, for a person may trouble himself to make an amulet resembling tefillin. Beis Yosef. See what I wrote at the beginning of se'if katan 11.
Pair by pair. Tosafos Yom Tov asks: Since we rule that Shabbos is not a time for tefillin, why not bring in two pairs at once? He does not violate adding to a mitzvah when it is not the mitzvah's time, as stated in the Gemara. One must answer that since we are not expert in positioning tefillin, as stated in siman 34, a pair placed outside its proper location would be a burden. He should therefore wear only one pair. See what I wrote at the end of siman 651. Maharsha writes that even according to the view that Shabbos is not a time for tefillin, one may not wear more than one pair, since the Sages permitted only what he ordinarily wears during the week, as stated in the Gemara. Ran writes likewise. The Gemara establishes the Mishnah according to the view that Shabbos is a time for tefillin because the Mishnah follows Rabbi Meir, who holds that view; see there. A woman, however, may not bring them in by wearing them. Although we rule that one may go out wearing a sack because shepherds ordinarily do so and it is therefore considered clothing for everyone, women are a separate people for this purpose. Shabbos 62. It therefore appears to me that a woman who goes out wearing a fringed tallis is liable to a sin-offering. A woman may, however, go out wearing something that is fully considered a man's garment. See Yoreh Deah 182. A ring, by contrast, is not considered a complete garment.
