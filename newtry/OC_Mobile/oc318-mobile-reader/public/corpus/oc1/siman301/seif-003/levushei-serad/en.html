Seif katan 5. (Beit Yosef in the name of the Tosefta.) This was brought in Taz, seif katan 1, and the Taz explained the reason: since the pleasure does not come until afterward. But strolling is permitted even though the pleasure comes afterward, because strolling itself has no prohibition. The Magen Avraham follows his view, for he brought in seif katan 4 the words of the Bach that it is permitted to run for pleasure that will continue afterward. Therefore he asked and answered that he does it for healing, as in siman 328. Therefore strolling is permitted, because it is not recognizable that he does it for healing, unlike running, where it is evident that he does it for healing, which is forbidden. Similar to this is in Magen Avraham there, seif katan 37.
