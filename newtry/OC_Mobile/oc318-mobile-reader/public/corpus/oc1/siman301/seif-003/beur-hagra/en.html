s.c. may skip and jump etc. and better he skip — Rashi there: permitted even to skip, forbidden to jump. S.D. to skip. Appears his intent: Gemara challenged whether depends on if they can; hangs on able and unable; asked why forbidden to place an amah — where able, even skip permitted; where unable, even without skipping forbidden — how possible? Must say in jumping that uproots both feet as one, as Yerushalmi end ch. 19 and Hagahot Maimoniyot brought; see Rambam on chapter 8 of Ohalot mishnah 5, holds jumping lighter than skipping, prohibition here is interrupting feet in width like coarse stride said there; rather an amah, for at start prohibition also thus; therefore S.D. to skip that even skip permitted; likewise Tur language "better he skip" etc.; did not write forbidden to go around — surely if not skip, permitted to go around; but regarding pass through wrote forbidden to pass through, for always forbidden for optional matter as explained in his words see there.
