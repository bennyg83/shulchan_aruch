(In Magen Avraham, seif katan 31.) And not like what the Bach wrote. Note: It seems to me that the Tur speaks of a sponge that is not attached to the wound except by wrapping the string around it; in that case the Rambam agrees that it is permitted. But the Rambam speaks of a case where the lint and sponge are already attached to the wound without this, and therefore the thread and the cord are significant to him.
