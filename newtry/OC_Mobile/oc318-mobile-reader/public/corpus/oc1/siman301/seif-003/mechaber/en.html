If one is walking and he comes to the edge of water, one may jump across it even if it is an area where he is not able to place one foot down on the opposite side before uprooting the foot on the first side. And it is better that he jump across it instead of going around it so as not to increase his / her walking and it is forbidden to pass through [the water] out of a concern for coming to squeeze [the water out of his / her clothes].
