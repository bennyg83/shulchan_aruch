And forbidden to pass through — it implies from his language that if he cannot jump, better to go around than pass through, for he ruled and taught "forbidden to pass through," etc.; and so in the Gemara: to jump increases walking; to pass through water sometimes leads to squeezing — implies better to go around before passing through water; and when passing through water there is concern he come to a d'oraisa prohibition; and what is written in siman 613 seif 8 is where it is permitted to pass through water, since the Sages did not decree there — he should not increase walking — Bach; and so is Rama's language there.
