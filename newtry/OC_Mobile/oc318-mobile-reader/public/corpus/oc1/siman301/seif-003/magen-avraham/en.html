It is forbidden to cross. His language implies that if one cannot leap over it, he should go around rather than cross through it, for the ruling states unequivocally that crossing is forbidden. The Gemara likewise says that going around increases his walk, while crossing water may sometimes lead to wringing. This implies that going around takes precedence over crossing through water. Furthermore, crossing through water creates concern that he may violate a Torah prohibition. The ruling in siman 613:8 concerns a case in which crossing water is permitted; since the Sages did not issue a decree there, he should not increase his walk. Bach. Rama's language there also implies this.
