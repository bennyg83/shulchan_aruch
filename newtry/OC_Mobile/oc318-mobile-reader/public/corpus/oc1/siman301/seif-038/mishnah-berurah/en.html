142) In accordance with its law. See Mishnah Berurah above, siman 13:1, where all details are explained.
143) He is liable. Not because of the tallis: although he does not fulfill the positive mitzvah of tzitzis, it remains clothing and creates no Shabbos liability. Liability is for carrying the strings.
144) And he intends to use them. They therefore do not become subordinate to the tallis as canopy straps do below in se'if 39.
145) Although it has no techeiles. We rule that techeiles does not prevent fulfillment with the white strings.
