And his intent is upon them until he completes them. This is the view of the Rishonim who explain that what the Gemara says, these are significant, is because his intent is to complete them. There are also many Rishonim who explain that these are significant because they are threads of a mitzvah, or alternatively threads of techeles, as Rashi explained. There is a practical difference between these explanations for several matters; see Nishmas Adam, klal 56. This is not the place to elaborate.
