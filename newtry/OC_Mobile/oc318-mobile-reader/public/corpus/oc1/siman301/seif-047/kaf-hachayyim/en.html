47) There: Inserted into his garment, etc. It requires examination whether a man may go out with a needle inserted to join the edges of his robe and garments. See below, siman 303, laws 9 and 18, which imply that this is permitted specifically for a woman. See Beis Yosef in this siman regarding coins sewn into a garment: since his intent is not to carry the coin but to fasten his garment, the coin is subordinate to the garment. Mahara Azulai, of blessed memory, in his manuscript glosses; Birkei Yosef 2. See Shulchan Aruch HaRav 4 and 5, however, who writes that even when the needle is inserted for purposes of wearing, if it has an eye he is liable to a sin-offering, and if it has no eye he is exempt but the act is forbidden. All this concerns a man; regarding a woman, see siman 303.
