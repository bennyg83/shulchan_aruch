(ב) Mechaber Semag wraps in hide, in sheet, returns covers with clothes, lest leave in contempt — Rambam Pithei Teshuvah Hagahot 25; carrying kele'achar yad see there; requires study; Tosafot Shantz Shabbat 62a s.v. all.

(ג) In Magen Avraham matter of lighting — siman 334 note 9, there 11 only once; Taz 7 and Magen Avraham 14 garments 11 specifically joined courtyard, 11 also unjoined, explained there. Magen Avraham wet garments Abudraham: even fell in water before wearing permitted wear, no decree lest squeeze; Levush on Semag there no need; gloss Semag he who particular about water means does not want water in them; cloths always soaked no concern, moving permitted.

(ד) From this learn Mechaber seif 46 forbidden dry near fire, reason cooking or bleaching; Magen Avraham note 57 cloth fell in water forbidden spread on stove kalkalin where yad soledes bo, instead spread table Shabbat where yad soledes bo cooking byproduct light and bleaching; likewise baby windeln soiled in urine forbidden dry at stove where yad soledes bo bleaching; if cloth fell in beer/honey liquid, dispute Beit Yosef 87 whether cooking after cooking; possible dyed liquids bleaching; explained siman 302. Know "garments" includes wool.

(ה) Magen Avraham seif 49 permitted wash hands in river Shabbat — siman 326 laws of washing, not dry near fire, wash whole body in cold, explained there.

(ו) If permitted carry panels linen and wood long, dismantle and lean like kilah, temporary tent — explained siman 315. If slanted from erev Shabbat, permitted carry for sun or lest rain distress flesh; though seifim 13–14 and Magen Avraham 22–23 all distress permitted only wearing as clothing, not full clothing unlike carrying in hand not clothing at all, real carrying in hand, appears forbidden unjoined courtyard.
