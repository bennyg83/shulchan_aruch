One who finds a Torah scroll in a field — if it is not a time of danger, he sits and guards it until dark (the Rambam's wording; and in danger he leaves it and goes). If rain is falling, he wraps it in a hide, returns and covers it, and enters with it.() ואם היו גשמים יורדים מתעטף בעור וחוזר ומכסה אותו ונכנס בו:
