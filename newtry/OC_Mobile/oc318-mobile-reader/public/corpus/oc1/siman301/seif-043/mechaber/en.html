43. If one finds a Torah scroll in a field, if it is not a time of danger, one sits and watches over it until dark (the words of the Rambam; and in a time of danger he should leave it alone and come back for it). If it is raining, he should wrap it in in a skin and return and cover it and enter with it.() ואם היו גשמים יורדים מתעטף בעור וחוזר ומכסה אותו ונכנס בו:
