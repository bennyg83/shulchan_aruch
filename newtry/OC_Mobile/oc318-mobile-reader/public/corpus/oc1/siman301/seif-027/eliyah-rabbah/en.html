The grasshopper, etc. A locust also helps as a segulah for weakness of the hip bones. The tooth of a live fox is hung on one who has slept too long, to wake him; if it is from a dead fox, it does the opposite. A nail from the wood of one who was hanged is hung on the throat of one who has tertian fever (Rambam and Maggid, chapter 19). Rashi explained that they place the nail on the swelling in the wound.
With every amulet, etc. Meaning, even in the house.
