104) With a grasshopper egg. A grasshopper is a locust. See the Gemara for the uses of all these objects. In their time they were established remedies and had the status of an established amulet, so one could go out with them suspended on his body.
105) And it is not recognizable as a remedy. Although the listed objects are not visibly medicinal, knowledgeable people recognize their curative use as a segulah, so they are treated as recognizable.
106) Every incantation is permitted. Even if it is not yet established and we do not know that it heals, it is permitted on a weekday. Since some incantations heal, perhaps this one will too, making its purpose somewhat recognizable. Only incantations known to be ineffective are forbidden.
107) With any amulet that is not established. Gra asks that the Mishnah forbids going out with a non-established amulet, implying it is permitted on a weekday; Levushei Serad offers a strained resolution. If the illness may be dangerous, it appears that all agree it is permitted in every case.
