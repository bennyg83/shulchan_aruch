Se'if 27: One may go out. Since Abaye and Rava hold like him. See the Rambam in his commentary.
But if, etc.; but. There: 'but if there is no,' etc. Rashi explains, s.v. 'that there is,' etc., that it is not recognizable, etc. They said in the Yerushalmi, chapter 'Eight Creeping Creatures': they whisper charms, etc., for the intestines and for the eye on Shabbos, etc. The Rosh asked from all the charms that we find in the Gemara, and answered that this refers to doing an act, etc.
And it is not forbidden. According to the Ran's explanation, which explains the matter regarding anything, whether a whispered charm, etc.; it speaks of those that have been tested.
And there is one who. He explains 'but if there is no' as meaning in such a case, where it is not known. This requires study, for in the mishnah we learned, 'and not with an amulet,' etc., which implies that on a weekday it is permitted.
