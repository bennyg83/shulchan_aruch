Hat — Rambam chapter 22: hat with rim making shade like tent on his clothing — permitted to wear; if extended from garment around head or face like tent, tight on head, rim very hard like roof — forbidden, making temporary tent — end quote; he wrote all this specifically if hard and does not bend; Hagahot Maimoni: our hats permitted, for they slope downward; Semag implies this is what they call britel in Poland; see siman 315 notes 8 and 11; some refrain from placing tallit on head so tallit not from here and there like walls, siman 315; requires study for them, for all poskim wrote prohibition and Gemara too — and it is difficult: should Gemara be emended? It should distinguish: when he has tallit on head vs. not — thus Rosh chapter HaMevi proves from Eruvin chapter 2: since main intent is tent for shade, forbidden even without walls — thus is Rambam's language precise; therefore if hat very hard, place at slope as written; Bach: where custom is lenient per Rashi, custom; Maharil to permit.
