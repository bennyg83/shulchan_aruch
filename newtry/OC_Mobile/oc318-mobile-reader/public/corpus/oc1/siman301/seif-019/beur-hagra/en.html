Se'if 19: One who is. On folio 63b, 'they would attach a chain,' etc. They said this only because of the concern that she may remove it and show it, according to Rashi's explanation; according to the Rambam, it is because it is loose. This is unlike here, where they are firmly set, as Tosafos wrote on folio 58a, 'What is different,' and regarding that which the mishnah teaches, etc., that presumably they are well attached, etc.
