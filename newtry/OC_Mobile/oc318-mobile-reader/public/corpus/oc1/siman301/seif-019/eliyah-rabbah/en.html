And some say the opposite, etc. See Nachalas Tzvi and the later authorities, who wrote at length about this. I have shortened it, for they dispute which needle was considered the normal way of carrying it out in the time of the Gemara. Now everything depends on the time: if it is their way to go out that way on weekdays and it is not an ornament, he is liable; if it is not the normal way to carry it out like that, he is exempt but it is forbidden. If most people do not go out that way, even though the people of one place do carry it that way, he is exempt, for their view is nullified relative to all people.
