138) With gloves. They are complete garments made to protect from cold. The stringent opinion fears that he may need to use his hands to remove a louse or flea that bites him, forget, and carry the gloves four cubits in the public domain.
139) Called gloves. These are what we call hentschich. Regarding a muff into which both hands are inserted, Taz permits it according to all views: even if one hand is removed, it remains worn on the other. Others say it is more stringent because it easily falls, and people sometimes carry it by hand. Since the Mechaber advises stringency for gloves, one should be stringent for a muff as well.
