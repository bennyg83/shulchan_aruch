Called guantes — in our language handsches; reason to permit: clothing on hands; forbidders: cannot use clothes well, sometimes must scratch for lice/flea, must remove lest forget and carry four amos in public domain — Shelah. My humble opinion: specifically handsches — removing one hand carries removed glove in other hand, concern four amos; our arbe'el warms both hands in one sleeve, removing one hand arbe'el remains clothing on other hand still inside, no fall concern, holds in hand like wooden shoe Rama seif 16 — no concern; Mateh Moshe wrote arbe'el here — clear, practiced arbe'el everywhere, Heaven forbid all unlawful — but handsches be careful as here. What Rama writes "or tie them" — puzzling: if hanging by knot not on hand, burden on him — what help tying, not batel to garment, important; all the more tied to belt, as siman 303 on handkerchief.
