And it is forbidden to cross, etc. Even if going around is impossible. As for what he wrote, lest he come to squeezing, one should not say that in any event it is forbidden because soaking it is its laundering. Tosafot in Shabbos 111 answered that we say this only where there is dirt on the garment. One may also say that we do not say that this is its laundering with something in which the dirt is not visible. However, I found in Sefer Yere’im, page 39, and in Piskei Rekanti, siman 113, that they answered that here it discusses leather garments. This requires study; see siman 302, seif 9.
