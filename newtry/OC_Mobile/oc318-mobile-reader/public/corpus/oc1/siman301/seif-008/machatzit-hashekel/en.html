Se'if katan 8: To guard, etc. Guarding one's produce is somewhat of a mitzvah, so the outward journey was permitted. The return was not, because this is not a complete mitzvah. Taz gives another reason: Regarding guarding produce, one cannot permit the return so that the person will undertake the initial journey. One cannot say that without permission to return he would not go, because he fears a loss to his produce and will certainly go; moreover, what concern is it of ours if he does not go?
