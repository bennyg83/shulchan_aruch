(א) s.ch. pierced liable — per Rashi; nevertheless Rosh in name most poskim Ulla only on ring.

(ב) And some say opposite — Rif and Rambam explained also on needle. Difficult on Rambam who wrote ring with seal on man permitted l'chatchila as s.t. and as explained in his words; here wrote pierced exempt; also difficult why not resolve needle in Gemara on difficulty "and this carrying" etc. Appears certainly needle reason not ornament, both not ornaments, even for woman they said "on Shabbat for what is it seen" etc. and Tosafot there; as Rosh challenged Rif here; rather reason: this is way of carrying, this is not; must divide thus, else difficult from "some say" and Tosafot difficulty "and if"; must say woman way of carrying thus, explicit Yerushalmi this distinction said there: R' Meir tailor with needle if went out exempt, here liable; there way of carrying thus etc. and Tosafot difficulty and answer: this compelled them Ulla also on needle, perforce man exempt pierced; those who challenged Ulla "and this carrying" not on mishnah but on his words: you divide man between needles because kele'achar yad carrying; precise Rambam language on ring: with seal is man's ornaments not woman's; without seal etc.; on needle: woman pierced liable, man exempt; man unpierced liable, woman exempt as her ornaments etc.; did not write on man reason because ornament; per Rashi and commentators pierced liable must be as Magen Avraham s.k. 19; appears Rashi like Rosh both exempt man; therefore explained only ring; Gemara difficulty and answer compelled as explained.
