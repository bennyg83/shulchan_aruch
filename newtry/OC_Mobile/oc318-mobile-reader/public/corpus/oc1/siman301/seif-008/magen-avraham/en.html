(א) In his garment — but in his hand they are liable in every case, man and woman, pierced and unpierced; and the gloss writer was not precise at all.

(ב) Pierced — in Beit Yosef there is a scribal error here, but in Shulchan Aruch it is written properly; and Bach wrote: nevertheless there is an opinion that a man is liable with pierced — see there, not required; and the general rule is per the time: if their custom is to carry out thus on weekdays and it is not an ornament, it is their way of carrying out and he is liable; if not their way of carrying out on weekdays — exempt but forbidden because not an ornament; they disagreed which machat is the way of carrying out in Talmudic times; if most of the world does not carry out thus, even if people of another place do, exempt — their opinion is batel to everyone, as in the Gemara daf 92.
