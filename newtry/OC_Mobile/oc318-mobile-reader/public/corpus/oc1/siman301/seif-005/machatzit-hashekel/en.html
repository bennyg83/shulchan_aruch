Se'if katan 5: It is permitted, etc. This requires examination: Does he not derive pleasure from it? At first glance, what is the question? He does not enjoy himself while running, only afterward when exercise makes him perspire. Magen Avraham must therefore hold that permission does not require pleasure during the running itself. From this difficulty concerning the Tosefta, Taz proves that one must enjoy the running at the time. Magen Avraham answers that the case must be, etc., as stated in siman 328: Food eaten by healthy people may be eaten for medicinal purposes. Thus, if he walks in the ordinary manner, even to warm himself or for healing, such walking is common to everyone and resembles ordinary food, which may be eaten medicinally. Running, however, is not everyone's ordinary practice and is therefore forbidden for healing.
