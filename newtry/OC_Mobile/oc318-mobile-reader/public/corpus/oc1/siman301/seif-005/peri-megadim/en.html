He may not go out — Taz expanded. Scribal error should read Rosh conclusion: man pierced or unpierced exempt, not way of carrying in garment; in hand liable as siman 252 seif 6; woman pierced liable as Tosafot, artisan by his craft sewing and mending garments, like artisan, way of carrying to stick pierced in garment liable; unpierced uses cloak keys and scarf, explained siman 303 seif 9 not burden but not her way with pierced in scarf; appears pierced even in scarf liable chatas, explained siman 303. Challenged Beit Yosef: when man took unpierced in hand exempt, answer: only reason stuck in garment exempt; examiner Beit Yosef intent: stuck in garment man exempt pierced and unpierced; third dispute: stam is Maharam Maimonides Pithei Teshuvah Hagahot Geonim; second Rif and Rambam, Mechaber omitted Rosh and Tur entirely, man both exempt, Rav Betzalel did not correct — somewhat difficult. Why wrote first opinion stam and say Rif Rambam when custom to rule Rif Rambam as two pillars of three as in introduction and Choshen Mishpat; Be'er HaGolah letters 90 and 100: first Rif Rambam, second Rashi, did not cite Hagahot Geonim per Beit Yosef text, requires study.
