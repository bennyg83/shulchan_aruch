The cures are not attributed to the physician, but to the patient's favorable fortune. Although, for purposes of establishing an individual amulet, we do not attribute the cures to the patient, the distinction was explained above in se'if katan 57 in Rashi's name.
Later authorities ask why the same patient should be forbidden to use it. If the cures established the physician, it may be worn by everyone; if they depended upon the patient's fortune, that patient should at least be permitted to wear it. It appears that Magen Avraham agrees he is permitted and that words were omitted in printing. The text should read: If the physician subsequently lost his established status, even that patient is forbidden, because perhaps the prior cures depended upon the physician and his expertise is now gone.
