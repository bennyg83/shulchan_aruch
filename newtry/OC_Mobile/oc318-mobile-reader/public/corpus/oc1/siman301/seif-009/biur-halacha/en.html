And if he went out, he is liable. See Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham, with whom he agreed, that nowadays, when it is the way of men on weekdays to go out with a ring that has no seal, it is included in the category of an ornament, and he is exempt, though it is forbidden. See below in siman 303, seif 18, where he copied the view of the Ran regarding this as, there is one who says, because he himself rules like Rabbeinu Yerucham above.
