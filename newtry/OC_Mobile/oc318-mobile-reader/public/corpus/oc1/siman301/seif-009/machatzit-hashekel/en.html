Se'if katan 9: He is liable, etc. He incurs kares; if he was warned beforehand, he incurs stoning. Magen Avraham omitted this because courts today do not adjudicate capital cases.
Exempt means exempt even from a sin-offering, because the Torah prohibited intentional creative labor, and he had no intention whatsoever to perform labor.
Regarding the law of a public domain, see siman 345 concerning whether a true public domain exists today.
