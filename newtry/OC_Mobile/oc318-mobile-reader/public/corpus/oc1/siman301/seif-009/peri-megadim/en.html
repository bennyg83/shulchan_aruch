And if Taz are Rashi's words Shabbat 11b s.v. in something — Rashi holds man and woman equal with pierced in garment liable chatas seif 8 and Taz 5; Rashi Shabbat 62a beginning and reverse; tailor possibly worse than other man, only when stuck in garment to proclaim he is tailor, Rashi wrote there even craftsman implies all the more other man; per Rashi other man liable pierced like woman, requires study; Magen Avraham from this. Rambam Pithei Teshuvah Hagahot 21 ruled R' Meir: halacha 5 man pierced (in garment) exempt, even tailor exempt, unlike Mechaber seif 8 first opinion stam; say as Magen Avraham note 19 deals stuck where no way to stick, if so difficult Magen Avraham ruled R' Meir against R' Yehudah who establishes Mishnah and baraita on R' Meir; should say Shabbat and stuck where customary to stick in garment, like other man liable; for Rambam fine as Magen Avraham; Mechaber seif 8 and here credited document to two holders: perhaps establishment on hand or Shabbat craftsman not precise, requires study.
