50) [Se'if 9] If he went out, he is liable. A woman sometimes gives the ring to her husband to take to an artisan, and he holds it in his hand until he arrives. Thus, even during the week this is its normal mode of carrying, and he is liable, since it is not an ornament for a man. Magen Avraham 16; Olas Shabbos 14. All the more is carrying it directly in his hand its normal mode. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 16; Olas Shabbos, ibid. See paragraph 46 above.
51) There: If he went out, he is liable. See below, siman 303:18, which states that nowadays, when men customarily go out wearing a ring without a seal, it is an ornament for them and is permitted; see there. See paragraph 48 above, where we wrote that everything depends upon contemporary practice. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 16, therefore writes that nowadays one should at least not protest against men who do so, for the reasons mentioned in siman 303:18. All the more so since Rabbeinu Tam and Rambam permit a man to do so - meaning because he will not remove it and show it; see there. See below, paragraph 53.
