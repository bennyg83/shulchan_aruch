And it also heals, etc. It implies that if it does not heal, but one does it so that he will not be injured, it is forbidden, just as in seif 22 it is forbidden to wrap a thread or cord around a wound; so wrote the Bach and Magen Avraham. This requires study, for it does not seem so from Shabbos 65, which asks: If you say that anything hard is beneficial for her, let her make for herself an earthenware shard, etc.; examine this. So too it implies from Rabbeinu Yerucham, page 79. There is also proof from seif 13, where she ties it so that she should not suffer from the blood. In truth, above as well regarding a thread or cord it requires study, for one may say that the reason is that it does not save her so much from pain; but a band of rags is permitted so that the garments should not scratch, and the like. What was said above regarding a band for the plaster was stated as a novelty, that even over the plaster it is permitted. Although the Bach, seif katan 40, did not explain this way, I have written what appears in my humble opinion. So too it implies in Shiltei Giborim, page 120a, and also in Maharil in the name of the Rash. See what I wrote at the end of this siman.
