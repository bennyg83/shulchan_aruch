(א) And it also heals — from Tur's language; implies in something that does not heal but only prevents his foot being struck, forbidden; and he goes to his reason in seif 22 see there; but in Shulchan Aruch HaRav he brought in name of several poskim that even if it does not heal but only protects from pain, also permitted to go out; and so Tosafot Shantz in name of Maharshak; see above seif 22 what we wrote in name of Gra that Rambam's view is likewise; therefore if he cut his finger on Shabbat and wraps a garment piece so it not scratch his clothes, permitted to go out; nevertheless better to wrap on that place with some rag, for on this Peri Megadim sides that according to all permitted to go out, not considered and batel to wound; nevertheless in every case must wash first blood on his finger so it not stain.

(ב) Permitted to go out — specifically when he tied the coin to his foot piece from before Shabbat with unimportant rag, not considered and batel; but if tied with something important, forbidden to go out on Shabbat, as above seif 22.
