108) And it also heals. Tur implies that an item that merely prevents his foot from being struck is forbidden, consistent with his view in se'if 22. Eliyah Rabbah cites several poskim permitting even an item that only protects from pain; Tosefes Shabbos cites Maharshal similarly. As noted above, se'if 22, Gra understands Rambam this way. Thus, if one cuts his finger on Shabbos, he may wrap cloth around it to prevent his clothing from scraping it. It is preferable to use an insignificant rag, which Peri Megadim says all views permit because it is subordinate to the wound. In every case he must first wash away the blood so the cloth will not be dyed.
109) One may go out. Only if he tied the coin to the sole of his foot before Shabbos with an insignificant rag that is subordinate to it. If he used a significant material, he may not go out on Shabbos, as above in se'if 22.
