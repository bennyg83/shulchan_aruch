Seif 15. The amputee. See the Maggid Mishneh, chapter 3 of Hilchos Shevisas Asor, halacha 7: it is permitted to go out with a cork sandal, etc.; and there is one who forbids going out with them into the public domain, whether on Shabbos or on Yom Kippur, because of the prohibition of carrying, etc.; and this is not the main view. See Machatzit HaShekel, siman 614, seif katan 2.
