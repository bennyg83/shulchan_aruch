An amputee may not go out [to the public domain wearing] his prosthetic, i.e., a form of a leg was made and a small hollow was carved into it in which he could insert the stump of his leg. This prosthetic leg is not made for the sake of walking, because regardless, he requires a cane [in order to be able to walk]. Rather, his intent is that he is not seen missing a leg, merely as lame in his leg. Therefore, since [the prosthetic leg] is not worn to enable him to walk, (it is considered as a burden) and it is forbidden for him to go out wearing it.
