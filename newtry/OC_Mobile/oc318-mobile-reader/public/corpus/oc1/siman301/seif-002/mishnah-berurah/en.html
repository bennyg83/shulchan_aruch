6) And likewise to see. This means that anyone may run in order to see something from which he derives pleasure.
7) It is likewise permitted to take a walk. Even if his intent is to exercise and warm himself for health purposes, it is permitted because it is not evident that he is doing so as a remedy. It is forbidden, however, to run in order to warm himself as a remedy, because his purpose is evident and this is prohibited because of grinding medicinal ingredients. Some authorities are stringent even regarding walking when his intent is therapeutic exercise.
