Ibid., seif 25: One may not go out with an amulet that is not proven effective, but if it is proven effective, one may go out with it. Note: The apparent difficulty from Rambam’s Commentary to the Mishnah, chapter 8 of Yoma, where he writes that one may violate Shabbos only for a treatment that operates naturally, is no difficulty. Regarding going out with an amulet on Shabbos, it is permitted because the amulet is considered an ornament. Thus, an amulet written by an expert is regarded as an ornament for the person wearing it, whether or not it actually heals him, and for this reason he may go out with it. Administering something to a sick person, however, or a similar act, would constitute an actual violation of Shabbos if it is not a genuine treatment. Rambam’s words are therefore certainly correct: anything that is not a natural treatment does not justify violating Shabbos. This is clear. Examine carefully.
