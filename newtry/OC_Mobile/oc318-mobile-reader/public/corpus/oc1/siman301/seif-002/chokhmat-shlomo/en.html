There seif 25 one may not go out in non-expert amulet, if expert may — Mishna Berurah: difficulty from Rambam commentary Mishnayot chapter 8 Yoma wrote only Shabbat concern is natural healing; for going out since ornament permitted; expert amulet for bearer is ornament whether heals or not, therefore permitted; but to feed etc. if not healing, actual Shabbat desecration; Rambam correct all not natural healing do not desecrate Shabbat, simple, requires study.
