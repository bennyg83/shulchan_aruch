(א) And their running is permitted — it implies even l'chatchila, unlike Bach who was pressed.

(ב) And likewise to see — meaning even though it is forbidden to see portraits, as written in siman 307 seif 175; nevertheless if the seeing is pleasure for him, it is permitted to see; and Bach explained that it is permitted to run in order to see something pleasurable.

(ג) Permitted to stroll — even if his intent is to exercise and warm himself thereby; but forbidden to run in order to warm himself [Beit Yosef in the name of Tosefta]; and requires study, for he enjoys it — it should refer to one who does so for healing, as written in siman 328 seif 42.
