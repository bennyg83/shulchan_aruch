Their running is permitted. This implies that it is permitted even initially, unlike Bach's forced interpretation.
Likewise, to see. Although looking at images is forbidden, as stated in siman 307:15, if seeing them gives him pleasure, he may look. Bach explains that one may run in order to see something pleasurable.
It is permitted to stroll. This applies even if his intent is to exercise and warm himself by doing so. It is forbidden, however, to run in order to warm himself. Beis Yosef in Tosefta's name. This requires examination, for he derives pleasure from it. One must say that it concerns doing so for medical treatment, as stated in siman 328:42.
