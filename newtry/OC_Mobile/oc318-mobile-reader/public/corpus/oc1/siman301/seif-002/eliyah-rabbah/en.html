Those who delight themselves, etc. Meaning youths who play together, Agudah. This is only after the fact, where they are accustomed to this, but initially one should not accustom them to it, Bach. On Simchas Torah it is permitted to dance, and likewise at the joy of a wedding, in honor of the groom and bride, as in the Talmud in the fifth chapter of Beitzah.
And similarly to see, etc. Meaning to run in order to see.
