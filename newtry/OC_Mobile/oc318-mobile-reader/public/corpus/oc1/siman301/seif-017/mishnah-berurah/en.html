(Seg) Without a stick is allowed - Dachion Dainu can walk at all without a cane, like a locket:

(brace) But if possible, etc. - as well as an old man who walks in his house without a cane and when he goes outside he leans on his cane because his strength is weak and he is not taken but to hold himself even though his body is moving, it is forbidden to fade the cane with him because his bear walks without a cane, but if the old man is so supplicated for him to walk without a cane at all, it is permissible:

(sa) and it was not taken, etc. - And when a person walks in a place where there is fear that he will fall because it has rained and the place is sloping, or when he walks in the winter on icy water [Shak Korin 17] and is afraid that he will fall, it is permitted according to the law to go out with a dead stick to the hip [22 16] and Eliahu Raba wrote about it that his words are obligatory and also among the latter I saw that they were of the opinion that it should not be permitted only where there is mixing:

(Su) It is forbidden - and even a stick of glory carried in honor is forbidden. A daff, which is a piece of jewelry, is not allowed to carry a piece of jewelry in one's hand, and so what the important used to go out with a stick in their hand is prohibited on Shabbat except for mixing, but during mixing it is allowed if it is carried for honor or if it is needed a little but without any need at all.

"We would have been in the midst of our sins, if we could walk without a stick."
