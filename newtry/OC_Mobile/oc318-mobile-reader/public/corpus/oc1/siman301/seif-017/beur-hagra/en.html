Se'if 17: One who is lame. This is from the law in se'if 15. Even according to Rabbeinu Porat's explanation, it is proven from the benches in se'if 16, and likewise from one amputated in both legs who walks, etc., that he certainly needs a cane. See Tosafos in Shabbos and Yoma.
