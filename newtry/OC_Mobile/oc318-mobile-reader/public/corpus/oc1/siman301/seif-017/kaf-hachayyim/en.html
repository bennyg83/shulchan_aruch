17) There: Lest he come to wring it out. Although wetting the garment should independently be forbidden because soaking it constitutes laundering it, Tosafos, Shabbos 111, answers that this principle applies only when the garment contains dirt. One may alternatively say that the principle does not apply to something whose dirty state is not visible. I found, however, that Sefer Yereim, page 39, and Piskei Recanati 113 answer that the case concerns leather garments. This requires examination. Eliyah Rabbah 8. See below, siman 302:9.
