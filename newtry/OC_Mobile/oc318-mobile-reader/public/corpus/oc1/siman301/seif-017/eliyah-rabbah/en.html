Where there is no wound, etc. I found that the Yerushalmi proves that the halacha follows Rav Huna. Therefore it seems to me that a God-fearing person should not go out with either one alone, Malbushei Yom Tov. So too the Bach ruled, and this is primary, for I found in Sefer Yere’im, page 48, that the Sages did not decide whose view the halacha follows; therefore both are forbidden.
