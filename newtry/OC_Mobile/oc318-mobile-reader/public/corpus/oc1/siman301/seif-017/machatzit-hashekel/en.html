(א) (s.k. 17) Or forms — Rama per Kelim; Rama: seal protruding; Yoreh Deah 141:1 protruding human form forbidden leave weekdays — Mechaber omitted; there complete human form; face or body alone permitted; animal forms permitted. Magen Avraham brought Rambam to prove forbidden even on pad.

(ב) Keys etc. — sheath has law of eyeglass case if silver; if eyeglasses inside, Magen Avraham like silver keys dispute Rama; same sheath for knife — if no knife depends dispute; if knife inside all forbid like eyeglasses in case — Rama all forbid, do not say eyeglasses batel to case; eyeglasses primary — make case for eyeglasses not eyeglasses for case; knife sheath same, forbidden all.
