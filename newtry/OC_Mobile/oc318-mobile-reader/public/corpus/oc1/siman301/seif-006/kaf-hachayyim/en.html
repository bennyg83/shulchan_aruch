26) [Se'if 6] One who goes to guard his fruit, etc. Guarding one's property is also a partial mitzvah. Magen Avraham 8; Tosefes Shabbos 11. It is not a complete mitzvah, however, so the Sages did not permit his return because of his initial journey. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 1.
27) There: He may cross it, etc. This is provided that he does not extend his hands from beneath the hem of his robe, as stated above in se'if 4. Gemara.
28) There: But not on his return. The rationale of not discouraging them in the future is inapplicable here. Beis Yosef; Taz 3. Taz gives another reason: Since a person is anxious about his property, he will not refrain from going there in the future, unlike when performing a mitzvah.
