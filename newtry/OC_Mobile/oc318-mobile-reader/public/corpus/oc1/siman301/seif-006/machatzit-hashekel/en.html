(Seif katan 6) And it is forbidden, etc. So too it is stated in the Gemara, page 113b.
And that which he wrote in siman 613. Meaning that the Rama wrote regarding Yom Kippur: Wherever it is permitted to pass through water, such as to receive the face of one’s rebbe, even if he has another route by which he could go around, it is permitted to pass through, for minimizing walking is preferable.
