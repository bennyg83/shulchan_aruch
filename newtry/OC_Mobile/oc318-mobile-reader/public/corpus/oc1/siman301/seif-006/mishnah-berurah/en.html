14) To guard his produce. Guarding one's property is also somewhat of a mitzvah. Nevertheless, they did not permit him to cross on the return journey, because it is not so fully a mitzvah. The concern that he may be discouraged in the future does not apply: it does not matter to us if he refrains from taking pity on his property, and in any event he will certainly not refrain from guarding it, because a person is anxious about his property.
15) To pass through the water. Provided he does not take his hands out from beneath his clothing, etc., as stated above in se'if 4.
