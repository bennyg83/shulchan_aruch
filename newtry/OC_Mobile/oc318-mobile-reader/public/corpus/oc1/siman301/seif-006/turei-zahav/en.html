But not on his return. Here there is no concern that he will refrain in the future, because that concern applies specifically to the case of carrying mentioned there. Guarding one's property is not entirely an optional matter. This is the reason given by the Beit Yosef. In my humble opinion, however, here, because a person is anxious about his property, he will not refrain from going there in the future. This differs from performing a mitzvah. Yet since we find here that, in connection with a mitzvah, the Sages were concerned that people might refrain in the future, it is difficult why Ulla did not count this among the three matters whose conclusion the Sages permitted because of their beginning, in the first chapter of Beitzah, folio 11. This requires further study.
