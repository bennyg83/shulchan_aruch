Se'if 44: And he removes. This is the language of the Tur. But in siman 334 he wrote that the Rambam distinguished the matter, since he omitted it, and he holds that this is the conclusion of Rabbi Yosi's words, but the first Tanna does not hold so. Although in the baraisa there Rabbi Meir holds so, the first Tanna of the mishnah is not Rabbi Meir, but a third view. His words are astonishing, for at the beginning of the last chapter of Eruvin we say: and from where do we know that it is Rabbi Meir, etc.?
