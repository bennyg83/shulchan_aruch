Because he needs. This is the language of the Tur. It requires study, for we rule that Shabbos is not the time for tefillin, and the Rambam omitted this reason; he wrote only the latter clause, that one who goes out with tefillin is exempt, and some teach, etc. After analysis it appears that although there it says, 'Do not say this according to the one who says,' etc., and we rule that Shabbos is not the time for tefillin, that statement there is according to Rabbi Yose HaGelili in Menachos 36b, who derives it from 'as a sign.' But we rule like Rabbi Akiva, who derives it from 'and you shall guard,' as stated there; he holds that it is a halachah but we do not instruct so, and there is no prohibition against putting them on on Shabbos and Yom Tov, as Tosafos write in Niddah 51b, s.v. 'and for the people of the West,' and in Yom Tov 15a, s.v. 'this is what he said,' etc., and as the Beis Yosef writes in siman 29, with the Magen Avraham there. The Gemara could have said that all of it follows the opinion that Shabbos is not the time for tefillin, but it teaches us that even according to the one who says it is the time for tefillin, the law is so. The Rambam, who did not write this, follows his own view that night is not the time for tefillin, for he wrote that one transgresses a negative commandment. The Tur follows his own view that it is the time for tefillin, as written in simanim 29 and 30. And that which is said in Menachos there, 'doubtful darkness,' etc., 'but certainly,' etc., applies even according to Rabbi Akiva, as stated there, namely when one puts them on for the sake of the mitzvah of tefillin, as the Magen Avraham writes in siman 29; see Tosafos there.

On which there is not, etc. The Rif's reading is like Chiyya bar Rav, but according to our reading perhaps it is merely a rejection. The Rif's view is difficult, and the Baal HaMaor already challenged it. It is also explicit in the Yerushalmi, chapter 6, halachah 2: Rabbi Yochanan said, 'He puts it on the one that has a wound.' Shimon bar Ba was serving before Rabbi Yochanan and handed him his sandal, etc. Rabbi Yochanan said to him, 'Babylonian, do not do this, for the earlier ones would not do so,' etc. 'This means he puts it on the one that has a wound.' He said to him, 'Yes, Babylonian, you have stood upon it.' This is Rabbi Shimon bar Abba of our Gemara. Thus Rabbi Yochanan holds like Rav Huna.
