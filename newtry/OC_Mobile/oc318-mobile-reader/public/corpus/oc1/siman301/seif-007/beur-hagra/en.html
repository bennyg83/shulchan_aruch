(א) s.z. whoever goes out with non-ornament — see Rashi beginning ch. 6 and Gemara there 59b what they dispute etc.; there "there is on it" etc.; there 62a and 63a in mishnah.

(ב) And not way of garment — 60a in mishnah: even though not ornaments for Rabbanan there 63a; see Rashi mishnah 60a and Gemara 61a why way etc.

(ג) And they took out in manner — 62a and mishnah 92a.

(ד) And every ornament — in garments 41b and Yoma 78b; ornament per his explanation in tefillin frontlet and sanbunin and city of gold and chains lest turn — requires study on city of gold; see Gemara 59b.

(ה) That heals — as explained in Peri Megadim on large shoe and Yoma on stump and sandal in water.

(ו) And woman may not go out — see Rashi beginning ch. 6 and Gemara 59b dispute etc. kilah etc.; there 62a and 65a; and said woman specifically as Yerushalmi; Rosh brought because women are arrogant etc. and man not arrogant permitted, explained s.t.

(ז) And not vessels — learned from above no dispute R' Eliezer and sages except if ornaments.

(ח) Because must remove — Tur language; requires study: we hold Shabbat not time for tefillin; Rambam omitted this reason, wrote only end one who goes with tefillin exempt; some versions etc. After consideration appears: even though there "do not say per view" etc. we hold not time for tefillin there said per R' Yehudah HaNasi Menachot 36b derives from "preserved"; but we hold R' Akiva derives from "guarded" as said law and not taught thus, no prohibition lay them Shabbat and Yom Tov as Tosafot Niddah 91b and Yom Tov 15a and Beit Yosef siman 29 and Magen Avraham there; could say all per view not time but teaches even per view time for tefillin likewise; Rambam omitted this follows his view night not time, wrote transgresses negative command; Tur follows his view time for tefillin as siman 29 and siman 80; what we say Menachot there doubt darkness etc. surely also R' Akiva as said there when laying for mitzvah of tefillin as Magen Avraham siman 29 and Tosafot there.

(ט) Lest they laugh — as teachers of Rashi there in mishnah see there.

(י) That has no etc. — Rif girsa Chiyya bar Rav; but our girsa "lest it" mere question; Rif view astounding, Razah challenged, explicit Yerushalmi ch. 6 halacha 2: R' Yonatan said on foot with wound he places; Shimon bar Ba served R' Yehuda, handed him sandal etc.; R' Yehuda Babylonian: do not do so, ancients did not; D.A. on foot with wound he places; said no, Babylonian custom on it; he is R' Shimon bar Aba of our Gemara; thus R' Yonatan holds like Rav Huna.
