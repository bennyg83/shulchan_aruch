With the one that has no wound — for then they will not mock him; and this is Chiyya bar Rav. But in Hagahot Rashi Lublin he brings a responsum of Maharam, who ruled like Rabbenu Chananel that one goes out with the one that has the wound; and Mateh Moshe of blessed memory wrote that therefore one should be stringent not to go out at all with one shoe, whether there is a wound or not.
