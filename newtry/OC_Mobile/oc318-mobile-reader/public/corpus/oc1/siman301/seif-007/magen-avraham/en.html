(א) Liable — know that wherever it says "liable," if done intentionally he is liable for kares, and if unintentionally — meaning he forgot it was Shabbat, or knew Shabbat but did not know this melacha is forbidden — he is liable for a chatas [and see Shulchan Aruch HaRav 4667 in the gloss]; but if he did melacha without intent, such as forgot an item with him and went out to the public domain, he is exempt; and the law of public domain — see siman 345.

(ב) With a sword — even if girded on his waist he is liable, for this is the way of carrying out on weekdays — so in the Gemara.

(ג) With a helmet — it is an iron hat, and boots are iron leggings for war.

(ד) Because he must remove them — Beit Yosef challenged: it should be sufficient that Shabbat is not time for tefillin — see there; and it appears to me it comes to teach regarding a written amulet with divine names: even if expert, forbidden on Shabbat unless covered with hide — and so end of siman 308; and see what is written in siman 308 seif 14 for the practical difference.

(ה) With a shoe — the one who wrote glosses did not look at siman 303 seif 13.
