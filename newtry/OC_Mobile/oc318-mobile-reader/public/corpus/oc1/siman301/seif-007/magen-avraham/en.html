Liable. Know that wherever 'liable' is stated, if one acted intentionally he is liable to kares. If he acted unwittingly - meaning he forgot it was Shabbos, or knew it was Shabbos but did not know this labor was forbidden - he must bring a sin-offering. See the gloss to siman 334:27. If, however, he performed labor unintentionally, such as when he forgot that an object was with him and went out with it into the public domain, he is exempt. Regarding the law of a public domain, see siman 345.
With a sword. Even when it is girded around his waist, he is liable, because this is its ordinary manner of being carried during the week. The Gemara also implies this.
