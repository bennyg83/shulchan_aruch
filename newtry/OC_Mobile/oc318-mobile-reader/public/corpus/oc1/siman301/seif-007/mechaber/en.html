One who utilizes something that is not a piece of jewellery, and is not 'in the normal way its worn', and he goes along the path where people walk to take it out is liable. And any ornament that is insecurely fastened and could easily fall off by itself, one is forbidden from going out with it and if he does go out, he is exempt. A woman may not go out with ornaments that are able to be removed and (easily) displayed. RAMA: And see Siman 303 Seif 18 if it is forbidden in the courtyard or in the house. For this reason a man may not go out with a fencer, a bow, a shield, a club, a spear or with items that are not ornaments, and if he does go out (with these) he is liable to bring a Chatat (offering). And not with armor, or a helmet, or boots and if he went out (with these) he is exempt, because they are usually worn. One may not go out with Tefillin because he has to take them off when he goes to the bathroom. A minor may not go out with big shoes for he might fall and have to carry it with him. However, (a minor) may go out with a big robe. He may not go out with one shoe if he doesn't have an injury to his legs because he might hurt himself that way and have to carry it (the one shoe). However if he has an injury to his legs/feet, he may may go out with (a shoe on) the one that is not injured.
