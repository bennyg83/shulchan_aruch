Magen Avraham, seif katan 27: "that it should be like an ornament." This is difficult, for he himself writes in seif katan 14 that carrying it in her hand creates liability even for a woman when it is not perforated, although it is her ornament. If so, why did Magen Avraham here seek only a rabbinic prohibition? There should be actual liability. There is no difficulty. An ornament designed to be worn is not considered an ornament at all when it is carried in the hand. A cane is different, because its normal manner as an ornament is precisely to be carried in the hand.
