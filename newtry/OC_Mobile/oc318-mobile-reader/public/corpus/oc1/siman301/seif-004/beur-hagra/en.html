(ב) And provided etc. so that etc. — Rosh and Ran there challenged from above s.b. and forbidden to pass through etc.; answer: since they permitted only as way it is worn as said below "provided that not" etc. distinguishes the matter and not etc.; Ran brought proof from chapter 2 Yoma 18a: Rav Yosef said decree lest squeeze, challenged etc. Niddah etc. and immerses in her garments — different there since not permitted except via garment, she remembers from their words not burden prohibition per Rashi but recognition; therefore "provided" etc. — not meaning change from their wearing manner, rather they must walk as they wear, but change from way of passing in water they are not accustomed to walk; therefore per Rosh reason "lest remove" etc. is recognition; if burden why distinguish, since there too needs thus; but now we say recognition, not way of passing in water in garments where garments do not reach water as said sometimes attached etc. occasionally accidental no recognition; Tur learned from Rosh who said since they did not permit etc. implies not permitted because of water, unlike Rashi who would have said since forbidden only etc. and they did not permit only because of fence they did not permit; as Yom Tov there "since not permitted" etc. not like Beit Yosef siman 613 and they hold not burden, explained siman 31 see there.
