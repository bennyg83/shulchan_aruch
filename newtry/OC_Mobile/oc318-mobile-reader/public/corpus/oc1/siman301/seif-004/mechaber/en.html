If one is walking to perform a mitzvah, such as to greet his Rabbi or greet someone greater than he in wisdom, he may pass, provided he does so differently, such as he does not take his hand out of his robe in order to remember and in order to avoid the melachah of squeezing . And it is forbidden to travel in his sandles, so that he will not be able to tighten or tie them well, as we worry it might fall and he may come to bring it; but with shoes it is permitted. {Rama: [and see Siman 613 Seif 5 and the Rama in Seif 8]}
