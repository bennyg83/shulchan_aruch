30) There: He is liable. Know that wherever the word 'liable' appears, one who acts intentionally is liable to kares; if he was formally warned, he is liable to stoning. Machatzis HaShekel. If he acts unwittingly - meaning that he forgot it was Shabbos, or knew it was Shabbos but did not know this labor was forbidden - he must bring a sin-offering. If, however, the labor was wholly unintended, such as when he forgot that an object was with him and went out with it into the public domain, he is exempt. Magen Avraham 9. 'Exempt' means even from a sin-offering, because the Torah forbade calculated labor and he had no intention to perform labor at all. Machatzis HaShekel. Regarding the law of the public domain, see below, siman 345:7, and our comments there, with God's help.
