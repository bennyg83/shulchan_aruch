115) With the tallis around his neck. This does not contradict the preceding se'if, which concerns places where people ordinarily wear it this way at home on weekdays; it is therefore clothing even folded around the neck, like the scarf permitted below in se'if 34. Magen Avraham and Tosefes Shabbos. Gra instead explains that the two upper sides are gathered around the neck while the lower rear edge hangs down. Rama adds that placing the right side over the left shoulder is also permitted because it is an ornamental manner of wearing. This applies even in our countries.
