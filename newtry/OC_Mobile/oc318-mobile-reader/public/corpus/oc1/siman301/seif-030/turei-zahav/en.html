Permitted to go out in the public domain, etc. — this is from the Rashba's responsum Beit Yosef brings below this siman: they carry tallit under cloak around neck in public domain, and Ari liable for folded tallit, etc. Answer: all Spain went out thus modestly even in courtyards, manner of clothing for them, like those who go out with sudar on shoulders, not only carriers but everyone, carriers' custom being thus; folded tallit — R' Yochanan, Rashi: after placing on head he raised hem on shoulders, not manner of clothing — end quote. Rashba's intent: he permitted folded tallit placed around neck when carrying to synagogue, then removes coat and wraps — permitted as manner of wearing there; R' Yochanan forbade folded tallit only wearing not wrapping full width but raising hem after wearing lest soil — not manner of clothing at all. Thus what Shulchan Aruch writes: permitted in public domain — tallit folded around neck; difficulty why previous seif says permitted to wrap — implies folded part raised from below permitted because shoulders and body covered, here permitted even around neck alone. Our mitzvah: even on shoulders only around neck — not Shulchan Aruch's intent, else main novelty would be not on head, not mentioned; and no novelty that head not covered, for daily sometimes head uncovered as in Hilchot Tzitzit — rather intent as above: folded at end permitted since shoulders and body covered; difficulty later seif permits even around neck if folded; also did not understand Rama's end: right side on left shoulder — not in Rashba's source; Shulchan Aruch wrote not this-and-that: first permit folding down with body covered, then even folded around neck alone; seems right to rely to be lenient, for Beit Yosef in Shelah in R' Chananel's name: prohibition only when removing entirely, folding, carrying on shoulder — end quote.
