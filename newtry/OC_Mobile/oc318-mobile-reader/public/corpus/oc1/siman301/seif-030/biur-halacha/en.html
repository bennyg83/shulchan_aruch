It is permitted to go out in the public domain, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, where we cited Magen Avraham and Tosefes Shabbos from Rashba's responsum. It is explicit there that this concerns countries where people customarily go out this way. Nehar Shalom and Shulchan Atzei Shittim write likewise. Taz introduces a novel ruling in se'if katan 34: According to Rashi and Tur, taking a folded tallis and wrapping it around one's neck is permitted everywhere. He explains that this is the permitted manner of wearing the folded scarf over one's shoulder in se'if 34, and the manner in which Shulchan Aruch permits a tallis here. In his view this is more favorable than se'if katan 29; see his reason. The later authorities did not cite this practically, implying that they reject his distinction. Taz himself writes at the end of se'if katan 24 that Rambam forbids even this. In practice one should be stringent in accordance with those words. He thereby retracts his statement at the end of se'if katan 19 that one may rely on the lenient view, as Eliyah Rabbah already observes. I therefore did not include his explanation of this se'if.
With a tallis around the neck. Magen Avraham implies that this applies even to a mitzvah tallis. Pri Megadim raises the concern that the tzitzis should be forbidden. Even if this is a manner of wearing, the person is not obligated in tzitzis through this manner of walking, since the tallis is not presently covering his body; the tzitzis should therefore be a burden. One may answer that tzitzis adorn the garment. This resembles nighttime, when one may wear the tallis although not then obligated in tzitzis, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 13. It does not resemble se'if 38, where he intends to complete the threads; see there.
