12) There: Likewise, to see anything, etc. This means that one may also run or leap in order to see something from which people derive pleasure. Bach. Olas Shabbos 3 explains likewise. Taz 1, however, writes that one may not run in order to see it; the ruling permits only the viewing itself, meaning that one may see on Shabbos something he enjoys. Magen Avraham 4 explains similarly: Although one may not look at images, as stated in siman 307:15, he may look if the viewing gives him pleasure. Eliyah Rabbah 6, however, explains like Bach. Chemed Moshe 2 and Maamar Mordechai write likewise, and this appears to be the view of the later authorities.
