If he went out, he is exempt. Although carrying it displays his trade, he did not carry it in the ordinary manner. Ramban; Gemara. This concerns inserting it into his garment at a place where it is never ordinarily carried, solely to show that he is an artisan. If it is a place where people sometimes insert it during the week, he is liable, as stated in se'if 8 and siman 303:9. Tosafos 11b. Olas Shabbos's words are thereby refuted.
