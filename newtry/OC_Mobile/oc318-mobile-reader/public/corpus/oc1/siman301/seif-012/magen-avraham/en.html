And if he went out he is exempt — even though it is his craft's way, since he did not carry out in carriers' way [Ramban, Gemara]; and it deals with stuck in his garment where there is no way of carrying out at all, only to show he is a artisan; but where the custom is to stick in on weekdays for intervals, he is liable, as Mishnah Berurah and siman 303 seif 9 (Tosafos daf 11b); and on this the gloss writer's words are void.
