Made to protect from the sun. Se'if 40 likewise concerns such a hat; otherwise the prohibition of forming a tent would not apply there. Rif and the other Rishonim state this explicitly. This se'if teaches that even according to the prohibitive view, whose concern is only that the hat may fall and he may carry it, the prohibition applies only to such a hat, for the reason stated in Mishnah Berurah in Beis Yosef's and the later authorities' names.
