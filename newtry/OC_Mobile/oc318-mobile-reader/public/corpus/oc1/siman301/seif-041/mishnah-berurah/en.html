153) Made to protect, etc. An ordinary hat worn on the head does not create concern that it will be carried four cubits, since one will not go bareheaded. Their outer hat, however, was worn over a small hat. Likewise, when our hats are worn over a small hat in the public domain, they must fit securely lest they fall and be carried. The same applies to a broad soft hat where the custom is lenient: if a small hat is beneath it, one should not wear it in the public domain unless it is secure, because he will not become bareheaded if it falls.
