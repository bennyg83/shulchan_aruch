In complete privacy. See Mishnah Berurah for what we wrote in Tosafos' and Rosh's names. Tosafos' words appear in Kesubos, chapter Af Al Pi, page 60, s.v. memaachan. This is not an undisputed conclusion: Ran, first chapter of Beitzah, gives a different answer to Tosafos' question, and Shabbos 65 implies that Rav states a general rule in all cases, as Magen Avraham himself writes. Nevertheless, it appears that one may rely upon this distinction in practice. Several poskim - Rav Nissim Gaon, Razah, and Sefer HaTerumah - reject Rav's rule entirely. Although one cannot rely upon them against Rav's primary ruling, since Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Semag, and Ran all rule like Rav, as Beis Yosef writes, for a rabbinic prohibition one may at least combine the lenient authorities' view so as to prohibit because of appearances only before the public, not in one's courtyard.
