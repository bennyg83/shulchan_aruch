(א) And he may not spread them — implies specifically when soaked in water; if only a little water fell, permitted to spread, for with little water as if no laundering concern — somewhat in Sefer Yereim; and in siman 302 seif 9 some permit l'chatchila putting little water; but seif 47 implies somewhat forbidden, for even from sweat forbidden.

(ב) Even in inner chambers — ruled like Rav who challenged in Shach chapter Chavis; in siman 305 seif 11 ruled woman walks with bell in courtyard but not public domain, lest appear going to market; Gemara 65a: for Rav even courtyard forbidden; Beit Yosef siman 336 seif 79 in Tosafos: specifically where they suspect d'oraisa prohibition — forbidden in inner chambers — see there; if so practical difference; nevertheless difficult to emend thus Shabbat 65a; perhaps Tosafos hold Shabbat sugya rejected from Ketubot — see Ran first chapter Beitzah.
