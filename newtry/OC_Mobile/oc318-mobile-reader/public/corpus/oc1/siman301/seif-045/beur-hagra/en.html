Se'if 45: And they did not forbid. In the first chapter of Yom Tov, folio 9a, the Rashba explained that one who sees him setting the ladder against a dovecote, and this is what Beis Hillel mean by 'his dovecote,' etc., meaning one on a roof. Beis Shammai said 'from a dovecote,' etc., which implies that with the first dovecote, setting up the ladder is permitted for every dovecote.
