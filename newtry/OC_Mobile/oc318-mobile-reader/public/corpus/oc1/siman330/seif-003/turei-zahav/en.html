(א) She is called a woman in childbirth to desecrate. It appears to me that this is regarding matters that can be done here without delay — therefore they do not desecrate Shabbat for this until one of these occurs; but regarding calling a skilled midwife these time measures do not apply, for they are closer to birth than the time for the midwife's arrival; rather from when she feels somewhat, even in doubt, it is permitted to call her, as I explained from reasoning, and no proof is needed for this.

(ב) Or from when the blood drips, etc. In the Tur it is written first from when she sits on the birth-stool and the blood drips and flows down — and this is the view of the Rif; and it is difficult on the Rif, for he wrote as Abbaye acts, and Abbaye mentioned only sitting on the birth-stool, whereas Rav Huna mentioned afterward from when the blood flows down and argues on Abbaye and is more lenient than he; nevertheless for Abbaye we are not strict about the blood. And it appears to me that the Rif holds that those two signs — sitting on the birth-stool and friends carrying her — are not a clear measure to rely on, for many times a woman errs and sits on the birth-stool and the time to give birth has not yet arrived; and likewise friends carrying her — sometimes she has weakness though the time of birth has not arrived. But those signs are effective after the blood flows down, for then they are a good sign for birth; and Abbaye holds that both are required — blood and birth-stool — and so too the Rif holds.

(ג) Or from when her friends, etc. This is the view of Ramban as explained in Beit Yosef; and it is difficult on the Tur that he did not bring in the name of Ramban that which friends carry her, etc., only blood and birth-stool. And it appears to me that the Tur holds that friends carrying her is learned a fortiori from blood flowing, which is a sign closer to birth than blood alone.
