Once one of these is seen — And Rambam wrote in his composition the measure from when blood drips; and all wrote the reason of one who holds so is that dripping blood is before sitting on the birth-stool, as R' Chananel and Rif, and he ruled leniently in life-threatening danger; and the foundation of their view is that dripping blood is before; otherwise it should say until blood begins to drip; but one who examines Rambam's explanation of the mishnah will see clearly that he holds dripping blood is after sitting on the birth-stool, and without doubt so is his view also in his composition; and he ruled like R' Huna son of R' Yehoshua because he is later; and from Abbaye and Rava onward halacha is like the later authorities; and for practical law we hold as Shulchan Aruch.
