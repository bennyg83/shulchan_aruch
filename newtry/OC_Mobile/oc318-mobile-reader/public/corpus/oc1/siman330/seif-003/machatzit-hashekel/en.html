(א) (s.k. 3) They change, etc. And if she needs oil, etc. — for it is better, etc. — for if she brings in a vessel tied in her hair there is no addition of carrying — meaning the vessel, for the vessel is nullified relative to the oil.

(ב) And as stated in Shabbos daf 93b in the mishnah. And see what Magen Avraham wrote siman 328 s.k. 71; and when she places in her hair there is an additional prohibition of squeezing what she needs afterward to squeeze the oil from the hair — even though we rule there is no squeezing liability in hair, nevertheless a d'rabbanan prohibition exists as Rabbeinu Yonah wrote.
