3) There: A midwife is summoned for her, etc. This applies even when she is three parsaos away. If the patient cannot walk, she may be carried to the midwife, because possible danger to life overrides Shabbos. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 2. A chachamah is the woman who receives the child at birth, called a midwife in Hebrew. Rambam in his Commentary to the Mishnah.
