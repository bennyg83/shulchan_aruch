(1) [Seif 1] And they desecrate Shabbat for her, etc. — Therefore when she reaches the ninth month she should prepare everything from erev Shabbat so they not desecrate Shabbat. Sefer Chassidim siman 495. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. And they should be careful to kindle lamp for her on erev Shabbat with much oil sufficient until morning light. Ben Ish Chai parashat Tetzaveh section 14.
