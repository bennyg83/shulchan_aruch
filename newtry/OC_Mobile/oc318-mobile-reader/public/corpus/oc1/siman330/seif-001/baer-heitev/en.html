(א) And they desecrate. Therefore when she reaches the ninth month she should prepare everything from erev Shabbat so they not desecrate Shabbat (Sefer Chassidim siman 328 [495]).

(ב) And they kindle. And Tosafos wrote that here an expert is not required as is required for a sick person on Yom Kippur, for here the woman in childbirth can become endangered more through fear — she may fear that perhaps they are not doing properly what she needs — than a sick person is endangered from hunger.

(ג) Tied. And if she needs oil she should bring it in a vessel tied in her hair, for it is better to do carrying through a change than to add another prohibition of squeezing. Beit Yosef.
