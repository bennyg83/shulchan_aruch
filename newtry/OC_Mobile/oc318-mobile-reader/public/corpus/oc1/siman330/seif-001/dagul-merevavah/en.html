His words are somewhat confused and they are true: this is clearly proven from Ramban's words that even if known her months completed they do not desecrate Shabbat for the fetus even if she sat on birth-stool while mother alive — for even if months completed permitted to kill him, meaning cut limb by limb while head not emerged; but if mother died it is no longer her mother's thigh — perhaps even in doubt months completed they desecrate Shabbat for him.
