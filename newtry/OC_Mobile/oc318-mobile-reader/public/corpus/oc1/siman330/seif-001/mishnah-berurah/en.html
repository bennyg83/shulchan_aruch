(א) And they desecrate — Therefore it is fitting for a woman who reached the tenth month to prepare on every erev Shabbat all things needed for her, lest birth happen on Shabbat and she need to desecrate Shabbat (Sefer Chassidim siman 495).
(ב) From place to place — Even outside the techum.
(ג) Lamp — If it is night; and even though her friends know to do all things she needs, nevertheless the Sages established that a woman in childbirth is not settled in mind when she is in darkness; and old Tosafos at the end of Yoma wrote that therefore even if she says nothing and the skilled midwife also says nothing — they still kindle; and see in Biur Halacha.
(ד) Blind — Even though this reason does not apply — for otherwise she is in darkness — nevertheless she is settled by a lit lamp, for she says if I need anything my friend sees and does for me; and even though the lamp's kindling is not essentially for healing, nevertheless they desecrate, for the Sages established that settling a woman in childbirth's mind is a matter she can be endangered by otherwise.
(ה) They change — And even for one who says in siman 328 s.k. 4 that for pikuach nefesh a change is not required — here it is different because pain of childbirth is natural, and not one in a thousand dies from childbirth; therefore they were strict to change [Magen Avraham in the name of Maggid Mishneh, and so Meiri]; and all this when there is no delay for the sick person; but if it is not done quickly with a change as without a change, one must do without a change to hurry the matter with all his power [Ran's chiddushim in the name of R' Yehonatan and Hagahot Maimoniyot, and so explained in 328 several times].
(ו) To bring her a vessel — With oil and the like.
(ז) Tied in her hair — Not in the manner of carriers, for it is only shvus; and this is preferable to if she puts oil in hair and brings to her and squeezes the hair — for there is also prohibition of squeezing; and even though we rule no squeezing liability in hair, certainly forbidden because of shvus; but if sufficient for her from what she brings oil in hand — this is more preferable than all the above [as stated in gemara].
