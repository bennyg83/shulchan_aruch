(א) And they desecrate Shabbat for her. Therefore when she reaches the ninth month she should prepare everything from erev Shabbat so they not desecrate Shabbat (Sefer Chassidim siman 328).

(ב) Even if she is blind. Lest you say since she does not see it is forbidden — it teaches us she is settled in mind; she thought if there is anything her friend sees and does for her (gemara). And it is asked: they will say in her presence that they kindled the lamp and she will be settled — and it is possible that nevertheless she sees the appearance of the lamp; or she can test by showing them a finger or two if they know; nevertheless it requires study — why is the reason of settling her mind needed, for they need the lamp's kindling to see what she needs.

(ג) They change. And even for one who says in siman 328 s.k. 12 that a change is not required — here it is different because the pain of a woman in childbirth is natural, and not one in a thousand dies from childbirth; therefore they were strict to change (nevertheless). And if she needs oil she should not place oil in her hair and come to her and squeeze it; rather she should bring it in a vessel tied in her hair, for it is better to do carrying through a change without adding another prohibition of squeezing (Beit Yosef). And see siman 328.
