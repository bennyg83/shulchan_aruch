Magen Avraham s.k. 10 sometimes can live, etc. — meaning: and therefore also if uprooted one can say it is only minority that lives; and whether uprooted or not majority fetus dies first; nevertheless they tear lest they find fetus alive — in pikuach nefesh we do not follow majority; therefore he afterward wrote not like Semag who wrote if uprooted inherits and bequeaths — from poskim it implies no distinction; and even if uprooted not certain mother died first, only minority; and as he wrote since it is minority of minority we establish in presumption of mother's heirs; and likewise if doubt equally balanced as siman 280 — except truth of matter is only minority alone. And what he wrote minority of minority — appears per Rama's responsum language who wrote sometimes distant, etc. — see there; and perhaps word demi'uta is typo.
