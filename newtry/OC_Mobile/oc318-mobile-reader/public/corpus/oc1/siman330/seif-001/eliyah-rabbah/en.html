[1] And we desecrate Shabbos, etc. Therefore, when a woman reaches the ninth month, she should prepare everything before Shabbos so that Shabbos need not be desecrated, as stated in Sefer Chasidim.
[2] [Levush] Even if the women do not, etc. This requires study, for it seems necessary to light a lamp to see what she needs, as stated by the Magen Avraham. According to the Levush, however, this is resolved, because seeing is unnecessary. The further question, that they could tell her in her presence that the lamp was lit and thereby calm her, is also not difficult, because when they actually perform what she needs she will feel it through touch.
[3] A vessel, etc. Even oil should be brought in a hanging vessel. She should not place oil in her hair and then squeeze it out afterward, as stated by the Beit Yosef.
[4] [Levush] Who observe, etc. Therefore one may assist Karaite women in childbirth because they observe Shabbos, as stated by Knesset HaGedolah. It appears that this applies specifically to matters that do not themselves involve desecration of Shabbos, such as when she is already on the birthstool and the fetus has begun to leave. Before that point there may be liability for uprooting something from its place of growth.
