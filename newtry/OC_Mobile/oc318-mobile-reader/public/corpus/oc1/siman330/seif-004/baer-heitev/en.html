And if a skilled midwife and doctor say she does not need it — they listen to them, for many women in childbirth do not have hot food made for them every day; and so is practiced now that they eat yesterday's hot food (Beit Yosef, Rama). And if her friends say she needs it and there is no skilled midwife or doctor — even if she eats ordinary people's food we say she is not aware at the moment, and if she eats cold food she will be endangered. Rashba, Magen Avraham.
