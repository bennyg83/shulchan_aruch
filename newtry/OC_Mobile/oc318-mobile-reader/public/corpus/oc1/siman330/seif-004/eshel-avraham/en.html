(א) Three days. Those measures count from time of niddah; count according to order of days, not hour to hour. Responsum Magen Avraham section 10, 11.

(ב) They do not desecrate. And that she said she needs — they desecrate, and it is woman in childbirth knows today is Shabbat. Responsum Magen Avraham section 12.

(ג) Even. Specifically when she says she needs because of birth; and when friends say she needs because of birth — they desecrate. Responsum Magen Avraham sections 13, 14.
