Even if she said she does not need, etc. Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Rashba wrote: we consider a woman in childbirth like one with danger because her limbs are dislocated and she is crushed, only she is not aware at the moment; if she eats cold food or things that one with danger does not eat, they will bring her to danger — end of his words. And it is difficult from the passage later (Eruvin daf 68): that infant whose hot water was spilled — Rava said let us ask his mother if she needs it; a non-Jew through his mother. Rav Mesharshia said: his mother is eating dates. Rashi explained: since she eats cold food she does not need hot food. Perhaps she is merely out of her mind — Rashi explained tavnav in Aramaic: she eats and does not know what. It is not implied that if she were lucid it would help if she ate cold food. And one can say that there it deals after three days when the permission is not from being endangered but from her saying she needs it.
