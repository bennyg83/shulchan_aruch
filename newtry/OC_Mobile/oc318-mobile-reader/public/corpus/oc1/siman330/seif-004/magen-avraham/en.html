(א) Three days. And we do not count from hour to hour as written siman 317 (Terumat HaDeshen).

(ב) She said she does not need. That is, if her friends say she needs it — even if there is no skilled midwife or doctor they do for her, for the matters done for a living creature on a weekday are known (Ramban, Tur); even if she eats ordinary people's food we say she is not aware at the moment, and if she eats cold food she will be endangered (Rashba); but if a skilled midwife and doctor say she does not need it — they listen to them, for many women in childbirth do not have hot food made every day (Beit Yosef, nevertheless); and so is practiced now that they eat yesterday's hot food.

(ג) They do not desecrate. Meaning: even though her friends say she needs it — but when a doctor says she needs it, it is obvious they desecrate, as written siman 318.
