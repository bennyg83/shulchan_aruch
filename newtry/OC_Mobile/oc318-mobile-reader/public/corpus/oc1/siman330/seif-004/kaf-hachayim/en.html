(4) There. They call skilled midwife, etc. — If there is midwife here and only this one is more skilled — nevertheless permitted. Tiferet Yisrael. Petach Einayim section 1: Jewish midwife delivering Jewish women on Shabbat takes payment and goes before judges — responsum Mahari Brunna siman 114; and if twins were born and she claims two payments — if born within time of speech they give only addition; if in two births even one day she takes two payments; and if each twenty-four hours and womb opening still like first — she takes half payment for second birth — there siman 115; and see above siman 306 section 36.
