(א) Three days — And we do not count from hour to hour as siman 317 regarding Yom Kippur; and therefore if she gave birth on day four before twilight — again forbidden to desecrate for her Shabbat unless she said I need it — so Magen Avraham; and see siman 317 where Gra wavered greatly there and wrote from Rosh's language in chapter Merubah it implies he holds plainly the gemara's intent is from hour to hour, not only as answer to Behag; and so proven explicitly in Gra's responsum general rule 26 siman 3 — see there; and so Ritva in chapter R' Eliezer regarding milah; and see Avnei Nezer that from Issur VeHeter it also implies we require three complete days, meaning from hour to hour; afterward I found in Sefer Nazer Yisrael in the name of responsum of Beit Efrayim he also wrote as we say; and it is found accordingly that for Shabbat melacha and for Yom Kippur until three from hour to hour we consider her endangered and permitted even if she did not say I need it; and from three until seven from hour to hour when she said I need it; and it appears for Yom Kippur certainly one can lean leniently, for it is doubt of life-threatening danger; and even for Shabbat likewise if non-Jew did not happen then to do through him — permitted through Israelite.
(ב) Early authorities — From completion of birth [Lechem Mishneh]; and see Biur Halacha.
(ג) Even if she said she does not need — And these are matters her friends say they do for a living creature, and every living creature needs this, and there is no doctor or skilled midwife to see if this living creature needs this.
(ד) They desecrate — For since all women in childbirth need this, even though we do not know about this one and perhaps she can wait until evening — they desecrate from doubt and do not listen to her, lest perhaps weakness of mind applies to her that she loathes all food or is not aware at the moment [Ramban]; and this is Rashba's language on woman in childbirth within three who eats ordinary food and says she does not need hot food cooked for her on Shabbat — he answered: we consider like endangered for woman in childbirth because her limbs are dislocated and she is crushed, only not aware at the moment; if she eats cold or things one with danger does not eat — they bring her to danger; therefore all who lack sustaining foods and healthy people's food — they desecrate Shabbat for her — end of his words; and nevertheless where there is skilled midwife or doctor and they also say she does not need — they listen, for many living creatures do not have hot food made daily; and Magen Avraham wrote that now women in childbirth eat yesterday's hot food on Shabbat — end of his words; and nevertheless it appears all per the sick person.
(ה) From three until seven she said she does not need — Because she is not in presumption of danger for those matters her friends regularly do for her; and further she can wait; therefore they rely on her when she says she does not need; and all this when she said explicitly; but presumptively they desecrate, for nevertheless there is concern of danger; and if doctor or skilled midwife says she needs — obviously they desecrate even if the living creature says she does not need; but if other friends say she needs — they do not listen to her to contradict the living creature. And know further: if the living creature says she needs — even if a hundred doctors say she does not need — they listen to her, for the heart knows the bitterness of its soul, as explained siman 318.
(ו) From there onward, etc. — Since we know she has no other illness and she also does not say so — all are expert that from childbirth she has no danger for hot water and other needs of woman in childbirth; and it is obvious she can wait until evening [Ramban in his chiddushim].
(ז) Like sick without danger — Who tells a non-Jew and he does [gemara].
