A Jewish woman who delivers Jewish children on Shabbos may collect payment through the courts. Responsa Mahari Bruna, siman 114. If twins were born and she demands two fees, when both were born within the period required for a brief utterance she receives only an additional payment. If they were two separate births, even on the same day, she receives two fees. If each was born within the period required for a brief utterance and the birth canal remained open as initially, she receives half the fee for the second birth. There, siman 115.
