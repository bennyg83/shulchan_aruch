(א) (s.k. 4) Ishmaelites, etc. — even Ishmaelites who are not idolaters — for it enters your mind the reason is because he delivers a son of idolatry; and what is the reason even on a weekday it is forbidden unless because of hatred as written Yoreh De'ah siman 154 — it teaches even if not idolatry it is forbidden because of Shabbat prohibition.

(ב) Nevertheless Karaites are permitted. Since they observe, etc. — in my humble opinion this is specifically in a matter without Shabbat desecration — such as when she already sat on the birth-stool, for then they permit because of hatred; for according to them it is not relevant that one who does not observe Shabbat — they do not desecrate, etc. — for they observe Shabbat; but a matter with Shabbat desecration — that was not permitted because of hatred, and as Tosafos wrote — certainly also for Karaites forbidden; and also what is permitted — in my humble opinion it is forbidden to take payment, for it is Shabbat payment; and so in Knesset HaGedolah — according to what was presumed permitted to deliver for one who is not idolatry — at least she should not take payment; therefore only through absorption — see there.
