(א) Seif 7, and they cut — as R' Nissim explained Shabbos 129b; and there 'and R' Nissim said,' etc. — all that is stated, etc.

(ב) Unless. As R' Yehuda in Yevamos chapter 2; and it requires study, for from there it implies the halacha is not like R' Yehuda unless she remained thirty days — for we rely on Rashbag alone; and see Even HaEzer siman 4 in Hagahah; and some say specifically, etc., and it is as I wrote that we rely only when both are present, as many do; and the words of Tur and Bach there require study — for in ordinary births there is no view that completion is required; and see below siman 331 seif 3; and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch hold that there they spoke only of a definite eight-month infant as I wrote, but in an ordinary case we rely on Rashbag alone as written in Shabbos 136a that the halacha is like Rashbag in ordinary births; and see Tosafos there and there; and it appears to me that even Tosafos and Rosh have no difficulty except regarding moving him and circumcising him — for circumcision has no prohibition as written there, only because of moving; in that we rely on R' Yehuda alone; and it requires study.

(ג) Or doubt. In the gemara 135a and in Tosafos there s.v. ben — he wrote that it is permitted in such a case.
