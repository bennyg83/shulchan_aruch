(א) They do all his needs — Meaning what is explained below.

(ב) And they wash — Not according to order explained in gemara; and he derives from verse; and there it states they cut cord and afterward wash him; and so Rambam chapter 2 Hilchot Shabbat. And know from what Magen Avraham brought in the name of Tosafos it is proven they do not do d'oraisa melacha for these matters — for preventing them is not danger only distress; if so accordingly if he has no knife to cut cord and must bring through public domain — forbidden to bring, only tie for now; and likewise other matters mentioned here; and in explanation of halacha I brought early authorities' view that permitted to desecrate d'oraisa melacha for this — see there; and in any case to bring through non-Jew or heat hot water through him to wash infant — certainly permitted.

(ג) And they salt him — So flesh will harden [Rashi]; and it implies salting should be in salt [according to Peri Megadim]; and in Rambam's explanation of mishnah he wrote they place myrtle powder and the like on him; and so in Arukh entry lamed peh — see there.

(ד) And they bury placenta — And not in ground — for for this all agree we do not desecrate Shabbat; rather such was their custom when birth was on Shabbat: rich buried it in oil bowls or wool sponges, poor in rags or straw; and on weekday both bury in earth [Yerushalmi].

(ה) So infant will be warmed — And this was their segulah practice.

(ו) And they cut — After tying it; and see Magen Avraham that cutting cord is mere shvus; and in Kovetz to Rambam he wrote in the name of Sefer Shoshanim LeDavid he proved there is d'oraisa prohibition here, and only here permitted — see there.

(ז) But born eighth — For certainly not viable.

(ח) They do not desecrate — For since we doubt whether he was ever viable we do not desecrate; and it is not similar to one sitting on birth-stool in seif 5 — for there her months were completed [Magen Avraham]; and see Biur Halacha what we wrote on this.

(ט) Unless hair and nails completed — Refers also to eight-month stone; and reason we say he is seven-month and remained — is because we say it remained; and see explanation of Gra that remained in Shulchan Aruch's law requires study; and his view: for Shabbat desecration we do not rely on completion alone unless he also remained thirty days; only for moving infant we can rely on sign of completion alone.
