[10] A doubt, etc. Olat Shabbos asks why, since in matters of danger to life we are lenient in cases of doubt, as above in seif 5. He answers that here the matter concerns only the infant's discomfort. Where there is danger, even if it is uncertain whether the child is a seven-month infant, Shabbos is desecrated. This requires study, because Yoreh Deah siman 266, seif 11, appears to say that even in a dangerous situation one does not desecrate Shabbos, as stated in the Gemara there. The main answer to the question appears to me to be that since the infant's hair and nails were not fully formed, there is a presumption that he is an eight-month infant. See siman 331, seif 3. The Magen Avraham answers that the earlier case concerns a fetus whose months were completed.
[11] Or perhaps, etc. This applies specifically when she immersed and her husband was with her once and then left. Otherwise there is an uncertainty and it is permitted. Therefore the practice is to move every infant, as stated by the Agudah in Perek Rabbi Eliezer. Regarding that which causes her pain, etc., this applies specifically when it is not expressed into a cup or pot, as explained above in siman 328, seif 34.
