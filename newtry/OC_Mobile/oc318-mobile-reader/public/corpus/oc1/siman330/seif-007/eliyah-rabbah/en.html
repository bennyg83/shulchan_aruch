[ז] [Levush] If it appears, etc. It implies that presumptively they do not desecrate; and from Shulchan Aruch it does not imply so; and perhaps the Levush wrote only to exclude also her friends — we say she does not need and they do not desecrate; but when her friends do not know they desecrate; and so Nachalat Tzvi; and likewise when her friends say she does not need and she is silent they desecrate; and so Bach ruled.
