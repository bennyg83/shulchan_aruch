For a newborn who was born — they do all his needs: they wash him, salt him, bury the placenta so the infant will be warmed, and cut the umbilical cord; and these matters apply to one born at nine months or seven months; but one born at eight months,' or where there is' doubt whether he is a seven-month' or eight-month infant, they do not desecrate Shabbat for him unless his hair and nails were completed.
