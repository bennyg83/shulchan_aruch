(ב) And they salt. So the flesh will harden.

(ג) Or doubt, etc. Olas Shabbat asked: behold in Sefer HaHashlamah it is stated that even doubt of one who never had presumption of life — such as one sitting on the birth-stool — they desecrate Shabbat for him; if so why did he rule here, etc. — see there; and this is not a difficulty, for there it deals when her months were completed; and it appears this depends on the dispute that Rosh and Ran wrote in chapter 2 of Yoma regarding her fetus that smelled — if she does not eat, her fetus will miscarry even though it is doubt of a viable infant, doubt of miscarriage — we give and bring proof from one sitting on the birth-stool, etc., that on doubt they desecrate Shabbat at the beginning of Erchin; and Ramban wrote they do not desecrate because of miscarriages, and that case of one sitting on the birth-stool has another reason — for it is considered like birth, for it is no longer her mother's thigh since she died, rather it is alive and the door is locked before it, and there is no concern except that it has no presumption of life — end of his words; it implies that for Ramban at least they must know her months were completed, even for one sitting on the birth-stool; for he brings proof from that it is permitted to kill him — see there in Sefer Torat HaAdam and in Ran in Yoma; and here since there is an indication that hair and nails were not completed we are concerned from doubt; and see Tosafos Niddah s.v. vehahorego; and see Eruvin siman 156; and in Beit Yosef Choshen Mishpat siman 276.
