[ח] [Levush] Presumptively they desecrate, etc. Even if her friends do not know; but when they say she does not need they do not desecrate since she is silent; and when she says she does not need and a doctor says they need to — they do not desecrate — so Bach wrote; and in Shiltei HaGibborim chapter Mefanin he is uncertain also in this; therefore one should be lenient; and so it appears in responsum of Radbaz siman 66 that they desecrate; and greater than this it appears to me that even if she eats ordinary people's food we say she is not aware at the moment; and though Rashba in Beit Yosef wrote on the law that within three days — perhaps the act was so; and further Bach brings clear proof from Eruvin daf 68 — perhaps she is out of her mind — meaning she does not know what she eats; and there it was after three as Taz wrote; and in truth Taz was not precise — for Rashi explained there it deals even after seven, and they warmed through Kutim; and what Taz wrote when her mind is clear it helps if she eats cold — is not implied in the gemara; and so Bach and Ran in the name of Ramban chapter Mefanin — rather in all women they permit from this reason. And it is obvious that if the woman in childbirth is sick even after seven she is no worse than any other sick person.
