(א) Bonfire — And likewise in our time to heat the oven.
(ב) All forty — If she has distress from cold — for he holds there is danger to living creature all forty from cold; therefore if impossible through non-Jew they do through Israelite [Maggid Mishneh and other authorities]; and for other sick through non-Jew permitted even without danger if he needs bonfire; and if there is danger if he says he needs — permitted even through Israelite when impossible through non-Jew [Bach and Avnei Nezer]; and see Biur Halacha where I brought view of many great early authorities who hold living creature after seven — its law for bonfire is also like other sick without danger.
