6) There: Even if she is blind. Even if the women attending her do not need a lamp and could provide all necessary care without one, a lamp is nevertheless lit for her. Although she cannot see, her mind is more settled when she knows that a lamp is present, because she says that if she needs something, her companion will see and provide it. Levush. Nevertheless, when a non-Jew is available, it is preferable to have the non-Jew act, because a woman giving birth is treated more leniently than another ill person in this regard, although in several respects her law is more stringent. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 2. See below, paragraph 8.
