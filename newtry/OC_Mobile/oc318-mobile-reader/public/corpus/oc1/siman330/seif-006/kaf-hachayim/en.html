Even if she is blind — And even if women attending her do not need lamp and could attend all her needs without lamp — nevertheless they kindle lamp for her sake, for even though she does not see she is more settled when she knows there is lamp here, saying if there is something I need my friend sees and does. Levush. Nevertheless when there is non-Jew it is more to do through non-Jew, for woman in childbirth is lighter than other sick in several matters though stricter in some.
