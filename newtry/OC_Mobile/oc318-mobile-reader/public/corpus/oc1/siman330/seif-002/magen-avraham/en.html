(א) A non-Jew may not deliver. Even Ishmaelites who are not idolaters (and so in Rambam chapter 2); nevertheless for Karaites it is permitted since they observe Shabbat (Knesset HaGedolah).

(ב) May not deliver. For there is no hatred so they can say that one who does not observe Shabbat — they do not desecrate for him; it implies that if there is concern for hatred it is permitted if there is no Shabbat desecration.

(ג) That involves no desecration. Such as when she sits on the birth-stool — for the fetus was already uprooted to emerge; for they said one who extended his hand into a woman's womb and caused the fetus to miscarry while in her womb is liable for uprooting something from its growth; or since her months were completed her months were already severed (Tosafos); and we are not expert whether her months were completed as written in Eruvin; and see siman 15.
