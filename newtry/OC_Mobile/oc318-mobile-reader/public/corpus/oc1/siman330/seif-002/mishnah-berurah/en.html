(8) A non-Jew may not deliver — Even for payment; for on a weekday they deliver because of hatred as explained Yoreh De'ah siman 154; here forbidden because she can evade and say they do not desecrate Shabbat except for one who observes Shabbat; and Magen Avraham wrote: doctors in our time, even the more proper, are not careful at all — it happens every Shabbat they travel many parsaos to heal idolaters and write and grind drugs themselves; and they have nothing to rely on — for even if we say permitted to desecrate Shabbat in d'rabbanan prohibition because of hatred with the idolater [though this too is unclear — see Peri Megadim], d'oraisa prohibition is certainly forbidden for all, and they are full Shabbat desecrators intentionally — may Hashem protect us.
