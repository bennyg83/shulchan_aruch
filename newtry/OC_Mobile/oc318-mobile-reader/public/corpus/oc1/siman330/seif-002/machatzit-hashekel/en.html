(א) (s.k. 2) etc. and nevertheless it requires study — why is it needed, etc. — meaning: why is she not settled in mind lest she need something and in darkness her friends will not know from this.

(ב) Or that it will not be possible to do for her what she needs in darkness. The reason itself — even if she was not concerned about the lamp, nevertheless it is permitted to kindle a lamp so she knows what she needs and they can do that thing properly.

(ג) And later authorities brought in the name of Levush to settle — even if she is in a situation where her friends can do all things she needs in darkness, nevertheless it is permitted because she is not settled in mind, fearing they will not do the thing properly without a lamp even if it is not true.
