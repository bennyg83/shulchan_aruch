(א) Non-Jew. Even Ishmaelites who are not idolaters; nevertheless Karaites are permitted since they observe Shabbat (Knesset HaGedolah, Magen Avraham). But in Gan Eden VeRimmonim Chayei Adam general rule 3 siman 1 he wrote that this ruling is not correct — see there at length.

(ב) Shabbat. For there is no hatred so they can say that one who does not observe Shabbat — they do not desecrate for him; the gemara implies that if there is concern for hatred it is permitted if there is no Shabbat desecration. Magen Avraham.
