In Mechaber seif 2, non-Jew. NB: See responsa Chasam Sofer Yoreh De'ah siman 231 [there they asked: in Togar country the ruler commanded towns and villages to hire for them an expert midwife who learned in their house of study; and even if they have sharp living creatures they need specifically to hire her who stood trial before medical sages. And in some villages they found only Israelite — may she hire herself to them weekday and Shabbat. And at end of his responsum he wrote: in any case for halacha it appears to me to rely on Ramban who acted accordingly in practice, halacha l'ma'aseh; if so permitted even on Shabbat in what has no Shabbat desecration — sitting on birth-stool, for fetus already uprooted, as Magen Avraham wrote siman 330 s.k. 6. But to cut cord which is d'oraisa melacha — command healthy non-Jew standing by to cut; and if in this hatred there is concern of life-threatening danger — permit even d'oraisa melacha, even though from Magen Avraham's language s.k. 34 it implies only extinguishing which is melacha she'einah tzricha legufah was permitted, not full melacha; nevertheless see Eruvin 44b in mishnah and Shiltei HaGibborim there — it appears clearly even d'oraisa melacha permitted if impossible without it].
