(א) (s.k. 10) One sitting, etc. — it dies first — so Tosafos in Niddah daf 44 on that which they said there: infant one day old inherits his mother and bequeaths to brothers from the father — such as Yaakov who married Rachel and Leah, Rachel bore Reuven, Leah bore Shimon and Leah died, Shimon her son remained even if Shimon did not live after his mother's death except one day; and likewise one hour he inherits his mother (and such as if Yaakov died — otherwise the husband inherits his wife); and when Shimon died his brother from the father — Reuven — inherits him; but fetus does not inherit to bequeath to brothers from the father — such as if Leah died while pregnant — then her fetus dies first; we do not say the fetus in the grave will inherit the mother and afterward bequeath to brothers from the father; on this Tosafos wrote: if so that which Shmuel said in Erchin 7:7 one sitting on birth-stool on Shabbat they bring knife, etc., lest they find alive — behold the fetus dies before the mother; nevertheless Shmuel deals when she sat on birth-stool, for the fetus was already uprooted and has life on its own — in such a case perhaps the mother died and the fetus lives.

(ב) And even though it twitches, etc. — these are the gemara's words in Niddah, Bava Batra, and Erchin on that which they said fetus dies first — they ask behold it is a creature and the fetus twitches after the mother's death three twitches; and answer Mar bar R' Elazar: like the tail of a lizard — and this twitching is not a sign of life. And in responsum, etc. — they always tear even if fetus not uprooted — for although fetus dies first, nevertheless sometimes the fetus lives after mother's death; therefore they desecrate Shabbat from doubt; and he inferred so from Rashi's words as Magen Avraham wrote below; and to settle the gemara's words according to him it must also be as Magen Avraham wrote below. But Rosh ruled as written — namely only when fetus uprooted they tear; and Rama in the responsum wrote — end of his words: nevertheless also Rosh's answer is correct and that distinction is mentioned in gemara Erchin 7 for another matter — end of his words; meaning from that which they taught: woman who went out to be executed they do not wait until she gives birth, for she is one body with the mother; but one who sat on birth-stool they wait until she gives birth, since fetus already uprooted it is another body — so the gemara now, and Rama holds since it is another body it also has life on its own; and although the mother died perhaps the fetus remained alive.

(ג) Nevertheless from Rambam and Mechaber being silent, etc. — although he wrote one sitting on birth-stool — since she sits on birth-stool certainly fetus uprooted and need not further explain only when uprooted — nevertheless Magen Avraham wrote their silence means the reason is because fetus already uprooted; not that he intended to explain only when uprooted; but it is true he holds so that even if not uprooted they tear; and 'one sitting on birth-stool' is ordinary language — when she struggles in birth until she died without doubt she sat on it; but likewise if she did not sit on it and fetus not uprooted they tear; or truly he holds so that even if not uprooted they tear, and 'sat on birth-stool' is ordinary language.

(ד) It implies he holds as Rashi wrote, etc. — this is Rama's inference in the responsum from that Rashi wrote according to Shmuel's view one on birth-stool they bring knife, etc. — end of his words: sometimes it happens she dies first — end of his words; and Rama infers from 'sometimes it happens' it implies per the majority it is not so — meaning without uprooting yet; and with this all Tosafos' difficulties are resolved that they challenged on fetus dies first; and they taught one who slaughters the animal and finds nine-month alive is not permitted through its mother's slaughter — how do you find this that you find living fetus, behold fetus dies first; they further challenged from Chullin regarding 'when it is born' seven days under its mother — 'when born' excludes one exiting the womb that is invalid for sacrifice; 'under its mother' excludes orphan; and asked: this orphan how is it — if you say she gave birth and afterward mother died, this cannot be called orphan, will the mother live forever; rather she died and afterward gave birth — if so impossible she gives birth after death except through tearing womb or belly already diminished from 'when born'; with this also how do you find she died and afterward gave birth — behold fetus dies first; answer specifically she died through angel of death; but if executed perhaps mother died first as they said in Erchin (and this is what R' Yehuda challenged there regarding woman going to execution (and she is pregnant) they strike her opposite the womb so fetus dies first so she not come to disfigurement (Rashi: if there is life in fetus it emerges after mother's death and disfigurement is) — and they taught infant one day inherits, etc., but fetus not because fetus dies first; why strike opposite womb; and answer this applies to death — since fetus is small its life enters tips of angel of death and cuts the signs; but if executed she dies first — end of gemara; further answer it deals when fetus uprooted, for then mother dies first; and they challenged accordingly why teach infant one day inherits, etc., but fetus not because fetus dies first — behold you find also fetus inherits, etc., how when mother dies first such as executed or fetus uprooted; answer truly so — only the mishnah changed wording and stated infant certainly inherits, etc., what is not so for fetus — sometimes he does not inherit. And Semag copied in siman 276 seif 17 on that which Shulchan Aruch wrote fetus does not inherit, etc. — if fetus uprooted or mother executed he inherits, etc., but from poskim's silence in Choshen Mishpat from siman 276 it implies they hold there is no distinction between executed or died or uprooted fetus or not; and they hold as Magen Avraham wrote according to Rashi in Erchin that even if fetus not uprooted nevertheless sometimes mother dies first; with this Tosafos' difficulty is settled from that which they found in her living nine-month, and from 'excludes orphan,' and from one sitting on birth-stool they bring knife, etc. — for sometimes mother dies first.

(ה) Since it is minority of minority we establish inheritance in presumption of mother's heirs as stated and as written there siman 280 — with this he comes to settle: even if not uprooted nevertheless sometimes mother dies first — why does fetus not bequeath to this; he brings proof: since doubt who died first, property in presumption of mother's heirs, for property comes through them; and if mother died first, etc., property in presumption of mother's heirs and brothers have nothing; if so here too fetus does not inherit, etc., even though sometimes mother died first nevertheless from doubt nothing emerges; therefore property in presumption of mother's heirs — lest fetus died first; and that Magen Avraham needed to say it is minority of minority — even if it were doubt equally balanced as siman 280 — nevertheless property in presumption of mother's heirs; this is because it is difficult for him on his own words that he wrote there is no distinction between executed or died or uprooted or not — behold in gemara they say there is distinction; and certainly that which Rama proved in responsum there is distinction between uprooted or not from that which they said for one sitting on birth-stool they wait until she gives birth — since she sat on birth-stool fetus uprooted and is another body — nevertheless one can say it is not necessary to wait after mother's death; but that there is no distinction between died and executed for fetus's life after mother — this is proven in gemara, as Tosafos also wrote — why did gemara distinguish that which Shmuel said going to execution they strike opposite womb — since when executed mother dies first, it implies but if she died fetus dies first; for this Magen Avraham needed to say died and fetus not uprooted is minority of minority that fetus lives after mother's death; therefore if not for executed we would not say to strike opposite womb, for blows are forbidden and concern of disfigurement if fetus emerges after mother's death is only minority of minority; but for executed and likewise uprooted fetus it is doubt equally balanced or only one minority — properly they are concerned for doubt of disfigurement and not concerned for prohibition of blows — better for her in suffering than her disgrace; therefore for Shabbat in every case even if she died and also fetus not uprooted, since minority of minority perhaps fetus lives after mother — in pikuach nefesh we do not follow the majority and we clear away the fetus and tear the mother; not so for inheritance and we do not have an answer when died and fetus not uprooted except even if uprooted fetus if executed nevertheless it is doubt and we do not remove property from presumption of mother's heirs; therefore fetus does not inherit, etc.; if so what Magen Avraham wrote about minority of minority does not refer except to died — and the law is confused in Magen Avraham.

(ו) And with this one answers what poskim ruled for inheritance that in every case fetus does not bequeath; and also what they ruled for Shabbat that they tear even if fetus not uprooted — nevertheless one can still say and certainly not difficult how they ruled fetus does not inherit — behold you find he inherits, etc., such as if they tore mother after death and fetus found alive — answer this is not called fetus but newborn since he emerged to the world; but this is difficult why gemara needed to say in that case three twitches like lizard's tail — behold you find minority of minority he lives after mother's death; and perhaps it is from minority of minority; and it must be gemara truly answers; and difference is: if you find fetus twitches after mother's death — even so he does not inherit to bequeath to brothers from mother, for twitching is nothing.
