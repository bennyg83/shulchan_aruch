So his limbs not become distorted. And in Rosh chapter 17 they wrote that even to wrap him to straighten his limbs is permitted forever; and in Ramazim it is found to straighten limbs; and the gemara gives the reason because it is their custom thus, and it is like feeding and giving drink; and it implies that for an infant whom they do not wrap on a weekday and his limbs became distorted — it is forbidden to wrap him on Shabbat.
