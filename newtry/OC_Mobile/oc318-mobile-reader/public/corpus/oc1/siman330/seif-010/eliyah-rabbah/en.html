[י] Doubt, etc. Olas Shabbat asked: behold doubt of life-threatening danger is resolved leniently as above seif 5; and he answered that here it deals with matters that are only the infant's distress; but in a place of danger even in doubt of seven months they desecrate — until here; and it requires study in Yoreh De'ah siman 266 seif 11 — it implies even in danger they do not desecrate as written in the gemara there; and the essence of his wonder appears to me: since hair was not completed, presumption is that he is an eight-month infant; and see siman 331 seif 3; and Magen Avraham answered that above it deals when her months were completed.
