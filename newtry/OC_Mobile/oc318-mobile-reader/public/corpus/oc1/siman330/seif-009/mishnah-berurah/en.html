(א) They straighten — By hand.
(ב) Because of birth pain — Magen Avraham wrote specifically on day of birth; but afterward forbidden; but in truth from many poskim it implies no distinction — even after month or two months permitted; and see explanation of Gra that Shulchan Aruch's view is also to be lenient as them; and therefore one should not be strict; and nevertheless if vertebra dislocated from its place — for all agree forbidden to set by hand vertebrae one on another, for it appears like building, unless on day of birth when one can lean even in this.
