(s.k. 9) They do not desecrate — this refers from three until seven: if she said she does not need they do not desecrate; and so it should be — even though her friends say she needs; but when skilled midwife and doctor say she needs, it is obvious they desecrate as siman 318; and so he emended in Sefer Tashbetz; only he wrote it refers from seven until thirty and she said she needs — therefore he emended the beginning of Magen Avraham's words: even though her friends also say, etc.
