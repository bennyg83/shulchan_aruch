9) If she needs oil, another woman should not place oil in her hair, come to the woman giving birth, and squeeze it out. Rather, she should bring the oil in a utensil suspended in her hair. It is preferable to carry unusually without adding another prohibition than to add the prohibition of squeezing. Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham there.
