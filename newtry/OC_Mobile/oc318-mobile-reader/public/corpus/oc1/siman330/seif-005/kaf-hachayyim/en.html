5) There: A lamp is lit for her. This applies even if no practical use of its light is required, but only to give her peace of mind. The Baraisa's statement, 'if she needed a lamp,' does not mean that it is not lit when she does not need its light. Rather, it excludes daytime, when no lamp is needed, or a nighttime place where a lamp is already present. Whenever she lies in a dark house, or at night when there is no light from either a lamp or the moon, a lamp is lit in every case. Beis Yehudah, siman 59; see there. One who lights a lamp for a woman giving birth need not fast, as stated above in siman 329, paragraph 25.
