And they kindle lamp for her — Even if not needed for any use of lamp light, only for her peace of mind; and that which baraita teaches 'if she needed lamp' — not to say if she does not need lamp they do not kindle; rather to exclude if it was day she does not need lamp, or night where there is lamp; but whenever she is distressed in dark house or at night in every case they kindle if there is no light at all — neither lamp nor moonlight. Beit Yehudah siman 59; and see there; and one who kindles lamp for woman in childbirth need not fast as written above siman 329 section 25.
