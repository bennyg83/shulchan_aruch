(א) One sitting on the birth-stool. For the fetus was uprooted to emerge; but if the fetus was not uprooted it dies first — even if it twitches there is no life, as one view holds like the tail of a lizard that twitches; see Bava Batra daf 142b and responsum of Rama siman 40 — it implies they always tear her, for in matters of danger we go leniently; but Mahara Shteinhart ruled as written, and so Tosafos in Niddah 4:4; and see R' Yehuda the Chassid part 1 simanim 97–99; nevertheless from the fact that Rambam and Mechaber are silent it implies they hold as Rashi at the beginning of chapter of Erchin that they always tear, for sometimes it can live — and with this all Tosafos' difficulties in Bava Batra and Niddah are resolved; and so from the fact that poskim are silent in Choshen Mishpat siman 276 — see there in Beit Yosef — not like Semag who brought the view of Tosafos that there is a distinction between she was killed and died, or between uprooted fetus and not; from the silence of poskim it implies there is no distinction, since it is a minority of a minority we establish inheritance in presumption of the mother's heirs, as written there siman 280.

(ב) We are not expert. For perhaps she fainted, and if they cut her she will die; and they need to wait, and meanwhile the fetus dies.
