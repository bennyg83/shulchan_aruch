(note 1) "One should not [separate]," etc. — for indeed, etc., it is like the blessing. And see siman 119 — meaning that there it is explained that one may add in any blessing that is in the middle of the eighteen [blessings] something like that blessing, except that one must begin with the blessing and afterward say the addition; but one should not say the addition first and afterward begin the blessing. And the reason is that one must make the main element the coin that the Sages stamped; therefore one must begin with the coin that the Sages stamped. And the same applies here: in the middle of a blessing it is permitted, since one began with the Sages' coin; and also the addition of the piyyut is like the blessing. Not so if one says the piyyut before one began the blessing — that is forbidden, for then the piyyut is the main element and not the Sages' coin.
