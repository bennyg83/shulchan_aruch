{Rama: And one who is lenient has not lost if he did not say them.} And so shall one do who does not say piyyutim with the congregation: immediately when the prayer leader begins the blessing Yotzer Or, he too shall begin and say all the blessings in order and read Keriat Shema, and he too shall say Emet veYatziv until "le'amo" at the time they call to him — and there he shall be silent until the prayer leader also reaches there, and then he concludes with the congregation Ga'al Yisrael and connects redemption to prayer (Tur in the name of Rema). And from this spread the custom to stand from one's place and prepare oneself for prayer when reaching Tehillot leEl Elyon (Maharal).
