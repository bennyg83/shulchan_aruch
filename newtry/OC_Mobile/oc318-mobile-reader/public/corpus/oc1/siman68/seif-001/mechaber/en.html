[The Laws Regarding] That One Should Not Interrupt With Piyyutim. Containing 1 Se'if: There are places where they interrupt in the blessing of Keriat Shema to say piyyutim, but' it is proper to refrain from saying them, since they constitute an interruption.And there are those who say that there is no prohibition in the matter [Ri at the end of the chapter Ein Om'dim, and the Rashba, and the Tur], and such is the custom in all places to say them. And one who is lenient and does not say them has not lost. Nevertheless, one should not engage in any matter; even in words of Torah it is forbidden to interrupt and engage while the congregation is saying piyyutim — all the more so is it forbidden to speak any idle talk. However, one who studies by meditation, who looks in a book and meditates — there is no prohibition in that, for meditation is not like speech; except that through this they may come to speak and come to make an interruption. Therefore, one should not separate oneself from the congregation in a place where they have the custom to say them, and one should say them with them.
