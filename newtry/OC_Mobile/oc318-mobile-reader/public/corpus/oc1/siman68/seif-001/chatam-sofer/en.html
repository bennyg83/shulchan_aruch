In Magen Avraham 'equal for all.' N.B.: Had Rashdam known this — Orah Chaim part 34 — he would not have written as he wrote there. And see responsa Chatam Sofer Orah Chaim siman 15, 16 [there he wrote regarding one who wipes the prayer leader so that he not pass before the ark in wool garments to pray in the Sephardic nusach, because one who wears wool is not fit for it, and he wrote that there is nothing to fear from this. And in the middle of the responsum he brings what he received from his rabbi-teachers the geonim z"l regarding prayer nusachot, that all of them are equal one to the other and all that is in one is in the other, except that we do not know to what extent. And we pray per the enactments of the prayers and these nusachot, for all of them intended one matter, like one style for several prophets — and two prophets do not prophesy in one style; and each nusach is only one style differing from its fellow, but they ascend to one place. And he expanded further there and in the responsum after it and in siman 197 on this matter.
