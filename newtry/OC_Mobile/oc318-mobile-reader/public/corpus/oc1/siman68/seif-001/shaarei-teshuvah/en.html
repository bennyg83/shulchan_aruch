(א) Similar. Be'er Heitev. See Pri Chadash who permits not to say and to study then by meditation; and in Be'er HaGolah it is written that in the piyyutim of Kalir and the like one should not do so. However, in later piyyutim of poets, if one meditates in words of Torah, it is lengthy to do so.

(ב) From the congregation. Be'er Heitev. And in Shaarei Teshuvah he wrote that he saw in Jerusalem, may it be rebuilt speedily in our days, the book Kavanot in manuscript from Maharash Vital son of Maharach z"l, who wrote about his father's words that wrote "my teacher would not say," etc., and thus: "Shmuel said: although my father Mari z"l wrote this, I saw him when he was shaliach tzibur in the congregation on the Days of Awe he would say all the confessions and all the piyyutim" — end quote; "let a wise man hear and learn wisdom and moral discipline from him" — end quote. The Shaarei Teshuvah related there things involving arrogance and that do not contain the Name. And see in the responsa of Mahari ben Magash z"l siman 47: he wrote that if the prayer leader returns the matter of the blessing before the blessing reaches him, it is permitted — and this is a simple custom for the whole world; and likewise is the opinion of the Rav z"l in this (meaning our Rabbi the Rif) — end quote. And in Be'er Yosef siman 112 he brought this from the words of Rambam in the responsum called Pe'er haDor siman 64 and siman 129, who forbids in Keriat Shema blessings the Name; and in what Tosafot wrote that R' Elazar Kalir is Rabbenu Shimon ben Yochai — see in Ma'aseh Menachem and Zikaron Yosef siman 13–14; and in responsa Noda B'Yehuda second edition who expanded greatly on this; and in Machatzit haShekel he brought the responsum of Rabbeinu Tam brought in Shibolei Leket in manuscript, and wrote that it is deep and wide; and there Maharatz sensed in the cleanness of the palms of the sukkah and another piyyut "my sister whom you hid for this reason"; and he wrote in resolving the matters; and in Machatzit haShekel he expanded, and at the end of his words wrote: "Come see how mighty they are — that in their wisdom they surpass a prophet: our Rabbi the Ari z"l revealed that the poets had in them a spark from the soul of R' Elazar ben Shimon, as it is written 'myrrh and calamus and the wing of joyous birds'" — end quote.
