Magen Avraham: and if praying alone one should not say any piyyutim in the middle of a blessing. In the source of the law in the responsum of Rav Mintz it is explained that one who prays alone should not say piyyutim at all — since there is no obligation to say them, why should he enter himself into this doubt. And this is for himself; when he prayed with the congregation, in order not to separate from the congregation, he said the piyyutim before beginning the blessing of Yotzer Or — see there. Therefore it seems these words "in the middle of the blessing" are a scribal error, and perhaps it should read that he should not say — not in the middle of the blessing nor between Pesukei Dezimra and Yotzer. But it is permitted to say them separately after prayer.
