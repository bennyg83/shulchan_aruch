Magen Avraham: And if one prays alone one should not say any piyyutim in the middle of the blessing. In the source of the law, in the responsum of Rav Mintz it is explained that one praying alone should not say piyyutim at all, since there is no obligation to say them — why should he put himself in this doubt. And this applies to himself: when he prayed with the congregation, so as not to separate from the congregation he said the piyyutim before beginning the blessing Yotzer Or — see there. Therefore it seems these words in the middle of the blessing are a scribal error, and perhaps it should read that one should not say them neither in the middle of the blessing nor between Yishtabach and Yotzer; but one may say them separately after prayer.
