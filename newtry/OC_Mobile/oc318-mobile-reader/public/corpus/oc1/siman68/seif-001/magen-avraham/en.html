And if there are not ten — even one who did not hear, they say Barekhu likewise (Agur — that in Ashkenaz they practiced everywhere like Rashi and Rokeach, that they pray even for one individual). But Tosafot and Rosh wrote that six are required who did not hear — meaning a majority of the minyan; and so is implied in the Tur. And if there are not six who did not hear, the prayer leader does not pray aloud except Barekhu and the rest of the prayer in a whisper. And if there are six who did not hear, he may pray aloud. And if there are ten who did not hear, he prays aloud for everything. And see Mishnah Berurah.
