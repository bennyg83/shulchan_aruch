If one was unavoidably prevented and did not recite the evening Keriat Shema until daybreak—since the sun has not yet budded—one recites Keriat Shema and fulfills the obligation of evening Keriat Shema. And if at that hour one was unavoidably prevented to travel to a place of bands of wild animals and bandits, one does not then recite Keriat Shema a second time to fulfill the obligation of daytime Keriat Shema—for once one made that hour "night," it is impossible to return and make it "day."
