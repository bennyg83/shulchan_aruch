Taz seif katan 2—Keriat Shema and he prays. In Shulchan Aruch there he ruled that one prays in his house and Keriat Shema alone he says on the road; but in the Tur there he brings poskim who hold that one also prays on the road, and Taz did not clarify in this matter to explain, etc. (it seems the text should read: ki) this proof is for the words of all.
