Taz, seif katan 2. Krias Shema and he prays. In the Shulchan Aruch there, at the end of siman 89, he rules that he prays at home and says only Krias Shema on the road. However, the Tur there brings authorities who hold that he also prays on the road. The Taz was not precise in this matter to explain, etc.; it appears to me the text should read 'for' this proof applies according to everyone.
