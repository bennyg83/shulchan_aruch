Of the daytime. The restriction applies specifically to reciting both the evening and morning Krias Shema immediately after dawn, or to reciting both before sunrise. It is permitted, however, to recite the evening Krias Shema immediately at dawn, before the time for the morning Krias Shema, and then recite the morning Krias Shema at its proper time, even though both are recited before sunrise.
