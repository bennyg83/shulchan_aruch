4) There: Its time extends until the end of three hours, etc. From what point are those hours measured, and where do they end? Terumas HaDeshen, siman 1, writes that they begin at dawn and end at the emergence of the stars. Levush, in simanim 233 and 266, writes that they begin at sunrise and end at sunset. Maadanei Melech, page 7a, likewise writes this and discusses the subject at length; see there. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, paragraph 8. Magen Avraham, seif katan 1, however, writes that with respect to the present law, all opinions agree that the hours are counted from dawn; see there. So rule Halachah Berurah, paragraph 1; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 93c; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 5; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch there; and Shitilei Zeitim, paragraph 2.
