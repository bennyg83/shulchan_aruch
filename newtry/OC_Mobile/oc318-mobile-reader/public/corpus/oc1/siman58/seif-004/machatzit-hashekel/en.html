(Seif katan 4) Until "upon," etc.—for he holds one must stand until "upon your heart" like R' Natan who holds thus on page 13d; although we rule like Beit Hillel that each reads in his manner, nevertheless while walking when his mind is not settled upon him he must stand until "upon your heart"—for until here is the main intention; therefore he ruled that if he cannot stand on the road until "upon your heart" and he must read while walking, he is permitted to read from alot hashachar. But according to what Beit Yosef wrote in siman 63 like Rava—that when walking, while standing in the first verse, specifically verse one requires the main intention—if so, if he can read on the road only verse one while standing, even if he cannot stand until "upon your heart" it is not applicable to permit reading from alot hashachar.
