(Seif katan 4) Until 'upon,' etc. For he holds that one must stand until 'upon your heart,' like Rabbi Nosson, who holds so on page 13. Although we rule like Beis Hillel that each person reads in his normal manner, nevertheless, while walking, since his mind is not settled, he must stand until 'upon your heart,' for until there is the primary intent. Therefore he ruled that if he cannot stand on the road until 'upon your heart' and would need to read it while walking, he may read from alot hashachar. But according to what Beis Yosef wrote in siman 63 like Rava, that it is enough when walking to stand for the first verse, for only the first verse requires primary intent, then if he can read on the road with only the first verse standing, even if he cannot stand until 'upon your heart,' he should not have permitted reading from alot hashachar.
