[4] "It is like the measure," etc.—meaning the shining of the sun on the earth until the entire solar body has risen is about an hour; and likewise at sunset the solar body sets in one hour; so wrote Perishah and Darkei Moshe. And from alot ha-shachar until sunrise Tosafot ruled. And it is astonishing: Rambam at the beginning of Berakhot wrote it is a full hour and a fifth; and see later in siman 89 in Magen Avraham what he challenged and resolved on this matter. And see in Ma'adanei Melekh page 6 and in Tosafot Yom Tov there and in Kesef Mishneh as explained there; and I abbreviated.
