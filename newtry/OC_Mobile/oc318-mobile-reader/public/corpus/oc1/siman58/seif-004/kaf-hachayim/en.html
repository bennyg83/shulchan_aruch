(4) There [its time] extends until the end of three hours, etc. And in truth its beginning and end: Baal Turei Zahav siman 1 wrote that its beginning and end are from alot hashachar until the emergence of the stars. And Baal HaLevush in simanim 233 and 266 wrote that they begin from hanetz hachama until its setting; and so too Maggid Mishneh on page 7a, and see there that he elaborated much on this. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef sign 8—but Magen Avraham seif katan 1 wrote that here according to all we count from alot hashachar; see there. And so Peri Megadim sign 1. Yad Aharon in Hagahot Taz. Eliyah Rabbah sign 2. Raz sign 3. Shomer Tzion Hagadot 233 page 73. Chayei Adam sign 5. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch there, Shateilei Zeitim sign 2.
