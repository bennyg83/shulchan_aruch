(18) Amud hashachar—some say amud hashachar is a little before the east lightens; some say amud hashachar is called from when the east lightens—and see in Beit Yosef Lekutim. And this time is before the time of recognizing one's fellow mentioned above in seif 1.

(19) He fulfilled—since some people rise at that time, and we read Keriat Shema with "when you rise" in it; and ex post facto they established him on Torah law; and specifically in an occasional case—that is, once in a month such is sufficient ex post facto; but if he is accustomed to do so, the Sages said even ex post facto he has not fulfilled and must return and read it; however, if it is a time of pressure, even if he is very accustomed—it is permitted, for what could he do?
