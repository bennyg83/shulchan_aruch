Alot hashachar. Some say that alot hashachar is slightly before the eastern sky has lit, and some say that alot hashachar is when the eastern sky has lit. See Biur Halacha. This time is before the time mentioned in seif 1, when one can see his fellow.
He fulfilled the obligation. For some people rise at that time, and it is called 'when you rise.' After the fact, they left it on the Torah law. This is specifically occasional, meaning once in a month, that he fulfills after the fact. But if he regularly does so, the Sages said that even after the fact he did not fulfill and must read it again. However, if it is a pressing circumstance, even if he is very accustomed to do so, it is permitted, for what else could he do.
