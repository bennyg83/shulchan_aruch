If the third hour passed, etc. In Tur he wrote here if compelled and did not read, etc.—in truth the same applies to deliberate [violation], as Beit Yosef wrote; only he mentioned compelled because of the latter part "nevertheless from here onward he lost"; in Beit Yosef chapter 1 of Kriat Shema: there are those who ask—what is the reason we expound "when you lie down" only when people lie down, as stated in the Gemara—even before netz of the sun one fulfills nighttime Kriat Shema because there are people who sleep at that hour; and therefore the explanation is not that at that hour they go up on their bed, for it is not the way of people to lie down at that hour; but certainly the explanation is while they are still lying down; and why did they not expound likewise "when you rise"—only when people rise, that is, all day; and it seems so we expound it; and why does one who reads all day not lose the blessings, except because he needs to adjoin redemption to prayer, and prayer's time is until the fourth hour like the daily offering—therefore Kriat Shema's time is three hours, and they based it on the verse "when you rise" at the hour people stand, etc.; and I wondered at these holy words—for certainly the exposition of "when you rise" is a complete exposition like "when you lie down"; and furthermore here he ruled that after four hours he lost the blessings; and the objection seems to me to resolve: specifically regarding lying down they said "while they lie down," for all the time one lies down the name of lying does not change—unlike rising, for the name of rising changes to sitting and walking; and if so, it is not called "rise" except at the beginning of rising, as mentioned—obvious:
