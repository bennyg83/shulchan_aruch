Although Peri Chadash follows Rambam, who placed no fixed limit upon the blessings, most later authorities, indeed almost all of them, rule like the Rosh, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch. See Responsa Mishkenos Yaakov, siman 77, who inclines to rule after the fact that the blessings may be recited until midday, as with the Amidah. We rule below in siman 89 that one who missed the proper time for the Amidah may nevertheless pray until midday. See there. Perhaps this may be relied upon when one was prevented by circumstances beyond his control from reciting the blessings before the end of four hours. I found in Maharil, Laws of Prayer, that he writes in the name of Maharam Schick that the custom is to be lenient for one who was prevented by circumstances beyond his control, so that he does not lose the blessings.
