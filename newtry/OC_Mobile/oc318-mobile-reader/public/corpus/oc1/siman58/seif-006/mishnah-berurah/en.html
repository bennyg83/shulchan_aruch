(24) He read it—even negligently; and it seems straightforward that what he wrote "and did not read it" does not mean only that; rather even if he read it entirely beforehand in its time without its blessings—for extra emphasis he recorded it, lest one say since he did not read it at all until now it is worse; and so explicitly in Beit Yosef at the end of siman 46—see there; and in responsum Meshkenot Yaakov siman 77 he doubts this where at the outset he read it all—see there.

(25) The fourth hour—for the blessings are not connected to Keriat Shema, for although they were enacted before Keriat Shema, nevertheless they are not a blessing of Keriat Shema—for behold he does not bless "Who has commanded us to read the Shema"; and behold they are like prayer, and therefore their law is like Shacharit prayer which is only until a third of the day.
