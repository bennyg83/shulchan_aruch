Even though its time continues, etc. The Beit Yosef wrote in chapter 1: Some ask—what is the reason they expounded thus regarding Keriat Shema of the night [i.e., "when you lie down" means as long as people lie down]—regarding Keriat Shema of the day too let us expound, and "when you rise" [means] as long as people rise, i.e., all day? And one may answer that it is not so, for we say one who reads from here onward has not lost the blessings—and if it were not the time of Keriat Shema, his blessings would be void; and that which they say "at the hour when people rise" is merely a support in the world—know that are the sons of kings the majority of the world? Rather, certainly as we said—end quote. And his words require investigation: one, that the blessings are not connected to Keriat Shema as written in siman 60, and if so there is no proof from the blessings; and there is a difficulty—if so, even after the fourth hour he should recite the blessings; and further, according to his words Keriat Shema is biblically [obligatory] and its time is all day and all night, and it is a positive commandment whose time is not the cause—as with tefillah as written in siman 106 seif 2; whereas in the Gemara it is explicitly a positive commandment whose time is the cause. And it seems to me the difficulty from the outset does not exist: for granted, in "when you lie down" they explained "as long as" that he is lying down; but rising is not called except when one stands up from his bed—but afterward it is not called rising, only standing or sitting or walking—and so is in Sefer Chinuch and so too in Mishnah Berurah ch. 5 seif 45; and what he wrote from the sons of kings—so too from this in Sefer HaShachar.
