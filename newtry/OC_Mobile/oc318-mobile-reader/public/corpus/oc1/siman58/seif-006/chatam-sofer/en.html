(there) In the author, seif 6: the fourth hour and he did not read—52: this wording is difficult in Tur and Beit Yosef, for it appears that according to what we hold later at the beginning of siman 89 that prayer, its time is until chatzot bedi'eved, the same applies to these blessings; and it is a fortiori, for prayer we certainly hold like R. Yitzchak that its time is until the fourth hour, and nevertheless one prays until chatzot—all the more so blessings, which are uncertain per the Rosh whether they are comparable to prayer or not—all the more so it should be permitted to say them until chatzot; except that he lost the reward of blessings at their [proper] time, as in my humble opinion.
