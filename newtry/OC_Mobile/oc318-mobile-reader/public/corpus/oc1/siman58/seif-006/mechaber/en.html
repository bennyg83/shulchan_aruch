Even though its [proper] time continues until the end of the third hour, if' the third hour passed and one did not read it, one may read it with its berakhot all the fourth hour, since it is [within] a third of the day; and [if one does this] one does not have reward like one who reads in its [proper] time. And if the fourth hour passed and one did not read it, one may read it without its berakhot all day.
