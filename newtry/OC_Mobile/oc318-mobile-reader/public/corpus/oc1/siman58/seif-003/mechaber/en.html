Whoever is unavoidably prevented—such as one' who rose early to travel to a place where there are bands of wild animals and bandits such that one cannot stand and concentrate even for the first section until "upon your heart," or members of the caravan are going quickly and will not wait for him at all—may recite it with its blessings from when the column of dawn arises, since it is fitting that we call daybreak "when you rise" and it is also fitting to call it "Yotzer Or." But if one is not in a place with bands of wild animals or bandits and the members of the caravan are not so rushed, even if one set out to travel after daybreak, one does not recite until its time arrives.
