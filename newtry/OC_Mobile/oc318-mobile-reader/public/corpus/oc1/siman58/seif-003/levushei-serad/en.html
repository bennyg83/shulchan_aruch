There. In Darkei Moshe, where he wrote that there is not. Although the Darkei Moshe wrote there that the truth is like the explanation of Maharai, that refers to his explanation of the Rambam's phrase 'when the sun rises' as the end of its rising. But Maharai's view that the Rambam counts the hour from the beginning of the reading, as is explicit from Maharai's question, the Darkei Moshe disputes; the Rambam speaks only from the beginning of netz, as written here in the gloss. See there in Darkei Moshe, where this is explicit, and not like the Chidushei Hagahos around the Tur, who objected to these words of the Magen Avraham; see there, for there is no difficulty. What the Magen Avraham wrote, 'and so wrote in Darkei Moshe,' although he differs from the Bach in explaining the Rambam's words, the Magen Avraham means that regarding the textual reading he agrees with the Bach, and likewise that netz hachamah means the beginning of netz. This is clear.
