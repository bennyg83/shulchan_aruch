There—in Darkhei Moshe he wrote that although he wrote in Darkhei Moshe there that the truth is like the explanation of Mahari Abuhav—that is, the matter of what he explains that "until the sun rises" in Rambam means the end of its rise—but what Mahari Abuhav holds, that Rambam counts the hour from the beginning of its reading as explained explicitly from Mahari Abuhav's difficulties—Darkhei Moshe disagrees, that Rambam deals only from the beginning of netz, as written here in the hagahah; see there—in Darkhei Moshe it is explained thus explicitly, and not like the novelties of the hagahot surrounding the Tur that challenged these words of Magen Avraham; see there. And for our purposes: what Magen Avraham wrote "and so too in Darkhei Moshe"—although he disputes Bach in explaining the words of Rambam, only Magen Avraham says that regarding the girsa he agrees with Bach, and likewise regarding hanetz hachama its meaning is the beginning of netz, and it is simple.
