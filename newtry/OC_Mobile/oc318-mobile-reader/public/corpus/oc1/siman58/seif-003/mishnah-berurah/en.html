Compelled. The same applies where there is a burial in the city, or a milah, or Hoshana Rabbah, when they increase supplications and he must begin early; he may read it from alot hashachar.
Rises early. Meaning that he has no time to wait and delay until the time when one can see, such as if he must set out on the road; the same applies to other similar matters where he has no time, as stated in the Tur.
At all. If he wants to read in the middle of the road. It is speaking where his travel with the caravan, or anything similar, will continue until the end of the time of Krias Shema.
He may, etc. He should also pray at home after Krias Shema and its blessings.
To read it. He should put on tefillin, but should not bless on them before the time arrives when one can see his fellow at a distance of four amos and recognize him, as above in siman 30.
Yotzer Or. The Magen Avraham and Peri Megadim ruled that one should not say Yotzer Or so early, until one can distinguish between blue and white, which is the time when one can see his fellow at a distance of four amos. The Biur HaGra also inclines to the Magen Avraham. In any case, initially one must wait if he can, because of the mitzvah of tefillin, as above.
