"And one who is under compulsion"—see Mishnah Berurah in the name of Magen Avraham; and he speaks of "with its blessings," similar to the others. And it is difficult regarding tefillin: how will he put them on then before the time of putting them on, as above in siman 30; and this is not similar to what we copied in s.k. 16 in the name of Pri Megadim, for Pri Megadim deals with one who rose early to set out on a journey as explained in Shulchan Arukh, and there the Sages were lenient to put them on before the time and to feel them when the time arrives and to bless on them, as above in siman 30 seif 3; unlike here; and it is better that one act as I wrote earlier in the section "the time of Kriat Shema" regarding one for whom the hour is pressing—see there.

"Until your heart"—see what Magen Avraham challenged and resolved on this; and see later in siman 89 seif 8 in the hagahah; and in Levush one verse is also mentioned, and it is difficult; and it appears that the Gra had this in mind, and it is astonishing regarding Magen Avraham that he did not mention this.
