One who is under duress. See Mishnah Berurah in the name of Magen Avraham. He means that Krias Shema is recited together with its blessings, as in the other cases. This requires examination regarding tefillin. How can he put them on then, before their proper time, as discussed above in siman 30? This is not comparable to what we cited in seif katan 16 in the name of Peri Megadim. The Peri Megadim addresses one who arose early to travel, as described in the Shulchan Aruch, where Chazal permitted him to put them on before the proper time, touch them when the time arrives, and then recite the blessing, as stated above in siman 30, seif 3. That leniency does not necessarily apply here. It is preferable that he act as I wrote earlier, s.v. “The time for Krias Shema,” regarding one who is pressed for time. See there.
Until “upon your heart.” See Magen Avraham's question and resolution. See also below, siman 89, seif 8, in the gloss. Levush also mentions one verse there. This requires study. It appears that Biur HaGra intended to address this there. It is surprising that Magen Avraham did not mention it.
