Section 63: "And who [recites at night]," etc. From what is written in chapter 8 paragraph 2: the halakhah follows R. Shimon—Beit Yosef—and they challenged: behold we hold like "others" and like the vatikin; and the Rif and Shakh explained that there it deals with bedi'eved or in a time of pressure, such as one who rose early to set out on a journey and the like, as is written in the other [passage] of R. Shimon ben Yochai "when he came," etc., in a time of pressure; and they challenged on this from what is written: per the view of the one who says one who rose early, etc., when he arrives, etc.; and Rashba answered that that [passage] in the first chapter [deals with one who] fulfills [his obligation] in a place, etc.; and R. Yonah explained that the members of the caravan, etc., as we wrote; and that which we say in Yoma there "one who recites with," etc., means lekhatchilah and one does not fulfill the mitzvah in the optimal manner—Beit Yosef; and see Tosafot 12a s.v. "because," etc., and there is a textual corruption and it should read as Yoma says "one who recites," etc., and as Rashi writes there: there "and perhaps the baraita," etc., means the baraita of Yoma mentioned above, meaning and he does not hold that of R. Shimon ben Yochai; and likewise it is proven explicitly in the first chapter of Yerushalmi Berakhot that the baraita mentioned above argues on R. Shimon ben Yochai, for there it says on R. Shimon ben Yochai that R. Gamliel comes—R. Gamliel like R. Shimon, for he taught in the name of R. Shimon, etc., "one [hour] before [sunrise]," etc.—behold R. Gamliel like R. Shimon: in the evening, likewise in the morning; or it should be as R. Zeira showed Rav Chana, etc., "one who recites with the men of the watch," etc.; and from this is resolved the objection of Razah on the Rif from the baraita mentioned above, for the Rif's reason is that he ruled like R. Shimon ben Yochai who argues on that baraita; and likewise it is proven in the Gemara there that R. Yehoshua ben Levi [said] the halakhah is like R. Shimon ben Yochai; and at first glance, who argues on R. Shimon ben Yochai, for he says "halakhah"—as is written in Knesset haGedolah: "halakhah" implies, etc.; if so, that baraita argues on him as mentioned; and R. Yehoshua ben Levi ruled like R. Shimon ben Yochai, and therefore the Rif ruled like him; but Tur explains that that [ruling] of R. Shimon ben Yochai is in a casual [case], but as a regular practice one does not fulfill even bedi'eved; and in such a case that [passage] of Yoma deals; and this is Tur's meaning; and likewise if one read bedi'eved one has fulfilled and need not read a second time, provided one is not accustomed to do so; and likewise it appears from the words of Rashba and Shulchan Arukh.

"And likewise it is fine," etc. And he derives from "the men of the watch"—R. Yehoshua ben Levi—who said Yotzer Or; and it is difficult, for the conclusion of the Gemara there is that they would say Ahavah rabbah and not Yotzer Or because its time had not arrived; and likewise in Yerushalmi; and likewise ruled Rambam; and according to Tosafot's explanation it is not in accordance with the halakhah.
