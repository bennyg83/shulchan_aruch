[3] Like the vatikin, etc. Rashi explained: humble ones. It appears to me that it is from the expression regarding one who drinks white shade, that weakness seizes him, and Rashi explains it as weakness. Rabbeinu Yerucham explained that vatikin means sharp and penetrating scholars, from the expression 'uprooting mountains,' which the Targum renders as sweetening the mountains. Beer HaGolah wrote that vatikin means students.
