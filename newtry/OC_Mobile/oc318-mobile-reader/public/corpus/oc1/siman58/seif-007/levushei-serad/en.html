Seif katan 3—the Gemara "deyakhol." The language of the Gemara: it was taught—Rashbi says: sometimes a person reads Keriat Shema twice at night, once before alot hashachar and once after alot hashachar, and fulfills one of the night and one of the day. And Rosh wrote: specifically one before alot hashachar and one after alot hashachar, but not two after alot hashachar. And on this Taz wrote: there is no novelty in this except that the main novelty is that one can read two times adjacent, etc., and do not challenge why the Tur did not write at least this matter of Rashbi—therefore he answered that since Ravina revealed to us, etc.
