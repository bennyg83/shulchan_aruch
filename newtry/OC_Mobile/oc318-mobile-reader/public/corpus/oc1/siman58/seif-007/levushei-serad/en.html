Seif katan 3. The Talmud says he can. The language of the Gemara is: it was taught, Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai says that sometimes a person reads Krias Shema twice in one night, one before alot hashachar and one after alot hashachar, and fulfills his obligation, one for night and one for day. On this the Rosh wrote that this is specifically one before alot hashachar and one after alot hashachar, but not two after alot hashachar. On this the Taz wrote that there is no novelty in that; rather, the main novelty is that he can read two times close together, etc. So that you should not ask why the Tur did not at least write this ruling of Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai, he answered that since Rabbeinu revealed to us, etc.
