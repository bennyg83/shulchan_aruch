(Seif katan 7) Although, etc. They are not connected to Krias Shema, as written in siman 60, that if one read Krias Shema without its blessings, he returns and says the blessings without Krias Shema according to the law. The reason was written by the Rashba, cited by Beis Yosef: although the blessings before Krias Shema were instituted, nevertheless they are not blessings of Krias Shema, for he does not bless 'Who commanded us to read the Shema.'
For if so, even after the fourth hour he should read the blessings. In truth, according to all poskim, except the Rambam, who holds that one blesses the blessings all day, after four hours one may not say the blessings. According to the Kesef Mishneh, what is the reason? Since the time of the blessings depends on the time of Krias Shema according to the Torah, and according to the Torah the time of Krias Shema is all day, he would likewise bless the blessings all day.
It is a positive commandment not caused by time, like prayer. Meaning, do not say that it is still a time-caused positive commandment, since in any event the nighttime Shema is not during the day and the daytime Shema is not during the night. This is not so, for the time of Krias Shema, combining night and day, is both by day and by night. Prayer is also this way, for the time of night prayer is not by day and the time of day prayer is not by night, yet it is called not time-caused, since the times of prayer together are at all times.
And what he wrote from princes. Meaning, what the Kesef Mishneh brought as proof that their time according to the Torah is all day from the fact that the time of Krias Shema extends until the end of the third hour, because princes rise at three hours. Are princes the majority of the world? On this the Magen Avraham wrote: I already wrote in seif 5, seif katan 6, a resolution to this.
