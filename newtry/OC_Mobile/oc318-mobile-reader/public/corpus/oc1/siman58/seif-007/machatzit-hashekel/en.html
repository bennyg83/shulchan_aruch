(Seif katan 7) Although, etc.—they are not connected to Keriat Shema as written in siman 60—that if one read Keriat Shema without blessings, he returns and reads the blessings without Keriat Shema from the perspective of law. And the reason Rashba wrote, which Beit Yosef brought, and said: and although the blessings before Keriat Shema were enacted, nevertheless they are not blessings of Keriat Shema—for behold he does not bless "Who has commanded us to read the Shema," etc.

If so, even after the fourth hour he would read the blessings—and in truth for all the poskim (except Rambam who holds one blesses the blessings all day), after four hours one may not say the blessings. And for Kesef Mishnah, what is the reason? Since they tied the time of blessings to the time of Keriat Shema from the Torah, and since from the Torah the time of Keriat Shema is all day—likewise one blesses the blessings all day.

And this would be a positive commandment whose time is not the cause—not like tefillah. Meaning do not say it is still a positive commandment whose time is not the cause—for in any case of the night its time is not in the day, and likewise of the day its time is not at night. For if nevertheless in any case the time of Keriat Shema combining night to day exists both in day and in night—for behold tefillah is also like this, that the time of evening prayer is not in the day and morning prayer is not at night—and nevertheless it is called a positive commandment whose time is not the cause, since in any case the time of the prayers together are at all times.

And what he wrote "from the sons of kings." Meaning what Kesef Mishnah brought—that their time from the Torah is all day, from that the time of Keriat Shema extends until the end of the third hour, and the sons of kings stand three hours. And are the sons of kings the majority of the world? And therefore Magen Avraham—I already wrote in seif 5 seif katan 6 will resolve this.
