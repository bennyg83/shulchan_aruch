7) There: The preferred performance of the mitzvah is to recite it like the vasikin, etc. There are people who rise early on winter mornings to snatch Krias Shema and prayer in darkness and gloom, and they call it the prayer of the vasikin. Many unlearned people act arrogantly and pursue reward in this manner, yet no radiance shines upon them; "in darkness they walk." They follow neither this authority nor that authority and do not fulfill their obligation at all. Their practice is nothing but arrogance and an excessive spirit. These are youngsters or elders who are not precise, and they hang empty vessels upon the vasikin. Although we have seen elderly sages who acted this way, they were far more precise, knew the law well, and were highly expert in calculating the proper time, for all their deeds were for the sake of Heaven. Not everyone who wishes to assume the title may do so. Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 1, in the name of Mor U'Ketziah. See Mor U'Ketziah there, where he writes at length in open rebuke of this practice. See also Lev Chaim, volume 2, siman 19, concerning this. A further consequence of this early rising in darkness and gloom is rivalry between one person and another: whoever begins and finishes first is deemed praiseworthy. This also leads them to omit letters, words, and the intentions of the Divine Names. Let the living take this too to heart, so that it not become a stumbling block on the Day of Judgment, God forbid. See what we wrote above in siman 5, with Heaven's help, paragraphs 1, 2, and 5.
