(7) And mitzvah min hamuvchar to recite it like the vatikim, etc. There are early risers in the morning in winter days to snatch Keriat Shema and tefillah in darkness and gloom, and they call it "tefillat vatikin," and many commoners pride themselves and chase reward thereby—and no brightness shines upon them; "in darkness they walk" (Job 12:25)—neither like this master (the vatikim) nor like that master have they fulfilled at all, and it is only arrogance and excessive spirit. And they are childish or elderly who are not precise—they hang empty nonsense on vatikin. And although we have seen elderly rabbis who did thus—they were more precise and knew and were very expert to fix the time properly, for all their deeds were for the sake of Heaven—but not everyone who wishes to take the Name may take it. Yafeh LaLev sign 1 in the name of Moreh Uketzi, and see there in Moreh Uketzi what he elaborated in open rebuke on this; and further see in Sefer Lev Chaim vol. 2 siman 19 what he wrote on this. And further continuing from this: early risers in morning, darkness and gloom—jealousy of one man against his fellow; and everyone who is quick to begin and finish earlier—this is praiseworthy, and thereby they will also come to omit letters and words and kavanot of the names. "Let the living give his heart" also to this, that this not be a stumbling-block on the day of judgment, God forbid. And see above siman 5 in seder sign 1 and sign 2 and sign 5.
