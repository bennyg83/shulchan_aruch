That it has compensation. The intent is not that he fulfills Krias Shema through this, since its time has already passed and he is only like one reading the Torah. Rather, initially he must complete it like prayer; therefore he must first give precedence to the obligation of the present time, and immediately after Maariv he should read Shema again for the time he missed, if he missed it inadvertently, as explained below in siman 108.
And there are those who disagree. The Gra and the Birkei Yosef ruled like this opinion.
