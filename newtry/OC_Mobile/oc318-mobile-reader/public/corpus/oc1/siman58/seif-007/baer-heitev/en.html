At Maariv. He must first fulfill the obligation presently incumbent upon him. Magen Avraham. See Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 3 of Hilchos Tefillah, who raises a difficulty there. I am surprised by his question, for we have an established teaching: ‘That which is crooked cannot be made straight,’ referring to one who omitted Krias Shema. See there. His intention is to challenge the opinion that maintains that Krias Shema can be made up. With due respect, however, he did not examine the sugya carefully at the beginning of the chapter Tefillas HaShachar. There the Gemara concludes that the teaching refers to one who deliberately omitted it, and Krias Shema is equated with prayer, as the Gemara states, ‘one who omitted Krias Shema or prayer.’ It answers concerning this that he omitted it deliberately. Examine this carefully. See Yad Aharon and Peri Chadash.
