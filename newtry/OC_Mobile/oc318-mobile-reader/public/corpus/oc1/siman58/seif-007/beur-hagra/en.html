Seif 7, If he did not, etc.; some say, etc. This view is derived from the fact that the Gemara groups the two cases together there on 26a. It understands that the answer, “when it was intentional,” applies to both.
And some disagree, etc. Prayer is different, as stated there: “Since it is a plea for mercy,” etc. Nevertheless, this requires study: what concept of compensation applies here, and what prohibition is involved, since he is merely like one reading from the Torah?
