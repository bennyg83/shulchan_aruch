Section 67: if he did not call it vatikin. Some say, etc. From that they drew them on one stroke there 26a; and he explained that regarding intentional [violation] he holds that it refers to both of them.

And there are those who disagree, etc. For granted prayer—as is written there—since it is mercy, etc.; but nevertheless it is difficult: what "make-up" applies here, and what prohibition is there since it is only like one who reads in the Torah.
