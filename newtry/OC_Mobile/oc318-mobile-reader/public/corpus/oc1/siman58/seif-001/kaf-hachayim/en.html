(1) [Seif 1] The time of the morning Kriat Shema, etc. And one blesses two before it and one after it; and in the evening two before it and two after it; and they based them on the verse "seven times a day I praise You," etc.—Tur; and see in Sefer haTikkunim end of tikkun 12 the reason for these seven blessings; and the reason they arranged four blessings in the evening and three in the morning—Levush wrote: because in the order of time evening preceded morning, as it is written "and there was evening and there was morning," etc., therefore they took the four—which are the majority—for the evening, which comes first, and left the remaining three for the morning—so his words; and R. Avudraham folio 1b wrote the reason in the name of Raavad: they gave the extra to the evening because the evening prayer is optional and we want them to be equal. And Beit David prepared Hagahat Tashbetz. And Rokeach siman 318 wrote: because in the morning there is the mitzvah of tzitzit, unlike in the evening. And they brought Hagahat Beit Yosef letter 1; Avodat haGolah letter 1. And see Sha'ar haKavanot in the discourse on the evening prayer 52, 53 s.v. Hashkivenu; and Peri Etz Chayim gate of Minchah and Ma'ariv chapters 4 and 5.
