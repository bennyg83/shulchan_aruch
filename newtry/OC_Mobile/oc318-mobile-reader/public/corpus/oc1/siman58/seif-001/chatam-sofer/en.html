In Magen Avraham s.k. 1—folio 3a in Tosafot 52—see Derekh haChayim; and in my humble opinion there is no necessity to say: one who sleeps in a dark house when he hears a woman conversing with her husband knows that amud ha-shachar has risen, and he calculates from there the measure of an hour and a quarter; and it is known that then is sunrise; and likewise from that of King David—peace upon him—is no proof, for if so he preceded by more than two watches, and in total two hundred maneh.
