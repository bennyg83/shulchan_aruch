In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1: Page 3a in Tosafos. Gloss: See Dagul MeRevavah. In my humble opinion, there is no necessary proof. One who sleeps in a dark house could know that dawn had risen when he heard a woman conversing with her husband; from that point he could calculate one and one-quarter hours, knowing that sunrise would then occur. The conduct of King David, peace be upon him, is likewise no proof, because he may indeed have risen even earlier than two watches before morning; a quantity of two hundred includes one hundred.
