Three hours. And here according to all we count from alot hashachar. Those who delay Keriat Shema on account of tzitzit and tefillin err; rather he should recite Keriat Shema in its time with its blessings, and when he has tzitzit and tefillin he puts them on and reads with them Keriat Shema or another parashah or a psalm of Tehillim for wrapping. And Eliyahu of Tzfas wrote: specifically if the time of Keriat Shema will pass—then he should not wait for tallit and tefillin since they do not impede each other; but otherwise he should wait, for anyone who recites Keriat Shema without tefillin is as one who testifies false testimony against himself.

One. And some read as "like a tenth of one hour"—and so is the principal view. Peri Chadash, Minchat Kohen, and Magen Avraham.
