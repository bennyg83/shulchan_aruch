Three hours. Here, according to all opinions, the hours are counted from dawn. Magen Avraham. Those who delay Krias Shema because they are waiting for tzitzis and tefillin are mistaken. Rather, one should recite Krias Shema with its blessings at the proper time, and when he later obtains tzitzis and tefillin he should put them on and recite Krias Shema, another passage, or a psalm of Tehillim while wearing them. Levush. Olas Tamid writes that this applies specifically where the time for Krias Shema will otherwise pass. In that case he should not wait for a tallis and tefillin, since the mitzvos do not prevent one another. Otherwise, however, he should wait, for whoever recites Krias Shema without tefillin is considered as though he bears false testimony against himself.
One. Some versions read, ‘approximately one tenth of an hour,’ and this is the primary reading. Peri Chadash and Minchas Kohen. See Magen Avraham.
