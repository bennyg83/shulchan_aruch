That it is a fourth of the day. Whether it is long or whether it is short—therefore one must be careful in winter days to hurry with Keriat Shema, since the day is short.

His reward is very great and he is not harmed all that day (Berakhot ch. 1).

The measure of hanetz hachama is like the measure of one hour; and in some versions in the words of Rambam, in place of "measure of one hour" it is written "a tenth of an hour." But the principal matter is: from alot hashachar until the sun rises upon the earth is a walk of four mil, which is one hour and a fifth; therefore from alot hashachar until hanetz hachama would be only a fifth of the hour—and this is not in the meaning [of the sources]. So too Beit Yosef.
