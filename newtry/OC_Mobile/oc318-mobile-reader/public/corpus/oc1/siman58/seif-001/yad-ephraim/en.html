In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1: It appears to me that here all agree, etc. His intent appears to be that in siman 243 some calculate the day from sunrise until sunset; see there. He therefore writes that here all agree that we count from dawn. The Gemara asks, 'But it is daytime,' and Tosafos there explains that the end of the night watch is dawn. Regarding the question from David, the Gemara answers that he follows R' Yehoshua: six hours of the night and two of the day, etc.; see there. Thus, immediately at the end of the watch, which is dawn, the three-hour period begins. Magen Avraham means that this is proven by combining the Gemara's discussion of David with Tosafos's explanation. The Gemara alone provides no proof, for one could have said that the watch ends at sunrise.
