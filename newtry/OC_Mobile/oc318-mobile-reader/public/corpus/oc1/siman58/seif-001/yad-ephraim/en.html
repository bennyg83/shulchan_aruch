In Magen Avraham s.k. 1—it seems in my humble opinion that here for everyone, etc.; it appears his intention: per siman 243 that some count from netz ha-chamah until sheki'ah—see there; and therefore he wrote here for everyone we count from alot ha-shachar; for in the Gemara it challenges "it is daytime" and in Tosafot there it is explained that the end of the watch is the time of alot ha-shachar; and regarding that which it challenges from David—we say he held like R. Yehuda, etc., "six of night and two of day," etc.—see there; behold immediately at the end of the watch, which is like alot ha-shachar, the time of three hours begins; and the intention is that from the Gemara on that which it challenges from David, combined with the words of Tosafot, it is proven thus; and from the Gemara alone there is no proof—it was possible to say the watch ends at netz ha-chamah:
