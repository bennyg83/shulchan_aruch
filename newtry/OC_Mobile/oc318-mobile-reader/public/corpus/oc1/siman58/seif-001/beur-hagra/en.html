[Seif] 1—time of Keriat Shema, etc. As Tosafot [wrote] on Yoma 37b, that Abbaye concedes to Rav Ashi that the halakhah follows Others, only that it is a mitzvah min hamuvchar like the vatikim in order to adjoin, etc., and before then one cannot adjoin—and so Tur and Rosh, unlike Rambam who ruled absolutely like Abbaye, and so [is clear] in Rif.

A slightly familiar [friend], etc. Yerushalmi, brought in Tosafot on Berakhot there, and Shulchan Arukh of the Rav [Rema].

And it continues, etc. They concluded in Gemara there that halakhah is like Rabbi Yehoshua, and even the vatikim concede to Rabbi Yehoshua, as written there 25b. Tosafot there and there, and Shulchan Arukh of the Rav, as written in Yerushalmi there: "in order that he be at a distance of four amot," as one says between tekhelet and white; but they said its mitzvah is with hanetz hachama in order, etc., and so in Tosefta ch. 1: Others say, in order that his fellow be at a distance of four amot and recognize him—its mitzvah is with hanetz hachama in order that he adjoin to it, etc.

End of three hours, etc. Rambam and Hagahot Maimuni and Shulchan Arukh of the Rav, that the language "three hours" implies all three hours, for otherwise it should have said "until the third hour." And in Yerushalmi ch. 1: Rabbi Zeira in the name of Rabbi Ami—in the days of Rabbi Yochanan we would go out to fast and read Shema after three hours, and Rabbi Yosei and Rabbi Acha would not rebuke us. Rabbi Yosei and Rabbi Acha went out to fast; the congregation came and read Keriat Shema after three hours. Rabbi Acha asked to rebuke them; Rabbi Yosei said to him: but they already read it in its time—do they read it only in order to stand in prayer from words of Torah? He said to him: because of the am ha'aretz who might say they read it in its time. And in ch. 4 there: Rabbi Yosei prays in the third hour; Rabbi Chiya bar Abba prays in the third hour; Rabbi Berekhiah, his mother-in-law, read Keriat Shema and prayed after three hours—and we learned "one who reads from here onward has not lost," etc. He already accepted what is written "in its time," and in Gemara 7a: in those first three hours, etc.—unlike Ra'avad and Rashi, who wrote until the beginning of the third hour literally, as written "three" [hours] and two-thirds of the day. See Tosafot Avodah Zarah 4b s.v. "in three," etc., and thereby their proof is hidden that all of the third hour stands, and in Hagahot Maimuni a variant reading "four amot," etc.

That it is, etc. And Hagahot AGR wrote that it is found in midrash that on the day Yehoshua stopped the sun, it was the season of Tammuz, and we say in tractate Avodah Zarah 25a, etc., and see above siman 459 seif 1.

To recite it a little, etc. So Rambam and Tur explained that this is what is written "they finish," etc., unlike Rosh who explained "they finish" means they read after hanetz hachama, from what is written "they shall fear You with the sun," as Tosafot in his name on Yoma there—and he contradicted his words, for "they shall fear You" refers to tefillah, as written in ch. 4 of Berakhot. And this is what he said "in order that he adjoin," etc., and further in ch. 3 we learned "if he can ascend," etc., and also unlike Rashi who explained not like the vatikim, etc.

"And who [recites at night]," etc. See Tosafot there s.v. "kol," etc.

"The measure, etc." There is a textual corruption here and it should read: the measure is a third of a tenth of an hour.
