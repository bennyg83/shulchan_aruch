Three hours. It seems to me that here according to all we count from alot hashachar, and so in the Gemara page 3a in Tosafot s.v. "rather for one who sleeps," and in the Gemara regarding that which it challenges from David, alot hashachar, and see siman 233.

Measure of an hour. It implies in Beit Yosef from when it begins to shine until the entire body of the sun rises is an hour; and there are who have the girsa like a tenth of an hour, and Kesef Mishnah wrote that this is the principal; and Bach agreed to the first girsa, and so too in Darkhei Moshe who wrote that the explanation is not as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Mahari Abuhav, rather from hanetz hachama until the entire body of the sun rises completely is an hour—and the matter is known to one who knows a little in the science of astronomy, etc. And in Minchat Cohen in Mevo HaShemesh ch. 7 he wrote that the girsa "tenth of an hour" is the principal—see there; and it seems to me that for the law there is no difference between the girsaot—and examine.
