Three hours. It appears to me that here, according to all opinions, we calculate from dawn. This is also implied by the Gemara, 3a, Tosafos s.v. “Rather, according to the one who lies down,” and by the Gemara's discussion of David's statement. See there, and see what I wrote in siman 233.
The measure of one hour. The Beit Yosef implies that from when the sun begins to shine until the entire body of the sun has risen is one hour. Some versions read “approximately one tenth of an hour,” and Kesef Mishneh writes that this is the correct reading. The Bach agrees with the first version. Darkei Moshe writes likewise, explaining that it does not mean what the Beit Yosef cites in the name of Mahari Abuhav, but rather that from sunrise until the entire body of the sun has risen is one hour, a matter known to anyone with even a little knowledge of astronomy. Minchas Kohen, in Mevo HaShemesh, chapter 7, writes that the reading “one tenth of an hour” is correct. It appears to me that, as a matter of law, there is no difference between the readings. Examine this carefully.
