2) There: When he can recognize a person with whom he is somewhat familiar, etc. If he is very familiar with him, he will recognize him even from a great distance, while if he is not familiar with him at all, he will not recognize him even nearby. Rather, this refers to someone like a guest at an inn who is accustomed to come at intervals. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2, in the name of the Yerushalmi, as cited by Beis Yosef.
