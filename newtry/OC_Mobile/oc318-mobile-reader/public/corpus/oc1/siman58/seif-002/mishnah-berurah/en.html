To advance. For the zealous advance themselves to mitzvos. Nevertheless, one need not pray individually for this, nor read without tefillin. See the end of siman 46 in Mishnah Berurah.
Quickly. From here there is open rebuke for the people of Ashkenaz who greatly delay Krias Shema. Chazal said: had the world been created only for acceptance of the kingdom of Heaven, it would suffice. See below in siman 89, Mishnah Berurah seif katan 22, that it is proper to refrain from drinking tea and coffee in the morning before prayer in company with friends; see there.
