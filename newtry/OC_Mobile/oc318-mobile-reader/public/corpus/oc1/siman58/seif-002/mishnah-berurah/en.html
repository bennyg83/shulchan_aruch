(10) To hurry—for the zealous precede to mitzvot; nevertheless one need not pray individually on account of this, or read without tefillin—and see at the end of siman 46 in Mishnah Berurah.

(11) In haste—from here is open rebuke to Ashkenazic Jews who greatly delay Keriat Shema; and they said: if the world were created only for accepting the kingdom of Heaven it would suffice. And see below in siman 89 in Mishnah Berurah sk 82 that it is proper to refrain from drinking wine and coffee in the morning before prayer in the gathering of friends—see there.
