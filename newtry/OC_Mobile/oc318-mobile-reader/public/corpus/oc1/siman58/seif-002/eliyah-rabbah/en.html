[2] A quarter of the day, etc. Therefore, in the winter days one must hurry to read Krias Shema, for a quarter of the day is short. See Beis Yosef and Kesef Mishneh, who brought proof from the Yerushalmi that it means until the end of three hours. In my humble opinion there is clear proof from our Gemara at the end of the chapter Seder Ta'anis, which says that before nine they should not complete the fast, as Rashi explains there, and this requires study. The Levush wrote the reason: it is the way of princes to lie in bed until three hours, and all Jews are called princes, the children of Avraham, etc., and it is written, 'when you rise.' This is surprising, for Maharai already wrote that although we do not rule that all Jews are princes, nevertheless, since it is still the time of rising for some people, one fulfills the obligation after the fact; and so we rule below in siman 327. The Magen Avraham wrote that these three hours, according to everyone, are counted from alot hashachar. As to why we do not expound 'when you rise' as long as people are lying down, meaning the entire night, one may answer that the expression rising applies only when one rises from his bed; afterward it is called standing, sitting, or walking. So wrote the Taz and Magen Avraham, and they challenged the Kesef Mishneh, for the Tumas Yesharim, siman 13, escaped their notice; see there, for I have abbreviated.
