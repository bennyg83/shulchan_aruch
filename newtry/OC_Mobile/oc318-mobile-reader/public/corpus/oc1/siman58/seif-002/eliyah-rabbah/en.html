[2] "A fourth of the day," etc. Therefore in winter days one should hurry to recite Kriat Shema, because the fourth of the day is short; and see Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh who brought proof from Yerushalmi that until the end of three hours he speaks. And in my humble opinion there is clear proof from our Talmud at the end of chapter Seder Ta'anit, which says: before nine they do not finish—and Rashi explained—see there; and it requires examination. Levush wrote the reason: the way of the sons of kings is to lie until three hours; and all Israel are called sons of kings, sons of Avraham, etc., and it is written "when you rise"—until here. And it is astonishing, for Mahari already wrote: even though we do not hold that all Israel are sons of kings, nevertheless since it is still the time of rising for some people, one fulfills bedi'eved; and thus we hold later in siman 287. Magen Avraham wrote: these three hours for everyone we count from alot ha-shachar—until here. And that which we do not expound "when you rise" all the time people lie—that is, all night—and one may say that the language of rising is only when one stands from his bed; but afterward it is called standing, sitting, walking; so wrote Taz and Magen Avraham; and they challenged Kesef Mishneh, for he omitted for them Tomer Devorah siman 13—see there, for I abbreviated.
