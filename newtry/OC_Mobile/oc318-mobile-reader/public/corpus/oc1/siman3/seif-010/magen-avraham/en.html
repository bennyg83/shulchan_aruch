“With the right hand.” Because with the right one indicates Torah trop, and the Gemara gives another reason; for all those reasons a left-handed person [it’ter] wipes with his left hand, which counts as most people’s “right.” But Rava said: because the Torah was given from the right—“From His right hand a fiery law for them”—therefore even a left-handed person may not wipe with “the world’s right” [his own left hand, which for him is the primary hand, parallel to others’ right]. This requires study: does halacha follow Rava as the later authority, or perhaps since all the Tannaim disagree with him the halacha does not follow him—so it appears to me [kenireh li]. What the Taz wrote—that a lefty wipes with most people’s left, as Beit Yosef siman 43—is not correct: “lefty” is not mentioned there. Moreover it appears to me [ve’od nireh li]: if one writes with his left and does other tasks with his right, he should wipe with his right [most people’s right], for two reasons apply—he writes with that hand and ties tefillin on it. Shelah wrote: one should not wipe with the middle finger around which he wraps a tefillin strap—as in siman 27 s. 8.
