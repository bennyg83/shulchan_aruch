[י] “So they are accustomed.” See Levush; Levushei Yom Tov wrote that lands’ nature or times may change, as in Even HaEzer 156—end of quotation. Shelah 79a, Shabbat chapter HaMotzi: three acts empower sorcerers against one—(1) pulling spit from a cluster, (2) killing a louse on one’s clothes, (3) wiping with pottery—therefore “guard your soul” and avoid them—end of quotation. They also warned: three do not pass between nor squeeze between—dog, palm, woman; some add pig. If it happens, Rav Pappa said open with a Divine Name and close with a Name—“What has G-d wrought…” or open with “lo” and close with “lo” like “not a man of G-d…” until “she shall not establish”—he listed many dangers, e.g. relieving between a palm and a wall without four amot between—see there. See later siman 170.
