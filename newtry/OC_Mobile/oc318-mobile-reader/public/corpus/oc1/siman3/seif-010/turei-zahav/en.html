With the right hand — Many reasons are stated in the Gemara: (1) because one binds tefillin on it; (2) because people are accustomed to bring food to the mouth with it; (3) because one shows Torah trop marks with it. For these reasons, if he is left-handed he wipes with his right. But there is a fourth reason in the Gemara — because the Torah was given with the right hand; therefore even for a left-handed person we follow the right hand of every person, and he must wipe with the left (meaning of every person). And one must say that even for the third reason, nevertheless it is permitted to show Torah trop marks with the left even though it is designated for wiping, since a left-handed person wipes with the left as stated, even though he shows Torah trop marks with it, as above. But Beit Yosef wrote in siman 43 that it is forbidden to wipe with the right; therefore one must say that a left-handed person also wipes with the right (meaning of every person), for his left is their right, and this is proper even for the fourth reason.
