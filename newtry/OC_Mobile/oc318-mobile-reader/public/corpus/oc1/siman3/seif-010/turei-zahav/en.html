“With the right hand.” — The Gemara gives several reasons: tefillin are tied on the left; one shows Torah trop with the hand; for those reasons a left-handed writer wipes with his “right” (his left for most people). A fourth reason: Torah was given from the right—even a lefty wipes with most people’s left. Still, showing trop with the left should be fine; a lefty may wipe with his left even if he shows trop with it. Beit Yosef in siman 43 forbids wiping with the “right”—so a lefty must wipe with most people’s right, i.e. his own left, satisfying all reasons.
