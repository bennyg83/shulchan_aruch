(s.k. 10) “Hemorrhoids,” “if he wiped…”—Gra, siman 306:1, on the law explained in siman 12: Chazal permitted bringing stones into a privy on Shabbat for wiping, for human dignity, only when they meet the size explained there; if the stone is marked from prior wiping [ma’eveshayim] so it is clearly prepared for wiping, it is permitted in any case. The question: if it was already used once, how may one wipe with it again on Shabbat—this parallels hemorrhoids here. The Rema’s gloss: one of the three cases Magen Avraham listed—he himself wiped with it, or it dried, or he wiped from another side.
