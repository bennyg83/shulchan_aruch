“He shall not wipe with the right hand.” See Mishnah Berurah. A left-handed person who writes with his left and does his other tasks with his right: Magen Avraham and Levushei Serad hold he wipes with most people’s left—we do not follow his writing hand even though he ties tefillin with it—as below siman 27. If he writes with his right and does other work with his left, one must examine per their reasons—see there. Bekhor Shor implies: if his left hand is accustomed to writing sacred matters and not to indicating Torah trop (see Rashi Berachot 62a—not our custom), that hand counts as his “right” and he does not wipe with it; he wipes with his left, which is everyone else’s right. If he writes holy words with his right and is used to that, and is not used to showing trop, and his other work is with his left, he is like everyone else and wipes with most people’s left. See Biur Halacha who elaborated; per him the law is as we wrote for Magen Avraham and Levushei Serad; if he writes with the right and other work with the left, per him too he is like everyone else.
