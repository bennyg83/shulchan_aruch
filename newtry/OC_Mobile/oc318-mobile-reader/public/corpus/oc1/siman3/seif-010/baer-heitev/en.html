“The right [hand].” Because one binds tefillin with it, and the Gemara gives other reasons; a left-handed person wipes with his left hand, which is everyone else’s right—Magen Avraham. So too from the words of the Taz—see Mageinei Eretz, Amsterdam edition, and the earlier Baer Heitev before me, which wrote in the name of Magen Avraham and the Taz that a left-handed person wipes with his right hand—see there; he was not precise. If he writes with his left and does his other actions with his right, he should also wipe with the right of the world—see Magen Avraham. Shelah wrote that one should not wipe with the middle finger, for one wraps the tefillin strap around it.
