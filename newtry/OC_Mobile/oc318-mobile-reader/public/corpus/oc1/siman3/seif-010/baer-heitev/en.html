“The right [hand].” Because one ties tefillin with it, and the Gemara gives other reasons; a left-handed person wipes with his left hand, which is everyone else’s “right”—Magen Avraham. Similarly from the Taz—see Meginnei Eretz (Amsterdam edition) and earlier Baer Hetev, which cited Magen Avraham and Taz that a lefty wipes with his right hand—see there; that was imprecise. If he writes with his left and does his other tasks with his right, he should also wipe with most people’s right hand—see Magen Avraham. Shelah wrote: one should not wipe with the middle finger around which he wraps a tefillin strap.
