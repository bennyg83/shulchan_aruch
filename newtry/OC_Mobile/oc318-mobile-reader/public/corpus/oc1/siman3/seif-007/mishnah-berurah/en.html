(13) He should not sit with his face, etc. — thus it should read. Taz and other Acharonim.

(14) Rather to the north, etc. — and the same if his back is toward the Sanctuary, urinating is permitted. See Biur HaGra, who agreed that even from the lookout and beyond, if his face is toward the Sanctuary, urinating is forbidden. It is forbidden to stand and relieve oneself openly facing a synagogue or study hall, lest he be among those of whom it is said "their buttocks toward the Temple of the Lord." Similarly one should not make the privy aligned facing a synagogue or study hall so the anus is not exposed toward them [Shulchan Aruch HaRav siman 384, cited in Eliyah Rabbah and glosses of Rav Akiva Eiger]. If one builds a wall in the middle to separate between the privy partition and the synagogue, seemingly one need not be stringent; see Aratzot HaChaim.
