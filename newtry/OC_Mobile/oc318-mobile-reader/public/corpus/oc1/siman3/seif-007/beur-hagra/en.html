Seif 7 "one who urinates..." etc. The correct reading is as written in Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch, not as Kesef Mishnah and the gloss state; rather, where it says "and his face" it should read "and his back," and so too in Tosefta Megillah, last chapter: one who shields his legs—his face toward the Sanctuary; one who urinates—his back toward the Sanctuary. R' Yosi: in what case? From Scopus inward; but from Scopus outward—not required. But the Rambam's words require study, for R' Yosi follows his view, and as stated in the aforementioned Yerushalmi—R' Yosi says, etc.; but R' Akiva forbids everywhere. So too there regarding one who shields his legs—R' Yosi says from Scopus inward; R' Eliezer permits everywhere. So too in the Gemara there—R' Yehuda permits because it was R' Eliezer's view, etc.; another baraita teaches, etc.; and R' Yehuda permits because it was, etc.:
