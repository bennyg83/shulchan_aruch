Ibid.: If so, in Galilee why did the first Tana forbid north–south? He means: since we say a majority is not dragged after a minority, in Galilee where only a small area faces Jerusalem, why forbid Galilean sides north–south when there the sides are a majority? Answer: they divide honor for all Eretz Yisrael, not Jerusalem alone, and it is forbidden because it faces Judah specifically—unlike chutz la’Aretz, where even if we say they honor all Eretz Yisrael, still the sides are a majority—see Tosafos.
