“From the lookout post [toward the Temple].” — A place from which the Temple Mount was visible; inward from there, not. Here Shulchan Aruch says one sits facing the Sanctuary—Rambam, Hilchot Beit Habechirah 7; Kesef Mishneh calls it a scribal error and emends: one who urinates from the lookout inward should not face the Sanctuary but north or south, or turn the Sanctuary aside—so too corrected Rambam manuscripts. Shulchan Aruch should match Beit Yosef’s emendation; perhaps this was written before Kesef Mishneh. Beit Yosef later wrote he explained all this in his Orach Chayim and that here there is a typo to fix—Maharshal agrees Shulchan Aruch needs the same correction.
