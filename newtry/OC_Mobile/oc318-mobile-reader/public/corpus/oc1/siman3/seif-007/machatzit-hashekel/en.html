(s.k. 7) “Between east and west”: Rashi—the Shulchan Aruch does not spell out whether the bed’s length runs east–west or its width; from Rashi it is clear he means the bed’s width lies along the east–west axis.

And Magen Avraham there: head/feet orientation as Zohar; one may also read the Gemara that way—meaning the bed’s length runs east–west. Levush, etc.; alternatively the bedroom is a place of Shechinah—even at relations one should attach his soul to holy matters, as Taz cites from R. Chaim Vital.
