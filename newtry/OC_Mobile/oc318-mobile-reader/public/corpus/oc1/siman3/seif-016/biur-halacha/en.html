“It was not permitted,” etc.—but rubbing [for pleasure] is not. What was permitted for an unmarried man below the corona was only for urinating (Eliyahu Rabbah). See Mishnah Berurah I wrote: he may comb the lower beard—it is no worse than by means of a thick cloth, especially if we hold like Tosafos cited in Magen Avraham that “below the navel” means at the site of the organ.
