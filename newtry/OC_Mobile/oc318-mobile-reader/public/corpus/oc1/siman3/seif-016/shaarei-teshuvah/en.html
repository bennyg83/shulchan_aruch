Bal teshaktzu — Baer Hetev. See Tevuot Shor on Yoreh Deah siman 13 sub-paragraph 12, who was uncertain whether one who holds back his openings and transgresses bal teshaktzu is by Torah law or by rabbinic law; and there he wrote on that passage in Yevamot that five Amoraim uprooted the chapter heading of Rosh Hashanah — see there. See what is written in Responsa Beit Ephraim, Even HaEzer part 2 siman 2 on this; see below siman 92. And Erech Lechem of Maharikash wrote that one who holds back flatulence does not transgress bal teshaktzu — see siman 103, end of his words; see Magen Avraham end of sub-paragraph 4.
