“You shall not make yourselves disgusting” — Baer Hetev. See Tevuot Shor on Yoreh De’ah siman 13 sub-paragraph 12, who was uncertain whether holding back one’s openings [nekeivav] and transgressing bal teshaktzu is Biblical or rabbinic; there he wrote on Yevamot “five Amoraim uprooted the chapter heading of Rosh Hashanah”—see there. See Responsa Beit Ephraim, Even HaEzer vol. 2 siman 2 on this; see below siman 92. Erech Lechem of Maharikash wrote that holding back flatulence does not violate bal teshaktzu—see siman 103, end; see Magen Avraham end of sub-paragraph 4.
