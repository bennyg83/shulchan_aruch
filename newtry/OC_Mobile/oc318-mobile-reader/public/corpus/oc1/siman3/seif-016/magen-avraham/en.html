“To urinate.” It appears in Niddah 13a: with a thick cloth it is permitted to hold the organ if it does not warm [arouse] it. The Rif brought it in Shabbat ch. 14 and Even HaEzer 182:3—not like the Ra’avad in Niddah ch. 2 who wrote this was said only regarding terumah; accordingly the Rif should not have cited it.
