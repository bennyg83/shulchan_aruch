15) There: When there are no partitions—but if there are partitions, even a single partition to the west suffices, for the Shechinah is west (Beit Yosef); Levush Orach Chayim s. 3; Sheyarei Knesset Hagedolah s. 1—he should turn his back to the west. Yeshu’ot Yaakov there Even HaEzer s. 5, Raavad s. 8; some permit even if the partition is on the east when he turns his back east (Magen Avraham, Raavad there). Tosafos Shabbat “Keitzad” “S. Plaim”; it also fits Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 4 s. 2—the partition mentioned is not precise but conventional, one partition also suffices, Tosafos there. But Perisha/Magen and Yeshu’ot Yaakov there forbid if the partition is on the east—and their view warrants concern. See Rav Neuwirth (Shemirat Shabbat Kehilchata) s. 5; partition height—Rav Ben Ohev Chayim there s. 9: it need not be ten handbreadths high provided the exposure is not visible, so if short he relieves right next to it so it shields him. Some say even with partitions avoid east-west lechatchila; it is proper to heed them where possible. Where one can build the privy initially, do not place it east-west but north-south (Raavad s. 7, Ben Ohev Chayim there); see Perisha/Magen s. 5. Some are careful not to build a privy except north-south.
