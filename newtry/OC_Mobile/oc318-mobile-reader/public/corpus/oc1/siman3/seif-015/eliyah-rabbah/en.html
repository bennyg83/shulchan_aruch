[טו] “To rub [for pleasure],” etc. Chelkas Mechokek in Even HaEzer cites Sefer Chasidim 176: if one fears he might sin with another’s wife or his own niddah Heaven forbid, better to emit seed wastefully than come to eshet ish or niddah—only he should fast forty summer days or sit in the cold in winter—end of his wording. Our ruling there that this sin is graver than all Torah sins means when the yetzer incites him deliberately; here where desire comes on him, better wasteful emission than transgressing with another’s wife or a niddah as above—not like Beit Shmuel there. See later siman 240 on these laws.
