[15] To rub, etc. The Chelkas Mechokek in Even HaEzer, siman 23, wrote in the name of Sefer Chasidim, siman 176, that if a person fears that he may stumble with a married woman or with his wife while she is a niddah, Heaven forbid, it is better for him to emit seed in vain than to come, Heaven forbid, to the prohibition of a married woman or a niddah, provided that he fasts forty days in the summer or sits in ice during the winter; end of his words. And that which we hold there, that this sin is more severe than all the sins in the Torah, means when he incites the yetzer hara against himself. But here, where the desire came upon him, it is better that he emit seed in vain than that he transgress, Heaven forbid, with a married woman or a niddah, as mentioned above, unlike the Beis Shmuel there. See below, siman 240, for more of these laws.
