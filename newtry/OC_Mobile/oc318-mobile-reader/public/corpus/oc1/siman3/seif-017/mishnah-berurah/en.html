(31) "You shall not make yourselves detestable" — and if one holds himself back from urinating, he transgresses on account of "you shall not make yourselves detestable" and on account of "there shall not be among you a sterile one." One who holds himself back from passing gas does not transgress "you shall not make yourselves detestable." Also, the prohibition of "you shall not make yourselves detestable" is set aside before human dignity, such as one who holds back relieving himself until he finds a discreet place, and the like. One should be very careful to wipe well, for feces in its place [on the body] with any amount [is a problem], as written in siman 76, seif 5. It is good to wash the anus with water or saliva.
