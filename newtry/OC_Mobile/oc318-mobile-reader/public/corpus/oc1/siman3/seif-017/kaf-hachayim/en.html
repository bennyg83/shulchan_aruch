17) [Section 8] When relieving in a field, etc.—so too from a house behind a wall he may relieve even if his companion hears him sneeze—Eliyahu Rabbah s. 10, especially per Gemara Berachot 62a; Eliyahu Rabbah s. 8, Raavad s. 12, and Maharatz s. 3, Ben Ohev Chayim there s. 7—unlike Levush who seems to distinguish field from city—Eliyahu Rabbah there.
