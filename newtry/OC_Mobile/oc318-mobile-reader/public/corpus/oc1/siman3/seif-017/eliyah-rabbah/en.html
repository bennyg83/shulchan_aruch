[טז] “One who restrains [relief],” etc. That is when he delays defecation or urination; “to press [urine] out” is not as implied later siman 103—so Lechem Chamudos 24a. Taz wrote: for children the reason is also lest they become unable to bear children. Ma’adanei Melech wrote bal tishaktzu applies only when he is forced to void the openings, not to ordinary arousal. See later siman 92 s. 2.
