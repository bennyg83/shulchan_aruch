“And below [the corona].” Even if the organ is in erection, he may hold from the corona and below—so it appears from Tosafos at the beginning of chapter Kol HaYad, s.v. ocheiz, etc., since they did not answer that there it speaks of one in erection, for it teaches “his organ trembled.” Eliyah Rabbah.

“The married man.” The Taz wrote that by strict law it is forbidden—see there; if his wife is not with him or she is a niddah, he is not called married for this matter—see Taz, Magen Avraham, and Even HaEzer 23, seif 4, and what they cite there.
