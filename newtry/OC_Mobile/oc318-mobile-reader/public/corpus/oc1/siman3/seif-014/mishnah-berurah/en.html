(25) Downward—and even if the organ is erect, it is permitted from the corona downward.

(26) Because he brings forth—that is to say, by touching the organ he comes to heat and improper thoughts and comes to this; and regarding the severity of this sin, see Even HaEzer siman 23.

(27) He is married—for he has bread in his basket and his yetzer is not so strong; but if she is a niddah or he is on the road, it is forbidden. See Even HaEzer siman 23 seif 4, that even a married man may not touch his organ at all except when he needs to urinate.

(28) One who is married — and if he stands in a place where he fears he might fall, one need not be stringent at all with a married man. [Aratzot HaChaim]
