(Taz s.k. 9) Beit Yosef siman 43—cited Taz s.k. 6 and Magen Avraham there—Magen Avraham already noted there is no mention of a lefty; since Beit Yosef wrote the reason he holds tefillin in the right is because he wipes with the left, it follows a lefty holds tefillin in his left when he wipes with his right—see Bekhor Shor Berachot 62a.
