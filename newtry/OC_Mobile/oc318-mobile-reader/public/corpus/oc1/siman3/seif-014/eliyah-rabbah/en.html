[12] That he should not grasp, etc. Meaning, even through a garment, unless it is with a thick cloth that does not warm; Niddah 13.
[13] From the corona and downward, etc. Even though it is erect, it is permitted to hold from the corona and downward. Therefore it was necessary to teach us that from the corona and downward means the side facing the ground. Even though at the moment when he comes to grasp it, it is from the corona and upward, it is not called downward and upward except relative to when it is not erect. So wrote the Beit Yosef.
[14] He is married, etc. The Taz explained that this is specifically when at that time he is commonly with his wife, in a manner where he has bread in his basket. Similarly, the Magen Avraham wrote that if his wife is not with him, or she is a niddah, it is forbidden. See Even HaEzer, siman 23, and the Taz to Yoreh Deah.
