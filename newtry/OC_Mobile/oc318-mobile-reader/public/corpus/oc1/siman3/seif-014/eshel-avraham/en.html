Married, etc. In section 17 Maran z”l wrote: a married man was not permitted to hold his member except to urinate, but to rub [for pleasure]—no. From this there is no ruling whether a married man may rub below the corona; only to urinate is permitted. See Beit Yosef what he wrote in the name of Beit Yosef in Semag, who brought a doubt of Maharil z”l on this regarding holding the member—see there. In Responsa Shevut Yaakov siman 4 he elaborated at length and ruled that even for a married man rubbing is not permitted except from the corona downward via a shirt specifically; before him, even in such a manner, it is forbidden.
