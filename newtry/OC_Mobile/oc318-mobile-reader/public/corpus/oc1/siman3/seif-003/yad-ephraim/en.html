Taz, sub-paragraph 9 — Yet the Beit Yosef writes in Orach Chayim siman 43 that one may not wipe with the right hand, etc. One can say his intent here was only lechatchilah; therefore for a left-handed person he would still hold that one wipes with the “left” of most people in order to satisfy the fourth reason [in the Gemara], and perhaps he may wipe whichever way he wishes since for the other reasons he should wipe with the right of most people—that is, his own left. But per Beit Yosef’s wording in siman 43 that it is forbidden—i.e., there is an actual prohibition—then one would have to explain how a left-handed person wipes and on which reason he relies. Therefore one must say that even for the fourth reason he may properly wipe with the right of most people, i.e. his own left; hence the Beit Yosef’s general statement that one may not wipe with the right means that a left-handed person also may not wipe with what he calls his right. See Eshel Avraham and the words of the Ateret Zekenim in Bikkur Shor on Berachot 62a on explaining the Taz; Mishbetzot Zahav cites him but the printed edition omitted the words “thus it should read.” Bikkur Shor writes that he referred to what was said in siman 43—see there.
