“Unless.” — Taz challenged Beit Yosef, who explained the Tur’s words as piety; in Even HaEzer siman 23 (a scribe’s error in the printed text) on Rambam, who permits when married—the Tur wrote, “and I wrote in Orach Chayyim that it is forbidden,” implying law, not piety; Rambam writes only “by law”—hence the meaning: from what they omitted about anxiety—one is not expert whether anxiety prevents [impure thoughts]; likewise with his wife present one is not expert who counts as “his wife with him,” and slander about children would be a Torah prohibition, and we rule stringently on doubt—Eshel Avraham—and Rabbeinu Yonah in Sefer HaYir’ah that stringency on doubt is not piety—Magen Avraham, letter 14.
