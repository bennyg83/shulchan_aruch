“Modestly at night.” Meaning: he should not uncover more than by day, but he need not distance himself. If no one is there he may defecate in the street—but not before gentiles (Avodah Zarah 17a). All this is for defecation; urinating is permitted even by day before many if there is danger. A woman should not stand exposed before a child, but [relieving] off to the side we have no issue with—Bekhorot 44a.
