“Modestly at night.” Meaning that he should not uncover himself more than by day; but he need not distance himself. If no person is there he may relieve in the street—but not before gentiles (Avodah Zarah 47a). All this is for defecation; but to urinate is permitted even by day before many if he needs to, because there is danger. A woman should not stand exposed before a child; but to relieve to the side we have no concern—Bechoros 44a.
