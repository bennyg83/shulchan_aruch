(22) Modestly at night—that is to say, he should not expose himself more than by day, and he should not expose himself until he sits, and the like; for before Heaven, may He be blessed, darkness is like light. But he need not distance himself. If no one is there, he may relieve himself in the street, provided he is careful not to do so where people walk, lest people be soiled because of him; but if someone is there, even a non-Jew, it is forbidden to relieve himself before him. All this is for defecation; but to urinate is permitted even by day before many people if he must, for he has no place—and even before a woman, since there is danger if he restrains himself. A woman may not stand to urinate even opposite a baby’s face, since there is immodesty; but to the side, where she is not urinating directly opposite his face, we have no concern.
