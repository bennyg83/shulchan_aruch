Seif 12 — One should relieve oneself modestly. Pesachim 112a: we say one who stands naked before a lamp will be stricken [with convulsions]. Ri Korkus copied this. In She’arei Chayyim (siman 620) he wrote: when one leaves the bathroom he should inspect the place he sat on so there is no filth—lest someone come suddenly, or at night, and sit on it. A person should not make one bathroom directly opposite another so that the privy opening is exposed toward the other bathroom or bathhouse.
