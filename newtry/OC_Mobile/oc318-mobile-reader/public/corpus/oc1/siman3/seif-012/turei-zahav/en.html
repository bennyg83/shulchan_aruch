“As in daytime.” — Rashi explains it limits peru’ah (how much genital area may show) to a handbreadth or two while seated; it also limits how far one must distance from others. As in the Gemara there: Raba by day would go up to a mil; at night he told Shmuel’s son, “Clear me a spot in the town alley.” Rav Ashi: even in one place one must distance at night—e.g. a corner. Rashi: if a corner is nearby, one should withdraw there even at night.
