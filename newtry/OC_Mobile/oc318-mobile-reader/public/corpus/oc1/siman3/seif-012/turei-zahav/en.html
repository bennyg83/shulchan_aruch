As in daytime — Rashi explains regarding exposure: a handbreadth or two while seated, end of his words; it comes to limit regarding distancing from a place, as in that case where it says there — Raba by day would go up to a mil, and at night he said to Shmuel's son, "Clear me a place in the alley of the town." Rav Ashi said: even say in one place one must distance at night, such as in a corner. Rashi explained: if there is a corner nearby, one must withdraw there even at night.
