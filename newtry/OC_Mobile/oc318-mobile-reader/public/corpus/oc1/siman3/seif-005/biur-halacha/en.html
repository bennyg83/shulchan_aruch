“To urinate in any direction is permitted.” The Vilna Gaon holds west is forbidden even for urinating because the Shechinah is westward and he exposes himself while urinating—as he wrote in s. 4—rather he should turn east, other directions, or where a partition adjoins him—end of quotation. The custom follows the lenient Shulchan Aruch; in our places where people wear trousers and do not customarily expose themselves, one may be lenient even for the Gra.
