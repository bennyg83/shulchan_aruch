Partitions — Baer Hetev. See Magen Avraham, who wrote in the name of Beit Yosef that a partition on the west side suffices; and Magen Avraham wrote that since urinating is permitted and we are not strict about exposure before him, therefore a partition behind him suffices; and Baer Hetev abbreviated this — see the later authorities and Bikkur Shor on Berachot, who elaborated on this at length, and there the law for relieving back-to-back is explained — see there; and see what is written in Shulchan Aruch. It is likewise forbidden to sleep between east and west: Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Maharam Padua — that is, his head to the west and his feet to the east — and I do not know why Baer Hetev omitted it. I saw someone who wrote to emend Magen Avraham "his head to the west," and he gives a weak reason according to what they said — Adam Harishon, his head in the Land of Israel and his body in Babylonia, etc. — see there; and this requires study, for Rashi on Chagigah folio 12 wrote that Adam Harishon had his head to the east; see Responsa Yaavetz volume 1 siman 46, who brought in the name of Likkutei Pardes on Rashi that there it states his head was to the east; see Responsa Shev Yaakov siman 3.
