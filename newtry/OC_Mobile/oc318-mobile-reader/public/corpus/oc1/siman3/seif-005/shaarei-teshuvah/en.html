Partitions — Baer Hetev. See Magen Avraham citing Beit Yosef: a partition on the west side suffices. Magen Avraham wrote that since urinating is permitted and we are not strict about exposure to the front, a partition behind him suffices; Baer Hetev abbreviated this—see later authorities and Bikkur Shor on Berachot, who elaborates at length; there the law for “back-to-back” [relieving] is explained—see there; and see what is written in Shulchan Aruch. It is likewise forbidden to sleep between east and west: Magen Avraham cites Maharam Padua—head to west and feet to east—and I do not know why Baer Hetev omitted it. I saw someone emend Magen Avraham to “head to west” [sic—text discusses confusion east/west] with a weak reason from “Adam Harishon’s head in Israel and body in Babel,” etc.—see there. This requires study: Rashi on Chagigah 12a writes Adam’s head was to the east; see Responsa Yaavetz vol. 1 siman 46, citing Likuttei Pardes to Rashi that there his head was east; see Responsa Shev Yaakov siman 3.
