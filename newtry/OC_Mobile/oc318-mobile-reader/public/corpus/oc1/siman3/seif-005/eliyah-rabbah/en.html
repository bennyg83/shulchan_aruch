[ה] “His wife is not with him,” etc. So Beit Yosef inferred from Rambam’s wording “they do not sleep.” The Perishah explained he spoke only of marital relations; “sleeping” parallels defecation—something repulsive and foul—end of quotation. It appears to me there is slight support from Chagigah: “Adam the first spanned from one end of the world to the other”—Rashi explained when he lay, his head was east and his feet west.
