9) [Section 3] If he wants to move his bowels, etc.—if he must relieve and cannot, he should stand and sit repeatedly until he relieves or distract his mind with other things. Gemara Shabbat 82a, Mateh Yehudah letter 5; what Magen Avraham s.k. 3 says—to walk four cubits and sit—requires study, for the Gemara did not mention four cubits for standing and sitting, only another remedy—two times forward or four times backward—see there. Later I saw in Chayei Olam al Magen Avraham s.k. 3 that he asked this on Magen Avraham and left it tzarich iyun.
