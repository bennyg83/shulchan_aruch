[ט] “With earthenware,” etc. In Eliyahu Zuta I wrote: the Bavli at the end of HaMotzi means wiping with a potsherd [ugni keli cheres] has no sorcery concern—this resolves Bach’s and later authorities’ difficulties here and in siman 12, and what Bach pressed there on Rashi—see there; I abbreviated and will explain further with Heaven’s help. The Yerushalmi end of HaMotzi: one may not wipe with a dog’s mouth, before a dog, with water a dog lapped, with water lacking forty se’ah, with something that entered fire and emerged, or with bathhouse water—see Eliyahu Zuta for a longer discussion.
