[9] With earthenware, etc. In Eliyah Zuta I wrote that it seems to me that the explanation of the Gemara at the end of chapter HaMotzi is that one who wipes with the rim of an earthenware vessel has no concern of sorcery. With this, all the questions of the Bach and the later authorities here and below in siman 312 are resolved, as well as what the Bach asked there against Rashi's explanation. Examine this carefully, for I have been brief, and below it will be explained further, with the help of Heaven. It is stated in the Yerushalmi at the end of chapter HaMotzi: one does not wipe with the mouth of a dog, nor before a dog, nor with water from which a dog lapped, nor with water that does not contain forty se'ah, nor with an item that entered fire and came out, nor with bathhouse water. See Eliyah Zuta for an extended discussion of this.
