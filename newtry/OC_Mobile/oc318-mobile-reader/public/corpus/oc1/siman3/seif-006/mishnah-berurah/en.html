(11) Between east, etc. — rather he should intend that the head of the bed be to the north and its foot to the south. In the responsa of Menachem Azariah he ruled as the Zohar wrote in Parshat Bamidbar that the head and foot of the bed should be east and west; he concluded there that the head should be to the west; so too in Aratzot HaChaim in the name of several Acharonim. Nevertheless, it is better lechatchilah to practice according to Shulchan Aruch, for in responsa Binyan Shel Simcha he wrote in the name of the Gra that he said the intention of the Zohar is also as the Gemara, not as the interpretation of Menachem Azariah above.

(12) Even when there is not, etc. — see Aratzot HaChaim that one need not be stringent in this except when lying naked without curtains around the bed.
