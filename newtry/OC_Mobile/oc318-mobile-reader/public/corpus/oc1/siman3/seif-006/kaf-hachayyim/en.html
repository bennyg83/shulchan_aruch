16) [Section 6] It is forbidden to sleep between east and west, etc.; Rashi explains aligning head and feet north-south; so Shulchan Aruch at the end of siman 240. However Zohar Bamidbar writes the opposite (the one who lays his bed north to south means head east feet west) Bach cites. Shaar HaMitzvos Bereishit: bed placement north-south parallels Adam’s creation and the upper stature; head east toward da'at called “east … I will bring your seed,” feet west Yismach Moshe as we note for lulav, right south chesed left north gevurah. Rashi in Likutei Pardes withdrew that view and agreed with kabbalists; Birkei Yosef s. 2; Tashbetz Yaakov siman 3 rules like Zohar. Though ShKH wrote in Hagahot Beit Yosef s. 4 that nowadays the world is not careful (see there) and Olas Tamid s. 6 “ke-Mar …”; still one should be careful like Zohar and Arizal.
