It is likewise forbidden to sleep along the east–west axis if his wife is with him; there is no difference between Eretz Yisrael and other lands, for when we say the Shechinah is “in the west” it does not mean specifically west of the Mikdash where the community is, but the west of the world—the Shechinah’s “west” (Mahari)—meaning his head should not be east and feet west or the reverse, but head north and feet south or the reverse. See Magen Avraham—they both intended the same point.
