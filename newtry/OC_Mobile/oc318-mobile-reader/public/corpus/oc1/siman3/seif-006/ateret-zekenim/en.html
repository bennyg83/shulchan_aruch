It is likewise forbidden to sleep between east and west if his wife is with him; there is no difference between Eretz Yisrael and other lands, for when we say the Shechinah is in the west it does not mean specifically west of the Sanctuary where the congregation is, but the west of the world—the Shechinah is there (Mahariv)—meaning his head should not be to the east and his feet to the west or the reverse, but his head to the north and his feet to the south or the reverse. See Magen Avraham—they both intended the same point.
