(15) He should relieve himself immediately — for his companion does not see his exposure. Similarly in a courtyard, behind the house wall. Even if his companion hears the sound of his sneezing below, it makes no difference, for hearing sneezing below entails no prohibition of modesty — only shame and embarrassment for people; one who is not particular is not particular.

(16) His exposure — meaning his uncovering, what he exposes of his flesh before or behind him; and even though one sees his body and hears his sneezing, it is permitted.
