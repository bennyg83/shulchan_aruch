[13] From the corona and downward, etc. Even though it is erect, it is permitted to hold from the corona and downward. Therefore it was necessary to teach us that from the corona and downward means the side facing the ground. Even though at the moment when he comes to grasp it, it is from the corona and upward, it is not called downward and upward except relative to when it is not erect. So wrote the Beit Yosef.
