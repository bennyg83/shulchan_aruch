“One who restrains.” — Taz: see siman 103 in Darkei Moshe printed separately, Even HaEzer siman 107—children and adults: there is bal teshaktzu, not only for withholding [urine]; Magen Avraham, letter 92. Know that there “bal teshaktzu” is rabbinic and was set aside for human dignity—see there, letters 92 and 103, and Magen Avraham there—and likewise Rambam, Hilchos Ma’achalos Asuros chapter 17, halacha 31—see there.
