“Because of sparks.” — Rashi: when the stream nears its end he fears sparks may fall on his feet and stops—but on high ground or fine dust there are no sparks (Berachot). His wording sounds like one would be forced to stop, which would be dangerous; yet we say one who forgot and entered a privy wearing head-tefillin waits until the first jet finishes, then there is no danger. Hence the Tur adds: we also worry sparks may fall on his feet so it looks like arched urine (semen), inviting slander about his children—standing, urine does not arc that way, nor does semen; the Tur still fears he may finish urinating standing without minding soiling his feet, and we worry from another angle. One can also resolve Rashi: he did not mean “fear he must interrupt,” but explains Rabbi Chiya’s “urine completes only sitting”—one who wishes to urinate standing and stop when almost done commits no sin; only one who must void completely has no remedy but sitting. Question on Beit Yosef’s proof (no fine dust): “Better slander on his children than be a momentary wicked person before Heaven”—holding the organ—he could stop near the end; why permit causing slander? Answer: they asked about one who lacks fine dust and cannot naturally stop before finishing.
