(Magen Avraham s.k. 8) What Taz wrote—a lefty wipes with his own left as Beit Yosef siman 43—is incorrect. Above I explained Magen Avraham: for Rava a lefty wipes with his own right, but we may not rule like Rava, and he disputes Taz on two counts—Taz adopts Rava’s reason as halacha against the other reasons and also holds a lefty wipes with his own left even for Rava. “He should wipe with his right” means there are two reasons he writes with the left—question: writing is not a Gemara reason; also the right has eating and trop, and for Rava the right has Torah-from-the-right too—Magen Avraham disputes Taz, Pri Megadim emends that he wipes with his own right even with two reasons, and the first difficulty stands. It appears to me to emend: he wipes with his own right for two reasons even though he writes with the left and ties tefillin—intending his earlier words disputing Taz on both counts; hence “it appears to me” means Taz agrees in such a case. For siman 27 s. 6 explains: if he writes with the left, his “right” is reversed—he lays tefillin on it though he does other work with the right—so he rules to wipe with his “right,” i.e. his left, even though it has the “writing” advantage; still he ties tefillin on it, yet the [physical] right has trop and eating—we reject one of two. If we do not rule like Rava—or all the more if we do—the right gains Torah-from-the-right per Magen Avraham against Taz. His “two reasons” means: even if we did not rule like Rava, one might say Taz rejects this ruling because for him halacha follows Rava and he also holds for a lefty Torah was given on most people’s left—then against the right’s two advantages the left has tying and Torah—do as you will—Magen Avraham teaches Taz agrees here: even writing with the left, most people’s right is still his “right” and Torah was given there; it is not treated like the left except for tefillin per Taz 43:7—see there and Bekhor Shor Berachot 62a.
