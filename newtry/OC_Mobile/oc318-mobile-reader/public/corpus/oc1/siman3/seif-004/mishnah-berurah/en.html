(6) A handbreadth — and not more, on account of modesty; and before him two handbreadths, for when he strains for bowel movement he may come to urinate, and two handbreadths are needed on account of urine spraying far. Bach wrote it is fitting to be stringent that before him too there should be only one handbreadth, but many Acharonim did not agree with him. One should be careful not to soil his clothes, body, or shoes there.
