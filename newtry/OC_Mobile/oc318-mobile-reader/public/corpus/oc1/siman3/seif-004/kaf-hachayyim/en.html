j) [Section 4] He shall not expose himself, etc.; when disrobing he should not stand exposed but only after sitting; after finishing he should cover before standing. Tamid 27b.
11) There: “Unless behind him a handbreadth,” etc.; this is the principal view per Maran, not Bach. Yad Aharon on Bach, Mateh Yehudah s. 6, Amudei Eretz s. 1, Perisha/Magen s. 2.
12) There: And two handbreadths before him (even if he passes small without large, two handbreadths before him) Perisha/Magen there.
13) There: Two handbreadths before; and if the place is filthy and clothes might soil, he need not be strict but uncover as needed for the place and minimize exposure as Chasdei Lemalachim wrote; wherever there is soiling concern one may be lenient in a partitioned privy with a closed door; therefore nowadays when most privies have partitions and a closed door people are not strict about exposure. Ben Ohev Chayim there s. 5.
