He may not uncover himself except one tefach behind and two before him; a woman, etc. Some say one before and two behind—this is the Tur’s text and so in Sefer HaYirah of Rabbeinu Yonah, Orach Chayyim, and Rashi in the Alfasi, chapter HaRo’eh. Reason favors this reading: before him he can take extra care not to soil his clothes with urine, since he sees what is before him—one tefach suffices; behind he does not see—two are needed. Nevertheless, one may be stringent like the stricter textual versions—that is, one tefach before and one behind.
