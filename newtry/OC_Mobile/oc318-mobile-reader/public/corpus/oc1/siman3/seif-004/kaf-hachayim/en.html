d) There in hagaha: Two men should not go together—and likewise two women should not enter together. Chasdei Lemalachim wrote to warn children in this—partly so they grow accustomed to modest matters and partly lest they grow accustomed to immodesty; even young children who “do not know evil”—for they said in the Gemara a woman should not rise to urinate before a nursing infant, kal va-chomer somewhat older children. Bnei Chayim year 1 Vayeitzei s. 6.
