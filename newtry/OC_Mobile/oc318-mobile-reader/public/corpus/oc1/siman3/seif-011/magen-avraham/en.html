“With dry grass.” Even nowadays, when we are not concerned for sorcery, it is forbidden because it cuts the flesh—see Shulchan Aruch 306.

“Hemorrhoids.” If he himself wiped with that [stone], or it was dry, or he wiped from another side—we have no issue with that (Gra). See 306:1.

“So they are accustomed [to wipe with flammable material].” I do not know the reason for this custom; since the masses follow it, “The L-rd preserves the simple” (Tehillim 116:6) (Darkhei Moshe). It appears to me [vele’aniyas da’ati] the reason is that sorcerers use it in witchcraft so teeth fall out. Nowadays we do not concern ourselves that this will reach sorcerers.
