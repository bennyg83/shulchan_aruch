He shall not wipe with pottery because of sorcery. Or Zarua: on a weekday, even with smooth vessel rims that are smooth and have no danger concern, he still does not wipe with them because of sorcery—rather with a pebble. But on Shabbos, where there is a pebble and there are vessel rims, he wipes with the vessel rims even though there is a sorcery concern, and he does not wipe with the pebble, for it is not fit for carrying (Bach).
