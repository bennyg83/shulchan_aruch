“Because of.” — Taz: Rashi per his surface reading; but Shulchan Aruch explains because of arched semen; Rashi concludes it is not danger but one who wishes to finish urinating, and it is good advice. From Berachot 25a–b there it was stated: if he forgot and defecated, he finishes the first stream—and likewise later siman 43 se’if 8; for urinating it was not said—but we learn from there that if he entered a fixed privy, forgot, and urinated, he finishes until the first jet, for there it says “returning stream,” etc. Magen Avraham 43:15—after one jet there is no danger like after one defecation “pillar.” The difficulty: then let Rabbi Elazar say he may stop—and Beit Yosef’s answer “perhaps in caravan sitting,” etc. His commentary implies when he stops there are no sparks at all—whence? Perhaps even when stopping there are some sparks; only when fully voiding are there many—see Magen Avraham letter 13 on this. See Bach Berachot 44a—he challenged him—not such a strong difficulty—see there.
