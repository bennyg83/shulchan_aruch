“Because of.” — Taz: Rashi according to his apparent view; but Shulchan Aruch explains on account of keri, and Rashi concludes it is not danger but one who wishes to finish urinating, and it is good advice. From Berachot 25a there it was stated: if he forgot and defecated, he finishes the first column — and likewise below siman 43 seif 8; for urinating it was not stated, but we learn from there that if he entered a fixed privy, forgot, and urinated, he finishes until the first jet, for there it says “returning stream,” etc. Magen Avraham 43:15 — after one jet there is no danger as after one defecation “pillar.” The difficulty: then let Rabbi Elazar say he may stop — and Beit Yosef’s answer “perhaps in caravan sitting,” etc. His commentary implies when he stops there are no sparks at all — whence? Perhaps even when he stops there are some sparks; only when he fully voids are there many — see Magen Avraham letter 13 on this. See Bach Berachot 44a — he challenged him — not such a strong difficulty — see there.
