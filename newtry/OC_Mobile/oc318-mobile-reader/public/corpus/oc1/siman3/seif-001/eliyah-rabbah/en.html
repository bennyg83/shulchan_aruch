[א] “And two shall not go [together],” etc. When there is concern for harmful spirits, another person may place his hand on [the first one’s] head through a window (Bach). It is taught on Shabbat: if he cannot defecate, he walks four amot, sits, stands, and sits until he can, or diverts his mind to other matters. He may not defecate standing—Sifrei, Parashat Ki Teitzei.
