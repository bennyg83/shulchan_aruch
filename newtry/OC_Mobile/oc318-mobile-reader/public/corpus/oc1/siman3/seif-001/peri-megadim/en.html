“But nowadays.” — The Taz, Eshel Avraham, Bach, and Shulchan Aruch write that we are not accustomed to recite it. The Levush implies it is forbidden to recite because it appears arrogant (michazi k’yuhara); Beit Yosef likewise writes, citing Avudraham, that it appears arrogant.
