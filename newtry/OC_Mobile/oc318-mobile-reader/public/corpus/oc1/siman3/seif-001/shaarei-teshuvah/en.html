Shulchan Aruch — “But nowadays we are not accustomed [to recite it].” See Machazik Berachah citing Mitzvot Shemurim, which urges saying it; Maharam Paprash wrote that the Arizal innovated to say it even nowadays, and thereby one avoids idle thoughts of Torah in the bathroom—see there. Perhaps those remarks were only for individuals whose craft is Torah and not for others; as he wrote there in the name of Mekor Chayyim of Maharach Kohen—that it looks like arrogance—see there.
