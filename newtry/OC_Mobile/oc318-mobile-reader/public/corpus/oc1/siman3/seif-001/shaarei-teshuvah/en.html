In Shulchan Aruch — "But nowadays we are not accustomed." See Machazik Berachah citing Mitzvot Shemurim, which warns to say it; and Maharam Paprash, may his memory be a blessing, wrote that the Arizal, may his memory be a blessing, innovated to say it even nowadays, and thereby one is spared idle thoughts of words of Torah in the bathroom — see there. Perhaps these words were said only for individuals whose craft is Torah and not for others; as he wrote there citing Mekor Chayyim of Maharach Kohen, may his memory be a blessing — that it appears like arrogance — see there.
