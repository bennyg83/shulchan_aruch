“And two shall not go [together].” If one is afraid, another may place his hand on [the first person’s] head through a window (Berachot 62a, incident of Rava — Gemara).

“He shall not speak there.” It is taught in Sanhedrin: they enacted that women should talk [audibly] at the privy because of yichud; see Even HaEzer 22:6. Where the privies are in town there is no such concern.
