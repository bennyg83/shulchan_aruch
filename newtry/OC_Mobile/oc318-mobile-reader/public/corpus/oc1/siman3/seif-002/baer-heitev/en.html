“Two people [shall not go together].” If one is afraid, another may place his hand on his head through a window—Shas, chapter HaRo’eh (Berachot 62a), incident of Rava. Magen Avraham.

“He shall not speak there.” All the more so he shall not eat. It is taught in Sanhedrin: they enacted that women should converse at the privy because of yichud; see Even HaEzer 22. Where the privies are in town there is no concern.
