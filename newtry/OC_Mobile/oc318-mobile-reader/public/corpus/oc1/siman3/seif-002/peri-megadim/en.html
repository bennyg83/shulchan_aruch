“Behind him.” — The Taz notes Bach cites a reversed text: a handbreadth before him and two behind; a woman—a handbreadth behind and nothing before. The reason is she need not be careful before her body; behind her a handbreadth suffices—unlike a man, who must be careful before himself too, hence two handbreadths behind. One could raise that we should worry about two textual traditions and [hold] only a handbreadth before and after—see there. We rule like the Mechaber and Rabbi Yochanan’s silence [toward the stringency] concedes agreement. For us, [we give] children two handbreadths even without adults present, for per Beit Yosef’s reading there is no difficulty for Bach, and we need not divide between needing caution from two sides or not.
