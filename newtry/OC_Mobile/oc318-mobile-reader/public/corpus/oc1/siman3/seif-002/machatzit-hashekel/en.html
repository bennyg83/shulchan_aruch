Mishnah Berurah s.k. 2—“they shall not speak there”: Sanhedrin 19a—they enacted that women should talk [audibly] at the privy because of yichud, i.e. lest a man enter a privy where a woman already is without realizing and become secluded with her; by chatting they signal that a woman is inside so he will not enter. Where privies are in town there is little yichud concern—Rema, Even HaEzer there.
